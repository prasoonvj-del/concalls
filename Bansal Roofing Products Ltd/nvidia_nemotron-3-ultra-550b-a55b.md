**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY growth across revenue and profitability, completion of Phase 5/6 expansions, commercial launch of solar MMS, and progress on insulated panel JV.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: raised (FY27 revenue target increased from Rs 180–200 crore to "cross Rs 200 crore")
- Tone and demand view: More confident on execution with expansions completed and solar MMS generating initial revenue (135 MT supplied); demand visibility improved with order book of Rs 30 crore plus H1 revenue of ~Rs 96 crore (management cited Rs 120 crore including October)
- New topics: Insulated panel JV (Durapuf) with Rs 25–30 crore capex, >50% stake, first-year revenue target Rs 50 crore; solar MMS commercial metrics (135 MT supplied, 200 MT orders, 1000 MT FY27 target); capacity utilization specifics (50% current, 40% with new capacity); labor automation plans (robotic welding); Gujarat industrial policy subsidies (25% capital, 5% interest) on term loans for solar machinery
- Old topics resolved: Phase 5/6 expansion completed (was under construction); solar MMS machine commissioned and trial production successful (was commissioned in Q1); Mumbai office closure not mentioned
- Promises delivered: Phase 5/6 completed by mid-September as guided; solar MMS machine operational in Q1 as guided; revenue growth strong (35.99% YoY H1)

### Highlights
- Phase 5 & 6 manufacturing expansions completed and operational; revenue contribution expected in 2-3 months as departments shift
- Solar Module Mounting Structure (MMS) business: high-speed roll-forming line commissioned in Q1, trial production successful, 135 MT supplied in Oct, 200 MT orders in hand, targeting 1000 MT (Rs 9-15 crore) by FY27 end
- Insulated panel JV (Durapuf Insutech Panels Pvt Ltd) proposed: Rs 25-30 crore capex, BRPL >50% stake, partner handles operations; first-year revenue target Rs 50 crore, gross margin 15-20%, net 6-7%; line capacity 2000 m/shift
- Geographic concentration: 91% revenue from Gujarat; expanding to Maharashtra (Vapi, Surat), MP, Rajasthan
- Product mix: Roll forming ~55%, PEB ~45% (Q2); PEB 45%, Roll forming 50%, Other 5% (per Q&A)
- Customer profile: Roll forming 80% repeat; PEB 90% new customers
- Installation/services revenue: ~6-7% of turnover
- Outsourcing minimal: ~0.23% of turnover
- Labor cost rising; automation in progress (HB welding, planning robotic welding)
- Advance policy: 30% standard; difficult to increase due to market norms
- Payment terms: Suppliers 7 days (some advance); Customers 80-100% advance, credit only to large proven clients (30 days)
- PEB project execution: ~4 months; bottleneck: labor availability during festivals
- Competition: Segmented market; large players (Kirby, Interarch, Pennar, M&B) target >10,000 ton orders; BRPL focuses on up to 5,000 ton, competitive pricing

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue target raised to >Rs 200 crore (from Rs 180-200 crore)
- FY28 priority: Increase capacity utilization to 65-70% (from ~50%), target revenue >2x FY27 (Rs 400+ crore)
- Solar MMS: Target 1000 MT (Rs 9-15 crore) by Mar 2027; machine capacity 25 MT/day, potential 2000 MT/year
- Insulated panel JV: First year Rs 50 crore revenue, +Rs 25 crore/year thereafter; commissioning timeline subject to finalization
- Margin outlook: EBITDA margins to improve with capacity utilization; steel price volatility causes ±0.5-1% margin fluctuation; inventory hedging (30% advance used to pre-buy materials)

### Demand & industry commentary
- Gujarat remains core market (91% of sales); expansion targeting Maharashtra (Vapi, Surat), MP, Rajasthan
- Solar MMS demand: Engaging 1000+ EPC contractors; 1000-2000 MT inquiries in hand; first customer (Tanvolta Energy) gave positive feedback
- PUF panel demand driven by data centers, cold storage, pharma, clean rooms, MNC factories, warehouses; export potential noted
- Order book ~Rs 30 crore; average PEB project size Rs 3 crore (~17 projects for Rs 50 crore PEB revenue); roll forming has 200-250 customers
- No credit offered to solar MMS buyers except reputable EPCs; working capital cycle expected to lengthen slightly

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel price volatility primary margin driver: stable prices support margins, fluctuations cause ±0.5-1% impact
- Company hedges by pre-buying materials using 30% customer advance
- Solar MMS margins lower than core (gross 4-5%, net 2.5-3% per Q1 call; not reiterated but implied "low margin, fast moving")
- Insulated panel JV expected gross margin 15-20%, net 6-7%
- Labor costs rising due to compliance (PF, gratuity); automation reducing headcount per unit (10 to 4 workers), robotic welding planned
- Other expenses expected to decrease with new facilities (shared overheads)

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phase 5 & 6 completed: adds ~300-400 tons/month lightweight fabrication capacity for PEB; total premises 3 lakh sq ft fully utilized
- Solar MMS line: High-speed roll-forming machine (25 MT/day) operational since Q1
- Proposed insulated panel continuous line: Rs 25-30 crore capex via Durapuf JV; 2000 m/shift capacity; one-shot investment (line costs Rs 20 crore)
- No major capex in Q2 except routine machinery
- FY28 focus: Utilize existing capacity (PEB 15,000 TPA, Purlin 3,600 TPA, Roofing 15,000 TPA post-expansion)

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth increased to Rs 4483.44 lakhs (Sep 2026)
- Net debt/equity 0.13x (up from 0.07x) due to term loans for solar MMS machinery
- Gujarat industrial policy: 25% capital subsidy, 5% interest subsidy on term loans; effective cost to be reimbursed
- Trade receivables reduced to Rs 162.8 lakhs from Rs 350.47 lakhs
- Inventory stable at Rs 1987.5 lakhs
- Current ratio 1.45x (down from 1.57x)
- Cash flow not a problem per management

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Steel price volatility impacts margins despite inventory hedging
- Labor availability risk during festivals (Navratri, Chhath) causing absenteeism
- Solar MMS working capital cycle longer (30-45 days credit to EPCs) vs historical <2 weeks
- Insulated panel JV not finalized: investment structure, capex, timeline subject to change
- Capacity utilization low (~50%); ramp-up dependent on labor hiring and order inflow
- Geographic concentration in Gujarat (91%); expansion to other states may have lower profitability due to logistics

Bansal Roofing delivered 46% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, completed Phase 5/6 expansions, launched solar MMS commercially (135 MT supplied), and advanced a Rs 25-30 crore insulated panel JV, targeting Rs 200+ crore FY27 revenue and doubling thereafter via capacity utilization ramp-up.
