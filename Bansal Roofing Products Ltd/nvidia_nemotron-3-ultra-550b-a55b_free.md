**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY growth in revenue and profitability, capacity expansion completed, solar MMS commercialization underway, insulated panel JV planned.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: raised (FY27 revenue target increased from Rs 180 crore to Rs 200 crore)
- Tone and demand view: Remains positive; management more confident with Phase 5&6 operational and solar MMS generating initial revenue.
- New topics: Insulated panel JV (Durapuf) detailed with financial projections (Rs 50 cr first year revenue, 15-20% gross margin); solar MMS actual supply (135 tons) and revenue (Rs 1.21 cr) disclosed; Phase 5&6 completed; net debt/equity increased to 0.13x; receivables sharply reduced.
- Old topics resolved: Phase 5&6 construction completed (was under construction); solar MMS machine commissioned and commercial production started.
- Last quarter's promises delivered: Phase 5&6 completed by mid-September as guided; solar MMS machine commissioned in Q1 (June 30) and trial production successful; revenue target raised.

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Revenue: Rs 49.69 crore (Rs 4968.76 lakhs), up 45.77% YoY.
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 4.23 crore (Rs 422.78 lakhs), up 64.95% YoY; margin 8.47% (+97 bps YoY).
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 2.62 crore (Rs 261.82 lakhs), up 79.19% YoY; margin 5.27% (+98 bps YoY).
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 95.58 crore, up 35.99% YoY; EBITDA margin 8.74% (+75 bps); PAT Rs 5.28 crore, up 51.77%.
- Net worth: Rs 44.83 crore (Sep 2026); Net debt/equity: 0.13x (vs 0.07x Mar 2026).
- Current ratio: 1.45x (vs 1.57x); Trade receivables: Rs 1.63 crore (vs Rs 3.50 crore); Inventory: Rs 19.88 crore (vs Rs 19.46 crore).
- ROCE ~31%, ROE ~24% for H1 FY27.
- Solar MMS: 135 tons supplied, 200 tons orders in hand, targeting 1000 tons by March 2027.
- Capacity utilization: ~50% (without new capacity), ~40% (with Phase 5&6); target to increase 15-20 percentage points over 12-18 months.

### Highlights
- Phase 5 and Phase 6 manufacturing expansion completed and operational; added 300-400 tons/month lightweight fabrication capacity for PEB.
- Solar Module Mounting Structure (MMS) business: dedicated high-speed roll-forming machine commissioned in Q1, trial production successful, commercial supply started in Q2 (135 tons supplied, Rs 1.21 crore revenue); 200 tons orders in hand (Rs 1.8 crore); targeting 1000 tons (Rs 9-15 crore) by FY27 end.
- Proposed insulated panel JV (Durapuf Insutech Panels Pvt Ltd) with individual partner: estimated capex Rs 25-30 crore, BRPL stake >50%, first year revenue target Rs 50 crore growing Rs 25 crore/year, gross margin 15-20%, net margin 6-7%; investment structure and timeline not finalized.
- FY27 revenue guidance raised to Rs 200 crore from Rs 180 crore; H1 revenue Rs 95.58 crore, order book Rs 30 crore plus Rs 120 crore executed.
- Geographic concentration: 91% revenue from Gujarat; targeting Maharashtra (Vapi, Surat), MP, Rajasthan for diversification.
- Product mix: Roll forming ~55%, PEB ~45% in Q2.
- Repeat customers: Roll forming 80% repeat; PEB 90% new customers.
- Outsourcing minimal (~0.23% of turnover); 100% internal production for projects.
- PEB project execution cycle: ~4 months.
- Labor availability key bottleneck (festival migrations); automation initiatives (HB welding, planning robotic welding in 1-2 years).
- Rooftop solar 300 kW meets 53% of factory power consumption (up from 45% in Q1).
- Gujarat industrial policy subsidies: 25% capital subsidy, 5% interest subsidy on term loans for solar MMS machinery.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue target: Rs 200 crore (raised from Rs 180 crore).
- Solar MMS: conservative target 1000 tons by March 2027 (Rs 9-15 crore revenue); machine capacity allows up to 2000 tons.
- Capacity utilization: aim to increase by 15-20 percentage points over next 12-18 months (2-3% per month).
- Insulated panel JV: first year Rs 50 crore revenue, +Rs 25 crore/year thereafter; commissioning timeline subject to finalization.
- FY28 priority: improve utilization of existing infrastructure, strengthen execution, deepen customer relationships, develop adjacent opportunities; target more than double FY27 revenue.
- EBITDA margins expected to improve with capacity utilization; steel price stability key for margin expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PEB market segmented: large players (Kirby, Interarch, Pennar, M&B) target >10,000 ton orders; BRPL focuses on up to 5,000 ton orders, considers itself leader in this segment.
- Solar MMS demand driven by EPC contractors; 1 MW solar plant requires ~18 tons of MMS; management sees significant opportunity with 5 GW annual projects.
- Insulated panels (PUF) demand growing from data centers, cold storage, pharmaceuticals, clean rooms, MNC factories, warehouses; export potential noted.
- Gujarat remains core market (91% of revenue); expansion targeting Vapi/Surat (Maharashtra), MP, Rajasthan.
- Order book: Rs 30 crore plus Rs 120 crore executed in H1; average PEB order size Rs 3-5 crore, largest recent Rs 25 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel price volatility primary margin driver; margins fluctuate 0.5-1% with price movements; company hedges by purchasing 30% advance against orders.
- Solar MMS: low-margin, high-volume business (gross margin 4-5%, net 2.5-3% per Q1 call); competitive pricing.
- Insulated panels: expected gross margin 15-20%, net margin 6-7%.
- Labor costs rising due to compliance (PF, gratuity); automation reducing headcount per task (10 to 4 workers); robotic welding planned in 1-2 years.
- Other expenses expected to decrease with new facilities due to shared overheads (security, housekeeping).
- Installation revenue ~6-7% of turnover.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phase 5 & 6 completed: added 300-400 tons/month lightweight fabrication capacity; shifting departments ongoing, revenue contribution expected in 2-3 months.
- Q2 capex limited to routine machinery additions; no major new capacity expansion.
- Proposed Durapuf JV: continuous insulated panel line, capex Rs 25-30 crore (one-shot investment), timeline not finalized.
- Current capacity utilization: ~50% (excluding new capacity), ~40% (including new capacity).
- Two-shift production model; rooftop solar 300 kW (53% power consumption).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth Rs 44.83 crore (Sep 2026) vs Rs 42.20 crore (Mar 2026).
- Net debt/equity 0.13x vs 0.07x; increase due to term loans for solar MMS machinery.
- Current ratio 1.45x vs 1.57x.
- Trade receivables reduced to Rs 1.63 crore from Rs 3.50 crore.
- Inventory stable at Rs 19.88 crore vs Rs 19.46 crore.
- Supplier payments: 7 days (advance for POSCO/JSW/Tata).
- Customer terms: 80-100% advance for most; 30-day credit only for large corporates (L&T, Adani, etc.).
- Expect interest subsidy (5%) and capital subsidy (25%) under Gujarat policy to offset borrowing cost.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Steel price volatility directly impacts margins (0.5-1% fluctuation).
- Labor availability and cost escalation (festival migrations, regulatory compliance).
- Working capital pressure: current ratio moderated, net debt increased.
- Solar MMS: competitive market, low margins, credit risk (mitigated by avoiding credit sales to non-EPC customers).
- Insulated panel JV: not finalized; capex may increase, timeline may slip; execution risk.
- Geographic concentration: 91% Gujarat; lower profitability outside due to logistics costs.
- Advance payment limited to 30% (market standard); cannot increase to 50-70% due to customer resistance.

### Notable analyst Q&A

Management clarified: no money invested yet, only board approval; target is existing trading company (Durapuf) to become manufacturing JV; partner to handle operations; BRPL stake >50%; concept stage, investment in 1-2 months.
2. **Manish Kela (Individual):** Asked capacity utilization with new phases. Management: 50% without incremental, ~40% with; will consider new project at 70% utilization. Confirmed FY27 Rs 200 crore target reasonable (not conservative), FY28 utilization focus.
3. **Lakshman Easwaran:** Asked PUF panel rationale and potential. Management: PUF panels used in data centers, cold storage, pharma, MNCs; already supplied Rs 1.5 crore to game zone, copper factory, ESIC hospital; first year revenue Rs 50 crore minimum, up to Rs 75 crore at full capacity.
4. **Vaibhav Kapur (Swaraj Holdings):** Asked insulated panel margins and investment structure. Management: gross margin 15-20%, net 6-7%; BRPL stake >50%; one-shot investment (line costs Rs 20 crore); leveraging Durapuf's existing distribution + BRPL's network; confident of Rs 50 crore first year sales.
5. **Aditi Ghadge (Edelweiss MF):** Asked revenue split (PEB 45%, Roll forming 50%, Others 5%), repeat vs new (Roll forming 80% repeat, PEB 90% new), installation revenue (~6-7%).

### One-line takeaway
Bansal Roofing delivers strong 46% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, completes Phase 5&6 expansion, commercializes solar MMS (135 tons supplied), raises FY27 target to Rs 200 crore, and details a Rs 25-30 crore insulated panel JV, while managing steel price volatility and labor constraints.
