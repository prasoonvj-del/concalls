**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong ARR-driven RevPAR growth, margin expansion, and expansion pipeline execution despite renovation headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (no explicit guidance change; management remains positive on H2)
- Tone and demand view: shifted from foreign arrival headwinds due to West Asia crisis to "encouraging signs of revival in foreign bookings" and "healthier growth in Q3"
- New topics: Wildflower Hall bid won (revenue from Q4, 47.1% revenue share), Hyderabad land acquisition (<10 acres, Trident 300 keys), Bangalore Hebbal ground breaking early next calendar year, Mauritius recovery post UK travel ban lift, Rajgarh Palace EBITDA positive in Q3, Oberoi Grand opening confirmed September 2028, one-time finance cost from Oberoi Delhi lease settlement
- Old topics disappeared: West Asia crisis/Iran-US war impact, Hormuz crisis power/fuel cost pressure, employee working hours reduction initiative, Rajgarh Palace ramp-up concerns, Kolkata construction halt incident
- Last quarter's promises delivered: Mumbai renovations completed (Oberoi Mumbai 34 keys, Trident Nariman Point 135 keys, Trident BKC 80 keys all back in inventory), Rajgarh Palace progressing to EBITDA positivity in Q3, foreign bookings recovery evident, expansion pipeline advanced (Hyderabad land acquired, Hebbal ground breaking imminent, London ongoing, Goa approvals pending)

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue growth: 16% YoY; EBITDA growth: 19% YoY; PAT: Rs 153 cr (vs Rs 142 cr, +21% YoY)
- Standalone revenue and EBITDA growth: 16% each
- Overall RevPAR (domestic incl. managed): +18.3% YoY; occupancy 72%→71%; ARR growth compensated occupancy dip
- Owned hotels RevPAR: +22% YoY; occupancy 74%→71% (on reduced inventory 76% occupancy, RevPAR ~Rs 16,650)
- Luxury segment (Oberoi) RevPAR: +20.9% vs industry +18.2%; Upper Upscale (Trident) RevPAR: +14.8% vs industry +10.1%
- RGI improved 121%→130%; 13/15 hotels ranked 1st/2nd in STR benchmarking
- MICE share of room nights: 12%→16% YoY; Direct share: 48%→45%
- OFS (flight catering) revenue: Rs 150 cr (+20% YoY)
- Cash reserves: >Rs 1,100 cr; capex funded by operating cash flow, small funds dilution
- Renovation keys: Oberoi Mumbai 34 (done), Trident Nariman Point 135 (done), Trident BKC 80 (done), Bangalore 18 (phased, ongoing)
- Expansion: ~1,100 owned keys by 2030 (incl. Bangalore 7.63 lakh sq ft commercial); ~1,900 managed keys by 2031
- Hyderabad: freehold land <10 acres, Trident 300 keys; Bangalore Hebbal: 1.7M sq ft, 2 hotels + commercial + retail, ground breaking early next calendar year
- Oberoi Grand Kolkata: renovation ongoing, heritage building, opening September 2028, 197 keys
- Wildflower Hall: revenue booking from Q4 FY27, 47.1% revenue share with state government
- Rajgarh Palace: EBITDA positive in Q3 FY27; first full season, strong feedback
- Mauritius: Q2 occupancy ~67% after UK travel ban lifted first week of Q2; Indonesia, Egypt, Marrakesh performed well
- Finance cost elevated due to one-time lease settlement at Oberoi Delhi (prior periods), will normalize

### Highlights
- Industry Q2: occupancy +1-3% YoY, ARR +8-10% YoY; domestic air traffic contracted but hotel demand buoyant
- EIH outperformed competition on MPI, ARI, RGI; 13/15 hotels 1st/2nd in STR
- Oberoi brand RevPAR +20.9% vs luxury segment +18.2%; Trident +14.8% vs upper upscale +10.1%
- BRICS summit boosted NCR ARR in September; Vanyavilas Ranthambore open 2/3 months vs full closure last year
- Foreign bookings revival: Q2 increase, healthier growth expected in Q3
- Awards: 7 hotels Michelin Key Guide (2/1 keys); Oberoi Rajgarh Palace "finest hotels list 2027"
- Managed pipeline: ~1,900 keys by 2031 (mostly domestic, some international)
- Goa: design final stages, awaiting approvals; London: work ongoing on site; Tirupati: land allocation changed, slight delay
- Bhubaneswar: expansion planning stage (not EIH hotel); Raidurg Hyderabad: Trident hotel 2-year commitment, plus Oberoi management contract with Prestige

### Guidance & outlook
- Management positive on Q3/Q4 if no unknown global events; "fingers crossed for good Q3 and Q4"
- Foreign bookings healthy for Q3; geopolitical situation only risk
- Renovations largely done except Bangalore (phased 18 keys); avoid large renovations in Q3/Q4
- Open to acquisitions (brownfield preferred) if suitable opportunity arises; cash reserves >Rs 1,100 cr support flexibility
- No specific numerical guidance provided

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand robust; foreign arrivals recovering after West Asia crisis impact in prior quarter
- MICE segment growing (12%→16% share); direct bookings share declining (48%→45%)
- NCR benefited from BRICS summit; Jaipur/Ranthambore from shorter Vanyavilas shutdown and off-season rate pushes
- International portfolio strong: Mauritius rebounded post UK travel ban lift; Indonesia, Egypt, Marrakesh performed well
- Industry supply limited, supporting ARR growth trajectory

### Costs, margins & raw materials

- Finance cost one-time spike from Oberoi Delhi lease settlement (prior periods), will normalize
- Most expense lines grew in line with revenue; no specific margin percentages disclosed
- Power/fuel cost pressures from prior quarter (Hormuz crisis) not mentioned
