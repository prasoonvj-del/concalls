**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong ARR-led RevPAR growth of 18.3%, market share gains (RGI 121% to 130%), and healthy cash position funding ~3,000-key expansion pipeline.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained – positive outlook for H2 reiterated with more concrete visibility on foreign booking revival and renovation completion.
- Tone and demand view: Q1 flagged West Asia crisis hurting foreign arrivals; Q2 reports foreign bookings reviving with healthy Q3 forward bookings. Domestic demand remains robust.
- New topics: Wildflower Hall operational from Q4 (47.1% revenue share), Hyderabad land acquisition for 300-key Trident, Mauritius recovery post UK travel ban lift, Rajgarh Palace EBITDA breakeven in Q3. Old risks (West Asia impact on foreigners, Hormuz-driven fuel costs) not mentioned.
- Last quarter's promises: Mumbai renovations completed as indicated; Kolkata Grand on track for Sept 2028; London ongoing; Goa awaiting approvals; Hebbal ground-breaking early CY2027; Rajgarh ramp-up progressing better than expected.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue growth: 16% YoY; EBITDA growth: 19% YoY; PAT: Rs 153 cr (vs Rs 142 cr, +21% YoY)
- Standalone revenue and EBITDA growth: 16% YoY
- Overall RevPAR growth: 18.3% YoY (ARR-driven; occupancy 71% vs 72% last year)
- Owned hotels RevPAR growth: 22% YoY (occupancy 71% vs 74%; on reduced inventory 76% with RevPAR ~Rs 16,650)
- Luxury (Oberoi) RevPAR growth: 20.9% vs industry 18.2%; Upper Upscale (Trident) RevPAR growth: 14.8% vs industry 10.1%
- RGI improved from 121% to 130%; 13 of 15 hotels ranked 1st/2nd in STR benchmarking
- MICE share of room nights: 16% vs 12% YoY; Direct share: 45% vs 48%
- OFS revenue: Rs 150 cr (+20% YoY)
- Cash reserves: >Rs 1,100 cr; capex in H1 funded by operating cash flow
- Renovations: Mumbai (Oberoi 34 keys, Trident Nariman Point 135 keys, Trident BKC 80 keys) completed; Bangalore 18 keys ongoing
- Expansion: ~1,100 owned keys by 2030 (incl. 7.63 lakh sq ft commercial Bengaluru); ~1,900 managed keys by 2031
- Hyderabad: ~10 acres freehold for 300-key Trident
- Wildflower Hall: revenue from Q4 FY27, 47.1% revenue share with state govt
- Oberoi Grand Kolkata: renovation ongoing, opening Sept 2028 (197 keys)
- London: work ongoing; Goa: design final, awaiting approvals; Hebbal Bengaluru: ground-breaking early CY2027
- Mauritius Q2 occupancy ~67% after UK travel ban lifted early Q2; international portfolio (Indonesia, Egypt, Marrakesh) performed well
- Rajgarh Palace: EBITDA positive in Q3, first full season better than expected

- ARR growth across portfolio drove 18.3% RevPAR increase despite slight occupancy dip from renovation inventory
- Market share gains: RGI 130% (vs 121%), MPI and ARI improved vs comp set
- 13 of 15 hotels ranked 1st/2nd in STR; 7 hotels awarded Michelin Keys; Rajgarh Palace on world's finest hotels list 2027
- MICE contribution rose to 16% of room nights (from 12%)
- Foreign bookings reviving; healthy forward bookings for Q3
- Renovations largely complete except Bangalore (18 keys phased)
- Wildflower Hall to contribute from Q4; Rajgarh EBITDA positive in Q3
- Expansion pipeline on track: Hyderabad land acquired, London/Goa/Hebbal progressing

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects strong Q3/Q4 if no global unknown events; fingers crossed for good H2
- Foreign bookings healthy for Q3; geopolitical situation monitored
- Renovations mostly done for the year (except Bangalore)
- Wildflower Hall revenue from Q4; Rajgarh EBITDA positive in Q3
- Always open to acquisitions if suitable brownfield/operating hotel opportunities arise

- Vanyavilas shorter shutdown (open 2 of 3 months) and off-season rate drives helped Jaipur/Ranthambore
