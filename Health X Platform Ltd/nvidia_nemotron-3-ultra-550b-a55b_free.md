**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and margin expansion, but EBITDA loss continues due to strategic investments in technology, private label, and geographic expansion.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Long-term revenue target for FY29-30 revised down from INR 6,000 crores to INR 4,900 crores due to JITO private label pricing (one-third of branded), but EBITDA target raised from 5-6% to "more than 5-6%" on lower revenue. Gross margin trajectory guided: 8-9% in FY27, 9-10% in FY28, 10-11% in FY29. JITO revenue contribution target 13% by FY29-30.
- Tone and demand view: Remains positive on 30%+ compounded growth; new geographies (Jharkhand, Odisha, Bihar, UP, Rajasthan) showing strong growth; Tier 2/3 penetration high (76% SastaSundar, 66% RetailerShakti).
- New topics/risks: RetailAir mobile SaaS launched (live on Google Play); AI consultation/counselling app delayed to Oct-Nov 2027; JITO franchise model expanded (52 live, 81 pipeline); merger SEBI observation expected by Dec 2026; private label margin warned as unsustainable at current 60%.
- Old topics disappeared: Less focus on Healthbuddy-to-JITO conversion; no mention of RetailerShakti EBITDA breakeven in Q3 FY27 (previously guided).
- Last quarter's promises: RetailAir launched as promised; JITO revenue grew from INR 79 lakh to INR 2.2 crores; Noida and Guwahati warehouse upgrades completed; new offices opened in Bhubaneswar, Patna, Jharkhand; Udaipur warehouse plinth completed; merger application filed with SEBI.

- Organic growth investment cost ~30% of incremental revenue vs acquisition cost 1-5x revenue; payback 3-4 years.

- Long-term targets: RetailerShakti INR 4,000 crores, SastaSundar ~INR 900 crores (revised from INR 2,000 crores), total INR 4,900 crores by FY29-30; EBITDA >5-6%; JITO 13% revenue contribution.
- IPM growth: 12% (up from 9%).
- AI consultation app launch target: Oct-Nov 2027.

### Highlights
- Revenue growth accelerated to 59% YoY (vs 58% in Q1) with gross margin expanding 80 bps YoY to 8.3%.
- Operating leverage improving: opex ratio down 160 bps YoY to 10.8%; EBITDA loss margin narrowed 170 bps YoY to 3.5%.
- SastaSundar B2C grew 39% YoY, 6th consecutive quarter of growth since regaining full control from Flipkart.
- RetailAir mobile-first billing/inventory SaaS launched live on Google Play; targets retailer adoption to reduce inventory from 40 to 10 days and working capital to 25%.
- JITO private label scaling: INR 2.2 crores this quarter (170% QoQ), 52 franchise outlets live, 81 in pipeline, 1,000+ SKUs.
- Geographic expansion: new offices in Bhubaneswar, Patna, Jharkhand; warehouses upgraded in Noida, Guwahati; large capacity additions planned (5.95 lakh sq ft).
- Merger/demerger progressing: SEBI observation expected Dec 2026, NCLT to follow.
- Management emphasizes capital discipline: no debt, cash-based model, organic growth investment at 30% of incremental revenue vs 1-5x for acquisitions.
- Tier 2/3 markets drive 76% of SastaSundar and 66% of RetailerShakti revenue; 90% orders delivered within 24 hours.
- Record: RetailAir launch delivers on Q1 promise; JITO revenue up 28x from Q1 (INR 79 lakh to INR 2.2 crores); warehouse upgrades completed as guided.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's medicine distribution consolidating toward fewer national distributors; Health X aims to be a survivor.
- Preventive care, devices, wellness, personal care growing faster than acute medicine with better economics.
- IPM growth accelerated to 12% from 9%, driven by 80 crore underserved population and women's bank account transfers (10% to medicine, 25-30% to trade generics).
- Tier 2/3 markets (76% SastaSundar, 66% RetailerShakti) are logistically difficult but served profitably within 24 hours for 90% orders.
- Chronic medicine suits online delivery (planned, fill rate 100%, traceability, pricing); acute medicine served via RetailerShakti pharmacies.
- JITO positioned as "trade generic" (branded generic) for first-time medicine buyers (70-80 crore) and time-cost-conscious gig workers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion driven by improved sourcing, private label mix, channel optimization; gross margin growing faster than revenue (77% vs 59% YoY).
- Operating leverage: opex ratio fell 160 bps YoY to 10.8% despite employee cost increase (INR 31 crores vs 27.7 crores) for new geography hiring.
- Employee cost rise due to capacity building ahead of revenue in new markets (Bhubaneswar, Patna, Jharkhand).
- Depreciation & finance costs only 0.8% of revenue, highlighting asset-light, unlevered model.
- Private label (JITO) current gross margin ~60% but management warns not sustainable; will normalize lower.
- Data monetization (RetailAir, podcast, retailer conference) expected to add ~1% to gross margin over time.
- Technology costs (AI, RetailAir, WMS) expensed through P&L; viewed as moat, not cost.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Consolidated EBITDA loss persists (3.5% of revenue) due to front-loaded investments in tech, logistics, private label, geographic expansion.
- JITO private label margin (~60%) explicitly flagged as unsustainable; will decline as scale increases.
- SastaSundar revenue growth target halved (100% to 50%) due to JITO pricing impact, though margin improves.
- AI consumer adoption risk: management delaying AI counselling launch 12 months to avoid complicating systems before user readiness.
- Retailer credit adoption (NBFC/bank partnerships) slow due to KYC/documentation hurdles; product launched but low uptake.
- Merger timeline dependent on SEBI/NCLT; Rohto Pharmaceuticals not selling shares, limiting buyback options.
- High reliance on West Bengal (~70% revenue historically), though new geographies scaling.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Q: Advantages of large vs small distributors - quantified metrics on delivery cost, fill rate, procurement pricing?**  
   **A:** Three advantages: bulk purchase discounts, lower operating cost per unit, geographic diversification. Quantification not possible; no market study exists; small distributors can be efficient too.

2. **Q: Healthbuddy delivery time and why customers wait vs faster options?**  
   **A:** Online for chronic medicine (planned, 100% fill rate, traceability, pricing); acute medicine via nearby pharmacy. Customers wait for fill rate, trust/genuity (barcoded), and discount on second-day delivery.

3. **Q: NBFC partnership for retailer working capital to attract credit-preferring retailers?**  
   **A:** Already partnered with 2 national banks; product launched but adoption slow due to KYC/documentation habits. Ready to deploy own funds too once retailers adopt structured credit.

4. **Q: Fulfillment center capacity and incremental spend for growth?**  
   **A:** Current centers cover 18 months to 3-4 years growth. 5.95 lakh sq ft new capacity in pipeline. Incremental cost for 2 years extra growth ~27-28% of incremental revenue; payback 3-4 years. Detailed segmentation not reportable per accounting standards yet.

5. **Q: Gross margin levers at scale (INR 1,000 crores/quarter) and JITO contribution?**  
   **A:** Four levers: data monetization (~1% margin), JITO private label, volume discounts, device/OTC/nutrition mix. Gross margin expansion 50-100 bps annually "safe side". JITO at INR 2.2 crores (60% margin, 170% QoQ); target INR 6-10 crores next quarter.

6. **Q: JITO brand performance and franchise expansion vs Healthbuddy?**  
   **A:** JITO INR 2.2 crores this quarter; 52 franchises live, 81 pipeline. Three models: Healthbuddy (last-mile delivery, no inventory), JITO Healthbuddy (adds JITO inventory), JITO independent franchise. Both channels aggressive. JITO revenue target 13% by FY29-30.

### One-line takeaway
Health X Platform delivers 59% YoY revenue growth with expanding margins, launches RetailAir SaaS and scales JITO private label, but accepts near-term EBITDA loss to build a capital-efficient, data-driven distribution platform targeting >50% ROE.
