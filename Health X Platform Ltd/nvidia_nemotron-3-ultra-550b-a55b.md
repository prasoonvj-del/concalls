**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong 59% YoY revenue growth, narrowing losses, launch of RetailAir SaaS, detailed capacity expansion plan, and clear long-term margin trajectory despite continued investment phase.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained 30% compounded growth target; but long-term revenue target for FY29-30 cut from INR 6,000 cr to INR 4,900 cr due to JITO private label pricing (lower revenue, higher margin), with EBITDA target raised above previous 5-6% estimate. Gross margin trajectory guided at 8-9% in FY27, 9-10% in FY28, 10-11% in FY29.
- Tone and demand view: Remains positive; emphasis shifts to JITO private label as strategic growth driver (target 13% revenue contribution by FY29-30) and data monetization (podcast, retailer conference, RetailAir). New geographies (Jharkhand, Odisha, Bihar, UP, Rajasthan) showing strong growth; West Bengal share declining but still dominant.
- New topics/risks: RetailAir launched (was in pipeline); AI consultation app delayed to Oct-Nov 2027 (6 months build + 6 months launch); merger SEBI observation expected end-Dec 2026; detailed warehouse expansion plan (5.95 lakh sq ft across 7 locations); JITO franchise model clarified into three tiers. No new risks flagged; West Bengal concentration risk persists but new markets scaling.
- Last quarter promises: RetailAir launched (delivered); RetailerShakti EBITDA breakeven target for Q3 FY27 not explicitly reconfirmed but consolidated EBITDA loss narrowed to 3.5%; JITO revenue grew from INR 0.79 cr to INR 2.2 cr (170% QoQ); Noida and Guwahati warehouse upgrades completed; merger process advanced to SEBI observation stage.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR 489 crore (Q2 FY27), up 59% YoY, 11% QoQ; H1 FY27: INR 895 crore.
- RetailerShakti (B2B): INR 429 crore, up 60% YoY, 18% QoQ.
- SastaSundar (B2C): INR 55 crore, up 39% YoY; 6 consecutive quarters of growth from INR 32 crore in Q1 FY26.
- Diagnostics: INR 0.8 crore.
- Gross margin: INR 40.7 crore at 8.3% of revenue, up 80 bps YoY (7.5%) and 40 bps QoQ (7.9%); gross margin grew 77% YoY vs revenue 59%.
- Operating expenditure: 10.8% of revenue, down 160 bps YoY (12.4%) and 80 bps QoQ (11.6%).
- EBITDA: Loss of INR 17.2 crore (-3.5% margin), improved 170 bps YoY (-5.2%); sequentially flat as % of revenue.
- PAT: Loss of INR 12.7 crore (-2.6% margin), improved from -5.0% YoY.
- Net working capital: 23 days (down from 28 days in June), 6% of revenue; inventory 35 days, receivables 2 days, payables 13 days.
- Employee cost: INR 31 crore (up from INR 27.7 crore QoQ) due to hiring for new geographies.
- Depreciation + finance cost: 0.8% of revenue.
- JITO private label: INR 2.2 crore cumulative revenue this quarter, ~60% gross margin, 170% QoQ growth; target next quarter INR 6-10 crore (INR 2-3.5 crore/month).
- JITO franchises: 52 live, 81 in pipeline.
- Tier 2/3 contribution: 76% of SastaSundar, 66% of RetailerShakti; 90% orders delivered within 24 hours.
- Sourcing: >90% purchases directly from pharma companies via authorized channels; zero purchase rights paid.
- Cash model: No meaningful debt; treasury income ~15% annualized on cash reserves (INR ~50 crore/year) but not deployed to preserve ROE.

### Highlights
- RetailAir mobile-first billing & inventory SaaS launched on Google Play; retailers billing on it today; solves adoption via sales-first, no inventory setup; AirPad enables one-click reorder on RetailerShakti; provides sell-out data layer, retention engine, and own revenue stream.
- Fulfillment capacity: West Bengal FC automated for 18 months growth; Noida new warehouse double capacity for 3-4 years; Guwahati upgraded for 3 years; new 5.95 lakh sq ft across Udaipur (18 months), Lucknow, Patna, Bhubaneswar, West Bengal, Guwahati.
- Data monetization: SastaSundar Health & Happiness podcast at 60,000 subscribers generating pharma ad revenue; first-ever 10,000-retailer conference planned Jan 2027; RetailAir data valuable for pharma companies; target ~1% gross margin addition from data.
- Private label pivot: JITO priced at one-third of branded generics; targeting 13% revenue contribution by FY29-30; shifts SastaSundar growth from 100% to 50% YoY (volume same, price lower) but raises profitability; revised FY29-30 revenue target INR 4,900 cr (RetailerShakti INR 4,000 cr + SastaSundar INR 900 cr) vs earlier INR 6,000 cr.
- Merger/demerger: SEBI observation letter expected end-Dec 2026; NCLT filing within 15 days; 4-6 months for approval; effective 1 April 2026.
- Healthbuddy model: 6-7% of sales to franchisee, 3% cost, 4% retention; INR 10 lakh/month sales yields INR 40,000 income; inventory from central FC, JITO brands trialed at stores.
- NBFC/Bank credit partnerships live (2 national banks + NBFCs) but adoption low due to KYC/documentation; own funds also available.

### Guidance & outlook
- 30% compounded annual growth target maintained.
- Gross margin trajectory: 8-9% in FY27, 9-10% in FY28, 10-11% in FY29.
- EBITDA loss to close via 2% gross margin expansion + 2% operating cost reduction from scale; target EBITDA margin above previous 5-6% estimate on revised INR 4,900 cr revenue by FY29-30.
- JITO revenue target 13% of total by FY29-30; next quarter target INR 6-10 crore.
- AI consultation/counselling app launch targeted Oct-Nov 2027 (after 6 months build + 6 months adoption wait).
- RetailAir adoption data to be shared next quarter.
- Merger completion expected mid-2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India medicine distribution consolidating toward fewer national distributors; Health X aims to be a survivor via scale, capital discipline, trust.
- Preventive care, devices, wellness, personal care growing faster than acute medicine with better economics; category expansion targeted there.
- Tier 2/3 markets (76% SastaSundar, 66% RetailerShakti) served profitably within 24 hours for 90% orders; built for that India first.
- Chronic medicine suits online (fill rate, trust, pricing); acute medicine served via RetailerShakti/Healthbuddy network.
- Women's bank account transfers (state schemes) driving 10% into medicine market, 25-30% into trade generics; JITO positioned as trade generic with brand identity.
- Time cost of labor rising; home delivery of chronic meds saves daily wages for gig workers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion driven by improved sourcing, private label mix, channel optimization; gross margin growing faster than revenue (77% vs 59% YoY).
- Operating leverage: opex/revenue down 160 bps YoY, 80 bps QoQ.
- Employee cost up INR 3.3 crore QoQ for new geography hiring (capacity ahead of revenue).
- Incremental growth investment cost ~27-28% of incremental revenue, payback 3-4 years.
- Technology/AI costs expensed through P&L (not capitalized); viewed as moat, not cost.
- No receivable risk (2-day cycle), cash-based model.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Existing FCs: West Bengal (18 months headroom), Noida (3-4 years), Guwahati (3 years).
- New capacity: 5.95 lakh sq ft total across Udaipur (80k sq ft, 18 months), Lucknow (80k), Patna (20k then 1.2 lakh), Bhubaneswar (20k then 1.2 lakh), West Bengal (1.2 lakh), Guwahati (75k).
- Warehouse ownership mixed: leased for speed, owned land via state MOUs (Udaipur own land); pharma-grade requirements (RCC framed, temperature controlled).
- AI-led picking/sorting/routing in FCs; manpower cost per order falling; new centers on same automated stack.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No meaningful debt; fully capitalized; all decisions in boardroom.
- Net working capital 23 days (6% of revenue), down 18% QoQ.
- Receivables 2 days on INR 489 crore quarter; zero credit risk.
- Payables 13 days, inventory 35 days.
- Treasury income ~15% annualized (INR ~50 crore/year) but not deployed to avoid diluting ROE.
- Investments declined INR 70 crore in H1 FY27 deployed into FC infrastructure (Noida INR 7 crore, Kolkata upgrade, new warehouse sign-ups).
- Merger/demerger pending SEBI observation (expected Dec 2026), then NCLT.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Consolidated EBITDA still negative (-3.5%) due to front-loaded tech, logistics, market expansion, and private label investments.
- West Bengal still dominant (~70% revenue per prior quarter; not explicitly restated but implied).
- JITO private label at very early stage (INR 2.2 crore quarterly); margin ~60% not sustainable per management.
- RetailAir adoption unproven; trial phase only.
- AI consumer adoption uncertain; launch delayed to Oct-Nov 2027.
- Merger timeline dependent on regulators (SEBI, NCLT).
- NBFC/bank credit adoption low due to retailer KYC/documentation friction.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Large vs small distributor economics (Emkay):** Mittal cited three advantages for scale: bulk procurement margins, lower per-unit operating costs, and geographic diversification smoothing. Quantification not possible due to lack of market data.
2. **Retailer financing via NBFCs (Helios):** Already partnered with 2 national banks and NBFCs; technology ready but adoption low due to KYC/documentation burden on retailers; own funds also available for structured credit.
3. **Healthbuddy unit economics (Helios):** Franchisee earns 6-7% of sales, 3% cost, 4% retention (INR 40k/month on INR 10L sales); inventory from central FC, JITO brands being trialed at stores for acute meds.
4. **Fulfillment capacity & incremental cost (Perpetuity):** Detailed 5.95 lakh sq ft expansion plan across 7 locations; incremental growth investment ~27-28% of incremental revenue with 3-4 year payback; detailed cost breakdown not shared due to accounting standards.
5. **Gross margin levers at scale (Perpetuity):** Four levers: JITO private label, volume procurement discounts, category mix (devices/OTC/nutrition), data monetization (podcast, retailer conference, RetailAir sell-out data). Management affirmed 50-100 bps annual gross margin expansion is achievable.
6. **JITO strategy & revised targets (SJA):** JITO at INR 2.2 crore (60% margin, 170% QoQ); target INR 6-10 crore next quarter; three franchise models clarified; FY29-30 revenue target cut to INR 4,900 cr (from 6,000 cr) with 13% JITO contribution; EBITDA margin target raised above 5-6%; AI consultation app launch Oct-Nov 2027; merger SEBI observation expected Dec 2026.

### One-line takeaway
Health X Platform delivers 59% YoY growth with narrowing losses, launches RetailAir SaaS to lock in retailer ecosystem, and pivots decisively toward high-margin JITO private label (targeting 13% of revenue by FY29-30) while maintaining 30% compounded growth guidance and a clear path to gross margin >10%.
