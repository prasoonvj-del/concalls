**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong order book and capacity expansion, but execution ramp-up slower than earlier guided  

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (Rs 400 crore FY27 revenue target unchanged)
- Tone and demand view: shifted from post-IPO optimism to detailed execution focus; added rail electrification (fault locators) and refurbishment as new demand drivers
- New topics: Unit 3 land allotment in Gwalior, automatic fault locator development with Korean partner, Hind Rectifiers as developmental competitor, commodity price pass-through formula, refurbishment market potential; old topics dropped: InnoTrans exhibition, DSIR recognition, specific R&D expense figures
- Last quarter's promises: September target of 10 propulsion systems achieved 7; November target of 25/month revised to 15 in November and 25 in December; Unit 2 completion delayed to mid-December from "commencing" assembly; AFL prototype testing underway as planned; EMU prototype timeline consistent (Q2/Q3 CY2027)

### Key numbers
- New order: Rs 275 crore (received ~2 days before call)
- Production: 7 propulsion systems in September, 7 in October, targeting 15 in November, 25 in December (Q3 total ~50 vs earlier 70 target)
- Capacity: Unit 1 fully operational; Unit 2 to be fully built by 15 Dec 2026; Unit 3: 10-acre land in Gwalior (lease Rs 1/sq m/year), construction start Jan/Feb 2027
- Target run-rate: 40 propulsion systems/month from January 2027
- Propulsion system price: ~Rs 2 crore per set
- Automatic fault locator: ~Rs 1.25 crore per substation section set
- Maintenance revenue: 3-5% of PO value annually
- Payment terms: Indian Railways pays within 15 days of dispatch
- Raw material sourcing: ~50% import, ~50% domestic
- EBITDA positive expectation: Q3 or Q4 FY27
- FY27 revenue guidance: Rs 400 crore (maintained)
- Combined Unit 1+2 capacity: 50/month, operating at 40/month for stability
- EMU/MEMU prototype: expected Q2/Q3 CY2027
- AFL prototype clearance: expected ongoing month or early December 2026

### Highlights
- Received new Rs 275 crore order from Indian Railways, underscoring customer confidence
- Unit 2 near completion (mid-December), Unit 3 land allotted in Gwalior (10 acres, virtually free)
- MEMU/EMU propulsion development progressing; prototype expected Q2/Q3 CY2027
- Automatic fault locator (with PNC Technologies) in prototype testing; clearance expected early December
- Maintained FY27 revenue guidance of Rs 400 crore despite slower initial ramp-up
- Expect EBITDA turn positive by Q3/Q4 FY27 as high-cost development inventory consumed and value engineering kicks in
- Welcomed Hind Rectifiers' entry as developmental vendor; sees need for broader indigenous supplier ecosystem
- Identified large refurbishment market (15,000-16,000 existing locomotives, ~1,500/year reaching 15-year age)

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue target Rs 400 crore maintained
- Stable production of 40 propulsion systems/month from January 2027
- EBITDA positive by Q3 or Q4 FY27
- MEMU/EMU prototype ready Q2/Q3 CY2027, followed by RDSO trials
- Automatic fault locator prototype clearance by early December 2026, then RDSO approval and tender participation
- Unit 3 construction start Jan/Feb 2027, likely dedicated to EMU propulsion
- Order visibility strong; Indian Railways demand limited only by industry capacity

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways produces 1,500-1,600 locomotives/year; total fleet ~15,000-16,000
- Growing demand for EMU/MEMU, Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat, freight locomotives, metro/RRTS
- Rail electrification doubling (2x25 kV) drives demand for fault locators
- Refurbishment market potential equal to new locomotive market as fleet ages
- International opportunities opening for Indian propulsion system suppliers

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Initial margins constrained by high-cost components bought in minimum order quantities during development
- Component prices reducing with scale; value engineering pending to further lower costs
- Raw material split: ~50% imported, ~50% domestic
- IGBTs and capacitors secured with 2-3 suppliers, bulk POs placed, no supply issues
- Commodity price (copper, steel, aluminium) pass-through via IEEE formula in purchase orders
- Minor component shortages (sensors, LEDs, small electronics) being resolved

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Unit 1: fully operational and occupied
- Unit 2: completion by 15 Dec 2026, test setups being delivered
- Unit 3: 10-acre land in Gwalior MP IIDC (lease Rs 1/sq m/year), construction start Jan/Feb 2027, capex not yet quantified
- Combined Unit 1+2 capacity: 50 propulsion systems/month; will operate at 40/month for stability
- R&D Center Unit 2 adjacent to existing; test setups ordered, recruitment started
- Unit 3 likely dedicated to EMU/MEMU propulsion production

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Indian Railways payment cycle: within 15 days of equipment dispatch
- No specific debt, net debt, or working capital figures disclosed
- IPO proceeds raised for working capital to scale operations (per previous quarter)

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ramp-up slower than previously guided (50 sets in Q3 vs 70 target)
- Supply chain irregularities for minor components causing production volatility
- Initial margin pressure from high-cost development-phase inventory
- Unit 3 capex not yet estimated
- Dependence on international suppliers with lead times up to 6 months for some components
- Competitive intensity may increase as more vendors (e.g., Hind Rectifiers) qualify

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Paras Kulkarni (Ignite Capital) on Japan export order:** Management clarified the reported order is for a different propulsion system type; MV Electrosystems' product not qualified; cautioned against media reports.
2. **Paras Kulkarni on delivery shortfall vs 70-set Q3 target:** Management explained aggressive ramp-up from September start; minor monthly variations (±2-4 sets) due to component shortages; no mention of liquidated damages.
3. **Ayush Agarwal (MAPL) on order allocation drop to 20% from 25%:** Management said Indian Railways allocates 15-25% across suppliers to maintain supply chain; not due to aggressive price cuts.
4. **Ayush Agarwal on Hind Rectifiers competition and EMU update:** Management welcomed more indigenous suppliers; EMU/MEMU prototype expected Q2/Q3 CY2027 (MD leading development).
5. **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital) on supply chain for 40/month target:** Management confirmed bulk POs placed with domestic/international suppliers; components mostly under control; 4 test setups needed, 2 operational, 2 arriving by 15 Dec.
6. **Darshil Jhaveri on margin trajectory:** Management said PAT will improve dynamically over next 2 quarters as high-cost inventory consumed, volumes increase, and value engineering implemented; declined to give specific margin numbers.
7. **Moksha Shah on FY27 guidance revision:** Management maintained Rs 400 crore target, citing matured deliveries from January.
8. **Yash Sharda on automatic fault locator opportunity:** Management detailed 3-equipment set per substation section (~Rs 1.25 cr/set); potential across existing, doubling, and new lines.
9. **Rounak Bajpai on long-term revenue vision:** Management said orders not a constraint; target Rs 1,000-1,200 crore turnover naturally next year from loco propulsion, then EMU/MEMU addition.
10. **Khush Shah on market size speculation (1,500 to 1,000 locos):** Management explained push-pull arrangement (2 locos/train), freight corridors, and migration to distributed power increase propulsion revenue despite loco count changes.
11. **Khush Shah on maintenance and refurbishment:** Maintenance 3-5% of PO value annually; refurbishment market potential equal to new market as 1,500-1,600 locos/year reach 15-year age.

### One-line takeaway
MV Electrosystems is executing a multi-pronged capacity expansion (Unit 2, Unit 3, new R&D) to convert its strong order book into Rs 400 crore FY27 revenue, while adding MEMU/EMU propulsion and rail electrification products to drive the next growth leg.
