# MV Electrosystems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reaffirmed its FY27 targets and scale-up timelines, but highlighted initial component availability delays and elevated initial material costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 revenue guidance of Rs 400 crore; expects EBITDA to turn positive in Q4 FY27 (or potentially Q3 FY27).
- Tone and demand view: Transitioned from IPO preparation to active execution and testing infrastructure ramp-up; remains optimistic on long-term propulsion and EMU/MEMU demand.
- New topics or risks: Allocated 10 acres of land in Gwalior (Unit 3) virtually free of cost (Rs 1/sq meter/year); automatic fault locators developed under ToT with a South Korean company submitted for prototype testing; entry of Hind Rectifiers as a developmental vendor.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered 7 sets in September; revised Q3 target to 50 sets (7 in Oct, 15 in Nov, 25 in Dec) and maintained target run-rate of 40 sets/month from January 2027.

### Key numbers
- Dispatches: 7 propulsion sets delivered in September 2026 and 7 sets in October 2026; targeting 15 sets in November 2026, 25 sets in December 2026 (50 sets in Q3).
- Recent order win: Received an order of ~Rs 275 crore (representing ~20% allocation in the tender).
- Unit pricing: Locomotive propulsion system priced at ~Rs 1.96 crore to Rs 2.00 crore per set; automatic fault locator set priced at ~Rs 1.25 crore per set.
- Land allocation: 10 acres allocated at Gwalior MP IIDC industrial area at a nominal lease rate of Rs 1 per sq meter per year.

### Highlights
- Unit 2 setup is under execution and expected to be fully built and operational by mid-December 2026.
- Unit 3 land allocation letter of intent for 10 acres in Gwalior to be formally received; construction execution expected to start in January or February 2027.
- R&D Center Unit 2 established directly opposite the existing R&D center, with technical recruitment underway to support MEMU prototype development by Q2/Q3 CY27.
- Automatic fault locator (AFL) prototype under South Korean Transfer of Technology (ToT) is ready and submitted for testing, awaiting RDSO approval to participate in rail electrification tenders.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its FY27 revenue target of Rs 400 crore.
- Production expected to mature to a run-rate of 40 propulsion systems per month by January 2027.
- Expects EBITDA to turn positive in Q4 FY27, with a possibility of turning positive in Q3 FY27.
- Management expects Unit 2 capacity to reach 50 propulsion systems/month, maintaining a capped run-rate of 40/month to leave room for EMU/MEMU developments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Annual Indian Railways locomotive production runs at 1,500–1,600 units; total active fleet stands at 15,000–16,000 locomotives.
- Locomotives older than 15 years will require complete rehabilitation, creating a refurbishment market comparable in scale to the new propulsion market.
- High growth expected in MEMUs, EMUs, and intercity Chair Car trains as regional city clusters expand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Initial component purchases were made at higher prices due to low minimum order quantities (MOQ) during the prototype/initial phase, weighing on near-term margins; value engineering and volume scale are expected to drive lower raw material costs.
- Raw material supply is split roughly 50:50 between imports and domestic procurement.
- Key long-lead components such as IGBTs and capacitors are fully secured via long-term supply arrangements with multiple vendors.
- Contract pricing includes IEEE-indexed price variation formulas covering fluctuations in major raw materials (copper, steel, aluminum).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Two out of four required test setups are currently operational; the remaining two test setups are scheduled for installation by December 15, 2026.
- Gwalior Unit 3 capex plan is yet to be finalized as factory and office layouts are under design.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Indian Railways payment terms remain prompt, with receipts typically coming within 15 days of dispatch.
- Maintenance contracts account for 3% to 5% of total purchase order value, collected annually.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Short-term delivery irregularities caused by international component lead times extending up to 6 months for certain semiconductors/sensors.
- Tender allocations split among multiple qualified suppliers (ranging between 15% and 25%), limiting single-vendor market share gain.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ayush Agarwal (MAPL Value Investing Fund):** Asked about the ~20% allocation in the recent tender and new competitor entry (Hind Rectifiers). *Management answered that Indian Railways allocates quantities among counted suppliers (15%-25% band) to protect supply chain stability, and welcomed ecosystem expansion as indigenous demand across freight, passenger, and bullet train programs is large enough.*
- **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital):** Inquired about the supply chain security for scaling to 40 units/month in Q4 and raw material cost pass-through. *Management confirmed advance purchase orders are placed with global suppliers, test setups will complete by Dec 15, and contracts contain IEEE price variation formulas.*
- **Moksha Shah:** Asked if the FY27 Rs 400 crore revenue guidance will be revised due to slower initial ramp-up. *Management stated the Rs 400 crore target is maintained and achievable as dispatches reach mature levels in January.*
- **Yash Sharda:** Asked about the addressable market for automatic fault locators. *Management explained AFL sets (substation, mid-section, intermediate) sell at ~Rs 1.25 crore per set across existing lines, track-doubling projects, and new freight corridors, with full in-house manufacturing planned.*

### One-line takeaway
MV Electrosystems is overcoming initial component lead-time bottlenecks to scale production toward 40 propulsion sets per month by January 2027, maintaining its Rs 400 crore FY27 revenue target.
