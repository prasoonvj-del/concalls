**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong order book, capacity expansion on track, new product development progressing, guidance maintained.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (Rs 400 crore revenue for FY27)
- Tone and demand view: similar positive outlook, but more concrete on ramp-up timeline (January matured deliveries) and new Rs 275 crore order received.
- New topics: Unit 3 land allotment in Gwalior (10 acres), automatic fault locator development with South Korean TOT, MEMU propulsion prototype timeline (Q2/Q3 CY2027), refurbishment market potential quantified.
- Old topics that disappeared: InnoTrans exhibition participation decision, DSIR recognition (already achieved).

- Revenue guidance FY27: Rs 400 crore (maintained)

- Automatic fault locator set price: ~Rs 1.25 crore

- PAT margin target: 10%+ at full run-rate (from previous quarter)

### Highlights
- Received new order of ~Rs 275 crore from Indian Railways (CLW) post-Q1.
- Unit 2 (Palwal) to be fully operational by 15 Dec 2026, enabling 50 units/month capacity (will run at 40 for stability).
- Unit 3: 10-acre land allotted in Gwalior (MP IIDC) at nominal lease; construction to start Jan/Feb 2027.
- MEMU/EMU propulsion development: prototype expected Q2/Q3 CY2027, R&D center Unit 2 operational.
- Automatic fault locator (with South Korean PNC Technologies TOT): prototype testing underway, clearance expected Nov/Dec 2026.
- Indian Railways payment terms: 15 days post-dispatch; raw material price variation formula in contracts (IEEE-based).
- Welcomed Hind Rectifiers as potential competitor; emphasized need for indigenized ecosystem.
- Order allocation varies 15-25% per tender; no aggressive price wars.
- Refurbishment market for 15,000-16,000 locomotives could match new propulsion market size.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue target Rs 400 crore maintained.
- Matured deliveries from January 2027 at 40 propulsion systems/month.
- EBITDA positive by Q3 or Q4 FY27.
- PAT margins to improve continuously over next two quarters; 10%+ at full run-rate.
- MEMU propulsion revenue to start subsequent year after prototype approval.
- Automatic fault locator revenue to start after RDSO approval (expected soon).
- Long-term: Indian Railways demand exceeds industry capacity; no order visibility concerns.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways produces ~1,500-1,600 locomotives/year; total fleet ~15,000-16,000.
- Growing demand for EMUs, MEMUs, Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat, freight/passenger locomotives.
- Rail electrification doubling (2x25 kV lines) creates demand for fault locators.
- Refurbishment market for locomotives >15 years old could equal new production volume.
- International opportunities opening as Indian suppliers gain expertise.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Initial margins constrained due to low-volume component purchases at high prices (MOQ).
- Component prices reducing with bulk ordering; value engineering pending.
- IGBTs and capacitors (long lead items) secured with multiple suppliers, bulk POs placed.
- Minor components (sensors, LEDs) still facing minor supply issues, resolving.
- Raw material split: ~50% imported, ~50% domestic.
- Price variation clause in contracts (copper, steel, aluminium) based on IEEE formula.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Unit 1: fully operational.
- Unit 2: ready by 15 Dec 2026; capacity 50 units/month, will operate at 40 for stability.
- Unit 3: 10-acre land in Gwalior allotted (lease Rs 1/sq m/year); construction start Jan/Feb 2027; likely dedicated to EMU propulsion.
- R&D Center Unit 2: adjacent to existing; one floor manned; test setups ordered.
- No further machinery capex needed for current capacity; only test setups and construction.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds used for working capital to scale operations.
- Working capital requirement ~Rs 200 crore for Rs 1,000 crore turnover (from previous quarter).
- Payments from Indian Railways within 15 days of dispatch.
- Receivable days: ~15 days.
- R&D expensed (not capitalized); ~Rs 9 crore annually (previous quarter).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain irregularities for minor components (sensors, LEDs) may cause monthly delivery fluctuations.
- Initial scale-up phase: thin/negative margins due to high-cost inventory from development phase.
- Dependency on international suppliers for key components (IGBTs, capacitors) with long lead times.
- Execution risk in ramping to 40 units/month by January 2027.
- Capex for Unit 3 not yet quantified.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Paras Kulkarni (Ignite Capital) on Japan order:** Management clarified Japanese order is for different propulsion system; not applicable to MV Electrosystems. Warned against relying on media reports.
2. **Paras Kulkarni on delivery target (70 vs 50 sets):** Management explained monthly targets: 15 in Nov, 25 in Dec, 7 in Oct; minor fluctuations due to component shortages; matured run-rate by January.
3. **Ayush Agarwal (MAPL) on order allocation (20% vs 25%):** Management said allocation varies 15-25% per tender to keep supply chain intact; no aggressive price wars.
4. **Ayush Agarwal on Hind Rectifiers competition:** Management welcomed indigenized competitors; emphasized ecosystem need; Hind Rectifiers has developmental PO but not yet qualified.
5. **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital) on supply chain for 40 units/month:** Management confirmed bulk POs placed with domestic/international suppliers; test setups (4 needed) – 2 operational, 2 arriving by 15 Dec.
6. **Darshil Jhaveri on margins:** Management declined PAT specifics but said PAT dynamic, improving next two quarters; component costs dropping with volume; value engineering pending.
7. **Vignesh Iyer on IGBT/capacitor procurement:** Management confirmed long-term deals with 2-3 suppliers; bulk POs placed; no issues for these critical components.
8. **Moksha Shah on FY27 guidance and EBITDA positivity:** Management maintained Rs 400 crore guidance; EBITDA positive by Q3 or Q4 FY27.
9. **Yash Sharda on automatic fault locator opportunity:** Management estimated ~Rs 1.25 crore per set (substation + mid + two between); market includes existing lines, doubling, new lines, freight corridors.
10. **Rounak Bajpai on FY27 revenue math:** Management explained: 40 units/month * 3 months * Rs 2 crore = Rs 240 crore + regular business = Rs 400 crore achievable.
11. **Khush Shah on market size and refurbishment:** Management said 1,500-1,600 locomotives/year; refurbishment market could equal new market as 15,000+ fleet ages.
12. **Khush Shah on maintenance revenue:** Management said 3-5% of PO value annually, limited to original suppliers.

### One-line takeaway
MV Electrosystems is executing a rapid scale-up from 7 to 40 propulsion systems/month by January 2027, backed by a Rs 1,000-1,200 crore order book, new Rs 275 crore order, Unit 2 completion, Unit 3 land allotment, and two new product lines (MEMU propulsion, automatic fault locators) nearing commercialization, maintaining Rs 400 crore FY27 revenue guidance.
