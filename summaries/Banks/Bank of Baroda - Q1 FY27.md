# Bank of Baroda - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong core balance sheet and asset quality metrics were overshadowed by a large exceptional out-of-court settlement impact on net profit.

### Key numbers
- Global Business: Rs 30.5 lakh crore (+15.4% YoY)
- Global Advances: +17.4% YoY (Domestic +16.1%, International +23.3%)
- Global Deposits: +13.8% YoY (Domestic +14.7%, International +8.9%)
- Domestic CASA Ratio: 37.72%
- Operating Profit: Rs 8,127 crore (flat YoY)
- Net Profit: Rs 1,278 crore (after USD 600 million / ~Rs 5,680 crore exceptional out-of-court settlement for NMC group); Normalised Net Profit: Rs 5,528 crore
- Net Interest Margin (NIM): Global 2.77%, Domestic 2.93%
- GNPA: 1.99% (-29 bps YoY)
- Net NPA: 0.50% (-10 bps YoY)
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) including TWO: 93.28%
- Slippage Ratio: 0.91% (-25 bps YoY)
- Credit Cost: 0.29% (vs 0.55% in Q1 FY26)
- Capital Adequacy (CRAR): 16.30% (CET1 at 13.90%, Tier 1 at 14.41%)

### Highlights
- Bank entered into an out-of-court settlement with joint administrators of NMC Health PLC, NMC Healthcare Ltd, and NMC Holding Ltd, paying USD 600 million (~Rs 5,680 crore) on July 1, 2026, fully debited to Q1 FY27 P&L without admission of liability.
- Floating provisions of Rs 2,500 crore remain untouched and are earmarked for expected credit loss (ECL) migration.
- RAM advances grew strongly: organic retail +18.4%, agriculture +18.7%, organic MSME +20.3%.
- CRILC SMA 1 & 2 reduced to 0.07% of standard advances (vs 0.40% in June 2025).

### Guidance & outlook
- Credit Growth: Maintained at 12% to 14%.
- Deposit Growth: Maintained at 10% to 12%.
- Credit-Deposit (CD) Ratio: Operating band of 84% to 86%.
- Net Interest Margin (NIM): Maintained at 2.75% to 2.95%.
- Credit Cost: Below 0.60% (factoring potential ECL impact of 15-20 bps).
- Slippage Ratio: Maintained at 1.00% to 1.25%.
- Return on Assets (ROA): Full-year guidance under review; management expects ROA to exceed 1% in Q2, Q3, and Q4.
- Return on Equity (ROE): Maintained at 15% to 16%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted robust domestic economic parameters and retail/MSME demand, though remaining cautious due to prolonged global geopolitical headwinds.
- Treasury income declined YoY due to higher G-Sec yields (6.78% in Q1 FY27 vs ~6.10-6.13% in Q1 FY26).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of deposits reduced by 12 bps QoQ to 4.66%.
- Yield on advances stood at 7.37%.
- Staff cost increase over March was attributed to AS15 actuarial provisions tracking yield movements.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- IT budget (Opex + Capex) is in excess of Rs 4,000 crore.
- Planned addition of 7,000 headcounts in FY27.
- Evaluating international footprint expansion aligned with India's FTAs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Domestic CD ratio stood at 83.31%.
- Raised nearly USD 600-700 million via FCNR(B) so far, targeting USD 4 to 5 billion total mobilization across FCNR, MTN, and ECB routes by window close.
- Planned capital raising: Rs 8,500 crore of equity in the medium term (by March 2028) and ~Rs 6,000 crore of Tier 2 bonds in FY27 (subject to pricing).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential impact of ECL transition estimated at ~110 bps impact on CRAR (Rs ~12,000 crore total impact, amortized over 4-5 years) and 15-20 bps on credit cost.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Subrata Panda (Business Standard) on FCNR mobilization and NMC settlement:** Management stated they raised USD 700 million so far, targeting USD 4-5 billion. On NMC, management clarified the settlement was based on advanced trial stages and commercial prudence without admitting liability.
- **Joel Rebello (Economic Times) on the rationale for settling the NMC case:** Management explained that the trial stage and legal advice favored a commercial settlement to clear a legacy cross-border dispute overhang, while legal recovery actions against principal individuals continue.
- **Ashish Agashe (PTI) on credit growth guidance:** Management stated that despite outperforming, guidance is held at 12-14% due to macroeconomic uncertainties and deposit growth visibility.
- **Kunal Shah on the mechanics and non-disclosure of NMC transaction details:** Management reiterated that bilateral confidentiality clauses prevent disclosing transaction details, and verified that domestic books are insulated.
- **Jayant Kharote on NIM sustainability and ECL impact:** Management stated asset repricing (moving non-MCLR corporate books to MCLR) and lower incremental bulk deposit costs provide confidence to hold NIM guidance within 2.75%-2.95%.

### One-line takeaway
Bank of Baroda delivered robust core operating growth and asset quality improvements, but its net profitability was heavily impacted by a USD 600 million out-of-court settlement for the legacy NMC dispute.
