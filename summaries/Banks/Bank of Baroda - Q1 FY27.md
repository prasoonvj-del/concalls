# Bank of Baroda - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27 (Quarter ended 30th June 2026)
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong balance sheet growth and core operating metrics were offset by a sharp hit to net profit due to a legacy out-of-court settlement.

### Key numbers
- **Global Business:** Rs 30.5 lakh crore (+15.4% YoY)
- **Global Advances:** Rs 21+ lakh crore (+17.4% YoY); Domestic advances +16.1% YoY; International advances +23.3% YoY.
- **Retail, Agri & MSME (RAM) Advances:** Organic Retail +18.4% YoY (Home: +14.7%, Auto: +25.3%, Mortgage: +27.4%, Education: +10.8%); Agri +18.7% YoY; Organic MSME +20.3% YoY.
- **Corporate Advances:** +15.3% YoY.
- **Total Deposits:** +13.8% YoY (Domestic +14.7% YoY, International +8.9% YoY).
- **Domestic CASA:** +10.0% YoY; CASA Ratio at 37.72%; Domestic CD Ratio at 83.31%.
- **Operating Profit:** Rs 8,127 crore.
- **Net Profit:** Rs 1,278 crore (Impacted by an exceptional NMC legal settlement of USD 600 million / ~Rs 5,680 crore). Net Profit *ex-exceptional item* would have been Rs 5,528 crore.
- **Margins & Yields:** Global NIM at 2.77%; Domestic NIM at 2.93%; Yield on Advances at 7.37%; Cost of Deposits at 4.66% (-12 bps QoQ). Net Interest Income (NII) grew 9.5% YoY.
- **Asset Quality:** Gross NPA at 1.99% (-29 bps YoY); Net NPA at 0.50% (-10 bps YoY); Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR incl. TWO) at 93.28%; Slippage Ratio at 0.91% (-25 bps YoY); Credit Cost at 0.29% (vs 0.55% in Q1 FY26).
- **Capital & Liquidity:** CRAR at 16.30% (CET1: 13.90%, Tier 1: 14.41%); Average LCR at ~127%.
- **Returns:** ROA at 0.25% (1.10% ex-exceptional item); ROE at 3.89% (16.57% ex-exceptional item).

### Highlights
- **NMC Group Litigation Settlement:** Bank settled a legacy cross-border dispute involving NMC Group out-of-court for USD 600 million without admission of liability or wrongdoing. The liability was debited to the P&L in Q1 FY27. Discontinued claims in Abu Dhabi and UK courts.
- **Floating Provision Maintained:** The bank did not utilize its existing floating provision of Rs 2,500 crore to offset the settlement, preserving it fully for upcoming ECL transition costs.
- **Strong RAM Traction:** Organic MSME growth crossed 20% YoY, alongside robust growth in Auto (+25.3% YoY) and Mortgage (+27.4% YoY) retail loans.
- **Write-Off Strategy:** Technical write-offs were conservative at Rs 625 crore in Q1 FY27 compared to Rs 2,200 crore in Q1 FY26 to protect PCR, resulting in a marginal sequential uptick in absolute NPA numbers.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Credit Growth:** Maintained at 12% to 14% YoY.
- **Deposit Growth:** Maintained at 10% to 12% YoY.
- **CD Ratio:** Target operational band of 84% to 86%.
- **NIM:** Guidance maintained at 2.75% to 2.95%.
- **Credit Cost:** Maintained below 0.60%.
- **Slippages:** Guidance maintained at 1.00% to 1.25%.
- **ROA:** Full-year guidance under review due to Q1 exceptional item; management expects ROA to remain >1% for Q2, Q3, and Q4 individually.
- **ECL Impact Management:** Management estimates total net ECL transition impact at ~110 bps on CRAR (~Rs 12,000 crore total net provision requirement, against Rs 2,500 crore floating provisions already held). Credit cost impact expected at 15-20 bps per annum over the amortization period.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Credit demand across Domestic RAM and International syndications remains strong.
- Bank observed customer deposits shifting toward capital markets, resulting in high industry dependence on wholesale/bulk deposits over retail deposits.
- Global geopolitical prolonged conflicts remain a headwind, leading management to maintain conservative overall credit and deposit guidance despite Q1 outperformance.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating income was impacted by lower treasury gains (Rs 893 crore vs Rs 2,200+ crore YoY) due to G-Sec yield movements (6.78% vs 6.10% YoY).
- Interest income growth (+6.8%) outpaced interest expense growth (+5.2%).
- Staff costs rose sequentially due to AS-15 actuarial provisions tracking yield changes.
- Commission, exchange, and brokerage fee income declined YoY, which management plans to focus on and optimize in upcoming quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **IT Capex/Opex Budget:** Management earmarked in excess of Rs 4,000 crore for IT (Capex and Opex combined) in FY27.
- **Branch Expansion:** Added ~260 branches last year; planning to add ~7,000 headcounts in FY27 to support network expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Fundraising Plans:** Mobilized ~$700 million via FCNR(B) deposits to date (targeting $1 billion by month-end and $4–5 billion overall across FCNR, OFCB, and ECB routes by December 31).
- **Capital Raising:** Board approved medium-term equity raising plan of Rs 8,500 crore up to March 2028 and a Tier 2 bond raising target of Rs 6,000 crore in FY27, subject to pricing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Treasury and Fee Income Drag:** Fee income and treasury profits were lower YoY, weighing on operating profit growth.
- **Sequential Asset Quality Uptick:** Sequential gross and net NPA percentages increased marginally due to a lower denominator effect and lower write-offs in Q1.
- **Global Macro / Geopolitical Prolongation:** Management cited uncertainty over deposit flows and global economic spillovers as a reason not to raise full-year credit guidance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** Why did the bank pay USD 600 million to settle the NMC case if there was no admission of guilt?  
  **A:** Management stated the call was a prudent, legal-advised commercial decision based on the advanced trial stage, uncertainty, time, and prolonged litigation cost, aimed at eliminating a legacy overhang. Terms remain confidential, but recovery actions against principal individuals continue.
- **Q:** How will the bank manage the transition cost for ECL?  
  **A:** Management clarified the net ECL impact is ~110 bps on CRAR (~Rs 12,000 crore), of which Rs 2,500 crore is already covered by floating provisions. The remaining impact will be amortized over 4–5 years (~15–20 bps annual impact on credit costs), fully manageable within their capital buffer (CRAR 16.30%) and credit cost guidance (<0.60%).
- **Q:** Why did corporate loans de-grow YTD sequentially?  
  **A:** Management consciously let go of low-yielding, non-MCLR corporate assets to protect margins in an elevated cost-of-deposit environment.
- **Q:** What is the mobilization status and yield impact of the FCNR(B) program?  
  **A:** Bank raised >USD 600 million to date, targeting ~$4–5 billion total foreign currency inflows. Rupee deposit landed cost via swap is ~6.4-6.5%, helping lower dependency on high-cost domestic bulk deposits.

### One-line takeaway
Bank of Baroda delivered strong core business growth (+17.4% advances, +13.8% deposits) and robust asset quality, while absorbing a one-off USD 600 million legacy legal settlement without tapping its Rs 2,500 crore floating provision.
