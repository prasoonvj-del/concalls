# Punjab & Sind Bank - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported consistent business growth, improved profitability, and upward guidance on key performance parameters.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Business:** Rs 2,79,231 crore (+15.73% YoY).
*   **Deposits:** Rs 1,53,312 crore (+12.97% YoY).
*   **Advances:** Rs 1,25,919 crore (+19.28% YoY).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 412 crore (+40% YoY).
*   **Operating Profit:** Rs 617 crore (+22% YoY).
*   **NIM:** 2.77% (+17 bps YoY).
*   **Gross NPA:** 2.09%.
*   **Net NPA:** 0.61%.
*   **CD Ratio:** 82.13%.
*   **CASA Ratio:** 28.83%.
*   **ROA:** 0.88%; **ROE:** 13.21%.
*   **Capital Adequacy:** 16.55% (CET-1: 15.49%).

### Highlights
*   **RAM Growth:** RAM advances grew >31% YoY, now accounting for 61.37% of gross advances.
*   **Segmental Growth:** Retail grew 42%, Agri 16%, and MSME 28.69%.
*   **Fee Income:** Core fee income grew 23.6% YoY.
*   **Digital Adoption:** 12 products available via single QR code; 50%+ of vehicle loans and 40%+ of housing loans sourced via digital/STP processes.
*   **Asset Quality:** SMA percentage reduced to below 4%.
*   **Operational Updates:** GIFT City branch expected to be live by November end; forex trade module expected in Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Business Growth:** Management expects deposits to grow 13%–14% and advances 18%–20%.
*   **RAM Strategy:** Target RAM share of 65% for the current year, reaching 70% by March 2028.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management expects Gross NPA below 2% and Net NPA at 0.65% by March end.
*   **Recovery:** Target of Rs 1,000 crore+ in recovery and upgradation.
*   **Business Targets:** Aims for Rs 3 lakh crore business by end of FY27 and Rs 4 lakh crore by FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Treasury performance was muted due to geopolitical conditions and hardening yields.
*   Management expects NIMs to be positively impacted by recent and potential future repo rate hikes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Cost-to-income ratio marginally declined in Q2.
*   Management is investing heavily in IT infrastructure (cybersecurity, resilience operations center) to improve long-term efficiency.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Expanding network to 300 potential centers in top 200 districts.
*   HR transformation and capacity building programs are ongoing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Corporate Pipeline:** Rs 20,000 crore (Rs 12,000 crore pipeline + Rs 8,000–9,000 crore in-principle sanctions).
*   **ECL Impact:** Management expects an impact of ~5% of net worth (approx. Rs 700 crore); minimal impact on CRAR expected due to glide path.
*   **Credit Cost:** Steady-state credit cost under ECL framework expected at 50–60 bps.
*   **Capital Raising:** Bank is exploring the QIP route; merchant bankers have been appointed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   CASA ratio slipped to 28.83%; management noted challenges in maintaining CASA.
*   Co-lending has slowed down due to system integration requirements for RBI compliance.
*   Cybersecurity and DPDP Act compliance are identified as zero-tolerance areas requiring ongoing investment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Impact of ECL on capital?** A: Management stated an expected impact of ~5% of net worth (Rs 700 crore), which includes MTM on HTM investments.
*   **Q: Strategy on corporate vs. RAM mix?** A: Management intends to increase RAM to 70% by FY28; corporate book will be managed selectively based on pricing competitiveness.
*   **Q: Status of consolidation chatter?** A: Management stated they are not privy to any information regarding bank consolidation.
*   **Q: Digital/Tech spend?** A: Management noted they are investing heavily but staggering investments to ensure the bank derives full benefit in the near future.
*   **Q: Capital raising timeline?** A: Management is waiting for the right market opportunity to proceed with the QIP.

### One-line takeaway
Punjab & Sind Bank delivered strong operational growth and improved asset quality, with management maintaining an upward bias on guidance supported by ongoing digital and structural transformation.
