# City Union Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent business growth, improved asset quality, and expanding margins in line with expectations.

### Key numbers
- Total business crossed: Rs 150,000 crore
- Advances (YoY growth): Rs 72,442 crore (26% YoY, up from Rs 57,561 crore in 2QFY26); grew over Rs 4,800 crore QoQ (~7%)
- Deposits (YoY growth): Rs 83,893 crore (21% YoY, up from Rs 69,486 crore in 2QFY26); grew Rs 4,500+ crore QoQ (~6%)
- CD Ratio (average for Q2 FY27): 86%
- CASA ratio: 27%
- Gross NPA: 1.44% (down 29 bps QoQ from 1.73%); absolute Gross NPA reduction ongoing
- Net NPA: 0.48% (absolute: Rs 344 crore vs Rs 513 crore in 2QFY26)
- NIM: 3.87% (up from 3.78% in 1QFY27); 1HFY27 NIM at 3.83%
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 416 crore (up 27% YoY from Rs 329 crore)
- PAT (1HFY27): Rs 798 crore (up 26% YoY from Rs 635 crore)
- Cost-to-Income ratio: 45.96% (vs 49.16% in 2QFY26)
- ROA: 1.62% (vs 1.59% in 2QFY26); 1HFY27 ROA at 1.59%
- SMA-2 to total advances: 0.67% (vs 0.94% in 1QFY27 and 1.34% in 2QFY26)

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 1.5 lakh crore in total business, reaching the milestone in four quarters after crossing Rs 1.2 lakh crore in 2QFY26.
- MSME portfolio grew from Rs 40,262 crore to Rs 42,862 crore; secured retail loans grew from Rs 6,668 crore to Rs 7,802 crore; gold loans grew from Rs 20,715 crore to Rs 21,777 crore.
- Mobilized $69 million under RBI FCNR swap ($15 million from new-to-bank customers), swapped into INR and deployed for domestic loans.
- Sold accounts worth Rs 803 crore (mix of live and technical write-offs) to an ARC for a consideration of Rs 131 crore against provisions of Rs 792 crore.
- Provision of Rs 50 crore made towards contingent buffer for unseen future economic uncertainties.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Credit Growth:** Management aims to achieve credit growth 2% to 3% above the industry level.
- **NIM:** Projected in the range of 3.85% to 3.95% for Q3 and Q4 FY27.
- **ROA:** Expected to move above the 1.6% plus range (1.6% to 1.65%).
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** Expected to largely remain in the range of 45% to 47% for FY27.
- **Tax Rate:** Projected around 21% to 23% for the full year.
- **Deposit Growth:** Management expressed doubt on sustaining 20%+ deposit growth over the next two years, citing it as a challenge.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Credit demand supported by MSME, secured retail, and gold loans.
- Gold loan portfolios categorized into agri (bullet repayment based on harvest) and consumption (bullet/non-bullet). Management noted gold loans are primarily emotional products in South India (96% of gold loans are from Southern India).
- SME credit utilization improved to 71%-72% from 69% previously.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of deposits remained stagnant at 5.56% QoQ in 2QFY27; cost of funds decreased 4 bps QoQ to 4.74%.
- Operating expenses increased 24% YoY to Rs 562 crore due to annual wage hikes disbursed in Q2 and higher leave encashment provisions (Rs 32 crore).
- Establishment expenses increased 25% YoY to Rs 280 crore; other operating expenses increased 22% YoY to Rs 282 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bank's branch network has crossed 1,000 branches (up from 800 previously), with newly opened branches contributing consistently.
- Verticalization implemented for CASA, TASC, Government, NRI, and salary accounts.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total SMA to gross advances stood at 2.53% (SMA-0: 1.23%, SMA-1: 0.64%, SMA-2: 0.67%).
- Incremental ECL provision requirement estimated at ~Rs 299.42 crore as of September 30, 2026 (down from Rs 445 crore as of March 31, 2025), with unprovided part (~Rs 270 crore) to be adjusted against capital reserves with no P&L impact.
- PCR with technical write-off at 82%; PCR without technical write-off improved to 67%.
- Total technically written-off pool stands at Rs 900 crore (fully provided).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted that sustaining 20%-21% deposit growth over the next 12-18 months will be challenging due to competition and rising rates.
- Potential 10 bps increase in deposit costs anticipated in the upcoming quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Suresh Ganapathy (Macquarie Capital) asked about gold loan NPAs and auction rates:** Management clarified that out of Rs 9.5 crore Gross NPA in gold loans, only Rs 20 lakh went to auction, while the rest (Rs 9.3 crore) was fully repaid by borrowers within 45 days. Management does not do top-up loans or finance existing NPA customers.
2. **Suresh Ganapathy asked about deposit growth sustainability:** Management stated that sustaining 20%+ deposit growth for the next two years will be a challenge, though granular deposits (term deposits < Rs 3 crore form 48%) and new branch productivity provide comfort.
3. **Rohan M (Equirus Securities) asked about the rationale and collateral mix of the ARC sale:** Management explained that Rs 803 crore of legacy assets (predominantly >3 to 5 years old) were sold, comprising Rs 303 crore fully collateralized and Rs 500 crore without collateral (residual unsecured portions after security sales).
4. **Jai Prakash Mundhra (ICICI Securities) asked about the expected ECL transition impact:** Management clarified that the incremental ECL requirement is Rs 299.42 crore, which will be consumed via capital reserves with zero P&L impact.
5. **M.B. Mahesh (Kotak Securities) asked about gold loan yield headroom:** Management stated the blended gold loan yield is 10.8%, with a minor headroom of 5-10 bps as older loans roll over.

### One-line takeaway
City Union Bank reported robust Q2 FY27 performance with 26% YoY advance growth, expanding margins (NIM at 3.87%), and multi-quarter lows in Gross/Net NPAs, driven by disciplined secured lending and core MSME focus.
