# IDFC First Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a second consecutive quarter of PAT above Rs 1,000 crore, strong operating profit growth of 38.5% YoY, and an established profitability trajectory.

### Key numbers
- **PAT:** Rs 1,045 crore (up 196.5% YoY); core PAT at Rs 1,036 crore (vs Rs 897 crore in Q1).
- **Loan Book / Advances:** Rs 3.47 lakh crore (up 30.1% YoY, 13.6% QoQ including FCNR; up 22.2% YoY, 5.7% QoQ excluding FCNR-backed loans).
- **Total Deposits:** Rs 3.48 lakh crore (up 25.9% YoY, 11.8% QoQ); Customer deposits crossed Rs 3.4 lakh crore (up 26.9% YoY, 14% QoQ).
- **FCNR(B) Mobilization:** USD 3.6 billion (Rs 34,390 crore) raised under RBI scheme; leverage extended at USD 2.6 billion (approx Rs 25,000 crores).
- **NIM:** 5.58% (vs 5.90% in Q1 excluding income tax refund).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.32% (improved by 19 bps QoQ from 1.51%).
- **Net NPA:** 0.40% (improved by 4 bps QoQ).
- **Credit Cost:** 1.38% (vs 1.53% in previous quarter; 1.30% excluding FCNR-backed loans).
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 14.66% with CET-1 at 13.01%.
- **CASA Ratio:** 47.6% (average CASA ratio at 48.4%).
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 70.2% (improved by 57 bps QoQ, 358 bps lower YoY, excluding trading gains).

### Highlights
- Crossed 5 million credit cards in force; credit card spends grew 15% YoY.
- MFI book stands at Rs 6,825 crore (2% of funded assets); 97% covered under CGFMU scheme.
- Obtained debut international ratings from S&P and CareEdge (investment grade) and raised USD 950 million via debut bond issuance from offshore IBU branch.
- Operating jaws at 592 bps for the quarter (553 bps for H1).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects NIM to recover to approximately 5.8% by Q4 FY27, driven by deployment of excess liquidity and expected repo rate hikes.
- Management expects incremental cost-to-income ratio to stay around 55% for FY27.
- Credit cost guidance maintained at 1.5% to 1.6%.
- Blended cost of funds for FY28 expected to increase by about 5 to 10 bps over FY27.
- Loan growth visibility remains at least 20%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Business banking and wholesale loans grew by more than 30% YoY.
- Management is watchful of El Niño effects and drought declarations in a few states (like Maharashtra and Karnataka) on rural/MFI portfolios, though no immediate impact seen in Q3/Q4.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- NIM moderated to 5.58% due to: FCNR lending denominator impact (16 bps), excess liquidity parked in low-yielding treasury/nostro (7 bps), asset mix changes towards wholesale/business banking (5 bps), and day count convention (4 bps).
- FCNR deposit cost annualized at 6.86% plus hedging costs; estimated 1% overall benefit compared to domestic deposits including PSL benefits.
- Opex grew 16% YoY to Rs 5,942 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No specific capex guidance mentioned outside of technology and operational expenses.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Bulk deposit ratio reduced from 17.1% to 13.9%; certificate of deposits repaid upon maturity.
- LCR averaged 125% for the quarter, ending September higher at 139% due to FCNR inflows, expected to normalize to ~115% in a month.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential IRDAI consultation paper on commission caps could reduce fee income, though management expects to offset it via FCNR benefits and repo rate hikes.
- Macro and geopolitical uncertainties, alongside El Niño and agricultural stress monitoring.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Akshay Jain (Autonomous):** Asked about funding cost moderation and El Niño asset quality impact. Management stated blended cost of funds impact for FY28 will be minimal (5-10 bps) and credit metrics remain stable.
2. **Param Subramanian (Investec):** Questioned the sustainability of the 55% incremental cost-to-income ratio and NIM recovery path. Management confirmed 55% is firm for FY27 and detailed that Q3 NIM will be range-bound before Q4 recovery (day count, deployment of liquidity, repo hike).
3. **Piran Engineer (CITIC Securities International):** Inquired about CASA ratio moderation and wholesale banking moats/delinquencies. Management noted CASA is stabilizing around high 40s percentage-wise, and corporate banking has experienced near-zero delinquencies over 7 years with 76% of the book rated 'A and above'.
4. **Abhishek Murarka (HSBC):** Asked about the quantifiable hit from the IRDAI insurance circular. Management did not provide absolute figures but stated the impact is expected to be neutralized by FCNR savings and repo rate increases.
5. **Jai Prakash Mundhra (ICICI Securities):** Asked about ECL transition impact. Management reiterated that revised operational/credit risk guidelines will broadly offset the capital impact of ECL, leaving CET-1 net neutral.

### One-line takeaway
IDFC First Bank delivered its second consecutive quarter of PAT above Rs 1,000 crore, supported by strong loan growth, successful FCNR(B) mobilization, and expanding operating jaws, while guiding for NIM recovery to 5.8% by Q4 FY27.
