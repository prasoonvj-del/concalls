# Capital Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong loan growth (23.4% YoY), improved asset quality (GNPA 2.3%), and steady NIMs despite a shifting rate environment.

### Key numbers
*   **Gross Advances:** Rs 9,755 crore (+23.4% YoY).
*   **Total Deposits:** Rs 10,831 crore (+16.2% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 46 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **NIM:** 4.24% (vs 4.04% YoY).
*   **GNPA:** 2.3% (vs 2.47% in Q1 FY27).
*   **CASA Ratio:** 34.5%.
*   **Cost of Deposits:** 5.68% (vs 5.92% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 1,095 crore (+36% YoY).
*   **Asset Quality:** Net NPA improved to 1.12% (vs 1.14% in Q1 FY27). Credit cost for the quarter was 0.18%.
*   **Efficiency:** Cost-to-income ratio improved to 59.8% (vs 61.7% YoY).
*   **Portfolio:** 97% of the loan book is secured (89% excluding corporate book).
*   **Return Ratios:** ROA at 1.39% (vs 1.26% YoY); ROE at 12.23% (vs 10.22% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Vision 2029:** Management maintains targets of >Rs 16,000 crore loan book and >300 branches by FY29.
*   **Universal Bank Aspiration:** Management aims to apply for a universal banking license post-FY29, targeting a balance sheet size of Rs 21,000–23,000 crore.
*   **Long-term Targets:** Targeting ROA of 1.6% and ROE of 15%+ by FY29.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management expects productivity gains and operating leverage to drive future earnings.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Banking industry credit grew ~19.4% YoY vs deposit growth of 17.8%.
*   Management notes the RBI has moved to "calibrated tightening" (repo rate raised 25 bps to 5.50%), shifting the focus to managing a higher-for-longer rate environment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Management expects NIM expansion of 7-12 bps and opex-to-asset ratio improvement of 8-20 bps by FY29.
*   Average credit-to-deposit ratio was 85.4% (vs 81.5% YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Network:** 222 branches across 5 states and 2 union territories.
*   **Geographic Strategy:** Targeting >30% of branches outside Punjab by FY29 (currently 21%).
*   **New Markets:** Following an "asset-first" approach in new geographies like Rajasthan and NCR to build trust before scaling deposits.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Capital Adequacy:** 20.8% (Tier 1: 18.5%).
*   **Liquidity:** Average Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) at ~216%.
*   **Loan Mix:** Business Loans (28%), Agriculture (26%), Mortgages (24%), Corporate (14%).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Potential impact of the IRDAI draft circular on banca commission income; management stated they are awaiting the final draft but have internal strategies to diversify fee income.
*   **Macro:** Uncertainty regarding inflation and growth cycles.
*   **Weather:** Management noted no material impact on the agriculture portfolio despite deficit rainfall in some regions, citing irrigation facilities in Punjab/Haryana.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Impact of monsoon/agri stress?* Management: No impact seen; secured lending and irrigation infrastructure in Punjab/Haryana mitigate risk.
*   *Q: Universal bank aspiration?* Management: Aspire to apply post-FY29 once balance sheet reaches Rs 21,000–23,000 crore.
*   *Q: IRDAI draft circular impact?* Management: Awaiting final draft; have internal strategies to diversify fee income and minimize ROTA impact.
*   *Q: Growth strategy in new geographies?* Management: "Asset-first" approach; build trust, then deposit franchise follows.
*   *Q: NIM expansion levers?* Management: Expect 7-10 bps expansion driven by CD ratio improvement and opex efficiency.

### One-line takeaway
Capital Small Finance Bank delivered robust growth and improved asset quality, maintaining a disciplined, secured-lending focus while executing its "Vision 2029" roadmap toward universal banking.
