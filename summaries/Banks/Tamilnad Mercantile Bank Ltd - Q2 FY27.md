# Tamilnad Mercantile Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever net profit of INR451 crores, strong asset growth exceeding industry by 8%, and record business growth.

### Key numbers
- Total business: INR1,30,237 crores (up 27.25% YoY)
- Deposits: INR69,546 crores (up 25.49% YoY); Term deposits up ~29% YoY
- Advances: INR60,691 crores (up 29.32% YoY)
- Net Interest Income (NII): INR807 crores (up INR35.17 crores YoY)
- Net Profit: INR451 crores (up 42.09% YoY)
- Net Interest Margin (NIM): 4.19% (up 36 bps YoY, down 10 bps QoQ)
- Gross NPA: 0.61%; Net NPA: 0.14%
- Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR): 32.39%
- Return on Assets (ROA): 2.18%; Return on Equity (ROE): 16.92%
- Cost-to-Income ratio: 38.28%
- Credit Cost: 9 bps (0.09%)
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): On-book PCR at 77.49%, overall PCR at 96.61%
- Expected Credit Loss (ECL) estimated impact as of September 30, 2026: INR385 crores (provisions and existing contingency funds cover ~80% at INR304.24 crores)

### Highlights
- Marked the 8th consecutive quarter of exceeding previous performance.
- Achieved highest YoY growth in 18 years, growing 8% faster than the industry.
- Fully provided 100% for SMA 1 and SMA 2 as per expected credit loss (ECL) norms ahead of implementation on April 1, 2027 (following 100% provision for stressed non-fund facilities in Q1).
- Unsecured exposure remains minimal at 20 basis points.
- RAM (Retail, Agriculture, MSME) focus stands at 94.33%; MSME share increased to 23.62%.
- Opened 9 branches in H1 FY27; committed to opening 60 branches in the full year (with 20+ outside Tamil Nadu).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised guidance for FY27: Deposits growth at 20%+ (previously 18%+), Advances growth at 22%+, CASA growth expected at ~17%.
- Cost-to-income ratio guided to remain well below 44%.
- NIM guided at 4.10% plus/minus 5 bps for the rest of the year.
- Long-term growth strategy is to outpace the industry by 4% to 5% sustainably.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Gold loan demand continues to be resilient; average ticket size is INR2.66 lakhs across 12 lakh+ customers. Portfolio LTV is 62.35%.
- Management stated no impact observed yet from crude oil price increases or drought conditions in states like Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra on exports or MSME/agri portfolios.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of deposits increased to 5.85% (from 5.68%) driven by faster growth in term deposits compared to CASA.
- Yield on advances increased to 10.32% (from 10.10%) to offset rising deposit costs.
- Cost of funds at 5.93%, impacted partly by INR1,000 crores of refinance picked up from SIDBI at 7% to 7.5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investments being made in IT space and business process automation to drive productivity.
- 37 branches opened YoY (15 outside Tamil Nadu); targeting 60 total branches in FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Credit-Deposit (CD) ratio stands at 87.27%, with cushion to go up to 98%.
- Leverage ratio at 11.99%, LCR at 139.43%, and NSFR at 146.15%.
- Non-callable deposits accounted for 40.62% of incremental term deposits for the half year.
- FCNR(B) deposit growth at 47.14% (total collection of USD51.94 million).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in gold prices monitored daily; portfolio can sustain up to a 20% price crash with only 3.13% of gold loans exceeding market value.
- Potential impact of IRDAI insurance norms assessed at ~20% reduction in commission income, which management expects to offset via incremental business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Darshan Deora (Indvest Group)** asked about gold loan split. *Management replied total gold loans are INR29,280 crores (Agri: INR22,000 crores, Retail: INR6,084 crores).*
- **Varun Dubey (Share India Securities)** asked about revised guidance. *Management raised deposit growth to 20%+ and advances growth to 22%+.*
- **Parth Gutka (360 ONE Capital)** asked about the sharp 22 bps increase in yield on advances. *Management explained it was a conscious decision to tweak the portfolio (gold loans, MSMEs) to offset rising cost of deposits.*
- **Sarvesh Gupta (Maximal Capital)** asked about gold loan LTV adjustments across the industry due to price softening. *Management stated TMB uses dynamic daily tracking at the individual account level and has reduced per-gram rates by up to INR800 recently.*
- **Deepak J. (Individual Investor)** inquired about the status of disputed share ownership cases and ED attachments. *Management clarified that the Appellate Tribunal issued an order on July 9, no appeals were filed within the window, and an application has been filed with RBI to regularize share transfers.*

### One-line takeaway
Tamilnad Mercantile Bank delivered its highest-ever quarterly net profit of INR451 crores (up 42.09% YoY) with robust advances growth of 29.32%, while proactively provisioning 100% for SMA 1 and SMA 2 assets ahead of ECL norms.
