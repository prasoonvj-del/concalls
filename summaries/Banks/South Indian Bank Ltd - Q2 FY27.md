# South Indian Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest quarterly net profit of Rs 426 crore, lower GNPA/NNPA, and stable asset quality metrics.

### Key numbers
- **Net Profit:** Rs 426 crore (up 21% YoY from Rs 351 crore in Q2 FY25-26).
- **Total Deposits:** Rs 1,37,279 crore (up 19% YoY from Rs 1,15,635 crore).
- **Gross Advances:** Rs 1,09,517 crore (up 19% YoY, or 20% excluding technical write-off, from Rs 92,286 crore).
- **Total Business:** Rs 2,46,796 crore (up 19% YoY).
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 3.33% (up 10 bps QoQ, up 53 bps YoY).
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 1,116 crore (up 38% YoY from Rs 808 crore).
- **Return on Assets (ROA):** 1.08% for the quarter.
- **Return on Equity (ROE):** 14% for the quarter.
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 19.40% (Tier 1 at 18.71% as of September 30, 2026).
- **CASA Deposits:** Rs 43,310 crore (up 18% YoY from Rs 36,841 crore).
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 97.84% including write-offs; 92.42% excluding write-offs.
- **Gross NPA:** 1.24% (down 169 bps YoY from 2.93%).
- **Net NPA:** 0.09% (down 47 bps YoY from 0.56%).
- **Slippage Ratio:** 0.09% for the quarter.
- **Credit Cost:** 0.14% for the quarter.

### Highlights
- Declared the highest quarterly net profit in the bank's history at Rs 426 crore.
- Retail segment currently outperforming the wholesale segment in disbursements.
- Board permitted raising Rs 500 crore of Tier 2 bonds; no major capital raising required currently due to strong capital adequacy.
- Gold loan portfolio grew by 41%, reaching 25% of the total portfolio (split as 60% retail gold and 40% agri gold).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains loan and deposit growth guidance of 15% to 16%, aiming for the higher bucket (~16%).
- Medium-term ROA target of 1.25% to 1.30%, with a long-term target of 1.5%.
- Medium-term NIM target of 3.40% to 3.50%.
- Cost-to-income ratio expected to trend down toward 50% over time.
- Advance mix target is 35% corporate and 65% RAM (Retail, Agri, MSME).
- Gold loan share targeted to increase to 30%-32% immediately and 35% in the first phase.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Retail, MSME, and agri segments showing robust uptick in disbursements, whereas wholesale segment growth is muted.
- Corporate loans are primarily priced against Repo and T-Bills, which have seen 25-30 bps upticks over the last 3 months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of deposits decreased due to repricing of higher-rate contracts from last year and lower general liquidity costs.
- Bulk deposits increased to capitalize on lower bulk rates following FCNR(B) liquidity changes.
- Opex growth expected to remain lower than loan growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- EBLR-linked book stands at 43% (roughly split evenly between T-Bills and Repo), MCLR-linked at 5%, and fixed-rate at 47%.
- AFS reserve has a debit balance (loss) of Rs 43 crore as of September 30, with a Q2 impact of Rs 52-53 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Recovery amounts from technical write-offs are expected to decline over the next 4 to 6 quarters as the underlying stock exhausts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Akshat Agrawal (Nirmal Bang):** Asked about the rationale behind increased credit costs / higher PCR (92%). Management answered that there are no major asset quality concerns, but being conservative, they made account-based provisions rather than floating/contingent provisions.
2. **Akshat Agrawal (Nirmal Bang):** Inquired about the sharp Q-o-Q increase in current account balances. Management clarified it was driven by one-off IPO fund flows at the end of September and may see minor slippages.
3. **Jai Mundra (ICICI Securities):** Asked about fee income components (specifically the Rs 190 crore "other" line). Management (Vinod Francis) clarified it includes Rs 73 crore from technical write-off recoveries and Rs 30-35 crore from bancassurance.
4. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company):** Asked about the treasury impact from hardening yields. Management stated every 1 bps change in yield results in an MTM impact of roughly Rs 1 to 1.1 crore.
5. **Vivek Singh (Raise Securities):** Questioned whether underwriting or branch strategy is changing under new leadership. Management stated there is no change in strategy, focusing instead on strengthening the existing ecosystem.

### One-line takeaway
South Indian Bank delivered a record Q2 FY27 net profit of Rs 426 crore, supported by strong asset quality (Gross NPA at 1.24%), an improved NIM of 3.33%, and disciplined credit growth.
