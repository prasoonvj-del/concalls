# Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever PBT, PAT reached Rs 326 crores in H1 (equal to full year FY26 PAT), leading management to raise full-year PAT growth guidance from 80% to 100%.

### Key numbers
- **PBT:** Highest ever in history; H1 PBT reached Rs 326 crores.
- **PAT Growth Guidance:** Upgraded from 80% to 100% YoY.
- **Advances / Loan Book Growth:** 22% YoY to Rs 38,752 crores (secured assets up 24% YoY).
- **Deposit Growth Guidance:** Maintained at 23% to 25% YoY (Total liability book at Rs 38,221 crores; CASA growth 13% YoY, TD growth 17.5% YoY).
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 7.54% for Q2 (up from 6.5% in Q2 last year; H1 FY26 was 6.5%, current H1 at 7.5%).
- **Cost of Funds:** 7.4% (down from 7.9% in Q2 last year).
- **Secured Assets Share:** 73.7% (target is around 80%).
- **Credit Cost:** H1 credit cost at 0.43% and 0.49% (total 0.92% for H1); guided to remain below 2% for the full year.
- **Gross NPA:** 2.17%; **Net NPA:** 1.08% (expected to drop below 1% once guarantee claims of Rs 68 crores are realized).
- **Collection Efficiency:** 99% (including all BCs).
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 64.3% for Q2 (down from 67.0% in Q1).
- **Return on Assets (RoA):** 1.4% for H1; **Return on Equity (RoE):** Close to 14% for H1.
- **Capital Adequacy (CRAR):** 20%.
- **Book Value per Share:** Rs 455.

### Highlights
- Secured assets continue secular improvement, reaching 73.7% of the book.
- Positive operating jaws of 20% (NII up 35% YoY, opex up 15% YoY); absolute opex in Q2 is marginally lower than Q1.
- Headcount is lower compared to March 2026 despite business growth, driven by AI-led collection/calling processes and reduced deep-bucket collector headcount.
- Total unsecured gross NPA at Rs 673 crores with provisions of Rs 377 crores; uncovered portion of Rs 296 crores is completely covered under guarantee programs (total guarantee program at Rs 319 crores, with Rs 68 crores claimed and Rs 251 crores to be lodged next year in Q3).
- CGFMU portal is currently not open for fresh guarantees due to an ongoing approval process, but management has accrued for the 1% requirement in H1 results and expects the portal to reopen soon.

### Guidance & outlook
- **PAT Growth:** Upgraded from 80% to 100% YoY.
- **Advances Growth:** Maintained at 19% to 21%.
- **Deposit Growth:** Maintained at 23% to 25%.
- **Credit Cost:** Expected to be below 2% for the full year, with H2 credit cost expected to be lower than H1.
- **Cost-to-Income:** Moving towards a 60% benchmark over coming quarters (long-term goal to reach March 2024 level of 57%).
- **Secured Assets:** Expected to reach around 75% by the end of the year (towards a long-term target of 80%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- MFI market is experiencing subdued growth due to tighter credit criteria and customers with poor bureau histories; 97% of Jana's MFI customers have <=2 lenders vs 93% for the industry.
- MSME growth is robust, driven by PLI and "Make in India" tailwinds.
- Affordable housing and vehicle loans (2-wheelers and used cars) show strong secular demand across expanded city presences.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of funds stable at 7.4%; 96.1% of deposits are contractual bookings of 1 year and above.
- NIM expected to sustain around current levels (~7.5%).
- Opex growth managed at 15% YoY for H1 while revenue grew 18% YoY.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 40 branches out of the planned 78 new branches for the year (total network around 820+ branches across 220+ cities for housing/micro LAP and vehicle loans).
- **Upcoming Product Launches:** Credit Line on UPI (employee POC ongoing, live soon); Loans against shares and mutual funds in wealth management (live in November); Trade FX / AD-1 license for export, imports, and FX trade (live by December); Digital onboarding for non-face-to-face CASA accounts by January.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total advances: Rs 38,752 crores (up 22% YoY).
- Total liability book: Rs 38,221 crores.
- Gold loan book flat QoQ (up 81% YoY) due to maturity/renewals of 6-month loans from Jan-Mar 2026 and gold price drops requiring margin calls.
- Vehicle loans grew 76% YoY (offered in 135 cities for 2-wheelers and 50 cities for used cars).
- Affordable housing grew 28.2% YoY (average ticket size Rs 11.9 lakhs); MSME grew 20.6% YoY.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Drought conditions declared in select talukas of Karnataka and Maharashtra impact 0.3% of MFI slippages; management expects minor additional slippages but not material.
- IRDAI consultation paper on insurance commissions: management acknowledges potential impact on commission income, planning to counter it via AI-led cost efficiency and increased penetration.
- CASA growth has been slower than expected in H1 (absolute CASA grew 13% YoY, but percentage dropped 2%), requiring intensified focus in H2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jai Chauhan (Yes Securities) on slippage trends:** Management clarified that Q2 slippages included two one-offs (a secured loan collateralized for recovery and drought impact in Assam, Maharashtra, Karnataka). With SMA dropping in Q2, H2 slippages and credit costs are expected to decline.
2. **Mayuresh (Invest Value Capital) on sustainable cost-to-income reduction:** Management stated that headcount reduction, lower DSA sourcing via direct sourcing increase, and AI/RPA automation drove opex down, and sub-64% levels are sustainable with a longer-term goal of reaching 57%.
3. **Rajiv Gade (Equentis Wealth) on CGFMU scheme impact:** Management noted that if the guarantee scheme does not reopen, P&L buffers kept ready will absorb risks, though they expect the National Guarantee Corporation to clear lodged claims within 2 months (by end of November).
4. **Bhavya Agarwal (Share India Securities) on gold loan flat growth:** Management explained that a large chunk of 6-month loans from Q4 FY26 matured in Q2, leading to heavy renewals/margin calls amid gold price drops; growth is expected to resume at 10% per quarter from Q3.
5. **Vandana Shah (Investo Capital) on media speculation regarding a merger:** Management reiterated their prior stance communicated to stock exchanges: any strategic opportunity that evaluates value for shareholders will be taken to the Board for consideration.

### One-line takeaway
Jana Small Finance Bank posted its highest-ever PBT in Q2 FY27, matching H1 PAT with FY26's full-year PAT, prompting management to upgrade its full-year PAT growth guidance to 100%.
