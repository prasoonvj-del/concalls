# ESAF Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Improved profitability, asset quality, and successful completion of a major digital transformation project.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Business:** Rs 52,400 crore (up 25% YoY).
*   **Gross Advances:** Rs 23,934 crore (up 25% YoY).
*   **Total Deposits:** Rs 28,466 crore (up 24% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 160 crore (doubled sequentially from Rs 80 crore).
*   **NIM:** 8.2%.
*   **GNPA:** 2.8% (down from 8.5% YoY).
*   **Net NPA:** 0.3% (down from 3.8% YoY).
*   **CRAR:** 24.1%.

### Highlights
*   **Digital Transformation:** Successfully went live with "StratoNeXt" (ESAF 2.0) on October 26, 2026, migrating to Oracle FLEXCUBE.
*   **Portfolio Mix:** Secured loan book grew 35% YoY, now comprising 64% of total gross advances.
*   **MARG Portfolio:** Rs 13,938 crore (up 38% YoY), with Gold Loan portfolio reaching Rs 10,456 crore.
*   **Emerging Household (EH):** Segment grew 134% YoY, contributing 32% of total gross advances.
*   **Microfinance:** Group loan portfolio degrew 65% YoY as part of a strategic shift toward secured lending.
*   **Write-offs:** Technical write-off of Rs 626 crore for legacy NPAs.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **ROA:** Management expects 2% ROA for the full year FY27.
*   **Cost-to-Income:** Aiming for ~60% for the year.
*   **Credit Cost:** Guidance of ~2% for FY27; expects ~1.5% for FY28.
*   **Growth:** Management expects growth to normalize in FY28 following the IT migration focus in Q2.
*   **Capital:** No urgent need for capital raise given 24.1% CRAR, though management remains open to strategic infusion.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management views the Indian economy as resilient despite RBI's 25 bps repo rate hike.
*   Management is cautious regarding global energy prices, El Nino conditions, and drought impacts in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Funds:** Expected to rise marginally in Q3.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Increased 2% QoQ; management noted that StratoNeXt capitalization will add to expenses in H2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Added 10 banking outlets; total distribution footprint: 831 Banking Outlets, 722 ATMs, 1060 Customer Service Centers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **CD Ratio:** 82%.
*   **Liquidity Coverage Ratio:** 141.48%.
*   **CASA Ratio:** 23.3%.
*   **Retail Deposits:** Rs 25,475 crore (89% of total deposits).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Gold Loan SMA:** Spike in SMA-0 (Rs 990 crore) and SMA-1 (Rs 200 crore) attributed to gold price volatility; management expects recovery.
*   **Geopolitical/Macro:** Potential impact of global tensions and weather-related issues on collections.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did growth moderate to 3% QoQ?** A: Management stated the focus on the StratoNeXt IT migration diverted team attention, but expects growth to resume in FY28.
*   **Q: Is there a plan for equity capital raise?** A: Management clarified there is no urgent need due to 24.1% CRAR, but they are exploring strategic options.
*   **Q: Why the spike in Gold Loan SMA?** A: Management attributed it to gold price fluctuations in Sept/Oct and expects 100% recovery once prices stabilize.
*   **Q: How will you maintain 2% ROA with rising costs?** A: Management cited PPOP stability, controlled slippages, and lower provisioning requirements as offsets.
*   **Q: Impact of insurance guidelines?** A: Insurance income was Rs 25 crore for the quarter; management is awaiting final IRDA guidelines.

### One-line takeaway
Strong profitability and asset quality improvement driven by a strategic pivot to secured lending and successful IT transformation, despite temporary gold loan SMA volatility.
