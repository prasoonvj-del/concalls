# Banks - Private banks: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across private sector banks reported a largely positive Q2 FY27, characterized by strong advances growth, record net profits for several players (such as DCB Bank, South Indian Bank, and Tamilnad Mercantile Bank), and continued improvements in asset quality. However, margins faced temporary compression across multiple banks due to high deposit costs and FCNR(B) liquidity mobilization, though most managements expressed confidence that NIMs have bottomed out and will recover in H2 FY27 via floating-rate asset repricing and repo rate pass-throughs. Regulatory headwinds—specifically draft norms on bancassurance commission caps and impending Expected Credit Loss (ECL) transitions—remained key discussion points.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board reported healthy momentum in retail, MSME, and secured lending segments. Consumer demand and high-frequency indicators remain resilient.
*   **Divergence:** While Bandhan Bank successfully arrested de-growth in its microfinance (EEB) portfolio, other banks like City Union Bank, CSB Bank, DCB Bank, and Tamilnad Mercantile Bank focused predominantly on secured retail, gold loans, and MSMEs. In wholesale banking, IDFC First Bank and Yes Bank reported strong corporate growth, whereas South Indian Bank noted that wholesale demand remains muted compared to retail.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Most banks experienced margin moderation or pressure in Q2 due to elevated cost of funds and surplus liquidity carrying costs (noted by DCB Bank, IDFC First Bank, HDFC Bank, and Yes Bank). Opex increased across institutions due to ongoing investments in technology, branch expansion, and annual wage revisions.
*   **Disagreements & Variations:** NIM levels varied widely based on business mix—ranging from 2.6% at Yes Bank and 3.17% at Karnataka Bank, to 3.87% at City Union Bank and 4.19% at Tamilnad Mercantile Bank. While deposit costs generally remained sticky, banks like South Indian Bank reported a decrease in cost of deposits due to repricing of higher-rate contracts.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Network Expansion:** Expansion continued steadily. City Union Bank crossed 1,000 branches; Karnataka Bank is on path to add 31 branches in FY27 to cross 1,000 branches; CSB Bank operates 872 branches; DCB Bank plans ~20 new branches; Tamilnad Mercantile Bank is targeting 60 total branches for FY27; Bandhan Bank added 13 branches and 14 banking units.
*   **Technology:** CSB Bank, DCB Bank, HDFC Bank, and Tamilnad Mercantile Bank highlighted heavy ongoing investments in IT infrastructure, AI rollouts, core system upgrades, and digital platforms to drive operational efficiency.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Bandhan Bank:** Shifted tone from "cautious" to "balanced and steady progress"; arrested EEB portfolio de-growth and maintained FY27 loan growth target of ~14%.
*   **Tamilnad Mercantile Bank:** Raised FY27 guidance—deposit growth target increased to 20%+ (from 18%+) and advances growth to 22%+.
*   **HDFC Bank:** Management noted that NIMs have bottomed out in Q2 FY27, projecting an upward trajectory starting Q3.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Margin Recovery:** Proof of NIM expansion as repo rate hikes and floating-rate assets reprice (noted by HDFC Bank, IDFC First Bank, DCB Bank, and Yes Bank).
*   **Deposit Mobilization:** Sustainability of deposit growth and CASA ratio trends amid intense sector competition (highlighted as challenging by City Union Bank).
*   **Regulatory Updates:** Final guidelines on bancassurance commission caps and progress on ECL transition impacts.

## Company one-liners
*   **Bandhan Bank Ltd:** Bandhan Bank showed strong recovery in profitability and asset quality, with management successfully arresting EEB de-growth and maintaining a disciplined, diversified growth strategy despite regulatory headwinds in bancassurance.
*   **CSB Bank Ltd:** CSB Bank delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 14% YoY net profit growth, stable asset quality (GNPA at 1.58%), and improved margins, successfully transitioning into its scale phase.
*   **City Union Bank Ltd:** City Union Bank reported robust Q2 FY27 performance with 26% YoY advance growth, expanding margins (NIM at 3.87%), and multi-quarter lows in Gross/Net NPAs, driven by disciplined secured lending and core MSME focus.
*   **DCB Bank Ltd:** DCB Bank delivered a record quarter with 29% YoY PAT growth to Rs 236 crore, a 1% ROA, and steady asset quality improvements, while guiding for NIM expansion and imminent capital raise updates.
*   **HDFC Bank Ltd:** HDFC Bank's operational momentum and asset quality remain robust, with margins poised to recover from Q2 lows as floating loans reprice and retail asset execution accelerates under incoming leadership.
*   **IDFC First Bank Ltd:** IDFC First Bank delivered its second consecutive quarter of PAT above Rs 1,000 crore, supported by strong loan growth, successful FCNR(B) mobilization, and expanding operating jaws, while guiding for NIM recovery to 5.8% by Q4 FY27.
*   **Karnataka Bank Ltd:** Karnataka Bank delivered robust 37% YoY PAT growth, expanding loan growth at 25% and maintaining healthy asset quality metrics with GNPA down to 2.27%.
*   **South Indian Bank Ltd:** South Indian Bank delivered a record Q2 FY27 net profit of Rs 426 crore, supported by strong asset quality (Gross NPA at 1.24%), an improved NIM of 3.33%, and disciplined credit growth.
*   **Tamilnad Mercantile Bank Ltd:** Tamilnad Mercantile Bank delivered its highest-ever quarterly net profit of INR451 crores (up 42.09% YoY) with robust advances growth of 29.32%, while proactively provisioning 100% for SMA 1 and SMA 2 assets ahead of ECL norms.
*   **Yes Bank Ltd:** Yes Bank reported a strong 65% YoY jump in net profit (Rs 1,080 crore) and robust balance sheet growth, but faces a pro forma 240 bps hit to its CET-1 ratio (down to ~11.4%) following the Supreme Court's verdict setting aside the March 2020 AT-1 write-down.

*Based on: Bandhan Bank Ltd, CSB Bank Ltd, City Union Bank Ltd, DCB Bank Ltd, HDFC Bank Ltd, IDFC First Bank Ltd, Karnataka Bank Ltd, South Indian Bank Ltd, Tamilnad Mercantile Bank Ltd, Yes Bank Ltd*
