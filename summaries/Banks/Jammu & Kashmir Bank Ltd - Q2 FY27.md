# Jammu & Kashmir Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust business momentum, improving profitability, and moderation in slippages.

### Key numbers
- Total business: INR3.10 lakhs crores (up 19.4% YoY)
- Deposit growth: 16.3% YoY (7% YTD)
- Advances growth: 23.7% YoY
- Net profit (PAT): INR546 crores (up ~29% QoQ)
- Operating profit: INR789 crores (up 27% YoY, 12% QoQ)
- Cost-to-Income Ratio: 56.57%
- Yield on advances: 8.55%
- Cost of deposits: 4.85%
- NIM (Half-Year): 3.22%
- Annualized Gross Slippage Ratio (Half-Year): 0.4% (Gross slippages at INR234 crores for H1)
- GNPA: 2.23%
- NNPA: 0.51%
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 91.76% (up from 90.53% in Q1)
- Capital Adequacy Ratio: 16.57% (CET 1 at 13.88%)
- RoA: 1.07%
- RoE: 13.68%
- Capital raise plans: INR1,000 crores equity and INR500 crores debt

### Highlights
- Net incremental credit entirely driven by Retail, Agriculture, and MSME (RAM) loans.
- Auto loans grew >25% YoY; Housing loans in Rest of India posted double-digit YoY growth.
- CASA ratio maintained at 41.97% (supported by positive CASA inflows in Q2 after Q1 outflows).
- Bank carries an unutilized prudential buffer of around INR300 crores.
- Pending unlisted stock revaluation/stake sale gain of ~INR100 crores expected in Q3 post-quarter completion.

### Guidance & outlook
Management revised guidance for FY26-27:
- Credit growth: 14%+ (aiming around 18% with retail focus)
- Deposit growth: 12%+
- CASA ratio: 42%+
- NIM: "Considerable improvement"
- RoA: ~1.20%+
- GNPA: Below 2%

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian economy growing at 7.8% in Q1; IMF revised GDP growth forecast to 7%.
- Jammu & Kashmir geography noted as underinsured with high potential for insurance penetration.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenditure growth controlled at 2% QoQ and 6.5% YoY.
- Employee cost expected to remain flat or slightly lower for the next 2 quarters due to retirements.
- High-cost bulk deposits reduced to less than INR3,000 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- 31% of the loan book is linked to MCLR and 65% to repo (EBLR).
- Total high-cost bulk/certificate of deposits stand at under INR3,000 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties, West Asia conflict escalation driving energy prices and inflation.
- CASA outflows experienced in Q1 (though partially recovered in Q2).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yuvraj Choudhary (Anand Rathi) on CASA steps:** Management highlighted MOUs for salary accounts, aggressive QR/POS merchant tracking, and a new 5-year Jammu & Kashmir government salary MOU.
- **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital) on RoA target:** Management clarified that the 1.20%+ RoA target is conservative, with H2 RoA expected at ~1.40% driven by other income and recoveries.
- **Amansingh Sahajsinghani (ICICI Securities) on write-off recoveries durability:** Management stated that the pipeline for written-off (TWO) recoveries is robust enough to last through this year and the next.
- **Jai Prakash Mundhra (ICICI Securities) on ECL readiness & SMA:** Management stated the ECL model will go to the Board in December and the hit will be less than the industry average; carrying an INR300 crore buffer.
- **Kayur Asher (PNB MetLife) on capital raising:** Management confirmed plans to raise INR1,000 crores of equity and INR500 crores of debt, with debt planned for the current quarter and equity at an opportune time.

### One-line takeaway
JK Bank delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with a 29% QoQ jump in PAT to INR546 crores, decadal-best asset quality (GNPA at 2.23%), and management upgrading its growth and return guidance.
