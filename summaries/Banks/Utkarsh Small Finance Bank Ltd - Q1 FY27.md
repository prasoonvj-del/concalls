# Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-07 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management noted that actions taken over recent quarters are translating into measurable improvements in business momentum, asset quality, funding costs, and operating performance, with significant reduction in losses.

### Key numbers
- **Total Disbursements Growth:** 49% YoY (JLG disbursements grew 5% YoY; non-JLG disbursements grew 93% YoY)
- **Gross NPA (GNPA):** 5.9% as of June 2026 (down ~550 bps YoY and ~160 bps QoQ)
- **Fresh NPA Slippages (net):** ~INR 125 crore (compared to ~INR 170 crore in Q4 and ~INR 400 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Credit Cost:** 2.3% (compared to 5.3% in Q4 FY26 and 8.5% in Q1 FY26), aided by ~INR 75 crore P&L impact mitigation from the CGFMU scheme
- **Net Loss:** INR 34 crore (reduced by >80% YoY and QoQ)
- **PPOP:** INR 64 crore (fivefold increase compared to INR 12 crore in Q4 FY26)
- **Cost of Funds:** 7.7% (down ~40 bps YoY and ~15 bps QoQ)
- **Total Deposits Growth:** 3% YoY
- **CASA Growth:** 15% YoY; **Retail Term Deposits Growth:** 15% YoY
- **CASA Ratio:** 22% as of June 2026
- **CASA + Retail Term Deposit Ratio:** 83% (compared to 74% a year ago)
- **Capital Adequacy (CRAR):** 17.4%
- **Liquidity & LCR:** Surplus liquidity of ~INR 3,200 crore; LCR at 216%; CD Ratio at 83.8%
- **Micro Banking Business Loan (MBBL) Portfolio Growth:** 147% YoY and 11% sequentially (constitutes >30% of Micro Banking portfolio)
- **MSME Portfolio:** INR 4,482 crore (up 12% YoY); Micro LAP disbursement yield ~18%
- **Housing Loan Portfolio:** INR 1,005 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **Business Banking Group (BBG) Portfolio Growth:** 40% YoY
- **CGFMU Coverage:** ~60% of microfinance book for disbursements up to Q4 FY26, increasing to ~80% including Q1 FY27 disbursements
- **JLG X-bucket Collection Efficiency:** 99.7% (up from 98.6% in Q1 FY26)
- **Micro Banking SMA Pools:** 1.2% (down from 1.3% in March 2026 and 5.1% in June 2025)
- **Employee Headcount Rationalization:** Reduced by ~1,700

### Highlights
- Secured lending increased to 51% of the gross loan book from 45% a year ago.
- JLG portfolio represents 26% of the gross loan book (28% including BC JLG exposure), down from 88% in March 2020.
- Planned ARC sale of stressed JLG and wheels portfolio to accelerate balance sheet cleanup.
- NCLT heard the proposed amalgamation of holding company (UCL) on August 6, 2026; reverse merger expected in the next few months.
- Utkarsh 2.0 technology transformation underway; new Core Banking System (CBS) and Loan Management System (LMS) implemented.
- Board plans to raise ~INR 500 crore through Tier 2 NCDs, which is expected to boost CRAR by ~250 bps. Concurrently, an existing INR 195 crore tranche carrying a 12.5% coupon is being prematurely redeemed in mid-August to save ~INR 20 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Loan Book Growth:** 25% to 30% YoY for FY27; JLG/Micro Banking growth expected to be below 20% (around 15% to 20%).
- **Portfolio Mix:** Secured lending to comprise ~55% of the portfolio; JLG to remain around 25% of the overall portfolio over the next 2 to 3 years.
- **Credit Cost:** Expected to be around 3% to 3.5% on the upper side for the rest of the year.
- **NIM:** Around 8%.
- **Return Metrics:** Aiming for ~15% ROE by FY28; target of 2-digit positive ROE by the exit of FY27.
- **Capital Raise:** No additional equity capital raise anticipated until the end of FY27 beyond the planned INR 500 crore Tier 2 NCD issuance.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Disbursement trends improved significantly, driven by customer demand and calibrated growth.
- Industry experienced ~30% degrowth during the recent challenging cycle, leaving potential in core geographies where new customer acquisition was paused.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of funds declined due to phased rate cuts on savings and term deposits following RBI repo rate cuts (retail term deposit peak rate for senior citizens reduced from 9.1% to 8.25%, and general card rate from 8.5% to 8.1%).
- Operating expenses/cost-to-income ratio expected to improve as income denominator recovers while branch network expansion remains paused.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No branch expansion planned; growth is driven by existing branch productivity (1,100+ branches), technology, and digital investments. Employee headcount rationalized by ~1,700.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Surplus liquidity maintained at ~INR 3,200 crore; LCR at 216%; CD ratio at 83.8%.
- Total CGFMU coverage stands at 80% as of June 2026 (including Q1 FY27 disbursements).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Legacy stress in secured retail/SARFAESI portfolios taking 6 to 7 months to resolve through legal/auction processes.
- Near-term performance may continue to be influenced by sectoral headwinds and regulatory transitions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shreya Chatterjee (Ageless Capital Finance):** Asked about the JLG portfolio rundown and credit cost guidance.
   - *Management Answer:* Clarified they are not running down JLG significantly; it will be maintained around 25% of the portfolio, with JLG/Micro Banking growing at 15% to 20%. Credit cost guided at 3% to 3.5% on the upper side.
2. **Sagar Shah (Spark PWM):** Inquired about slow recoveries and non-MFI NPAs.
   - *Management Answer:* Explained that secured retail recoveries take longer due to SARFAESI/legal processes (6-7 months), but expect pickup in Q2 and Q3 as dedicated collection staff are shifted to legacy write-offs and recoveries.
3. **Saurabh Jain (SSJ Finance & Securities):** Questioned the rationale and confidence behind continuing to grow the loan book (especially JLG/MBBL) given past industry and bank asset quality stress.
   - *Management Answer:* Stated that current growth is based on a consolidation approach using existing branches operating well below optimum capacity, supported by new guardrails, CGFMU guarantee protection, and a small base in non-MFI segments.
4. **Ashlesh Sonje (Kotak Securities):** Requested a breakup of MFI and non-MFI NPAs and CGFMU provisions.
   - *Management Answer:* Provided exact figures: total non-MFI NPA is INR 425 crore (MSME INR 169 crore, CV&CE INR 62 crore, WSL INR 30 crore, Housing INR ~49-50 crore); MFI gross NPA covered under CGFMU is INR 170 crore with INR 29 crore provided out of INR 46 crore lifetime provisioning required.
5. **Henil Shah (Retail Investor):** Asked about cost-to-income and PPOP trajectory.
   - *Management Answer:* Explained that cost-to-income appears elevated due to a contracted income denominator; top-line recovery with static costs will improve ratios, aiming for 2-digit ROE exiting FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Utkarsh Small Finance Bank reported a reduced net loss of INR 34 crore in Q1 FY27, backed by 49% disbursement growth, a decline in GNPA to 5.9%, and an 80% CGFMU guarantee coverage on micro banking assets, steering toward a 25-30% loan book growth target for FY27.
