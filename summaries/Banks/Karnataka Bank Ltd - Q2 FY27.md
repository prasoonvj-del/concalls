# Karnataka Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive – Management highlighted record aggregate business, margin and return resilience, strong asset quality improvements, and continued operational execution.

### Key numbers
- **Aggregate Business:** Rs 2,07,132 crore (+17% YoY, +5% QoQ)
- **Gross Advances:** Rs 92,015 crore (+25% YoY, +6% QoQ)
- **Aggregate Deposits:** Rs 1,15,117 crore (+12% YoY, +4% QoQ)
- **CASA Ratio:** 31.80% (vs 32.42% in Q1 FY27, 31.01% in Q2 FY26)
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 982.44 crore (+35% YoY, +5% QoQ)
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 3.17% (vs 3.20% in Q1 FY27, 2.72% in Q2 FY26)
- **Yield on Advances:** 8.76% (+8 bps QoQ)
- **Cost of Funds:** 5.15% (-1 bps QoQ)
- **Operating Profit:** Around Rs 602 crore (+36.8% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 435.89 crore (+37% YoY, +4% QoQ)
- **Asset Quality:** Gross NPA at 2.27% (down 31 bps QoQ, down 106 bps YoY); Net NPA at 0.74% (down 13 bps QoQ, down 61 bps YoY)
- **Credit Cost:** 0.002% (vs 0.03% in Q1 FY27)
- **Slippages:** 0.11% (vs 0.14% in Q1 FY27)
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 68.05% (85.78% including technically written-off accounts)
- **Cost-to-Income:** 52.68% (vs 55.14% in Q1 FY27, 58.93% in Q2 FY26)
- **Return on Assets (RoA):** 1.26% (vs 1.29% in Q1 FY27, 1.03% in Q2 FY26)
- **Return on Equity (RoE):** 12.51% (vs 12.48% in Q1 FY27, 10.14% in Q2 FY26)
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR):** 20.36% (Tier-1: 19.20%, Tier-2: 1.17%)

### Highlights
- Retail, Agri, and MSME (RAM) book grew 19% YoY / 7% QoQ to Rs 56,968 crore.
- Corporate mix shift: Management actively reduced IBPC exposure from Rs 1,375 crore in Q1 FY27 to Rs 1,309 crore.
- Retail term deposits (<Rs 3 crore) increased 10% YoY / 4% QoQ to Rs 72,197 crore.
- Standard restructured advances reduced 15% QoQ / 31% YoY to Rs 649.37 crore.
- Launched Express Digi Gold Loan (Sep 2026), GST overdraft, loan against property for MSME, dropline overdraft, and pin-less CBDC transactions.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Advances Growth:** 15% to 20% growth expected.
- **Liabilities Growth:** 10% to 15% growth.
- **Overall Business Growth:** Targeted around 15% (aiming for Rs 18,000 crore addition in H2 to meet Rs 33,000 crore annual plan).
- **Portfolio Mix:** Retail guided to move from 56% to 60%, mid-corporate from 20% to 22%, and large corporate to reduce from 24% to 18%.
- **NIM & RoA:** NIM target of 3.0% to 3.4%; RoA target maintained at 1.1% to 1.35%.
- **Cost-to-Income:** Targeting 51% to 52%.
- **Network Expansion:** Planned 31 branches in FY27 (crossing 1,000 branches total), with 15 branches opening before December-end.

### Demand & industry commentary
- RBI MPC hiked repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% in October 2026, shifting stance to calibrated tightening.
- India Q1 GDP grew 7.8%; underlying domestic consumption and investment demand remain healthy despite global geopolitical issues and energy price volatility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins moderated 3 bps QoQ to 3.17% due to base effects and absence of a one-off interest item received on income tax in Q1.
- Cost of funds improved by 1 bp sequentially to 5.15%, aided by reduction in bulk deposits and growth in granular retail deposits.
- About 70%+ of loan book is floating rate linked to EBLR (largely T-bill linked), with ~5% on MCLR; asset yields repricing within ~3 months.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 24 branch centers finalized with 17 under fit-out/construction; on path to add 31 branches in FY27 to cross 1,000 branches.
- IT investments ongoing: ~40–50% spent on licenses/upgrades, ~20–25% on compliance/regulatory, and ~25–30% directly for business enablement.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Credit-Deposit (CD) ratio stood at 79.93% (up from 78.45% in Q1 FY27 and 71.63% in Q2 FY26).
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) stood at 164% (statutory requirement: 100%).
- Total investment portfolio at Rs 27,000 crore, with very limited exposure in the HFT book.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Scanty monsoon/drought conditions declared across Maharashtra, Karnataka, and another state, though management sees no immediate portfolio threat.
- Potential 1.0% to 1.75% impact on capital from upcoming ECL norms effective April 1, 2027 (management stated this can be absorbed or spread over 5 years).
- Geopolitical uncertainties and commodity/energy price pressures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **NIMs and Treasury Impact:** Asked about the sequential dip in NIM despite higher loan yields, management clarified that Q1 included interest on an income tax refund, and maintained full-year NIM guidance of 3.0% to 3.4%.
- **Fee Income Stagnation:** Questioned on flat YoY fee income (~Rs 206–207 crore) despite higher disbursements, management explained Q1 had one-off locker fees and Q4 typically has card income; pipeline digital fee-based products (mutual fund, shares platform) should accelerate this.
- **Branch Expansion Pace:** On slow branch additions in H1, management stated it was cautious on rental costs and location quality; 15 will open before December and all 31 before March 31, 2027.
- **Asset Quality & Major Recoveries:** On H2 asset quality risks and pipeline recoveries, management reaffirmed its zero-tolerance stress stance and noted 1–2 large NPA recoveries slipped from Q2 into Q3.
- **CEO Tenure:** Asked about the MD & CEO's term ending in November, Raghavendra Bhat replied that the Board is actively deciding and a decision will follow soon.

### One-line takeaway
Karnataka Bank delivered robust 37% YoY PAT growth, expanding loan growth at 25% and maintaining healthy asset quality metrics with GNPA down to 2.27%.
