# Indian Bank - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong credit and deposit growth, stable asset quality, and healthy profitability supported by core performance and one-off NSE listing gains.

### Key numbers
- **Total business growth:** 14.22% YoY
- **Deposit growth:** 12.50% YoY
- **Advances growth:** 16.39% YoY (Corporate: 9.92%, RAM: 17.74%, Retail: 17.37%, Agriculture: 17.18%, MSME: 19.06%)
- **CASA ratio:** 39.36% (improved from 38.87% YoY; declined sequentially due to FCNR(B) deposits)
- **Net profit:** Rs.3,462 crore, up 14.71% YoY
- **Operating profit:** Crossed Rs.7,000 crore, up 46.58% YoY (up 18% excluding NSE contribution)
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Up 17.83% YoY
- **NIM:** Improved by 8 bps YoY (Domestic NIM +1 bps QoQ, Global NIM +2 bps QoQ, Overseas NIM +8 bps QoQ)
- **Return on Assets (RoA):** Flat at 1.31% sequentially
- **Return on Equity (RoE):** 19.66%, expansion of 18 bps
- **Cost-to-income ratio:** 44.59% including NSE gains; 39.30% excluding NSE gains
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 98.22%
- **Credit cost:** 0.22% (declined by 1 bp QoQ)
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR):** 16.21% (CET1: 15.18%, Tier 2: 1.03%)
- **Gross NPA:** 1.61%
- **Net NPA:** 0.14%
- **Slippage ratio:** 0.69% (declined by 8 bps QoQ)
- **SMA book:** 4.19% or Rs.29,748 crore in absolute terms (SMA-0: Rs.15,683 crore, SMA-1: Rs.7,222 crore, SMA-2: Rs.6,843 crore)
- **LCR:** 116%

### Highlights
- Booked a profit of Rs.252 crore and an MTM gain of Rs.1,134 crore due to the listing of the unlisted NSE shareholding.
- Bulk deposits reduced from Rs.1.62 lakh crore to Rs.1.52 lakh crore (reduction of ~Rs.10,000 crore).
- Raised USD 2.3 billion (~Rs.21,000 crore) in FCNR(B) deposits; loans against FCNR(B) stood at roughly USD 2 billion.
- ECL provision of Rs.1,500 crore made this quarter (total ECL provision stands at Rs.2,500 crore against an expected total impact of Rs.3,500 crore).
- Other income included Rs.271 crore of interest on an income tax refund.
- Launched AI conversational banking ('TARA') and Smart Wealth (AI-powered personal finance management tool) in Q2.
- Agricultural gold-loan book grew by 22% YoY to Rs.1.16 lakh crore (+11% QoQ).
- ECGLS sanctions stood at Rs.8,000 crore with disbursements of Rs.7,000 crore across 31,000 accounts.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised deposit growth guidance from 9-11% to 11-12%.
- Management revised advances growth guidance from 11-13% to 13-14%.
- Management expects NIM expansion of around 5-6 bps (or 6-7 bps) in the upcoming quarter.
- Management expects recurring credit cost impact under ECL to remain at 10-15 bps.
- Corporate credit growth targeted at 10-12% (lower double digits).
- Expected advances growth for next quarter around 14-15%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted that competition remains very high across all segments, including corporate and retail housing.
- 92% of the retail book has a CIBIL score above 750.
- WCDL, cash-credit, and MSME undrawn limits have remained stable or declined; management sees no increase in drawdowns due to geopolitical crises.
- Minimal exposure (under Rs.1,000 crore) in states where drought has been declared.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Staff expenses flat at Rs.3,037 crore (14.56%).
- Other expenses declined by 0.29% to Rs.1,545 crore.
- Cost of deposits increased by 9 bps sequentially.
- Yield on advances increased by 8 bps sequentially (Corporate yield at 8.49% vs 8.36% in June).
- Yield on investments increased by 2 bps sequentially.
- AFS reserve moved from positive Rs.503 crore in June to negative Rs.123 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Management mentioned taking approval to raise up to Rs.5,000 crore through Infrastructure Bonds.
- May raise Rs.1,000-2,000 crore of Tier 2 capital in January depending on market conditions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- MCLR-linked book constitutes 43.19% of advances; external benchmark-linked book constitutes 49.65%.
- Raised USD 400 million through OFCB.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential one-time MTM impact on the HTM book upon transition to new valuation guidelines from April 1st (HTM book constitutes 60-74% of the investment portfolio for banks).
- Regulator's view on Unconditionally Cancellable Commitments (UCC) requires computing credit RWA including the unutilized portion and disallows certain provisions from forming part of Tier 2 capital.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Unidentified Analyst):** Inquired about the surge in standard asset provisions and miscellaneous income. **Answer:** Management explained the Rs.1,500 crore ECL provision (bringing total to Rs.2,500 crore) and noted that miscellaneous income included a Rs.271 crore interest on an income tax refund.
- **Q (Unidentified Analyst):** Asked about the sustainability of FCNR(B)-backed deposit/loan growth and impact on NIM. **Answer:** Management clarified that FCNR(B) inflows were fully utilized (replacing bulk/retail deposits and funding loans) and guided for 14-15% advances growth and 5-6 bps NIM expansion next quarter.
- **Q (Unidentified Analyst):** Asked if credit cost guidance under ECL (10-15 bps) should be revised due to hardening yields. **Answer:** Management maintained the 10-15 bps guidance, stating default rates remain under control with declining SMA books.
- **Q (Unidentified Analyst):** Inquired about the HTM MTM impact under new valuation guidelines. **Answer:** Management acknowledged it would impact the entire banking sector given large HTM shares, noting banks would need to discuss it with the RBI.
- **Q (Unidentified Analyst):** Asked about the impact of Unconditionally Cancellable Commitments (UCC) on capital. **Answer:** Management confirmed it is a one-time adjustment removing specific provisions from Tier 2 and factoring unutilized limits into credit RWA, but noted capital adequacy (16.21%) remains very comfortable.

### One-line takeaway
Indian Bank delivered a robust Q2 FY27 performance with 16.39% advances growth, stable asset quality (GNPA at 1.61%), and a 14.71% rise in net profit, bolstered by solid RAM expansion and proactive provisioning.
