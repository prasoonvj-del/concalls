# UCO Bank - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-16 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong business growth across all sectors, improved profitability, and asset quality metrics outperforming initial guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Business:** Rs 6,37,280 crore (up 18.81% YoY).
*   **Global Advances:** Rs 2,87,690 crore (up 24.70% YoY).
*   **Total Deposits:** Rs 3,49,590 crore (up 14.36% YoY).
*   **Operating Profit:** Rs 1,872 crore (up 16.04% YoY).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 786 crore (up 26.77% YoY).
*   **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 2,886 crore (up 13.96% YoY).
*   **Gross NPA:** 1.96% (down 60 bps YoY).
*   **Net NPA:** 0.18% (down 25 bps YoY).
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** 18.85% (Tier 1: 17.27%).
*   **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 50.39%.
*   **Credit Cost:** 0.65%.

### Highlights
*   **RAM Portfolio:** Total RAM (Retail, Agri, MSME) portfolio reached Rs 1,68,736 crore, growing 26.92% YoY. Retail grew 30.23%, Agri 29.59%, and MSME 20.32%.
*   **CASA:** CASA ratio maintained at 36.77%.
*   **Asset Quality:** Slippage ratio for the quarter was 0.85%. Total provision coverage ratio (including TWO) is 98.36%.
*   **Digital/Tech:** Launched CBDC marketplace and IVR services, implemented robotic process automation (7 processes), and launched UPI Tap and Pay. Digital business balance sheet grew to over Rs 48,000 crore.
*   **Network:** 3,439 branches; opened a new GIFT City IBU branch during the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Management Stance:** Management stated they are currently outperforming initial guidance for deposits (14%+ vs 10-12%), advances (24.7% vs 12-14%), GNPA (<2% vs <2%), and Net NPA (0.18% vs <0.25%).
*   **Credit Growth:** Management expects to maintain growth momentum above initial guidance, driven by all sectors (Retail, Agri, MSME, Corporate).
*   **Credit Cost:** Guidance maintained below 0.75%.
*   **Cost-to-Income:** Aiming to maintain around 50% for the full year.
*   **NIM:** Global NIM at 3% and domestic at 3.19%; management expects to sustain NIM beyond initial guidance (2.80%-2.90%) due to repo rate pass-through.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Corporate Pipeline:** Sanctioned pipeline for corporate advances stands at Rs 10,000 crore.
*   **Rate Environment:** 66% of the loan portfolio is linked to the repo rate (EBLR), allowing for immediate yield benefits from rate hikes.
*   **Deposit Rates:** No immediate need to increase deposit rates; management will monitor supply and demand before taking a call.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Funds:** Increased to 4.44%.
*   **Yield on Advances:** 8.11% (domestic) and 7.79% (global).
*   **Operating Expenses:** Increased by Rs 160 crore, attributed to business growth, network expansion, and IT investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **ECL Transition:** Estimated requirement of ~Rs 3,200 crore for April 2027 transition; bank has already made provisions of Rs 2,500 crore.
*   **Treasury:** Limited exposure in FVTPL portfolio; management does not view treasury losses as a major concern.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Slippages:** Q2 saw a spike in slippages (Rs 549 crore vs Rs 428 crore QoQ), primarily due to the half-yearly classification exercise for agriculture/crop loans.
*   **SMA:** Slight increase in SMA 1 and 2, though management maintains it is within control at 0.42% (for Rs 1 crore and above).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is the Rs 301 crore recovery from written-off accounts (TWO) sustainable?** Management clarified the previous quarter had a one-off recovery of Rs 785 crore; Rs 300 crore is the normalized run rate they expect to maintain.
*   **Q: Why the spike in slippages?** Management explained it is a seasonal effect due to the September half-yearly marking of agriculture/crop loans.
*   **Q: Will you revise credit growth guidance upwards?** Management noted they typically do not change guidance mid-year but confirmed they are currently growing significantly faster than the 12-14% target.
*   **Q: Impact of rate hikes on NII vs. Treasury?** Management emphasized that the 66% EBLR-linked loan book will more than compensate for any treasury stress.
*   **Q: How is the second-hand car loan business performing?** Management admitted they have not analyzed this segment separately but acknowledged it yields 100-150 bps higher than normal car loans.

### One-line takeaway
UCO Bank is delivering strong growth and asset quality improvements that are consistently outpacing its own conservative annual guidance, supported by a high share of EBLR-linked assets.
