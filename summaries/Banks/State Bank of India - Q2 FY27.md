# State Bank of India - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong core operating performance, multi-decade best asset quality, robust deposit growth, and steady long-term guidance despite treasury yield headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Net Profit:** Rs 23,038 crore.
- **Operating Profit:** Rs 34,226 crore (up 7.28% YoY).
- **Domestic Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 3.11%.
- **Return on Assets (RoA):** 1.14% for H1 FY27.
- **Return on Equity (RoE):** 18.68% for H1 FY27.
- **Total Business:** Rs 115 trillion (Deposits: Rs 63 trillion; Advances: >Rs 52 trillion).
- **Deposit Growth:** 12.27% YoY (Term deposits up 14.26% YoY).
- **Gross NPA Ratio:** 1.39% (lowest in over two decades).
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 74%.
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** 16.09% (up 147 bps YoY).
- **Credit Cost:** 0.22% (22 bps).
- **MTM Treasury Loss:** Rs 1,275 crore (Rs 1,277 crore recorded in global markets).
- **Exceptional Gain:** Rs 7,288 crore pre-tax / post-tax gain from selling 6.35% stake in SBI Funds Management Limited (routed via P&L).

### Highlights
- **Subsidiary Listing:** Listed SBI Funds Management Limited during the quarter, booking gains in P&L.
- **Project SARAL:** Digitally completed over 1.2 crore Re-KYC updates since June, saving 50,000 man-hours daily; deployed >9,500 Seva Sarathis across high-footfall branches.
- **Cybersecurity:** Inaugurated SBI CyRIS (Cyber Resilience, Integrity, and Security Center of Excellence).
- **Home Loans:** Crossed Rs 10 lakh crore in home and home-related loan portfolio.
- **FCNR(B) Mobilization:** Mobilized $10.9 billion in FCNR(B) deposits, with net leverage of $8.83 billion funded through foreign offices.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Credit Growth:** Maintained full-year credit growth guidance of 14%–15% YoY.
- **Through-the-Cycle Guidance:** Maintained RoA at 1%, NIM at not less than 3%, and RoE at 15%.
- **Corporate Credit Pipeline:** Stand at ~Rs 7 lakh crore to Rs 7.8 lakh crore.
- **Interest Rate & Market Outlook:** Management expects a 50 bps repo rate hike in December 2026 and projects 10-year G-Sec yields to drop below 7% by FY-end.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management sees strong domestic macro momentum with RBI's FY27 real GDP growth estimated at 7.1%.
- Home loan market growth slowed industry-wide, attributed partly to inventory build-up and fewer new project launches rather than purely interest-rate sensitivity.
- Corporate salary package credits show stable 12%–14% growth with no slowdown observed.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margin Drivers:** Domestic NIM expanded to 3.11% driven by improved yield on advances and lower cost of deposits (focus on daily average balances and replacing wholesale deposits with FCNR(B) flows).
- **Cost Upfronting:** Upfronted ~Rs 850 crore of OpEx in Q2 (IT services ~Rs 250 crore, vendors ~Rs 150 crore, map services ~Rs 450 crore) to prevent Q4 cost spikes.
- **Overheads Target:** Target to cap YoY overhead growth under 10%.
- **PSLC Purchases:** Incurred PSLC costs of Rs 118 crore in Q1 and Rs 583 crore in Q2.
- **PLI Provisioning:** Booked Rs 940 crore provision for Performance Linked Incentive (PLI) in Q2 (run-rate to continue across all 4 quarters).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity Buffer:** Excess SLR stands at Rs 3.5 lakh crore to Rs 3.7 lakh crore (was Rs 4.8 lakh crore in September 2026).
- **ECL Impact:** Standalone net Expected Credit Loss (ECL) transition impact on loan book estimated at Rs 19,500 crore (net of DTA adjustments and existing provisions); to be transitioned over 4 years.
- **Loan Book Mix:** MCLR-linked 27%, EBLR-linked 32%, T-Bill linked 17%, Fixed rate 21%.
- **NSE Stake Value:** Mark-to-market current valuation gain of remaining NSE holding is Rs 11,000 crore, routed directly to AFS reserve.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global economic uncertainties, trade policy shifts, and financial market volatility.
- Bond yield movements causing MTM treasury losses of Rs 1,275 crore in Q2.
- Drought declared in 3 states created ~Rs 10,000 crore of potential agri loans eligible for restructuring.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ashok Ajmera (Ajcon Global):** *Why were NSE stake sale gains not routed through P&L while SBI Mutual Fund gains were?*  
   **Management:** NSE shares were classified under Available For Sale (AFS), so gains went directly to AFS reserves. SBI Mutual Fund was held under FVTPL (Associates & Subsidiaries), requiring gain routing through P&L.
2. **Jai Mundhra (ICICI Securities):** *What is the transition impact of ECL and agri restructuring stress due to drought?*  
   **Management:** Net ECL transition impact on standalone loans is Rs 19,500 crore post-tax and existing provisions. Potential agri portfolio for restructuring across 3 drought-affected states is ~Rs 10,000 crore, though past trends indicate low actual utilization by farmers.
3. **Nitin Agarwal (Motilal Oswal):** *What drove domestic NIM expansion to 3.11% and why was YTD corporate credit growth lower?*  
   **Management:** NIM expanded due to lower deposit costs and higher advance yields (exiting fine-priced corporate working capital loans). Corporate loan growth usually picks up in H2 and is supported by a ~Rs 7.8 lakh crore pipeline.
4. **Mahesh (Kotak):** *What accounts for the provisioning line items and slowdown in retail products?*  
   **Management:** Provisioning includes Rs 940 crore PLI cost per quarter. Auto loan pricing was tweaked to regain market share, while personal credit momentum shifted partially into gold loans; home loan weakness reflects supply-side issues (developer project delays).

### One-line takeaway
SBI delivered robust core operational performance with Gross NPAs at a two-decade low of 1.39% and maintained full-year credit growth guidance of 14%–15%.
