# Bank of India - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY growth in global business, deposits, and advances, along with improved profitability and asset quality.

### Key numbers
- Global Business: Rs. 18.92 lakh crore as of Sept’26, up 21.13% YoY (incremental growth of Rs. 3.30 lakh crore).
- Global Deposits: Rs. 10.37 lakh crore, up 21.59% YoY. Domestic Deposits: Rs. 9.00 lakh crore, up 23.29% YoY.
- CASA: Rs. 3.07 lakh crore; CASA Ratio: 34.16%.
- Global Gross Advances: Rs. 8.55 lakh crore, up 20.58% YoY. Domestic Gross Advances: Rs. 7.04 lakh crore, up 17.84% YoY.
- RAM Advances: Rs. 4.13 lakh crore, up 18.68% YoY (58.63% of Gross Advances).
- Operating Profit: Rs. 5,089 crore, up 33.18% YoY.
- Net Profit: Rs. 3,202 crore, up 25.32% YoY.
- Net Interest Income (NII): Rs. 7,007 crore, up 18.52% YoY.
- Non-Interest Income: Rs. 2,770 crore, up 24.77% YoY.
- Global NIM: 2.47% (vs 2.41% in Sept’25 and 2.52% in June’26).
- Gross NPA Ratio: 1.60% (improved by 94 bps YoY); Net NPA Ratio: 0.42% (improved by 23 bps YoY).
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 94.45% (vs 93.39% in Sept’25).
- CRAR: 17.78% as of Sept’26 (vs 16.69% as of Sept’25).
- Slippage Ratio: 0.15%; Credit Cost: 0.12%.

### Highlights
- Implemented institutional initiatives including Star Radar (predictive risk model), a dedicated Gold Loan Cell, Yuva Banking Mitra for youth banking, and OCR-based CTS validation.
- Standard asset provisions reduced by Rs. 213 crore in the quarter due to improved credit monitoring and reduced SMA.
- Recovery in written-off accounts surged to Rs. 978 crore in the quarter (190% YoY growth).
- FCNR deposit mobilization stood at $2.2 billion (approx. Rs. 40,000 crore including leverage).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided FY27 global advances growth at 15-16% and global deposits growth at 16-17%.
- Management expects full-year net profit to reach around Rs. 12,000 crore based on H1 run-rate.
- Management aims to maintain ROA above 1% on a quarter-on-quarter basis and for the full year FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India’s Q2 GDP and domestic demand remain resilient despite global headwinds (West Asia crisis, elevated crude, El Nino).
- GST collections consistently exceed Rs. 2 lakh crore per month; e-way bills generated at 13-14 crore per month.
- IMF projects FY27 GDP growth at 6.7%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Global NIM stood at 2.47%, impacted by 5 bps due to the FCNR raising of $2.2 billion.
- Repo rate hike of 25 bps was passed on immediately to external benchmark-linked loans (66% of domestic advances), expected to improve NIM by 6 to 7 bps, while term deposit repricing is staggered.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Corporate credit pipeline stands at Rs. 65,000 crore, RAM pipeline at Rs. 20,000 crore, pool/co-lending/supply chain pipeline at Rs. 4,000 crore, and digital platform pipeline at Rs. 3,000 crore (total pipeline of Rs. 86,000-87,000 crore).
- Major corporate pipelines include renewable energy (>Rs. 12,000 crore), power (>Rs. 9,000 crore), roads (>Rs. 5,000 crore), and oil & gas (>Rs. 5,000 crore).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Government stake stands at 73.8% (within the 75% SEBI limit); no OFS or capital-raising plans currently.
- Gold loan book stands at Rs. 61,000 crore, with majority under agriculture, followed by MSME and retail; NPA in gold loans is 0.13%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential impact of West Asia crisis on crude prices and inflation.
- Anticipated ECL provisioning starting April 1, 2027; management estimates an annual impact of ~0.5% on CRAR over 4 years, which will be comfortably absorbed by annual profitability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ashok Ajmera (Ajcon Global Services):** Asked about ECL preparedness and provision reversals. *Management stated ECL impact on CRAR is estimated at ~0.5% per annum over 4 years, to be absorbed by projected ~Rs. 12,000 crore annual profit, with front-loading expected in Q3/Q4.*
- **Sushil Choksey:** Inquired about the corporate/RAM pipeline and supply chain financing. *Management detailed the Rs. 86,000+ crore pipeline across sectors and highlighted the centralized supply chain branch targeting Rs. 1,500 crore in its first year.*
- **Mayur Gala (True Alpha Finserv):** Asked about government stake and ROA targets. *Management confirmed no OFS plans and reiterated the target for ROA to stay above 1% for FY27.*
- **Niteen Dharmawat:** Asked about drivers of recovery in written-off accounts and NPA provisions. *Management attributed write-off recoveries to lumpy accounts (2-3 accounts) and stated provisions are as per IRAC norms for fresh slippages.*
- **Manoj Alimchandani:** Questioned the lack of clarity on front-loading ECL provisions compared to peers. *Management reiterated that formal Board approval and provisioning will commence in Q3/Q4.*

### One-line takeaway
Bank of India delivered robust Q2 FY27 performance with a 25.32% YoY net profit growth to Rs. 3,202 crore, strong asset quality improvements, and an above-1% ROA.
