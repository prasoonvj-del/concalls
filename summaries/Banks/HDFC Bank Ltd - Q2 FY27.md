# HDFC Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - positive macroeconomic momentum and retail/MSME asset growth, offset by margin pressure and extraordinary deposit outflows.

### Key numbers
- **Deposits:** +18.8% YoY on a reported basis (includes 4 percentage points from special FCNR(B) window).
- **Retail CASA Growth:** 11.2% YoY.
- **Total AUM Growth:** +15.3% YoY (includes 1.9% impact of FCNR(B) leverage).
- **Retail Disbursements:** Crossed Rs 90,000 crores in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 76,000 crores in Q1 FY27).
- **Retail Assets (ex-agri) EOP Growth:** 11.2% annual growth as of September '26 (vs 9.1% in June '26 and 7.4% in March '26).
- **New Credit Card Issuances:** 1.4 million (+19% QoQ).
- **MSME and Mid-Market EOP Loan Growth:** 23.5% YoY.
- **ECLGS 5.0 Disbursals:** Rs 41,000 crores (highest across all banks as of August '26).
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 3.32%.
- **Cost of Funds:** Flat QoQ at 4.4%.
- **Gross NPA:** 1.11% (13 bps lower YoY).
- **Credit Costs:** 44 bps.
- **Risk Weights to Total Assets:** 69%.
- **ROA:** 1.8%.
- **Quarterly PAT Growth:** ~12.7% YoY (excluding trading gains).
- **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR):** 113%.

### Highlights
- Management transition: Sashidhar Jagdishan completed his tenure, and Anup Bagchi took over as MD and CEO. Kaizad Bharucha requested early retirement by the end of December.
- Strong macro indicators: Robust retail consumption with passenger vehicles and 2-wheeler sales growing 30%+, and consumer durables growing 11% YoY.
- RBI’s special FCNR(B) swap window generated record inflows of about $133 billion for the banking system.
- Daily operating rigor and digital adoption reduced product delivery TATs by 10%–15% and service delivery TATs by 30%.
- Bank added 1.7 million liability relationships in Q2 (+25% QoQ).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects NIM trajectory to be upward from Q3 FY27 onwards, viewing Q2 as the bottom of the rate cycle.
- Incoming MD & CEO Anup Bagchi stated he will engage with stakeholders and unveil a perspective on growth, margins, quality, and productivity in about a month.

### Demand & industry commentary
- High-frequency indicators reflect sustained momentum in industrial production, e-way bills, cargo activity, and credit growth.
- Kharif sowing remains largely constant with a marginal decrease of 1.5% to 2% (approx 108–109 million hectares). Agri asset quality held steady.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of funds remained flat QoQ at 4.4%, impacted by the mix of incoming FCNR deposits and time deposits.
- Floating rate book stands at approximately 70% of total loans, providing a tailwind for repricing following the 25 bps repo rate hike in October.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Distribution network doubled over the last 5 years, with vintage branches maturing.
- Customer visits per resource per day increased by ~50% QoQ due to improved turnaround times and process efficiencies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total deposit growth of Rs 1,57,000 crores matched total loan growth of Rs 1,58,000 crores during the quarter.
- Corporate book grew 2.8% QoQ and 16.4% YoY.
- LCR stood at 113%; management noted overall liquidity remains comfortable despite lower LCR.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Extraordinary outflows in retail, MSME, government, and inherited legacy deposits masked underlying core deposit momentum during the quarter.
- Proposed draft insurance norms featuring lower commission rates could significantly impact bancassurance fee income.
- Persistent scarcity of small and marginal farmer (SMF) assets creates shortfalls in Priority Sector Lending (PSL) sub-targets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mahrukh Adajania (Tara Capital Partners) on retail deposit momentum and agri impact:** Management explained that extraordinary large deposits and legacy outflows masked underlying core retail deposit momentum. Agri asset quality remained stable despite slight monsoon shortfalls.
- **Kunal Shah (IIFL Capital) on margin decline and cost of funds:** Management stated cost of funds was flat QoQ at 4.4% due to FCNR deposit additions offsetting back-book repricing. Back-book repricing in retail is expected to normalize margins over the next two quarters.
- **Rikin Shah (IIFL Capital) on FCNR deployment and NIM trajectory:** FCNR deposits were deployed immediately to fund loan growth and meet PSL requirements. NIM trajectory is expected to be neutral to positive going forward.
- **Parag Thakkar on margin bottoming:** Management confirmed that Q2 represents the bottom of the margin cycle, with repo rate hikes acting as a tailwind from Q3 onwards.
- **Chintan on CASA trends and ROEs:** Management noted substantial expansion headroom across 765 districts (with low market share in 54%+ districts) and cited structural shifts in household savings behavior.
- **Nitin Aggarwal on employee count and LCR:** Management stated headcount has remained relatively flat due to digitization and productivity gains, with staff shifting toward frontline roles. LCR is maintained around 113% against an internal comfort range of 110%–120%.

### One-line takeaway
HDFC Bank delivered stable asset quality and robust retail/MSME loan growth in Q2 FY27, while management flagged Q2 as the margin bottom and anticipates an upward NIM trajectory ahead of a leadership transition.
