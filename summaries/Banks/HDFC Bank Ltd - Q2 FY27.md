# HDFC Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong core operational momentum in retail/MSME disbursements and pristine asset quality despite temporary margin pressure and senior executive transitions.

### Key numbers
* **PAT:** Rs 19.5 billion, up approximately 12.7% YoY (excluding trading gains).
* **Total AUM Growth:** 15.3% YoY (includes 1.9% impact of FCNR (B) leverage).
* **Total Deposit Growth:** 18.8% YoY on reported basis (includes 4 percentage point lift from FCNR (B) swap).
* **CASA Growth:** Overall CASA grew 10.8% YoY; retail CASA grew 11.2% YoY.
* **Retail Disbursements:** Crossed Rs 90,000 crores in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 76,000 crores in Q1 FY27).
* **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 3.32% in Q2 FY27.
* **Cost of Funds:** Flat QoQ at 4.4%.
* **Gross NPA:** 1.11%, down 13 bps YoY.
* **Credit Cost:** 44 bps (0.44%).
* **Risk Weights to Total Assets:** 69%.
* **Return on Assets (ROA):** 1.8%.

### Highlights
* **Leadership Transitions:** Sashidhar Jagdishan's tenure as MD & CEO is concluding, with Anup Bagchi appointed as incoming MD & CEO. Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha requested early retirement by end of December 2026.
* **Customer Acquisition:** Added 1.7 million liability relationships in Q2 FY27 (up 25% QoQ and 33% higher than Q4 FY26).
* **Credit Cards:** Issued 1.4 million new credit cards, up nearly 19% QoQ.
* **MSME Momentum:** ECLGS 5.0 disbursals reached Rs 41,000 crores, highest across all banks as of August 2026. Business Banking loan growth stood at 28% YoY.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Product turnaround times (TAT) improved 10-15%, service delivery TAT dropped by 30%, and customer visits per resource/day increased by ~50% QoQ.
* **Tech & AI Rollouts:** Introduced Edge Security (Kavach, Secure Lock), AI conversational banking, AI collections bot, and real-time fraud prevention.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management expects NIMs to have bottomed out in Q2 FY27 and project an upward trajectory starting Q3 FY27 due to repricing of floating-rate assets.
* Manpower strategy aims to optimize back-office headcount via digitization while shifting resources to customer-facing frontline roles, maintaining total headcount flat before resuming expansion in 3-4 years.
* Strategic focus going forward will be on maximizing core retail deposits while using non-deposit balance sheet adjustments to optimize funding costs.

### Demand & industry commentary
* GDP growth in Q2 FY27 was better than expected, supported by high-frequency indicators like industrial production, e-way bills, and credit growth.
* Retail consumption demand remains robust ahead of the festive season, with 2-wheeler and passenger vehicle sales growing over 30% YoY and consumer durables growing ~11% YoY.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Cost of funds remained flat QoQ at 4.4%, as back-book repricing and AT1 bond redemptions were offset by higher time deposits and FCNR borrowings.
* Out of Rs 1,57,000 crores deposit accretion during the quarter, Rs 1,30,000 crores (Rs 1.3 trillion) was in time deposits.
* Cost to assets remains low at ~1.73% to 1.74%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Branch productivity improved significantly: core incremental deposits per branch/day rose 2.3 times from Q1 FY27, and savings accounts opened per branch/day increased 19% QoQ.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Quarterly loan accretion stood at Rs 1,58,000 crores against deposit growth of Rs 1,57,000 crores.
* Floating rate loans comprise ~70% of the total loan book, mostly linked to repo rates.
* Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) stood at 113% (target operating band is 110%–120%). Branch-driven deposits constitute ~80% of total deposits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Monsoon shortfalls in peninsula and Northern India, though kharif sowing dropped marginally by 1.5% to 2%.
* Run-off of high-ticket legacy deposits (HDFC Ltd inherited, government, MSME, and FCNR leverage) created temporary LCR pressure.
* Shortfall in Priority Sector Lending (PSL) sub-targets for small & marginal farmers due to high certificate market costs and supply scarcity.
* Potential adverse impact on bancassurance fees due to draft regulatory limits on insurance commission structures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Mahrukh Adajania (Tara Capital):** Asked about retail deposit proportion timing and agri portfolio impact from dry weather.  
  *Management Response:* Outflow of large legacy/FCNR/government deposits masked strong retail branch productivity. Sowing area remains stable (~108-109m ha), and agri gross NPA remained steady QoQ.
* **Kunal Shah (IIFL):** Inquired why NIM dropped to 3.32% despite moderate corporate growth and cost of funds behavior.  
  *Management Response:* Cost of funds was flat at 4.4% due to time deposit weighting. Retail loan back-book repricing cycles run over 2-4 years, which will support NIM expansion over the next two quarters.
* **Rikin Shah (IIFL Capital):** Asked how FCNR funds were deployed and NIM outlook for Q3.  
  *Management Response:* FCNR funds are fully deployed into assets matching quarterly loan growth. Repo rate hikes covering 70% floating loans will provide margin tailwinds, signaling NIMs have hit bottom.
* **Chintan (Analyst):** Asked about industry-wide CASA pressures and long-term ROEs given competitive pricing.  
  *Management Response:* Retail CASA ratio remains healthy in late 40s%. CASA moderation was driven by substituting Rs 2.6 lakh crore of inherited HDFC Ltd borrowings with term deposits and structural household saving shifts.
* **Nitin Aggarwal (Analyst):** Questioned headcount trajectory and liability mobilization strategy.  
  *Management Response:* Productivity gains from AI and straight-through processing allow shifting back-end staff to frontline. Headcount will remain largely steady before inflecting upward in 3-4 years.
* **Ankit Bihani (Analyst):** Clarified LCR constraints on loan growth, draft insurance norms, and PSL shortfalls.  
  *Management Response:* Operating LCR around 113% is adequate for planned growth. Insurance commission caps would impact fee income but could boost volumes via affordability. Bank remains short on small/marginal farmer PSL sub-targets due to market scarcity.

### One-line takeaway
HDFC Bank's operational momentum and asset quality remain robust, with margins poised to recover from Q2 lows as floating loans reprice and retail asset execution accelerates under incoming leadership.
