# Central Bank of India - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial results with growth across total business, advances, and net profit, along with improved asset quality.

### Key numbers
- Total business: Rs. 8,89,541 crore (up 20.54% YoY)
- Total domestic deposits: Rs. 5,08,509 crore (up 14.41% YoY)
- CASA deposits: 44.65% of total deposits (Rs. 2,26,774 crore, up 9.23% YoY)
- Global advances: Rs. 3,81,032 crore (up 29.83% YoY); Domestic advances: Rs. 3,72,939 crore (up 27% YoY)
- Net profit for Q2 FY2027: Rs. 1,469 crore (up 21% YoY, up 11% QoQ)
- Operating profit: Rs. 2,26,800 crore (Rs. 2,268 crore, up 27% YoY)
- Net Interest Income (NII): Rs. 4,146 crore (up 26.28% YoY, up ~6% QoQ)
- Total income: Rs. 11,737 crore (up ~15% YoY)
- Cost-to-income ratio: 58.95% (vs 62.42% in Q2 FY2026)
- Return on Assets (ROA): 1.03% (up 3 bps QoQ)
- Net Interest Margin (NIM): 3.01%
- Gross NPA: 2.39%
- Net NPA: 0.31% (17 bps improvement)
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 97.16%
- Slippage ratio: 0.24% (quarterly slippage ratio at 1.24%)
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR): 156%
- Credit cost: 0.53%
- Yield on advances: 7.87%
- Cost of deposit: 4.60%
- FCNR(B) mobilization: 926.74 million USD across 731 accounts (overseas loans provided against this: 794.60 million USD)

### Highlights
- Opened GIFT City Branch on June 29, 2026, contributing Rs. 8,093 crore to overseas advances.
- RAM (Retail, Agriculture, MSME) business grew by 21.11% to reach Rs. 2,54,273 crore (Retail up 24.49% to Rs. 1,12,299 crore; Agriculture up 19.92% to Rs. 67,778 crore; MSME up 17.36% to Rs. 74,196 crore).
- Corporate book grew by 42% YoY to Rs. 1,18,666 crore.
- Recovery in written-off accounts was Rs. 485 crore during the quarter; written-off pool stands at Rs. 31,301 crore.
- Business per employee improved to Rs. 25.26 crore from Rs. 21.48 crore YoY.
- Planned rollout of 116 customized gold loan branches and focus on Self Help Groups (SHGs) via tie-ups with 26 state NRLMs.
- Recruited 300 additional marketing officers.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects credit cost to remain up to 0.75% for the next year.
- Management is confident that ROA will remain above 1%.
- Management expects ECL provisioning requirement to be approximately Rs. 5,000 crore to Rs. 5,500 crore (with Rs. 1,525 crore already kept as additional provision).
- Management expects recovery from the written-off pool to exceed Rs. 2,500 crore for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- RAM sector continues to be the bread and butter, supported by 65% branches in rural and semi-urban areas.
- Corporate pipeline includes Rs. 7,000 crore of undisbursed sanctions, Rs. 5,000 crore under process, and Rs. 11,000 crore under consideration (total corporate pipeline of ~Rs. 24,000 crore). Focus areas include renewable energy, roads, data centers, real estate, hospitality, manufacturing, NBFCs, and agri-food processing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Treasury yields hardening (yield at 7.19% as of September 30, 2026); AFS portfolio faced MTM challenges. Treasury earned Rs. 56 crore from IPOs.
- Cost-to-income ratio improved to 58.95% but missed internal guidance of less than 56%.
- 61% of the loan book is linked to external benchmarks (repo rate), which management expects will benefit yields in a rising interest rate scenario.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up 116 customized gold loan branches with a target TAT of maximum 30 minutes.
- Re-visited Credit Processing and Approval Center (CPAC) structure with 32 places identified with separate team managers for higher-ticket MSME loans.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total business reached Rs. 8,89,541 crore; total domestic deposits at Rs. 5,08,509 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Treasury outlook remains challenging due to global turmoil, rising interest rates, inflation, and high US rates creating MTM pressures in the AFS portfolio.
- CASA ratio closed at 44.65%, missing market guidance of 48% (plus or minus 1%) due to FCNR(B) mobilization and other aspects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sushil Choksey (Indus Equity) asked about the growth drivers within RAM and CASA sustainability.** Management highlighted gold loans (116 new branches) and SHGs as key growth engines alongside housing and car loans, while noting strong geographical presence in Bihar, UP, Maharashtra, and MP.
2. **Ashok Ajmera (Ajcon Global) asked about ECL provisioning preparedness and corporate pipeline.** Management stated total ECL requirement is estimated at Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 5,500 crore against which Rs. 1,525 crore is already provided, and the bank is well-prepared. Corporate pipeline stands at ~Rs. 24,000 crore.
3. **Rajesh Agarwal (AUM Capital Markets) questioned why provisions went up despite improving asset quality.** Management clarified that provisions increased due to building adequate buffers during good times (raising PCR) and growth in standard advances.
4. **Ashlesh Sonje (Kotak Securities) asked if ROA will sustain above 1% for FY2028/FY2029.** Management expressed confidence that ROA will remain above 1% given external benchmark linkages and growth engines like gold loans and SHGs.
5. **Deepak Kala (Trimurti Enterprise) asked about steps to improve yield on advances and technology investments.** Management noted that repo rate cuts in the past impacted yields, but rising rates, gold loans (~8.5% yield), and SHGs will help. For technology, a new New Banking Emerging Department (NBET) has been formed to overhaul digital journeys.
6. **Siddharth Rajpurohit (Systematix Institutional Equities) inquired about the FCNR(B) mobilization amount.** Management responded that 926.74 million USD was raised across 731 accounts.

### One-line takeaway
Central Bank of India delivered a strong Q2 FY2027 with a 21% YoY increase in net profit, robust advances growth of 29.83%, stable asset quality (Gross NPA at 2.39%), and focused expansion into RAM, gold loans, and corporate sectors.
