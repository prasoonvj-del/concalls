# Bank of Maharashtra - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Meeting guidance across metrics, strong growth in advances/deposits, and improved asset quality and profitability.

### Key numbers
- Total business: Crossed Rs 7 lakh crore for the first time
- Business growth YoY: 25%
- Advances growth YoY: 30% (Domestic credit growth: 23%; Large credit growth: 38% or Rs 36,000 crore, with Rs 18,000 crore from GIFT City IBU and Rs 18,000 crore domestic)
- Deposit growth YoY: 22% (ex-FCNR(B): 19%); CASA growth YoY: 12% (added Rs 19,000 crore in absolute terms)
- GNPA: 1.25% (down 47 bps YoY); NNPA: 0.12% (down 6 bps YoY)
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 98.53%
- Total stress in loan book: 3.26% (down 167 bps YoY); SMA 1 & 2: 1.37% (down 50 bps YoY)
- Cost of deposits: 4.38% (down 4 bps YoY); Cost of funds: 4.07% (down 25 bps YoY)
- Operating profit growth YoY: 31%; Net profit growth YoY: 41%
- Return on Equity (ROE): 26.98% (up 440 bps YoY)
- Net Interest Margin (NIM): 3.97% (up 12 bps YoY)
- Return on Assets (ROA): 2.02% (up 20 bps YoY)
- Capital Adequacy: CET1 at 15.61%, CRAR at 18.51%
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR): 123%; Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR): 127%
- ECL provisioning: Done Rs 500 crore in Q2 (Total ECL provision: Rs 750 crore including Rs 250 crore upfront previously; target assessed at Rs 2,500 crore over 4 years)
- Write-off recovery: Rs 737 crores in Q2 (Total FY27 so far: Rs 1,023 crores); Write-off book stands at Rs 21,500 crore.

### Highlights
- Crossed milestone of Rs 7 lakh crore total business and credit crossed Rs 3 lakh crore.
- GIFT City IBU branch contributed Rs 18,000 crore to large credit growth.
- Refinance book outstanding at Rs 18,300 crore (at 6% to 6.5%); infrastructure bonds outstanding at Rs 2,400 crore.
- First-ever foreign bond issuance (MTN program) raised $1.65 billion (FCNR(B) and OFCB combined) with 3x subscription.
- Implemented branch-level profitability reviews and tied them to employee annual performance ratings.
- Employee strength increased from 13,000 two years ago to 17,500 to support branch expansion and specialized functions.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated all guidance numbers set at the start of the year are being consistently met.
- Credit growth guidance maintained at 18% (bank is currently outperforming at 23% ex-IBU).
- Target to recover Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,500 crore annually from the write-off book for the next 4 to 5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- RAM (Retail, Agri, MSME) verticals all grew individually above 20% YoY.
- Corporate pipeline stands at Rs 23,000 crore, with expected disbursement of Rs 10,000 crore by March 2027. Sector exposure: 18% infra power, 12% infra roads (HAM/NHAI), 10% renewable energy, 10% metals/allied, and data centers.
- Agriculture gold loans grew from Rs 6,500 crore (Sept '25) to Rs 18,600 crore (Sept '26). Total gold loan outstanding stands at Rs 33,000 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of deposits reduced by 4 bps YoY to 4.38%; overall cost of funds reduced by 25 bps YoY to 4.07%.
- EBLR book proportion stands at 47%.
- IT budget for FY24-25 was Rs 900 crore and for FY25-26 is Rs 985 crore, heavily allocated toward cybersecurity and digital infrastructure.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opening 200 branches annually (77 branches made functional in H1 FY27).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Outstanding refinance book: Rs 18,300 crore.
- Outstanding infrastructure bonds: Rs 2,400 crore.
- Total surplus provisions remaining (including global uncertainties/COVID/ECL): Rs 1,600 crore to Rs 1,800 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Monitoring potential El Nino impact closely, though no visible stress or distress has been experienced in the agriculture book so far.
- Maharashtra and Karnataka drought situation prompting SLBC restructuring discussions (crop loss triggers offering 1-year moratorium and repayment up to 3-5 years); historical experience shows only 10% to 15% avail restructuring.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ashok Ajmera (Ajcon):** Asked about ECL preparedness and provisioning shifts. Management replied they have done Rs 750 crore of ECL provisioning so far against a 4-year assessment of Rs 2,500 crore, covering more than 1 year ahead. Management did not dodge.
2. **Parth (360 ONE Capital):** Asked about the sustainability of cost of deposits and interest income impact from write-off recovery. Management stated FCNR(B) and OFCB rates are fixed (e.g., OFCB at 6.11%), and high-cost bulk deposits are consciously let go in favor of low-cost stable retail deposits. Management did not dodge.
3. **Amansingh Sahajsinghani (ICICI Securities):** Inquired about the decline in retail gold loans QoQ. Management explained that RBI guidelines reclassified certain gold loans into MSME and Agri based on customer profile/Udyam registration, causing a shift between buckets rather than a customer exodus. Management did not dodge.
4. **Sameer Bhise (Dymon Asia):** Asked about drivers of the sequential jump in yield on advances. Management cited selective upward price revisions in fine-rate MCLR accounts (such as state PSUs) executed in Q1. Management did not dodge.
5. **Jai Prakash Mundhra (ICICI Securities):** Questioned the disconnect between lower-yielding IBU growth and overall rising yields, and inquired about Maharashtra debt waiver timelines. Management explained global vs. domestic yields and noted the state government's target timeline for debt waiver tranches is March 31, 2027. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
Bank of Maharashtra reported a robust Q2 FY27 with business crossing Rs 7 lakh crore, a 41% YoY jump in net profit, an ROA above 2%, and strong asset quality backed by proactive ECL provisioning and profitable growth strategies.
