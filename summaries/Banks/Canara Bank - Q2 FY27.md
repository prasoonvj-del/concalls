# Canara Bank - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-17 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted record quarterly net profit and NII, crossing the Rs 30 lakh crore total business milestone while beating all major full-year guidance metrics.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained formal guidance ranges, but raised ECL one-time impact estimate from Rs 10,000–12,000 crore to Rs 12,000–15,000 crore due to yield hardening on the HTM book.
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone remained strongly positive with broad-based loan growth across Retail, MSME, and Agri, alongside a robust corporate loan pipeline.
- **New topics or risks:** 
  - Garnered $5.8 billion (~Rs 55,000 crore) of FCNR(B) deposits, fully replacing high-cost bulk deposits.
  - Plan to divest stake in subsidiary Canbank Factors, expecting a 4X return on a Rs 14 crore initial investment.
  - Exposure of Rs 13,900 crore to drought-declared regions in Karnataka and Maharashtra (expecting SLBC dispensations/restructuring).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Exceeded FCNR(B) deposit target ($5.8 billion raised vs $2.3–2.5 billion targeted). Reconfirmed strategy to absorb ECL impact within 1–2 years instead of 5 years.

### Key numbers
- **Total Global Business:** Rs 30,72,000 crore (+15.88% YoY).
- **Global Deposits:** Rs 16,97,000 crore (+13.13% YoY).
- **Global Advances:** Rs 13,75,000 crore (+19.46% YoY).
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Ever-highest at Rs 11,000+ crore (+20.39% YoY).
- **Net Profit:** Ever-highest at Rs 5,175 crore (+8.40% YoY).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.33% (down from 1.57% QoQ; beats FY27 guidance of 1.50%).
- **Net NPA:** 0.30% (down from 0.36% QoQ; beats FY27 guidance of 0.40%).
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 95.34% (+175 bps YoY).
- **CRAR:** 17.19% (CET1 at 12.80%).
- **Slippage Ratio (Annualized):** 0.58% (-18 bps YoY).
- **Credit Cost:** 0.47% (-21 bps YoY).
- **Return on Equity (ROE):** 18.49% (beats guidance of 16.5%).
- **Return on Average Assets (ROAA):** 1.04%.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Rs 22.06 (+5% YoY).
- **LCR:** 115% as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
- Crossed the landmark Rs 30 lakh crore total global business milestone.
- Advanced ahead of FY27 guidance targets across business growth, advance growth, deposit growth, asset quality, slippages, credit cost, ROE, and EPS.
- *Our Record Check:* Continues the pattern set in Q1 FY27 of outperforming annual operational and asset quality targets.

### Guidance & outlook
- **NIM:** Expected to be sustained around 2.55%.
- **ECL Impact:** One-time provision impact revised upward to Rs 12,000–15,000 crore (equivalent to 1.0%–1.2% of CRAR), including MTM impact on HTM investments. Management intends to absorb it over 1–2 years max out of operational profits.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust credit growth driven by Retail (+31.61% YoY), Vehicle loans (+25.50% YoY), Housing loans (+17.35% YoY), RAM credit (+21.03% YoY), and MSME (+15.49% YoY).
- Active corporate loan pipeline of ~Rs 47,000–50,000 crore (Rs 30,000 crore hot leads; Rs 17,000 crore sanctioned undisbursed), led by green energy, battery storage, power evacuation, data centers, and HAM projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Quarterly NIM stood at 2.55% (2.53% annual).
- Secured $5.8 billion in FCNR(B) deposits (utilized $3.2 billion for advances, with the balance replacing bulk deposits and saving ~100 bps in funding costs plus CRR/SLR savings).
- Corporate loan incremental yield improved QoQ to 7.72% (vs 7.68%), while Gold loan yield rose to 9.10% (vs 8.96%).
- MTM loss on FVTPL/treasury stood at Rs 61 crore for Q2 FY27 (vs MTM profit of Rs 611 crore in Q1 FY27 and Rs 762 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Actuarial provisions (AS 15) for pension and gratuity were Rs 950 crore for the quarter.
- Income tax refund interest income received was Rs 90 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investing in digital payment platforms and tools, including their payment service provider platform *ai1Pe*.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **CASA Ratio:** Maintained at 29% despite massive term deposit inflows via FCNR(B).
- **Gold Loan Portfolio:** Total Rs 2.82 lakh crore (Agriculture Gold: Rs 1.74 lakh crore; Non-Agriculture Gold: Rs 1.08 lakh crore).
- **Stress & SMA Book:** Total SMA (0, 1, and 2 across all ticket sizes) stood at 2.42% (Rs 33,340 crore). SMA > Rs 5 crore stood at 0.51% (Rs 7,043 crore).
- **Quarterly Slippages:** Rs 1,844 crore (Agriculture: Rs 687 crore, MSME: Rs 777 crore, Retail: Rs 327 crore, Gold: Rs 40 crore, Corporate: Rs 13 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- MTM vulnerability on non-HTM investment books and ECL HTM revaluation due to hardening bond yields.
- Shrinking written-off loan pool, which will reduce absolute recovery amounts in future quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jay Mundra:** *Why are NIMs expected to be flat despite 53% of loans repricing up and saving 100 bps on bulk deposits via FCNR(B)?*  
  **Management:** The $3.2 billion leveraged portion of FCNR(B) carries a narrow spread of 40–50 bps, offsetting higher loan yields and keeping NIM steady around 2.55%.
- **Ashlesh / Shobhit:** *Why did the ECL estimate increase to Rs 12,000–15,000 crore, and how will ROA be protected?*  
  **Management:** Hardening yields require higher MTM provisions on HTM investments under ECL (adding Rs 2,000–3,000 crore). Capital ratios (CRAR 17.19%, CET1 12.80%) and high PCR (95.34%) allow absorption over 1–2 years without denting ROA below 0.80%–1.00%.
- **Sushil Choksey:** *What is the update on subsidiary monetization?*  
  **Management:** The bank plans to complete the sale of its stake in Canbank Factors during Q2/Q3, expecting a 4X payout on its original Rs 14 crore investment.
- **Jay Mundra:** *What is the exposure to drought-affected districts?*  
  **Management:** Total exposure in affected parts of Karnataka and Maharashtra is Rs 13,900 crore. SLBC dispensations, restructuring, and moratoriums will prevent major NPA translation.

### One-line takeaway
Canara Bank delivered record profit and NII while beating operational targets, anchored by strong broad-based loan growth, stable margins, and robust asset quality.
