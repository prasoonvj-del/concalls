# Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong credit growth, stable asset quality, and record PAT, with management indicating a likelihood of overshooting the upper end of their ROA guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Total deposits:** INR50,643 crores, up 29.2% YoY (CASA at 26%)
- **Gross loan book:** INR45,699 crores, up 32.1% YoY (12.4% YTD growth)
- **Total income:** INR2,450 crores
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** INR1,244 crores, up 35% YoY
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 8.4% (8.45% in Q2)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR365 crores (all-time high)
- **ROA:** 2.4%
- **ROE:** 19.7%
- **Gross NPA:** 2.1%
- **Net NPA:** 0.3%
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 85%
- **Credit cost:** 0.7% of ATA (H1 annualized)
- **Slippage ratio:** 1.6% of average gross loan book
- **CRAR:** 20.69% (up 33 bps QoQ)
- **Proposed QIB equity capital raise:** INR409 crores

### Highlights
- Crossed INR50,000 crores in deposits milestone.
- Affordable housing crossed INR10,000 crores mark (reaching INR10,056 crores).
- Secured business lines now form 52.1% of the loan book; target to reach 56% by end of FY27.
- Launched a brand campaign featuring Padma Shri Awardee Mr. R. Madhavan.
- Added 104 branches and 1,335 employees in H1 FY27.
- Proposed equity capital raise of INR409 crores from QIBs (IFC, Bajaj Life Insurance, HDFC Life Insurance, Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance) on a preferential basis, which will increase CRAR by ~1.2% and lead to accretion of INR0.8 per share.
- Enhanced funding mix by increasing securitization pool by INR500 crores in October.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they could overshoot the guided ROA range due to strong advance growth and lower opex-to-ATA.
- Micro banking growth guidance maintained at 12% to 14% for the full year.
- Full-year credit cost guided to remain within the guided range (90 to 100 bps / H1 run-rate similar).
- Opex-to-ATA ratio expected to end around 6.2% (saving 0.2% vs previous indication of 6.4%).
- NIM expected to moderate to ~8.35% in H2 due to a 25 bps repo rate hike impact (~10 bps).
- Remaining capital raise under the INR2,000 crores enabling resolution to be completed before July 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's GDP, gross capital formation, and high-frequency indicators remain healthy.
- RBI raised FY27 GDP forecast to 7.1%; repo rate increased by 25 bps with a shift to calibrated tightening.
- Deficient Southwest monsoon and El Nino conditions pose risks to rural demand; urban demand remains stable.
- Micro banking collection efficiency remains strong at 99.7% (Bucket X). Added 2 lakh new customers in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of funds maintained at around 6.9%, but management expects it to move upwards due to rate hikes.
- Operating expenses increased by 6% QoQ (employee expenses up 3% QoQ, other opex up 10%) due to brand campaign and branch expansion.
- Other income increased 17% QoQ.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 117 gold loan branches in H1 and plan to expand a similar number in H2. Total H1 branch addition is 104 branches.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Secured portion of advances is 52.1%; target to reach 56% by end of the financial year.
- Micro banking portfolio stands at INR22,037 crores (growing ~6% in H1). Affordable housing and micro mortgages combined crossed INR12,000 crores (up 38.1% YoY). MSME reached INR3,871 crores (up 51.3% YoY). Gold loan reached INR1,275 crores (up 209.8% YoY). Agri banking reached INR914 crores (up 79.2% YoY). 2-wheeler loans reached INR1,081 crores (up 64.8% YoY).
- Total write-offs for the quarter were INR59 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Renewed Middle East conflict and volatility in global energy/freight prices adding to inflationary pressures.
- Deficient Southwest monsoon and El Nino creating drought-like situations in parts of Karnataka and Maharashtra (though management noted direct eligible agri exposure under SLBC relief is minimal at INR15.1 crores in Karnataka and INR18 crores in Maharashtra, with >60% of MFI borrowers having alternate income sources).
- IRDAI consultation paper on insurance commissions watched closely, though it remains a draft.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shreepal Doshi (Equirus Securities) on margins and drought impact:** Asked about NIM trajectory and state-level stress in Karnataka/Maharashtra. Management answered that NIM will drop by ~10 bps to 8.35% in H2 due to repo rate hike, and drought impact is minimal as eligible SLBC exposure is minuscule and 60%+ borrowers have multiple income sources.
2. **Sarvesh Gupta (Maximal Capital) on microfinance growth vs industry slowdown:** Asked why MFI growth is calibrated. Management responded that 12% to 14% is the sweet spot, choosing locations carefully while maintaining healthy new customer acquisition.
3. **Rajiv Mehta (YES Securities) on capital raise timeline and opex/credit cost:** Asked about remaining capital raise and opex/credit cost expectations. Management clarified that the INR2,000 crore enabling resolution is valid till July 2027, opex-to-ATA will likely end lower at 6.2%, and credit cost will mirror H1 run rate.
4. **Ashlesh Sonje (Kotak Securities) on CASA pressures and ROA upgrade:** Asked if CASA can recover and if ROA guidance is upgraded. Management noted CASA may stay ~2% lower than 30%, but confirmed a high probability of a significant beat on the upper end of ROA.
5. **Shubham Karvande (JM Financial) on MD search timeline:** Asked about the status of the new MD appointment. Management stated the NRC is active and expects to complete the process in 3 to 4 months starting September 1.

### One-line takeaway
Ujjivan SFB delivered a record quarter with PAT of INR365 crores and 32% loan growth, prompting management to signal a likely positive beat on full-year ROA guidance despite macro margin headwinds.
