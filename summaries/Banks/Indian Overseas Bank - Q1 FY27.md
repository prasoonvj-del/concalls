# Indian Overseas Bank - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-24 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved all-time high quarterly net profit, robust NIM expansion, and consistent asset quality improvement.

### Key numbers
- **Net Profit:** Rs 1,659 crore (up 49.32% YoY from Rs 1,111 crore in Q1 FY25)
- **Operating Profit:** Rs 2,693 crore (up 14.21% YoY)
- **Business Mix:** Rs 6,98,325 crore (up 17.72% YoY)
- **Total Deposits:** Rs 3,76,193 crore (up 13.72% YoY)
- **Total Advances:** Rs 3,22,132 crore (up 22.75% YoY)
- **CASA Global:** Rs 1,54,415 crore; CASA Ratio Global at 41.05%, Domestic at 41.45%
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM) Global:** 3.37% (up 12 bps QoQ from 3.25%)
- **NIM Domestic:** 3.48% (up from 3.35% in Q4 FY26)
- **Gross NPA:** Rs 4,292 crore; GNPA ratio at 1.33% (down 64 bps YoY from 1.97%)
- **Net NPA:** Rs 588 crore; Net NPA ratio at 0.18% (down 14 bps YoY from 0.32%)
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 97.67% (vs 97.47% YoY)
- **Slippage Ratio:** 0.06% (vs 0.10% in Q1 FY25)
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR):** 19.36% (vs 11.50% regulatory requirement)
- **Return on Assets (RoA):** 1.41% (up 27 bps YoY)
- **Return on Equity (RoE):** 22.69% (up 369 bps YoY from 19%)
- **Book Value Per Share:** Rs 15.79 (vs Rs 12.41 in June 2025)
- **Credit Cost:** 0.14% for June quarter

### Highlights
- Accumulated losses have been reduced to nil through internal accruals of net profit and netting off by share premium.
- Total SMA stood at Rs 13,000 crore (4.05% of portfolio); SMA 0 at Rs 5,733 crore, SMA 1 at Rs 3,068 crore, and SMA 2 at Rs 4,246 crore.
- FCNR(B) deposits mobilized stand at $300 million; targeting $600 million–$650 million by September.
- Board approved raising up to Rs 5,000 crore via equity and Rs 1,000 crore via Tier 2 bonds.
- Received IFSCA license for GIFT City; internal budget to build a book of $500 million by the end of the financial year.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Credit/Loan Growth:** Minimum 13% to 14% growth targeted for the year.
- **Corporate Book Growth:** Expected to grow by 12% to 13% by the end of the year, supported by a Rs 14,000 crore sanctioned pipeline.
- **NIM:** Expected to be maintained in the range of 3.3% to 3.4%.
- **RoA:** Maintained above 1.20% internally; looking at around 1.46% by the end of the financial year.
- **Credit Cost:** Expected to be around 0.35% to 0.40% for the full year.
- **ECL Provisioning:** Total probable requirement estimated at Rs 3,000 crore; total provision created so far is Rs 2,150 crore (including Rs 400 crore added this quarter). Management does not intend to use the 4-year dispensation.
- **Capital Raising Timeline:** Planned in Q3 and Q4 in one or more tranches depending on market conditions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- No systemic stress observed in small borrowers or SME sectors due to the West Asian crisis.
- Demand for ECLGS remains strong; out of a total eligible universe of Rs 4,400 crore, Rs 2,600 crore has been disbursed, and the remaining 95%–100% is expected to be disbursed by August end or September first week.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Net Interest Income (NII) grew by 34.30% YoY.
- Cost of deposits declined due to a focus on CASA and a low bulk deposit ratio (6% to 7%). All term deposit repricing is complete.
- Yield on advances increased due to pricing adjustments; 54% of credit is linked to MCLR and 37% to RLLR.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Digital transactions account for ~96% of total transactions; customer onboarding is ~75% digital.
- GIFT City branch at international operations to commence physical existence in 2 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stands at Rs 29,256 crore, supported by Rs 200 crore DTA and ~Rs 300 crore MTM addition on the AFS book.
- Corporate loan book declined 10% QoQ due to exiting a ~Rs 10,000 crore low-yielding corporate account in April.
- CGTMSE coverage accounts for 15% to 16% of the total credit portfolio.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Looming West Asian crisis mentioned as a potential macroeconomic uncertainty, though no stress is currently visible on books.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ashok Ajmera (Ajcon Global) asked about the sustainability of PSLC income and tech written-off recoveries.** Management responded that these are routine activities occurring consistently over the last 8-9 quarters and form an integral part of non-interest income.
- **Ashlesh Sonje asked about the 10% QoQ decline in corporate advances and yield increases.** Management explained it was due to exiting a sub-7% yield Rs 10,000 crore corporate exposure, prioritizing margin protection over loss-making volume.
- **Aditya Mundra queried the difference between reported book value and calculated figures.** Management addressed guidance metrics, noting RoA will be driven by NIM improvements.
- **Aryan Rana asked about the timeline and execution method for the Rs 5,000 crore equity raise given the government's 92.44% stake.** Management stated plans to hit the market in Q3/Q4 in multiple tranches after statutory approvals.

### One-line takeaway
Indian Overseas Bank delivered an all-time high quarterly net profit of Rs 1,659 crore, driven by robust NII growth, strict margin protection, and pristine asset quality, while eliminating historical accumulated losses.
