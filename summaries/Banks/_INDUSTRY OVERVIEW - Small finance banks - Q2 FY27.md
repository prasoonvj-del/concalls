# Banks - Small finance banks: what managements are sayings (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the Small Finance Bank (SFB) sector reported a generally positive quarter characterized by robust deposit and loan growth, alongside a strategic pivot toward secured lending to de-risk balance sheets (notably ESAF, Jana, Utkarsh, Ujjivan, and Suryoday). Profitability metrics largely improved, with several banks posting strong PAT expansions (e.g., AU SFB up 53% YoY, Capital SFB up 31% YoY, Jana achieving its highest-ever PBT). However, headwinds such as tightening system liquidity, rising interest rates, and localized weather concerns surfaced across multiple calls. Utkarsh SFB stood out with a net loss of INR 70 crore due to normalized provisioning after previous quarter one-offs.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements agree that systemic credit demand remains healthy, with robust traction in MSMEs, affordable housing, and retail segments. 
* **Divergence:** Views diverge on rural and microfinance demand. While AU, Ujjivan, and Suryoday noted potential risks from deficient monsoons and drought conditions in parts of Karnataka and Maharashtra, AU, Ujjivan, and Capital reported minimal direct impact on their portfolios due to diversified income sources or strong irrigation infrastructure. Meanwhile, ESAF and Jana are actively calibrating or scaling back microfinance group loans to focus on secured assets.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Cost of Funds & Margins:** Tightening system liquidity and recent repo rate hikes (by 25 bps to 5.50%) have put pressure on funding costs. Ujjivan expects NIMs to moderate by ~10 bps in H2, and AU noted that rising rates may cap further cost-of-funds improvements. Conversely, Jana maintained a stable cost of funds at 7.4% with a NIM of 7.54%, and Capital SFB expects a NIM expansion of 7-12 bps by FY29.
* **Operating Efficiency:** Several banks reported improving operating leverage and cost-to-income ratios through tech automation and frugality (e.g., Jana, Capital, Utkarsh, and Suryoday).

## Capex & capacity
Expansion and digital transformation investments continued across the board:
* **AU SFB:** Added 45 deposit branches and 100+ asset centers.
* **Capital SFB:** Operating 222 branches, targeting >300 by FY29.
* **ESAF:** Successfully went live with its "StratoNeXt" digital transformation (Oracle FLEXCUBE) and added 10 banking outlets.
* **Fino Payments Bank:** Incurring SFB transition capex of Rs 120-150 crore, targeting a Q1 FY28 go-live.
* **Jana SFB:** Opened 40 of 78 planned new branches for the year.
* **Suryoday SFB:** Plans to open ~50 branches over the next 4-5 months (10 already opened).
* **Ujjivan SFB:** Added 104 branches and 117 gold loan branches in H1.
* **Utkarsh SFB:** Halting branch expansion to focus on productivity across its existing 1,100+ branches.

## What changed since last quarter
* **AU Small Finance Bank:** Tone shifted from a focus on high competition to tightening system liquidity and rising interest rates; credit rating outlook upgraded to Positive by ICRA.
* **Utkarsh Small Finance Bank:** Tone shifted from "green shoots" (Q1) to confirming that "business momentum is recovering" and it is "ready to pick pace."

## What to watch next quarter
* Reopening and government notification of the CGFMU guarantee portal for fresh microfinance disbursements (noted by Jana, Suryoday, and Utkarsh).
* The trajectory of NIMs and cost of funds following recent repo rate hikes (Ujjivan, AU).
* Post-IT migration execution and scaling for ESAF (StratoNeXt) and Fino (SFB transition readiness toward Q1 FY28).
* Potential lagged asset quality impacts from drought conditions in select rural pockets of Karnataka and Maharashtra (Suryoday, Ujjivan).

## Company one-liners
* **AU Small Finance Bank Ltd:** AU Small Finance Bank delivered a robust Q2 with 53% YoY PAT growth and improved return ratios, successfully leveraging technology and geographic expansion to drive granular deposit growth despite a tightening liquidity environment.
* **Capital Small Finance Bank Ltd:** Capital Small Finance Bank delivered robust growth and improved asset quality, maintaining a disciplined, secured-lending focus while executing its "Vision 2029" roadmap toward universal banking.
* **ESAF Small Finance Bank Ltd:** Strong profitability and asset quality improvement driven by a strategic pivot to secured lending and successful IT transformation, despite temporary gold loan SMA volatility.
* **Fino Payments Bank Ltd:** Fino Payments Bank is progressing on its Q1 FY28 SFB transition roadmap, backed by strong CASA growth, a low cost of funds advantage, and a rapidly scaling loan referral business.
* **Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd:** Jana Small Finance Bank posted its highest-ever PBT in Q2 FY27, matching H1 PAT with FY26's full-year PAT, prompting management to upgrade its full-year PAT growth guidance to 100%.
* **Suryoday Small Finance Bank Ltd:** Suryoday Small Finance Bank delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 34.6% advance growth, a sharp drop in Gross NPAs to 2.9% via CGFMU claims and write-offs, and stable profitability, while maintaining a cautious stance on drought-prone regions and awaiting CGFMU scheme renewal for new disbursements.
* **Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Ltd:** Ujjivan SFB delivered a record quarter with PAT of INR 365 crores and 32% loan growth, prompting management to signal a likely positive beat on full-year ROA guidance despite macro margin headwinds.
* **Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Ltd:** Utkarsh SFB reported a net loss of INR 70 crore in Q2 FY27, with management emphasizing a recovery in business momentum, improved operating profitability, and a continued shift toward secured lending.

*Based on: AU Small Finance Bank Ltd, Capital Small Finance Bank Ltd, ESAF Small Finance Bank Ltd, Fino Payments Bank Ltd, Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd, Suryoday Small Finance Bank Ltd, Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Ltd, Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Ltd*
