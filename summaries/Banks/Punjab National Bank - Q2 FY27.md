# Punjab National Bank - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained strong business momentum, improved margins, robust asset quality, and expanding digital capabilities.

### Key numbers
- Gross global business: Rs 31.18 lakh crore (up 11.9% YoY)
- Advances: Rs 13.41 lakh crore (up 14.7% YoY; core advances up 17.4% YoY excluding IBPC reduction of Rs 23,700 crore)
- Global deposits: Rs 17.77 lakh crore (up 9.9% YoY)
- Domestic NIM: 2.74% (up 10 bps QoQ from 2.64%)
- Global NIM: 2.58% (up from 2.50%)
- Net Interest Income (NII): Rs 11,601 crore (up 10.8% YoY, 7.4% QoQ)
- Operating profit: Rs 7,532 crore
- Core operating profit: up 29.4% YoY
- Net profit: Rs 5,532 crore (up 12.8% YoY)
- Return on Assets (RoA): 1.05%
- Return on Equity (RoE): 17.32%
- Cost-to-income ratio: 50.45% (vs 51.20% YoY)
- Gross NPA: 2.55%
- Net NPA: 0.25%
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 97.32%
- Fresh slippages (Q2): Rs 2,395 crore; Slippage ratio at 0.77%
- Recoveries (Q2): Rs 2,805 crore (1.17x of slippages)
- Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR): 17.95% (CET1 at 14.44%, Tier 1 at 15.89%, Tier 2 at 2.06%)
- CD Ratio: 75.5%
- LCR: 133%
- FCNR (B) raised: USD 2.7 billion

### Highlights
- Opened 162 branches out of 250 targeted for FY27 (119 in Southern and Western regions).
- Retail advances (ex-IBPC) grew 16.3%, MSME 19.8%, and priority sector agriculture 18.1% YoY.
- Raised USD 2.7 billion via FCNR campaign, utilized to shed high-cost bulk deposits and CDs (CDs brought down from ~Rs 60,000 crore to ~Rs 40,000 crore).
- Digital sanctions in H1 FY27 crossed Rs 41,500 crore; over 95% of customer transactions are digital (up 31% YoY).
- Deployed quantum-safe encryption in 246 customer-facing applications and established a Quantum Finance Innovation Hub in Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains firmly on track to deliver its FY26-27 guidance across asset quality (GNPA/NNPA) and PCR (>96%).
- Expects domestic NIM to further improve by 7 to 9 bps in the next quarter, aided by the recent 25 bps repo rate hike acting immediately on the 56% repo-linked loan book.
- Full-year recovery targeted between Rs 12,000 crore to Rs 13,000 crore.
- Expected steady-state credit cost around 0.25% to 0.30% (factoring ~15 bps ECL impact).
- RoA expected to stay above 1.04%–1.05%, and RoE around 15% to 17%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted robust Indian GDP growth expectations of 7.1% for FY26-27, driving credit growth of 13%–14% nationwide.
- Strong corporate sanction pipeline of Rs 1.58 lakh crore, following Rs 4 lakh crore sanctioned in corporate credit lines in FY26 and >Rs 2.5 lakh crore in H1 FY27.
- Emerging demand noted in renewable energy, roads, data centers, food processing, transmission, and real estate.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Domestic cost of deposit reduced to 4.97% (from 5.18% in September 2025).
- Domestic cost of funds down to 4.34% (from 4.57% YoY).
- Bulk deposits and CDs now constitute around 17% of total deposits (down from 19%).
- Employee retirement-related provisions stood at Rs 340 crore for the quarter (vs Rs 490 crore in Q1 FY27).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Targeted 250 new branches in FY27, with 162 already opened.
- Expanding non-interest income drivers: wealth management services launching in January, revamped digital trade finance modules, credit cards (11 million cards), supply chain finance, and cash management services.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total floating provisions stand at Rs 2,560 crore after adding an additional Rs 125 crore in Q2.
- Additional standard asset provision of Rs 1,150 crore kept for ECL migration.
- Total combined SMA (0, 1, and 2) portfolio at an all-time low of 2.49% of the overall loan book.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management acknowledged potential drought/stress monitoring in agriculture (specifically noting Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh), though no current stress is visible in SMA numbers.
- Cumulative AFS reserves are negative by Rs 1,286 crore due to G-Sec yield hardening.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kunal Shah (Citi) asked about FCNR fund utilization and NIM trajectory.** Management replied the USD 2.7 billion was fully utilized to replace high-cost bulk deposits and CDs, bringing down the CD/bulk share to ~17%, and guiding for an additional 7–9 bps NIM expansion next quarter.
2. **Sameer Bhise (Morepen/Institutional) asked about credit cost sustainability and ECL impact.** Management stated that estimated ECL impact on the loan book is around Rs 8,000 crore (lower due to strong underwriting at 0.42% NPA on recent books), and steady-state credit cost will remain at 0.25%–0.30%.
3. **Jayant Kharote (Axis Capital) questioned structural SA deposit growth and CASA headwinds.** Management clarified that institutional SB deposits are declining due to government funds moving to RBI (just-in-time), but individual SB accounts are growing at over 8.5% YoY.
4. **Bhavik Shah (Investor) asked about LCR and self-funding of FCNR.** Management stated LCR is 133% and FCNR was 30% self-funded with 70% leveraged.
5. **Jai Prakash Mundhra (ICICI Securities) asked for specific SMA breakdowns and AFS reserves.** Management provided exact absolute and percentage breakdowns for SMA-0 (1.26%), SMA-1 (0.56%), and SMA-2 (0.67%), and noted cumulative AFS reserve loss of Rs 1,286 crore.
6. **Saket Kapoor (Analyst) asked about treasury income accounting and provisions.** Management (CFO) clarified that treasury income contribution was Rs 753 crore and explained the net P&L provision of Rs 97 crore after accounting for investment provision write-backs.

### One-line takeaway
Punjab National Bank delivered a robust Q2 FY27 marked by a 10 bps sequential NIM expansion to 2.74%, a 12.8% YoY net profit increase to Rs 5,532 crore, and disciplined balance sheet rebalancing away from high-cost bulk funds.
