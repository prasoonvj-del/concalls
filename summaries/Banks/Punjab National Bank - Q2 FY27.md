# Punjab National Bank - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported margin expansion, sustained asset quality improvements, strong credit growth across segments, and robust profitability.

### Key numbers
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 5,532 crore (up 12.8% YoY).
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 11,601 crore (up 10.8% YoY, up 7.4% QoQ).
- **Global NIM:** 2.58% (up 8 bps QoQ from 2.50%).
- **Domestic NIM:** 2.74% (up 10 bps QoQ from 2.64%).
- **Operating Profit:** Rs 7,532 crore.
- **Core Operating Profit (ex-recoveries from written-off accounts and treasury gains):** Rs 5,971 crore (up 29.4% YoY, up 8% QoQ).
- **Gross Global Business:** Rs 31.18 lakh crore (up 11.9% YoY).
- **Gross Advances:** Rs 13.41 lakh crore (up 14.7% YoY); core advances excluding IBPC grew 17.4% YoY.
- **Global Deposits:** Rs 17.77 lakh crore (up 9.9% YoY).
- **Gross NPA (GNPA) Ratio:** 2.55% (down 90 bps YoY from 3.45%).
- **Net NPA (NNPA) Ratio:** 0.25% (down 11 bps YoY from 0.36%).
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 97.32%.
- **Credit Cost:** 0.25%.
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 50.45% (improved from 51.20% in Q2 FY26).
- **Return on Assets (RoA):** 1.05%.
- **Return on Equity (RoE):** 17.32%.
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR):** 17.95% (CET1 at 14.44%, Tier 1 at 15.89%).

### Highlights
- Core RAM portfolio advances (excluding IBPC) registered broad-based expansion YoY: Retail grew 16.3%, MSME grew 19.8%, and Agriculture grew 18.1%.
- Mobilized USD 2.7 billion in FCNR deposits, fully utilizing the funds to shed high-cost bulk deposits and Certificates of Deposit (CDs), bringing CDs down from Rs 60,000 crore to Rs 40,000 crore.
- Retail term deposits and individual savings account balances grew 8.1% YoY, while institutional savings deposits de-grew 3.84% YoY due to government fund migration.
- Fresh slippages for the quarter were contained at Rs 2,395 crore (slippage ratio of 0.77%), with overall recoveries standing at Rs 2,805 crore (1.17x of slippages).
- Made an additional prudential floating provision of Rs 125 crore in Q2 (total floating provision at Rs 2,560 crore) and created a standard asset provision of Rs 1,150 crore.
- Digital sanctions crossed Rs 41,500 crore in H1 FY27; digital transactions reached over 95% of total transactions.

### Guidance & outlook
- **NIM:** Management expects a minimum 7 to 9 bps further expansion in NIM in Q3 FY27, aided by repricing from the 25 bps repo rate hike.
- **Slippages & Credit Cost:** Management maintained full-year slippage ratio guidance below 0.9% and expects credit cost to remain between 0.25% and 0.30% (including ECL transition).
- **PCR:** Management expects PCR to be maintained above 96%.
- **Recoveries:** Management expects full-year recoveries between Rs 12,000 crore and Rs 13,000 crore (~Rs 3,500 crore targeted for Q3 FY27).
- **Return Ratios:** Management expects to maintain RoA above 1.04%–1.05% and RoE between 15% and 17%.
- **ECL Impact:** Expected loan book ECL provision requirement stands at ~Rs 8,000 crore, of which existing provisions leave an incremental requirement of ~Rs 5,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted strong underlying momentum in the Indian economy, supported by domestic credit demand, real GDP growth of 7.8% in Q1 FY27, and revised annual GDP estimates of 7.1%.
- Corporate credit demand remains healthy; corporate credit pipeline stands at Rs 1.58 lakh crore following sanctions of over Rs 2.5 lakh crore in H1 FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Domestic cost of deposits declined to 4.97% (from 5.02% in Q1 FY27 and 5.18% in Q2 FY26); global cost of deposits dropped to 4.96%.
- Domestic cost of funds stood at 4.34%.
- Combined bulk deposits and CDs represent ~17% of total deposits (down from ~19% in Q1 FY27).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 162 branches out of a target of 250 for FY27 (119 opened in Southern and Western regions).
- Expanding wealth management services (launching January) and revamping the trade finance digital module.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Credit-to-Deposit (CD) ratio stood at 75.5%; Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) was 133%.
- Combined SMA-0, SMA-1, and SMA-2 balances stood at 2.49% of total loans (Rs 33,410 crore).
- Externally rated corporate advances above Rs 25 crore: >87.6% rated A and above; >52.4% rated AAA.
- Loans underwritten/disbursed between July 1, 2020, and September 30, 2026, represent 71.6% of outstanding book, with an NPA ratio of 0.42%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Agriculture segment slippages are being closely monitored due to localized stress/drought conditions in select states like Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh.
- High-base recovery from write-offs is expected to moderate over the next 2–3 years as gross slippages decrease.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kunal Shah (Citi):** *How were FCNR funds utilized, and what is the margin impact from the repo hike?*  
  **Management:** USD 2.7 billion raised via FCNR was fully used to replace high-cost bulk deposits and CDs (CDs reduced from Rs 60,000 crore to Rs 40,000 crore). The repo rate hike will reprice 56% of the repo-linked book immediately, driving a 7–9 bps NIM expansion in Q3 FY27.
- **Sameer Bhise:** *What is the long-term ROE and credit cost trajectory post-ECL implementation?*  
  **Management:** Total loan book ECL impact is estimated at ~Rs 8,000 crore, with ~Rs 5,000 crore pending. With fresh underwriting NPAs at 0.42%, normalized credit cost will remain low at 0.25%–0.30%, sustaining RoA above 1.04%–1.05% and RoE at 15%–17%.
- **Jayant Kharote (Axis Capital):** *What is the breakdown of Savings Accounts (SA) and management's stance on deposit rates?*  
  **Management:** Individual SA grew 8.5% YoY to Rs 4,82,700 crore, while Institutional SA de-grew 3.84% to Rs 60,100 crore due to government just-in-time account shifts. Bank has no immediate plans to raise deposit rates as current liquidity and a 75.5% CD ratio provide adequate headroom.
- **Jai Prakash Mundhra (ICICI Securities):** *What is the traction in high-yield segments like Gold Loans?*  
  **Management:** Gold loans grew 97%–98% YoY to Rs 36,000 crore (74% agriculture). Credit card base grew to 11 lakh cards (vs 6 lakh in March 2025).
- **Sushil Choksey:** *What is the corporate sanction pipeline and target RAM-to-Corporate mix?*  
  **Management:** Disbursable corporate sanction pipeline stands at Rs 1.58 lakh crore. Target portfolio mix by year-end is 58%–59% RAM and 41%–42% Corporate, with low-yielding corporate loans continuously being replaced by higher-yielding corporate/RAM assets.

### One-line takeaway
Punjab National Bank delivered robust operational performance in Q2 FY27 marked by 10 bps domestic NIM expansion, strong balance sheet rebalancing, multi-year asset quality highs, and comfortable guidance for 15%–17% RoE.
