# Fino Payments Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady progress on SFB transition, recovery in transaction and B2B alliance volumes, and strong growth in loan referrals and CASA deposits.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 333.6 crore (up 9% QoQ, down 17% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue at ~Rs 640 crore (down 25% YoY).
- **Net Revenue:** Rs 127.1 crore (down 14% YoY, up 3% QoQ); H1 FY27 net revenue margin at 40.3% vs 35.2% YoY.
- **Net Revenue Margin:** 38.1% in Q2 (lower by 450 bps QoQ, higher by 100 bps YoY).
- **Throughput:** Rs 1.15 lakh crore (up 4% QoQ).
- **Operating Costs:** Up 5.8% YoY and 4.5% QoQ; H1 operating cost at ~Rs 180 crore (up 1.6% YoY).
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 27.6% in Q2 vs 28.7% in Q1.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 35.1 crore (EBITDA margin: 10.5%); Q1 FY27 EBITDA was Rs 43.1 crore, Q2 FY26 EBITDA was Rs 61.6 crore.
- **CASA / Deposits:** Total CASA accounts at 1.91 crore (8.2 lakh new accounts opened in Q2); Average total deposits at Rs 2,794 crore (up 12% YoY). CASA renewal income at a record Rs 74.6 crore.
- **Cost of Funds:** 1.4% for Q2 FY27; expected around 4% post-SFB conversion (250-300 bps advantage over traditional SFBs).
- **Digitally Active Customers:** 64.9 lakh in September 2026; 87.9 lakh for Q2 FY27.
- **CMS Throughput:** Rs 20,511 crore (up 13% QoQ, up 12% YoY).
- **Loan Referral Disbursals:** Rs 762 crore in Q2 (up 286% YoY); H1 FY27 disbursals at Rs 1,390 crore (surpassing full-year FY26 level of Rs 1,285 crore).
- **Capex Guidance:** Anticipated Rs 120 crore to Rs 150 crore over 1 year to 15 months for SFB transition.
- **First-Year SFB Disbursal/AUM Target:** ~Rs 2,000 crore disbursals in the first 12 months; targeting an Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 10,000 crore book over 3 to 4 years.

### Highlights
- Making steady and visible progress on Small Finance Bank (SFB) readiness across technology, people, and governance with support from PwC; on track to meet RBI stipulated conditions within the prescribed timeline (go-live target Q1 FY28).
- Strengthened leadership team with four senior executives for branch banking, liabilities, credit risk, and MSME lending.
- Launched a 'daily deposit plan' for merchants to deposit fixed daily amounts and a 'value savings account' via B2B2C strategy.
- Launched 'Cash@UPI' for cardless/Aadhaar-free cash withdrawals via QR codes at merchant points and a 3-party cash deposit product via AEPS with NPCI.
- B2B and Alliance business recovered in Q2 after a reduction in Q1 due to skepticism surrounding an event. UPI B2B P2M segment relaunch on track for Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management treats FY27 as a transition year focused on sustainable long-term vision, disciplined execution, and investments for growth.
- Refrained from giving medium-term percentage guidance for revenue growth, EBITDA margins, and ROA for the current financial year.
- Guided for ~Rs 2,000 crore of disbursals in the first 12 months of SFB operations, scaling to an Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 10,000 crore book over 3-4 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CMS throughput growth reflects renewed confidence of alliances, NBFCs, fintechs, and e-commerce partners.
- Intense pricing competition continues to weigh on take rates in the B2B CMS segment.
- Gold loan asset quality stress reported in media articles is not visible in Fino's referral demographics or partner underwriting data.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- CASA contributes Rs 174 crore or 52% of total revenue.
- Cost of funds maintained at a structural advantage of 1.4% currently, expected to be ~4% post-SFB.
- Operating costs maintained through frugality while investing in technology and talent.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned SFB branch footprint addition: ~40 liability branches and 25-30 asset centers in the first year of operations (existing 125 branches/contact service points to be converted).
- Capex of Rs 120 crore to Rs 150 crore envisaged over 12-15 months, primarily focused on technology systems (LOS, LMS, collateral management, enterprise NPA).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth and capital adequacy remain well above regulatory requirements for the proposed SFB.
- 90% of deposit balances are sticky and core savings account balances.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Intense pricing competition in B2B/CMS alliances impacting take rates.
- Short-term profitability may be impacted by ongoing investments in people, process, and technology for SFB transition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankit Kanodia (Zen Nivesh) on SFB timeline confidence:** Management reiterated they are tracking the Q1 FY28 go-live schedule on Slide 12 but cannot comment on RBI's internal review timelines. (Not dodged)
2. **Ankit Kanodia on gold loan stress:** Management stated that as a referrer, they do not underwrite directly, but partner data and demographics show no signs of stress. (Not dodged)
3. **Manish Kumar Jain (Prudent Securities) on branch expansion status:** Management clarified locations and districts are identified, branch plans will be finalized by Q4 before applying to RBI, alongside converting 125 existing branches/contact points. (Not dodged)
4. **Harsh (Individual Investor) on deposit stickiness for lending:** Management explained that 99% of deposits are savings accounts, and behavioral data shows 90% of balances remain sticky/core. (Not dodged)
5. **Ankit Kanodia on conservative Rs 2,000 cr disbursal target vs strong referral run-rate:** Management clarified that year one is about testing guardrails and processes built, keeping targets realistic before scaling to Rs 8,000-10,000 crore over 3-4 years. (Not dodged)

### One-line takeaway
Fino Payments Bank is progressing on its Q1 FY28 SFB transition roadmap, backed by strong CASA growth, a low cost of funds advantage, and a rapidly scaling loan referral business.
