# Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted recovering business momentum, reduced fresh stress, and improving operating profitability, despite a reported net loss due to the absence of one-off provisions/credits seen in the previous quarter.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 25% to 30% loan book growth for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "green shoots" (Q1) to "business momentum is recovering" and "ready to pick pace."
- **New topics or risks:** New Core Banking System (CBS) launched in Q2. The CGFMU guarantee window is currently closed for fresh loans (awaiting government approval).
- **Last quarter's promises:** The bank successfully raised INR 300 crore of the planned INR 500 crore Tier 2 NCDs in September 2026 and redeemed INR 195 crore of Tier 2 NCDs in August 2026. The reverse merger of Utkarsh CoreInvest Limited is still in progress (NCLT hearing scheduled for Nov 19, 2026).

### Key numbers
- **PPoP:** INR 85 crore (vs. loss of INR 3 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Net Loss:** INR 70 crore (H1 FY27 loss: INR 104 crore).
- **Gross Loan Portfolio Growth:** 7.5% YoY (2.3% sequentially).
- **Disbursements Growth (H1 FY27):** 52% YoY (JLG +2%, non-JLG +94%).
- **GNPA:** 5.9% (as of September 2026).
- **Credit Cost (H1 FY27):** 2.8% (down from 9.1% in H1 FY26).
- **Total Deposits Growth:** 7% YoY.
- **CASA Ratio:** 21.4% (as of September 2026).
- **Cost of Funds (H1 FY27):** 7.4% (down ~50 bps YoY).

### Highlights
- JLG portfolio reduced to 24% of gross loan portfolio (26% including BC JLG).
- Secured lending increased to 52% of gross loan portfolio (from 47% a year ago).
- Micro Banking Business Loan (MBBL) grew 146% YoY and 15% sequentially.
- CGFMU coverage: ~80% of microfinance book (including H1 FY27 disbursements).
- Employee headcount rationalized by ~1,100 YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Loan Book Growth:** 25% to 30% YoY for FY27.
- **Secured Lending Target:** ~55% of portfolio.
- **NIM Target:** ~8%.
- **ROE Target:** ~15% for FY28; aiming for 2-digit ROE by exit of FY27.
- **Capital:** No immediate equity raise planned; looking to raise remaining INR 200 crore Tier 2 capital this quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management stated business momentum is recovering and ready to pick pace.
- Drought and external factors had temporary impacts on collections in specific geographies (e.g., Bihar) but did not significantly impact the overall NPA trajectory.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost-to-income ratio improved to 84% (from 110% in Q3 FY26); targeting 70-75% for FY27 and ~60-65% for FY28.
- Funding costs are declining due to deposit repricing and improved liability mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No branch expansion planned; focus remains on productivity of existing 1,100+ branches.
- Headcount rationalization focused on non-business departments and middle management.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Surplus liquidity: ~INR 2,540 crore.
- LCR: 177%.
- CD Ratio: 81%.
- CRAR: 17%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Fresh slippages in Q2 FY27 were INR 239 crore.
- Legacy stress in secured retail portfolios (CV/CE, MSME, WSL) remains a focus; recovery processes (SARFAESI) take time.
- CGFMU window currently closed for fresh guarantees.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raj (Individual Investor):** Asked about the provision increase. *Management:* Explained that Q1 had one-off credits (CGFMU/ARC) that made Q1 look artificially low; Q2 is a normalized figure.
- **Sagar Shah (Spark PMS):** Asked about sequential slippage increase. *Management:* Attributed it to legacy book maturity and confirmed it is under control.
- **Vinay Nadkarni (Hathway Investments):** Questioned low branch throughput. *Management:* Cited past challenges and focus on productivity/technology to drive growth.
- **Ashlesh Soni (Kotak Securities):** Asked about CV slippages. *Management:* Stated they are from the legacy book and flow rates are improving.

### One-line takeaway
Utkarsh SFB reported a net loss of INR 70 crore in Q2 FY27, with management emphasizing a recovery in business momentum, improved operating profitability, and a continued shift toward secured lending.
