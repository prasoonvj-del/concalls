# CSB Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Banks*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy sequential growth in profitability, core net interest income, and asset quality metrics.

### Key numbers
- **Net Profit:** Rs. 183 Crore (up 22% QoQ, 14% YoY)
- **Operating Profit:** Rs. 276 Crore (up 10% QoQ)
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs. 510 Crore (up 7% QoQ, 20% YoY)
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 3.81% (vs. 3.66% in Q1 FY2027 and 3.81% in Q2 FY2026)
- **ROA:** 1.29% (vs. 1.09% in Q1 FY2027)
- **ROE:** 14.74% (vs. 12.71% in Q1 FY2027)
- **EPS:** Rs. 42 (vs. Rs. 35 in Q1 FY2027)
- **Cost to Income Ratio:** 64.8%
- **Deposits Growth:** 18% YoY
- **CASA Ratio:** 18.96%
- **Credit-Deposit (CD) Ratio:** 90.39%
- **Average LCR:** 127%
- **NSFR:** 124%
- **Gross Advances Growth:** 22% YoY
- **Yield on Advances:** 10.92% (vs. 10.65% in Q1 FY2027)
- **Gross NPA (GNPA):** 1.58%
- **Net NPA (NNPA):** 0.33%
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 87.65% with PWO, 79.19% without PWO
- **Credit Cost:** 24 bps
- **CRAR:** 19.55% (Tier 1: 18.54%)
- **Book Value per Share:** Rs. 290
- **Branch Network:** 872 branches and 844 ATMs

### Highlights
- Core fee income grew 27% YoY, while overall fee income saw a degrowth due to market-linked non-core lines (treasury/PSLC).
- Gold loans and corporate segments registered robust YoY growth of 36% and 44% respectively.
- Fully provided vintage book on credit cards and unsecured segments was written off with no impact on credit costs; recoveries remained strong.
- Technology transformation (including core system upgrade, ServiceNow, transaction banking, etc.) is complete and starting to yield benefits.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to grow 30% to 50% faster than the system.
- FY2028 onwards, CASA is expected to grow faster than term deposits, raising the CASA ratio.
- Gold loan mix is targeted to come down to around 50% by the end of this year and further down to 32% by 2030 to create a balanced portfolio.
- ROA target: Between 1.30% and 1.5% for this year; 1.5% to 1.8% next year (aiming closer to 1.5%).
- NIM target: Retain around 3.75% to 3.8% levels.
- Cost of funds target: Aiming to reduce from 6.55% to around 6% next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian economy remains resilient and one of the fastest-growing major economies, supported by robust domestic demand and government infrastructure spending, despite rising crude prices and global uncertainties.
- SME portfolio issues have been largely corrected; growth in SME is expected to pick up from the current quarter.
- Retail asset strategy focuses on secured/income-generating, collateral-backed products; unsecured lending remains calibrated.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other opex increased due to heavy investments in technology, distribution, and infrastructure, alongside a 17% YoY increase in staff cost.
- Cost of funds stands at 6.55%, which management plans to lower via current account focus and mix optimization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Distribution footprint expanded to 872 branches and 844 ATMs, with increased focus on metro and urban markets in North, West, South, and partly East.
- Continued investments in technology, digital capabilities, and product innovation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Wholesale banking is balanced across corporate banking, mid-market, and FIPS (financial institutions).
- Loan against security (primarily loan against gold for repledger businesses) has been effectively stopped and is near zero.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential risks from rising crude oil prices, Gulf conflicts, and second-order impacts of El Niño.
- Short-term challenges expected from draft IRDAI consultation paper on corporate agency fee income, though management believes impact will be minimal (0.5% to 1%).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Akshat Agrawal (Bang Institutional Equities) on opex spike:** Management explained the 11% QoQ opex increase is driven by technology investments, distribution expansion, and infrastructure.
- **Parth Gutka (Capital) on yield on advances:** Management attributed the improvement to better asset quality, careful yield management across wholesale and SME, and strong gold yields.
- **Rishabh Jogani on elevated write-offs vs. credit costs:** Management clarified that fully provided unsecured/credit card vintage books were written off with zero impact on credit costs (credit cost at 24 bps).
- **Shivaji Thapliyal (Yes Securities) on balance sheet growth & retail deposits:** Management stated balance sheet growth will sustain organically, with retail growth following the build-up of the liability franchise and CASA growth from FY2028.
- **Sameer Baisiwala (Sakman Capital) on ROA trajectory:** Management guided for an ROA of 1.30% to 1.5% this year and 1.5% to 1.8% next year.

### One-line takeaway
CSB Bank delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 14% YoY net profit growth, stable asset quality (GNPA at 1.58%), and improved margins, successfully transitioning into its scale phase.
