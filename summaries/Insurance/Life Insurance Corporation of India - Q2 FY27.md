# Life Insurance Corporation of India - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - driven by 21.52% YoY growth in PAT, VNB margin expansion to 22.3%, and strong growth in non-par and protection segments.

### Highlights
- Launched 5 new non-par individual products in H1 FY27 and 3 more on October 19, 2026, bringing the total suite to 63 products.
- Bima Sakhi initiative: 3.04 lakh women appointed, covering 66% of gram panchayats (1,60,928 out of 2,44,524), selling 10.64 lakh policies and generating Rs 1,548.80 crore in new business premium.
- Digital ANANDA app completed 12,40,676 policies (+41.23% YoY); MyLIC app reached 98,44,421 unique logins with a 4.7 rating; Super Sales Saathi app adopted by 5,38,250 agents and 16,175 development officers with a 4.8 rating.
- Note on Record: While Q2 standalone net profit grew 20% YoY to Rs 12,076 crore, management highlighted strong H1 cumulative performance.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a positive outlook on VNB margins for FY 2027-28, driven by optimized product mix (higher non-par and protection) and expense rationalization.
- Final decisions on capital distribution/deployment will be made once Risk-Based Capital (RBC) and IFRS reporting guidelines are rolled out.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management remains optimistic about IRDAI's proposed distribution sector regulation consultation paper (September 2026), believing caps on distribution expenses place LIC at an advantageous position due to its controlled overall expense ratio.
- ULIP growth was muted across the industry due to market conditions, but management expects a rebound alongside a focus on higher ticket sizes via monthly eNACH.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Overall expense ratio improved by 87 bps YoY to 10.41%.
- VNB margin expanded by 470 bps YoY to 22.3%, aided by business mix (non-par savings up 35% on APE basis, term business growth) and assumption changes (RFR movements), offset partly by expense realignments (-1.4%). Q1 to Q2 margin dipped slightly from 22.9% to 22.3% due to a ~40 bps RFR decrease.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Office rationalization and process re-engineering are ongoing; optimization benefits expected in coming quarters.
- Bancassurance partnerships expanded (added Yes Bank and RBL Bank); dependence on IDBI Bank moderated to ~20% of total bank-driven business and ~30% of total bancassurance.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Solvency ratio robust at 2.35.
- Hedging program for non-par products continues, covering ~80% of premium with maturities hedged up to 8 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Persistency metrics remain a mixed bag: policy persistency improved in 13th, 37th, and 49th month cohorts, but premium persistency lagged due to a higher concentration of persistent policies in lower sum assured/premium cohorts.
- Yield curve/risk-free rate (RFR) fluctuations negatively impacted Q-o-Q VNB margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Avinash Singh (Emkay Global) on distribution reforms and product competitiveness:** Management stated that product pricing depends on total expenses, and LIC's overall expense ratio keeps it at an advantageous position even if distribution caps are applied.
2. **Nischint Chawathe (Kotak Securities) on EV walk negatives:** Management clarified that the negative variance in IEV since March was primarily mark-to-market (MTM), while core business operations contributed positively.
3. **Swarnabha Mukherjee (360 ONE Capital) on APE vs. WRP divergence and ULIP impact:** Management explained that APE includes a 12x multiplier for monthly modes, and the de-growth in monthly ULIP eNACH (ticket size rationalization) created the delta between APE and WRP growth.
4. **Mohit Mangal (Centrum) on annuity IRR revisions:** Management confirmed no rate or IRR revisions were made for annuity products; growth will be driven through agent training and retirement planning outreach.
5. **Shobhit Sharma (Elara Securities) on bancassurance strategy post-consultation paper:** Management noted openness to expanding partnerships beyond existing banks while leveraging existing ties, expecting higher traction under proposed commission caps.
6. **Dipanjan Ghosh (Goldman Sachs) on FY28 margin levers:** Management reiterated that product mix optimization (scaling non-par and protection) and operational/expense efficiencies will support margins in FY28 despite RFR volatility.

### One-line takeaway
LIC delivered a strong H1 FY27 anchored by a 21.52% jump in PAT, a 470 bps expansion in VNB margins to 22.3%, and robust growth in non-par and protection segments despite muted ULIP performance.
