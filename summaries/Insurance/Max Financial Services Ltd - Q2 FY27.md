# Max Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in navigating regulatory changes, expects H2 growth acceleration, and highlighted strong VNB margin expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Individual Adjusted First Year Premium (APE):** Grew 9% in Q2 and 12% in H1.
*   **Total APE:** Grew 8% in Q2 and 11% in H1.
*   **VNB:** INR 1,180 crore in H1 (up 21% YoY).
*   **VNB Margin:** 25.4% in H1; 27% in Q2.
*   **MFSL Revenue (excl. investment income):** INR 17,484 crore (up 16%).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** INR 219 crore (up 137%).
*   **Gross Written Premium (GWP):** INR 18,066 crore in H1 (up 17%).
*   **Renewal Business:** INR 11,247 crore (up 18%).
*   **Embedded Value:** INR 31,437 crore (up 17% YoY).
*   **AUM:** INR 2.06 lakh crore (up 11% YoY).
*   **Policyholder Opex Ratio:** 13.7% (improved 174 bps YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Channel Performance:** Proprietary channels grew 9% in Q2; E-com grew 29%. Partnership channels grew 8% in Q2, with Axis Bank growing 4%.
*   **Product Growth:** Annuity business grew 130% in Q2; Pure Retail Protection grew 31%; Group credit life grew 41%.
*   **Product Innovation:** Launched "Smart RISE" (variable annuity), which contributes ~8% of total APE.
*   **Digital:** AI-enabled models delivered nearly INR 1,000 crore of risk avoidance.
*   **Solvency:** Ratio stands at 197%.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects APE growth to accelerate in H2.
*   **Margins:** Management maintains a VNB margin guidance range of 25-26%, with confidence leaning toward the 26% mark.
*   **EOM Targets:** Management is confident in meeting the 12.5% Expenses of Management (EOM) target over the next 2.5 years through commission realignment and cost optimization.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Regulatory Environment:** Management views proposed commission and EOM changes as a "meaningful reset" but supports the industry's evolution toward transparency.
*   **Bank Consolidation:** Management expects banks to consolidate the number of insurers they deal with, which they believe will benefit their counter share.
*   **Impact Assessment:** Management estimates that ~90-95% of their business is insulated from negative impacts of the proposed regulatory changes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Opex Efficiency:** Policyholder opex ratio improved by 174 bps YoY to 13.7%. Adjusted for GST, the ratio improved by 300 bps to 11.7%.
*   **Margin Drivers:** VNB margin expansion is attributed to product mix and operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Distribution:** Expanded distribution ecosystem with five new partnerships.
*   **GIFT City:** Proposition gaining traction, contributing >5% of ULIP APE.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Raised INR 686 crore of debt in September.
*   **Capital:** No immediate plans for capital raise.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Uncertainty regarding the final shape of commission caps and EOM limits.
*   **Base Effect:** High base for the protection business in H2.
*   **Persistency:** Noted persistency stress in non-participating product designs launched in the previous year; management stated these products were discontinued in March.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Shreya Shivani (Nomura) on H2 growth/margins:** Management reiterated confidence in H2 acceleration and stated that margin expansion is structural, driven by operating leverage and mix.
*   **Swarnabh Mukherjee on Axis Bank growth:** Management attributed the 4% growth in Axis Bank to delayed product launches and market volatility, noting that "Parivartan Plus" transformation is underway to drive efficiency.
*   **Prayesh Jain on protection channel mix:** Management argued that protection is not just a pricing game and that the economic value to distributors remains insulated even under new regulations.
*   **Madhukar Ladha on Axis Bank counter share:** Management acknowledged counter share dipped to 62-63% in Q2 but expects to recover this by year-end.
*   **Sanketh Godha on variable annuity:** Management confirmed the new product contributed >50% of annuity sales in Q2 but noted its margin profile is similar to previous products.

### One-line takeaway
Max Financial reported strong H1 performance with 21% VNB growth and expects to navigate regulatory headwinds while maintaining a 25-26% VNB margin trajectory.
