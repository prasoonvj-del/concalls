### Highlights
- Company renamed to "ICICI Life Insurance Limited" (formerly ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited).
- Board appointed Sidharatha Mishra as MD and CEO for a tenure of 5 years beginning October 14, 2026 (assumed COO role on October 6, 2026); Anup Bagchi's tenure as MD and CEO ended.
- Flagship term plan “iProtect Smart Plus” awarded “Product of the Year 2026-27” by Nielsen IQ.
- 54% of savings policies issued on the same day.
- Maintained track record of zero NPAs since inception; ICRA reaffirmed [ICRA] AAA (Stable) rating on the Rs 26 billion sub-debt program.
- Non-par mix within non-linked category moved to a broadly 60:40 par-to-non-par split in Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated no short-term guidance is provided, maintaining focus on medium-term sustainable VNB growth and top-line growth.
- Management expects retail protection growth numbers to moderate from the 40%-50% levels seen post-September 2025 into H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Amish Banker noted global macroeconomic uncertainty from geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions, and elevated inflation, with equity markets under pressure and continuous FII selling.
- Equity market volatility in late Q2 impacted investor sentiment in linked business.
- IRDAI released a consultation paper on recalibrating insurance distribution economics, focusing on policyholder-centricity, expense of management (EOM) frameworks with revised limits/glide paths, and differentiated commission caps.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses (excluding GST and stamp duty) reduced at a CAGR of 8% over the last three years (absolute 22% reduction in FY2026 vs FY2023).
- Margin expansion driven largely by product mix shift toward protection.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Distribution network stands at over 2.41 lakh advisors, 52 bank partnerships with 27,000+ bank branches, and 1,600+ non-bank partnerships.
- Focus on deepening presence in NRI segment through GIFT City and expanding micro-market strategies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Solvency ratio robust at 229.9%.
- Sum of variances in EV walk is positive, with economic variance close to zero.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- GST input tax credit challenges post-September 2025 created input tax credit friction.
- Uncertain equity markets impacting linked business sentiment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Swarnabha Mukherjee (360 ONE Capital) asked about VNB margin drivers, cost metrics including GST, and consultation paper feedback.** Management replied that VNB margin movement is primarily driven by product mix, ex-GST cost metrics are used for like-to-like comparisons due to post-September 2025 GST changes, and consultation paper feedback is premature as it is open for comments until month-end.
2. **Supratim Datta asked about bancassurance growth slowdown (ICICI Bank vs non-ICICI) and agency channel acceleration.** Management stated ICICI Bank banca portion is steady at Rs 100-120 crores/month while growth comes from other partners; agency is showing positive sequential growth via micro-market technology strategies.
3. **Samant Singh asked about retail savings softness, credit life partner split, and 25th month persistency drop.** Management stated market volatility impacted linked savings while non-par annuity grew 50%; credit life has a diverse partner base including the parent bank; 25th-month persistency movement is a flow-through from previous 13th-month trends.
4. **Madhukar Ladha asked for non-par/credit life shares and H2 Retail APE growth outlook.** Management stated par-to-non-par split is 60:40 in Q2, credit life sits at roughly a third of total protection, and reiterated they do not provide short-term growth guidance.
5. **Prayesh Jain asked about margin drivers and implications of IFRS, RBC, and consultation paper converging by FY2028.** Management stated margin expansion is product-mix driven, operational leverage is supported by continuous opex reduction, and welcomed Ind AS and RBC as modern frameworks providing clearer financial visibility.

### One-line takeaway
ICICI Life delivered resilient H1-FY2027 performance with 22.3% VNB growth and 26.0% margins, driven by a strategic pivot toward protection and annuity alongside disciplined opex reduction.
