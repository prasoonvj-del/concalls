# Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong H1 and Q2 financial growth offset by regulatory uncertainties regarding proposed insurance distribution reforms.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None provided explicitly for the full year, unlike Q1's adjusted EBITDA breakeven target; management chose not to pinpoint exact numbers for FY28/FY29 due to draft consultation regulations.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from pure operational optimism in Q1 to a defensive/analytical stance on the proposed IRDAI distribution/commission consultation paper.
- New topics or risks: Detailed deep-dive into the IRDAI consultation paper implications (POSP vs. agent commission differentials, product caps removal, B2C contract maintenance transition); introduction of "Ninja AI" assistants. Disappeared: Specific mentions of Supreme Court TP reserving.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered continued premium and revenue growth in Q2 (35% YoY revenue growth) and sustained overhead cost discipline, while AI tool deployment (AI tutor, Ninja AI) progressed as planned.

### Key numbers
- **H1 Platform Premium:** Rs 2,419 crore (up 52% YoY)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 637 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **H1 Renewal Revenue:** Over Rs 136 crore (up 52% YoY)
- **H1 Service EBITDA:** Rs 85 crore (up 66% YoY; margin up from 11% to 13%)
- **H1 Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** -7% (improved from -17% YoY)
- **Q2 Platform Premium:** Rs 1,214 crore (up 55% YoY)
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 342 crore (up 35% YoY)
- **Q2 Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** -6% (improved from -14% YoY)
- **Total EBITDA Loss (H1):** -16.7 crore (-5% of revenue)
- **Digital Partners (DP) Base:** 7,28,000 (added 69,000 new DPs in H1)
- **P3M-Active DP Count:** 96,368 (up from 80,253 YoY)
- **Turtlemint Academy MAUs:** 55,000+
- **Cash Balance:** Rs 740 crore as of end of Q2

### Highlights
- Strong adoption of the Turtlefin platform drove a 55% YoY increase in Q2 premium.
- Launched AI-based product training (AI tutor) and "Ninja AI" assistant for agents to scale support and improve productivity.
- 85% of policies are end-to-end digitally issued on the platform.
- Corporate overhead costs as a percentage of revenue dropped from 28% to 21% in H1 (and 26% to 19% in Q2).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to close the year with a strong cash balance, higher than the Q2-end figure due to working capital movements and tax utilization.
- Management anticipates protecting service EBITDA on an absolute value basis next year despite regulatory changes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Over 50% of business comes from Tier 3 and Tier 4 geographies (districts with 5 or less general insurer offices).
- Proposed distribution regulations remove product caps for POSPs in health, life, and SME, opening a >Rs 50,000 crore opportunity in SME and expanding life insurance distribution potential.
- 76% of premiums come from POSPs distributing across 8+ insurance companies, and 60%+ across 4+ subcategories.
- 88% of premiums come from PoSPs distributing 5+ insurance companies.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed corporate overhead costs below service EBITDA remained nearly flat (growing only 2.5–3% YoY in H1).
- Management cited potential to reduce discretionary fixed costs by ~Rs 50 crore+ if required under adverse regulatory scenarios.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *(Not discussed)*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash balance stood at Rs 740 crore at the end of Q2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Proposed IRDAI consultation paper on distribution reforms suggests a reduction in commission earnings for agents and POSPs, potentially leading to churn among single-product/low-volume POSPs.
- Standalone motor third-party (TP) economics are not favorable under proposed commission caps.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Supratim Datta (Jefferies):** Asked about cash burn and profitability under the proposed distribution paper. Management answered that absolute service EBITDA will be protected due to contract maintenance transitioning churned POSP customers into direct/B2C servicing.
2. **Karthikeya Mohata:** Inquired about on-ground reaction and October growth trends. Management stated churn is on historical trends, daily active metrics are up, and training engagement has surged.
3. **Prayesh Jain:** Asked about cross-sell feasibility for shopkeepers/mechanics and worst-case FY28 scenario. Management explained that multi-category partners already represent 40% of premiums, and fixed costs have a ~Rs 50 crore discretionary buffer.
4. **Mahesh Soni:** Asked about Turtlemint's feedback to IRDAI and B2C web lead generation plans. Management stated they actively engaged via broker associations sharing granular data, and noted that turtlemint.com will not be a primary aggressive B2C marketing focus due to potential dark pattern regulations.
5. **Visnu:** Asked about renewal book protection and path to operating profitability. Management stated health renewals remain sticky (90%+ retention) and operational leverage alongside employee productivity will drive profitability.

### One-line takeaway
Turtlemint posted robust H1 and Q2 growth with expanding margins and controlled overheads, but faces a strategic transition as it navigates proposed IRDAI distribution reforms.
