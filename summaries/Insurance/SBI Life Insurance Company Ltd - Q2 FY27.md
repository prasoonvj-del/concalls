# SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong profit, EV, and VoNB growth, reiterating full-year growth and margin guidance despite regulatory shifts.

### Key numbers
- **Gross Written Premium (GWP):** Rs 48,400 crore, up 13% YoY.
- **New Business Premium (NBP):** Rs 20,260 crore, up 10% YoY.
- **Individual Rated Premium (IRP):** Rs 9,720 crore, up 12% YoY.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 1,270 crore, up 17% YoY (Rs 1,690 crore excluding GST impact, up 39% YoY).
- **Value of New Business (VoNB):** Rs 3,270 crore, up 19% YoY.
- **VoNB Margin:** 27.9% for H1 FY27; 29.3% for Q2 FY27.
- **Embedded Value (EV):** Rs 87,350 crore, up 15% YoY.
- **Assets Under Management (AUM):** Rs 5,30,000 crore, up 9% YoY.
- **Solvency Ratio:** 1.97 (against regulatory minimum of 1.50).
- **Persistency:** 13th month at 87.6% (+44 bps YoY); 25th month at 78.4% (+110 bps YoY); 49th month at 69.3% (+40 bps YoY).

### Highlights
- **Product Mix:** Non-ULIP products increased share; ULIP mix stood at 62% in H1 FY27 compared to 65% in H1 FY26.
- **Segment Performance:** Guaranteed non-par savings grew 27% to Rs 2,470 crore. Total annuity and pension business reached Rs 4,620 crore. Pure protection APE grew 26% YoY.
- **Distribution Channels:** Bancassurance (SBI & RRB) contributed 53% to total APE. Agency channel individual APE grew 12% YoY to Rs 3,200 crore with over 72,000 agents added gross. Other channels (direct, web, other banks) grew 67% YoY.
- **Digital Adoption:** 99.9% of individual proposals submitted digitally, with 71% processed through automated underwriting.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its full-year FY27 IRP growth guidance of 14% to 15%.
- Management reaffirmed its sustainable VoNB margin guidance band of 26% to 28%.
- Target product mix for full-year FY27 is set at ~63% ULIP and ~37% non-ULIP.
- Management plans to launch a deferred annuity product in Q3 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rising household aspirations and demand for financial protection continue to support long-term growth in the life insurance sector.
- Management views proposed IRDAI distribution consultation reforms as constructive for policyholder transparency and expects limited impact on SBI Life due to its existing structure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expense ratio stood at 6.9% (5.8% excluding GST impact). Total cost ratio stood at 11.7%.
- Product returns were repriced in Q2 FY27 to pass on yield movement benefits to customers while preserving margins. Interest rate exposure hedging remains optimal at ~80%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 14 new branches and expanded the agency workforce by over 72,000 gross agents during the half-year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Solvency buffer remains strong at 1.97, well above the regulatory threshold of 1.50.
- Death claim settlement ratio stood at 99.1%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Short-term market volatility impacting customer product preferences between ULIPs and guaranteed savings.
- Regulatory framework transitions regarding insurance distribution recalibration and Ind AS/IFRS implementation timing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Growth Outlook:** Analysts questioned achieving full-year guidance given 12% H1 IRP growth. Management reiterated 14%–15% growth guidance, citing historical H2/Q3 operational seasonality and channel momentum.
- **VoNB Margins & Yield Curve:** Analysts asked about margin sustainability above 29% in Q2. Management clarified that dynamic repricing balances customer IRR and margins, maintaining its structural 26%–28% range guidance.
- **Regulatory Impact on Distribution:** Analysts asked about potential impact from IRDAI’s distribution paper on bancassurance and credit life. Management noted SBI Life is among the least impacted insurers and anticipates potential market share opportunities.
- **Ind AS / IFRS Transition:** Analysts inquired about the accounting transition approach and opening CSM. Management stated it is operating under IRDAI forbearance and will finalize options near year-end, promising a dedicated investor event.

### One-line takeaway
SBI Life delivered robust 19% VoNB growth and 29.3% quarterly VoNB margins, backed by product diversification and strong solvency, while reiterating its 14%–15% annual growth target.
