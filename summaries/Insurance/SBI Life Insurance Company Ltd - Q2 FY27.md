# SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a 17% growth in PAT and 19% growth in VoNB, sticking to their full-year guidance despite short-term variations.

### Key numbers
- **New business premium:** INR202.6 billion (10% YoY growth, 20% private market share)
- **Individual rated new business premium (IRP):** INR97.2 billion (12% YoY growth, 22.6% private market share)
- **Gross written premium (GWP):** INR484 billion (13% YoY growth)
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** INR12.7 billion (17% YoY growth); excluding GST impact of INR5.3 billion, PAT stood at INR16.9 billion (39% YoY growth)
- **Value of new business (VoNB):** INR32.7 billion (19% YoY growth); VoNB margin at 27.9% (29.3% for the quarter)
- **Embedded value (EV):** INR873.5 billion (15% YoY growth); Operating return on EV at 18.8%
- **Assets under management (AUM):** INR5.3 trillion (9% YoY growth)
- **Renewal premium:** INR281.5 billion (15% YoY growth, 58% of GWP)
- **Individual APE:** INR97.7 billion (11% YoY growth); Total APE INR117.5 billion (18% YoY growth)
- **Group new business premium:** INR65.3 billion (6% YoY growth)
- **Guaranteed non-par savings:** INR24.7 billion (27% YoY growth)
- **ULIP share:** INR62% of product mix (vs. 65% in H1 FY26)
- **Individual protection APE:** INR4.3 billion (16% YoY growth); pure protection up 26%
- **Group protection APE:** INR16 billion (134% YoY growth)
- **Annuity and pension NBP:** INR46.2 billion
- **Banca individual APE:** INR59.9 billion (10% YoY growth, 53% of total APE)
- **Agency individual APE:** INR32 billion (12% YoY growth)
- **Other channels growth:** 67% (contributing 19% of total APE)
- **Opex ratio:** 6.9%; **Total cost ratio:** 11.7% (Excluding GST impact, opex ratio was 5.8%)
- **Persistency (13th, 25th, 49th month):** 87.6%, 78.4%, 69.3% (improving by 44, 110, and 40 bps YoY)
- **Solvency ratio:** 1.97 (vs. regulatory requirement of 1.50)
- **Death claim settlement ratio:** 99.1%
- **Misselling ratio:** 0.035%

### Highlights
- Sold 9.78 lakh new policies covering 1.43 crore lives in H1 FY27.
- Individual and group new business sum assured grew by 36% and 78% YoY, respectively. Rider sum assured accounts for 40% of individual sum assured.
- Added over 72,000 agents on a gross basis and opened 14 new branches in H1 FY27.
- Digital adoption: 99.9% of individual proposals submitted digitally, 71% processed through automated underwriting.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its full-year IRP growth guidance of 14% to 15%.
- Management maintained its VoNB margin guidance range of 26% to 28%.
- Management plans to launch a regular pay deferred annuity product soon.
- Company plans to conduct an Investor Day towards the end of the financial year regarding IFRS/Ind AS adoption.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted that India's resilient economy and rising household aspirations provide a sound foundation for long-term sector growth.
- IRDAI’s consultation papers on distribution economics are viewed as a constructive step for policyholder interests. Management expects limited impact on SBI Life as its existing structure is broadly aligned.
- Pure protection demand is rising due to increased financial awareness.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins benefited from a favorable product mix shift toward non-par and annuity.
- The company hedges 80% of the premium as per policy.
- Commission rates are uniform across all distributors (agency, banca, other banks).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 14 new branches and onboarded J&K Bank as a new banca partner during the quarter.
- Average agent productivity stands at around INR2 lakhs; SBI branch productivity stands at INR5 million on individual APE terms.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- AUM reached INR5.3 trillion (9% growth).
- Solvency stands robust at 1.97.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as material risks by management, aside from navigating evolving regulatory guidelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Avinash Singh (Emkay Global) asked about IRP growth falling short of 13%–15% aspirations in H1, and the negative operating adjustment in VoNB.** Management responded that they stick to their full-year guidance of 14%–15% IRP growth and clarified that VoNB assumptions were last refined in March 2026, with no new assumption changes made in the current period.
2. **Shreya Shivani (Nomura) asked about October banca trajectory and non-par availability across channels.** Management stated they do not share monthly/quarterly numbers but maintain the yearly trend, and confirmed all products are uniformly available across all distribution channels.
3. **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal) asked if H1 margin gains from non-par/yields are sustainable and about the regulatory impact on credit life.** Management stated they continuously reprice products to optimize value and do not foresee adverse margin impacts in subsequent quarters. They reiterated that SBI Life is least impacted by distribution recalibration norms.
4. **Samant Singh (PhillipCapital) asked about deferred annuity launch plans and Ind AS transition approach.** Management stated deferred annuity will be launched soon with margins equal to or higher than company average, and Ind AS transition will follow IRDAI mandates and forbearance evaluations by March 2027.
5. **Madhukar Ladha (JPMorgan) asked about channel-wise APE breakdown and hedging levels.** Management declined to divulge channel-wise targets but confirmed 80% of premium is hedged as an optimal value.

### One-line takeaway
SBI Life delivered a resilient H1 FY27 with 17% PAT growth and 19% VoNB growth, maintaining its full-year 14%–15% IRP growth and 26%–28% VoNB margin guidance.
