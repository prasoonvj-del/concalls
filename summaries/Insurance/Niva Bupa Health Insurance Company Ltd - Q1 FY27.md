# Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Retail growth comfortably outpaced the market, profitability improved with PAT at INR137.8 crores, and the combined insurance service ratio improved by 300 bps YoY to 100.2%.

### Key numbers
- Retail health industry growth: 31.6% (reported)
- Niva Bupa retail health growth: 47.1% (reported), 35.5% (like-to-like)
- Niva Bupa overall growth: 31.7% (reported), 23.0% (like-to-like)
- Retail market share: 11.1% in Q1
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR137.8 crores in Q1
- Combined insurance service ratio (CISR): 100.2% (vs 103.2% in Q1 FY27 [stated as Q1 last year])
- Expense of management ratio: 35.2% (vs 38.0% in Q1 last year)
- Retail loss ratio: 67.5% (improved by 90 bps)
- Renewal loss ratio in retail: around 75%
- Solvency ratio: 2.25 (vs regulatory minimum of 1.50)
- AUM (book value): INR9,963 crores; (mark-to-market): over INR10,000 crores
- Annualized investment yield: 7.2%
- Post-tax ROE (rolling 4 quarters): 11.8%
- Claims settlement rate: 95.6% in Q1
- NPS score: 62 (up from 57 last year)
- PPN strategy footprint: 49 cities, over 1,000 hospitals, 22% claims flow

### Highlights
- Fully transitioned to Ind AS in Q1 while continuing to report parallel accounts as required.
- Group business growth was flat due to a challenging pricing environment on the B2B side; SME opportunity grew at 50%+.
- Ranked 41st in India's top 50 Great Places to Work.
- Board approved the formation of a debt raising committee with an enabling resolution to raise up to INR500 crores in one or more tranches.
- Issuer rating affirmed at AAA by ICRA.
- Health partner app achieved 570,000+ monthly active users, with 62,000+ people/month consuming health checkups/diagnostics and 6,600+ monthly doctor consults.
- Advanced GenAI initiatives deployed in production (built in-house via AI Lab or third-party).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to sustain 8 to 10 percentage points faster than market growth on retail health specifically.
- Internal retail-to-group mix target remains around 70% retail and 30% group.
- EOM ratio is expected to stabilize closer to 32% to 33%.
- Reiterated FY29 guidance for CISR and mid-to-high teens ROE.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry awareness campaigns continue apace (industry committing ~INR120 crores/year, ~50% toward health).
- Experiencing GST tailwinds; overall health industry grew at 22.4% (reported).
- Dr. Prakash health insurance committee working on standardization, care pathways, position statements on cancer and oncology; common empanelment initiative has MOUs with over 3,000 hospitals.
- B2B pricing environment remains challenging, with the market operating at "claims minus" pricing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Increase in expense ratio by 90 bps driven primarily by mix change toward retail health.
- Loss ratio improved by 3.8 percentage points overall, driven by retail new business and disciplined group underwriting.
- Reinsurance expense movements have no structural differences; profit commissions fluctuate quarterly.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continued investments in distribution expansion in Tier 2, Tier 3 towns and beyond ("Bharat initiative").
- Added advisor network at a pace similar to last year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Board passed an enabling resolution to raise up to INR500 crores (to address an existing INR250 crores NCD call option due this financial year and future growth plans).
- AIF exposure is at about 4% of AUM (committed capital), yielding 12% to 15% (regulatory maximum is 5%).
- Nifty ETF allocation increased to about 3.5% of the investment book.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Challenging pricing environment on the B2B/group side with aggressive market pricing.
- Seasonal infection season ahead.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shreya Shivani (Nomura Holdings):** Asked about the July loss ratio trend and reinsurance expense spikes. Management stated July is in line with expectations, and reinsurance movements have no structural anomalies.
2. **Harsh Shah:** Inquired about growth strategy gaps vs the industry and group-to-retail mix. Management reaffirmed targeting 8-10% above market retail growth and a 70:30 retail-group mix.
3. **Supratim Datta:** Asked about fresh vs renewal retail growth and the impact of PPN on loss ratios. Management stated fresh retail grew 41%, overall retail grew 46.5%, fresh is ~35% of retail mix, and PPN yields a 15%-20% lower average claim size.
4. **Prayesh Jain:** Asked about book loss ratio trajectory, debt raise rationale, and AIF strategy. Management explained the INR500 crore enabling resolution covers the maturing INR250 crore NCD call option and growth plans; AIFs are largely performing credit.
5. **Hitaindra Pradhan:** Asked about Ind AS insurance revenue divergence and EOM stabilization. Management stated revenue divergence is due to multiyear policy amortization, and EOM will stabilize near 32%-33%.

### One-line takeaway
Niva Bupa reported robust Q1 FY27 retail health growth of 47.1% (reported) and a PAT of INR137.8 crores, driven by favorable retail mix and improved claims ratios despite a flat group business due to aggressive B2B pricing.
