# Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by 49% YoY platform premium growth, 40% revenue growth, and significant margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Platform Premium:** Rs 1,204 crore (up 49% YoY) / Rs 1,205 crore
- **Revenue:** Rs 294 crore (up 40% YoY)
- **Service EBITDA:** Rs 39 crore (up 89% YoY; ~13% of quarterly revenues)
- **Adjusted EBITDA Loss:** Rs 26 crore loss (-9% of revenues vs. -21% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) Loss:** Rs 38 crore loss (-13% of revenues vs. Rs 47 crore loss / -22% YoY)
- **Renewal Revenue:** Rs 65 crore (up 66% YoY); renewal share of revenue grew from 19% to 22%
- **P3M Active Digital Partners (DP):** 90,791 (up ~33% YoY from 72,244)
- **Total Digital Partners:** Over 6,90,000 (added 32,000+ DPs in Q1)
- **Turtlemint Academy Monthly Active Users:** ~55,000
- **Total Policy Count:** Touched 3.1 crore up to Q1
- **Claims settled handled:** Over Rs 26 crore; 1.5 lakh+ unique servicing requests handled in Q1
- **Corporate Overhead Cost:** Declined from 30% of revenue to 22% YoY; management expects it to come down to 7-8% over the next 4 to 5 years.
- **ESOP Cost:** ~Rs 7 crore for the quarter

### Highlights
- Recruited 32,000+ digital partners in Q1, taking total DP base to 690k+.
- Health business grew at 45%+ YoY; Term life grew at 60%+ YoY.
- Deployed voice AI agents for renewal calls (achieving a renewal rate higher by 500 bps). AI handles 55% of support tickets and processed ~2 million documents in Q1. 75% of new code generated using AI.
- Cross-sell activation funnel grew motor-only DPs starting to sell health by 40%+ YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects corporate overheads as a percentage of revenue to decline to 7-8% over the next 4 to 5 years.
- Reiterated full-year goal to try for adjusted EBITDA breakeven in the current financial year, with profitability heavily weighted toward H2 due to business seasonality.
- Expects service EBITDA margins to incrementally improve by ~2% in the near term, scaling toward 23-24% over 4-5 years driven by renewal book expansion.
- Projected 40% YoY overall business growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Insurance penetration in India (especially B30 and underserved markets) offers a large opportunity.
- Motor industry H1 constitutes ~45% and H2 ~55% of the market. Q1 is typically 16-18% lower than Q4 due to seasonality (festivals in OND, financial year-end in JFM).
- Over 55% of vehicles remain uninsured even for Third Party (TP); regulatory initiatives (like fuel denial for uninsured TP) could create massive demand surges similar to the 2019 two-wheeler fines.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Renewal service EBITDA is stated to be 2.5x to 3x higher than new service EBITDA due to lower CAC, negligible sales/marketing effort, and better terms/loss ratios with insurers.
- Fixed costs below service EBITDA (people and vendor costs) grew by only 5% in Q1 to Rs 66 crore.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prayesh Jain (Prabhudas Lilladher / ICICI Securities inferred):** Asked about seasonality drivers and take-rate sustainability. *Management clarified motor industry H1/H2 split (45/55) and stated take rates remain stable with mix changes driven by renewal vs. fresh.*
2. **Aditya Miglani:** Inquired about the bridge from 13% service EBITDA to 24-25% long-term margins and lower CAC on renewals. *Management explained that renewal CAC is lower, sales effort/marketing is negligible, and quality/scale incentives from insurers drive higher margins.*
3. **Ansuman Deb (ICICI Securities):** Asked about cohort productivity improvements and AI impact on EBITDA. *Management stated all cohorts show improving productivity over prior years, and while AI drives onboarding/renewal efficiencies, it is too early to quantify long-term EBITDA impact concretely.*
4. **Dharshil Jhaveri:** Asked about PAT breakeven timeline and competitive landscape versus players like Policybazaar. *Management reiterated adjusted EBITDA breakeven guidance for the current year (with H2 profitability) and noted Turtlemint holds the highest share among broker-appointed PoSPs in an oligopolistic 2-3 player market.*

### One-line takeaway
Turtlemint Fintech Solutions delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 49% platform premium growth, a 40% rise in revenue, significant margin expansion, and a halved adjusted EBITDA loss (-9%), driven by scaling renewals, partner productivity, and AI integration.
