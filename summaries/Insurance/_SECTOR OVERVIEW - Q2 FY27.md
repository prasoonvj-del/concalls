# Insurance: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Across the Indian insurance sector in Q2 FY27, managements reported resilient performances marked by robust top-line growth, margin expansions, and structural underwriting turnarounds despite global macroeconomic uncertainties, equity market volatility, and FII selling. A dominant theme across both life and health segments is the navigation of evolving regulatory landscapes—specifically IRDAI consultation papers on distribution economics, expense of management (EOM) limits, and commission structures. Additionally, GST-related tailwinds and changes regarding Input Tax Credit (ITC) on group health and life insurance created both operational adjustments and demand boosts.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board remain optimistic about H2 growth acceleration, driven by rising domestic economic consumption, robust automotive sales volumes, and strong structural demand for retail health and protection products. Life insurers (ICICI Life, Max Financial, SBI Life) anticipate stronger retail APE and protection demand moving into the second half.
*   **Divergence:** Demand performance varied by channel and segment. While retail health surged (e.g., Niva Bupa retail growth at 47%, ICICI Lombard retail health up 66.4%), equity market volatility in late Q2 negatively impacted investor sentiment in linked life savings businesses (noted by ICICI Life and Max Financial). Furthermore, Medi Assist highlighted that the IT-ITeS sector is lagging in net employee headcount growth (impacting group health timing lags), whereas manufacturing, consumer goods, and oil & gas sectors are expanding. Max Financial also noted localized growth friction with partner Axis Bank (growing at 4%) due to delayed product launches and transformation efforts.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Life Insurance Margins & Opex:** Life players maintained healthy margins through product mix shifts toward protection and annuities. ICICI Life reported a VNB margin of 26.0% and reduced its ex-GST cost-to-premium ratio to 18.3%. Max Financial posted an H1 VNB margin of 25.4% (27% in Q2) and improved its policyholder opex ratio by 174 bps YoY to 13.7%. SBI Life reported a VoNB margin of 27.9% (29.3% for Q2) and a total cost ratio of 11.7%.
*   **Health Insurance Loss & Combined Ratios:** Health and general insurers saw structural improvements in underwriting metrics. Star Health marked its 5th successive quarter of loss ratio improvement, achieving a Combined Insurance Service Ratio (CISR) of 98% in Q2 and an underwriting profit of Rs. 99 crore. Niva Bupa reported an improved CISR of 99.5% and a retail loss ratio of 67.4%. ICICI Lombard faced industry-wide motor combined ratio pressures (industry motor combined ratio at 131.4% in Q1) and CAT losses of Rs. 1.18 billion in Q2, but maintained superior underwriting discipline with an overall combined ratio of 104.5% (102.7% ex-CAT). Medi Assist drove EBITDA margin recovery to 20.4% in Q2 via Paramount integration and headcount rationalization.

## Capex & capacity
Managements focused heavily on digital scaling, AI deployment, and agent network expansions rather than heavy physical capex:
*   **ICICI Life:** Network stands at over 2.41 lakh advisors, 52 bank partnerships (27,000+ branches), and 1,600+ non-bank partnerships.
*   **Max Financial:** Expanded distribution with five new partnerships and growing GIFT City traction.
*   **SBI Life:** Added over 72,000 agents on a gross basis and 14 new branches in H1 FY27.
*   **Star Health:** Added 39,000 new agents in H1 FY27, bringing total agency count to 8.7 lakh, alongside digital D2C scaling via Salesforce and AI bots.
*   **Medi Assist:** Completed Paramount integration, migrated all group customers to its platform, and expanded its international footprint in Southeast Asia.
*   **Niva Bupa:** Scaled its PPN setup across 49 cities and 1,132 hospitals.

## What changed since last quarter
*(Drawn from Niva Bupa and general company updates)*
*   **Niva Bupa:** Shifted its tone to emphasize continuing volume growth and demand elasticity post-GST, while addressing detailed broker feedback regarding retail vs corporate claims rejections and preparing for upcoming distribution norm regulations.
*   **ICICI Lombard:** Addressed the impact of the Shishupal Supreme Court judgment on Motor TP claims (fully provisioned in Q1, now business-as-usual).
*   **Star Health:** Discontinued the recognition of insurance finance charges (prior periods restated with no PAT impact).

## Company one-liners
*   **ICICI Life:** ICICI Life delivered resilient H1-FY2027 performance with 22.3% VNB growth and 26.0% margins, driven by a strategic pivot toward protection and annuity alongside disciplined opex reduction.
*   **ICICI Lombard:** ICICI Lombard delivered a resilient Q2 FY2027 performance with PAT growing 27.5% YoY to ₹10.31 billion, supported by strong retail health outperformance, sequential recovery in commercial lines, and superior underwriting discipline relative to industry pressures.
*   **Max Financial:** Max Financial reported strong H1 performance with 21% VNB growth and expects to navigate regulatory headwinds while maintaining a 25-26% VNB margin trajectory.
*   **Medi Assist:** Medi Assist completed its Paramount integration, delivering a 20.4% Q2 EBITDA margin and announcing a buyback, while positioning its AI platform and international business for structural growth.
*   **Niva Bupa:** Niva Bupa delivered a strong H1 FY27 with GWP reaching Rs 5,004 crore and PAT doubling to Rs 273 crore, underpinned by 47% retail growth and a lower combined insurance service ratio of 99.5%.
*   **SBI Life:** SBI Life delivered a resilient H1 FY27 with 17% PAT growth and 19% VoNB growth, maintaining its full-year 14%–15% IRP growth and 26%–28% VoNB margin guidance.
*   **Star Health:** Star Health delivered its 5th successive quarter of structural underwriting turnaround in Q2 FY27, backed by proprietary distribution growth, disciplined risk selection, and lower loss ratios, leading to a threefold surge in reported PAT to Rs. 265 crore.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Finalization and feedback implementation of IRDAI consultation papers regarding distribution economics, commission caps, and Expense of Management (EOM) limits.
*   H2 volume and growth acceleration, particularly in retail health and protection segments as high base effects kick in.
*   Progress on Ind AS and IFRS/RBC transitions, with several companies planning investor updates or tracking regulatory mandates towards FY28.
*   Bancassurance partner performance and recovery of bank counter shares (e.g., Max Financial's Axis Bank transformation).

*Based on: ICICI Life Insurance Ltd, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Ltd, Max Financial Services Ltd, Medi Assist Healthcare Services Ltd, Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Ltd, SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd, Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Ltd*
