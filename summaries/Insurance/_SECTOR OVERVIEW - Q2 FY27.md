# Insurance: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian insurance sector reflected a resilient operating environment supported by robust domestic economic growth (Real GDP at 7.8% in Q1 FY27), strong automotive sales volumes, and a rising focus on retail health and protection. Life insurers experienced steady Value of New Business (VNB) growth and margin expansion driven by product mix shifts, while general and health insurers reaped the benefits of structural turnaround, risk selection, and productivity enhancements. However, headwinds persisted in the form of equity market volatility, regulatory transitions (such as IRDAI consultation papers on distribution economics and Expense of Management frameworks), and weather-related CAT losses.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board acknowledge strong secular tailwinds for the sector, driven by rising financial awareness and household aspirations. In health and general insurance, retail health demand remains robust, with Star Health noting fresh retail health GWP up 35% YoY in Q2, and ICICI Lombard reporting retail health growth outperforming the industry. In life insurance, pure protection and annuity segments are witnessing strong traction (e.g., ICICI Life retail protection grew 47.2% YoY; Max Financial's annuity business grew 130%).
*   **Divergence:** Views diverge on savings and linked business demand. While ICICI Life noted that equity market volatility in late Q2 impacted investor sentiment in linked businesses (with non-linked savings de-growing 4.5%), SBI Life maintained a dominant ULIP share (62%) and guided for steady 14%-15% IRP growth for the full year. Furthermore, while Max Financial and ICICI Life expect H2 growth acceleration, ICICI Life noted that retail protection growth numbers will likely moderate from the 40%-50% post-September 2025 levels into H2.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Costs & Opex:** Insurers are aggressively focusing on opex efficiency. ICICI Life reported an 18.3% cost-to-premium ratio (ex-GST), down 30 bps YoY. Max Financial improved its policyholder opex ratio by 174 bps YoY to 13.7% (11.7% ex-GST). SBI Life reported an opex ratio of 6.9%. Star Health benefited from commission rationalization (such as senior citizen commission reductions from 15% to 10%) flowing through the book.
*   **Margins:** Life insurers posted healthy VNB margins: ICICI Life at 26.0%, Max Financial at 25.4% (27% in Q2), and SBI Life at 27.9% (29.3% for the quarter). Margin expansion was primarily attributed to favorable product mix shifts toward protection, non-par savings, and annuities. In general insurance, ICICI Lombard maintained its motor loss ratio within the 65%-67% band, offsetting a deteriorated industry motor combined ratio of 131.4% in Q1 FY27. Star Health's Combined Insurance Service Ratio (CISR) improved to 98% for Q2, marking its 5th successive quarter of underwriting improvement.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Distribution Expansion:** Expansion continues across proprietary and partnership channels. ICICI Life expanded its network to over 2.41 lakh advisors, 52 bank partnerships (27,000+ branches), and 1,600+ non-bank partners. SBI Life added over 72,000 agents and 14 new branches, onboarding J&K Bank as a new banca partner. Star Health added 39,000 agents in H1 FY27, reaching 8.7 lakh agents, noting that fresh business growth in semi-urban and rural geographies is over 3x that of metros. Max Financial added five new partnerships and saw its E-com channel grow 29%.
*   **Technology & Digital Capacity:** Heavy investments in AI and digital infrastructure were highlighted. ICICI Lombard showcased its WhatsApp-based GenAI bot used in over 40% of agency health underwriting daily. Star Health scaled up AI bots processing 7.5 million minutes of call volume at <20% cost of human deployment and integrated Salesforce. SBI Life reported 99.9% of individual proposals submitted digitally.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **ICICI Life Insurance Ltd:** Renamed from "ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited" to "ICICI Life Insurance Limited". Sidharatha Mishra was appointed MD and CEO effective October 14, 2026, succeeding Anup Bagchi.
*   **Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Ltd:** Discontinued the recognition of insurance finance charges with restated previous periods, achieving its 5th successive quarter of underwriting turnaround with a threefold surge in reported Q2 PAT to Rs. 265 crore.
*   **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Ltd:** Addressed the reserve provisioning for the Shishupal Supreme Court judgment made in Q1, normalizing it as business-as-usual in Q2 while absorbing ₹1.18 billion in CAT losses.

## Company one-liners
*   **ICICI Life:** ICICI Life delivered resilient H1-FY2027 performance with 22.3% VNB growth and 26.0% margins, driven by a strategic pivot toward protection and annuity alongside disciplined opex reduction.
*   **ICICI Lombard:** ICICI Lombard delivered a resilient Q2 FY2027 performance with PAT growing 27.5% YoY to ₹10.31 billion, supported by strong retail health outperformance, sequential recovery in commercial lines, and superior underwriting discipline relative to industry pressures.
*   **Max Financial:** Max Financial reported strong H1 performance with 21% VNB growth and expects to navigate regulatory headwinds while maintaining a 25-26% VNB margin trajectory.
*   **SBI Life:** SBI Life delivered a resilient H1 FY27 with 17% PAT growth and 19% VoNB growth, maintaining its full-year 14%–15% IRP growth and 26%–28% VoNB margin guidance.
*   **Star Health:** Star Health delivered its 5th successive quarter of structural underwriting turnaround in Q2 FY27, backed by proprietary distribution growth, disciplined risk selection, and lower loss ratios, leading to a threefold surge in reported PAT to Rs. 265 crore.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Final regulatory developments and industry feedback on IRDAI's consultation papers regarding distribution economics, commission caps, and Expense of Management (EOM) frameworks.
*   H2 volume momentum in retail protection, motor sales during the festive season, and commercial lines pricing recovery.
*   Preparation and disclosures by life insurers regarding the upcoming Ind AS / IFRS and Risk-Based Capital (RBC) convergence framework by FY2028.

*Based on: ICICI Life Insurance Ltd, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Ltd, Max Financial Services Ltd, SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd, Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Ltd*
