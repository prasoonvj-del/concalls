# Insurance - Life insurance: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the life insurance sector reported a positive Q2/H1 FY27, characterized by robust Value of New Business (VNB) growth, margin expansions, and disciplined cost controls. Companies like **ICICI Life**, **Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC)**, **Max Financial Services**, and **SBI Life** navigated macroeconomic volatility and equity market fluctuations by pivoting toward high-margin non-par savings, annuities, and protection products, even as ULIP growth faced short-term headwinds. Furthermore, managements are proactively preparing for forthcoming regulatory changes regarding distribution economics and expense of management (EOM) frameworks.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Indian life insurers have historically driven growth through vast tied-agent networks and bancassurance partnerships, transitioning from asset-heavy participating products toward market-linked (ULIP) products and, more recently, high-margin non-participating guaranteed savings, annuities, and protection lines [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **Pivot to non-par, annuity, and protection products**: This shift is scaling across the industry as insurers counter volatile ULIP demand. ICICI Life reported a 47.2% growth in retail protection and a 50% jump in annuities; LIC grew individual protection APE by 58.59% and non-par savings by 35%; Max Financial saw pure retail protection grow 31% and annuity grow 130%; SBI Life grew guaranteed non-par savings by 27% and pure protection by 26% [ICICI Life concall Q2 FY27; LIC concall H1 FY27; Max Financial concall Q2 FY27; SBI Life concall Q2 FY27]. This shift is critical for driving structural VNB margin expansions across all players.

**Government push**
- **IRDAI Distribution and Commission Reforms**: The regulator's ongoing review of distribution economics and commission caps aims to enhance policyholder-centricity and rationalise expenses of management [Govt: IRDAI set up a panel to review commission plan, Nov 2026]. Established players like **LIC**, **SBI Life**, and **Max Financial** expressed confidence that their controlled expense ratios and existing distribution structures insulate them or position them advantageously to consolidate market share.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
- **Consensus**: Managements across **Max Financial** and **SBI Life** expect demand to accelerate in H2 FY27, backed by seasonal operational momentum, rising household aspirations, and strong demand for financial protection.
- **Divergence**: While non-par, protection, and annuity segments witnessed stellar growth across the board, demand for linked businesses (ULIPs) showed divergence and softness due to equity market volatility and late-quarter FII selling, noted by **ICICI Life** and **LIC** (which cited monthly eNACH ticket size rationalization).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Margin Expansion**: VNB margins expanded across majors. **ICICI Life** expanded VNB margins to 26.0% (+130 bps from FY full-year); **LIC** expanded net VNB margins by 470 bps YoY to 22.3%; **Max Financial** reported an H1 VNB margin of 25.4% (touching 27% in Q2); and **SBI Life** recorded an H1 VoNB margin of 27.9% (hitting 29.3% in Q2).
- **Cost Ratios**: Expense efficiencies improved industry-wide. **LIC** reported an overall expense ratio improvement of 87 bps YoY to 10.41%; **Max Financial** improved its policyholder opex ratio by 174 bps YoY to 13.7% (11.7% ex-GST); **ICICI Life** reduced its cost-to-premium ratio (ex-GST) to 18.3% (down 30 bps YoY); and **SBI Life** reported an operating expense ratio of 6.9%.

## Capex & capacity
- **Distribution Expansion**: Insurers continued scaling physical and digital footprints. **SBI Life** added 14 new branches and over 72,000 gross agents; **ICICI Life** expanded its advisor network to over 2.41 lakh, alongside 52 bank partnerships and 1,600+ non-bank partners; **LIC** appointed 3.04 lakh women under the Bima Sakhi initiative covering 66% of gram panchayats, while adding Yes Bank and RBL Bank to its bancassurance fold. **Max Financial** added five new distribution partnerships.

## What to watch next quarter
- Final shape and implementation timelines of IRDAI’s distribution and commission cap regulations.
- Progress on Ind AS / IFRS accounting transitions and Risk-Based Capital (RBC) framework rollouts.
- H2 FY27 operational seasonality and growth acceleration in retail APE.
- Yield curve (RFR) movements and their impact on VNB margins and embedded values.

- **ICICI Life Insurance Ltd:** Resilient H1 performance with 22.3% VNB growth and 26.0% margins, driven by a strategic pivot toward protection and annuity alongside disciplined opex reduction.
- **Life Insurance Corporation of India:** LIC delivered a strong H1 FY27 anchored by a 21.52% jump in PAT, a 470 bps expansion in VNB margins to 22.3%, and robust growth in non-par and protection segments despite muted ULIP performance.
- **Max Financial Services Ltd:** Max Financial reported strong H1 performance with 21% VNB growth and expects to navigate regulatory headwinds while maintaining a 25-26% VNB margin trajectory.
- **SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd:** SBI Life delivered robust 19% VoNB growth and 29.3% quarterly VoNB margins, backed by product diversification and strong solvency, while reiterating its 14%–15% annual growth target.
