### Highlights
- Marked 25 years of operations, launching 25 innovative solutions (including 5 industry-first and 7 industry-leading solutions) and health insurance product 'Vibe' for Gen-Z.
- Hosted Digital Day highlighting AI and Agentic AI integration; GenAI precision rates on leakage/fraud triggers improved from 45% to 60%; digital DIY customer interactions reached 73% vs 39% last year.
- WhatsApp-based GenAI bot used in over 40% of agency new business daily for health underwriting.
- GST Council recommended removal of restrictions on Input Tax Credit (ITC) on Group-Health and Life Insurance.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects motor sales to continue surpassing previous trends going into the festive season, though growth may moderate on a higher base.
- Management remains optimistic about H2 in commercial lines and expects to maintain momentum in revenue growth, earned premiums, and retail health outperformance.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic economic growth remains firm, supported by private consumption, manufacturing, and investment spending.
- Automobile sector witnessed double-digit growth; EV penetration across PVs and 2Ws surged 1.5x.
- General insurance industry GDPI growth steady at 8.9% in H1 FY2027 (12.6% excluding Crop and Mass Health).
- Fire insurance segment continues to de-grow, though the pace of decline is moderating.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industry motor combined ratio deteriorated to 131.4% in Q1 FY2027, creating a 23.1 percentage point delta versus ICICI Lombard's motor combined ratio (108.3% in Q1 FY2027).
- ICICI Lombard motor combined ratio movement in Q1 was impacted by the Shishupal Supreme Court judgment; management stated no additional reserves are required as it is now business-as-usual.
- Overall motor loss ratio targeted in the 65% to 67% band; Retail health loss ratio comfortable between 65% to 70%; Group health underwritten at mid-90s levels (97.9% in Q2 FY2027).

- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Industry underwriting performance remains under pressure with high combined ratios (industry motor combined ratio at 131.4%).
- CAT losses impacted Q2 performance (₹1.18 billion in Q2 FY2027 vs ₹0.73 billion in Q2 FY2026).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nischint Chawathe (Kotak) asked about the sequential change in Motor TP claims ratio and the impact of the Supreme Court verdict.** Management responded that the Q1 reserve provision (₹1.65 billion) fully addressed the Supreme Court judgment impact, and it is now integrated as business-as-usual with no further specific charges anticipated.
2. **Supratim Dutta (Jefferies) asked about Group Health and retail health loss ratios and IFRS accounting transition.** Management noted Group Health loss ratio improved sequentially to 97.9% in Q2 FY2027 (from 99.9% in Q1), and retail indemnity was 64.6%. On IFRS, management stated they will share details at a later point and recommended consistency in reporting formats.
3. **Nidhesh (Avendus Spark) asked about the consultation paper's impact on renewals/CV business and rationale for growing CV given flat pricing.** Management stated that retention has improved by 3% to 5% across retail lines, and CV mix is strategically managed within a 20% to 25% band where it creates ROE value.
4. **Sanket Godha (Abans / Spark) asked about Motor OD loss ratio drivers and reinsurance accepted business.** Management clarified that Q2 OD loss ratios were impacted by flood/CAT events (₹1.18 billion total), and excluding CAT, OD loss ratios improved sequentially. Reinsurance accepted is maintained at a comfortable single-digit mix.
5. **Madhukar (JPMorgan) asked if Motor loss ratios will trend back to historical bands and if commercial lines deterioration has peaked.** Management confirmed overall motor loss ratio range remains 65% to 67%, and commercial lines trajectory looks positive with denominator growth, though exposure-based CAT events remain unpredictable.
6. **Shobhit Sharma (Elara Securities) asked about EV impact on OD loss ratios and competitive intensity.** Management stated EV book constitutes high single digits (7%-8%) with OD loss ratios remaining even-steven versus non-EV books, and noted industry motor combined ratio of 131.4% is unsustainable, necessitating market corrections.

### One-line takeaway
ICICI Lombard delivered a resilient Q2 FY2027 performance with PAT growing 27.5% YoY to ₹10.31 billion, supported by strong retail health outperformance, sequential recovery in commercial lines, and superior underwriting discipline relative to industry pressures.
