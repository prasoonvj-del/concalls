# HDFC Life Insurance Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-22 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Business is seeing broad-based growth, normalization of competitive pressures in key channels, and strong momentum in protection, annuity, and agency segments.

### Key numbers
- **Individual APE growth YoY:** 7%
- **WRP growth YoY:** 8%
- **Overall APE growth YoY:** 9%
- **Retail private market share:** 16.3%
- **Retail protection growth YoY:** 42%
- **Agency channel growth YoY:** 21%
- **Non-bank alliances retail protection growth YoY:** 60%
- **Product Mix (Individual APE):** ULIPs 44%, Non-par savings 22% (run-rate close to 25%), Participating products 15%, Retail protection 8%, Annuities 11%
- **Value of New Business (VNB):** Rs 879 crore (up 9% YoY)
- **New Business Margins (NBM):** 25% (up 100 bps sequentially vs Q4; residual GST impact at 60 bps)
- **Renewal collections growth YoY:** 19%
- **13-month persistency:** 84%
- **61st month persistency:** 65% (up over 150 bps)
- **Assets under Management (AUM):** Exceeded Rs 4 lakh crore (HDFC Pension AUM at approx Rs 1.75 lakh crore, up 33% YoY with 43% market share)
- **Solvency Ratio:** 185% (post HDFC Bank preferential capital issuance)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 611 crore (up 12% YoY, or 17% excluding GST impact)
- **Embedded Value (EV):** Rs 65,860 crore (5-year absolute EV accretion compounded at 18%)
- **Credit protect growth YoY:** 19%
- **Banca beyond HDFC Bank growth YoY:** 15%

### Highlights
- Growth was broad-based across all channels excluding HDFC Bank, which grew at 17%, led by agency and non-bank alliances.
- HDFC Bank channel volumes were subdued due to a large base, but account share within the bank improved through the quarter, trending closer to previous year levels.
- Variable annuity proposition launched in Q4 FY26 accounts for just under half of the annuity mix.
- New branches opened in the agency channel over the past 24 months contribute nearly 16% to agency APE.
- Additional sub-debt capacity of Rs 500 crore available for solvency.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains aspirations to grow in line with or faster than the industry and deliver VNB growth broadly in line with APE growth.
- Margins are expected to remain range-bound at current levels (~25%) as management prioritizes growth over margin expansion.
- Non-par savings run-rate expected to hold around mid-20s.
- Protection growth rates may moderate in H2 as tailwinds normalize.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Macro environment is constructive for long-term savings and protection.
- Customer preference is gradually shifting towards guaranteed return products amidst evolving market conditions.
- Demand for unit-linked products remained resilient despite market volatility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Residual GST impact on margins is 60 bps; management remains on track to fully neutralize it over coming quarters.
- Agency channel profitability aided by term and annuity share rising from 15% to 27%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Branch expansion in agency channel focused on Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets for deepening reach; expansion slowed down selectively.
- A typical branch takes 12 to 18 months in larger markets and 18 to 30 months in smaller markets to break even.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Solvency ratio stands at 185%, providing a 15–18 month runway with current capital and sub-debt capacity before transition to Risk-Based Capital (RBC).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical escalations, oil price volatility, and El Nino are external watchpoints.
- 13-month persistency moderated by 200 bps to 84% due to specific cohorts and softer collections in unit-linked products.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Avinash (Emkay Global) on margin evolution vs peers and growth confidence:** Management attributed historical margin variations to regulatory shifts (withdrawal of tax exemptions above Rs 5 lakh, surrender charge changes, and GST), and noted confidence in channel growth independent of HDFC Bank.
2. **Shreya Shivani (Nomura) on product-wise outlook and agency expansion:** Management stated product mix will remain balanced with non-par in mid-20s and protection stable, while agency growth is a bottoms-up funnel across Tier 1, 2, and 3 markets.
3. **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark) on HDFC Bank market share and par slowdown:** Management clarified that market share in HDFC Bank has recovered to prior levels as irrational competitor pricing mellowed down.
4. **Swarnabh Mukherjee (360 One Capital) on variable annuity opportunity and solvency runway:** Management highlighted variable annuity appeals to slightly younger demographics and current solvency provides an 15-18 month runway.
5. **Nischint (Kotak) on non-bank alliances and term tailwinds:** Management noted non-bank alliance growth is primarily driven by protection business from aggregators and post-GST re-organization.
6. **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal) on industry growth assumptions and VNB guidance:** Management assumed a 15-17% industry growth base case, stating VNB growth will track APE while margins remain range-bound near current levels.

### One-line takeaway
HDFC Life delivered a stable Q1 FY27 with 9% APE growth and a 25% NBM, driven by strong traction in agency, protection, and variable annuities, alongside a recovery of market share within HDFC Bank.
