# HDFC Life Insurance Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-22 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong broad-based growth across non-bank channels, VNB expansion, and recovery in bank counter share despite muted total volume at HDFC Bank.

---

### Key numbers
* **Individual APE Growth:** 7% YoY (WRP grew 8% YoY; 2-year CAGR at 10%).
* **Overall APE Growth:** 9% YoY.
* **Value of New Business (VNB):** Rs 879 crore (up 9% YoY; 2-year CAGR at 11%).
* **New Business Margin (VNB Margin):** 25.0% (up 100 bps QoQ vs Q4 FY26).
* **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 611 crore (up 12% YoY; up 17% YoY excluding GST impact).
* **Embedded Value (EV):** Rs 65,860 crore (5-year CAGR of 18%).
* **Assets Under Management (AUM):** Exceeded Rs 4,000,000 crore (Rs 4 trillion).
* **Solvency Ratio:** 185% (following preferential capital issuance by HDFC Bank).
* **Persistency:** 13th month at 84% (down ~200 bps YoY); 61st month at 65% (up >150 bps YoY).
* **Product Mix (Individual APE):** Unit-linked (ULIP) 44%, Non-par savings 22%, Participating 15%, Annuities 11%, Retail protection 8%.

---

### Highlights
* **Retail Market Share:** Retail private market share stood at 16.3%.
* **Channel Performance:** Non-HDFC Bank channels grew collectively at 17% YoY. Agency grew 21% YoY, and proprietary channels grew >20% YoY. 
* **HDFC Bank Channel:** Volume was flat/subdued YoY due to a large base, but counter share within the bank improved through the quarter, trending back to Q1 FY26 levels. HDFC Bank contributed 47% of retail APE in Q1 FY27.
* **Product Performance:** Retail protection grew 42% YoY. Credit Protect grew 19% YoY, driven by recovery in the microfinance (MFI) segment. Variable annuity accounted for nearly half of the annuity mix.
* **Subsidiaries:** HDFC Pension Fund Management AUM reached ~Rs 1,75,000 crore (up 33% YoY) with a 43% market share.

---

### Guidance & outlook
* **FY27 Growth Target:** Aspirations maintained to grow in line with or faster than the industry (industry growth estimated at 15%-17%) for full-year APE.
* **VNB & Margins:** Guidance maintained to deliver VNB growth broadly in line with APE growth; margins expected to remain range-bound around current levels (~25%), prioritizing growth over margin expansion.
* **Product Mix Outlook:** Expect ULIP mix to remain stable, non-par savings share to improve gradually into the mid-20s, annuity share to remain higher than last year, and protection growth to moderate slightly in H2 due to high base effects.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* Macroeconomic environment is favorable for long-term savings and protection.
* Customer preference is showing a gradual shift towards long-term guaranteed return products amid financial market volatility and a favorable yield curve.
* Irrational competitive pricing in non-par savings across banking channels has moderated, creating a level playing field.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* New business margin was impacted by residual GST effects of ~60 bps in Q1 FY27 (down from 110 bps entering the year after absorbing 50 bps in Q1). Management expects to fully neutralize the remaining 60 bps in upcoming quarters.
* Negative assumption change impacted VNB margin by 40 bps upfront to align with persistency variances observed in prior periods.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Over 700 branches across 600+ cities are operational. 
* Agency branches opened in the last 24 months contributed 16% to total agency APE in Q1 FY27 (up from high single digits last year).
* Branch breakeven timeline: 12–18 months for larger markets and 18–30 months for Tier 2/3 markets. Network expansion has been deliberately slowed down to focus on deepening existing locations.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Solvency ratio improved to 185% following a Rs 1,000 crore preferential capital issuance to HDFC Bank.
* Additional sub-debt capacity of Rs 500 crore provides a potential +4% boost to solvency. Management comfortable with a 15–18 month capital runway under the current growth pace while awaiting the Risk-Based Capital (RBC) framework transition.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **Persistency Moderation:** 13th-month persistency dropped to 84%, primarily due to smaller ticket sizes following tax law changes, softer ULIP collections, and lower March collection windows.
* **Geopolitical Risks:** Global escalations tracking oil price impacts, along with potential El Nino weather conditions, remain active monitorables.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A

* **Avinash (Emkay Global):** Why have margins remained relatively flat over a 7-8 year horizon despite scale and favorable mix?
  * **Management Response:** Structural margins continuously expanded from FY16 to FY22, but were subsequently hit by three major regulatory/tax impacts: tax cap on >Rs 5 lakh non-par policies (90 bps impact), revised surrender value rules (~100 bps impact), and GST absorption (~300 bps total impact, 60 bps remaining). Excluding these, inherent margins expanded.

* **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark):** Is the share recovery in HDFC Bank back to mid-60s counter share levels?
  * **Management Response:** Irrational competitive pricing from peers in the channel has ended. HDFC Bank counter share has returned to early Q1 FY26 levels, and management is selectively focusing on non-par, par, and protection products rather than chasing headline market share in ULIPs.

* **Nischint (Kotak):** What is driving the sharp growth in non-bank alliances?
  * **Management Response:** Growth was primarily driven by retail protection business through web aggregators, alongside base effects post-GST adjustments.

* **Nidhesh Jain (Investec):** What explains the drop in traditional business persistency to 83-84% and is this the new steady state?
  * **Management Response:** Drop was driven by lower ticket sizes post tax rule changes, policy design issues (which have since been corrected), and seasonality. Persistency is expected to settle around 84%–85%.

* **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal):** What industry growth rate is assumed, and how will VNB and margin trade-offs be managed?
  * **Management Response:** Base case assumes 15% industry growth; company needs ~16% growth over the remaining 9 months to hit industry levels. Margins will remain range-bound near current levels (25%), with growth being prioritized over margin expansion.

---

### One-line takeaway
HDFC Life delivered steady Q1 FY27 earnings with 9% VNB growth and 25% margins, supported by strong agency momentum, retail protection demand, and recovering counter share within HDFC Bank.
