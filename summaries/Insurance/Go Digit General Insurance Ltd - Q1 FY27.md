# Go Digit General Insurance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · Insurance*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management chose to prioritize profitability over growth in a soft market with declining premium rates and rising claims costs.

### Key numbers
- **Gross Written Premium (GWP):** Down 8% YoY (or down 2% excluding crop and group health reinsurance inward where pricing was unattractive).
- **Net Earned Premium (NEP):** Up 8% YoY to INR 2,007 crore.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT with DAC, excluding discounting and MTM):** INR 190 crore (vs. INR 200 crore a year ago, a 5% drop).
- **Combined Ratio (with DAC):** 107.2% (Loss ratio: 73.3%, up from 70.3% in Q1 previous year; own damage loss ratio increase driven by private cars).
- **Combined Ratio (with discounting):** 104.3%.
- **Solvency Ratio:** 2.43x.
- **Net Worth (IGAAP):** INR 4,674 crore (up from ~INR 4,600 crore as of March).
- **Net Worth (IFRS basis):** INR 8,200 crore (vs. INR 7,600 crore as of March 31).
- **ROE (non-annualized, on NEP with DAC without discounting/MTM):** 4.1% (vs. 4.9% in the previous quarter).
- **Motor Market Share:** 5.58% (or 5.6%) compared to 6.25% for the full year FY26.
- **Motor Segment Growth:** Flat at 0.4% (2-wheeler new business grew 26% with collected premium at INR 546 crore vs INR 433 crore YoY; Commercial Vehicles at -27%).
- **Long-term Advance Premium:** INR 3,387 crore (Motor: INR 2,879 crore, Non-motor: INR 508 crore).
- **Total DAC Pretax (as of June 30, 2026):** INR 2,609 crore.
- **Real IFRS PAT (including MTM and discounting):** INR 372 crore (vs. INR 325 crore last quarter).
- **Fire Business De-growth:** -37% (Industry de-grew by -27% to -28%). Gross fire loss ratio at 67% in Q1.
- **Investment / AUM:** Equity asset allocation at 9.5% of AUM (sitting at INR 268 crore of unrealized gains). Fixed income unrealized gains at ~INR 220–225 crore. Lower fixed income yield due to higher equity allocation led to a INR 14 crore interest income loss.

### Highlights
- First multiline insurer in India to declare results under Indian Accounting Standards (IFRS) as per proforma prescribed by IRDAI.
- Consciously stepped back from growth in private car SAOD, non-new cars, commercial vehicles, fire, crop, and group health due to unattractive pricing and rising claims.
- Decided to protect treaty profitability in fire rather than maximize reinsurance commission; retained fire loss ratios are protected on a gross basis.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not give formal guidance.
- Management expects own damage loss ratios to stabilize in the second quarter following corrective underwriting actions taken in February, April, and May.
- Management is operating under the assumption that soft market conditions and pricing pressures will continue through March 31, 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Zero increase in third-party motor rates for 5 years while claims costs (parts, paint, labour, TP wages) continue to rise due to inflation.
- Expense of Management (EOM) rules pushed commissions up and drove companies to write crop and group health at lower rates.
- Industry profit for top players over the last 3 years heavily dependent (~40%) on capital gains.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Overall loss ratio increased to 73.3% (from 70.3% YoY), primarily driven by the private car own damage portfolio.
- Tax rate under Indian Accounting Standards is 25.2%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Equity asset allocation reached 9.5% of AUM (close to the 10% first-step target).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Fixed income duration moved from 4.5 to 4.9 in Q1; reinvestment yield at 7.8%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inflationary pressures on claim severity (spare parts, labor, paint, and third-party wages linked to minimum wage revisions).
- Supreme Court judgment on third-party claims (Shishupal case) impacting valuations, though high courts (Allahabad and Karnataka) have subsequently delivered contrasting interpretations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Supratim Datta (ICICI Securities) on growth slowdown post-listing vs historical rates:** Management stated that the slow growth is a deliberate choice of discipline rather than shrinkage due to market forces, prioritizing profitability over growth in an unfavorable rate environment.
2. **Supratim Datta on the Supreme Court ruling (11th June) regarding TP and large losses in Fire/Marine:** Management explained their reserving methodology includes legal inflation and minimum wage hikes, noted aggressive compromise settlements (83% historically settled via compromise), and highlighted recent divergent High Court rulings.
3. **Avinash Smith (Emkay Global) on industry profitability and commission pressures:** Management noted that the IRDAI Chairman's recent interviews indicate upcoming distribution reforms and commission reviews, while reiterating Digit's focus on underwriting fundamentals rather than chasing market music.
4. **Ansuman Deb (ICICI Securities) on TP price hike scope and dividend policy:** Management indicated that 90%-95% of vehicle segments warrant a TP rate hike, and stated that dividend payouts will be evaluated in Q4 based on IGAAP surpluses and upcoming Risk-Based Capital (RBC) norms.
5. **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark) on TP court-settled claims and long-term premium unwinding:** Management clarified that 83% of 36,000 historical claims were settled via compromise and only 17% via court awards, emphasizing consistent conservative reserving.
6. **Nidhesh (Investec) on why motor OD loss ratios rose to 73%-74% despite historical foresight:** Management (Kamesh Goyal) openly owned the failure of waiting too long to tighten private car SAOD renewals before implementing corrective cuts.

### One-line takeaway
Go Digit reported a soft, intentionally disciplined quarter with a 5% drop in core PAT and a flat GWP (-8% headline, -2% ex-crop/health), prioritizing underwriting profitability over growth amid a challenging soft market.
