# Mold-Tek Packaging Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sales volumes grew by 10.1% YoY, profits jumped by 36% YoY, and EBITDA per kg reached a healthy INR46.5.

### Key numbers
- Sales volume growth: +10.1% YoY (Q2), +8% for H1.
- EBITDA per kg: INR46.5 (up from ~INR39 in last Q2).
- Pharma segment volume growth: +26.3% YoY.
- Food and FMCG volume growth: +23.7% YoY.
- Paints volume growth: ~9% YoY (value-wise up by 26%).
- Lubricant pack volume growth: -12.3% decline YoY.
- H1 EBITDA: ~INR100 crores plus.
- H1 Operating cash flow: ~INR93 crores.
- Full-year EBITDA guidance: ~INR250 crores (compared to INR173 crores last year).
- Full-year capex guidance: ~INR75 crores (INR60 crores already spent in first 7 months).
- Full-year cash flow from operations target: INR215 crores (aiming to double from INR107 crores).
- IML contribution: 77% of volume, 78.25% of value.

### Highlights
- Consolidation of Hyderabad units into a single facility and clubbing production units at the Sultanpur unit resulted in considerable cost savings and improved efficiencies.
- Iodex balm packs project cleared after a 3 years hold; commercial production expected to start in December or January, running into millions of pieces a month.
- 3 new products under development for 3 MNCs to be launched by end of December or January.
- Panipat plant started picking up clients and numbers are getting added; expected to witness good numbers starting next season in Jan/Feb.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of maintaining EBITDA per kg around the INR45–INR46 range for the full year.
- Full-year volume growth target remains double-digit (aiming towards 10%).
- Pharma segment turnover target: INR50+ crores for the full year (reached INR24 crores in H1), and INR70 crores - INR75 crores for next financial year.
- Vibe specialty closures top line targeted at $5 million to $6 million (INR50 crores to INR60 crores) with ~30% EBITDA margin 2 years down the line.
- Short-term finance cost expected to be around 4.5% - 4.7% of total revenue for the year.
- Short-term goal for EBITDA per kg: INR50 in the next 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Paint industry showing healthy growth (8% to 10% general growth); Cheyyar plant started taking orders from nearby South clients.
- Lubricant segment continues to be a drag due to input issues faced by major private lube companies and Mold-Tek not serving the public sector.
- Food, FMCG, and Qpack demand growing rapidly with light thin-wall section products adding more value to revenues and EBITDA.
- Semiconductor trays demand expected to grow significantly starting around '28-'29 in India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices went up by 60% in the last 6 months, but customers accepted price hikes, enabling better bottom-line performance.
- Inventory holding time is 52 days (raw material ~25 days; SFG and FG ~25-27 days).
- Finance cost moved up from 4.1% to nearly 5% due to higher utilization of working capital for higher raw material prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capacity expected to reach 70,000 tonnes by the beginning of the next financial year (from 66,900 tonnes).
- Newly registered land in Sultanpur B block will be available for future growth (auto-injector pens or semiconductor trays).
- Phase 1 investment for Vibe estimated at INR10 crores to INR15 crores in molds and machinery, targeted to yield INR40 crores to INR50 crores turnover.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash surplus expected by the end of the year; long-term debt or overall debt expected to start coming down.
- Dividend outflows and capex (INR75 crores - INR80 crores) will be comfortably met by cash inflows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Lubricant segment continues to face a decline due to client input issues.
- Exports are constrained due to increasing freight costs and geopolitical conflicts.
- Raw material prices remain elevated due to West Asia conflicts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shirish Pardeshi (Motilal Oswal) on paint volume growth:** Management clarified paint volumes grew 8.6% (value up 26%), aligning with industry growth, and expected to touch double digits in H2.
- **Kaushal Sharma (Equinox Capital Ventures) on pharma/semiconductor timeline:** Management stated ophthalmic eye dropper packs will begin commercial production by March; semiconductor trays are targeted for '28-'29 with preliminary talks underway with top semiconductor companies and visits to China/Korea planned.
- **Chirag (Keynote) on lubricant segment drag:** Management explained the 15% H1 decline is due to input issues at major private lube clients (Mold-Tek does not serve the public sector).
- **Chirag (Keynote) on capacity utilization:** Management noted that 70% utilization is healthy and anything beyond 75% is difficult in injection molding due to mold complexities.
- **Richa Agarwal (Equitymaster) on raw material price pass-through:** Management confirmed customers readily accepted price hikes of 60% in raw materials, protecting margins and driving profit growth.
- **Tushar (Omega Portfolio Advisors) on EBITDA/kg aspirations:** Management affirmed an internal short-term goal of INR50 EBITDA/kg over the next 2-3 years, aided by pharma, FMCG, Vibe, and potentially semiconductors.

### One-line takeaway
Mold-Tek delivered robust Q2 FY27 performance with a 36% jump in profits and higher EBITDA per kg (INR46.5), driven by strong growth in pharma and food/FMCG segments offsetting declines in lubricants.
