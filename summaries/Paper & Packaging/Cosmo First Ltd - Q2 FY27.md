# Cosmo First Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated sales grew 51% YoY and EBITDA rose 38% YoY, driven by higher specialty volumes, improved base margins, and strong performance in the US and new B2B verticals.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained target of reducing net debt by Rs. 300-500 crore over the next two years. US market target updated to exceed $100 million next year.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to acknowledge ongoing port-related challenges and high volatility linked to the West Asia crisis, though film demand remains supportive.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Non-recurring items discussed in detail (US customs duty refund write-back of Rs. 24.8 crore and inventory loss of Rs. 7 crore). Port congestion remains a monitored risk.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Two new specialty assets (16,000 MT coating line and 9-layer barrier sheet line) remain on track for commissioning in H2 FY27 with capex largely included in Q2 financials.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated sales: Rs. 1,388 crore (up 51% YoY)
- Sales volume growth: 15% YoY (Specialty volume up 20% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 177 crore (up 38% YoY from Rs. 128 crore in Q2 FY25)
- Normalized EBITDA (excluding Rs. 24.8 crore US customs refund write-back and Rs. 7 crore inventory loss): Rs. 159 crore (up 24% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 12.8% (vs 14.0% in Q2 FY25)
- BOPP gross margin: Rs. 29 per kg (vs Rs. 22 in Q2 FY25 and Rs. 30 in Q1 FY27)
- BOPET gross margin: Rs. 12 per kg (vs Rs. 6 in Q2 FY25 and Rs. 9 in Q1 FY27)
- Specialty film margin: Broadly stable at close to Rs. 62 per kg
- Specialty Chemicals H1 FY27 revenue: ~Rs. 155 crore (up 59% YoY)
- Cosmo Plastech H1 FY27 revenue: Rs. 75 crore (up 73% YoY); Q2 revenue Rs. 39 crore (up 90% YoY) with 8% EBITDA margin
- Cosmo Consumer H1 FY27 revenue: Up 2.6x YoY; Q2 revenue up 70% YoY with EBITDA loss of Rs. 4.5 crore
- Zigly H1 FY27 revenue growth: 58% YoY; Q2 revenue growth 47% YoY; 48 centers including 3 hospitals
- Net debt: Rs. 1,057 crore as of September 2026 (1.9x EBITDA), down from Rs. 1,159 crore at March-end
- Full year FY27 capex guidance: Rs. 100 to Rs. 110 crore (H1 capex at ~Rs. 90 crore)

### Highlights
- All B2B businesses continue to be profitable, with specialty chemicals and rigid packaging showing strong revenue and margin expansion.
- US film business grew 35% YoY in volume terms, expected to reach ~$70 million this year and targeted to exceed $100 million next year.
- Renewable power share expected to reach approximately two-thirds of total requirements by next year, generating estimated annual savings of Rs. 25 crore to Rs. 30 crore.
- Film capacity utilization is running at approximately 90%.
- Special specialty lines (16,000 MT coating line and 10,000 MT 9-layer barrier sheet line) slated for commissioning in H2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Specialty volume mix target: 60% in FY27, 65%-70% next year, and ~80% in the medium term.
- Net debt reduction target: Rs. 300-500 crore over the next two years.
- Next year (FY28) capex expected to be moderate at Rs. 50-70 crore (maintenance and minor specialty growth capex).
- Consumer businesses (Zigly and Cosmo Consumer) projected to maintain >60% growth.
- Specialty chemicals medium-term revenue target of close to Rs. 500 crore with 20%+ ROCE.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q3 typically has a seasonality aspect (Christmas holidays in exports, Diwali festival in India) impacting margins up or down.
- India is poised to consume 1.5 to 2 new big BOPP lines every year.
- PPF market in India estimated at ~Rs. 500 crore, growing at ~30% YoY with low penetration (1%-2%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Revenue grew 51% on 15% volume growth, driven by raw material price pass-throughs.
- Q2 EBITDA impacted by a non-recurring US customs duty refund write-back of ~Rs. 24.8 crore and a Rs. 7 crore inventory loss due to raw material price drops.
- Specialty chemicals subsidiary posted 23% EBITDA margin in Q2.
- Consumer business margins impacted by upfront TV advertising and brand-building investments started in July 2026.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- High investment capex cycle of Rs. 1,250 to 1,300 crore over the last three years is complete; upcoming assets will leverage existing investments.
- 16,000 MT high-end chemical coated film line and 10,000 MT 9-layer barrier sheet line launching in H2 FY27; capex largely accounted for in Q2 financials.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced to Rs. 1,057 crore (1.9x EBITDA) from Rs. 1,159 crore at March-end, despite a Rs. 148 crore increase in net working capital due to higher raw material prices post-West Asia war.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High operating volatility driven by West Asia conflict, petroleum market volatility, and global uncertainties.
- Port-related shipping/container congestion challenges reappeared during the quarter.
- Raw material prices remain volatile (increased further in November).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nirav Jimudia (Anvil Wealth) asked about US topline growth potential and BOPP capacity utilization/timelines.** Management stated US is targeted to exceed $100 million next year, capacity utilization is ~90% (8%-10% room left), and new BOPP lines across the industry come over two years while India absorbs 1.5-2 lines yearly.
2. **Aman Agarwal asked for clarification on BOPP base spreads matching Q1 and full-year capex.** Management clarified reported spreads were Rs. 29/kg (vs Rs. 30/kg in Q1) and guided FY27 capex to Rs. 100-110 crore, with FY28 estimated at Rs. 50-70 crore.
3. **Aman Kumar Sonthalia asked about third-party vs internal chemical sales and consumer business sequential growth slowdown.** Management noted third-party chemical sales were 17% (moving toward 50-50 long-term) and explained consumer businesses faced summer/monsoon seasonality in Q2.
4. **Saransh Gupta asked about the timeline for consumer business breakeven and specialty mix levers.** Management stated marketing investments will continue for 2 years with losses tapering off; without marketing, the business is close to operational break-even.
5. **Aryan asked about additional revenue potential and value addition for the two new H2 FY27 lines.** Management stated coating adds US$0.70 to US$1.00 per kg and barrier sheets add US$0.70 per kg over base film, though customer approvals will take time.

### One-line takeaway
Cosmo First delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 51% revenue growth and 38% EBITDA growth, anchored by robust specialty volumes, profitable B2B verticals, and active deleveraging.
