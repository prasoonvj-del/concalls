# Dhaval Packaging Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 results discussed)
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted profit growth outstripping revenue growth, debt reduction post-IPO, and progress on new high-margin product lines despite geopolitical headwinds in End Caps.

### Key numbers
- **IPO Details:** Listed on BSE SME platform on August 6, 2026; issued 37,48,800 fresh shares at Rs 97/share, raising Rs 36.30 crore.
- **H1 FY27 Financials:**
  - Revenue: Stated as Rs 24.94 crore (up from Rs 34.64 crore reported in prior period context, with total segment sales summing to ~Rs 34.94 crore; up 15% vs previous 6 months).
  - EBITDA: Rs 7.83 crore, up 21% YoY from Rs 6.45 crore.
  - Net Profit (PAT): Rs 4.72 crore, up 25% YoY from Rs 3.79 crore.
  - EPS: Rs 4.20 per share for the half-year.
  - Net Margin: ~13.50% compared to ~11% in the prior year period.
- **Segment Performance (H1 FY27):**
  - **IML & Plastic Products:** Revenue Rs 26.01 crore (up ~4% YoY, up ~17% vs previous 6 months); segment profit Rs 5.78 crore (up 39% YoY from Rs 4.15 crore).
  - **End Caps:** Revenue Rs 8.79 crore (down ~7% YoY, up ~8% vs previous 6 months); segment profit Rs 2.47 crore (down ~19% YoY from Rs 3.50 crore).
  - **Filling Machines:** Recorded first-ever revenue of Rs 14 lakh.

### Highlights
- Core In-Mold Labelling (IML) business serves major FMCG clients including ITC, Vadilal, Havmor, and Sheetal Ice Cream; company is in process of signing a contract with Vadilal.
- One machine, one mold strategy executed to optimize plant productivity and boost profitability.
- Introduced two-piece DRD metal cans, positioning as the first company to launch this product line in India.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a 35%-40% revenue hike in Q1/H1 of FY27/FY28 driven by the upcoming metal packaging line.
- Q4 IML demand is expected to see strong growth YoY due to seasonal summer demand for ice cream containers (January–June).
- Management declined to provide revenue or growth guidance beyond FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rising prices for traditional sweets (Mithai) are driving customer demand for superior, branded packaging solutions in India.
- In-Mold Labelling (IML) remains the main growth driver supported by long-standing customer relationships spanning 25 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion in IML was driven by smart bulk procurement of imported plastic granules at favorable prices while keeping selling prices aligned with market rates.
- Raw material cost increases are passed on to customers; packaging cost is a minimal component relative to end-product value (e.g., Rs 23-25 container for Rs 1,200–1,500 sweets).
- Management expects metal packaging to yield significantly higher profit margins than the IML business due to its specialized nature.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Spent Rs 9.18 crore on new plant, machinery, and buildings during H1 FY27.
- New manufacturing facility is 75%-80% complete and is scheduled to be operational by March 2027.
- Ordered advanced machinery for two-piece DRD metal packaging lines, scheduled to launch by March 2027.
- In talks with prospective clients in Latin America and the United Kingdom for multi-machine (4 to 6 machines), year-round IML projects; if finalized, management may commence production early on rented premises prior to plant completion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth doubled from ~Rs 31 crore (March 2026) to ~Rs 67 crore (September 2026).
- Bank loans reduced from Rs 24.13 crore to Rs 20.74 crore; debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.31x from 0.79x over the past 6 months.
- Customer receivables increased from ~Rs 8 crore to ~Rs 13 crore in H1 FY27, impacting operational cash flows, driven by seasonal pre-Diwali sales; management expects fast collections post-Diwali.
- Top 10 customers account for approximately 55%-60% of total company revenue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- End Caps segment performance hit by external geopolitical factors, specifically the Iran conflict impacting pipe exports to Saudi Arabia and the Middle East, along with elevated international container shipping freight rates.
- Working capital stretched temporarily due to higher receivables at the end of the half-year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mit Gada (Balaji Capital):** What are the key business segments?
  - *Management answer:* Key segments are In-Mold Labelling (serving FMCG) and End Caps (serving global SAW pipe manufacturers), with metal packaging coming up.
- **Hrishit Jhaveri (Capital Bridge Advisors):** What is the status of the new facility and expected peak revenue?
  - *Management answer:* Facility is 75%-80% complete, scheduled for March 2027 completion, and expected to drive a 35%-40% revenue hike.
- **Hrishit Jhaveri (Capital Bridge Advisors):** Can raw material cost increases be passed on without hurting H2 margins?
  - *Management answer:* Yes, bulk granule imports keep input costs low, and customers readily accept price pass-throughs given packaging cost is minor relative to their retail product price.
- **Uttam Purohit:** What is the timeline and backup plan for new IML capacity?
  - *Management answer:* Ordered machines for March 2027 operationalization; if UK/Latin America deals finalize sooner, production will start on rented premises immediately.
- **Shashi Kalwani:** What is the margin profile expected for the new metal line?
  - *Management answer:* Margins will be much higher than IML because it is a first-in-India product, though exact figures will be disclosed post-commissioning in FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Dhaval Packaging delivers strong H1 FY27 profit growth post-IPO debt reduction while expanding into high-margin metal packaging to offset geopolitical softness in its End Caps segment.
