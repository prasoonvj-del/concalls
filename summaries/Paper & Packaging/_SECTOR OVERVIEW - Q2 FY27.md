# Paper & Packaging: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the sector report strong top-line momentum driven by volume growth, specialty product mix improvement, and geographic diversification. Most companies posted double-digit revenue growth, with several recording highest-ever quarterly sales. Margin trajectories diverge: some expanded EBITDA margins through pricing power and operating leverage, while others absorbed sharp raw material cost spikes with a 1-2 quarter pass-through lag. Geopolitical instability (West Asia conflict) and logistics disruptions are universally cited as near-term risks to input costs and shipping. Capex cycles remain elevated, focused on high-value packaging, recycling, and new adjacencies like battery separator films.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Indian packaging has historically monetized commoditized flexible films, paperboard, and rigid containers sold to FMCG and pharma customers on cost-plus contracts. Over the last ~5 years, the model has shifted toward specialty films, sustainable substrates, and value-added converting, with leading players integrating backward into raw materials and forward into design-to-delivery services while expanding overseas to de-risk domestic cyclicality [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **Specialty/value-added mix increase** | scaling | Cosmo First targeting 60% specialty mix in FY27 (80% medium-term) [Cosmo First concall Q2 FY27]; EPL Beauty & Cosmetics now 44% of portfolio [EPL concall Q2 FY27]; Kuantum Papers targeting 25-30% specialty revenue in 12-15 months [Kuantum Papers concall Q2 FY27]; Uflex targeting 50% value-added by FY29 [Uflex concall Q2 FY27]; TCPL entering battery separators [TCPL Packaging concall Q2 FY27].
- **Global expansion driving growth** | scaling | Uflex overseas ops contributed 56% of incremental revenue and 74% of incremental EBITDA [Uflex concall Q2 FY27]; Cosmo First US film business grew 35% YoY, targeting >$100M next year [Cosmo First concall Q2 FY27]; EPL broad-based growth across all regions [EPL concall Q2 FY27]; Knack Packaging exports to 75 countries [Knack Packaging concall Q2 FY27]; Dhaval Packaging in talks for Latin America/UK projects [Dhaval Packaging concall Q2 FY27].
- **Sustainable packaging & circular economy** | early | Huhtamaki sustainable tubes at 41% of mix [Huhtamaki India concall Q2 FY27]; Uflex commissioned 39,600 MTPA recycling plant [Uflex concall Q2 FY27]; Cosmo First renewable power to reach two-thirds of requirement [Cosmo First concall Q2 FY27]; EPL sustainable tubes 41% [EPL concall Q2 FY27]; Kuantum clonal plantations >22,000 acres [Kuantum Papers concall Q2 FY27].
- **Backward integration for raw material security** | early | Kuantum Papers lignin precipitation system and clonal propagation target 1 crore clones by 2030 [Kuantum Papers concall Q2 FY27]; Cosmo First specialty chemicals revenue ~Rs 155 crore H1 [Cosmo First concall Q2 FY27]; Uflex recycling unit operational [Uflex concall Q2 FY27]; TCPL battery separator wet-process coating [TCPL Packaging concall Q2 FY27].
- **New adjacencies (EV battery, metal packaging, consumer brands)** | early | TCPL incorporated Energy Materials for battery separators [TCPL Packaging concall Q2 FY27]; Dhaval launching two-piece DRD metal cans [Dhaval Packaging concall Q2 FY27]; Cosmo Consumer and Zigly scaling [Cosmo First concall Q2 FY27]; Knack Mexico JV ramping WPP bags [Knack Packaging concall Q2 FY27]; Seshaasai eSIM qualification underway [Seshaasai Technologies concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **5% GST on paper bags** (HSN 48194000) [Govt: Karnataka AAR, Nov 2026] – benefits paper-based packaging players (Kuantum, TCPL, Huhtamaki).
- **5% GST on biodegradable bags** subject to valid biodegradability claim [Govt: Gujarat AAR, Nov 2026] – supports sustainable packaging innovators (Huhtamaki, EPL, Cosmo).
- **Anti-dumping duty (ADD) / Minimum Import Price (MIP) representation** for writing/printing paper [Kuantum Papers concall Q2 FY27] – pending; would protect domestic paper makers (Kuantum, others).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: Broad-based volume growth across geographies and end-markets (FMCG, pharma, pet food, beauty & cosmetics). Export demand remains robust; domestic demand resilient despite macro headwinds. Most managements see H2 FY27 stronger on seasonality and price hikes.

**Divergence**:
- **Kuantum Papers**: Q2 lean season; expects upbeat Q3/Q4 with 7-11% price hikes.
- **Dhaval Packaging**: End Caps segment hit by Iran conflict disrupting pipe exports to Middle East.
- **Knack Packaging**: West Asia conflict spiked polypropylene prices 50% in weeks, but export volumes grew 13% YoY.
- **Huhtamaki India**: Outperformed broader customer trends (flat/declining volumes) with 7% YoY volume growth.
- **Cosmo First**: US market strong; port congestion and West Asia volatility create shipping uncertainty.
- **EPL**: Beauty & Cosmetics surpassed Oral Care (44% each); growth broad-based across categories.
- **Uflex**: Overseas demand strong; domestic shifting to higher-margin products, accepting lower base volumes.
- **TCPL Packaging**: Flexible packaging outperforming paperboard; Noida subsidiary weak on smartphone slump.
- **Seshaasai Technologies**: Payment Solutions strong; IoT moderation QoQ due to retail seasonality.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Consensus**: Raw material volatility (polypropylene, BOPET, chemicals) driven by West Asia conflict and crude prices. Pass-through lags of 1-2 quarters compress near-term margins; most expect recovery as price adjustments flow through.

**Divergence**:
- **Cosmo First**: BOPP gross margin Rs 29/kg (vs Rs 22 YoY); BOPET Rs 12/kg (vs Rs 6); specialty stable ~Rs 62/kg. Non-recurring US customs refund write-back (Rs 24.8 cr) and inventory loss (Rs 7 cr) hit reported EBITDA.
- **Knack Packaging**: Polymer prices surged from ~Rs 92 to Rs 143-160; EBITDA/kg fell to ~Rs 45 from ~Rs 54 in Q1; EBITDA margin contracted 285 bps to 16.35%.
- **Kuantum Papers**: Chemical costs up ~2% on sales; fuel up 3-3.5%; wood prices softened; wheat straw temporarily high.
- **EPL**: Fully passed on cost inflation; underlying EBITDA margin 20.4% (reported 18.9%).
- **Uflex**: Plain BOPET spreads consistent Rs 25-35/kg; normalized EBITDA margin expanded 550 bps to 15.6%.
- **Huhtamaki India**: Sudden RM inflation in September caused timing lag; comparable EBIT up 18% YoY excluding one-offs.
- **TCPL Packaging**: Margin expansion 100 bps to 17.7% from pricing, mix, operating leverage.
- **Dhaval Packaging**: Smart bulk procurement kept input costs low; pass-throughs accepted by customers.
- **Seshaasai Technologies**: Input costs rose faster than revenue YoY; gross margin expanded 89 bps QoQ to 42.5% on mix and operating leverage.

## Capex & capacity
**Consensus**: Elevated capex continuing for specialty capacity, geographic expansion, and new ventures. Several major projects nearing commissioning in H2 FY27.

**Key projects**:
- **Cosmo First**: 16,000 MT coating line + 10,000 MT 9-layer barrier sheet line commissioning H2 FY27; FY27 capex Rs 100-110 cr, FY28 Rs 50-70 cr.
- **Dhaval Packaging**: New Sanand-II facility 75-80% complete, operational by March 2027; metal packaging line launch March 2027.
- **EPL**: H1 capex INR 3,150M (+90% YoY); full-year INR 625-700 cr; Poland printing site delays (2-3 quarters to stabilize).
- **Huhtamaki India**: Baddi labels plant closed (volumes shifted); Daman land for sale; Khopoli solar plant operational (23% renewable electricity).
- **Knack Packaging**: Added 5,040 MT capacity August 2026; targeting 69-72k MT by Oct 2027; Mexico JV (400 MT/month installed) ramping to breakeven 120-130 MT.
- **Kuantum Papers**: Major modernization complete (PM3 commissioned Aug 2026); remaining CWIP Rs 139 cr for environmental projects; no major near-term capex.
- **TCPL Packaging**: Incorporated TCPL Energy Materials for battery separators (Phase 1: wet-process coating); flexible packaging expansion previously guided for Jan/Feb 2027 (no update this quarter).
- **Uflex**: Q2 capex Rs 5,689M; Egypt aseptic packaging (12B packs) H2 FY27; Dharwad BOPP line H1 FY28; Noida recycling (39,600 MTPA) operational.
- **Seshaasai Technologies**: New metal card facility (3x capacity) coming soon; consistent infrastructure investments.

## What changed since last quarter
- **Cosmo First**: Maintained net debt reduction target (Rs 300-500 cr over 2 years); US target updated to >$100M next year; acknowledged port congestion and West Asia volatility; detailed non-recurring items; new specialty assets on track for H2.
- **EPL**: Maintained guidance (high-teens revenue, 20%+ underlying EBITDA); Beauty & Cosmetics surpassed Oral Care; new risks: Poland regulatory delays (6-9 months), Middle East conflict; Indovida merger NCLT application filed.
- **Uflex**: Guidance slightly adjusted from ~35% to ~30% revenue/EBITDA growth; Egypt aseptic timeline shifted from H1 to H2 FY27; West Asia conflict flagged as new risk.
- **TCPL Packaging**: Guidance maintained; detailed Noida subsidiary underperformance (smartphone volume decline); clarified battery separator tech (wet process); no update on flexible packaging expansion timeline.
- **Dhaval Packaging**: IPO completed Aug 2026 (Rs 36.3 cr); net worth doubled to ~Rs 67 cr; debt-to-equity improved to 0.31x; new facility progress; metal packaging launch timeline firm.
