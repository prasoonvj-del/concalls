# Paper & Packaging: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Paper & Packaging sector reflects a resilient top-line environment characterized by robust volume growth and strong demand across exports, specialty segments, and value-added product lines. However, profitability across several players came under pressure due to sudden, unhedged raw material price spikes (polymers, chemicals, and fuel) driven by West Asia geopolitical tensions and petroleum market volatility. Companies with integrated setups, robust export pipelines, or pricing-power levers navigated these headwinds better, while many managements expressed optimism for a stronger H2 FY27 supported by seasonality, price adjustments, and newly commissioned capacity.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board note healthy volume demand. Players like **Cosmo First** (15% YoY volume growth), **Huhtamaki India** (7% YoY volume growth), and **Knack Packaging** (13% YoY volume growth) outperformed broader market trends. Demand for specialty films, value-added packaging, pet food bags, and transit solutions remains strong.
*   **Divergence:** While **Kuantum Papers** noted that Q2 was a lean seasonal period for writing and printing paper (expecting an upbeat Q3), flexible packaging and technical players like **Huhtamaki** and **Seshaasai Technologies** reported strong sequential growth, driven by payment cards, transit programs, and specialized export markets.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Almost all managements highlighted margin compression or input cost headwinds caused by sudden raw material inflation (polypropylene, polymers, sulfuric acid, and fuel) resulting from the West Asia conflict and ocean freight/logistics challenges. Port congestion and semiconductor/memory chip lead times also posed operational friction.
*   **Disagreements & Disparities in Pass-through:** 
    *   **Knack Packaging** suffered an immediate ~7% gross margin hit because raw material prices spiked 25%–30% instantly while its production cycle carries a 1-to-1.5-month lag and contracts operate on monthly or quarterly reset lags. 
    *   **Huhtamaki India** similarly experienced temporary timing lags in passing on aggressive September polymer price hikes.
    *   **Cosmo First** mitigated base margin pressures through strong specialty volume growth and a non-recurring US customs duty refund write-back of Rs. 24.8 crore, offset by a Rs. 7 crore inventory loss.
    *   **Kuantum Papers** countered chemical and fuel cost pressures using Total Cost Management (TCM) initiatives and planned 7%–11% price hikes across Q3 and Q4.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Cosmo First:** Completed its high-investment capex cycle of recent years, with two new specialty lines (16,000 MT coating line and 10,000 MT 9-layer barrier sheet line) launching in H2 FY27. FY27 capex is guided at Rs. 100–110 crore, tapering to Rs. 50–70 crore in FY28.
*   **Huhtamaki India:** Sees no capacity constraints for the next 2 to 3 years and requires no major investments due to ongoing efficiency gains. It closed its unprofitable Baddi plant to consolidate operations into Ambernath and Bangalore.
*   **Knack Packaging:** Scaled total capacity to 42,000 MT with a new 5,040 MT addition in August, targeting 69,000–72,000 MT by October 2027. Its Mexico JV is nearing breakeven.
*   **Kuantum Papers:** Successfully completed its major modernization cycle (commissioning PM 3 and achieving 600–675 TPD optimized capacity); remaining CWIP is strictly for environmental projects.
*   **Seshaasai Technologies:** Continuing infrastructure investments, including setting up a new metal card facility with 3x current capacity.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Cosmo First:** Maintained net debt reduction targets (Rs. 300–500 crore over two years) and updated US market targets to exceed $100 million next year; tone shifted slightly to acknowledge port congestion risks and West Asia volatility.
*   **Knack Packaging:** Secured a major 3-year contract renewal and new orders worth ~Rs. 150 crore/year with a US pet food client.

## Company one-liners
*   **Cosmo First Ltd:** Delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 51% revenue growth and 38% EBITDA growth, anchored by robust specialty volumes, profitable B2B verticals, and active deleveraging.
*   **Huhtamaki India Ltd:** Delivered strong Q3 CY26 performance with 24% revenue growth and positive volume momentum, offset by a one-time Baddi plant restructuring charge and short-term raw material cost lags.
*   **Knack Packaging Ltd:** Delivered strong 52.5% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by export volumes, but EBITDA margins contracted by 285 bps to 16.35% due to a sharp, unhedged lag in passing on sudden polypropylene price spikes.
*   **Kuantum Papers Ltd:** Completed its major modernization cycle and expects a stronger H2 FY27 driven by price hikes and operational efficiencies, despite lingering cost pressures from fuel and chemicals.
*   **Seshaasai Technologies Ltd:** Delivered 15% YoY revenue growth and sequential margin recovery in Q2 FY27, anchored by strong Payment Solutions and IoT expansion, while maintaining a net cash balance sheet.

## What to watch next quarter
*   The realization and stickiness of planned price hikes (e.g., Kuantum Papers' 7%–11% target and Knack/Huhtamaki's raw material pass-throughs).
*   Commissioning milestones and customer adoption rates for new specialty assets at Cosmo First and Knack Packaging.
*   The pace of debt reduction and working capital normalization amid ongoing geopolitical and supply chain volatility.
*   Breakeven progress for new ventures, including Knack's Mexico JV and Cosmo's consumer verticals.

*Based on: Cosmo First Ltd, Huhtamaki India Ltd, Knack Packaging Ltd, Kuantum Papers Ltd, Seshaasai Technologies Ltd*
