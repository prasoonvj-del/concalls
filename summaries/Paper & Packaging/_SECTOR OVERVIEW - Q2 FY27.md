# Paper & Packaging: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the Paper & Packaging sector reported a resilient Q2 FY27, characterized by strong top-line growth driven by export demand, volume expansion, and recent modernization efforts. However, this growth was universally challenged by geopolitical conflicts in West Asia, which triggered sudden, volatile spikes in raw material costs (polymers, chemicals) and freight inflation. While flexible and rigid packaging players successfully passed on price hikes or leveraged high-value product mixes, short-term timing lags compressed margins for several companies.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Companies across segments noted that Q2 generally represented a lean or transitional season, but expressed strong optimism for an upbeat H2 driven by festive demand, agricultural/rice cycles, and new customer additions.
* **Divergence (Sector & Segment performance):** 
  * **Knack Packaging** and **Huhtamaki India** highlighted robust export volumes and deep wallet share with global FMCG, pet food, and agri-customers.
  * **Mold-Tek Packaging** experienced divergent segment performance: strong volume growth in pharma (+26.3%), food & FMCG (+23.7%), and paints (~9%), which successfully offset a 12.3% decline in the lubricant segment due to private client input issues.
  * **Kuantum Papers** noted stable import conditions due to high ocean freight acting as a natural barrier, alongside structural migration from paper pinch-bottom bags to plastic alternatives.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreed Views:** All companies pointed to West Asia geopolitical tensions as the primary catalyst for sudden inflation in raw materials (polymers, polypropylene, chemicals) and rising fuel/power and freight costs. 
* **Disagreements / Varying Impacts:**
  * **Knack Packaging** and **Huhtamaki India** suffered temporary margin compression (Knack's EBITDA margin down 285 bps YoY to 16.35%) due to a 1-to-2-month lag in passing on instantaneous 25%–60% polymer price spikes under existing customer contracts.
  * **Mold-Tek Packaging**, conversely, successfully absorbed a 60% raw material price increase as customers readily accepted price hikes, enabling EBITDA per kg to expand to INR46.5.
  * **Kuantum Papers** utilized internal Total Cost Management (TCM) and a single-vendor chemical solution to deliver 1.5%–2% cost savings, projecting EBITDA margins around 14% for H2.

## Capex & capacity
* **Huhtamaki India:** Closed the unprofitable Baddi labels plant, shifting volumes to Ambernath and Bangalore; put Daman land up for sale and expanded renewable energy via the Khopoli solar captive plant (now 23% of power).
* **Knack Packaging:** Added 5,040 metric tonnes of capacity in August, scaling total capacity toward 69,000–72,000 metric tonnes by October 2027.
* **Kuantum Papers:** Successfully commissioned PM 3 on August 28 after a 59-day modernization shutdown, completing the modernization of all four paper machines and reducing steam consumption.
* **Mold-Tek Packaging:** Consolidated Hyderabad and Sultanpur units for cost efficiencies, targeting total capacity of 70,000 tonnes by early next fiscal year.

## Company one-liners
* **Huhtamaki India:** Delivered strong Q3 CY26 revenue growth (24%) and positive volume momentum, offset by a one-time Baddi restructuring charge and short-term raw material lags.
* **Knack Packaging:** Achieved 52.5% YoY revenue growth driven by exports, though EBITDA margins contracted due to unhedged raw material price lags.
* **Kuantum Papers:** Completed its major modernization cycle and expects a stronger H2 FY27 driven by price hikes and operational efficiencies.
* **Mold-Tek Packaging:** Delivered robust Q2 performance with a 36% profit jump and higher EBITDA per kg, led by pharma and FMCG volume growth.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Huhtamaki India:** Progress on bidder evaluation for Daman land sale and realization of productivity/efficiency gains without capacity constraints.
* **Knack Packaging:** Margin recovery following price adjustments and the pace of breakeven for the Mexico JV.
* **Kuantum Papers:** Implementation and sustenance of planned 7%–11% price hikes through Q3/Q4 and deleveraging progress.
* **Mold-Tek Packaging:** Commercial production rollout of the Iodex balm packs and new MNC products starting December/January.

*Based on: Huhtamaki India Ltd, Knack Packaging Ltd, Kuantum Papers Ltd, Mold-Tek Packaging Ltd*
