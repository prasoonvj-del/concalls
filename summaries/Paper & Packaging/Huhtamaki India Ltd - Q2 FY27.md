# Huhtamaki India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q3 CY26
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent volume growth, highest sales in recent quarters, and improved YTD margins despite geopolitical disruptions and volatile raw material costs.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 748 crore (up ~24% YoY / ~23.7%)
- Volume growth: 7% YoY for Q3, 4% YTD
- 9-month Revenue growth: 16% YoY
- EBITDA (9M CY26): Rs 204 crore (~10% EBITDA margin, up 25% YoY)
- 9-month EBITDA margin: 10% (vs 9.2% YoY, an 80 bps improvement)
- Reported EBIT (Q3): Flat YoY (up ~18% YoY on a comparable basis excluding last year's one-off gains)
- EBIT (9M CY26): Rs 153 crore (up 22% YoY after offsetting ~Rs 8.8 crore prior period depreciation correction)
- PAT (Q3): Rs 30 crore (down 19% YoY due to a INR 96 million Baddi restructuring charge)
- 9-month PAT: Up 13% YoY
- EPS (9M): Improved by 13% YoY
- Renewable energy share (electricity): Increased to 23% following the Khopoli solar captive generation plan starting August.

### Highlights
- Recorded highest quarterly sales of ~Rs 748 crore in recent years.
- Delivered volume growth for multiple consecutive quarters this year (7% in Q3, 4% YTD), outperforming broader customer trends of flat/declining volumes.
- Announced closure of the Baddi labels plant (smallest output, unprofitable) with operations smoothly shifted to Ambernath and Bangalore plants by November 3; incurred a one-time restructuring charge of INR 96 million.
- Won three prestigious IFCA (Indian Folding Carton and Flexible Packaging) awards for innovative sustainable products (OmniLock, blueLite, and a UK metallic label).
- Zero safety incidents (no first aid, medical, or lost-time incidents) reported in Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains an aspiration to grow profitably and improve margins every quarter/year, but does not provide formal annual forecasts or guidance.
- Management sees no capacity constraints for the next 2 to 3 years and expects no need for major investments to meet projected market demand due to ongoing productivity/efficiency gains.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Segment-agnostic across FMCG, pharma, etc., but focuses heavily on high-value, complex products rather than commoditized/low-complexity segments (e.g., biscuits).
- The packaging market remains highly fragmented; Huhtamaki holds less than double-digit market share, leaving significant room for expansion.
- Customer conversion cycles range from 3 months to a couple of years depending on artwork, pre-press, technology selection, trials, and customer approval processes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices experienced sudden, aggressive inflation in September, creating a temporary timing lag in pass-throughs to customers that impacted Q3 margins.
- Comparable Q3 EBIT margin improved by 18% YoY when excluding last year's one-off employee retiral reversals.
- Other expenses increased YoY due to baseline normalization (removal of last year's retiral reversals) and Middle East-driven power and fuel inflation, CSR expenses, and provisions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Baddi plant closed to optimize costs and operations, moving volumes to Ambernath and Bangalore label plants. All three remaining label plants are FSC certified.
- Daman land (former plant site) is up for sale; management is evaluating bids.
- Khopoli solar captive generation plant commenced operations in August, boosting renewable energy share to 23%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet asset valuations reflect adequate assets for growth aspirations.
- No challenge anticipated from an income tax perspective regarding the IGAAP depreciation correction passed earlier in the year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions, supply issues, and sudden, volatile weekly/daily raw material cost fluctuations.
- Delays in passing on sudden raw material price hikes to customers due to contractual time lags.
- Low customer willingness to adopt more expensive sustainable packaging products without regulatory enforcement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Khetan (SMIFS Institutional Equities) on margin sustainability and top-line jump:** Management explained that top-line growth is a mix of pricing pass-throughs, product mix (focusing on high-value items), and 7% volume growth. Margin moderation in September was due to a timing lag in passing on sudden raw material cost hikes.
2. **Ajit Darda (Nirzar Enterprises) on regulatory changes & customer conversion:** Management noted existing regulations on recycled plastic (PCR) content (e.g., 40% for simple mono-materials, 10% for complex aluminum laminates) benefit them via blueloop and innovation. New customer conversion takes anywhere from 3 months to a couple of years.
3. **N Modi on held-for-sale land and depreciation tax impact:** Management stated that the Daman land price will align with prevailing market rates through ongoing bidder evaluation, and confirmed the IGAAP depreciation adjustment had no adverse tax assessment implications.
4. **Dinesh Parekh on PBT margins and AI usage:** Management clarified that prices cannot be raised arbitrarily as they are linked to underlying commodities, but high-margin product mix and productivity plans drive profitability. AI initiatives are currently underway for operations, quality, productivity, and safety.
5. **Dhruv T. on other expenses increase:** Management attributed the rise in other expenses to base effects (absence of last year's one-off retiral reversals) and power/fuel inflation.
6. **Magesh P. on segment performance and peer margin comparison:** Management reiterated that they are segment-agnostic and focus on value propositions and high-value innovations rather than commoditized segments, noting that peer comparisons often reflect different product mixes.

### One-line takeaway
Huhtamaki India delivered strong Q3 CY26 performance with 24% revenue growth and positive volume momentum, offset by a one-time Baddi plant restructuring charge and short-term raw material cost lags.
