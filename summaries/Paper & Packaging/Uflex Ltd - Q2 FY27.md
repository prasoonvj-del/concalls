# Uflex Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust YoY revenue growth and margin expansion driven by overseas operations and localized sourcing premiums despite challenging macro conditions.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained ~30% revenue and EBITDA growth guidance for FY27 (slightly adjusted from ~35% stated in Q1).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, emphasizing strong capacity utilization (~80%) and robust overseas demand.
- New topics or risks: Mentioned West Asia conflict causing price/demand volatility and elevated logistics costs.
- Last quarter's promises: Egypt aseptic packaging commissioning timeline shifted slightly from H1 FY27 to H2 FY27.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs. 48,694 million (up 26% YoY)
- H1 FY27 revenue: Rs. 102,666 million
- Normalized EBITDA: Rs. 7,588 million (up 95% YoY)
- Normalized EBITDA margin: 15.6% (expanding 550 bps YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs. 15,961 million
- Consolidated net profit (PAT) after non-controlling interest: Rs. 2,106 million (up 689% YoY)
- Net profit margin: 4.3% (expanding 360 bps YoY)
- Total sales volume: 163,532 metric tonnes (up 1.5% YoY)
- Packaging films volume: 129,025 metric tonnes (up 3% YoY; 78.9% of total)
- Packaging business volume: 34,507 metric tonnes (down 3.9% YoY)
- Capital expenditure in Q2 FY27: Rs. 5,689 million
- Debt-to-EBITDA leverage ratio: 3.33x

### Highlights
- Overseas operations contributed 56% of incremental revenue and 74% of incremental normalized EBITDA.
- Key overseas contributors included Egypt, Mexico, Hungary, U.S.A, Poland, and Nigeria.
- Capital expenditure focused on three key projects: aseptic packaging facility in Egypt, recycling unit at Sector 155 Noida, and BOPP line at Dharwad.
- Leverage ratios improved from 4.5x+ in FY25/26 to 3.33x in Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guides for ~30% growth in revenue and EBITDA for FY27.
- Management expects leverage ratios to be in the range of 3x to 3.5x at the end of the financial year, targeting below 3x by FY28/FY29 as new capacities ramp up.
- Value-added products are targeted to reach 50% of total top line by FY29.
- Egypt aseptic plant capacity utilization is targeted at 30% in year one, 60% in year two, and 100% in year three.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Converters prefer sourcing packaging films from local and regional producers, driving overseas volumes.
- Indian flexible packaging business is strategically shifting towards higher-margin products, accepting lower base volumes.
- Aseptic packaging in India faces margin pressure due to cheap imports from Indonesia and other countries.
- Value-added products currently contribute about 30% of total sales.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating environment marked by elevated inflation, higher crude and energy prices, rising logistic costs, and supply chain disruptions.
- Plain BOPET film spread has remained consistent, ranging between Rs. 25 to Rs. 35 over nine months.
- Plain BOPET film prices ranged from Rs. 108 in January 2026 to Rs. 151 in October end.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q2 capex stood at Rs. 5,689 million.
- Noida Sector 155 recycling facility has a capacity of 39,600 metric tonnes per annum.
- Mexico WPP bag facility has a capacity of 80 million units.
- Dharwad BOPP line commercial operations expected in H1 FY28.
- Egypt aseptic packaging facility (12 billion packs) slated for commercial production in H2 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt leverage is at 3.33x EBITDA.
- Approximately Rs. 1,000 crore of capex is tied to assets yet to be commercialized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated inflation, crude and energy prices, rising logistic costs, and supply chain disruptions.
- Price and demand volatility amid the ongoing West Asia conflict.
- Threat of cheap imports in the domestic aseptic packaging segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Wilson Lewis (Individual Investor):** Asked when debt of Rs. 8,000 crore will be reduced. *Management stated leverage is at 3.33x, expected to be 3-3.5x by year-end, and will drop as new capacities are fully utilized.*
- **Kaushik Poddar (KB Capital Markets):** Inquired about profit margin expansion reasons. *Management attributed it to ~80% capacity utilization and higher overseas margins.*
- **Shashank Mehta (Parekh Family Office):** Asked about customer approvals for the Egypt aseptic plant. *Management stated validation batches are underway with big MNC clients, targeting H2 FY27 commercial production.*
- **Sumangal Pugalia (RaRe Enterprises):** Asked about spreads and raw material pass-throughs. *Management confirmed BOPET spreads remained consistent at Rs. 25-35 with automated raw material pass-throughs.*
- **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company):** Asked about regional volume dips. *Management explained quarterly shifts are minor and overall utilization is nearing 80%, aiming for 90%.*

### One-line takeaway
UFlex posted a strong Q2 FY27 with a 26% YoY revenue increase and a 95% jump in normalized EBITDA, driven by strong overseas profitability, high capacity utilization, and a strategic tilt toward value-added products.
