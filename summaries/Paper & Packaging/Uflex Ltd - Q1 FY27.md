# Uflex Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong acceleration in revenue and profitability driven by overseas operations and localized sourcing premiums.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs. 53,972 million (up 38% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 9,198 million (up 92% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 17% (expanding 480 bps YoY)
- Normalized EBITDA (post Rs. 825 million forex derivative adjustment): Rs. 8,373 million (up 78% YoY, margin at 15.5%)
- Consolidated net profit (PAT): Rs. 4,233 million (compared to Rs. 580 million in Q1 FY26); net margin at 7.8% (vs 1.5% in Q1 FY26)
- Total sales volume: 173,471 metric tonnes (up 1.7% YoY)
- Packaging Films volume: 136,186 metric tonnes (up 4.9% YoY)
- Overall Packaging volume: 37,285 metric tonnes (down 8.4% YoY)
- Incremental revenue: Rs. 14,753 million (80% from overseas, 20% from India)
- Total capex incurred in Q1: Rs. 4,782 million

### Highlights
- Highest EBITDA performance in the last 21 quarters.
- Overseas operations contributed about 91% of incremental EBITDA and 80% of incremental revenue.
- Commissioned a 39,000 metric tonnes per annum recycling plant at Noida Sector 155 on April 30, 2026.
- Commissioned an 80-million-unit WPP bags plant in Mexico on July 31, 2026.
- Greenfield Aseptic project in Egypt (12 billion packs) remains on schedule for commissioning in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects ~35% growth in top line and a similar growth in EBITDA for FY27 compared to the previous financial year.
- Management expects Debt-EBITDA leverage to drop below 3x by FY28 as capacities reach full utilization.
- Management aims to reduce interest costs by at least 1% over the next year.
- Volumes are targeted to double in 3 years (by FY29) from the FY26 base.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Packaging Films demand in India improved sequentially in May and June after price normalization.
- Americas sales volume grew 18% YoY to 31,724 metric tonnes due to U.S. government onshoring policies.
- Europe sales volume remained flat YoY at 35,653 metric tonnes amid low-priced imports.
- Middle East and Africa volumes grew 14.9% YoY to 39,486 metric tonnes driven by local sourcing and supply chain derisking.
- Aseptic packaging volumes in India faced softness due to duty-free import competition from Indonesia, but demand is expected to recover from Q3.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- BOPP prices increased by 25%-32% and BOPET prices by 30%-35% between February 2026 and the reporting date.
- Margin expansion was supported by operational leverage, higher realizations, raw material cost pass-throughs, currency tailwinds, and localized sourcing premiums.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q1 capex of Rs. 4,782 million allocated across 4 projects: Egypt Aseptic facility (Rs. 1,236 million), Mexico WPP bags (Rs. 205 million), Noida Sector 155 recycling unit (Rs. 320 million), and Dharwad BOPP Line (Rs. 215 million).
- 60% to 70% of future incremental capex will be directed toward value-added products.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-EBITDA improved from 4.5x in FY26 to 3.5x in Q1 FY27.
- Company maintains a dual rating of AA- by CRISIL and India Ratings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia and potential supply chain/consignment disruptions.
- Low-priced imports and duty-free entry from Indonesia impacting domestic aseptic packaging volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Randhir Kumar Singh (Randhir HUF):** Asked about the sustainability of Q1 margins and top-line growth. *Management stated that numbers are sustainable and guided for ~35% top line and EBITDA growth for FY27.*
- **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.):** Inquired about the impact of expected Q2 normalization given the massive Q1 jump. *Management clarified that "normalization" refers to guarding against excessive quarter-on-quarter volatility while reiterating the full-year YoY 35% growth guidance.*
- **Chirag Singhal (First Water Fund):** Asked about cash flow utilization between debt reduction, capex, and shareholder returns. *Management explained that capital allocation will favor growth and value creation (with 60-70% going to value-added products), which increases shareholder wealth instead of buybacks, alongside steady debt reduction.*
- **Kaushik Poddar (KB Capital Markets):** Asked about the profitability gap between domestic and international operations and volume trajectory. *Management noted international realizations are roughly 2.5x India levels, and volume is projected to double in 3 years by FY29.*

### One-line takeaway
UFlex delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with a 38% YoY revenue increase and a 92% surge in EBITDA, underpinned by robust overseas operations, price realizations, and a strategic shift toward value-added capacities.
