# Paper & Packaging - Packaging: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the packaging sector reported a robust Q2 FY27, characterized by strong revenue growth and healthy demand across both domestic and international markets. Several companies achieved milestone performances—TCPL Packaging crossed Rs 500 crore in quarterly total income and Rs 100 crore in EBITDA for the first time, while EPL posted its highest-ever reported revenue growth of 30% YoY, marking its 16th consecutive quarter of double-digit EBITDA growth. Growth was largely anchored by specialty portfolios, premiumization (such as Beauty & Cosmetics overtaking Oral Care at EPL), and strong overseas contributions (notably at UFlex, where overseas operations drove 56% of incremental revenue). However, profitability was broadly challenged by geopolitical tensions (specifically the West Asia conflict and Red Sea disruptions), volatile raw material and crude-linked polymer prices, port congestion, and currency headwinds (USD-INR). 

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Demand remains fundamentally healthy across domestic and international markets, with double-digit top-line expansions reported widely (e.g., Cosmo First up 51%, Knack Packaging up 52.5%, Huhtamaki up ~24%, TCPL up 20.2%, UFlex up 26%, EPL up 30%). Converters focusing on value-added, complex, or sustainable portfolios are experiencing outperformance compared to commoditized segments.
* **Divergence:** While flexible packaging, IML, and beauty segments are thriving, niche segments faced sector-specific headwinds. For instance, TCPL Packaging noted underperformance in its Noida hard packaging subsidiary due to a decade-low in Indian smartphone production driven by high memory chip prices. Similarly, Dhaval Packaging's End Caps segment faced pressure due to the Iran conflict impacting pipe exports to Saudi Arabia, even as its IML ice cream container business performed strongly.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Cost Pressures:** Sudden, volatile spikes in crude oil, petrochemicals, and polypropylene prices—exacerbated by the West Asia conflict—pushed up input and logistics costs. Knack Packaging noted that polymer prices surged from ~Rs 92 to a peak of ~Rs 143-Rs 160.
* **Margin Impact & Pass-throughs:** Most companies experienced timing lags (ranging from 1 to 2 months) in passing on sudden raw material price hikes due to existing contract structures, leading to temporary gross margin contractions (e.g., Knack Packaging's EBITDA margin dropped 285 bps YoY to 16.35%; Huhtamaki faced temporary margin moderation in September). Conversely, companies like Dhaval Packaging and EPL successfully leveraged bulk procurement or immediate pricing actions to protect and recover underlying margins.

## Capex & capacity
* **Active Expansions:** Companies continue to invest heavily in specialized and sustainable capacities. EPL reported H1 capex of INR 3,150 million (up ~90% YoY); UFlex reported Q2 capex of Rs. 5,689 million; Dhaval Packaging spent Rs. 9.18 crore on a new plant (75-80% complete); and Knack Packaging added 5,040 MT capacity in August, scaling toward 69,000–72,000 MT by October 2027.
* **New Strategic Ventures:** TCPL Packaging incorporated a wholly owned subsidiary (TCPL Energy Materials Ltd.) to manufacture lithium-ion battery separator films using the wet process, targeting a potential scale of Rs. 1,000–2,000 crore over the next few years. Cosmo First is commissioning two new specialty assets (a 16,000 MT coating line and a 9-layer barrier sheet line) in H2 FY27.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Revisions & Affirmations:** EPL maintained its near-term guidance of high-teens revenue growth and 20%+ underlying EBITDA margins (having raised it from early double digits in Q1). UFlex slightly adjusted its annual growth guidance to ~30% for revenue and EBITDA (from ~35% in Q1). Cosmo First maintained its net debt reduction target of Rs. 300-500 crore over two years.
* **Tone Shifts:** Tone remained predominantly positive, though managements at Cosmo First, UFlex, Knack Packaging, and Huhtamaki turned increasingly mindful of port congestion, supply chain bottlenecks, and sudden raw material volatility linked to West Asian geopolitical friction.
* **Timeline Adjustments:** UFlex noted a minor timeline shift for its greenfield aseptic packaging project in Egypt, moving from H1 FY27 (stated in Q1) to H2 FY27 due to ongoing MNC client validation batches.
* **Last Quarter's Promises Delivered:** EPL delivered on high reported revenue growth and advanced its Indovida merger (filing an NCLT application). Cosmo First kept its specialty line expansions on track for H2 FY27 commissioning. Dhaval Packaging progressed its Sanand-II plant construction using IPO proceeds, remaining on track for March 2027 completion.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Margin Recovery & Pass-Throughs:** Whether companies lagging in raw material cost pass-throughs (such as Knack Packaging and Huhtamaki) successfully recover normalized EBITDA spreads in Q3.
* **Project Commissionings & Scale-ups:** Progress on key upcoming assets, including Cosmo First's specialty coating and barrier lines, Dhaval Packaging's March 2027 plant and metal packaging line, UFlex's Egypt aseptic plant (H2 FY27), and TCPL's initial-phase developments for battery separator films.
* **Geopolitical & Logistical Risks:** The evolution of port congestion, container freight rates, and West Asian supply chain volatility impacting raw material pricing and export shipments.

## Company one-liners
* **Cosmo First Ltd:** Delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 51% revenue growth and 38% EBITDA growth, anchored by robust specialty volumes, profitable B2B verticals, and active deleveraging.
* **Dhaval Packaging Ltd:** Delivered strong H1 FY27 profit growth post-IPO debt reduction while expanding into high-margin metal packaging to offset geopolitical softness in its End Caps segment.
* **EPL Ltd:** Delivered its highest-ever reported revenue growth of 30% (20.3% underlying) in Q2 FY27 as Beauty & Cosmetics surpassed Oral Care, while maintaining underlying EBITDA margins above 20% despite inflationary and geopolitical headwinds.
* **Huhtamaki India Ltd:** Delivered strong Q3 CY26 performance with 24% revenue growth and positive volume momentum, offset by a one-time Baddi plant restructuring charge and short-term raw material cost lags.
* **Knack Packaging Ltd:** Delivered strong 52.5% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by export volumes, but EBITDA margins contracted by 285 bps to 16.35% due to a sharp, unhedged lag in passing on sudden polypropylene price spikes.
* **Seshaasai Technologies Ltd:** Delivered 15% YoY revenue growth and sequential margin recovery in Q2 FY27, anchored by strong Payment Solutions and IoT expansion, while maintaining a net cash balance sheet.
* **TCPL Packaging Ltd-$:** Delivered record-breaking financial results while maintaining a disciplined, phased approach to its new battery separator film venture.
* **Uflex Ltd:** Posted a strong Q2 FY27 with a 26% YoY revenue increase and a 95% jump in normalized EBITDA, driven by strong overseas profitability, high capacity utilization, and a strategic tilt toward value-added products.
