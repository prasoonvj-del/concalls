# Cosmo First Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Core flexible packaging maintained momentum, new businesses scaled up, and all B2B businesses turned profitable.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated sales: Rs 1,166 crore (up 46% YoY)
- Sales volume growth: 9% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs 147 crore (up 26% YoY from Rs 116 crore in June 2025Q)
- EBITDA margin: 12.6% (vs 14.5% in June 2025Q)
- BOPP specialty sales volume growth: 12% YoY
- BOPP gross margin: Rs 30 per kg (vs Rs 20 in March 2026Q and Rs 23 in June 2025Q), including non-repetitive stock gain
- BOPET gross margin: Rs 9 per kg (vs Rs 18 in March 2026Q and Rs 13 in June 2025Q)
- Semi-specialty contribution: Rs 45 per kg (vs Rs 36 in Q4)
- Specialty film margins: Stable at Rs 63+ per kg
- Specialty Chemicals topline growth: 34% YoY with 25% EBITDA margin
- Cosmo Plastech (Rigid Packaging) topline growth: 58% YoY with 7% EBITDA margin (excluding incentives, 10%+ with incentives)
- Zigly revenue growth: ~70% YoY (annualized GMV run rate close to Rs 100 crore); gross margin at 50% / 47%
- Net Debt (June 2026 end): Flat at Q4 FY26 level of Rs 1,166 crore (2.3x to EBITDA), despite a Rs 85 crore working capital increase from raw material price hikes.
- Strategic capex invested over last 3 years: Rs 1,200 crore
- Zigly quarterly loss: ~Rs 15 crore (loss % on revenue down from 82% to 69%)
- Zigly centers: 47

### Highlights
- All B2B businesses are now profitable.
- Major phase of capital investment is behind; focus is now on leveraging assets, improving ROCE (target 15% to 20% over next 12-24 months), and strengthening cash generation.
- Export volumes were suppressed by 13% due to port congestion, resulting in higher in-transit inventory.
- US subsidiary received a refund of about USD 7 million in July 2026 following the reversal of additional custom duties.
- Product launches in films: synthetic paper film for high-end digital printing, PVC-free green graphic films, anti-fog transparent BOPET lidding films; secured 1 international patent (6 granted, 11 in pipeline).
- Cosmo Consumer started brand building with TV advertisements in July 2026 and launched the first 4C Cosmo Car Care Centre in Pune.
- Plastech is augmenting 50% growth in capacity over the next 2 quarters with minimal capex.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects topline to grow by about 20% in FY26-27 with a commensurate increase in bottom-line.
- Four new businesses are expected to grow by about 60%.
- US business expected to grow 25% to 30% in FY27 post duty rationalization.
- Management expects net debt to EBITDA to reduce to below 2x in the next 12 months, and a total debt reduction of Rs 400 crore to Rs 500 crore over the next 2 years.
- Renewable power PPAs are expected to yield Rs 25 crore per annum in savings; one project kicks in from Q3 and another in Q1 of next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall film demand is quite strong.
- Film capacity utilization is at 85%; management expects the remaining 15% spare capacity to be utilized over the next two quarters.
- BOPET industry in India is slightly over-capacity, which management expects to correct.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Revenue rose 46% while volume rose 9%; the difference reflects raw material price pass-through following the West Asia conflict.
- Gross margin per kg improved across base, semi-specialty, and specialty categories.
- BOPP margins included non-repetitive stock gains.
- Zigly losses widened slightly due to upfront investments ahead of revenue (4 new retail centers, 2 hospital acquisitions, 20+ private label launches).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure cycle is largely complete; incremental capex is directed at specialty assets.
- Plastech capacity is expanding by 50% over the next 2 quarters with minimal capex, aiming to scale revenue to Rs 150-160 crore this year and Rs 200+ crore next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands at Rs 1,166 crore (2.3x EBITDA) as of June 2026.
- Net working capital increased by Rs 85 crore due to higher raw material prices post West Asia war.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in foreign currency impacting other income.
- Port congestion leading to a 13% reduction in export volumes and higher in-transit unbilled inventory.
- Raw material availability constraints for polymer procurement during the West Asia conflict.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nirav Jimudia (Anvil Wealth) asked about the trajectory of ROCE from 11% to the target 15-20% over 2 years.** Management answered that levers include 15% spare film capacity utilization, 50% capacity expansion in Plastech with minimal capex, high growth in B2C and specialty chemical segments, and 25-30% growth in the US market post-duty rationalization.
2. **Raman KV (Sequent Investments) questioned why margins lagged revenue growth (46% revenue vs 26% EBITDA growth).** Management explained that the revenue gap was driven by raw material price pass-through inflation, whereas absolute EBITDA and contribution per kg actually improved.
3. **Jahnvi Shah (Share India) asked about the breakeven revenue for Zigly and Cosmo Consumer.** Management stated Zigly breakeven is expected around Rs 250 crore of revenue (currently at ~Rs 100 crore annualized GMV run rate), while Cosmo Consumer can break even earlier than Rs 100 crore.
4. **Aman Kumar Sonthalia (AK Securities) asked about the widening of Zigly losses from Rs 10 crore to Rs 15 crore.** Management explained they are investing ahead of revenue (new centers, hospital acquisitions, private label launches), noting that unit economics are improving and loss % on revenue has dropped from 82% to 69%.
5. **Sanya Kothari (AUM Capital) asked if debt reduction relies on EBITDA or absolute repayment, and about renewable savings.** Management clarified that debt reduction relies on both (having already reduced net debt by ~Rs 70 crore in 9 months), and targeted a Rs 400-500 crore debt reduction over 2 years. Renewable savings of Rs 25 crore will begin kicking in from Q3 FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Cosmo First reported strong 46% revenue growth (9% volume growth) and 26% EBITDA growth in Q1 FY27, with all B2B segments turning profitable and management prioritizing ROCE improvement, specialty mix expansion, and net debt reduction.
