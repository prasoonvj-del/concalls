# Kuantum Papers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expects a stronger H2 FY27 with price hikes and improved operational efficiencies following the completion of the modernization program.

### Key numbers
*   **Operational Income:** Rs 279 crore (down 8% QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 Operational Income:** Rs 533 crore (up 16% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 35 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 12.4% (down 84 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 2 crore.
*   **PAT Margin:** 0.8%.
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 9 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Modernization:** Successfully commissioned PM 3 on 28th August 2026 (rebuild completed in 59 days). All 4 paper machines are now modernized.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Double Displacement Digester System improved wood pulp yield by 1% and reduced steam consumption from 1.8 metric tons to 0.8 metric tons per tonne of pulp.
*   **Production:** Achieved highest-ever monthly copier paper production of 4,482 tonnes in August 2026.
*   **Sustainability:** Highest-ever clonal saplings production (17.93 lakh); total plantation coverage >22,000 acres.
*   **Product Launch:** Commercially launched PFAS-free 40 GSM Oil & Grease Resistant paper in Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management maintains annual target of Rs 1,300 crore+.
*   **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to be around 14% for H2 FY27.
*   **Pricing:** Expects price increases in the range of 7% to 11% through Q3 and Q4.
*   **Specialty Paper:** Targeting 25%-30% revenue mix from specialty paper in the next 12-15 months (currently 17%-18%).
*   **Long-term:** Management expects "far better" performance in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Demand:** Q2 was a lean season; Q3 is expected to be upbeat with tailwinds.
*   **Imports:** Imports are stable; high ocean freight rates have acted as a natural barrier, reducing import volumes.
*   **Consolidation:** Industry is seeing consolidation (e.g., ITC/Century, JK Paper).
*   **Policy:** Industry has represented to the government for Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD), Anti-Subsidy, and Minimum Import Price (MIP) for writing/printing paper.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Pressures:** Chemical costs (sulfuric acid) impacted by West Asia conflict (~2% impact on sales); fuel costs increased by 3%-3.5%.
*   **Raw Materials:** Wood prices have softened. Wheat straw prices are temporarily high due to labor scarcity during harvest but expected to normalize post-Diwali.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Total Cost Management (TCM) initiative with single-vendor chemical solution delivered cost savings of 1.5%-2% on chemical consumption.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Major modernization capex is complete. Remaining CWIP of Rs 139 crore is for environmental projects (lime kiln, CNCG).
*   **Capacity:** Optimized production capacity is ~600 tonnes per day (can reach 675 tonnes depending on GSM mix).
*   **Future Capex:** No further major capex planned in the near future.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Peak debt is ~Rs 700 crore.
*   **Repayment:** Annual debt repayment is ~Rs 180 crore for FY27.
*   **Deleveraging:** Management expects deleveraging to start after FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Operational:** Planned shutdown of PM 3 impacted Q2 production by ~6,300 tonnes and added ~Rs 40 crore cost impact on top line.
*   **External:** Geopolitical tensions (West Asia) impacting chemical and freight costs.
*   **Supply Chain:** Coal supply diverted to power sector, forcing reliance on open market at higher prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is production capacity lower than the 675-tonne potential?** Management explained that market demand is shifting toward lower GSM (52-58 GSM), which restricts throughput compared to higher GSM copier paper.
*   **Q: Will price hikes be sustained?** Management stated the ~4% hike taken in late Q2 is sustained, and further hikes are planned for Q3/Q4, supported by seasonal demand.
*   **Q: What is the status of Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD)?** Management noted the government is reviewing the representation; determination typically takes 3-6 months.
*   **Q: Why did payables jump?** Management clarified the Rs 139 crore CWIP is for ongoing environmental projects (lime kiln/CNCG) scheduled for Q3.
*   **Q: Will you consider buybacks?** Management stated the priority is cash flow generation for debt repayment; no changes to capital structure are planned.

### One-line takeaway
Kuantum Papers completed its major modernization cycle and expects a stronger H2 FY27 driven by price hikes and operational efficiencies, despite lingering cost pressures from fuel and chemicals.
