# EPL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever revenue growth of 30% YoY, with Beauty & Cosmetics becoming the largest category, alongside the 16th consecutive quarter of double-digit EBITDA growth.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained near-term guidance of revenue growth in high teens and underlying EBITDA margins of 20%+ (vs Q1 where guidance was raised from early double digits to high teens).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted even more positively as Beauty & Cosmetics revenues surpassed Oral Care in September, reaching a balanced 44% mix each.
- New topics or risks: Regulatory approval delays (6-9 months) for the centralized Poland printing site leading to outsourcing costs; Middle East conflict driving commodity inflation and supply chain disruptions; NCLT application filed for the Indovida merger.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on high revenue growth (30% reported), accelerated capex (H1 capex at Rs 315 crore / INR 3,150 million), and advanced the Indovida merger process (NCLT application filed).

### Key numbers
- Revenue Growth: Up 30% YoY reported (highest-ever); underlying revenue growth of 20.3% YoY (excluding raw material pass-through).
- EBITDA: Up 17.6% YoY; reported EBITDA margin at 18.9%; underlying EBITDA margin at 20.4%.
- PAT: Up 5.6% YoY (impacted by higher depreciation, higher effective tax rate due to country mix, and adverse forex movements).
- H1 Capex: INR 3,150 million (up ~90% YoY). Full-year capex guided at INR 625 crore to INR 700 crore.
- Category Growth YoY: Beauty & Cosmetics up 42.9%; Oral Care up 19.2%.
- Regional Revenue Growth YoY: EAP up 40%, Europe up 30%, AMESA up 28%, Americas up 25%, India standalone up 30%.
- Sustainable Tubes Mix: 41% of product mix.
- Market Share: Gained 130 bps globally over the last 1 year.

### Highlights
- Marked the 16th consecutive quarter of double-digit EBITDA growth.
- Beauty & Cosmetics surpassed Oral Care in September to become the largest category (each representing ~44% of overall portfolio).
- Fully recovered raw material and operational cost increases through pricing actions.
- Filed application with the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) for the proposed merger with Indovida, following CCI and foreign antitrust approvals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management retained near-term guidance of revenue growth in high teens (landing on the higher end, closer to 20%) and underlying EBITDA margins at 20%+.
- Full-year capex guided between INR 625 crore and INR 700 crore.
- Management expects the Indovida merger to be completed by Q4 of this year, targeting Q4 results at consolidated levels.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broad-based growth across all categories and geographies.
- Growth in India driven by domestic market expansion and premiumization, primarily through existing customers.
- Growth in lower-share markets (e.g., China extruded tubes) driven by new customer acquisition and white space entry.
- Global B&C market growing in high single digits.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Reported gross contribution margin at 56.7% (60.5% underlying excluding inflation impact).
- Incurred additional opex on B&C capability building, innovation center, outsourced solutions for metallization, and superior customer service (including air freight and safety stock).
- Fully passed on cost inflation through pricing actions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex investments stood at INR 3,150 million (~90% increase YoY).
- Investments focused on B&C growth, extruded tube capabilities in China, India, Europe, and the U.S., alongside advanced printing and decoration capabilities.
- Poland printing site centralization experienced 6-9 months of regulatory approval delays, resulting in higher outsourcing costs; operations expected to take 2-3 quarters to fully stabilize.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Post-Indovida merger, debt-to-EBITDA ratio expected to be around 0.2 to 0.25 (Indovida carries zero debt).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing Middle East conflict keeping commodity prices elevated and disrupting supply chains.
- Stubborn operational challenges in Europe (Poland plant transition) requiring 2-3 quarters to resolve and normalize margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) on capex jump:** Management attributed the capex surge to strong B&C momentum and global market share gains (130 bps), noting capex will remain elevated in H2 (INR 625-700 crore total).
- **Raman KV (Sequent Investments) on margin impact and synergies:** Management stated 100% of raw material inflation was recovered via pricing and reiterated the expected USD 35 million to USD 50 million total synergy benefit from the Indovida merger, predominantly on the top line.
- **Sameer Gupta (IIFL Capital) on revenue guidance:** Management clarified that while guidance is maintained at high teens, they expect to land on the higher end (~20%), supported by superior customer service and strong B&C demand.
- **Bharat Sheth (Quest Investment) on operating leverage:** Management explained that opex investments in B&C capabilities, innovation, and freight offset some operating leverage from strong revenue growth.
- **Shivakshi Bhardwaj (Retail Investor) on B&C growth drivers:** Management noted mature markets like India grow via existing customers and market expansion, whereas low-share markets grow via new customer acquisition in white spaces.

### One-line takeaway
EPL delivered its highest-ever reported revenue growth of 30% (20.3% underlying) in Q2 FY27 as Beauty & Cosmetics surpassed Oral Care, while maintaining underlying EBITDA margins above 20% despite inflationary and geopolitical headwinds.
