# EPL Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-28 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record revenue growth and passed on entire cost inflation while raising full-year revenue guidance.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Up 25.3% YoY (underlying growth of 20% excluding raw material pass-through).
- EBITDA: Up 15.2% YoY; EBITDA margin at 18.8% (underlying margin at 19.6%).
- PAT: Down 1.4% YoY (due to lower base year ETR phasing); PBT up 10% YoY.
- Segment Revenue Growth YoY: EAP up 34.3%, Americas up 29.4%, Europe up 20.2%, AMESA up 17%.
- Category Growth YoY: Beauty & Cosmetics and Oral Care both grew over 20%.
- Personal Care & Beyond portfolio share: 54%.
- Sustainable tubes mix: 44%.
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 18.5%.
- Indovida Q1 FY27 Financials (publicly available): Volume up 11%, Revenue up 25%, EBITDA up 62% to Rs 383 crore, EBITDA margin expanded 614 bps to 27%.

### Highlights
- Fifth consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth; 15th consecutive quarter of double-digit EBITDA growth.
- Successfully passed on entire cost increases (raw materials, freight, currency depreciation) through judicious pricing.
- Received Competition Commission of India approval for the proposed merger with Indovida; transaction remains on track.
- Won multiple innovation and quality awards, including the ETMA Tube of the Year Award, FIPSA Awards for Responsible Packaging, and the IMC Ramkrishna Bajaj National Quality Award.
- Recognized as one of India’s Best Companies to Work For 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised revenue growth guidance from early double digits to high teens for the next few quarters.
- Management maintained underlying EBITDA margin guidance at 20%.
- Full-year PAT projected to deliver double-digit growth.
- Effective Tax Rate (ETR) expected between 20% to 22% for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broad-based growth across all categories and geographies.
- Beauty & Cosmetics maintained strong momentum (>20% growth); Oral Care recovered strongly (>20% growth) due to high service levels and customer engagement.
- Thailand operations beginning to ramp up, serving as a significant B&C market growth driver.
- Long-term goal is to expand from tubes into higher-growth consumer packaging formats and emerge as an emerging market leader.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating in a high inflationary environment; raw material prices, freight costs, and currency depreciation all saw sharp increases.
- Entire cost impact successfully recovered from customers via blended cost contracts and pricing adjustments.
- Opex investments include setting up a B&C center of excellence in India and divisionalizing front-end sales teams.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Making proactive growth investments ahead of the curve in capex and front-end capabilities (e.g., European capacity expansion in extruded tubes, printing capabilities, die and tooling for B&C, and Thailand operations).
- European manufacturing footprint consolidated in Poland and Germany.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased due to higher inventory values driven by raw material price inflation, safety stock building for supply security, and ahead-of-the-curve capex investments.
- Accounts receivable aging and quality remain solid.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical environment and trade dynamics continue to evolve and remain volatile.
- Operational challenges in Europe identified and under focus for resolution in coming quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sameer Gupta (IIFL Capital) on working capital/debt spike:** Management clarified it was driven by higher inventory pricing, safety stock building, and upfront capex. Receivables aging remains under control.
- **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) on PBT vs. revenue growth after guidance upgrade:** Management reiterated that PBT grew 10% and PAT is on track for double-digit full-year growth, with Q1 PAT impacted solely by ETR phasing.
- **Sanjesh Jain on inventory gains:** Management stated consumption cost includes old inventory and pricing lags, but net-net the entire cost impact was fully recovered.
- **Jaymin (Ardeko Asset Management) on opex/capex split:** Management noted investments in front-end sales team split and B&C capabilities are structural, but steady-state EBITDA will grow faster than revenue.
- **Sameer Gupta on Europe margins and footprint:** Management explained Europe investments are for long-term B&C growth; operational inefficiencies are being addressed with margins expected to recover to mid-teens.

### One-line takeaway
EPL delivered record Q1 underlying revenue growth of 20% with 19.6% underlying EBITDA margins, successfully absorbing high inflation and prompting a revenue guidance upgrade to high teens.
