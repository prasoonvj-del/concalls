# Xpro India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Paper & Packaging*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and profits grew YoY, new capacities are ramping up, and structural demand remains strong.

- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 176.69 crore vs Rs 119.91 crore YoY (up 47.4%)
- **Operating PBIDT before other income and forex (Q2 FY27):** Rs 16.4 crore vs Rs 10.68 crore YoY (up 53%)
- **PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 12.79 crore vs Rs 7.6 crore YoY
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 9.52 crore vs Rs 5.86 crore YoY (up 62%)
- **Revenue Growth (Dielectric films, Q2 YoY):** 64%
- **Revenue Growth (Sheets and cast film, Q2 YoY):** 41%
- **Aggregate Production Volume (H1 FY27):** 19,218 tonnes vs 16,795 tonnes YoY (up 14%)
- **Net Revenues (H1 FY27):** Rs 351.11 crore vs Rs 264.81 crore YoY (up 32.6%)
- **Total Exports (H1 FY27):** Rs 17.09 crore vs Rs 9.39 crore YoY
- **Forex Loss (H1 FY27):** Rs 32 lakh (including Rs 78 lakh unrealized)
- **PBDT before non-operating forex (H1 FY27):** Rs 36.70 crore vs Rs 30.27 crore YoY

### Highlights
- Dielectric film nameplate capacity in India doubled to 8,000 metric tonnes/year with the new Barjora line operational.
- Production on the new Barjora line grew by 35% QoQ; the new line achieved EBITDA-positive status in the final month of the quarter.
- Received three awards from ELCINA for quality, R&D, and environment protection/sustainable development.
- Refrigerator sheet and liners volume grew by 26% in Q2 YoY, outperforming the Indian refrigerator production growth of 8%.
- Subsidiary in UAE (Al Ghail) has key equipment installed; utility commissioning is underway, with commercial operations expected later in FY27. The subsidiary recorded a loss of AED 1.73 million during H1 for pre-operative and implementation expenses.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to exceed a turnover of Rs 700 crore for FY27 if current market conditions sustain.
- Management expects the new Barjora line to operate between 70% to 80% of rated capacity in Q4 FY27.
- Co-extruded business EBITDA margin expected to sustain in the 5% to 7% range.
- Dielectric film EBITDA margins vary between 35% and 55% on conventional thicknesses, increasing further for thinner gauges.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand drivers for dielectric films are structural (electrification, renewable energy, grid modernization, data centers, electric mobility, and traction systems).
- Global capacitor film capacities in advanced markets are aging, supporting long-term global demand.
- Indian refrigerator production grew 8% in Q2 YoY (compared to 4% in Q1).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Polymer (PP resin) and shipping costs have seen volatility and increases; full cost pass-through is ongoing and not yet complete.
- Operating costs, depreciation, and finance costs of the new Barjora line continue to weigh on profitability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- UAE subsidiary project is broadly on track; key equipment is installed and utilities commissioning has started. Commercial operations expected later in FY27.
- Internal work for potential future lines (Line 4 and Line 5) is ongoing, though slightly tempered by global crises. Management does not anticipate raising equity directly for the main system expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No outstanding long-term borrowings for existing operations; borrowings comprise supplier credit in the nature of ECB for the Barjora line.
- Net debt to equity remains negligible.
- Working capital days impacted temporarily by global shipping uncertainties, prompting higher raw material stockholding.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conditions in West Asia and ongoing regional conflicts create uncertainty, elevated costs, and trade/transport disruptions affecting shipping and raw material landed costs.
- Volatility in raw material prices and shipping freight makes full cost pass-through gradual.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Debarshi Deb (Ashva Holdings) asked about sustainable EBITDA margins and realization for specialty films.** Management stated co-ex EBITDA is 5% to 7%, while dielectric film EBITDA ranges from 35% to 55% depending on thickness (modal price ~Rs 470-480/kg, 3.5 micron at over Rs 600/kg, and 3 micron near Rs 900/kg).
- **Tej Patel (Niveshaay Investment Advisors) asked about raw material cost pass-through.** Management clarified that full cost neutralization of rising raw material and shipping costs is not yet complete and is being handled dynamically.
- **Tej Patel asked about UAE line dry run timelines.** Management stated utility commissioning is ongoing and main equipment dry runs could start partially in December/January, depending on specialist engineer travel availability.
- **Vishal Prasad asked when significant margin improvement will be visible.** Management declined to give a specific quarter for target margins, reiterating that volume ramp-up (targeted at 70%-80% utilization in Q4 for the new line) comes first, followed progressively by value ramp-up.
- **Anand Kumar asked about export vs domestic split for the new Barjora line.** Management stated the majority of the volume will go into the domestic market, while maximizing exports from the Middle East unit when operational.

### One-line takeaway
Xpro India delivered strong YoY revenue (47.4%) and PAT (62%) growth in Q2 FY27, driven by market share gains in refrigerator sheets and strong momentum in newly doubled dielectric film capacities despite geopolitical and shipping headwinds.
