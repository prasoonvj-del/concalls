# Petronet LNG Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a strong start to the year with highest-ever standalone and consolidated PBT and PAT for any first quarter, driven by commercial and operational efficiencies despite lower volumes.

### Key numbers
- Standalone PBT: Rs 1,514 crore, up 33% YoY (from Rs 1,136 crore)
- Standalone PAT: Rs 1,133 crore, up 33% YoY (from Rs 851 crore)
- Consolidated PBT: Rs 1,491 crore
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 1,137 crore
- Dahej LNG volume processed: 192 TBTU (vs 207 TBTU in Q1 FY26, 201 TBTU in previous quarter)
- Overall company LNG volume processed: 207 TBTU (vs 220 TBTU in Q1 FY26, 219 TBTU in previous quarter)
- Dahej capacity utilization: 66% (65.6% on expanded nameplate capacity of 22.5 MMTPA, compared to 92% YoY and 90% QoQ)
- Company capacity utilization: 58% (vs 76% YoY and QoQ)
- Kochi capacity utilization: 23.27%
- Inventory gains: Rs 193 crore
- Trading gains: Rs 301 crore
- Regasification revenue: Rs 1,214 crore
- Ind AS impact: Gross margin positive Rs 14 crore, forex loss Rs 5 crore, other expenses positive Rs 8 crore, depreciation Rs 66 crore, finance cost Rs 48 crore
- Petrochemical project physical progress: ~40% completed
- Cumulative capex spent on petrochemicals: Rs 679 crore (with Rs 472 crore spent during the quarter)
- Budgeted/projected capex for FY27: Rs 9,064 crore
- Dahej tariff: Around Rs 69
- Kochi tariff: Around Rs 98

### Highlights
- Dahej nameplate capacity increased from 17.5 MMTPA to 22.5 MMTPA starting March 31, 2026.
- Despite lower volumes from the long-term Qatar contract due to Gulf disruptions/Force Majeure, robust financial performance was achieved due to trading gains (Rs 301 crore), inventory valuation gains (Rs 193 crore), and replacement tolling volumes.
- Deepak Fertilizers contract commenced in May 2026, bringing in 2 cargoes so far.
- Time charter operations for LNG carriers have been suspended using specific contract clauses (not Force Majeure).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the Gulf issues to resolve soon and long-term volumes from Qatar to resume.
- Capex for FY27 is projected at Rs 9,064 crore, with a similar level expected for FY28.
- New Qatar Energy contract starting in 2028 will be on a DES basis (formula-driven price including shipping cost). Discussions on contract terms with offtakers are ongoing and expected to close in the next 2 to 3 quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Qatar Energy declares Force Majeure on a month-on-month basis (currently declared through end of August).
- More than two-thirds of the missing Middle East volume is currently being compensated by alternative replacement/tolling volumes brought in by customers (such as GAIL, IOCL, BPCL, and Deepak Fertilizers).
- Indian gas demand remains healthy, with the government working to ensure equitable gas supply across sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin improvement was driven by trading gains (Rs 301 crore) and inventory gains (Rs 193 crore).
- Management noted that high spot prices creating a gap with long-term prices, alongside lower throughput, historically triggers spot trading opportunities which protect the bottom line (consistent with the last 5-6 years' business model).
- Propane for the petrochemical plant will be sourced from the Middle East (Saudi CP pricing) and the USA, with contracts to be explored/signed around 2027.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dahej nameplate capacity is now 22.5 MMTPA.
- Kochi terminal pipeline connectivity is expected to be mechanically completed by the end of this quarter.
- Petrochemical plant project is on schedule with 40% physical progress completed; expected useful life is taken as 25 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific balance sheet or debt figures provided other than capex spend. Time-charter vessel lease payments have been suspended.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions at the Strait of Hormuz impacting long-term LNG supplies from Qatar.
- Force Majeure declarations by Qatar Energy on a month-on-month basis.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities):** Asked about volume mix sustainability and margin drivers. Management explained that trading gains (Rs 301 crore) and inventory gains (Rs 193 crore) drove margins, reflecting the established business model during high spot-price parity periods.
- **Nitin Tiwari (Nitin Tiwari / Analyst):** Inquired whether tolling cargoes offset use-or-pay liabilities. Management clarified that current year commitments take priority, after which excess tolling/long-term adjustments apply to retire past use-or-pay liabilities faster, depending on the offtaker.
- **Mayank Maheshwari (Analyst):** Asked about Qatar sourcing visibility. Management stated they are in constant touch with Qatar Energy, who declare FM weekly/monthly, and noted Qatar is ramping up production based on recent Bloomberg reports.
- **Kishan (Analyst):** Asked about Deepak Fertilizers and ExxonMobil volumes. Management confirmed both contracts have commenced, with Deepak Fertilizers bringing in 2 cargoes since May 2026.
- **Bineet Banka (Analyst):** Inquired about the propane contract and DES contract impact on LNG carriers. Management replied propane sourcing contracts will be signed around 2027, and time-charter vessel leases end concurrently with current SPAs (except one).
- **Vivekanand (Analyst):** Asked about visibility of replacement volumes and trading business mechanics. Management reiterated that replacement volumes flow dynamically based on monthly FM notices, and trading gains are independent of spare capacity and driven by market opportunities.

### One-line takeaway
Petronet LNG delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with a 33% YoY surge in standalone PAT to Rs 1,133 crore, successfully offsetting lower long-term Qatari volumes through robust spot trading gains, inventory revaluations, and customer tolling volumes on its expanded 22.5 MMTPA Dahej capacity base.
