# Reliance Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-01 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated EBITDA and PAT reached all-time highs driven by structural strength in O2C and steady scale-up in Jio, offsetting ongoing transition investments in Retail.

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 62,409 crore, up 24% YoY.
* **Consolidated PAT:** Close to Rs 30,000 crore, up 34% YoY (crossing the Rs 50,000 crore mark for H1).
* **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** Up 28% YoY.
* **O2C EBITDA:** Rs 25,203 crore, up 69% YoY.
* **Jio Platforms (JPL):**
  * Revenue from operations: Rs 40,475 crore, up 11.4% YoY.
  * EBITDA: Rs 21,879 crore, up 16.6% YoY.
  * PAT: Rs 8,387 crore.
  * ARPU: Rs 218.8, up 3.5% YoY.
  * Total Subscribers: 542 million (net additions of 9.1 million in Q2; 36 million over the last 12 months).
  * 5G Subscribers: 300 million; Fixed Broadband Connections: 30 million (15.1 million FWA).
  * Average Data Consumption: 44.2 GB per user per month (+14% YoY).
* **Reliance Retail (RRVL):**
  * Revenue Growth: Up 6.4% YoY.
  * Gross EBITDA: Rs 6,228 crore (pre-exceptional item), with operating EBITDA down 11% YoY; EBITDA margin at 7.8% (down 140 bps YoY).
  * One-time Exceptional Charge: Rs 108 crore due to shutting down the 7-Eleven format.
  * PAT: Rs 2,611 crore, down 24% YoY.
  * Registered Customers: Crossed 400 million, up 10% YoY.
* **Reliance Consumer Products (RCPL / FMCG):** Revenue at Rs 8,266 crore (1.6x YoY growth); H1 revenue reached Rs 16,900 crore.
* **JioStar (Media):** Revenue grew 10% YoY; monthly active users (MAU) on JioHotstar reached 440 million (up 10% YoY).
* **Oil & Gas (E&P):** EBITDA down 2% YoY; sustained D6 gas production at ~25 MMSCMD.

### Highlights
* **Jio Listing:** Listed Jio Platforms on stock exchanges at a 26.7% premium on the first day, attaining a market valuation of ~$137 billion.
* **Retail Strategy:** Prioritized disciplined digital commerce and quick-commerce growth over short-term EBITDA margins; funding online expansion from offline store cash flows.
* **FMCG Segment:** Expanded into dairy with "Bombay Creamery" ice creams; Campa recorded H1 revenues of Rs 5,250 crore (~90% of FY26 total sales).
* **Media Innovation:** Integrated OpenAI's ChatGPT conversational discovery feature inside JioHotstar (engagement up 75% QoQ) and launched the app in the UK, Canada, and Singapore.
* **Ethane Advantage:** Delivered first new VLEC (Very Large Ethane Carrier) out of three ordered; ethane-to-naphtha cracking spread widened to $557/tonne in Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
* **O2C:** Management tracks annualized EBITDA well over Rs 80,000 crore based on H1 FY27 trends, supported by tight global fuel balances.
* **Jio:** Management expects high-teen growth visibility driven by customer additions across home, mobility, and enterprise segments, layered with digital/AI services.
* **Retail:** Management expects digital commerce investments to turn bottom-line accretive starting in FY28/FY29 as dark store and digital network utilization hits threshold levels.
* **New Energy:** Earnings contributions from the new energy complex will start in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Refining & Fuel Cracks:** Gasoline cracks surged ~3x YoY to ~$25/bbl; diesel cracks rose 3.5x YoY to $65.8/bbl; jet fuel cracks rose 3.7x YoY to $59.3/bbl amid global supply outages and low inventories.
* **Petrochemicals:** Global cracker utilization dropped from 81% to 71% (Middle East operating rates dropped from 80% to 50%) due to raw material supply constraints and shipping disruptions.
* **Domestic Energy Demand:** Indian petrol (MS) demand grew 8.5% YoY, and diesel (HSD) grew 7.5% YoY.
* **Retail Macro:** Two-year high inflation, currency depreciation, and a delayed festive calendar (shifting major festive sales into Q3) impacted discretionary offline buying in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Freight & Insurance:** Freight rates escalated sharply (AG to Japan naphtha freight jumped to $122/tonne average in Q2, up 3x YoY), offset by Reliance maintaining a time-chartered (TC) fleet of ~25 crude/product vessels.
* **Ethane vs Naphtha:** Naphtha cost rose 43% YoY, while US ethane remained flat (+5% YoY at $24.3/tonne), generating high margins for ethane-fed crackers.
* **SAED & Price Under-Recovery:** Margins were partially dragged down by SAED levies and under-recoveries in domestic fuel retailing where international cost hikes were not fully passed through.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **New Energy Rollout:** Module capacity reached 6+ GW (targeting 20 GW in 1H CY27); cell capacity is approaching 5 GW (targeting 20 GW in 1H CY27). BESS pack/container facility (40 GWh) will commission in 1H CY27, and cell manufacturing (35–40 GWh) will start late CY27.
* **Jio Network:** Bulk of 5G and Unlicensed Band Radio (UBR) core network capex is completed; capex is tapering down, generating high free cash flow (EBITDA less capex stood at Rs 32,130 crore).
* **Data Center Capex:** Currently building 44 MW (completing in 4–6 months) with plans for an additional 120 MW.
* **E&P Development:** Contracted a deepwater rig back to KG-D6 for workovers and additional development wells to augment recovery.
* **Store Footprint:** Opened 303 new stores and shut 500 unviable stores in Q2 (total store count ~20,000 spanning 77.5 million sq. ft.).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Consolidated net debt-to-EBITDA ratio stood at 0.47x as of September 2026.
* Overall quarterly capex remained broadly flat QoQ.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **Geopolitical & Shipping Disruptions:** Continued turmoil around the Strait of Hormuz, Red Sea, and attacks on Russian refineries create extreme crude sourcing and freight rate volatility.
* **Retail Quick-Commerce Margin Compression:** Aggressive price discounting and customer acquisition costs in quick commerce across metro cities are hurting short-term retail EBITDA margins.
* **Upstream Ceiling Price Restrictions:** Domestic gas realizations remain capped by the government-set price ceiling ($9.9/MMBtu for H2 FY27), despite spot LNG market prices trading near $29/MMBtu.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities):** Asked if the 5 million b/d affected global refining capacity will take 6–9 months to normalize. Management agreed, stating geopolitics remains highly uncertain with low global inventories keeping refining margins elevated.
* **Jal Irani (Nuvama):** Asked for a breakdown of O2C profit drivers across refining, petchem, and ethane. Management answered that petchem contributed significantly via wide ethane-naphtha deltas, while refining benefited from higher diesel cracks ($2.8/bbl QoQ gain), gasifier operations replacing costly natural gas, and product export premiums.
* **Analyst:** Enquired about the timeline for New Energy module/cell commissioning and revenue generation. Management confirmed module/cell capacity will hit 20 GW in early/1H CY27, battery cell production will ramp up between June–December CY27, and commercial revenues will accrue in FY28.
* **Analyst:** Asked about Retail EBITDA margin floors and IPL media rights bidding. Management responded that retail dark store investments will yield margin accretion from next year; on IPL rights, management affirmed keen interest but stated bidding will strictly follow disciplined, profitable growth criteria.
* **Vikash Jain (CLSA):** Queried if online grocery growth (42% YoY) is lagging competitors and whether doubling Retail EBITDA in two years is feasible. Management answered that growth is intentionally measured to ensure strong LTV/CAC economics without irrational couponing, leveraging RIL's existing 400 million retail customer base and store network to drive accelerating margins post-investment phase.

### One-line takeaway
Reliance delivered record consolidated quarterly EBITDA and net profit on exceptional downstream O2C tailwinds and Jio's peak-capex cash generation, while strategically funding its long-term quick-commerce and new energy transitions.
