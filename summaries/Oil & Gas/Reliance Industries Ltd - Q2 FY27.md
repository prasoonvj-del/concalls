# Reliance Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-01 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever consolidated EBITDA and PAT driven by exceptionally strong O2C performance and digital services growth.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 62,409 crore, up 24% YoY
- Consolidated PAT: Close to Rs 30,000 crore, up 34% YoY
- O2C EBITDA: Rs 25,203 crore, up 69% YoY
- Digital Services (JPL) Revenue: Rs 40,475 crore, up 11.4% YoY; EBITDA: Rs 21,879 crore, up 16.6% YoY; ARPU: Rs 218.8, up 3.5% YoY
- Retail (RRVL) Revenue: Rs 6,120 crore (implied segment / as stated for retail operations or 6% overall growth); EBITDA: Rs 6,228 crore (before exceptional item); Net Profit: Rs 2,611 crore
- FMCG (RCPL) Revenue: Rs 8,266 crore in Q2 (1.6x YoY); H1 Revenue: Rs 16,900 crore
- JioHotstar MAU: 440 million, up 10% YoY
- Debt Metrics: Net debt to equity ratio (analysed) at 0.47

### Highlights
- Jio Platforms completed a major IPO, listing at a 26.7% premium and becoming the second largest company by market cap.
- Jio reached 542 million total subscribers, 300 million 5G customers, and 30 million home connections (including 15.1 million FWA connections with 78% market share).
- Reliance Retail opened 303 new stores, closed 500 unviable stores, and shut down the 7-Eleven format (taking a one-time exceptional charge of Rs 108 crore).
- RCPL launched ice creams under "Bombay Creamery" and expanded international footprint into Australia, Kenya, and other regions.
- JioHotstar integrated OpenAI's ChatGPT for conversational discovery and expanded international availability to the UK, Canada, and Singapore.
- E&P segment benefited from elevated oil and gas prices, with a deepwater rig re-entering KG-D6 for workovers and development wells.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains very constructive on O2C, tracking annualized EBITDA well over Rs 80,000 crore for the segment based on H1 performance.
- New Energy earnings contribution is slated to begin in FY 2028; solar module and cell capacity expected to reach 20 GW each in early / first half of next calendar year, while BESS capacity (40 GWh) will commission in the next half-year (CY).
- Retail expects online digital commerce investments to start being accretive to the bottom line from next year onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global energy markets experienced severe turbulence, tightness, and inventory drawdowns due to geopolitical disruptions (Middle East conflicts, Strait of Hormuz issues, and drone attacks on Russian refineries).
- Domestic fuel demand remained strong: diesel up 7.5% YoY and gasoline up 8.5% YoY, supported by deficient monsoons and prices not fully aligned with international markets.
- Global cracker operating rates dropped to 71% (down from 81%), and Middle East cracker operating rates dropped to 50% due to evacuation challenges.
- China has structurally moved towards EVs and LNG trucks, reducing certain demand, but global demand is expected to rebound by ~2 million bpd in 2027.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- High OpEx headwinds included elevated crude premiums, surging freight rates, and insurance costs.
- Ethane-naphtha differential widened significantly to $557 in Q2 (up 2.3x YoY), favoring ethane cracking.
- Retail EBITDA margins were impacted by 140 bps YoY (down to 7.8% reported) due to conscious margin investments in quick commerce, discounting, and heavy investments in digital commerce infrastructure.
- Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED) levy weighed on refining margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex has remained largely flat/stable across quarters, with telecom capex tapering down as 5G and UBR core network deployments are largely complete.
- Two new ethane carrier vessels delivered (with a third shortly expected) to capture the naphtha-ethane arbitrage.
- Retail added 303 new stores and expanded its dark store network for quick commerce.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt to equity ratio stands at 0.47.
- Cash flows from traditional store businesses are funding digital commerce growth in retail.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High geopolitical volatility (Middle East, Russia-Ukraine), continuing attacks near shipping lanes, and persistent risks of sanctions and refinery outages.
- Currency depreciation impacting imported product landed costs.
- Food inflation and monsoon deficits causing demand pressure across agri-dependent states and eating into discretionary demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities) on Refining Outages & Normalization:** Management stated that ~5 million barrels per day remain affected in the Middle East and Russia, and given ongoing attacks and low inventories, normalization is not expected immediately.
2. **Jal Irani (Nuvama) on Profit Drivers & Chinese EV/LNG Impact:** Management clarified that O2C growth was a combined effect of petrochemicals, higher diesel cracks, secondary unit utilization (gasifier advantage), and TC fleet freight mitigation. While China has structurally shifted to EVs/LNG, global demand recovery and geopolitical memory of supply risks will keep margins supported.
3. **Vikash Jain (CLSA) on Retail Growth vs Quick Commerce & EBITDA Target:** Management reiterated their target of doubling retail EBITDA over the next two years, defending their measured approach to quick commerce burn and emphasizing leverage of their existing 400M+ customer base and store infrastructure.
4. **Unnamed Analyst on New Energy Commissioning Timeline:** Management reaffirmed module and cell capacities reaching 20 GW by early/1H next calendar year, BESS commissioning in the next half-year, and revenue realization from FY 2028.
5. **Unnamed Analyst on Capex, Data Centers, and IPL Bidding:** Management stated they are building infrastructure for hyperscalers (44 MW moving to 120 MW), and confirmed they are keen on the upcoming IPL renewal, subject to disciplined bidding and profitable growth philosophy.

### One-line takeaway
Reliance delivered its highest-ever consolidated EBITDA (Rs 62,409 crore) and PAT (nearly Rs 30,000 crore), supercharged by an exceptional O2C performance amid global energy tightness, alongside continued expansion in Jio and strategic investments in retail digital commerce.
