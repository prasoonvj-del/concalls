# GSPL Transmission Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Broad-based volume growth across grids, debt-free balance sheet, and progress on expansion capex.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 290 crore (up 8% YoY, up 4.5% QoQ vs INR 277 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR 567 crore (up 4.5% YoY vs INR 542 crore in H1 FY27)
- **EBITDA (excluding non-operating income, Q2 FY27):** INR 169 crore (vs INR 168 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 58.4%
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 362.9 crore
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 64%
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 120 crore (up 2% YoY vs INR 118 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 258 crore (up 1.2% YoY vs INR 255 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Total Transmission Volumes (Q2 FY27):** 45 MMSCMD (up 12% YoY vs 40 MMSCMD in Q2 FY26)
- **HP Grid Volumes Growth:** Up 9% YoY
- **LP Grid Volumes Growth:** Up 30% YoY
- **HAPi Grid Volumes Growth:** Up 16% YoY
- **Net Worth:** Approximately INR 7,150 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **H1 FY27 Capex Executed:** Approximately INR 300 crore
- **Net Cash/Deposits:** Approximately INR 1,800 crore
- **Expansion Capex Program:** Approximately INR 3,500 crore covering >700 km across >10 projects
- **Current Network Capacity:** 36.5 MMSCMD

### Highlights
- GTL operates a ~2,900 km natural gas transmission network across 26 districts in Gujarat (India's second largest), with 100+ customers and 200+ exit points.
- Gujarat accounts for ~22% of India's natural gas consumption in FY26 and hosts four operational LNG terminals; GTL is the only grid connected to all operational LNG gateways in Gujarat.
- Volumes now reported as contractual numbers rather than physical molecules to better reflect revenue alignment.
- Subsidiary GIGL operates ~1,439 km across Gujarat, Rajasthan, Haryana, and Punjab; GITL operates ~365 km connecting Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to complete at least ~INR 3,000 crore of the INR 3,500 crore capex by March 2028.
- Overall network length expected to expand from ~2,900 km to 3,600 km upon project completions.
- Capacity expected to increase by around 8 to 9 MMSCMD.
- Maintenance capex guided between INR 60 crore to INR 100 crore annually over the next couple of years.
- Dividend payout levels expected to be maintained at current rates, subject to board approval.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India aspires to increase natural gas share in its energy mix from ~6% currently to 15% by 2030.
- Natural gas consumption projected to rise from 188 MMSCMD in FY26 to 297 MMSCMD by 2030.
- Gas availability is expected to be supported by upcoming global liquefaction projects in the US, Qatar, Canada, and Argentina by 2028–2030.
- Sun Petro's offshore Dwarka gas field expects to produce a minimum of 2.5 MMSCMD, supplying the Jamnagar-Dwarka pipeline.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses increased in Q2 due to longer compressor operating hours required to pump gas up north (pass-through costs).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- INR 3,500 crore capex program comprises 10+ projects (700 km).
- In July 2026, awarded EPC contracts worth INR 1,800 crore for the Anjar–Palanpur Pipeline Project.
- Other active projects include Jamnagar-Dwarka, Vapi-Tarapur, Dahej-Bhadbhut, spur lines, and customer connectivities.
- GIGL has an outstanding near-term capex of INR 100–110 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is debt-free as of September 30, 2026.
- Expansion plans will primarily be funded through internal accruals and current cash balances (~INR 1,800 crore); management noted they can raise debt if exciting opportunities arise since financing approvals are in place.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subsidiary entities (GIGL and GITL) are currently making losses and operating at low-double-digit capacity utilization due to volume ramp-up lags.
- Pending tariff appeals at the PNGRB appellate tribunal level.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Yogesh Patil (Dolat Capital) asked about debt-to-equity for the INR 3,500 crore capex and volume reporting changes.** Management stated that expansion will primarily use internal accruals and existing cash balances without ruling out debt, and clarified that volume reporting was shifted from physical molecules to contractual numbers to align with revenue.
2. **Bineet Banka asked about pipeline useful life and upcoming tariff revisions.** Management stated the next tariff revision will occur around 2028, and pipeline life recertifications can extend life by up to 10 years without reducing tariffs.
3. **Shreyansh Mehta (SREE Capital) asked about the status of the PNGRB tariff order challenge and true-up mechanisms.** Management replied that the matter is pending at the PNGRB appellate tribunal, and tariff true-ups apply on a prospective basis only.
4. **Ajay Raj Singh asked about buyback considerations given the large net worth and cash position.** Management acknowledged it as a good idea but stated the immediate focus is executing the growth capex to deliver medium-term returns rather than prioritizing buybacks.
5. **Sriram raised concerns regarding capital allocation, questioning why idle cash/investments capped at 12% IRR are preferred over a value-accretive buyback.** Management defended the capital allocation, stating investments are rooted in well-discovered demand and aimed at creating long-term shareholder and national value.

### One-line takeaway
GSPL Transmission delivered steady Q2 FY27 results with 8% revenue growth and 12% volume growth, remaining debt-free while progressing on its INR 3,500 crore expansion capex.
