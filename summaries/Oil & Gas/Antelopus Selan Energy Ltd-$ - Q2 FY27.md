# Antelopus Selan Energy Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong production ramp-up, robust margins, signing of new DSF IV contracts, and debt-free balance sheet.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 121.7 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **Total income:** Rs. 127.2 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 90 crore (reported, includes Rs. 6.5 crore one-off refund); Rs. 83 crore (excluding one-off)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 71% (reported); 69% (ex-one-off)
- **Net profit (PAT):** Rs. 57.3 crore (vs. Rs. 11.8 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Realized oil price:** $88.2 per barrel (Q2 FY27 avg, down from $95.3 in Q1 FY27; July: ~$76, August: ~$84, September: >$100)
- **Realized gas price:** $8.84 per MMBtu (Q2 FY27 avg, down from $10.33 in Q1 FY27)
- **OPEX and handling costs:** Rs. 6.1 crore (down from Rs. 7.5 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Royalty and Cess:** ~16% of revenue (~Rs. 20.5 crore)
- **Depreciation:** Rs. 13.2 crore
- **H1 FY27 Sales / Revenue / EBITDA / PAT:** Sales averaged ~1,700 BOEPD; Revenue: Rs. 254.8 crore; EBITDA: Rs. 180 crore; PAT: Rs. 112 crore
- **Shareholder Equity:** ~Rs. 780 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Cash and bank balances / liquid investments:** ~Rs. 100 crore
- **Production / Exit Guidance:** October 2026 production at ~2,000 BOEPD; exit FY27 guidance at 2,500 BOEPD; targeting 3,500 BOEPD in FY28; targeting 5,000 BOEPD by FY29
- **Capex Guidance:** Rs. 250 crore for FY27 (vs. Rs. 220 crore last year; Rs. 87 crore prior; Rs. 160 crore; Rs. 51 crore in FY23)
- **Bakrol production:** ~950+ BOEPD (average for October)
- **CWIP:** ~Rs. 190 crore (~Rs. 100 crore towards Duarmara)

### Highlights
- Signed Revenue Sharing Contracts (RSCs) for two large contract areas on October 22, 2026: KG Onshore (East India) and Cambay Basin (West Coast).
- Added 7 new discoveries in KG Onshore (total 9 fields in KG; Mukkamala currently producing; includes large fields like Bantumilli and Dangeru). Management states KG Onshore has a resource base potentially larger than the company's offshore portfolio.
- Added new fields in Cambay via DSF IV (Pasunia-1 and Pasunia-2 near Bakrol; Oganj; Uber gas field).
- Completed 10-well drilling and fracking campaign in Bakrol; Karjisan drilling is ahead of plan with 7 wells planned for this fiscal (2 wells already drilled, perforating soon).
- Maintained a debt-free balance sheet with ~Rs. 100 crore in cash and liquid investments.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident on exiting FY27 at 2,500 BOEPD, scaling to 3,500 BOEPD next year, and reaching 5,000 BOEPD by FY29 (onshore-focused; negligible contribution from KG/Duarmara in this target).
- Capex guidance for FY27 is set at Rs. 250 crore.
- Endeavor is to maintain EBITDA margins above 65%-70% for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Crude oil prices swung during the quarter ($76 in July, $84 in August, $100+ in September).
- Private buyers' ability to purchase crude at higher prices is limited when oil nears $100/bbl; bulk of volumes sold to public sector buyer Indian Oil, with ~20% to private buyers at slightly higher discounts.
- In Assam (Duarmara), gas evacuation is delayed due to lack of connection to the National Grid and pending pipeline infrastructure (Duliajan–Numaligarh gas pipeline). Oil is preferred for faster monetization via trucking.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed costs constitute 70% to 75% of the cost base, driving operating leverage as production increases. Incremental barrel cost is lower than base barrel cost.
- Karjisan enjoys concessional Cess and royalty rates (statutory levies <5%, compared to ~30% in Bakrol/Cambay), providing a positive impact on P&L and margins.
- OPEX and handling costs declined to Rs. 6.1 crore in Q2 from Rs. 7.5 crore in Q1.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bakrol 10-well campaign complete; full impact of fracking reflected in October production (~950+ BOEPD). Bakrol expected to require 1-2 wells annually to offset decline.
- Karjisan rig drilling 7 wells this fiscal; 2 wells drilled and awaiting perforation.
- Cambay field (South, near Khambat): 2 optionality wells planned in late Feb / early March under 50:50 JV with Synergia.
- KG Onshore: Planning a single-rig multi-well exploration and exploitation strategy; estimated well cost around $10 million (higher due to HP-HT basin, compared to $1.5 million in Cambay).
- Duarmara (Assam): Well cost is Rs. 60-70 crore due to deeper wells (3.5 km vs 1.5-1.6 km in Cambay); formulating an integrated plan for zones 1, 2, and 4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-free company with shareholder equity at ~Rs. 780 crore.
- Cash and bank balances / liquid investments stand at ~Rs. 100 crore.
- Capital expenditure funded internally so far; future funding for aggressive KG ramp-up yet to be finalized (debt/equity raise open depending on appetite).
- Deferred tax liabilities accumulating due to 100% tax write-off on drilling capex in the year of incurrence.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain constraints previously caused minor operational delays in Bakrol fracking (now sorted).
- Assam gas evacuation constraints (lack of National Grid connectivity and pipeline delays) impact gas monetization timelines.
- Private buyers reduce purchases at higher oil prices ($100/bbl), concentrating sales on public sector buyers.
- Commodity price volatility is outside management's control (though benchmarked to dated Brent at ~10% discount).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raman K. V. (Sequent Investments) on margin sustainability:** Management stated that fixed costs constitute 70%-75% of the cost base, creating operating leverage. Karjisan's lower statutory levies (<5%) also aid blended margins, and management endeavors to maintain high margins.
2. **Shubham Jain (NV Alpha Fund) on KG Onshore capex intensity:** Management clarified that KG wells will cost ~$10 million each (vs. $1.5 million in Cambay) due to HP-HT conditions, but will be approached cautiously with a single-rig strategy before scaling.
3. **Venkatesh Subramanian (Individual Investor) on pricing and crude sensitivity:** Management explained that crude is sold at ~10% discount to dated Brent based on physical lab product yield tests. For sensitivity, management targets projects that break even and return cash within 1 to 1.5 years at $50-$60/bbl oil prices.
4. **M. N. Kumar on CWIP and deferred tax liabilities:** Management clarified that out of Rs. 190 crore CWIP, ~Rs. 100 crore is tied to Duarmara (to be capitalized upon production). Deferred tax liabilities accumulate because 100% of drilling capex is expensed for tax purposes in the year incurred.
5. **Manprit Arora on Duarmara oil vs. gas monetization:** Management confirmed that finding light oil instead of gas in TIPAM-1 allows faster monetization via trucking (months) rather than waiting for gas pipeline grid connectivity.

### One-line takeaway
Antelopus Selan delivered a strong Q2 with EBITDA up nearly 3x YoY at Rs. 90 crore (71% margin), backed by tight fixed-cost control and a rising production trajectory toward its 2,500 BOEPD exit target and 5,000 BOEPD FY29 vision.
