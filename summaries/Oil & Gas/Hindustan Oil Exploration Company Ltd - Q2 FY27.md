# Hindustan Oil Exploration Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong sequential financial recovery, resolution of past inventory issues, and a clear volume ramp-up roadmap targeting 10,000 barrels of oil equivalent net HOEC by June 2027.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall production targets, but noted a minor 15-day delay for the B-80 workover rig arrival (now late November or early December).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more optimistic on domestic gas demand and evacuation, citing active intervention by NRL to bypass the DNPL line for repairs without a full shutdown.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: The B-80 crude off-take dispute with HPCL is seeing resolution with progressive liquidations (INR 80 crore realized in H1), though discussions are ongoing. Hot tapping for the DNPL line was dropped in favor of an NRL bypass line to accelerate repairs.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: B-80 workover rig contracts were awarded as promised, though mobilization slipped slightly from November 15 to late November/early December due to ONGC rig release logistics.

### Highlights
- All assets except B-80 and PY-1 operated 100% of the days in the first half of the year.
- B-80 FSO tanker and HPCL crude liquidation progressing; total potential inflow estimated around INR 250 crore once fully monetized.
- Awarded B-174 DSF block (800 sq km area), adding 21 million barrels of 2P reserves (DGH quoted), taking company total 2P reserves to ~104 million BOE.
- Dirok production increased to ~0.7 MMSCMD gross; potential to reach 1.2 MMSCMD upon full pipeline clearance, with further upside to 2.0 MMSCMD via approved new wells.
- Management introduced Debabrata Das as the new CFO (effective October 16, 2026), while former CFO Allen Andrade transitioned to a financial advisor role.
- *Comparison with past record:* The B-80 crude off-take dispute mentioned in earlier records is actively seeing progressive lifting and amicable settlement discussions, matching the November 2026 operational update.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Production Guidance:** Management targets 10,000 barrels of oil equivalent net HOEC by June 2027, scaling to 22,000 barrels by FY28, and 30,000–32,000 barrels by FY29.
- **B-80 Workover Timeline:** Rig expected on location by end of November or early December; workover production expected by end-December/early January, with a minor 15-day impact on Q4.
- **Assam Grid Connectivity:** DNPL pipeline capacity restoration/bypass via NRL expected to be completed by December 2026, allowing gas capacity ramp-up.
- **Funding Outlook:** Exploring a total bank facility target of INR 600 crore to INR 700 crore (totaling up to INR 1,000 crore including existing lines) to fund upcoming offshore drilling peaks.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global oil market pulled in 2nd directions by Gulf supply disruptions and weak economic demand (IEA projects 2026 demand fall of 2.6 million barrels/day).
- Asian spot LNG averaged $21.5 per MMBTU from March to October due to Strait of Hormuz disruptions and 17% of Qatar's LNG capacity offline.
- India's crude import bill rose 46% to $91 billion in H1, with import volumes up less than 1% and domestic crude/condensate production falling 3.5% to 13.7 million tonnes. Domestic gas production fell 4.4%. India's crude import dependence remains above 88%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Company-wide operating cost ranges from $20 to $30 BOE depending on commodity/diesel prices.
- B-80 margins range between 30% to 40%; every $10 increase in oil prices contributes INR 30–40 crore in revenue flows.
- B-80 operating costs are the highest due to offshore support vessels (OSVs), SBM maintenance, and tanker maintenance.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **B-80 Capex:** Wells cost budgeted at $45 million and workover at $15 million ($60 million total program); deploying mud suspension well technology to save platform costs.
- **Kharsang:** 9 new wells approved; tubulars ordered, rig awarded, drilling to start January/February 2027, aiming to double oil production from 680-700 bopd to 1,200-1,400 bopd. A 24-km pipeline survey to connect stranded gas is being initiated (18-month timeline).
- **Cambay:** Teaming up with GSPC to optimize Asjol, North Balol, and Palej blocks; Palej production already doubled; PSC extension for Palej R2 imminent by January.
- **PY-1:** Coil tubing intervention with SLB delayed due to mobilization issues; new well drilling contingent on firm take-or-pay contracts with IOCL or GAIL. Potential partnership models being explored at the project level.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stands at INR 211.11 crore; net debt at INR 44.90 crore. Net debt-to-EBITDA is 0.71x.
- Raised INR 179 crore total credit facilities, including INR 125 crore specifically for B-80 workovers.
- Closing cash and cash equivalents at INR 166 crore.
- Awaiting cash flows of ~INR 250 crore from the full liquidation of HPCL stock and FSO B-80 crude inventory.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Water coning in B-80 D2 well (93%-94% water cut), necessitating immediate rig workover.
- Monsoon disruptions in Western Offshore requiring drilling cessation by May 15 to avoid standby charges and loss of tow insurance.
- Evacuation bottlenecks in Assam constraining Dirok and Kharsang gas production.
- Delays in rig mobilization for B-80 workovers due to ONGC schedule slippages (impacting Q4 by ~15 days).
- Local compensation disputes and road access issues delaying exploration in Assam Block B-19.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anubhav Goel (Cosma Ventures) asked about asset-wise EBITDA/PAT margins at $70 crude and monsoon risk mitigation for B-80.**
   - *Management answered:* Operating costs range from $20–$30 BOE; B-80 margins sit at 30%–40% ($30–$40 margin level). For monsoons, drilling stops by May 15, and MOPU storage capacity has been expanded to allow 2–3 days of buffer production during SPM hose repairs.
2. **Nirbhay Mahawar asked for volume guidance clarification for H2 FY27 and FY28.**
   - *Management answered:* Net HOEC production is targeted to reach 10,000 BOE per day by June 2027, scaling to 22,000 barrels by FY28 and 30,000–32,000 barrels by FY29.
3. **Manpreet Arora (Northern Lights Wealth) questioned the DNPL pipeline upgrade timeline and volume sustainability at Dirok.**
   - *Management answered:* The DNPL hot-tapping was replaced by an NRL bypass line to depressurize the line using national grid gas; DNPL restoration is expected by December 2026. Dirok volumes (~0.7 MMSCMD) are sustainable and backed by Oil India/IOCL offtake.
4. **Andrey Purushottam (Cogito Advisors) inquired about the exact timeline for grid connectivity in Assam.**
   - *Management answered:* Confirmed December 2026 as the target for completion of the DNPL spur line/bypass by stakeholders.
5. **Uday Vernekar asked about the B-174 development concept and Palej PSC status.**
   - *Management answered:* Palej PSC extension is imminent with the MoPNG by January. For B-174, management is evaluating innovative concepts like tolling charges via ONGC facilities or minimum facility platforms, taking about two years to develop once drilling begins in late 2028.

### One-line takeaway
HOEC delivered a strong Q2 financial recovery backed by higher gas volumes and progressive inventory monetization, while maintaining an aggressive production ramp-up roadmap to reach 10,000 BOE net by mid-2027.
