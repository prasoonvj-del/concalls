# Oil & Gas: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian oil and gas sector reflects a bifurcated reality. Upstream players and oilfield services companies (such as Antelopus Selan Energy and Deep Industries) are thriving, benefiting from robust volume growth, strong margins, and aggressive exploration/production tailwinds backed by government policy shifts like the 'Samudra Manthan' scheme. Conversely, City Gas Distribution (CGD) companies (Adani Total Gas, IRM Energy, Mahanagar Gas) and downstream/lubricant players (Gulf Oil Lubricants) are navigating significant margin pressures caused by volatile raw material costs, West Asian geopolitical tensions, domestic HPHT (High-Pressure High-Temperature) gas allocation shifts, and the withdrawal of pooled gas support.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** CNG and domestic PNG demand remain resilient and structurally strong across all CGD operators, driven by expanding vehicle fleet additions and household connections. Upstream and service providers report robust asset utilization and multi-year order visibility.
* **Divergence:** 
  * **Industrial/Commercial Segments:** Mahanagar Gas reported supply restrictions and volume contractions in industrial/commercial segments due to RLNG shortages and relative fuel economics. In contrast, Adani Total Gas noted that H1 industrial and commercial additions reached 1.4x the total additions for the entirety of FY26. IRM Energy noted sequential recovery in industrial PNG after pressure from alternate fuels like fuel oil and coal.
  * **Crude Buyers:** Antelopus Selan highlighted that private buyers reduce purchases when crude approaches $100/bbl, forcing reliance on public sector buyers like Indian Oil.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreement:** Managements across the CGD sector (ATGL, IRM Energy, Mahanagar Gas) cited severe input cost pressures stemming from higher Brent/LNG prices, currency depreciation, and regulatory changes in domestic gas allocation (HPHT and the withdrawal of pooled gas support). 
* **Disagreement & Divergence in Mitigation:** 
  * *Pricing strategy:* ATGL and IRM Energy utilized calibrated price hikes, absorbing some costs to protect volume growth. Mahanagar Gas indicated that a price hike of INR 1 to INR 1.5 per SCM would restore target unit margins. 
  * *Sourcing mixes:* ATGL reduced spot reliance to 7-8%, while IRM Energy kept spot near 0.5% and Mahanagar Gas utilized ~4% pure spot LNG priced between $22–$23. 
  * *Lubricants:* Gulf Oil successfully protected per-litre margins in an inflationary base-oil environment through calibrated price increases, despite optical compression of percentage EBITDA margins.

## Capex & capacity
* **Antelopus Selan:** Set FY27 capex at Rs 250 crore, focusing on Bakrol, Karjisan, and KG Onshore.
* **Deep Industries:** Planned ~Rs 150 crore capex for production enhancement contracts and Rs 100–200 crore for Dolphin Offshore.
* **Gulf Oil:** Revised capex to Rs 60–62 crore for plant expansions in Chennai and Silvassa.
* **Adani Total Gas:** Aiming for 80–90 CNG stations and >2 lakh household connections in FY27.
* **IRM Energy:** Total FY27 capex targeted at Rs 250–260 crore.
* **Mahanagar Gas:** Maintaining annual capex guidance at Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 1,800 crore, expecting negative free cash flows of Rs 200–300 crore.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Adani Total Gas:** Shifted tone from "waiting for pooled gas reinstatement" (Q1) to "navigating pressures through portfolio diversification and agile sourcing" (Q2); maintained aggressive expansion targets.
* **IRM Energy:** Flagged caution for Q3 due to regulatory HPHT gas allocation changes and raw material cost spikes, contrasting with strong Q2 volume growth.
* **Mahanagar Gas:** Noted increased caution due to West Asian conflicts, Strait of Hormuz uncertainties, and industrial supply restrictions impacting profitability.

## Company one-liners
* **Adani Total Gas:** Navigating high input costs via agile sourcing and volume growth while pivoting EV strategy to B2B captive charging.
* **Antelopus Selan:** Delivering strong cash flows and rising production toward its 2,500 BOEPD exit target on a debt-free balance sheet.
* **Deep Industries:** Capitalizing on India's upstream push with a ~Rs 3,089 crore order book and strong double-digit revenue growth.
* **Gulf Oil:** Driving volume-led growth (16% YoY) and protecting per-litre margins while expanding EV and lubricant footprints.
* **IRM Energy:** Maintaining a net-cash balance sheet and strong Q2 earnings while proactively managing temporary regulatory shifts in HPHT gas.
* **Mahanagar Gas:** Facing margin compression from high spot LNG and industrial supply curbs, yet maintaining aggressive capex and volume growth plans.

## What to watch next quarter
* **CGD Operators (ATGL, IRM Energy, MGL):** Resolution and financial impact of the phased MoPNG HPHT gas allocation formula changes and spot LNG price volatility.
* **Upstream & Services (Antelopus Selan, Deep Industries):** Execution on KG Onshore and Cambay basin development wells, along with ONGC production enhancement contract milestones.
* **Gulf Oil:** Progress on plant expansions (Chennai/Silvassa) and raw material price stability (base oils and crude).
