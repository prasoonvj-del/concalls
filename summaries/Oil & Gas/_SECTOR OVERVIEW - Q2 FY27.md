# Oil & Gas: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Oil & Gas sector reflected a resilient domestic demand environment paired with significant external headwinds. Upstream and refining players like **Reliance Industries (RIL)**, **MRPL**, and **Deep Industries** reported robust financial performances driven by strong domestic fuel demand and high asset utilization. However, City Gas Distribution (CGD) players and lubricant manufacturers—such as **Mahanagar Gas (MGL)**, **Adani Total Gas (ATGL)**, **IRM Energy**, and **Castrol India**—faced margin squeezes due to surging raw material costs, adverse currency movements, West Asian geopolitical conflicts, and regulatory shifts in domestic HPHT (High-Pressure High-Temperature) natural gas allocations. 

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that structural domestic energy demand remains resilient. India's macro growth continues to drive consumption in transportation (CNG, retail fuels) and residential segments (DPNG, LPG).
*   **Divergence (CGD & Industrial):** While CGD players report strong volume growth in CNG and domestic PNG (e.g., ATGL CNG +17% YoY, MGL CNG +9.14% YoY, IRM CNG volume up 23% in H1), industrial and commercial segments faced severe friction. **MGL** reported a 7.48% YoY decline in industrial/commercial volumes due to ~70% RLNG supply restrictions. Conversely, **Deep Industries** and **Selan Energy** noted strong upstream demand tailwinds, though **Selan** observed that private crude buyers reduce purchases when oil nears $100/bbl, concentrating sales on public sector buyers like Indian Oil.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Raw Material Pressures:** Geopolitical tensions in West Asia and disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz drove up Brent, LNG, and freight costs. **Castrol India** reported a >60% YoY increase in raw material bills, severely impacting quarterly profits. **ATGL** noted a 43% YoY surge in natural gas costs compounded by the withdrawal of pooled gas support. 
*   **CGD Sourcing & Unit Margins:** CGD operators had to dynamically manage their sourcing mixes. **IRM Energy** reported an average sourcing cost of ~$9.5/MMBtu, with EBITDA per SCM at Rs 7.73. **MGL** reported a unit EBITDA margin of INR 6.77 per SCM and noted that a price hike of INR 1–1.5/SCM is needed to reach INR 7.
*   **Margin Defense:** Lubricant players like **Castrol India** and **Gulf Oil** emphasized defending absolute margin-per-litre rather than fixed percentage margins amid extreme base-oil and crude price volatility.

## Capex & capacity
Capital expenditure plans across the sector remain aggressive to capture long-term structural growth:
*   **Petronet LNG:** Maintained FY27 capex guidance at around Rs 9,000 crores, focusing on its petrochemical project.
*   **Aegis Logistics:** Projected aggregate capex investments of ~$5 billion through FY31, alongside a cumulative capex expectation of ~$1.2 billion by next year for Aegis Vopak.
*   **GSPL Transmission:** Advancing on an expansion capex program of ~INR 3,500 crore covering >700 km across >10 projects.
*   **Deep Industries:** Guiding ~Rs 150 crore for production enhancement contracts over the next 12 months, plus Rs 100–200 crore for Dolphin Offshore.
*   **MRPL:** FY27 capex guided between Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,500 crore for maintenance and retail expansion.
*   **ATGL:** Targeting 80–90 new CNG stations and >2 lakh household connections in FY27.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Adani Total Gas:** Shifted tone from "waiting for pooled gas reinstatement" in Q1 to actively "navigating pressures through portfolio diversification and agile sourcing" in Q2, while explicitly factoring in HPHT gas fluctuations.
*   **Aegis Logistics:** Announced a transformational definitive agreement to acquire an 89.8% stake in Tristar Holdings Limited for US$809 million.
*   **Petronet LNG:** Detailed active claims of Rs 625 crores regarding LNG charter companies alongside the suspension of 3 time-charter vessels.
*   **MRPL:** Navigated a planned shutdown and a localized fire incident at the Coker Hydrotreater Unit (CHTU) on September 30, 2026.

## Company one-liners
*   **Adani Total Gas:** Navigating high input costs via diversified gas sourcing and volume growth while shifting EV strategy to B2B.
*   **Aegis Logistics:** Delivered a record H1 supported by gas distribution efficiencies while launching a major $809M global acquisition (Tristar).
*   **Antelopus Selan Energy:** Debt-free onshore producer delivering stellar EBITDA margins (71%) and rapid volume scaling.
*   **Castrol India:** Facing short-term margin compression from raw material inflation while prioritizing per-litre profitability and new data center cooling products.
*   **Deep Industries:** Benefiting from upstream structural tailwinds, full asset utilization, and a secure ~Rs 3,089 crore order book.
*   **GSPL Transmission:** Debt-free grid operator delivering steady volume growth while funding its INR 3,500 crore expansion from internal accruals.
*   **Gulf Oil Lubricants:** Achieving 2x–3x industry volume growth while maintaining per-litre margins and expanding into EV infrastructure.
*   **IRM Energy:** Generated strong Q2 earnings with a net-cash position, proactively absorbing regulatory HPHT allocation shifts.
*   **Mahanagar Gas:** Hit by industrial gas supply restrictions and high spot LNG costs, leading to lower sequential EBITDA.
*   **MRPL:** Posted strong refining profits and aggressive debt reduction despite operational disruptions and freight inflation.
*   **Petronet LNG:** Achieved record H1 profitability through commercial efficiencies that offset lower long-term Qatari volumes.
*   **Reliance Industries:** Delivered record consolidated financials powered by O2C resilience and sustained growth in digital and retail segments.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Geopolitical & Freight Developments:** Ongoing West Asian conflicts, Strait of Hormuz shipping disruptions, and their impact on crude premiums, LNG spot prices, and freight rates (monitored closely by RIL, MRPL, MGL, and Petronet).
*   **Domestic Gas Policy:** Implementation pace of the phased changes to the HPHT gas allocation formula and its net impact on CGD margins (ATGL, MGL, IRM Energy).
*   **Project Execution & M&A Progress:** Closing and integration plans for Aegis Logistics' Tristar acquisition; Petronet LNG’s petrochemical project financial acceleration; and MRPL's CHTU restoration by mid-December 2026.
