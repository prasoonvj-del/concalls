## Big picture
Managements across the sector describe a **two-speed market**: upstream and midstream players are riding a structural volume and capex upcycle, while city-gas distributors and lubricant makers defend per-unit margins against volatile, elevated input costs. The common thread is **aggressive capital deployment** — from Aegis’s $809 mn Tristar buy and Petronet’s petchem pivot to Oil India’s ₹13,500 cr drilling plan and Reliance’s new-energy rollout — funded by strong balance sheets and internal accruals.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Indian oil & gas has historically monetised regulated returns (transmission, marketing margins) and import-parity pricing in refining. Over the last ~5 years the model has shifted toward **integrated value chains** (gas sourcing → transmission → last-mile distribution), **asset-light services** (compression, workover rigs, logistics), and **new-energy adjacencies** (EV charging, CBG, solar manufacturing) to de-risk commodity cyclicality [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **CGDs lock in long-term LNG to replace pooled/spot gas** – *scaling* – Adani Total Gas (75% non-APM mix, spot cut to 7-8%), Gujarat Energy (10-yr Henry Hub deals for 0.8 MTPA), Mahanagar Gas (40,000 MMBtu/d term contracts), IRM Energy (sourcing visibility to 2030) – locks in margins and reduces regulatory dependence [Adani Total Gas Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Gujarat Energy Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Mahanagar Gas Ltd concall Q2 FY27; IRM Energy Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **Upstream players accelerate drilling to reverse domestic production decline** – *scaling* – Oil India (42 wells H1, 100-110/yr target, 4 MMT run-rate), HOEC (B-80 workover, Kharsang 9 wells, 10k BOE target Jun-27), Antelopus Selan (Bakrol 10-well frac, Karjisan 7 wells, 2,500 BOEPD exit FY27) – directly grows high-margin barrels [Oil India Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Hindustan Oil Exploration Company Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Antelopus Selan Energy Ltd-$ concall Q2 FY27].
- **Midstream/logistics firms build global scale via M&A and petchem integration** – *early* – Aegis Logistics (Tristar $809 mn, $5 bn capex to FY31), Petronet LNG (₹3,300 cr petchem capex, 25-yr asset life), GSPL Transmission (₹3,500 cr, 700 km expansion), MRPL (OPML JV with ONGC/OPaL) – creates tolling-style earnings less exposed to crack spreads [Aegis Logistics Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Petronet LNG Ltd concall Q2 FY27; GSPL Transmission Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **Lubricant majors pivot to EV thermal management and data-centre cooling** – *early* – Castrol India (two NVIDIA-certified direct-to-chip fluids), Gulf Oil Lubricants (Tirex 3,000 chargers by year-end, Rs 33 cr Q2 revenue) – opens high-margin, non-commodity revenue streams [Castrol India Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Gulf Oil Lubricants India Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **Integrated conglomerates fund new-energy and digital transitions from O2C/telecom cash flows** – *mainstream* – Reliance Industries (Jio listed at $137 bn, 20 GW solar module/cell by 1H CY27, 44 MW data centre building) – demonstrates self-funding model for energy transition [Reliance Industries Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **Longer PSC extensions** – rewards upstream investment certainty; benefits Oil India (Palej extension), HOEC (Dirok 10-yr extension) [Govt: Finance Ministry, Nov 2026].
- **Piped-gas consumer incentive** – supports CGD volume growth; benefits Gujarat Energy, Mahanagar Gas, Adani Total Gas, IRM Energy [Govt: Ministry of Power, Nov 2026].
- **Ethanol blending 20% achieved** – structural demand for grain/sugar feedstock; indirect positive for OMCs and CBG players like Adani Total Gas, Gujarat Energy [Govt: Finance Ministry, Nov 2026].
- **Export duty hikes on diesel/ATF** – protects domestic supply but compresses refinery margins; impacts MRPL, Reliance O2C [Govt: Finance Ministry, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: **CNG/PNG demand growing 10-20% YoY** (Adani Total Gas, Gujarat Energy, Mahanagar Gas, IRM Energy, Confidence Petroleum). **Industrial gas demand resilient** but **fuel-switching risk** where propane/coal undercut RLNG (Gujarat Energy Morbi volumes halved QoQ, IRM Energy notes sequential recovery). **Refining cracks structurally tight** – gasoline ~$25/bbl, diesel ~$66/bbl, jet ~$59/bbl (Reliance). **Upstream gas evacuation bottlenecks** constrain Assam/Assam volumes (Oil India, HOEC, Prabha Energy).

**Divergence**: **Spot LNG price elasticity** – Petronet sees demand resilient at $20-25/MMBtu; Mahanagar Gas flags industrial curtailments at same prices. **EV adoption pace** – Castrol sees pilots only; Gulf Oil scales Tirex to 3,000 chargers. **CBG commercial viability** – Adani Total Gas “slightly lower steps” on feedstock/tech; Gujarat Energy issued EOIs.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Gas cost inflation universal**: Adani Total Gas +43% YoY (Brent/LNG, FX, pooled gas withdrawal); Gujarat Energy 55% imported mix; Mahanagar Gas spot LNG $22-23; IRM Energy avg $9.5/MMBtu. **CGD unit margins compressed** – Mahanagar Gas ₹6.77/SCM (target ₹7 with ₹1-1.5 hike), Gujarat Energy guidance cut to ₹5-6/SCM, IRM Energy guides ~15% EBITDA margin H2. **Lubricants**: Castrol raw material bills +60% YoY, Gulf Oil base oil lag 45-60 days, both defend per-litre margin. **Refining**: MRPL freight $7-9/bbl (3x YoY), Reliance AG-Japan naphtha freight $122/t (3x). **Upstream**: HOEC $20-30/BOE opex, Antelopus Selan fixed cost 70-75% of base (operating leverage).

## Capex & capacity
**Massive, multi-year programs**: Aegis Logistics $5 bn to FY31 (Tristar + organic), Oil India ₹13,500-14,000 cr FY27 (drilling, surveys, equipment), Petronet LNG ~₹9,000 cr FY27/FY28 (petchem + regas), Gujarat Energy ₹1,000 cr FY27, Adani Total Gas 80-90 CNG stations, GSPL Transmission ₹3,500 cr (700 km), Reliance 20 GW solar module/cell by 1H CY27, 44 MW data centre. **Services**: Deep Industries ₹150 cr production enhancement + ₹100-200 cr Dolphin Offshore, Jindal Drilling ₹600 cr rig refurb/JV over 15-18 months. **CGD infrastructure**: Confidence Petroleum 100 CNG stations Bengaluru in 3-4 quarters, IRM Energy ₹250-260 cr FY27.
