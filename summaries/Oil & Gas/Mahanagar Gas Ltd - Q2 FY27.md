# Mahanagar Gas Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited West Asia conflicts, Strait of Hormuz uncertainties, supply restrictions in industrial/commercial segments, and volatile gas costs affecting margins, though CNG and domestic PNG volumes grew well.

### Key numbers
- **Sales volume:** Overall 4.89 MMSCMD (up 6.49% YoY from 4.59 MMSCMD; up 2.63% QoQ from 4.766 MMSCMD).
- **CNG volume:** 3.55 MMSCMD (up 9.14% YoY from 3.26 MMSCMD; up 1.63% QoQ from 3.496 MMSCMD).
- **Domestic DPNG volume:** 0.64 MMSCMD (up 9.84% YoY from 0.58 MMSCMD; up 2.62% QoQ from 0.623 MMSCMD).
- **Industrial & Commercial volume:** 0.70 MMSCMD (down 7.48% YoY from 0.76 MMSCMD; up 8.07% QoQ from 0.648 MMSCMD).
- **EBITDA from operations (Q2 FY27):** INR 287 crore (compared to INR 343 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 630 crore (compared to INR 839 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Net Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27):** INR 137 crore (compared to INR 194 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **H1 FY27 Net Profit After Tax:** INR 331 crore (compared to INR 513 crore in H1 FY26).
- **DPNG Conversions:** 70,501 added in Q2; cumulative 2.24 million as of September 30, 2026.
- **Pipeline added:** 65 km of steel and PE pipeline in Q2; total over 8,500 km.
- **CNG Stations added:** 9 added in Q2; total 528 stations.
- **Industrial & Commercial customers added:** 248 added in Q2; total 6,409.
- **CNG vehicles added:** 29,524 added in Q2; total over 1.34 million registered.

### Highlights
- Sourced ~4% pure spot LNG and ~11% including IGX HPHT spot at company level volume for Q2. Spot LNG priced between $22–$23.
- Implemented long-term pay correction for employees during the quarter, causing a one-time actuarial valuation impact of INR 10-12 crore due to interest rate and salary revision changes.
- Continued marketing schemes for large-scale commercial vehicles (>10 tons); registered over 800 vehicles, disbursed INR 3.5-4 crore for 110 delivered vehicles in Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects overall volume growth to be more than 8% to 9% for FY27 and FY28 once normal supply levels are restored.
- Management maintains capex guidance in the range of INR 1,500 crore to INR 1,800 crore for the year.
- Free cash flow (operating cash flow minus capex) is expected to be negative by INR 200-300 crore for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic PNG and CNG supplies are 100% / majorly sourced from domestic gas, ensuring uninterrupted supply.
- Gas supplies to industrial and commercial customers are partly curtailed (~70% RLNG supplies restricted).
- CNG is offered at ~45% discount compared to petrol and ~10%-11% discount compared to diesel.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Unit EBITDA margin for Q2 was around INR 6.77 per SCM. Management stated that a price hike of INR 1 to INR 1.5 per SCM could achieve a INR 7 margin if conditions remain stable.
- Sourcing mix for CNG: ~60% domestic gas (split equally between APM/NWG and HPHT) and ~40% term/spot gas (~4% spot). Domestic PNG is 100% APM. Industrial & commercial is 100% RLNG term contracts (Henry Hub and Brent-linked).
- HPHT allocation formula is being phased out over the next 7-8 months (25% new formula in Nov-Dec, 50% in Jan-Mar, 75% in Apr-Jun, 100% from July onwards), which management expects will increase their HPHT allocation from ~5,000-6,000 MMBtu/day to ~16,000-18,000 MMBtu/day.
- Tied up new term gas of around 40,000 MMBTU (Brent and Henry Hub linked) with formula tweaking to manage costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- First-half capex run rate is seasonally lower due to monsoon restrictions on laying pipelines from mid-May to October; work picks up from October.
- Scaling up capex is subject to availability of manpower and materials.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company holds a cash surplus of almost INR 1,000 crore.
- Debt is not warranted for funding capex; cash inflows from operations are expected between INR 1,000 to INR 1,200 crore, alongside customer deposits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia conflicts and Strait of Hormuz uncertainties impacting global LNG availability, prices, and RLNG supply restrictions.
- Volatility in gas costs and potential cuts/curtailments on signed term contracts.
- Industry-wide constraints on manpower and material availability for scaling up capex.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities):** Asked about spot LNG percentage and pricing during the quarter. *Management answered that pure spot was ~4% of CNG volumes (~2.5% at company level) priced at $22-$23, and detailed the HPHT formula phase-out.*
2. **Yogesh Patil:** Asked about unit EBITDA margin guidance and required CNG price hikes for INR 7 margin. *Management stated Q2 margin was ~6.77, and a INR 1-1.5 hike would achieve INR 7; declined to give explicit annual margin guidance due to volatility.*
3. **Bineet Banka (Nomura):** Inquired if negative free cash flow would necessitate debt or liquidating mutual funds. *Management replied that debt is not warranted as they hold ~INR 1,000 crore surplus and positive operating cash flows.*
4. **Khushboo Ravalani (Aquarius Securities):** Asked about long-term sourcing strategy and the 40,000 MMBTU term contract. *Management confirmed the 40,000 MMBTU (Brent and HH-linked) is signed and flowing, and that term contracts are tied up incrementally while relying on spot only for peaks.*

### One-line takeaway
Mahanagar Gas reported lower Q2 EBITDA (INR 287 crore) and PAT (INR 137 crore) due to industrial gas supply curtailments and high LNG spot prices, but remains optimistic on achieving 8-9% volume growth and maintaining INR 1,500+ crore capex.
