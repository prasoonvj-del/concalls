# Petronet LNG Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the company's best-ever second quarter and half-year in terms of both PBT and PAT, driven by operational and commercial efficiencies despite lower long-term Qatari volumes.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 capex guidance at around Rs 9,000 crores (noted as Rs 9,064 crores last quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remained positive; demand resilience was highlighted despite high spot prices ($20 to $25 range).
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Detailed claims regarding LNG charter companies (Rs 625 crores) and the suspension of 3 time-charter vessels were discussed in depth.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Capex execution is on schedule with major orders placed; Kochi-Bangalore pipeline connectivity is now guided within this financial year (GAIL stating December).

### Key numbers
- LNG Processed Volume: 231 TBTU (up 12% QoQ from 207 TBTU, up 1% YoY from 228 TBTU)
- Dahej Throughput: 216 TBTU (vs 192 TBTU in Q1 FY27, 211 TBTU in Q2 FY26)
- Dahej Capacity Utilization: 73% (vs 66% in Q1 FY27, 92% in Q2 FY26) on expanded 22.5 MMTPA capacity
- Overall Capacity Utilization: 64% (vs 58% in Q1 FY27, 78% in Q2 FY26)
- Standalone PBT: Rs 1,659 crore (up 10% QoQ from Rs 1,514 crore, up 53% YoY from Rs 1,083 crore)
- Standalone PAT: Rs 1,235 crore (up 9% QoQ from Rs 1,133 crore, up 53% YoY from Rs 806 crore)
- H1 FY27 PBT: Rs 3,174 crore (up 43% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 2,368 crore (up 43% YoY)
- Operational and Commercial Efficiency Gains: Rs 658 crore (vs Rs 494 crore in Q1 FY27, Rs 41 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Regasification Revenue: Rs 1,272 crore
- Forex Loss on Lease Accounting: Rs 27 crore
- H1 FY27 Capex Spent: Rs 1,626 crore (Petrochemical project cumulative capex: Rs 3,300 crore up to Sept 30)
- Interim Dividend: Rs 7 per share

### Highlights
- Delivered best-ever Q2 and H1 PBT and PAT in company history.
- Dahej throughput and utilization figures are based on the expanded nameplate capacity of 22.5 MMTPA (vs 17.5 MMTPA last year).
- Time-charter agreements for 3 vessels are suspended; lessors are free to use them. Claims of Rs 625 crores exist with LNG charter companies alongside counterclaims.
- Gorgon volume at Dahej stood at 2 TBTU for the quarter (12 TBTU YTD).
- Board approved an interim dividend of Rs 7 per share.

### Guidance & outlook
- Capex guidance for FY27 maintained at around Rs 9,000 crores; FY28 expected at a similar level, with a gradual trough starting from FY29 onwards.
- Physical project progress for the petrochemical plant is on schedule; financial progress expected to accelerate in H2.
- Kochi-Bangalore pipeline expected to connect to the national grid within this financial year (management conservative vs GAIL's December timeline).
- Long-term capacity tie-up discussions with existing off-takers and new players are ongoing.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's gas demand remains resilient despite spot prices in the $20–$25 range (March to September).
- Calendar year 2026 (first 9 months) imports reached 20.15 million tonnes.
- Decline in Qatari and UAE volumes was compensated by sources including the US, Oman, Nigeria, Angola, Trinidad, and Australia.
- Key consuming sectors driving demand are City Gas Distribution (CGD), Fertilizer, and Power. Refinery and petchem sectors are currently optimizing with liquid fuels due to volume shortages but could drive further upside if prices soften.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operational and commercial efficiency gains reached Rs 658 crore, capturing internal efficiencies, fuel cost savings, boil-off minimization, and commercial opportunities.
- Business model balances high spot prices (gaining via operational efficiencies/trading) with low spot prices (protecting margins via long-term volumes).
- Useful life of the upcoming petrochemical facility is taken as 25 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total H1 FY27 capex incurred was Rs 1,626 crore. Petrochemical project incurred cumulative capex of Rs 3,300 crore; physical progress is on schedule.
- Dahej capacity remains 22.5 MMTPA.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Time-charter vessel lease payments have been suspended along with vessel operations.
- Interim dividend of Rs 7 per share declared.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions and Force Majeure declarations by Qatar Energy impacting Qatari LNG flows through the Strait of Hormuz.
- Potential volatility in depreciation and lease accounting due to ongoing force majeure modifications.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mayank Maheshwari (Morgan Stanley):** Asked about India's gas demand elasticity and petchem progress. Management noted strong demand resilience despite high prices and confirmed major petchem orders are placed with H2 capex set to accelerate.
- **Bineet Banka (Nomura):** Inquired about the Rs 625 crore charter claims and vessel sub-chartering. Management explained claims involve associated costs and counterclaims, and suspended vessels are under lessor control.
- **Achal Shah (Analyst):** Asked if efficiency gains would reverse with falling spot prices. Management stated efficiencies are permanent, but monetization is optimized when prices are high.
- **Amit Murarka (Analyst):** Asked about contract renewals starting April 2028 and take-or-pay for the 5 MMTPA Dahej expansion. Management stated discussions are ongoing and expect tie-ups to finalize before contract commencement and upon spot market normalization.
- **Saurabh Handa (Analyst):** Asked about Qatar sourcing US LNG to service contracts. Management noted Qatar's US tie-ups start in 2030-2031, making them currently irrelevant for immediate contract remediation.

### One-line takeaway
Petronet LNG posted its highest-ever second quarter and half-year PBT and PAT, driven by resilient domestic gas demand and Rs 658 crore in operational and commercial efficiencies that offset lower Qatari long-term volumes.
