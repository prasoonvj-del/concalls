# Maximus International Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 28% YoY in Q2 (39% YoY in H1) driven by strong international demand and new customer acquisitions, despite margin headwinds from geopolitical tensions.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Raised. Management raised its FY27 top-line guidance from crossing Rs 200 crore (stated in Q1) to a pessimistic basis of Rs 225 crores to Rs 250+ crores.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more optimistic on top-line execution and new customer additions from regions like China and North Africa, despite ongoing Middle East war pressures.
- New topics or risks: Mention of boarding major customers from China, exploring expansion in Southeast Asia, and the Board approval for an export-oriented manufacturing facility and product development center in Vadodara, India, alongside acquiring a 40% stake in Quebec Petroleum Resources Limited.
- Last quarter's promises: Grease manufacturing facility in Kenya remains on track for commissioning in Q3 FY27. Tanzania warehousing entry is targeted for the end of this financial year.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 58.74 crore (up 28% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 118.64 crores (up ~39% YoY from Rs 85 crores in H1 FY26)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 4.60 crores
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 9.18 crores (up 5.65% YoY from Rs 8.69 crores in H1 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 7.74% (compared to 10.18% in H1 FY26)
- **Consolidated Net Profit / PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 2.05 crores
- **Consolidated Net Profit / PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 4.10 crores (compared to Rs 5.12 crores in H1 FY26)
- **Consolidated Borrowings (H1 FY27):** Approximately Rs 51 crores
- **Manufacturing Capacity:** Combined ~50,000 KL across UAE and Kenya
- **Capacity Utilisation:** ~50% on average, aiming for 55% to 58% by the end of FY27

### Highlights
- Board approved establishing an export-oriented manufacturing facility and product development center in India (acquiring ~190,000 sq. ft. of industrial land near Vadodara) to manufacture conventional, grease, and high-end/specialty lubricants.
- Progressing on acquiring a 40% equity stake in Quebec Petroleum Resources Limited to enter the Indian domestic market.
- Onboarded major customers from China and expanded distribution footprints in North Africa and Southeast Asia.
- Setting up a grease manufacturing facility at the Kenya plant, targeted for Q3 FY27.
- Exploring expansion in Tanzania, targeting warehousing and distribution operations by the end of FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a consolidated top line of Rs 225 crore to Rs 250+ crore for FY27 and FY28.
- Aiming for a 50 bps to 100 bps increase in EBITDA margins through better operating efficiency, optimized financing, and credit terms.
- Indian manufacturing facility expected to generate Rs 100 crore in revenue in its first year and Rs 125 crore to Rs 150 crore in the second financial year, with EBITDA margins of 8.5% to 9% and PAT of 6.5% to 7%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rising industrialization, infrastructure investments, and increasing vehicle ownership support global lubricant demand, particularly in the Middle East and East Africa.
- The company successfully stepped in to execute orders where peers and local competitors failed to meet local market demands due to supply chain disruptions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins compressed YoY due to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East causing volatility in base oils and additives, alongside a 50% to 60% surge in landed logistics, freight, insurance, and distribution costs.
- Lubricant manufacturing gross margins stand at 17% to 18%, while trading margins average 11% to 12%. Specialty lubricants command ~50% higher margins than conventional lubricants.
- Finance costs increased proportionately with higher borrowings (approx. Rs 51 crore) used to fund top-line growth and new customer deals in UAE and Kenya.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Combined capacity stands at ~50,000 KL (UAE and Kenya), currently running at ~50% utilization.
- New Vadodara facility will cater to exports, featuring units for conventional automotive/industrial oils, grease, and high-end/specialty lubricants.
- Tanzania warehousing and distribution setup targeted for operationalization by the end of FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated borrowings reached approximately Rs 51 crores in H1 FY27, driven by higher working capital needs for scaled top-line operations.
- Management noted that upcoming Indian facilities will benefit from government capital and interest subsidy schemes to reduce the finance cost burden.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing Middle East war and US tariff wars causing volatility in base oil raw material prices and inflated freight/landed costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arnav (Dianta Corporates) asked why customers choose Maximus over peers:** Management explained they maintained supply commitments and product quality without revenue dips throughout historical crises (GST, demonetization, COVID, wars), stepping in when competitors failed to deliver. *(Answered clearly)*
- **Arnav asked about the rationale behind increased borrowings and finance costs:** Management stated that borrowings grew proportionately with the top line to fund new customer deals in UAE and Kenya, and noted that upcoming Indian facilities will benefit from government subsidies to lower finance burdens. *(Answered clearly)*
- **Arnav asked about FY27 and FY28 outlook:** Management guided for consolidated revenues of Rs 225 crore to Rs 250+ crore, aiming for a 50 bps to 100 bps improvement in EBITDA margins. *(Answered clearly)*
- **Prit Patel (Patel Investments) asked about segment-wise contribution and margins:** Management stated that lubricant manufacturing contributes ~65% and trading ~35% (with manufacturing/toll blending expected to lead long-term), with manufacturing gross margins at 17% to 18% and trading at 11% to 12%. *(Answered clearly)*
- **Prit Patel asked about revenue and margin expectations for the new Indian facility:** Management expects Rs 100 crore in year one and Rs 125 crore to Rs 150 crore in year two, with 8.5% to 9% EBITDA margins and 6.5% to 7% PAT margins. *(Answered clearly)*

### One-line takeaway
Maximus International posted a 39% YoY revenue increase to Rs 118.64 crores for H1 FY27, though profitability faced pressure from Middle East war-induced raw material and logistics inflation.
