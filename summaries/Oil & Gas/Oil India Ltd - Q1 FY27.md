# Oil India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, PBT, and PAT, alongside robust crude oil production growth.

### Key numbers
- Crude oil production: 0.95 MMT (+11%+ YoY)
- Total oil and oil equivalent production: 1.707 million tonnes
- Highest daily crude production: 10,921 MT per day on 27 June 2026 (subsequently increased to 11,017 MT per day on 3 August 2026)
- Crude oil price realization: USD 98.73 per barrel (vs USD 66.20 per barrel YoY)
- Natural gas price: USD 7.19 per MMBtu (vs USD 6.72 in previous quarter of last FY)
- Standalone Operating Revenue: INR 7,958 crore
- Standalone EBITDA: INR 4,605 crore (Margin: 54%+ vs 34%+ in previous quarter of last year)
- Standalone PBT: INR 3,742 crore
- Standalone PAT: INR 2,870 crore (vs INR 813 crore YoY)
- Standalone EPS: INR 17.65 per share (vs INR 5 YoY)
- NRL Operating Income: INR 9,146 crore (+45% YoY)
- NRL Capacity Utilization: 105% (Distillate yield: 87%)
- NRL Gross Refinery Margin (GRM): USD 35.95 per barrel (vs USD 5.02 YoY), including USD 2 inventory gain
- NRL EBITDA: INR 1,843 crore (vs INR 786 crore YoY)
- NRL PAT: INR 1,305 crore (vs INR 488 crore YoY)
- Consolidated Operating Revenue: INR 12,886 crore
- Consolidated PAT: INR 4,026 crore
- Q1 FY27 Total Capex Spent: INR 3,050 crore (Survey: INR 450 cr, Exploratory drilling: INR 1,230 cr, Development drilling: INR 700 cr, Capital equipment/projects: INR 350 cr, Subsidiary/JV/overseas investment: INR 350 cr)
- Total Group Debt: INR 37,233 crore (includes USD 1.4 billion Mozambique loan, USD 500 million Singapore bond, and INR 19,000+ crore NRL debt)

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly standalone revenue, EBITDA, PBT, and PAT since listing in FY09-10.
- Drilled 17 new wells during the quarter (7 exploratory and 10 development).
- NRL subsidiary delivered stellar performance with 105% capacity utilization and a GRM of USD 35.95 per barrel.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets ~1 MMT crude production every quarter, aiming for total production of ~3.9 to 4.0 MMT in FY27.
- Management targets crude production of ~4.2 MMT by FY29.
- Drilling target for FY27 is 100 wells (42 exploratory, 57 development).
- NRL expansion: CDU VDU mechanically completed; DHDT and SRU units expected by October/November 2026; remaining units targeted by 31 March 2027. Management expects to hit 75% capacity utilization of the 9 MMT capacity by the end of FY28.
- Paradip-Numaligarh pipeline: Mechanical completion targeted for October 2026, commissioning by December 2026.
- Natural gas production targeted at ~3.8 BCM for next year, scaling towards 5 BCM by FY29 with upcoming pipeline connectivity (DFL/DNPL).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Gas off-take remains constrained by regional downstream shutdowns (e.g., BCPL) and seasonal variations (e.g., NEEPCO utilizing hydro power when gas prices are high).
- DNPL designated as a common carrier; a 200-meter connectivity within NRL to IGGL will enable capacity ramp-up from 1 to 2-2.5 MMSCMD within 2-3 months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin expanded to 54%+ due to higher crude price realizations.
- NRL normalized GRM is USD 33 per barrel (excluding USD 2 inventory gain).
- NRL provided discounts to OMCs based on Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED); petrol discounts ranged from INR 13 to INR 3 per liter, and diesel discounts from INR 10 to nil.
- NRL per barrel operating cost expected to decline from USD 4.5-5.0 to USD 3.5 post-expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Full-year FY27 capex budget estimate stands at INR 8,600 crore (subject to revision).
- Total spend on NRL project so far is INR 30,000 crore; refinery project completion expected at INR 34,000-35,000 crore, and PPU project at INR 7,200-7,300 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total group debt is INR 37,233 crore.
- GST on royalty principal liability is ~INR 2,500 crore (excluding interest); fully provided for historically, with payment planned within 6 weeks per court undertaking.
- Assam land tax case: Government of Assam gave an undertaking to withdraw the law; carried as contingent liability with no financial impact.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Downstream evacuation bottlenecks for natural gas in the North-East pending completion of major pipelines (DFL and DNPL connectivity).
- Geopolitical and international product price volatility impacting OMC product discounts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities):** Asked about the crude production run-rate and gas monetization/pipeline timelines. Management confirmed expectations of ~1 MMT per quarter and a major gas evacuation jump by FY28/FY29.
2. **Vivekanand:** Asked about drilling plans and NRL normalized GRM. Management stated 100 wells are targeted for FY27, and NRL's normalized GRM is USD 33 per barrel.
3. **Somaiya V:** Asked about NRL expansion status and debt levels. Management noted CDU VDU is mechanically complete, total NRL spend is INR 30,000 crore, and NRL debt is INR 19,000+ crore.
4. **Bineet:** Asked about GST on royalty and Assam land tax. Management clarified that the ~INR 2,500 crore GST on royalty principal is already provisioned and will be paid shortly, while the land tax is a contingent liability awaiting formal withdrawal.

### One-line takeaway
Oil India posted record-high Q1 FY27 financial and operational results, propelled by strong crude realizations, higher output, and exceptional profitability at subsidiary Numaligarh Refinery.
