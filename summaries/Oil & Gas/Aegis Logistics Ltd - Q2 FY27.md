# Aegis Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong year-on-year growth in EBITDA and PAT, highlighted by a record first half and a transformational acquisition announcement.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained a disciplined funding approach with a target gearing of no more than 0.6x and a cumulative capex plan of ~$5 billion through FY31, alongside a cumulative capex expectation of ~$1.2 billion by next year for Aegis Vopak.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from geopolitical sourcing challenges in Q1 to normalization of global supply conditions while sustaining robust growth driven by capacity additions.
- New topics or risks: Announced the planned acquisition of Tristar Holdings Limited for US$809 million.
- Last quarter's promises: Commissioned the 35,000 metric ton LPG bottling plant at JNPT on October 15, 2026.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,213 crore (Aegis Logistics consolidated) / Rs 505 crore (AVTL H1 FY27, up 15% YoY)
- **Normalized EBITDA:** Rs 456 crore (up 32% YoY for Aegis Logistics) / Rs 212 crore (up 22% YoY for AVTL) / Rs 1,184 crore (H1 FY27, up 97% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 321 crore (up 32% YoY for Aegis Logistics) / Rs 96.4 crore (AVTL Q2 FY27) / Rs 866 crore (H1 FY27, up 108% YoY)
- **EPS:** Rs 7.42 (up 45% YoY for Q2) / Rs 21.23 (H1 FY27, up 140% YoY)
- **Gas Division EBITDA:** Rs 309.25 crore (Q2 FY27, up 34% YoY) / Rs 900 crore (H1 FY27, up 137% YoY)
- **Gas Distribution Volumes:** 2.41 lakh metric tons (up 25% YoY) / 5.18 lakh metric tons (H1 FY27, up 54% YoY)
- **Logistics Throughput Volumes:** 1.34 million metric tons (Q2 FY27, down 5% YoY, up 19% QoQ)
- **Sourcing Volumes:** 0.9 lakh metric tons (Q2 FY27) / 2.1 lakh metric tons (H1 FY27)
- **Liquid Division Revenue:** Rs 187 crore (Q2 FY27, up 21% YoY) / Rs 366 crore (H1 FY27, up 22% YoY)
- **Liquid Division EBITDA:** Rs 147 crore (Q2 FY27, up 27% YoY) / Rs 284 crore (H1 FY27, up 28% YoY)
- **AVTL Revenue:** Rs 217 crore (Q2 FY27, up 16.6% YoY)

### Highlights
- Signed a definitive agreement on October 13, 2026, to acquire an 89.8% stake in Tristar Holdings Limited for US$809 million, expected to complete in Q4 FY27.
- Commissioned an LPG bottling plant with an annual capacity of 35,000 metric tons at JNPT on October 15, 2026.
- Liquid Division delivered its highest H1 performance ever with revenues of Rs 366 crore and EBITDA of Rs 284 crore (78% margin).
- Mangalore liquid storage capacity (75,000 cubic meters added last year) is fully operational at 100% utilization.
- Acquired an additional 3 acres of land at Haldia Port.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to touch 1 million tons of distribution volumes in FY27.
- Management projects aggregate capex investments of approximately $5 billion through 2030-2031, with cumulative capex reaching ~$1.2 billion by next year.
- Management targets a debt gearing of no more than 0.6x for AVTL.
- The Tristar acquisition is expected to close in Q4 of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global supply conditions have largely normalized, with growth driven by new capacity additions, increased geographic reach, and new product additions (ammonia).
- India's LPG demand is driven by income effects, GDP growth, and energy transition away from dirty fuels like wooden briquettes.
- Management highlighted that India holds roughly 1 million metric tons of static LPG storage capacity (less than 10 days storage), leaving significant room for infrastructure expansion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Distribution business maintained a healthy EBITDA margin of approximately Rs 7,000 per metric ton due to supply chain and procurement efficiencies.
- Liquid Division EBITDA realization is tracking toward Rs 3,000 per CBM for the year.
- AVTL EBITDA margins expanded by approximately 372 bps YoY to 78.6% in Q2 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Mumbai Port:** Developing an additional 64,000 cubic meters of liquid storage at an investment of Rs 125 crore; progressing on schedule.
- **JNPA Port:** Greenfield expansion of 318,000 cubic meters of liquids and ~77,286 metric tons of LPG storage (total investment ~Rs 1,675 crore); first phase of ~100,000 cubic meters of liquid storage to be commissioned in Q3 FY27. Also progressing on a 52,000 metric ton refrigerated double-wall LPG storage tank.
- **Kandla Port:** CRL-4 liquid terminal adding 94,148 cubic meters is underway, with commissioning targeted for next financial year.
- **Kochi Port:** Expansion of 49,577 cubic meters is underway, expected to be commissioned in early FY28, taking total capacity to 132,000 cubic meters.
- **Mangalore Port:** Evaluating the addition of a further 60,000 cubic meters of liquid storage on newly secured land.
- **Pipelines:** Jamnagar-Loni pipeline is complete and operational. Kandla-Gorakhpur pipeline is approximately 96% physically complete, with the Kandla-Lucknow section charged with LPG.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated available cash stood at Rs 6,400 crores as of September, supporting the Tristar acquisition without taking on excessive debt.
- EGM passed a resolution to fund up to Rs 6,000 crores.
- AVTL capex of $5 billion is planned to be funded via a balanced mix of equity, internal accruals, and debt, targeting a gearing of <=0.6x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher finance costs and tax expenses impacted profit after tax growth at the AVTL level.
- Geopolitical uncertainties and global market fluctuations previously impacted Q1 logistics throughput.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vibhav Zutshi (JP Morgan) on Tristar integration and synergies:** Asked about integration challenges and procurement synergies. Management stated continuity of Tristar's 30-year management will ensure no operational disturbance, while sharing of capabilities and global assets will create mutual value; detailed synergies will be shared post-completion. (Management did not fully detail specific numerical synergies due to deal constraints).
- **Vibhav Zutshi on distribution profitability:** Asked if distribution margin returns to Rs 7,000/ton post-normalization. Management confirmed Q2 achieved Rs 7,000+ per ton driven by supply chain efficiencies rather than pricing power.
- **Satyam Kesarwani (Prabhudas Lilladher) on LPG market share:** Asked about current market share in static LPG capacity. Management stated Aegis holds over 30% of India's LPG static capacity.
- **Priyankar Biswas (JM Financial) on Tristar source/destination synergies:** Asked if Tristar bridges source and destination. Management noted deep relationships with Middle East producers (ADNOC, Saudi Aramco) and global accounts (Shell, BP, Total, Glencore) will be leveraged for Aegis.
- **Kunal Mehta (InCred Equities) on funding the Tristar acquisition and capex:** Asked how the company plans to fund the $809 million acquisition and $5 billion capex. Management reiterated that existing available cash of Rs 6,400 crores covers the acquisition, while AVTL capex will follow a disciplined 0.6x gearing model funded by equity and internal accruals.

### One-line takeaway
Aegis Logistics reported a record first half in FY27, backed by robust gas distribution margins and liquid capacity ramp-ups, while announcing a transformational US$809 million acquisition of Tristar Holdings Limited.
