# Oil India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Exceptionally well performance across all parameters, with highest ever half-yearly EBITDA, PBT, and PAT.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained ~4.0 million ton oil production target for FY27; PNCPL crude oil pipeline commissioning target shifted from December to Q4 (March).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly regarding natural gas constraints and pipeline delays, noting continued regional off-take limitations while oil production momentum remained strong.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: A fire in the installation tank farm (ITF) impacting operations temporarily (managed via bypass); specific well write-offs discussed in detail (Vijayapuram-1/Andaman wells).
- Last quarter's promises: Paradip-Numaligarh pipeline (PNCPL) completion slipped from December 2026 to Q4; NREP expansion target for March 2027 remains on track.

### Highlights
- Drilled 42 new wells during H1 (16 exploratory, 26 development); targeting ~100 wells for the full year.
- Management highlighted a gas discovery in KG-DSF and an oil discovery in the Cambay Basin.
- NRL NREP project completion targeted for March 2027; PNCPL pipeline 95% complete and targeted for Q4 commissioning.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain crude production around the 4 million tonne run rate (+/- 2%-3%) for the next couple of years.
- Gas production growth constrained in the near term by evacuation/pipeline limits, targeting full connectivity in 18-20 months.
- NRL capacity expansion ramp-up targeted at 50%-60% in Q3 of next financial year, reaching 100% in FY28-FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Natural gas off-take constrained by regional factors (e.g., fertilizer plant shutdowns).
- Global product FOB prices softened sequentially in Q2 compared to Q1, impacting refinery margins while crude prices remained relatively stable.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Provision for dry/abandoned wells increased due to Vijayapuram-1 (Andaman) write-off of INR 1,408 crores, alongside other minor field write-offs totaling INR 280 crores.
- NRL inventory gain for Q2 FY27 reported at $5.78 per barrel.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Full-year capex breakup: Survey INR 1,200 crores, exploration and development drilling INR 4,000 crores, capital equipment INR 4,500 crores, JVs and subsidiaries INR 300-350 crores.
- Existing 3 MMT NRL refinery turnaround planned for October 2027 (one-month shutdown); no shutdown required for existing 3 MMT refinery during commissioning of the new 6 MMT capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- NRL total debt drawdown stands at INR 20,500 crores against a tie-up of INR 28,000 crores.
- Dividend income for H1 totaled INR 894 crores (foreign subsidiary INR 466 crores, NRL INR 336 crores, IOCL INR 91 crores).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gas off-take constraints due to pipeline connectivity delays and downstream consumer shutdowns.
- Temporary operational impact from an installation tank farm (ITF) fire, mitigated via bypass systems.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities):** Asked about multi-year oil/gas production outlook and NRL GRM dip. Management stated oil production will be maintained at ~4 MMT, gas is constrained near-term, and NRL GRM fell due to lower international product FOB prices despite stable SAED.
2. **Vivekanand:** Asked about Numaligarh-Siliguri/PNCPL pipeline update and NRL debt/capex. Management stated PNCPL is 95% complete and targeting Q4 commissioning, debt drawdown is INR 20,500 crores, and FY27/FY28 capex is INR 7,500 crores and INR 6,000 crores respectively.
3. **Yogesh Patil:** Asked about crude discount to Brent and condensate share. Management explained pricing uses a 12-14 international crude basket across three fields, and condensate share is negligible (~0.002 MMT).
4. **Bineet Banka:** Asked about well write-off details and pre-NELP gas pricing. Management explained Vijayapuram-1 offshore well write-off was INR 1,408 crores due to initial mob/bundle services costs, and pre-NELP gas volume is ~0.5 MMSCMD enjoying ceiling-free pricing.
5. **Sabri Hazarika:** Asked about ITF fire impact. Management confirmed minor throughput impact handled via temporary bypass systems with no material long-term effect.

### One-line takeaway
Oil India delivered strong H1 FY27 financial and operational growth, backed by robust crude realizations, higher drilling intensity, and stellar performance at subsidiary NRL.
