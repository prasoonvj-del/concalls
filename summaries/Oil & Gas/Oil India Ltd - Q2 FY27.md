# Oil India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong crude production growth (+20% YoY in Q2) and highest-ever half-yearly EBITDA, PBT, and PAT.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained ~4 MMT crude production target for FY27 (currently at ~4.05 MMT run-rate). FY27 capex estimate updated to Rs 13,500–14,000 crore (vs Rs 8,600 crore initial budget in Q1).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains optimistic on crude output; gas off-take remains constrained by regional downstream customer shutdowns and pipeline evacuation bottlenecks.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Minor temporary production control due to a fire at the Internal Tank Farm (handled via bypass); new gas/oil discoveries in KG-DSF and Cambay Basin; high deepwater well write-off provisions (Rs 1,408 crore for Vijayapuram-1). Legal issues (GST on royalty, land tax) were not discussed this quarter.
- Last quarter's promises: Drilling momentum delivered with 42 wells drilled in H1 toward the 100-well annual target; PNCPL pipeline timeline pushed slightly from Dec 2026 to March 2027 alongside main refinery completion.

### Key numbers
- **Crude oil production:** 1.021 MMT in Q2 FY27 (+20% YoY); current daily production hovering at 11,000–11,200 MT (~4.05 MMT annual run-rate).
- **Natural gas production:** 0.786 BCM in Q2 FY27. Combined oil & gas production stood at 1.807 MMT for Q2 and 3.514 MMT for H1 FY27.
- **Price realizations:** Crude oil at $92.00/bbl in Q2 ($95.38/bbl in H1 vs $67.22/bbl YoY); natural gas at $7.19/MMBtu in Q2 (vs $6.75/MMBtu YoY).
- **Standalone H1 FY27 financial results:** Operating Revenue at Rs 16,029 crore; EBITDA at Rs 8,450 crore (highest ever half-yearly, margin 48.58% vs 34.32% YoY); PBT at Rs 6,609 crore; PAT at Rs 5,130 crore; EPS at Rs 31.54 per share.
- **Consolidated H1 FY27 financial results:** Operating Revenue at Rs 24,801 crore; PAT at Rs 6,547 crore.
- **NRL Q2 FY27 results:** Operating Revenue at Rs 7,991 crore (+24% YoY); Capacity utilization at 107.02%; Distillate yield at 87.01%; GRM at $20.71/bbl (H1 GRM at $28.24/bbl vs $7.73/bbl YoY) including $5.78/bbl inventory gain; EBITDA at Rs 1,108 crore; PAT at Rs 701 crore (H1 PAT at Rs 2,007 crore).
- **Dividend:** Board approved first interim dividend of Rs 7 per equity share.

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 registered highest-ever half-yearly EBITDA, PBT, and PAT.
- Drilled 42 wells in H1 FY27 (16 exploratory, 26 development).
- Declared a new gas discovery in KG-DSF and an oil discovery in the Cambay Basin; testing currently underway for Kerala-Konkan well.
- H1 dividend income received: Rs 894 crore (Rs 466 cr from foreign subsidiary, Rs 336 cr from NRL, Rs 91 cr from IOCL).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Crude production:** Management expects to maintain ~4 MMT in FY27 and scale to 4.2–4.3 MMT in subsequent years.
- **Gas production:** Expected to remain largely flat or slightly higher YoY for the next 18–20 months pending completion of evacuation pipeline infrastructure.
- **Drilling intensity:** Targeting ~100–110 wells per year as business-as-usual, utilizing 29 current active rigs plus 5–6 additional incoming rigs.
- **NRL expansion (NREP):** Mechanical completion targeted for March 2027; management expects capacity ramp-up to 50–60% by Q3 FY28 and 100% in FY29.
- **Pipelines:** 1,640 km PNCPL crude oil pipeline is 95% complete (1,600 km done) and expected to complete by March 2027; NRL-IGGL gas interconnect expected to begin gas flow by end-Jan 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Natural gas demand in the region remains constrained by downstream customer shutdowns (e.g., a local fertilizer plant shut down during the quarter).
- Company crude realizations are determined by a monthly average basket of 11–12 international crudes mapped across three field zones (Digboi, Moran, Naharkatiya).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone H1 EBITDA margin expanded by ~1,426 bps YoY to 48.58% on higher crude price realizations.
- NRL Q2 GRM moderated to $20.71/bbl (vs $35.95/bbl in Q1) as international product FOB prices dropped faster than crude prices while SAED impact remained flat.
- Provisions taken for unsuccessful/exploratory wells in H1 include Rs 1,408 crore for Vijayapuram-1 (Andaman), Rs 168 crore for Western satellite, Rs 65 crore for Central fields, Rs 44 crore for Chandmari, and Rs 20 crore for Ashoknagar JV.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Full-year FY27 capex targeted at Rs 13,500–14,000 crore (H1 actual spend: Rs 6,000 crore), including Rs 1,200 crore for surveys, Rs 4,000 crore for drilling, Rs 4,500 crore for capital equipment, and Rs 300–350 crore for subsidiaries/JVs.
- NRL capex target is Rs 7,500 crore for FY27 and ~Rs 6,000 crore for FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- NRL total debt drawdown stands at Rs 20,500 crore out of a total tied-up debt facility of Rs 28,000 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Downstream evacuation and off-take bottlenecks continuing to constrain gas production growth.
- High cost and write-off risks associated with deepwater offshore exploratory drilling campaigns.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities):** *Asked about multi-year oil/gas production trajectory.* Management stated oil run-rate of 4 MMT will be sustained and grown to 4.2–4.3 MMT; gas output will remain flat until pipeline connectivity is established in 18–20 months.
2. **Vivekanand:** *Asked for updates on PNCPL pipeline, NRL debt, and capex.* Management answered PNCPL is 95% complete (1,600 km done) with completion expected by March 2027; NRL debt drawdown is Rs 20,500 crore; NRL capex is Rs 7,500 crore in FY27 and ~Rs 6,000 crore in FY28.
3. **Yogesh Patil:** *Asked about crude price realization discount to Brent and write-off details.* Management explained crude pricing is calculated on an international benchmark basket of 11–12 crudes without arbitrary discounting; well write-off increase was driven by Rs 1,408 crore provision for Vijayapuram-1.
4. **Bineet Banka:** *Asked why NRL GRM fell QoQ and why gas realization exceeded $7/MMBtu ceiling.* Management explained product FOB prices fell sharper than crude while SAED stayed constant; higher gas realization was due to ~0.5 MMSCMD output from a pre-NELP block with no price ceiling.
5. **Sabri Hazarika (Emkay):** *Asked about IGGL-DNPL hookup and ITF fire impact.* Management confirmed physical interconnect finishes by end-Nov with gas flowing by Jan 2027; ITF fire impact was mitigated using bypass lines with minimal ongoing volume loss.

### One-line takeaway
Oil India delivered record-high H1 earnings driven by 20% YoY Q2 crude production growth, maintaining its 4 MMT target while progressing on its Numaligarh refinery expansion for March 2027 completion.
