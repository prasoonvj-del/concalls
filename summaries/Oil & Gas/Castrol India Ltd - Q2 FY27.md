# Castrol India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** 3Q & 9M FY 2026
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth offset by a sharp decline in profits due to raw material inflation and adverse currency movements.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (3Q):** Rs 1,735 crore, up 27% YoY, down 7% QoQ
- **Revenue (9M):** Rs 5,151 crore, up 20% YoY
- **EBITDA (3Q):** Rs 262 crore, down 19% YoY, down 47% QoQ
- **EBITDA Margin (3Q):** Approximately 15%
- **EBITDA (9M):** Rs 1,086 crore, up 11% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin (9M):** Approximately 21%
- **PAT (3Q):** Rs 176 crore, down 23% YoY, down 49% QoQ
- **PAT (9M):** Rs 766 crore, up 9% YoY
- **PBT (9M):** Rs 1,043 crore, up 10% YoY
- **Distribution Reach:** ~167,000 outlets (~46,000 in rural small-town India)
- **Auto Care Presence:** ~50,000 physical stores
- **Service Ecosystem:** ~870 Castrol Auto Service centers, 37,000 independent bike workshops, 16,000 multi-brand workshops, over 1,000 Rural Service Express locations
- **Verified Mechanics (Fast Scan platform):** 180,000 (24% higher YoY)

### Highlights
- Raw material bills increased by over 60% YoY in the quarter.
- Industrial business delivered double-digit volume growth; added close to 400 net new customers over 9M.
- Launched Magna CTX (circulating oils) and localized boron-free metalworking fluids (Hysol 35 XBB, Hysol 61 XBB).
- Won ET Brand Equity Award; Silvassa and Paharpur facilities won Gold at regional CQC chapters.
- Two latest thermal management products certified by NVIDIA for direct-to-chip data center cooling.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the operating environment to remain volatile and inflationary in the short to medium term.
- Volumes may remain soft in the short to medium term, though rural and industrial growth engines are expected to grow ahead of the market.
- Management does not provide short-term volume or quarterly margin guidance due to unprecedented raw material and FX volatility.
- Medium-term EBITDA margin frame target remains 21% to 24%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Market growth on a regular year in India is estimated at 3% to 4%. Castrol is tracking well ahead of market growth.
- Sequential business performance should not be read directly as Q2 is traditionally the strongest quarter due to seasonal agri demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- High double-digit commodity inflation and adverse currency movements impacted margins with a lag effect in 3Q.
- Margin per liter remains the true measure of profitability during extreme volatility; management stated that unit profitability/margin per liter is fully absorbed and steady to slightly higher.
- Levers for margin defense include diversified supplier network, localization, and product reformulation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Historical capex run-rate is Rs 100 crore to Rs 150 crores, spent on plant innovation, capacity, and in-market consumer/trade visibility.
- Plant utilization levels run at around 70% to 80%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sharp rise in raw material costs linked to crude oil (fluctuating from highs of $120 to lows of $80 and back to $114-$115).
- Adverse currency movements.
- Short-term softness in overall volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sucrit Patil (Fintrade) on volume guidance and pricing:** Management stated they do not give short-term volume guidance due to unprecedented raw material movements, but intend to grow volume at 1.5x to 2x market growth over the medium term.
2. **Deepak Malhotra (CapGrow Capital Advisors LLP) on Stonepeak's acquisition of BP's global Castrol business:** Management stated that obtaining global operational and legal licenses across over 50 countries is progressing well; SEBI filings will follow once completion is achieved.
3. **Nitin Tiwari (Phillip Capital) on EBITDA per liter vs. percentage margin:** Management clarified that percentage margin guidance remains 21% to 24% for the medium term, though short-term volatility may push margins out of this band.
4. **Kirtan Mehta (Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund) on data center cooling pilots:** Management confirmed ongoing pilots and noted that two formulations recently received NVIDIA certification for direct-to-chip cooling, though revenue contribution is currently small.
5. **Dhaval Popat (Choice International) on base oil trends and plant utilization:** Management reiterated that plant utilization operates in a 70% to 80% range, and formulations rely heavily on Group 2, Group 3, and PAO base oils.

### One-line takeaway
Castrol India delivered resilient 9-month revenue growth of 20%, but 3Q profitability was severely squeezed by a 60%+ rise in raw material costs and adverse currency impacts, prompting management to prioritize margin-per-liter defense over short-term percentage margins.
