# Oil & Gas - Upstream oil & gas: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the upstream oil and gas sector reported a robust Q2 FY27, characterized by strong production growth, surging profitability, and the active revival of domestic capital expenditure cycles led by ONGC. Companies are aggressively expanding their asset bases, executing workover and drilling campaigns, and anticipating major volume inflection points tied to upcoming pipeline infrastructure. However, operational hurdles—such as regional gas evacuation bottlenecks in Assam, regional pipeline delays, and occasional offshore logistics snags—remained key talking points.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Upstream players in India explore, develop, and produce hydrocarbons under government-awarded blocks and revenue-sharing contracts, bearing heavy upfront capital expenditure with monetization tied to domestic pricing formulas and state-controlled offtake [Background].

**Shifts underway** - 
* **Aggressive asset scaling and production ramp-up:** Upstream operators and service providers are actively deploying capital for drilling, fracking, and rig refurbishments to scale output. Stage: Scaling. Companies: Antelopus Selan completed a 10-well fracking campaign in Bakrol targeting a 2,500 BOEPD exit rate; Deep Industries is deploying ~Rs 150 crore for production enhancement contracts; HOEC is executing a $60 million B-80 workover to hit 10,000 BOE by June 2027; Jindal Drilling is investing ~INR 600 crores in rig refurbishments; Oil India drilled 42 wells in H1 toward its 100+ annual target; Prabha Energy is scaling CBM commercial production toward 1.5 MMSCMD. Importance: Unlocks multi-year revenue and cash flow growth [Antelopus Selan concall Q2 FY27; Deep Industries concall Q2 FY27; HOEC concall Q2 FY27; Jindal Drilling concall Q2 FY27; Oil India concall Q2 FY27; Prabha Energy concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push** - 
* Revised policy for extending pre-NELP oil and gas contracts and allowing longer production sharing contract (PSC) extensions rewards upstream operators seeking long-term operational visibility, directly benefiting HOEC with its imminent Palej PSC extension [Govt: Allow longer production sharing contract (PSC) extensions, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that India's macro fundamentals—highlighted by crude import dependence remaining stubbornly above 88% and rising domestic gas demand—provide a strong structural tailwind. Upstream service providers like Jindal Drilling and Deep Industries noted that ONGC's domestic capex cycle has definitively started, driving higher tender activity and contract awards.
* **Divergence:** While companies like Oil India and Antelopus Selan face localized gas off-take and evacuation bottlenecks due to regional grid delays (e.g., in Assam), others like Prabha Energy are eagerly awaiting upcoming pipeline links (Urja Ganga and IOCL networks) to transition from test cascades to full-scale commercial sales. Furthermore, Antelopus Selan noted that private buyers' ability to absorb crude near $100/bbl is limited, forcing a heavier concentration of sales toward public sector buyers.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Operating leverage & fixed costs:** Antelopus Selan highlighted that fixed costs constitute 70% to 75% of its cost base, driving strong operating leverage as production volumes scale.
* **Statutory levies:** Antelopus Selan pointed out that Karjisan enjoys concessional statutory levies of <5%, compared to ~30% in Bakrol and Cambay, providing a structural lift to blended margins.
* **Operating cost per BOE:** HOEC noted that company-wide operating costs range between $20 and $30 per BOE, with B-80 carrying higher offshore support costs.
* **Refining margins:** Oil India noted that Numaligarh Refinery's (NRL) gross refining margin (GRM) moderated to $20.71/bbl in Q2 (down from $35.95/bbl in Q1) as international product FOB prices dropped faster than crude prices.

## Capex & capacity
* **Antelopus Selan:** Set an FY27 capex guidance of Rs 250 crore, focusing on on-shore drilling and workovers.
* **Deep Industries:** Planned capex of about Rs 150 crore for production enhancement contracts over the next 12 months, plus Rs 100 crore to Rs 200 crore for Dolphin Offshore.
* **HOEC:** Budgeting a $60 million program ($45 million wells, $15 million workover) for B-80, alongside upcoming drilling in Kharsang.
* **Jindal Drilling:** Total fund requirement of approximately INR 600 crores over the next 15–18 months for rig refurbishments (INR 70–90 crores per rig) and potential JV rig acquisitions.
* **Oil India:** Revised full-year FY27 standalone capex estimate to Rs 13,500–14,000 crore, alongside NRL's Rs 7,500 crore capex target.
* **Prabha Energy:** Planning ~Rs 500 crore in capex for Jharia's upcoming 47 wells starting next fiscal year.

## What changed since last quarter
* **HOEC:** Experienced a minor 15-day delay for the B-80 workover rig arrival due to ONGC rig release logistics (now late November/early December); sentiment turned more optimistic on gas evacuation via an NRL bypass line rather than hot-tapping.
* **Oil India:** Updated its FY27 capex estimate upward to Rs 13,500–14,000 crore (compared to the initial Rs 8,600 crore budget in Q1).
* **Jindal Drilling:** Shifted tone from cautious to positive as ONGC tender issuance materially improved and rehiring contracts materialized.
* **Deep Industries:** Encountered a 5–6 month delay in the production enhancement incremental program due to the Mori 5 incident, which has now been resolved with rig deployment resuming in Q3.

## What to watch next quarter
* **B-80 Workover & Production:** Execution and timeline of the B-80 rig arrival and subsequent production resumption (HOEC, Antelopus Selan).
* **Assam Pipeline Connectivity:** Progress on the DNPL bypass and NRL gas interconnect completion expected around December 2026 / January 2027 (HOEC, Oil India, Antelopus Selan).
* **Rig Logistics:** Reopening of the Strait of Hormuz to facilitate the return and deployment of *Jindal Pioneer* (Jindal Drilling).
* **CBM Commercialization:** Timeline for pipeline hookups to the Urja Ganga and IOCL networks to unlock bulk revenue (Prabha Energy).
