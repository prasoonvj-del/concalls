# Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong profit growth and debt reduction achieved despite geopolitical headwinds and a planned shutdown.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Net Profit:** Rs 1,881 crore (vs Rs 639 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 3,210 crore.
*   **H1 Net Profit:** Rs 2,796 crore (vs Rs 2,367 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Throughput:** 4.18 MMT in Q2.
*   **Fuel & Loss:** 9.86% for H1 (vs ~11% previously).
*   **Borrowings:** Rs 9,245 crore (reduced by over Rs 5,000 crore in six months).
*   **Debt-Equity Ratio:** 0.55 (improved from 1.01 at end of March 2026).
*   **Interim Dividend:** Rs 3 per share (total payout Rs 526 crore).
*   **Exceptional Item:** Provisional loss of Rs 118.76 crore booked for fire incident (estimated replacement value ~Rs 225 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Resilience:** Achieved strong performance despite a planned shutdown in August and a fire incident at the Coker Hydrotreater Unit (CHTU) on September 30, 2026.
*   **Crude Sourcing:** Diversified basket; Russian barrels accounted for 32% of Q2 intake (vs 28% in Q1). Domestic crude was ~25%, term contracts ~20%, and spot purchases the remainder.
*   **Retail Expansion:** Network stands at 257 outlets; targeting 500 outlets by end of FY28.
*   **Strategic Initiatives:** Formed a JV (OPML) with ONGC and OPaL for petrochemical marketing.
*   **Regulatory/Legal:** Received authorization for a petroleum/ATF pipeline from Devanagonthi to Bengaluru airport (completion by 2029-30; cost Rs 200 crore+). Won a tax dispute case, receiving a Rs 212 crore refund.
*   **Credit Rating:** Assigned 'BBB- (Stable)' by Fitch International.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Throughput:** Management expects Q3 throughput to be close to Q2 levels despite the CHTU fire incident.
*   **Restoration:** CHTU restoration targeted by mid-December 2026.
*   **Balance Sheet:** Management expects to maintain a strong balance sheet for the current year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Dynamics:** Refining industry facing challenges from West Asian conflict, leading to supply constraints, longer voyage times, and higher freight costs.
*   **Freight Costs:** Freight rates have risen to $7–$9 per barrel.
*   **Marketing:** Retail and direct marketing volumes saw significant growth (50% YoY in retail; 30-40% in direct marketing) as private OMCs curtailed operations.
*   **Exports:** Exports are expected to remain low in the near term due to strong domestic demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Freight:** Management is mitigating high freight costs by increasing Delivered at Place (DAP) cargo purchases to reduce uncertainty.
*   **Refinery Margins:** Management decided not to publish quarterly Gross Refining Margin (GRM) numbers due to methodology differences across the industry.
*   **Intermediate Feedstock:** Currently not processing VGO or high-sulphur fuel oil as intermediates; evaluating market offers continuously.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex Guidance:** Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,500 crore, focused on refinery maintenance, revamping, and retail expansion.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** Targeting specific energy consumption of 9.5; expects improvement once grid power project is fully online by financial year-end.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Reduction:** Borrowings fell by over Rs 5,000 crore in six months.
*   **Working Capital:** Debt levels remain variable based on working capital requirements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** West Asian conflict continues to impact supply chains and freight costs.
*   **Operational:** Fire incident at CHTU on September 30, 2026; unit is under investigation and reconstruction.
*   **Product Mix:** CHTU fire causes a marginal impact on distillate yield as the unit was designed for heavier crudes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Impact of fire on distillate yield?** Management stated the impact is marginal, estimated at less than a couple of percentage points of distillates.
*   **Q: Is there a government cap on GRM at $15/barrel?** Management clarified there is no such cap; margins are determined by landed price and cracks, which vary by refiner.
*   **Q: Can MRPL switch between ATF and diesel production?** Management confirmed swing capability exists and can be executed quickly (monthly basis) depending on crude availability.
*   **Q: Are Middle Eastern suppliers offering DAP terms permanently?** Management noted this is currently reactionary but expects some variation of these terms to persist in future systems.
*   **Q: Why stop publishing GRM?** Management cited lack of standardized computation methodology across the industry as the reason for discontinuing quarterly disclosure.

### One-line takeaway
MRPL delivered a robust financial performance with significant debt reduction and strong domestic market share gains, successfully navigating geopolitical supply chain disruptions and a localized fire incident.
