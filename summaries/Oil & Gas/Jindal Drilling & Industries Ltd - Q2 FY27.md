# Jindal Drilling & Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a significantly good quarter with improved EBITDA, increased earnings and EPS, and a stronger net cash position.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Guidance was not explicitly stated as raised or cut, but management expressed increased confidence as ONGC's capex cycle has started and dehirings are being successfully redeployed.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to positive as ONGC tender issuance improved and new contracts/rehiring materialized.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: The Strait of Horumz closure delaying the movement of *Jindal Pioneer* back to India was introduced as a new operational bottleneck.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: *Jindal Pioneer* refurbishment was completed in UAE, but deployment is awaiting the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz.

### Key numbers
- Order book: INR1,652 crores (as of 30th September 2026); expected to reach INR2,600 crores to INR2,700 crores once two new contracts are received.
- EBITDA: Increased QoQ from INR104 crores to INR131 crores.
- Net cash position: Improved from INR128 crores as on 31st March 2026 to INR295 crores as on 30th September 2026.
- Day rate for rehired rig: Approximately $55,700 per day.
- Refurbishment cost: INR70 crores to INR90 crores per rig.
- Total fund requirement (refurbishment and potential JV rig acquisition over next 15-18 months): Approximately INR600 crores.

### Highlights
- *Jindal Pioneer* completed refurbishment in the UAE, awaiting the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz to return to India and commence a 3-year contract with ONGC.
- Received a contract for one rig at approximately $55,700 per day, with two more rig rehirings expected from ONGC in the current quarter.
- Net cash position improved significantly to INR295 crores, driven by strong cash flows and order book.
- Exploring the acquisition of a rig owned by a joint venture entity to consolidate value creation within Jindal Drilling, subject to Board and shareholder approval.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects blended EBITDA margins to operate in the historical 35% to 40% range, with potential for slight improvement in upcoming quarters as *Jindal Pioneer* commences operations.
- Management expects the net cash position to improve further due to a strong order book and robust cash flows.
- ONGC capex cycle has started, reflected in an increased number of issued tenders.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ONGC domestic capex cycle has actively started, resolving previous concerns regarding redeploying dehired rigs.
- Strong domestic demand indicated by ongoing and upcoming contract awards from ONGC.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins historically operate in the 35% to 40% range due to a blend of owned and rented rigs; owned rigs yield higher margins beyond certain rates.
- Refurbishment exercise costs INR70 crores to INR90 crores per rig (required for *Discovery I*, *Virtue I*, and *Jindal Star*).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total fund requirement of approximately INR600 crores over the next 15 to 18 months for rig refurbishments (INR70 crores to INR90 crores per rig for 3 rigs) and potential JV rig acquisition.
- Not looking to buy external rigs outside the Jindal Group; focused on consolidating Singapore-entity owned rigs and potential JV rig acquisition using internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position strengthened to INR295 crores as of 30th September 2026 (up from INR128 crores as of 31st March 2026).
- Cash flows and internal accruals are expected to comfortably fund upcoming capex and acquisitions without heavy borrowing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Awaiting the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz to transport *Jindal Pioneer* from the Middle East to India.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vikram Kotak (Ace Lansdowne) on EBITDA margins:** Asked about margin sustainability and guidance following fluctuations. Management stated historical range is 35%-40% and expects slight improvements as *Jindal Pioneer* starts operations.
2. **Vikram Kotak (Ace Lansdowne) on Capex and Acquisitions:** Inquired about maintenance capex and new rig acquisitions. Management clarified they are not buying external rigs, but are pursuing the buyout of Singapore-entity owned rigs and a JV rig, totaling ~INR600 crores over 15-18 months.
3. **Vikram Kotak (Ace Lansdowne) on Business Outlook:** Asked if ONGC/Oil India capex is executing. Management confirmed the ONGC capex cycle has started, evidenced by rising tender issuances and recent contract wins.
4. **Vikram Kotak (Ace Lansdowne) on Cash Deployment:** Asked about dividend hikes or buybacks given the net cash position. Management stated cash will be prioritized for core business needs: rig refurbishments and the JV rig acquisition.
5. **Arya Patel (Emkay Global) on Order Book:** Asked about order book progression with new contracts. Management stated the INR1,652 crore order book will expand to INR2,600-INR2,700 crores upon receiving contracts for *Virtue-1* and *Jindal Star*.

### One-line takeaway
Jindal Drilling posted a strong Q2 FY27 with surging EBITDA, a robust net cash position of INR295 crores, and positive momentum from the revival of ONGC's domestic capex cycle.
