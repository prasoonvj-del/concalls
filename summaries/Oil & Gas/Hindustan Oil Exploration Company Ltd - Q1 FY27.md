# Hindustan Oil Exploration Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong realization growth and Kharsang volume ramp-up were offset by ongoing operational delays at B-80 and restricted gas evacuation at Dirok.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone revenue from operations:** INR 117.5 crore (vs negative INR 194 crore in previous quarter).
- **Consolidated revenue from operations:** INR 124 crore (net consolidated revenue of INR 114.17 crore after profit petroleum and revenue sharing).
- **Standalone PBT before exceptional items:** INR 12.54 crore (vs INR 30.4 crore in previous quarter).
- **Consolidated PBT before exceptional items:** INR 6.5 crore (vs INR 9.01 crore in previous quarter).
- **Standalone other income:** INR 19.37 crore (includes INR 8 crore escrow release, INR 2 crore insurance claim, and INR 2.3 crore Adbhoot acquisition topping up).
- **Consolidated other income:** INR 20 crore.
- **Standalone total expenses:** INR 114 crore (includes depletion of INR 51 crore, royalty/statutory dues of INR 13 crore, and stock adjustments of INR 41 crore).
- **Consolidated total expenses:** INR 128 crore (includes depletion of INR 58 crore, royalty/cess/statutory dues of INR 15 crore, and stock adjustments of INR 41 crore).
- **Average crude/condensate realization (standalone):** $95.5/barrel (vs $70.8/barrel in previous quarter).
- **Average gas realization (blended):** $12/MMBTU (vs $9.8/MMBTU in previous quarter); B-80 gas realization at $16.5/MMBTU and Dirok at $12.5/MMBTU.
- **Kharsang production increase:** From ~12,300 barrels of oil to 17,400 BOE.
- **Gearing / Net Debt:** Gearing at 0.04; bank loan stands at INR 20 crore.

### Highlights
- B-80 offshore production impacted by high water cut in one well; compressor configuration changed to lower suction pressure to sustain flow.
- B-80 two-well workover (D1 and D2) on track; rig contract discussions ongoing for award this month, with mobilization by October to shut off water zones and open new zones.
- B-15 block field development plan (FDP) under preparation, evaluating multiple concepts including tying into ONGC's platform; drilling planned for FY28 with 16 MMBOE reserves.
- PY-1 awarded contract for rig-less intervention to boost short-term production; new well drilling contingent on firm take-or-pay gas sales agreements with IOCL or GAIL.
- Dirok field production constrained to 50%-70% due to pipeline evacuation limits; PNGRB designated DNPL line as a common carrier.
- Kharsang completed 5-6 workovers on 23 shut wells; preparing for the second phase of 9-well drilling and initiated a pipeline route survey to monetize stranded gas.
- Gujarat Cambay assets (Asjol, Balol, Palej) saw 5%-6% production increase last quarter, targeting 20%-30% growth via new belt technology and debottlenecking.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects B-80 crude inventory stored at HPCL Chembur to be fully cleared by end-October or early November.
- Management targets December for completing DNPL pipeline capacity restoration via hot tapping.
- Management plans to bring two workover wells on production at B-80 by November/December 2026, and three new wells by June 2027.
- Management expects Dirok cash flows to fully support the balance sheet once the field is completely on stream.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic hydrocarbon production and infrastructure development remain a heavy lift amid continued focus on energy security to propel India's 7%-8% growth.
- Oil and gas sector globally sees rigs in very short supply as rising oil prices drive drilling demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inventory mark-to-market losses on B-80 crude sales estimated by management at 7% to 10% (realized loss of INR 4-6 crore on initial 15% sold).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- B-80 campaign requires debt funding; management is in talks with investors and banks to raise a funding pool for workovers and three new wells.
- DNPL pipeline capacity expected to return to 2.5 MMSCMD (from degraded 1.1-1.5 MMSCMD) once hot taps and asset integrity work are finished.
- Kharsang 24 km pipeline to connect to Oil India's grid is projected to take 14 to 18 months to lay once the right-of-way is secured.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone debt stands at INR 20 crore with a low gearing of 0.04.
- Around INR 260+ crore (approx. INR 300 crore with interest) in invoices remained stuck due to the HPCL dispute, delaying prior capex plans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High water cut in B-80 wells and subsea intervention complexities.
- Evacuation bottlenecks and degraded sections in Assam's DNPL pipeline restricting Dirok offtake.
- Slow third-party lifting of B-80 crude from HPCL tanks.
- Local protests in Tamil Nadu regarding exploratory drilling approvals.
- Regulatory and forest clearance risks for laying the Kharsang gas pipeline.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhruv Sitlani (Leo Capital) asked about the timeline for Northeast grid connectivity (Dirok).**
   - *Management replied:* December is the target for completion via hot tapping by Assam Gas Company Limited without requiring an NRL shutdown.
2. **Shubham Jain asked about the P&L impact of MTM losses on B-80 inventory and B-80 rig alignment status.**
   - *Management replied:* Inventory losses are estimated at 7%-10% depending on Brent prices (INR 4-6 crore incurred on 15% sold); B-80 workovers are planned for late 2026 and three new wells by June 2027 using an upcoming rig campaign.
3. **Riddhesh Gandhi asked why the company is absorbing B-80 crude resale losses instead of passing the liability to HPCL.**
   - *Management replied:* Management chose an amicable path by signing an agreement to reverse the sale and use third-party tankers to preserve long-term partner relations, while conciliation proceedings remain ongoing.
4. **Manpreet Arora requested asset-wise gas and oil realizations in future presentations.**
   - *Management replied:* Acknowledged the feedback and confirmed asset-wise disclosures will be resumed, noting current B-80 gas realization at $16.5/MMBTU and Dirok at $12.5/MMBTU.
5. **Nishant Maheshwari (Individual Investor) questioned management credibility regarding production guidance given historical delays.**
   - *Management replied:* Acknowledged delays caused in part by the "Black Swan" event of the stuck HPCL invoice, but maintained that technical reservoir models support the projected output ranges.
6. **Anubhav Goel asked about prioritising PY-1 vs B-80 drilling given funding constraints.**
   - *Management replied:* B-80 relies on raised debt and stepwise well drilling; PY-1 drilling is strictly contingent on signing a firm gas sales agreement (GSA), though near-term rig-less interventions will be internally funded.

### One-line takeaway
HOEC's Q1 FY27 performance benefited from higher crude realizations and Kharsang volume growth, but management remains heavily dependent on upcoming debt-funded B-80 workovers and the resolution of Assam pipeline bottlenecks to scale production.
