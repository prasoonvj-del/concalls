# Gandhar Oil Refinery (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly profitability and revenue growth, driven by agile sourcing and favorable market conditions despite geopolitical challenges.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,731.9 crore (up 92% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 281 crore (up 512% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 16.20% (vs 5.1% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 206 crore (up 688% YoY).
*   **Sales Volume:** ~131,000 kilolitres (up 8% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin Spread:** Rs 28,145 per kilolitre (vs Rs 8,274 per kilolitre in Q1 FY26).
*   **Export Contribution:** 51% of consolidated revenue (vs 37% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Interim Dividend:** 100% of face value declared.

### Highlights
*   **Record Performance:** Management stated this was the highest quarterly profit in the company's history, exceeding the total profit generated in the entire FY26.
*   **Segmental Growth:** PHPO (Personal Care, Healthcare, Performance Oil) grew 18% YoY; PIO (Process and Insulating Oil) grew 28% YoY.
*   **Operational Resilience:** Despite geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and Strait of Hormuz disruptions, the company maintained supply continuity through agile sourcing (shifting to South Korea and domestic producers) and prudent inventory management.
*   **Subsidiary Performance:** Texol (Sharjah) operations were temporarily impacted by logistics/vessel disruptions but are normalizing.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management is hopeful of maintaining margins at current levels for the next 1-2 quarters.
*   **Volume Growth:** Management targets 8% to 11% volume growth for the full year.
*   **Exports:** Anticipates export revenue and sales to remain at current levels in coming quarters.
*   **Capex:** Plans for future capacity expansion will be announced in the next quarter or so.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Growth:** Management cited a CRISIL report estimating global industry growth at >5% CAGR.
*   **Demand Drivers:** Sustained demand from personal care, healthcare, pharmaceutical, transformer, power, and rubber sectors.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Management noted the industry has few large players in this region, supporting their growth outlook.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Expansion was attributed to favorable market conditions, disciplined sourcing, and effective inventory management rather than inventory gains (inventory holding is typically 30-40 days).
*   **Pricing:** Price increases were implemented across segments (except automotive lubricants, where it was delayed) to pass on costs and justify higher spreads due to supply chain constraints.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Currently at ~97% on a 2-shift basis; management noted they can move to a 3rd shift if required.
*   **Fungibility:** Production capacity is fungible across products, allowing for flexible product mix adjustments.
*   **New Geographies:** Strategy for South Africa is being worked out; clarity expected in 1-2 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Status:** Standalone entity is effectively debt-free; consolidated borrowings relate to Texol (working capital and term loans for plant setup).
*   **Working Capital:** Trade payables increased, but management stated 90% of payables are within 90 days and the number of days has not increased.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical Risks:** Ongoing Middle East tensions and Strait of Hormuz closure remain a concern for raw material availability and freight/insurance costs.
*   **Exceptional Conditions:** Management cautioned that the current high spreads should be viewed in the context of "exceptional market conditions" witnessed during the quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Sustainability of Margins:** Management admitted this was a "historic" quarter due to exceptional market conditions but expects margins to remain at "stellar levels" for at least 1-2 quarters.
*   **On Volume vs. Price Growth:** Management clarified that the 9% annual growth target refers to volume, while value growth is expected to be higher.
*   **On Customer Concentration:** Management stated the top 5 customers are not significant given a base of 4,000+ customers.
*   **On Capex Funding:** Management stated they have sufficient internal accruals to fund capex without relying solely on term loans.
*   **On Inventory Gains:** Management clarified that they do not carry enough inventory to justify significant inventory gains; the performance was driven by selling at higher prices.

### One-line takeaway
Gandhar Oil delivered a record-breaking quarter driven by agile sourcing and favorable market spreads, with management cautiously optimistic about sustaining these elevated margins for the next 1-2 quarters.
