# Oil & Gas - City gas distribution: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the city gas distribution (CGD) sector report resilient volume growth—led by strong demand for CNG—despite significant headwinds from volatile international gas prices, West Asian geopolitical tensions, and shifting domestic gas allocations (such as HPHT and new well gas fluctuations). While companies like Adani Total Gas (ATGL), Gujarat Energy Ltd (GEL), and IRM Energy posted steady financial numbers, others like Mahanagar Gas (MGL) faced margin squeezes due to restricted industrial RLNG supplies. Companies are actively diversifying their sourcing mixes, locking in long-term Henry Hub-linked contracts, and pivoting towards green energy alternatives like compressed biogas (CBG) and B2B EV charging to protect margins.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Historically, Indian CGD players made money by securing exclusive geographical area (GA) licenses from PNGRB, building out physical steel and MDPE pipeline networks, and relying heavily on subsidized domestic APM gas allocations to supply high-margin CNG and domestic PNG. Over the past ~5 years, the model has shifted from pure geographic monopolies toward aggressive retail footprint expansion (CNG stations), digital customer acquisitions, and navigating structural volatility in international RLNG and spot markets by diversifying into B2B transport, LNG for heavy vehicles, and green energy adjacencies [Background].

**Shifts underway** - 
* **Diversification into Compressed Biogas (CBG) and LNG for Heavy Transport:** Scaling up; GEL issued EOIs for CBG partners and is developing 6 LNG stations for long-haul transport [Gujarat Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall]; ATGL is operating its Barsana CBG plant and planning capacity expansion [Adani Total Gas Q2 FY27 concall]. This diversifies revenue away from pure gas trading and hedges against domestic gas allocation cuts.
* **Pivoting EV Charging to B2B Captive Fleets:** Scaling up; ATGL is shifting its EV strategy from retail chargers toward B2B charging infrastructure targeting Adani Cement, Ports, and Logistics to drive up dismal asset utilization rates [Adani Total Gas Q2 FY27 concall].
* **Securing Long-Term International Gas Contracts:** Mainstream; GEL signed 10-year Henry Hub-linked LNG contracts for 0.8 MTPA to build a total long-term book of 4 MTPA, mitigating spot market volatility [Gujarat Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].

**Government push** - 
* New piped-gas incentive for consumers announced by the Ministry of Power, which rewards household adoption of piped natural gas infrastructure and benefits select CGD stocks [Govt: Ministry of Power, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** CNG demand remains robust across the board, driven by strong vehicle conversions and fleet adoption (e.g., ATGL CNG volume +17% YoY, IRM Energy CNG volume +23% in H1, MGL CNG volume +9.14% YoY). 
* **Divergence (Industrial/Commercial):** While ATGL and IRM Energy experienced solid commercial/industrial customer additions, GEL suffered a massive normalization in Morbi industrial volumes (dropping from ~8 MMSCMD in Q1 to 3.8 MMSCMD in Q2 due to alternate fuel competition from propane) [Gujarat Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall]. Similarly, MGL reported restricted RLNG supplies impacting its industrial segment, with volumes down 7.48% YoY [Mahanagar Gas Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreed Views:** Managements universally cited headwinds from rising natural gas costs, West Asian geopolitical disruptions, and currency depreciation. 
* **Divergence:** 
  * **ATGL** absorbed a 43% YoY surge in gas costs by adopting a calibrated retail pricing approach and reducing spot reliance to 7-8% [Adani Total Gas Q2 FY27 concall].
  * **GEL** cut its CGD EBITDA margin guidance to Rs 5-6/SCM (from Rs 5.5-6.5/SCM) following Morbi volume moderation and force majeure issues on long-term Qatar contracts [Gujarat Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
  * **IRM Energy** reported an increase in Q2 raw material costs by ~Rs 1.2 per SCM due to a 20-day transition window under MoPNG's HPHT allocation circular, guiding for a stable 15% EBITDA margin in H2 [IRM Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
  * **MGL** reported a lower unit EBITDA margin of ~Rs 6.77/SCM and noted that a Rs 1–1.5/SCM price hike is required to reach a Rs 7 margin [Mahanagar Gas Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
  * **Confidence Petroleum** faced temporary working capital strains and rising short-term debt as it shifted LPG imports from the Middle East to the USA, resulting in a Rs 298 crore jump in supplier advances [Confidence Petroleum India Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].

## Capex & capacity
* **ATGL:** Maintained aggressive expansion targets, adding 16 CNG stations (total 723) and ~40k households in Q2; guiding for 80-90 new CNG stations in FY27 [Adani Total Gas Q2 FY27 concall].
* **Confidence Petroleum:** Targeting the rollout of 100 CNG stations in Bengaluru within the next 3 to 4 quarters (by next Diwali) [Confidence Petroleum India Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
* **GEL:** Reaffirmed FY27 capex guidance of ~Rs 1,000 crore, adding 18 new CNG stations in H1 (total 857) [Gujarat Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
* **IRM Energy:** Incurred Rs 111 crore in H1 FY27 out of a total target of Rs 250–260 crore; station count reached 156 [IRM Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
* **MGL:** Maintained annual capex guidance of INR 1,500 crore to INR 1,800 crore despite seasonal monsoon slowdowns, expecting negative free cash flow of INR 200-300 crore funded entirely via internal cash surpluses [Mahanagar Gas Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].

## What changed since last quarter
* **ATGL:** Shifted tone from "waiting for pooled gas reinstatement" in Q1 to "navigating pressures through portfolio diversification and agile sourcing" in Q2 [Adani Total Gas Q2 FY27 concall].
* **Confidence Petroleum:** Accelerated its Bengaluru retail rollout timeline following completed infrastructure capex [Confidence Petroleum India Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
* **GEL:** Formally cut its CGD EBITDA margin guidance to Rs 5-6/SCM and confirmed the anticipated Morbi volume drop from Q1 [Gujarat Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].
* **IRM Energy:** Highlighted regulatory shifts from MoPNG's September HPHT allocation circular, anticipating a temporary Q3 margin moderation before recovery in Q4 [IRM Energy Ltd Q2 FY27 concall].

## What to watch next quarter
* Implementation timeline and volume impact of MoPNG's phased HPHT gas allocation formula changes (notably for MGL and IRM Energy).
* Sourcing stability and the actual impact of US LPG procurement logistics on Confidence Petroleum’s working capital and short-term debt.
* Recovery of industrial volumes in Morbi for GEL and the execution of its long-term LNG contracts.
* Progress on B2B EV charging monetization for ATGL and retail station rollouts in Bengaluru for Confidence Petroleum.

## Company one-liners
* **Adani Total Gas Ltd:** ATGL is successfully navigating a high-cost environment through diversified gas sourcing and volume growth, while shifting its EV strategy toward B2B to improve utilization.
* **Confidence Petroleum India Ltd:** Confidence Petroleum delivered stellar Q2 FY27 growth with revenue up 127% YoY, leveraging its integrated infrastructure and new customer acquisitions to neutralize Middle Eastern supply disruptions.
* **Gujarat Energy Ltd:** Gujarat Energy Limited delivered strong H1 growth and secured long-term LNG supply to mitigate future volatility, though it lowered its CGD margin guidance amid normalization of Morbi industrial volumes.
* **IRM Energy Ltd:** IRM Energy generated strong Q2 FY27 earnings with a net-cash balance sheet, while proactively managing temporary regulatory changes in domestic HPHT gas allocation.
* **Mahanagar Gas Ltd:** Mahanagar Gas reported lower Q2 EBITDA and PAT due to industrial gas supply curtailments and high spot LNG prices, but remains optimistic on achieving 8-9% volume growth.
