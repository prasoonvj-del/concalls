# Gulf Oil Lubricants India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth (16% YoY) and consistent execution despite inflationary pressures and supply chain challenges.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue Growth:** 44% YoY.
*   **Volume Growth:** 16% YoY (Core lubricants volume: 47,000 KL vs 40,500 KL in Q2 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Growth:** 37% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 11.8%.
*   **Tirex Revenue:** Rs 33 crore (for Q2).
*   **Dividend Payout:** 65%–70% (historical trend).
*   **Capex:** Revised to Rs 60–62 crore (from Rs 55 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Volume Performance:** Achieved 16% volume growth in Q2, with H1 volume growth at 17% YoY.
*   **Segmental Growth:** High double-digit growth in OEM franchisee workshops and B2B industrial segments.
*   **Market Share:** Management claims to be growing 2x–3x the industry growth rate.
*   **Brand/Marketing:** Launched a major advertising campaign for the 'Pride' product and repositioned the brand with the 'Born to be Behad' (Performance Beyond Limits) theme.
*   **Sustainability:** Chennai plant received IGBC Platinum certification.
*   **Tirex/EV:** Tirex revenue reached Rs 33 crore in Q2; capacity expansion to 3,000 chargers expected by end of this year.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume:** Expects H2 volume growth to be 2x–3x the industry average (industry growth estimated at 3%–4%), targeting double-digit volume growth in lubricants for H2.
*   **Margins:** Medium-term target remains 14%–16% EBITDA margin; currently focused on protecting per-litre margins in an inflationary environment.
*   **AdBlue:** Targeting 10%–15% volume growth going forward.
*   **Capex:** Chennai expansion on track for completion this quarter; Silvassa expansion may see a 1–2 month spillover into Q1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Seasonality:** Q2 was a monsoon quarter, which typically sees lower vehicle movement and AdBlue consumption.
*   **Demand:** Softness noted in the agri-season segment for the current month.
*   **EV Adoption:** EV car sales in H1 FY27 are nearly equal to the total sales for the full year FY26.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Implemented calibrated price increases to pass on cost inflation.
*   **Raw Materials:** Base oil prices have a time lag (45–60 days) relative to crude oil movements. Management is monitoring crude levels ($100–$115 range) closely and will take further price hikes if necessary.
*   **Freight:** Sea freight costs have increased due to supply chain recalibrations (e.g., Strait of Hormuz issues), but these are built into CIF-based procurement.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Chennai Plant:** Expansion on track for completion by end of Q3 FY27.
*   **Silvassa Plant:** Expansion progress is good, but completion likely delayed to Q1 FY28.
*   **Tirex:** Capacity expansion to 3,000 chargers expected by end of this year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Inventory and receivable values increased due to higher product pricing and tactical buying to secure supplies. Management expects moderation if prices stabilize.
*   **Cash Position:** Holds approximately Rs 1,200 crore in cash; prioritizing M&A opportunities in the lubricant space and EV value chain.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Inflation:** Continued volatility in crude oil and base oil prices remains a key monitorable.
*   **Supply Chain:** Geopolitical tensions (e.g., West Asia) impacting freight and logistics.
*   **Working Capital:** Elevated working capital requirements due to high input costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the increase in working capital?** Management clarified it is driven by higher absolute pricing of raw materials and finished goods, not an increase in the number of days (which remains in the 95–105 day band).
*   **Q: Can you maintain margins if crude rises further?** Management stated they prioritize protecting per-litre margins and will use calibrated price hikes if crude sustains at elevated levels.
*   **Q: What is the status of the fire incident at Valsad?** Management confirmed stocks were insured, production loss was minimal (4–5 days), and no disruption to customer supply is expected.
*   **Q: Why is the EBITDA margin lower than the 14%–16% target?** Management explained that in high-inflation environments, the percentage margin optically looks lower, but per-litre margins are stable or growing.
*   **Q: Will you continue to prioritize M&A over dividends?** Management indicated they prioritize M&A and business investment but will return cash to shareholders via dividends if no immediate utilization is found.

### One-line takeaway
Gulf Oil delivered robust volume-led growth (16% YoY) and maintained per-litre margins despite inflationary headwinds, while continuing to invest in EV infrastructure (Tirex) and brand premiumization.
