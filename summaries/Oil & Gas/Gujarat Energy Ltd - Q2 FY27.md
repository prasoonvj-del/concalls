# Gujarat Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial growth and strategic long-term LNG sourcing secured, despite normalization of Morbi volumes.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** CGD EBITDA margin guidance cut to Rs 5-6/SCM (from Rs 5.5-6.5/SCM). Capex guidance maintained at Rs 1,000 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "stellar/excellent" performance to "rewarding/awarding" with a focus on long-term structural growth (CBG, LNG stations) vs Q1's focus on immediate volume surge.
- **New topics or risks:** Entry into compressed biogas (CBG) ecosystem; development of 6 LNG stations for long-haul transport; clarification on GSPC LNG debt (no GEL guarantee).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Morbi normalization confirmed (volumes dropped from ~8 MMSCMD in Q1 to 3.8 MMSCMD in Q2, as anticipated).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 9,826 crore (+59% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 1,019 crore (+20% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 639 crore (+22% YoY)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 19,496 crore (+61% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 2,502 crore (+43% YoY)
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 1,637 crore (+51% YoY)
- **CGD Volume:** 10.7 MMSCMD (Q2); 11.5 MMSCMD (H1)
- **Industrial PNG Volume:** 6.1 MMSCMD (Q2); 6.9 MMSCMD (H1)
- **CNG Volume:** 3.67 MMSCMD (Q2); 3.72 MMSCMD (H1)
- **Capex:** Rs 181 crore (Q2); Rs 308 crore (H1)
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** Rs 7,000 crore to Rs 7,500 crore

### Highlights
- Signed 10-year Henry Hub-linked LNG contracts for 0.8 MTPA (estimated pricing $7-$9/MMBTU DES).
- Initiated entry into compressed biogas (CBG) ecosystem; issued EOI for partners.
- Won PNGRB awards for Narmada and Bathinda GAs and Golden Peacock Award for Risk Management.
- Morbi volumes moderated to 3.8 MMSCMD in Q2 from ~8 MMSCMD in Q1.
- Management stated GSPC LNG is a separate entity and GEL has not provided any guarantees for its debt.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Capex:** Reiterate FY27 guidance of ~Rs 1,000 crore.
- **CGD EBITDA Margin:** Revised to Rs 5-6/SCM (previously Rs 5.5-6.5/SCM).
- **Gas Trading Margin:** Maintained at 5-6%.
- **Long-term Sourcing:** Targeting a total long-term LNG book of 4 MTPA.
- **Morbi Strategy:** Management expects to regain Morbi customers by 2028 using competitive long-term LNG contracts to compete with propane.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CNG volumes grew 11% YoY; Gujarat recorded 12% growth, while outside Gujarat grew 19%.
- Industrial PNG demand normalized in Morbi; strategy is to broaden industrial demand beyond Morbi to Rajkot, Surendranagar, Surat, Bharuch, Ankleshwar, Valsad, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli.
- Management noted that propane remains a competitor in Morbi, with a current price differential of Rs 7-8/SCM.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sourcing mix: 55% imported gas, 29% domestic/RLNG, 10% APM, 6% new well gas.
- Gas trading EBITDA increased 89% YoY to Rs 329 crore.
- Trading margins impacted by force majeure conditions on Qatar contracts, requiring replacement with spot volumes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 18 new CNG stations in H1 FY27; total 857 stations.
- Targeting 75+ new CNG stations in FY27.
- Evaluating propane import/storage infrastructure, though focus remains on securing competitive LNG.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased due to cargoes arriving in late September.
- Expecting tax refunds of ~Rs 900 crore by the end of the financial year.
- Deposits with GSFS are ~Rs 3,300 crore; management stated funds are parked based on highest interest rates.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility in West Asia impacting gas sourcing and pricing.
- Force majeure on long-term Qatar contracts causing volatility in trading margins.
- Propane competition in Morbi remains a structural challenge in the near term.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vivekanand (Ambit):** Asked about treasury management and GSFS deposits. Management clarified there is no linkage between GEL's deposits and GSPC LNG's loans from GSFS.
- **Amit Murarka (Axis Capital):** Asked about Morbi volumes and propane competition. Management confirmed Morbi volume at 3.8 MMSCMD and stated they are building a long-term LNG book to compete with propane by 2028.
- **Bineet Banka (Nomura):** Asked about trading margin volatility. Management explained that force majeure on Qatar contracts forced reliance on spot gas, impacting margins.
- **Deepak Malhotra (CapGrow Capital):** Asked about GSPC LNG debt. Management confirmed GEL has not provided any guarantees for subsidiary debt.
- **Somaiah V:** Asked about the $7-$9 LNG contract assumption. Management stated it assumes Henry Hub at $3-$3.5.

### One-line takeaway
Gujarat Energy Limited delivered strong H1 growth and secured long-term LNG supply to mitigate future volatility, though it lowered its CGD margin guidance amid normalization of Morbi industrial volumes.
