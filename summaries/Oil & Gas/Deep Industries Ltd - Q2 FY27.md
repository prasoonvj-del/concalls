# Deep Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong consolidated revenue and profit growth, healthy asset utilization, and robust multi-year demand tailwinds in India's oil and gas sector.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs 295.43 crore (Q2 FY27, +33.7% YoY); Rs 574.34 crore (H1 FY27, +36.6% YoY).
- Total Income (H1 FY27): Rs 616 crore.
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 124 crore (Q2 FY27, EBITDA margin 39.6%); Rs 255.83 crore (H1 FY27, EBITDA margin 41.6%, +23% YoY).
- Consolidated Net Profit (PAT): Rs 81.22 crore (Q2 FY27, PAT margin 26%, +14% YoY); Rs 170.37 crore (H1 FY27, PAT margin 28%, +28% YoY).
- Consolidated Cash Profit: Rs 106.53 crore (Q2 FY27, cash PAT margin 34%); Rs 219.93 crore (H1 FY27, cash PAT margin 36%).
- Free Cash Flow from Operations (H1 FY27): Rs 155 crore (+35% YoY).
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): Improved by 230 bps from 19.3% to 21.6% in H1 FY27.
- Order Book: Approx Rs 3,089 crore.
- Fleet: Over 80 compressors; 20 onshore rigs (14 workover, 6 drilling).
- Production Enhancement Contract (ONGC): Rs 1,402 crore contract over 15 years.
- Capex Guidance: Approx Rs 150 crore for production enhancement contracts over the next 12 months (some spilling over to next financial year); Rs 100 crore to Rs 200 crore in Dolphin Offshore depending on contract awards.
- Revenue Guidance: 30% to 35% growth in FY27 and FY28.
- PAT Guidance: Rs 350 crore to Rs 370 crore in FY27; Rs 475 crore to Rs 500 crore in FY28.
- Bidding Pipeline: Rs 700 crore to Rs 800 crore for FY27-FY28.

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 total income (Rs 616 crore) exceeded full-year FY25 total income (Rs 608 crore).
- Onshore drilling, workover services, gas processing, and production enhancement assets maintained healthy utilization; onshore rig fleet is fully utilized.
- Mori 5 incident caused a 5-6 month delay in the production enhancement incremental program; rig is now deployed, workover jobs resumed, and new well drilling starting, with incremental production expected from Q3 FY27.
- Dolphin Offshore step-down subsidiary revived; DP2 barge *Prabha* is in operation.
- Kandla chemical facility production revival initiated, expected to see contribution in Q4.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 operating revenue growth of 30% to 35% and PAT of Rs 350 crore to Rs 370 crore.
- Management expects FY28 operating revenue growth of 30% to 35% over FY27 and PAT of Rs 475 crore to Rs 500 crore.
- More than 90% of FY28 growth is secured by the existing order book (management statement).
- Yearly EBITDA margins are expected to be in the range of 42% to 43%, though individual quarters can see 300 bps to 400 bps variance due to operational cadence and maintenance costs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's crude production fell to 28 MMT in 2025-26, and import dependence reached a record 88.7%.
- Union Cabinet approved the 'Samudra Manthan' National Offshore Exploration scheme with an outlay of Rs 84,084 crore through FY2030-31.
- Government shifted policy focus from revenue sharing to an exploration-first approach, introducing monthly OLAP rounds for deepwater/ultra-deepwater blocks.
- Operational gas pipeline network crossed 25,000 km, with over 10,000 km under construction. National PNG Drive 3.0 launched.
- Government targeting a tenfold rise in compressed biogas (CBG) output via the Rs 23,731 crore GOBARdhan Scheme, with 3% CBG blending mandated this year, rising to 5% in FY28-29.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Global shipping, logistics, and insurance costs remain elevated due to supply disruptions and geopolitical issues.
- Project-based business cadence can cause quarterly EBITDA margin fluctuations of 300 bps to 400 bps due to equipment transitions and R&M costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of about Rs 150 crore for production enhancement contracts over the next 12 months.
- Additional capex of Rs 100 crore to Rs 200 crore planned for Dolphin Offshore subject to firm contract awards.
- Exploring entry into higher-capacity drilling rigs via a joint venture with an overseas partner to acquire necessary pre-qualification criteria.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Capex to be funded through internal accruals and debt.
- Receiving positive responses on Dolphin Offshore receivables; provided under Expected Credit Loss (ECL), with no write-offs considered currently.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External macroeconomic volatility, elevated global crude prices, and supply uncertainty.
- The Mori 5 incident led to a 5 to 6-month delay in the production enhancement incremental program.
- Equipment transitioning between completed and newly awarded projects causes quarterly margin and revenue variance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sudhir Bheda (Bheda Family Office) asked about the growth drivers for Q3 and Q4 given subdued Q2 growth.** Management replied that growth will be driven by incremental project contributions from Q1 and Q2, alongside revenue contributions from the production enhancement contract starting in Q3.
2. **Krish Salot (Veer Growth AIF) asked about the status of ONGC's PEC tender.** Management stated the tender due in September was extended to November, and they are confident of securing awards.
3. **Bhavya Gandhi (Bajaj Alternate Investment Management) asked if consolidated EBITDA margins would trend towards 48% due to PEC and Dolphin contributions.** Management stated EBITDA margins will improve with higher-margin revenue contributions, maintaining their annual guidance range of 42% to 43%.
4. **Sanjay Shah (Individual Investor) asked about revenue expectations from the PEC contract for the remainder of the year and next year.** Management expects Rs 50 crore to Rs 60 crore in revenue contribution from production enhancement in the balance period of FY27, and about Rs 150 crore on a full-year basis in FY28.
5. **Anubhav Goel (Cosma Ventures) asked about compressor fleet addition plans and asset pricing.** Management stated they do not overtrade or buy equipment without contracts; pricing is fixed from day one of contract signing, and core compression division growth averages 25% to 35%.
6. **Chandan Kumar (Narnolia Financial Services) asked how much of FY28 growth is secured by the existing order book.** Management stated that more than 90% is secured by the existing order book.

### One-line takeaway
Deep Industries delivered robust H1 FY27 performance with 36.6% revenue growth and strong cash flows, backed by structural tailwinds in India's upstream sector, a ~Rs 3,089 crore order book, and clear multi-year guidance.
