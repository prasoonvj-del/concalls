# Confidence Petroleum India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust growth across all key parameters with revenue up 127% YoY and PAT up 246% YoY.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,235 crore (+127% YoY from Rs 983 crore).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 161 crore (+93% YoY from Rs 83 crore).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.2% (expanded by 120 bps QoQ).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 69 crore (+246% YoY from Rs 20 crore).
- **EPS:** Rs 2.06 (vs Rs 0.62 in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 4,644 crore (+122% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 304 crore (+92% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 132 crore (+222% YoY).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 820 crore.

### Highlights
- Promoter shareholding increased to over 60%.
- Successfully maintained uninterrupted supplies amid Middle East geopolitical disruptions by sourcing 80,000 metric tons via imports from the USA.
- Added major new industrial and commercial customers, including Patanjali, Parle, and Pizza Hut.
- *Check against record:* The reported financial numbers align with the preliminary results released on November 14, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 performance to be better than H1.
- Management targets completing the rollout of 100 CNG stations in Bengaluru within the next 3 to 4 quarters (by next Diwali).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's LPG consumption grew from ~26.3 million metric tons in FY20 to 35.04 million metric tons in FY26.
- Geopolitical developments and supply uncertainties in the Middle East caused international LPG price volatility and availability challenges.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Procurement cycles lengthened and working capital requirements rose due to shifting LPG imports from the Middle East to the USA (leading to a Rs 298 crore increase in other current assets / supplier advances).
- Short-term borrowings increased temporarily to fund advance payments for US imports and are expected to normalize once Middle East supplies stabilize.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operational infrastructure includes 4 time-chartered LPG vessels, 68 bottling facilities, 478+ LPG tankers, 225 HCVs/LCVs, 3,400 dealers, 315+ auto LPG dispensing stations, 52+ CNG retail stations, 82+ CNG cascade vehicles, 15+ LPG cylinder manufacturing plants, and 3 high-pressure cylinder manufacturing plants.
- GAIL JV (Bengaluru CNG): 56 stations have completed capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands at Rs 820 crore (which management notes can be viewed lower if adjusting for ~Rs 290 crore in supplier advances).
- Short-term debt increased due to extended procurement cycles for US LPG imports.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing Middle East geopolitical developments and supply uncertainties impacting procurement logistics and working capital.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vinay Chaudhary (Individual Investor):** Asked about the jump in other current assets. Management clarified it reflects a ~Rs 298 crore increase in supplier advances for US LPG imports, which temporarily elevated short-term debt and working capital.
- **Garvit Goyal (Finsec/Individual):** Asked about customer retention against PSUs after long-term contracts expire. Management highlighted their unique selling proposition (USP) of "Gin Kar Lo Gin Kar Do" (cylinder weighing and residual gas refunds) and price competitiveness as key moats.
- **Sammed Vardhaman (Financial Services):** Asked about delays in the GAIL JV Bengaluru CNG station rollout. Management stated 56 stations are complete and the target for 100 stations is set for next Diwali (3-4 quarters).
- **Indu Bhushan Samal (Investor):** Asked about increasing dividends and conducting a share buyback. Management stated suggestions would be discussed internally and subject to board approval.

### One-line takeaway
Confidence Petroleum delivered stellar Q2 FY27 growth with revenue up 127% YoY, leveraging its integrated infrastructure and new customer acquisitions to navigate Middle Eastern supply disruptions.
