# IRM Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Oil & Gas*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong Q2 performance and volume growth driven by CNG, but caution flagged for Q3 due to regulatory HPHT gas allocation changes and raw material cost spikes.

### Key numbers
* **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations (net of excise):** Rs 334 crore (+29% YoY)
* **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 47 crore (+77% YoY)
* **Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin:** 14.2% (+388 bps YoY)
* **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 26 crore (more than doubled YoY); PAT margin at 7.7%
* **Q2 FY27 EBITDA per SCM:** Rs 7.73 (vs Rs 4.89 in Q2 FY26)
* **Q2 FY27 Total Sales Volume:** 61.23 MMSCM (+12% YoY)
* **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 660 crore (+26% YoY)
* **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 109 crore (+108% YoY); EBITDA margin 16.5% (+646 bps YoY)
* **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 59 crore (+124% YoY)
* **H1 FY27 Sales Volume:** 120.16 MMSCM (+10% YoY; CNG +23%, Commercial PNG +61%)

### Highlights
* CNG mix reached ~66% of total sales volume in H1 FY27.
* Geographical volume split in H1 FY27: Banaskantha GA ~47%, Fatehgarh Sahib 33%, Namakkal & Trichy (NT) 11% (up from ~6% in FY26), Diu & Gir Somnath 9%.
* Executed MoU with Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation (TNSTC); converted 83 buses to CNG, expanding fleet to 183 buses in Namakkal and 55 in Tiruchirappalli.
* Tied up connectivity hookups with IOC in NT and GTN in Diu & Gir Somnath.
* Maintained stable gas sourcing in Q2 FY27, with spot gas comprising ~0.5% of total requirements.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management guides for an EBITDA margin of ~15% for H2 FY27.
* Management expects temporary margin/volume moderation in Q3 FY27 due to HPHT gas supply realignments, with sequential recovery anticipated in Q4 FY27.
* Reiterated focus on profitable, sustainable volume growth rather than aggressive expansion at depressed margins.
* Sourcing visibility secured up to 2030 through contracts with Shell India, GSPC, IOC, and GAIL.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Industrial PNG volumes faced pressure from relative fuel economics (alternate fuels like FO and coal), but showed sequential recovery in Q2 FY27.
* MoPNG revised HPHT gas allocation methodology on September 3, temporarily impacting domestic gas availability for smaller CGD operators; a follow-up circular on October 19 provided partial relaxation via phased implementation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Q2 Sourcing portfolio mix: APM ~21% (capped at USD 7/MMBtu), New Well Gas ~13% (~USD 10.2/MMBtu), HPHT ~33% (~USD 8.9/MMBtu), RLNG ~30% (USD 11–12/MMBtu), and Spot ~0.5% to 1-2%. Average sourcing cost stood at ~USD 9.5/MMBtu.
* Q2 raw material cost increased by ~Rs 1.2 per SCM. 
* Cost increases were passed on to end-customers via calibrated price hikes where competitive dynamics allowed.
* Other expenses include a promoter royalty fee of 2% on revenue from operations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Total FY27 CapEx target: Rs 250–260 crore (Rs 111 crore incurred in H1 FY27: Rs 54 crore in Q1, Rs 57 crore in Q2).
* IPO Proceeds utilization: Rs 358.68 crore spent out of Rs 496 crore raised (Rs 170.18 crore for NT CGD network, Rs 135 crore for debt repayment, Rs 53.50 crore for general corporate purposes). Unutilized balance of Rs 137.08 crore allocated for NT network development.
* H1 FY27 Infrastructure status: CNG stations at 156 (+34% YoY, 576 dispensing points); MDPE pipeline network at 7,313 km (+20% YoY); Steel pipeline network at 3,441 inch-km (+17% YoY).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Gross debt stood at ~Rs 45 crore as of September 30, 2026.
* Cash, bank balances, and mutual fund investments totaled Rs 253 crore, maintaining a net-cash position.
* Net worth increased to ~Rs 1,050 crore.
* Average borrowing interest rate is 7–8%; finance costs include bank guarantee charges (0.5–0.6% rate).

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Geopolitical instability in West Asia driving volatility in Brent crude prices and foreign exchange rates.
* Policy and allocation shifts in domestic HPHT natural gas by MoPNG.
* Potential customer switching in the industrial segment if natural gas loses cost competitiveness against coal or fuel oil.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Moksh Ranka (Aurum Edge):** Asked if management is reconsidering promoter royalty.  
  * **Management:** Comfortable with the arrangement; the 2% royalty on revenue from operations will continue as disclosed in the IPO prospectus.
* **Kiran Gadge (Knightstone Capital):** Requested details on sourcing mix and unit costs.  
  * **Management:** Q2 portfolio had ~21% APM ($7/MMBtu), ~13% New Well Gas ($10.2), ~33% HPHT ($8.9), ~30% RLNG ($11–12), and ~0.5% spot; overall average cost was ~$9.5/MMBtu.
* **Nilesh Ghuge (HDFC Securities):** Asked why Q3 margins will moderate sequentially despite low spot exposure in Q2.  
  * **Management:** September HPHT allocation circular temporarily reduced domestic gas allocations during a 20-day transition window, lifting raw material costs. With 80% of the impact mitigated via the October circular and long-term contracts, H2 EBITDA margin should stay ~15%.
* **Abhir Pandit (Old Bridge Mutual Fund):** Asked if management/promoters would consider a share buyback given market capitalization is below gross block.  
  * **Management:** Acknowledged the merit of the suggestion for board/promoter consideration, but stated no immediate buyback plan exists as capital remains committed to NT GA CapEx and energy diversification opportunities.
* **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company):** Asked about the QoQ decrease in depreciation expense (Rs 13.8 crore in Q2 vs Rs 16.4 crore in Q1).  
  * **Management:** Q1 included an exceptional prior-period asset verification charge; Q2 levels reflect the normalized ongoing depreciation run rate.

### One-line takeaway
IRM Energy generated strong Q2 FY27 earnings with a net-cash balance sheet, while proactively managing temporary regulatory changes in domestic HPHT gas allocation.
