# Enviro Infra Engineers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management expressed confidence in meeting annual guidance despite margin pressure, citing a strong order book and execution momentum.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 359.2 crore (+49% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 75.7 crore (+17.87% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 21.07% (vs 26.65% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 45.2 crore (+6.47% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 12.38%.
*   **Total Order Book:** Rs 6,721 crore (Water/Wastewater: Rs 3,694 crore; Renewable/BESS: Rs 3,027 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Segment Contribution:** Water/wastewater contributed 71% of revenue (Rs 255 crore); renewable energy contributed 29% (Rs 104 crore).
*   **New Wins:** Secured Rs 113 crore contract from Sardar Sarovar Narmada Nigam; Rs 207.5 crore renewable contract via Suyog Urja; Rs 256.9 crore for two HAM projects in Varanasi.
*   **HAM Portfolio:** Increased to five projects.
*   **Team Expansion:** Employee count increased from 900 to 2,300 to support execution.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue:** Management reiterated guidance of Rs 2,000 crore.
*   **FY27 PAT:** Management reiterated guidance of Rs 260-270 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Management expects 19-20% at a blended level.
*   **Long-term:** Management targets 25-30% annual growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Water/Wastewater:** Management described the segment as "bullish" with a bidding pipeline of Rs 6,000-7,000 crore.
*   **Renewables:** Focus is on executing the current order book before pursuing significant new orders.
*   **Expansion:** Exploring overseas opportunities and desalination projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Compression:** Management attributed the decline in EBITDA margin to higher raw material costs, the renewable business mix, and increased employee costs.
*   **Cost Normalization:** Management expects employee costs to normalize to 5-5.5% of sales and finance costs to 3-3.5% of sales as the year progresses.
*   **Input Costs:** Raw material price impact is estimated at 1-2% of topline.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Suyog Urja:** Total acquisition value is Rs 311 crore. First tranche paid; second tranche of Rs 100 crore due after FY27.
*   **Strategy:** Integrating across solar, wind, and BESS; developing cattle feed/biogas projects in MP.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Management acknowledged the cycle is "bloated" but stable; expects improvement by September.
*   **Cash Flow:** Management expects OCF to turn positive.
*   **Asset Quality:** No bad debts reported.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution Timing:** Management noted that while Q1 revenue was lower than historical seasonality (20% of annual), they expect a jump in Q3/Q4.
*   **Government Payments:** Slowdown in cash flow receipts from government clients remains a factor.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the significant decline in EBITDA margins?** A: Management cited a combination of raw material cost increases, the shift in project mix toward renewables, and a sharp increase in employee headcount.
*   **Q: Can you achieve Rs 2,000 crore revenue given Q1 performance?** A: Management confirmed they are well on target, noting that BESS projects from NTPC will drive significant revenue growth from Q3 onwards.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Suyog Urja acquisition?** A: Management explained the three-tranche payment structure and confirmed they expect Rs 400-450 crore revenue from this subsidiary in FY27.
*   **Q: Are you chasing growth at the cost of margins?** A: Management denied this, stating they are maintaining disciplined bidding and will not pursue low-margin projects.

### One-line takeaway
Enviro Infra Engineers is banking on a strong Rs 6,721 crore order book and a ramp-up in renewable execution to meet its Rs 2,000 crore revenue target, despite current margin headwinds from input costs and a bloated working capital cycle.
