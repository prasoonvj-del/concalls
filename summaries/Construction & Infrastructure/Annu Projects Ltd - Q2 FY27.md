# Annu Projects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reiterated that business execution is heavily H2-skewed and defended fundamentals, while acknowledging project execution delays linked to awaiting right-of-way (ROW) approvals and steep post-IPO share price erosion.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 15.74 crore (Total income: Rs 16.54 crore vs Rs 3.70 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 2.94 crore; EBITDA margin: 18.68%.
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 1.01 crore (vs Loss Before Tax of Rs 3.48 crore in Q2 FY26); EPS: Rs 0.19.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 35.65 crore (vs Rs 6.51 crore in H1 FY26; growth of 5.7x).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 7.00 crore; EBITDA margin: 19.64%.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 2.29 crore (PAT margin: 6.4%, EPS: Rs 0.45; vs Loss Before Tax of ~Rs 10 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Order book (as of Sep 30, 2026):** Rs 1,018.39 crore across 30 ongoing projects (aggregate contract value of Rs 1,987 crore).
- **Order book composition:** Telecom 81.23%, Sewerage 14.65%, Railway signalling 2.49%, Gas pipelines 1.63%.

### Highlights
- Maiden earnings call post-listing on BSE and NSE.
- Continues past pattern of expanding into renewable energy through an MoU with GEIE for JVVNL rooftop solar supply worth Rs 44.3 crore (8,000 systems in Dausa, Rajasthan).
- Secured an OFC backbone contract worth Rs 12.81 crore (143 km) from Northeast Frontier Railway.
- Bid for ~Rs 3,000 crore worth of tenders; LOI is awaited for ~Rs 125 crore in November 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Expects total order book to reach around Rs 2,000 crore by the end of FY27 (management expectation).
- Expects business momentum to strengthen in Q3 and be particularly strong in Q4 FY27, maintaining its historical second-half weighting.
- Management remains confident of maintaining its multi-year revenue growth CAGR and sustaining EBITDA margins at current levels (~18–20%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Market bidding has turned highly competitive, putting pressure on bidding margins.
- Management reiterated it will not bid below internal margin thresholds merely to expand order volume.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total IPO proceeds received: Rs 175.06 crore; Issue expenses: Rs 20.5 crore.
- Earmarked capex: Rs 15.4 crore for purchase of HDD machines (to expand in existing and new states); no additional capex or funding needs anticipated currently.
- As of September 30, 2026, total IPO utilization stood at Rs 70.3 crore (Rs 54.4 crore for working capital, balance for issue expenses).
- Evaluating expansion into turnkey solar projects (~100 MW currently under negotiation).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables reduced from Rs 156 crore (March 2026) to Rs 142 crore; unbilled revenue declined from Rs 76 crore to Rs 59 crore.
- Collected ~Rs 73 crore during H1 FY27 from previous-year billings.
- Receivable days decreased to 192 days from 237 days in March 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Right-of-Way (ROW) and regulatory delays:** BharatNet Phase III Kerala package (EPC portion of ~Rs 500+ crore) cannot commence execution until ROW approvals are granted by the Department of Telecommunications.
- **Seasonality and milestone billing rules:** Q1 and Q2 execution is historically weak due to monsoons stopping trenching, leaving milestones incomplete (if 1% is unfinished, billing cannot occur).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q on sequential revenue decline (Rs 15.74 cr in Q2 vs ~Rs 20 cr in Q1):** Management attributed this to monsoon seasonality disrupting trenching work and project milestone billing criteria.
- **Q on execution and timeline of BharatNet Kerala project:** Management clarified the project accounts for ~Rs 741–800 crore of the order book, of which Rs 260 crore is an ongoing 10-year AMC (~Rs 50 crore recurring AMC revenue annually alongside sewerage). The remaining ~Rs 500+ crore EPC portion awaits ROW clearances before physical execution can start.
- **Q on stock price dropping ~60% post-IPO and lack of investor communication:** Management answered that the issue price was set via book-building based on performance and market conditions, arguing operational fundamentals will show through in subsequent quarters via disciplined execution.
- **Q on achieving FY26 EPS levels (~Rs 7 vs H1 FY27 EPS of Rs 0.45):** CMD expressed 100% confidence, emphasizing that execution and billing heavily crystallize in Q3 and Q4.

### One-line takeaway
Annu Projects relies entirely on an H2 execution ramp-up and pending government ROW approvals to convert its Rs 1,018 crore order book into revenue and meet aggressive full-year expectations.
