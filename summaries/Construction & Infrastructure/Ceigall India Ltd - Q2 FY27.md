# Ceigall India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong revenue and profitability growth, driven by execution across diversified segments like renewables and power transmission.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained minimum 15% revenue growth guidance and same EBITDA margin guidance.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positively toward the contribution of new verticals (renewables and T&D) driving outperformance.
- New topics or risks: Addition of major power transmission project (Lakadia-Jam Khambhaliya-Jamnagar) and Arunachal Pradesh frontier highway packages. 
- Last quarter's promises: Appointed dates for VRK 11, VRK 12, and Indore-Ujjain received as previously targeted.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 974.5 crore (up 23.8% YoY from Rs 787 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 130.5 crore (up 42.3% YoY from Rs 91.8 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 13.4% (up from 11.7% in Q2 FY26)
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 82 crore (up 46.7% YoY from Rs 55.9 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Standalone PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 8.4% (up from 7.1% in Q2 FY26)
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,022.5 crore (up 26.8% YoY from Rs 806.6 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 174 crore (up 53.2% YoY from Rs 113.6 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 17% (up from 14.1% in Q2 FY26)
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 92.2 crore (up 64.1% YoY from Rs 56.2 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Order Book:** Approximately Rs 22,681 crores as of September 30, 2026
- **Standalone Debt:** Approximately Rs 436 crores as of September 30, 2026
- **Consolidated Debt:** Approximately Rs 1,730 crores as of September 30, 2026

### Highlights
- Secured a letter of intent from REC Power Development and Consultancy Limited for the Lakadia-Jam Khambhaliya-Jamnagar transmission project in Gujarat (~Rs 5,700 crores including GST).
- Secured 5 packages for NH 913 Frontier Highway in Arunachal Pradesh via JV, aggregating ~Rs 3,128 crores.
- Received appointed dates for three HAM projects: Varanasi-Ranchi-Kolkata greenfield highway packages 11 and 12, and Indore-Ujjain Greenfield Highway project (worth ~Rs 4,050 crores).
- Wholly owned subsidiary commissioned 10 megawatts of solar capacity ahead of schedule under the 147 megawatts solar power plant in Maharashtra.
- Achieved provisional COD for the Jalbehra-Shahbad project; received completion certificate for the Delhi-Saharanpur Highway project post-quarter.
- Order book composition: roads and highways 55%, transmission and distribution 21%, renewable energy 16%, metro projects 4%, and industrial infrastructure 3%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a minimum of 15% revenue growth for the year and expects to overachieve it.
- EBITDA margin guidance maintained, with expectations to overachieve.
- Targeting order inflows of Rs 6,000 crores for FY2027 (management noted ~Rs 5,400 crores already achieved).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management sees strong momentum in infrastructure driven by renewables, T&D, and roads, with the company targeting a minimum 25% project IRR across bids.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin expanded to 13.4% and consolidated EBITDA margin to 17%, aided by healthy margins in renewables, transmission, and new HAM projects (VRK 11 and 12).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total equity requirement for the entire asset portfolio estimated at Rs 2,632 crores (Rs 669 crores infused till September; remaining balance is Rs 1,962 crores). Targeting ~Rs 700 crores equity infusion in H2 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.2x; consolidated debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.8x.
- Asset monetization expected to yield ~Rs 250 crores in inflows and reduce debt by ~Rs 540 crores (Bathinda-Dabwali targeted for Q3, Jalbehra-Shahbad for Q4).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Morena solar transmission line finalization is still underway, making execution start this year tough.
- Consolidated operating cash flow remained negative in H1 due to HAM project payment cycles (receiving 40% from NHAI).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial) asked about revising revenue growth and margin guidance given strong H1 performance.** Management replied they guide conservatively for a minimum of 15% and will try to overachieve, keeping guidance figures unchanged.
2. **Vaibhav Shah asked about execution timelines for the Morena solar/BESS project.** Management stated that starting this year is tough as the transmission line contractor is still being finalized.
3. **Vaibhav Shah asked about expected inflows and debt reduction from HAM asset monetization.** Management stated inflows should be ~Rs 250 crores and debt reduction ~Rs 540 crores, targeting completion before March 31.
4. **Yash Parkar (Patel Investments) asked about the conversion and execution trajectory of the Rs 22,681 crore order book.** Management stated they have a good order book for the next 4 years with a developer-side share of 73%.
5. **Yash Parkar asked about reasons for negative consolidated operating cash flow in H1 despite profitability.** Management explained it is due to HAM project milestone payment structures where 40% is received from NHAI.

### One-line takeaway
Ceigall India reported a strong Q2 and H1 FY27 with robust revenue and PAT growth, backed by order book diversification into renewables and T&D, alongside steady progress in asset monetization.
