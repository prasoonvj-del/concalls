# Construction & Infrastructure - Roads & highways EPC: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings calls across the Roads & Highways EPC sector reveal a bifurcated environment: traditional road construction and awarding continue to face headwinds (including slow execution, land acquisition delays, and aggressive bidding in small packages), prompting aggressive strategic diversification. Companies are expanding into non-road verticals such as power transmission (TBCB), renewables (solar/BESS), mining MDO, railways, water infrastructure, and international EPC. Balance sheet deleveraging through asset monetization (such as HAM asset transfers to InvITs or private equity) was a dominant theme for several players.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the board note that while national highway construction has faced near-term deceleration or contraction, the broader infrastructure bidding pipeline remains large, supported by government thrust on non-road sectors.
* **Divergence:** 
  * **Ashoka Buildcon** and **KNR Constructions** highlighted ongoing headwinds in national highway construction and delayed state government receivables (e.g., irrigation dues in Telangana for KNR), leading Ashoka to trim its FY27 revenue guidance.
  * In contrast, **G R Infraprojects** raised its FY27 standalone revenue growth guidance to ~20% (from 15%), and **Ceigall India**, **Dilip Buildcon**, and **B.R. Goyal** reported robust top-line growth (up 23% to 61% YoY) driven by execution ramp-ups and diversification into mining, renewables, and toll collection contracts.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Cost Pressures:** West Asian geopolitical tensions have kept crude oil, diesel, and bitumen prices elevated, putting pressure on operating margins across the sector. 
* **Mitigation:** Most EPC players have price escalation clauses with central agencies like NHAI/MoRTH (though often with a lag), while toll collection players like B.R. Goyal noted that toll operations require minimal working capital and yield high ROCE (30%-50%) despite lower EBITDA margins.
* **Margin Divergence:** 
  * **KNR Constructions** and **Ashoka Buildcon** reported compressed standalone EBITDA margins (9.2% and 9.88% respectively) due to fixed overheads and input cost inflation.
  * Conversely, **Ceigall India** expanded standalone margins to 13.4% and consolidated margins to 17%, aided by healthy contributions from renewables and transmission projects.

## Capex & capacity
* **Asset Monetization for Equity Recycling:** Companies actively utilized asset sales to fund new growth. **KNR Constructions** completed the monetization of all four of its HAM SPVs to Indus Infra Trust; **Dilip Buildcon** signed definitive agreements with Alpha Alternatives for transmission and solar projects; **Ceigall India** and **G R Infraprojects** are advancing monetization of completed HAM assets.
* **Non-Road Capex:** **KNR Constructions** and **Dilip Buildcon** reported significant capital allocation toward mining equipment and infrastructure (e.g., KNR targeting ~Rs 300–Rs 320 crore in mining capex for FY27). 

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Revisions:** 
  * **Ashoka Buildcon** lowered its revenue growth guidance from 10-15% to flattish/~5% for FY27 due to sector-wide construction pressures.
  * **G R Infraprojects** raised its FY27 revenue growth guidance to ~20% (previously 15%).
  * **KNR Constructions** trimmed its full-year order inflow guidance to Rs 4,000–5,000 crore specifically for H2 FY27.
* **Strategic Milestones:** **PNC Infratech** secured a legal stay from the Delhi High Court on NHAI debarment orders, allowing it to resume bidding. **Dilip Buildcon** finalized its asset co-investment deal with Alpha Alternatives within six weeks of board approval.

## What to watch next quarter
* Realization of cash proceeds from asset monetization deals (e.g., Ashoka Buildcon, Ceigall India, KNR Constructions, G R Infraprojects).
* Execution ramp-up in non-road segments (mining, transmission, renewables) to offset sluggishness in highway construction.
* Resolution of pending Appointed Dates and state-level payment backlogs (such as irrigation receivables in Telangana for KNR).

* **Ashoka Buildcon Ltd:** Flattish standalone revenues and trimmed FY27 growth targets prompted deleveraging via asset monetization and TBCB/railway diversification.
* **B.R.Goyal Infrastructure Ltd:** Delivered 61% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27, balanced by high-ROCE toll collection and traditional EPC execution.
* **Ceigall India Ltd:** Posted strong Q2 profit growth fueled by execution across roads, renewables, and power transmission.
* **Desco Infratech Ltd:** Scaled H1 revenues up 78.5% YoY while expanding from CGD into solar, power T&D, and compressed biogas.
* **Dilip Buildcon Ltd:** Advanced "DBL 2.0" by executing major asset monetization agreements with Alpha Alternatives, expanding mining cash flows, and maintaining growth targets.
* **G R Infraprojects Ltd:** Raised full-year revenue guidance to ~20% following strong 64% standalone revenue growth in Q2.
* **KNR Constructions Ltd:** Completed its 4-HAM asset monetization to de-lever while reallocating capital into heavy mining equipment and urban projects.
