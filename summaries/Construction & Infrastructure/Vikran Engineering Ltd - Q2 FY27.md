# Vikran Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong standalone growth and order wins were offset by consolidated margin pressure due to NOPL project disbursement delays and un-recognized price variation revenue.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 standalone revenue guidance of Rs 2,300 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "clear execution roadmap" to acknowledging "challenging task" for H2 execution due to NOPL disbursement delays (now resolved).
- **New topics or risks:** NOPL disbursement delays (new); IREDA loan secured (new); Data center plans (downplayed/ignored).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Debtor days reduction (on track, ~200+ crore reduction achieved); NOPL financing (delivered).

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 220 crore (+25% YoY).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 20.12 crore (9.1% margin).
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 7.87 crore.
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 211.84 crore (+20% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 22.26 crore (10.5% margin).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 2.75 crore.
- **H1 Standalone Revenue:** Rs 424.12 crore (+27% YoY).
- **H1 Standalone PAT:** Rs 25.37 crore (+71% YoY).

### Highlights
- Secured two Power Grid orders: Rs 120.69 crore (400 kV GIS substation) and Rs 153.76 crore (400 kV AIS substation).
- Commissioned 132 kV Miao-Namsai transmission line (Arunachal Pradesh) and 400 kV GIS substation at Malerkotla (ahead of schedule).
- NOPL Solar: 18 sites (90 MW) commissioned; 100 MW in advanced stages.
- NOPL secured IREDA financing of Rs 2,941.19 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets >Rs 2,300 crore standalone revenue for FY27.
- Expects Rs 40-50 crore revenue from solar power sales in H2 FY27.
- Peak solar power sales expected to reach >Rs 500 crore annually.
- Consolidated revenue target for FY27: Rs 950-1,000 crore.
- FY28 consolidated revenue target: Rs 2,400-2,500 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Power T&D demand remains strong; Solar EPC execution planned across 8 clusters.
- Data center opportunities: Currently ignoring low-margin/time-delayed projects; focusing on high-profit, urgent opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated margins impacted by higher direct costs and un-recognized price variation revenue (pending client certification).
- Management expects to maintain ~15% EBITDA margins for substation EPC.
- NOPL EBITDA margin expected at 85-88% (pre-interest).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- NOPL project: 969 MW AC capacity (1,163 MW peak).
- Planning international expansion in UAE to build relationships with large investors for co-investment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone operating cash flow: Rs 258 crore in H1.
- Debtor days reduced by ~200 crore; targeting 40-50 days reduction by year-end.
- Jal Jeevan project: Received Rs 45 crore in Q2.
- Quarterly interest cost expected at Rs 18-20 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- NOPL disbursement delays (now resolved).
- Price variation revenue recognition lag (pending client certification).
- Crude oil price volatility impacting non-pass-through items.
- Execution risk in remaining 10% of NOPL land acquisition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prathamesh Sawant (Abakkus):** Asked about margin recovery. Management: Price variation revenue recognition in H2 will help.
- **Rajesh:** Asked about NOPL completion. Management: Expected by end of Q1/Q2 FY28.
- **Saurabh Singhvi:** Asked about interest servicing. Management: Solar cash flows are sufficient; project is self-sufficient.
- **Mahesh Kaushik:** Asked about H2 revenue targets. Management: Confident in hitting Rs 2,300 crore standalone target.
- **Chirag Gandhi:** Asked about NOPL cost/revenue recognition. Management: Revenue recognized only on panel installation; delay in disbursement caused temporary slowdown.

### One-line takeaway
Vikran is navigating a transition phase where NOPL solar project execution and financing delays temporarily pressured consolidated margins, but management remains confident in standalone growth and long-term asset-backed cash flows.
