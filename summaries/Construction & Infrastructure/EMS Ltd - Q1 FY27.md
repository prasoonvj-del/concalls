# EMS Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in revenue and margin recovery, expecting full-year performance to match FY25 levels despite Q1 seasonality.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Operating Income:** Rs. 125.72 crores (up ~50% QoQ)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs. 25.53 crores (margin: 25.53%, up ~39.81% QoQ)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs. 15.03 crores (margin: 11.95%, up ~184.65% QoQ)
- **Consolidated Operating Income:** Rs. 157.24 crores (up ~30% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 28.14 crores (up ~31.62% QoQ)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs. 15.49 crores (up ~1.28% QoQ)
- **Order Book:** Rs. 2,329 crores (as of July 2026)
- **New Order Inflow (Q1 FY27):** ~Rs. 317 crores
- **Post-Q1 Order Inflows:** ~Rs. 158 crores (in Q2 up to the call)
- **Working Capital Cycle:** ~120 days turnover

### Highlights
- Standalone and consolidated financials showed sequential recovery compared to previous quarters.
- Secured Rs. 317 crores in new work orders during Q1 FY27, plus an additional Rs. 158 crores in Q2 up to the date of the call.
- Management highlighted being L1 for a project in Banaras exceeding Rs. 100 crores.
- Geographic revenue contribution for the quarter: ~42% from Uttar Pradesh and ~61% from Uttarakhand.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year FY27 revenue, EBITDA, and PAT to match FY25 levels (targeting Rs. 900 crores to Rs. 950 crores in revenue).
- Q2 revenue is expected to be 30% to 35% higher than Q1, with stronger growth (>50% QoQ) expected in Q3 and Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Active bidding continues in Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka.
- West Bengal (Kolkata) project execution was previously impacted by election-related moral code of conduct restrictions, which have now been lifted. Execution acceleration is expected from Q3.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed establishment, labor, and machinery rental costs compressed margins during previous quarters when revenues dropped due to site hindrances (rains, elections).
- Margins are expected to return to historical levels as revenue scales up.
- Management noted that while competition is increasing slightly, margins will remain close to historical (pre-2023-2024) levels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major standalone capex plans for FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital requirement is typically maintained at around 120 days turnover (Rs. 300 crores to Rs. 350 crores for a Rs. 1,000 crore turnover).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Heavy rains and site restrictions (e.g., in Uttarakhand) temporarily stall execution, leading to fixed-cost absorption pressure and delayed government payments.
- Government bureaucracy delays the approval of revised estimates and escalations following site disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Darshil Pandya (Margin recovery & working capital):** Asked why margins dropped and if fixed costs would always impact operations during rains. Management answered that fixed establishment/machinery costs cannot be removed during temporary hindrances, but margins recover as revenue scales. Working capital remains at ~120 days.
2. **Dhruv Hingorani (H2 Revenue feasibility & QoQ comparisons):** Questioned if the Rs. 900–950 crore revenue target is achievable given required H2 run rates and suggested YoY comparisons over QoQ. Management affirmed confidence in achieving H2 targets, noting Q3 and Q4 will be the strongest quarters.
3. **Daksh Prashar (West Bengal project):** Asked about the execution run rate following the lifting of election restrictions. Management clarified that sewerage network projects take time to ramp up, and better execution will be visible from Q3 and Q4.
4. **Sanjay (Geographic expansion & order book):** Inquired about order book size and state expansion. Management stated the order book is Rs. 2,329 crores (as of July 2026) and they are bidding in Bihar, MP, Maharashtra, and Karnataka.

### One-line takeaway
EMS Limited reported a sequential recovery in Q1 FY27 and maintained its full-year guidance to match FY25 revenue and profit levels, driven by an order book of Rs. 2,329 crores and an expected acceleration in H2 execution.
