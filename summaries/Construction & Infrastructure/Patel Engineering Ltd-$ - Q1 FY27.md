# Patel Engineering Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong profit growth and improved credit metrics, supported by steady execution and a clear strategy for non-core asset monetization.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 1,281 crore (up ~4% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs. 93.5 crore (up 24.5% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 14.02% (vs 13.4% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Consolidated Order Book:** Rs. 14,636 crore.
*   **Consolidated Debt:** Rs. 1,293 crore (up ~Rs. 100 crore QoQ).
*   **Debt-Equity Ratio:** 0.28.

### Highlights
*   **Credit Rating:** Upgraded to 'A stable' (long-term) and 'A1' (short-term) in June 2026.
*   **Asset Monetization:** Completed sale of a 27-acre land parcel in Telangana for Rs. 26 crore.
*   **Project Milestones:** Sleemanabad Tunnel (11.95 km) breakthrough achieved; Subansiri Lower HEP has 4 units operational (1,000 MW contribution).
*   **Order Book Mix:** Hydropower (62%), Irrigation (17%), Roads and Urban Infrastructure (17%), Tunneling (4%).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects ~10% revenue growth in FY27, with significant growth contribution expected in H2.
*   **New Orders:** Targeting ~Rs. 8,000 crore in new orders for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management expects to maintain EBITDA margins between 13%-14%.
*   **Asset Monetization:** Targeting Rs. 150-200 crore from non-core asset sales in FY27.
*   **Future Growth:** Management projects 15% revenue growth in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Pipeline:** Rs. 9,000 crore of bids under evaluation; identified a near-term opportunity pipeline of Rs. 60,000 crore.
*   **Sector Focus:** Management highlighted significant opportunities in Hydropower (133 GW potential) and Pump Storage (100 GW target by 2035).
*   **Key Projects:** Mentioned upcoming opportunities including Kalai-II HEP (Rs. 14,000 crore), Kamla HEP (Rs. 26,000 crore), Fotu La Tunnel (Rs. 1,198 crore), and the second Mumbai-Pune expressway (Rs. 15,000 crore).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Finance Costs:** Reduced by ~Rs. 10 crore YoY due to debt reduction.
*   **Cost Control:** Implementing IoT and AI-enabled equipment to monitor diesel consumption and improve process efficiency.
*   **Margin Drivers:** Margins are primarily driven by project mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Management indicated that working capital requirements of Rs. 100-200 crore may be needed to support execution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Increase in debt due to utilization of additional working capital limits for new projects.
*   **Receivables:** Days outstanding are between 40-45 days.
*   **Retention Money:** Rs. 200-250 crore currently held on the balance sheet.
*   **Promoter Pledge:** Currently 85-90%; management expects a 15-20% reduction this year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Competition:** Management noted increased competitive pressure impacting potential margin expansion.
*   **Litigation:** Pending litigation regarding the 'Patel Smondo' project (OC pending); management expects resolution within this financial year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the divergence between 4% revenue growth and 25% PAT growth?** A: Management attributed this to a Rs. 10 crore reduction in finance costs and operating leverage.
*   **Q: Can we expect 15% margins?** A: Management stated this is unlikely due to competitive pressure and reiterated the 13-14% target.
*   **Q: Status of promoter pledge reduction?** A: Management is in discussions with lenders and expects a 15-20% reduction this year.
*   **Q: Are there more exceptional items expected?** A: Management confirmed no further exceptional items are anticipated for FY27.
*   **Q: How will you fund working capital?** A: Through client advances, internal accruals, and potential incremental working capital borrowings of Rs. 100-200 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Patel Engineering delivered strong profit growth driven by finance cost reduction and operational efficiency, with a clear focus on monetizing non-core assets to deleverage while maintaining a 13-14% margin profile.
