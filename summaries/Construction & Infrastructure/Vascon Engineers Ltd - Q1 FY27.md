# Vascon Engineers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Lower EPC execution and revenue due to temporary cash flow constraints in two major projects, offset by a strong order book, intact EPC margins, and positive real estate momentum.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs 152 crore (vs. Rs 221 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 10 crore (vs. Rs 34 crore in Q1 FY26, which included a Rs 18 crore onetime gain from sale of an investment)
- Adjusted EBITDA (ex-onetime gain in Q1 FY26): ~Rs 15 crore
- Adjusted EBITDA Margin: ~6%
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 2 crore (vs. Rs 22 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EPC Revenue: ~Rs 148 crore (vs. Rs 203 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EPC EBITDA: ~Rs 13 crore
- EPC EBITDA Margin: ~9% (vs. 8% in Q1 FY26)
- Real Estate Revenue: ~Rs 4 crore
- Real Estate EBITDA: Loss of ~Rs 4 crore
- Total EPC Order Book (as of June 30, 2026): ~Rs 2,850 crore (External: Rs 2,531 crore, Internal: Rs 319 crore)
- Real Estate Sales Bookings (Q1 FY27): Rs 66 crore (~30,690 square feet)
- Real Estate Collections (Q1 FY27): ~Rs 20 crore
- Orchid Bookings (Q1 FY27): ~Rs 38 crore (cumulative bookings ~Rs 87 crore)
- Net Debt (as of June 30, 2026): ~Rs 152 crore
- Total Sanctioned Banking Limits: ~Rs 760 crore (Unutilized: ~Rs 355 crore)

### Highlights
- Secured a Rs 295 crore order from CPWD for constructing the RBI Colony in Guwahati.
- Received an LOI worth Rs 126 crore (excluding GST) from Maharashtra PWD on August 12, 2026, for a 300-bedded General Hospital at Wardha.
- Launched Tranquil Heights in Powai during the second week of June 2026; received RERA and other approvals for Prakash in Santacruz West for a near-term launch.
- Strengthened real estate leadership across business development, sales, marketing, quality, and customer experience.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a target to achieve Rs 1,200 crore top line for FY27, factoring in real estate revenue recognition in H2 and anticipated EPC ramp-up.
- Management targets new EPC order inflows of Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 2,000 crore in FY27.
- Long-term real estate ambition: Annual booking value of Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,500 crore by FY31.
- Expects EPC execution to normalize progressively from August 2026 as fund flows for affected projects commence.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Mumbai redevelopment market offers significant scope, with high demand for Grade A listed developers.
- Intense competition noted in Mumbai redevelopment from local and large developers; Vascon targets projects with GDV between Rs 250 crore and Rs 300 crore (and up to Rs 500 crore–Rs 750 crore) where large developers are less active.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EPC gross margins remain stable between 13% and 15%.
- Net working capital cycle stretched from ~45 days to ~65–70 days over the last 6 months due to geopolitical instability and local issues, but management expects normalization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total sanctioned banking limits stand at Rs 760 crore with ~Rs 355 crore unutilized.
- Raising Rs 80 crore through preferential warrants over an 18-month horizon (25% initial tranche received), intended primarily for real estate, EPC working capital, and general corporate purposes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased to ~Rs 152 crore as of June 30, 2026, driven by higher CC limit utilization in EPC projects (milestone-based payments and stretched working capital) and capital infusion in real estate.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary cash flow constraints in two major government EPC projects (Bihar Supaul and Sindhudurg) impacted Q1 execution and revenue recognition.
- Royal Rides project in Goa and Vashi Hospital project remain stalled/pending kick-off.
- Thane land parcel acquisition is progressing slowly with no immediate timeline (at least a year plus) for a continuous parcel.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Himanshu Upadhyay (SteadFort) asked about the increase in debt and cash flow allocation despite flat order books.** Management explained that short-term debt and working capital increased due to milestone-based payments and extended working capital cycles in two government EPC projects, alongside real estate capital needs.
- **Himanshu Upadhyay asked about real estate project timelines and viability in a competitive Mumbai market.** Management responded that Santacruz is targeted to complete by the end of FY27, Powai is a 3-year development, and they focus on mid-sized GDV projects (Rs 250–300 crore) where large developers aren't competing aggressively.
- **Kunal Shah (Individual Investor) asked for clarity on the two government projects facing cash flow issues.** Management identified them as the Bihar Supaul project and the Sindhudurg project in Maharashtra, noting that issues are now sorted and fund flows are available.
- **Kunal Shah asked about the status of the Royal Rides project.** Management stated it remains a live order worth Rs 225 crore (BOT ropeway project where Vascon is building an admin block), but advised assuming zero revenue from it for the current financial year due to stagnation.
- **Chaitanya Mantra (Individual Investor) asked why a Reliance order was cancelled.** Management clarified it was a mutual decision because Reliance changed its construction method from non-Mivan to Mivan, rendering original concrete rates unviable.

### One-line takeaway
Vascon Engineers reported a weak Q1 FY27 due to temporary cash flow constraints in two major EPC projects, but management remains confident of achieving a Rs 1,200 crore top line for the full year backed by a Rs 2,850 crore order book and upcoming real estate completions.
