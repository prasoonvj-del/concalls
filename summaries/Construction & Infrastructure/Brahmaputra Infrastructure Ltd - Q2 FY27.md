# Brahmaputra Infrastructure Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported consolidated revenue growth of 31% YoY and PAT growth of 37%, supported by an expanding order book and lower finance costs.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 118 crore (up 31% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 27.48 crore; EBITDA margin at 23.3%
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 21.11 crore (Umang Prithani cited PAT at Rs 20 crore, up 37% YoY)
- Standalone Revenue: Rs 104 crore
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 27 crore; EBITDA margin at 25%
- Standalone PAT: Rs 19.64 crore
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue: Rs 226.31 crore
- H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 50 crore
- H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT: Rs 36.59 crore
- Order Book: Rs 2,000 crore (including joint operations)
- Net Worth: Rs 381 crore
- Total Debt: Cash credit limit of Rs 104 crore and term loans of Rs 12.85 crore (Debt-to-equity at 0.345x)
- OCCPS (interest-free): Rs 165.1 crore

### Highlights
- Secured 4 new projects worth Rs 317 crore in Q2, including a bridge over River Ravi in Punjab, Turga Pumped Storage Project in West Bengal, and healthcare renovation in Assam.
- EPC division Q2 revenue grew 33% YoY to Rs 114 crore, with segment profit rising 73%.
- City Central Mall in Guwahati is almost fully operational with over 150 brands.
- Arbitration awards decided in company's favor stand at Rs 35 crore principal plus interest (approx Rs 50 crore total); management is arranging bank guarantees to release deposited amounts.
- Joint venture debt and operations consolidated starting April 1, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets order inflows of Rs 1,200 crore for FY27 (and Rs 1,500 crore over the next 18-20 months) and an order book of Rs 2,500 crore by FY28.
- Management aims to maintain EBITDA margins above 20%.
- Management targets scaling recurring real estate rental income from ~Rs 20 crore to Rs 60 crore by FY29.
- Management plans pan-India expansion from 10 to 15 states.
- Management expects a stronger H2 as 50% of the recent order book enters execution.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong tailwinds from public capex, PM Gati Shakti, and Northeast border connectivity.
- Government departments are enforcing stricter qualification criteria, causing 30%-40% bidder disqualifications, which benefits the company's strong documentation and in-house AI tools.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Plant operation, material, and construction costs accounted for ~74% of revenue in Q2.
- Fixed overheads (employee cost Rs 1.5 crore, other expenses Rs 1.96 crore) remained lean at ~3% of revenue.
- Finance costs declined 31% in Q2 due to repayment of un-ticketed loans and lower term loans.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested Rs 7.9 crore in joint ventures during H1.
- Planning common infrastructure (crushers, equipment, branch offices) across priority states to lower costs.
- Planned a new shopping mall plaza and residential complex (estimated value Rs 500 crore), expected to add Rs 30 crore+ in annual rental yield from FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity ratio stands at a stable 0.345x.
- OCCPS of Rs 165.1 crore is interest-free, with repayments scheduled from June 2027 to March 2034.
- Trade receivables halved to Rs 9.4 crore; trade debt reduced by Rs 50 crore.
- Consolidated cash and bank balance stood at Rs 23.42 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government departments have challenged won arbitration awards under Section 34, requiring bank guarantees to release deposited funds.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arnav Khan (BugleRock Capital)** asked about cash flow conversion and debt. Management clarified that cash was reinvested into working capital and capex for new contracts, and term loans will be fully repaid by FY27.
- **Akshay Jhawar** asked about arbitration awards and balance sheet debt increases. Management stated Rs 35 crore principal plus interest is under dispute/appeal requiring bank guarantees, and debt increased due to JV consolidation from April 1.
- **Rishabh Bora** asked about expansion outside the Northeast and margins. Management stated margins outside are comparable because they selectively bid on technically complex projects (e.g., West Bengal pump storage) with fewer bidders.
- **Arpan Gandhi** asked about revenue growth sustainability. Management stated 50% of the new order book is starting execution in the current month, driving H2 revenue growth.

### One-line takeaway
Brahmaputra Infrastructure reported robust Q2 FY27 performance with 31% revenue growth, a Rs 2,000 crore order book, and disciplined cost control.
