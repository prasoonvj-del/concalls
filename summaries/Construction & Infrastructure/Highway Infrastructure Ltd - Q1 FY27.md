# Highway Infrastructure Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong YoY top-line growth was offset by temporary profitability pressure in Q1 due to geopolitical strains impacting toll traffic.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 304.3 crore (+170.6% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 4.8 crore.
- **PAT:** Rs 1.1 crore.
- **Order Book:** ~Rs 778 crore as of June 30, 2026 (~Rs 500 crore EPC, remainder toll); grew to ~Rs 900 crore following recent project wins.
- **New Toll Wins:** ~Rs 28.7 crore (Kozhinjipatti toll plaza, NH-44) and ~Rs 80 crore (Krishnagiri-Thumbipadi section, NH-44) in Tamil Nadu.
- **Beverly Hills EPC Project:** ~Rs 70 crore value, execution started in Q1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Operating across tollway collection (asset-light), EPC, and real estate segments.
- Q1 profitability suffered due to localized traffic volume drops at the Moti Naroli toll project caused by geopolitical trade disruptions, a temporary NHAI bidding restriction, and voluntary surrender of an unviable toll contract.
- Toll traffic trends started recovering toward normal levels in July 2026.
- Geographic footprint expanded into South India (Tamil Nadu) on the NH-44 corridor, with ongoing evaluation to enter East/Northeast India (West Bengal, Assam, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets consolidated revenue of ~Rs 850 crore for FY27 (Rs 700 crore from tolling, Rs 150 crore from EPC) and ~Rs 1,200 crore for FY28.
- Management expects EPC revenue realization from the executable order book to be ~Rs 150 crore in FY27 and ~Rs 200 crore in FY28.
- Management expects profitability to recover sharply in Q3 and Q4 FY27 following seasonal trends and traffic normalization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Selective bidding focused on commercially viable EPC contracts (roads, bridges, urban infrastructure, commercial/industrial buildings) and larger toll collection projects.
- Company is evaluating opportunities in new government departments (MES, CPWD) and renewable energy production/transmission infrastructure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management is implementing internal technology integration, BIM software, and AI analytics to centralize multi-site operations, optimize manpower deployment, and lower execution error ratios.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company entered Tamil Nadu tolling for the first time and is conducting ground research to expand into West Bengal and Assam.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Toll business operates on an asset-light, H1 fixed-cost payment model to NHAI.
- EPC model operates on fixed contract value with variable execution costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical strains impacted trade, factory production, and commercial freight traffic along port-linked Western front corridors in Q1 FY27.
- Temporary NHAI bidding restrictions and traffic estimation risks on large toll projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nachiket Kale (NK):** *How is the recovery in traffic volumes?*  
  **Management:** Traffic in non-port regions has normalized, while Western front corridors linked to ports will take more time to fully recover; overall traffic trends are improving significantly.
- **Deep Shah (Equities):** *Will Q1 weakness impact full-year financial expectations?*  
  **Management:** Full-year outlook will not be significantly impacted; the company typically breakevens in Q1/Q2 and recovers rapidly in Q3/Q4.
- **Rahul Verma (Alpha Investments):** *What is the execution timeline for the Rs 507 crore EPC order book?*  
  **Management:** Realization is expected at ~Rs 150 crore in FY27 and ~Rs 200 crore in FY28 across 24 executable projects over a three-year span.
- **Priyam Shah (Value Equity):** *What is the company's bid success ratio?*  
  **Management:** Success ratio stands at 25% to 30% across both tolling and EPC segments.
- **Pratik Shah (Shah Associates):** *What is the execution timeline and contribution of the Beverly Hills project?*  
  **Management:** Total value is ~Rs 70 crore over a 15–16 month timeline, contributing 40–45% of expected EPC revenues over that timeframe.

### One-line takeaway
Highway Infrastructure expects full-year revenue of Rs 850 crore as toll traffic normalizes and execution ramps up following temporary Q1 geopolitical disruptions.
