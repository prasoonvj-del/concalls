# Telge Projects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and profit growth, though Q2 margins moderated due to deliberate investments in leadership and integration.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs 34.2 crore (up 104.6% YoY vs H1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 7.0 crore (up 134.8% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 20.8% (up 270 bps YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 5.0 crore (up 157.7% YoY)
- PAT Margin: 14.6% (up 300 bps YoY)
- H1 FY27 Order Book: Rs 14 crore
- Total Headcount: 305 employees globally (25 in US, 280 in India across Pune, Latur, Nashik)
- Net Worth (as of 30 Sept 2026): Rs 43.91 crore
- Cash and Cash Balances (as of 30 Sept 2026): Rs 20.17 crore
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio: 0.15x

### Highlights
- Celebrated the completion of the first year as a publicly listed company.
- Evolving from a specialist engineering partner into an integrated architecture and engineering platform; architecture and related design services represented ~17% of H1 revenue.
- Edward Farr Architects (EFA) contributed around Rs 5 crore in revenue in H1.
- Q2 revenue remained broadly consistent with Q1, but EBITDA margin moderated to 17.1% due to upfront investments in senior US leadership, software, and integration costs.
- *Continuity note:* While previous investor communication emphasized scaling an integrated AEC platform globally through acquisitions, management stated that potential M&A (like MEP) is on hold for the rest of FY27 to focus on internal integration and commercial conversion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue to land between Rs 70 crore to Rs 80 crore, targeting higher 70s to early 80s (aiming for ~60% CAGR growth).
- Management aims to maintain previous year's PAT percentages as the target for the full year.
- Expects Q3 and Q4 to be better than Q1 and Q2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- United States is the largest market, accounting for ~80% of H1 revenue; Australia accounts for 10%, and Europe/UK/nearby countries account for 10%.
- Served ~95 customers in H1, including 28 new customers added.
- Delivery centers remain in Pune, Latur, and Nashik, with particular growth and positive client feedback noted for the Nashik center (currently 40 people).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee expenses doubled to Rs 15.5 crore due to team and leadership expansion.
- Software and subscription expenses increased to Rs 2.7 crore (from Rs 1.1 crore) due to expanded licenses and design capacities.
- Depreciation increased to Rs 1.1 crore (from Rs 30 lakh).
- Finance costs declined due to lower utilization of borrowing capital for working capital.
- Q2 margin moderation driven by: employee costs (+410 bps QoQ impact), software/subscription costs (+190 bps QoQ impact), and other operating/integration expenses (+130 bps QoQ impact). Software expenses are expected to remain more or less stagnant going forward.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major acquisitions or investments planned for the remainder of FY27; focus is on integration and execution.
- Warrants were issued previously to prepare for a potential significantly larger acquisition (5x to 6x in scale) in future periods.
- Initiated "AI Revelation 1.0" program to improve productivity, operational efficiency, and scalability for repetitive administrative and coordination tasks without direct human replacement.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) currently around 77 days; management aims to control it back to 45 to 50 days.
- Operating cash flow stood at Rs 1.2 crore against EBITDA of Rs 7.1 crore due to working capital absorption (including GST credit amounts and income taxes paid).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Quarterly results can vary in a project-based business due to project timing and investment phases.
- Integration risks associated with acquired entities (EFA, Wheaton Detailing) and the need to convert pipeline proposals into confirmed awarded work.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Riya Jain (Sapphire Capital):** Asked if higher costs will continue in H2 and what sustainable margins are. Management stated major investments for the year are already made, expecting better margins in H2 to meet the Rs 70–80 crore revenue guidance, with sustainable operating margins around 14% and scope for improvement.
2. **Karan Shah (Param Capital):** Asked about the timeline and funding for the previously discussed MEP acquisition. Management answered that the MEP acquisition plan is on hold until at least March 2027 to focus on integration, though warrants were issued earlier to prepare for larger future opportunities.
3. **Karan Shah (Param Capital):** Asked about Edward Farr's H1 revenue contribution. Management stated EFA contributed around Rs 5 crore.
4. **Taher Hyderabadwala (Grobiz Fund):** Inquired about software expense scaling. Management responded that software capacity is largely secured through block deals and acquired licenses, and costs will remain more or less stagnant from here aside from incremental licenses for added manpower.
5. **Adarsh Mehta (Unnati Capital):** Asked about India vs. US employee split. Management clarified total global headcount is 305, with 25 in the US and 280 in India (Pune, Latur, Nashik).

### One-line takeaway
Telge Projects delivered strong YoY revenue and profit growth in H1 FY27, but experienced margin moderation in Q2 due to upfront investments in US leadership and platform integration, prompting a shift in focus toward commercial execution and cost discipline.
