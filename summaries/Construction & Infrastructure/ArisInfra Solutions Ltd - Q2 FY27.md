# ArisInfra Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2/H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered growth in revenue, profitability, and margins on both sequential and YoY bases despite monsoon seasonality.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 308 crore (up 28% YoY, up 6% QoQ)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 36 crore (up 58% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 11.6% (expanded 220 bps YoY)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 23 crore (up 50% YoY)
- **PAT margin (Q2 FY27):** 7.4%
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 599 crore (up 32% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 66 crore (up 63% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin (H1 FY27):** 11.1% (expanded 210 bps YoY)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 43 crore (vs Rs 20 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Operating cash flow (H1 FY27):** Rs 60 crore (91% of EBITDA)
- **Net working capital days:** 54 days (improved from 84 days in previous September)
- **Contract manufacturing & DaaS revenue share:** 66% of Q2 FY27 revenue (vs 53% in Q2 FY26); 64% in H1 FY27
- **Partner manufacturing capacity:** Increased from 9.1 million tonnes to 11.7 million tonnes in H1 FY27 across 11 partners and 4 markets
- **DaaS GDV under execution:** Rs 2,750+ crore across 15 active projects (vs Rs 1,839 crore at Q1 FY27 and Rs 1,267 crore at end of FY26)
- **Vendor deposits:** Down to ~Rs 162 crore (long-term down to Rs 149 crore, short-term at Rs 12 crore) from ~Rs 200 crore

### Highlights
- Shift in revenue mix towards higher-margin Contract Manufacturing and DaaS (Developer as a Service), driving profitability.
- Proprietary technology platform has read over 14 lakh delivery notes (including handwritten ones) to maintain real-time visibility over margins, credit exposure, and reconciliation.
- Vendor deposits reduced by Rs 13 crore while capacity expanded, utilizing bank FDs for non-fund-based support to earn interest.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects YoY revenue growth of around 30-35% going forward.
- Management guides for an EBITDA margin of 11.5% - 12% for FY27 and 12-13% by FY30.
- Management stated requirement of about Rs 290 crore to Rs 300 crore to reach FY30 growth targets, to be funded largely by working capital lines and internal accruals without any new equity fund raise.
- Management does not plan any capital investments in FY26 or FY27 for DaaS or further manufacturing assets.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Construction materials sourcing and manufacturing remain highly fragmented, presenting formalization opportunities.
- Infrastructure segment is viewed as a much larger opportunity for materials (stone aggregates, concrete, asphalt), while real estate covers materials and services.
- Stone aggregates and concrete/asphalt categories are expected to formalize and consolidate first.
- DaaS model faces no direct single-roof competition; outsourcing trends in real estate support long-term growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher margins driven by increasing contribution from Contract Manufacturing and DaaS segments (contributing ~92% of segment EBITDA before non-cash IND AS adjustments in H1).
- Raw material price increases are passed on almost real-time to customers due to limited-quantity, short-duration (couple of weeks to a month) purchase orders.
- Current capacity utilization stands at 42% to 45%, providing headroom for the next 6 to 9 months.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Asset-light model: capacity increased to 11.7 million metric tonnes without investing in plant assets.
- No minimum offtake guarantees given to manufacturing partners; partnership contracts are long-term (mostly 5 years).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Steady-state net working capital days expected between 60 to 70 days (currently trending at 54 days).
- Long-term fixed deposits of Rs 114 crore held as security against borrowings, bank guarantees, and corporate guarantees for partners.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Working capital and cash flow uncertainty remain key areas requiring strict credit structuring, technology monitoring, and customer selection.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital)** asked about capacity utilization and future plans. Management clarified current utilization is 42-45%, providing 6-9 months of headroom, and capacity additions will precede demand.
2. **Amit Mehendale (RoboCapital)** inquired about equity dilution for FY30 guidance. Management stated no equity raise is needed, as growth will be funded via working capital and internal accruals (~Rs 290-300 crore required).
3. **Love Gupta (Counter Cyclical Investments)** asked if DaaS requires working capital financing. Management clarified that DaaS requires zero capital investment and only earns service fees.
4. **Ishit Desai (FOD Family Office)** asked about visibility for a 4x jump in contract manufacturing to Rs 2,000 crore by FY30. Management cited massive uncaptured market share (<1%) and scaling wallet share with existing and new customers.
5. **Nikhil Gupta (Vaayu Capital)** questioned the impact of a real estate downturn. Management noted that 45-50% exposure to plotted development (short 6-month execution cycles) and shorter 10-14 storey residential towers insulate them from deep multi-year cycles.
6. **Vaibhav KR (Orelius Asset Management)** asked about contingent liabilities regarding partner credit facilities. Management explained that FDs are placed with banks to allow partners to draw non-fund-based facilities, keeping cash on Arisinfra's balance sheet to earn interest.

### One-line takeaway
ArisInfra delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 28% YoY revenue growth and a 220 bps expansion in EBITDA margins to 11.6%, propelled by a high-margin product mix shift toward Contract Manufacturing and DaaS.
