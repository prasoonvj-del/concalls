# Oriental Rail Infrastructure Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Supported by improved order execution, healthy volume growth, and faster-growing profitability.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 172.5 crore, up 29% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 27.9 crore, up 29% YoY
- EBITDA Margin: 16.2%
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 15.3 crore, up 44% YoY
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue: Rs 310.1 crore, up 23% YoY
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 48.8 crore, up 35% YoY
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 26.1 crore, up 58% YoY
- OFPL (Subsidiary) H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 250 crore, up 41% YoY
- Q2 FY27 Wagon Production: 390 wagons (vs. 300 wagons in Q1 FY27)
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: Rs 115 crore (H1 FY27 cash flow stood at approx. Rs 42 crore)
- Long-term Debt: Reduced to Rs 16 crore
- Consolidated Order Book: Approx. Rs 1,571 crore as of October 21, 2026 (2.74x FY26 revenue)

### Highlights
- Profitability grew faster than revenue due to a favorable product mix, backward integration, improved operating leverage, and better cost absorptions.
- Wagon subsidiary OFPL manufactured 390 wagons in Q2 FY27, representing a 30% sequential growth.
- Entered a joint venture agreement (ABOR Projects LLP) for railway coach interior products and refurbishment. Management expects the parent company's interior business to exceed 80% of FY27 turnover.
- Partnered with HUM International for smart wagon technology tenders (300 to 400 smart wagons).
- Signed a technology development agreement with Russia-based Uniwagon Design and Engineering Center for a 25-ton axle load bogie and solid-bottom gondola car.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 revenue and execution to be at least 25% to 30% higher than H1.
- Management targets a production run rate of 600 wagons per quarter starting from Q3.
- Leasing business operations and approach to leasing tenders are planned to start from April 2027.
- Prototype clearance and field trials for the 25-ton axle load bogie are targeted within 6 to 9 months, with regular supply orders expected from FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways operates about 4 lakh wagons. Management estimates an annual requirement of 30,000 to 35,000+ wagons for replacement and capacity growth.
- The industry's total manufacturing capacity is estimated between 30,000 to 35,000 wagons.
- Average wagon leasing tenure is about 10 years (ranging from 9 to 12 years).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material volatility (such as steel) is covered under price variation formulas applicable on wagon tenders.
- Short-term borrowings increased during H1 to support higher working capital requirements for increased production volumes and raw material advance procurement.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Existing installed capacity stands at 3,600 coach sets annually for interiors and 2,400 wagons annually for OFPL.
- Capacity augmentation plans for existing facilities are intended to be finalized by February/March 2027 and implemented from April 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents: Rs 115 crore.
- Long-term debt: Rs 16 crore.
- Short-term borrowings increased due to higher working capital needs and advance raw material planning.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Plant technical changes and capital adjustments delayed Q2 wagon production from a target of 500 down to 390 units.
- International volatility in the Middle East impacted steel supply chains, prompting higher advance procurement and increased inventory levels.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Harshit Khadka (RoboCapital)** asked about the shortfall from the targeted 500 wagons in Q2. Management explained that plant technical and capital changes were delayed by about 15 days, resulting in 390 completed units, but work on 500 was substantially done and the backlog will be covered in Q3.
- **Maitri Shah (RoboCapital)** inquired about the coach refurbishment tender pipeline and competition. Management stated they bid for over Rs 150 crore, expect total quoted tenders to reach Rs 400 crore by December, compete with 2-4 players, and target a 30% strike rate.
- **Sahil Suresh Lendave (RoboCapital)** asked about the smart wagon tender status. Management stated financial bids will finalize post-Diwali in November, expecting a conservative minimum order of 200 units out of 400.
- **Saket Kapoor (Individual Investor)** questioned the long-term stock market performance perception. Management stated they do not comment on market perception but remain focused on sustained growth, overcoming past COVID and wheel shortage challenges.
- **Padmanaban (Analyst)** asked about export and component initiatives. Management revealed they are in advanced discussions for two major wagon components and exploring export opportunities via a public sector network.

### One-line takeaway
Oriental Rail reported a 29% YoY revenue increase to Rs 172.5 crore in Q2 FY27 with a robust order book of Rs 1,571 crore, while addressing execution bottlenecks to scale wagon production toward 600 units per quarter in H2.
