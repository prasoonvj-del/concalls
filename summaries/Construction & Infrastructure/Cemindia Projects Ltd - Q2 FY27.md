# Cemindia Projects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 25% YoY revenue growth, robust order inflow, and upgraded credit ratings.

### Key numbers
- **Total Operating Income:** Rs 2,749 crore (up 25% YoY from Rs 2,195 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 at Rs 5,470 crore (up 15% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 280 crore (up 15% YoY from Rs 243 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 at Rs 565 crore (up 12% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10.2% in Q2 FY27 compared to 11.1% in Q2 FY26; H1 FY27 at 10.3%.
- **PAT:** Rs 131 crore (up 21% YoY from Rs 108 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 at Rs 271 crore (up 10.8% YoY).
- **Net Worth & Debt (as of Sep 30, 2026):** Net worth stood at Rs 2,619 crore; Net debt at Rs 773 crore; Net debt-to-equity ratio at approximately 0.3x.
- **QIP Proceeds:** Raised Rs 1,400 crore by issuing 1.26 crore equity shares at Rs 1,108 per share.
- **Order Inflows:** Secured Rs 5,968 crore since the start of the quarter (Rs 3,487 crore in Q2 FY27 and Rs 2,481 crore in October 2026).
- **Order Book:** All-time high of Rs 32,715 crore.
- **Capex:** Rs 150 crore incurred in H1 FY27.
- **Working Capital:** 96 days; Inventory at 28 days.

### Highlights
- Received positive credit rating upgrades: ICRA upgraded long-term and short-term ratings to AA (Stable); CARE upgraded to AA- (Stable); CRISIL assigned a fresh rating of AA (Stable).
- QIP issue attracted bids of around 2.3x the base deal size of Rs 1,000 crore from domestic mutual funds and financial investors.
- Received Rs 85 crore additional claim payment for the Udangudi project.
- Vadhavan Port work has resumed after being stalled for 1.5 years.
- Bangladesh project substantially completed and payments received; Abu Dhabi and domestic metro projects (Pune, Delhi, CMRL, Bengaluru) progressing well.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 order inflow guidance of Rs 25,000 crore, expecting to cross it.
- Management maintains revenue growth guidance of 20%-25% for the year.
- Management expects to maintain an average EBITDA margin of around 10% plus for the full year.
- Management targets a 25% CAGR on the top line for the next three years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- External bid pipeline stands at approximately Rs 1,30,000 crore (Marine: Rs 25,000 crore; Underground metro: Rs 6,000 crore; Tunnels: Rs 25,000 crore; PSP: Rs 10,000 crore; Elevated metro: Rs 5,000 crore; Nuclear: Rs 25,000 crore).
- Group's annual capital expenditure plan of Rs 2 lakh crore presents an addressable market opportunity of around Rs 50,000 crore for the company.
- Data center order book is close to Rs 5,000-6,000 crore, with significant opportunities across Vizag, Gujarat, and Mumbai.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Data center segment margins are expected to be around 8%-10% due to non-specialized nature, while underground and marine sectors offer better margins.
- Margin threshold delays on recently secured new jobs impacted current quarter margins, but management expects conversion in upcoming quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex for H1 FY27 stood at Rs 150 crore. Normal H2 capex expected at Rs 150-200 crore.
- Large TBM (Tunnel Boring Machine) deployment of Rs 1,000-1,500 crore is planned to be executed and deployed in FY28 if large ticket jobs are secured.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stands at Rs 2,600+ crore with an ROE of 27% and ROCE of 33%.
- Working capital days at 96 days and inventory at 28 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution challenges include certification delays and starting execution on newly won projects.
- Short-term impact on ROE acknowledged due to aggressive capital deployment and large machinery investments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhananjay Mishra (Centrum Broking):** Asked about margin sustainability and Q2 order details. Management stated they are confident of 10%+ margins for the year and detailed wins including Andhra Pradesh data center (Rs 1,000 crore), Dhamra Port (Rs 800 crore), and Project Varsha (Rs 700 crore).
2. **Aditya Sahu (Analyst):** Inquired about group vs. non-group order book bifurcation. Management clarified that group orders constitute roughly 53%-54% of the total order book, and H1 revenue mix was ~35%-36% from the group.
3. **Abhinav (Analyst):** Asked about data center execution timelines. Management stated civil work takes ~24 months, MEPF starts afterward, and large revenue chunks from data centers will reflect starting January/February (Q4).
4. **Jainam Shah (Analyst):** Questioned TBM sourcing risks regarding China. Management stated China is not an issue as TBMs can be sourced from other countries.
5. **Nipurn Khemka (Analyst):** Raised concerns over ROE dilution from capital-intensive investments. Management responded that it is a long-term strategic call and balance sheet leverage headroom remains strong.
6. **Sunil Shah (Calsyst Asset Manager):** Asked about addressable market from the Adani group's Rs 2 lakh crore annual capex. Management stated the addressable market is around Rs 50,000 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Cemindia Projects reported a strong Q2 FY27 with a 25% YoY revenue jump, an all-time high order book of Rs 32,715 crore, successful QIP fundraising, and broad-based execution across marine and urban infrastructure.
