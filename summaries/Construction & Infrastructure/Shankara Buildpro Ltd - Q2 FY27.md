# Shankara Buildpro Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT delivered, driven by robust steel volume growth and recovering non-steel sales despite challenging market conditions.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): Rs. 2,280 crores (up 43% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs. 77 crores (up 53% YoY)
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 3.39%
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs. 46.2 crores (up 57% YoY)
- PAT margin (Q2 FY27): 2.02%
- Steel volume (Q2 FY27): 3.03 lakh tonnes (up 27% YoY)
- Steel revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs. 2,100 crores (up 46% YoY)
- H1 steel volumes: 5.53 lakh tonnes (up 19% YoY)
- Non-steel revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs. 181 crores (up 17% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- Working capital: Maintained at 22 days in Q2
- Finance cost (Q2 FY27): 0.52%
- ROCE (Q2 FY27): 43%
- Store/fulfillment center count: 134 total (5 added in H1, including Jaipur and Nanded)

### Highlights
- Management acknowledged the loss of Founder and Managing Director Mr. Sukumar Srinivas on October 30, 2026. Mr. Dhananjay Mirlay Srinivas was appointed Managing Director by the board on November 1, 2026.
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT in Q2 FY27 (consistent with the record dated 2026-11-05).
- Western region contribution to top line expanded significantly to 20% over the last few years.
- Dealer financing covered approximately Rs. 15 crores of receivables in Q2.
- 3 more stores/fulfillment centers are in the pipeline for coming quarters.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its target/aspiration to achieve steel volume of ~12.0 lakh tonnes (1.2 million tonnes) for the full year.
- Steady-state EBITDA margin guided at around 3.5%, with a long-term aspiration of ~4% in 2 to 3 years.
- Non-steel revenue share targeted to increase to 12%-15% of total revenue in 2 to 3 years.
- Steady-state store additions targeted at ~5 stores every year (though ~8 are planned for FY27).
- Steady-state working capital days guided around 30 days.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 was a soft quarter for the industry, but Buildpro grew volumes by 10%; Q2 outlook was more positive.
- Growth driven by government infrastructure spending, increased demand for steel structures/buildings, aggressive marketing, and wider product ranges (flats and roofing).
- Non-steel segment saw continued recovery (H1 growth of 16%) aided by recovery in the Karnataka market and increased focus on key categories.
- Direct stores contribute ~50% of sales; retail and non-retail split is roughly 50-50.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin contraction to 3.39% in Q2 was attributed to market competition and aggressive marketing/discounts for entering new markets during a seasonally subdued quarter.
- Steel price movements netted out in Q2 with no major inventory gain or loss; fixed pricing in consumer markets (quarterly resets) prevents immediate pass-through.
- Non-steel EBITDA margin is around 5%, while steel is around 2.5% to 3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Average store Capex: Rs. 2.5 crores to Rs. 3 crores for a 4,000 to 5,000 square feet space.
- Store payback period: 4 to 5 years (breakeven in 1.5 to 2 years; mature store EBITDA at ~4%).
- Expanded footprint into Rajasthan (first fulfillment center in Jaipur) and Maharashtra (Nanded store).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital held at 22 days in Q2 (aided by dealer financing of ~Rs. 15 crores).
- Finance cost held steady at 0.52%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- October and November are leaner months due to festival seasons across the country.
- Macro headwinds in previous years (FY25-FY26) kept the building materials environment tepid.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Viraj Mehta (Investment Partners LLP) asked about margin pressure despite high steel volumes.** Management explained it was due to competitive intensity and marketing outlays for new markets in a subdued quarter.
- **Rahul Kumar (Vaikarya Fund) asked about the impact of higher payables and steel price volatility on margins.** Management stated payables were manageable via supplier support, and steel price fluctuations netted out in Q2 with zero inventory gain/loss.
- **Kiran D asked if steady-state EBITDA margins should be modeled at 3.5%.** Management confirmed 3.5% is the steady-state expectation.
- **Gunit Singh suggested share buybacks instead of dividends given the stock price fall.** Management noted the suggestion and stated they will discuss it with the board.
- **Riddesh Ram Gandhi asked about store unit economics and steel price neutrality.** Management stated average Capex is Rs. 2.5-3 crores, payback is 4-5 years, and mature stores yield ~4% EBITDA; they noted Q1 had a ~10% hit on inventory due to falling steel prices, while Q2 was balanced.

### One-line takeaway
Shankara Buildpro reported record Q2 FY27 financial performance with strong 43% revenue growth, driven by robust steel volumes and recovering non-steel demand, while maintaining tight working capital control under leadership transition.
