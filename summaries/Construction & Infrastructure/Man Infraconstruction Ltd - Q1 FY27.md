# Man Infraconstruction Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-26 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong start with growth across execution, new launches, financial performance, and balance sheet strengthening.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 218 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) attributable to shareholders:** Rs 72 crore (up 29% YoY)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs 768 crore as of June 2026 (vs Rs 686 crore at end of previous FY)
- **Total Borrowings:** Rs 78 crore (modest; management stated this is largely partner contributions/utilizations rather than institutional debt)
- **Net Debt:** Net debt-free
- **Q1 Pre-sales:** >85,000 square feet, translating to ~Rs 290 crore
- **Upcoming Launches (FY27):** 1.1 million square feet carpet area, ~Rs 6,600 crore estimated GDV
- **Cumulative Pre-sales Target:** Rs 5,000 crore over the next two years
- **Vision 2031 GDV Target:** Over Rs 35,000 crore (management expects to achieve this ahead of schedule)
- **US Operations:** ~$35 million transferred; Ritz-Carlton pre-sales at ~$25 million; $5 million invested in an oceanfront property

### Highlights
- **Project Deliveries & Progress:** 
  - *Mira-Bhayendar (Aaradhya Parkwood):* Received OC for 50% of the project (Tower C & D; 5.3 lakh sq ft carpet area, 1,000 units, Rs 925 crore sales potential); 90% inventory sold across 4 towers.
  - *Vile Parle (Jade Park):* >60% of 3.5 lakh sq ft carpet area sold; 100% RCC expected to be completed by next year.
  - *Bandra:* Launched ultra-luxury *Marina Vista* (Pali Hill) under the MS Collection (Rs 500 crore potential); sold 30% inventory in 2 months. Second launch *Artek Park* (BKC) sold >25% inventory. Secured IOA for *Berkeley House* at Mount Mary (off Bandstand) with >Rs 1,000 crore GDV.
  - *Mulund West (Atmosphere O2 / O2 High Street):* Launched commercial building *O2 High Street*; Tower G (3.2 lakh sq ft carpet area) 75% sold, expected delivery by December 2027.
  - *Ghatkopar (Aaradhya One Park):* >60% of 5.3 lakh sq ft carpet area sold across 11 towers; finishing underway, expected delivery before March 2027.
  - *South Mumbai (Aaradhya Avaan, Tardeo):* >60% sold of 6.5 lakh sq ft carpet area; delivery split into phases with the first half (~32 habitable floors) targeted for March 2028 (nearly 2 years prior to RERA schedule of Dec 2030).
  - *Tardeo 2.0:* Acquired; received IOA, potential >Rs 2,000 crore GDV; launch expected within this financial year.
- **Upcoming Launches:** Marine Lines project targeted for launch this March (GDV >Rs 3,000 crore), alongside Mount Mary, Tardeo 2.0, and Pali Hill.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Guidance:** Maintained >25% growth in PAT over FY26.
- **Sales Guidance:** Cumulative pre-sales target of Rs 5,000 crore over the next two years.
- **Long-term Vision:** Group-level GDV of Rs 35,000 crore by 2031 to be achieved ahead of schedule.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong and continuous demand from homebuyers, particularly in premium Mumbai micro-markets (Bandra, South Mumbai).
- Mumbai real estate maintains robust momentum irrespective of global macro headwinds.
- Raw material costs (marble, tiles, steel) are rising by 13-15%; management expects prices to move upwards or stabilize.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (marble, tiles, steel) have increased by 13% to 15%.
- Bottom-line percentage remains steady due to in-house EPC execution and zero debt interest drag.
- Ultra-luxury projects carry higher per-sq-ft margins than volume gated communities.
- Development Management (DM) projects yield 2.5x to 3x returns on invested money, with investment typically <10% of GDV.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total in-house future construction order book of Rs 9,000-10,000 crores across residential and commercial portfolio.
- No current intention to expand outside MMR for the next couple of years due to superior margin realizations in Mumbai.
- US operations (Miami, Florida): Completed villas (one sold, second held as show house, third up for sale at ~$15M); Ritz-Carlton branded residences targeted for completion by December 2030. Entire transferred capital (~$35M) expected to be recovered back by 2031 with profits.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents at Rs 768 crore (as of June 2026).
- Total borrowings at a modest Rs 78 crore (primarily partner contributions/utilizations rather than institutional debt); company remains net debt-free.
- Last fiscal year saw a slight dip in cash flow due to new project acquisitions and working capital deployment for under-construction assets, but upward trajectory has resumed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global macro events (e.g., US/UAE conflicts) can cause temporary global liquidity shifts, though Mumbai market resilience insulates local demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **On Q1 sales being lower than run-rate for the Rs 5,000 cr target:** Management explained real estate sales are lumpy and not linear like a Netflix subscription; major launches (Marine Lines, Tardeo 2.0, Berkeley House) are slated for later in the year, keeping them confident in the 2-year cumulative target. (Management did not dodge).
2. **On expanding outside MMR:** Management stated they have no intention to move outside Mumbai for the next couple of years because per-sq-ft profit margins in Mumbai significantly exceed other cities and face fewer cycle downturn risks. (Management did not dodge).
3. **On US investments rationale and cash repatriation:** Management noted that currency depreciation (USD moving from 75 to 95 INR) provided strong capital appreciation alongside project margins, with full capital recovery expected by 2031. (Management did not dodge).
4. **On Marine Lines launch delay:** Management explained the delay was due to FSI changes, policy adjustments, and plot expansion acquisitions, which ultimately strengthened the plot size and top/bottom-line potential. (Management did not dodge).
5. **On future PAT reaching the Rs 500 crore mark:** Management affirmed that the ambition is to hit Rs 500 crores in the next 2 to 3 years, backed by strong cash flows and marquee launches like Aavan and Marine Lines. (Management did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
Man Infraconstruction delivered a stable Q1 FY27 with 8% revenue growth and 29% PAT growth, maintaining a net-debt-free balance sheet with Rs 768 crore in cash while gearing up for Rs 6,600 crore in mega-launches this fiscal.
