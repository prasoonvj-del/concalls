# Ashoka Buildcon Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While diversification into international and non-road segments showed progress and order inflows were healthy, domestic highway awarding remained subdued and revenue/margin performance faced pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Total Income:** INR 1,320 crore (flat YoY vs INR 1,339 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** INR 126 crore, down 17% YoY; EBITDA margin at 9.5%.
- **Standalone PAT:** INR 31.5 crore, up 3% YoY vs INR 30.6 crore in Q1 FY26.
- **Consolidated Total Income:** INR 1,534 crore, down 21% YoY (largely due to Q3 FY26 asset monetization).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 292 crore, down 55% YoY; EBITDA margin at 19.0%.
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 127 crore.
- **Order Book:** INR 15,251 crore as of June 30, 2026 (excluding ~INR 450 crore orders received post-June 30).
- **Segment-wise Order Book Breakdown:** Roads & Railways INR 9,648 crore (63.3%); Power T&D INR 5,066 crore (33.2%); Building EPC INR 536 crore (3.5%).
- **Road Order Book Sub-segment:** HAM ~INR 1,500 crore; EPC ~INR 6,780 crore; Railways INR 1,346 crore.
- **Toll Collection:** Jaora-Nayagaon road project gross total toll collection at INR 75 crore, up 8% YoY.
- **HAM Equity Invested:** INR 638 crore up to June across six HAM projects. Pending equity: INR 179 crore for FY27; INR 72 crore each for FY28 and FY29.
- **Capex:** INR 25 crore incurred in Q1 FY27 (INR 7 crore for international, balance domestic).

### Highlights
- Received letter of award (LoA) from Central Housing and Planning Authority, Guyana, for 4-laning of Versailles-Parika Highway (Region 3) valued at USD 35.42 million (~INR 328 crore).
- Joint venture received LoA from Chhattisgarh State Industrial Development Corporation for the development of Gems and Jewellery Park in Raipur under PPP mode (Ashoka holding 51%, project value ~INR 450 crore, 5-year construction, 30-year lease extendable to 90 years).
- Diluted stake in Ashoka Purestudy Technologies Private Limited from 59% to 39.33% via preferential allotment, reclassifying it from a subsidiary to an associate company.
- International revenue contribution in Q1 stood at INR 145 crore (noted as INR 1451 crore in transcript, clarified as 80% from Guyana).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management lowered revenue growth guidance for FY27 from 20% to between 10% and 15% due to flat Q1 performance and supply chain uncertainties.
- Management guided EBITDA margin for FY27 to be around 9.5% to 10.5% (lowered by 0.5% due to Q1 shortfall), expecting margin improvement in H2 as revenues ramp up.
- Order inflow guidance maintained at INR 6,000 crore to INR 8,000 crore for the remaining 3 quarters (secured INR 780 crore in Q1, L1 in ~INR 1,800 crore).
- Capex target for FY27 maintained at approximately INR 125 crore.
- Third-party debt post asset monetization expected to be in the range of INR 500 crore to INR 600 crore (total debt including subsidiary debt around INR 1,100–1,200 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic highway awarding environment continues to remain subdued; NHAI awarded ~5 km in June vs 102 km in May.
- Construction activity in June declined 32% YoY to ~274 km; first 2 months of FY27 construction down ~34% YoY to ~638 km.
- Sector focus shifting from awarding volume to proper project appraisal, land availability, and execution efficiency.
- Strong opportunities identified in railways (budget allocation of ~INR 2.93 lakh crore, 7 high-speed rail corridors, dedicated freight corridor), power transmission & distribution, and international infrastructure.
- Management expects good road awarding in Q3 and Q4, with NHAI shifting towards larger-sized packages that rationalize competition.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone margins impacted in Q1 due to initial establishment expenses, mobilization, and administrative/employee costs loaded for new verticals.
- Interest cost paid to subsidiaries was INR 17 crore in Q1. Standalone interest cost expected to trend down towards INR 200–210 crore over the next 2 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Working capital increased by ~INR 250 crore in Q1 due to funds deployed for new projects where billings have not yet commenced.
- Bowaichandi HAM Appointed Date expected in the first week of October, with ~15% work execution targeted for FY27. Mithi River work has started; Angola work to commence in ~2 months.
- Asset monetization: 4 assets expected to close by September/early October (realizing ~INR 700 crore out of total INR 1,150 crore deal value), and the remaining 2 assets by December/Q4. Chennai ORR monetization expected by year-end; Jaora-Nayagaon by March end or H1 next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total third-party debt and working capital being managed down through WIP/debtor realizations and asset monetization. Pending collections on previously monetized BOT (~INR 50 crore) and HAM (~INR 90 crore) assets are 30% received, with balance under process/compliance.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subdued domestic highway awarding and lower pace of execution across the sector.
- Supply chain uncertainties impacting Q1 performance.
- Potential holdbacks of INR 30 crore to INR 40 crore during asset monetization transactions pending compliances.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Sahu (HDFC Securities):** Asked about bid pipeline and reasons for lowering revenue growth guidance. Management stated NHAI/MoRTH pipeline is ~INR 1,000 billion, states INR 250 billion, railways INR 500 billion, alongside power T&D and renewables; guidance was trimmed to 10–15% due to a flat Q1 and supply chain uncertainties.
2. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial):** Inquired about debt levels and subsidiary debt treatment post-monetization. Management clarified third-party debt will drop to INR 500–600 crore, while subsidiary debt (~INR 500–600 crore) is internal to 100% subsidiaries and will not require third-party outflows.
3. **Bhavin Modi (Anand Rathi):** Asked about the strategy behind increasing international orders. Management noted they operate an independent international vertical across 7 countries (planning for up to 10) to capture diversified growth while domestic road awarding remains muted.
4. **Vasudev Ganatra (Nuvama):** Inquired about monetization status of Chennai ORR and Jaora-Nayagaon. Management stated Chennai ORR could happen by year-end and Jaora-Nayagaon by March end or H1 next year.
5. **Vishal Periwal (PL Capital):** Questioned the business model of the Gems and Jewellery Park JV. Management explained it is a 90-year lease development project (51% stake) expected to involve ~INR 1,000 crore total spend over 5 years, executed via long lease sales.

### One-line takeaway
Ashoka Buildcon delivered a flat Q1 standalone performance, prompting a conservative downgrade to its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 10-15%, while leveraging international wins, non-road diversification, and upcoming asset monetization to streamline its balance sheet.
