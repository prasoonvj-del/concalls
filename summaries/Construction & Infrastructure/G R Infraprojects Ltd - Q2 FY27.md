# G R Infraprojects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong standalone revenue growth of 64% YoY and a robust order pipeline, despite slight margin pressure from input costs and working capital expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 2,019 crore (+64% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,325 crore (+45% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 9.29% (vs 9.76% YoY).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 161 crore (vs Rs 131 crore YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 290 crore (vs Rs 189 crore YoY).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 24,577 crore.
*   **Standalone Debt-Equity Ratio:** 0.04x.

### Highlights
*   Management reported a 64% YoY increase in standalone revenue.
*   Received appointed date for the Agra-Gwalior DBFOT toll project; two projects worth Rs 3,562 crore are still awaiting appointed dates.
*   Working capital days increased to 152 days (from 128 days in FY26) due to higher debtor and inventory days.
*   Company plans to monetize 5-6 completed HAM projects (equity invested: Rs 1,250 crore) within the current fiscal year.
*   Trade receivables (standalone) stood at Rs 2,636 crore, including Rs 1,490 crore of HAM debtors.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to ~20% (previously 15%).
*   **Order Inflows:** Targeting Rs 20,000 crore in new order inflows for the year.
*   **Margins:** Management expects to maintain ~11% EBITDA margin if input costs stabilize; otherwise, expects to remain at ~10%.
*   **Equity Contribution:** Expects to contribute ~Rs 500 crore for HAM/BOT projects in H2 FY27, and ~Rs 2,500 crore in FY28.
*   **Capex:** Guidance of Rs 200-250 crore for FY27; ~Rs 200 crore for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management observes early signs of a broader investment cycle with increasing institutional participation.
*   Focus sectors: Integrated connectivity, energy transition, logistics, and warehousing.
*   **Transport:** MoRTH identified a PPP pipeline >Rs 8 lakh crore over the next three years.
*   **Hydro/Pumped Storage:** CEA estimates a requirement of 73.93 GW of storage capacity by 2031-32.
*   **Power Transmission:** Green Energy Corridor Phase-III approved with ~Rs 1.86 lakh crore outlay.
*   **Oil & Gas:** Samudra Manthan scheme (Rs 84,000 crore) launched to accelerate offshore exploration.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin was impacted by 1.5%-2% due to rising costs of diesel, fuel, and commodities driven by geopolitical tensions.
*   Government is compensating for bitumen price increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   H1 FY27 capex: Rs 83 crore.
*   Net block (PPE + CWIP + Intangibles): Rs 1,015 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Standalone debt: Rs 340 crore; Consolidated debt: Rs 5,938 crore.
*   Consolidated debt-equity ratio: 0.60x.
*   Oil & Gas receivables: ~Rs 740 crore.
*   Unbilled revenue: Rs 1,132 crore (standalone); Rs 618 crore (consolidated).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions impacting battery project (NTPC) and input costs.
*   Execution challenges in the BharatNet project due to Right of Way (RoW) issues.
*   Uncertainty regarding the actual pace of project awards despite a robust pipeline.
*   Working capital increase due to inventory buildup in power transmission projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Why did the order book decline sequentially despite new wins? **Management:** Clarified it reflects the net impact of scope changes, escalations, and execution, and that projects awaiting appointed dates are not yet fully reflected in the executable order book.
*   **Analyst:** What is the outlook for Oil & Gas turnover? **Management:** Noted seasonality and stated they target Rs 1,000 crore revenue for FY28.
*   **Analyst:** Why the 50-60 bps margin contraction? **Management:** Attributed it to a 2%-2.5% impact from rising construction and fuel costs.
*   **Analyst:** What is the strategy for Pumped Storage (PSP) projects? **Management:** Confirmed they are qualified (some via JV, some direct) and are targeting project sizes of ~Rs 3,000 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Strong top-line growth and a healthy order pipeline support a positive outlook, though margin pressure from input costs and working capital expansion remain key monitorables.
