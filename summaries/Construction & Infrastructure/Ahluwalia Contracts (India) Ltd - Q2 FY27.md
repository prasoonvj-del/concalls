# Ahluwalia Contracts (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Performance recovered sequentially post Q1 black swan events, but YoY profitability remains under pressure due to GRAP construction bans and statutory PF ceiling hikes.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth target at 15%+ and order inflow target at INR 4,000 crore. Capex guidance refined to ~INR 225 crore (vs INR 220–250 crore in Q1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from cautious to mixed; execution bounced back sequentially (revenue up 30.01% QoQ), though management remains conservative due to GRAP pollution bans in NCR during Q3.
- **New topics or risks:** Increase in EPF wage ceiling from INR 15,000 to INR 25,000 adding ~0.5% hit to margins; GRAP construction bans affecting NCR sites (except Central Vista and Max Hospital).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Central Vista on track (~INR 150 crore targeted in Q3 FY27 vs target of INR 700 crore for FY27); CST Railway station execution picked up (~INR 150 crore billing targeted in Q3 FY27); Gems & Jewellery Park remains delayed without notice to proceed.

---

### Key numbers
- **Turnover (Revenue):** INR 1,463.67 crore (+24.32% YoY, +30.01% QoQ)
- **PAT:** INR 55.86 crore (-29.33% YoY, +389.2% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.58% (vs 10.92% in Q2 FY26, 4.29% in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT Margin:** 3.78% (vs 6.63% in Q2 FY26, 1.0% in Q1 FY27)
- **EPS:** INR 8.34 (vs INR 11.80 in Q2 FY26, INR 1.70 in Q1 FY27)
- **Net Order Book:** INR 19,627.92 crore (to be executed over 3–3.5 years)
- **Order Inflow (till 30/09/2026):** INR 558.07 crore; post-Sept order won worth ~INR 350 crore (Bennett University); L1 position in 1 project worth INR 379.00 crore

---

### Highlights
- Execution rebounded strongly in Q2 as slow-moving North India/NCR projects started yielding revenue.
- Central Vista project (Delhi) and Max Hospital (Gurgaon) are exempt from GRAP construction bans and running at full speed.
- Regional order book diversification continues: Mumbai (24%), East (15%), Bangalore (~INR 2,000 crore order book), and growing presence in Assam and Hyderabad.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Maintained at 15%+ for FY27; projected at 15%–20% for FY28. Expect Q3 FY27 revenue to dip ~10% QoQ to ~INR 1,300 crore due to GRAP restrictions before picking up in Q4 FY27.
- **Margins:** EBITDA margin for H2 FY27 expected to be similar to Q2 FY27 (7.58%); targeted to expand by 100 bps to 8.5%–9.0% in FY28.
- **Order Intake:** Maintained at ~INR 4,000 crore for FY27 (~INR 1,000 crore achieved/L1 till date).
- **Project Billing Targets:** Central Vista to log ~INR 150 crore in Q3 FY27 and INR 200+ crore in Q4 FY27; CSMT Railway Station expected to bill ~INR 150 crore in Q3 FY27 and ~INR 200 crore in Q4 FY27.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- NCR construction is impacted by NGT/GRAP pollution bans in Q3; Q2 and Q4 are emerging as the strongest operational quarters annually due to recurring winter bans.
- Industry faces ongoing structural supply chain disruptions and skilled labor shortages, driving a gradual push toward standardization and off-site prefabrication/pre-engineered structures over a 2–3 year horizon.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Material costs were higher by 4–5 percentage points YoY due to contract execution mix (higher billing proportion of materials in early-stage EPC contracts like Central Vista and CSMT), which will balance out in later structural stages.
- Employee/staff costs increased to INR 133 crore due to salary increments, staff additions, and EPF wage ceiling revision (from INR 15,000 to INR 25,000), impacting margins by ~50 bps.
- Contracts mix: Fixed-price contracts represent 9.19% of the order book, EPC accounts for 46%, and the balance are item-rate contracts with price escalation clauses built in.
- Management has secured labor escalation agreements as per minimum wage indices from ~50% of clients where explicit clauses did not exist.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex stood at INR 142 crore; Q2 capex was INR 66 crore.
- Full-year FY27 capex guided at ~INR 225 crore (primarily spent on cranes, batching plants, loaders, and specialized aluminum/system shuttering). FY28 capex projected between INR 220 crore and INR 250 crore.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Mobilization Advances:** INR 939 crore (32% interest-bearing).
- **Retention Money:** INR 437 crore.
- **Unbilled Revenue:** INR 925 crore.
- **Depreciation:** Stood at INR 35 crore in Q2; guided at ~INR 35 crore/quarter for FY27 and INR 37–38 crore/quarter in FY28.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **GRAP Construction Ban:** 1 to 1.5 months expected to be lost in Q3 FY27 across non-exempt NCR sites due to winter pollution controls.
- **Statutory Wage Burden:** Hike in EPF wage ceiling adds ~50 bps cost pressure unless reimbursed by clients.
- **Supply Chain Delays:** Electromechanical items (chillers, panels, transformers) now require 5–8 weeks lead time vs 2–3 weeks earlier.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Love Gupta (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked why the company is not considering a share buyback given high treasury cash (~INR 1,000 crore) and low stock price.**  
  *Management answered:* The cash balance is being maintained as a war chest to control supply chain volatility, increase efficiency, and support project execution, though they noted the buyback suggestion.
- **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS) asked why EBITDA margins lagged smaller peers like B.L. Kashyap.**  
  *Management answered:* Scale-up costs, higher workforce headcount, JDC costs, and PF outlay increases (~INR 3 crore extra in Sept) impacted short-term margins, but the capacity build-up prepares the company for INR 5,500 crore turnover scale.
- **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital) queried the execution status of India Jewellery Park.**  
  *Management answered:* Notice to proceed has not yet been issued by the client due to internal permission issues and earthwork/dumping bans in Mumbai; no major turnover is expected in Q3 or Q4.
- **Ankit (Individual Investor) asked why Q3/Q4 margins won't be higher despite the Central Vista buffer.**  
  *Management answered:* Margin guidance remains conservative due to electromechanical price increases, a 0.5% margin hit from PF ceiling hikes, and ongoing geopolitical supply chain uncertainty.

---

### One-line takeaway
Ahluwalia Contracts logged a sequential operational recovery in Q2 FY27 with 30% QoQ top-line growth, while keeping FY27 growth guidance at 15%+ despite winter GRAP bans and labor/PF cost headwinds.
