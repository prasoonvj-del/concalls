# IRCON International Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited a challenging operational environment with sectoral headwinds, rising competition, and a dip in other income, though they remain optimistic about securing orders in the coming quarters.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Total Revenue: Rs 2,364 crore (Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated Operating Revenue: Rs 2,272 crore (Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 100 crore (Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated Core EBITDA: Rs 227 crore (Q2 FY27)
- EPS: Rs 1.09 per equity share (face value Rs 2 per share, unannualized, Q2 FY27)
- Order Book: Rs 21,296 crore as of September 30, 2026 (55% competitive, 45% nomination; 91% domestic, 9% international)
- Order Inflows: Close to Rs 1,000 crore secured (including post-September 30 updates; ~Rs 200 crore up to September 30, 2026)
- Bypassed/Quoted Tenders: Close to Rs 25,000 crore quoted, opening pending
- Total SPV/Subsidiary/JV Investments: Around Rs 3,100 crore invested, with another Rs 800 crore-Rs 900 crore expected to be put in assets under construction
- Working Capital Loan: Nominal amount of Rs 100 crore tested previously; currently primarily using own cash and bank balance
- Own Funds: In the range of Rs 800 crore-Rs 900 crore

### Highlights
- Order book stands at approximately 2x of annual revenue.
- Key projects driving faster execution are the Sivok project (West Bengal and Sikkim; pending order book Rs 2,000 crore-Rs 2,500 crore) and the T-2 package for the bullet train project (pending order book Rs 3,000 crore-Rs 3,500 crore).
- Sri Lanka project has been successfully completed, which involved a one-off tax liability of Rs 12 crore-Rs 13 crore during the quarter.
- Management has no official knowledge regarding any merger between RVNL and IRCON.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects standalone operating turnover for FY27 to be in the range of Rs 9,000 crore-Rs 9,500 crore (implying a marginal 5%-6% growth).
- Management aims to secure total order inflows of Rs 9,000 crore-Rs 10,000 crore for FY27 to maintain an order book around Rs 25,000 crore by the end of the financial year.
- Standalone PAT margin guidance maintained at 6%-7%; Consolidated core EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 8%-9%.
- Effective tax rate guidance for the full year maintained at 23%-24%.
- Strategic goal is to increase international orders from the current 9% up to 15% of the total order book.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operational environment remains challenging with sectoral headwinds and rising competition, particularly in railways.
- Ample opportunities exist in domestic railways and highways, as well as international markets like Africa (Algeria), Eastern Europe, and Sri Lanka.
- Coal connectivity projects (such as CERL) are facing teething issues, with breakeven expected about two years down the line.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other income declined due to the end of the concession period for the ISTPL PPP project (stopping dividend income), temporary timing differences in dividend generation from newly constructed HAM projects (expected to complete by financial year-end), deployment of surplus cash into equity for PPP projects reducing temporary interest income, and lower foreign exchange gains compared to last year.
- Average tax rate spiked in Q2 due to a one-off tax liability of Rs 12 crore-Rs 13 crore from the completed Sri Lanka project.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Around Rs 800 crore-Rs 900 crore remaining to be invested in SPVs/subsidiaries/JVs for assets currently under construction.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company holds Rs 800 crore-Rs 900 crore in own funds.
- Primarily funding projects and working capital needs through internal cash and bank balances rather than loans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile and sensitive macroeconomic environment globally creating uncertainties.
- Higher competition and sectoral headwinds in domestic markets.
- Initial losses and teething issues in coal connectivity joint venture projects (e.g., CERL).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gaurav (Individual Investor) on Order Inflow Math vs. Actuals:** Management clarified that while the order book increase up to September 30 showed ~Rs 200 crore, total secured orders reached close to Rs 1,000 crore when accounting for wins post-September 30.
2. **Vishal Periwal (PL Capital) on Higher Tax Rate:** Management explained the spike was due to a one-off Rs 12 crore-Rs 13 crore tax expense on the completed Sri Lanka project, guiding normalized annual tax rates to 23%-24%.
3. **Divesh Deria on JV/Subsidiary Losses:** Management stated that coal connectivity JVs (like CERL) are facing teething issues and will take about two years to reach breakeven, though losses are reducing.
4. **Manoj Shah (Laxco Investments) on RVNL-IRCON Merger Rumors:** Management explicitly stated they have no knowledge of any such merger. (Direct answer, did not dodge).
5. **Manoj Shah on Slowdown vs. FY23-FY24 Momentum:** Management adopted a conservative stance for FY27, noting that visibility for FY28 growth will become clearer in a few months (around February).

### One-line takeaway
IRCON is prioritizing execution and international expansion to offset domestic sector headwinds, guiding for flat-to-marginal revenue growth of Rs 9,000-9,500 crore for FY27 while targeting Rs 9,000-10,000 crore in new order inflows.
