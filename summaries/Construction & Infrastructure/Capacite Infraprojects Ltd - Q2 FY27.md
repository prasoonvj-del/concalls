# Capacite Infraprojects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly revenue and strong execution, despite seasonal challenges and commodity pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 771 crore, up 19% YoY (H1 FY27: Rs 1,400 crore, up 13% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 119 crore, up 10% YoY (H1 FY27: Rs 218 crore, up 4% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 15.5% (H1 FY27: 15.6%)
- **PAT:** Rs 48 crore compared to Rs 51 crore in Q2 FY26 (H1 FY26: Rs 51 crore; H1 FY27: Rs 87 crore vs Rs 98 crore in H1 FY26)
- **PAT Margin:** 6.1% (H1 FY27: 6.2%)
- **Order Book:** Rs 13,013 crore as of September 30, 2026 (54% public sector, 46% private sector)
- **Order Inflows (FY27 so far):** Rs 2,487 crore
- **Formwork Purchases (H1):** Rs 64.2 crore (Plant & machinery: Rs 10.57 crore; total formwork/machinery budgeted at Rs 135-140 crore)
- **Working Capital:** Reduced by 11 days compared to Q1 FY27

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 771 crore.
- Added Mahindra Lifespace Developers Limited as a new client.
- Reorganized business into four business units (BUs), each headed by a CEO.
- Total pledged shares for company limits reduced; only 50 lakh shares pledged with SBI (limit reduced from Rs 608 crore to Rs 140 crore); management targets complete removal by March 31, 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is fairly confident of improving profitability over the medium term.
- Confident of surpassing the FY27 order inflow target of Rs 4,500 crore.
- Reaffirmed FY27 revenue guidance and FY28 revenue target of Rs 4,000 crore.
- EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 15.5% to 16.5%; cash PAT margin guidance at 10.5% to 11.25%.
- Expects to reach a quarterly run rate of over Rs 300 crore by Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Active bid negotiations ongoing for Rs 3,270 crore.
- Construction constrained by elections, NGT issues, and weather (approx. 10 working months per year).
- Anticipates Rs 45-50 crore revenue loss in Delhi NCR due to NGT winter restrictions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Elevated commodity prices exerted margin pressure; steel prices hovered around Rs 62,000 per metric tonne, and aluminum formwork cost rose to Rs 14,500 per square metre.
- Margin impact largely mitigated through contractual escalation clauses; gap between market inflation and government price variation indices narrowed to ~2% in Q2.
- No provisions for commodity volatility taken in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Core fixed asset turnover ratio stood at 4.7x as of September 30, 2026.
- Budgeted procurement of Rs 135-140 crore for formwork and machinery expected to be completed in H2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total net contract assets/receivables (debtors, WIP, retention) stand at approximately Rs 2,400 crore on a net basis. Retention money steady at Rs 170 crore.
- Debt expected to remain around current levels or see a slight reduction by March 31, 2027, with a sizeable buildup in cash position.
- Recovered ~Rs 200 crore of old stuck monies historically; holds Rs 48 crore of properties for sale targeted for realization in H2 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in oil, commodity prices, and US dollar impacting costs.
- Delhi NCR NGT restrictions (expected complete suspension or stricter rules) posing potential execution risks for H2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rajesh Jain (Centrum/Investor) on contract assets and unbilled revenues:** Management clarified that net contract assets and debtors total around Rs 2,400 crore, representing ~77-78% of full-year revenue, and defended that peer metrics align closely.
- **Tanmay Jain (Dolat Capital) on aluminum prices and capex:** Management stated that higher aluminum prices impact cash flows rather than profitability since formwork is depreciated over 7 years, and confirmed H1 formwork purchases of Rs 64.2 crore.
- **Vasudev Ganatra (Nuvama) on project run-rates (MHADA, CIDCO, Signature Global):** Management noted CIDCO is running at ~Rs 30 crore/month and MHADA is scaling up, with meaningful revenue acceleration expected next year.
- **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital) on PAT growth vs revenue:** Management explained that last year's base was positively impacted by bad debt recovery; operating EBITDA guidance remains at 15.5%-16.5%.
- **Parikshit Gupta (Fair Value Capital) on promoter pledge:** Management confirmed that pledged shares have reduced to 50 lakh shares (linked to SBI working capital limits) and reiterated the target to remove the pledge by March 31, 2027.

### One-line takeaway
Capacit'e Infraprojects reported its highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 771 crore (up 19% YoY) in Q2 FY27, backed by a robust order book of Rs 13,013 crore and improving working capital efficiency, while maintaining its FY28 revenue target of Rs 4,000 crore.
