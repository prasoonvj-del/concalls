# Denta Water and Infra Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management maintains 20% growth guidance for FY27, citing a strong order book and a strategic focus on circular water economy projects despite H1 headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Order Book:** Rs 7824.44 million.
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1278.87 billion (as stated in transcript).
*   **Receivables (Sept 30, 2026):** 164.60 million.
*   **Receivables (Oct 31, 2026):** 57.62 million.
*   **Projects:** 550 completed; 32 under execution.

### Highlights
*   Secured two new irrigation projects in July 2026 (Rs 655 million) involving jack wells and 12-14 check dams.
*   Strategy focuses on a "circular water economy," utilizing sewage treatment plants (STPs) with CAMUS, NABR, and HAP technologies to treat sewage for groundwater recharging.
*   Management states the company is "virtually debt-free."
*   *Pattern Break:* Previous records indicated expansion into Gujarat, MP, and UP; this call identifies active bidding efforts in Odisha, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu instead.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains 20% revenue growth guidance for FY27, expecting H2 improvement due to favorable weather and normalized administrative processes.
*   Expects a 15-20% increase in the order pipeline in Q3/Q4 compared to H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Water infrastructure remains a critical long-term development priority.
*   Jal Jeevan Mission 1 projects are 75-80% complete; no work orders captured yet for Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0.
*   Sandur town water supply project tender is delayed due to government transition.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Management aims for a minimum 20% profit margin.
*   EBITDA decline in H1 FY27 attributed to project mix (initial phases of new projects requiring high inventory/procurement) and inflationary pressure on HDPE/PVC pipes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Capable of executing single projects up to Rs 300 crore on a standalone basis.
*   Actively bidding for projects in Odisha, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu; tenders in these regions have faced postponements.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Receivables reduced from 164.60 million (Sept 30) to 57.62 million (Oct 31).
*   Company maintains a "virtually debt-free" status.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Dependency on government infrastructure budgets (Jal Jeevan/AMRUT).
*   Raw material inflation (petrol costs impacting HDPE/PVC prices).
*   Geographic concentration in Karnataka.
*   Project delays due to government transitions and monsoon conditions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 impact?* A: No orders captured yet; will communicate once secured.
*   *Q: 20% growth guidance feasibility?* A: Maintained; H2 expected to improve after H1 monsoon/transition headwinds.
*   *Q: Sandur town project status?* A: Delayed due to government transition; hope to process in H2.
*   *Q: Why EBITDA decline?* A: High inventory costs for new projects in initial phases and raw material inflation.
*   *Q: Technology proprietary?* A: Partnering with technology providers (e.g., CAMUS) for long-term maintenance contracts.

### One-line takeaway
Management remains confident in achieving 20% FY27 growth through a circular water economy strategy, despite H1 margin pressure and geographic concentration risks.
