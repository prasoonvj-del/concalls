# Ahluwalia Contracts (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** 1QFY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Earnings impacted by black swan events, including a final dispute bill on AIIMS Jammu, regional SIR drives/elections, and a steep hike in NCR labor costs.

### Key numbers
- Turnover: INR 1,125.81 crores (up 12.03% YoY)
- PAT: INR 11.42 crores (down 77.65% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 4.29% (vs 8.59% in 1QFY26)
- PAT Margin: 1% (vs 5.01% in 1QFY26)
- EPS: INR 1.70 (vs INR 7.63 in 1QFY26)
- Net Order Book: INR 20,663.52 crores (to be executed over 3 to 3.5 years)
- Order Inflow (FY27 till 30/06/2026): INR 512.81 crores
- Trade Payables: INR 776 crores
- Trade Receivables: INR 632 crores
- Retention Money: INR 401 crores
- Inventory (including real estate): INR 391 crores
- Mobilization Advances: INR 924 crores
- Unbilled Revenue: INR 946 crores
- Gross Debt: INR 2.28 crores
- Cash and Bank Balances: INR 920 crores

### Highlights
- AIIMS Jammu final bill value reduced by INR 29 crores (a 2.6% negative impact on EBITDA) due to a dispute that will be taken to arbitration.
- West Bengal and Assam SIR drives and elections reduced turnover and increased IDC costs.
- Significant minimum wage increase of 35% to 40% for skilled and unskilled labor in NCR (which accounts for ~50% of the portfolio).
- Increased employee base and staff costs to mobilize resources for enlarged project portfolios (Central Vista, Dahlias, DLF Downtown).
- RML Hospital order (INR 500 crores) remains L1 but has not yet converted into a work order.
- Bhubaneswar University fixed-price bid validity extension was refused by management due to cost volatility.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management ruled out achieving double-digit EBITDA margins for the full financial year due to black swan events and labor pricing.
- Top-line growth guidance maintained at 12% to 15%, subject to potential NGT (National Green Tribunal) impacts in Q3.
- Order inflow guidance for the full year reduced from INR 8,000 crores to around INR 4,000 crores to INR 5,000 crores due to market volatility.
- Capex guidance for the full year reduced to INR 220 crores – INR 250 crores (with INR 60 crores done in Q1).
- Project-specific billing targets: Gems and Jewellery Park at INR 100 crores in FY27 and INR 450 crores in FY28; CST project at INR 400 crores – INR 450 crores in FY27 and INR 700 crores in FY28; Central Vista at INR 700 crores in FY27 and INR 1,000 crores in FY28 (with the entire project completing in FY29).

### Demand & industry commentary
- NCR region faces acute skilled labor shortages (carpenters, bar binders, masons) operating at 40% to 50% strength.
- Projects are increasingly complex with heightened safety requirements, pushing safety budgets from 1% to nearly 3% and project staff costs in excess of 5%.
- Large developers (Godrej, DLF, Signature Global) have started offering stage-wise timely completion incentives.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin fell to 4.29% driven by the AIIMS Jammu bill reduction (2.6% impact), higher NCR labor rates, and increased employee/staff costs.
- Fixed-price contracts represent 10.34% of the total order book.
- Material price increases for cement and steel in private contracts operate on a 30-day lag pass-through, while government contracts use quarterly WPI indices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q1 capex was reported at INR 60 crores; full-year capex revised to INR 220 crores – INR 250 crores.
- Focus on digitizing, improving efficiency, and investing in machinery to offset labor shortages.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stands low at INR 2.28 crores, with cash and bank balances at INR 920 crores.
- Working capital days represented 119 days; management expects it to ease by year-end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential stringent measures by NGT in Delhi and Haryana expected to impact Q3 execution and margins.
- Unpaid bills for 5 months on a Central University project in Himachal Pradesh due to cost overruns.
- Supply chain disruptions for switchgear and electrical panels, extending delivery times from 4-6 weeks to 3-4 months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital) asked about the timeline for returning to double-digit margins.** Management stated double-digit margins are ruled out for FY27 due to black swan events and labor hikes, and declined to give a specific date for Q3/Q4 due to potential NGT headwinds.
- **Sandip Sabharwal (asksandipsabharwal.com) questioned why the labor cost impact was not foreseen during the previous earnings call.** Management responded that April-June is traditionally a low labor supply quarter exacerbated by festival timing and unexpected government minimum wage hikes in NCR.
- **Vishal Periwal (PL Capital) asked if labor hikes qualify as a change of law (statutory increase).** Management clarified they are not statutory increases, but discussions are underway with NCR clients for compensation.
- **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked why share buybacks are not being considered despite a long-term low CAGR and surplus cash.** Management replied that surplus cash is needed as a war chest to grow the business, digitize, and invest in machinery amidst sector volatility.
- **Parikshit Kandpal (HDFC Securities) asked for the exact quantifiable EBITDA impact of the labor issue.** Management estimated a company-level impact of about 1.5% (or ~100 bps net of escalation clauses).

### One-line takeaway
Ahluwalia Contracts reported a steep 77% YoY drop in PAT and a historic low EBITDA margin of 4.29% for 1QFY27, weighed down by an AIIMS Jammu disputed bill write-down, unbudgeted NCR labor rate hikes, and regional disruptions, prompting management to moderate full-year inflow and margin expectations.
