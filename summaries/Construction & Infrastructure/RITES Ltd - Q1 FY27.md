# RITES Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in the roadmap, citing YoY growth in revenue (9-10%) and bottom line (8%), while emphasizing execution focus for the remainder of the year.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue growth:** 9-10% YoY.
*   **PAT growth:** 8% YoY.
*   **Order book:** Rs 9,450 crore.
*   **RITES Videsh order book:** Rs 2,100 crore.
*   **New orders won (Q1):** 128 orders worth Rs 670 crore.
*   **Turnkey projects:** Contributed 30-33% of Q1 revenue.
*   **Employee count:** 3,125 (up from 2,675).
*   **REMCL Q1 profit:** Rs 22 crore.
*   **REMCL dividend received:** Rs 10 crore.
*   **QA revenue:** ~Rs 70 crore.

### Highlights
*   Management maintains a "strike rate" of ~1.4 orders per day.
*   70% of fresh orders are won on a competitive basis.
*   Export revenue recognition for the first Bangladesh rake (20 coaches) is expected in Q2 due to final dispatch logistics.
*   Employee strength increased by ~450 to support execution.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management aims for substantial YoY growth for the full FY.
*   Export revenue target for FY27 is at least Rs 300 crore.
*   Management expects export orders to account for ~15% of total revenue this year.
*   Management aims to maintain a "red line" of 20% EBITDA margin and 15% PAT margin on a consolidated basis.
*   Employee cost increase for FY28 is projected in the range of 8-10%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management sees strong demand, winning orders at a rate of one per day.
*   Export pipeline includes locomotives, coaches, and DMUs.
*   Vande Bharat export: Management has initiated discussions with Indian Railways to develop a prototype on a standard gauge platform for international markets.
*   Turnkey projects: Clients prefer this model for single-point contact, though margins are lower (1.5-2%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin held at 22% in Q1; PAT margin held at 17%.
*   Margins are under pressure due to competitive bidding (70% of orders), travel costs, and impending pay revisions.
*   Employee costs increased by ~Rs 10 crore YoY in Q1.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Management described the business as having a "low CapEx" model.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Company is debt-free.
*   Management does not foresee any major shift in dividend payout policy (historically 90%+).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Competitive bidding pressure on margins.
*   Rising employee costs (hiring + impending pay revision).
*   Travel cost pressures.
*   Execution delays (e.g., first Bangladesh rake delayed from Q1 to Q2).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Export Revenue Timing:** Management clarified that Bangladesh rake revenue was delayed to Q2 due to final approval and dispatch logistics.
*   **Turnkey Margins:** Management explained that Turnkey projects have lower margins (1.5-2%) because the entire project value flows through the P&L, unlike consultancy fees.
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Management reiterated the "red line" of 20% EBITDA margin despite competitive pressures.
*   **Employee Cost Outlook:** Management clarified that the ~20% increase in employee cost is not expected; rather, 8-10% is the projected range for FY28.

### One-line takeaway
RITES is focused on accelerating execution to meet full-year growth targets while maintaining strict margin "red lines" amidst a competitive order environment.
