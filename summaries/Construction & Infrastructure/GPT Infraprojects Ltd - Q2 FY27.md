# GPT Infraprojects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - H1 execution was temporarily impacted by assembly elections and extended monsoons, but management expects a strong H2 backed by a robust order book.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 291 crore (+5% YoY vs Rs 279 crore).
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 275 crore (+2% YoY vs Rs 269 crore).
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 594 crore (vs Rs 591 crore).
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 41 crore (+4.5% YoY); Margin: 14.2%.
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 44 crore (+17.7% YoY); Margin: 16% (+215 bps YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2):** Rs 18 crore; Margin: 6.3%.
- **Standalone PAT (Q2):** Rs 20 crore; Margin: 7.3%.
- **Outstanding Order Book:** ~Rs 5,373 crore (4.3x FY26 revenues).
- **H1 Cash Flow from Operations:** ~Rs 54 crore (vs Rs 12 crore in H1 FY26).

### Highlights
- Execution witnessed temporary moderation in H1 due to West Bengal assembly elections (April-May) and an extended monsoon, but workforce availability has now normalized.
- Secured a railway infrastructure order of Rs 484 crore from RVNL for a 544-meter long open web steel girder bridge over Mahanadi in Odisha.
- Subsidiary Alcon Builders & Engineers secured multiple signaling contracts aggregating ~Rs 428 crore, taking Alcon's order book to ~Rs 480 crore.
- Subsidiary GPT Concrete Products South Africa secured a 5-year contract valued at ~Rs 385 crore from Transnet Freight Rail.
- Board proposed an interim dividend of Rs 1 per share (10%).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year FY27 revenue growth of 25% to 27% (crossing Rs 1,600 crore, requiring ~Rs 1,000 crore in H2).
- Consolidated EBITDA margins expected to remain in the range of 14% to 15%.
- Targeting minimum Rs 3,000 crore in order inflows for FY27 (secured ~Rs 1,490 crore in H1).
- Africa operations expected to contribute ~Rs 80 crore in FY27 revenue.
- Alcon revenue expected to be ~Rs 150 crore in FY27 and over Rs 200 crore next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Continued support from public sector investments and favorable infrastructure spending environment.
- Indian railways signaling market sized at ~$1.5 billion within a planned railway outlay of ~$1 trillion over the next 6 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin expanded by 215 bps YoY to 16% in Q2, aided by the high-margin signaling business and Africa operations.
- H1 profitability includes a non-cash goodwill amortization charge of Rs 8 crore related to the Alcon acquisition.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Contract assets stand at ~Rs 600 crore (increase of ~Rs 45-55 crore from March due to new projects like Chambal, Kashi Ganga, and Rapti where initial billing is pending); ~Rs 10 crore is older than one year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange currency fluctuations and geopolitical developments in international operations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ritesh Bhagwati (Alpha Plus Capital)** asked about H2 revenue requirements to meet earlier guidance; management stated full-year growth is revised to 25%-27%, requiring ~Rs 1,000 crore in H2.
- **Ritesh Bhagwati** inquired about contract asset aging; management explained that ~Rs 45 crore increase stems from new unbilled contracts, with only ~Rs 10 crore older than a year.
- **Santosh Shetty** asked about order inflow targets; management confirmed ~Rs 1,490 crore secured out of the target of minimum Rs 3,000 crore for FY27.
- **Pahal Sharma** asked about L1 project status; management stated there are currently no L1 projects to announce.
- **Soumya Raghuvanshi** asked about Alcon's financial contribution; management expects ~Rs 150 crore revenue and ~Rs 30 crore EBITDA (~20% margin) for FY27.

### One-line takeaway
GPT Infraprojects reported subdued H1 execution due to elections and monsoons, but guides for ~25-27% full-year growth backed by a record order book of Rs 5,373 crore and scaling high-margin segments.
