# GPT Infraprojects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in doubling execution in H2 FY27 driven by a record order book, recovery in project momentum, and addition of high-margin signaling and international orders.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Q2 FY27:** Revenue Rs 275 crore (+2% YoY); EBITDA Rs 44 crore (+17.7% YoY); EBITDA margin 16% (+215 bps YoY); PAT Rs 20 crore (PAT margin 7.3%).
- **Consolidated Q2 FY27:** Revenue Rs 291 crore (+4.6% YoY); EBITDA Rs 41 crore (+4.5% YoY); EBITDA margin 14.2%; PAT Rs 18 crore (PAT margin 6.3%).
- **Standalone H1 FY27:** Revenue Rs 557 crore; EBITDA Rs 83 crore (+13.4% YoY), EBITDA margin 14.9% (+227 bps YoY); PAT Rs 42 crore (PAT margin 7.6%).
- **Consolidated H1 FY27:** Revenue Rs 594 crore vs Rs 591 crore YoY; EBITDA Rs 89 crore (+16.1% YoY), EBITDA margin 15% (+203 bps YoY); PAT Rs 43 crore (PAT margin 7.2%).
- **Segment Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Infrastructure Rs 262 crore (~90% of total); Concrete Sleepers Rs 30 crore (~10% of total).
- **Segment EBIT (Q2 FY27):** Infrastructure Rs 33 crore (~92% of overall EBIT); Concrete Sleepers Rs 2.5 crore.
- **Cash Flow from Operations (H1 FY27):** Consolidated net cash flow from operations surged ~5x YoY to ~Rs 54 crore (vs Rs 12 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Dividend:** Board declared an interim dividend of Rs 1 per share (10%).

### Highlights
- Execution in H1 FY27 was temporarily moderated by West Bengal assembly elections (April–May) and extended monsoons; execution normalized and billing momentum restarted in October.
- Outstanding order book reached ~Rs 5,373 crore (~4.3x FY26 revenue), comprising Infrastructure at Rs 4,521 crore and Concrete Sleepers at a record Rs 852 crore.
- Key order wins included a Rs 484 crore EPC bridge over Mahanadi from RVNL, Rs 428 crore in signaling contracts via subsidiary Alcon, and a 5-year ~Rs 385 crore contract from Transnet Freight Rail in South Africa.
- H1 FY27 reported profitability included a non-cash goodwill amortization charge of Rs 8 crore related to the Alcon acquisition.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-Year FY27 Revenue:** Expected to cross Rs 1,600+ crore (25%–27% full-year YoY growth), requiring Rs 1,000+ crore revenue in H2 FY27 (~Rs 160 crore monthly run-rate).
- **Order Inflows:** Target of minimum Rs 3,000 crore for FY27 (secured ~Rs 1,450–1,490 crore till date; expecting minimum Rs 2,000 crore in H2).
- **EBITDA Margins:** Guided at 14%–15% on a consolidated basis for FY27.
- **Subsidiaries & Segments:** Alcon revenue guided at ~Rs 150 crore for FY27 (EBITDA margin ~20%, EBITDA ~Rs 30 crore) and Rs 200+ crore in FY28. Africa operations guided to deliver ~Rs 80 crore revenue in FY27 (South Africa contributing ~Rs 70–75 crore annually). Domestic sleeper business expected to grow ~15% over the next 2 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins expanded YoY due to a higher-margin product mix, operating leverage, and early contributions from signaling and African operations.
- Management noted that international and domestic contracts contain price escalation clauses, mitigating raw material cost risks.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Executing sleeper export orders for Bangladesh and local domestic orders out of the Panagarh facility.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Contract assets stood at ~Rs 543 crore to ~Rs 600 crore, up ~Rs 45 crore from March 2026, driven by upfront mobilization spend on new projects (Chambal, Kashi Ganga Bridge, Rapti) prior to billing milestones.
- Out of total contract assets, only ~Rs 10 crore is aged over one year; retention money accounts for ~Rs 100 crore.
- Working capital efficiency and collection of older receivables led to operating cash flow improving to ~Rs 54 crore in H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange volatility and currency fluctuations in international geographies.
- Geopolitical developments and political/government changes in overseas markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q on H2 FY27 growth feasibility (requiring ~Rs 1,100 cr in H2):** Management expressed high confidence in achieving Rs 1,000+ crore in H2 (~30%–40% growth in H2) to hit 25%–27% full-year growth, citing monsoon clearance, accelerated execution, and a Rs 5,373 crore order book.
- **Q on rising contract assets (~Rs 600 cr):** Management clarified that ~Rs 40–45 crore of the H1 increase came from initial expenses incurred on newly awarded contracts (Chambal, Kashi Ganga Bridge, Rapti) before the first billing milestones, and only ~Rs 10 crore is over one year old.
- **Q on Alcon's execution and financial expectations:** Management updated Alcon’s FY27 revenue projection upwards to ~Rs 150 crore (EBITDA ~Rs 30 crore at ~20% margin) and Rs 200+ crore in FY28, supported by its highest-ever order book of ~Rs 480 crore.
- **Q on execution of international sleeper contracts:** Management indicated the Rs 385 crore South Africa contract spans 5 years (~Rs 70–75 crore revenue per year), with additional contributions starting from Ghana and Ivory Coast.

### One-line takeaway
GPT Infraprojects is poised for strong H2 execution and margin expansion driven by a robust Rs 5,373 crore order book, recovery from monsoon delays, and scaling of high-margin signaling and South African operations.
