# SPML Infra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth accompanied by positive operating cash flows and disciplined project selection.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained >25% YoY revenue and profit growth, though management noted potential to overperform if H2 trends continue.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positively regarding JJM as government fund releases and old dues clearance accelerated, and JJM 2.0 brings full funding clarity.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: None significant; focus remained on BESS scale-up, price variation clauses, and arbitration realization.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: NTPC BESS design and drawing approvals achieved; revenue contribution on track for H2 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 238.7 crore (+20% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 26.3 crore (+33% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.0% (vs 10.0% YoY, +100 bps)
- **PAT:** Rs 22.2 crore (+46% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 9.3%
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 505 crore (+45% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 55 crore (+33% YoY)
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 45 crore (+64% YoY)
- **Order Book:** ~Rs 5,056 crore (includes ~Rs 1,161 crore legacy orders)
- **Net Worth:** Rs 1,185 crore as of September 2026
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Rs 82.4 crore (vs outflow of Rs 44.1 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs 119 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 63 crore as of March)
- **Debt Reduction:** Repaid Rs 330 crore out of ~Rs 700 crore legacy obligations
- **Arbitration Claims:** ~Rs 3,827 crore total claims; ~Rs 655 crore backed awards with accumulating interest

### Highlights
- Secured a Rs 186 crore contract from Power Grid Corporation of India for 765 kV substation extension (Bhadla-III and Ramgarh), marking entry into the extra high-voltage transmission segment.
- Received design and drawing approvals for the NTPC Barauni BESS project (~Rs 1,128 crore); revenue expected to start flowing in H2 FY27 (~Rs 300 crore).
- Proprietary 104.4 kWh battery packs cleared key international certifications and testing.
- Recognized at the Construction World Global Awards 2026 as the Fastest Growing Construction Company in the Prime segment.
- Two high-value arbitration matters of around Rs 100 crore are at a very advanced stage.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue & Profit:** Maintained guidance of at least 25% growth in top line and bottom line for FY27; management expects H2 to outperform H1 due to monsoon normalization.
- **Order Inflow:** On track with guidance of Rs 5,000 crore for FY27.
- **BESS Revenue:** Expecting around Rs 300 crore of revenue from the NTPC project in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Water sector saw a consolidation phase in H1, but tenders are picking up with upcoming projects like Parbati-Kalisindh-Chambal (PKC) link, Ken-Betwa, Wainganga-Nalganga, and NVDA.
- Government accelerating pending JJM payments and launching JJM 2.0 with full funding clarity.
- Power transmission (substations) and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) seeing robust multiyear investment cycles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management maintains strict discipline of only bidding for contracts with Price Variation (PV) clauses to insulate against commodity price and forex fluctuations.
- Legacy orders (backed by back-to-back subcontracting) carry lower margins but involve no negative cash flow impact.
- New orders carry expected operating margins of 10% or higher.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- BESS manufacturing facility in Pune operational with Phase 1; working towards 5 GWh capacity by H1 FY28.
- Work started on the BESS container manufacturing facility (600 container capacity), expected to complete in H1 of next financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow turned positive at Rs 82.4 crore in H1 FY27.
- Remaining legacy obligation of ~Rs 370 crore is fully backed by arbitration awards of ~Rs 655 crore with accumulating interest.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Tendering activity in the water sector experienced unevenness and consolidation during H1.
- Monsoon months seasonally constrain site activity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arpan Gandhi (AJ Capital) on BESS orders:** Management stated they are actively bidding on PSU/non-PSU tenders and expect one more conversion in FY27, alongside focusing on OEM battery pack orders with shorter turnaround times.
- **Harshal Sharma (ACE Advisors) on guidance:** Management noted that if current H2 trends persist, they may overperform the 25% guidance, but kept it unchanged for now.
- **Harshal Sharma on commodity prices:** Management reiterated that Price Variation clauses in water, power, and BESS contracts protect margins from cost escalation.
- **Shalini (Individual Investor) on legacy orders:** Management clarified that legacy orders will be mostly completed by this year and next year with zero negative impact or losses due to back-to-back subcontracting.
- **Satyam Agarwal (Shradha Projects) on BESS pipeline:** Management stated they have participated in over Rs 8,000 crores of BESS tenders, targeting orders ranging from Rs 500 crore to Rs 1,000 crore.

### One-line takeaway
SPML Infra delivered robust H1 FY27 performance marked by 45% revenue growth, positive operating cash flows, disciplined PV-backed bidding, and scaling of its BESS manufacturing platform.
