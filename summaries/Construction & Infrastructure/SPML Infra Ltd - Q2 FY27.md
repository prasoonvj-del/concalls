# SPML Infra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent YoY operational growth, healthy operating cash generation, and disciplined bidding in high-margin water, transmission, and BESS opportunities.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained annual guidance of at least 25% growth in top line and bottom line; management indicated potential to overperform in H2 FY27. Annual order inflow guidance maintained at Rs 5,000 crore.
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive; demand outlook expanded to highlight the 765 kV extra high-voltage transmission segment following a new Power Grid win, alongside a pickup in water tenders post-monsoon.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Water segment experienced temporary consolidation/softness during H1 due to monsoons. Direct discussion of bank surety bond lines was absent this quarter.
- Last quarter's promises: NTPC BESS design and drawing approvals (promised by December 2026) were achieved ahead of schedule in Q2 FY27. International testing and certification of the proprietary 104.4 kWh battery pack were completed.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 238.7 crore (+20% YoY, -16.5% QoQ). H1 FY27 at Rs 505 crore (+45% YoY).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 26.3 crore (+33% YoY, -6.1% QoQ); EBITDA margin at 11% (up ~100 bps YoY from 10%). H1 FY27 at Rs 55 crore (+33% YoY).
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 22.2 crore (+46% YoY, -2.2% QoQ); PAT margin at 9.3%. H1 FY27 at Rs 45 crore (+64% YoY).
- **Order Book:** ~Rs 5,056 crore (includes SPML's share of JVs). Legacy projects account for ~Rs 1,161 crore; balance carries expected operating margins $\ge$10%.
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Positive Rs 82.4 crore (vs outflow of Rs 44.1 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs 119 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up 88% from Rs 63 crore at end-March 2026).
- **Net Worth:** Rs 1,185 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
- Secured an order worth Rs 186 crore from Power Grid Corporation of India for 765 kV substation extensions (Bhadla-III and Ramgarh), marking entry into the 765 kV EHV segment.
- Received design and drawing approvals for the Rs 1,128 crore NTPC Barauni BESS project; revenue generation to begin in H2 FY27.
- Proprietary 104.4 kWh battery pack cleared key international certifications and testing.

### Guidance & outlook
- Maintained at least 25% growth guidance for both top line and bottom line for FY27, with higher contributions expected in H2 than H1 due to post-monsoon site activity.
- Targeting cumulative order inflows of Rs 5,000 crore for FY27; currently participating in over Rs 8,000 crore of BESS tenders and expects to convert at least one more BESS contract in FY27 (value ranging Rs 500–1,000 crore).
- NTPC BESS project expected to contribute ~Rs 300 crore in revenue during FY27.
- Targets 25% annual top-line and bottom-line growth over the next 3–4 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Tracked pipeline across water schemes: Jal Jeevan Mission, AMRUT 2.0, Namami Gange, and river linking, including Ken-Betwa (Rs 44,605 crore) and PKC-ERCP (Rs 72,000 crore).
- Upcoming water project pipeline includes tenders from Wainganga-Nalganga and NVDA by Q4 FY27.
- Transmission capex under the National Electricity Plan envisages Rs 9.15 lakh crore by 2032; Green Energy Corridor Phase 3 approved at Rs 1.36 lakh crore.
- Management noted that the central government has been clearing pending JJM contractor dues across states like Maharashtra and UP, preparing the ground for JJM 2.0 awards with clear funding.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management maintains a strict bidding rule requiring a Price Variation (PV) clause on all contracts across water, power, and BESS to fully hedge commodity and forex volatility; fixed-price bids are avoided.
- Order selection enforces a hurdle of at least 10% operating margin.
- High-margin O&M typically accounts for ~5% of an EPC contract's total scope and carries superior profitability relative to EPC execution.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- BESS Phase 1 is operational; working toward 5 GWh capacity and a 600-container manufacturing facility in Pune targeted for completion in H1 FY28.
- The 25-acre Pune site can scale up to 10–20 GWh depending on long-term demand.
- Container plant will supply part of the containers for the NTPC order and 100% of containers for subsequent BESS orders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Of the legacy obligations of ~Rs 700 crore, Rs 330 crore has been repaid; remaining Rs 370 crore is fully backed by arbitration awards of ~Rs 655 crore (with accumulating interest).
- Total outstanding arbitration claims stand at ~Rs 3,827 crore.
- Two high-value arbitration matters totaling ~Rs 100 crore are at an advanced stage, with settlement proceeds expected soon to reduce debt.
- Net worth rose to Rs 1,185 crore, aided by profit accretion and warrant conversion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slower tendering and execution in the water segment during the monsoon period.
- Government expenditure constraints in the Jal Ministry, though management views core water infrastructure priorities as non-deferrable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **BESS Order Momentum & Moat:** Management stated they are selectively bidding for PSU and non-PSU projects with price escalation clauses and expect to win another Rs 500–1,000 crore order in FY27. Moat stems from proprietary certified pack design, Pune manufacturing, and system integration via Energy Vault, alongside supplying as an OEM to other EPC contractors.
- **Legacy Order Execution & Liabilities:** The Rs 1,161 crore legacy order book is largely subcontracted on a back-to-back basis with negligible working capital needs and protected margins. Management clarified there is no residual performance guarantee (BG) or liquidated damages (LD) risk exposure even if subcontractors fail.
- **Water Segment Inflows Shortfall:** Inflow softness in H1 was attributed to monsoon seasonality and deliberate bidding discipline; tendering is expected to rebound in H2 FY27 with major river link and canal tenders.
- **Commercial & Industrial (C&I) BESS Presence:** Management confirmed the Pune facility has technical capability for C&I/building BESS installations, but currently chooses not to participate because project lot sizes are too small compared to utility-scale projects.

### One-line takeaway
SPML Infra is driving profitable growth and balance sheet clean-up by enforcing price-variation clauses, monetizing arbitration awards, and expanding its Pune-based BESS manufacturing infrastructure.
