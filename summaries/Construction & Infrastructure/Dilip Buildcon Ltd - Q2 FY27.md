# Dilip Buildcon Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Signed definitive agreements for major asset monetization deals, entered the oil & gas sector, and reiterated full-year revenue and debt-reduction guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (FY27 standalone revenue growth of 30%-40%, EBITDA margin of 10%-10.5%, fresh order inflows of Rs 10,000-12,000 crore, standalone net debt reduction of Rs 600-800 crore, standalone net-debt free by FY28, capex flat or < Rs 100 crore).
- Tone and demand view: Remains confident in execution ramp-up starting Q3 as recently awarded projects clear mobilization; road sector remains quiet with aggressive competition in small packages, but opportunities in non-road sectors (transmission, solar, water, oil & gas) are expanding.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Signed definitive agreements with Alpha Alternatives for solar and transmission projects within six weeks of board approval; entered the oil & gas infrastructure segment with an LPG pipeline project; rail evacuation constraints at Siarmal coal mine were largely resolved; Wayanad tunnel discussion was absent.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Executed definitive transaction agreements with Alpha Alternatives; delivered Rs 7,589 crore fresh inflows in H1 FY27 (achieving ~90% of full-year inflow guidance including post-Q2 additions).

- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,686 crore (+18.98% YoY vs Rs 1,417 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 175 crore (Margin: 10.4% vs 10.73% in Q2 FY26)
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 55 crore (+34.15% YoY vs Rs 41 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Standalone H1 FY27:** Revenue Rs 3,615 crore (+5.5% YoY); EBITDA Rs 374 crore (Margin: 10.3%); PAT Rs 91 crore

- **Order Book:** Rs 33,081 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Roads: 15.6%)

- **Standalone Net Debt:** Rs 2,146 crore (Net debt-to-equity: 0.31x)

### Highlights
- Signed definitive agreements with Alpha Alternatives for the Mekhali transmission project (Enterprise Value ~Rs 2,914 crore vs project cost ~Rs 2,171 crore) and a 1,363 MW solar portfolio (EV ~Rs 6,829 crore vs project cost ~Rs 6,263 crore).
- Transaction structure: Alpha co-invests 49% equity during construction (~Rs 820 crore out of ~Rs 1,680 crore total equity), while DBL invests ~Rs 850 crore. Alpha acquires DBL's remaining 51% stake post-commissioning, yielding ~Rs 1,900 crore total return (cash and units) to DBL over 2-3 years. DBL retains EPC contracts worth >Rs 7,300 crore.
- Expanded into oil & gas infrastructure by securing an LPG pipeline project.
- Mining MDO order book snapshot at ~Rs 3,998 crore (3-year rolling snapshot) and total balance contract value at ~Rs 98,618 crore.
- InvIT units held stood at Rs 1,578 crore (across Anantam Highways Trust and Shrem InvIT) as of September 30, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Reiterated FY27 standalone revenue growth guidance of 30%-40% YoY, supported by acceleration starting Q3 FY27.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10%-10.5% for FY27.
- **Order Inflow:** Maintained full-year order inflow guidance of Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 12,000 crore. Bids worth Rs 23,000 crore are submitted and pending opening; total bid pipeline stands at ~Rs 3.30 lakh crore.
- **Debt Reduction:** Reiterated target to reduce standalone net debt by Rs 600 crore to Rs 800 crore in FY27, accelerating from Q3. Maintained objective to be standalone net debt-free by FY28.
- **Mining Operations:** FY27 coal production targets maintained at ~27 million tonnes for Siarmal and 7 million tonnes for Pachhwara (total 34 million tonnes). Long-term target of 57 million tonnes annual production by FY29 generating ~Rs 1,000 crore operating cash flow.
- **InvIT Platform:** Expects total road InvIT unit holdings to reach Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 3,200 crore with annual distributions of Rs 350 crore once all remaining HAM transfers complete.
- **FY29 Cash Flow Profile:** Management projects ~75% of cash flows by FY29 to come from long-duration assets (mining cash flows of ~Rs 1,000 crore + InvIT distributions of Rs 300-400 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 execution was seasonally soft due to monsoons.
- NHAI order pipeline remains active with >Rs 1 lakh crore floated, but bidding in small EPC road packages remains aggressive. DBL is maintaining strict return criteria and accepting a lower road share in its order book.
- Strong growth opportunities observed in non-road infrastructure sectors including transmission, renewable energy, railways, water, and oil & gas.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- West Asia geopolitical tensions keep crude oil, diesel, and bitumen prices elevated.
- Fuel cost increases are passed through via price adjustment mechanisms with a lag.
- Mining SPVs generated Rs 795 crore revenue in H1 FY27 at an EBITDA margin of 25%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Standalone capex guided to remain flat or below Rs 100 crore for FY27, limited to replacement needs.
- Siarmal Coal Handling Plant (CHP) commissioning targeted by March 2028, which will unlock full 100% mining fee entitlements (up from 78% currently).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone net debt stood at Rs 2,146 crore (Net debt-to-equity: 0.31x); consolidated net debt stood at Rs 8,950 crore.
- Working capital days expected to improve in H2 to close FY27 at ~120 days.
- DBL's gross fixed asset block stands at ~Rs 3,600 crore (Net block ~Rs 800 crore, largely debt-free).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated crude oil and bitumen price volatility impacting overall supply chain costs.
- Pending Appointed Dates and LOAs for newly won orders (e.g., LPG pipeline requires 6-8 months for land acquisition and approvals).
- Pachhwara dispatches in H1 were lower (1.98 million tonnes coal) due to a strike at a power plant in Punjab, though dispatches resumed in September.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Deepak Purswani (Svan Investments):** Asked how H2 will achieve the 55%-60% execution pace needed to hit full-year growth guidance.  
  *Management answered:* Historically, H2 accounts for 60% of industry revenues; residual legacy projects will finish while newly won solar, transmission, and Gurgaon Metro projects clear mobilization and ramp up execution from Q3.
- **Deepak Purswani (Svan Investments):** Asked about the financial structure and cash returns from the Alpha Alternatives transaction.  
  *Management answered:* DBL will invest ~Rs 850 crore equity and receive back ~Rs 1,900 crore over ~3 years in cash and units, ensuring all invested cash capital is returned to DBL's balance sheet without leaving a cash drain.
- **Shravan Shah:** Asked about pending Appointed Dates (AD) and execution timelines across the order book.  
  *Management answered:* ~Rs 7,000-8,000 crore worth of projects (including transmission, road HAM, and LPG) are awaiting AD/LOA. Power transmission and road projects will start execution in Q3/Q4, while the LPG pipeline will take 6-8 months due to land acquisition requirements.
- **Vishal Periwal:** Clarified how mining cash flows grow from ~Rs 400 crore in FY27 to ~Rs 1,000 crore by FY29.  
  *Management answered:* Volume increases from 34 million tonnes to 57 million tonnes (+60%), combined with the March 2028 commissioning of the Siarmal CHP, which adds a 22% fee entitlement increase (moving from 78% to 100% payout) with minimal additional operating cost.

### One-line takeaway
Dilip Buildcon is successfully transitioning to "DBL 2.0" by monetization of solar/transmission assets, growing its mining cash flows, and maintaining full-year growth and net debt reduction targets.
