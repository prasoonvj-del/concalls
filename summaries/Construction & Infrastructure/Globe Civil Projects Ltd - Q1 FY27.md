# Globe Civil Projects Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-27 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited steady progress across ongoing projects, robust revenue and PAT growth, and a supportive demand environment for EPC companies.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated total income: Rs 929.23 million (Q1 FY27) vs Rs 676.98 million (Q1 FY26), up 37.26% YoY.
- EBITDA: Rs 15.80 crores vs Rs 11.88 crores in Q1 FY26, up 33.03% YoY.
- EBITDA margin: 17.01%.
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 7.09 crores vs Rs 5.05 crores in Q1 FY26, up 40.42% YoY.
- Net profit margin: 7.63%.
- Current order book: Around Rs 700 crores (specifically mentioned as around Rs 730 crores in FY26 / ~Rs 700 crores currently).
- Target order book milestones: Rs 1,000 to Rs 1,200 crores within the next three quarters; Rs 1,500 crores by the end of FY27.
- Revenue execution guidance for FY27: 10% to 15% growth from existing projects, with expected execution of Rs 300 crores to Rs 350 crores.
- Top 5 projects contribution: Around 70% of turnover.
- Key project values mentioned: Jhajjar International Cricket Stadium (Rs 222.2 crores), Central University of Punjab project (Rs 172.99 crores).

### Highlights
- Delivered strong top-line and bottom-line growth driven by post-IPO project wins (Central University at Bathinda, Haryana Cricket Association, and Kanpur).
- Completed 3 projects in March 2026 (including an NIT project and another in the same campus).
- Diversified project presence across education, health care, housing, sports, commercial, and transportation infrastructure, working with clients like CPWD, NBCC, IITs, and DPS.
- Handled 11 to 12 projects simultaneously during the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain current EBITDA margins around 17%, with potential for improvement as larger projects scale.
- Targeting 10% to 15% revenue growth over last year from existing projects, alongside additional revenues from upcoming wins.
- Aiming to reach an order book of Rs 1,500 crores by the end of the financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Government-led capital expenditure in roads, urban infrastructure, and public works continues to provide a supportive demand environment for EPC companies.
- Anticipates a large wave of sports infrastructure and central government projects, supported by a new master plan in Delhi.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Maintained a selective bidding strategy focusing on central government and restricted-eligibility tenders to secure better pricing and higher EBITDA margins.
- Inventory increased due to advance procurement of materials (such as tiles and metals) in February and March to hedge against war-induced price hikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company is eligible for normal projects up to Rs 500 crores to Rs 650 crores independently, and larger projects via joint ventures.
- Current setup can easily support a turnover of Rs 500 crores to Rs 600 crores without increasing fixed costs.
- Expanded geographical bidding footprint into new states like Tamil Nadu and Bihar.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables and inventory increased due to final and pre-final billings for completed projects (like NBCC Aligarh and TCIL) taking time to reconcile and realize, and advance inventory procurement.
- Normal running sites take 30 to 40 days for receivables realization.
- Mobilization advances (up to 10% against bank guarantees) are available, but management chose to use internal funds as they are sufficient.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Initial project startup delays can occur due to pending statutory approvals and permissions (e.g., Haryana Cricket Association stadium).
- Higher trade receivable days attributed to pending final/pre-final bill reconciliations on completed projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Riya Sharma (Individual Investor) on revenue growth drivers:** Management explained that post-IPO wins (Bathinda, Haryana Cricket Association, Kanpur) reached full execution flow, though the cricket stadium faced initial approval delays.
2. **Purvesh Mehta (PM Consultancy) on bidding strategy and competition:** Management stated they selectively bid on central government projects with fewer competitors (e.g., 5-6 bidders vs 10-15 in NHAI projects) to protect EBITDA margins.
3. **Purvesh Mehta on top customer concentration:** Management noted that the top 5 projects contribute about 70% of turnover, led by larger projects like the Jhajjar stadium and Central University of Punjab.
4. **Purvesh Mehta on trade receivables/inventory increase:** Management clarified that inventory rose due to advance material buying ahead of price spikes, and receivables rose due to pending final bill reconciliations for completed NIT and NBCC Aligarh projects.
5. **Akash Agarwal (Individual Investor) on margin sustainability:** Management expressed confidence that current 17% EBITDA margins are sustainable for the next couple of years.

### One-line takeaway
Globe Civil Projects reported a strong Q1 FY27 with 37% revenue growth and 40% PAT growth, backed by a ~Rs 700 crore order book, a 17.01% EBITDA margin, and a selective bidding strategy targeting central institutional projects.
