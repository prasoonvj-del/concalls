# Markolines Pavement Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by steady revenue growth, margin expansion, higher profitability, and new sector expansion into marine infrastructure.

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 86.02 crore (up 10.75% YoY from Rs 77.67 crore; up 13.39% QoQ).
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 11.34 crore (up 24% YoY from Rs 9.10 crore; up 25.53% QoQ).
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin: 13.18% (up from 12.10% in Q1 FY27 and 11.72% in Q2 FY26).
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 4.95 crore (up 21.21% YoY from Rs 4.08 crore; up 13.53% QoQ).
- Q2 FY27 Basic EPS: Rs 2.23 (up from Rs 1.70 YoY).
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 161 crore (up 7.64% YoY from Rs 150 crore).
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 20.51 crore (up 16.87% YoY from Rs 17.55 crore).
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 9.31 crore (up 18.15% YoY from Rs 7.88 crore).
- H1 FY27 Basic EPS: Rs 4.19 (up from Rs 3.42 YoY).
- Unexecuted order book: ~Rs 800 crore as of the call date (vs Rs 625 crore as of September 20).
- Bidding pipeline: ~Rs 2,000 crore.
- Order book mix: 60% specialized construction, 40% major maintenance.
- Revenue segment mix (till September): 35% specialized construction, 65% maintenance.
- Long-term target: Rs 1,000 crore company in 3 years, with 25%-30% YoY growth.

### Highlights
- Management stated the company is positioned as India's "road doctor," offering lifecycle maintenance, preventive maintenance, and advanced technologies.
- Successfully expanded beyond highways into marine infrastructure, securing two projects in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands (bridge and road connecting two islands) and a rehabilitation facility project in Andhra Pradesh.
- Amalgamation of Markolines Infra Limited: Management stated SEBI clearances have been received and submissions are underway to NCLT, expecting completion before this financial year or early next financial year.
- *Note on Record:* The record of the M&A exchange ratio (1:1.05) is not explicitly stated in the call transcript, but the merger status is confirmed.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a 25%-30% YoY growth rate over the next few years.
- Management targets achieving a Rs 1,000 crore revenue milestone in 3 years.
- Expects 20%-25% conversion rate from the Rs 2,000 crore bidding pipeline (adding Rs 400 to Rs 500 crores in orders).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's road construction has expanded from 20 km/day in 2014 to nearly 34 km/day; focus is now transitioning to lifecycle asset management and maintenance.
- Maintenance allocation in India is ~15% compared to 40% internationally, presenting room for future allocation increases.
- In May 2026, the government allowed FDI and MNC participation in the construction phase, increasing demand for well-maintained roads.
- Roads require complete overlay and maintenance every 5 to 7 years per IRC and MoRTH codes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expansion driven by operational efficiencies, project management, and a higher percentage contribution from specialized construction activities versus MMR activities.
- Service quotes are structured based on capital employed, machinery required, depreciation, and interest calculations to secure a fixed bottom line.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding into adjacent infrastructure sectors such as marine (port constructions, jetty developments, bridges) and sports infrastructure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term MMR orders spread over 6 to 12 months (or 6 to 8 months); specialized construction orders spread over 18 to 24 months (or 3 to 4 years).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management as major negative risks, though terrain and initial substrata variations in greenfield alignments were discussed in Q&A regarding construction quality.
