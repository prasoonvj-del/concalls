# OM Infra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-26 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue and operating margins improved, but net profitability declined due to seasonal monsoon impact and real estate registration costs.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 270 crore (up 15.29% YoY from Rs 234 crore)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 21.99 crore (up 72.33% YoY from Rs 12.76 crore)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 8.12% (expanded by 2.69 percentage points YoY from 5.43%)
- **H1 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 60.38 crore (down 89% YoY from Rs 602.15 crore; *Note: transcript states 60.38 vs 602.15 as per text*)
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 0.22% (down from 2.56%)
- **H2 FY27 Revenue Target (October '26 to March '27):** Approximately Rs 400 crore
- **Real Estate Registration Rates (Om Pallacia):** New registrations at Rs 12,000 to Rs 15,000 per sq ft average.
- **Order Additions / Pipeline:** Supplementary order of ~Rs 29 crore from UP Jal Nigam (Indore); L1 in joint venture (MCC-Om) worth ~Rs 32 crore from Vidarbha Irrigation Development Corporation; ~Rs 47 crore order from Isarda; expected upcoming orders of ~Rs 1,000 to Rs 1,100 crore.

### Highlights
- H1 is typically subdued due to monsoon-related challenges affecting construction pace and resource mobilization.
- Real estate subsidiary (Om Pallacia) booked registrations for older units (booked in 2011-12 at Rs 5,000/sq ft benchmark) against current construction costs of Rs 8,000/sq ft, impacting reported net profitability. Unsold inventory is being furnished with low-cost interiors to yield rental income (>6% yield) before outright sale.
- Management expects FY28 to be a four-digit revenue year, driven by non-JJM priority sector orders.
- *Continues Pattern / Breaks Record:* Unlike prior quarters heavily reliant on Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM), management stated they have "lost faith on JJM revenue" and are basing future growth on newly added non-JJM orders.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a top line of approximately Rs 400 crore for H2 FY27 (October '26 to March '27).
- Management targets four-digit revenue for FY28.
- Target EBITDA margin for next year (March '28) is approximately 12%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Government focus has shifted heavily to drinking water supply (Jal Jeevan Mission), river interlinking, and water infrastructure.
- Management notes competition is limited to 3-4 players per bid, avoiding hyper-competitive segments like roads and highways.
- Data centers represent an emerging future opportunity due to water/pondage requirements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Turnkey execution involving civil works has diluted historical core hydro-mechanical margins, but broadens the addressable market.
- Bidding target margins are in the upper teens (double-digit).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major execution capacity constraints in civil works; management stated they can scale up to Rs 1,500-2,000 crore annual execution if required.
- Appointed IRPR to enhance investor relations, digital/print media presence, and prepare for potential future fundraising after 2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- State-level financial crunches have caused temporary revenue recognition delays for certain projects.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- State government financial crunches leading to delayed revenue recognition.
- Past Jal Jeevan Mission funding pauses by central government causing execution delays over a 2-year period.
- Pending arbitration cases are still in the hearing stage and not finalized.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jitesh Jain asked about the confidence behind the Rs 400 crore H2 target and FY28 four-digit revenue visibility.** Management replied they are conservative on the Rs 400 crore target and confident in FY28 reaching four digits because it relies on newly secured non-JJM orders rather than JJM.
2. **Jitesh Jain questioned the poor profitability of the real estate project (Om Pallacia).** S.K. Jain explained that units booked at Rs 5,000/sq ft in 2011-12 were registered this quarter against an actual construction cost of Rs 8,000/sq ft due to years of project delay; new registrations are at Rs 12,000–15,000/sq ft.
3. **Om Sangani asked why core operating profit margins (OPM) have not expanded despite doubled revenue over 10 years.** Vikas Kothari explained that shifting from pure hydro-mechanical to turnkey civil works diluted margins, alongside margin squeezes from Jal Jeevan Mission project delays.
4. **Jagdish Mundada asked about the status of pending large orders worth ~Rs 1,000 crore and arbitration cases.** Vikas Kothari stated they are L1 and awaiting LOIs (delayed due to municipal elections / Achar Sanhita in Rajasthan). S.K. Jain confirmed arbitration hearings are still ongoing and not in the final stage.
5. **Jagdish Mundada asked if promoters would hike their stake to 75%.** Vikas Kothari acknowledged it as a great suggestion and stated they would discuss it with promoters.

### One-line takeaway
Om Infra reported a subdued H1 FY27 net profit due to legacy real estate registrations and monsoon headwinds, but management guides for a strong H2 and a four-digit revenue scale in FY28 driven by new non-JJM water infrastructure orders.
