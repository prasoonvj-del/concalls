# Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sequential improvement in profitability and revenue execution.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Cut/adjusted top-line and bottom-line growth expectations to around 10% (down from ~15% top-line and 15-20% bottom-line in Q1).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to acknowledge monsoon impacts in Q2, but remains confident in a strong rebound in H2.
- New topics or risks: Mentioned the winding up of Kyrgyzstan JV and South African subsidiary due to non-starter status/lack of progress, while maintaining focus on other African nations, Serbia, Israel, and Sierra Leone.
- Last quarter's promises: Vande Bharat prototype launch target shifted slightly from December 2026 to Q1 2027 (March-June 2027).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 4,700 crore, up 8.79% QoQ (H1 FY27: Rs 9,022 crore)
- **Standalone Revenue (Turnover):** Rs 4,663.27 crore, up 5.5% YoY
- **Consolidated PBT:** Rs 325 crore, up 45.59% QoQ (from Rs 223 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 253.7 crore, up 59.04% QoQ and 10.06% YoY (from Rs 159.52 crore in Q1 FY27; H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 413 crore, up 13.25% YoY)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 214 crore, up 37.94% QoQ and 9.46% YoY (H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 370 crore, up 14.29% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 294 crore (Margin: 6.26%, up from 4.4% in Q1 FY27)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 230 crore (Margin: 4.94%, up from 3.99% in Q1 FY27)
- **Consolidated EPS:** Rs 1.20 (vs Rs 0.76 in Q1 FY27)
- **Order Inflow (H1 FY27):** Rs 7,132 crore
- **Total Order Book (as of September 30, 2026):** Rs 90,099 crore (Railways: Rs 56,816 crore; S&T: Rs 12,224 crore; Building works: Rs 6,504 crore; Metros: Rs 5,346 crore; Power & transmission: Rs 3,876 crore; Ports, roads, & highways: Rs 3,534 crore; Hydro & irrigation: Rs 1,237 crore; Other works: Rs 562 crore)
- **Consolidated Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Positive Rs 589 crore (vs negative Rs 1,250 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Total Liquidity (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 10,181 crore (Cash & cash equivalents: Rs 691 crore)

### Highlights
- Secured Rs 7,132 crore in order inflows during H1 FY27; targeting Rs 25,000 crore total work orders for the year.
- NMDC contract execution commenced (total value Rs 6,504 crore, with Rs 3,869 crore received during H1 FY27).
- Vande Bharat sleeper train set SPV (Kinet Railway) received the Bronze A’ Design Award 2026 in Italy; prototype delivery targeted for Q1 2027 (March-June 2027).
- Rishikesh-Karnaprayag rail project reached 79% overall progress (97% tunnel excavation done); completion targeted for December 2029.
- Employee productivity improved with revenue per employee rising to Rs 10.22 crore QoQ.
- Closed non-performing subsidiaries/JVs in Kyrgyzstan and South Africa, while focusing on new international geographies (Serbia, Israel, Sierra Leone, Ghana).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets work orders of around Rs 25,000 crore for the year.
- Top-line revenue projected to grow by around 10%, and bottom line by approximately 8-10% for FY27.
- Core EBITDA margins targeted at 6-7%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Government sustained thrust on railways, real estate, ports, hydro, highways, and sustainable infrastructure provides headroom for growth.
- Active pursuit of international markets (Maldives, Saudi Arabia, Rwanda, Ghana, Serbia, Israel, Sierra Leone).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Core EBITDA margin (without other income) targeted at 6-7%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operations supported by internal cash generation and internal resources.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated cash flow from operating activities turned positive at Rs 589 crore in H1 FY27.
- Total liquidity stood at Rs 10,181 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Q2 execution temporarily held back by monsoon season and geopolitical situations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vivek Nayak (Individual Investor):** Asked about international project status. Management highlighted ongoing works in Maldives, Saudi Arabia, Rwanda, shortlisting in Serbia and Israel, and L1 status in Sierra Leone.
- **Abhishek Leekha (Neste Wealth LLP):** Inquired about Vande Bharat prototype readiness and delays. Management stated teething troubles and approvals are resolved, with the prototype delivery expected between March and June 2027 (Q1 of next year).
- **Abhishek Leekha (Neste Wealth LLP):** Asked about the bidding win rate. Management stated the win ratio on bidding projects is around 7-10%.
- **Vishal Periwal (PL Capital):** Asked about H1 flattish revenue vs 10% full-year guidance and margin sustainability. Management explained that H2 (specifically Q3 and Q4) drives the majority of execution, and core EBITDA margins are targeted at 6-7%.
- **Rushank Mody (Choice Institutional Equities):** Asked about the closure of South African and Kyrgyz JVs. Management clarified that non-starter/non-progressing units are closed, but expansion continues in other African countries and new European/Middle Eastern geographies.

### One-line takeaway
RVNL posted a strong sequential rebound in Q2 FY27 profitability with PAT jumping 59% QoQ, supported by a healthy Rs 90,099 crore order book and an improved H1 operating cash flow of Rs 589 crore.
