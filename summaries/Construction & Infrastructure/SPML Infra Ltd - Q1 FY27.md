# SPML Infra Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY top-line and bottom-line growth driven by new execution, with healthy order intake and expansion into BESS.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 286 crore (+74% YoY, flat QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 28 crore (+81% YoY, +17% QoQ).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10.0% (vs 9.0% in Q1 FY26, +100 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 22.7 crore (+87% YoY, -20% QoQ due to a one-off tax reversal in Q4 FY26).
- **Order Book:** ~Rs 5,100 crore (~Rs 1,251 crore legacy orders, balance consists of new orders with expected operating margins $\ge$10%).
- **Order Intake (Q1 FY27):** Rs 1,293 crore; L1 status in additional Rs 212 crore.
- **Credit Rating:** ICRA upgraded long-term rating to BBB (Stable); CRISIL assigned BBB (Stable).

### Highlights
- Execution momentum accelerated due to new order wins under "SPML 2.0".
- Completed Phase 1 of the 2.5 GW Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) assembly facility at Supa MIDC, Pune; IEC/UL certifications underway.
- Exclusive technology tie-up with US-based Energy Vault for BESS grid-scale assembly.
- Design and drawing approvals for the NTPC BESS order expected by December 2026; container supplies to commence in Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue & Profit:** Management maintained guidance of >25% YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT for FY27.
- **Order Intake:** Target to surpass Rs 5,000 crore in order intake for FY27.
- **BESS Revenue:** Anticipate Rs 200 crore to Rs 300 crore execution from the NTPC BESS order in Q4 FY27.
- **Potential BESS Plant Revenue:** 2.5 GW capacity holds Rs 2,000–2,500 crore revenue potential; 5 GW holds Rs 4,500–5,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Tracked a pipeline of 134 upcoming water/power projects worth ~Rs 98,725 crore across 11 states (water supply & irrigation >80%).
- Government outlay for JJM 2.0 stands at Rs 8.69 lakh crore and energy sector allocation in Union Budget 2026-27 is Rs 1,09,029 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Under SPML 2.0, management only bids for projects with Price Variation (PV) clauses to stay insulated from lithium, steel, and currency fluctuations.
- EBITDA margin targeted at >10% for all new order bids.
- Employee costs rose due to hiring across water, power, and BESS business units.
- Other expenses decreased due to lower legal and professional charges.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- BESS setup total capex: Rs 236 crore (includes acquisition of 25 acres of land), funded via equity and a Rs 10 crore sanctioned term loan.
- BESS battery pack capacity expansion from 2.5 GW to 5 GW along with a 600-unit annual container facility targeted for completion in H1 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Promoters infused ~Rs 400 crore over the last 3 years; Net worth doubled from ~Rs 500 crore to >Rs 1,000 crore.
- Debt-to-equity ratio improved from 1.1x to 0.4x.
- Cash/FD balances stood at ~Rs 150 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- Total legacy NARCL debt obligation of Rs 700 crore (including interest) reduced by Rs 325 crore (including Rs 45 crore prepayment); remaining Rs 375 crore is backed by Rs 678 crore of arbitration awards. Total arbitration claims stand at ~Rs 4,526 crore.
- Enhanced credit facility from PSU banks to Rs 860 crore (from Rs 505 crore) plus a ~Rs 300 crore surety bond line.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Standard timeline risks related to design and drawing approvals from public clients (e.g., NTPC).
- Timing of arbitration award conversions and realizations to offset legacy debt.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Order Book composition:** Management confirmed that the Rs 3,843 crore non-legacy order book reflects SPML’s net share in JVs only. Legacy orders (~Rs 1,251 crore) will be mostly executed during FY27 and FY28.
- **BESS Business Model:** SPML operates as both an EPC player and an OEM battery pack supplier to other EPC contractors, creating dual revenue streams.
- **Sourcing & Technology for BESS:** Lithium cells are imported from China, while battery container integration uses Energy Vault’s proprietary technology.
- **Contractual Risk Mitigation:** Management reiterated that it does not take any project without a Price Variation (PV) clause or an escrow payment mechanism.
- **NARCL Debt Paydown:** Management expects to resolve and reduce NARCL debt liability to ~Rs 300 crore during FY27 through arbitration award receipts.

### One-line takeaway
SPML Infra is executing a turnaround led by balance sheet deleveraging, disciplined bidding with price variation clauses, and expansion into grid-scale battery energy storage systems (BESS).
