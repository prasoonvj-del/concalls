# Welspun Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and EBITDA growth driven by asset monetization and disciplined execution, with management maintaining full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,035 crore (+32% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 425 crore (+122% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 39.5% (vs 23.9% in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 296 crore (+201% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,808 crore (+11% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 610 crore (+53% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** ~Rs 18,700 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 450 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Asset Monetization:** Completed 51% stake sale in Mukarba Chowk-Panipat project, contributing Rs 194 crore to revenue and EBITDA.
*   **Oil & Gas:** Received DGH order approving the use of ONGC’s existing facilities for gas evacuation from the Mumbai block; management expects this to reduce capital investment by $300-400 million.
*   **Transportation:** Debt sanctioned (Rs 6,334 crore) for Pune-Shirur Road Project; appointed date expected by Q4 FY27.
*   **Welspun Michigan (WMEL):** Q2 revenue Rs 139 crore, EBITDA Rs 25 crore, PAT Rs 12 crore.
*   **Awards:** Received multiple HSE and sustainability awards, including CRISIL ESG score improvement to 60.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains FY27 guidance of 10-20% growth.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Management maintains guidance of 18%+.
*   **Order Inflow:** Targeting incremental orders of Rs 8,000-10,000 crore in FY27, expected primarily in H2.
*   **Pune-Shirur Revenue:** Expected Rs 300-400 crore in FY27; Rs 1,000-1,200 crore in FY28.
*   **Welspun Michigan:** Expects to add Rs 700-1,200 crore in orders.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Pipeline:** Order pipeline focused on water (transmission and treatment), rehabilitation, and tunneling.
*   **TBM Sourcing:** Management stated the Chinese export ban on Tunnel Boring Machines (TBMs) has no impact, as they source from Germany and Japan (with operations in India/Thailand).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Core EBITDA margins remain 20%+ excluding the one-time gain from the Mukarba Chowk-Panipat divestment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capital Efficiency:** The DGH order for the Mumbai gas block allows for the use of existing ONGC infrastructure, significantly optimizing capital deployment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Cash and bank balance of Rs 1,432 crore.
*   **Leverage:** Net debt to equity stands at 0.2x.
*   **Credit Rating:** CRISIL long-term credit rating upgraded to AA stable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution Disruptions:** Bhandup Water Treatment Plant experienced temporary execution delays due to debris disposal restrictions and BMC-mandated suspensions related to air quality.
*   **Phasing:** H1 revenue moderation in Welspun Michigan attributed to the timing of order releases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Impact of Chinese TBM export ban?** Management (Saurin Patel) confirmed no impact, as they source from Germany/Japan-based vendors with local manufacturing.
*   **Q: Oil & Gas revenue/profitability?** Management (Sandeep Garg) declined to provide specific numbers until the revised Early Development Plan (EDP) is approved by DGH.
*   **Q: Welspun Michigan H1 degrowth?** Management (Saurin Patel/Sandeep Garg) clarified it was a phasing effect due to order release timing; they remain confident in full-year growth.
*   **Q: Order book guidance?** Management (Sandeep Garg) reiterated the Rs 8,000-10,000 crore target for FY27, noting that most orders are expected in H2.

### One-line takeaway
Welspun Enterprises delivered strong Q2 results supported by asset monetization and maintains its full-year growth guidance, while the DGH approval for gas evacuation significantly de-risks its oil and gas vertical.
