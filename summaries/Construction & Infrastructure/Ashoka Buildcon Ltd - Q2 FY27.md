# Ashoka Buildcon Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Flat standalone revenue growth and a consolidated net loss, alongside trimmed near-term execution targets, balanced by robust order inflows and progress on HAM asset monetization.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Cut. Standalone FY27 revenue growth guided to ~5% to 10% (~Rs 6,600 crore) from 10–15% earlier. FY27 EBITDA margin expectation trimmed to ~9–9.5% for H2 and ~10% over the next 2–3 years (vs 9.5–10.5% in Q1). Standalone third-party debt target for March 2027 adjusted to Rs 600–700 crore (vs Rs 500–600 crore guided earlier).
- **Tone and demand view:** Highway construction activity deteriorated (-18% YoY in H1 FY27), prompting management to increase strategic focus on diversification into railways, Tariff-Based Competitive Bidding (TBCB) Power T&D, and overseas projects.
- **New topics or risks:** Entry into the TBCB framework in the transmission sector with the Sakoli project; execution start on Bowaichandi HAM following recent receipt of the appointed date.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Monetization of four HAM assets signed off, with closing expected within 2 weeks (delayed slightly from September/October timeline).

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Total Income:** Rs 1,356.24 crore, flat YoY (vs Rs 1,302.62 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 133.94 crore; EBITDA margin at 9.88%.
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 37.96 crore (vs Rs 139.18 crore in Q2 FY26, which included exceptional items of Rs 112 crore).
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 1,609.21 crore, down 15% YoY (vs Rs 1,907.88 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 314.67 crore; EBITDA margin at 19.55%.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Negative Rs 118.96 crore.
- **Order Book:** Rs 15,964 crore as of September 30, 2026 (excluding Rs 1,257 crore orders received/L1 post-Q2, total stands at Rs 17,222 crore).
- **Order Book Breakdown:** Roads & Railways Rs 9,275 crore (58%); Power T&D Rs 3,820 crore (23.93%); Building EPC Rs 941 crore (5.9%); SPV/Subsidiaries Rs 1,928 crore (12.08%).
- **Road Order Book Sub-breakdown:** HAM Rs 1,436 crore; EPC Rs 6,173 crore.
- **Segment Revenue Share (Q2 Standalone):** Road EPC 51.2%, Power T&D 22.94%, Railways 13.57%, Road HAM 8.6%, Building EPC & Others 3.69%.

### Highlights
- Secured LoA from RVNL for a new BG rail line in Uttarakhand worth Rs 602 crore (inclusive of GST).
- Entered the TBCB power transmission sector via an LoA from REC Power Development for lines in Sakoli, Bhandara (Maharashtra) with 24-month construction and 35-year O&M.
- Post-Q2 wins include an LoA from North Eastern Railway worth Rs 290 crore and L1 status for Vadodara-Mumbai Expressway Package 9 worth Rs 866 crore.
- Achieved commercial operation (COD) for balance 45.05 km of Tumkur-Shivamogga HAM project in Karnataka and third provisional COD for package 3 (4.22 km after de-scoping), making the SPV eligible for full annuity.
- *Record Check:* Order book replenishment remains on track with recent national expressway wins, though profitability margins compressed in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Targeting ~5% to 10% standalone growth in FY27 (~Rs 6,600 crore target); targeting a 3-year CAGR of 15%.
- **Margins:** H2 FY27 standalone EBITDA margins targeted at 9%–9.5%; expected to average 10%–10.5% over a 2–3 year period.
- **Order Inflow:** Expecting additional order inflows of Rs 3,000–5,000 crore in the remaining 4–5 months of FY27.
- **Bid Pipeline:** Immediate bid pipeline stands at Rs 51,000 crore (bids submitted/pending opening total Rs 8,000 crore across 170 km).

### Demand & industry commentary
- National highway project awards by the ministry reached 2,236.3 km in H1 FY27 (up 62.9% YoY). NHAI targets awarding 5,200 km in FY27 (+66% YoY).
- Sector execution remains constrained: cumulative construction during H1 FY27 stood at ~2,069 km, down 18% YoY. NHAI lowered its FY27 construction target by 7% YoY to 4,950 km.
- Government emphasis shifting toward thorough appraisal and land availability prior to execution, accelerating Ashoka's diversification into railways, TBCB power T&D, and overseas markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone H1 EBITDA margin stood at 7.1%. Management attributes expected H2 margin recovery (to 9.5%) to the execution ramp-up in ongoing international projects and Maharashtra road contracts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex:** Incurred Rs 62 crore in H1 FY27; full-year target maintained at ~Rs 125 crore.
- **HAM Equity Infusion:** Rs 68 crore invested in H1 FY27. Total pending equity infusion across HAM projects is Rs 340 crore (Rs 153 crore in H2 FY27, Rs 114 crore in FY28, and Rs 72 crore in FY29). Major H2 deployment of ~Rs 25 crore planned for Bowaichandi HAM following appointed date receipt.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Total Debt:** Consolidated gross debt stood at Rs 2,781 crore as of September 30, 2026. Standalone lenders' debt stood at Rs 1,169 crore, with the balance being internal inter-company debt.
- **Debt Guidance:** Standalone third-party debt targeted at Rs 600–700 crore by March 2027. Consolidated debt will drop to ~Rs 2,000 crore (Rs 800 crore project debt + balance working capital) post HAM asset sales.
- **Asset Monetization:** Transaction docs signed for four HAM assets; closing and initial proceeds of ~Rs 600 crore expected within two weeks. Remaining two HAM assets (Basawantpur and Tumkur-Shivamogga 4) expected to close in Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28, generating ~Rs 400 crore. Total proceeds across 6 HAM assets targeted at ~Rs 1,000 crore.
- **BOT Sales:** Monetization options actively pursued for Chennai ORR and Jaora-Nayagaon toll road.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued industry-wide weakness in road construction execution.
- Delays in order awards and execution timelines impacting near-term revenue targets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial):** What is leading the margin recovery from 7.1% in H1 to 9.5% in H2?  
   *Management:* Execution ramp-up in international projects and existing domestic road projects in Maharashtra will drive the margin expansion in H2.
2. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial):** What is the revised revenue guidance for FY27?  
   *Management:* Given late order inflows, standalone revenue growth may be around 5% to 10% (achieving ~Rs 6,600 crore by March 2027), though over a 3-year horizon execution will average 15% CAGR.
3. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial):** What is the exact status and cash inflow timeline for the 6 HAM assets monetization?  
   *Management:* Four assets are signed off and will close in two weeks with Rs 600 crore initial proceeds. The remaining two assets will close in Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28 for Rs 400 crore, totaling ~Rs 1,000 crore.
4. **Vasudev (Nuvama):** What is the total bid pipeline currently?  
   *Management:* Total immediate bid pipeline across NHAI, MoRTH, railways, and states is Rs 51,000 crore, of which Rs 8,000 crore of bids are already submitted and pending opening.
5. **Bhavin Modi (Anand Rathi):** Are you open to bidding for the upcoming large-ticket BOT road projects?  
   *Management:* Yes, Ashoka is well-placed, evaluating opportunities, and actively working with financial investors for BOT projects where traffic risk visibility is clear.

### One-line takeaway
Ashoka Buildcon is trimming its near-term execution guidance due to broader industry slowdowns, but is well-positioned for balance sheet deleveraging via imminent Rs 1,000 crore HAM asset monetization proceeds and non-road diversification.
