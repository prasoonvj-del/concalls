# Ashoka Buildcon Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Standalone revenue was flat YoY and margins remained suppressed, while sector-wide construction activity faces headwinds despite improving order inflows and diversification.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Revenue growth guidance lowered from 10-15% to flattish/~5% up for FY27 (management noted standalone growth may achieve ~10% or around Rs 6,600 crore revenue by March end). EBITDA margin guidance revised to 9%-9.5% for H2, averaging 10%-10.5% over the next 2-3 years. Order inflow guidance set at Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 5,000 crore.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more cautious due to ongoing sector-wide construction pressures (August construction down 12% MoM, H1 down 18% YoY), though bidding and awarding pipelines show recovery.
- New topics or risks: Entry into the TBCB transmission sector via the Sakoli project in Maharashtra. Old risks like highway construction slowdown and execution delays persist.
- Last quarter's promises: Asset monetization of four HAM projects progressed towards closure, with the first tranche of proceeds expected soon.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Total Income:** Rs 1,356.24 crore vs Rs 1,302.62 crore in Q2 FY26 (flat YoY).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 133.94 crore; EBITDA Margin: 9.88%.
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 37.96 crore vs Rs 139.18 crore in Q2 FY26 (which included exceptional items of Rs 112 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 2,676.68 crore; EBITDA: Rs 259.47 crore (margin 9.69%); PAT: Rs 69.50 crore.
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 1,609.21 crore, down 15% YoY vs Rs 1,907.88 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 314.67 crore; EBITDA Margin: 19.55%.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Negative Rs 118.96 crore.
- **Order Book:** Rs 15,964 crore as of September 30, 2026 (excluding post-September orders of Rs 1,257 crore, taking total to Rs 17,222 crore).
- **Order Book Breakdown:** Roads & Railways Rs 9,275 crore (58%); Power T&D Rs 3,820 crore (23.93%); Building EPC Rs 941 crore (5.9%); Subsidiaries/Associates Rs 1,928 crore (12.08%).
- **Total Gross Debt:** Rs 2,781 crore (standalone lenders' debt Rs 1,169 crore). Target standalone third-party debt by March 2027: Rs 600 crore to Rs 700 crore.
- **Capex Incurred:** ~Rs 62 crore up to date; full-year target at ~Rs 125 crore.
- **HAM Pending Equity:** ~Rs 340 crore total (~Rs 153 crore for the second half of FY27, Rs 114 crore for FY28, Rs 72 crore for FY29). Infused Rs 68 crore in H1.
- **Toll Collection:** Jaora-Nayagaon gross total toll collection at Rs 73.5 crore in Q2.

### Highlights
- Received LOA from RVNL for a new BG rail line in Uttarakhand valued at Rs 602 crore (including GST) in Q2.
- Received LOA from REC Power Development for establishing 400, 220, and 132 KV lines at Sakoli district Bhandara on TBCB (Tariff-Based Competitive Bidding) mode in Maharashtra (first-ever TBCB project for the company).
- Post Q2: Received LOA from North Eastern Railway (NER) worth Rs 290 crore (including GST).
- Received commercial operation date (COD) for the Tumkur-Shivamogga HAM project (balance 45.05 km), making the SPV eligible for the first annuity; also received 3rd provisional COD for 4.22 km after de-scoping.
- Post-September order inflows include Rs 391 crore and being L1 for Vadodara-Mumbai Expressway package 9 worth Rs 866 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Management targets ~5% to 10% growth for FY27 (~Rs 6,600 crore revenue by March end), with a 3-year CAGR target of 15%.
- **EBITDA Margin:** Expected to inch up by 1.5% by year-end, with H2 margins at 9%-9.5% and long-term average at 10%-10.5%.
- **Order Inflows:** Management targets an additional Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 5,000 crore for the rest of the year.
- **Capex:** ~Rs 125 crore targeted for FY27.
- **Debt Reduction:** Consolidated debt expected to reduce to ~Rs 2,000 crore post asset monetization of four HAM projects (leaving project debt of Rs 800 crore and balance working capital).

### Demand & industry commentary
- NHAI awarded 329 km of highways by August end; national highway awards reached 2,236.3 km during April-September FY27 (up 62.9%). NHAI plans to award 5,200 km in FY27 (up 66%).
- Construction activity remains under pressure: August construction declined 12% MoM to ~182 km; cumulative April-September construction stood at ~2,069 km (down 18%). NHAI targeted construction of 4,950 km in FY27 (down 7% YoY).
- Sector focus shifting from awarding kilometers to proper project appraisal, land availability, and execution efficiency.
- Strong structural opportunities identified in railways (Union Budget allocation of ~Rs 2.93 lakh crore, 7 high-speed rail corridors, dedicated freight corridors), Power T&D (interstate transmission systems), and international infrastructure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin stood at 9.88% in Q2 and 9.69% in H1 FY27.
- Management expects margins to improve in H2 due to the ramp-up of road projects in Maharashtra and international projects.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Infused Rs 68 crore equity into HAM projects in H1; pending equity commitment stands at ~Rs 340 crore.
- Monetization of four HAM projects is near closure, with proceeds expected in the coming weeks and actual realization in Q4 or Q1 next year (~Rs 1,000 crore total expected for four + two assets, with first tranche of ~Rs 600 crore).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total gross debt stood at Rs 2,781 crore, with standalone lenders' debt at Rs 1,169 crore.
- Standalone third-party debt targeted at Rs 600 crore to Rs 700 crore by March 2027.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sector-wide construction activity remains under pressure and declining YoY/MoM.
- Execution delays across projects due to various external factors.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial):** Asked about order inflows, capex, equity commitments, asset monetization timelines, and debt reduction targets. Management clarified that four asset sales will close soon with proceeds realizing in Q4/Q1, dropping consol debt to ~Rs 2,000 crore.
2. **Vasudev (Nuvama):** Inquired about BOT stake sale receipts, Chennai ORR/Jaora-Nayagaon plans, and the bid pipeline. Management stated Chennai ORR and Jaora-Nayagaon sales are being pursued, and the total bid pipeline is Rs 51,000 crore.
3. **Bhavin Modi (Anand Rathi):** Asked about appetite for BOT projects and non-road/international growth strategy. Management confirmed they evaluate BOT projects where traffic risk is visible and continue expanding across railways, metros, TBCB, and international markets.

### One-line takeaway
Ashoka Buildcon reported flat standalone revenues and trimmed its FY27 growth outlook amid sector construction headwinds, while advancing balance sheet deleveraging via asset monetization and diversifying into TBCB transmission and railways.
