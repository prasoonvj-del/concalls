# Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Robust execution, improved profitability, and strong order inflows.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 4,300 crore, up 9.62% YoY
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 4,321 crore, up 10.55% YoY
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 171 crore, up ~110% YoY; Margin at 3.99% (vs 2.08% YoY)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 155 crore, up 21.72% YoY
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 190 crore (Margin at 4.41%, up from 1.66% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 159.52 crore, up 18.73% YoY
- **Order Inflow (Q1):** Rs 5,417 crore
- **Total Order Book (as of June 30, 2026):** Rs 93,492 crore
- **Order Book Mix:** Railways Rs 58,000 crore, S&P Rs 12,000 crore, Metros Rs 5,700 crore, Power & Transmission Rs 4,000 crore, Ports, Roads & Highways Rs 3,651 crore, Hydro & Irrigation Rs 1,626 crore.

### Highlights
- Secured Rs 5,417 crore in new orders during Q1; targeting Rs 20,000-25,000 crore total new work orders for the year.
- BharatNet project (Rs 13,000 crore initiative for 82,000 km OFC infrastructure) progressing well in UP West and UP East after initial execution and payment challenges.
- Vande Bharat sleeper train project (Rs 14,400 crore with 35-year maintenance) on track; first prototype trainset targeted for launch in December 2026. Kinet Railway Solutions Limited (SPV) received the Bronze A’ Design Award 2026 in Italy.
- Rishikesh-Karnaprayag rail project (Rs 37,000 crore) achieved 78% overall progress (97% tunnel excavation completed); targeted for completion by December 2029.
- Subsidiaries contributed Rs 126 crore to consolidated revenue and Rs 12.70 crore to PAT; JV/associate profit share was Rs 6.16 crore, with dividend income of Rs 12.07 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects top-line growth of around 15% and bottom-line growth of approximately 15-20% for FY27.
- Targeting order book split of 50-50% between railway management works and bidding works over the next three years.
- Long-term EBITDA margin vision: 5-7% over the next three years.
- Long-term ROE vision: 12-13% over the next three years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Infrastructure sector witnessing strong growth driven by government investment, modernization, multimodal connectivity, and green mobility transition.
- Focus areas for new business include ports, hydro, highways, green energy/solar, real estate, and overseas markets.
- International expansion targets include Central Asia, the Middle East, Eastern European countries (Georgia, Serbia), Southeast Asia, and Africa (power transmission, railways, roads, hydropower, and the Tel Aviv Metro in Israel).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Contracts generally covered by price variation clauses mitigating inflation impact.
- Bidding margins expected at 5-6% for domestic works and 15-20% for overseas projects; railway management works yield 8-10%, and PSU PMC works yield ~7%.
- Onerous project losses fully provisioned in FY26; no further major loss provisions anticipated.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Funded primarily through internal accruals; working capital bank lines arranged at 5.5-5.9% if required.
- Employee productivity improved with revenue per employee rising from Rs 4.29 crore to Rs 4.97 crore QoQ.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free, managing operations from internal cash and internal resources.
- Railway receivables/outstanding stands at approximately Rs 2,500 crore, collected on a dynamic monthly basis within 30 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations impacting execution and international operations.
- Labor availability constraints requiring extra effort.
- Client payment delays, though regular follow-ups are ongoing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vishal Periwal (PL Capital):** Asked about L1 order size and fixed-price contract share. Management stated 42% is railway management work, and overall target is Rs 20,000-25,000 crore in order inflows.
- **Ashutosh Kumar (Centrum Broking):** Inquired about mobilized vs unexecuted order book and BharatNet/Vande Bharat timelines. Management clarified Rs 40,000 crore is under active execution and gave a December 2026 launch target for the Vande Bharat prototype.
- **Sunil Bhat (Choice Limited):** Asked about revenue mix and ROI/ROE targets. Management stated 63% of Q1 revenue came from management works and guided for 12-13% ROE over three years.
- **Abhishek Leekha (Neste Wealth):** Addressed geopolitical risks in the Middle East and Israel. Management noted proper risk assessments, risk premiums, and government support are factored in.
- **Mayur Pednekar (Individual Investor):** Asked about provisions and railway receivables. Management confirmed onerous provisions are complete and railway receivables stand at Rs 2,500 crore.

### One-line takeaway
RVNL delivered a steady Q1 FY27 with 10.55% consolidated revenue growth and a 18.73% jump in PAT, backed by a robust Rs 93,492 crore order book and a clear 15-20% earnings growth guidance for the year.
