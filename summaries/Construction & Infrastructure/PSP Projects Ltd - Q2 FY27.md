# PSP Projects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue supported by healthy execution and labor availability.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR1,113 crores (up 58% YoY) in Q2 FY27; INR1,967 crores (up 61% YoY) in H1 FY27.
- **EBITDA:** INR69 crores (up 38% YoY) in Q2 FY27; INR124 crores (up 66% YoY) in H1 FY27.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 6.21% in Q2 FY27; 6.30% in H1 FY27 (vs 6.12% in H1 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR30 crores (up 87% YoY) in Q2 FY27; INR48 crores (up 193% YoY) in H1 FY27.
- **PAT Margin:** 2.69% in Q2 FY27 (vs 2.28% in Q2 FY26).
- **Outstanding Order Book:** INR12,872 crores (up 30% YoY) as of September 30, 2026.
- **Order Inflow:** INR1,305 crores in H1 FY27, including INR675 crores in Q2 FY27.
- **Employee Costs:** INR50 crores in Q2 FY27 (vs INR33 crores YoY).
- **Depreciation:** INR26 crores in Q2 FY27 (vs INR20 crores YoY).
- **Capex:** INR62 crores in Q2 FY27; INR91 crores in H1 FY27. Gross block at INR851 crores, net block at INR451 crores.
- **Borrowings:** Long-term borrowings at INR30 crores (including short-term maturity of INR14 crores); Short-term borrowings at INR238 crores.
- **Working Capital & Balance Sheet Metrics:** Net unbilled revenue at INR632 crores; Trade receivables at INR809 crores; Trade payables at INR499 crores; Retention money at INR229 crores; Mobilization advance at INR933 crores; Inventories at INR416 crores.
- **Credit Limits:** Revised sanctioned credit facilities at INR1,387 crores (reduced by INR110 crores). Non-fund-based utilization at INR753 crores; fund-based utilization at INR142 crores; free limit available at INR492 crores.
- **Fixed Deposits:** Total FDs at INR237 crores (lien-free: INR44 crores; under lien: INR193 crores).
- **Expected Credit Loss (ECL):** INR8 crores provided during the quarter.
- **Labor Strength:** 23,000 to 25,000 workforce.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue.
- Completed "Project Ninety" RCC structure in 121 days using precast technology (3 basements, ground, and 18 upper floors); new corporate house completed in 240 days.
- Successfully completed 17 projects during the quarter, including NHSRCL precast concrete cable ducts/noise barriers for L&T in Gujarat and Navbharat Mega Developers civil/structural/MEP work.
- Key project progress: Shree Ambaji Mata Temple (INR931 crores), SMC high-rise building (INR613 crores), Fintech Building GIFT City (INR251 crores), Human and Biological Science Gallery Science City (INR238 crores), GIFT City residential project (INR187 crores), Dharoi Dam region (INR171 crores), Sabarmati Riverfront Phase 2 (INR155 crores), and RVNL project (2 of 3 buildings handed over).
- Reduced sanctioned credit limits by INR110 crores as they were no longer required.
- Received INR20 crores to INR25 crores out of pending UP project dues, with the remainder expected by the third quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY25 [stated as spoken in transcript] revenue growth guidance of approximately 25% YoY, though internal targets are to cross INR4,000 crores (representing 30% to 35% growth).
- Full-year order inflow guidance maintained in the range of minimum INR4,000 crores to INR5,000 crores (or INR5,000 crores plus).
- EBITDA margins expected to remain in the H1 range with a potential improvement of plus/minus 1%, moving towards 7.5% to 8% over time as projects progress to finishing stages.
- Capex guidance for future years projected at 3% to 4% of revenue annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Bid pipeline stands at INR7,800+ crores (52% group projects, 48% external projects).
- Strong scope seen in tourism infrastructure (Ambaji, Dharoi, Kashi) and Adani Group infrastructure projects (airports, realty, internal housing, industrial, Commonwealth-related projects, and Dharavi redevelopment).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- ~75% of the order book (Adani Group projects) operates on a cost-plus percentage model where material cost differences are reimbursed, insulating the company from material cost risks.
- Remaining 25% of projects are largely near completion; new projects like Ambaji use RBI indexation for cement and steel price escalation.
- Initial project phases (RCC structure accounting for ~30% of project cost) have lower overhead absorption and lower margins; subsequent phases (70% comprising MEP, interiors, and finishing) execute faster and support higher margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Precast facility operating at full potential with a maximum revenue capacity of INR250 crores.
- Future precast expansion will only require small machinery/mold capex of INR5 crores to INR10 crores as sheds and infrastructure are already in place.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Adani Group projects feature efficient payment cycles (75% of bills cleared in 7-10 days, remaining 25% in the following month).
- Unbilled revenue elevated due to delayed certification processes by government authorities on the last month's billing.
- Surat Diamond Bourse (SDB) dues of INR90 crores remain stuck with no clear commitment yet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delayed bill certifications by competent government authorities resulting in higher unbilled revenue.
- Lack of commitment/payment delays from Surat Diamond Bourse (SDB).
- Provision for expected credit loss (ECL) of INR8 crores taken during the quarter based on Ind AS metrics and aging of receivables.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhananjay Mishra (Centrum Broking) asked about the order inflow outlook and full-year margin trajectory.** Management stated order inflows will be INR4,000 crores to INR5,000 crores, and margins will hover around H1 levels with a potential upside up to 7.5%. *(Not dodged)*
2. **Lakshminarayanan KG (Tunga Investments) asked how cost inflation (labor/materials) is managed across Adani and non-Adani projects.** Management explained that Adani projects are on a cost-plus model with reimbursed material costs and 7-10 day payment cycles, while non-Adani projects use RBI escalation indices. *(Not dodged)*
3. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital) asked whether EBITDA margins are capped at 7% due to the Adani mix.** Management clarified that margins can move towards 7.5%-8% as projects transition from the 30% structural phase to the 70% finishing and MEP phase. *(Not dodged)*
4. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial) asked for updates on Surat Diamond Bourse (SDB) and UP project receivables.** Management stated SDB has no commitment yet, while INR20-25 crores of UP dues have been received and the rest is expected by Q3. *(Not dodged)*
5. **Ishita Lodha (SVAN Investments) asked about precast revenue potential and capacity utilization.** Management stated full potential revenue is INR250 crores, currently running at full capacity with only minor INR5-10 crore capex needed for expansion. *(Not dodged)*
6. **Lokesh Kashikar (SMIFS Institutional Equities) asked about the increase in unbilled revenue and labor strength.** Management explained unbilled revenue is due to delayed government bill certifications and GST invoicing, and labor strength stands at 23,000 to 25,000. *(Not dodged)*

### One-line takeaway
PSP Projects delivered record H1 revenue with 58% YoY growth in Q2, backed by robust execution in Adani and infrastructure projects, and maintains a healthy INR12,872 crore order book.
