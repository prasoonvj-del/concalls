# Glass Wall Systems (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-13 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong revenue growth, robust order book visibility, and healthy long-term demand across verticals despite temporary shipment/freight timing impacts.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 107 crore (up 36% YoY)
  - Domestic business: Rs. 62 crore (up 93% YoY)
  - International business: Rs. 39 crore (down 12% YoY)
  - Fenestration business: Rs. 6 crore (up 127% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 23 crore (up 9% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 21.3%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 17 crore
- **Order Book:** Rs. 982 crore as of July 31, 2026

### Highlights
- Company completed its IPO (total size Rs. 428 crore, comprising a fresh issue of Rs. 60 crore and an OFS of Rs. 368 crore).
- Fresh issue proceeds of Rs. 50 crore (supplemented by internal accruals) are being utilized for backward integration into an in-house glass processing unit; construction is underway with commissioning expected in the next 8 months.
- International footprint expansion is underway, with management exploring new geographies like Canada and Israel.
- Maintained a debt-free balance sheet status.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects ~25% revenue growth for FY27.
- EBITDA margins are guided to be in the range of 23% to 25%, and PAT margins at 18% to 20% at the consolidated level for the full year.
- Glass processing backward integration is expected to deliver a 1% to 1.5% improvement to the bottom line once operational.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic facade market is a Rs. 9,000 crore market growing at ~15.9% CAGR (expected to reach ~Rs. 14,500 crore by FY30).
- US facade addressable market for exporters is close to Rs. 30,000 crore, growing at 6.5% to 7% CAGR, driven by China-plus-one de-risking trends.
- Fenestration market is growing at ~25% CAGR, expanding from a Rs. 1,000 crore market to a Rs. 3,000 crore market.
- Domestic order book is heavily skewed towards commercial real estate where tenants are waiting to occupy, resulting in faster turnarounds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 EBITDA margins were impacted by lower international business contribution (which carries higher margins) and increased employee costs (due to periodic salary revisions and manpower for new capacity).
- Freight costs are now borne by the company under a DDP (delivered duty paid) model and fully passed through to clients on a cost-to-cost basis with no margin impact, though it inflates the top and bottom line numbers equally (freight was Rs. 5.5 crore in Q1 revenue).
- All contracts across business verticals now include pass-through clauses for major raw materials (aluminum and glass).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current manufacturing capacity is 130 panels a day at the Vile Bhagad (Mangaon) facility (utilizing ~30% of the 1 lakh square meter land parcel).
- Total project cost for the glass processing backward integration unit is Rs. 80 crore (funded by Rs. 50 crore primary IPO proceeds and internal accruals), which will occupy an additional 25% of the land.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains a debt-free company.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Business is project-based and subject to schedule fluctuations, leading to seasonality (historically, the first two quarters are slower and the last two are heavier).
- International shipping cost volatility and potential import duties, though mitigated by full pass-through agreements with clients.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nirav Bhanushali (PMS & AIF) asked about the margin impact from freight and international delivery timing.** Management clarified that freight is a 100% pass-through with no margin dilution, and international revenues will pick up in Q3/Q4 based on project schedules.
- **Naitik Mutha asked why margins are structurally higher post-COVID compared to pre-COVID levels.** Management explained this is due to a business mix shift toward high-margin exports and fenestration, a pivot from slow-turnaround residential to faster commercial domestic projects, the introduction of raw material pass-through clauses, and manufacturing consolidation.
- **Vaidik Parwal asked about the timeline for the glass processing unit and expected margin gains.** Management stated an 8-month timeline to commissioning and an expected 1% to 1.5% bottom-line improvement.

### One-line takeaway
Glass Wall Systems delivered a strong Q1 with 36% revenue growth, backed by a robust Rs. 982 crore order book and ongoing backward integration to protect and enhance its 23-25% EBITDA margin profile.
