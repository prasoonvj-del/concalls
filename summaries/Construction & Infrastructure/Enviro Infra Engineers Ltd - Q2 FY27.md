# Enviro Infra Engineers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management maintained annual guidance and emphasized strong execution visibility, attributing recent margin compression to accounting adjustments rather than operational failure.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Revenue: Rs 2,000 crore; PAT: Rs 240-250 crore).
- **Tone:** Shifted from "confident despite pressure" to "explaining accounting-led margin moderation" while reiterating long-term strategy.
- **New topics:** Detailed explanation of accounting entries (interest costs on Suyog Urja acquisition and profit reversal on EPC-to-IPP projects) impacting consolidated margins.
- **Promises:** Previous expectation of OCF improvement by September was not met; management now targets OCF positive by Q4 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 363.8 crore (+60% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 74.9 crore (+15.3% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 20.58% (vs 28.56% in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 39.5 crore (vs Rs 49.5 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 24.38%.
- **Total Order Book:** Rs 6,778 crore (Water: Rs 3,439 crore; Renewable: Rs 3,340 crore).

### Highlights
- **New Wins:** Secured two HAM projects in Varanasi (Rs 257 crore) and two wind EPC projects via Suyog Urja (Rs 418 crore).
- **Renewable Growth:** Renewable energy contributed 29% of H1 FY27 consolidated revenue.
- **Project Status:** 58 water/wastewater projects completed to date (1,018 MLD capacity).
- *Record check:* The company continues to aggressively expand its renewable order book, consistent with its pivot noted in previous quarters.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue:** Maintained at Rs 2,000 crore.
- **FY27 PAT:** Maintained at Rs 240-250 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin:** Target 19-20% at consolidated level.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Water/Wastewater:** Remains the core business; management sees strong opportunities in ZLD, reuse, and desalination.
- **Renewables:** Scaling BESS and Wind EPC; management views this as a fast-execution segment that complements the water business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margin Compression:** Attributed to accounting entries (interest on Suyog Urja acquisition, profit reversal on internal EPC-to-IPP projects) and higher depreciation/finance costs.
- **Input Costs:** Management noted that while price escalation clauses exist, they do not cover 100% of inflation, leading to the lower margin guidance.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Suyog Urja Acquisition:** Total cost Rs 311 crore; Rs 111 crore paid, balance Rs 200 crore payable over next two years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Debt:** Consolidated borrowings increased to Rs 703.9 crore (vs Rs 422.3 crore in March 2026).
- **Receivables:** Consolidated trade receivables rose to Rs 321.8 crore (vs Rs 155.3 crore in March 2026).
- **OCF:** Currently negative due to working capital deployment; expected to turn positive by Q4.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Margin Volatility:** Accounting adjustments and higher finance costs are impacting reported profitability.
- **Working Capital:** Receivables and debt levels have increased significantly compared to year-end FY26.
- **Execution:** Reliance on Q3/Q4 to meet annual revenue targets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why the 800-900 bps margin decline?** A: Management clarified it is largely due to accounting entries (interest on acquisition, profit reversal on group projects) and higher depreciation, not operational inefficiency.
- **Q: Is the company shifting focus away from water?** A: No, water remains the core business; renewable growth is an extension of engineering capabilities.
- **Q: When will OCF turn positive?** A: Management expects OCF to turn positive by Q4 FY27.
- **Q: What is the status of the Bangalore projects?** A: Projects are under execution and moving smoothly; management expects to complete them ahead of schedule.

### One-line takeaway
Enviro Infra maintains its FY27 revenue guidance of Rs 2,000 crore, attributing recent margin compression to accounting adjustments from the Suyog Urja acquisition and higher finance costs, while expecting OCF to turn positive by Q4.
