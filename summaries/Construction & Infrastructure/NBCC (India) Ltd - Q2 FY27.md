# NBCC (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong standalone revenue and EBITDA growth driven by redevelopment execution and real estate monetization; management maintains annual guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Consolidated revenue target of Rs 16,000-17,000 crore for FY27).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive; management emphasized "fast track" execution and secured new MoUs with state governments (West Bengal, Bihar).
- **New topics:** Inauguration of branch office in Melbourne, Australia; specific land monetization in Jaipur (Rs 433 crore revenue expected in Q4); signing of 9 MoUs with West Bengal government.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Amrapali Phase 1 completed (delivered); 7 GPRA Avenue Towers sale executed (delivered).

### Key numbers
- Standalone Revenue from Operations: Rs 2,890 crore (38% YoY increase).
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs 3,396 crore.
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 219 crore (154% YoY increase from Rs 86 crore).
- Standalone PAT: Rs 225 crore (31% YoY increase).
- H1 Standalone Revenue: Rs 4,713 crore (26% YoY increase).
- H1 Standalone EBITDA: Rs 379 crore (105% YoY increase).
- H1 Standalone PAT: Rs 376 crore (31% YoY increase).
- Standalone Order Book: Rs 1,12,000 crore; Consolidated Order Book: Rs 1,26,000 crore.
- New business secured (Q2): Rs 3,600 crore (consolidated).

### Highlights
- Secured major projects: RBI Residential Complex, Amaravati (Rs 800 crore) and Housing Project, Seychelles (Rs 600 crore).
- Redevelopment: Sold 8.8 lakh sq ft at Avenue Towers, Sarojini Nagar for Rs 5,191 crore.
- Amrapali: Phase 1 completed (24 projects); Phase 2 in progress with one site reaching 26 floors.
- International: Opened branch office in Melbourne, Australia (Oct 28, 2026).
- HSCC merger: Management stated it is on "fast track."
- *Record check:* The company continues to execute its large order book; management noted that potential execution delays remain a risk factor.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 standalone revenue guidance of Rs 13,000-14,000 crore and consolidated revenue of Rs 16,000-17,000 crore.
- FY28 revenue guidance: Rs 21,000-22,000 crore.
- FY27 consolidated PAT guidance: Rs 1,100-1,200 crore.
- Order inflows (FY27): Management expects Rs 50,000-60,000 crore.
- New awards (next 2 quarters): Management expects Rs 15,000-16,000 crore (includes Supertech Rs 9,600 crore and J&K projects).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand for the "self-sustainable" redevelopment model from state governments (Bihar, West Bengal, J&K) as it requires no upfront government investment.
- Construction restrictions (GRAP/AQI) in Delhi affect local projects, but management stated other regions are unaffected.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin: ~8% (vs 5-6% historical range).
- Margin drivers: Marketing fees (Rs 100 crore in H1) and fixed cost structure leveraging revenue growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Land monetization: Sold two Jaipur parcels (Rs 433 crore revenue expected in Q4).
- Pipeline: Meerut and Ghaziabad land parcels in auction process.
- Total land inventory value: ~Rs 1,600 crore (per IBBI valuer report).
- Dubai: Groundbreaking expected Dec 2026/Jan 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not explicitly discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Construction restrictions (GRAP/AQI) in Delhi.
- Delays in approvals and financial arrangements for state government projects (e.g., MAHAPREIT).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Marketing Fees:** Management clarified H1 marketing fee was ~Rs 100 crore; expects another Rs 100 crore in H2.
- **Order Book/Execution:** Analysts questioned the gap between order book and execution; management cited 24-30 month execution cycles and approval delays.
- **Land Monetization:** Management confirmed ~Rs 433 crore revenue from Jaipur land sales to be booked in Q4.
- **West Bengal:** Signed 9 MoUs; potential size ~Rs 10,000 crore.
- **Supertech:** Tenders uploaded (Rs 9,600 crore); awards expected in Q3.

### One-line takeaway
NBCC delivered strong Q2 growth driven by real estate monetization and redevelopment margins, with management maintaining its FY27 revenue guidance and highlighting a robust pipeline of state government projects.
