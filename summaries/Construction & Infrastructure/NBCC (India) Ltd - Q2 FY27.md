# NBCC (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and EBITDA grew strongly YoY, boosted by execution scale and self-sustainable marketing fees.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (FY27 standalone revenue Rs 13,000–14,000 crore, consolidated revenue Rs 15,000–17,000 crore, consolidated PAT Rs 1,100–1,200 crore; FY28 revenue Rs 20,000–22,000 crore, PAT Rs 1,500 crore).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive; expanded focus to new state-level redevelopment self-sustainable models (signed 9 MoUs with West Bengal and secured Bihar government projects).
- New topics or risks: Delhi winter pollution/GRAP construction halts flagged as a localized risk; opened an international branch office in Melbourne, Australia.
- Last quarter's promises: Dubai project design/ drawings approved with RERA in progress, groundbreaking expected Nov–Dec 2026; land parcel monetization initiated with 2 Jaipur land auctions.

### Key numbers
- Standalone Revenue from Operations: Rs 2,890 crore (up 38% YoY).
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs 3,396 crore.
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 219 crore (up 154% YoY from Rs 86 crore).
- Standalone PAT: Rs 225 crore (up 31% YoY).
- H1 Standalone Revenue: Rs 4,713 crore (up 26% YoY); H1 Standalone PAT: Rs 376 crore (up 31% YoY).
- Order Book: Standalone at Rs 1,12,000 crore; Consolidated at Rs 1,26,000 crore.
- Business Secured (Q2 Consolidated): Rs 3,600 crore.
- Tenders Awarded (Q2 Consolidated): Rs 1,500 crore (H1 total: Rs 3,200 crore).

### Highlights
- Major orders secured in Q2: RBI Residential Complex at Amaravati, AP (Rs 800 crore) and Seychelles Housing Project (~Rs 600 crore).
- Sold 8.8 lakh sq ft of residential apartments at Avenue Towers, Sarojini Nagar, New Delhi for Rs 5,191 crore via e-auction.
- Completed 24 Amrapali Phase I projects with handing over in progress; Phase II fast-tracked with Rs 8,000 crore balance workload.
- HSCC merger is on fast track for better synergy.
- Sold two land parcels in Jaipur via open e-auction for Rs 433 crore (Rs 250 crore and Rs 180 crore against book value of Rs 195 crore), realizing Rs 239 crore profit to be recognized in Q4.
- In-pipeline tenders: Uploaded Rs 9,600 crore tenders for Supertech (to award in Q3); technical bids received for J&K tenders (~Rs 2,000 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to award Rs 15,000–16,000 crore of new tenders in H2 FY27 (including Supertech, J&K, Bihar, and West Bengal).
- Total Order Inflows Guidance for FY27: Management maintains Rs 50,000–60,000 crore expected order inflows, aiming for a closing order book of Rs 1,85,000–2,00,000 crore by year-end.
- Target running project portfolio expected to reach Rs 40,000 crore on a consolidated level by end of FY27 (currently at Rs 33,000 crore).
- Expected order inclusions: Rs 30,000–35,000 crore from 5 GPRA pending Cabinet approval.
- Marketing Fee Revenue: Expected around Rs 100 crore in H2 FY27 and Rs 250 crore in FY28 (up 20-25%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Order book mix: Redevelopment projects account for 60% and PMC accounts for 40% (consolidated split: 53% redevelopment / 47% PMC).
- State governments and CPSEs (e.g., HPCL, ONGC, Mumbai Port Trust) are shifting to NBCC’s self-sustainable redevelopment model to avoid upfront capital expenditure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher EBITDA margin in H1 was aided by ~Rs 100 crore of marketing fees (1% fee earned on self-sustainable project sales like 7 GPRA and Amrapali; 1.25% in Bihar).
- Fixed-cost operating leverage positively impacts EBITDA as execution revenues scale up.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dubai Project: Design approved and RERA registration in progress; groundbreaking scheduled for late November/December 2026, with Phase 1 sales launching Dec 2026/Jan 2027.
- Land Bank Monetization: Total land inventory across 54 acres carries a book value of Rs 720 crore and an IBBI valuation of over Rs 1,600 crore; Meerut and Ghaziabad parcels next in auction pipeline.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Handled via self-sustaining models where project approvals and initial land monetization fund long-term execution.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Air Quality Index (AQI) GRAP restrictions during winter temporarily stop construction on Delhi-based projects.
- Statutory approvals, financial arrangements, and clearance delays impact conversion timelines for self-sustainable state government projects (e.g., MAHAPREIT).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Reason for revenue/EBITDA jump and one-offs?**  
  *Management response:* Maintained that revenue growth is perennial execution income. Higher EBITDA margin (8-9%) is due to fixed cost leverage on higher turnover combined with ~Rs 100 crore H1 marketing fee income.
- **Why is the consolidated order book flat QoQ despite inflows?**  
  *Management response:* Stated there are zero order cancellations; timing differences in project conversion/billing account for the minor Rs 1,000 crore net difference.
- **Status of 5 GPRA and state redevelopment projects?**  
  *Management response:* 5 GPRA (Rs 30,000–35,000 crore) is under Cabinet approval. Signed 9 MoUs with West Bengal (Slum development, riverfront, toilets, urban work) worth ~Rs 10,000 crore, and expecting Rs 10,000 crore work from Bihar.
- **Timeline and recognition of Jaipur land parcel sales?**  
  *Management response:* Cash collection occurs in Q3, while top-line (Rs 433 crore) and profit (~Rs 200–239 crore) will be recognized in Q4 FY27.

### One-line takeaway
NBCC delivered strong Q2 profit expansion driven by high-margin marketing fees and robust execution, while targeting Rs 15,000+ crore of H2 contract awards to meet its Rs 16,000–17,000 crore FY27 revenue guidance.
