# J. Kumar Infraprojects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong execution excellence, operational efficiency, and a healthy net cash position supporting profitable growth.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained ~15% revenue growth for FY27 (~Rs 6,500 crore) and order inflow guidance of Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 10,000 crore for the full year.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from temporary execution headwinds in Q1 to a more confident demand outlook, noting tighter MoRTH pre-qualification conditions reducing competition in niche segments.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Discussion around Delhi Metro canceling the previous L1 order and re-issuing a limited tender; debate over Chinese TBM export restrictions (dismissed as a myth); potential selective exploration of HAM projects. Old risks like BMC water restrictions disappeared as work normalized.
- Last quarter's promises: Vizag land monetization is progressing well with deals finalized and money received in the quarter; GMLR and Chennai projects are tracking ahead or on schedule.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 1,539 crore (up 15% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 229 crore (up 18% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 14.9% (vs 14.5% YoY)
- PAT: Rs 113 crore (up 25% YoY)
- H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 3,050 crore (up 8% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 443 crore (up 8% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 221 crore (up 9% YoY)
- Net debt / Net cash: Negative Rs 69 crore (Net cash) as of September 30, 2026
- Net working capital days: 100 days (vs 99 days for FY26)
- Total order book: Rs 22,711 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Metro 7%, elevated corridors 47%, road tunnels 19%, others 26%)
- Order inflows booked in H1 FY27: Rs 5,540 crore
- Mobilization advance balance: Rs 627 crore; Unbilled revenue: Rs 572 crore; Security deposits/retention: Rs 490 crore
- Capex done in H1 FY27: Rs 80 crore

### Highlights
- Delhi Metro canceled the previous L1 underground metro order due to project changes, and a fresh limited tender for previous bidders will be issued in 10-15 days.
- GMLR tunneling is progressing with both TBMs functional; project completion is targeted ahead of the original timeline before the end of 2029.
- Chennai project foundation work is 70-75% complete, targeted for completion by December 2028 or January 2029.
- Vadhvan port project mobilization has started with service road excavation and ground clearing; revenue expected from Dec/Jan.
- Gross debt-to-equity stands at 0.23x.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue growth guidance: Maintained at ~15% growth over last year (~Rs 6,500 crore).
- FY28 top-line guidance: 15% growth on FY27 top line.
- Long-term target: Rs 10,000 crore revenue by FY30 with EBITDA margins improving by 100 bps to 15%-16%.
- Order inflow guidance for H2 FY27: Additional Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 5,000 crore, totaling Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 10,000 crore for FY27.
- Capex guidance: Broadly Rs 150 crore per year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust bid pipeline with ~Rs 30,000 crore of projects expected to be bid in the next six months (elevated corridors/roads/flyovers ~Rs 10,000 crore, metros ~Rs 15,000 crore, buildings ~Rs 5,000 crore); Maharashtra alone has Rs 50,000 crore to Rs 100,000 crore planned.
- MoRTH has made pre-qualification and net-worth conditions more stringent, which is expected to lower competition in niche projects.
- Management indicated a selective willingness to explore lucrative HAM projects with decent numbers, though core focus remains on EPC.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins expanded due to disciplined cost management and project execution.
- Depreciation is expected to increase in Q3 due to the recent launch of the TBM during Diwali.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- TBM supply remains regular; Herrenknecht has a large facility in Chennai, and Chinese assembly accounts for less than 10% of TBMs in India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position stands at negative Rs 69 crore.
- Fixed deposits stand at ~Rs 700 crore, largely maintained as margin money and bank guarantees for project performance and mobilization advances.
- Fund-based and non-fund-based total sanctioned limits are Rs 6,290 crore, out of which Rs 4,400 crore is utilized. Non-fund limits utilized are ~Rs 3,500 crore out of Rs 5,000 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Competition level remains quite competitive.
- Labor shortages exist due to simultaneous execution of multiple works, though managed through mechanization and pre-casting.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Varun Aggarwal (Individual Investor):** Asked about the status of the Delhi Metro L1 order.
   * *Management answer:* Explained that DMRC canceled the order due to project changes and will issue a fresh limited tender for original bidders in 10-15 days. (Did not dodge)
2. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital):** Inquired about depreciation trends following TBM launches.
   * *Management answer:* Stated depreciation will rise from Q3 onwards as the recently launched TBM starts getting depreciated. (Did not dodge)
3. **Dhvaneet Savla (Savla Family Office):** Asked if drought-like water restrictions in Maharashtra impact execution.
   * *Management answer:* Clarified that water restrictions are not affecting work and pointed to strong Q2 results. (Did not dodge)
4. **Vipul Shah (Sumangal Investments):** Asked whether restrictions on Chinese TBM exports impact operations.
   * *Management answer:* Clarified that Chinese assembly is under 10%, major components come from Europe/US, and Herrenknecht manufactures locally in Chennai, creating no supply issues. (Did not dodge)
5. **Pritesh (Daga Investments):** Questioned why fixed deposits (~Rs 700 crore) are not used to reduce debt to improve ROE/ROCE.
   * *Management answer:* Explained that FDs are mandatory margin money for bank guarantees (performance and mobilization advances) and bid security. (Did not dodge)

### One-line takeaway
J. Kumar Infraprojects reported a 15% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 with a net cash balance, maintaining its FY27 guidance backed by a robust Rs 22,711 crore order book.
