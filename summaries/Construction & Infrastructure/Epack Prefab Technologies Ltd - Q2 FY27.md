# Epack Prefab Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue and order inflows, but margins compressed by rising steel costs and operating cash flow turned negative due to higher working capital.

### Key numbers
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 867 crore (up 19% YoY)
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 501 crore (highest ever)
- **H1 Profit (PAT) growth:** 2% YoY
- **Q2 EBITDA Margin:** 10.1% (recovered from 9.4% in Q1)
- **H1 Order Inflow:** Rs 1,124 crore (up 71% YoY)
- **Pending Order Book:** Rs 1,462 crore (visibility of 8 to 9 months)
- **Working Capital:** Increased to almost 60 days (guidance typically 40 to 55 days)
- **Receivables Increase:** Rs 144 crore (due to milestone billing delays, backed by letters of credit)
- **Inventory Increase:** Rs 92 crore (deliberate steel stocking)
- **Sandwich Panel Capacity Utilization:** Rose from ~35% in Q1 to ~47%-48% in Q2
- **FY27 Guidance:** Rs 1,900 crore+ revenue with 10% to 10.5% EBITDA margin

### Highlights
- Commissioned 13,000 tonnes of steel processing capacity at Ghiloth Greenfield project (Unit 5) and a new sandwich panel line in Ghiloth on October 30, 2026.
- Gujarat Greenfield plant on track for commissioning in Q4 FY27, taking total potential revenue capacity to Rs 2,800 crore - Rs 3,000 crore.
- ICRA reaffirmed A+ rating; IPO proceeds being deployed with discipline.
- Top 5 customers contribute ~28% to 30% of the existing order book; 60% of order book comes from new customers historically.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 guidance of Rs 1,900 crore+ revenue with 10% to 10.5% EBITDA margin.
- Management expects to grow at 25% to 30% CAGR over the next couple of years.
- H2 expected to be better than H1 in both revenue and margins due to current input pricing on new orders and inventory buffers.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains strong across warehousing, logistics, automobile, building materials, and new-age industries.
- Entering the data center segment with a first structural steel supply order; seeing inquiries for semiconductor facilities.
- PEB construction continues during GRAP restrictions in NCR if civil fronts are ready, though execution may see temporary supply-chain shifts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin fell by ~2.3% in H1 due to steel and input costs rising faster than pass-through on ongoing orders.
- Raw material prices rose 17% to 18% during Q2, but margins recovered sequentially via price increases on existing orders.
- Steel prices peaked near Rs 70,000/tonne due to Middle East war impacts and 11.5% safeguard import duty; management sees limited further upside.
- Steel exposure is hedged by covering 48% to 50% of steel requirements (3 to 4 months) and pricing new orders at current costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ghiloth Unit 5 and sandwich panel line commissioned in late October 2026.
- Gujarat plant under construction, starting with 50,000 tonnes capacity in phase 1 (design capacity 80,000+ tonnes), operational in Q4 FY27.
- No new capex needed for FY27-FY28; focus will be scaling capacity utilization to 80% to 85%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow turned negative in H1 due to a 60-day working capital cycle (up 15-18 days from guidance).
- Receivables increased by Rs 144 crore due to milestone billing delays, but are backed by LCs and expected to normalize in Q3.
- Company remains net cash positive.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slippage in working capital days (up to 60 days) and temporary negative operating cash flows.
- Input cost volatility (steel price spikes) pressuring gross margins before price adjustments take effect.
- Milestone billing delays from customers affecting receivable timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rohit Taparia (Individual Investor)** asked about long-term growth guidance and peak revenue potential. Management guided to 25%+ CAGR for the next couple of years and stated peak revenue potential of Rs 2,800-3,000 crore with current and ongoing capex.
2. **Devang Patel (Sameeksha Capital)** asked if the company faced delays/curbs in NCR due to GRAP. Management replied that PEB construction and material supply can continue if civil work is ready, though execution may stretch past GRAP.
3. **Anboli D.R. (Individual Investor)** asked how the company hedges against steel prices and if client competition exists. Management explained they cover 48%-50% of steel exposure, price new orders dynamically, and are unaware of clients setting up PEB facilities.
4. **Dhirendra Patro** asked if steel price hikes are causing customer capex deferments. Management stated they see no pushback or slowdown because building cost is a small part of overall customer capex.
5. **Moksh (Motilal Oswal)** inquired about customer concentration. Management clarified concentration is low, with top 5 customers contributing 28%-30% of the order book and an average PEB order value of Rs 10-12 crore.

### One-line takeaway
EPACK Prefab delivered record H1 revenues and strong order inflows, but faced margin compression and negative operating cash flows due to surging steel costs and stretched working capital cycles, which management expects to normalize in H2.
