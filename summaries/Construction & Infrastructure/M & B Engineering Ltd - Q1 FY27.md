# M & B Engineering Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited rising geopolitical tensions, freight cost spikes, and cost uncertainties, though demand remains robust and growth guidance is maintained.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: Rs 291 crore (up 22.5% YoY from Rs 238 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 22 crore (up 22% YoY from Rs 18 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 33 crore; Operating EBITDA margin: 11.4%
- Total Order book on hand: Rs 1,053 crore (up 25% YoY)
  - Proflex: Rs 216 crore (21%)
  - Phenix: Rs 837 crore (79%), including export orders outstanding of Rs 278 crore
- Export revenue: Rs 28 crore (representing 10% of total revenue); domestic revenue: Rs 263 crore (90%)
- Export volume dispatched: 1,400 metric tonnes
- Capital expenditure incurred in Q1 FY27: ~Rs 27 crore
- Proflex installed capacity: Increased to 21 lakh square metres per annum via two new mobile manufacturing units
- Phenix Sanand PEB capacity: 72,000 tonnes per annum (expanding to 92,000 tonnes by October 2026)
- Heavy structural steel capacity: 12,000 tonnes (expanding by 10,000 tonnes by Q1 FY28 via a Rs 30 crore investment)
- Cheyyar capacity expansion: 20,000 tonnes per annum (planned for Q3 FY28 completion, taking total capacity to ~1,54,000 tonnes per annum)
- Inquiry pipeline: Phenix ~Rs 4,000 crore, Proflex ~Rs 200 crore

### Highlights
- Both divisions (Phenix for PEB/heavy structural steel and Proflex for self-supported steel roofing) showed strong growth potential.
- Received AISC certification for the South India plant in Cheyyar; exports to the US West Coast via the Pacific route expected to begin next fiscal year.
- Utilized Rs 146.69 crore (57%) of net IPO proceeds of Rs 259.32 crore as of 30th June 2026 (utilizing Rs 8.88 crore during Q1).
- Board approved additional heavy structural steel capacity expansion at Sanand (Rs 30 crore investment for 10,000 tonnes).
- Captive 2.5 MW ground-mounted solar power plant developed in Gujarat.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of delivering revenue growth of over 25% in FY27.
- Management expects a gradual improvement in margins in H2 FY27 supported by US Section 232 sectoral duty reduction (from 50% to 25%), increased export revenue, and Sanand capacity expansion.
- Specific margin guidance is withheld for one more quarter due to West Asia crisis cost uncertainties.
- Medium-term plan targets over 20% CAGR over the next 3 to 4 years with progressive profitability improvement.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains robust with strong inquiry pipelines across domestic and international markets.
- Data center expansion in India is creating a USD 12-14 billion addressable opportunity for steel-intensive construction over 5 years (supported by USD 60-70 billion announced investments).
- High-rise construction is seeing increased steel adoption via hybrid PEB and heavy structural steel approaches.
- Sectors driving inquiries include automobiles, high-rise buildings, logistics/warehousing, defence/aviation, data centers, renewable energy, railways, agri-warehousing, and SME manufacturing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses increased by ~3% due to sharp increases in freight costs from India to the US and inland US transportation caused by West Asia geopolitical tensions.
- International freight costs reached sky-high levels (2 to 2.5 times normal, USD 10,000 to USD 12,000 per container).
- Worst-case export EBITDA margin is calculated at around 15% at current peak freight costs (down from a normal 16%-18%), while domestic EBITDA margins are around 11%.
- US Section 232 duties reduced from 50% to 25%.
- Company hedges 80% to 85% of standard raw material at order booking; ~15% to 20% remains open for variations. Raw material prices increased 10% to 12% from March to current.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sanand brownfield expansion (20,000 tonnes PEB) at an advanced stage, commissioning in October 2026 (benefiting H2 FY27).
- Cheyyar plant capacity utilization targeted at ~60% for the current fiscal; brownfield expansion implementation to start this fiscal, completing in Q3 FY28 (adding 20,000 tonnes).
- Total capacity to reach ~1,54,000 tonnes per annum by Q3 FY28.
- Exploring locations for a third facility in the North.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow is positive in Q1 FY27, supporting cash purchases.
- 57% of IPO proceeds utilized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical tensions and West Asia situation creating uncertainties in freight costs and international logistics.
- Fixed-price contracts limit immediate pass-through of sudden raw material or freight cost spikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Saumil Mehta (Kotak Mutual Funds) asked about the slower order inflow in Q1 and structural freight outlook.** Management answered that larger inquiries rubber-banded due to longer design-freezing times and will convert in Q2; order intake is up 25% YoY. Freight costs are at peak/unsustainable levels, but export orders still yield ~15% EBITDA in the worst case.
- **Kanishk Gupta (SS Family Office) questioned the company's lower hit rate (12%-15%) compared to peers (20%).** Management stated they prioritize bottom-line margins over chase-volumes and noted plants (Sanand) are operating near full capacity (75%-80%), limiting the ability to take on excess orders without delivery risks.
- **Aasim asked why domestic PEB realization was flat despite volume growth.** Management clarified that product mix (primary vs. sheeting) varies by quarter, and current execution reflects orders booked when raw material prices were stable.
- **Vijay Sarda (Systematix) asked what went wrong with margins over the past 4-5 quarters.** Management reiterated that demand is robust, but fixed-price contracts and soaring freight/steel costs impacted margins temporarily.

### One-line takeaway
M&B Engineering reported a 22.5% YoY revenue growth in Q1 FY27 backed by strong execution, but held back on specific margin guidance due to freight and geopolitical cost headwinds while maintaining its 25%+ annual revenue growth target.
