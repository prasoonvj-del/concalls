# M & B Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue, successful commissioning of capacity, and secured significant new orders in data centers and high-rise segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained >25% revenue growth for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "Cautious" (Q1) to "Confident" (Q2) due to capacity commissioning and order wins.
- **New topics or risks:** Data center order (hyperscaler), high-rise building order, CE certification for Sanand (Europe market entry).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Sanand expansion (20k MTPA) commissioned Oct 26, 2026 (Delivered).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 385 crore (Q2, +25.4% YoY); Rs 676 crore (H1, +24.1% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 43 crore (Q2, +17.6% YoY); Rs 79 crore (H1, +12.1% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 29 crore (Q2, +32.8% YoY); Rs 51 crore (H1, +28% YoY)
- **Order Book:** Rs 1,109 crore (+19% YoY)
- **Order Inflows (Q2):** Rs 441 crore (+12% YoY)
- **Export Revenue (Q2):** Rs 67 crore (+25% YoY)
- **Capex (H1):** Rs 70 crore (Rs 43 crore in Q2)

### Highlights
- Highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue achieved.
- Secured large data center order (hyperscaler) and significant high-rise building order (post-Q2).
- Sanand PEB expansion (20,000 MTPA) commissioned Oct 26, 2026.
- Sanand facility received CE certification (TÜV Nord) for UK/European market entry (target FY28).
- Proflex division: 3 additional mobile units commissioned over last 3 quarters.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains >25% revenue growth guidance for FY27.
- Targets >20% CAGR over next 3-4 years with progressive profitability improvement.
- Expects better margins in H2 FY27 vs H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Phenix inquiry pipeline: >2.5 lakh tons (domestic); >40,000-50,000 tons (North America).
- Proflex inquiry pipeline: >20 lakh sq meters; railway backlog >3 lakh sq meters.
- Data center industry: Projected to reach 6 GW by 2029; M&B sees significant opportunities.
- Freight rates: Cooling down (peak $12k/container, now $7k-$8k), aiding export outlook.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin impacted by steel price variation; PAT protected by volume and product mix.
- Freight costs: Still higher than pre-war levels but softening; customers supporting partial cost absorption.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sanand PEB capacity: Increased to 92,000 MTPA (Total: 124,000 MTPA).
- Structural steel expansion (Sanand, 10,000 MTPA): On track for Q1 FY28.
- Cheyyar expansion (20,000 MTPA): Work commenced, operational Q3 FY28.
- Total capacity target: 154,000 MTPA by Q3 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds: Rs 259.32 crore total; Rs 155.27 crore utilized (60%) as of Sept 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility (Iran/Hormuz) affecting freight.
- Fixed-price contracts limit immediate pass-through of cost spikes.
- Data center order conversion/timing uncertainty.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Resham Jain (VVD Asset Managers):** Asked about data center/high-rise order size and margins. Management: Significant orders, margins similar to existing industrial projects.
- **Saurabh Beria:** Asked about US political impact/freight. Management: Expect faster inquiry-to-order conversion; freight softening.
- **Vandit Shah (Abakkus Asset Management):** Asked about Cheyyar utilization (55-60%) and Proflex capacity. Management: Proflex at 99% utilization; evaluating specialized seam technology.
- **Nirav Bhanushali:** Asked about gross margin dip. Management: Impacted by steel price variation; PAT protected by volume/mix.

### One-line takeaway
M&B Engineering delivered record revenue and successfully commissioned capacity, with management expressing confidence in sustained growth and margin improvement in H2 despite lingering geopolitical freight headwinds.
