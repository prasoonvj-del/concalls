# Desco Infratech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth, successful diversification into new energy segments, and reiterated long-term revenue targets.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 75 crore (vs Rs 42 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Revenue Growth (YoY):** 78.52%.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 14.6 crore (vs Rs 8.98 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Growth (YoY):** 62.43%.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 19.45% (vs 21.38% in H1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 9.5 crore (vs Rs 6.17 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **PAT Growth (YoY):** ~54%.
*   **PAT Margin:** 12.66% (vs 14.68% in H1 FY26).
*   **EPS:** Rs 12.38 (vs Rs 8.04 in H1 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 370 crore+.
*   **Tender Pipeline:** Rs 740 crore+.
*   **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.45x.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Diversification:** Expanding beyond City Gas Distribution (CGD) into power distribution, solar EPC, and compressed biogas (CBG).
*   **Governance:** Appointed industry veterans (Arun Kumar, Shailesh Naik, Kalpesh Patel) to the board to enhance execution efficiency and transparency.
*   **Clean Energy:** Pilot CBG project successful; management expects commercial injection into the Adani Total Gas pipeline before Diwali.
*   **International:** UAE subsidiary (Desco Global FZ-LLC) is actively participating in ADNOC tenders following a pause due to regional geopolitical tensions.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 190 crore – Rs 200 crore.
*   **Long-term Goal:** Aiming for Rs 1,000 crore revenue by FY30.
*   **Segment Margin Outlook (PAT):** CGD (12.5%–13.5%), Solar EPC (10%–11%), Power T&D (11%–12%).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   CGD demand is supported by government mandates (PNGRB) requiring piped natural gas adoption.
*   Management views renewable energy (Solar/CBG) as essential services aligned with national priorities.
*   Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East are being monitored for potential impacts on crude-linked material prices and logistics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins saw a YoY compression (EBITDA 19.45% vs 21.38%). Management attributed this to project mix and execution schedules.
*   High-interest NBFC loans (16-17%) taken in 2024 are scheduled for closure/restructuring by December 15, 2026.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Completed infrastructure projects worth ~Rs 340 crore.
*   Project size execution capability now exceeds Rs 50 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management emphasized improving cash flow conversion and working capital management.
*   Increase in "other current assets" is attributed to executed state jobs awaiting certification/billing; management expects conversion to cash in 45-60 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical risks in the Middle East affecting supply chains and material costs.
*   Working capital intensity of infrastructure projects.
*   Potential margin volatility due to external price spikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did revenue growth not meet the 70-80% guidance?** A: Management clarified that the business has a natural execution cycle where H2 (Oct-Mar) is stronger; reiterated FY27 guidance of Rs 190-200 crore.
*   **Q: Status of UAE subsidiary?** A: Operations were paused due to geopolitical tensions but have resumed; currently participating in ADNOC tenders.
*   **Q: High-interest debt?** A: Management confirmed plans to close/restructure expensive NBFC loans by Dec 15, 2026.
*   **Q: Why the increase in "other current assets"?** A: Management stated it reflects executed work awaiting certification/payment, expected to convert to cash in 45-60 days.

### One-line takeaway
Desco Infratech is aggressively diversifying into renewable energy and power infrastructure while maintaining a strong CGD core, with management focused on professionalizing governance to meet a Rs 1,000 crore revenue target by FY30.
