# LCC Projects Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (34% YoY), margin expansion, and a robust order book supported by a healthy L1 pipeline.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 886 crore (vs Rs 661 crore in Q2 FY26), up 34% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 137 crore (vs Rs 94 crore in Q2 FY26), up 46% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.5% (vs 14.2% in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 77 crore (vs Rs 49 crore in Q2 FY26), up 58% YoY.
*   **PAT Margin:** 8.6% (vs 7.3% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 8,154 crore as of Sept 30, 2026.
*   **L1 Pipeline:** Rs 1,583 crore.
*   **Net Worth:** Rs 1,276 crore (post-IPO).
*   **Total Debt:** Rs 885 crore.
*   **Cash & Bank Balance:** Rs 535 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Execution:** Strong momentum across landmark projects (Sondwa Lift Irrigation, Sidhi Bansagar, Gandhi Sagar, Bishrampur Mining).
*   **IPO Impact:** Successfully raised Rs 258 crore via fresh issue; debt-to-equity improved to 0.71x from 0.97x.
*   **Operational Strategy:** Asset-light model; increasing focus on private sector clients (targeting 35% mix).
*   **Labor:** Currently employing ~12,000 laborers; mitigating scarcity via precast factories in Ahmedabad and at project sites.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Targeting ~20% or more growth for the full year; expects Rs 4,300–4,400 crore revenue for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management guides for stable EBITDA margins of 14%–15%.
*   **Order Book:** Targeting Rs 10,000 crore order book by year-end; aims to maintain order book-to-revenue ratio >2.5x.
*   **Order Inflow:** Expects Rs 6,000 crore of new orders for the full year (including L1).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Core Focus:** Water infrastructure (irrigation, water supply, WTP, desalination) remains the primary focus (80% of revenue).
*   **New Opportunities:** Seeing demand in inter-river linking, data centers (water requirements), and nuclear power plants.
*   **Government Schemes:** Strong thrust on Jal Jeevan Mission and state irrigation schemes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Asset-light model, increased purchasing power (A-rating benefits), and faster-than-scheduled execution.
*   **Inflation Protection:** All contracts are secured with price escalation clauses (linked to WPI or "belt star rates").
*   **Capex:** Invested Rs 30 crore in H1; expects another Rs 30 crore in H2. Sustainable annual capex is Rs 50–60 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Days stood at 123 days (vs 99 days previously) due to milestone-related billing at quarter-end; expects to normalize to 90–100 days by year-end.
*   **Receivables:** Trade receivables at Rs 674 crore; management expects significant realization in Q3.
*   **Mobilization Advance:** Rs 29 crore as of Sept 30, 2026; company only accepts interest-free advances.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Working Capital:** Temporary spike in working capital days and receivables due to quarter-end billing cycles.
*   **Cash Composition:** Cash balance includes IPO proceeds and margin money for bank guarantees (not 100% free cash).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did EBITDA margins spike this quarter?** A: Management attributed it to a favorable project mix and faster execution, but reiterated that 14-15% is the sustainable range.
*   **Q: Are you entering new sectors like STP/ETP?** A: Focus is currently on irrigation, water supply, WTP, and desalination; STP/ETP are future considerations but not immediate priorities.
*   **Q: How do you secure private sector orders?** A: Unlike government tenders (L1), private sector work (Adani, Reliance, Tata) is won based on technical expertise and past performance.
*   **Q: Is the current unutilized limit sufficient for growth?** A: Yes, management confirmed 38% unutilized non-fund-based limits are sufficient for the projected growth.
*   **Q: Why is the debtor cycle higher?** A: Management explained it is a timing issue related to month-end certification and billing, with realization occurring in the following month.

### One-line takeaway
LCC Projects delivered strong Q2 growth and margin expansion, backed by a robust Rs 8,154 crore order book and a clear strategy to maintain 14-15% margins while scaling revenue by ~20%.
