# PNC Infratech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed – Strong revenue growth and successful legal stay on debarment are offset by concerns regarding order book growth, competitive intensity, and the cancellation of the BESS project.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,146 crore (+17% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 138 crore (12.1% margin).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 73 crore (6.3% margin).
*   **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** Rs 2,664 crore (+26% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 513 crore (19.2% margin).
*   **Unexecuted Order Book:** >Rs 18,000 crore.
*   **Standalone Net Debt:** Rs 1,312 crore (Net debt-to-equity of 0.21x).

### Highlights
*   **Legal:** Delhi High Court granted a stay on NHAI debarment orders; the company is actively participating in new bids.
*   **Arbitration:** Received an arbitration award of Rs 244 crore (Sonauli to Gorakhpur section); management stated this is not yet booked in revenue as it awaits realization.
*   **BESS Project:** The NHPC Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project was cancelled due to non-signing of the Power Sale Agreement (PSA) within the stipulated time; bid security was refunded.
*   **International Expansion:** Incorporated a subsidiary in Kazakhstan to target road infrastructure; submitted a bid for a road project in Georgia.
*   **Payments:** Realized Rs 267 crore in pending water payments and Rs 183 crore from the Andhra Pradesh government for irrigation projects.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue:** Maintained at Rs 6,000 crore.
*   **FY28 Revenue:** Maintained at Rs 7,500 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained at 12% (excluding one-time arbitration claims).
*   **Order Inflow:** Maintained guidance of Rs 12,000–15,000 crore for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Highway sector showing recovery; NHAI awarded ~230 route km in Q2 vs 107 route km in Q1.
*   Management identified a pipeline of 80 projects worth ~Rs 1,80,000 crore (35 EPC, 25 HAM, 20 BOT/TBCB) to be bid over the next 2-3 months.
*   Competition remains high in EPC/smaller projects; management expects less intense competition in large-scale, fund-based projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Input costs (diesel/bitumen) remain elevated due to the West Asia conflict.
*   Partial escalation coverage exists via MoRTH/NHAI mechanisms, but management noted persistent pressure on operating margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex Guidance:** Rs 200 crore (H1 spent Rs 155 crore).
*   **FY28 Capex Guidance:** Rs 150 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash & Bank Balance (Standalone):** Rs 1,252 crore.
*   **Equity Commitment:** Rs 913 crore remaining for 14 HAM projects, to be invested over the next 2-3 years.
*   **Working Capital:** Retention money Rs 272 crore; Unbilled revenue Rs 490 crore; HAM debtors Rs 418 crore; Water debtors Rs 786 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Input cost inflation impacting margins.
*   Execution delays in new HAM projects (land acquisition).
*   Competitive bidding intensity in the road sector.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Parikshit Kandpal (Dolat Capital):* Questioned growth strategy given the BESS cancellation and competitive intensity. Management defended the strategy, citing a robust pipeline and core competency in roads.
*   *Sarvesh Gupta:* Asked about potential buybacks given the stock price. Management confirmed they are "thinking in the same line."
*   *Deepak Purswani:* Asked about international strategy. Management clarified they are focusing on non-fund-based (EPC) road projects funded by multilateral agencies (World Bank/ADB) to minimize risk.
*   *Vaibhav Shah:* Asked if the NHAI debarment stay is temporary. Management expressed confidence that the relief would continue beyond the next hearing on November 26th.
*   *Vishal Periwal:* Questioned why standalone debt increased despite high cash levels. Management explained it is for equipment finance and short-term working capital requirements.

### One-line takeaway
PNC Infratech maintains its growth guidance and remains focused on its core road business, leveraging a strong order book and improved liquidity, while cautiously exploring international EPC opportunities.
