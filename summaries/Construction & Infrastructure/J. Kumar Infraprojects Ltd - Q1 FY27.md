# J. Kumar Infraprojects Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust order inflows, strong balance sheet resilience, and an optimistic growth trajectory despite near-term execution headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 1,511 crore (up 2% YoY from Rs 1,484 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 215 crore (down 1% YoY from Rs 217 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 14.1% (compared to 14.6% in Q1 FY26)
- PAT: Rs 97 crore (down 6% YoY from Rs 103 crore in Q1 FY26)
- PAT margin: 6.4% (compared to 7% in Q1 FY26)
- Net debt: Negative Rs 45 crore as of June 30, 2026
- Net working capital days: 103 days (compared to 99 days for FY26)
- Total order book: Rs 22,246 crore as of June 30, 2026 (Comprising elevated corridors and flyovers 48%, roads and road tunnels 20%, metro projects elevated and underground 9%, others 23%)
- Order inflows booked: ~Rs 5,500 crore to date in FY27; additional L1 of Rs 1,500 crore (Delhi Metro underground)
- Capex done in Q1 FY27: Rs 34 crore

### Highlights
- Execution during the quarter was tempered by external factors, including the US-Iran war and BMC water usage restrictions at construction sites.
- Chennai project (NHAI packages worth Rs 3,570 crore) execution is at ~20%, expected completion extended to December 2028 due to Cooum river restrictions; prolongation costs were successfully recovered from NHAI with no impact on margins. State government package (Rs 580 crore) is ~65% complete and targeted for completion by March 2027.
- Anand Nagar project (Mulund) completion is targeted for October 2028; 3 km stretch issue sorted out, execution is at ~15%.
- GMLR project is in full swing with first TBM ready for launch; internal target to complete by June 29 to December 2029 (recovering from initial Supreme Court tree-cutting delays which pushed original client timelines).
- Vadhvan port project mobilization in full swing, with actual excavation/heavy work starting post-monsoon in October.
- Gross debt-to-equity stands at 0.24; gross debt is Rs 840 crore (primarily due to TBM purchase and Chennai project term loans). Fund-based limit utilized at 39%, non-fund-based limit utilized at 65%.
- Vizag land monetization deal done for Rs 180 crore for 30 acres; money expected in Q2/Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue growth guidance: ~15% over last year's top line (~Rs 6,500 crore).
- FY27 order inflow guidance: Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 10,000 crore (already at ~Rs 5,500 crore + Rs 1,500 crore L1).
- EBITDA margin guidance: 14% to 15% for the year, with a long-term intent to improve by 50-100 bps.
- Capex guidance: Rs 150 crore per year for the next 2 years (including Rs 100 crore maintenance capex and balance for ongoing projects like GMLR/Chennai).
- Previous top-line target of Rs 7,500 crore is now shifted to FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust bid pipeline with Rs 50,000 crore to Rs 1 lakh crore worth of jobs expected to be explored in the next 9-12 months (including MSRDC Shaktipeeth corridors, MMRDA Uttan-Virar corridor worth Rs 15,000-16,000 crore, NHAI, flyovers, and DMRC contracts).
- Management emphasized a strict focus on margin-accretive bidding over mere top-line chasing.
- Cash flows from clients like DMRC, MMRDA, MSRDC, BMC, and NHAI are prompt, unlike issues seen in PWD/irrigation sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Moderation in Q1 margins attributed to timing factors and the evolving mix of projects under execution.
- Other income increased to Rs 19 crore due to deployment of surplus cash in debt securities and FD margins maintained for non-fund-based facilities; management expects this to be recurring.
- Effective tax rate was higher at 29.7% in Q1, but expected to normalize towards last year's levels once the Rs 300 crore TBM is capitalized and provides tax shelter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gross block was backed by Rs 300 crore CWIP in TBM machinery as of March 2026, which is set to be capitalized upon launch.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt is negative Rs 45 crore.
- Gross debt increased to Rs 840 crore due to term loans for TBMs and Chennai project capex; expected to reduce below Rs 800 crore by the end of FY27.
- Working capital days maintained at 103 days; expected to hover between 100 to 110 days.
- Interest-bearing mobilization advances stand at Rs 470 crore at a borrowing rate of 8.5% to 10%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary execution headwinds from BMC water usage restrictions (now resolved).
- Delays in land acquisition, tree-cutting permissions, and GAD finalizations in large ticket-size projects (like GMLR, Vadhvan, and VDCR).
- Monsoon impacts on earthwork/tunnelling activities (e.g., Vadhvan).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vedant Kabra (Investor):** Asked why FY26/Q1 FY27 revenue was flat despite a strong order book, and what operational changes ensure the 15% growth target is met.
   * *Management answer:* Explained that execution delays were driven by external conception-stage bottlenecks like tree-cutting permissions, land acquisition, and GAD finalizations for large projects (GMLR, Chennai, VDCR) which are now resolving; reiterated confidence in 15% growth for FY27.
2. **Shravan Shah (Investment Analyst):** Inquired if the gross debt of Rs 840 crore will remain elevated and about Vizag monetization timings.
   * *Management answer:* Gross debt rose due to final TBM payments and will taper down as term loans are repaid; Vizag land deal (Rs 180 crore) is expected to materialize in Q2/Q3.
3. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial):** Asked about the execution timeline and status of the Chennai project.
   * *Management answer:* State project is 65% complete (target March 2027); NHAI packages are at 20% execution (target December 2028), with prolongation costs successfully recovered from the client with zero margin impact.
4. **Chandramouli (Retail Investor):** Suggested a share buyback given the net cash balance and low market cap.
   * *Management answer:* Acknowledged it is lucrative and on their cards, but currently prioritizing capital deployment for ongoing project capex.
5. **Dinesh Karwa (Kirti Creation):** Questioned whether recent non-Maharashtra orders (like stadiums) would impact margins.
   * *Management answer:* Stated they evaluate Pan-India orders strictly based on threshold margins; if margins are not met, they prefer a flat top line over unprofitable expansion.

### One-line takeaway
J. Kumar Infraprojects remains focused on margin-disciplined execution and a robust Rs 22,246 crore order book, guiding for 15% revenue growth in FY27 despite temporary execution lags from external clearances.
