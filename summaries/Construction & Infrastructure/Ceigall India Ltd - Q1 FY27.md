# Ceigall India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy operational and financial performance with revenue growth, margin improvement, and strategic asset monetization.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 901 crore (up 10.2% YoY from Rs 818 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 121 crore (up from Rs 94 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 13.4% (up from 11.4% in Q1 FY26)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 75 crore (up from Rs 56 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Standalone PAT Margin:** 8.4% (up from 6.8% in Q1 FY26)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 970 crore (up 15.7% YoY from Rs 838 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Order Book:** Rs 18,568 crores as on June 30, 2026 (comprising 19 EPC, 10 HAM, 1 DBFOT, and 9 tariff-based renewable and transmission projects)
- **Capex done in Q1:** Close to Rs 14 crore
- **Equity invested in Q1:** Rs 23 crore (cumulative HAM and solar)
- **Historical/Projected Equity Commitments:** IPO equity put was Rs 253 crore; current stands at Rs 692 crore (additional Rs 439 crore put); balance equity commitment for FY27 is Rs 859 crore (Rs 310 crore solar, Rs 550 crore highway/HAM); FY28 is Rs 744 crore (Rs 296 crore solar, Rs 444 crore HAM)

### Highlights
- Successfully monetized the first HAM asset via divestment of the Malout-Abohar-Sadhuwali project, validating the capital recycling strategy.
- Signed PPA for a Solar BESS at Morena and emerged as L1 for a standalone battery storage project.
- Signed the concession agreement for the Ambala-Chandigarh-Zirakpur HAM project.
- Received appointed dates post quarter-end for VRK 11, VRK 12, and the Indore-Ujjain Greenfield HAM project.
- Emerged as L1 bidder in a JV for a highway project in Arunachal.
- Diversified order book across highways, expressways, metro rail, renewable energy, T&D, industrial infrastructure, and specialized projects.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth Guidance:** Management guided for a minimum of 15% revenue growth for FY27.
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Management maintained the full-year EBITDA margin guidance at 11% to 12.5%.
- **Capex Guidance:** Full-year capex guidance at Rs 30 crore to Rs 35 crore.
- **Order Inflow Guidance:** Targeting around Rs 6,000 crores of order inflows for the year (management noted ~Rs 600 crores achieved so far).
- **Execution Target:** Targeting 20% to 25% execution for VRK 11 and VRK 12 projects in FY27, and around 15% for Southern Ludhiana due to land availability challenges (62% available).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 is generally a seasonally softer quarter for construction due to the onset of monsoons in several parts of the country.
- Government's continued focus on energy, transmission, distribution, transportation, and urban infrastructure provides positive momentum.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin expanded YoY to 13.4% in Q1 FY27, driven by the start of new projects (Maharashtra solar MH1 and MH2, Indore-Ujjain HAM).
- Depreciation fell due to assets sold in the previous financial year and the WDV depreciation method.
- Other income decreased due to no royalty charges currently and lower interest returns following 100% utilization of IPO proceeds by the end of FY26 (FD balance stands at close to Rs 320 crores).
- Commercial papers of up to Rs 100 crores approved on a private placement basis to carve out from working capital limits, lowering finance costs (ROI of 6.8% to 7% vs WCDL rate of 7.5% to 7.8%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q1 capex was close to Rs 14 crores (primarily for a launcher purchased in Danapur). From IPO proceeds, over Rs 100 crores worth of machinery was paid for.
- International expansion: Being very conservative due to the war situation, currently bidding for a few projects in Romania and Dubai while prioritizing a robust domestic order book.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital intensity is expected to improve in FY27 due to government relaxations, and improve further in FY28.
- Decline in consolidated ROE in FY26 was primarily a function of the rise in equity share capital post-IPO/QIP (equity share capital at end of FY26 was close to Rs 2,098 crores).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Land availability challenge in Southern Ludhiana (only 62% land available).
- Geopolitical/war situation makes management conservative regarding international markets (Romania and Dubai).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial) asked about the lower execution in Northern Ayodhya bypass in Q1.** Management replied that execution is proceeding steadily and hitting milestones before time, and variations are normal based on milestone timing and monsoon rains.
2. **Mahesh Patil (ICICI Securities) asked about the margin sustainability given Q1 beat guidance.** Management stated that while new projects commenced, they maintain their full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 11% to 12.5%.
3. **Parth Thakkar (JM Financial) asked about the expected Appointed Dates for Bihar and Punjab HAM projects.** Management stated Bihar Financial Closure (FC) is due next month and Punjab around November, expecting ADs by Q3/Q4.
4. **An individual investor (Yash Parkar) asked about the rationale behind issuing commercial papers.** Management explained it is carved out of working capital limits to reduce finance costs (saving on interest rates vs WCDL) and increase market visibility.
5. **Yash Parkar asked about the drop in consolidated ROE despite stable ROCE.** Management clarified it is purely a function of the expanded equity base post-IPO and QIP capital raises.

### One-line takeaway
Ceigall India delivered a resilient Q1 FY27 with 10.2% standalone revenue growth and margin expansion, supported by solid execution visibility, successful asset monetization, and strategic diversification into renewable energy and T&D sectors.
