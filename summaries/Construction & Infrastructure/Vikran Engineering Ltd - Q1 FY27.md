# Vikran Engineering Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong standalone growth (28% revenue, 212% PAT), successful commissioning of key projects, and a clear execution roadmap for the NOPL solar project.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 204 crore (+28.2% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 28 crore (+23.7% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 13.7%.
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 17.5 crore (+209.9% YoY).
*   **Standalone PAT Margin:** 8.6%.
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 141.6 crore.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 11.3 crore (8% margin).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 4 crore (2.8% margin).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 6,496 crore (62% Solar EPC, 28% Power T&D, 10% Water).

### Highlights
*   Acquired 100% equity in NOPL Solar Projects Private Limited; realigned 969 MW AC solar project in Maharashtra (EPC value: Rs 3,518 crore).
*   Commissioned 132 KV Miao-Namsai transmission line in Arunachal Pradesh.
*   Secured Rs 530 crore orders from MSEDCL and Rs 120 crore from Power Grid.
*   Management advised investors to focus on standalone financials until the NOPL project construction phase concludes.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets FY27 revenue execution of Rs 2,200 crore – Rs 2,500 crore.
*   Expects Rs 1,500 crore revenue from NOPL project in FY27 (Rs 100 crore in Sept, Rs 1,400 crore in H2).
*   Targeting at least one data center infrastructure order by end of FY27.
*   Management expects to be cash positive by end of FY27 upon commissioning 650 MW of NOPL.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Power T&D benefiting from rising power demand and renewable energy integration.
*   Solar EPC is the largest order book contributor.
*   Middle East project negotiations delayed; focusing on domestic opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Consolidated margins impacted by Rs 6.5 crore provision for water division (Jal Jeevan Mission) and overheads on NOPL project without corresponding revenue recognition in consolidation.
*   Management maintains 14-17% EBITDA margin guidance for EPC business.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   NOPL project: 45 MW commissioned; 15 MW ready for commissioning; 240 MW in advanced stages.
*   Project funding for NOPL (Rs 4,000 crore cost) expected to be 75:25 debt-equity; final sanction expected in Q2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Debtor days at 296; management expects this to be the peak and to decline.
*   Rs 120 crore of debtors linked to Jal Jeevan Mission; Rs 23 crore recovered in Q1.
*   Management claims no impact on future working capital requirements for Jal Jeevan as investment is capped.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Seasonality in EPC business (H1 typically lower).
*   Jal Jeevan Mission project receivables (though management notes recovery has started).
*   Potential for project slippages between quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Ankit Madhwani (Steptrade Capital):** Questioned revenue growth vs. order book size. Management cited seasonality and H2-heavy execution.
*   **Sidhaant Lodaya:** Asked about the gap between standalone and consolidated revenue. Management explained NOPL consolidation mechanics and advised focusing on standalone.
*   **Mahesh Kowshik:** Asked about Jal Jeevan receivables. Management stated they are capped and recovery has begun.
*   **Myra Mittal:** Asked about NOPL financing. Management confirmed final sanction expected in Q2.
*   **Sandeep Majhi:** Asked about the purpose of separate entities. Management explained it facilitates future asset monetization (InvIT/IPO).

### One-line takeaway
Vikran Engineering is pivoting toward a developer-EPC model with the NOPL solar acquisition, prioritizing standalone performance while managing working capital and aiming for cash positivity by FY27-end.
