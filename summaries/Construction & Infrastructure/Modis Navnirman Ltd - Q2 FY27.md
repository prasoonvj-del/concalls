# Modis Navnirman Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth alongside improved margins and robust sales momentum.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** INR 69.38 crores (up from INR 58.26 crores in Q1 FY27)
- **Total income (Q2 FY27):** INR 69.54 crores
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 14.50 crores (up 25% QoQ from INR 11.63 crores in Q1 FY27)
- **EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 20.96% (up from 20.00% in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 10.54 crores (up from INR 8.54 crores in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT margin (Q2 FY27):** 15.16% (up from 14.54% in Q1 FY27)
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** INR 127.64 crores (up 53% YoY from INR 83.39 crores in H1 FY26)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 26.19 crores (up 63% YoY from INR 16.05 crores in H1 FY26)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 19.08 crores (up 59% YoY from INR 12.00 crores in H1 FY26)
- **Sales volume (H1 FY27):** ~78,560 square feet
- **Sales value (H1 FY27):** INR 202 crores
- **Per square foot realization:** INR 25,724
- **Sales book / revenue visibility (As of Sept 30, 2026):** INR 202 crores (ongoing projects), INR 156 crores yet to be recognized from committed sales, INR 505 crores estimated potential value from unsold units; total ~INR 661 crores across current projects.
- **Portfolio scale:** 25 projects (~29.83 lakh sq ft total); 14 completed (7.22 lakh sq ft), 6 ongoing (12.11 lakh sq ft), 5 upcoming (~10.50 lakh sq ft).
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow:** Negative INR 16.51 crores (driven by inventory build-up).

### Highlights
- Delivered strong YoY growth across revenue (+53%), EBITDA (+63%), and PAT (+59%) in H1 FY27.
- Initiated two new projects in H1 FY27: Rashmi Icon and Rashmi Avenue.
- Festive demand post-Navratri has been strong, supporting expectations for a solid Q3 FY27.
- Management plans to launch Rashmi Paradise around October 30, 2026, targeting realizations of INR 27,000 to INR 30,000 per sq ft.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets procuring GDV upwards of INR 500 crores every year.
- Expects to turn cash flow positive by FY28.
- Targeting completion of major projects (including Rashmi Square, Rashmi Signature, Rashmi Manorath, and Rashmi Delight) before March 31, 2027, with expected completion revenue in H2 of approximately INR 200 crores.
- EBITDA margins expected to remain on an upward trajectory.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Festive demand picked up post-Navratri; Q3 FY27 expected to be stronger than FY26.
- Mumbai market has strong appetite for supply despite multiple developers operating.
- Redevelopment focus remains on housing society partnerships rather than MHADA/SRA land acquisitions, keeping the model asset-light regarding land capex.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Major cost components are government statutory approvals and construction costs.
- Raw material price stabilization and project completion stages expected to support better EBITDA margins going forward.
- Margins vary slightly by location and ready reckoner rates, but blended margins remain healthy.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company remains debt-free and plans to fund all 5 upcoming projects internally without raising debt.
- Micro-market presence includes Kandivali, Borivali, Malad, Goregaon, Dahisar, and Khar.
- Upcoming projects include Rashmi Paradise (Malad), Rashmi Sheetal (Borivali), Rashmi Royale (Kandivali), Rashmi Aurea (Khar), and Govind Dalvi Nagar. All 5 upcoming projects are residential-cum-commercial.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is debt-free. Management stated no debt will be required for the 5 upcoming projects or the annual INR 500+ crore GDV additions.
- Reported negative operating cash flow in H1 FY27 due to capital deployed into inventory build-up; expected to be cash flow positive by FY28.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Negative operating cash flow reported in H1 FY27 due to capital allocation toward project inventory.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jahnvi Shah (Share India Securities) asked about the negative operating cash flow despite healthy profits.** Management explained it is due to inventory build-up across projects and expects cash flows to turn positive by FY28 as projects near completion.
- **Unmish Shah (Moneywisars) inquired about the drivers behind the 273% sales value growth and commercial vs. residential realizations.** Management stated growth was driven by Rashmi Signature and the launch bookings of Rashmi Icon and Rashmi Avenue; commercial projects enable faster realization cycles (6-12 months) compared to pure residential.
- **Rajinder Thakkar (Individual Investor) asked about new project agreements and yields in Khar vs. Goregaon-Borivali.** Management noted Rashmi Aurea is in Khar, Rashmi Sheetal in Borivali, Rashmi Royale in Kandivali, and Rashmi Paradise in Malad, with blended margins expected to remain similar.
- **Myra Mittal (Mittal Investments) asked about competition from established vs. regional developers.** Management responded that they compete with large developers on larger land parcels (>5,000 sq meters) in western suburbs while remaining selective on locations and yields.
- **Manaswani (Oracle Investments) questioned potential cost overruns or execution delays.** Management affirmed there are no delays or cost overruns; all projects are proceeding according to committed timelines.

### One-line takeaway
Modis Navnirman delivered robust H1 FY27 growth (Revenue +53%, PAT +59%) backed by strong sales velocity, zero debt, and a self-funded redevelopment pipeline exceeding INR 500 crores in annual GDV potential.
