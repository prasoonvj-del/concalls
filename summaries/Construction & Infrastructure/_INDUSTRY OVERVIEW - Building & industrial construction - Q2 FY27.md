# Construction & Infrastructure - Building & Industrial Construction: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector presents a dichotomy in Q2 FY27: while top-line execution remains robust—with several companies reporting record-high quarterly revenues—bottom-line performance is being squeezed by raw material volatility and working capital pressures. Companies focused on industrial, redevelopment, and specialized infrastructure (data centers, cleanrooms) are seeing strong demand, but geopolitical tensions and commodity price spikes (particularly steel) are creating margin headwinds. While some players like **Sugs Lloyd**, **PSP Projects**, and **NBCC** are delivering aggressive growth, others like **Afcons** and **Sterling & Wilson** are navigating transitional challenges or liquidity constraints.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is broad agreement on strong demand across industrial, warehousing, data centers, and renewable energy sectors. **Interarch**, **Fabtech**, and **Epack Prefab** all highlighted traction in high-growth areas like semiconductors and data centers.
*   **Divergence:** While the order pipeline is universally described as healthy, there is a divergence in execution confidence. **NCC** and **Afcons** flagged uneven award pacing and execution constraints (geopolitics, logistics, and delayed certifications). Conversely, **Sugs Lloyd** and **PSP Projects** are aggressively scaling, with **Sugs Lloyd** diversifying its order book geographically to reduce reliance on single-state projects.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **The Steel Headwind:** Rising steel prices (peaking near Rs 70,000/tonne, per **Epack Prefab**) are a universal concern. **Fabtech** and **Interarch** noted that raw material volatility pressured H1 margins.
*   **Mitigation Strategies:** There is a clear split in how companies manage this.
    *   **Pass-through:** **Capacite**, **LCC Projects**, **Interarch**, and **PSP Projects** (for non-Adani projects) rely on contractual escalation clauses to pass costs to clients. **PSP Projects** noted that ~75% of its order book (Adani projects) operates on a cost-plus model, insulating it from material risks.
    *   **Margin Compression:** **Epack Prefab** and **Fabtech** admitted that input costs rose faster than they could pass them on, leading to temporary margin compression.
*   **Startup Costs:** **Interarch** noted that margins are temporarily suppressed by startup expenses for new verticals (exports and heavy structures), which they expect to normalize over time.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Expansion:** The trend remains expansionary. **Interarch** has spent over Rs 250 crore on capex in two years, and **Fabtech** added a 50,000 sqm Hyderabad plant. **Epack Prefab** is commissioning new units in Gujarat.
*   **Asset-Light Preference:** **LCC Projects** and **Modis Navnirman** continue to favor asset-light models, with **Modis Navnirman** remaining debt-free and funding upcoming projects internally.
*   **Utilization:** **PSP Projects** noted its precast facility is at full capacity, requiring only minor future capex.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance:** **NBCC** maintained its consolidated revenue target of Rs 16,000-17,000 crore. **Afcons** held back full-year guidance due to geopolitical uncertainties, while **NCC** maintained a conservative guidance despite a strong H1. **Capacite** reaffirmed its FY27 revenue guidance.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Afcons** reported a shift to an automatic bucket-based ECL (Expected Credit Loss) matrix, impacting provisions. **Capacite** successfully reduced pledged shares, targeting complete removal by March 2027.

## Company one-liners
*   **Afcons:** Reported weak H1 due to delayed execution and stretched collections, but remains confident in H2 recovery.
*   **Brahmaputra:** No financial data provided; filing was only an earnings call intimation.
*   **Capacite:** Delivered record Q2 revenue of Rs 771 crore, maintaining FY28 revenue targets.
*   **Epack Prefab:** Achieved record H1 revenue but faced margin pressure from steel costs and negative operating cash flows.
*   **Fabtech:** Revenue jumped 75% YoY with a doubled order book, prioritizing market share over short-term margins.
*   **Interarch:** Stable H1 performance with 10.7% growth and a record Rs 2,000+ crore order book.
*   **LCC Projects:** Strong 34% YoY growth and margin expansion, supported by an asset-light model.
*   **Modis Navnirman:** Debt-free developer with 53% revenue growth and strong sales momentum.
*   **NBCC:** Strong growth driven by redevelopment and land monetization; maintained FY27 guidance.
*   **NCC:** Highest-ever Q2 revenue; management maintains conservative full-year guidance despite strong H1.
*   **PSP Projects:** Record H1 revenue with 58% YoY growth in Q2, backed by robust execution in Adani projects.
*   **Sterling & Wilson:** Subdued H1 due to project transitions, but expects H2 execution pickup.
*   **Sugs Lloyd:** Strongest half-year ever with 73% revenue growth and a diversified Rs 1,067 crore order book.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Working Capital/Collections:** Monitoring if the "stretched liquidity" flagged by **Afcons** and the "delayed bill certifications" noted by **PSP Projects** and **Epack Prefab** persist.
*   **Steel Price Impact:** Whether the margin compression seen in **Epack Prefab** and **Fabtech** stabilizes as they pass on costs or if prices remain elevated.
*   **Delhi/NCR Execution:** The impact of GRAP/AQI restrictions on projects in the Delhi region for **Capacite**, **Epack Prefab**, and **NBCC**.
*   **Order Conversion:** The pace at which the massive order books (e.g., **Interarch**, **NCC**, **PSP**) convert into actual revenue in H2.
