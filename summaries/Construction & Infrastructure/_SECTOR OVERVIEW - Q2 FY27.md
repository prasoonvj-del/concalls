# Construction & Infrastructure: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Construction & Infrastructure sector presents a mixed narrative characterized by healthy revenue growth and robust order books on one hand, and margin compression from rising input costs (especially steel and energy), stretched working capital, and delayed project execution fronts on the other. While companies with specialized or diversified models (such as ArisInfra, LCC Projects, Modis Navnirman, and Oriental Rail) reported strong YoY earnings growth, heavy EPC players (Afcons Infrastructure, PNC Infratech, Epack Prefab) faced headwinds from geopolitical tensions in West Asia, logistics bottlenecks, and inventory/receivable build-ups. 

---

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that underlying domestic infrastructure and construction demand remains structurally sound, with active pipelines in roads, water, railways, and industrial warehousing.
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   *Roads & Highways:* PNC Infratech notes a recovery in NHAI awards (230 route km in Q2 vs 107 route km in Q1), but flags high competitive intensity in smaller/EPC road projects.
    *   *Water & Irrigation:* LCC Projects highlights continued momentum in water infrastructure (Jal Jeevan Mission and state irrigation), while Afcons flags stretched payments and collections specifically in the U.P. Jal Jeevan Mission.
    *   *Real Estate & Housing:* Modis Navnirman and ArisInfra report strong urban residential demand and festive sales velocity, with Modis noting a preference for housing society redevelopment to keep land capex asset-light.

---

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Raw Material Pressures:** Surging steel prices—driven by Middle East conflicts and safeguard import duties—compressed margins for companies like Epack Prefab (where gross margins fell ~2.3% in H1) and Oriental Rail, which had to increase advance raw material procurement. Afcons and PNC Infratech also cited persistent input cost inflation and energy availability challenges impacting logistics.
*   **Pass-Through & Mitigation:** Epack Prefab and ArisInfra noted that short-cycle purchase orders or dynamic pricing allow them to pass on raw material spikes. LCC Projects highlighted that all its contracts are secured with WPI or "belt star rate" price escalation clauses.

---

## Capex & capacity
*   **Afcons:** Maintaining CapEx guidance at Rs 700 crore for FY27 and Rs 650 crore for FY28.
*   **ArisInfra:** Zero capital investments planned for FY26 or FY27, maintaining an asset-light model where partner capacity expanded to 11.7 million tonnes.
*   **Epack Prefab:** Commissioned Unit 5 at Ghiloth and a new sandwich panel line; Gujarat plant on track for Q4 FY27 (taking total potential revenue capacity to Rs 2,800–3,000 crore); no new capex needed for FY27-FY28.
*   **LCC Projects:** Invested Rs 30 crore in H1, expects another Rs 30 crore in H2 (sustainable annual capex of Rs 50–60 crore).
*   **Modis Navnirman:** Funding all 5 upcoming residential-cum-commercial projects internally with zero debt.
*   **PNC Infratech:** CapEx guidance set at Rs 200 crore for FY27 (H1 spent Rs 155 crore) and Rs 150 crore for FY28.

---

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Afcons Infrastructure:** Withheld full-year revenue/growth guidance due to geopolitical and energy issues in Bangladesh, but reinstated FY28 guidance (20%-25% growth over FY26).
*   **PNC Infratech:** Successfully obtained a legal stay from the Delhi High Court on NHAI debarment orders, allowing active participation in new bids; however, the NHPC BESS project was cancelled due to non-signing of the PSA.

## Company one-liners
*   **Afcons Infrastructure:** Weak H1 due to delayed execution and stretched collections, but management expects stronger execution and cash flows in H2 alongside Rs 30,000 crore inflows.
*   **ArisInfra Solutions:** Delivered a strong Q2 with 28% YoY revenue growth and 11.6% EBITDA margins via high-margin Contract Manufacturing and DaaS mix.
*   **Brahmaputra Infrastructure:** Regulatory intimation filing only, containing no financial results or operational data.
*   **Epack Prefab Technologies:** Record H1 revenues countered by steel-led margin compression and stretched working capital cycles expected to normalize in H2.
*   **LCC Projects:** Delivered strong Q2 growth and margin expansion, backed by an Rs 8,154 crore order book and sustainable 14–15% margins.
*   **Modis Navnirman:** Robust H1 growth supported by zero debt, high sales velocity, and self-funded redevelopment projects exceeding INR 500 crores in annual GDV potential.
*   **Oriental Rail Infrastructure:** 29% YoY revenue growth supported by a Rs 1,571 crore order book while scaling wagon production toward 600 units/quarter.
*   **PNC Infratech:** Maintained growth guidance and core road focus, leveraging a legal stay on debarment and healthy liquidity while exploring international EPC opportunities.

---

## What to watch next quarter
*   Execution pick-up and cash flow realization (especially from U.P. Jal Jeevan Mission for Afcons and milestone billings for Epack Prefab and LCC Projects).
*   Conversion of L1 pipelines and new order inflows (Afcons' Rs 30,000 crore target, LCC's Rs 6,000 crore target, and PNC's Rs 12,000–15,000 crore target).
*   Steel price stabilization and its impact on gross margins for PEB and railway component manufacturers (Epack Prefab and Oriental Rail).
*   Progress on new project launches and execution timelines across Modis Navnirman’s redevelopment portfolio and ArisInfra’s DaaS GDV ramp-up.

*Based on: Afcons Infrastructure Ltd, ArisInfra Solutions Ltd, Brahmaputra Infrastructure Ltd, Epack Prefab Technologies Ltd, LCC Projects Ltd, Modis Navnirman Ltd, Oriental Rail Infrastructure Ltd, PNC Infratech Ltd*
