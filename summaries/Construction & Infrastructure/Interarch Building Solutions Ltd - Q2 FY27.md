### Highlights
- Commissioned AP Phase 1 and Gujarat plants; AP heavy steel structure plant became operational on 24th September 2026.
- Strategic partnership with ER Steel Canada for Open Web Steel Joist (OWSJ) systems with initial funding of Rs 50 crore.
- Record residual order book exceeding Rs 2,000 crore (~9 to 10 months of capacity).
- *Record Check:* The call confirms the commissioning of AP and Gujarat facilities, matching the company's recent record updates.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Management maintains FY27 revenue guidance of Rs 2,150 crore to Rs 2,200 crore.
- **Margin Outlook:** No major margin improvement expected in FY27 due to startup expenses for new verticals (exports and heavy structures) and high steel prices; expects EBITDA margins to reach 11% to 12% within two years and 14% to 15% in the long term.
- **Capacity Plans:** Aiming to add ~100,000 tonnes of salable capacity by March 2028, and reach ~400,000 tonnes by 2029.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains strong across manufacturing, logistics, warehousing, multi-storey buildings, data centers, semiconductors, and EV infrastructure.
- Export vertical expanding, particularly in the African continent and North America.
- Heavy structures (data centers, hospitals, hotels, commercial offices, airport hangars) seeing strong traction.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel prices at a 4-year high with supply chain disruptions.
- Shift towards variable pricing (complete pass-through); ~35% to 40% of the order book is currently on variable pricing, and recent orders are taken entirely on variable price terms.
- Erection and installation costs vary by project uniqueness and location, fully passed through to customers.
- Higher depreciation and lower interest income impacted PAT due to recent plant capitalizations and capex spends.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Spent over Rs 250 crore on capex over the last two years.
- Heavy structure capacity currently at 25,000 tonnes, targeted to reach 75,000 to 80,000 tonnes by the end of next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Healthy operating cash flows maintained through H1.
- QIP formalities completed; timing left to Ambit Capital at a 72-hour notice.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Erratic steel supply chains and high raw material price inflation.
- Startup expenses in new verticals (exports and heavy structures) temporarily suppressing margins ahead of revenue realization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Chintan Patel (PhillipCapital) on heavy structure margins:** Management explained that heavy structures carry better net margins and pass-through steel pricing, leading to better operational leverage.
2. **Rahul Kumar (Vaikarya Fund) on working capital increase:** Management attributed it to longer execution cycles of larger orders, stocking up on steel inventory due to supply chain volatility, and ramp-up of new plants.
3. **Akshay Kolekar (Dalal & Broacha) on FY27 revenue guidance:** Management reiterated confidence in achieving the Rs 2,150-2,200 crore revenue target driven by stronger H2 execution.
4. **Dhirendra Kumar (Spark PMS) on steel price pass-through:** Management stated that ~35%-40% of the order book is variable price, and recent orders are booked dynamically at current steel prices.
5. **Darshan (Electrum Portfolio) on variable pricing shift:** Management noted they are insisting entirely on variable pricing to remove steel price volatility risks.

### One-line takeaway
Interarch reported stable H1 FY27 financial performance with 10.7% revenue growth, backed by a record Rs 2,000+ crore order book and ongoing capacity expansions in heavy structures and exports.
