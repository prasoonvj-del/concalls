# RITES Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Performance was muted due to marginal revenue milestone slippages into Q3, though management remains confident in full-year guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained double-digit top-line growth and EBITDA/PAT margin red lines (18-20% and 15-16%).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more cautious regarding quarterly lumpiness and export slippages, but resilient on yearly outlook.
- New topics or risks: 8th Pay Commission (CDA) / 3rd Pay Commission (IDA) provisioning starting from Q4 effective January 1, 2027; NICC JV not materializing.
- Last quarter's promises: Bangladesh rake delivery slipped past Q2 by 10-12 days into Q3 (instead of Q2 recognition as expected last quarter).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated EBITDA margin:** 22%
- **Consolidated PAT margin:** 17%
- **Turnkey YoY increase:** ~Rs 70 crore
- **Export YoY dip:** ~Rs 60 crore (zero booking in Q2)
- **Bangladesh export order remaining:** 9 rakes (182 coaches; 18 coaches delivered in the first rake)
- **Mozambique export order:** 5 locomotives (INR 150 crore)
- **Sri Lanka export order:** $41,000,000 (~INR 400 crore) declared L1
- **REMCL renewable energy order book:** ~Rs 10 crore (7-8 orders)
- **Competitive fresh order inflow:** 70%+ across segments

### Highlights
- First Bangladesh rake delivered after a 10-12 day slide into Q3; subsequent rakes to accelerate.
- Declared L1 for Sri Lanka export order of ~Rs 400 crore.
- JV with NICC did not materialize and is currently on hold.
- Quality assurance vertical saw a 15%+ YoY revenue growth contribution, recovering due to business reengineering.
- REMCL signed an MOU with ISA for international consultancy and secured 7-8 renewable energy orders.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year double-digit top-line growth guidance on a consolidated basis.
- EBITDA margin red line maintained at 18-20% (referred to as 20% in parts) and PAT margin at 15-16%.
- Target to maintain fresh order inflow of one order a day and reach an order book of Rs 10,000 crore.
- Mozambique locomotives delivery aimed by Q4, with the order finished latest by H1 of the coming FY.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Turnkey order book is young (12-18 months old) with execution picking up pace; management clarified RITES is not an EPC/construction company, and turnkey contracts are cost-plus with consultancy scope (accounted via P&L for client preference).
- Fresh order inflow is 70%+ on a competitive basis, shifting the overall order book mix away from nomination (now ~60%+ competitive vs ~37% nomination).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin mix impacted by higher turnkey contribution and zero export booking in Q2.
- 8th Pay Commission (CDA) and 3rd Pay Commission (IDA) provisions will start from Q4, creating a substantial hit effective January 1, 2027.
- Long-term EBITDA margins of 25%+ seen 5 years ago are not expected to return due to the structural shift toward competitive bidding.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No specific capex numbers discussed beyond low-capex consultancy model references.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or working capital figures stated.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Revenue milestones sliding by 10-15 days into subsequent quarters impacting quarterly performance.
- Substantial provisioning impact from upcoming Pay Commissions starting Q4.
- Margin compression due to global competitive bidding replacing nomination orders.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vishal Periwal (PL Capital) on Bangladesh/Mozambique Deliveries:** Management explained the first Bangladesh rake slipped 10-12 days into Q3 due to final checks, but subsequent production will accelerate; Mozambique first locomotive is aimed for Q4. Management answered clearly.
- **Priyankar Sarkar (Square 64 Capital Advisors) on Margin Sustainability:** Asked if 25%+ EBITDA margins can return. Management stated structural shift to competitive bidding makes 25%+ unlikely, but reiterated the 18-20% EBITDA and 15-16% PAT red lines. Management answered directly without dodging.
- **Meghal Patil (ANB Capital) on NICC JV & Business Model:** Asked about NICC JV status and turnkey vs consultancy identity. Management clarified the NICC JV is not materializing and is on hold, and reiterated RITES remains purely a consultancy company using turnkey only for accounting preference. Management answered clearly.
- **Parimal Mithani (Credential Investments) on JVs/MOUs:** Asked about DMV and other tie-ups. Management clarified these are MOUs rather than JVs, used to supplement capabilities. Management answered directly.

### One-line takeaway
Despite muted Q2 performance due to minor milestone slippages and upcoming pay commission hits, management remains confident in achieving double-digit top-line growth for FY27 while defending core margin red lines.
