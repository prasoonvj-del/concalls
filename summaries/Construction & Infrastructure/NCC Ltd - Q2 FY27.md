# NCC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever Q2 consolidated revenue with improved payment cycles, despite management maintaining a conservative full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 6,042 crore (up 32% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 4,957 crore (up 31% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 8.8% (vs 8.7% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 80,167 crore.
*   **New Order Inflow (H1):** Rs 11,108 crore.
*   **Standalone Net Debt:** Rs 2,293 crore.

### Highlights
*   Reported highest-ever second-quarter consolidated revenue in company history.
*   Order book is 3.5x trailing 12-month revenue, providing multi-year visibility.
*   Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) payments are improving; H1 receipts for JJM totaled Rs 1,720 crore.
*   Vizag receivable outstanding stands at Rs 270 crore; management expects recovery by Q4 FY27.
*   Smart meter project installation is 51% complete.
*   *Note:* The call confirms the financial results released on 2026-11-05; management maintained FY27 guidance despite strong H1 execution.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Guidance (Maintained):** Order inflow of Rs 22,000 crore–Rs 25,000 crore, revenue growth of 8%–10%, and EBITDA margin of 8.5%–9%.
*   **Capex Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 500 crore for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Bid pipeline remains at Rs 2.5 lakh crore.
*   Project execution continues to be influenced by fund allocation, client approvals, land availability, and utility shifting.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   81% of contracts contain escalation clauses to offset raw material price volatility.
*   Depreciation is expected to increase in H2 due to the capitalization of TBM (Tunnel Boring Machine) and capex for mining projects.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   H1 Capex: Rs 253 crore.
*   Mining project in Jharkhand (Amrapali) is capital-intensive; management indicated potential capex requirements for FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Standalone Net Debt:** Rs 2,293 crore (vs Rs 1,890 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Working Capital:** 96 days (Rs 5,532 crore).
*   **Trade Receivables:** Decreased to Rs 2,697 crore (53 days).
*   **Retention Money:** Rs 2,547 crore.
*   **Unbilled Revenue:** Rs 7,174 crore (37% of revenue).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Retention Money:** Remains high (5%-20% depending on contract type); management noted this will persist until contract completion or bank guarantee substitution.
*   **Stuck Investment:** Rs 108 crore investment in a Hyderabad project is currently held up in NCLT/RERA proceedings.
*   **Seasonality:** Q2 execution was impacted by monsoon.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Analyst:* Why maintain 8-10% revenue growth guidance despite strong H1? *Mgmt:* Guidance is for the full year; while H1 was strong, they prefer to stick to the original conservative estimate.
*   *Analyst:* Can retention money be reduced? *Mgmt:* It depends on the contract mix; some allow bank guarantee substitution, but retention will continue to be a feature of the business.
*   *Analyst:* Status of JJM payments? *Mgmt:* Improving; H1 receipts were Rs 1,720 crore; legacy JJM projects are expected to be substantially complete by March 2027.
*   *Analyst:* Depreciation outlook? *Mgmt:* Will increase in H2 due to TBM capitalization and mining capex.

### One-line takeaway
Strong execution and improved cash flows in H1, with management maintaining a conservative full-year outlook despite a robust order book.
