# NBCC (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong standalone revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth, supported by redevelopment execution and real estate monetization.

### Key numbers
- Standalone Revenue from Operations: Rs 1,823 crore (10% YoY increase)
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs 2,260 crore
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 160 crore (62% YoY increase from Rs 99 crore)
- Standalone EBITDA Margin: 8.77%
- Standalone Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 151 crore (32% YoY increase)
- Standalone Order Book: Rs 1,12,000 crore
- Consolidated Order Book: Rs 1,27,000 crore
- Consolidated Business Secured (Q1): Rs 1,600 crore
- Consolidated Tenders Awarded (Q1): Rs 1,700 crore
- Cash and Bank Balance: Rs 666 crore (as on June 30, 2026)

### Highlights
- Secured major projects in Q1: Schools in Odisha (Rs 253 crore), J&K campus (Rs 171 crore), and New Andhra Pradesh Bhavan in Delhi (Rs 106 crore).
- Secured projects in July/August: Housing project in Seychelles (Rs 620 crore), RBI residential/office complex in Amravati (Rs 780 crore), and science laboratories in Rajasthan schools (Rs 431 crore).
- Sold the entire Bharat Business Park under 7 GPRA projects, generating Rs 10,000 crore in 3 auctions within 3 months.
- Completed Phase 1 of the Amrapali project (23 of 24 projects completed, 24th ready); Phase 2 is in full swing.
- Board approved the merger of HSCC (wholly-owned subsidiary) into NBCC to create operational and financial synergies.
- Board approved in-principle incorporation of a wholly-owned subsidiary as an SPV for the proposed CPSE REIT.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 revenue target of Rs 15,000 to Rs 17,000 crore (specifically Rs 16,000 to Rs 17,000 crore on a consolidated basis; standalone around Rs 13,000 to Rs 14,000 crore).
- FY28 revenue guidance: Around Rs 21,000 crore.
- FY29 revenue guidance: Rs 24,000 to Rs 25,000 crore.
- Consolidated PAT guidance: Rs 1,100 to Rs 1,200 crore for FY27; Rs 1,300 to Rs 1,400 crore for FY28; around Rs 2,000 crore for FY29.
- Management expects to award Rs 18,000 to Rs 20,000 crore (or Rs 20,000 to Rs 25,000 crore) of new works in the next 2–3 quarters.
- EBITDA margin guidance: Minimum 6.5% to 7%; PAT margin guidance: 6% to 6.5%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Redevelopment projects and Amrapali contribute more than 60% of total revenue.
- Strong demand for real estate monetization and redevelopment models where state governments lack funds.
- Approached by private stalled projects in Delhi-NCR and across India for takeover, similar to Amrapali.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expanded due to marketing fees earned from selling Bharat Business Park and Amrapali inventory.
- HSCC turnover dipped due to the merger and foreclosure of a low-margin (2%) Maharashtra government health project.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acquired a base tower in Bharat Business Park at a base price of Rs 39,100 per sq ft (payment on construction-linked plan over 2 years); cash outflow will be treated as Capex.
- Dubai operations: Obtained developer license, land purchased for 66 housing units, broker RFO floated, and construction tender likely in October.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stood at Rs 666 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- Redevelopment projects have sufficient funds following the Bharat Business Park monetization (Rs 10,000 crore generated).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in project awarding and execution due to statutory clearances (environmental, airport, traffic clearances taking up to 6 months), fund constraints, and state government procedures.
- MAHAPREIT project delayed due to pending approvals and seed money requirements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Order inflow delays and INR 50,000-60,000 crore pipeline:** Management explained that 5 GPRA projects are pending Cabinet approval after clearance by PIB and Group of Ministers, causing timing delays.
- **Sustainability of high EBITDA margins:** Management stated margins will stay robust due to high-value PMC and marketing fees from upcoming real estate monetization (Africa Avenue, Vinayak Mandir, Amrapali inventory).
- **HSCC revenue drop and merger rationale:** Management clarified that low-margin projects (2%) were foreclosed to avoid losses, and the merger will combine execution strengths and improve long-term scale.
- **CPSE REIT timeline and structure:** Management stated 75,000 sq ft of rentable assets will initially transfer to an SPV, with a national REIT to be launched once a considerable portfolio is gathered across multiple CPSEs.
- **Execution pace vs. order book:** Analysts questioned the revenue conversion pace against the large order book; management reiterated confidence in achieving Rs 16,000–17,000 crore revenue backed by active redevelopment packages and Amrapali Phase 2.

### One-line takeaway
NBCC reported robust Q1 FY27 profitability driven by real estate monetization and redevelopment margins, and management remains confident in meeting its annual revenue target of Rs 16,000–17,000 crore despite project approval delays.
