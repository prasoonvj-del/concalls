# Patel Engineering Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue growth guidance revised downwards due to delayed project commencements from forest clearances.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Revenue growth guidance cut from ~10% to ~7-8% for FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from confident ~10% growth to marginal 7-8% growth due to delayed forest clearances holding up project execution.
- New topics or risks: Export restrictions on Tunnel Boring Machines (TBMs) from China; old risks regarding monsoon disruptions and working capital timing persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Subansiri Unit 5 commissioning target delivered; non-core asset monetization on track with Rs 81 crore achieved in H1.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,211 crore (vs Rs 1,208 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA:** Rs 150 crore; **Margin:** 12.42% (vs 13.13% in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 83 crore (up 7.64% YoY).
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,203 crore; **EBITDA:** Rs 147 crore (12.22% margin); **PAT:** Rs 51 crore.
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,491 crore; **EBITDA:** Rs 330 crore (13.24% margin); **PAT:** Rs 177 crore (up 15.94% YoY).
- **Order Book:** Rs 13,546 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Hydropower 64%, Irrigation 14%, Tunnelling 4%, Roads/Urban/Other 18%).
- **Bids Submitted:** ~Rs 30,000 crore across ~15 projects.
- **Asset Monetization (H1):** Rs 81 crore total (Rs 55 crore from ACP Tollways stake sale + Rs 26 crore from land parcel).
- **Consolidated Debt:** Rs 1,312 crore as of September 2026 (Term debt Rs 350 crore, Working capital debt Rs 960 crore).
- **Client Advances:** Rs 609 crore.
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.28.
- **Promoter Pledge:** Reduced to 67%.

### Highlights
- **Subansiri Project:** Unit 5 (250 MW) wet commissioned and integrated with the National Grid (5 of 8 units now operational, totaling 1250 MW); remaining 3 units on track for completion within the current financial year.
- **Sleemanabad Tunnel:** Achieved breakthrough of India's longest irrigation tunnel (~12 km) in Madhya Pradesh.
- **Asset Monetization:** Transferred entire 32% stake in ACP Tollways Private Limited for Rs 55 crore.
- **Project Progress:** Kwar dam concreting crossed 4 lakh cubic meters (out of 7.5 lakh); Kiru dam concreting reached 12 lakh cubic meters (out of 12.5 lakh).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised FY27 revenue growth guidance downwards to ~7-8% (from ~10%) due to delayed project commencement from pending forest clearances.
- Management expects better revenue visibility and growth in FY28 as new orders materialize.
- Bidding pipeline of ~Rs 50,000 crore expected over the next 12 months (road/tunnel projects in Ladakh/J&K, railway infrastructure, pumped storage in Maharashtra, MP, Andhra Pradesh, and hydropower).
- Capex guidance: ~3% new capex required for new projects (~Rs 150 crore annually for the next 2-3 years).
- Non-core asset monetization and award realizations targeted at Rs 100-150 crore annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Limited competition in large hydropower projects with only 4-5 active bidders.
- Exploring emerging private pumped storage projects (PSP), conventional hydro, irrigation, and tunneling.
- International bidding exploration focused on Bhutan and Nepal.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated EBITDA margin stood at 12.42% in Q2 FY27.
- Margin compression factors included lower revenue booking, monsoon disruptions, and initial project mobilization phases.
- Most projects include escalation clauses for raw material cost pass-through, subject to realization lags.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company owns ~4 TBM machines; can utilize existing machines or explore Indian manufacturing and German alternatives if needed.
- New project capex (~3%) is funded via client advances and internal cash flows.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated debt increased to Rs 1,312 crore as of September 2026 (from Rs 1,187 crore in March).
- Promoter pledge reduced from 88-90% down to 67%.
- Working capital utilization increased temporarily due to deferred milestone payments from clients.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Hilly region monsoons caused temporary construction disruptions.
- Pending forest clearances delayed the commencement of certain new projects.
- China TBM export restrictions pose potential sourcing considerations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Promoter Pledge Reduction:** Management stated the pledge dropped from 88-90% to 67%, and further reductions will be discussed with lenders next year. (Did not give an immediate timeline for zero pledge).
- **Revenue Growth Revision:** Management explained that FY27 revenue growth was revised to 7-8% because forest clearances delayed the commencement of new projects.
- **China TBM Restrictions:** Management noted they own 4 TBMs and can look at Indian manufacturers or German alternatives if China restrictions persist.
- **Cash Flow / Working Capital:** Management clarified that working capital utilization spiked due to a temporary timing issue in milestone payment realizations from central PSUs.
- **Buyback / Shareholder Return:** Management stated capital is reserved for funding new projects upon bid wins, so no buyback or capital distribution is planned for this year.

### One-line takeaway
Management revised FY27 revenue growth to 7-8% due to delayed forest clearances, while progressing key hydropower milestones and reducing promoter pledge to 67%.
