# KNR Constructions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expresses confidence in the long-term pipeline, diversification into mining and railways, and active efforts to resolve irrigation payment delays.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 587.9 crore.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 96.4 crore (16.4% margin).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 80.7 crore.
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 436.7 crore.
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 65 crore (15% margin).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 282.3 crore.
*   **Total Order Book:** Rs 15,234 crore (includes HAM and mining).
*   **Consolidated Debt:** Rs 1,975 crore (vs Rs 2,438 crore in March '26).

### Highlights
*   **Order Book Composition:** 38% roads, 11% irrigation, 6% pipeline, 45% mining.
*   **Mining Expansion:** Secured coal mining project (Kusmunda, Chhattisgarh) worth Rs 3,361 crore.
*   **Divestment:** Completed transfer of 100% stake in KNR Ramagiri and KNR Palani SPVs to Indus Infra Trust.
*   **One-off Impact:** Q1 EBITDA includes a net one-off gain of Rs 46 crore (Rs 76 crore revenue impact offset by Rs 30 crore expenditure).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management targets Rs 2,200 crore – Rs 2,300 crore for FY27 and >Rs 3,000 crore for FY28.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Management expects 8%-9% for FY27 (excluding one-offs) and 11%-12% for FY28.
*   **Order Inflow:** Targeting Rs 8,000 crore – Rs 10,000 crore in FY27.
*   **Mining Revenue:** Expects ~Rs 150 crore from mining in FY27, scaling to ~Rs 400 crore in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Road Sector:** Awarding activity remains subdued; NHAI awarded ~107 km in Q1 FY27.
*   **Diversification:** Management is actively pivoting toward railways (Rs 2.93 lakh crore allocation) and urban mobility (metro/mass rapid transit) to diversify the order book.
*   **Mining:** Emerging as a core growth vertical, with management targeting >6% PAT margins in mining projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Normalization:** Reported Q1 EBITDA margin of 15% (standalone) would be ~5.5% excluding the one-off gain.
*   **Mining Capex:** Management estimates Rs 500 crore – Rs 600 crore for Kusmunda project equipment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex Guidance:** Rs 350 crore – Rs 400 crore, primarily for mining equipment.
*   **Equipment Strategy:** Management is utilizing buyback policies for certain dumpers (Caterpillar/Komatsu) to optimize maintenance costs and efficiency.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Debt/Equity:** 0.9 (vs 0.49 in March '26).
*   **Working Capital:** 133 days (vs 78 days in March '26).
*   **Receivables:** Outstanding irrigation receivables from Telangana government are ~Rs 1,300 crore; management is in talks for recovery in 5-6 installments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Payment Delays:** Significant outstanding receivables from the Telangana government for irrigation projects.
*   **Execution:** Weather-related delays (heavy rains) impacting mining site preparation (haul roads).
*   **Land Acquisition:** Delays in land handover for certain HAM projects (e.g., Mysore-Kushalnagara) and mining sites (Risdi village).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial):** Asked about FY27/FY28 growth. Management guided for Rs 2,200-2,300 cr in FY27 and >Rs 3,000 cr in FY28 as new projects (ECR Chennai, Telangana, Mining) ramp up.
*   **Shravan Shah:** Asked about mining capex. Management clarified Rs 500-600 cr for Kusmunda and noted that they are using a mix of new and old equipment to manage costs.
*   **Faisal Hawa:** Asked about potential buyback. Management confirmed a buyback is under consideration and a board decision is pending.
*   **Bhavin Modi:** Asked about unbilled revenue. Management stated total unbilled revenue in the order book is Rs 1,220 crore.
*   **Vasudev:** Asked about irrigation recovery. Management stated they are pushing for monthly installments of Rs 80-90 crore to clear the Rs 1,300 crore outstanding.

### One-line takeaway
KNR is aggressively diversifying into mining and railways to mitigate the slowdown in road awarding, while focusing on recovering Rs 1,300 crore in irrigation receivables and evaluating a potential share buyback.
