# KNR Constructions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While project awards and order inflows show positive momentum and asset monetization is complete, execution challenges, aggressive competition, and payment delays weigh on margins and revenue recognition.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained revenue guidance (Rs 2,200 crore - Rs 2,300 crore for FY27; >Rs 3,000 crore for FY28). Order inflow target for H2 FY27 is Rs 4,000 crore - Rs 5,000 crore (lowered from the broader annual target of Rs 8,000 crore - Rs 10,000 crore stated last quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more cautious on near-term margins due to rising input and overhead costs and delayed appointed dates, though management remains optimistic about urban infrastructure and mining diversification.
- New topics or risks: Royalty issue with the Telangana government delaying the Gudebellur HAM project start; increased focus on urban flyovers in Hyderabad (HMDA) due to slow NHAI awarding. Old risks of irrigation payment delays persist.
- Last quarter's promises: Completed the monetization of all four HAM assets to Indus Infra Trust (fulfilling the asset monetization strategy). Banhardih mine start is still delayed by 4-5 months.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 477 crore
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 44 crore (9.2% margin)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 281 crore (includes an exceptional profit of Rs 292 crore from sale of KNR Guruvayur Infra; adjusted standalone PAT is Rs 30 crore)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 600 crore
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 147 crore (24.6% margin)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 102 crore
- **Total Order Book:** Rs 11,709 crore (excluding recent wins); Rs 16,355 crore including recent HAM and HMDA wins
- **Consolidated Debt:** Rs 1,180 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 2,438 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- **Net Debt to Equity (Consolidated):** 0.22x as of June 30, 2026 (vs 0.49x as of March 31, 2026)
- **Working Capital Debt (Standalone):** 104 days (vs 78 days as of March 2026)
- **Irrigation Receivables:** ~Rs 660 crore (plus HAM receivables of Rs 330 crore; unbilled irrigation is Rs 830 crore)
- **Capex:** Rs 220 crore incurred in H1 FY27; estimated at around Rs 400 crore for the full year

### Highlights
- Completed the monetization of all four HAM assets to Indus Infra Trust, transferring KNR Guruvayur Infra (received consideration of Rs 486 crore) and KNR Ramanattukara Infra (received consideration of Rs 549 crore).
- Received LOA from Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA) for an elevated corridor from ICCC Banjara Hills to Shilpa Layout Flyover Road, Hyderabad, worth Rs 1,396 crore (24-month construction period).
- Achieved financial closure for KNR Manyamkonda Infra Private Limited (Telangana HAM project) on October 1, 2026.
- Order book composition: 43% roads, 10% irrigation, 5% pipeline, 42% mining. Third-party clients constitute 74% and captive HAM 26%.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Management maintains revenue guidance of Rs 2,200 crore to Rs 2,300 crore for FY27 and expects growth in FY28.
- **Order Inflow:** Targeting Rs 4,000 crore to Rs 5,000 crore in H2 FY27 across roads, mining, and other sectors.
- **Mining Revenue:** Targeting ~Rs 100 crore in H2 FY27 from the mining project, scaling to ~Rs 400 crore in FY28 (with Banhardih mine expected to start in 4-5 months).
- **Equity Requirement:** Remaining equity requirement for four HAM projects is Rs 202 crore (Rs 110 crore in H2 FY27, Rs 85 crore in FY28). Additional equity of ~Rs 510 crore required for Chennai and Telangana NHAI HAM projects.

### Demand & industry commentary
- National highway construction declined ~19% YoY to 2,369 km (April–September 2026), but fresh project awards increased ~63% YoY to 2,236 km.
- Ministry of Road Transport and Highways targets 10,000 km of national highway construction in FY27.
- Andhra Pradesh outlined a national highway development pipeline of ~Rs 1.93 lakh crore (107 projects worth ~Rs 75,000 crore and 7 corridors worth ~Rs 1.18 lakh crore).
- Telangana secured central approval for five urban infrastructure projects worth Rs 3,892 crore (including the Hyderabad-Godavari drinking water supply scheme worth ~Rs 2,000 crore).
- Coal India offtake increased ~9% YoY to 454.6 million tonnes (April–October 2027), supporting strong demand in the mining sector.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin dropped to 9.2% in Q2 FY27 due to aggressive market competition, inflation, dollar fluctuations, unutilized equipment, and increased overheads/manpower retention costs.
- Depreciation is expected to be around Rs 30 crore per quarter in the next two quarters.
- Mining projects are expected to yield net profits of around 5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex for FY27 is guided at around Rs 400 crore (of which ~Rs 300–320 crore pertains to mining assets).
- Kusmunda and Banhardih mines require an estimated capex of ~Rs 500 crore each.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated debt reduced significantly to Rs 1,180 crore as of September 30, 2026, down from Rs 2,438 crore as of March 31, 2026, following asset monetization.
- Standalone working capital days increased to 104 days as of September 30, 2026, compared to 78 days in March 2026.
- Outstanding irrigation receivables from the Telangana government stand at Rs 660 crore, with management expecting a release of ~Rs 600 crore in December/March.
- Total withhold amount on monetized HAM assets is Rs 295 crore for the first three assets and Rs 130 crore for the fourth asset.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution delays due to land acquisition challenges, longer approval timelines, and statutory clearances.
- Royalty issue with the Telangana government holding up the start of the Gudebellur HAM project.
- Delayed payments (3-4 months due) from departments like GHMC (approx. Rs 600 crore of city works).
- Aggressive bidding competition and rising input/food prices putting pressure on margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital):** Asked about order inflow targets and margin outlook for FY28. Management confirmed an H2 inflow target of Rs 4,000-5,000 crore but declined to guarantee a 13% EBITDA margin for FY28 due to rising input costs.
2. **Vasudev (Nuvama):** Asked about adjusted PAT and segmental revenues. Management stated adjusted standalone PAT was Rs 30 crore (excluding the exceptional item) and provided a breakdown of H1 irrigation (Rs 20 crore) and pipeline execution plans.
3. **Bhavin Modi:** Asked about pending receivables from monetized assets and mining capex. Management clarified that Rs 295 crore is withheld on the first three assets, Rs 130 crore on the fourth, and total mining capex for the two mines will reach ~Rs 1,000 crore.
4. **Sudeep Bora:** Asked about appointed date timelines for new HAM projects and scope changes in Mysore-Kushalnagar packages. Management expects appointed dates by early December once the zero-royalty GO issue is resolved; 7-8 km of ROW remains pending.
5. **Faisal Hawa:** Asked about potential share buybacks and AP/Telangana tender pipelines. Management explained that cash inflows are currently prioritized for heavy mining capex (Kusmunda and Banhardih) and working capital rather than a promoter/company buyback.

### One-line takeaway
KNR Constructions successfully monetized its HAM portfolio to significantly reduce consolidated debt to Rs 1,180 crore, while pivoting toward mining and urban infrastructure to offset a sluggish road execution environment.
