# KNR Constructions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Construction & Infrastructure*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Asset monetization completed and order book expanded, but near-term execution and margins face headwinds from input inflation and payment delays.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 standalone revenue target of Rs 2,200 crore – Rs 2,300 crore; order inflow target revised to Rs 4,000 crore – Rs 5,000 crore for H2 FY27 (vs Rs 8,000 crore – Rs 10,000 crore for full FY27 guided in Q1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Highway execution remains subdued due to land acquisition and approval delays, despite national highway awards increasing 63% YoY in H1 FY27. EBITDA margins dropped due to cost inflation and equipment underutilization.
- **New topics or risks:** Zero-royalty GO resolution pending in Telangana, halting initial execution on the Gudebellur HAM project; high capex commitments in mining (up to Rs 1,000 crore total across two mines) delaying share buyback plans.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on monetization of the remaining two HAM assets (KNR Guruvayur and KNR Ramanattukara) to Indus Infra Trust.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 477 crore
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 44 crore (EBITDA margin: 9.2%)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 281 crore (includes exceptional profit of Rs 292 crore; adjusted PAT: Rs 30 crore)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 600 crore
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 147 crore (EBITDA margin: 24.6%)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 102 crore
- **Total Order Book:** Rs 11,709 crore (as of Sept 30, 2026); includes unawarded/newly won HAM & HMDA projects to reach Rs 16,355 crore.

### Highlights
- Completed monetization of all 4 HAM SPVs to Indus Infra Trust. Transferred KNR Guruvayur (Sept 2026; received Rs 486 crore against Rs 193 crore equity/sub-debt) and KNR Ramanattukara (Oct 2026; received Rs 549 crore against Rs 226 crore equity/sub-debt), recognizing a Rs 292 crore pre-tax exceptional profit in Q2.
- Secured LOA for the HMDA elevated corridor project (Banjara Hills to Shilpa Layout) in Hyderabad worth Rs 1,396 crore with a 24-month execution period.
- Physical progress on active HAM projects: Magadi to Somwarpet (~92%), Marripudi to Somavarappadu (~94%), Mysore to Kushalnagar Pkg IV (~29%), Mysore to Kushalnagar Pkg V (~25%).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Order Inflows:** Targeting Rs 4,000 crore – Rs 5,000 crore in H2 FY27 across highways, mining, urban infrastructure, and railways.
- **Segment Revenue:** Targeting ~Rs 100 crore turnover from Kusmunda mining project in H2 FY27; expecting ~Rs 400 crore from Kusmunda and ~Rs 300 crore from Banhardih mine in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- National highway construction fell ~19% YoY to 2,369 km during H1 FY27, while fresh project awards grew 63% YoY to 2,236 km.
- Strong project pipelines emerging in Andhra Pradesh (Rs 1.93 lakh crore national highway pipeline) and Telangana (Rs 3,892 crore urban infrastructure projects approved).
- Coal India offtake grew 9% YoY to 454.6 MT in April–October FY27, supporting mining expansion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin contracted to 9.2% due to elevated fixed overheads, unutilized equipment, input price inflation (food, general inflation, FX fluctuations), and competitive bidding.
- Expected second-half EBITDA margins to remain in a similar pattern before recovering in FY28.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Spent Rs 220 crore capex in H1 FY27 (Rs 180 crore for mining equipment).
- FY27 total capex targeted at ~Rs 400 crore (Rs 300–Rs 320 crore for mining).
- Total estimated mining capex per mine is ~Rs 500 crore (spent ~Rs 370–Rs 380 crore on Kusmunda to date; Banhardih mine will require another ~Rs 500 crore over 5 years).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Consolidated Debt:** Reduced to Rs 1,180 crore as of Sept 30, 2026 (vs Rs 2,438 crore as of March 31, 2026).
- **Consolidated Net Debt to Equity:** 0.22x as of June 30, 2026 (vs 0.49x as of March 31, 2026).
- **Working Capital Days:** 104 days (vs 78 days in March 2026).
- **Receivables:** Outstanding irrigation receivables from Telangana government stand at Rs 660 crore (total unbilled and receivables across irrigation and HAM stand at ~Rs 1,400 crore).
- **Withholdings:** Rs 295 crore withholdings remain across the first three monetized assets; Rs 130 crore withholdings expected on the fourth asset.
- **Equity Commitments:** Remaining equity requirement of Rs 202 crore for existing 4 HAM projects (Rs 117 crore in H2 FY27, Rs 85 crore in FY28). Additional Rs 510 crore equity required for Chennai and Telangana NHAI HAM projects.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued delay in receiving certified irrigation bill payments from the Telangana government.
- Delay in obtaining appointed date and commencing work on Gudebellur HAM project due to pending zero-royalty GO state government clearances.
- Capital allocation for share buyback put on hold due to intensive mining capex and working capital needs for city infrastructure contracts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital):** *Where are order inflows being targeted and what is the margin outlook?*  
  **Management:** Targeting Rs 4,000–5,000 crore inflows in H2 FY27 from highways, mining, and city works. H2 EBITDA margins will follow the current subdued pattern due to overheads and input costs; margin visibility will improve in FY28 as mining and new HAMs scale up.
- **Vasudev (Nuvama):** *What was the adjusted PAT for Q2 excluding exceptional items?*  
  **Management:** Adjusted PAT for Q2 FY27 was Rs 30 crore (excluding Rs 292 crore pre-tax gain from SPV sale).
- **Bhavin Modi:** *How much total capex is planned for the two coal mining orders?*  
  **Management:** Each mine requires ~Rs 500 crore in total capex over 5 years (~Rs 370-380 crore already spent on Kusmunda; Banhardih will require another Rs 500 crore). Monetization proceeds are being diverted to mining capex and HAM equity.
- **Sudeep Bora:** *What is the status of the zero-royalty issue and appointed dates for new HAM projects?*  
  **Management:** Revenue ministry has cleared the zero-royalty document, currently awaiting Chief Secretary approval. Appointed date expected by first/second week of December 2026.
- **Faisal Hawa:** *Is a share buyback planned given cash inflow from asset monetization?*  
  **Management:** Buyback is delayed due to high mining capex requirements and working capital needs for city works, alongside pending collection of irrigation dues. Promoters have limited personal liquid capital for secondary market purchases.

### One-line takeaway
KNR completes its 4-asset HAM monetization to de-lever its balance sheet while reallocating capital into high-capex mining projects and urban infrastructure to compensate for delayed road awards and slow irrigation collections.
