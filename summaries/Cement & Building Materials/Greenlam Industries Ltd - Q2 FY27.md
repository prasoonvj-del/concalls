# Greenlam Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong growth in chipboard and plywood volume, but core laminate sales were flat due to severe logistical and export challenges.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Consolidated revenue growth guidance lowered from 18%-20% to 16%-18%. Laminate segment growth guidance revised down (may not achieve the 10%-12% range). Debt reduction target of Rs 100 crore may see slight variance due to lower revenue and margins.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from positive to more cautious/mixed due to logistical bottlenecks in exports and margin compression, though management maintains Q2 is an aberration.
- New topics or risks: Container availability, vessel delays, and high freight costs heavily impacting exports.
- Last quarter's promises: Laminate expansion (two new lines at Andhra Pradesh) remains on schedule for Q4 FY27 commercial production.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 875 crore (up 8% YoY, up 10% QoQ)
- **Consolidated Net Profit:** Rs 24.5 crore
- **EBITDA (before forex):** Rs 87 crore (down 19% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (before forex):** 9.9% (down 330 bps YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 51.5% (down 310 bps YoY, down 140 bps QoQ)
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,672 crore (up 13% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA (before forex):** Rs 168 crore (up 4% YoY)
- **H1 Net Profit:** Rs 46 crore
- **Laminates Revenue:** Rs 658 crore (flat YoY)
- **Laminates EBITDA Margin:** 12% (down 590 bps YoY)
- **Laminates Production Volume:** 4.83 million sheets (79% utilization)
- **Laminates Sales Volume:** 5.09 million sheets (down 12% YoY)
- **Laminates Average Realization:** Rs 1,241 per sheet and board (up 12% YoY)
- **Plywood & Allied Revenue:** Rs 110 crore (up 7.5% YoY)
- **Plywood EBITDA Loss:** Rs 1.4 crore (narrowed)
- **Plywood Sales Volume:** 1.94 million square meter (up 23% YoY)
- **Plywood Average Realization:** Rs 257 per square meter (up 4% YoY)
- **Chipboard Revenue:** Rs 107 crore (up 124% YoY)
- **Chipboard EBITDA:** Rs 9 crore
- **Chipboard Production Volume:** 44,241 cubic meter (61% utilization)
- **Chipboard Sales Volume:** 46,871 cubic meter (up 78% YoY)
- **Chipboard Average Realization:** Rs 22,591 per cubic meter (up 24% YoY)

- **Net Debt:** Rs 922 crore as of September 30, 2026 (reduction of Rs 19 crore vs March 26)

### Highlights
- Core laminates business saw zero YoY revenue growth due to international logistics challenges.
- Plywood, decorative veneer, engineered flooring, and engineered doors all turned EBITDA positive individually, though the broader plywood segment recorded a marginal net EBITDA loss of Rs 1.4 crore.
- Chipboard revenue crossed Rs 100 crore (Rs 107 crore) and generated positive EBITDA for the second consecutive quarter (Rs 9 crore).
- Tier-two plywood brand introduced in southern markets in October to drive volume and utilization.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be better than H1 FY27.
- Consolidated revenue growth guidance revised to 16%-18% for FY27 (previously 18%-20%).
- Laminate segment growth expected to fall short of the earlier 10%-12% guidance range.
- Debt reduction for the full year expected to be very near Rs 100 crore, despite lower H1 cash flows.
- Full capacity utilization for plywood targeted around FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic laminate demand impacted slightly by a high base from last year's flagship catalogue launch.
- International export markets faced severe headwinds from container shortages, vessel availability, high freight costs, and longer transit times.
- Local European manufacturing plants are struggling, allowing Greenlam to continue winning market share despite flat organic market growth in Europe.
- Plywood secondary sales and network expansion ongoing, with tier-two brand introduction in South India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined due to flat laminate sales, raw material price fluctuations, and adverse product mix changes.
- Incremental SG&A and overhead expenses remain minimal as existing resources and plant capacities are utilized.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Two new press lines at the Andhra Pradesh (Naidupeta) plant are running on schedule for commercial production by Q4 FY27.
- Post-expansion, the AP plant will have 5 laminate lines (expandable up to 8) and 1 chipboard line (expandable by 1 more).
- Current asset base across all existing lines is capable of delivering up to Rs 5,000 crore in revenue at full utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 922 crore as of September 30, 2026, marking a Rs 19 crore reduction from March 2026.
- Working capital improved to 53 days compared to 56 days in Q1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Export logistical disruptions (vessel availability, container delays, high freight).
- Geopolitical volatility affecting international trade flows.
- Subscale operations in the single-plant plywood segment restricting pan-India reach and margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Manan Shah (Navneet Family Office) on laminate degrowth reasons:** Management attributed it to a high domestic base from last year's catalogue launch and export logistics issues (containers/vessels/freight), deeming Q2 an aberration.
2. **Manan Shah on plywood optimal utilization timeline:** Management stated FY29 is a relevant year for full capacity utilization, aided by the October launch of a tier-two brand in South India.
3. **Jay Unadkat on subsidiary/allied segment profitability and asset turns:** Management clarified that veneer, engineered floors, and doors are profitable, while plywood has a minor loss; incremental capex is minimal, and profitability will scale with volume.
4. **Payal Shah on consolidated revenue and margin guidance:** Management revised full-year revenue guidance to 16%-18% and refrained from explicit H2 margin guidance while noting it will improve over H1.
5. **Manoj Mehta on laminate 10%-12% growth guidance:** Management admitted that while H2 will be better, achieving the full 10%-12% growth rate for laminates is unlikely.

### One-line takeaway
Greenlam's Q2 growth stalled due to severe export logistics bottlenecks and margin compression, but strong traction in chipboard and improving allied segment profitability point to a stronger H2 recovery.
