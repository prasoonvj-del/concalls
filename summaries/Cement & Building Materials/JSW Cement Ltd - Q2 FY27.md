# JSW Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust volume growth, EBITDA breakeven achieved in the North region, and price increases implemented to counter cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,912 crore (Up 33% YoY, 0.8% QoQ)
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA:** Rs 278 crore (Up 4% YoY)
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 708
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 77 crore
- **Total Cement Volumes:** 2.29 million tonnes (Up 40% YoY)
- **Total Volumes (Cement + GGBS + Clinker):** 3.93 million tonnes (Up 26% YoY)
- **GGBS Volumes:** Grown by 7% YoY
- **Cement Realization:** Rs 4,781 per tonne (Up 3% YoY, down 3% QoQ)
- **GGBS Realization:** Rs 3,749 per tonne
- **Blended Fuel Cost:** Rs 1.84 per MCal (up from Rs 1.80 per MCal in Q1)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 4,287 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Net Debt-to-EBITDA:** 3.2x
- **Average Cost of Debt:** 7.67%
- **Capex:** Rs 483 crore in Q2 FY27; Rs 820 crore in H1 FY27

### Highlights
- Achieved EBITDA breakeven (slightly positive contribution) in the North region (Nagaur) during Q2.
- Commissioned waste heat recovery system (WHRS) and alternate fuel rehandling co-processing system (AFR) at Nagaur.
- Added 36 megawatt of wind capacity at Nandyal and Vijayanagar, increasing green power share to 41%.
- RMC revenues stood at Rs 170 crore for Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains constructive on cement demand for the balance of the year as infrastructure execution and construction activity gather pace.
- Expects North region variable/production costs to reduce by Rs 550 to Rs 600 per tonne due to recent operational commencements.
- Expects North EBITDA to hit around Rs 650 to Rs 700 in Q4.
- Cement volume growth guidance maintained at high-teens over last year.
- GGBS volume growth guidance maintained at high single-digits for the year.
- Full-year capex spend expected to close around Rs 2,200 crore to Rs 2,300 crore.
- Net debt-to-EBITDA targeted to stay around 3x for a year or so, and move below 3x in coming years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Cement demand remained robust during the quarter, supporting price increases across markets in September and October.
- GGBS volumes driven by growing demand from infrastructure and RMC sectors.
- Maharashtra demand supported by infrastructure growth around the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel costs expected to face pressure; blended fuel cost projected to move from INR1.84 to INR2.10 per MCal in Q3, impacting cement cost by approx. INR90 per tonne.
- Green energy share expected to move upwards of 50%, aiding power cost reduction.
- Clinker-to-cement factor increased to 58% due to ramp-up of North operations and higher OPC sales.
- Lead distance increased by 9 kilometers QoQ to 294 kilometers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned additional 1 MTPA grinding capacity at Nagaur and the overland belt conveyor (OLBC) in early October, taking total grinding capacity at Nagaur to 3.5 MTPA.
- Planned expansions: Nagaur 2.5 MTPA (Q4 FY28), Fujairah 1.7 MTPA (Q1 FY28), Dolvi 2 MTPA (within 20-21 months from January 2027), and Vijayanagar (commencing December 2026, 1.5 years to commissioning).
- Total remaining capex to reach 43.5 MTPA is estimated at INR7,500 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at INR4,287 crore with net debt-to-EBITDA at 3.2x.
- Foreign debt unhedged as per policy resulted in forex losses contributing to finance cost amid US dollar appreciation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties from the West Asia crisis and weaker monsoon impacting the broader operating environment.
- Elevated pet coke prices creating cost pressure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prateek Kumar (Jefferies)** asked about the 3% QoQ decline in cement realization and divergence from peers. Management attributed it to geographic spread and regional mix changes rather than aggressive price cuts.
2. **Sanjeev Kumar Singh (Motilal Oswal)** questioned the cost reduction expectations in the North region. Management guided a cost reduction of INR550 to INR600 per tonne from WHRS, AFR, and OLBC.
3. **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities)** asked about comfort levels with leverage given aggressive expansions. Management stated the net debt-to-EBITDA will stay around 3x for another year and trend below 3x thereafter.
4. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital)** asked about the timeline for upcoming capacities. Management outlined timelines for Nagaur (Q4 FY28), Fujairah (Q1 FY28), and Dolvi (FY29).
5. **Rajesh Ravi (HDFC Securities) inquired about trade payables and acceptances.** Management explained it reflects the growth phase and lag in cash generation, which will drop once new units start throwing up cash.

### One-line takeaway
JSW Cement reported a 33% YoY rise in Q2 FY27 revenue and 26% growth in total volumes, achieving EBITDA breakeven in the North region, while managing cost pressures through green energy scaling and upcoming operational efficiencies.
