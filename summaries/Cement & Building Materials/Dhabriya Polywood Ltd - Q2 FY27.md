# Dhabriya Polywood Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Company reported its strongest quarter in history with record revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, supported by a robust order book.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 77.78 crore (+16.1% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 16.38 crore (+19.9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 21.1% (+70 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 9.21 crore (+20.9% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 146.10 crore (+13.2% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 18.06 crore (+27.6% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** >Rs 240 crore (all-time high).

### Highlights
*   Polymer Product business (PVC/uPVC) revenue: Rs 69.5 crore (+27% YoY).
*   Aluminum Window & Facade division: Secured order book >Rs 100 crore (total segment order book is Rs 140 crore).
*   Extrusion capacity: Increased to 27,600 MTPA from 24,000 MTPA.
*   CRISIL rating upgraded to BBB+/Stable.
*   The company noted that competition from Chinese imports has "almost vanished" due to domestic capacity build-up and higher import tariffs.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets 25-30% revenue growth in the long run.
*   Aluminum division revenue conversion expected to commence from Q4 FY27.
*   WPC door revenue target for FY27: ~Rs 5 crore.
*   Modular Furniture export target for FY27: Rs 6-7 crore.
*   Management expects H2 to be seasonally stronger than H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management remains positive on building materials/interior solutions due to urbanization and infrastructure development.
*   Growing market acceptance for uPVC windows, doors, and decorative panels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margin expansion is attributed to a refined product mix and premiumization rather than price hikes.
*   Aluminum division margins are expected to be 2-3% lower than the company average initially.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Ongoing Rs 100 crore capex program (FY26-FY28) funded by internal accruals and debt.
*   Additional 8,000 MTPA extrusion capacity planned in the next 3-6 months.
*   Aluminum facility: Two lines commissioned; full implementation expected by end of FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net worth: Rs 147.7 crore.
*   Debt-to-equity ratio: 0.68x.
*   Debtor cycle: 30-45 days (excluding retention money).
*   Retention money: Rs 8-10 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Project execution delays (e.g., WPC doors, Modular Furniture) impacted H1 performance.
*   Initial margin compression in the new Aluminum segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Sudhir Shah (SSS Finserv):** Asked about segment split and capacity utilization. Management confirmed >65% utilization for extrusion in H1.
*   **Runit Kapoor (Equitree Capital):** Asked about WPC door launch and Aluminum timeline. Management confirmed WPC launch last month and Aluminum revenue impact starting Q4.
*   **Akshay Jawahar (Banyan Tree Associates):** Clarified Aluminum revenue timeline. Management confirmed execution starts in Q3, with revenue reflection in Q4.
*   **Gunit Singh (Counter Cyclical):** Asked about receivable aging. Management clarified that >6 months receivables are primarily retention money (Rs 8-10 crore).
*   **Prithvi (Abakkus Asset Management):** Asked about import competition. Management stated it has "almost vanished" due to domestic manufacturing and tariff increases.

### One-line takeaway
Record-breaking quarter driven by premiumization and a strong Rs 240 crore order book, with new Aluminum and WPC segments poised to drive future growth.
