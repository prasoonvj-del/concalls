# India Cements Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating performance improved, capacity utilization was higher, and demand and pricing momentum remained strong despite cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Volume Growth:** 13.8% YoY
- **Capacity Utilization:** 71% (vs 66% YoY)
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 1.26x as of September '26 (vs 0.94x at March end); expected below 1x by March.
- **Fuel Costs:** Increased from INR 45 per bag to INR 51.5 per bag QoQ; fuel rates climbed to ~INR 2.09–2.1 per kcal (expected to go to ~INR 2.2 per kcal).
- **Capex:** INR 5,882 crores incurred in H1; full-year capex guidance maintained at ~INR 10,000 crores.
- **Ultravolt Investment:** INR 1,800 crores planned (INR 1,100 crores spent/committed to date).
- **Blended Cement Share:** 67.3%
- **Premium Cement Share:** 37%

### Highlights
- Commissioned 4.6 million tonnes of capacity in September (3.6 million tonnes greenfield at Petnikota in Andhra, 1 million tonnes debottlenecking across 3 units), taking domestic cement capacity to 204.7 million tonnes.
- Added 116 MW of renewable power and 10 MW of waste heat recovery during the quarter; green power meets 47% of power needs, crossing 2 gigawatts of green power for captive consumption.
- Launched the Wires and Cables business under the brand **Ultravolt** on September 3rd; retail reach crossed 80,000 points with >1,500 UBS channel partners signed up.
- Other fixed costs per tonne were 6% lower YoY due to operating leverage (absolute fixed costs grew 4%).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to reach 240 million tonnes of capacity as planned.
- Full-year capex guidance maintained at ~INR 10,000 crores (plus/minus).
- Net debt to EBITDA expected to be below 1x by the end of March.
- Management remains confident of delivering the structural vision/guidance of INR 1,400 EBITDA per tonne.
- Next organic phase of growth taking capacity beyond 240 million tonnes will be detailed before the end of the financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry cement demand expected to grow closer to 10% YoY; company grew 13.8% YoY.
- Regional growth: North >20%, South 15%-20%, East ~5%, West ~10%, Central ~10%.
- Demand drivers include urban housing, individual homebuilders (IHB), private sector capex, data centres, and government infrastructure (metro extensions, industrial parks).
- Rural demand may soften in some drought areas, but 40% of the rural economy is non-crop/MSME driven, backed by the last 4 years of good monsoons.
- East region was disrupted by prolonged, heavy monsoons and floods.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Pet coke prices spiked to around $180 per tonne; company reduced pet coke dependence to 25% of overall mix.
- Industrial diesel prices are at an all-time high, impacting mining and transportation costs.
- Domestic coal availability is constrained as Coal India prioritizes thermal power plants (TPPs).
- Cost pressures expected to persist due to the West Asia crisis, but management believes price increases and operating leverage will offset them.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total domestic cement capacity stands at 204.7 million tonnes post September commissioning.
- Ultravolt setup includes a mother plant in Jhagadia (Gujarat), 1 main warehouse, 5 regional distribution centres, and 16 branch warehouses. 
- Ultravolt is targeting an annualized copper consumption capacity of 78,000 tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to EBITDA stood at 1.26x at end of September '26 (up from 0.94x at March end) due to capex and dividend payouts. Raised NCDs of ~INR 5,000 crores at 7.25% during the quarter.
- Corporate guarantee given to UHG Holdings (41% UltraTech, 9% Grasim, 50% Hindalco) to help them secure finer interest rates for buying aircraft and ships for business use.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia crisis driving up pet coke prices ($180/tonne) and fuel costs.
- Increase in industrial diesel prices impacting logistics/mining.
- Squeezed domestic coal availability due to prioritization of TPPs by Coal India.
- Localized heavy rains and flooding in the East impacting construction activity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Indrajit Agarwal (CLSA) asked about variable cost inflation in H2 based on spot pet coke at $180.** Management stated that pipeline inventory will insulate Q3, with only a marginal impact expected in Q4.
- **Amit Murarka (Axis Capital) asked about the India Cements Tamil Nadu levy.** Management confirmed it was fully accounted for in Q2 and no additional costs are expected going forward.
- **Raashi (Citigroup) asked about Ultravolt revenue potential and copper pricing assumptions.** Management clarified that the previously stated INR 10,000 crore revenue target is on an autopilot mode to scale up to INR 16,000–18,000 crores depending on copper price fluctuations.
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec India) questioned how cost curve increases would be avoided given sharp inflation.** Management cryptically advised looking at low-price fuel contracts. They also highlighted that India Cements' plants will undergo major shutdowns for upgrades in FY28 to reach a 4-digit EBITDA per tonne.
- **Sanjeev Kumar Singh (Motilal Oswal) asked about muted infrastructure in the South.** Management clarified that South capacity is fully sold out, driven by housing, auto, and data centres despite temporary aggregate/movement curbs.

### One-line takeaway
UltraTech delivered robust Q2 volume growth of 13.8% and higher capacity utilization (71%), while exiting September with firm price hikes and strong demand momentum that offset mounting fuel cost pressures.
