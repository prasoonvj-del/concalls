# UltraTech Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating performance improved with higher capacity utilization, stable realizations, efficiency gains, and a 21% increase in net profit.

### Key numbers
- **Capacity Utilization:** 71% (vs 66% last year)
- **Net Profit Growth:** 21% YoY increase
- **Volume Growth:** 13.8% YoY
- **Fuel Costs:** Increased from Rs 45 to Rs 51.5 per bag (fuel rates climbed to INR 2.09 per kcal, and further to INR 2.2 per kcal)
- **Other Fixed Costs:** 6% lower per ton YoY
- **Fixed Costs Absolute Growth:** 4% YoY
- **Capex:** Rs 5,882 crores incurred in H1; full-year guidance around Rs 10,000 crores (+/-)
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 1.26x at the end of September '26 (vs 0.94x at March end); management expects to be below 1x by March.
- **Ultravolt Capex / Commitment:** Rs 1,100 crores spent/committed out of planned Rs 1,800 crores
- **Green Power Share:** 47% of power needs met from non-fossil fuel sources; reached 2 gigawatts of green power for captive consumption
- **Thermal Substitution Rate (TSR):** 7.2%
- **Blended Cement Share:** 67.3%
- **Premium Cement Share:** 37%

### Highlights
- Commissioned 4.6 million tonnes of capacity in September, taking total domestic cement capacity to 204.7 million tonnes (includes 3.6 million tonnes greenfield at Petnikota, Andhra Pradesh, and 1 million tonne debottlenecking).
- Launched Wires and Cables business under the brand "Ultravolt" on September 3rd; over 1,500 UltraTech UBS channel partners signed up, retail reach crossed 80,000 points.
- Added 116 MW of renewable power and 10 MW of waste heat recovery (WHRS) during the quarter.
- Management stated absolute fixed costs grew 4% (below volume growth of 13.8%), driving operating leverage.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects capacity to reach 240 million tonnes as planned without surprises.
- Management expects to deliver the previously stated EBITDA per ton vision/guidance of Rs 1,400 by March '28, supported by price increases covering cost pressures.
- Management plans to release detailed capacity expansion plans for the North region before the end of the financial year.
- Management aims to bring Net Debt to EBITDA below 1x by March.
- Management expects fiscal '28 to be a mature performance year for the Ultravolt Wires and Cables business.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry cement demand is estimated to grow close to 10% YoY. UltraTech grew 13.8% YoY this quarter.
- Regional volume growth: North grew >20%, South grew 15% to 20%, East grew ~5%, West grew ~10%, Central grew ~10%.
- Capacity utilizations: North and South were upwards of 70%, West close to 70%, Central and East around 60% due to prolonged and heavy monsoons.
- Individual homebuilder (IHB) retail demand remained resilient; public capex was strong, though road construction was weak.
- Non-crop activities contribute almost 40% of the rural economy, acting as a cushion alongside the last 4 years of good monsoons, offsetting weak monsoon impacts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel costs rose 13% QoQ; raw material costs rose 3% QoQ.
- Pet coke prices climbed to around $180 per ton due to the West Asia crisis; UltraTech reduced pet coke consumption to ~25% of its overall mix.
- Industrial diesel prices are at an all-time high, impacting mining and transportation costs.
- Energy kcal cost rose from INR 2.09 to INR 2.1 / INR 2.2 per kcal.
- Domestic coal availability is constrained as Coal India prioritizes thermal power plants (TPPs).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex stood at INR 5,882 crores; full-year capex guidance maintained at around INR 10,000 crores (+/-).
- Ultravolt (Wires and Cables): INR 1,100 crores spent/committed out of INR 1,800 crores. Jhagadia mother plant, 1 main warehouse, 5 regional distribution centres, and 16 branch warehouses established. Copper consumption capacity set at 78,000 tonnes annually.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to EBITDA stood at 1.26x as of September '26 (compared to 0.94x in March '26), driven by capex and dividend payouts.
- Raised NCDs of ~INR 5,000 crores at 7.25% during the quarter.
- Provided a corporate guarantee to UHG Holdings (41% UltraTech, 9% Grasim, 50% Hindalco) to facilitate cheaper borrowings for acquiring aircraft and ships for business use.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia crisis creating volatility in pet coke and fuel costs.
- Weak monsoon impacting farm incomes and rural housing with a lag in specific drought-affected pockets (e.g., South Maharashtra).
- Squeezed domestic coal availability due to priority allocation to thermal power plants.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Indrajit Agarwal (CLSA):** Asked about H2 variable cost inflation and Ultravolt strategy/pricing. Management stated fuel cost inventory lag will have a marginal impact in Q4, and Ultravolt is priced at market parity with differentiation driven by product proposition (Class 5 insulation/e-beam tech) rather than undercutting.
- **Amit Murarka (Axis Capital):** Asked about India Cements Tamil Nadu levy and interest cost jump. Management confirmed the levy was already accounted for in Q2, and interest cost rose due to the 2-month impact of INR 5,000 crores NCDs raised at 7.25%.
- **Raashi (Citigroup):** Asked about percentage price increase in Sep/Oct and Ultravolt revenue targets. Management stated spot realizations are ~5% higher than Q2 average, and clarified that the INR 10,000 crores revenue target for Wires & Cables will scale to INR 16,000-18,000 crores based on copper price fluctuations.
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec India):** Asked about low-price fuel contracts and cost improvement drivers. Management noted India Cements kilns will undergo major shutdowns and upgrades for WHRS coolers and preheaters, targeting a robust performance by Q4 FY28.
- **Siddharth Mehrotra (Kotak Securities):** Asked about the corporate guarantee given to UHG Holdings. Management explained it is a shared group entity (UltraTech 41%, Grasim 9%, Hindalco 50%) procuring aircraft and ships for business use, with the guarantee securing finer interest rates.

### One-line takeaway
UltraTech delivered strong volume growth and a 21% increase in net profit in Q2 despite monsoon headwinds and fuel cost pressures, driven by operating leverage, robust retail/non-trade demand, and rising exit prices.
