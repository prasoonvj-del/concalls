# UltraTech Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong volume growth of 13.8%, market share gains, improving realizations exiting September, and resilient demand despite severe cost inflation in fuel.

### Key numbers
- **Net Profit Growth:** 21% YoY.
- **Volume Growth:** 13.8% YoY (vs estimated industry growth of ~10% YoY).
- **Capacity Utilization:** 71% (vs 66% in Q2 last year).
  - North and South: >70%.
  - West: ~70%.
  - Central and East: ~60%.
- **Domestic Cement Capacity:** 204.7 million tonnes (added 4.6 million tonnes in September).
- **Fuel Costs:** Increased YoY from Rs 45/bag to Rs 51.5/bag (up 13% QoQ; rate at Rs 2.09/kcal).
- **Fixed Costs:** Other fixed costs per ton dropped 6% YoY as absolute fixed costs grew only 4% YoY against 13.8% volume growth.
- **Product Mix:** Blended cement share at 67.3%; Premium cement share at 37%.
- **Trade Sales Ratio:** Maintained steady between 65% and 67%.

### Highlights
- Launched the Wires and Cables business under the brand name **Ultravolt** on September 3, 2026. Over 1,500 UltraTech Building Solutions (UBS) channel partners signed up, with retail reach exceeding 80,000 points across 15,000 pin codes.
- Added 116 MW of renewable energy and 10 MW of waste heat recovery systems (WHRS) during the quarter; non-fossil fuel mix reached 47%. UltraTech became the first manufacturing sector company to achieve 2 GW of captive green power capacity.
- Thermal substitution rate (AFR) reached 7.2% after commissioning AFR systems at 2 plants.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects strong cement demand momentum to continue through March 2029 (FY29), with industry growth led by infrastructure, urban real estate, private capex, and industrial projects.
- Management maintains full-year capex guidance at around Rs 10,000 crore.
- Capacity expansion plans to reach 240 million tonnes remain on track without surprises, and management expects to announce its next phase of organic expansion beyond 240 million tonnes before the end of FY27.
- Management expects the EBITDA per ton target of Rs 1,400 to be delivered as cost inflation gets offset by price increases.
- Net debt to EBITDA is expected to reduce below 1.0x by March 2027 (end of FY27).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall cement industry Q2 YoY volume growth estimated near 10%. 
- Regional volume growth for UltraTech: North grew >20%, South grew 15%–20%, West grew ~10%, Central grew 10%, and East grew ~5%.
- South markets are seeing strong momentum in volume, capacity utilization, and pricing.
- Weak monsoons across India kept construction activity going with fewer lost days, offsetting the typical seasonal volume dip.
- Torrential rains and severe flooding in Eastern markets (West Bengal, Bihar) temporarily impacted volumes and prices in the region.
- 40% of the rural economy is now driven by non-crop activity (MSME, non-agri sectors), cushioning rural demand against localized rainfall deficits.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel costs increased by 13% QoQ and raw material costs rose 3% QoQ due to the West Asia conflict and pet coke price spikes (pet coke spot around $180/tonne).
- Consumption mix of pet coke was reduced to 25% to mitigate cost pressures. Spot fuel kcal cost rose to ~Rs 2.1–Rs 2.2/kcal ($140–$150/tonne equivalent).
- Coal availability for non-power sector remains tight as Coal India prioritizes thermal power plants. Industrial diesel prices hit all-time highs, driving up mining and freight costs.
- Pan-India trade realization exited September 3% above the Q1 FY27 average, with prices rising Rs 7 to Rs 10 per bag month-on-month in late September and further in October.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned 4.6 million tonnes of domestic cement capacity in September: 3.6 million tonnes greenfield capacity at Petnikota (Andhra Pradesh) and 1.0 million tonnes via debottlenecking at 3 units.
- Ultravolt Capex: Spent/committed ~Rs 1,100 crore out of the total planned Rs 1,800 crore investment for 78,000 tonnes annualized copper consumption capacity. 
- Refurbishment, kiln upgrades, WHRS installation, and preheater height extension at India Cements plants are underway, with significant cost benefits expected by Q4 FY28.
- UBS store footprint currently stands at 6,500+ stores, targeted to expand to 10,000 stores over the next 3 years (FY28-FY29).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to EBITDA stood at 1.26x at end-September 2026 (up from 0.94x as of March 2026), affected by seasonal dividend payouts and capex spending.
- Issued Rs 5,000 crore NCDs at 7.25% coupon rate for 2 months to lock in borrowing costs.
- Issued corporate guarantee for group shared asset entity UHG Holdings (41% owned by UltraTech, 50% Hindalco, 9% Grasim) to acquire aircraft and ships for business operations at optimal interest rates.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical conflict in West Asia pushing pet coke, coal, and energy prices higher.
- Elevated industrial diesel prices increasing raw material mining and transport expenses.
- Severe localized drought situations in parts of Southern Maharashtra and potential risks to rural housing demand from weak farm income.
- Tamil Nadu state levy on India Cements (Rs 160 per ton) accounted for in Q2 without relief.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Indrajit Agarwal (CLSA):** *What is the variable cost inflation outlook for H2 FY27 given high pet coke prices ($180/t)?*  
  **Management:** Spot fuel cost is Rs 2.1–Rs 2.2/kcal. Because of inventory lag and a mix of domestic and imported fuel, Q3 will see minimal marginal impact; slight cost increase of Rs 10–Rs 20 per bag might filter into Q4, but operating leverage will offset fixed costs.
- **Amit Murarka (Axis Capital):** *Clarification on Ultravolt product lines and full-year capex guidance?*  
  **Management:** Ultravolt will produce wires, light-duty cables, power/control cables, and PVC conduits. It will not enter consumer appliances like fans, decorative lighting, or bulbs. H1 capex was Rs 5,882 crore; full-year capex guidance is maintained at ~Rs 10,000 crore.
- **Prateek Kumar (Jefferies):** *Will EBITDA per ton stay higher YoY in H3/H4 given recent price increases?*  
  **Management:** Yes, price hikes taken in September/October (~5% over Q2 average) are intended to fully absorb cost pressures and keep YoY unit EBITDA higher.
- **Raashi (Citigroup):** *What revenue potential does Ultravolt hold at full ramp-up?*  
  **Management:** Installed copper capacity (78,000 tonnes) makes UltraTech #2 in the market. Due to higher copper prices since early 2025, the initial Rs 10,000 crore revenue potential at full capacity translates to ~Rs 16,000–Rs 18,000 crore on current copper prices. Fiscal 2028 will see a mature operational performance.
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec):** *What is the timeline for profitability improvement at India Cements?*  
  **Management:** Currently clocking Rs 500/ton EBITDA in India Cements books. After kilns undergo major shutdowns for WHRS and efficiency retrofits, profitability will reach four-digit EBITDA per ton by Q4 FY28.

### One-line takeaway
UltraTech demonstrated strong volume growth of 13.8% and high capacity utilization (71%), relying on operating leverage and exit-quarter price hikes to absorb rising fuel and energy costs.
