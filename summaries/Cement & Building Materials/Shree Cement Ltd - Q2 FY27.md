# Shree Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong volume growth (18% YoY in India) and capacity expansions despite seasonal quarters and higher fuel costs.

### Key numbers
* **India Sales Volume:** 9.31 million tonnes, up ~18% YoY (7.91 million tonnes in Q2 FY26) and down 11% QoQ (10.49 million tonnes in Q1 FY27).
* **Consolidated Sales Volume:** 10.52 million tonnes, up ~14% YoY (9.23 million tonnes in Q2 FY26) and down 8% QoQ (11.45 million tonnes in Q1 FY27).
* **Realization:** Rs 4,870 per ton, up 1% YoY (Rs 4,840 per ton) and down 1% QoQ (Rs 4,919 per ton).
* **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 1,016 crore, up ~4% YoY (Rs 974 crore) and down QoQ (Rs 1,272 crore).
* **Consolidated EBITDA per Ton:** Rs 966, down YoY from Rs 1,055 and down QoQ from Rs 1,111.
* **Blended Cost of Production:** Rs 4,636 per ton compared to Rs 4,329 QoQ and Rs 4,245 YoY.
* **Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) Revenue:** Rs 129 crore, up 182% YoY; volume up 153% YoY to 2.73 lakh cubic meters.

### Highlights
* India capacity utilization reached 56% compared to 52% in Q2 FY26.
* Premium cement sales share increased to 29% of total volume (vs 21% YoY and 23% QoQ).
* Green power share reached 65.2% of total electricity consumption (total green power capacity at 667 MW).
* RMC commercial plant network reached 28 plants (35 including captive units) and remained EBITDA positive.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management targets full-year FY27 India volumes between 40 million to 41 million tonnes.
* Target overall capacity objective remains 80 million tonnes in the short term.
* Premium product share aimed to cross 40% in the coming years.
* Tax rate guidance stands at 31% to 32% for FY27 under the old tax regime.
* Management expects H2 FY27 capex to be between Rs 700 crore and Rs 800 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Management estimates industry demand growth was between 7% to 10% in Q2 FY27.
* Growth continues to be supported by infrastructure spending, roads, railways, housing, and data centers, offset slightly by a weak monsoon impacting rural demand.
* Regional volume growth was led by South India, followed by North and East.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Landed fuel cost rose to Rs 2.09 per kCal vs Rs 1.66 YoY and Rs 1.95 QoQ; expected to increase to Rs 2.15–2.20 per kCal in Q3 FY27.
* Fuel mix in Q2 FY27: Pet coke 11% (down from 66% last year), imported coal 75%, Indian coal 2%, alternate fuel 12%.
* Thermal Substitution Rate (TSR) / AFR consumption improved to 5% from ~2% level.
* Lead distance reduced to 427 km (vs 445 km QoQ and 441 km YoY). Rail transport share was 8.5% vs 9% QoQ and 11% YoY.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* H1 FY27 capex stood at Rs 895 crore.
* Step-down subsidiary Union Cement Company (UAE) commissioned a 3 million tonnes cement mill in September 2026, bringing total UAE capacity to 7 million tonnes per annum.
* Greenfield integrated plant construction in Meghalaya is progressing and scheduled for commissioning in Q4 FY28.
* Total power capacity is 1,170 MW (503 MW thermal CPP, 667 MW green power); 80 MW of additional green power capacity is under execution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Consolidated net cash balance stands at Rs 8,168 crore.
* Trade receivables increased by ~Rs 300 crore in H1.
* Inventory change impact in Q2 FY27 was ~Rs 100 crore due to a 33% YoY increase in clinker production; full-year inventory change expected at Rs 60 crore to Rs 70 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Fuel cost inflation impacting per-ton EBITDA margins.
* Weak monsoon potentially dampening rural demand recovery in the near term.
* External cost volatilities such as packaging bag costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Q: Outlook on fuel costs for coming quarters?**  
  *A:* Landed cost was Rs 2.09 per kCal in Q2 FY27 and is guided to be around Rs 2.15 to Rs 2.20 per kCal in Q3 FY27 based on purchase pipelines.
* **Q: Are you withdrawing from low-remunerative markets as per channel feedback?**  
  *A:* Management clarified this is untrue; penetration is actively expanding in regions like J&K, Gujarat, and South Karnataka.
* **Q: Rationale for setting up a subsidiary in China via UAE?**  
  *A:* China is a major hub for equipment/spares; the unit serves as an experimental initiative to learn Chinese engineering practices, source cheaper equipment/spares, and bypass complex direct European/Indian logistics routes via UAE.
* **Q: What is the cause of the drop in freight costs per ton?**  
  *A:* Achieved via reduced run-to-market distances, seasonal lean-season railway discounts, and new data-driven freight fixation systems; no one-offs involved.

### One-line takeaway
Shree Cement delivered robust double-digit volume growth and expanding premiumization despite seasonal margin pressure from higher fuel costs.
