# Shree Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Volume growth was strong at 18% YoY in India despite a soft season, and pricing/realizations improved sequentially in October.

### Key numbers
- **India Sales Volumes:** 9.31 million tonnes, up 18% YoY (down 11% QoQ from 10.49 million tonnes).
- **Consol Sales Volumes:** 10.52 million tonnes, up 14% YoY (down 8% QoQ from 11.45 million tonnes).
- **Realizations:** INR4,870 per ton, up 1% YoY from INR4,840 per ton (down 1% QoQ from INR4,919 per ton).
- **Consol EBITDA:** INR1,016 crores, up 4% YoY from INR974 crores (down 20% QoQ from INR1,272 crores).
- **Consol EBITDA per ton:** INR966, down from INR1,055 YoY and INR1,111 QoQ.
- **Capacity Utilization (India):** 56% compared to 52% in Q2 FY26.
- **Landed Fuel Cost:** INR2.09 per kCal vs INR1.66 YoY and INR1.95 in Q1 FY27.
- **Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) Volumes:** 2.73 lakh cubic meters, up 153% YoY.
- **RMC Revenue:** INR129 crores, up 182% YoY.
- **Green Electricity Share:** 65.2% of total electricity consumption.
- **Green Power Capacity:** 667 MW.
- **H1 Capex:** INR895 crores.
- **Consolidated Net Cash:** INR8,168 crores.
- **Premium Product Share:** 29%.

### Highlights
- Passed away: Management paid tribute to late Special Advisor Mr. Ashok Bhandari.
- Union Cement Company (UAE step-down subsidiary) commissioned a new 3 million tonnes cement mill in September '26, taking total UAE capacity to 7 million tonnes per annum.
- RMC network reached 28 commercial plants (35 total including captive units); segment remained EBITDA positive.
- Established a subsidiary/office in China to evaluate sourcing project spares and equipment more efficiently.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Volume Guidance:** Management expects full-year volumes between 40 million to 41 million tonnes, anticipating growth slightly better than the industry (industry growth estimated at 7% to 10%).
- **Fuel Cost Guidance:** Landed fuel cost expected to be around INR2.15 to INR2.20 per kCal in Q3.
- **Capex Guidance:** H2 FY27 capex expected to be INR700 crores to INR800 crores.
- **Tax Rate Guidance:** Effective tax rate expected to remain between 31% to 32% for the year (old tax regime).
- **Fuel Cost Outlook:** Q3 cost expected to be broadly similar to Q2.
- **Capacity Goal:** Committed to reaching 80 million tonnes capacity in the short term.
- **Premium Share Goal:** Aiming to cross 40% premium product share in a few years.
- **UAE Volume Outlook:** UAE operations expected to do around 4 million to 5 million tonnes annualized total volume.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 is traditionally a softer quarter, further impacted by West Asian conflict and weak monsoons in most regions (East was an exception).
- Demand supported by infrastructure, railways, housing, and data centers.
- Regional volume growth led by South India, followed by North and East.
- Trade to non-trade mix stood at 61% trade and 39% non-trade.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA per ton declined YoY primarily due to higher fuel costs.
- Fuel mix in Q2: Imported coal 75%, alternative fuels 12%, pet coke 11%, Indian coal 2%.
- Thermal Substitution Rate (TSR/AFR) increased to ~5%.
- Blended cost of production: INR4,636 per ton (vs INR4,329 in Q1 and INR4,245 in Q2 FY25).
- Freight cost reduced due to lower run-to-market, special railway lean-season discounts, and new data-based freight fixation tools. Rail mix was 8.5% and lead distance 427 km.
- Blended cement share was 58%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Greenfield integrated plant in Meghalaya is progressing; workforce and contractors mobilized, expected to be commissioned by 31st March 2028.
- Organic capacity additions typically take about 3 years to reach full utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net cash stands at INR8,168 crores.
- Trade receivables increased by INR300 crores during the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Weak monsoon posing near-term risks to rural demand.
- External cost volatility (e.g., packaging bag costs) remains difficult to forecast.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Parth Bhavsar (Investec) on Fuel Costs:** Management clarified landed cost rose to INR2.09 per kCal and guided to INR2.20 for Q3.
- **Pinakin (HSBC) on Pricing:** Management noted October prices are better than September, and internal premium product push will help support realizations.
- **Rashi (Citigroup) on Volume Guidance:** Management confirmed full-year volume guidance of 40-41 million tonnes.
- **Amit Murarka (Axis Capital) on Trade/Non-Trade Strategy:** Management clarified that non-trade increase is driven by participation in core national/infrastructure projects, and trade remains their core bread-and-butter.
- **Siddharth Mehrotra (Kotak Securities) on Meghalaya Plant Protests:** Management stated state authorities cleared doubts, contractors are mobilized, and construction will start very soon with no delay anticipated beyond FY28 commissioning.
- **Jashandeep Chadha (Nomura) on UAE Rationale:** Management defended UAE expansion, citing robust local demand (e.g., Dubai airport expansion), the ability to convert clinker to cement, and export potential for specialized products like oil well cement.

### One-line takeaway
Shree Cement delivered robust 18% YoY volume growth in India during a seasonally weak quarter, backed by efficiency gains, improved premium product penetration, and a strong net cash balance sheet of INR8,168 crores.
