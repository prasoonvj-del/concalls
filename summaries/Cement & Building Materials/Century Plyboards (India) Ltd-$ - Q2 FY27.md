# Century Plyboards (India) Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever consolidated revenue and improved profitability driven by robust growth across segments.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 1,780 crore (up 28.5% YoY)
- EBITDA (excl. forex losses): Rs 232.3 crore (margin at 13.1%, compared to Rs 181.6 crore YoY)
- PAT: Rs 103.6 crore (up 46% YoY from Rs 70.9 crore)
- Plywood segment revenue growth: 28.6% YoY and 14% QoQ; EBITDA margin: 15.7%
- Laminate segment revenue growth: 20.6% YoY; EBITDA margin: 11.6%
- MDF segment revenue growth: 10.5% YoY and 13.6% QoQ; EBITDA margin (excl. forex): 8.9% (sequential improvement)
- Particle Board segment revenue: Crossed Rs 100 crore milestone; growth: 211% YoY and 16.8% QoQ; EBITDA margin (excl. forex): 7.4% (up from 3.7% in Q1)
- Logistics segment revenue: Rs 58.8 crore (up 72.7% YoY)
- Return on Equity (ROE): 16.6% (vs 13.1% in FY26)
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 16.67% (vs 13.4%)
- Long-term debt-to-EBITDA target: 1:1

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly consolidated revenue of Rs 1,780 crore.
- Launched a new campaign featuring Aamir Khan for the Total Cover Guarantee (TCG) in August, a warranty covering specified manufacturing defects across plywood, laminates, veneers, adhesives, labor, and transportation.
- Plywood 60,000 CBM greenfield plant at Hoshiarpur commenced operations in Q3 FY27.
- Particle Board crossed Rs 100 crore quarterly revenue for the first time.
- Logistics business neared EBITDA breakeven in only its second quarter of operation.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects MDF industry growth of 17%-18% for the current year and aims for double-digit EBITDA margins in the second half of the year.
- Management expects a gradual recovery in MDF margins from next year, and a return to the 20% margin trajectory from FY29 onwards.
- Laminates segment revenue growth targeted at 20% plus for the year.
- Plywood margin guidance set at 14%-15% for the year due to marketing spends.
- Long-term debt-to-EBITDA target is maintained at 1:1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- MDF industry is experiencing an overcapacity situation with 30%+ capacity additions happening over a 12-month window.
- Wood availability has improved compared to last year; company faces no wood availability challenges.
- BIS regulation implementation is a long process across 3,000-4,000 domestic manufacturers, though imports have stopped.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Plywood margins impacted slightly by upcoming marketing expenses for the TCG campaign.
- MDF margins under pressure due to overcapacity and raw material cost pressures.
- Focus on green energy and renewables to reduce fuel and energy costs in MDF and Particle Board.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Hoshiarpur plywood plant (60,000 CBM) commenced operations in Q3 FY27.
- Guwahati and Kandla brownfield plywood expansions progressing as planned for production start in July '27.
- Received letter of allotment from the UP government for land acquisition; Phase 1 will be a plywood capacity of 108,000 cubic meters/annum. MDF plant in UP has no firm commissioning date yet.
- Debt has peaked out; large-scale capex will only be considered once net debt-to-EBITDA returns to disciplined levels.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to EBITDA is elevated at 2.4x as of September due to capex and a 60-day working capital cycle supporting high revenue growth.
- Cash flows remain robust; management plans to channel most profit generation into debt repayment, expecting a substantial reduction in debt levels next year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Overcapacity in the MDF industry leading to depressed margins (around 10% in H2, down from historical 20%).
- Elevated debt levels and working capital requirements due to rapid revenue growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sneha (Nuvama) on MDF growth/margins:** Management clarified that 17%-18% growth is targeted for the year and double-digit margins are expected only for H2 due to overcapacity.
- **Rahul Agarwal on Debt/Capex:** Management confirmed that total debt has peaked out, long-term debt-to-EBITDA target is 1:1, and large capex will not be pursued if debt-to-EBITDA exceeds 1x.
- **Sagnik Sarkar on Plywood volume growth vs peers:** Nikita Bansal explained growth is driven by 7% price hikes in April, brand building, distribution, and stakeholder focus rather than immediate large-scale impacts from BIS compliance.
- **Bhavin Rupani on Wood Sourcing:** Management stated that wood availability has actually improved compared to last year and is 100% domestic for MDF.
- **Hrishikesh Bhagat on Debt Peak:** Management confirmed debt has peaked out and cash flows/EBITDA (targeted near Rs 900 crores) will be utilized for debt repayment in the absence of large capex.

### One-line takeaway
Century Plyboards delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with record revenue of Rs 1,780 crore and improved return ratios, while managing elevated working capital debt and an oversupplied MDF market.
