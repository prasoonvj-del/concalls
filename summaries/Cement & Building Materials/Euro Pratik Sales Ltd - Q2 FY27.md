# Euro Pratik Sales Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever revenue, quarterly EBITDA, and profit in the history of the company despite challenging operating environments and input cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 115.7 crore (up 19.8% YoY from Rs 96.6 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 219 crore (up 35.9% YoY from Rs 161.1 crore)
- Operating EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 37.4 crore (up 18% YoY from Rs 31.7 crore)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 31.4% (vs 32.5% in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27): 29.6%
- Profit After Tax - PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 26.2 crore (up 14.9% YoY from Rs 22.8 crore)
- PAT Margin (Q2 FY27): 22%
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 46.2 crore
- PAT Margin (H1 FY27): 20.6%
- Core Business Gross Margin: 44.8%
- Core Business EBITDA Margin: 36%
- Core Business Net Profit Margin: 26.5%
- Drashti Surfaces Acquisition Stake & Cost: 51% stake for up to Rs 31.12 crore plus a loan commitment of up to Rs 20 crore
- Drashti Surfaces Peak Turnover Capacity: Rs 180 crore
- Drashti Surfaces Capacity Utilisation: 70% to 75% in laminates, 45% to 50% in veneer
- Drashti Surfaces EBITDA Margin: 16% to 17%
- Working capital: Around 150 days plus or minus 10% (excluding integration distortions)
- Cash and cash equivalents: Rs 8.4 crore as of September 2026 (down from Rs 16.4 crore in March 2026)
- Monthly Net Cash Surplus from Operations: Rs 7.5 crore

### Highlights
- Achieved the highest ever revenue, quarterly EBITDA, and profit in company history.
- Announced the acquisition of a 51% gross stake in Drashti Surfaces Private Limited (with manufacturing in Bhilad and Silvassa), adding in-house manufacturing for laminates and veneers, institutional relationships, and export presence. (Our Record note: The call aligns with the Nov 5 investor deck regarding the acquisition for ₹31.12 crores to build an integrated platform with in-house manufacturing).
- Core business continues to be driven by new product launches, with approximately 75% of core sales coming from products launched within the last 12 to 14 months.
- Decorative wall panels contribute 65.5% of Q2 FY27 revenue, while laminates contribute 24%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a target to grow at 25% to 30% CAGR over the next 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management sees strong long-term prospects driven by increased premiumization in living and commercial spaces, rising urbanization, demand for sustainable/design-led solutions, and a shift towards organized brands.
- Demand is strong in upper-middle-class and luxury segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating environment challenged by geopolitical uncertainties, crude oil volatility, elevated raw material costs, and rising freight expenses.
- Consolidated EBITDA margin slightly moderated to 31.4% (from 32.5% YoY) due to the consolidation of acquired entities operating at lower margins (18% to 19% EBITDA level) compared to the core business.
- Core business margins remained steady due to operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Drashti Surfaces acquisition adds manufacturing capacity in paper laminates and veneers with headroom for growth.
- Drashti's Bhilad facility includes surplus FSI of close to 180,000 square feet, which can be utilized for future expansion (including potential future wall panel manufacturing).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents declined to Rs 8.4 crore in September 2026 from Rs 16.4 crore in March 2026 due to deployment in acquisitions and operations.
- Acquisitions (URO Veneer World, Chawla Brothers, and Drashti Surfaces) funded through internal accruals and operational cash flow.
- Company maintains a monthly operating cash surplus of Rs 7.5 crore and aims to remain a net-debt-free company.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External headwinds include ongoing geopolitical uncertainties, volatile crude oil prices, elevated raw material costs, and rising freight expenses impacting supply chains and input costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Santosh Shetty (LGC Capital) asked about increasing working capital days.** Alpesh Sangoi explained that balance sheet consolidation of URO Veneer World distorted March 2026 figures, and normalized working capital remains around 150 days (+/- 10%). (Management did not dodge).
- **Saumya Raghuvanshi (Nirva Securities) asked about the revenue share contribution of laminates.** Kulmeet Saggu answered that wall panels will contribute 50% to 55%, with the rest from laminates and veneers, maintaining a stable product composition. (Management did not dodge).
- **Prashant Sanghvi (SME Universe) asked about Drashti's peak capacity value and margin impact.** Alpesh Sangoi stated Drashti's peak turnover potential is Rs 180 crore, with current EBITDA margins at 16% to 17% which will expand with higher capacity utilization. (Management did not dodge).
- **Harsh (Kabra Group Office) asked if the Drashti acquisition will help manufacture wall panels.** Alpesh Sangoi clarified Drashti currently makes laminates and veneers, but the surplus 180,000 sq. ft. FSI lays the groundwork to evaluate in-house wall panel manufacturing in stages. (Management did not dodge).
- **Nimish Verma (Ventura Securities) asked about declining cash balances and increasing borrowings.** Alpesh Sangoi stated cash was utilised for past acquisitions (URO Veneer World and Chawla Brothers totaling Rs 110 crore cash outflow), but healthy monthly operational cash generation of Rs 7.5 crore maintains balance sheet strength. (Management did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
Euro Pratik delivered record-high Q2 revenue and profit despite external cost headwinds and expanded its manufacturing capabilities via the strategic acquisition of Drashti Surfaces.
