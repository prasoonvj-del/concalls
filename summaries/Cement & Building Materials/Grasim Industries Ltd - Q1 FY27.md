# Grasim Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever consolidated revenues with robust growth across core and new business engines.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: INR48,716 crore (up 21% YoY)
- Standalone Revenue: INR11,795 crore (up 28% YoY)
- Standalone EBITDA: INR1,094 crore (up 107% YoY)
- Birla Opus Paints Revenue: INR1,661 crore (up 64% YoY, up 17% QoQ)
- Birla Pivot Revenue: INR2,548 crore (up 75% YoY)
- Cellulosic Fibres (CSF) Revenue: INR4,530 crore (up 12% YoY); Volumes down 4% YoY
- Chemicals Segment Revenue: INR2,640 crore (up 10% YoY); EBITDA at INR491 crore (up 16% YoY)
- Consolidated Cement Sales Volume: 41.31 million tonnes (up 12% YoY); EBITDA at INR5,146 crore (up 12% YoY)
- Standalone Capex (Q1 FY27): INR375 crore (12% of budget); Full-year guidance at INR3,157 crore
- Consolidated Net Debt to TTM EBITDA: 1.45x (as of 30th June 2026) vs 1.62x YoY
- Standalone Net Debt: INR9,899 crore

### Highlights
- Achieved 24th consecutive quarter of YoY revenue growth.
- Birla Opus emerged as India's third largest decorative paints brand by revenue and largest by installed capacity; premium/luxury portfolio contributes ~65% of sales value.
- Birla Pivot annualized run rate crossed INR10,000 crore.
- UltraTech added 8.7 million tonnes of gray cement capacity in Q1, taking total capacity to 205.5 million tonnes.
- Aditya Birla Capital lending portfolio grew 32% to ~INR2,20,000 crore.
- Implemented a brand royalty payment of 0.25% of standalone revenue payable to Birla Holdings effective 1st June (estimated ~INR100 crore impact for Grasim standalone).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for over 50% YoY revenue growth for Birla Opus in FY27 and maintained the target of reaching INR10,000 crore revenue by FY28.
- Birla Opus and Birla Pivot both reiterated expectations to reach EBITDA break-even by the exit of FY27.
- Standalone capex for FY27 is budgeted at INR3,157 crore (45% dedicated to growth capex).
- Management expects consolidated net debt to EBITDA to remain below 2.0x for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India remains a bright spot; RBI revised FY27 GDP growth forecast upwards to 6.7%.
- Global economy faces challenges from geopolitical conflicts, supply chain realignments, freight volatility, and subdued recovery in China.
- Cotton constraints are structurally driving demand toward cellulosic fibers (Liva brand).
- Paints industry absorbed a double-digit raw material cost shock in Q1, leading to phased price increases (8.8% cumulative impact in Q1 for Birla Opus).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Birla Opus absorbed a raw material cost shock (COGS up 20-25%), managed via phased price increases.
- CSF specialty fiber mix improved from 21% to 27%, supporting margins despite volume degrowth.
- Caustic soda ECU realizations up 6% YoY to INR37,955 per tonne.
- UltraTech benefited from lower logistics and power costs, with green power mix rising to 45.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Birla Opus manufacturing footprint stands at 1,332 million litres per annum across 6 plants.
- CSF Phase 1 Lyocell project (55,000 TPA; INR1,350 crore) undergoing civil work; Phase 2 (110,000 TPA; INR3,094 crore) awaiting environmental clearance.
- Chemicals: CPVC commissioned; ECH commissioning in Q2 FY27; chlorine integration expected to reach 68% by exit of FY27.
- Cement: Gray cement capacity reached 205.5 million tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt to TTM EBITDA improved to 1.45x (vs 1.62x YoY).
- Standalone net debt rose to INR9,899 crore due to timing differences between investments in AB Capital and upcoming UltraTech dividends.
- Grasim's equity contribution to AB Renewables transaction expected to be less than INR1,000 crore in FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts in Europe and the Middle East disrupting shipping routes, freight costs, and raw material pricing.
- Near-term margin pressure in chemicals due to expensive inventory from Q1 and volatile petrochemical feedstock/shipping markets.
- Subdued downstream demand and planned maintenance impacting CSF volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities):** Asked about sequential paint growth parity with peers. Management clarified that Q1 industry numbers included extra channel stocking due to price hikes, which new entrants like Birla Opus couldn't leverage to the same extent, reiterating long-term guidance.
- **Prateek Kumar (Antique Stock Broking):** Inquired about the new brand royalty payment. Management confirmed a 0.25% royalty on standalone revenue (approx. INR100 crore impact) payable to Birla Holdings, capped at INR225 crore.
- **Jay Doshi (Kotak):** Asked about full-year revenue growth expectations for paints. Management confirmed expectations of over 50% YoY revenue growth for FY27.
- **Raashi (Anubhuti Advisors):** Asked about chemical segment sustainability. Management highlighted high volatility due to Gulf conflicts, stating second-quarter margins face pressure from high-cost Q1 inventory.
- **Nirav Jimodia (Withers/Elara):** Asked about caustic soda pricing risks and domestic capacity additions. Management stated domestic prices track international import parity and chlorine integration (CPVC/ECH) shields them.

### One-line takeaway
Grasim delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 21% consolidated revenue growth, propelled by compounding core operations and rapid scaling of new growth engines like Birla Opus and Birla Pivot.
