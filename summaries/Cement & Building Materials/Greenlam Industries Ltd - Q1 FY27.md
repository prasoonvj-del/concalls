# Greenlam Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 18% YoY, and the chipboard segment turned EBITDA positive for the first time, despite geopolitical headwinds impacting exports and input costs.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 797 crore (up 18% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (before forex):** Rs 81 crore (up 48% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (before forex):** 10.2% (vs 8.1% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Gross Margin:** 52.9% (vs 53.1% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 21 crore (vs net loss of Rs 15.5 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Finance Cost:** Rs 20 crore (down 25% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 934 crore (as of June).

### Highlights
*   **Chipboard Segment:** Revenue grew ~200% YoY to Rs 95 crore; segment turned EBITDA positive (Rs 3.4 crore before forex) vs a loss of Rs 10 crore in Q1 FY26. Capacity utilization improved to 61% (vs 30% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Laminate Segment:** Revenue grew 7% YoY to Rs 596 crore. Sales volume was 4.62 million sheets (down 6% YoY due to postponed export shipments). Capacity utilization was 80%.
*   **Plywood & Allied:** Revenue grew 20% YoY to Rs 106 crore. EBITDA loss narrowed to Rs 5 crore (vs Rs 9 crore in Q1 FY26). Sales volume grew 19% YoY.
*   **Export Impact:** Approximately Rs 27 crore of export shipments were postponed to Q2 due to container/vessel availability issues caused by West Asia conflicts.
*   **Price Management:** Implemented price hikes in April/May to offset raw material inflation (chemicals); reduced prices in June/July as raw material costs softened. Net price hike passed on was 7-8%.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains the 10-12% revenue growth target for the laminate segment for FY27.
*   **Plywood:** Management expects the segment to achieve EBITDA breakeven within FY27.
*   **Chipboard:** Management expects to maintain ~70% capacity utilization for FY27.
*   **Debt:** Management expects debt to reduce by approximately Rs 100 crore in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Domestic Demand:** Remained stable; retail and distribution-led business held up well, while project business faced challenges due to rising building material costs.
*   **Geopolitics:** West Asia conflicts caused volatility in input prices, freight costs, and logistics lead times.
*   **Chipboard Market:** Management believes there is a shift from unorganized to organized players, supported by high product acceptability for new categories like HMR.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Chemical Costs:** Chemicals constitute ~one-third of raw material costs; prices were volatile, impacting laminate and chipboard segments most significantly.
*   **Operating Leverage:** EBITDA margin expansion was driven by revenue growth, cost controls, and operating leverage in decorative veneer, engineered flooring, and chipboard.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex Budget:** Rs 130-135 crore, including Rs 70 crore for laminate expansion.
*   **Laminate Expansion:** Two new press lines are on track for commercial production by Q4 FY27.
*   **Chipboard:** No major capacity additions planned; focus is on sweating existing assets and increasing the share of pre-laminated chipboard.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Cycle improved by 3 days to 56 days (vs 59 days in Q1 FY26).
*   **Debt Reduction:** Management plans to use cash flows (after maintenance capex) to reduce debt over the next 2-3 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Logistics:** Ongoing container and vessel availability issues could impact export sales if they persist through the current quarter.
*   **Raw Material Volatility:** Management noted the "hazy" picture regarding chemical price trends due to geopolitical instability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Plywood Breakeven:** Management clarified that the expected EBITDA breakeven is for the full year (or specific quarter), not necessarily a permanent state, and PAT-level breakeven will be a subsequent step.
*   **On Chipboard Margins:** Management stated that 18-20% margins are achievable once the segment reaches optimum capacity utilization, likely by FY29.
*   **On Export Postponement:** Management confirmed that the Rs 27 crore of postponed export revenue is not lost but moved to the current quarter.
*   **On Debt Reduction:** Management confirmed that despite Rs 135 crore capex, they target a Rs 100 crore debt reduction in FY27, with debt repayment becoming the primary focus from FY28 onwards.

### One-line takeaway
Greenlam delivered a strong turnaround in the chipboard segment and improved overall profitability despite export shipment delays and raw material volatility, with a clear focus on debt reduction and sweating existing assets.
