# Ambuja Cements Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reported resilient operation amid high energy costs, monsoon disruption, and regional drought conditions, while outlining a major Rs 14,500 crore expansion plan.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 total cost target of Rs 4,250/tonne; reaffirmed FY28 cost target of ~Rs 4,000/tonne.
- **Tone and demand view:** Demand view turned more cautious for near-term H2 due to drought declarations in key states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra, Rajasthan), though management raised its H2 volume growth expectations to high single/near double digits.
- **New topics or risks:** Detailed breakdown of a new 30 MTPA cement and 10.3 MTPA clinker expansion plan across 14 projects; drought declaration across key operating states flagged as a fresh demand headwind.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on planned Q2 capacity additions (Dahej, Salai Banwa, Kalamboli commissioned; Bathinda and Jodhpur stabilized).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 9,197 crore
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1,271 crore
- **EBITDA Margin:** ~14% (EBITDA per tonne at Rs 770)
- **PAT:** Rs 236 crore
- **Cement Sales Volume:** 16.5 million metric tonnes
- **Total Operating Cost:** Rs 4,410 per metric tonne (Rs 4,182/tonne on a normalized basis excluding geopolitical impact)
- **Trade Volume Share:** 74% (up 6% YoY; volume up 8.6% YoY)
- **Premium Product Share:** 37% of trade sales (vs 35% YoY)
- **Blended Cement Share:** 82% (vs 77% YoY)
- **Green Power Share:** 61% (Net power cost at Rs 4.8 per unit vs Rs 6.0 in Q2 FY26)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs 1,070 crore
- **Net Worth:** Rs 72,238 crore

### Highlights
- Commissioned 4.6 MTPA capacity across Dahej (Gujarat), Salai Banwa (UP), and Kalamboli (Maharashtra).
- 3.2 MTPA capacity across Bathinda (Punjab) and Jodhpur (Penna, Rajasthan) has stabilized and will ramp up in H2.
- Warisaliganj (2.4 MTPA, Bihar) scheduled for commissioning in H2 FY27.
- Shareholder approval received for the proposed mergers of ACC and Orient Cement into Ambuja Cements; final NCLT approvals expected within FY27.
- Primary logistics lead distance reduced from 265 km to 254 km YoY.
- Business working capital cycle improved by 10 days.
- Realized a Rs 10 per bag price increase in October 2026.
*Call continues aggressive capacity additions breaking past pauses by formalizing a 14-project plan.*

### Guidance & outlook
- **Capacity Roadmap:** Target of 149 MTPA by FY29 and 155 MTPA by FY30.
- **Cost Targets:** Maintained FY27 target of Rs 4,250/tonne and FY28 target of Rs 4,000/tonne.
- **Volume Growth:** Targeted H2 FY27 growth ahead of the industry (high single-digit to near double-digit YoY growth).
- **Capacity Utilization:** Expected to reach ~75% exit rate by end of FY27 (up from 61–62% in Q2).
- **Product Mix:** Target to sustain blended cement ratio at 80% blended / 20% OPC.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural demand drivers remain intact, supported by infrastructure spending, urbanization, rural housing, and industrial activity.
- Near-term industry demand impacted by monsoons, global geopolitical uncertainty, and fuel cost volatility.
- Industry demand growth expected at 6% to 7% for full fiscal year FY27.
- Drought declared in several key states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra, Rajasthan), which could moderate construction activity with a lag.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel cost stood at Rs 1.87 per 1,000 kcal (blended). Fuel mix shifted heavily to domestic coal (>60% share), limiting imported fuel to <40% (petcoke ~10–12%).
- Current spot imported fuel cost stands at Rs 2.80–2.85 per 1,000 kcal.
- Green power sales generated Rs 136 crore revenue (352 million kWh units sold) in Q2, recorded under other operating income.
- Fly ash sales generated Rs 25 crore revenue in Q2. Group thermal power plants have 20 MT of fly ash supply tied up long-term.
- Combined RE power and fly ash sales contributed ~Rs 97/tonne to EBITDA.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Announced 14 expansion projects adding ~30 MTPA cement and 10.3 MTPA clinker capacity:
  - Grinding: West cluster (+15.2 MTPA), East cluster (+11.6 MTPA), North cluster (+2.9 MTPA).
  - Clinker: Maratha (+4 MTPA, Maharashtra), Umrangso (+4 MTPA, Assam), Mundra (+2.25 MTPA, Gujarat).
- Total capex for the 30 MTPA expansion estimated at ~Rs 14,500 crore (~Rs 7,500 crore/year over FY28–FY29).
- Total FY27 capex guided at Rs 6,500–7,000 crore (Rs 3,500 crore spent in H1; Rs 3,000–3,500 crore budgeted for H2).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Remains zero-debt at consolidated level with AAA (long-term) and A1+ (short-term) credit ratings.
- Net cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 1,070 crore.
- Announced capex program projected to be self-funded via internal cash flows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Deficient monsoons and official drought declarations in major states impacting near-term cement demand.
- Geopolitical conflicts causing escalation in global fuel and energy prices.
- Underutilization of acquired assets (Sanghi and Penna operating at ~50% utilization in Q2).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital): What is driving the revised expansion plan to 149 MTPA by FY29 and how will capex be funded?**  
  *Management:* Confirmed the roadmap to 149 MTPA by FY29 and 155 MTPA by FY30 is fully supported by available/planned clinker. Total capex of ~Rs 14,500 crore over 3 years (~Rs 7,500 crore/year) will be fully self-funded.
- **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities): Why launch major capex now despite soft near-term industry demand and negative H1 volume growth?**  
  *Management:* Stated that quarterly demand fluctuations do not dictate long-term strategic capex. The first capex wave is complete, and new brownfield additions target high-EBITDA, high-brand-equity markets with lower execution risk and superior logistics economics.
- **Pinakin (HSBC): Is the company shifting focus from profitability to market share through aggressive expansion?**  
  *Management:* Denied a strategy shift, emphasizing that market share growth will be calibrated with a disciplined focus on trade mix and value. Earnings expansion will rely heavily on structural cost optimization rather than market price reliance.
- **Jashandeep Chadha (Nomura): What is the current utilization rate of acquired assets like Penna and Sanghi?**  
  *Management:* Utilization currently sits at ~50%. Upgrades and kiln revamps will drive higher utilization and cost efficiencies across both assets in Q3 and Q4 FY27.
- **Rajesh Ravi (HDFC Securities): How will cost targets be met if fuel prices continue to escalate?**  
  *Management:* Expected offsets include higher domestic coal usage, rising RE power consumption, commissioning of WHRS (18 MW at Sanghi in Q3), BCFC fly ash logistics, and passing on unabsorbed cost inflation through price increases (Rs 10/bag realized in October).

### One-line takeaway
Ambuja Cements is combining cost-optimization initiatives and trade-led sales with an internally funded Rs 14,500 crore expansion roadmap to reach 149 MTPA by FY29 while retaining a debt-free balance sheet.
