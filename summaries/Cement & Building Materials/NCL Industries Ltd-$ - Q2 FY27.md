# NCL Industries Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Healthy cement volume growth and demand were offset by compressed EBITDA margins due to lower selling prices and rising raw material costs.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 386 crore (Up 12% YoY and 12% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 37 crore (Down 26% YoY and 6% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.64% (Down 502 bps YoY and 189 bps QoQ)
- **PAT:** Rs 20 crore (Up 1% YoY and 15% QoQ)
- **PAT Margin:** 5.18% (Down 60 bps YoY, up 12 bps QoQ)
- **Cement Sales Volumes:** 16% growth YoY; H1 cement sales stood at 14.59 lakh tonnes vs 12.63 lakh tonnes YoY
- **Cement EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 480 for Q2; Rs 475 for H1
- **RMC Volume Growth:** Up 9% QoQ, but H1 volume declined 18%
- **Bison Panel Volume Growth:** Up 6% YoY for Q2 and H1
- **Green Energy Generation (Q2):** 9.22 million units (Hydropower)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 730 crore (Up 7% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 77 crore (Down 25% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 10.53% (Down 453 bps YoY)
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 37 crore (Down 7.2% YoY)
- **Net Worth:** Rs 969 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Total Renewable Project Cost (130 MW):** Rs 987 crore
- **Debt Funding for Renewable Project:** Rs 675 crores (70% via ICICI Bank and HDFC Bank at ~8.5% floating interest)

### Highlights
- Cement segment contributed ~84% of H1 revenue, Bison Panel ~10%, and RMC ~7%.
- Board approved Phase 2 and 3 of the solar and wind power project at Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu (80 MW balance capacity, taking total to 130 MW) for an estimated cost of Rs 595 crore (total project cost now Rs 987 crore, up Rs 68 crore due to domestic content requirement/DCR mandates).
- Acquired Vishwamber Cements Ltd with a limestone mining lease spread across 322 acres for backward integration.
- Exploring setting up a cement plant in Orissa (proposal at a preliminary stage).
- Doors division was closed in the previous financial year; fixed assets and inventories are currently classified as "assets held for sale".
- Note on Record: The call continues the capacity expansion announcements tracked in our record, providing specific financial and structural details on the Tuticorin solar-wind project and Orissa evaluation.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects cement EBITDA per tonne to improve to around Rs 550 for the full year.
- Management expects power cost savings of around Rs 70 crore per annum once the Tuticorin renewable project is commissioned.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 witnessed healthy demand primarily driven by housing and infrastructure development, despite being a seasonally subdued monsoon quarter.
- Nagarjuna Cement continues to sell at a premium (Rs 5 to Rs 10 higher than competitors) due to strong brand image in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
- Smaller cement plants (< 1 million ton capacity) are shutting down or perishing due to pricing pressures, aiding existing players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cement realization prices remained subdued while coal and fuel costs exerted pressure on margins.
- The company is currently using 100% Indian coal in kilns, with the flexibility to switch back to pet coke overnight.
- Bison Panel selling prices were reduced by 17% to 20% to improve affordability and combat duplicate products.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- The 130 MW hybrid renewable project (170 MW DC solar + 31 MW wind capacity) at Tuticorin is scheduled to be commissioned by February/March 2028 in a single phase.
- Debt-to-equity ratio is expected to increase from the current 0.26 to approximately 0.9 upon project implementation.
- Vizag grinding unit capacity utilization stood at 77% in Q2, manufacturing OPC, PPC, slag cement, and GGBS.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company holds a CRISIL A Stable rating.
- Debt for the renewable project has a 3-year moratorium and 9-year repayment period (12 years total).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cement selling prices remain subdued while coal/fuel costs rise.
- RMC division faces challenging market conditions, manpower constraints, and intense unorganized sector competition.
- Duplicate products are being sold under the Bison Panel brand name.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kinnar Mehta (Eureka Investments) asked why the renewable project cost increased from Rs 920 crore to Rs 987 crore and questioned the generation/ROI metrics.**
   - *Management answered* that the increase was due to government mandates for domestic solar panels (non-DCR to DCR). They clarified that captive use saves ~Rs 3 per unit, surplus power sold via exchange fetches ~Rs 4 per unit, generating an estimated Rs 70 crore in annual power cost savings.
2. **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS) questioned the low ROE (~10%) and high upfront capex relative to net worth, asking why buybacks weren't preferred.**
   - *Management answered* that cement is power-intensive, and long-term captive power security is essential to remain competitive, especially for future expansions. They confirmed the 70% debt is at ~8.5% floating.
3. **Vikram Damani (Damani Family Office) asked for clarification on power cost savings.**
   - *Management confirmed* that net annual power savings could reach approximately Rs 70 crore, reducing total power expenses significantly from current levels.
4. **Aditya Nair (Front Wave Research LLP) asked about kiln fuel cost increases and coal/pet coke mix.**
   - *Management answered* that they currently use 100% Indian coal and can switch between pet coke and coal overnight with existing infrastructure.
5. **Divyesh Vora (Individual Investor) asked about EPC contractor credibility and project guarantees.**
   - *Management answered* that they are not using an EPC contractor; materials will be sourced directly by the company, land (418 acres) is already acquired, and execution will be handled in-house. They stated the project ROE is around 10%.

### One-line takeaway
NCL Industries reported a 12% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, but EBITDA declined 26% due to subdued cement prices and higher fuel costs, even as management aggressively pushed forward with a large Rs 987 crore renewable energy capex and backward integration plans.
