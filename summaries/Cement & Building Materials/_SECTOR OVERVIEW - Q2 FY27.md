# Cement & Building Materials: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is in a **high-capex, high-cost, volume-growth phase**. Large cement players (UltraTech, Ambuja/ACC, Dalmia, JSW, Shree, JK Cement) are commissioning capacity aggressively—collectively adding ~100+ MTPA over the next 3–4 years—while battling **fuel-cost inflation** (petcoke >$180/t, domestic coal auctions up >200%, diesel at all-time highs). Most have taken **price hikes of Rs 10–20/bag in September–October** to protect per-tonne EBITDA. Building-materials companies (tiles, plywood, sanitaryware, glass) report **strong domestic demand** but **export weakness** from freight/geopolitics. The consensus industry volume growth for FY27 is **6–10%**, with North and South outperforming East.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian cement has been an asset-heavy, volume-driven game where scale and low-cost fuel/power determine margins. Building materials were fragmented and unbranded; the last 5 years have seen organized players gain share via branding, dealer expansion, and premiumization. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **Green power & fuel flexibility** – *mainstream* – Cement majors are raising RE share (UltraTech 47%, Ambuja/ACC 61%, Dalmia 52%, Shree 65%, JSW 41%) and AFR/TSR (UltraTech 7.2%, Dalmia 13%, JSW adding AFR). Companies explicitly tie green power to per-tonne cost targets [UltraTech concall Q2 FY27; Ambuja concall Q2 FY27; Dalmia concall Q2 FY27; Shree concall Q2 FY27; JSW concall Q2 FY27].
- **Premiumization & value-added mix** – *scaling* – Blended cement share rising (Ambuja/ACC 82%, Nuvoco 83%, UltraTech 67%). Tiles shifting to GVT/large formats (Kajaria, Somany, Varmora). Sanitaryware/faucets pushing value-added share (Cera 47%, Somany GVT target 65–70%). Cement premium product share up (Ambuja/ACC 37%, Shree 29%, UltraTech 37%) [Ambuja concall Q2 FY27; Nuvoco concall Q2 FY27; UltraTech concall Q2 FY27; Kajaria concall Q2 FY27; Somany concall Q2 FY27; Cera concall Q2 FY27; Shree concall Q2 FY27].
- **Massive capacity expansion wave** – *mainstream* – UltraTech (240 MTPA target), Ambuja/ACC (149 MTPA by FY29 via Rs 14,500 cr), Dalmia (67 MTPA), JSW (43.5 MTPA), Shree (80 MTPA), JK Cement (50 MTPA by FY30), JK Lakshmi (25.1 MTPA by FY30), Star (6 MTPA North), Nuvoco (35 MTPA by FY28) [UltraTech concall Q2 FY27; Ambuja concall Q2 FY27; Dalmia concall Q2 FY27; JSW concall Q2 FY27; Shree concall Q2 FY27; JK Cement concall Q2 FY27; JK Lakshmi concall Q2 FY27; Star concall Q2 FY27; Nuvoco concall Q2 FY27].
- **Consolidation & structural simplification** – *early* – Ambuja/ACC/Orient merger proceeding to NCLT; Shiva Cement amalgamation into JSW approved; Greenply diluting SAMET JV from 50% to 19%; Somany raising stake in sanitaryware JV to 76% [Ambuja concall Q2 FY27; JSW concall Q2 FY27; Greenply concall Q2 FY27; Somany concall Q2 FY27].
- **Diversification into adjacent businesses** – *early* – UltraTech launched Ultravolt (wires/cables, Rs 1,800 cr capex); Ambuja/ACC growing RMC (1.07 M m³, +19%); JSW RMC Rs 170 cr/Qtr; Nuvoco, Dalmia, JK Cement adding RMC/construction chemicals; Bansal Roofing entering solar MMS & insulated panels; Visaka pushing ATUM solar & construction chemicals [UltraTech concall Q2 FY27; Ambuja concall Q2 FY27; JSW concall Q2 FY27; Nuvoco concall Q2 FY27; Dalmia concall Q2 FY27; JK Cement concall Q2 FY27; Bansal Roofing concall Q2 FY27; Visaka concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **Road/Infra capex** – Rs 1 lakh cr road projects + 250-km Kolkata ring road + Rs 11,000 cr construction equipment PLI – supports cement/RMC demand [Govt: MoRTH, Nov 2026; Cabinet, Nov 2026].
- **Housing** – Karnataka 15 lakh plots/1 lakh houses – regional demand tailwind [Govt: Karnataka, Nov 2026].
- **Metro** – Noida Aqua Line extension (Rs 2,390 cr) – local construction boost [Govt: Cabinet, Nov 2026].
- **Construction ban** – Noida construction ban (Nov 13) – near-term headwind for NCR players [Govt: Noida Admin, Nov 2026].
- **Mining/land rulings** – SC quashed Odisha land acquisition for Dalmia (Gram Sabha consent); SC ruled mining lease cannot override tribal consent – raises execution risk for greenfield projects [Govt: Supreme Court, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: Industry cement demand growth **6–10% YoY in FY27** (UltraTech ~10%, Ambuja/ACC 6–7%, Dalmia 7–8%, Shree 7–10%, Nuvoco H2 7–8%, Star full-year 7–8%). **Domestic building materials demand resilient** – tiles (Kajaria 10% vol, Somany 5.5% vol but 25% rev), plywood (Century 28.6% rev, Greenply 15.5% val), sanitaryware (Cera 24% vol), glass (Sejal 33% rev, Euro Pratik 19.8% rev).

**Divergence**:
- **Near-term caution**: Star (floods/elections in Northeast), JK Lakshmi (monsoon in Gujarat/Chhattisgarh), Birla Corp (drought in Maharashtra/Rajasthan, UP elections), Greenlam (export logistics bottlenecks), Global Surfaces (US tariffs), Sagar (expensive shutdowns).
- **Strong momentum**: UltraTech (13.8% vol, North >20%, South 15–20%), Shree (18% India vol), JSW (40% cement vol), Dalmia (South/West double-digit), Ambuja/ACC (trade vol +8.6%), JK Cement (grey vol +19% H1), Century Ply (28.5% rev), Kajaria (10% vol), Somany (25% rev), Cera (27% rev), Varmora (89% utilization Q2).
- **Regional split**: North & South strong; East weak (floods, low utilization); West mixed (Maharashtra drought flagged by Ambuja/ACC, but UltraTech West ~10% growth).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Fuel & power** – Universal pressure: petcoke spot $180–200/t (UltraTech, Dalmia, JK Cement, JSW, Nuvoco, Sagar). **Blended fuel cost per tonne up QoQ**: UltraTech Rs 51.5/bag (Rs 2.09/kcal), Ambuja/ACC Rs 1.87/kcal (spot Rs 2.80–2.85), Dalmia Rs 1.66/kcal (Q3 guide Rs 1.86), JK Cement Rs 1.71/kcal (Q3/Q4 guide ~Rs 2), JSW Rs 1.84/kcal (Q3 guide Rs 2.10), Nuvoco Rs 1.59/kcal (Q3 guide Rs 1.80), Shree Rs 2.09/kcal (Q3 guide Rs 2.15–2.20), Sagar Rs 1,473/t (covered till Jan at sub-Rs 2/kcal). **Mitigation**: higher domestic coal share (Ambuja/ACC >60%, Dalmia, JSW), RE/WHRS (UltraTech 2 GW green, Ambuja/ACC 61% green, Dalmia 450 MW RE, Shree 667 MW green, JSW 41% green), AFR (UltraTech 7.2%, Dalmia 13%), clinker factor reduction (Ambuja/ACC blended 82%, Nuvoco 83%, UltraTech 67.3%).

**Other costs**: Packaging (PP granules up), freight (diesel at highs – UltraTech, Dalmia, JSW, Sagar Rs 900/t), raw materials (gypsum, slag, chemicals up 25–50% for Global Surfaces, brass 2x for Somany/Cera).

**Price hikes**: Rs 10–20/bag taken Sep–Oct (UltraTech, Ambuja/ACC, JK Cement, JK Lakshmi, Star, Sagar, Nuvoco). **EBITDA/tonne dispersion**: UltraTech targets Rs 1,400; Shree Rs 966; Ambuja/ACC Rs 770 (norm Rs 4,182 cost); Dalmia Rs 698 (ex-Central Rs 746); JSW Rs 708; JK Cement ~Rs 700+ (implied); Nuvoco ~Rs 650 H2 guide; Sagar guide Rs 650 H2; Star Rs 1,372 (premium Northeast); JK Lakshmi Rs 510–520 (weak Q2); Birla Corp not disclosed; NCL Rs 480.

**Building materials margins**: Kajaria EBITDA 19.5% (stable), Somany 11.7% (up 200–250 bps YoY), Cera 12.2% (guide 13–14%), Varmora H1 EBITDA margin +17% YoY, Greenlam 9.9% (down 330 bps), Greenpanel 7.5% (down), Century Ply 13.1%, Greenply 11.2%, Euro Pratik core 36%, Sejal 18.35%, Carysil 19.4%, Stylam ~18%, Rushil 9.5%, Duroply 7.1%, Glass Wall 25.3%.
