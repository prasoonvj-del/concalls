# Cement & Building Materials: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is navigating a "tale of two halves." While H1 FY27 was characterized by monsoon-related disruptions, geopolitical cost pressures, and soft trade prices, management sentiment is broadly optimistic for H2. A significant strategic pivot is underway among industry leaders: a shift from "volume-at-any-cost" to "value-led growth." Companies like **ACC** and **Ambuja Cements** have explicitly moved away from shedding low-margin business to pursuing calibrated, value-accretive expansion. However, cost inflation—specifically in fuel (petcoke/coal) and logistics—remains the primary headwind, forcing companies to balance price hikes with market share retention.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Most managements expect H2 to outperform H1, driven by peak construction activity and a normalization of monsoon-impacted regions.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Regional Weakness:** **Dalmia Bharat**, **Birla Corporation**, and **Sagar Cements** flagged severe monsoons and floods in the East and Northeast as major demand dampeners. **UltraTech** and **Shree Cement** noted that while South and North regions remained resilient, the East was the outlier.
    *   **Segmental Shifts:** **Greenpanel** and **Greenply** reported headwinds in the OEM segment, which was slow to respond to price hikes, whereas retail demand remains robust. **Glass Wall Systems** noted that while domestic demand is strong, US developers are pausing new job launches due to geopolitical uncertainty.
    *   **Growth Targets:** **UltraTech** reported strong 13.8% YoY volume growth, while **JSW Cement** saw a 40% YoY jump in cement volumes. Conversely, **JK Lakshmi Cement** reported a more modest 5% volume growth, citing monsoon impacts.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Fuel Inflation:** This is the universal pain point. **Shree Cement** reported landed fuel costs rising to INR 2.09/kcal, with expectations of INR 2.20/kcal in Q3. **Nuvoco Vistas** noted energy costs rose to INR 1,080/tonne.
*   **Pricing Power:** There is a clear struggle to pass on costs. **Kajaria Ceramics** and **Somany Ceramics** successfully implemented price hikes (16-17%) to offset gas price volatility. **JK Cement** noted that while price hikes of INR 20/bag were observed, an additional INR 10-15/bag is needed to fully cover cost pressures.
*   **Logistics:** **Global Surfaces** and **Stylam Industries** highlighted that elevated ocean freight rates (driven by Middle East tensions) are squeezing margins, with **Stylam** noting a 3% to 3.5% EBITDA margin impact.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Aggressive Expansion:** **ACC** and **Ambuja Cements** are leading with a massive 30 million tonne (MT) expansion plan. **UltraTech** continues its march toward 204.7 MT capacity. **JK Cement** is targeting 50 MT by FY30.
*   **Disciplined Approach:** **JK Lakshmi Cement** notably revised its capacity target down from 30 MT to 25.1 MT to maintain balance sheet discipline. **Century Plyboards** confirmed that large-scale capex will be paused if net debt-to-EBITDA exceeds 1x.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Strategic Pivots:** **ACC** and **Ambuja Cements** shifted from "sacrificing volume for value" (Q1) to "value-led growth" (Q2), signaling a more aggressive stance on market share.
*   **OEM Headwinds:** **Greenpanel** and **Greenply** faced a reversal in OEM demand, as price-sensitive customers turned to imports following Q1 price hikes.
*   **Export Dynamics:** **Somany Ceramics** and **Varmora Granito** noted that the U.S. ban on engineered marble/quartz is creating new export opportunities for Indian players, despite current container availability issues.
*   **Debt Management:** **Sagar Cements** and **Varmora Granito** are aggressively focusing on debt reduction, with **Varmora** using IPO proceeds to bring interest costs near zero.

## What to watch next quarter
1.  **Fuel Price Volatility:** Will the West Asia conflict continue to drive petcoke/coal prices, or will domestic coal availability improve?
2.  **Pricing Power:** Can companies successfully implement the planned price hikes (e.g., JK Cement, JK Lakshmi) without sacrificing volume?
3.  **H2 Demand Recovery:** Will the anticipated post-monsoon demand surge materialize in the East and Northeast?
4.  **OEM/Export Recovery:** Will the OEM segment (Greenpanel/Greenply) and export markets (Stylam/Somany) show the expected rebound?
