# Cement & Building Materials: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian cement and building materials sector reflects a bifurcated reality: robust structural volume growth driven by infrastructure and retail demand, offset by severe near-term margin headwinds from geopolitical conflicts, erratic monsoons, and rising energy costs. While large players like **ACC/Ambuja** and **Shree Cement** reported strong volume growth (18% YoY for Shree Cement; 8.6% trade volume growth for ACC/Ambuja), profitability across the sector faced pressure from soft trade prices in core regions and a sequential spike in fuel costs (petcoke and imported coal). 

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements broadly agree that full-year industry demand growth will settle at **6% to 8%**, supported by structural tailwinds in urbanization, infrastructure execution, and rural housing. 
*   **Divergence on Regional Performance:** 
    *   **North & West:** Highlighted as strong outperformers. ACC/Ambuja reported trade volume growth of 13% in the North, 14% in the West, and 14% in the South. Shree Cement also noted robust volume leadership in the South, followed by North and East.
    *   **East & Central:** Faced headwinds. Birla Corporation and Nuvoco Vistas cited severe monsoons, floods (Bihar/UP), and drought conditions in Maharashtra and Rajasthan impacting construction cycles. Birla Corporation specifically noted that consolidation in the industry has failed to yield immediate pricing improvements in operating regions due to aggressive market-share tactics by large players.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Energy & Fuel Inflation:** Fuel costs rose across the board due to West Asian geopolitical tensions. ACC/Ambuja reported fuel costs at Rs 1.87 per 1,000 kcal (spot rates touching Rs 2.85). Nuvoco Vistas saw fuel costs rise to INR 1,080/tonne (INR 1.59 per million kcal) with Q3 expected to touch INR 1.80. Shree Cement’s landed fuel cost increased to INR 2.09 per kCal (guided to INR 2.15–2.20 in Q3). Birla Corporation guided for a ~Rs 100/tonne sequential fuel cost increase in Q3.
*   **Mitigation Strategies:** Companies are aggressively scaling up green energy share to combat power costs (e.g., ACC/Ambuja at 61%, Shree Cement at 65.2%, JSW Cement at 41%) and optimizing primary lead distances (e.g., ACC/Ambuja reduced lead distance from 265 km to 254 km).
*   **Pricing Reality:** While cost inflation pushed up production expenses, pricing power remained weak in early Q2. However, multiple companies (**Sagar Cements**, **JSW Cement**, **ACC/Ambuja**) reported successful price recoveries and increases of Rs 10 to Rs 20 per bag in September and October.

## Capex & capacity
The sector remains deeply committed to aggressive capacity expansion, backed by strong balance sheets:
*   **ACC / Ambuja Cements:** Detailed a massive 30 million tonne expansion plan across 14 projects (~Rs 14,500 crore total capex), targeting 149 MTPA by FY29 and 155 MTPA by FY30. FY27 capex is guided at Rs 6,500–7,000 crore.
*   **JSW Cement:** Total remaining capex to reach 43.5 MTPA is estimated at INR 7,500 crore, with a net debt-to-EBITDA target hovering around 3x.
*   **Nuvoco Vistas:** Revised full-year FY27 capex to INR 1,200 crores, front-loading execution for the Vadraj Cement integration and Kutch project.
*   **Greenply Industries & Bigbloc Construction:** Greenply announced a new plywood expansion at Sandila (Rs 125 crore capex, operational Q4 FY28). Bigbloc kept its Madhya Pradesh expansion on hold due to macro conditions.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **ACC / Ambuja Cements:** Shifted tone from "shedding low-margin business" (Q1) to "calibrated expansion" and "value-led growth" (Q2), unveiling the 30 MT expansion roadmap.
*   **Greenpanel Industries:** Experienced margin contraction in Q2 due to delayed pass-through of price hikes and higher opening inventory, contrasting with its more positive Q1 momentum.
*   **Sagar Cements:** Transitioned from volume-focused execution to aggressive operational normalization after heavy maintenance shutdowns at Mattampally and Jeerabad weighed on Q2 margins.

## Company one-liners
*   **ACC Ltd:** Aggressively pursuing a 30 million tonne expansion plan while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet to drive value-led growth.
*   **Ambuja Cements Ltd:** Pivoted to a "value-led growth" strategy, backing it with a massive 30 MT expansion plan while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet.
*   **Bigbloc Construction Ltd:** Reported record October sales, focusing on passing through remaining input cost inflation to drive H2 profitability.
*   **Birla Corporation Ltd:** Subdued by cost inflation and depressed trade prices in the Central region, maintaining a cautious H2 outlook.
*   **Global Surfaces Ltd:** Navigating high input costs and U.S. trade tariffs by shifting focus to the domestic market and engineered quartz.
*   **Greenpanel Industries Ltd:** Battled near-term margin contraction and OEM headwinds, anticipating retail-led recovery and volume normalization in H2.
*   **Greenply Industries Ltd:** Delivered solid value growth and margin expansion via premiumization despite plywood capacity constraints.
*   **JSW Cement Ltd:** Delivered 33% revenue growth and achieved EBITDA breakeven in the North, mitigating cost pressures through green energy scaling.
*   **Nuvoco Vistas Corporation Ltd:** Delivered resilient volume growth despite severe macro cost headwinds, driven by premiumization and Vadraj execution.
*   **Orient Cement Ltd:** Delivered a resilient quarter with stable margins and a clear roadmap for a self-funded 30 MT expansion under the Ambuja umbrella.
*   **Sagar Cements Ltd-$:** Achieved 26% volume growth constrained by maintenance shutdowns, guiding for margin recovery to ~Rs 650 EBITDA/tonne in H2.
*   **Shree Cement Ltd:** Posted 18% YoY volume growth in India backed by efficiency gains, premiumization, and an INR 8,168 crore net cash balance sheet.

## What to watch next quarter
1. **Execution & Ramping of New Capacities:** Progress on ACC/Ambuja's 30 MT rollout, Nuvoco's Vadraj/Surat asset integration, and Shree Cement's capacity milestones.
2. **Fuel Cost Pass-Through & Realizations:** Whether companies successfully sustain October price hikes (Rs 10–20/bag) to offset projected Q3 fuel cost increases (petcoke/imported coal).
3. **Demand Recovery in H2:** Pick-up in infrastructure and rural construction activity post-monsoon, particularly in sluggish East and Central markets.
4. **Leverage & Debt Trajectories:** Monitoring net debt-to-EBITDA levels for aggressive expanders like JSW Cement and Sagar Cements.
