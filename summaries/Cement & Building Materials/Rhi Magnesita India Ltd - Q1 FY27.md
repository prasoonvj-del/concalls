# Rhi Magnesita India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited a strong start to the financial year with revenue growth and significant profitability improvement despite a volatile environment.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 1,014 crore (9% QoQ, 6% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 147 crore (42% YoY increase)
- **EBITDA margin:** 14.5% (compared to 10.8% in Q1 FY26)
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** INR 65 crore (up from INR 35 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Cash and cash equivalents:** INR 452 crore
- **Volume growth guidance:** Management expects 7% to 8% volume growth for the rest of the year (while noting 9% is a bit of a stretch).
- **MINPRO JV initial investment:** Roughly INR 35 crore over the next 2 years.

### Highlights
- Management transition: Mr. Parmod Sagar continues as Chairman, and Mr. Pankaj Malhan took over as Managing Director and CEO.
- Growth was primarily driven by the steel business, supported by favorable realizations and healthy demand; the cement segment recovered due to seasonal maintenance demand.
- Profitability improved due to strong execution, favorable price realization, operating leverage, and productivity initiatives.
- Formed a joint venture named MINPRO with Khemka Refractories to establish a greenfield mineral processing facility in Odisha (subsidiary status, 51% held by RHI Magnesita India).
- Auditors resigned mid-year to align with the parent group's strategy and mandatory rotation; the company intends to adopt the same group auditors at the upcoming AGM.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its annual EBITDA margin guidance of 13%.
- Capex guidance of INR 80 crore to INR 100 crore per year is maintained (covering Dalmia plant modernization, 4PRO machinery/robotic solutions, and maintenance).
- Expects to continue outperforming the underlying market.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment remains competitive with pricing pressure, rising input costs, and greenfield/brownfield expansions by domestic and multinational players.
- Steel sector shows steady growth in production and volumes, aided by strong domestic demand and high-capacity utilization.
- Cement industry maintained strong growth momentum in Q1 FY27, though facing margin pressures from fuel, energy, and raw material costs.
- Industrial sector (non-ferrous, glass) was a bit weak in Q1 due to a lack of projects in H1, with projects expected in H2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Facing inflationary pressures in raw materials, logistics, and energy costs.
- Magnesite prices increased by 6% to 8% over the preceding 2 months.
- Backward integration via quartzite mines (Chiraipani and Bhikampali) is nearing operation by the end of Q1/early Q2, expected to provide structural cost benefits.
- Alumina prices have been stable over the last 6 months.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex of INR 8 crore was incurred in Q1 FY27.
- MINPRO JV investment of ~INR 35 crore planned over 2 years (51:49 funding by RHIM and Khemka), targeting 8% to 10% EBITDA margin and a payback period of <3 years post-production; commercial production expected by Q4 FY27.
- Captive quartzite mines (Chiraipani and Bhikampali) expected to open by the end of the quarter, providing supply resilience and cost advantages.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains net cash positive with INR 452 crore in cash and cash equivalents.
- Working capital is well-controlled despite strategically increasing inventory levels to ensure supply continuity.
- All goodwill of Dalmia has been written off the balance sheet (non-cash impairment below EBITDA).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile operating environment, competitive intensity, excess capacity, and geopolitical uncertainties (Ukraine-Russia war, Middle East tensions) impacting exports and raw material costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital) asked about the realization split and sustainability:** Management stated price increases were primarily war surcharges, with growth mainly driven by product mix; annual volume growth is guided at 7%-8%.
- **Sahil Sanghvi (Monarch Networth Capital) asked about structural margin levers:** Management highlighted structural changes via the MINPRO JV and captive quartzite mines to sustain margins.
- **Rajesh Majumdar (360 ONE Capital) asked about project orders in Q1:** Management clarified there was no major project impact in Q1, but coke oven and glass projects are in the pipeline for H2 (Q3/Q4).
- **Rajakumar Vaidyanathan (RK Invest) asked about the auditor resignation:** Management explained it is to align with the parent group's strategy and mandatory audit rotation.
- **Praveen Jayaraman (Avendus Spark) asked about quartzite mining details:** Management noted Chiraipani and Bhikampali mines are close to opening, providing supply resilience and structural cost benefits starting next quarter.

### One-line takeaway
RHI Magnesita India delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 6% revenue growth and nearly doubled PAT, supported by robust steel demand, while focusing on structural cost optimization via captive mining and the MINPRO joint venture.
