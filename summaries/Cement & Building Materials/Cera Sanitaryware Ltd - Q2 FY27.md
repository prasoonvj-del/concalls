# Cera Sanitaryware Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in surpassing earlier revenue growth targets and meeting the full-year EBITDA margin outlook, supported by strong volume growth and sequential margin recovery.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Revenue growth guidance was raised (now confident of surpassing the earlier 18%-20% target); EBITDA margin outlook was maintained at 13%-14%.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from "mixed" (due to one-offs and margin drag in Q1) to positive, driven by sustained, broad-based volume growth and recovering retail demand.
- New topics or risks: Introduction of automation and AI tools for cost efficiency; old project orders weighing on discounts/margins which are expected to clear out by late Q3/Q4. Old topic of wage settlements disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises: Faucetware capacity increase to 5 lakh units per month was delivered (effective September via balancing equipment); sanitaryware capacity utilization improved to 82% from 61%.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue:** Rs 603 crore (up 27.4% YoY).
* **EBITDA Margin:** 12.2% (vs 14.2% in Q2 FY26 and 10.1% in Q1 FY27).
* **PAT:** Rs 56 crore (vs Rs 57 crore in Q2 FY26).
* **EPS:** Rs 43.52 (vs Rs 43.92 in Q2 FY26).
* **Segment Revenue Growth YoY:** Sanitaryware up 24% (volume 14.3%, price 6.7%, mix 2.7%); Faucetware up 38% (volume 24%, price 10.3%, mix 1.9%); Tiles up 9%; Wellness up 71%.
* **Channel Mix:** Retail 61%, Projects 39%.
* **Input Costs:** Average gas cost Rs 47.29 per cubic meter (vs Rs 33.79 YoY); Average brass cost Rs 932 per kg (vs Rs 589 YoY and Rs 837 QoQ).
* **Capacity Utilization:** Sanitaryware facilities at 82%. Faucetware production increased from 4 lakh to 5 lakh units per month.
* **Working Capital:** Net working capital days improved to 58 days from 77 days YoY (inventory days 69 vs 83, receivable days 28 vs 33, payables 39 vs 39).
* **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 897 crore as of September 30, 2026.
* **Capex:** Rs 27 crore incurred in H1 FY27 against a planned full-year outlay of approximately Rs 43 crore.

### Highlights
* Growth was predominantly volume-driven (16.6% overall volume contribution, 9.1% price, 1.7% mix).
* Monthly faucetware production increased from 4 lakh units to 5 lakh units by adding balancing equipment within the same facility.
* Selectively brought certain fast-moving products in-house to reduce dependence on external manufacturers and ensure consistent product availability amid Morbi cluster challenges.
* Uttar Pradesh emerged as an important market, alongside traction in Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, and MP.
* Management is actively evaluating automation and AI tools for cost efficiency.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management is confident of surpassing the earlier revenue growth target of 18%-20% and maintaining the full-year EBITDA margin outlook of 13%-14%.
* Profitability is expected to improve progressively in H2 FY27, supported by pricing actions, cost efficiencies, and operating leverage.
* No further price hikes are currently planned for Q3; faucetware prices were hiked by ~8% effective September 1, 2026.
* Greenfield sanitaryware project decision will be reviewed by the end of Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Retail demand continued to recover, complemented by a steady projects business.
* Business momentum remained consistent across July, August, and September.
* Operating environment remains challenging due to elevated input costs (brass and gas) and supply-side constraints.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Sequential margin improvement from 10.1% in Q1 to 12.2% in Q2, driven by higher turnover and operating leverage (1.9%), offset by adverse impacts from higher brass prices (-2.1%), higher discounts (-1.7%), publicity expenses (-0.5%), and sanitaryware/tiles procurement (-0.3%).
* Faucetware price hike of ~8% effective September 1st to offset brass inflation; benefits expected to flow in late Q3 and Q4.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* H1 FY27 capex stood at Rs 27 crore against an annual guidance of ~Rs 43 crore.
* Faucetware capacity expanded to 5 lakh units/month via balancing equipment; expandable to 6-7 lakh units in a phased manner if demand warrants.
* Greenfield sanitaryware plant construction decision deferred to the end of Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 897 crore as of September 30, 2026.
* Working capital days improved significantly YoY to 58 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Elevated input costs, particularly brass and gas.
* Time lag in passing on price hikes to existing project orders, which impacted margins via higher effective discounts in Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Srivastava (360 ONE Capital):** Asked for the volume/price/mix growth split. *Management answered that overall volume contributed 16.6%, price 9.1%, and mix 1.7% (Sanitaryware: 14.3% volume, 6.7% price, 2.7% mix; Faucetware: 24% volume, 10.3% price, 1.9% mix).*
2. **Amit Srivastava (360 ONE Capital):** Asked about the bridge from Q1 margin (10.1%) to Q2 margin (12.2%) and full-year guidance confidence. *Management detailed the positive factors (one-off reversals, price hikes, operating leverage) and negative factors (brass, discounts, publicity) and confirmed confidence in the 13%-14% annual margin guidance.*
3. **Bhupesh Kawdia (ICICI Direct):** Inquired about capex plans and sanitaryware capacity expansion. *Management stated sanitaryware capacity utilization reached 82% and a decision on the greenfield project will be taken by the end of Q4.*
4. **Resha Mehta (GreenEdge Wealth):** Asked if price hikes are reversed when raw material costs drop. *Management stated they do not anticipate price rollbacks in sanitaryware or faucets even if raw materials taper, but discounts may be adjusted depending on demand.*
5. **Utkarsh Nopany (Anand Rathi):** Asked why sanitaryware gross margins came under pressure despite low-cost APM gas. *Management explained that outsourcing partners (representing ~59% of sanitaryware) face doubled gas costs and pass them on via OEM procurement price hikes, alongside freight and other inflationary pressures.*

### One-line takeaway
Cera reported strong 27.4% YoY revenue growth driven by robust volumes, and while margins remained pressured by input inflation and legacy project pricing, management raised its revenue growth outlook while maintaining its 13%-14% EBITDA margin target for FY27.
