# Star Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - demand was subdued in H1 due to elections and severe floods in Assam and Nagaland, resulting in lower YoY EBITDA and PAT, though management expects a demand rebound in H2.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Cement volume growth target for the full year maintained at 7%-8%; H2 volume growth expected at 12%-13% YoY. Total capex for the North expansion (Nimbol/Jhajjar) revised to ~INR3,080 crores for a 6 million tonne capacity.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from heavily weather-impacted and cautious in Q1 to anticipating pent-up demand and double-digit volume growth in H2.
- New topics or risks: Detailed evaluation of Siliguri grinding unit (~INR350 crore) vs. Bihar greenfield (~INR550 crore) based on West Bengal's new industrial policy; EC for Nimbol nearly secured with ground-breaking set for December 1. Silchar railway siding delayed to January.
- Last quarter's promises: Fuel cost moderation to INR1.45 delayed due to coal shortages, sustained at INR1.52 in Q2.

- **Clinker Production:** 9.08 lakh tonnes (vs 9.18 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Cement Production:** 11.56 lakh tonnes (vs 10.83 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Cement Sales Volume:** 11.23 lakh tonnes (vs 10.72 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Clinker Sales Volume:** 0.74 lakh tonnes (vs 1.01 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Geographical Cement Sales:** Northeast: 7.96 lakh tonnes (vs 7.84 lakh tonnes YoY); Outside Northeast: 3.27 lakh tonnes (vs 2.88 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Blend Mix:** ~18% OPC, rest PPC
- **Total Revenue:** INR831 crore (vs INR811 crore YoY); H1: INR1,774 crore (vs INR1,723 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR164 crore (vs INR194 crore YoY); H1: INR367 crore (vs INR424 crore YoY)
- **PAT:** INR39.58 crore (vs INR71 crore YoY); H1: INR113 crore (vs INR169 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA per tonne:** INR1,372 (vs INR1,651 YoY); H1: INR1,438 (vs INR1,716 YoY)
- **Premium Sales Share:** 17.3%
- **Trade Share:** 77%
- **Lead Distance:** 208 km

- **Green Power Share:** 28%
- **Fuel Mix:** FSA 45%, Washery/coke fine coal 40%, Biomass 11.5%, Spot auction coal 10%
- **Non-cement Revenue:** INR22 crore in Q2 (INR37-38 crore in H1; ~INR100 crore targeted for full year)
- **Incentives Accrued:** INR36 crore in Q2; H2 expected at INR65 crore - INR70 crore

### Highlights
- Profitability declined YoY due to subdued regional demand and high input costs.
- Assam Norway subsidy approval obtained for the remaining period (extended tenure of 12 years), increasing subsidy accruals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects 12%-13% volume growth YoY in Q3 and Q4; full-year cement volume growth target of 7%-8% remains intact.
- Blended fuel cost expected to be maintained at ~INR1.5 in Q3 and Q4.
- Full-year EBITDA guidance maintained by management.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 demand impacted by elections, heavy floods in Assam and Nagaland, and political unrest in Manipur (~25% de-growth in Manipur).
- October demand grew by over 12%, boosting confidence for H2.
- Prices in September were flattish; October saw a ~INR10 increase in Northeast and INR10-INR15 increase outside Northeast (West Bengal).
- Northeast industry volume growth for H2 estimated at 7%-8%; outside players account for 15%-16% market share (~2.4 million tonnes) in Northeast via rakes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended fuel consumption cost was INR1.52 per unit in Q2.
- Industry-wide coal shortages mitigated for Star by maintaining 3 months of coal stock and resumption of FSA rake deliveries (5-10 rakes in November).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Nimbol & Jhajjar Project:** EAC meeting for Nimbol EC successful; formal EC expected in 1-2 weeks. Ground-breaking starts December 1. Total North expansion capex is ~INR3,080 crores for 6 million tonnes total capacity (3.3 mt clinker, 3 mt cement at Nimbol, 3 mt grinding at Jhajjar), targeting commissioning by Q2 FY29 (July/August 2028).
- **Siliguri / Bihar Grinding Unit:** Evaluating Siliguri (~INR350 crore capex) vs. Bihar (~INR550 crore capex) based on West Bengal's new industrial policy; EC already applied for Siliguri.
- **Silchar Railway Siding:** Expected to be operational by January, bringing fly ash cost savings.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-EBITDA threshold of 1.5x remains the limit for funding expansions via debt and internal accruals.
- QIP currently not planned due to market correction.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe floods in Assam/Nagaland and political unrest in Manipur depressing regional demand.
- Industry-wide coal delivery delays and pricing volatility from Coal India e-auctions.
- Long-term pricing pressure risks in Northeast from incoming capacities by large players (Ambuja, JK Lakshmi, Shree Cement).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harsh Mittal (Emkay Global) on H2 volume outlook:** Management expects 12%-13% YoY volume growth in Q3 and Q4, keeping the full-year 7%-8% growth target intact.
2. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital) on capex updates:** Management clarified the North expansion capex of ~INR3,080 crores for 6 million tonnes, with Nimbol ground-breaking on December 1.
3. **Rajesh Ravi (HDFC Securities) on fuel costs:** Management explained that despite industry coal shortages, Star's 3-month inventory and resuming FSA rakes will keep fuel costs flat at ~INR1.5 in H2.
4. **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities) on Northeast competition:** Management acknowledged new capacities by peers but noted incumbents maintain high trade shares and long-term margins will stabilize.

### One-line takeaway
Star Cement reported lower Q2 FY27 profitability due to floods and elections, but anticipates a strong H2 demand recovery and is progressing on its North India and eastern expansion plans.
