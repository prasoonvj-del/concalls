# Somany Ceramics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - driven by operational efficiencies, strong capacity utilization, and profitable JVs.

### Key numbers
- **Sales volume growth:** ~3% YoY (moderated due to a 1.5-month Morbi shutdown in April).
- **Value growth:** 24% YoY.
- **Stand-alone capacity utilization:** 83% in Q1 FY27 vs 72% in Q1 FY26.
- **EBITDA margin:** 11.6% (up 3.6% / 360 bps YoY).
- **JV performance:** Profit of Rs 3 crore in Q1 FY27 vs loss of Rs 10 crore in Q1 FY26.
- **Somany Max performance:** Loss reduced to slightly over Rs 1 crore in Q1 FY27 vs loss of Rs 7 crore in Q1 FY26.
- **Working capital days:** Reduced from 17 to 12 days.
- **Natural gas blended prices:** ~Rs 68 in North, mid-70s in South and Morbi.
- **Price hikes taken:** 16% to 17% average.
- **New greenfield plant outlay:** ~Rs 220 crore for a 9+ million square meter plant in the South (operational in next 12-15 months / Q3-Q4 of next year).
- **Total capex outlay (now to end of FY27):** ~Rs 275 crore (includes the South plant, balancing equipment, and bathware/sanitaryware expansions), funded 65-70% via internal accruals.
- **Peak revenue potential at current prices:** ~Rs 3,700 crore (including JVs).

### Highlights
- Sales volume growth impacted in April due to Morbi being shut for 1.5 months; expected to be made up in the current quarter.
- Operational efficiencies and higher capacity utilization drove EBITDA margin expansion.
- JVs turned profitable (Rs 3 crore profit vs Rs 10 crore loss YoY).
- Somany Max losses drastically reduced to ~Rs 1 crore.
- Debtor days, inventory days, and creditor days reported healthy; inventory reduced due to Morbi shutdown clearing old stock.
- Construction chemicals capacity expanded in the South and a very large capacity added in the North last month.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains that EBITDA margins delivered in Q1 will be sustained and aims to better them (targeting 12% and above).
- Volume growth guidance for the year: Mid-single digits.
- South plant (9+ million square meters, ~Rs 220 crore capex) to be operational by end of Q3 / beginning of Q4 of next year.
- Additional capacity addition of 4 million to 5 million square meters via balancing equipment in existing lines (Bahadurgarh, Gujarat, Morbi, South) ready by mid-Q3 to Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand in May, June, and July has been "pretty decent" despite rains in many parts of the country.
- Morbi operations resumed 100% production by end-May using expensive natural gas supplied by GEL (formerly GSPCL).
- Morbi exports down by 50% to 60% from peak due to geopolitical reasons and freight settlements.
- Channel inventory is lean due to gas price volatility; dealers are cautious about potential price cuts.
- Project segment (retail + government) expected to grow by 3% to 4% in totality.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Natural gas prices are extremely volatile with small monthly increases; current price increase is between 16% and 18%.
- Blended natural gas price: ~Rs 68 in North, mid-70s in South and Morbi.
- Price hikes of 16% to 17% successfully passed on to the market.
- Price gap between Somany and Morbi has narrowed because Morbi took double the price hikes and shifted to natural gas from propane/LPG.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 9+ million square meter plant announced in the South: ~Rs 220 crore outlay, operational in 12-15 months (Q3/Q4 of next year). Potential revenue of ~Rs 350 crore.
- 4 million to 5 million square meters additional capacity via debottlenecking/balancing equipment in existing plants (Bahadurgarh, Gujarat, Morbi, South) ready by mid-Q3 to Q4 FY27, adding to value-added mix.
- Total capex from now to end of FY27: ~Rs 275 crore (65-70% internal accruals, remainder via small loans in JVs without Somany corporate guarantee).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital days reduced from 17 to 12 days.
- Receivables and advertising spends are in line; old stock reduced significantly.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gas price volatility is high, creating channel caution regarding potential price reductions.
- Morbi exports are down 50%-60% due to geopolitical reasons.
- Monsoon rains in July impacted near-term operations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sneha (Nuvama) on margin sustainability:** Management attributed margins to 83% standalone capacity utilization and JV turnaround, expressing high confidence in sustaining/improving them.
2. **Gunit Singh (Counter Cyclical PMS) on share buyback:** Suggested a share buyback given low valuations; management acknowledged the suggestion ("Thank you for your suggestion").
3. **Keshav Lahoti (HDFC Securities) on volume growth and margin risks:** Asked if normalized Morbi supply could pressure margins; management reiterated margins are driven by operational efficiencies, not pricing gains, and will sustain.
4. **Viraj Kacharia (SiMPL) on industry volume growth vs Somany's guidance:** Asked why Somany guides for mid-single digits when peers report higher; management stated they are being cautious and conservative compared to smaller players whose bases are small.
5. **Kalpesh (Valentis Advisors) on Morbi domestic dumping:** Asked if weak Morbi exports would lead to domestic dumping; management stated high gas prices prevent them from doing so.
6. **Shruti Mulchandani (Ikigai Asset Management) on capex funding:** Asked about total capex and debt impact; management clarified total capex to FY27 end is Rs 275 crore, funded 65-70% via internal accruals with no corporate guarantee pressures.

### One-line takeaway
Somany Ceramics delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with EBITDA margins expanding 360 bps YoY to 11.6% on the back of 83% standalone capacity utilization and a profitable JV turnaround, while guiding for mid-single-digit volume growth and setting up a major greenfield expansion in the South.
