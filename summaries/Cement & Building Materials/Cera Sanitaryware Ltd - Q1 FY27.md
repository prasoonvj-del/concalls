# Cera Sanitaryware Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume-led revenue growth was offset by margin compression due to one-time wage settlements, under-absorption of fixed costs, and delayed price pass-through in project orders.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 486 crore (up 19.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 10.1% (vs 13.1% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 45 crore (vs Rs 47 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EPS:** Rs 35.15 (vs Rs 36.08 in Q1 FY26).
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 943 crore.
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** Improved to 50 days (from 75 days YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Accounting Change:** Turnover discounts are now deducted from revenue (previously an expense). This reduced reported revenue by 2.5% in Q1 FY27 and 3% in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.
*   **Segment Performance:** Sanitaryware revenue grew 14% (10% volume, 2% price); Faucetware grew 25% (18% volume, 4% price).
*   **One-time Margin Impacts:** Management attributed the 300 bps margin decline to:
    *   Long-term wage settlement (Rs 6.3 crore impact).
    *   Under-absorption of fixed costs due to temporary kiln shutdown (Rs 3.7 crore impact).
    *   Increased closure of old project orders (Rs 4 crore impact).
    *   Delayed price pass-through in project segment (1.5% margin impact).
*   **Brand/Digital:** Launched 'Your Moment of Cera' campaign with Kriti Sanon; extended Dealer Management System (DMS) to retailer loyalty program.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains FY27 guidance of 18% to 20%.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Management maintains FY27 guidance of 13.5% to 14% (adjusted for the accounting change).
*   **Marketing:** Planned spend of Rs 85 crore for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand environment is evolving positively with improving retail sentiment and sustained project momentum.
*   Cumulative price increases stand at 12% for Sanitaryware and 16% for Faucetware.
*   Management noted that larger players are better positioned to leverage scale and supply chain efficiencies amidst industry disruptions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gas Costs:** Weighted average cost was Rs 48.43/cubic meter (vs Rs 33.17/cubic meter YoY).
*   **Brass Prices:** Rising trend; reached ~Rs 900 in July. Management is evaluating further price hikes if costs exceed current levels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Sanitaryware at 61%; Faucetware at 96%.
*   **Expansion:** Faucetware expansion expected to be effective from Q4. Greenfield Sanitaryware expansion is under review; management stated a decision will depend on demand trends.
*   **Capex Guidance:** Approximately Rs 43 crore planned for FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Inventory days reduced to 68 (from 80).
*   Receivables reduced to 30 days (from 38).
*   Payables increased to 48 days (from 43).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Elevated input costs (brass and gas) impacting gross margins.
*   Project segment margins remain pressured until old, lower-priced orders are fully cleared (expected post-Q2).
*   Geopolitical/supply chain uncertainties affecting the Morbi cluster.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you explain the volume vs. price growth split?** A: Sanitaryware growth was 10% volume/2% price; Faucetware was 18% volume/4% price.
*   **Q: Does the 19.5% revenue growth trigger a revival of greenfield capex?** A: Management is reviewing the project; a decision will be made based on demand trends later this year.
*   **Q: Why did margins drop despite strong revenue?** A: Management cited one-time factors (wage settlement, kiln under-absorption, project closures) totaling a 4.35% impact on margins.
*   **Q: Will you take further price hikes?** A: Evaluating the situation; if brass prices rise beyond Rs 900, further hikes may be necessary.
*   **Q: How does the accounting change affect margin guidance?** A: Guidance remains 13.5-14%; the percentage is now calculated on a lower revenue base (post-discount deduction).

### One-line takeaway
Cera delivered strong volume-led growth, but reported margins were temporarily depressed by one-time wage settlements and project order closures, with management maintaining full-year guidance.
