# Bigbloc Construction Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted record October sales, strong volume growth, and confidence in H2 demand despite near-term margin pressure from input costs.

### Key numbers
*   **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 83 crore (up ~23% YoY).
*   **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 3 crore (up 74% YoY).
*   **Q2 EBITDA Margin:** 3.98% (up 116 bps YoY).
*   **Q2 Net Loss:** Rs 2.75 crore.
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 162 crore (up ~31% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 10 crore (up from Rs 3 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 5.93% (up 334 bps YoY).
*   **H1 Net Loss:** Rs 3.5 crore.
*   **H1 Sales Volume:** 4,46,769 cubic meters (up 22% YoY).
*   **Q2 Sales Volume:** 2,25,241 cubic meters (up 13% YoY).
*   **Capacity Utilization:** 69.34% (Q2) and 68.74% (H1).

### Highlights
*   October 2026 recorded the highest-ever monthly turnover and sales volume for the company.
*   AAC blocks contributed 87-88% of H1 revenue; AAC panels and construction chemicals contributed 5-6% each.
*   Construction chemicals plant (operational since Q1 FY27) has a revenue potential of Rs 20-25 crore.
*   Sustainability: Rooftop solar capacity reached ~3.5 MW across manufacturing facilities.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1 due to peak construction activity.
*   Management targets 75% capacity utilization in H2.
*   Management expects to pass on the remaining input cost increases (5-7% differential) in upcoming quarters.
*   Operational efficiency initiatives (boiler automation, electric forklifts) are expected to improve EBITDA margins by 2-3% in Q3/Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains strong; October saw "excellent" demand.
*   Some industrial AAC block units have shut down, reducing competitive pressure.
*   Red bricks are 10-15% more expensive than AAC blocks due to transportation costs (density difference).
*   AAC panels are gaining traction with orders from AdaniConneX, bullet train stations, and other large conglomerates.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Input costs (lime, coal) rose 8-10% in Q2; 5-7% was passed on to customers.
*   Raw material cost structure (by quantity): Fly ash (65%), Cement (20-22%), Lime (10-12%).
*   Raw material cost structure (by value): Cement (25-30%), Lime (15-20%), Fly ash (10-15%), Aluminum powder (12-15%).
*   Logistics cost is 20-30% of total volume.
*   Aluminum powder prices rose 15-20% in Q2 but saw a reversal in October.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Madhya Pradesh land (57,000 sq meters) acquired; construction is on hold due to economic/geopolitical conditions.
*   Management will decide on expansion in the next 1-2 quarters.
*   Current debt-to-equity is 1.4x (or 1.2x if including promoter unsecured loans).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Collection cycle is 70-80 days.
*   Company has taken credit insurance from Tata AIG to safeguard receivables.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical developments and monsoon-related disruptions impacted input costs and construction activity.
*   Liquidity squeeze in the market affecting collections.
*   Margin pressure due to raw material inflation (West Asia war impact).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: How are you dealing with inflationary pressures?* A: Passed on 5-7% of the 8-10% cost increase; will pass on the rest in upcoming quarters.
*   *Q: Why are EBITDA margins under pressure?* A: Due to raw material inflation (West Asia war) and monsoon impact; management is confident of recovery in H2.
*   *Q: What is the status of the MP expansion?* A: Land acquired, but construction is on hold; decision in 1-2 quarters.
*   *Q: What is the revenue potential of the construction chemical business?* A: Rs 20-25 crore.
*   *Q: What is the debt-to-equity ratio?* A: 1.4x reported, 1.2x adjusted for promoter loans.

### One-line takeaway
Bigbloc reported strong volume growth and record October sales, with management focusing on passing through remaining input cost inflation and improving operational efficiency to drive H2 profitability.
