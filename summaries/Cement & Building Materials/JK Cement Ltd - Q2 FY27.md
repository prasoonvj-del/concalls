# JK Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a 21% YoY increase in net sales driven by strong grey cement volume growth, along with progress on expansion projects and paint business profitability.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Net Sales:** Increased 21% YoY to Rs 3,561 crore (Q1 was Rs 2,754 crore; H1 up 25% at Rs 7,301 crore).
- **Consolidated Net Sales (H1):** Up 21% YoY at Rs 7,457 crore (vs Rs 6,167 crore).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 500 crore for Q2 (vs Rs 440 crore YoY and Rs 639 crore in Q1). H1 EBITDA higher by 2% at Rs 1,138 crore (vs Rs 1,113 crore).
- **Grey Cement Volumes (H1):** Increased 19.38% YoY to 11.3 million tonnes (vs 9.49 million tonnes).
- **White Cement Volumes (H1):** Increased 19% YoY to 10.32 lakh tonnes (vs 8.62 lakh tonnes).
- **Paint Revenue (Q2):** Rs 116 crore (H1 revenue reached around Rs 250 crore).
- **Paint EBITDA (Q2):** Loss of Rs 2 crore.
- **RMC & Construction Chemicals Revenue (Q2):** Rs 60 crore (vs Rs 50 crore in Q1).
- **Incentives (Q2):** Rs 45 crore.
- **Fuel Cost (Q2):** Rs 1.71 per Kcal.
- **Gross Debt:** Rs 6,255 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 5,136 crore).
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 1,684 crore (vs Rs 1,755 crore).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 4,571 crore (vs Rs 3,370 crore).
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.63.
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 1.95.

### Highlights
- Commissioned a 0.6 million tonne wall putty plant at Nathdwara, taking total white cement and putty capacity to 3.65 million tonnes.
- Board approved the JK Maxx paint plant proposal of 75,000 KL at an overall cost of Rs 130 crore (within the overall Rs 600 crore paint business limit). Management stated no further investment requirement from the parent company post this investment as the paint business is nearing break-even.
- Fuel mix for Q2: 55% Indian coal, 13% alternative fuels (AFR), and balance petcoke (30%–35%).
- Clinker utilization was 95% in Q2. Bihar plant capacity utilization is closer to 50%.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Volume Guidance:** Management maintained the annual volume guidance of 23 million tonnes plus.
- **Capex Guidance:** Around Rs 3,500 crore for FY27; expected to be in the vicinity of Rs 2,500 crore yearly in subsequent years for brownfield expansions.
- **Debt Peak:** Net debt-to-EBITDA expected to peak between 2.25x to 2.35x mostly at the end of FY27, with net debt peaking up to closer to Rs 6,000 crore on the 50 million tonne plan. Post expansion (from FY29 onwards), net debt-to-EBITDA is targeted well within 1.5x.
- **Capacity Goal:** 50 million tonne capacity by FY30.
- **Paint Business:** Expected to be EBITDA positive for the full fiscal year FY27, with subsequent long-term EBITDA margins targeted at 35%–38% and annual profitability increasing by 2% to 3%. Paint revenue guidance of Rs 500–550 crore for FY27 maintained.
- **Fuel Cost Outlook:** Cost per Kcal may go up closer to Rs 2 in Q3/Q4 due to rising Indian coal and petcoke prices, with an estimated impact of Rs 80–Rs 100 per Kcal in each of Q3 and Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
- North market growth has been good; East was slightly depressed.
- Price hikes of around Rs 20 per bag (trade and non-trade combined) observed from mid-September/October across regions. Management noted an additional Rs 10–Rs 15 per bag price increase is still required to cover cost pressures.
- South plants constitute about 15% of total volumes; management expects minimal impact from water shortage/drought issues in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel cost per Kcal was 1.71 in Q2, expected to rise towards Rs 2 due to rising Indian coal and petcoke spot prices.
- Premium products command an average extra profit of around Rs 150 per bag.
- Packaging costs remain high due to PP granule costs and geopolitical factors.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Integrated site at Jaisalmer is in advanced stage, on schedule, and scheduled for commissioning in H1 FY28.
- Grinding station at Bikaner is progressing for H1 FY28 commissioning. Bathinda plant received approvals; targeting commissioning within September 2027.
- Post-FY28 brownfield expansions planned at Muddapur and Panna with ~5 to 6 million tonnes capacity addition at each location.
- Mahan coal block work has started; targeting operation in the first half of FY29 with expected landed coal cost within Rs 1.00–Rs 1.25.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stands at Rs 6,255 crore and cash at Rs 1,684 crore as of September 30, 2026. Net debt is Rs 4,571 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential construction activity disruptions due to upcoming state elections (e.g., UP) or pollution controls/construction bans in Delhi.
- Rising global petcoke and Indian coal spot prices impacting fuel costs.
- Currency fluctuations (rupee depreciation) impacting packing and raw material costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities) asked about fuel cost trajectory:** Management replied that fuel cost per Kcal will rise towards Rs 2, with an impact of Rs 80–Rs 100 per Kcal felt partially in Q3 and fully in Q4.
- **Sanjeev Singh asked about peak debt and capex:** Management guided that net debt-to-EBITDA will peak at 2.25x–2.35x by end of FY27, net debt will peak near Rs 6,000 crore, and subsequent yearly capex will be around Rs 2,500 crore.
- **Prateek Kumar (Jefferies) questioned the rationale for entering the oversupplied paint segment:** Management explained that existing paint capacity is getting fully utilized (targeting ~Rs 500 crore revenue), requiring new capacity (75,000 KL plant for Rs 130 crore) to sustain growth alongside wall putty, with the business expected to be EBITDA positive this fiscal.
- **Amit Murarka (Axis Capital) asked about low fuel cost drivers:** Management cited increased usage of Indian coal (55%) and lower-cost old inventory as reasons for the lower Q2 fuel cost (1.71 per Kcal).
- **Ritesh Shah asked about the status of Mahan and West Shahdol coal blocks:** Management stated work on the Mahan coal block has started, targeting operations in H1 FY29 with expected coal cost of Rs 1.00–Rs 1.25.

### One-line takeaway
JK Cement reported a 21% YoY rise in Q2 net sales driven by strong grey cement volumes, maintained its 23 million tonne+ volume guidance, and advanced its expansion and paint profitability timelines despite near-term cost headwinds.
