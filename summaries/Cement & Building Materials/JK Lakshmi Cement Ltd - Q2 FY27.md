# JK Lakshmi Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management acknowledged seasonal weakness and cost pressures in Q2 but expressed optimism for H2 demand recovery and price improvements.

### Key numbers
*   **Volume Growth:** 5% (Q2), 7% (H1).
*   **Fuel Cost:** Rs 1.82 per 1,000 kcal (Q2), up from Rs 1.54.
*   **Power Cost:** Rs 6.24 per unit (Q2), up from Rs 5.52 (Q1).
*   **Capex (H1):** Rs 450 crore total; Rs 200 crore specifically for Durg.
*   **Non-Cement Revenue (Q2):** Rs 183 crore (RMC: Rs 86 crore; AAC block: Rs 70 crore).
*   **Clinker Sale (Q2):** 1,50,000 tonnes.
*   **Green Energy Share:** 48%.
*   **CC Ratio:** 1.48.
*   **Lead Distance:** 363 km.

### Highlights
*   Q2 performance impacted by heavy monsoons in key markets (Gujarat, Chhattisgarh) and planned maintenance shutdowns.
*   Management noted price increases in non-trade segments starting September and trade segments in October.
*   Strategy focuses on premiumization to improve realizations and narrow the gap with market leaders.
*   Management maintains a long-term focus on trade sales (58-59% share) despite current non-trade profitability.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Fuel Cost:** Management expects ~Rs 2 per 1,000 kcal in Q3 and Rs 2.15-2.20 in Q4.
*   **Volume:** Targets matching industry growth of 7-8% in H2.
*   **Capex:** Guidance of Rs 1,500 crore for FY27; Rs 2,000 crore for the subsequent two years.
*   **Net Debt/EBITDA:** Management aims to keep the ratio below 3.5x; expects to peak at 2.5x by year-end.
*   **Realization:** Management anticipates price increases of Rs 10 in November and Rs 15 in December.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand is improving post-Diwali with no major seasonal impediments.
*   North India remains a supplier-driven market; capacity expansion is dictated by limestone availability.
*   Management views Northeast as a future supply point for Eastern India (Bihar, North Bengal).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Domestic coal auction prices have risen >200%.
*   Power costs are expected to decrease in H2 due to the completion of shutdowns, improved solar output, and operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Durg Expansion (2.3 MT clinker, 1.6 MT cement):** 50% civil work complete; on track for March 2028.
*   **East Expansion (4.6 MT total):** Comprises Durg (1.6 MT), Madhubani (1.5 MT), and Mejia (1.5 MT).
*   **Northeast:** Targeting commissioning around March 2029.
*   **Patratu:** Project reconfigured to Mejia due to land and regulatory (Chota Nagpur Tenancy Act) challenges.
*   **Capacity Target:** Revised to 25.1 million tonnes by FY30 (from 30 million tonnes) to ensure balance sheet discipline.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Working capital blocked in H1 is expected to be released in H2.
*   Management emphasized prioritizing balance sheet health over aggressive capacity targets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   High volatility in fuel and energy costs.
*   Uncertainty regarding geopolitical impacts on fuel prices.
*   Regulatory and land acquisition challenges (e.g., Patratu project).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Why is EBITDA/tonne weak (Rs 510-520)? **Management:** Attributed to seasonality and cost pressure; expects recovery via price hikes.
*   **Analyst:** Why reduce capacity target from 30 MT to 25 MT? **Management:** To maintain prudential norms and avoid over-leveraging the balance sheet.
*   **Analyst:** Why is volume growth (5%) lagging industry (9-10%)? **Management:** Monsoon impact in core geographies; confident in matching industry growth in H2.
*   **Analyst:** Status of Patratu? **Management:** Reconfigured to Mejia due to land/regulatory issues; Mejia is faster to execute.

### One-line takeaway
JK Lakshmi Cement faced a seasonally weak Q2 but expects a recovery in H2, while prioritizing balance sheet discipline over aggressive capacity expansion.
