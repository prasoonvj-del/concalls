# Global Surfaces Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited significant headwinds from geopolitical instability, raw material cost inflation, and new U.S. trade tariffs impacting margins and pricing power.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 60 crore (up 10.7% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 125 crore (down 2.6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 1.3 crore (vs. loss of Rs 3.6 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 2.17% (vs. -6.65% in Q2 FY26; 12.69% in Q1 FY27).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 9.6 crore (vs. Rs 4.4 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 7.66% (up 424 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Loss of Rs 5.4 crore (vs. loss of Rs 4.6 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **H1 PAT:** Loss of Rs 5.3 crore (vs. loss of Rs 5.1 crore in H1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Shift:** Discontinued the Natural Stone unit in Bagru (effective March 31, 2026) to focus on engineered quartz; engineered stone now contributes ~98% of revenue.
*   **Debt Conversion:** Board approved converting Rs 110.44 crore of unsecured loans into equity for the subsidiary Global Surfaces FZE; Rs 111 crore reclassified as advance towards equity share subscription.
*   **Domestic Expansion:** Commenced domestic dispatches in November 2026 to reduce export dependence; domestic market currently accounts for ~4% of revenue.
*   **Product Mix:** "Marquartz" (patented technology) offers 20-30% higher selling prices compared to regular products.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management targets a quarterly revenue run rate of Rs 70-80 crore, potentially achievable by Q4 FY27, depending on geopolitical stability.
*   **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to improve "dramatically" once capacity utilization increases and geopolitical conditions stabilize.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **U.S. Safeguards:** New U.S. Section 201 measures (effective Aug 15, 2026) impose a 25% duty on the first 13 million square meters of quartz imports, rising to 50% for volumes above the quota.
*   **Market Impact:** The company has not been able to pass on cost increases to customers due to industry disruptions and the new U.S. tariff environment.
*   **Geopolitics:** Unpredictable geopolitical situations and high freight costs (up 5x) are currently impacting production and logistics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Input Costs:** Certain raw material prices increased by 25% to 50%, impacting selling prices by an estimated 10%.
*   **Freight:** Freight costs for imported raw materials rose nearly 5x.
*   **Mitigation:** The company is attempting to source more materials domestically to curtail freight costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity:** Combined annual production capacity of 1.14 million square meters across India and UAE facilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Receivables:** Increased by Rs 17 crore since March due to production/dispatch disruptions in March and May; expects normalization by end of Q3.
*   **Cash Flow:** Management noted that ~70-75% of H1 operating cash flow was derived from higher payables.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Tariffs:** U.S. Section 201 tariffs are a major concern; India and UAE received no exemptions.
*   **Pricing Power:** Inability to pass on cost inflation to customers due to market adjustments following new tariffs.
*   **Volatility:** Geopolitical instability remains the primary risk to production and raw material availability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the sharp drop in EBITDA margin from 12.7% in Q1 to 2.2% in Q2?** A: Management attributed this to Q1 having inventory spillover from pre-war situations and the subsequent impact of increased raw material/freight costs in Q2.
*   **Q: Can you pass on the 10% cost increase to customers?** A: Management stated they have not been able to pass on major increases yet, as the market is still adjusting to the new U.S. tariff environment.
*   **Q: When will the UAE business break even?** A: Management clarified the UAE business is already generating cash profits despite low utilization.
*   **Q: Are U.S. tariffs on Chinese quartz helping?** A: Management noted Chinese tariffs have been in place for 5-6 years and have no current impact on revenue; Section 201 tariffs are paid by customers, not the company.

### One-line takeaway
Global Surfaces is navigating a difficult period of high input costs and new U.S. trade tariffs, focusing on domestic market entry and product mix optimization to restore margins while waiting for geopolitical stabilization.
