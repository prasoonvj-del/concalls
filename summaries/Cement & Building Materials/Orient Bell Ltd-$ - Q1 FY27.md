# Orient Bell Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and profitability grew significantly YoY, supported by higher realizations, operating leverage, and manufacturing efficiencies.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 203 crore, up 42.8% YoY
- **Volume growth:** 22.9% YoY
- **ASP increase:** ~15.9% YoY
- **Gross margin:** 39.7% (highest ever)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 17.6 crore (vs Rs 5.6 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 8.7%, expanded by 480 bps YoY
- **PBT:** Rs 11.2 crore (vs loss of Rs 0.6 crore YoY)
- **Working capital (DSO):** Improved to 18 days (from 20 days sequentially)
- **Net cash/investments:** Over Rs 47.7 crore net of debt (debt-free balance sheet)
- **Capacity utilization:** 73% in Q1 (vs 64% in Q4)
- **Tile adhesive revenue:** Rs 2.5 crore in Q1

### Highlights
- Tech-driven ecosystem (InstaLook, PMT, Lakshya, Drishti AI chatbot) helped drive demand generation, with secondary sellout reaching ~40% of primary sales volume in Q1 (vs 26% last year).
- Continuous 24/7 TV advertising for over a year supported brand preference and inquiry generation.
- Supply gap emerged due to Morbi plant shutdowns from end-March to mid-May, which was bridged by organized players like OBL and dealer inventory drawdown.
- South business grew 37% YoY in volume and West business grew 60% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its policy of not providing any future guidance for top line, margins, or volume growth due to macro volatility.
- Management remains optimistic based on strong structural growth drivers (housing, infrastructure, renovation) and positive input KPIs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment favored branded players with lower dependence on Morbi.
- Dealers are seeking supply security and diversifying sourcing locations away from single-geography dependence on Morbi.
- Exports are down (averaging ~Rs 800 crore/month in the first two months of the year vs historical Rs 1,500-1,600 crore) due to elevated freight costs and West Asian geopolitical tensions (freight costs up 5x-6x).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Price increases of ~18% to 19% taken vs pre-war situation, largely sustained.
- Average gas price in Q1 was ~Rs 60; Sikandrabad plant gas prices moved from pre-war Rs 44-45 to Rs 60-62.
- Price gap between OBL and Morbi players narrowed from ~Rs 100 to Rs 50-55.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of around Rs 10 to 15 crore to convert 1 million meters of existing ceramic capacity to GVT, replace DPMs, and add polishing machines (internally financed).
- Focus remains on maximizing existing asset utilization before incremental capacity additions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free with cash and liquid investments over Rs 47.7 crores net of debt.
- Working capital improved to 18 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile operating environment, ongoing geopolitical uncertainties in the Middle East, and fluctuating gas/propane prices.
- Export markets remain impacted by high freight rates and regional conflicts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gunit Singh (Counter Cyclical PMS) on gas price impact/sustainability:** Management stated no price cuts have been taken as gas prices remain volatile around ~Rs 60, and sustainability depends on broader industry pricing trends.
- **Ashvath Rajan (Arihant Capital) on Dora plant and GVT mix:** Management clarified that GVT is 47% of Q1 sales value, with 15-20% from Dora, bulk from SKD, and low single-digit from Morbi.
- **Apurva Sharma (Raas Capital) on gas contracts:** Management clarified that Morbi JV ties up with Gujarat Gas, while outside Morbi, gas is sourced via GAIL.
- **Ashvath Rajan (Arihant Capital) on cash deployment:** Management stated they are debating options internally for the cash pile and will announce expansion plans over the next 2-3 months.
- **Ashvath Rajan (Arihant Capital) on bathware entry:** Management stated they have no plans to enter bathware and remain focused on tiles and tile adhesives.

### One-line takeaway
Orient Bell delivered its highest-ever gross margins and strong revenue growth (+42.8% YoY) in Q1 FY27, capitalizing on Morbi supply disruptions through tech-driven demand generation and a debt-free balance sheet.
