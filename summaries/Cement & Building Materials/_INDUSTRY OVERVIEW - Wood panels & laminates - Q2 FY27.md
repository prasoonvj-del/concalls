# Cement & Building Materials - Wood panels & laminates: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector experienced a robust Q2 FY27 characterized by record-breaking revenues and profits for several players—such as **Century Plyboards**, **Euro Pratik Sales**, **Rushil Decor**, **Duroply Industries**, and **Dhabriya Polywood**—driven by strong domestic retail demand, premiumization, and capacity expansions. However, the picture is nuanced: companies heavily reliant on exports or international subsidiaries (like **Greenlam Industries** and **Birlanu**) faced severe logistical bottlenecks or foreign market headwinds. Meanwhile, the MDF segment grappled with overcapacity and pricing pressures, noted by **Century Plyboards**, **Greenpanel Industries**, and **Rushil Decor**.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board (including **Century Plyboards**, **Duroply**, **Euro Pratik**, **Greenpanel**, **Greenply**, and **Rushil**) agree that domestic retail demand remains strong and resilient, supported by urbanization and premiumization.
*   **Divergence:** International and export demand showed stark contrasts. **Stylam Industries** reported robust export demand and market share gains due to high energy costs in Europe. Conversely, **Greenlam Industries** and **Greenpanel Industries** cited severe export headwinds, including container shortages, vessel delays, and spiking freight rates. In the OEM segment, **Greenpanel** noted a temporary slowdown as price-sensitive buyers initially resisted price hikes.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Cost Pressures:** Multiple companies (**Stylam**, **Duroply**, **Greenpanel**, **Rushil**) flagged rising chemical costs, volatile crude prices, and surging ocean freight rates due to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East. **Stylam** quantified freight and PNG impacts at Rs 8.15 crore and Rs 2.5 crore respectively.
*   **Margin Divergence:** Oversupply in MDF led to margin compression and pricing pressure for **Century Plyboards**, **Greenpanel**, and **Rushil**. In contrast, companies focusing on value-added products and operational leverage—such as **Duroply**, **Euro Pratik**, and **Greenply**—reported stable or expanding margins. **Birlanu's** India business marked its 5th consecutive quarter of margin expansion, while its European subsidiary Parador struggled with persistent losses.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Expansions Ongoing:** Capacity additions are widespread. **Century Plyboards** commissioned its Hoshiarpur plywood plant and planned UP expansions. **Dhabriya Polywood** is scaling extrusion and aluminum capacity under a Rs 100 crore capex program. **Greenlam** is on track for Q4 FY27 commercial production at its Andhra Pradesh plant. **Stylam Industries** successfully operationalized its new manufacturing plant in September 2026. **Greenply** announced a new plywood expansion at Sandila, Lucknow.
*   **Asset-Light Strategies:** To mitigate capex risks, **Duroply Industries** entered the MDF category (Duro HDMMR) via an asset-light model utilizing excess peer manufacturing capacity.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Greenlam Industries:** Lowered consolidated revenue growth guidance from 18%-20% to 16%-18% and turned more cautious due to export logistics bottlenecks.
*   **Duroply Industries:** Shifted tone from mixed to positive following strong top-line recovery, delivering on Q1 guidance for double-digit growth.
*   **Greenpanel Industries:** Maintained a cautious tone due to volume degrowth and margin contraction stemming from expensive inventory and slow OEM recovery.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Parador Turnaround:** Birlanu’s execution of the BCG cost-out program to make its European subsidiary EBITDA positive in H2 FY27.
*   **MDF Oversupply & Pricing:** Whether MDF margins bottom out and stabilize for Century Plyboards, Greenpanel, and Rushil.
*   **Export Logistics:** Resolution of container availability and freight rate normalization for Greenlam and Stylam.
*   **New Plant Ramp-ups:** Capacity utilization progress at Stylam’s new plant, Greenply’s new lines, and Century’s Hoshiarpur facility.

## Company one-liners
*   **Birlanu:** India operations are booming, but European subsidiary Parador remains a heavy drag on profitability.
*   **Century Plyboards:** Delivered record revenues and robust return ratios despite working capital debt and an oversupplied MDF market.
*   **Dhabriya Polywood:** Achieved its strongest quarter in history, supercharged by premiumization and a massive Rs 240 crore order book.
*   **Duroply Industries:** Hit record revenues and profits through operational leverage and an innovative asset-light MDF entry.
*   **Euro Pratik Sales:** Posted record-high revenue and profit while expanding manufacturing footprint via the Drashti Surfaces acquisition.
*   **Greenlam Industries:** Growth stalled due to severe export logistics bottlenecks, though chipboard and allied segments showed bright spots.
*   **Greenpanel Industries:** Suffered near-term margin contraction and OEM volume headwinds, but retail MDF sales hit record highs.
*   **Greenply Industries:** Delivered solid value growth and margin expansion by prioritizing premiumization amid capacity bottlenecks.
*   **Rushil Decor:** Propelled to record revenues by soaring domestic MDF demand and expanding Jumbo Laminate scale.
*   **Stylam Industries:** Successfully commissioned a major new plant and captured export market share despite freight cost headwinds.
