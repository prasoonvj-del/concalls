# Star Cement Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - muted demand in early quarters due to elections and severe flooding in the Northeast, alongside subsidy headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Clinker Production:** 9.10 lakh tonnes (vs 8.90 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Cement Production:** 13.08 lakh tonnes (vs 12.31 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Cement Sales Volume:** 13.02 lakh tonnes (vs 12.22 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Clinker Sales Volume:** 0.52 lakh tonnes (vs 0.74 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Geographical Cement Sales:** Northeast: 8.71 lakh tonnes (vs 8.67 lakh tonnes YoY); Outside Northeast: 4.31 lakh tonnes (vs 3.55 lakh tonnes YoY)
- **Blend Mix:** ~15% OPC, rest PPC
- **Premium Sales:** 15.9% of overall sales
- **Trade Share:** ~80%
- **Lead Distance:** 210 km
- **Clinker Factor:** 66.5%
- **Total Revenue:** Rs 902 crore (vs Rs 847 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 203 crore (vs Rs 230 crore YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 74 crore (vs Rs 98 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 1,497 (vs Rs 1,774 YoY)
- **Fuel Cost per tonne:** 1.55 (vs 1.33 in Q4 FY26)
- **Green Share:** ~3% (including WHRS)
- **Capex Spent (Q1):** ~Rs 93 crore
- **Incentives Accrued/Outstanding:** Total outstanding from Assam is Rs 130 crore; expected annual subsidy reduced from Rs 145 crore to Rs 115 crore due to a new Assam government circular dividing the total subsidy over 12 years.

### Highlights
- EBITDA decreased YoY due to reduced subsidies, increased packing material costs, and kiln shutdown expenses.
- Subsidy impact included a ~Rs 40 crore hit in Q1 due to the GST rate revision from 28% to 18% implemented last year.
- Non-cement Building Solutions division (AAC and RMC) is scaling up, aiming to reach an annualized revenue run-rate (ARR) of Rs 150 crore by Q4.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised full-year cement volume growth down from 11%-12% to 8%-9%.
- Clinker sales for FY27 expected to be stagnant or degrow by 5%-10% YoY.
- Full-year EBITDA per tonne estimated between Rs 1,500 - Rs 1,600 (though Q2 may dip to ~Rs 1,400 due to monsoons and fixed cost absorption).
- Full-year capex guidance of Rs 500 crore maintained for FY27. Total capex for Rajasthan and Haryana projects expected at ~Rs 2,700 crore (excluding GST, or ~Rs 2,900 crore including GST).
- Fuel cost expected to moderate to ~1.45 in Q2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 and Q2 demand impacted by elections in Assam and severe flooding in the Northeast.
- Pent-up demand expected to release in H2 (Q3 and Q4), driving higher-than-usual growth.
- Northeast industry growth for FY27 estimated at ~7%; company expects 8% to 9% growth in the region.
- Cement prices remained broadly flat across Northeast and outside Northeast (Bihar and West Bengal) between Q1 and Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel cost per tonne rose to 1.55 in Q1 from 1.33 in Q4 FY26 due to Coal India diverting coal to power plants, forcing reliance on more expensive spot contracts (30% fuel mix; FSA was 45%).
- PP bag prices remain high due to international turmoil and war situations.
- Cost-saving initiatives over the next 3-6 months: commissioning railway siding in Silchar, introducing EVs on select routes, and adding a wagon tippler in Siliguri (expected savings of ~Rs 150 per tonne on Siliguri sales).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Rajasthan Project (Nimbol):** Public hearing for Environmental Clearance (EC) scheduled for end of August; EC expected in early October. On-ground work targeted to start mid-October to November. Project timeline is 18-20 months (Q4 FY28 or Q1 FY29). Standard approved incentive package secured.
- **West Bengal / Bihar Grinding Unit:** Company is evaluating the upcoming West Bengal industrial policy (expected around August 15th) to potentially prioritize a brownfield expansion in Siliguri over the Bihar (Begusarai) greenfield project.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-EBITDA expected to be manageable around 1.5x - 1.6x for North expansion; no active QIP plans currently, though to be considered at an opportune time.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe flooding in Assam causing temporary shutdowns and a 12% YoY volume degrowth in July.
- Continued high packing material costs due to geopolitical tensions.
- Subsidy payout modifications by the Assam government reducing near-term cash flow recognition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital) on volume and clinker growth revisions:** Management clarified that heavy floods in Assam reduced Q1/Q2 expectations, leading to a volume growth revision of 8%–9% and flat-to-negative clinker sales growth.
2. **Rajesh Ravi (HDFC Securities) on Northeast demand and competition:** Management stated that the dip was due to elections and monsoons, not Dalmia's capacity ramp-up, and market share was maintained.
3. **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities) on West Bengal vs. Bihar capex:** Management explained they are waiting for West Bengal's industrial policy to decide whether to shift the planned 2 million tonne grinding capex from Bihar to Siliguri.
4. **Harsh Mittal (Emkay Global) on non-cement revenue guidance:** Management confirmed the target is an annualized revenue run-rate (ARR) of Rs 150 crore for Building Solutions by Q4.
5. **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities) on Assam subsidy changes:** Management detailed the outstanding incentive amounts and explained that the new circular prorating subsidies over 12 years reduces FY27 expected subsidy to Rs 115 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Star Cement delivered a subdued Q1 FY27 impacted by floods, elections, and GST-driven subsidy cuts, but management expects a demand rebound in H2 alongside structural cost-saving initiatives and progress on its North India expansion.
