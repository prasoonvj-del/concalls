# Orient Cement Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a resilient performance, strong trade volume growth, improved product and regional mix, cost reduction measures, and clear progress on expansion and the One Cement platform.

### Key numbers
- **Cement sales volume:** 16.5 million metric tonnes (Q2 FY27)
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 9,197 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1,271 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **EBITDA margin:** ~14% (Q2 FY27)
- **EBITDA per metric tonne:** Rs 770 (Q2 FY27)
- **PAT:** Rs 236 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **Net worth:** Rs 72,238 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **Cash and cash equivalents:** Rs 1,070 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **Trade volume growth:** +8.6% YoY
- **Trade share:** 74% (+6% YoY)
- **Premium products share in trade sales:** 37% (+2% YoY)
- **Blended cement share:** 82% (up from 77%)
- **Ready-mix concrete volume:** 1.07 million cubic metres (+19% YoY)
- **Green power share:** 61% (company level, consumption and sales)
- **Net power cost:** Rs 4.8 per unit (vs Rs 6 in Q2 FY26 and Rs 4.3 in Q1 FY27)
- **Fuel cost:** Rs 1,875 per 1,000 kilo calorie (Rs 1.87 per 1,000 kcal)
- **Primary lead:** Reduced from 265 km to 254 km
- **Total cost:** Rs 4,410 per metric tonne (Rs 4,182 per tonne on a normalized basis excluding geopolitical conflict impact)
- **Capex guidance:** ~Rs 6,500 crore to Rs 7,000 crore for FY27; ~Rs 7,500 crore per year for the 30 million tonne expansion program (total ~Rs 14,500 crore)
- **Capacity targets:** 149 million metric tonnes by FY29; 155 million metric tonnes by FY30
- **Realization improvement:** +Rs 10 per bag achieved in October

### Highlights
- Trade volumes grew 8.6% YoY with trade share rising to 74%.
- Commissioned 4.6 million tonnes of capacity during Q2 across Dahej, Salai Banwa, and Kalamboli; another 3.2 million tonnes stabilized at Bathinda and Jodhpur (Penna), while 2.4 million tonnes at Warisaliganj is slated for commissioning in H2 FY27.
- Business working capital cycle improved by 10 days due to better receivables and inventory management.
- Proposed mergers of ACC and Orient Cement with Ambuja Cements are proceeding to NCLT for final approvals and are expected to be completed within the current financial year.
- Green power sales revenue stood at Rs 136 crore for Q2 (352 million kWh units sold); fly ash revenue stood at Rs 25 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track to achieve the FY27 cost target of Rs 4,250 per metric tonne.
- Volume growth is expected to stay ahead of the industry in H2 FY27, supported by accelerated trade volumes and calibrated institutional sales.
- Industry demand growth is projected at 6% to 7% for full fiscal year FY27.
- Planned expansions aim to reach 149 MTPA by FY29 and 155 MTPA by FY30 via a ~Rs 14,500 crore capex program.

### Demand & industry commentary
- North cluster led on EBITDA and total volume growth (trade volumes up 13% YoY).
- Central cluster delivered the second highest regional EBITDA with 9% trade volume growth.
- West recorded 14% trade volume growth and the most resilient overall EBITDA performance.
- East maintained trade volumes broadly in line with last year; South grew trade volumes by 13% YoY and delivered a strong sequential volume recovery.
- Near-term conditions remain challenging due to geopolitical conflicts, global uncertainties, higher energy costs, and weaker/deficient monsoons leading to drought declarations in certain states.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Green power share reached 61% at the company level; net power cost reduced to Rs 4.8 per unit.
- Fuel mix optimized with higher domestic coal share (>60%) compared to high-cost pet coke (10% to 12%); imported thermal coal spot at Rs 2.80–Rs 2.85 per 1,000 kcal.
- Logistics costs moderately higher due to diesel costs, partly offset by primary lead reduction (265 km to 254 km) and fly ash movement via BCFC infrastructure.
- 20 million tonnes of fly ash tied up with group thermal power plants under long-term agreements.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Announced 14 expansion projects adding ~30 million tonnes of cement and 10.3 million tonnes of clinker capacity.
- Grinding capacity additions: 15.2 million tonnes in West, 11.6 million tonnes in East, and 2.9 million tonnes in North.
- Clinker capacity additions: 4 MTPA at Maratha, 4 MTPA at Umrangso, and 2.25 MTPA at Mundra.
- Capex outlay projected at ~Rs 6,500–7,000 crore for FY27 (Rs 3,500 crore incurred in H1) and ~Rs 7,500 crore annually for the next two years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company continues to remain debt-free with a net worth of Rs 72,238 crore and cash/cash equivalents of Rs 1,070 crore.
- Credit ratings maintained at AAA (long-term) and A1+ (short-term).
- Working capital cycle improved by 10 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts affecting fuel costs and global supply chains.
- Monsoon-related headwinds and deficient rainfall/drought declarations in key states impacting construction activity.
- Constraints in domestic coal availability and elevated energy/fuel costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital):** Asked about the rationale for accelerating capex and total outlay. *Management answered that the plan follows previously communicated targets to reach 149–155 MTPA and requires ~Rs 14,500 crore, funded via internal cash flows.*
- **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities):** Questioned the change in stance regarding aggressive capex given soft first-half volumes. *Management clarified that quarterly numbers do not drive long-term capex, the first wave of expansion is concluding, and new investments focus on high-value, low-cost brownfield expansions with available clinker.*
- **Pinakin (HSBC):** Inquired if the company is prioritizing volume growth and market share over profitability and pricing. *Management stated they are balancing both, focusing on value-led growth in high-margin markets supported by strong brand equity.*
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec):** Asked about fly ash transfer pricing and group synergies. *Management highlighted long-term tie-ups for 20 million tonnes of fly ash at competitive landed costs via group thermal power plants and BCFC.*
- **Rahul Gupta (Morgan Stanley):** Asked about H2 volume expectations and spot fuel costs. *Management guided for higher single-digit to double-digit volume growth in H2 and noted imported spot fuel costs at Rs 2.80–2.85 per 1,000 kcal.*

### One-line takeaway
Orient Cement (via Ambuja Cements) reported a resilient Q2 FY27 with stable margins, ongoing cost optimization, and a clear roadmap for a self-funded 30 million tonne expansion to reach 149 MTPA by FY29.
