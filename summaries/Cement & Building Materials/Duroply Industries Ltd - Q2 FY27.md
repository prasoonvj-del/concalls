# Duroply Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest quarterly revenue and most profitable quarter to date with double-digit revenue growth across all segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (Delivered on Q1 guidance of double-digit growth for Q2; expects H2 FY27 to be better than H1 FY27).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from Mixed to Positive following strong top-line recovery and margin expansion.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Entered MDF category (Duro HDMMR) via an asset-light model; potential construction halts in Delhi/NCR flagged; elevated shipping rates and chemical costs cited. Specific reference to West Asia war cost pressures disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises: Target of double-digit revenue growth in Q2 FY27 was delivered (17% YoY).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 122.2 crore (up 17% YoY, up 22.7% QoQ).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 4.86 crore (up 82% YoY from Rs 2.67 crore).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 4.38 crore.
- **In-House Manufactured Goods Revenue:** Rs 62.7 crore (up 18.5% YoY, up 22% QoQ).
- **Contract Manufacturing Revenue:** Rs 59.5 crore (up 15.4% YoY, up 23% QoQ).
- **Gross Margin:** 34.6% (vs 34.8% in Q2 FY26, 35.5% in Q1 FY27).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 8.62 crore (up 33.4% YoY, up 79% QoQ).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.1% (up 90 bps YoY from 6.2%, up from 4.8% in Q1 FY27).
- **Operating Expenses:** 27.5% of revenue (vs 28.9% in Q2 FY26).
- **Brand Spend:** 3.6% of sales (vs 3.9% in Q1 FY27, 2.7% in FY26 overall).
- **Employee Cost:** 10.4% of sales (vs 12.8% in Q1 FY27).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 221.9 crore (up 12% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 13.44 crore (up 13.4% YoY) at a 6.1% margin.
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 5.9 crore (up 29.5% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 4.98 crore (up 25.9% YoY).

### Highlights
- Recorded highest quarterly revenue to date at Rs 122.2 crore, beating the previous peak of Rs 111.6 crore in Q4 FY26.
- Entered a new product category, launching Duro HDMMR in the MDF segment using an asset-light model without capex.
- Achieved double-digit overall revenue growth for H1 FY27 (12% YoY) despite a weak Q1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 revenue and profit growth momentum to continue and perform better than H1 FY27.
- Management expects double-digit revenue growth for full-year FY27.
- EBITDA margins achieved in Q2 (7.1%) are expected to be sustainable over H2 FY27, subject to raw material cost pass-through.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 blip in April and May was fully overcome from June onwards.
- Strong retail demand observed for high-density products (HDMMR/MDF) in home construction and project sites.
- Overcapacity among larger MDF peers allowed Duroply to leverage external manufacturing capacities asset-lightly.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expansion to 7.1% was driven by volume growth over flat overhead expenses.
- Gross margin slipped slightly to 34.6% due to raw material cost increases and general inflationary pressures.
- Management noted cost risks from unyielding shipping rates and creeping chemical costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Modernization at the Rajkot manufacturing plant is progressing extremely well.
- Entry into the MDF/HDMMR category requires zero capex as it leverages third-party manufacturing capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **H1 FY27 Debtor Days:** 38 days (improved from 42 days in FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Inventory Days:** 151 days (improved from 164 days in Q1 FY27; vs 145 days in FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Creditor Days:** 88 days (vs 72 days in FY26).
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 101 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delhi/NCR region seasonal construction challenges/restrictions during Q3/H2.
- Volatility and uncertainty in raw material pricing, chemical costs, and ocean shipping rates, alongside pass-through limitations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Maitri Shah:** *What drove Q2 EBITDA margin to 7.1% and is it sustainable?*  
  **Management:** Margin expansion came from higher volume leverage over flat overheads. Management believes these margins are sustainable over H2 FY27, provided raw material cost increases can be passed through to customers.
- **Jojo Shaju:** *Why enter MDF now when management earlier stated no new segment entry for 12-18 months and peers are struggling with overcapacity?*  
  **Management:** Entry was executed on a zero-capex, asset-light model by leveraging excess manufacturing capacity of peers who struggled with sales/marketing. It plugs a gap in Duroply's retail product offering for project sites.
- **Jojo Shaju:** *Are there plans for more products in the MDF segment?*  
  **Management:** Focus remains strictly on establishing the Duro HDMMR high-density line for retail demand; further extensions will be evaluated only after seeing response.

### One-line takeaway
Duroply achieved record quarterly revenues and profits in Q2 FY27 led by operational leverage, asset-light MDF entry, and strong retail demand, maintaining guidance for H2 to outperform H1.
