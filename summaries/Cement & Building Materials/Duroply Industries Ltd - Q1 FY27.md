# Duroply Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth was below expectations, but gross margins improved due to product mix and manufacturing efficiencies, despite increased brand spending impacting EBITDA.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 99.6 crore (up 6.5% YoY, down 10.7% QoQ)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 1.04 crore (vs Rs 1.88 crore in Q1 FY26, Rs 1.31 crore in Q4 FY26)
- **In-house Manufactured Goods Revenue:** Rs 51.3 crore (up 3.7% YoY, down 17% QoQ)
- **Contract Manufacturing Revenue:** Rs 48.3 crore (up 9.6% YoY, down 2.9% QoQ)
- **Gross Margins:** 35.5% (vs 34.1% in Q1 FY26, 34.3% in Q4 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 4.82 crore (down 10.5% YoY, down 7% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 4.8% (vs 5.8% in Q1 FY26, 4.6% in Q4 FY26)
- **Brand Spend:** Close to 4% of sales (vs 2.2% in Q1 FY26)
- **Debtor Days:** 38 days (vs 42 days in Q1 FY26)
- **Inventory Days:** 164 days (vs 145 days in Q1 FY26)
- **Creditor Days:** 93 days (vs 72 days in Q1 FY26)

### Highlights
- Duroply entered its 70th year in business on June 26, 2026.
- Gross margin expansion was driven by a better product mix and manufacturing efficiencies built over the last 18 months, alongside stronger relationships with contract manufacturers.
- The company consciously increased brand spend to close to 4% of sales to boost brand visibility for medium-to-long-term gains, which impacted EBITDA margins.
- Working capital metrics showed improvement in debtor days, while inventory and creditor days were elevated.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue growth to improve into double digits in Q2, supported by increased brand spend.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth numbers in Q1 were below expectation.
- West Asia war created significant cost pressures.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost pressures from the West Asia war were mitigated through better material usage, passing on cost increases, and negotiating better terms with vendors.
- Gross margins expanded to 35.5%.
- EBITDA margins declined to 4.8% due to a conscious decision to increase brand spends to ~4% of sales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debtor days decreased to 38 days of sales (vs 42 days last year).
- Inventory days were slightly elevated to 164 days (vs 145 days last year).
- Creditor days were elevated to 93 days (vs 72 days last year).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia war created significant cost pressures.
- Q1 growth numbers were below expectations.
