# Rushil Decor Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue driven by strong laminate and MDF performance, along with expanding margins and capacity utilization.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs. 2,953 million (up 25.3% YoY, 29.0% QoQ)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs. 280 million (up 24.5% YoY); Margin: 9.5%
- Consolidated PAT: Rs. 101 million (up 96.6% YoY); Margin: 3.4%
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs. 5,243 million (up 26.4% YoY); EBITDA: Rs. 462 million (Margin: 8.8%); PAT: Rs. 121 million
- Laminate Revenue: Rs. 884 million (up 61.7% YoY); EBITDA: Rs. 85 million (Margin: 9.6%); Realisation: Rs. 847 per sheet (up 21.7% YoY)
- Jumbo Laminates Revenue: Rs. 217 million (up 97.3% QoQ); EBITDA: Rs. 42 million (Margin: 19.4%); Capacity Utilisation: 41%
- MDF Revenue: Rs. 1,962 million (up 15.8% YoY); EBITDA: Rs. 194 million (up 6.0% YoY); Margin: 9.9%; Capacity Utilisation: 87% (up from 66% in Q1)
- PVC Revenue: Rs. 103 million (up 14.3% YoY); EBITDA: Rs. 2 million (Margin: 2.4%)
- Net Debt: Rs. 2,715 million as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs. 2,546 million as of March 31, 2026); Net Debt-to-Equity: 0.41
- Working Capital: Inventory days reduced to 146 (from 195 in FY26); Receivable days improved to 78 (from 81); Payable days reduced to 157 (from 189)

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue in Q2 FY27, led by strong laminate and domestic MDF demand.
- Jumbo Laminates sales volumes more than tripled sequentially, with revenue up 97.3% QoQ.
- MDF domestic revenue grew 41% YoY; no MDF exports were undertaken during the quarter due to global trade uncertainties and elevated freight costs.
- Value-added products contributed 44% of MDF volumes and 55% of MDF revenues in H1 FY27.
- Expanded domestic distribution by adding 8 new direct distributors and 27 dealers.
- Completed product certifications and approvals for global markets (including Europe and Australia).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 performance to improve over H1 FY27.
- Full-year MDF capacity utilization is targeted between 82% and 85%.
- Target for value-added MDF products: 50% of volume and 60% of revenue in FY27.
- Full-year FY27 capex guidance: Rs. 15 crore to Rs. 20 crore maximum, funded from internal accruals (Rs. 14 crore already incurred in H1).
- Management declined to provide specific FY28 guidance.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic MDF demand remained strong across retail and OEM channels.
- Freight rates remain elevated (USD 8,000), keeping exports constrained.
- BIS standards in India and high freight costs have largely insulated the domestic market from cheap imports (though Malaysia, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia are key source regions).
- Management views India as well-positioned to replace China in global furniture sourcing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material and chemical prices have been volatile, causing price pressure and a 5% to 10% competitive pricing pressure in MDF.
- Laminate price hikes were implemented post-October 10 (Q3).
- Jumbo Laminate EBITDA margin stood at 19.4%, while regular laminate EBITDA margin trended around 7% due to raw material spikes and freight costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major expansion capex planned for FY27 beyond normal operational capex (Rs. 15-20 crore).
- Jumbo laminate utilization reached 41% in Q2 and is targeted to reach 80%-85% by March.
- Any fresh capex decisions for FY28/FY29 (greenfield vs. brownfield) are under internal review and will be announced later this quarter. Brownfield machinery lead time is about 8 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs. 2,715 million with a net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.41.
- Significant working capital improvements achieved through reduced inventory and payable days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile chemical raw material prices and high global shipping/freight costs (USD 8,000).
- Domestic pricing pressure and 5%-10% competition in MDF.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Agarwal (Mutual Fund) on MDF realization decline QoQ:** Management explained that lower QoQ realization was driven by volatile chemical raw material prices and 5%-10% domestic competitive pressure, alongside a shift to domestic-only sales.
2. **Rusmik Oza (9Rays Equi Research) on Jumbo Laminate revenue potential:** Management stated that at 80%-85% capacity utilization, Jumbo Laminates can generate Rs. 80 crore to Rs. 100 crore+ in revenue, with a business-level EBITDA margin target of 12% to 14%.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Investments) on BIS impact and import threat:** Management clarified that out of 52-53 foreign companies, only 2 have BIS certification, and high freight costs make imports unviable, leaving the domestic market open to Indian players.
4. **Dhruvin Doshi on export geographies for Jumbo Laminates:** Management noted traction in Europe (including Russia and specialized applications like exterior facades and BBQ tabletops) and expanded export reach from 52-54 to 63 countries, with plans to add 7 more in H2.
5. **S. Agarwal on brownfield capex lead time:** Management stated that the lead time for the main machinery in a brownfield expansion is generally 8 months.

### One-line takeaway
Rushil Decor delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue in Q2 FY27, propelled by record domestic MDF demand, rising Jumbo Laminate scale, and improved working capital efficiency, despite raw material and freight headwinds.
