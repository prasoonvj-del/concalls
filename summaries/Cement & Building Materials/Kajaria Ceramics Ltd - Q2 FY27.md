# Kajaria Ceramics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved 10% YoY volume growth, 24% revenue growth, and improved EBITDA margins.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: INR 1,468 crores (up 24% YoY)
- Tiles segment revenue: INR 1,291 crores (up 23% YoY)
- Bathware segment revenue: INR 124 crores (up 22% YoY)
- Adhesives segment revenue: INR 54 crores (up 69% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 19.5% (compared to 17.94% in Q2 FY26)
- PBT: INR 254 crores (compared to INR 181 crores in Q2 FY26)
- PAT: INR 190 crores (compared to INR 133 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Volume growth: 10% YoY
- Capital cycle: 48 days (as of September 30, 2026, up by 2 days QoQ)
- Nepal JV capacity utilization: 85% in Q2 FY27

### Highlights
- Volume growth of 10% YoY achieved across all regions and end-user segments (retail and projects), driven by market share gains and demand.
- Price hikes taken cumulatively YTD: North and South plants increased prices by 12% to 13% across all product categories; Morbi price increases were 18% to 19%. No price hikes were taken in Q2.
- Promoters continue to forgo their salaries out of goodwill.
- Total dealer network stands at ~1,800 across India, of which ~450 are exclusive.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects double-digit volume growth to continue over the next two quarters and the coming 6 months.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to remain stable around current levels (~18% to 19%), focusing on absolute EBITDA and volume/market share gains rather than margin expansion.
- Adhesives revenue is targeted at ~INR 240 crores for the current year, scaling up to ~INR 400 crores next year.
- Tile sales volume is expected to reach ~145 million square meters next year (with outsourcing reducing from 42 million square meters to ~36-37 million square meters).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry market size is estimated at ~INR 60,000 crores for the full financial year (Morbi exports ~INR 12,000 crores, domestic industry ~INR 48,000 crores).
- Export demand in H1 FY27 remained subdued due to elevated freight rates and limited container availability.
- Morbi unorganized players and manufacturers face severe pressure due to soaring gas/propane prices and unorganized market struggles with higher price points.
- The price gap between Kajaria and Morbi players narrowed from ~40% pre-February to ~20%-25%.
- 40%-50% of plants in Morbi are reported shut down due to export slowdown and high production costs.
- Market growth is estimated at 5%-6%, with Kajaria outperforming through market share gains.

### Costs, raw materials & margins
- Average gas cost for Q2 FY27: North INR 70.56/SCM, South INR 63/SCM, West INR 75/SCM, Blended average INR ~70/SCM.
- Spot gas prices globally are very high ($28 to $30 per SCM). Propane prices in Morbi increased recently, reducing its discount to natural gas to ~5%.
- Gas costs are expected to benefit from internal firing cycle optimization and potential cheaper gas sourcing starting January.
- Other expenses increased to INR 138 crores due to higher advertisement expenses, which management expects to maintain going forward.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Srikalahasti capacity expansion increased from 10 million square meters to 12.5 million square meters (capex revised to INR 240 crores), with commissioning expected by the end of June next year.
- Gailpur expansion is 11 million square meters; both expansions are brownfield and focused entirely on GVT (Glazed Vitrified Tiles).
- Nepal JV plant utilization was 85%; total investment in Nepal is ~INR 200 crores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Loans given of INR 155 crores in H1 form part of normal treasury operations extended to NBFCs for optimizing returns with safety as a priority.
- Current borrowings/drawdowns of INR 178 crores relate to working capital optimization and joint venture bank limits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated international freight rates, limited container availability, and geopolitical tensions (Middle East conflict) continue to impact export demand and gas/propane price volatility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Praveen Sahay (PL Capital) on gross margins vs. pricing:** Management explained that EBITDA margins improved through operational leverage and pricing, while realization shifts were minor.
2. **Sonali S. (Jefferies) on margin sustainability:** Management stated margins will remain stable around current levels, passing extra benefits to gain volume share.
3. **Ritesh Shah (Investec India) on gas costs and pricing gap:** Management detailed regional gas costs and explained how the narrowing price gap with Morbi (from 40% to 20-25%) drives sustainable volume growth.
4. **Varun Julasaria (360 ONE Capital) on adhesive business outlook:** Management highlighted rapid scaling of adhesives from INR 125 crores last year to a target of INR 400 crores next year due to structural shifts toward larger tiles.
5. **Dhananjay Mishra (Centrum Broking) on Srikalahasti capex increase:** Management clarified that the capex increase from INR 210 crores to INR 240 crores and capacity from 10 to 12.5 MSM includes larger sheds and warehouses to accommodate future expansions at lower incremental costs.

### One-line takeaway
Kajaria Ceramics delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 10% volume growth and 24% revenue growth, leveraging cost advantages in North/South plants and narrowing price gaps with Morbi to capture market share.
