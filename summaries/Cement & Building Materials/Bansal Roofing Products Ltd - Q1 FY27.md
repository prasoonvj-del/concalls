# Bansal Roofing Products Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company reported strong YoY growth in revenue and profitability, supported by ongoing capacity expansion and entry into the solar structure segment.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 45.89 crore (up 26.8% YoY from Rs 36.20 crore).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 4.12 crore (up 35% YoY from Rs 3.01 crore).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** ~9% (vs ~8% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 2.67 crore (up 31.9% YoY from Rs 2.02 crore).
*   **EPS:** Rs 2.02 (vs Rs 1.53 in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Business Model:** Integrated metal building solutions provider (PEB, roofing, cladding, purlins, decking, and solar structures).
*   **New Segment:** Entered Solar Module Mounting Structure (MMS) business in Q2 FY27; installed high-speed roll-forming machinery.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Company operates on a partial two-shift model (8-12 hours), expandable to 16 hours.
*   **Sustainability:** Added 100 kW rooftop solar capacity (total 300 kW), meeting 45% of electricity consumption.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Target:** Management targets Rs 180–200 crore for FY27.
*   **Solar Potential:** Management stated a capacity of 25,000 tonnes/month for solar structures, with a revenue potential of ~Rs 20 crore/month if fully utilized.
*   **Growth Projections:** Management characterized the current 25.32% growth projection as "very conservative" as it does not fully account for solar revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Order Book:** Currently holds ~2 months of revenue in orders.
*   **Order Size:** Average order size is Rs 3–5 crore; management noted capability to handle larger orders (up to 20% of turnover).
*   **Solar Opportunity:** Management views the solar sector as a significant opportunity, citing 5 GW of annual projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Solar Margins:** Management expects lower margins for solar MMS (Gross: 4–5%, Net: 2.5–3%) due to bulk supply nature, but expects higher absolute profit.
*   **Capex:** Invested Rs 5 crore in machinery during Q1 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Expansion Status:** Phase 5 and Phase 6 are under construction, expected to complete by mid-September 2026.
*   **Capacity Post-Expansion:**
    *   PEB: 15,000 tonnes/annum.
    *   Purlin: 3,600 tonnes/annum.
    *   Roofing Sheets: 15,000 tonnes/annum.
*   **PEB Throughput:** Current capacity is 1,200 tonnes/month, scalable to 2,000 tonnes/month with shift adjustments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Net debt is ~Rs 6 crore (introduced in Q1 FY27 for machinery capex).
*   **Working Capital:** Historically, receivable days have been <2 weeks. Management expects solar business to extend this to 30–45 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Margins:** Solar business will likely compress overall margins compared to the core PEB business.
*   **Working Capital:** Solar business requires longer credit cycles (30-45 days) compared to the company's historical <2-week cycle.
*   **Operational:** Mumbai office is currently closed due to staff attrition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Will solar business impact return metrics?** Management acknowledged ROCE may decrease slightly due to lower solar margins but expects overall results to remain strong.
*   **Q: Are you planning further debt?** Management stated no further debt is planned for the current premises; future expansion (e.g., sandwich panels) may use term loans, but not currently required.
*   **Q: Can you explain the PEB vs. component sales?** Management clarified they sell both the "car" (complete PEB) and "spare parts" (roofing, purlins, decking), providing an advantage over conventional PEB players.
*   **Q: Why are projections conservative?** Management noted that solar revenue was not fully factored into the initial 25.32% growth projection.

### One-line takeaway
Bansal Roofing is leveraging its integrated manufacturing base to enter the high-volume solar MMS market, balancing lower margins in the new segment with significant top-line expansion potential.
