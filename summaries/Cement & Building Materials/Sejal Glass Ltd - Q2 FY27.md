# Sejal Glass Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue growth, improved capacity utilization at Taloja and Erode, and margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated total income: Rs 139.86 crore (up 33.14% YoY from Rs 105.04 crore)
- H1 FY27 consolidated total income: Rs 259.29 crore (up 41.84% YoY from Rs 182.81 crore)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 25.66 crore (up 43.44% YoY from Rs 17.89 crore)
- Consolidated EBITDA margin: 18.35% (up 132 bps YoY from 17.03%)
- H1 FY27 consolidated EBITDA: Rs 43.68 crore (up 43.79% YoY from Rs 30.38 crore)
- H1 FY27 consolidated EBITDA margin: 16.85% (up 23 bps YoY from 16.62%)
- Consolidated Net Profit (PAT): Rs 14.42 crore (up 77.61% YoY from Rs 8.11 crore)
- Net Profit Margin: 10.31% (up 258 bps YoY from 7.73%)
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Net Profit: Rs 21.63 crore (up 72.6% YoY from Rs 12.53 crore)
- H1 FY27 Net Profit Margin: 8.34% (up 148 bps YoY from 6.86%)
- EPS: Rs 12.52 (vs Rs 7.95 YoY); H1 FY27 EPS: Rs 18.79 (vs Rs 12.28 YoY)
- UAE order book: AED 78 million as of September 2026 (approx. Rs 200 crore mentioned in Q&A)
- UAE order win (July 2026): AED 27.6 million (approx. Rs 71 crore)
- Domestic architectural glass order book: Approx. Rs 150 crore secured / Rs 85 crore active.
- Taloja facility utilization: Tempering at 69%, IG glass at 40%
- Erode facility utilization: Tempering at >24%, IG glass at >18%

### Highlights
- Company is evolving from traditional glass to an integrated architectural glass manufacturing company with four plant networks (Silvassa, Taloja, Erode, and Ras Al Khaimah, UAE) with an annual capacity of approximately 61.94 lakh square meters.
- Erode facility is generating positive EBITDA of around 10%, while Taloja is at break-even.
- UAE subsidiary secured five significant architectural glass orders worth AED 27.6 million in July 2026.
- Progressing in digital printing, bullet-resistant glass (testing in India completed), and approved vendor status for railway-grade glass at three railway coach factories.
- Fire-rated glass manufacturing line imported; trial production and approvals targeted for end of Q3 FY27, with commercial production in Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth in the range of 25% to 40%.
- Management targets sustaining EBITDA margin at around 18% and PAT margin at approximately 10% for the full year FY27.
- Specialty/value-added products expected to contribute 5% of revenue in FY27 and 15% to 20% in FY28.
- Peak revenue from existing infrastructure targeted around Rs 600 crore to Rs 650 crore by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand driven by real estate activity, high-rise residential and commercial projects, infrastructure development, and healthcare/hospitality sectors.
- Indian glass industry size is more than USD 3.9 billion, with processed/architectural glass contributing more than 60%.
- Government's 2047 Viksit Bharat targets provide long-term industry potential.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- COGS improved due to optimized raw material composition (testing and utilizing global vendors for silicon and PVB) and better purchase negotiations.
- UAE operations saw a 3% improvement in COGS and contribution from the facade division.
- Specialized fire-rated products expected to yield EBITDA margins above 30%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Remaining capex for UAE tempering third line and fire-rated facility is approximately AED 20 million, to be funded 60% via internal accruals and 40% via banking finance.
- Peak capacity utilization targets: near 100% for tempering, and 80%-85% for IG and laminated glass.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt consists mainly of long-term bank loans paid via regular EMIs; no immediate prepayment plans.
- Working capital is managed through internal resources/ploughing back profits, and 25% of UAE business utilizes LC terms discounted to improve cycle.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher shipment and logistic costs in the Middle East due to geopolitical tensions (passed on to customers).
- Working capital blockage due to advance purchase requirements for specific coated/reflective glass runs from manufacturers like Saint-Gobain.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gaurav Shukla (Finvestors) asked about Taloja and Erode utilization and margins.** Management stated Erode is at ~10% EBITDA margin, Taloja is at break-even, with Taloja tempering utilization at 69% and IG at 40%.
2. **Disha (Analyst) asked about peak capacity utilization.** Management responded that tempering should be near 100% and IG/laminated glass at 80% to 85%.
3. **Parag Shah (Analyst) asked about the capex funding for UAE expansion.** Management stated it will be funded 60% through internal accruals and 40% via banking finance.
4. **Parag Shah asked about railway tender win ratios.** Management stated the conversion rate/bid-win ratio is currently less than 5% as a new entrant.
5. **Rachana Kukreja (Analyst) asked about working capital and cash flows.** Management explained that advance raw material procurement for specified glass causes inventory blockage, managed through internal accruals and LC discounting.

### One-line takeaway
Sejal Glass reported robust Q2 FY27 performance with 33% revenue growth and an 18.35% EBITDA margin, supported by improving capacity utilization at its Taloja and Erode plants and a strong order book in both India and the UAE.
