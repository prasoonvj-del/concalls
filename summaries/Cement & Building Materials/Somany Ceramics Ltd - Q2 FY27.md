# Somany Ceramics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - driven by ~25% revenue growth, improved EBITDA margins, and a profitable turnaround in joint ventures.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: EBITDA margin improvement guidance raised from 1%-1.5% to 2%-2.5% over FY26 figures (old: 1%-1.5%; new: 2%-2.5%).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious regarding monsoon and gas volatility to more optimistic, citing robust domestic retail/project demand and steady pass-through of gas costs.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Mention of U.S. banning engineered marble and quartz, potentially benefiting Indian tile slab exports; 35% of Morbi export-oriented units currently shut down.
- Last quarter's promises: Plant upgradations and maintenance mentioned in Q1 were completed, leading to better operational performance in Q2 as anticipated.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 854 crore (~25% growth YoY)
- **Sales Volume Growth (Tiles):** 5.5% YoY
- **Tiles Segment Revenue:** Rs 723 crore (27% growth YoY)
- **Bathware Segment Revenue:** Rs 88 crore (15% growth YoY)
- **Building Solutions Revenue:** Rs 45 crore (28% growth YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.7%
- **JV Performance:** Profit of Rs 3 crore in Q2 FY27 and Rs 6 crore in H1 FY27 (vs loss of Rs 12 crore in Q2 FY26 and Rs 23 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Natural Gas Blended Prices:** ~Rs 70 per standard cubic meter (up from Rs 45 in FY26 and Rs 43 in Q2 FY26)
- **Brand/Marketing Spend:** Maintained at 2.3% to 2.5%
- **Dealer Additions:** 156 dealers added in H1; net showroom increase of ~35 in H1
- **GVT Mix:** 45% (up from 40% last year)
- **Siravit (South JV) Total Capex:** ~Rs 250 crore (funding 50% debt and 50% equity; ~Rs 60 crore from Somany side)
- **Modernization Capex:** Rs 15 crore for Sudha, Rs 8 crore for Vintage, plus Rs 70-80 crore routine standalone capex

### Highlights
- Operating plants ran at optimum/100% capacity utilization, excluding days lost to scheduled maintenance and line upgradations.
- Complete pass-through of price increases (16%-17% from April to current) achieved alongside a better product mix.
- Most plants have become fungible between LPG, propane, and natural gas, helping control fuel pricing.
- Building solutions and sanitaryware segments expanding; sanitaryware grew at 18%-20%.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue/Volume Guidance:** High single-digit volume growth for the full year FY27 and FY28.
- **Bathware Guidance:** +20% growth.
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Upward of 11.5% to 12% for next year, with a 2%-2.5% improvement over FY26 figures for the current year.
- **Capacity Expansion:** Adding ~11-12 million square meters in the South (Siravit plant) and ~4 million square meters via debottlenecking, expected to be operational by around September next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remains resilient across both retail and projects.
- Industry exports declined significantly (31.2% in the first five months; 44%-45% in volume), impacting Morbi where approximately 35% of units are shut.
- Freight rates have started reducing, offering slight relief to exports; U.S. ban on engineered marble/quartz may create export opportunities for large slab tiles from India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Natural gas prices volatile at ~Rs 70 per standard cubic meter due to geopolitical escalations.
- Price increases of 16%-17% have been fully passed on to consumers.
- Faucet margins faced pressure due to doubled brass prices over the past 1.5 years; sanitaryware margins remain slightly above tiles.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Increasing shareholding in sanitaryware JV from 51% to 76%.
- Siravit plant expansion in the South increased to 11-12 million square meters (~Rs 250 crore total capex, 50:50 debt-equity).
- Modernization capex of Rs 15 crore for Sudha and Rs 8 crore for Vintage, alongside Rs 70-80 crore routine standalone capex funded via internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivable days maintained at healthy levels (reported as best in industry).
- Expansion capex funded through a mix of internal accruals and 50% debt for the South JV.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gas price volatility and geopolitical tensions.
- Export downturn and plant shutdowns in Morbi affecting industry dynamics.
- Upward pressure on brass prices impacting faucet margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nilesh Sharma (Monomer Capital) on gas price pass-through:** Management stated price hikes of 16%-17% fully absorb current gas costs (~Rs 70/scm), and further hikes depend on major fuel spikes.
2. **Sagnik Sarkar on GVT mix and capex:** Management noted GVT is currently at 45% (targeting 65%-70% in 2-3 years) and detailed the Siravit and subsidiary modernization capex plans.
3. **Sneha on volume growth vs peers:** Management explained that plant maintenance/upgradations temporarily impacted Q2 volumes, and focused on protecting realizations rather than chasing cheap volume.
4. **Shruti Mulchandani on margin trajectory and Q3 outlook:** Management expects Q3 volumes to be better than H1, guided for FY28 high single-digit growth, and anticipates further margin expansion from JV turnarounds and operational efficiencies.
5. **Karan Bhatelia on bathware/faucet margins:** Management noted sanitaryware margins are robust and passed on, whereas faucet margins face headwinds due to unabsorbed brass price inflation.
6. **Bharat Kudikyala on capacity plans:** Management reiterated current sales volume targets of 79-80 million square meters and upcoming capacity additions of 10-11 million square meters by next mid-year.

### One-line takeaway
Somany Ceramics posted a strong Q2 FY27 with ~25% revenue growth, an EBITDA margin of 11.7%, and a profitable JV turnaround, while raising its full-year margin expansion guidance and progressing on major capacity expansions.
