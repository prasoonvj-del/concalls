# Somany Ceramics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - driven by strong top-line growth (~25%), margin expansion to 11.7%, full pass-through of gas costs, and JV turnaround.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised. EBITDA margin expansion target increased from 1%-1.5% over FY26 levels to 2%-2.5% over FY26 levels. Full-year volume growth guided at high single digits (vs mid-single digits last quarter).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained positive; domestic demand resilient in retail and projects despite monsoon disruptions. Morbi export environment deteriorated further (exports down ~31% YoY in first 5 months).
- **New topics or risks:** American ban on engineered marble and quartz highlighted as a potential export opportunity for Indian large tile slabs; ~35% of Morbi export-oriented plants are currently shut.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered JV turnaround (Rs 3 crore profit in Q2 FY27 vs Rs 12 crore loss in Q2 FY26); scaled up South JV expansion target from 9 million to 11-12 million sq meters; completed plant upgradations adding ~4 million sq meters annualized capacity.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 854 crore (up ~25% YoY).
- **Volume growth:** 5.5% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.7%.
- **Tiles Revenue:** Rs 723 crore (up 27% YoY).
- **Bathware Revenue:** Rs 88 crore (up 15% YoY).
- **Building Solutions Revenue:** ~Rs 45 crore (up 28% YoY).
- **JV Profitability:** Profit of Rs 3 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs loss of Rs 12 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 profit of Rs 6 crore (vs loss of Rs 23 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Gas Costs:** ~Rs 70-71 per standard cubic meter (SCM) vs Rs 43/SCM in Q2 FY26.
- **Marketing Spends:** Maintained at 2.3%-2.5% of revenue.
- **Dealer & Showroom Additions:** Net dealer additions of ~156 in H1 FY27; net showroom additions of ~35 in H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Total revenue grew ~25% YoY to Rs 854 crore, driven by robust domestic demand across retail and projects.
- Full pass-through of gas price inflation achieved via 16%-17% price hikes taken since April.
- Joint venture operations swung to a Rs 3 crore profit in Q2 FY27 compared to a Rs 12 crore loss in Q2 FY26.
- Shareholding in sanitaryware JV to be increased from 51% to 76%.
- Plant flexibility improved with most facilities now fungible between LPG, propane, and natural gas.
- *Record check:* Scope of the South JV expansion expanded from 9 million to 11-12 million sq meters.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Volume Growth:** High single digits for full-year FY27.
- **Bathware Growth:** Upward of 20% for FY27 and FY28.
- **EBITDA Margins:** 2.0%-2.5% improvement over FY26 levels, targeting 11.5%-12%+ for FY27 and further expansion in FY28.
- **Product Mix:** GVT share targeted to expand from 45% currently to 65%-70% over the next 2-3 years.
- **Building Solutions Segment:** Revenue share targeted to increase from 16%-17% to 20%-25% of total business over 2-3 years (growing at 40%-50%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic retail and project demand remain resilient.
- Industry exports declined 31.2% in value and 44%-45% in volume during the first five months of FY27; full-year exports projected at Rs 12,000-13,000 crore vs Rs 18,750 crore last year.
- ~35% of Morbi's export-oriented capacity is shut, likely rising to 10% more over Diwali due to high inventory and export slowdown.
- Freight rates began easing in recent weeks, driving a mild export recovery in November.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average blended gas cost currently stands at ~Rs 70-71/SCM.
- Faucet margins faced pressure as brass prices nearly doubled over 1.5 years; price hikes were only partially passed on due to competitive dynamics led by Jaquar.
- Sanitaryware price hikes fully passed through as gas impact is minimal.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **South JV Expansion (Siravit):** Capacity increased to 11-12 million sq meters with total capex of ~Rs 250 crore (Somany's equity share is ~Rs 60 crore; 50:50 debt-equity mix). Expected commissioning around September FY28.
- **Modernization Capex:** ~Rs 15 crore investment in Sudha plant and ~Rs 8 crore in Vintage plant.
- **Routine Capex:** ~Rs 70-80 crore annually for standalone operations.
- **Debottlenecking:** Added ~4 million sq meters of annualized capacity across existing plants.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained healthy working capital with industry-best receivable days.
- Siravit expansion funded via 50% debt and 50% equity; Sudha/Vintage upgrades and JV equity top-ups funded entirely via internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gas price volatility stemming from ongoing geopolitical conflicts.
- High brass prices impacting faucet segment profitability.
- Domestic market pressure from unabsorbed excess Morbi production, although organized players continue to hold prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nilesh Sharma (Monomer Capital) on gas cost pass-through & Morbi shut-downs:** Management clarified that 16%-17% price hikes fully covered gas cost increases. Confirmed ~35% of Morbi export units are shut, which creates domestic supply pressure but has not impacted Somany's pricing power.
- **Sagnik Sarkar on GVT share & capex updates:** Management stated GVT mix reached 45% (target 65%-70% in 2-3 years) and confirmed the South JV expansion capacity was upgraded to 11-12 million sq meters.
- **Sneha (Nuvama) on volume growth gap vs peers:** Management explained Q2 volume (5.5%) was impacted by 15-20 days of planned line upgrades/maintenances in July/August; volume will rebound in H2 while maintaining pricing realization.
- **Shruti Mulchandani (Ikigai Asset Management) on Q3 seasonality & margin sustainability:** Management confirmed Q3 absolute volume and performance would be better than H1/Q2, expecting margins to sustain at 11.5%-12%+ and expand further in FY28.
- **Karan Bhatelia on Bathware & Building Solutions:** Management noted brass cost inflation prevented full price pass-through in faucets, while sanitaryware costs were passed on. Building solutions continues to grow at 40%-50%.

### One-line takeaway
Somany Ceramics delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with revenue up 25% and margins improving to 11.7% on JV profitability and cost pass-through, while raising margin guidance and expanding its South capex scale.
