# Varmora Granito Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Cement & Building Materials*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust operating efficiency, product mix improvements, and margin expansion following a disruption-hit Q1.

### Key numbers
- IPO size: Rs 708 crore (Fresh issue: Rs 320 crore for debt repayment)
- H1 FY27 Revenue growth: 3% YoY (Domestic revenue grew 9%)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin growth: 17% YoY; Gross margin growth: 8% YoY
- H1 FY27 PAT improvement: 498% YoY
- Average realization improvement: Rs 75 YoY (Rs 50 due to price hikes, Rs 25 due to product mix)
- Capacity: 50.6 million square meters
- Utilization: Q1 FY27 at 63%, Q2 FY27 at 89%, H1 FY27 at 76%
- Volume breakup (H1 FY27): Total volume 15.04 MSM (Exports: 2.03 MSM; vs H1 FY26 Total: 17.63 MSM, Exports: 3.42 MSM)
- Product mix (H1 FY27): GVT 82%, PVT 9%, Ceramic 8% (vs H1 FY26: GVT 75%, PVT 14%, Ceramic 11%)
- Domestic sales split: 72% retail, 28% project
- Ad spend: 1.9% of revenue in H1 FY27
- Export revenue de-growth: 18% in H1 FY27
- Gas prices (Propane average): Q1 FY27 at Rs 84.73, Q2 FY27 at Rs 80.53; current price at Rs 92
- Maintenance capex: Rs 9 to 10 crore
- Simola divestment revenue impact: H1 FY26 at Rs 60 crore vs H1 FY27 at Rs 9 crore

### Highlights
- Listed on September 29, 2026.
- Fresh issue proceeds of Rs 320 crore from the IPO will make interest costs almost zero going forward.
- Divested Simola entity to stop bottom-line losses.
- Northeast plant has started commercial operations, reaching 50% utilization last quarter and expected to reach 75% in Q3.
- Changed depreciation method from WDV to SLM and revised useful life of machinery to 10 years, creating an approximate Rs 17 crore difference (Rs 14 crore at PAT level).
- Installed Asia's first Integrated Stone Technology (IST) machine last year, featuring continuous press for full-body natural marble replicas.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year volume growth of around 10% and value growth of around 15%, aiming to outperform the industry by 2% to 3%.
- Expects utilization to improve to 80% to 85% for the year, with peak utilization capability at 92% to 93%.
- Margins in Q3 and Q4 are expected to be in line with or better than Q2.
- Long-term revenue split target remains approximately 80% domestic and 20% exports.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic GVT volume grew 14% in H1.
- Exports are currently weak due to container availability and freight costs, leading to an 18% de-growth in export revenue; however, freight rates have started declining (e.g., US freight dropped from $7,000-$7,500 to $5,500).
- Morbi industry tile exports have decreased by about 40%, whereas Varmora's decline was limited to 18% due to a focus on branded business rather than white-label.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average realization improved by 21% (17% price hike, 3.5% product mix).
- Propane input costs increased by 14% to 15%, while cumulative price hikes taken are 16% to 17%, leaving a 3% leeway.
- Northeast plant benefits from CGST/SGST government incentives, interest subventions, and massive freight savings (local freight of Rs 2-3 vs Rs 120 from Morbi).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total current capacity is 50.6 million square meters. 
- No immediate large capex plans are in place as current utilization is 76%.
- Northeast plant adds 6.4 million capacity next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO debt repayment will make interest costs nearly zero.
- Outsourced mix stands at 14% (primarily lower-value PVT and ceramics).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues and Middle East crisis creating a challenging atmosphere for exports.
- Fluctuation in propane gas prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital):** Asked about the drivers of Q2 margin expansion. *Management explained it was driven by a 21% realization improvement (17% price hike, 3.5% product mix moving to larger sizes like 6x4 and 8x4 feet) and operating leverage from 89% utilization.*
2. **Bhavin Rupani:** Inquired about the rationale for shifting from WDV to the SLM depreciation method. *Management stated SLM aligns with organized Morbi peers and reflects the reality that technological shifts require new capex rather than fungible repairs over a 10-year useful life.*
3. **Anup Parikh:** Asked about calorific value differences in gas sourcing compared to peers like Kajaria. *Management highlighted that propane has an 11,000 calorific value, which must be factored in alongside raw price per SCM.*
4. **Chinmay Parab:** Questioned the plan to backfill revenue lost from the Simola divestment. *Management stated Simola was margin-dilutive, and growth will be driven by improved domestic utilization, the new Northeast plant capacity, and a focus on bottom-line profitability.*
5. **Karan B.:** Asked about the outlook for the bathware category. *Management noted bathware constitutes 10% of revenue and will likely remain at 10% to 12%, as their core strength and primary focus remain on tiles.*

### One-line takeaway
Varmora Granito reported a strong Q2 recovery with 89% capacity utilization and robust margin expansion driven by price hikes and premium product mix, supported by its recent IPO and debt-reduction roadmap.
