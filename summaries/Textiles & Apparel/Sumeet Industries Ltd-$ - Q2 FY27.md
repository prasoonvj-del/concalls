# Sumeet Industries Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew robustly by 35.23% YoY to Rs 364 crores, and management expects margin recovery driven by falling crude and raw material prices, despite margin compression in Q2 due to narrowed spreads and volume prioritization.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained >30% revenue growth and target of 5-6% EBITDA margin for FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to positive as the war ended, crude and raw material prices declined, and wedding season demand improved.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Receivables increased by 42% (from Rs 133 crores in March to Rs 189 crores in September) due to stopping cash discounts and higher raw material prices; old concerns on immediate debt pressure eased following rights issue and borrowing reduction.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Rights issue funds (Rs 194.9 crores net) were deployed (Rs 100 crores working capital, Rs 85.9 crores Nakoda asset integration, Rs 9 crores debt repayment); 40 TPD FDY capacity was added, taking capacity from 120 to 160 TPD.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 364 crores in Q2 FY27 (up 35.23% YoY from Rs 269 crores; up 33.71% QoQ from Q1 FY27); H1 revenue Rs 636 crores (up ~23% YoY from Rs 517 crores).
- EBITDA (including other income): Rs 9.22 crores in Q2 FY27 vs Rs 14 crores YoY.
- EBITDA margin: 2.53% in Q2 FY27 vs 5.39% YoY.
- Profit after tax and share of associates (PAT): Rs 4.40 crores in Q2 FY27 vs Rs 9.86 crores YoY; H1 PAT Rs 5.5 crores vs Rs 17.84 crores YoY.
- Finance cost: Fell to ~Rs 2.202 crores in Q2 FY27 (25% down QoQ from ~Rs 3 crores).
- Net worth: Doubled to Rs 417 crores from Rs 211 crores in March.
- Borrowings: Came down to Rs 137 crores from Rs 160 crores.
- Borrowing to net worth: Improved from 0.75x to 0.33x.
- Cash: Stood at Rs 160 crores vs Rs 38 crores.
- Operating cash flow: Positive Rs 10.80 crores for H1.
- Sales volume: Q1 FY27 at ~23,000 tons; Q2 FY27 at ~29,000 tons.
- Depreciation: ~Rs 5 crores for the quarter.
- Power cost: H1 FY27 power cost at Rs 39 crores.
- Nakoda asset integration cost: Rs 85.9 crores (from rights proceeds).
- Nakoda plant expected annual turnover: ~Rs 1200 crores; EBITDA: ~Rs 70 crores (5-6% margin); operational by Q2 FY28.
- Solar project: 12 megawatt capacity, cost approx Rs 40 crores, expected commissioning by December 2026; expected to cover 50-55% of total power consumption, saving power costs and increasing revenue by Rs 23-25 crores from FY27-28.

### Highlights
- Completed rights issue in Q1 FY27 with net proceeds of Rs 194.9 crores utilized for working capital (Rs 100 crores), Nakoda asset integration (Rs 85.9 crores), and debt repayment (Rs 9 crores).
- Plant running at optimum capacity utilization of 95-98%.
- Added 40 TPD of FDY capacity, increasing total FDY capacity from 120 to 160 TPD.
- Reallocated previous debt repayment funds for solar and expansion purposes without raising additional debt.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to achieve the Rs 1300 crore revenue target for FY27 based on current running capacity.
- Targeting 5% to 6% EBITDA margin on turnover for the current year, recovering in the next two quarters.
- Expect demand to improve with the wedding season and falling crude/raw material prices.
- Nakoda PET chips plant expected to be operationalized by Q2 FY28, adding ~Rs 1,200 crores of annual turnover and ~Rs 70 crores of EBITDA.
- Solar project to commission by December 2026, bringing first benefits in Q4 of the current year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand expected to improve with the wedding season and normalization of crude prices.
- Bottle-grade PET chips face industry shortage with approx 50% imported from China and other countries; virgin chips market size for FY26-27 is 18.10 million metric tons vs recycled chips market of 6 million metric tons.
- High-value application yarns (Dope-dyed, Bright, Cationic, BSY) have a niche, sensitive market with better margins.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin moderation to 2.53% caused by spread over material cost narrowing from 23% to 17.1%, volatility in crude and raw material prices, and time lag in passing on price hikes, alongside choosing to scale volumes.
- Raw material prices expected to decline due to crude price drops, aiding margin recovery.
- Discontinued extra cash discounts to agents/customers, increasing receivables but saving financial costs long-term.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FDY capacity increased by 40 TPD to 160 TPD.
- Nakoda subsidiary (Sumeet Specialty Chips) acquired to operate a 1.4 lakh ton per annum (400 tons/day) bottle-grade PET chips plant in Surat, operational by Q2 FY28.
- 12 MW solar project costing ~Rs 40 crores commissioning by December 2026, funded via existing bank term loans.
- Moving up to 50% of capital capacity into value-added yarn (Dope-dyed, Bright, Cationic, BSY), expected to add ~Rs 300 crores of annual revenue.
- Evaluating forward integration to convert POY to DTY.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables swelled by 42% from March 2026 (Rs 133 crores) to September 2026 (Rs 189 crores) due to stoppage of cash discounts and higher finished goods prices; management states receivables remain in line with industry standards.
- Net worth at Rs 417 crores; total borrowings down to Rs 137 crores; cash at Rs 160 crores.
- H1 operating cash flow positive at Rs 10.80 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in crude oil and raw material prices causing time lags in price pass-through.
- Low liquidity in the lower market impacting receivables realization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anand Gupta on sales volumes and receivables:** Asked for volume sold and cause of 42% increase in receivables (Rs 133 cr to Rs 189 cr). Management stated Q1 sales were 23,000 tons and Q2 sales were 29,000 tons; receivables rose because cash discounts given during tight liquidity were stopped, and finished goods prices were marked up due to raw material inflation.
2. **Anand Gupta on solar project funding:** Asked about reallocating funds for solar and Nakoda. Management clarified that the Rs 40 crore 12 MW solar plant is funded through existing pre-rights term loans originally approved for expansions, with no additional debt raised.
3. **Priya Mehta on margins and cash flows:** Inquired about FY27/FY28 revenue/EBITDA growth and working capital management. Management expressed confidence in recovering EBITDA margins to 5-6% over the next two quarters and managing working capital effectively as crude prices decline.
4. **Sanjay Ladha on Nakoda plant viability:** Challenged the expansion into PET chips due to industry overcapacity. Management clarified that Nakoda produces virgin *bottle-grade* PET chips (market size 18.10 million tonnes), which face a domestic shortage with ~50% imported, distinguishing it from recycled chips players like Ganesha Ecosphere.
5. **Anand Gupta on H2 depreciation and power cost:** Asked about H2 depreciation and H1 power cost. Management stated quarterly depreciation is around Rs 5 crores, and H1 FY27 power cost stood at Rs 39 crores.

### One-line takeaway
Sumeet Industries delivered robust 35% YoY revenue growth (Rs 364 crores) and scaled volumes despite compressed Q2 margins (2.53%), supported by a strengthened balance sheet post-rights issue and strong operational tailwinds from upcoming solar and Nakoda integrations.
