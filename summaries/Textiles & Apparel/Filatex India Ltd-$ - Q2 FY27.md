# Filatex India Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong sequential improvement in revenue and operating profit, but timelines for key projects slipped by a few weeks and sales volumes declined YoY.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall capex and long-term project targets, but noted timeline slippages of a few weeks for Ecosis and steam projects due to logistical disruptions and equipment delivery delays.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more cautious due to turbulence from sharp crude oil price increases and logistics disruptions keeping buyers hesitant, though structural tailwinds from FTAs and domestic PTA remain intact.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Skilled labor availability during the peak installation period emerged as a challenge; old short-term worries about MEG import disruptions were supplemented by positive updates on domestic PTA commissioning (GAIL, IOCL).
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Ecosis depolymerization trials started November 15 (vs late October/early November previously expected); brownfield expansion is 67% commissioned with the balance by November 23; steam project first phase charged for flushing by November 10 (vs September previously).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,304 crore (up 13.9% QoQ from Rs 1,145 crore; up 21.2% YoY from Rs 1,075 crore)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 91.03 crore (up 16.8% QoQ from Rs 77.92 crore; up 2.4% YoY from Rs 88.93 crore)
- **PAT:** Rs 53.48 crore (up 8.8% QoQ from Rs 49.1 crore; up 12.4% YoY from Rs 47.58 crore)
- **Sales Volume:** 92,998 tonnes (up 3.5% QoQ from 89,872 tonnes; down 8.3% YoY from 1,01,390 tonnes)
- **EBITDA per kg:** Rs 9.79 (vs Rs 8.67 in Q1 FY27; Rs 8.77 in Q2 FY26)
- **Total ECB / Term Loan Borrowings:** ~Rs 230 crore (including ~Rs 200 crore for machinery)
- **Ecosis Capacity:** 27,000 tonnes per annum (INR 300 crore capex)
- **Dahej Expansion Capacity:** 55,000 tonnes per annum across POY, FDY, DTY (INR 235 crore capex)
- **Renewable Energy Capex:** INR 30 crore (targeting 55% renewable share from 26%)

### Highlights
- Comprehensive capex program of ~INR 690 crore is in its final stages, though some timelines moved by a few weeks due to West Asian shipping disruptions, turbine delivery issues, and skilled labor availability.
- Dahej direct melt line and 7 POY extruder lines commissioned (67% total); balance FDY lines to be commissioned by November 23, 2026.
- Ecosis depolymerization and purification trials started November 15, 2026; polymerization expected by first week of December 2026, with first commercial product out by December 10–15, 2026. H&M approved Filatex as a global supplier.
- India-UK trade agreement (effective July 15, 2026) and India-New Zealand agreement (effective October 20, 2026) provide duty-free access for Indian textiles.
- Domestic PTA capacity additions: Indian Oil mechanically completed its Paradip complex, and GAIL commissioned its 1.25 million tonnes per annum Mangalore PTA plant on November 5, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Ecosis capacity utilization expected around 40% to 50% in Q4 FY27, reaching full production by mid-FY28.
- Management targets minimum INR 85 crore to INR 90 crore of EBITDA from Ecosis in FY28.
- Renewable power supply expected to commence by mid-January 2027.
- Steam distribution project supply to start November 20, 2026, with full capacity utilization expected by January end, contributing ~Rs 60–65 crore in EBITDA gains once fully operational.
- Planned future expansion for Ecosis: two plants of 1,50,000 tonnes each (one in India, one in Vietnam) to be commissioned by Q1 CY2029.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Sharp crude price increases and logistics disruptions created uneven demand with subdued buying followed by sudden spurts.
- Volume commitments of 7,000 to 8,000 tonnes secured for Ecosis from brands like Decathlon, A&E, IKEA, and H&M; individual brands have the capacity to take over the entire 27,000-tonne production.
- Recycled polymer pricing operates on its own base and is not directly tied to virgin polymer price fluctuations; recycled chips expected around INR 130 to INR 140 per kg with an EBITDA margin of INR 30 to INR 35 per kg.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Unit-level operating profitability improved with EBITDA per kg rising to Rs 9.79 from Rs 8.67 in Q1 FY27.
- Long-term foreign currency borrowings (ECB) kept unhedged, as management believes long-term hedging does not save money over 5-to-8 year spreads. Finance cost reported at Rs 3.09 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Comprehensive capex program of ~INR 690 crore nearing completion, comprising Dahej brownfield expansion (INR 235 crore), Ecosis recycling plant (INR 300 crore), renewable energy (INR 30 crore), and steam distribution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term ECB borrowings stand at ~Rs 230 crore for machinery.
- Working capital supported by 90-day LCs for imported raw materials.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in petrochemical feedstock prices, elevated energy costs, and geopolitical uncertainties holding back buyer commitments.
- Shipping disruptions in West Asia delaying imported equipment schedules.
- Availability of skilled labor during the peak installation period caused project delays.

### One-line takeaway
Filatex reported a resilient Q2 FY27 with higher unit profitability and advanced its major strategic capex projects toward commercialization despite short-term logistical and raw material volatility.
