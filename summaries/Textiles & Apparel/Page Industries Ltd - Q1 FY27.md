# Page Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong underlying volume growth and demand were offset by temporary logistics/manpower constraints and input cost inflation.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,420.4 crore (up 7.9% YoY).
*   **Sales Volume:** 61.9 million pieces (up 5.7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 289 crore (down 1.9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 20.3% (vs 22.4% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 192.8 crore (down 4% YoY).
*   **Inventory Days:** 66 days (vs 73 days at start of quarter).
*   **Net Working Capital:** 54 days (vs 56 days at start of quarter).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Constraints:** Reported revenue growth was moderated by temporary quarter-end logistics and manpower-related constraints, leading to undelivered billing (estimated at ~3 days of billing).
*   **New Products:** Launched "Groove 3" (street fashion line) across ~500 exclusive brand stores; saw encouraging response.
*   **Digital/Tech:** Implementing a Consumer Data Platform (CDP) to go live by end of August; Distribution Management System (DMS) rollout is underway, with full network coverage targeted by end of FY27 or Q1 FY28.
*   **Network:** 115,871 multi-brand outlets, 1,640 exclusive brand stores, and 930 large format stores.
*   **Speedo:** Offline multi-brand outlet network consolidated to ~700 stores; online contribution to Speedo business is >35%.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Management remains committed to double-digit volume growth for FY27.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained guidance range of 19% to 21%.
*   **Recovery:** Expects Q2 to see above-average volume growth to make up for Q1 shortfalls.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Consumer demand remains healthy across all channels (exclusive brand stores, e-commerce, retail network).
*   Secondary sales performance was better than primary sales due to undelivered inventory.
*   E-commerce strategy is shifting toward an "outright" model (especially for quick commerce) to improve last-mile delivery and consumer experience.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Input Costs:** Faced inflationary pressure in cotton, synthetic materials, and packaging.
*   **Pricing:** Took a 2.2% weighted average price increase in May (following a 2.5% hike in January). Management noted the May hike was partial and did not fully absorb input cost increases.
*   **Margin Outlook:** Expects stabilization in gross margins in Q2 as the May price hike flows through and raw material prices stabilize.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Scaling up manufacturing facilities in Odisha and K.R. Pet.
*   Actively augmenting in-house capacities and outsourcing garment supplies to meet demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Inventory levels are considered at an "optimum level"; management does not intend to reduce them further.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Logistics/Manpower:** Temporary disruptions in third-party logistics and labor availability impacted quarter-end deliveries.
*   **Input Cost Volatility:** Potential for further escalation in raw material prices if the Middle East situation worsens.
*   **Minimum Wage:** Ongoing monitoring of minimum wage developments in Karnataka; management noted that current garmenting-related wage hikes are already accounted for.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the miss on volume growth vs. Q4 expectations?** Management clarified it was due to a structural change in scheme design (to flatten quarterly performance) and temporary logistics/manpower disruptions at quarter-end.
*   **Q: Are you losing pricing control with the shift to "outright" e-commerce models?** Management stated they maintain strategic partnerships to ensure price governance and avoid diluting consumer pricing.
*   **Q: Will the May price hike fully offset input costs?** Management clarified it was a partial hike; if input costs remain at April/May levels, the current hike is insufficient, but they are hopeful for stabilization.
*   **Q: Any impact from Karnataka minimum wage hikes?** Management noted the garmenting-specific wage hike was actioned in April; other recent government announcements regarding minimum wages were either withheld or do not materially impact the garmenting workforce.

### One-line takeaway
Page Industries delivered a soft quarter due to temporary supply-side bottlenecks and input cost pressure, but management remains confident in achieving double-digit volume growth for the full year.
