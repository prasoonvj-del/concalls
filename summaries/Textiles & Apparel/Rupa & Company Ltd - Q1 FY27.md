# Rupa & Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth is healthy at 10.1% YoY, but management highlighted intense competitive pressure and difficulty in sustaining price hikes, leading to margin compression relative to previous guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 202.4 crore (up 10.1% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 15.7 crore (up 29.1% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 7.8% (improved 120 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 8.3 crore (up 50.2% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 4.1% (improved 110 bps YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 37.4% (vs 37.7% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Net Cash Surplus:** Rs 7 crore.

### Highlights
*   Growth was driven by healthy volume traction, particularly in the value segment.
*   Exports contributed 4% to revenue; modern trade and e-commerce contributed 5%.
*   Advertisement and marketing spend was 10.5% of revenue in Q1; management plans to rationalize this to 6-7% going forward to support EBITDA margins.
*   Company is implementing IT initiatives (SAP, AI) and a Dealer Management System (DMS) to improve inventory visibility and working capital management.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Expected to grow 10% to 12% in the coming quarter.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Expected to be in the range of 9% to 10% in the coming quarter.
*   Management expects athleisure to see double-digit growth from Q2 onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management expects growth momentum to broaden across segments beyond the value segment as the year progresses.
*   Thermal segment: Order book is sound; management expects better performance than last year, contingent on winter conditions.
*   Athleisure: Q1 saw a 5-7% YoY de-growth, but management anticipates double-digit annual growth.
*   Women's segment: Currently 8% of revenue; management is rebranding "Softline" and building a dedicated team to improve performance.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Yarn prices are on an upward trajectory.
*   Management took a 4-5% price hike in Q1 but struggled to realize it due to intense competition and extra trade schemes; they plan to implement new rates in August.
*   Management noted that old, lower-priced inventory in the industry is largely exhausted, which should support the implementation of new, higher pricing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   No specific capex figures provided.
*   Focus is on shifting from a wholesale-driven market to a secondary-sales-driven market.
*   Expanding presence in modern trade, LFS (Large Format Stores), and e-commerce, where competition is lower and margins are generally better.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Maintained a net cash surplus of Rs 7 crore as of June 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Several organized players are aggressively using discounts and extended sale periods to gain market share, suppressing industry margins.
*   **Pricing Power:** Difficulty in passing on cost inflation due to competitive behavior.
*   **Return Ratios:** Management acknowledged that current ROCE and ROE levels are "not desirable" but expects improvement through channel shifts and efficiency initiatives.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the shortfall in EBITDA margins vs. guidance?** A: Management attributed it to high marketing spend (10.5% of revenue) and plans to rationalize this to 6-7% to meet margin targets.
*   **Q: Why did the Q1 price hike not reflect in realizations?** A: Management explained that intense competition forced them to pass the hike back to the trade via extra schemes; they are hopeful of implementing new rates in August.
*   **Q: Is the current margin profile structural?** A: Management stated it is not desirable and they are shifting to secondary-sales-driven models and new channels (e-commerce/LFS) to improve return ratios.
*   **Q: What is the strategy for the women's segment?** A: Rebranding "Softline," changing the font, introducing new fabrics, and building a dedicated team for better retail execution.
*   **Q: Are you using ARS (Automatic Replenishment System)?** A: It is in the works, but requires a proper DMS (Dealer Management System) first, which is being rolled out state-by-state.

### One-line takeaway
Rupa & Company is navigating a highly competitive environment by focusing on channel diversification (e-commerce/LFS) and cost rationalization, while hoping that the exhaustion of old industry inventory will finally allow for successful price hikes in Q2.
