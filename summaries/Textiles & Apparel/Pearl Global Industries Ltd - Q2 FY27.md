# Pearl Global Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by record-high revenue, EBITDA, and PAT backed by strong US consumer demand, multi-location presence, and new FTAs.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 3,330 crore (up 31.1% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 348 crore (up 47.7% YoY, excluding ESOP expenses)
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 10.5% (up 120 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 225 crore (up 63.6% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 1,802 crore (up 37.2% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 184 crore (up 51.1% YoY, excluding ESOP expenses)
- **Q2 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 10.2% (up 90 bps YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 126 crore (up 74.7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Shipment Volume:** 43.6 million pieces (vs. 37.1 million pieces in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** INR 648 crore (up 22.1% YoY); PAT at INR 29 crore
- **Q2 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** INR 308 crore (up 16.8% YoY); PAT at INR 16 crore
- **Net Worth (as of Sept 30, 2026):** INR 1,634 crore (vs. INR 1,438 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** INR 534 crore (excluding ~INR 70 crore earmarked for LC payments); Liquid/Mutual Fund investments at ~INR 104 crore
- **Working Capital Days:** 56 days as of September 30, 2026 (receivable days increased from 30 to 50 days)
- **Interim Dividend:** INR 5 per equity share (100% of face value)

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever consolidated revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and PAT for H1 and Q2 periods.
- Value-added logistics/shipping services generated INR 98 crore in revenue with INR 96.5 crore in expenses (yielding ~INR 1.5 crore EBITDA) to assist customers during container shortages.
- Net tariff adjustment gain stood at ~INR 10 crore in Q2.
- Inaugurated a new manufacturing unit in Bangladesh adding 7 million pieces of annual capacity at optimum utilization; started first phase of 10 tonnes knitting capacity and a new washing laundry in September.
- New factory in Bihar is ramping up as planned.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of crossing the INR 6,000 crore+ top line milestone in FY27 itself.
- Long-term EBITDA margin trajectory targeted at 12%-14% till FY30.
- Effective tax rate projected around 15% on a long-term perspective.

### Demand & industry commentary
- US consumer spending remains strong despite cautious retail inventory planning ahead of a compressed holiday selling window.
- Gasoline prices remaining below $4 in the US support discretionary apparel spending.
- Strong growth witnessed in Canada and U.K. markets; European Union is inching up in volume; Japan and Australia remain steady.
- India-U.K. FTA (effective July 15, 2026) has triggered elevated interest and volume increases from U.K. customers. EU FTA discussions are driving similar pre-interest.
- Diversification by US retailers out of concentrated regions like Vietnam and China is benefiting multi-location players like Pearl Global.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Haryana operations faced a 35% wage increase and subsequent PF adjustments affecting the bottom line; Karnataka garment manufacturing was temporarily exempted from wage hikes.
- Bangladesh wage board committee tasked to implement wage revisions by early December (with arrears in January); management notes current wages and potential hikes will be partly offset by currency depreciation (Taka/USD) and productivity improvements.
- Shipping spot rates surged up to $12,500 per container during peak months (August–October).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capitalization and capex commitment planned from FY27 to FY30 is INR 900 crore - INR 950 crore, expected to be capitalized before or by FY29.
- Capital Work in Progress (CWIP) stands at INR 225 crore as of September 30, 2026 (pertaining mainly to Bangladesh, to be capitalized in Q3/Q4 FY27).
- INR 325 crore - INR 350 crore earmarked for capacity expansion across geographies (INR 100 crore likely to be capitalized in FY27; remainder in next two years for Vietnam and Indonesia).
- Backward integration capex in Bangladesh allocated at INR 350 crore - INR 375 crore (INR 200 crore finalized by the board; operations for processing to begin by early 2028).
- India capacity utilization was around 68%-69% in H1.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term loans increased by INR 61 crore (funding Bangladesh capex); short-term borrowings increased by INR 210 crore (working capital).
- Working capital days increased to 56 days due to heavy late-quarter shipments deferring factoring and use of internal accruals; management expects normalization in Q3/Q4, driving better free cash flow generation in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Mob disturbances on Dhaka streets in Bangladesh caused short-term operational halts (up to 2 days of loss in 3 factories), though attendance remained high and factories returned to full operation.
- Cautious retail inventory posture in the US due to macroeconomic forecasts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kishore Kumar (Unifi Capital) on US inventory restocking:** Management stated retailers maintain lean inventories due to cautious forecasts, but discretionary spending remains robust, favoring a mix of national and private label brands.
2. **Kishore Kumar on Bangladesh wage hike impact:** Management noted Bangladesh wages remain substantially lower than competitors ($100-$104 base), and while hikes are expected, efficiency and currency depreciation will mitigate margin impact.
3. **Harsh Mittal (Emkay Global) on value-added services:** Management clarified that the INR 98 crore top-line/INR 1.5 crore EBITDA service was a one-off measure for peak holiday season logistics support and is not factored into core forecasts.
4. **Bharat Gulati (Institutional Investor) on India capacity utilization:** Management explained utilization is at 68%-69% as Bihar operations ramp up hiring/training and UK/EU customers route initial ramp-ups through Bangladesh before shifting programs to India.
5. **Pulkit Singhal (Institutional Investor) on FY27 revenue vs second-half slowdown:** Management reaffirmed confidence in crossing INR 6,000 crore+ revenue for FY27, noting strong order books entering Q3 without any visible demand slowdown.

### One-line takeaway
Pearl Global delivered record H1 FY27 financial performance with 31% revenue growth and margin expansion, underpinned by robust US demand, multi-country manufacturing flexibility, and new FTA tailwinds.
