# Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - 4th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth and 5th consecutive quarter of margin expansion, with healthy retail momentum.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,266 crore (up 11% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 4,311 crore (up 11% YoY)
- Lifestyle Brands revenue: Rs 1,906 crore (up 9% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 371 crore (up 10% YoY, margin at 19.5%)
- Emerging Brands revenue: Rs 371 crore (up 27% YoY)
- Retail Like-to-Like (LTL) growth: 5.3% (Total retail revenue up 8% YoY); H1 LTL growth: 6.6%
- Wholesale growth: 18% YoY
- E-commerce growth: 12% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs 390 crore (up 15% YoY); Margin: 17.2% (up 60 bps YoY); H1 EBITDA: Rs 717 crore (up 15% YoY, margin 16.6%)
- Emerging Brands EBITDA margin: 6.8% (up 530 bps YoY)
- PAT: Rs 53 crore (up 126% YoY); H1 PAT: Rs 82 crore (up 73% YoY)
- Net debt: Rs 956 crore

### Highlights
- Added over 100 stores in Q2 (highest quarterly addition in over 2 years), taking total network to 3,434 stores across 800+ cities (5.1 million sq. ft.).
- Small-town network (stores in towns under 1.5 lakh population) posted 6th consecutive quarter of double-digit LTL growth across 500+ stores (contributing 5-6% of overall sales).
- Reebok crossed a milestone with its 250th store in India.
- Peter England's new line "VYBE" achieved 10% of sales in stores where it was introduced and acquired 50,000 new consumers in their early 20s.
- Other new product launches: Motorsport line in Louis Philippe, sport range in Van Heusen.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects double-digit steady-state revenue growth to continue.
- Reaffirmed target to bring net debt down to close to Rs 600 crore by the end of Q4.
- Capex guidance for the year maintained at around Rs 300 crore.
- Reebok aims to cross 300 stores by the end of the year.
- Festive season started on an encouraging note, giving confidence for a stronger H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic consumption environment remained broadly stable with steady demand despite global macroeconomic uncertainties.
- Festive calendar shift from September to October impacted September LTL (zero growth in September), which would have otherwise been 8%+.
- Casual lines, athleisure, and activewear drove Q2 growth due to fewer weddings; Q3 is expected to be more wedding-heavy, driving suits and blazers.
- Sales split between formal/wedding and casual is roughly half-and-half (49-51 / 51-49).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage and discount reductions drove margin expansion.
- The industry faced cost increases, prompting a 6% price hike taken by the company for the season to mitigate cost inflation.
- Advertising investments were stepped up.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Accelerated retail expansion: added over 100 stores in Q2; pace expected to be even better in Q3 and beyond.
- Capex for the year expected to reach around Rs 300 crore.
- Gross store additions in H1 were 180+ with ~90 closures (net addition of ~86 stores), driven by relocations to better/bigger sweet-spot locations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 956 crore, expected to reduce to close to Rs 600 crore by Q4.
- H2 is seasonally stronger on cash flows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Consumer acceptance of the 6% price hike needs to be monitored alongside discount management.
- Store closures (~90 in H1) occur due to relocations or locations becoming irrelevant.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shravan Vohra (Morgan Stanley)** asked if double-digit momentum continues given tough bases, and about inflation/pricing. Management stated steady-state double-digit growth is expected, a 6% price increase was taken for the season, and net debt target is ~Rs 600 crore by Q4.
2. **Abhijeet Kundu (Equirus)** asked about brand performance and innerwear profitability. Management noted consistent growth across brands, successful new launches (VYBE, Motorsport), and stated innerwear is close to breakeven with a profitable quarter targeted this year.
3. **Mohammad Harris** inquired about small-town store definitions and festive category drivers. Management defined small towns as population <1.5 lakh (led by Peter England), and noted suits/blazers drive wedding quarters while casual/athleisure drive others.
4. **Rajiv Bharati** questioned the pre-Ind-AS margins and Reebok leverage model. Management clarified they do not report quarterly pre-Ind-AS numbers to avoid confusion, and stated Reebok leverage stems from overhead control, throughput growth, and lower discounts.
5. **Parth Shah** asked about e-commerce growth moderation (12%) and casual vs. formal mix. Management explained e-com primary vs. secondary sales timing causes quarterly variation (secondary is healthy double-digit), and confirmed a ~50-50 mix between formal and casual.

### One-line takeaway
Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands delivered its 4th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth (11% YoY to Rs 2,266 crore) and 5th consecutive quarter of margin expansion, backed by robust retail expansion, new product traction, and strong festive demand expectations for H2.
