# Siyaram Silk Mills Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited stable demand, healthy revenue growth, and a positive outlook for the upcoming festive season, prompting an upward revision in full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 827 crores (up 15.1% YoY from Rs. 719 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs. 1,293 crores (up 15.6% YoY from Rs. 1,118 crores).
- **Revenue breakdown (Q2 FY27):** Fabrics 77%, Garments 16%, Yarn and others 7%.
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 131 crores (vs. Rs. 121 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs. 170 crores.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 75 crores (vs. Rs. 87 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (H1 FY27):** Rs. 86 crores.
- **Debt-Equity Ratio:** 0.58 as of September 30, 2026.
- **Retail Store Count:** 38 ZECODE stores and 23 DEVO stores (total 61 stores).
- **Retail Revenue (H1 FY27):** Approx. Rs. 65 crores combined for ZECODE and DEVO.
- **Capex (H1 FY27):** Approx. Rs. 51 crores (out of ~Rs. 100 crores annual guidance).
- **Cumulative Price Hike:** 4%-6% over the last 6 months.

### Highlights
- Declared a first interim dividend of Rs. 4 per equity share of Rs. 2 each for FY27.
- Retail expansion continues to be funded entirely through internal accruals.
- Larger format stores for ZECODE are delivering better outcomes and display capabilities.
- DEVO stores are currently clustered in North India (Delhi, UP, Punjab, Rajasthan), while ZECODE is focused in South India (Karnataka, Hyderabad, Chennai).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised revenue guidance upwards to **14%-15%** for the year (previously implied lower).
- Maintained store count guidance of **70 stores** across ZECODE and DEVO formats by the end of FY27.
- Reaffirmed expected retail revenue of approximately **Rs. 160 crores** by the end of the year.
- Maintained expectation of a maximum 150 bps EBITDA drag from the retail segment (ZECODE and DEVO) for the year.
- Maintained legacy EBITDA margin guidance of about 14% (excluding the retail impact).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand across the textile and apparel sector remained broadly stable during H1 FY27 despite uneven monsoon and raw material inflation.
- Tier-2 and Tier-3 markets show recovery in rural demand, benefiting the traditional fabric business.
- Fast fashion is growing at more than two times the speed of the regular apparel industry.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin and EBITDA pressures were primarily driven by drastic raw material inflation.
- Cumulative price hikes of 4%-6% were implemented gradually over the last 6 months to offset raw material pressures.
- Retail expansion drag on EBITDA is kept within the guided 150 bps drop.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex for H1 FY27 stood at Rs. 51 crores; full-year capex guidance remains at around Rs. 100 crores.
- Store economics per retail outlet: Rs. 1-1.5 crores of capex (furniture and security deposit) and Rs. 70-80 lakhs of inventory.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-equity ratio stood at 0.58 as of September 30, 2026.
- Strong cash flows continue to support internal accrual-led retail expansion and regular dividend payouts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Uncertain geopolitical scenario impacting raw material pricing volatility.
- Complexities and startup-like operational challenges inherent in scaling a new retail business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dev Gulwani (Care PMS) asked about volume vs. value growth in Fabrics and retail revenue contributions.** Management stated fabric volume grew ~2.5% and value grew ~11%, while combined H1 retail revenue was ~Rs. 65 crores. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Dixit Doshi (Advisors Pvt Ltd) asked about the timeline for preference shares listing.** Management stated it would take a minimum of around 6 months based on exchange feedback. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Santosh Shetty asked if ZECODE EBITDA-positive store count increased in Q2.** Management clarified they do not track store-by-store counts publicly, but noted positive directional progress and improved metrics for newer stores. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Lohit Saini asked how management differentiates ZECODE and DEVO from competitors and long-term profitability.** Management highlighted their textile lineage, backend expertise, and custom interpretation of fast fashion and ethnic wear as key moats. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Praveen Sharma asked why e-commerce/D2C channels are not enabled for DEVO and ZECODE.** Management explained that e-commerce is consciously kept on the back burner as retail operations are under 2 years old and focus remains on physical clusters to avoid diversion. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Siyaram Silk Mills reported stable 15.1% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 and upgraded its full-year revenue guidance to 14%-15%, driven by festive anticipation and steady retail expansion, despite gross margin compression from raw material inflation.
