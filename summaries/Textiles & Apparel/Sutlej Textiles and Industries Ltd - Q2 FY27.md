# Sutlej Textiles and Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed / Cautious - EBITDA margins remained flat QoQ due to exceptionally higher freight costs and raw material volatility, despite a return to profitability YoY.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (home textiles target of INR250 crores for the year and technical textiles commercialization by FY28 remain on track).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to acknowledge near-term headwinds from a 3x spike in freight costs (e.g., Turkey containers moving from USD2,000 to USD6,000, Latin America from USD2,500 to USD10,000) and raw material volatility.
- New topics or risks: Exceptionally higher freight costs; expansion of an asset-light model for finished fabrics (PV fabrics) in Africa and the Middle East using external weaving partners.
- Last quarter's promises: Home textiles continue on track for the INR250 crore annual target; technical textiles qualification remains on track for FY28 commercial contribution.

### Key numbers
- Standalone total income (revenue) for Q2 FY27: INR833 crores (vs INR642 crores in Q2 FY26).
- H1 FY27 total income: INR1,537 crores.
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: INR53.4 crores (vs INR17 crores in Q2 FY26).
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin: 6.4%.
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR101 crores with a margin of 6.5%.
- Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT): INR6.7 crores (vs net loss of INR18 crores in Q2 FY26).
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR9.5 crores (vs net loss of INR44.1 crores in H1 FY26).
- Debt-to-equity ratio: Approximately 1.1x.
- Total headcount: Approximately 15,500 employees.
- Home textiles targeted revenue for the year: INR250 crores (with a gross block of INR110 crores).

### Highlights
- All 3 business verticals continued to remain EBITDA positive.
- Home textiles volume (meterage) grew by almost 30% YoY.
- Total headcount is deliberately reducing, keeping absolute labor costs static or declining despite annual wage hikes.
- Home textiles order pipeline visibility is booked for almost 5 months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains cautiously optimistic, expecting structural initiatives to drive better traction in coming quarters.
- Home textiles business aims to achieve INR250 crores in revenue this year and grow further in coming quarters.
- Technical textiles commercialization and contributions are expected to begin from FY28 (4 products already approved and market-ready; equipment settling by end of Q4).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global environment remains characterized by geopolitical uncertainties, fluctuating raw material prices, and changing trade dynamics.
- Cotton prices have remained firm, while polyester and synthetic fibers continue to be influenced by global crude oil and petrochemical markets.
- Opportunities are emerging from global sourcing diversification and India's growing position as a preferred textile manufacturing destination.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin stood at 6.4%, impacted by exceptional freight cost increases and raw material volatility with a pass-through lag.
- Power costs have declined from 12%-12.5% last year to 9.9% and are expected to reduce further.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocation is directed toward spinning capacity modernization, value-added segments, and renewables.
- Modernizing the entire fiber dye unit in the yarn business (expected to reduce unit manpower from 10 employees to 2).
- Expanding yarn and weaving capacities in home textiles.
- Pursuing an asset-light model in newer export markets (Africa and Middle East) for finished fabrics (including PV fabrics) using external weaving partners.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital increased slightly due to higher material prices and inventory requirements, remaining within management's envisaged range.
- Debt-to-equity ratio of ~1.1x remains within internal thresholds.
- Capital investments are funded through a prudent mix of internal accruals and existing financing arrangements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and raw material price volatility.
- Exceptionally higher freight costs during the quarter.
- Older machine infrastructure in certain segments affecting productivity and yield compared to modern peers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Aggarwal (Leeway Investments) on flat EBITDA margins QoQ:** Management explained that a huge increase in freight costs and raw material volatility, combined with a pass-through lag, capped margin expansion.
- **Jay Unadkat (Navneet Education) on employee cost reduction potential:** Management noted peer employee costs range from 9% to 12.5%, and targeted a 1.5% to 2% delta reduction through phased modernization.
- **Rahul Singh (Individual Investor) on historical underperformance and equipment age:** Management acknowledged that older machine infrastructure and higher manpower costs impacted past competitiveness, but emphasized ongoing modernization and a shift toward downstream/value-added segments.
- **Deepak Karwa (Individual Investor) on home textiles order pipeline:** Management stated they are booked for almost 5 months.
- **Deepak Karwa on technical textiles progress:** Management confirmed 4 products are approved, equipment is on the way to be settled by Q4, and FY28 commercialization remains on track.

### One-line takeaway
Sutlej Textiles sustained its profitable turnaround in Q2 FY27 with a PAT of INR6.7 crores, driven by structural value-add and efficiency initiatives despite freight and raw material headwinds.
