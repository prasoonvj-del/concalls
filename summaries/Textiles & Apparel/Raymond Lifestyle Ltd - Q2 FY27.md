# Raymond Lifestyle Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted record quarterly revenue, improved working capital, and a shift to a net cash position despite macroeconomic headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 2,125 crore (up 14% YoY).
*   **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 2,075 crore (up 13% YoY).
*   **H1 Total Income:** Rs 3,685 crore (up 10% YoY).
*   **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 270 crore (up 4% YoY); Margin: 12.7% (down 120 bps YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 404 crore (up 6% YoY); Margin: 11%.
*   **Net Cash Surplus:** Rs 69 crore (vs. net debt of Rs 246 crore in Sept 2025).
*   **Net Working Capital:** 82 days (improved from 105 days in Sept 2025).

### Highlights
*   **Segment Reclassification:** Portfolio now split into five segments: Branded Textile, Branded Apparel, Garmenting, High Value Shirting, and Emerging Businesses.
*   **Branded Apparel:** Achieved record quarterly revenue of Rs 559 crore (up 20% YoY), driven by casual brands (ColorPlus and Parx).
*   **Garmenting:** Revenue Rs 339 crore (up 26% YoY); EBITDA surged 75% YoY to Rs 25 crore.
*   **Emerging Businesses:** Revenue touched Rs 100 crore; segment EBITDA at negative Rs 23 crore due to incubation phase.
*   **Retail Footprint:** 1,634 stores; net rationalization of 29 stores over the last 12 months to improve productivity.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Guidance:** Management reiterated targets of double-digit top-line growth, mid-double-digit EBITDA growth, and >Rs 300 crore net cash.
*   **H2 Outlook:** Management expects stronger performance in H2, citing the wedding season and delayed festive demand.
*   **PBT:** Management expects double-digit growth in PBT for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro Environment:** Management noted persistent geopolitical tensions, shipping disruptions, and elevated Brent crude (USD 105-110/bbl).
*   **Festive Season:** Demand is strong; management noted a 20-25 day delay in the festive season compared to last year.
*   **Marriage Season:** Management expects strong demand through mid-December and into Q4, which is critical for the suit/jacket portfolio.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** Q2 EBITDA margin moderated by 120 bps YoY due to peak freight rates, elevated wool/cotton prices, and increased marketing investments.
*   **Marketing:** Spent Rs 116 crore on brand building (7.2% of B2C sales vs 6.8% last year).
*   **Pricing:** Management stated that in B2B businesses, passing on raw material inflation is difficult; they can typically pass on 50-60% of cost increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** FY27 capex guidance is Rs 200 crore.
*   **Garmenting Expansion:** Hindupur plant is expanding from 3 lines to 10 lines; 18-21 months required for full capacity.
*   **Retail:** Aiming for a net addition of ~20 stores above last year's closing count by year-end.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Position:** Net cash surplus of Rs 69 crore, representing a net cash accretion of >Rs 300 crore YoY.
*   **Working Capital:** Absolute net working capital contracted to Rs 1,622 crore (from Rs 1,870 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical/Macro:** Potential impact of US election outcomes and unpredictable global trade policies.
*   **Raw Materials:** Volatility in wool and cotton prices.
*   **Demand:** Potential impact of inflation and repo rate hikes on discretionary consumption.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is the strong apparel growth due to channel stuffing?** Management clarified that growth is driven by secondary offtake (like-to-like stores growing at double digits) and better product/supply chain management, not just primary loading.
*   **Q: Why the discrepancy between balance sheet and cash flow working capital?** Management explained that the cash flow statement includes other current assets/liabilities (e.g., unutilized GST credits) and that the build-up is seasonal/normal for Q2.
*   **Q: What is the path to breakeven for Emerging Businesses?** Management expects Home to be breakeven this year; Ethnix will take 18-24 months to reach profitability.
*   **Q: What is the status of the EU market opportunity?** Management noted that while the FTA is not yet implemented, inquiries are up, and they expect shipments to improve post-December.
*   **Q: Why did EBITDA margin drop in High Value Cotton Shirting?** Management attributed this to elevated raw material prices and the B2B nature of the business, where price pass-through lags.

### One-line takeaway
Raymond Lifestyle delivered a resilient Q2 with record revenue and improved cash flows, maintaining its FY27 guidance despite input cost pressures and a delayed festive season.
