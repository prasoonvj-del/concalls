# GHCL Textiles Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 31% YoY revenue growth and 20.6% EBITDA margin, despite a one-time fire-related inventory loss.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained long-term goal of Rs 2,500 crore revenue at 15%–18% EBITDA margin by FY30.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "steady" to "cautiously optimistic" due to raw material price volatility, though demand remains robust.
- **New topics or risks:** Reported a one-time exceptional loss of Rs 27 crore due to a fire at a rented warehouse in Tuticorin in August 2026.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Knitting expansion (40 machines) remains on track for Q3 completion; 11 MW solar project remains on track for December 2026.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 446 crore (+31% YoY, +9% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 92 crore (+141% YoY, +31% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.6% (vs 11.2% YoY)
- **Reported PAT:** Rs 36 crore (+124% YoY)
- **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 56 crore (+249% YoY, +42% QoQ)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 856 crore (+40% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 162 crore (+129% YoY)
- **H1 Adjusted PAT:** Rs 95 crore (+223% YoY)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 48 crore (Net debt-to-equity 0.03x)
- **Fabric share in revenue:** 16% (vs 11% in Q2 FY26)

### Highlights
- Meenakshi unit reached full capacity; overall capacity utilization at ~99%.
- Exceptional loss of Rs 27 crore (inventory fire in Tuticorin); no impact on manufacturing operations; insurance claim in progress.
- Repaid Rs 74 crore of debt during H1.
- Received 56 acres at PM MITRA Park for processing.
- Board approved Rs 410 crore investment for 58,368 spindles and 5 MW rooftop solar.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains long-term goal of Rs 2,500 crore revenue at 15%–18% EBITDA margin by FY30.
- Management acknowledged a participant's estimate of ~Rs 1,800 crore revenue for FY28 as "very likely."
- Margins: Q3 expected to be between Q1 and Q2 levels; Q4 expected to improve.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains robust across export and domestic markets.
- FTAs (UK, New Zealand) are in force; benefits expected in a year. India-EU agreement expected early 2027.
- Cotton prices rose from Rs 64,000/candy to Rs 69,000/candy, currently hovering around Rs 67,000/candy.
- Bangladesh LDC status extension viewed as neutral for the industry.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cotton spreads: Q1 (160), Q2 (173). Q3 spreads expected to be lower than Q2.
- Green energy: 11 MW ground solar project commissioning by end of Q3 (total 76 MW).
- Margins supported by product mix (fabric share), green energy, and strategic customer focus.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Knitting expansion: 40 machines to be completed by Q3 end.
- PM MITRA Park: Processing unit project expected by FY30.
- New project: 58,368 spindles + 5 MW solar (Rs 410 crore) to be completed by end of FY28; full benefit in FY29.
- Total investment under TN MOU: Rs 675 crore invested out of Rs 1,035 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt: Rs 48 crore.
- Net debt-to-equity: 0.03x.
- Capital advances increased due to cotton procurement.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material price volatility (cotton) may pressure near-term margins.
- Fire incident at rented warehouse (Tuticorin) caused Rs 27 crore inventory loss.
- PM MITRA Park project timeline pushed to FY30 due to a consultative approach with SIPCOT regarding infrastructure specifications.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ritesh Ram Gandhi (Discover Capital):** Asked about capex timeline. Management confirmed 58k spindles by FY29, knitting by Q4, and solar by Q3.
2. **Saransh Gupta (SVAN Investments):** Asked about fabric contribution. Management targets 16.5%–17% by year-end.
3. **Resham Jain (VVD Asset Managers):** Asked about PM MITRA delay. Management explained it is due to a consultative approach with SIPCOT on infrastructure specs.
4. **Resham Jain (VVD Asset Managers):** Asked about garmenting. Management is looking at it favorably as a logical next step.
5. **Shreyas Pimple (SMIFS Limited):** Asked about fire incident. Management confirmed inventory was insured and recovery is expected by Q3 end.

### One-line takeaway
GHCL Textiles delivered strong H1 growth with 20.6% EBITDA margins and a robust balance sheet, while maintaining long-term expansion plans despite a one-time fire-related inventory loss.
