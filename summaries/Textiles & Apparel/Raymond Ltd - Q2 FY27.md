# Raymond Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Resilient performance driven by strong execution across precision engineering and aerospace platforms, robust revenue and EBITDA growth.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income (Q2 FY27):** INR 683 crores (up 21% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 103 crores (EBITDA margin of 15.1%)
- **Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27):** INR 1,311 crores (up 17% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 203 crores (EBITDA margin of 15.5%)
- **Aerospace Revenue (JKMGAL) (Q2 FY27):** INR 139 crores (up 72% YoY)
- **Aerospace EBITDA (JKMGAL) (Q2 FY27):** INR 29 crores (EBITDA margin of 21%)
- **Aerospace Revenue (JKMGAL) (H1 FY27):** INR 262 crores (up 56% YoY, EBITDA margin of 21.1%)
- **Precision Technology & Auto Revenue (JKMPTL) (Q2 FY27):** INR 471 crores (up 15% YoY)
- **Precision Technology & Auto EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 61 crores (EBITDA margin of 13%)
- **Precision Technology & Auto Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR 915 crores (up 13% YoY)
- **Precision Technology & Auto EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 122 crores (up 24% YoY, EBITDA margin of 13.4%, up 110 bps YoY)
- **Precision Technology Segment Breakdown (Q2 FY27):** B2B (OEM business) approx. INR 350 crores (up 17%-18% YoY); B2C (Tools & Hardware + Automotive Aftermarket) approx. INR 120 crores (up early teens YoY)
- **Aerospace Peak Revenue Potential (Current Bangalore Facility):** Upgraded to INR 700-750 crores
- **Andhra Pradesh Greenfield Facility Investment:** Projected around INR 400 crores by commissioning (out of a total 5-year commitment of INR 1,000 crores across aero and auto)

### Highlights
- Operationalized the automotive aftermarket business during Q2 FY27, entering with nearly 800 part numbers.
- Aerospace business witnessed commercial ramp-up of newly commissioned capacities, expanding shipset values with integrated subassemblies.
- Set up an interim brownfield facility in Andhra Pradesh for automotive capacity augmentation and training for both aerospace and precision technology segments.
- Name changed from Raymond Limited to reflect the core engineering, aerospace, and auto component business post-demerger.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects aerospace revenue growth in the "high mid-20s" percentage range rather than 40%+.
- Long-term ROCE target remains between 20% and 25%.
- Andhra Pradesh greenfield facility construction remains strictly on schedule to achieve output by Q3 FY28 (December 2027).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand supported by strong manufacturing activity, healthy GST collections, and pre-festive channel restocking in passenger vehicles and premium 2-wheelers.
- Global Tier 1 OEMs/suppliers continue to derisk cross-border supply chains and consolidate vendors, benefiting India.
- Commercial aerospace production accelerating; major airframers ramping up monthly build rates.
- LEAP engine market share spans 35% to 65% across various parts, with total volume scaling.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity, raw material, and tool costs remain firm at elevated levels alongside freight and logistics volatility due to geopolitical tensions.
- Raw material contracts generally feature a pass-through mechanism (subject to implementation lag).
- R&D expenses write-offs impact near-term margins by approximately 4% every quarter as the company develops roughly one new part every day.
- Strong operating leverage and cost optimization initiatives compensated for cost headwinds, expanding margins YoY.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bangalore aerospace capacity debottlenecking increased revenue potential to INR 700-750 crores prior to the Andhra facility launch.
- Andhra Pradesh greenfield facility remains on track for Q3 FY28 production; a brownfield facility 2-3 km away acts as an interim capacity and training hub.
- Nozzle guide range capacity doubling from 8,000 to 16,000 units per year, with machines arriving and revenue reflecting from the upcoming quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Precision technology and auto components business remains net debt-free.
- Liquidity from share warrants is held at the holdco level as a war chest for inorganic growth opportunities.
- Safety stocks maintained to hedge against global shipping disruptions, placing mild pressure on working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Persistent freight and logistics volatility driven by global geopolitical tensions.
- Time lag in raw material cost pass-throughs and customer price renegotiations.
- Requirement for safety stock leading to working capital pressure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dheeraj (InCred Equity) asked about aerospace revenue potential and segment bifurcations:** Management clarified that Bangalore capacity is upgraded to INR 700-750 crores, and provided B2B (INR 350 crores) and B2C (INR 120 crores) breakdowns for precision tech.
- **Sahil Karia (White Pine Investment) asked about aftermarket targets and LEAP engine content:** Management stated the aftermarket was launched in August with 800 part numbers yielding better margins, and confirmed 35%-65% market shares across LEAP components.
- **Balasubramanian (Arihant Capital) asked about defense orders and growth sustainability:** Management noted components/subassemblies orders are secured for defense, and guided for high mid-20s growth going forward rather than 40%.
- **Keval Shah (Sanctum Wealth PMS) asked about Andhra facility integration and medical/gas turbine adjacencies:** Management explained that moving existing parts to the new plant will compress customer approval times (3-6 months), and confirmed medical/gas turbines are in the exploratory adjacency funnel.
- **Rahul Rao (Athletico Analytic Services LLP) asked about sourcing from Indian raw material suppliers (Inconel/titanium):** Management stated that Indian mills are getting qualified over a 2-3 year process, which will eventually reduce working capital and boost competitiveness.

### One-line takeaway
Raymond delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 21% YoY revenue growth and 15.1% EBITDA margin, underpinned by strong aerospace momentum, expanding precision engineering capabilities, and disciplined execution of its upcoming Andhra Pradesh greenfield facility.
