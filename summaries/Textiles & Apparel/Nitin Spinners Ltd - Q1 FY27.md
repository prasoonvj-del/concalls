# Nitin Spinners Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly revenue for the second consecutive quarter, driven by improved yarn realizations, better demand, and strong EBITDA/PAT growth.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR875 crores (up 10.3% YoY, 1.8% QoQ)
- **EBITDA (before other income):** INR155.6 crores (up 39.85% YoY, 19.3% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17.78% (vs 14.02% in Q1 FY26; up 376 bps YoY, 261 bps QoQ)
- **PAT:** INR75.3 crores (up 83.63% YoY, 31.2% QoQ)
- **EPS / Cash EPS:** INR13.39 / INR20.08 per share
- **Capacity Utilisation:** Spinning at 98%, Woven fabric over 92%, Knitted fabric at 55% to 60%
- **Geographical Revenue Split:** Exports ~65%, Domestic ~35%
- **Spreads (Yarn):** INR130 currently (vs ~INR110 in the last quarter of FY26)
- **Cotton Price Increase:** 8% to 10% during Q1
- **Cotlook Index vs Indian Cotton Delta:** Indian cotton prices changed from being 5% to 7% higher than Cotlook Index in '23-'24 to 1% to 2% below Cotlook Index currently.

### Highlights
- Reported highest-ever quarterly revenue for the second consecutive quarter at INR875 crores.
- Improvement driven by higher yarn realizations, better demand environment, and optimum capacity utilization.
- Fabric business witnessed stable pricing compared to the previous quarter.
- Renewable power expansion is on schedule to be operational by the end of Q3 FY27, expected to reduce power costs further.
- Company is not considering a buyback as it is in a growth phase deploying capital for expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management anticipates closing FY27 on a stronger note than FY26, supported by partial contributions from new capacities in H2 FY27.
- New capacities to add INR200 crores to INR300 crores in revenues (fabric revenue expected to scale up to INR1,200 crores in FY28).
- Margins guided to remain in the 16% to 20% range.
- Renewable power expected to save ~INR50 crores annually at the EBITDA level once fully operational (impact starting Q4).
- Blended power cost expected to be around INR5.50 per unit once complete capacity is running.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand supported by removal of additional U.S. tariffs and increased export demand from China (Chinese cotton yarn imports from India increased to 110-115 million kgs in calendar year '26).
- Price parity between international and domestic cotton improved after Indian cotton was outpriced for 1.5 to 2 years.
- India-U.K. FTA and upcoming EU-FTA expected to support Indian textile exports.
- Industry consolidation occurred over the last 1–2 years with weaker players and unviable capacities going out of business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins improved from 34%-35% to 40%+ levels due to higher yarn realizations.
- Power cost savings contribute ~0.5% to EBITDA; permanent cost savings achieved via energy efficiency and automation.
- Government of India removed import duty on cotton due to higher domestic and international prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ongoing expansion: adding 35 million meters of fabric capacity and 74,000 spindles (adding ~22,000 metric tons of yarn capacity, of which 16,000 to 18,000 metric tons will go towards internal fabric production).
- Weaving capacities to start in a couple of months; processing capacities around Diwali; spinning capacity by December.
- No new capex planned beyond announced projects until after FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is deploying internal accruals and taking loans for growth capex.
- Working capital cycle has remained similar compared to previous quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Logistics challenges (non-availability of vessels) caused slight inventory build-up at the end of Q1, dispatched later in July.
- Uncertainties still prevailing in the U.S. market have kept knit fabric utilization lower (55% to 60%).
- Rains in the next four weeks will decide the exact size of the upcoming cotton crop.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked about spreads and the delta between Indian and international cotton.** Management stated spreads moved from ~110 to ~130, and Indian cotton moved from a 5-7% premium over the Cotlook Index in '23-'24 to 1-2% discount currently.
2. **Ankit Gupta asked if low-cost inventory exhaustion would impact Q3 margins.** Management stated they do not rely solely on inventory and have successfully passed on raw material price hikes.
3. **Narayan Danak asked about the volume de-growth in yarn. Logical flow?** Management clarified it was due to higher in-house consumption by the fabric division and logistics-related dispatch delays, not lower production.
4. **Abhishek Shah asked about fabric price pass-through and capacity revenue split.** Management stated fabric raw material increases are passed on, but margin expansion is harder in fabric; of the INR1,000 crore incremental revenue, fabric will account for INR600+ crores.
5. **Reena asked about knitted fabric utilization and long-term margins.** Management stated knit utilization is at 55%-60% due to U.S. uncertainties, and reiterated the long-term margin guidance band of 16% to 20%.

### One-line takeaway
Nitin Spinners delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue and strong margin expansion in Q1 FY27, backed by favorable cotton price parity, improved yarn realizations, and ongoing efficiency initiatives.
