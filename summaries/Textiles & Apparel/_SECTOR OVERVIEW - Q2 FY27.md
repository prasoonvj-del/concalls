# Textiles & Apparel: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is navigating a complex environment characterized by strong operational performance and volume growth, offset by significant cost headwinds. Managements are generally optimistic, citing structural tailwinds like the shift toward man-made fibers (MMF), export opportunities (FTAs), and a move away from Chinese commodity dominance. However, profitability is being squeezed by volatile raw material prices (crude-linked inputs and cotton) and geopolitical disruptions in West Asia, which have spiked freight and insurance costs. Companies are increasingly focusing on value-added products (VAPs), operational efficiency, and captive renewable energy to protect margins.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is broad agreement that demand is healthy. Domestic consumption is recovering, and export markets (particularly Europe and the US) are viewed as key growth engines, with upcoming FTAs (UK/EU) providing a structural boost.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Export vs. Domestic:** While **Sportking** and **Vardhman** report robust export demand (notably from China and Bangladesh), **Century Enka** and **RSWM** noted that fabric/apparel exports faced temporary headwinds due to tariff uncertainties and order booking lags.
    *   **Segment Performance:** **Banswara Syntex** and **Sanathan Textiles** are seeing strong traction in MMF and value-added products. Conversely, **Vardhman** noted that while the yarn business is thriving, the fabric business is struggling with pricing pressure. **Lohia Corp** highlighted "heavy traction" in domestic capital goods, contrasting with the more cautious tone on apparel retail from others.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Raw Material Volatility:** Almost all companies flagged rising input costs. **Banswara Syntex**, **Century Enka**, and **RSWM** cited 25-30% increases in polyester/PTA/MEG prices due to West Asian geopolitical tensions. **Vardhman** and **RSWM** highlighted a significant surge in cotton prices.
*   **Pass-through Challenges:** There is a clear divide in pricing power. **Indo Rama** and **Sportking** successfully passed on costs. However, fabric manufacturers like **RSWM** and **Vardhman** faced margin compression because they operate with 30-40 day order booking lags, preventing immediate price adjustments.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** A major theme is the aggressive pivot to captive renewable energy to combat high power costs. **Sangam India** (aiming for 70% renewable by next year), **Sportking**, and **Vardhman** are all investing heavily in green power to structurally lower their cost base.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Expansion Mode:** The sector is in a significant capex cycle.
    *   **Specialty/Value-Added:** **Indo Rama** (Rs 200 cr for specialty filament), **Garware Hi-Tech** (TPU line), and **Banswara Syntex** (Surat facility) are investing to move up the value chain.
    *   **Capacity Additions:** **Sportking** is nearing completion of its 150,000-spindle Odisha plant. **Sanathan Textiles** is scaling its Punjab facility. **Lohia Corp** is investing Rs 75 cr in recycling and conversion machinery.
*   **Delays:** **Vardhman Textiles** noted an 8-9 month delay in the PM MITRA Park land handover, a notable shift from previous timelines.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Upgrades:** **Sanathan Textiles** raised its FY27 EBITDA target (to >Rs 560 cr). **Siyaram Silk Mills** upgraded its revenue guidance to 14-15%.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Lohia Corp** shifted from "strong" to "heavy traction" regarding domestic demand. **Vardhman** noted a shift in the fabric order pipeline following US election results.
*   **Operational Milestones:** **Sanathan Textiles'** Punjab facility turned PAT positive. **Sangam India** achieved its highest-ever quarterly revenue.

## Company one-liners
*   **Banswara Syntex:** Pivoting to value-added MMF and export garments to drive double-digit growth.
*   **Century Enka:** Auto momentum is strong, but profitability is normalizing due to raw material and energy costs.
*   **Garware Hi-Tech:** Record performance driven by premium films; margin expansion continues despite cost headwinds.
*   **Indo Rama:** Operational efficiency and reduced Chinese competition driving significant PAT growth.
*   **Lohia Corp:** Record order book and expansion into defense/recycling machinery.
*   **RSWM:** Transformation strategy yielding top-line growth, though fabric margins remain sensitive to cost spikes.
*   **Sanathan Textiles:** Punjab facility success and volume growth led to upgraded full-year targets.
*   **Sangam India:** Record-breaking quarter supported by high utilization and renewable energy savings.
*   **Siyaram Silk Mills:** Steady growth and festive anticipation led to an upward revision in revenue guidance.
*   **Sportking:** High margins and imminent Odisha capacity commissioning position it for continued growth.
*   **Vardhman Textiles:** Yarn business is thriving, but fabric segment faces temporary tariff and input cost challenges.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Raw Material Pass-through:** Will fabric manufacturers (RSWM, Vardhman) successfully recover margins as order books reset?
*   **Commissioning Timelines:** Watch for the operationalization of **Sportking's** Odisha plant and **Banswara Syntex's** Surat facility.
*   **Geopolitical Impact:** Continued monitoring of West Asian tensions and their impact on freight costs and crude-linked raw materials.
*   **Renewable Energy Benefits:** Tracking the extent to which captive power projects (Sangam, Vardhman, Sportking) actually lower unit costs.
