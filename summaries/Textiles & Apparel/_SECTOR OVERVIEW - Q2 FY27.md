# Textiles & Apparel: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is navigating a complex environment characterized by resilient domestic consumption and strong export demand, balanced against persistent raw material inflation and geopolitical headwinds. While most companies reported top-line growth, profitability remains a mixed bag; those with strong brand positioning or integrated value-added chains (like Sportking and Aditya Birla Lifestyle) are seeing margin expansion, while others (like Monte Carlo and RSWM) are grappling with cost pressures. A recurring theme is the strategic pivot toward value-added products and operational efficiency to mitigate the inability to fully pass on input cost spikes.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements generally agree that domestic demand remains stable, with a positive outlook for the second half of the year driven by the wedding and festive seasons.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Festive Timing:** Several companies (Aditya Birla Lifestyle, Monte Carlo, Raymond Lifestyle) noted a 20–25 day shift in the festive calendar, which impacted Q2 performance but is expected to bolster Q3.
    *   **Export Markets:** Sportking reports robust demand from China and Bangladesh, while RSWM notes a slowdown in apparel exports despite growth in man-made textiles.
    *   **Segment Performance:** Siyaram Silk Mills highlights that fast fashion is growing at double the rate of the regular apparel industry.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Inflationary Pressures:** Almost all players cited raw material inflation (polyester, PTA, MEG, and cotton) as a primary headwind. RSWM noted polyester prices surged from Rs 96 to Rs 146 in six months.
*   **Pricing Power:** There is a clear divergence in the ability to pass on costs. Aditya Birla Lifestyle successfully implemented a 6% price hike, and Siyaram implemented 4–6% hikes. Conversely, RSWM and Raymond Lifestyle noted that B2B/fabric businesses face significant lags (30–40 days) in passing on costs, leading to compressed margins.
*   **Operational Levers:** Companies are relying on internal efficiencies to protect margins. Sportking’s EBITDA margin jumped to 19.8% (vs 10.4% YoY) due to operational initiatives, while Aditya Birla Lifestyle benefited from reduced discounting.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is in a significant expansion phase, with a focus on both retail footprint and manufacturing capacity:
*   **Retail Expansion:** Aditya Birla Lifestyle added 100+ stores in Q2. Siyaram is scaling its ZECODE and DEVO formats. Monte Carlo is targeting 40–45 new EBOs.
*   **Manufacturing/Greenfield:** Sportking is nearing completion of its 150,000-spindle Odisha project. Banswara Syntex is preparing to operationalize its Surat facility by Q1 FY28. Raymond Lifestyle is expanding its Hindupur garmenting plant (3 to 10 lines).

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Revisions:** Siyaram Silk Mills upgraded its full-year revenue guidance to 14–15%.
*   **Tone Shifts:** Raymond Lifestyle highlighted a shift to a net cash position (Rs 69 crore surplus vs. net debt previously).
*   **Operational Milestones:** Sportking commissioned a solar project to reduce power costs by ~15%. Banswara Syntex confirmed the timeline for Surat de-notification (Jan/Feb).

## Company one-liners
*   **Aditya Birla Lifestyle:** Delivered 11% revenue growth and margin expansion, driven by aggressive retail expansion and new product traction.
*   **Banswara Syntex:** Improved profitability by pivoting to value-added MMF products and scaling export garments.
*   **Monte Carlo:** Reported H1 losses due to raw material inflation and inventory discounts, but maintains double-digit growth guidance for FY27.
*   **RSWM:** Achieved 47% YoY EBITDA growth through efficiency, despite fabric division margin compression.
*   **Raymond Lifestyle:** Achieved record revenue and a net cash position, navigating input cost pressures with strong brand performance.
*   **Raymond (Engineering):** Delivered 21% revenue growth, underpinned by aerospace momentum and precision engineering expansion.
*   **Siyaram Silk Mills:** Upgraded FY27 revenue guidance to 14–15% on the back of stable demand and successful retail scaling.
*   **Sportking:** Posted strong 19.8% EBITDA margins and is set to commission major Odisha capacity by March 2027.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Festive/Wedding Season Impact:** Whether the delayed festive season (Q3) translates into the expected surge in wedding-related sales for companies like Aditya Birla Lifestyle and Raymond Lifestyle.
*   **Raw Material Volatility:** Monitoring if cotton and polyester prices stabilize or continue to pressure margins for fabric manufacturers like RSWM and Monte Carlo.
*   **Capacity Commissioning:** The operational ramp-up of Sportking’s Odisha plant and Banswara Syntex’s Surat facility.
*   **Retail Profitability:** The performance of new retail formats (ZECODE/DEVO for Siyaram; Emerging Businesses for Raymond Lifestyle) as they move toward breakeven.
