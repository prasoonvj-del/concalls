## Big picture
Managements across the sector describe a **structural upturn** driven by three forces: **FTAs unlocking export markets** (UK live, EU expected mid-2027, US bilateral talks), **global sourcing diversification** away from China and Bangladesh (China+1, Bangladesh+1), and a **deliberate product-mix upgrade** toward man-made fibres, value-added yarns, recycling and technical textiles. Most companies posted double-digit revenue growth and margin expansion in Q2 FY27, but the tone splits between **spinners** (who pass raw-material costs quickly) and **fabric/garment makers** (who face 30-40 day pass-through lags). Geopolitical volatility in West Asia keeps freight and petrochemical feedstocks unpredictable.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian textiles has historically made money through **low-cost cotton spinning and basic garmenting**, exporting volume to the US/EU with limited branding. Over the last ~5 years the model has shifted toward **MMF/blended fabrics, vertical integration (fibre-to-garment), multi-country manufacturing footprints, sustainability/recycling, and direct-to-consumer retail** [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **MMF & value-added product mix** – **scaling** – Banswara (internal yarn consumption 40%, stretch yarn 450-500 tpm), Century Enka (VAP target 50% of yarn portfolio), Filatex (Ecosis recycled chips 27k TPA), Indo Rama (specialty filament/fiber Rs 200 cr), RSWM (RSWM 2.0 profitability-over-volume), Sanathan (polyester 87% of mix), Sumeet (50% capacity to value-added yarns) [Banswara concall Q2 FY27; Century Enka concall Q2 FY27; Filatex concall Q2 FY27; Indo Rama concall Q2 FY27; RSWM concall Q2 FY27; Sanathan concall Q2 FY27; Sumeet concall Q2 FY27].
- **Circularity & recycling** – **early** – Filatex Ecosis depolymerization trials started Nov 2026 (H&M global supplier approval), RSWM LNJ GreenPET bottle-to-bottle recycling commercial Q1 FY28 (~Rs 200 cr revenue), Sumeet moving to dope-dyed/cationic/BSY yarns [Filatex concall Q2 FY27; RSWM concall Q2 FY27; Sumeet concall Q2 FY27].
- **Multi-country garment manufacturing** – **scaling** – Pearl Global (Bangladesh +7M pcs, Bihar ramping, Vietnam/Indonesia capex Rs 325-350 cr), Gokaldas (Africa 45M pcs capacity, two new FY28 customers), S.P. Apparels (Sri Lanka 1,700 machines, targeting Rs 400 cr FY28), Indo Count (US utility bedding 3 plants >65% util.) [Pearl Global concall Q2 FY27; Gokaldas concall Q2 FY27; S.P. Apparels concall Q2 FY27; Indo Count concall Q2 FY27].
- **Vertical integration (spinning → fabric → garment)** – **scaling** – Sangam (cotton yarn +70% capacity, 40-50% captive for denim, garmenting 50-60% util.), Vardhman (garment capacity doubling to 13-14% EBITDA target), GHCL (fabric share 16% → 16.5-17%, PM MITRA processing), Banswara (Surat garment SEZ +Rs 150-200 cr over 2 yrs) [Sangam concall Q2 FY27; Vardhman concall Q2 FY27; GHCL concall Q2 FY27; Banswara concall Q2 FY27].
- **Direct-to-consumer retail** – **early** – Siyaram (ZECODE/DEVO 61 stores → 70 by FY27, Rs 160 cr target), Garware Home Solutions (14 studios → 50 by Dec 2027, D2C revenue targets Rs 50/100/200 cr over 3 yrs) [Siyaram concall Q2 FY27; Garware concall Q2 FY27].
- **Technical textiles & industrial yarns** – **early** – Century Enka (PTCF commercial Q4 FY27), RSWM (NDS9 JV premium denim/knit Rs 208 cr Phase-1), Sutlej (4 products approved, FY28 commercialization), Lohia (defence UHMWPE line, South Korea order) [Century Enka concall Q2 FY27; RSWM concall Q2 FY27; Sutlej concall Q2 FY27; Lohia concall Q2 FY27].
- **Asset-light export models** – **early** – Sutlej pursuing external weaving partners in Africa/Middle East for PV fabrics [Sutlej concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **PLI scheme incentive rules eased** – rewards incremental investment in MMF/technical textiles; benefits all capex-heavy players (Filatex, Indo Rama, Century Enka, RSWM, Sanathan, Sangam) [Govt: India govt, Nov 2026].
- **MP govt approves textile parks** – land for greenfield expansion; Vardhman’s PM MITRA land handover delayed 8-9 months, GHCL received 56 acres at PM MITRA [Govt: MP govt, Nov 2026].
- **UK FTA effective July 2026** – duty parity with Bangladesh/Vietnam, 12% advantage vs China; already driving order inquiries (Indo Count, Pearl, Gokaldas, Banswara, S.P. Apparels) [Govt: Commerce Ministry, Jul 2026].
- **EU FTA expected early 2027** – next major export catalyst; managements citing pre-interest and inquiry surge (Banswara, GHCL, Indo Count, Pearl, RSWM, Sanathan) [Govt: EU Commission, pending].
- **US tariff developments** – proposed 25% or capped 17% under trade deal; creates uncertainty for export-oriented garmenters (Gokaldas, Pearl, Indo Count, Vardhman fabric) [Govt: US Govt, pending Nov 2026].
- **Duty-free cotton import (2-month window)** – too short for Brazilian/Australian shipments (35-50 days); Vardhman calls it ineffective [Govt: India govt, 2026].
- **Certificates of Origin for NZ exports authorised** – procedural ease for exporters [Govt: Commerce Ministry, Nov 2026].
- **Airfare subsidy for micro/small exporters** – minor support for smaller players [Govt: Commerce Ministry, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: **Export demand improving** – UK FTA live, EU FTA anticipated, US retailer inventory lean but discretionary spending strong (Pearl: gasoline <$4 supports apparel), China+1/Bangladesh+1 sourcing shift benefiting multi-location players (Pearl, Gokaldas, S.P. Apparels). **Domestic demand stable/recovering** – festive season, rural/Tier-2-3 recovery (Siyaram, Banswara), government tenders (Sangam H1 ~Rs 200 cr). **Volume commitments secured** for new capacities (Filatex Ecosis 7-8k tonnes from Decathlon/A&E/IKEA/H&M, Pearl order books strong entering Q3).

**Divergence**: **Spinners vs fabric/garment** – Vardhman, RSWM, Sportking, Sanathan report near-full spinning utilisation and spread stability; RSWM, Vardhman, S.P. Apparels flag fabric/garment margin compression from 30-40 day pass-through lag. **Geography** – Mallcom says Europe downturn “worst behind us” but still weak; Gokaldas proactively de-risked a weak African customer (20-25% of Africa revenue) cutting H2 Africa target to $50-55M from $112-115M annual run-rate. **Raw-material volatility** – Filatex notes “uneven demand with subdued buying followed by sudden spurts” from crude spikes; Sutlej cites 3x freight spikes (Turkey $2k→$6k, LatAm $2.5k→$10k) capping margins.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Cotton** – Domestic prices **Rs 64,000-69,000/candy** (Vardhman 64-65k, GHCL 67-69k, Sportking 65.5k) vs ~54-55k previously; CAI crop 28-29M bales vs 35-36M consumption → structural shortfall. **Polyester chain** – PTA/MEG/polyester **up 25-30%** (Banswara, Century Enka, Indo Rama) on West Asia tensions and crude ~$115. **Recycled PET chips** now cheaper than virgin (Filatex: recycled ~Rs 130-140/kg, EBITDA Rs 30-35/kg). **Power/freight** – Grid tariffs up (Century Enka Bharuch, Banswara), freight **3x spike** (Sutlej), logistics headwind Rs 37 cr (Garware). **Pass-through** – Spinners largely pass on (RSWM, Vardhman); fabric/denim/garment face **30-40 day lag** compressing margins (RSWM, Vardhman, S.P. Apparels). **EBITDA margins** – widening range: Sportking 19.8%, Indo Count 15.3%, Pearl 10.2%, Banswara 10.2%, Sanathan Rs 12,725/ton, Century Enka 5.9% (Q2) but H1 10.7%, RSWM 8.7%, Sutlej 6.4%, Sumeet 2.5% (compressed, guiding 5-6% FY27).

## What changed since last quarter
- **Filatex** – Ecosis/steam timelines slipped **weeks** (West Asia shipping, turbine delivery, skilled labour); domestic PTA commissioning (IOCL Paradip, GAIL Mangalore) positive.
- **GHCL** – Tone shifted to **“cautiously optimistic”** (cotton volatility); **Rs 27 cr fire loss** at Tuticorin warehouse (insured).
- **Gokaldas** – Capex guidance **raised** to Rs 150-175 cr (was Rs 70-75 cr); Africa H2 target **cut** to $50-55M (de-risking weak customer); BTPL merger **NCLT judgment reserved** (10 days).
- **Indo Count** – Confidence to **exceed FY27 guidance** (Rs 5,500 cr, 13% EBITDA); Bhilad flood **Rs 45 cr inventory loss** (insurance claim filed, Rs 14 cr interim received).
- **Mallcom** – Tone **more optimistic**: export order booking improving, logistics easing, “worst behind us” in Europe.
- **S.P. Apparels** – Revenue guidance **~95% of Rs 2,000 cr**; Sri Lanka **delayed** (one factory awaiting customer approval, full FOB FY28).
- **Sumeet** – Tone **positive post-war** (crude/raw material down, wedding season); receivables **+42%** (stopped cash discounts, higher prices).
- **Sutlej** – **Freight costs 3x** capped margins; new **asset-light model** for Africa/Middle East using external weavers.
