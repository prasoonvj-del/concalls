# Kewal Kiran Clothing Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Macro headwinds, Vapi factory flooding, and festive shift impacted Q2 sales, though strong profitability and profitability targets were maintained.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27): INR 373 crore (up 5% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): INR 80 crore (up 12% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 21%
- Profit After Tax / PAT (Q2 FY27): INR 54 crore (up 15% YoY)
- Kraus Brand Growth: Over 25% YoY in Q2
- Retail Channel Growth: 8% YoY
- Killer EBO Store Count: 477 stores (Total network: 675 EBOs as of September 30, 2026)
- Killer EBO SSSG (H1 FY27): Negative 10%
- Net Working Capital: ~130 days

### Highlights
- Q2 operations impacted by ongoing geopolitical tensions, inflationary pressures, and heavy rainfall/localized flooding in Vapi and Daman causing temporary factory disruptions (estimated 3% to 4% impact on Q2 sales).
- Festive season (Puja/Diwali) shifted from Q2 (last year) to Q3 (this year), affecting seasonal retail billing.
- Management reviewed Vision 2028: Optimized EBO store target to 800 stores (down from 900), retaining revenue and profitability targets.
- Killer store count target unchanged with a shift to larger formats (800-1,000 sq ft); Lawman target revised from 175 to 125 stores (D2C-led, larger formats); Kraus target increased from 50 to 75 stores; K-Lounge format to be gradually phased out.
- Non-retail channel growth driven by the e-commerce segment.
- Integriti brand delivered double-digit growth in H1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in pursuing double-digit growth target for the full financial year FY27.
- Full-year retail SSSG expected to remain flattish based on 9-month expectations.
- Vision 2028 revenue and profitability objectives remain unchanged despite store count target optimizations.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer sentiment weighed down by sustained inflationary pressures and geopolitical tensions.
- Demand itself was not a problem, but production was constrained due to Vapi factory flooding and weather disruptions.
- Discounting in the market has grown; company strategy is to reduce unnecessary discounts, focus on realistic pricing and fresh sales to protect brand value and EBITDA.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Facing inflationary pressures in cotton, commodities, oil, and gas prices, leading to fabric price hikes.
- Gross margins remained intact in H1. Management aims to absorb/pass on pressure through MRP adjustments and discount reduction.
- EBITDA margins are expected to remain stable around industry-average levels rather than seeing incremental margin expansion from store throughput changes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus shifting away from multi-brand K-Lounge format toward single-brand retailing in main streets with larger store sizes (1,000-1,200+ sq ft) and category expansions (kids wear, formals, blazers, winter wear).
- Kraus expanding into Middle East exports, online channels, and MBO formats.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital days at ~130 days, in line with last year.
- Inventory was slightly higher due to festive dispatches shifting from Q2 (last year) to Q3 (this year).
- Properties in Goregaon and Gautam Chemical Compound: maintained as-is with no immediate investment, sale, or development plans finalized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and inflationary pressures weighing on consumer sentiment.
- Weather disruptions and factory flooding (Vapi/Daman) affecting production schedules.
- Winter season sales limited to a tight 60-day window, which can impact discounting and Q3 EBITDA mix by 1% to 2%.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gunit Singh (Counter Cyclical PMS)** asked about the market value/plans for Goregaon and Gautam Chemical properties and requested a share buyback.
   - *Management:* Properties are kept as-is with no current plans or investments. Buyback suggestion noted for future consideration.
2. **Shreya Chatterjee (Analyst)** asked about volume drops, retail vs non-retail mix, and SSSG.
   - *Management:* Apparel quantity and realization went up; volume drops were in non-apparel categories like accessories/deodorants. Killer EBO SSSG was -10% in H1 due to festive shifting.
3. **Giriraj (Analyst)** inquired about the exact impact of the Vapi floods and Navratri shift.
   - *Management:* Vapi floods impacted Q2 sales by 3% to 4%. Festive shift requires merging Q2 and Q3 to view normalized retail performance.
4. **Arpan (Analyst)** asked about the rationale behind EBO rationalization and fresh vs. discount sales strategy.
   - *Management:* K-Lounge is being phased out in favor of single-brand larger stores. The company is actively cutting down market discounting to protect brand equity and improve fresh sales.
5. **Sahil Doshi (Thinqwise)** asked about the reasons for lowering 15% growth expectations and supply-chain issues.
   - *Management:* Floods caused a 7-10 day complete stoppage in production at Vapi, leading to supply bottlenecks despite steady underlying demand.
6. **Manish Goyal (Analyst)** questioned how lower store counts (800 vs 900) can generate the same Vision 2028 revenue and whether EBITDA margins will expand.
   - *Management:* Throughput will rise via larger store formats (1,000-1,200 sq ft) and added categories (kids wear, winter wear, blazers), but EBITDA margins will not expand further as incremental gains are shared with franchisees or spent on digital marketing.

### One-line takeaway
KKCL delivered resilient Q2 financial performance with 5% revenue and 15% PAT growth despite Vapi flood disruptions and festive shifts, while strategically realigning its Vision 2028 retail footprint toward larger single-brand EBOs and reduced discounting.
