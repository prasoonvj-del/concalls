# Sanathan Textiles Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by the Punjab facility turning PAT positive, volume growth, and higher capacity utilization.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,804 crore (up 35.16% QoQ, >120% YoY from Rs 818 crore).
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 992 crore (up 22% QoQ, 29% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 155 crore (up 43.2% QoQ, 144.94% YoY from Rs 63 crore).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 84 crore (down 11.7% QoQ, up 17.7% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA per Ton:** Rs 12,725 per ton (up 13% QoQ).
- **Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 61 crore (up 157% QoQ from Rs 24 crore, 204% YoY from Rs 20 crore). PAT margin at 3.4%.
- **Sales Volumes (Consolidated):** 1.22 lakh metric tonnes (58,000 metric tonnes from Silvassa, 64,000 metric tonnes from Punjab). Production at 1.15 lakh metric tonnes.
- **Standalone Sales Volume:** 58,000 metric tonnes.
- **Earnings Per Share (Basic, not annualized):** Rs 7.3.

### Highlights
- Punjab facility Phase 1 is fully operational, achieved >90% average capacity utilization, and turned PAT positive.
- Expanded technical textiles capacity at Silvassa commenced production on August 20, 2026, doubling capacity from 9,000 MTPA to 18,000 MTPA.
- Silvassa facility maintained ~95% capacity utilization.
- Revenue mix (consolidated): Polyester ~87%, cotton ~10%, technical textiles ~3%. Standalone: Polyester ~77%, cotton ~18%, technical textiles ~6%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised FY27 EBITDA target to north of Rs 560 crore (previously Rs 520 crore to Rs 550 crore).
- Management targets FY27 revenue of more than Rs 6,000 crore, FY28 revenue of Rs 7,200–7,300 crore, and FY29 revenue of Rs 7,700–8,000 crore (Paresh Dattani).
- Punjab Phase 2 (250 TPD portion): first portion commissioning in December, full ramp-up to 900 TPD by June/July.
- Gross debt expected at ~Rs 1,350 crore and net debt at ~Rs 1,150 crore by the end of FY27; gross debt to reduce by ~Rs 120 crore next year (Sanjay Shah).
- Capex guidance: ~Rs 200 crore for FY27 and ~Rs 400 crore for next year (Sanjay Shah).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remained very good; customers returned to normal buying patterns after deferring purchases in Q1.
- Chinese operations hampered by PTA/PX availability; landed Chinese material is more expensive than domestic, and BIS removal has resulted in minimal imports.
- EU FTA expected to provide an advantage for yarn exports and boost export share to double digits from Silvassa.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Volatility in PTA, MEG, and raw cotton in April/May eased; raw material pass-through played out via higher realizations.
- Depreciation was Rs 36.6 crore and finance costs were Rs 42 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Silvassa technical textiles capacity doubled to 18,000 MTPA (commissioned Aug 20, 2026; currently operating at 94–95% utilization).
- MP cotton yarn project (Rs 400 crore) is on track: ground work in early January, production by end of calendar year, full stabilization by end of financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt expected at ~Rs 1,350 crore and net debt at ~Rs 1,150 crore by end of FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global environment remains fluid; October crude and PTA price movements were noted as a bit hazy and wavering.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Harsh Mittal (Emkay Global)** asked about demand and October spreads. Management stated crude/PTA rose in October but crude is coming down; Punjab phase 2 part commissioning is expected in December.
- **Aradhana Jain (360 ONE Capital)** asked about gross margin percentage decline and debt/capex guidance. Management explained gross margins increased in absolute terms while percentage looks lower due to higher selling price base, and provided debt/capex guidance.
- **Roshan (Antique Stock Broking)** asked about Silvassa volume dip. Management cited product mix changes.
- **Tejash Thakkar (T&J Investments)** asked about EBITDA target achievement and Chinese imports. Management confirmed per-ton EBITDA will drive the target, and Chinese imports are not a threat due to higher landed costs.

### One-line takeaway
Sanathan Textiles delivered robust Q2 FY27 results fueled by the Punjab facility turning PAT positive and scaling volumes, leading management to upgrade full-year revenue and EBITDA targets.
