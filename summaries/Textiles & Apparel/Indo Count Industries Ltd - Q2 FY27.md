# Indo Count Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever quarterly performance with record revenue, doubled EBITDA, and strong margin expansion exceeding guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 targets of INR5,500 crore revenue and 105-110 million meters volume, but management explicitly stated they are well-positioned to *exceed* full-year guidance for both top-line and EBITDA (with H1 EBITDA margin already at 14.4% vs 13% full-year guidance).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from "early signs of recovery" and weathering disruptions to robust momentum with healthy order books and expanding non-U.S. opportunities following the UK FTA implementation in July.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Bhilad flood claim details, inventory loss of INR45 crores 2 lakhs recognized along with equivalent insurance claim exceptional item, and an interim claim payment of INR14 crores 99 lakh approved post-quarter end. Container constraints disappeared from discussion.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: U.S. utility bedding utilization improved/remained solid at over 65% across all three plants; brand Wamsutta expanded successfully beyond direct-to-consumer into broader U.S. retail markets.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income / Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR1,699 crore (up 57% YoY, 39% QoQ)
- **Total Income / Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR2,923 crore (up 42% YoY)
- **Core Business Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR2,033 crore (achieving ~50% of INR4,000 crore target)
- **New Business Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR502 crore (~30% contribution to total income)
- **New Business Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR890 crore (achieving ~60% of INR1,500 crore target)
- **Volumes (H1 FY27):** 51 million meters (~49% of 105-110 million meters guidance)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR259 crore (more than doubled YoY from INR123 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 15.3% (expanded 380 bps YoY, exceeding 13% full-year guidance)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR420 crore (up 73% YoY from INR243 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 14.4% (expanded 250 bps YoY)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR132 crore (up 2.5x YoY from INR39 crore)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** INR195 crore (up 150% YoY from INR78 crore)
- **Net Debt (as of Sep 2026):** INR518 crore (lowest-ever level; net debt to equity at 0.2x)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents (as of Sep 2026):** INR315 crore (vs INR114 crore as of March 2026)
- **Annualized ROCE (H1 FY27):** 21.4%
- **Annualized ROE (H1 FY27):** 15.3%
- **Utility Bedding Facility Utilization:** Over 65% across all three U.S. plants
- **Capex spent (H1 FY27):** INR40 crore out of INR250 crore planned (12-18 month horizon)

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly performance driven by market share gains, increased wallet share, strong branded business momentum, and newer businesses.
- Brand Wamsutta successfully expanded beyond the direct-to-consumer platform into broader U.S. retail markets.
- Bhilad facility experienced temporary flooding from unprecedented rainfall; recognized an inventory loss (net of salvage) of INR45 crores 2 lakhs and an equivalent insurance claim as an exceptional item. An interim claim payment of INR14 crores 99 lakh was approved after the quarter end.
- Core non-U.S. business currently accounts for around 30% of core revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they are well-positioned to *exceed* full-year FY27 guidance for both top line (INR5,500 crore) and EBITDA margin (13%).
- Reaffirmed CY2028 long-term revenue target of doubling FY25 revenue to INR8,000 crore, with management noting no growth capex needed to reach this milestone.
- Reaffirmed branded business target of reaching $100 million by CY2028.
- Non-U.S. revenues expected to grow by over 20% in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry navigating tariff uncertainties, evolving trade dynamics, and varying consumer demand.
- U.K. FTA implemented in July has driven much better customer interactions and a surge in inquiries; E.U. FTA is yet to happen.
- U.S. midterm election results are too recent to comment on, but management expects normal business continuity and a positive outlook with a healthy order book.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (cotton and related) have been rising; company maintains a 4 to 5-month inventory cushion which delays impact, and significant cost increases are successfully passed on to customers within 1 to 2 quarters.
- Gross margins on an annualized basis expected to range between 50% and 55%.
- New business EBITDA margins are at lower double-digits and expected to converge with core business margins (15%) over coming quarters as scale increases.
- Freight costs have minimal impact as most business is conducted on FOB or CIF basis, with freight hikes borne by customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned INR250 crore capex is progressing over a 12-18 month horizon, with INR40 crore deployed in H1 FY27. 
- Major projects include effluent treatment (spilling over to next year) and spinning capacity expansion (majority completion expected by fiscal year-end).
- No growth capex required to achieve the CY2028 INR8,000 crore revenue target.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt declined to INR518 crore with net debt-to-equity at a healthy 0.2x.
- Working capital cycle improved, with the number of days coming down by at least three weeks compared to March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary operational and inventory impact at the Bhilad facility due to heavy rainfall and flooding (fully covered by insurance).
- Macro tariff uncertainties and evolving trade dynamics globally.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sani Vishe (PL Capital) asked about input cost pressures and pass-through timeline.** Management explained they hold a 4 to 5-month inventory buffer and pass significant cost increases to customers within 1 to 2 quarters with no pressure on margins.
- **Abhishek Shankar asked about U.S. operating environment post-midterm elections and drivers of strong performance.** Management stated the election results are too recent to comment on, order books remain healthy, and strong results were driven by a combination of market share gains and increased customer wallet share.
- **Jiten Doshi (Investor) congratulated management and suggested doubling the company size faster towards INR10,000 crore with 20% EBITDA margin by 2030.** Management thanked him and noted they currently stick to their CY2028 INR8,000 crore guidance while evaluating greater aspirations.
- **Prerna Jhunjhunwala asked about UK FTA customer reactions after 3-4 months.** Management highlighted much better interactions and high inquiry levels following the July implementation, awaiting translation into final volumes.
- **Karina asked about utility bedding utilization timelines and debt reduction plans.** Management stated utility bedding will reach 100% capacity utilization by CY2028, and surplus free cash flows will continue to be utilized for net debt reduction.

### One-line takeaway
Indo Count delivered record-breaking Q2 performance with revenues up 57% YoY and EBITDA more than doubling, reinforcing management's confidence in outperforming full-year guidance.
