# Brahm Virat Industries Corporation Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-26 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27 (ended June 30, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in the company's transformation, new product launches, and expanding global footprint following the integration into the Brahmcorp Group.

### Key numbers
- **Machine count & capacity:** 145 machines with a capacity of 9 million pairs of socks per annum.
- **Selling price per pair of socks:** 
  - March 2025: Rs 42.60
  - March 2026: Rs 52.93
  - June 30, 2026 (Q1): Rs 63.00 (nearly 47% increase over the last two years).
- **Transaction investment:** Rs 95 crore primary investment for a 70.28% controlling stake in Brahmcorp Lifestyle Products Private Limited (proposed name: Brahm Wellbeing and Lifestyle Corporation Private Limited).
- **Cash position post-transaction:** Approximately Rs 105-106 crore total liquidity (Rs 95 crore investment plus ~Rs 11 crore existing cash on hand).
- **Lord Walker target:** Target of approximately 1 million pairs of socks in calendar year 2027.
- **Fresh by Brahmcorp sales:** Expected to reach Rs 100 crore in sales in the current financial year with a ~10% EBITDA margin.
- **Skill by Brahm sales target:** Aiming for Rs 5 crore in sales in the first year through memberships.

### Highlights
- Virat Industries is transitioning its name to **Brahm Virat Industries Corporation Limited** (pending ROC approval).
- Company received trading permission on the National Stock Exchange (NSE), adding to its existing BSE listing.
- Highlighted the operationalization of the UK-India FTA from July 15, bringing import duty on socks from India into the UK down to 0% (from 11%), enhancing cost competitiveness.
- Launched the premium DTC socks platform **Lord Walker by Brahm** (Lord Walker for men, Lady Walker for women, Little Walker for children) across marketplaces (Amazon, Myntra, Nykaa) and quick commerce (Instamart, Blinkit).
- Investment in Brahmcorp Lifestyle Products adds three key verticals: Ahikoza (luxury handbags), Skill by Brahm (fitness/wellness centers, starting in Pune), and Fresh by Brahmcorp (D2C fresh seafood and meats, formerly Pesca Fresh, app launching mid-September).
- Proposed board includes Vilas Poddar (Chairman), Adi Madan (Managing Director), Namrata, Vijay Sane, Supriya Shete, and Dr. Neha Tikum.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the branded socks and lifestyle platform rollout to more than double standalone EBITDA over time.
- Management targets selling 2 million pairs of branded socks at Rs 200–250 per pair, and 6 million contract manufacturing socks at Rs 60–70, aiming for Rs 100 crore (1 billion rupees) in total socks revenue in two years.
- Management outlined a multi-year aspiration toward a Rs 2,000–3,000 crore market cap.
- *Forward-looking statement:* Management stated that if the company achieves 400 to 500 crores in sales in 3 to 4 years with a ~20% EBITDA margin (~Rs 100 crore EBITDA), management would be satisfied.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted that Virat's contract manufacturing supplies upscale retailers in the UK (John Lewis, Waitrose, Barbour, FootAsylum) and Switzerland (Migros, Galaxus), alongside Premier League football clubs (supplying brands like Umbro, Castore, Sudu, Kukri).
- Noted a growing trend among Indian youth (Gen Z) shifting toward protein-based diets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Branded socks carry significantly higher potential EBITDA margins (projected at 20% to 30% conservative/practical, up to 30–40% with branding) compared to contract manufacturing (~10-12%).
- Luxury handbags retail at approximately $1,200 per bag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rs 95 crore primary capital infusion into Brahm Lifestyle Products to drive organic and inorganic growth.
- Geographical expansion planned into North America, Latin America, the Middle East, Brazil, Canada, and Europe.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Post-transaction, the company will hold ~Rs 105–106 crore in liquid cash.
- Handbag and Fresh by Brahmcorp businesses stated to be 0 debt and cash positive/self-sufficient.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted that actual results may differ materially due to various risks and uncertainties as per the safe harbor statement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Parag Mehta asked about managing bandwidth across multiple simultaneous business verticals.** 
   - *Management reply:* Stated that the socks and handbag businesses are mature/funded with existing teams, and acquired platforms like Ahikoza and Fresh by Brahmcorp are already self-sufficient, requiring minimal operational intervention.
2. **Rohit Suresh asked about the 4-5 main product segments to focus on for the listed entity.** 
   - *Management reply:* Highlighted socks, handbags, Fresh by Brahmcorp, Skill by Brahm fitness centers, bed and breakfast (Brunton), restaurants, spas, wine, and athleisure wear as immediate focus areas.
3. **Girish Jain inquired about the specific utilization of the Rs 95 crore fresh capital.** 
   - *Management reply:* Explained the funds will be held in the company as watch-chest liquidity for evaluated inorganic acquisition opportunities (under NDA) and selective growth across verticals, while noting existing businesses are already funded.
4. **Kunal Kumar asked for a 12-24 month outlook or sketchy revenue/return ranges.** 
   - *Management reply:* Provided targets of reaching ~Rs 100 crore in socks revenue within two years, scaling handbag sales to 2,000 bags at $2,000 each, and aiming for Rs 400-500 crore in overall sales over 3-4 years with a ~20% EBITDA margin.
5. **Praveen asked if Virat would manufacture other knits like gloves.** 
   - *Management reply:* Confirmed intent to expand into undergarments, shirts, t-shirts, and kids' knitwear (Little Walker) after establishing the core socks business.

### One-line takeaway
Virat Industries is transforming into Brahm Virat Industries Corporation Limited via a Rs 95 crore cash infusion into the Brahmcorp lifestyle platform, pivoting from pure-play contract sock manufacturing to a high-margin global D2C wellness, apparel, and lifestyle brand.
