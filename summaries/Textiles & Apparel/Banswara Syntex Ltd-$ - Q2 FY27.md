# Banswara Syntex Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Improved profitability, higher value-added product sales, and strong order visibility despite raw material and freight challenges.

- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR66.0 crores (up 18.9% YoY from INR55.5 crores)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 9.7% (vs 8.5% in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR12.5 crores (up 124.6% YoY from INR5.6 crores)
- **Net Debt:** INR515.0 crores as of 30th September 2026 (increased by INR43.28 crores in H1 due to working capital and raw material price increases)

### Highlights
- Growth driven by better realizations and higher contribution from value-added products rather than volume expansion.
- Internal yarn captive consumption increased to about 40%, with stretch yarn internal consumption rising to 450-500 tons per month (from 300 tons).
- Strong traction in garment exports, particularly onboarding C&A (pipeline of INR5 crores this year, INR20 crores+ next year) and Polish company Reserved, alongside deepening relationships with Next and Marks & Spencer.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reconfirmed FY27 guidance and targets overall double-digit growth and double-digit EBITDA.
- Expects H2 to be stronger, supported by a healthy order book in garments and fabrics.
- Expected FY27 garment sales between INR360 crores to INR380 crores.
- Post-Surat operationalization, management sees a clear pathway to reach INR550 crores to INR600 crores in garment revenue over the next 2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian textile sector witnessed gradual improvement in production levels and domestic order activity.
- Structural shift toward man-made fibers (MMF) and blended fabrics offers medium-to-long-term growth headroom for Indian manufacturers.
- U.K. FTA and upcoming EU FTA (expected around June/July next year) provide a strong boost to export demand, with Europe offering the biggest growth opportunity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (polyester, PTA, MEG) increased by 25% to 30% due to West Asia challenges and oil price increases, putting pressure on margins.
- Power and employee costs also apply pressure on the cost base.
- European and U.K. client margins in the garment division are expected to be healthier, between 12% to 15% EBITDA.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Full year capex guidance maintained around INR130 crores to INR140 crores; INR36 crores completed in H1, with maximum projects (including ETP and environment-related areas) completing in H2.
- Surat facility (in an SEZ) is expected to complete custom de-notification by January/February and bring the plant into production by Q1 of next financial year (April).
- Additional Surat capex estimated between INR30 crores to INR50 crores, which will add INR150 crores to INR200 crores in top line when fully operationalized over 2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased by INR43.28 crores in H1 to INR515 crores as of 30th September 2026, driven by working capital requirements and higher raw material prices.
- Focus remains on reducing slow-moving inventories, improving debtor positions, and optimizing cycle times.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility in West Asia causing spikes in raw material prices (polyester, PTA, MEG) and exorbitant export freight costs.
- Vessel availability and logistical challenges impacting customer lifting schedules.
- Rising power and employee costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nirbhay (N Square Capital) on volume decline vs. top-line growth:** Management clarified that the strategy prioritizes value-added product sales over sheer volume, protecting margins despite lower volumes.
2. **Rohit Kumar (KDM Advisors LLC) on inventory rationalization:** Management stated they are tackling inventory management, debtor positions, and cycle times to gradually reduce total working capital requirements.
3. **Runit Kapoor (Equitree Capital) on Surat facility and garment expansion:** Management confirmed Surat de-notification by Jan/Feb, production start by April, and an expected additional top line of INR150-200 crores over 2 years with INR30-50 crores capex.
4. **Runit Kapoor on U.K./EU FTA client additions:** Management highlighted onboarding C&A (INR5 crore pipeline this year, INR20 crore+ next year), Reserved, and expanding with Next and Marks & Spencer.
5. **Alkesh Jain (Jain Securities) on internal yarn consumption:** Management stated that captive consumption has hit about 40%, driven by higher internal usage of siro and stretch yarns.
6. **Sheetal Shah (Individual Investor) on breaking single-digit growth:** Management affirmed confidence in achieving double-digit growth and double-digit EBITDA supported by tailwinds, though noting power cost and raw material headwinds.

### One-line takeaway
Banswara Syntex delivered sequential growth and margin expansion in H2/H1 FY27 by pivoting to value-added MMF products and scaling export garments, despite raw material and freight headwinds.
