# Gokaldas Exports Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated income grew 13% YoY and EBITDA rose 48% YoY, supported by robust execution, market share gains, and clear trade policy visibility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Capex guidance updated to INR 150–175 crores for the current year, with annual capex expected to exceed INR 200 crores for the next three years (vs INR 70–75 crores guided last quarter). Africa H2 revenue target adjusted to $50 million–$55 million (down from prior $112–$115 million annual run-rate) due to proactive de-risking of a weak customer. Year-end net debt target guided at INR 650–700 crores (including INR 300 crores BTPL debt).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious optimism on inventory destocking to observing clear recovery signs in August, finalized U.S./U.K. trade policies, and strong long-term demand visibility leading to expected double-digit volume growth in FY28.
- New topics or risks: Customer credit risk in Africa leading to deliberate order slowdown; media allegations regarding historical Bombay Rayon/BTPL asset transfers addressed via legal review.
- Last quarter's promises: BTPL merger process remains on track with NCLT judgment reserved and expected within 10 days; Bhopal Phase 2 facility is ramping up as promised.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated income growth: 13% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA growth: 48% YoY
- India business revenue growth: 10% YoY (vs 1.2% growth for overall Indian apparel exports)
- Africa business revenue growth: 32% YoY
- Net debt: INR 429 crores as of September 2026 (down from INR 553 crores as of March 2026)
- Target year-end net debt (including BTPL): INR 650 crores to INR 700 crores (factoring in INR 300 crores BTPL debt and INR 80–100 crores shareholder cash settlement)
- BTPL Q2 revenue: ~INR 170 crores (EBITDA slightly negative); Q3 revenue expected at INR 220–225 crores (EBITDA breakeven); Q4 revenue expected at INR 250–260 crores (PBT breakeven)
- Hedged exchange rate: ~INR 93 in Q3 and INR 94–95 in Q4 (vs INR 91 in Q2)
- Capacity utilization: India expected at ~92%–93% in Q3; Africa dipped to ~40% in Q2 due to seasonality, expected to climb to 80%–85%
- Capacity by year-end: India ~59 million pieces; Africa ~45 million pieces
- BTPL accumulated losses (tax shield): In excess of INR 600 crores
- RoSCTL incentive: ~3.5%; Duty drawback: 1.5%–2%

### Highlights
- India and Africa businesses outperformed through focused execution and market share gains despite a seasonally weak quarter.
- BTPL merger process is nearing completion, with the NCLT judgment reserved and expected within 10 days.
- Secured two new customers for Africa (one American, one European) starting operations in FY 2028.
- Expanded business significantly with European customers in India.

### Guidance & outlook
- Double-digit volume growth expected from FY 2028 onwards.
- Steady-state EBITDA margins targeted at ~13% for India, ~10% for Africa, and ~10% for BTPL.
- Capex guided at INR 150–175 crores for FY27, moving to upwards of INR 200 crores annually over the next three years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Apparel imports into U.S., EU, and U.K. declined YTD August '26 due to high base (forward buying ahead of tariffs) and retailer inventory discipline, but turned positive in August.
- National Retail Federation projects U.S. holiday retail sales growth of 4.2% YoY.
- Retailers driving toward shorter lead times and supplier consolidation, benefiting large players like Gokaldas.
- U.S. Section 301 tariffs (effective July 21, 2026) place India equal to competitors and advantageously positioned versus China and Vietnam (12.5% tariff).
- India-U.K. FTA (in force July 15, 2026) provides duty parity with Bangladesh/Vietnam and a 12% advantage over China.
- AGOA extended through December 2028, including third-country fabric provisions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Wage cost increases of 8%–9% were absorbed through productivity gains and pricing.
- Customer discounts (~0.5% of gross revenue) ended starting Q3.
- Foreign exchange hedges provide an estimated 1.3%–1.4% EBITDA margin gain in H2 as rates improve to ~INR 93–95.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bhopal Phase 2 facility is ramping up and will be fully operational by financial year-end (adding ~3.5 million pieces / ~INR 200 crores revenue capacity).
- Construction started on a 1,000-machine factory in Ranchi (2,000 machines across two shifts, ~INR 400 crores top line), expected to ramp up around Dussehra/Diwali next year (impacting FY29).
- Actively evaluating at least two additional 1,000-machine factories.
- Debottlenecking yields an ongoing 4%–5% extra revenue gain across factories.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt decreased to INR 429 crores as of September 2026.
- Working capital days targeted around 75 days.
- Trade payables increased due to alignment of vendor credit terms with customer credit cycles.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Uneven consumer environment with inflation and fuel pressures impacting household budgets, particularly lower-income groups.
- Credit risk associated with one African customer (representing 20%–25% of African revenue) prompting proactive order slowdown and de-risking.
- Potential reduction in RoSCTL incentive (currently 3.5%); management confident of absorbing any reduction via productivity and pricing.
- Media reports regarding historical Bombay Rayon/BTPL asset transfers; management stated legal review indicates no substance to allegations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jignesh Kamani (Nippon Mutual Fund) on Atraco margins/debt and BTPL financials:** Management explained Q2 seasonality caused a temporary dip in Atraco margins, but expects a return to 10% EBITDA margin in 6 months. BTPL carries ~INR 300 crores of debt, targeting EBITDA breakeven in Q3 and PBT breakeven in Q4.
2. **Soham Samanta (Motilal Oswal) on India volume growth and realization sustainability:** Management confirmed double-digit volume growth starts in FY28. Realizations are sustainable and mix-driven, benefiting from high-ASP outerwear in H1.
3. **Vishal Mehta (IIFL Capital) on Africa revenue guidance and H2 margin tailwinds:** Management noted Africa H2 revenue was moderated to $50 million–$55 million due to de-risking a weak customer. H2 margins will benefit from favorable currency hedges (~INR 93–95 vs INR 91), elimination of tariff discounts, and cost pass-throughs.
4. **Aashish Upganlawar (InvesQ PMS) on PAT potential and RoSCTL status:** Management anticipates significantly higher PAT from FY28 onwards due to scale and operating leverage. RoSCTL discussions are ongoing, and a worst-case 1.75% reduction is fully absorbable.
5. **Jinit Savla on BTPL legal investigation and NCLT status:** Management stated legal checks show no merit in the Bombay Rayon/BTPL allegations. The NCLT merger judgment was heard today, with the verdict reserved and expected in 10 days.

### One-line takeaway
Gokaldas Exports delivered a strong Q2 with 13% revenue and 48% EBITDA growth, backed by favorable trade policies and robust forward visibility, with profitability set to accelerate from FY28 as BTPL integrates and capacities expand.
