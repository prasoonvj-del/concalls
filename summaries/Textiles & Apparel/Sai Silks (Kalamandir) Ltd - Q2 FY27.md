# Sai Silks (Kalamandir) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While financial performance in H1 was impacted by a challenging discretionary environment, Adhik Maas, and a calendar shift in Dussehra resulting in a minor YoY revenue decline and negative SSSG, management noted strong festive momentum in Q2/H2 (navigating towards a recovery).

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 434 crore (down 2.3% YoY from Rs 444 crore in Q2 FY26); up 15.7% QoQ from Q1 FY27.
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 183 crore; Gross margin at 42% (up 20 bps YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 62 crore; EBITDA margin at 14.2% (up about 37 bps QoQ).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 31 crore; PAT margin at 7.2%.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 809 crore (down 1.7% YoY from Rs 823 crore in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 340 crore; Gross margin maintained at 42%.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 113 crore; EBITDA margin at 14%.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 57 crore; PAT margin at 7%.
- **Q2 FY27 SSSG:** Decline of 10.6% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 SSSG:** Decline of 9.2% YoY.
- **Store Network:** 86 stores spanning close to 820,000 square feet of retail area as of September 30, 2026.
- **H1 Store Additions / Closures:** Opened 6 new stores (adding ~56,000 sq. ft.); closed 1 KLM Fashion Mall store (~20,000 sq. ft.).
- **Cash Balance / Debt:** Net debt negative with a cash balance of approximately Rs 249 crore.
- **Silver Jewellery Inventory:** Rs 6-7 crore.
- **Tamil Nadu Store Productivity (H1):** Reached about Rs 36,000 per square foot.

### Highlights
- First half impacted by Adhik Maas in Q1 and a calendar shift of Dussehra into October (Q3), alongside a high base effect.
- Maintained higher inventory levels to prepare for the festive and wedding season, ensuring wider assortment.
- Expanded 6 new stores in H1 FY27, primarily focusing on cluster-led expansion with Karnataka as an important growth market.
- Silver jewellery experiment in select existing stores is kept minimal due to high metal price fluctuations and emerging preference for fashion jewellery; no aggressive expansion planned for this category.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its earlier full-year turnover guidance between Rs 1,850 crore and Rs 1,900 crore.
- Expects double-digit SSSG and double-digit revenue growth for the full year, driven by robust H2 festive and wedding demand.
- Management deferred specific annual EBITDA margin guidance range finalization until the end of Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Festive-to-festive (Dussehra last year vs. this year) business grew by more than 27-30%.
- All KPIs including ASPs and ABVs showed considerable growth across all formats during Navaratri/Dussehra.
- Wedding calendar in H2 is evenly spread out with a healthy pipeline of dates.
- Regional competition in AP and Telangana has been active via temporary exhibition formats in function halls, which management countered successfully with targeted store-level advertising.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices increased by about 5-6%; company absorbs cost increases where difficult to pass on, while ensuring products remain cheaper than competitors.
- Gross margins improved by 20 bps YoY in Q2 to 42% due to sourcing capabilities, pricing discipline, and merchandise mix management.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Follows a 100% company-owned, company-operated (COCO) model.
- Cluster-led expansion model: penetrating a location with one brand, then adding subsequent formats to leverage warehousing and administration.
- Exploring expansion into newer territories outside current 4 states (such as Maharashtra and Kerala).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains net debt negative with approx. Rs 249 crore cash balance.
- Inventory appears higher due to seasonal build-ups ahead of Dussehra (as of Sept 30) and upcoming Sankranti/Pongal, but management stated inventory days have structurally improved over the last 3 years.
- Board evaluated and rejected a share buyback option, stating deployment of funds into the business will deliver better results given the current PE valuation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subdued discretionary demand and calendar shifts impacting short-term SSSG.
- Potential impact of extreme weather events (e.g., cyclones in Andhra Pradesh causing temporary single-day store closures).
- Volatility in silver metal prices dampening expansion enthusiasm for the silver jewellery category.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked about inventory turns and capital structure optimisation.** Management responded that inventory levels are optimal for a wedding/festive business to drive high revenue per square foot, and inventory days have improved over the last 3 years.
2. **Madhur Rathi suggested considering a share buyback given trading below book value/low PE.** Management answered that a buyback is not appropriate since deploying funds into the business yields better outcomes at current valuations.
3. **Sanket Kelaskar (360 One) asked to quantify Navaratri demand and brand-wise SSSG.** Management stated Dussehra/festive business grew over 27-30% YoY, helping offset negative SSSG caused by the calendar shift.
4. **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital) questioned the feasibility of achieving full-year targets and the recovery path for KLM Fashion Mall.** Management confirmed comfort with the Rs 1,850–1,900 crore revenue target and noted KLM is on a recovery path with specific interventions.
5. **Rahul Jain (Credence Wealth) inquired about Tamil Nadu store productivity and the rationale for store additions in Karnataka.** Management replied that Tamil Nadu reached ~36,000 per sq. ft. in H1 and cluster strategies drove Karnataka expansion where store counts were previously lower.

### One-line takeaway
Despite a challenging H1 marked by festive calendar shifts and negative SSSG, Sai Silks expects a strong recovery in H2 driven by robust wedding and festive momentum, maintaining its full-year revenue guidance of Rs 1,850-1,900 crore.
