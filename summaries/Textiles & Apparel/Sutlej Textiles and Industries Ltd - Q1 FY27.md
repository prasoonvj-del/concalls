# Sutlej Textiles and Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted the first profitable quarter after a prolonged difficult period, driven by structural improvements rather than market tailwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 704 crore (up 17% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 47.2 crore (vs Rs 5 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 6.7% (vs 0.8% in Q1 FY26 and 5.3% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Gross Margin:** 46.8% (expansion of ~370 bps YoY).
*   **PBT:** Rs 4.2 crore (vs loss of Rs 38.6 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 2.7 crore (vs loss of Rs 25.7 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Finance Cost:** Rs 18.3 crore (vs Rs 17 crore in preceding quarter).
*   **Depreciation:** Rs 27.4 crore.

### Highlights
*   Fifth consecutive quarter of margin expansion.
*   All 3 business clusters and 6 manufacturing units delivered positive EBITDA.
*   No exceptional items reported this quarter (vs Rs 22.5 crore exceptional charge in FY26).
*   Yarn segment revenue grew 16% to Rs 640 crore; segment EBITDA rose to Rs 34.4 crore (vs Rs 6.5 crore YoY).
*   Home textiles revenue grew 22% YoY and turned profitable.
*   Sutlej Green Fibre (SGF) business is seeing structural demand driven by European regulations.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to maintain or improve upon Q1 FY27 numbers in subsequent quarters.
*   Targeting double-digit EBITDA margins in the future (no specific timeline provided).
*   Home textiles business aims to double in size over the next 2 years.
*   Protective textiles (Protech) business is expected to be an FY28 contributor.
*   Interest cost for FY27 is estimated at ~Rs 75 crore (vs Rs 67 crore in FY26) due to ongoing capex.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Global demand remains relatively stable; geopolitical uncertainty and raw material volatility persist.
*   Cotton prices moved upward; polyester and viscose prices remained firm.
*   Export growth driven by new markets in Far East and Africa; exposure to any single geography is lower than 2 years ago.
*   Bangladesh market demand is stable but has not reached levels seen 2 years ago.
*   Customers are rebuilding inventories cautiously.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Raw material consumption improved to 53.2% of revenue (vs 56.9% YoY).
*   Employee cost stood at 16.6% of revenue (vs 17.7% YoY).
*   Power and fuel costs improved to 11% of revenue (vs 12.2% YoY).
*   Management attributes 60% of margin improvement to product mix upgrades and the balance to operational efficiency.
*   Raw material outlook: Cotton is neutral to bullish; synthetics may soften.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Maintenance capex is proceeding as planned.
*   Capital growth is funded from internal accruals and existing facilities; no additional equity required.
*   Focus is on upgrading existing assets rather than building new capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Working capital utilization remains comfortable against sanction limits.
*   Inventory is calibrated to demand; receivable cycles are steady.
*   Management intends to reduce absolute finance costs as operating cash generation strengthens.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical uncertainty and raw material price volatility remain outside management control.
*   Employee costs remain a concern for investors, though management noted they are working on reductions.
*   The company is not yet at best-in-class modernization levels compared to global peers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you quantify the value-added yarn mix?** A: Currently 10%–15% of the yarn portfolio; target is 30%–35%.
*   **Q: Why is employee cost consistently higher than peers?** A: Management acknowledged the concern, citing the complex nature of their business (melange, dyeing, job processing) as a factor, but committed to future reductions.
*   **Q: How much of the EBITDA improvement is sustainable?** A: Management stated the results are not due to market tailwinds but structural changes (product mix, integration), making them repeatable.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Protech business?** A: It has moved from qualification to sampling/trials; expected to contribute in FY28.
*   **Q: Are you chasing volume?** A: No, the strategy is profitability through product mix, not volume.

### One-line takeaway
Sutlej Textiles has returned to profitability through a structural shift toward value-added products and operational efficiency, with management signaling a focus on sustainable, double-digit margin growth rather than volume-led expansion.
