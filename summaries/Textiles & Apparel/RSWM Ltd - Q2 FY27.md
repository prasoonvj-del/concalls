# RSWM Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line and PAT growth YoY, but fabric division profitability remains under pressure due to raw material cost pushes.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 1,322 crore (up 15% YoY, 13.9% QoQ)
- Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27): Rs 2,484 crore (up 7.1% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 115.9 crore (up 47.1% YoY, 23.2% QoQ)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 8.7% (expanded 191 bps YoY, 66 bps QoQ)
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 210 crore (up 31.4% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27): 8.4% (expanded 156 bps YoY)
- Profit After Tax - PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 24.5 crore (up from Rs 6.3 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Profit After Tax - PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 41 crore (up from Rs 13 crore in H1 FY26)
- EPS (H1 FY27): Rs 8.76 (vs Rs 2.81 in H1 FY26)
- Standalone Gross Debt: Rs 1,480 crore
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 6.5% in H1 FY27 (vs 5.1% in FY26)

### Highlights
- Domestic sales (Q2 FY27): Up 16.5% YoY; Export sales: Up 11.6% YoY.
- Implemented RSWM 2.0 transformation strategy prioritizing profitability over volume-led expansion, rationalizing non-returning products, and focusing on high-value segments.
- One-time exceptional provision of Rs 11.5 crore recognized for electricity duty on captive solar power generation following an adverse Rajasthan High Court judgment (SLP filed in Supreme Court; Rs 7.42 crore deposited under protest).
- Other equity increased by Rs 206 crore to Rs 1,531 crore in H1 FY27, primarily due to unrealized fair valuation gains of Rs 181 crore from equity investments received via the composite scheme of arrangement involving HEG Limited, HEG Graphite Limited, and Bhilwara Energy Limited.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the second half of FY27 to be better than the first half.
- Knitting expansion of Rs 92 crore expected to be completed by December, with printing and additional 250 metric tonnes of knitted production available in Q4.
- LNJ GreenPET commercial production expected to commence in Q1 FY28, adding approximately Rs 200 crore in revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Textile industry faced challenges from West Asia geopolitical tensions (US, Israel, Iran conflict), creating supply chain disruptions, fluctuating energy prices, and higher freight/insurance costs.
- Rising polyester and cotton prices increased input costs for synthetic yarn and fabric manufacturing, with limited ability to pass increases immediately to customers.
- India's textile and apparel exports saw moderate growth in H1 FY27 led by cotton and man-made textiles, while apparel exports experienced a slowdown.
- RBI projected GDP growth at 7.1% for FY27, supported by strong domestic consumption, manufacturing, and services.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cotton prices increased by almost Rs 16,000 per candy (approx Rs 60/kg increase on cotton yarn).
- Polyester prices surged from Rs 96 to Rs 146 in the last 6 months (increase of Rs 50).
- Gas, dyes, chemical prices, freight, and insurance costs also increased.
- Power and fuel costs decreased by Rs 16.9 crore in H1 FY27 YoY.
- Spinners were largely able to pass on raw material price increases, but fabric manufacturers (Denim and Knit) faced compressed margins due to 30-40 day order booking lags.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Knitting expansion of Rs 92 crores to be completed by Q4 FY27.
- Formed a strategic joint venture with Spain-based NDS9 (LNJ NDS9 Global Private Limited) for premium denim and knit garments (RSWM holds 74%, NDS holds 26%); Phase-1 investment at Rs 208 crore (RSWM equity contribution approx Rs 46 crore).
- LNJ GreenPET project equity contribution estimated at Rs 62 crore (Rs 29 crore already infused); expected revenue contribution of ~Rs 200 crore.
- LNJ Textiles Advisory preferential issue expected to bring in Rs 36 crore (of which Rs 27 crore will be infused in bottle-to-bottle recycling).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone debt stands at Rs 1,480 crore.
- Debt repayment of Rs 95 crore due in H1 FY27; FY28 annual debt repayment estimated around Rs 180 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments, export uncertainties, raw material volatilities, and competition.
- Inability to immediately pass on sharp raw material price increases to fabric customers due to existing order books.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Avinash Nahata (Parami Financial Services) asked about negative PBIT in the fabric division despite 100% denim utilization.** Management explained that raw material cost spikes (cotton/polyester/gas/dyes) outpaced selling prices due to 30-40 day order lags, but expects improvement by Q4 and Q1. (Management did not dodge).
2. **Avinash Nahata asked about equity deployment for ongoing projects.** Management clarified Rs 46 crore for LNJ NDS9 and Rs 62 crore for LNJ GreenPET (Rs 29 crore already drawn). (Management did not dodge).
3. **Jatin Damania (Investments) asked about the interest/depreciation impact on the fabric division and future ROCE.** Management stated only interest applies currently as the project isn't commissioned, and standalone ROCE will improve from current levels. (Management did not dodge).
4. **Jatin Damania asked about peak debt and annual repayments.** Management stated standalone debt is Rs 1,480 crore with Rs 180 crore repayment scheduled for next fiscal year. (Management did not dodge).
5. **Saket Kapoor asked if Q2 numbers included inventory/trading one-offs.** Management clarified that cotton yarn constitutes less than one-fifth of turnover, and uniform purchase policies mean no abnormal stocking gains were engineered. (Management did not dodge).
6. **Adarsh Sharma (SpringTree Investor) asked about LNJ GreenPET revenue contribution.** Management stated it is a 100% subsidiary expected to commence in Q1 FY28 and add ~Rs 200 crore to revenue. (Management did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
RSWM reported a strong 47% YoY jump in Q2 EBITDA and a PAT of Rs 24.5 crore, driven by internal efficiencies and transformation efforts, even as fabric margins faced temporary pressures from sharp raw material inflation.
