# Sumeet Industries Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue grew YoY, but profitability was severely impacted by input cost inflation and raw material volatility.

### Key numbers
- Income/Revenue: INR 272.74 crores (up over 9% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 8.85 crores
- EBITDA Margin: 3.24%
- PAT: INR 1.14 crore
- Production Volume: Reduced by 17% QoQ due to raw material volatility, scarcity, and a 15-day maintenance shutdown.
- Rights Issue Proceeds: INR 199.75 crores raised (net proceeds approx. INR 194.90 crores).
- Long-term Debt: INR 86 crores (before/around rights issue repayment; guided to reduce by INR 23 crores).
- Short-term Borrowings: INR 74 crores.
- Value-Added Yarn Share: 30% to 35% of total yarn production.
- Renewable Energy Share: Currently services around 20% to 30% of power requirements.

### Highlights
- Completed a rights issue raising INR 199.75 crores (16.84 crore equity shares at INR 11.86 per share).
- Net rights issue proceeds earmarked for: ~INR 100 crores for working capital, ~INR 50 crores for operationalizing the Nakoda CP plant, ~INR 23 crores for debt repayment, and ~INR 22 crores for a solar captive power plant.
- Acquired Nakoda Limited CP plant under CIRP for INR 23.47 crores (capacity of 1,40,000 tons per annum of PET chips), which is included in the ~INR 90 crores capex/expansion pool.
- A new 30,000 tons per annum capacity was commissioned in July/August (Q2 FY27).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects more than 30% revenue growth for FY27 compared to the last financial year.
- Guided for an EBITDA margin of around 6% for FY27.
- Guided for PAT in the range of 3.5% to 4% for FY27.
- Nakoda CP plant targeted for commissioning in the next financial year (FY28, specifically Q2), expected to double total capacity within 60 days of reaching optimum utilization.
- Renewable solar power plant commissioning expected in the last quarter (Q4) of FY27, targeting an annual power cost savings of around INR 25 crores once fully commissioned.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying demand across apparel, home textiles, and industrial applications remained healthy.
- Q1 challenges were entirely driven by input cost inflation and volatility rather than end-market demand weakness.
- Pre-Diwali period is expected to drive better demand in the apparel/weaving sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and US caused sharp crude oil price spikes, creating volatility in raw materials (PTA and MEG) and elevated freight/logistics costs.
- Order cycle is ~15 days; rapid price fluctuations prevented full cost pass-through in Q1.
- Management expects gross margins to recover toward over 25% as raw material prices normalize.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CP plant capacity: 300 tons/day; Yarn drawing capacity: 240 tons/day.
- Nakoda CP plant (1,40,000 TPA PET chips) restoration work is underway, expected to commission in FY28 and reach optimum capacity within 60 days.
- 6.5 MW captive solar power plant expected to be commissioned in Q4 FY27, taking renewable energy share to 50%-60%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term debt reduced by INR 23 crores using rights issue proceeds, bringing term loan down to around INR 50 crores (from INR 83 crores).
- Working capital utilization optimized with INR 100 crores from rights issue, lowering CC interest costs to near-negligible levels.
- Normal inventory and receivable/payable days: receivables collected in 30 to 45 days; credit of around 30 days from raw material suppliers. No receivables older than 6 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material price volatility driven by crude oil fluctuations and geopolitical conflicts.
- Share price decline post-rights issue due to massive pricing gap between market price and rights issue price (INR 11.86).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Himanshu Dugar (Stylus Holdings) on Debt & Capacity:** Asked about gross debt and new capacities. Management clarified long-term debt is INR 86 crores and short-term is INR 74 crores, with INR 23 crores being repaid via rights proceeds, and a new 30,000 TPA capacity recently commissioned in Q2.
2. **Riddhi Jain on Power Cost Savings:** Inquired about renewable energy savings. Management stated an expected overall power cost reduction of INR 25 crores per annum once the solar plant is fully commissioned.
3. **Vidhi Purohit on Nakoda CP Plant Timeline:** Asked about pending work and commercial production. Management replied that machine orders and restoration are ongoing, targeting commissioning in FY28.
4. **Rohan Shah on Share Price Drop:** Raised concerns over the stock hitting lower circuits post-rights issue. Management acknowledged that retail investors selling off due to the gap between market price (INR 34-35) and rights price (INR 11.86) was expected, but expressed confidence in future operational results.
5. **Kurin on Margin Protection:** Asked how EBITDA will be protected if volatility continues. Management stated they will procure raw material at the right time and maintain finished product prices rather than absorbing rapid fluctuations.

### One-line takeaway
Sumeet Industries navigated a volatile Q1 with compressed margins (3.24% EBITDA) due to crude spikes, but successfully strengthened its balance sheet via a ~INR 200 crore rights issue and remains confident in achieving >30% revenue growth and ~6% EBITDA margins for FY27.
