# Mallcom (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited volatile markets, West Asia crisis, seaport congestions, and weak demand in the West, though profitability improved via price realization and operational efficiency.

### Key numbers
- Operating revenue: Rs. 110 crore (down 25% QoQ)
- EBITDA: Rs. 14 crore (stable QoQ)
- EBITDA margin: 12.51% (expanded 317 bps QoQ)
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs. 7 crore (up 5% QoQ)
- PAT margin: 6.03% (expanded 174 bps QoQ)
- Domestic revenue: Rs. 64 crore (up 10% QoQ, highest ever Q1 domestic revenue)
- International revenue: Rs. 46 crore
- Sanand plant yearly target: Rs. 40 crore

### Highlights
- Launched new mold for EN812 Certified Bump Caps and commenced manufacturing at the Sanand plant.
- Introduced European and American certified Flame Retardant workwear.
- Expanded the "SMILE" Reseller program to over 1,000 resellers across India.
- Sanand plant expanded from two to three operating lines; helmet, foam cap, and nasal cap production underway.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the Rs. 1,000 crore top-line guidance, acknowledging it is a stretch.
- Management expects domestic/export revenue split to become 50-50 by the end of the year.
- Management expects Q1 numbers to form a base, with top-line and bottom-line only looking to increase going forward.
- Management expects Sanand unit to achieve its minimum yearly target of Rs. 40 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- International revenues moderated due to geopolitical disruptions (West Asia crisis) and bleak demand from the West.
- India-Europe Free Trade Agreement and India-UK Free Trade Agreement are generating more interest and inquiries.
- Developing nations like India, Africa, and the Middle East are witnessing safety requirement shifts and increasing safety awareness.
- US market remains volatile and uncertain due to changing news and tariffs.
- Latin American customers were holding stocks following a strong last year, but order flows continue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability supported by lower raw material cost, improved operational efficiency at Sanand, and price pass-throughs.
- Margin expansion partially offset by lower operating cost absorption from reduced turnover.
- Crude-related raw material prices remain at escalated levels, alongside higher transport and energy costs.
- Company is gradually passing on cost increases to customers; own-brand prices are passed on faster, while white-label has a lag due to long-term contracts.
- No forex gain as mostly hedged.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Working capital borrowings expected to remain at the same level; capex continuing into increasing capacity and machinery.
- Term loans borrowed/refinanced against Sanand capex primarily to claim incentives.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Port congestion at major seaports caused delays in critical raw material procurement and customer deliveries.
- West Asia crisis disrupted shipments and container deliveries.
- Economic situation and weak demand in Western markets.
- Long-term contracts create a time lag in passing raw material price hikes to white-label customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sagar Parekh (Trinetra Asset Managers) on the Rs. 1,000 crore guidance vs current run-rate:** Management stated they will not revise guidance downwards as it keeps them striving, and rely on manufacturing capability, distribution expansion, new geographies, and FTAs.
- **Sagar Parekh on valuation compared to technical textile peers like Kusumgar:** Management clarified that Mallcom is a garmenter, whereas Kusumgar is a technical textile mill supplying fabrics/parachutes, making them not directly comparable.
- **Viraj Kacharia on raw material under-recovery and forex gains:** Management stated there is no under-recovery on passed-on costs (though white-label has a lag) and no forex gains because they are hedged.
- **Viraj Kacharia on European customer wins:** Management noted new customer wins in the UK, Portugal, Spain, Turkey, Italy, and Russia, with many discussions ongoing and customers waiting for the FTA in H2.
- **Zakir Nasser on Sanand headgear production:** Management confirmed headgear production has started at Sanand, increasing revenue and being exported regularly to Europe.

### One-line takeaway
Mallcom navigated a challenging Q1 marked by supply chain congestions and muted Western demand with stable EBITDA and improved margins, while banking on domestic growth, new product launches, and upcoming FTAs for recovery.
