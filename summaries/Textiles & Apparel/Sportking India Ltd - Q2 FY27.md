# Sportking India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance driven by healthy demand, operational efficiency, and a stable raw material environment.

### Key numbers
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 19.8% (Q2 FY27) vs 10.4% (Q2 FY26).
*   **Exports:** ~52% of total revenue.
*   **Capacity Utilisation:** 99%.
*   **Cotton Price (Domestic):** Moderated from Rs 70,000 to Rs 65,500 per candy.
*   **Imported Cotton Price:** ~Rs 67,000 per candy.
*   **Depreciation:** Rs 14 crore (Q2); expected to run at ~Rs 17 crore/quarter going forward.
*   **Trade Receivables:** Rs 473.5 crore (Sept 2026) vs Rs 396.2 crore (March 2026).
*   **New Capacity Contribution (FY27):** Expected Rs 150-200 crore.

### Highlights
*   Commissioned solar power project, expected to reduce annual power costs by ~15%.
*   Merger of fabric and garment companies in progress; expected to be completed in the next couple of months.
*   Operational efficiency initiatives implemented over the last 2-3 years are yielding results.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 margins to be similar to H1 levels.
*   New Odisha capacity expected to contribute Rs 150-200 crore in FY27.
*   FY28 revenue target remains ~Rs 4,000 crore; management indicates margins could exceed the previously guided 15-16% range, though maintains 15% as a conservative floor.
*   Commercial production at Odisha plant expected to start in 30-40 days; project completion by March 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand from export markets (China, Bangladesh) remains robust.
*   Domestic market was slow in the last 2-3 months but expected to pick up post-Diwali.
*   Global brands are diversifying sourcing; India is well-positioned due to an integrated ecosystem and sustainability focus.
*   Observed a shift from polyester to cotton-friendly blends.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   H1 spreads (yarn vs cotton) averaged ~Rs 141/kg; management expects them to stay between Rs 130-145/kg for the next 3-4 months.
*   Odisha unit expected to have 4-6% higher margins than existing operations.
*   Management expects Indian cotton prices to potentially decrease post-Diwali.
*   Import duty waiver on cotton is expected to be extended.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   150,000 spindle Greenfield project in Odisha: Construction complete, machinery erection underway.
*   Odisha incentives: 30% capital subsidy (paid in 5 installments), power at Rs 4/unit for 10 years, and employment guarantee scheme (Rs 7,000-8,000/labor/month).
*   Future expansion: Land available in Odisha to scale up by another 3 lakh spindles; potential garment sector expansion in H2 next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Trade receivables increase attributed to higher turnover; collection period remains stable at 58 days.
*   Peak long-term debt by March 2027 expected at ~Rs 700-750 crore; working capital debt expected at ~Rs 200 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Volatility in cotton prices.
*   Compliance and regulatory requirements for the upcoming merger.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Sustainability of 19.8% EBITDA margin?* A: Management expects H2 margins to be similar to H1 levels.
*   *Q: Impact of cotton price volatility on spreads?* A: Management expects spreads to remain stable (Rs 130-145/kg) for the next 3-4 months.
*   *Q: Why did trade receivables increase?* A: Due to higher turnover; days outstanding remain consistent at 58 days.
*   *Q: Status of merger with fabric/garment entities?* A: Working on compliance; expected in the next couple of months.
*   *Q: Future capex plans?* A: Will stabilize Odisha plant first; next phase of expansion likely in 6-8 months depending on debt levels.

### One-line takeaway
Sportking delivered strong Q2 results with 19.8% EBITDA margins and is nearing the commissioning of its 150,000-spindle Odisha expansion, which is expected to drive incremental growth and margin expansion.
