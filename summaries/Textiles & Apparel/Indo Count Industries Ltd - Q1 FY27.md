# Indo Count Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue, strong margin recovery, and reaffirmed full-year guidance despite temporary logistical and weather disruptions.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income / Revenue:** INR1,224 crore (up 13% QoQ, 27% YoY).
- **Core Business Revenue:** INR837 crore (up 4% QoQ).
- **Core Business Volumes:** 23 million meters (up 12% QoQ from 20.5 million meters in Q4 FY26; down 3% YoY).
- **New Business Revenue:** INR387 crore.
- **EBITDA:** INR160 crore (up 38% QoQ, 34% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 13.1% (expanded 241 bps QoQ, 74 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** INR63 crore (up 2.5x QoQ, 62% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 5.2% (expanded 294 bps QoQ, 114 bps YoY).
- **EPS:** INR3.19 per share.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue, driven by core volume growth and rapid scale-up of new business (nearly tripled over the past year).
- U.S. manufacturing operations maintained 60% to 65% utilization despite the North Carolina greenfield facility addition in January 2026.
- Received three awards from the Confederation of Indian Textile Industry (CITI) during Bharat Tex 2026.
- Bhilad (Gujarat) plant temporarily disrupted by heavy rainfall and flooding from July 23, 2026; partially resumed operations on August 12, 2026, with insurance claims initiated.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reaffirmed FY27 guidance of INR5,500 crore revenue with approximately 13% EBITDA margins.
- Reaffirmed FY27 volume guidance of 105 million to 110 million meters and core business revenue of ~INR4,000 crore.
- Non-U.S. market revenue expected to grow by 20%+ in FY27.
- New business revenue target maintained at INR1,500 crore for FY27, progressing towards the USD275 million ambition for 2028.
- Long-term revenue aspiration of INR8,000 crore by CY2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Core business experienced early signs of recovery following last year's U.S. tariff situation.
- Q1 volume was impacted by container availability constraints due to the West Asia conflict.
- Q1 is typically a softer quarter, while Q2 and Q3 are stronger due to the U.S. festive period.
- FTAs with the UK and EU (along with existing duty-free access to Australia, Japan, New Zealand, and the Middle East) provide favorable tailwinds; UK FTA impact will take 12 to 18 months to fully pan out.
- India is extremely well-positioned globally as a sourcing partner, with U.S. tariffs stabilizing around 10%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin recovery supported by improving operating leverage and better volumes.
- Employee costs increased due to the commencement of the greenfield facility and ongoing ramp-ups.
- Long-term margin targets: 15% for bed linen, 15% for utility bedding, and 100 to 200 bps higher for branded business.
- Input cost inflation is passed on to customers with a brief laggard effect, supported by global sourcing and value engineering.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- U.S. facility utilization maintained at 60% to 65% post-commissioning of the North Carolina greenfield facility.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Interest cost run rate is ~INR30 crore per quarter (~INR120 crore annually) with a weighted average cost of 6% to 7%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Container availability constraints caused by the West Asia conflict.
- Temporary operational disruption at the Bhilad plant due to heavy rainfall and flooding.
- Input cost inflation in non-cotton products due to the Middle East crisis.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhishek asked about the impact of the Bhilad plant disruption on volumes.** Management stated they are fully insured for property, inventory, and loss of profit, and are confident of fulfilling customer orders by making up lost ground across other plants.
- **Bhavin Chheda asked about potential U.S. tariff refunds.** Management stated they do not expect material financial benefits from potential refunds currently, as 80% of exports are on an FOB basis where tariffs are borne by importers.
- **Shradha Agrawal asked about utility bedding utilization expectations.** Management guided for 60% to 65% utilization for the full year, considering the recent commencement of the new facility.
- **Jatin Damania asked about the brand and licensing business breakdown.** Management noted the mix is two-thirds utility bedding manufacturing and one-third brands, targeting USD100 million in the branded segment over the next three years.

### One-line takeaway
Indo Count reported record Q1 revenue and strong margin recovery, reaffirming its full-year FY27 guidance of INR5,500 crore revenue and ~13% EBITDA margin despite temporary weather and logistical headwinds.
