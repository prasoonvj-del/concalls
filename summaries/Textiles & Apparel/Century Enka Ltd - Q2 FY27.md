# Century Enka Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Healthy auto and volume momentum offset by raw material volatility, energy cost pressures, and import competition.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs. 558 crore (up 37% YoY)
- Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs. 1,113 crore (up 37% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs. 33 crore (up 4% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 5.88%
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs. 119 crore (up 130% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27): 10.66%
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs. 19 crore (down 17% YoY)
- PAT Margin (Q2 FY27): 3.31%
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs. 80 crore (up 113% YoY)
- PAT Margin (H1 FY27): 7.21%
- Total Volume (Q2 FY27): 20,162 metric tonnes (up 13% YoY)
- Total Volume (H1 FY27): 39,361 metric tonnes (up 13% YoY)
- Tyre Cord Fabric (TCF) Sales (Q2 FY27): Rs. 251 crore (up 37% YoY)
- Filament Yarn Sales (Q2 FY27): Rs. 293 crore (up 39% YoY)
- TCF Sales (H1 FY27): Rs. 557 crore (up 53% YoY)
- Filament Yarn Sales (H1 FY27): Rs. 523 crore (up 30% YoY)

### Highlights
- Automotive sector witnessed healthy Q2 momentum driven by passenger and commercial vehicles, supported by festive demand.
- Nylon filament yarn business recorded sales volume improvement driven by the festive season.
- Benefit of lower-cost opening inventory from Q1 moderated in Q2, normalizing profitability.
- Capital Work-In-Progress increased from ~Rs. 13 crore in FY26 to ~Rs. 38 crore in H1 FY27.
- Capacity utilization averaged ~85% in Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Polyester Tyre Cord Fabric (PTCF) commercial sales to commence in Q4 FY27.
- Management expects hybrid power capacity to start delivering cost benefits from Q4 FY27, estimating an annualized saving of ~Rs. 10 crore per annum.
- Management targets normal operating EBITDA margins to be between 7% to 10% over the coming quarters.
- Management aims for Value-Added Products (VAPs) to touch ~50% of the yarn portfolio in the near term.
- Management anticipates PTCF capacity utilization to reach a high/good level in H2 FY28 following a gradual multi-stage customer approval ramp-up.

### Demand & industry commentary
- NTCF underlying demand remained healthy, but sales volume was impacted by increased imports.
- Tyre imports/fabric bookings spiked in Q1 as a risk mitigation exercise by tyre companies due to raw material and LNG availability uncertainties.
- OEM demand remained robust up to September across categories except a normalization in tractor sales; secondary/replacement market demand was slightly less robust.
- Long-term demand continues to draw positive momentum from the post-GST reduction baseline set last year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (crude-based caprolactam and polymerized chips) increased significantly QoQ, driven by crude oil price volatility and Middle East war developments.
- Caprolactam prices ranged between USD 2,000 to USD 2,100; nylon chips ranged between USD 2,100 to USD 2,200.
- Energy cost pressures increased due to higher grid power tariffs at Bharuch and elevated LNG prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ongoing Capex includes Mother Yarn expansion at Pune, VAP capacity increase at Bharuch, energy-saving Capex at both locations, safety improvements, and old asset replacements.
- No immediate capacity expansion required on the nylon side; sufficient headroom exists (utilization typically 80%-90%).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased to ~Rs. 313 crore at the end of H1 FY27, primarily due to higher raw material prices rather than physical quantity increases.
- The company maintains a strong net cash position and limited borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments, ongoing wars, fluctuations in crude oil prices, deficit monsoons, inflation, and interest rate movements.
- Continued influx of competitively priced imports from China, particularly in base and low-grade filament yarn, constraining margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pratham Nayak asked about revenue growth drivers and sustainability:** Management stated growth was driven by volume and higher raw material realizations, noting that future pricing depends heavily on volatile raw material costs.
- **Dhruv Shah asked about power cost savings and capacity utilization:** Management guided for ~Rs. 10 crore annualized savings from hybrid power starting Q4 FY27, and noted capacity utilization was ~85% in Q2 with sufficient headroom up to 90%+.
- **Sanjay Patil asked about NTCF import impacts and demand outlook:** Management explained that Q1 uncertainty caused tyre companies to book excess imports as a risk mitigation exercise, which they expect to normalize.
- **Sakshi asked about normalized EBITDA margins:** Management stated normalized margins are targeted between 7% and 10%, explaining that high raw material prices temporarily compress percentage margins despite stable absolute profits.
- **Rahul Jain asked about export contribution and opportunities:** Management stated exports are 4% to 5% of turnover, highlighting future opportunities in EU and UK markets once FTAs are operationalized, aided by the 'Nuenyl' recycled yarn portfolio.

### One-line takeaway
Century Enka reported a 37% YoY revenue growth for Q2 FY27, but profitability normalized sequentially due to volatile raw material costs, higher energy tariffs, and import pressures, even as management prepares for PTCF commercialization and hybrid power savings in Q4 FY27.
