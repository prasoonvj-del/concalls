# Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by improved product contribution, operating efficiency, cost discipline, and significant growth in EBITDA and PAT.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,344 crore, up 10% YoY from Rs 1,222 crore
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 117.7 crore, up 46.4% YoY from Rs 80.4 crore
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 8.8%, expanded by 220 bps YoY from 6.6%
- **Profit After Tax - PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 74.1 crore, up 201.8% YoY from Rs 24.6 crore
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 5.5%, up from 2%
- **Finance Cost (Q2 FY27):** Rs 29 crore, down from Rs 33.6 crore
- **Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 2,284 crore, down 9.7% YoY from Rs 2,531 crore (impacted by supply chain issues)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 225.7 crore, up 24.9% YoY from Rs 180.7 crore
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 9.9%, expanded by 280 bps YoY from 7.1%
- **Profit After Tax - PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 137.8 crore, up 78.3% YoY from Rs 77.3 crore
- **PAT Margin (H1 FY27):** 6%, up from 3.1%
- **Net Cash from Operating Activities (H1 FY27):** Rs 328.5 crore, compared to ~Rs 92 crore in H1 FY26
- **Net Debt (as of Sep 30, 2026):** ~Rs 852 crore, down 23.5% from Rs 1,113 crore at the end of FY26
- **Debt-to-Equity (H1 FY27):** 1.31x, improved from 2.18x
- **Return on Equity - RoE (TTM):** 38.2%, up from 33.85% at the end of FY26
- **Return on Capital Employed - ROCE (TTM):** 27%, up from 20.34%
- **Specialty Expansion Capex:** ~Rs 200 crore approved by the board
- **Capacity Utilization:** FY23 at 58%, FY24 at 55%, FY25 at 58%, FY26 at 68%

### Highlights
- Delivered 8 consecutive quarters of profitability.
- Operational performance supported by better capacity utilization, cost control, and ramp-up of PET resin and textured yarn capabilities.
- Exports contributed 5.59% of total operating revenue, with a customer reach spanning 19 states/UTs in India and exports to 28 countries.
- Board approved ~Rs 200 crore additional investment across the company and its wholly-owned subsidiary (Indorama Ventures Yarns Private Limited) for specialty filament and specialty fiber production at Butibori.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the ~Rs 200 crore specialty expansion to deliver around 15% top-line growth and 20% EBITDA growth, with a payback period of less than 2 years (subject to execution, ramp-up, and market conditions).
- Commercial operations for the specialty expansion are expected within Calendar Year 2027.
- EBITDA margins are expected to be maintained around 9.9% (or double digits) in the next two quarters.
- Volume is expected to be around 4 lakh tonnes for the current year due to H1 supply chain impacts, returning to 4,75,000+ tonnes next year.
- Management expects full year turnover to exceed last year's level (FY26 revenue was ~Rs 5,000 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- China is gradually moving out of the commodity business, and currency depreciation (Chinese yuan moving from ~Rs 12 early 2025 to ~Rs 14.5) has reduced competitive pressure from Chinese imports.
- Cotton is stagnant in area and production, with India importing high amounts of cotton, creating tailwinds for polyester demand.
- India-UK trade agreement (effective July 2026) and upcoming India-EU FTA and US bilateral trade agreements expected to improve export opportunities.
- Domestic PTA capacity additions (IOCL's 1.2 million tonnes/annum at Paradip and GAIL's 1.25 million tonnes/annum at Mangalore) will broaden domestic sourcing and reduce import dependence.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Crude oil prices stood around USD 115; management stated that raw material price increases have been successfully passed on to customers.
- Raw material inventory is maintained at around 15 days to 1.5 months (approx. 30 days plus/minus). Price transmission lag is about 1 week to 10 days.
- Recycled PET chips, which earlier sold at an 8 to 10 Rs/kg premium over virgin chips, are currently cheaper due to virgin chip prices rising by Rs 30–40 over the last 6-8 months.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total manufacturing capacity at Butibori is 6.72 lakh tonnes per annum, including 2.31 lakh tonnes of polyester staple fiber (PSF), 1.7 lakh tonnes of draw textured yarn (DTY), and 2.28 lakh tonnes of bottle-grade PET resin.
- Captive power backup capacity stands at 71.08 MW with a sanctioned DISCOM load of 51 MW.
- The new ~Rs 200 crore capex will be funded through a combination of term loans and internal accruals. It includes adding extruders to make specialty yarns (cationic, colored, dope-dyed) and restarting a closed fiber line for hollow fiber.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced by 23.5% to ~Rs 852 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Financial priorities remain disciplined working capital management, healthy cash generation, and careful management of borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia pose risks to energy and petrochemical supply chains, impacting raw material availability, freight, and insurance costs due to disruptions around shipping routes like the Strait of Hormuz.
- Potential tight power supply conditions in India driven by data centers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dev Mehta (Unique PMS) on spreads and Chinese import pressure:** Management stated EBITDA margins averaged 9.9% in H1 and are expected to hold; Chinese competition has eased due to currency movement (Yuan moving from 12 to 14.5) and China exiting commodity businesses. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Udit Sehgal (PinpointX Capital) on capacity utilization and revenue potential:** Management clarified that PET chip plant is running at 60% while filament and fiber run at 100%. Volume is expected around 4 lakh tonnes this year, returning to 4,75,000+ tonnes next year. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Niraj Khandelwal (NMDT Family Office) on raw material inventory and pricing lag:** Management shared that inventory is held for ~30 days, and the price-passing lag is 7 to 10 days on variable market-priced contracts. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Rajesh (CP Investment) on historical cyclicality and structural changes:** Management highlighted that the removal of anti-dumping duty and QCOs on PTA/MEG, upcoming domestic PTA capacities, trade agreements (EU/US), and uncompetitiveness of China/Vietnam structurally support future profitability. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Ansh Khimavat (Capital One) on specialty project specifics:** Management detailed that the capex involves adding extruders for cationic, dope-dyed, and colored filament yarn (115-120 tpd + 70 tpd) and restarting a closed fiber line for hollow fiber. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Indo Rama Synthetics reported a robust Q2 FY27 with a 201.8% jump in PAT and 220 bps EBITDA margin expansion, underpinned by operational efficiencies, falling Chinese import pressure, and plans for a ~Rs 200 crore specialty expansion.
