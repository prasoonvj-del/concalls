# Kusumgar Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY growth driven by parachute contract execution, though management cautioned about inherent volatility in the business model.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 247.2 crore (vs Rs 122.5 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Revenue Growth:** ~102% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 75.9 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** ~31% (vs 22% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 42.6 crore (vs Rs 6.6 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT Margin:** ~17% (vs 5% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Capacity Utilization:** 55% to 60%.

### Highlights
*   Company listed on BSE/NSE on July 15, 2026.
*   Revenue growth driven by the execution of ready parachute contracts.
*   Management noted Q4 FY26 was an anomaly due to expedited export shipments following US tariff resolution; Q1 FY27 is considered more reflective of an average quarter.
*   Business operates across four segments: Aerospace & Defense (A&D) fabrics, A&D solutions, Industrial/Automotive fabrics, and Outdoor/Lifestyle fabrics.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management declined to provide formal guidance due to structural uncertainties regarding product approval cycles and government tender unpredictability.
*   Management expects FY27 EBITDA margins to be similar to FY25/FY26 levels.
*   Management expects a "steady, good year of growth" for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand is a mix of steady (consumables like uniforms) and surge (geopolitical events).
*   Management views the Middle East situation as a potential long-term tailwind for defense spending.
*   "China plus one" and Indian indigenization are cited as key growth drivers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin expansion of ~900 bps YoY attributed to a richer product mix (parachute contracts).
*   Sequentially, margins moderated from 41% in Q4 FY26 due to lower operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   No large capex planned for FY27; focus remains on maintenance capex (5-10% of gross block).
*   Management is exploring new business areas which may require future capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Operating cash flow is positive.
*   Receivables position has normalized following the Q4 FY26 anomaly.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Inherent unpredictability in government tenders and long product approval cycles.
*   Geopolitical volatility and potential tariff impacts, though management noted mitigation arrangements are in place.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Segmental revenue breakup?* A: Management stated they operate as a single segment; segmental reporting is not applicable.
*   *Q: Impact of Q4 spillover?* A: A significant portion of Q1 revenue came from parachute contracts; Q4 was an anomaly due to US tariff resolution.
*   *Q: Capacity utilization?* A: 55-60%; no major capex planned.
*   *Q: Order book?* A: Management declined to comment, citing various stages of orders (POs, LOIs, projections).
*   *Q: Competitive landscape?* A: Management views peers like Gliders India as partners/peers rather than direct competitors in all segments.

### One-line takeaway
Kusumgar delivered strong YoY growth in Q1 FY27, supported by parachute contract execution, while management maintains a conservative stance on guidance due to the inherent volatility of defense and government-linked business.
