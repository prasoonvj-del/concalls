# Arvind Fashions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA delivered, backed by strong channel performance, gross margin expansion, and robust festival demand.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,559 crore, up 10% YoY (nearly 15% adjusted for festive and online tower event shifts).
- **EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 216 crore vs Rs 187 crore in Q2 last year (up 15.5% YoY in absolute terms; 67 bps margin expansion).
- **PAT:** Rs 48 crore vs Rs 37 crore in Q2 last year (up 29% YoY).
- **Retail LTL growth:** 7.3% (10% adjusted for festive shifts).
- **Online B2C growth:** 28% YoY (45% adjusted for festive shifts).
- **Gross Margin Expansion:** Up 136 bps YoY.
- **Marketing Investment:** Up 24 bps YoY.
- **H1 Capex:** ~Rs 100 crore.
- **Expected H2 Free Cash Flow (FCF) release:** Rs 150-175 crore.
- **GWC days:** 157 days (flat QoQ).
- **NWC days:** 65 days (flat QoQ).

### Highlights
- Delivered highest ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA for Q2 ended September 30, 2026.
- Direct channels (D2C) mix reached 53% of sales, up nearly 380 bps YoY.
- Added 24 Exclusive Brand Outlets (EBOs) during the quarter.
- Launched the Tommy Hilfiger website and app in September; remaining brands to follow.
- Setting up a state-of-the-art shirt manufacturing facility in Madhya Pradesh (capacity 3 lakh units/month; meets 25-30% of total shirt requirements; payback expected in 3-3.5 years) to de-risk imports from Bangladesh and mitigate rupee depreciation.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of delivering guided growth and EBITDA objectives.
- H2 Pujo and Diwali sales are tracking well to expectations, clocking near high double-digit LTL.
- Expects to generate Rs 150-175 crore of free cash flow in H2 FY27, largely to be used to pare down debt.
- Gross opening target of ~150 stores, with 12% to 15% net square feet addition targeted for the fiscal year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand environment remains stable and positive with encouraging offtake across channels during the festive season.
- Consumption pattern remains positive during festive, with consumer sales across all channels showing encouraging offtake.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion driven by lower discounting, product innovation, premiumization, and changing channel mix.
- Raw material procurement cost inflation seen in the zone of 5% to 6%.
- Cost increases to be mitigated by an approximate increase of 1.5% to 2% in MRPs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex of ~Rs 100 crore spent on a new shirt manufacturing factory in MP, a new corporate office, and store expansion.
- Shirt factory will produce 3 lakh units/month at steady state.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory days increased, offset by a corresponding decline in receivable days; GWC days stable at 157 days and NWC days flat at 65 days.
- Net debt-to-EBITDA ratio expected to remain below one, moving towards very low levels due to strong FCF generation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Watchful of external headwinds: inflation, geopolitical developments, potential El Niño effect, and repo rate changes.
- Depreciating rupee adding to sourcing costs for dollar-denominated Bangladesh purchases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Avinash Karumanchi (MOFSL)** asked about the attribution of gross margin expansion. Management clarified that since they are in a long lead-time cycle, current gains are from discount improvement and channel mix, with new cost impacts arriving from SS27 inwards in January.
- **Karan Gupta (Asset C. Mehta Investment)** inquired about inventory and cost impact. Management stated cost increases will be about 5% and mitigated by 1.5% to 2% MRP hikes.
- **Kaustubh Pawaskar (ICICI Direct)** asked about competition from funded D2C brands. Management responded that their focus on strong brand positioning, product innovation, premiumization, and a direct-to-consumer strategy differentiates them from labels.
- **Rajiv B (Nuvama)** questioned the rationale for setting up an in-house manufacturing facility when group entities exist. Management and Kulin Lalbhai explained it is a strategic asset to ensure nimble supply chain design, shorter cycles, and de-risking from Bangladesh geopolitical issues.
- **Aabhash Poddar (Aionios Alpha Investment Management)** asked about Arrow's initiatives. Management stated Arrow is focused on modern professional wear with functional product innovation and direct-to-consumer acceleration.

### One-line takeaway
Arvind Fashions delivered record Q2 revenue and EBITDA driven by strong direct-to-consumer momentum, lower discounting, and robust festive demand, while initiating strategic in-house manufacturing to de-risk its supply chain.
