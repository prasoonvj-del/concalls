# S.P. Apparels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in achieving full-year guidance despite a soft Q1, citing strong order visibility for H2 and structural tailwinds like FTAs.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 401 crore (vs Rs 403 crore YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 61.4 crore (up 15.9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.3% (vs 13.1% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 24.9 crore (up 20.4% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 265.9 crore.
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 17.5%.
*   **SPUK Revenue:** Rs 33.3 crore (up 125.2% YoY).
*   **Retail Revenue:** Rs 18.83 crore (up 26.7% YoY).
*   **Total Order Book:** Rs 570 crore (SPAL: Rs 430 crore; YBA: Rs 100 crore; SPUK: Rs 40 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Corporate Actions:** Board approved a dividend of Rs 3 per share and a stock split (face value Rs 10 to Rs 2).
*   **Garment Division:** Remains the primary growth driver; improved product mix and yarn spreads supported margin expansion.
*   **Young Brand Apparels (YBA):** Production scaling continues; Palladum facility commenced, with full commercial production expected by October. Planning to introduce a new luxury molded bra product line (investment ~Rs 10 crore).
*   **SPUK:** Added 3 new brands; management targets GBP 13 million+ in 3 years and GBP 50 million in 5 years.
*   **Retail:** Angel & Rocket India achieved EBITDA breakeven.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management maintained FY27 consolidated revenue guidance of Rs 2,000 crore.
*   **Trajectory:** Growth is expected to be weighted towards H2, driven by order inflows, U.K. FTA traction, and normalized shipment schedules.
*   **Margins:** Management guided for >15% EBITDA margin for the garment division for the full year.
*   **Segment Targets:** YBA revenue target of Rs 340-350 crore; Infant export revenue target of Rs 1,300-1,400 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Global Sourcing:** Customers are actively evaluating India and Sri Lanka due to "China Plus-One" and "Bangladesh Plus-One" strategies.
*   **U.K. FTA:** Engagement from the U.K. market has improved, with better visibility for future order volumes.
*   **U.S. Market:** While some legislative uncertainty exists, management stated the current order book is safe through January.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Efficiency:** Standalone EBITDA margin improved to 17.5% due to operating discipline and product mix.
*   **Interest:** Q1 interest cost impacted by Rs 1.75 crore due to exchange volatility on packing credit; full-year interest guidance is Rs 30-35 crore.
*   **Logistics:** Air freight cost in SPUK was ~50,000 GBP.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity:** India added 750 machines in the current year; potential to add 300 more.
*   **Sri Lanka:** Currently operating 1,650 machines; planning to add 500-600 machines via job work.
*   **Total Scale:** Management stated the company is set for a 10,000-sewing machine capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Standalone Gross Debt:** Rs 258 crore.
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 46.6 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 211 crore (as of June 30, 2026).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **U.S. Tariff/Legislative Uncertainty:** Customers remain cautious regarding potential future bills, though current orders are secure.
*   **Q1 Softness:** Attributed to U.S. tariff impacts and shipment schedule spillover.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why was capacity utilization lower?* A: Management cited order slowness in March-May due to U.S. tariff issues.
*   *Q: Can you name the new customers?* A: Management declined until first shipments occur, but noted 2 from U.K. and 1 from EU; mentioned Marks & Spencer as a client.
*   *Q: Is the 17.6% margin in Garmenting sustainable?* A: Management guided for 15% for the year; current performance driven by efficiency and product mix.
*   *Q: How will you reach Rs 2,000 crore revenue from Rs 400 crore in Q1?* A: Management expects H2 to be stronger, supported by current order book and normalization of shipments.

### One-line takeaway
S.P. Apparels delivered a resilient Q1 with margin expansion despite soft revenue, maintaining its Rs 2,000 crore FY27 revenue guidance on the back of strong H2 order visibility and structural export tailwinds.
