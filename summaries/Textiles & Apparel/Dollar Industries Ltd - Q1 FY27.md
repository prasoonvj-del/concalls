# Dollar Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Textiles & Apparel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong EBITDA/PAT margin expansion and significant debt reduction were partially offset by flat top-line growth (1.4% YoY) and volume contraction (-1.6% YoY).

### Key numbers
* **Revenue/Operating Income:** Rs 405 crore, up 1.4% YoY.
* **Gross Profit:** Rs 151 crore, up 6.9% YoY; Gross margin expanded 192 bps YoY to 37.4%.
* **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 48 crore, up 11.4% YoY; EBITDA margin expanded 106 bps YoY to 11.8%.
* **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 26 crore, up 22.1% YoY; PAT margin expanded 108 bps YoY to 6.4%.
* **Diluted EPS:** Rs 4.59 vs Rs 3.76 in Q1 FY26.
* **Net Debt:** Rs 192 crore as of June 30, 2026 (reduced by Rs 85 crore from Rs 277 crore as of March 31, 2026).
* **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 160 days.

### Highlights
* Total volume declined 1.6% YoY in Q1 FY27, driven primarily by Dollar Man (-3% to -3.5%) and socks (-7%), while Dollar Woman grew 1.5%-2% and Dollar Always remained flat.
* Quick commerce channel value grew 59.4% YoY and volume grew 15.1% YoY, contributing 5% to revenue (vs 3.1% in Q1 FY26). Total online ecosystem (including standard e-commerce at 4.5%) contributed ~10%.
* Southern region revenue grew 22.9% in value and 7.3% in volume YoY, contributing 8.9% of overall revenue (aided by brand ambassador Mahesh Babu).
* Export revenue reached Rs 19 crore across 15 countries (up 16.2% value and 15.5% volume YoY), representing 4.9% of revenue.
* G.O.A.T JV partnership recorded revenue of Rs 16.44 crore (+21% YoY) with PAT of Rs 2.27 crore (PAT margin of 13.8%).
* Brand portfolio contribution: Dollar Always 44%, Dollar Man 38%, Dollar Woman (Missy) 8%, Dollar Protect 6%, Force NXT 4%.

### Guidance & outlook
* **FY27 Revenue:** Expected to grow between 11% and 13% YoY, targeted via double-digit volume plus value growth in the remaining three quarters.
* **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Expected between 11.5% and 12.5%.
* **Ad Spend:** Capped at Rs 100 crore annually; expected to settle at ~5% of revenue for FY27 (down from 5.5% in FY26), providing ~0.5% margin relief.
* **Force NXT:** Management targets 20% to 25% YoY revenue growth over the next 2-3 years.
* **G.O.A.T JV Revenue:** Targeted at Rs 65 to Rs 75 crore for FY27 (closed FY26 at ~Rs 50 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
* Price discipline is gradually improving after two years of intense deep discounting across the market, though full stabilization will take another 1-2 quarters.
* Low single-digit volume contraction occurred due to price increases taken in April 2026, buyer hesitation, and strict credit/supply controls.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Implemented a 4% to 5% price hike across product lines in April 2026. ASP increased for Dollar Man/Big Boss (Rs 82 to Rs 85), Dollar Always (Rs 47 to Rs 49), and Dollar Woman (Rs 100 to Rs 104).
* Q1 margins benefited from low-cost inventory processed prior to yarn/cotton price inflation in late February.
* Cotton and yarn prices have currently stabilized; management sees no need for additional price hikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* No major capital expenditure commitments planned in the near term.
* **Project Lakshya Phase-2:** Commenced rollout focusing on reactivating ~1 lakh inactive mapped retailers in existing states (Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Telangana, Rajasthan) rather than entering new states.
* Active Lakshya retailers stood at 80,000 in Q1 FY27 (enrolled base at 1,73,561 across 327 distributors in 14 states); targeting 90,000 active retailers by end of FY27.
* Developing a direct-to-consumer (D2C) website for Force NXT.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Net Debt to Equity reduced to 0.20x; Net Debt to Operating EBITDA improved to 1.01x.
* Generated ~Rs 96 crore positive cash flow in Q1 FY27; repaid ~Rs 86 crore debt.
* Management reiterates target to achieve total zero net debt by FY28 (working capital comprises 90% of total debt).
* Working capital targeted to drop by 6-7 days in FY27 and by 15-18 days over 3 years (to 130-135 days), driven primarily by debtor day reduction (targeting 85-90 days).

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Near-term top-line volume friction (-1.6% in Q1 FY27) post price increases and competitive market discounting.
* Pause on expanding Project Lakshya into new states (e.g., UP) due to potential market disruption lasting 5-6 months per state rollout.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit AM):** Asked if recent price hikes are sustainable and what the full-year guidance is.  
  *Management Answer:* Pricing stabilization will take a quarter or two, but processes have begun. Management guided for 11%-13% revenue growth and 11.5%-12.5% EBITDA margins for FY27.
* **Prerna Jhunjhunwala (Elara Securities):** Inquired about the sustainability of margin gains and further price hike needs.  
  *Management Answer:* Expansion was driven by a 4-5% price hike and strict control over extra market discounting. Cotton and yarn prices have stabilized, eliminating the need for further price hikes.
* **Gunit Singh (Counter Cyclical PMS):** Asked about the timeline for zero debt and suggested a share buyback.  
  *Management Answer:* Confirmed company is on track for zero net total debt by FY28 after repaying Rs 86 crore in Q1. Agreed to take the share buyback suggestion under advisement.
* **Shubhankar Gupta (Equitree Capital):** Asked about celebrity endorsement costs and Lakshya working capital metrics.  
  *Management Answer:* Celebrities (Akshay Kumar, Saif Ali Khan, Yami Gautam, Mahesh Babu) account for 2%-3% of total ad spend; media buying is the bulk cost. Lakshya states yield better debtor days and higher ROI for distributors (17%-24%).
* **Ashwin Reddy (Samatva Investments):** Asked about Phase-2 Lakshya changes and new state entries.  
  *Management Answer:* Phase-2 focuses on reactivating existing inactive retailers rather than adding distributors. Paused entering large new states (like UP) temporarily to avoid 5-6 months of sales disruption during hyper-competitive market conditions.

### One-line takeaway
Dollar Industries traded market volume growth in Q1 FY27 for strong operational execution, expanding margins via calibrated price hikes and lowering debt toward its zero-net-debt FY28 target.
