# Navneet Education Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth and curriculum super cycle momentum, offset by margin pressures in stationery and losses in Indiannica.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Up ~31%-32% YoY from INR246 crores to INR324 crores.
- **Consolidated Operational Revenue:** Up 31% YoY to INR324 crores.
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Up 150% YoY to INR20 crores; margins expanded from 3.3% to 6.2%.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR11 crores; margins expanded from 0.4% to 3.4% YoY.
- **Standalone PAT:** INR6 crores (shift from a net loss of INR6 crores YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** INR1,109 crores.
- **H1 FY27 Standalone PAT:** INR154 crores.
- **Publication Segment Revenue:** Up 53.8% YoY to INR140 crores (partly driven by Q1 spill-over); H1 Publication revenue up 6.7% to INR544 crores.
- **Total Stationery Revenue:** Up 18.7% YoY to INR184 crores.
- **Domestic Stationery Revenue:** INR52 crores (up ~34% YoY).
- **Export Stationery Revenue:** INR132 crores.
- **Indiannica Revenue:** ~INR1 crore (impacted by sales returns); Loss: ~INR9.5 crores.
- **K12 Techno Stake Sale Proceeds:** INR330 crores received; additional INR70 crores expected in 15-20 days; remaining stake post-transaction will be around 8%.
- **Paper Price Increase:** Increased by INR2,000 per tonne (~3%) in the last month.

### Highlights
- Management stated they are introducing India's first educator-led AI and unlocking the curriculum super cycle (began in FY26, continuing over next two financial years).
- Domestic stationery growth driven by aggressive strategy focusing on high-margin branding and non-paper stationery verticals (introduced ~70+ new items).
- Export stationery showed signs of revival; additional discounts given due to US tariffs have been discontinued as of June/July.
- K12 Techno partial stake sale proceeds realized (INR330 crores received in Q3); special dividend or buyback options under board consideration.
- Indiannica to be merged into Navneet Education Limited to streamline operations and save costs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a U-shaped recovery in exports in the near term.
- Management expects minimum 15% growth in the publication segment next year driven by higher grade curriculum changes (7th, 9th, and 11th in Maharashtra; 4 grades in Gujarat).
- Full-year FY27 stationery margins expected to be around 2% lower YoY due to export pricing impacts in early H1.
- H2 FY27 export stationery revenue estimated at around INR250 crores and domestic stationery at around INR200 crores (rough numbers).
- Paper mills indicating incremental pricing pressure of 5% to 10% over the next 1 year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- US retail intake of stationery has reduced due to inflation, leading Navneet to expand its product categories.
- US tariffs stabilized after announcements in August; a flat 10% tariff applies across countries/products without sidelining Indian products.
- Government restrictions mandate private publishers can only deliver books 1 month after government textbook introductions, causing some school purchasing delays (estimated INR20-INR30 crores delayed to next year).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Domestic stationery margins dragged down by branding activities, a one-time GST reversal on existing inventory, and higher manpower costs for non-paper stationery.
- Export profitability impacted in H1 due to discounted pricing given last year following US tariffs (discounts discontinued in June/July).
- A new plastic products plant faced delays and fixed cost/depreciation pressures due to surging raw material prices amid geopolitical conflicts.
- Paper prices increased by INR2,000 per tonne in the last month; heavy buying season runs from current month through April.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- A new plant for plastic products was commissioned but faced sales delays due to raw material price volatility.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Received INR330 crores from K12 Techno stake sale; another INR70 crores expected shortly. Proceeds to be evaluated for special dividend, buybacks, or inorganic growth by the board next quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in paper prices (potential 5% to 10% increase projected by mills).
- Geopolitical tensions and export demand dynamics.
- Losses in Indiannica (central curriculum business) though narrowing/offsetting expected via upcoming merger.
- Government regulations restricting private book sales until 1 month post state text-book launches.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Himanshu Upadhyay (SteadFort Investment Managers) asked about the sudden confidence in export recovery and margin erosion in domestic stationery.**
   - *Management answered* that US recovery is driven by new category additions, and US tariff uncertainties have subsided. Domestic stationery margins were impacted by one-time GST reversals, aggressive branding/marketing, and new non-paper stationery manpower costs.
2. **Nilesh Doshi asked about Indiannica's ongoing losses and rationale for staying in central curriculum.**
   - *Management answered* that central curriculum is strategic for long-term K12 education. To solve quarterly loss volatility and reduce indirect costs (estimated at INR5-6 crores annually), Indiannica is being merged into Navneet.
3. **Arihant (Bowhead Investment Advisors) asked about curriculum change grades for next year and paper price trends.**
   - *Management answered* that 7th, 9th, and 11th grades in Maharashtra and 4 grades in Gujarat change next year, driving an expected >15% growth in publishing. Paper prices rose by INR2,000/tonne (~3%) in the last month.
4. **Praneeth asked about the expected quantum of cost savings from the Indiannica merger.**
   - *Management answered* that marketing, printing, and content creation synergies will save approximately INR5 crores to INR6 crores annually.
5. **Vidisha asked about segment margin bifurcation and remaining stake in K12 Techno.**
   - *Management answered* that exact margins cannot be derived due to common facilities, though exports are generally more profitable. Navneet will hold around an 8% stake in K12 Techno post all transactions.

### One-line takeaway
Navneet Education reported robust Q2 standalone revenue growth of ~31%-32% and a return to net profit, driven by the publication curriculum cycle and domestic stationery expansion, despite temporary margin drags from export discounts, branding investments, and Indiannica losses.
