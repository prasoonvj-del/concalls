# CL Educate Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - EBITDA margins expanded by 300 bps YoY, PAT returned to positive with a 350% YoY growth, and overhead cost optimization yielded significant savings.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 305 crore in H1 FY27 (down 4% YoY from Rs 319 crore in H1 FY26).
- EBITDA: Rs 57 crore in H1 FY27 (up from Rs 50 crore in H1 FY26).
- EBITDA Margin: 18.6% in H1 FY27 (up 300 bps YoY from 15.7%).
- PAT: Returned to positive with a 350% YoY growth in H1 FY27.
- Overhead Cost Savings: Rs 12.5 crore in H1 FY27; projected at Rs 20 to 22 crore for the remaining part of H2 FY27.
- DEXIT Multi-year Contract Wins: Aggregating to about Rs 172 crore (to be delivered over the next 2-3 years, with ~Rs 40 crore accruing in the current year).
- DEXIT Capital Reduction: Preference shares of about Rs 203 crore redeemed and repaid to NSE Investments in August 2026.
- Long-term Acquisition Debt Outstanding: Approximately Rs 174 crore as of 30th September 2026.
- Short-term Working Capital Requirements: Around Rs 55 to 60 crore.
- Annual Debt Repayment: Rs 24 to 28 crore annually.
- Potential Debt Refinancing: Term sheet received for Rs 100 crore debt raise via listed NCDs.
- Tax Savings Post-Merger: Estimated between Rs 11 to 15 crore annually for the next three years.
- Administrative Savings Post-Merger: Estimated between Rs 3 to 5 crore annually.

### Highlights
- Completed NCLT approval and closure of DEXIT Redeemable Preference Shares (RPS) redemption of ~Rs 203 crore, trimming the balance sheet.
- Registered office successfully shifted to Mumbai; ROC Mumbai approved Form INC-22.
- Merger scheme between CL Educate and DEXIT approved by both boards; filing with NCLT expected in November 2026.
- MarTech international revenues accounted for 40% and India revenues 60%; executed Dell Forum at Jio World Convention Centre and secured global implementation wins with Infosys.
- DEXIT secured new customer wins including Edsel, IIT Madras (GATE exam implementation), GPSC, and Bihar Civil Services; renewed contracts with IIBF and DGFMS.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 numbers to look significantly better than the last H2.
- EdTech platform monetization vertical (CL Media) expected to see a solid H2 and significant revenue growth due to delayed admission cycles starting in October.
- DEXIT revenues expected to expand in H2 over the previous year driven by implementation of multi-year contract wins.
- UG segment expected to become a bigger share of the pie compared to PG.
- EdTech revenues likely to remain around current levels for another year before increasing as UG volumes and market share grow (management commentary).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Test prep market facing a flattening trajectory across multiple segments, affecting most players.
- Admission cycle seasonality has shifted; college admissions now run through the end of September, pushing the business cycle start to October.
- NEET exam announcement by the government to move to CBT (Computer Based Test) mode creates a huge future opportunity for digital assessments (DEX). Offline assessments market size was ~Rs 2,500 crore in 2023, expected to grow to ~Rs 3,500 crore.
- UG vs PG behavior: UG is mandatory and parents typically pay (higher propensity to pay, longer programs), whereas PG is optional and students pay themselves (shorter programs).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Service delivery costs declined pro-rata with revenue.
- Overhead costs optimized by Rs 12.5 crore in H1 FY27 across people, infrastructure, and other overheads; total cost savings of Rs 20 to 22 crore expected in H2.
- EdTech EBITDA margin expanded by ~450 bps to close to 22% (from 17.5%) driven by cost optimization, infrastructure rationalization, and a higher mix of franchise network partners yielding higher ARPU.
- DEX EBITDA margin expanded by 171 bps YoY in H1.
- MarTech EBITDA grew over 26.7% with a margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Increasing DEX capacity to be eligible for NEET and other large online exams.
- Exploring pilots for internal corporate exams via DEXIT with 3 to 5 customers in BFSI and IT sectors.
- mySathi platform executing pilots; ~15,000 tests executed to date, targeting 25,000-35,000 students by the end of the year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet size trimmed by over Rs 200 crore following DEXIT capital reduction.
- Term sheet received for Rs 100 crore debt raise via listed NCDs to refinance existing long-term acquisition lenders over the next 4 to 6 weeks.
- Annual debt repayment runs at Rs 24 to 28 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Flattening trajectory and headwinds in the test prep market affecting the industry.
- Base effect decline in EdTech and DEXIT revenues in H1 FY27 due to rollover contracts from FY25 executing in Q1 FY26.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Merger route and timeline:** Management stated they chose the NCLT route over the fast-track route because timeframes are comparable; advisors estimate NCLT approval will take 9 to 15 months (targeting completion by Sept/Oct 2027).
- **DEXIT IPO status:** Management noted that while an IPO is ruled out in the short run if the merger proceeds, they remain open to exploring attractive equity funding offers or cheques for DEXIT on merit during the 12-15 month journey.
- **IIT GATE contract scope:** Management clarified that the contract is not exclusive; order volumes are split with the existing vendor, awarded based on technical qualifications and pricing.
- **NEXIT / Corporate exam expansion:** Management indicated initial pilots are underway with 3-5 corporate clients in BFSI and IT; broader commercialization will follow in 2 quarters.
- **Debt repayment and Net Debt goal:** Management reiterated their desire to be a net debt-free company as soon as possible, continuing repayments of Rs 24-30 crore annually against long-term acquisition debt of ~Rs 174 crore.

### One-line takeaway
CL Educate delivered an expanding EBITDA margin (up 300 bps YoY to 18.6%) and returned to positive PAT growth in H1 FY27 despite a 4% revenue dip, aided by aggressive overhead optimization and strong multi-year contract wins in its DEXIT and MarTech segments.
