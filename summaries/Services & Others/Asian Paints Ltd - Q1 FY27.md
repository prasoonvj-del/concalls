# Asian Paints Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive – Strong volume growth and margin performance, though management remains cautious regarding raw material volatility and high competitive intensity.

### Key numbers
*   **Volume Growth:** 9% YoY.
*   **Decorative Value Growth:** 16.6% YoY.
*   **Industrial Business Growth:** 16.5% YoY (includes Auto and General Industrial).
*   **Weighted Average Price Increase:** ~6.8% - 7%.
*   **New Product Contribution:** 17% of overall revenues.
*   **Standalone PBDIT Margin:** 22%.
*   **Consolidated PBDIT Margin:** 20.6% (+240 bps YoY).
*   **International Business Growth:** 27% YoY.
*   **International PBT Margin:** 7.9% (+275 bps YoY).
*   **Auto OE PBT Margin:** 15.7% (-119 bps YoY).
*   **General Industrial PBT Margin:** 6.9% (lower YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Demand Drivers:** Rural markets outperformed urban markets; B2B business remains strong, driven by infrastructure projects (airports, ports, bullet trains).
*   **Innovation:** Launched Anti-Damp technology, color warranty, and "Emporio Orano" (luxury product). New products contributed 17% to revenue.
*   **Services:** Expanded 'Beautiful Homes Painting Service', 'Total Assure' (B2B), 'Smart Assure' (waterproofing), and 'MetaCare' (asset protection).
*   **Backward Integration:** White cement plant (Fujairah) is fully operational. Phase 1 of the VAM-VAE manufacturing ecosystem commences in August 2026.
*   **Decor Business:** Operates 74 'Beautiful Home Stores'.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Management targets 8-10% volume growth for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management maintains PBDIT margin guidance of 18-20%.
*   **Pricing:** Management does not intend to take further price increases unless the situation becomes "alarming."
*   **Industrial Outlook:** Management expects the industrial business to grow at a higher pitch than the decorative business.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management believes they grew slightly higher than the industry average.
*   Competitive intensity is described as "all-time high" across all segments (economy, premium, and luxury).
*   Demand in T1/T2 cities was lower than expected, compensated by B2B growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Material inflation was ~25%.
*   **Margins:** Gross margin pressure from inflation was partially offset by price increases and the benefit of low-cost inventory in Q1.
*   **Volatility:** Management noted raw material prices (crude derivatives, TiO2) remain volatile, though some monomers started showing price declines in June.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **VAM-VAE Plant:** Phase 1 starts August 2026; management expects to reach 150,000 MT capacity over 2-2.5 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Inventory days improved; raw material inventory reflects higher costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro/Geopolitical:** Renewed conflict and volatility in raw material prices (crude derivatives).
*   **Supply Chain:** Logistics challenges regarding freight availability and price fluctuations.
*   **International:** Ethiopia operations faced currency devaluation challenges.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on demand:** Asked if 9% volume growth is satisfactory. Management noted demand was decent but would have preferred more vibrancy in T1/T2 cities; noted price hikes may have caused some preponement of demand.
*   **Avi Mehta (Macquarie) on FY27 outlook:** Asked for full-year volume guidance. Management reiterated the 8-10% band for FY27.
*   **Mihir Shah (Nomura) on mix:** Asked if gross margin expansion was due to product mix. Management confirmed a focus on premiumization and noted a +3% impact from a favorable product mix.
*   **Aditya Bhartia (Investec) on Q2 margins:** Asked about potential margin compression. Management noted Q2 traditionally has lower margins due to product mix and that inflation effects will be more visible in Q2.
*   **Jay Doshi (Kotak) on VAM-VAE:** Asked about capacity utilization. Management stated it will take 2-2.5 years to reach 150,000 MT capacity and that the output can be used across various categories, not just premium.

### One-line takeaway
Asian Paints delivered strong Q1 FY27 volume growth of 9% and healthy margins, supported by premiumization and innovation, while navigating high raw material inflation and intense competition.
