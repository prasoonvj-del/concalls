# ECOS (India) Mobility & Hospitality Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - EBITDA margins fell below guidance due to higher-than-anticipated operating costs and intense competitive pricing pressure, particularly in employee transportation services.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 2,113.72 million (up 16.7% YoY, up ~2.2% QoQ)
- **Trip volumes:** ~1.48 million trips (up 27% YoY, up ~7% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 218.47 million (vs Rs 219.18 million in Q1 FY26, Rs 241.53 million in Q4 FY26)
- **EBITDA margin:** 10.3% (vs 12.0% in Q1 FY26, 11.7% in Q4 FY26)
- **Profit before tax (PBT):** Rs 194.04 million (vs Rs 186.68 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 145.50 million (vs Rs 132.87 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Employee benefit expenses:** Rs 237.63 million (up 21.9% YoY)
- **Other expenses:** Rs 70.32 million (vs Rs 81.54 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Cost of services:** Up 20.7% YoY
- **Cash and investments:** Rs 1,558 million as of June 30, 2026
- **New clients added:** 61 clients (total active enterprise clients: 1,400, up ~18% YoY)
- **Cities footprint:** 151 cities (added 20 new cities in Q1)
- **Fleet size:** ~19,500 vehicles (owned and vendor-operated combined)
- **EV fleet:** 460 vehicles (vs 390 at end of Q4 FY26)
- **Online/App booking share:** 14%
- **Segment revenue contribution:** ETS at 59%, CCR at 41%

### Highlights
- Expanded domestic footprint to 151 cities across India and international network to over 100 countries.
- Onboarded 61 new clients (15 in ETS, 46 in CCR); active enterprise client base reached 1,400.
- 51% of revenue continues to come from customers associated with ECOS for more than five years.
- Upgraded proprietary technology platform and launched direct web booking portal; planning B2C app launch in Q1/current quarter.
- Board recommended a final dividend of Rs 2.38 per equity share for FY26.
- Hired a senior professional in strategic finance in June to evaluate inorganic acquisition opportunities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised FY27 EBITDA margin guidance to **around 10%** (lowered from the initial 11% to 13% range) due to competitive pricing pressure.
- Revenue growth guidance maintained at **15% to 18%**.
- Employee cost expected to grow by around 20% for the year due to leadership and talent strengthening.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ETS benefited from stable demand from IT/ITeS customers and GCCs.
- CCR saw a healthy recovery in corporate and travel-related mobility demand, though inbound international travelers remain on the lower side.
- Corporate mobility in India remains highly fragmented, with only 15% to 20% organized.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins compressed due to higher-than-anticipated operating costs and intense competitive pricing pressure, especially in ETS.
- Gross margins declined in ETS as ECOS absorbed part of the lower client rates against vendor costs to win business.
- Other expenses dropped YoY due to the non-recurrence of a one-time provision for doubtful debts recorded in the previous year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Maintained an asset-light model with the vast majority of the ~19,500 vehicle fleet coming from vendor partners.
- Daily vehicle utilization runs between 10,000 to 11,000 vehicles, with the remainder kept for demand spikes.
- Taking a measured approach to EV adoption (460 EVs currently) pending vehicle reliability, charging infrastructure, and favorable economics.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and investments stood at Rs 1,558 million as of June 30, 2026, indicating low leverage and a strong financial position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Intense competitive pressure and pricing aggression in the market, particularly within ETS, impacting gross and EBITDA margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jigar Jani (Nuvama PCG Research) asked what changed drastically from Q4/early guidance to force a margin cut to 10% and what is driving pricing pressure.** Management answered that competition was higher than anticipated, especially in ETS, prompting internal margin thresholds to be revised.
2. **Pulkit Singhal (Institutional Investor) asked for details on cost-cutting/automation measures and quantified savings.** Management explained that automation spans contact centers, dispatch, billing, and apps, which will primarily reduce employee costs and improve vehicle utilization, though specific savings were not quantified.
3. **Swechha Jain (ANS Wealth) asked why gross margins declined and whether they dropped in both ETS and CCR.** Management clarified that gross margin compression was more evident in ETS due to lower client realization rates forced by competition.
4. **Swechha Jain asked about client churn and if any major clients were lost.** Management stated they did not lose major clients to competition and successfully grew revenues by 17%.
5. **Samay Shah (Institutional Investor) asked when operational efficiencies will translate to margin enhancement and at what revenue scale.** Management indicated operating leverage will start kicking in at revenue scales over Rs 1,000 crores.
6. **Saloni Shah asked about the key drivers behind the 20% expected increase in employee costs.** Management attributed this to building leadership bandwidth for future growth and handling operational scale.

### One-line takeaway
ECOS reported a 16.7% YoY revenue increase in Q1 FY27, but lowered its FY27 EBITDA margin guidance to ~10% due to severe competitive pricing pressure in employee transportation services.
