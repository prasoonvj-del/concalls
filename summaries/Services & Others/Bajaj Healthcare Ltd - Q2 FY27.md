# Bajaj Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong revenue growth (18.9% YoY), successful launch of Cenobamate, and clear strategic progress in specialty segments (peptides/oncology) despite geopolitical headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth (10-15%) and EBITDA margin (18-20%) targets.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted focus from domestic API reliance to formulation and export momentum; management highlighted a strategic pivot toward a "professional, institutional-driven" organization.
- **New topics or risks:** NCLT approval for Genrx; commercial launch of Cenobamate; appointment of a new senior leadership team (COO, Institutional Sales, Global API, R&D, etc.); increased inventory due to West Asia crisis.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on Cenobamate launch (September); received NCLT approval for Genrx; R&D facility operational (August).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 175.9 crore (+18.9% YoY, +6.2% QoQ).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 341.5 crore (+15.1% YoY).
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 85.4 crore (+12.4% YoY).
- **Gross Margin:** 48.3%.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 32.1 crore (+12.2% YoY, +8.6% QoQ).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 18.2% (vs 17.8% in Q1).
- **PAT:** Rs 14.1 crore (+26.9% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 27.8 crore (+21.1% YoY).
- **Revenue Mix:** API Export and Formulation combined now >60% of total revenue.

### Highlights
- **Formulations:** Revenue grew 82% YoY; business vertical established for branded generic exports.
- **API Exports:** Revenue grew 28.5% YoY.
- **Cenobamate:** First company in India to receive DCGI approval; launched in September via 8 marketing partners.
- **Acquisitions:** Received NCLT approval for Genrx Pharmaceuticals to strengthen formulation capabilities.
- **Regulatory:** 12 CEPs filed (8 approved); 17 KDMF registrations; 3 products registered in Egypt.
- **Leadership:** Appointed new COO, and Heads for Institutional Sales, Global API, R&D, ROW Formulations, and Oncology Sales.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Target:** Management aims for Rs 900-1,000 crore by FY29.
- **EBITDA Margin Target:** 18-21% by FY29.
- **Peptide Facility:** Commercial production scheduled for Q2 FY28 (expected revenue Rs 70-100 crore in first year; Rs 300 crore cumulative post-FY29).
- **Oncology API Plant:** Commencing Q2 FY28 (expected revenue ~Rs 200 crore in 2-3 years).
- **Formulation Plant (Nashik):** Expected operational by Q1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Domestic API:** Moderated in Q2 but grew 10.1% YoY in H1.
- **Pricing:** API pricing remains under pressure; management expects a "straight line" trend.
- **Product Mix:** Management expects formulation business to reach 45-50% of revenue mix in 3-4 years.
- **Magnesium L-Threonate:** Contributes 11-12% of total turnover.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **R&D Spend:** Increased to ~2.5-3% of revenue.
- **Margins:** EBITDA margin improved sequentially (17.8% to 18.2%) driven by favorable product mix (exports/formulations).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **FY27 Capex:** Guidance of Rs 65-80 crore.
- **Annual Capex:** Management plans to infuse Rs 50-60 crore annually for growth.
- **Peptide Capacity:** 250 kg/annum.
- **R&D Team:** Currently 80+ professionals; target is 120.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Worth:** Rs 557 crore (as of Sept 30, 2026).
- **Debt-to-Equity:** ~0.5x.
- **Receivable Turnover:** Improved from 145 to 135 days.
- **Inventory:** Increased due to strategic stocking amid West Asia crisis.
- **Rating:** India Ratings upgraded long-term rating to IND A Stable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Geopolitical:** West Asia crisis impacting supply chain and necessitating higher inventory levels.
- **Market Volatility:** API pricing and demand pressure.
- **Regulatory:** Timelines for new product approvals (2-3 years).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why the inventory increase?** Management: Strategic decision to ensure supply continuity due to the West Asia conflict.
- **Q: Revenue potential of Genrx?** Management: Expects Rs 200-250 crore in 2-3 years.
- **Q: Oncology API revenue potential?** Management: Expects ~Rs 200 crore in 2-3 years post-operation.
- **Q: Retention of senior hires?** Management: Using performance-based incentives and ESOPs.
- **Q: Cenobamate revenue?** Management: Declined to quantify, but noted repeat orders indicate positive secondary sales.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Healthcare is aggressively scaling its specialty portfolio (Peptides, Oncology, Formulations) and strengthening its leadership team, with management targeting Rs 900-1,000 crore revenue by FY29.
