# Sunrakshakk Industries India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strongest quarter in the company's history with robust broad-based demand and revenue growth, despite input cost headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 276.33 crore, up 120.64% YoY (vs Rs 125.24 crore in Q1 FY26) and 39.85% QoQ (vs Rs 197.59 crore in Q4 FY26).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 22.59 crore, up 94.41% YoY (vs Rs 11.62 crore in Q1 FY26) and 12.16% sequentially.
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 8.18% (vs 9.28% in Q1 FY26 and 10.19% in Q4 FY26).
- **FMCG Segment EBITDA Margin:** 8.55% (vs 7.90% in Q1 FY26).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 15.04 crore, up 130.67% YoY (vs Rs 6.52 crore in Q1 FY26) and 24.30% QoQ (vs Rs 12.11 crore implied).
- **PAT Margin:** 5.44% (vs 5.21% in Q1 FY26 and 6.12% in Q4 FY26).
- **Basic EPS:** Rs 4.85, up 101.24% YoY (vs Rs 2.41 in Q1 FY26).
- **Capacity:** Aggregate FMCG and FMCG intermediate capacity increased to 20,840 tonnes per month (from 19,640 tonnes per month in Q4 FY26).

### Highlights
- FMCG, FMCG intermediate, and edible businesses contributed approximately 90.60% of consolidated revenue (up from ~83% in FY26).
- Commissioned a new soap production line at the Roorkee facility, adding ~1,700 metric tonnes of monthly capacity.
- Segment contributions: Roorkee facility contributed Rs 27.50 crore (soap) and Rs 87.62 crore (noodle); Bhilwara contributed Rs 40.98 crore (home care/detergent) and Rs 30.34 crore (edible); Guwahati contributed Rs 26.15 crore (cosmetics) and Rs 37.75 crore (noodle).
- Current capacity utilization is around 50% to 55%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a medium-term aspiration to achieve approximately Rs 1,000 crores in revenue by FY28 (targeting Rs 900 crores to Rs 1,000 crores for FY27).
- Expects a revenue growth of 15% to 20% in FY28 over FY27.
- Profitability expected to close around 6% for FY27, improving by an additional 0.75% to 1% in FY28.
- Long-term steady-state EBITDA margin expected to increase by another 2% to 2.5% from current levels by FY28.
- Textile business contribution expected at 8% to 10% over the long term.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Witnessed strong broad-based demand across FMCG, FMCG intermediate, and edible businesses.
- Operates primarily on a B2B model with long-term commitments and monthly firm orders from over 200 customers.
- Spices margins are on the lower side, while savories margins are higher.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Geopolitical tensions caused crude oil and crude-linked derivative price volatility, impacting input costs, packing materials (PP spikes), and supply chain planning.
- Textile business was heavily impacted due to high dyes, chemical, fuel, and boiler costs without the ability to pass on price hikes; raw material costs like LABSA were also affected.
- Margin moderation QoQ and YoY attributed to higher raw material costs; management expects normalization by Q3 or Q4.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Most major expansion activities are completed; no major capex for capacity addition expected for the next 1 to 1.5 years (only balancing capex).
- Funds raised via preferential issue were deployed towards acquiring the Bhilwara food facility and Guwahati soap noodle/cosmetic facility. Roorkee expansion funded via subsidiary's retained earnings.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital is maintained at a balanced level; management aims to optimize further to prevent it from rising in line with revenue growth.
- No immediate plans for additional fundraising.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Crude oil and crude-linked raw material volatility (LABSA, PP packaging).
- Boiler fuel, dyes, and chemical price inflation affecting the textile segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shravan Modi (Syndicate Family Office) asked about crude-linked input cost behavior and impact:** Management detailed the impact on dyes, chemicals, LABSA, and PP packaging costs, noting severe pressure on the textile business.
- **Mudit (Individual Investor) asked about textile business demerger plans:** Management stated they have no demerger plans and expect the textile business to recover by Q3.
- **Muskan Patel (JK Investments) asked about capacity utilization and headroom:** Management replied that current utilization is 50%-55% and expects another 25%-30% increase by FY28 without fresh major capex.
- **Aniket Redkar (Individual Investor) asked about sustainability of EBITDA margins:** Management indicated EBITDA margins will expand by 2% to 2.5% by FY28 driven by operating leverage and higher capacity utilization.
- **Seema Mishra (Individual Investor) asked about utilization of Rs 98.65 crore preferential funds:** Management clarified funds were deployed into Bhilwara food and Guwahati soap noodle/cosmetic facilities, not for Sunrakshakk Agro acquisition.

### One-line takeaway
Sunrakshakk Industries reported its strongest quarter to date with a 120.64% YoY jump in revenue, driven by its strategic pivot to an integrated FMCG platform, even as raw material headwinds compressed margins.
