# Black Buck Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management reported strong growth in revenue and EBITDA despite macro headwinds in April, with significant acceleration in the "growth" business segment.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** 38% YoY growth.
*   **Revenue from Operations:** 42% YoY growth (10% QoQ).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 55 crore (16% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA:** ~Rs 50 crore (23% YoY growth).
*   **PAT:** Rs 42 crore (25% YoY growth).
*   **Monthly Average Transacting Customers:** ~900,000 (13% YoY growth).
*   **Tolling GTV:** 16% YoY growth.
*   **Contribution Margin:** 93%.
*   **Super Loads Sequential Growth:** 44% (accelerated from 20% in the previous quarter).

### Highlights
*   **Telematics:** Achieved highest-ever sale of new devices in a single quarter.
*   **Super Loads:** Expanded presence to 14 cities (up from 4). Business is evolving as an AI-led model, driving productivity gains.
*   **AI Integration:** Management reported an 85% reduction in headcount for KYC desks and 65-70% cost reduction in specific processes due to AI implementation.
*   **User Engagement:** App usage remains stable at ~45 minutes daily.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Vehicle Finance:** Management expects the business to converge into profitability by the end of FY27.
*   **Super Loads:** Management plans to continue aggressive investment to mature the first four cities and scale newer ones.
*   **Taxation:** Management expects the deferred tax asset impact to continue for the next two quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Tolling:** Experienced a sharp contraction in April due to macro headwinds, but business-as-usual (BAU) has largely been restored.
*   **Fueling:** Seeing partial recovery, but management remains cautious until the business stabilizes.
*   **Competition:** Management views the entry of other players (e.g., Delhivery) into the space as a positive sign of industry potential and does not see a near-term threat.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Operating Leverage:** 60-85% of revenue growth is converting into EBITDA.
*   **Depreciation:** Increased due to upfront investment in telematics devices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Super Loads:** Targeting 5,000 loads/month per hub to achieve maturity. Current playbook building is progressing, with newer cities scaling faster than the initial four.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **PAT Variance:** The sequential decline in PAT was attributed to the recognition of a deferred tax asset in the previous quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Volatility:** High volatility in logistics movement observed in April.
*   **Fueling Uncertainty:** Continued caution regarding the fueling business due to price and demand uncertainty.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On AI Productivity (Atul Borse):** Management explained their AI framework: "New-New" (AI-first efforts), "Old-New" (improving existing processes, e.g., KYC), and "Old-Old" (physical tasks). AI-enabled placements now account for 40-50% of daily loads in Super Loads.
*   **On Super Loads Maturity (Monica Joshi):** Management noted that while they are not yet at the 5,000 loads/month target for all hubs, they are "more than halfway there" and newer cities are scaling faster than the initial ones.
*   **On Telematics Renewals (Monica Joshi):** First-year renewals are in the early 70% range, while second and third-year renewals stabilize in the 80% range. Higher-end products (e.g., fuel sensors) see 5-10% higher renewal rates.
*   **On GTV Metric Change (Lokesh Manik):** Management clarified that the GTV figure now represents only tolling; the fueling component was removed due to uncertainty in that business.

### One-line takeaway
BlackBuck delivered strong operational growth and margin stability, with management successfully leveraging AI to drive productivity and scaling its "growth" businesses (Super Loads and Vehicle Finance) despite macro-induced volatility in the core logistics sector.
