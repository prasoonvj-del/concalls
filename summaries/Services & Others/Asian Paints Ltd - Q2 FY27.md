# Asian Paints Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Mixed – Strong volume and value growth in a challenging inflationary environment, though margins faced pressure due to raw material costs and a lag in price realization.

### What changed vs last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Maintained PBDIT margin guidance of 18-20% for the year. Volume growth guidance for the remainder of the year is now 8-9% (previously 8-10% for FY27).
*   **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious" to acknowledging a "turbulent environment" with persistent inflation. Rural markets continue to lead over urban, but B2B is providing a buffer in urban areas.
*   **New topics/risks:** Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on TiO₂ (effective 24th October) is a new, specific headwind.
*   **Last quarter's promises:** VAE facility commissioned in September (as promised for August/September timeline).

### Key numbers
*   **Volume Growth:** 6.4% YoY (Decorative).
*   **Value Growth:** 14.5% YoY (Decorative).
*   **Decorative + Industrial Value Growth:** 16.4% YoY.
*   **Decorative + Industrial Volume Growth:** 6.8% YoY.
*   **Standalone PBDIT Margin:** 17.1% (vs 18.5% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Consolidated PBDIT Margin:** 15.6% (down 202 bps YoY).
*   **Standalone PAT Growth:** 15% YoY.
*   **Consolidated PAT Growth:** 7% YoY.
*   **New Product Contribution:** 18% of revenues.
*   **Interim Dividend:** Rs. 5.75 per share.
*   **Price Increases:** ~7% in Q1, ~5% in Q2, and ~3.5% announced for November.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Performance:** H1 performance remains strong with ~8% volume growth and ~16% value growth.
*   **Backward Integration:** VAE facility commissioned in September; management expects to reach full capacity of Phase 1 within a year.
*   **Industrial JVs:** PPGAP (Auto OE/Refinish) grew 34% YoY; General Industrial JV grew 28% YoY.
*   **Innovation:** Launched "Apcolite" premium emulsion (satin) and a universal wood/metal coating with 4-year warranty.
*   **Services:** Continued focus on 'Beautiful Homes Painting Service', 'Total Assure' (B2B), 'Smart Assure' (waterproofing), and asset management.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Management expects 8-9% volume growth going forward.
*   **Margins:** Management aims to maintain the 18-20% PBDIT margin band for the full year.
*   **Pricing:** Management will continue "calibrated" price increases to balance inflation and consumer demand.
*   **Q3 Outlook:** Expects benefit from the festive season (Diwali) and wedding season.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Rural markets are leading over urban markets.
*   B2B business is performing strongly in T1/T2 cities, balancing the urban decorative slowdown.
*   Competitive intensity remains "very strong and challenging" at the network level.
*   Management noted that price increases are an "art and science"; they are avoiding full pass-through of inflation to prevent demand destruction/down-gradation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Experienced ~3% inflation in Q2.
*   **Gross Margins:** Experienced a dip despite price hikes; management expects the 41-43% gross margin band to be the target.
*   **VAE Benefits:** Management expects 300-500 bps benefit on relevant products once the VAE plant reaches full potential.
*   **ADD Impact:** Anti-Dumping Duty on TiO₂ could impact costs by 2-3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **VAM-VAE Plant:** Total Capex of Rs. 3,250 crore. Phase 1 commissioned; full capacity utilization of Phase 1 expected next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Raw Material Volatility:** Persistent inflation in crude derivatives and TiO₂.
*   **Geopolitical:** International markets (Middle East/Africa) facing challenges due to geopolitical crises.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty:** New duty on TiO₂ effective 24th October.
*   **Demand Elasticity:** Risk of consumer down-gradation or deferment of maintenance painting if prices rise too sharply.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Mihir Shah (Nomura) on VAE:** Asked if VAE products are unique. Management confirmed it is a "very differentiated VAE" with technological ingenuity, not standard market VAE.
*   **Avi Mehta (Macquarie) on VAE benefits:** Asked for quantification. Management stated 300-500 bps benefit on relevant products.
*   **Aditya Bhartiya (Investec) on ADD:** Asked about TiO₂ duty impact. Management estimated a 2-3% cost impact and noted they are using alternative chemistries to mitigate.
*   **Manoj Menon (ICICI Securities) on competitive intensity:** Asked if the industry is passing on inflation. Management noted competition is in the same boat, not fully covering inflation, and intensity remains high.
*   **Amit Purohit (Elara Capital) on demand mix:** Asked if luxury is outperforming economy. Management stated they are focused on all buckets, including economy, but are driving premiumization.

### One-line takeaway
Asian Paints delivered resilient H1 growth despite a turbulent inflationary environment, relying on strong industrial performance, new product innovation, and the commissioning of its VAE facility to protect margins.
