# CCL Products (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth (double-digit) and robust profit growth, with management maintaining full-year guidance despite commodity price fluctuations.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 15% volume growth and similar EBITDA growth for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "wait and watch" to seeing more medium and long-term contracts returning as coffee prices stabilized in a range-bound manner.
- **New topics:** Discussion on potential strategic partnerships or joint ventures to manage capacity expansion; expansion of "Malgudi" snacks (banana chips) and tea segment.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Deleveraging continues; net debt reduced to Rs 742 crore from Rs 963 crore in Q1.

### Key numbers
- **Turnover:** Rs 1,215.99 crore (up 7.8% YoY).
- **Volume Growth:** Double-digit.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 217.3 crore (up 9.5% YoY).
- **PBT:** Rs 150.86 crore (up 18.7% YoY).
- **Net Profit:** Rs 130 crore (up 29% YoY).
- **Domestic Business:** Rs 210 crore gross turnover (branded sales ~Rs 140 crore, growing at 25%).
- **Gross Debt:** Rs 1,192 crore.
- **Net Debt:** Rs 742 crore.
- **EBITDA per kg:** Maintained in the Rs 135-140 range.

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 performance: Turnover Rs 2,419.58 crore (up 11.6%), EBITDA Rs 414 crore (up 15%), Net Profit Rs 246.96 crore (up 42%).
- Domestic branded business growth is backed by volume, not price increases.
- International branded business (e.g., Percol) expected to end the year at Rs 25-30 crore revenue; targeting profitability by next year.
- New brand launches planned for the US market; official launch in Nepal completed.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Volume Growth:** Reaffirmed 15% for FY27.
- **EBITDA Growth:** Expected to move in tandem with volume growth (approx. 15%).
- **Debt:** Maintaining guidance of Rs 1,000-1,200 crore gross debt and ~Rs 800 crore net debt by year-end.
- **Branded Business:** Maintaining Rs 550-600 crore revenue target for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Green coffee prices range-bound between USD 3,400-3,800/tonne; pre-harvest prices are holding steady, which is considered a positive sign.
- Freeze-dried (FD) demand remains stronger than spray-dried (SD) demand globally.
- Premiumization trend observed in coffee consumption as prices stabilize.
- Order book for Q3 and Q4 looks "robust."

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses increased due to higher freight/insurance costs and increased A&P spends for domestic market expansion.
- Management noted that freight/insurance costs are largely passed on to customers via CIF contracts.
- EBITDA per kg is expected to remain stable as product mix (FD vs SD) has normalized in the base.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Overall capacity utilization ~70%; FD utilization is higher than SD.
- No immediate capacity expansion needed for SD (good for 2-3 years).
- FD capacity may reach full utilization in 1-1.5 years; management evaluating brownfield expansion (likely ~6,000 tonnes) or strategic partnerships/JVs.
- Lead time for new capacity: 9-12 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Interest rate on debt: ~7.25%.
- Strong cash flow generation allows for funding potential capex via internal accruals.
- Working capital may increase in the near term due to the Vietnam procurement season.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Daily fluctuations in green coffee prices (USD 50-100/day) keep clients cautious.
- Potential impact of El Nino on Vietnam coffee crop (though current reports suggest harvest is holding up).
- "Leaking bucket" nature of client retention (small clients churn, but no significant loss of major clients).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why did EBITDA growth (9.5%) trail volume growth (10-15%) this quarter?** A: Management attributed it to the base effect (previous quarters had mix benefits) and increased A&P/freight costs; reiterated long-term guidance remains intact.
- **Q: Will you prioritize debt reduction or capex?** A: Management stated the balance sheet is strong enough to handle both simultaneously; no "either-or" scenario.
- **Q: Any M&A plans?** A: Open to lucrative, well-priced opportunities in coffee or adjacencies, but nothing definitive; currently evaluating.
- **Q: Why are other expenses higher?** A: Combination of volume growth, increased marketing spend for domestic brands, and higher freight/insurance costs.
- **Q: Strategy for capacity expansion?** A: Evaluating both internal brownfield expansion and strategic partnerships/JVs to ensure growth beyond FY28.

### One-line takeaway
CCL Products delivered a resilient performance with strong volume growth and continued deleveraging, while management remains confident in maintaining 15% growth guidance and is actively evaluating strategic options to fuel long-term capacity needs.
