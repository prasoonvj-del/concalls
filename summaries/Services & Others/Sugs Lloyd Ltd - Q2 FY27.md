# Sugs Lloyd Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the strongest half year in the company's history with strong revenue and profit growth, an expanding order book, and improved geographic diversification.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** INR 134.91 crores (up 12% YoY, up 72% QoQ)
- **H1 Revenue:** INR 213.31 crores (up 73% YoY from INR 123.03 crores)
- **H1 EBITDA:** INR 31 crores (up 64% YoY, margin at 14.5%)
- **Q2 EBITDA:** INR 19.1 crores (up 91% YoY, up 59% QoQ)
- **H1 PAT:** INR 20.01 crores (up 69% YoY)
- **Q2 PAT:** INR 12.51 crores (up 108% YoY, up 67% QoQ)
- **H1 EPS:** INR 8.62 (vs INR 6.86 YoY)
- **H1 Interest Coverage:** Improved to 5.2x (vs 4.8x YoY)
- **Finance Costs:** 2.8% of revenue (vs 3.2% YoY)
- **Q2 Order Inflow:** INR 395 crores excluding GST (INR 466 crores including GST) across 7 new orders
- **Order Book (as of 30 Sept 2026):** INR 1,067 crores (3.5x last year's revenue; ~INR 929 crores in power T&D, balance in solar)
- **Trailing 12-Month Receivable Days:** Improved to ~150 days (vs 179 days in June '26 and 193 days in March)
- **H1 Operating Cash Outflow:** INR 12 crores

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 delivered 71% of the revenue recorded in the entire FY26.
- Power T&D and smart grid contributed ~80% of H1 revenue, with solar EPC contributing the balance.
- Order diversification: All 7 new orders in Q2 came from outside Bihar, reducing Bihar's share of the order book from ~82% in July to ~55% across 11 operating states.
- Client base: ~9/10 of the order book is with government utilities, PSUs, and centrally funded schemes, with the rest with private utilities like Tata Power's DISCOM.
- Notable new wins: Odisha maintenance contract (INR 60 crores/year recurring for 3 years), Punjab RDSS work (15-month execution), and Amritsar solar plant (completion by December).
- Compact FPI (Fault Passage Indicator) completed testing, with first supply in an existing order within the quarter; exploring gain-share and rental deployment models.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its FY28 revenue target of INR 1,000 crores.
- Target to reach ~10% of revenue from products by FY28.
- Expects full-year EBITDA margins to remain in the 14% to 15% band (around 15%).
- Expects debt-to-equity ratio to remain around 1.2x to 1.3x (projecting debt around INR 230-240 crores by early FY28 and around INR 300 crores by end of FY28).
- Expects to add INR 400-500 crores more in orders over the next 6-9 months (tracking a bid pipeline of INR 19,150 crores, with INR 6,600 crores bid for and INR 1,260 crores under final evaluation).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Power T&D sector is witnessing massive upgrading of electrical networks across India (RDSS, underground cabling, smart grids).
- Government push to develop indigenous SCADA players and discourage international competitors is creating large domestic opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Quarterly margin fluctuations are normal depending on the mix of project execution stages and items with varying margins.
- Finance charges/interest rates are expected to decline further due to improving external credit ratings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FPI manufacturing capacity is currently utilized at less than 5% to 10%; capacity is not a constraint.
- Working on backward integration to manufacture high-purchase items in-house (vacuum circuit breakers and ring main units [RMUs]), expecting some billing next financial year.
- Subsidiaries like Sugs Renewables will bid for and hold RESCO projects for recurring revenue.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows remain negative due to aggressive growth (~70% YoY revenue growth).
- Tangible assets under work-in-progress of INR 37 crores relate to a hybrid capex/REC project for the Bihar government, with central CFA/SFS subsidies of INR 6 crores already received and balance expected in 40-45 days.
- Utilizing surety bonds in place of bank guarantees and trade/purchase bill discounting to ease working capital pressure.
- Promoters may extend unsecured loans if required, but further equity dilution is not currently planned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rapid top-line growth keeps operating cash flows negative; management noted that achieving positive cash flow would require slowing down growth.
- Policy changes and local issues can impact tender awards (e.g., a transmission tender in Jammu & Kashmir where the company was L1 got scrapped due to state policy changes).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Mishra (Finvestors Research) on margin dip:** Management explained that quarterly margin variations occur due to the specific mix of project milestones executed, reiterating a 15% margin expectation for the full year.
2. **Avnish Burman (Vaikarya Investments) on debt trajectory for FY28:** Management stated debt would reach ~INR 230-240 crores by the beginning of FY28 and ~INR 300 crores by the end of FY28, maintaining a debt-to-equity ratio of 1.2x to 1.3x.
3. **Achuth Pabat (Dukan Capital) on FPI capacity and competition:** Management clarified that FPI capacity utilization is under 5-10%, and their main competitor is Siemens, noting high entry barriers due to customisation and type-testing requirements.
4. **Puneet on operational scalability:** Management highlighted that key head office departments (procurement, accounts, HR) have remained nearly the same size while handling a 10x jump in project scale (scaling up to a INR 640 crore project from a previous max of INR 120 crores) by scaling local site execution teams and using subcontractors.
5. **Krish on tender disqualification:** Management clarified that a minor delay in submitting a bank certificate format caused a disqualification in a Haryana utility tender, but it was not related to technical qualification or eligibility.

### One-line takeaway
Sugs Lloyd delivered its strongest half-year ever in H1 FY27 with 73% revenue growth, a record INR 1,067 crore order book diversified across 11 states, and reiterated its INR 1,000 crore revenue target for FY28 while actively managing working capital and debt.
