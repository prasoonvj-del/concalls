# BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, significant operating leverage, and consistent demand despite gold price volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue Growth:** 49% YoY.
*   **Pre-Ind AS EBITDA:** Rose 135% YoY.
*   **Operating Margin:** 7.5% (expansion of 273 bps).
*   **Same Store Sales Growth (SSG):** 39%.
*   **Repeat Revenue Contribution:** ~60% (up 9 percentage points YoY).
*   **Marketing Spend:** 6.9% of revenue (consistent with last year).
*   **Inventory:** ~Rs 2,800 crore.
*   **Studded Mix:** 57%.

### Highlights
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management noted that revenue scaled against a cost base that rose more slowly, driving EBITDA growth to nearly 3x the rate of revenue growth.
*   **Business Model:** Management defines the company as a "consumer internet company" using jewellery as its category, with >80% of sales originating online.
*   **Store Strategy:** Currently operates 352 stores. Management emphasized that stores act as a "continuous journey" with online discovery, leading to high conversion productivity.
*   **Manufacturing:** ~95% of products are manufactured in-house, which management cites as a strategic choice to maintain design differentiation and premium pricing.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Long-term Targets:** Management reiterated goals of ~30% SSG over the next four years and scaling total revenue to ~Rs 12,000 crore in the same period.
*   **Margins:** Management expects operating EBITDA margins to expand from 7.5% toward a 15% handle over the next four years, driven by scale and vintage-driven operating leverage.
*   **Marketing Spend:** Management expects marketing spend as a percentage of revenue to decline from 6.6% (FY26) to ~4.5%-4.6% over the next five years.
*   **Distribution:** Management targets a 20% CAGR in store count over the next four years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Gold Price Impact:** Demand softened in May following the custom duty hike (6% to 15%) but normalized through June and July.
*   **Consumer Behavior:** Management noted that their customers are price-point based rather than investment-driven; stable gold prices are viewed as the most conducive environment for demand.
*   **Competition:** Management stated they have not seen lab-grown diamonds dent natural diamond demand, noting that lab-grown products represent <1% of their revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margins:** Management noted gross margins and contribution margins have been largely stable; they advised focusing on contribution margin rather than quarterly gross margin fluctuations.
*   **Rentals:** Management stated that while they are taking larger spaces (approx. 4,500 sq. ft. for new stores), the unit economics (rent per store/revenue per store) remain stable. Annual lease escalation is 3-5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Inventory Turns:** Management expects blended inventory turns to improve toward 1.7x-1.8x over the next four years as store vintages mature.
*   **Store Strategy:** New stores are being opened with larger footprints to accommodate future productivity, but management maintains that operational square footage remains in historical territory.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Hedging:** The company maintains a 50% hedge on total inventory (gold and diamond). Management stated this policy is for risk management (liquidity/capital structure) rather than taking a view on gold prices.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Merchandise Dislocation:** Management acknowledged a "merchandise dislocation" occurred due to sharp gold price increases, which impacted new customer acquisition (down to ~40,000/quarter from ~50,000). They stated this is being fixed and trends are improving.
*   **New Customer Acquisition:** Management declined to provide specific targets for new customer additions, noting that as the customer base ages (nearly 1 million customers), the business is increasingly driven by repeat revenue and AOV.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On 50% revenue growth math:** An analyst questioned how 30% SSG and 20% distribution growth leads to 50% revenue growth. Management clarified they refer to "fundamental" long-term SSG of mature cohorts, not reported SSG, which includes younger, high-growth stores.
*   **On inventory turnover:** An analyst noted a decline in inventory turnover. Management explained this is a mathematical function of the high mix of new stores and gold price inflation, and expects improvement as the portfolio matures.
*   **On marketing spend:** Management clarified that quarterly marketing spend as a % of revenue is seasonal and should not be analyzed on a quarterly basis; the long-term trend is downward.
*   **On store size/rent:** An analyst questioned the 9.5% gap between area expansion (25.5%) and rent growth (35%). Management argued that per-store unit economics are the correct metric, and absolute rent per store remains stable.

### One-line takeaway
BlueStone is leveraging its tech-first, in-house manufacturing model to drive operating leverage, aiming for a 15% EBITDA margin and Rs 12,000 crore revenue over four years, while navigating gold price volatility through a focus on design-led consumer demand.
