# Adani Total Gas Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted resilient operational growth and volume expansion despite significant headwinds from high gas costs and the withdrawal of pooled gas support.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained aggressive expansion targets; aiming for >2 lakh household connections for the full year.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "waiting for pooled gas reinstatement" (Q1) to "navigating pressures through portfolio diversification and agile sourcing" (Q2).
- **New topics/risks:** Explicit mention of "new well gas" and "HPHT" (High-Pressure High-Temperature) gas fluctuations as part of the sourcing mix.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on volume growth (12% YoY) and infrastructure expansion (added 16 CNG stations, ~40k households).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 2,102 crore (+34% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** INR 340 crore (+12% YoY).
- **PAT:** INR 167 crore (+3% YoY).
- **Volumes:** 315 MMSCM (+12% YoY); H1 FY27 volumes at 618 MMSCM (+13% YoY).
- **Segment Volumes:** CNG +17% YoY (223 MMSCM); PNG +3% YoY (92 MMSCM).
- **Infrastructure:** 723 CNG stations (16 added in Q2); 11.81 lakh PNG households (39,574 added in Q2); 16,305 inch-km steel pipeline.
- **Customers:** 10,864 industrial/commercial customers (900 added in H1 FY27).
- **E-Mobility:** 5,513 EV charging points across 225 cities (63 MW capacity).

### Highlights
- Achieved 12% EBITDA growth despite a 43% YoY surge in natural gas costs.
- Volume mix of new geographical areas (GAs) improved to 41% from 35% in Q2 FY26.
- NSE Sustainability and SES score reached 75/100.
- Joint Venture (IOAGPL) network: 1,185 CNG stations, 14.31 lakh PNG households, 12,925 industrial/commercial connections, 29,156 inch-km pipeline.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to add 80–90 CNG stations in FY27.
- Targeting >2 lakh new household connections for the full year.
- Management plans to continue a "calibrated approach" to pricing to manage input cost volatility.
- Management is evaluating further CBG (Compressed Biogas) projects but is taking "slightly lower steps" until feedstock and technology confidence is established.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CNG demand remains strong, with industry growth at ~20% and ATGL growing at 17-18%.
- Management noted that industrial and commercial customer additions in H1 FY27 are ~1.4x the total additions for the entire FY26.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Natural gas costs rose 43% YoY due to higher Brent/LNG prices, currency depreciation, and the withdrawal of pooled gas support.
- **Sourcing Mix:** ~25% APM gas; ~75% other sources (including ~40% linked to Henry Hub/Brent/Spot, and variable HPHT/new well gas).
- Spot gas reliance reduced to 7-8% from 15% previously.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 expansion: 732 inch-km of steel pipeline, 21 CNG stations, 77,870 household connections.
- ~70 CNG stations are currently under advanced construction with equipment procurement underway.
- EV strategy shifting focus to B2B charging infrastructure (e.g., Adani Cement, Ports, Logistics) to improve utilization rates.
- CBG plant in Barsana: Producing 5-6 tonnes; capacity expansion to double in the next 5-6 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in Brent/LNG prices and currency depreciation.
- Geopolitical risks (West Asia crisis).
- Fluctuations in domestic gas availability (HPHT and new well gas production cycles).
- Challenges in CBG business: Feedstock availability, land requirements, and technology validation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sridhar Chandran (Prasidh Capital) on margin recovery:** Management stated that while current margins are impacted by temporary geopolitical issues, they are managing through long-term contracts and operational efficiencies.
- **Vivekanand S. (AMBIT Capital) on CNG pricing:** Management confirmed they do not pass on the *entire* cost to consumers, preferring a calibrated approach to maintain volume growth.
- **Arya Patel (Emkay Global) on HPHT gas:** Management explained that HPHT gas allocation varies by 5-10% month-to-month based on production cycles.
- **Sridhar Chandran (Prasidh Capital) on EV utilization:** Management admitted utilization is a concern and is pivoting to B2B captive charging (trucks) to drive higher utilization.
- **Vivekanand S. (AMBIT Capital) on CBG:** Management clarified that the Barsana plant uses a mix of cow dung and paddy straw, with Thermax as a technical partner.

### One-line takeaway
ATGL is successfully navigating a high-cost environment through diversified gas sourcing and volume growth, while shifting its EV strategy toward B2B to improve utilization.
