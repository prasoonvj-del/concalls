# Updater Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 11% YoY and PAT grew 62% YoY with margin improvements across segments.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: INR812 crores (up 11% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR43 crores / INR43.2 crores (up 37% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 5.3% (up 100 bps YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR32 crores / INR32.1 crores (up 62% YoY)
- IFM revenue: INR560 crores (up 11% YoY)
- IFM EBITDA: INR24 crores (margin: 4.2% vs 3.8% in Q2 FY26)
- BSS revenue: INR267 crores (up 11% YoY)
- BSS EBITDA: INR21 crores (margin: 7.7% vs 5.3% in Q2 FY26)
- Athena revenue: INR29 crores; EBITDA: INR5.5 crores (margin: 19%); PAT: INR3 crores (up 25% YoY)
- Denave revenue: INR175 crores (up 17% YoY); EBITDA: INR10 crores (margin: 5.6% vs 3.2% in Q2 FY26); PAT: INR8.5 crores (up 160% YoY)
- Matrix revenue: INR32 crores (up 2% YoY); EBITDA: INR4 crores (margin: 12% vs 9.4% in Q2 FY26); PAT: INR2 crores (up 14% YoY)
- EBGC revenue: INR14 crores (up 15% YoY)
- H1 FY27 consolidated revenue: INR1,577 crores (up 10% YoY); EBITDA: INR85.5 crores (up 21% YoY); PAT: INR62.4 crores (up 28% YoY)
- Total workforce: 77,157 (IFM: 60,152; BSS: 17,005)
- Cash and cash equivalents (including mutual funds and fixed deposits): INR404 crores as of September 2026

### Highlights
- Appointed Rajeev Bhardwaj as COO for IFM to separate day-to-day operations from new business development.
- Appointed Mrs. Jigyasa Sharma as CEO-Designate for Matrix effective April 1, 2027.
- Denave initiated the closure of two small step-down subsidiaries (Denave Europe and Denave Korea) to reduce organizational complexity.
- ICRA reaffirmed long-term rating as AA- (stable) and short-term rating as A1+.
- Athena signed 2 new client contracts in July and 2 in the BFSI sector in October; live agentic AI deployments generating revenue for BFSI and industrial clients.
- Matrix large audits deferred from May were executed in Q2; compliance and business process services are being digitized.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 numbers to build on H1, suggesting multiplying H1 earnings by 2 as a rough estimate for the full year, while noting headwinds in Denave and the aviation/global business.
- Management expects margins to remain broadly sustainable despite anticipated sector headwinds.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India office market absorption reached 66 million square feet in the first 9 months of calendar year 2026, driven by GCCs (28 million square feet).
- New labor code implementation has raised compliance bars, favoring organized providers like UDS.
- White-collar hiring growth moderated to ~2% YoY in September; BPO/ITES/GCC hiring grew ~4%, while traditional IT remains subdued.
- Aviation environment softer due to airline capacity adjustments and West Asian airspace restrictions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee benefit expenses grew 10% YoY to INR628 crores (77.3% of revenue vs 78.6% in Q2 FY26).
- New PF ceiling hike from INR15,000 to INR25,000 could impact full-year unhedged costs by INR5 crores to INR6 crores, but with pass-throughs, net impact is estimated at INR1 crore to INR2 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocation priorities: technology investments, selective value-accretive acquisitions, and shareholder returns.
- Target acquisition talks fell apart due to valuation differences; currently considering other opportunities without an active deal guaranteed for this year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash from operating activities for H1 was INR46 crores.
- No long-term borrowings; net debt to equity is negative 0.21x as of September 2026.
- ROCE for H1 FY27 is 16.2%.
- Partial recovery and court deposits seen for Avon fraud amounts; expected to bring some back to P&L upon legal/police process completion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Headwinds and uncertainty in Demand Gen (Denave) and aviation/global ground handling business.
- Uneven white-collar hiring trends impacting verification volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sakshi Pratap (Pratap Securities)** asked about full-year EBITDA margin expectations and labor law impact. Management stated H1 numbers multiplied by 2 offer a reasonable estimate, and net labor code impact post-pass-through will be minimal (INR1-2 crores).
2. **Praatik Shah (Ascent Capital)** inquired about Avon fraud collections and aviation headwinds. Management stated partial recovery is anticipated through legal/court processes, and winter schedule cuts by airlines impact aviation, though offset by the School of Aviation.
3. **Jay Jain (JJ Capital)** asked about tax rate sustainability and long-term 5-year growth. Management noted the tax rate benefits from employment creation incentives (80JJAA) and expects it to remain in the current range while overall 11-12% growth rates could accelerate over time.
4. **Vivek Desai (Investec)** asked about the lower tax rate reasons. Management explained it is impacted by employment creation incentives (80JJAA), though UDS relies on it less than pure staffing peers.

### One-line takeaway
Updater Services delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 11% revenue growth, 62% PAT growth, and broad-based margin expansion, supported by solid IFM execution and tech-led BSS improvements.
