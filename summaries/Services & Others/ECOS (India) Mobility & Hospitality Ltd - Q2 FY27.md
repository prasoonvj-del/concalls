# ECOS (India) Mobility & Hospitality Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Meaningful sequential improvement in revenue, trip volumes, and EBITDA margins driven by cost efficiencies and operating leverage.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Full-year revenue growth guidance adjusted to 13% to 15% (compared to 15% to 18% in Q1); EBITDA margin guidance maintained/narrowed to 10% to 11% (vs. around 10% previously).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from cautious to positive following an expansion in EBITDA margins (11.9% vs. 10.3% in Q1) and steady enterprise demand.
- **New topics or risks:** Adopted AI tools across 8-10 internal projects, deployed Microsoft Dynamics ERP last month, and launched a B2C app in beta testing. Old risks of pricing pressure persisted.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on physical expansion (added 9 new cities to reach 160) and enterprise client additions (72 new clients added vs. 61 in Q1).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 2,345 million (~Rs 234.50 crore), up 9.5% YoY and 10.9% QoQ.
- **EBITDA:** INR 279 million (~Rs 27.90 crore), up 13.8% YoY and 27.9% QoQ.
- **EBITDA margin:** 11.9% (vs. 11.5% in Q2 FY26 and 10.3% in Q1 FY27).
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** INR 177.32 million (~Rs 17.73 crore), up 21.4% YoY.
- **Trip volumes:** ~1.58 million trips (up 15% YoY and 7% QoQ).
- **Active client base:** 1,538 enterprise organizations (onboarded 72 new clients in Q2).
- **Network fleet:** ~20,500 vehicles (up 12% YoY; ~95% vendor-operated).
- **EV fleet:** 475 vehicles (up from 460 in Q1).
- **Trade receivables:** INR 1,114.56 million (~46 days of revenue).
- **Cash and investments:** INR 1,256.11 million.

### Highlights
- Segment revenue mix: Employee Transport Services (ETS) contributed 58%, Chauffeur-Driven Car Rentals (CCR) contributed 42%.
- Expanded operating footprint to 160 cities by adding 9 new cities during the quarter.
- ~50% of revenue continues to come from customers associated with ECOS for more than 5 years.
- Launched B2C app in beta testing on Android and Apple Play Store.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year revenue to grow between 13% to 15%.
- Reiterate target to reach the INR 1,000 crores revenue mark by the end of FY28.
- Expected EBITDA margins projected between 10% to 11% for the remaining quarters of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ETS continues to benefit from stable demand from IT, ITeS, and Global Capability Centers (GCCs).
- CCR growth was supported by corporate customer demand and event-based requirements.
- Corporate mobility remains highly fragmented with aggressive pricing competition from peers chasing top-line growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of service improved to 74.2% of revenue (down 100 bps QoQ from 75.1%).
- Employee benefit expenses reduced to ~10% of revenue (from 11.2% in Q1) due to operating leverage.
- EBITDA margin expanded by 160 bps QoQ to 11.9% driven by cost optimization, operating leverage, and a slightly higher CCR business mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex stood at INR 273.90 million, largely toward vehicles.
- Planned vehicle capex expected to remain in the INR 35 crores to INR 45 crores range.
- Maintained an asset-light model with own vehicles kept at 5% to 10% of the total fleet.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free with a net worth of INR 2,826.69 million as of September 30, 2026.
- Cash flow from operations for H1 stood at INR 109.52 million.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Intense competitive pricing pressure in large enterprise contracts from competitors bidding below sustainable cost thresholds.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked why EBITDA margins are lower than historical 15% levels.** Management explained that the shift in business mix toward ETS and sustained competitive pricing pressure reduced gross margins.
2. **Pranay Agrawal (Shah Family Office) asked about event-driven seasonality and competitive intensity in ETS.** Management noted a dedicated event team is growing and that competitors taking losses on large contracts will eventually face economic limits.
3. **Pulkit Singhal (Institutional Investor) asked about AI adoption and productivity impacts.** Management stated AI projects are automating repetitive tasks and improving shared service productivity without immediate headcount cuts.
4. **Arya Shah asked about future capex and own vehicle targets.** Management guided to INR 35-45 crores capex and maintaining own vehicles at 5-10% of total fleet.

### One-line takeaway
ECOS delivered a solid sequential recovery in Q2 FY27 with 9.5% YoY revenue growth and margin expansion to 11.9%, though management guides conservatively for 10-11% margins due to persistent industry pricing pressure.
