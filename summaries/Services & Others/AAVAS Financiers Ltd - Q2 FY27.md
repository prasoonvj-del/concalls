# AAVAS Financiers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong operational momentum, improved productivity per resource, and robust asset quality, while maintaining a confident outlook on growth despite a rising interest rate environment.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained disbursement growth target of >20% YoY.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from expecting spread compression (Q1) to now being confident in maintaining spreads above 5% (Q2).
- **New topics/risks:** Explicit discussion on the impact of deficient rainfall (drought) in certain regions (e.g., Rajasthan, Maharashtra), which management dismissed as having no material impact on the book.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on productivity improvements (RO productivity moved from 1.1 to 1.2 files/month).

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 248.0 billion (+16.1% YoY).
- **Disbursements (H1FY27):** Rs 35.1 billion (+30% YoY).
- **Net Profit (H1FY27):** Rs 3.5 billion (+16.4% YoY).
- **NIM:** 7.81% (+11 bps QoQ).
- **Cost of Borrowing:** 7.64% (stable).
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 42.9% (improved 195 bps YoY).
- **Asset Quality:** Gross Stage 3 at 1.11% (improved 13 bps YoY); Net Stage 3 at 0.70% (improved 16 bps YoY); 1+ DPD at 3.81% (improved 18 bps YoY).
- **ROA:** 3.28% (+9 bps QoQ); **ROE:** 13.63% (+28 bps QoQ).
- **Productivity:** RO productivity improved to 1.2 files/month in Q2 (vs 1.1 in Q1), reaching 1.3 in September.

### Highlights
- **Milestone:** On track to cross Rs 250 billion AUM in October 2026.
- **Productivity:** Aiming for 1.5 files/month by end of FY27, scaling to 2 files/month eventually.
- **Strategy:** Focus on "customer-first" approach, system-driven controls, and rigorous training to improve governance.
- **Liability:** Diversified liability franchise; 30% of borrowings linked to Repo, balance to MCLR/Fixed.
- **Insurance:** Management noted insurance income is ~4% of PBT; plans to increase attachment rates to offset potential regulatory changes.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth:** Confident of delivering >20% growth over the long term.
- **Spreads:** Confident of maintaining spreads above 5% despite the rising interest rate cycle.
- **Opex:** Aiming to reduce Opex-to-assets ratio to below 3% and Cost-to-Income ratio to below 40% in the medium term.
- **Productivity:** Target of 1.5 files/month by end of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Demand:** No pressure on demand; management sees no impact from deficient rainfall on the portfolio.
- **Competition:** Management does not see competitive pressure from larger HFCs/NBFCs due to the distinct nature of their target customer segment.
- **Segment:** Exposure to pure Agri-based income is very limited.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Borrowing:** Cost of borrowing stable at 7.64%; expects increase following the 25 bps repo rate hike.
- **Yields:** Expects increase in lending rates to support yields on incremental disbursements.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Branch Network:** Expanded to 443 branches across 15 states.
- **Focus:** Investing in branch expansion while monitoring new branch performance for faster breakeven.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** Rs 19.6 billion in cash/equivalents and unavailed CC limits; Rs 3 billion in documented unavailed sanctions.
- **Capital:** Well-capitalized with CRAR of 43.77%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Interest Rates:** Acknowledged a rising interest rate cycle; monitoring potential impact on spreads.
- **Regulatory:** Upcoming changes to insurance distribution (effective April 1, 2027) are being evaluated; management plans to offset potential fee income loss via higher attachment rates and yield transmission.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhijit Tibrewal (Motilal Oswal) on drought impact:** Management stated they have a "very keen eye" on the ground and see no stress; exposure to pure Agri-income is limited.
- **Chintan Shah (Emkay Global) on AUM mix:** Management clarified the 65:35 (HL vs. NHL) steady-state target and explained that the recent uptick in LAP is due to conscious efforts to offer top-up loans to existing customers.
- **Renish Bhuva (ICICI Securities) on spread sustainability:** Management reiterated confidence in maintaining >5% spreads, citing the ability to pass on rate hikes and leverage the liability franchise.
- **Nischint Chawathe (Kotak) on insurance fee income:** Management noted that insurance is a "right product" for their segment and they have room to increase attachment rates to cover potential regulatory-driven income loss.
- **Gaurav Purohit (Systematix) on incentives:** Management confirmed a revamped incentive policy to reward higher productivity (1.5+ files/month) and confirmed an ESOP plan is active for ~200 employees.

### One-line takeaway
AAVAS is prioritizing productivity and operational efficiency to drive growth, with management expressing confidence in maintaining >5% spreads despite a rising interest rate environment.
