# Glen Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and net profit grew strongly in H1 FY27 despite raw material pressures, and the major expansion project is progressing toward completion.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 115.30 crore (up 20.66% YoY from Rs 95.56 crore)
- H1 FY27 Total income: Rs 116.47 crore (up 20.62% YoY from Rs 96.56 crore)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 22.71 crore (up 12.47% YoY from Rs 20.19 crore)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 19.5%
- H1 FY27 Finance cost: Rs 4.29 crore (down 11.16% YoY from Rs 4.82 crore)
- H1 FY27 Profit before tax (PBT): Rs 14.30 crore (up 28.50% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Net profit (PAT): Rs 10.91 crore (up 31.36% YoY from Rs 8.31 crore)
- H1 FY27 Net profit margin: 9.37%
- FY26 Revenue from operations: Rs 203.13 crore (up 19.02%)
- FY26 Net profit: Rs 16.5 crores
- Revenue mix (H1 FY27): Thin-wall food containers 80.75%, PLA straws 12.63%, paper straws 6.62%
- Geographic mix (H1 FY27): Domestic 73.66%, Exports 26.34%
- Current monthly capacity: Thin-wall food containers 665 metric tons, PLA straws 160 metric tons, paper straws 95 metric tons
- Total capex for new facility: Rs 100.49 crore
- New capacity: Plastic food containers (injection and thermoforming) 21,095 metric tons per year (additional 13,109 metric tons per year); paper products (including paper cups) 7,696 metric tons per year
- FY27 Revenue guidance: Around Rs 225 crore to Rs 230 crore
- Steady-state revenue potential at full capacity: Rs 500 crore to Rs 550 crore (expected by FY28/FY29)
- Existing depreciation: Rs 7 crore per year; incremental depreciation post-expansion: Rs 10 crore to Rs 11 crore per year (total Rs 17 crore to Rs 18 crore)
- Term loan debt: Existing term loan is negligible (expected at Rs 5 crore by end of year), plus new bank term loan of Rs 50 crore (total Rs 55 crore repayable over 7 years)
- Cost of debt: 8% to 8.5% for banking loans; working capital loans 0.2% to 0.25% lower

### Highlights
- Operations are certified under FSSC 22000, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, HACCP, FSC, and SEDEX 4-Pillar.
- Project delay of 6 months caused by elections/SIR process, non-functional government machinery, and severe monsoon rains in West Bengal (excess rain of 30%).
- Management noted that unlike peers, Glen successfully managed raw material shortages by multiplying resources and importing from multiple alternative sources instead of depending on a single supplier.
- Continuing past pattern, management highlighted strong volume demand and premium positioning over competition.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue around Rs 225 crore to Rs 230 crore (reduced from prior expectations due to project delays).
- New facility revenue contribution in FY27 expected between Rs 25 crore to Rs 40 crore.
- Full realization of the new capacity (turnover of Rs 400 crore to Rs 500 crore in FY28, reaching Rs 550 crore by FY28-FY29).
- Target to achieve Rs 1,000 crore turnover by 2030.
- EBITDA margin expected to remain between 18% to 19% at steady state.
- Expected steady-state PAT between Rs 50 crore to Rs 55 crore when full revenue is realized.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Sustained demand for eco-friendly food packaging solutions across domestic and export markets.
- Export outlook remains strong and unaffected by geopolitical tensions; management targets increasing export base to 35% (and up to 50% for thin-wall food containers).
- North America (Canada, Mexico, USA) and Europe identified as major growth opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (crude-linked) surged from ~Rs 100 to ~Rs 160 post-Middle East tensions, leading to a ~35% jump in selling prices (implemented in stages from April to July-August).
- Value addition per metric ton remains constant in absolute terms, causing a 1% to 2% squeeze in percentage margins.
- Raw material prices have recently started coming down slightly.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up a new manufacturing facility in Howrah with an investment of Rs 100.49 crore.
- Implementation timeline: Injection molding to start in January 2027, followed by thermoforming and paper products by April 2027. Stabilization expected within 5 to 6 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total bank working capital liability stands at around Rs 80 crore.
- Total term debt post-expansion will be Rs 55 crore, carrying an interest rate of 8% to 8.5%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and volatility in raw material prices.
- Previous implementation delays caused by elections and severe monsoon weather in West Bengal.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Lala Ram Singh (Lala Ram Singh):** Asked about the reasons for project delays. **Management** explained the delays were due to elections, stalled government machinery, and severe monsoon rains in West Bengal (30% excess rain).
2. **Mukund (Saber Edge):** Asked about revenue contribution from the new facility in FY27. **Management** stated it would be around Rs 25 crore to Rs 40 crore depending on execution speed.
3. **Aniket Nikumb (Individual Investor):** Asked about pricing actions taken in response to raw material inflation. **Management** stated selling prices increased by ~35% (from Rs 200 to Rs 260-270) in stages as raw material costs rose from Rs 100 to Rs 160.
4. **Lala Ram Singh (Lala Ram Singh):** Asked about return on capital. **Management** stated they had not calculated ROCE explicitly, but presumed it would not be below 15%.
5. **Anmol Karnam (Individual Investor):** Asked about future plans and main board listing. **Management** stated plans to evaluate further projects targeting Rs 1,000 crore turnover by 2030, and to plan for main board listing after completing 3 years on the SME platform.

### One-line takeaway
Glen Industries reported a strong H1 FY27 with 20.6% revenue growth and 31.4% PAT growth, and remains on track to commission its Rs 100.49 crore expansion project by early 2027 to scale towards a Rs 550 crore revenue run-rate.
