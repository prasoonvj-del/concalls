# Alldigi Tech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with steady revenue growth, significant profitability improvement, healthy cash generation, and robust international and Tech & Digital expansion.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 303.6 crores (up 4.2% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 87.7 crores (up 20.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 28.9% (vs. 24.9% in H1 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 153.3 crores (up 4% YoY, 2% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 46.3 crores (up ~29% YoY, up 12% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 30.2% (vs. 24.4% in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 26.6 crores (up ~51% YoY, up 46.7% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 17.3% (vs. 12% in Q2 FY26)
- **Cash Position:** INR 127.2 crores as of September 2026
- **H1 Collections:** INR 316.9 crores (up 4.3% YoY)
- **H1 Operating Cash Flow:** INR 70.2 crores (up 31.3% YoY; ~80.1% OCF-to-EBITDA conversion)
- **International Revenue Contribution:** ~70% of consolidated revenue (vs. ~63% last year)
- **Tech & Digital Q2 Revenue:** INR 41.7 crores (up 12.9% YoY, 2.7% QoQ; segment margin at 43.9%)
- **Tech & Digital H1 Revenue:** INR 82.3 crores (up 12.4% YoY)
- **Employee Records Processed (H1):** 101.3 lakh records (51.1 lakh in Q2, up 7.2% YoY)
- **H1 New ACV Added:** INR 16.1 crores (Tech & Digital) and INR 66.5 crores (BPM)
- **BPM Q2 Revenue:** INR 111.6 crores (up ~1% YoY, 1.8% QoQ; international BPM up 12.6% YoY)
- **BPM H1 Revenue:** INR 221.3 crores (up 1.4% YoY)
- **Q2 New Business ACV:** INR 37.2 crores (up 86.5% YoY)
- **Share Buyback:** Approved buyback of up to 4.8 lakh equity shares at INR 1,000 per share, totaling INR 48 crores.

### Highlights
- International BPM now contributes ~83% of BPM revenue (vs. 75% in corresponding quarter last year).
- Launched two new products in October: Alldigi 360 (HCM suite including payroll) and Alldigi 360 Analytics (AI analytics module).
- Appointed a new Chief Business Officer for BPM, expanded international sales teams (U.S. leadership with BPM background, and Southeast Asia).
- Board approved a share buyback of up to INR 48 crores at INR 1,000 per share.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects overall BPM margins to continue to improve as domestic business is phased out.
- Tech & Digital segment target is early double-digit growth over the next 3 years.
- Full impact of employee headcount reduction started in mid-Q2 will be visible in Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
- International BPM and Tech & Digital are growing strongly, while headline growth is moderated by planned exits from structurally low-margin domestic BPM business.
- Embedding AI across BPM delivery to improve productivity, quality, and customer outcomes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability grew significantly faster than revenue due to operating leverage, improved business mix, and disciplined cost management.
- Employee costs reduced by ~INR 5 crores QoQ due to operating efficiencies (lower replacement costs), location mix optimization in Manila, retiral benefits true-up, and headcount reductions.
- DSO improved by 3 days YoY.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- ROU assets increased due to office replacements with longer lease commitments; annual lease payments expected to remain in the range of FY26 (around INR 35 crores) or lower due to resource optimization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-free balance sheet with healthy cash and liquid funds of INR 127.2 crores as of September 2026.
- Operating cash flow conversion remains healthy at ~80% of EBITDA.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as adverse risks, other than acknowledging that tech and digital revenue incubation/implementation takes longer than BPM.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harsh Kundnani (Equities):** Asked about the divergence between headcount and employee cost reduction. Management explained it was driven by lower replacement hiring costs, Manila location optimization, retiral valuation true-ups, and full-quarter impact expected in Q3.
2. **Raghuram (Eurindia Ventures):** Inquired about international growth sustainability and BOT (Build-Operate-Transfer) deals. Management highlighted a new 1,000+ FTE BOT relationship with 2-3x growth potential and emphasized technology-led services.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments):** Asked about payroll pricing erosion and SaaS partnerships (Workday, TaxSpanner). Management stated transaction pricing includes transparent client gain-shares for efficiencies, while new modules and HCM suites expand service cross-selling.
4. **Simran Thakkar (Beas Capital Limited):** Asked about segment results versus reported EBITDA. Management clarified segment margins represent PBT before finance costs, depreciation, and unallocated corporate costs.

### One-line takeaway
Alldigi Tech delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 29% YoY EBITDA growth and 51% YoY PAT growth, driven by a higher-margin international mix, disciplined cost actions, and expanding tech-digital capabilities.
