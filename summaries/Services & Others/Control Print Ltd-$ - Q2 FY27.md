# Control Print Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Core coding and marking business remains steady and profitable, but international subsidiaries continue to incur losses despite structural cost-reduction and turnaround efforts.

### Key numbers
- Standalone Total Revenue (Q2 FY27): INR 134 crore
- Standalone Total Revenue (H1 FY27): INR 241 crore
- Standalone Operating Revenue (Q2 FY27): INR 131 crore
- Standalone Operating Revenue (H1 FY27): INR 235 crore
- Consolidated Operating Revenue (Q2 FY27): INR 144 crore (vs. INR 112 crore in Q2 FY25-26)
- Standalone Cost of Goods Sold: 41.65% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Standalone Manufacturing Costs: 2.26% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Standalone Employee Costs: 17% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Standalone Depreciation: ~3.5% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Standalone Other Expenses: ~15% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated Cost of Goods Sold: 44% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated Employee Costs: ~20% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated Depreciation: ~3.5% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated Other Expenses: 14% of operating revenue (Q2 FY27)
- Subsidiary PBT Losses: INR 12 crore (March quarter), INR 8 crore (June quarter), INR 5 crore (September quarter)
- Historical International Business Losses: INR 20 crore (FY25), INR 36 crore (FY26)
- Track & Trace (QRiousCodes) cumulative investment over last six years: >INR 100 crore

### Highlights
- Coding and marking contributes ~94% to 95% of total operating revenue.
- Company holds an oligopolistic position with other players, dominating ~80% of the domestic coding and marking market.
- International subsidiary Markprint continues to be profitable year-on-year.
- CPL MEA (Middle East operations, started January 2025) is expected to break even by the end of the year.
- V-Shapes / Codeology operations have reduced cost base by ~25% and replaced 65% to 70% of staff with contract roles and external consultants; sales team expanded to three people.
- Unnati manufacturing project in Guwahati has been suspended due to incentive issues; temporary manufacturing setups are being identified in Assam to reduce material costs and improve manufacturing consistency.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain good performance in H2 assuming no downward surprises.
- Core coding and marking targets: ~60% gross profitability and ~30% EBIT.
- V-Shapes platform developments and machine retrofits are targeted for completion/testing around January or February, with major numbers not expected until Q4 or later.
- International business structured for potential scale-up in the next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Top performing verticals for coding and marking: pipes, food, dairy, cable, wire, FMCG, steel, and wood; market leader in cement, plywood, sugar, and dairy.
- Coding and marking business outlook for the next half is exciting with new customer additions and consumable/printer innovations.
- Track & Trace business outlook is very positive with growing domestic and international pipeline orders.
- Government Track & Trace mandates: Top 300 brands started; upcoming mandates expected for top 1,000 brands, anti-infectives, vaccines, and psychotropic/narcotic medicines across 2027-2028. Total current market size estimated at INR 600 crore to INR 700 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management is implementing price increases, optimizing procurement costs, and reviewing operational economy and efficiency.
- V-Shapes cost basis successfully reduced by ~25%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Two manufacturing facilities and 12 offices in India; branch in Sri Lanka; subsidiaries in Netherlands, Italy, UK, and UAE.
- Temporary manufacturing setup being arranged in Assam for material production.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail beyond ongoing subsidiary loss reductions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution challenges in ramping up new technology platforms like V-Shapes.
- Geopolitical and macro disruptions (e.g., prior Iran issues affecting Q1).
- Guwahati Unnati project incentive issues resulting in temporary suspension.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhu Rathi vs Shiva Kabra on V-Shapes subsidiary losses and breakeven timeline:** Management defended ongoing losses as intentional long-term technology and IP-protected investments rather than wasted money, comparing it to software/AI investments that build long-term moats. Management did not provide a hard shutdown deadline, citing steady progress in execution and cost reduction.
2. **Kewal Shah vs Shiva Kabra on subsidiary loss reduction trend:** Management stated that the trend is structurally downward, though quarterly and half-yearly variations/lumpiness will occur based on sales bookings.
3. **Saket Kapoor vs Jaideep Barve & Shiva Kabra on core business H2 outlook and segment gross margin targets:** Management reiterated a target of 60% gross profit and 30% EBIT for the core coding and marking business, pointing to operating leverage from consumable innovations.
4. **Samad Singh vs Shiva Kabra on V-Shapes operational progress:** Management detailed a 25% reduction in cost base, replacement of 65%-70% of staff, addition of sales personnel, machine design retrofits expected to be tested in January, and temporary manufacturing relocation in Assam.
5. **Jayesh Gandhi vs Shiva Kabra on Track & Trace opportunity size:** Management outlined upcoming 2027-2028 government mandates for 1,000 brands and specific pharmaceuticals, estimating the current addressable market at INR 600-700 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Control Print's core coding and marking business remains steady and profitable, while management continues to absorb deliberate, long-term technology investments and subsidiary losses in packaging and track-and-trace segments.
