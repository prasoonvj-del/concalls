# Veranda Learning Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance in continuing operations with 17% revenue growth, a turnaround in profitability, and successful completion of the commerce demerger.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Updated due to the commerce demerger; continuing business (government test prep and academics) guidance maintained at INR 175 crore revenue, INR 46 crore EBITDA, and INR 32 crore PAT (previous consolidated guidance was INR 670 crore revenue, over INR 250 crore EBITDA, and INR 140 crore to INR 144 crore PAT).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to focus strictly on continuing businesses (government test prep, academics, managed schools, and SNVA stake) following the effective demerger of the commerce business on September 1, 2026.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Accounting treatment of fair valuation gain (INR 1,047 crore exceptional gain) and discontinued operations reporting for the commerce business (INR 53 crore PAT for 5 months).
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The NCLT scheme of demerger for the commerce business became effective on September 1, 2026, and record date passed on October 6, 2026, as promised.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (continuing business): INR 51 crore, up 17% YoY.
- H1 Revenue from operations (continuing business): INR 104 crore, up 34% YoY.
- Government test prep top-line growth: 31% YoY.
- Gross profit (Q2): INR 28.2 crore, up 12% YoY; gross margin at 56%.
- EBITDA (Q2): INR 12.7 crore, up 28% YoY (and up 8% QoQ from INR 11.8 crore in Q1); EBITDA margin at 25% (vs 23% in Q1).
- H1 EBITDA: INR 24.4 crore (vs loss of INR 17.6 crore in H1 FY26).
- Finance costs (Q2): INR 4.8 crore, down 58% YoY from INR 11.5 crore.
- PAT (Q2, continuing operations): INR 13.4 crore (vs net loss of INR 12.8 crore in Q2 FY26).
- H1 PAT: INR 25.6 crore (vs loss of INR 24.5 crore in H1 FY26).
- Discontinued commerce business PAT (5 months): INR 53 crore (including INR 21.6 crore in Q1).
- Exceptional gain: INR 1,047 crore (fair valuation of demerged commerce business).
- Book value per share (post-demerger parent): INR 54.
- Managed schools: 6 schools with over 5,400 students; target to scale to over 30 schools in the next few years.
- Karnataka expansion capex: INR 30 lakh to INR 35 lakh per center (interiors and rental deposit).
- FY27 guidance (continuing business): Revenue INR 175 crore, EBITDA INR 46 crore, PAT INR 32 crore.
- Long-term 5-year target (continuing business): Revenue INR 600 crore, EBITDA INR 120 crore to INR 140 crore.

### Highlights
- Scheme of demerger for the commerce business approved by NCLT, effective September 1, 2026; record date passed on October 6, 2026; J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited received in-principle approval for listing from SEBI, NSE, and BSE, expected to list in the next few days.
- Financial results from Q2 onwards reflect continuing operations (government test prep and academics), excluding the commerce vertical.
- Government test prep expanded course portfolio with Group 1 programs, Junior IAS, subscription-led offerings, and expansion into Karnataka (opened a center in Mysore, planning 4 centers this year and 4 next year).
- Advertising spends reduced materially, and balance sheet deleveraging lowered quarterly financial costs by 58% YoY.
- Consolidated financial statements recognized a one-time non-operational exceptional accounting gain of INR 1,047 crore from the fair valuation of the demerged commerce business.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 guidance for the continuing business at INR 175 crore revenue, INR 46 crore EBITDA, and INR 32 crore PAT.
- Management set a long-term 5-year target for continuing businesses to reach INR 600 crore in revenue and INR 120 crore to INR 140 crore in EBITDA.
- Management expects J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited to list in the next few days and anticipates a significant increase in value.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Increasing emphasis on employability, competitive examinations, and scalable education continues to validate the Veranda 2.0 strategy.
- Private equity entering the K-12 segment helps sector valuations and drives faster aggregation of smaller schools under management models.
- Government test prep sees strong 30% to 35% CAGR expectations in Karnataka, targeting INR 20 crore to INR 25 crore EBITDA in 4 to 5 years, matching Tamil Nadu and Kerala levels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Government test prep fees increase by 7% to 10% annually, while costs increase by about 4% annually.
- Government test prep student counts grew through incremental capacity utilization rather than new capacity additions.
- Current EBITDA margins of 23% to 25% are considered sustainable by management, even with initial expansion costs in Karnataka.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Government test prep network spans over 40 centers across South India.
- Capex for new government test prep centers (interiors and rental deposit) is INR 30 lakh to INR 35 lakh per center.
- Managed schools portfolio stands at 6 schools with over 5,400 students; target to exceed 30 schools in the next few years. Entering the preschool segment under the name "Veranda Sprouts".

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Post-demerger book value per share stands at INR 54.
- Finance costs declined by 58% YoY to INR 4.8 crore due to balance sheet deleveraging and refinancing initiatives.
- Free cash flows from non-commerce businesses will be plowed back into building government test prep and adding managed schools.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Suresh Babu (Individual Investor) on dividends and AGM location:** Management stated cash flows in the demerged commerce business will be evaluated for dividends by its separate board in future years, while non-commerce cash flows will be reinvested into growth; management agreed to consider organizing specific investor meetings at school locations instead of physical AGMs.
- **Riya Shah (Acme Capital) on academic segment stagnation and test prep margins:** Management explained academic revenue dips in Q2 were due to Q1 having book sales during the admission season, while H1 academic revenue actually grew 12% YoY; test prep margin expansion was driven by 7%-10% annual fee hikes, fixed operational costs, and higher capacity utilization.
- **Ashish Jain (AG Capital) on Karnataka expansion economics and accounting gain:** Management stated Karnataka center capex/deposit is INR 30 lakh to INR 35 lakh per center with expected 30%-35% CAGR; fair valuation gain on demerger was accounted under Ind AS 10 as no single shareholder holds over 51%, precluding common control accounting.
- **Sudarsan Narasimhan (Individual Investor) on PE in K-12, long-term guidance, and SNVA listing:** Management stated PE interest helps sector valuation and drives school aggregation; provided 5-year continuing business targets of INR 600 crore revenue and INR 120-140 crore EBITDA; stated SNVA Veranda (40% owned, operating online/physical universities in US, Europe, Middle East, and India employability training) is working on options for a listing in the near future.
- **Ravi Khandelwal (Individual Investor) on J.K. Shah listing value:** Management stated the demerger was executed to create sharply focused businesses with dedicated leadership, and expressed confidence that J.K. Shah's market leadership as the sole national player will command a significant valuation multiple.
