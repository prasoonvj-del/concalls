# Ardee Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - geopolitical disruptions and supply chain challenges affecting import raw material procurement and margins.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs. 338.8 crores (YoY growth of 35.2% vs Rs. 250.6 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 33.8 crores (vs Rs. 33.9 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10%
- **PBT:** Rs. 26.7 crores (YoY growth of 5%)
- **PAT:** Rs. 19.9 crores (YoY growth of 6%)
- **PAT Margin:** 5.9%
- **Sales Volume:** 17,645 metric tons (vs 15,529 metric tons in Q1 FY26)
- **Revenue Mix:** Domestic 61.3%, Exports 38.7%
- **Installed Capacity:** 1,56,950 MTPA as of June 30, 2026 (expanded from 1,04,025 MTPA effective May 29, 2026 via brownfield debottlenecking)
- **Working Capital:** Around 90 to 110 days
- **Net Debt:** Working capital limit utilization is almost zero due to IPO proceeds; long-term debt at around Rs. 20 crores created on 14th August. Cash/FDR on books: around Rs. 150 crores.

### Highlights
- Completed successful listing on BSE and NSE.
- Received empanelment of 'Ardee lead 99.97' brand with the London Metal Exchange (LME).
- Completed registration and handover of a 5.56-acre land parcel at Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh.
- Gross brownfield expansion capex stood at around Rs. 15 crores.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a 10% to 20% top-line and volume growth range for FY27, citing a conservative stance due to geopolitical disruptions.
- Expects optimal capacity utilization of 70% to 75% to be achieved by FY28.
- Sustainable ROCE expected to be maintained in the 20-plus range in coming years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying demand from lead-acid batteries remains broad-based across automobiles, backup power, telecom, and renewable energy storage.
- Top 4-5 clients cater to the maximum business; customer concentration of the largest customer has reduced over the years from 70% to around 40%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins moderated to 10% in Q1 FY27 (vs ~13% in FY26) due to higher domestic raw material and petroleum product costs, alongside geopolitical freight cost increases.
- Back-to-back LME-linked hedging mechanism is utilized daily to mitigate commodity price risk.
- Domestic scrap procurement faces challenges regarding availability, higher prices, and GST-related issues due to unorganized sectors.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Installed refining capacity increased by 50.9% to 1,56,950 MTPA via brownfield debottlenecking effective May 29, 2026.
- Naidupeta land parcel provides medium-to-long-term expansion flexibility.
- Evaluating multiple opportunities, including lithium-ion battery recycling and other metal recycling, but nothing concrete has been finalized.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds of Rs. 220 crores earmarked for incremental working capital requirements (scrap sourcing and export credit).
- Gross working capital requirement targeted at Rs. 400 crores plus for FY26-27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical conflicts causing supply chain disruptions, Middle East route blockages, and elevated trade/freight costs impacting raw material imports.
- Domestic scrap procurement constraints including higher pricing compared to imports and unorganized sector tax challenges.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aniket Madhwani (Steptrade Capital) asked about sequential volume decline despite new capacity.** Management explained it was due to geopolitical war disruptions affecting imported raw material supplies, leading to a conservative operational approach.
2. **Viviek Jhala (Jhala Family Office) asked about customer concentration and IPO proceeds deployment.** Management stated top 5 customers will continue to dominate, with single-customer concentration down to ~40%; IPO proceeds will fund new scrap sources and export credit.
3. **Anirudh Shamma (Ekaant Investments) asked about export strategy and LME hedging effectiveness.** Management confirmed exports enjoy better net margins due to government incentives/drawbacks, and daily hedging fully covers price risks with zero timing mismatches.
4. **Shruti Sharma (Sharda Family Office) asked about tangible progress from LME listing.** Management noted increased export inquiries and order conversions stemming directly from the LME registration.
5. **Ankur Gulati (Genuity Capital) asked about gross profit per ton dip.** Management cited higher domestically procured raw material costs and petroleum product expenses.

### One-line takeaway
Ardee Industries reported a 35.2% YoY revenue growth to Rs. 338.8 crores for Q1 FY27 alongside a capacity expansion to 1,56,950 MTPA, but guided conservatively for 10-20% growth due to geopolitical supply chain disruptions.
