# Bajaj Housing Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in AUM and disbursements, with resilient asset quality and stable return metrics.

### Key numbers
*   **AUM:** Rs 1.496 lakh crore (Growth: 24% YoY).
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 19,509 crore (Growth: 33% YoY; 11% QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 715 crore (Growth: 23% YoY).
*   **ROA (Annualized):** 2.3% (Stable).
*   **ROE:** 12.5% (Improved from 11.6% in Q1 FY26).
*   **GNPA:** 29 bps; **NNPA:** 12 bps.
*   **Credit Costs (Annualized):** 5 bps.
*   **Cost of Funds:** 7.2% (down 7 bps QoQ).
*   **NIM:** 3.7% (down 14 bps QoQ).
*   **Opex to Net Income:** 19.6% (improved from 21.2% in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Strong Growth:** Achieved highest-ever quarterly AUM growth (Rs 8,918 crore) and highest-ever quarterly disbursements.
*   **Portfolio Mix:** Home loans (54.1%), LRD (23.1%), Developer Finance (11.4%), LAP (10.3%).
*   **Sambhav Housing:** Monthly disbursement run rate increased to Rs 450-465 crore (from Rs 410-425 crore in Q4 FY26). Business is operational in 73 urban and 72 rural locations.
*   **Digital/AI:** Implemented AI initiatives across the loan lifecycle (voice agents, credit intelligence, collateral assessment, AI interview agents) to improve conversion and controllership.
*   **Asset Quality:** Stage 1 assets improved to 99.39%; Stage 2 assets improved to 0.32%.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **NIM:** Expected to moderate by 20-25 bps during FY27 compared to FY26 levels.
*   **Opex to Net Income:** Estimated at 19% to 20%.
*   **Asset Quality:** GNPA expected to remain in the 30-35 bps range; credit costs guided at 10-15 bps for the year.
*   **Profitability:** ROA expected in the 2.1% to 2.3% range; ROE expected between 12.5% to 13% for FY27.
*   **Leverage:** Likely to fluctuate between 5.8x to 6.3x.
*   **Sambhav Housing:** On track to achieve disbursement target of Rs 600 crore+ in the next 9 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Growth:** Home loan industry growth is estimated at 9-10.5% for FY27.
*   **Demand Dynamics:** Management described demand as "stabilized" or "slightly muted" compared to the previous two years, noting a shift from a price-momentum phase to a stabilization phase.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Acquisition pricing remains stable; management sees limited opportunities for upward repricing in the current stable interest rate regime.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Funds:** Moderated by 7 bps sequentially to 7.2%. Management expects Q2 cost of funds to be sideways with a downward bias.
*   **Borrowing Mix:** Money market (52%), Bank borrowings (38%), NHB refinance (10%).
*   **Margin Compression:** Driven by the natural attrition of higher-yield historical book being replaced by lower-yield new business in a stable interest rate environment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 21.59% (above regulatory requirements).
*   **Assignment:** The company assigned ~Rs 2,300 crore of assets during the quarter, contributing to lower Stage 1 provisioning.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Uncertainty:** Geopolitical factors and inflation impact remain areas of watch.
*   **BT-out Pressure:** While BT-out (balance transfer) pressure moderated slightly in Q1, management is watching for another quarter to confirm if this is a sustained trend.
*   **Asset Quality:** LAP GNPA inched up from 46 bps to 62 bps due to the movement of one account; Developer Finance GNPA stood at 12 bps due to one account moving from Stage 2 to Stage 3.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On NIM compression:** Management clarified that the 20-25 bps NIM moderation guidance is primarily due to yield compression as the portfolio reprices in a stable interest rate environment, rather than just cost of funds.
*   **On Credit Costs:** Analysts questioned the low 5 bps credit cost. Management explained this was a one-off due to the Rs 2,300 crore assignment and the absence of the Stage 2 provisioning accretion seen in Q4 FY26.
*   **On LAP/DF Asset Quality:** Analysts asked about the increase in LAP/DF NPAs. Management clarified these were isolated account movements (one in DF, one in LAP) and not indicative of broader segment stress.
*   **On Sambhav Housing:** Management confirmed the ticket size is stable at ~Rs 28 lakh, with a slight downward bias expected as they expand into non-metro/Tier 2/3 locations.
*   **On Regulatory Environment:** Management stated they do not view recent regulatory thematic audits as "overbearing" but rather as efforts to improve market resilience and customer interest.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Housing Finance delivered a strong Q1 with record growth and stable asset quality, though management remains cautious, guiding for NIM compression and muted industry demand for the remainder of FY27.
