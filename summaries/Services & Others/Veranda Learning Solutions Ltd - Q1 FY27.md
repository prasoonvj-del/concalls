# Veranda Learning Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 42% revenue growth, a 472% jump in PAT, and progress on the commerce demerger.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR 150 crore, up 42% YoY.
- PAT: INR 34 crore, up 472% YoY from INR 5.9 crore (6th consecutive quarter of positive PAT).
- EBITDA: INR 54 crore, up 10% YoY, with an EBITDA margin of ~36%.
- Total Enrollments: 1.03 lakh students, up 35% YoY.
- Collections: INR 165 crore, up 27% YoY.
- Segment Performance:
  - Commerce Test Prep: Revenue INR 108.6 crore (+53% YoY); EBITDA INR 42.7 crore (+58% YoY; ~40% margin).
  - Government Test Prep: Revenue INR 32.5 crore (+41% YoY); EBITDA nearly INR 4 crore.
  - Academic Segment: Revenue INR 12.2 crore (+22% YoY); EBITDA INR 9.2 crore (+53% YoY).
- Debt: Commerce debt at INR 125 crore; Non-commerce debt at INR 145 crore. Cost of debt at 9% to 9.5%.
- FY27 Guidance: Revenue ~INR 670 crore, EBITDA over INR 250 crore (or INR 260 crore), PAT ~INR 140 crore to INR 144 crore.

### Highlights
- Launched "Commerce Virtuals", a live and recorded digital delivery format for Class 11 and 12 students.
- Commerce demerger process on track: NCLT hearing scheduled for August 17, 2026; completion expected by first half of September 2026. JK Shah Commerce Education Limited to be separately listed with a 1:1 share swap.
- Transitioned fully into Veranda 2.0 (focused on consolidation, deleveraging, and profitable scale).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided FY27 revenue at ~INR 670 crore, EBITDA over INR 250 crore / INR 260 crore, and PAT at INR 140 crore to INR 144 crore.
- Commerce business FY27 target: Revenue ~INR 450 crore, EBITDA ~INR 215 crore, PAT ~INR 110 crore. Long-term aspiration: cross INR 1,000 crore revenue by FY30.
- Non-commerce business FY27 target: Revenue ~INR 220 crore, EBITDA ~INR 46 crore, PAT ~INR 34 crore.
- FY27 strategic priorities: Expand government test prep into Karnataka, enter preschool managed operations, add 15 new managed commerce colleges, and establish offline presence in North/West India (UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Gujarat).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural tailwinds in Indian education and skilling, including employability-linked learning, rising competitive exam aspirations, and digital-first delivery models.
- Commerce segment holds the #1 market position across CA, CS, CMA, and ACCA programs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated EBITDA margin at ~36%. Offline commerce margins peak at 35%; online margins at 45%-48%.
- EBITDA growth impacted by a high base in Q1 FY26 (which included a INR 17 crore one-time other income), higher advertising/marketing spend for the commerce brand ahead of demerger, and initial expenses for 15 new managed commerce colleges.
- Sustainable quarterly finance cost expected at INR 7.5 crore to INR 8 crore.
- Tax expense turned negative (reversal of INR 3 crore) due to a INR 7.35 crore tax reversal from earlier years following a subsidiary merger.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Offline commerce network spans over 105 centers with 50 new commerce colleges under management in the pipeline (adding 15 new managed colleges in FY27).
- Managed schools: 6 schools with over 5,400 students.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt stands at INR 270 crore (INR 125 crore in commerce, INR 145 crore in non-commerce) at a reduced interest rate of 9% to 9.5%.
- No deferred consideration payouts for the next 12 to 15 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya (Investor) on market recognition:** Management noted that completed restructuring, reduced debt costs (9-9.5%), and the upcoming JKSC listing will drive valuation, alongside consistent PAT-positive quarters.
- **Prachi Shah on revenue/collection divergence and segment splits:** Management explained offline commerce revenue is ~INR 330 crore and online is ~INR 120 crore; enrollment vs collection divergence is driven by subject-wise offerings and Ind AS 11 deferred revenue recognition.
- **Ishitaa on EBITDA growth vs revenue growth:** Management clarified that adjusting for the Q1 FY26 one-time other income of INR 17 crore, actual EBITDA grew ~30%, and margins were temporarily impacted by pre-demerger ad spends and college expansion costs.
- **Yash Modi on NCLT demerger timeline:** Management stated that orders are reserved, the next hearing is on Monday, and listing is expected by next month (September 2026).
- **Rahil Kothari on debt allocation and valuation:** Management stated the commerce business is an undisputed market leader and expects it to command a superior multiple upon listing.

### One-line takeaway
Veranda Learning Solutions reported a strong Q1 FY27 with 42% revenue growth and its sixth consecutive PAT-positive quarter, while nearing the separate listing of its commerce business by September 2026.
