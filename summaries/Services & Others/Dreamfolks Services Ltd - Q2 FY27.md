# Dreamfolks Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited ongoing geopolitical challenges, revenue contraction YoY, and continued net losses, though noting sequential margin improvements and traction in non-airport services.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 31.1 crore (Q2 FY27), compared to Rs 39.0 crore (Q1 FY27) and Rs 205.5 crore (Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 70.1 crore, compared to Rs 554.5 crore (H1 FY26).
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Negative Rs 8.8 crore (Q2 FY27), improved from negative Rs 16.4 crore (Q1 FY27).
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA:** Negative Rs 25.2 crore.
- **Loss After Tax (PAT):** Negative Rs 10.7 crore (Q2 FY27), narrowed from negative Rs 13.8 crore (Q1 FY27).
- **H1 FY27 Loss After Tax:** Negative Rs 24.6 crore.
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 8.7% for H1 FY27 (return to positive gross profit).
- **Bank Balances and Liquid Investments:** Rs 187.8 crore as of September 30, 2026, compared to Rs 148.9 crore as of March 31, 2026.
- **Net Worth:** Rs 290.1 crore as of September 30, 2026, against Rs 313.8 crore as of March 31, 2026.
- **Non-Airport Lounge Services Contribution:** More than one-third of the topline during Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
- Improved adoption of new-age boarding-pass-based benefits programmes within credit card offerings.
- Expansion of railway lounge presence with the addition of a new lounge at Nashik; Ten11 Hospitality owns and operates railway lounges in Chennai, Mumbai, and Vadodara.
- Lifestyle offerings like Coffee BOGO and members-only private club access saw encouraging traction and accounted for over 33% of Q2 FY27 topline.
- DF Club Membership went live on the loyalty platforms of multiple major banks.
- Launched Flight Cancellation for Any Reason service with Mastercard at Global Fintech Fest in September 2026.
- Partnered with Elixir for a wellness-focused credit card (powering the premium spa benefit) and with The Card Company for a premium prepaid metal card on the RuPay network.
- Launched DreamFolks Club Nagavalli Wilderness Estate (private club in the Eastern Ghats across ~30 acres, including a 25-acre golf course, 37 cottages, spa, pool, and restaurant).
- Global lounge network stands at more than 1,100 lounges, with encouraging traction in South-East Asia.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated priorities for the rest of FY27 include deepening distribution/adoption of launched services, driving programme activity across global networks, scaling the railway business through Ten11, and expanding lifestyle offerings.
- Focused on execution, improving contribution and revenue absorption, disciplined capital allocation, and translating growth investments into sustained scale.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Banks and card networks are increasingly showing interest in services beyond traditional lounge benefits, such as Coffee BOGO, private club access, and flight cancellations.
- Growing preference among affluent credit card customers for exclusive, differentiated, and personalized experiences beyond conventional travel benefits.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sequential improvement in profitability was partly driven by the absence of one-time upfront minimum guarantee payments made in Q1 (incurred for global lounge expansion). No similar one-off expense was incurred in Q2.
- Gross profit margin reached 8.7% for H1 FY27, supported by an improved business mix and economics of newer service offerings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phasing capital expenditure appropriately while closely monitoring service-level economics and operating cash flows.
- Expanding direct operational presence in the railway lounge segment via subsidiary Ten11 Hospitality.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Prudent liquidity management with a focus on aligning investment pace with business development.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical challenges impacting business.
