# Bajaj Auto Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company delivered record-breaking financial performance across all parameters despite supply chain disruptions and hyperinflationary commodity costs.

### Key numbers
*   **Volumes:** 1.4 million units (+29% YoY).
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 17,244 crore (+37% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 3,596 crore (+45% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 20.9% (+110 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** ~Rs 3,000 crore (+42% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 21,689 crore (+65% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 3,226 crore (+46% YoY).
*   **Free Cash Flow:** >Rs 2,300 crore.
*   **Surplus Cash:** >Rs 21,000 crore (as of June quarter end).

### Highlights
*   **Exports:** Achieved record 7,32,000 units; accounts for 40% of total revenue.
*   **EV Business:** Achieved largest-ever quarter; contributes 30% of domestic revenues with double-digit EBITDA margin.
*   **Chetak:** Volumes grew 80% YoY; now EBITDA positive.
*   **Pro-Biking (KTM & Triumph):** Combined domestic volumes ~40,000 units (+50% YoY).
*   **BACL (Bajaj Auto Credit Ltd):** AUM reached Rs 20,000 crore (+70% YoY); ROE >25%.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Management** expects supply chain disruptions to ease in Q2.
*   **Management** aims to increase exports beyond 2,50,000 units/month.
*   **Management** plans to complete a portfolio makeover for the 150cc segment within 6 weeks and introduce 2 new brands in the 125cc segment within FY27.
*   **Management** targets capacity expansion from 7 million to 9 million units per annum in the medium term.
*   **Management** expects Q2 to reflect broader cost inflation across proprietary components, electronics, labor, and logistics.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Domestic 2W industry grew 14% YoY; motorcycle industry grew 7-8%.
*   Growth is driven by EV scooters and the 150cc-400cc motorcycle segment.
*   100cc and 125cc segments are growing at low single digits.
*   **Management** noted a "K-shaped" recovery where the upper half of society is economically stronger than the bottom of the pyramid.
*   EV penetration in scooters is ~25% all-India, with some states >50%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Commodity inflation was ~4.5% of revenue in Q1.
*   Steel prices rose >10%; aluminum, platinum, and rhodium rose ~40%.
*   Pricing actions offset ~50% of inflation.
*   Rupee depreciation (realized USD-INR rate 94.4 vs 90.6 in Q4) provided a cushion against cost increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Current capacity: 7 million units/annum.
*   Planned capacity: 9 million units/annum.
*   Immediate focus: Unlock EV 2W capacity to 60,000 units/month.
*   Network: Chetak reach >530 exclusive stores; KTM-Triumph joint stores ~90 operational.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Surplus cash >Rs 21,000 crore.
*   Cash conversion: ~80% of PAT.
*   BACL Capital Adequacy Ratio: 19%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Supply chain and logistics disruptions (impacted availability by 10-15%).
*   Ransomware attack (defended, but caused temporary production suspension).
*   Hyperinflationary commodity environment (metals, proprietary components, energy, labor).
*   Geopolitical developments in West Asia affecting logistics and fuel availability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is the 100cc segment weak?** **Management** cited economic pressure on the bottom of the pyramid and a consumer shift toward EV scooters.
*   **Q: Strategy for 100cc market share?** **Management** stated they will participate on their own terms regarding profitability, prioritizing the 125cc+ segment.
*   **Q: Cash usage?** **Management** clarified that July payouts (dividend + buyback) total Rs 10,000 crore, reducing cash, but expects to rebuild to ~Rs 15,000 crore by year-end.
*   **Q: KTM/Bajaj synergy?** **Management** clarified KTM is run independently; manufacturing collaboration continues based on business logic.
*   **Q: EV 2W profitability?** **Management** confirmed double-digit EBITDA margin for the EV portfolio, with Chetak now EBITDA positive.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Auto delivered record-breaking Q1 FY27 results driven by premium segment growth and EV scaling, while management remains focused on navigating broad-based cost inflation and capacity constraints.
