# Grasim Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly consolidated revenues driven by strong execution in core businesses alongside rapid scaling in paints and B2B e-commerce.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 48,716 crore, up 21% YoY.
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 11,795 crore, up 28% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 1,094 crore, up 107% YoY.
- **Birla Opus Paints Revenue:** Rs 1,661 crore, up 64% YoY and 17% QoQ.
- **Birla Pivot Revenue:** Rs 2,548 crore, up 75% YoY (annualized run rate above Rs 10,000 crore).
- **Cellulosic Fibres (CSF) Revenue:** Rs 4,530 crore, up 12% YoY; sales volume down 4% YoY; EBITDA roughly doubled YoY.
- **Chemicals Segment Revenue:** Rs 2,640 crore, up 10% YoY; EBITDA Rs 491 crore, up 16% YoY.
- **Caustic Soda ECU Realization:** Rs 37,955 per ton, up 6% YoY (SEA caustic soda exit price USD 483/ton).
- **UltraTech Cement (Consolidated):** Sales volume 41.31 million tons (+12% YoY); EBITDA Rs 5,146 crore (+12% YoY).
- **Aditya Birla Capital:** Total lending portfolio Rs 2,20,000 crore (+32% YoY); housing finance portfolio crossed Rs 50,000 crore (+50% YoY).

### Highlights
- Grasim reported its 24th consecutive quarter of YoY consolidated revenue growth.
- **Birla Opus Paints:** Emerged as India's 3rd largest decorative paints brand by revenue; sequential market share expanded by 30 bps. Total retail distribution expanded to 12,100 towns, 55,000+ billed dealers, and 1,450+ exclusive franchise outlets.
- Premium and luxury products contributed ~65% of Birla Opus sales value; institutional business grew 85% YoY.
- **Birla Pivot:** Private label sales more than doubled YoY; platform repeat purchases remained robust across infrastructure, manufacturing, and industrial sectors.
- **Royalty Fee:** Adopted a structured governance framework to pay a group brand royalty of 0.25% on standalone revenues (effective June 1, 2026), with a company cap of Rs 225 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Paints (Birla Opus):** Management reiterated its guidance to achieve Rs 10,000 crore revenue by FY28 and reach profitability at that scale. Expects >50% YoY revenue growth in FY27.
- **B2B E-commerce (Birla Pivot):** On track to achieve EBITDA break-even by the exit of FY27.
- **Chlorine Integration:** Management expects chlorine integration to reach 68% by the exit of FY27.
- **Leverage Target:** Management guided to maintain consolidated Net Debt to EBITDA below 2.0x for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global economy faces uncertainties from geopolitical conflicts, shipping disruptions, transit delays, and commodity price fluctuations.
- India's macro backdrop remains resilient, supported by strong consumption and infrastructure capex, with RBI raising FY27 GDP growth forecast to 6.7%.
- In paints, double-digit price increases across the industry led to channel stocking in Q1.
- In VSF/cellulosic fibers, global supply remained disciplined (led by China), while cotton land/water constraints continue to widen the structural "cellulosic gap."

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Paints absorbed raw material cost shocks (COGS inflation of 20–25%) via phased price hikes totaling 8.8% in Q1 FY27, with the remainder flowing into Q2.
- Chemical EBITDA margins improved due to strong realization across caustic soda, chlorine derivatives, and specialty chemicals.
- Cement segment benefited from lower power and logistics costs, with green power mix rising to 45.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Standalone FY27 Capex Budget:** Rs 3,157 crore (45% targeted at growth capex); Q1 FY27 spent Rs 375 crore (~12% of full-year budget).
- **Paints:** Total installed manufacturing capacity stands at 1,332 million liters per annum across six operational plants.
- **Cellulosic Fibres:** 55,000 TPA Lyocell Phase 1 project (Rs 1,350 crore) civil work is progressing; 110,000 TPA Phase 2 project (Rs 3,094 crore) is under environmental clearance.
- **Cement:** Added 8.7 million tons of gray cement capacity in Q1 FY27, bringing total global capacity to 205.5 million tons.
- **AB Renewables:** Grasim’s equity contribution toward the business will be under Rs 1,000 crore in FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Consolidated Net Debt to TTM EBITDA:** Improved to 1.45x as of June 30, 2026 (vs 1.62x as of June 30, 2025).
- **Standalone Net Debt:** Increased to Rs 9,899 crore, primarily due to a timing mismatch between a Rs 2,880 crore equity investment in Aditya Birla Capital (June 2026) and incoming UltraTech dividend inflows (August 2026).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile shipping, container shortages, and raw material cost fluctuations in international chemical markets.
- Planned plant maintenance and soft downstream domestic demand temporarily impacted Q1 VSF and caustic soda sales volumes.
- Seasonal slowdown expected in Q2 demand due to monsoons and channel inventory destocking in decorative paints.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities):** Asked whether Paints sequential growth (17% QoQ) matching industry peers indicates maturing growth or prioritization of breakeven over scale.  
  *Management Answer:* Clarified that peer Q1 growth was boosted by trade channel pre-stocking ahead of price hikes. Birla Opus did not benefit from channel stocking to the same extent and maintains its focus on market share gains and the Rs 10,000 crore revenue guidance.
- **Prateek Kumar:** Queried the impact and cap of the new group brand royalty fee.  
  *Management Answer:* Royalty is 0.25% of standalone revenues effective June 1, 2026, with an overall cap of Rs 225 crore. At an estimated Rs 50,000 crore standalone revenue, the impact is ~Rs 125 crore (direct standalone impact ~Rs 100 crore for the year); subsidiaries will pay independently based on their own revenues.
- **Raashi:** Inquired about short-term margin sustainability in the Chemicals segment given global uncertainties.  
  *Management Answer:* Gulf refinery slowdowns and erratic petrochemical feedstocks make forecasting difficult; management uses daily pricing and avoids large inventory positions to control margin risk. Q2 will face some pressure from consuming higher-cost Q1 inventory.
- **Siddharth Mehrotra:** Asked if standalone net debt has peaked and sought clarity on equity outlay for the Renewables business.  
  *Management Answer:* Confirmed Net Debt to EBITDA will stay comfortably below 2.0x for the full year. Equity infusion into AB Renewables will be less than Rs 1,000 crore in FY27.
- **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities):** Asked about Birla Pivot’s profitability timeline and its exposure to UltraTech Building Solutions (UBS) outlets.  
  *Management Answer:* Front-loaded tech/manpower investments accelerated growth; Birla Pivot is on track to exit FY27 at EBITDA break-even. Retail accounts for 15–20% of mix, with UBS contributing 70–75% of that segment.

### One-line takeaway
Grasim delivered strong 21% YoY revenue growth in Q1 FY27 as core cement, fiber, and chemical cash flows effectively funded rapid market share gains in paints and B2B e-commerce.
