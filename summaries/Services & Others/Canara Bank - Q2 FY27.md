# Canara Bank - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-17 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported "ever highest" profit and business milestones, with performance metrics consistently beating initial guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management maintained original guidance but noted they are "bettering" it across all parameters (e.g., Advances growth of 19.46% vs 11-12% guidance).
- **ECL Provisioning:** Estimate for ECL impact increased to Rs 12,000-15,000 crore (from Rs 10,000-12,000 crore in Q1) due to hardening yields.
- **Treasury:** Shifted from a profit of Rs 555 crore in Q1 to a loss of Rs 50 crore in Q2 (MTM impact).
- **CASA:** Ratio stood at 29% (vs 29.70% in Q1), attributed to the influx of FCNR(B) term deposits.
- **New Topics:** Management confirmed the sale of the 'Canbank Factors' subsidiary (expected 4X gain) and addressed the drought situation in parts of Karnataka/Maharashtra (minimal impact expected).

### Key numbers
- **Total Global Business:** Rs 30,72,000 crore (+15.88% YoY).
- **Global Deposits:** Rs 16,97,000 crore (+13.13% YoY).
- **Global Advances:** Rs 13.75 lakh crore (+19.46% YoY).
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 11,000+ crore (+20.39% YoY).
- **Net Profit:** Rs 5,175 crore (+8.40% YoY).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.33% (Guidance: 1.50%).
- **Net NPA:** 0.30% (Guidance: 0.40%).
- **CRAR:** 17.19%.
- **PCR:** 95.34% (+175 bps YoY).
- **NIM:** 2.55% (Quarterly).
- **ROE:** 18.49%.
- **EPS:** Rs 22.06 (+5% YoY).
- **Slippage Ratio:** 0.58% (annualized).
- **Credit Cost:** 0.47% (-21 bps YoY).

### Highlights
- **RAM Growth:** 21.03% YoY (Retail: 31.61%, Housing: 17.35%, Vehicle: 25.50%, MSME: 15.49%).
- **FCNR(B) Deposits:** Garnered $5.8 billion (approx. Rs 55,000 crore), which replaced higher-cost bulk deposits.
- **Gold Loan Portfolio:** Total Rs 2.82 lakh crore (Agri: Rs 1.74 lakh crore; Non-Agri: Rs 1.08 lakh crore). Yield improved to 9.10% (from 8.96% in Q1).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management intends to sustain NIM at 2.55%.
- Management plans to absorb the ECL impact (Rs 12,000-15,000 crore) over 2 years, despite having a 5-year regulatory window.
- No plans to revise guidance; management is focused on "bettering" existing targets.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Retail lending demand remains "very robust."
- Corporate pipeline: Rs 47,000-50,000 crore (sanctioned/hot leads) in sectors including green energy, battery storage, data centers, and power.
- Drought impact: Management stated exposure to affected areas is Rs 13,900 crore and expects minimal impact on recovery due to SLBC dispensations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Treasury:** MTM loss of Rs 61 crore for the quarter due to yield hardening.
- **Bulk Deposits:** Maintained at June levels (Rs 4.38 lakh crore) by replacing them with FCNR(B) funds, saving 100 bps in cost.
- **Repo-linked book:** 53% of the book is repriced.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **LCR:** 115% as of 30th September.
- **SMA-2 (all tickets):** 1.43%.
- **SMA-0 (all tickets):** 0.99%.
- **Total Stress (all tickets):** 2.42%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Yield Hardening:** Increased provisioning requirements for HTM/investment books.
- **Treasury Volatility:** Rising yields negatively impacted treasury performance this quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Q: Why did CASA dip?* Management explained it was due to the large influx of FCNR(B) term deposits; without this, CASA would have met guidance.
- *Q: ECL impact timeline?* Management clarified they aim to absorb the Rs 12,000-15,000 crore impact over 2 years, not 5, given strong profitability.
- *Q: Gold loan competition?* Management expressed confidence in their internal ecosystem (appraisers/safes) and noted yields improved to 9.10% despite competition.
- *Q: Merger rumors?* Management denied any government-led merger pressure, stating early results were a top-management decision to free up operational time.
- *Q: Treasury loss?* Management explained the shift from Q1 profit to Q2 loss was due to 44 bps hardening of yields in the quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Canara Bank delivered a strong quarter with record profits and improved asset quality, successfully navigating treasury headwinds by replacing high-cost bulk deposits with FCNR(B) funds.
