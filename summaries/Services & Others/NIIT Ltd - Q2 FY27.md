# NIIT Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue declined YoY due to GSI spending compression, but EBITDA improved and BIPS/AI segments grew.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 982 million (down 6% YoY, up 3% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 1,939 million (up 3% YoY)
- **Enterprise Revenue (Q2):** INR 625 million (down 11% YoY)
- **Enterprise BIPS Revenue (Q2):** INR 200 million (all-time high, up 17% YoY, up 66% QoQ)
- **Consumer Revenue (Q2):** INR 357 million (up 3% YoY)
- **Technology Programs Revenue (Q2):** INR 661 million (down 13% YoY)
- **BIPS Product Revenue (Q2):** INR 321 million (up 11% YoY)
- **Order Intake (Q2):** INR 1,301 million (10 new logos acquired)
- **EBITDA (Q2):** INR 27 million (margin of 2.7%, up from INR 13.5 million in Q2 FY26; negative INR 14 million in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 EBITDA:** INR 12.5 million (vs negative INR 49 million in H1 FY26)
- **Net Other Income (Q2):** INR 65 million (includes treasury income of INR 41 million)
- **Net Loss (Q2):** INR 22 million (vs PAT of INR 14 million in Q2 FY26)
- **EPS (Q2):** Negative INR 0.16 (vs INR 0.10 in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 PAT:** INR 59 million; EPS: INR 0.43
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** INR 6,889 million
- **Capex (Q2):** INR 44 million (vs INR 69 million in Q2 FY26)
- **BR Days:** 63 days (up from 53 days in Q1)
- **Employee Count:** 816 (down 50 QoQ, down 124 YoY)
- **AI Contribution to Revenue:** 12.4% (up from 9% in Q1)

### Highlights
- Appointed Anish Sarkar as new CEO.
- Consumption from the top 4 GSI clients dropped by as high as INR 100 million YoY in Q2, impacting overall revenue.
- Enterprise versus consumer revenue mix was 64:36 in Q2 (vs 67:33 in Q2 FY26).
- Partnered with two large banks to establish dedicated wealth and sales academies.
- Selected as the learning transformation partner by a top bank in India.
- Concluded a legacy tax matter in favor of NIIT, resulting in a small legal cost exceptional item (INR 3.5 million).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue to be broadly flat for the remainder of the year.
- Management expects low single-digit EBITDA margin to continue for the year.
- Volumes from GSIs are expected to remain under pressure for the next couple of quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- GSI clients experienced consumption compression due to lower onboardings and cost-cutting pressures leading to in-sourcing.
- AI is reshaping enterprise demand, driving talent transformation and rapid growth in AI/GenAI programs.
- Generic AI training is becoming commoditized and in-sourced by companies, while specialized leadership training and productivity-linked AI tools remain in demand.
- GCCs are battling cost pressures but present an unpenetrated opportunity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses declined 8% YoY due to cost optimization measures, simplification, and productivity initiatives.
- Headcount reduction driven by un-replaced attrition, back-office trimming, and AI-led development efficiencies (over 50% gains in working efficiencies).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure moderated in Q2 (INR 44 million) as original projects got capitalized.
- Continued evaluation of inorganic opportunities that enhance capabilities, channels, or IP.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at INR 6,889 million.
- BR days increased to 63 days due to delayed collections (subsequently collected in October).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic uncertainty, rising interest rates, and cybersecurity threats.
- Capital-intensive manufacturing and client sectors grappling with higher interest rates and energy costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kashish Mehta (Dolat Capital) on headcount trimming vs. AI efficiencies:** Management explained that reductions stem from a mix of AI development/sales efficiencies, back-office trimming, and variable cost adjustments, while strategic investments continue in targeted areas.
- **Kashish Mehta on revenue mix stabilization:** Management stated that the historical two-thirds technology and one-third BIPS/other industries mix is expected to remain approximately stable.
- **Ganesh Shetty (Individual Investor) on investment phase and EBITDA:** Management noted that capex has moderated as projects capitalize, and internal efficiencies have driven positive EBITDA despite lower revenue.
- **Ganesh Shetty on integration of RPS and StackRoute:** Management stated that combining sales teams under ETLS (Enterprise Technology Learning Solutions) brings synergies and a larger solution array, though integration cost-structure benefits take a couple of quarters to realize.
- **Ganesh Shetty on expansion into new sectors (manufacturing/EV):** Management confirmed work-in-progress initiatives in modern manufacturing and EV, but noted that capital-intensive sectors face near-term economic pressures.

### One-line takeaway
NIIT’s Q2 FY27 revenue declined 6% YoY due to GSI spending pullbacks, but aggressive cost optimization enabled an EBITDA turnaround, while AI and BIPS segments showed strong growth.
