# Glottis Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Good revenue growth, higher sequential EBITDA, and expanding business mix despite a mixed operating environment.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 2,345 million (+39.5% YoY, +19.7% QoQ)
- **Total income:** Rs. 2,366 million
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 163 million (vs. Rs. 169 million in Q1 FY26 and Rs. 105 million in Q4 FY26)
- **EBITDA margin:** 6.9% (+150 bps QoQ)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 107 million
- **PAT margin:** 4.6%
- **Ocean freight container throughput:** 21,841 TEUs
- **Sea Import revenue:** Rs. 1,647 million (+24.1% YoY, 70% of revenue)
- **Sea Export revenue:** Rs. 467 million (+83.5% YoY, 19.9% of revenue)
- **Air Import revenue:** Rs. 66 million (+97.1% YoY)
- **Air Export revenue:** Rs. 50 million (+240.4% YoY)
- **Road transport revenue:** Rs. 107 million (+107.8% YoY, 4.6% of revenue)
- **Warehousing revenue:** Rs. 9 million
- **Trade receivable days:** 77 days (down from 87 days at the end of FY26)
- **Owned fleet strength:** 80 vehicles (added 38 owned vehicles in Q1)
- **Customer additions:** 260 new customers; repeat customers at ~75%; top 5 customers contributed ~29%

### Highlights
- Revenue growth supported by better realizations and a favorable business mix, offsetting lower container throughput.
- Broadening customer base: top 5 customer concentration reduced to ~29%; management aims to reduce top 5 concentration to 15%-20% in the next 2-3 years.
- Backward integration advancing: 42 trailers added in Q1 (total fleet 80); container deployment slated for Q3, with the full Rs. 132 crore IPO capex program on track to be implemented by March.
- CRISIL monitoring agency report confirmed IPO proceeds were used as planned with no deviations.
- Geographical expansion plans include scaling up field focus in Hyderabad, Kolkata, and Ahmedabad.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue to exceed FY25 numbers (management).
- Targets higher margins compared to the current quarter (management).
- Entire IPO capex of Rs. 132 crores to be fully implemented by the end of March (management).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment across global logistics and freight markets remained mixed with uneven trade lane activities.
- India's merchandise exports grew, driven by engineering, chemical, and electronic goods.
- Renewable energy remains the largest industry vertical at 38% of revenue (opening remarks noted 13%, later clarified in CFO remarks as 38% for the quarter). Consumer durables contributed 10%, engineering products 8%, and chemicals 7%.
- Asia contributed 84% of revenue, North America 10%, Europe 3%, Africa 2%, and South America 1%. US trade concentration increased closer to 10% from 4%-5% previously.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating costs increased in line with higher business activity while pricing remained competitive.
- Focus remains on improving operating leverage, managing costs, and maintaining working capital discipline.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 38 owned vehicles in Q1 (taking total owned fleet to 80; CFO mentioned adding 42 trailers in Q1).
- Container deployment to start in Q3.
- Full IPO capex of Rs. 132 crores to be deployed by March.
- Expanding network presence with field-focused development in Hyderabad, Kolkata, and Ahmedabad.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivable days improved to 77 days at quarter-end compared to 87 days at the end of FY26 due to normalization of newer customer accounts entering steady billing cycles.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Minor impact from geopolitical situations (such as the conflict involving Iran and the US) on supply availability and pricing, though mitigated well as an Asia-dominated player.
- Higher operating cost base impacted profitability despite revenue growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Disha (Sapphire Capital) asked about geopolitical impacts on trade lanes and vessel availability:** Management replied that while there are minor impacts on price and supply availability, it is not major given Glottis is primarily an Asian-dominated player focusing on imports from the Far East to India.
- **Disha (Sapphire Capital) asked about the target for top 5 customer concentration:** Management stated they expect it to reach 15%-20% over the next 2-3 years as they diversify their customer base.
- **Rajiv Pandey asked about air freight strategy:** Management noted that while historically a sea-based company, they have scaled air freight over the last 2 years and expect it to give a major revenue boost going forward.
- **Rajiv Pandey asked about network expansion beyond Ahmedabad:** Management highlighted plans to expand and increase field focus in Hyderabad and Kolkata alongside Ahmedabad.

### One-line takeaway
Glottis reported a 39.5% YoY jump in Q1 FY27 revenue to Rs. 2,345 million driven by better realizations and export growth, while advancing its backward integration capex and customer diversification goals.
