# Bajaj Auto Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company achieved record-breaking performance across volumes, revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and free cash flow, despite persistent hyperinflationary pressures and supply chain constraints.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained focus on capacity expansion (6.25L to 7.75L units/month) and export run rates (~7.5L/quarter).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "expecting supply chain to ease" (Q1) to acknowledging "increasingly volatile and erratic" supply chains that restricted EV plans to 75% of target.
- **New topics/risks:** Explicit mention of "steel prices firming up" as a new Q3 cost pressure; management highlighted a "K-shape" economic recovery becoming more accentuated.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the 150cc portfolio refresh (N-series/Classic); Chetak capacity expansion to 80k/month is now targeted for Q4 (previously Q2/Q3 focus).

### Key numbers
- **Volumes:** >15 lakh units (+20% YoY).
- **Revenue:** >Rs 20,000 crore (+34% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 4,172 crore (+37% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.9% (flat QoQ, +40 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 3,354 crore (+35% YoY).
- **Free Cash Flow:** ~Rs 4,000 crore (100%+ of PAT).
- **Export Volumes:** 7.8 lakh units (+41% YoY).
- **EV Business:** ~Rs 5,500 crore revenue (H1); contributes >20% of enterprise revenue.
- **PLI Accrual:** Effective rate reached 18% in Q2 (16% base + 2% booster).

### Highlights
- **Record Performance:** Highest ever volumes, revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and free cash flow at the corporate level.
- **Exports:** Second consecutive quarter above 7 lakh units; market share in 2W exports from India rose to 48% (from 44% in FY26).
- **Domestic 2W:** Focus on 125cc+ segment; new launches (Pulsar 125/150/160) now account for 33% of festive sales.
- **EV Business:** Chetak achieved 1 million units milestone; business is now margin-accretive at the corporate level.
- **Pro-Biking:** 40,000 units sold in Q2 (+31% YoY); 120 joint KTM-Triumph showrooms operational.
- **BACL:** AUM ~Rs 21,000 crore; ROE 26%.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Management** expects September-December festive growth to be in double digits, though likely lower than the 18% seen in April-August due to base effects.
- **Management** plans to expand total capacity from 6.25 lakh to 7.75 lakh units/month over the next 6-9 months.
- **Management** targets 80,000/month Chetak capacity by Q4.
- **Management** expects to launch 2 new brands in the 125cc-250cc space in FY27.
- **Management** expects PLI accrual rate to remain at 18% for the second half of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Industry Trends:** 2W industry grew 18% (April-August); festive registrations (first 16 days) grew 10%, driven by 150cc+ and EV scooters (+24%).
- **K-Shape Recovery:** **Management** noted the "weaker section of society has become weaker," while the top half of the pyramid drives growth; expects this trend to persist.
- **EV Penetration:** Scooter segment penetration is ~25%; **Management** believes this will continue to grow due to economic factors and fuel availability concerns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Inflation:** Input cost inflation was 3-3.5% of revenue in Q2 (following 4-4.5% in Q1).
- **Cost Drivers:** Electrical components (mid-teens), proprietary parts/conversion charges (double-digit), and metals (high single-digit to double-digit).
- **Mitigation:** Pricing actions covered 50-60% of H1 inflation; balance offset by favorable currency (realized USD-INR 95.5) and cost optimization.
- **Pricing:** Took ~30% price hike in October to cover estimated Q3 inflation; future hikes depend on market absorption.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capacity:** Currently 6.25 lakh units/month; expanding to 7.75 lakh units/month.
- **Network:** Chetak exclusive stores at 550+; plan to add 75 more in Q3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash Conversion:** 100%+ of PAT.
- **BACL:** Capital adequacy ratio ~19.5%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Supply Chain:** "Increasingly volatile and erratic" disruptions at both back-end (components) and front-end (logistics/shipping).
- **Commodities:** Steel and copper prices remain "firm and stiff."
- **Currency:** Recent RBI measures to support the Rupee have made it a potential headwind for Q3 realizations.
- **Demand:** Potential drought-like conditions in some states (Maharashtra, South, Northeast) could impact Q4 harvests and demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why is market share not reflecting in VAHAN data?** **Management** explained that the 100cc segment (where they are losing volume by choice to protect margins) and the "Classic" Pulsar channel stock clearance are dragging down overall numbers, while the new 150cc+ portfolio is gaining share.
- **Q: Sustainability of EV margins post-PLI?** **Management** stated they are working on platform cost reductions to mitigate the eventual loss of PLI benefits.
- **Q: Export run rate sustainability?** **Management** aims for 7.5 lakh units/quarter; noted that Q3/January are seasonally lower, but remains optimistic due to new segment openings and KTM collaboration.
- **Q: Impact of weak monsoon on rural demand?** **Management** noted the "K-shape" recovery is becoming more accentuated, with rural/bottom-of-pyramid demand under pressure, potentially impacting Q4.
- **Q: Pricing strategy?** **Management** clarified they will not take pricing actions to cover currency movements (as they are transient) but remain agile on commodity-linked pricing.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Auto delivered a record-breaking quarter driven by premiumization and EV scaling, but faces a challenging Q3 characterized by persistent commodity inflation, volatile supply chains, and a K-shaped domestic demand environment.
