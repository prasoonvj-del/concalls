# Novus Loyalty Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth across all segments, successful international expansion, and strategic inorganic growth via the AutoPay acquisition.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 98 crore (vs Rs 71 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** ~38% YoY (implied from total revenue figures).
*   **Revenue Segments (H1 FY27):**
    *   Technology: Rs 7.5 crore (~50% YoY growth).
    *   Redemption: Rs 36 crore (~25% YoY growth).
    *   Merchant Promotion: >Rs 5 crore (~37% YoY growth).
    *   Digital Vouchers: Rs 49 crore (~45% YoY growth).
*   **AutoPay Acquisition:** 80.87% stake; deal value ~Rs 130 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Business Model:** Operates four revenue lines: Technology/Platform fees (7% of gross revenue), Reward Redemption (39%), Merchant Promotion (6%), and Digital Vouchers (48%).
*   **Strategic Acquisition:** Signed SPA to acquire 80.87% of AutoPay Payment Solutions to gain access to payment technology, PPI licenses, and transit payment systems (IRCTC, Metro).
*   **Product Innovation:** Revamped product suite using 12 specialized AI agents ("Noxcy AI") to automate campaign suggestions and improve merchant monetization.
*   **Client Wins:** Onboarded Central Bank of India; expanded international footprint with two banks in Africa and new engagements in Saudi Arabia.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Targets:** Management targets Rs 500 crore (consolidated) by FY29 and Rs 600-650 crore by FY30.
*   **International:** Expects ~Rs 7-8 crore revenue from Middle East/Dubai in FY27, growing to Rs 12-15 crore in FY28.
*   **AutoPay:** Expects 15-20% YoY growth and sustainable PAT margins of 17-20% in the long run.
*   **H2 FY27:** Management expects performance to be at least in line with H1 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Growth:** Global loyalty market growing at ~15% CAGR; Indian loyalty market expected to double by 2034.
*   **Trends:** Shift toward low-value, high-frequency redemptions in banking loyalty programs.
*   **Seasonality:** No major seasonality, though summer holidays, Diwali, and New Year see 2-10% higher engagement.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins by Segment:** Technology and Merchant Promotion are high-margin (100% net revenue); Redemption business has 16-18% gross margins; Digital Vouchers currently have 5-7% margins (expected to improve with PPI license).
*   **AI Impact:** AI agents currently reduce account management costs; management plans to monetize AI separately in the future.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **International:** Dubai subsidiary established; planning US/Australia expansion via local subsidiaries or small acquisitions to address data localization and liability requirements.
*   **Funding:** AutoPay acquisition funded via share swap (20%), internal accruals (Rs 40 crore), and a planned preference round for the remaining ~Rs 40-45 crore due in June 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Status:** Company is debt-free.
*   **Inventory/Working Capital:** Inventory buildup in H1 due to unbilled work-in-progress (e.g., Bank of Abyssinia, NPCI) and unsold gift voucher inventory.
*   **Payment Cycles:** Merchant promotion payments typically received in 30-45 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Client Hesitancy:** US and Australian BFSI clients hesitant to work with Indian entities due to data localization and liability concerns (necessitating local presence).
*   **AutoPay Margins:** AutoPay PAT margins declined from 21.9% to 13.8% due to R&D investments in new DMRC virtual card technology and minimum monthly guarantees (MMG).
*   **Unbilled Revenue:** Significant portion of revenue is "unbilled" work-in-progress, creating timing differences in revenue recognition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did AutoPay margins drop?** Management explained it was due to R&D costs for new DMRC virtual card technology and MMG payments, which will normalize as revenue kicks in.
*   **Q: Why acquire a payment company?** Management stated it provides access to PPI licenses, reduces card issuance fees, and allows collection of payments from merchants, improving cash flow.
*   **Q: How will you monetize AI?** Currently part of the enterprise platform; management plans to charge separately for AI agents as they mature.
*   **Q: Why was H2 FY26 lower than H1 FY26?** Management attributed this to client-specific agendas and campaign pushes rather than inherent business seasonality.
*   **Q: What is the competitive landscape?** Competes with BillDesk (Loyalty Rewards) domestically; GIIFT, Concilion, and Vernost internationally.

### One-line takeaway
Novus Loyalty is aggressively scaling its enterprise loyalty platform through AI integration and inorganic expansion into payments (AutoPay), aiming for a 5-6x revenue increase by FY30.
