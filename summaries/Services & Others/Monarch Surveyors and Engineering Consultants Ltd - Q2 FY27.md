# Monarch Surveyors and Engineering Consultants Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited healthy financial performance, expanding capabilities, international entry via Australia, and a strong unchurned order book providing multi-year visibility.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 99.36 crore, up 38.19% YoY (vs Rs 71.9 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Standalone EBITDA excluding other income (H1 FY27):** Rs 26.18 crore, up 27.33% YoY (vs Rs 20.56 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 26.35% (vs 28.60% in H1 FY26).
- **Standalone PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 20.11 crore, up 54.93% YoY (vs Rs 12.98 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Standalone PAT Margin (H1 FY27):** 20.24%, up 219 bps YoY (vs 18.05% in H1 FY26).
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 100.91 crore.
- **Consolidated EBITDA excluding other income (H1 FY27):** Rs 24.89 crore.
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 24.67%.
- **Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 18.82 crore.
- **Consolidated PAT Margin (H1 FY27):** 18.65%.
- **Unchurned Order Book:** Rs 850 crore to Rs 860 crore as of September 30, 2026; estimated around Rs 950 crore to Rs 970 crore including October orders.
- **Employee Count:** Increased to 972 employees as of the current presentation (from roughly 700 in March).
- **Trade Receivables:** Rs 77 crore total; unbilled revenue at Rs 10.8 crore; debtors outstanding for more than 1 year at Rs 6.5 crore to Rs 8 crore.
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** Rs 14.8 crore as of September (down from Rs 28.5 crore due to working capital deployment).
- **Total Debt:** Rs 11 crore.

### Highlights
- Completed the acquisition of GM & FE Ryan Pty Limited (Australia), reflecting consolidated operations for the expanded group in H1 FY27.
- Secured major orders post-September: approximately Rs 48.10 crore from Western Central Railway and Rs 51.20 crore from Southern Railway.
- Secured a Rs 130 crore Northern Railway DPR project (3-year execution horizon, including ~2,000+ railway track DPRs, land acquisition, and clearances).
- Entered the exclusive clearances vertical, winning a mandate for a Maharashtra airport development greenfield area to obtain central and state government clearances.
- Government-funded projects account for approximately 90% of revenue dependence.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated that the top-line trend of 20% to 25% growth could be exceeded based on execution.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to revert closer to the 30% range in H2 FY27.
- Unchurned order book is executable within a 24 to 60 month (2.5 to 3 years) horizon.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Infrastructure sector is shifting toward greater sophistication, requiring detailed engineering, accurate surveys, geospatial data, land services, and digital tools (LiDAR, drone surveys, GIS mapping, 3D scanning).
- Growth is supported by government initiatives like PM Gati Shakti, National Infrastructure Pipeline, and sustained investments in railways, roads, urban development, water, and logistics.
- Around 90% of the company's business depends on government-funded projects (Indian Railways, state road departments, Government of Maharashtra).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin moderated by 225 bps YoY to 26.35% due to the new wages code increasing gratuity amounts, Diwali bonuses, annual increments, and increased manpower deployment treated as an investment.
- Deferred/higher site expenses, traveling, and training costs impacted sequential margins in H1.
- Effective tax rate stood at 10% to 12% in H1 FY27 due to deferred tax assets (DTA) carried forward.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanded international presence through the acquisition of Australian entity GM & FE Ryan Pty Limited (contributing Rs 2.5 crore in revenue and a loss of Rs 1.29 crore over 3 months post-acquisition).
- Scaled up employee strength from ~700 in March to 972 employees to support rising execution demands.
- Deployed cash reserves from fixed deposits into working capital to fund project execution and target higher IRRs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt stands at ~Rs 11 crore (primarily vehicle/machinery loans); debt-to-equity ratio is 0.4% with an interest coverage ratio of 25x.
- Cash and bank balance reduced from Rs 28.5 crore to Rs 14.8 crore due to deployment into project working capital.
- Trade receivables total Rs 77 crore, with ~80% outstanding for less than 6 months, ~10% between 6 months and 1 year, and ~10% (Rs 6.5 crore to Rs 8 crore) outstanding for more than 1 year. Unbilled revenue stands at Rs 10.8 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government project execution can face timing delays, milestone dependencies, and contractor availability constraints.
- Subsidiary GMR recorded a net loss of Rs 1.29 crore in its initial 3 months under consolidation during the gestation/integration phase.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rahul Jain asked about revenue translation speed from the large order book and margin normalization.** Management replied that execution works on a FIFO basis with manpower ramp-up acting as an investment, and expects H2 and FY27 consolidated results to show acceleration with EBITDA margins returning closer to 30%.
2. **C. Jagannathan questioned why standalone and consolidated employee costs appeared identical and why Australian revenue contribution was low.** Management explained that Australian accounting standards classify certain employee expenses under project-related expenses, and the Australian entity was consolidated for only 3 months post-July acquisition.
3. **Divya Daga asked for GMR's H1 financial contribution and trade receivable aging details.** Management stated GMR reported Rs 2.5 crore revenue and a Rs 1.29 crore loss over 3 months, while total receivables stood at Rs 77 crore with unbilled revenue at Rs 10.8 crore and >1 year dues at Rs 6.8 crore.
4. **Gunit Singh inquired about the recoverability of old receivables and ECL provisioning.** Management stated that older dues primarily relate to variations and security deposits awaiting conversion to realizations, with zero risk of bad debts despite government payment delays.
5. **Yash Parker questioned the drop in cash balances.** Management explained that cash previously held in FDs was redeployed into project working capital to capture higher IRRs.

### One-line takeaway
Monarch Surveyors reported robust standalone revenue (up 38.19% YoY) and PAT growth in H1 FY27 alongside a record order book of ~Rs 950-970 crore, while consolidating its Australian acquisition and guiding for margin recovery in H2.
