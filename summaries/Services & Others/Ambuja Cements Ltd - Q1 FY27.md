# Ambuja Cements Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasized disciplined execution, structural cost leadership, and a strategic shift toward value-accretive trade sales despite geopolitical cost pressures.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 9,500 crore.
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1,589 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 16.7% (improved 331 bps).
*   **EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 931 (Gray cement: Rs 911).
*   **Net Operating Cost:** Rs 4,241 per metric tonne (reduction of Rs 206 per tonne QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 660 crore.
*   **Trade Sales Share:** 78% (up from 74%).
*   **Premium Products:** 34% of trade sales.
*   **Clinker Factor:** 64% (improved ~3%).
*   **Blended Cement Share:** 85%.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Shift:** Management is prioritizing value over volume, resulting in a 2% YoY decline in trade volumes and a 21% YoY decline in non-trade volumes.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Performed scheduled maintenance on 12% of kilns (cost impact Rs 50/tonne).
*   **Inventory:** Maintained 1 month of clinker and 3 months of coal inventory.
*   **Logistics:** Reduced primary lead distance by 20 km, saving Rs 10/tonne.
*   **Energy:** RE power capacity reached 973 MW (up 500 MW YoY); WHRS capacity at 228 MW. Power cost reduced from Rs 5.9/kWh to Rs 4.9/kWh.
*   **Capacity Optimization:** Temporarily suspended ~3.5 million tonnes of capacity (old ACC/acquired assets) for ~6 months to optimize costs and efficiency.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Targeting 8% growth for FY27.
*   **Cost Target:** Reaffirmed guidance of Rs 4,250 per tonne for FY27.
*   **Green Power:** Targeting 60% green power share by FY28.
*   **AFR:** Targeting 10-15% utilization in FY27.
*   **Capacity:** Targeting 119 million tonnes by end of FY27.
*   **Capex:** Budgeted ~Rs 6,500 crore for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains stable, supported by infrastructure, housing, and construction.
*   July trade volumes showed 8% YoY growth.
*   South cluster: Consciously reduced low-margin volumes; focusing on channel network expansion.
*   North/West/East: Seeing good traction.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Absorbed Rs 110/tonne cost impact from West Asia geopolitical tensions.
*   Targeting additional savings of Rs 130-150/tonne via logistics, green power, and heat/power consumption efficiency.
*   Fly ash and RE power sales (Rs 140 crore revenue in Q1) are being netted off against operating costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current Capacity:** 109 million tonnes.
*   **Commissioned/Trial:** Dahej (1.2 MT), Salai Banwa (2.4 MT), Bhatinda (1.2 MT), Jodhpur (2 MT).
*   **Upcoming:** Kalamboli (1 MT) and Warisaliganj (2.4 MT) expected in Q2.
*   **Future:** Maratha clinker line expected in FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Ambuja (standalone) has zero debt.
*   Capex spent in Q1: ~Rs 1,500-1,600 crore (~25% of annual budget).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions in West Asia impacting fuel and freight costs.
*   Input cost volatility.
*   Monsoon impact on near-term demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Volume Decline:** Management clarified that the 7% total volume decline was a calculated move to exit low-margin non-trade business, not a loss of market share in the high-margin trade segment.
*   **Mothballing:** Management clarified that the suspension of ~3.5 MT capacity is a temporary optimization measure (~6 months), not a permanent closure.
*   **Green Power Strategy:** Management explained they are in a transition phase; while they sold surplus power (Rs 140 crore revenue in Q1), the goal is 100% in-house consumption within 3 quarters.
*   **Acquisitions:** Management defended the acquisition of Sanghi, Penna, and Orient as valuable assets, noting they are in advanced stages of merger and operational integration.
*   **Cost Reconciliation:** Management acknowledged the complexity of reconciling cost-per-tonne figures due to the netting off of power/fly ash sales and offered to provide detailed breakdowns offline.

### One-line takeaway
Ambuja is aggressively prioritizing value over volume, evidenced by a shift to 78% trade sales and a Rs 206/tonne sequential cost reduction, while maintaining a clear path to 119 MT capacity by FY27-end.
