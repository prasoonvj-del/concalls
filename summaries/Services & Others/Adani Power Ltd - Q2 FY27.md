# Adani Power Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly results driven by strong power demand, improved PLFs, and successful capacity expansion, despite higher fuel costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained capacity expansion targets (45 GW by 2032).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "summer heatwave" focus in Q1 to "structural baseload demand" and "data center" growth potential in Q2.
- **New topics/risks:** Derecognition of Rs 1,970 crore (approx. Rs 20 billion) in revenue related to Haryana DISCOM claims (conservative accounting).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Korba Phase-II (Unit 1) achieved grid synchronization (on track); Mahan Phase-II remains targeted for early FY28.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 7,019 crore (up 141.5% YoY).
- **Continuing Revenue:** Rs 17,528 crore (up 28.5% YoY).
- **Reported Revenue:** Rs 19,951 crore (includes Rs 2,422 crore one-time prior period revenue).
- **Continuing EBITDA:** Rs 6,321 crore (up 18.5% YoY).
- **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 8,743 crore (up 45.7% YoY).
- **Power Sales:** 26.4 billion units (up 11.5% YoY).
- **PLF:** 70.1% (vs 62.8% in Q2 FY26).
- **Total Debt:** Rs 61,860 crore (vs Rs 53,556 crore in March '26).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 45,846 crore (vs Rs 45,022 crore in March '26).

### Highlights
- **Credit Rating:** Upgraded to AA+ by CARE and India Ratings.
- **Capacity Expansion:** Secured 25-year PPA for 1,320 MW from Uttarakhand Power Corporation (to be supplied from Korba 2).
- **New Ventures:** Declared successful resolution applicant for GVK Energy (includes 330 MW Alaknanda hydro plant); signed JV with Druk Green Power for 770 MW hydro project in Bhutan.
- **PPA Pipeline:** 62% of the 24 GW thermal pipeline is now tied up under long-term PPAs.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Capacity:** Targeting 45 GW total capacity by 2032 (18 GW current + 24 GW thermal pipeline + 3 GW inorganic).
- **Peak Debt:** Expected to reach Rs 1 lakh crore by FY30/FY31.
- **Nuclear:** Awaiting notification of government rules/regulations before proceeding with capacity expansion.
- **Demand:** Management expects continued strong demand, noting data centers will be "significant power guzzlers" requiring reliable 24/7 thermal baseload.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Market:** India's power demand rose 11.5% YoY to 502 billion units in Q2.
- **Merchant Market:** Merchant/short-term volumes were 43% lower YoY as capacity was strategically tied up under medium-term contracts; however, merchant tariffs surged 33% per unit.
- **Hydro:** Lower hydro output due to weak monsoons increased reliance on thermal power.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Fuel Cost:** Rs 10,229 crore (up 41.8% YoY), driven by higher volumes and ~50% increase in imported coal landed costs.
- **Finance Cost:** Rs 760 crore (down 9.7% YoY) due to disciplined debt management.
- **Accounting:** Reversed Rs 1,970 crore revenue claim regarding domestic coal shortfall with Haryana DISCOM to adopt conservative accounting.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Korba Phase-II:** First 660 MW unit synchronized; commercial commissioning expected in Q3; second unit planned for Q4 FY27.
- **Mahan Phase-II (1,600 MW):** Targeted for early FY28.
- **Raipur Phase-III:** Adding 1,600 MW ultra-supercritical plant (total site capacity 4,570 MW).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** Capex is primarily funded through internal accruals; debt is used only for funding gaps.
- **Receivables:** Received ~Rs 6,000 crore from GUVNL, aiding internal accruals and keeping net debt flat despite gross debt increase.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Accounting/Claims:** Management reversed revenue recognition for Haryana DISCOM claims (domestic coal shortfall) because the issue is not fully settled.
- **Fuel Price Lag:** Imported coal price increases have a time lag in pass-through mechanisms (e.g., Gujarat PPAs), potentially impacting margins in subsequent quarters.
- **Seasonality:** PLFs are expected to normalize to ~65% as hydro generation recovers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On Haryana DISCOM claims:** Management clarified they reversed the revenue recognition (50% of claim) on auditor advice despite having received the cash, as the dispute is ongoing.
- **On Data Centers:** Management clarified they are not signing direct PPAs with data centers; power supply is handled via trading platforms or DISCOMs.
- **On Interest Costs:** Management expects finance costs to remain flat despite rising market rates, citing the AA+ rating upgrade and disciplined debt management.
- **On EBITDA Growth:** Management attributed growth to full utilization of newly acquired capacities and shifting from merchant to medium-term PPAs.
- **On Nuclear/Hydro Targets:** Management reiterated that Adani Power focuses on international hydro (Bhutan), while Adani Green handles domestic hydro.

### One-line takeaway
Adani Power posted record profitability and secured a credit rating upgrade, leveraging strong thermal demand and disciplined PPA tie-ups to fund its 45 GW expansion roadmap.
