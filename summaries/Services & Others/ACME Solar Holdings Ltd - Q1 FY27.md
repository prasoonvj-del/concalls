# ACME Solar Holdings Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record revenue and EBITDA growth driven by high capacity utilization and early BESS deployment.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Revenue:** Rs 954 crore (+63% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 831 crore (+56% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** ~87% (Renewable: 88-89%; BESS: ~82%).
*   **PAT:** Rs 235 crore (+80% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** ~25%.
*   **CUF:** 30.9% (vs 28.5% YoY).
*   **Power Generation:** 2,020 million units (+23% YoY).
*   **BESS Revenue:** Rs 226 crore.

### Highlights
*   Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA.
*   Successfully completed QIP to prepone capex for under-construction projects.
*   **BESS Operations:** Commissioned 2.3 GWh in Q1; cumulative capacity 3.62 GWh (40% of India's commissioned share).
*   **Incident Update:** Confirmed ACME Suryodaya BESS incident was caused by an electrical short circuit in AC cabling; no damage to battery systems.
*   Secured Rs 6,000 crore financing for 700 MW FDRE projects.
*   Debt tied up for ~85% of PPA-signed portfolio.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Capex:** Upgraded to Rs 15,000 crore – Rs 20,000 crore for FY27.
*   **BESS Commissioning:** Upgraded guidance to >10 GWh by FY27 (brought forward by 3 quarters).
*   **Renewable Capacity:** Expect to commission 1.5 GW of contracted capacity in FY27, subject to transmission/substation availability.
*   Management expects BESS revenue run rate to remain strong for the next 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Aggregate electricity demand grew 8.8% YoY to 485 billion units; peak demand reached 271 GW.
*   Management expects peak power demand to rise due to "duck curve" phenomena and EV adoption.
*   Expects large upcoming bids in FDRE, RTC, and peak power categories; no pure solar bids expected in the future.
*   States are increasingly evaluating BESS to meet peak power requirements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **BESS Capex:** ~Rs 93 lakh/MW (includes transformer, EPC, etc.).
*   **Module Prices:** Sub-Rs 12/unit (range ~Rs 11.60).
*   **Core Renewable EBITDA Margin:** ~91% (excluding BESS).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Total Portfolio:** 8,070 MW (includes 20 GWh battery storage).
*   **Under-construction:** 5,080 MW.
*   Signed 600 MW FDRE/hybrid PPAs with SECI in Q1.
*   1,200 MW of projects pending PPA signing.
*   Ordered >15 GWh of batteries (CATL, Hithium).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Asset Base:** ~Rs 25,000 crore.
*   **Q1 Capex:** ~Rs 3,000 crore.
*   **Hedging:** Hedged >$300 million.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Curtailment:** Contributed <1% to revenue; management plans to add batteries to state projects to mitigate.
*   **Transmission/GNA:** Delays in transmission readiness affect commissioning timelines.
*   **BESS Volatility:** Lithium carbonate prices and supply chain fluctuations.
*   **Regulatory:** Changes in ISTS waiver and GNA connectivity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Realizations for BESS short-term contracts?* A: Realizations range from Rs 8 to Rs 10 per unit.
*   *Q: Strategy for merchant BESS?* A: Management aims for 0 open capacity by year-end; all BESS is intended for PPA-backed operations.
*   *Q: Impact of CERC's free exit option?* A: Management views this positively as it frees up connectivity for better utilization.
*   *Q: Sustainability of BESS spreads?* A: Management does not see a slowdown in pricing for the next 3 years due to unmet peak power demand.
*   *Q: Interest in data centers?* A: Management is exploring this segment and building internal capabilities/teams.

### One-line takeaway
ACME Solar delivered record Q1 performance driven by high CUF and early BESS deployment, with management aggressively scaling BESS capacity and targeting long-term PPA-backed growth.
