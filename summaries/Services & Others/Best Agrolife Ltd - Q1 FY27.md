# Best Agrolife Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management highlighted resilient performance and significant profitability growth driven by a strategic shift toward high-margin patented products, despite weather-related demand challenges.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 396 crore (+4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 78 crore (+70% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 20% (vs 12% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 41 crore (+104% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 10% (vs 5% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Gross Profit:** Rs 146 crore (+32% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 37% (vs 29% in Q1 FY26).
*   **OpEx (incl. finance/depreciation):** Rs 92.97 crore (+4.5% YoY).
*   **Inventory:** Rs 764 crore (down 6% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Shift:** Increased contribution of patented products to 64% of branded sales (vs 45% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Volume Growth:** Branded sales volume grew 13% YoY; patented portfolio volume grew 37% YoY.
*   **Strategy:** Discontinued select generic products to focus on higher-value branded and patented offerings.
*   **Pricing:** Successfully passed on raw material cost inflation (linked to US-Iran conflict) via selective price increases.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management targets a 10-15% revenue CAGR over the next 3-4 years.
*   **Margins:** Management believes a 13-14% EBITDA margin is sustainable under normal circumstances.
*   **Demand:** Management expects demand for herbicides, insecticides, fungicides, and PGRs to improve in coming quarters as crops enter vegetative/reproductive cycles.
*   **Sentiment:** Management remains "cautiously optimistic" for the Rabi season.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Weather Impact:** Q1 demand was subdued due to delayed monsoons, irregular rainfall, and above-normal temperatures.
*   **Crop Impact:** Lower crop establishment reduced demand for seed treatment and early-stage herbicide/insecticide applications in soybeans, cotton, vegetables, chilies, and groundnuts.
*   **Reservoirs:** Current storage is 81.5 billion cubic meters (vs 87.6 billion expected); management expects improvement in the South.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Expansion attributed to favorable product mix (patented focus), selective price hikes, and operational efficiencies.
*   **Product Margins:** Patented products typically command ~40% gross margins, while generics range from 15-30%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Status:** Capex plans are currently on hold to focus on strengthening existing business and stabilizing financials.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Inventory levels reduced by 6% YoY; creditor payments have normalized.
*   **Fundraising:** The previous QIP warrant conversion did not occur; management is in discussions regarding future fundraising options.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Seasonality/Volatility:** Management created a Rs 60 crore provision for sales returns to mitigate potential volatility in Q3/Q4.
*   **External Factors:** Ongoing monitoring of El Niño impact and raw material price fluctuations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did performance drop over the last 3 years?** A: Management cited a China price crash, inventory issues, and high R&D/market development spend as primary factors.
*   **Q: Are the current margins sustainable?** A: Management stated that the shift toward a higher patented product mix (60-65% of branded sales) supports the sustainability of 13-14% EBITDA margins.
*   **Q: How prudent is the sales return provision?** A: Management provided Rs 60 crore (approx. 20% of sales) as a buffer to handle potential returns in Q3/Q4.
*   **Q: Will you raise funds via QIP?** A: Management confirmed the previous QIP closed without full conversion and is currently discussing future options with investors.
*   **Q: Why is Capex on hold?** A: Management stated the priority is to stabilize the existing business and improve profitability before committing to new capacity.

### One-line takeaway
Best Agrolife delivered strong profitability growth through a strategic shift toward high-margin patented products, despite a challenging, weather-impacted Q1.
