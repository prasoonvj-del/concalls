# Steamhouse India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted the best Q2 in the company's history with YoY volume and profitability growth, despite seasonal monsoon headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Steam Volumes (Q2 FY27):** 3,01,642 tonnes (up 12.06% YoY; down 1.36% QoQ)
- **Steam Volumes (H1 FY27):** 6,07,459 tonnes (up 14.35% YoY)
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 128 crore (up 2.4% YoY from Rs 124.94 crore)
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 256.62 crore (up 7.63% YoY from Rs 238.42 crore)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 24.68 crore (up 73.38% YoY from Rs 14.24 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 19.28% (vs 11.39% YoY)
- **Clean EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 22.50 crore (up 79.15% YoY)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 7.77 crore (up 167.14% YoY from Rs 2.91 crore)
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 6.07% (vs 2.33% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 55.62 crore (up 65.72% YoY from Rs 33.56 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 21.67% (up 759 bps YoY from 14.08%)
- **Clean EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 51.72 crore (up 71.13% YoY)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 26.11 crore (up 99.56% YoY from Rs 13.09 crore)
- **PAT Margin (H1 FY27):** 10.18% (vs 5.49% YoY)
- **EBITDA per tonne (H1 FY27):** ~Rs 916 (up 44.93% YoY from Rs 632)
- **Capacity Utilization:** Q2 FY27 at 42.41% (vs 35.28% in Q2 FY26, 41.40% in Q1 FY27); H1 FY27 at 41.91% (vs 37.03% YoY)
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.16x at H1 FY27 end (vs 1.57x in FY26)
- **ROCE / ROE (Not annualized, H1 FY27):** ROCE at 12.97%; ROE at 4.53%
- **Una (Himachal Pradesh) EPC Contract:** ~Rs 311 crore inclusive of GST

### Highlights
- Delivered the best Q2 in the company's history despite seasonal monsoon challenges and a temporary shutdown at a third-party steam procurement facility.
- Signed an MoU with the Haryana Enterprise Promotion Center for a proposed investment of Rs 500 crore to explore industrial infrastructure/common boiler opportunities.
- Secured Una (Himachal Pradesh) Bulk Drug Park community boiler project (300 tonnes per hour) with an EPC mandate of ~Rs 311 crore and long-term O&M.
- Repeat customers accounted for ~99.48% of H1 FY27 revenue.
- Transitioned coal purchases from credit to cash, securing cash discounts and improving gross profit/EBITDA per tonne.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a target of 25%+ volume growth for FY27 over FY26 (implying ~13,25,000 tonnes total volume).
- Management expects FY27 volume growth to accelerate in H2 (>35% YoY growth in H2 over H1) driven by new facilities coming live.
- Management guided that full-year EBITDA per tonne should range between Rs 1,100 to Rs 1,200, supported by upcoming waste-to-steam facilities.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 is seasonally a soft quarter due to monsoons impacting industrial demand, wet coal, and transmission losses.
- Demand drivers remain intact supported by industrial parks, manufacturing clusters, and government sustainability initiatives.
- New sectors added include food processing and adhesives, alongside core chemical, agrochemical, pharmaceutical, and textile (via Pirana) clusters.
- ~95% of steam sales volume comes from continuous usage (24-hour) customers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steam supply agreements include pass-through mechanisms for coal price changes (with an approximate 15-day lag).
- Imported Indonesian coal (4,200 GCV) cost ranged between Rs 9,000 to Rs 11,000 per tonne during the quarter.
- Waste-to-steam facilities feature ~50%-55% lower opex compared to coal-fired projects, though capex is 1.5x to 1.75x higher.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Jhagadia facility is commissioning in November 2026, with ~14.8 tonnes per hour contracted out of 30 tonnes per hour.
- Expansions underway at Nandesari, Vapi, Tarapur, and Pirana; Dahej SEZ and Haldia operations are being planned.
- IPO-funded projects include Ankleshwar Phase 3, Panoli Phase 2, and Dahej GIDC Phase 2.
- Industrial gases: A 750 nm³ per hour PSA nitrogen plant at Ankleshwar is planned for commissioning by the end of CY2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity improved to 0.16x at H1 FY27 end, down from 1.57x in FY26, following debt repayment from IPO proceeds raised in September 2026.
- Operating cash flow turned negative due to paying off trade payables early to utilize IPO proceeds and capture cash discounts; trade payables are now near zero.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Q2 seasonality (monsoons, floods in Surat/Vapi/Ankleshwar/Nandesari) causes lower operational efficiency and temporary demand softening.
- Availability and quality of waste represent the sole limiting factors for expanding waste-to-steam projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rabindra Nath Nayak (Bang Institutional Equities) asked about the QoQ drop in EBITDA per tonne and higher interest costs:** Management clarified that interest costs rose in Q2 because debt repayments via IPO proceeds occurred late in the quarter (September 17), and interest run-rates will drop below Q1 levels in subsequent quarters. The EBITDA per tonne drop was attributed strictly to monsoon weather headwinds.
- **Alex Matthews (Trinity Capital) questioned the credibility of guidance given the sudden Q2 margin drop:** Management reiterated that Q2 softness is a known historical trend communicated previously, and expressed full confidence in achieving the guided 25% volume growth and margin recovery in H2.
- **Pawan Kumar (Institutional Investor) asked about negative operating cash flows:** Management explained it resulted from utilizing IPO proceeds to clear trade payables to secure cash discounts, reducing payables to near zero.
- **Mihir questioned the rationale of adding capacity while existing plants are underutilized:** Management explained that new plants (like Vapi Phase 2) target distinct clusters or are geographically separated by highways where laying cross-pipeline permissions take 1.5 to 2 years.
- **Krisha Kansara (Boring AMC) inquired about supply disruption penalty clauses:** Management noted that most contracts supply steam subject to availability, with minor clauses tied to minimum guaranteed supply on a quarterly basis (excluding mandatory annual maintenance), and no penalties have ever been paid.

### One-line takeaway
Steamhouse India reported its best-ever Q2 with strong YoY volume and EBITDA growth despite seasonal monsoon headwinds, and remains confident in achieving its 25% volume growth guidance for FY27 driven by new waste-to-steam capacity additions.
