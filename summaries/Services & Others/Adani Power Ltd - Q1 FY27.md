# Adani Power Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-29 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by record quarterly performance, robust power demand, and successful capacity expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 17,550 crore (up 28% YoY).
*   **Total Continuing Revenue:** Rs 17,936 crore (up 27% YoY).
*   **Reported Revenue:** Rs 19,322 crore (up ~33% YoY).
*   **Continuing EBITDA:** Rs 6,983 crore (up 22% YoY).
*   **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 8,369 crore (up 36% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 4,867 crore (up 47% YoY).
*   **Power Generation:** 31 billion units (highest ever).
*   **Power Sales Volume:** 28.8 billion units (up 17% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PLF:** 78% (vs 67% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 47,643 crore (Total debt: Rs 58,381 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Performance:** Achieved highest-ever quarterly generation and sales, supported by high summer temperatures and peak demand (271 GW in May '26).
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Acquired Jaiprakash Associates' power assets (180-MW Churk plant, 24% stake in Jaiprakash Power Ventures, 11.49% stake in Prayagraj Power Generation Company).
*   **PPA Signings:** Signed a 25-year PPA with Maharashtra DISCOM for 1,600 MW (2x800-MW Ultra-Supercritical plant).
*   **Brand Recognition:** Ranked India's most valued energy brand by Brand Finance (USD 1.8 billion value, AAA rating).
*   **Accounting:** Reported a one-time net recognition of prior period revenues of Rs 1,386 crore due to historical energy charge revisions.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Capacity Target:** Management revised the expansion vision to 45 GW (up from 42 GW).
*   **Capex:** Projected at Rs 25,000 crore for FY27, Rs 33,000 crore for FY28, and >Rs 35,000 crore thereafter.
*   **Nuclear:** Targeting 10 GW capacity by 2035; awaiting government rules/guidelines before finalizing technology or investment plans.
*   **Funding:** Board approved an enabling provision for a QIP to manage the interim gap between cash accruals and capex.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Conditions:** Strong demand driven by sustained heatwaves; thermal power remains the mainstay for baseload requirements.
*   **Merchant Market:** Merchant volumes decreased to 4 billion units (vs 6 billion units in Q1 FY26) as capacity is increasingly tied up under long-term PPAs.
*   **Future Bids:** Management expects significant new thermal capacity bids from various states due to resource adequacy deficits.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Fuel Costs:** Rs 9,513 crore (up 30% YoY), driven by higher dispatch volumes and imported coal indices.
*   **Realizations:** PPA tariff realization improved 8% to Rs 5.93/unit; merchant/short-term realization improved 13% to Rs 7.04/unit.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Projects:**
    *   **Korba Phase-II (1,320 MW):** On track for commissioning by end of 2026.
    *   **Mahan Phase-II (1,600 MW):** Scheduled for commercial operation in Q1 FY28.
    *   **Raipur/Raigarh Phase-II:** Progressing at 62% and 54% respectively.
    *   **Mirzapur (1,600 MW):** Execution commenced.
*   **Strategy:** 56% of upcoming capacity already tied up under long-term PPAs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Leverage:** Net debt-to-EBITDA is slightly above 2x; management expects it to remain between 2x and 3x.
*   **Bangladesh Receivables:** Currently ~USD 400 million; receiving ~USD 100 million monthly.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory Uncertainty:** Nuclear expansion plans are contingent on pending government rules.
*   **Seasonality:** Power drawdown typically decreases during monsoon months.
*   **Execution:** The 180-MW Churk plant is not yet operational and requires revival (no contribution expected in FY27).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On QIP/Equity Dilution:** Management clarified the QIP approval is an "enabling provision" to avoid seeking shareholder approval repeatedly; funding for capex will primarily come from internal accruals.
*   **On Nuclear Strategy:** Management confirmed they are evaluating both domestic and foreign technologies but cannot finalize plans until government rules under the Act are notified.
*   **On Bangladesh Receivables:** Management noted a significant reduction in receivables compared to the previous year and confirmed regular monthly payments.
*   **On EBITDA Growth:** Management attributed the robust EBITDA to higher capacity charges, increased volumes, and improved energy charge realizations.
*   **On Dividend:** Management stated they are prioritizing reinvestment of surplus into the capex program over dividend distribution.

### One-line takeaway
Adani Power delivered record Q1 results driven by high demand and operational efficiency, while aggressively pursuing a revised 45 GW capacity target funded by strong internal accruals and disciplined debt management.
