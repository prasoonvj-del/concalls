# Best Agrolife Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Despite revenue pressure, the company delivered significant margin expansion and profit growth, driven by a strategic pivot toward patented products and cost discipline.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management expressed uncertainty regarding the 10-15% revenue growth target mentioned previously, stating it is "hard to say" if it will be achieved, but reiterated confidence in profitability growth.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautiously optimistic" to acknowledging a challenging Kharif season with uneven rainfall and reduced acreage, leading to a "bottomed-out" year in FY26 and a recovery trajectory now.
- **New topics/risks:** Income tax department issues (raid-related) are largely resolved with zero liability; 47 MT shipment to Sudan ($4-5 million value, corrected to Rs 5 crore); incorporation of a step-down subsidiary in Brazil.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Capex remains deferred; sales return provisions were implemented as promised to avoid Q3/Q4 surprises.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 407 crore (vs Rs 517 crore in Q2 FY26, -21% YoY). H1 Revenue: Rs 803 crore (vs Rs 898 crore in H1 FY26, -11% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 101 crore (vs Rs 78 crore in Q2 FY26, +30% YoY). H1 EBITDA: Rs 178 crore (vs Rs 123 crore in H1 FY26, +45% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 25% (vs 15% in Q2 FY26). H1 EBITDA Margin: 22% (vs 14% in H1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 57 crore (vs Rs 39 crore in Q2 FY26, +48% YoY). H1 PAT: Rs 98 crore (vs Rs 59 crore in H1 FY26, +67% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 45% (vs 33% in Q2 FY26). H1 Gross Margin: 41% (vs 31% in H1 FY26).
*   **Operating Expenses:** Rs 81 crore (vs Rs 92 crore in Q2 FY26, -12% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Strategy:** Deliberate reduction of generic products (-30% YoY) in favor of patented products (+20% YoY). Patented portfolio now constitutes 60% of branded sales (vs 50% in H1 last year).
*   **New Launches:** Launched *Midcotin* (fungicide/insecticide combination) for paddy and *SECTOR* (insecticide) for pomegranate and chili.
*   **International Expansion:** Incorporated a step-down subsidiary in Brazil; received 13 CIB registrations; patent applications for Fluxametamide combinations in UAE, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Philippines, and Egypt have reached the examination stage.
*   **Tax/Legal:** Income tax assessments related to the 2023 raid are 95% closed with zero liability; received Rs 5 crore refund for subsidiary Seedlings India, with another ~Rs 15 crore expected.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management is not changing the revenue target but noted it is "hard to say" if the 10-15% growth target is achievable; expects to close near last year's revenue levels.
*   **Profitability:** Management expects profitability to continue increasing in H2, similar to the H1 trend.
*   **Rabi Season:** Management is monitoring soil moisture and irrigation availability; currently cautious and not making strong assumptions about market recovery.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Kharif Impact:** Uneven monsoon, delayed planting, and reduced acreage in Central and North India (Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana) impacted demand for cotton, paddy, and fungicide segments.
*   **Generic Pressure:** Pricing pressure and competition in generic product categories persisted.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Improved product mix (higher patented share) and operational efficiency.
*   **Cost Discipline:** Operating expenses (excluding finance/depreciation) reduced by 12% YoY in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Plans remain deferred; management will only consider new capex once profitability and business stability are fully secured.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Long-term borrowings increased to Rs 73 crore (from Rs 27 crore in March) due to a government-backed facility (20% of existing limits) to manage inventory costs amid geopolitical tensions (Israel-Iran).
*   **Working Capital:** Management expects inventory levels to decrease by Feb/March, strengthening the balance sheet.
*   **Cost of Funds:** Blended cost of funds is 9.75%–10%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Sales Returns:** Management has created higher provisions for sales returns to absorb potential shocks in H2; 95% of returns cleared by end of October.
*   **Geopolitical:** Inventory costs were impacted by the Israel-Iran conflict.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did top-line drop despite the strategy?** A: Management clarified that the revenue decline is intentional, driven by the exit from generic products, while the patented portfolio grew 20%.
*   **Q: How will H2 perform?** A: Management believes H2 will not be loss-making and expects profitability to improve compared to last year, having created buffers for sales returns.
*   **Q: Why is the balance sheet stretched?** A: Management attributed this to inventory buildup for a specialized product that took longer to commercialize; expects normalization by March.
*   **Q: Is the 10-15% revenue growth target still valid?** A: Management admitted it is "hard to say" if this is achievable but emphasized that profitability growth is the primary focus.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Sudan shipment?** A: It was a Rs 5 crore shipment; management is in discussions for repeat orders.

### One-line takeaway
Best Agrolife is successfully sacrificing top-line generic revenue to drive significant margin expansion and profitability through a patented-product-led strategy, while proactively managing sales return risks.
