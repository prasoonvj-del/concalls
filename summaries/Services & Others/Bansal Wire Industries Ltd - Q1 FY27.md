# Bansal Wire Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While the company faced temporary operational disruptions and margin pressure due to geopolitical tensions and gas shortages, management remains confident in a recovery, citing normalized margins and strong volume growth prospects.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,168 crore (up ~25% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 57 crore.
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 20 crore.
*   **Sales Volume:** 112,000 metric tonnes (vs 104,000 metric tonnes in Q1 FY26).
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 121 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin (per kg):** Blended ~Rs 4-4.5/kg for the quarter (impacted by first 45 days at Rs 2/kg; recovered to Rs 7-8/kg from mid-May).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Disruption:** Geopolitical tensions in West Asia caused natural gas shortages, impacting production and input costs in the first half of the quarter.
*   **Strategic Progress:** Received first trial order for Steel Cord from a leading Indian tire manufacturer.
*   **B2C Expansion:** New range of steel wire products for Farming, Fencing, and Poultry contributed ~10% of sales in Q1.
*   **Capacity:** Current operating capacity stands at ~680,000 tonnes.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth Target:** Management maintains a 20% volume growth target for the remainder of the year.
*   **EBITDA Guidance:** Targets 20% growth in EBITDA for the year.
*   **Speciality Portfolio:** Expects IHT/OHT wires to reach optimum utilization (60-80%) by the end of the year.
*   **B2C Ambition:** Aims for B2C to eventually contribute 25% of total sales (50% of low-carbon segment).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Demand Recovery:** Demand indicators are improving across all sectors; automotive remains strong.
*   **Market Share:** Management emphasizes a strategy of gaining market share, noting the industry grows at 7-8% while the company targets 20%.
*   **Exports:** Export demand has started to perform well.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Impact:** The company absorbed a ~Rs 5,000/tonne increase in consumables (primarily gas) for existing orders during the first 45 days to maintain customer relationships, resulting in a temporary margin hit.
*   **Pricing Model:** Operates on a cost-plus model; new orders booked after the cost spike were priced to maintain margins.
*   **Gas Costs:** Gas prices remain escalated; the company is using alternative energy sources to mitigate impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Strategy:** Plans to cap annual capex at Rs 200-250 crore to support 20-25% volume growth.
*   **Flexibility:** Over 50% of equipment is manufactured in-house, allowing for flexible deployment of capacity based on demand.
*   **Speciality Capacity:** Currently has 9,000 tonnes for IHT; combined IHT/OHT capacity to reach 15,000 tonnes by year-end. Steel Cord pilot project is at 20,000 tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Flow:** Generated Rs 121 crore in operating cash flow through disciplined working capital management.
*   **Working Capital:** Focusing on reducing receivable days and inventory days; utilizing channel financing to improve cash flow.
*   **Sanand Facility:** Clarified that the company is not selling the entire facility; expansion is ongoing, though they may sell excess land.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical Volatility:** Ongoing tensions in West Asia continue to pose risks to global supply chains and energy costs.
*   **Raw Material Costs:** High input costs (gas) persist, though they are being passed on to customers.
*   **Trial Timelines:** Steel Cord business is in the early stages of trial orders; full-scale commercialization timelines are subject to customer approval processes (6-8 months).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Margin Recovery:** Management confirmed they are back to Rs 7-8/kg EBITDA levels since mid-May after absorbing costs in the first 45 days.
*   **On Steel Cord Competition:** Management noted that exporting is not currently lucrative due to raw material price differentials vs. China; the focus is on domestic import substitution.
*   **On Vendor Financing:** Management explained they avoid negotiating for longer payable days with suppliers to maintain their leverage as the largest purchaser of wire rods in India.
*   **On ROCE Concerns:** An analyst questioned the low asset turn of the Speciality business; management argued that per-tonne EBITDA and volume growth will drive returns, targeting 25% ROCE.
*   **On Volume Conviction:** Management attributed the 20% growth target to a three-pronged approach: growing with existing customers, gaining market share, and new product development (250+ SKUs/year).

### One-line takeaway
Bansal Wire navigated a challenging Q1 marked by temporary gas-related cost spikes and margin compression, but management remains bullish on hitting 20% volume growth for the year, supported by a recovering demand environment and the scaling of high-margin speciality products.
