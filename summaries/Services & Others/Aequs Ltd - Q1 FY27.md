# Aequs Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (55% YoY) and 3.5x sequential improvement in operational EBITDA, despite reported EBITDA being impacted by lower other income.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 3,955 million (up 55% YoY, 8% QoQ).
*   **Reported EBITDA:** INR 215 million (5% margin).
*   **Operational EBITDA (excl. other income):** INR 148 million (up from INR 42 million in Q4).
*   **Aerospace Revenue:** INR 3,222 million (up 40% YoY, 6% QoQ).
*   **Consumer Revenue:** INR 734 million (up 190% YoY, 16% QoQ).
*   **Consumer EBITDA Loss:** INR 361 million (narrowed from INR 473 million in Q4).
*   **Aerospace Order Book:** > USD 1 billion (up 13% QoQ).
*   **Net Working Capital Days:** 125 days (improved from 127 days in FY26).

### Highlights
*   Aerospace order book crossed the USD 1 billion milestone.
*   Signed a 15-year agreement with Safran Landing Systems for fully integrated Airbus A320 wheels (100% Make in India).
*   Consumer segment EBITDA loss narrowed by INR 112 million sequentially.
*   Aerospace added 86 new parts; total portfolio reached 5,740 parts.
*   CFO transition: Harish Bang is leading the finance function in the interim.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects ~45-50% YoY top-line revenue growth for FY27.
*   Targeting a doubling of operational EBITDA for FY27, weighted toward the second half.
*   Consumer segment EBITDA breakeven targeted by Q4 FY27.
*   Aerospace revenue growth target: 25-30% with segment EBITDA margins >20%.
*   Long-term (5-year) capex plan: USD 350 million to USD 400 million.
*   Hosur facility revenue expected to commence from FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Aerospace growth driven by higher customer build rates and progression of parts into production.
*   Consumer segment growth driven by increased production volumes and improved throughput.
*   Management stated no specific impact from global tariff or trade policy changes.
*   Raw material sourcing remains ~99% imported; management noted limited qualified sources in India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Reported EBITDA impacted by lower other income (INR 67 million in Q1 vs INR 279 million in Q4).
*   Depreciation: INR 453 million (stable vs Q4).
*   Finance cost: Reduced to INR 189 million (from INR 358 million in Q4) due to debt reduction.
*   Consumer segment EBITDA loss narrowed by ~24% sequentially.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   FY27 total capex guidance: INR 660 million (INR 500 million consumer, INR 160 million aerospace, though aerospace may accelerate).
*   Hosur facility: Total investment of INR 1,900 crore over 10 years; focused on engine and landing gear components.
*   Aerospace capacity: Adding machines continuously (approx. one per week).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Total Equity: INR 14,332 million.
*   Cash & Equivalents: INR 2,340 million.
*   Debt reduction: Loan repayments of INR 2,527 million and net reduction of INR 789 million in short-term/working capital borrowings.
*   Cash flow from operations: Negative INR 414 million (due to working capital for higher operating cycle).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Reported EBITDA volatility due to "other income" (FX gains).
*   High dependence on imported raw materials (99%).
*   Consumer segment currently loss-making (though narrowing).
*   Management indicated a potential future capital raise of ~USD 150 million, though none is planned for this year unless capex is pulled forward.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why did consumer utilization dip QoQ despite revenue growth?* A: Management clarified it was not due to capacity expansion but better throughput/yield; utilization is expected to improve through the year.
*   *Q: Details on the Safran A320 wheels contract?* A: 15-year agreement; first time fully integrated wheels (forging to assembly) done in India; single-source order.
*   *Q: How will the Hosur plant be funded?* A: Management clarified the INR 1,900 crore investment is spread over 10 years, not next year.
*   *Q: Are consumer electronics and toys growing in tandem?* A: Yes, both are underutilized and growing in tandem to reach breakeven.
*   *Q: What is the long-term ROCE target?* A: Management targets a steady-state ROCE of 18% to 20%.

### One-line takeaway
Aequs delivered strong 55% YoY revenue growth and significant operational EBITDA improvement, with a clear path to consumer segment breakeven by Q4 FY27 and a robust USD 1 billion+ aerospace order book.
