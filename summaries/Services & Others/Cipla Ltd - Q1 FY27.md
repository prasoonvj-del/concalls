# Cipla Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Record revenue growth in India offset by margin pressure from launch-related investments, inventory write-offs, and war-related costs.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** INR 7,119 crore (2% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 16.7% (excluding other income).
*   **Gross Margin:** 62.5%.
*   **R&D Spend:** INR 486 crore (6.8% of revenue).
*   **PAT:** INR 789 crore (11% of sales).
*   **Net Cash:** INR 9,494 crore (post INR 1,050 crore dividend payment).
*   **Debt:** INR 600 crore (including lease liabilities).
*   **India Business Growth:** 12% YoY.
*   **North America Revenue:** USD 162 million.

### Highlights
*   **Accounting Change:** Effective April 1, 2026, marketing and proportional expenditures are presented as a reduction from revenue rather than operating expenditure (Note 9).
*   **India Performance:** Branded prescription business grew 15.4% (IQVIA data). Chronic mix reached 60.4%.
*   **New Launches:** Launched Duolin Synchrobreathe, Bilafav M, UPADACIP, and Byefilm.
*   **US Market:** Commenced commercial shipments of generic Ventolin; holds #1 position in Albuterol MDI market (21% share).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management maintains FY27 EBITDA margin guidance of 18.5% to 20%.
*   **US Growth:** Management expects sequential growth in North America, targeting a USD 1 billion exit run rate for the segment in FY27.
*   **Outlook:** Management expects margin improvement in coming quarters as new products launch and winter season demand increases.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **India:** Strong volume expansion in key chronic therapies: Respiratory (15%), Anti-diabetes (43%), Cardiac (20%), and Urology (double-digit).
*   **South Africa:** Private market secondary growth of 6.5%, outperforming the 5.7% market growth.
*   **Obesity Segment:** Yurpeak (collaboration with Eli Lilly) is the #2 brand in the category; management expects continued growth despite generic competition.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Expenses:** Total expenses were INR 3,260 crore (8.3% increase QoQ) due to planned investment in upcoming product launches and manufacturing readiness.
*   **Margin Headwinds:** Margins were impacted by product mix, higher-than-normal inventory write-offs, and war-related costs (estimated 1-2% of revenue impact).
*   **Incentives:** PLI incentives were minimal in Q1 but are expected to phase in during later quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **US Pipeline:** 4 significant launches planned (3 respiratory assets, 1 peptide).
*   **Lanreotide:** Management is pursuing a two-pronged approach: remediation at the partner site and tech transfer to a US site.
*   **Manpower:** No significant manpower additions planned for India this year; focus is on productivity and reorganization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   The company maintains a healthy net cash position of INR 9,494 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** US FDA completed a routine GMP inspection at the Invagen (New York) facility, resulting in one Form 483 observation.
*   **South Africa:** Tender business has declined, with continued impact expected in coming quarters.
*   **Inventory:** Higher-than-normal inventory write-offs were recorded due to expiry, demand shifts, and quality issues.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **US $1bn Guidance:** Analysts questioned the feasibility of the USD 1 billion exit run rate. Management stated it is achievable through the ramp-up of Ventolin and the 4 upcoming significant launches (3 respiratory, 1 peptide).
*   **Accounting Change:** Analysts asked for the impact on growth rates. Management clarified the change primarily impacted South Africa reporting.
*   **Margin Dip:** Analysts asked why margins are below pre-Revlimid levels. Management attributed this to "transit phase" costs, including launch-related OpEx, war-related costs, and inventory write-offs, which they expect to normalize.
*   **Lanreotide:** Analysts asked for a timeline. Management stated it is not included in Q4 projections due to regulatory dependencies.
*   **Yurpeak:** Analysts asked about the anti-obesity market. Management noted they are #2 in the category and see the market as a high-involvement therapy where they are gaining traction.

### One-line takeaway
Cipla delivered record Q1 revenue, but profitability remains under pressure from launch-related OpEx and geopolitical costs, with management relying on US pipeline execution to meet FY27 margin and revenue targets.
