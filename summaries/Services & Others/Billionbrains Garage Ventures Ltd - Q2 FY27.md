# Billionbrains Garage Ventures Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-16 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted steady growth in MTF, successful integration of acquisitions, and resilience in core metrics despite regulatory headwinds and market volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **MTF Book Growth:** Adding ~Rs 600 crore - Rs 700 crore on a quarterly basis.
*   **Cost to Serve (One-time hit):** Rs 10 crore - Rs 13 crore due to a file delay issue on an expiry day.
*   **Employee Cost:** Rs 169 crore (down from Rs 182 crore in Q1 FY27).
*   **Cost to Grow (Annual Budget):** Typically Rs 400 crore - Rs 500 crore per year; management noted flexibility based on market conditions.
*   **Customer Metrics:** >50% of customers use >2 products; >5% use >4 products.
*   **MTF Contribution:** MTF-led cash turnover is currently slightly above 5%.

### Highlights
*   **Wealth Management:** First-time disclosure of wealth-related AUM; MF Prime is the fastest-growing segment, though currently small.
*   **Operational Changes:** Reclassified IPO income and BSDA income into "Other Operating Income" (previously under Stocks revenue).
*   **AI Integration:** In-house tech stack development is reducing per-token costs compared to commercial models.
*   **Acquisitions:** Integration of the acquired company (Fisdom) is complete, with products now mainstreamed across apps.
*   **IPO Activity:** Q2 saw ~30 IPOs, including large ones like SBI AMC and NSE, contributing significantly to other operating income.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **MTF Trajectory:** Management expects the growth trend in the MTF book to continue for the foreseeable future.
*   **Market Share:** Long-term goal is for cash and derivative market share to converge toward NSE active market share levels, though this is a multi-year horizon.
*   **Cost to Grow:** Management stated that if the market performs well in Q3/Q4, spending could exceed the Rs 125 crore quarterly average.
*   **US Stocks:** Currently in testing/beta phase; no fixed timeline for full launch.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Competition:** Management maintains a "customer-first" approach rather than reacting to competitor pricing or AI tool launches.
*   **CAS (Circular on Derivatives):** Management expects the impact to be minimal on the derivatives segment as the closing will likely be based on VWAP. They anticipate volume normalization once the circular becomes applicable.
*   **UPI MDR:** Management expressed concern regarding potential high capping (e.g., Rs 300) on UPI transactions for capital markets, noting it would be unaffordable for the industry and customers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost to Serve:** Increased due to AI-related costs, higher compute costs on trading days, and a one-time Rs 10-13 crore hit from an expiry day file delay.
*   **Cost to Grow:** Decreased QoQ due to the absence of IPL-related branding events.
*   **Employee Expenses:** Structurally expected to remain in line with previous quarters; Q1 had a one-time appraisal cycle impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **MTF Strategy:** Implementing stricter, real-time policy execution. The company is now monitoring industry-level books for specific stocks to manage risk, including excluding certain stocks or increasing haircuts (up to 80% in some cases) beyond regulatory requirements.
*   **Product Pipeline:** Focus remains on integrating acquired products and expanding the wealth management suite.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Treasury:** Investments are in NCDs, bonds, and debt mutual funds.
*   **Float Income:** No change in strategy; yields are consistent with market returns for treasury and prescribed SEBI/exchange-approved FDs for customer wallets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Potential implementation of MDR on UPI for capital markets remains a risk if caps are set too high.
*   **Market Volatility:** MTF book volatility is inherent; management noted that while they optimize haircuts, market-wide downturns require proactive risk management.
*   **Execution:** Policy implementation in MTF is critical; management emphasized that "policy is as good as the execution."

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On MTF Risk:** Analyst asked about one-off costs from market dips. Management clarified they have implemented stricter, real-time policy execution and are monitoring industry-level exposure to avoid systemic risks.
*   **On CAS Impact:** Analyst asked if volumes would return to normal. Management stated they expect normalization once the circular is effective, as the current impact is largely due to the disruption.
*   **On Order Intensity:** Analyst noted higher order-per-ADTV despite lower volumes. Management clarified that revenue is largely order-based (fixed Rs 20 per order for F&O/Commodity), not ADTV-based, and revenue is not directly correlated with ADTV.
*   **On AI Investment:** Analyst asked about AI ROI. Management stated they are building an in-house stack to reduce per-token costs and prioritize customer experience over immediate revenue maximization.

### One-line takeaway
Groww is focusing on internalizing its tech stack (AI) and tightening risk controls in its MTF book while maintaining a "customer-first" strategy amidst regulatory uncertainty regarding UPI charges and derivative circulars.
