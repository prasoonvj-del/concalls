# AU Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in deposits and loans, improved margins, and management confidence in long-term compounding.

### Key numbers
*   **PAT:** Rs 796 crore (+37% YoY).
*   **Deposits:** Rs 1.58 lakh crore (+24% YoY, +3.3% QoQ).
*   **Loan Portfolio:** +23% YoY.
*   **Disbursements:** +42% YoY.
*   **NIM:** 5.9% (+47 bps YoY, -7 bps QoQ).
*   **Slippages:** Rs 798 crore (-22% YoY).
*   **Credit Cost:** 0.8% (down from 1.4% YoY).
*   **ROA:** 1.7% (vs 1.5% YoY); **ROE:** 15.6% (vs 13.3% YoY).
*   **CASA Ratio:** 29% (marginal QoQ improvement).

### Highlights
*   Management elevated Mr. Yogesh Jain to Deputy CEO.
*   Gold loan business grew 130% YoY to Rs 4,500 crore; distribution expanded to 1,300+ branches.
*   Technology: AI-enabled gold loan platform is live; 90% of transactions/service requests processed via AU 0101.
*   One-time provision of Rs 23 crore taken for tightening provisioning norms in selected products.
*   Employee count declined slightly due to AI-led efficiencies in backend operations.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to sustainably compound at 2x to 2.5x of India's nominal GDP growth rate.
*   Plan to add 100+ branches this year.
*   Management expects cost-to-assets ratio to improve on a full-year basis.
*   Management expects other income to improve in the next 6-9 months.
*   ECL transition impact: Management expects a neutral impact, with more details to be provided by the end of Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Indian economy and banking sector continue to demonstrate resilience despite geopolitical uncertainty (West Asia crisis).
*   Liquidity remains tight with high competition for deposits and elevated interest rates.
*   Microfinance industry seeing positive traction post-MFIN guardrails.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Cost of funds stable at 6.48%.
*   NIM decline QoQ (7 bps) attributed to the reversal of seasonal benefits that aided the previous quarter.
*   Operating expenses increased 26% YoY but declined 1% QoQ.
*   Technology expenditure is ~12-13% of total opex (approx. Rs 1,000 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Added 16 new deposit branches in Q1.
*   Expanding into newer geographies (South, East, UP, Bihar) to transition into a pan-India franchise.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Stable deposits (CASA + retail TDs + non-callable bulk TDs) at 79% of total deposits.
*   CD ratio (ex-refinance) is ~80%.
*   96% of MFI book is covered under the CGFMU guarantee scheme.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Heightened geopolitical uncertainty.
*   Tight liquidity conditions and elevated interest rates.
*   Slight uptick in commercial banking NPAs (SME book) on a QoQ basis, described by management as seasonal.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Impact of ECL framework?* Management stated it is premature to quantify but expects a neutral impact given low LGDs; will provide color by Q3.
*   *Q: Why did employee count decline?* Management attributed this to AI-led efficiencies in backend operations, allowing for headcount optimization.
*   *Q: Sustainability of MFI growth?* Management noted industry revival post-MFIN guardrails and confirmed a cap of 10% of the book for MFI.
*   *Q: Why the decline in NIM QoQ?* Management explained it was due to the reversal of seasonal benefits that aided the previous quarter.
*   *Q: Strategy for newer geographies?* Management emphasized building a pan-India franchise to support long-term growth targets.

### One-line takeaway
AU Small Finance Bank delivered strong Q1 FY27 performance with 37% YoY PAT growth and robust asset quality, leveraging AI-driven efficiencies and geographic expansion to support long-term growth targets.
