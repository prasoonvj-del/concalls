# NIS Management Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported healthy growth, strong order inflows, and expressed confidence in achieving FY27 revenue targets while maintaining operational discipline.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 115.44 crore (up 15.68% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 9.22 crore (up 36.24% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 7.99% (expanded 121 bps YoY).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 6.40 crore (up 35% YoY).
*   **Net Profit Margin:** 5.54% (expanded 81 bps YoY).
*   **EPS:** Rs 3.23 (up 3.53% YoY).
*   **Employee Count:** 19,154 (as of July 2026, up from 18,673 in March 2026).

### Highlights
*   **Order Wins:** Secured orders worth Rs 45.71 crore from Reliance Group (housekeeping, MEP, ancillary services), Rs 1.94 crore from Nesco Limited, and Rs 36.71 lakh from West Bengal Public Department.
*   **Operational Strategy:** Shifting focus toward higher-margin technology-driven solutions (CCTV, access control, mechanization) within existing security and facility management contracts.
*   **Client Retention:** Reported a high renewal rate of approximately 96%-97%.
*   **IPO Funds:** Rs 36.85 crore of IPO proceeds remain unutilized as of June 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management reiterated the target to cross Rs 500 crore in consolidated revenue for FY27.
*   **FY28 Target:** Management targets revenue of Rs 630-650 crore for FY28.
*   **Margins:** Management expects a 1%-2% increase in EBITDA margins over the next 2 years driven by the inclusion of electronics and technology in service offerings.
*   **CCTV Business:** Expects revenue to scale to Rs 30 crore in FY27 (vs Rs 13-14 crore in FY26).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Trends:** Observing increased formalization in the security and facility management industry, favoring compliant players.
*   **Sector Focus:** Seeing opportunities in government tenders and private sector demand (warehousing, manufacturing, banking).
*   **Seasonality:** Very little seasonality; business is driven by regular maintenance and surveillance requirements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Dynamics:** First-quarter EBITDA is typically higher, with moderation in Q2/Q3 and recovery in Q4.
*   **Project Business:** CCTV and DDU-GKY projects are high-margin but have longer working capital cycles and gestation periods.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Skill Development:** Investing in infrastructure and labs for skill development projects (e.g., DDU-GKY project in Odisha).
*   **Technology:** Investing ~Rs 1 crore to set up a command and control center for HDFC Bank.
*   **Acquisitions:** Management is open to inorganic growth, particularly in technology-driven areas, but is currently focused on internal growth and avoiding pure manpower-based acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Consolidated net debt is Rs 82 crore (standalone Rs 69 crore). Management stated there will be no increase in debt levels.
*   **Working Capital:** DSO cycle is approximately 3 months.
*   **Cash:** Standalone cash position was Rs 60 crore as of June 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **CCTV Segment:** Reported a loss in Q1 FY27 due to project execution costs; management expects stabilization by September.
*   **Contract Renewals:** Awaiting renewal for the Directorate of Technical Education and Training contract; management is accounting for a potential loss of this contract.
*   **GeM Bidding:** Government e-Marketplace (GeM) bidding has brought down margins to 3.85% in some manpower segments, forcing the company to be selective.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why not use cash for a share buyback given the stock price decline?** Management stated they intend to utilize the IPO capital for business growth, specifically in technology and skill development, rather than returning cash to shareholders.
*   **Q: Is there a plan for inorganic growth?** Management is open to acquisitions, particularly in technology, but is currently cautious about pure manpower-based acquisitions due to labor sensitivities.
*   **Q: What is the geographic concentration?** West Bengal accounts for 72%-73% of revenue; management expects growth in Bihar, Odisha, and Maharashtra to help diversify, though West Bengal is also expected to grow.
*   **Q: Why the loss in the CCTV segment?** Management explained it is due to project execution costs before billing milestones are met; expects stabilization by September.

### One-line takeaway
NIS Management is prioritizing long-term growth through technology-integrated service offerings and government/private sector expansion, while maintaining a conservative stance on capital allocation and debt.
