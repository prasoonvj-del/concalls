# Sunrakshakk Industries India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Most profitable quarter to date with robust YoY revenue and PAT growth, driven by scaling FMCG and edible operations.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management stated they are well ahead of pace to reach the Rs 1,000 crore revenue milestone (previously targeted for FY28) and expect to cross it much sooner than planned (vs maintaining FY28 target last quarter).
- **Tone and demand view:** Maintained a bullish tone; reported a mixed Diwali season with edible segment demand being very good and others normal.
- **New topics or risks:** Disclosed that group company RCM contributes ~42% to 44% of total revenue. Old risks (crude volatility, input cost pressures) persisted.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Roorkee soap line capacity successfully contributing; overall capacity utilization maintained around 50% to 55%.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 265.17 crore, up 119.20% YoY (vs Rs 120.97 crore in Q2 FY26) and down 4.04% QoQ (vs Rs 276.33 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 23.14 crore, up 98.26% YoY (vs Rs 11.67 crore in Q2 FY26) and up 2.42% QoQ (vs Rs 22.59 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.73% (vs 9.65% in Q2 FY26 and 8.81% in Q1 FY27).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 16.00 crore, up 130.10% YoY (vs Rs 6.95 crore in Q2 FY26) and up 6.38% QoQ (vs Rs 15.04 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT Margin:** 6.03% (vs 5.75% in Q2 FY26 and 5.44% in Q1 FY27).
- **Basic EPS:** Rs 5.16 (vs Rs 2.24 in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 541.50 crore, up 119.93% YoY. H1 EBITDA: Rs 45.73 crore (up 96.32% YoY). H1 PAT: Rs 31.04 crore (up 130.39% YoY).
- **FMCG Segment Revenue:** Rs 240.92 crore, up 154.28% YoY; segment result Rs 22.32 crore (margin 9.27%).
- **Textile Segment Revenue:** Rs 24.25 crore, down 7.53% YoY; segment loss of Rs 0.97 crore.

### Highlights
- FMCG, FMCG intermediates, and edible businesses accounted for ~90.9% of Q2 FY27 revenue (~90.7% in H1 FY27).
- Aggregate installed FMCG and intermediate capacity stands at ~20,840 tonnes per month.
- Facility contributions in Q2 FY27: Roorkee soap Rs 25.85 crore, noodle Rs 82.74 crore; Bhilwara home care/detergent Rs 27.14 crore, edibles Rs 49.67 crore; Guwahati cosmetics Rs 25.16 crore, noodle Rs 30.35 crore.
- Group company RCM contributed ~42% to 44% of total revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to cross the Rs 1,000 crore revenue milestone much sooner than the previously targeted FY28.
- Expects 25% to 30% YoY revenue growth for the next 2 to 3 years, and 20% to 25% over a 5-year horizon.
- Targeting a profitability level of 7% by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- FMCG industry value growth improved to 8.6% in July-September 2026 (Bizom data), with rural growing at 10.6% and urban at 5.8%.
- Diwali season demand was mixed (edibles very good, others normal).
- Operates on a B2B model with 1-month firm and 2-month tentative plans from over 200 customers (including Godrej, Wipro, and ITC).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material cost ratio improved sequentially to 74.37% of revenue (from 75.60% in Q1 FY27), though higher than 67.42% YoY.
- Crude-linked inputs (LABSA, palm material) remained volatile, while soap noodle prices softened in mid-2026.
- Textile segment weighed down by high crude prices affecting consumables, dyes, and chemicals, alongside machine installation downtime.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Average capacity utilization across FMCG facilities is around 50% to 55% (textile utilization at 80% to 85%).
- No immediate major capex or fresh fundraising planned; future expansions to be funded through internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital optimized; H1 funds largely deployed into bulk inventory buying for seasonal/commodity edible items.
- FMCG business is debt-free; existing debt pertains solely to the textile business.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Crude oil and crude-linked input price volatility (LABSA, palm material).
- Volatile raw material and consumable costs impacting textile segment profitability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash Parkar (Patel Investments) asked about revenue target timelines:** Management stated they will reach the Rs 1,000 crore milestone sooner than FY28 due to strong H1 performance.
- **Shravan Modi (Syndicate Family Office) asked about long-term textile strategy:** Management clarified textiles will be maintained at current levels (~9%-10% mix) without aggressive capacity expansion.
- **Kartik Maheshwari (Aamrakunj Realty) asked about RCM group revenue contribution:** Management stated RCM contributes ~42% to 44% of total revenue.
- **Manan Dalal (Dalal Family Office) asked about cash conversion vs PAT:** Management explained H1 funds were deployed into bulk inventory buying for seasonal edibles, which will be released in coming quarters.

### One-line takeaway
Sunrakshakk Industries delivered its most profitable quarter to date with a 119.20% YoY surge in revenue, propelled by strong scaling across its FMCG and edible business segments.
