# Quess Corp Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong broad-based growth across all segments, significant PAT expansion, and a healthy zero-debt balance sheet.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 4,182 crore (up 15% YoY, 7% QoQ)
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 85 crore (up 21% YoY)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 2.02% (expansion of 11 bps YoY)
*   **PAT:** Rs 82 crore (up 61% YoY, 28% QoQ)
*   **EPS:** Rs 5.5 (up 61% YoY)
*   **Headcount:** ~4,82,214 associates (up 4.5% YoY)

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance:** All three core segments (General Staffing, Professional Staffing, Overseas) delivered double-digit YoY revenue growth.
*   **One-time Items:** Revenue included a Rs 176 crore pass-through impact due to new Labor Code implementation; Other income included Rs 261 crore from income tax refunds (including Rs 22 crore interest).
*   **Quess 2.0 Strategy:** Repositioning towards higher-margin, capital-light, partner-led talent corridors (Healthcare, Tech, MEP, Hospitality, Finance). Japan corridor is live; Europe/Nordics in advanced discussions.
*   **Awards:** Certified "Great Place to Work" for the 7th consecutive year; CMMI Level 3 certification.
*   **Dividend:** Final dividend of Rs 3 per share approved for AGM on 25 August 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Long-term Goal:** Aspires to reach 1 million headcount by 2030.
*   **Revenue Mix:** Aims for 20%–25% of revenue from higher-margin businesses in 3-4 years.
*   **Professional Staffing:** Target quarterly EBITDA run rate of Rs 30 crore; margin guidance maintained at 11%–12%.
*   **Overseas:** Margin guidance of 6.5%–7%.
*   **General Staffing:** Expects 10%–11% headcount growth annually (40k–50k additions).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **General Staffing:** Growth led by Manufacturing and Consumer/Retail/Telecom (CRT) verticals; BFSI remains a laggard due to regulatory headwinds on outsourcing.
*   **GCCs:** India has ~2,407 GCCs; Quess has captured ~10%. Management remains bullish on continued GCC growth.
*   **Festive Season:** Expects strong Q2 demand across the client base.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Dynamics:** Sequential EBITDA margin dip (20 bps) attributed to annual merit cycles/variable pay resets and the Labor Code pass-through revenue.
*   **General Staffing Margins:** Normalized EBITDA margin is ~1.5% excluding the Labor Code impact. Manufacturing contracts offer higher gross margins (5%–6%) compared to Consumer (2%–3%).
*   **Labor Code:** Rs 176 crore impact in Q1 is a pass-through liability; management expects remaining liability to be booked by end of Q2/early Q3.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Strategy:** Capital-light, partner-led model for international expansion (Japan, Israel, UK, Germany, Nordics, Canada, US).
*   **Infrastructure:** 65 offices; presence in 6,464 pin codes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Zero-debt company with a healthy net cash position.
*   **Working Capital:** DSO remains tight at 15 days AR (28 days including UBR).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Ongoing regulatory headwinds in the BFSI sector regarding what can be outsourced.
*   **Geopolitical/Visa:** Potential visa/labor mobility issues for international expansion, though management expects FTAs to mitigate these.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Labor Code impact:** Management clarified the Rs 176 crore is a one-time pass-through liability; it does not impact profitability and is not recurring revenue.
*   **On Quess 2.0 economics:** Management noted that while they won't disclose specific margins before execution, the goal is to shift the profit pool mix from 50:50 (GS vs. Others) to 35:65 over 3-4 years.
*   **On BFSI laggard:** Management explained that while core banking is slow, they are focusing on NBFCs and fintechs, though it will take time to scale.
*   **On ELI Scheme:** Management confirmed no benefits from the Employment Linked Incentive (ELI) scheme have been accrued or considered in Q1 results yet.

### One-line takeaway
Quess delivered strong Q1 growth and profitability while aggressively pivoting toward a higher-margin, capital-light "Quess 2.0" model, supported by a robust zero-debt balance sheet.
