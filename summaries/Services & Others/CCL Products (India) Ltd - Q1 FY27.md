# CCL Products (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong 20% volume growth and significant deleveraging, with management maintaining consistent guidance despite commodity price volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Turnover:** Rs 1,203.59 crore (up 13.76% YoY).
*   **Volume Growth:** ~20% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 196.69 crore (up 21.84% YoY).
*   **PBT:** Rs 129.02 crore (up 36.98% YoY).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 116.87 crore (up 61.31% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 963 crore (down from Rs 1,073 crore in March '26).
*   **Domestic Branded Business:** Rs 180 crore turnover (approx. Rs 125 crore from branded products).
*   **EBITDA per kg:** ~Rs 140.

### Highlights
*   Company achieved 20% volume growth, though revenue growth was 13.76% due to green coffee price fluctuations.
*   Deleveraging strategy continues; net debt is now sub-Rs 1,000 crore.
*   Domestic branded business is growing; management targets Rs 550-600 crore revenue for this segment in FY27.
*   Capacity utilization is 65-70% across India and Vietnam.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains 15% volume growth guidance for FY27.
*   EBITDA per kg expected to remain stable at current levels throughout the year.
*   Domestic branded business growth target: 25-30%.
*   No major capex planned for the next 2-3 years; FY27 capex estimated at Rs 25-50 crore for minor upgrades.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Green coffee prices range between Rs 3,300-3,800; volatility persists due to bearish (Brazil supply) and bullish (El Nino/Vietnam crop concerns) factors.
*   Coffee consumption remains inelastic to price changes.
*   Domestic market share: >6% in South India; double-digit market share in Reliance and DMart chains; high single-digit in quick commerce.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Business operates on a cost-plus model; margins are naturally hedged against forex fluctuations.
*   Freeze-dried coffee (FDC) earns 30-40% higher EBITDA than spray-dried coffee.
*   Logistics and packaging costs faced some volatility due to the Middle East crisis.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Current capacity: 77,000 tonnes.
*   No major capacity expansion planned for the next 2 years.
*   Management confident in ability to add capacity via brownfield expansions (gestation 9-12 months) if utilization crosses 75-85%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Gross debt: Rs 1,268 crore (Term loan: Rs 517 crore; Working capital: Rs 751 crore).
*   Working capital cycle: 166 days.
*   Debt repayment plan: Rs 200 crore in FY27, Rs 200 crore in FY28, balance in 2028.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Green coffee price volatility and potential speculative trading.
*   Logistics and packaging cost fluctuations (Middle East crisis impact).
*   Potential for "wait and watch" behavior from clients during high price volatility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is there a gap in EBITDA/kg compared to peers?** A: Management declined to comment on peers, emphasizing their own sustainable, long-term growth philosophy and broad-based customer profile.
*   **Q: Any plans for acquisitions?** A: Management is open to opportunities but not actively seeking expansive acquisitions; focus remains on leveraging existing brands (Percol) and distribution.
*   **Q: Is the 15% volume guidance conservative?** A: Management stated it is prudent given market volatility and client "wait and watch" behavior.
*   **Q: Why is standalone performance muted?** A: Management clarified it is a baseline effect and logistics/packaging cost impact; growth is shifting to subsidiaries.

### One-line takeaway
CCL Products delivered strong volume growth and continued deleveraging, maintaining a 15% volume growth guidance while focusing on domestic branded expansion and operational efficiency.
