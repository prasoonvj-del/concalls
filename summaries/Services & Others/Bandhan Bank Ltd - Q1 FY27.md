# Bandhan Bank Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited external macroeconomic headwinds (geopolitical, energy crisis, and funding costs) as reasons for revising ROA guidance downward, despite stable internal execution.

### Key numbers
*   **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 502 crore (up 35% YoY).
*   **Gross Advances:** Rs 1.56 lakh crore (up 16% YoY, 1% QoQ).
*   **Total Deposits:** Rs 1.65 lakh crore (up 7% YoY).
*   **NIM:** 6.2% (stable).
*   **Gross NPA:** 3.1%; **Net NPA:** 0.9%.
*   **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 71.1% (74.3% including security receipts).
*   **Credit Cost:** 1.8% (down from 2.0% in Q4 FY26).
*   **CASA Ratio:** 29.4% (sequential improvement).
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 18.2% (Tier 1 at 17.5%).

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Mix:** Non-EEB portfolio grew 27% YoY, now contributing 2/3 of the loan book. Secured portfolio constitutes 57% of total advances.
*   **EEB Segment:** EEB portfolio stood at Rs 52,641 crore. Management noted seasonal moderation was lower than in previous years.
*   **Asset Quality:** Gross slippages were Rs 1,079 crore. EEB slippages improved sequentially to Rs 604 crore (from Rs 690 crore in Q4 FY26).
*   **One-off:** Rs 61 crore additional gratuity provision due to new wage code changes.
*   **Strategic Shift:** Deliberate reduction in high-cost bulk deposits (down 13% YoY); bulk deposits now 26% of total deposits.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **ROA Guidance:** Revised to 1.2%–1.4% for exit of Q4 FY27 (down from previous 1.6%–1.8% aspiration) due to external factors.
*   **Credit Growth:** Targeting 14% for FY27 (EEB: 5–10%; Non-EEB: 20%+).
*   **Portfolio Mix Goal:** Aiming to maintain EEB book at ~33% and non-EEB at ~65% of total loans.
*   **Credit Cost Guidance:** Maintained at 1.6%–1.8%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro Environment:** Management highlighted uncertainty due to Middle East geopolitical developments, unpredictable monsoon patterns, and potential energy crises affecting the microfinance sector.
*   **Funding:** Despite no repo rate hike, banks are increasing deposit rates due to competitive pressure and system liquidity constraints.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Opex:** Operating expenses were Rs 2,166 crore (up 19% YoY), driven by IT investments and employee costs.
*   **IT Costs:** IT costs are ~8% of total opex (9.5% including depreciation). Management expects these to remain elevated for ~18 months before tapering as investments yield productivity gains.
*   **Margins:** NIMs are under pressure due to rising cost of funds; management aims to hold current levels rather than expand.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Technology:** Significant ongoing investment in Loan Origination Systems (LOS) and Customer Relationship Systems (CRS) to reduce reliance on DSAs and shift sourcing to branches.
*   **Branch Channel:** Branch-sourced assets increased to over Rs 900 crore/month in the last quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** LCR remains comfortable at 140% with a CD ratio of 94%.
*   **Bulk Deposits:** 86% of bulk deposits are non-callable, providing stability.
*   **Asset Sale:** Sold Rs 291 crore of housing NPA loans to an ARC.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **External Factors:** Energy crisis and Middle East conflict impacting operating costs (tech/hardware) and customer sentiment.
*   **Funding Costs:** Savings bank cost of funds increased by ~20–25 bps QoQ.
*   **EEB Vulnerability:** The microfinance segment is viewed as most vulnerable to energy-related cost escalations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why cut ROA guidance if credit costs are improving?** Management clarified the cut is purely due to external factors (rising cost of funds and higher tech/opex costs) rather than internal asset quality issues.
*   **Q: Why grow wholesale banking if margins are dilutive?** Management explained that wholesale banking is an entry point to capture "wallet share" and generate other income (forex, cash management, etc.), not just for interest income.
*   **Q: Can you pass on higher funding costs to MFI customers?** Management stated they have no current plans to hike MFI yields further, focusing on quality over aggressive growth.
*   **Q: Why is IT cost rising?** Management noted that after 3 years of CBS implementation, they are now investing in LOS/CRS to drive future efficiency, which is currently inflating opex.
*   **Q: Is the SMA-0 increase a concern?** Management attributed the rise in SMA-0 to holiday-related disruptions in April and election effects in West Bengal, noting that forward slippage to SMA-1 has been arrested.

### One-line takeaway
Bandhan Bank delivered a resilient Q1 with improved asset quality and strong non-EEB growth, but management has lowered its FY27 ROA guidance to 1.2%–1.4% to account for persistent external macro headwinds and elevated operating costs.
