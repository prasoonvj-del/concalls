# Bluspring Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Quarterly revenues crossed ₹1,000 crore for the first time, EBITDA more than doubled, and PAT nearly doubled, driven by organic growth and acquisitions.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (excl. foundit): ₹1,250 crore, up 49% YoY and 34% QoQ.
- H1 FY27 Revenue: ₹2,180 crore, up 35% YoY.
- EBITDA (excl. foundit): ₹67 crore, up 130% YoY and 90% QoQ.
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: ₹102 crore, up 93% YoY.
- EBITDA Margin: 5.4%, expanding 188 bps YoY and over 156 bps QoQ.
- PAT (excl. foundit): ₹32 crore, up 95% YoY and 96% QoQ.
- H1 FY27 PAT: ₹48 crore, up 76% YoY.
- PAT Margin: 2.5%, expanding 60 bps YoY.
- Segment Revenues: Smart Infra, Energy & Engineering at ₹449 crore (up 189% YoY); Facility & Food Services at ₹601 crore (up 17% YoY); Security Services at ₹201 crore (up 20% YoY).
- Foundit Sales: ₹30 crore (up 62% YoY, 19% QoQ); Revenue: ₹22 crore; EBITDA losses reduced from ₹14 crore to ₹12 crore QoQ.
- Net Debt: ₹126 crore (Gross debt: ₹420+ crore, Cash: ₹320 crore).
- Working Capital Days: 38 days.

### Highlights
- Quarterly revenue crossed ₹1,000 crore for the first time, excluding foundit.
- Successfully completed acquisitions of STEAG Energy Services (India) and LSG Sky Chefs India (consolidated effective August 2026); LSG added a leading international airline to its portfolio.
- Maintained zero on-the-job fatalities during Q2; received 'Zero Harm Performance' award from a major client.
- Mobilized 16 additional contracts in Smart Infra with a combined ACV of ₹641 crore, and 20 new clients in Security Services with an ACV of ₹30 crore.
- Workforce crossed 100,000 employees, adding over 8,000 in H1 (including 2,800 from inorganic additions).
- *Continuation from record:* M&A integration of STEAG and LSG Sky Chefs is ongoing, contributing significantly to inorganic expansion and higher margins as outlined in earlier decks.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in achieving FY27 guidance of crossing ₹4,700 crore in revenue, ₹200 crore in EBITDA, and ₹100 crore in PAT.
- Expects to exit Q4 FY27 near breakeven for foundit, with full-year EBITDA burn below ₹40 crore.
- Working capital days expected to settle around 40 days for the full year.
- Acquisition debt intended to be reduced to ₹150 crore levels by the end of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India attracted USD 351 billion in greenfield FDI between 2021 and 2025; FY26 FDI exceeded USD 94 billion, with another USD 40+ billion in the first five months till August 2026.
- Estimated ₹23 to 26 lakh crore investment expected in power generation over the next five years; metals and mining sector expected to attract ₹7.5 to 9 lakh crore.
- Peak power demand reached 271 gigawatts this summer, with thermal accounting for 66%; thermal expected to retain 50% to 55% share through FY30-FY36.
- Crude steel capacity on track to reach 300 million tonnes by 2030 (currently touching 222 million tonnes).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expansion driven by business mix shift towards higher-margin Energy and Aviation verticals, mobilization of large new contracts, and reversals in estimated credit loss provisions on aged receivables due to collections.
- Depreciation and amortization rose from ₹10 crore in Q1 FY27 to ₹16 crore in Q2 FY27 due to amortization of intangibles (customer relationships and brands) from H1 acquisitions.
- Interest cost increased from ₹8 crore in Q1 FY27 to ₹16 crore in Q2 FY27 due to acquisition debt.
- Segment EBITDA margins: Smart Infra at 10.9% (+266 bps YoY); Facility & Food Services at 4.7% (+72 bps YoY); Security Services at 3.2% (+19 bps YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Purchase consideration for the 2 acquired entities was ₹340 crore, funded largely by acquisition debt (approx. ₹300 crore) and accruals; acquired entities brought in cash balances of approx. ₹188 crore.
- LSG Bengaluru kitchen annual revenue run-rate at ₹110-120 crore with 17-18% margins; aims to expand revenues by 15-20% over 2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total gross debt at over ₹420 crore; cash at around ₹320 crore; net debt at ₹126 crore.
- Working capital debt hovering around ₹150-200 crore. Total loans lent to foundit stand at ₹106 crore against a total board-approved limit of ₹150 crore.
- Unbilled revenue >1 year stands at only ₹11 crore (classified as non-current).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inflationary pressures impacting margins in the food services business.
- Borrowing costs expected to remain elevated due to the prevailing macroeconomic environment and tighter liquidity conditions.

Management stated ₹106 crore has been lent to foundit, guided H2 other expenses at similar levels, and reiterated net working capital settling around 40 days.

### One-line takeaway
Bluspring delivered a landmark Q2 FY27 with revenues crossing ₹1,000 crore and EBITDA more than doubling, propelled by strategic acquisitions (STEAG and LSG) and a strong pivot toward high-margin industrial and energy infrastructure services.
