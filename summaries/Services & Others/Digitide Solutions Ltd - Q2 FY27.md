# Digitide Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue declined sequentially due to deliberate contract renegotiations and exits, but profitability, sales momentum, and AI-led revenues improved.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 760 crores (down 2% QoQ); H1 FY '27 revenue at INR 1,535 crores (up 2.3% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** INR 79 crores (up 2.5% QoQ); EBITDA margin at 10.4% (up 45 bps QoQ).
- **Reported PAT:** INR 8.9 crores (up from INR 2.9 crores in Q1).
- **Tech and Digital Revenue:** INR 244 crores (up 6.6% YoY; 32.1% of revenue).
- **International Revenue:** INR 323 crores (up 13.5% YoY; 42.5% of revenue).
- **TCV Bookings:** Almost INR 400 crores (up 95% sequentially from INR 205 crores in Q1).
- **AI-led Revenue:** INR 30.5 crores (doubled sequentially from INR 1.5 crores in Q1 / 4.1% of overall revenue).
- **Operational Cash Flow (H1):** INR 51 crores (up 27.5% YoY from INR 40 crores).
- **Billed plus Unbilled DSO:** ~84 days (vs 82 days in Q1).
- **Net Cash:** ~INR 76 crores.
- **Headcount:** ~50,000 employees (down 7% QoQ, down 11.6% YoY).

### Highlights
- Restructured operating model into 5 business units supported by two corridors (India++ and International).
- Advanced AI strategy across three layers: AI-infused BPM (35%), AI-enabled platforms (35%), and AI professional services (30%).
- Signed strategic alliances with AWS (achieved advanced tier status) and Anthropic.
- Secured a large Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) mandate in Philippines (up to 1,000 people, ~700 already in mobilization).
- Alldigi board proposed a buyback of INR 48 crores, which is expected to improve liquidity in Digitide standalone.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated the commitment to the previously guided 200 bps EBITDA margin improvement for the full year (delivered 45 bps QoQ improvement in Q2).
- H2 focus: scale GTM engine, accelerate AI enterprise-wide, right-size cost structures, and enforce working capital discipline.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Focus sectors remain insurance, banking, and healthcare.
- Re-pricing and renegotiating contracts resulted in a 2.5x increase in quantum of renegotiations compared to previous quarters, causing near-term top-line pressure.
- International business is shifting towards human-in-the-loop AI models, while India business focuses on outcome-based pricing models.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Segment EBITDA margins expanded by 55 bps to 12.8%; Tech and Digital margins expanded by 315 bps (from 8% to 11.1%), while BPM margins slightly declined to 13.6%.
- Revenue per staff increased by ~12% YoY/QoQ.
- Management is executing high-impact cost optimization initiatives and right-sizing the cost structure, with impacts visible in H2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Established an AI lab in Bangalore and an AI COE in Coimbatore (hired 25+ people).
- Delivery capability centers set up in Canada (insurance platform) and Chennai (EXM platform).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash closed at ~INR 76 crores.
- Cash proceeds from the Alldigi buyback will predominantly be used to reduce standalone debt.
- Billed plus unbilled DSO stood at ~84 days; stronger cash conversion targeted for H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term pressure on revenue due to deliberate exits from low-margin business and repricing.
- Wage revisions and micro/macro impacts affecting the India BPM book.
- Lease liabilities impacting PBT levels.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Manthan Patel (Patel Investments) on Cash Deployment & Profitability:** Management clarified that subtracting Alldigi's profits from Digitide is incorrect as group leadership and profit capabilities sit within Digitide. Cash from Alldigi buyback will reduce standalone debt.
2. **Zaki Nasser (Retail Investor) on Headcount Reduction:** Management noted headcount dropped from 53,000 to 50,000 (~7% QoQ), while revenue per staff rose 12%, showing decoupling from headcount dependency.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclic Investments) on Tech & Digital Margins:** Management explained that platform businesses operate on an ARR/subscription mindset with 2x-3x higher profitability potential than BPO, and they are expanding EXM and insurance platforms internationally and into SMBs.
4. **Simran Thakkar (Beas Capital) on TCV Conversion & BOT Mandate:** Management stated that upwards of 30% to 35% of the ~INR 400 crore TCV will convert to revenue this fiscal. The Philippines BOT mandate involves hiring up to 1,000 people (~700 onboarded).
5. **Manoj Jethwa (KSA Shares) on Investor Trust & Turnaround Confidence:** Management emphasized that Q2 execution on operating models, TCV growth, and AI-led revenues reflect the initial impact of their turnaround strategy.

### One-line takeaway
Digitide Solutions delivered a mixed Q2 FY27 with a 2% sequential revenue dip due to deliberate contract exits, but saw expansion in EBITDA margins (10.4%), a 95% surge in TCV bookings (nearly INR 400 crores), and doubled AI revenues.
