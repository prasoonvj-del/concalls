# One Point One Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth driven by organic expansion, new multi-year mandates, and successful integration of AI-led solutions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 174.96 crore (up 146.9% YoY, up 10.5% QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 333.28 crore (up 138% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 45.32 crore (up 108.6% YoY, up 15.1% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 25.9% (up ~100 bps QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 84.69 crore (up 100% YoY, margin 25.4%).
*   **PAT:** Rs 18.41 crore (up 56% YoY, up 12.9% QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 34.72 crore (up 80% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Business Wins:** Secured a 3-year mandate with a global security depository; a $10.8 million, 4-year contract with a Costa Rican National Training Institute; a Rs 39.32 crore customer experience center mandate from an Indian power utility; and a 3-year mandate with VRL Logistics.
*   **ResolX Platform:** Launched 5 months ago, now has 14 deployments across 7 enterprise clients (insurance, aviation, banking, automotive, digital assets).
*   **AI Strategy:** Clariva (AI summarization tool) processing >100,000 records/month with 99.7% accuracy.
*   **Awards:** Received Silver Award at WORLDCOB’s THE BIZZ Awards 2026 (Texas) for automation/AI.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management targets 15-20% YoY growth from a strategy perspective, excluding potential acquisitions.
*   **H2 Outlook:** Management expects to maintain the current momentum for the remainder of the year.
*   **Long-term:** The Rs 2,000 crore revenue guidance by FY30 remains intact.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Focus:** North America remains the primary focus geography.
*   **Pricing Model:** ResolX uses outcome-based pricing (charging per resolution/consumption) rather than platform/license fees to align with client growth. Core business remains time-and-material (T&M) based.
*   **Client Expansion:** Strategy focuses on deepening relationships within existing accounts and replicating proven workflows across sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Sequential margin improvement driven by sustained renewals at better terms, operating efficiency, and AI-led value addition.
*   **YoY Margin Dip:** Management attributed the YoY margin decline (from 30.6% to 25.9%) to the impact of the Netcom acquisition.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **CWIP:** Rs 80 crore in Capital Work in Progress (CWIP) relates to the AI platform under development. Management expects to capitalize this soon as the product nears completion.
*   **Acquisitions:** Management is actively evaluating potential acquisitions to accelerate growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** The company is debt-free, excluding a specific loan taken for the Netcom acquisition.
*   **Borrowings:** Short-term borrowings increased from Rs 11 crore to Rs 54 crore; cost of debt is ~9%.
*   **Subsidiary Stake:** Parent company increased its stake in a subsidiary by converting Rs 25 crore of receivables/advances into equity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Development Phase:** The AI platform is still under development, requiring further expenditure before it can be fully capitalized and depreciated.
*   **Integration:** Future margin performance depends on scaling AI deployments to a level where they contribute materially to consolidated earnings.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On ResolX Revenue:** Management declined to provide a specific revenue breakup for ResolX, stating it is currently billed through the parent entity, but plans to provide visibility in coming quarters.
*   **On CWIP/Depreciation:** Management clarified that depreciation will commence once the AI product development is complete and revenue lines are established.
*   **On Pricing Strategy:** Management explained that they avoid license fees because automation reduces headcount, which would hurt license-based models; outcome-based pricing aligns their revenue with client growth.
*   **On Debt:** Management clarified that the company is debt-free except for the specific acquisition-related loan.

### One-line takeaway
One Point One Solutions is aggressively scaling its AI-led "Resolution as a Service" model, delivering triple-digit YoY revenue growth while maintaining stable margins and a clear path toward its FY30 revenue targets.
