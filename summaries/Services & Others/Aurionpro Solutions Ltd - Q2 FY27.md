# Aurionpro Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a record revenue quarter, strong order book growth, and the transition from R&D investment to commercial execution.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained the stance of not providing specific revenue/margin guidance, citing market uncertainties and the need to prioritize long-term growth over short-term targets.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "mixed/cautious" to "strong momentum," with management noting that R&D intensity has peaked and the company is now focused on revenue conversion.
- **New topics or risks:** Explicitly mentioned the "atoms-to-bits" strategy (fusing software with hardware) and the move toward in-house AI coding agents to reduce reliance on third-party models.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the promise of revenue acceleration (16% QoQ growth); R&D intensity has begun to moderate as planned.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 415 crore (up 16% YoY and QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 71 crore (17.2% margin).
- **PAT:** Rs 50 crore (12% margin).
- **Order Book:** >Rs 2,000 crore.
- **Data Center Revenue Mix:** 40-50% of TIG (Technology Innovation Group) business.
- **Borrowings:** Increased to Rs 150 crore (from Rs 50 crore in March) due to specific large data center contract execution.

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue.
- Banking & Fintech: Transitioning to "AI-native" banking stack; commercial activity picking up with multiple proofs of concept in Asia and Europe.
- Transit: Expanding globally (e.g., Finland win); leveraging "atoms-to-bits" strategy by manufacturing critical components.
- Data Centers: Rapid growth driven by hyperscaler demand; shifting toward full-stack AI data center partnership.
- R&D: Management stated the peak of R&D investment intensity occurred in H1; capacity is now being diverted to revenue-generating projects.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1.
- Expects to maintain 17-18% margin band for FY27/FY28.
- No specific revenue guidance provided; management prefers to focus on long-term compounding and market leadership.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Banking: Global shift toward agentic AI-native systems is driving demand; decision timelines are stabilizing after geopolitical headwinds.
- Data Centers: Experiencing an "extraordinary" demand cycle for high-density compute; management views this as a generational infrastructure cycle.
- Transit: Global smart mobility opportunity is growing; company is taking its domestic full-stack execution blueprint to international markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins: Enterprise margins are expected to remain stable; mix shift toward data centers (which currently have lower margins than enterprise software) may create optical pressure, but volume expansion is expected to compensate.
- R&D: Capitalization has tapered off as major product builds (Verdict, Infinity OS) are nearing completion.
- AI Costs: Management expects token costs for third-party AI tools to remain stable or decrease; company is building in-house coding agents to optimize costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Strategy: Focusing on "atoms-to-bits"—integrating backward to manufacture critical components for data centers and transit to control quality and unit economics.
- No significant acquisitions planned; focus remains on organic growth and internal product development.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings: Increased to Rs 150 crore due to temporary cash flow pressure from large data center contracts; management expects to be in a better position by year-end.
- Cash Flow: OCF-to-EBITDA target of 60-70% for the year; H2 is expected to see stronger cash conversion.
- Unbilled Revenue: Management acknowledged some "kinks" in billing for new large data center projects but expects these to be resolved in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution: Large data center contracts require "retooling" and have caused temporary cash/billing pressure.
- Geopolitical: Middle East headwinds have eased, but remain a factor in deal cycles.
- Project Timelines: Increasing client pressure to shrink project delivery timelines (from long gestation to 12-18 months).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why the margin drop/pressure?** A: Management explained it is a mix shift; data center business is growing faster (70-80%) and has lower margins than enterprise software, but volume growth makes it accretive.
- **Q: Can you quantify H2 growth?** A: Management declined, stating they prefer to focus on execution rather than providing numbers that might force short-term compromises.
- **Q: Why did borrowings increase?** A: Due to specific large-scale data center contracts that required upfront capital; management expects this to normalize by year-end.
- **Q: Will you separate Data Center as a segment?** A: Management noted they are considering it as the business gains scale and predictability.
- **Q: Is the order book executable?** A: Management stated ~70-75% of the Rs 2,000+ crore order book is executable over the next 4-6 quarters.

### One-line takeaway
Aurionpro has successfully pivoted to an AI-native product strategy and is scaling its data center business, with management signaling that the heavy R&D phase is over and the focus has shifted to revenue conversion and H2 acceleration.
