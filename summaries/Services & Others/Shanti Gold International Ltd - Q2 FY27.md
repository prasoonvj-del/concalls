# Shanti Gold International Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained strong top-line and volume growth, driven by formalization in the industry and capacity expansions, despite a temporary moderation in EBITDA margins due to growth and investment phases.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 746 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 73.4% YoY from Rs 430 crore); Rs 1,462.3 crore in H1 FY27 (up 102.3% YoY from Rs 722.9 crore).
- **Volume:** 535.5 kgs in Q2 FY27 (up 25.4% YoY); 1,057.6 kgs in H1 FY27 (up 41.0% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 42.5 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 15.6% YoY from Rs 36.8 crore); Rs 114 crore in H1 FY27 (up 29.2% YoY from Rs 88.2 crore).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 29.6 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 13.5% YoY from Rs 26 crore); Rs 80 crore in H1 FY27 (up 32.5% YoY from Rs 60.4 crore).
- **Capital Raise:** Raised approximately Rs 99.83 crore via a rights issue during H1 FY27, fully deployed toward working capital.
- **Debt-to-Equity:** Comfortably sitting at 0.5x, with a management comfort level up to 1x.
- **Export Share:** Reached 6% to 7% of sales in H1 FY27 (up from 4%).
- **Gold Metal Loan (GML):** Comprises 15% to 20% of the stock profile, with comfort going up to 30%.
- **Average Holding Cost of Inventory:** Approximately Rs 13,500 per gram.

### Highlights
- Commencement of operations at the extended Marol manufacturing facility added capacity and flexibility, supporting volume growth.
- Broadened product portfolio to include designer jewelry, Turkish jewelry, and upcoming machine-made plain gold jewelry.
- Established a Dubai office with all approvals in place; export volumes expected to ramp up once market conditions settle.
- Rights issue proceeds of ~Rs 99.83 crore fully utilized for working capital requirements.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated full-year FY27 revenue guidance of **Rs 3,200 crore to Rs 3,500 crore** (representing ~60% growth compared to FY26).
- Sustainable EBITDA margin guidance provided at **5% to 7%** due to an evolving product mix and upfront investment costs.
- Historically growing at a CAGR of ~43%; volume growth expected to stay around 25% to 30%.
- Management plans to provide a 3-to-5-year roadmap in the next earnings call.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong festive and wedding season demand (Dhanteras, Diwali) as retailers build inventory.
- Accelerated shift toward organized retail and formalization in both B2C and B2B segments, benefiting large organized manufacturers with scale and design capabilities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins moderated sequentially due to upfront costs from new product verticals, capacity expansions, and scaling investments. Management views this as transitional rather than structural.
- Risk management relies primarily on natural hedging and inventory discipline, alongside GML (15%-20% utilization) and potential MCX levers if required.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Marol Facility:** Extended facility is operational and contributing to volumes.
- **Jaipur Facility:** Spread across 3 acres with a ~65,000 sq. ft. building. Interior and electrical work are underway; commercial operations expected to commence by January or February (delayed slightly from prior timelines).
- **Dubai Office:** Operational approvals in place; export sales expected to scale up in coming months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital intensive industry; short-term borrowings and debt increased to fund inventory build-up for new facilities and product launches.
- Debt-to-equity ratio is at 0.5x, with room to scale up to 1x if needed.
- Treasury investments of ~Rs 18 crore in Ratnaveer and Lalithaa Jewelry described by management as short-term treasury deployment rather than strategic diversification.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary margin dilution due to upfront ramp-up costs of new facilities and evolving product mixes.
- Working capital intensity requires ongoing funding via debt or equity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Preeyam Tolia (Equities) asked about the inventory cost, delayed Jaipur facility timeline, and full-year volume expectations.** Management stated inventory holding is ~Rs 13,500/gram, Jaipur is delayed to Jan/Feb due to interior/electrical detailing, and volume growth is targeted at 25%-30% (reiterating revenue guidance of Rs 3,200-3,500 crore). *(Direct answer)*
2. **Saurabh Gupta asked why EBITDA margins declined compared to peers (at ~8%) and whether Utssav and Shanti Gold would be merged.** Management explained that margin compression is transitional due to new product mix additions and scaling costs, and noted they are open to a merger in the future if required, though both operate with separate managements and distinct product lines currently. *(Direct answer)*
3. **Anshul Thakkar asked about the rationale behind the Rs 18 crore investment in Ratnaveer and Lalithaa Jewelry.** Management clarified this was strictly for short-term treasury deployment to optimize returns and not a core strategic diversification. *(Direct answer)*
4. **Varth Sanghavi asked about the rationale for having two promoter-level companies (Utssav and Shanti Gold) in the B2B jewelry space.** Management explained Utssav was started in 2007 focusing on MRP, shop-in-shop, and online channels with separate management, and there is no customer overlap due to different product lines. *(Direct answer)*
5. **Ayush Aggarwal asked about plans to enter the high-margin "advanced gold" segment.** Management confirmed plans to enter the advanced gold segment in the coming years. *(Direct answer)*

### One-line takeaway
Shanti Gold delivered robust H1 FY27 revenue (up 102.3% YoY) and volume growth driven by capacity expansion and formalization tailwinds, though near-term EBITDA margins saw transitional moderation due to heavy investments in scale and product mix diversification.
