# PAN HR Solution Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and net profit growth driven by expanding workforce deployment and new client additions.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 164.44 crore vs Rs 108.59 crore in H1 FY26 (up 51.43% YoY).
- Total income: Rs 167.12 crore vs Rs 108.69 crore in H1 FY26 (up 53.75% YoY).
- EBITDA: Rs 6.70 crore vs Rs 3.69 crore in H1 FY26 (up 81.64% YoY).
- EBITDA margin: 4.01% vs 3.39% in H1 FY26 (up 62 bps YoY).
- Net profit (PAT): Rs 6.21 crore vs Rs 3.18 crore in H1 FY26 (up 95.28% YoY).
- Net profit margin: 3.72% vs 2.93% in H1 FY26 (up 79 bps YoY).
- Earnings per share (EPS): Rs 8.61 vs Rs 5.88 in H1 FY26.
- Personnel deployed: Over 15,000 as of 30th September 2026.
- Shareholder funds: Rs 43.72 crore as of 30th September 2026.
- Working capital IPO proceeds utilized: Rs 2.85 crore out of Rs 9.75 crore allocated, as of 30th September 2026.
- Unbilled revenue: Around Rs 20-23 crore.
- Trade receivables: Around Rs 21 crore.

### Highlights
- Entered into a temporary staffing agreement with Ciel Et Terre Solar Private Limited for manpower deployment, payroll, and compliance management, valid till April 2027.
- Received the Best Supporters Vendor Award from Shadowfax in September 2026.
- Transitioning from "collect and pay" to "pay and collect" model using IPO proceeds to fund payroll for large enterprise clients.
- Continuing progress toward the three-year revenue target of Rs 1,000 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the 3PL (third-party logistics) warehousing model, planned to start by April 2027, to drive multiple revenue growth and improve EBITDA margins without relying on other income.
- Management maintains its target of achieving Rs 1,000 crore in revenue over the next 2 to 3 years.
- Pradhan Mantri Rojgar Yojana employment scheme reimbursement (other income) is expected to continue until next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong sustained demand observed across e-commerce, quick commerce, logistics, warehousing, manufacturing, and infrastructure sectors.
- Quick commerce highlighted as a very fast-growing segment.
- Major enterprise clients include Flipkart, Myntra, Shadowfax, Jindal Group (JWL), and Euler Motors.
- Typical contract duration is 1 year with annual renewals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other income stood at Rs 2.67 crore in H1 FY27, including Rs 1.67 crore related to the employment scheme reimbursement. Excluding other income, EBITDA grew 12.23% to Rs 4.03 crore.
- Margins were temporarily impacted due to upfront salary payouts for employees in anticipation of billing or expansion purposes before formal invoicing.
- Unskilled salary range: Rs 20,000 to Rs 21,000; semi-skilled: Rs 24,000 to Rs 25,000; skilled: Rs 27,000 to Rs 28,000.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding beyond the Delhi NCR region into Tier-2 cities and key markets across South and West India.
- Scaling presence in quick commerce, FMCG, healthcare support services, and green logistics.
- Developing a 3PL warehousing model where the company controls the warehouse and provides comprehensive services to clients like Flipkart.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Business remains asset-light and debt-light.
- Pay-and-collect model requires higher working capital, supported by IPO proceeds.
- Trade receivables and unbilled revenues remain a normal part of the working capital cycle due to monthly payroll processing by the 7th and subsequent client billing cycles.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High attrition rate inherent in the blue-collar staffing business model.
- Working capital gets stretched and cash flows are impacted as the company shifts towards the pay-and-collect model and hires heavily for festive seasons like Big Billion Days.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gunit (Counter Cyclical PMS)** asked about the reason behind the margin drop and cash flow impact. **Management** explained it was due to upfront payroll investments for employees not yet billed, and festive season hiring (e.g., Big Billion Days), but noted profitability and revenue will benefit later from the 3PL model.
2. **Gunit** asked about the proportion of pay-and-collect contracts. **Management** stated it is approximately 20% currently, with the rest on collect-and-pay, and they are gradually increasing pay-and-collect.
3. **Gunit** inquired about unbilled revenues and trade receivables. **Management** clarified that unbilled revenue (around Rs 20-23 crore) arises because billing occurs after paying salaries on the 7th of the subsequent month.
4. **Keshav (Counter Cyclical PMS)** asked about differentiation from large competitors like Quess Corp and TeamLease. **Management** stated their USP lies in blue-collar and grey-collar deployment in e-commerce, manufacturing, and logistics, unlike white-collar focused peers, with listed peer ITCONS being closer in the space.
5. **Keshav** asked about the feasibility of the Rs 1,000 crore revenue target. **Management** reiterated that they are currently operating on that run rate and the upcoming 3PL model in Feb/March will support target achievement.

### One-line takeaway
PAN HR Solution reported strong 51% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27 to Rs 164.44 crore and near-doubling of PAT, backed by expanding blue-collar staffing deployments and strategic shifts toward pay-and-collect and 3PL warehousing models.
