# Services & Others: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Quarterly results across the Services & Others sector reflect a dual reality: strong top-line momentum in technology-enabled, asset-light, and infrastructure-linked services, contrasted against margin compression in traditional BPO, freight logistics, and overseas student recruitment. Companies capitalizing on domestic infrastructure execution (Monarch Surveyors, Steamhouse India), managed workspace/campus infrastructure (Dev Accelerator, Elevate Campuses), and high-margin specialized staffing (Bluspring Enterprises, PAN HR Solution) posted robust revenue growth. Conversely, players exposed to global trade rate volatility (Glottis), regulatory/policy shifts in overseas education (Crizac), or IT/GSI spending slowdowns (NIIT, TeamLease) faced margin pressures and volume softness.

Consolidation and strategic M&A featured heavily this quarter. Bluspring Enterprises scaled past ₹1,000 crore quarterly revenue post its acquisitions of STEAG and LSG Sky Chefs, while Veranda Learning completed the demerger of its commerce vertical. Across the board, balance sheet discipline remained a key priority, with multiple companies deploying IPO proceeds to clear debt or fund working capital requirements.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Traditional service providers in India relied heavily on manpower-intensive delivery, localized physical operations, simple agent/brokerage transactions, and standard cost-plus or time-and-material (T&M) pricing. Over the last five years, corporate demand has driven a shift toward managed infrastructure platforms, asset-light delivery models, and outcome-based pricing structures. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **AI integration and outcome-based pricing models** | scaling | One Point One Solutions launched its ResolX platform across 7 enterprise clients using outcome-based pricing rather than license fees; Physicswallah noted AI now writes 90% of internal code; NIIT reported AI/GenAI programs contributing 12.4% of overall revenue [One Point One Solutions Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Physicswallah Ltd concall Q2 FY27; NIIT Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **Expansion of managed infrastructure and platform models** | scaling | Dev Accelerator scaled operational footprint to 1.16 million sq. ft. across 28 centers while signing an 8.6 lakh sq. ft. development project; Elevate Campuses expanded to over 20,000 student beds and contracted 2,450 beds at O.P. Jindal Global University [Dev Accelerator Ltd concall Q2 FY27; Elevate Campuses Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **Pivot toward high-margin specialized staffing and pay-and-collect models** | early | PAN HR Solution is deploying IPO proceeds to shift to a pay-and-collect model for enterprise clients; TeamLease pruned 5,500 low-margin general staffing associate roles to focus on specialized staffing and GCC client additions [PAN HR Solution Ltd concall Q2 FY27; TeamLease Services Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus points**
- **Domestic infrastructure and industrial momentum:** Strong, continuous demand is visible across industrial steam utilities (Steamhouse India), government infrastructure surveying and railway DPRs (Monarch Surveyors), and facility management tied to Swachh Bharat and railway mandates (Krystal Integrated Services).
- **Flexible workspace and enterprise GCC expansion:** Global Capability Centers (GCCs) continue to drive robust office space leasing and staffing demand across Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities (Dev Accelerator, Updater Services, ECOS Mobility).

**Divergences**
- **Overseas study and international mobility:** Crizac faced a 36.7% YoY revenue decline in Q2 due to West Asia geopolitical disruptions and currency swings causing student deferrals to UK universities. Conversely, domestic education and test prep players like Physicswallah (online revenue up 26.4%) and Veranda Learning (continuing revenue up 17%) saw healthy demand despite specific headwinds in NEET exam cycles.
- **Freight and logistics pricing:** Glottis reported a 344% YoY surge in sea export revenue but suffered severe margin compression due to global spot freight volatility, whereas domestic corporate transportation providers like ECOS Mobility sustained steady enterprise volume growth (trips up 15% YoY).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Margin compression in freight and paper inputs:** Glottis saw EBITDA margins drop to 1.2% due to volatile crude/charter rates and spot pricing. Navneet Education faced pricing pressures in export stationery and a recent ₹2,000/tonne (~3%) increase in paper prices.
- **Operating leverage driving expansion:** True Colors reported gross margins expanding by 405 bps YoY to 30.2% due to a higher revenue mix of high-margin ink and paper consumables. Dev Accelerator expanded cash EBIT margins by 625 bps YoY to 23.8% on flat employee costs.
- **Labor code implementation and wage pressures:** Krystal Integrated Services and NIS Management noted temporary margin pressure from new labor code adjustments and expanded ESIC coverage limits (>₹21,000), though Updater Services and TeamLease confirmed these costs remain contractually recoverable from clients over time.

## Capex & capacity
- **Asset-light and debottlenecked brownfield expansion:** Ardee Industries increased refining capacity by 50.9% to 1,56,950 MTPA through brownfield debottlenecking without heavy capex. Steamhouse India commissioned its Jhagadia facility while scaling waste-to-steam projects across industrial clusters.
- **Backward integration investments:** True Colors committed ₹40–45 crore toward INKIA Phase-1 ink capacity (150 tons/month) and announced a 50/50 JV for a ₹320–340 crore paper mill project.
- **Balance sheet deleveraging via equity proceeds:** Elevate Campuses fully deployed ₹750 crore of IPO proceeds toward debt reduction, lowering its cost of debt to ~8.9%. Steamhouse India and PAN HR Solution similarly utilized listing proceeds to clear trade payables and fund payroll working capital.

## What changed since last quarter
- **Guidance revisions:** ECOS Mobility adjusted full-year revenue growth guidance to 13–15% (from 15–18% in Q1) while narrowing EBITDA margin targets to 10–11%. Sunrakshakk Industries accelerated its expectation to cross the ₹1,000 crore revenue target ahead of its previous FY28 timeline.
- **Tone shifts:** Crizac shifted from positive to mixed due to Q2 student deferrals, while maintaining full-year flat revenue guidance. NIS Management adopted a cautious tone on full-year margins (adjusting target to 7–7.5%) due to delays in high-margin CCTV invoicing.
- **Completed promises:** Veranda Learning successfully completed the demerger of its commerce business (J.K. Shah Commerce Education). Bluspring Enterprises completed the consolidation of STEAG Energy Services and LSG Sky Chefs India.

## What to watch next quarter
- **H2 Intake Recovery in Study Abroad:** Monitoring Crizac's expected Q3 revenue rebound (guided at 10-12% YoY growth) as student deferrals convert into winter intake enrollments.
- **Freight Realizations and Container Rollout:** Tracking Glottis's Q3 rollout of owned container fleets and potential shipment profitability recovery under lower crude oil price scenarios.
- **Listing of Demerged Entities:** Watching the formal market listing of J.K. Shah Commerce Education following SEBI/BSE/NSE approvals post-demerger from Veranda Learning.
- **Waste-to-Steam Plant Commissioning:** Execution and volume ramp-up of Steamhouse India's Jhagadia project (commissioning November 2026) and upcoming industrial gas units.
