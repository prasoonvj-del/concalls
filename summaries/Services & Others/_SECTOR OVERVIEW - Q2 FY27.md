# Sector Overview: Services & Others (Q2 FY27)

As a senior analyst covering this diverse basket of 27 companies, the overarching theme for Q2 FY27 is **resilience amid volatility**. Managements are largely navigating a "K-shaped" economic environment where premium and urban demand remains robust, while rural and mass-market segments show signs of fatigue. While most companies delivered strong operational results, the focus has shifted from aggressive top-line growth to "value-led growth," operational efficiency, and margin protection against persistent inflationary pressures.

---

## Big picture
The sector is characterized by a divergence in strategy. Capital-intensive players (ACC, Ambuja, Adani Power) are aggressively expanding capacity, banking on structural demand. Conversely, consumer-facing and tech-oriented firms (Asian Paints, Birlasoft, Best Agrolife) are prioritizing margin protection and operational discipline over pure volume growth. A common thread across the board is the successful management of supply chain and geopolitical headwinds, with many companies reporting record profitability despite inflationary inputs.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is broad agreement that urban and premium demand remains healthy. Companies like **AAVAS Financiers** and **Bajaj Housing Finance** report resilient housing demand, while **Adani Power** highlights structural baseload demand driven by data centers.
*   **Divergence:** A clear split exists regarding rural demand. **Bajaj Auto** and **AWL Agri Business** explicitly flag rural sluggishness and a "K-shaped" recovery where the bottom of the pyramid is under pressure. In contrast, **Asian Paints** notes that rural markets are currently leading over urban markets, highlighting the inconsistency in demand signals across the sector.
*   **Sector-Specifics:** **ACME Solar** reports a structural shift in state procurement from RTC thermal to BESS-based peak power, creating a new growth engine. **Bandhan Bank** notes stabilization in its EEB (microfinance) portfolio, suggesting a bottoming out of stress in that segment.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Inflationary Pressure:** Persistent inflation in crude derivatives, steel, and energy remains a primary concern. **Asian Paints** and **Bajaj Auto** are actively managing this through "calibrated" price increases, balancing inflation pass-through with the risk of demand destruction.
*   **Margin Defense:** Companies are increasingly relying on operational efficiency to protect margins. **ACC** and **Ambuja** are leveraging green power and logistics optimization (reducing lead distances) to offset fuel costs. **Acutaas Chemicals** and **Best Agrolife** are successfully expanding margins by shifting their product mix toward patented/specialty products, moving away from low-margin generics.
*   **Energy Costs:** Energy remains a significant headwind. **Adani Total Gas** reported a 43% YoY surge in gas costs, while **ACC/Ambuja** noted that spot fuel costs are significantly higher than their quarterly averages, forcing a shift toward domestic coal to mitigate impact.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Aggressive Expansion:** **ACC** and **Ambuja Cements** have announced a massive 30 million tonne expansion plan. **ACME Solar** has raised its 2030 renewable target to 15 GW. **Bajaj Auto** is expanding capacity from 6.25L to 7.75L units/month.
*   **Cautious/Deferred:** Conversely, **Best Agrolife** has deferred capex, prioritizing profitability and balance sheet stability. **Aequs** is recalibrating its consumer segment path to profitability, pushing back breakeven timelines.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Shifts:** **Acutaas Chemicals** raised revenue growth guidance to ~30% (from 25%). **ACME Solar** raised its BESS commissioning target. **Asian Paints** lowered its volume growth guidance slightly (8-9% vs 8-10%).
*   **Strategic Pivots:** **ACC** and **Ambuja** shifted from "shedding low-margin business" to "value-led growth." **Aurionpro** moved from a phase of heavy R&D investment to a focus on revenue conversion. **Aurum PropTech** completed the acquisition of Housing.com and is now in active integration mode.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **AAVAS Financiers** shifted from expecting spread compression to being confident in maintaining >5% spreads. **Bandhan Bank** moved from a "cautious" stance to reporting "balanced and steady progress."

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Rural Demand Recovery:** Will the "K-shaped" recovery persist, or will rural demand improve in the second half? (Watch: Bajaj Auto, AWL).
*   **Interest Rate Impact:** How will the repo rate hike impact NIMs and customer attrition in the housing finance sector? (Watch: Bajaj Housing, AAVAS).
*   **Commodity/Fuel Prices:** Will the volatility in energy and raw material prices stabilize, or will it continue to pressure margins? (Watch: ACC, Ambuja, Asian Paints).
*   **Integration Execution:** Can companies successfully integrate recent acquisitions and realize synergies? (Watch: Aurum PropTech, Bandhan Bank).
*   **Supply Chain Stability:** Will the "erratic" supply chain issues reported by Bajaj Auto and Bansal Wire resolve?
