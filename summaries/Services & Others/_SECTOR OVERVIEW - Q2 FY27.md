# Services & Others: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

The Services & Others sector in Q2 FY27 presents a bifurcated narrative. While infrastructure-linked services (Sugs Lloyd), organized retail/manufacturing (Shanti Gold), and flexible workspace providers (Smartworks) are reporting robust growth and aggressive capacity expansion, consumer-facing and travel-linked segments (Crizac, Dreamfolks) are grappling with external geopolitical and regulatory headwinds.

## Big picture
Managements are largely focused on scaling operations and internalizing technology to drive efficiency. A common theme across the sector is the "transitional" nature of margin pressure, with companies like Shanti Gold and Billionbrains Garage Ventures (Groww) prioritizing long-term market share and tech-stack development over immediate margin maximization. Conversely, the sector is highly sensitive to external shocks; Crizac and Dreamfolks highlighted how geopolitical tensions and visa/travel policies can create immediate, material impacts on revenue.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus on Growth:** There is a strong consensus among the growth-oriented players that demand remains robust. Smartworks reported a record leasing environment, raising its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 33%–35% (from 28%–30%). Sugs Lloyd reported its strongest half-year ever, with a record order book of Rs 1,067 crore.
*   **Divergence on External Factors:** While domestic demand remains strong, companies with international exposure are facing volatility. Crizac reported a 36.7% YoY drop in operating income due to student deferrals caused by West Asia geopolitical tensions and currency fluctuations. Similarly, Dreamfolks cited ongoing geopolitical challenges as a primary factor in its revenue contraction.
*   **Regulatory Uncertainty:** Billionbrains Garage Ventures noted that while MTF (Margin Trading Facility) growth is steady, the industry faces potential headwinds from regulatory changes, specifically the potential implementation of UPI MDR for capital markets, which management labeled as "unaffordable."

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Investment-Led Margin Compression:** Several companies are accepting near-term margin dilution to fund growth. Shanti Gold saw EBITDA margins moderate due to upfront costs from new product verticals and capacity expansions. Crizac’s EBITDA margin declined 418 bps YoY to 32.0% due to a lower revenue base and increased tech/team investments.
*   **Tech & Operational Costs:** Billionbrains Garage Ventures reported a one-time "cost to serve" hit of Rs 10 crore – Rs 13 crore due to an expiry day file delay, while also noting higher compute costs for AI integration.
*   **Sequential Improvement:** Dreamfolks noted a sequential improvement in profitability, driven by the absence of one-time upfront minimum guarantee payments made in Q1.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Aggressive Expansion:** Smartworks is in the midst of its largest capacity block opening in history (~45,000 seats in H2 FY27). Shanti Gold has operationalized its Marol facility and is finalizing its Jaipur facility (expected Jan/Feb).
*   **Tech-Led Capex:** Crizac committed USD 2.5 million toward its "EduMentor" AI platform. Billionbrains Garage Ventures is focusing on in-house tech stack development to reduce per-token costs.
*   **Backward Integration:** Sugs Lloyd is pursuing backward integration to manufacture high-purchase items (vacuum circuit breakers and RMUs) in-house to improve margins.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Upgrades:** Smartworks raised its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 33%–35% from 28%–30%.
*   **Tone Shifts:** Billionbrains Garage Ventures noted a decrease in "Cost to Grow" QoQ due to the absence of IPL-related branding events.
*   **Performance Trends:** Dreamfolks saw a return to positive gross profit (8.7% for H1 FY27) and a narrowing of net losses compared to Q1.

## Company one-liners
*   **Billionbrains Garage Ventures:** Focusing on internalizing its AI tech stack and tightening MTF risk controls amidst regulatory uncertainty.
*   **Crizac:** Facing temporary student deferrals but maintaining full-year guidance based on strong H2 intake visibility.
*   **Dreamfolks:** Reporting revenue contraction and net losses while scaling non-airport lifestyle and global lounge initiatives.
*   **Shanti Gold:** Delivering robust revenue growth driven by capacity expansion, despite transitional margin moderation.
*   **Smartworks:** Raising revenue guidance on the back of strong enterprise demand and aggressive capacity additions.
*   **Sugs Lloyd:** Achieving record H1 performance and order book growth while diversifying geographically across 11 states.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Regulatory Impact:** The actual implementation and volume impact of the derivative circular (Billionbrains) and potential UPI MDR capping.
*   **H2 Recovery:** Whether Crizac’s pipeline converts into the expected 10-12% YoY growth in Q3.
*   **Capacity Ramp-up:** The occupancy trajectory for Smartworks as it brings its massive 45,000-seat capacity online.
*   **Cash Flow:** Whether Sugs Lloyd can manage its working capital pressure while maintaining its aggressive growth trajectory.

*Based on: Billionbrains Garage Ventures Ltd, Crizac Ltd, Dreamfolks Services Ltd, Shanti Gold International Ltd, Smartworks Coworking Spaces Ltd, Sugs Lloyd Ltd*
