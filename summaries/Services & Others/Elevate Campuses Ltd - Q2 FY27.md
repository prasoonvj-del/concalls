# Elevate Campuses Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust 28% YoY revenue growth, strong execution on expansion pipeline, and successful deployment of IPO proceeds for debt reduction.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Total Income):** Rs 247 crore in Q2 (up 28% YoY); Rs 478 crore in H1 (up 29% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 167 crore in Q2 (up 16% YoY; margin 69%); Rs 343 crore in H1 (up 21% YoY; margin 74% adjusted).
- **PAT:** Reported PAT was Rs 67 crore in H1 (down 58% YoY due to base-period exceptional gain); PAT before exceptional items stood at Rs 72 crore.
- **Normalized EBITDA:** Rs 158 crore for Q2; Rs 315 crore for H1 (up 20% YoY).
- **Pro forma Cash PAT:** Rs 78 crore for Q2; Rs 156 crore for H1.
- **Portfolio & Occupancy:** 20,000+ owned and managed beds (95% occupancy in owned and managed student accommodation); 2,100 beds under development; 58,000+ managed beds across 23 institutions; 18 K-12 campuses (16 in India, 2 in UAE); total capacity catered exceeds 107,000.
- **Cost of Debt:** Reduced by 30 bps post-Nov 1 to ~8.9%.
- **Leverage:** Net debt to annualized H1 EBITDA stood at 3.5x.

### Highlights
- First earnings call since listing on September 30, 2026.
- Entire Rs 750 crore from IPO proceeds earmarked for debt repayment has been fully utilized.
- Executed definitive agreements post-Q2 for acquiring an additional 2,450 beds at O.P. Jindal Global University for Rs 1,350 crore (payable over 9 years, including Rs 150 crore upfront).
- Commenced pre-construction activities post-Q2 for an 1,800+ bed student accommodation at IIT Madras under the PPP model, with MoF approval received for viability gap funding.
- Undertook active preventive repair and maintenance (PPM) during Q2 summer vacation window.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be better than H1.
- Management intends to grow from late teens to 20% going forward (pro forma historical growth cited at ~23%).
- Four acquisition deals currently in diligence are expected to close within the next six months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- TAM is a $9 billion revenue market ($5 billion K-12, $4 billion student accommodation), targeting private unaided institutions.
- India has ~30 crore enrolled students (GER of 58%), with ~12 crore in private education institutions.
- Elevate Campuses holds less than 1% market share in the highly fragmented market.
- Student accommodation segment represents 55/45 split with K-12, but portfolio is expected to move slightly more towards student accommodation due to operating leverage and value-added services.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin impacted by summer preventive planning and maintenance (PPM), IPO-related expenses of ~Rs 2 crore, higher employee expenses of ~Rs 5 crore, and share-based compensation of ~Rs 6 crore.
- H1 total share-based expense was Rs 87 million (~Rs 9 crore).
- Cost of debt reduced by 30 bps to 8.9% post-November 1, saving ~Rs 7 crore in interest costs so far.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 6 greenfield/brownfield projects under development to be delivered between FY28 and FY30, comprising 2,100+ student beds and ~3.8 lakh sq. ft. (total project cost ~Rs 646 crore, pending capex ~Rs 350 crore, entry yield on cost 10%-12%).
- 10 total acquisition deals in pipeline (4 in diligence stage, 5-7 in early to mid-stage discussions).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stands at Rs 3,676 crore.
- Net debt to EBITDA is 3.5x (guardrail/cap maintained around 4.5x).
- Weighted average tenure of loan book is ~9 years (new loans raised for 12-15 years).
- Portfolio operates on a negative working capital cycle.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Actual results may differ due to risks and uncertainties in forward-looking statements.
- Summer vacation closures in July/August create seasonal maintenance windows impacting Q2 margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Agrawal (IIFL Capital) on acquisition pipeline stage and EBITDA contribution:** Management stated 4 of 10 assets are in diligence for closure in 6 months; H2 will be better than H1, and these 4 deals will be enough to deliver high-teens to 20% growth.
- **Mohit Agrawal on IRR/yield thresholds:** Management reiterated entry yield on cost of 10%-12% for greenfield asset acquisitions.
- **Sumit Kumar on leverage guardrails:** Management confirmed net debt to EBITDA cap is ~4.5x (currently 3.5x) and weighted average loan tenure is 9 years with no near-term financing pressure.
- **Sumit Kumar on seasonality:** Management explained Q2 involves summer maintenance (July-August) and a smaller ~20-day window between mid-December and early January, creating minor Q2/Q4 seasonality.

### One-line takeaway
Elevate Campuses delivered robust H1FY27 revenue growth of 29% post-IPO, backed by strong execution on its expansion pipeline, reduced cost of debt, and robust contractual cash flows.
