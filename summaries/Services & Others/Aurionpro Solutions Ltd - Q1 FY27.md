# Aurionpro Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27

**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew YoY, but management acknowledged execution challenges, project timing shifts, and margin pressure due to strategic R&D investments.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 358 crore (up 6.3% YoY).
*   **Banking & FinTech Revenue:** Rs 200+ crore (up ~5% YoY).
*   **TIG (Technology Innovation Group) Revenue:** Rs 157 crore (up 8.4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 61 crore (17% margin).
*   **PAT:** Rs 45 crore.
*   **New Customer Logos:** 23 (record for Q1).

### Highlights
*   Secured largest-ever order in the United States.
*   Won a large transaction banking mandate.
*   Commenced execution on one of the company's largest data center projects.
*   Management initiated a "mini pivot" to diversify geographic dependence away from the Middle East (MEA) toward Southeast Asia and Europe.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects growth to accelerate through the year, with stronger momentum anticipated in H2.
*   No specific full-year revenue or margin guidance provided due to market uncertainties.
*   Data center business growth is expected to move above the recent 40-50% trajectory to a higher band.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains strong with a healthy order book and pipeline.
*   Enterprise software is undergoing an architectural shift toward AI-native stacks (AurionAI, Arya.AI, Lexi labs).
*   Data center business is at an "inflection point" driven by India's AI infrastructure super cycle.
*   Transit business continues to see success with mandates from Mumbai and Delhi Metro.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margin compression attributed to revenue mix, capacity deployment for R&D/product build-outs, and project execution timing.
*   R&D expenditure expected to remain at 10.5-11% of revenue for the year.
*   Data center margins currently 4-5 points below enterprise margins; management expects volume to offset this gap.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Capacity is being built in advance to meet data center demand.
*   Management is prioritizing internal product build-outs ("banking software 2.0") over short-term margin optimization.
*   No significant acquisitions planned for the near term.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management is focused on improving cash conversion (EBITDA to cash) for FY27.
*   H1 typically sees negative OCF due to payment cycles; management aims to be in "good shape" by the end of H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical disruption in the Middle East (MEA) impacted deal closures and revenue growth.
*   Project timing shifts and supply chain pressures.
*   Execution bottlenecks in converting large, long-duration projects into revenue.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the margin drop despite banking growth?** A: Management clarified it is due to capacity diversion toward R&D and product build-outs, not pass-through costs.
*   **Q: Why the sequential decline in TIG?** A: Large deals take time to ramp up; management expects acceleration in Q2-Q4.
*   **Q: Are you losing market share by focusing on R&D?** A: Management stated win rates remain strong (>50%) and believes AI-driven productivity gains will eventually offset capacity diversion.
*   **Q: Can you provide a revenue/margin number for FY27?** A: Management declined, citing too many uncertain factors.
*   **Q: Will H1 OCF be positive?** A: Management noted H1 is typically negative/near-zero due to payment cycles; the focus is on H2 acceleration.

### One-line takeaway
Aurionpro is prioritizing long-term AI-native product development and data center expansion over short-term margin optics, with management expecting growth to accelerate in the second half of FY27.
