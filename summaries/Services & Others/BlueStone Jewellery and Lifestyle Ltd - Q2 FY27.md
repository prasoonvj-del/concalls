# BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported 44% YoY revenue growth and EBITDA more than doubling, marking the fourth consecutive profitable quarter.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained long-term revenue target of Rs 12,000 crore by 2030.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from navigating gold price volatility to focusing on "earning" customers; management remains confident in the business model despite festive seasonality shifts.
- **New topics:** In-principle board approval for Rs 300 crore NCD fundraising to retire high-cost debt; experimental launch of a flagship store in Kolkata; dismissal of "lab-grown gold" as a hypothetical/science-fiction concern.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on store expansion (27 new stores opened in Q2).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 738 crore (+44% YoY).
- **SSG (Same Store Sales Growth):** 28%.
- **Marketing Spend:** 5.7% of revenue.
- **Studded Mix:** 55.7%.
- **Customer Base:** Crossed 1 million customers.
- **Store Expansion:** 27 new stores opened; entered 7 new cities.

### Highlights
- **Customer Milestone:** Crossed 1 million customers; management views this as the point where the brand becomes a "legacy."
- **Design Strategy:** Expanded to 10,500+ designs; launched a handcrafted/antique range at the new Kolkata flagship store.
- **Business Model:** Reaffirmed "online-first" discovery strategy; management stated that online marketing is the most efficient channel for both brand building and performance marketing.
- **Entry-level focus:** Redesigned entry-level range to improve affordability and customer acquisition.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Reiterated target of Rs 12,000 crore by 2030.
- **Marketing Spend:** Management expects to reduce marketing spend to ~4.5% of revenue over the next four years.
- **Fundraising:** Board approved in-principle Rs 300 crore NCD issuance to optimize the debt stack and retire high-cost debt.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Festive Season:** Navratri started late, shifting some festive demand into Q3; management noted that jewellery demand does not operate in tandem with other consumer categories.
- **Tier 2/3 Markets:** Management sees no behavioral difference between metros and Tier 2/3 cities; growth is broad-based.
- **Lab-Grown Diamonds (LGD):** Management noted LGDs are not impacting their jewellery business, as LGD traction is limited to the solitaire space (which is <1% of their business).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Contribution Margin:** Declined 50 bps YoY; management attributed this to non-homogeneous product mix and noted that manufacturing productivity improvements have helped offset costs.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Management highlighted factory consolidation and productivity gains over the last 4-5 quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Store Strategy:** Opened 27 stores in Q2; flagship store in Kolkata is an experiment to test new product lines (e.g., Kundan, handmade); management may open 1-2 more flagship stores.
- **Inventory:** No specific inventory target; older cohorts receive more inventory allocation based on demonstrated capital productivity and ROIC.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Debt:** Planning to use NCDs to refinance high-cost debt and improve the blended cost of debt.
- **Hedging:** 50% hedging policy on inventory remains unchanged.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Gold Price Volatility:** Management acknowledged that sharp gold price movements can dislocate entry-level price points, impacting new customer acquisition.
- **Lab-grown gold:** Dismissed as a "PR gimmick" and "science fiction," noting that creating gold via fusion is currently impossible and experimental.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **AOV Growth:** Management expects 10-15% ticket growth even if gold prices stabilize, driven by repeat customers maturing in the system.
- **Cohort Data:** Management declined to provide quarterly cohort data, stating it is better analyzed in blocks rather than quarterly increments.
- **Studded Mix:** Management clarified that the studded mix is an outcome of consumer behavior, not a target, and they focus on GMROI rather than margin mix.
- **Flagship Stores:** Management clarified that flagship stores are an experiment to handle more use cases and techniques; they are not yet ready to scale this format.
- **Inventory Turns:** Management reiterated that inventory turns will improve as the store portfolio matures and vintages build.

### One-line takeaway
BlueStone is leveraging its tech-first, in-house manufacturing model to drive scale, with management focusing on long-term capital productivity and repeat customer growth while dismissing external threats like lab-grown gold.
