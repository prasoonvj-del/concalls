# Ambuja Cements Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted resilient performance, a shift toward positive volume growth in H2, and a clear, value-accretive expansion roadmap despite geopolitical and monsoon headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 cost target of Rs 4,250/tonne. Capex guidance for FY27 remains Rs 6,500–7,000 crore.
*   **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "sacrificing volume for value" (Q1) to "expecting positive volume growth" in H2 FY27. Management now expects industry demand to grow in the high single digits for FY27.
*   **New topics:** Announced a 30 million tonne (MT) expansion plan across 14 projects (149 MTPA by FY29; 155 MTPA by FY30). Merger of Orient Cement with Ambuja is proceeding to NCLT.
*   **Last quarter's promises:** Q1 target of 119 MT capacity by FY27-end has been superseded by the new, more aggressive long-term expansion roadmap.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 9,197 crore.
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1,271 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** ~14%.
*   **EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 770.
*   **PAT:** Rs 236 crore.
*   **Cement Sales Volume:** 16.5 million metric tonnes.
*   **Trade Volume Growth:** 8.6% YoY.
*   **Trade Share:** 74% (up 6% YoY).
*   **Premium Products:** 37% of trade sales (up 2% YoY).
*   **Blended Cement Share:** 82% (up from 77%).
*   **Total Cost:** Rs 4,410/tonne (normalized: Rs 4,182/tonne).
*   **Fuel Cost:** Rs 1.87/1,000 kcal (vs Rs 1.60 YoY, Rs 1.66 QoQ).
*   **Green Power:** 61% share (352 million kWh sold).
*   **RMC Volume:** 1.07 million cubic meters (up 19% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Expansion:** 14 new projects announced to add ~30 MT cement and 10.3 MT clinker capacity.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Completed major plant shutdowns; working capital cycle improved by 10 days.
*   **Logistics:** Primary lead distance reduced from 265 km to 254 km.
*   **Green Energy:** Green power revenue of Rs 136 crore; fly ash revenue of Rs 25 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume:** Expects volume growth ahead of the industry in H2 FY27.
*   **Cost:** Reaffirmed FY27 cost target of Rs 4,250/tonne.
*   **Capacity:** Targeting 149 MTPA by FY29 and 155 MTPA by FY30.
*   **Merger:** Expects completion of ACC and Orient Cement mergers within this financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Regional Performance:** North (13% trade volume growth), West (14% trade volume growth), South (13% trade volume growth), Central (9% trade volume growth). East was broadly flat.
*   **Industry Demand:** Management expects high single-digit growth for the full fiscal year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Fuel Mix:** Increased reliance on domestic coal vs. high-cost pet coke to mitigate geopolitical cost escalations.
*   **Logistics:** Moderately higher costs due to diesel prices, partially offset by reduced lead distances.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Q2 Commissioning:** 4.6 MT capacity added (Dahej, Salai Banwa, Kalamboli).
*   **Ramping Up:** Bathinda and Jodhpur (Penna) stabilizing; Warisaliganj (2.4 MT) expected in H2 FY27.
*   **Capex Budget:** Rs 6,500–7,000 crore for FY27; ~Rs 7,500 crore/year for the next two years for the 30 MT expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Remains debt-free with a net worth of Rs 72,238 crore.
*   **Cash:** Cash and equivalents of Rs 1,070 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical conflicts, monsoon-related headwinds, higher energy costs, and global uncertainty.
*   Potential moderation in construction cycles in drought-affected states.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Capex Strategy:** Analysts questioned the "change of heart" regarding aggressive capex. Management stated the plan is "calibrated," utilizes surplus clinker, and focuses on value-accretive markets rather than just volume.
*   **Volume vs. Value:** Management clarified they are not sacrificing market share but are prioritizing "value-led growth" and exiting negative-EBITDA institutional volumes.
*   **Fuel Costs:** Analysts noted spot fuel costs (Rs 2.85/kcal) are higher than Q2 average (Rs 1.87/kcal). Management noted domestic coal mix helps mitigate this.
*   **Execution Confidence:** Analysts questioned the ability to execute 30 MT expansion. Management cited "robust scheduling" and 14–18 month completion timelines, noting they have learned from past contractor issues.
*   **Utilization:** Management admitted current utilization (61-62%) is low but expects improvement in Q3/Q4 as new capacities ramp up.

### One-line takeaway
Ambuja has pivoted from a "value-only" stance to a "value-led growth" strategy, backing it with a massive 30 MT expansion plan while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet.
