# Smartworks Coworking Spaces Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and profitability are growing sequentially, and management has raised its FY27 revenue growth guidance.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 591 crore (up 39% YoY, 8% QoQ)
- Normalised EBITDA: Rs 117 crore (up 68% YoY) at a margin of 19.8% (up 340 bps YoY)
- Normalised PAT: Rs 43 crore (up 136% YoY); Reported PAT: Rs 26 crore
- Contracted rental revenue: ~Rs 5,800 crore
- Total platform footprint: 17.5 million sq ft across 71 centres (10.9 million sq ft across 58 centres operational)
- H1 capex deployed: Rs 307 crore
- Net debt: Rs 83 crore
- Annualised normalised ROCE: 21.6% (up 800 bps YoY)
- Overall occupancy: ~81%; Committed occupancy: ~86% (Mature centres at 88% occupancy and 91% committed)
- Normalised operating cash flow (H1): Rs 202 crore (Rs 107 crore in Q2); OCF to EBITDA: 0.9x

### Highlights
- Sixth consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth since listing.
- Large enterprises contribute 91% of rental revenue; 35+ clients have more than 1,000 seats (representing >41% of rental revenue).
- Multi-city revenue stands at 35% of rental revenue (70+ clients in multiple cities).
- GCCs account for 22% of rental revenue. IT/ITES share reduced to ~36% of rental revenue.
- Singapore operations run 4 centres with ~1,600 seats at ~86% committed occupancy.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Raised to 33% to 35% (from earlier 28% to 30%).
- **Normalised EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Maintained at 19% to 20%, trending towards the upper end.
- **Operational Space Guidance:** Expects >12.5 million to 13 million sq ft of operational space by March 2027.
- **ROCE Guidance:** Expects ROCE to reach ~28% by FY28.
- Management expects free cash flow to turn positive as the mature base approaches 13 million sq ft.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India leased a record 66.4 million sq ft of office space in the first 9 months of 2026 (up 8% YoY, per CBRE). Flexible space operators took 24% of all office leasing in July-September.
- GCCs leased a record 28 million sq ft in the first 9 months (42% of leasing activity).
- Management states that uncertainty drives flex adoption as companies avoid locking into long leases and large upfront capex.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Energy efficiency and cost excellence initiatives across campuses are adding savings.
- Cost of borrowing remains below 9% following a credit rating upgrade.
- Initial capex per sq ft is around Rs 1,350 to Rs 1,400 plus 4.5% to 5% YoY inflation.
- Annual maintenance capex is estimated at 12% to 15% of the initial gross block (~Rs 95 crore to Rs 100 crore for the year).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested Rs 307 crore in capex in H1.
- Added ~1 million sq ft of operational space in H1; 4.3 million sq ft is under fit-out or awaiting handover.
- Eastside (Pune) and East Bridge (Mumbai) campuses will go live in H2 FY27.
- H2 will see the opening of the largest capacity block in history (~45,000 seats).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Best-in-class debtor days at 7 days.
- Free cash flow for H1 was negative at ~Rs 104 crore due to heavy capex running ahead of cash generation from ramping centres.
- Client lock-ins cover rent commitments to landlords ~2x, with no asset-liability mismatch through FY30.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Blended occupancy may temporarily dip below 80% in H2 due to the massive capacity addition (Eastside and East Bridge) increasing the denominator before ramping up.
- Reported cash conversion will remain below 1x while paying deposits to block new supply, though operational centres remain above 1x.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Karan Khanna (AMBIT Capital)** asked about pre-leasing and demand mix for Eastside and East Bridge. *Management stated ~35% prefill is visible, driven largely by existing enterprise clients expanding (BFSI, manufacturing, logistics, IT/ITES).*
- **Sourabh (JM Financial)** asked about occupancy trajectories and capex per sq ft. *Management reiterated that mature centres trend at ~90% occupancy with a ~18-month payback, and capex per sq ft remains aligned with guidance plus inflation.*
- **Jaykant Beria (IIFL Capital)** queried the 125% surge in other operating revenues. *Management explained it stems from expanding footprints and the scaling of newer verticals like Smartworks Vantage, VAS, and FaaS (Fit-out-as-a-Service).*
- **Devang Patel (Sameeksha Capital)** asked about the breakeven timeline for new large campuses. *Management stated they expect to reach breakeven occupancy within 11 to 12 months.*
- **Aditya Shah (Individual Investor)** asked about Keppel's stake and ROCE peaks. *Management responded that Keppel holds long-term balance sheet capital with no immediate monetisation plans, and ROCE is expected to reach ~28% by FY28 as growth capex matures.*

### One-line takeaway
Smartworks reported robust Q2 FY27 growth with revenue up 39% YoY and EBITDA margins expanding by 340 bps, prompting management to raise its full-year revenue growth guidance to 33%–35% despite an aggressive capacity expansion cycle.
