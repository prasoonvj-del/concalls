# SIS Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong revenue growth, record quarterly revenue in India Security, and clear tailwinds from Labor Code implementation.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 4,604 crore (+29.7% YoY, +2.5% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 207 crore (+36.2% YoY, flat QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 101.7 crore.
*   **India Security Revenue:** Rs 2,004 crore (+37.3% YoY).
*   **Facility Management (FM) Revenue:** Rs 642 crore (+8% YoY).
*   **International Security Revenue:** Rs 1,982 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Consolidated 4.5%, India Security 5.1%, FM 5.5%, International Security 3.5%.
*   **ROCE:** 16.7%.
*   **DSO:** 66 days.

### Highlights
*   **Labor Codes:** Management stated that Labor Code rules were notified on May 8-9, 2026, and implementation is underway. Management expects this to be a significant tailwind, ensuring a level playing field and becoming accretive to EBITDA and PAT over the next 3-4 quarters.
*   **Capital Return:** Board approved a fifth buyback of Rs 106 crore via the open market route (max price Rs 478.50). Cumulative capital returned to shareholders since IPO exceeds Rs 700 crore.
*   **Operational Milestones:** India Security crossed the Rs 2,000 crore quarterly revenue milestone for the first time.
*   **International Performance:** International Security reported its highest-ever quarterly revenue run rate, with 7% growth on a constant currency basis.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management aims to move India Security margins to a 5.5%-6% range and FM margins toward 6%.
*   **Strategic Goal:** Management aims to position SIS as a company delivering 15% annualized growth and a 15%+ return profile.
*   **Labor Code Impact:** Management expects the impact of Labor Code implementation to become more evident in Q2 and Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Labor Codes:** Management described the new definition of "employer" as a "tectonic shift," as it places the onus of compliance on the principal employer (the customer) rather than just the contractor.
*   **Minimum Wage Hikes:** Several states (Haryana, UP, Karnataka, Telangana, Uttarakhand) have implemented minimum wage hikes ranging from 20% to 47%. Management views these as revenue-boosting and EBITDA-accretive due to pass-through contract structures.
*   **Labor Availability:** Management reported no exceptional labor shortages, noting that typical seasonal shortages subside after the monsoon.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Pass-through Mechanism:** Management clarified that minimum wage hikes are passed through to clients via "rise and fall" clauses in contracts. While there is a timing mismatch in invoicing, it is not dependent on contract renewal dates.
*   **International Margins:** The decline in International Security margins from Q4 to Q1 is attributed to historical seasonality, as Q4 typically includes high-margin events.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Acquisitions:** Management noted they hold a 51% stake in a company and are scheduled to acquire the remaining 49% by 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **DSO:** Increased by 3 days QoQ to 66 days, which management stated is expected as Q4 is historically the best quarter for DSO.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **IPO Plans:** The IPO for the Cash business is currently on hold due to market conditions and the de-rating of peer comparables.
*   **Timing Mismatch:** Management acknowledged a timing lag between the effective date of wage hikes and the realization of payments from customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How will Labor Codes impact the competitive landscape?** Management explained that the "principal employer" liability forces customers to use compliant providers, shifting the arbitrage discussion to the customer's doorstep and favoring organized players like SIS.
*   **Q: Are current margins sustainable?** Management stated that while acquired assets have lower margins, there is a clear path to integration and synergy-driven margin expansion over 4-8 quarters.
*   **Q: Is the buyback a tender offer?** Management clarified it is an open market program, making SIS one of the first companies to utilize the reopened window.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Cash business IPO?** Management stated they are waiting for a more opportune time given the current IPO rush and peer de-rating.
*   **Q: Is the Updater Services share purchase a treasury operation?** Management confirmed it is a treasury operation.

### One-line takeaway
SIS delivered a strong Q1 with record revenues and is positioning itself to benefit from the "tectonic shift" of Labor Code implementation, while maintaining a consistent capital return policy.
