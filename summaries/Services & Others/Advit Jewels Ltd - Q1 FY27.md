# Advit Jewels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong YoY growth across all financial metrics with a clear strategic roadmap for B2C expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 35.40 crore (up 37.35% YoY from Rs 25.78 crore).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 11.72 crore (up 33.02% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 33.12%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 8.19 crore (up 37.86% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 23.12%.
*   **EPS:** Rs 2.46.

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Mix:** B2B accounted for 78% of revenue, while B2C contributed 22% in Q1.
*   **New Categories:** Introduced pret jewellery (targeting Gen Z/millennials) and men's jewellery to diversify product offerings.
*   **IPO Proceeds:** Company is now debt-free; remaining funds are earmarked for strategic expansion and working capital.
*   **Artisan Base:** Increased artisan headcount; management is focusing on skill development and welfare for artisans and their families.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects continued growth in financial performance, citing strong demand in the bridal and festive seasons.
*   **Flagship Store:** The 30,000 sq. ft. Jaipur flagship store is expected to be operational by November 2026, despite weather-related construction delays.
*   **International Expansion:** Management plans to explore international markets (US, UK, Middle East) following domestic expansion.
*   **Marketing:** Management intends to onboard a brand ambassador for the launch of the Jaipur flagship store.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Bridal Demand:** Management reported strong demand for bridal jewellery.
*   **Ticket Size:** Despite rising gold prices, customers are maintaining their budgets by opting for lighter-weight gold designs with more stones and Polki.
*   **Consumer Behavior:** Gen Z and millennials are increasingly seeking transparent, designer-led jewellery.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gold Hedging:** Gold is procured against confirmed orders; prices are hedged at the time of order booking if an advance is received.
*   **Inventory Management:** Inventory is rotated across stores to optimize business; designs are reworked or recycled if they do not sell.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Jaipur Flagship:** Estimated investment of ~Rs 25 crore.
*   **Store Strategy:** Company-owned stores planned for metro cities; franchise-led expansion planned for Tier 2 and smaller cities.
*   **Franchise Model:** Franchise partners will fund the inventory (estimated requirement of Rs 6-8 crore per store). Protocols for the franchise model are currently under development.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution:** Management acknowledged the challenge of transitioning to a luxury B2C boutique model, which requires higher investment in interiors and brand positioning.
*   **Gold Prices:** Rising gold prices pose a risk to consumer purchasing power, which the company is mitigating through design innovation.
*   **Construction:** Weather-related delays in Jaipur have impacted the timeline for the flagship store.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **IPO Proceeds:** Management confirmed the company is debt-free and is holding a "chunk" of funds for future expansion and working capital needs.
*   **Franchise Model:** Management clarified that franchise partners are responsible for funding inventory; they are currently finalizing protocols and have not yet invited franchises.
*   **Jaipur Store ROI:** Management stated that the Jaipur flagship will act as a hub for all operations (B2B and B2C), making individual store ROI calculations difficult to isolate.
*   **Gold Hedging:** Management confirmed that procurement is matched against sold stock to ensure margins remain intact.
*   **Inventory Rotation:** Management confirmed that inventory can be rotated across stores to maximize sales.

### One-line takeaway
Advit Jewels delivered strong Q1 growth and is leveraging its debt-free status to pivot toward a B2C luxury retail model while maintaining its core B2B legacy.
