# ACC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reports resilient trade volume growth and successful cost optimization, but acknowledges headwinds from geopolitical conflicts, monsoon-related demand softness, and rising energy costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 cost target of Rs 4,250 per metric tonne.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "shedding low-margin business" (Q1) to "calibrated expansion" and "value-led growth" (Q2). Management now emphasizes that both trade and institutional segments will contribute to positive volume growth in H2.
- **New topics:** Detailed the 30 million tonnes expansion plan (Rs 14,500 crore capex) and the progress of the Orient Cement merger.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on capacity commissioning (4.6 million tonnes commissioned in Q2).

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 9,197 crore
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1,271 crore
*   **EBITDA Margin:** ~14%
*   **EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 770
*   **PAT:** Rs 236 crore
*   **Net Worth:** Rs 72,238 crore
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 1,070 crore
*   **Trade Volume Growth:** 8.6% YoY
*   **Trade Share:** 74% (up 6% YoY)
*   **Premium Product Share:** 37% of trade sales (up 2% YoY)
*   **Blended Cement Share:** 82% (up from 77%)
*   **Green Power Share:** 61%
*   **Net Power Cost:** Rs 4.8 per unit (vs Rs 6 in Q2 FY26; Rs 4.3 in Q1 FY27)
*   **Fuel Cost:** Rs 1.87 per 1,000 kcal (vs Rs 1.60 in Q2 FY26; Rs 1.66 in Q1 FY27)
*   **Total Cost:** Rs 4,410 per tonne (normalized Rs 4,182 per tonne)
*   **RMC Volume:** 1.07 million cubic meters (up 19% YoY)

### Highlights
*   **Expansion:** Announced 14 expansion projects adding ~30 million tonnes of cement and 10.3 million tonnes of clinker capacity.
*   **Merger:** Proposed merger of ACC and Orient Cement with Ambuja proceeding to NCLT; expected completion within FY27.
*   **Capacity Commissioning:** Commissioned 4.6 million tonnes in Q2 (Dahej, Salai Banwa, Kalamboli); 3.2 million tonnes stabilized (Bathinda, Jodhpur); 2.4 million tonnes (Warisaliganj) expected in H2 FY27.
*   **Logistics:** Primary lead distance reduced from 265 km to 254 km.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Management expects higher single-digit growth in H2 FY27, aiming for double-digit growth.
*   **Capacity:** Targeting 149 million tonnes by FY29 and 155 million tonnes by FY30.
*   **Capex:** Rs 6,500–7,000 crore for FY27 (Rs 3,500 crore spent in H1); ~Rs 7,500 crore/year for the next two years.
*   **Cost Target:** Reaffirmed FY27 target of Rs 4,250 per tonne.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management expects industry demand growth of 6-7% for FY27.
*   Structural demand remains supported by infrastructure spending, urbanization, and rural housing.
*   Near-term demand is impacted by monsoon-related headwinds and geopolitical uncertainty.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Fuel Mix:** Higher share of domestic coal used to mitigate high-cost pet coke.
*   **Fly Ash:** 20 million tonnes tied up with group thermal power plants.
*   **Green Power:** Revenue from green power sales was Rs 136 crore (352 million kWh).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Execution:** Management stated 14 months required for Grinding Units (GU) and 18 months for Integrated Units (IU).
*   **Strategy:** Focus on brownfield expansions to reduce construction risk and utilize surplus clinker.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Company remains debt-free with AAA long-term and A1+ short-term ratings.
*   Working capital cycle improved by 10 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical conflicts and global uncertainty.
*   Constraints in domestic coal availability.
*   Monsoon-related headwinds impacting construction cycles in certain states.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Capex Strategy:** Management defended the "calibrated" expansion, noting that the first phase of capex is complete and the new investments are value-accretive, utilizing existing clinker surplus.
*   **On Volume vs. Value:** Management clarified they are not sacrificing volume for value, but prioritizing profitable trade volumes; both trade and institutional segments are expected to show positive growth in H2.
*   **On Fuel Costs:** Management noted current spot fuel costs are ~Rs 2.85 per 1,000 kcal, higher than the Q2 average of Rs 1.87, but expects domestic coal and efficiency measures to mitigate impact.
*   **On Utilization:** Current utilization is ~61-62%; management targets 75% by FY27 end.
*   **On "Mothballed" Plants:** Management confirmed they are re-evaluating inefficient assets and may permanently shut down 2-3 million tonnes of capacity over time.

### One-line takeaway
ACC/Ambuja is aggressively pursuing a 30 million tonne expansion plan while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet, banking on structural cost efficiencies (green power, logistics, clinker factor) to offset inflationary fuel pressures and drive value-led growth.
