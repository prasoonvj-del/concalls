# Ardee Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - ongoing geopolitical conflicts and rising procurement/fuel costs are pressuring margins, though volume growth targets are maintained.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (volume growth target of 10% to 15% maintained for the full year, compared to 10% to 20% in Q1).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious on both top-line and margins to focusing primarily on absolute revenue growth and volumes, as raw material prices remain elevated and sticky.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Evaluated lithium-ion recycling further but noted low machine uptime and tight availability as hurdles; evaluated copper recycling more seriously. Old risks regarding Hormuz/shipping continued.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Naidupeta 6-acre land parcel official handover completed in company's name.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs. 325 crore (YoY growth of 33.5% vs Rs. 243 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 revenue stood at Rs. 664 crore (YoY growth of 34% vs Rs. 494 crore in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 29 crore for Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 EBITDA stood at Rs. 63 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.9% for Q2 FY27.
- **PAT:** Rs. 18 crore for Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 PAT stood at Rs. 38 crore.
- **Sales Volume:** 15,656 metric tons for Q2 FY27 (vs 15,000 metric tons in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 sales volume stood at 33,300 metric tons (vs 30,700 metric tons in H1 FY26).
- **Borrowings:** Rs. 35 crore as of 30th September 2026.
- **Export Contribution:** Approximately 37% of H1 FY27 revenue (Rs. 244 crore).
- **Installed Capacity:** 1,56,950 metric tons per annum.
- **Domestic Sourcing Proportion:** Increased from ~15% last year to 27%-28% currently.

### Highlights
- Enhanced installed manufacturing capacity to 1,56,950 metric tons per annum through debottlenecking, with focus moving toward 70% to 75% optimal utilization.
- Secured official handover of an additional 6-acre land parcel at Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh.
- Brand 'Ardee Lead 9997' empanelled with the London Metal Exchange (LME), alongside MCX listing.
- Exporting to 8 countries, expanding focus into Southeast Asia (Thailand, Malaysia, China).
- Sourcing import geographies expanded from 50-plus to 80-plus countries.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the full-year volume growth target of 10% to 15%.
- Management targets absolute revenue growth over margin expansion in the near term due to high procurement and operating costs.
- Expects margins to remain around current levels for the remainder of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains robust across automotive, renewable energy, telecom, inverters, UPS, and industrial applications.
- Regulatory developments such as EPR collection targets and RCM on battery scrap purchases are expected to formalize the industry over the next 3 to 4 years.
- Sourcing constraint is the primary bottleneck rather than customer demand, as scrap traders hold back inventory when LME prices dip.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin fell to 8.9% in Q2 FY27 due to higher procurement costs of raw materials bought 2-3 months prior, rising freight costs, and increased fuel/OPEX expenses.
- Domestic raw material sourcing has increased to 27%-28% to reduce import uncertainty, though domestic scrap is sometimes more expensive due to unorganized sector competition.
- Oxygen enrichment helps save around 5% of fuel costs.
- Value-added lead alloys constitute around 30% of the product mix, while pure lead makes up around 70%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Debottlenecked capacity provides a buffer to reach optimum utilization levels without heavy capex.
- 6-acre land parcel at Naidupeta acquired for future business growth.
- Evaluating adjacent areas like copper and plastic recycling based on commercial viability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings stood at Rs. 35 crore as of 30th September 2026.
- Working capital utilization is lower due to IPO proceeds deployment.
- Net foreign currency exposure remains between 5% to 10% due to natural hedging between imports and exports.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical conflicts, war, and shipping/freight cost uncertainties leading to high procurement costs.
- High domestic scrap prices and competition from the unorganized sector.
- Sourcing constraints as scrap traders wait for higher LME levels.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Yash Kabra (Nirmal Bang Securities) asked about the sharp fall in absolute EBITDA and margins.** Management explained that high-cost raw materials procured a few months prior, rising fuel/OPEX, and freight costs impacted margins, and advised looking at absolute revenue growth. Management did not dodge.
2. **Shubham Purohit (SBI Securities) asked if the 10-15% volume growth target is maintained despite lagging in H1.** Management confirmed the target is maintained and will be achieved in H2. Management did not dodge.
3. **Nayan (Ertiga) asked about customer mix, repeat business, and time taken for new customer approval.** Management stated top clients have been with them since 2021, repeat business is high, and customer approval takes 1 to 1.5 years. Management did not dodge.
4. **Nirav Shah (GeeCee Holdings) asked about lithium-ion recycling plans following government export prohibitions.** Management stated they are evaluating lithium-ion but see more immediate capital value in copper recycling due to tight raw material availability and low machine uptime in lithium. Management did not dodge.
5. **Pranav Jain (Ageless Capital and Finance) asked about trade receivables increase and job work volumes.** Management explained working capital utilization details and noted job work volumes were very low compared to past levels. Management did not dodge.
6. **Mandira A (Investo) asked about lead recovery per ton and power/fuel/oxygen costs.** Management declined to share specific recovery rates due to confidentiality of battery recipes, but provided details on oxygen enrichment saving ~5% on fuel. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
Ardee Industries reported Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs. 325 crore and EBITDA of Rs. 29 crore with margins moderating to 8.9% due to high procurement costs, while maintaining its 10-15% annual volume growth guidance.
