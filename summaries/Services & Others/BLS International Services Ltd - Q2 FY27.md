# BLS International Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly revenue and strong growth in Digital Services, despite margin compression due to revenue mix shift and geopolitical headwinds in Visa volumes.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 973 crore (+32% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 248 crore (+17% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 25.5% (vs 28.9% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,863 crore (+29% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 500 crore (+20% YoY).
*   **Visa & Consular Revenue:** Rs 510 crore (+11% YoY).
*   **Digital Services Revenue:** Rs 463 crore (+66% YoY).
*   **Net Cash:** Rs 1,556 crore.

### Highlights
*   Achieved record quarterly revenue of Rs 973 crore.
*   Processed 500 million applications globally to date; 11 lakh applications processed in Q2.
*   Visa & Consular segment: EBITDA margin of 41.9%; net revenue per application increased 10% to Rs 3,553.
*   Digital Services segment: GTV of Rs 41,400 crore; 1.88 lakh touch points; loan disbursements over Rs 9,500 crore.
*   Achieved CMMI Maturity Level 5 certification.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets FY30 revenue of Rs 6,300 crore to Rs 6,500 crore.
*   Management aims for 20-25% annual revenue growth and 28-30% EBITDA margin by FY30.
*   Management expects net revenue per application to grow 5-6% annually.
*   Management expects Visa & Consular EBITDA margin to stabilize at 38-40%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Visa volume decline YoY attributed to geopolitical situations in the Middle East.
*   Q2/Q3 typically see seasonal moderation in volumes.
*   Management stated that current operations are not impacted by e-visa models.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Consolidated margin compression (25.5% vs 28.9% YoY) driven by revenue mix shift toward lower-margin Digital Services.
*   Digital Services EBITDA margin: 7.4%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Management stated no further investment in the hotel business until performance is assessed over 1-2 years.
*   Focus remains on integrating Atyati and expanding G2C activities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net cash position: Rs 1,556 crore.
*   ICRA rated the company AA- (long term) and A1+ (short term).
*   Loans in Digital business are not on the company's books; no credit risk exposure.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East impacting visa volumes.
*   Seasonal moderation in Q2/Q3.
*   Margin dilution due to the faster growth of the Digital Services segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Buybacks:** Management stated the proposal is under consideration and will be discussed with the Board.
*   **Visa vs. Digital Growth:** Management expects a 60-65% revenue contribution from Visa and the balance from Digital over time.
*   **Spain/Slovakia/Vietnam issues:** Management clarified there are no issues with these governments.
*   **Hotel Business:** Management confirmed no further investments until performance is assessed.
*   **Margin Expansion:** Management noted scope for margin improvement in the Digital business.

### One-line takeaway
Record revenue growth driven by Digital Services expansion, despite margin compression from revenue mix shift and geopolitical headwinds in Visa volumes.
