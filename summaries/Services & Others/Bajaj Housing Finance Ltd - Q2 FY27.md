# Bajaj Housing Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-24 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong AUM growth and disbursement momentum, with stable asset quality and improved operating efficiency, despite potential margin headwinds from interest rate volatility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained. Management kept the FY27 guidance unchanged despite the repo rate hike, citing too many moving parts.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "stabilized/muted" to a focus on managing potential "attrition pressure" in the prime segment due to interest rate hikes.
- **New topics/risks:** The impact of the recent repo rate hike on acquisition pricing and potential margin compression is now the primary focus.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on Sambhav growth (crossed Rs 500 crore monthly run rate).

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 1.58 lakh crore (25% YoY growth).
- **Disbursements:** Rs 19,900 crore (25% YoY growth).
- **PAT:** Rs 980 crore (18% YoY growth).
- **ROA:** 2.2%; **ROE:** 12.7%.
- **GNPA:** 29 bps (flat QoQ); **NNPA:** 12 bps (flat QoQ).
- **Cost of Funds (COF):** 7% (down 17 bps QoQ).
- **NIM:** 3.7% (down 2 bps QoQ).
- **Opex to NTI:** 19.1% (improved from 19.7% in Q2 FY26).
- **Capital Adequacy:** 21.24%.

### Highlights
- **Milestone:** Crossed Rs 1.5 lakh crore AUM landmark.
- **Sambhav Housing:** Crossed Rs 500 crore monthly disbursement run rate; now constitutes 16% of overall home loan originations.
- **Borrowing Mix:** Shifted toward NCDs (50.8%, up 430 bps QoQ) to optimize costs.
- **Operational Network:** 223 branches across 182 locations.
- **Product Growth:** Home loans (22% YoY), LRD (39% YoY), LAP (26% YoY), Developer Finance (13% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Growth:** Management noted that while current growth is 25%, they are watching for potential attrition in the prime segment due to rate hikes, which could impact future growth.
- **Margins:** Expect 20-25 bps NIM compression for the year; management warned of a potential 1-2 bps additional hit if acquisition pricing does not move in line with repo hikes.
- **Sambhav Target:** Aiming for 20% of home loan mix by year-end; 30% over the next 3-4 years.
- **Cost of Funds:** Management expects ~80% of repo rate hikes to flow through to COF.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Interest Rate Impact:** Management believes housing demand is driven more by expectations of real estate price appreciation than by interest rate cycles.
- **Competitive Intensity:** Banks are increasing MCLR quickly; management is waiting to see how acquisition pricing for new loans evolves in the market.
- **Strategy:** To counter prime segment pricing pressure, the company plans to accelerate non-prime (Sambhav) and LAP segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **COF Optimization:** Reduced by 17 bps QoQ due to conversion of fixed-rate NCDs to floating-rate instruments.
- **Yields:** Portfolio yield moderated by 10 bps QoQ to 8.8%.
- **Spread:** Gross spreads improved by 7 bps QoQ to 1.8%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Manpower:** Salary expenses increased 19% YoY due to heavy investment in Sambhav and non-top-six market structures; management expects this to remain elevated for the next 1.5–2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Assignment:** Assigned ~Rs 1,428 crore of assets in Q2 (vs Rs 2,600 crore in Q1).
- **PBC:** Principal Business Criteria stood at 60.49% (regulatory requirement is 60%).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Margin Compression:** Potential for compression if acquisition pricing (new loans) does not rise in line with repo rate hikes.
- **Attrition:** Risk of higher BT-out (balance transfer) pressure in the prime housing and LRD segments if market pricing remains competitive.
- **Regulatory:** Potential impact of draft insurance commission guidelines on PBT (estimated 6-7% impact if implemented).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On Developer Finance (DF) decline:** Management clarified the decline was due to higher-than-expected repayments and slower disbursements, not a strategic pullback; they remain committed to growing this to 15-17% of the portfolio.
- **On Interest Rate Transmission:** Management confirmed they have already hiked PLR by 15 bps (effective 16th Oct) and expect portfolio yields to move in line with COF.
- **On Insurance Commission Draft:** Management noted that while some offset is possible via higher processing fees, a 6-7% impact on PBT is likely if the draft guidelines are implemented.
- **On Assignment Income:** Management explained that income on derecognized loans fluctuates based on the quantum and type of assets assigned (LRD vs. LAP).

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Housing Finance delivered a robust quarter with strong AUM growth, but management is bracing for potential margin and growth headwinds as they navigate the impact of repo rate hikes on competitive pricing and customer attrition.
