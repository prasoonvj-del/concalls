# NIS Management Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Consolidated revenue grew 16.1% YoY, but EBITDA and margins contracted due to lower-margin contract renewals, ITI revenue shrinkage, and start-up losses in the DDU-GKY skill development project.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Revenue target of over Rs 500 crore for FY27 maintained; EBITDA margin guidance adjusted to a conservative 7% to 7.5% for FY27 due to margin pressures.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from strongly positive to cautious regarding margins, as execution and invoicing delays in high-margin CCTV and skill development segments pushed realizations to H2.
- **New topics or risks:** Detailed discussion on RA bills, a Rs 12 crore collection delay in August-September, and labor law changes involving ESIC coverage above Rs 21,000 impacting contract renewals. ITI business contraction to Rs 30 lakhs highlighted.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Expected CCTV revenue scale-up and DDU-GKY invoicing by Q2/Q3 were delayed; execution is now slated to start contributing in H2 / Q4.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 133.62 crore (up 16.1% YoY).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 7 crore; **EBITDA Margin:** 5.24%.
- **Net Profit / PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 3.99 crore; **Net Profit Margin:** 2.98%.
- **Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 249.06 crore (up 15.9% YoY).
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 16.22 crore; **EBITDA Margin:** 6.51%.
- **Net Profit / PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 10.39 crore; **Net Profit Margin:** 4.17%.
- **New Work Orders Secured (Q2 FY27):** Rs 55.55 crore. Total since IPO: ~Rs 120 crore.
- **Workforce:** >19,000 personnel across 14 states, covering ~1,500 sites.

### Highlights
- Secured Rs 55.55 crore in new work orders during Q2 FY27 from government departments and institutions (including WBSEDCL, ORDMS Odisha, Patna Building Construction Dept, and Government of West Bengal).
- Post-quarter, subsidiary NIS Facility Management secured Rs 6.24 crore from CRM Enterprise Business and two orders from West Bengal Police Housing (Rs 53.15 lakhs and Rs 33.31 lakhs).
- Received Rhodium status under State Bank of India's SME Star Rating Program in August 2026.
- ICRA upgraded long-term credit rating to A- stable (from BBB+ positive) and short-term to A2+ (from A2).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets consolidated revenue of over Rs 500 crore in FY27.
- Management projects a conservative EBITDA margin of 7% to 7.5% for FY27, dependent on H2 conversion of high-margin CCTV tenders and DDU-GKY invoicing.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Encouraging demand for organized facility management, security services, and skill development from corporate, institutional, and government segments.
- Growing customer interest in hybrid security models integrating access control, CCTV, intrusion detection, and central command room monitoring.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin declined to 5.24% in Q2 due to lower-margin contract renewals driven by new ESIC coverage rules (>Rs 21,000), negative EBITDA in Keertika (DDU-GKY execution costs), shrinking ITI revenue (down to Rs 30 lakhs), and lower-margin FMS manpower contracts.
- New contracts typically require 5 to 8 months to settle operational expenses and stabilize margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Scaling training capacity for DDU-GKY projects, targeting training for >3,000 youth over the next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables expanded due to a Rs 12 crore collection delay in August and September; collections were sorted out before Durga Puja.
- Management projects trade receivables to reduce by Rs 10-12 crore by March and expects positive operating cash flow by year-end, aided by a TDS refund of Rs 6.5-7 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution and invoicing delays in government contracts and skill development (DDU-GKY) projects.
- Lower renewal pricing rates and margin contraction in manpower contracts due to competitive pressures and regulatory changes.
- Working capital blockage due to bank guarantees and 3-month receivable cycles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya Verma asked about revenue drivers and EBITDA margin drop.** Management cited new contract growth offset by lower-margin renewals, ESIC rule changes, ITI revenue shrinkage, and Keertika startup losses.
- **Darshan Chandra questioned the feasibility of FY27 EBITDA guidance after the Q2 drop.** Management stated a conservative 7% EBITDA is expected for FY27, contingent on the conversion and execution of high-margin CCTV tenders in H2.
- **Nitin Shah asked about the execution timeline and margin of the Rs 6.24 crore CCTV order.** Management stated implementation will complete by mid-January due to clearances, with a budgeted contribution margin of 18%-19%.
- **Vikash Sharma asked about the Odisha DDU-GKY training order execution.** Management explained that billing occurs post-training and placement over a 9-month period in tranches, with Q4 invoicing expected.
- **Rohit Parmanandka asked about cash flow improvement steps.** Management cited resolved August-September collection delays and projected a Rs 10-12 crore reduction in receivables by March.
- **Tejal asked about contract profitability thresholds and mobilization.** Management explained that sites take 6-8 months to stabilize operationally, while government orders face a 2-3 month paperwork lag before invoicing begins.

### One-line takeaway
NIS Management delivered 16.1% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, but profitability contracted due to lower-margin contract renewals, ITI shrinkage, and startup costs in skill development.
