# Grasim Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Record consolidated revenue of Rs 50,105 crore driven by core and growth engines, but reported net profit was hit by a Rs 767 crore exceptional charge for idling a Canadian pulp mill alongside Q2 seasonal paints softness and elevated input costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 standalone capex at Rs 3,157 crore, paints full-year YoY revenue growth at 50%, Birla Opus H2 double-digit revenue market share target, and Birla Pivot H2 EBITDA breakeven (with FY27 revenue run rate target raised to cross Rs 12,000 crore vs >Rs 10,000 crore run rate in Q1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone shifted from positive to mixed. Management noted seasonal Q2 softness and heavy trade scheme discounting in paints, along with sequential raw material cost pressures in VSF (sulfur/pulp) and caustic price pressure.
- **New topics or risks:** Idling of AV Group NB’s Nackawic dissolving grade pulp mill in Canada resulting in an exceptional charge of Rs 825 crore (standalone) / Rs 767 crore (consolidated); one-time ESOP/employee expense of Rs 10 crore in Birla Pivot; reduction of price gap vs paints market leader down to 2–4% (excluding 10% free volume offer).
- **Last quarter promises delivered:** Retail dealer network for Birla Opus reached 61,000+ (exceeding the 60,000 target); CPVC (50k TPA in Aug 2026) and Epichlorohydrin (50k TPA in Oct 2026) commissioned at Vilayat; CCI approval received for the Sprng Energy acquisition.

---

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 50,105 crore, up 26% YoY (TTM consolidated revenue reached Rs 194,234 crore, up 7% vs FY26).
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 12,747 crore, up 33% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 4,615 crore, up 2.6x YoY (boosted by UltraTech dividend of ~Rs 4,000 crore).
- **Standalone Cash Profit:** Rs 3,968 crore, up 2.5x YoY.
- **Adjusted Consolidated PAT:** Rs 536 crore (down 10% YoY, excluding exceptional charge of Rs 767 crore; reported PBT grew 34% YoY).
- **Adjusted Standalone PAT:** Rs 3,132 crore (excluding exceptional charge of Rs 825 crore).
- **Birla Opus Paints Revenue:** Rs 1,337 crore (+35% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue stood at ~Rs 3,000 crore (+50% YoY). Q2 volume up 20% YoY, H1 volume up 33% YoY.
- **Birla Pivot Revenue:** Rs 2,785 crore (+66% YoY), annualized run rate crossed Rs 11,000 crore.
- **Cellulosic Fibres Revenue:** Rs 5,344 crore, up 29% YoY.
- **Chemicals Revenue:** Rs 2,759 crore, up 15% YoY.
- **UltraTech Cement:** Sales volume 38.2 million tonnes (+13% YoY); EBITDA up 17% YoY.
- **Aditya Birla Capital:** Consolidated revenue Rs 13,990 crore, up 32% YoY.
- **Other Businesses (Textiles, Insulators, Renewables):** Revenue grew 16% YoY; EBITDA grew 14% YoY. Renewable capacity added 269 MW YoY to 2.2 GWp.

---

### Highlights
- Standalone business now contributes 24% of overall consolidated revenue earnings.
- Consolidated revenue achieved its highest-ever quarterly level of Rs 50,105 crore.
- UltraTech cash dividend of ~Rs 4,000 crore received in standalone, eliminating at the consolidated level.
- Exceptional charge of Rs 825 crore (standalone) / Rs 767 crore (consolidated) recorded due to Board decision to idle the AV Group NB Nackawic pulp mill in Canada.
- *Pattern Continuity:* Standalone cash generation remains anchored by UltraTech dividend inflows while organic growth engines scale up.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- **Paints (Birla Opus):** Maintained guidance of 50% YoY revenue growth for full-year FY27; aiming for double-digit revenue market share in H2 FY27 (Birla Opus standalone) and Rs 10,000 crore revenue by exit FY28 with profitability at scale.
- **Birla Pivot:** Target to cross Rs 12,000 crore revenue in FY27 with EBITDA breakeven on track for H2 FY27.
- **Renewables:** Aiming for 20 GW capacity post-consummation of the Sprng Energy acquisition (current operational + under-construction target is 10 GW).
- **Cement:** UltraTech on track to reach 242.9 MTPA capacity by exit March 2028.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- IMF revised India's FY27 GDP growth forecast upward to 7%; RBI raised repo rate by 25 bps to 5.5% in October.
- Global CSF prices rose for 4 consecutive quarters, averaging USD 1.85/kg in Q2 (+23% YoY); China CSF operating rate stood at 93% with inventory at 6 days.
- Paints industry H1 grew 16–17% YoY (8–9% price realization, 7–8% volume), with organizing of the 25% unorganized market driving growth. H2 industry growth expected at 15–20% YoY. October demand accelerated post-monsoon.
- Caustic soda pricing hit 5-year lows globally (CFR SEA USD 390/ton) due to Middle East alumina refinery outages and increased Chinese output, though domestic Indian prices were cushioned by rupee depreciation.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- VSF Q2 margins contracted QoQ due to elevated sulfur and pulp costs; sulfur prices began moderating from October.
- Crude oil volatility (toggling between USD 90–110) kept paint raw material prices elevated; Birla Opus narrowed its price gap vs the market leader to 2–4% by September (excluding 10% extra volume offer).
- B2B e-commerce EBITDA was impacted by a one-time employee/ESOP expense of ~Rs 10 crore, while its retail segment achieved EBITDA breakeven.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Standalone Capex Guidance:** Retained at Rs 3,157 crore for FY27; Rs 1,022 crore spent in H1 FY27. Cumulative paints capex spend remains near the planned Rs 10,000 crore mark.
- **Cellulosic Fibres Specialty Capacity:** Expanding 165,000 TPA in 2 phases: Phase I (55,000 TPA) procurement nearly complete; Phase II (110,000 TPA) environmental clearance in advanced stages.
- **Chemicals Integration:** Commissioned 50,000 TPA CPVC in August 2026 and 50,000 TPA Epichlorohydrin (ECH) in October 2026 at Vilayat, raising expected chlorine integration to 67%. Auxiliary boiler commissioned at Vilayat power plant to eliminate supply disruptions.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Grasim will infuse Rs 625 crore (its 45% share) into AV Group NB to pay off liabilities and idle the Nackawic mill, expected to yield a payback within 4 years by stopping recurring cash losses.
- Alternative pulp sourcing secured to cover the 20% dissolved grade pulp requirement previously sourced from Nackawic.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing cash losses and restructuring liabilities at AV Group NB Canadian pulp operations.
- Heavy trade scheme discounting across decorative paints during Q2 seasonal slowdowns.
- Vulnerability of epoxy value chain margins to sluggish demand alongside high BPA/ECH feedstock costs.
- Sustained soft global caustic soda pricing due to geopolitical tensions and Middle East refinery disruptions.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rahul Gupta (Morgan Stanley):** *Are paints revenue growth targets accelerating in H2?*  
  *Management Answer:* Confirmed guidance of 50% YoY full-year revenue growth. October momentum accelerates H2, with H2 historically contributing 57-58% of annual paints revenue.
- **Sanjeev Singh (Motilal Oswal):** *What is the profitability outlook for VSF post Q2 margin compression?*  
  *Management Answer:* Q2 margin drop was driven by sulfur and pulp cost spikes. Sulfur prices began moderating in October; Q3 margins expected to be range-bound with Q2.
- **Jayant Dhobley on Chemical Margins (Nirav Jimudia, Motilal Oswal):** *How are chlorine derivatives and caustic performing?*  
  *Management Answer:* Domestic chlorine prices (especially in Gujarat) turned neutral-to-positive due to local supply-demand, but caustic remains under pressure due to Middle East alumina refinery outages and Chinese output.
- **Raashi:** *Why did Q2 paints revenue decline 20% sequentially?*  
  *Management Answer:* Realization was flat QoQ despite dealer list price hikes due to industry-wide trade schemes. Birla Opus relies on consumer retail sell-through rather than wholesale dealer stocking.
- **Navin Sahadeo:** *Why infuse an additional Rs 625 crore into AV Nackawic?*  
  *Management Answer:* Grasim holds a 45% stake; the cash infusion settles plant liabilities and borrowing to permanently idle operations from October 2026, stopping cash drain with a 4-year investment payback.

---

### One-line takeaway
Grasim maintained robust top-line momentum with standalone revenue surging 33% YoY, absorbing a temporary pulp plant idling charge while positioning Birla Opus and Birla Pivot for H2 profitability and scale.
