# Gamco Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue and profit growth, return to profitability in Q2, successful asset monetization, and an expanding pipeline across verticals.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 254 crore (up 79% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 140.6 crore (up 61.7% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 52.9 crore (margin of 21%)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 12 crore (margin of 9%, compared to EBITDA loss of Rs 7 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (H1 FY27):** Rs 38.5 crore (nearly 6x of Rs 6.5 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27):** Rs 6 crore (compared to loss of Rs 8 crore YoY and Rs 32 crore in Q1)
- **Net Worth:** Rs 206.6 crore (up 24% in 6 months from Rs 167 crore in March 2026)
- **Total Assets:** Rs 557 crore
- **Borrowings / Debt:** Rs 327 crore
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** Improved to 1.6x from 1.7x
- **Quoted Equity Treasury:** Around Rs 230 crore
- **Visco Advisory Asset Sale:** Rs 15.8 crore (158x return over 3 years)

### Highlights
- Company hosted its first-ever earnings conference call.
- Consolidated revenue for H1 reached about 84% of the entire FY26 revenue.
- Sold warehousing asset Visco Advisory to a global institutional fund for Rs 15.8 crore.
- Acquired a 33.33% stake in Blissara Resorts for a luxury hospitality project in Port Blair, Andaman.
- Promoters increased holding to 72% over recent years.
- Merged subsidiary Complify Trade into GAMCO.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects residential revenue from Uma Properties (Alipore) to start hitting books by March/April 2028.
- Warehousing sector expected to generate revenues starting from calendar year 2028.
- Blissara Resorts (Port Blair) targeted to start operations and see revenues by 2030–2031.
- Management aims to maintain a 25% overall portfolio in liquid treasury like equity markets.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Zero supply of Grade A warehouses currently in Calcutta, with massive demand.
- Strong corporate and leisure travel demand in locations like Port Blair, Lucknow, Himachal, and Ayodhya, alongside constrained supply.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Total expenses for Q2 FY27 were Rs 129 crore (compared to Rs 94 crore YoY and Rs 73 crore in Q1).
- Finance cost for Q2 FY27 was Rs 6 crore (reflecting borrowings for real estate, warehousing, and hospitality expansion).
- International hotel operator base fee expected around 1.5% to 2.5% on gross revenue; total operator payouts under 8.5%.
- Target rental yields for warehousing projects at 8% to 10%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Hospitality:** 11-acre sea-facing cliff property at Kodiyaghat, Port Blair, planning a 140-key upper upscale hotel designed by Alexis Dornier.
- **Warehousing:** Acquiring land parcels in Dankuni, Delhi Road with 40 lakh square feet development potential and a capex of around Rs 600 crore.
- **Real Estate (Kolkata):** 13.5 lakh square feet of proposed residential development launching over the next 1-2 years across Alipore and Jessore Road. Alipore project expected to generate Rs 250 crore revenue; Jessore Road project (via JV Elika Realestate) expected to generate Rs 1,100 crore revenue over 5 years.
- **Lucknow Township (OneWorld):** 9.7% stake in a 212-acre township (1,206 units completed, 1,750 under development); investment revalued at Rs 153 crore (unrealized gain of Rs 134 crore).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings stood at Rs 327 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Other financial liabilities reduced to Rs 3 crore.
- Net worth grew 24% in 6 months to Rs 206.6 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Construction challenges historically include monsoon seasons.
- Gestation periods and risk appetite required for long-term real asset development.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya Gupta (AJ Capital) asked about the long-term value creation across verticals and timelines:** Management highlighted excitement for the hospitality pipeline (Port Blair) and Tier-2/Tier-3 warehousing expansion (Calcutta, Guwahati, Patna, Jaipur).
- **Suvankar asked about the financial model, payback period, and revenue recognition for the hospitality operator:** Management stated the payback period is expected in 5.5 to 7 years, with international operators charging a 1.5% to 2.5% base fee plus GOP percentage bonuses.
- **Ranbeer Singh inquired about funding the Rs 900–1,500 crore capex plan over 3 to 5 years:** Management responded that funding will come from the liquid equity treasury (maintaining ~25% in liquid equity), market borrowings/construction funding, and internal accruals.
- **Dhruv Dubey asked why GAMCO chose to enter hospitality as a landowner rather than an operator:** Management explained they lack direct operational capability and prefer partnering with international experts for day-to-day operations while capturing higher value-creation upsides from development-stage land ownership.

### One-line takeaway
GAMCO delivered strong H1 FY27 financial growth and profitability, strategically scaling its asset-backed verticals in real estate, warehousing, and hospitality while leveraging its liquid equity treasury.
