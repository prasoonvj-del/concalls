# Birlasoft Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported sequential revenue growth, improved operational metrics (DSO, cash), and expressed confidence in H2 performance despite a soft Q3 furlough impact.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained the stance that H2 FY27 financial performance will be better than H1 FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious/mixed" to more confident; management noted that the "exit from low-margin/negative accounts" is now "done and dusted."
- **New topics/risks:** E&U vertical softness due to a specific European client ramp-down (not expected to bounce back in the short term).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Wage hikes (effective July 2026) were implemented as planned; management successfully absorbed the impact within the 15% EBITDA margin range.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** $148.3 million (up 2.1% QoQ); INR 1,416.2 crore (up 2.7% QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** INR 215.3 crore ($22.5 million).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 15.2%.
- **PAT:** INR 159.6 crore ($16.7 million).
- **TCV Signings:** $116.1 million (up 9% YoY); H1 TCV at $285 million (up 15% YoY).
- **DSO:** 51 days (2-year best).
- **Cash & Equivalents:** $300.4 million.
- **Effective Tax Rate (ETR):** 29%.
- **Interim Dividend:** INR 3 per share.

### Highlights
- **Vertical Growth:** Manufacturing (+5.2% QoQ), BFSI (+3.6% QoQ), and Life Sciences (+2.1% QoQ) drove growth.
- **AI Progress:** Earned AWS AI Services Competency status; became an OpenAI select partner. ~33% of code is now generated using Agentic AI tools (up from ~25% a few months ago).
- **Client Rationalization:** Management confirmed the exit from non-profitable accounts is complete.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Utilization is at a multi-quarter high; management considers this "best-in-class" and expects it to remain steady.

### Guidance & outlook
- **H2 Outlook:** Management expects H2 FY27 financial performance to be better than H1 FY27.
- **Margins:** Endeavor is to maintain EBITDA margins at or around the 15% level, despite ongoing investments in sales and capabilities.
- **Q3/Q4:** Q3 is expected to be soft due to seasonal furloughs; management expects Q4 to be the strongest quarter of the year, provided no major shocks occur.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Demand Environment:** Remains unchanged/steady; not getting worse or better.
- **E&U Vertical:** Experienced softness due to a specific European client ramp-down; no short-term bounce back expected.
- **Pricing:** Competitive pressure exists, particularly in competitive deal bidding, but management is confident in maintaining margins through AI-led productivity gains.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Wage Hikes:** Impacted margins by 170 bps QoQ.
- **Other Expenses:** Increased due to higher use of complementary workforce (niche skills) for specific client projects.
- **Depreciation/Amortization:** Increased due to reinvestment in physical and tech infrastructure.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Sales Force:** Management continues to invest in sales and service line leadership; hiring for these roles is "almost done."
- **Infrastructure:** Ongoing investment in tech infrastructure and office refurbishment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash:** Strong cash position of $300.4 million; Board is "seized" of the matter regarding capital allocation (no new updates).
- **Collections:** Strong collection performance ($164 million) led to a 51-day DSO.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Order Book:** Management expressed dissatisfaction with Q2 deal signings, noting some deals were pushed to Q3/Q4.
- **Execution:** Management emphasized that while the company has turned a corner, "a lot more work" is needed to secure future growth (FY28 and beyond).
- **Seasonality:** Q3 furlough impact is expected to be in line with last year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On Deal Pushes:** Management clarified that deal signings were soft because some deals were pushed to Q3/Q4, not lost.
- **On AI Margin Impact:** Management noted that while AI-led engagements are competitive, the productivity gains (machines writing code) are helping offset margin pressure.
- **On Sales Hiring:** Management confirmed sales headcount has increased, while support staff has been rationalized.
- **On E&U Turnaround:** Management clarified that the E&U ramp-down is a one-time client-specific issue and does not reflect a structural change in the vertical.
- **On Cash Deployment:** Management stated there is "no new news" regarding the surplus cash; the Board is evaluating options.

### One-line takeaway
Birlasoft delivered sequential growth and improved operational metrics, successfully absorbing wage hikes while pivoting toward AI-led transformation, though management remains focused on accelerating deal signings to secure long-term growth.
