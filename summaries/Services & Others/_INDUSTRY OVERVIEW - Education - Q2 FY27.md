# Services & Others - Education: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Education and Services sector reflects a bifurcated operating environment. While companies exposed to institutional sales, school infrastructure, and specialized professional training (such as Robokidz Eduventures, Elevate Campuses, and Veranda Learning) reported robust revenue expansion and margin improvements, players reliant on consumer test-prep and overseas student mobility faced notable headwinds. Test-prep segments encountered flattening trajectories and specific exam-related delays (particularly in NEET and government vacancy notifications), while overseas education placement felt the transitory pinch of geopolitical disruptions in West Asia and currency volatility. Across the board, managements leaned heavily on cost optimization, tech-driven efficiencies (AI-led content and code generation), and balance sheet restructuring (notably demergers and capital reductions) to protect profitability.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus Views:** Managements broadly agree that H2 FY27 will significantly outperform H1, driven by delayed admission cycles, peak seasonal intakes, and the execution of confirmed institutional order books. 
* **Divergence & Segment Pressures:** 
  * *Overseas Education:* Crizac reported severe Q2 impacts from student deferrals due to West Asia tensions and currency shifts, though pipeline visibility for H2 intake remains high.
  * *Test-Prep & NEET:* Physicswallah and CL Educate highlighted flattening test-prep trajectories and direct headwinds from NEET exam disruptions and lower government exam notifications. Conversely, Physicswallah noted strong non-JEE/NEET growth (CA, CLAT), while CL Educate pointed to future opportunities in digital assessments as NEET transitions to a Computer Based Test (CBT) model.
  * *Enterprise & Institutional:* NIIT suffered from consumption compression and spending pullbacks from its top GSI clients, whereas Robokidz Eduventures saw stellar demand driven by government budget cycles and CBSE skill lab mandates.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Cost Optimization & AI:** Almost all players leveraged internal cost rationalization. NIIT and CL Educate cut overheads and utilized AI to drive backend and content-creation efficiencies. Physicswallah noted that AI now writes 90% of its code, helping maintain margin targets despite growth investments.
* **Input Costs & Inflation:** Navneet Education faced margin compression in domestic stationery due to one-time GST reversals, aggressive branding, and paper price increases (~INR 2,000 per tonne), alongside export pricing pressure. Conversely, infrastructure and housing players like Elevate Campuses and Veranda Learning benefited from reduced cost of debt (Elevate down to ~8.9%) and strict operational leverage, allowing stable EBITDA margins (Elevate at 69% in Q2, Veranda at 25%).

## Capex & capacity
* **Expansion Strategies:** 
  * *Infrastructure & Accommodation:* Elevate Campuses expanded aggressively post-IPO, adding beds via acquisitions (O.P. Jindal Global University) and PPP greenfield developments (IIT Madras). Veranda Learning pursued a low-cost franchise/rental model for Karnataka government test-prep centers (INR 30–35 lakh per center).
  * *Digital & Tech Capex:* Physicswallah maintained a calibrated offline center expansion (25–30 centers annually) while investing in its AI tutor and digital university initiatives. Crizac committed USD 2.5 million toward its AI matching platform, "EduMentor".
  * *Institutional Kits:* Robokidz Eduventures scaled its Young Engineers Academy (YEA) network and school laboratory setups ahead of impending NEP and CBSE deadlines.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Veranda Learning:** Successfully completed the NCLT-approved demerger of its commerce business (J.K. Shah) effective September 1, 2026, narrowing its continuing operations guidance to INR 175 crore revenue and INR 46 crore EBITDA.
* **Navneet Education:** Received INR 330 crore from the K12 Techno partial stake sale, with management evaluating special dividends, buybacks, or inorganic growth options.
* **CL Educate:** Completed the NCLT approval and redemption of ~Rs 203 crore in preference shares to NSE Investments, trimming its balance sheet and moving its registered office to Mumbai.
* **Elevate Campuses:** Executed its first earnings call post-September 2026 listing, fully deploying its INR 750 crore IPO proceeds toward debt reduction.

## What to watch next quarter
* Execution of H2 order books and pipeline recoveries (e.g., Crizac's Q3 revenue growth recovery of 10%–12%, Robokidz's execution of its Rs 75 crore order book).
* Progress on structural mergers and corporate actions (CL Educate's NCLT merger filing for DEXIT, Navneet's merger of Indiannica, and the public listing of Veranda's demerged commerce business).
* Paper price trends and raw material volatility impacting Navneet Education and stationary margins.
* GSI spending stabilization and AI monetization milestones for NIIT.

## Company one-liners
* **CL Educate Ltd:** Delivered expanding EBITDA margins (18.6%) and returned to positive PAT growth in H1 despite a revenue dip, aided by aggressive overhead optimization and DEXIT contract wins.
* **Crizac Ltd:** Q2 performance was hit hard by external student deferrals, but market share gains and strong intake visibility for H2 allowed management to maintain full-year flat revenue/PAT guidance.
* **Elevate Campuses Ltd:** Delivered robust H1 FY27 revenue growth of 29% post-IPO, backed by strong execution on its expansion pipeline, reduced cost of debt, and strong contractual cash flows.
* **NIIT Ltd:** Q2 FY27 revenue declined 6% YoY due to GSI spending pullbacks, but aggressive cost optimization enabled an EBITDA turnaround, while AI and BIPS segments showed strong growth.
* **Navneet Education Ltd:** Reported robust Q2 standalone revenue growth of ~31%–32% and a return to net profit, driven by publication curriculum cycles and domestic stationery expansion.
* **Physicswallah Ltd:** Strong core performance and margin expansion driven by online growth and AI efficiencies successfully offset NEET-related headwinds, with management maintaining full-year guidance.
* **Robokidz Eduventures Ltd:** Delivered stellar H1 FY27 growth backed by strong institutional order execution, with management confident in sustaining 30%–35% annual growth.
* **Veranda Learning Solutions Ltd:** Reported Q2 FY27 continuing revenue of INR 51 crore (up 17% YoY) and PAT of INR 13.4 crore, while successfully completing the demerger of its commerce business.

*Based on: CL Educate Ltd, Crizac Ltd, Elevate Campuses Ltd, NIIT Ltd, Navneet Education Ltd, Physicswallah Ltd, Robokidz Eduventures Ltd, Veranda Learning Solutions Ltd*
