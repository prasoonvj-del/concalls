# Aequs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong aerospace performance and order book growth offset by a recalibration of the consumer segment’s path to profitability due to slower-than-expected volume ramps.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management withdrew the Q4 FY27 EBITDA breakeven target for the consumer segment, citing a need to recalibrate based on slower learning curves and supply chain constraints.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "on track for Q4 breakeven" to "recalibrating path." Aerospace remains strong and on track.
- **New topics:** Promoter group committed INR 6,500 million investment via preferential warrants (50% upfront) to support growth.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The Q4 FY27 consumer breakeven target was not met/is being pushed back.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 4,091 million (up 45% YoY, 3% QoQ).
- **H1 Revenue:** INR 8,046 million (up 50% YoY).
- **Aerospace Revenue:** INR 3,514 million (up 44% YoY, 9% QoQ).
- **Consumer Revenue:** INR 577 million (up 52% YoY, down from INR 734 million in Q1).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 245 million (6% margin).
- **Consumer EBITDA Loss:** INR 401 million (widened from INR 361 million in Q1).
- **Aerospace Order Book:** USD 1.1 billion (up 9% QoQ).
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.5x (vs 0.2x at March end).
- **H1 Capex:** INR 1,597 million.

### Highlights
- **Aerospace:** Added 238 parts (total 5,978); capacity increased to 229 CNC machines.
- **Hosur Expansion:** Groundbreaking completed for the new 250-acre SEZ/DTA site; commercial production targeted for FY29.
- **Safran Partnership:** Signed a new long-term agreement with 6 Safran entities, including landing gear contracts for the Hosur ecosystem.
- **Management Change:** Reenah Joseph joined as CFO.
- **Restructuring:** Amalgamation of three wholly-owned subsidiaries into Aequs Limited was not approved under the fast-track route; management is evaluating next steps.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Aerospace:** Remains on track; management maintains the 4-6x growth target by 2031.
- **Consumer:** Management is recalibrating the path to EBITDA breakeven; no new date provided.
- **Capex:** Total FY27 capex will be lower than the initial INR 660 crore guidance; focus shifting toward aerospace.
- **Fundraising:** Promoter group committed INR 6,500 million via preferential warrants to support Hosur development, capacity expansion, and working capital.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Aerospace:** Demand remains healthy; titanium content in products is increasing.
- **Consumer:** Volume ramp-up in toys (Mattel) and electronics was hindered by supply chain constraints, weather-related logistics disruptions, and longer-than-expected product qualification cycles.
- **Inventory:** Working capital remains elevated due to aerospace growth; normalization expected by Q1 of next year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Aerospace EBITDA Margin:** ~22%.
- **Consumer Loss Drivers:** Operating costs of new capacity are now expensed (previously pre-operative), plus costs for new product variants and higher headcount.
- **Finance Costs:** Increased due to forex impact on borrowings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Hosur:** Phase 1 investment begins Q4 FY27; will focus on engine and landing gear components (forging, machining, heat/surface treatment).
- **Allocation:** Of the new funding, ~INR 289 crore for capex (aero/consumer), ~INR 100 crore for working capital, and ~INR 100 crore for JV/strategic initiatives.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash Balance:** INR 1,710 million.
- **Borrowings:** Increased by INR 1,576 million in H1.
- **Working Capital:** Net working capital days improved to 136 days (from 151 days at March end).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Consumer Profitability:** Slower-than-anticipated learning curve and volume ramp-up in consumer electronics and toys.
- **Execution:** Dependence on customer-specific qualification cycles and supply chain stability.
- **Capital Intensity:** Significant ongoing capital requirements for both aerospace and consumer segments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why did consumer EBITDA loss widen?** A: Management explained that revenue fell sequentially (INR 734m to INR 577m) while fixed costs (depreciation, overheads) remained in place.
- **Q: Is the consumer breakeven delay structural?** A: Management stated it is a timing/learning curve issue, not a structural failure; they are recalibrating scenarios.
- **Q: Will you still raise external equity in FY28?** A: Management indicated that given the 4-6x growth target by 2031, additional capital will likely be required in FY28 despite the current promoter infusion.
- **Q: What is the bottleneck in consumer electronics?** A: Product updates require annual qualification cycles; management underestimated the time required for these cycles and in-house capability building.
- **Q: Are you getting into 3D printing/additive manufacturing?** A: Management explicitly stated they are not focused on adding 3D printing capabilities.

### One-line takeaway
Aequs continues to scale its high-growth aerospace business, but the consumer segment is facing significant headwinds, forcing management to pause its breakeven timeline and rely on a large promoter-led capital infusion to fund ongoing expansion.
