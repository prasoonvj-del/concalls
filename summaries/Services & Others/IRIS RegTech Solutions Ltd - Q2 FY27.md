# IRIS RegTech Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited sustained revenue growth over 30% YoY in both Q1 and Q2, driven by strong enterprise SaaS traction (IRIS CARBON) and a large enterprise deal.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth: Over 30% YoY in both Q1 and Q2 FY27.
- IRIS CARBON ARR: Jumped 52% from March 31, 2026.
- Enterprise clients on IRIS CARBON: More than 50.
- Sales and marketing spend: Little less than $1 per net ARR earned in the first six months of FY27 (excluding the large enterprise deal ARR).
- RegTech segmental revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 158 million.
- RegTech segmental margins (H1 FY27): Rs 35.5 million.

### Highlights
- Sustained growth momentum over the past six months despite increasing global economic uncertainty.
- Secured a large enterprise deal that contributed to a sharp uptick in IRIS CARBON ARR.
- Maintained a comfortable cash position while planning higher outlays on SaaS sales and marketing expenses.
- Continued offering end-to-end features for regulatory customers, including Risk-based supervision and licensing modules.
- *Record Check:* The call's discussion on expanding product suites and enterprise SaaS focus is consistent with the November 2026 investor deck, but management noted that Middle East instability has impacted new order intake in the SupTech segment.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 revenues and profitability to be better than H1 FY27, aided by higher ARR in the RegTech business as Q3 unfolds.
- Re-stated target to grow SaaS revenues at about 35% on an annual basis in the foreseeable future (which has been surpassed halfway through the year).
- Aim to keep the sales and marketing spend ratio around $1 to $1.4 per $1 of net ARR earned.

### Demand & industry commentary
- IRIS CARBON benefits from fast client onboarding and integration with Microsoft Office 365.
- BFSI automated regulatory reporting solution (IRIS iDEAL) is seeing larger deal sizes through end-to-end implementations; completed one in India and pursuing opportunities in Mauritius and Qatar, alongside India.
- SupTech new order intake has been impacted by global economic uncertainty and instability in the Middle East, though existing customers are being successfully mined for additional work, and RFPs are active.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- RegTech EBITDA margins are a function of sales and marketing investments in developed markets (including the U.S.); management noted it is a little early to meaningfully expand EBITDA margins, but they expect to do so sooner than later.
- RegTech gross margin for IRIS CARBON runs at about 20%.
- SupTech allocated expenses increased QoQ due to partner payments associated with higher revenues.
- RegTech allocated expenses saw a small drop due to lower travel during the Q2 vacation period.
- Unallocated expenses comprise corporate expenses, including management salaries and rent.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned higher outlay on SaaS sales and marketing expenses to build the IRIS CARBON brand, particularly in the U.S. market, strengthen products, and build partnership networks.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash position is quite comfortable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global economic uncertainty and instability in the Middle East have delayed new order intake in the SupTech segment, with customers being guarded in loosening purse strings.
- Long sales cycles persist in the BFSI automated reporting business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Surender Singh (My Equity Sherpa) on replacing competitor platforms and using POCs:** Deepta Rangarajan explained that they engage on overall product offerings, XBRL capabilities, support, and AI functionalities, waiting for contract renewals. They occasionally use POCs, limited dry runs, and workshops.
2. **Surender Singh on AI agent positioning:** Management clarified that AI agents are embedded directly into products like IRIS CARBON and supervisory solutions for existing customers, not offered as a standalone P&L line item.
3. **Anirudha Bhandari (Street Capital) on RegTech vs. SupTech allocated expenses:** Management explained SupTech expenses rose due to partner payments tied to higher revenues, while RegTech expenses saw a slight drop due to lower travel in Q2.
4. **Anirudha Bhandari on RegTech revenue trajectory:** Vineet Kandoi noted that while growth may not be strictly linear, the trajectory is upward, supported by strong ARR growth and the large enterprise deal.
5. **Anirudha Bhandari on Middle East impact:** Management stated that while execution on current business continues, new orders are delayed as clients wait to release budgets, though conditions have improved slightly since Q1.

### One-line takeaway
IRIS RegTech delivered robust over 30% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27, underpinned by a 52% jump in IRIS CARBON ARR and a large enterprise win, even as Middle East geopolitical issues delayed SupTech order intake.
