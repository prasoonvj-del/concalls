# Birlasoft Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While the company reported stable revenue and strong deal wins with an AI focus, management acknowledged continued softness in key verticals (Manufacturing, E&U) and a near-term margin dilution due to wage hikes.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** INR 1,379.4 crore (up 2.3% QoQ, 7.4% YoY).
*   **Revenue (USD):** $145.2 million (flattish QoQ; 0.3% growth in constant currency).
*   **EBITDA:** INR 222 crore (up 40.3% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 16.1%.
*   **PAT:** $16.9 million (INR 161 crore).
*   **EPS:** INR 5.72 (non-annualized).
*   **TCV Signings:** $168.7 million (up 20% YoY).
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** INR 2,878.6 crore (up 9% QoQ, 26% YoY).
*   **DSO:** 55 days.
*   **Effective Tax Rate (ETR):** 29.4%.

### Highlights
*   **AI Focus:** Management highlighted significant progress in AI capabilities, launching platforms like *LynX* (test engineering) and *Sigma* (AIOps/AMS), alongside the existing *Cogito* platform. Over 25% of code is now generated via agentic AI tools.
*   **Deal Wins:** Secured strategic AI-led engagements in U.S. financial services and European banking, plus a pharmacovigilance deal in the LSS vertical.
*   **Sales Force:** Management is actively expanding the sales team, with a "substantial increase" expected over the coming months.
*   **Client Rationalization:** Active client count is ~213; management stated the rationalization process is largely complete.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Momentum:** Management expects to sustain the current momentum but noted that execution remains key.
*   **Margins:** Management maintains a target of 15%+ EBITDA margin.
*   **Tax Rate:** Expects ETR for FY27 to be in the 29%–30% range.
*   **Order Book:** Management stated H1 FY27 deal signings will be "much better" than H1 FY26.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Verticals:** Financial Services and Life Sciences (LSS) are performing strongly.
*   **Weakness:** Manufacturing and Energy & Utilities (E&U) are experiencing softness. Management expects E&U to turn around from Q3 onwards, while Manufacturing remains a focus for turnaround efforts.
*   **Pricing:** Management noted "enormous" pricing pressure on renewals due to the integration of AI-driven productivity gains.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Wage Hikes:** Effective July 1, 2026, wage hikes will impact margins by 170–200 bps in Q2. Management expects to recover a significant portion through productivity improvements.
*   **One-offs:** Q1 margins normalized to 16.1% without the 170 bps tailwind benefit seen in Q4 FY26.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Sales Investment:** Heavy investment is being directed toward expanding sales and domain capabilities.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Management is open to capability-based acquisitions but emphasized that the current priority is delivering organic growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Position:** Strong cash balance of INR 2,878.6 crore.
*   **Collections:** OCF to EBITDA ratio was 108%.
*   **Unbilled Revenue:** Management noted that high unbilled revenue from March 2026 was regularized in Q1, contributing to improved cash collections and DSO.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Cyclicality:** Manufacturing and E&U verticals are cyclical and currently under stress.
*   **Pricing Pressure:** Competitive bidding and client demands for productivity gains (via AI) are creating a mismatch between TCV growth and revenue growth.
*   **Execution:** Management acknowledged that revenue growth is the primary lagging parameter and requires consistent sequential improvement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Capital Allocation:** Analysts (Priyank Chheda, Amit Chandra) questioned the high cash balance relative to market cap and the lack of a defined policy (buybacks/acquisitions). Management stated the Board is "seized" of the matter and will take action at the appropriate time.
*   **On Revenue vs. TCV Gap:** Amit Chandra asked when TCV and revenue growth will converge. Management indicated it will take 3–4 quarters to streamline as the industry stabilizes.
*   **On Sales Team Size:** Priyank Chheda noted sales headcount is below pre-COVID levels. Management clarified that while the overall "sales and support" number looks lower, they have shifted the balance toward high-value sales/domain roles and are actively hiring.
*   **On Client Rationalization:** Sandeep Shah asked if rationalization is a headwind. Management confirmed the process is "almost done" and they are now focused on growing the existing 200+ marquee accounts.

### One-line takeaway
Birlasoft is prioritizing AI-led deal wins and sales force expansion to drive organic growth, while managing near-term margin dilution from wage hikes and persistent weakness in manufacturing and utility verticals.
