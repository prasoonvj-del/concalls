# AWL Agri Business Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management reported broad-based growth, improved profitability, and successful execution of the transition toward a diversified Food and FMCG portfolio.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 20,048 crore (+18% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 693 crore (+34% YoY).
*   **PBT:** +48% YoY; **PAT:** +40% YoY.
*   **Food & FMCG Revenue:** Rs 1,726 crore (+22% YoY).
*   **Food & FMCG EBITDA:** Rs 104 crore (6% margin).
*   **Edible Oil Volume Growth:** 2% YoY.
*   **Industry Essentials:** 13% volume growth; 28% revenue growth.
*   **Quick Commerce Growth:** 56% YoY.
*   **Distribution:** 970,000 direct outlets; 2.6 million total reach.

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Shift:** Management is actively transitioning from a predominantly Edible Oil company to a diversified Food and FMCG player.
*   **Category Performance:** Rice grew >40% YoY; Tops range (sauces/pickles) grew 23% YoY.
*   **Madhur Integration:** Successfully integrated the 'Madhur' sugar brand (licensed from Shree Renuka Sugars) to strengthen the sugar portfolio.
*   **Reporting Change:** Management will now report Food & FMCG performance via revenue, volume growth, and EBITDA margin, while Edible Oil and Industry Essentials will be reported on a per-ton basis.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Food & FMCG:** Management targets mid-teen revenue growth with EBITDA margins in the 3%-4% range.
*   **Edible Oil:** Management expects 5%-6% volume growth for the remaining nine months of the year; EBITDA guidance is Rs 4,000-Rs 4,500 per metric ton.
*   **Industry Essentials:** Management expects 8%-9% volume growth; EBITDA guidance is Rs 3,000-Rs 3,500 per metric ton.
*   **Madhur Sugar:** Management targets 20,000 tonnes/month volume by year-end, with potential full-year revenue of Rs 700-Rs 800 crore.
*   **Long-term (2030):** Management aims for >Rs 100,000 crore revenue and >Rs 4,000 crore EBITDA.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Edible Oil:** Experienced temporary channel de-stocking in Q1 due to global price volatility, but underlying consumer demand remains resilient.
*   **Quick Commerce:** Management views this as a structural shift in consumer behavior, enabling faster product discovery and higher purchase frequency.
*   **Import Dependence:** India’s edible oil import dependence (~70%) is expected to persist for the long term.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Edible Oil EBITDA:** Per metric ton increased 33% YoY.
*   **Industry Essentials EBITDA:** Increased 47% YoY.
*   **Sourcing:** ~70% of edible oil is imported; ~1/3 of imports are sourced from Wilmar at arm's length.
*   **Inventory:** Management maintains 30-35 days of raw material stock due to import voyage times.
*   **Royalty:** The company pays a 0.5% royalty on Madhur brand sales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Management estimates an annual capex requirement of ~Rs 700 crore.
*   **Capacity:** Edible oil refining capacity utilization is currently 60%-61%.
*   **Strategy:** Management intends to convert contractual/tolling food operations into in-house operations and is expanding specialty chemical capacity at the Southern facility.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Volatility:** Global edible oil price volatility and supply chain disruptions impacted Q1 volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why was the Food & FMCG EBITDA margin 6% this quarter?** Management clarified this should not be used as guidance; they advised using the 3%-4% average of the last 4-5 quarters, as the business remains in an investment phase.
*   **Q: What is the nature of the Madhur sugar agreement?** Management confirmed it is a licensing agreement with a 0.5% royalty; the focus is on scaling volume rather than immediate margin expansion.
*   **Q: How does the company hedge raw materials?** Management stated that the brand itself acts as a hedge, supplemented by forward sales; they do not engage in speculative hedging.
*   **Q: Why was Edible Oil volume growth only 2%?** Management attributed this to temporary channel de-stocking and supply chain disruptions in Q1, expecting a recovery in the coming quarters.

### One-line takeaway
AWL is successfully scaling its Food and FMCG business while maintaining resilient margins in its core Edible Oil and Industry Essentials segments, despite short-term commodity volatility.
