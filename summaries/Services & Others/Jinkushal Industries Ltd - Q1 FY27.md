# Jinkushal Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew, but profitability was impacted by geopolitical headwinds, increased logistics costs, and upfront investments in organizational scaling.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 51.29 crore (up 37.4% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 56.57 crore (up 15.9% YoY).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 3.31 crore (vs Rs 3.76 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 2.2 crore (vs Rs 6.51 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Consolidated Inventory:** Rs 96.8 crore (with Rs 84.4 crore positioned at overseas subsidiaries).
*   **Shipping/Logistics Costs (Standalone):** Rs 3.86 crore (vs Rs 2.36 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Consolidated Employee Benefit Expenses:** Rs 3.83 crore (vs Rs 2.21 crore in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Geographic Shift:** Africa accounted for ~32% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from ~4.47% in Q1 FY26, compensating for reduced activity in the Middle East.
*   **Inventory Strategy:** Management is deliberately holding higher inventory overseas to improve product availability and reduce delivery timelines for the used/refurbished equipment business.
*   **Organizational Build:** Significant investment in personnel across operations, procurement, finance, and international sales to support future scaling.
*   **HexL Brand:** Continued investment in product development, marketing, and distribution; management views this as a long-term contributor to the business.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Long-term Revenue Target:** Management maintained the target of Rs 600 crore to Rs 700 crore over the next 2-3 years.
*   **Investment Payback:** Management expects to see meaningful improvements from current strategic investments in 3 to 6 quarters.
*   **Revenue Mix Goal:** Management aims for an equal revenue split across its three verticals (new equipment, used/refurbished, and HexL) in 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Dynamics:** Geopolitical developments have created operational and logistical challenges, particularly impacting freight and shipping costs.
*   **Demand Drivers:** New customized equipment is currently seeing higher demand due to end-user requirements for faster availability and lower breakdown times.
*   **International Markets:** Africa and Latin America are viewed as large, developing regions with significant long-term potential.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Profitability Pressure:** Margins were hit by rising shipping/logistics costs and increased employee benefit expenses.
*   **Cost Absorption:** Management is absorbing a "substantial amount" of the logistics cost hit to maintain customer relationships and revenue growth, while partially passing costs to suppliers and customers.
*   **Commodity Inflation:** Management noted that commodity prices are swinging wildly and are not being fully passed through to customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Strategy:** Focus is on expanding international presence (Africa, Latin America, Middle East) and strengthening internal systems/processes.
*   **Inventory:** Inventory levels are expected to remain in the "lower double digits" (percentage-wise) relative to current levels.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Management expects elongated working capital cycles to persist due to current geopolitical conditions and longer shipping times.
*   **Liquidity:** The company has a stronger capital base post-IPO and enhanced banking facilities to support business volumes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical Instability:** Cited as the primary cause for logistical challenges, increased shipping costs, and longer working capital cycles.
*   **Inventory Risk:** High inventory levels (Rs 96.8 crore) tied up in working capital, though management believes it is necessary for competitive availability.
*   **Execution Risk:** The company is transitioning from an SME to a larger corporate structure, which involves upfront costs that may not immediately yield returns.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the sharp rise in employee costs?** Management: It is a deliberate investment to build a professional organization and achieve future operating leverage; they do not intend to stop this despite the impact on current numbers.
*   **Q: Can you pass on higher logistics costs?** Management: They are taking a "substantial" hit on margins but are partially passing costs to suppliers and customers to maintain revenue growth.
*   **Q: When will HexL become EBITDA positive?** Management: It is in nascent stages; profitability will improve as volumes pick up, with a target of 12-15% PAT margins long-term.
*   **Q: Will inventory continue to grow with revenue?** Management: They expect inventory levels to remain similar (plus or minus a few percentage points) as they adjust to demand/supply.

### One-line takeaway
Jinkushal is sacrificing short-term profitability to fund aggressive international expansion and organizational scaling, betting that these investments will yield returns within 3-6 quarters.
