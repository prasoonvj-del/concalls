# PAN HR Solution Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-15 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** H2 FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and PAT growth, successful IPO completion, and a robust balance sheet supporting a three-year revenue target of INR1,000 crores.

### Key numbers
- H2 FY26 total income: INR12,656.59 lakh (or INR126.57 crores), up 28% YoY
- H2 FY26 PAT: INR432.20 lakh (or INR4.32 crores), up 158% YoY
- FY26 PAT: INR750.29 lakh
- Net worth (as of FY26 end): INR3,751 lakh
- Cash and bank balance (as of FY26 end): INR2,434 lakh
- Workforce deployment base: Over 10,000 personnel (currently crossed 11,000 personnel)
- Client base: More than 17 clients
- Revenue target: INR1,000 crores by FY29
- FY27 turnover growth guidance: Approximately 40%

### Highlights
- Completed IPO in February 2026 and listed on the BSE SME platform.
- Balance sheet became significantly stronger with a net worth of INR3,751 lakh, cash of INR2,434 lakh, and a virtually debt-free status.
- Transitioning focus toward the pay-and-collect model and third-party logistics (3PL) following fund infusion.
- Expanded geographical presence into the South and West in addition to existing operations in the North and East.
- Extended service offerings beyond blue-collar manpower deployment into white-collar hiring (one-time staffing), payroll outsourcing, compliance management, HR outsourcing, and consultancy services.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 turnover to increase by approximately 40%.
- Management set an aspiration/target to achieve INR1,000 crores in revenue by FY29.
- Priorities for FY27 include deploying IPO proceeds for working capital expansion, accelerating the pay-and-collect model, expanding geographies, and scaling higher-value service offerings.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand observed in e-commerce, quick commerce, logistics, warehousing, manufacturing, and facility management sectors.
- Businesses increasingly seek outsourcing partners to provide skilled manpower, statutory compliance, and operational efficiency.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management noted that certain contracts come with lower margins but create significant operating leverage.
- Focus remains on profitable growth, return ratios, and sustained improvement in earnings rather than top-line growth alone.
- Group consolidation and utilization of existing internal infrastructure without heavy additional overheads are expected to reduce infrastructure costs and boost profit margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- IPO proceeds are being deployed for working capital expansion.
- Evaluating inorganic growth/acquisitions of companies in workforce management, staffing, and payroll services with strong client bases and complementary profiles to aid expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company ended FY26 virtually debt-free with zero liability.
- Strong liquidity position with cash and bank balance of INR2,434 lakh and net worth of INR3,751 lakh.
- Moving from the collect-and-pay model to the pay-and-collect model enabled by improved capital availability.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management acknowledged that operations are subject to competitive market conditions, geographical scenarios, and labor compliance requirements.
- Management noted that return ratios might experience temporary fluctuations due to capital deployment for growth and expansion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shaan Patel (SPAM) on full-year vs. half-year performance interpretation:** Management explained that H2 FY26 revenue grew 28% YoY to INR126.57 crores and PAT grew 158% YoY to INR4.32 crores. Full-year FY26 growth appeared moderate due to capital constraints early in the year preventing certain pay-and-collect contracts, and an elevated FY25 base caused by a one-time exceptional prior period income of INR6.5 crores. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Shaan Patel on capital deployment:** Management stated that IPO proceeds will prioritize business expansion, working capital needs, and evaluating accretive acquisition opportunities in staffing and payroll services. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Nishita Sanklesha on drivers for the INR1,000 crore revenue target by FY29:** Management highlighted geographic expansion (moving into the South and West), entering third-party logistics (3PL) and B2B models previously constrained by funding, scaling workforce deployment (already crossed 11,000), and adding high-margin services like white-collar hiring and compliance. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Satvan Thakkar on industry differentiators:** Management cited three pillars: 100% compliance credibility since 2015, strong financial footing post-IPO, and an integrated single-window model handling end-to-end lifecycles, salaries, and compliance. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Hiral Nandu on ROE/ROCE expectations:** Management stated a high focus on maintaining healthy return ratios, noting that while temporary fluctuations may occur during capital deployment, every major investment is evaluated for its impact on EPS, ROE, ROCE, and shareholder value. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Fueled by a successful BSE SME IPO and a debt-free balance sheet with INR2,434 lakh in cash, PAN HR Solution is scaling its workforce base past 11,000 personnel, expanding into 3PL and high-margin services, and targeting INR1,000 crores in revenue by FY29.
