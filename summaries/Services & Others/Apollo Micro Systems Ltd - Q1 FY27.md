# Apollo Micro Systems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the best June quarter in history with record margins and announced a major strategic acquisition.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 156 crore (17% YoY growth).
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 251 crore (88% YoY growth).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 28 crore (43% YoY growth).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 25 crore (43% YoY growth).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 48 crore (includes other income).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 54 crore (31% YoY growth).
*   **Standalone PAT Margin:** 18%.
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 31%.
*   **Standalone Order Book:** Rs 1,224 crore.
*   **Consolidated Order Book:** Rs 1,704 crore (includes Rs 480 crore from Ideal Explosives).
*   **R&D Spend (FY26):** Rs 72.53 crore (~9.5% of revenue).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Acquisition:** Entered a definitive agreement to acquire a 41.33% promoter stake in Premier Explosives Limited for ~Rs 1,550 crore (all-cash transaction).
*   **Operational Milestones:** Handed over Safety and Detonation Device (SDD) to the Indian Navy; received Make-II prototype sanction for the SAVIOR Anti-Submarine Warfare system; selected as prime development agency for the IPREC program (Indian Air Force).
*   **Ideal Explosives:** Now PAT positive; restructuring is ongoing with full profitability expected from the next financial year.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management reiterated guidance of 40%-45% CAGR on both consolidated and standalone bases.
*   **Order Book:** Expects consolidated order book to reach Rs 3,500-4,000 crore by the end of FY27.
*   **Unit 3:** First phase of production has started; full operational capacity expected before March 2027.
*   **Promoter Pledge:** Management aims to reduce promoter pledge to zero within one year (by Q1 FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **QRSAM:** Bidding/negotiation complete; total order value for BDL expected at Rs 11,000-12,000 crore. Apollo expects to supply >Rs 1 crore of components per missile.
*   **MIGM:** Expecting order by Dec/Jan; total budget ~Rs 3,500 crore, with Apollo expecting a 70% share.
*   **Pinaka:** 2,000 units planned; Apollo component value ~Rs 85 lakh per unit.
*   **Akash-NG:** 1,000 units planned; Apollo component value >Rs 1 crore per unit.
*   **Exports:** Currently nil; management plans to be aggressive once Unit 3 is fully operational.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to remain better than current levels.
*   **Synergy:** The Premier Explosives acquisition provides backward and forward integration, allowing in-house production of explosives/propellants, which management expects will improve margins compared to outsourcing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Unit 3:** Significant new facility under construction; first phase is operational.
*   **Future Acquisitions:** Management is planning to acquire 2-3 additional companies (MOU stage pending).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Acquisition Funding:** ~Rs 2,500 crore allocated for the Premier Explosives acquisition; ~Rs 500 crore for working capital, debt, and general corporate purposes.
*   **Advances:** 20% advance payment expected on the MIGM deal.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution:** Defense contracts involve long procurement cycles and milestone-based execution, leading to uneven quarterly revenue recognition.
*   **Ideal Explosives:** Currently a loss-making acquisition undergoing restructuring; full turnaround expected by next fiscal year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs):** Asked about Premier Explosives synergies. Management stated it enables complete weapon system integration (electronics + propulsion) and reduces interdependency.
*   **Shreyans Gathani:** Asked about Ideal Explosives turnaround. Management noted it is now PAT positive and expects full turnaround by next fiscal year.
*   **Deepak Sharma:** Asked for EBITDA guidance. Management declined to give specific EBITDA guidance but stated momentum will continue.
*   **Meet Gada:** Asked about missing links/further acquisitions. Management confirmed plans to acquire 2-3 more companies.
*   **Vinay Kumar Daggolu:** Asked about export process. Management clarified exports are currently nil but expects breakthrough orders next fiscal year.

### One-line takeaway
Apollo is aggressively scaling through strategic acquisitions (Premier Explosives) and capacity expansion (Unit 3) to capture large-scale defense opportunities (QRSAM, MIGM, Pinaka) while targeting 40-45% revenue CAGR.
