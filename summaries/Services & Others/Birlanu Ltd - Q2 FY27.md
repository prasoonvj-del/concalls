# Birlanu Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong performance in the India business (double-digit growth, margin expansion) is overshadowed by persistent losses and demand headwinds at the European subsidiary, Parador.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 885 crore (+9.2% YoY).
*   **India Business Revenue:** Rs 588 crore (+17.3% YoY).
*   **India Business EBITDA:** Rs 40 crore (vs Rs 12 crore YoY); Margin at 6.8% (+400 bps YoY).
*   **Roofs Segment Revenue:** Rs 244 crore (+28% YoY); EBITDA margin 11.5% (+320 bps YoY).
*   **Walls Segment Revenue:** Rs 164 crore (+5.4% YoY); EBITDA margin 12.3% (+120 bps YoY).
*   **Pipes Segment Revenue:** Rs 132 crore (+20% YoY); EBITDA margin improved by 630 bps.
*   **Construction Chemicals Revenue:** +35% YoY.
*   **Parador Revenue:** -4% YoY (in INR terms); -11% in local currency.
*   **Total Debt:** Rs 896 crore (vs Rs 852 crore as of March 31, 2026).
*   **Parador Provision:** Additional Rs 90 crore provision for investment diminution (total cumulative provision: Rs 164 crore).

### Highlights
*   **India Business:** Delivered 5th consecutive quarter of EBITDA margin expansion.
*   **Roofs:** Strong growth driven by 21% volume growth and a richer product mix (value-added products share +35% YoY).
*   **Pipes:** Strong turnaround with value-added products (including fittings) now 47% of the portfolio.
*   **Construction Chemicals:** Growth driven by market development and portfolio expansion; Clean Coats acquisition revenue up ~50% YoY.
*   **Parador:** Facing demand headwinds in Europe; management has engaged BCG for a cost-out program targeting EUR 9-14 million in annual EBITDA savings.
*   **Strategic Initiatives:** Commissioned rooftop solar at Golan and Chennai units; green power expected to exceed 20-25% of energy mix within 2 years.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Parador:** Management aims for the business to be EBITDA positive in H2 FY27.
*   **Capacity:** Greenfield boards plant (Nellore) on track for Q4 FY27 commercial production; ground-breaking for a new project in Hyderabad planned for December 2026.
*   **Clean Coats:** Integration expected to be completed by end of 2026.
*   **Overall:** Management confident in delivering a strong second half and a strong FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Roofs:** Benefited from price corrections and a shift in demand from steel roofing (which saw price hikes) to the company's products.
*   **Parador:** European market remains weak, though there is a slight uptick in new house permits/constructions which may impact flooring demand with a 12-18 month lag.
*   **Risks:** Geopolitical uncertainties, global supply disruptions, and inflation in raw materials/freight/currency continue to pose risks.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **India Business:** Margin expansion driven by operating leverage, better realizations, and improved product mix.
*   **Parador:** Profitability impacted by cost pressures and lower volumes.
*   **Solar:** Annual cost savings of Rs 4-5 crore expected from current solar projects.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity Utilization:** ~85% in the Walls segment.
*   **New Ventures:** Launching an AI-enabled materials discovery platform for architects/designers; initial budget of Rs 5 crore over 12-18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Increase in debt attributed to investment in greenfield projects and capacity expansion.
*   **Exposure:** Total exposure to Parador is EUR 40 million (EUR 20 million SBLC + EUR 20 million corporate guarantee).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Parador Performance:** Persistent losses and repeated missed guidance led to significant investor concern regarding value destruction and capital allocation.
*   **External Environment:** High inflation, tight liquidity, and potential El Nino impact on rural demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why continue supporting Parador given the history of missed targets and write-downs?** Management stated they are in an investment phase, diversifying geographically (US, India, Middle East), and implementing a deep cost-restructuring program with BCG.
*   **Q: Can you quantify the volume vs. price growth in Pipes?** Management clarified 4% volume growth and 15% pricing/mix growth.
*   **Q: What is the objective of the AI design platform?** It is an asset-light, independent business line aimed at engaging the specifier community to drive B2B business.
*   **Q: Why increase financial support to Parador while writing down investments?** Management clarified that the EUR 40 million exposure includes acquisition financing and is not purely operational support; they emphasized that provisions are reversible if cash flows improve.
*   **Q: Is the Parador cost-out program internal or volume-dependent?** Management confirmed it is based on internal cost takeouts, not sales volume, targeting a reduction in monthly breakeven by ~EUR 1 million.

### One-line takeaway
Birlanu’s India business is firing on all cylinders with strong margin expansion and growth, but the persistent underperformance and capital drain of the European subsidiary, Parador, remains a significant overhang on shareholder sentiment.
