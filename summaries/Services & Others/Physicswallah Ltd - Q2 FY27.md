# Physicswallah Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and EBITDA growth, with PAT doubling YoY, despite specific exam-related headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,242 crore (up 18% YoY).
*   **Pre-Ind AS EBITDA:** Rs 233 crore (up 45% YoY; 18.8% margin).
*   **PAT:** Rs 142 crore (doubled YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 2,293 crore (up 21% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 55 crore (vs Rs 57 crore loss YoY).
*   **Online Revenue:** Rs 694 crore (up 26.4% YoY).
*   **Offline Revenue:** Rs 541 crore (up 9% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Enrollments:** Unique enrollments up 12.9% to 1.5 million; unique transacting users at 3.44 million.
*   **ACPU:** Up 9.2% to Rs 4,280.
*   **Product Performance:** Infinity Pro collections up 32% (share of collections rose to 35% from 31%); Skills enrollment up 10.3x to 1.69 lakh.
*   **Strategic Moves:** Sold FinZ loan book (Rs 96 crore) to Auxilo Finserve; acquired 11% stake in Sarrthi IAS (Rs 71 crore).
*   **Digital University:** PW Digital University officially notified in Punjab Government Gazette; focus on online degrees/diplomas.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management maintained full-year revenue growth guidance of ~30%.
*   **NEET Recovery:** Management expects NEET demand to recover over the medium-to-long term, supported by the transition to computer-based exams in FY2028.
*   **Profitability:** Offline Vidyapeeth business expected to be EBITDA positive this year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **NEET Impact:** Full cycle online NEET collections are 7% below last year; offline NEET enrollments are 8% lower.
*   **Government Exams:** Enrollment down 24% due to lack of vacancies and form notifications.
*   **Diversification:** Categories beyond JEE/NEET contributed 73.8% of H1 enrollments (up from 64.8%).
*   **South India:** Represents 9% of H1 revenue; management utilizing an online-first playbook with AI-driven Indic language translation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Online Margins:** Online EBITDA margin at 34.4%.
*   **Employee Costs:** Ex-ESOP costs lower by 2.3% YoY.
*   **Marketing:** H1 spends at Rs 216 crore (improved 1.2% YoY as % of revenue).
*   **Tech/AI:** H1 tech spends at Rs 104 crore; AI now writes 90% of code.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Offline Centers:** 370 centers; calibrated expansion of 25-30 centers annually.
*   **Treasury:** Rs 5,268 crore (includes Rs 1,800 crore IPO proceeds).
*   **M&A:** Robust pipeline of online/AI-first companies; management in active discussions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Lending:** Exited direct lending; student financing now via third-party NBFCs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **NEET Delay:** Impact on both online and offline collections/enrollments.
*   **Subsidiary Impact:** Xylem (subsidiary) impacted by high NEET dependence.
*   **External Factors:** Government exam notification delays affecting Utkarsh Classes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Gaurav Malhotra (Axis Capital):** Asked about NEET recovery and online growth. Management noted NEET impact is real but is being offset by strong performance in CA, CLAT, and other categories.
*   **Abhishek Pathak (Motilal Oswal):** Asked about margin reinvestment. Management stated they are investing in AI tutor but are maintaining overall guidance and margin targets.
*   **Swapnil:** Asked about AI Tuto unit economics. Management noted price discovery is pending, but current cost is <Rs 20/hour.
*   **Pankaj Mehndiratta (Bank of America):** Asked about M&A scale. Management confirmed <USD 20 million allocation for inorganic growth.
*   **Jinesh Joshi:** Asked about the dip in employee count. Management attributed this to AI efficiencies in content, quality checks, and test-making.

### One-line takeaway
Strong core performance and margin expansion driven by online growth and AI efficiencies successfully offset NEET-related headwinds, with management maintaining full-year guidance.
