# Advit Jewels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong financial growth and clear progress on retail expansion, despite a slight dip in EBITDA margins due to marketing/promotional spend.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained focus on retail expansion; no specific numerical guidance provided (management emphasized "dreams" over data).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong bridal demand" to "encouraging shift in consumer preferences" toward handcrafted/art-led luxury; management remains optimistic about the upcoming wedding season.
- **New topics/risks:** Explicit mention of the Defence Colony, Delhi showroom launch (Q4 FY27); increased focus on B2C marketing expenses.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Jaipur flagship store construction is completed, with interior work currently underway (previously expected to be operational by Nov 2026).

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 59.1 crore (up 23.93% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 94.5 crore (up 28.6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 16.4 crore (up 18.8% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 27.8% (H1 EBITDA margin: 29.8%).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 12.76 crore (up 28% YoY).
*   **Net Profit Margin:** 21.6% (expanded 68 bps YoY; H1 net profit margin: 22.2%, expanded 51 bps).
*   **Inventory:** Rs 169 crore.
*   **Artisan Base:** 56 skilled karigars.

### Highlights
*   **Retail Expansion:** Launching a company-owned showroom in Defence Colony, Delhi in Q4 FY27.
*   **Flagship Store:** Jaipur flagship store (approx. 35,000 sq. ft.) construction completed; interior work ongoing.
*   **B2C Strategy:** Targeting a shift in revenue mix to 30% B2C and 70% B2B next year (from current 80:20 B2B:B2C ratio).
*   **Product Strategy:** Focusing on "Kundan Meena Phool Ki Jadau" diamond jewelry; expanding into contemporary men's jewelry and lightweight pieces for Gen Z/millennials.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects "fabulous" response in the upcoming wedding season.
*   **Expansion:** Plans to grow network across India and eventually enter international markets.
*   **Franchise Model:** No active discussions yet; company-owned stores planned for major cities, with franchises targeted for Tier-2 cities.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Consumer Trends:** Growing preference for handcrafted, design-led, and heritage-inspired jewelry among younger, affluent customers.
*   **Seasonality:** Q1 and Q2 are typically "lighter" seasons; bridal demand is expected to increase in the coming quarters.
*   **Competition:** Management noted that international brands are increasingly adopting Indian art forms (e.g., enameling), validating the company's focus on handmade jewelry.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** EBITDA margin decline (YoY) attributed to higher marketing and exhibition expenses for B2C preparation.
*   **Taxation:** Effective tax rate of 17-18% due to special manufacturing tax slab (post-2019 government scheme).
*   **Gold Hedging:** No exchange-based hedging; company uses "natural hedging" by buying back gold against sales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Manufacturing:** Integrated Jaipur facility has an annual capacity of 400 kgs of jewelry.
*   **Outsourcing:** Specific art forms are outsourced to specialized karigars as needed, while core processes remain in-house.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Status:** Company is "totally debt-free" following IPO proceeds utilization.
*   **IPO Proceeds:** Rs 65 crore used for debt repayment; Rs 30-35 crore utilized for building/working capital; remainder for general corporate purposes.
*   **Inventory:** Increase in inventory (Rs 27.7 crore rise since March '26) is to support upcoming flagship store and market demand; primarily finished goods.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Volatility:** Gold price fluctuations are managed via natural hedging.
*   **Execution:** Management acknowledged that the B2C transition requires extensive marketing and "experience center" development, which impacts short-term margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Inventory Days:** Management stated they do not track inventory in "days" because holding stock is necessary for a luxury B2C model; inventory is rotated to maximize sales.
*   **Delhi Store Success:** Management declined to provide specific sales targets for the new Delhi store, stating that future data is a "dream" and they prefer to focus on execution.
*   **B2B vs B2C Margins:** Management confirmed B2C fetches better margins than B2B.
*   **Platinum/Silver:** Management is open to manufacturing platinum if demand arises, but currently focuses on gold/diamond/Polki as they are more "sellable" in their target market.
*   **Top 10 Customers:** Management did not provide the specific concentration data on the call, offering to provide it via email.

### One-line takeaway
Advit Jewels is successfully pivoting toward a B2C luxury retail model, utilizing its debt-free balance sheet to fund expansion, while accepting short-term margin pressure from increased marketing and brand-building activities.
