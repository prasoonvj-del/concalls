# Bajaj Healthcare Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Revenue growth, margin expansion, and significant deleveraging, supported by a clear roadmap for high-margin specialty segments.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 165.6 crore (vs Rs 148.8 crore YoY; Rs 153.1 crore QoQ).
*   **Gross Profit:** Rs 80.4 crore (up 16.2% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 48.3% (+210 bps YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 29.6 crore (17.8% margin; +70 bps YoY, +110 bps QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 13.9 crore (up 14.1% YoY; 8.4% margin).
*   **Revenue Mix:** Domestic API (Rs 92.3 crore), Exports (Rs 50.1 crore), Formulations (Rs 23.3 crore).
*   **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.45 (improved from 0.48 in FY25 and 1.19 in FY24).
*   **Receivable Days:** 131 days (down from 145 days).

### Highlights
*   **Regulatory Milestones:** First company in India to secure SEC recommendation for Cenobamate Tablets; completed bioequivalence studies for Suvorexant Tablets.
*   **Oncology:** Launched "Bajaj Oncocare" (15+ brands, present in 23 states/3 UTs); first in India to launch Posaconazole 300 mg tablet.
*   **Acquisitions:** Acquired Gen Rx Pharmaceutical facility (Nashik) via NCLT; commercial production expected H2 2027.
*   **Nutraceuticals:** Secured exclusive licensing for Magnesium L-Threonate (Magtein); currently supplies to US innovator.
*   **R&D:** R&D spend increased to 2.2% of sales (from 0.4% in 2024).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management guides for 10-15% growth for FY27 and FY28.
*   **Long-term Target:** Management targets Rs 900-1,000 crore revenue in 2-3 years.
*   **Margins:** Targeting 18-20% EBITDA margin.
*   **Peptides:** Plant (250 kg/annum) commissioning Q4 2027; peak revenue potential Rs 200-300 crore.
*   **Oncology API:** Plant commissioning target Q4 2028.
*   **Cenobamate:** Expected revenue of Rs 10-12 crore in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Domestic API was the primary growth driver (+27% YoY).
*   API prices remained stable during the quarter.
*   Management expects export revenue mix to improve as geopolitical conditions ease.
*   Magnesium L-Threonate contributes ~10% of total revenue; expects 15-20% growth in FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margin expansion driven by a shift in product mix toward high-margin branded/specialized products.
*   Backward integration in 8-10 products (including Vitamin C) is expected to improve margins by 1-2% over the next 1-2 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Annual capex allocation: Rs 40-50 crore.
*   New 10,000 sq. ft. R&D facility at Savli operational by August 2026.
*   Two loss-making units in Tarapur remain to be sold.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net worth: Rs 533 crore (as of March 31, 2026).
*   Cash & equivalents: Rs 37.2 crore.
*   Receivable days target: 110-120 days.
*   Inventory increase in Q4 was attributed to strategic stocking due to geopolitical concerns.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical instability impacting API pricing and export demand.
*   Regulatory timelines for new product approvals (e.g., Cenobamate, Suvorexant).
*   Dependency on government contracts for certain business segments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did inventory levels increase?** Management (CFO) explained it was a strategic decision in Q4 to stock up due to the war situation to ensure production continuity.
*   **Q: What is the revenue potential of the new peptide plant?** Management (MD) stated that at peak utilization, it could generate Rs 200-300 crore.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Tarapur plants?** Management (CFO) confirmed two units are still pending sale; they are currently looking for buyers.
*   **Q: What balance sheet metric should investors focus on?** Management (CFO) identified Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Assets (ROA) as the key metrics for the next three years.
*   **Q: Is the capex cycle over?** Management (MD) clarified that they will continue to allocate Rs 40-50 crore annually for capex.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Healthcare is pivoting toward high-margin specialty segments (Peptides, Oncology, CNS) and aggressive deleveraging, with management targeting Rs 900-1,000 crore revenue in 2-3 years.
