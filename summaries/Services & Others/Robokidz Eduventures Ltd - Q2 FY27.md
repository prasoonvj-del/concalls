# Robokidz Eduventures Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with significant growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by robust institutional order execution.

- **H1 FY27 Standalone Total Income:** Rs 52.37 crore (up ~182% YoY from Rs 18.55 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 11.53 crore (up ~195% YoY from Rs 3.91 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Standalone PAT:** Rs 6.85 crore (up ~395% YoY from Rs 1.39 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 60.67 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 60.86 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Total Expenses:** Rs 46.94 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 13.93 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** ~22.9%
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Depreciation:** Rs 0.32 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Finance Costs:** Rs 2.65 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated PBT:** Rs 10.96 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Tax:** Rs 3.02 crore

### Highlights
- Successfully listed on the BSE SME platform on September 28, 2026, with an overall subscription of 834 times.
- Revenue mix is split roughly as 75% from education laboratory setups and 25% from subscription and other services.
- Government business accounts for ~75% of the revenue mix, while private business accounts for ~25%.
- Expanding the Young Engineers Academy (YEA) network with three new centers in Pune (Hadapsar, Amanora Park Town, Kharadi, Viman Nagar) and 5 to 10 more in the pipeline in Western India.
- Private school customer retention ratio stands at ~80%, with some clients associated for 14-15 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects sustainable EBITDA margins in the ballpark of 22% to 23%.
- Management expects a long-term compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 30% to 35% every year.
- Management expects to execute the current confirmed order book of Rs 75 crore before March 31, 2027, and anticipates an additional Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore in pipeline orders.
- Management plans to scale YEA centers to 12 to 15 owned stores around March.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is driven by government budget cycles, New Education Policy (NEP) initiatives, and CBSE mandates requiring composite skill labs in all schools by August 2027.
- Government bodies pay on milestone and budget cycles, leading to collections concentrated heavily in Q4/H2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Lab setup EBITDA margins are around 18% to 20%, while other services (subscriptions/YEA centers) yield around 28% to 30% EBITDA margins.
- YEA centers yield around 17% to 18% PAT margins.
- Operating leverage is expected to aid margins as the business scales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding YEA centers through company-run and franchisee models via the wholly-owned subsidiary Robokidz Retails Private Limited.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- High working capital requirements driven by government and institutional clients who pay on milestone and budget cycles; H1 collections typically materialize in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Working capital intensity and delayed payments from government institutional clients.
- Government business is tender-driven and relies heavily on technical qualifications and execution capabilities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar asked about the high working capital and negative/stretched cash flows relative to revenue:** Management explained that government and institutional customers pay on milestone and budget cycles, resulting in a working capital cycle of 120-130 days where H1 executions are collected in H2.
2. **Abhi Jain questioned the supply chain of Robokidz Retail in FY26 given no related-party transactions with Robokidz Eduventures:** Management stated that Robokidz Retail works with private schools requiring customized bills of quantities (BOQs), so retail sources those specific SKUs from its own separate set of suppliers.
3. **Abhi Jain asked about the tender winning process and competitive strength:** Management highlighted that execution capability and a strong network of local channel partners who understand government departments are the primary strengths, alongside strict technical qualification criteria.
4. **Rahil Dasani inquired about future tender visibility and CBSE mandates:** Management pointed to the CBSE mandate for composite skill labs by August 2027 and favorable government budgets under the NEP supporting multi-year demand.

### One-line takeaway
Robokidz Eduventures delivered stellar H1 FY27 growth backed by strong institutional order execution, and management remains confident in sustaining 30%-35% annual growth supported by government skilling mandates and expanding franchise networks.
