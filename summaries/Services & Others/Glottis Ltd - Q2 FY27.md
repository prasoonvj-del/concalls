# Glottis Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth driven by exports, but profitability faced significant pressure due to fluctuating freight rates and costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained bullish outlook on revenue growth, expecting to surpass earlier numbers and achieve double-digit YoY growth; management noted bottom-line improvement is a key focus.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from positive to mixed; while export volumes surged, profitability compressed sharply due to high volatility in crude prices and vessel charter hires, with the entire industry moving to spot pricing.
- **New topics or risks:** Incorporation of a wholly owned subsidiary (Glottis Logistics SDN. BHD.) in Malaysia; promoter extending an interest-free loan of Rs. 60 crores (with a 5-year tenure) to support working capital and business expansion; container deployment delayed from Q2 to Q3 due to raw material and geopolitical issues. Old risks around geopolitical tensions continued.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Container deployment slipped from Q3 (wait, Q1 notes said Q3, transcript says Q3 roll out, so on track for Q3); IPO capex implementation remains on track for completion in 2027. Trade receivables deteriorated from 77 days to around 70-70+ days (CFO noted trade receivable days are around 70 days, expected to improve by Q3/Q4).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 2,765 million (+28.8% YoY, +17.9% QoQ)
- **Total income:** Rs. 2,783 million
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 34 million
- **EBITDA margin:** 1.2%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs. 5,110 million (+33.5% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 196 million (margin of 3.8%)
- **Ocean freight container throughput:** 21,375 TEUs (vs 21,972 in Q2 FY26 and 21,841 in Q1 FY27)
- **Sea Import revenue:** Rs. 1,371 million (-20.8% YoY, 50% of revenue)
- **Sea Export revenue:** Rs. 1,160 million (+344.4% YoY, 42% of revenue)
- **Air Import revenue:** Rs. 76 million (+68.9% YoY)
- **Air Export revenue:** Rs. 78 million (+420% YoY)
- **Road transport revenue:** Rs. 68 million (2% of revenue)
- **Warehousing revenue:** Rs. 13 million
- **Trade receivable days:** Around 70 days
- **Owned fleet strength:** 85 vehicles
- **Promoter unsecured interest-free loan:** Rs. 60 crores (5-year tenure)

### Highlights
- Significant shift toward export business: Sea Export revenue surged 344.4% YoY, increasing its revenue share to ~42% from 12% in Q2 FY26.
- Customer concentration reduced: Top 5 customers' contribution dropped to ~33% from 41% in Q2 FY26.
- Industry mix diversified: Renewable energy contribution declined to ~16% (from 46% in Q2 FY26), while Logistics contributed 20% and Home Appliances 18%.
- Incorporated a wholly owned subsidiary, Glottis Logistics SDN. BHD., in Malaysia.
- Promoters infused Rs. 60 crores as an interest-free, 5-year loan to support working capital and global expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is very bullish on revenue growth for the next 2 quarters, expecting to surpass earlier numbers with double-digit YoY growth, provided crude levels stabilize below USD80-USD85/barrel.
- Focused on improving shipment-level profitability and bottom-line growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment remained mixed across global trade and logistics due to geopolitical developments, freight rate volatility, and uneven trade lane activity.
- Long-term contracts are not being fulfilled by shipping lines due to high volatility in crude and vessel charter hires; the entire industry is operating on spot pricing.
- India's merchandise exports grew, supported by engineering goods, electronic goods, and chemicals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability faced severe pressure due to changes in business mix, fluctuating freight realizations, higher operating costs, and volatile crude/vessel charter rates.
- EBITDA margin dropped to 1.2% (Rs. 34 million).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Owned fleet strength stands at 85 vehicles equipped with GPS trackers.
- Container deployment is planned to begin in Q3 FY27 (delayed from earlier due to raw material and geopolitical issues).
- Planned capital expenditure program using IPO proceeds remains on track for completion in 2027.
- Signed a long-term deal for 200,000 square feet of warehousing space to scale up in South and West India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivable days stand at around 70 days, with management taking measures to reduce them further.
- Promoters provided an interest-free loan of Rs. 60 crores (up to a 5-year tenure) to bridge the working capital gap caused by a 4x to 5x jump in freight rates and global expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in crude oil prices and vessel charter hires impacting operating costs and margins.
- High freight rate levels creating challenging credit and working capital situations for the entire industry.
- Absence of long-term contracts across shipping lines, forcing operations entirely onto volatile spot pricing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Venkatachalam (Aldebaran Capserv) asked if cooling oil prices would drive higher profitability and guidance over the next 2 quarters:** Management stated they are very bullish for the next 2 quarters, expecting positive growth if crude maintains levels below USD80-USD85 a barrel.
- **Kedar asked whether importers/exporters are committing to long-term contracts or spot pricing:** Management clarified that no shipping lines are fulfilling long-term contracts due to high crude and vessel charter volatility, so everything is moving on spot.
- **Shashank Mehta asked about the performance and contribution of the US and Malaysia subsidiaries:** Management noted the US subsidiary started operations last month and will show revenue impact from Q4, while Malaysia is undergoing incorporation formalities with revenue expected in Q3 or Q4.
- **Shiv Muttoo asked if the Rs. 60 crore promoter funding is short-term and reversible:** Management stated it is an interest-free loan with a tenure of up to 5 years, which can be partially or fully withdrawn based on the company's cash flow.
- **Nakul Dev asked about the status of backward integration and container deployment:** Management replied that fleet strength is 85 vehicles, and container production is complete with deployment rolling out in Q3.

### One-line takeaway
Glottis posted a 28.8% YoY revenue increase to Rs. 2,765 million in Q2 FY27 driven by surging exports and diversification, but EBITDA margins compressed to 1.2% amid volatile freight rates and high operating costs.
