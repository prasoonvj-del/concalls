# AAVAS Financiers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance, improved efficiency, and healthy asset quality, though management anticipates some spread compression.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 16.1 billion (+41% YoY).
*   **AUM:** Rs 239.3 billion (+15.4% YoY).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 1.71 billion (+23% YoY).
*   **NII Growth:** 18% YoY.
*   **NIM:** 7.70% (+22 bps YoY).
*   **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 43.7% (improved 254 bps YoY).
*   **Operating Cost-to-Assets Ratio:** 3.37% (improved 9 bps YoY).
*   **Asset Quality:** 1+DPD at 3.76% (improved 39 bps YoY); Gross Stage 3 at 1.11% (improved 11 bps YoY); Net Stage 3 at 0.71% (improved 13 bps YoY).
*   **Credit Costs:** 24 bps.
*   **ROA:** 3.19% (+25 bps YoY); **ROE:** 13.34% (+78 bps YoY).
*   **Borrowings:** Raised Rs 14.74 billion at 7.74% cost; total outstanding borrowings at Rs 207 billion.
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** 44.66%.

### Highlights
*   Monthly AUM addition improved by nearly 50% YoY.
*   Branch network expanded to 440 branches across 15 states.
*   Implemented a 10 bps PLR reduction in June 2026 (cumulative 25 bps reduction since March 2026).
*   Management focus: Accelerating customer acquisition, improving productivity per resource, and enhancing branch profitability.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Management targets:** 22-23% YoY growth in disbursements and 17-18% YoY growth in AUM for FY27.
*   **Medium-term:** Management aims for sustainable 20% AUM growth.
*   **Spreads:** Management expects spreads to go sub-5% for the full year.
*   **Productivity:** Management aims to double productivity per resource (from Rs 8-10 lakhs to Rs 20-22 lakhs) within two years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Healthy competition observed across geographies.
*   Tractor sales showing signs of a rebound.
*   No specific geographical or customer segment stress observed.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Cost of funds improved by 38 bps YoY.
*   Spread stood at 5.06% during the quarter.
*   76% of borrowings are linked to floating benchmarks (42% external benchmarks, 34% sub-3-month MCLR).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Continued investment in branch expansion with a focus on faster branch-level break-evens.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Liquidity position: Rs 18.8 billion (cash, cash equivalents, and unavailed CC limits).
*   Documented unavailed sanctions: Rs 4.85 billion.
*   Maintained positive ALM across all time buckets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Spread Compression:** Management expects spreads to trend below 5% due to competitive pressures.
*   **Macro Environment:** Monitoring potential impacts from the West Asia conflict and expected rainfall shortfall.
*   **Underwriting:** Proactive policy changes implemented for segments potentially impacted by macro factors (e.g., tours and travels, restaurants).
*   **Regulatory:** RBI circular on asset classification for repossessed assets is currently under evaluation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Competitive Environment (Prashant Poddar, ADIA):** Management acknowledged competitive pressure and expected spread compression but stated that operating leverage and productivity gains would maintain ROA/ROE targets.
*   **Repayment Rates (Renish Bhuva, ICICI Securities):** Management noted that repayment rates, which saw an uptick in early Q1, normalized by June.
*   **Asset Quality (Shreepal Doshi, Equirus):** Management confirmed no stress in the portfolio and noted that underwriting policies were proactively adjusted in February for specific sectors.
*   **Productivity Goals (Raghav Garg, Ambit Capital):** Management clarified the goal to double productivity per resource within two years and emphasized the strategic focus on regaining home loan market share.
*   **PLR Cut (Rajiv Mehta):** Management explained that PLR adjustments are transparent, ALCO-driven, and reflect both internal and external dynamics.

### One-line takeaway
AAVAS delivered strong Q1 FY27 growth and improved efficiency, with management focusing on productivity gains to offset expected spread compression and maintain ROA/ROE targets.
