# Acutaas Chemicals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 59.1% YoY and successful commercialization of the battery chemicals plant, despite geopolitical headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 329.7 crore (+59.1% YoY).
*   **Gross Profit:** Rs 190.9 crore (+73% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 57.9% (+466 bps YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 113.1 crore (more than 2x YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 34.3% (+973 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 74.9 crore (+70.4% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 22.7% (+151 bps YoY).
*   **Net Cash:** Rs 314 crore (as of June 30, 2026).
*   **Working Capital:** 99 days (vs 91 days in Q4 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Battery Chemicals:** Successfully completed trial runs at the new plant and commenced commercial supply.
*   **Pharma Intermediates:** Revenue of Rs 292.7 crore (+76.5% YoY).
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Revenue of Rs 37 crore (-10.6% YoY), reflecting the planned phase-out of commodity chemicals.
*   **Certifications:** Received "Great Place to Work" and "Responsible Care" (Indian Chemical Council) certifications.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management remains confident in delivering 25% revenue growth for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management expects stable margins for FY27, similar to FY26 levels.
*   **CDMO:** Management reiterated confidence in achieving the Rs 1,000 crore revenue target for the CDMO business.
*   **Indichem (Semiconductor):** Capex expected to be completed by end of Q2 FY27; revenue contribution expected from next financial year.
*   **Battery Chemicals:** Management expects rapid ramp-up, targeting full capacity utilization within 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Battery Chemicals:** Demand is "unprecedented" due to tight global supply.
*   **Semiconductor Chemicals:** AI is driving structural demand for CPUs and memory chips, which in turn drives demand for materials manufactured at BFC and planned for Indichem.
*   **CDMO:** Healthy RFP activity and product pipeline.
*   **Pharma Intermediates:** Growth driven by top products and new products gaining volume traction.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Gross margin expansion driven by higher contribution from Pharma Intermediates; EBITDA margin expansion driven by gross margin and operational efficiencies.
*   **Employee Cost:** Management guided for ~Rs 150 crore for FY27 (includes annual increments and one-time performance bonuses).
*   **Other Expenses:** In line with previous quarters; savings realized from solar project.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Q1 FY27 Capex:** Rs 56 crore (Rs 15 crore at Indichem, Rs 41 crore at ACL site/battery project/pilot plant).
*   **Future Capex:** Planning for a new R&D center and land acquisition for future Pharma/Chemical capacity expansion.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Sachin (83%), Ankleshwar (23%), Jhagadia (55%).
*   **Battery Chemicals Capacity:** 2,000 MT VC + 2,000 MT FEC (4,000 MT total).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Increase to 99 days driven by higher inventory days, partially offset by improved debtors and creditors.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Tensions in the Gulf are impacting raw material supply and logistics.
*   **Transition:** Potential transition gap in Specialty Chemicals between phasing out old products and ramping up new ones.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Battery Chemicals:** Management confirmed multiple customers across North America and Korea; clarified they will stick to electrolyte additives and have no plans to enter electrolyte solution/LiPF business.
*   **CDMO Strategy:** Management declined to share specific molecule details due to confidentiality but reiterated confidence in the product pipeline and the Rs 1,000 crore revenue target.
*   **Value Chain:** Management reiterated their policy of not moving into API manufacturing to avoid competing with their own customers.
*   **Indichem:** Construction is ahead of schedule; management expects a "slow start" but full capacity utilization within 3-4 years.
*   **Other Income:** Management explained the dip in other income (Rs 1.8 crore vs Rs 11 crore in previous quarter) was due to lower exchange fluctuation gains.

### One-line takeaway
Acutaas delivered strong Q1 growth driven by Pharma Intermediates, while successfully initiating commercial supply in battery chemicals and progressing on semiconductor expansion.
