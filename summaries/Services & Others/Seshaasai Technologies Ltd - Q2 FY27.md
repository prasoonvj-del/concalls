# Seshaasai Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy year-on-year growth and sequential recovery in profitability and margins.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 4,053.1 million (Up 15% YoY, 7.7% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 revenue from operations:** Rs. 7,817.8 million (Up 17.9% YoY)
- **Gross profit:** Rs. 1,724.4 million 
- **Gross margin:** 42.5% (Up 89 bps QoQ, down 331 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 1,065.6 million (Up 12% YoY, 12.9% QoQ)
- **EBITDA margin:** 26.3% (Up 121 bps QoQ)
- **Operating EBITDA (excluding other income):** Rs. 1,006.2 million (Margin of 24.8%, up 7% YoY, 15.2% QoQ)
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs. 697.3 million (Up 21% YoY, 15.6% QoQ)
- **PAT margin:** 17.2%
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 2,009.7 million (Margin of 25.7%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 1,300.7 million (Margin of 16.6%)
- **Cash and liquid investments:** Rs. 3,144 million
- **Net cash:** Rs. 2,407 million
- **Unutilized IPO proceeds:** Rs. 1,196.1 million (Cumulative utilization of Rs. 4,803.9 million)
- **IoT revenue growth:** 45.6% YoY to Rs. 551.9 million
- **Payment Solutions revenue:** Rs. 2,182.6 million (Up 21.7% YoY, 38% QoQ)
- **Communication and Fulfillment Solutions revenue:** Rs. 1,290.1 million

### Highlights
- Payment Solutions driven by higher payment card volumes across banks, metal cards growth, mass transit programs, and exports.
- Won one multi-year tender in Payment Solutions; retained wallet share with top customers.
- Renewed a 3-year contract with a leading PSU bank for fulfillment solutions and a 3-year contract with a leading PSU life insurer.
- Added one new enterprise account in Communication and Fulfillment Solutions.
- Filed 2 new patent applications (total 21 cumulative filings, 5 granted); subsidiary Atoll Solutions filed 4 new patents (total 7 cumulative filings, 2 granted).
- Cumulative IPO utilization includes Rs. 849.2 million for capex, Rs. 3,000 million for debt repayment, Rs. 343.7 million for issue expenses, and Rs. 611 million for general corporate purposes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the expectation that H2 will be better than H1, supported by customer card volume pipelines, premium/metal card execution, transit program execution, and volume ramp-up in the IoT/SIM business.
- Management targets FY27 IoT revenue to grow 45% YoY over FY26 IoT revenue.
- eSIM revenue has not been factored into this year's business plan; qualification expected to finish in the next six months to set the platform for next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Payment Solutions growth broad-based, led by mass transit programs, debit cards, metal cards, and exports to Europe and Africa.
- IoT revenue moderation QoQ reflected retail sector seasonality and normalization after a large retail customer migration in Q1 (where legacy inventory was bulk-tagged).
- Exploring new IoT/RFID verticals: scaling work in renewable space, eyewear space (revenue begun), FMCG, pharma, jewelry, and cosmetics (POCs/pilots ongoing).
- Potential introduction of MDR on high-value/chargeable UPI transactions is viewed by management as a factor with a bias in favor of card usage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input material costs increased faster than revenue YoY due to elevated USD-INR, geopolitical situations, fuel prices impacting logistics, and petrochemical input costs.
- Gross margin expanded 89 bps QoQ to 42.5% due to operating leverage and a favorable product mix.
- Finance costs declined 74.9% YoY due to debt repayment.
- Management notes price hikes are being passed on where possible or factored into upcoming contract renewals.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up a new metal card facility (3x current capacity) to be operational very soon.
- Consistent infrastructure and equipment investments continuing through the year across verticals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with cash and liquid investments of Rs. 3,144 million and net cash of Rs. 2,407 million.
- Inventory buildup maintained to insulate against supply chain shocks, memory chip price volatility, holiday lead times, and to support SIM card vertical expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated USD-INR exchange rate and geopolitical headwinds impacting logistics and input material costs.
- High lead times for semiconductor supplies and supply chain unreliability during December-January.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Segment split for Payment Solutions:** Management noted export revenue in H1 is close to 5% of the total payment card business, while declining to give exact revenue share for the mass transit program.
- **IoT trackability & Seasonality:** Management explained that Q1 typically sees higher preparation volumes for festive seasons in retail, creating a predictable annual seasonality pattern.
- **Input price pressures & Margins:** Management stated that Q2 inventory consumption reflects normalized rupee levels to a great extent, and Q3 will reflect current state realities.
- **Inventory buildup rationale:** Management clarified that holding higher inventory mitigates semiconductor lead-time risks and ensures supply agility for new SIM card customers.
- **eSIM commercialization timeline:** Management noted eSIM qualification with telcos and module integration testing by end-customers takes about six months, with commercial revenue targeted for the next financial year.

### One-line takeaway
Seshaasai Technologies delivered 15% YoY revenue growth and sequential margin recovery in Q2 FY27, anchored by strong Payment Solutions and IoT expansion, while maintaining a net cash balance sheet.
