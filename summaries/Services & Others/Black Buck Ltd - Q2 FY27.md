# Black Buck Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong compounding growth across core and emerging businesses, with management highlighting successful scaling of new initiatives despite seasonal headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained vehicle finance EBITDA break-even target for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious" regarding macro headwinds in Q1 to "business as usual" for Q2, noting that seasonal rain-related weakness was compensated by strong performance.
- **New topics:** Introduction of "pre-debit notification" (PDN) strategy to mitigate potential MDR impact on UPI transactions for tolling.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Continued aggressive investment in Super Loads (14 cities maintained) and telematics (record device sales achieved).

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 253 crore (51% YoY growth).
*   **Revenue from Operations:** 56% YoY growth (15% QoQ).
*   **Net Revenues:** Rs 183 crore (35% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 51 crore (40% YoY growth).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 55 crore (30% YoY growth).
*   **PAT:** Rs 41.4 crore (42% YoY growth).
*   **Transacting Customers:** ~9 lakh (13% YoY growth).
*   **Power Users:** ~4,80,000 (20% YoY growth).
*   **Tolling GTV:** Rs 7,070 crore (17% YoY growth).
*   **Growth Businesses (Loads & Finance):** 166% YoY growth (25% QoQ).
*   **Contribution Margin:** >90%.

### Highlights
*   **Telematics:** Achieved highest-ever new device sales, breaking the record set in Q1.
*   **Super Loads:** Scaling well in 14 cities; AI-driven matchmaking is significantly improving productivity and unit economics.
*   **Tolling:** Significant uptick in issuing Blackbuck Fastags via subsidiary Blackbuck TZF.
*   **Distribution:** Network remains at 10,000 touchpoints, covering 80% of districts.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Vehicle Finance:** Management reiterated the target to achieve EBITDA break-even by the end of FY27.
*   **Super Loads:** Management plans to continue aggressive investment and scaling as the playbook matures.
*   **Tolling MDR:** Management expects the impact of UPI MDR to be "significantly close to zero" by utilizing a mix of sub-Rs 2,000 transactions and pre-debit notification (PDN) mechanisms.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Seasonality:** Q2 is typically a seasonally weak quarter due to rains; however, performance remained strong.
*   **Telematics:** Growth is driven by AIS (Automotive Industry Standard) mandates across states, with new product launches like fuel sensors and dash cams contributing.
*   **Competition:** Management believes their pan-India service network and specialized focus on operational complexity provide a competitive moat against OEMs in the telematics space.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Total Expenses:** Grew 36% YoY, driven by growth in core businesses and investments in Super Loads.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Core business operating leverage is currently ~50% due to upfront investments in customer acquisition.
*   **Telematics Margin:** Telematics has a lower contribution margin compared to the core business, which impacted the overall blended margin slightly.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Super Loads:** Present in 14 cities (2 legacy, 12 newer). The business is described as "working capital light" (cash-and-carry model).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Deferred Revenue:** Continues to increase on the balance sheet due to the nature of telematics revenue recognition over the device life.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Upfront CAC for telematics and other products suppresses EBITDA in the quarter of acquisition, though it is recouped over the customer lifetime.
*   **MDR Impact:** Potential risk from UPI MDR on tolling transactions above Rs 2,000, though management believes it can be mitigated.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Telematics vs. OEMs (Prateek Kumar Jha):** Management argued that OEMs are not focused on the specialized, state-level operational requirements of AIS mandates, and their own pan-India service network provides a superior edge.
*   **On MDR Mitigation (Avnish Sharma):** Management clarified that for transactions >Rs 2,000, they will nudge customers toward the "zero-PDN mandate" route to avoid MDR.
*   **On Tolling GTV Growth (Gaurav Malhotra):** Management noted that GTV is indexed to industry movement and expects growth to pick up in Q3 and Q4, consistent with historical seasonal trends.
*   **On Depreciation (Avnish Sharma):** Management stated that depreciation is linked to telematics growth and will follow the trajectory of device sales; no specific guidance provided.

### One-line takeaway
BlackBuck delivered robust 50%+ revenue growth and maintained strong profitability, successfully balancing aggressive investments in new growth engines (Super Loads/Telematics) with a high-margin core business.
