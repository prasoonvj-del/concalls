# True Colors Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported revenue growth in line with guidance and profits growing at twice the rate of revenue, supported by a higher share of high-margin consumables and positive operating cash flow.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 183.01 crore (up 21.1% YoY from Rs 151.11 crore; up 21.7% QoQ from H2 FY26).
- **Gross Margin:** 30.2% (expanded by 405 bps YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 32.34 crore (up 40.1% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17.7% (improved by 240 bps YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 21.09 crore (up 43.3% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 11.5% (rising by 178 bps YoY).
- **Sublimation Paper Volume:** 9.97 crore meters (more than doubled from 4.92 crore meters in H1 FY26).
- **Paper Revenue:** Rs 51.81 crore (up 79% YoY; accounts for 28% of total revenue).
- **Ink Volume (INKIA):** 689 tons (up 22% YoY; supplying >150 tons a month).
- **Digital Printing Fabrics Volume:** 99.71 lakh meters (up 7% YoY).
- **Digital Printing Fabrics Revenue:** Rs 49.12 crore (up 25% YoY).
- **Machine Installations:** 56 units in H1 FY27 vs 68 units in H1 FY26.
- **Active Installed Base:** 956 machines (up 11% YoY).
- **Recurring Revenue (Ink & Paper):** 56.2% of total revenue (up from 48.7% a year ago).
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 27.16 crore in H1 FY27 (vs outflow of Rs 10.90 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Trade Receivables:** Rs 116.03 crore.
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** Rs 21.64 crore as of September end (up from Rs 1.27 crore in March).
- **Ink Capacity Expansion (INKIA Phase-1):** 150 tons a month planned by end of FY27 at a capex of Rs 40-45 crore. Phase-2 targets 500 tons a month in FY28, with a long-term goal of 1,000 tons a month.
- **Paper JV Project Cost:** Rs 320-340 crore for an integrated capacity of approximately 8 to 10 crore meters of coated media per month (funding: 25% equity, 75% project debt; commercial production targeted for H2 FY29).

### Highlights
- Delivered first-half revenue growth in line with guidance of >20%.
- Sublimation paper coating capacity of 2 crore meters a month is running at optimum utilization (up from 30 lakh meters 3 years ago).
- Partnerships with ITACA of Spain added premium pigment ink, with commercial supplies beginning in H1.
- Started exporting ink and printed fabric to the UAE market.
- Entered a 50/50 joint venture with Swastik Strategic Ventures LLP named **True Colors Advanced Materials Private Limited** to manufacture specialized lower GSM-coated media from pulp.
- Both the ink manufacturing plant and the paper JV project qualify for subsidies under the Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 (35% capital subsidy for ink, 25% capital subsidy for the paper JV).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Management maintained the full-year FY27 guidance of 20% to 22% top-line growth.
- **Margins:** Management stated they will try to achieve the current half-year's profitability levels in H2 FY26/H2 FY27.
- **IRR:** Management targets an IRR of approximately 3 to 3.5 years for the paper project (though it could be achieved earlier).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global digital textile printing market is expected to nearly double from USD 3.89 billion in 2025 to USD 7.63 billion by 2030.
- Digital printing accounts for only 8% to 9% of textile printing in India compared to 80% to 95% in Europe.
- India currently remains significantly dependent on imports for lower GSM sublimation media and digital printing inks (sourcing almost all digital printing ink from China, Japan, and Europe).
- True Colors estimates it accounts for 16% to 18% of India's digital fabric printing volume.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Global price correction in ink prices has led to lower average realizations per meter despite volume growth.
- Machine sales carry thin margins (gross margin of roughly 5%), acting as a feeder for high-margin consumables (ink and paper).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Ink Manufacturing (INKIA Phase-1):** Capex of Rs 40-45 crore, capacity of 150 tons/month by end of FY27.
- **Paper JV (True Colors Advanced Materials Private Limited):** Capex of Rs 320-340 crore (25% equity, 75% debt), capacity of 8-10 crore meters/month of 29 GSM coated media from pulp at Bardoli, Gujarat; targeted for commercial production in H2 FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade payables pressure eased after moving to domestic MSME suppliers with 45-day payment terms.
- Normal payment cycle for trade receivables runs around 90 to 120 days.
- Funding for future projects may involve internal accruals, project-level debt, strategic JV capital, and potentially equity if it creates long-term shareholder value.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Trade receivables have expanded significantly over prior years alongside revenue growth (reported at Rs 116.03 crore in H1 FY27), though management stated receivables are under control and aligned with industry norms.
- Import dependency for lower GSM substrates and raw materials.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS) on Receivables:** Asked why receivables have scaled significantly compared to historical years. Management responded that receivables are in control, standing at Rs 116.03 crore against revenue of Rs 183.01 crore, with payment cycles at normal industry norms of 90 to 120 days.
2. **Vishal Prasad (Raj Mahal Capital) on Paper Backward Integration:** Questioned the rationale of entering the capital-intensive paper manufacturing business from pulp rather than importing. Management explained that India faces technological and supply constraints in sourcing ultra-low GSM base paper (29 GSM) needed for coating, and backward integration secures the ecosystem for future growth.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) on Ink Realizations & Margins:** Asked why ink revenue lagged volume growth and whether in-house manufacturing improves margins. Management attributed it to global price corrections in ink, and stated that manufacturing will directionally improve margins once INKIA's merger is finalized.
4. **Naveen (Individual Investor) on INKIA Sourcing Mix:** Asked how much ink is sourced from INKIA. Management stated that INKIA currently produces ~80 tons/month (supplying 35-40% to True Colors and the rest to other distributors), and will supply 100% of True Colors' consumption once capacity expands to 250-500 tons.
5. **Kaushal Aggarwal (Veera Bharat Opportunities Fund) on JV Partner Background:** Inquired about Swastik Strategic Ventures LLP. Management stated the partner is a Surat-based group primarily experienced in textile weaving, infrastructure, and construction, and will handle operational activities while True Colors focuses on market development.
6. **Rohan (Individual Investor) on INKIA Merger Valuation:** Asked about the valuation parameters for the INKIA merger. Management stated INKIA was valued at 15x FY '25 PAT (based on Rs 4 crore PAT on Rs 24 crore revenue), translating to a valuation of Rs 60 crore at a share price of Rs 240, subject to NCLT and regulatory approvals.

### One-line takeaway
True Colors delivered robust H1 FY27 results with profits outpacing revenue growth due to a higher share of high-margin consumables, while aggressively pursuing backward integration into ink manufacturing and a large-scale paper joint venture.
