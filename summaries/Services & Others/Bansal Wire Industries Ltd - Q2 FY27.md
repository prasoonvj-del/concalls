# Bansal Wire Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record-high revenue, EBITDA, and profit, citing successful recovery from Q1 operational disruptions and strong operational efficiency.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 20% YoY volume growth target for H2 FY27 (despite Q1 losses).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "recovering from disruption" to "landmark quarter" with record profitability; demand remains healthy across sectors.
- **New topics/risks:** Flagged acute raw material (wire rod) shortages in October due to supplier production shifts to TMT and plant breakdowns; previously, the focus was on gas supply disruptions.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on margin recovery (achieved ~Rs 8,000/tonne EBITDA vs Q1's blended Rs 4-4.5/kg); Dadri facility ramp-up is contributing to economies of scale as promised.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,308 crore (up 24% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 105 crore (up 29% YoY).
- **Net Profit:** Rs 53 crore (up 38% YoY).
- **Sales Volume:** 129,000 metric tonnes (vs 114,609 metric tonnes in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 2,476 crore.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 162 crore.
- **H1 FY27 Net Profit:** Rs 73 crore.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 218 crore (Q2); Rs 339 crore (H1).
- **Debt:** Reduced by 40% in H1; current debt ~Rs 300 crore (Debt-to-Equity ~0.2).
- **ROCE:** ~22% (annualized for Q2).

### Highlights
- **Record Performance:** Highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and EBITDA per tonne.
- **Specialty Wires:** IHT wires reached positive EBITDA; OHT capacity expansion to 6,000 tonnes is underway.
- **Steel Cord:** Received first trial order last quarter; expecting second trial order within 10-15 days. Aiming for commercialization within FY27.
- **B2C Business:** Contributed 10-12% of total volumes; introduced products like grape trellis and poultry cage wires.
- **Stock Split:** Rationale was to increase retail shareholder base and liquidity.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Volume Growth:** Targeting 20% YoY volume growth for H2 FY27.
- **EBITDA Margin:** Targeting Rs 7,000–7,500 per tonne for the year; long-term target is Rs 8,000–10,000 per tonne as specialty products scale.
- **ROCE:** Near-term objective remains 25%.
- **Cash Flow:** Targeting cumulative operating cash flow of Rs 800 crore over FY27 and FY28 combined.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Sectoral Demand:** Healthy demand across automotive, infra, agriculture, power, and transmission.
- **Steel Cord Market:** Sees significant opportunity as domestic demand (2.5–3 lakh tonnes) is largely met by imports; radialization in truck/bus segment (~60%) supports growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **EBITDA per tonne:** Reported ~Rs 8,000/tonne for the quarter; includes ~Rs 200/tonne inventory gain.
- **Raw Materials:** Facing acute short-term shortages of wire rods due to supplier diversion to TMT and supplier plant breakdowns; management expects this to be a temporary "blip."
- **Gas Costs:** Prices are ~2.5x higher than historical levels, but costs are passed on to customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Dadri Facility:** Brownfield expansion of 60,000 tonnes (low/high carbon) nearing completion; expected to be capitalized by end of Q4.
- **Sanand Facility:** Greenfield project for stainless steel and low carbon wires; capex of ~Rs 150 crore (excluding land).
- **Total Capacity:** Targeting increase from 6.8 lakh tonnes to ~8.3 lakh tonnes by end of next year.
- **Annual Capex:** Indicative range of Rs 200–225 crore, funded via internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Working Capital:** Strong focus on optimization; generated Rs 339 crore OCF in H1.
- **Inventory:** Maintains ~1.5 months of inventory.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Raw Material Availability:** Short-term supply constraints in October impacting production.
- **Steel Cord Ramp-up:** Initial commercialization phase expected to be slow (first 2 quarters); management avoids over-reliance on single customers (caps supply at 20-25% per customer).
- **LRPC:** Currently a low-margin product with excess industry capacity; contributes <1% of sales.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Is the Rs 8,000/tonne EBITDA sustainable?** A: Management clarified that while they achieved Rs 8,000, their guidance remains conservative at Rs 7,000–7,500/tonne.
- **Q: Why the raw material shortage?** A: Suppliers diverted production to TMT; management expects this to be temporary and does not see it impacting the 20% volume growth target.
- **Q: Status of Steel Cord capacity?** A: Currently 20,000 tonnes (pilot); goal is 2 lakh tonnes. Expansion will trigger upon customer approvals.
- **Q: Why is LRPC in the portfolio if it's <1% of sales?** A: Management stated they have 3,500 products; diversification ensures consistency even if some products underperform.
- **Q: Will you hit 20% volume growth despite Q1?** A: Yes, the 20% target is YoY for H2; management is confident in overcoming temporary supply blips.

### One-line takeaway
Bansal Wire delivered a record-breaking quarter with strong cash generation and margin expansion, and despite short-term raw material supply constraints, management remains confident in achieving its 20% volume growth target for the second half of the year.
