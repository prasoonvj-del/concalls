# Krystal Integrated Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and PAT growth, alongside strategic business expansions and order wins.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 414.47 crores (up 46.25% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 775.18 crores (up 27.82% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 18.67 crores (up 41.66% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 36.04 crores (up 22.09% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (without other income):** Rs. 24.86 crores (up 38.82% YoY) with a margin of 6% (vs 6.32% in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 47.66 crores (up 21.39% YoY) with a margin of 6.15% (vs 6.47% in H1 FY26)
- **Net Debt (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs. 63.84 crores (Borrowings of Rs. 144.64 crores, Cash and bank balance of Rs. 80.80 crores)
- **Net Worth:** Rs. 539.09 crores
- **Trade Receivable Days:** ~118 days
- **Effective Tax Rate (Q2 FY27):** 12.5% (vs 16.6% in Q2 FY26)
- **Customer Metrics:** Served >540 customers across >4,700 locations with 49,971 on-site employees as of Sept 30, 2026; added 59 new corporate customers in H1 FY27 (31 in Q2).

### Highlights
- Secured major contracts including Rs. 26 crores from South-East Central Railway Bilaspur Division, Rs. 134 crores from MSRTC, Rs. 296 crores (40% share via LC Infra-Krystal Consortium) for sewage treatment and networks under Swachh Bharat Mission Urban 2.0, Rs. 33 crores and Rs. 6.6 crores from Maha Mumbai Metro, Rs. 27.6 crores from Andhra Pradesh Backward Classes Welfare Department, Rs. 17 crores from Maharashtra Rajya Sahakari Sangh, Rs. 6.6 crores from Shri Khatu Shyam Mandir Committee, and Rs. 8.75 crores from Kosol Energie.
- Tender evaluation team evaluated tenders worth >Rs. 12,000 crores and participated in bids worth >Rs. 4,500 crores.
- Completed 100% acquisition of Citelum India to enter city lighting and urban infrastructure.
- Secured first solar EPC order from DMER (15-year contract).
- Introduced technical facility management as a separate segment.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets annual revenues of Rs. 300-350 crores from the Citelum vertical over the next 3-4 years, supported by a planned investment of ~Rs. 100 crores.
- Management aspires to keep EBITDA margins in the zone of 6.25% to 7.5%.
- Confident in delivering a strong revenue number at the end of March 31, 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian facility management industry growing at ~8% to 9% annually.
- Shift towards integrated facility management contracts.
- Formal flexi-staffing market estimated at ~Rs. 2.2 lakh crores in FY26 by Indian Staffing Federation; formal workforce expected to increase from 7.2 million in FY25 to 9.2 million by FY27.
- Implementation of four labour codes on Nov 21, 2025, expected to favour organized players with strong compliance.
- Swachh Bharat Mission Urban 2.0 has an outlay of ~Rs. 1.41 lakh crores.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin moderated by 32 bps YoY due to employee costs rising to 75.9% of Q2 revenue (vs 72.3% last year) driven by scaling new contracts and 33 new customer additions in Q2, partly offset by lower material and other expenses.
- Initial investment and stabilization phase of newly acquired large contracts temporarily depress margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned investment of ~Rs. 100 crores in Citelum for engineering capabilities, technology platforms, and business expansion.
- Working to increase solid waste processing capacity from ~400 tons per day (TPD) to 800-1000 TPD.
- Evaluates inorganic growth opportunities with startups having O&M edges, robotics manufacturing, and AI-related matters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs. 63.84 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- Trade receivable days maintained at ~118 days.
- Revenue composition split remains around 60%-65% government and 35%-40% corporate.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Transition regarding new labour codes requires industry-wide adjustments.
- Initial startup phase of new contracts attracts higher working capital and expenses.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash Parker (Patel Investments) asked about the reasons for EBITDA margin contraction despite high revenue growth.** Management explained that initial investments and stabilization costs for newly acquired larger contracts temporarily increase expenses, lowering margins initially before they stabilize.
- **Yash Parker asked about the Citelum execution model and targets.** Management reiterated a target of Rs. 300-350 crores over 3-4 years, noting they acquired existing pre-qualifications, capabilities, and the captive team, operating an asset-light model for project design, installation, and O&M.
- **Myra Mittal asked about operational measures to improve EBITDA margins.** Management stated they target a 6.25% to 7.5% band through operational efficiencies, cost rationalization, and cross-selling multiple services to single customers.
- **Vikrant Sahu asked about the proportion of government vs. corporate clients in receivables and revenue.** Management stated the composition is around 60%-65% government and 35%-40% corporate, with outstanding receivables following a similar ratio.
- **Krisha asked if management expects to recover statutory employee costs through price revisions.** Management answered that they are working closely with customers, some have already started paying, and communication is underway to recover costs from others.

### One-line takeaway
Krystal Integrated Services delivered robust H1 revenue growth of 27.82% and PAT growth of 22.09%, while absorbing margin dilution from scaling new contracts and expanding aggressively into adjacent verticals like city lighting (Citelum), solid waste management, and solar EPC.
