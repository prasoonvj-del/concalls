# Aurum PropTech Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights the third consecutive quarter of profitability, improved margins, and the strategic acquisition of Housing.com.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 121 crore (up 57% YoY).
*   **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 111.84 crore (vs Rs 65.44 crore in Q1 FY26, up 70.9%).
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 2.31 crore (vs loss of Rs 10.81 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PBT Margin:** 2.1% (vs -16.52% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   Rental: Rs 54.35 crore revenue (EBITDA profit Rs 0.63 crore).
    *   Distribution: Rs 55.94 crore revenue (EBITDA profit Rs 7.97 crore).
    *   Capital: Rs 1.55 crore revenue (loss Rs 0.05 crore).
*   **Discontinued Operations:** Rs 54.14 crore revenue and Rs 52.40 crore PBT (includes Rs 112 crore building sale).
*   **Housing.com (FY25):** Revenue Rs 343 crore; Expenses Rs 531 crore; Annualized burn ~Rs 200 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Housing.com Acquisition:** Management views this as value-accretive, expanding the addressable market and providing a "pole position" in the online portal space (24% market share).
*   **AI-Native Transformation:** AI is being embedded across all five business layers (Discovery, Intelligence, Transaction, Financing, and Living) to optimize productivity and revenue per team member.
*   **Operational Metrics:** Rental business maintained 81% occupancy across 21 cities; developer platform supports 1,200+ developers across 1,50,000+ projects.
*   **Profitability:** Achieved third consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Housing.com Profitability:** Management expects to achieve profitability for Housing.com within 4-6 quarters (internal target 3-4 quarters).
*   **Strategic Goal:** Management aims to become India’s leading AI-native real estate operating system.
*   **Growth:** Management expects to surpass the Rs 1,000 crore ARR target faster due to the Housing.com integration.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Size:** India's annual residential transaction value exceeds Rs 15,00,000 crore.
*   **Digital Penetration:** Online portals currently capture only ~Rs 1,200 crore market, representing <5% penetration.
*   **Housing.com Reach:** Attracts 58 million average monthly visits and 12 million monthly active users.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Expansion:** PBT margins expanded by 1,590 bps YoY; adjusted EBITDA margins improved by 1,320 bps YoY.
*   **Efficiency:** Management plans to streamline Housing.com’s cost structure, citing opportunities in marketing expenditure and reducing "top-heavy" management layers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **War Chest:** Promoters are subscribing to shares, creating a ~Rs 200 crore war chest for Housing.com operations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Tax:** Management is evaluating the impact of carry-forward tax losses and will provide clarity on effective tax rates in Q3.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Integration:** Management acknowledged the challenge of integrating Housing.com, specifically regarding cultural alignment and operational restructuring.
*   **Losses:** Housing.com is currently loss-making with an annualized burn of ~Rs 200 crore.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Housing.com Integration:** Management stated the priority is to increase the revenue pool by moving beyond "intent monetization" to transaction fulfillment and upselling existing SKUs.
*   **Dilution Concerns:** Management defended the dilution to REA Group as strategic, noting that REA is the largest PropTech group globally and provides significant intangible value.
*   **QoQ Revenue Dip:** Management explained the QoQ revenue decline was due to seasonality in the distribution business, where revenue recognition is linked to annual slab-based targets achieved later in the year.
*   **Cannibalization:** Management denied risks of internal cannibalization between Housing.com and Aurum’s existing lead generation models, citing strong synergies.
*   **Housing.com Burn:** Management confirmed an annualized burn of ~Rs 200 crore and reiterated the 4-6 quarter timeline to reach break-even.

### One-line takeaway
Aurum PropTech delivered a profitable quarter and is pivoting toward an AI-native model, with the Housing.com acquisition serving as the primary catalyst for scaling operations and achieving long-term profitability within 4-6 quarters.
