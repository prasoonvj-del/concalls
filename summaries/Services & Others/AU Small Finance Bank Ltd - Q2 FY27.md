# AU Small Finance Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in deposits and loans, improved profitability metrics (RoA/RoE), and stable asset quality despite macro headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained. Management reiterated a target of 25%+ deposit growth and a long-term through-the-cycle credit cost of 90-100 bps.
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone remains confident. Management noted that while domestic activity remains healthy, system liquidity is tightening and interest rates are rising, a shift from the previous quarter's focus on high competition.
- **New topics or risks:** Management highlighted the potential impact of an insurance fee income consultation paper as a headwind. ICRA upgraded the bank's credit rating outlook to Positive.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The bank continued its expansion, adding 45 deposit branches and 100+ asset centers in Q2, consistent with the goal of building a pan-India franchise.

### Key numbers
- **PAT:** Rs 858 crore (+53% YoY).
- **Deposits:** Rs 1.71 lakh crore (approx. based on 29% YoY growth); +29% YoY, +8% QoQ.
- **Loan Portfolio:** +25% YoY, +7% QoQ.
- **NIM:** 5.8% (-9 bps QoQ).
- **CASA Ratio:** 29.5% (+72 bps QoQ).
- **GNPA:** 1.99% (-42 bps YoY).
- **Credit Cost:** 0.8% (-45 bps YoY).
- **RoA:** 1.7% (vs 1.4% YoY); **RoE:** 16.1% (vs 12.4% YoY).
- **PPoP:** Rs 1,480 crore (+31% YoY).
- **Provisions:** Rs 359 crore (-25% YoY).

### Highlights
- **Expansion:** Added ~2,400 employees and 45 new deposit branches. Inaugurated new corporate office in Mumbai.
- **Technology:** Agentic AI platform and Cross Lead Platform enabled ~5% of total disbursements; score-card led underwriting delivered Rs 500 crore+ in unsecured loans.
- **Rating:** ICRA upgraded credit rating outlook to Positive from Stable.
- **Wheels Business:** Book crossed Rs 51,000 crore (+27% YoY).
- **Gold Loan:** Grew 102% YoY to ~Rs 4,700 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth:** Management expects to grow deposits at 25%+ YoY.
- **Credit Cost:** Long-term through-the-cycle guidance remains 90-100 bps.
- **Efficiency:** Management expects the cost-to-assets ratio to improve on a full-year basis.
- **Margins:** Management expects to navigate the NIM cycle by leveraging a 30% floating-rate book and a more granular deposit franchise.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Macro:** Operating environment influenced by geopolitical risks and monetary policy tightening (MPC raised policy rate by 25 bps to 5.5%).
- **Liquidity:** System liquidity is drying up; rates are rising, which may cap further improvement in cost of funds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margins:** Sequential NIM decline of 9 bps driven by higher share of investments to total assets and seasonality.
- **Cost of Funds:** Declined 7 bps QoQ.
- **Opex:** Increased 26% YoY due to investments in distribution, brand, and technology.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Commercial Banking:** Grew 39% YoY to ~Rs 35,300 crore.
- **Inclusive Banking (MFI):** Grew 23% YoY; 99% of book covered under CGFMU guarantee scheme.
- **Floating Rate Book:** ~30% of total loan book.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Macro:** Potential impact of drought/El Niño on rural segments (though no stress observed yet).
- **Regulatory:** Potential impact of insurance fee income consultation paper.
- **Competition:** Tightening deposit competition; management plans to maintain an 80-100 bps premium on term deposits vs peers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why the decline in NIMs?** Management attributed it to higher liquidity/investment book and seasonality (day count impact), noting that loan spreads remained stable.
- **Q: Is the 29% deposit growth sustainable?** Management stated the goal is to grow in line with nominal GDP (approx. 25%+), noting that the current growth is driven by granular, relationship-led acquisition rather than one-offs.
- **Q: Why the slippage in Q2?** Management clarified that slippages are seasonal due to tractor billing cycles (June/December) and are not comparable to Q1; they emphasized looking at YoY trends.
- **Q: Strategy for mortgage growth?** Management clarified they are not chasing affordable housing growth due to competitive intensity and cost of funds, but will focus on cross-selling to existing customers.
- **Q: Will you change deposit rates?** Management stated they will maintain an 80-100 bps premium to peers to build the franchise, regardless of market-driven events.

### One-line takeaway
AU Small Finance Bank delivered a robust Q2 with 53% YoY PAT growth and improved return ratios, successfully leveraging technology and geographic expansion to drive granular deposit growth despite a tightening liquidity environment.
