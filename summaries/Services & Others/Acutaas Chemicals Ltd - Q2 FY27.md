# Acutaas Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and margin expansion, with management raising full-year guidance and accelerating CDMO targets.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised revenue growth guidance to ~30% (from 25% in Q1). Raised EBITDA margin guidance to 38%-40% (previously "stable").
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "stable" to "strong" outlook; CDMO target of Rs 1,000 crore moved from FY28 to FY27 (one year ahead of schedule).
- **New topics:** Inauguration of South Korea semiconductor plant (ahead of schedule).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Battery chemicals commercial revenue started (delivered).

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 4,369 million (+42.7% YoY).
*   **Gross Profit:** Rs 2,715 million (+59% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 62.1% (+637 bps YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 1,760 million (+84.6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 40.3% (+914 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 1,231 million (+71.4% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 28.2% (+472 bps YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 7,666 million (+49.3% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 2,890 million (+97.7% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 1,981 million (+71% YoY).
*   **Net Cash:** Rs 3,009 million (as of Sept 30, 2026).

### Highlights
*   **Pharma Intermediates:** Revenue of Rs 4,020 million (+53.1% YoY).
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Revenue of Rs 349 million (-20% YoY), impacted by phasing out of legacy commodity products and a temporary shipment pause in BFC.
*   **CDMO:** Achieved Rs 1,000 crore revenue target one year early (FY27). Management set a new target of Rs 2,000 crore by FY30.
*   **Battery Chemicals:** First quarter of commercial revenue; 3rd product commercialized, 4th product in pilot-scale validation.
*   **Semiconductor:** South Korea plant inaugurated ahead of schedule; currently submitting qualification batches to customers.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Revised to ~30% for FY27.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 38%-40% for FY27.
*   **CDMO:** Rs 1,000 crore in FY27; Rs 2,000 crore by FY30.
*   **Electronic-grade chemicals:** Production start expected by H1 FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Pharma Intermediates:** Demand remains robust; cost pressures are being passed on.
*   **Battery Chemicals:** Demand continues to exceed supply.
*   **BFC:** Experienced a temporary shipment pause due to the acquisition of a U.S. customer; operations have since resumed.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** Expansion driven by higher contribution from the Pharma Intermediate business.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Expansion driven by gross margin improvement and operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **H1 FY27 Capex:** Rs 1,239 million.
*   **FY27 Total Capex:** Guided at ~Rs 3,000 million.
*   **H2 FY27 Planned Capex:** Rs 1,650 million (Rs 1,000 million for electronic-grade chemicals, Rs 450 million for electrolyte additive phase 2, remainder for maintenance).
*   **Ankleshwar Unit Utilization:** 30% in Q2 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** 115 days (vs 99 days in Q1 FY27), driven by higher debtor days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Ongoing tensions impacting supply chain and logistics.
*   **Semiconductor Ecosystem:** Potential delays in the development of the Indian semiconductor ecosystem could impact local implementation.
*   **Competition:** Management noted that competition must be considered when evaluating growth opportunities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **CDMO Pipeline:** Management confirmed 8-10 products are in the pipeline but declined to provide product-wise revenue splits.
*   **Battery Chemicals Pricing:** Management clarified they have long-term contracts with price revisions based on raw materials/FX, rather than riding spot price volatility.
*   **Electronic Chemicals:** Management clarified this refers to "bulk electronic-grade chemical and solvent products," not just battery or semiconductor-specific chemicals.
*   **Semiconductor Validation:** Management emphasized that validation is a long-term, robust process; current focus is on Korea, Japan, and Taiwan markets.
*   **Darolutamide:** Management declined to comment on specific product potential or wallet share, noting it is an innovator-driven product.

### One-line takeaway
Acutaas delivered strong Q2 growth driven by Pharma Intermediates and CDMO, with management raising FY27 revenue and margin guidance while accelerating long-term CDMO targets.
