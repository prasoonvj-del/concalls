# ACME Solar Holdings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and EBITDA growth driven by BESS operations and capacity additions, with management raising long-term growth targets.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised FY27 BESS commissioning target from 10 GWh to >12 GWh. Raised 2030 renewable target from 10 GW (with 25 GWh BESS) to 15 GW (with 50 GWh BESS).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted to a more aggressive growth stance; management emphasized that BESS is now a "cash engine" and a "new form of power" replacing RTC thermal procurement.
- **New topics/risks:** Discussed the impact of Chinese export taxes (6%) on battery supply chains; clarified the "one-time" nature of ROC expenses (Rs 2.5 crore per SPV) that impacted previous quarters.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on BESS commissioning (5.2 GWh cumulative, meeting the 5 GWh half-yearly target).

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** Rs 1,007 crore (+67.5% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 874 crore (+63.6% YoY).
- **Blended EBITDA Margin:** 86.7% (Renewable: 90.8%; BESS: 78.3%).
- **PAT:** Rs 269 crore (+133.5% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 26.7%.
- **Asset Base:** Rs 28,462 crore (growth of Rs 11,466 crore YoY).
- **CUF:** 27.8% (vs 24.1% YoY).
- **Power Generation:** 1,840 million units (+19.6% YoY).
- **BESS Short-term Sales:** 324 million units.
- **Debt:** Operational debt book ~Rs 15,000 crore; weighted average cost of debt ~8.4%.

### Highlights
- **BESS Leadership:** Commissioned 5.2 GWh of BESS capacity to date (second largest in India).
- **Contracting:** Signed 430 MW of FDRE PPAs during the quarter, plus an additional 400 MW with NTPC post-quarter; 92% of 8,370 MW portfolio now tied up.
- **Revenue Visibility:** Locked in >Rs 1,600 crore for partial FY27 capacity and >Rs 1,500 crore for FY28 via short-term BESS contracts.
- **Regulatory/Policy:** Benefiting from CTUIL non-ROFR BESS connectivity, Green Energy Corridor Phase III, and the Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana (floating solar).
- **Rating:** CRISIL upgraded outlook to AA- positive from AA- stable.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 BESS:** >12 GWh commissioning target.
- **FY27 Generation:** Targeting 1.5 GW capacity addition, potentially higher (2.5–3 GW) depending on GNA (General Network Access) timelines.
- **2030 Target:** 15 GW renewable capacity with 50 GWh BESS.
- **Capex:** Expecting higher H2 capex than H1 (H1 spend was Rs 6,500 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Shift in Procurement:** States are moving from RTC (Round-the-Clock) thermal procurement to BESS-based peak power, as RTC is often more expensive and includes unnecessary volume.
- **BESS Market:** Management sees 200–250 GWh of short-term peak power contracts expected in the near-to-medium term.
- **Bidding Environment:** SECI is floating bids only after pre-tying up power with DISCOMs, leading to faster PPA conversions (within ~2 months).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **BESS Capex:** Capitalized between Rs 90 lakh to Rs 1 crore per MWh; savings of ~Rs 20 lakh/MWh achieved by installing at existing operational substations.
- **Module Pricing:** Negotiating for future projects (post-June 2027) at <Rs 17/watt; management does not expect price increases despite potential supply chain shifts.
- **Interest Costs:** Flat QoQ due to QIP proceeds (Rs 2,800 crore) used to pay down debt and refinancing of higher-cost bonds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Execution:** 1.9 GW of contracted projects partially commissioned.
- **Battery Inventory:** 19 GWh contracted with suppliers (CATL, Hithium, Ganfeng, Trina, CLOU).
- **Connectivity:** 10 GW of RE connectivity inventory secured for future bids; 5 GW+ BESS connectivity granted.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash Position:** Significant cash balance from September disbursements, earmarked for capex.
- **Debt Structure:** 65% of borrowings from PFC, REC, IREDA, and NaBFID, limiting interest rate volatility.
- **Refinancing:** Actively refinancing under-construction projects as they reach COD to reduce interest rates to sub-9%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Transmission/GNA:** Delays in GNA commissioning remain a dependency for full project operationalization.
- **Interest Rates:** Acknowledged global/Indian interest rate environment is moving toward stabilization or slight increase, though long-term institutional debt mitigates impact.
- **Supply Chain:** Potential impact of Chinese export taxes (6%) on battery supply, though management remains confident in pricing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Q: How does the math work for the Rs 6/unit peak power tariff?* Management stated they achieve high-teen returns due to early BESS commissioning and selling power before full solar project completion.
- *Q: Why is there a mismatch between gross block increase and debt increase?* Management explained that debt is often drawn down early for battery capex (factory acceptance tests), while capitalization happens later.
- *Q: Is the 15 GW 2030 target realistic given grid connectivity challenges?* Management argued that connectivity is already planned/secured for these timelines and they are prioritizing revenue generation over absolute MW numbers.
- *Q: Are you factoring in ACME Cleantech's demand?* Management confirmed the 15 GW target excludes any captive demand from ACME Cleantech.
- *Q: Why did BESS spreads narrow?* Management clarified that tariffs for current contracts were already signed in previous quarters; new bids are seeing higher tariffs.

### One-line takeaway
ACME Solar is aggressively scaling its BESS-led "cash engine" strategy, leveraging early battery deployment to generate revenue ahead of full project commissioning while raising long-term capacity targets to 15 GW by 2030.
