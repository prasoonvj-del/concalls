# Aurum PropTech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasizes the successful acquisition of Housing.com, fourth consecutive quarter of profitability, and a clear roadmap for integration and synergy realization.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained Housing.com profitability target (12-18 months mentioned in opening remarks, though management later clarified an internal target of 4-6 quarters and external target of 6-8 quarters).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "preparing for acquisition" to "active integration." Management is now focused on execution and cost optimization post-acquisition.
- **New topics:** Detailed cost-cutting measures at Housing.com (INR 120 crore cut in first week), KuberX loan disbursement run rate (INR 2,400 crore), and utilization of INR 1,020 crore in carry-forward tax losses.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Completed Housing.com acquisition (Sept 29, 2026); successfully maintained profitability trend.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 137 crore (up 57% YoY).
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 130.2 crore (up 64% YoY, 16% QoQ).
- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 11.5% (expanded 840 bps).
- **PBT Margin:** 4.8% (improved 1,270 bps).
- **Profit After Tax (Continuing Operations):** Rs 4.3 crore.
- **Distribution Segment:** Rs 69.7 crore revenue (16.3% margin); 117,000 leads sold (up 79% YoY); Rs 838 crore home sales facilitated.
- **Rental Segment:** Rs 56.1 crore revenue; 16,500 beds at 82% occupancy; 8,900 signed units.
- **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 191.6 crore.
- **Housing.com Metrics:** 58 million+ monthly visits, 12 million monthly active users.

### Highlights
- **Housing.com Acquisition:** Completed on Sept 29, 2026, in an all-stock transaction. Management aims to make it profitable within 12-18 months (internal target 4-6 quarters).
- **Cost Optimization:** Management cut ~Rs 120 crore in costs at Housing.com within the first week of acquisition.
- **KuberX (Financing):** Launched loan business; September disbursement of Rs 200 crore (annualized run rate of Rs 2,400 crore), targeting Rs 25,000 crore in 4-6 quarters.
- **AI Initiatives:** Revenue per team member is increasing; management targets Rs 56 lakh by year-end.
- **Operational Discipline:** Fourth consecutive profitable quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Profitability:** Management targets Housing.com profitability within 4-6 quarters (internal) to 6-8 quarters (external).
- **Revenue Growth:** Management expects revenue growth to be the primary driver of Housing.com profitability, targeting an additional Rs 100-150 crore revenue growth over the next 4-6 quarters.
- **Operational Focus:** Focus for rest of FY27 is on financial metric improvement, Housing.com integration, and deepening AI application.
- **Inorganic Growth:** Management stated they are not proactively looking at any new acquisitions in the short run as "hands are full" with integration.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Market Dynamics:** Management believes that in a real estate slowdown, developers increase marketing spend to attract customers, which benefits platform companies like Aurum.
- **Broker Ecosystem:** Management aims to increase target channel partners from 18,000-19,000 to 50,000.
- **Developer Reach:** Aiming to increase developer base from 1,300+ to 4,000+ in existing geographies.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Employee Costs:** 26.2% of revenue (vs 27.9% in Q1).
- **Other Expenses:** 47.8% of revenue (vs 45% in Q1), attributed to higher variable marketing/fulfillment costs in the growing distribution business.
- **Debt:** Effectively debt-free (bank/other borrowings < Rs 1 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex:** Rs 8.8 crore (mostly capitalized product development).
- **Warrants:** Received Rs 29.5 crore from promoter (Aurum Real Estate Developers) as 25% upfront payment on convertible warrants; remaining Rs 88.5 crore payable within 18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Assets:** Total assets increased to Rs 1,641 crore (from Rs 948 crore in March) due to Housing.com acquisition.
- **Tax:** Acquired ~Rs 1,020 crore in carry-forward tax losses; management is evaluating optimization strategies.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Integration Risk:** Management acknowledged the need for significant management bandwidth to integrate Housing.com and execute the roadmap.
- **Market Cycles:** Management noted that while they have not seen a price dip recently, they have risk mitigation plans (cost base adjustments) for potential "black swan" events or market softening.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: How will Housing.com be made profitable?** A: Management stated cost optimization (Rs 120 crore cut) is done; future profitability will be driven by revenue growth (targeting Rs 100-150 crore increase).
- **Q: Will Housing.com integration dampen inorganic growth?** A: Management confirmed they are not looking at new acquisitions in the short term as they are focused on integrating the current portfolio.
- **Q: Why did rental units decline?** A: Management explained this was a planned rationalization/consolidation of properties to improve unit economics and profitability, which has now been completed.
- **Q: What is the take rate on loans?** A: Management earns a commission of 1% to 1.2% on disbursed value.
- **Q: What are the Board's main concerns?** A: Management noted the Board spent significant time on integration plans, risk mitigation, and capital allocation.

### One-line takeaway
Aurum PropTech has successfully acquired Housing.com, shifted to a "platform" approach, and is now prioritizing the integration and monetization of this asset to drive profitability within 4-6 quarters.
