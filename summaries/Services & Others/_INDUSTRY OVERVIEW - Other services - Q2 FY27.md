# Services & Others - Other services: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the Other Services sector reported a robust revenue performance for Q2/H1 FY27, driven by strong underlying demand in sectors such as FMCG contract manufacturing, digital services, shared workspace leasing, mobility, and specialized engineering. However, profitability was frequently constrained by external headwinds, including fluctuating raw material and procurement costs, volatile global freight rates, and competitive pricing pressures. Several companies are pursuing aggressive backward integration, capacity debottlenecking, and global expansion (e.g., into Australia and Malaysia) to secure their ecosystems and capture long-term structural growth.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across nearly all sub-segments report fundamentally strong volume and underlying demand from corporate and institutional clients, supported by government infrastructure initiatives and rising manufacturing clusters.
*   **Divergence on Global Headwinds vs. Domestic Resilience:** While companies with heavy domestic exposures (such as Sunrakshakk Industries in FMCG and Dev Accelerator in flexible workspaces) report booming domestic consumption and enterprise/GCC demand, firms tied to international trade and logistics (such as Ardee Industries, Glottis, and BLS International) faced headwinds from geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and volatile shipping/freight markets. 
*   **Divergence on Volume vs. Margin Focus:** Ardee Industries shifted its focus strictly toward absolute revenue and volume growth due to sticky input costs, whereas players like Steamhouse India and True Colors reported rising profitability outstripping or matching strong volume expansion.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Margin Compression Pressures:** Several managements highlighted margin pressures stemming from higher procurement costs, revenue mix shifts toward lower-margin segments, and volatile freight or crude-linked inputs.
*   **Agreed Views:** 
    *   **Ardee Industries** reported EBITDA margin falling to 8.9% due to high-cost raw materials procured 2-3 months prior, rising freight, and fuel expenses.
    *   **BLS International** experienced consolidated margin compression (25.5% vs 28.9% YoY) driven by a revenue mix shift toward its faster-growing, lower-margin Digital Services segment (7.4% EBITDA margin).
    *   **Glottis** faced severe bottom-line pressure (EBITDA margin down to 1.2%) due to fluctuating freight realizations, volatile crude/vessel charter rates, and the entire industry shifting to spot pricing.
    *   **Monarch Surveyors and Engineering Consultants** noted EBITDA margin moderation by 225 bps YoY to 26.35% due to new wage codes increasing gratuity, Diwali bonuses, annual increments, and manpower ramp-up treated as an investment.
*   **Counterpoints & Efficiencies:** 
    *   **Steamhouse India** improved its EBITDA margin to 19.28% (vs 11.39% YoY) and H1 EBITDA per tonne to ~Rs 916 by transitioning coal purchases from credit to cash, securing cash discounts.
    *   **ECOS (India) Mobility & Hospitality** expanded EBITDA margins sequentially by 160 bps to 11.9% via cost optimization and operating leverage, despite ongoing industry price competition.
    *   **True Colors** expanded gross margins by 405 bps YoY to 30.2% and EBITDA margins to 17.7%, supported by a higher share of high-margin consumables (ink and paper).

## Capex & capacity
*   **Ardee Industries:** Enhanced installed manufacturing capacity to 1,56,950 metric tons per annum via brownfield debottlenecking and acquired a 6-acre land parcel at Naidupeta.
*   **Dev Accelerator:** Scaled its operational portfolio to ~1.16 million sq. ft. across 17,400 seats (28 centers), with a pipeline capacity of 2.8 million sq. ft. across 17 new centers.
*   **ECOS (India) Mobility & Hospitality:** Maintained an asset-light model (own vehicles at 5%–10% of total fleet) with H1 vehicle capex at INR 273.90 million.
*   **Glottis:** Deployed container operations in Q3, secured 200,000 sq. ft. of warehousing space in South and West India, and expanded owned fleet to 85 vehicles.
*   **Monarch Surveyors:** Scaled employee strength to 972 and expanded internationally by acquiring Australia's GM & FE Ryan Pty Limited.
*   **Steamhouse India:** Progressed on multiple cluster expansions (Jhagadia, Nandesari, Vapi, Tarapur, Pirana) and a ~Rs 311 crore Una EPC contract.
*   **Sunrakshakk Industries:** Maintained capacity utilization around 50%–55% across FMCG facilities, relying on internal accruals without immediate major capex.
*   **True Colors:** Commenced INKIA Phase-1 ink expansion (150 tons/month by FY27 end at Rs 40-45 crore capex) and partnered in a 50/50 JV (True Colors Advanced Materials) for a Rs 320-340 crore paper manufacturing project targeting H2 FY29 production.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Adjustments:** 
    *   **Ardee Industries:** Maintained full-year volume growth target of 10% to 15% (compared to 10% to 20% in Q1) while shifting focus to absolute revenue growth.
    *   **ECOS (India) Mobility & Hospitality:** Adjusted full-year revenue growth guidance to 13%–15% (from 15%–18% in Q1) while narrowing EBITDA margin guidance to 10%–11%.
    *   **Sunrakshakk Industries:** Stated they are well ahead of pace to cross the Rs 1,000 crore revenue milestone much sooner than the previously targeted FY28.
*   **Strategic & Structural Shifts:**
    *   **Glottis:** Incorporated a Malaysian subsidiary and received a Rs 60 crore interest-free promoter loan to bridge working capital gaps caused by soaring freight rates.
    *   **Steamhouse India:** Cleared trade payables to near zero using IPO proceeds to capture cash discounts, resulting in temporarily negative operating cash flows.
    *   **True Colors:** Entered a 50/50 JV for backward integration into ultra-low GSM paper media and secured subsidies under the Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Execution and Margin Normalization:** Watch whether Monarch Surveyors and Steamhouse India successfully achieve their targeted margin recoveries in H2.
*   **Working Capital & Cash Conversion:** Monitor trade receivables aging for Monarch Surveyors (Rs 77 crore total) and True Colors (Rs 116.03 crore), alongside the cash-flow normalization for Steamhouse India.
*   **Global Logistics & Sourcing:** Track the impact of crude volatility and freight rate normalization on Glottis and Ardee Industries, as well as the operational integration of Monarch’s Australian acquisition and Glottis’s Malaysian subsidiary.
*   **Expansion Milestones:** Track progress on True Colors' paper JV and ink plant expansion, as well as Dev Accelerator's large-scale managed office developments.

## Company one-liners
*   **Ardee Industries Ltd:** Reported Q2 revenue of Rs. 325 crore and EBITDA of Rs. 29 crore (margin 8.9%) with sticky procurement costs, maintaining its 10–15% annual volume growth target.
*   **BLS International Services Ltd:** Delivered record quarterly revenue of Rs 973 crore (+32% YoY) powered by Digital Services, despite margin compression from revenue mix shift.
*   **Dev Accelerator Ltd:** Delivered record Q2 consolidated revenue of Rs 80.2 crore (+54.8% YoY) and tripled PAT to Rs 5.5 crore on strong operating leverage and enterprise demand.
*   **ECOS (India) Mobility & Hospitality Ltd:** Posted Q2 revenue of INR 2,345 million (+9.5% YoY) with EBITDA margins expanding to 11.9% on cost optimization and operating leverage.
*   **Glottis Ltd:** Achieved a 28.8% YoY revenue increase to Rs 2,765 million driven by exports, but EBITDA margins compressed to 1.2% amid volatile freight rates.
*   **Monarch Surveyors and Engineering Consultants Ltd:** Reported strong H1 standalone revenue up 38.19% YoY to Rs 99.36 crore and a record unchurned order book of ~Rs 950–970 crore.
*   **Steamhouse India Ltd:** Posted its best-ever Q2 with a 73.38% YoY jump in EBITDA to Rs 24.68 crore and maintained its 25%+ annual volume growth guidance.
*   **Sunrakshakk Industries India Ltd:** Delivered its most profitable quarter with consolidated revenue surging 119.20% YoY to Rs 265.17 crore, propelled by scaling FMCG and edible operations.
*   **True Colors Ltd:** Reported H1 revenue of Rs 183.01 crore (+21.1% YoY) and a 43.3% YoY jump in PAT, driven by a higher share of high-margin consumables and aggressive backward integration.
