# Bandhan Bank Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported stabilization in the EEB portfolio, strong growth in non-EEB segments, and improved profitability metrics (PAT up 5.5x YoY).

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 loan growth target of ~14% and exit ROA guidance of 1.2%–1.4%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious" to "balanced and steady progress." EEB portfolio de-growth has been arrested.
- **New topics/risks:** Draft regulations on bancassurance commission caps (potential headwind); impact of floods in Assam (moratorium provided for ~Rs 406 crore).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on stabilizing EEB portfolio and improving net CD ratio (down to 89.5% from 92%).

### Key numbers
*   **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 622 crore (up 5.5x YoY, 23.9% QoQ).
*   **Gross Advances:** Rs 1.58 lakh crore (up 13% YoY, 1.8% QoQ).
*   **Total Deposits:** Rs 1.73 lakh crore (up 9.2% YoY, 4.7% QoQ).
*   **NIM:** 6.1% (stable).
*   **Gross NPA:** 3.1%; **Net NPA:** 0.9%.
*   **Credit Cost:** 1.8% (stable sequentially).
*   **CASA Ratio:** 26.7%.
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 18.3% (Tier 1 at 17.7%).
*   **Operating Profit:** Rs 1,556 crore (up 19% YoY, 14.6% QoQ).

### Highlights
*   **EEB Portfolio:** Stabilized; de-growth arrested. Portfolio stood at Rs 52,616 crore.
*   **Portfolio Diversification:** Non-EEB book grew 20% YoY, now 66.7% of total advances. Secured portfolio grew 18% YoY, now 57.3% of total advances.
*   **Credit Cards:** Launched four variants (Sparks, Ignite, Flare, Lumina) to target diverse customer segments.
*   **Asset Quality:** Bank-level collection efficiency at 99.4% (up 36 bps QoQ). EEB collection efficiency improved to 99.3% (from 98.9% in Q1).
*   **Network:** Added 13 branches and 14 banking units (Total: 2,001 branches, 4,414 banking units).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Loan Growth:** Targeting ~14% for FY27.
*   **ROA:** Aims to improve quarter-on-quarter; maintains exit ROA guidance of 1.2%–1.4% for Q4 FY27. Medium-term aspiration of 1.6%–1.8% remains.
*   **NIM:** Expects to maintain in the 6.1%–6.2% range.
*   **PSLC Cost:** Full-year cost expected to be ~Rs 120 crore; expects to neutralize/zero out by next year.
*   **Opex:** Guidance of 4.2% operating expenses to total assets remains unchanged.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Bancassurance:** Acknowledged potential headwinds from draft commission cap regulations; model is retail-focused and consent-based.
*   **Repo Rate:** 49% of the loan book is repo-linked; management expects positive NIM impact in Q4 from repricing, though Q3 will be largely unaffected.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Operating Expenses:** Rs 2,109 crore (up 15.4% YoY).
*   **Efficiency:** Replaced a major platform, generating savings of ~Rs 12 crore per quarter (Rs 50 crore annually).
*   **Funding:** Bulk deposits increased by ~Rs 4,300 crore (10% QoQ) for liquidity management, while borrowings declined by ~Rs 3,400 crore (25% QoQ).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **ECL Transition:** Estimated impact of ~Rs 2,200 crore (based on March 2026 data); will be amortized over 5 years via capital, not P&L.
*   **Assam Floods:** 4 districts affected; Rs 406 crore loans under moratorium. 96% of this book is standard.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Savings Account:** Balances moderated sequentially due to interest rate recalibration.
*   **Bancassurance:** Potential 60% hit to bancassurance income if draft norms are implemented as-is (though management is working to mitigate).
*   **External Environment:** Requires careful monitoring of external operating environment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is the EEB book flat while peers are growing?** Management stated they are prudent but not against growth; expects 8-10% growth in Q3/Q4.
*   **Q: Why did the ECL impact estimate rise to Rs 2,200 crore?** Management clarified this is the total impact based on March 2026 data; expects the final outcome to be better due to improved asset quality since then.
*   **Q: Will wholesale banking dilute ROA?** Management noted wholesale banking is becoming ROA-accretive through fee income (forex, trade finance, etc.) rather than just interest income.
*   **Q: Can you pass on higher funding costs to MFI customers?** Management confirmed no current plans to increase MFI rates (currently ~21.69% yield).
*   **Q: Is the rise in SMA-0 in Assam a concern?** Management attributed it to temporary flood disruption and noted collection efficiency remains steady at 99.1%.

### One-line takeaway
Bandhan Bank showed strong recovery in profitability and asset quality, with management successfully arresting EEB de-growth and maintaining a disciplined, diversified growth strategy despite regulatory headwinds in bancassurance.
