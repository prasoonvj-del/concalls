# ACC Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasizes structural cost leadership and a "value-over-volume" strategy, noting that Q1 volume declines were a calculated move to shed low-margin business.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 9,500 crore
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1,589 crore
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 16.7% (up 331 bps)
*   **EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 931
*   **Net operating cost:** Rs 4,241 per tonne (down Rs 206 PMT QoQ)
*   **PAT:** Rs 660 crore
*   **Net Worth:** ~Rs 72,000 crore
*   **Trade sales share:** 78% (up from 74%)
*   **Premium products:** 34% of trade sales
*   **Clinker factor:** 64% (improved ~3%)
*   **Blended cement share:** 85%
*   **Green power capacity:** 973 MW (up 500 MW YoY)
*   **WHRS capacity:** 228 MW

### Highlights
*   **Strategy:** Management is prioritizing "value creation over volume," resulting in a 2% YoY decline in trade volumes and a 21% YoY decline in non-trade volumes during Q1.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Net operating cost reduced by Rs 206 PMT sequentially.
*   **Temporary Suspension:** Management clarified that the suspension of certain old ACC/acquired plants (approx. 3.5 million tonnes capacity) is a "temporary suspension" for optimization and maintenance, not a permanent closure.
*   **July Momentum:** Management reported an 8% YoY improvement in trade volumes for July.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Management targets 8% volume growth for FY27.
*   **Cost Target:** Management reiterated guidance to achieve a total cost of Rs 4,250 PMT by the end of FY27.
*   **Capacity:** Targeting 119 million tonnes capacity by FY27 end (adding 10.2 million tonnes this year).
*   **Green Power:** Targeting 60% green power share by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains stable, supported by infrastructure, housing, and construction.
*   Near-term demand is influenced by monsoon and input cost volatility.
*   Management noted that the industry is facing pressure from higher imported fuel prices and elevated freight costs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Drivers:** Savings achieved through logistics optimization (lead distance reduced by 20km), RE power utilization, clinker factor reduction, and fly ash sourcing.
*   **Mitigation:** Management absorbed a Rs 110 PMT cost impact from West Asia geopolitical tensions.
*   **Inventory:** Holding 1 month of clinker inventory and 3 months of coal inventory.
*   **Future Savings:** Management expects an additional Rs 130–150 PMT in savings from ongoing efficiency initiatives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex:** ~Rs 6,500 crore (25% spent in Q1).
*   **Commissioning:** Trial runs commenced at Dahej (1.2 MT), Salai Banwa (2.4 MT), Bhatinda (1.2 MT), and Jodhpur (2 MT).
*   **Upcoming:** Kalamboli (1 MT) and Warisaliganj (2.4 MT) expected in Q2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management stated that Ambuja Cements has zero debt and is managing cash flows from operating activities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions in West Asia impacting fuel and freight costs.
*   Disruptions in packing bag availability and diesel shortages experienced in Q1.
*   Monsoon impact on near-term demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On volume decline:** Management clarified that the decline was a "very well calculated" decision to shed low-margin non-trade volumes to protect EBITDA.
*   **On "mothballed" plants:** Management clarified these are "temporary suspensions" for 6 months to optimize costs and efficiency, not permanent closures.
*   **On Green Power/Fly Ash sales:** Management explained that selling surplus power/fly ash is a "transition phase" while connectivity infrastructure is being built; the ultimate goal is 100% in-house consumption.
*   **On ICDs to parent:** Management stated that Inter-Corporate Deposits (ICDs) are within approved shareholder limits and compliant with regulations.
*   **On cost reconciliation:** Management acknowledged the complexity of reconciling cost per tonne due to accounting treatments (e.g., netting off power/fly ash sales) and offered to connect offline for detailed breakdowns.

### One-line takeaway
ACC/Ambuja is aggressively pivoting to a "value-over-volume" strategy, sacrificing low-margin non-trade volumes to drive EBITDA, while banking on structural cost reductions (green power, logistics, clinker factor) to hit a Rs 4,250 PMT cost target by FY27.
