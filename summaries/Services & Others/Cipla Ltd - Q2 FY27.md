# Cipla Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 4% QoQ, EBITDA margins expanded 200 bps, and management reiterated FY27 guidance despite regulatory and geopolitical headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 EBITDA margin guidance (18.5%–20%) and US exit run rate target ($1 billion).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "margin pressure" (Q1) to "sequential improvement" (Q2). Management remains confident in US supply-led growth.
- **New topics or risks:** Partnership with Qilu for Pembrolizumab (Keytruda biosimilar); Invagen Unit 3 (NY) classified as VAI; Invagen Long Island GMP inspection concluded with 0 Form 483s.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Ventolin (launched in Q1) is in the ramp-up phase; Advair (DPI) received approval in Q2.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 7,404 crore (4% QoQ growth).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 18.7% (200 bps sequential improvement).
- **Gross Margin:** 64.4% (7% QoQ improvement).
- **R&D Spend:** INR 423 crore (5.7% of revenue).
- **PAT:** INR 878 crore (11.9% of sales).
- **Net Cash:** INR 9,823 crore.
- **Debt:** INR 699 crore (including lease liabilities).
- **North America Revenue:** $172 million (up $10 million QoQ).
- **EMEU Revenue:** $101 million.

### Highlights
- **One India:** Recorded highest ever quarterly revenue (12% YoY growth).
- **US Market:** Secured approval for first DPI (generic Advair); holds #1 position in Albuterol MDI market (22% share).
- **Biosimilars:** Partnered with Qilu for Pembrolizumab (North America); initiated clinical development for one respiratory biosimilar asset.
- **New Launches:** Revesmart (respiratory), Galvus DM/SR (antidiabetes), Dytor E (cardiovascular), and Rizontem (anti-infectives).
- **Regulatory:** Invagen Unit 3 (NY) classified as VAI; Invagen Long Island GMP inspection concluded with 0 Form 483 observations.

### Guidance & outlook
- **US Growth:** Management reiterates target to exit FY27 with a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate.
- **Margins:** Management maintains FY27 EBITDA margin guidance of 18.5% to 20%.
- **R&D:** Guidance maintained at 6% to 7% of revenue for the full year.
- **Tax:** Expected ETR for full year is 27% to 27.5%.
- **Pipeline:** 45-50 filings planned over the next 3 years for the US.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **India:** Chronic mix at 60.3%. Respiratory grew 11%, antidiabetes 50% (including GLP), urology 11%, and dermatology 16%.
- **GLP-1:** Yurpeak is the #2 brand in the category with 5-10% MoM growth; management expects continued adoption.
- **South Africa:** Private market secondary growth of 6.5% (outperforming 5% market).
- **Trade Generics:** Delivered double-digit QoQ growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Total Expenses:** INR 3,382 crore (4% QoQ increase), driven by planned investments in product launches and manufacturing readiness.
- **Margin Drivers:** Gross margin expansion supported by improved product mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **US Pipeline:** 3 additional significant launches planned for FY27 (2 respiratory, 1 peptide).
- **Biosimilars:** 4 programs under development; Aspergen (JV with Kemwell) is scaling up drug substance/product capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Cash:** INR 9,823 crore.
- **Debt:** INR 699 crore (including lease liabilities).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Regulatory:** Pithampur facility received 7 observations in Form 483 during a follow-up GMP inspection; management is engaging with the FDA.
- **Geopolitical:** EMEU business is operating in a challenging environment.
- **Lanreotide:** Remediation ongoing; management stated this is not included in the $1 billion exit run rate guidance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **US Ramp-up:** Analysts questioned the feasibility of the $1bn exit rate. Management stated it relies on the ramp-up of Ventolin and 3 upcoming launches (2 respiratory, 1 peptide).
- **Ventolin:** Management clarified that the ramp-up is supply-constrained from the new US facility, not demand-constrained.
- **Biosimilars:** Management confirmed they expect to file Pembrolizumab next year for patent expiry in 2029.
- **India Growth:** Management defended 15% branded prescription growth, noting they are beating the market across therapies despite a respiratory-skewed portfolio.
- **Cash Deployment:** Management is looking for complex/differentiated acquisitions (US/Europe) but remains selective.

### One-line takeaway
Cipla delivered a strong quarter with margin expansion and record India revenue, while management remains confident in achieving its $1 billion US exit run rate through supply-led ramp-ups and upcoming pipeline launches.
