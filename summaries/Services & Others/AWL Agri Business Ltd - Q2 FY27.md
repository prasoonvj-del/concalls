# AWL Agri Business Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed – Strong revenue and volume growth, but PAT declined slightly due to tax, and operating cash flow turned negative due to significant inventory buildup.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 guidance for Edible Oil (Rs 4,000-Rs 4,500/MT) and Industry Essentials (operating parameters). Management projects ~Rs 85,000 crore revenue for FY27 based on current run rate.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "resilient" to noting rural demand pressure and potential risks from rainfall deficits on Rabi sowing.
- **New topics:** Standardized pack sizes (viewed as favorable), inventory buildup of Rs 6,000 crore, and clarification that Rs 8,500 crore trade credit is non-funded.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Tops (GD Foods) growth confirmed at >25%; management reiterated focus on North India before expanding to East/Central.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 22,202 crore (+26% YoY).
- **Volume Growth:** 14% YoY.
- **Normalized Operating EBITDA:** Rs 623 crore (+11% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 239 crore (-2% YoY).
- **Food & FMCG Revenue:** Rs 2,216 crore (+32% YoY).
- **Edible Oil EBITDA:** Rs 4,100/MT.
- **Industry Essentials EBITDA:** Rs 4,300/MT.

### Highlights
- **Food & FMCG:** Crossed Rs 2,200 crore quarterly revenue; Fortune pulses grew >40% YoY.
- **Industry Essentials:** Oleochemicals and specialty chemicals now contribute 45% of segment revenue.
- **Distribution:** 9.7 lakh direct outlets; total reach 26 lakh outlets.
- **Channels:** Quick commerce grew 40% YoY; HoReCa grew 22% (LTM revenue >Rs 800 crore); Branded exports grew 72% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Food & FMCG:** Management targets 3%-4% EBITDA margin for FY27; aspires for high single-digit margins from FY28 onwards.
- **Edible Oil:** Management guides for mid-single-digit volume growth and EBITDA of Rs 4,000-Rs 4,500/MT.
- **Industry Essentials:** Management expects 10%-11% growth (down from the 26% one-off seen this quarter).

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Demand:** Urban demand is outperforming rural; rural demand shows signs of sluggishness.
- **Macro Risks:** Rainfall deficit (12-13% overall, >30% in some areas) poses risks to Kharif harvest and Rabi sowing.
- **Regulation:** Standardized pack sizes are viewed as favorable for branded players as they create a level playing field and prevent grammage wars.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Inventory:** Increased by Rs 6,000 crore due to sunflower supply chain disruptions and sluggish September sales; management expects normalization in the OND (Oct-Nov-Dec) quarter.
- **Finance Cost:** Increased due to inventory funding, but management stated pricing power allows recovery of these costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Tops (GD Foods):** Growing >25%; management is focusing on North India, with plans to expand into East and Central India over the next 1-1.5 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **OCF:** Negative for H1 due to inventory buildup.
- **Debt:** Management clarified that the Rs 8,500 crore trade credit is non-funded (Letters of Credit) and should not be included in net debt calculations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Rural Demand:** Sluggishness compared to urban markets.
- **Inflation:** Headline CPI and food inflation (7-7.5%) may impact demand.
- **Supply Chain:** Sunflower supply chain disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Inventory/Cash Flow:** Management explained negative OCF is due to a Rs 6,000 crore inventory buildup (sunflower supply chain), which they expect to correct in the OND quarter.
- **Industry Essentials Growth:** Management clarified the 26% growth is a one-off; sustainable growth is 10-11%.
- **Debt:** Management clarified the Rs 8,500 crore trade credit is non-funded (LCs), not debt.
- **Demerger:** Management explicitly stated there are no plans to demerge the Industry Essentials business.
- **Tops Strategy:** Management confirmed the focus remains on North India, with East/Central expansion planned for the next 1-1.5 years.

### One-line takeaway
AWL delivered strong volume-led revenue growth, though profitability was impacted by tax and inventory-related working capital pressures, which management expects to normalize in the coming quarter.
