# Canara Bank - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-29 · Services & Others*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted that performance bettered guidance across all key parameters, including business growth, asset quality, and profitability.

### Key numbers
*   **Global Business:** Rs 29.05 lakh crore (+14.37% YoY).
*   **Global Deposits:** Rs 16.11685 lakh crore (+11.63% YoY).
*   **Global Advances:** Rs 12.93381 lakh crore (+17.97% YoY).
*   **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 10,215 crore (+13.39% YoY).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 4,856 crore (+2.19% YoY).
*   **Gross NPA:** 1.57% (down 112 bps YoY).
*   **Net NPA:** 0.36% (down 27 bps YoY).
*   **CET1 Ratio:** 12.91% (+62 bps YoY).
*   **Total CRAR:** 17.17% (+65 bps YoY).
*   **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 94.76% (+159 bps YoY).
*   **NIM:** 2.52%.
*   **Return on Equity (ROE):** 18.07%.
*   **Return on Average Assets (ROAA):** 1.04%.

### Highlights
*   **RAM Credit:** Grew 21.20% YoY to Rs 7,64,675 crore.
*   **Retail Credit:** Grew 35.88% YoY to Rs 3,19,893 crore.
*   **Housing Loans:** Grew 17.85% YoY to Rs 1,29,036 crore.
*   **Vehicle Loans:** Grew 26.34% YoY to Rs 27,315 crore.
*   **MSME Credit:** Grew 15.12% YoY to Rs 1,68,815 crore.
*   **Slippage Ratio:** Contained at 0.60% (improved by 20 bps YoY).
*   **Credit Cost:** Reduced by 23 bps to 0.49%.
*   **Gold Loan Portfolio:** Rs 2.59 lakh crore (Agri: Rs 1.51 lakh crore; Retail: Rs 1.07 lakh crore).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **NIM:** Management aims to maintain NIM between 2.50-2.60%.
*   **FCNR(B) Deposits:** Management expects to raise $2.3-2.5 billion; raised $775 million in July, aiming for $1 billion by month-end.
*   **ECL Implementation:** Management plans to absorb the estimated Rs 10,000-12,000 crore impact over 2 years rather than the 5-year regulatory window.
*   **Deposit Growth:** Targeting 11-12% growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted "good traction" in better-rated corporate loans.
*   Corporate pricing environment is showing improvement (getting 10-20 bps better terms).
*   86% of the corporate book is A and Plus rated.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Treasury Income:** Rs 654 crore (vs Rs 1,617 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PSLC Commission:** Rs 1,947 crore.
*   **Digital Spend:** Earmarked over Rs 3,000 crore (approx. 8% of total IT cost).
*   Management aims to replace high-cost bulk deposits with retail deposits and FCNR(B) funds to improve margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Plans to open 250 new branches in FY27 (34 already opened).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **CASA Ratio:** 29.70%.
*   **CD Ratio:** Increased from 75 to 80.
*   **Credit Pipeline:** Approx. Rs 50,000 crore (Rs 18,000 crore sanctioned but undisbursed; Rs 32,000 crore in proposals).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **SMA-2 Increase:** Management attributed the increase to two high-value government-guaranteed accounts, stating these have since corrected/oscillated back to SMA-0.
*   **ECL Impact:** Management estimates a potential 4-5 bps increase in credit costs upon transition to ECL.
*   **M2M Risk:** Uncertainty regarding the M2M impact on the investment book due to ECL norms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Strategy on NIM vs. Growth?* Management emphasized striking a balance, prioritizing efficiency parameters while maintaining growth.
*   *Q: SMA-2 increase?* Management clarified it was due to two specific high-value accounts that have since corrected.
*   *Q: ECL impact?* Management estimates a Rs 10,000-12,000 crore impact, plans to absorb over 2 years; stated capital adequacy is sufficient.
*   *Q: Fee income sluggishness?* Management attributed this to specific government commissions/service charges booked in the previous quarter.
*   *Q: Gold loan LTV?* Management confirmed LTV is between 60-65%.

### One-line takeaway
Canara Bank delivered a strong quarter with performance exceeding guidance across key metrics, supported by robust asset quality and a strategic focus on improving efficiency parameters like CASA and NIM.
