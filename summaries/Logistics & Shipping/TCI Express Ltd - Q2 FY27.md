# TCI Express Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted margin expansion (best in five quarters), double-digit growth in new service lines, and strong operational performance.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 336 crore (up 9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 43 crore (up 11% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 12.7% (up from 12.4% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 27 crore (up 6% YoY).
*   **Volume:** 265,000 tonnes (up 7.5% YoY).
*   **Net Cash:** Rs 77 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Segment Growth:** E-Commerce Express grew 51.6% YoY; Domestic Air Express grew 29% YoY; International Air Express grew 26% YoY.
*   **Core Business:** Surface Express (largest segment) grew 9%+ YoY.
*   **Strategic Focus:** Management is prioritizing D2C and SME segments to drive profitability and margin enhancement.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Automation in sorting centers reduced labor components and truck turnaround time, contributing to a 40-50 bps reduction in direct costs.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects 14%-15%+ growth in the next two quarters.
*   **Volume:** Management expects 11%-12% growth for the remainder of the year.
*   **Margins:** Management targets EBITDA margins of 13%+ for H2 FY27 and aims for a 50-100 bps improvement over the previous year.
*   **Long-term:** Management aims to increase the revenue share of new service lines (Air, Rail, E-com) to 22% by 2030.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market:** Management noted "full momentum" in the economy with growth across both SME and large client segments.
*   **Pricing:** The pricing environment remains stable; the company successfully passed on fuel price increases (net ~1.5% price hike).
*   **Competition:** Aggregators (e.g., Shiprocket) are not significantly impacting B2B business; the company is selectively registering with them only for profitable E-commerce segments.
*   **Railways:** Indian Railways' entry into express parcel is not viewed as a threat due to the lack of door-to-door service and operational agility compared to TCI Express.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margin expansion was driven by better operating performance, higher capacity utilization, and cost discipline.
*   Management noted that the initial investment in owning sorting centers weighs on returns in the short term but will improve long-term control and cost efficiency.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **H1 FY27 Capex:** Rs 80 crore.
*   **Future Capex:** Management plans Rs 100 crore+ annually for the next 4-5 years.
*   **Infrastructure:** The company is converting 12-15 sorting centers from lease to own (9 completed or in-progress). Current projects include centers in Lucknow, Ahmedabad, and Kolkata.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Net cash of Rs 77 crore (Liquid assets of Rs 93 crore vs. total debt of Rs 15 crore).
*   **Working Capital:** Receivable days at 59; Payable days at 32; Net working capital at 27 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **C2C Express:** This segment declined due to fuel price fluctuations and unfavorable rates; management expects a recovery in Q3.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Ravi Naredi (Shareholder):** Questioned the lack of growth over the last 2-3 years despite capex. Management responded that the growth journey has started, citing Q2 traction and commitment to double-digit growth.
*   **Ashwin Reddy (Equirus):** Asked about the impact of aggregators. Management clarified they focus on profitable, direct B2B business and only use aggregators for specific, profitable E-commerce segments.
*   **Ashwin Reddy:** Asked about the "own vs. lease" strategy. Management explained that owning centers provides better control and reduces direct costs (40-50 bps reduction).
*   **Vikram Suryavanshi (PhillipCapital):** Asked about the threat from Indian Railways. Management dismissed it, citing the lack of door-to-door service and the specialized nature of express cargo.

### One-line takeaway
TCI Express delivered margin-accretive growth in Q2 FY27, driven by strong performance in new service lines and cost discipline, with management guiding for 13%+ EBITDA margins in H2.
