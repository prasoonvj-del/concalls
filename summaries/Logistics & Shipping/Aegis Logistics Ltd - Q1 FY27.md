# Aegis Logistics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record financial performance with significant YoY growth in EBITDA and PAT, driven by strong operational performance in both the Liquids and Gas divisions.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,357 crore (up 37% YoY)
- **Normalized EBITDA:** Rs 727 crore (up 184% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 545 crore (up 212% YoY)
- **EPS:** Rs 13.80
- **Gas Division EBITDA:** Rs 591 crore (up 296% YoY)
- **Liquids Division Revenue:** Rs 178 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **Liquids Division EBITDA:** Rs 136 crore (up 28% YoY)
- **Gas Distribution Volumes:** 2.77 lakh metric tons (up 91% YoY, up 19% QoQ)
- **Logistics Throughput Volumes:** 1.124 million metric tons
- **Sourcing Volumes:** 1.21 lakh metric tons (marginal growth YoY)

### Highlights
- Achieved a milestone of crossing Rs 500 crore in PAT in Q1 FY27 compared to Rs 175 crore in Q1 FY26.
- Liquids division recorded its highest-ever Q1 EBITDA and 5 consecutive quarters of EBITDA growth.
- Commissioned a 36,000 metric ton static storage capacity specialized ammonia terminal at Pipavav Port, backed by a 15-year take-or-pay agreement with Hindustan Zinc.
- ITOCHU Corporation acquired a 10% stake in Aegis Terminal Pipavav Limited with intentions to increase it to 25% over the next 3 years.
- Jamnagar-Loni LPG pipeline is complete and operational; Kandla-Gorakhpur pipeline expected to connect during H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the distribution margin of around Rs 7,000+ per ton to be sustainable going forward, supported by volume ramp-ups and procurement efficiencies.
- Management expects to continue EPS CAGR growth of 25%+ going forward.
- Cumulative capex targeted to reach ~$1.2 billion in this fiscal year, with a identified capex pipeline of ~$5 billion through FY31, targeting a gearing ratio of ~0.6.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Gas distribution volume growth was driven by robust demand across customer segments and continued private sector customer additions.
- Geopolitical tensions and Middle East war disruptions made sourcing and logistics challenging, but the vertically integrated model enabled supply reliability and stability.
- Distribution margin baseline of Rs 4,000 (historical up to FY25) shifted to Rs 7,000+ due to uncertainty premiums and future procurement/shipping efficiencies from VLGC usage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sourcing per unit margin is around $85 to $90.
- Logistics throughput volumes deliver close to Rs 1,000 EBITDA per ton.
- Distribution EBITDA margin averaged around Rs 7,000+ for the quarter/year outlook.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Mumbai Port:** Developing 64,000 cubic meters of liquid storage at ~Rs 125 crore (commissioning H1 FY27). Current: 334,000 cbm liquids, 21,000 MT static LPG.
- **JNPA Port:** Executing liquid storage expansion (first phase of ~100,000 cbm expected in Q3 FY27 out of 318,100 cbm total), 77,236 MT LPG capacity, and a 35,000 MT annual capacity LPG bottling plant with total outlay of ~Rs 1,675 crore. Board approved a 52,000 MT refrigerated double-walled steel LPG tank.
- **Haldia:** Acquired 3 additional acres of land for liquid business expansion. Current: 25,000 MT LPG terminal (exclusive agreement with HPCL through 2038), 226,890 cbm liquid storage.
- **Kandla:** CRL 4 liquid terminal adding 94,148 cbm is progressing (commissioning next year). Signed non-binding MoU with L&T for potential joint development of ammonia terminals. Current: 952,000 cbm liquids, 48,000 MT static LPG.
- **Pipavav:** Commissioned 36,000 MT ammonia terminal. Jetty development by APM Terminals, liquid rail gantry with 0.5 MMTPA take-or-pay agreement for 15 years, and pipeline connectivity ongoing.
- **Kochi:** Board approved expansion of 49,577 cbm storage capacity (commissioning early next fiscal year), taking total capacity to 132,122 cbm.
- **Mangalore:** Developing LPG rail loading gantry and bottling plant (Rs 52.5 crore). Liquid capacity of 75,000 cbm added last year is fully utilized (total 193,000 cbm); evaluating further 60,000 cbm addition.
- **Vadhavan Port:** Signed non-binding MoU with potential investment of ~Rs 20,000 crore, subject to approvals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a liquidity reserve/cash balance on a consolidated basis (fortress balance sheet approach).
- Aegis Vopak is self-funded via equity dilution and borrowing capacity.
- Distribution business requires minimal capex and very low working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global backdrop marked by ongoing geopolitical tensions and Middle East war disruptions.
- Volatility in the global LPG industry; management highlighted they are not speculative global traders taking inventory positions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vibhav Zutshi (Vibhuti / Institutional Investor) on Distribution Sustainability & Margins:** Asked if distribution EBITDA per ton peaked and sustainability of Rs 7,000+. Management stated Rs 7,000+ is sustainable due to volume scale and procurement efficiencies (using VLGCs instead of MGCs).
- **Yash Desai (Dalal & Broacha) on Volume Growth:** Asked about 25% CAGR guidance upside. Management replied 25% is the baseline benchmark for logistics throughput, with potential for step-up growth via enablers (pipelines, jetties).
- **Vinith Jain (Siddh Capital) on Ammonia Distribution & Margins:** Asked when ammonia distribution starts and EBITDA/ton. Management stated distribution starts immediately (weeks/months) for industrial clients, but declined to give specific margin guidance upfront.
- **Vinith Jain on Low Tank Utilization / Sourcing:** Asked why tanks aren't fully filled given national LPG deficits. Management clarified they are distributors, not speculative traders, and avoid taking directional inventory/price risks.
- **Kunal Mehta (InCred Equities) on Cash Deployment:** Asked how the robust cash reserves across subsidiaries will be used. Management stated they maintain a fortress balance sheet and will deploy cash opportunistically for profit-accretive organic/inorganic growth.

### One-line takeaway
Aegis Logistics delivered a record Q1 FY27 driven by exceptional growth in its Gas division and stable cash generation from Liquids, backed by ongoing major infrastructure expansions and new ammonia-handling capabilities.
