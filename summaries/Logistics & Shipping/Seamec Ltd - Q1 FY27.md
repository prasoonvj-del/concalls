# Seamec Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue growth, healthy fleet utilization, and robust demand in the offshore energy sector.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 297 crore (vs Rs 211 crore YoY, up 41%)
- **Stand-alone Revenue:** Rs 283 crore (vs Rs 201 crore YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 124 crore
- **Stand-alone EBITDA:** Rs 117 crore
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 81 crore (vs Rs 76 crore YoY)
- **Stand-alone PAT:** Rs 81 crore (vs Rs 80 crore YoY)
- **Seamec ANANT Purchase Consideration:** USD 70 million
- **Fleet Contract Mix:** 35% to 40% short-term (including EPC contracts), balance long-term/mid-term (IMR).

### Highlights
- Revenue growth of 41% YoY was supported by healthy fleet utilization, efficient project execution, disciplined cost management, and improved operational efficiency across domestic and international assignments.
- Seamec Paladin returned to Indian shores after being delayed by conflict in the Middle East / Strait of Hormuz, and has resumed operations.
- Acquisition of Seamec ANANT from HAL Offshore for USD 70 million is expected to be completed by the end of August 2026, financed 50-50 through internal equity and loans.
- Gallant vessel sale process expected to be completed in September post-shareholder approval.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a sustainable annualized EBITDA margin of 40% to 42%.
- Management targeted a 15% to 20% revenue CAGR over the next 3 to 5 years.
- Strong demand for Diving Support Vessels (DSVs) expected to continue for the next 3 to 5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global offshore energy industry witnessing healthy structural growth driven by energy security and conventional oil and gas investments.
- Middle East remains a key growth market (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Abu Dhabi); gradual reopening of offshore activities in Iran expected to support regional vessel demand over the medium to long term.
- Indian offshore potential highlighted by Andaman and Mahanadi basin discoveries.
- Limited availability of quality offshore support vessels and disciplined fleet additions are supporting favorable supply-demand balance and charter rates.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Segment margins in offshore were materially better than onshore; onshore margins were pulled down by costs incurred while Paladin was non-operational.
- EPC work costs vary seasonally and include raw material and fuel costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Seamec ANANT acquisition (USD 70 million) to be funded 50% via internal equity and 50% via loans.
- Fleet modernization and selective evaluation of vessel acquisitions remain ongoing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains healthy with sufficient flexibility for selective growth opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and conflicts in the Middle East (e.g., Strait of Hormuz impact on Paladin).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Siddharth Chauhan (360 ONE Capital) on offshore vs onshore margins:** Management explained that higher vessel deployment drove offshore margins, while onshore margins were impacted by idle costs on Paladin.
2. **Dhaval Popat (Choice International) on charter rate sustainability:** Management stated charter rates depend on demand/supply, but DSV demand will remain strong for the next 3 to 5 years, boosted further if Iran sanctions lift.
3. **Aditya Banerjee (Retail Investor) on Seamec ANANT financing and status:** Management confirmed the USD 70 million purchase via 50% equity and 50% debt, stating it will resume its ONGC contract within a month after acquisition formalities end.
4. **Tejas (TNJ Investment) on royalty audit report:** Management stated Grant Thornton submitted a satisfactory report to independent directors, and it is an internal document that will not be shared publicly; the 4% revenue royalty continues.
5. **Tejas (TNJ Investment) on Pearl vessel status:** Management clarified that Pearl is a disputed bulk carrier under arbitration in their Dubai subsidiary, and they have given up thoughts on purchasing other small supply vessels of that nature.

### One-line takeaway
Seamec reported a robust Q1 FY27 with 41% YoY revenue growth, maintaining a 40-42% margin outlook and expanding its fleet with the upcoming acquisition of Seamec ANANT amid strong multi-year offshore demand.
