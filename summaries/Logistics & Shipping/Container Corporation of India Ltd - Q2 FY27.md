# Container Corporation of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted resilience amidst geopolitical turbulence, record volumes, and optimism for H2 performance.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Handling Volume:** 1,448,379 TEUs (highest ever).
*   **Operating Turnover:** Rs 2,373 crore (highest ever).
*   **Total Income:** Rs 2,466 crore (highest ever).
*   **Revenue Growth:** 0.93% YoY.
*   **Standalone PBT:** Rs 462 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 25.79%.
*   **Dividend:** Rs 1.9 per share (Total H1 dividend: Rs 3.5 per share).
*   **Capex:** Rs 299.75 crore (Q2); Rs 418.05 crore (H1).
*   **Rail Freight Margin:** 28.3%.
*   **Operating Margin:** 31.5%.

### Highlights
*   **Volume Growth:** EXIM throughput increased 4.57% YoY; Domestic increased 4.22% YoY.
*   **Operational Metrics:** Average lead distance increased to 817 km (from 779 km YoY).
*   **Fleet Expansion:** Added 13 new rakes in Q2 (Total: 456); added 7 reach stackers and 414 containers.
*   **New Services:** Launched "Assured Transit" train from Whitefield to Tughlakabad; initiated cabotage service from JNPT to Dadri.
*   **Market Share:** JNPT market share rose to 63.75% (+3.5% YoY).
*   **Shipping:** Generated Rs 12 crore revenue from shipping activities in H1; targeting Rs 50 crore for FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Guidance:** Management declined to revise volume guidance, citing evolving geopolitical conditions in West Asia; will review in December.
*   **Outlook:** Management remains optimistic for strong performance in H2, citing recovery in domestic segments (tiles, gunny bales) and new contracts (GAIL).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Geopolitical Impact:** Gulf ceasefire collapse and Strait of Hormuz closure caused trade turbulence; trade shifted to Khor Fakkan and Fujairah.
*   **Domestic Drivers:** Morbi tile traffic recovered in Q2; gunny bale traffic recovered from August; bulk cement volumes growing via tank containers.
*   **Food Grains:** Experienced a setback due to busy season surcharge (10% rate differential vs BCN wagons); expects recovery from October as surcharge ended.
*   **DFC Impact:** WDFC is operational; management expects significant modal shift from road to rail in the medium term.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Tariff Adjustments:** Reduced upper deck rates for EXIM traffic (Dadri to JNPT) and handling costs at Tughlakabad by 10-20% to incentivize rail usage.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Increase driven by higher staff costs, fuel price rises, and general inflation.
*   **Empty Running Cost:** Total Rs 98.82 crore (EXIM: Rs 21.73 crore; Domestic: Rs 77.09 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Guidance:** Internal target of Rs 945 crore for FY27 (MOU target: Rs 800 crore).
*   **Terminals:** Developing 4 new terminals (Salawas, Pathri Haridwar, Amritsar, Chharodi); Rs 200 crore allocated to these projects.
*   **Bharat Container Shipping Line:** Proposal under government approval; CONCOR to hold 30% stake.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   *Not explicitly discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** West Asia crisis remains a major uncertainty affecting shipping schedules and empty container availability.
*   **Competition:** Intense competition in domestic segment impacting yields.
*   **Operational:** Erratic ship schedules and port congestion affecting double-stacking efficiency (currently ~30% at JNPT).
*   **Coastal Shipping:** Currently on hold due to high lease rates and market turbulence.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Domestic Margins:** Management attributed the dip to volume loss in specific segments (tiles) and fixed costs (land/staff); expects margins to improve with volume recovery.
*   **On DFC Benefits:** Management clarified that while DFC is operational, full benefits are delayed due to erratic ship schedules and port yard constraints; expects improvement in coming quarters.
*   **On Tariff Rationalization:** Management confirmed they are working with the Ministry to get level playing field for fertilizer and food grains in containers, similar to bulk cement.
*   **On CFS vs. ICD:** Management explained that if port yards become more efficient, cargo shifts to ICDs; if weak, it goes to CFS/road.
*   **On Revenue Impact of Rate Cuts:** Management stated that rate reductions in Dadri have already led to increased volumes and revenue growth, contrary to concerns of revenue loss.

### One-line takeaway
CONCOR delivered record volumes and turnover despite geopolitical headwinds, with management banking on DFC-led modal shifts and domestic recovery to drive H2 performance.
