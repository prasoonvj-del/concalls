# Shiprocket Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-11 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 34% YoY, transactions up 36%, and adjusted EBITDA grew 9x to Rs 8.9 crore, driven by strong core performance and emerging segment expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Transactions:** Up 36% YoY
- **GMV:** Up 31% YoY (TTM GMV over Rs 34,600 crore)
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 592 crore, up 34% YoY
- **Core Revenue:** Rs 412 crore, up 22% YoY
- **Emerging Revenue:** Rs 180 crore, up 70% YoY (share of total revenue moved from 24% to 30%)
- **Merchant Count:** Up 14% YoY (TTM merchant base over 2,24,000)
- **ARPU:** Up 18% YoY
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 8.9 crore (up 9x YoY)
- **Adjusted EBITDA per transaction:** Rs 1.45 (compared to Rs 0.22 last year)
- **Core Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 52.7 crore (margin 12.8%, 50 bps higher YoY)
- **Emerging Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** Improved from -38% to -24% (approx. 1380 bps improvement YoY)
- **Loss Before Tax:** Improved from Rs -18 crore to Rs -13.7 crore
- **Cross-sell rate:** 8.8% of core merchants bought emerging products (up from 7.3% last year)

### Highlights
- Revenue growth (34%) closely tracked transaction growth (36%).
- Emerging business growth was led by Omnichannel (up 92%) and MarTech.
- Cross-border business was down marginally due to global volatility impacting merchant confidence.
- Launched new product features: Quikpay (checkout and UPI preference saver), Steal Deal (cart recommendation and gamification), AI Assist (pre/post-order chatbot built via URL reading), AI Ads (automated creative generator), and Quick Commerce automated slot/truck aggregation for D2C brands.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management did not provide specific financial or timeframe guidance, noting that margins and contribution will be modeled based on historical overhead growth and segment mix.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's retail penetration is relatively low at ~8%, with half of e-commerce already originating in Tier 2+ cities where Shiprocket has a majority of its GMV.
- Addressing a massive addressable market of ~6 crore MSMEs in India.
- Larger D2C merchants performed strongly this quarter, driving higher retention and growth in the core segment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Contribution margin went up 43% YoY and 10% sequentially, growing faster than revenue.
- Core segment benefited from input cost rate compression from partners and favorable mix shift towards larger merchants.
- Emerging segment contribution margin improved from ~9-10% last year to 15.3% this quarter.
- Share-based payment expenses (non-cash) increased slightly, creating a delta between adjusted EBITDA and PAT.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company operates an asset-light model connecting over 250 partners, 42 couriers, payment gateways, and WMS without owning physical assets.
- Organization headcount is close to 1,500 people, with significant investments directed toward emerging businesses, product, and tech.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt, cash balance, or working capital absolute figures were discussed, aside from noting that adjusted EBITDA reflects cash generation into the bank.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global volatility has hit merchant confidence, impacting the cross-border sub-segment.
- Q3 seasonality: Unlike general e-commerce where Q3 is peak, Shiprocket tends to see slower trends in Q3 as direct-to-consumer merchants pull out of marketing and redirect inventory to marketplaces.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA Securities) on core EBITDA margin stabilization:** Asked if 12.5%–13% is steady-state. Management stated that adjusted EBITDA has scaled from 6% to 12.8% over three years, and the design of the core business allows incremental transactions to be margin-accretive after overheads.
2. **Della Desai (Axis Capital) on Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Inquired about fluctuations in core CAC (moving up to ~Rs 3,600). Management explained CAC reflects experimental digital marketing channels and short-term testing, but quick customer break-even ensures it stays within a manageable range.
3. **Avnish Sharma (JM Financial) on Power Merchant ARPU vs. Count:** Asked why power merchant count is lower while power ARPU is up 25%. Management noted that larger merchants grew more strongly while the overall merchant base acts as a funnel.
4. **Kunal Thanvi (Banyan Tree Advisors) on large D2C churn and Martech profitability:** Asked if large D2C brands bypass Shiprocket to work directly with 3PLs, and about Martech profitability. Management answered that instant multi-partner routing data remains valuable even for large brands, and Martech acts as a high-margin growth driver.
5. **Shreyansh Talesra (JM Financial) on realization per shipment decline:** Highlighted the implied drop in average realization based on TTM transactions and revenue. Management clarified that TTM transactions include non-shipping items (like checkout), and the revenue mix shift toward emerging businesses influenced the blended figure.

### One-line takeaway
Shiprocket reported robust Q1 FY27 results with 34% revenue growth and a 9x jump in adjusted EBITDA to Rs 8.9 crore, propelled by a 31% expansion in core transactions and rapid 70% growth in the emerging business segment.
