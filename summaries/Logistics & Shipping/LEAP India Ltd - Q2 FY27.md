# LEAP India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong year-on-year growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, alongside strategic acquisitions (Vimprotech and TIPS) and credit rating upgrades.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 216 crore (Total income Rs 2159 million, up 23% YoY; Asset pooling income Rs 1759 million, up 19%; MHE income Rs 393 million, up 52%)
- **Revenue Growth YoY:** 22.5% (Total income growth at 23% YoY; H1 total income up 26% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 110 crore (Rs 1,097 million, up 25% YoY; H1 EBITDA Rs 2,239 million, up 23%)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 50.8% (up 91 bps YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 17.8 crore (Rs 178 million, up 62% YoY; H1 PAT Rs 425 million, up 42%)
- **Net Debt / Debt to Equity:** Post-IPO debt-to-equity is about 0.48x; net debt-to-EBITDA is about 1.9x; company holds around INR 110 crores as investments.
- **Asset Base:** 15.5 million assets compared to 13.8 million a year ago (addition of 1.7 million). Pallet utilization at 89.3%, container utilization at 73.7%, MHE utilization at 82.3%.

### Highlights
- Acquired 100% of the business of Vimprotech and TIPS for an equity value of INR 142 crores via slump sales into wholly-owned subsidiaries (Vimprotech as the manufacturing arm, TIPS as the crate and tray pooling arm). 
- Vimprotech and TIPS reported FY26 revenue of INR 61.7 crores at an EBITDA margin of 28%, with a 44% revenue CAGR over the last three years.
- Set up wholly-owned subsidiaries in Saudi Arabia and the UAE (GCC expansion) with licenses and foundational infrastructure in place.
- India Ratings and Research upgraded LEAP's and TARON's long-term rating from A (stable) to A+.
- Added 37 new customers in Q2 (24 by LEAP, 13 by TARON), taking H1 additions to 85.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated organic revenue growth guidance between 20% to 27%.
- Management expects PAT growth to be around 60% to 80% for the year.
- Full-year capex is guided around INR 450 crores.
- DSO expected to be brought below 90 days by the end of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 is typically a subdued rainy season for the business, but strong momentum is expected in Q3 and Q4 due to pre-building in sectors like beverages (water, cola, juices, dairy).
- Automotive sector demand remains high, though constrained temporarily by fluctuation in plastic granule prices.
- Textile industry is facing a lull, functioning at low capacities due to granule costs.
- Quick-commerce and 3PL pallet penetration grew by 355% in the quarter.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Timber costs for pallets remain under control via Plinova and FOB contracts, shielding the company from container freight volatility.
- Plastic/granule costs saw sharp volatility, temporarily impacting container procurement decisions.
- EBITDA margin supported by cost optimization and network synergies from CHEP integration.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q2 capex stood at INR 213 crores (H1 FY27). FY27 total capex targeted at INR 450 crores.
- Vimprotech acquisition provides backward integration into plastic manufacturing, in-house design, and mold-making, expected to improve automotive margins by 10-12% and shorten turnaround times.
- GCC expansion is proceeding cautiously, evaluating potential acquisitions before aggressive customer pricing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Out of the INR 480 crores IPO fresh issue, INR 359.6 crores has been used to repay debt.
- DSO improved slightly to 122 days from 128 days at the start of the year, with a target of sub-90 days by year-end driven by SAP S/4HANA implementation.
- Cash flow from operations is expected to comfortably cover standard capex requirements starting FY28.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued fluctuation in timber and plastic granule costs.
- Customer working capital challenges related to purchase order (PO) limits and decentralized billing locations.
- Subdued demand/operations in certain sectors like textiles and plastic goods due to high raw material costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Krupashankar N.J. (Avendus) on Working Capital:** Asked about DSO reduction. Management stated SAP S/4HANA implementation will help bring DSO below 90 days by the end of FY27.
- **Angad Saluja on Vimprotech Acquisition Rationale:** Asked why LEAP is entering manufacturing. Management explained it provides in-house mold-making, protects intellectual property, ensures 1-week turnaround times for automotive clients, and improves margins.
- **Viraj Kadam on GCC Market Strategy:** Asked about operations amidst regional situations. Management replied they have licenses and are in touch with 26 customers, but are weighing potential local acquisitions before launching aggressively.
- **Vishal Mehta on Vimprotech Revenue Model:** Asked if outright sales of plastic products will continue. Management stated initial focus is on internal requirements (50-50 currently, moving to 100% in-house over time), cross-selling, and expanding pooling revenue.
- **Amit Thawani on Movement Hire Impact on Margins:** Asked about EBITDA improvement from higher movement hire. Management stated that increased movement hire increases per-pallet yields significantly without reducing EBITDA margins.

### One-line takeaway
LEAP India reported strong Q2 FY27 growth (revenue up 22.5% YoY, PAT up 62% YoY), bolstered its balance sheet via debt repayment from IPO proceeds, and expanded through strategic acquisitions in plastic manufacturing (Vimprotech) and crate pooling (TIPS).
