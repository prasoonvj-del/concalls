# Gateway Distriparks Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a sequential volume recovery, normalization of container business performance back to pre-war quarterly levels (100,000 TEUs and Rs 100 crore EBITDA), and positive long-term prospects from Western DFC operations and Ankleshwar EXIM integration.

### Key numbers
- **Rail Volumes:** Maintained a bare minimum target of 100,000 TEUs per quarter (Samvid Gupta).
- **Blended Realization:** Improved by ~5% YoY and ~7% QoQ (Samvid Gupta).
- **EXIM Trade Imbalance:** Trending at 55% import and 44% export (Rajguru Behgal).
- **Snowman Pallet Storage Growth:** ~10% YoY increase in terms of number of pallets stored (Padamdeep Singh Handa).
- **Snowman Capacity Addition:** Bringing up close to 23,000 pallets in the next few months (Padamdeep Singh Handa).
- **Snowman Utilization:** Utilized at 85%-86% throughout the year (Padamdeep Singh Handa).
- **Snowman EBITDA:** On track to cross Rs 100 crore EBITDA again this year (Samvid Gupta).
- **New Project Return Targets:** At least 15% IRR and a 7-year payback (Samvid Gupta).

### Highlights
- Gateway Distriparks operated the first double-stack train on 20th June when the DFC was completed.
- Ankleshwar MMLP has received all EXIM permissions in place; Q3 will see volumes start to contribute.
- Snowman Logistics focused on warehousing and transport improvements while keeping 5PL growth selective due to lower margins.
- Timetable trains started from the Gurgaon terminal and trials conducted for JNPT, improving transit reliability and customer confidence.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Rail Volumes:** Management targets a bare minimum of 100,000 TEUs per quarter for the rest of the year, with double-digit growth anticipated next year due to a full year of Ankleshwar EXIM operations (Samvid Gupta).
- **Snowman EBITDA:** On track to cross Rs 100 crores EBITDA for the full year (Samvid Gupta).
- **Indore ICD:** Targeted to be operational by December 2027, with volume contributions expected within the first year (Samvid Gupta).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Macro uncertainty persists with monthly fluctuations, but volumes have recovered to normalized levels of 100,000 TEUs per quarter and Rs 100 crores EBITDA for the container business (Samvid Gupta).
- EXIM trade imbalance stands at 56% import and 44% export (Rajguru Behgal).
- Rail coefficient at JNPT is currently low at 14% to 15%, but expected to increase with the Western DFC and rail-road shift (Rajguru Behgal).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel price hikes were passed on to customers along with corresponding cost increases (Samvid Gupta).
- Snowman segment margins impacted due to wage code implementation (gratuity and PF ceiling limit changes) and state minimum wage hikes (Karnataka up ~70%, Haryana 35%) which are pending annual contract renewals (Samvid Gupta).
- Park-and-pay capacity growth in Snowman carries thinner spot margins under 10% (Samvid Gupta).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Snowman is constructing around 23,000 odd pallets, with another 20,000 pallets under discussion (Padamdeep Singh Handa).
- Indore ICD civil and rail works are in full swing, targeting operational status by December 2027 (Samvid Gupta).
- Jaipur ICD construction is subject to a court order; management expects a favorable ruling soon and an 18-month timeline to get it up and running fully (Samvid Gupta).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Snowman continues to reinvest operational cash flows into long-term assets, resulting in ongoing interest and depreciation costs (Samvid Gupta).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global macro issues, ongoing wars, and lack of clear tariff situations (Samvid Gupta).
- Shipping lines have not yet given indications of shifting vessel calls heavily to JNPT (Samvid Gupta).
- Regulatory cost pressures including minimum wage hikes and gratuity/PF rule changes impacting operating margins before contract resets (Samvid Gupta).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jainam Shah (Equirus Securities) asked about the sustainability of the sequential rail volume recovery (11%) and next year's outlook.** Management (Samvid Gupta) responded that 100,000 TEUs per quarter is the bare minimum target and guided for double-digit growth next year driven by Ankleshwar EXIM operations.
2. **Prashant Kale (Individual Investor) questioned why Snowman Logistics generates negligible net profit despite long operations.** Management (Samvid Gupta) explained that heavy reinvestment into 30-40 year assets drives high depreciation and interest, and they prioritize operational cash flow and EBITDA growth over accounting PAT figures.
3. **Aditya Mongia (Kotak Securities) asked about the EBITDA impact and timeline of the Western DFC linkage.** Management (Samvid Gupta & Rajguru Behgal) stated it is hard to predict exact EBITDA timing because it depends on shipping line vessel routing decisions at JNPT, though 10,000 to 15,000 TEUs moving by road to North are addressable targets over time.
4. **Kunal Tokas asked why Snowman's warehousing margins have structurally declined over the years.** Management (Samvid Gupta & Padamdeep Singh Handa) clarified that the expansion of low-margin park-and-pay spot capacity, build-to-suit assets, and 5PL business mix dilutes the overall segment percentage margin despite absolute EBITDA growth.
5. **Prateek Giri (Shubh Labh Research) asked if Indian Railways is offering tariff interventions to compete with Mundra/Pipavav post-DFC.** Management (Rajguru Behgal) confirmed representations have been made to rationalize tariffs post-JNPT, but it is too early to comment on ultimate benefits.

### One-line takeaway
Gateway Distriparks reported a sequential volume recovery back to normalized pre-war levels of 100,000 TEUs/quarter, banking on Western DFC connectivity and Ankleshwar EXIM integration to drive double-digit growth next year.
