# JSW Infrastructure Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue and logistics growth driven by domestic volumes, offset by Fujairah operational halts, forex losses, and lower PAT.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Operating Revenue:** Rs 1,481 crore (+17% YoY). (H1 FY27: Rs 2,926 crore, +18% YoY).
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA:** Rs 674 crore (+11% YoY). (H1 FY27: Rs 1,348 crore, +13% YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 328 crore (vs Rs 369 crore in Q2 FY26, -11.1% YoY).
- **Consolidated Cargo Volume:** 29.4 million tonnes (+2% YoY; India operations grew 8% YoY). H1 FY27 volume: 60.5 million tonnes (+10% YoY for India operations).
- **Port Segment Revenue & EBITDA:** Revenue at Rs 1,202 crore (+9% YoY); EBITDA at Rs 594 crore (+1.5% YoY); Port EBITDA Margin dipped to 49.4% (vs 53.1% in Q2 FY26).
- **Logistics Segment EBITDA:** Rs 81 crore (vs Rs 25 crore in Q2 FY26, +224% YoY); EBITDA Margin expanded to 29% (vs 15% in Q2 FY26).
- **Third-Party Cargo Share:** 45% of total volumes in Q2 FY27 (India third-party volume grew 13% YoY).

### Highlights
- Signed a 30-year concession agreement for the 13 MTPA Outer Container Terminal at NSD Dock, Kolkata (total container handling capacity in Kolkata to reach ~1.4 million TEUs).
- Commenced interim operations at the 6.3 MTPA Kolkata container terminal during the quarter.
- Logistics rail rake fleet expanded from 42 rakes in Q1 FY27 to 78 rakes by Q2 FY27-end (72 operational since October 2026).
- Navkar Corporation handled domestic cargo volumes of 459,000 MT (+16% YoY) and EXIM volumes of 88,000 TEUs (+12% YoY) in Q2 FY27.
- Expanded logistics network via acquisition of NCR Rail Infrastructure Ltd, ICD approval at Kudathini (Ballari), and award of Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal at Kulipalaiyam (Tamil Nadu).

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 EBITDA Guidance:** Management target remains at Rs 2,950 crore to Rs 3,000 crore overall (Ports EBITDA targeted at Rs 2,550–2,560 crore; Logistics EBITDA targeted at >Rs 400 crore).
- **Logistics Margin Guidance:** Revised FY27 Logistics EBITDA margin guidance upward to 28% (from 24%) due to netting off haulage charges for specialized (LSFTO) rakes.
- **Port Capacity Targets:** Management reiterated expanding total port capacity from 186 MTPA to 300 MTPA by FY28 and 400 MTPA by FY30.
- **Volume Growth:** Management expects H2 FY27 domestic cargo volume growth between 8% and 10% YoY.
- **Fleet Expansion:** Rake fleet targeted to reach at least 110 rakes by March 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India port operations grew 8% YoY in Q2 FY27, outperforming general Indian industry cargo growth of ~5%.
- Captive steel cargo remains stable, while third-party growth is being driven by higher utilization at Paradip Coal, Ennore Bulk, Mangalore, Tuticorin, and newly added Kolkata terminals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Port EBITDA margin contracted to 49.4% from 53.1% YoY due to nil contribution from Fujairah and higher volume contribution from lower-margin coal terminals.
- Depreciation increased to Rs 175 crore (vs Rs 149 crore in Q1 FY27) and Finance Costs rose to Rs 108 crore (vs Rs 99 crore in Q2 FY26).
- PBT fell to Rs 431 crore (from Rs 463 crore in Q2 FY26) due to unrealized forex losses from rupee depreciation, yield curve shifts, and higher depreciation, partially offset by higher other income from unutilized QIP proceeds.
- PAT was impacted by higher effective tax rates due to missing tax-exempt earnings from Fujairah.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex Outlay:** Cumulative spent capex reached Rs 8,250 crore as of September 2026 (Rs 2,100 crore spent in H1 FY27, including Rs 1,500 crore in Q2 FY27). Outstanding committed capex orders total ~Rs 6,000 crore, with capex creditors at ~Rs 950 crore. Total expected FY27 spend is Rs 5,000–6,000 crore out of the Rs 16,500 crore multi-year capex plan.
- **Slurry Pipeline:** ~257 km pipe lowering completed (~88% scope); management expects commissioning by March 2027.
- **Jatadhar Port:** Mechanical and berth works on track for completion by March/April 2027 (Panamax handling); Cape-size facilities target Q3 FY28. Waterfront can accommodate expansion up to 100–120 MTPA.
- **Jaigarh & Dharamtar Expansion:** Jaigarh LPG berth operations expected by March 2027 (tankage completion Q3 FY28); mechanization complete by Q1 FY28 for Jaigarh and Q4 FY27 for Dharamtar.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 1,693 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Funds from the Rs 6,400 crore net QIP proceeds are currently invested in fixed deposits and mutual funds, earning interest until drawn down for capex.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Fujairah Liquid Terminal:** Generated zero revenue and an EBITDA loss of ~Rs 16 crore in H1 FY27 due to ongoing fixed overheads and regional non-operation. Testing of 8 tanks is underway for early November restart, but local product storage demand remains low/fluid.
- **Paradip Iron Ore Terminal:** Experienced a temporary drop in Q2 volumes due to regulatory restrictions on transportation moisture limits following a ship sinking off the coast in August, causing inventory to stack up to 8 lakh tonnes; operations normalized post-September.
- **Keni Port:** Pending Environmental Clearance (EC); KCZMA recommended it to MOEFCC, with EC expected in 3 to 4 months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** *What is the margin profile of the specialized rail rake business?*  
  **Management:** LSFTO rake margins are ~90% because haulage charges are netted off, and only premiums and rebates are recognized as revenue.
- **Atul Tiwari (JPMorgan):** *When will Fujairah operations and EBITDA resume?*  
  **Management:** Testing of 8 tanks is underway with final permission expected in late October to start operations in early November. However, tank storage demand in the region remains fluid and is being watched closely.
- **Priyankar Biswas (JM Financial):** *How will Jatadhar Port accommodate JSW Steel's capacity expansions beyond 30 MTPA?*  
  **Management:** The port has sufficient waterfront to scale capacity up to 100–120 MTPA. Inbound/outbound logistics will be supported by a common rail corridor (taking ~2 years) and the slurry pipeline.
- **Koundinya Nimmagadda (Jefferies):** *How will management achieve the full-year FY27 EBITDA target given Fujairah disruptions?*  
  **Management:** Revised Port EBITDA expectation to Rs 2,550–2,560 crore and Logistics EBITDA target to >Rs 400 crore (driven by full H2 deployment of 78–110 rakes), delivering overall EBITDA in the Rs 2,950–3,000 crore range.

### One-line takeaway
JSW Infrastructure is successfully scaling its high-margin logistics and domestic port terminals to offset geopolitical disruptions at its Fujairah facility and stay on track for its long-term expansion goals.
