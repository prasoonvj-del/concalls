# Allcargo Global Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - The company returned to net profitability in Q2 FY27 driven by elevated freight rates, operational cost controls, and yield management.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained target of reaching Rs 500 crore annualized EBITDA in 2027; management noted current operational performance is running 2-3 quarters ahead of trajectory due to industry tailwinds.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from Cautious to Positive; returning to net profitability driven by elevated freight rates and internal operational initiatives despite ongoing Middle East volume headwinds.
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared:** Proposed changing the financial year alignment to a January–December calendar (shorter FY ending December 2026); highlighted historical bad debt cleanup from closed Middle East businesses targeted for completion by December 2026. Asset monetization discussions from last quarter were not raised.
- **Last quarter's promises and delivery:** Promised flat SG&A/staff costs in USD terms was delivered (~8% drop YoY in USD; flat in INR); promised return to net profitability was delivered (PAT Rs 32 crore vs loss of Rs 28 crore in Q1 FY27).

---

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 4,320 crore (up 27% YoY, 23% QoQ)
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 837 crore (up 21% YoY, 14% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 122 crore (vs Rs 37 crore in Q2 FY26 and Rs 33 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 2.8% (up 170 bps YoY, up 190 bps QoQ)
- **EBIT:** Profit of Rs 71 crore (vs loss of Rs 49 crore in Q2 FY26 and loss of Rs 23 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT:** Profit of Rs 32 crore (vs loss of Rs 30 crore in Q2 FY26 and loss of Rs 28 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 7,842 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 1,570 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 155 crore (vs Rs 6 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 4 crore (vs loss of Rs 117 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Volumes YoY:** LCL down 5.7%, FCL down 12%, Air down 10%
- **Volumes QoQ:** LCL up 2.9%, FCL up 1.3%, Air up 16%
- **Net Debt:** Rs 587 crore (vs Rs 571 crore as of June 30, 2026)
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 412 crore (vs Rs 374 crore at start of year)

---

### Highlights
- Reached quarterly profitability with net profit of Rs 32 crore.
- Board considering moving global reporting cycle to a January–December fiscal year, creating a shorter financial period ending December 2026.
- Management estimated 70% of recent operational improvement is driven by internal efficiency initiatives (procurement, AI/automation, trade lane selection) and 20–30% by industry freight rate tailwinds.
- *Continues record pattern:* Validates the strong sequential financial recovery and return to profitability reported in recent disclosures.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- Reiterated guidance of achieving Rs 500 crore annualized EBITDA for CY2027 (Jan–Dec 2027).
- Target outgrowing industry volume with 6%–7% growth in both LCL and FCL over a normal status quo baseline (industry expected at 5–6% LCL, 3–4% FCL).
- Management targets expanding gross profit to Rs 900–1,000 crore per quarter (~Rs 3,600 crore annually) over the next two years.
- Freight rates expected to gradually soften over the next 4–5 months as peak festive demand ebbs.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Geopolitical tensions, West Asia disruptions, and port congestion continue to constrain global container capacity, keeping ocean freight rates elevated.
- YoY volume declines were primarily driven by market headwinds in the Middle East region.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Staff and admin costs remained flat YoY and QoQ in INR terms, while declining ~8% YoY in USD terms.
- Strategy is to keep absolute operating cost base flat by absorbing inflation through automation, AI tools, and process centralization.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Business remains asset-light with minimal P&L impact from capex; depreciation line is largely non-cash goodwill amortization from past acquisitions and is expected to trend down.
- No major acquisitions planned in the near future.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt slightly increased to Rs 587 crore due to higher working capital absorption from elevated freight rates.
- Receivables DSO maintained at 46–47 days.
- Cash tax payouts will remain lower than accounting P&L provisions due to tax loss carry-forwards in recently turned-around overseas entities.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential near-term P&L volatility if ocean freight rates fall faster than operational efficiencies compound.
- Ongoing geopolitical conflicts (West Asia crisis) disrupting volume flows across Middle East lanes.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A

- **Impact of Freight Rates on Performance:** Management noted that 20–30% of current margin expansion is rate-driven, which may soften over 4–5 months, but core operational improvements will offset the decline over 2–3 quarters. *(Answered clearly)*
- **SG&A Cost Reduction vs Global Peers:** Management explained European peers reduce SG&A through post-acquisition staff cuts, whereas Allcargo's organic model focuses on keeping local costs flat (~8% USD cost reduction achieved YoY). *(Answered clearly)*

### One-line takeaway
Allcargo Global returned to net profitability in Q2 FY27 with Rs 122 crore EBITDA, leveraging tech-led cost containment and elevated freight rates to remain ahead of its guidance path toward CY2027 targets.
