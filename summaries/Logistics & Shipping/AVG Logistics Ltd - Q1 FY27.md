# AVG Logistics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-29 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 5.97% YoY while PAT grew 29.98% YoY, driven by operational efficiency and improved asset utilization.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 132.48 crore (up 5.97% YoY).
*   **PBT:** Rs 8.71 crore (up 24.42% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 6.46 crore (up 29.98% YoY).
*   **PBT Margin:** 6.58% (up 98 bps YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 4.87% (up 89 bps YoY).
*   **Net Debt (as of March '26):** ~Rs 173 crore.
*   **Capex (FY26):** Rs 62 crore.
*   **Dividend:** Rs 1.20 per equity share (for FY26).

### Highlights
*   **New Business:** Secured a long-term contract from Haldiram, Nagpur for 100 dedicated vehicles (40 deployed as of Aug 21, 2026; remaining 60 in fabrication).
*   **Green Logistics:** Incorporated a JV with Baidyanath Group (Carbonlite Logistics Private Limited) to focus on LNG, EV, and CNG transportation; operations expected to commence Oct 1, 2026.
*   **Capital Raise:** Raised Rs 52.93 crore via rights issue to strengthen working capital and support expansion.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Management is focusing on "sweating assets" by improving fleet productivity, reducing empty runs, and optimizing freight planning.
*   **Technology:** Implemented in-house software for real-time vehicle tracking and dashcams to improve customer visibility and asset utilization.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains an objective of 15% to 20% revenue growth for FY27.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Management expects ~7-8% growth from new business and ~8-10% from existing customer expansion.
*   **Seasonality:** Management expects higher volumes in Q3 and Q4 compared to Q1 and Q2.
*   **Margins:** Management expects margins to improve as assets acquired in FY26 are fully utilized throughout FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Logistics sector is benefiting from manufacturing growth, infrastructure spending (PM Gati Shakti, National Logistics Policy), and e-commerce expansion.
*   Customers are increasingly demanding "cleaner" transportation solutions (LNG, CNG, EV).
*   Shift observed toward organized logistics partners capable of managing supply chains rather than just providing transportation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Fuel escalation clauses in many contracts help mitigate fuel price volatility.
*   Specialized segments (dedicated vehicles, cold chain, warehousing) offer higher margins than traditional transportation.
*   EV and LNG operations are expected to yield better margins due to lower operational costs compared to diesel.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex Guidance:** Rs 50-60 crore.
*   **Strategy:** Balancing asset ownership with leasing (especially for EVs and tankers) to maintain balance sheet health.
*   **Fleet Addition:** Targeting addition of ~200 vehicles by March '27 (CNG, LNG, EV).
*   **Expansion:** Haldiram management has requested an additional 100 vehicles by Dec '26.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.67x.
*   **Current Ratio:** ~1.97x.
*   Rights issue proceeds are being used to strengthen working capital and support expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management noted that depreciation will increase due to the addition of new assets.
*   Seasonality: Q1 and Q2 are historically slower than the second half of the fiscal year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: How much growth is organic vs. new business?* A: Management expects 15-20% total growth, split roughly 50/50 between new business and existing customer expansion.
*   *Q: Impact of rights issue on financials?* A: Management stated it is difficult to isolate specific revenue/PAT impact, but proceeds strengthen working capital and support overall growth.
*   *Q: Why lease vs. buy?* A: To maintain balance sheet discipline while supporting growth; leasing is preferred for EVs/tankers due to high upfront costs.
*   *Q: Progress on liquid logistics?* A: Two tankers purchased; future tankers/trains will be leased.
*   *Q: How does the in-house software help?* A: It provides real-time tracking and ETAs to customers, which allows for better loading/unloading planning and increased vehicle utilization.

### One-line takeaway
AVG Logistics is prioritizing margin expansion through asset sweating and specialized, green-energy logistics, while maintaining a balanced capital allocation strategy between ownership and leasing.
