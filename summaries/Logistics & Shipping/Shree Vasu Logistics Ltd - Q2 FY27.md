# Shree Vasu Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT driven by new client additions, repeat business in new territories, and turnaround of new businesses.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 144.98 crore (up 37.2% YoY from Rs 105.7 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 37.2 crore (up 38.6% YoY from Rs 26.0 crore)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 25.7% (vs 24.6% in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 8.4 crore (up 171% YoY from Rs 3.1 crore)
- **H1 FY27 EPS:** Rs 7.25 (vs Rs 2.68 in H1 FY26)
- **Q1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 64.8 crore (up 30% YoY from Rs 49.8 crore)
- **Q1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 16.7 crore (up 34% YoY from Rs 12.4 crore)
- **Q1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 3.3 crore (up 74% YoY from Rs 1.9 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 80.2 crore (up 43.2% YoY from Rs 56.0 crore; up 23.9% QoQ from Q1 FY27)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 20.5 crore (vs Rs 13.6 crore in Q2 FY26; Rs 16.7 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 25.6% (vs 25.8% in Q1 FY27)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 5.1 crore (vs Rs 1.2 crore in Q2 FY26; Rs 3.3 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Client Concentration:** Top 20 clients account for around 30% of business out of 136 total clients.
- **Fleet Size:** Around 125 owned vehicles (out of total 750 vehicles).
- **Network Reach:** 16 states, 1 union territory, 43 cities, 2.6 million square feet of warehousing space, 10,000+ employees.

### Highlights
- Delivered strong first-half performance with robust growth across turnover and earnings, supported by a strong order book for new clients and enhanced executive leadership.
- Generated repeat business from current clients in new territories and successfully turned around new businesses started last year.
- Operations are now running across all of India with proper regional team setups.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they will be further scaling up in all zones in the coming months.
- Strategy focuses on scaling up warehousing footprints across high-demand zones, adopting advanced supply chain automation, and deepening relationships with marquee enterprise clients.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Long-term partnerships characterize the business model, reducing reliance on short-term renewal activities since the company holds crores of stock for clients.
- Serving enterprise clients like Dabur, Parle, and JK Tyre across multiple locations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EV adoption is currently moving slow because overall acquisition and running costs are higher than normal vehicles; management treats it as a comparative cost analysis since costs are back-to-back cost-backed for clients.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Open to acquisition offers, but currently focusing heavily on incoming organic growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management regarding operations or assets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rajkumari Duggad (Individual Investor):** Asked about client concentration and churn. Management replied that the client book is well-threaded with 136 clients, top 20 contributing ~30%, and long-term partnerships minimize renewal/churn risks.
- **Rajkumari Duggad (Individual Investor):** Asked about inorganic growth vs. greenfield capacity. Management stated they are open to acquisitions but are currently prioritizing organic growth.
- **Zoom User:** Asked about fleet mix (owned vs. attached/hired) and EV plans. Management clarified they operate ~125 owned vehicles out of 750 total vehicles (alongside market-hired vehicles, contract transporters, and couriers) and are going slow on EVs due to higher acquisition and running costs.
- **Zoom User:** Asked about insurance covers and GST demands on insurance claims. Management stated incidents are rare (1-2 per year), and claims are handled directly via the client's insurance policies since the goods carried are on the client's invoice.

### One-line takeaway
Shree Vasu Logistics reported a strong H1 FY27 with revenue up 37.2% YoY to Rs 144.98 crore and PAT surging 171% to Rs 8.4 crore, driven by new client additions, repeat business in new territories, and successful execution.
