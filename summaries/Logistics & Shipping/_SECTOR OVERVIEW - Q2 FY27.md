# Logistics & Shipping: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector delivered a broadly positive Q2 FY27 with most companies reporting revenue growth, margin expansion, and strong order books. Domestic demand remains resilient across FMCG, auto, e-commerce, quick commerce, and infrastructure. Green logistics (EV/CNG/LNG fleets) is scaling rapidly in heavy industry. The multimodal shift to rail via the Western DFC is moving from infrastructure readiness to volume migration. Technology and AI are being deployed at scale for route optimization, sorting automation, and customer-facing platforms. International expansion is accelerating through acquisitions and JVs. A few pockets of weakness appear in cash management (manpower cost spikes), EXIM trade (geopolitical headwinds), and shipping (late-quarter rate correction).

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Indian logistics has historically been fragmented, asset-heavy, road-dominated, and low-tech. Over the last ~5 years, organized players have gained share through asset-light platforms, tech-enabled marketplaces, multimodal integration, and a shift from spot transactions to contracted, yield-managed relationships. Green logistics and specialized services (cold chain, warehousing, express) have emerged as margin-accretive verticals. [Background]

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: Domestic consumption strong – FMCG, textiles, auto, engineering, chemicals, e-commerce, quick commerce. Green logistics demand rising from cement/steel (carbon targets). Quick commerce & D2C/SME driving express parcel volumes (Delhivery 16–20% FY27 guidance, Shadowfax 27–30% CAGR, TCI Express e-com +51.6% YoY). Infrastructure/manufacturing capex supporting project logistics (Caliber Mining OB removal 72% YoY, Knowledge Marine maintenance dredging ~150M cu.m/yr market). EXIM trade resilient in value but facing geopolitical headwinds (Middle East, Red Sea routing disruptions).

**Divergence**:
- **Radiant Cash Management**: “Underwhelming” – flat cash volumes (Rs 0.41T), margins halved (standalone EBITDA 7.6% vs 15.2% YoY) due to manpower cost spikes (min wages, armed guard shortage).
- **Snowman Logistics**: “Mixed” – macro uncertainties, 5PL sluggish (one customer), wage cost pressures (Karnataka +70%, Haryana +35% min wages) ahead of contract renewals.
- **Great Eastern Shipping**: Late-quarter crude tanker rate correction (Suezmax $73k vs $93k Q1) as Hormuz traffic normalized; “shuttle service” inefficiency (8–9 days) unwinding.
- **Allcargo Global**: Middle East volume headwinds persist despite return to profitability.
- **Western Carriers**: EXIM TEUs down QoQ (30.4k vs 34.4k) but domestic +21% QoQ; SAARC/Bangladesh green shoots.
- **CONCOR**: Geopolitical uncertainty (West Asia) affecting shipping schedules, empty container availability; domestic tile/gunny bale recovery but food grains hit by busy season surcharge.
- **Skyways**: Exports strong (pharma, electronics, defence, auto) but imports only 5–6% of revenue; targeting 40–42% import growth.
- **Caliber Mining**: Customers pushing 105–110%+ of contracted coal volumes on rising power demand.
- **Knowledge Marine**: Maintenance dredging market ~Rs 2,000 cr/yr; green tug programme targets 98 tugs across major ports by 2047.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Fuel**: Pass-through mechanisms widely used – AVG 75–80% corporate clients (10–15 day lag); VRL contractual 17–18% revenue (fuel escalation clauses); Western Carriers DPVC (road) & railway haulage linkage (rail); TCI 2/3 of revenue growth pricing-led (bunker $95–98k/tonne → freight +10–15%); Seamec long-term charters (Swordfish, Paladin, Agastya, Anant) – fuel borne by charterer. Great Eastern blended fleet break-even ~$13k/day.

**Manpower / wages**: Major pressure point – Radiant (70% costs, min wage revisions, armed guard shortage South); Snowman (gratuity/PF ceiling, Karnataka +70%, Haryana +35% min wages); Mahindra (PF slab Rs 15k→25k, not fully passed through); CMS (statutory min wages +6–60% across states); LEAP (timber/plastic granule volatility); TCI Express (automation reducing labor component 40–50 bps direct cost).

**Margin expansion**: VRL EBITDA 23.3% (+367 bps YoY); AVG PAT 4.7% vs 3.6%; Allcargo Logistics EBITDA 13.9% (+240 bps); Allcargo Terminals EBITDA/TEU +40.8% YoY; Flomic EBITDA 10.88% (+~300 bps); TCI Express 12.7% (+30 bps, best in 5 quarters); Shadowfax 5% adjusted EBITDA (first time); Delhivery 4% adjusted EBITDA (target 16–18% long-term); Mahindra 5.9% (+90 bps); TVS 7.3% (+60 bps); Gateway blended realization +5% YoY, +7% QoQ; Seamec steady-state EBITDA target ~40%; Knowledge Marine H1 EBITDA 55%; LEAP 50.8% (+91 bps); Maharaja & Speedex 19.2%; Shree Vasu 25.6%; Skyways 4.46%; Caliber Mining reported 16.6%.

**Green fleet economics**: AVG – EV/CNG margins better than diesel; solar charging Rs 4–5/unit vs industrial Rs 8–10/unit; optimized routing & higher trip counts.
