# Logistics & Shipping: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

The Indian logistics and shipping sector demonstrated remarkable resilience in Q2 FY27. While geopolitical headwinds—specifically in West Asia—created turbulence for international trade routes, domestic demand remained robust. Managements are increasingly focusing on operational efficiency, automation, and capacity expansion to navigate inflationary pressures and labor cost hikes.

## Big picture
The sector is characterized by a "growth with discipline" narrative. Companies are reporting record volumes and revenues (Aegis, CONCOR, Shadowfax, TCI Express), but the tone is tempered by a watchful eye on global trade patterns. The consensus is that while the macro environment remains volatile, the structural shift toward organized logistics and infrastructure-led growth (DFC, warehousing) is providing a strong tailwind.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is broad agreement on strong volume growth. **Allcargo Terminals** reported 12.6% YoY volume growth, **CONCOR** achieved record handling volumes, and **Shadowfax** saw a 70% YoY surge in orders.
*   **Divergence:** While most are bullish, **Delhivery** is prioritizing profitability over "unfettered" volume growth, citing macro volatility and consumption declines in specific verticals. **Mahindra Logistics** noted that while demand is strong, the monsoon's uneven distribution created regional variations that required internal management.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Fuel Pass-through:** Most players are successfully passing on fuel price hikes. **VRL Logistics** noted that every Rs 2 change in fuel rates leads to a 1% change in freight rates. **Mahindra Logistics** and **TCI** confirmed fuel costs are fully passed through via contracts.
*   **Labor & Wage Pressures:** A recurring theme is rising labor costs due to minimum wage hikes and PF slab changes. **Mahindra Logistics**, **Gateway Distriparks**, and **Shadowfax** highlighted this as a margin headwind that is not always fully passable.
*   **Efficiency Levers:** Automation is the primary defense against margin erosion. **Delhivery** and **TCI Express** specifically credited automation (sorters, tractor-trailers) for productivity gains and cost reduction. **VRL Logistics** maintained industry-leading margins through high-density infrastructure and low claim ratios.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is in a heavy investment cycle:
*   **Aggressive Expansion:** **Aegis Vopak** is pursuing a $5 billion capex plan through 2030. **Trishakti Industries** launched "Mission 2030" to exceed a Rs 1,000 crore gross block. **CONCOR** has an internal capex target of Rs 945 crore for FY27.
*   **Strategic Infrastructure:** **Allcargo** is finalizing land for a new Chennai facility. **Gateway Distriparks** is focused on the Indore ICD (operational by Dec 2027) and Western DFC connectivity. **TCI Express** is converting leased sorting centers to owned assets to improve long-term control.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Tone Shift:** Several companies shifted from cautious to optimistic. **Allcargo** moved from "stable/positive" to "highly optimistic" following record October volumes. **Black Buck** shifted from "cautious" regarding macro headwinds to "business as usual."
*   **Geopolitical Focus:** The impact of the West Asia crisis became more explicit. **CONCOR** noted trade shifts to Khor Fakkan/Fujairah, while **Allcargo** and **Flomic** highlighted that transshipment gains are temporary and transit times remain impacted.
*   **Guidance:** Most companies maintained their guidance, signaling confidence in their internal execution despite external volatility.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Geopolitical Stability:** Will the West Asia situation stabilize, or will transit times/freight rates remain volatile?
*   **Labor Costs:** Will companies successfully pass on the rising wage/PF costs, or will margins compress?
*   **DFC Impact:** Will the Western DFC lead to a meaningful modal shift from road to rail for Gateway and CONCOR?
*   **Festive Season Spillovers:** How did the Q3 festive season impact the "build phase" for companies like TCI Express and Shadowfax?
