# Logistics & Shipping: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Across the sector, Q2 FY27 was characterized by robust top-line expansion, scaling of tech and automation, and extensive infrastructure investments. Most companies reported resilient performance despite seasonal rains and macroeconomic volatility, banking on upcoming festive demand (Q3) and structural tailwinds like the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board anticipate stronger momentum in Q3 and Q4 due to the festive season pre-building and peak volume inflows. 
*   **Divergence:** While companies like **Shadowfax Technologies** and **Jet Freight Logistics** are aggressively scaling D2C, e-commerce, and courier volumes, **Delhivery** noted that it is actively prioritizing profitability over "unfettered" volume growth to avoid claims and losses in a volatile macro environment. Meanwhile, **VRL Logistics** and **Transport Corporation of India (TCI)** experienced stable demand across core freight, with VRL noting a 20% October tonnage jump driven by vehicle shortages among competitor networks.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Fuel & Pricing:** Rising fuel and bunker costs were a common theme. **TCI**, **VRL Logistics**, **Jet Freight**, and **Gateway Distriparks** reported passing on fuel price hikes through freight rate adjustments. **VRL** specifically noted that every Rs 2 change in fuel rates typically leads to a 1% change in freight rates.
*   **Statutory & Wage Pressures:** **Gateway Distriparks** and **Snowman Logistics** highlighted margin pressures from out-of-turn statutory wage hikes (gratuity, PF ceiling limit changes, and sharp state minimum wage increases like Karnataka up ~70% and Haryana 35%), which are awaiting annual contract renewals.
*   **Raw Material Divergence:** **LEAP India** noted that timber costs remain under control via long-term contracts, but sharp volatility in plastic/granule costs temporarily impacted container procurement.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Heavy Infrastructure & Fleet Expansion:** Several companies are aggressively expanding physical footprints. **LEAP India** guides for FY27 capex of ~INR 450 crores and acquired Vimprotech/TIPS for backward integration. **TCI** guides for Rs 500-550 crore capex for FY27, adding new ships (e.g., *TCI Setu*). **VRL Logistics** projects H2 FY27 capex at Rs 150-160 crore for commercial vehicles and hub facilities. **Gateway** and **Snowman** are building out ICDs (Indore, Jaipur) and ~23,000 new pallet spaces. **Shadowfax** expanded operating space to 69 lakh sq. ft. and is building out micro-fulfillment centers (MFCs).
*   **Asset-Lite Alternatives:** **Jet Freight Logistics** continues to maintain a strictly asset-lite model, outsourcing all warehouses and surface transportation without owning real estate.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Black Buck Ltd:** Shifted tone from "cautious" regarding macro headwinds in Q1 to "business as usual" in Q2, noting that seasonal rain-related weakness was compensated by strong performance.

## Company one-liners
*   **BlackBuck:** Delivered robust 50%+ revenue growth and maintained strong profitability, successfully balancing aggressive investments in new growth engines with a high-margin core business.
*   **Delhivery:** Delhivery is successfully leveraging automation to drive profitability and efficiency, maintaining its growth guidance despite a volatile macro environment.
*   **Gateway Distriparks:** Gateway Distriparks reported a sequential volume recovery back to normalized pre-war levels of 100,000 TEUs/quarter, banking on Western DFC connectivity and Ankleshwar EXIM integration.
*   **Jet Freight Logistics:** Jet Freight Logistics is leveraging its asset-lite model, expanding into South American trade lanes and retail e-commerce courier, and pursuing a Dubai acquisition to drive scale.
*   **LEAP India:** LEAP India reported strong Q2 FY27 growth (revenue up 22.5% YoY, PAT up 62% YoY), bolstered its balance sheet via IPO proceeds, and expanded through strategic acquisitions.
*   **Shadowfax Technologies:** Shadowfax delivered a milestone Q2 FY27 with Rs 1,500+ crore revenue and a 5% adjusted EBITDA margin, driven by rapid D2C/SME expansion and scaling infrastructure.
*   **Shree Vasu Logistics:** Shree Vasu Logistics reported a strong H1 FY27 with revenue up 37.2% YoY to Rs 144.98 crore and PAT surging 171% to Rs 8.4 crore.
*   **Snowman Logistics:** Snowman and GDL are expanding infrastructure and leveraging the Western DFC and new MMLPs, though near-term profitability is tempered by statutory cost inflation.
*   **Transport Corporation of India:** TCI is navigating a transition phase with significant capex and fleet expansion, maintaining a 10-12% growth outlook while managing cost pressures.
*   **VRL Logistics:** VRL Logistics delivered a robust H2/H1 FY27 marked by 20% revenue growth, a 367 bps YoY expansion in EBITDA margin to 23.3%, and strong cash generation.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Execution and volume contributions from the festive season (Q3) across express parcel, courier, and freight segments.
*   Ramp-up of operations at Ankleshwar MMLP and initial volume flow from Western DFC linkages (**Gateway Distriparks** / **Snowman**).
*   Crude oil and diesel/bunker price fluctuations and their subsequent pass-through into freight rates (**VRL**, **TCI**, **Jet Freight**).

*Based on: Black Buck Ltd, Delhivery Ltd, Gateway Distriparks Ltd, Jet Freight Logistics Ltd, LEAP India Ltd, Shadowfax Technologies Ltd, Shree Vasu Logistics Ltd, Snowman Logistics Ltd, Transport Corporation of India Ltd, VRL Logistics Ltd*
