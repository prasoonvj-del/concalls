# Allcargo Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Back-to-back quarters of profitable growth driven by disciplined pricing, cost control, and operational agility.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 555 crore (up 3.4% YoY from Rs 537 crore; up 1.6% QoQ from Rs 546 crore)
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,101 crore (up 7.1% YoY from Rs 1,028 crore)
- **Gross Profit (Q2 FY27):** Rs 174 crore (up 13% YoY, up 6.7% QoQ)
- **Gross Margin (Q2 FY27):** 31.4% (up 270 bps YoY from 28.7%; up 150 bps QoQ from 29.9%)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 77 crore (up 24.2% YoY from Rs 62 crore; up 8.5% QoQ from Rs 71 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 13.9% (up 240 bps YoY from 11.5%; up 90 bps QoQ from 13%)
- **PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 24 crore (up 166.7% YoY from Rs 9 crore; up 26.3% QoQ from Rs 19 crore)
- **Express Logistics Gross Margin (H1 FY27):** 27%
- **Consultative Logistics Gross Margin (H1 FY27):** 36.5% (Post-Ind AS 29.7%, Pre-Ind AS 8.8%)
- **Consultative Logistics Revenue Growth (Q2 FY27):** 5.8% YoY
- **Express Logistics Revenue Growth:** 7.7% YoY

### Highlights
- Management noted July and August saw market softness, followed by a visible inflection and demand improvement in September.
- Implemented a new booking application embedding AI to simplify workflows and reduce costs.
- Announced leadership transition: Mr. Vijay Nehra takes over as Managing Director and CEO effective January 1, 2027; Mr. Ketan Kulkarni moves to a new role in the Allcargo Group.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated the 5-year growth plan outlined in the previous analyst meet: 10% CAGR revenue growth over 3 years, scaling to 12% over 5 years.
- Gross margin CAGR guidance: 10% over 3 years, going up to 15% over a 5-year trajectory.
- PBT CAGR guidance: 20% over a 5-year term.
- Target to achieve 15% EBITDA margin by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India’s Q1 FY27 GDP growth stood at 7.8%, with similar expectations for the full year.
- High-frequency indicators: September E-Way Bill generation crossed 141 million run rate; GST collections maintained 14.7% YoY growth.
- Strong demand traction seen in e-commerce, auto-engineering, and chemical verticals, alongside warehousing and transportation solutions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Direct operating expenses and G&A overheads maintained under tight control.
- Express Logistics realization per ton indexed to about 107.5; net service levels scaled to 110.2 indexed in Q2 FY27.
- Price corrections implemented in Q1 and Q2 drove gross margin and EBITDA expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Express segment annual capex expected to be around Rs 15 crore to Rs 19-20 crore for hub-related and expansion needs.
- Consultative Logistics capex is linked to new business growth and warehouses, estimated around Rs 20 crore one year down the line, executed via operating lease models.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- General market softness and sluggish demand across several trade corridors during July and August monsoon months.
- Chasing unviable low-margin tonnage destroys long-term value.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pratiti Khara (Param Capital) asked about volume degrowth in Express and Consultative segments.** Management clarified they are deliberately upgrading the portfolio mix, prioritizing high-yield service quality over unviable low-margin tonnage, with Express revenue up 7.7% YoY.
2. **Saurav Rathod (Knightstone Capital) asked about segment EBITDA margins and pre-Ind AS numbers.** Management stated Consultative Logistics post-Ind AS EBITDA margin is 29.7% (Pre-Ind AS 8.8%) and Express is 6.5%.
3. **Ruthvi (Individual Investor) inquired about contract logistics customer segments and pricing competition.** Management highlighted e-commerce, auto engineering, and chemicals as key drivers, adding they periodically prune suboptimal contracts to protect gross margins.
4. **Achal Lohade (Nuvama Institutional Equities) asked about 3-5 year growth plans and capital outlay.** Management reiterated their 3-to-5-year CAGR targets (10-12% revenue growth, 15% EBITDA margin by FY28) and guided annual Express capex of Rs 15-20 crore.
5. **Jay (Individual Investor) asked about EBITDA margin sustainability and operating leverage.** Management noted operating leverage accounted for about 18% of the 30% H1 EBITDA growth (over 12% direct gross profit growth) and expects to sustain this towards the 15% EBITDA target by FY28.
6. **Gaurav Agrawal (Nine One Capital) asked if revenue growth guidance could be surpassed.** Management stated they maintain their investor presentation revenue guidance and are confident in achieving it backed by a strong festive Q3.

### One-line takeaway
Allcargo delivered its second consecutive quarter of profitable growth in Q2 FY27, backed by strict service-led pricing discipline, cost controls, and operating leverage, keeping the company on track for its multi-year margin targets.
