# Radiant Cash Management Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-24 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth was supported by a new IDBI Bank mandate, but standalone EBITDA margins contracted due to rising manpower costs, and the company is awaiting price revisions to restore profitability.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1.08 billion (+5.8% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue Growth:** 7.05% YoY.
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 13.5% (vs 15.9% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 11.01% (vs 10.7% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 52 million (vs Rs 57 million in Q1 FY26).
*   **Cash Handled:** Rs 0.43 trillion (+2.2% YoY).
*   **Gross Cash Losses:** Rs 2.06 million (0.0005% of total cash handled).
*   **Radiant Valuable Logistics (RVL) Revenue:** Rs 22.1 million (+24% QoQ).
*   **Ace Money Transaction Volume:** Rs 170 crore.
*   **Cash Balance:** Rs 2.1 billion (Free cash: Rs 644 million).
*   **Debtors:** 65 days (down from 70 days in March 2026).

### Highlights
*   **Core Business:** Growth driven by a new large mandate with IDBI Bank. Direct client revenue contribution increased to 18.4% (vs 14.3% YoY).
*   **Margins:** Standalone EBITDA margin decline attributed to higher manpower costs (minimum wage hikes and shortage of licensed gunmen in southern states).
*   **Subsidiaries:**
    *   **Radiant Valuable Logistics:** Revenue grew 24% QoQ; management expects to reach breakeven in the current quarter.
    *   **Ace Money:** Focus shifted to transaction revenues from POS/Soundbox/QR code deployments after PIDF subsidy discontinuation. Management expects EBITDA breakeven in Q3 FY27.
*   **Operational:** E-commerce logistics segment returned to positive growth.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management targets double-digit revenue growth for the full year.
*   **Margins:** Management expects standalone EBITDA margins to reach 17%-18% for the full year, factoring in anticipated price revisions.
*   **FY28 Outlook:** Management targets mid-teens revenue growth (12%-13%) and consolidated EBITDA margins of 19%-20%.
*   **Price Revisions:** Negotiations with banks/Indian Bank Association are ongoing; management expects resolution in Q2 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Pressure:** Banks have reduced the allocation of fresh points, leading to sluggish growth in some segments.
*   **Competition:** Management noted the industry is skewed with only 3-4 pan-India players; they expect competition to abate as the industry collectively pushes for price revisions.
*   **Digital Impact:** While digital transactions have grown, cash in circulation remains high (Rs 42 lakh crore). Management views their service as a cost-effective alternative to credit card transactions for retailers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Manpower Costs:** Employee costs, driver, and gunman costs increased significantly due to inflationary pressures and regulatory minimum wage hikes.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Ongoing efforts to use technology for cost reduction; management expects impact in coming quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Ace Money:** Target to deploy over 50,000 Soundboxes in FY27.
*   **Payment Aggregator License:** Application resubmission planned for late August/early September 2026; license expected by early 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Position:** Gross cash of Rs 3.18 billion (includes Rs 1.96 billion used in operations/bank guarantees).
*   **Working Capital:** Debtors reduced to 65 days of revenue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Margin Compression:** High dependence on price revisions to offset rising manpower costs.
*   **Regulatory/Macro:** Potential impact of geopolitical volatility on gold movement (affecting RVL).
*   **Execution:** Difficulty in scaling direct client business at a pace sufficient to offset declining bank-led volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Price Hikes:** Management clarified that contracts are long-term with no automatic escalation. They are negotiating with the Indian Bank Association and expect a "substantial" increase effective Q2.
*   **On Ace Money Strategy:** Management explained the subsidiary aims to leverage the company's 15,000-pin-code network to provide "phygital" services and compete with payment banks.
*   **On Buyback:** Management acknowledged investor feedback regarding a potential buyback and committed to discussing it at the next board meeting.
*   **On Polymer Notes:** Management stated the core business is indifferent to currency type (paper vs. polymer) but noted a potential one-time "bump" in cash-in-transit opportunities during any transition.

### One-line takeaway
Radiant is navigating margin pressure through price hike negotiations with banks while attempting to pivot toward direct clients and fintech-led growth via its Ace Money subsidiary.
