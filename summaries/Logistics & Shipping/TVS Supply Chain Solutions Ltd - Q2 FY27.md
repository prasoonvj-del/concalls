# TVS Supply Chain Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly revenue and doubled PBT, driven by broad-based segment growth and structural enhancements.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained >20% revenue growth target for FY27 across both segments; management remains firmly on track to double operational profitability in FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more confidently on GFS structural turnaround and sustainability of volume-led growth, while order pipeline expanded to Rs. 8,200 crores (from Rs. 7,500 crores).
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Signed strategic MOU with Sankyu Inc. (Japan); no specific macro recession warning mentioned this quarter compared to last.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: ISCS margins rebounded as promised to 9.2% (from 8.1% in Q1); ALA JV share allotment approved by board on Oct 6.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 3,687 crore, up 38.5% YoY and 10.6% QoQ.
- **ISCS Revenue:** Rs. 2,488 crore, up 24.8% YoY and 2.9% QoQ.
- **GFS Revenue:** Rs. 1,199 crore, up 79.1% YoY and 30.6% QoQ.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 268 crore (approx, 7.3% margin), up 50.2% YoY; margin up 60 bps YoY and 30 bps QoQ.
- **ISCS EBITDA:** Rs. 228 crore at 9.2% margin.
- **GFS EBITDA:** Rs. 47 crore at 3.9% margin.
- **PBT:** Rs. 50.6 crore, up from Rs. 24.4 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **PAT:** Rs. 36.5 crore, up 123.9% YoY from Rs. 16.3 crore.
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 7,022 crore, up 34% YoY.
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs. 500 crore, up 42.2% YoY.
- **H1 PBT:** Rs. 82.7 crore, up 91.4% YoY.
- **New Business Revenue:** >Rs. 523 crore (third consecutive quarter crossing Rs. 500 crore).
- **Business Development Pipeline:** Rs. 8,200 crore.

### Highlights
- Highest ever quarterly revenue and PBT crossing Rs. 50 crore.
- India geography delivered standout 64.1% YoY revenue growth (ISCS up 36%).
- Rest of the world grew 29% YoY (ISCS 21%, GFS 55%).
- Signed strategic MOU with Sankyu Inc. (Japan), which intends to acquire a 0.5% equity stake subject to regulatory approvals.
- ALA MENA joint venture achieved EN 9120:2018 warehouse certification; board approved share allotment on October 6 (51% TVS SCS, 49% ALA MENA).
- Swamy & Sons 3PL acquisition contributed in a margin-accretive manner, fully funded from internal accruals.
- India Ratings reaffirmed issuer rating at IND AA and revised outlook from stable to positive in July.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets >20% revenue growth across both segments for FY27.
- Management reaffirmed the goal to double operational PBT in FY27 (targeting ~Rs. 200 crore base operational PBT compared to Rs. 97 crore operational PBT in FY26).
- Aspiration to reach 4% PBT margin by Q4 FY28.
- GFS EBITDA margin aspiration of 4.5% to 5% by FY28 (potentially last quarter of FY27).
- ISCS margin target of 10.5% to 11% needed to achieve 4% PBT margin.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was broad-based across segments and geographies, driven by new business wins and deeper account expansions.
- GFS growth was driven 2/3rd by volumes rather than rates, supported by structural lane optimization and supplier consolidation.
- India growth heavily led by renewables and automobiles (India GFS up 136%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Material-related costs: Rs. 630 crore (down sequentially from Rs. 666 crore due to business mix).
- Freight, clearing, forwarding, and handling: Rs. 1,287 crore (up due to GFS volume growth).
- Employee costs: Rs. 724 crore (reflecting annual cost inflation and business growth).
- Subcontracting costs: Rs. 467 crore; Other expenses: Rs. 312 crore.
- Depreciation: Rs. 166 crore; Lease interest: Rs. 27 crore (impacted by new North America project started in H2 last year).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Swamy & Sons acquisition fully funded from internal accruals without additional debt.
- No specific new capex number guided; focus is on executing current pipelines and integrations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt increased by Rs. 140 crore, primarily to fund additional working capital for significant GFS volume growth.
- Generated Rs. 234 crore of cash in H1, reinvested into the business.
- Maintained strong cadence on DSO and DPO tracking; 100 customers are from Fortune 500.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange volatility impacts PBT/forex lines (hedged actively, but influenced by geopolitical/currency movements).
- Global ocean and air freight rate fluctuations (though mitigated by volume-driven growth and contract structures).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sukrit D. Patil (Eyesight Fintrade) asked about GFS rate drop risks and working capital/debt management:** Management stated 2/3rd of GFS growth is volume-driven, insulating them from rate drops; working capital is funded by strong cash generation (Rs. 234 crore generated in H1) and Fortune 500 customer base lowers credit risk.
2. **Kunal Sabnis (Nine Rivers Capital) asked about ISCS sustainability and GFS structural changes:** Management noted ISCS growth is broad-based globally and sustainable (>20% targeted for FY27, mid/high-teens for FY28); GFS improvements stem from regional empowerment, lane optimization, and supplier consolidation.
3. **Saumil Shah (Paras Investments) asked about JV contributions and FY27 PBT targets:** Management expects ALA JV revenues to commence in Q4 FY27 and Sankyu partnership impacts in FY28; reiterated target to double operational PBT to ~Rs. 200 crore in FY27.
4. **Rudraksh Raheja (i thought Financial Consulting) asked about GFS volume metrics and entity profitability:** Management agreed to consider reporting GFS volumes semi-annually and confirmed all major regional entities (UK, US, Singapore) are PBT profitable.

### One-line takeaway
TVS Supply Chain Solutions posted its highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs. 3,687 crore and doubled PBT, powered by robust 38.5% YoY growth and structural operating efficiencies across ISCS and GFS segments.
