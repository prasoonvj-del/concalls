# Logistics & Shipping - Logistics & 3PL: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is characterized by broad-based optimism, with all nine companies reporting positive growth trajectories. Managements are successfully balancing top-line expansion with margin preservation, largely through operational efficiencies, automation, and the ability to pass on inflationary costs. While the macro environment remains volatile, companies are focusing on internal levers—such as network optimization, technology adoption, and strategic acquisitions—to drive profitability.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is universal agreement on the strength of the festive season, with companies like **Delhivery**, **Mahindra Logistics**, and **VRL Logistics** citing it as a key driver for Q3/Q4 volume growth. E-commerce and Quick Commerce are identified as universal growth engines.
*   **Divergence:** A clear split exists regarding the macro environment. **Delhivery** and **Mahindra Logistics** explicitly flagged macroeconomic volatility and consumption drops in specific verticals as risks. Conversely, **Black Buck** shifted its tone from "cautious" in Q1 to "business as usual" in Q2, and **VRL Logistics** reported strong volume growth across all industries, seemingly insulated from the broader macro concerns cited by peers.
*   **Sector-Specifics:** **LEAP India** noted high demand in automotive but a lull in textiles due to raw material costs. **TCI** observed a "wait and see" approach from auto consumers, despite stable dispatches.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Pricing Power:** A dominant theme is the ability to pass on fuel price hikes. **TCI** noted that ~2/3 of its revenue growth was pricing-led (diesel/bunker hikes), while **VRL Logistics** and **Mahindra Logistics** confirmed that fuel cost increases are being passed through to customers.
*   **Labor Pressures:** **Delhivery** and **Mahindra Logistics** highlighted rising manpower costs (minimum wages, PF slab changes) as a persistent headwind that is not always fully passed through, necessitating productivity gains.
*   **Margin Strategy:** Companies are aggressively using automation to protect margins. **Delhivery** is deploying robotics to offset workforce challenges, and **LEAP India** is leveraging network synergies from the CHEP integration to support a 50.8% EBITDA margin. **VRL Logistics** achieved a 367 bps YoY EBITDA margin expansion (to 23.3%) through strict cost control and pricing discipline.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Asset-Heavy vs. Asset-Lite:** A distinct strategic divide remains. **TCI** and **VRL Logistics** continue to invest heavily in owned assets (vessels, fleet, land/warehousing). **Jet Freight** and **Flomic** maintain strictly asset-lite models, outsourcing warehouses and surface transport.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Inorganic growth is a key theme. **LEAP India** acquired Vimprotech and TIPS to backward integrate into manufacturing. **Jet Freight** is pursuing a Dubai-based acquisition (NatWest) to capture charter brokerage margins.
*   **Capex Guidance:** **TCI** maintained its Rs 500-550 crore capex guidance for FY27, while **Mahindra Logistics** is keeping its capex modest at Rs 100-110 crore, focusing on contract logistics and technology.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance:** **Delhivery** maintained its 20-30% revenue growth guidance despite macro volatility. **Black Buck** maintained its vehicle finance EBITDA break-even target for FY27.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Black Buck** moved from "cautious" to "business as usual."
*   **Operational Milestones:** **Mahindra Logistics** achieved its first-ever EBITDA-positive quarter for its express business (Rivigo), a significant turnaround from previous losses. **Flomic** successfully completed its Bhiwandi warehouse vertical expansion.

## Company one-liners
*   **Black Buck:** Delivered 50%+ revenue growth while balancing aggressive investments in Super Loads and telematics.
*   **Delhivery:** Leveraging automation to drive record profitability while maintaining growth guidance despite macro volatility.
*   **Flomic:** Capitalized on elevated global freight rates and efficient warehousing expansion to drive a 90% jump in EBITDA.
*   **Jet Freight:** Pursuing an asset-lite expansion into South American trade lanes and retail courier services to hit Rs 580-600 crore revenue.
*   **LEAP India:** Reported 22.5% revenue growth and 62% PAT growth, bolstered by strategic acquisitions in plastic manufacturing.
*   **Mahindra Logistics:** Achieved a sharp profitability rebound led by contract logistics and the first-ever EBITDA-positive quarter for Rivigo.
*   **Shree Vasu:** Reported a strong H1 with 37.2% revenue growth and 171% PAT surge driven by new client wins.
*   **TCI:** Navigating a transition phase with significant capex and fleet expansion while maintaining a 10-12% growth outlook.
*   **VRL Logistics:** Delivered 20% revenue growth and 367 bps margin expansion, underpinned by its distinct owned-asset model.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Fuel Volatility:** **TCI** and **VRL Logistics** highlighted the unpredictability of bunker/fuel prices as a primary risk to margins.
*   **Rivigo's Path:** **Mahindra Logistics** management noted the next milestone for the express business is PAT positivity.
*   **MDR Impact:** **Black Buck** will be monitored on its ability to mitigate UPI MDR impacts on tolling via its "pre-debit notification" strategy.
*   **GCC Expansion:** **LEAP India's** progress in the Saudi/UAE markets will be a key indicator of its international scaling capability.
