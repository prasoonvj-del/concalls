# Shadowfax Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Hit Rs 1,500 crores in top line for the first time and reported a 5% adjusted EBITDA margin alongside accelerated growth.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,538 crore, up 57% YoY (first half revenue: Rs 2,896 crore, up 60% YoY).
- **Orders Delivered:** 27.1 crore orders, up 70% YoY.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 77 crore at a 5% margin.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 67 crore.
- **Express Parcel Revenue:** Rs 1,096 crore, up 55% YoY and 10% QoQ (volumes up 72% YoY to 21.7 crore shipments).
- **Hyperlocal Revenue:** Rs 341 crore, up 88% YoY and 26% QoQ (realization per order rose 13% sequentially to over Rs 60).
- **Other Logistics Services Revenue:** Rs 100 crore, up 13% QoQ.
- **Operating Space:** Expanded from 53 lakh sq. ft. to 69 lakh sq. ft. in Q2 (middle-mile footprint expanded from 24 lakh to over 34 lakh sq. ft.).
- **Delivery Partner Network:** Ramped up 31% from 2.9 lakh partners in Q1 to 3.8 lakh partners in Q2.
- **Shadowfax 360 (Self-serve platform):** Over 6,700 sellers within 200 days of launch.
- **Prime Large Run Rate:** Crossed Rs 100 crore annualized run rate (up 33% sequentially).

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 1,500 crore top line for the first time; achieved 6th consecutive quarter of growth above 55%.
- Main competitive advantage highlighted as speed (same-day/next-day delivery across over 120 cities) rather than price.
- Launched a capability enabling small sellers at their own locations to offer same-day/next-day delivery on marketplaces without prepositioning inventory.
- D2C business more than doubled YoY; D2C market share rose 2 percentage points QoQ to above 14%.
- Deployed an in-house AI optimization engine across the network ahead of the festive season to plan and optimize freight movement daily.
- Announced entry into micro-fulfillment centers (MFCs) to hold brand inventory for 8-12 hour citywide delivery across 50 cities over the next few years.
- Amazon became a top 5 customer within a year of launch, contributing to both Express Parcel and Quick Commerce.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains long-term cumulative growth guidance of 27% to 30%.
- Capex guidance maintained at 3.8% to 4% of revenue for the year.
- Express Parcel segment expected to grow between 27% and 28% CAGR.
- Quick Commerce industry expected to compound at 30%+; management expects to maintain market share.
- Exposure to the main customer is down at least 3 to 4 percentage points from the long-term average as a percentage of overall revenue; management aspires to bring it significantly lower over time.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 did not include the festive sales season (which shifted to October/Q3), yet the company grew 13% QoQ.
- Quick Commerce is expanding into Tier 2 cities; horizontal players operate across more than 4,000 unique dark stores (70 opened by Shadowfax across 9 cities towards a 100-dark-store target).
- Food delivery growth accelerated due to the 0% commission model, yielding higher realizations due to longer delivery distances.
- Emerging AI agent commerce is expected to drive further parcel demand based on speed and price.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Rental costs increased from 3.2% to 3.5% of revenue during the quarter due to infrastructure scaling.
- Depreciation increased slightly due to higher capex.
- Partner expenses increased sequentially due to a higher revenue mix contribution from Hyperlocal (moving from 20% to 22%).
- Low-hanging fruit on lost shipments has been captured; accelerated reductions seen in past quarters will moderate.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operating space expanded to 69 lakh sq. ft., including pop-up facilities constituting roughly 5% of the footprint for the festive peak.
- Two mega sorters and volume profilers under completion are set to go live in Q4.
- Opened 70 out of the planned 100 Dark Stores across 9 cities by the end of Q2.
- Commenced building out the Micro-Fulfillment Centers (MFCs) business (40,000–50,000 sq. ft. facilities) over the next 6 to 12 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Capex cycle remains on track at 3.8% to 4% of revenue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management remains watchful of the ongoing festive season and will update guidance if necessary after reviewing Q3 performance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Krupashankar NJ (Avendus) on guidance revision:** Management stated they prefer to wait for Q3 performance before revising guidance further, while maintaining the 27%-30% long-term CAGR.
2. **Kartik Kohli (Kotak Institutional Equities) on cost buildup and top client exposure:** Management noted rental costs increased by ~30 bps to 3.5% and confirmed top-client exposure dropped by 3 to 4 percentage points from the long-term average.
3. **Swapnil (JM Financial) on MFC capex and Express RPO:** Management clarified that MFC capex is included in the stated 3.8%-4% revenue guidance and advised not to read too much into short-term Express RPO fluctuations as they are mix-driven.
4. **Dhruv Jain (Ambit Capital) on Amazon ramp-up:** Management noted Amazon is a top 5 customer within a year, with full e-commerce integration benefits expected in the next financial year.
5. **Abhisek Banerjee (ICICI Securities) on D2C market share and lost shipments:** Management stated D2C share gains can continue or accelerate, while noting that lost shipment savings have normalized after initial low-hanging fruits were captured.

### One-line takeaway
Shadowfax delivered a milestone Q2 FY27 with Rs 1,500+ crore revenue and a 5% adjusted EBITDA margin, driven by rapid D2C/SME expansion, Prime speed capabilities, and scaling infrastructure ahead of the festive season.
