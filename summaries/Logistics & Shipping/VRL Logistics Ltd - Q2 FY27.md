# VRL Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong volume growth, higher realizations, significant margin expansion, robust cash generation, and balance sheet strengthening.

### Key numbers
- Total income: Rs 967 crore, up ~20% YoY (from Rs 804 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Goods transport volumes: 1,079,000 tonnes, up ~11% YoY (from 976,000 tonnes)
- Freight realization per tonne: Rs 8,840, up 9% YoY (from Rs 8,079)
- EBITDA: Rs 226 crore, up 43% YoY (from Rs 158 crore)
- EBITDA margin: 23.3%, up 367 bps YoY (from 19.7%)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 103 crore, up ~106% YoY (from Rs 50 crore)
- PAT margin: 10.7%, up from 6.2%
- H1 FY27 Capex: Rs 158 crore (Rs 76 crore in Q1, Rs 82 crore in Q2)
- Net debt: Rs 337 crore as of September 30, 2026 (down from Rs 440 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- Net debt-to-EBITDA: 0.3x (down from 0.4x at the end of FY26)
- Total fleet: 5,941 vehicles as of H1 FY27 (76% debt-free, 11% fully depreciated)
- Branches added: 51 in Q2; 67 total in H1

### Highlights
- Delivered strong financial and operating performance, supported by earlier freight rate hikes, rationalization initiatives, customer engagement, and operating leverage.
- Employee costs declined as a percentage of income from 18% to 16.48% (despite rising absolute terms to Rs 159 crore). Administrative expenses declined from 2.28% to 1.43%. Other operating expenses declined from 10.48% to 10.06%.
- Strong H1 operating cash flow before working capital changes at around Rs 415 crore (vs Rs 314 crore in H1 FY26); net cash from operating activities rose to Rs 363 crore (vs Rs 334 crore).
- Trade receivable cycle remains healthy at approximately 10-11 days due to paid and to-pay collection models.
- Board recommended a share buyback of up to Rs 280 crore at Rs 320 per share (5% of paid-up equity); tendering closed Nov 2, 2026, with settlement on Nov 9, 2026 (promoters did not participate).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects sustainable EBITDA margins of around 21%–22% going forward.
- Tonnage growth target for the second half and medium term is expected at around 9%–10%.
- Revenue growth is expected to normalize at around 10%–12% going forward, assuming no further price realization changes.
- Capital expenditure for H2 FY27 is projected at around Rs 150 crore to Rs 160 crore; annual capex of Rs 250 crore to Rs 300 crore expected to continue.
- ROCE for the year is expected to be at least 30% plus.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was observed across all industries, with particular strength in FMCG and textiles during the festival season.
- October tonnage grew by over 20% YoY, driven by peak festival demand and competitors facing vehicle shortages due to heavy reliance on hired fleets.
- Strategic branch expansion into Northern and Eastern states (e.g., UP, Bihar, West Bengal) is yielding strong zone-to-zone growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel cost per litre increased from ~Rs 85 to ~Rs 97, putting pressure on operating costs and reducing bulk fuel procurement efficiency.
- Management stated that every Rs 2 change in fuel rates typically leads to a 1% change in freight rates; a substantial fuel price reduction (>5%) would trigger a proportional pass-through to customers while preserving target margins.
- Maintained strict control over toll and hamali expenses relative to income despite toll rate hikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex of Rs 158 crore directed toward commercial vehicle additions and land/building facilities in locations including Vijayawada, Nagpur, Lucknow, and Raipur.
- Fleet procurement going forward will focus on higher-capacity vehicles to align with tonnage growth while prioritizing owned fleet expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced by Rs 103 crore during H1 to Rs 337 crore.
- 76% of the fleet is debt-free and 11% is fully depreciated.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments and crude oil price volatility creating fuel cost pressures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jainam Shah (Equirus Securities):** Asked about pricing vs. volume strategy and sustainability. Management replied that 9%-10% volume growth is sustainable with no pushback on current rates, guiding to a 21%-22% sustainable EBITDA margin.
- **Achal Lohade:** Inquired about fuel price vulnerability and contractual customer contribution. Management stated that contractual customers make up 17%-18% of revenue and feature fuel escalation clauses, while non-contractual rates are managed flexibly.
- **Madhur Rathi:** Asked about land/building acquisition IRRs and ROCE impact. Management responded that larger property acquisitions target a 10-12 year payback, and ROCE is expected above 30% post-buyback.
- **Nitin Jain:** Questioned the impact of potential fuel price cuts. Management noted that a Rs 5 fuel reduction would lead to roughly a 2.5% reduction in realization, with margins maintained via route-level selectivity and operational efficiencies.
- **Alok Deora:** Asked how VRL sustains industry-leading margins. Management attributed it to superior service quality, high-density own-infrastructure network, very low claim ratios, and strong asset utilization compared to peers dependent on hired vehicles.

### One-line takeaway
VRL Logistics delivered a robust H2/H1 FY27 marked by 20% revenue growth, a 367 bps YoY expansion in EBITDA margin to 23.3%, and strong cash generation, underpinned by its distinct owned-asset model and pricing discipline.
