# Afcom Holdings Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-17 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q4 FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Phenomenal revenue and PAT growth driven by strong operational performance and strategic cargo demand.

### Key numbers
- **Q4 FY26 Revenue:** INR 191.88 crores (up 87.80% YoY)
- **Q4 FY26 EBITDA:** INR 74.08 lakhs (up 51.52% YoY)
- **Q4 FY26 PAT:** INR 44.66 crores (up 72.85% YoY)
- **FY26 Total Revenue:** INR 587.72 crores (up 143.86% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA:** INR 238.14 crores (up 211.72% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA Margin:** 40.52% (up 40.52% YoY)
- **FY26 PAT:** INR 121.90 crores (up 230.05% YoY)
- **FY26 PAT Margin:** 20.74% (up 542 bps YoY)
- **FY26 EPS:** INR 48.65 (vs INR 16.47 YoY)
- **Total Equity:** INR 457 crores
- **Total Assets:** INR 935 crores
- **ROE:** 26.69%
- **ROCE:** 35.62%

### Highlights
- Awarded fastest-growing airline in the freighter market by Aviation Cargo Express.
- Awarded top airline by Air to Air Import 2026 and freighter of the year at Velana Awards (Maldives).
- Forayed into Australian Pacific region via strategic relationship with Nauru Air Corporation.
- Achieved key milestones including QIB capital infusion, fleet expansion, designated carrier certification from the Indian government, and credit rating.
- Transitioned financial reporting to Ind AS standards.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated that with fleet capacity expanding beyond double, top-line growth for FY27 will be "much more than double" on a conservative estimate.
- Fourth and fifth narrow-body aircraft will be operational before next quarter.
- Four Boeing 777 wide-body aircraft additions planned; full fleet of 9 aircraft expected by the second half of the next calendar year. Financial closure for all four wide-bodies is complete.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q4 demand spiked due to West Asian conflicts and Eid in the Middle East, causing a shortage of scheduled belly capacity and stranded cargo, leading to a surge in charters.
- Chennai international cargo volume grew 12.5% YoY (2.3x the national average).
- Management notes that air cargo demand remains robust in the region and high prices do not fully retrace post-crisis.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- ATF price increased steeply from INR 85,000 per KL to INR 2,24,000 per KL; effective price for AFCOM was INR 1,69,000 per KL due to designated carrier VAT benefits.
- ATF cost is a direct pass-on via fuel surcharges (100% passed on to customers).
- Designated carrier status saves 5% to 7% on overall fuel costs.
- Cost structure for FY26: Direct expenses 54.4%, Depreciation 12.3%, Finance cost 7.3%, Employee 2.4%, Others 5.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total lease liability stands at INR 338 crores for residual periods; current lease due is INR 49 crores.
- Operations commencing at Noida airport cargo terminal (first cargo aircraft to land there on 17th).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow turned positive at close to INR 36 crores.
- Trade receivables are well within control at around 60-80 days; no outstanding receivables older than 6 months.
- Outstanding tax liability of INR 33 crores from FY26 current tax of INR 36 crores is being addressed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and West Asian conflicts causing disruptions in scheduled airline operations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Priyanshu asked about fleet induction timelines.** Management answered that the third aircraft is operational, and the fourth and fifth will be operational before next quarter. Wide-body Boeing 777s will be inducted by the end of the calendar year, with at least one operational in the last quarter of FY27.
2. **Chintan asked about trade receivables and ATF VAT benefits.** Management clarified that receivables are within 60 days with no items over 6 months, and the designated carrier VAT benefit gives a 5% to 7% saving on fuel costs.
3. **Rohit asked about ATF price hikes and margin impact.** Management stated that 100% of increased fuel cost is passed on via fuel surcharges, and margins may increase due to hardened freight rates.
4. **Pinkesh asked why Q4 yield was lower despite rate hikes.** Management explained that a higher proportion of short-sector charters (e.g., Chennai-Colombo-Male) were flown to clear stranded cargo during the West Asian crisis, impacting average weight yields.
5. **Praseen asked about revenue potential of wide-body 777s.** Management estimated each 777 will generate an average revenue of 3x the current 737-800 level, utilizing a conservative 75% cargo load factor.

### One-line takeaway
AFCOM delivered blockbuster FY26 performance (revenue up 144% YoY, PAT up 230% YoY) and is well-funded to aggressively expand its fleet with wide-body aircraft to capture surging regional air cargo demand.
