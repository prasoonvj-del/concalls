# TVS Supply Chain Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust Q1 performance with record quarterly revenue, significant growth across segments, and strong new business wins.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 3,335.2 crores, up 28.7% YoY and 10% QoQ.
- **ISCS Revenue:** Rs. 2,417 crores, up 21.9% YoY and 5.9% QoQ.
- **GFS Revenue:** Rs. 918 crores, up 50.6% YoY and 22.6% QoQ.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs. 232.2 crores, up 34% YoY; margin expanded by 30 bps YoY to 7%.
- **ISCS Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs. 196.3 crores at 8.1% margin (vs 8.3% in Q1 FY26).
- **GFS Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs. 38 crores at 4.1% margin (vs 2.1% in Q1 FY26).
- **Adjusted PBT:** Rs. 32.1 crores (vs Rs. 18.8 crores in Q1 FY26).
- **Operational PAT (excluding prior-year InvIT/exceptional items):** Rs. 8.8 crores, up 156% YoY. Total reported PAT was Rs. 22.5 crores.
- **New Business Wins:** Rs. 543 crores (representing 21% of Q1 revenue).
- **Order Pipeline:** Rs. 7,500 crores plus.

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs. 3,300 crores in quarterly revenue for the first time.
- India geography registered 44% YoY growth.
- Completed the acquisition of Swamy & Sons 3PL (40 days of performance included in Q1).
- Announced a joint venture with ALA Group for defense and aerospace supply chain solutions (targeting Rs. 2,000 crores by year 5 of operations; H2 revenue commencement expected).
- Implemented Oracle ERP for the India ISCS business.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aspires to achieve mid-teen revenue growth for FY27.
- Aspiration to reach 4% PBT margin (management noted it is an aspiration for FY27, definitely achievable in FY28).
- GFS EBITDA margin expected to trend around 4% to 5%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ISCS growth aided by new business wins and healthy performance in India and Europe; North America aided by large projects.
- GFS growth fueled by significant volume growth in ocean freight (especially in India) and rising freight rates.
- Strong new business wins across marquee clients in automobile, consumer products, industrial, renewable energy, and clean energy.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Material-related costs increased to Rs. 666 crores due to new businesses in India and higher volumes in North America and Europe.
- Freight clearing, forwarding, and handling expenses rose to Rs. 991 crores in line with GFS volume growth.
- Employee costs rose to Rs. 676 crores due to cost inflation and revenue growth, partially offset by cost-takeout savings.
- Subcontracting costs increased to Rs. 444 crores; other expenses rose to Rs. 329 crores due to higher short-term lease rentals.
- ISCS margins were marginally lower (8.1%) due to upfront implementation and transition costs for newly onboarded contracts; management expects margins to return above 9% by Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Warehouse capacity utilization is in the vicinity of 85%.
- Exploring expansion into the Middle East and Africa markets, preferentially via partnerships.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential macro risks include geopolitical conflicts, war situations, and a subsequent global recession causing supply chain disruptions.
- Manpower availability remains an ongoing operational risk across geographies.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sucrit Patil (Paras Investments) asked about top execution priorities and biggest risks:** Management (Vikas Chadha) stated the top 3 priorities are growth, technology integration (AI, robotics, Oracle ERP), and partnerships (Swamy & Sons, ALA Group). The biggest risk flagged is potential recession from geopolitical tensions.
2. **Saumil Shah (Paras Investments) asked about the dip in ISCS EBITDA margins despite revenue growth:** Management (Vaidhyanathan/Chadha) explained it was due to upfront implementation/transition costs on new contracts. They clarified this is a one-to-two-quarter gestation impact and ISCS margins will return to 9% by Q2.
3. **Saumil Shah asked about the ALA Group JV revenue potential and margins:** Management (Vikas Chadha) stated they aspire to reach Rs. 2,000 crores by year 5, with H2 FY27 seeing initial revenues, and noted it will be margin-accretive due to high entry barriers in aerospace/defense.
4. **Kunal Sabnis (Nine Rivers Capital) questioned whether the ISCS margin drop to 8.1% was too steep for start-up costs:** Management explained that Q4 typically benefits from customer price corrections that are absent in Q1, and reiterated confidence in returning to 9% in Q2 and 9.5%-10% by Q4.
5. **Bharat Sheth (Quest Investment Managers) asked about container cost volatility and pass-through:** Management (Vikas Chadha) stated that freight costs are passed on to customers through contract structures, and local revenues/costs in overseas markets insulate overall profitability from FX fluctuations.

### One-line takeaway
TVS Supply Chain Solutions delivered a record Q1 FY27 with 28.7% YoY revenue growth and a 34% jump in adjusted EBITDA, driven by strong new business wins despite minor margin headwinds from contract transition costs.
