# Delhivery Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted record profitability and successful automation integration despite macro volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 3,066 crore.
*   **Freight Volume:** 545K metric tonnes.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 4%.
*   **Cash Balance:** Rs 4,676 crore.
*   **Local (On-demand) ARR:** Rs 240-250 crore.
*   **International Business Growth:** 55% QoQ.

### Highlights
*   Achieved record profitability and crossed the Rs 3,000 crore revenue milestone.
*   Automation investments (tractor trailers and sorters) are yielding productivity benefits and improved payback periods.
*   "Local" (on-demand commercial vehicle service) is performing ahead of original expectations.
*   Software improvements, including auto-bidding and map optimization, have reduced operational costs.
*   Unbilled receivables have shown sharp improvement.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintained the FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20-30%.
*   **Margins:** Management indicated a 16-18% margin band is reasonable for the future.
*   **Local Service:** Targeting an exit ARR of Rs 270-280 crore.
*   **Q3 Outlook:** Management expects Q3 to be seasonally stronger due to the festive season.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Macro environment remains challenging and volatile.
*   Consumption in certain verticals and subsegments has declined.
*   The company is prioritizing profitability over volume, avoiding "unfettered" volume growth that could lead to claims and losses.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   96-97% of contracts have automatic adjustments to pass on inflationary headwinds.
*   Productivity measures and load unitization are underway to support margins.
*   Management noted that workforce availability and productivity in India are becoming increasingly challenging, necessitating automation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Continuing to invest in footprint and client base expansion.
*   Deploying robotic and autonomous guided vehicles for in-facility movements.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   The company maintains a strong cash position of Rs 4,676 crore.
*   Collection cycles and billing accuracy have improved due to automation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Macroeconomic volatility and potential for further consumption drops.
*   Regulatory changes impacting workforce compliance and wages.
*   Seasonal fluctuations in workforce availability.
*   Potential for "nasty" claims and loss provisions if volume growth is not carefully managed.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Vijit Jain (Capital) asked about the 20-30% revenue guidance:** Management confirmed the guidance remains intact despite the challenging macro environment.
*   **Gaurav Rateria asked about margin sustainability:** Management emphasized that they are prioritizing profitability over volume and are not sacrificing margins for growth.
*   **Abhishek Banerjee asked about the 16-18% margin band:** Management stated that 17% is a reasonable expectation for the future.
*   **Sachin Salgaonkar asked about Q3 outlook:** Management noted that Q3 is seasonally strong due to heavy goods transport during the festive season.
*   **Jinesh Joshi asked about Local business losses:** Management noted that the Local business is improving and they are hopeful for breakeven as they expand across more cities.

### One-line takeaway
Delhivery is successfully leveraging automation to drive profitability and efficiency, maintaining its growth guidance despite a volatile macro environment.
