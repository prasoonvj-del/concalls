# Delhivery Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly profitability, crossed the Rs 3,000 crore revenue milestone, and demonstrated robust operational execution across core express parcel and PTL segments despite macroeconomic headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 3,066 crore in Q2 FY27 (crossed Rs 3,000 crore for the quarter).
- **Express Parcel volume:** 232 million shipments delivered (up 13.2% YoY).
- **PTL Freight volume:** 545K metric tonnes delivered (up 14.2% YoY).
- **Adjusted EBITDA margin:** 4%.
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 4,676 crore on the balance sheet.
- **Delhivery Local (on-demand LCV service):** Exit ARR crossed Rs 240-250 crore (targeting Rs 270-280 crore).
- **Delhivery Saver (International):** ARR grew 55% QoQ from Q1 FY27 to Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
- Achieved record quarterly profitability alongside revenue crossing Rs 3,066 crore.
- Express Parcel shipment volume expanded to 232 million packages in Q2 FY27.
- Delhivery Local expanded to 8 cities, expecting Contribution Margin (CM) breakeven by mid to end Q4 FY27.
- International express service (Delhivery Saver) delivered 55% QoQ ARR growth.
- AI and software optimization in maps saved millions of dollars in Google API costs while driving sharp improvements in billing accuracy and reducing unbilled receivables.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Express Parcel Volume:** Management revised FY27 Express Parcel volume growth guidance to 16-20% YoY (down from the initial 20-30% guidance due to Q1 macro slowdown).
- **EBITDA Margins:** Management maintains confidence in its long-term target band of 16-18% consolidated EBITDA margin, expecting 130-150 bps annual margin expansion.
- **PTL Freight:** Management expects medium-term tonnage growth to remain in the 15-20% range.
- **Overheads:** Corporate overhead expenses are expected to fall below 7% of revenue starting Q4 FY27 onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumption outside top e-commerce marketplaces (D2C, SME brands) remains strong, growing organically at 40-45%.
- Formalization of logistics is driving long-term structural tailwinds, shifting demand from unorganized regional operators to integrated players.
- TAM for PTL unorganized market is conservatively estimated at $9-10 billion (Rs 90,000-100,000 crore).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sequential yield improvement was driven by automatic indexation and contractual repricing following state-level minimum wage increases in Q1/Q2.
- 96-97% of customer contracts feature automatic wage inflation pass-through mechanisms.
- Continued automation investments (tractor-trailers, automated sorters, AGVs) are helping offset labor availability constraints and wage inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Deployed pilot experiments for self-developed Autonomous Guided Vehicles (AGVs) inside facilities and robotic mid-mile operations.
- Expanding footprint of Supply Chain Services (SCS) larger fulfillment centers across top major cities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a net cash position with Rs 4,676 crore in cash and cash equivalents.
- Sharp compression in unbilled receivables driven by faster software cycle time and automated claim resolution.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in air freight rates and international trade cycles.
- Regulatory changes around workforce compliance and state minimum wage adjustments.
- Seasonal labor availability bottlenecks and extreme weather impacts on workforce productivity.
- Extended sales conversion cycles for enterprise logistics contracts due to wage inflation uncertainty.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vijit Jain (Citi):** Asked if FY27 Express Parcel growth guidance of 20-30% remains intact.  
  *Management:* Revised volume growth expectation down to 16-20% for FY27 due to Q1 macro weakness, though October festive trends are solid.
- **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley):** Inquired about margin trajectory and handling low-margin marketplace volumes.  
  *Management:* Prioritizes network reliability and margin optimization over low-margin volume surges; disciplined volume selection keeps claims loss provisions significantly lower than industry averages.
- **Abhishek Banerjee (ICICI Securities):** Asked why Delhivery scaled back its Rapid/dark store initiative.  
  *Management:* Clarified that sub-scale dark stores were economically inefficient; shifted focus back to larger SCS fulfillment hubs serving multi-speed inventory pools.
- **Jinesh Joshi (Prabhudas Lilladher):** Asked about sequential fluctuations in non-operating income.  
  *Management:* Stated the variance was purely mark-to-market (MTM) adjustments on mutual fund/bond portfolios affected by interest rate movements, while core yield remained flat.

### One-line takeaway
Delhivery delivered strong Q2 FY27 profitability and Rs 3,066 crore revenue, driven by automation efficiencies, pricing discipline, and scale in new verticals despite macro volume adjustments.
