# Mahindra Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects strong revenue growth, turnaround of the express business (Rivigo) to EBITDA positivity, and improved profitability and returns.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,095 crore, up 24% YoY.
- **PAT:** Rs 26 crore (compared to a loss of Rs 10 crore YoY and Rs 25 crore QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 124 crore (up from Rs 85 crore YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 5.9% (up 90 bps YoY). Adjusted EBITDA margin (post Ind AS 116) at 2.9% (up 110 bps YoY).
- **ROCE (H1 FY27):** 12.9% (vs 1.7% in H1 FY26).
- **ROE (H1 FY27):** Positive 8.7% (vs -5.3% in H1 FY26).
- **Free Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Rs 52 crore (vs -Rs 210 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Net Debt / Cash:** Total consolidated debt of Rs 55 crore (mostly vehicle loans), cash balance of Rs 363 crore, resulting in net cash of Rs 308 crore.
- **Contract Logistics Revenue:** Rs 1,688 crore, up 28% YoY; EBITDA at Rs 115 crore (vs Rs 89 crore YoY).
- **MESPL (Rivigo / Express) Revenue:** Rs 169 crore, up 69% YoY; EBITDA at Rs 2.3 crore (vs loss of Rs 9.1 crore in Q1 FY26 / -Rs 9 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Mobility Revenue:** Rs 119 crore, up 33% YoY; EBITDA at Rs 3.4 crore (vs Rs 2.1 crore YoY).
- **Last-Mile Delivery (LMD) Revenue:** Rs 68 crore (down 23% YoY due to portfolio calibration); EBITDA at Rs 1.6 crore (vs Rs 40 lakh YoY).
- **Freight Forwarding Revenue:** Rs 50.6 crore, up 12% QoQ (down 44% YoY); EBITDA at Rs 1.7 crore.

### Highlights
- MESPL (Rivigo) achieved its first-ever EBITDA-positive quarter and achieved EBITDA positivity for H1 FY27. Ankur Bansal took over as CEO of MESPL in June.
- Contract logistics whitespace reduction target (committed in Q1 of last year to reduce 1.6 million vacant space by 95%) has been successfully achieved (vacant space reduced to 5% of June 2025 levels). Whitespace will no longer be called out as it is business as usual.
- Standalone balance sheet has been debt-free since September 2025.
- Declared a one-time dividend of Rs 15 crore in a wholly-owned subsidiary (knocked off in consolidated financials).
- Last-Mile Delivery (LMD) business is pivoting from a no-loss strategy towards a growth strategy.
- Capex spend of ~Rs 100-110 crore approved for the year (H1 spend is nearly half).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims for a 100 bps gross margin improvement over the medium term.
- MESPL's next milestone is to achieve PAT positivity; management views it as a multi-year growth story and will reinvest back into the business/network as profits grow.
- Freight forwarding H2 will be a build phase, with results expected to deliver in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- E-commerce and quick commerce continue to be important growth engines with favorable market tailwinds.
- Automotive, manufacturing, and industrials remain strong volume drivers across contract logistics and express.
- Monsoon was 14% below LPA nationally with uneven state distribution, but management expects to meet overall company targets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel cost increases have been fully passed on via contracts.
- Manpower costs remain under pressure (minimum wages, PF slab change from Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000) and are not fully passed through; efficiency and productivity actions are underway.
- Pre-festive build-up and flex-site costs impacted Q2/Q3 margins temporarily.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex guidance maintained at ~Rs 100-110 crore for the year, primarily directed towards contract logistics and the 2x2 joint venture (car carriers).
- A new technology transformation project for MLL and MESPL was approved by the Board to unify platforms, automate processes, and reduce manual intervention.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone balance sheet is debt-free. Consolidated debt stands at Rs 55 crore (vehicle loans).
- Consolidated net cash stands at Rs 308 crore (cash of Rs 363 crore).
- H1 FCF generated at Rs 52 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical challenges and prohibitive spot container rates impacting freight forwarding.
- Manpower wage pressures and government rule changes (PF slabs) increasing labor costs without full contract pass-through.
- Short notice windows for festive season labor deployment causing temporary cost spikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jinesh Joshi (Prabhudas Lilladher):** Asked about steady-state EBITDA margins for the B2B express business and gross margin drivers in contract logistics. *Management answered that the next milestone is PAT positivity, viewing Rivigo as a decade-long growth story requiring ongoing investments; noted that fuel pass-through is complete, but manpower costs face lingering headwinds.*
2. **Krupashankar NJ (Plutus Equities):** Asked about MESPL Q-o-Q growth drivers (yield vs volume) and festive spillovers. *Management clarified Q-o-Q growth was driven more by yield than volume due to selective unit-economic calibration.*
3. **Achal Lohade (JM Financial):** Asked about monsoon impact and M&M revenue mix in contract logistics. *Management stated state-level variations are internally managed via other pedals, and M&M mix remains in the ~60% zone as a valued anchor customer.*
4. **Saania Jain:** Inquired about remaining low-hanging fruit for cost optimization. *Management noted cost optimization has largely played out and future expansion will be driven by business growth and 3.5x new business wins.*
5. **Ankita Shah:** Asked about profitability, capital employed, and strategy for low-margin segments (LMD, freight forwarding, mobility). *Management explained LMD is pivoting to growth, freight forwarding is in a build phase for FY28, and mobility focuses heavily on B2B long-term contracts while keeping B2C restricted to select cities with low capital employed.*

### One-line takeaway
Mahindra Logistics delivered strong 24% YoY revenue growth and a sharp rebound in profitability led by contract logistics momentum and Rivigo achieving its first-ever EBITDA-positive quarter.
