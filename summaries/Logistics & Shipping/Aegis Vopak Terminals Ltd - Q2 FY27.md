# Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered a record-breaking performance in H1 FY27 and strong Q2 growth, driven by expansion capacities, high utilization, and a transformational global acquisition announcement.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained long-term capex objective of $5 billion through 2030-2031, with cumulative capex expected to reach ~$1.2 billion by next year and gearing capped at no more than 0.6x.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from managing geopolitical disruptions in Q1 to noting normalization of global supply conditions and recovery in logistics volumes (up 19% QoQ).
- New topics or risks: Announced the definitive agreement to acquire an 89.8% stake in Tristar Holdings Limited for US$809 million, expanding into a global integrated energy logistics platform.
- Last quarter's promises: Commissioned the 35,000 metric ton annual capacity LPG bottling plant at JNPT on October 15, 2026.

### Highlights
- Signed a definitive agreement on October 13, 2026, to acquire an 89.8% stake in Tristar Holdings Limited for US$809 million, expected to close in Q4 FY27.
- Commissioned an LPG bottling plant with a 35,000 metric ton annual capacity at JNPT on October 15, 2026.
- Acquired an additional 3 acres of land at Haldia for future liquid storage and allied handling expansion.
- Secured additional land at Mangalore to evaluate a further 60,000 cubic meters of liquid storage.
- Partnered with ITOCHU Corporation, which acquired a 10% stake in Aegis Terminal Pipavav Limited (ammonia facility) with intentions to increase to 25% over 3 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to complete the Tristar acquisition in Q4 of FY27.
- Management is confident of touching 1 million tonnes of distribution volumes in the current financial year.
- Cumulative capex is expected to reach ~$1.2 billion by next year, with an aggregate capex investment of ~$5 billion through 2030-2031, targeting a debt gearing of no more than 0.6x.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global supply conditions have largely normalized, with growth driven by new capacity additions, geographic reach, and new products (ammonia).
- LPG demand is supported by income effects, rising per capita energy consumption, and substitution away from dirty fuels (wooden briquettes).
- The country's static LPG capacity benefits from large-scale terminals enabling very large gas carriers (up to 50,000 metric tonnes) to unload within 24 hours, minimizing freight and demurrage costs.
- CRISIL Ratings report notes the Kandla-Gorakhpur LPG pipeline is ~96% physically complete, with the Kandla-Lucknow section charged with LPG.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Liquid division EBITDA margin reached 78% in H1, with realization tracking towards INR3,000 per CBM for EBITDA.
- Distribution margin sustained at ~INR7,000 per ton due to supply chain and procurement efficiencies.
- AVTL EBITDA margins expanded by ~372 bps YoY to 78.6% in Q2, driven by higher-realization locations like JNPA.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Mumbai port: Developing 64,000 cubic meters of liquid storage with an INR125 crore investment; progressing on schedule.
- JNPT greenfield expansion (INR1,675 crores): Comprises 318,000 cubic meters of liquids and 77,286 metric tonnes of LPG storage; first phase of liquid storage (~100,000 cubic meters) targeted for commissioning in Q3 FY27.
- JNPT: Progressing on the ~52,000 metric ton refrigerated double-wall LPG storage tank.
- Kandla: CRL-4 liquid terminal adding 94,148 cubic meters, with commissioning targeted for next financial year.
- Kochi: Expansion of 49,577 cubic meters underway, taking total capacity to 132,000 cubic meters (~60% increase), expected to be commissioned in early FY28.
- Mangalore: Recently added 75,000 cubic meters operating at 100% utilization (total capacity 193,000 cubic meters); evaluating an additional 60,000 cubic meters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at INR6,400 crores as of September.
- Shareholders passed an EGM resolution to fund up to INR6,000 crores.
- Target debt gearing capped at no more than 0.6x for AVTL's $5 billion capex plan, funded via debt, internal accruals, and equity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sourcing volumes were lower during the period due to some contracts expiring.
- AVTL PAT declined slightly YoY due to higher finance costs and tax.
- Completion of the Tristar acquisition remains subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vibhav Zutshi (JP Morgan) asked about Tristar integration challenges and synergies.** Management answered that Tristar's existing management will maintain operational continuity, and shared that Tristar's global liquid terminals, shipping fleet, and truck distribution will create mutual skill-set and commercial synergies. Management dodged specific quantitative synergies due to transaction completion constraints.
- **Satyam Kesarwani (Prabhudas Lilladher) asked about LPG market share and demand drivers.** Management stated they hold over 30% of India's static LPG capacity and cited income effects and substitution of dirty fuels as primary demand drivers.
- **Priyankar Biswas (JM Financial) asked about strategic sources and destination advantages from Tristar.** Management highlighted that Tristar's deep relationships with Middle East producers (ADNOC, Saudi Aramco) and global assets will benefit Aegis' global footprint and accelerate international integration.
- **Kunal Mehta (InCred Equities) asked about balance sheet stress from the Tristar acquisition and AVTL capex.** Management clarified that Aegis Logistics holds INR6,400 crores of free cash with an approved INR6,000 crore funding resolution, making the acquisition cash-backed without crazy debt, while AVTL's capex is self-funded via equity dilution and internal accruals under a 0.6x gearing cap.
- **Siddharth Chauhan (360 ONE Capital) asked about competitive intensity from Petronet LNG and others building LPG storage.** Management welcomed infrastructure additions given India's low storage capacity relative to a 90-day global ideal, emphasizing that execution speed and cost are key.

### One-line takeaway
Aegis delivered a record H1 FY27 driven by strong gas and liquid division performances and announced a transformational US$809 million acquisition of Tristar Holdings to expand globally.
