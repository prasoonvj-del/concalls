# Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 results)
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong operational performance, landmark contract wins in large-scale dredging and green tugs, and an upward revision in growth guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to 80% YoY (up from >60% last quarter; initially 30% to 40%).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted toward a larger addressable market via entry into large-volume maintenance dredging with the acquisition of TSHD 'River Pearl 48'.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Myanmar and Bahrain operations reported as nil/paused due to safety considerations. 
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Preferential issue (Rs 150 crores) and secondary market sale (Rs 100 crores) highlighted as completed; Saphale Phase 1 completion remains on track for February 2027.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 186.21 crores, up 89% YoY
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 101.71 crores, up 155% YoY
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 55%
- H1 FY27 Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 84.27 crores, up 267% YoY
- H1 FY27 PAT margin: 45%
- Order book: ~Rs 1,732.55 crores (Dredging: Rs 467.75 crores; Charter and Hire: Rs 1,100 crores; Shipbuilding: Rs 164 crores, excluding captive shipbuilding of Rs 275 crores)
- Bid pipeline: >Rs 3,500 crores
- New Mangalore Port maintenance dredging contract: Rs 256.92 crores (15.9 million cubic meters across 3 annual maintenance cycles)
- 3rd Green Tug contract (Mumbai Port Authority): Rs 236 crores over 15 years (aggregate of 3 green tug contracts: ~Rs 928 crores)
- IWAI shipbuilding contract: Rs 10 electric hybrid passenger ferries for Rs 62 crores
- Equity fundraising: Rs 150 crores preferential issue and Rs 100 crores secondary market sale

### Highlights
- Secured the largest dredging contract in company history: Rs 256.92 crores maintenance dredging contract from New Mangalore Port Authority.
- Acquired Trailing Suction Hopper Dredger (TSHD) 'River Pearl 48', expanding hopper capacity nearly fourfold to target large-scale maintenance dredging.
- Secured 3rd Green Tug contract from Mumbai Port Authority worth Rs 236 crores over 15 years.
- Secured IWAI contract for constructing 10 electric hybrid passenger ferries worth Rs 62 crores under the Harit Nauka initiative.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to 80% YoY (previously guided to >60%).
- EBITDA margins expected to remain higher than historical projections of 40%, potentially stabilizing around 45% or higher (H1 achieved 55%).
- Effective tax rate guided at approximately 15% for the full year.
- Shipbuilding revenue contribution targeted at 40% of overall revenue in FY28 and FY29, with dredging and chartering comprising the remaining 60%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's major and minor port maintenance dredging market volume is close to 150 million cubic meters per annum (port maintenance market ~Rs 2,000 crores/yr; capital dredging ~Rs 3,000 crores/yr).
- Government's Green Tug Transition Programme identifies a requirement of ~98 green tugs across major Indian ports.
- Global dredging market by top players is ~$20 billion annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Effective tax rate was 23% in Q2 due to interest income and shipbuilding profit mix, but full-year effective tax rate is guided at ~15%.
- Shipbuilding profits attract a corporate tax rate of 25%, while dredging and chartering revenues have near-negligible tax.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Saphale shipyard Phase 1 completion targeted by February 2027 to support captive green tug construction and external/export orders.
- Total planned capex of Rs 1,000 crores over a 2-year period (6-7 months elapsed).
- Asset turnover ratio expected to remain in the 1:1 to 1:1.5 range.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Raised Rs 150 crores via preferential issue and Rs 100 crores via secondary market sale.
- Shipbuilding working capital requirements remain low due to stage-based milestone payments and 10% advance drawdowns via bank guarantees/surety bonds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Myanmar and Bahrain operations remain paused/nil due to safety and security considerations for manpower and materials.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shubham (3A Financial Services):** Asked about tax rates and unallocable expenses. Management guided to a ~15% full-year effective tax rate and explained that unallocable expenses comprise corporate overheads not attributable to specific segments.
- **Sarang (Vimana Capital):** Inquired about working capital advances and deployment of the new TSHD 'River Pearl 48'. Management stated the vessel can generate >Rs 150 crores/yr and is currently bidding for multiple port and defense contracts.
- **Nipun Sharma (VLS Finance):** Asked for an update on FY27 guidance. Management formally upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to 80%.
- **Tanishka Agarwal (Niveshaay Investment):** Asked about the timeline for the Rs 1,000 crores capex and global opportunity size. Management confirmed a 2-year execution window (6-7 months completed) and detailed global/domestic market potentials.

### One-line takeaway
Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 89% revenue growth and 55% EBITDA margins, prompting management to raise FY27 revenue growth guidance to 80% on the back of landmark dredging and green tug contract wins.
