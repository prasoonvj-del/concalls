# Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by upgraded FY27 revenue guidance, margin expansion, and major contract wins in maintenance dredging and green tugs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: FY27 revenue growth target raised from northwards of 60% YoY to 80% YoY. Long-term EBITDA margins guided to stabilize above 45% (up from 35%-40% guided last quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains highly positive, backed by entry into high-volume maintenance dredging and expansion of the green tug chartering portfolio.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared:
  - New: Bahrain operations paused due to safety concerns until insurance/stability is restored; Myanmar revenue currently nil; Rs 55 crore working capital outflow in other current assets due to vendor advances in shipbuilding.
  - Old: Issues regarding pending DCI Mangrol receivables were fully resolved last quarter and not re-discussed.
- Last quarter's promises and delivery: Delivered on acquiring a large TSHD (*River Pearl 48*) for the New Mangalore contract; Saphale shipyard Phase 1 development remains on track for February 2027 completion.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 186.21 crore, up 89% YoY.
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 101.71 crore, up 155% YoY.
- EBITDA margin (H1 FY27): 55%.
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 84.27 crore, up 267% YoY.
- PAT margin (H1 FY27): 45%.
- Q2 FY27 Shipbuilding Revenue: ~Rs 40 crore (3x growth YoY).
- Order Book: Rs 1,732.55 crore (Dredging: Rs 467.75 crore, Charter & Hire: Rs 1,100 crore, Commercial Shipbuilding: Rs 164 crore, excluding captive shipbuilding of Rs 275 crore).
- Bid Pipeline: Exceeding Rs 3,500 crore (Rs 2,500 crore to Rs 3,000 crore active bids in pipeline).

### Highlights
- Secured landmark 3-year maintenance dredging contract worth Rs 256.92 crore from New Mangalore Port Authority for 15.9 million cubic meters.
- Acquired *River Pearl 48* (TSHD), nearly quadrupling hopper capacity to service high-volume maintenance dredging with annual revenue potential north of Rs 150 crore.
- Awarded 3rd Green Tug contract from Mumbai Port Authority (Rs 236 crore over 15 years); total 3 Green Tug contracts stand at ~Rs 928 crore.
- Secured IWAI shipbuilding order for 10 electric hybrid passenger ferries valued at Rs 62 crore under the Harit Nauka initiative.
- Secondary market equity placement of Rs 100 crore with SBI Funds Management following a Rs 150 crore preferential issue with institutional investors.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to 80% YoY.
- Projected revenue growth for FY28 and FY29 at 40% to 60% YoY.
- Shipbuilding projected to contribute ~40% of overall revenue by FY28/FY29 (60% from dredging and chartering).
- Full-year effective tax rate guided at around 15% (less than 20%).
- Order book expected to remain continuously in excess of Rs 2,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian port maintenance dredging market size estimated at ~150 million cubic meters annually (~Rs 2,000 crore per year); capital dredging averages ~Rs 3,000 crore annually.
- Government Green Tug Transition Programme targets ~98 green tugs across major Indian ports; over 1,000–1,500 coastal/inland crafts targeted for green conversion by 2047.
- Q2 is seasonally weak due to monsoons; management expects a strong seasonal rebound in dredging execution in Q3 and Q4.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Unallocable corporate overhead expenses were Rs 10 crore for H1 FY27.
- Shipbuilding profits incur a standard 25% corporate tax rate, while dredging and chartering face minimal effective tax, maintaining a blended effective tax rate of ~15%.
- EBITDA margins expected to range between 45% and 55% during current execution phases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rs 1,000 crore 2-year capex plan ongoing (6–7 months executed): covers TSHD *River Pearl 48*, 3 green tugs, 1 conventional tug, and Saphale shipyard.
- Phase 1 development of Saphale shipyard scheduled for completion by February 2027 (full commissioning post Q4 FY27).
- Asset turnover ratio on capex expected to be maintained between 1:1 and 1:1.5.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Equity structure expected to remain stable in the near future; expansion plans will utilize debt and internal accruals as needed.
- Working capital outflow of Rs 55 crore recorded under other current assets due to advance payments to vendors for shipbuilding materials.
- Shipbuilding contracts feature 5–10 stage-wise milestone payments (including 10% advance against bank guarantees/surety bonds), avoiding major working capital lockup.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Operations in Bahrain are temporarily suspended due to regional safety and security concerns; waiting for insurance clarity before resuming.
- Revenue recognition in dredging and shipbuilding is time-sensitive and subject to strict contract execution timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shubham (3A Financial Services):** Inquired about the effective tax rate guidance and unallocable line item. *Management explained the ~15% tax rate reflects segment mix and unallocable expenses (~Rs 10 crore) represent non-segment corporate overheads, not operational losses.*
- **Sarang (Vimana Capital):** Asked about working capital advance outflows and deployment of *River Pearl 48*. *Management stated Rs 55 crore outflow was advances to vendors for shipbuilding, and RP 48 can generate >Rs 150 crore/year across New Mangalore and additional coastal/naval contracts.*
- **Nipun Sharma (VLS Finance):** Asked about guidance revisions and long-term segment revenue mix. *Management raised FY27 growth guidance to 80% and indicated shipbuilding will scale to 40% of revenues in FY28/FY29.*
- **Noor (MN Ventures):** Asked about asset utilization and international operations. *Management noted chartering is at 100% utilization, dredging resumes 100% post-monsoon (Oct–May), while Myanmar revenue is nil and Bahrain is paused for safety.*
- **Tanishka Agarwal (Niveshaay Investment):** Asked for timelines on the Rs 1,000 crore capex and market potential. *Management reiterated the 2-year timeline (6–7 months in) with 1:1 to 1:1.5 asset turnover, noting a ~150mn cu.m domestic maintenance dredging market.*

### One-line takeaway
Knowledge Marine upgraded its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 80% YoY, backed by high-margin maintenance dredging and long-term green tug contracts while building capacity at its Saphale shipyard.
