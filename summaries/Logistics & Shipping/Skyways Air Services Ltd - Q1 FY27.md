# Skyways Air Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-23 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stellar operating and financial performance across all segments with strong volume and revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- Operating Revenue: Rs 1,216.53 crore (up 90.4% YoY from Rs 639 crore)
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 50.12 crore (up 83.5% YoY from Rs 27.31 crore)
- PAT: Rs 26.79 crore (up 143.3% YoY from Rs 11.01 crore)
- PAT Margin: 2.2% (vs 1.72% in Q1 FY26)
- Air Cargo Volume Growth: 23% YoY
- Ocean Freight Realization Growth: >64% YoY; Volume Growth: 18% YoY
- Express Revenue: Rs 63 crore (up 64% YoY) with 59,700 shipments across 31 locations
- Employee Benefit Expense: 2.94% of revenue (down from 4.41% YoY)
- Other Expenses: 1.76% of revenue (down from 2.5% YoY)
- Debt Repayment post-listing: Approx. Rs 140 crore repaid

### Highlights
- Ranked as the number one air freight forwarder in India and 44th globally for Q1 FY27 (WorldACD market data).
- Indian air cargo market share improved from 5.9% to 6.2% QoQ.
- Successfully navigated the West Asia crisis by creating alternate airline capacities, strengthening customer relationships and acquiring new customers.
- Board approved expansion into 5 new geographies in Asia (China, Philippines, Malaysia, etc.) with a total investment of Rs 30 crore; Rs 20 crore investment approved for existing overseas subsidiaries (UAE, KSA, Vietnam).
- Tech platforms (SLS Hike, Cargo Dash, SLS 100X) driving operational efficiencies; new customer acquisition platform "ASAP" launching in 30-60 days.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the trend of volume increases to continue into Q2 FY27.
- Management aims to provide volume guidance every quarter during earnings calls.
- Targeted ROE range to remain around 15% going forward.
- International offices gestation period expected to be 2 to 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian logistics industry across transportation means expected to grow by 40% to 50% over the next 5 years, reaching USD 500 billion by 2031.
- Ministry of Civil Aviation targets 10 million metric tons over the next 5 years (up from 3.96 million metric tons).
- Key growing sectors for the company include pharmaceuticals, textiles, readymade garments, automotive, consumer electronics, auto parts, and fruits & vegetables.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel costs and freight per-unit costs act as a full pass-through to customers.
- Pharmaceutical gross margins are higher than the overall group average gross margin profile.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Generic annual capex plan for the group is around Rs 35 crore to Rs 40 crore per annum (excluding the cold chain warehouse project operational next quarter).
- International expansion investment approved at Rs 30 crore (10%-15% towards initial setting up/capex, rest for setup cost and working capital).
- Additional Rs 20 crore investment approved for overseas subsidiaries in UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Vietnam.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Approx. Rs 140 crore of borrowings repaid to financial institutions and banks post-listing.
- Historical ROE is 14% to 15%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- EOW legal matter is sub judice and under investigation; management stated they see no possible financial impact.
- Fuel prices and freight realizations are dynamic and market-driven.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Utkarsh Maheshwari (IndusInd General Insurance) on steady-state revenue and yields:** Management clarified that 23-25% of revenue growth is volume-driven, while yield increases are primarily due to rising global fuel index costs, which are fully passed through to customers.
- **Subhanu Bangal (Investor) on volume growth sustainability:** Management stated they are very confident of sustaining a 25-30% volume growth trend.
- **Rohit Mehra (Investor) on international capex and gestation:** Management stated that new international markets typically take 2 to 4 years to mature, but they aim for 2 to 3 years. EBITDA break-even generally occurs in 15 to 18 months.
- **Piyush Parag (Investor) on pharma contribution and Odyssey acquisition:** Management noted pharma contribution grew from 8-9% pre-Odyssey to 23% last year and has been sustained. Odyssey standalone revenue grew to Rs 182 crore.
- **Ameet Kishorpuria (Investor) on ROE dilution from debt repayment:** Management stated that the debt reduction is strategic, and profitability from future expansion will support the targeted 15% ROE range.
- **Jasmine (Investor) on the EOW legal disclosure:** Management reiterated that the matter is sub judice, they have submitted representations, and they are confident of a positive outcome based on the merits of their case.

### One-line takeaway
Skyways Air Services delivered a stellar Q1 FY27 with a 90.4% YoY surge in operating revenue and an 83.5% jump in operating EBITDA, driven by robust 23% air cargo volume growth, successful market share gains, and strategic debt reduction post-listing.
