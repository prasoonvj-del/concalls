# CMS Info Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Fourth consecutive quarter of record services revenue with strong EBITDA growth and margin expansion despite high inflation and currency supply disruption.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 services revenue guidance of INR 2,650 crores - INR 2,750 crores, total revenue guidance of INR 2,750 crores - INR 2,850 crores, EBITDA margin of 27%, and capex of INR 100 crores - INR 125 crores.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from "mixed" (due to severe currency supply squeeze) to more positive as currency supply recovery continued, ATM dispense rates improved, and private sector pricing revisions were successfully concluded.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: IBA working committee discussions for PSU bank pricing concluded/pending finalization; focus moved to execution of order book.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Tech & payments segment reached 20% of services revenue two quarters ahead of the Q4 target set in the previous quarter.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** INR 650 crore, up 6.7% YoY and 2.4% QoQ
- **Services Revenue:** INR 644 crore, up 16.2% YoY and 3% QoQ (highest ever)
- **EBITDA:** INR 173 crore, up 24.8% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 26.7% (H1 at 27%, 290 bps higher YoY)
- **PAT:** INR 81 crore, up 10.3% YoY
- **EPS:** INR 5.05, up 14.9% YoY
- **H1 Operating Cash Flow:** INR 177 crore (51% of EBITDA)
- **Net Cash:** INR 473 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **H1 Net Capex:** INR 50 crore
- **Order Wins (H1):** INR 700 crore
- **Tech & Payments Share:** 20% of services revenue

### Highlights
- Delivered fourth consecutive quarter of record services revenue.
- Reached the 20% Tech & Payments revenue contribution two quarters ahead of forecast.
- Won second large PSU bank branch RMS mandate (650 branches command center and 8,000+ branches monitoring) and added third marquee bank for ALGO MVS software platform (12,000 machines).
- Maintained market share at 60% in ATM cash management and 38% in retail cash solutions.
- Continued disciplined approach by cutting transaction-linked ATM capex by 63% YoY in H1 (INR 10.6 crore) and focusing exclusively on fixed-fee contracts.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reaffirmed FY27 guidance: Services revenue of INR 2,650 crore - INR 2,750 crore; Total revenue of INR 2,750 crore - INR 2,850 crore; EBITDA margin of 27%; Capex of INR 100 crore - INR 125 crore.
- Management expects depreciation to peak around current levels and moderate relative to revenue starting FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Currency in circulation reached a record high of ~INR 43.5 lakh crore, up 13.5% YoY (fastest growth in 5 years).
- Retail same-store cash throughput grew 3.7% YoY in Q2.
- Currency supply recovery continued; average dispense per ATM per day up 8% from May low (though still 10% below last year).
- Off-site ATMs in India declined from 88,000 to 73,000 over two years due to network rationalization, while total ATM base held steady at 2.1 lakh with on-site ATMs growing ~4% annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Statutory minimum wages rose 6% to 60% across key states; fuel costs increased 8%.
- Revised pricing successfully implemented across private sector banks and retail customers; PSU bank pricing process led by IBA working committee expected to conclude soon.
- Three structural drivers behind margin expansion: price revisions, route optimization productivity, and favorable business mix (higher tech/fixed-fee share).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 net capex stood at INR 50 crore; full-year FY27 capex guided at INR 100 crore - INR 125 crore.
- Focus remains on executing order book and integrating synergistic acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position at INR 473 crore after returning INR 168 crore via buyback in June.
- DSO improved by ~18 days in H1 compared to last year.
- Customer loan to a specific customer stood at INR 64 crore as of September 2026 (down from INR 75 crore peak inclusive of due amounts, with INR 12 crore repaid).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High inflation environment driven by steep statutory minimum wage hikes and 8% fuel cost inflation.
- Lower transaction-linked ATM revenue and higher depreciation weighing on Managed Services segment EBIT margins (10.6%).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sooraj Nair (Acuitas Capital) on growth drivers vs CIC and HAWKAI moat in non-banking:** Management explained that CIC indicates macro demand while integrated contracts drive revenue; for HAWKAI, the right to win stems from being OEM-agnostic, plugin-ready, and backed by a national distribution and service network. Management did not dodge.
2. **Gaurav (Investor) on capex reconciliation, working capital, and DSO:** Management clarified that capex included asset/contract purchases for Securens (INR 50 crore) and CWIP transfers, and noted collections improved in H1 with DSO down ~18 days. Management did not dodge.
3. **Keshav Garg (Investments) on recurring bad debt provisions and write-offs:** Management explained provisions follow ECL methodology (risk cost ~4%) based on handling INR 14 lakh crore of cash, and offered offline reconciliation. Management did not dodge.
4. **Akhilesh (NorthStar) on FY28/29 depreciation outlook and asset life impact:** Management estimated next year's depreciation to be around INR 300 crore (5% variation) and noted the prior year accounting impact from asset life change was INR 4.7 crore. Management did not dodge.
5. **Akshit Shetty (Individual Investor) on EBITDA margin lease/ESOP adjustments and customer loan:** Management clarified that leases are part of integrated BLA contracts and operational, and walked through the customer loan repayment tracking (INR 75 crore gross down to INR 64 crore net). Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
CMS Info Systems delivered its fourth consecutive quarter of record services revenue with 24.8% EBITDA growth and 26.7% margins, driven by strong Tech & Payments scale and rigorous cost discipline.
