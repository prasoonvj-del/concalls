# Jet Freight Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong volume growth, expansion into new international trade lanes, and consistent margin preservation despite freight rate fluctuations.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Top line (Revenue):** Rs 297 crore
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 1.55% (stated as 1.52% by CFO)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 4.58%
- **H1 FY27 Tonnage Growth:** 32.84% YoY (reaching 744 tonnes from 545 tonnes)
- **H1 FY27 Shipments Growth:** 56% YoY (reaching 674 shipments from 430 shipments)
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Rs 580 crore to Rs 600 crore
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 12.5 crore
- **Global Industry Size (2026):** $6.72 trillion (projected to reach $8.84 trillion by 2032)
- **Indian Logistics Sector Market Size (2026):** $259 billion (projected to reach $429 billion by 2034)
- **Pharmaceutical Exports (FY25):** Exceeded USD 21 billion

### Highlights
- Celebrating 40 years of existence; handles ~95% of commodities by air and sea globally across 14 locations with ~250 employees and ~20,000 shareholders.
- Feature among the top 10 in air freight in India and top 3 for most airlines out of India (Air India, Emirates, Qatar, Lufthansa, etc.).
- First company to dispatch cargo out of Navi Mumbai Airport (via Air India).
- Transitioning from 2PL to 4PL and launching retail courier services (B2B/B2C) alongside wholesale courier.
- Developing a proprietary unified ERP technology platform (analogous to a "MakeMyTrip for cargo and courier") to automate bookings, KYC, and live tracking for franchisees and customers, targeting completion by the end of the year.
- In the process of acquiring a Dubai-based company, NatWest, to capture Gulf/Middle East/Saudi market share directly and handle charter brokerages internally.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to close FY27 with a top line of Rs 580 crore to Rs 600 crore.
- Management expects bottom-line margins to increase quarter-on-quarter and year-on-year because current infrastructure can handle double the existing business volumes without incremental fixed infrastructure costs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- E-commerce and courier are growing rapidly internationally; wholesale courier is seeing large volumes to the US, London, Australia, New Zealand, and Frankfurt.
- Perishable goods account for 15-20% of India's air cargo volumes, and the company is the number one player in perishables out of India (highest volume of mangoes and Indian roses).
- Ministry of Civil Aviation target: 10 million tons of air cargo annually by 2030 (2.7x current volume).
- India's e-commerce logistics market is projected to reach $103.83 billion by 2034 from $19.54 billion in 2025.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industry margins typically range from 10% to 25% (sometimes dipping to 7-8%).
- Management stated that when freight charges go up, they maintain their absolute margins rather than compressing them.
- Fixed infrastructure costs remain stable; current infrastructure can support double the volume.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Follows an asset-lite model: all warehouses and surface transportation are outsourced/leased; no land or warehouses are owned.
- Expanding into South America (India-Brazil/Argentina trade lanes), starting with a senior hire in Brazil focused on sales, with future plans for local operations.
- Scaling up Ocean Freight operations, centralized out of Delhi, targeting large-scale volume growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt figures provided, but management noted that borrowing costs remain constant relative to top-line growth. No owned land or real estate on the balance sheet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and fuel price volatility impact freight rates, though management noted fuel price fluctuations drive corresponding freight adjustments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Hardik on freight charge increases and impact on margins:** Management replied that they protect their margins even if freight rates double or triple, as they do not operate without preserving margins.
2. **Angira Patel on the NatWest (Dubai) acquisition rationale and financials:** Management explained the acquisition will allow them to capture charter brokering margins directly instead of outsourcing, boosting margins in the next financial year (minimal impact in FY27 due to ongoing paperwork).
3. **Angira Patel on NatWest valuation given its low revenue:** Management stated valuation is based on future order pipelines from promoters, synergies, and software distribution in the UAE.
4. **Hasti on scaling Ocean Freight:** Management noted operations are centralized in Delhi while sales teams handle cross-selling to existing agri and pharma clients who already utilize sea freight.
5. **Drashti on Q2 volume vs. pricing drivers:** Management attributed growth primarily to continuous, process-driven volume expansion and new customer additions via sales efforts, while fuel prices dictate short-term pricing adjustments.
6. **Hardik on net profit margin scope for improvement:** Management reiterated that PAT margins will expand because current infrastructure can handle double the volume without added costs.

### One-line takeaway
Jet Freight Logistics is leveraging its asset-lite model, expanding into South American trade lanes and retail e-commerce courier, and pursuing a Dubai acquisition to drive scale toward its Rs 580-600 crore FY27 revenue target while improving profitability through operating leverage.
