# Flomic Global Logistics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and significant EBITDA margin expansion driven by operational efficiency and rising freight rates.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 120 crore (vs Rs 101.38 crore YoY).
*   **Revenue Growth:** 18.4% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 11.21 crore (vs Rs 6.68 crore YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 9.34% (vs 6.59% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 2.06 crore (vs Rs 2.98 crore in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Integrated Model:** Operates as an integrated logistics platform (freight forwarding, customs, warehousing, project cargo) rather than a pure freight forwarder.
*   **Asset-Light Strategy:** Management emphasized maintaining an asset-light model to avoid unnecessary capital blocking.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Implemented AI-based software (effective July 1st) to automate documentation and reduce manpower intensity.
*   **Network Optimization:** Rationalized branch network by closing non-performing locations to focus on profitable hubs.
*   **Customer Base:** ~52% of revenue comes from customers with a 3+ year relationship.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects the current growth momentum to continue for the next 3-4 quarters.
*   Targeting to increase warehousing contribution to ~20% of total business.
*   Focus remains on "profitable growth" rather than chasing top-line volume.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Freight Rates:** Global freight rates are rising, which is a positive for the industry.
*   **Volume vs. Rate:** Revenue growth was a mix of ~6-7% volume growth and the remainder from freight rate increases.
*   **Sector Demand:** Seeing strong demand in pharma, engineering, oil & gas, aerospace, and solar energy.
*   **Geopolitics:** Middle East volatility has shifted some ocean cargo to air, providing an additional boost to the company's air freight segment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Expansion:** EBITDA margin expanded by 275 bps YoY.
*   **Warehousing Margins:** Operates at 15-20% gross margin; management noted that Ind AS 116 accounting makes warehousing margins appear disproportionately high in the books.
*   **Cost Rationalization:** Manpower costs are being reduced through automation and digitization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Warehousing:** Expansion will be leased and customer-backed; no plans for heavy asset-heavy capex.
*   **Project Cargo:** Hiring specialized teams to grow this high-margin vertical.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** DSO (collection cycle) improved from 72 days to 56 days.
*   **Target:** Management aims to maintain a 30-60 day collection cycle.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Volatility:** High volatility in global freight rates and trade conditions.
*   **Execution Risk:** Project cargo involves higher complexity and execution risk compared to standard freight.
*   **Manpower Intensity:** The industry is historically manpower-intensive, though management is using technology to mitigate this.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How does the company prioritize between top-line growth and margins?** A: Management stated the priority is the asset-light model and profitable growth; they are not chasing turnover for the sake of it.
*   **Q: What is the scale of the project logistics business?** A: It is a high-margin, specialized vertical focusing on oversized cargo for sectors like oil & gas and aerospace; it is a key growth driver.
*   **Q: How does technology impact profitability?** A: AI-based automation reduces paperwork and turnaround time (TAT), allowing for operating leverage without proportional increases in manpower costs.
*   **Q: Why did PAT decline YoY despite revenue growth?** A: Management did not explicitly explain the PAT decline but emphasized that operating profitability (EBITDA) improved significantly due to efficiency and cost rationalization.

### One-line takeaway
Flomic delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with significant margin expansion, leveraging an asset-light model and rising freight rates while focusing on high-margin project logistics and digital automation.
