# Flomic Global Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by elevated freight rates and expansion in warehousing capabilities.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained target to increase warehousing contribution to 17%-18% over coming quarters and eventually to ~20% of total turnover.
- Tone and demand view: Continued positive momentum with elevated freight rates persisting across international trade routes through Q2.
- New topics or risks: Detailed specific capacity additions at Bhiwandi (multi-tier storage and pallet racking); highlighted challenges in Gulf shipping routes increasing lead times.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on warehousing capacity expansion (Bhiwandi vertical expansion completed between July and September 2026).

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 156.00 crore (vs Rs 113.72 crore YoY, up ~37%)
*   **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 275.65 crore (vs Rs 215.10 crore YoY, up 28.15%)
*   **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 16.94 crore (vs Rs 8.91 crore YoY, up 90%)
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 10.88% (vs 7.84% YoY, expansion of ~300 bps)
*   **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 28.14 crore (vs Rs 15.60 crore YoY, up 80.48%)
*   **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 10.21% (vs 7.24% YoY, expansion of ~296 bps)
*   **PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 6.09 crore (vs loss of Rs 0.78 crore YoY)
*   **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 4.32 crore (vs loss of ~Rs 1 crore YoY)
*   **PBT (H1 FY27):** Rs 8.82 crore (vs loss of Rs 4.07 crore YoY)
*   **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 6.39 crore (vs loss of Rs 3.99 crore YoY)
*   **Warehousing Capacity Addition:** 15,000 sq. ft. storage across three tiers + over 2,000 pallet positions (up to ~8,000 pallets total) at Bhiwandi.
*   **Warehousing Revenue Contribution:** ~13% in Q2 FY27; ~14% in H1 FY27.
*   **Freight Forwarding Revenue Contribution:** ~86% in Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
- **Segment Revenue Mix (Q2 FY27):** Sea export 38%, air and sea import 30%, air export 14%, warehousing 13%, and air and sea import clearance 5%.
- **Bhiwandi Warehousing Expansion:** Expanded vertically without leasing new space. Commissioned a ground plus three-tier structure (adding 15,000 sq. ft.) occupied by a quick commerce customer in September; installed G plus five-level pallet racking (adding >2,000 pallet positions) occupied by an FMCG customer in October.
- **Customer Metrics:** Repeat customers accounted for 51% of business in FY26, and top 15 customers contributed >20% of turnover.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to gradually increase warehousing contribution to 17%-18% over coming quarters and eventually to ~20% of total turnover.
- Prioritizing scaling warehousing based on customer demand, strengthening project cargo/specialized logistics, and deepening existing customer relationships.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Freight rates remained elevated across several international trade routes, particularly in July, August, and September (e.g., India to US 40-footer rates rose from ~$2,800 early in the year to ~$9,600 during August/September).
- Geopolitical uncertainties and Gulf challenges have increased transit times and lead times, though cargo continues to move via alternative routings.
- Strong growth in air export/import volumes, supported by new airport developments like Navi Mumbai Airport.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed-cost asset-light model allowed higher turnover to drive operating leverage and expand EBITDA margin by ~300 bps YoY in Q2.
- Ind AS 116 accounting treats rent on leased warehousing premises under interest and depreciation rather than operating costs, making gross warehousing margins appear high.
- Tight control maintained on operating costs, including employment expenses, aided by new software for cost optimization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bhiwandi warehouse expansion achieved via internal infrastructure optimization (shelving/racking) rather than leasing new space. Overall space utilization operates at ~97.5%.
- Expansion is strictly customer-backed with clear visibility rather than speculative.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital investment increased slightly due to higher turnover, impacting interest costs, but was offset by overall EBITDA growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unpredictable geopolitical situations and challenges in the Gulf region impacting transit times.
- Volatility in global freight rates.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Chatrika Deshpandey (Capital Advisors):** Asked about domestic network expansion criteria. *Management answered that new sales offices are based on manufacturing potential and import/export activity in specific geographies.*
2. **Divya Reddy:** Asked about the 3-5 year business mix and warehousing strategy. *Management stated warehousing will grow from 13-14% to 17-18% and eventually 20%, driven strictly by customer-backed expansion and high utilization rather than speculative buildout.*
3. **Shravan Modi:** Asked about the drivers of 36-37% revenue growth and EBITDA outperformance. *Management explained growth was primarily driven by surging freight rates (e.g., US route rates doubling/tripling), which flowed through to EBITDA due to fixed operating costs.*
4. **Shravan Modi:** Asked for revenue/profitability contribution of the Bhiwandi expansion. *Management stated the 15,000 sq. ft. adds ~Rs 50 lakh annually, the pallet positions add Rs 40-50 lakh annually, totaling ~Rs 1 crore in potential annual revenue at full capacity.*
5. **Isha Jain (Jain Investments):** Asked about specialized warehousing and global partnerships. *Management highlighted booming e-commerce/quick-commerce demand and noted current international presence is maintained via networks like WCA (World Cargo Alliance).*

### One-line takeaway
Flomic delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 37% revenue growth and a 90% jump in EBITDA, capitalizing on elevated global freight rates and expanding its warehousing footprint efficiently.
