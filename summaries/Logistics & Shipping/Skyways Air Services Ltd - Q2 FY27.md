# Skyways Air Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent scale-up, broad-based growth across all segments, and margin expansion driven by operational leverage and technology.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management targeted revenue of over Rs 5,000 crores for the full year FY27; no specific prior quantitative revenue guidance number was given last quarter other than expecting continued volume growth.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to a more explicit full-year revenue target backed by strong H2 seasonality and booming Indian non-petroleum merchandise exports.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Mention of the Birmingham, UK warehouse and investment in Sageroute Logistics (via Brace Port) were new; EOW legal matter from last quarter was not mentioned.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: ASAP tech platform launch timeline was updated to early Q4 (final testing stages); international office expansion into 5 geographies is on track for Q4 operationalization.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 1,311.45 crores (up 90.3% YoY, up 7.8% QoQ)
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27): Rs 2,527.9 crores (up 90.3% YoY)
- Operating EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 58.47 crores (up 96% YoY); EBITDA Margin: 4.46%
- Operating EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 108.60 crores (up 90.3% YoY); EBITDA Margin: 4.3%
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 33.18 crores (up 129.6% YoY); PAT Margin: 2.53% (up 43 bps YoY)
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 59.97 crores (up 135% YoY); PAT Margin: 2.37% (up 46 bps YoY)
- Air Cargo Volumes (Q2 FY27): 27,629 tonnes (up 27% YoY); Air Cargo Revenue: Rs 983.42 crores (up 90.1% YoY)
- Ocean Freight Volumes (Q2 FY27): 9,165 TEUs (up 15.9% YoY); Ocean Revenue: Rs 247.1 crores (up 113.8% YoY)
- Express Cargo Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 58.79 crores (up 34.6% YoY); Shipments: 72,197 (up 8.4% YoY)
- Non-air H1 Revenue: Rs 566 crores (vs Rs 315 crores in H1 last year)
- Annualized ROCE: Approx. 23% (H1 ROCE reported as 10.46%)
- ROE (H1 FY27): 8.23%
- Gross Debt-to-Equity: 0.58 as of September 30, 2026
- Gross Debtors Realization Period: 62.77 days (vs 75.19 days in FY26)
- Second Interim Dividend: Rs 0.40 per equity share (4% of face value of Rs 10)

### Highlights
- Ranked #1 air cargo player in India and improved global ranking by 2 positions to #42 at the end of Q2.
- Renewed annual global agreement with Qatar Airways; received awards from Emirates SkyCargo and IAG Cargo.
- Established a warehouse in Birmingham, UK, to target automotive and trade flows under the India-UK FTA.
- Acquired 76% in Sageroute Logistics via subsidiary Brace Port to build landside operations for the renewable energy sector.
- Group customer base stabilized at over 9,500 with no customer losses reported.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets full-year revenue of over Rs 5,000 crores, expecting H2 to be stronger than H1.
- Management expects a sustained volume growth of 25% to 30% YoY over the next 4 to 5 years.
- Aiming for an early breakeven for the 5 new international geographies targeting operationalization in Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian non-petroleum merchandise grew 18% to USD 259 billion between April and October; October merchandise exports stood at USD 49.47 billion.
- Growth driven by pharmaceuticals, consumer electronics, semiconductors, defence, automotive, textiles, and agri-products.
- Import volumes into India growing, with management targeting 40% to 42% growth in import numbers this year (imports currently represent 5% to 6% of the business).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage resulting in lower employee and overhead cost ratios relative to revenue; headcount grew ~15% while revenue grew ~90%.
- Fuel surcharges can cause 5% to 10% quarter-on-quarter variations in yields, acting as a pass-through.
- International offices' margins are expected to be better than India export margins post-breakeven, driving overall EBITDA margin expansion over 1 to 2 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company maintains an asset-sensitive (largely asset-light) model, investing in critical assets like temperature-controlled cold chain facilities (e.g., 200,000 cubic feet facility in Delhi) only when backed by customer commitments.
- Setting up a subsidiary in Abu Dhabi Free Zone (Avito Global Freight) and rolling out 5 international geographies starting Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt-to-equity reduced to 0.58.
- Collections cycle improved with gross debtor days reducing to 62.77 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global shipping and logistics disruptions and geopolitical issues impacting container rotation and routing changes, though ocean capacities are expected to remain range-bound.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gunesh Wagh (RPI Advisors) on technology platform productivity and ROI/ROE trend:** Management answered that platforms speed up pricing, routing access, and document processing via AI/OCR, expanding wallet share. On ROE dilution, management explained it reflects the denominator effect from pre-IPO/IPO equity expansions, with H1 annualized ROE translating upwards of 16%-16.5%.
2. **Yash Parker (Private Investor) on sustainability of air/ocean yields:** Management stated that general freight rates are stable, and yield variations are primarily driven by fuel surcharges (5-10% QoQ fluctuations). Ocean rates are range-bound due to Red Sea/Middle East routing disruptions.
3. **Karan Sharma (Investor) on export vs import mix:** Management stated imports currently form 5% to 6% of revenue but are targeted for high growth (40-42% this year), aided by new Asian footprint expansion.
4. **Divya Reddy (Investor) on digital platform adoption:** Management stated online bookings contribute 15% to 20% overall, while the Express/e-commerce segment operates at 95%-96% digital penetration.
5. **Deepankar Bisht (Investor) on the upcoming ASAP platform launch:** Management stated ASAP is in final testing (2-5 weeks remaining) for a Q4 launch, enabling real-time booking and payments for MSMEs in Tier 3/Tier 4 cities, expecting a 15% to 20% business addition from smaller markets.

### One-line takeaway
Skyways Air Services posted a stellar H1 FY27 with revenue up 90.3% YoY to Rs 2,527.9 crores and PAT up 135% YoY, driven by strong 27% air cargo volume growth and disciplined execution.
