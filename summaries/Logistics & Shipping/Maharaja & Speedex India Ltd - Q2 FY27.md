# Maharaja & Speedex India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 36% YoY revenue growth, improved margins, debt reduction, and strong demand tailwinds from BIS and anti-dumping duties.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 78.5 crore (up 36% YoY, up 21% vs second half of the year)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 15.1 crore (up 52% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 19.2% (vs 17.2% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 9.9 crore (up 50% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 12.5%
- **Debt-Equity Ratio:** 0.04 (short-term borrowings of Rs 22.7 crore fully repaid)
- **Current Annual Capacity:** 72 lakh bottles per year
- **Monthly Revenue Run-rate:** ~Rs 16 crore (as of November 2026)
- **Upcoming IPO-funded Capacity Addition:** 31 lakh units going live in Q4 FY27, taking total capacity to ~103 lakh units per year
- **Online Business Contribution:** Increased to 14.5% of total sales

### Highlights
- First earnings call as a listed company.
- Management stated balance sheet is in the best shape ever after repaying all short-term borrowings of ~Rs 22.7 crore.
- Outsourcing reduced from ~11-12% to 6.5% in H1, contributing to gross margin expansion.
- Value-added/novelty products contribution increased from 19% to over 23%.
- Continued capacity expansion from internal accruals brought current capacity to 72 lakh bottles/year, with November monthly revenue running at ~Rs 16 crore (confirming our record of capacity expansion to 72 lakh units funded from internal accruals).
- IPO-funded expansion of 31 lakh units scheduled to go live in Q4 FY27, reaching ~103 lakh units/year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to grow annual capacity by 40-50% and revenue by at least 50% (targeting >45% revenue growth for FY27).
- EBITDA margins expected to remain stable around 19% to 19.5% in the long run, with excess efficiency gains reinvested into brand marketing and team building.
- Online business target: 20-22% of monthly business by end of this year, reaching ~30% by end of next year.
- Own brand mix target: To increase own brand share to 65% in next 3-4 years (from current ~45-46%), reducing OEM/ODM share to 35%.
- Distributor network target: More than 150 distributors by the end of the year (from current 100).
- Lunch/bento boxes and tiffin boxes: Planned for launch in H2 of next year via trading/sourcing partners, followed by manufacturing lines once revenue share exceeds 5%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry tailwinds driven by BIS quality norms and anti-dumping duties introduced in March 2025, shifting buyers from unorganized to organized certified players.
- Imports made costlier by 20-25% due to anti-dumping duties, dollar price increases, and higher freight costs.
- Demand is supply-constrained in India as very few players can manufacture economically and efficiently.
- Replacement cycle is fast (within 2-6 months for kids/gym shakers due to usage, wear and tear, and lifestyle/trend shifts).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins expanded due to lower outsourcing (down to 6.5%), higher share of value-added/novelty products (>23%), an ~8% price increase taken due to past war-related turmoil, and in-house execution of job work (printing, coloring, internal polishing) saving ~1.5%.
- Employee cost increased as a percentage of sales (to ~8.75%) due to hiring talent, which management noted helped reduce rejections and improve gross margins.
- Working capital: Inventory days expected not to exceed 90 days; debtor cycle at 45-55 days; creditor cycle at 55-65 days.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current capacity: 72 lakh units/year.
- Upcoming capacity: 31 lakh units via IPO funds going live in Q4 FY27, taking total capacity to 103 lakh units/year.
- Peak revenue potential from 103 lakh units: ~Rs 23-24 crore per month (Rs 180-190 crore from 62 lakh units base).
- Additional peak capacity from current land (with minor modifications): 30-40% more, requiring an estimated Rs 20-25 crore in capex when needed.
- New lines take about 4-5 months to install and reach 90% efficiency.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term borrowings of ~Rs 22.7 crore fully repaid.
- Debt-equity ratio stands low at 0.04.
- No further funding required for the targeted 50% growth; short-term borrowings can be utilized for minor requirements if needed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cost of acquiring clients for own brand increases marketing and branding expenses, capping net EBITDA margin expansion despite operating leverage.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Piyush Bangar (Vijit Global Securities) asked about gross margin drivers and revenue potential.** Management explained the drivers were lower outsourcing (6.5%), higher value-added product mix (>23%), an ~8% price hike, and in-house job work, and stated the 103 lakh capacity has a revenue potential of Rs 23-24 crore/month.
2. **Anil Shenoy (Individual Investor) asked about market share goals and peak capacity on current land.** Management stated a long-term goal of 10% market share (from 2%) by FY31, and noted current land can support 30-40% more capacity with Rs 20-25 crore capex.
3. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) asked about revenue vs capacity growth targets and segment mix.** Management guided to 40% capacity growth and >45-50% revenue growth for FY27, noting ODM/OEM is 54-55% and aiming for 65% own brand share in 3-4 years.
4. **Aadi Menon (Abakkus Asset Management) asked about segment-wise EBITDA margins.** Management stated H1 EBITDA margins were ~18% for OEM, ~20% for own brand distributor network, and ~21% for online business.
5. **Vipul Shah (Individual Investor) asked about employee cost increase and seasonality.** Management explained employee costs rose due to hiring quality talent to reduce rejections, and noted no major seasonality as H2 brings festive and winter demand.
6. **Abhishek (Individual Investor) asked about wallet share with ODM clients.** Management clarified they do not provide design monopolies, offering proprietary ODM designs where clients take 15% to 100% of supplies, capped voluntarily at 10-11% concentration per client.

### One-line takeaway
Maharaja & Speedex delivered a strong post-listing H1 with 36% revenue growth, 52% EBITDA growth, debt eradication, and aggressive capacity expansion plans backed by favorable BIS and anti-dumping tailwinds.
