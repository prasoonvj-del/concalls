# Sanghvi Movers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reaffirmed full-year guidance and highlighted strong order book growth despite seasonal monsoon impacts and logistical delays in the Middle East.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (Q2):** Rs 277 crore (up 32% YoY).
*   **Revenue (H1):** Rs 657 crore (up 36% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 106 crore (up 20% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q2):** 36%.
*   **PAT (Q2):** Rs 40 crore (vs Rs 36 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 1,582 crore (as of Oct 23, 2026; up 26% from July 24, 2026).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 391 crore (Gross debt Rs 805 crore less treasury position of Rs 414 crore).
*   **Capex (H1):** Rs 182 crore capitalized.
*   **Crane Utilization (India & Botswana):** 78% (H1).
*   **Crane Utilization (GCC):** 86% (H1).

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Mix:** H1 revenue comprised 62% crane rental, 36% renewable E&C, and 2% project EPC. Management revised full-year expectations to a 60:40 mix (previously 2/3 and 1/3).
*   **Renewable E&C:** Segment generated Rs 365 crore in H1 with ~14% EBITDA margin and 63% ROCE (TTM).
*   **Working Capital:** Group DSO improved to 81 days (GCC DSO reduced from 201 days in Q1 to 165 days in Q2).
*   **Project EPC:** Management is winding up this segment and expects to reverse negative EBITDA in Q3/Q4.
*   **Operational Metrics:** Customer satisfaction score rose to 9.18.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Guidance:** Reaffirmed total income of Rs 1,400 crore – Rs 1,500 crore and EBITDA of Rs 525 crore – Rs 575 crore.
*   **Order Book Execution:** Management noted an 8-10% potential spillover of the current order book into the next financial year due to project execution uncertainties (ROW, grid connectivity, site availability).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **India:** Strong investment-led growth; infrastructure, steel, cement, refining, and nuclear power sectors driving demand.
*   **Renewables:** Wind sector added 6 GW in FY26; management sees a strong pipeline with 29 GW under construction.
*   **Middle East:** High demand for cranes driven by defense, oil/gas, and global commitments (FIFA World Cup, World Expo).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Q2 crane rental margins impacted by one-time leadership incentives, statutory PF law changes, and insurance costs.
*   **Middle East Margins:** GCC EBITDA margin at 24%; management expects improvement as the business scales and start-up costs normalize.
*   **E&C Contracts:** Fixed-price contracts; no pass-through for cost escalations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Plan:** FY27 ceiling revised to Rs 583 crore; Rs 401 crore pending for H2.
*   **Middle East Strategy:** Shipping constraints and port congestion caused delays in crane delivery; a portion of KSA capex re-phased to FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Gross debt-to-equity ratio is 0.58x (within FY27 ceiling of 0.72x).
*   **Funding:** No working capital limits utilized in H1; expansion funded via internal accruals and debt. Management stated no plans to raise equity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Logistics:** Shipping constraints and port congestion in the Middle East affecting crane deployment timing.
*   **Execution:** Monsoon-led moderation in domestic activity and potential project delays (ROW, PPA offtake).
*   **Geopolitics:** West Asia conflict impacting logistics and shipping costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did GCC margins remain at 24% despite 86% utilization?** Management explained this reflects start-up costs incurred during the first 6-8 months; they expect margins to pull up by 1-2 percentage points as operations scale.
*   **Q: Is the Middle East business at risk due to geopolitical issues?** Management stated the business is EBITDA positive, demand is high, and assets are mobile/relocatable if necessary.
*   **Q: Why is the order book execution uncertain?** Management clarified that E&C projects face external dependencies (grid, land, OEM supply) leading to potential 8-10% spillover.
*   **Q: Will you raise equity for expansion?** Management explicitly stated they are not looking to raise equity at this time.
*   **Q: How is the crane yield calculated?** Monthly rental yield = Monthly hiring rental / Historical cost of acquisition.

### One-line takeaway
Sanghvi Movers delivered a strong H1 with 36% revenue growth and reaffirmed full-year guidance, leveraging a robust order book and asset-light renewable E&C growth despite logistical headwinds in the Middle East.
