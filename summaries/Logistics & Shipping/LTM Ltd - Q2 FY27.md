# LTM Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Broad-based growth, strong traction in AI capabilities, and sequential margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: USD 1.235 billion (1.1% QoQ, 5.1% YoY in constant currency; USD growth of 1.0% QoQ, 4.7% YoY)
- Revenue in INR: Rs 11,808 crore (1.7% QoQ, 13.6% YoY)
- EBIT margin: 15.6% (10 bps QoQ improvement)
- PAT margin: 11.7% (down from 12.7% in Q1 due to a previous one-time gain)
- Basic EPS: Rs 47.2 (compared to Rs 49.5 in Q1 FY2027)
- Order inflow (TCV): USD 1.3 billion
- AI revenue: 13.4% of overall revenue (up 40% YoY; cumulative AI including enterprise AI grew 47% YoY)
- Headcount: 88,835 (net addition of 949 employees QoQ; fresher additions of 1,743)
- Trailing 12-month attrition: 13.3%
- DSO: 84 days (improved by 1 day QoQ)
- Cash and investment balances: USD 1.6 billion or Rs 15,300 crore
- Interim dividend: Rs 25 per equity share (13.6% increase YoY)

### Highlights
- AI revenue reached 13.4% of total revenue, driven by the BlueVerse™ ecosystem (including BlueVerse™ OneToken, BlueVerse™ RightLogic, and BlueVerse™ SovereignSphere™ Models).
- Added a financial services client to the USD 100 million-plus client category.
- Expanded partnerships with Anthropic (embedding Claude and Claude Code), Glean, Cognition, IBM, Red Hat, and Chainguard.
- Opened a new Global Delivery and AI Competency Center in Kolkata and expanded BlueVerse™ Studio footprint in Hyderabad.
- Randstad Digital business acquisition approvals in Europe and Australia remain on track for closure in Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the growth trajectory to continue to strengthen, with H2 FY2027 performance expected to be better than H1.
- Management aims for overall FY2027 growth to come as close as possible to FY2026 growth.
- Management expects EBIT margins for the full year to be maintained at levels similar to the previous year.
- Management anticipates Randstad Digital acquisition closures and realignments to position the company well for FY2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Financial services grew 3.1% QoQ and 0.4% YoY.
- Tech and services grew 7% YoY, but declined 4% QoQ due to seasonal renewal cycles and Saudi business impact.
- Consumer segment grew 0.3% QoQ and 8.6% YoY; travel and hospitality were temporarily impacted by fuel prices.
- Production segment grew 3.7% QoQ and 7.4% YoY across manufacturing, energy, and utilities.
- Americas grew 1.5% QoQ, Europe grew 1% QoQ, and Rest of the World declined 1.4% QoQ due to Middle East business impacts in Saudi Arabia.
- Client spend is shifting toward AI deployment cycles (Enterprise AI, Business AI, Industrial AI, and Marketing/Creative AI), leading to shorter-duration, faster-realization deals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Wage hikes are fully absorbed; organic margins held and expected to improve further.
- Forex losses below EBIT may continue for the next couple of quarters based on hedging policies and rupee movement.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Utilization excluding trainees remained flat at 86.4% QoQ.
- Total headcount reached 88,835 with 1,743 fresher additions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow to PAT ratio declined to 39% (from 79% in Q1) due to advance payments, tax outflows, and funding towards a one-time gratuity provision under new labor laws.
- Free cash flow to PAT ratio stood at 24% (down from 63% in Q1).
- Cash flow hedges stood at USD 4.49 billion; balance sheet hedges at USD 334 million.
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) was 28.5% (versus 29.8% in Q1).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Middle East conflict/war impacted revenue in the Saudi Arabia business under the Rest of the World and Tech segments.
- Hardware pricing and delivery delays impacted the CBDT deal ramp-up (though improving compared to Q1).
- Traditional large deals face intense pricing pressures and unit price declines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ravi Menon (Axis Capital) on TCV vs. Revenue:** Asked if lower TCV reflects shorter-duration, T&M AI deals. Management confirmed they are intentionally pushing shorter-tenure, AI-led deals for faster revenue acceleration and strategic positioning, though traditional large deals will still occur intermittently.
2. **Vibhor Singhal (Equities) on growth acceleration to double digits:** Asked when the company can return to high-single-digit or double-digit growth. Management stated H2 will be better than H1, aiming for FY27 growth close to FY26, supported by BFSI recovery and the upcoming Randstad Digital acquisition for FY28.
3. **Rishi Jhunjhunwala (Capital) on shareholder payout/cash allocation:** Highlighted that peers pay out 80-100% while LTM sits on over Rs 15,000 crore cash with a ~45% payout. Management responded that cash is maintained to balance reinvestments in AI, GCC strategies, and selective M&A while adhering to disciplined group frameworks for long-term value creation.
4. **Girish Pai (Equities) on pricing and deflation:** Asked about pricing weakness and the deflation cycle. Management noted traditional deals face margin/pricing headwinds, but AI-native deals command a premium with separate AI rate cards, and LTM has largely passed through the major productivity deflation cycle in its top accounts.

### One-line takeaway
LTM reported steady Q2 FY27 performance with 5.1% YoY constant currency revenue growth and 15.6% EBIT margins, aggressively pivoting towards short-tenure AI-led deals despite regional headwinds in the Middle East.
