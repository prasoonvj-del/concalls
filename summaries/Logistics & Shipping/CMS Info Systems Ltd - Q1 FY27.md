# CMS Info Systems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong services revenue and margin expansion, but impacted by an industry-wide currency supply squeeze and lower EBIT.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** Rs 635 crore
- **Services Revenue:** Rs 625 crore, up 9.3% YoY and 2.6% QoQ (impacted by Rs 25 crore due to currency supply squeeze: Rs 18 crore in BLA, Rs 7 crore in Cash Logistics)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 173 crore, up 8.9% YoY and 6.8% QoQ
- **EBITDA Margin:** 27.2%, expanding 170 bps QoQ
- **PAT:** Rs 84 crore, down 10.6% YoY and up 5.8% QoQ
- **PAT Margin:** 13.2% (down 170 bps YoY, up 70 bps QoQ)
- **EBIT (Managed Services):** Rs 32 crore at 10.3% margin (vs 14.1% YoY)
- **Order Wins (Q1):** Rs 500 crore (including HDFC Bank mandate of 6,000 ATMs over 5 years at TCV of Rs 400 crore)
- **Market Share:** 60% in ATM cash management, 38% in retail solutions
- **Tech & Payments Share:** 18% of services revenue in Q1
- **Share Buyback:** Completed June 19, 2026, for 49.39 lakh shares at Rs 340 per share, totaling Rs 168 crore
- **Cash on Books:** Approximately Rs 400 crore

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever services revenue despite currency supply bottlenecks.
- Expanded EBITDA margins by 170 bps QoQ driven by route/process automation, pricing focus, and higher Tech & Payments mix.
- FSS integration contributed ~Rs 20 crore accrual in Q1; synergy benefits expected to flow in H2.
- Securens acquisition turnaround allowed recognition of deferred tax asset, lowering tax rate to 20.3%.
- Will Poole (Co-founder and lead at Capria Ventures, ex-Microsoft) joined the Board.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full Year Services Revenue Goal:** Trimmed to Rs 2,650 crore - Rs 2,750 crore (from Rs 2,700 crore - Rs 2,800 crore).
- **Total Revenue Goal:** Estimated at Rs 2,750 crore - Rs 2,850 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Raised to about 27% (from 25% - 26% guided in May).
- **Platform Growth Guidance:** ATM Management and Retail/Currency logistics expected to grow 11% to 14%; Tech & Payments to grow 35% to 40%; combined services revenue growth expected in 15% to 19% range.
- **Capex Guidance:** FY27 capex estimated significantly lower at Rs 100 crore - Rs 125 crore (compared to peak capex of Rs 350 crore in FY26).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry faced worst currency supply squeeze in a decade; banks supplied only ~70% of indented currency daily in Q1 (recovering to ~80% in Q2).
- Currency in circulation is up 12% YoY; retail same-store volumes held constant (demand is intact, constraint is supply/distribution).
- IBA (Indian Banks' Association) constituted a committee to review the situation and evaluate repricing legacy PSE contracts.
- Private sector bank and retail customer pricing discussions progressing well to absorb steep minimum wage hikes (6% to 60% across states) and 8% fuel cost inflation.
- Expecting a product refresh cycle from mid-sized and large banks in FY27; won 2 ATM recycler mandates in Q1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Carrying higher-than-normal wage and fuel inflation.
- Unit costs structurally lowered via automation and technology investments in route and process automation.
- Depreciation is higher due to H2 FY26 capex for HAWKAI/ALGO products and incremental FSS depreciation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY26 capex was Rs 350 crore (vs historical average of Rs 200 crore) for large project wins and HAWKAI/ALGO product development.
- FY27 capex expected to normalize to Rs 100 crore - Rs 125 crore range.
- More than 50% of overall ATM touch points are now from end-to-end integrated ATM contracts.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained track record of converting 65% to 70% of EBITDA into operating cash flows.
- Will prune persistently underperforming ATMs out of the BLA estate if currency supply and transactions do not improve.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe currency supply squeeze by banks impacted Q1 revenue by Rs 25 crore (Rs 18 crore in BLA, Rs 7 crore in cash logistics) and created operating deleverage.
- Steep state-level minimum wage increases (6% to 60%) and 8% fuel inflation impacting cost base.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Praveen Kumar (Aequitas Capital Advisors) on currency crunch transient vs structural:** Management stated supply has improved from 70% to 80%-85%, and CIC is up 12% YoY, indicating it is a supply/distribution constraint rather than a demand issue.
2. **Umang Shah (Banyan Tree Advisors) on FSS revenue and UPI MDR impact:** Management noted ~Rs 20 crore accrual from FSS in Q1. On potential UPI MDR, management stated it is too early to anticipate details, but cash cost to merchants ranges between 10 to 20 bps.
3. **Krushi Parekh (BugleRock) on HAWKAI TAM sizing:** Management explained BFSI TAM is based on ~400,000 ATMs/branches and expected outsourcing shift over 5-7 years; non-BFSI TAM covers familiar use cases like gold loan branches, dark stores, and warehouses.
4. **Umang Shah on BLA business outlook:** Management reiterated they will not invest incremental capital in transaction ATM (BLA) business and will direct outsized investments towards Tech & Payments.
5. **Dhruv (Individual Investor) on capital allocation and stock multiple:** Management responded that buybacks/dividends are mechanisms to return unneeded capital, and the focus remains on running a quality business to let fundamentals reflect in the stock price over time.

### One-line takeaway
CMS Info Systems reported a resilient Q1 with record services revenue and expanded EBITDA margins of 27.2%, despite a Rs 25 crore revenue impact from an industry-wide currency supply squeeze, prompting a trimmed revenue outlook but raised margin guidance for FY27.
