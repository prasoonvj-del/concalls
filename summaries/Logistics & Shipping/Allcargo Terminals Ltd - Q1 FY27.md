# Allcargo Terminals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stable operational performance, container volume growth of 7% YoY, and continued profitability improvements supported by operational efficiencies and technology.

### Key numbers
- **Container Volumes:** 1,76,499 TEUs (up 7.2% YoY)
- **Revenue:** Rs 214 crore (vs Rs 187 crore in Q1 FY26, Rs 208 crore in Q4 FY26)
- **EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 47 crore (vs Rs 35 crore in Q1 FY26, Rs 44 crore in Q4 FY26)
- **EBITDA per TEU:** Rs 2,898 for Q1 FY27 (erroneously mentioned as Rs 2,690 in the call)
- **PAT:** Rs 6 crore (vs Rs 9 crore in Q1 FY26 and Q4 FY26)
- **LCL Business Contribution:** 10% to 12% of overall revenue
- **Nepal Joint Venture Contribution:** 2% to 3% of overall revenue and profitability
- **Capacity Utilisation:** Hovering at 80% to 85%
- **Capacity Expansion Target (Vision 2030):** 1.3 million TEUs (ANNUAL handling capacity currently at ~1.03 million TEUs)
- **Total Capex Plan (Plan 2030):** ~Rs 400 crore
- **FY27 Capex Guidance:** ~Rs 100 crore (including ~Rs 20 crore for Speedy JNPA and substantial amount for Farukhnagar)
- **Tax Rate Guidance:** 25% going forward (concessional tax regime)

### Highlights
- Pranav Choudhary appointed as MD Designate from Adani Ports, taking over from Suresh Kumar who superannuates on September 1, 2026.
- Rolled out smart yard management system across locations with a pilot phase at JNPT.
- Speedy JNPT contract successfully renewed for 10 years, with tendering completed for facility upgrades (costing ~Rs 40 crores shared 50:50 with JNPA) adding ~60,000 TEUs of annual handling capacity; work starts post-monsoon and finishes by Jan-Feb 2027.
- Farukhnagar Private Freight Terminal (PFT) construction on track for completion by March 2027 (PFT standalone initially, full ICD operational by Q3 FY28).
- Equity raised of Rs 120 crore (Rs 25 crore called, ~Rs 90 crore yet to be called) to fund the Rs 400 crore capex plan alongside internal accruals and debt.

### Guidance & outlook
- **EBITDA per TEU Guidance:** Management expects to maintain around the Rs 2,400 to Rs 2,500 level (targeting Rs 2,750 including Farukhnagar in the 3-year plan).
- **Tax Rate Guidance:** Expected to remain at 25% due to transition to the concessional tax regime.
- **Guidance:** Maintained

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's trade and logistics ecosystem remains resilient supported by steady economic activity and infrastructure.
- DFCC is expected to improve port efficiencies, giving customers speed and choice, with positive spillover effects for CFSs and ICDs located along the corridor.
- Import volumes dominate CFS operations (75% to 80% of total volume handled).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee costs rose due to ~10% annual increments and a ~Rs 1 crore impact from ESOP issuance to a KMP.
- Margin expansion driven 50% by yield management and 50% by operational efficiencies (RST modernization, yard management systems, pan-India supplier negotiations).
- Input cost inflation (fuel, transport contracts) successfully absorbed through commercial rate revisions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Plan 2030 total capex estimated at ~Rs 400 crore, funded via Rs 200 crore from existing balance and future cash flows, ~Rs 90 crore from uncalled equity, and Rs 100 crore to Rs 150 crore from debt.
- Farukhnagar PFT commissioning slated for Feb/March 2027; ICD portion slated for Q3 FY28 (October-December 2027).
- Advanced negotiations ongoing to identify a facility closer to Kattupalli port cluster in Chennai.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Existing cash balance/accruals: ~Rs 50 crore.
- Planned debt requirement: Rs 100 crore to Rs 150 crore (tied up with banks).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External macroeconomic uncertainties and geopolitical developments affecting trade flows.
- Expiration of Adani landlord licenses for Mundra facilities by 2030-2031 (contingency plans under evaluation).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raj Doshi (Individual Investor) asked about the detailed funding mix and quantum for the Rs 400 crore capex plan.** Management clarified that Rs 200 crore will come from existing cash and accruals, Rs 90 crore from uncalled equity, and Rs 100-150 crore through tied-up debt. *(Directly answered)*
- **Devraj (Individual Investor) inquired about synergies and business flows from group company Allcargo Global.** Management explained that Allcargo Terminals handles LCL containers for Allcargo Logistics across JNPA, Mundra, and Chennai, contributing 10-12% of revenue at market rates, alongside cross-selling opportunities across the group. *(Directly answered)*
- **Vikram (Kotak Capital) questioned the sustainability of the 22%+ EBITDA margins achieved in Q1.** Management stated that while yield management has limits in a competitive market, process efficiencies, cargo mix optimization, and scale will help sustain EBITDA/TEU around Rs 2,400–2,500. *(Directly answered)*
- **Janvi Sharma (Individual Investor) asked about a formal dividend policy given the cash-rich status.** Management stated that given the growth phase and ongoing Rs 400 crore capex cycle, dividend payouts are currently on hold in favor of capital redeployment. *(Directly answered)*
- **Utsav B. (Individual Investor) asked about the adoption rate and functional scope of the proprietary 'myCFS' app.** Management highlighted that ~70-80% of import documentation and clearing workflows are automated, seeing a 70-80% monthly adoption rate amongst major CHAs. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Allcargo Terminals delivered stable Q1 FY27 performance with 7% volume growth and improved profitability, while progressing multi-year expansion projects like Farukhnagar and Speedy JNPT under its Vision 2030 roadmap.
