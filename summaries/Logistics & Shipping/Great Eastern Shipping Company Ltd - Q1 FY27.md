# Great Eastern Shipping Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported their most profitable quarter ever, driven by significant freight rate strength resulting from geopolitical disruptions.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Profit:** Rs 1,309 crore.
*   **Standalone Profit:** Rs 1,157 crore.
*   **Consolidated EPS:** Rs 91-92/share.
*   **NAV:** Standalone Rs 1,512/share; Consolidated < Rs 1,900/share.
*   **Interim Dividend:** Rs 14.40/share.
*   **Cash Position:** $700 million total cash; $600 million net of debt.

### Highlights
*   Achieved highest-ever quarterly profit and dividend.
*   Fleet: 40 ships (avg age 14.5 years), 19 offshore vessels, 4 rigs.
*   Transactions: Sold 2 MR tankers (replaced with 1), bought 1 Kamsarmax dry bulk carrier. In July, sold Jag Lokesh (LR2) and replaced with Jag Lakshya (LR2, 6 years younger, eco-ship).
*   Strategy: Predominantly spot market (25-26% on time charter).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management does not provide earnings guidance or market forecasts.
*   Strategy remains "replace, not expand" until asset prices align with long-term return thresholds.
*   Management expects to continue incremental fleet modernization (1-2 ships) rather than aggressive capacity expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strait of Hormuz disruptions caused significant tanker market strength; MR spot earnings reached ~$50,000/day.
*   Bulk carrier market strong due to grain trade and coal demand (replacing LNG).
*   LPG market strong; freight rates >$100,000/day.
*   Order books: Crude tankers (27%), VLGCs (35%), Product tankers (20-21%), Bulk carriers (14%).
*   Offshore: Jack-up rig utilization stable; rigs suspended by Saudi Aramco are returning to work.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Newer ships (built post-2013) offer fuel savings of 20-25% compared to pre-2013 vessels, translating to $1,000-$2,500/day in earnings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Invested Rs 1,200 crore last year; Rs 300 crore in Q1 FY27; Rs 250-300 crore in July.
*   No specific annual capex budget; decisions are ship-specific based on age and tradability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Maintained net cash position for >3 years.
*   Debt repayment schedule continues through Nov 2028.
*   Treasury: No U.S. treasuries; cash held in banks (USD) and debt funds/bank deposits (INR).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Uncertainty regarding the duration of geopolitical disruptions (Strait of Hormuz, Russia-Ukraine war).
*   Rising order books (especially crude tankers) increase the risk of market softening due to oversupply.
*   Current high asset prices reduce the margin of safety for new investments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Buyback:* Management stated they have no specific policy or target multiple for buybacks; it is treated as a capital allocation decision alongside other options. Tax leakage for buybacks is no longer a concern.
*   *Incremental Cash Deployment:* Analyst suggested using incremental cash to buy 1-2 ships/quarter. Management acknowledged the merit but emphasized caution to avoid buying at cycle peaks.
*   *Offshore/Samudra Manthan:* Management noted it is too soon to plan capex based on government announcements; waiting for on-ground demand.
*   *Strait of Hormuz:* Management confirmed they have no ships currently transiting the Strait of Hormuz.

### One-line takeaway
Record profitability driven by geopolitical-led freight rate spikes, with management maintaining a disciplined "replace, not expand" capital allocation strategy amidst high asset prices.
