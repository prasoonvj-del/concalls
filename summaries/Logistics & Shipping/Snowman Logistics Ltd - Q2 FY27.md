# Snowman Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management highlighted good sequential volume recovery and progress on strategic expansions, but noted ongoing macroeconomic uncertainties and lower margins in the 5PL segment.

### Key numbers
- **Rail Volumes (GDL):** Targeted at a bare minimum of 100,000 TEUs per quarter for the rest of the year.
- **Blended Realization (GDL):** Improved by ~5% YoY and ~7% QoQ.
- **Snowman Pallet Volume Growth:** ~10% increase YoY in terms of pallets stored.
- **Snowman Capacity Utilization:** Maintained around 85% to 86% throughout the year.
- **Snowman 5PL Segment Growth:** Grew by 1% to 2% YoY.
- **Snowman EBITDA:** On track to cross Rs 100 crore for the year.
- **Snowman Capacity Additions:** Close to 23,000 pallets under construction in the next few months; another ~20,000 pallets under discussion.
- **EXIM Trade Imbalance:** 56% import and 44% export.

### Highlights
- Gateway Distriparks (GDL) started its first double stack train on the Western DFC on June 20th.
- Ankleshwar MMLP has received all EXIM permissions in place; EXIM operations to commence in Q3.
- Jaipur ICD court case: Final arguments have concluded and the order is reserved; management expects a favorable ruling to resume construction (taking up to 18 months to operationalize).
- Indore ICD construction is in full swing (civil and rail works started), targeting operationalization by December 2027.
- Snowman is expanding capacity via both Build-to-Suit (BTS) and owned models, focusing on cold temperature-controlled facilities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains double-digit growth guidance for next year for GDL, driven by a full year of Ankleshwar EXIM operations and Western DFC benefits.
- Snowman is on track to cross Rs 100 crore EBITDA for the year.
- Indore ICD targeted to be operational by December 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Macroeconomic situation remains uncertain with fluctuating monthly trends; geopolitical conflicts and tariff situations persist.
- Western DFC opening and timetable trains are improving transit reliability, helping rail compete effectively with road transport.
- EXIM imbalance remains stable at 56% import and 44% export.
- Snowman's 5PL segment saw sluggish growth due to one customer underperforming, but management expects a recovery in Q3 and Q4.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- GDL passed on fuel price hikes through realization increases, which were offset by corresponding cost increases.
- Snowman's margins have seen structural reduction over the years due to the addition of park-and-pay models (low single-digit margin), dry warehousing, and lower-margin 5PL business.
- Out-of-turn wage code changes (gratuity, PF ceiling hikes, and sharp state-level minimum wage increases like Karnataka up 70% and Haryana 35%) impacted costs, which are gradually passed on during annual contract renewals.
- Power cost increases are factored into contract renewals with anticipated annual escalations of 6% to 8%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Snowman is adding ~23,000 pallets under construction and evaluating another ~20,000 pallets. New facilities are primarily cold temperature-controlled.
- GDL's new projects target at least a 15% IRR and a 7-year payback period.
- Park-and-pay temporary asset-light warehousing capacity is adjusted based on short-term customer requirements.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Snowman continues to reinvest cash flows into long-term assets (30-40 year horizon), leading to ongoing interest and depreciation costs impacting PAT.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macro uncertainties, ongoing wars, and unclear tariff situations.
- Slower-than-anticipated shipping line adoption of JNPT port, limiting the immediate impact of the Western DFC shift.
- Sharp statutory wage hikes (minimum wages, gratuity, PF) exerting pressure before contract renewals catch up.
- Continued legal delay regarding the Jaipur ICD (awaiting reserved court order).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prashant Kale (Individual Investor) on Snowman's low PAT despite operations:** Management explained that continuous reinvestment in long-term cold storage assets leads to ongoing depreciation and interest expenses, prioritizing operating cash flows and long-term asset generation over near-term accounting PAT.
2. **Aditya Mongia (Kotak Securities) on Western DFC EBITDA impact:** Management stated it is difficult to predict exact EBITDA gains as it depends on competitor pricing, cost absorption, and shipping lines shifting vessel calls to JNPT.
3. **Kunal Tokas (Analyst) on Snowman's declining margin trend over 7-8 years:** Management clarified that the percentage decline is driven by the introduction of low-margin park-and-pay models, dry warehousing, and the 5PL segment, while core cold storage margins remain intact.
4. **Jojo Shaju (Tirthan Capital) on Ankleshwar MMLP volumes:** Management stated that EXIM operations are just starting with B/L points opening, and location-wise throughput is not publicly shared.
5. **Prateek Giri (Shubh Labh Research) on JNPT rail evacuation and tariff interventions:** Management noted that while rail infrastructure is ready for double-stacking, volume growth requires time for shipping lines to adjust vessel routes and potential railway tariff rationalization.

### One-line takeaway
Snowman and GDL are expanding infrastructure and leveraging the Western DFC and new MMLPs for long-term growth, though near-term profitability is tempered by statutory cost inflation and ramp-up phases.
