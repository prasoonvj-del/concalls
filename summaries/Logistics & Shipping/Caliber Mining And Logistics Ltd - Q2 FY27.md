# Caliber Mining And Logistics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted the best H1 and Q2 operational performance in the company's history, driven by record overburden removal and coal extraction volumes despite seasonal monsoon headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 guidance of 45%+ revenue growth and 35%+ growth in both EBITDA and PAT YoY.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted even more positive regarding coal demand, with customers pushing for volumes beyond contracted limits due to rising power demand.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Management highlighted the successful implementation of 100% diesel pass-through (mitigating Iran war-related fuel price risks) and the launch of AI initiatives (Pulse 1 and Pulse 2). The previous quarter's concern regarding timing mismatches on diesel escalation disappeared as full pass-through clearance was secured.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The expected Q2 OB removal volume of ~34 million cubic meters was largely met with an actual 33 million cubic meters. Capex of Rs 584.88 crore was deployed in H1 to mobilize three new contracts (Dhoptala II, Dudhichua II, and Jayant II).

### Highlights
- Delivered the highest-ever half-yearly volume growth and Q2 performance in the company's history despite monsoon disruptions.
- Secured 100% diesel cost pass-through clearance from customers, eliminating fuel price volatility risks.
- CRISIL upgraded the company's long-term rating to A minus with a positive outlook.
- Order book covers nine sites with an average tenure of ~2.5 years; participated in net new tenders worth over Rs 11,000 crore.
- Implemented 'Pulse 1' for centralized live vehicle breakdown management and launched 'Pulse 2' (beta version) for MIS and HR integration.
- Continued execution of three new contracts: Dhoptala II, Dudhichua II, and Jayant II.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated FY27 guidance: 45%+ revenue growth and 35%+ growth in both EBITDA and PAT YoY.
- Management guided for ~60 million cubic meters of OB removal in each of Q3 and Q4 FY27.
- Management expects H2 performance to be stronger, historically running at ~1.5x of H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Coal demand continues to rise amid increased domestic power generation, with power generation projected to grow at 6.8% annually through FY30.
- Customers are urging the company to increase production beyond contracted volumes (targeting 105%-110%+ volume execution in select contracts).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Depreciation and finance costs are largely fixed and rose due to fleet expansion.
- 100% of consumed diesel is now reimbursed by customers on a monthly/15-day billing cycle, insulating margins from crude price fluctuations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total H1 capex stood at Rs 584.88 crore to mobilize three new contracts and meet higher customer volume demands.
- Fleet expanded to 2,226 units.
- Management stated that core capex for existing orders is largely complete, with future capex dependent on new contract wins or further customer volume escalations beyond 100%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Post-IPO net worth increased to Rs 1148.42 crore.
- Net debt-to-equity improved to 0.95x from 1.48x as of March 31, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Seasonal monsoon impact during Q2 causes temporary disruptions to haul roads and lowers operating days, though Q2 FY27 impact was lower than the previous year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pawan Kumar asked about the drop in EBITDA margin and H2 margin outlook.** Management explained that Q2 margins are seasonally impacted by monsoons, and H2 margins will expand due to uninterrupted operations and full pipeline execution of newly won orders.
2. **Dhananjai Bagrodia asked how crude price movements impact margins under the new pass-through.** Management clarified that with a 100% pass-through mechanism in place, crude price fluctuations do not impact margins.
3. **Rahil Bohra asked about the status of the INR 11,000 crore tender pipeline and MDO opportunities.** Management stated they are serious bidders focusing on operational efficiency to win contracts, and are also exploring MDO opportunities with private players.
4. **Rahil Bohra asked about the delay/extension at the Jayant Open Cast Mine.** Management clarified that they received an official extension order from Jayant with enhanced quantities due to available workspace and the customer's need for uninterrupted coal.
5. **Pawan Kumar asked about the quantification and status of AI implementation.** Management explained that 'Pulse 1' (live vehicle breakdown tracking) and 'Pulse 2' (MIS/HR beta version) have been implemented to drive fleet and operational efficiencies.

### One-line takeaway
Caliber Mining and Logistics posted record H1 volumes and revenue, backed by a strong post-IPO balance sheet, secured 100% diesel pass-through, and guided for a strong H2 ramp-up.
