# Radiant Cash Management Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Negative - Performance was described as "underwhelming" by management, with profitability collapsing due to sharp manpower cost increases, despite management's confidence in a recovery from Q4.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (management expects recovery from Q4 onwards).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "Mixed" to "Negative" regarding current profitability; demand remains muted but stable.
- **New topics:** Management highlighted the deployment of AI tools for cost optimization (route management, banking, recon, billing).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Management promised price hike resolutions in Q2; this was delivered (12-15% hikes secured).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1.12 billion (+4.4% YoY).
- **Standalone Revenue Growth:** 7.52% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 7.62% (vs 15.2% in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 6.8% (vs 13.1% in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT Margin:** 3.5%.
- **ROCE:** 5.4%.
- **Cash Handled:** Rs 0.41 trillion (flat YoY).
- **Gross Cash Losses:** Rs 1 million (0.0002% of total cash handled).
- **Radiant Valuable Logistics (RVL) Revenue:** Rs 30 million (+35% QoQ).
- **Direct Client Revenue:** 19.2% of standalone revenue.
- **Debtors:** 80 days.
- **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 1.5 billion.

### Highlights
- Management secured price revisions of 12% to 15% from the majority of clients; impact to be reflected from Q4 onwards.
- Radiant Acemoney achieved near EBITDA breakeven.
- RVL revenue grew 35% QoQ; management expects profitability by the end of FY27.
- *Record Check:* The company previously struggled with margin compression; this quarter confirms the "worst is behind us" narrative, though the margin drop was severe compared to historical levels.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects "healthy improvement" in profitability from Q4 onwards, driven by price hikes, cost reduction, and subsidiary turnarounds.
- Management aspires to achieve mid-teens revenue growth in the long term.
- Management expects RVL to achieve positive EBITDA in the current financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Cash volumes handled were flat YoY (Rs 0.41 trillion).
- Industry-wide issue: Manpower cost increases (minimum wages, armed guard shortage) led to a unanimous industry push for price hikes.
- Management noted that retail cash management penetration is low (~1%); focus is on organized retail, NBFCs, and hospitals to drive growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Manpower costs (70% of total costs) spiked due to minimum wage revisions and armed guard shortages in southern states.
- No pass-through mechanism exists for fuel costs.
- Management is deploying AI tools for route management, banking, and billing to optimize costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major capex expected; most vans are leased.
- Acemoney: 20,000 BC network; 21,000 sound boxes deployed in Q2.
- Target: 50,000+ sound boxes in FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt: Nil.
- Cash & equivalents: Rs 1.5 billion.
- Debtors: 80 days (management noted this was a one-time spike, collected in early October).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Manpower cost volatility (specifically armed guard shortage).
- Profitability of subsidiaries (RVL breakeven delayed).
- Execution risk in scaling direct client business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On margin divergence from peers:** Management attributed the collapse to uniform manpower cost spikes and stated they lack visibility into competitor segment-level costs.
- **On share buyback:** Management acknowledged the suggestion due to the net cash position but noted diverse shareholder opinion; they will "have a look at it."
- **On wealth destruction since IPO:** Management cited the confluence of margin pressure and delayed subsidiary breakeven as reasons, reiterating confidence in recovery.
- **On levers for margin recovery:** Management cited cost reduction via AI, subsidiary turnaround, and direct client growth as key levers beyond price hikes.
- **On election impact:** Management stated there is no material impact on cash movement during election periods.

### One-line takeaway
Radiant reported a weak quarter with margins halved due to manpower cost spikes, but management expects a recovery from Q4 onwards following secured 12-15% price hikes and subsidiary turnarounds.
