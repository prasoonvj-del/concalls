# Blue Dart Express Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-08 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27 (ended June 30, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management noted a good quarter with positive indications and numbers moving in the right direction, supported by disciplined execution and operational resilience.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR 1,658 crore (vs INR 1,442 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Profit after tax (PAT): INR 87 crore
- Total tonnage: 364,430 tonnes (up 7% YoY)
- Number of shipments: 96.15 million (up 2% YoY)
- Air volume growth: ~2.6% YoY; Ground volume growth: ~9% YoY
- E-commerce revenue growth: >10% YoY; Ground B2B revenue growth: ~14% YoY
- Revenue mix (B2B vs B2C): ~70% B2B and ~30% B2C
- Revenue mix (Air vs Ground): ~60% Air and ~40% Ground
- Weight mix (Air vs Ground): ~25% Air and ~75% Ground
- BFSI segment exposure: 10% to 15% of total revenue (documents and cards/couriers combined approx. 25% to 30%)
- Freighter pallet utilization: 85% to 90%
- Belly cargo proportion of overall load: Typically 30% to 40% over a month

### Highlights
- Revenue and profitability improved due to disciplined execution, operational resilience, and yield-improvement actions (GPI exercise and pricing corrections on loss-making lanes/customers).
- Fuel surcharges were adjusted upwards (Brent oil prices rising from mid-March, local diesel prices rising in mid-May) to neutralize fuel cost inflation.
- E-commerce surface segment experienced the highest rate of growth within the portfolio.
- Hub consolidation and expansion initiatives are underway in North, East (medium-sized hubs), and future plans for South (Bangalore, Chennai) and Mumbai.

### Guidance & outlook
- Annualized standalone capex is guided to remain in the range of INR 100 crore to INR 150 crore, primarily for replacement, network expansion, and ongoing organic growth (representing roughly 20% to 25% on top of depreciation).
- Management aims for stable, consistent improvement in margins and absolute returns, balancing resource flex with market demand.
- No specific forward-looking numerical guidance provided for upcoming quarters or full-year volume growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Auto industry demand shows good traction in surface B2B (growing in high teens consistently over recent years) and selective air movements, operating in a niche time-critical express segment.
- BFSI segment (credit/debit cards) is experiencing elongated cycles and is no longer a high-growth segment.
- E-commerce remains competitive with available market capacity; Blue Dart focuses on niche trade lanes, time-premium quality, and profitability rather than mass volume plays.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating costs were higher during the quarter, but neutralized via auto-adjusting fuel surcharge mechanisms for air (Brent crude) and surface (diesel).
- Margin trajectory depends on the ability to flex resources to mirror volume pickups, especially heading into the peak second half of the year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Standalone capex guided at INR 100 crore to INR 150 crore annually; aviation capex varies based on engine maintenance cycles.
- Two major hubs added in the North and medium-sized hubs in the East previously; future pipeline includes consolidating/expanding hubs in Bangalore, Chennai, and Mumbai.
- Freighter pallet utilization consistently maintained at 85% to 90% via daily network planning and quota allocations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *No specific details discussed regarding total debt or working capital.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher operating costs and fuel price volatility (diesel and aviation turbine fuel/Brent crude).
- Intense competition in the e-commerce and express logistics market keeping pricing pressure alive.
- Challenge of meeting peak second-half volume demands with optimum incremental resource utilization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Krupashankar on Volume vs. Value Growth:** Asked about the divergence between 2% shipment growth and 7% tonnage growth. Management explained it reflects yield improvements, GPI price actions, and fuel surcharge pass-throughs rather than pure volume expansion.
2. **Krupashankar on E-commerce Strategy:** Asked why Blue Dart isn't aggressively scaling e-commerce given industry consolidation. Management stated they prioritize profitability, network synergy, and time-critical premium quality over mass volume plays.
3. **Raman on Fuel Cost Pass-Through:** Inquired if diesel price hikes were fully passed on. Management clarified that auto-adjusting fuel surcharges for both air (from April based on Brent) and surface (from post-May based on diesel) neutralize cost impacts.
4. **Dhaval Shah on Auto Sector Traction:** Asked about auto sector growth and market share. Management noted surface B2B auto grows in high teens, operating in a time-critical express niche rather than bulk PTL.
5. **Achal on Freighter Utilization:** Asked about aircraft utilization levels. Management confirmed pallet utilization remains steady at 85% to 90% managed through daily dynamic flight scheduling and quota sharing.

### One-line takeaway
Blue Dart delivered a resilient quarter with revenue of INR 1,658 crore and PAT of INR 87 crore, driving profitability via disciplined yield management, targeted price corrections, and fuel surcharge pass-throughs amidst competitive headwinds.
