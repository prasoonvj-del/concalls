# Allcargo Terminals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational and financial performance with robust volume growth of 12.6% YoY and PAT growth of 84.9% YoY.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from stable/positive to highly optimistic and bullish, supported by record October volumes across JNPA, Chennai, and Kolkata.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Transshipment (ITP) volume impact on revenue and EBITDA per TEU was explicitly highlighted this quarter due to regional maritime/geopolitical issues.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Chennai facility land finalization and construction timelines remain on track (expected to apply for customs clearance by end of November 2026, construction starting Q1 next financial year); Farukhnagar PFT/ICD construction is on track.

### Key numbers
- **Container Volumes:** 1,90,100 TEUs (up 12.6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Container Volumes:** 3,66,599 TEUs (up 10% YoY)
- **Revenue:** Rs 252 crore (up 21.5% YoY, 17.4% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 466 crore (vs Rs 394 crore in H1 FY26)
- **EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 59 crore (up 45.9% YoY, 24.0% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 106 crore (vs Rs 75 crore in H1 FY26)
- **EBITDA per TEU:** Rs 3,366 (up 40.8% YoY, 16.1% QoQ); includes an impact of around Rs 400-Rs 450 per TEU from transshipment (ITP) shipments.
- **Revenue per TEU:** Rs 14,388 (normalised ex-ITP at ~Rs 13,900)
- **PAT:** Rs 21 crore (up 85.0% YoY, 228.0% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 27 crore (vs Rs 20 crore in H1 FY26)

### Highlights
- October delivered the highest monthly volumes in ATL history, driven by strong JNPA, Chennai, and Kolkata volumes.
- Transshipment volumes benefited performance due to prevailing geographical/regional issues.
- Capacity utilization runs at 90% to 95% across terminals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains constructive on medium- to long-term demand supported by India's expanding trade base and logistics infrastructure.
- Transshipment gains are expected to taper down in the next few months/short run.
- Rate increases implemented in Q2 are expected to sustain in coming quarters.
- Guidance: Maintained

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's EXIM trade demonstrated strong volume and value resilience; merchandise exports and imports maintained momentum.
- Core export momentum (engineering, pharma, electronics) and import demand (industrial demand, pre-festive manufacturing inventory, elevated crude, electronics) kept volumes elevated.
- CFS and ICD sectors reflect the broader port sector and EXIM trade growth of 10% to 12%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses included ~Rs 1 crore of onetime consultancy and related costs.
- Employee costs reflect normal run rates.
- EBITDA per TEU expansion driven by ITP shipments and rate increases/better cargo mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Chennai facility (120,000 TEU capacity): Land finalization expected by end of November, customs clearance application to follow; construction to start in Q1 of next financial year and take ~9 months. Strategically close to Kattupalli and Ennore terminals.
- Speedy JNPT facility: Renovation underway to increase capacity utilization from ~8,000 TEUs towards ~18,000 TEUs.
- Farukhnagar ICD/PFT project: On track; PFT construction completion expected by end of financial year with limited operations starting; full-fledged ICD operations targeted for Q4 of the coming financial year.
- Total internal capex pipeline and growth/M&A evaluations amount to ~Rs 400 crore, with debt raising capacity up to a 1:1 debt-equity level (~Rs 200 crore to Rs 250 crore).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is currently un-leveraged; management is open to raising debt up to a 1:1 level to fund future growth and M&A.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global trade patterns and geopolitical instability may remain dynamic in the near term, impacting freight rates and transit times.
- Transshipment volume gains are temporary and expected to taper down.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kiran Gadge (Knightstone Capital Management) asked about the revenue per TEU increase split between transshipment and rates, and sustainability.** Management stated revenue per TEU was Rs 14,388 (~Rs 13,900 ex-ITP), and EBITDA per TEU included a ~Rs 400-450 ITP impact; rate increases are here to stay, while ITP will taper as Gulf situations normalise. *(Directly answered)*
- **Devansh Mehta (Individual Investor) asked about margin sustainability and the upcoming ICD project timeline.** Management clarified that transshipment gains will taper, but operational improvements, Speedy JNPT renovation (scaling from 8,000 to 18,000 TEUs), and yield initiatives will sustain margins; Farukhnagar full ICD operations are slated for Q4 of next financial year. *(Directly answered)*
- **Chinmay Parab (Individual Investor) inquired about competitive intensity and JV/tie-up possibilities.** Management noted that competition remains stable, utilization is at 90-95%, and the company is evaluating all JVs, tie-ups, and organic/inorganic expansion options. *(Directly answered)*
- **Utsav Bahety (Individual Investor) asked about the 'myCFS' app adoption benefits.** Management stated 70% of processes are automated, improving customer stickiness and ease of doing business rather than just employee cost savings. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Allcargo Terminals posted a strong Q2 FY27 with 12.6% volume growth and an 84.9% jump in PAT, backed by high capacity utilization, temporary transshipment tailwinds, and disciplined execution of expansion projects.
