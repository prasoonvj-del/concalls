# Transport Corporation of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong double-digit top-line growth and remains confident in achieving guidance despite a flat PAT due to lower JV dividends.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** ~14.85-15% YoY.
*   **Standalone Revenue Growth:** ~10% YoY.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 8.3% for the quarter.
*   **Capex (H1):** ~300 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** ~90-100 crore (current); expected to reach 150-200 crore by FY27 end.
*   **Tax Rate:** 9.5-10%.

### Highlights
*   **Buyback:** Completed a 150 crore buyback; oversubscribed 2x, promoter holding increased to 70%.
*   **Seaways:** Took delivery of the first 500 TEU vessel (*TCI Setu*); second vessel expected in early January.
*   **Segments:** Cold chain business grew ~50% YoY; LTL share in freight is ~37%.
*   **Operational:** Fuel (bunker) prices rose from 70,000-80,000/tonne to 95,000-98,000/tonne, prompting a 10-15% increase in freight rates.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Top-line Growth:** Maintained at 10-12% for FY27.
*   **FY28 Outlook:** Management expects growth to accelerate to 12-15% as new shipping capacity comes fully online.
*   **Capex:** Guidance of 500-550 crore for FY27 maintained.
*   **Margins:** Management expects Seaways margins to remain in the 35-40% range.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Auto Sector:** Management noted a slight pullback in sales and a "wait and see" approach from consumers, though dispatches remain okay.
*   **Growth Sectors:** Strong demand in high-tech, electronics, consumption, and Quick Commerce.
*   **General:** Management observed that the market is currently waiting for increased offtake.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Pricing:** ~2/3 of revenue growth was pricing-led (due to diesel/bunker price hikes) and ~1/3 was volume-led.
*   **Depreciation:** Expected to increase to 2.6-2.7% of top line due to ongoing capex.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Shipping:** Total capacity expansion of ~17-18% expected from new vessels; target is to reach 1 lakh tonnes capacity by FY30.
*   **Warehousing:** Continued investment in warehouse equipment and handling systems.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Management plans to utilize some borrowing to fund capex and manage working capital, keeping net debt manageable at 150-200 crore by year-end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Volatility:** Management highlighted high unpredictability in shipping and bunker prices.
*   **Competition:** Potential for margin pressure in FTL (Full Truck Load) business.
*   **Macro:** Potential overcapacity in shipping and consumer confusion regarding EV adoption.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Alok Deora (Motilal Oswal):** Asked about normalized Seaways margins. Management stated 35-40% is the reasonable range and expects to maintain it.
*   **Abhinav (Aequitas):** Questioned if new ships would see peak utilization immediately. Management clarified it takes a few months to calibrate and reach full stream.
*   **Ronak Jain (Avendus):** Asked about pricing-led growth. Management confirmed ~2/3 of growth was pricing-led, reflecting the pass-through of diesel/bunker costs.
*   **Priyanka (SKP Securities):** Asked about debt peaking. Management stated net debt should be 150-200 crore by FY27 end.
*   **Pravesh:** Asked about competition in the freight division. Management noted they do not compete with pure-play express players like Delivery, focusing instead on integrated services.

### One-line takeaway
TCI is navigating a transition phase with significant capex and fleet expansion, maintaining a 10-12% growth outlook while managing cost pressures through freight rate adjustments.
