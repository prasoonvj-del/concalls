# Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-02 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong operating leverage, specialized asset execution in dredging, and robust revenue/PAT growth.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR115.41 crores, up 138% YoY
- EBITDA: INR73.41 crores, up 258% YoY
- EBITDA margin: approx. 64%
- Profit after tax (PAT): INR62.75 crores, up 466% YoY
- PAT margin: approx. 54%
- Order book: >INR1,300 crores (INR240 crores dredging, INR850 crores charter and hire, INR240 crores shipbuilding), excluding INR200 crores intercompany order book
- Bid pipeline: INR3,500+ crores (INR1,200+ crores dredging, INR1,100+ crores charter and hire, INR1,400+ crores shipbuilding)
- Shipbuilding contract (IWAI): INR62.40 crores (10 hybrid electric passenger ferries)
- Preferential issue & block deal: INR150 crores preferential issue and INR100 crores block deal in secondary market

### Highlights
- Executed two key dredging projects: capital rock dredging at JNPA (using self-propelled backhoe dredger River Pearl 47) and maintenance dredging at Pondicherry port (using TSHD River Pearl 18).
- Secured a shipbuilding contract from the Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI) for 10 hybrid electric passenger ferries under the Harit Nauka mission.
- Total ongoing project portfolio includes 10 accommodation boats, 10 work boats, 4 Cutter Suction Dredgers, 2 60-ton Bollard Pull Green Tugs, and 10 hybrid electric passenger ferries.
- Raised capital through a INR150 crores preferential issue with institutional investors (360 ONE PIPE Fund, FLC Investco LLC, Bank of India Mutual Fund) and a INR100 crores block deal with SBI Funds Management.
- Received all pending funds (INR16 crores) related to the DCI Mangrol project.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to northwards of 60% YoY (initially 30% to 40%).
- Targeting a top line of INR1,000 crores by FY29.
- Long-term revenue mix target (2-3 years): dredging 45%-50%, shipbuilding 40%-45%, chartering 5%.
- EBITDA margins guided to remain between 35% and 40% (though Q1 was higher at ~64%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Dredging demand supported by Government of India's push for the maritime sector and upcoming post-monsoon tender finalizations.
- Preparing for long-term deep-water mineral extraction opportunities under the government's "Samudra Manthan" initiative (expected in 2-3 years).
- Shipbuilding demand driven by inland waterways, green vessels, and potential export inquiries from the European market (3,000 DWT capacity vessels).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Subcontracting charges expected to be 5% to 6% of turnover from FY26 onwards (down from past years due to moving away from JV-led billing).
- Shipbuilding margins pre-subsidy are 15% to 20%, and post-subsidy can exceed 35%.
- Green Tug EBITDA margin stated at 75% on long-term contracts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total planned capex of close to INR1,000 crores over the next 1.5 years: ~INR450 crores in dredging, ~INR250 crores in Green Tugs/green vessels, and the balance in Saphale shipyard creation.
- Saphale shipyard Phase 1 to be operational by the end of FY27, scaling capacity to 14 vessels (ultimately 18 vessels per annum across phases).
- Evaluating additions of larger trailing suction hopper dredgers (>5,000 to 7,000 cubic meters), cutter suction dredgers, backhoe dredgers, and water injection dredgers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management stated the company is fully capitalized to reach the INR1,000 crores top line and cash flows will support future growth without needing further equity dilution.
- Shipbuilding working capital is supported by stage payments (10%, 20%, 30%, 50%), requiring no additional loans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Project-driven business causes quarterly revenue and profitability lumpiness based on project mix and asset deployment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sandeep Agarwal (Naredi Investment):** Asked about FY27 growth guidance and status of DCI Mangrol dues. *Management raised guidance to >60% and confirmed all INR16 crores DCI funds have been received.*
- **Pankaj (Axis Capital):** Inquired about margin impact from lower dredging order book share. *Management clarified dredging volume will be higher year-on-year and operational margins will stay robust between 35%-40%.*
- **Dhananjai Bagrodia (Alchemy):** Asked about DCI's reducing margins vs KMEW's rising margins, and promoter dilution plans. *Management attributed KMEW's success to better asset sweating and efficiency, and stated promoters are comfortable with current stake (>50%).*
- **Yash Master (Finnacle Family Office):** Questioned the nature of subcontracting expenses. *Management explained past high costs were due to JV structures; going forward, subcontracting will only be 5-6% outside shipbuilding transfers.*
- **Chirinda Mohanty (Individual Investor):** Raised concerns about lack of technological moat in dredging and risk of private port competition. *Management defended the business model, stating navigation remains a government responsibility with consistent domestic competitive bidding up to INR250 crores.*

### One-line takeaway
Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works reported a stellar Q1 FY27 with 138% revenue growth and 64% EBITDA margins, prompting an upgrade in FY27 growth guidance to >60% on the back of robust dredging execution and expansion into shipbuilding and green assets.
