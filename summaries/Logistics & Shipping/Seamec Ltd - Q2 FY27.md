# Seamec Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Logistics & Shipping*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue growth, improved operational performance, and successful fleet expansion with the acquisition and deployment of Seamec Anant.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained long-term target of ~15% revenue growth and 18-20% PAT CAGR over 4-5 years; reiterated ~40% stable-state EBITDA margin.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more bullish on DSV supply tightness (only one DSV under production globally) and strong domestic policy tailwinds like the Samudra Manthan initiative.
- New topics or risks: Detailed breakdown of Swordfish operational hurdles in Saudi Aramco; discussion on potential new-build DSV orders if second-hand options dry up.
- Last quarter's promises: Seamec Anant was successfully taken over on October 14, 2026, and deployed on a long-term charter starting October 21, 2026 (delivering on the Q1 target). Gallant vessel was sold.

- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 198.1 crore (vs Rs 97.4 crore in Q2 FY26, up 103% YoY)
- **Stand-alone Revenue:** Rs 183.6 crore (vs Rs 82.4 crore in Q2 FY26, up ~120% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 495 crore (vs Rs 308.3 crore in H1 FY26, up ~61% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Stand-alone Revenue:** Rs 467 crore (vs Rs 283.8 crore in H1 FY26, up 65% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 38.3 crore (vs Rs 7.8 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Stand-alone EBITDA:** Rs 31.2 crore (vs Rs 0.2 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 162.2 crore (vs Rs 104 crore in H1 FY26, up 55% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Stand-alone EBITDA:** Rs 148 crore (vs Rs 94.3 crore in H1 FY26, up 57% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 50 crore (vs loss of Rs 25.7 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Stand-alone PAT:** Rs 4.2 crore (vs loss of Rs 25.8 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT:** Rs 131.8 crore (vs Rs 50 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Stand-alone PAT:** Rs 85 crore (vs Rs 54 crore in H1 FY26, up 59% YoY)
- **Seamec Anant Acquisition Cost:** USD 70 million (~Rs 350 crore debt taken for acquisition)
- **Seamec Anant Charter Value:** USD 55 million excluding GST for approximately 1,225 days with HAL Offshore

- **Total Debt Profile:** Rs 450 crore to Rs 470 crore
- **Expected Full-Year Depreciation:** Rs 200 crore to Rs 250 crore over next two years

### Highlights
- Seamec Anant was formally taken over on October 14, 2026, at Kakinada and commenced operations under a long-term charter with HAL Offshore on October 21, 2026.
- Financial performance was bolstered by the operation of Sevak, Prabha, and Agastya, which were not part of the active fleet in Q2 FY26.
- Gallant vessel was sold off after receiving a good market deal.
- *(Note vs record)*: Subsequent to quarter end, the company secured a Rs 244 crore order from ONGC for subsea pipeline replacement, aligning with management's commentary on active contract wins for Seamec Princess.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a 15% revenue growth and 18% to 20% PAT CAGR over the next 4 to 5 years.
- Management guided for a steady-state EBITDA margin of around 40% for the next 3 to 4 years.
- Planned capex of USD 200 million to USD 250 million over the next 2 to 2.5 years, utilizing a 60:40 or 70:30 debt-equity ratio.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Government of India approved the Samudra Manthan National Offshore Exploration Scheme with an outlay of Rs 84,084 crore through 2030-2031.
- Global Diving Support Vessel (DSV) fleet stands at around 65 to 70 units with only 1 DSV under production globally, creating structural market tightness.
- Indicative charter rates for new-build DSVs expected around USD 70,000 to USD 80,000 per day or higher; replacement/second-hand DSV acquisition costs range from USD 110 million to upwards of USD 150 million.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee expenses are rising in correlation with fleet and crew additions.
- Fuel costs for long-term chartered vessels (Swordfish, Paladin, Agastya, Anant) are borne free of supply by the charterer, neutralizing fuel cost impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Incurred USD 70 million capex in H1 for Seamec Anant.
- Evaluating further vessel acquisitions (second-hand or new-build options with a 2-3 year lead time) maintaining discipline on pricing.
- European subsidiary investments planned for liquidation over the next 12 to 15 months to repatriate cash to India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt increased to Rs 450-470 crore following INR 350 crore debt raised for Seamec Anant.
- Future capex funding planned at 60-40 or 70-30 debt-to-equity ratios.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High operational utilization of vessels like Swordfish in Saudi Aramco leads to stringent third-party scrutiny and occasional minor breakdowns/off-hire days.
- Inherent monsoon seasonality creates quarterly volatility in offshore vessel operations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Siddharth Chauhan (360 ONE Capital) on DSV market outlook & second-hand prices:** Management noted extreme market tightness with only 1 DSV under production globally, pushing new-build/second-hand asset replacement costs above $110-$150 million and supporting rising charter rates.
2. **Dhaval Popat (Choice International) on Seamec Princess contracts:** Management stated they are at an advanced stage of finalizing a contract with L&T for the PRP-X project at day rates in line with Seamec II ($100,000/day).
3. **Yash Somani (YES Securities) on capex plans and debt:** Management outlined a projected capex of $250 million over the next 2 to 2.5 years, funded via 60:40 or 70:30 debt-equity.
4. **Tejash (Tejash & Investment) on Swordfish breakdowns:** Management explained that Saudi Aramco's stringent operational standards and intensive crane usage cause temporary third-party inspections/off-hires, cushioned by a built-in 2-3 day off-hire buffer per vessel.
5. **Tejash (Tejash & Investment) on long-term guidance:** Management guided for a 15% revenue growth and 18-20% PAT CAGR over the next 4 to 5 years.
6. **Sonali Shah (Individual Investor) on ESG & fuel costs:** Management clarified that vessels use low-sulfur fuel compliant with statutory norms, and long-term charterers directly bear fuel supply costs.

### One-line takeaway
Seamec posted a strong Q2 FY27 with consolidated revenue surging 103% YoY, backed by active fleet expansions like Seamec Anant and a structurally tight multi-year DSV outlook.
