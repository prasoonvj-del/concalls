# Rainbow Children's Medicare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered robust revenue and volume growth despite a seasonally soft quarter due to delayed monsoons, driven by strong underlying paediatric and maternal care demand.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 548 crore, up 23% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 1,018 crore, up 27.6% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 170 crore, up 14.2% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 304.6 crore, up 20.6% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 31% for Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27: 29.9%)
- PAT: INR 83.9 crore, up 10.9% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 146.4 crore, up 13.1% YoY)
- Operational growth YoY (Q2): Outpatient consultations +19%, Deliveries +20%, Inpatient discharges +15%
- Payer mix (Q2 FY27): 63.3% cash, 36.7% insurance
- Pharmacy cost: 14% of net revenue
- Cash equivalents, fixed deposits, and mutual funds: INR 542.8 crore as of September 30, 2026
- Capex incurred in Q2: INR 81.9 crore
- Pipeline: 1,050 beds at various stages of construction (total 2,300 beds expected over next 3 years)

### Highlights
- Commenced operations at a 70-bed acquired hospital in Nellore and a 50-bed greenfield hospital in Guntur.
- Andhra Pradesh government allotted a 2-acre land parcel in Amaravati to build a pediatric specialty hospital (150 beds planned in 2 phases).
- Successfully performed a complex living-related liver transplant on a 6-week-old baby weighing 2.9 kg at Banjara Hills, Hyderabad.
- Rolled out new HIS in the flagship Hyderabad hospital with 100% EMR usage; upgraded to SAP S/4HANA.
- Introduced loyalty card 'Shield' to drive patient acquisition and lifetime value tracking.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains expectation of high-teens top-line growth, with optimism for delivering higher growth.
- Pre-Ind AS EBITDA margins expected to be sustained around 25%–26% despite expansion and new hospital additions.
- New hospitals generally expected to achieve EBITDA break-even within 2 years (Hyderabad: 1st year; Bengaluru: 12–15 months; Chennai: 15–18 months).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 was a seasonally soft quarter due to a lack of monsoon/absence of usual seasonal diseases, but performance was driven by underlying paediatric and maternal care.
- Private insurance penetration in Tier-2 cities like Vijayawada and Rajahmundry has increased to 30%–35%.
- Government-sponsored schemes account for less than 1% of business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- New capacity additions impact margins by around 0.5% in a given quarter, which management considers manageable.
- Pharmacy cost is 14% of net revenue (approx. 7% is vaccinations, which are patient choice/unimpacted by price caps; oncology drugs and other price-capped medicines account for less than 0.5% of net revenue).
- Marketing expense is slightly over 2% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Indore: Clinical operations expected in Q3; groundbreaking for adjacent 120-bed greenfield on Diwali.
- Malad, Mumbai: 100-bed brownfield under active construction, targeting Q1 FY28 clinical operations.
- Coimbatore: 130-bed greenfield targeted for internal works in April 2027, operations in Q3 FY28.
- Gurgaon, NCR: Sector 56 (125 beds) targeted for Q3 FY28; Sector 44 hub (325 beds) for Q1 FY29.
- Seegehalli, Bengaluru (100 beds) and Pune (150 beds) in early construction, expected in H2 FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains robust with cash, FDs, and mutual fund investments of INR 542.8 crore.
- Expansion to be funded via internal cash generation and existing cash balances.
- Maximum valuation paid for past M&A has been around 11x EV/EBITDA.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential minor project execution delays capped at plus or minus one to two quarters (barring exceptional circumstances).
- New hospital category reported an EBITDA margin of around negative 16% due to recent 6-8 month commencement phases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prithvi Raj (Unifi Capital) asked about console margin pressure from continuous bed additions.** Management answered that emerging and mature hospitals offset this, limiting margin impact from new beds to roughly 0.5% per quarter, maintaining ~25% pre-Ind AS margins.
- **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC) asked about ARPOB growth headroom and mature hospital occupancy ceilings.** Management stated ARPOB is driven by obstetrics growth, fertility contribution, and case mix (normal expectation 6-8%), while mature hospital occupancy tops out around 60%.
- **Shubh Kalra (Asit C. Mehta Investments) asked for specialty-wise EBITDA margins (pediatrics vs. obstetrics vs. IVF).** Management declined to disclose individual vertical margins as IVF is still a growing 5% revenue contributor and pediatrics/obstetrics run closely together.
- **Alankar Garude (Kotak Institutional Equities) asked about the hub-and-spoke expansion strategy in North India.** Management stated North India is underserved and a major opportunity; they will scale methodically over 3 to 5 years leveraging managerial teams and defined capex.
- **Bino Pathiparampil (Elara Capital) questioned the impact of potential broad drug price capping on pharmacy costs.** Management reiterated that exposure to price-capped drugs is under 0.5% of net revenue, rendering any impact insignificant.
- **Tushar Raghatate (Omega Portfolio Advisors) asked about competitive pressure from Cloudnine (Kids Clinic).** Management noted that Rainbow is a much larger, institutionally driven full-service pediatric and maternal player and does not view them as a significant competitive threat.

### One-line takeaway
Rainbow Children's Medicare delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 23% revenue growth and steady margins, supported by robust core pediatric and maternal demand as it presses ahead with its pan-India expansion roadmap.
