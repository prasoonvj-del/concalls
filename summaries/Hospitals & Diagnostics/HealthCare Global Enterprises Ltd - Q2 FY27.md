# HealthCare Global Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained growth momentum, improved revenue mix, and narrowing losses in new facilities, offset by regulatory headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 7,318 million (Up 16% YoY; up 14% YoY excluding North Bengaluru).
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR 14,100 million (Up 15% YoY).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 1,425 million (Up 17% YoY, including North Bengaluru loss of INR 61 million).
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 19.7% (vs. 19.2% in Q2 FY26).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 2,740 million (Up 18% YoY).
- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 19.7% (vs. 18.8% in H1 FY26; +84 bps YoY).
- **Adjusted PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 287 million (Up 30% YoY).
- **Patient Volume Growth:** 9% YoY (Q2 and H1 FY27).
- **Average Revenue Per Patient (ARPP) Growth:** 7% YoY in Q2 FY27; 6% YoY in H1 FY27.
- **Robotic Surgeries Growth:** Up 37% YoY in Q2 FY27.
- **Non-Institutional Revenue Growth:** Up 21% YoY, share improved by over 300 bps to 69%.
- **North Bengaluru EBITDA Loss:** INR 61 million in Q2 FY27 (improved from INR 75 million in Q1 FY27).
- **South Cluster Revenue:** INR 2,941 million (Up 18% YoY, 40% of total revenue).
- **West Cluster Revenue:** INR 3,147 million (Up 10% YoY, 43% of total revenue).
- **East Cluster Revenue:** INR 928 million (Up 23% YoY, 11% of total revenue).
- **Kenya Operations Revenue:** INR 302 million (Up ~54% YoY).
- **Net Debt (excluding capital leases):** INR 5,751 million as of September 30, 2026.
- **H1 FY27 Capex:** INR 1,279 million.
- **Finance Costs (H1 FY27):** INR 775 million (Down 11% YoY).
- **Debt Repayments (H1 FY27):** INR 1,700 million.

### Highlights
- Financial figures exclude Milann Fertility business (divested in June 2026); priors adjusted for like-for-like comparison.
- Added 25 operational beds in Q2 FY27 (13 in Kenya, 7 in Kolkata, 5 in Nagpur); 146 operational beds added in H1 FY27.
- Operationalized MR-LINAC at North Bengaluru and cardiac surgical robotic system in Ahmedabad.
- Performed first CAR T-cell therapy at HCG Ahmedabad; introduced robotic breast surgery, robotic spine surgery, and transoral robotic surgery across various centers.
- Recognized exceptional expense of INR 116 million towards EPCG provisioning following export obligation assessment.
- Recognized non-cash ESOP expense of INR 29.8 million in H1 FY27.
- Acquired remaining 34% stake in Vizag operations utilizing Right issue proceeds.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains guidance of mid-teens revenue growth and high-teens to 20% EBITDA growth.
- North Bengaluru hospital on track to hit monthly EBITDA breakeven by end of Q4 FY27.
- Gujarat expected to return to mid-teens growth by Q4 FY27 following deliberate rationalization of low-margin institutional business.
- Trade margin cap on anti-cancer drugs expected to have a mid-to-high single-digit percentage impact on EBITDA on an annualized basis. Management expects to offset this via trade channel optimization, volume expansion, asset utilization, and operating efficiencies, with no material impact expected from FY28 onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust underlying demand supported by rising cancer incidence, improved screening, and affordability.
- International patient (MVT) business growing at 25%, contributing 4%-5% of overall revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion in Q2 FY27 moderated by incremental employee costs of ~INR 60 million due to the Karnataka Wage Act 2026 revision. Excluding wage revision, EBITDA growth was over 20% YoY.
- Majority of oncology drugs are now under the 30% trade margin cap (barring immunotherapy where margins are lower than the cap). Trade margin cap impact effective November 2026 (Q3 onwards).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex of INR 1,279 million incurred in H1 FY27 towards capacity expansion, medical equipment, and infrastructure upgrades.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt (excl. capital leases) at INR 5,751 million as of September 30, 2026.
- Right issue proceeds utilized: INR 150 crore towards MG Vizag acquisition, INR 170 crore towards debt repayment, and INR 100 crore for general corporate purposes.
- Cash conversion impacted in H1 FY27 due to ~INR 35 crore received from AP Government under Aarogyasri scheme (classified under borrowings) and a 30-day increase in credit period from a major supplier.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Mid-to-high single-digit annualized EBITDA impact from regulatory trade margin caps on anti-cancer drugs starting Q3.
- Geopolitical disturbances in the Middle East and Bangladesh impacted export projections, resulting in a one-time EPCG provision of INR 116 million.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya Chheda (Incred Asset Management) asked about the math behind the trade margin cap impact and EPCG provisions.** Management stated that 137 capped drugs represent a small subset of the medical oncology portfolio, resulting in an annualized mid-to-high single-digit EBITDA impact, and clarified that the INR 11.6 crore EPCG provision adequately covers export obligations with no immediate cash flow impact.
- **Devang Patel (Sameeksha Capital) asked about North Bengaluru breakeven and H1 cash flows.** Management reaffirmed monthly EBITDA breakeven by Q4 FY27 (losses down to INR 6 crore in Q2 from INR 7.5 crore in Q1) and attributed lower H1 OCF-to-EBITDA conversion (54%) to temporary working capital changes and tax payouts.
- **Nitin Agarwal (DAM Capital) questioned the network effect of North Bengaluru and Gujarat slowdown.** Management noted North Bengaluru is in a separate micro-market and has driven a positive 20%+ revenue growth spin-off at the KR Road Center of Excellence, while Gujarat slowdown is a deliberate strategy to cut low-margin institutional business to expand margins.
- **Gopal Bhatt (Baroda BNP Paribas MF) inquired about cash usage and MVT contributions.** Management confirmed cash outflows of ~INR 150-160 crore for the Vizag acquisition and INR 170 crore for debt repayment from Right issue proceeds, and noted MVT is growing at 25% despite geopolitical headwinds.

### One-line takeaway
HCG delivered 16% YoY revenue growth and 17% EBITDA growth in Q2 FY27 driven by higher volumes and complex treatments, while absorbing wage revisions and preparing for upcoming anti-cancer drug trade margin caps.
