# Jupiter Life Line Hospitals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent operational performance, sharp narrowing of EBITDA losses at the Dombivli facility, and strategic land acquisition for the Pune South hospital.

### Key numbers
- **Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 456.5 crore
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 106.9 crore
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 56.4 crore
- **Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 867.5 crore
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 186.2 crore
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 93.9 crore
- **ARPOB:** Rs 71,800
- **IP ARPOB:** Rs 58,700
- **ALOS:** 3.79 days
- **Occupancy (H1 FY27, all hospitals):** 59.5%
- **Occupancy (H1 FY27, excluding Dombivli):** 64.8%
- **Dombivli EBITDA impact/loss (Q2 FY27):** Rs 20 lakh
- **Dombivli occupancy:** Mid-30s% (approx. 35%)
- **Thane occupancy:** Close to 75%
- **Cash and cash equivalents (H1 FY27):** Rs 400+ crore
- **Consolidated debt (H1 FY27):** Rs 600 crore
- **Net debt (H1 FY27):** ~Rs 170 crore
- **Pune South land acquisition cost:** Rs 150 crore total consideration (incremental cash outflow of Rs 100 crore after adjusting Rs 50 crore prior deposit; land measures 11,452 square meters)
- **Cumulative capex spent:** Pune South: ~Rs 320 crore (including land); Mira Road: ~Rs 75-80 crore
- **Insurance/cashless mix (Group blended):** ~55%

### Highlights
- Board approved acquisition of leased land for the Pune South hospital for Rs 150 crore; registration expected in days.
- Dombivli hospital saw a sharp narrowing of EBITDA loss to Rs 20 lakh in Q2 compared to Rs 9.5 crore in Q1, aided by higher seasonal infectious disease admissions.
- Pune South project remains on schedule; Mira Bhayandar is in the regulatory stage (activities to commence before fiscal year-end); BKC is in the planning stage.
- Maintenance capex estimated to average Rs 10-20 crore per unit (averaged over 4-5 years for biomedical equipment replacement).
- Acquired an IV fluid manufacturing facility near Ujjain with an equity value of Rs 3.8 crore and debt of ~Rs 7-10 crore, resulting in Rs 5 crore of goodwill.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a self-imposed ceiling on debt of not exceeding 3x EBITDA.
- New hospitals generally expected to take 2 years for EBITDA break-even.
- Management refrained from giving formal financial guidance on revenue, profit, EBITDA growth, or margins.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 historically sees higher infectious disease burden and higher occupancy compared to Q1 and Q3 across all existing units.
- Medical admissions in Q2 generally carry lower ARPOBs compared to surgical admissions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Dombivli fixed costs run at ~Rs 6 to 8 crore per quarter.
- Indore is in a growth phase with ongoing investments in medical teams, causing near-term fluctuations in top-line, EBITDA, and occupancy.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pune has no further scope for expansion; Thane has potential for some bed additions subject to regulatory clearance.
- Pune South, Mira Road, and BKC are the three live active projects funded by internal accruals and term loans.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Historical EBITDA to net cash conversion averages around 70% (may temper slightly to 60%-plus as net debt levels rise).
- Total term loan sanctioned: Rs 500 crore (partially utilized for Pune land and ongoing projects).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory and legal uncertainties regarding Supreme Court deliberations on private healthcare commercialization and medicine/consumable markups (management stated they will abide by the law of the land once finalized).
- Upcoming 30% margin cap on onco drugs effective November 1 (management has not modeled the impact yet).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abin Benny (JM Financial) asked about Dombivli revenue and occupancy exit rates.** Management stated unit-wise revenues are not disclosed and current occupancy is in the mid-30s.
2. **Janargan asked about the potential impact of Supreme Court observations on healthcare pricing and economic moats.** Management declined to comment on ongoing court deliberations and reiterated their strong local infrastructure and clinical teams.
3. **Dhvani Shah asked why Indore top-line grew 18% while EBITDA remained flat.** Management explained it is in a growth phase with ongoing medical team investments causing fluctuating quarterly metrics.
4. **Aryan Jain asked about the financial impact of the 30% margin cap on onco drugs.** Management stated they have not modeled it and will observe actual impact over the quarter.
5. **Abdulkader Puranwala asked about the utilization of the Rs 500 crore term loan.** Management responded it will be utilized toward the three live projects (Pune South, Mira Road, BKC), including the Rs 100 crore incremental cash outflow for Pune land.

### One-line takeaway
Jupiter Life Line Hospitals reported a stable Q2 FY27 performance led by seasonal occupancy highs, a near break-even at the Dombivli facility, and a strategic land buy for Pune South.
