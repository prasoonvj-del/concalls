# Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record revenue and profits, with new hospitals (Faridabad Sector 20) achieving EBITDA breakeven ahead of schedule.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 392.7 crore (up 51% YoY, 15% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 91.7 crore (up 39% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 23.3%.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA Margin (excl. New Delhi & Faridabad Sector 20):** 28.1%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 45.4 crore.
*   **ARPOB:** Rs 34,758 (up 7% YoY).
*   **Cash Profit (PAT + Depreciation):** Up 32% YoY.
*   **Attrition Rate:** 7% at group level (senior doctors <3-4%).

### Highlights
*   **New Hospitals:** Greater Faridabad, New Delhi, and Agra contributed 27% to total revenue.
*   **Breakeven:** Faridabad Sector 20 achieved EBITDA breakeven in 9 months.
*   **Operational Metrics:** Existing hospitals (Noida, Jhansi-Orchha) grew 22% YoY; occupancy reached ~75%.
*   **Clinical Focus:** Oncology contributes ~10% of revenue; adding LINAC machines at Faridabad Sector 20 and New Delhi.
*   **Shareholder Returns:** Board approved a maiden interim dividend of 5% of face value.
*   **Talent:** Launched ESOP Scheme 2026 to retain talent.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Capacity:** Targeting 5,000 beds in <3 years (current announced capacity >3,200 beds).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Targeting upwards of 24% for FY27.
*   **ARPOB Growth:** Expecting 9-10% YoY growth.
*   **New Hospital Performance:** Management expects new hospitals to reach 15-20% EBITDA margins within 15-18 months post-breakeven.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management expects FY27 growth to surpass last year's 37% YoY growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Payer Mix:** Government payer mix is ~40% overall; newer hospitals maintain <10% government business.
*   **International:** Opened Yatharth Information Center in Uzbekistan; expanding outreach in Asia, Africa, and Middle East.
*   **Regulatory:** Management noted recent discussions on capping hospital room rents but stated they have not seen significant impact from past regulatory interventions (e.g., stent/implant caps).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Interest Cost:** Increased to Rs 6.6 crore in Q1 (from Rs 5 crore previously) due to debt taken for acquisitions.
*   **Depreciation:** Expected to be Rs 20-30 crore per quarter for the remainder of the year.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management expects EBITDA drag from new hospitals to reduce as they scale.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Gurugram:** 250-bed facility expected to go live in Q1 FY28.
*   **Brownfield:** 450-bed expansion at Noida Extension and Greater Noida expected to be operational in 15-19 months.
*   **Capex per bed:** Projected at Rs 75-80 lakh for future additions.
*   **Funding:** Future capex to be funded via internal accruals and debt (comfortable with debt up to 2x trailing 12-month EBITDA).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Construction Delays:** Monsoon season has caused minor delays in construction at Greater Noida.
*   **EBITDA Drag:** New hospitals (New Delhi, Faridabad Sector 20) continue to exert pressure on consolidated margins until they scale.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Delhi Model Town Hospital occupancy:** Management clarified that occupancy is not stagnant; it appears lower because the census bed count increased from 100 to 150 beds this quarter.
*   **On ALOS decline:** Management attributed the drop below 4 days to a higher surgical mix and lower government patient volume in new hospitals.
*   **On competitive intensity:** Management stated they are confident in the Delhi market due to high population density and plans to introduce quaternary care and oncology programs.
*   **On ESOPs:** Management noted ESOPs are now a necessity to retain star clinical talent, alongside providing doctors with autonomy over their departments.

### One-line takeaway
Yatharth Hospital is aggressively scaling capacity and improving its payer mix, with new assets showing strong early-stage performance and a clear path to margin expansion.
