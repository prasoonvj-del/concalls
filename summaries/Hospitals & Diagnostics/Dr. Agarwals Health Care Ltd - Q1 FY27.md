# Dr. Agarwals Health Care Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-07 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record surgical facility additions, robust revenue growth, and margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 614 crore (up 26% YoY, 8.8% QoQ)
- **Total income:** Rs 620 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **Ind AS EBITDA:** Rs 177 crore (up 25.2% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 28.5% (up 30 bps YoY)
- **PAT margin:** 8.9% (up 127 bps YoY)
- **Total network:** 285 facilities across 14 states, 5 UTs, and 165 cities (31% Tier 1, 63% other, 6% international)
- **Patient footfalls:** Over 8.8 lakh patients served
- **Surgeries performed:** Over 90,000 surgeries (15.5% YoY growth)
- **Cataract surgeries:** ~67,000 total (high-end cataract at 29.3%); Robotic (Femto) cataract grew 33.4% YoY to 1,548 procedures
- **Lenticular (SMILE) procedures:** Up 36.2% YoY
- **Retinal surgeries:** 3,861 procedures (up 30% YoY)
- **Corneal transplants:** 285 procedures
- **SSSG (Cohort up to FY23):** 16.3% YoY growth, contributing Rs 465 crore (75.9% of group revenues)
- **FY24 vintage SSSG:** 19.6% (contributed Rs 48 crore)
- **FY25 vintage SSSG:** 38.6% (contributed Rs 62 crore)
- **FY26 vintage revenue:** Rs 33 crore
- **Region-wise revenue:** South Rs 387 crore (up 22.8% YoY, 63% of group revenue); West Rs 91 crore (up 24% YoY, 15% share); North Rs 57 crore (up 50.5% YoY, 9% share); East Rs 16 crore (up 21% YoY)
- **Payer mix:** Cash 63.6%, Insurance & TPA 27.7%, Government schemes 8.6%
- **Greenfield losses:** ~Rs 20 crore at corporate EBITDA level (including FY26 and FY27 centers and pre-operating losses)
- **Acquisition liabilities paid:** ~Rs 25 crore in Q1
- **Interest on lease liability:** ~Rs 18 crore in Q1
- **Interest on deferred acquisition payable:** Dropped to Rs 3.6 crore (from Rs 6.8 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Finance cost:** INR 23.5 crore (compared to INR 24.7 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Owner's share of PAT:** 82.2% (up from 79% in Q1 FY26)
- **Doctor attrition rate:** 16% to 17% overall (2% to 3% for senior doctors)

### Highlights
- Launched 16 new surgical facilities in Q1 FY27 (18 total facilities including 2 primary), the highest ever in a single quarter.
- Total network expansion of 166 new greenfield facilities since FY23.
- Highlighted a proprietary clinical innovation: Pinhole Pupilloplasty (PPP) as an alternative to corneal transplants (over 500 procedures performed last financial year with ~94% significant visual acuity improvement).
- Leadership change in Gujarat and Kolkata driving stronger patient traction.
- Merger completion expected around mid-November.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in delivering on the full-year plan of adding 60 new facilities (including 40 surgical centers).
- Planned additions: 12 facilities for Q2 and 30 in the second half.
- Pipeline of 30+ signed LOIs in place.
- Management aims for a sustainable SSSG around 12% to 13% over the long term, while maintaining momentum.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Stronger patient traction seen across new regions due to network scale and trusted brand equity.
- Premiumization trend continuing as patients opt for better lenses, aided by rising disposable incomes and insurance penetration.
- Punjab and J&K bounced back strongly this quarter following previous impacts from Operation Sindoor and floods.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Doctor and employee costs increased slightly to 34% of total revenues due to talent additions for Q1 FY27 launches.
- Other expenses stable at 16.8% of total revenue.
- Gross margins improved by ~1% (COGS improvement of ~1%), aided by a higher surgical mix.
- Femto cataract generates an additional charge of ~Rs 35,000 with a click fee/license cost of Rs 10,500 to Rs 11,000, expanding rupee gross margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned 18 new facilities in Q1: 1 large tertiary (Thane), 15 secondary, and 2 primary facilities.
- Delhi NCR footprint expanded to 8 facilities with new additions in Faridabad, Shahdara, and Ghaziabad; 3 to 5 more facilities planned for the remainder of the year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Paid ~Rs 25 crore toward acquisition liabilities in Q1.
- Senior doctor attrition remains very low at 2% to 3%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Greenfield losses stood at ~Rs 20 crore at the corporate EBITDA level due to recent expansions (23 surgical facilities launched in the last 6 months).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Maulik (360 One Capital) asked about the breakup of revenue growth vs. volume growth and greenfield losses.** Rahul Agarwal (COO) stated that value growth was driven by ~7.5% premiumization and ~0.5% price hikes, and greenfield losses were ~Rs 20 crore. Management answered directly without dodging.
2. **Nikhil Upadhyay asked about SSSG volume vs. value split and North region drivers.** Adil Agarwal and Rahul Agarwal explained that SSSG volume grew ~8% and value grew ~8% (with OPD volume/value contributing 6% and conversion 2%), and noted that Delhi expansion and recovery in Punjab drove North growth. Management answered directly.
3. **Tushar Manudhane (Motilal Oswal) asked if technology adoption (e.g., Femto) would compress margins due to higher costs.** Adil Agarwal explained that rupee gross margins expand because additional patient charges (~Rs 35,000) exceed the click fee (~Rs 10,500–11,000). Management answered directly.
4. **Paras Sarkar (Individual Investor) asked about the expected timeline for the merger completion.** Adil Agarwal stated they expect to close it around mid-November. Management answered directly.

### One-line takeaway
Dr. Agarwal's Health Care delivered a record Q1 FY27 with 26% revenue growth and 25.2% EBITDA growth, driven by a record 16 new surgical facility additions and strong SSSG of 16.3% for mature centers.
