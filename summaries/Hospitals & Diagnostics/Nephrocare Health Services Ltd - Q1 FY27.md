# Nephrocare Health Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and margin growth driven by international expansion, operating leverage, and disciplined capacity addition.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 282 crore (+23.7% YoY).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 65.1 crore (+30.7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 23.1% (+120 bps YoY).
*   **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 37 crore (+41.7% YoY).
*   **Active Guests:** 38,262 (+13% YoY).
*   **Treatments:** 10.3 lakh (+13.3% YoY).
*   **Revenue per Treatment (RPT):** Rs 2,733 (+9.2% YoY).
*   **Annualized Adjusted ROCE:** 21%.

### Highlights
*   **Network:** 550 clinics across 5 countries and 357 cities. Added 26 clinics in Q1 (19 in India, 7 in Philippines).
*   **International:** Contributes ~45% of revenue. Philippines network reached 50 clinics across 39 cities.
*   **Saudi Arabia:** First clinic operational in Riyadh; awaiting Ministry of Health tender documents.
*   **Kazakhstan:** Subsidiary incorporated to evaluate Central Asian opportunities.
*   **Clinical Quality:** Launched NephroPlus International Dialysis Academy (NIDA) to train renal nurses.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Maintained medium-term growth guidance of 15% to 20% over the next 3-5 years.
*   **Expansion:** Targets 40-50 clinics/year in India and 10-15 clinics/year in Philippines.
*   **New Markets:** Aims to enter one new international market every 12-18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Structure:** 80% of dialysis capacity in India remains in the unorganized (hospital-run) sector; management expects a long-term shift to organized, pure-play networks.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Dialysis is a fixed-capacity business (3 cycles/day); growth is driven by capacity addition rather than same-store sales growth.
*   **Pricing:** Price increases are lumpy and periodic (every 5-10 years) rather than annual.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **COGS:** Improved by 175 bps as a percentage of revenue due to global procurement and operating leverage.
*   **Depreciation:** Flat at 8.6%-8.7% of revenue; management cited RFID-based asset tracking for improved discipline in India.
*   **Other Expenses:** Includes ECL provisions (2%-2.5% of revenue), public company compliance costs, and international business development investments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Rs 44 crore spent in Q1.
*   **IPO Proceeds:** 68% (Rs 207 crore) utilized as of June 2026.
*   **Model:** Asset-light, build-own-operate (BOO) model with hospitals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Structurally high due to government payment cycles (3-4 months).
*   **AR Days:** Improved to 101 days from 121 days in the previous year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Saudi Arabia:** Tender timelines are uncertain; management described it as a "binary" market where they must win the tender or exit.
*   **Regulatory:** Dialysis is a state subject in India; standalone clinics face regulatory and advocacy hurdles compared to hospital-based models.
*   **AI/Clinical:** Attempts to use AI for predicting adverse clinical events are in early stages and have not yet met confidence thresholds.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did depreciation decline sequentially despite acquisitions?** Management stated that RFID asset tracking improved capital discipline in India, offsetting the amortization of intangibles from Philippines acquisitions.
*   **Q: What is the strategy for Saudi Arabia?** Management clarified it is an investment phase to demonstrate clinical outcomes; they will either win a tender and scale or exit the market.
*   **Q: How does the company grow if it's a fixed-capacity business?** Management explained that growth is strictly driven by capacity addition (new clinics/machines) rather than increasing utilization of existing, already-maxed-out capacity.
*   **Q: Why are working capital days high?** Management noted this is structural due to government reimbursement cycles, but they have improved AR days by 20 days through digitization.

### One-line takeaway
Nephrocare is leveraging its India-proven operating platform to scale internationally, focusing on disciplined capacity addition while navigating the binary nature of tender-based markets like Saudi Arabia.
