# Krsnaa Diagnostics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted crossing the milestone of Rs 507 crore in H1 FY27 revenue (27% YoY growth) and strong long-term platform fundamentals despite temporary margin moderation and elevated receivables.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 507 crore (27% YoY growth)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 271 crore (32% YoY growth)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (before CSR and ESOP):** Rs 72 crore (20% YoY growth)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin (before CSR and ESOP):** 27% (vs 29% in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (after CSR and ESOP):** Rs 71 crore (Margin: 26%)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 25 crore (3% YoY growth, 9% margin)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA (before CSR and ESOP):** Rs 131 crore (16% YoY growth, 26% margin vs 28% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 41 crore (Margin: 8%)
- **Q2 FY27 Retail Revenue:** Rs 23.5 crore (35% YoY growth, ~9% of overall group revenue)
- **Receivables:** Approx. Rs 470 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Potential Q3 Collections Visibility:** Approx. Rs 200 crore.
- **Promoter Investment:** Rs 92 crore via convertible warrants (Rs 23 crore contributed so far).
- **Network Stats (Q2 FY27):** 259 pathology labs, 196 CT/MRI centers, 8,400+ patient touch points across 334 districts in 19 states/UTs (75% in rural areas). 57 NABH pathology labs and 75 NABL radiology centers. Over 350 radiologists.

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 500 crore in revenue for the first 6 months for the first time.
- Retail business registered 35% YoY revenue growth, with patient volumes up 45% and test volumes up 48%.
- Retail achieved operational breakeven in September and expects overall EBITDA breakeven by Q3/early Q4.
- Generated livelihood opportunities for ~5,000 local people, particularly for Rajasthan operations.
- Strategic association with Apulki Healthcare commenced operations in Pune, with a 2,000-bed hospital platform pipeline across Borivali, PCMC, KDMC, and Chembur-Govandi.
- Won cash-based PPP tenders for Himachal Pradesh (34 CT scan machines) and Punjab (PET-CT project).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects cash-based revenues to contribute 40% to 45% of overall revenue mix (approx. Rs 400 crore) by the end of FY27.
- Management aims to grow retail contribution to double-digits by the end of FY27, and towards higher teens to 20%+ in the next 2 to 3 years.
- Rajasthan project annualized revenue potential is expected to reach ~Rs 250 crore next year.
- Management targets bringing working capital back to historical levels of ~90 to 100 days.
- Management aspires to achieve double-digit ROCE in the higher teens.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Volumes saw an uptick due to a combination of retail activations and PPP ramp-ups (like Rajasthan).
- Blended revenue per test decreased due to higher volume contributions from larger PPP projects with lower per-test prices.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins moderated temporarily to 27% in Q2 FY27 (vs 29% YoY) due to upfront operating costs of new implementations (Rajasthan), higher depreciation, and finance costs.
- Fees to hospital and channel partners increased directly due to Rajasthan revenue ramp-ups managed via partners.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rajasthan implementation is 75% to 80% complete.
- Expanded specialized diagnostics via new PET-CT PPP project in Punjab.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables increased to ~Rs 470 crore due to business expansion and delayed fund allocations/disbursements from certain states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Himachal Pradesh).
- Payables rose to Rs 230 crore (combination of trade creditors and project enhancement/capital creditors).
- Visibility on ~Rs 200 crore collections during Q3 based on government confirmations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Extended collection cycles and delayed payment disbursements from certain state government authorities.
- Upfront operational, depreciation, and finance costs temporarily impacting margins during project ramp-ups.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital) asked about Rajasthan cost absorption, run-rates, and the confidence behind the Rs 200 crore receivable collection.** Management stated that majority costs and depreciation are already in the P&L, Rajasthan annualized revenue potential is ~Rs 250 crore (mostly next year), and receivable confidence stems from direct ground confirmations from government quarters.
2. **Surya Narayan Patra (PhillipCapital) asked about the drop in revenue per test and swing in hospital/channel partner fees.** Management explained the blended price drop is due to higher PPP volume shares (like Rajasthan), and partner fee increases directly align with Rajasthan's partnership execution model.
3. **Rushabh (RBSA Investment Manager) asked about cash-based B2C mix targets and ROCE improvement levers.** Management noted a target of 40-45% cash mix by FY27 end, with ROCE levers including sale-leaseback models, cash businesses, and cost optimizations targeting higher teens.
4. **Deepak Ajmera (Elpro International) asked about growth drivers for FY29 and retail contribution targets.** Management highlighted new PPP tenders (HP, Punjab), retail scale-up, and Apulki hospital tie-ups, with retail targeted to reach higher teens to 20%+ in 2-3 years.
5. **Aditya Chheda (InCred Asset Management) asked about the surge in payables from Rs 63 crore to Rs 230 crore.** Management answered it is a combination of trade creditors and project/capital-related creditors.
6. **Anil Gupta (Individual Investor) asked about Rajasthan execution percentage and retail EBITDA status.** Management stated 75-80% of labs are implemented, and retail achieved operational breakeven in September with full EBITDA breakeven expected by Q3/early Q4.

### One-line takeaway
Krsnaa Diagnostics crossed Rs 507 crore in H1 FY27 revenue driven by strong scale-up in PPP and retail segments, while managing temporary margin moderation and targeting aggressive cash-based revenue growth.
