# Gujarat Kidney and Super Speciality Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, positive operating cash flow, and successful recent acquisitions while addressing margin fluctuations due to integration costs.

### Key numbers
- **FY26 Consolidated Revenue:** INR 80 crores (up 104% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA:** INR ~27 crores (up 63% YoY)
- **FY26 PAT:** INR ~17 crores (up 77% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA Margin:** 32.8%
- **FY26 PAT Margin:** 20.05%
- **FY26 Average Bed Occupancy:** ~65%
- **FY26 ARPOB:** INR 9,221 per day
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** INR 72.4 crores (up 157% YoY from INR 28.2 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 18.6 crores (up 35% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** ~25.7%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 11.9 crores (up 44% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** ~16.5%
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 38.1 crores (up 11% QoQ, up 195% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 10.2 crores (up 22% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 26.8% (up from 24.4% in Q1)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 6.6 crores (up 24% QoQ, up 129% YoY)
- **Net Worth (as of September 30, 2026):** INR 263.6 crores
- **Cash and Bank Balance (as of September 30, 2026):** INR 32.2 crores
- **Borrowings (as of September 30, 2026):** INR 17.3 crores
- **H1 Deployments:** INR 16.7 crores towards acquisitions and INR 6.2 crores in capex

### Highlights
- Operating out of 11 hospitals with 10 pharmacies across 9 cities.
- Core strength remains renal science and urology, alongside expansions into cardiology, oncology, orthopedic, OB-GYN, and general surgery.
- Acquired a 51% stake in Blue Tree, an established polyclinic in Dubai with an existing revenue base of ~INR 20 crores, intended as a medical tourism gateway.
- Operating cash flow turned positive during H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide specific numerical revenue or margin guidance for FY27, pointing to historical quarterly patterns and urging reliance on past growth trends.
- Priorities moving forward: upgrading existing hospitals, pursuing selective inorganic opportunities, and capturing a larger share of the patient journey (OPD, diagnostics, IPD, ICU, surgery, pharmacies, follow-ups).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted that the hospital industry experiences seasonal fluctuations based on festivals and doctor leaves, unlike manufacturing sectors.
- Government schemes and insurance/TPA setups involve processing delays and queries, but collections are on track.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin fluctuations and employee cost increases are attributed to recent acquisitions (such as the Blue Tree Clinic in Dubai, which carries higher employee costs) and consolidation/integration phases.
- Cost control focus includes shifting towards full-time doctors and utilizing internal auditors to monitor leakages across subsidiaries.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pipeline includes two greenfield projects: the expansion of the Ahmedabad hospital and the Bharuch hospital.
- Inorganic strategy prioritizes acquiring remaining stakes in existing subsidiaries (Blue Tree in Dubai, Raj Farm in Bharuch, and Patel Multispecialty in Ankleshwar) and evaluating hospitals with a 15+ year doctor-run history, no litigations, and a 7-year commitment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Positioned as net cash with INR 32.2 crores in cash/bank balances against INR 17.3 crores in borrowings.
- Stricter receivables management implemented from April 1st to control pendency.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government health schemes see decreasing margins; management noted they selectively avoid heavy exposure (catering to ~2%).
- Insurance and government payment cycles involve query resolutions and discharge bill processing times.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gagandeep Singh (Doon Funds) asked about plans to increase ARPOB (INR 9,221/day).** Management stated that consolidated ARPOB reflects newly acquired hospitals and that they have an internal committee formulating strategic plans to improve it.
2. **Vedant Rathi (Investor) inquired about capex strategy, acquisition pipeline, and geographic focus.** Management responded that priority is acquiring the remaining 49% stakes in existing subsidiaries (Blue Tree, Raj Farm, Patel Multispecialty) and evaluating acquisition targets inside/outside Gujarat with strict criteria.
3. **Devan Madlani (Investor) asked about accumulated receivables and insurance/customer payments.** Management stated that receivables are accumulated historical balances and that recovery/cash collection processes are stricter since April 1st, though government/insurance portals involve query delays.
4. **Preeti Agarwal (Investor) questioned the drop in H1 EBITDA margins (from 48% in H1 FY26 to 25% in FY27) and jump in employee costs.** Management explained that margins and employee costs fluctuate due to recent acquisitions like the Dubai polyclinic.

### One-line takeaway
Gujarat Kidney and Super Speciality Limited delivered strong H1 and Q2 FY27 revenue growth driven by its asset-light acquisition model, while maintaining a net cash balance sheet despite temporary margin dilution from new integrations.
