# Paras Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and margin expansion, supported by successful IPO-led deleveraging and rapid turnaround of new hospitals.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 511 crore (+23% YoY); H1 Revenue: Rs 981 crore (+25% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 120 crore (+47% YoY); H1 Operating EBITDA: Rs 225 crore (+58% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 23.5% (+390 bps YoY).
*   **PBT:** Rs 54 crore (2.7x YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 40.3 crore (3.3x YoY).

### Highlights
*   **IPO & Debt:** Successfully listed on Oct 28, 2026; utilized Rs 375 crore of IPO proceeds to repay term debt.
*   **New Hospitals:** Srinagar and Kanpur turned EBITDA positive (18.7% margin in H1), significantly outperforming the previous year's loss.
*   **Mature Hospitals:** Gurgaon, Patna, Panchkula, and Darbhanga grew revenue by 8.5% with 29.5% margins.
*   **Emerging Hospitals:** Ranchi and Udaipur grew revenue by 27.2% in Q2.
*   *Record Check:* The call confirms the IPO completion and debt repayment, aligning with our records.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets steady-state EBITDA margins in the mid-20s.
*   No specific revenue guidance provided.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Occupancy:** Group level 76.3% (vs 62.5% in Q2 FY26).
*   **ARPOB:** Rs 48,900 (H1 FY27).
*   **ALOS:** 2.8 days (H1 FY27).
*   **Payor Mix:** 32% cash/self-pay, 24% insurance, 44% government schemes/PSUs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Material cost improved by >3% due to procurement efficiencies and case mix.
*   Doctor costs improved by 70 bps due to operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current Capacity:** 1,500 operational beds.
*   **Near-term:** 200-250 beds to be operationalized in Kanpur and Ranchi.
*   **Future Pipeline:** 300 beds in Golf Course Extension (Gurgaon) and 500 beds in Ludhiana (next 12-18 months).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Gross borrowings at Rs 979 crore; pro forma net debt at Rs 143 crore post-IPO repayment.
*   **Working Capital:** Negative Rs 172 crore impact in H1; DSOs increased to 92 days due to lumpy payments and payor mix changes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Udaipur Softness:** Performance impacted by the temporary suspension of state government schemes in Rajasthan.
*   **Working Capital:** Increased receivables/DSOs are a management priority.
*   **Talent:** Finding tertiary/quaternary care specialists remains an industry-wide challenge.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Udaipur softness:* Management attributed it to Rajasthan state government scheme reviews; they are hopeful for resumption.
*   *Kanpur ramp-up:* Occupancy rose from 22.7% (H1 FY26) to 78.4% (H1 FY27); more beds opening by year-end.
*   *Cash flow/Receivables:* Management explained negative cash flow was due to working capital absorption; expects settlement as payor mix improves.
*   *Asset-light model:* Management confirmed focus on asset-light expansion but remains open to all models (greenfield/brownfield/M&A) if opportunities arise.
*   *ALOS:* Management defended the low ALOS (2.8 days) as sustainable, driven by case mix and technology.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational leverage and successful IPO-led deleveraging are driving profitability, with new hospitals turning profitable faster than expected.
