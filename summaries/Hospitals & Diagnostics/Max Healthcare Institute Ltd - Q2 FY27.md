# Max Healthcare Institute Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew by 20% YoY, operating EBITDA by 18%, and PAT by 10% alongside steady occupancy of 77% despite a 14% increase in operational bed capacity.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained expansion pipelines; Board newly approved an investment of Rs 415 crore towards 209 brownfield beds at Max Noida, Max Vaishali, and BLK-Max, including a Rs 85 crore multi-level car park tower at BLK.
- Tone and demand view: Maintained a strong growth trajectory; management noted quick brownfield ramp-ups despite temporary pre-operative/ramp-up drags.
- New topics or risks: Government-imposed trade margin caps on non-scheduled drugs (impact under assessment); de-empanelment of BLK for CGHS/Railways and Shalimar Bagh for ECHS due to payment issues.
- Last quarter's promises: Max Smart 260 beds achieved EBITDA breakeven within 3 months as promised; Kalinga Hospital shareholding increased to 66% with new leadership and renovation initiated.

### Key numbers
- Gross revenue: Rs 3,219 crore (up 20% YoY, 8% QoQ)
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 820 crore (up 18% YoY, 16% QoQ)
- Operating EBITDA margin: 26.8% (flat YoY, up ~200 bps QoQ)
- PAT: Rs 448 crore (up 10% YoY, 26% QoQ)
- Average occupancy: 77%
- In-patient ARPP: Rs 2.31 lakhs (up 8% YoY)
- International patient revenue: Rs 307 crore (up 33% YoY; 10% of healthcare revenue)
- Max@Home revenue: Rs 86 crore (up 37% YoY)
- Max Lab revenue: Rs 66 crore (up 24% YoY)
- Free cash flow generated: Rs 500 crore
- Net debt: Rs 2,369 crore

### Highlights
- Operational bed capacity increased by 14% YoY, with occupied bed days (OBDs) up 13% YoY and 7% QoQ.
- Max Smart's newly commissioned 260 beds achieved EBITDA breakeven within 3 months of operations.
- Kalinga Hospital (Bhubaneswar) reported revenue of Rs 38 crore and EBITDA of Rs 4 crore for the quarter; shareholding increased to 66% via rights issue.
- Institutional bed share declined to 29% from 34% last year.
- Oncology share of in-patient revenues dropped to 21% from 26% in Q2 FY26 due to the discontinuation of select high-value chemotherapy drugs for institutional patients following CGHS rate revisions last year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the balance 205 beds at Max Nanavati and Max Smart to open post-Diwali puja.
- Sector 56 Gurgaon (500 beds): Management expects to start phase commissioning by the end of this financial year, with peak cumulative EBITDA loss estimated around Rs 40-50 crore.
- Zirakpur, Mohali (400 beds): Management expects commissioning by December 2027, with estimated peak EBITDA loss around Rs 30 crore.
- Expanded capacity projects (Noida, Vaishali, BLK-Max) expected to become available over the next 18 to 24 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand for quality healthcare remains strong across the network.
- Digital revenue reached Rs 1,099 crore (34% of overall revenue), with website traffic growing 44% YoY to 1.14 crore sessions.
- Free treatment provided to over 57,000 economically weaker section patients worth Rs 90 crore at hospital tariff (up 47% YoY).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Saket complex experienced an EBITDA drag during Q2 due to the initial ramp-up of newly commissioned beds at Max Smart. Excluding the Saket complex, network revenue grew by 20% and EBITDA by 25%.
- Operating EBITDA per bed grew by 4% YoY to Rs 77 lakhs per annum.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total free cash flow generated was Rs 500 crore, of which Rs 292 crore was invested in ongoing capacity expansion projects and Rs 195 crore was disbursed towards dividends.
- 202 brownfield beds were added during the quarter (Max Smart, Max Lucknow, Max Noida, Max Dwarka).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 2,369 crore (vs Rs 2,384 crore in June 2026).
- Net debt-to-EBITDA ratio remained below 1x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government trade margin cap on non-scheduled drugs; management is assessing the potential financial impact.
- De-empanelment of BLK hospital for CGHS/Railways and Shalimar Bagh for ECHS due to non-payment issues.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tausif Shaikh (BNP Paribas) asked about hospital de-empanelments.** Management clarified that BLK and Shalimar Bagh were de-empanelled due to payment realization issues, noting it is a normal trajectory for older, mature hospitals to distill payor mix.
- **Neha Manpuria (BofA Securities) asked about the Saket complex EBITDA drag normalization.** Management explained that brownfields carry a one-quarter pre-operative and initial ramp-up drag before occupancy and operating leverage normalize in subsequent quarters.
- **Rahul Jeewani asked about greenfield EBITDA losses for Gurgaon and Zirakpur.** Management estimated peak cumulative EBITDA losses of around Rs 40-50 crore for Sector 56 Gurgaon and ~Rs 30 crore for Zirakpur.
- **Bino Pathiparampil asked for the pharmacy revenue contribution.** Management stated that pharmacy and services are bundled (e.g., packages and chemotherapy mixing charges), making it difficult to unbundle specific pharmacy revenue percentages.

### One-line takeaway
Max Healthcare delivered solid Q2 FY27 performance with 20% revenue growth and 18% EBITDA growth, absorbing greenfield/brownfield ramp-up drags while expanding its bed capacity pipeline.
