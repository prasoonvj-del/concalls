# Dr. Agarwals Health Care Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record facility additions in a single quarter, upwardly revised guidance, and strong financial growth despite start-up losses.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Raised. The target for total facility additions was increased from 60 to 65 (surgical centers raised from 40 to 55; total capex guidance raised from Rs 400 crore to Rs 500 crore).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positively regarding expansion momentum, driven by faster-than-expected ramp-ups in core and new markets like Delhi-NCR and Mumbai.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Regulatory discussions around potential price caps/margins on medical devices and implants (like Intraocular Lenses).
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Delivered record facility additions (18 surgical facilities in Q2, 39 in H1) and progressed on the merger timeline (subsidiary company trading to stop by November 17, 2026).

- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 655 crore (up 31.4% YoY, 6.7% QoQ)
- **Total income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 665 crore (up 31.3% YoY)
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,269 crore (up 28.7% YoY)
- **Total income (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,286 crore (up 27.6% YoY)
- **Ind AS EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 186 crore (up 28.8% YoY; margin broadly stable at ~28%)
- **Ind AS EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 362 crore (up 27% YoY; margin 28.2%)
- **Pre-Ind AS EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 143.2 crore (margin 21.5%) vs. Rs 111.2 crore in Q2 FY26
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 57 crore (up 55.8% YoY; margin 8.5%, up 134 bps YoY)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 112 crore (up 50.1% YoY; margin 8.7%, up 130 bps YoY)
- **Total network:** 324 facilities overall (305 in India across 14 states, 5 UTs, 172 cities; 32% Tier 1, 62% other, 6% international)
- **Patient footfalls (H1 FY27):** ~18.5 lakh patients (~12,300 daily)
- **Surgeries performed (H1 FY27):** >1.86 lakh surgeries (~1,250 daily; up 19% YoY)
- **Cataract surgeries (H1 FY27):** 1,37,170 total (~73% of surgeries); high-end cataract at ~30%
- **Femto (robotic) cataract surgeries (H1 FY27):** 3,284 procedures (up 25.5% YoY)
- **Lenticular/SMILE procedures (H1 FY27):** Up 41.9% YoY
- **Retinal surgeries (H1 FY27):** 7,849 procedures (up 26.5% YoY)
- **Corneal transplants (H1 FY27):** >579 procedures
- **Pinhole Pupilloplasty (PPP) surgeries (H1 FY27):** >540 procedures
- **SSSG (Q2 FY27):** 18.8%
- **SSSG (H1 FY27):** 17.7% (mature cohort prior to FY23 grew 17.2% to Rs 948 crore, contributing 75% of revenues; FY24 vintage grew 20.9% to Rs 96 crore; FY25 vintage grew 67.8% to Rs 127 crore; FY26 vintage generated Rs 71 crore)
- **Region-wise revenue (H1 FY27):** South Rs 790 crore (up 24.6% YoY, 62% share); West Rs 195 crore (up 30% YoY, 15% share); North Rs 115 crore (up 60% YoY, 9% share); East Rs 35 crore (up 30% YoY); Africa operations up 31.1% YoY (10.4% share)

- **Finance cost (H1 FY27):** Rs 46 crore (3.6% of total income)
- **Gross margin (H1 FY27):** 78.9% (up 50 bps YoY; gross profit Rs 1,014 crore, up 28.4% YoY)
- **Doctor and employee costs (H1 FY27):** Broadly stable at 33.8% of total income
- **Other expenses (H1 FY27):** 16.8% of total income

### Highlights
- Added 18 new surgical facilities in Q2 FY27, the highest ever in a single quarter (21 total facilities including 19 greenfields and 2 acquisitions in Delhi NCR).
- Total H1 facility additions reached 39 (34 surgical centers).
- Inaugurated the world's first Pinhole Pupilloplasty (PPP) capable department at the Chennai flagship facility; performed >540 PPP procedures in H1 FY27.
- Completed a strategic bolt-on acquisition in South Delhi and Loni (Ghaziabad) via Business Transfer Agreement (BTA) from Dr. Anchal Gupta.
- Merger update: Subsidiary company trading to stop by November 17, 2026, and operate as a single consolidated entity from November 18, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised guidance for total facility additions to 65 for the full year (including 55 surgical centers and 10 primary centers), up from the previous target of 60 facilities (40 surgical centers).
- Planned H2 facility additions: 26 facilities (including 21 surgical centers: 25 in South, 20 in North, 15 in West, 5 in East).
- Capex guidance revised upwards from Rs 400 crore to Rs 500 crore for the year.
- High-end cataract mix expected to stabilize around 30% to 35% over the next few years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Patient footfalls and surgical volumes rising strongly across both established and new centers.
- Market showing increased receptivity toward advanced refractive procedures (lenticular/SMILE surgeries) over traditional LASIK.
- Premiumization trend supported by rising disposable incomes, higher insurance penetration, and broader coverage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Ind AS EBITDA margins broadly stable at ~28% (28.2% in H1) despite absorbing ramp-up costs from 51 surgical facilities launched over the last 12 months.
- COGS remains stable at ~21.2% to 21.5% of revenue.
- Doctor and employee costs stable at 33.8% of total income.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total network reached 324 facilities (305 operational in India across 172 cities).
- Delhi-NCR network expanded to 15 facilities within 15 months of operations, with another 8 to 12 centers planned over the next 9 to 12 months.
- Greenfield engine has added 185 facilities since FY23.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Senior doctor attrition reported to be very low at 1% to 2% (overall attrition around 14%).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Greenfield start-up losses stood at ~Rs 42 crore for H1 across FY26 and FY27 cohorts.
- Potential regulatory risks and court discussions regarding markup caps or price controls on drugs, medical devices, and intraocular lenses (management noted it is premature to comment).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harith Ahamed (Avendus Spark) asked about the financial profile and rationale of the Delhi acquisition, and the revised capex plan.** Adil Agarwal explained the acquisition comprises 2 centers in Greater Kailash and Loni with Rs 13.5 crore revenue and Rs 7.16 crore EBITDA, acquired for Rs 55 crore (Rs 33 crore upfront, milestones over 4 years). He also confirmed capex is revised to Rs 500 crore for 55 surgical centers. (Management answered directly).
2. **Maulik (360 ONE Capital) asked about the SSSG volume vs. value split and high-end procedure mix.** Rahul Agarwal split SSSG (17.7% H1) into ~8% volume (7.5% OPD, 0.5% conversion) and ~9.5% value (8% premiumization, 1.5% price). Adil Agarwal stated premiumization will likely stabilize at 30% to 35%. (Management answered directly).
3. **Nikhil Upadhyay (SIMPL) asked why management is confident in accelerating facility rollout pace and whether bolt-on acquisitions will continue.** Adil Agarwal and Ashar Agarwal explained that planning occurs 12–18 months in advance with proven playbook execution, successful breakeven in core markets, and strong traction in Delhi-NCR; bolt-on acquisitions will be pursued selectively if valuations and strategic fit are right. (Management answered directly).
4. **Diya Shah (Alder Capital) asked about doctor attrition rates across regions and trainee retention.** Adil Agarwal stated senior doctor attrition is low at 1% to 2% (overall ~14%), and agreed to provide exact region-wise and fellowship retention figures offline. (Management answered directly, partially taking data offline).
5. **Harith Ahamed (Avendus Spark) asked about risks from potential regulatory pricing controls on intraocular lenses and implants.** Rahul Agarwal and another executive noted that while affordability is appreciated, discussions between the Supreme Court and government are ongoing, making it premature to comment on risks or impacts. (Management did not dodge, but stated it is premature).

### One-line takeaway
Dr. Agarwal's Health Care posted robust H1 FY27 performance with 28.7% revenue growth and a record 39 facility additions, prompting management to raise its full-year facility and capex guidance.
