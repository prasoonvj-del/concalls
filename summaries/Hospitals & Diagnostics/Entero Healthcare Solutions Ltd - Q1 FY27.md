# Entero Healthcare Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a strong start to the year with revenue growth, significant margin expansion meeting full-year targets in Q1, and improved return ratios.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: INR 1,940 crores, up 38.2% YoY (40% on a like-for-like basis).
- Organic Revenue Growth: 17.8% reported (19.6% like-for-like).
- Inorganic Revenue Growth: 20.4% YoY.
- Gross Margin: 11.4%, up 147 bps YoY.
- EBITDA Margin: 5.0%, up 143 bps YoY.
- EBITDA: Up 94% YoY.
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 52 crores, up 72% YoY (PAT margin at 2.7%).
- Profit After Tax Attributable to Owners: INR 38 crores, up 37% YoY.
- Non-controlling Interest: INR 14 crores (approx. 27% of profit before minority interest).
- Net Working Capital Days: 61 days (improved from 66 days YoY).
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 21.1% (doubled YoY from 11.5%).
- Return on Equity (ROE): 20.4% (up from 9.0%).
- MedTech Organic Revenue: On track to cross INR 1,000 crores in FY27.
- Low-margin business exits impact on reported growth: Around 2.5%.

### Highlights
- Achieved full-year FY27 EBITDA margin guidance of 5% in the first quarter itself.
- No new acquisitions made in Q1 FY27; stated priority for the year is consolidation, integration, and organic building.
- Conscious exit from certain low-margin businesses continued to release working capital.
- Network scale: Serving over 72,000 retail customers, >2,300 hospital customers, distributing >83,400 SKUs from >3,000 healthcare product manufacturers via 138 warehouses across 475 districts in 19 states.
- Subsidiaries funded via intercompany loans/deposits, which are pulled back as they generate cash flow.

### Guidance & outlook
- Maintained FY27 Guidance: Consolidated revenue growth of approx. 23% YoY (excluding new acquisitions), 5% EBITDA margin, and 50% EBITDA-to-operating cash flow conversion.
- Medium-term Outlook (3-4 years): Management targets sustainable growth in excess of 20% (combined organic and inorganic), with potential ROCE improving to 25%-30% and EBITDA margins >6%.
- MedTech Segment: Targeted to grow at ~20% CAGR over a 3-5 year period.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying pharmaceutical market (IPM) growth has shifted to double-digits (10% to 12%), primarily driven by positive volume growth across all therapy areas.
- Entero's growth continues to outpace IPM due to a unique value proposition (one-stop shop, high fill rates, wide range of 3,000+ companies) and a 2-way network effect.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by scale-led procurement economies, a growing share of MedTech revenue (full commercial/demand-generation role), and exits from low-margin accounts.
- Employee expenses spiked in Q1 due to the annual increment cycle starting April 1; no major further increase expected.
- Interest costs rose from Q4 to Q1 due to the full-quarter impact of ~INR 200 crores debt taken for the last acquisition; expected to remain in the same range.
- Effective income tax slab guidance: 22.5% for FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major acquisitions or heavy capex planned for FY27, though management remains open to compelling opportunities, especially in Q4.
- Small bolt-on acquisition done via Vishal Sai Pharma in Andhra Pradesh to build surgical consumable capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital improved to 61 days.
- Buy-out options for residual minority stakes in subsidiaries have contractually agreed valuation multiples and time horizons spanning 2 to 5 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management as structural risks; general industry risks associated with forecasting IPM growth and cyclicality.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bharat C. Shah (BCS Capital Ideas) on 3-4 year growth without acquisitions:** Management stated they can grow in excess of 20% organically with ROCE reaching 25%-30%. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Akshat Mehta (Seven Rivers Holding) on Q4 to Q1 margin sustainability:** Management explained that Q4 had one-off items that brought down reported margins, making Q1 normalized and sustainable. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Alok Dalal (Jefferies) on IPM growth drivers:** Management noted they cannot isolate a specific reason for the shift to double-digit IPM growth, attributing it to broad-based volume recovery. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Kumar Saurabh (Scientific Investing) on OCF conversion and debt reduction:** Management clarified that 50% OCF conversion means EBITDA minus working capital changes, and excess cash will be deployed into debt payment, minority buyouts, or EPS-accretive investments. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Sidharth Negandhi (CWC) on minority buyout funding:** Management stated they have flexibility to fund minority buyouts from either the parent or subsidiary level based on what makes the most sense. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
Entero Healthcare Solutions delivered a robust Q1 FY27 with a 38.2% YoY revenue growth, achieved its full-year 5% EBITDA margin guidance in Q1 itself, and significantly improved its return ratios (ROCE at 21.1%).
