# Jeena Sikho Lifecare Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasized strong YoY growth, scaling of the preventive healthcare model, and disciplined expansion while urging investors to judge performance on an annual basis rather than quarter-on-quarter.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR224 crores (up 29% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR92 crores (up 17% YoY)
- PAT: INR65 crores
- EBITDA margin: 41%
- PAT margin: 28%
- Panchakarma revenue growth: 13% YoY
- IPD patients volume growth: 33% YoY
- Day care volume growth: 31% YoY
- Ayurveda healthcare product business growth: 47% YoY
- Cash and bank balance (from March balance sheet): INR108 crores
- Operational beds: 2,400 with an occupancy rate of 59%

### Highlights
- Shifted focus from treatment-after-disease to preventive healthcare ("healthspan"), integrating awareness, consultations, OPD, lifestyle coaching, products, and longevity guidance.
- Deliberately scaled down low-margin, high-receivable government credit business (dropping from INR15 crores to INR5 crores in Panchakarma) to build a cash-rich model.
- Entered the ultra-luxury wellness segment under the "Jeena Sikho Premium" (formerly Arjuna Wellness) brand, starting with a property in Manali (108 rooms, 22 villas).
- Total ecosystem reach expanded from 4,87,000 people in Q4 to 5,34,000 people in Q1 across OPD, IPD, daycare, COD, e-commerce, and consultations.
- Upgraded institutional processes, including Salesforce for real-time data, Oracle accounting software, and audits by Grant Thornton and Forvis Mazars.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated long-term targets: 30% YoY revenue growth, reaching INR3,000 crores turnover within 3 to 5 years, scaling to 7,000–10,000 beds, and targeting INR1,000 crores PAT.
- Operational beds guidance for FY27: 3,000 to 3,500 beds.
- Target steady-state financial metrics: EBITDA margin of 40%+ and net profit margin of 27% to 30%.
- Premium Manali wellness center expects 50% occupancy in Year 1 with an ADR of INR32,000 to INR35,000, gross margins of 50%-55%, and 35%-40%+ EBITDA margins; Year 2 occupancy targeted at 60%. Plans to launch 3 to 4 more such luxury wellness centers across India.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rising acceptance of Ayurveda, supported by recent government reminders to health insurance companies to properly reimburse and provide cashless claims for Ayurveda treatments on par with Allopathy.
- Consumers increasingly prefer resort-style healing over strictly clinical environments, demonstrated by strong traction in flagship wellness properties like Meerut.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other income increased to INR14 crores, which included INR7 crores in one-time income (INR5 crores warrant valuation and INR2.5 crores capital gain). Normalized other income is expected to be INR4-5 crores.
- Incurred one-time incremental expenses in Q1: INR4 crores extra on ad campaigns (booked for 6-month integrations in shows like Khatron Ke Khiladi, Anupamaa, Laughter Chefs), INR2 crores for software/retention app development, and ~INR50 lakhs for higher audit fees.
- Luxury wellness lease model has minimalistic operating costs (9% to 10%) as host properties cover F&B, housekeeping, security, and utilities; Jeena Sikho bears only marketing and wages for doctors and healers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 100 operational beds in Q1, taking total operational bed capacity to 2,400.
- Manali luxury wellness center scheduled to start operations around September-October.
- Planning to launch a new chain of daycare centers and introduce discounted pricing in underutilized small hospitals for BPL/Ayushman beneficiaries to improve occupancy and doctor practice.
- OTC products are expanding through e-commerce and upcoming placement across Entero distributors within 15 days, following completed software integrations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Actively reducing exposure to government credit schemes to eliminate stuck receivables and bad debt risk, transitioning to a cash-rich operational model.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government business involves long payment realization cycles, prompting management to deliberately scale it down.
- Regulatory and statutory approvals (e.g., OC, fire, pollution NOCs) can occasionally delay hospital scaling (noted in Panvel).
- Seasonal weather constraints in hill stations like Manali (extreme cold in Jan-Feb).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Karanveer Singh (Nuvama Health) on flat QoQ revenue despite bed additions:** Management clarified that the QoQ dip/flatness was due to deliberately cutting down low-margin government credit business (from INR15cr to INR5cr) and booking upfront seasonal ad expenses, while true private volume grew 10%.
2. **Rusmik (9 Rays EquiResearch) on the surge in other income to INR14 crores:** CFO Nanak Chand explained it included INR7 crores of one-time items (INR5cr warrant valuation, INR2.5cr capital gains), while sustainable other income is INR4-5 crores.
3. **Aditya Chheda (InCred Asset Management) on lower revenue growth relative to IPD/OPD volume growth:** Management stated that ticket sizes were optimized/made more affordable for lower-income brackets, and there is a natural lag between initial consultations and ultimate Panchakarma conversion.
4. **Deepak Poddar on identifying one-off costs in the quarter:** Management detailed INR4 crores in incremental TV/ad commitments, INR2 crores for tech/app software, and ~INR50 lakhs in audit fees.
5. **Naveen Baid on product pricing differences versus competitors like Pet Saffa:** Manish Grover explained that Pet Shuddhi has a 4x higher manufacturing cost because it targets root-cause gut health, liver/spleen metabolism, and includes multiple shots rather than just basic stomach cleaning.

### One-line takeaway
Jeena Sikho Lifecare is intentionally prioritizing cash-generative private healthcare, preventive ecosystems, and asset-light luxury wellness expansion over delayed government credit business, maintaining its 41% EBITDA margin despite upfront tech and advertising investments.
