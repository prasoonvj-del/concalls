# Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY 27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong execution, 26.7% YoY revenue growth, volume growth of 16.5%, and 350 bps YoY expansion in EBITDA margins.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: ~Rs 255 crore (up 26.7% YoY)
- Volume growth: 16.5% YoY
- Existing centers growth: 20% YoY
- Hyderabad growth: >20% YoY (67% of revenue contribution)
- Rest of AP & Telangana revenue contribution: ~20%
- Pune revenue growth: ~15.5% YoY (5% of revenue contribution)
- West Bengal revenue contribution: ~5%
- Remaining geographies revenue contribution: ~3%
- B2C revenue contribution: 92%
- Radiology contribution: ~37%
- Revenue per test: Rs 513
- Revenue per footfall: Rs 1,865
- EBITDA: ~Rs 113 crore (up 37.8% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 44.1% (up 350 bps YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 59 crore (up 37.4% YoY)
- PAT margin: 23.3%
- H1 FY 27 Consolidated revenue: Rs 486 crore (up 24.8% YoY)
- H1 FY 27 EBITDA: Rs 211 crore (up 36% YoY), margin at 43.4%
- H1 FY 27 PAT: Rs 113 crore, margin at 23.1%
- Surplus cash position: ~Rs 355 crore
- Cash PAT: >30%
- ROCE: ~25%
- Return on Equity (ROE): ~20%
- Total network: 168 centres (51 hubs, 117 spokes); Hyderabad has 100 centres (19 hubs, 80+ spokes)

### Highlights
- Delivered strong H1 performance with 24.8% YoY revenue growth and 36% EBITDA growth.
- Commissioned 2 hub centres and 7 spoke centres in H1 FY 27, including the flagship hub at JP Nagar, Bengaluru.
- Planned third flagship diagnostic centre in Western Hyderabad (following Himayatnagar and Panjagutta).
- Entering Northeast region via business transfer agreement to acquire Arya Wellness Centre in Guwahati, Assam (expected completion in next two months; current top line >Rs 25 crore, pre-Ind AS EBITDA >20%).
- PH Pune cluster delivering double-digit growth and EBITDA margins >35%.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY 27 revenue growth guidance: At least ~20% is fairly achievable.
- Long-term 3-5 year sustainable revenue guidance: Mid-teen growth above 15%.
- FY 27 EBITDA margin guidance: 42% and above.
- Expansion target: 8 hubs and 14 spokes by Q1 FY 28 (Estimated capex: Rs 155 crore - Rs 160 crore). Expects 3 more hubs in H2 FY 27, and 3 additional hubs in Q1 FY 28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by 16.5% volume growth, favorable test mix (shift to advanced radiology like CT angio), and CGHS tariff revisions.
- Wellness segment contributes 14.5% to total revenue and grew >25% YoY this quarter.
- Strong demand for advanced modalities and complex cases (stroke, vertigo, cardiac CT).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 65%-70% of expenses are fixed in nature, driving operating leverage as revenue grows beyond 15%-18%.
- EBITDA margin expanded by 350 bps YoY to 44.1%, aided by operating leverage and ~2% benefit from CGHS tariff hikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Estimated capex of Rs 155 crore - Rs 160 crore for 8 hubs and 14 spokes up to Q1 FY 28.
- New Panjagutta central lab with automated track system operationalizing in next few months (Capex: ~Rs 20 crore).
- Purchased Kadapa land in Tier-2 AP for ~Rs 9 crore to set up a hub center in two years.
- Pune expansion: Relocating Satara and Mayur Colony hubs to larger premises. Market Yard hub capex estimated at Rs 18 crore - Rs 20 crore (launching in FY 27); Mayur Colony building purchase for Rs 19 crore (commissioning in FY 28).
- Upgraded 2 CT equipment to high-end 160-slice cardiac CTs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a strong balance sheet with surplus cash of ~Rs 355 crore.
- Strategy remains asset-light (leased premises), though select prime hub locations in metro markets or Tier-2 towns (like Kadapa) may be purchased.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rashmi Shetty (Dolat Capital) asked about sustainability of H1 revenue run rate (25%) in H2.** Management stated full-year growth of at least 20% is achievable, with a long-term sustainable growth guidance above 15%. (Did not dodge).
- **Prince Goenka (Narnolia Financial Services) asked about EBITDA margin drivers and sustainability.** Management attributed it to fixed cost leverage and CGHS tariffs, guiding for 42%+ EBITDA margins for FY 27. (Did not dodge).
- **Surya Narayan Patra (PhillipCapital) asked about the rationale behind owning land versus the asset-light model.** Management clarified that the asset-light lease model remains core, but purchasing premises for 1 out of 10 locations in key metro/Tier-2 markets is done strategically when leases are unavailable. (Did not dodge).
- **Sumit Gupta (Antique Stock Broking) asked about Guwahati (Arya Wellness) margins.** Management stated it currently operates at >20% pre-Ind AS EBITDA and will act as a platform for Northeast expansion. (Did not dodge).
- **Amey Chalke (JM Financial) asked if mom-and-pop shops are reducing in Hyderabad.** Management noted no new large competitor has opened in Hyderabad, and growth is driven by advanced modalities, specialized test offerings, and market share gains from unorganized players. (Did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
Vijaya Diagnostic Centre reported strong Q2 FY 27 performance with 26.7% YoY revenue growth and 350 bps EBITDA margin expansion, backed by robust volume growth, operating leverage, and aggressive cluster-based expansion.
