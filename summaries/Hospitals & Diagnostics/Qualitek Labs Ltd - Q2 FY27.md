# Qualitek Labs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-27 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted robust revenue growth, expanding accreditations, and strong traction in both organic operations and new OPEX-led business models despite seasonal margin moderation.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 72.3 crore (up 33% YoY from Rs 54.2 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Organic Revenue Growth:** 26% YoY (with 7% from M&A)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Moderated compared to H2 FY26 (operating margins reported at 22% in H1 FY27 vs 26% in H2 FY26)
- **CAPEX Invested:** Rs 35 crore invested in H1 (aligned with broader plans)
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** $3.2 billion (out of a $9 billion Indian TIC market)
- **Revenue Mix:** 82% from testing and R&D, 18% from CAPEX-light / OPEX models
- **Pipeline:** Rs 25 crore+ order pipeline for the industrial inspection business
- **Long-term Target:** 4x revenue growth by FY30 compared to FY26 revenue of Rs 125 crore

### Highlights
- Delivered consolidated H1 FY27 revenue of Rs 72.3 crore, driven by strong growth in the transport and food segments, alongside double growth in the EWS (Environment, Water and Sustainability) vertical.
- Acquired a USFDA pharmaceutical lab from Wipro in Bangalore via an asset purchase in April; after 5 months of compliance and method validation, commercial operations start in December.
- Secured NABCB accreditation for ISO 17020 (inspection body) and won major inspection orders, including a Rs 9 crore order from a big Indian company over 3 years and an order from GSW.
- Expanded geographical presence to 11 owned labs and 8 PPP (Public-Private Partnership) labs across India.
- *Record check:* The call confirms the planned capex and Rs 35 crore investment in H1, aligning with our earlier record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year revenue growth in the range of 34% - 36%.
- Maintained long-term guidance to achieve a 4x revenue increase by the end of FY30 compared to FY26 revenue of Rs 125 crore.
- Anticipates EBITDA margins to continue in the 25% - 26% range near-term, with potential to reach ~30% and PAT of 18% - 20% once revenue scales beyond Rs 300 - Rs 400 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian TIC (Testing, Inspection, and Certification) industry is growing at 7% - 10% YoY, driven by stringent regulations, quality assurance focus, and the "China Plus One" manufacturing shift.
- The company acts as an R&D and technology partner for automotive OEMs like Tata Motors, Mahindra, MG Motor, GSW, and global brands like VW Skoda, Stellantis, and Suzuki.
- EWS business is expanding rapidly, securing preferred vendor status for IOCL's Panipat expansion plant and HPCL in Mumbai.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 profitability moderated because annual salary increments (10.5% - 11%) and fixed expense increases (rent, AMC, CMC) take effect at the start of the financial year in April.
- OPEX-led inspection model avoids depreciation and interest costs, yielding higher PBT/operating margins (up to 30%).
- High-end equipment imports for potential future projects like EMI/EMC testing have faced higher capital costs due to USD appreciation against the Indian Rupee.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested Rs 35 crore in H1 FY27 toward maintenance capex and new facilities (including the USFDA lab in Bangalore and Panchkula lab upgrades).
- Planned future expansions include setting up an automobile lab presence in Chennai (South India) and exploring cybersecurity acquisitions and European/Southeast Asian footprints.
- EMI/EMC lab setup (estimated above Rs 30 crore) has been temporarily slowed down due to currency movements affecting import prices, while testing is currently handled via outsourcing and basic Pune lab scope.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital is elevated primarily due to delayed receivables from the government sector (which contributes ~20% of revenue and takes 3 to 6 months to pay).
- Fundraise of over Rs 40 crore is awaiting BSE approval, with funds expected in December to be utilized for expansion, South India auto lab setup, and potential cybersecurity acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government receivables take 3 to 6 months to realize, keeping working capital stretched.
- Currency fluctuations (USD appreciation vs INR) increase the cost of importing high-end testing equipment (e.g., EMI/EMC labs).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rohit Singh asked about the contribution of M&A vs. organic growth and moderation in H1 margins.** Management clarified that base revenue grew strongly from Rs 29 crore at IPO to Rs 90 crore organically, while H1 margins dipped seasonally due to April annual salary increments (10.5% - 11%) and fixed cost resets.
2. **Shubham Agarwal asked about the fundraise utilization and EMI/EMC lab status.** Management stated the Rs 40+ crore fundraise is expected in December for South India auto expansion and cybersecurity acquisitions; EMI/EMC investment has been paced slower due to currency-driven import cost increases.
3. **Rajesh Kumar asked about the asset-light OPEX model impact on return ratios.** Management noted that OPEX-led inspection avoids depreciation and interest drag, supporting higher operating margins of up to 30%.
4. **Karan Singh questioned competitive moats against entrenched MNCs.** Management responded that technical capabilities, accreditations (like USFDA), identical high-end machinery, and industry-veteran talent allow Qualitek to match MNC standards.
5. **Rahul Jain asked why working capital is high.** Management explained it is driven by government receivables, which account for ~20% of revenue and experience longer payment cycles.

### One-line takeaway
Qualitek Labs reported a robust 33% YoY revenue growth to Rs 72.3 crore in H1 FY27, backed by strong organic traction and strategic CAPEX-light expansions, while managing seasonal margin moderation and working capital pressures.
