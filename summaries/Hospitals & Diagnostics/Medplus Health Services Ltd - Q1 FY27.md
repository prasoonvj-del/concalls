# Medplus Health Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-24 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management faced strong pushback on capex plans and margin pressures from lower private label mix, franchise dilution, and higher labor costs, though core store additions remain on track.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR18,796 million
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA:** INR651 million (3.5% margin)
- **Pharmacy Operating EBITDA:** INR588 million (3.2% margin)
- **Pharmacy Revenue YoY Growth:** 21.8%
- **Store Network:** 5,476 stores across 2.9 million+ sq. ft. (average size 539 sq. ft.)
- **Net Store Additions:** 146 stores (222 gross additions, 52 closures including 9 relocations and 27 franchisee outlets; 24 COCO stores in conversion to franchise)
- **Private Label Sales Contribution:** 20% (Pharma 10.7%, Non-pharma 9.3%)
- **Stores Older than 12 Months Revenue:** INR16,436 million (94% of pharmacy revenue) with store-level EBITDA margin of 10.4%
- **Diagnostics Revenue:** INR370.8 million (up from INR302.9 million in Q1 FY26) with operating EBITDA of INR65.9 million (up from INR41.3 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Net Working Capital:** 54 days (Warehouse inventory: 33 days; First-year store inventory: 100 days; Stores >12 months inventory: 36 days)
- **Private Label Memberships:** ~4.4 to 4.5 million active members (membership fee increased from INR99 to INR149)
- **Promoter Debt:** ~INR1,150 crores (including interest)

### Highlights
- Management decided to put all approved non-core capex proposals (food park, oil extraction unit, and wellness/concierge services facility) on hold following market and investor feedback.
- Core business capex (warehousing, stores) will continue without interruption.
- Effective July 7, the company tweaked its discount structure, reducing the 20% discount to 19% for purchases greater than INR1,000 (excluding UPI payments) to mitigate margin pressures.
- Retention bonus plan for new store employees stopped effective March 1 to counter rising minimum wages in Karnataka (60% increase) and Telangana (25%+ increase).
- 24 COCO stores are being converted to franchises as part of an entrepreneurship retention strategy for senior staff (3+ years tenure).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the outlook for adding 800 net new stores (including franchisee stores) in FY27.
- Private label share is expected to recover, building up at a rate of 0.3% to 0.5% per quarter going forward.
- Management deferred full-year margin and financial guidance revisions to the next quarter after assessing the impact of recent countermeasures.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Private label non-pharma saw a steeper sequential fall due to temporary supply disruptions in categories like diapers, which are now coming back on track.
- Franchisee model is experiencing some attrition and expectation mismatches among first-time entrepreneurs, requiring ongoing model adjustments.
- Quick commerce competition is heavily cash-bleeding and subsidized; management stated they are happy to compete if customers pay delivery costs, but do not view it as an immediate threat to pharmacy dynamics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin contracted sequentially by ~200 bps due to: (1) 100 bps from lower private label mix, (2) 70 bps from normal inventory provisioning (compared to a low base in Q4 FY26 due to liquidating provisioned stock), and (3) 40 bps from the absence of year-end supplier discounts.
- Employee costs rose due to full-quarter impacts of warehouse additions and sharp minimum wage hikes in Karnataka and Telangana.
- Annual inventory provisioning charge runs at roughly 0.8% to 1% of total sales (up to 5% considered normal for private label due to make-to-order obsolescence risk).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex for the food park and wellness facility put on hold indefinitely.
- Expansion of the diagnostics radiology business will not be significantly scaled up due to lower-than-expected subscription ramp-up in B2B/B2C.
- Core pharmacy expansion continues contiguously into Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, and Kerala.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital stood at 54 days.
- Balance sheet remains debt-free at the company level, with strong cash flows and evaluation of buybacks/dividends ongoing.
- Promoter debt against pledged shares stands at ~INR1,150 crores including interest.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Permanent wage inflation in key states (Karnataka, Telangana).
- Franchisee churn and expectation mismatches leading to outlet closures.
- Dilution in gross margins from lower private label penetration and higher franchisee mix.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sudarshan Agarwal (Axis Capital):** Asked about the rationale for putting the food park and wellness capex on hold. *Management answered that they respected investor and market feedback against non-core adjacencies and paused the capex indefinitely.*
2. **Saion Mukherjee (Nomura):** Inquired about employee cost inflation and attrition mitigation. *Management highlighted that the employee retention bonus plan was frozen for new hires from March 1, and wage hikes are being absorbed via structural incentives.*
3. **Divyansh Gupta (Latent PMS):** Questioned the closure of early-age franchise stores. *Management acknowledged expectation mismatches among first-time entrepreneurial franchisees and stated they are tweaking the model to speed up breakevens.*
4. **Jasdeep Walia (Clockvine Capital):** Asked about scaling the diagnostics business and private label inventory provisions. *Management stated diagnostics expansion is capped due to slow subscription growth, while private label provisioning is maintained at ~5% as a normal make-to-order risk.*
5. **Anil Sarin (K16 Advisors):** Asked about the sustainability of margin contractions and promoter pledge levels. *Management explained the specific drivers of the 200 bps gross margin dip and noted that promoter debt (~INR1,150 crores) is handled by the family office.*

### One-line takeaway
MedPlus delivered strong 21.8% YoY pharmacy revenue growth in Q1 FY27 but faced margin contraction from lower private label mix, franchise dilution, and wage inflation, prompting management to halt non-core capex and tweak discount policies.
