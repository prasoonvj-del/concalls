# Laxmi Dental Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and robust growth across core dental segments.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 79.5 crore (up from INR 72.3 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 154.2 crore (vs INR 137.9 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Gross Profit:** INR 61.2 crore (up 23.5% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 76.9% (vs 68.5% in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** INR 15.3 crore (up 38.5% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 19.2% (vs 15.3% in Q2 FY26)
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 16.0 crore (includes recorded EBITDA, 60% of Kids-e-Dental PAT, 49% of IDBG AI Dental PAT, and ESOP expenses)
- **PAT:** INR 10.0 crore (up 17.6% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 12.6% (vs 11.8% in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 29.6 crore (margin 19.2%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 20.4 crore (margin 13.2%)
- **Dental Lab Segment Growth:** 22.7% YoY (International lab up 32%, Domestic lab up 15%)
- **Aligner Solutions Growth:** 23.5% YoY (Bizdent up 27.7%, Vedia up 19.1%)
- **Kids-e-Dental Growth:** 16.7% YoY
- **Scanner Sales Contribution:** INR 3.5 crore (vs INR 8.9 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Cash and Bank Balances (incl. FD/investments):** INR 97.0 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **H1 FY27 Capex:** INR 16.7 crore

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA.
- Shareholders approved updating objects and extending timelines for unutilized IPO funds of INR 48.1 crore: >INR 27 crore for land acquisition and building construction for labs/aligner facilities, and >INR 21 crore for machinery, computer equipment, and movable assets (to be deployed across FY27 and FY28).
- Digital penetration in domestic lab business reached around 78% (medium-term target >90%).
- Received a tariff refund of approximately INR 5.5 crore (excluding interest), to be reflected in Q3 FY27 results.
- Maintained a debt-free balance sheet.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management plans to transition from leased premises in Mira Road to a self-owned, purpose-built horizontally integrated facility by the end of FY28.
- International expansion strategy includes evaluating opening a semi-automated lab in the US.
- Management remains confident about medium to long-term demand and scaling capabilities.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was broad-based across dental labs, aligner solutions, and pediatric dental segments.
- Domestic demand is driven by increasing digital adoption and the hub-and-spoke model.
- Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities are showing strong growth in aligners due to affordability, phased delivery options, and public awareness campaigns (featuring brand ambassador Kareena Kapoor).
- International momentum reflects strong relationships across the USA, UK, and Europe, with upcoming participation in the International Dental Show in Cologne, Germany.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion to 76.9% driven primarily by a favorable product mix.
- Other expenses stood at INR 14.8 crore, driven by business promotion, freight costs, and AI-led automation spend.
- Kids-e-Dental share of profits dropped due to the expiry of the 3-year startup tax benefit (now paying regular tax).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Deployed INR 16.7 crore in H1 FY27 capex.
- Unutilized IPO proceeds (INR 48.1 crore) to be deployed across FY27 and FY28 for land, construction, machinery, and equipment.
- New facility in India is planned to be around 1.25 to 1.5 lakh square feet (3x to 3.5x the current size).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free.
- Cash, bank balances, FDs, and investments stood at INR 97.0 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Receivable credit terms generally range between 30 to 60 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Laptop prices for scanners have doubled, pushing adoption costs higher and prompting the introduction of a subscription model.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Akshay Thakur (Helios Capital):** Asked about differentiation in Tier 2/3 cities and overseas lab strategy. Management explained the use of phased aligner delivery and brand campaigns, and clarified that overseas labs will be hybrid models utilizing Indian digital infrastructure for design/entry and local assembly for physical touchpoints.
2. **Varth Sanghavi (DYDX Advisors):** Inquired about Kids-e-Dental profit drop and scanner subscription structure. Management clarified the drop was due to the lapse of a 3-year tax holiday, and detailed the subscription model as INR 50,000 deposit, INR 3,000/month subscription + GST, and a commitment of INR 25,000 monthly business.
3. **Sanjay Chawla (Fintech/Equity Analyst):** Asked about constant currency international growth and gross margin drivers. Management stated international constant currency growth is roughly 8% to 10% and noted gross margins are stable around 77% with future margin expansion primarily driven by people and process efficiency.
4. **Tushar Manudhane (Motilal Oswal):** Asked if scanners under subscription can be reallocated and whether existing buyers can switch. Management confirmed lock scanners can be reallocated if minimum commitments aren't met, and noted that no existing buyers have requested a switch to subscription.
5. **Lakshmi Narayan (Durga Investment):** Asked about hospital vs. clinic mix and debtor days. Management stated clinics constitute the vast majority (>99%) and credit terms are 30–60 days.

### One-line takeaway
Laxmi Dental reported its highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA in Q2 FY27, backed by strong growth in core dental labs and aligners, while introducing a scanner subscription model and progressing on its self-owned manufacturing infrastructure plans.
