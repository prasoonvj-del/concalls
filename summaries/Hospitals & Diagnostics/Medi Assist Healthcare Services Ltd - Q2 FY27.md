### Highlights
- Paramount acquisition integration fully complete; all group customers migrated to Medi Assist platform and all claims running on MAtrix.
- Capital allocation framework established: returning ~50% of cumulative adjusted free cash flow over rolling 5-year periods via dividends and buybacks; board approved buyback of INR49.3 crores at INR425/share.
- Managed INR15,104 crores of premiums in India TPA (up 18.8% YoY) and INR9,648 crores on the platform model (up 24.8% YoY).
- Group market share improved to 34.1%; retention rate held at 90.5% in H1, expected to return to historical 93%-94% in 3-4 quarters.
- Processed over 7 million claims in H1 via platform; AI-powered platforms handling over a million claims a month.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects retention rates to return to historical levels of 93%-94% in 3 to 4 quarters.
- Management expects same-store growth to continue in the 8% to 9% range.
- Steady-state EBITDA margins are viewed as 22%-23% in the core business, with a focus to return there.
- No specific revenue guidance provided; company expects to grow at par or faster than the market from a premium share perspective.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry group premiums in H1 grew at ~15.8% (GI Council numbers); Medi Assist grew over 21% inclusive of acquisitions.
- IT-ITeS sector is not a net contributor to employee headcount growth currently, leading to a timing lag, but expansion seen in manufacturing, consumer goods, and oil & gas.
- Nearly a third of India's health insurance premiums are served by the platform (TPA and platform models combined).
- GST-related announcements allowing corporates input tax credit (ITC) on group health insurance may drive benefit expansion and 9-10% same-store growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin recovery (reaching 20.4% in Q2) driven by Paramount integration completion, headcount rationalization (~800+ lower headcount since acquisition), and AI tool deployment.
- Revenue per average headcount (excluding government business) annualized at 13.7 lakhs (up 4.5%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Mayfair expanding its international footprint; platform live in Southeast Asia.
- 4 new insurers went live on the platform in Q2, with incremental contracted premiums of INR758 crores for H1.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong, debt-free (zero debt) balance sheet maintained.
- Contract liabilities increased to INR321.7 crores from INR280.2 crores at FY26 end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cyber incident in Paramount systems last year (December) impacted renewals from January through April.
- Churn in the tail-end of the integrated Paramount portfolio and installment premium accounting impacts near-term retention and revenue recognition lag.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Navid Virani (Bastion Research) on organic growth lag and industry comparison:** Management explained that accounting revenue translation lags premium growth by 2-3 quarters, and retention dipped due to Paramount churn and cyber incidents, which will normalize in 3-4 quarters.
- **Vikas Sharda (NT Asset Management) on Q2 TPA revenue growth slowing to low single digits:** Management clarified that 1/365 revenue booking and timing lags from Q1 losses affect current numbers, but 80% of lost retention was replaced by new business additions.
- **Prithvish Uppal (Nuvama) on IRDA EOM consultation paper impact:** Management stated that TPA fees are transparently part of policyholder-grossed premiums, and EOM rules could present opportunities for insurers to outsource basic claim operations and leverage AI-based claim controls.
- **Dhiraj Kaswan on technology segment and SaaS revenue contribution:** Management noted that bulk of reported tech revenues are pure SaaS (MAtrix base of ~INR100-120 per claim), while value-adding bundles (fraud, auto-adjudication) represent incremental revenue upside of 5 to 10 times per claim.
- **Mihir Shah (Meritus Capital) on group retention rates despite tech investments:** Management explained that strict tracking includes companies shutting down or moving to ESIC, and deliberate commercial exits of non-viable accounts, but retention is expected to return to 93-94%.

### One-line takeaway
Medi Assist completed its Paramount integration, delivering a 20.4% Q2 EBITDA margin and announcing a buyback, while positioning its AI platform and international business for structural growth.
