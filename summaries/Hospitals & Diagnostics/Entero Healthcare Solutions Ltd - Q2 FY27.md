# Entero Healthcare Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (32.9% YoY) and margin expansion (135 bps), with management confident of exceeding full-year guidance despite a one-off customer default provision.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management stated they are in a "comfortable position to deliver better" than the stated FY27 guidance (23% revenue growth, 5% EBITDA margin), though they did not provide specific revised figures.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive; management noted IPM growth is currently high (~15%) but expressed uncertainty regarding the sustainability of this specific rate.
- **New topics:** Disclosed a one-off provision of Rs 12 crore due to a customer default in a subsidiary.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on margin expansion; working capital was temporarily impacted by a drought-related government payment delay (Rs 55 crore received in Nov).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,087 crore (+32.9% YoY).
- **Gross Margin:** 11.5% (+126 bps YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 5.3% (+135 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 61 crore (+66% YoY).
- **ROCE:** 22.0%; **ROE:** 22.4%.
- **NWC Days:** 64 days (would be 61 days adjusted for the one-off government payment delay).

### Highlights
- **Organic Growth:** 18.2% (20% on a like-for-like basis).
- **Inorganic Growth:** 14.7% (calendarisation of previous acquisitions).
- **MedTech:** Revenue contribution is ~15% of total revenue.
- **One-off:** Rs 12 crore provision taken in Q2 due to a customer default in a subsidiary; fully provided in "Other Expenses".
- **Government Business:** Represents ~3% of total revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Guidance:** Management is confident of delivering better than the stated 23% revenue growth, 5% EBITDA margin, and 50% EBITDA-to-OCF conversion.
- **M&A:** Evaluating multiple opportunities; targeting deals >Rs 100 crore. Management is hopeful to close a deal this year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **IPM Growth:** Currently high (~15%), driven by volume growth; management is unsure if this rate is sustainable.
- **Trade Margin Caps:** Management sees no material impact from the MRP reduction on 137 molecules, as they were not capturing those margins (hospitals/manufacturers were).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margin Expansion Levers:** Scale-led procurement, growing MedTech share, and exit from low-margin accounts.
- **Interest Cost:** Expected to decrease from Q3 as debt repayment commences.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Acquisitions:** No new acquisitions in H1 FY27.
- **Strategy:** Focus on MedTech and portfolio expansion; not evaluating small acquisitions due to current scale.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Working Capital:** NWC days were 64 (impacted by Rs 55 crore government payment delay in Maharashtra; received in Nov).
- **Debt:** Mandate to keep net debt to EBITDA <2x.
- **Cash Deployment:** Priority is Growth/M&A > Debt repayment > Minority buyouts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Customer Default:** Rs 12 crore provision taken in Q2.
- **Working Capital:** Temporary delay in government payments (drought-related).
- **Regulatory:** Potential impact of trade margin caps (management downplayed this).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit):** Asked about upgrading guidance. Management: Will "better" the guidance but not giving specific numbers yet.
- **Govindarajan Chellappa:** Asked about the Rs 12 crore write-off. Management: Confirmed it was a customer default in a subsidiary; fully provided in Q2.
- **Yash Mehta:** Asked about M&A size. Management: Not looking at <Rs 100 crore deals; evaluating large opportunities.
- **Alok Dalal:** Asked about organic growth sustainability. Management: Confirmed high-teens/20%+ growth is the endeavor even without acquisitions.

### One-line takeaway
Entero delivered strong Q2 results with 32.9% revenue growth and margin expansion, and management is confident of exceeding full-year guidance despite a one-off customer default provision.
