# Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-08 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and successful deleveraging via QIP, with management emphasizing the stabilization of recently commissioned assets.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Revenue:** Rs 1,196 crore (+36.1% YoY, +10.3% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 240 crore (+20.1% YoY, +10.9% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 20.1% (vs 20.27% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 37 crore (vs Rs 85 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 505 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** ~Rs 2,400 crore (post-QIP repayment).

### Highlights
*   **Capital Raise:** Successfully raised Rs 1,500 crore via QIP; Rs 1,100 crore utilized to reduce debt. Promoters infused Rs 600 crore via preferential allotment (25% upfront).
*   **New Units:** Opened a new unit in Palakkad, Kerala. Kondapur unit (new) grew 40% in its first month (July).
*   **Operational Milestones:** Mahadevapura (Bangalore) achieved EBITDA breakeven.
*   **Clinical Achievements:** KIMS Nagpur performed the first robotic implantation of the Perceval Plus sutureless aortic valve in the Asia-Pacific region.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management aims for 20% YoY growth in clusters.
*   **Margins:** Management targets 30-35% EBITDA margins for the Telangana cluster long-term.
*   **Cluster Targets:** Management expects the Kerala cluster to reach mid-teens EBITDA next year, stabilizing at 20-22% in 2-3 years. The Bangalore cluster is targeted to be EBITDA neutral for the full year.
*   **Kondapur:** Management targets 30% EBITDA margin at full scale (Rs 1,200 crore revenue potential).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Volumes:** IP volumes grew 26.6% YoY; OP volumes grew 28.5% YoY.
*   **ARPOB:** Average revenue per operating bed grew 9.7% YoY.
*   **Insurance:** Management noted that 50% of insurance empanelments for newer assets (Thane, Nashik, Bangalore) are complete, with the remainder expected by mid-September.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margin saw a slight compression YoY (20.1% vs 20.27%).
*   **Operating Costs:** Preoperative costs for Kondapur were Rs 1.5-2 crore/month. The old Kondapur hospital incurs ~Rs 90 lakh/month in rent plus Rs 3-4 crore/year in operating expenses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Maintenance capex guided at Rs 100 crore/year. Additional Rs 60-75 crore planned for the Rajahmundry facility. Total capex for the next 9 months estimated at Rs 100-125 crore.
*   **Strategy:** Management is prioritizing the stabilization of current assets before pursuing new greenfield projects in FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Debt reduced from Rs 3,250 crore (Mar '26) to ~Rs 2,400 crore (post-QIP).
*   **Receivables:** Management noted that government receivables are showing a positive trend.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Ramp-up:** Management highlighted the need to ramp up newer units (Thane, Electronic City) to mitigate financial drag.
*   **Doctor Alignment:** Management noted that the transition of doctors to a full-time practice model in Maharashtra is slower compared to the South.
*   **Old Assets:** The old Kondapur hospital remains a temporary drag on margins due to rental and operating costs until it is shut down (expected in ~6 months).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the discrepancy in occupancy vs. bed count?** A: Management clarified that Kondapur beds were added late in Q1, and Secunderabad beds are currently under renovation, which skews the occupancy percentage.
*   **Q: Why is Thane ramp-up slower than other units?** A: Management cited Maharashtra's specific doctor practice culture (part-time to full-time transition) and empanelment delays as the primary reasons.
*   **Q: What is the long-term margin target for Kondapur?** A: Management targets 30% EBITDA margin at full scale (Rs 1,200 crore revenue).
*   **Q: Will you reduce debt further?** A: Management stated they intend to deploy internal accruals for growth/capex rather than aggressive debt reduction, aiming to maintain a 2.5:1 debt-equity ratio.

### One-line takeaway
KIMS is prioritizing the stabilization and ramp-up of recently commissioned assets (Kondapur, Bangalore, Kerala) while utilizing QIP proceeds to deleverage the balance sheet, with a long-term focus on maintaining 20%+ growth.
