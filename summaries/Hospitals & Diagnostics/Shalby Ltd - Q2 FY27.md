# Shalby Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy improvement across financial KPIs, occupancy gains, and continued positive EBITDA in the MedTech and Gurugram units with a reduction in net debt.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 318 crore (up ~9.8% YoY from Rs 290 crore)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 46.4 crore (up 0.8% YoY from Rs 46.1 crore)
- Consolidated EBITDA margin: 14.6% (vs 15.9% YoY)
- Consolidated PBT: Rs 16.5 crore (vs Rs 19.1 crore YoY); PBT margin: 5.2%
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 7.9 crore (flat YoY); PAT margin: 2.5%
- Standalone revenue: Rs 250 crore (up 9.1% YoY from Rs 229 crore)
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 45.7 crore (vs Rs 44.8 crore YoY); margin: 18.2% (vs 19.5% YoY)
- Standalone PBT: Rs 31.6 crore; margin: 12.6%
- Standalone PAT: Rs 23.5 crore (up from Rs 19.8 crore YoY); margin: 9.4% (vs 8.6% YoY)
- Standalone debt: Rs 14.5 crore (down from Rs 54.5 crore YoY)
- Gearing ratio: 0.45x; Net debt: approx Rs 458 crore (decreased YoY)
- Annualized ROCE (standalone): 9.4%
- ARPOB (standalone): Rs 42,581 (up 4.3% YoY from Rs 40,794)
- ALOS (standalone): 3.58 days (vs 3.74 days YoY)
- Occupancy (standalone): 52% (up 400 bps YoY from 48%); excluding Gurgaon, it was 55% (vs 50% YoY)
- Payer mix: 33% self-pay, 38% insurance, 29% government payers
- Gurgaon unit (Shalby International) revenue: approx Rs 30 crore (up ~50% YoY from Rs 20 crore); ARPOB: Rs 96,388 (up 18% YoY); ALOS: 3.3 days
- MedTech consolidated revenue: Rs 316 million; SMTL (India) revenue: Rs 224 million (up 40% YoY); SAT Inc. (US) revenue: Rs 286 million (up 4% YoY)
- MedTech consolidated EBITDA: Rs 3.6 million (double Q1's Rs 1.6 million)
- MedTech inventory days: 784 days (down 16% in H1; down 20% YoY over last 2 years)
- H1 Capex: Rs 10 crore (vs Rs 160 crore last year)

### Highlights
- Hospital growth was broad-based, with faster growth in nephrology, urology, oncology, general surgery, and critical care; joint replacement remains flagship.
- Clinical milestones: treated family member of Kenya's Chief of Defense Forces for hip replacement; former Nigerian President returned for a second knee replacement; Gujarat's first telerobotic surgery performed at SG Highway Hospital, Ahmedabad.
- Transplant program active across 5 hospitals (Ahmedabad, Indore, Jaipur, Surat, Delhi NCR).
- Shalby Academy added several hundred new students/trainees across nursing, physiotherapy, allied health, and hospital management.
- MedTech unit delivered its 5th consecutive quarter of positive EBITDA; reinstated internal passivation line and recommissioned secondary clean line.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects double-digit top-line growth for FY27, supported by H2 momentum.
- Management expects a relatively better EBITDA margin than the previous year and strong bounce in the coming quarters.
- Gurugram unit expects cash breakeven around exit of Q4 and PBT neutrality with an additional 20%-30% growth.
- MedTech new supply chain benefits expected to start accruing in P&L from Q4 with full quarter impact in Q1 FY28.
- Lower capex going forward (restricted to replacement capex) to strengthen cash flows and support debt reduction.
- Effective Tax Rate (ETR) expected around 45%-50% for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Payer mix remains stable at 33% self-pay, 38% insurance, and 29% government.
- MedTech products seeing strong reception among surgeons in India and outside.
- NPPA price hike expected in mid-November, pending for the last 2 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Outsourced manpower efficiencies already reflecting in H1.
- Materials and consumables cost-saving initiatives expected to flow into P&L from Q3 onwards.
- MedTech new supply chain model expected to significantly reduce cost of goods sold (COGS) starting Q4.
- Government oncology price capping (180 molecules effective mid-November) expected to have an insignificant impact as most surgeries are packaged and medicines are not sold separately.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex restricted to Rs 10 crore (primarily replacement capex).
- FSI utilization at Gurugram hospital to add more beds, operational in the next 12-18 months.
- Mumbai expansion delayed significantly due to pending approvals and working with trustees.
- 80%-90% of new capital going forward will be invested in the hospital business.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt decreased during the period to approx Rs 458 crore.
- Standalone debt declined to Rs 14.5 crore from Rs 54.5 crore YoY.
- MedTech inventory days reduced to 784 days from ~1,200 days a year ago.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Mumbai expansion delayed due to pending approvals.
- Currency headwinds (stronger US dollar) impacted quantum of net debt reduction.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Moksh Morbia (Prospero Tree) asked why management continues to invest in the implant subsidiary given historical losses instead of focusing purely on the hospital business.** Shanay Shah answered that MedTech COGS has halved and will drop another 30%-40% with the new supply chain; however, 80%-90% of fresh capital moving forward will be deployed in the hospital business.
- **Moksh Morbia asked when the Gurugram facility will contribute positively at the PBT level.** Amit Kumar and Shanay Shah replied that with an additional 20%-30% growth from current levels (following a 50% YoY revenue jump), the unit will be PBT neutral, expecting cash breakeven near Q4 exit.
- **Kashish Thakur (Elara Securities) asked about the impact of oncology drug price capping.** Amit Kumar and Shanay Shah stated the impact will be insignificant because most surgeries are done as fixed-price packages where medicines are not billed separately.
- **Supraja (Cuz Limited) asked about the 2-3 year road map and debt reduction for the MedTech business.** Shanay Shah and Amit Kumar noted that debt will continue to decline due to lower cash requirements, improved EBITDA, and utilization of existing inventory rather than adding new product pipelines.
- **Kashish Thakur asked about the ETR and capex guidance for FY27.** Amit Kumar guided H2 capex to remain similar to H1 (around Rs 10 crore total for H1, restricted to replacement) and ETR to settle around 45%-50%.

### One-line takeaway
Shalby delivered stable Q2 FY27 performance with 9.8% consolidated revenue growth and 52% standalone occupancy, while prioritizing internal hospital efficiencies, curbing capex, and targeting MedTech supply chain cost reductions to drive debt reduction.
