# Global Health Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial and operational performance, robust volume growth, and Noida hospital achieving EBITDA breakeven within a year of launch.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** INR 14,690 million (up 34% YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA:** INR 3,701 million (up 60% YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 25.2% (compared to 21% in Q2 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 2,191 million (up 38% YoY)
- **Net Cash Surplus:** INR 7,815 million as of September 30, 2026 (compared to INR 5,906 million as of March 31, 2026)
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flows:** INR 5,678 million
- **Inpatient Volumes:** Increased by 30% YoY
- **Outpatient Volumes:** Increased by 38% YoY
- **Occupied Bed Days:** Increased by 22% YoY
- **Network Occupancy:** 67% on expanded bed capacity (compared to 64% in Q2 FY26)
- **Census Bed Capacity:** 3,115 beds (up 17% YoY from 2,658)
- **Total Bed Capacity:** 3,811 beds (compared to 3,435 in Q2 FY26)
- **Average Revenue Per Patient (ARPP):** INR 2.06 lakhs (up 3% YoY)
- **Average Length of Stay (ALOS):** 2.89 days (compared to 3.06 days in Q2 FY26)
- **International Patient Revenue:** INR 993 million (up 30% YoY)
- **OPD Pharmacy Revenue:** INR 699 million (up 53% YoY)
- **Noida Hospital Q2 Performance:** Revenue of INR 1,059 million; Operating EBITDA of INR 86 million
- **Cluster 1 Revenue:** INR 8,229 million (up 18% YoY); Operating EBITDA of INR 1,810 million (up 29% YoY; 22% margin)
- **Cluster 2 Revenue:** INR 5,790 million (up 63% YoY); Operating EBITDA of INR 1,837 million (up 110% YoY)
- **Ghaziabad Land Parcel Acquisition:** 2.61 acres for INR 166 crores; total capex approved at approximately INR 1,000 crores for a 500-bed hospital

### Highlights
- Noida hospital achieved EBITDA breakeven in Q2 FY27 within a year of inauguration.
- Acquired a 2.61-acre land parcel in Ghaziabad via e-auction for INR 1,000 crore 500-bed hospital project.
- Added 74 operational beds during the quarter (49 in Noida, 25 in Lucknow), bringing total operational capacity to 3,811 beds.
- Enhanced clinical infrastructure by adding Siemens ARTIS icono biplane system at Gurugram, Siemens ARTIS ceiling-mounted cath lab and VELYS hip navigation system at Patna, and onboarding over 100 doctors (including over 65 clinicians).
- Modified financial reporting format to "revenue from operations" (excluding other/interest income) and "operating EBITDA" (excluding other income, after ESOP expenses).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management plans to add approximately 3,450 beds over the next few years, nearly doubling network capacity to over 7,600 beds.
- Planned capital expenditure of over INR 6,000 crores over the next 4 to 5 years to support upcoming hospitals, capacity expansions, and supporting infrastructure.
- Management reiterated focus on three priorities: strengthening existing hospitals, accelerating scale-up of developing hospitals, and executing the expansion pipeline.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Witnessed sustained healthy demand across all specialties, growing proportion of complex cases, and increased inpatient/outpatient volumes.
- Management stated that recent regulatory updates on oncology drug margin capping are still evolving (matters at court and government levels), making it too early to evaluate direct financial impact; philosophy remains aligned with expanding access and affordability.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin expanded to 25.2% from 21% YoY, driven by operating leverage, improved scale, and Noida breakeven.
- Cluster 1 operating margin stood at 22%; Cluster 2 margins benefited from the absence of corporate cost allocations (which are loaded onto Gurugram/Cluster 1).
- ARPP/ARPOB movements remain stable with nominal 1-3% changes, driven by mix and ALOS rather than aggressive tariff hikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expansion pipeline includes 6 new greenfield hospitals.
- South Delhi (GK) project progressing well (subject to potential short-term pollution-linked construction bans).
- Guwahati and Mumbai projects have received approvals; construction underway in Guwahati and piling/digging starting in Mumbai.
- Varanasi drawings submitted for approval. Pitampura project is slightly delayed due to revised drawings and approvals.
- Ghaziabad added to pipeline for a 500-bed hospital.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash surplus improved to INR 7,815 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to INR 5,906 million as of March 31, 2026.
- Strong operating cash flows of INR 5,678 million generated in H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential slowdown or stoppage of construction activity in South Delhi due to upcoming pollution-linked norms over the next 1–2 months.
- Regulatory uncertainties surrounding oncology drug pricing caps (though management views it as too early to quantify).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tushar Manudhane (Oswal Financial Services) asked about Cluster 2 volume growth ex-Noida and sustainability of ARPPs.** Management responded that ex-Noida inpatient volume growth was ~26%, driven by Lucknow and Patna scale-up, and reiterated a steady, volume-led ARPP strategy with nominal tariff hikes.
2. **Soumen Samanta (YES Securities) asked about Cluster 2 margin drivers and corporate cost allocations.** Management clarified that corporate costs are entirely loaded onto Cluster 1 (Gurugram), meaning Cluster 2 margins do not bear corporate overhead charges.
3. **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC) asked about the completion timelines for greenfield projects (South Delhi, Mumbai, Guwahati).** Management indicated completion/operations are likely around FY30.
4. **Alankar Garude (Kotak) asked whether upcoming hospitals will launch with full bed capacities and all specialties (like Noida/Lucknow) versus a staggered launch.** Management stated their philosophy favors building full-scale infrastructure upfront to support complex clinical workflows, though operationalizing talent may be nuanced by region.
5. **Amey Chalke (JM Financial) asked about the oncology margin impact following government price cuts.** Management responded that Medanta treats diseases rather than isolating specialties for financial reporting, and investment decisions are governed by underlying clinical demand.

### One-line takeaway
Global Health delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 34% revenue growth, a 60% jump in operating EBITDA, and Noida achieving EBITDA breakeven within a year, while expanding its greenfield pipeline with a new 500-bed hospital project in Ghaziabad.
