# Gaudium IVF and Women Health Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited steady execution, integration of AI tools, and confidence in meeting annual targets despite a slower Q1.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: ₹19.4 crore (Up 9.1% YoY from ₹17.8 crore)
- EBITDA: ₹2.4 crore (Down 52.96% YoY from ₹5.2 crore)
- EBITDA margin: 12.5% (Contracted 1,651 bps YoY from 29%)
- Adjusted EBITDA (ex-expansion costs): ₹5.35 crore (Margin of ~27.63%)
- PAT from continuing operations: ₹1.8 crore (Down 42.27% YoY from ₹3.1 crore)
- Cash and bank balances: ₹8.12 crore (as of June 30, 2026)
- Borrowings: ₹6.74 crore
- Consolidated debt-to-equity ratio: ~0.04 times
- Clinical pregnancy success rate: Over 58% (growing to 62% in the first attempt; cumulative rate at 85%)
- Average revenue per patient: ₹3.5 lakh (increasing to ₹4 lakh when excluding low-value/minimum services)
- Average capex per hub: ₹2.5 crore (₹1 crore construction + ₹1.5 crore lab machinery)
- Lucknow Women Hospital project cost: Up to ₹15 crore

### Highlights
- First full quarter as a publicly listed company.
- Formally integrated AI-led embryology tools (SiD and ERICA) in partnership with IVF 2.0 across centers starting April 1, 2026, showing an approximate 8% improvement in first-attempt success rates.
- Commissioned the South Extension (New Delhi) centre on July 16, 2026, housing the first proprietary "Gaudium Signature Lab".
- Board approved an estimated project cost of up to ₹15 crore for the Gaudium Women Hospital at Lucknow, to be funded via internal accruals.
- Current network stands at 8 hubs and 28 spokes.
- International spokes operational in Paris, Nigeria, and Sydney; international patients contribute ~25% to 30% of the patient mix.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in achieving stated FY27 revenue targets and a ~30% YoY revenue growth rate for the full year.
- Management expects to sustain EBITDA and PAT margins at historical/FY26 levels by the end of the year.
- Planned rollout of 10 new hubs in FY27 (South Extension operational, Gurgaon in 10 days, Nagpur in 25 days), followed by 8 hubs in FY28 and 1 in FY29.
- Commercialization of the Lucknow hospital project expected in FY28–FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian fertility market is large, underpenetrated, and aided by the implementation of the ART and Surrogacy Act (introduced in 2022), which is expected to drive industry consolidation and increase organized player share (currently ~30% organized vs 70% unorganized).
- Approximately 27.5 million couples in India are affected by infertility, with ~3 lakh IVF cycles performed annually.
- Industry CAGR is ~10% to 12%, while Gaudium has historically grown at ~30%.
- Q1 and Q3 are seasonally sluggish due to extreme heat and festivals, respectively.
- Medical tourism offers significant opportunity, with Indian IVF cycle costs roughly 1/5th of the US.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin contraction in Q1 FY27 was driven by front-loaded expansion costs, pre-operational spends at South Extension, preparatory expenses for upcoming centers, clinical talent hiring, and pan-India marketing for SiD and ERICA.
- Pharmacy and hospital segments carry structurally lower margins compared to core IVF.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY27 hub capex of ~₹25 crore for 10 new hubs is on track (funded via IPO proceeds and internal accruals).
- New hub breakeven historically achieved within 3 months (guided for 6 months).
- Matured hub annual cycles: ~30 cycles in Tier 2 cities, ~50 cycles in metros. Initial packages start at ₹2 lakh and go up to ₹20 lakh.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains robust and debt-light post-IPO.
- Lucknow hospital project will be funded mainly through internal accruals, without disturbing IPO proceeds earmarked for the 19 IVF hubs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ground implementation of the ART Act is at various stages across states (some states lack fully formed boards or bank accounts for fees) and will take another year or so to execute properly.
- Seasonality in specific centers (e.g., Srinagar is a seasonal center performing better in winter).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Arman (Blue Sky Fintech) asked about the sluggish 9% YoY revenue growth in Q1 and confidence in 40% growth for FY27.** Management attributed the slower quarter to seasonality (heat) and front-loaded tech/hub preparation, but reaffirmed confidence in achieving ~30% annual revenue growth and normalized margins by year-end. (Did not dodge)
2. **Vileh Rai (KamayaKya Wealth Management) asked about the rationale for entering the hospital business in Lucknow.** Management explained that Lucknow was already targeted for an IVF hub, and the hospital extends their vision into holistic women's health (gynaecology, cancer screening, surgeries) using internal accruals without touching IPO proceeds. (Did not dodge)
3. **Anuj Goyal (Bastion Research) asked about the ground implementation of the ART Act and competition.** Management noted implementation is uneven across states and will take ~1 year to execute fully, which will ultimately drive consolidation away from unorganized "mushroom centers" in favor of organized players like Gaudium. (Did not dodge)
4. **Manish Kela (Swastik Investments) asked about average revenue per patient excluding low-value services.** CFO Rakesh Sharma stated that average revenue rises from ₹3.5 lakh to ~₹4 lakh when excluding minimum services for fresh pickups. (Did not dodge)
5. **Raman KV (Sequent Investments) asked about unit economics, capex per hub, and potential revenue.** Management responded that capex averages ₹2.5 crore per hub (₹1 crore construction + ₹1.5 crore machinery), cycles range from 30 (Tier 2) to 50 (metros) annually, and package values range from ₹2 lakh to ₹20 lakh. (Did not dodge)
6. **Jyotish Nair asked for breakdown of volume vs pricing growth and annual guidance.** Management reiterated seasonality factors for Q1 and guided for 30% YoY growth with sustainable EBITDA/PAT. (Did not dodge)

### One-line takeaway
Gaudium IVF reported a muted Q1 FY27 due to seasonality and front-loaded expansion/marketing costs for its AI tech and new hubs, but management remains strongly confident in achieving ~30% annual revenue growth and margin recovery for FY27.
