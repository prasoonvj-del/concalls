# Aster DM Quality Care Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever revenue, operating EBITDA, and operating margins driven by robust volume growth and successful post-merger integration.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained target of 24%-25% EBITDA margin within 2 to 3 years post-merger.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to a full-quarter combined operating perspective; demand remains strong with a 12% YoY increase in patient volumes.
- New topics or risks: Quantified merger synergies at Rs 16.6 crore for the quarter (Rs 65 crore annualized run-rate, aiming for Rs 200 crore total potential); discussed potential regulatory pricing developments in oncology and state committees (Kerala).
- Last quarter's promises: Seamless merger integration delivered with zero operational disruption and strong talent retention.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,829 crore, up 19% YoY.
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 678 crore, up 25% YoY.
- Operating EBITDA Margin: 24%, up 111 bps YoY.
- Normalised PAT (after non-controlling interest): Rs 281 crore, up 34% YoY (margin at 9.9%, up from 8.8% YoY).
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 5,429 crore (+20% YoY); Operating EBITDA: Rs 1,255 crore (+27% YoY, margin 23.1%); Normalised PAT: Rs 457 crore (+27% YoY).
- Total Patients Served: 2.2 million, up 12% YoY.
- Operational Bed Capacity: 10,840 beds (up 4% YoY); total pipeline of 4,230 additional beds targeting >15,000 beds.
- Blended Occupancy: 68%, up 335 bps YoY (occupied beds grew 8%).
- Gross Debt: Rs 3,959 crore; Cash, bank balances, and current investments: Rs 2,240 crore; Net Debt: Rs 1,719 crore.
- Net Debt to EBITDA (excl. lease): 0.87x; (incl. lease): 1.72x.
- ROCE: 23%, up 152 bps YoY.
- Synergies Realized: Rs 16.6 crore in Q2 FY27 (annualized run-rate of Rs 65 crore; target full potential of Rs 200 crore).

### Highlights
- Marked the first full quarter as a unified entity (Aster DM Quality Care Ltd.) post-merger effective July 1, 2026.
- Clinical complexity scaling: Robotic surgeries +39%, kidney transplants +31%, joint replacements +29%.
- New Centres of Excellence launched: ARISE Robotic Spine Institute at Aster CMI, CARE Cancer Institute in Raipur, and Foetal Medicine Centre at Aster Medcity.
- Accreditations: CARE HITEC City received JCI accreditation; Aster MIMS Kasaragod received NABH accreditation.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains expectation to reach 24%-25% EBITDA margin on a full-year basis within 2 to 3 years post-merger.
- Expansion roadmap aims to scale total capacity from 10,840 beds to over 15,000 beds via a 4,230-bed pipeline (47% brownfield, 53% greenfield).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand across Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets, which account for nearly 75% of operations.
- Growth supported by increasing clinical complexity and a favorable payor mix.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage from higher patient volumes and efficient fixed-cost absorption expanded operating EBITDA margins.
- People cost growth remained significantly lower than patient volume growth due to integration efficiencies and talent sharing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested over INR 600 crore in medical technology over the last 18 months (including 1 LINAC, 1 digital PET-CT, 3 robots, 2 cath labs, and 1 MRI in Q2 FY27).
- H1 FY27 capex exceeded INR 400 crore across ongoing projects and medical equipment; total capex over the last 18 months exceeded INR 1,200 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stood at Rs 3,959 crore and net debt at Rs 1,719 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management acknowledged media reports on state-level pricing committees (e.g., Kerala) and oncology drug price caps, noting limited direct impact currently as the hospital business operates primarily on integrated service packages rather than retail pharmacy models.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tausif Shaikh (BNP Paribas)** asked about synergy timelines and procurement savings. Management stated that synergies will be front-loaded on a run-rate basis over the next 3 to 4 quarters.
- **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC)** asked about integration priorities and oncology price capping views. Management highlighted clinical continuity, talent retention, and viewed oncology price caps as supportive of broader healthcare access.
- **Kunal Randeria (Axis Capital)** asked about hospital maturity categories and Kerala leadership integration. Management explained distinctions between focus and underperforming assets and cited seamless local leadership alignment.
- **Dhvani** asked about census bed breakup and mature segment margin sustainability. Management stated ~80-81% are census beds and highlighted high productivity and complexity growth in mature units.
- **Bino Pathiparampil** asked about margin sustainability and Kerala pricing regulations. Management guided to 24%-25% margins in 2-3 years and emphasized the critical macro need for private healthcare investment in India.

### One-line takeaway
Aster DM Quality Care reported a record Q2 FY27 with 19% YoY revenue growth and 25% YoY operating EBITDA growth, underpinned by robust patient volumes, scaling complex care, and early synergy realization.
