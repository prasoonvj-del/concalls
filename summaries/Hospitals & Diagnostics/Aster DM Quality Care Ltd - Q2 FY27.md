# Aster DM Quality Care Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Company delivered highest-ever revenue, operating EBITDA, and margins in its first full merged quarter.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained full-year target of 24%-25% EBITDA margin within 2 to 3 years post-merger.
- Tone and demand view: Stronger execution tone following 100 days of integration; demand driven by volume growth and complex clinical cases.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Regulatory scrutiny around oncology drug pricing and potential Kerala hospital price caps discussed.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Operationalized merger without service disruption; generated Rs 16.6 crore in Q2 operational synergies; Trivandrum project remains on track for FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,829 crore, up 19% YoY.
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 678 crore, up 25% YoY.
- **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 24%, expanded by 111 bps YoY.
- **Normalised PAT (after NCI):** Rs 281 crore, up 34% YoY.
- **Total Patients Served:** 2.2 million, up 12% YoY.
- **Occupancy Rate:** 68%, up 335 bps YoY.
- **Synergies Realized:** Rs 16.6 crore in Q2 FY27 (annualized run-rate of Rs 65 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 5,429 crore, up 20% YoY; Operating EBITDA Rs 1,255 crore, up 27% YoY (margin at 23.1%).

### Highlights
- First full quarter reporting as Aster DM Quality Care Limited under pooling of interests method following merger effective July 1, 2026.
- Operational bed base expanded 4% YoY to 10,840 beds; occupied beds grew 8% YoY.
- Complex care expansion: Robotic surgeries up 39%, kidney transplants up 31%, and joint replacements up 29% YoY.
- Launched ARISE Robotic Spine Institute at Aster CMI, CARE Cancer Institute in Raipur, and Foetal Medicine Centre at Aster Medcity.
- Segment performance:
  - **Mature Hospitals (64% beds):** Revenue up 18% YoY, contributing 73% of hospital revenue and 83% of operating EBITDA.
  - **Focus Hospitals (15% beds):** Revenue up 13% YoY.
  - **Emerging Hospitals (10% beds):** Revenue up 62% YoY; patient volumes up 53% YoY.
  - **Underperforming Hospitals (11% beds):** Revenue up 24% YoY; operating EBITDA more than doubled.
- Over the last 18 months, deployed over Rs 1,200 crore in total capex and over Rs 600 crore specifically in medical technology.

### Guidance & outlook
- Target to reach total capacity of over 15,000 beds by adding 4,230 beds (47% brownfield, 53% greenfield).
- Reaffirmed guidance to achieve 24%-25% full-year EBITDA margin within 2 to 3 years post-merger.
- Expects to achieve full run-rate of Rs 200 crore merger synergies within 4 quarters, with complete P&L realization in 6 to 7 quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by broad-based patient volume increases (+12% YoY) and shifting case mix toward complex quaternary care.
- India needs an estimated 1.5 million hospital beds over the next decade ($130 billion investment needed), underscoring strong long-term private healthcare macros.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin expanded to 24% due to higher fixed-cost absorption and rationalization of administrative and material procurement rates across the merged entity.
- People cost growth was kept significantly lower than patient volume growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested over Rs 400 crore in capex during H1 FY27 for ongoing expansion projects and medical equipment.
- 454-bed Aster Trivandrum Hospital progressing on track for FY27 expansion.
- Added 1 LINAC, 1 digital PET-CT, 3 surgical robots, 2 cath labs, and 1 MRI in Q2 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Gross Debt:** Rs 3,959 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Cash, Bank & Investments:** Rs 2,240 crore.
- **Net Debt:** Rs 1,719 crore.
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 0.87x (excluding lease liabilities) and 1.72x (including lease liabilities) on a trailing twelve-month basis.
- **ROCE:** 23% in Q2 FY27, up 152 bps YoY.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential regulatory pricing control measures on hospitals being explored by the Kerala state assembly committee.
- Margin seasonality in Q3 and Q4 due to festivals.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tausif Shaikh (BNP Paribas):** *Can you clarify the delivery timeline and front-loading of the Rs 200 crore synergy targets?*
  - **Management:** Synergy realization will be front-loaded; the current Rs 65 crore run-rate will reach the full target run-rate within 3-4 quarters, and full financial impact will be realized in 6-7 quarters.
- **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC):** *What is the impact of recent regulatory price capping on oncology drugs and potential Kerala price controls?*
  - **Management:** Hospital revenues are package and service-led rather than retail drug-led. Capping drug prices improves accessibility in Tier 2/3 markets and boosts volume without adverse operational impact; management will engage constructively on state regulatory discussions.
- **Kunal Randeria (Axis Capital):** *Will initial losses from 1,200 greenfield beds offset merger synergies?*
  - **Management:** No; greenfield facilities in existing clusters break even quickly (e.g., Whitefield in 3 months, Kasaragod in 9 months), preventing significant P&L drag.
- **Bino Pathiparampil (Elara Capital):** *Is the 24% Q2 EBITDA margin a new sustainable base?*
  - **Management:** Cautioned against treating a single quarter as the baseline due to festive seasonality in Q3/Q4, maintaining the full-year target of 24%-25% within 2 to 3 years.

### One-line takeaway
Aster DM Quality Care delivered strong combined Q2 FY27 results with 19% revenue growth and 24% EBITDA margin, supported by fast-tracked merger synergies and volume expansion across mature and emerging assets.
