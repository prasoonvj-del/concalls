# GPT Healthcare Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-07 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong YoY revenue and PAT growth, improving ARPOB, and steady expansion progress.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income / Revenue from Operations:** INR 126.2 crore (up 18.2% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 26.2 crore
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.4%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 12.7 crore (up 66% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 9.9%
- **Network Occupancy:** 45.5% (Mature hospitals occupancy at 58.07% excluding Raipur)
- **Average Revenue Per Occupied Bed (ARPOB):** INR 42,350
- **Raipur Q1 Loss:** Minus INR 3 crore

### Highlights
- Salt Lake flagship hospital exceeded 800 cumulative robotic-assisted surgeries; occupancy at 62% (up from 60% YoY); ARPOB at INR 45,300; received DRNB approval in medical gastroenterology.
- Dumdum occupancy reached 65% (up from 60% YoY); ARPOB stood at INR 43,041; renal transplants exceeded 700.
- Agartala ARPOB grew by 70% to INR 41,573 (note: transcript states 70% as spoken by management, though base is unstated); cancer care center completed over 700 radiation procedures.
- Howrah revenue grew by 31% YoY and occupancy increased by 16% YoY.
- Raipur bed occupancy improved to 17% (from 7% YoY); achieved NABH accreditation in 13 months and conducted its first liver transplant.
- Jamshedpur 155-bed tertiary care hospital construction remains on schedule for commissioning in Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to close the year with an EBITDA margin of around 21% (compared to 19% last year), translating to approximately INR 110 crore to INR 115 crore in EBITDA.
- Raipur is expected to close the year at around 30% occupancy.
- Jamshedpur hospital is expected to be commissioned by late Q4 FY27 and is projected to break even within approximately 24 months.
- Network capacity is targeted to exceed 1,000 beds over the next 2 years with the addition of the 7th hospital.
- Long-term ROE and ROCE targeted around 25%.
- Incoming debt for Jamshedpur is expected to be around INR 25 crore during this FY.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Healthcare sector shifting towards quality care, organized hospitals, and specialized clinical expertise.
- Substantial gap persists between demand and healthcare infrastructure supply, particularly in Eastern and Central India.
- Cash and insurance patients continue to generate approximately 90% of revenues.
- International patient inflows from Bangladesh are improving due to policy changes on targeted visas, though still below pre-disruption levels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- No tariff increase was taken in Q1; annual inflation-linked tariff increases generally occur in October.
- ARPOB increases are primarily driven by incremental changes in case mix and higher-end tertiary care.
- Raipur rental is in the mid-30s per sq ft (market standard), and Jamshedpur rental will be on similar market terms under the asset-light model.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding via asset-light rental models for Raipur and Jamshedpur, reducing real estate investment in favor of medical assets and allied finishing.
- Evaluating greenfield land acquisitions and acquisition opportunities in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities of Eastern India (e.g., Cuttack, Ranchi, Patna, Banaras, Prayagraj) for the 7th hospital.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Overall debt position remains the same as last year, with an expected incoming debt of around INR 25 crore for Jamshedpur during the FY.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Agartala experienced a temporary dip in occupancy due to local tribal elections restricting movement for nearly a month.
- Raipur and Agartala face higher doctor/staff attrition rates around 10% due to geographic challenges (compared to 6%–7% in Kolkata).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rucheeta (C.J. Shah Group) on Agartala occupancy dip:** Management explained it was due to local tribal elections restricting movement, but noted July occupancy rebounded to 51% with record revenues. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Abhishek Maheshwari (Skyridge Fund) on Raipur loss:** Management stated Q1 loss was minus INR 3 crore and expects it to taper down through the year. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Pranay Shah (Caron Capital) on Dumdum occupancy drop from Q4:** Management clarified that Q4 to Q1 seasonal variations apply, and compared YoY, Dumdum occupancy actually improved from 59.75% to 65%. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Varth Sanghavi (DyDx Advisors) on Raipur ARPOB reduction:** Management stated ARPOB slightly reduced from INR 44,500 to INR 42,300 due to taking a limited amount of Ayushman Bharat patients to utilize spare capacity in the new hospital. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Soumya Raghuvanshi (Nirva Securities) on ARPOB drivers:** Management clarified that annual tariff hikes occur in October, but 50% to 60% of ARPOB growth comes from improved specialty mix and case mix. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
GPT Healthcare delivered strong Q1 FY27 performance with an 18.2% YoY revenue increase and a 66% YoY PAT jump, driven by robust mature hospital occupancy, higher ARPOB from complex specialties, and disciplined asset-light expansion.
