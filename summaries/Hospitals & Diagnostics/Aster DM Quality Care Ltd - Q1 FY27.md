# Aster DM Quality Care Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong proforma revenue and EBITDA growth, successful merger integration, and margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- Proforma Revenue: Rs 2,597 crore, up 20% YoY.
- Proforma EBITDA: Rs 576 crore, up 30% YoY.
- Proforma EBITDA Margin: 22.2%, up 170 bps YoY.
- Total Patients Treated: Over 2 million, up 13% YoY.
- Blended Occupancy: 64%, up 510 bps YoY.
- Medical Value Travel (MVT) Revenue: Up 62% YoY.
- Mature Units Revenue Growth: 19% YoY (contributing 73% of total revenue); EBITDA margins sustained at 30% (up 230 bps YoY).
- Focus Units Revenue Growth: 16% YoY (contributing 15% of revenue); EBITDA margins up 60 bps.
- Emerging Segment Revenue Growth: 63% YoY; EBITDA surged 240% YoY with margins up 640 bps to 12.4%.
- Aster DM Healthcare standalone Revenue: Rs 1,311 crore, up 22% YoY.
- Aster DM Healthcare Operating EBITDA: Rs 277 crore, up 29% YoY (margin at 21.1%, up 117 bps YoY).
- Aster DM Healthcare Normalised PAT (excl. exceptional costs): ~Rs 125 crore, up 39% YoY.
- Aster DM Healthcare Exceptional Expense: Rs 114 crore (merger-related transaction costs).
- Aster DM Healthcare ROCE: 22.6%, up ~190 bps YoY.
- Quality Care standalone Revenue: Rs 1,287 crore, up 19% YoY.
- Quality Care Operating EBITDA: Rs 299 crore, up 32% YoY (margin at 23.2%, up 216 bps YoY; occupancy at 65.4%, up 656 bps YoY; ARPP IP ~Rs 144k).
- Combined Net Debt: Rs 1,162 crore as of June 30, 2026 (Aster net cash of Rs 511 crore; Quality Care debt of Rs 1,673 crore).

### Highlights
- Completed the merger between Aster DM Healthcare and Quality Care, forming a unified entity (Aster DM Quality Care Ltd.) effective July 1, 2026, operating ~39 hospitals with over 45,000 healthcare professionals and 7,000+ doctors.
- Enterprise-wide #GOGREATER celebration held on July 1, 2026, with zero operational friction or service disruption.
- Kasaragod facility achieved EBITDA breakeven in June 2026 within 9 months of operation.
- Commissioned the 159-bed Aster Women & Children block at Aster Whitefield, Bengaluru in April 2026.
- Clinical milestones: Care Hi-tech secured JCI accreditation; various units recognized by NABH, Medical Dialogues, and The Economic Times; Executive Chairman Dr. Azad Moopen and Dr. M.I. Sahadulla honored with Lifetime Achievement Awards from Financial Express.
- Specialties like Orthopaedics, Neurology, and Oncology grew in excess of 24% YoY; Robotics volumes grew ~80% YoY; joint replacements up 39% YoY; transplants up 19% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its broader guidance to hit an EBITDA margin of 24%-25% within 2 to 3 years post-merger (targeted between FY28 to FY29).
- Management reiterates expectation of 10%-15% incremental EBITDA on account of post-merger synergies based on FY24 pro forma EBITDA.
- Roadmap to add over 4,170 beds over the next 3 to 4 years, taking total capacity over 15,000 beds (53% brownfield-led).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by higher patient volumes and realizations supported by an increasingly complex case mix.
- MVT growth driven by addition of new geographies, CRM deployment, digital interventions, and benchmarked clinical outcomes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA outpaced revenue growth due to operating leverage from material cost savings and stronger fixed cost absorption.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital deployment focuses on deepening presence across Southern, Central, and Eastern regions.
- Upcoming pipeline includes Trivandrum (Aster Capital) targeted for H2 FY27 (Jan 2027), Hyderabad hospital in April 2027, and Sarjapur Phase 1 in H2 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Combined net debt stands at Rs 1,162 crore as of June 30, 2026 (Aster net cash Rs 511 crore; Quality Care debt Rs 1,673 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as material risks by management.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tausif Shaikh (BNP Paribas) asked about the new organizational structure and maturity cuts.** Management (Varun Khanna) explained that the business is structured around four maturity categories (Mature, Focus, Emerging, Underperforming) alongside geographical/operational continuity and clinical vertical leads.
- **Damayanti Kerai asked about low-hanging fruit for margins and synergy realization.** Management (Varun Khanna) clarified that past performance was independent, and synergy realization (targeting 10%-15% incremental EBITDA over FY24 baseline) will start kicking in from this financial year.
- **Nitin Shakdher asked about the promoter vision and MVT strategy.** Management (Alisha Moopen) noted the priority is making the 39 hospitals run cohesively, scaling MVT to double-digit contribution over the next couple of years, and taking advanced specialties to Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
- **Siddharth asked about Kerala/Karnataka performance recovery and future reporting segments.** Management (Sunil Kumar M R and Varun Khanna) attributed Kerala and Karnataka growth to system-led operational fixes, doctor hiring (18 new doctors in Bangalore in Q1), and stated that reporting will continue to emphasize maturity cuts and consolidated metrics.
- **Bino Pathiparampil asked about the sustainability of high growth rates in Bangalore/Kerala.** Management (Sunil Kumar M R) stated that sustainable organic growth involves 5%-6% volume growth and 7%-8% ARPP growth, supporting double-digit to mid-teen growth.
- **Harith asked about the greenfield bed pipeline timelines.** Management (Sunil Kumar M R) detailed timelines for Trivandrum (Jan 2027), Hyderabad (April 2027), and Sarjapur Phase 1 (H2 FY28).

### One-line takeaway
Aster DM Quality Care reported a strong proforma Q1 FY27 with 20% revenue growth and 30% EBITDA growth, driven by volume ramp-ups, improved case mix, and robust execution as it commenced post-merger integration.
