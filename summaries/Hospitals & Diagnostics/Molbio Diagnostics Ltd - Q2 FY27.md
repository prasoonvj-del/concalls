# Molbio Diagnostics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth was impacted by a one-month delay in government rate contract renewals, though management remains confident in meeting full-year guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (25% top-line growth, 24-25% EBITDA margin).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong growth" to "softer" due to contract negotiation delays; management emphasized that the business is non-linear and H2 is expected to be stronger (60% of annual business).
- **New topics:** Introduction of a veterinary diagnostic vertical (radiology + Truenat assays); details on three new platforms (Trueamp®, Truecyte®, Truepoc™).
- **Last quarter's promises:** OptraScan US FDA approval was delivered (mentioned in Q1); rate contract renewal was delayed (now resolved).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 667 crore (vs Rs 680 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Export revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 120 crore.
- **Devices sold (H1 FY27):** 559.
- **Kits sold (H1 FY27):** 94.7 lakh.
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 153 crore (23% margin).
- **EBITDA loss (OptraScan):** Rs 15 crore (2.2% impact).
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 60 crore.
- **Receivable days (TTM):** 77 days.
- **Inventory turnover:** 1.2x.
- **Net cash position:** Achieved as of September 2026 (vs Rs 57 crore net debt in March 2026).

### Highlights
- **Rate Contract:** Revenue dip in H1 attributed to a one-month delay in government rate contract negotiations; contract is now signed, and orders are being shipped.
- **Veterinary Vertical:** Launched radiology products for veterinary use; developing 5-6 Truenat assays for veterinary applications (e.g., rabies, parvo, distemper).
- **New Platforms:**
    - **Trueamp®:** Multiplexing platform for syndromic testing (respiratory, STI, sepsis).
    - **Truecyte®:** Point-of-care cell morphology and CBC counter.
    - **Truepoc™:** Swab-based screening tool for hinterland reach (supported by Gates Foundation).
- **OptraScan:** US FDA approval received; commercial efforts ongoing in US and globally; meaningful contribution expected from next fiscal.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Guidance:** Maintained 25% top-line growth and 24-25% EBITDA margin.
- **Seasonality:** Reiterated that 40% of business occurs in H1 and 60% in H2.
- **Veterinary:** Products expected in market by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Government Procurement:** Process involves rate contracts; government is transitioning from microscopy to molecular diagnostics (TB, Hepatitis, HPV).
- **Veterinary Market:** Currently unorganized; Molbio aims to create a market for point-of-care molecular diagnostics in pet/companion animal clinics.
- **Syndromic Testing:** Viewed as a "no-brainer" for both public and private sectors to identify causes of illness rapidly.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margins:** EBITDA margin at 23% for H1, impacted by OptraScan losses (which were not present in H1 FY26).
- **Interest:** Interest income increased and interest expense reduced in Q2 vs Q1 due to moving to a net cash position.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capacity:** Existing capacity sufficient for growth; no new major capex required.
- **Automation:** Purchase orders placed for equipment using Rs 72 crore of IPO funds; automation expected to support 3x revenue growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Debt:** Company moved to a net cash position as of September 2026.
- **Receivables:** TTM receivable days improved to 77 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Non-linearity:** Management reiterated that public health business is not linear and quarterly comparisons are misleading.
- **Regulatory:** New platforms require CDSCO licenses (4-5 month process) before launch.
- **Execution:** Scaling new verticals (veterinary, new platforms) requires building new GTM strategies.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sidharth Negandhi (Analyst) asked about the revenue fall.** Management explained it was a one-month delay in rate contract renewal, not a demand issue.
- **Dhruv Sitlani (Leo Capital) asked about the government order process.** Management explained the tender/rate contract process and confirmed they have a unique, proprietary platform (Truenat) with ~10,000 installed machines.
- **Nirali Gopani (Analyst) asked about price pressure in the new contract.** Management clarified that realizations are similar to the previous contract, with no drastic price cuts.
- **Dhawal Khut (Jefferies) asked about the 3 new platforms.** Management confirmed they are developed in-house (no royalties), with Truepoc™ supported by the Gates Foundation (no royalties).
- **Aniket Singh (Kotak) asked about OptraScan progress.** Management stated real action/uptake is planned for the next fiscal year.

### One-line takeaway
Molbio's H1 revenue was impacted by a temporary delay in government contract renewals, but the company remains net cash positive, maintains its 25% growth guidance, and is aggressively expanding into veterinary diagnostics and new molecular platforms.
