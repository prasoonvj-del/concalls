# Metropolis Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27 (filed November 10, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted robust revenue growth exceeding guidance, expanding EBITDA margins, and structural tailwinds from organized shift and favorable regulations.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained growth and margin trajectory; noted H1 revenue growth at 16.2% was ahead of stated guidance, with EBITDA margin expansion of 180-190 bps.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted to more pronounced optimism regarding regulatory clarity (NMC remote authorization, price display) and successful integration of recent acquisitions driving North India contribution to 17%.
- **New topics or risks:** Discussed the impact of the weakest monsoon in 2 decades, introduced upcoming technology rollouts (CRM, dynamic pricing, finance reengineering), and evaluated a calibrated price increase for early next year.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Lab platform transition remains on track for completion in Q3; Insta60 scaled from Mumbai to Chennai and Bengaluru.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue Growth:** 16.2% YoY (organic growth step-up to 15-16% over the last 4-6 quarters)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 26.2% (expansion of 190 bps YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 497 crore (growing 16% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 135 crore (growing 24% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 27.2% (expansion of 180 bps YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 75 crore (growing 43% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 15.2% (expansion of 290 bps YoY)
- **B2C Revenue Growth (Q2 & H1):** 17% YoY (patient volumes up 13.5%, test volumes up 11%)
- **B2B Revenue Growth (Q2):** 15% YoY (patient volumes up 7%, test volumes up 11%; partner network crossed 9,000)
- **Specialty & TruHealth Growth:** Both grew at ~20% YoY in Q2 (TruHealth contribution at 17%, Specialty at 40%)
- **Tier 3 / Rural Revenue Growth:** ~20% YoY (accounting for 26% of domestic revenue)
- **North India Contribution:** ~17% of domestic revenue
- **Network Additions:** 116 collection centers added in Q2 (700 net additions over last 24 months)
- **Net Working Capital:** 13 days (compared to 16 days in FY26)
- **Cash and Liquid Investments:** INR 348 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs INR 203 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- **Interim Dividend:** INR 1 per share declared

### Highlights
- Delivered 16% revenue growth despite one of the weakest monsoons in over 2 decades (11% test volume growth, 10% patient volume growth) without any price increases during the period.
- Acquisitions (DAPIC in Dehradun, Scientific in Agra, and Core Diagnostics) are performing well; Core Diagnostics operates at near double-digit margins (near breakeven 1.5 years ago) with an estimated 13% IRR.
- NMC advisory on digital authorization of reports clarified rules, enabling centralized specialist review for remote/Tier 3 labs and faster mini-lab setup.
- Inter-state network density improved, with center-to-lab ratio targeted to move from 24:1 toward 30:1 and 35:1 over 2 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects similar growth and margin trajectories to continue for the rest of the year.
- Evaluating a calibrated price revision in Q4 / early next year for part of the business (B2C) due to rising input, transportation, logistics, and service costs.
- Capex guidance maintained at 4% to 5% overall (approx. INR 65 crore to INR 70 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural shift from unorganized to organized segment continues, supported by government focus on quality, transparency, and access rather than price controls.
- Mandatory display of rates at centers welcomed by management; hospital price caps do not apply to diagnostics.
- Preventive healthcare (TruHealth) growing at ~20% annually; expanding allied services (basic radiology, doctor consultations, vital checks) to improve prescription completion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by volume-led operating leverage, disciplined cost management, and productivity gains (manpower base stable while test volumes grew 10-11%).
- Laboratory platform transition standardizing menu, equipment, reagents, and vendors will complete in Q3, aiding gross margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Stopped adding new labs, maintaining operations across 750 towns, while accelerating collection center additions in peripheral areas.
- Insta60 (60-minute home visit service) successfully expanded from Mumbai to Chennai and launching in Bengaluru in November.
- Core, DAPIC, and Scientific acquisitions expanded North India's domestic revenue share to 17%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and liquid investments increased to INR 348 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Net working capital days reduced to 13 days from 16 days in FY26.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- M&A valuations for poor-quality vs high-valuation assets are closely monitored to avoid deals with sub-10% IRRs; management prioritizes disciplined, value-accretive acquisitions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Surya Patra (PhillipCapital) on test-per-patient count and center-to-lab ratio:** Management clarified that a TruHealth wellness package with 50-60 parameters is counted as a single test. The center-to-lab ratio rises because lab count is capped while collection centers and partner retention (via MetLink) increase.
2. **Kunal Thanvi (Banyan Tree Advisors) on Insta60 traction and costs:** Management stated Insta60 sees strong traction and new customer additions in Mumbai and Chennai without higher incremental cost due to omnichannel resource utilization.
3. **Tausif Shaikh (BNP Paribas) on margin guidance and price hikes:** Management confirmed H1 margin expansion of 180-190 bps will trend similarly for the rest of the year, with price revisions planned for B2C early next year.
4. **Anshul Agrawal (Emkay) on NMC remote authorization:** Management clarified the NMC advisory provides pan-India clarity for pathologists to remotely review reports using digital signatures, enabling quality scaling in Tier 3 markets.
5. **Shyam Srinivasan (Goldman Sachs) on hospital pricing impact:** Management noted hospital outsourcing contracts are pre-fixed and unaffected by hospital-specific price caps, while watching for any secondary outsourcing impacts.
6. **Akshaya Shinde (Centrum Broking) on Tier 3 growth:** Tier 3 markets are growing at ~20%, contributing 26% of domestic revenue through a mix of routine and specialty tests.

### One-line takeaway
Metropolis Healthcare delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 16.2% revenue growth and a 190 bps EBITDA margin expansion, driven by accelerating specialty/TruHealth segments, digital initiatives, and strong operating leverage.
