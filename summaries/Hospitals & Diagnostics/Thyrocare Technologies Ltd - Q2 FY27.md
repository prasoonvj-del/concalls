# Thyrocare Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong 25% YoY consolidated revenue growth, expanding franchisee network, and robust operating efficiencies.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 271 crore, up 25% YoY.
- Stand-alone pathology revenue: Rs 256 crore, up 26.7% YoY.
- Consolidated gross margin: 74.2%, up >190 bps YoY.
- EBITDA margin: 33%, with YoY growth of 25%.
- Profit after tax before exceptions (PAT): Rs 60 crore, up 26% YoY (22% margin).
- EPS before exceptions: Rs 3.78, up 26% YoY (vs Rs 3.01 YoY).
- H1 FY27 consolidated revenue growth: 25% YoY; EBITDA growth: 29% YoY; PAT growth: 30% YoY.
- Active franchisee base: Over 12,911 (added ~1,180 franchisees in Q2).
- Laboratory network: 49 labs (48 in India, 1 in Tanzania; 5 new labs added in Q2).
- Specialty segment contribution: ~4% of pathology revenue.
- Complaints: 2.9 per million tests in Q2.
- Average turnaround time (TAT): 3.5 hours in Q2.
- Net cash and equivalents: Over Rs 211 crore; zero debt as of September 30, 2026.
- Operating cash flow (H1 FY27): Rs 133 crore.
- Capex (H1 FY27): Rs 30 crore.
- NHL (Nueclear Healthcare Limited) divestment consideration: Rs 141 crore (mix of Rs 81.9 crore cash and Rs 59.5 crore equity) to Trovera.
- NHL ROCE: 6.95% vs Thyrocare ROCE of 34.9%.
- NHL impairment charge: Rs 9 crore in standalone books, Rs 45 crore in consolidated financials.
- Pregnancy test kit MRP: Rs 60; Beta hCG blood test coupon: Rs 399 (instead of Rs 800); availability: 10,000+ pharmacies via Ascent and PharmEasy, plus quick commerce. Launch marketing spend: ~Rs 1.5 crore in Q2, expected ~Rs 3 crore in Q3.
- ESOP cost: ~Rs 4 crore in Q1 and Q2 each.

### Highlights
- Delivered 25%+ consolidated revenue growth for the second consecutive quarter, extending a 20%+ growth momentum over the last 9 quarters.
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly franchisee additions in 3 years (1,180 added in Q2), taking active base to 12,911 (4x base at acquisition).
- Expanded lab network with 5 new labs in Q2 (4 regional processing labs at Asansol, Nashik, Madurai, Jabalpur; 1 hybrid lab at Jamshedpur).
- Scaled specialty diagnostics (allergy and genomics) with a dedicated technical sales team; specialty currently contributes ~4% of pathology revenue.
- Stepped into consumer products with the launch of Thyrocare Pregnancy Test Kit endorsed by brand ambassador Yami Gautam.
- Initiated shareholder approval process to divest radiology business (NHL) to Trovera for Rs 141 crore to focus on high-return pathology business.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects early 20s revenue growth for the full year (revised from mid-to-high teens).
- Franchisee additions expected to normalize in Q3 after a strong Q2 surge, aiming for 1,500 to 2,500 annual additions (targeting 20,000 to 25,000 franchisees in 5 years).
- Long-term EBITDA margin guidance anchored around 31% to 31.5%, reinvesting operating leverage into growth initiatives.
- Specialty segment targeted to reach 10% to 15% of revenue in a 3 to 5-year horizon.
- Pregnancy test kit / point-of-care category guidance to be provided next financial year after 2 quarters of execution.
- Capex for the balance of FY27 expected to be similar to H1 (~Rs 30 crore).
- API Group ex-Thyrocare targeted to turn profitable by Q4 FY27; no corporate action/merger evaluation until this milestone is achieved.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Diagnostics shifting from back-end support to the center of clinical decisions and preventive care.
- Specialty diagnostics in India is underpenetrated, expensive, and fragmented.
- Flagship preventive brand (Aarogyam) and curative/chronic range (Jaanch) saw strong momentum; Jaanch grew 58% (now 2.5% of pathology revenue).
- Partnership business grew 28% YoY, led by insurance, health tech, and online platforms (PharmEasy contributing ~11%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated gross margin improved by >190 bps YoY to 74.2%, driven by 27% volume growth.
- Employee and other overhead costs increased YoY due to inflation, higher volumes, lab expansion, and investments in specialty and pregnancy kits.
- Pregnancy kit business gross and EBITDA margins expected to be comparable to pathology business post-stabilization, but near-term profitability impacted by marketing investments over the next 3 quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex of Rs 30 crore deployed toward new lab expansion and volume growth capacity; similar capex expected in H2.
- 108+ MD pathologists; 96% of samples processed in NABL-accredited labs.
- Phlebotomist network exceeds 2,100, achieving 99% on-time visits.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with net cash and equivalents over Rs 211 crore and zero debt as of September 30, 2026.
- Generated Rs 133 crore from operating activities in H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- NHL (Nueclear) was dragging down consolidated revenue growth by 2% and EBITDA margin by 1%, with low operating PAT and substantial end-of-life equipment replacement costs.
- High volume of daily tests (1 lakh/day) results in ~1,000 daily delayed phlebotomy visits (1%), occasionally surfacing as customer complaints on social media despite a 99% on-time tracking average.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Surya Patra (PhillipCapital) asked whether the record Q2 franchisee addition run rate is sustainable.** Management (Rahul Guha) responded that Q3 is historically seasonally tough for franchisee additions, so it is too early to commit to this run rate as a new norm, though annual guidance of 1,500 additions is surpassed.
- **Mohit Saini (Individual Investor) asked about API-Thyrocare merger rumors.** Management clarified that no corporate action is under evaluation until the API Group (ex-Thyrocare) turns profitable (targeted by Q4 FY27) and achieves debt-free status.
- **Chintan Sheth (Standalone query) questioned the rationale for the NHL write-off given the sale price vs. net worth.** Management (Vikram Gupta) explained that the impairment is the difference between the carrying value of the investment in Thyrocare's books and the sales consideration, independent of NHL's standalone net worth.
- **Aditya Chheda (InCred Asset Management) asked about ESOP and launch costs.** Management quantified ESOP cost at ~Rs 4 crore each for Q1 and Q2, and pregnancy kit launch expenses at Rs 1.5 crore in Q2 with an anticipated Rs 3 crore in Q3.
- **Sujit (Individual Investor) inquired about API Holdings' shareholding percentage in Thyrocare.** Management stated there is no intent to reduce the promoter shareholding below 51%.

### One-line takeaway
Thyrocare delivered robust Q2 FY27 performance with 25% revenue growth and 33% EBITDA margins, anchored by aggressive franchisee expansion, operational efficiencies, and strategic forays into specialty diagnostics and point-of-care consumer products while divesting drag-asset NHL.
