# Vimta Labs Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth slowed to 10% YoY due to longer customer decision cycles and headwinds in the food testing segment, though management remains optimistic about a recovery in H2.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 1,149 million (up 9.9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 408 million (up 10.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 35.5%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 206 million (up 3.5% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 17.9%.
*   **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 2,278 million (up 11.8% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 819 million (up 13.2% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 417 million (up 7.3% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Biologics:** Commenced revenue generation from biologics services in Q2; currently at an early stage with modest contribution.
*   **Preclinical:** Identified as a strong growth driver with a 30% CAGR over the last four years.
*   **Electronics Testing:** Remained stable; capacity doubled for EMI/EMC chambers to capture demand from defense OEMs and EV sectors.
*   **Food Testing:** Export/import sample flows, which were severely impacted in Q1, are showing signs of recovery.
*   **Clinical Research:** Business development efforts are underway to diversify the client base following the loss of major clients last year.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Medium-term:** Management remains confident in achieving a turnover of Rs 700 crore for FY28 with EBITDA margins of 35-36%.
*   **H2 Outlook:** Management expects improvement in Contract Research and Development Services (CRADS) and clinical research in Q3 and Q4, driven by a healthy pipeline of active proposals.
*   **Biologics:** Expected to take a couple of years to become a significant revenue contributor; targeting a 1:1 return on investment by the second or third year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Pharma:** Experiencing a "lull" in R&D pipelines, leading to longer decision-making cycles, though inquiries and proposals are picking up.
*   **Food:** Export market growth is measured; company is pivoting focus to domestic opportunities to broaden the customer base.
*   **Electronics:** Industry outlook is encouraging, supported by a 39% growth in electronics exports and increased defense localization.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** EBITDA margins saw a slight correction due to new capacity additions and increased material consumption.
*   **Margin Outlook:** Potential for 100-200 bps correction in the medium term due to service mix shifts (moving toward biologics), though economies of scale from top-line growth may offset this.
*   **Employee Costs:** Management aims to maintain headcount at ~1,400, focusing on productivity gains to offset rising manpower costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex:** Budgeted at Rs 80 crore (includes Rs 45-50 crore maintenance capex).
*   **Capacity:** Infrastructure built over the last two years is sufficient for the next 5-7 years; future capex will focus on equipment and specific technology upgrades.
*   **Preclinical:** Currently expanding large animal facilities due to hitting capacity constraints.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Remains a net debt-free company with cash and cash equivalents of Rs 701 million.
*   **Borrowings:** Added Rs 15 crore in long-term borrowings in H1, citing interest rate arbitrage (borrowing costs lower than using own cash) and maintaining liquidity for potential opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **External Headwinds:** Slowdown in pharma R&D pipelines and geopolitical issues affecting food export/import sample flows.
*   **Clinical Research:** Lead times for recovery are 12-18 months; the company is a newcomer in Clinical Trials (CT), which requires time to establish.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** High competition and regulatory complexity remain key focus areas.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why has revenue growth slowed to 10% despite Rs 100 crore capex?** Management attributed this to external factors (pharma R&D lull, food export headwinds) rather than internal capability issues, noting that infrastructure was built for 5-7 years of growth.
*   **Q: What are the ticket sizes for biologics?** Management noted that lab-scale biosimilar development projects range from Rs 4 crore to Rs 7 crore.
*   **Q: Why borrow Rs 15 crore when holding Rs 70 crore cash?** Management explained it as an interest rate arbitrage strategy and a desire to keep cash available for quick deployment.
*   **Q: What are the constraints in clinical research?** Management admitted to losing major clients last year and noted that rebuilding the client base takes 12-18 months.
*   **Q: Will margins sustain at 35-36%?** Management indicated a possible 100-200 bps correction in the medium term due to the shift in service mix toward biologics.

### One-line takeaway
Vimta Labs is navigating a temporary lull in pharma R&D and food testing volumes while investing in new biologics capabilities, maintaining a net-cash position and a confident medium-term growth outlook.
