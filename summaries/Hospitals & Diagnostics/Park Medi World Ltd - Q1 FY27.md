# Park Medi World Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-08 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 19% YoY revenue growth, strategic asset acquisitions, and an expanding bed capacity.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 476 crore (up 19% YoY)
- **EBITDA (excluding other income):** Rs. 126 crore (up 20% YoY, margin at 26.5%)
- **PAT:** Rs. 89 crore (up 35% YoY, margin at 18.6%)
- **IPD volumes:** 26,304 patients (up 16% YoY)
- **OPD volumes:** 2,23,446 patients (up 17% YoY)
- **ARPOB:** Rs. 30,444 (up 12% YoY)
- **ALOS:** 5.9 days (improved 8% YoY from 6.4 days)
- **Network occupancy:** 56% (compared to 68% in Q1 FY26)
- **Term debt (excluding lease liability):** Rs. 25.6 crore as of June 30, 2026 (vs Rs. 28.2 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- **Fixed deposits:** Rs. 300 crore
- **Net worth:** Rs. 2,100 crore
- **Total bed capacity:** 3,960 beds as of June 30, 2026 (up 32% YoY)
- **FY27 guidance:** Revenue of Rs. 2,080 crore (24% growth), EBITDA of Rs. 530 crore (25% growth), and PAT of Rs. 360 crore (32% growth).

### Highlights
- Commissioned a 330-bed NABH-accredited Multi-Super Speciality Hospital in Rudrapur, Uttarakhand (The Medicity Hospital) on August 2, 2026, marking entry into the 6th state.
- Announced a 100-bed extension at Palam Vihar, Gurgaon ('Park Platinum') on June 30, 2026, taking consolidated Gurgaon capacity to 750 beds.
- Announced definitive agreement to acquire Mehar Hospital, a 150-bed hospital in Zirakpur, for approximately Rs. 107 crore on August 3, 2026.
- High-end specialties contributed ~62% of revenue (an increase of 440 bps YoY).
- 9 hospitals now have NABL-accredited labs (up from 8 earlier), with 4 more planned in FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to end FY27 with 4,740 beds, add another 1,000 beds in FY28, reaching 5,740 beds by FY28, funded via internal accruals and IPO proceeds without fresh debt.
- FY27 guidance: Revenue of Rs. 2,080 crore (24% YoY growth), EBITDA of Rs. 530 crore (25% YoY growth), and PAT of Rs. 360 crore (32% YoY growth).
- Blended EBITDA margin expected to hold between 26% to 27% for FY27.
- ARPOB growth guidance is in the 10%-12% band on an annualized basis.
- Debtor days expected to trend towards a medium-term target of 125-130 days.
- Payer mix targeted to shift towards a 70:30 split (government schemes vs cash/TPA/private insurance) over the next 12-18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Network occupancy for FY27 is expected to moderate from FY26 levels (64%) due to the addition of 1,490 beds in calendar year 2026.
- Upper North India (J&K, Himachal Pradesh, hilly areas) patient traffic is increasingly flowing towards the Tricity (Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula) cluster.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- PAT margin expanded by 220 bps YoY to 18.6%, largely driven by lower interest outgo from term debt repayment.
- CGHS rate hike impact (7% to 7.5% benefit expected) is partially visible in Q1, with fuller impact expected from Q2 onwards. Additional benefits will be utilized for high-end equipment upgrades and maintenance CAPEX rather than directly inflating EBITDA.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total bed additions in calendar year 2026 will be 1,490 beds (a 46% increase over CY25 capacity of 3,250 beds).
- Rudrapur acquisition cost: Rs. 177 crore for 330 beds; additional CAPEX expected to be Rs. 10-12 crore.
- Zirakpur acquisition: Rs. 107 crore for 150 beds, scheduled to commission in November 2026.
- Palam Vihar extension: Rs. 25 crore for 100 beds (CAPEX per bed of Rs. 25 lakh).
- Blended CAPEX per bed across new facilities up to March 2028 is maintained at Rs. 36 lakh per bed (total CAPEX of Rs. 767 crore for 2,130 beds).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Term debt (ex-lease) stood at Rs. 25.6 crore.
- Fixed deposits at Rs. 300 crore; net worth at Rs. 2,100 crore.
- Promoter equity dilution timeline: Regulatory window allows up to December 2028; management is evaluating opportunities and has no immediate timeline to share.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Occupancy moderated to 56% in Q1 FY27 from 68% in Q1 FY26 due to the immediate addition of 960 beds (Bhatinda, Agra, Panchkula) entering the denominator.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anshul Agrawal (Emkay Global) asked about the trajectory of EBITDA losses at Greenfield/new units like Panchkula and Agra.** Rajesh Sharma clarified that no EBITDA losses are expected; new units start with 10%-12% margins in year one, helping maintain consolidated EBITDA margins at 26%-27%.
2. **Kashish Thakur (Elara Capital) asked if the CGHS rate hike caused heat in the oncology segment like peers.** Dr. Sanjay Sharma answered that Park did not face heat, maintains good vendor relations, and oncology continues to contribute 9%-10% of revenue.
3. **Nirali Shah (Ashika Investment Managers) asked about management bandwidth and integration bottlenecks for multiple acquisitions.** Dr. Sanjay Sharma explained that a second line of top-level management is continuously trained in existing hospitals for 6-8 months before being deployed to new assets to ensure smooth protocol replication.
4. **Shubham Padhiar (Chhattisgarh Investments) questioned the increase in acquisition CAPEX per bed (e.g., Rudrapur at ~Rs. 54-55 lakh/bed).** Rajesh Sharma clarified that on a blended basis across all ongoing projects (including extensions like Palam Vihar), the total CAPEX per bed remains at Rs. 36 lakh.
5. **Sumit Gupta (Antique Stock Broking) asked about doctor retention and HoD attrition rates.** Dr. Sanjay Sharma stated that attrition is negligible, driven by high paymasters, performance bonuses based on monthly clinical outcomes/patient satisfaction, and robust ESOPs, with doctors acting as partners rather than facing commercial volume pressures.

### One-line takeaway
Park Medi World reported a strong Q1 FY27 with 19% revenue growth and 35% PAT growth, remaining fully funded for its expansion to 5,740 beds by FY28 while maintaining steady 26.5% EBITDA margins.
