# Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the strongest quarter to date with 52% YoY revenue growth, newer hospitals turning EBITDA positive during H1, and a strategic primary capital investment partnership announced with Advent International.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained EBITDA margin guidance of upwards of 24% despite a 0.3% impact from cancer drug price capping.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to a more aggressive expansion outlook supported by the Advent International partnership, aiming for 7,000 to 8,000 beds over the next 3 to 5 years (up from the previous 5,000 beds target).
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Cancer medication capping introduced as a new topic with a quantified impact of 0.3% reduction on EBITDA; IT matter resolved/demand capped. Monsoon construction delays disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Faridabad Sector 20 and Agra maintained positive EBITDA status; Model Town on track to break even next quarter.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 431.4 crore (up 52% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 824.1 crore (up 52% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 102.0 crore (up 50% YoY, 11% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 23.6%
- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin (excl. ramp-up losses at New Delhi and Faridabad Sector 20):** 28.0%
- **PAT:** Rs 48.5 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 93.9 crore
- **H1 Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 111.3 crore (up 49% YoY)
- **ARPOB:** Rs ~36,000 (up 11% YoY)
- **Occupancy:** 72% blended on operational beds
- **Cash and Bank Balances:** Rs 428.9 crore
- **Net Debt:** Rs 39.2 crore
- **Advent International Investment:** Rs 3,150 crore through preferential issue

### Highlights
- Existing hospitals (Noida, Greater Noida, Noida Extension, Jhansi-Orchha) grew 24% YoY with EBITDA margins upwards of 28% and occupancy at 78%.
- Newer hospitals contributed 28% of group revenue (Rs 120.5 crore) and turned EBITDA positive collectively in H1.
- Faridabad Sector 20 generated ~Rs 40 crore revenue in Q2; Model Town reported ~Rs 26 crore revenue (losses narrowed to Rs 3.8 crore); Agra reported ~Rs 20 crore revenue.
- Total capacity reached ~2,800 beds.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to build a franchise of 7,000 to 8,000 beds over the next 3 to 5 years.
- Existing organic plan to reach 5,000 beds remains intact, with an additional 2,000 to 3,000 beds expected from the Advent partnership.
- Group-level EBITDA margin guidance maintained at upwards of 24%.
- Model Town Hospital, Delhi, expected to break even in H2 FY27 (specifically December).
- Gurugram hospital expected to operationalize next financial year and break even in 15 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Payer mix: Government institution business at 34% (down from 37% last quarter). Private insurance and cash patients make up the rest equally, with private insurance slightly higher. International business at 4%-5%.
- Newer hospitals maintain a low government business mix of 15%-20%.
- International business grew over 35% comparing H2 last year to H1 this year, despite some headwinds in the Gulf.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cancer drug price capping (30% margin cap) impact estimated at a minor 0.3% reduction in EBITDA, which management plans to offset via operational efficiencies.
- Gross debt increased due to ~Rs 100 crore spent on the Gurugram facility and ~Rs 100 crore for medical equipment/construction across Model Town, Faridabad Sector 20, and Gurugram. Management stated this is the peak level of debt.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Advent investment of Rs 3,150 crore to be received in two tranches: Rs 1,750 crore post-CCI approval this month, and the next after 15 to 18 months.
- Advent funds will primarily be deployed to acquire 100% equity-owned, EBITDA-positive running assets/chains (adding ~2,000 beds), with 40%-50% of those beds potentially going live next fiscal year.
- Gurugram Sector 40 (250 beds) construction/revamping is underway for a high-premium launch next financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balances stood at Rs 428.9 crore.
- Net debt stood at Rs 39.2 crore.
- H1 OCF conversion is lower seasonally, but management expects full-year OCF-to-EBITDA conversion upwards of 70%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues (war, Gulf situation) affected international patient inflows in recent months.
- Construction work in Delhi Model Town currently on hold due to GRAP 2 restrictions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Praveen (Fortune Towers Investments) asked about the impact of cancer medication capping.** Management replied it causes a 0.3% EBITDA reduction, which will be offset by operational efficiencies, leaving overall EBITDA guidance unchanged.
- **Shrikant Akolkar asked about the tranches of the Advent deal.** Management stated Rs 1,750 crore comes in the first tranche post-CCI approval this month, and the rest in 15-18 months.
- **Akshat Mehta asked about rising gross debt.** Management explained it funded Gurugram and equipment for Model Town/Faridabad, noting debt has peaked and will decline.
- **Meet Mehta asked about oncology status in Model Town and Faridabad.** Management stated Faridabad Sector 20 radiation/nuclear onco will start in 45-60 days, while Model Town will take 6-9 months due to construction/bunker requirements under GRAP rules.
- **Kashish Thakur asked about potential Supreme Court capping on other drugs.** Management noted they have not seen final rulings, have made industry representations, and will adapt strategies to meet targets.

### One-line takeaway
Yatharth delivered record financial performance in Q2 FY27 and secured a transformative Rs 3,150 crore primary capital partnership with Advent International to accelerate its bed capacity expansion to 7,000-8,000 beds over the next 3 to 5 years.
