# Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strongest quarter to date with 52% YoY revenue growth, 50% EBITDA growth, new hospitals turning EBITDA positive, and transformative Advent partnership announced.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained EBITDA margin guidance of 24%+ despite 0.3% oncology drug cap impact; capacity target expanded from 5,000 to 7,000-8,000 beds with Advent capital.
- **Tone and demand view:** More bullish – "strongest quarter to date"; new hospitals now 28% of revenue (vs 27% in Q1) and collectively EBITDA positive in H1; international business growing but facing geopolitical headwinds.
- **New topics/risks:** Advent partnership (Rs 3,150 cr), quantified oncology drug cap impact (0.3% EBITDA), Supreme Court drug capping risk, IT matter resolved, GRAP 2 construction delays for Model Town oncology.
- **Old topics disappeared:** Dividend/ESOP not mentioned; monsoon construction delays not referenced.
- **Last quarter's promises delivered:** Faridabad Sector-20 EBITDA positive with Rs 40 cr revenue; Gurugram on track for FY28; government payer mix in new hospitals at 15-20% (target met); ARPOB growth 11% YoY (guidance 9-10%); revenue growth 52% YoY (guidance to surpass 37%).

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 431.4 crore (up 52% YoY, 10% QoQ); H1 Rs 824.1 crore (up 52% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 102 crore (up 50% YoY, 11% QoQ); margin 23.6%; adjusted margin (excl. New Delhi & Faridabad Sector-20) 28%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 48.5 crore (up 17% YoY); H1 Rs 93.9 crore.
*   **Cash Profit (PAT + Depreciation):** Up 36% YoY.
*   **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 111.3 crore (up 49% YoY).
*   **ARPOB:** Rs 36,000 (up 11% YoY); Noida Extension ~Rs 51,000, Model Town ~Rs 47,000, Greater Noida ~Rs 43,000.
*   **Occupancy:** 72% blended on operationalized beds; existing hospitals 78% (Noida, Jhansi >90%), Greater Faridabad 77%, Model Town 41%, Faridabad Sector-20 59%, Agra 58%.
*   **Payer Mix:** Government 34% (down from 37% QoQ); private insurance and cash roughly equal (private slightly higher); international 4-5%.
*   **Balance Sheet:** Cash Rs 428.9 crore; net debt Rs 39.2 crore (peak debt reached, expected to decline).
*   **Advent Deal:** Rs 3,150 crore primary investment (equity + warrants); first tranche Rs 1,750 crore post CCI approval (expected Nov 2026); second tranche after 15-18 months.

### Highlights
*   Existing hospitals (Noida, Greater Noida, Noida Extension, Jhansi-Orchha): Rs 310.9 crore revenue, 24% YoY growth, EBITDA margin >28%.
*   Newer hospitals (Greater Faridabad, New Delhi, Faridabad Sector-20, Agra): Rs 120.5 crore revenue, 28% of group revenue (vs 11% YoY), collectively EBITDA positive in H1.
*   Faridabad Sector-20: EBITDA positive in record time, Rs ~40 crore revenue, ARPOB ~Rs 40,000, >80% cash/private insurance.
*   Model Town Delhi: Rs ~26 crore revenue, ARPOB ~Rs 50,000, losses narrowing (Q1 Rs 55M, Q2 Rs 38M), breakeven expected Dec 2026.
*   Agra: EBITDA positive, Rs ~20 crore revenue, adding CTVS, pediatric cardiac, robotics; renovation underway.
*   Gurugram (250 beds): Operationalization FY28, target ARPOB >Rs 50,000, breakeven target 15 months.
*   Clinical excellence: Rare liver condition and brachial plexus surgery at Greater Noida; first memory clinic in Faridabad Sector-20; ASSOCHAM Hospital Excellence Award; top rankings in Times of India and Outlook.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Upwards of 24% for coming quarters/years (unchanged despite oncology cap).
*   **Organic Capacity:** 5,000 beds in 3-4 years (unchanged).
*   **Advent-Driven Capacity:** Additional 2,000-3,000 beds in ~4 years; 40-50% of these beds live by next FY.
*   **Overall Target:** 7,000-8,000 beds in 3-5 years.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Existing plan 30% YoY for 3.5 years (~2.5x); with Advent deployment, target >3x current EBITDA in 3.5-4 years.
*   **Acquisition Strategy:** EBITDA-positive, 100% equity-owned assets; focus on multi-specialty chains in North India; open to other regions for hospital clusters.
*   **Government Payer Mix:** Target 20% overall; new hospitals already at 15-20%; Gurugram similar.
*   **International:** Targeting CIS, Africa; Noida airport opening a catalyst; separate team driving >35% growth H2 FY26 vs H1 FY27 (recent geopolitical headwinds).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Payer mix shifting favorably: government share down to 34% from 37% QoQ.
*   International markets: CIS (Uzbekistan center noted earlier), Africa (Mauritius, Ethiopia, South Sudan) via channel partners and hospital alliances.
*   Geopolitical issues (war, Gulf situation) recently affecting international patient flow.
*   Oncology drug price cap (30% margin on listed drugs): 0.3% EBITDA impact, to be offset by operational efficiencies.
*   Supreme Court judgment on broader drug capping: matter sub judice, industry representations made; management will adapt strategies to maintain guidance.
*   Competitive landscape: Greater Noida/Noida Extension oncology volumes increasing, adding leading doctors, no talent pressure; Model Town Delhi in dense area with competition but strong growth.
