# Manipal Health Enterprises Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-27 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust volume-led growth with revenue up 38% YoY and network EBITDA up 26% YoY.

### Key numbers
- Q1 revenue: INR3,091 crore (up >38% YoY)
- Network EBITDA: INR749 crore (up >26% YoY)
- Operating margin (ex-Sahyadri): 25%
- Network operating margin (including Sahyadri): 24.2%
- Inpatient volumes: up 39% YoY
- Outpatient volumes: up 26% YoY
- Average occupancy: 65% (up 290 bps YoY)
- ALOS: 2.7 days
- ARPOB (ex-Sahyadri): INR77,200 per day (up ~9% YoY)
- Digital revenue: INR710 crore (~23% of overall revenue)
- Sahyadri revenue: INR332 crore (up >13% YoY)
- Sahyadri EBITDA: INR58 crore (up ~19% YoY, ~17.5% margin)
- Sahyadri ARPOB: ~INR45,000 per day (up 15% YoY)
- Sahyadri ALOS: 2.8 days (down 8% YoY)
- Net debt to EBITDA (post-IPO debt repayment in Q2): 0.9x (down from 2.8x at Q1 level)
- Planned 3-4 year capex: ~INR4,000 crore
- FY27 capex guidance: ~INR2,000 crore (~INR900 crore spent in Q1)

### Highlights
- Inaugural earnings call for Manipal Health Enterprises Limited.
- Growth driven by volumes and high-acuity Centres of Excellence (CONGO-R: Cardiology, Oncology, Neurology, Gastroenterology, Orthopaedics, Renal Sciences), which contributed ~65% of Q1 revenue and saw IP revenues grow by 45% YoY.
- Oncology contribution to network revenue increased to 12.2% from 10.8% YoY.
- Digital health initiatives: e-pharmacy processed >15,000 orders across 23 hospitals in 4 metro cities; telehealth completed >17,000 virtual consultations; chatbot/MAI handled >9,600 interactions.
- Clinical milestones included world's first robotic pancreatic surgery on a 1-month-old infant at Whitefield (Bangalore), state's first emergency living-donor liver transplant in Vijayawada, and first robotic renal transplants at greenfield facilities Kanakapura and Yelahanka.
- Commissioned 103 brownfield beds along with linear accelerator and PET-CT at Nashik (taking licensed capacity to 307 beds).
- Launched 50th hospital (300 beds) in Electronic City, Bangalore in July 2026, bringing total Bangalore capacity to nearly 3,000 beds across 13 facilities.
- Announced acquisition of business operations and assets of Kinder Hospital in Whitefield, Bangalore (adding 100 beds with land and building included).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects secular demand tailwinds, volume growth, and ramp-up of recent greenfields to sustain performance.
- Raipur greenfield expected to commission in Q4 FY27.
- Kinder Hospital expected to be remodeled into a multispecialty facility within 8-9 months.
- Ahilya Nagar expected to add ~80 beds in FY28.
- Long-term leverage comfort level at 1.5x to 2x net debt to EBITDA.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was volume-led across both inpatient (up 39%) and outpatient (up 26%) segments.
- International patient growth was strong at ~55% YoY, representing ~3% of overall revenue.
- Collection from government scheme patients was slightly slow during the quarter, reflecting broader industry trends. In the East (AMRI, Medica, Columbia Asia), cash and TPA business grew 22% while government mix remained flat.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Ex-Sahyadri margin dip of 1.5% YoY (from 26.5% to 25%) was attributed to a one-off material cost benefit of INR15 crore in Q1 last year (Medica pre-acquisition contract) and fully-staffed greenfields ramping up.
- Greenfields in Bangalore (Kanakapura and Yelahanka) achieved 13% EBITDA margin in Q1, with Kanakapura breaking even in month 5 and Yelahanka in month 2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- ~INR900 crore front-ended capex spent in Q1 out of an anticipated ~INR2,000 crore for FY27.
- Planned ~INR4,000 crore capex over the next 3-4 years for ~3,000 bed additions.
- Pipeline includes Raipur (commissioning Q4 FY27), Kinder Whitefield (100 beds, acquisition pending CPs, to be remodeled), Juhu (Mumbai), Wakad (Pune), and Ahilya Nagar.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to EBITDA stood at 2.8x in Q1 and will reduce to 0.9x in Q2 following the deployment of IPO proceeds to repay debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slow collections from government scheme patients observed during the quarter.
- Transition in scheme mix in the Eastern region (under discussion with authorities).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC Bank) on Sahyadri integration timeline and playbook:** Management stated integration takes 16-18 months following a proven playbook (regional HR structure, clinician interoperability with 58 doctors moving across brands, digital adoption, and service strategy). EBITDA margin already reached ~17.5% in Q1.
2. **Neha Manpuria (BofA Securities) on ex-Sahyadri margin drivers:** Management explained that greenfield ramp-ups temporarily dilute composite margins, but mature hospitals exceed the 24% network average, providing headroom as greenfields scale.
3. **Bansi Desai (JP Morgan) on bed commissioning schedule for FY27:** Management confirmed 300 beds added via Electronic City in Q2, Nashik expansion completed (103 beds), Raipur expected in Q4 FY27, and Kinder Whitefield addition possible in FY27.
4. **Shyam Srinivasan (JP Morgan) on core/ex-Sahyadri margin bridge:** Management quantified the YoY ex-Sahyadri margin dip, isolating a 0.6% one-off base effect from last year and ~0.5% greenfield doctor cost loading.
5. **Karan Vora (Equirus Capital) on medical college entry:** Management clarified they have *no plans* to bring medical colleges into the listed entity, remaining strictly focused on tertiary and quaternary care delivery.

### One-line takeaway
Manipal Health Enterprises delivered a strong inaugural Q1 FY27 with 38% revenue growth and 26% EBITDA growth, driven by 39% surge in inpatient volumes, expanding high-acuity Centres of Excellence, and successful initial turnaround of the Sahyadri acquisition.
