# Dr. Lal PathLabs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest quarterly growth in the last 5 years, driven by strong operational metrics and robust volume growth across the country.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 874 crore (up 19.6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,672 crore (up 19.4% YoY)
- **Sample Volume:** 28.8 million (up 13.6% YoY)
- **Patient Volume:** 9 million (up 9% YoY)
- **Revenue per patient:** Rs 976 (up 9.7% YoY from Rs 889)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 280 crore (up 25.1% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 32.1%
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 528 crore (up 26.7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 31.6%
- **PBT:** Rs 261 crore (up 28.1% YoY); PBT Margin: 29.8%
- **PAT:** Rs 194 crore (up 27.5% YoY); PAT Margin: 22.2%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 364 crore (up 27.3% YoY); PAT Margin: 21.8%
- **EPS:** Rs 11.5 (vs Rs 9 in Q2 FY26)
- **Net Cash:** Rs 1,746 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Working Capital Days:** Net working capital negative at 16 days (DSO: 17 days, DIO: 32 days as of September 30, 2026)
- **Dividend:** 2nd interim dividend of 50% (Rs 5 per share)

### Highlights
- Acquired S N GeneLab in Surat to strengthen genomics portfolio (oncogenomics and reproductive genomics). S N GeneLab generated Rs 60 crore top line and Rs 15 crore EBITDA in FY26.
- Acquired remaining 30% stake in Centrapath Labs, making it a wholly-owned subsidiary.
- Expanded international footprint via acquisition of Sunshine diagnostics in Ghana and a JV in Uzbekistan.
- Launched around 140 new tests in H1 FY27 and 2 first-in-India assays in Q2 FY27.
- Rolled out a new physical patient report format with trend charts and anti-counterfeit watermarks (reaching >80% of patients daily).
- Established an enterprise-wide digital pathology foundation via a hybrid cloud platform.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth to be in high-teens.
- Management expects full-year EBITDA margin to be slightly better than last year's 27.2%.
- Longer-term organic revenue growth expected in mid-teens.
- Long-term patient volume growth expected at 6% to 7%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Patient volume growth is broad-based across all regions of the country.
- Industry is experiencing general buoyancy, alongside migration from unorganized to organized testing.
- Suburban brand has turned the corner, returning to double-digit growth with increased on-ground investments.
- Tier 3 towns and below account for close to 39-40% of revenues.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- CGHS price increase provided a 2% to 3% advantage to revenue growth in Q2.
- Cost pressures noted from dollar appreciation, geopolitical situations, minimum wage changes, PF rule changes, and logistics.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 32 labs over the last 2 years, with another 15 to 18 labs planned for FY27.
- Continued organic expansion in South India alongside searches for inorganic opportunities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong net cash position of Rs 1,746 crore.
- Net working capital remains negative at 16 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- CGHS price increase benefit is temporary and will fade after 4 quarters.
- Potential inflationary pressures from foreign exchange movements and regulatory cost notices (PF, gratuity).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tausif Shaikh (BNP Paribas) on S N GeneLab valuation:** Management defended the acquisition, stating it is a profitable business (Rs 60 crore revenue, Rs 15 crore EBITDA in FY26) and a strategic fit for genomics. *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Prakash Kapadia (Entrust Family Office) on growth drivers:** Management attributed volume growth to industry buoyancy, previous lab/collection center expansions, and geographic reach. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Akshaya Shinde (Centrum Broking) on radiology pilots:** Management clarified that radiology is not a standalone investment but a service extension to retain patient loyalty in core markets like Delhi NCR. *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Yogesh Soni (Haitong Securities) on other expenses increase:** Management explained that costs rose due to infrastructure expansion (32 new labs over 2 years, 15-18 planned for FY27) and wage/logistic inflation. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Shyam Srinivasan (Goldman Sachs) on price hikes and cash deployment:** Management stated there is no philosophical aversion to price hikes, but timing depends on cost pressures and market benchmarks; larger M&A platform deals are being evaluated for cash deployment. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
Dr. Lal PathLabs reported its highest quarterly growth in 5 years (19.6% YoY) for Q2 FY27, backed by strong volume uptake, disciplined working capital, and strategic genomics acquisitions.
