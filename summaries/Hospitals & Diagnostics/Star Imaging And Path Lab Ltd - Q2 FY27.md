# Star Imaging And Path Lab Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue grew by 8% YoY, but EBITDA and PAT margins moderated due to upfront investments in human capital and advertising, machine maintenance, and a change in geographical mix, alongside higher trade receivables.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 48.9 crore (vs Rs 45.2 crore YoY, ~8% growth)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 14.1 crore (vs Rs 17.3 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 28.9% (vs 38.2% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 7.8 crore (vs Rs 10 crore YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 16% (vs 22.2% YoY)
- **Finance Cost:** Rs 0.33 crore (down 74% YoY from Rs 1.3 crore)
- **Net Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** Negative 0.1x (vs negative 0.3x)
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** 18.5%
- **Return on Equity (ROE):** 14.2%
- **Trade Receivables:** Rs 64 crore (vs Rs 49.9 crore as of March)
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Negative Rs 6.2 crore for H1 FY27 (due to a trade receivable outflow of approximately Rs 17.5 crore)
- **Capex / Capital Commitment for New Projects:** Cumulative Rs 30 crore (including Dwarka and government project)
- **Planned FY27 Capex:** Rs 20 crore to Rs 25 crore

### Highlights
- Added 2 new hospitals to the empanelment network, taking total hospital tie-ups to over 150.
- Established a CT scan facility in collaboration with RG Stone in Faridabad and expanded MRI capacity in Tilak Nagar.
- Dedicated laboratory infrastructure for genome sequencing is under development.
- Management noted that trade receivables increased to Rs 64 crore, largely tied to legacy COVID transitions and Delhi Union Territory government dues, which require historical transaction record resubmission following a change in government. Received Rs 7 crore against outstanding receivables during the first week of Diwali.
- *Comparison with Record:* The call confirms the planned FY27 capex of Rs 20-25 crore for equipment and the new Dwarka centre as stated in the earlier record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA-level margins to normalize toward the 35% range and PAT margins to normalize towards the 25% range in the medium term as investments are absorbed and the mix evolves.
- Management targets cash flow neutrality by the end of FY27.
- The new full-fledged Diagnostic Center in Dwarka is expected to generate annual revenue of Rs 5 crore to Rs 6 crore in its initial year, achieving operational breakeven within 6 to 8 months and optimal performance after 2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- B2B and B2C segments showed strong growth, growing by 20% and 21% respectively, while the B2G segment remained broadly stable.
- B2B and B2C segments combined contribute approximately 48% of total revenue during the first half.
- For B2B partnerships with private institutions/hospitals, the payment cycle is 30 to 45 days; B2C operates on cash payment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin moderation was driven by: (1) expenses toward human capital, advertising, and marketing; (2) minor upgradation and maintenance work on machines; and (3) a change in geographical mix where Uttar Pradesh operations slightly declined and Delhi accounted for a higher share.
- Finance costs declined by 74% YoY to Rs 0.33 crore following the full repayment of 2 loans during the period.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- A full-fledged Diagnostic Center in Dwarka equipped with CT, MRI, ultrasound, and X-ray facilities is targeted for commissioning in November (subject to PCPNDT approval).
- Site preparation and machine orders are completed for a pipeline covering CT, MRI, ultrasound, and X-ray services, with MoU signings pending.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables increased to Rs 64 crore from Rs 49.9 crore in March, resulting in negative operating cash flow of Rs 6.2 crore for H1 FY27.
- Around Rs 16 crore of receivables are pending for more than 6 months, primarily related to legacy COVID transitions and government dues.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High trade receivables and delayed collections from B2G contracts, specifically subcontracted models where collection depends on the payment cycle of both the government department and the intermediary contractor.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments)** asked about the age and composition of receivables. Management clarified that trade receivables stand at Rs 64 crore, with about Rs 16 crore pending for more than 6 months, largely tied to legacy COVID transitions and B2G contracts.
2. **Nitin (Investor)** asked about H2 revenue expectations based on guidance. Management stated they expect around Rs 60 crore to Rs 65 crore in H2.
3. **Kinjal Jain (InvestNova Advisory)** asked about the revenue mix evolution for B2B and B2C. Management stated B2B and B2C will remain in the 50% to 60% ratio range and are the primary focus since B2G tenders have no fixed guarantee.
4. **Maitri Shah (Sapphire)** asked about the performance of new hospital tie-ups. Management stated the two additional tie-ups with RG Stone hospital installed with CT machines are performing well.
5. **Akshi Chauhan (Valora Advisors)** asked how the company stays competitive against new players and if there is pricing pressure. Management stated they bid strictly based on feasibility and do not engage in aggressive L1 bidding like others.

### One-line takeaway
Star Imaging reported an 8% YoY revenue increase to Rs 48.9 crore in H1 FY27, but experienced margin moderation and negative operating cash flow due to upfront growth investments and delayed B2G collections.
