# Park Medi World Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 25% YoY, but EBITDA margin contracted to 19.9% due to operating drag from newly opened hospitals and elevated consumable/F&B expenses.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (FY27 revenue guided to ~Rs 2,050–2,060 crore vs Rs 2,080 crore previously; FY27 EBITDA guided to Rs 540–550 crore vs Rs 530 crore previously).
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone shifted from fully positive to mixed due to margin compression (19.9% vs 26.5% in Q1 FY27) and consumable cost spikes, though IPD/OPD patient demand remained strong (+31% / +29% YoY).
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter:** Spike in consumable and F&B expenses (+50% per patient meal); receivable stretch to 160 days in Sept 2026; oncology price capping analysis (negligible impact).
- **Last quarter's promises and delivery:** Delivered commissioning of 330-bed Rudrapur hospital (August 1, 2026) and added 680 beds in H1 FY27; however, failed to keep new units margin-neutral (3 new facilities had an 8% EBITDA loss drag in H1).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 511 crore (up 25% YoY from Rs 410 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Operating EBITDA ex-other income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 102 crore (margin of 19.9%)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 54.5 crore (margin of 10.7%)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 987 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 228 crore (up 5% YoY, margin of 23.1%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 143 crore (margin of 14.5%)
- **IPD Volume (Q2 FY27):** 31,000 patients (up 31% YoY)
- **OPD Volume (Q2 FY27):** 259,000 patients (up 29% YoY)
- **ARPOB (Q2 FY27):** Rs 30,646 (up 13% YoY from Rs 27,007)
- **ALOS (Q2 FY27):** 5.4 days (improved 14% YoY from 6.3 days)
- **Network Occupancy:** 56% in Q2 and H1 FY27 (vs 68% in Q2 FY26)
- **Operational Bed Days (Q2 FY27):** 166,742
- **Doctor Count:** 1,400 as of September 2026 (up by 232 doctors from March 2026)
- **Term Debt (ex-lease):** Rs 178 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Fixed Deposits:** Rs 325 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Net Worth:** Rs 2,164 crore as of H1 FY27 (vs Rs 1,196 crore in H1 FY26)

### Highlights
- Existing 14 established hospitals delivered an H1 FY27 EBITDA margin of 26% (EBITDA of Rs 234 crore on Rs 915 crore revenue) and PAT margin of 17%.
- Three newly added facilities (Agra, Panchkula, Rudrapur) generated Rs 72 crore revenue in H1 FY27 with an EBITDA loss of 8% (Rs 6 crore EBITDA drag).
- High-end specialties contributed ~64% of Q2 FY27 revenue (up 730 bps YoY) and ~63% in H1 FY27 (up 640 bps YoY).
- City Hospital Rudrapur (V3 Healthcare, 80% stake) reached EBITDA positive in month one with a ~Rs 5 crore/month revenue run rate; management plans to acquire the remaining 20% stake by FY27-end or Q1 FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Financial Guidance:** Management expects ~Rs 2,050 to Rs 2,060 crore revenue and Rs 540 to Rs 550 crore EBITDA.
- **FY28 Outlook:** Management expects ~Rs 2,550 crore revenue, with new facilities contributing ~20% of topline and 10%–12% of EBITDA.
- **CGHS Rate Hike Benefit:** Management estimates an effective revenue benefit of ~6% (~Rs 58–60 crore upside across 7 months in FY27).
- **Payer Mix Target:** Transitioning towards a 70% government insurance / 30% private insurance & self-pay split over 12–18 months (currently 76% / 24%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Occupancy moderated to 56% due to 1,040 beds added across Agra, Panchkula, and Rudrapur over the past 12 months.
- Case mix continues to shift toward high-end tertiary/quaternary care (transplants, interventional cardiology, robot-assisted joint replacements).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 cost of materials consumed jumped to Rs 116 crore from Rs 77 crore in Q1 FY27 (+65% YoY).
- Patient F&B expenses saw a one-time 50% cost escalation (per-meal cost increased from Rs 270 to Rs 400), creating an Rs 8 crore cost hit in Q2.
- Product mix shift toward high-end procedures led to higher consumption of low-margin specialized medicines and implants.
- Management expects oncology drug price capping to have zero/negligible financial impact on the company.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- On track to expand bed capacity by 40% in FY27 (1,430 beds); 680 beds added in H1 FY27, with 750 beds planned for H2 FY27.
- Currently has 2,300 beds under execution to reach 6,590 total beds by December 2028 (a 54% increase over 4,290 beds as of September 30, 2026).
- Remaining execution capex for 2,300 beds is Rs 750 crore (maintaining capex at Rs 30–35 lakh per bed).
- Prayagraj PPP project: 550 beds, total capex of Rs 210 crore with Rs 85 crore government reimbursement (net cost Rs 125–130 crore / Rs 25 lakh per bed), commissioning by Nov 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables days increased to 160 days as of September 30, 2026, due to scheme collection seasonality and Rs 45 crore acquired receivables from Agra and Rudrapur.
- Collections in October 2026 reduced receivable days to 135–136 days; management targets bringing it below 130 days by March 31, 2027.
- Claims disallowance rate stood at 13.5% in H1 FY27 vs 11.0% YoY.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- EBITDA drag of Rs 6 crore (-8% margin) from 3 newly commissioned hospitals.
- Receivables stretch to 160 days at the end of Q2 FY27.
- Spike in consumable, F&B, and medical implant costs impacting Q2 gross margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anshul Agrawal (Emkay Global):** *Asked for exact financial drag and breakdown of the 3 new hospital units.*  
   - **Management:** Established 14 units generated Rs 915 crore revenue and Rs 234 crore EBITDA (26% margin). The 3 new units generated Rs 72 crore revenue and an EBITDA loss of Rs 6 crore (-8% margin). Brownfield units break even in 4–6 months; greenfield takes 12–15 months.
2. **Kashish Thakur (Elara Capital):** *Asked about impact of oncology price capping and CGHS rate hikes.*  
   - **Management:** Oncology price capping impact is zero/negligible because insurance patients purchase via government dispensaries or ECHS contract discounts apply. CGHS rate hike provides an effective ~6% revenue lift (~Rs 58–60 crore impact in FY27 over 7 months).
3. **Mukesh Astor (Moonshot Ventures):** *Asked why consumable costs jumped from Rs 77 crore to Rs 116 crore sequentially.*  
   - **Management:** Driven by an Rs 8 crore non-pass-through hit from patient F&B cost increases (+50% per meal) and lower margins realized on high-end tertiary care medicines/implants during initial volume ramp-up.
4. **Vivek (Emkay Global):** *Asked about the reasons behind working capital stretch and higher receivable days.*  
   - **Management:** Total debtors stood at Rs 871 crore (160 days), including Rs 45 crore acquired receivables from Agra/Rudrapur. Strong collections in October reduced debtor days to 135–136, and it will end FY27 under 130 days.
5. **Shravan Modi (Syndicate Family Office):** *Asked about FY28 revenue/EBITDA contribution from new beds and operational status of Rudrapur asset.*  
   - **Management:** FY28 revenue expected at ~Rs 2,550 crore, with new units contributing 20% of revenue and 10%–12% of EBITDA. Rudrapur is already EBITDA positive with Rs 5 crore/month revenue, targeting Rs 10 crore/month by April 2027.

### One-line takeaway
Park Medi World continues its rapid expansion toward 6,590 beds by December 2028 with 25% revenue growth, while managing temporary margin drag and consumable inflation from newly integrated assets.
