# Park Medi World Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever H1 revenue and continued aggressive bed expansion while managing short-term margin drags from new units.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained revenue and profitability trajectory, but updated total capacity target to 6,590 beds by December 2028 (vs 5,740 beds by FY28 previously).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, emphasizing structural shift to high-end specialties and steady volume growth despite occupancy dilution from aggressive capacity additions.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Detailed discussion on F&B cost inflation, consumable margin pressures, and detailed quantification of the new hospital EBITDA drag.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Delivered on adding beds in H1 (680 beds added across Panchkula and Rudrapur) and progressing towards the 40% capacity expansion target for FY27.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 987 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 228 crore (margin at 23.1%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 143 crore (margin at 14.5%)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 511 crore (up 25% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 102 crore (margin at 19.9%)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 54.5 crore (margin at 11%)
- **Q2 FY27 IPD volumes:** 31,000 patients (up 31% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 OPD volumes:** 259,000 patients (up 29% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 ARPOB:** Rs 30,646 (up 13% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 ALOS:** 5.4 days (improved 14% YoY from 6.3 days)
- **Network occupancy:** 56% for Q2 and H1 FY27 (vs 68% in Q2 FY26)
- **Term debt (excl. lease liabilities):** Rs 178 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Fixed deposits:** Rs 325 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Net worth:** Rs 2,164 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Total bed capacity:** 4,290 beds as of September 30, 2026

### Highlights
- Served a record 5.39 lakh patients in H1 FY27.
- Established 14 mature hospitals delivered an EBITDA margin of 26% and a PAT margin of 17% in H1 FY27.
- Added 680 beds across Panchkula and Rudrapur so far in FY27.
- Doctor count increased by 232 to 1,400 doctors by September 2027.
- High-end specialties contributed ~64% of Q2 FY27 revenue (up 730 bps YoY).
- Government insurance schemes contributed ~76% of Q2 revenue, with self-pay/private insurance/TPA at 24%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue close to Rs 2,050 crore and EBITDA/PAT figures around Rs 540-550 crore (Note: transcript mentions EBITDA/PAT interchangeably for this range, citing guidance figures).
- Total bed capacity targeted to reach 6,590 beds by December 2028 via 2,300 beds under execution.
- Capex per bed for ongoing 2,300 beds expected to remain in the Rs 30-35 lakh range.
- Debtor days expected to trend down to 125-130 days by the close of FY27.
- Payer mix targeted to shift to a 70:30 split over the next 12 to 18 months.
- Rudrapur asset expected to scale from ~Rs 5 crore/month run rate to over Rs 10 crore/month by April 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Network occupancy diluted to 56% due to the step-up in capacity over the past 12 months (1,040 beds added across Agra, Panchkula, and Rudrapur).
- Case mix continues to shift toward high-end tertiary and quaternary care, led by transplant, interventional cardiology, and robot-assisted joint replacement programs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Three newly opened/acquired hospitals (Agra, Panchkula, Rudrapur) generated Rs 72 crore in H1 revenue with a negative 8% EBITDA margin (-Rs 6 crore EBITDA impact).
- F&B costs saw a one-time 50% increase per meal (rising from Rs 270 to Rs 400), creating a ~Rs 7.5 crore impact across 14 hospitals for the 6-month period.
- Material and medicine costs increased due to a product mix shift toward advanced procedures with lower immediate realization margins.
- Claims disallowed currently at 13.5% (vs 11% YoY), expected to normalize by year-end.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total beds under execution: 2,300 beds to be added by December 2028 for a total capex of Rs 750 crore.
- Prayagraj project includes 550 PPP beds to be commissioned by November 2028 with a government pass-through capex of Rs 85 crore (net capex of Rs 125-130 crore).
- Acquisition and capex spend directed toward Rudrapur and Zirakpur, commissioning costs of Park Platinum and Febris Hospital, and network equipment upgrades.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Term debt (excluding lease liabilities) stood at Rs 178 crore; fixed deposits at Rs 325 crore.
- Net worth increased to Rs 2,164 crore in H1 FY27 from Rs 1,196 crore in H1 FY26.
- Receivables stood at 160 days as of September 30, 2026 (including Rs 40-45 crore from newly acquired Agra and Rudrapur units), which dropped to 135-136 days by October 31, 2026.
- Management committed to buying out the remaining 20% stake in V3 Healthcare (Rudrapur) by the close of FY27 or Q1 FY28.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- EBITDA drag from new/greenfield units (Agra, Panchkula, Rudrapur) impacting blended margins.
- Elevated working capital days and raw material/F&B cost inflation during the quarter.
- Lower near-term network occupancy (56%) due to capacity additions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anshul Agrawal (Emkay Global) asked about revenue, EBITDA drag, and gross margin contraction for the three new units.** Management clarified that the 3 units generated Rs 72 crore revenue in H1 with a -8% EBITDA margin (-Rs 6 crore impact), and attributed consumable cost increases to F&B inflation and advanced procedure mix.
2. **Kashish Thakur (Elara Capital) asked about the financial impact of oncology price capping and CGHS rate hikes.** Management stated the oncology price capping impact is negligible since government scheme patients buy medicines from government dispensaries, and that 95-98% of the CGHS rate hike effect is already factored into current numbers (~6% effective rate hike on revenue).
3. **Mukesh Astor (Moonshot Ventures) questioned the sharp rise in cost of materials consumed.** Management explained it was driven by a 50% jump in F&B costs (taking a ~Rs 8 crore hit in Q2) and a deliberate push into advanced medical procedures consuming more implants and antibiotics.
4. **Vivek (Emkay Global) asked about the increase in H1 receivable days.** Management clarified that days stood at 160 as of September (including Rs 45 crore from new acquisitions), but normalized to 135-136 days by October and will close the year under 130 days.
5. **Shravan Modi (Syndicate Family Office) asked about revenue/EBITDA contribution of FY27 bed additions and Rudrapur's performance.** Management stated FY28 revenue is expected at ~Rs 2,550 crore with 20% coming from newly added hospitals, and noted Rudrapur is already EBITDA positive with a target of Rs 10 crore/month run rate by April 2027.

### One-line takeaway
Park Medi World delivered robust H1 FY27 revenue of Rs 987 crore (up 22% YoY) and absorbed short-term margin dilution from new hospital ramps while expanding its execution pipeline to 6,590 beds by December 2028.
