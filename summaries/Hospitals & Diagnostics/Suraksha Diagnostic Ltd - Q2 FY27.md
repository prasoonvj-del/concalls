# Suraksha Diagnostic Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered healthy high double-digit growth across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT with continued operational improvement.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained 15% to 18% annual revenue growth guidance; steady-state margin next year expected around 34% to 35%.
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, noting seasonal softness expected in Q3 due to Durga Puja/festivities, followed by a pick-up in Q4.
- New topics or risks: Entry into Jharkhand marked; Tripura operations expected in H2.
- Last quarter's promises: Progressing on expansion goals, adding 5 new centers in Q2 to reach 77 total centers.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (Total Income): Rs. 1,005.7 million (+26.4% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 363.2 million (vs Rs. 249.7 million in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin: 36.7% (vs 31.7% in Q2 FY26)
- PAT: Rs. 149.5 million (+69% YoY)
- PAT Margin: 15.1%
- Patients served: 0.42 million
- Tests performed: 2.4 million
- Revenue per patient: Rs. 2,348
- EBITDA per patient: Rs. 861
- Mature centres EBITDA margin: 42%
- Centres under 2 years old EBITDA margin: 11.8% (vs negative 16.8% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs. 1,893.1 million (+23.7% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs. 678.5 million (Margin: 36.3%)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs. 277.96 million (+54% YoY)

### Highlights
- Commissioned 5 new centers during Q2, marking entry into Jharkhand (Dhanbad).
- Tripura infrastructure and equipment installation underway, with operations expected to commence in H2.
- Total network reached 77 centers at the end of H2 FY27.
- Strategic collaborations highlighted: Asian Institute of Immunology and Rheumatology and fetal medicine unit Fetomat.
- Home collection contributes around 10% of total revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects steady-state EBITDA margin next year around 34% to 35%.
- Target to reach 100 centers by the end of FY28, with subsequent expansion slowing down to 5 to 10 centers annually.
- Annual revenue growth guidance maintained at 15% to 18%.
- Q3 revenue expected to decline due to seasonality (festivals), with Q4 expected to recover.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q3 is seasonally muted for the industry due to festivities (Durga Puja and Diwali); Q4 is historically better.
- East India remains underpenetrated in genomics, providing a large growth runway.
- Integrated model (pathology, radiology, consultations) acts as a key differentiator for patient retention and convenience.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded by 1% in Q2 due to inventory consumption and reporting doctor fees.
- Increase in finance costs and depreciation attributed to IndAS 116 (new center lease agreements).
- Corporate overhead expenses include employee cost, marketing, and IT.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Hub-and-spoke model utilized to maximize asset productivity for expensive equipment (MRI, CT scanners).
- Expansion continuing into adjacent states (Jharkhand, Assam, Bihar).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- B2B segment involves marginal pricing and competitive pressure, though offset by holistic service offerings.
- Seasonal impact on Q3 due to festive holidays.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prasenjit Paul (129th Wealth Fund) on Q3 seasonality:** Management confirmed a sequential dip in Q3 due to festivals like Durga Puja, followed by a recovery in Q4.
2. **Suhani Singh (Ross Capital) on mature vs new center margins:** Management explained that mature centers operate at a sustainable 42% EBITDA margin, while under-2-year centers are improving via aging and trajectory.
3. **Abhijeet (129 Wealth Fund) on test volume/revenue per test:** Management clarified a correction in test volumes (up 9% YoY) and noted that rising revenue per test reflects higher high-end and genomic test volumes.
4. **Yash Mehta (Kriti Securities) on integrated model advantages:** Management stated that vertical integration and one-stop convenience drive patient preference and brand loyalty in Eastern India.
5. **Akhilesh Bhatter (Ampersand Capital) on price hikes:** Management clarified that no price hikes were taken; growth in revenue per patient is driven by a richer test mix (genomics and high-end tests).

### One-line takeaway
Suraksha Diagnostic posted a robust Q2 FY27 with revenue crossing Rs. 100 crores (Rs. 1,005.7 million), driven by strong operating leverage, maturing newer centers, and expansion into new geographies like Jharkhand.
