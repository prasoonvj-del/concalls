# Nephrocare Health Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue growth, network expansion into Kazakhstan, and steady operating performance despite upfront international investments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained medium-term revenue growth guidance of 15% to 20% over 3 to 4 years.
- Tone and demand view: Remained bullish on India and international expansion; successfully entered Kazakhstan and technically qualified for the Saudi Arabia Ministry of Health tender.
- New topics or risks: Saudi Arabia MOH tender technical qualification (1 of 12 bidders, bids due February 2027); pharmacy margin capping discussion (immaterial as pharmacy is <1% of revenue); launch of NIDA (NephroPlus International Dialysis Academy) for nurse training.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on entering Kazakhstan (acquired 2 clinics in Almaty) and progressing the Saudi Arabia footprint.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 303 crore (+23.5% YoY, +7.7% QoQ).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 585 crore (+23.6% YoY).
- **Q2 Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 68.9 crore (+3.3% YoY, +5.8% QoQ) at a 22.7% margin.
- **H1 Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 134 crore at a 23% margin.
- **H1 Adjusted PAT:** Rs 76.1 crore (+34.7% YoY) at a 13% margin.
- **Active Guests:** 38,814 (+10% YoY).
- **Q2 Treatments Growth:** +12% YoY.
- **H1 Revenue per Treatment (RPT):** Rs 2,785 (+9.7% YoY).
- **Network:** 570 clinics across 6 countries (500 in India, 70 international).
- **Cash, Deposits & Investments:** Rs 507 crore against borrowings of Rs 80 crore as of September 30.
- **H1 Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 89 crore (67% of adjusted EBITDA).
- **H1 Capital Deployment:** Rs 102 crore.

### Highlights
- Entered Kazakhstan by acquiring two clinics in Almaty.
- Technically qualified for Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Health tender (one of 12 providers and the only one from Asia).
- First clinic in Riyadh operational, with home dialysis started in Riyadh and Jeddah.
- Launched NephroPlus International Dialysis Academy (NIDA) to train renal nurses for international markets.
- India network reached 500 clinics across 314 cities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated medium-term guidance of 15% to 20% revenue growth over 3 to 4 years.
- Management does not provide annual guidance.
- Management expects FY27 capital deployment to exceed FY26's Rs 165 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Dialysis requires scaling volume to amortize overheads over a larger revenue base.
- Network capacity utilization stands at 74%.
- Pharmacy products represent less than 1% of overall revenue, with negligible margins, insulation from potential pharmacy price/margin capping.
- Saudi Arabia MOH tender covers ~11,720 patients across 4 clusters; bids due February 2027.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 EBITDA margin at 23.0% compared to FY26 full-year margin of 23.8% (down 80 bps).
- H1 COGS improved by 95 bps YoY to 22% of revenue.
- H1 Professional and hospital fees improved by 83 bps YoY to 18.4% of revenue.
- Other expenses increased from 18.2% in FY26 to 20% in H1 due to international business development, technology, listed company costs, and ECL provisioning.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capital deployment was Rs 102 crore.
- Network totals 570 clinics across 6 countries.
- Targets 40-50 clinic additions per year in India and 10-15 in the Philippines.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position with Rs 507 crore in cash and investments against Rs 80 crore of borrowings.
- H1 OCF reached Rs 89 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Saudi Arabia tender is highly competitive with a binary outcome (win or lose), remaining in an investment phase until awards are finalized.
- New geographies incur an initial couple of years of suboptimal margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Akshay Thakur (Helios Capital) on India tender models:** Management noted a mix of L1 price and QCBS (quality and cost-based selection) models across states, with QCBS gradually increasing.
- **Divyanshi Shah on margin trends:** Management explained that quarterly YoY margins are not directly comparable due to listed company audit true-ups this year versus year-end only audits previously; margin compression is driven by international expansion investments and public company costs.
- **Nilanjan Karfa on RPT and forex:** Management stated that 3% of revenue growth came from forex (mainly Uzbekistan som appreciation), while 97% came from organic, inorganic, and pricing factors driven by international mix.
- **Naman Bagrecha on utilization:** Management noted network capacity is at 74%, constrained naturally by lower demand for the third shift ending at 10 PM.
- **Deepika Murarka on Saudi capex:** Management did not disclose specific per-country capex, noting Saudi is significantly more capex-heavy than India.

### One-line takeaway
Nephrocare delivered steady H1 growth, expanded into Kazakhstan, and advanced in the Saudi tender process while absorbing upfront international investments.
