# Fortis Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported healthy revenue and EBITDA growth across hospital and diagnostics segments, supported by network expansion, despite emerging regulatory headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 margin guidance (flattish at ~22% post-ESOP), but management refrained from providing future margin guidance due to uncertainty regarding new drug price capping regulations.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from purely operational focus to acknowledging regulatory risks (drug price caps) while maintaining a positive outlook on volume and expansion.
- **New topics:** Drug price capping on 137 oncology drugs emerged as a new potential headwind.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The forensic audit process is ongoing; management confirmed they have submitted the requested information to auditors as per the court-mandated timeline.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,735 crore (+17.3% YoY).
- **Hospital Revenue:** Rs 2,340 crore (+18.6% YoY).
- **Diagnostics Revenue:** Rs 448 crore (+12.2% YoY).
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA (pre-ESOP):** Rs 663 crore (+19.2% YoY).
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA Margin:** 24.2% (vs 23.9% YoY).
- **Hospital EBITDA:** Rs 545 crore (Margin 23.3% vs 22.9% YoY).
- **Diagnostics EBITDA:** Rs 118 crore (Margin 26.2% vs 26.1% YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT (ex-exceptional):** Rs 317 crore (+4% YoY).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 2,188 crore (Net Debt/EBITDA 0.87x).
- **Occupancy:** 72% (vs 71% YoY).
- **ARPP:** Rs 2.1 lakh (+6.8% YoY).

### Highlights
- Acquired 110-bed Suguna Hospital in Bengaluru; facility has potential to expand to 150 beds.
- Entered long-term healthcare services agreement for a 400-bed greenfield super specialty hospital in Ashok Vihar, New Delhi.
- 15 hospital facilities reported EBITDA margin >20%, contributing 84% of hospital revenue.
- Diagnostics: 4,551 customer touchpoints; B2C revenue mix increased to 54% (vs 52% YoY).
- *Record check:* The company's ongoing expansion and the Suguna Hospital acquisition align with the previously stated strategy to scale up the hospital footprint.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets adding 500+ beds this year, plus the Suguna acquisition.
- Brownfield expansions (200 beds/year) planned for the next two years.
- Diagnostics revenue growth expected in the 12-13% range for FY27.
- Management expects FY27 EBITDA margins to be "flattish" at ~22% (post-ESOP).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Key specialties (renal, neuro, orthopedics) recorded YoY revenue growth of 27%, 28%, and 30%, respectively.
- Digital channel revenue grew 18.6% YoY, contributing 29.5% to hospital revenue.
- Management noted that drug price capping on 137 oncology drugs is a recent development; impact assessment is ongoing, but it is expected to be negative for profitability.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- ESOP expenses impact reported EBITDA by ~1.5% of revenue.
- Management stated that price increase capability is limited in the industry; ARPP growth is primarily driven by case mix (e.g., robotic surgeries, complex cases).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Committed capex is ~Rs 3,500 crore, spread over the next 3-3.5 years.
- Focus clusters: NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Punjab.
- FMRI new block is ready and awaiting final approvals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-EBITDA stands at 0.87x (vs 0.96x in Q2 FY26).
- Management stated that IHH remains committed to increasing its stake to 50%+; capital is not a constraint for growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory uncertainty regarding drug price capping on oncology drugs.
- Forensic audit: Management confirmed information has been submitted to auditors; the process is expected to take 6 months as per court timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Neha Manpuria (BofA):** Asked about bed expansion visibility. Management confirmed 3-4 year visibility and focus on cluster-based growth.
- **Shyam Srinivasan (Goldman Sachs):** Questioned diagnostics volume growth vs peers. Management attributed the gap to brand transition (now behind them) and expects to bridge the gap with B2C focus.
- **Dhvani Shah (DSP MF):** Inquired about margin guidance and drug price impact. Management refrained from future margin guidance due to regulatory noise.
- **Saion Mukherjee (Nomura):** Asked about capex and IRR benchmarks. Management stated an internal benchmark of 15% IRR and 8-10 year payback, though strategic fit can override financial metrics.
- **Abdulkader Puranwala (ICICI Securities):** Asked about forensic audit status. Management confirmed they are cooperating and looking to put the matter behind them.

### One-line takeaway
Fortis delivered strong operational growth and continued its cluster-based expansion, though management turned cautious on future margin guidance due to new regulatory risks regarding oncology drug pricing.
