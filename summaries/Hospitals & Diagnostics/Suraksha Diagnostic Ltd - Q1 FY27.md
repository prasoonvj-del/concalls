# Suraksha Diagnostic Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 21% YoY revenue growth, 40% PAT growth, and margin expansion driven by operating leverage and maturing centers.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (Total Income): Rs. 887 million (vs Rs. 735 million in Q1 FY26, +21% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 315 million (vs Rs. 247 million in Q1 FY26, +28% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 36% (vs 34% in Q1 FY26)
- PAT: Rs. 128 million (vs Rs. 92 million in Q1 FY26, +40% YoY)
- PAT Margin: 14.7%
- Patients served: 0.38 million
- Tests performed: 2.10 million
- Tests per patient: 5.56
- Revenue per patient: Rs. 2,321
- EBITDA per patient: Rs. 835
- Total network: 72 centres at the end of the quarter
- Mature centres EBITDA margin: 40.9%
- Centres under 2 years old EBITDA margin: 6.5% (turned positive from negative 5.5% last quarter)
- Genomics revenue: Rs. 13.7 million (+136% YoY)

### Highlights
- Commissioned 1 hub and 3 spoke centres in Q1 FY27; added 2 hubs and 3 spoke centres subsequently in July and August.
- Extended footprint beyond West Bengal by commissioning a hub in Jharkhand; a hub in Tripura will follow shortly.
- Suraksha Genomics recorded five consecutive quarters of sequential growth, with 80% B2C and 20% B2B split.
- Introduced a blood test for Alzheimer's and initiated a project on early detection of Alzheimer's from plain brain MRIs using AI with CSIR.
- Shifted main biochemistry business from Siemens to Roche under a better negotiated deal, aiding COGS margins.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects overall EBITDA margin not to go below 34% for the year.
- Maintained target to open 100 centres by FY28.
- Capex guidance for the year is around Rs. 70 crores to Rs. 80 crores.
- Genomics business expected to grow around 20% quarterly for the financial year.
- Management expects centre additions going forward to be split around 50-50 between new geographies and existing core territories.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 is seasonally strong due to the disease/rainy season; Q3 is muted due to festivities and winter; Q4 is strong.
- Demand for genomics is growing due to personalized oncology treatments.
- Organized players' market share in East India is slightly lower, with the market largely dominated by mom-and-pop stores and small diagnostic centres. Suraksha is the largest organized player in the East.
- Wellness piece stands at around 4% to 5% of revenue, with corporate sales expected to pick up.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by volume expansion and operating leverage on fixed costs.
- Mature centres' EBITDA margin at 40.9% is deemed sustainable.
- Genomic margins currently range between 15% and 20%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Hub centre capex: Rs. 10 crores to Rs. 10.5 crores.
- Spoke centre capex: Rs. 1.5 crores to Rs. 2 crores.
- Spoke center-level break-even takes 3 to 4 months; HO-level break-even takes 8 to 9 months.
- Genomics capex over the last 3 years is around Rs. 22 crores; no major additional capex expected in the next 2 to 3 years.
- Planned additions for the rest of the year: 4 hubs and 7 spokes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cannibalization impact occurs when setting up spoke centres near existing collection centres or mature locations, though overall business and patient reach expand.
- B2B segment involves margin pressures due to competitive realizations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Om (Phillip Capital) on margin sustainability:** Management stated that mature centre margins are sustainable, and less-than-2-year-old centres will continue showing positive trends, keeping overall EBITDA at a minimum of 34%.
2. **Rajat Baldewa (Kizuna Wealth) on repeat patient rate:** Management clarified that the percentage drop in repeat rates is due to a faster increase in new patient numbers; absolute repeat numbers are not degrowing.
3. **Ankur Kumar (Alpha Capital) on ROCE/ROE gap vs peers:** Management explained that lower ROCE is solely due to the heavy skew of new centres (27 out of 72 centres being less than 2 years old).
4. **Aditya (Securities Investment Management) on Kolkata market potential:** Management stated there are currently 9 hubs in Greater Kolkata and scope exists to at least double this count.
5. **Mohammed Patel (Alpesh/Edelweiss) on conservative revenue guidance:** Management noted they are a relatively new organization coming to terms at their own pace, hence the conservative stance.
6. **Bhavya Sanghavi (Phillip Capital) on CGHS benefits:** Management noted that a CGHS rate increase in mid-October 2025 provided an incremental benefit, with public sector corporate contribution at around 15%.

### One-line takeaway
Suraksha Diagnostic delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 21% revenue growth, 40% PAT growth, and an EBITDA margin of 36%, driven by operating leverage, robust regional expansion, and improving profitability in newer cohorts.
