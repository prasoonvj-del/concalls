# Manipal Health Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Best-ever quarter in company history driven by robust patient volume growth, strong high-acuity performance, and expanding margins.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall outlook; long-term margin guidance for Sahyadri (23%-24% over 4-5 years) reiterated, and bed addition roadmap clarified (~440-500 beds in H2, ~100 beds in FY28 plus Kinder).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to even higher optimism with occupancies moving up from 65% to 70.7% and greenfields like Kanakapura (25%) and Yelahanka (24%) reaching high EBITDA margins rapidly.
- New topics or risks: Gurugram land lease registration and deployment of Da Vinci 5 robotic surgical system discussed; oncology drug capping addressed by management as having no significant impact. Old risks around government scheme transitions in the East persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Acquisition of Kinder Hospital in Bangalore completed as planned; Sahyadri margin expanded further to 19.5% from 17.5% in Q1.

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Revenue: ~INR3,400 crore (up >37% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: INR882 crore (up ~30% YoY)
- Q2 EBITDA margin (ex-Sahyadri): 27%
- Network EBITDA margin (including Sahyadri): 26%
- H1 FY27 Revenue: INR6,490 crore (up ~38% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Network EBITDA: INR1,631 crore (up ~28% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR639 crore (10% margin)
- Inpatient volumes: up 35% YoY
- Outpatient volumes: up 27% YoY
- Average occupancy: 70.7% in Q2 vs 65% in Q1
- ALOS: 2.8 days
- ARPOB (ex-Sahyadri): ~INR77,000 per day (up >8% YoY)
- ARPOB (network level): ~INR70,600 per day
- Digital revenue contribution (H1): ~23% of overall revenue
- Sahyadri Q2 Revenue: INR375 crore (up 13% QoQ)
- Sahyadri Q2 EBITDA: INR73 crore (up 26% QoQ, 19.5% margin)
- Sahyadri H1 Revenue: INR707 crore (up 18% YoY)
- Sahyadri H1 EBITDA: INR131 crore (up 24% YoY)
- Sahyadri digital revenue contribution: 11% of Sahyadri revenue
- Kanakapura Q2 EBITDA margin: 25%
- Yelahanka Q2 EBITDA margin: 24%
- Electronic City Q2 EBITDA loss: INR13 crore
- Planned bed additions: ~440-500 beds in H2, ~100 beds in FY28 (excluding Kinder)

### Highlights
- Best-ever quarter in company history; growth driven by volumes.
- Centers of Excellence (CONGO-R) revenue contribution at 64.2% in H1, led by oncology, neurology, and orthopaedics (growing ~43%).
- Installed world's latest Da Vinci 5 robotic surgical system at Dwarka, Delhi; introduced advanced robotic-assisted orthopedic surgery in Patiala.
- Completed registration of long-term lease for 3.5 acres of land to develop a 500-bed multi-specialty tertiary care hospital in Gurugram (Phase 1 completion in 3-4 years).
- Completed acquisition of Kinder Hospital in Bangalore, to be repositioned as a neighborhood multi-specialty hospital in Whitefield.
- Sahyadri Q2 EBITDA margin improved 200 bps QoQ to 19.5%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic about H2 FY27 based on robust October performance and continuing demand trends.
- Raipur greenfield commissioning expected in Q4 FY27 (350 beds in Phase 1).
- Gurugram Phase 1 construction expected to complete in 3-4 years for 500 beds.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Inpatient volumes increased 35% and outpatient volumes 27% YoY.
- Government scheme volumes in East India impacted temporarily by transition from Swasthya Sathi to Ayushman Bharat equivalent; offset by growth in cash and TPA segments.
- Onco drug price capping expected to have no significant impact due to small share of chemotherapy drugs in total oncology revenue (~12%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Ex-Sahyadri EBITDA margin at 27%; network margin at 26%.
- Sahyadri EBITDA margin expanded to 19.5% in Q2 from 17.5% in Q1 and 15.5% in FY26, aided by digital revenue ramp-up (11%), procurement synergies, and clinical mix.
- Electronic City greenfield recorded an EBITDA loss of INR13 crore in Q2 while ramping up.
- Finance costs remained flat sequentially because debt was repaid late in the quarter (September 15), with interest cost benefits expected from Q3.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned ~440-500 bed additions in H2 FY27 (greenfield and brownfield) and ~100 beds in FY28, plus ~100 beds from Kinder.
- Kanakapura and Yelahanka greenfields added 150 beds each (with ~100 occupancy each) and achieved 25% and 24% EBITDA margins respectively.
- Sahyadri to add ~50 beds in Hadapsar in Q3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt repayment completed on September 15, 2026, with interest expense reduction expected to flow through from Q3.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary volume disruption in Eastern region due to state government health scheme transitions (Swasthya Sathi).
- Greenfield ramp-up drag in initial phases (e.g., Electronic City EBITDA loss of INR13 crore in Q2).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC) on East India volume/payer mix:** Management explained that Swasthya Sathi scheme transition caused government volume de-growth (-17% in H1), but revenue remained robust driven by cash and TPA segments, alongside a new oncology team joining Dhakuria.
2. **Aman Goyal (Aman Goyal) on Sahyadri integration and Electronic City drag:** Management stated integration is on track with digital adoption up to 11% and new clinical teams added; Electronic City posted a INR13 crore EBITDA loss in Q2 but is ramping up rapidly.
3. **Karan Vora (Goldman Sachs) on bed additions and insurance pricing:** Management outlined 440-500 bed additions in H2 and ~100 beds in FY28 (plus Kinder); Sahyadri insurance rate revisions are proceeding on contractual inflation terms.
4. **Neha Manpuria (BofA) on Sahyadri margin trajectory:** Management reiterated their 4-5 year guidance of reaching 23%-24% margins from acquisition date, noting they remain comfortable with it despite a faster-than-expected initial ramp-up to 19.5%.
5. **Alankar Garude (Equites) on greenfield strategies (Mumbai/Raipur vs Bangalore):** Management stated Mumbai and Raipur are larger box formats (Juhu 602 beds, Raipur 350 beds in Phase 1) without densified micro-market networks, and will be commissioned in phases (starting with ~1/3 capacity) with longer breakeven timelines than Bangalore.

### One-line takeaway
Manipal Health Enterprises delivered its best-ever quarter in Q2 FY27 with revenue of ~INR3,400 crore (up 37% YoY) and EBITDA of INR882 crore, underpinned by 35% inpatient volume growth and strong margin expansion across greenfields and the Sahyadri network.
