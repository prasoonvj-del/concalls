# Manipal Health Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Best ever quarter with 37% revenue growth, 30% EBITDA growth, volume-led expansion, and accelerated Sahyadri turnaround.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (Sahyadri margin target 23-24% over 4-5 years unchanged; no new financial guidance issued)
- Tone and demand view: more confident; occupancy jumped to 70.7% from 65%, volume growth sustained (IP +35%, OP +27%), East India government scheme transition ongoing but cash/TPA focus offsetting
- New topics: Gurugram land lease (3.5 acres, 500 beds Phase 1), Kinder acquisition completed, Electronic City launched (EBITDA loss INR13 cr but revenue doubling monthly), Mumbai/Raipur greenfield phasing details, Sahyadri digital revenue 11%, Hadapsar 50 beds Q3, insurance renegotiation status, oncology drug capping minimal impact, finance cost benefit from Q3
- Old topics disappeared: medical college question, international patient growth, government collection slowness, one-off material cost benefit
- Last quarter's promises: Raipur commissioning Q4 FY27 on track; Kinder acquisition completed (was announced); Kinder remodelling to start soon (was 8-9 months); Ahilya Nagar FY28 not mentioned; capex FY27 ~INR2,000 cr no update; net debt/EBITDA to 0.9x achieved via 15 Sept repayment; Kanakapura/Yelahanka margins now 24-25% (exceeded Q1 13%); Electronic City launched as planned

### Key numbers
- Q2 revenue: INR3,400 crore (+37% YoY)
- Q2 EBITDA: INR882 crore (+30% YoY)
- Q2 EBITDA margin (ex-Sahyadri): 27%; network (inc. Sahyadri): 26%
- H1 revenue: INR6,490 crore (+38% YoY)
- H1 EBITDA: INR1,631 crore (+28% YoY)
- H1 PAT: INR639 crore (~10% margin)
- Inpatient volumes: +35% YoY; Outpatient volumes: +27% YoY
- Average occupancy: 70.7% (vs 65% in Q1 FY27)
- ALOS: 2.8 days
- ARPOB/day (ex-Sahyadri): INR77,000 (+8% YoY); network: INR70,600
- Digital revenue: ~23% of H1 revenue
- Sahyadri Q2 revenue: INR375 crore (+13% QoQ); EBITDA: INR73 crore (+26% QoQ); margin: 19.5% (+200 bps QoQ)
- Sahyadri H1 revenue: INR707 crore (+18% YoY); EBITDA: INR131 crore (+24% YoY)
- Kanakapura EBITDA margin: 25% (1+ year old); Yelahanka: 24% (<1 year)
- Electronic City Q2 EBITDA loss: INR13 crore
- Gurugram: 3.5 acres leased, Phase 1 500 beds, construction 3-4 years
- Kinder acquisition completed, to be repositioned as neighborhood multi-specialty hospital

### Highlights
- Best ever quarter historically; growth volume-led across IP and OP
- CONGO-R centers contributed 64.2% of H1 revenue; oncology, neurology, orthopedics grew ~43% YoY
- Clinical milestones: Da Vinci 5 robotic system at Dwarka; two-wheeler first responder fleet in Bangalore; robotic-assisted orthopedic surgery at Patiala; first laser-guided renal artery angioplasty in Eastern India (Kolkata); 5-ALA fluorescence-guided brain tumor surgery in South India (Electronic City); first covered self-expandable metallic stent for airway blockage in Goa
- Sahyadri integration progressing: margins improved from 15.5% (FY26) to 17.5% (Q1) to 19.5% (Q2); digital revenue now 11%; liver transplant team (42 clinicians) joined November 1
- Greenfield ramp-up: Kanakapura and Yelahanka at 24-25% EBITDA margin; Electronic City revenue more than doubled July to September, onco team joining later
- Bed additions: H2 FY27 440-500 beds (greenfield + brownfield); FY28 ~100 beds brownfield; Kinder 100 beds end Q2 FY28; total ~550 beds H2+FY28 plus Kinder
- Mumbai (Juhu) 602 beds, Raipur 350 beds Phase 1; large box model commissioning ~1/3 beds initially
- Sahyadri Hadapsar 50 beds in Q3 FY27
- Payer mix: cash/TPA ~80%, government ~13-14%; East India government volumes down 17% H1 due to Swasthya Sathi to Ayushman Bharat transition, but cash/TPA growth offset
- Oncology drug price capping impact negligible (onco revenue ~12% of network, chemotherapy fraction, controlled drugs even smaller)
- No plans for diagnostics, pharmacy retail, or medical colleges; focus on tertiary/quaternary care and out-of-hospital digital/home care
- Occupancy headroom to ~80% aggregate; length of stay 2.8 days supports turnover

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains Sahyadri margin target of 23-24% over 4-5 years from acquisition; could be pleasantly surprised but not committing
- No formal financial guidance for FY27/FY28; confident on H2, October started well
- Raipur greenfield commissioning Q4 FY27
- Gurugram Phase 1 (500 beds) construction 3-4 years
- Mumbai and Raipur large box model: phased commissioning over 3-4 phases
- Kinder remodelling to start soon, operational end Q2 FY28
- Insurance renegotiations at Sahyadri ongoing per contractual inflation; rebranding to Manipal Hospitals and legal entity change will take time, better pricing expected in next negotiation cycle post-rebranding

### Demand & industry commentary
- Volume-led growth sustained: IP +35%, OP +27% YoY
- Occupancy improved to 70.7% from 65% in Q1 without adding capex
- East India: government scheme transition (Swasthya Sathi to Ayushman Bharat equivalent) caused 17% YoY decline in government volumes in H1, but cash/TPA grew strongly; transition expected to smoothen
- Tier 2/3 expansion positive: Raipur greenfield Q4 FY27, affordability and aspiration for high-end healthcare exist; doctor/manager availability not a constraint
- International patient growth not discussed this quarter
- Government collections slowness not mentioned

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Ex-Sahyadri EBITDA margin 27% (Q2), 26% (H1)
- Network EBITDA margin 26% (Q2), 25% (H1)
- Sahyadri margin trajectory: 15.5% (FY26) → 17.5% (Q1) → 19.5% (Q2), driven by procurement synergies, digital revenue (11%), clinician hiring, high-end programs, operating efficiencies
- Sahyadri ARPOB declined 2% QoQ (INR45,000 to INR44,100) due to higher medical cases (febrile season) increasing ALOS slightly
- Oncology drug price capping: minimal impact (controlled drugs small fraction of onco revenue)
- Finance costs: debt repaid 15 September, benefit from Q3 onward

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H2 FY27: 440-500 beds (one greenfield + brownfield)
- FY28: ~100 beds brownfield
- Kinder: 100 beds end Q2 FY28
- Total H2+FY28 ~550 beds plus Kinder
- Mumbai (Juhu) 602 beds, Raipur 350 beds Phase 1: large box model, commission ~1/3 initially
- Sahyadri Hadapsar: 50 beds Q3 FY27
- Nashik Lenad block added March 2026
- Gurugram: 3.5 acres leased, Phase 1 500 beds, construction 3-4 years
- Electronic City launched late July/early August, ramping well
- Kanakapura and Yelahanka: ~150 beds each added, occupancy ~100 each, EBITDA margin 24-25%
- FY27 capex guidance ~INR2,000 crore (from previous call, not reiterated); Q1 spend ~INR900 crore

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt repaid on 15 September 2026 using IPO proceeds; net debt/EBITDA reduced to 0.9x (from 2.8x in Q1)
- Finance cost benefit to reflect from Q3
- No other balance sheet details discussed

### Risks & concerns flagged
- East India government scheme transition (Swasthya Sathi to Ayushman Bharat) causing volume volatility; transition not yet concluded
- Sahyadri rebranding and legal entity change will take time, delaying insurance pricing improvements
- Mumbai and Raipur greenfields in new markets will take longer to breakeven than Bangalore greenfields
- No specific new risks flagged beyond above

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC) on East India cluster volume impact from payer mix change:** Management explained reduction due to Swasthya Sathi scheme transition to Ayushman Bharat equivalent; expects transition to conclude, revenue robust; added leading onco team and commissioned annex building at Dhakuria to drive volumes; cash/TPA focus offsetting government volume decline.
2. **Aman Goyal on oncology drug exposure and Electronic City drag:** Management stated onco revenue ~12% of network, chemotherapy fraction, controlled drugs even smaller; no adverse impact expected. Electronic City Q2 EBITDA loss INR13 crore but revenue more than doubled July to September; onco team joining later to build traction.
3. **Karan Vora (Goldman Sachs) on bed addition plans:** H2 FY27 440-500 beds (greenfield + brownfield); FY28 ~100 beds brownfield; Kinder 100 beds end Q2 FY28; Mumbai and Raipur large box model phased commissioning; Sahyadri Hadapsar 50 beds Q3.
4. **Karan Vora on Sahyadri insurance renegotiations:** Management said renegotiations aligning with contractual inflation, no headwinds; rebranding to Manipal Hospitals and legal entity change needed for better pricing, expected in next negotiation cycle post-rebranding.
5. **Neha Manpuria (Bank of America) on Sahyadri margin inflection:** Management maintained 23-24% margin target over 4-5 years from acquisition; could be pleasantly surprised but not over-projecting; ex-Sahyadri margin 27% shows potential.
6. **Alankar Garude (Equirus) on greenfield approach in new markets vs Bangalore:** Mumbai and Raipur different from densified Bangalore; large box model, phased commissioning (~1/3 initially), marketing and clinical recruitment strategy different; breakeven slower than Bangalore.

### One-line takeaway
Manipal Health delivered its best-ever quarter with 37% revenue growth, 30% EBITDA growth, and accelerating Sahyadri turnaround (19.5% margin), while advancing greenfield ramp-ups and new expansion projects (Gurugram, Mumbai, Raipur).
