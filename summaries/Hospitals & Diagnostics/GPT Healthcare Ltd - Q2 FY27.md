# GPT Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-14 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong YoY revenue and PAT growth, expanding margins, and improving utilization across the mature network.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 revenue growth expectation of 15% to 16% over last year and EBITDA margin around 21%. Raipur breakeven delayed from Q3 FY27 to Q1 FY28 due to clinical talent attrition.
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, citing strong structural growth drivers and improving mature hospital occupancy, though noting regional competition challenges in Raipur.
- New topics or risks: Clinical talent attrition in Raipur due to new competition (e.g., Artemis); Supreme Court trade margin caps on essential medicines discussed. Old risks: Agartala tribal election disruptions from last quarter disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises: Jamshedpur commissioning remains on track for March/April 2027. Raipur breakeven timeline was pushed out from Q3 FY27 to Q1 FY28.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 135.6 crore (up 14.1% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 29.8 crore (up 23.9% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 21.5% (expansion of 150 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 15.4 crore (up 45.2% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 11.1% (expansion of 230 bps YoY)
- **Network Occupancy:** 50.3% (vs 47.4% YoY); mature hospitals occupancy excluding Raipur at 59.8%
- **ARPOB:** INR 40,721 (up 7.1% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 261.8 crore (up 15.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 56.0 crore (up 30.3% YoY; margin 21.0%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 28.1 crore (up 53.9% YoY; margin 10.5%)
- **Raipur Q2 Loss:** INR ~4.5 crore (H1 losses at INR 6.5 crore)
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** INR ~45 crore in mutual funds
- **Total Borrowings:** INR ~35 crore

### Highlights
- Introduced robotic-assisted surgery at ILS Salt Lake and ILS Howrah; Salt Lake crossed 850 robotic surgeries.
- ILS Dum Dum commenced a cardiothoracic and vascular surgery unit; nephrology contribution reduced to under 15% from ~30%.
- ILS Agartala completed over 3,200 radiation therapy procedures via LINAC and over 1,000 PET scans.
- ILS Howrah revenue grew 25% YoY in H1; introduced MAKO robotic knee surgery, nearing 100 robotic procedures.
- ILS Raipur achieved NABH accreditation, performed its first TAVI, first liver transplant, and 11 renal transplants.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth of 15% to 16% over last year.
- Raipur expected to achieve monthly operational breakeven by Q1 FY28; projected FY27 loss of around INR 10 crore and FY28 net positive by a minor amount.
- Jamshedpur 155-bed tertiary care hospital on track to commence in March/April 2027; expected EBITDA loss of INR 10 crore in FY28.
- Long-term ROE and ROCE targeted at approximately 25%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural growth supported by rising health awareness, lifestyle diseases, insurance penetration, and an aging population.
- International patient flows from Bangladesh showing slow recovery with increasing visa requests.
- Cash and insurance patients generate over 90% of revenues.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raipur lease expense is INR ~4 crore a year; Jamshedpur lease expense expected to be similar.
- Supreme Court judgment on trade margins of essential medicines: management noted service pricing will be adjusted to maintain margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Jamshedpur 155-bed project progressing as planned for March/April 2027 commissioning.
- Agartala and Raipur each have brownfield expansion capacity of another 100 beds when needed.
- Continuously scouting for greenfield and acquisition opportunities in Eastern India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with INR ~45 crore in mutual funds and INR ~35 crore in borrowings. Plan to use a healthy mix of debt and equity for future expansions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Clinical talent attrition in Raipur due to new market entrants (like Artemis), causing a slight dip in productivity and delaying breakeven to Q1 FY28.
- Seasonality in Q2 (high fever season) caused a slight shift in specialty mix and minor sequential ARPOB dip.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Richa Chowdhary (Electrum PMS) on ARPOB drop and Raipur losses:** Management explained ARPOB dip was due to seasonal fever variations and stated H1 Raipur losses were INR 6.5 crore, expected to break even by Q1 FY28. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Aditya Chheda (InCred Asset Management) on Dum Dum nephrology and lease expenses:** Management stated nephrology contribution is now under 15% (down from 30%) and quantified Raipur/Jamshedpur lease expenses at INR ~4 crore/year each. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Naysar Shah (Independent Investor) on Raipur breakeven delay and bed additions:** Management confirmed breakeven pushed to Q1 FY28 due to clinical attrition and noted each of Agartala and Raipur can add 100 brownfield beds. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Anubhav Mukherjee (Prescient Capital) on hospital size model and Ranchi project:** Management explained midsized 200-bed hospitals ensure faster breakeven and cost control. Confirmed Ranchi MOU dropped due to owner's legal troubles. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Darsh Mahida (Institutional Investor) on international patients and oncology:** Management stated Bangladesh patient inflow is showing slow recovery; oncology contributes ~4% to revenue and expected to reach 10%-15% in 4 quarters. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
GPT Healthcare delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with revenue up 14.1% YoY and PAT up 45.2% YoY, driven by improved mature hospital utilization and advanced clinical capabilities, despite a slight breakeven delay at its new Raipur facility.
