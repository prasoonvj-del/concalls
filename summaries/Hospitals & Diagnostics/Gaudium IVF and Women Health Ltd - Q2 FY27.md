# Gaudium IVF and Women Health Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted steady revenue growth, successful launch of new hubs, and strong core business performance despite front-loaded expansion costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 revenue guidance of INR130 crores.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from a seasonally slower Q1 due to heat to expectations of stronger H2 demand, festival tailwinds, and peak medical tourism in December.
- New topics or risks: Introduction of a private insurance partnership for IVF cycle packages; named specific upcoming expansion locations (Lucknow, Calcutta, Guwahati, Surat, Hyderabad); appointed a Head of National Expansion.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on launching South Extension on July 16, 2026, and Gurgaon on September 24, 2026; Nagpur center completed and awaiting regulatory approval.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: INR37.4 crores (up 17.9% YoY from INR31.7 crores)
- H1 Consolidated Revenue from Operations: INR56.8 crores (up 14.7% YoY from INR49.5 crores)
- Standalone IVF Revenue (Core): INR28.1 crores in Q2 (up 22.3% YoY); INR41.7 crores in H1 (up 18.5% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA: INR13.5 crores (margin 36%, down 737 bps YoY from 43.4%)
- H1 Consolidated EBITDA: INR15.9 crores (margin 28% vs 38.3% YoY)
- Consolidated PAT: INR9.4 crores (margin 25.2% vs 29.6% YoY)
- H1 Consolidated PAT: INR11.2 crores (margin 19.7% vs 25.3% YoY)
- First-attempt clinical pregnancy success rate: 62% (post-AI introduction)
- Average revenue per patient: INR3.07 lakhs in Q2; INR3.19 lakhs in H1 (vs INR2.58 lakhs in H1 FY26)
- Network size: 9 hubs and 38 spokes as of September 30, 2026
- Cash and cash equivalents: INR20.5 crores; unutilized IPO proceeds in fixed deposits: INR39.5 crores (total unutilized with interest: INR39.9 crores)
- Borrowings: INR4.8 crores; Consolidated debt-to-equity ratio: 0.03x
- Standalone Receivables: INR32.83 crores as of September 30, 2026 (vs INR42.6 crores on March 31, 2026)
- Consolidated Receivables: INR45.30 crores as of September 30, 2026 (vs INR53.37 crores on March 31, 2026)
- Total pick-up cycles in Q2: 889 cycles

### Highlights
- Commenced operations at the South Extension hub (New Delhi) on July 16, 2026, and the Gurgaon hub on September 24, 2026.
- Nagpur hub completed and awaiting regulatory approval.
- Added 7 new spokes during the quarter (4 around South Extension, 3 around Gurgaon).
- Integrated AI-led embryology tools (SiD and ERICA) via "Gaudium Signature Labs" at new hubs, boosting first-attempt success rates to 62%.
- Partnered with a private insurance player for a 3-cycle IVF package model backed by clinical pregnancy success rates.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reaffirmed its FY27 revenue guidance of INR130 crores.
- Management expects H2 to be stronger driven by mature hub growth, new hub scaling, and peak medical tourism in December.
- Management projects long-term revenue growth aspirations of ~25% for FY28 and FY29.
- Management stated mature hubs can reach peak utilization of 100 to 120 cycles without additional capex.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Core existing hubs grew ~28% YoY in H1.
- Q2 is seasonally strong, and December is anticipated to see high medical tourism inflows from over 30 countries.
- Deferred payment plans and NBFC aggregators are being utilized to facilitate patient affordability.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated EBITDA margin contracted by 737 bps YoY to 36% due to front-loaded rollout/pre-operational expenses for new hubs and increased marketing spend.
- Margins in the existing IVF business remained stable.
- New hubs carry high initial fixed costs (clinical teams, lab, premises) prior to volume maturity.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Average capex per hub is approximately INR2.5 crores (INR1.5 crores for lab/OT, remainder for center development) for an average 3,000 sq ft facility.
- New hubs historically break even within 3 to 6 months, with a payback period of 1.5 to 2 years.
- 5 new hub locations identified under National Expansion Head Ravish: Lucknow, Calcutta, Guwahati, Surat, and Hyderabad.
- Gaudium Women Hospital in Lucknow estimated at up to INR15 crores project cost on a 30-year land lease, with commercialization expected in FY28-29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains debt-light with a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.03x.
- Out of INR90 crores fresh issue proceeds, INR50.7 crores utilized as of September 30, 2026 (INR13.5 crores for new centers, INR18.1 crores for debt repayment, INR11.4 crores for general corporate purposes, INR7.7 crores for issue expenses).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Front-loaded expansion costs and early-stage fixed costs of new hubs weigh temporarily on consolidated margins.
- Temporary operational halts/shifting in locations like Patna created short-term drags.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pratiti Khara (Param Capital)** asked about Q3 demand and medical tourism. Management stated demand is rising and December holidays will drive medical tourism. *(Not dodged)*
- **Amit Mehendale** asked about FY28/FY29 revenue growth aspirations. Management guided for ~25% growth. *(Not dodged)*
- **Swechha Jain (Param Capital)** asked about high receivable days (165 days). Management explained it is driven by clinical delays (e.g., treating underlying conditions before transfer) and financial support/deferred payment plans, secured by frozen embryos in the lab. *(Not dodged)*
- **Ramesh Kataria** asked about direct credit vs NBFC aggregators. CFO stated Gaudium extended INR60–80 lakhs directly while aggregators handled INR4 crores this quarter. *(Not dodged)*
- **Amit Lohiya** asked about positioning in Kolkata against competition. Management cited clinical expertise, AI signature labs, and non-star doctor SOPs. *(Not dodged)*

### One-line takeaway
Gaudium IVF delivered 17.9% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 alongside front-loaded expansion and marketing costs, while maintaining stable core margins and reaffirming its full-year guidance.
