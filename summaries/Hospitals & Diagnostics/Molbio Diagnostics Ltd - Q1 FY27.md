# Molbio Diagnostics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-12 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong business growth, robust recurring revenue model, and key strategic approvals (US FDA for OptraScan, EU IVDR for CT/NG).

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: Rs 408 crore (Q1 FY27)
- Devices sold: 164 devices (Q1 FY27)
- Test kits sold: 62.4 lakh kits (Q1 FY27)
- Export revenue: Rs 408 crore total, out of which export revenue was Rs 67 crore (16% of total revenue from operations)
- EBITDA: Rs 104 crore (25% margin)
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 52.7 crore (13% margin)
- Trailing 12-month receivable days: 87 days (June) vs 86 days (March)
- Trailing 12-month inventory days: 230 days (June) vs 280 days (March)
- Total installation base: 12,500+ machines across 90+ countries
- R&D spend: 5% to 6% of revenue (annual R&D investment increased from Rs 598 million in FY24 to over Rs 2.16 billion over the last 3 years)
- Capacity utilization: Test kits at ~58%, devices at ~42%
- Prognosys FY26 revenue: Rs 154 crore with ~Rs 12 crore PAT (19% EBITDA)
- OptraScan FY27 Q1 loss: ~Rs 9 crore loss

### Highlights
- Management highlighted overwhelming response to the company's IPO.
- Raised Rs 200 crore in the IPO, with Rs 72 crore allocated for automation and ~Rs 105 crore to build an integrated R&D center in Bangalore. Minimal capex required to grow top line 2x from FY26 base.
- Truenat platform has ~43 different assays for 30 different diseases. Working on 22 additional diseases with 34 assays.
- Prognosys received US FDA approval for digital imaging solutions and EU MDR certification for X-ray products (PRORAD handheld X-ray).
- OptraScan (60% subsidiary) received US FDA clearance in July for end-to-end digital pathology (hardware, software, cloud storage, and AI). Fully funded by USD 30 million fresh equity infusion.
- Secured a two-year rate contract for TB testing with the Government of India last month.
- First Indian company to receive EU IVDR certification for the CT/NG (Chlamydia and Gonorrhoeae) test kit.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for top-line growth of roughly ~25% for FY27.
- Management guided for an EBITDA margin of ~24% to 25% for FY27.
- OptraScan is expected to remain unprofitable in FY27, with meaningful financial contributions expected from FY28 and FY29 onwards.
- Annual device sales historically range between 2,200 to 2,600 levels, expected to remain in line for the current year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Point-of-care diagnostics market is a USD 30 billion opportunity growing at a 17.9% CAGR; infectious diseases represent about a third of this market.
- Over 56 crore tests are conducted worldwide in infectious diseases; Truenat has scaled to ~1.5 crore tests annually.
- Transition from conventional testing methods to molecular diagnostics is a strong global and domestic trend.
- TB remains the highest priority; management stated there is a long runway for growth as penetration in India has reached ~8,000-9,000 out of 25,000-30,000 TB diagnostic centers, with remaining centers still using traditional microscopy.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standard gross margin across devices and test kits is maintained at 60% to 63%.
- Recurring business from test kits contributes ~75% of total revenues.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Test kit capacity utilization is at ~58%; device capacity utilization is at ~42%.
- IPO proceeds of Rs 200 crore to be utilized for automation (Rs 72 crore) and an integrated R&D center in Bangalore (~Rs 105 crore). No major capacity capex required.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Collections and working capital remain efficient with trailing 12-month receivable days at 87 days in June vs 86 days in March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted that the public health business is non-linear, and no quarter should be tracked sequentially or linearly.
- OptraScan will not turn profitable in FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tushar Manudhane (Motilal Oswal) asked about HPV test progress and volumes.** Management (Shiva Sriram) answered that AIIMS and international agencies validated Truenat HPV as meeting all international criteria, with recommendations made to the Government of India; volume guidance will be provided next quarter.
- **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC) asked about device life cycle and replacement rates.** Management answered that early installations from 2017-2018 are still running without replacements, showing rugged design and no replacement cycle kicked in yet.
- **Nikhil Mathur (HDFC Mutual Fund) asked about the revenue split and drag from subsidiaries.** Management (Manan Khokhani) clarified that Q1 standalone Molbio revenue was Rs 399 crore, Prognosys was ~Rs 11 crore, and OptraScan was minuscule, while adding that OptraScan incurred a ~Rs 9 crore loss in Q1.
- **Anandha Padmanabhan (PGIM India) asked about long-term visibility of government TB funding.** Management answered that WHO guidelines mandate moving from microscopy to molecular, and Truenat is the only point-of-care battery-operated test, leaving significant room for further penetration.
- **Aniket Singh (Kotak Institutional Equities) asked about non-communicable disease platforms and timelines.** Management stated that R&D is working on multiple platforms for non-communicable diseases (TAM ~10x of molecular tests), deferring exact timelines to future calls.

### One-line takeaway
Molbio Diagnostics delivered a solid Q1 FY27 with Rs 408 crore revenue and 25% EBITDA margin, underpinned by its recurring test kit model, strong public health positioning in TB, and strategic US FDA/EU regulatory approvals in digital pathology and STI testing.
