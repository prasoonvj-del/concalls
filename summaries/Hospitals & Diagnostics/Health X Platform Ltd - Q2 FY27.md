# Health X Platform Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue growth (+59%), gross margin expansion (+80 bps YoY), and successful rollout of the RetailAir SaaS platform.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Cut long-term FY30 total revenue target from Rs 6,000 crore to Rs 4,900 crore (SastaSundar target lowered from Rs 2,000 crore to Rs 900 crore) due to product mix shift toward lower-priced JITO trade generics; however, FY30 EBITDA margin target was raised to 5–6% (up from 5%). Near-term gross margin path specified at 8–9% for FY27, 9–10% for FY28, and 10–11% for FY29.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained positive, highlighting strong tailwinds in Tier 2/3 markets driven by government direct cash transfers into women's bank accounts, with ~10% flowing into healthcare.
- **New topics or risks:** Commercial launch of RetailAir SaaS on Google Play; scheduled 10,000-pharmacy retailer conference in Jan 2027; update on SEBI observation letter expected by end-Dec 2026 for the group merger/demerger.
- **Promises delivered:** Delivered the planned launch of RetailAir SaaS platform in Q2 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 489 crore, up 59% YoY and 11% QoQ (H1 FY27: Rs 895 crore).
- **RetailerShakti (B2B):** Rs 429 crore (+60% YoY, +18% QoQ).
- **SastaSundar (B2C):** Rs 55 crore (+39% YoY; 6 consecutive quarters of growth).
- **Diagnostics:** Rs 0.8 crore.
- **Gross Profit & Margin:** Rs 40.7 crore; margin at 8.3% (+80 bps YoY, +40 bps QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** Loss of Rs 17.2 crore (-3.5% margin vs -5.2% YoY).
- **PAT:** Loss of Rs 12.7 crore (-2.6% margin vs -5.0% YoY).
- **Working Capital Cycle:** 23 days (down from 28 days in June); Inventory 35 days, Receivables 2 days, Payables 13 days.
- **JITO Private Label Revenue:** Rs 2.2 crore (+170% QoQ) with ~60% gross margin.

### Highlights
- Operating expenditure declined to 10.8% of revenue from 12.4% a year ago (160 bps absorption YoY).
- SastaSundar generates 76% of business and RetailerShakti generates 66% of business from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities; 90% of orders are delivered within 24 hours.
- Over 90% of purchases are made directly from pharmaceutical companies via authorized channels on cash terms.
- JITO footprint stands at 52 live franchise outlets with 81 in the pipeline and over 1,000 SKUs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Target compounded top-line growth of 30%+ over the next 5 years.
- Targets 13% revenue contribution from JITO branded medicines by FY30.
- RetailerShakti FY30 top-line target maintained at Rs 4,000 crore.
- Target quarterly JITO brand sales of Rs 6–10 crore in Q3 FY27.
- Expects 50 to 100 bps annual gross margin expansion driven by sourcing scale, private label mix, higher-margin OTC/devices/supplements, and data monetization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management sees India's medicine distribution consolidating from thousands of local intermediaries into fewer than 100 national distributors.
- Trade generic demand is accelerating as lower-income population and gig workers seek accessible, affordable home-delivered chronic medications to save wage hours.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sequential employee costs increased from Rs 27.7 crore to Rs 31.0 crore due to upfront hiring for geographical expansion in new state markets (Odisha, Jharkhand, Bihar, UP).
- Depreciation and finance costs combined represent 0.8% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Upgraded Noida warehouse to double its capacity (servicing 3–4 years of growth) and expanded Guwahati warehouse (3 years growth capacity).
- Pipeline of 5,95,000 sq. ft. additional fulfillment capacity planned: West Bengal (1,20,000 sq. ft.), Guwahati (75,000 sq. ft.), Lucknow (80,000 sq. ft.), Udaipur (80,000 sq. ft.), Patna (1,40,000 sq. ft. across 2 phases), and Bhubaneswar (1,40,000 sq. ft. across 2 phases).
- Owned warehouse under construction at Udaipur scheduled to come online in 18 months. Intermediate 20,000 sq. ft. leased facilities starting up in Ranchi, Patna, and Bhubaneswar in ~6 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Zero meaningful debt; net working capital reduced to 6% of revenue (23 days).
- Company spent Rs 100 crore in the past year to buy back 10% equity from Mitsubishi Corporation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued consolidated EBITDA losses (-3.5% of revenue) due to upfront operating cost absorption for new regional penetrations.
- Pending corporate merger/demerger structure limits treasury cash deployment options until completed.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** *Why take investment losses in P&L instead of buying growth via M&A?*  
  **A:** Management stated acquiring an asset with ~50% ROE costs 1x–5x revenue (capitalized on balance sheet), while organic market investments cost ~30% of incremental revenue generated (expensed in P&L with a 3–4 year payback via contribution margin).
- **Q:** *What is the progress and timeline on the AI consultation app?*  
  **A:** Advisory/counselling features will take 6 months to complete development and another 6 months for rollout, targeting ready launch around October–November 2027 to ensure customer adoption readiness.
- **Q:** *Why was the long-term SastaSundar revenue guidance reduced?*  
  **A:** Unit volume targets are unchanged, but JITO private label products are priced at ~one-third of conventional branded drugs. Higher JITO share reduces gross top-line numbers while expanding gross margins and overall EBITDA.
- **Q:** *What is the status of the legal merger/demerger process?*  
  **A:** Application is currently pending for SEBI observation letter (expected by end of December 2026). NCLT filing will occur within 15 days of SEBI approval and typically takes 4–6 months.

### One-line takeaway
Health X Platform delivered robust 59% revenue growth and improving margin leverage, choosing organic P&L reinvestment into fulfillment, private label JITO expansion, and SaaS tools over short-term consolidated EBITDA breakeven.
