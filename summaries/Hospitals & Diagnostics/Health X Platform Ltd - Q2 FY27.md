# Health X Platform Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong top-line growth (59% YoY), expanding gross margins, and successful launch of the RetailAir platform.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management revised FY29/30 revenue estimates from Rs 6,000 crore to Rs 4,900 crore due to higher JITO private label mix (lower unit pricing), but guided that EBITDA percentage will improve from 5% to above 6%.
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains confident, highlighting the successful commercial launch of RetailAir and acceleration in Tier 2/3 and trade generic demand.
- New topics or risks: Formal launch and live status of RetailAir discussed in detail; data monetization and pharmacy convention plans introduced. No old topics disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises: RetailAir launched as promised in Q2 FY27; warehouse scaling and fulfillment plans progressing as outlined.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 489 crore, up 59% YoY and 11% QoQ.
- **H1 Healthcare Network Revenue:** Rs 895 crore.
- **RetailerShakti (B2B) Revenue:** Rs 429 crore, up 60% YoY and 18% QoQ.
- **SastaSundar (B2C) Revenue:** Rs 55 crore, up 39% YoY (6 consecutive quarters of growth).
- **Diagnostics Revenue:** Rs 0.8 crore.
- **Gross Margin:** 8.3% (Rs 40.7 crore), up 80 bps YoY and 40 bps QoQ.
- **Operating Expenditure:** 10.8% of revenue, down 160 bps YoY and 80 bps QoQ.
- **EBITDA:** Negative Rs 17.2 crore (-3.5% margin), compared to negative Rs 16 crore (-5.2% margin) a year ago.
- **PAT:** Loss of Rs 12.7 crore (-2.6% margin), compared to loss of Rs 15.4 crore (-5.0% margin) YoY.
- **Net Working Capital:** 23 days (6% of revenue); inventory 35 days, receivables 2 days, payables 13 days.
- **JITO Private Label Sales:** Cumulative Rs 2.2 crore in Q2 with ~60% gross margin.

### Highlights
- SastaSundar B2C platform operating with complete ownership and control following the end of the Flipkart partnership.
- RetailAir mobile-first inventory and billing SaaS platform officially live on Google Play and operational.
- Over 76% of SastaSundar and 66% of RetailerShakti business comes from Tier 2 and Tier 3 India.
- JITO private label network reached 52 live franchise outlets with 81 in the pipeline and over 1,000 healthcare SKUs.
- Planning India's first pharmacy retailers' conference in January with around 10,000 participants.
- Merger scheme with subsidiary awaiting SEBI observation letter, expected by end of December, prior to NCLT filing.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets 30% compounded annual growth.
- Guided consolidated gross margin range: 8% to 8.6% for FY27, 9% to 10% for FY27-28, and 10% to 11% for FY28-29.
- For FY29/30, management expects total revenue of Rs 4,900 crore (RetailerShakti at Rs 4,000 crore, SastaSundar at Rs 900 crore) due to JITO mix, with an upgraded EBITDA margin target of over 6%.
- JITO brand monthly revenue targeted between Rs 6 crore to Rs 10 crore next quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's medicine distribution is consolidating from thousands of intermediaries into fewer national distributors.
- Preventive care, wellness, devices, and personal care are growing faster than acute medicine with better economics.
- Trade generic (JITO) demand is surging as consumers value time savings and affordable home delivery.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded by 80 bps YoY to 8.3% due to improved sourcing, private label mix, and channel optimization.
- Employee costs rose to Rs 31 crore due to hiring for new geographical markets and capacity building ahead of demand.
- Depreciation and finance costs totaled only 0.8% of revenue.
- 90% of purchases are made directly from pharmaceutical companies through authorized channels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Noida warehouse upgraded with double capacity; Guwahati upgraded for 3 years of growth; West Bengal fully automated.
- Pipeline additions total 5.95 lakh square feet across new large warehouses (Udaipur at 80,000 sq ft, Lucknow at 80,000 sq ft, Guwahati at 75,000 sq ft, West Bengal at 1.2 lakh sq ft) and smaller initial hubs (Patna, Odisha, Ranchi).
- Udaipur fulfillment center construction is underway with plinth area completed (expected online in 18 months).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Fully capitalized with no meaningful debt; asset-light model.
- Working capital improved to 23 days (down from 28 days in June).
- Receivable cycle remains exceptionally low at 2 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Consolidated EBITDA remains negative at -3.5% due to upfront investments in fulfillment centers, market expansion, and technology.
- Structured credit adoption via partnered banks/NBFCs is slow due to the friction of KYC and paperwork.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Priyam Srivastava (Emkay Capital):** Asked about advantages of large vs small distributors and delivery wait times. *Management stated large distributors benefit from bulk procurement discounts, lower operating costs, and market diversification; customers wait for chronic medicines due to 100% fill rates, traceability/genuineness, and pricing.*
2. **Vaibhav (Helios Asset Management):** Inquired about partnering with NBFCs for retailer credit. *Management stated they have partnered with 2 national banks and NBFCs, but adoption is slow because retailers are unaccustomed to the required structured documentation and KYC.*
3. **Abhishek Singhal (Perpetuity Ventures):** Asked about fulfillment infrastructure capacity and gross margin levers. *Management detailed the 5.95 lakh sq ft warehouse pipeline and cited data monetization, JITO private label expansion, volume scale, and logistics revenue as gross margin levers.*
4. **Praneeth Bommisetti (SJA Investments):** Asked about JITO revenue guidance and the revision of long-term targets. *Management explained that due to lower unit pricing of JITO trade generics, revenue targets for FY30 are adjusted to Rs 4,900 crore while EBITDA margin expectations are raised to over 6%.*
5. **Preet Shah (Individual Investor):** Asked about upcoming warehouse capex models. *Management noted a mixed approach, utilizing state-concession MoUs and owned land (like Udaipur) alongside rented premises for rapid scaling.*

### One-line takeaway
Health X Platform posted 59% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 alongside improving operating leverage and the commercial rollout of its RetailAir SaaS platform, prioritizing long-term ecosystem expansion over near-term profit reporting.
