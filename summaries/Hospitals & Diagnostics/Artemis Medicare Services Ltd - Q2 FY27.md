# Artemis Medicare Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance in Gurugram and successful launch of the Raipur facility, with management expressing confidence in future capacity expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 311 crore (up 13.2% YoY).
*   **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 598.4 crore (up 13% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 62.89 crore (margin 20.2%).
*   **Adjusted Operational EBITDA:** Rs 71.5 crore (margin 23.3%).
*   **H1 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 124.7 crore (margin 20.8%).
*   **H1 Adjusted Operational EBITDA:** Rs 133.32 crore (margin 22.4%).
*   **PAT:** Rs 31 crore (up 3.7% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 62.5 crore (up 22.2% YoY).
*   **Gurugram Hospital EBITDA Margin:** 23.8%.
*   **Raipur Hospital Q2 Loss:** Rs 9 crore (on Rs 5.3 crore revenue).

### Highlights
*   Gurugram hospital achieved its highest-ever EBITDA margin of 23.8% with 68% occupancy.
*   Artemis Shanti Hospital (Raipur) commenced operations in July 2026; reported 10% occupancy and an ARPOB of Rs 30,000–32,000.
*   International patient business contributed ~30% of quarterly revenue.
*   Cardiac Care subsidiary reported positive EBITDA of Rs 70 lakh and PAT of Rs 20 lakh.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management aims to reach ~2,000 operational beds in 3–5 years.
*   Raipur breakeven timeline maintained at 12–15 months.
*   Gurugram occupancy target: 75% (from current 68%).
*   Fundraising planned for Jan/Feb 2027 to support asset acquisitions.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong demand observed across cardiac sciences, oncology, neurosciences, orthopaedics, and transplants.
*   International patient base is diversified across 150+ countries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Raipur startup costs: ~Rs 10 crore (fixed + variable).
*   Oncology drug price capping: Management estimates a potential EBITDA impact of 0.5%–0.8% if implemented.
*   Room rent regulation: No impact expected as current rates are already at 3-star levels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Gurugram Tower 4:** Adding 250 beds (total 700 to 900+), expected H2 FY28.
*   **South Delhi (Lajpat Nagar):** 650+ bed project (VIMHANS Trust O&M contract), expected Q4 FY29–FY30.
*   **Raipur:** 300-bed multi-specialty hospital now operational.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Other income: Expected to remain at Q2 levels for the remainder of the year, then diminish.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Regulatory uncertainty regarding potential oncology drug price capping.
*   Potential impact of Delhi GRAP (pollution norms) on construction timelines for the South Delhi project.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Oncology Impact:** Management clarified that even in a worst-case scenario, the EBITDA impact is limited to 0.5%–0.8% because the company already operates at those margin levels.
*   **Raipur Performance:** Management noted that the 10% occupancy and ARPOB of Rs 30k–32k exceeded initial estimates of Rs 26k–27k.
*   **Fundraising:** Management confirmed they are actively finalizing assets and expect to initiate the raise between late Jan and early Feb 2027.
*   **Cardiac Care:** Management confirmed the unit is profitable and plans to add 4–5 more units post-fundraise.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational performance in Gurugram and the successful launch of the Raipur facility drive growth, with management focused on capacity expansion and disciplined capital allocation.
