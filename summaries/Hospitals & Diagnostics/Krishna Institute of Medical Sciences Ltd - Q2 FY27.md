# Krishna Institute of Medical Sciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Recorded growth under all key parameters, both QoQ and YoY, with significant rise in revenue and successful ramp-up of new units.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall growth trajectory; management guided for INR1,100 crores total capex outflow over the next 18 months on three ongoing projects.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positively regarding the new Kondapur hospital ramp-up, which grew 70% in revenue in just three months.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Trade margin rationalisation on anti-cancer drugs introduced by the government became a new topic (though impact deemed insignificant by management). Provision for Doubtful Debt (INR8 crores) specifically impacted AP cluster margins this quarter.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Kondapur and new oncology programs (Vizag, Anantapur) were successfully launched/scaled as planned.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** INR1,363 crore (+41.3% YoY, +14% QoQ)
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR1,344 crore (+39.9% YoY, +14% QoQ)
- **EBITDA (Consolidated):** INR279 crore (+34% YoY, +16.3% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.5% (vs 20.1% in Q1 FY27, 21.7% in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA (Pre-Ind AS):** INR250 crore (+24.7% YoY, +12.7% QoQ)
- **PAT:** INR76 crore (vs INR37 crore in Q1 FY27, INR72 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **EPS:** INR1.8 (+8.5% YoY)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** INR420 crore as on 30th September 2026
- **Net Debt:** INR2,150 crore
- **IP Volumes:** 81,668 (+27% YoY, +12.7% QoQ)
- **OP Volumes:** 731,010 (+23.3% YoY, +11% QoQ)
- **ARPOB Growth:** +14.9% YoY, +2.3% QoQ

### Highlights
- Acquired 51% equity stake in Sarvottam Health Care Private Limited (KIMS Trust Hospital, Kakinada, 183 beds) for INR80 crore.
- Acquired 6% stake in Ushahkal Abhinav Specialty Hospital, Sangli, Maharashtra (316 beds) for INR15 crore.
- Entered into O&M agreements for Arete Hospitals (Gachibowli, Hyderabad, 300 beds) and Westfort Hospital (Thrissur, Kerala, 300 beds).
- Doubled capacity of KIMS Saveera Hospital, Anantapur, from 250 to 500 beds.
- Advanced an agreement for cooperation with NHS University Hospitals of Liverpool, U.K.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to recover towards previous levels (30% for Kondapur over next 3-4 quarters, and AP cluster normalizing as new oncology units scale).
- Management stated no significant bed additions (around 400-500 beds on the lower side) are planned over the next 24 months after current expansions (Rajahmundry, Ongole) conclude in 4-5 months, focusing instead on stabilization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Organ transplantations have been expanded and are now performed across tier-2 and tier-3 units in Andhra Pradesh.
- Kondapur unit saw a 70% revenue growth in three months post-commissioning, operating at 70% occupancy on 500 beds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- AP cluster margins faced a drag due to upfront costs of two new oncology programs and a one-time Provision for Doubtful Debt (PBDT) of INR8 crores.
- Rental costs increased due to the full-quarter impact of the new Kondapur hospital lease (INR5.5 crore/month including GST).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex stood at INR650 crore, with ~INR300 crore spent on Kondapur.
- Total capex outflow over the next 18 months is estimated at INR1,100 crore for Secunderabad, Rajahmundry, and remaining Kondapur capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at INR2,150 crore as of 30th September 2026, with a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 1.9x.
- QIP and share warrant proceeds parked in mutual funds/bank accounts for general corporate purposes, loss funding, and potential future acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Insurance empanelment negotiations for newer units (Thane, Mahadevapura) are ongoing with 2-3 major companies remaining.
- Fixed cost drag of INR1.5 crore/month continues for the old Kondapur hospital.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** What is the potential impact of trade margin rationalization on anti-cancer drugs? **A:** Management stated final details are pending, but oncology is not a major part of revenue, so impact may not be significant. (Management did not dodge).
- **Q:** Why did AP cluster margins dip despite revenue growth? **A:** Management attributed it to fixed costs from new onco programs in Vizag and Anantapur, and an INR8 crore PBDT provision which will reverse when cash is received. (Management did not dodge).
- **Q:** When will Electronic City (Bangalore) break even at the quarterly level? **A:** Management expects quarterly break-even by Q4 FY27, though September already saw a monthly break-even. (Management did not dodge).
- **Q:** What is the plan for the rental partner at Kondapur? **A:** Management is evaluating a potential merger within 12 months to eliminate INR60-65 crore annual rent and 18% GST, weighed against taking on ~INR400 crore of associated debt. (Management did not dodge).
- **Q:** What is the outlook for bed additions post-current expansion? **A:** Management stated only 400-500 beds will be added over the next 24 months to focus on stabilization before larger expansions resume around FY29/FY30. (Management did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
KIMS delivered strong Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 41.3% YoY, driven by rapid ramp-ups in new units like Kondapur, while managing near-term margin drags from new facility rollouts and provisions.
