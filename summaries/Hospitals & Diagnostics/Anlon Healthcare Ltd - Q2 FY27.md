# Anlon Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q2/H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, substantial sequential margin recovery, and ongoing strategic expansion across subsidiaries.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 105.61 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 52.2 crore (Q2 FY26), up 102.3% YoY; Rs 193.17 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 85.5 crore (H1 FY26), up 125.9% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 25.24 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 13.71 crore (Q2 FY26), up 84.1% YoY; Rs 40.83 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 19.95 crore (H1 FY26), up 104.7% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 23.9% in Q2 FY27 (up over 600 bps QoQ from 17.8% in Q1 FY27); 21.1% in H1 FY27.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 17.18 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 9.32 crore (Q2 FY26), up 84.4% YoY; Rs 25.46 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 12.86 crore (H1 FY26), up 97.9% YoY.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Net cash used in operating activities stood at Rs 7.94 crore in H1 FY27 vs Rs 79.44 crore for full year FY26.
- **Capacity:** Consolidated installed manufacturing capacity stands at approximately 1,900 to 2,000 metric tonnes per annum (MTPA). Standalone capacity utilization is at 85%-86%, while consolidated capacity utilization is at 65%-67%.

### Highlights
- **Subsidiary & Vertical Progress:** 
  - Apiqo Organics opened an industrial and fine chemicals vertical.
  - Anlon Medicos (formerly Remember India) enters finished dosage formulations (tablets, capsules) targeting domestic retail and hospital markets in Q3 FY27.
  - Newly incorporated subsidiaries: Anlon Biologics (peptides, oligonucleotides, biosimilars) and Anlon Medico (surgical implants and medical devices). First revenues expected in Q2 FY28 and Q4 FY28 respectively.
- **Segment Revenue Split (H1 consolidated):** Anlon standalone 44.6%, Apiqo Organics 43.7%, Bizotic Lifescience 11.7%. Within Anlon standalone: APIs 17.1%, intermediates 81.1%, balance from nutraceuticals.
- **Regulatory & Filings:** 21 DMFs filed globally; 5 to 7 products expected to commercialize in FY27. Ketoprofen DMF filing for the U.S. is underway (currently on hold awaiting U.S. FDA customer trigger).
- **CDMO:** Developing 3 molecules for 2 global innovators. Validation quantities for the first molecule supplied; commercial supplies targeted for late Q3 FY27 or Q1 FY4 [Q4] FY27. Remaining two molecules commercialization targeted between Q4 FY27 to Q1 FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Reiterated between Rs 380 crore to Rs 400 crore.
- **FY28 Revenue Guidance:** Projected between Rs 650 crore to Rs 700 crore (or Rs 680 crore to Rs 700 crore).
- **Margin Outlook:** Management expects to operate within a sustainable EBITDA margin band of 25% to 30% (specifically aiming for 25% to 27% / 23% to 25% consolidated).
- **Anlon Medicos Revenue:** First revenue expected in Q4 FY27, reaching peak revenue of Rs 80 crore to Rs 90 crore from current tablet/capsule setup, with potential 2x-3x higher revenue upon Loxoprofen topical gel launch.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global API and CDMO markets benefit from outsourcing (73% of novel drug approvals in 2025 involved outsourced API manufacturing).
- India remains the primary beneficiary of China Plus One.
- Management stated China is not in direct competition for their specialized molecules (Loxoprofen, Ketoprofen, Dexketoprofen trometamol), where Anlon is priced ~10% lower than Chinese alternatives and holds high regulatory barriers (PMDA, NMPA, ANVISA, EDQM).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin moderated to 17.8% due to a spike in crude and petrochemical solvent prices from geopolitical situations and the turnaround phase of Anlon Medicos.
- Q2 margin recovered to 23.9% as price revisions were successfully passed on to customers.
- Raw material prices have not normalized and remain elevated.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Rajkot Greenfield Expansion:** Adds ~1,200 MTPA through 8 new clean lines, commissioning in Q1 FY28 (expected revenue contribution from Q2 FY28). Estimated capex is Rs 130 crore (Rs 15-20 crore spent so far; major outflows expected by December via advances).
- **Apiqo Expansion:** Adding 500 to 600 MTPA, expected to be complete by Q1 FY28.
- **Funding:** Planned bank term loan of Rs 65 crore to Rs 700 [Rs 70] crore for ongoing capex, with the balance met via internal accruals. Fund raising up to Rs 250 crore is being finalized for parent and subsidiary capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Nominal working capital cycle is 170 to 180 days.
- Receivable days improved due to tighter credit controls (reduced from 130-140 days down to 90-100 days).
- Shortened payable terms (approx. 90 days) are utilized to secure better raw material pricing, boosting overall PAT.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential delays in greenfield expansion timelines due to statutory procedures like public hearings scheduled for December.
- U.S. Ketoprofen DMF filing is on hold pending a U.S. FDA audit/trigger by a customer.
- Integration and turnaround costs of newly acquired subsidiaries temporarily weigh on consolidated return ratios (ROE).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital):** Asked about FY27/FY28 revenue targets and margin sustainability. Management confirmed Rs 380-400 crore for FY27 and Rs 650-700 crore for FY28, with EBITDA margins targeted at 25%-27%.
2. **Vaibhav Mishra (Finvestors Research):** Inquired about the status of CDMO commercial supplies. Management clarified it is delayed by 1-2 months, expected by late Q3 or early Q4 FY27.
3. **Prateek Srivastava (Nivesh Vista):** Questioned the reduction in DMF filing targets from 7 to "3 to 5" (clarified as ~6) and audit coverage of subsidiaries. Management confirmed consolidated accounts are reviewed by auditors and explained slight delays due to data complexity.
4. **Bhavesh Patel (Patel Investments):** Asked about strategies to improve ROE given the downward trend from acquisitions. Management stated profitability improvement via CDMO, formulations, and new API launches will drive ROE recovery by FY27.
5. **Priyansh Miri (NGP Family Office):** Asked about the rationale for entering medical devices. Management explained it captures the downstream ecosystem of pain management (orthopedic hospitals using NSAIDs and surgical implants/joints), targeting Rs 70-80 crore revenue in 3 years with superior EBITDA margins.

### One-line takeaway
Anlon Healthcare reported a robust Q2 FY27 with revenue doubling YoY to Rs 105.61 crore and EBITDA margins recovering 600 bps QoQ to 23.9%, driven by successful price pass-throughs and aggressive multi-vertical expansion.
