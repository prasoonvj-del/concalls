# Health X Platform Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Hospitals & Diagnostics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest quarter turnover in company history with strong top-line momentum, expanding geographical footprint, and narrowing operating losses.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 440 crore, up >58% YoY and up 16% QoQ (from Rs 378 crore in Q4 FY26).
- **July 2026 Monthly Revenue:** Rs 150+ crore (subject to audit).
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 34 crore, up 64% YoY and up 24% QoQ.
- **Gross Margin:** 7.8% (improved from 7.3% in Q4 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Loss of Rs 15 crore (EBITDA margin of -3.4%), narrowing sequentially from loss of Rs 20 crore (-5.5%) in Q4 FY26.
- **PAT:** Rs 2 crore, compared to a loss of Rs 13 crore in Q4 FY26 and Rs 26 crore in Q1 FY26.
- **Platform Scale:** ~75,000 retail pharmacies served, 50,000+ SKUs, 1,000+ vendors, 350+ health buddies, and 3,500+ employees.
- **Working Capital Cycle:** ~28 days (~8% of revenue).

### Highlights
- **Segment Performance:** RetailerShakti grew 48% YoY (gross margin ~7.8%, near EBITDA breakeven), while SastaSundar grew 44% YoY.
- **Order Basket Size:** RetailerShakti saw 69.4% of business from orders >Rs 2,500 and 42.4% from orders >Rs 5,000. For SastaSundar, 69.2% of orders were >Rs 1,000.
- **Geographic Footprint:** 70% revenue comes from West Bengal; 30% from expanded regions including Odisha, Jharkhand, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Assam, Haryana, UP, and Rajasthan. 
- **Tier 2/3 Penetration:** Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets contribute 67% of RetailerShakti business and 78% of SastaSundar business.
- **Private Label (JITO):** Revenue grew to Rs 79 lakh in Q1 FY27 from Rs 25 lakh in Q4 FY26, carrying a gross margin >50%.
- **Sourcing Efficiency:** Over 95% of purchases are direct from pharmaceutical companies without purchase rights, with purchase returns remaining below 1%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 to be the best year in the company's history, exceeding turnover achieved during the Flipkart partnership era.
- RetailerShakti is expected to turn EBITDA positive in Q3 FY27.
- Planned launch of AI-monitored "RetailAir" SaaS for retailers in Q2 FY27 to help optimize retailer inventory levels from 30 days down to 5–6 days.
- Management targets expanding consol gross margins from 8%+ in FY27 towards 12% over the next 2-3 years as private label (JITO) and non-pharma/wallet share scale up.
- Company does not prioritize near-term consolidated EBITDA breakeven, choosing to continue investing 15% of SastaSundar growth into tech/branding.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Deep tier 2/3 and underpenetrated regions (such as the Northeast and Jharkhand) are demonstrating the fastest growth rates.
- Management noted that JITO private label products target an emerging base of 70–80 crore first-time medicine buyers looking for affordable healthcare.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- SastaSundar investments/burn broken down into ~25–30% on marketing/advertisement, ~40–45% on tech-related costs, and ~30% on customer acquisition.
- Other expenses declined as a percentage of revenue due to operating leverage on fixed overheads.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capacity Utilization:** Existing fulfillment centers run at ~90% utilization. Existing facilities combined with side extensions can support revenues up to Rs 2,500–3,000 crore (sufficient through FY28).
- **Warehouse Infrastructure:** Operations active in Noida; building/expanding facilities in Guwahati (adding 75,000 sq ft owned capacity), Udaipur, Patna, Lucknow, and Ranchi.
- **Strategy:** Takes space on rent to launch operations immediately, while side-by-side constructing large owned fulfillment facilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Capital-efficient digital distribution model keeping working capital lean at ~28 days.
- Company owns logistics infrastructure, employing riders directly on payroll to maintain operational compliance and temperature control across 50,000 SKUs.
- Treasury income from cash reserves generated 7–8% of revenue.
- SEBI approval for company's merger/demerger scheme is on track.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Consolidated EBITDA remains negative (-3.4%) due to front-loaded technology and market expansion expenditures.
- Heavy reliance on West Bengal market (~70% of revenue), though new regions are scaling up quickly.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** *What is the long-term gross margin target and impact of JITO scaling up?*  
  **A:** JITO offers >50% gross margins. Combined B2B/B2C gross margin is targeted to expand from current ~8% to industry benchmark level of ~12% driven by JITO scale and higher-margin non-pharma wallet share.
- **Q:** *When will the company achieve consolidated EBITDA breakeven, and why continue burning cash on SastaSundar?*  
  **A:** Management clarified that RetailerShakti will break even in Q3 FY27. SastaSundar B2C burn (~15% of revenue) is spent on building software/AI tools and brand equity without acquiring external companies. Consolidated EBITDA breakeven is not prioritized for the next 2-3 years as tech reinvestment remains a strategic goal.
- **Q:** *Can you detail the Health Buddy franchisee model and JITO conversion plans?*  
  **A:** Health Buddies act as zero-inventory franchisees handling last-mile customer acquisition and delivery. 19 Health Buddies have converted into JITO clinics/pharmacies, with 25 more in pipeline; management aims to convert ~50% of Health Buddies to JITO in 3 months while rolling out standalone JITO franchisees.
- **Q:** *How are active retailers and wallet share performing on RetailerShakti?*  
  **A:** Out of 75,000 registered pharmacies, ~40,000 transacted in the last 30 days. Average wallet share is ~2%, which management targets to double via the upcoming RetailAir AI SaaS tool.

### One-line takeaway
Health X Platform delivered 58% YoY revenue growth in Q1 FY27 led by RetailerShakti and B2C recovery, staying focused on geographical expansion and tech investments over immediate consolidated EBITDA margin optimization.
