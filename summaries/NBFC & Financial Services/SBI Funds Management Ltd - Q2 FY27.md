# SBI Funds Management Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady industry growth, sustained leadership with 15% QAAUM market share, and strong SIP inflows despite market volatility.

### Key numbers
- **Industry Mutual Fund QAAUM:** Rs 87.5 trillion (up 13% YoY, 5% QoQ)
- **Industry Monthly SIP Inflows (Sept 2026):** Rs 32,490 crore (all-time high)
- **Industry Unique Customers:** 6.3 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **Company QAAUM Market Share:** 15% (leadership maintained)
- **Company Total Mutual Fund QAAUM:** Rs 13.14 lakh crore (up 10% YoY from Rs 12 lakh crore)
- **Active QAAUM:** Rs 8.99 lakh crore (up 9% YoY); 5-year CAGR at 17%
- **Passive QAAUM:** Rs 4.15 lakh crore (up 11% YoY)
- **PMS & Advisory QAAUM:** Rs 13.32 lakh crore
- **AIF QAAUM:** Rs 7,437 crore (up 27% YoY)
- **Offshore Funds Contribution:** Approximately Rs 42,100 crore
- **Unique Customers (Company):** 1.85 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up 10% YoY)
- **SIP AUM:** Rs 2.07 lakh crore (up 15% YoY)
- **Monthly SIP Inflows (Company):** About Rs 3,900 crore (or ~Rs 40 billion)
- **Distribution Network:** Nearly 136,000 partners (125,000+ IFAs, 10,000+ national distributors, 96+ banking partners with 23,000+ SBI branches and 297 SBIFM branches)
- **InvesTap App Registered Users:** Over 4.3 million; monthly digital volumes exceed 1.4 million transactions
- **H1 FY27 Operating Revenue:** Rs 2,336 crore (up 12% from Rs 2,089 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Operating Profit:** Rs 1,797 crore (up 11% from Rs 1,626 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 1,566 crore
- **Net Worth:** Rs 7,270 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Yields (Q2 FY27):** Equity yield ~62-63 bps; Debt yield 34-35 bps; Liquid yield 11-12 bps
- **Liquid QAAUM:** Rs 1.2 lakh crore (up 31% YoY)
- **SIF AUM:** Increased from Rs 35 billion to Rs 44 billion in the quarter

### Highlights
- Launched 5 new products in Q2, including one SIF.
- Magnum Equity Ex-Top 100 Long Short Fund closed its NFO as one of the largest in its category.
- Scaled GIFT City franchise with the launch of SBI Global Thematic Advantage Fund for global investments via LRS.
- Showcased 'Aarambh' tech platform at the Global Fintech Festival for assisted digital onboarding and Aadhaar-based biometric KYC.
- Appointed a new dedicated business head for the AIF practice to drive private equity and private credit capabilities.
- 94% of transactions done digitally.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects technology and human resource-related one-off expenses in Q2 to moderate significantly going forward, with a small part remaining till year-end.
- 50-60% of the additional spend is expected to recur due to senior management investments, while cloud and AI tech investments will continue incrementally as the business grows.
- Management plans to launch Category III AIFs in coming months and build Category II capabilities.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry saw steady domestic participation absorbing foreign outflows driven by crude, US yields, and strong dollar.
- Investor sentiment shows a shift towards safety, driving higher demand for SIPs and hybrid schemes despite a YTD NIFTY correction of 8.2%.
- B-30 contribution remains healthy, with nearly 2/3rd of SIP inflows originating from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
- Long-term investors show maturity, while newer investors (past 2-3 years) experience some anxiousness, countered by extensive education by the fund house and distributors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating costs were higher in Q2 due to heavy investments in cloud infrastructure, AI-driven systems, and senior management hiring.
- Gross margins across equity, debt, liquid, and passive strategies remained stable, with continued expansion in gross yields YoY.
- Q2 revenue yields are considered the new baseline run rate.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company is building its own data center, going live in a couple of months, which will shift cloud instances to on-premise instances and moderate certain tech costs.
- Expanding AIF, PMS, and SIF platforms.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong net worth of Rs 7,270 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global pressures from higher crude, firm US yields, and stronger dollar triggered foreign outflows and market volatility.
- Point-to-point NIFTY correction of 8.2% YTD noted.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal) on cost drivers and yields:** Management clarified that Q2 employee and other expenses included one-off investments in tech/cloud/AI and senior hiring, which will moderate. Yields remain stable with equity at 62-63 bps, debt at 34-35 bps, and liquid at 11-12 bps.
2. **Supratim Datta (Analyst) on AIF strategy and capital allocation:** Management stated AIF is currently developed as an internal division focusing on private equity and private credit with a new business head; subsidiary-like capital allocation will be considered later when it scales.
3. **Shreyas Pimple (Analyst) on SIF inflows:** Management noted SIF has ~16%+ market share with two funds launched and four more SEBI-approved options pending, though specific SIF-SIP data is unavailable.
4. **Lalit Mohan Deo (Analyst) on PMS AUM decline:** Management explained the decline was due to the one-time relocation of a large investor mandate in the advisory business, with no material impact on revenue.
5. **Abhijeet Sakhare (Analyst) on fund performance:** Management (Head of Investments) stated that 70% of equity and hybrid funds over 5 years are in the top 2 quartiles, and aggregate performance is seeing an encouraging upward trend.

### One-line takeaway
SBI Funds Management sustained its industry leadership with a 15% QAAUM market share and robust H1 FY27 PAT of Rs 1,566 crore, driven by record SIP inflows and strong digital adoption despite quarterly cost bumps.
