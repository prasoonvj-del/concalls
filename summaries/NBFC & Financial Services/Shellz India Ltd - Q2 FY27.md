# Shellz India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** H1 FY2027  
**Tone:** Positive - strong margin expansion, capacity expansion underway, alternate channels doubling, clear growth guidance

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs.95.7 Cr (reported +4% YoY, like-for-like +9% YoY after netting discounts)
- EBITDA: Rs.17.6 Cr (+21% YoY), margin 18.4% (+260 bps YoY)
- PAT: Rs.8.2 Cr (+31% YoY), margin 8.6% (+180 bps YoY)
- Trade receivables: Rs.37.8 Cr (down from Rs.48.6 Cr Mar'26)
- Inventory: broadly stable; Trade payables: Rs.37.1 Cr (up from Rs.35.4 Cr)
- Working capital cycle improved ~20+ days
- CWIP: Rs.65 Cr (entirely Phase 1 Sonipat)
- Capacity utilisation: ~75% (Delhi), target 90%+ post-Sonipat
- Alternate channels: 9% of H1 revenue (vs 4% FY2026), private label 5%
- Product mix H1: ~40% baked treats, ~35% choco delights, balance jellies/panned sugar
- Channel split: 91% super-stockist, 9% alternate (quick commerce, exports, modern trade, food ingredients)

### Highlights
- Maiden earnings call; GST rate cut from Sept'25 drove margin expansion (part reinvested in products)
- Sonipat Phase 1 (26,000 MTPA, Rs.350 Cr revenue capacity) partially operational, equipment on-site, completion in 6-8 months; Phase 2 (~35,000 MTPA, Rs.500 Cr) over following 12-18 months
- Alternate channels on track to more than double FY2026 contribution; private label already with one large South Asian player, talks with several MNCs/large Indian cos (visits scheduled)
- Tosca premium brand on Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, Amazon, Flipkart, modern trade; ratings >4 stars; marketing budget 15-20% per SKU
- Exports ~Rs.1 Cr last year (own brand), pivoting to developed markets with Sonipat certifications (BRCGS/SQF)
- IPO fund debt repayment Rs.22 Cr in process, completion in 2-4 weeks
- Earlier event: Capacity expansion to ~35,000 MTPA confirmed – Phase 1 to 26,000 MTPA underway, equipment arrived, partially operational

### Guidance & outlook
- H2 FY2027: ~40% YoY revenue growth (Sonipat activation + seasonality)
- FY2027 full year: >20% YoY growth
- Next two years: >40% YoY revenue growth, profits growing faster
- FY2029 aspiration: 2-2.5x FY2026 revenue
- Net margin target: 10-12% over time (vs current 8.6%)
- Debt to rise slightly by fiscal end then decline; WC requirement to grow slower than revenue
- Phase 1 capex ~Rs.60 Cr this year; asset turnover target 4x+

### Demand & industry commentary
- India confectionery: large, fast-evolving, impulse-led, majority demand at Rs.10 and below
- Growth drivers: rising disposable incomes, favorable demographics, expanding retail, new channels (quick commerce)
- Quick commerce compresses brand build timeline from 5-8 years to 1-2 years
- MNCs stick to core global portfolios, leaving gap for innovative, India-specific price-point products
- Per capita income growth shifting discretionary spend toward chocolates/unique confectionery, attracting large players to private label

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expansion driven by GST reduction (industry-wide) + mix/cost management
- Structural margin improvement expected from Sonipat: lower material wastage (automation), lower manpower, lower power/steam costs, operating leverage
- Sub-Rs.10 price points protected via shrinkflation (grammage reduction, packaging optimization) + economies of scale
- Private label: single-digit % points lower contribution margin but negative cash conversion cycle, zero marketing/sales expenses

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sonipat Phase 1: 17,000→26,000 MTPA, Rs.350 Cr revenue capacity, ~Rs.60 Cr capex, completion 6-8 months
- Phase 2: ~35,000 MTPA, Rs.500 Cr revenue capacity, over following 12-18 months
- Delhi facility to be upgraded into Sonipat in Phase 1
- Global certifications (BRCGS/SQF) targeted for MNC private label & developed market exports
- Regional distribution centers planned for West, Central, South to service existing super-stockist network

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables down Rs.10.8 Cr, payables up Rs.1.7 Cr, inventory stable → WC cycle +20 days
- Sonipat funded by debt + internal accruals; debt to peak at fiscal end then decline
- IPO proceeds: Rs.22 Cr debt repayment underway (2-4 weeks)
- Focus on sustainable positive operating cash flow, balancing capex/WC/cash generation

### Risks & concerns flagged
- H2 growth heavily dependent on Sonipat activation timeline (6-8 months for Phase 1 completion)
- Margin sustainability relies on successful automation, mix shift, operating leverage
- Private label lower margin offset by WC benefits but concentration risk (one large client currently)
- Developed market exports require customer audits (1-6 months conversion funnel)
- Competition from deep-pocketed MNCs in core categories
- Current capacity constraints limiting Hugs growth in West/Central/South
- Leadership depth: promoters actively managing, N-1 team in place, active recruitment ongoing
