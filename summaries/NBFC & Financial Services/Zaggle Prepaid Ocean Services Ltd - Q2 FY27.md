# Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth, festive momentum, and positive operating cash flows were offset by lower EBITDA margins and moderated program fee growth.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management did not explicitly reiterate the 40% annual revenue growth guidance, noting EBITDA margins would stay around 7% to 8% to prioritize cash flow.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to a greater focus on operating cash flow turnarounds and disciplined capitalization, noting early wins in cash flow management.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Full absorption of Dice employee costs and novation progress (65%-70% completed) were key updates. ADGM subsidiary opened.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Dice revenue realization commenced in Q2 as promised, with 65%-70% contracts novated.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Revenue Growth:** 34% YoY
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Crossed Rs 1,000 crore for the first time
- **Adjusted Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** ~7.7%
- **Propel Points Margin (Consolidated):** ~5.3%
- **Cashback as % of Program Fees:** 66.2%
- **86400 (Mobileware) Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 22.4 crore
- **86400 EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 6 crore
- **86400 PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 3 crore
- **86400 H1 FY27 Transaction Value:** Rs 3 lakh crore (26% YoY growth)
- **GreenEdge Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 58 crore
- **GreenEdge EBITDA & PAT (Q2 FY27):** ~Rs 2.6 crore each
- **TaxSpanner H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 2.1 crore (22.6% YoY growth, EBITDA breakeven)
- **Consolidated Revenue Mix (Q2 FY27):** SaaS platform fees Rs 16 crore, Program fees Rs 195 crore, Propel Points Rs 369 crore
- **Consolidated Revenue Mix (H1 FY27):** SaaS platform fees Rs 28.5 crore, Program fees Rs 355 crore, Propel Points Rs 619 crore

### Highlights
- Achieved positive operating cash flow on both a standalone and consolidated basis for Q2 FY27.
- Completed 65% to 70% of Dice contract novations, inheriting marquee clients like Hindalco, Trident, Bajel, Aakash, IDFC First Bank, and HDB Financial.
- Deployed full-stack Agentic accounts payable platform phase one at a large manufacturing/metals client with 7 AI agents.
- Zagg.money scaled users and transactions significantly while maintaining fraud defenses (RASP, SMV).
- 86400 went live with Fino Payments Bank for the Bharat Bill Payment stack and progressed on LankaPay integration.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for EBITDA margins to remain in the 7% to 8% range, focusing on operating cash flow and controlled capitalization rather than aggressive margin expansion.
- Remaining 30% to 35% of Dice contracts are expected to novate in Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Festive season fueled strong growth in the loyalty and reward segment for Zaggle and GreenEdge.
- Anticipates tailwinds from the potential introduction of MDR on UPI for Zagg.money.
- Strong adoption in Zaggle Save driven by favorable income tax regulation updates (higher limits, new tax regime inclusion).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Full three-month salary base for 100+ Dice AI professionals was recorded under employee benefit expenses.
- Other expenses rose due to direct P&L expensing of certain tech and AI costs.
- Cashback as a percentage of program fees improved marginally to 66.2% in Q2 FY27 compared to Q4 FY26 levels (~69%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened a subsidiary, Zaggle Holdings Limited, in ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Market), UAE.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Prioritizing banking partners willing to work on suitable invoicing patterns and payment terms.
- Altered payment cycles for existing Propel customers to half-monthly invoicing and began taking customer advances.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Zagg.money will continue to experience some burn over the next few quarters as the user base is scaled up.
- Need to remain mindful of spends on open sourcing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rohan (Equirus Securities) on FY27 EBITDA guidance:** Management stated EBITDA margins are expected to stay around 7% to 8% as the focus remains on operating cash flow and controlled capitalization.
- **Rohan on Save GTV and Program Fees:** Management noted loads grew 6x, which will positively impact program fees, though they remained cautious on giving explicit EBITDA guidance.
- **Siva (iThought PMS) on Dice contribution and costs:** Management stated Q2 SaaS revenue includes a small part from Dice, with full impact expected in Q3 and Q4 as remaining contracts novate. Steady-state revenues were ~Rs 14 crore annually.
- **Siva on Program Fee growth slowdown:** Management explained they are balancing growth with controlled cashback (at ~66%) and faster cash realization, expecting momentum to pick up in Q3 and Q4.
- **Harsh on prepaid card loading balance:** Management clarified that the balance represents card loads maintained for near real-time fulfillment, which is standard industry practice.

### One-line takeaway
Zaggle achieved positive consolidated operating cash flows and strong top-line growth in Q2 FY27, successfully integrating the Dice acquisition while prioritizing working capital discipline over short-term margin expansion.
