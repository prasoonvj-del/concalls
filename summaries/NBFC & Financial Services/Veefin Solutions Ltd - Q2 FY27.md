# Veefin Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, expanding operating margins, and improving DSO.

### Key numbers
- **Group Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs. 238.82 crores (up 116.2% YoY)
- **Group PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs. 24.85 crores
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs. 49.19 crores (up 83.6% YoY)
- **Standalone PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs. 13.97 crores (up 79.4% YoY)
- **Group EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs. 60.81 crores (up 159.5% YoY; margin at 25.5% vs 21.2% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs. 27.2 crores (up 107.4% YoY; margin at 55.3% vs 49% YoY)
- **Group Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 124.66 crores (up 9.2% QoQ)
- **Group EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 36.7 crores (up 52.2% QoQ; margin at 29.4% vs 21.1% in Q1)
- **Group PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 15.32 crores (up 60.7% QoQ; margin at 12.3% vs 8.4% in Q1)
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 25.86 crores (up 10.9% QoQ)
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 13.97 crores (margin at 54%)
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 6.99 crores (margin at 27%)
- **Open Opportunity Pipeline:** Approx. Rs. 920 crores (with ~Rs. 240 crores added in Q2; ~Rs. 46 crores contracted out)
- **DSO:** Improved to 88 days in H1 FY27 (vs 99 days in FY26, 135 days in FY25, 149 days in FY24)
- **Product Development Investment (H1 FY27):** ~Rs. 58 crores at group level; ~Rs. 35 crores on a standalone basis

### Highlights
- Group and standalone revenues and operating profits showed strong YoY and sequential expansion.
- Recurring fees accounted for 82.1% of H1 standalone revenue (89.8% in Q2). Existing customers contributed 73.9% of H1 revenue (69.9% in Q2).
- Developed Veefin AIOS (AI Operating System) with over 1,000 skills, deployed internally across three factories (implementation, support, and testing).
- BSE in-principle approval for migration is in place; application is currently with NSE.
- Voluntarily adopted Ind AS to prepare for the main board transition.
- Strengthened leadership: Pavan joined as Chief Delivery Officer (ex-Tech Mahindra) and Akash Munjal moved to Chief Customer Officer.
- White Rivers Media (WRM) DRHP expected to be filed in January.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they are at the tail end of the product development capitalization cycle, and from next year 99% of product development expenses will flow directly through the P&L.
- Management guided that investors should track EBITDA margins in the 45% to 50% range.
- Management expects the historical growth rate to continue for the next 2 years (forward-looking statement by management).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pipeline breakdown: Supply chain finance (35%), digital lending (23%), trade and cash (18%), digital banking (16%), and fraud and risk (single digits). 65% of pipeline value is beyond supply chain finance.
- Out of 77 pipeline opportunities, 38 involve more than one product. 75% of the pipeline is fully qualified.
- Geographical distribution of the pipeline: 16% in India, 84% international (Southeast Asia, Middle East, Africa).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin was 54% in Q2 (vs 56.7% in Q1). Group EBITDA margin expanded to 29.4% in Q2 (vs 21.1% in Q1).
- Increase in cost of earnings of revenue is variable, project-based, and impacted by the full 6-month consolidation of White Rivers Media in H1 FY27 vs 3 months in H1 FY26.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No equity or debt raising plans currently beyond existing sanctioned limits.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Negative cash flows from operations in H1 were attributed to a temporary working capital gap (payment of current/statutory liabilities, unbilled revenue, and prepaid expenses), which management expects to cover in H2 as billings increase.
- Working capital credit lines from banks remain partially undrawn.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management other than competitive sensitivities preventing granular product-level revenue disclosures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kenil Modi (Nuvama Wealth)** asked about 3-year revenue drivers. Management replied growth will be a mix of existing clients, new clients, and new product lines, with international markets dominating the pipeline.
2. **Tushar Sharma** asked about the rationale for employee/product development cost changes. Management explained that product development capitalization is tapering off and will flow fully through P&L next year.
3. **Imran Khan** questioned past fundraising numbers. Management clarified the breakdown between Rs. 166 crores raised in subsidiaries in Sep 2024 and Rs. 88 crores raised at Veefin level in Dec 2025.
4. **Abhishek Shah** asked about PSB Xchange revenue for Q2 and details on AIOS. Management declined product-level revenue disclosure for competitive reasons and explained AIOS has dual use (integrated with Veefin products or standalone).
5. **Paras Chheda** asked about negative cash flows and WRM listing status. Management stated unbilled revenue will convert in H2, and WRM DRHP will be filed in January.
6. **Jeet Gala** asked about PSB Xchange run-rate and peak debt. Management declined to share specific PSB transaction/AUM data due to competitive sensitivities and partner bank restrictions, and stated no additional debt beyond sanctioned limits is planned.

### One-line takeaway
Veefin delivered strong H1 FY27 performance with triple-digit group revenue growth, expanding operating margins, and improving DSO, while scaling its multi-product transaction banking portfolio and AIOS framework.
