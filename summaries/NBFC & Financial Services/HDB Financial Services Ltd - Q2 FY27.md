# HDB Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-20 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong profit growth (34% YoY) and resilient disbursement growth (18.7% YoY) despite macro headwinds and a rising interest rate environment.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained steady-state credit cost guidance of ~2.3%. Maintained NIM target of 8%+.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "resilient" to "resilient despite global headwinds and uneven monsoon." Management remains optimistic about H2 demand.
- **New topics or risks:** 25 bps repo rate hike (Oct 7, 2026); drought declared in some Maharashtra districts (monitorable for inflation/food production).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Unsecured business loans returned to sequential growth (as promised in Q1). AI "Shikhar" project is now yielding measurable results in onboarding and collections.

### Key numbers
- **PAT:** Rs 780 crore (+34% YoY).
- **Disbursements:** Rs 18,522 crore (+18.7% YoY).
- **Gross Loan Book:** Rs 1,24,469 crore (+11.7% YoY, +2.2% QoQ).
- **Gross Stage 3 (GNPA):** 2.43% (vs 2.81% YoY, 2.34% QoQ).
- **NIM:** 8.23% (vs 7.95% YoY, 8.35% QoQ).
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 39.3% (vs 40.7% YoY, 39.9% QoQ).
- **Credit Cost:** 2.37% (vs 2.32% in Q1 FY27).
- **Customer Franchise:** 24.8 million (+18.4% YoY).
- **Capital Adequacy:** 21.37%.

### Highlights
- **Enterprise Lending:** Disbursements grew 25.5% YoY; Gold loans grew >60% YoY; Unsecured Business Loans returned to sequential growth.
- **Consumer Finance:** Disbursements grew >25% YoY; Consumer Durables book grew 49% YoY.
- **Asset Finance:** Commercial Vehicle book grew 8% YoY; Construction Equipment book grew 12% YoY.
- **Technology:** "Shikhar" AI project is reducing customer onboarding time; 1 in 5 early-bucket collection calls are now handled by an AI bot.
- **Dividend:** Interim dividend of Rs 2 per equity share declared (Record date: Oct 23, 2026).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Management expects** strong H2 performance driven by festive demand.
- **Management expects** NIM to hold in a narrow range (8%+).
- **Management targets** an annualized ROA of 2.5%+ in the short to medium term.
- **Management expects** Asset Finance growth to pick up in coming months.
- **Management expects** Unsecured Business Loans momentum to continue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Economy:** Real GDP growth projection for FY27 revised to 7.1%.
- **Monetary Policy:** Repo rate hiked by 25 bps on Oct 7, 2026.
- **Risks:** Uneven monsoon/drought in some Maharashtra districts is a key monitorable for inflation and food production.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Borrowing cost:** 7.04%.
- **Management notes** the ability to pass on rate hikes via the floating-rate Loan Against Property (LAP) book.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Distribution:** Retail and dealer touchpoints grew 10% to 1.65 lakh+.
- **Headcount:** Management stated headcount reduction is driven by productivity gains from AI/automation, not a reduction in footprint or coverage.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Portfolio Mix:** Secured loans comprise 73.9% of the gross loan book.
- **Liquidity:** Borrowing mix remains well-diversified with positive cumulative mismatch up to 5 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Asset Quality:** Slight buildup in Stage 2 assets in CV/CE portfolios due to seasonality; management expects normalization in coming quarters.
- **Macro:** Potential impact of drought on inflation and food prices.
- **Rates:** Uncertainty regarding future repo rate hikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **NIM/Rates:** Analysts questioned the impact of rate hikes; management clarified they have a floating-rate LAP book to pass on costs and will manage NIMs in a narrow range.
- **Asset Finance Growth:** Analysts questioned weak growth; management attributed it to internal "rejigging" and market challenges in Used CV, expecting a turnaround in Q3/Q4.
- **Stage 2 Buildup:** Analysts asked about higher Stage 2 accretion; management cited seasonality in CV/CE and expects normalization.
- **Headcount:** Analysts asked about headcount reduction; management clarified it is due to productivity gains from AI/automation, not branch closures.
- **Fee Income:** Analysts asked about potential regulatory impact on insurance commissions; management noted they are in discussions with regulators and have plans in place.

### One-line takeaway
HDB Financial delivered strong profit growth and improved operational efficiency via AI, while management remains confident in a H2 recovery despite macro headwinds and a rising interest rate environment.
