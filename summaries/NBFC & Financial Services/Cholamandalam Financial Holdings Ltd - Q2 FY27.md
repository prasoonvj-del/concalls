# Cholamandalam Financial Holdings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited tough operating conditions, elevated motor OD loss ratios, and severe NatCat impacts, though underlying sourcing metrics showed positive trends.

### Key numbers
- **GDPI (Industry H1):** 8.9% (Q1: 11%, Q2: 7%)
- **Motor Segment Industry Growth (H1):** 13%
- **Gross Premium (Chola MS Q2):** INR2,386 crore (up 7.4% YoY)
- **Gross Premium (Chola MS H1):** INR4,517 crore (up 7.1% YoY)
- **Motor Premium Growth (Chola MS H1):** 4.1%
- **Commercial Lines Growth (Chola MS H1):** 6%
- **NatCat Impact (H1):** INR71 crore (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Daman, and Diu)
- **Large Risk Incremental Impact:** INR14 crore
- **Combined Ratio (Chola MS H1):** 124.2% (excluding NatCat: 122.7%)
- **PBT (H1):** INR98 crore
- **PAT (H1):** INR78 crore
- **September Growth:** ~6%
- **October Growth:** ~4%
- **Investment Portfolio:** ~INR19,050 crore
- **Carrying Yield on Debt Book:** 7.33% (up 20 bps from March)
- **Realized Yield (H1):** 7.45% (Q2: 7.2%, Q1: 7.2%)
- **Net Worth:** INR3,409 crore
- **Solvency Ratio:** 1.88x
- **Paid-up Capital:** ~INR442 crore

### Highlights
- Management noted that prior caution driven by financial lenses led to lost counter share, which they are now actively regaining through an underwriting lens.
- Continued to stay away from the two-wheeler portfolio due to inadequate TP pricing.
- De-grown the group health business by shunning large accounts with unsustainable pricing.
- *Note on Record:* While consolidated CFHL growth remained strong due to the core NBFC subsidiary (as per record), the general insurance business faced severe profitability headwinds from NatCat events.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident that recent sourcing actions, pricing discipline, and NCB portfolio focus will moderate motor loss ratios downwards over the next 3 to 4 quarters.
- Management expects a further improvement of roughly 5% in sourcing loss ratios from current levels.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Retail health continues to be one of the fastest-growing segments.
- Group health (employer-employee) faces significant pricing pressures.
- Commercial fire insurance witnessed significant pricing competitive intensity, though discipline returned from mid-September.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sourcing loss ratios in motor (including NatCat) improved by 1% to 1.5% in H1 YoY; excluding NatCat, improvement is 4% to 5%.
- Average Claim Severity (ACS) on the private car book targeted for reduction.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No stressed assets in the investment portfolio; no exposure to securities rated BB and below.
- Headroom available via subordinated debt to support solvency if required (up to ~INR1,700 crore potential capacity based on net worth).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Multiple NatCat events across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Daman, and Diu without overlap windows caused high net accumulation impact (INR71 crore) due to reinsurance hours clauses.
- Legacy theft claims adjudicated through consumer forums with high interest components adversely affected loss ratios.
- Actual claim payouts for flood/total losses higher than carried reserves.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark):** Asked why motor OD loss ratios deteriorated despite sourcing changes and how long it takes to reflect. *Management answered that legacy consumer forum theft claims and higher-than-reserved flood payouts impacted numbers, but sourcing loss ratios are improving by 4%-5% (ex-NatCat), taking 3-4 quarters to fully reflect in reported financials.*
2. **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark):** Asked about the strategy on the two-wheeler portfolio given IFRS pressures. *Management stated nothing is off the table and they are evaluating pockets within two-wheelers, balancing profitability, compliance, and claims performance.*
3. **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark):** Asked about the impact of the proposed distribution/commission consultation paper on credit-linked insurance. *Management welcomed the customer-centric approach, stated stress scenarios show the impact is within risk appetite, and noted financing-linked arrangements may continue with customer consent under DPDP.*
4. **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark):** Asked if solvency (1.88x) requires capital raising given dragged ROEs. *Management replied there is sufficient headroom via subordinated debt and multiple capital levers (including premium in advance and retention adjustments).*

### One-line takeaway
Cholamandalam MS General Insurance faced profitability headwinds in H1 FY27 due to INR71 crore in NatCat impacts and legacy claims, but management expects underwriting and sourcing corrections to normalize loss ratios over the next 3 to 4 quarters.
