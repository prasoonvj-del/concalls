# NBFC & Financial Services - Consumer & diversified NBFCs: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the consumer and diversified NBFC sector reported a robust Q2 FY27, characterized by strong AUM growth, record profitability, and improved operational metrics. Significant profit jumps were reported across players—such as IIFL Finance (+93% YoY PAT), SBI Cards (+75% YoY PAT), Fedbank Financial Services (+63% YoY PAT), HDB Financial Services (+34% YoY PAT), and L&T Finance (+33% YoY PAT). 

A common thread across the sector is the heavy deployment of technology and AI (e.g., HDB's "Shikhar" project, L&T Finance's "Project Hercules", and IIFL/Jio's AI tools) to drive productivity, collections, and cross-selling. However, firms are also navigating macro headwinds, including recent repo rate hikes and potential regulatory changes from the IRDAI draft on insurance commissions.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board (including Fedbank, HDB, IIFL, L&T Finance, and SBI Cards) share a consensus that **H2 FY27 will be stronger** than H1, driven by seasonality, festive demand, and rural recovery. L&T Finance and HDB noted that rural consumption remains resilient and disbursement momentum is carefully increasing. SBI Cards expects retail spend per card to rise above 170k in Q3 due to festivals.
*   **Divergence:** While secured lending, gold loans, and credit cards show strong momentum, pockets of stress or muted growth exist. IIFL and HDB noted muted microfinance or asset finance (CV/CE) growth due to seasonality, drought conditions in certain pockets, or internal portfolio "rejigging." Jio Financial is consciously pivoting away from low-margin segments to margin-accretive areas in payments.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Cost of Funds & Rates:** Borrowing costs are seeing upward pressure or managing tight ranges due to repo and T-bill rate hikes. Borrowing costs stand at 6.8% for SBI Cards, 7.04% for HDB, 7.08% for Jio Financial, 7.80% for Fedbank, and 9.08% for IIFL Finance. 
*   **Margin Protection:** To combat rising funding costs, companies are actively protecting NIMs. SBI Cards has implemented a ~50 bps hike in POS installment lending and post-EMI conversion rates. HDB and Fedbank are utilizing floating-rate Loan Against Property (LAP) books and multiple yield levers to absorb shocks.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Branch Expansion:** Aggressive physical footprint expansion remains a priority. Fedbank opened 74 branches in Q2 (totaling 831) and plans 150+ more in H2. L&T Finance launched 172 new branches in H1, targeting 800 gold loan branches by FY-end. IIFL Finance crossed 5,000 total branches, and Jio Financial covered 19,000+ PIN codes with physical mortgage offices in major cities.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Most companies (Fedbank, HDB, IIFL, L&T Finance) maintained their AUM growth, RoA, and credit cost guidance, preferring consistency over upgrades despite strong H1 performances. SBI Cards maintained its mid-to-high teens spend growth and 6% credit cost guidance.
*   **Tone Shift:** The tone has universally shifted from "navigating regulatory headwinds/cautious on unsecured" in Q1 to **"confident momentum" and resilience** for H2.
*   **New Risks & Topics:** The IRDAI consultation paper on insurance commission rationalization emerged as a notable new regulatory risk for IIFL Finance, L&T Finance, HDB, and Jio Financial, with companies evaluating potential revenue impacts.

## Company one-liners
*   **Fedbank Financial Services:** Delivered a record-performance quarter with 17% ROE and 37% AUM growth, successfully managing regulatory transitions in gold lending.
*   **HDB Financial Services:** Delivered strong profit growth and improved operational efficiency via AI, while management remains confident in a H2 recovery.
*   **IIFL Finance Ltd:** Achieved record profitability and strong AUM growth driven by secured lending, while actively managing capital adequacy via a planned equity raise.
*   **Jio Financial Services Ltd:** Aggressively pivoting toward margin-accretive growth and AI-driven hyper-personalization across a scaling financial ecosystem.
*   **L&T Finance Ltd:** Achieved record profitability and improved RoA in Q2 FY27, executing its "Risk-First, Tech-First" strategy while managing monsoon risks.
*   **SBI Cards and Payment Services Ltd:** Delivered a 75% YoY PAT jump underpinned by multi-year low credit costs, disciplined opex, and strong new card additions.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **IRDAI Regulations:** Finalization of the draft norms on insurance commissions and the extent of fee-income impact on IIFL, L&T Finance, and HDB.
*   **Festive Season Translation:** Actual conversion of festive demand into higher receivables and spends, particularly for SBI Cards, HDB, and consumer-facing NBFCs.
*   **Gold Loan & CV Asset Quality:** Normalization of Stage 2/3 gold loan assets following technical regulatory model shifts (Fedbank/IIFL) and cyclical CV/CE stabilization (HDB).
*   **Capital Raises:** Execution and dilution impact of IIFL Finance's planned equity raise and Jio Financial's capital infusion tranche.
