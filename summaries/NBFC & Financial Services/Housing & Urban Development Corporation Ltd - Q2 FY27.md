# Housing & Urban Development Corporation Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong business momentum driven by government infrastructure mandates, with clear targets for loan book growth and margin sustainability.

### Key numbers
*   **Loan Book:** Rs 1.86 lakh crore (+29% YoY).
*   **Loan Disbursements (H1):** >Rs 35,000 crore (+37% YoY).
*   **Loan Sanctions (H1):** >Rs 1 lakh crore.
*   **Revenue from Operations:** +26% YoY.
*   **Net Profit (H1):** +31% YoY.
*   **Gross NPA:** 0.7%.
*   **Net NPA:** 0.04%.
*   **Blended Cost of Funds:** 7.07%.
*   **Spread:** ~2% (1.96% currently).
*   **NIM:** ~3% (2.93% currently).
*   **FY27 Disbursement Target:** Rs 65,000 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Growth:** Management attributes performance to government focus on "Viksit Bharat" and urban infrastructure (PMAY, AMRUT, Smart Cities).
*   **Asset Quality:** Management is aggressively resolving legacy assets via NCLT/DRAT; aims to become a zero Net NPA company shortly.
*   **Liability Management:** Successfully utilized RBI swap window for $2 billion in ECB at an effective cost of ~6.75%.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Launched "Urban Invest Window" (UiWIN) to identify ULB assets and facilitate financial closure.
*   **Asset Monetization:** Targeting creation of 1 lakh square meters of space over the next three years.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Loan Book:** Management targets Rs 2 lakh crore by end of FY27 and Rs 3 lakh crore by FY30.
*   **Margins:** Management maintains guidance for 2% spreads and 3% NIMs.
*   **ROA:** Management targets 2% - 2.2%.
*   **Credit Costs:** Management expects negative credit costs (reversals) for 1-2 years due to high provision coverage (95%).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Urban population expected to double by 2050; urban networks contribute 60%-70% of GDP.
*   Government initiatives like the Urban Challenge Fund (50% VGF) and City Economic Region program are expected to drive long-term demand.
*   Company remains sector-agnostic, financing housing, infrastructure, satellite towns, and mobility projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Funds:** Blended cost is 7.07%; management is diversifying funding sources (domestic/international) to optimize costs.
*   **Repricing:** Loans are reset on a 1-year tranche-wise basis; management is transitioning some to quarterly resets to pass on cost increases.
*   **Tax Rate:** ~21%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Not discussed as a manufacturing entity; focus remains on lending capacity and internal organizational strengthening.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Total Borrowing:** ~Rs 1.65 lakh crore.
*   **Currency Risk:** Foreign currency translation losses are zero; ECB exposure is adequately hedged via RBI swap window.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Environment:** Geopolitical uncertainties and rising corporate bond yields impacting money markets.
*   **PPP Financing:** Management emphasized strict risk mitigation for non-sovereign lending, including third-party due diligence (CRISIL/ICRA) and strong concession agreements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Sanket Chheda (DAM Capital):* Asked about yield/margin sustainability. Management confirmed 2% spread/3% NIM targets, noting 1-year reset cycles protect margins.
*   *Nemin Doshi:* Questioned the "other income" growth vs NII. Management clarified that higher NIMs are driven by return on equity and asset monetization.
*   *Divyam Doshi:* Asked about RBI Upper Layer classification. Management stated it poses no operational bottleneck, only increased compliance requirements.
*   *Harsh Hulsogi:* Asked about credit costs. Management expects negative credit costs/reversals for 1-2 years due to high provision coverage.

### One-line takeaway
HUDCO is delivering strong growth supported by government urban infrastructure mandates, with a clear strategy to maintain margins through active liability management and aggressive asset quality resolution.
