# PB Fintech Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Deliveries driven by strong 41% YoY premium growth, 40% YoY revenue expansion, and a 92% YoY surge in net profit.

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Operating Revenue:** ₹1,888 Cr (up 40% YoY).
* **Consolidated PAT:** ₹163 Cr (up 92% YoY); PAT Margin expanded to 9% (vs 6% YoY). 
* **Total Insurance Premium:** ₹8,372 Cr (up 41% YoY).
* **Core Business Growth:** Core insurance revenue up 46% YoY; Core credit revenue up 25% YoY.
* **Core Online New Premium:** Up 39% YoY including savings; up 48% YoY excluding savings.
* **Renewals & Trail Revenue:** ₹1,003 Cr for the trailing 12 months (up 55% YoY).
* **Credit Disbursals (Paisabazaar):** ₹4,366 Cr (up 33% YoY).
* **PB Partners (POSP):** Premium grew 46% YoY to ₹1,637 Cr; Revenue grew 47% YoY to ₹561 Cr. Active partners reached 1.13 lacs (up 55% YoY).
* **UAE Business:** Premium grew 31% YoY.

### Highlights
* **Protection Focus:** New health and term insurance grew 53% YoY, with core new health growing 59% YoY.
* **Customer Acquisition:** 82% of incoming insurance business represents new customers (first-time policyholders).
* **Service & Claims:** Supported ~70,000 health claims in Q1 FY27 with Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) score above 90%. Over 30% of health insurance sales now run on a monthly payment mode.
* **Savings & GIFT City:** Savings business grew >20% YoY in fresh business; Waiver of Premium (WOP) plans crossed 60% of domestic business in select centers in July 2026. Retail dollar savings plans were launched via GIFT City.
* **AI Adoption:** AI touches 30–40% of the ~10 Cr monthly customer interactions, with 20–30% of service interactions fully automated. Saved ~₹10,000 Cr of sum assured on risk models.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Fresh Business Target:** Management continues to target a baseline fresh business growth rate of >30% YoY.
* **Renewal Revenues:** Renewal growth is projected to stay above 50% YoY for the near term, outstripping fresh growth.
* **PB Health Target:** Target internal annual revenue run rate of ~₹500 Cr with breakeven by March 2027 (Mar'27).
* **Paisabazaar EBITDA:** Expected full-year EBITDA of ~₹50 Cr (revised from prior expectation of ~₹100 Cr).
* **Product Expansion:** Launching daily SIPs and a proprietary bonds platform on PB Money by end of August 2026. Non-credit revenues on Paisabazaar expected to remain under 10-15% over 2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Health and term categories remain severely under-penetrated in India. Demand for organic pull-in insurance remains structurally low, requiring continuous investment in demand creation.
* South India has demonstrated higher market growth rates compared to the North and West regions over the last 3 years.
* Motor direct-to-consumer (D2C) direct business grew >30% YoY, while motor POSP grew ~50% YoY.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Operating Expenses:** Employee Benefit Expenses stood at 15% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (vs 12% in Q4 FY26 due to seasonality).
* **Hiring:** Gross addition of 5,000 employees during Q1 FY27 to support prospective growth.
* **Margin Strategy:** Management reiterated that top-line growth takes absolute priority over near-term margin expansion, continuing to reinvest efficiency gains into marketing and customer acquisition.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **PB Health:** Secured approval for billing at a second hospital; currently operating 2 hospitals with several others in the pipeline.
* **PB Partners:** 78% of GWP originates from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, spanning >19,000 PIN codes (99% coverage in India). The share of top 100 partners reduced to 16% of premium.
* **PB Connect:** Restructured business to focus exclusively on retail POSP; wholesale revenues were completely phased out (zero in Q1 FY27; retail revenue was ₹12 Cr).

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Regulatory headwinds and potential shifts in aggregator/broker commission structures or policies around customer contact details collection (phone numbers).
* Near-term high base effects from H2 FY26 (especially Q3) post-tax regulatory changes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA):** *How is PB Fintech growing health/term at 53-59% YoY vs sluggish industry growth, and how to view regulatory talk on effort-based commission changes?*
  * **Management:** Growth is driven by upfront demand-creation marketing, transparent pricing/product structures (like WOP and 3-month waiting period maternity plans), and strong claim support. On regulation: Legally, brokers perform the maximum effort in insurance distribution (enquiry generation, conversion, issuance, claim support); management noted platform economics operate at barely 1-2% profit margins on premium.
* **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal):** *What is the update and unit economics on PB Health initiatives?*
  * **Management:** PB Health reported a ₹7 Cr loss for Q1. Management targets an annual run rate of ~₹500 Cr and breakeven by March 2027, driven by synergistic integration with insurance claims and preventive care (Fitterfly monthly revenues expanded 4x post-acquisition).
* **Dipanjan Ghosh:** *What is the strategy for Paisabazaar beyond credit?*
  * **Management:** Launching daily SIPs targeting self-employed individuals with variable monthly income. Non-credit products (mutual funds, bill payments, bonds) serve primarily as user engagement and retention tools, contributing 5-10% of Paisabazaar’s revenue over the next 2 years.
* **Supratim Datta:** *What is the impact of potential Supreme Court verdicts extending motor Third Party (TP) insurance coverage periods?*
  * **Management:** Motor TP represents ~25% of D2C motor premium and ~50% of POSP motor business. Extended TP periods will raise upfront new-vehicle ticket sizes; stricter regulatory enforcement of TP insurance (via automated check posts/fuel pumps) could trigger significant demand surges.
* **Shreya Shivani (Nomura):** *Why did outside contribution expenses drop to 58% of revenue in Q1 FY27, and what is the trajectory of call center expenses?*
  * **Management:** Cost deceleration was not from active cost-cutting, but operating leverage (revenues grew 40% vs costs at 35%). Call center employee costs are fully captured under Employee Benefit Expenses.

### One-line takeaway
PB Fintech delivered robust 40% revenue growth with sharp PAT margin expansion, maintaining market share leadership while aggressively reinvesting into protection products, POSP expansion, and healthcare integration.
