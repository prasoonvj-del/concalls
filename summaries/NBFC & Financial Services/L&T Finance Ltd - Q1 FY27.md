# L&T Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-20 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly profit and robust retail disbursement growth, supported by disciplined, tech-led execution despite macroeconomic volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated PAT:** ₹902 Cr (+29% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Book:** ₹1,29,634 Cr (+27% YoY).
*   **Retail Disbursements:** ₹23,852 Cr (+36% YoY).
*   **RoA:** 2.48% (+11 bps YoY).
*   **RoE:** 12.71% (+185 bps YoY).
*   **NIMs + Fees:** 10.47%.
*   **Credit Cost:** 2.54% (-10 bps QoQ).

### Highlights
*   **Lakshya 2031 Strategy:** Management reported tracking well against the 20%+ CAGR book growth target, achieving 27% growth in Q1.
*   **Tech-First Approach:** "Project Cyclops" (AI-powered underwriting) is live in Two-Wheeler, Farm, Personal Loans, and SME businesses; "Project Nostradamus" (portfolio intelligence) is live in Two-Wheeler and Personal Loans.
*   **Personal Loans:** Highest-ever quarterly disbursement of ₹4,380 Cr (+126% YoY); focus remains on salaried customers.
*   **Gold Finance:** Book reached ~₹3,800 Cr (+180% YoY); network expanded to 343 branches.
*   **Risk Management:** Management proactively tightened credit guardrails, foregoing ₹1,000 Cr–₹1,200 Cr in potential disbursements to protect asset quality.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Profitability:** Management targets RoA of 2.8% by Q4 FY27 and 3.0–3.2% by FY31; RoE target of 16-18% by FY31.
*   **Credit Cost:** Management targets <2% over the Lakshya period, with a specific goal of 2.0%–2.2% by Q4 FY27.
*   **Cost of Funds:** Management expects a 4-5 bps increase in FY27, with WACB projected between 7.35%–7.40%.
*   **Payments Business:** Management is building an "agentic commerce" model; revenue flows are expected from the next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro Environment:** Management noted resilient domestic demand despite geopolitical uncertainties and monsoon variability.
*   **Rural Economy:** Rural demand indicators remain healthy; reservoir levels are sufficient to sustain agricultural output.
*   **Microfinance:** Management believes the industry has deleveraged, creating a "Goldilocks period" for asset quality.
*   **Tractor Business:** Q1 is seasonally soft; management expects volume pickup in July/August.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **NIMs:** Compressed 24 bps QoQ (8.78% to 8.54%) due to higher debt-equity and surplus liquidity maintenance; however, NIMs + Fees remained stable at 10.47%.
*   **IT Costs:** Quarterly IT cost trajectory is ₹100 Cr–₹120 Cr.
*   **Infrastructure:** Management expects the transition to a private cloud to be 70% cheaper than hyperscale clouds over 5 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Gold Finance:** Targeting ~500 new branches in FY27 (velocity of ~1.4 branches/day).
*   **Tech:** Investing in private cloud build-out and high-performance GPU server augmentations to support AI workloads.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Retail Book:** ₹1,27,535 Cr (+28% YoY).
*   **Liquidity:** Maintained ~₹9,000 Cr surplus at quarter-end (fluctuated during the quarter due to geopolitical risk).
*   **Debt-Equity:** 3.97x (up from 3.73x).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical Volatility:** Ongoing conflicts (e.g., West Asia) impacting energy prices and supply chains.
*   **Monsoon:** Potential El Nino impact on rainfall, though management believes risks are overdone.
*   **Regulatory:** New RBI Gold Loan guidelines impacted Q1 origination volumes; potential regulatory changes regarding insurance commissions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why did NIMs compress?* A: Management explained it was due to higher debt-equity and maintaining surplus liquidity (₹4,200 Cr+) amid geopolitical uncertainty, but emphasized that NIMs+Fees remained stable.
*   *Q: How is Personal Loan growth sustainable?* A: Management cited focus on salaried customers, digital partnerships (CRED, GPay, PhonePe, Amazon), and "Project Cyclops" underwriting, noting net non-starters are at their lowest levels.
*   *Q: Why did SME and Gold Loan growth slow?* A: SME growth was intentionally slowed due to caution regarding geopolitical fallout; Gold Loan growth was impacted by new RBI guidelines in April but is recovering.
*   *Q: What are the levers for 80 bps RoA improvement?* A: Management expects 20 bps from ARC portfolio resolution, 30-40 bps from credit cost/administration efficiency, and the remainder from business expansion.
*   *Q: Is the payments business a cash-burn model?* A: Management clarified it is not a "cash burn" model for acquisition but an "agentic commerce" build to reduce opex drag and diversify fee revenue.

### One-line takeaway
L&T Finance delivered a record Q1 FY27 with 29% YoY profit growth, leveraging AI-native tech stacks and disciplined underwriting to drive retail expansion while maintaining strict asset quality guardrails.
