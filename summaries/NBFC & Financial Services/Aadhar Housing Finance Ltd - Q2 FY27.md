# Aadhar Housing Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady execution, disciplined growth, and stable asset quality despite an evolving interest rate environment.

### Key numbers
- AUM as of September 30, 2026: Rs 32,643 crore (up 18% YoY, or 20% on a like-to-like basis)
- Q2 FY27 Disbursements: Rs 2,577 crore (up 22% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 591 crore (vs Rs 504 crore in H2/Q2 FY26 context; PAT up 22% YoY adjusted for ESOP)
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 308 crore (up 20% YoY adjusted for ESOP)
- Gross NPA: 1.34% as of September 2026 (improved by 8 bps YoY)
- Net NPA on AUM: 1.0%
- Collection efficiency: 99% for Q2 FY27
- Capital Adequacy Ratio: 41.7% (Tier 1 at 41.7%, Tier 2 at 0.5%)
- Cost of Funds (exit cost as of Sept 30, 2026): 7.7%
- Incremental borrowing cost for Q2 FY27: 7.5%
- Portfolio yield exit: 13.4%
- Exit spread: 5.73%
- BT out (Q2 FY27): 5.1% (improved by 40 bps YoY)
- Cost to income (adjusted for ESOP): 33.9% in Q2 FY27 (down 230 bps YoY); 34% in H1 FY27 (down 215 bps YoY)
- Q2 FY27 ROA and ROE (adjusted for ESOP): 4.4% and 15.8% (Unadjusted: 4.2% and 15.2%)
- Total Borrowings as of September 30, 2026: Rs 20,400 crore
- Liquidity / Undrawn Sanctions: Undrawn sanctions of Rs 1,466 crore; liquidity stood at Rs 2,040 crore.

### Highlights
- Management expressed condolences on the demise of Independent Director and Non-Executive Chairperson, Shri Raj Vikash Verma; Shri Deo Shankar Tripathi appointed as Interim Non-Executive Chairman.
- Shri Nikhil Goyal (MD, Blackstone) joined the Board as an Additional Director effective August 12, 2026.
- All three rating agencies (India Ratings, CARE, ICRA) upgraded long-term rating to AA+ stable.
- Transitioned from cheque handover to cheque realization basis for disbursement recognition starting Q1 FY27.
- *Record check:* Matches the reported net profit of Rs 308 crore for Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated medium-term guidance: 18% to 20% disbursement growth, 20% AUM growth, and 20% PAT growth for the full year.
- Expects disbursement growth of 20% plus in H2 FY27.
- Expected full-year credit cost guided at 25 bps; headline NPA expected in the range of 1.10% to 1.15% by year-end.
- Assignment guidance for the current financial year: Rs 2,000 crore to Rs 2,050 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Affordable housing demand remains supported by structural need for homeownership and formal credit expansion in Tier 2, Tier 3, and beyond.
- Increased competition noted in existing markets.
- Monitoring El Niño conditions and geopolitical factors, though no broad-based underwriting tightening is currently undertaken.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- ESOP charge included in P&L: Rs 28 crore in H1 and Rs 14 crore in Q2 FY27 (issued at an exercise price of Rs 425 per share in January 2026).
- 75% of assets and liabilities are floating in nature; management has the ability to pass on rate hikes via the RPLR model with a maximum lag of about a quarter.
- Borrowing mix: Banks 53%, NHB 22%, NCDs 15%, ECB 7%, Others 3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added one branch in Jammu during the quarter, taking the network to 632 branches across 23 states and over 555 districts.
- Plan to add 40 to 50 branches for the full financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings at Rs 20,400 crore across 43 borrowing relationships.
- Received a fresh sanction of Rs 1,000 crore from NHB post-quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Stage 2 slightly moved up QoQ from 3.3% to 3.55% (attributed by management to seasonality with no undue concern in specific states).
- Monitoring potential cash flow impacts on borrowers in semi-urban markets due to El Niño conditions (company has exposure in ~30 out of 88 high-priority El Niño districts, accounting for 5% to 6% of AUM).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kunal Shah (Citi) on GS2 increase & cost of funds transmission:** Management stated the 30 bps QoQ increase in GS2 is seasonal and not a cause for worry. On cost of funds, floating nature of 75% assets/liabilities allows passing on rates with a lag via ALCO/RPLR.
2. **Sonal Gandhi (Financial/Sonal) on Non-Home Loan (NHL) growth & asset quality:** Management clarified that NHL underwriting was tightened in Q4 of last year, levers are now reopening, and the mix should stabilize at ~28% by Q4. GNPA is ~1% for HL and ~2% for NHL.
3. **Arun (JM Financial) on Q3 disbursements & insurance regulations:** October disbursements averaged ~Rs 900 crore (up from ~Rs 830-800 run rate). On IRDAI draft guidelines, management noted they will calibrate via fee income/Opex levers once final guidelines are out.
4. **Chirayu Maloo on credit cost run rate:** Management clarified that annual credit cost guidance remains at 25 bps (Q2 was 31 bps) and expects to align by year-end.
5. **Laksh on branch additions and BT out:** Management confirmed guidance of 40-50 branch additions for the year (40 in pipeline) and noted BT out at 5.1% is comfortable and aided by analytics/AI retention efforts.

### One-line takeaway
Aadhar Housing Finance delivered a steady Q2 FY27 with 20% AUM growth and AA+ ratings across all three agencies, while maintaining asset quality and reiterating 20% AUM and PAT growth guidance for the year.
