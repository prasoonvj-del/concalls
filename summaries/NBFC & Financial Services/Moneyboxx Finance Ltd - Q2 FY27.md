# Moneyboxx Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong monthly disbursement recovery, improved asset quality, and scaling across multiple growth engines.

### Key numbers
- **Disbursements (Q2 FY27):** Rs 87 crore (14% QoQ increase from Rs 76 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Monthly Disbursements:** Rs 24.5 crore (August 2026), Rs 32.4 crore (September 2026), and crossed Rs 50 crore (October 2026).
- **Branches:** 148 branches across 12 states (as of September 2026).
- **Live Customers:** Approximately 13,000.
- **AUM:** Rs 802 crore (as of September 2026); total assets crossed Rs 1,057 crore.
- **Secured AUM:** Approximately 73% of total AUM.
- **Average Ticket Size (Secured):** Rs 6.7 lakh in Q2 compared to Rs 5.7 lakh in Q1.
- **Collection Efficiency:** 92.5% overall; ex-bucket above 98%.
- **Asset Quality:** 90+ PAR declined to 2.56% as of September 2026 compared to 6.25% as of March 2026; reported on-book gross NPA at 0.01%.
- **Funding & Borrowings:** Raised Rs 110 crore via NCDs in Q2 (NCD share at 46% of total borrowings); relationships with 36 lenders (including 12 banks).
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 26.35% (as of September 2026).
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 61 crore (11% YoY growth, 17% QoQ growth).
- **Net Interest Income & Fee Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 39 crore (16% YoY growth, 28% QoQ growth).
- **Pre-Provisioning Operating Profit (PPOP) (Q2 FY27):** Rs 9.97 crore (79% YoY growth).
- **Operating Expenses (Q2 FY27):** Rs 29.67 crore.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27):** Rs 0.9 crore (229% YoY growth from Rs 0.28 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 113.14 crore.
- **Net Interest & Fee Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 70 crore.
- **PPOP (H1 FY27):** Rs 12.41 crore.
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 1.11 crore (117% YoY growth from Rs 0.52 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Yields & Margins (H1 FY27):** Average lending IRR 24.8%; average borrowing IRR 12.7%; net interest margin (NIM) 11.9%; Opex to average AUM ratio 14.3%.
- **Solar AUM:** Reached Rs 17 crore in H1 (and Rs 22.5 crore after October).

### Highlights
- Classified as a middle-layer NBFC during the quarter.
- Evolving into a diversified NBFC platform via 4 growth engines: MSME lending, livestock finance, rooftop solar finance, and digital small lending.
- Commenced partnerships with Akshayakalpa (dairy/livestock) and Trusave Fintech / Bachatt platform ("SmartBiz" digital lending), both backed by FLDG.
- Continued record pattern: Results align with the November investor deck reporting post-Q2 disbursement momentum and asset quality improvements.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets H2 FY27 disbursements between Rs 500 crore to Rs 600 crore.
- Management expects AUM to reach Rs 1,100 crore to Rs 1,200 crore by March 2027.
- Medium-term objective: bring opex-to-AUM ratio below 10% over the next 2 years.
- Target to increase renewable energy loans contribution to approximately 10% of AUM over the next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's MSME sector plays an important role, with individual business borrowers presenting formal credit expansion opportunities.
- Growing importance of individual business borrowers and underserved micro-enterprises.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Borrowing costs increased by 25 to 30 bps in the MSME space, while bank funding remains competitive.
- Opex remains stable in absolute terms; operating leverage expected to improve as AUM scales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operated through 148 branches across 12 states as of September 2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong liquidity position of Rs 170 crore at the end of September 2026.
- Total lenders stand at 36, including Bandhan Bank (new entry) and top-up from IOB.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution risks in scaling new partnerships.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Riya Shah (Shah Family Office) on funding comfort and borrowing cost outlook:** Management stated liquidity is strong at Rs 170 crore, funding is not a constraint for the next 2-3 quarters, and borrowing costs saw a 25-30 bps rise in MSME space while bank funding is stable. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Chinmay Parab (Individual Investor) on disbursement sustainability and monthly run-rate:** Management noted a goal to reach a monthly disbursement rate of Rs 100 crore as soon as possible, boosted by top-ups to existing customers and new digital/solar channels. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Chinmay Parab on rooftop solar financing growth and credit risk:** Management highlighted lower opex, a 10% second loss guarantee cover (3% first loss), 4-year funding matching ALM, and current yields above 23%. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Rutvi on secured vs unsecured MSME mix:** Management stated 73% of the book is secured by property, with additional portfolios protected via FLDG and guarantee programs. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Rutvi on technology initiative impacts:** Management (Mayur Modi) stated the Moneyboxx ONE LOS platform reduced secured TAT from 10-11 days to 6.5-7 days and unsecured processing to under 1.5-2 days, while lowering customer acquisition costs. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Moneyboxx Finance reported a strong operational recovery in Q2 and October 2026—driven by scaling partnerships, technology integration, and top-ups to existing customers—aiming for an AUM of Rs 1,100-1,200 crore by March 2027.
