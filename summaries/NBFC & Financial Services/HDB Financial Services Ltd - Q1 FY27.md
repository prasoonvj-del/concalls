# HDB Financial Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-20 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly profit and improved asset quality despite macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **PAT:** Rs 785 crore (+38.3% YoY, +4.6% QoQ).
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 17,629 crore (+16.2% YoY).
*   **Gross Loan Book:** Rs 1,21,846 crore (+11.3% YoY, +2.8% QoQ).
*   **Gross Stage 3:** 2.34% (vs 2.56% in Q1 FY26, 2.44% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 8.35% (vs 7.74% in Q1 FY26, 8.23% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 39.9% (vs 42.7% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Customer Franchise:** 23.9 million (+18.6% YoY).
*   **Total CRAR:** 21.29% as of June 30, 2026.

### Highlights
*   **Record Profitability:** Achieved highest ever quarterly profit to date.
*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   **Consumer Finance:** Book grew 21% YoY; Consumer Durables grew >50% YoY.
    *   **Enterprise Lending:** Gold loan book doubled YoY; LAP+EBL grew 13.2% YoY.
    *   **Asset Finance:** Commercial Vehicles grew 10% YoY, Construction Equipment 8% YoY.
*   **Technology:** Launched "Shikhar," an AI-driven initiative to manage customer life-cycle journeys.
*   **ESG:** CRISIL assigned a "Strong" ESG rating with a score of 68.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects positive momentum to continue in Enterprise Lending and anticipates Asset Finance growth to pick up in coming quarters.
*   **Business Loans:** Management expects growth to turn positive from Q3 onwards.
*   **Credit Cost:** Management maintains a steady-state credit cost guidance of ~2.3%.
*   **NIMs:** Management expects to maintain NIMs at 8%+.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro:** Domestic economic activity remains resilient despite West Asia conflict concerns.
*   **Projections:** Real GDP growth expectations moderated to 6.6% for FY27; Inflation projections increased to 5.1%.
*   **Seasonality:** Consumer finance demand supported by seasonal demand for compressor products.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Funds:** Expected to remain range-bound for Q2.
*   **Liquidity:** Borrowing mix is well-diversified with positive cumulative mismatch up to 5 years; CP book is <2%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Network:** Presence in ~1,200 cities and 1.6 lakh+ retail distribution touchpoints.
*   **Gold Loans:** Enabled in ~500 existing branches.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Portfolio Mix:** Secured loans comprise 73.9% of the gross loan book.
*   **Current Ratio:** ~1.3.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **External Factors:** West Asia conflict and El Nino-related risks remain key monitorables.
*   **Monsoon:** Potential impact of monsoon on rural/ground-level operations.
*   **Fuel Prices:** Fuel price hikes noted, though management stated small, incremental doses allowed for absorption by operators.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Asset Finance Growth:** Analysts questioned why Asset Finance growth is lagging. Management explained they have "rejigged" the portfolio, focusing on risk-adjusted returns over high-value/low-return products, and expect growth to pick up in coming quarters.
*   **Credit Cost Guidance:** Analysts asked if credit costs would drop below 2.3% given strong performance. Management maintained 2.3% as the steady-state target but noted it could be lower if economic conditions improve.
*   **Microfinance:** Analysts asked if MFI would be shut down due to lack of scale. Management stated it provides a "great moat" for understanding rural markets and is not a P&L stressor, so they have no plans to shut it.
*   **Freight Rates/CV Exposure:** Analysts asked about the impact of fuel hikes on CV operators. Management clarified they have low exposure to large fleets (Top 20 customers = 0.3% of AUM) and focus on small operators where contracts are often per-day, making them less sensitive to long-haul fuel costs.

### One-line takeaway
HDB Financial delivered record quarterly profits and improved asset quality, with management signaling a shift toward AI-driven customer lifecycle management and a focus on risk-adjusted returns in the Asset Finance segment.
