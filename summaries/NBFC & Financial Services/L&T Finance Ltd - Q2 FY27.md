# L&T Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record consolidated PAT of ₹975 Cr and improved RoA to 2.60%, demonstrating resilience through AI-led underwriting and disciplined growth despite monsoon uncertainties.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained RoA target (2.8%-3.0% exit Q4 FY27) and credit cost corridor (2%-2.2% by Q4 FY27).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious/monitoring" to "carefully increasing" rural disbursement momentum as monsoon clarity emerged.
- **New topics or risks:** IRDAI consultation paper on insurance commission rationalization (potential revenue impact); Project Hercules (cross-sell engine) and Payments platform (agentic commerce) launch.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on branch rollout (172 new branches in H1FY27); credit cost moderated to 2.46% (vs 2.54% in Q1).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated PAT:** ₹975 Cr (+33% YoY).
- **Consolidated Book:** ₹1,36,635 Cr (+28% YoY).
- **Retail Disbursements:** ₹24,027 Cr (+27% YoY).
- **RoA:** 2.60% (vs 2.48% in Q1).
- **RoE:** 13.56% (vs 11.31% in Q2 FY26).
- **NIMs + Fees:** 10.48% (vs 10.47% in Q1).
- **Credit Cost:** 2.46% (vs 2.54% in Q1).

### Highlights
- **Project Hercules:** Phase 1 of AI-based cross-sell engine implemented; gold loan cross-sell conversion at 14%+.
- **Payments Platform:** UPI stack in closed user group testing; applied for Prepaid Issuer (PPI) license.
- **Branch Expansion:** 151 'Sampoorna' branches (integrated service hubs) scaled in ~18 months; 502 active gold loan branches.
- **Tech DNA:** 1,200+ employees trained in "AI Citizen" program; ~5,730 AI agents created for business tasks.

### Guidance & outlook
- **RoA:** Target 2.8%–3.0% exit Q4 FY27; 3.0%–3.2% by FY31.
- **Credit Cost:** Confident of achieving 2%–2.2% corridor by Q4 FY27.
- **NIMs + Fees:** Expect to remain in 10%–10.5% range.
- **Gold Loans:** Aiming for 14%–15% of overall AUM by FY31 (₹40,000 Cr–₹45,000 Cr).

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Macro:** India's domestic demand remains resilient; rural consumption supported by non-farm activities and DBT transfers.
- **Monsoon:** 2026 monsoon concluded at ~87% of Long Period Average; Kharif sowing at 101% of normal acreage.
- **Rural:** No visible risk buildup in rural portfolios; collection efficiency in Rural Business Finance at 99.7%–99.8%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Opex:** Range-bound at 4.08% despite branch/tech investments.
- **Cost of Borrowing:** Weighted average cost reduced by 25 bps QoQ through liability management and PSL-based borrowings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Branch Rollout:** 172 new branches launched in H1FY27; targeting 800 gold loan branches by end of FY27, with a long-term goal of 2,000.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Retail Book:** ₹1,34,600 Cr (+29% YoY).
- **Capital Adequacy:** Boosted by Tier II capital and capital release from CGTMSE/CGFMU risk weight benefits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Regulatory:** IRDAI consultation paper on insurance commission rationalization (potential revenue impact).
- **Macro:** Geopolitical volatility (West Asia) and crude oil price fluctuations.
- **Monsoon:** Localized rainfall deficits in parts of Karnataka/Maharashtra (though management noted minimal impact on portfolio).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Q: Impact of IRDAI insurance commission paper?* A: Management believes it's premature to quantify; expects "middle ground" through industry consultation and plans to offset via new revenue streams (payments/cross-sell).
- *Q: Why did Two-Wheeler collection efficiency drop in Sept?* A: Management attributed it to an "aggressive experiment" with AI-based collection curing that didn't yield results; October levels back to normal (99%+).
- *Q: Sustainability of cost of funds reduction?* A: Management noted they locked in medium-to-long-term rates; expects incremental borrowing costs to rise slightly but remains in an advantageous position due to lower base.
- *Q: Personal Loan disbursement growth?* A: Flattish QoQ due to "painstaking" implementation of Cyclops across various partners; expects growth to resume in Q3/Q4.

### One-line takeaway
L&T Finance achieved record profitability and improved RoA in Q2 FY27, successfully executing its "Risk-First, Tech-First" strategy while proactively managing monsoon-related risks and preparing for regulatory shifts in insurance distribution.
