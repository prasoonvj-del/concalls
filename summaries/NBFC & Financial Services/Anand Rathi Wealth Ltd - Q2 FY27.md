# Anand Rathi Wealth Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-16 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered 20th consecutive quarter of >20% YoY PAT growth, achieving record net flows despite market volatility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Revenue Rs 1,415 crore, PAT Rs 460 crore).
- **Tone and demand view:** Management remains confident in the business model, emphasizing "market agnostic" performance and the resilience of structured products.
- **New topics:** Confirmed Mutual Fund license application (filing expected by end of October 2026); GIFT City operations expected to be live in 2-3 months with two initial funds.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Continued focus on "inward" investment strategy for GIFT City; confirmed ESOP/non-cash expenses are high this year but expected to decline "dramatically" next year.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 693 crore (+17% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT (ex-items):** Rs 238 crore (+23% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 357 crore (+16% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 122 crore (+22% YoY).
- **Net flows (Q2):** Rs 4,186 crore (+39% YoY).
- **Equity MF net flow (Q2):** Rs 2,867 crore (+39% YoY).
- **Client families:** 14,309 (added 1,500 net in last 12 months).
- **Client attrition (AUM):** 0.17% (Q2), 0.26% (H1).
- **Non-cash charges (Q2):** Rs 46 crore.
- **MLD Issuance (Q2):** Primary Rs 2,073 crore; Secondary Rs 750 crore.

### Highlights
- Achieved highest ever quarterly net flow and equity mutual fund net flow in 20-year history.
- Recorded highest ever monthly client acquisition in September 2026.
- Board declared interim dividend of Rs 4 per share.
- Digital wealth AUM reached Rs 2,531 crore (+14% YoY).
- Operations commenced in London, UK.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to achieve full-year guidance of Rs 1,415 crore revenue and Rs 460 crore PAT.
- AUM target for the year is Rs 1.2 lakh crore.
- Management targets net flows of Rs 6,000-8,000 crore for the residual year.
- Management does not see near-term risk to 20-25% PAT growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management views Nifty as an imperfect barometer due to short build-ups; prefers agility in structured products.
- GIFT City strategy remains focused on "inward" remittances/investments; no plans to take capital out of India.
- Management believes focus is "underrated" and intends to master the Indian market rather than diversify globally.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management targets PBT margin >40% and PAT margin >30%.
- Management prefers reinvesting excess profits into business verticals rather than optimizing for higher margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Applied for Mutual Fund license; filing expected by end of October 2026.
- GIFT City operations to be live in 2-3 months; planning to launch two funds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Market volatility remains a factor, though management claims a low-beta portfolio mitigates impact.
- Management noted "regret" RM attrition is low (1-2 RMs in H1).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Wallet share:** Management estimates top 100 clients have 60-70% wallet share with the company.
- **MLD business:** Management is not worried about the business; emphasizes agility and a history of no losses at maturity.
- **AMC License:** Management clarified it will not be a "me-too" product; will be a fully owned subsidiary.
- **Client acquisition:** 62% of net flows came from existing clients, 38% from new clients in the quarter.
- **GIFT City:** Management confirmed the focus is exclusively on inward investments.

### One-line takeaway
Anand Rathi Wealth continues to deliver consistent, market-agnostic growth, supported by record net flows and a strategic push into the AMC and GIFT City businesses.
