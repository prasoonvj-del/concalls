# Laxmi India Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted solid AUM growth, improving funding costs, higher operating efficiency, and sequential improvement in asset quality indicators despite a one-off direct assignment provisioning impact.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 AUM growth target of around 25%, NII growth around 35% to 40%, and PAT growth around 35% to 40%.
- Tone and demand view: Remained constructive; noted strong collection efficiencies above 98% and expanding branch productivity.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Introduction of upcoming credit lines for MSME borrowers; additional 30% provisioning for the Up Money direct assignment transaction (taking cumulative provision to 90%).
- Last quarter's promises: Continued branch expansion on track (reached 209 branches from 196 in Q1).

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 1,844 crore (up ~33% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Disbursements:** Rs 506 crore (up ~63% YoY); Q2 FY27 Disbursements: Rs 274 crore
- **H1 FY27 Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 97 crore (up ~35% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT (including OCI):** Rs 31.7 crore (up ~66% YoY); Adjusted H1 PAT (excluding Up Money impact): Rs 36.34 crore (up 91% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 15.4 crore
- **H1 FY27 Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 11.28% (vs 10.88% in H1 FY26)
- **Gross NPA / Net NPA:** 1.75% / 0.49% as of September 30, 2026
- **Return on Assets (ROA):** 3.18% in H1 FY27 (3.65% adjusted)
- **Return on Equity (ROE):** 13.16% in H1 FY27 (15.01% adjusted)
- **Cost of Borrowing:** 10.59% (down 51 bps YoY)
- **Branch Network:** 209 branches across 6 states
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR):** 22.95% (Tier 1 at 22.54%)
- **Liquidity / Undrawn lines:** Rs 292 crore cash/bank balance, Rs 117 crore undrawn facilities

### Highlights
- Secured MSME portfolio stands at approx Rs 1,493 crore (81% of total AUM).
- External credit rating upgraded from A- to A with a stable outlook.
- Cumulative provisioning of 90% (~Rs 19.6 crore defaulted out of Rs 26 crore transaction) made for the Up Money direct assignment transaction; only 10% (Rs 1.90 crore) left unprovided.
- Planning to launch a new product offering revolving credit lines to existing MSME borrowers.
- Added ICICI Bank and City Union Bank as new lending partners during H1.

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Growth:** Expect to close FY27 with AUM of more than Rs 2,000 crore (~25% growth over FY26).
- **Financial Growth:** NII growth of around 35% to 40% and PAT growth of around 35% to 40% for FY27.
- **Medium-term ROA:** Working towards 3.5% to 3.75%.
- **Credit Cost Guidance:** Expected to float between 1% to 1.25% for coming quarters and the full year.
- **Equity Dilution:** Management expects to sustain 25% to 30% YoY AUM growth without fresh equity until Q1 of FY28, after which a subsequent round of equity raising is anticipated.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Semi-urban and rural entrepreneurs/self-employed individuals remain underserved by conventional lenders, providing a strong pipeline for secured MSME lending.
- Microfinance industry showing improvement in credit ratings (ICRA revised to stable), though company maintains an edge due to its secured model.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average portfolio yield stood at 21.59% in H1 FY27.
- Incremental cost of borrowing over the past 12 months stood at approximately 10.42%.
- Branch break-even is achieved in approximately 7 to 9 months at an AUM of Rs 1.5 crore to Rs 2.0 crore.
- H1 FY27 operating expenses stood at Rs 62 crore (vs Rs 49 crore in H1 FY26) due to branch network and employee expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanded branch count to 209 branches across 6 states (Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra) compared to 164 a year ago.
- Expansion remains selective, focusing on branch-level productivity and replication of underwriting/collection models.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt-equity ratio stood at 3.31 times as of September 2026 (vs 2.87 times in March 2026).
- Banks account for ~65% of the overall borrowing mix; raised ~Rs 611 crore in H1 FY27 including Rs 100 crore through NCDs.
- 80% to 87% of borrowings are fixed or MCLR-linked (6 to 12 months), insulating against immediate repo rate hikes; repo-linked borrowings (~13%, approx Rs 200-214 crore) reset every 3 months.
- ALM profile reflects cumulative surpluses across all maturity buckets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued need to monitor the vehicle financing portfolio for stress.
- Credit costs impacted by the one-off Up Money direct assignment default.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepesh Sancheti (Retail investor/Analyst) asked about branch break-even metrics and evergreening checks.** Management stated break-even occurs at Rs 1.75 crore AUM on average across categories, and evergreening is prevented via automated LOS dedupe systems checking repeat mobile numbers and KYC.
2. **Paras (Analyst) asked about sequential PAT decline and Up Money recovery status.** Management explained Q2 PAT was lower due to an additional 20% provision on Up Money (90% cumulative), leaving only 10% (Rs 1.90 crore) unprovided out of the defaulted Rs 19.6 crore exposure.
3. **Paras asked about capital adequacy and leverage limits.** Management clarified that factoring liquidity into bonds lowered reported CRAR to 22.95%, but equity raise would only be evaluated around Q1 FY28 when leverage hits 4.5 to 4.75x.
4. **Yash Gautam asked about the rating profile of liquidity buffer bonds and ALM.** Management replied that liquidity buffer bonds are parked in AA categories (AA-minus to AA).
5. **Janardhan asked about promoter shareholding and risk appetite.** Management stated promoters hold 60-61%, are willing to infuse more capital or dilute as needed, and enforce strict pin-code/territory no-go rules to manage risk.

### One-line takeaway
Laxmi India Finance delivered robust H1 FY27 performance with 33% AUM growth and expanding margins, absorbing a one-off direct assignment default while maintaining strong collection efficiencies above 98%.
