# OnEMI Technology Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in AUM and profitability, with management emphasizing disciplined underwriting and successful execution of their "high-quality customer" strategy.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised FY27 AUM growth guidance from "40% plus" to "60% plus." Raised impairment cost reduction target from "10-15%" to "emphatically more than 15% year-on-year."
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious/data-driven" to "high conviction" regarding growth and risk management. Management highlighted that they are actively avoiding leveraged borrowers, unlike the broader industry.
- **New topics or risks:** Explicit mention of "PHP 2Shell" cybersecurity patching. Detailed discussion on "location upgrade/downgrade" as a daily operational tool.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on LAP breakeven (achieved in Q2 vs. Q3 guidance). Successfully completed preferential allotment of Rs 832 crore.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 9,317 crore (up 68.4% YoY, 16.4% QoQ).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 125 crore (up 100% YoY, 31.6% QoQ).
- **Disbursements:** Rs 4,612 crore (up 56% YoY).
- **Total Income:** Rs 740 crore (up 43% YoY).
- **Credit Cost:** 6.2% of average AUM (down from 6.8% in Q1 FY27 and 9.3% a year ago).
- **Gross NPA:** 2.18% (down 7 bps QoQ, 75 bps YoY).
- **Net NPA:** 0.34% (down 2 bps QoQ).
- **Return on Average AUM:** 5.78% (up 73 bps).
- **Return on Average Equity:** 21.65%.
- **Registered Users:** 79.5 million (up 32.6% YoY).
- **Active Customers:** 3.61 million (up 58% YoY).
- **Average Ticket Size:** Rs 43,380.
- **Average Tenure:** 17.8 months.

### Highlights
- **LAP Business:** AUM reached Rs 737 crore (7.9% of total book). Business turned profitable in Q2, one quarter ahead of guidance.
- **Capital:** Net worth at Rs 2,377 crore; capital adequacy at 34.76%. Completed preferential allotment of Rs 832 crore.
- **Funding:** Average cost of borrowing fell to 13.76% (from 14.45% in Q1). Off-book AUM share is 55%.
- **Technology:** AI models (42nd iteration) achieved 75% AUC. Voice agents in early buckets now recover at >85% of human caller rates.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth:** FY27 AUM growth guidance raised to 60%+.
- **Impairment:** Targeting >15% reduction in credit cost YoY.
- **Profitability:** Reiterated ROE guidance of >20% and Gross NPA <2.25%.
- **Cost of Funds:** Expects ~150 bps reduction in incremental borrowing cost over the next 3-4 quarters as legacy debt rolls off.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Industry Trend:** Retail credit balances grew 19% while borrower base grew <9%, indicating rising indebtedness.
- **Kissht Strategy:** Deliberately lending less to leveraged borrowers (share of loans to customers with 3+ live loans fell >35% YoY).
- **Gold Loans:** Exposure reduced to sub-8% of total book; management claims to have acted 13 months ago to curb this exposure.
- **Macro:** Remains confident in India's economic resilience despite RBI repo rate hike.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Operating Leverage:** Management expects 25-40 bps improvement in OPEX-to-AUM ratio per quarter.
- **CAC:** Blended CAC is ~Rs 850; CAC for high-quality segment is ~Rs 1,200.
- **Tax Rate:** Effective tax rate fell to 19% due to ESOP-related tax benefits; expects this benefit to continue for 3-5 quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Branches:** LAP business operates across 107 branches in 9 states.
- **Strategy:** Shifting LAP personnel from payroll to third-party to improve performance management and flexibility.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Funding Mix:** 45:55 split between on-book (Rs 4,199 crore) and off-book (Rs 5,118 crore).
- **Provisioning:** Stage-3 coverage at 84.6%; Stage-2 coverage at 77.6%.
- **Debt/Equity:** On-book debt to equity is 0.94x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Leverage:** Flagged rising industry-wide indebtedness as a concern.
- **Specific Segments:** Monitoring self-employed revolvers (exposure Rs 25,000-40,000) and drought-affected areas (sub-6% of book).
- **Regulatory:** No material impact expected from proposed IRDAI changes on insurance commissions (insurance is <1.4% of total income).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On Margin vs. ROE:** Management explained they pass on lower funding cost benefits to customers to attract higher-quality borrowers, which protects ROE despite lower margins.
- **On Ticket Size Ceiling:** Management clarified a soft ceiling of Rs 3 lakh, with <5% of loans currently between Rs 2-3 lakh.
- **On Operating Leverage:** Management noted that while they seek efficiency, they will not hesitate to over-invest in tech-led collections if stress signals emerge.
- **On Future Fundraising:** Management stated no further fundraising is needed until the book reaches Rs 30,000 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Kissht is aggressively scaling its AUM (raised guidance to 60%+) while simultaneously improving asset quality and profitability, driven by a deliberate pivot to high-quality customers and tech-led underwriting.
