# Shriram Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance and disbursement growth despite macro headwinds from weak monsoon and geopolitical tensions.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 54,079.02 crore (up 25.71% YoY).
*   **AUM:** Rs 3,29,021.72 crore (up 16.96% YoY, 4.85% QoQ).
*   **NII:** Rs 8,661.04 crore (up 38.2% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 3,677.03 crore (up 59.37% YoY).
*   **NIM:** 9.52% (vs 8.19% YoY, 9.04% QoQ).
*   **Gross Stage 3:** 4.65% (vs 4.57% YoY, 4.64% QoQ).
*   **Net Stage 3:** 2.31% (vs 2.49% YoY, 2.33% QoQ).
*   **Credit Cost:** 2.03% (vs 1.68% YoY, 1.66% QoQ).
*   **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 22.52% (vs 27.76% YoY).
*   **EPS:** Rs 15.63.
*   **Dividend:** Rs 6.5 per share.

### Highlights
*   Management reported strong growth in disbursements and AUM, driven by expansion into new geographies.
*   NIM expansion was attributed to recent capital infusion.
*   MSME book growth is accelerating due to pan-India geographical expansion.
*   Construction equipment segment demand is recovering following increased government infrastructure spending.
*   Gold loan disbursements for the quarter were Rs 5,443 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management revised AUM growth guidance to 15-17% for FY27 (down from initial 18% guidance) due to caution regarding monsoon impact and geopolitical risks.
*   Long-term credit cost guidance remains 2%, though management expects a temporary elevation of 30-40 bps for the next 1-2 quarters due to seasonality.
*   NIM is expected to remain at current levels for 1-2 quarters before moderating to ~9% (medium term) and ~8.5% (long term).
*   Management expects the second half of the year to be busier and remains confident in performance.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   India's Q1 GDP growth was 7.8%.
*   Weak monsoon (87% of long period average) is impacting rural consumption and demand in drought-prone areas.
*   Commercial vehicle sales grew 28.7% YoY in Q2.
*   EV segments showed robust growth: Passenger vehicles (106%), Three-wheelers (30.61%), and Two-wheelers (90.93%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Cost of liabilities on balance sheet: 8.41% (down 15 bps QoQ).
*   Incremental cost of funds: 7.3% (vs 7.7% in previous quarter).
*   Management expects incremental cost of funds could rise to ~8.35% if market interest rates increase.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Total liability: Rs 2,36,271 crore.
*   Liability mix: Public deposits (31%), Bank/Institutional borrowing (21%), ECB/Bonds (16.5%), NCDs (15.71%), Securitization (14%).
*   Liquidity coverage ratio: 303.
*   Capital adequacy ratio: 33.24%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Weak monsoon/drought-prone areas causing temporary stress in Stage 2/Stage 3 assets across MSME, Gold, and Construction Equipment segments.
*   Geopolitical tensions (West Asia) impacting inflation and raw material costs.
*   Potential impact of IRDA draft guidelines on insurance commission income (estimated ~Rs 100 crore impact, though management noted it is immaterial to total income).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why the spike in Stage 2/3 assets?* A: Management attributed it to seasonal/geographical factors (drought) and customer unfamiliarity with EMI-based gold loan products.
*   *Q: Will growth moderate?* A: Management guided to 15-17% growth, citing caution in drought-prone areas.
*   *Q: Why the NIM expansion?* A: Temporary boost from capital infusion; expected to normalize to 8.5-9% over time.
*   *Q: Any impact from IRDA guidelines?* A: Management stated the impact is immaterial to total income.
*   *Q: Why the jump in MSME disbursements?* A: Driven by expansion into larger geographies across India.

### One-line takeaway
Shriram Finance delivered strong Q2 growth despite rural headwinds, with management maintaining a cautious but optimistic outlook for the second half.
