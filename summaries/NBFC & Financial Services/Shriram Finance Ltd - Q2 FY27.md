# Shriram Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reported solid profit growth and NIM expansion following capital infusion, but tempered AUM growth guidance and flagged temporary asset quality pressure due to weak, erratic monsoons and El Niño impacts in key regions.

### Key numbers
- **Disbursements:** Rs 54,079.02 crore, up 25.71% YoY (vs Rs 43,019.17 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **AUM:** Rs 3,29,021.72 crore, up 16.96% YoY and 4.85% QoQ.
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 8,661.04 crore, up 38.2% YoY (vs Rs 6,266.84 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 3,677.03 crore, up 59.37% YoY and 6.75% QoQ.
- **NIM:** 9.52% (vs 8.19% in Q2 FY26 and 9.04% in Q1 FY27).
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Rs 15.63 (vs Rs 12.27 in Q2 FY26 and Rs 14.83 in Q1 FY27).
- **Asset Quality:** Gross Stage 3 at 4.65% (vs 4.57% in Q2 FY26, 4.64% in Q1 FY27); Net Stage 3 at 2.31% (vs 2.49% in Q2 FY26, 2.33% in Q1 FY27).
- **Credit Cost:** 2.03% on total assets (vs 1.68% in Q2 FY26, 1.66% in Q1 FY27); 2.3% on loan book.
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 22.52% (vs 27.76% in Q2 FY26 and 25.48% in Q1 FY27).
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 6.5 per share (325%).

### Highlights
- Capital adequacy ratio stood at 33.24% (vs 34% in Q1 FY27).
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) improved to 303% (vs 262% in Q1 FY27).
- Leverage ratio was 2.16x (vs 2.14x in Q1 FY27 and 3.82x in March).
- Disbursed Rs 10,000 crore in the MSME segment during the quarter via pan-India expansion.
- Gold loan disbursements stood at Rs 5,443 crore with an LTV of 72%–73%; gold AUM stands around Rs 9,000 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Growth:** Cautiously guided to 15%–17% for FY27 (lowered from the earlier initial guidance of 18%).
- **Credit Cost:** Full-year target remains anchored around 2.0%, but may see a temporary spike of 30–40 bps for 1–2 quarters in monsoon-affected areas.
- **NIM:** Expected to hold near current elevated levels for 1–2 quarters before moderating to ~9% in the medium term, and settling around 8.5% in the long term.
- **Cost-to-Income:** Expected to normalize to around 25% in the medium-to-long term.
- **Gold Financing:** Target intact to double the book in 3 years from ~2.5% to ~5% of AUM.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Commercial vehicle (CV) industry sales rose 28.7% YoY in Q2 FY27 (M&HCV up 37%, LCV up 23.8%). Passenger vehicles grew 30.7%, 2-wheelers up 15.2%, 3-wheelers up 23.6%, tractors up 18.26%, and construction equipment up 23%.
- Electric Vehicles (EVs) saw strong momentum: PV EVs up 106%, 3-wheeler EVs up 30.61%, and 2-wheeler EVs up 90.93%.
- Rural consumption faces stress due to erratic 2023 southwest monsoon rainfall (87% of LPA), particularly in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Telangana, where 51.2% of area saw drought-like dry conditions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- On-balance-sheet liability cost declined 15 bps QoQ to 8.41% (down 18 bps from March).
- Incremental cost of funds for the quarter was 7.3% (vs 7.7% in Q1 FY27). Incremental funding cost is expected to remain capped around 8.0%–8.35% if policy rates harden.
- NIM expansion to 9.52% was driven primarily by fresh equity capital infusion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total liabilities stood at Rs 2,36,271 crore.
- Liability mix: Public deposits 31%, capital market borrowings (NCDs) 15.71%, ECB loans/bonds 16.5%, domestic bank and institutional borrowings 21%, and securitization 14%.
- Raised Rs 27,000 crore via borrowings in Q2; excess cash/investments moderated from ~Rs 38,000 crore to ~Rs 31,000–32,000 crore (covering 3 months of liability repayments). Going forward, borrowings will match AUM growth.
- MSME portfolio composition: 72% secured, 28% unsecured.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Weak Monsoon / El Niño:** Moisture stress in kharif crops and drought conditions in parts of Southern and Western India driving asset quality slippages in rural and MSME loans.
- **Geopolitical Disruption:** West Asia crisis creating crude and raw material inflation pressures.
- **Regulatory Changes (IRDAI):** Draft norms on insurance commissions may pose a potential ~Rs 100 crore impact on fee income (FY26 commission was ~Rs 350 crore), which management deems immaterial.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Asset Quality & Stage 2/3 Step-up:** Umesh Revankar explained that increases in Stage 2/3 assets are localized to drought-prone geographies (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana) and are seasonal; credit costs may temporarily exceed 2% by 30–40 bps before normalizing.
- **Growth Moderation:** Management confirmed trimming FY27 growth expectations to 15%–17% from 18% earlier due to caution surrounding monsoon deficiency and the West Asia situation.
- **Gold Loan Delinquencies:** Revankar clarified that Stage 2 and 3 increases in gold loans stem from customer transition issues toward monthly EMI/interest-servicing products rather than bullet repayments; stressed there is zero credit risk given adequate collateral value (72%–73% LTV).
- **Alleged Kerala Branch Fraud:** S. Sunder declined to address reports of unauthorized loan accounts in Kerala during the call, directing the analyst to discuss the matter offline with Investor Relations.
- **NIM Trajectory:** Management clarified that the 9.52% NIM is inflated by zero-cost equity infusion; as this capital gets deployed and lending mix tilts toward new vehicles (yielding 200–400 bps lower than used vehicles), NIM will trend to 9% and eventually 8.5%.

### One-line takeaway
Shriram Finance delivered strong earnings backed by capital-driven NIM expansion, but has prudently scaled back growth guidance to 15%–17% amid temporary rural stress from deficient rains.
