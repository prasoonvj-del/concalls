# 5paisa Capital Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited a "soft quarter" for the overall market with slower trading activity and lower profits due to continued heavy investment in technology and brand.

### What changed vs last quarter
*   **Guidance:** No specific numerical guidance provided; management reiterated a long-term focus on product-led growth.
*   **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "expecting growth to accelerate" (Q1) to acknowledging a "soft quarter" with slower trading activity and dropped derivatives volumes.
*   **New topics:** Acquisition of Trendlyne (pending closure) and its potential integration; launch of "Ask 5paisa" AI assistant; new IPO-related product launch in the near future.
*   **Last quarter's promises:** The company continued to invest in tech/product (as promised), but the anticipated acceleration in growth did not materialize in Q2 due to market conditions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 86.5 crore (Rs 865 million).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 175 crore (Rs 1,750 million), up 13% YoY.
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 7.1 crore.
*   **Customer Base:** 53.4 lakh (added ~81,600 in Q2).
*   **App Users:** >24 million.
*   **Average Daily Turnover (ADTO):** Rs 2.59 trillion.
*   **Net Worth:** Rs 1,136 crore (Rs 11,360 million).
*   **Average Funding Book:** ~Rs 480 crore.
*   **Mutual Fund AUM:** Rs 2,095 crore.
*   **Tech Capitalization:** Rs 6.2 crore (Rs 62 million) capitalized this quarter.

### Highlights
*   **Product Overhaul:** Rebuilt the trading app from the ground up with new OMS (Order Management System) and RMS (Risk Management System) for low-latency performance.
*   **AI Integration:** Launched "Ask 5paisa" for research, portfolio analysis, and trading insights; AI is being used internally to change engineering and design workflows.
*   **New Features:** Single-swipe orders, option chain trading, and consolidated collateral management (shares, MFs, ETFs) with instant withdrawals up to Rs 2 lakh.
*   **Algo Trading:** Launched "AlgoSpace" for automated strategies across cash, F&O without coding requirements.
*   **Trendlyne Acquisition:** Board approved acquisition (end of July); pending formal closure. Plan is to integrate features (baskets, screeners, AI stack) into the 5paisa app.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Strategy:** Management is prioritizing long-term growth over short-term profitability, continuing to invest in technology, product, and brand despite market softness.
*   **Product Pipeline:** Upcoming launch of an IPO-related product.
*   **Long-term View:** Management remains bullish, noting that India added ~16 million demat accounts in H1 and domestic households continue to hold the largest share of listed companies.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Environment:** Described as a "soft quarter" across the board.
*   **Volume Trends:** Derivatives volume dropped following the cash rollout; active NSE clients remained at ~46 million.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Profitability:** Net profit was lower than Q1 due to the deliberate choice to keep investing in technology, product, and brand.
*   **Tech Spend:** Capitalized Rs 62 million in costs related to the new app build, to be amortized over several years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Evaluating the integration of Trendlyne; decision on whether to merge operations or keep as a subsidiary pending formal deal closure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Capital Position:** Net worth is Rs 1,136 crore, significantly up from Rs 650 crore in March 2026, following the rights issue.
*   **Funding Book:** Average funding book is ~Rs 480 crore, described as spread across a large number of clients rather than concentrated.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Market Volatility:** Acknowledged slower trading activity across the industry.
*   **Volume Decline:** Specifically noted a drop in derivatives volume.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How will Trendlyne be positioned?** Management: Acquisition needs to formally close first. The goal is to surface their best features (baskets, screeners, AI) on the 5paisa app, regardless of whether it remains a separate entity or is merged.
*   **Q: Are AI tools driving higher retention/conversion?** Management: It is too early to tell; the tools have only been live for a few weeks/months. However, they believe mature, non-gimmicky products generally improve engagement.
*   **Q: Can you provide specific metrics on AI engagement?** Management: Did not provide specific numbers, reiterated it is "too early" to quantify the impact on engagement.

### One-line takeaway
5paisa is sacrificing short-term profitability to fund a major technology and AI-driven product overhaul, betting that these upgrades will drive long-term engagement despite current market-wide volume softness.
