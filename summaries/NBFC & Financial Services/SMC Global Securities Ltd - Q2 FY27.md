# SMC Global Securities Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While the company delivered healthy YoY growth in revenue and profit, management acknowledged a sequential decline in profitability due to regulatory changes impacting trading volumes and arbitrage strategies.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Operational Income:** Rs 532.5 crore (+20.9% YoY, +3.4% QoQ)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 101.6 crore (+20.4% YoY)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 19.1% (vs 19.2% in Q2 FY26)
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 28.2 crore (+34.4% YoY)
*   **PAT Margin:** 5.3% (+50 bps YoY)
*   **NBFC AUM:** Rs 1,031 crore (as of Sept 2026)
*   **NBFC NIM:** 7.8% in H1 FY27 (vs 7.3% in FY26)
*   **Broking Client Base:** 14.2 lakh

### Highlights
*   **Funding Update:** Successfully raised Rs 150 crore via NCDs (oversubscribed, listed Oct 2026).
*   **Broking:** Online penetration reached 75% (vs 72% in FY26). Wealth AUM/AUA grew ~25% in H1 to Rs 1,464 crore.
*   **NBFC Transformation:** Consciously shifting portfolio mix away from large-ticket LAP and unsecured loans toward granular, secured products (Micro-LAP, gold loans).
*   **Insurance:** Revenue grew 19.3% YoY in Q2; gross premium for H1 stood at Rs 1,598 crore.
*   **Market Structure:** Successfully adapted systems to SEBI’s new Closing Auction Session and realigned pre-open sessions.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **NBFC AUM:** Management revised FY27 AUM target to Rs 1,125–1,150 crore (down from previous Rs 1,300 crore target) due to faster-than-planned rundown of discontinued loan products.
*   **StoxKart:** No break-even expected this fiscal year; management plans to invest Rs 50 crore more in FY27, primarily for MTF (Margin Trading Facility) book growth. Revenue target is >Rs 100 crore for the year.
*   **Unsecured Loans:** Intention to reduce unsecured book to below 15% of AUM within a year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Environment:** Q2 was challenging; Nifty 50 fell ~5%, and foreign investors turned net sellers.
*   **Structural Growth:** Strong retail participation continues, with 90 lakh new demat accounts added in the quarter and record monthly SIP contributions of Rs 32,490 crore in September.
*   **Regulatory:** RBI’s revised framework for bank lending to brokers (effective July 1) requires full collateralization, favoring well-capitalized intermediaries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Management expects a ~20 bps increase in borrowing costs following the 25 bps repo rate hike in October.
*   **NIM Impact:** Despite rising borrowing costs, management expects "almost negligible" impact on NIMs due to higher yields from the shift toward a granular, secured retail loan book.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Technology:** Continuous investment in technology and manpower across the group.
*   **StoxKart:** Rs 50 crore investment planned for FY27 to scale the MTF business.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Consolidated Net Worth:** Rs 1,358 crore (as of Sept 2026).
*   **Debt-Equity Ratio:** 1.63x.
*   **NBFC Capital Adequacy:** 46.1%.
*   **Borrowing Profile:** >90% of NBFC borrowings are at floating rates.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory Impact:** SEBI’s new closing auction rules for F&O stocks led to investor confusion and reduced trading volumes/turnover in Q2.
*   **Treasury Performance:** Deficiency in arbitrage strategies during the quarter negatively impacted profitability.
*   **Insurance Regulation:** IRDAI’s draft consultation paper on commission caps and expense management creates uncertainty for future revenue/product economics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did PAT fall sequentially (QoQ) despite YoY growth?** Management (Subhash Chand Aggarwal) attributed this to regulatory changes (closing auction session) that reduced trading volumes and poor performance of arbitrage strategies in the treasury segment.
*   **Q: When will StoxKart break even?** Management (Pranay Aggarwal) stated they are prioritizing growth over profitability this year, aiming for >Rs 100 crore revenue, with break-even targeted for next year.
*   **Q: Why is the NBFC AUM target being cut?** Management (Himanshu Gupta) explained the rundown of discontinued large-ticket LAP and unsecured loans is happening faster than planned, which is positive for asset quality but lowers the total AUM base temporarily.
*   **Q: How will the repo rate hike affect the NBFC?** Management expects a 20 bps rise in borrowing costs but believes the shift to a higher-yielding, granular retail book will offset the impact on NIMs.
*   **Q: Are any business lines under review for exit?** Management confirmed no plans to exit any of the 11 business lines, noting they are sufficiently capitalized.

### One-line takeaway
SMC Global is navigating a transition phase, prioritizing a higher-quality, granular NBFC book and digital-first growth in StoxKart, while managing short-term volatility in broking volumes caused by regulatory shifts.
