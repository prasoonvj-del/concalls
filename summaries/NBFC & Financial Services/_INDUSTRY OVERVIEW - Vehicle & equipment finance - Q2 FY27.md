# NBFC & Financial Services - Vehicle & equipment finance: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The vehicle and equipment finance sector exhibited robust momentum in Q2 FY27, driven by strong underlying retail credit demand, geographical expansion, and scaling non-vehicle segments (such as SME, consumer loans, and digital health finance). However, management tones diverged: while companies like Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Ltd and Manba Finance Ltd remained distinctly positive with aggressive growth targets, others like Shriram Finance Ltd and Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Ltd adopted a cautious stance. This caution stems from localized rural stress driven by erratic monsoons, drought-prone districts, and softening festive commerce. Margins and profitability remained healthy, supported by rating upgrades and capitalization, though management teams are closely monitoring potential regulatory headwinds (such as draft insurance commission rules) and rising debt costs.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Historically, vehicle and equipment finance in India relied heavily on physical branch networks, dealer relationships, and manual underwriting of commercial and two-wheeler loans, with profitability driven by high asset yields and direct lending or co-lending arrangements. Over the past five years, the model has undergone a structural shift toward digital-first origination, data-science-driven credit scorecards, direct-to-consumer digital channels, and aggressive portfolio diversification into SME, consumer loans, and digital-anchored lending to reduce dependence on traditional vehicle cycles [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **Digital-First Sourcing and AI Underwriting:** Scaling rapidly, with lenders shifting from manual dealer-heavy channels to proprietary digital apps and AI-orchestrated workflows. Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services achieved Rs 1,663 crore in AI-orchestrated disbursements and automated 75% of back-office operations [Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services concall Q2 FY27]; Muthoot Capital moved its two-wheeler scorecards entirely in-house, raising approval rates from ~45% to ~75% [Muthoot Capital Services concall Q2 FY27]; Manba Finance deployed AI for MIS and reporting [Manba Finance concall Q2 FY27]. This lowers customer acquisition costs and accelerates turnaround times.
- **Portfolio Diversification Beyond Core Wheels:** Scaling across major players, reducing cyclicality. Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services grew its non-wheels business by 52%, reaching 20% of total AUM [Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services concall Q2 FY27]; Cholamandalam grew Consumer and SME AUM by 32% and 27% respectively [Cholamandalam Investment and Finance concall Q2 FY27]; Mufin Green Finance successfully pivoted its digital lending (Mediclaim Financing and Salary Saathi) to comprise 44% of AUM while scaling down legacy climate finance [Mufin Green Finance concall Q2 FY27]. This diversification protects earnings against auto-cycle downturns.
- **In-House Sourcing and Wind-Down of Co-Lending:** Mainstream shift toward proprietary origination to protect margins. Muthoot Capital Services recorded its first quarter in 48 months with 100% in-house sourcing and zero co-lending, while cutting its co-lending book by 52% YoY to expand net interest margins [Muthoot Capital Services concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- RBI mandate urging NBFCs to use CKYCR for onboarding standardizes customer verification and reduces compliance friction [Govt: RBI CKYCR onboarding mandate, Nov 2026], benefiting compliant NBFCs like Cholamandalam, Shriram Finance, and Muthoot Capital.
- RBI's final directions and dematerialization of securitisation activities effective April 2027 force transparent structure and minimum investments of Rs 1 crore per investor [Govt: RBI Securitisation directions, Nov 2026], impacting assignment/securitization economics for players like Muthoot Capital and Shriram Finance.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that underlying vehicle demand fundamentals remain structurally sound, supported by replacement demand, infrastructure activity, and festive season expectations in H2. 
*   **Divergence:** Divergence emerged on near-term momentum. Cholamandalam expects robust Q3 growth of 20-22% with October trending at 18-20% YoY [Cholamandalam Investment and Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Manba Finance remains optimistic about Dhanteras and Diwali converting deferred demand, noting record September 2-wheeler registrations [Manba Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Conversely, Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services noted softer-than-expected commerce during the first 10 days of Navratri, particularly in tractors, prompting a cautious/watchful stance [Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services concall Q2 FY27]. Shriram Finance trimmed its full-year AUM growth guidance to 15%–17% (from an initial 18%) due to monsoon stress [Shriram Finance concall Q2 FY27].

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Cost of Funds & Yields:** Cholamandalam anticipates a modest 10 bps yield decline offset by credit cost improvements, with cost of funds expected to rise by 15-20 bps YoY [Cholamandalam Investment and Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Muthoot Capital reduced overall borrowing costs (bank loans down 0.61%) and targets an additional 10-30 bps reduction by March [Muthoot Capital Services concall Q2 FY27]. Manba Finance expects a 25 bps reduction in borrowing costs in Q3/Q4 following a credit rating upgrade to A- [Manba Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Shriram Finance saw its incremental cost of funds cap at 7.3%, with overall liability cost down 15 bps QoQ to 8.41% [Shriram Finance concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Operating Expenses:** Opex pressures varied. Cholamandalam's employee costs rose due to transitioning outsourced staff to the payroll, though offset by outsourcing drops [Cholamandalam Investment and Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Manba Finance's opex grew 28% YoY due to aggressive expansion in Karnataka [Manba Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Mufin Green Finance reported a 27.7% increase in opex while scaling tech infrastructure [Mufin Green Finance concall Q2 FY27].

## Capex & capacity
*   **Branch & Network Expansion:** Cholamandalam is converting Resident Location Hubs (RLHs) into full branches, targeting 480 gold loan branches by year-end [Cholamandalam Investment and Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Manba Finance added 3 branches for small business loans in two months with 2 more planned [Manba Finance concall Q2 FY27]. Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services increased headcount to support wheels, SME, and mortgage growth [Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services concall Q2 FY27]. Muthoot Capital focused on deepening presence in existing states rather than new geographic expansion while scaling its digital fixed deposit platform [Muthoot Capital Services concall Q2 FY27].

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Cholamandalam Investment and Finance:** Maintained full-year AUM growth guidance of ~25% and credit cost guidance of ~1.4%; announced plans to raise up to Rs 5,000 crore [Cholamandalam Investment and Finance concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services:** Shifted tone from "strong growth" to "cautious/watchful" due to festive demand softness and tractor segment declines; maintained long-term AUM guidance of 16-18% and ROA of 2.2-2.5% [Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Manba Finance:** Adjusted AUM growth guidance slightly from 35-40% (Q1) to 30-35% (Q2); credit rating upgraded to A- (Stable) [Manba Finance concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Mufin Green Finance:** Shifted tone from "transitioning" to "optimistic" on digital lending; met employee headcount reduction target down to 306 [Mufin Green Finance concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Shriram Finance:** Trimmed FY27 AUM growth guidance to 15%-17% (from 18%); flagged temporary asset quality pressure from erratic monsoons [Shriram Finance concall Q2 FY27].

## What we were watching
- ✅ RBI: Urged NBFCs to use CKYCR for onboarding — Cholamandalam, Shriram Finance, and Muthoot Capital discussed onboarding enhancements, digital KYC integration, and Account Aggregator utilization for seamless credit checks.
- ✅ RBI: Final securitisation directions for banks, NBFCs — Muthoot Capital discussed DA transactions and assignment books; Cholamandalam noted it has largely stopped DA income to avoid upfronting profits.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Progression of rural asset quality and recovery in monsoon-affected states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana) for Shriram Finance and Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services.
*   Festive volume conversions and realization of H2 demand for Manba Finance and Muthoot Capital.
*   Regulatory clarifications and potential impact of the proposed insurance commission draft norms on fee income across lenders.
*   Execution of capital raises and liquidity buffer deployments at Cholamandalam and Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services.

## Company one-liners
*   **Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Ltd:** Cholamandalam delivered strong Q2 growth across all segments and maintained its full-year guidance, while proactively managing capital and opex to support long-term expansion [Cholamandalam Investment and Finance concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Ltd:** Management delivered steady growth and stable asset quality while maintaining long-term targets, despite adopting a cautious stance on festive demand and potential regulatory headwinds in the insurance business [Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Manba Finance Ltd:** Manba Finance is leveraging a credit rating upgrade to lower borrowing costs and fuel growth, while maintaining strict underwriting standards and focusing on festive-driven demand in H2 [Manba Finance concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Mufin Green Finance Ltd:** Mufin is successfully pivoting to a high-margin, tech-led digital lending model, with management prioritizing profitability and ROA over aggressive AUM expansion, while actively managing legacy EV portfolio risks [Mufin Green Finance concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Muthoot Capital Services Ltd:** Muthoot Capital delivered a steady Q2 FY27 with 25% AUM growth to Rs. 3,530 crore, an AA rating upgrade enabling PAN India deposit mobilization, and 100% in-house sourcing following the wind-down of co-lending [Muthoot Capital Services concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Shriram Finance Ltd:** Shriram Finance delivered strong earnings backed by capital-driven NIM expansion, but has prudently scaled back growth guidance to 15%–17% amid temporary rural stress from deficient rains [Shriram Finance concall Q2 FY27].
