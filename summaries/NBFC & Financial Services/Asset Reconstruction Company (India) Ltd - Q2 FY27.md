# Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth across AUM, acquisitions, recoveries, and profitability, backed by a robust balance sheet following its recent listing.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** INR20,274 crores (up 21% YoY from INR16,784 crores as of September 30, 2025)
- **H1 FY27 Acquisitions:** INR1,707 crores (up 18% YoY from INR1,445 crores in H1 FY26); Q2 acquisitions at INR1,354 crores (up 43% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Recoveries:** INR2,135 crores (up 21% YoY from INR1,765 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** INR516 crores (up 48% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Fee and Other Income:** INR227 crores (up 82% YoY from INR125 crores)
- **H1 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR290 crores (up 54% YoY from INR189 crores)
- **H1 FY27 Diluted Annualized EPS:** Around INR18.50
- **Return on Equity (Annualized):** 18%
- **Return on Assets (Annualized):** 12.6%
- **Net Worth:** INR3,337 crores as of September 30, 2026 (compared to INR3,079 crores in March 2026)
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** 70.5%
- **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.37x (Borrowings at INR1,224 crores)

### Highlights
- ARCIL became India's first listed ARC in September 2026, crossing 1 lakh shareholders.
- Retail AUM crossed INR5,000 crores for the first time during H1 FY27; AUM mix stands at Corporate (66%), Retail (25%), and SME (9%).
- Cumulative SR redemption ratio stands at about 52% (up from 50.8% as of March 31, 2026).
- Started pilots for "collection as a service" leveraging its retail technology platform and expects to scale it in H2.
- Standalone profit was INR290 crores vs. consolidated profit of INR219 crores for H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management did not provide specific numerical guidance on AUM or full-year numbers, pointing to historical performance and seasonal trends instead.
- H2 is seasonally the strongest period for acquisitions, accounting historically for about 60% of annual acquisitions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CRISIL estimates total stress across banks and NBFCs at about INR23.7 lakh crores in FY26 (approx. INR15.9 lakh crores in NPAs/written-off loans and INR7.8 lakh crores in special mention accounts).
- Corporate written-off loans account for INR6.4 lakh crores; retail and MSME account for INR9.5 lakh crores.
- Industry stress pool grows by about INR1 lakh crores a year, while ARCs buy below 10% of it annually (internal estimate).
- Retail and SME asset sales by lenders are increasing, with special mention accounts making up over 25% of retail accounts sold by number.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 FY27 PAT margin at ~56% vs 54% in H1 FY26.
- Investment upsides (other operating income) were INR122 crores in H1 FY27 (vs INR87 crores in H1 FY26).
- Write-backs contributed INR129 crores in H1 FY27 (vs INR37 crores in H1 FY26).
- Net unrealized MTM gain was INR26 crores (vs INR72 crores in H1 FY26).
- Employee expenses stood at INR39.2 crores (net of INR3.5 crores reversal of excess provision).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Maintained 15 offices across 12 states supporting 3.29 million retail borrowers via technology platforms.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net owned funds at INR2,882 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- Borrowings increased to INR1,224 crores to fund Q2 acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Resolution cycles are long, corporate recoveries are lumpy, and acquisitions are seasonal (concentrated in H2).
- Down cycles typically aid acquisitions due to higher supply but can temporarily reduce resolution speeds.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Murli on sustainability of non-fee revenue components:** Management stated that due to a large corporate AUM book (INR13,000 crores), lumpy resolutions and regular write-backs will occur consistently over time, and performance should be judged on regularity rather than individual items.
2. **Rikin Shah on ECL write-backs and full-year expectations:** Management explained that ECL provisions vary quarterly based on receivables (with Q1 provisions partially reversed in Q2), while macro-level annual ECL runs around INR15-20 crores per RBI regulations.
3. **Himanshu Taluja on retail underwriting risks and potential write-offs:** Management noted that unsecured retail books are acquired at single-digit prices and secured books in mid-40s percentages, with full capital recovery typically within 3 to 4 years, rendering write-offs minimal.
4. **Pulavarthi Saikiran on industry AUM stagnation vs ARCIL market share gains:** Management attributed market share gains to ARCIL's multi-asset class focus, pricing expertise, and experience in deal structuring compared to specialized competitors.
5. **Heet Khimawat on structured deals vs cash deals and upside capture:** Management clarified that structured deals provide continuous fee income and regular IRR recovery with lower dependence on back-ended upsides, whereas cash deals provide investment upsides in later years.

### One-line takeaway
ARCIL delivered strong H1 FY27 performance with a 54% YoY surge in PAT and a 21% increase in AUM, entering its seasonally stronger second half with a conservative debt-to-equity of 0.37x and 70.5% capital adequacy.
