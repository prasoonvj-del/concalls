# KFin Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted consistent growth across business lines, successful margin expansion, and confidence in meeting annual targets despite recent market volatility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 18-20% YoY revenue growth and 40% EBITDA margin target (including Ascent).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "tepid" corporate action outlook in Q1 to "back on track" for Q2; management remains confident despite recent 10-day market correction.
- **New topics/risks:** Highlighted the Middle East geopolitical situation impacting Ascent; clarified the Philippines pension deal is still under discussion (neither awarded nor cancelled).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on margin expansion (EBITDA margin reached 40% including Ascent); successfully integrated Ascent operations; continued rollout of AI-native platforms (Aria.AI).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** +30% QoQ (including Ascent); +10% QoQ (excluding Ascent).
- **EBITDA:** +18.7% QoQ; +32% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 40% (including Ascent); 45.8% (excluding Ascent).
- **PAT:** Rs 100 crore (+7.5% YoY; +33% vs same quarter last year).
- **PAT Margin:** 25% (including Ascent); >30% (excluding Ascent).
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 23% (including Ascent).
- **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 550 crore (post-dividend).
- **Diluted EPS (H1):** Rs 10.12.
- **International Pipeline:** US$ 47 million.
- **Market Share:** >33% (overall); ~33% (equity); ~37% (SIPs).

### Highlights
- **Business Performance:** KFin AUM grew 15% vs 13% industry growth.
- **Ascent Integration:** Business growing 30%+; management expects double-digit EBITDA margins in the next couple of quarters.
- **Pensions:** Grew 42% (vs 12-13% industry growth).
- **Technology:** Aria.AI (GenAI platform) beta is live, public launch scheduled for late November; FinEx (Mutual Fund platform) rollout expected by end of Q4.
- **Issuer Solutions:** Successfully managed the Jio Platforms IPO; 20% YoY revenue growth expected for the year.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Management reiterated 18-20% YoY growth target.
- **Margins:** Management targets 40% consolidated EBITDA margin for the full year.
- **Buffers:** Management stated they can pull back on discretionary spending (IT projects, expansion) if market conditions deteriorate significantly.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Mutual Funds:** Yields saw a marginal 2% QoQ expansion; management expects yields to remain consistent.
- **Issuer Solutions:** Corporate actions are a Q2-heavy phenomenon; management expects continued resilience due to IPO pipeline.
- **Ascent:** No new client additions this quarter as fund managers held back launches; however, 80-90 funds are contracted and awaiting launch.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Expenses:** Increased 3% QoQ, significantly lower than the 13% revenue growth.
- **Headcount:** Reduced slightly; resources reallocated to faster-growing businesses (Wealth, Alternatives, Pensions).
- **Cloud/Infra Costs:** Management noted costs have doubled/tripled, partially offsetting productivity gains from AI/RPA implementation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **New Office:** Hyderabad office becomes operational Dec 1st.
- **Lease Impact:** Rent increase of Rs 80 lakh/month; P&L impact of Rs 7 crore in FY27 and Rs 18 crore in FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash:** Rs 550 crore maintained after dividend payout.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Market Volatility:** Recent 10-day market downturn poses a risk if it becomes protracted.
- **Geopolitics:** Middle East situation impacts 25% of Ascent revenue.
- **Tax Rate:** Higher than 25.64% due to loss-making entities (GIFT City, Thailand, Ascent Singapore) where deferred tax is not recognized.
- **Philippines Deal:** Still under discussion; neither awarded nor cancelled.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why was Ascent client addition flat?** A: Management explained that while new wins are happening, fund managers are delaying launches due to macro conditions; 80-90 funds are already contracted.
- **Q: How will you reach 40% EBITDA margin if Q1 was 34%?** A: Management stated the 40% target is a full-year average, not a Q4 exit run rate.
- **Q: Can you break down the yield expansion?** A: Management attributed it to a mix of pricing discipline, AMC growth, and asset class mix; expects consistency going forward.
- **Q: Why is the tax rate increasing?** A: Management clarified it is due to loss-making entities (GIFT City, Thailand, Ascent Singapore) where they cannot recognize deferred tax assets.
- **Q: What is the status of the Philippines pension deal?** A: Management confirmed it is still under discussion with local authorities and they remain confident in a turnaround.

### One-line takeaway
KFin Tech delivered strong Q2 results with 30% revenue growth and 40% EBITDA margins, with management maintaining full-year guidance and emphasizing productivity gains to offset rising infrastructure costs.
