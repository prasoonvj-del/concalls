# Sammaan Capital Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by promoter IHC, credit rating upgrades, declining cost of funds, healthy recoveries, and business expansion.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** INR56,239 crores
- **Disbursements (Q1):** INR3,875 crores
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q1):** INR243 crores
- **Capital Adequacy:** 20.1%
- **Net NPA:** 0.15% (standard)
- **Gross Recoveries (Q1):** INR424 crores
- **Net Recoveries (Q1):** INR240 crores
- **Cost of Funds:** Started at 10.5%, reached 10% in Q1; target 9.3% by end of FY27 (incremental down to 9%, targeting 8.5% by end of year)
- **Disbursement Guidance:** INR10,000 crores for H1 FY27; INR20,000 crores for H2 FY27; INR30,000 crores total for FY27; INR40,000 crores to INR50,000 crores for next financial year
- **Branch Network:** Targeting ~240 branches by end of H1, increasing to ~270 branches; targeting ~800 branches by end of next year

### Highlights
- First earnings execution under the ownership of International Holding Company (IHC).
- Domestic credit ratings upgraded from AA to AA+; international ratings upgraded from B+ to BB-.
- Completed a $63 million buyback of dollar bonds.
- Expanded product suite beyond monoline mortgages to include personal loans, loans against securities (LAS), credit platform/credit funds, and portfolio buyouts.
- Implemented 53 AI use cases, with ~60% to be deployed within the financial year.
- Added IHC nominees Mr. Alwyn Crasta and H.E. Dalia Khorshid to the board.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects annual cost of funds to decline by 40 bps to 90 bps, aiming below 8% over the next 2-3 years.
- H1 FY27 disbursements planned at INR10,000 crores; H2 FY27 disbursements at INR20,000 crores (Total INR30,000 crores for FY27). FY28 disbursements guided at INR40,000 crores to INR50,000 crores.
- Cost-to-income ratio targeted in the ~50% range for FY27, declining from H2 of next year, and reaching 30% to 35% by fiscal 2029.
- Inorganic growth and new financial services products (asset management, HNI services) will be evaluated starting next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Private and foreign banks have returned to participate in the company's borrowing program and large underwritings.
- Demand remains strong for secured retail and MSME lending.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of funds declined from 10.5% to 10% during Q1; incremental cost of funds down to 9%.
- Cost-to-income expected to be around 50% in FY27 due to ongoing investments in physical infrastructure, tech, and hiring.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Branch count to reach ~240 by end of H1 and ~270 by end of H2, scaling to ~800 by end of next year, covering 20 states via 23 master service centers.
- Digital personal loans, micro LAP, and rural home loans to go live in H2 FY27 via app and branch expansion.
- Gold loans and digital two-wheeler/BNPL integrations planned for FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- 97% of disbursements and 97% of AUM are secured.
- Secured a deal with an Asia-based credit fund (20% retained, 80% with partner) worth INR840 crores.
- Using ARCs for resolution; historical recoveries on sold assets stand at 99% for FY23 sales and 95% for FY24 sales.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted the need to manage operational complexity as product lines and branch networks expand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raghav Garg (Ambit Capital) on pricing strategy vs spreads:** Management stated they will straddle yields from 8.5% to 20%, using an asset-light direct assignment model (at least 30% securitized) for low-yielding products while keeping higher-yielding products on the balance sheet.
2. **Bhanu Chauhan (Barclays Group) on upfront opex and cost-to-income:** Management expects cost-to-income to remain around 50% in FY27 due to expansion, before declining to 30%–35% by fiscal 2029 driven by scale and fee income.
3. **Abhiram Iyer (Deutsche Bank) on disbursement mix:** Management noted that ~75% of FY27 disbursements will be mortgage-backed (two-thirds retail, one-third wholesale) and 25% new products, moving from 90% secured this year to 80% secured / 20% unsecured next year.
4. **Vinit Agarwal on tangible benefits from IHC:** Management highlighted rating upgrades saving ~90 bps on a ~INR50,000 crore borrowing book (~INR450 crores annualized, scaling to ~INR1,000 crores with further upgrades), alongside 30%–50% cost reductions on software procurement.
5. **Niharika on ambitious 5-year guidance (ROE/ROA/NIM):** Management expressed full confidence, citing liability franchise improvements, credit rating upgrades, lower cost of funds, tech/digital enablement, and strict governance guardrails.

### One-line takeaway
Sammaan Capital delivered a stable Q1 FY27 marked by lower borrowing costs under IHC ownership, strong recoveries, and scaling disbursements as it transitions from portfolio management to multi-product growth.
