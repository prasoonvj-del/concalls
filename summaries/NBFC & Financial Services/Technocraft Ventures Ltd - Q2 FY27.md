# Technocraft Ventures Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue and profit growth, a robust order book driven by new project wins, and successful IPO listing.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 100.27 crores (vs. Rs. 60.32 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs. 15.24 crores (vs. Rs. 8.40 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 11.00 crores (vs. Rs. 6.21 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 192.98 crores, up 27.82% YoY (vs. Rs. 151.98 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 37.91 crores, up ~41% YoY (vs. Rs. 26.89 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs. 29.71 crores (vs. Rs. 20.90 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 21.70 crores, up 39.51% YoY (vs. Rs. 15.55 crores in H1 FY26)
- **Order Book:** Approximately Rs. 1,393 crores as of September 30, 2026 (~Rs. 1,100 crores newly awarded in Q1 and Q2)
- **Bids in Pipeline:** Rs. 870 crores (results awaited; ~Rs. 500 crores for wastewater treatment)
- **Delhi Jal Board Project Value:** Rs. 196 crores 47 lakhs
- **Working Capital Cycle / Receivables:** ~120 days (4.5 months)
- **Expected Mobilization Advance (Bihar):** ~Rs. 95 crores (interest-free, bank guarantee process ongoing)
- **Security and Retention Amount Released:** ~Rs. 15 crores

### Highlights
- Company completed its IPO in August 2026 and listed on BSE and NSE; IPO proceeds are being utilized for working capital requirements.
- Received a Letter of Award from Delhi Jal Board worth Rs. ~196.47 crores for sewer lines in Bajitpur Thakran and Auchandi areas of Bawana, Delhi.
- Operating income growth was driven by better execution of larger quantum projects procured recently.
- Successfully released ~Rs. 15 crores of security and retention amounts from past completed projects.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects higher growth in upcoming quarters (Q3 and Q4) as newly awarded projects (~Rs. 1,100 crores) move from the design/engineering phase into physical execution on the ground.
- For the Delhi Jal Board project, management expects 60% to 70% of the work to be completed across the balance of FY27 and FY28.
- Management plans to selectively bid for larger EPC contracts, expand into new geographies, and grow operations and maintenance (O&M) work for business continuity.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust structural demand driven by government push for clean drinking water, wastewater treatment, and sanitation under AMRUT Mission, AMRUT 2.0, Namami Gange, Swachh Bharat Mission, Urban Challenge Fund, NMCG, and Asian Development Bank-funded projects.
- Active presence across six states: Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, and Odisha.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 margins were slightly flat / impacted by the rainy season, which restricted underground excavation work while fixed overheads and staffing expenses remained constant.
- Working capital is managed by executing work commensurate with capabilities and maintaining profitability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company is qualified to independently execute large-value contracts up to Rs. 400 crores to Rs. 500 crores.
- Focus on strengthening presence in water supply and wastewater treatment, expanding to new geographies, and scaling up O&M services.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital cycle typically runs around 4.5 months (120 days), with minor delays attributed to external factors like the West Asia crisis.
- Applying for ~Rs. 95 crores in interest-free mobilization advances for the Bihar project against bank guarantees (10% of contract term).
- Cash flow is supported by the release of ~Rs. 15 crores in security and retention amounts from past projects and IPO proceeds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Monsoon / rainy season temporarily impacts underground excavation and sewer pipeline laying due to restrictions by local municipal authorities, slowing down revenue recognition.
- Timely fund allocation and disbursement procedures by central/state government stakeholders can introduce temporal delays in realizations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Manisha Joshi (Individual Investor):** Asked about the order book timeline and revenue expectations. Management responded that the Rs. 1,393 crore order book has a general 2-year execution window, with ~Rs. 1,100 crores newly awarded in Q1/Q2 ramping up in Q3/Q4, and the remaining ~Rs. 300 crores nearing completion in FY27.
- **Isha (Analyst):** Asked about revenue recognition for the Delhi Jal Board project and receivables cycle. Management stated that 60% to 70% of the DJB project will be executed over FY27 and FY28, and confirmed the normal working capital cycle is around 4.5 months.
- **Niti Jain (Analyst):** Inquired about debtor positions and cash flow management. Management explained that Q2 billings were being realized in October/November, and cash flow is further aided by ~Rs. 95 crores in mobilization advances (Bihar) and ~Rs. 15 crores in released retention money.
- **Prabhakar Venkatraman (Investor):** Questioned flat operating/net profit margins QoQ and requested quarterly investor presentations in future disclosures. Management attributed flat margins to the monsoon slowdown and ongoing design phases, and acknowledged the suggestion for future investor presentations (did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
Technocraft Ventures reported strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 27.82% YoY and a robust order book of Rs. 1,393 crores following its August 2026 IPO, with management guiding for accelerated execution in the second half of the fiscal year.
