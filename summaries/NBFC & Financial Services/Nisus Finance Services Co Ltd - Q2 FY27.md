# Nisus Finance Services Co Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Integrated platform execution delivering strong financial momentum despite a cautious macroeconomic environment.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 3,304 crore (closing target of Rs 4,000+ crore by March '27).
- **AUM Deployment (H1 FY27):** ~Rs 673 crore across 5 investments in India and the UAE (~Rs 330 crore in Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Pune).
- **Annualized Revenue-to-AUM Yield:** 3.9% (target was 3.5%, industry average 1% to 2%).
- **Investment Banking / Advisory Share:** Contributed roughly 68% of core revenue in H1.
- **NCCCL Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 333 crore (up 12.3% YoY).
- **NCCCL EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 34.3 crore (up 30.2% YoY).
- **NCCCL EBITDA Margin:** 10.3% (up 141 bps YoY).
- **NCCCL PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 7.7 crore (up 4x YoY from Rs 1.99 crore; up from Rs 3.5 crore pre-acquisition to Rs 16.4 crore post-acquisition).
- **NCCCL Order Book:** Exceeds Rs 2,760 crore (with >Rs 1,600 crore won post-Nisus acquisition, including >Rs 1,270 crore won in H1 FY27).
- **NCCCL Retention Money Released:** ~Rs 24.32 crore.
- **Promoter Share Pledge:** 22.4% as of September (2x cover on total exposure).
- **Guidance (FY27):** Core income range of Rs 140 crore to Rs 150 crore or more; PAT of Rs 60 crore to Rs 65 crore or more (revised/maintained from an earlier outlook range of Rs 65 crore-75 crore, reflecting conservatism); Closing AUM of Rs 4,000+ crore.

### Highlights
- Deployed ~Rs 673 crore in H1 FY27 across India and the UAE; added Dubai Islamic Bank for recent UAE asset acquisitions.
- Evolving from a specialized fund manager into an integrated urban infrastructure financial services and infrastructure player across 4 engines: Fund Management, Investment Banking, NBFC, and EPC (NCCCL).
- Expanded NCCCL internationally by winning a trade license in the UAE (NCCCL International Construction LLC) and fully implemented SAP S/4HANA ERP.
- *Record check:* Continues the strong Q2 FY27 recovery noted in the Nov 13 investor deck, beating past patterns of slower deployment by aggressively scaling order books and international EPC operations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 core income of Rs 140 crore to Rs 150 crore+, PAT of Rs 60 crore to Rs 65 crore+, and closing AUM of Rs 4,000+ crore by March '27.
- Long-term vision aims to scale AUM to Rs 30,000 crore - Rs 40,000 crore over the next decade through land banking, industrial/warehousing, data centers, and UAE credit/asset expansion.
- Plans to take NCCCL public within 24 to 36 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India credit demand remains strong due to structural urban infrastructure demand, elevated interest rates, and persistence of inflation, though residential sales show some slowdown.
- UAE real estate seeing a measured rebound following earlier price moderation and regional conflict; increasing institutional capital preference for Indian operators amid restrictions on certain nationalities.
- Deal sourcing pipeline remains robust with 400+ deals evaluated annually via 150 advisors, bank/NBFC exits, IPCs (JLL, CBRE, Anarock), industry bodies (CREDAI, NAREDCO), and direct inbound demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Short-term costs may edge into PAT due to investments in talent for the new SM REIT platform in India and tokenization/UAE expansion.
- Fund carry income of Rs 8 crore to Rs 9 crore expected to be recognized in H2 upon the wind-up of the RECOF fund (RESO fund to wind up next year).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up an SM REIT platform in India, GIFT City inbound/outbound fund offerings (Ni-YAM fund of Rs 2,500 crore targeting a first close soon), and UAE tokenization initiatives.
- NCCCL expanded operations into the UAE market via a wholly-owned subsidiary.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Zero delinquency, zero delays, and zero defaults in the India credit investment book.
- Debt can be fully repaid within the financial year, but management is optimizing capital mix against higher-yielding investments (20% return vs 12%-14% debt cost).
- Promoter share pledge stands at 22.4% (2x cover), down from 3x cover previously.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile macroeconomic environment and potential slowdown in residential sales warrant a cautious capital deployment approach.
- Short-term margin pressures from scaling new verticals (SM REIT, UAE tokenization, tech implementations).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jaswant Kumar Chajed (Individual Investor) on PAT guidance revision:** Asked why FY27 PAT guidance was slightly adjusted from Rs 65-75 crore to Rs 55-65 crore (management clarified it is maintained in the Rs 60-65 crore range, but incorporated a cautious stance due to residential sales slowdown and short-term expenses for SM REIT and UAE talent scaling).
2. **Dilip (Analyst) on why pursue an IPO for NCCCL rather than a demerger:** Management explained that a demerger wouldn't unlock intrinsic value or provide primary capital needed for NCCCL's fast growth; an IPO will unlock value and raise primary capital.
3. **Pavan (Investor) on debt repayment timeline:** Management stated they have the liquidity to clear debt within the financial year, but are balancing it against using equity for higher-yielding investments versus cheaper debt.
4. **Yogesh Shroff (Analyst) on promoter pledge:** Management confirmed the pledge is 22.4% as of September, representing a comfortable 2x cover on total exposure down from 3x.
5. **Shruti Malpani (Aarth AIF) on fund carry and macro impact:** Management confirmed Rs 8-9 crore carry is expected in H2 for RECOF wind-up, and that their fund strategies (like NiYAM) target 20%-24%+ returns, insulating them from rising risk-free safe asset rates.

### One-line takeaway
Nisus Finance delivered a disciplined H1 FY27 across its four-pillar integrated model, scaling its AUM to Rs 3,304 crore and driving strong growth at EPC subsidiary NCCCL, while navigating macroeconomic volatility with a cautious capital deployment strategy.
