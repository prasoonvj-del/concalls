# PTC India Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating momentum improved with AUM returning to growth, highest sanctions and disbursements in several years, and declining gross Stage 3 assets.

### Key numbers
- **Loan Assets / AUM:** Rs 3,684 crore (up 25% QoQ from Rs 2,946 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Loan Sanctions:** Rs 3,098 crore (36-quarters high)
- **Disbursements:** Rs 1,595 crores (19-quarters high)
- **Total Income:** Rs 114 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 44 crore (Q2 FY27)
- **Return on Assets (ROA):** 3.65% (annualized for Q2 FY27); 3.49% for H1 FY27
- **Return on Net Worth (RONW):** 5.64% (annualized for Q2 FY27)
- **Interest Spread:** 1.18% (Q2 FY27)
- **Gross Stage 3 Assets:** Rs 126 crore (down from Rs 190 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Net Stage 3 Assets:** Rs 105 crore
- **Average Yield:** 10.45%
- **Average Cost of Borrowing:** 9.27%
- **Target Balance Sheet Size / Loan Book:** Rs 5,000 crore by the end of FY27
- **Average Borrower Ticket Size:** Rs 100 crore to Rs 150 crore
- **Average Loan Seasoning:** ~4 years
- **Undisbursed Sanctioned Pipeline:** ~Rs 2,500 crore
- **Scheduled Repayments (H2 FY27):** ~Rs 300 crore

### Highlights
- Loan book returned to an upward trajectory after a gap of 10 quarters.
- Renewable energy portfolio increased to approximately 45% of the total loan book.
- Added a new bank lender to the lender base with a fresh sanction of Rs 200 crore from Central Bank of India (Rs 100 crore drawn in September). Another bank provided an in-principle approval of Rs 200 crore.
- Technical write-off executed for Danu Wind Parks during the quarter.
- Kundli Manesar slipped to NPA during the quarter, with an exposure of Rs 124 crore against which a provision of Rs 19 crore (15%) was created.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a loan book growth of at least 25% in coming years.
- Management targets reaching a net balance sheet/loan book size of approximately Rs 5,000 crore by the end of FY27.
- Spread metrics may tighten for a bit over the next couple of quarters due to the portfolio building phase.
- Management budgeted an ROA of 4.16% for the full year FY27.
- Management aims to optimize leverage to around 4x to 4.5 times over the next 3 to 4 years.
- Cost of borrowing is targeted to decrease continuously every quarter to align with market rates within a year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Focus remains centered on core infrastructure financing, diversified across ticket sizes and the infrastructure value chain.
- Focused exposure is maintained on sunshine sectors and renewable energy/energy transition.
- Infrastructure projects naturally involve high ticket sizes; management believes going for very low ticket sizes is not sustainable and involves less reliable developers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average cost of borrowing stands at 9.27% with a weighted average structure; most borrowings are linked to MCLR on an annualized basis.
- NIM is expected to moderate slightly as leverage increases, but management guides it will not go below 2%.
- Interest spread improved to 1.18% in Q2 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a comfortable liquidity position with positive cumulative net cash flow across ALM buckets and a strong liquidity coverage ratio (HQLA requirement included in total cash/liquidity of over Rs 600 crore).
- Gross Stage 2 net of provision stands at Rs 114 crore (related to IL&FS), with a provision of Rs 65 crore, leaving a net amount of Rs 65 crore.
- Data sharing for the divestment/strategic sale of PFS is ongoing via a designated nodal officer as per exchange filings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Exposure to legacy assets and legacy NPAs (such as Kundli Manesar, Varam Bio, and Danu Wind Parks).
- High prepayments from state utilities (DISCOMs) in the past created headwinds for net book growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya Shah (Investor) asked about the impairment provisions and movement in gross/net Stage 3 assets.** Dilip Srivastava explained that Kundli Manesar slipped to NPA (EAD of Rs 124 crore, provision of Rs 19 crore), Varam Bio was added with 100% provisioning, and Danu Wind Parks was technically written off. Net NPA increased due to the write-off of Danu Wind Parks despite gross numbers dropping.
- **Aditya Shah questioned the sustainability of low spreads/NIMs and past thermal asset issues.** Rajiv Malhotra clarified that PFS was never overexposed to thermal power and has primarily been a renewable energy player, diversifying into digital infrastructure and electric mobility.
- **Chandmala Halagode (Individual Investor) asked about the status of the strategic divestment.** Rajiv Malhotra responded that work is ongoing as per disclosures made to the exchanges, but declined to comment on the specific stage.
- **Amey Chheda (Banyan Capital) asked about expected recoveries in Danu Wind Parks.** Kalur Srinivas stated that Danu is undergoing the IBC process, and substantial recovery is expected next financial year once bidding concludes.
- **Manoj Kumar Pandian (Individual Investor) suggested a share buyback and dividend distribution to address the depressed stock price relative to book value.** Rajiv Malhotra noted the suggestion, stating it is beyond the management's pay grade as it involves the boards of both PFS and parent company PTC, but confirmed capital prioritisation currently rests on rebuilding the portfolio.
- **Vishal Biraia (Bandhan Asset Management) inquired about the borrowing linkage to repo/MCLR and incremental borrowing costs.** Dilip Srivastava clarified that most borrowings are linked to MCLR on an annualized basis, and weighted average cost of borrowing is 9.27%.

### One-line takeaway
PTC India Financial Services reported a strong turnaround in Q2 FY27 with a 25% QoQ growth in AUM to Rs 3,684 crore, driven by multi-year highs in sanctions and disbursements, while guiding for a Rs 5,000 crore loan book target by the end of FY27.
