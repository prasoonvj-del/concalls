# Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong profitability growth, improved asset quality, and successful execution of the diversification strategy.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone PAT:** 70% YoY growth.
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 927 crore (75% YoY growth).
*   **AUM Growth:** 13%.
*   **Asset Quality:** GS3 at 3.45% (8-year low); GS2+GS3 at 8.3% (8-year low).
*   **Credit Cost:** 1.5%.
*   **ROA:** 2.4%.
*   **ROE:** ~15%.
*   **Subsidiary Performance:** Housing Finance PAT at Rs 30 crore; Insurance Broking PAT growth at 83% YoY.
*   **Liquidity Buffer:** ~Rs 5,500 crore.
*   **PCR:** 58.1%.

### Highlights
*   **Growth Drivers:** Core wheels business grew 20%; non-wheels business (SME, mortgage, PL) grew 79%.
*   **Digital/AI:** 100% of wheels business disbursements (Rs 15,000 crore) processed on the "Udaan" phygital stack. AI agent "Samur.AI" coverage in CPC operations reached 45%.
*   **Productivity:** Manpower count remained flattish over the last 2-3 years while productivity increased.
*   **Diversification:** Management noted a shift in the asset mix, with non-wheels business becoming a key growth engine.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **AUM CAGR (FY26-FY31):** Management targets 16%–18% (Wheels at 12%, Non-wheels at 30%+).
*   **NIM:** Management targets a medium-term range above 7%–7.1%.
*   **Credit Cost:** Management maintains a through-cycle guidance of 1.3%–1.7%.
*   **Capital:** Management does not foresee a need for capital raise for at least 6-8 quarters; comfortable with current debt-to-equity ratio (5:1) and willing to lever to 6x+.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Rural/Urban:** Rural demand is growing faster than urban in the PV segment.
*   **Tractor:** Gained market share; Q1 saw strong growth as delayed monsoons elongated the buying cycle.
*   **CV:** Management is consciously reducing exposure to HCV/CE fleet operators due to ROA attractiveness and focusing on SCV/LCV.
*   **Inventory:** Management noted that OEMs are managing dealer inventory responsibly.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Opex:** Opex to average assets improved to 2.65% (from 2.8% sequentially). Management aims to keep this metric in the 2.5%–2.7% range.
*   **Cost of Funds:** Increased 10 bps QoQ.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   No major physical capex; focus remains on digital/AI stack investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Carrying an additional liquidity buffer of ~Rs 5,500 crore as a prudent measure against geopolitical and monsoon uncertainty; management will unwind this when stability returns.
*   **Capital Adequacy:** Tier 1 capital at 16.5%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Monsoon/El Nino:** Management is monitoring stress points in rural/agri cash flows; created overlays in Q3/Q4 FY26 to buffer against potential disruptions.
*   **Geopolitical:** Monitoring oil price volatility and its impact on inflation/cost of funds.
*   **Regulatory:** Potential IRDAI commission caps; management expressed confidence as their products are "clean" and basic.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is mobility growth only 12% despite tech investments?** A: Management stated they prioritize margin-accretive growth and consciously sit out low-IRR premium segments.
*   **Q: Status of housing subsidiary merger?** A: Management confirmed a proposal will be taken to both Boards by Q2 FY27.
*   **Q: Can credit costs drop below 1.3%?** A: Management reiterated the 1.3%–1.7% range, noting that while they aim for the lower end, they must account for cyclicality.
*   **Q: Why the high liquidity buffer?** A: Management explained it is a prudent response to geopolitical and monsoon uncertainty; it will be reduced when the situation normalizes.
*   **Q: Co-lending status?** A: Management confirmed they have gone live with one bank in the PV business and are testing for win-win outcomes.

### One-line takeaway
Strong profitability and asset quality improvement, driven by digital/AI efficiency and a pivot toward higher-growth non-wheel segments, while maintaining a cautious liquidity stance against macro uncertainties.
