# IIFL Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted record quarterly PAT, expanding gold loan momentum, improving opex efficiency, and stable asset quality while maintaining a disciplined, secured-first lending approach.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (AUM growth of ~25%, similar profit growth, and ROE at upper end ~20%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted more positive on demand, particularly in gold loans driven by rising gold prices and tightening unsecured lending industry-wide.
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared:**
  - New risk: Proposed IRDAI draft norms on insurance commissions, which could impact consolidated PBT by ~5% (mainly in Home Finance and Samasta).
  - New topic: Tap issuance of $300 million social bond (2.3x subscribed) and first-time issuance of tokenized bonds.
  - Old topic shift: AI strategy moved from initial pilots to full deployment, delivering a 30% reported productivity gain in audit and collections.
- **Last quarter's promises and delivery:** Equity raise process progressed from board approval to active investor discussions aimed at closing a 10–20% equity dilution in Q3 FY27; Gold tonnage grew from 64 tonnes to 69 tonnes.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Loan AUM:** Rs 1,24,268 crore (up 38% YoY, 8% QoQ).
*   **Consolidated PAT (after NCI):** Rs 805 crore (up 93% YoY, 13% QoQ); H1 PAT stood at Rs 1,518 crore.
*   **Total Income:** Rs 2,340 crore (up 6% QoQ).
*   **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 1,481 crore (up 13% QoQ).
*   **Pre-Provision Operating Profit (PPOP):** Rs 1,355 crore (up 8% QoQ).
*   **Loan Loss Provisions:** Rs 281 crore (down 5% QoQ).
*   **Gross NPA:** 1.48% (down 7 bps QoQ); **Net NPA:** 0.87%.
*   **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 91%.
*   **Portfolio Yield:** 16.5% (down 3 bps QoQ); **Cost of Borrowing:** 9.08% (up 3 bps QoQ); **Spread:** 7.42% (down 6 bps QoQ).
*   **Annualized ROE:** 20.1%; **Annualized ROA:** 3.1%.
*   **Opex to Average AUM:** 3.3% consolidated (down from peak of 4.0%); Standalone at 3.1%.
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** Consolidated 23.5%; Standalone NBFC 17.03%.
*   **Off-Book AUM:** Rs 42,796 crore (34% of consolidated AUM).
*   **Standalone AUM:** Rs 70,382 crore (up 11% QoQ; 97% secured, 93% gold loans); Standalone PAT: Rs 548.5 crore (up 17% QoQ).
*   **IIFL Home Finance AUM:** Rs 43,070 crore (up 4% QoQ); PAT: Rs 188.7 crore.
*   **Samasta Finance AUM:** Rs 10,816 crore; PAT: Rs 69 crore (up 13% QoQ); Gross NPA: 2.82%.

### Highlights
*   Consolidated quarterly PAT crossed the Rs 800 crore milestone for the first time.
*   Secured loans now constitute 90% of overall AUM, with Gold loans at 53% and Home loans at 27%.
*   Fitch upgraded the company's credit rating to BB-.
*   Gold loan tonnage expanded from 64 tonnes in Q1 FY27 to 69 tonnes in Q2 FY27.
*   Active co-lending partnerships scaled to 18 banks.
*   *Pattern continuation:* Standalone CAR remains tight at 17.03%, keeping the promised equity raise a crucial pending milestone.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Consolidated:** Maintained ~25% AUM growth, matching profit growth, and ROE at ~20% (management notes temporary dilution from planned equity raise will be caught up within 1–2 quarters).
*   **IIFL Home Finance:** Guided for 18–20% AUM growth in FY27; targeting 16–18% ROE next fiscal year.
*   **Samasta Finance:** Monthly disbursement target set at Rs 1,000 crore/month by Feb–March (up from Rs 550 crore in September).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Retail credit demand remains steady, supported by low formal credit penetration in tier-2/3 towns.
*   Gold loan demand is surging due to higher gold prices expanding household borrowing capacity and tightening risk parameters in unsecured credit/microfinance pushing borrowers toward gold collateral.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Operating efficiency improved as Opex-to-AUM moderated to 3.3% (and 3.1% standalone).
*   MSME loan yields moderated to 12.5%–13.5% as part of a strategy to move up-market to higher-quality borrowers, lowering long-term default and collection costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Total group physical branch network exceeded 5,000 branches.
*   IIFL Home Finance added 38 new branches in Q2 FY27 (totaling 350 branches), all focused on affordable housing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Cash and undrawn credit lines reached an all-time high of Rs 13,805 crore.
*   Consolidated net gearing stood at 4.1x (vs 4.0x QoQ); Standalone net gearing was 4.7x.
*   Shareholders' equity reached Rs 15,432 crore.
*   Cumulative ALM remains surplus across all maturity buckets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **IRDAI Insurance Commission Draft:** Potential loss of commission income could impact PBT by ~5% across group operations (primarily hitting Home Finance and Samasta).
*   **Gold Loan Asset Quality:** Gold loan GNPA increased by ~30 bps QoQ due to minor price fluctuations; 1–30 DPD bucket reached ~7% of the gold loan book.
*   **Gold LTV Risk:** The 90+ DPD gold loan cohort carries an average LTV of ~85%, making it sensitive to sudden gold price corrections.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Gold NPAs and DPD Increase:** Management clarified that small tick-ups in 90+ DPD (60 to 90 bps) and 1–30 DPD reflect temporary customer payment delays following gold price movements; ultimate Loss Given Default (LGD) remains negligible due to collateral coverage.
*   **On Capital Raise Progress:** Management stated they are actively in talks with investors for a primary raise (expecting 10% to 20% equity dilution) and intend to close the deal during Q3 FY27.
*   **On Off-Book Income Decline vs AUM Growth:** Management explained that off-book fee income is declining because growth is shifting to co-lending (where income accrues over time as NII) rather than Direct Assignment (DA) upfronting.
*   **On Insurance Regulatory Impact:** Management confirmed insurance revenue is ~Rs 250 crore (~Rs 160 crore in HFC, ~Rs 78 crore in Samasta), and they are working with insurance partners to mitigate the impact via alternative cross-sell products.
*   **On Home Loan Yield Compression:** Management noted that higher-yielding legacy residual portfolios (Micro LAP and BLC aggregating ~Rs 650 crore) are running off, while new onboarding yields for Home Loans have improved to 11.81%.

### One-line takeaway
IIFL Finance delivered record quarterly net profits backed by strong gold loan growth and improving operating leverage, while advancing plans to raise primary equity in Q3 FY27.
