# IIFL Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong AUM growth (38% YoY), record PAT (INR 805 crore), and improved ROE (20.1%), though management remains focused on capital adequacy and is actively planning an equity raise.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained AUM growth target at 25% and ROE target at 20%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious on unsecured" to "confident momentum" for H2 FY27.
- **New topics or risks:** IRDAI draft on insurance commissions emerged as a new risk; management estimates the impact on consolidated PBT to be less than 10%.
- **Last quarter's promises:**
    - **Equity Raise:** Last quarter promised enabling resolutions; this quarter confirmed approvals are in place and active discussions are ongoing to close the raise this quarter.
    - **Branch Expansion:** Last quarter planned 500 new branches; company now reports 5,000+ total branches.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** INR 1,24,268 crore (up 38% YoY, 8% QoQ).
- **PAT:** INR 805 crore (up 13% QoQ, 93% YoY).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.48% (down 7 bps QoQ).
- **Net NPA:** 0.87%.
- **ROE:** 20.1%.
- **Liquidity:** INR 13,805 crore (all-time high).
- **Capital Adequacy:** 23.5% (consolidated).
- **Off-book AUM:** 34% of total.

### Highlights
- First time PAT crossed the INR 800 crore milestone on a quarterly basis.
- 90% of AUM is now secured.
- Raised USD 300 million via social bonds.
- AI deployment: 30% productivity improvement in piloted processes (audit/collections).
- Gold loan tonnage grew from 64 tonnes to 69 tonnes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects momentum to continue through FY27.
- Targeting ROE >20% for the consolidated entity.
- AUM growth target: 25%.
- Home Finance ROE target: 16-18% for next year.
- Management expects to close equity raise this quarter (dilution expected between 10-20%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Macro:** Environment remains supportive of retail credit; formal credit penetration is low in small towns.
- **Gold Loans:** Competition is increasing, but the market is expanding; management is focusing on customer experience rather than aggressive rate wars.
- **Microfinance:** Growth was muted in some pockets due to drought conditions; management expects momentum to build in H2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Consolidated portfolio yield:** 16.5% (down 3 bps QoQ).
- **Cost of borrowing:** 9.08% (up 3 bps QoQ).
- **Interest spread:** 7.42% (narrowed 6 bps).
- **Opex to average AUM:** 3.3% (down from a peak of 4%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Branch network: 5,000+ branches.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** INR 13,805 crore (cash and undrawn lines).
- **Net gearing:** 4.1x.
- **Shareholders' equity:** INR 15,432 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Insurance Commissions:** IRDAI draft on insurance commissions poses a risk to fee income; management is working with partners to mitigate the impact.
- **Gold Prices:** Management noted that a sudden drop in gold prices could cause NPA spikes, though ultimate losses are expected to be minimal due to collateral.
- **Capital Adequacy:** Management highlighted the need for an equity raise to support growth in the standalone entity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gold NPA increase:** Management clarified the 60-90 bps increase is a minor variation due to gold price fluctuations; losses are expected to be negligible.
- **Equity raise:** Management confirmed board/shareholder approval; targeting 10-20% dilution; looking to close this quarter.
- **Off-book income:** Management explained that as co-lending increases, upfront income declines, but interest income remains stable.
- **Yield compression:** Management explained this is a strategic shift toward higher-quality, lower-yield borrowers (e.g., in MSME/Home loans) to reduce long-term credit costs.
- **Insurance impact:** Management noted significant exposure in HFC/Samasta and is working to mitigate the impact of the proposed IRDAI norms.

### One-line takeaway
IIFL Finance delivered record profitability and strong AUM growth driven by secured lending, while management actively manages capital adequacy via a planned equity raise and navigates regulatory changes in insurance commissions.
