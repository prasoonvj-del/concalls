# Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth and strategic acquisitions are currently offset by margin compression due to one-time integration costs and a conscious shift in accounting/capitalization policies.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 423 crore (28% YoY growth).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 390 crore (18% YoY growth).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 34.7 crore.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 8.2% (vs 10.1% in Q1 FY26).
*   **86400 (Mobileware) Revenue:** Rs 22 crore (29% YoY growth).
*   **86400 EBITDA:** Rs 8.8 crore (214% YoY growth).
*   **GreenEdge Revenue:** Rs 44 crore (160% YoY growth).
*   **GreenEdge EBITDA:** Rs 4.3 crore (66% YoY growth).
*   **TaxSpanner Revenue:** Rs 80 lakh (65% YoY growth).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Management is shifting from a "profitable growth" paradigm to a focus on improving cash flows and calibrating capitalization.
*   **DICE Acquisition:** Asset purchase completed for Rs 68 crore (excluding GST). Relocation of 100 AI professionals to Hyderabad is complete. Revenue realization begins in Q2 FY27.
*   **Unobanc (moneyHOP) Investment:** Strategic investment of Rs 8 crore for an AD Category-2 license to enable cross-border payments and forex services.
*   **Zagg.Money:** Annualized run rate reached 84,000 cards (2.3x increase).
*   **86400 (Mobileware):** Signed first international partner (LankaPay) and Union Bank of India.
*   **Cross-selling:** Successfully cross-selling Save and Zoyer products to existing clients (e.g., Campus Activewear, Quest Corp).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains 40% YoY consolidated revenue growth guidance for FY27.
*   **DICE Revenue:** Management expects Rs 15-16 crore revenue from DICE in FY27.
*   **TaxSpanner:** On target to break even this financial year.
*   **Growth Trajectory:** Management expects growth to accelerate through Q2 and the remainder of the fiscal year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Q1 is historically a soft quarter due to seasonality.
*   Management noted "geopolitical tailwinds" impacting travel spend.
*   Management welcomed proposed amendments in the Taxation and Other Laws Amendment Bill 2026 regarding limited MDR on high-value merchant transactions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Base:** Consolidated cost base (employee + other expenses) rose 25% QoQ.
*   **Margin Drivers:** Compression attributed to DICE acquisition costs (relocation, tech vendor payments), standard annual employee increments, and a shift in capitalization policy (expensing more costs to P&L).
*   **Cash Back:** Cash back as % of program fees was 66.3% in Q1 FY27 (vs 65.7% in Q1 FY26; 69% in Q4 FY26).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **International:** Opening a subsidiary in ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Markets) in Q2 FY27.
*   **Capital Deployment:** Intention to deploy QIP proceeds into adjacent sectors.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management is prioritizing cash flow optimization and moving volumes to banks with faster revenue realization.
*   Reviewing and updating capitalization policies to moderate new capitalization levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Volatility in EBITDA margins due to acquisition integration and accounting policy shifts.
*   Working capital cycles with certain banks led to a conscious decision to move customers to different banks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Analyst:* Why the slowdown in revenue growth? *Management:* Q1 is seasonally soft; DICE revenue starts in Q2; conscious decision to optimize for cash flow/capitalization over raw growth.
*   *Analyst:* Why the volatility in EBITDA margins? *Management:* Margins are in the 9-10% range historically; current dip is due to one-time acquisition costs and accounting shifts; long-term target is 14-15% over 5-7 years.
*   *Analyst:* When will AI become a revenue driver? *Management:* Already seeing payback; internal cost optimization (12.5% of expense base) and external complex problem solving for large corporates.
*   *Analyst:* Will promoters buy shares? *Management:* Bought Rs 5 crore worth of stock previously; will continue buying as liquidity permits.

### One-line takeaway
Zaggle is sacrificing short-term margin and growth metrics to integrate acquisitions, optimize cash flows, and shift accounting policies, while maintaining its 40% annual revenue growth guidance.
