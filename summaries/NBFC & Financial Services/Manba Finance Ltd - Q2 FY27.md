# Manba Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong YoY growth in NII and PAT, successful capital raise, and confidence in meeting annual growth targets despite a competitive environment.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained AUM growth guidance of 30% to 35% for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "35-40% growth" (Q1) to "30-35% growth" (Q2). Management remains optimistic about the festive season (Dussehra/Diwali) driving H2 performance.
- **New topics:** Credit rating upgrade to A- (Stable) from BBB+ (Positive). Change in Chairperson (Mrs. Neelam Tater).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Capital raise of Rs 100 crore via preferential issue is on track (expected to close by Nov 6, 2026).

### Key numbers
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 52 crore (up 40% YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 14 crore (up 20% YoY).
- **Assets Under Management (AUM):** Rs 1,811 crore (up 21% YoY).
- **Disbursements:** Rs 267 crore (vs Rs 233 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **GNPA:** 2.96% (improved from 3.41% in Q1).
- **NNPA:** 2.19% (improved from 2.52% in Q1).
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** 24.69%.
- **Average Cost of Borrowing:** 10.14% (declined 72 bps post-rating upgrade).
- **ECL Provision:** Rs 23 crore (vs Rs 13 crore requirement).

### Highlights
- **Operational Scale:** 148 locations across 7 states; 1,900+ dealer network.
- **Customer Base:** Added 32,330 new customers; total live base >2.45 lakh.
- **Product Mix:** 2-wheeler (83.10%), top-up loans (5.70%), 3-wheeler (3%), used vehicle loans (1.60%).
- **Credit Rating:** Upgraded to A- (Stable) by CARE Ratings.
- **Dividend:** Declared second interim dividend of Rs 0.25 per equity share.

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Growth:** Management expects 30% to 35% growth for FY27 and similar growth for FY28.
- **Capital Adequacy:** Current capital (24.69%) plus the upcoming Rs 100 crore infusion will support growth for the next 4-6 quarters.
- **Borrowing Costs:** Expects a further 25 bps reduction in cost of funds in Q3/Q4 as old, higher-cost borrowings (Rs 600 crore) are repaid by March 2027.
- **Product Expansion:** MSME LAP (ticket size Rs 8-25 lakh) expected to reach Rs 5 crore monthly disbursement by January 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Auto Sector:** 2-wheeler registrations reached ~53 lakh units in Q2; September saw record retail registrations of 25.37 lakh units (up 32% YoY).
- **Festive Season:** 78% of dealers expect growth in Oct-Dec; management expects conversion of deferred demand during Dhanteras and Diwali.
- **Market Penetration:** Karnataka operations added 100+ dealers in the first 60 days.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Opex:** Increased 28% YoY, driven by recruitment in new territories (Karnataka) and expansion of 16 offices in the last year.
- **Technology:** Annual spend of ~Rs 2 crore; focus on AI for MIS and reporting.
- **NIMs:** Currently at 12.3%; management expects the rating upgrade to offset potential interest rate hikes, maintaining margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Branch Expansion:** Added 3 branches for small business loans in the last 2 months; 2 more planned for this quarter.
- **BC Partners:** 10-12 active BC partners; 3 more under due diligence.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** Rs 600 crore of borrowing is at old, higher rates, which will be fully repaid by March 31, 2027.
- **Equity:** Rs 100 crore infusion via preferential issue expected to close by Nov 6, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Bounce Rates:** Average bounce rate of 18-20%; 7-8% of customers remain in bounce status across tenure.
- **Unsecured Lending:** Management remains "cautious" on personal loans, preferring to grow them slowly.
- **Battery Finance:** Currently testing the product for 18 months before scaling up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why did AUM growth appear slow in H1 (5.7%) vs annual guidance?**
  *A: Management clarified that the festive season (Dussehra/Diwali) is concentrated in H2, and October alone added Rs 120 crore in new AUM.*
- **Q: How does the company protect NIMs amid rising rates?**
  *A: Management expects a 50-75 bps reduction in borrowing costs due to the credit rating upgrade, which will offset RBI rate hikes.*
- **Q: What is the share of New-to-Credit (NTC) borrowers?**
  *A: NTC borrowers account for 40% of the book; management noted their performance is similar to other segments due to 75% LTV restrictions.*
- **Q: Are you seeing defaults from customers with loans from other lenders?**
  *A: Management stated onboarding defaulted customers is "impossible" due to CIBIL checks.*
- **Q: Why did operating expenses rise 28%?**
  *A: Driven by aggressive expansion in Karnataka and recruitment of sales teams; management argued this is necessary for growth and not a sign of inefficiency.*

### One-line takeaway
Manba Finance is leveraging a credit rating upgrade to lower borrowing costs and fuel growth, while maintaining strict underwriting standards and focusing on festive-driven demand in H2.
