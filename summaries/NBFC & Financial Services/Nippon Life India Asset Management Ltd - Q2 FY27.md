# Nippon Life India Asset Management Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Achieved highest-ever quarterly operating profit and strong AUM growth, but faced a sharp decline in PAT due to a one-time regulatory settlement and yield compression.

### What changed vs last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Maintained the 18-20% growth guidance for operating expenses over the next 6-8 quarters.
*   **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "record-breaking" to a more cautious tone regarding market volatility and industry-wide flow slowdowns.
*   **New topics/risks:** A major regulatory settlement regarding Yes Bank AT-1 bonds (INR 96.76 crore impact) was the primary new development.
*   **Last quarter's promises:** Continued focus on digital and SIPs delivered; SIF launch strategy remains in the "drawing board" phase, awaiting SEBI clearance.

### Key numbers
*   **Operating Profit:** INR 5.23 bn (up 25% YoY, 6% QoQ).
*   **Revenue:** INR 7.96 bn (up 21% YoY, 4% QoQ).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 3.24 bn (down QoQ and YoY due to exceptional item).
*   **Exceptional Item:** INR 96.76 crore paid for SEBI settlement (AT-1 bonds).
*   **Total AUM:** INR 8.68 trillion.
*   **Mutual Fund QAAUM:** INR 7.87 trillion (up 19.9% YoY, 4.7% QoQ).
*   **Market Share:** 9.02% (flat QoQ).
*   **Equity Market Share:** 7.40% (up 27 bps YoY, 2 bps QoQ).
*   **Yields:** Blended yield at 37 bps; Equity (ex-arbitrage) at 52 bps; Debt at 25 bps; Liquid at 12 bps; ETF at 25 bps.
*   **Monthly Systematic Book:** INR 37 bn (annualized INR 445 bn).

### Highlights
*   Fastest-growing AMC in the Top-10 in H1 FY27 on overall and equity AUM.
*   Interim dividend declared: INR 9.50 per share.
*   Digital business contributed 78% of total new purchase transactions in H1 FY27.
*   ETF AUM reached INR 2.49 trillion (21.12% market share).
*   AIF business raised INR 4.0 bn in commitments during Q2; cumulative commitments at INR 99.8 bn.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Expenses:** Management expects operating expenses to grow 18-20% over the next 6-8 quarters to fund technology, brand, and digital investments.
*   **AIF Costs:** Management expects incremental cost increases in the AIF subsidiary over the next 3-6 quarters due to senior hiring.
*   **ESOPs:** Total ESOP expense for FY27 is estimated at ~INR 58 crores.
*   **SIF Strategy:** Management is awaiting SEBI clearance and plans to launch two SIF strategies once approved.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Industry QAAUM grew 13% YoY to INR 87.2 trillion.
*   Industry SIP flows reached an all-time high of INR 325 bn in Sep-2026.
*   Management observed a slowdown in industry-wide investor growth and noted that some peers are losing market share to new entrants with aggressive NFO strategies.
*   Management noted HNI investors are showing signs of exiting large-cap funds due to market volatility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Operating expenses: INR 2.72 bn (up 14% YoY, flat QoQ).
*   Yield compression attributed primarily to "telescopic pricing" and growth in equity AUM.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Focus remains on scaling the AIF business and strengthening the digital franchise.
*   No specific capex figures provided, but continued investment in technology and people is planned.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Settlement amount of INR 96.76 crore fully paid and recognized in Q2 FY27 financials.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Ongoing regulatory matter regarding past investments in Yes Bank AT-1 bonds led to a settlement payment.
*   **Market Volatility:** Potential for higher redemptions if market correction continues, particularly in small-cap and sector-specific funds.
*   **Offshore:** Rupee depreciation has impacted FPI flows into India, affecting offshore AUM.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Yield Compression:** Analysts questioned why yields dropped while peers saw improvements. Management attributed this to "telescopic pricing" and the high growth rate of equity AUM.
*   **On AT-1 Bond Settlement:** Analysts asked for the trigger for the settlement. Management stated it was an "in-principle acceptance" of settlement terms by SEBI, and the board chose to settle to avoid prolonged legal uncertainty.
*   **On SIF Launch:** Management confirmed they are working on SIF strategies but are waiting for regulatory clearance before entering the market.
*   **On Unique Investor Count:** Analysts noted a marginal decline in unique investors. Management attributed this to the lack of NFO launches, which typically drive new investor acquisition.

### One-line takeaway
Nippon Life India AMC delivered record operating profits and maintained strong market share, but faced a one-time regulatory settlement hit and yield compression, while remaining cautious on market volatility.
