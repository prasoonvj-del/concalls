# One 97 Communications Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by steady 28% YoY revenue growth (>30% ex-PIDF), net payment margin expansion, strong lending momentum, and significant bottom-line upside from upcoming UPI MDR implementation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained revenue growth momentum (20s-30s%, ex-PIDF >30%) and device capex target (~Rs 400 crore on inward devices count basis).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted more bullish on near-term prospects due to festive tailwinds, accelerating MTUs (+2 million QoQ), and expected UPI MDR tailwinds post-Diwali.
- **New topics or risks:** 
  - **New:** Potential 2.5 bps margin upside from impending UPI MDR implementation on >Rs 2,000 transactions (~Rs 700+ crore EBITDA upside at current GMV scale).
  - **New:** Enabling resolution of up to Rs 500 crore over 12 months for Paytm Cloud Technologies Limited (PCTL) to fund overseas expansion (UAE, Luxembourg).
  - **New:** Resumed DLG (Default Loss Guarantee) arrangements with a long-standing lending partner.
  - **Risk:** Weakness/headwinds in equity broking/MTF, travel industry, and gold/wealth segments.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on steady device additions (~700,000 in Q2), revenue growth trajectory (28% YoY), and expanding operating leverage.

---

### Key numbers
* **Revenue Growth:** 28% YoY reported (ex-PIDF growth in the 30s%).
* **GMV Run-rate:** Rs 28 lakh crore.
* **Net Payment Margin (ex-UPI):** 8.5 bps to 9 bps (up from 8.4 bps in Q1 FY27).
* **UPI MDR Potential Upside:** 2.5 bps processing margin (~Rs 700+ crore incremental EBITDA at current GMV scale).
* **Device Additions:** ~700,000 devices deployed during the quarter.
* **Capex Target:** ~Rs 400 crore on inward device count basis.
* **Postpaid Disbursements:** ~Rs 9,000 crore.
* **Subsidiary Investment Resolution:** Up to Rs 500 crore enabling resolution over 12 months for PCTL.

---

### Highlights
* **AI-First Stack:** Launching proprietary AI agents/stacks for merchants to handle workflows, onboarding, and business functions, with monetization expected within 6–9 months.
* **UPI MDR Impact:** Management views the expected 40 bps MDR cap on >Rs 2,000 UPI transactions as purely bottom-line accretive (~2.5 bps net upside), requiring a simple system switch post-Diwali.
* **Merchant Quality & Discipline:** Exited low-margin/negative-margin large enterprise merchants, sacrificing non-profitable GMV to protect contribution margins.
* **Overseas Expansion:** Structuring Paytm Cloud Technologies Limited (PCTL) as an umbrella subsidiary to fund foreign operations (including existing setup in Singapore and planned entities in UAE and Luxembourg).

---

### Guidance & outlook
* **Revenue Growth:** Expects to sustain revenue growth in the 30s% range (excluding PIDF).
* **Merchant AI Stack Monetization:** Subscription/transaction monetization to start scaling in 6 to 9 months.
* **Device Capex:** Maintains capex expectation of ~Rs 400 crore (inward device count basis).
* **Operating Leverage:** Indirect expenses targeted to grow at a significantly slower pace than revenue and contribution margin.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Tailwinds (85–90% of business):** Strong festive season demand, personal and merchant loans expansion, higher MTU conversion, and scaling RuPay card acceptance.
* **Headwinds (10–15% of business):** Subdued market volumes impacting equity broking/MTF, soft travel industry demand, and muted gold/wealth sales.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Net Payment Margin:** Expanded to 8.5–9 bps due to strict pricing discipline, higher mix of EMI and Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC), and exit from unprofitable enterprise accounts.
* **Indirect Costs / Cloud Spend:** Temporary increase driven by a one-time cloud consolidation/migration project (merging separate online and offline payment stacks) and targeted AI investments.
* **CAC Efficiency:** Customer acquisition costs dropped significantly due to precise AI-driven targeting instead of broad promotional spending.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Deployed ~700,000 Soundbox/POS devices during the quarter.
* Device capex trajectory tracking at ~Rs 400 crore.
* Approved an enabling resolution of up to Rs 500 crore over 12 months for capital infusions into PCTL for international seed investments and partnerships.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Maintains zero balance sheet credit risk (distribution-only lending model).
* Reported EBITDA accounts for ESOP costs directly (no ESOP add-back in operational reporting).
* Capital deployment into foreign entities will be done in tranches based on board approvals over 12 months.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Implementation timeline for UPI MDR remains dependent on regulatory/NPCI final release.
* Upfront P&L cost recognition required under conservative accounting for DLG-backed lending models.
* Macro headwinds continuing in equity broking volumes and travel demand.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A

* **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA):** *How does the 2.5 bps UPI MDR flow to EBITDA, and will it change consumer acquisition spend?*  
  **Management:** It is 100% bottom-line accretive (~Rs 700+ crore EBITDA at Rs 28 lakh crore GMV scale). Acquisition aggressive strategy remains intact; MDR will not be used to subsidize inefficient CAC.

* **Manish Adukia (Goldman Sachs):** *How will MDR be executed post-Diwali, and are there growth headwinds?*  
  **Management:** It will be a flip-of-a-switch on day one for transactions >Rs 2,000 without individual merchant negotiations. Business enjoys 85–90% strong tailwinds, with minor headwinds isolated to travel, MTF/equity broking, and wealth.

* **Vijit Jain (Citi):** *What drove net payment margin expansion to 8.5–9 bps (ex-UPI)?*  
  **Management:** Strict pricing discipline, cutting negative-margin enterprise accounts, and higher growth in high-yield solutions like EMI and DCC.

* **Pranav Kshatriya (Emkay):** *What is the purpose of the Rs 500 crore PCTL resolution, and why did indirect expenses rise?*  
  **Management:** PCTL is an umbrella entity to seed overseas operations (UAE, Luxembourg). Indirect cost spike was driven by a one-time project consolidating online and offline cloud stacks alongside ROI-accretive AI spending.

* **Suraj Das (Sundaram MF):** *Why did DLG arrangements resume after being paused?*  
  **Management:** Resumption was requested by a long-standing key lending partner. While conservative accounting hits P&L upfront in the quarter incurred, it is EBITDA accretive over time and does not alter risk management.

* **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley):** *Are AI investments causing a drag on P&L?*  
  **Management:** Zero drag. The ROI is immediate via lower CAC, improved collection efficiency, faster code deployment (days vs. weeks), and operational productivity gains.

---

### One-line takeaway
Paytm is delivering strong growth (28% YoY) and net payment margin expansion while positioning for substantial EBITDA accretion from upcoming UPI MDR implementation and AI efficiencies.
