# OnEMI Technology Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted robust AUM growth, improved asset quality, and strong profitability, while maintaining a cautious, data-driven approach to risk.

### Key numbers
*   **AUM:** Rs 8,001 crore (up 61% YoY, 13% QoQ).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 95 crore (up 59% YoY, 16% QoQ).
*   **Total Income:** Rs 677 crore (up 45% YoY).
*   **Credit Cost:** 6.80% of average AUM (down from 7.02% in Q4 FY26 and 8.85% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Return on Average AUM:** 5.05%.
*   **Return on Average Equity:** 21.20%.
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 3,812 crore (up 37% YoY).
*   **Registered Users:** 74.6 million (up 33% YoY).
*   **Customers Served:** 12.25 million (up 26% YoY).
*   **Off-book Lending Share:** Increased from 49.7% to 53.6%.
*   **Gross NPA:** 2.25%; **Net NPA:** 0.36%.
*   **Collection Efficiency:** 96.82%.

### Highlights
*   **Capital Position:** Net worth grew to Rs 2,245 crore; capital adequacy in the NBFC subsidiary rose to 40.2% (from 25.3% in Q4 FY26), bolstered by the May 2026 IPO.
*   **Product Strategy:** Loan Against Property (LAP) AUM stands at Rs 617 crore (7.7% of total AUM) across 101 branches. The company has secured AMFI registration to enter mutual fund distribution.
*   **Technology:** The company utilizes transformer-based AI models for underwriting, achieving an AUC of 74% (up from 66% in 2023).
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Opex as a proportion of AUM improved to 18.0% (from 19.9%).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management remains confident in exceeding the previously stated FY27 AUM growth guidance of 40%.
*   **Credit Cost:** Management reiterated guidance for a 15% reduction in credit cost.
*   **Profitability:** Management remains committed to delivering an ROE of 20%+ and ROA of 5%+.
*   **Cost of Funds:** Management expects a reduction of at least 100 bps in the cost of borrowing in H2 FY27 as old debt runs off.
*   **LAP:** The LAP business is expected to achieve breakeven around Q3 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro:** Management noted the Indian economy remains steady. While headline asset quality in the industry appears benign, management flagged stress in small-ticket loans and among borrowers with multiple lenders.
*   **Sourcing:** Organic channel contribution to customer sourcing reached 31% in Q1 (vs. 27% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Strategy:** The company is deliberately acquiring higher-quality customers, which leads to lower revenue margins but better risk-adjusted returns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Revenue Margin:** Total income as a share of average AUM fell 249 bps QoQ, attributed to a shift toward higher-quality, lower-priced customers and an increased share of off-book lending.
*   **Cost of Funds:** Incremental debt raised in Q1 came at a loaded cost of ~12.9%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Geographic Footprint:** The company serves 17,000 pin codes. Last quarter, it paused lending in 450 pin codes; it has since resumed lending in 180 of those (40%) due to improved credit outlooks.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Funding Mix:** 46:54 split between on-book (Rs 3,716 crore) and off-book (Rs 4,284 crore) AUM.
*   **Provisioning:** Conservative approach maintained with Stage-3 ECL coverage at 84.1% and Stage-2 coverage at 80.4%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **External Factors:** Management is monitoring West Asia for potential second-order impacts (oil prices, shipping costs) and the Reserve Bank's stance on FCNR(B) deposits.
*   **Underwriting Adjustments:** The company has tightened underwriting for specific salaried profiles (due to potential employment loss) and self-employed segments showing banking credit volatility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Margin Compression:** Management clarified that revenue margin reduction is a deliberate strategy to acquire high-quality customers; they expect operating leverage and lower cost of funds to offset this, maintaining 20%+ ROE.
*   **On LAP Breakeven:** Management stated LAP branches take 7-8 months to break even, faster than the industry average, due to 40% of LAP customers being sourced from the existing personal loan base.
*   **On Asset Quality/Bounce Rates:** Management explained that while Q1 often shows seasonal bounce rate increases, their recent vintages (post-December 2025) are performing better, justifying the reopening of paused pin codes.
*   **On Future Products:** Management identified a pecking order for future products (Gold loan, Business loan, Education loan) but emphasized they are currently focused on scaling Personal Loans and LAP.

### One-line takeaway
Kissht delivered strong AUM and profit growth while successfully pivoting toward higher-quality customers, with management expressing high confidence in meeting FY27 guidance through operating leverage and risk-adjusted pricing.
