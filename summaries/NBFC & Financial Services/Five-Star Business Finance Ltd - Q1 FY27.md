# Five-Star Business Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-31 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted record disbursements, strong collection efficiency, and a return to a growth trajectory after previous challenges.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursements:** INR 1,496 crore (up 23% QoQ, 16% YoY).
*   **AUM:** INR 13,722 crore (up 4% QoQ).
*   **PAT:** INR 271 crore.
*   **Net Worth:** INR 7,653 crore.
*   **Collection Efficiency (Unique Customer):** 97.9% (vs 98.1% in Q4 FY26).
*   **x-bucket Collections:** 99.2% (vs 99.3% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Current Book:** 83.30% (vs 82.69% in Q4 FY26).
*   **30+ DPD Book:** 12.38% (vs 12.69% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Credit Cost:** 1.85% (vs 1.88% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Cost of Funds:** 8.80% (vs 8.95% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Active Loan Customers:** 500,000.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Performance:** Achieved historical best quarterly disbursements.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Added 12 branches (largely in Maharashtra), total 856 branches.
*   **Productivity:** Segregation of business and collection verticals has improved productivity.
*   **Asset Quality:** Slippages remained flat at 0.70%. Management expects slippages, credit costs, and NPLs to trend downward in coming quarters.
*   **Recoveries:** Clocked INR 35 crore in recoveries (write-offs and NPA settlements) vs INR 26-27 crore in Q4.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management is confident of comfortably achieving full-year growth guidance.
*   **Credit Cost:** Guidance for FY27 is 1.7% to 1.9%, with a trend toward 1.7%. For FY28, management expects 1.6% to 1.7%.
*   **Opex:** Cost-to-asset ratio expected to stay at 5.75%–6% for this year; steady-state target of 5.25%–5.5% from FY28 onwards.
*   **Borrowing Cost:** Incremental cost of borrowing expected around 8.5%.
*   **Yields:** Expects a further 10-15 bps contraction in yields over the next couple of quarters, settling around 22.25%.
*   **Write-offs:** Expects INR 225–250 crore for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains strong in the segment.
*   Management noted that gold loans are growing, but the pace has slowed compared to 6 months ago due to price corrections.
*   Microfinance overlap has trended down from 20-21% to 16-17%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Spreads:** All-inclusive borrowing cost was 8.33%. Spreads increased by 3 bps QoQ.
*   **Employee Costs:** Opex growth for the year is expected to be 20-21% YoY due to competitive employee compensation and incentives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Plans to continue adding 50-60 branches this year.
*   Management confirmed plans to introduce 2-3 new products, with one likely to launch in the next 3-6 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Leverage:** Targeting a debt-to-equity ratio of 2x in the medium term (6-8 quarters).
*   **Liquidity:** Holding ~INR 1,850 crore on the balance sheet.
*   **Provisioning:** Maintaining overall ECL coverage at 1.75%–1.8%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Energy Costs:** Potential for rising energy costs to impact customer cash flows.
*   **Regulatory/Macro:** Potential repo rate increases could negatively impact borrowing costs.
*   **Over-leveraging:** Management remains cautious about potential over-leveraging crises, though they believe current guardrails are strong.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Yield Compression:** Management clarified that yield contraction is primarily driven by interest reversals on NPAs, not just competitive pricing.
*   **On Operating Leverage:** Management stated that operating leverage will not kick in this year due to higher employee costs; it is expected from FY28 onwards.
*   **On New Products:** Management confirmed that new product launches will be additive to the current 20% growth guidance.
*   **On Debt-to-Equity:** Management clarified that reaching 2x leverage will take 6-8 quarters and depends on growth in existing and new products.
*   **On June Disbursements:** Management disclosed June disbursements were INR 670 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Five-Star delivered a strong Q1 with record disbursements and improving asset quality, signaling a successful return to growth while maintaining a conservative stance on credit costs and provisioning.
