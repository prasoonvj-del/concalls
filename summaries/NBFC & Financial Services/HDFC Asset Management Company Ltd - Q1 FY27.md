# HDFC Asset Management Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-17 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong AUM growth (13% YoY) and healthy equity inflows, with successful expansion into alternatives, despite some volatility in debt segments.

### Key numbers
*   **QAAUM:** INR9.35 trillion (up 13% YoY).
*   **Equity-oriented QAAUM:** INR5.74 trillion (up 16% YoY).
*   **Revenue from operations:** INR11 billion (up 14% YoY).
*   **Profit after tax (PAT):** INR8.4 billion (up 12% YoY).
*   **Operating profit growth:** 10% YoY.
*   **Operating margin:** 35 bps of AUM.
*   **Systematic transactions (SIP+STP):** INR48.1 billion (up 20% YoY).
*   **Alternatives AUM:** INR148 billion (up from INR60 billion YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Market Share:** 11.2% overall; 12.4% excluding ETFs.
*   **Equity Orientation:** 65.7% of QAAUM (vs 56.6% for the industry).
*   **Investor Base:** 17.1 million unique investors (28% industry penetration).
*   **Alternatives Expansion:** Closing a private credit fund this quarter; received approval for a second VC/PE fund, seeded with $50 million by a global investor.
*   **New Product:** Board approved first SIF (Systematic Investment Fund) offering: H-SIF equity ex top 100 long-short fund.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management remains constructive on long-term opportunities, citing early stages of financialization of savings.
*   Aims to be a "one-stop solution" across active/passive, MF, PMS, AIF, and private credit.
*   Operating margin target: Management aims to stay within the 33-35 bps range.
*   Non-cash ESOP cost guidance: FY27: INR79-80 crore; FY28: INR63 crore; FY29: INR41 crore; FY30: INR11 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry QAAUM:** INR83.1 trillion (up 15% YoY).
*   **Industry Equity AUM:** >INR47 trillion (up 16% YoY).
*   **Industry SIPs:** INR318 billion in June 2026 (up 17% YoY).
*   Management views SIP flows as structural, not just cyclical, comparing them to India's "401(k) movement."
*   Fintechs registered 8.6 million new SIPs in the quarter.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Total cost:** INR2.7 billion (vs INR2.1 billion in Q1 last year).
*   **Yields:** Equity (58 bps), Debt (28 bps), Liquid (13 bps).
*   Management noted the shift from TER to BER (Total Expense Ratio to Base Expense Ratio) and removal of 5 bps exit load incentive necessitated commission structure optimization.
*   CSR expenditure is lumpy and not evenly spread throughout the year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Hired senior resources for PMS and SIF efforts.
*   Investment team size: 37 (MF), 6 (PE/VC), 6 (Private Credit), 8 (PMS).
*   Focus remains on investing in people and digital/AI capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Treasury surplus:** ~INR8,000 crore (invested primarily in debt).
*   **Other income:** INR2.6 billion (largely MTM gains on treasury).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management remains watchful of investor behavior in an extended market downturn, noting this cohort has not yet experienced one.
*   **Debt segment:** Significant outflows due to interest rate and currency volatility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why the decline in debt AUM?* A: Management attributed it to interest rate/currency volatility and global factors, noting investors are redeeming from debt but moving into liquid funds.
*   *Q: Is the yield increase sustainable?* A: Management stated they are in a new regime (TER to BER) and advised not to read too much into quarterly movements; focus is on maintaining margins.
*   *Q: Are fintechs gaining share over banks?* A: Fintechs have grown significantly in SIPs; banks are still growing but their relative share is impacted by the rapid rise of fintech channels.
*   *Q: Why are mid/small-cap flows dominating?* A: Driven by historical outperformance and the fact that 75%+ of net flows are SIP-driven, which are long-term in nature.
*   *Q: Is the ESOP expense accelerated?* A: Management clarified the non-cash cost guidance for FY27-FY30 and noted the Q1 expense is consistent with the plan.

### One-line takeaway
HDFC AMC delivered steady growth in AUM and profitability while successfully navigating regulatory changes (TER to BER) and expanding its alternatives platform.
