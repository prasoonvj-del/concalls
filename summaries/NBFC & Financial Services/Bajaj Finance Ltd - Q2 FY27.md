# Bajaj Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Excellent performance across all key metrics including AUM, customer acquisition, credit costs, profits, ROA, and ROE.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** INR 5,84,624 crores (up 26.5% YoY, net AUM addition of close to INR 38,000 crores).
- **PBT:** INR 8,537 crores (up 29% YoY; grew 34% to INR 8,874 crores before macro overlay provision).
- **PAT:** INR 6,367 crores (up 29% YoY; grew 34% to INR 6,619 crores before macro overlay provision).
- **New loans booked:** Just below 13.5 million (up 11% YoY).
- **New customers added:** 4.42 million (total customer franchise at just below 129 million).
- **Cross-Sell franchise:** 82 million.
- **Cost of funds:** 7.31% (down 9 bps QoQ).
- **Deposit book:** INR 14% of borrowings.
- **Opex to total income:** 33%.
- **ROA / ROE:** 4.6% / 20.6%.
- **GNPA / NNPA:** 1.06% / 42 bps.
- **PCR:** 60%.
- **Loan loss to average AUF:** 1.56% (improved from 1.88% YoY; 1.32% excluding macro overlay).
- **Macroeconomic provision created:** INR 337 crores in Q2 (total macro provision at INR 226 crores; management overlay at INR 512 crores).
- **Gold loan branch network:** 2,022 branches across 3,000 branches target.

### Highlights
- Urban consumer finance grew 43%, rural consumer finance 63%, MFI 54%, CV and Tractor 89%, LAS 42%, and MSME 3%.
- Captive book is down to approximately INR 2,400 crores.
- Gold loan portfolio crossed INR 24,000 crores (4.1% of balance sheet); 320 gold loan branches added in Q2.
- Board approved INR 11,700 crores via QIP and preferential warrants by Bajaj Finserv to the tune of INR 5,800 crores, driven by growth and maintaining medium-term ROA/ROE goals.
- FINAI transformation scaled AI unit to 328 people; facial recognition deployed in 534 stores and 1,163 service branches.
- Bajaj Housing Finance Limited (BHFL) disbursements and AUM grew 25%, Sambhav affordable loans crossed INR 500 crores in monthly disbursements, opex-to-NTI improved to 19.1%, and PAT grew 18%.
- Bajaj Financial Securities Ltd (BFSL) PAT grew 36% and added 121,000 customers.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-year AUM/Loan Growth:** Management expects 26% to 27% growth for FY27; medium-term growth guidance maintained at 20% to 25%.
- **New Loans (FY27):** 60 million to 62 million.
- **New Customers (FY27):** 18 million to 20 million.
- **Gold Loan Network:** Close to 2,800 standalone branches by the end of FY27.
- **Opex to NTI:** 25 to 40 bps improvement expected in FY27.
- **Credit Costs:** FY27 expected to be one of the lowest years on credit costs.
- **NIM:** Marginal moderation expected due to rising cost of funds, offset by opex and credit costs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Secular growth across all business lines.
- Consumer debt-to-GDP continues to move forward slowly; management is proactively reducing 3PL (triple point leverage) origination from 7% down to 3.5% over 6 months to curb default risks.
- Maharashtra is noted as most impacted by monsoon/drought conditions, prompting pruning in certain rural/semi-urban districts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of funds came down sequentially by 9 bps to 7.31% due to interest rate hedging, though repo rate hikes pose headwinds for H2.
- Opex-to-NTI improved to 33%.
- Loan losses and provisions declined YoY by 12% (excluding macro overlay).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 320 gold loan branches in Q2 (total 2,022); target 2,800 standalone branches by year-end.
- Continued investments in MFI branches and technology transformation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity buffer strong at just below INR 20,000 crores.
- Stage 2 and Stage 3 assets combined improved from 1.9% to 1.88% (excluding gold loans).
- Stage 2 increase of INR 1,047 crores in gold loans due to short-tenor schemes (which were stopped in August; INR 550 crores already resolved back to Stage 1).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic factors like El Nino (monsoon at 87%) and oil prices hovering at USD 100-110 pose inflationary and interest rate risks, prompting management overlay provisions.
- Short-tenor gold loan schemes created accidental customer delinquencies, leading to a shift back to 12-month tenure options.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Piran Engineer (CITIC International) on gold loan short-term vs long-term schemes:** Management explained that 3-month short-tenor bullet loans caused cumbersome branch visits and accidental delinquencies, prompting a shift to 12-month schemes with flexible interest payment options.
- **Chintan (Autonomous) on MSME asset quality and leverage:** Management stated MSME GNPA is reflecting flat denominator growth and will peak, while 3MOB/6MOB metrics remain robust; 3PL leverage is being actively halved.
- **Kuntal Shah on macro/management overlays:** Management clarified overlays are for balance sheet resilience against macro volatility (oil prices, monsoons) and are maintained over and above ECL models.
- **Kunal Shah on NIM and growth guidance:** Management guided for 26%-27% growth for FY27 and noted marginal NIM moderation due to rising cost of funds.
- **Viral on fee income and gold loans:** Management stated long-term fee income growth is guided at 15%-18%, and gold loans prioritize profitability and customer interest over pure AUM chase.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Finance reported an excellent Q2 FY27 with 26.5% AUM growth, robust profitability, and proactive capital raising and overlay provisioning to fortify balance sheet resilience.
