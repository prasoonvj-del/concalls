# Network People Services Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported highest-ever quarterly income, strong YoY growth, scaling up of international/TSP deals, and major domestic wins while maintaining strong revenue guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 60%–70% (targeting Rs 342 crore for the year vs Rs 134 crore achieved in H1 FY27). Full-year EBITDA margin target discussed as ~25% for FY27 (vs ~30% mentioned in Q1 call).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted toward execution of massive multi-year contracts (e.g., 300+ bank central LOI order and Sri Lanka telecom deal) and expansion into DPDP/digital currency, alongside global execution.
- **New topics or risks:** Entry into DPDP (RegTech), digital currency orders, Vyapaar Saksham scheme evaluation, and purchase/OEM product inventory costs for turnkey projects in the Dubai subsidiary.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Acquired first major international telecom deal (execution starting Q4); IPO/preferential fund acquisition evaluation is ongoing and management expects to finalize/announce international acquisition within Q3-Q4.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 72.95 crore (up 50% YoY from Rs 48.68 crore, up ~20% QoQ from Rs 61.42 crore).
- **Consolidated Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 134 crore (up 60% YoY from Rs 83 crore).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Almost Rs 16 crore (up 17% YoY, up 15% QoQ).
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 13.21 crore (up 34% YoY, up 20% QoQ from Rs 11 crore [noted as Rs 11.4 crore in Q1 report]).
- **EPS (H1 FY27):** Rs 11.63 (up 34% YoY).
- **Segment Mix (Q2 FY27):** TSP revenue contributed 80% to 85% of overall business (compared to 15%–20% two years back).
- **Revenue Mix (Q2 FY27):** One-time/SaaS is ~20%–25%, License/AMC/recurring model represents the rest. One-time total revenue is <50%; recurring revenue is >50%.

### Highlights
- Won a central LOI for a single payment solution order valued in 10 digits (in INR), providing access to 300+ banks over 18 to 24 months with a 5-year deal length (execution cycle: 9 months).
- Secured first RegTech DPDP order from a private sector bank and first order in digital currency.
- International deal in Sri Lanka signed with a major telecom provider covering 3 deals in a single opportunity (multimillion-dollar value over 5 years); transaction fees in Sri Lanka estimated at ~15x higher per transaction than in India.
- Active discussions ongoing in over 10 countries (funnel of 5 to 15 prospective global deals).
- Hardware/OEM purchase costs of Rs 18 crore incurred within the global subsidiary for turnkey order execution.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Maintained 60% to 70% YoY growth (full-year revenue target of Rs 342 crore, requiring Rs 216 crore–Rs 226 crore in H2 FY27).
- **Margins:** EBITDA margin expected to gradually increase from 24% in H1 FY27 to around 25% for FY27, targeting 27%–28% in FY28.
- Management expects global business to deliver major upward cumulative growth in FY28, FY29, and FY30.
- AI deployment targeted to improve internal development/delivery productivity by ~40% by March.

### Demand & industry commentary
- High traction in global markets due to demand for mature Indian payment solutions; local international players lack comparable tech.
- UPI MDR implementation: NPCI/banks postponed post-October 15 rollout to avoid disruption during the festive season; management expects positive/incremental impact once finalized and adopted by banks.
- Credit on UPI: Solution is live, but bank adoption remains slow compared to private non-bank players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Turnkey project cost of goods sold (COGS) in Q2 FY27 was Rs 28 crore, including Rs 18 crore of hardware/OEM procurement in the international subsidiary to establish execution credentials.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acquisition status: Management is evaluating candidates to gain access to international accounts and consolidate domestic market; funded via existing funds (preferential/IPO proceeds), expected to finalize in Q3 or Q4 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- LOI for 300+ banks follows standard industry milestone billing (manpower, licenses, recurring fees), not typical slow government receivable terms.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Short-term EBITDA margin pressure due to client acquisition investments and lower-margin turnkey hardware fulfillment in overseas subsidiaries.
- Reliance on H2 FY27 for bulk of fiscal year revenue recognition due to execution timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Divyansh (Trinetra Asset Managers) asked about Sri Lanka deal scope and revenue break-up:** Management confirmed it is a multimillion-dollar 5-year deal involving license and transaction models (~15x India's per-transaction rate), with revenue starting in Q4 FY27; overall Q2 revenue has >50% recurring component.
- **Aman Soni asked about confidence in reaching H2 revenue targets (Rs 216-226 crore required):** Management reaffirmed H2 growth driven by completed project executions, closing of global discussions, and new POs, sticking to full-year guidance.
- **Hardik Gandhi (HPMG) asked about AI reverse engineering risk and acquisition timelines:** Management stated custom enterprise software is protected from open AI tool reverse-engineering; acquisition discussions using existing funds are near closure for global market access.
- **Swaraj Mehta (Valorem Advisors) asked about high COGS (Rs 28 cr) and Vyapaar Saksham scheme:** Management explained Rs 18 cr COGS in the subsidiary came from OEM/hardware turnkey items to build international credentials; Vyapaar Saksham scheme will open product deployment opportunities with banks.
- **Sajal Gupta asked about EBITDA margins and contract lock-in for global deals:** Management guided FY27 margins around 25% and FY28 margins at 27%-28%; client sticky nature and consultative revenue-share models make vendor replacement unlikely post-implementation.

### One-line takeaway
NPST delivered record quarterly revenue of Rs 72.95 crore and won a 10-digit INR central domestic bank-in-a-box LOI along with a landmark multi-year international telecom contract, keeping its 60%-70% FY27 revenue growth target intact.
