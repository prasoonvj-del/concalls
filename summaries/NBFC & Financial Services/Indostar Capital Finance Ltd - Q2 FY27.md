# Indostar Capital Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights improved asset quality, successful portfolio transition, and adherence to long-term growth targets.

### Key numbers
*   **Retail Disbursements:** Rs 1,399 crore (vs Rs 1,235 crore QoQ; Rs 927 crore YoY).
*   **Retail AUM:** Rs 8,459 crore (97% of total AUM).
*   **Net Total Income:** Rs 242 crore (vs Rs 190 crore YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 13 crore (vs Rs 11 crore YoY).
*   **Gross Stage 3 (Company):** 4.5% (vs 4.8% QoQ).
*   **Net Stage 3 (Company):** 2.4% (vs 2.5% QoQ).
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 36%.

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Quality:** Prime customers (CIBIL >725) increased to 85% of the portfolio (from 80% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Legacy Book:** Pre-Jan 2025 legacy portfolio reduced to 24% of AUM (down from 65% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Operational Expansion:** Field sales force increased 51% YoY to 1,868; branch network expanded to 475 branches.
*   **Management Overlay:** Management released the Rs 49 crore management overlay created in Q4 FY26, citing improved portfolio performance.
*   **Micro LAP:** AUM reached Rs 280 crore (up 177% YoY); 99.6% of the portfolio is current.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management maintains the target of 35% CAGR disbursement growth over 3 years.
*   **Profitability:** Management targets PAT of Rs 450 crore to Rs 500 crore in FY29.
*   **Credit Costs:** Management expects normalized credit costs of 2% to 2.5% for FY28 and anticipates significant reduction in credit costs starting Q4 FY27.
*   **ROAA:** Management targets 2% ROAA from H2 FY28.
*   **Micro LAP:** Management aims for Micro LAP to reach 10% to 15% of AUM in 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted the Indian economy remains resilient, with RBI revising FY27 GDP growth forecast to 7.1%.
*   Vehicle finance demand is supported by infrastructure execution and replacement demand.
*   NBFC vehicle finance credit grew 15.4% YoY as of August 2026.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **NIM:** 8.9%.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Weighted average cost of borrowing is 9.7%; incremental borrowing cost is 9.1%.
*   Management intends to pass on interest rate hikes to borrowers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Micro LAP launched in Bihar, UP, and West Bengal; management plans to launch in Karnataka and Madhya Pradesh in Q3/Q4.
*   Expansion is leveraging existing co-located branches, requiring no additional capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Liquidity stood at Rs 827 crore.
*   Direct assignment transaction of Rs 132 crore completed during the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro:** Inflationary pressures (CPI 4.82% in August 2026) and geopolitical developments.
*   **Rural Demand:** Weather-related challenges and uneven monsoon distribution created concerns around agriculture activity.
*   **Interest Rates:** MPC increased the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50%.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is NIM up only 10 bps QoQ despite a 100 bps increase in loan yields?**
    *   *Management:* Clarified that the 17.6% yield figure includes DA income and advised referring to Slide 40 for a P&L perspective.
*   **Q: Why release the Rs 49 crore management overlay now?**
    *   *Management:* Stated the portfolio absorbed diesel price increases well and delinquency trends have improved significantly, justifying the release.
*   **Q: Why is AUM accretion low despite high disbursement growth?**
    *   *Management:* Attributed the gap to write-offs of the legacy book and expects the gap to converge as the legacy book runs down.
*   **Q: How is the company balancing higher ticket sizes in Micro LAP with risk?**
    *   *Management:* Noted the median ticket size is Rs 9.5-10 lakh and they are growing cautiously; most loans remain below Rs 25 lakh.

### One-line takeaway
IndoStar is successfully transitioning its portfolio toward higher-quality retail assets, with management guiding for improved profitability and lower credit costs as the legacy book runs down.
