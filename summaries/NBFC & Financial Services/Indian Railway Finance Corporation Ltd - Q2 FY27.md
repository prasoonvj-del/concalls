# Indian Railway Finance Corporation Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY26-27

**Tone:** Positive – Management highlighted consistent double-digit PAT growth for five of the last six quarters and expressed confidence in the diversification strategy (IRFC 2.0) to sustain future growth.

### Key numbers
*   **H1 Revenue:** >20% YoY increase.
*   **H1 PAT:** >10% YoY increase.
*   **AUM Target:** ~Rs 5 lakh crore by end of FY27.
*   **WACC:** 6.55% (management claims this is the lowest among NBFCs).
*   **Hedging Cost:** 5.9% to 6.3%.
*   **Dividend:** Interim dividend of Rs 1.15 (vs Rs 1.05 in the previous year).
*   **ROCE:** ~14% (H1 YoY).
*   **ROTA:** ~5% (H1 YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Diversification:** IRFC is actively lending to non-railway entities within the railway ecosystem, including metros, ports, renewable energy, thermal power, and road linkages.
*   **Talent Acquisition:** Employee count doubled in the last year; leadership roles filled by industry veterans from REC, PFC, and PNB.
*   **Compliance:** Reported a "nil comment" report from C&AG for the third consecutive year.
*   **Strategy:** Management aims for a business mix of 60% pure Railways and 40% diversification by 2030.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Disbursements:** Management expects to surpass last year's disbursement figures in FY27.
*   **Future Pipeline:** Management projects that from FY28 onwards, for the next decade, IRFC will see disbursements of ~Rs 50,000 crore annually from Indian Railways.
*   **Performance:** Management expects H2 performance to be a "replica" of H1.
*   **AUM:** Management remains confident in reaching the ~Rs 5 lakh crore AUM target by the end of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Infrastructure Pipeline:** Management cited a massive infrastructure opportunity, including a >Rs 3 lakh crore Dedicated Freight Corridor (Dankuni to Surat, >2,000 km), seven high-speed rail corridors, and six freight lines (JV/PPP model).
*   **Market Size:** Total infrastructure pie estimated at >Rs 20 lakh crore.
*   **Role:** IRFC is acting as an aggregator, engaging with World Bank, multilaterals, and domestic players to fund large-scale projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Pure Railway business NIM is ~1.3% (Rs 0.35 margin on cost-plus). Non-railway business margins are 2x to 3.5x higher than the railway business.
*   **Margin Target:** Management targets a minimum margin of Rs 0.85–Rs 0.90 on diversified assets.
*   **Borrowing:** The company is increasing the share of 54EC bonds and ECB to manage costs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Borrowing Mix:** Term loans from banks comprise ~25% of the total mix.
*   **Prepayment:** Management reported no prepayment pressure, citing the absence of prepayment penalties, no floor price, and competitive interest rates as key factors.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Interest Rates:** Management acknowledged volatility in TONAR and Repo rates but stated they are managing this through ALM monitoring and flexibility in borrowing instruments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What are the top execution priorities?** A: Management is focused on disbursement targets in metros, ports, and renewables. They noted that IRFC is the sole financing arm for Railways and is well-positioned for the upcoming infrastructure pipeline.
*   **Q: Is there prepayment risk?** A: Management stated there is no prepayment pressure because they offer no prepayment penalties and maintain the lowest market rates.
*   **Q: Why the focus on AUM growth?** A: Management clarified they are not chasing AUM growth for its own sake but are prioritizing higher-margin diversified assets, which will drive PAT growth even if AUM growth is moderate.
*   **Q: How to improve share price/value?** A: Management advised investors to focus on fundamentals (consistent PAT growth) and noted that the company distributes ~40% of profits as dividends.

### One-line takeaway
IRFC is successfully executing its diversification strategy into higher-margin non-railway assets while maintaining a robust balance sheet and dividend growth, with management targeting an AUM of ~Rs 5 lakh crore by FY27.
