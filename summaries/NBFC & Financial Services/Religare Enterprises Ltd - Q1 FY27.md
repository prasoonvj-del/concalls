# Religare Enterprises Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While the insurance business (Care Health) delivered strong growth and market share gains, the proposed demerger scheme faced a setback with the RBI not acceding to the request, and the consolidated entity reported a net loss.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,358 crore (up 26% YoY from Rs 1,876 crore)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Negative Rs 46.9 crore
- **Religare Broking Total Income:** Rs 99.5 crore (up 7% YoY)
- **Religare Broking PAT:** Rs 7.5 crore (up 65% YoY from Rs 4.6 crore)
- **RFL Net Worth:** Rs 915 crore (Net owned funds: Rs 837.8 crore)
- **RFL Cash Balance:** Over Rs 600 crore
- **RFL Core SME Book:** Approx Rs 53 crore
- **RFL Collection Efficiency:** 98%
- **RFL NNPA:** 0.8%
- **RFL CRAR:** 238%
- **RFL PAT:** Rs 15 crore (vs Rs 17.9 crore YoY)
- **Religare Housing Finance AUM:** Rs 247 crore
- **Religare Housing Net Worth:** Rs 180 crore
- **Religare Housing GNPA / NNPA:** 4.4% / 3.3%
- **Religare Housing Net Loss:** Rs 5 crore
- **Care Health Insurance GWP Growth:** 37% YoY on full premium basis
- **Care Health Insurance Retail Growth:** 45% YoY on full premium basis
- **Care Health Insurance PBT (Ind AS):** Rs 163 crore (vs Rs 102 crore YoY)
- **Care Health Insurance Gross Opex Ratio:** 31.2% (improved by 100 bps YoY)
- **Care Health Solvency Ratio:** 1.58% as of June 30, 2026

### Highlights
- Completed critical leadership appointments across all four businesses (Srinivasan Karthik for RFL, Pavan Gupta and Pankaj Rathi for Religare Housing, Vijay Goel for Broking, Ajay Shah and Manish Dodeja elevated at Care Health).
- RBI conveyed that the request for approval regarding the demerger scheme has not been acceded to; management is engaging with the regulator.
- Care Health raised Rs 150 crore via a rights issue in Q1 FY27 and Rs 200 crore of sub-debt in August 2026.
- Religare Broking reported a strong rebound in profitability with PBT growing 53% YoY to Rs 10 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Care Health Insurance:** Management stated intent to beat the market growth and is committed to bringing the combined ratio down to 100% over the next two years. Management plans to maintain solvency around 1.7x.
- **Financial Services (RFL & Housing):** Management stated ambitions for a Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 15,000 crore book size backed by planned capital deployments of about Rs 250 crore further into housing and utilizing existing capital, aiming to commence business/lending in the next 3 to 4 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Affordable housing and retail credit growth remain robust with strong structural tailwinds.
- Health insurance sector benefited from GST tailwinds, delivering a 32% growth in retail health and 22.4% overall industry expansion. Industry-wide trust between hospitals and insurance companies is improving through NHCX adoption and proper codes of conduct.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Care Health Ind AS combined ratio improved by 30 bps YoY to 102.6%.
- Care Health debt yields remain consistent in the range of 7.2% to 7.3%.
- Religare Broking employee costs increased due to strategic hiring and annual salary revisions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Religare Broking is investing in its next-generation platforms, margin funding book, and third-party distribution.
- Care Health investment book increased by over Rs 800 crore compared to March 2026; investment leverage stands at 4.3x (expected to reduce to around 3.7x by year-end).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- RFL sits on cash of over Rs 600 crore and has a core SME book of ~Rs 53 crore due to legacy embargoes/turnaround.
- Out of Rs 1,500 crore earmarked for Care Health by promoters (REL and Kedaara), Rs 618 crore of proceeds have been collected through conversions up to August 2026, leaving Rs 881 crore due for conversion by March 2027.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- RBI declined the proposed demerger scheme.
- Care Health solvency ratio stood close to the regulatory minimum at 1.58% at the end of June 2026 prior to the August sub-debt raise.
- RFL's legacy NPA write-off recovery pool (~Rs 350 crore to Rs 400 crore) is a dwindling book with ongoing litigation and repossession complications.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Naresh Naiker (Systematix Shares) asked about the specific reasons for the RBI declining the demerger and alternative value-unlocking plans.** Management (Arjun Lamba) replied that the RBI letter was brief with no specific reasoning attached, and they are currently engaging with the regulator without looking at alternative plans yet.
- **Sarvesh Gupta asked why Care Health's combined ratio (102.7%) is higher than peers and why the consolidated accounts show a loss.** Management (Ajay Shah & Ambrish Jindal) explained that the higher combined ratio is due to upfront claims payment on corporate wellness books, and consolidated accounts show a loss because Ind AS 117 impacts are transitioning with a one-year forbearance at the Care Health standalone level.
- **Meet Bhuva asked about the AUM/growth targets for the financial services business given the current losses.** Management (Arjun Lamba) stated they have ambitions for a Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 15,000 crore book size supported by a total capital base of Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 2,000 crore.
- **Yash Matta asked about Care Health's solvency dropping to 1.58%.** Management (Ambrish Jindal) explained that sub-debt was planned for Q1 but delayed due to capital market turmoil; a Rs 200 crore sub-debt was raised in August 2026, and future capital actions will maintain solvency at 1.7x.
- **Lala Ram asked about remaining warrant conversions and timeline.** Management (Pratul Gupta) stated that Rs 618 crore has been collected out of Rs 1,500 crore, leaving Rs 881 crore due for conversion by March 2027.

### One-line takeaway
Religare Enterprises reported a mixed quarter marked by strong operational growth and capital raises in its star subsidiary Care Health Insurance, alongside a regulatory setback on its demerger scheme from the RBI and ongoing repair work in its financial services businesses.
