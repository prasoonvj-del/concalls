# Aye Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strongest-ever Q1 disbursement performance, six consecutive quarters of asset quality improvement, and robust profit growth.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 1,219 crore (+22% YoY).
*   **AUM:** Rs 7,324 crore (+28% YoY, +4% QoQ).
*   **Active Borrowers:** >6.7 lakhs (+38% YoY, +44,000 added).
*   **Gross NPA:** 4.49% (vs 4.77% in previous quarter, 4.6% YoY).
*   **Credit Cost:** 4.01% (improved 29 bps QoQ).
*   **NIM:** 15.9% (+20 bps QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 75 crore (+144% YoY).
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 42.4%.

### Highlights
*   Management reported the strongest-ever first-quarter disbursement performance.
*   AUM per employee increased 12% YoY.
*   Credit rating upgrade by India Ratings: Long-term rating to IND A+ (stable), Commercial Paper to IND A1+.
*   Management created a Rs 6 crore management overlay provision in Q1; total overlay on balance sheet is Rs 11 crore.
*   Foreign exchange movements moved to OCI from P&L starting this quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **AUM Growth:** Targeting 25% to 30% for FY27.
*   **Credit Cost:** Maintaining guidance of 3.5% to 4% for FY27.
*   **NIM:** Expected to remain flat.
*   **Branch Expansion:** Targeting 40-50 new branches (approx. 10% growth).
*   Management expects to refine guidance ranges by the end of H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Micro-MSME segment showing steady recovery with constructive demand for working capital.
*   Management views the market as significantly under-penetrated (only 2-3% penetrated).
*   Monsoon impact expected to be limited to Central/Southern India; North/Northeast expected to have normal/above-normal precipitation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Borrowing:** Weighted cost dropped to 10.78% (from 10.87% last quarter). Incremental borrowing cost is ~10.20%.
*   **Rating Impact:** Management expects the recent rating upgrade to reduce borrowing costs by 10-15 bps.
*   **Opex Ratio:** 8.9% in Q1; management aims for 8.25%-8.75% range for FY27.
*   **Portfolio Yield:** 22.4% (Mortgage ~23.5%, Hypothecation 27.5%-28%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Network:** 571 branches across 18 states and 3 UTs.
*   **Strategy:** Focus on deepening presence in existing markets rather than new geographies.
*   **Branch Splitting:** 44 branches split last year; strategy is to split branches once they cross Rs 20 crore AUM.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Leverage:** 3.15x.
*   **Capital Adequacy:** 42.4% (sufficient for 2-2.5 years of growth).
*   **Direct Assignment (DA):** 5% of AUM; strategy is to limit DA to 5-7%.
*   **ECLGS Book:** ~4.5%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management noted potential "imponderables" like monsoon deficits and geopolitical tensions (West Asia war), though these have moderated.
*   Residual stress from earlier vintages.
*   Higher opex compared to MFI peers due to credit underwriting and monitoring requirements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why maintain 3.5%-4% credit cost guidance if Q1 is already there?** A: Management noted this is a cross-cycle target; in good years, they create overlays (Rs 6 crore added this quarter) to buffer against future cycles.
*   **Q: Why is opex higher than MFI peers?** A: Management explained their model involves individual credit underwriting and monitoring, unlike group-based MFI models, which adds to costs but improves quality.
*   **Q: Strategy on Gold Loans/Fintech?** A: Management is surveying gold loans (potential 10% of portfolio in 3 years) but does not see digital fintech players as a major competitive threat due to the large, under-penetrated market.
*   **Q: Why not do more co-lending?** A: Management stated they have no liquidity issues and the small ticket size (Rs 1-1.5 lakhs) makes co-lending economics unfavorable as it does not defray processing costs.

### One-line takeaway
Aye Finance delivered a robust Q1 with strong disbursement growth and improving asset quality, supported by a healthy capital position and a clear strategy to deepen penetration in the under-served micro-MSME segment.
