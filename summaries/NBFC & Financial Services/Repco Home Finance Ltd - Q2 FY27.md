# Repco Home Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong disbursement growth crossing Rs 1,000 crore, improvements in asset quality with gross NPA at 2.4% (a 16-year low), and stable return metrics.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 16,425 crore (up 9% to 9.2% YoY)
- **Sanctions (Q2):** Rs 1,359 crore vs Rs 1,205 crore YoY
- **Disbursements (Q2):** Rs 1,205 crore vs Rs 1,069 crore YoY
- **Gross NPA:** Rs 396 crore or 2.4% (vs Rs 475 crore last September; Rs 427 crore in June 2026)
- **Stage 2 Assets:** Rs 1,154 crore vs Rs 1,324 crore YoY
- **Provision Coverage Ratio:** 66%
- **Borrowing Cost:** 8.35% (overall); 86% sourced from 19-20 banks
- **NHB Refinance:** Rs 600 crore sanctioned for current financial year (Rs 106 crore availed in Q1, Rs 125 crore in Q2)
- **Yield on Assets (Half-year):** 11.83% (Salaried: 11.25%, Non-salaried: 12.07%, Housing loans: 11.07%, Non-housing loans: 13.32%)
- **NIM:** 5.44%
- **Spread:** 3.47% (management expects stable spreads around 3.25% to 3.3%)
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 116 crore vs Rs 114 crore in Q1 (vs close to Rs 106 crore last September); H1 PAT stood at Rs 230 crore
- **ROA:** 2.9%
- **ROE:** 12.5%
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** Close to 37%
- **Credit Cost:** 0.1%
- **Branch Network:** 242 branches including satellite centers

### Highlights
- Completed the building of a strong base via technology revamp (Core and mobile applications, integration with CERSAI and CIBIL) and verticalization (separate verticals for sales, recovery, collections, operations, credit, and SARFAESI management).
- Q1 disbursements were impacted by a transfer and promotion exercise, which normalized in Q2 as employees settled into their roles.
- Board approved an interim dividend of Rs 5 per share (face value Rs 10).
- *Pattern Check:* Unlike past trends where Q3 disbursements were typically lower than Q2, management expects strong organic sequential growth in Q3 and Q4 driven by independent sales channels and DST/BSM scaling.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Disbursements:** Q3 FY27 guidance is Rs 1,300 to Rs 1,350 crore; Q4 guidance is Rs 1,650 to Rs 1,700 crore (totaling ~Rs 3,000 to Rs 3,050 crore for H2). October disbursements are in line with expectations.
- **Asset Quality:** Internal target is to bring gross NPA down below 2% by the end of FY27.
- **Credit Cost:** Expecting provisions charged to P&L to be Rs 5 to 6 crore per quarter for the current and next quarters.
- **Profitability:** Management expects full-year PAT to be around Rs 480 to Rs 490 crore.
- **Spreads:** Expected to stabilize around 3.25% to 3.3% due to borrowing cost increases and rate concessions given to existing good customers to arrest BT Out.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Book mix: Non-salaried accounts for 53.6%, salaried for 46.4%; housing loans account for 71%, non-housing loans for 29%. Zero exposure to builder loans.
- Average ticket size incrementally is Rs 23 to 25 lakh; overall book average is Rs 14 lakh. Tamil Nadu accounts for 57% of the book, with non-TN share expected to rise.
- No stress observed in the South (including Karnataka), and cheque return ratios remain stable.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Opex/salary cost increased due to incentive payouts for combined Q1/Q2 performance, ad hoc retirement valuation adjustments (gratuity and leave encashment), annual performance increments (5% to 10%), and higher sourcing/marketing expenses (DSA payouts) following higher disbursements.
- Cost of funds is expected to rise by 10 to 15 bps following RBI rate hikes, which management plans to pass on to borrowers with a one-quarter time lag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened ~50 branches over the last 2-3 years; plans to open another 13 to 14 branches this year, majority in non-TN regions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Raised Rs 75 crore via commercial papers at 7.6% and completed a pass-through certificate (PTC) worth Rs 74 crore at 7.9% in September.
- Entire asset and liability book is floating (except for special rate NHB borrowings).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Maintaining a 3.4% spread will be challenging given rising borrowing costs and rate concessions required to arrest BT Out.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhijit Tibrewal (Yes Securities):** Asked why historical trends of lower Q3 disbursements are breaking, and about repo rate sensitivity. Management replied that independent sales and credit verticals built over 2.5-3 years are driving organic growth, and liabilities are largely floating with a 1-quarter lag to pass on rate hikes.
2. **Harshit Toshniwal (Equirus):** Asked about the sustainability of growth and reasons for leadership changes (deputation of Mr. Vaidyanathan vs Mr. Kannan). Management clarified that Vaidyanathan's withdrawal was routine administrative action by Repco Bank due to their own service needs, and operations maintain an arm's length distance from the promoter.
3. **Saikiran Pulavarthi (Individual Investor):** Inquired about drivers of asset quality improvement and potential credit rating upgrades. Management attributed recovery to verticalization and specialized collection roles, noting that bringing gross NPA below 2% could trigger rating upgrades and lower borrowing costs.
4. **Sanjana Sivaram (DAM Capital):** Asked about spread expansion and drought/stress exposure in Maharashtra or Karnataka. Management stated no stress has been observed in urban/periphery Tier-1 and Tier-2 loans, and spreads will moderate to 3.25%-3.30% due to lagged cost pass-throughs and BT-out retention concessions.

### One-line takeaway
Repco Home Finance delivered a healthy Q2 FY27 with AUM reaching Rs 16,425 crore, gross NPA dropping to a 16-year low of 2.4%, and aggressive double-digit quarterly disbursement guidance backed by a revamped operating structure.
