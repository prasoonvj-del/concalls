# EAAA India Alternatives Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in fee-paying AUM (28% YoY) and PAT (35% YoY), supported by successful fundraising and consistent realization of assets.

### Key numbers
*   **Fee-paying AUM:** Rs 49,164 crore (as of September 30, 2026).
*   **H1 Total Income:** Rs 616 crore (+35% YoY).
*   **H1 Management/Advisory Fee:** Rs 333 crore (+24% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 169 crore (+35% YoY).
*   **Q2 PAT:** Rs 88 crore (+28% YoY, +9% QoQ).
*   **ROE:** 29.5% (up from 25% a year ago).
*   **Dividend:** Rs 7 per share (140% of face value).

### Highlights
*   Fee-paying AUM grew at a 23% CAGR on a 3-year rolling basis.
*   Raised ~Rs 20,000 crore in H1 FY27 (Rs 8,250 crore in flagship funds; Rs 12,000 crore in listed InvITs).
*   Realizations: Collected >Rs 6,300 crore across strategies in H1.
*   Infrastructure Fund 1 exited in H1, completing its lifecycle.
*   *Record Note:* The call confirms the strong growth trend noted in the Nov 11 results, with management emphasizing the 3-year CAGR as the primary metric for business performance.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management aspires for 20-30% growth in fee-paying AUM and PAT over the medium to long term.
*   Targeting PAT yields of 65-70 bps on fee-paying AUM.
*   Expects Variable Additional Returns (VAR) to contribute 25-30% of overall PAT.
*   Aims for 30% ROE and 40-50% dividend payout ratio.
*   Fundraising pipeline: Active across Special Situations, Infrastructure, Commercial Real Estate, and Private Equity (PE Fund 2 expected to be 3x the size of Fund 1).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Indian alternatives industry is underpenetrated (4% of GDP vs 30% in North America).
*   Yield and income strategies are projected to grow to 42% of the industry (from 33%).
*   Drivers: Rise of private wealth, institutionalization of commercial real estate, and demand for capital in private companies.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Cost-to-income ratio steady at 64-65%.
*   Distribution cost: ~30 bps (amortized over fund life; 1/3rd upfront, 2/3rd over trail period).
*   PAT yields stable at ~0.71-0.72% of fee-paying AUM.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Acquired TOT-19 road bundle (Rs 2,259 crore) and a 1 million sq. ft. Grade A office park in Pune.
*   Sekura (in-house operating platform) has 66+ domain specialists.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Total AUM: Rs 95,000 crore (includes dry powder).
*   Dry powder: Estimated Rs 20,000-25,000 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management noted that short-term costs of maintaining in-house capabilities (Sekura) may appear higher but are a long-term strategic advantage.
*   Q1 FY27 included one-time impact of amortizing deferred distribution costs due to the early exit of Infrastructure Fund 1.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Goldman Sachs (VAR visibility):** Asked about VAR visibility. Management: High visibility due to non-equity correlated nature and contracted cash flows of underlying assets.
*   **Goldman Sachs (Distribution costs):** Asked about distribution cost amortization. Management: Confirmed 1/3rd upfront, 2/3rd trail; no VAR sharing with distributors.
*   **Motilal Oswal (Unit economics):** Asked about unit economics differential between real assets and credit. Management: Stated they focus on net-of-cost returns and unit economics are similar across strategies.
*   **Analyst (Management fee lag):** Asked about management fee growth lag vs AUM growth. Management: Explained it's due to timing of fund raises/exits and daily computation; expects growth to track AUM growth.

### One-line takeaway
EAAA India Alternatives is delivering strong growth in fee-paying AUM and PAT, leveraging a 17-year track record and a robust pipeline of yield-focused alternative assets.
