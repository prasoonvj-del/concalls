# Bajaj Finserv Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Strong consolidated performance driven by robust lending growth and expanding life insurance margins, offsetting general insurance claim headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 43,416 crore, up 17% YoY.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 5,952 crore, up 25% YoY (attributable to owners: Rs 2,758 crore, up 23% YoY).
- **Bajaj General Insurance:**
  - Gross Written Premium (GWP): Rs 7,056 crore, up 10% YoY (up 21% YoY ex-crop and government health).
  - Net NATCAT Claims: Rs 75 crore; Underwriting Loss: Rs 115 crore (vs Rs 92 crore in Q2 FY26).
  - Combined Ratio: 104.5% (102% ex-NATCAT; 101.4% ex-NATCAT and 1/n impact).
  - PAT: Rs 420 crore, down from Rs 517 crore in Q2 FY26.
  - AUM: Rs 36,313 crore; Solvency Ratio: ~230%.
- **Bajaj Life Insurance:**
  - Retail Weighted Received Premium (RWRP): Rs 1,942 crore, up 5% YoY.
  - Value of New Business (VNB): Rs 512 crore, up 40% YoY.
  - New Business Margin (NBM): 21.6%, up 450 bps YoY (rolling 12-month NBM at 20.8%).
  - PAT: Rs 55 crore (vs Rs 13 crore in Q2 FY26); Solvency Ratio: 255%.
  - AUM: Rs 1,46,322 crore, up 11% YoY.
- **Bajaj Finance (BFL):**
  - AUM: ~Rs 5,84,000 crore, up 26% YoY.
  - PAT: ~Rs 6,367 crore, up 29% YoY; Net Total Income: Rs 15,974 crore, up 23% YoY.
  - Gross NPA / Net NPA: 1.06% / 0.42%; Loan Loss to AUF: 1.56%.
- **Bajaj Housing Finance (BHFL):**
  - AUM: Surpassed Rs 1.5 lakh crore, up 25% YoY.
  - Net Interest Income: Rs 1,055 crore, up 10% YoY; PAT: Rs 757 crore, up 18% YoY.
  - Gross NPA / Net NPA: 0.29% / 0.12%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Bajaj Markets to achieve quarterly breakeven in Q4 FY27.
- Bajaj Health is expected to consume <Rs 200 crore capital this year and reach full-year breakeven in FY29.
- Bajaj AMC is projected to breakeven at an AUM of Rs 90,000–1,000,000 crore (around FY29) with an additional capital requirement of ~Rs 300 crore.
- BFL expects to improve its Opex-to-Net Total Income ratio by 25 bps to 40 bps in FY27 via AI implementation.
- Transition of insurance entities to Ind AS accounting is scheduled for 1 April 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Insurance distribution consultation paper from IRDAI may cause short-term sector noise but will improve long-term industry structure.
- Health and motor segments in General Insurance grew 31% and 18% YoY, respectively (excluding crop/govt health).
- Industry-wide persistency dips observed in life insurance across specific cohorts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Life insurance NBM expanded 450 bps YoY to 21.6%, driven by a higher term mix (retail protection up 51% YoY and group protection up 81% YoY).
- General insurance combined ratio increased to 104.5% due to Rs 75 crore net NATCAT impact and higher tender-driven health NEP mix.
- BFL Opex-to-NTI ratio remained flat YoY at 33%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- BFL Board approved Rs 11,700 crore equity raise via QIP and Rs 5,800 crore preferential warrant issue to Bajaj Finserv.
- Bajaj Life expanded into GIFT City (Oct 2026) and commences retail distribution with IndusInd Bank in Q3 FY27.
- Group emerging business and ALTs capital requirements estimated at <Rs 2,000 crore over the next 18 months (~Rs 1,100 crore for ALTs sponsor capital).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Parent company holds a surplus cash capital of ~Rs 4,700 crore, sufficient alongside subsidiary dividend inflows to meet future entity investments.
- Solvency remains robust: Life Insurance at 255%, General Insurance at ~230%, BFL CAR at 20.3%, BHFL CAR at 21.2%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- General insurance PAT declined YoY due to lower equity capital gains, NATCAT losses, and Rs 50 crore capital reduction impact.
- Health loss ratios rose due to a larger proportion of tender-based business.
- Bajaj Finserv Health revenue de-grew due to business restructuring necessitated by RBI's revised NBFC conduct circulars.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mahek (Emkay):** *On high health loss ratios and slow agency channel growth in Life.*
  - *Management:* Health loss ratio reflects higher Net Earned Premium mix from tender business. Agency growth appeared muted due to a intentional pivot toward high-margin protection products (agency protection up 54% YoY).
- **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark):** *On potential regulatory hit to bank/credit attachment insurance business.*
  - *Management:* Credit attachment serves a critical risk-mitigation role for banks against NPAs. While draft distribution guidelines may adjust commissions, underlying customer demand and bank partnership value remain strong.
- **Nischint Chawathe (Kotak):** *On capital deployment plans across group businesses.*
  - *Management:* Capital requirements across Health, AMC, ALTs, and Reinsurance total <Rs 2,000 crore over 18 months, fully covered by current surplus capital (~Rs 4,700 crore) and incoming dividends.
- **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal):** *On life insurance Expense of Management (EOM) compliance.*
  - *Management:* Over a 3-year implementation window to FY29, product mix shifts, cost optimizations, and lower-cost renewal streams will allow comfortable compliance with proposed limits.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Finserv delivered strong Q2 growth with 25% PAT expansion, backed by solid lending metrics and expanding life insurance margins despite general insurance NATCAT impacts.
