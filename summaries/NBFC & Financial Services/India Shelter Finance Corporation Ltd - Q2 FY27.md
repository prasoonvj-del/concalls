# India Shelter Finance Corporation Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered profitable, quality-led growth with record disbursements, stable margins, and steady asset quality.

### Key numbers
- Gross AUM: Rs. 11,997 crores (26% YoY normalized growth)
- Disbursements: Rs. 1,126 crores (21% YoY growth)
- PAT: Rs. 146 crores (19% YoY growth; 25% excluding direct assignment transition)
- H1 PAT growth: 20% YoY (operational PAT normalizing for lower DA grew 28% YoY)
- Net Worth: Rs. 3,401 crores
- Collection Efficiency: 98%
- Gross NPA (Stage 3): 1.5%
- 30+ Delinquencies: 5.2%
- RoE: 17.3% (for the quarter; >17% for 6 consecutive quarters)
- RoA: 5.7% (for the quarter)
- Leverage: 3.1x
- Cost to AUM (Opex to AUM): 4%
- Cost to Income: 36%
- BT-Out rate: ~4%

### Highlights
- Delivered the highest-ever quarterly disbursements in the company's history at Rs. 1,126 crores.
- Digital sourcing disbursements increased from 4% in Q2 FY26 to 7% in Q2 FY27.
- Total ECL stood at Rs. 97 crores against a regulatory threshold of Rs. 57 crores; Stage 3 PCR at 25%.
- *Note on record:* The positive operational momentum and record disbursements confirm the robust Q2 FY27 results highlighted in the earlier investor deck.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated FY27 guidance:
  - Branch additions of 40-45 for the year.
  - Spreads of >6% in the medium term.
  - Credit cost of 40 bps to 50 bps (expecting ~50 bps for the full year).
  - Gross AUM growth of 25% to 30% (normalized around 25%-27%).
- Management expects H2 to outperform H1 in disbursements, collections, and credit costs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Affordable housing demand remains healthy, supported by urbanization, rising incomes, and PMAY.
- Self-employed customers form 75%-80% of the customer base.
- Demand and supply conditions remain strong in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Portfolio yield: 14.8% (stable QoQ).
- Finance cost / Cost of funds: 8.2% (stable QoQ); H1 marginal cost of borrowing at 8.1%.
- Net Interest Margin (NIM): Stable above 9%.
- 53% of the portfolio comprises variable (15%) and semi-variable (38%) rate products, providing repricing headroom.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 8 branches during Q2 FY27; on track to complete 40-45 branch additions in FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity position: > Rs. 700 crores with undrawn sanctions of > Rs. 1,400 crores; ALM positive across all buckets.
- Borrowing profile diversified across >30 counterparties; NHB share stable at 14%. Average borrowing tenure is >8 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delinquencies remain relatively higher in ticket sizes below Rs. 8 lakhs, prompting underwriting tightness and guardrails across ~700 identified pin codes.
- Draft IRDA regulations on insurance fees could impact non-interest income if implemented as-is, though management expects limited overall profitability impact.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kunal Shah (Citigroup)** asked about credit cost sustainability given H1 trends. Management stated H1 includes seasonality and a scheduled Rs. 6 crore write-off, and full-year credit costs will remain within the 40-50 bps guidance range.
- **Nischint** asked about the repricing timeline of the 53% variable/semi-variable portfolio. Management explained that semi-variable loans have a 3-year fixed period before turning variable, repricing gradually at 4%-5% per quarter starting Q4.
- **Akhil Gulecha** asked about the slow pace of branch openings in H1 (5 in Q1, 8 in Q2). Management clarified that H2 will see accelerated openings to meet the full-year target of 40-45 branches.
- **Sonal Gandhi** asked about co-lending status. Management stated co-lending is currently unlucrative due to RBI's CLM-1 TAT impacts and GST guidelines on excess interest spread, and they are not currently keen on it (management did not dodge).
- **Gaurav Purohit** asked about rating upgrades. Management stated rating agencies look for a 3-year vintage from the last upgrade, which will be achieved in upcoming quarters.

### One-line takeaway
India Shelter reported a robust Q2 FY27 led by record disbursements, stable margins, and strong asset quality, keeping its full-year growth and credit cost guidance intact.
