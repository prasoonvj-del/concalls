# WSFx Global Pay Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved record Q2 turnover and revenue with strong earnings growth despite a challenging market.

### Key numbers
- Turnover: Rs 2,914 crore (up 41.2% YoY, up 95.5% QoQ)
- Revenue: Rs 44.58 crore (up 30% YoY, up 91.9% QoQ)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): Rs 5.33 crore (up 9.6% YoY, 9.1x QoQ)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 4.26 crore
- H1 Turnover: Rs 4,405 crore
- H1 Revenue: Rs 67.81 crore
- H1 PBT: Rs 5.91 crore
- H1 PAT: Rs 4.56 crore
- Segment YoY Growth (Q2): Outward remittance turnover up 53.9%; Student segment turnover up 44.2%; Corporate turnover up 12.3%; Retail segment turnover up 19.1%
- Active base: 944 active corporates, 621 active agents, 26 branches (added Thane and Whitefield)
- Digital penetration: Around 70% (management aims for 90%)

### Highlights
- Recorded the highest-ever Q2 turnover and revenue, driven by strong seasonality and performance in the student segment. Crossed Rs 1,000 crores turnover in two separate months during the quarter.
- Received expanded, perpetual AD Category II license on July 30, allowing permissible non-trade and trade transactions up to Rs 25 lakh per transaction, and the appointment of forex correspondents (FXCs).
- Sunsetted bank partnership card programs and launched an in-house card distribution program, onboarding 22 regulated entity partners.
- Activated Nostro account and signed partnerships with Mastercard Move; card distribution went live.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic about the second half of the year, driven by the activation of newer solutions like trade remittances, forex correspondent rollout, and card distribution partnerships.
- Forex correspondent rollout is expected by November.
- Trade remittances are expected to go live by December or January.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Market conditions were challenging due to forex volatility, visa uncertainties, a dip in the US student market, and weaker discretionary travel caused by Middle East conflicts.
- Student segment growth was driven by UK and Australia, compensating for the US dip and offsetting the lack of broad segment growth.
- Corporate business has not reached pre-COVID levels, sitting at roughly 40% to 50%, but the company increased market share.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commission payout/expenses increased from 52% to 58% due to incremental business coming from B2B channels and strategic payouts to increase wallet share.
- Revenue grew 91.9% sequentially against expense growth of 29.4%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added two new branches (Thane and Whitefield) in H1; a couple more branches may roll out in the next 3 to 6 months.
- Expanding asset-light reach through the forex correspondent (FXC) framework to operate across smaller locations without physical infrastructure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Current financial liabilities and trade payables increased due to business growth and the settlement of liabilities following the launch of the in-house card program.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange is episodic and retail leisure travel faced headwinds due to geopolitical conflicts and advisory dips.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aniket (Individual Investor) on commission increases to 58%:** Management stated that higher payouts are part of the strategy to increase wallet share with partners, and rates depend on business potential.
2. **Aniket on trade remittance readiness and PA-CB license:** Management clarified they are pursuing FEDAI membership in November to operationalize SOPs, and will evaluate a PA-CB license later if needed.
3. **Lala Ram (Shareholder) on P&L impact of new models (FXCs and own cards):** Management stated that reach will expand significantly and asset-light growth will occur, though yields may be shared with partners.
4. **Lala Ram on trade remittance differentiation versus banks:** Management noted that Fintech advantages lie in convenience, service component, pricing, and technology, targeting smaller customers where banks may lack responsiveness.
5. **Aniket on remittance speed compared to banks:** Management highlighted that with Nostro accounts and Mastercard Move, transfers can happen within 2 to 3 hours or instantaneously (10 to 30 minutes), compared to waiting a full day.

### One-line takeaway
WSFx Global Pay delivered a record Q2 driven by student segment dominance and aims to scale multi-fold through newly acquired perpetual AD-II licenses, trade remittance activation, and asset-light forex correspondent expansion.
