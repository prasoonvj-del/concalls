# Regency Fincorp Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong AUM growth, surging revenues, improved profitability, and expansion into digital and secured lending.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 610.25 crores (as of 30th September 2026); secured loan book at Rs 452 crores (grew 7x YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 32.44 crores (up 240% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 9.01 crores (up 170.37% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 49.87 crores (up 164% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 16.3 crores (up 146% YoY).
- **NIM:** 7.54% in H1 FY27 (vs 3.51% in H1 FY26).
- **Asset Quality:** Gross NPA at 0.99%; Net NPA at 0.75% (as of September 2026).
- **Borrowings & Leverage:** Raised ~Rs 425 crores via NCDs and term loans in H1 FY27; total outstanding borrowings at Rs 505 crores; Capital Adequacy Ratio at 28.55%; Debt-to-Equity at 2.66x.
- **Guidance (FY27):** AUM target of Rs 900 crores to Rs 950 crores; PAT target of Rs 40 crores to Rs 42 crores or higher.

### Highlights
- Secured MSME cumulative disbursements reached ~Rs 252 crores across 2,366 active loans.
- Digital lending platform 'CashMySalary' recorded Rs 125 crores in disbursements with >87,000 active loans across 25 states and 15,000+ PIN codes in H1 FY27.
- Launched 'JetFund' (digital platform for instant salary advances and personal loans from Rs 20,000 to Rs 1 lakh).
- Board approved a proposed preferential issue of ~Rs 312 crores on 16th October 2026 to fund digital lending platform and gold loan business acquisitions, onward MSME lending, and general corporate purposes. Net worth projected to increase from Rs 190 crores to Rs 290 crores by March 2027 and Rs 615 crores by March 2028.
- Moved into a larger office in Noida and expanded internal team strength to 390 employees.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets AUM of Rs 900 crores to Rs 950 crores and PAT of Rs 40 crores to Rs 42 crores or higher by the end of FY27.
- Projects FY28 AUM at Rs 2,000 crores and FY29 AUM at Rs 3,000 crores.
- Targets blended borrowing cost reduction to 10.75%–11.25% by the end of FY27.
- Plans to maintain a portfolio split of ~70-75% secured MSME/gold loans and ~25-30% digital lending.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management sees strong growth opportunities for tech-enabled NBFCs addressing underserved MSMEs and retail borrowers due to India's expanding digital financial infrastructure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended cost of funds dropped 100 bps in the first 6 months to 12.35% (targeting 10.75%–11.25% by FY27 end).
- Operating leverage and cost management contributed to margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up a hub-and-spoke model for digital lending with potential centers in Bangalore, East, North, and West India, alongside branch network expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ALM shows no mismatch, with adequate cash flows to service lenders.
- Optimal leverage target is maintained between 3.0x to 3.5x debt-to-equity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management beyond standard underwriting discipline.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Shah (ACE Securities) on GNPA increase:** Management stated Gross NPA at 0.99% reflects strong underwriting, supported by asset-backed recoveries (LTV at 60%), and expects no unforeseen stress.
2. **Anirudh Sharma (Ekaant Investments) on cost of funds:** Management explained the drop of 100 bps in H1 to 12.35% and guided for another 100 bps reduction by March 2027.
3. **Vijay Ahluwalia on Regency Fin Technology subsidiary revenues and tech acquisition:** Management clarified that the subsidiary builds tech infrastructure with minimal profit retention, and the digital lending acquisition is an independent, arm's-length third-party transaction.
4. **Manoj Rajani on fundraise deployment and long-term AUM targets:** Management detailed that Rs 100-125 crores will fund the digital platform acquisition, ~Rs 75 crores for a majority stake in a gold loan business, and the rest for MSME lending and general corporate purposes; reaffirmed FY28/FY29 AUM trajectory.
5. **Mandira A on Jetfund and RegPay:** Management stated Jetfund is live with a target of ~Rs 50-55 crores AUM by March, while RegPay is awaiting RBI license approval expected this quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Regency Fincorp reported a robust H1 FY27 with a threefold YoY jump in AUM to Rs 610.25 crores, backed by strong MSME and digital lending growth, and announced a ~Rs 312 crore preferential issue to fund strategic acquisitions in digital lending and gold loans.
