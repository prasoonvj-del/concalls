# One 97 Communications Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted broad-based growth across business segments, expanding operating leverage, and successful AI-driven cost optimization.

### Key numbers
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8% for the quarter.
*   **Revenue Growth:** 28% YoY.
*   **Net Payment Margin:** 8.4 bps (down from 8.8 bps).
*   **Cash on Books:** Rs 13,500 crore.
*   **Device Deployment Target:** 25-30 lakh additions per year.
*   **Employee Cost:** 6.5% decline YoY.

### Highlights
*   **AI Integration:** Management is utilizing AI to optimize infrastructure (moving from 200 billion to 4 billion parameter models) and improve collection efficiencies.
*   **Board Changes:** Three new independent board directors appointed with expertise in technology, finance, and globalization.
*   **Business Focus:** Shift in strategy toward "monetization using wealth beyond our credit," specifically scaling brokerage and mutual fund distribution.
*   **Lending:** Merchant and personal loan businesses showing healthy growth; management remains committed to a distribution-only model without balance sheet risk.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management targets a 15-20% EBITDA margin in the medium term.
*   **Growth:** Management expects to continue aggressive investment in consumer and merchant expansion, noting that current growth momentum is sustainable.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Postpaid:** Management described the market opportunity as "massive" with strong product-market fit.
*   **UPI Monetization:** Management has no specific information regarding potential government policy changes on UPI charges and will "wait and watch."
*   **Fintech Ecosystem:** Management observed stress in the broader fintech ecosystem regarding private funding rounds and valuations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management noted that indirect revenues are growing at a slower pace than the business, leading to improved operating leverage.
*   **Payment Processing Costs:** Increased slightly due to higher card share and interchange costs associated with Paytm Postpaid.
*   **Employee Costs:** Despite appraisals and ESOPs, employee costs declined 6.5% YoY.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Device Deployment:** The company plans to continue adding 25-30 lakh devices annually.
*   **AI Infrastructure:** Significant focus on optimizing compute costs by building proprietary, efficient AI models.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Position:** The company holds Rs 13,500 crore in cash and is generating free cash flow.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Management is actively evaluating both organic and inorganic opportunities for cash deployment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **External Headwinds:** Management noted that ticket price increases in the travel and leisure sector have acted as a "headfire" (negative headwind) for that specific business segment.
*   **Regulatory Uncertainty:** Potential changes to UPI/MDR structures remain an external variable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Manish Adukia (Goldman Sachs) on long-term margins:** Asked about the path to 15-20% EBITDA margins. Management confirmed this is the medium-term target, driven by operating leverage and AI efficiencies.
*   **Sachin Salgaonkar (Bank of America) on UPI monetization:** Asked if potential UPI charges would be material. Management stated they are agnostic to MDR/incentive structures and focused on the bottom line.
*   **Vijit Jain (Citi) on AI and cloud costs:** Asked how AI impacts costs. Management explained that optimizing models from 200B to 4B parameters significantly reduced latency and compute costs.
*   **Pranav Kshatriya (Emkay Global) on GMV acceleration:** Asked if the acceleration is sustainable. Management confirmed that growth is broad-based and sustainable across offline and online segments.

### One-line takeaway
Paytm is prioritizing profitable growth through AI-driven cost optimization and diversified financial services, while maintaining a strong cash position to pursue strategic expansion.
