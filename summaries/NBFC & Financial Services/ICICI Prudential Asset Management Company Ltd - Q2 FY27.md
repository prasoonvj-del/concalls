# ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-16 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (ended September 30, 2026)
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong operating performance and market share retention, but PAT declined QoQ due to mark-to-market (MTM) impact on other income.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained ESOP cost guidance for the full year at Rs 64-68 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "rebound" in SIPs (Q1) to "stable" SIP inflows in Q2. Management noted increased redemptions in hybrid categories (BAF/MAF) and a shift in investor preference toward small/mid-cap strategies.
- **New topics/risks:** Board approved the acquisition of the PMS business from ICICI Securities Limited (approx. Rs 3,000 crore AUM).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the launch of Life Cycle Funds (2031, 2036, 2041) and other NFOs (Balanced Hybrid, Multi Asset Active FoF, etc.).

### Key numbers
- **Total Mutual Fund Quarterly Average AUM:** Rs 11.61 lakh crore (+14.4% YoY).
- **Operating Revenue:** Rs 1,674 crore (+16.3% YoY, +7% QoQ).
- **Operating Profit Before Tax:** Rs 1,181 crore (+15.7% YoY, +7.4% QoQ).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 889 crore (+6.1% YoY, -7.8% QoQ).
- **Operating Margins (H1 FY27):** 37.3 bps (vs 36.6 bps in H1 FY26).
- **Alternates Quarterly Average AUM:** Rs 80,965 crore.
- **Systematic Transactions (Sept 2026):** Rs 4,672 crore.
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 30 per share declared.

### Highlights
- Maintained 2nd largest AMC position with 13.3% market share.
- Leadership in equity/equity-oriented schemes (13.7% market share, Rs 6.59 lakh crore AUM).
- Largest market share in equity-oriented hybrid schemes (26.4%, Rs 2.29 lakh crore AUM).
- Launched NFOs: Balanced Hybrid Fund, Multi Asset Active FoF, Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FoF, BSE Insurance ETF, and three Life Cycle Funds.
- Unique customer base reached 1.78 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management plans to continue exploring the debt category for SIF (Specialized Investment Fund) launches at an appropriate time.
- Management intends to scale up real estate (pre-leased office funds) and private credit businesses.
- Management aims to scale up the newly acquired PMS business from ICICI Securities once regulatory approvals are received.
- Management expects to continue focusing on large-cap funds, citing better risk-reward compared to small/mid-caps.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry quarterly average AUM reached Rs 87.49 lakh crore (+13.4% YoY).
- Industry net flows for equity categories were Rs 1.06 lakh crore (approx. 7% reduction QoQ).
- Management noted that while gross flows remain consistent, net flows are impacted by elevated redemptions in hybrid categories (BAF/MAF).
- SIP inflows remained stable throughout Q2 FY27.
- Digital channels are driving customer acquisition in B30 (beyond top 30) towns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Annualized Margins (H1 FY27):** Equity (67 bps), Debt (32 bps), Liquid (12 bps), Passives (12 bps), Arbitrage (29 bps).
- **PMS/AIF Yields (H1 FY27):** Gross yield 1.92%, Net yield 0.96%.
- Operating expenses: Rs 493 crore (+17.9% YoY, +6.2% QoQ).
- Other income reduced QoQ due to MTM impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Board approved acquisition of PMS business from ICICI Securities Limited (approx. Rs 3,000 crore AUM).
- Expanding product bouquet with new SIF strategies; currently live with 4 strategies, planning a 5th.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or working capital concerns flagged.
- Investment book strategy remains conservative with higher allocations to debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Market volatility leading to elevated redemptions in hybrid schemes.
- International advisory business (assets under advisory) continues to face outflows; management is waiting for FII flows to return to India.
- Difficulty in tracking whether redemptions are switching to other funds or exiting the AMC entirely.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why the decline in PAT QoQ?** Management attributed it to lower other income due to MTM impact.
- **Q: Why are redemptions elevated?** Management explained it is a factor of market conditions, specifically in hybrid categories like BAF and MAF, rather than a decline in gross sales.
- **Q: What is the strategy for the acquired PMS business?** Management stated it will allow engagement with incremental new clients and offers complementary products.
- **Q: Why is the ICICI Bank channel AUM not growing?** Management stated they work equally across all channels (MFDs, banks, direct) and performance depends on the distributor's strategy.
- **Q: Any plans for infrastructure funds?** Management stated there are no current plans; focus is on scaling existing alternate categories (private credit, real estate, private equity).

### One-line takeaway
ICICI Prudential AMC maintained market leadership and grew operating profit by 7.4% QoQ, though PAT faced a 7.8% QoQ decline due to MTM-related volatility in other income.
