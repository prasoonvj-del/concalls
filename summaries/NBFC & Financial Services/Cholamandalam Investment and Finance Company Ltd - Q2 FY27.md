# Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong, broad-based growth across all business segments and improved profitability metrics.

### Key numbers
*   **Aggregate Disbursements:** Rs 35,872 crore (+47% YoY).
*   **AUM:** Rs 2,68,009 crore (+25% YoY).
*   **Vehicle Finance AUM:** Rs 1,29,787 crore (+21% YoY).
*   **SME Finance AUM:** Rs 72,356 crore (+27% YoY).
*   **Consumer Loan AUM:** Rs 45,902 crore (+32% YoY).
*   **Gross Stage 3:** 3.25% (vs 3.29% in June '26).
*   **GNPA (RBI norms):** 4.44% (vs 4.50% in June '26).
*   **ROE:** 19.4% (vs 18.1% YoY).
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 20.62% (Tier 1: 16.02%).

### Highlights
*   **Business Growth:** Consumer loan disbursements surged 103% YoY. SME finance disbursements grew 42% YoY.
*   **Profitability:** Credit cost reduced by 37 bps; PBT ROA expanded by 40 bps YoY.
*   **Capital Raise:** Board approved a proposal to raise up to Rs 5,000 crore to support future growth and maintain capital buffers.
*   **Opex:** Employee costs increased due to the transition of outsourced employees to the company payroll; management noted this was offset by a reduction in outsourcing expenses.
*   **Gold Loans:** Portfolio is ~99% bullet loans; management is expanding branch network to 480 by year-end.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management maintained full-year growth guidance of ~25%.
*   **Credit Cost:** Maintained full-year credit cost guidance of ~1.4%.
*   **Opex:** Expects Opex ratio to remain in the 3.0%-3.1% range in the near term, with a long-term goal of 2.8% over 2-3 years.
*   **Capital Raise:** Planned for the near to medium term (6-9 months), subject to market conditions.
*   **Seasonality:** Management expects the second half to be seasonally stronger than the first half.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Vehicle Finance:** Industry volumes remain robust, supported by replacement demand and healthy freight utilization.
*   **October Trends:** October growth is trending at ~18-20% YoY; management expects 20-22% growth in Q3.
*   **Drought Concerns:** Management stated that while some regions are drought-prone, sowing activity remains in line with last year; no meaningful impact on portfolio performance observed yet.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Funds:** Expected to increase by 15-20 bps YoY for the full year.
*   **Yields:** Management anticipates a modest decline of ~10 bps, expected to be offset by credit cost improvements.
*   **Borrowing Mix:** 25% repo-linked, 25% other market benchmarks, 50% fixed/MCLR.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Branch Expansion:** Continuing to convert Resident Location Hubs (RLHs) into full-fledged branches as they reach critical disbursement levels.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Total liquid assets of Rs 21,974 crore (including undrawn sanctioned credit lines).
*   **Assignment Deals:** Management stated they have largely stopped doing DA (Direct Assignment) income, except for one deal, and aim to keep 5-7% of the book as assigned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Potential impact of draft reforms on insurance commission income; management is evaluating scenarios and has alternative revenue levers.
*   **Macro:** Monitoring drought conditions and rainfall deficits, with a clearer assessment expected by December.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Capital Raise Strategy:** Management clarified the Rs 5,000 crore raise is for strategic flexibility and maintaining capital buffers, not an imminent need.
*   **Consumer Business Profitability:** Management expects the consumer business (CSEL/CD) to sustainably deliver >4% pre-tax ROA in the medium term.
*   **Opex Jump:** Management explained the Q2 opex increase was due to annual salary revisions and a one-time expense for an annual business meet in Australia.
*   **Gold Loan Structure:** Management clarified that 99% of the gold loan book is bullet loans, which helps keep delinquency lower compared to EMI-based products.
*   **Assignment Income:** Management confirmed they are moving away from DA income to avoid upfronting profits, aiming to keep assigned book at 5-7%.

### One-line takeaway
Cholamandalam delivered strong Q2 growth across all segments and maintained its full-year guidance, while proactively managing capital and opex to support long-term expansion.
