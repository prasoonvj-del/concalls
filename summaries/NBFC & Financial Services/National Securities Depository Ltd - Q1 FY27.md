# National Securities Depository Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-05 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27

**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted improved market share in demat additions and strong YoY income growth, despite margin moderation due to front-loaded strategic investments.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone:**
    *   Total Income: Rs 219.7 crore (+15.3% YoY).
    *   PAT: Rs 89.1 crore (+7.9% YoY).
    *   EBITDA Margin: 57.8%.
    *   PAT Margin: 40.6%.
*   **Consolidated:**
    *   Total Income: Rs 560.5 crore (+61.6% YoY).
    *   PAT: Rs 98.3 crore (+9.7% YoY).
    *   EBITDA Margin: 25.9%.
    *   PAT Margin: 17.5%.
*   **Operational:**
    *   Total Demat accounts: 4.56 crore.
    *   Incremental market share (net demat additions): 17.6% (vs 14% in Q4 FY26).
    *   Net demat additions: 12.4 lakh.
    *   Custody value: $5.7 trillion (INR 535 lakh crore).
    *   E-voting market share: 64% (vs 61% in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   "Yuva" and "Women's Demat" plans contributed 18-20% of incremental demat additions.
*   Enabled "SWAGAT-FI" framework for FPIs/FVCIs to simplify onboarding and reduce compliance burden.
*   Conducted 157 Investor Awareness Programs (IAPs) reaching 8,000+ participants.
*   Board approved a 20% stake investment in IIBH (subsidiary in GIFT City).
*   Appointed Subhash Kelkar as Executive Director, Critical Operations.
*   NSDL Payments Bank: Retail customers grew to 49.5 lakh (from 28.3 lakh in Q1 FY26).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects operating leverage to support normalized margins in the medium term as recent investments mature.
*   Management expects profitability and margins in the Payments Bank segment to gradually stabilize and improve as customers pivot to transactional banking services.
*   Management anticipates IPO activity to pick up for the remainder of the calendar year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Industry added 70 lakh new demat accounts in Q1 FY27 (vs 67 lakh in Q1 FY26).
*   Total industry demat count crossed 23.16 crore (+16.3% YoY).
*   FPIs were net sellers for the third consecutive quarter; DIIs remained strong buyers.
*   FinTech contribution to NSDL's incremental demat additions rose to ~20% (up from 2% a few quarters ago).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Standalone margins moderated due to front-loaded investments in technology resilience, cybersecurity, automation, and talent acquisition.
*   Payments Bank margins were impacted by upfront onboarding revenue sharing associated with a specific partner project.
*   Employee costs increased ~40% on a standalone basis; management stated hiring is largely complete and will be cautious going forward.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Capitalized Rs 7-8 crore in technology during Q1 FY27.
*   Added 6 new Depository Participants (DPs) in Q1 FY27.
*   Total service centers: 57,000+ across 2,000+ cities/towns.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical risks and crude price volatility (renewed hostilities in July).
*   Seasonality inherent in business (e-voting, dividend income).
*   Market conditions impacting future growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is the 40% employee cost increase the peak?** A: Management stated they are largely done with hiring for adequacy and will be cautious, though costs for recent hires will reflect in upcoming quarters.
*   **Q: Explain the banking revenue/margin impact.** A: A specific project involving card business caused a spike in onboarding revenue, which includes a cost-sharing arrangement with a partner. This is a one-time front-loading; transaction-based revenue will follow.
*   **Q: Why the heavy tech investment?** A: Driven by four themes: technology resilience (regulatory/market expectation), customer experience, automation (internal efficiency), and infrastructure refresh (end-of-life cycle).
*   **Q: Why the increase in FinTech share?** A: Result of multi-quarter efforts in relationship building, API launches, and positive word-of-mouth from back-office vendors.

### One-line takeaway
NSDL delivered steady YoY growth and improved market share in demat additions, while absorbing margin pressure from strategic investments in technology and talent that management expects to normalize over the medium term.
