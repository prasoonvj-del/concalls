# Satin Creditcare Network Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivering strong financial results, record standalone profit, and significantly improved asset quality while proactively building management buffers.

### Key numbers
- Standalone PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 292 crore
- Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 299 crore (vs Rs 98 crore in H1 FY26)
- Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 176 crore (up 231% YoY, 43% QoQ)
- Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 172 crore (up 231% YoY)
- Consolidated AUM: Rs 16,589 crore (up 31% YoY); Standalone AUM: Rs 13,608 crore (up 23% YoY)
- Consolidated Disbursements (Q2 FY27): Rs 3,697 crore (up 41% YoY); H1 FY27: Rs 7,192 crore (up 48% YoY)
- Consolidated ROA / ROE (Q2 FY27): Reported at 4.5% / 22.8%; Adjusted for management overlay at 5.5% / 27.7%
- Standalone ROA / ROE (Q2 FY27): Reported at 4.9% / 20.6%; Adjusted for management overlay at 6.0% / 25.0%
- Operating Expense Ratio: 6.6% (vs 7.2% YoY); Cost-to-Income: 41.6% (vs 49.8% YoY)
- Standalone GNPA: 1.9% (vs 2.2% in June, 3.5% YoY); Absolute GNPA: Rs 197 crore
- PAR 1: 2.9% (vs 5.79% YoY)
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 133% (vs 115% in June); On-book provisions: Rs 263 crore
- Credit Cost: 2.47% for Q2 FY27 (4.83% YoY); H1 FY27: 2.77% (or 2% excluding overlay)
- Management Overlay buffer: Increased from Rs 36 crore to Rs 50 crore
- Capital Adequacy (CRAR): 27.89% (vs 26.74% in June, 26.5% YoY); Net Worth: Rs 3,437 crore
- Marginal Cost of Borrowing: 10.28% (down 65 bps YoY)

### Highlights
- Marked the 21st consecutive profitable quarter.
- Slippages dropped 68% YoY to Rs 103 crore in H1 FY27 from Rs 317 crore in H1 FY26.
- Total on-book borrowings stood at Rs 10,033 crore with a leverage ratio of 2.9x; raised Rs 2,630 crore in Q2 and Rs 5,630 crore in H1.
- Promoters committed Rs 100 crore through warrants at Rs 260 per share (17% above SEBI formula price); first tranche of Rs 25 crore received.
- Non-MFI portfolio reached 20% of consolidated AUM (vs 15% YoY). Satin Finserv AUM grew 184% YoY to Rs 1,761 crore (GNPA at 3.07%); Satin Housing Finance AUM at Rs 1,219 crore (up 19% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to land within the guided range of 20% to 25% for full-year consolidated AUM growth (first-half annualized at 19%).
- Long-term target of Rs 32,000 crore consolidated AUM by 2030 remains unchanged, with 30% from non-MFI businesses.
- Standalone credit cost guidance maintained at 3% to 3.5% (currently tracking better at 2.77% for H1).
- Standalone ROA guidance maintained at 3.5% to 4.0% (currently tracking at 4.3% for H1).
- Planned branch additions: ~50 branches in H2 FY27 and 100-150 branches in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rural cash flows and monsoon recovery have been very encouraging following initial caution.
- Assam flood impact: 42,394 borrowers granted moratorium, nearly 90% resumed repayments during the moratorium period; negligible credit cost impact. Management decided to pass on the entire NATCAT insurance payout to affected customers without utilizing the management overlay.
- Geographical diversification protects Satin, with negligible exposure (1% each) to drought-hit Karnataka and Maharashtra, and no repayment stress witnessed there.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Stable cross-cycle Net Interest Margin (NIM) guided at approximately 14.5% (reported Q2 NIM was higher due to assignment/DA components).
- Cashless collections reached 25% organically. Disbursements remain 100% cashless.
- UPI MDR on loan repayments: Management expects negligible impact as installment amounts are mostly below Rs 2,000 and proposed MDR is capped at Rs 5 per transaction for loans.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total employee count stands at 18,978 across 2,075 branches, 27 states, 5 UTs, and 608 districts, serving 34.1 lakh clients. Loan officers exceed 11,000.
- Subsidiaries: Satin Finserv added 19 branches in Q2 (total 140 branches); Satin Housing Finance has 59 branches (slowed origination to pivot from urban to rural affordable housing).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Undrawn sanctions of roughly Rs 4,600 crore and liquidity of Rs 2,235 crore as of September 30.
- Broad lender base of 69 active lenders; top 10 lenders account for 55% of borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sector cyclicality and potential future macro events, for which management is proactively building a Rs 100 crore cumulative buffer (currently at Rs 50 crore).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital):** Asked about micro-market stress and management overlay strategy. Management stated no state is facing stress, Assam is recovering well, and the overlay target is to build a Rs 100 crore buffer to insulate against microfinance cyclically.
2. **Giriraj (Unnamed Firm):** Asked about branch opening guidance and FY28 growth. Management guided for ~50 branches in H2 and 100–150 branches in FY28, reiterating the long-term 2030 path.
3. **Saumil Shah (Unnamed Firm):** Inquired about Satin Housing degrowth and UPI MDR impact. Management explained Housing is recalibrating toward rural affordable housing, and UPI MDR impact on small loan installments will be negligible.
4. **Bunty Chawla (Unnamed Firm):** Asked about cost of funds and credit guarantee schemes. Management noted marginal borrowing cost is 10.28%, with strong sanctions and ability to pass on hikes, while credit guarantee schemes are currently unfeasible due to low NPAs.
5. **Shaju Paul (Unnamed Firm):** Questioned the sustainability of fair value/DA recognition in profits and market valuation mismatch. Management clarified that DA income operates in a stable 21%–23% band with no one-offs, and market valuation is outside their control, though promoter warrant infusion at a premium reflects confidence.

### One-line takeaway
Satin Creditcare delivered record H1 profitability (Standalone PAT at Rs 292 crore) and sharply lower credit costs while prudently absorbing 100+ bps of ROA into a growing management buffer to secure cycle resilience.
