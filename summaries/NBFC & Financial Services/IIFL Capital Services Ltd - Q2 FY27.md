# IIFL Capital Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational growth across core segments (Retail, Institutional, FPD) despite a mark-to-market loss on BSE shares.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained focus on closing the Fairfax transaction in November 2026.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "flat" revenue in Q1 to 14% QoQ growth in Q2; management remains optimistic about India's medium-term fundamentals despite global volatility.
- **New topics:** Announced succession planning (Vasudev Jagannath to become MD effective Jan 1, 2027) and plans to incorporate a wholly-owned NBFC subsidiary for HNI credit solutions.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Fairfax transaction is nearing completion (expected Nov 2026); AUM growth recovered as expected.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Operational Revenue:** INR722 crore (up 14% QoQ, 22% YoY).
*   **Retail Equities Income:** INR316 crore (up 6% QoQ, 17% YoY).
*   **Institutional & Banking Revenue:** INR245 crore (up 18% QoQ).
*   **FPD (Distribution) Income:** INR159 crore (up 27% QoQ, 23% YoY).
*   **Operational PBT (pre-MTM):** INR206 crore (up 40% QoQ).
*   **MTM Loss:** INR32 crore (due to BSE share price movement).
*   **Employee Costs:** INR194 crore (up 8% QoQ, 26% YoY).
*   **Finance Costs:** INR75 crore (up 25% QoQ, 40% YoY).
*   **Admin Expenses:** INR96 crore (up 9% QoQ, 11% YoY).
*   **Average Daily Turnover (ADTO):** INR3,05,073 crore (down 3% QoQ, up 16% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Fairfax Transaction:** Received all requisite regulatory and shareholder approvals for the 51% acquisition; closure expected in November 2026.
*   **Leadership Succession:** Vasudev Jagannath appointed as Additional Director and Whole-Time Director; will become MD on Jan 1, 2027. R. Venkataraman to remain on the Board.
*   **Strategic Expansion:** Proposed incorporation of a wholly-owned NBFC subsidiary to offer credit solutions to the HNI segment.
*   **BSE Investment:** Company holds 5.95 lakh shares of BSE, which caused a INR32 crore MTM loss this quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Fairfax Deal:** Management expects the preferential allotment and open offer to close in November 2026.
*   **Insurance:** Management expects an uptick in insurance income over the next 2 quarters.
*   **Wealth Business:** Management targets break-even for the wealth business in 6 to 18 months.
*   **Long-term Strategy:** Management indicated a shift in focus toward capital markets distribution over insurance distribution; open to selling the insurance business if it enhances shareholder value.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro Environment:** Global environment remains challenging due to geopolitical tensions, high oil prices, and interest rates; however, India remains resilient with 7.8% GDP growth in Q1 FY27.
*   **Regulatory Impact:** No significant impact from recent RBI/SEBI regulations as the company does not engage in proprietary trading.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Employee Costs:** Increased due to headcount additions, variable pay, and wage inflation. Management expects the current quarterly run rate of ~INR194 crore to continue.
*   **Finance Costs:** Increased due to higher borrowings to support the growing MTM book and margin funding.
*   **Distribution Yields:** In-house equity products yield ~2.0%–2.25%, while third-party products yield ~1.5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Technology:** Admin expenses grew 9% QoQ, driven by investments in technology.
*   **In-house Manufacturing:** AUM for in-house manufacturing reached ~INR4,500 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Fund Utilization:** Post-Fairfax infusion, funds will be primarily deployed for margin funding and repayment of borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Market Volatility:** Ongoing concerns regarding inflation, crude oil prices, and currency pressure.
*   **PMS AUM:** Experienced a decline in PMS AUM due to outflows in NDPMS (Non-Discretionary PMS).
*   **Insurance Performance:** Segment underperformed in Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is the impact of the Fairfax deal on the banking license?**
    *   *A:* Management noted that Fairfax's press release suggests the interests of IIFL Capital and Go Digit may eventually be subsumed into a bank, but deferred to Fairfax for long-term strategy.
*   **Q: Why did employee costs increase if RM headcount was flat?**
    *   *A:* Management clarified that costs rose due to bonus provisions, wage inflation, and headcount increases in the AMC business.
*   **Q: Will promoters tender shares in the open offer?**
    *   *A:* Management clarified that promoters will not tender shares; they will only sell if necessary to help the acquirer reach the 51% threshold.
*   **Q: What is the strategy for the insurance business?**
    *   *A:* Management admitted the business is marginal and they are open to selling it to enhance shareholder value.

### One-line takeaway
IIFL Capital Services delivered strong operational growth and announced a leadership transition, while awaiting the imminent closure of the Fairfax capital infusion and planning an entry into the NBFC space.
