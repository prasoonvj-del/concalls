# Piramal Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth momentum, successful capital raise, and stable asset quality support management's confidence in meeting FY27 targets.

### Key numbers
*   **PAT:** Rs 545 crore (+67% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** >Rs 1,000 crore (+67% YoY).
*   **Total AUM:** Rs 1,13,112 crore (+24% YoY).
*   **Growth AUM:** 98.5% of total AUM (+30% YoY).
*   **RoAUM (Growth business):** 2% (annualized).
*   **Capital Adequacy:** 20.1%.
*   **Branch Network:** 838.

### Highlights
*   Completed equity fundraise of Rs 3,850 crore (QIP of Rs 2,100 crore and preferential allotment of Rs 1,750 crore).
*   Retail AUM grew 30% YoY to Rs 97,462 crore; Wholesale AUM grew 24% YoY to Rs 13,978 crore.
*   Customer franchise reached 6.5 million (+26% YoY).
*   Legacy book reduced by 69% YoY, now representing only 1.5% of total AUM.
*   Management noted the 5-year anniversary of the DHFL merger, citing 5x AUM growth since the merger.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **AUM Growth:** Management expects AUM to double every 3 years, targeting Rs 1.5 lakh crore by FY28.
*   **Profitability:** Maintained FY27 profit growth guidance of 50% YoY.
*   **RoAUM:** Management targets 2.5% RoAUM by exit Q4 FY27.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Management expects borrowing costs on a stock basis to remain similar or lower over the next 12 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management expects H2 FY27 to see a broad-based increase in borrowing costs due to RBI's shift to calibrated tightening.
*   No notable slowdown in borrowing activity is expected despite rising global inflation.
*   Management flagged the increasing presence of gold loans on customer files as an emerging risk to monitor.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Net Income Margin (NIM):** 6.5% (flat QoQ).
*   **Growth Business NIM:** 6.8%.
*   **Retail Opex to AUM:** 3.48% (down 5 bps QoQ).
*   **Incremental Cost of Borrowing:** 8.46% in H1 FY27 (down from 9.36% in H1 FY26).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Gold Loan Branches:** Expanded to 100 (from 67 in Q1); target 200 by March 2027.
*   **Rural Branches:** Expanded to 203 (from 178 in Q1).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Worth:** Rs 31,180 crore.
*   **Leverage (AUM to equity):** 3.6x.
*   **Debt Maturity:** Rs 37,500 crore of long-term borrowings maturing in the next 18 months at rates higher than current incremental borrowing costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Potential IRDAI insurance commission capping could impact FY28 RoAUM by 18-24 bps (gross).
*   **Macro:** Rising global inflation and potential RBI rate hikes.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management noted a specific Rs 4 crore fraud case in housing loans and persistent risk in IT sector home loans (South India).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Shreya Shivani (Nomura) on rate hikes:** Management stated they will not pass on rate hikes to customers immediately; they will wait for their own cost of borrowing to stabilize/fall first.
*   **Renish (ICICI) on vintage risk:** Management clarified that "vintage risk" is a rolling metric and overall portfolio risk metrics remain stable.
*   **Abhijit Tibrewal (Motilal Oswal) on RoAUM levers:** Management identified opex reduction, cost of borrowing improvements, and mix shift toward unsecured/gold loans as key levers to reach 2.5% RoAUM.
*   **Anand Dama (Nuvama) on insurance capping:** Management is in discussions with the regulator and exploring mitigants like rationalizing sales incentives to cushion the impact.

### One-line takeaway
Strong growth momentum and a successful capital raise position the company well, with management confident in meeting FY27 targets despite potential rate headwinds and regulatory changes.
