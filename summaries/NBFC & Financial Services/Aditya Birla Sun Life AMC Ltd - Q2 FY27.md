# Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While AUM crossed the ₹11 lakh crore milestone and equity performance improved, profitability (PBT/PAT) declined YoY due to higher employee costs and base effects.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management clarified employee cost impact: ₹55-60 crore/year for existing ESOP schemes plus an additional ₹14-15 crore/year for a new scheme launched in Q2.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong AUM growth" to a focus on execution, profitability, and direct channel expansion.
- **New topics:** Launched "Birla Passives" brand; SIF (Structured Investment Funds) platform reached ~₹700 crore AUM; specific focus on direct channel improvement (80 people in 14 locations).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on mandate management (EPFO/ESIC) and continued focus on flagship funds (Flexi Cap, Balanced Advantage, Multi-asset, Small Cap).

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue (Q2 FY27):** ₹526 crore (vs ₹506 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** ₹296 crore (vs ₹316 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** ₹221 crore (vs ₹241 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Total Average AUM (incl. alternates):** ₹11.06 lakh crore.
- **Mutual Fund Quarterly Avg AUM:** ₹4.42 lakh crore (+4% YoY).
- **Equity Mutual Fund Quarterly Avg AUM:** ₹2.09 lakh crore (+9% YoY).
- **SIP Contribution (Sept 2026):** ₹1,094 crore.
- **SIP AUM:** ₹86,000 crore.

### Highlights
- **Equity Mix:** 47.4% for the quarter.
- **Passive Business:** Launched "Birla Passives" brand; Passive quarterly avg AUM ~₹39,860 crore (+10% YoY).
- **SIF Platform:** Comprises 3 products with ~₹700 crore AUM.
- **GIFT City:** Launched retail fund offering; fundraising underway for ABSL Flexi Cap Fund (inward remittance).
- **Real Estate:** AUM ~₹860 crore (+43% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Employee Costs:** Management expects costs to remain in the Q2 range, excluding inflationary impacts.
- **ESOP Impact:** Existing schemes to cost ₹55-60 crore/year; new scheme to add ₹14-15 crore/year.
- **Growth Strategy:** Management aims to maintain margins while scaling; focus remains on core flagship funds and increasing direct channel penetration.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Industry AUM:** ₹87.21 lakh crore as of Sept 30, 2026 (+13% YoY).
- **Industry SIP:** ₹32,490 crore for Sept 2026 (+11% YoY).
- **Investor Preference:** Shifting toward mid-cap, small-cap, and flexi-cap funds.
- **Market Sentiment:** Management noted that while near-term volatility may persist, long-term growth remains supported by domestic participation and SIPs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Employee Expenses:** Increased YoY due to ESOP costs (~₹12-13 crore/quarter) and a ₹6 crore provision reversal in the prior year.
- **Yields:** Equity (63-64 bps), Debt (24-25 bps), Liquid (12-13 bps), ETF (8 bps).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Direct Channel:** 80 dedicated staff operating in 14 locations to drive direct business.
- **Product Pipeline:** Planning to launch 6 funds across retail and non-retail categories in the coming quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Treasury Investment Book:** ₹2,890 crore, deployed across debt mutual funds, corporate bonds, and equity schemes (17-18% equity).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Macro Factors:** Geopolitical conflicts, inflationary pressures, El Niño, and potential asset price corrections.
- **Market Volatility:** High global bond yields, weaker rupee, and elevated crude prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On Employee Expenses:** Management explained the YoY increase was driven by ESOP costs and a one-time provision reversal in the base year.
- **On SIF Economics:** Management noted SIF yields sit between equity and debt categories; profitability will evolve as the category scales.
- **On Direct Channel:** Management acknowledged the need to improve direct channel performance and is deploying 80 dedicated staff to reach HNI and existing customers.
- **On Mandate "Rub-off":** Management stated EPFO/ESIC mandates are cost-neutral but provide significant credibility and visibility for future private and global mandates.
- **On SIP Flows:** Management noted that while industry SIPs are resilient, they are actively incentivizing teams to drive higher SIP success.

### One-line takeaway
AUM crossed ₹11 lakh crore, but profitability faced headwinds from elevated employee costs, while management pivots focus toward passive products and direct channel expansion.
