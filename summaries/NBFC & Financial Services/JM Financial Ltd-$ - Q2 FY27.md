# JM Financial Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong performance in corporate advisory/capital markets, robust deal pipelines, and successful growth in private markets and wealth management, despite some market volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Net Revenue:** Rs 712 crore (Q2 FY27); Rs 1,600 crore (H1 FY27).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 240 crore (Q2 FY27); Rs 531 crore (H1 FY27).
*   **Standard Loan Book:** Rs 10,270 crore (up 23% YoY).
*   **Corporate Advisory & Capital Markets Net Revenue:** Rs 286 crore (up 2.5x QoQ).
*   **Private Markets Disbursements:** Rs 977 crore (doubled QoQ).
*   **Wealth Management Recurring AUM:** Rs 40,000 crore (up 17% YoY).
*   **Affordable Home Loans AUM:** ~Rs 4,000 crore (up 30% YoY).
*   **Dividend:** Interim dividend of Rs 1.65 per share declared.

### Highlights
*   **Capital Markets:** Ranked #1 across IPOs and QIPs; closed transactions worth Rs 83,000 crore in Q2.
*   **Private Markets:** Standard loans grew 22% YoY. Cash and cash equivalents reduced from ~Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 1,700 crore due to disbursements and debt repayment.
*   **Wealth Management:** Recurring revenues grew 20% YoY to Rs 216 crore. Business broke even in September.
*   **Asset Management:** Achieved first close of maiden Pre-IPO Fund (Rs 700+ crore). AIF AUM stands at Rs 1,063 crore.
*   **Affordable Housing:** Disbursements grew 48% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management targets 15-20% growth in capital markets/corporate advisory and 20% growth in private markets loan book over the next 3-5 years.
*   **Wealth Management:** Expects 20-25% growth in AUM.
*   **Profitability:** Management expects PAT growth of 20-25% if revenue grows at 15-20% due to operating scale.
*   **Leverage:** Management plans to resume adding leverage to the private markets balance sheet over the next year to support growth.
*   **ARC:** Expects to resolve/recover Rs 250 crore of impairments by March 31, 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Environment:** Markets were choppy, but primary market activity revived strongly from June.
*   **Pipeline:** Extremely robust; Rs 130,000 crore worth of transactions filed with the regulator for execution over the next 12 months.
*   **Macro:** Management noted that recent global developments (U.S. election, etc.) are "pro-India."

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Wealth Management:** Gross yields on recurring assets are ~51 bps; net yields are ~35 bps. New assets are being added at yields >60 bps.
*   **Employee Costs:** Headcount rationalization in wealth management was a planned productivity measure; recruitment is expected to resume next year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Wealth Management:** 948 professionals, 71 branches, 898 franchisees.
*   **Affordable Housing:** 152 branches.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Consolidated Net Worth:** ~Rs 11,000 crore.
*   **Consolidated Gross Leverage:** 1x.
*   **Private Markets Leverage:** Debt-to-equity is at 0.8x; management stated this has "bottomed" and will increase.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Market Volatility:** Revenue in corporate advisory was impacted by ~Rs 45-50 crore due to difficult market conditions and reduced deal sizes.
*   **Lumpiness:** ARC and private credit businesses remain lumpy; management noted they may not always meet risk-adjusted return thresholds for new deals.
*   **Mark-to-Market:** The company faces quarterly MTM volatility on equity and alternative investments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Buyback/Capital Allocation:** An analyst questioned why the company doesn't buy back stock trading below book value. Management explained that liquidity is largely trapped in NBFCs (tax hit to extract) and holding company liquidity is locked in NSE shares until March 2027.
*   **Demerger:** An analyst asked about demerging the capital markets business. Management stated they prefer to focus on execution and scaling the wealth/asset management businesses first before considering restructuring.
*   **Private Credit Yields:** Management clarified that real estate lending requires a 200-300 bps premium over corporate lending, which is why the book hasn't grown aggressively despite demand.
*   **Wealth Management Profitability:** Management confirmed the wealth business broke even in September and expects a full year of profitability in FY28.

### One-line takeaway
JM Financial is leveraging a strong capital markets pipeline and diversified revenue streams (wealth, asset management, private credit) to target 20% long-term growth, while managing capital allocation constraints until liquidity unlocks in 2027.
