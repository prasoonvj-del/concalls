# Angel One Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY'27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasized resilience, diversified revenue streams (40% non-broking), and long-term growth despite seasonal moderation in trading volumes.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 14.3 billion (+25.4% YoY, -2.3% QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 2.3 billion (+102.1% YoY).
*   **EBDAT (Reported):** Rs 3.6 billion (32.7% margin).
*   **EBDAT (Normalized):** 43.6% margin.
*   **Registered Users:** >38 million.
*   **Average Client Funding Book:** Rs 61.4 billion.
*   **Period-end Client Funding Book:** Rs 71.5 billion.
*   **Credit Distribution:** Rs 5.3 billion (+130% YoY).
*   **Total AUM:** Rs 134.4 billion (+33.3% YoY).
*   **Ionic Wealth-tech AUM:** Rs 32.3 billion.
*   **UHNI Segment AUM:** Rs 87.3 billion (263 families).
*   **AMC AUM:** >Rs 6.2 billion.

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Diversification:** ~60% of gross revenue from core broking; ~40% from complementary businesses (client funding, distribution, depository, wealth, and AMC).
*   **Market Share:** 22.2% in equity derivatives, 17.4% in cash equity turnover, and 20.2% of overall retail equity turnover.
*   **AI Integration:** "Ask Angel" conversational assistant serves 1.1 million users; AI is embedded in onboarding, grievance automation, and portfolio intelligence.
*   **Dividend:** Board approved an interim dividend of Rs 1 per share.
*   **Asset Quality:** 83% of client funding exposure is <Rs 100,000 per client; 85% of the portfolio is <30 days old, with negligible delinquency.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management maintains a 45-50% operating margin guidance (on a standalone basis).
*   **Breakeven:** Management expects Wealth and AMC businesses to reach breakeven in 3-4 years.
*   **Costs:** Employee cost is expected to be ~Rs 11 billion for the year.
*   **Strategy:** Management remains bullish on India's long-term financialization journey and continues to invest in technology and AI.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Market activity moderated sequentially across the industry.
*   Management stated it is too early to comment on July volumes but sees no fundamental change in India's growth trajectory.
*   Credit distribution growth slowed; management attributed this to lender calibration of risk/pricing and funnel friction, rather than a structural issue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Reported margins were impacted by IPL-related marketing, annual employee increments, and ESOP grants.
*   Wealth and AMC businesses currently account for a ~400 bps burn on operating margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **AMC:** Currently a passive-only business; management is evaluating expansion beyond current offerings.
*   **Wealth:** Expanding an omnichannel approach (tech + Relationship Managers).
*   **U.S. Equities:** Obtained a GIFT City license; product launch is pending.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Worth:** Rs 64.2 billion.
*   **Borrowings:** Reduced during the quarter.
*   **Risk Management:** Client funding book is fully collateralized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Regulatory landscape continues to evolve; management monitors closely.
*   Short-term volatility in trading volumes and credit disbursals.
*   Customer feedback regarding a "restricted basket" of stocks preventing sales; management acknowledged the issue and promised to address it.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Credit slowdown:** Analysts questioned the ~30% decline in credit disbursals from peak. Management (Saurabh Agarwal) cited lender risk calibration and funnel friction, not a structural issue.
*   **AMC performance:** Analysts questioned the slow scale-up. Management (Amit Majumdar) noted it is a 15-month-old passive-only business and takes time to mature.
*   **Employee count:** Analysts noted a 15-20% decline in headcount. Management emphasized efficiency and productivity gains via AI/automation.
*   **Restricted basket:** A retail investor/analyst raised concerns about a "restricted basket" preventing stock sales. Management (Ambarish Kenghe) acknowledged the issue, apologized, and requested an offline connection to resolve.
*   **Wealth breakeven:** Analysts asked for absolute burn numbers. Management (Vineet Agrawal) reiterated the 3-4 year breakeven timeline and noted the ~400 bps margin impact from new ventures.

### One-line takeaway
Angel One delivered resilient performance with 25.4% YoY revenue growth and strong diversification, while navigating seasonal volume moderation and investing in long-term wealth and AMC capabilities.
