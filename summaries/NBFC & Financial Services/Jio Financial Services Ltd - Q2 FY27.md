# Jio Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Broad-based growth across lending (+154% YoY AUM), asset management (+57% YoY AUM), and payments, coupled with strategic partnerships with Bank of America and Allianz.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** none (no numeric revenue/PAT guidance provided).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted focus in payments from sheer volume to margin-accretive segments, intentionally churning low-margin TPV to maximize fee income and unit economics.
- **New topics or risks:** 
  - Agreed to acquire a 49.9% stake in Bank of America Credit entity, subject to approvals (bringing Rs 18,000 crore capital).
  - Partnered/incubated with Allianz for insurance initiatives.
  - Draft IRDAI regulations published; management expects potential industry consolidation.
  - Unveiled *JioFinance+* (value-back membership) and *JioFinance360* (financial health score platform).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Broking operations were scheduled to launch in Q2 FY27—delivered via the rollout of a self-directed investing platform with zero-brokerage pricing.

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Revenue (excl. dividends):** Rs 1,699 crore (+132% YoY).
* **Consolidated PPOP (excl. dividend impact):** Rs 1,079 crore (+60% YoY, +85% YoY per core operating basis).
* **Consolidated PBT (excl. dividend impact):** Rs 3,195 crore (+136% YoY).
* **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 1,171 crore.
* **NBFC AUM:** Rs 37,320 crore (+154% YoY).
* **NBFC Gross Disbursements:** Rs 13,135 crore (+98% YoY).
* **NBFC Borrowings:** ~Rs 42,000 crore (+250% YoY).
* **AMC AUM:** Rs 22,746 crore (+57% YoY, +24% QoQ).
* **Payments TPV:** Rs 14,682 crore (QoQ moderation due to intentional margin-accretive pivot).
* **Payments Bank Deposits:** +53% YoY; CASA ratio at 38%.
* **Consolidated Shareholders' Equity:** ~Rs 1.81 lakh crore.

### Highlights
* **Strategic Partnerships:** 
  - Agreement to acquire a 49.9% stake in Bank of America Credit entity (subject to regulatory approvals), bringing Rs 18,000 crore capital.
  - Incubation and prospective joint venture with Allianz for general/life insurance.
* **NBFC Asset Mix:** Mortgages (Home Loans/LAP) at 44.5% (avg ticket size Rs 1.4 crore, tenor 15.9 years), Corporate & SME loans at 44.2% (tenor 3.26 years), and Loan Against Securities at 11.3%. Book remains 100% secured with zero organic NPAs flagged.
* **App & Ecosystem:** Digital storefront clocked 3.2 crore downloads; launched *JioFinance+* (value-back program) and *JioFinance360* (multi-source asset/liability consolidation & financial health scoring).
* **Investing Vertical:** Launched self-directed broking platform offering zero-brokerage on stocks, ETFs, and IPOs; AMC partnered with 15,000 distributors.
* **Reinsurance & Insurance:** Operationalized reinsurance business; Fee & Commission income up 46% YoY in insurance broking.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Capital Infusion:** Management plans an initial capital infusion tranche of Rs 9,500 crore (subject to statutory approvals) into the NBFC to bolster the balance sheet.
* **Insurance Approvals:** General insurance entity is in advanced stages of securing regulatory approvals to commence operations.
* **Platform Vision:** Moving towards an AI-native, conversational marketplace ("N=1" dynamic personalization) integrating Borrow, Invest, Transact, and Protect pillars.

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Payments Strategy:** Management chose to deliberately let go of high-volume, low-margin payments engagements to prioritize profitable, bottom-line accretive business.
* **IRDAI Regulations:** Management welcomed draft IRDAI transparency regulations, noting they may trigger industry consolidation that benefits large, well-capitalized, tech-driven players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Fee Income:** JPSL fee and commission income rose 74% YoY following portfolio rationalization towards higher-margin merchant and exporter segments.
* **Borrowing Costs:** Average borrowing rate on TREPS stood at 7.08% (vs 7.07% earlier); 70% of NBFC borrowings are floating rate.
* **Staff Costs:** Staff costs decreased QoQ due to the base effect of annual increments and bonus payouts recorded in Q1.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Distribution Reach:** Business Correspondent (BC) network expanded to 633,000 touchpoints across 19,000+ PIN codes.
* **Physical Footprint:** Established dedicated physical offices in major cities to support physical verification and originations for mortgages.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **NBFC Capital Adequacy:** Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) stood at 18.50%.
* **Standalone Net Worth:** Rs 35,521 crore (up from Rs 29,305 crore as of March 31, 2026), aided by Rs 5,934 crore received from preferential warrant execution.
* **Borrowing Mix:** Term loans and working capital demand loans accounted for 50% of borrowings (down from 60% in FY26).

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **Reinsurance Churn/Moderation:** Gross Written Premium in reinsurance saw volume moderation as primary insurers recalibrated crop insurance exposure due to seasonal and environmental factors.
