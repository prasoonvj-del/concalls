# Jio Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management reported robust growth across all verticals, successful pivot to margin-accretive segments, and continued scaling of the "flywheel" ecosystem.

### What changed vs last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Maintained focus on "Borrow, Invest, Transact, Protect" pillars; no specific numerical guidance changes provided.
*   **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from pure volume growth to "margin-accretive" growth, particularly in Payments (TPV adjustment).
*   **New topics:** Unveiled "JioFinance+" (membership program) and "JioFinance360" (health assessment platform). Announced 49.9% stake acquisition in a credit entity (subject to approvals).
*   **Last quarter's promises:** Securities broking launched (zero-brokerage structure). General Insurance JV progressing; Life Insurance JV non-binding agreement in place.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Income (excl. dividends):** Rs 1,699 crore (+132% YoY).
*   **Pre-Provisioning Operating Profit (PPOP):** Rs 1,079 crore (+85% YoY).
*   **NBFC AUM:** Rs 37,320 crore (+154% YoY).
*   **NBFC Gross Disbursements:** Rs 13,135 crore (+98% YoY).
*   **Jio Payments Bank Deposits:** +53% YoY; 38% CASA ratio.
*   **JPSL (Payments) TPV:** Rs 14,682 crore (adjusted for pivot to margin-accretive segments).
*   **AMC AUM:** Rs 22,746 crore (+57% YoY).
*   **NBFC Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 18.50%.
*   **NBFC Borrowings:** ~Rs 42,000 crore (+250% YoY).
*   **Average Borrowing Cost:** 7.08% (vs 7.07% in FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Payments business (JPSL) pivoted away from high-volume, low-margin segments to prioritize margin-accretive business.
*   **New Product Launches:**
    *   **JioFinance+:** Value-back membership program sharing commission benefits with customers.
    *   **JioFinance360:** AI-powered financial health assessment tool.
*   **Partnerships:** Agreed to acquire 49.9% stake in a credit entity (expected to bring Rs 18,000 crore capital, subject to approvals).
*   **Broking:** Launched self-directed investing platform with zero-brokerage on stocks, ETFs, and IPOs.
*   **Reinsurance:** Underwrote premiums; recalibrating exposure to crop insurance due to seasonal factors.
*   **Consolidation:** RSHL (Reliance Strategic Investments) fully consolidated line-by-line effective April 29.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Strategy:** Management aims to continue building a "neural agentic marketplace" using AI to drive hyper-personalization.
*   **Insurance:** Monitoring draft IRDAI regulations; management expects potential industry consolidation but believes well-capitalized, tech-driven players will benefit.
*   **Capital:** Awaiting statutory approvals for a Rs 9,500 crore capital infusion tranche to strengthen the balance sheet.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Approach:** Leveraging "late entrant advantage" to observe market dynamics and adapt with agility.
*   **Customer Engagement:** 3.2 million properties served via storefront offerings; 633,000 Business Correspondent touchpoints.
*   **Digital Adoption:** 40% of mutual fund originations come from beyond top cities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Processing margins in Payments improved due to portfolio rationalization.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Staff costs increased due to annual increments and bonus payouts.
*   **Funding:** 50% of borrowings are in demand/term loans (down from 60% in FY26); 70% of borrowings are on floating rates.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Physical Footprint:** 19,000+ PIN codes covered; physical offices established in major cities for mortgage originations.
*   **Distribution:** 15,000 Mutual Fund distributors partnered.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Shareholders' Equity:** Rs 7,379 crore (NBFC level).
*   **Liquidity:** Maintained "comfortable headroom" for future expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Volatility:** Management noted the need to balance portfolios against macro volatility.
*   **Regulatory:** Closely monitoring draft IRDAI guidelines for potential impact on insurance business.
*   **Portfolio Quality:** NBFC book is entirely secured, focused on high-rated corporate exposures.
