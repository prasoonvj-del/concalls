# 360 ONE WAM LTD - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong wealth management flows and improved retention, despite market volatility impacting treasury income and some lumpy asset management outflows.

### What changed vs last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Maintained the 100-150 bps cost-to-income improvement target for the year.
*   **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious" on global allocations to highlighting resilience in domestic flows and primary markets (IPOs) despite FPI selling.
*   **New topics:** Detailed the impact of NSE listing on fair value/treasury income; provided specific progress on UBS collaboration ($300-325 million in flows to date); introduced "COMPASS" AI platform.
*   **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the UBS collaboration (onboarding initiated, flows started); continued progress on HNI business scaling (now Rs 8,000+ crore AUM).

### Key numbers
*   **Total AUM:** Rs 9 lakh crore (+31% YoY).
*   **ARR AUM:** Rs 3,53,000 crore (+19.7% YoY).
*   **ARR Net Flows:** Rs 12,482 crore (vs Rs 10,815 crore in Q1).
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 896 crore (+17.5% YoY).
*   **ARR Revenue:** Rs 706 crore (+27.5% YoY).
*   **Total Revenue:** Rs 853 crore (+4.8% YoY).
*   **Operating PBT:** Rs 434 crore (+19.7% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 302 crore (vs Rs 316 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **ARR Retention:** 79 bps (vs 74 bps in Q1).
*   **Cost-to-operating income ratio:** 51.5% (vs 54.3% in Q1).
*   **Tangible ROE:** 16.9%.

### Highlights
*   **Wealth Business:** ARR AUM grew 24.3% YoY to Rs 2,52,627 crore; 360 ONE Plus advisory platform crossed Rs 1 lakh crore.
*   **Asset Management:** ARR AUM at Rs 1,00,000 crore (+9.4% YoY); net flows improved to Rs 1,492 crore.
*   **Carry Income:** Contributed ~Rs 60 crore; NSE listing positively impacted fair value.
*   **Other Income:** Negative Rs 44 crore due to MTM impact on treasury investments (largely NSE exposure).
*   **Leadership:** Ashish Agarwal joined in a senior leadership role to spearhead business alongside the existing team.
*   **Technology:** "COMPASS" AI layer is live across wealth and asset management to improve RM efficiency.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Cost-to-income:** Management aims to move the ratio towards 46-47% over the next 12-18 months.
*   **UBS Collaboration:** Expects to reach $600-700 million in flows by March end.
*   **ROE:** Management targets ~20% ROE over the next 3.5 years.
*   **Capital:** No immediate need to raise capital; will utilize internal accruals and warrant conversion money for growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Wealth creation in the UHNI segment is growing at 13-15%.
*   Market volatility and FPI selling were offset by resilient domestic flows and record IPO activity.
*   Clients are increasingly moving to portfolio-level advisory mandates.
*   Competition remains high, but management believes the "moat" is the combination of people, product, and platform.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Total costs: Rs 462 crore (+15.5% YoY).
*   Employee costs: Rs 342 crore (+19.9% YoY), including a one-time Rs 9 crore restructuring cost.
*   Admin/other costs: Rs 120 crore (+4.6% YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   HNI business: Now manages Rs 8,000+ crore for 1,500+ clients; expects to break even on direct costs soon.
*   Capital Markets: Full-stack platform expected to be ready by Q1 FY28; currently winning mandates (8-10 issues).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Tangible net worth: ~Rs 7,400 crore; expected to reach Rs 10,000-12,000 crore in 3.5 years including warrant conversion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Market Volatility:** MTM impact on treasury investments (negative Rs 44 crore).
*   **Asset Management Outflows:** Potential for large redemptions in older funds (e.g., P1A fund) over the next 6-18 months.
*   **TBR Volatility:** Transaction and brokerage income (TBR) remains lumpy and soft; management expects growth in low-single to early-double digits.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the divergence in flows between Wealth and AMC?** A: Management noted Wealth is benefiting from brand strength and platform completeness, while AMC had institutional redemptions (now behind them) and is seeing renewed interest.
*   **Q: Impact of new leadership on costs?** A: Management stated it does not structurally change costs; they remain "hungry for talent" at the top quartile but will keep cost-to-income within targets.
*   **Q: How to think about ROE moving to 25%?** A: Management clarified they are comfortable aiming for 20% over 3.5 years, given the need to deploy capital in the NBFC and sponsor capital for Alternates.
*   **Q: Synergy from B&K acquisition?** A: Management quantified broking synergies at Rs 3-4 crore per month currently, with potential to grow as they leverage research for UHNI clients.
*   **Q: Why the low yields in mutual funds?** A: Management attributed it to a one-time admin fee accrual.

### One-line takeaway
360 ONE WAM delivered robust 27.5% ARR revenue growth and improved operating leverage, while successfully navigating MTM treasury losses and scaling its HNI and capital markets franchises.
