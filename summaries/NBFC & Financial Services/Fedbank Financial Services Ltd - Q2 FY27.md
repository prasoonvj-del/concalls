# Fedbank Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management reported the strongest quarter to date in terms of profitability and operating metrics, achieving record ROE despite heightened competitive intensity.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 AUM growth guidance of 20%-25%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "navigating regulatory headwinds" to confidence in resilience, citing H2 as historically stronger.
- **New topics:** Confirmed entry into Eastern India; management highlighted that the "technical" nature of gold loan delinquencies is becoming better understood by the market.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on branch expansion (74 branches opened in Q2 vs zero in Q1); maintained credit cost guidance below 1%.

### Key numbers
- **Disbursements:** Rs 6,676 crore (+28% YoY).
- **AUM:** Rs 22,035 crore (+37% YoY).
- **Gold Loan AUM:** Rs 11,917 crore (+77% YoY); Gold disbursements: Rs 5,822 crore (+31% YoY).
- **Mortgage AUM:** Rs 9,982 crore (+13.5% YoY); Mortgage disbursements: Rs 855 crore (+12% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 130.8 crore (+63% YoY).
- **ROA:** 2.8% (up 24 bps QoQ); **ROE:** 17% (up 153 bps QoQ).
- **GNPA:** 1.8% (vs 1.6% in Q1); **NNPA:** 1.2%.
- **Credit Costs:** Rs 27.1 crore (0.6% of average total assets annualized).
- **Capital Adequacy:** 21% (up from 20.7% in Q1).
- **Book Value per share:** 84.4.

### Highlights
- **Branch Expansion:** Opened 74 branches in Q2 (Total: 831); management plans to add at least 150 more in H2.
- **Gold Loan Transition:** Gold GNPA increase attributed to the shift from bullet to periodic interest-due model; management maintains this is technical/behavioral, not a structural credit concern.
- **Deleveraging:** Executed direct assignment of Rs 700 crore of gold loans to improve capital adequacy.
- **Doorstep Gold Loan:** AUM reached Rs 1,851 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Growth:** Management maintains 20%-25% entity-level growth guidance for FY27.
- **Profitability:** Targeting 3% ROA and 18% ROE.
- **Credit Costs:** Guidance remains below 1%.
- **H2 Outlook:** Management expects H2 to be stronger due to seasonality and increased branch productivity.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Gold Loan Competition:** Management described Q2 competitive intensity as "heightened" but stated they have multiple levers (yield, LTV, growth) to maintain ROA.
- **Small Ticket LAP:** Approval rates have improved to early 40% levels (from 30% previously); management expects better growth and yields in H2.
- **Market Dynamics:** Management noted that while banks operate in a different yield orbit, NBFC competition is aggressive; they are focusing on specific geographies to optimize yields.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Opex:** Opex to average total assets stood at 4.9% (vs 4.8% in Q1); Cost-to-income ratio was 52.5% (vs 52.8% in Q1).
- **Borrowing Costs:** Weighted average cost of borrowing remained flat at 7.80%.
- **Yields:** Core loan book yield remained constant at 16.40%.
- **Fixed Rate Borrowings:** Constitute 33% of the book.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Branch Economics:** A branch typically reaches profitability (Rs 6-7 crore AUM) in 12-13 months.
- **Synergies:** Management is increasingly using gold loan branches to distribute Small Ticket LAP products where market potential exists.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Debt-Equity Ratio:** Improved to 4.6% (from 4.9% in Q1).
- **Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR):** 35.4% (down from 38.4% due to higher proportion of gold loans in Stage III, which carry lower provisioning).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Asset Quality:** Gold loan Stage II increased from 2.7% to 3.6% and Stage III from 1.6% to 1.8% due to the new periodic interest-due model.
- **Competitive Intensity:** Management acknowledged pressure on yields in both gold and LAP segments.
- **Interest Rates:** Management noted potential headwinds from interest rate cycles but believes their mix of fixed and MCLR-linked borrowings provides insulation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Is the rise in Stage II/III gold loans a new normal?** A: Management stated it is a technical/behavioral transition due to the new regulatory model and does not reflect credit risk; they do not expect it to translate into credit costs.
- **Q: Can you upgrade guidance given strong H1?** A: Management declined, citing the volatile environment and preference for consistency; they prefer to stick to the 20-25% AUM growth guidance.
- **Q: How will you handle NIM compression from rate hikes?** A: Management stated they will use multiple levers (Opex, credit cost, yield) to absorb shocks and maintain ROA/ROE targets.
- **Q: Why did small ticket LAP yields drop?** A: Management clarified it was a mix issue (lower-yield states performed better in Q2) rather than a structural yield decline; expects improvement in H2.

### One-line takeaway
Fedbank Financial Services delivered a record-performance quarter with 17% ROE and 37% AUM growth, successfully managing regulatory transitions in gold lending while maintaining conservative guidance despite intense market competition.
