# IIFL Capital Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Operational PBT grew QoQ, but distribution income declined significantly due to the absence of seasonal insurance and lumpy transactional income.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Operational Revenue:** INR631 crore (flat QoQ and YoY).
*   **Retail Broking Revenue:** INR297 crore (flat QoQ; up 13% YoY).
*   **Institutional & Investment Banking Revenue:** INR207 crore (up 27% QoQ; flat YoY).
*   **Financial Product Distribution (FPD) Income:** INR125 crore (down 31% QoQ; down 14% YoY).
*   **Operational PBT:** INR149 crore (up 4% QoQ; down 9% YoY).
*   **Employee Cost:** INR179 crore (flat QoQ and YoY).
*   **Finance Cost:** INR60 crore (down 5% QoQ; up 50% YoY from INR40 crore).
*   **Average Daily Turnover:** INR3,15,780 crore (F&O: INR3,12,480 crore; Cash: INR3,300 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Fairfax Investment:** Fairfax India Holdings Corporation proposed increasing shareholding to at least 51% via a preferential equity issuance of INR2,000 crore at INR350 per share and a mandatory open offer. Shareholders approved the preferential issue on June 1, 2026; regulatory approvals (SEBI, NSE, BSE, IRDAI) are pending.
*   **Other Income:** Increased by INR90 crore, driven by mark-to-market (MTM) gains on BSE shares.
*   **Income Tax:** The company received a demand of INR124 crore following a January 2025 search; appeals have been filed.
*   **AUM Growth:** Net collections were approximately INR3,675 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Fairfax Transaction:** Management expects to receive all regulatory approvals in the next 2 to 3 months.
*   **AUM Growth:** Management expects to "catch up" on AUM growth in the next quarter.
*   **Regulatory Impact:** Management does not expect significant impact on volumes or turnover in the current quarter from the July 1st SEBI/RBI margin requirement regulations.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro Environment:** Management noted global uncertainty, geopolitical developments, and commodity price volatility (crude at $100/barrel) as factors influencing investor sentiment.
*   **Manufacturing Strategy:** The company is focusing on strengthening its manufacturing capabilities, including AIF, PMS, a credit fund, and a late-stage fund, while maintaining an "open architecture" model.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Fees & Commission Expenses:** Decreased 8% QoQ to INR139 crore, linked to lower variable payouts from the seasonal insurance business.
*   **Finance Cost:** Increased YoY to INR60 crore due to growth in the Margin Trading Facility (MTF) book.
*   **Yields:** The implied yield on distribution declined to 87 bps due to a shift in revenue mix (less non-ARR and insurance income).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Human Capital:** Added a small number of Relationship Managers (single-digit headcount increase).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Capital Base:** The proposed INR2,000 crore infusion from Fairfax is intended to strengthen the capital base and support growth across wealth management, asset management, and institutional equity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Pending regulatory approvals for the Fairfax transaction.
*   **Taxation:** Outstanding tax demand of INR124 crore currently under appeal.
*   **Market Volatility:** Potential impact of global geopolitical developments and crude oil prices on Indian markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How much of the AUM growth was organic and why was it slower?**
    *   *A:* Management stated net collections were INR3,675 crore and characterized the slower growth as a potential "one-off."
*   **Q: Have the Fairfax funds been received and what is the plan if the deal fails?**
    *   *A:* Management confirmed funds have not been received and are subject to regulatory approvals; they did not address the contingency of the deal failing.
*   **Q: What is the impact of the July 1st SEBI/RBI margin regulations?**
    *   *A:* Management noted only "marginal impact" so far as they do not engage in significant proprietary trading, but noted it is "too early to comment" on long-term effects.
*   **Q: Can you provide a breakup of Institutional Equities vs. Investment Banking?**
    *   *A:* Management estimated the split is roughly 50-50 or 60-40 within the INR200 crore revenue bucket.

### One-line takeaway
IIFL Capital Services is awaiting regulatory approval for a transformative INR2,000 crore capital infusion from Fairfax, while navigating a quarter of flat revenue and a decline in distribution income due to seasonal factors.
