# SBI Cards and Payment Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong PAT growth driven by improved credit costs, robust card additions, and encouraging early trends in the festive season.

### Key numbers
- **Cards in Force (CIF):** ~2.32 crore (up 8% YoY); added over 1 million new accounts (11,06,000 cards) during Q2.
- **Total Spends:** Rs 1,10,689 crore (up 3% YoY); retail spends at Rs 95,299 crore (up 6% YoY).
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 5,141 crore (up 4% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 778 crore (up 75% YoY).
- **Operating Cost:** Rs 2,649 crore (up 7% YoY); cost-to-income ratio at 58.7% (up 193 bps YoY).
- **Receivables:** Rs 58,376 crore.
- **Interest-Earning Assets (IEA):** Stable at 55%; revolver rate at 22%.
- **Portfolio Yield:** 16.1%.
- **Daily Weighted Average Cost of Funds (COF):** 6.8% (up 18 bps QoQ).
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** 10.8%.
- **Gross Credit Cost:** 5.6% (improved 98 bps QoQ and 342 bps YoY).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.9% (down 14 bps QoQ and 95 bps YoY); Net NPA at 0.77%.
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** >26%.
- **ROA:** 4.4% (up 180 bps YoY); ROE: 18.4% (up 631 bps YoY).

### Highlights
- Launched Google Pay Flex SBI Card and Advantage SBI Card on SBI YONO (secured by fixed deposit).
- Maintained a balanced sourcing mix: 49% from Banca and 51% from the open market.
- UPI spends on credit cards grew 17% QoQ, totaling Rs 21,300 crore during the quarter.
- Added an additional management overlay provision of Rs 30 crore, taking total additional provisions to Rs 100 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Reclassified about 10% of the portfolio in account classification granularity during Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain a quarterly new account run rate of more than 1 million.
- Retail spend growth guidance for the entire year is maintained at mid-teens to high teens (14% to 16%+ range).
- Cost-to-income ratio guidance for the year is maintained at 57% to 59%.
- Gross credit cost for the current financial year is expected to stay around 6% (subject to geopolitical impacts).
- NIM is expected to remain range-bound despite rising cost of funds.
- Receivables and spends are expected to pick up in H2 FY27, led by the festive season starting in Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's real GDP growth for FY27 projected by RBI at 7.1%.
- Industry cards in force crossed 124 million as of August 2026 (up 10.5% YoY); cumulative spends for the first 5 months of FY27 exceeded Rs 10 trillion.
- Credit card customer base is younger (nearly half of first-time users aged 30 or below) and shifting to Tier 2, Tier 3, and rural markets (46% of new users).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Working capital loans are cheaper than CPs by 70 to 80 bps.
- Portfolio yields are being protected by increasing subvention rates and EMI pricing by ~50 bps and building a larger EMI-based portfolio.
- Opex increased due to investments in business growth despite a slight dip in corporate spends.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity position remains strong with a capital adequacy ratio over 26%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Prolonged conflict in West Asia, higher energy prices, inflation, and rising interest rates may impact household cash flows.
- Cost of funds is expected to increase over the next few quarters due to repo and T-bill rate hikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kunal Shah (Citi) asked about the trajectory of spends translating into receivables and NIM margin pressures.** Management stated that H2 (starting Q3 due to later festival dates) will see receivable growth, and NIMs will be protected via EMI mix shifts and yield hikes.
2. **Piran Engineer (CITIC International) asked about EMI yield hikes and borrowing optimization.** Management confirmed a ~50 bps hike in POS installment lending and post-EMI conversion rates, and noted optimization across tenors, instruments, and lenders.
3. **Anuj Singla (JP Morgan) asked if credit cost guidance of 6% is overly conservative given current 5.5% levels.** Management clarified that 5.5% to 6% balances risk and return, and no further structural moderation is expected.
4. **M.B. Mahesh (Kotak Securities) asked about risk profiles of cohorts driving spend growth.** Management responded that spend patterns are stable, growth is driven by RuPay/UPI cards, and proactive risk filtering has kept cohorts healthy.
5. **Anand Dama (Nuvama) asked about spend-per-card trends and Stage 2 asset movements.** Management noted that spend per card will rise above 170k in Q3 due to festivals, and Stage 2 minor increases are quarterly seasonal variations.
6. **Nitin Aggarwal (Motilal Oswal) asked if risk filters are being relaxed to accelerate card growth.** Management stated risk filters have not been relaxed; growth is achieved through better targeting, data analytics, and robust incoming application volumes.

### One-line takeaway
SBI Cards delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with a 75% YoY jump in PAT to Rs 778 crore, underpinned by multi-year low credit costs, disciplined opex, and strong new card additions, while management braces for upcoming cost-of-fund pressures through EMI mix optimization and yield hikes.
