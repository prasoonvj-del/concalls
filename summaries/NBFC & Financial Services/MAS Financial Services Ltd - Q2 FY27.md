# MAS Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported robust growth across AUM and profitability, maintaining its long-term growth guidance despite interest rate headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated AUM:** Rs 17,042 crore (up 23.31% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT (Q2):** Rs 115 crore (up ~26% YoY).
*   **Standalone AUM:** Rs 16,011.38 crore (up 23.17% YoY).
*   **Standalone PAT (Q2):** Rs 110.05 crore (up 22.69% YoY).
*   **MAS Rural Housing AUM:** >Rs 1,000 crore (up 25.53% YoY).
*   **Gross NPA (Standalone):** 2.63%.
*   **Net NPA (Standalone):** 1.70%.
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 22.12%.
*   **Average Cost of Borrowing:** 9.19% (down 43 bps YoY).

### Highlights
*   Crossed the Rs 17,000 crore consolidated AUM milestone.
*   MAS Rural Housing subsidiary crossed Rs 1,000 crore AUM with a 55% YoY profit growth.
*   Management maintains a self-propelling capital model; no plans to raise capital in the near future.
*   Distribution mix: 68% direct, 32% via NBFCs.
*   Tech adoption: Loan origination system with Business Rule Engine (BRE) is reducing origination and operational costs.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains 20% to 25% AUM growth guidance.
*   Management aims to reach Rs 1 lakh crore AUM by 2036.
*   Management expects borrowing costs to increase by 20-25 bps due to repo rate hikes.
*   Management remains confident in maintaining ROA between 2.75% and 3%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Credit demand remains strong across segments.
*   Management is cautious on import/export, transportation/logistics, and metal-related businesses.
*   El Nino/drought impact: Only 10% of the portfolio is in affected states, with direct agri exposure <1%; management does not foresee significant asset quality stress.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   70-75% of borrowings are fixed or MCLR-linked, providing a buffer against immediate rate hikes.
*   65-70% of assets are fixed rate, allowing for repricing on shorter tenures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Strategy focuses on increasing "centres" rather than just physical branches to optimize infrastructure costs.
*   Management plans to increase branch count from 210 to ~300 over the next 2 years, with expansion focus in North and South India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Debt-to-equity ratio: 3.50x.
*   Liquidity: Maintained ~Rs 1,000 crore on balance sheet.
*   Off-book composition: 17% (Target range: 20-25%).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Interest rate hike cycle: Management expects a 20-25 bps increase in borrowing costs.
*   Asset quality: Broad-based delinquency uptick in the "Wheels" portfolio.
*   Macro risks: Potential disruptions in energy or logistics could trigger a more cautious lending stance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Abhijit Tibrewal (Motilal Oswal) asked about drought impact:** Management stated they see no stress as they do not lend directly to agri-income customers and have stringent underwriting.
*   **Deep Vakil asked about rising GNPA:** Management attributed the rise to the seasoning of the retail portfolio and the "Wheels" segment, emphasizing that they focus on ROA rather than just NPA fluctuations.
*   **Ishank Gupta asked about cost of funds:** Management expects the exit rate for cost of funds to be in the 9.4% to 9.5% range for FY27.
*   **Aditya asked about branch strategy:** Management clarified that they are increasing "centres" (1,000+ added in the last 4-5 quarters) rather than just branches to manage costs.
*   **Meghna Luthra asked about SME restrictions:** Management noted they are currently cautious on import/export, logistics, and metal-related businesses.

### One-line takeaway
MAS Financial Services delivered strong Q2 results with 23% AUM growth and remains confident in its 20-25% growth guidance, leveraging a self-propelling capital model and diversified distribution.
