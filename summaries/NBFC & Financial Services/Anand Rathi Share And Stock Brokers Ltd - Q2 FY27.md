# Anand Rathi Share And Stock Brokers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong growth in revenue and profitability, emphasizing business resilience despite market volatility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 targets for MTF book (Rs 1,750 crore to Rs 1,800 crore) and Distribution AUM (40% growth).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained positive; management highlighted that structural drivers of Indian capital markets remain intact despite short-term turbulence.
- **New topics or risks:** Addressed the decline in net cash position (attributed to clients converting cash margins to G-Secs) and the spike in employee expenses (one-time impact of moving outsourced staff to payroll).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Management confirmed they are on track, having achieved 57% of the annual MTF target in H1.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 2,732 million (Q2), Rs 5,193 million (H1).
- **PAT (before exceptional items):** Rs 419 million (Q2, +50% YoY), Rs 810 million (H1, +60% YoY).
- **PAT (after exceptional items):** Rs 653 million (H1, +29% YoY).
- **Broking revenue:** Rs 1,551 million (Q2, +34% YoY), Rs 2,831 million (H1, +25% YoY).
- **MTF interest income:** Rs 496 million (Q2, +36% YoY), Rs 924 million (H1, +43% YoY).
- **Distribution income:** Rs 327 million (Q2), Rs 603 million (H1).
- **MTF book:** Rs 1,499 crores (as of Sept 30, 2026).
- **Distribution AUM:** Rs 99,990 million (+29% YoY).
- **Assets under custody:** Rs 1.15 trillion (+13% YoY).
- **Debt-equity ratio:** 1.02.

### Highlights
- Digital platform contributed 36% of brokerage income in H1.
- 71% of active clients are located in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
- Network includes 99 branches and 1,071 authorized persons across 314 cities.
- 55% of clients have been associated with the company for >3 years.
- MTF book NPA is zero.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets 15% to 20% YoY growth in top line and 20% to 30% YoY growth in bottom line.
- Management aims to continue scaling the financing and distribution business while increasing recurring revenue streams.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Market sentiment remained subdued due to geopolitical tensions, rising interest rates, inflation, and El Nino.
- Nifty 50 declined 5.22% QoQ.
- Primary market was buoyant with 108 IPOs raising ~Rs 90,000 crores in Q2.
- Industry MTF book grew ~41% YoY to Rs 156,261 crores.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee expenses increased ~31% due to RM base expansion, product team strengthening, and moving outsourced employees to the payroll.
- MTF borrowing cost is ~9.5%; NIM on MTF book is ~4.5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus remains on strengthening existing business lines, specifically insurance, mutual funds, PMS, and AIF products.
- Management is actively strengthening the NRI business segment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position declined; management explained this is due to clients converting cash margins into G-Secs to meet regulatory requirements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Market volatility and uncertainty.
- Potential for further interest rate hikes.
- Regulatory changes impacting trading volumes (e.g., RBI circular on capital market exposure).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why did distribution income grow only 4% while AUM grew 29%?** A: Management explained this is due to a shift toward trail-based income and regulatory changes in ARN transfer cooling periods (now 12 months), which created a temporary aberration.
- **Q: What drove the 31% increase in employee expenses?** A: Management clarified it was a one-time impact from moving outsourced staff to the payroll, alongside ongoing RM base expansion.
- **Q: What is the strategy for the MTF book?** A: Management stated the book is for existing clients only, with strict internal evaluation criteria; they noted zero NPA over the last 8 years.
- **Q: Why did the net cash position decline?** A: Management explained that HNI clients converted cash deposits into G-Secs to provide security margins, leading to a change in format rather than a business outflow.

### One-line takeaway
Anand Rathi delivered steady growth in H1 FY27, successfully navigating market volatility by focusing on non-broking revenue and maintaining a disciplined, zero-NPA MTF book.
