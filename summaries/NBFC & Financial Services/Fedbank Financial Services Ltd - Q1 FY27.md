# Fedbank Financial Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management reported strong operational performance, robust AUM growth, and improved return metrics despite regulatory headwinds in the gold loan sector.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursements:** ₹6,760 Cr (+14% YoY).
*   **AUM:** ₹21,136 Cr (+35% YoY).
*   **Gold Loan AUM:** ₹11,191 Cr (+77% YoY).
*   **Mortgage AUM:** ₹9,777 Cr (+14% YoY).
*   **PAT:** ₹114.4 Cr (+52.5% YoY).
*   **ROA:** 2.6%.
*   **ROE:** 15.4%.
*   **GNPA:** 1.6% (down from 1.9% in Q4).
*   **Net NPA:** 1.0%.
*   **Credit Costs:** 0.8%.

### Highlights
*   **Regulatory Transition:** Effective April 1, RBI revised the LTV framework for gold loans, requiring onboarding LTVs on bullet loans to be calculated on the total amount due at maturity. The company shifted to a periodic interest-due structure to comply.
*   **Leadership Changes:** George Oommen appointed Business Head – Gold Loans; Shardul Kadam moved to Chief Transformation Officer; Jagadeesh Rao assumed additional responsibility for Small Ticket LAP and Home Loans.
*   **Shareholding:** True North Fund LLP exited its 6.86% stake; Nomura India Equity Fund acquired the shares.
*   **Doorstep Gold Loan:** AUM grew 96.5% YoY to ₹1,787 Cr.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **AUM Growth:** Management expects 20%-25% entity-level AUM growth for FY27.
*   **Segment Growth:** Gold AUM is projected to grow 25%-30% (assuming flat gold prices); Mortgage AUM is projected to grow 15%-20%.
*   **Profitability:** Management targets ROA expansion of 20-30 bps over the FY26 average (2.4%).
*   **Credit Costs:** Guidance remains sub-1%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Gold Sector:** Industry facing headwinds from a 15% decline in domestic gold prices (Jan 31 to June 30) and regulatory changes.
*   **Asset Quality:** Management views the rise in reported overdue levels as "customer behaviour in transition" due to new regulatory structures rather than an asset quality concern.
*   **LAP Competition:** Medium Ticket LAP is facing yield pressure; management is prioritizing yield over aggressive disbursement growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Opex:** Operating expenses declined 2.4% QoQ. Opex to average total assets improved 70 bps QoQ to 4.8%.
*   **Cost-to-Income:** Improved 400 bps QoQ to 52.8%.
*   **Borrowing Costs:** Weighted average Cost of Borrowing (COB) fell 3 bps QoQ.
*   **DA Income:** Reported negative ₹13 Cr due to a deliberate strategy to reduce reliance on Direct Assignment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Branch Expansion:** No new branches opened in Q1, though premises were identified. Management maintains the guidance of 200 new branches for FY27, with a spillover effect expected in Q2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **CRAR:** 20.71% (down from 22.4% in Q4).
*   **Leverage:** Increased from 4.6 to 4.89, attributed to regulatory transition issues with co-lending partners causing more assets to be booked on-balance sheet.
*   **PCR:** 38.36%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory Impact:** New RBI guidelines on gold loans are causing an "optical" rise in Stage 1 and Stage 2 delinquencies.
*   **Operational Costs:** Management expects sourcing-related expenses and operating costs to increase in coming quarters as originations pick up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did branch expansion stall in Q1?** A: Management clarified that premises were identified but openings were delayed; expects spillover in Q2.
*   **Q: Why did leverage increase?** A: Due to regulatory transition issues with co-lending partners, more assets were booked on-balance sheet; management expects this to normalize.
*   **Q: Explanation of negative DA income?** A: Management explained it is due to the unwinding effect of past DAs being higher than income from new DAs; the company is consciously reducing DA reliance.
*   **Q: Impact of new gold loan regulations on LTV/competition?** A: Management stated NBFCs are well-positioned; the industry is shifting to quarterly/monthly interest-due products to manage LTVs.
*   **Q: Why the drop in LAP disbursements?** A: Management noted they are prioritizing yield and quality over aggressive disbursement growth amid competitive pressure.

### One-line takeaway
Fedbank Financial Services delivered strong Q1 FY27 growth (35% YoY AUM increase) and improved profitability (15.4% ROE) while navigating regulatory shifts in gold lending and maintaining a sub-1% credit cost guidance.
