# 360 ONE WAM LTD - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in AUM and revenue, with management expressing confidence in the "flywheel" strategy and path to improved operating leverage.

### Key numbers
*   **Total ARR AUM:** Rs 3,42,000 crore (+19% YoY).
*   **Wealth AUM:** Rs 2,42,000 crore (+24.2%).
*   **Asset Management AUM:** Rs 1,00,000 crore (+8.2%).
*   **Overall AUM:** Rs 7.8 lakh crore (+17%).
*   **ARR Net Flows:** Rs 10,815 crore (vs Rs 8,985 crore in Q4).
*   **Wealth Net Flows:** Rs 13,379 crore (vs Rs 6,957 crore in Q4).
*   **ARR Revenues:** Rs 614 crore (+20.3% YoY).
*   **Total Revenue:** Rs 870 crore (+20% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 330 crore (+14.8%).
*   **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 51.3% (vs 53.5% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Tangible ROE:** 19.4%.

### Highlights
*   ARR revenue comprises 75% of total revenue from operations.
*   ARR retention at 74 bps (Wealth at 71 bps, Asset Management at 83 bps).
*   HNI segment: 60+ relationship managers, 12 locations, 800+ clients, Rs 5,000+ crore AUM.
*   Institutional equity franchise: 550+ mid/small-cap coverage, 300+ institutional clients.
*   Exceptional cost of Rs 12-13 crore recorded, related to ESOPs from the B&K acquisition.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects the cost-to-income ratio to improve to 49-49.5% by Q4 FY27, with a 100-150 bps improvement for the full year.
*   HNI business is expected to break even on direct costs this year.
*   ET Money business is expected to reach break-even by the end of FY27.
*   Management targets 350-400 senior RMs over the next three years.
*   Management expects to launch funds via the UBS collaboration in the coming quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Wealth creation at the top of the pyramid is outpacing the broader economy.
*   Private credit in India is at a "nascent stage" with significant growth potential; management sees it growing as fast as the private equity industry.
*   Global allocations to listed equity have been muted over the last 6-9 months.
*   Clients are migrating from transactional, product-driven engagement to portfolio-level, solution-oriented mandates.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Total cost: Rs 446 crore.
*   Retention decline (from 78 bps to 73-74 bps) is attributed to carry recognition (2-2.5 bps) and business mix.
*   Advisory retention: 30-35 bps; Discretionary: 40-45 bps; Distribution: 65-70 bps.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   HNI segment: Plans to migrate clients in the Rs 2-10 crore segment to the HNI team over the next three months.
*   ECM (Investment Banking): Building out the team; management expects to pitch actively starting Oct-Dec 2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   One large institutional mandate outflow in the asset management business caused negative net flows in that segment.
*   Retention pressure in the pure listed asset management business.
*   "Lumpy" transaction brokerage income is being reduced in favor of more annuity-like revenue.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is the net flow lopsided towards wealth?** A: Management expects a 70:30 or 75:25 split in favor of wealth; asset management flows should average out with upcoming product launches.
*   **Q: Cost-to-income trajectory?** A: Management targets 100-150 bps improvement for the year, driven by HNI/ET Money break-even and operating leverage.
*   **Q: UBS collaboration unit economics?** A: Management declined to provide specific unit economics but stated it is mutually accretive and expects $500-600 million in asset transfers over time.
*   **Q: Retention pressure?** A: Management clarified it is largely due to carry recognition and business mix, not margin compression.
*   **Q: CEO continuity?** A: Management (Karan Bhagat) stated his role is independent of equity shareholding and he remains fully invested in the business.

### One-line takeaway
360 ONE WAM delivered strong 20% revenue growth, driven by robust wealth flows, while management focuses on scaling HNI and ET Money segments to drive operating leverage and margin expansion.
