# NBFC & Financial Services - Fintech & payments: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the NBFC, Fintech, and Payments sector reflects robust top-line momentum, accelerating digital adoption, and a strong festive-season tailwind. Across the 11 companies analyzed, demand remains healthy, driven by credit penetration in Tier-2/3 cities, cross-border remittance growth, and enterprise software adoption. Companies are increasingly focusing on operating leverage, disciplined underwriting, and cash-flow generation over unbridled customer acquisition. However, regulatory headwinds—particularly IRDAI’s consultation papers on commission structures and growing debt/leverage concerns in retail lending—have introduced an undercurrent of caution among established distributors and lenders.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements universally report strong seasonal tailwinds from the Navratri and Dussehra/festive periods, alongside accelerating secular demand for digital credit, merchant solutions, and software-as-a-service (SaaS) offerings. 
*   **Divergence:** While digital lenders and B2B SaaS platforms (such as *Apollo Finvest*, *Moneyview*, *Kissht*, *Veefin*) note surging organic demand and high-conviction growth pipelines, consumer-facing digital platforms like *PB Fintech* report a tougher demand environment in Q2 compared to the high-base, GST-driven tailwinds of the previous year. Similarly, *WSFx Global Pay* faced headwinds in retail leisure travel due to geopolitical conflicts and US student market dips, which were offset entirely by strong UK/Australia student segment demand.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Cost of Borrowing & CAC:** Lenders like *Moneyview* and *Kissht* reported a downward trend in incremental borrowing costs (down to ~12.7% and ~13.76% respectively) aided by rating upgrades and legacy debt roll-offs. Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC) generally compressed or remained stable due to improving organic funnels.
*   **Tech, Employees & COGS:** Turnkey hardware procurement costs temporarily spiked operating expenses for infrastructure providers like *NPST* and *Pine Labs* as they built international deployment credentials. Tech-heavy firms (*Zaggle*, *Veefin*) absorbed salary expansions from recent acquisitions (e.g., Dice) and product development transitions, with *Veefin* noting the tail-end of its capitalization cycle.
*   **Margins:** Operating leverage is gradually expanding EBITDA and PAT margins across players like *One Mobikwik Systems*, *Moneyview*, and *Veefin*, while *Zaggle* consciously prioritized operating cash flow over short-term margin expansion (guiding EBITDA margins to stay at 7%–8%).

## Capex & capacity
*   CapEx expansions are primarily concentrated in technological infrastructure, platform security, and physical/field footprint scaling. 
*   *Pine Labs* reported elevated CapEx due to slip-overs of chip-related procurements from Q4 and required 80% vendor advances, alongside plans to deploy 1 million soundboxes. 
*   *Apollo Finvest* and *Veefin* noted adequate capital headroom without immediate needs for external equity raising, while *One 97 Communications* maintained its device CapEx target (~₹400 crore). International expansions (e.g., UAE, ADGM, Southeast Asia) drove localized subsidiary investments for *NPST*, *Pine Labs*, and *Zaggle*.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Upgrades/Cuts:** 
    *   *Kissht (OnEMI)* raised FY27 AUM growth guidance from "40% plus" to "60% plus" and impairment reduction targets to >15%.
    *   *NPST* maintained its 60%–70% revenue growth target while adjusting full-year EBITDA margin expectations to ~25%.
    *   *Zaggle* shifted focus away from rigid annual top-line targets toward disciplined cash-flow generation.
*   **Tone Shifts & Deliverables:** Tone shifted from cautious to high-conviction for digital lenders (*Kissht*, *Moneyview*), whereas *PB Fintech* turned more prudent due to regulatory overhangs. Deliverables were met across board items such as *Zaggle* completing 65%-70% of Dice novations and *Kissht* achieving LAP breakeven a quarter ahead of schedule.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Finalization and implementation timelines of proposed IRDAI commission structure regulations and their impact on insurance aggregators like *PB Fintech*.
*   Execution and revenue realization from recently secured massive multi-year contracts, such as *NPST’s* 300+ bank central LOI and international telecom wins.
*   Adoption rates and bottom-line pass-through of the impending UPI MDR implementation across transaction aggregators (*One 97 Communications*, *One Mobikwik Systems*, *Pine Labs*, *Zaggle*).
*   Progress on international market scaling (UAE, Southeast Asia) and newly acquired perpetual AD-II/remittance licenses (*WSFx Global Pay*).

## Company one-liners
*   **Apollo Finvest India Ltd:** Rapid organic volume scaling via Apollo Cash has positioned the platform for high-margin tech-led lending growth.
*   **Moneyview Ltd:** Delivered a strong inaugural post-IPO quarter with 16% QoQ PAT growth and falling borrowing costs.
*   **Network People Services Technologies Ltd:** Bagged landmark domestic central bank and international telecom contracts, securing its aggressive 60%–70% growth trajectory.
*   **OnEMI Technology Solutions Ltd:** Raised its AUM growth guidance to 60%+ while aggressively lowering credit costs through strict high-quality customer filtering.
*   **One 97 Communications Ltd:** Capitalizing on steady payment momentum and anticipating major EBITDA tailwinds from upcoming UPI MDR implementation.
*   **One Mobikwik Systems Ltd:** Marked its fourth straight profitable quarter with record earnings, positioning its merchant and lending verticals for scale.
*   **PB Fintech Ltd:** Core insurance and health verticals grew at >40%, though earnings visibility remains clouded by proposed IRDAI distribution payout caps.
*   **Pine Labs Ltd:** Displayed broad-based 24% revenue growth supported by offline market share gains and expanding international issuing/acquisitions.
*   **Veefin Solutions Ltd:** Delivered triple-digit group revenue growth with expanding margins and improving DSO as product capitalization tapers.
*   **WSFx Global Pay Ltd:** Reached record Q2 turnover fueled by student remittances and newly expanded AD-II licensing frameworks.
*   **Zaggle Ocean Services Ltd:** Prioritized operating cash flow discipline and successful Dice integration over short-term margin expansion.
