# Geojit Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew YoY, but profitability was pressured by high operating costs and market volatility; management is pivoting toward productivity over aggressive expansion.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained "no forward-looking statements" policy.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from a focus on "strategic investment" to "cautious" due to geopolitical headwinds and market volatility; management is now prioritizing productivity of existing staff over new hiring.
- **New topics:** Disclosed DIFC AUM of USD 2 million; Yield Plus AIF AUM grew to Rs 248 crore.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Hiring has slowed as promised; tech transformation investments continue as planned.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 165.82 crore (up 2.5% YoY, 3.38% QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 36.7 crore (H1: Rs 78.23 crore).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 20.62 crore.
- **Recurring Revenue AUM:** Rs 26,737 crore (H1).
- **Lending book:** Rs 780 crore (vs Rs 735 crore YoY).
- **SIP book:** Rs 156 crore (1.78 lakh clients).
- **Asset Management (PMS/AIF) AUM:** Rs 2,018 crore (vs Rs 1,449 crore in March).
- **Insurance gross premium:** Rs 175 crore (Q2).
- **Client base:** 17.25 lakh (added 61,657 in H1).
- **Offices:** 529 (vs 525 in Q1).

### Highlights
- Recurring income now accounts for 40% of total income (vs 25% in FY23).
- Asset management net inflows increased to Rs 322 crore in Q2 (vs Rs 71 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Employee benefit expenses increased by Rs 8.17 crore YoY due to tech team expansion, DIFC recruitment, and sales incentives.
- Market correction in September negatively impacted reported customer assets, which remained broadly stable at Rs 1.12 lakh crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they will not provide forward-looking statements.
- Focus for H2 is on increasing productivity of the existing field force rather than aggressive hiring.
- Management plans to significantly increase recurring revenue to reduce cyclicality risk.
- Expectation that EBITDA margins may remain subdued for 1-2 quarters if capital market activity remains weak.

### Demand & industry commentary
- NRI clients in the GCC are in a "wait-and-watch" mode due to geopolitical tensions and currency movements.
- Cash market activity has been subdued, impacting transaction-linked income.
- Cross-sell penetration remains low: <5% for life insurance and <3% for health insurance.
- Management noted that recurring revenue products are less sensitive to market drops compared to brokerage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins were impacted by high employee costs and ongoing IT transformation projects.
- Management emphasized that tech investments are "must-investments" to gain competitive edge.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No aggressive branch expansion planned; focus is on Tier 2/3 locations where costs are lower.
- No new international ventures planned beyond the small Bahrain investment previously announced.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Regarding cash returns to shareholders: Management is prioritizing strategic opportunities first; if none materialize, they will consider buybacks.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situation (Middle East) impacting NRI business sentiment.
- Market volatility and correction affecting AUM valuation.
- Subdued cash market activity impacting transaction-linked revenue.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: What are the measurable benefits of tech investments?** A: Management noted a 15% uptick in customer onboarding speed and improved operational efficiency in customer support.
- **Q: Why did bottom line not grow with revenue?** A: Management explained that high investments in technology and sales headcount were deliberate, but hiring will now be muted due to market conditions.
- **Q: How is the DIFC platform performing?** A: It is in a nascent stage with 50 relationships and USD 2 million AUM.
- **Q: Is the brokerage business being neglected?** A: Management clarified they are not pushing F&O/day trading because clients often lose money; they focus on long-term delivery-based business.
- **Q: How sensitive is recurring income to market levels?** A: Management stated it is not very sensitive compared to brokerage; a 6% market drop does not cause a proportional drop in recurring income.

### One-line takeaway
Geojit is prioritizing long-term recurring revenue growth and tech-driven productivity, but near-term profitability remains constrained by high operating costs and a cautious stance on expansion amid geopolitical headwinds.
