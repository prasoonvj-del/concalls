# Tata Capital Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated AUM crossed Rs 3 lakh crore milestone, PAT grew 51% YoY, and asset quality remained resilient with stable Stage 3 assets.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated AUM: Rs 3,08,030 crore (up 26% YoY, 6% QoQ)
- AUM excluding Motor Finance: up 32% YoY
- Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 1,660 crore (up 51% YoY, 7% QoQ)
- Return on Assets (RoA): 2.3% (2.5% excluding Motor Finance)
- Return on Equity (RoE): 14%
- Gross Stage 3 Assets: 1.9%
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 59%
- Annualized Credit Cost: 0.9% (vs 1.0% in Q1 FY27)
- Tata Capital Housing Finance AUM: Rs 95,098 crore (up 26% YoY, 6% QoQ)
- Tata Capital Housing Finance PAT: Rs 552 crore (RoA 2.5%, RoE 18.0%)
- Total Borrowings: Approximately Rs 2.61 lakh crore
- Available Liquidity: Approximately Rs 36,400 crore
- Cost of Funds: 7.33% (vs 7.28% in Q1 FY27)
- Cost-to-Income Ratio: 35.3% (improved by 438 bps YoY)
- Consolidated Equity: Rs 47,778 crore; Debt-to-Equity: 5.5x
- Standalone Capital Adequacy: 17.3% (CET1 at 14.7%)

### Highlights
- Consolidated AUM crossed the Rs 3 lakh crore milestone.
- Retail and SME businesses account for 85.4% of consolidated AUM.
- High-margin retail disbursements grew 40% YoY; AUM growth increased to 14% (up from 10% in Q1).
- Branch network expanded by net 24 branches to approximately 1,515 branches across 1,094 locations in 26 states and union territories.
- ALCO approved a 20 to 25 bps increase in PLRs across floating-rate products, effective November 1, 2026.
- De-recognition of associates included a private equity fund exit resulting in a gain shown in LODR, with Rs 27 crore flowing into Tata Capital's PAT.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Rajiv Sabharwal) maintained loan growth guidance of 23% to 25% for FY27 and over the 2025–2028 period.
- Management remains confident of meeting or exceeding medium-term guidance parameters.
- Capital raise is planned for the second half of FY28 (towards Q3-Q4) based on current growth trajectories.

### Demand & industry commentary
- GDP grew by 7.8% in Q1 FY27; RBI revised FY27 growth forecast to 7.1%.
- Festive demand is strong across two-wheelers, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, and dealer supply chains.
- Rural markets are seeing slower growth compared to urban areas; however, Tata Capital has limited rural exposure outside of microfinance.
- Headline inflation firmed up to 5.54% in September due to food and energy pressures; MPC raised repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% in October.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- NIM declined marginally due to a modest increase in cost of funds and competitive intensity in prime home loans.
- Floating-rate liabilities stand at 48% and floating-rate assets at 66%.
- Q3 NIM plus fee is expected to be stable, with an improving trajectory anticipated from Q4 onwards following PLR hikes.
- Cost-to-average assets remained well contained at 2.3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 23 locations in affordable and micro-housing during the quarter.
- Motor finance portfolio rundown is expected to stabilize in Q3 and resume growth in Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowing mix: Bank loans 39%, NCDs 35%, ECBs and MTNs 12%, Commercial Paper and WCDL 9%, NHB borrowings 3%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential regulatory changes on revolving credit (SRO recommendations submitted to RBI; management estimates impact at 3% to 4% of AUM if unconvertible).
- Proposed IRDAI commission capping on insurance distribution is currently at the draft stage; impact remains unquantified.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shrishti Sharma (ET BFSI) on revolving credit:** Management stated that revolving credit forms a small proportion of the book (about 2% to 2.5% retail flexi loans, with a maximum unconvertible impact estimated at 3% to 4%), and alternative products are being piloted.
- **Avinash Singh (Emkay Global) on Motor Finance asset quality and growth:** Management explained that Motor Finance GNPAs rose due to denominator impact from a shrinking book and seasonality (Q2 historically highest for slippages); growth is expected to resume in Q4.
- **Anuj Singla (JPMorgan) on SME growth and supply chain:** Management noted strong demand in supply chain finance and term loans, highlighting that auto constitutes 35% of the diversified supply chain book.
- **Kunal Shah (Citigroup) on NIMs and housing fee income:** Management attributed housing fee income growth primarily to a 58% YoY increase in assignment (DA) income, while core fee income remained flat as a percentage.
- **Viral Shah (IIFL Capital) on PLR hikes:** Management clarified that the 20–25 bps PLR hike effective November 1 responds to the October repo rate hike, and structural asset-liability mismatch supports margin stability.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Capital delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with AUM crossing Rs 3 lakh crore, 51% YoY PAT growth, and stable asset quality, while proactively addressing margin pressures through repricing and operational efficiencies.
