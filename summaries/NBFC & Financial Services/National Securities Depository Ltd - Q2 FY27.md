# National Securities Depository Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue growth driven by primary market activity and fintech partnerships, with management signaling a transition from "catch-up" to "build-up" in technology investments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained stance that tech expenses will flatten out; no specific numerical guidance provided.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "catch-up" to "build-up" mode regarding technology investments. Primary market activity rebounded strongly in Q2 compared to a "muted" Q1.
- **New topics:** Pilot launch of "Demat 2.0" (DLT-based, tokenized securities, CBDC integration); NSDL Payments Bank's role in Maharashtra's MSRTC bus travel (concessional travel).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Hiring is largely complete; management confirmed the pace of investment in critical talent has started moderating.

*   **Consolidated:**
    *   Revenue: Rs 582.6 crore (+34.8% YoY).
    *   PAT: Rs 122.5 crore (+10.9% YoY).
    *   EBITDA: Rs 178.5 crore (+12.5% YoY).
    *   EBITDA Margin: 30.6%.

*   **Standalone:**
    *   Revenue from operations: Rs 227.8 crore (+11.6% YoY, +25.1% QoQ).
    *   Total Income: Rs 279.8 crore (+11.7% YoY, +27.4% QoQ).
    *   EBITDA: Rs 171.6 crore (+6.8% YoY).
    *   EBITDA Margin: 61.3%.
    *   PAT: Rs 126.7 crore (+5.2% YoY, +42.1% QoQ).
    *   PAT Margin: 45.3%.
*   **Operational:**
    *   Total Demat accounts: 4.72 crore.
    *   Net demat additions (Q2): 15.6 lakh.
    *   Market share (incremental demat additions): 17.2% (vs 17.6% in Q1 FY27; 17.4% for H1 FY27).
    *   Fintech broker contribution to incremental sourcing: 23.5% (vs 9.3% in Q2 FY26).
    *   Custody value: USD 5.6 trillion (80% equity base).
    *   NSDL Payments Bank: 54.3 lakh retail customers (vs 33.5 lakh a year ago); Deposits: Rs 378.1 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Technology:** Pilot launch of "Demat 2.0" using distributed ledger technology (DLT) for tokenized bonds and real-time settlement via RBI's CBDC.
*   **Partnerships:** Added 4 new Depository Participants (DPs) in Q2 (10 in H1).
*   **Business Segments:** "Yuva" and "Women's Demat" plans account for ~45% of incremental demat additions.
*   **Payments Bank:** Achieved milestone with RuPay NCMC Smart Cards for MSRTC (Maharashtra) bus travel; subsidiary contribution to profit is now >10%.
*   **Leadership:** Appointed Ankit Sharma (ED, Regulatory and Compliance) and Ashish Dandekar (CTO).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Technology Spend:** Management expects tech expenses to be "flattish" compared to last year; focus shifting from "catch-up" to "build-up" (innovation/AI/cybersecurity).
*   **Market Share:** Management identified increasing retail market share as "priority number 1."
*   **IPO Pipeline:** Management expects the current momentum in IPO activity to continue.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Primary market raised >Rs 1.17 lakh crore in first 9 months of 2026, with Rs 90,000 crore raised in Q2 FY27 alone.
*   Total industry demat accounts crossed 24 crore; 90 lakh added in Q2 FY27 (vs 79 lakh in Q2 FY26).
*   Unlisted issuer segment: Market share at 72.5%; management noted the segment has "flattened out" after previous regulatory-driven growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Standalone EBITDA margin (ex-dividend) was 58.6%; PAT margin (ex-dividend) was 41.4%.
*   Capitalized Rs 23.9 crore in technology in H1 FY27.
*   Management noted the pace of investment in critical talent has started moderating.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Not explicitly discussed beyond tech capitalization figures.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Standalone net worth: Rs 2,263.4 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Pricing:** No progress on price increases for issuer/settlement charges despite ongoing discussions with regulators for over 1 year.
*   **Market Dependency:** Future growth remains subject to market conditions and regulatory evolution.
*   **Demat 2.0:** Still in pilot/sandbox stage; no global experience to lean on; currently restricted to institutional bond market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: When will the negative operating leverage from high tech spend end?** A: Management stated the base effect will kick in, and they expect expenses to flatten from next year as they move from "must-do" to "good-to-do" spending.
*   **Q: Status of price hikes for issuer charges?** A: Management confirmed there has been no development or outcome despite the ongoing conversation with regulators.
*   **Q: Potential for MDR/UPI revenue?** A: Management stated it is a "gradual process" and they have not quantified potential benefits while UPI MDR remains on "pause mode."
*   **Q: Why is the unlisted company onboarding slowing?** A: Management believes the market is at a "saturated cycle" after onboarding ~63,000 companies over the last 2-3 years.
*   **Q: Is Demat 2.0 a first-mover advantage?** A: Management clarified they are moving together with CDSL under SEBI's initiative, not acting alone.

### One-line takeaway
NSDL is leveraging strong primary market tailwinds and fintech partnerships to drive growth while transitioning its heavy technology investment cycle toward innovation, though regulatory-driven pricing pressure remains an unresolved headwind.
