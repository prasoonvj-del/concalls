# Manba Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong growth in NII and PAT, successful expansion into South India, and a clear strategy for product diversification.

### Key numbers
*   **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 42 crore (up 36% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 13 crore (up 36% YoY).
*   **Assets Under Management (AUM):** Rs 1,731 crore (up 22% YoY).
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 226 crore (up 37% YoY).
*   **Gross NPA:** 3.41%.
*   **Net NPA:** 2.52%.
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** 24.40%.
*   **Average Cost of Borrowing:** 10.86%.
*   **ECL Provision:** Rs 25 crore (vs IRAC requirement of Rs 7.57 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Product Mix:** Two-wheeler loans (84.1%), top-up loans (5.1%), three-wheeler loans (3.1%), used vehicle loans (1.5%). Over 95% of the portfolio is secured.
*   **Strategic Expansion:** Entered South India via partnership with Sreesastha (operating as Nammaloan) in Karnataka; Tamil Nadu entry planned for this fiscal year.
*   **New Product Launches:** Commenced MSME Loan Against Property (LAP) and battery replacement finance for electric three-wheelers.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** 60% of loans sanctioned in one minute; 92% sanctioned on the same day. 85% of collection is handled in-house.
*   **Dividend:** Declared first interim dividend of Rs 0.25 per equity share (face value Rs 10).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **AUM Growth:** Management expects 35% to 40% growth for FY27.
*   **Portfolio Diversification:** Management aims to reduce two-wheeler loan dependency from ~84% to 65% over the next three years by scaling personal loans, top-up loans, used two-wheeler loans, and MSME LAP.
*   **Profitability:** Management targets 3.5% ROA for FY27 and expects Net Interest Margins (NIM) to remain in the 13% to 14% range.
*   **Capital Raise:** Management plans to raise Rs 100 crore via preference shares by September/October 2026 to support growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted that Q2 and Q3 are historically the strongest quarters for AUM growth due to the festival season (Raksha Bandhan, Ganpati, Dussehra, Diwali).
*   The company is focusing on UP, MP, and Rajasthan for geographic expansion, alongside the new South Indian market.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Borrowing cost increased by 15-20 bps due to maintaining higher liquidity (Rs 200 crore) during the quarter.
*   Borrowing mix: 60% term loans, 25% NCDs, remainder PTC and CC.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   90% of technology (LOS, LMS, LAS) is proprietary.
*   Management plans to spend significantly on personal loan software this year.
*   No plans to enter new states beyond current expansion (UP, MP, Rajasthan, South India) for the remainder of the fiscal year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Liquidity position remains healthy; the company reduced debt in Q1 due to existing liquidity of Rs 350 crore as of March 31, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   None explicitly flagged by management, though they acknowledged the need to manage the transition of the product mix and the 9-12 month break-even period for the new South India partnership.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Capital Adequacy:** Management confirmed the CAR decline (from ~29% to ~24%) is anticipated and will be addressed by raising Rs 100 crore via preference shares.
*   **Nammaloan Partnership:** Management clarified this is a BC (Business Correspondent) model with 100% funding from Manba; expects Rs 60-75 crore AUM from this partner in FY27.
*   **Battery Replacement Finance:** Management explained this is a Rs 60,000 ticket size product targeting existing customers to prevent vehicles from becoming NPAs after 15 months of use.
*   **Collection Cost:** Management highlighted that 85% in-house collection is a key USP, keeping opex lower than peers who rely on third-party agencies.
*   **MSME LAP Strategy:** Management targets ticket sizes of Rs 8-20 lakh, focusing on shop owners.

### One-line takeaway
Manba Finance is aggressively diversifying its product mix away from two-wheelers while leveraging in-house collections and digital speed to maintain growth, supported by a planned capital raise.
