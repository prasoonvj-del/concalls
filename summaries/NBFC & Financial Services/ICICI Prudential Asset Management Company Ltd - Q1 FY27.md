# ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-16 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27 (ended June 30, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY growth in PAT (23.1%) and revenue (17.6%), supported by resilient equity AUM and positive mark-to-market gains.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Mutual Fund Quarterly Average AUM:** Rs 11.17 lakh crore (+18.3% YoY, +1.1% QoQ).
*   **Equity & Equity-oriented AUM:** Rs 6.31 lakh crore (+19.8% YoY, +1.7% QoQ).
*   **Operating Revenue:** Rs 1,564 crore (+17.6% YoY).
*   **Operating Profit Before Tax:** Rs 1,100 crore (+20.2% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 965 crore (+23.1% YoY).
*   **Operating Margins:** 36.9 bps (vs 36.1 bps in Q1 FY26).
*   **Alternates Quarterly Average AUM:** Rs 79,446 crore.
*   **Investment Book:** Rs 4,225 crore (approx. 50% seed capital).

### Highlights
*   Maintained position as the 2nd largest AMC with a 13.4% market share.
*   Retained leadership in active schemes (13.5% market share) and equity-oriented schemes (14% market share).
*   Equity-oriented hybrid schemes AUM reached Rs 2.22 lakh crore (26.6% market share).
*   Launched 4 Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) strategies; SIF AUM at Rs 2,678 crore.
*   Other income turned positive to Rs 181 crore due to mark-to-market gains.
*   Operating expenses: Rs 464 crore (+11.7% YoY, +14.3% QoQ).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to continue regular product launches over the next 9 months to build a comprehensive product bouquet.
*   Management plans to launch Life Cycle Funds (2031, 2036, 2041) in the near future.
*   Management intends to launch new series of commercial real estate products and other alternate investment ideas.
*   Management believes the SIF category has long-term potential but remains at an early stage of evolution.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Industry quarterly average AUM: Rs 83.28 lakh crore (+15.4% YoY).
*   June 2026 industry SIP contribution: Rs 31,781 crore.
*   Management noted that SIP flows rebounded in June 2026 after a dip in May 2026.
*   Management observed that the industry is shifting from a "push" to a "pull" product model due to lower charges.
*   Debt segment experienced institutional redemptions due to tight liquidity conditions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Annualized margins: Equity (66 bps), Debt (32 bps), Liquid (12 bps), Passives (12 bps), Arbitrage (30 bps).
*   Gross yield (PMS/AIF): 1.91%; Net yield: 0.95%.
*   Management confirmed no negative impact from TER regulation changes.
*   Operating expense increase was driven by employee expenses, including ESOP charges not present in the previous year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Present in 300+ towns; expansion decisions are dynamic and managed at the regional level.
*   Launched first inbound fund in GIFT City; management is evaluating further inbound and outbound opportunities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   No specific debt or working capital issues flagged; liquidity remains a focus for institutional clients.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Institutional redemptions in the debt segment due to tight liquidity.
*   Market volatility impacting SIP flows (stoppages vs. new SIPs).
*   International advisory business impacted by FII selling.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the sequential increase in employee expenses?** Management attributed it to ESOP charges (which were not in the P&L last year) and annual salary hikes effective April 1st.
*   **Q: Why the decline in debt AUM?** Management explained that institutional investors are redeeming funds to support working capital requirements amid tight liquidity.
*   **Q: What is the strategy for SIF/Alternates?** Management aims to build track records first; noted that SIFs are currently a small part of the P&L.
*   **Q: Is there a structural change in SIP behavior?** Management stated there is no structural change; SIPs are the standard way India saves, and performance depends on the quality of sales.

### One-line takeaway
ICICI Prudential AMC delivered strong Q1 FY27 results with 23.1% YoY PAT growth, driven by resilient equity AUM and positive MTM gains, despite institutional outflows in the debt segment.
