# Manappuram Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by 223% YoY surge in consolidated PAT to Rs 701 crore and 92.5% YoY expansion in core gold loan AUM.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Gold loan AUM growth target maintained at 25%-30% for FY27; ROA target of 3.5%-4% and ROE of 15%-18% maintained; exit cost of borrowing expected to reach ~9%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone remains strongly positive on gold loan demand; management noted domestic gold prices moderated from peak levels and remained volatile, underscoring prudent LTV management.
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter:** Rise in gold loan Stage 2 loans to ~5% (up from ~0.5% in Q1 FY27) due to customer behavior adjusting to newly introduced short-tenor/EMI products, prompting a calibrated shift back toward traditional 1-year bullet loans. Management transitions: former CEO resigned on personal grounds (new CEO recruited to join Jan 1, 2027) and CTO resigned due to frequent travel.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Target of adding 500 branches by March 2027 maintained (~100 added in H1 FY27, 250 in full swing). CEO recruitment target fulfilled with joining date confirmed for Jan 1, 2027.

---

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated AUM:** Rs 73,303 crore (up 5.3% QoQ, 60.1% YoY).
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 3,356 crore (up 10.2% QoQ).
- **Consolidated PAT (before OCI & minority interest):** Rs 701 crore (up 19.9% QoQ, 223% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT at Rs 1,286 crore (up 268% YoY).
- **Consolidated Net Worth:** Rs 17,289 crore; Book value per share: Rs 184.2.
- **Standalone AUM (MAFIL):** Rs 63,733 crore (up 4.5% QoQ, 68.5% YoY).
- **Standalone Gold Loan AUM:** Rs 58,097 crore (up 6.3% QoQ, 92.2% YoY), accounting for 91% of standalone AUM.
- **Consolidated Gold Loan AUM:** Rs 60,640 crore (up 6.4% QoQ, 92.5% YoY), forming 83% of consolidated AUM.
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 665 crore (up 20% QoQ, 77% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT at Rs 1,217 crore (up 58.4% YoY).
- **Standalone GNPA & NNPA:** 1.79% (vs 1.56% in Q1 FY27) and 1.24% respectively; Provision Coverage Ratio at 31.3%. Gold GNPA stood at 1.04%.
- **Standalone Credit Cost:** 1.4%.
- **Asirvad Microfinance:** AUM at Rs 7,919 crore (includes gold AUM of Rs 2,508 crore); MFI disbursements at Rs 1,461 crore (vs Rs 1,055 crore in Q1); PAT at Rs 32 crore (vs Rs 21 crore in Q1); Net NPA at 1.1%; CRAR at 29.2%.
- **Vehicle Finance AUM:** Rs 2,169 crore (down 15.3% QoQ, 46.5% YoY); GNPA at 16.9% (vs 13.4% in Q1).
- **MSME & Allied AUM:** Rs 3,361 crore (disbursements at Rs 238 crore); GNPA at 6.4% (vs 5.9% in Q1).
- **Home Finance AUM:** Rs 1,814 crore (down 1.7% QoQ, 4.5% YoY); disbursements at Rs 121 crore.
- **Capital Adequacy (CRAR):** 22.36% (standalone at 22.3%).
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 1 per share.

---

### Highlights
- Added 3.15 lakh new gold loan customers during Q2 FY27, taking total active customer count to 27.2 lakh (up 11.7% YoY).
- Online Gold Loan (OGL) accounts for ~91% of the total gold loan portfolio.
- Average gold loan portfolio LTV stood at 64.5% (vs 65.6% in Q1 FY27).
- Auctioned gold loans worth Rs 100 crore in Q2 FY27 (total Rs 115 crore in H1 FY27) at an average gold price of Rs 13,526 per gram.
- Paused credit disbursements in Vehicle Finance to focus on collections and rundown of the book until April 2028.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- **Gold Loan AUM Growth:** Guided at 25%-30% (targeting ~30%) for FY27, backed by branch expansion and digital channels.
- **Return Ratios:** Maintained target ROA of 3.5%-4.0% and ROE of 15%-18%.
- **Gold Loan Yields:** Management expects long-term yields to settle around 17%-18% (gross yield range of 17.5%-18.5%).
- **Cost of Borrowing:** Expected to rise by 25-30 bps towards the 9.0% handle by Q4 exit.
- **Asirvad MFI:** Calibrated growth expected to continue at steady current rates into next year.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic gold prices remained high YoY despite softening from peak levels, supporting collateral value while driving customer borrowing against household gold.
- Formalization of credit continues as borrowing shifts from unorganized to regulated lenders.
- Microfinance industry showing gradual recovery in collections and disbursements, though regional conditions remain non-uniform.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone borrowing cost declined by 2 bps QoQ in Q2 FY27.
- Branch operating expenses and employee costs to AUM are expected to trend down despite branch additions, aided by 91% OGL adoption and field staff rationalization.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- On track to add ~500 branches by March 2027 to the existing network of 5,375 branches (~100 added in H1 FY27; work underway for 250 more).

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity remains adequate with unutilized fund lines and cash buffers; CRAR at 22.36% is comfortably above the 15% regulatory minimum.
- Stage 2 gold loans increased to ~5% of gold AUM (vs ~0.5% in Q1 FY27), driven by customer transition to EMI/shorter-tenor products. Management noted early collection trends are showing regularizations.
- Included management overlays of ~70-80 bps within standalone credit cost/provisions as a prudent buffer. Reclassified Rs 14 crore of ARC sale losses into provisions.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated GNPA in Vehicle Finance at 16.9% and MSME segment at 6.4%.
- Gold price volatility necessitating strict LTV management and prudent appraisal practices.
- Temporary spike in Stage 2 gold loans due to product transition.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gaurav Gupta (Capital Farming Consultants):** *Asked about senior management stability following resignations post Bain Capital deal.*
  **Management:** Clarified that the former CEO resigned on personal grounds (replacement recruited, joining Jan 1, 2027) and CTO resigned due to travel between Mumbai and Kerala. Stated there is high stability among new recruitments with no other attrition.
- **Rajiv Mehta / Gaurav Khandelwal:** *Inquired about the reason for the spike in Stage 2 gold loans and strategy to address it.*
  **Management:** Explained that Stage 2 gold loans reached ~5% due to customer behavioral adjustment to new shorter-tenor and EMI-based schemes. Stated that early collection reads are encouraging as customers regularize payments upon contact, and the company is gradually shifting focus back to 1-year term products.
- **Shubhranshu Mishra:** *Asked if Manappuram is engaging in a price war by offering teaser rates <1% per month.*
  **Management:** Denied entering a price war; stated pricing is kept competitive to retain customers for long-term sustainability while maintaining board-approved pricing policies and sticking to guided 3.5%-4% ROA and 15%-18% ROE metrics.
- **Piran Engineer (CLSA):** *Asked about opex trends amid 500 branch additions.*
  **Management:** Responded that employee costs to AUM will fall because 91% of gold loans are online (OGL) and existing field employees are being rationalized without affecting service.

---

### One-line takeaway
Manappuram posted strong Q2 FY27 results with consolidated PAT up 223% YoY to Rs 701 crore on 92.5% gold AUM growth, while maintaining disciplined risk controls to manage a temporary Stage 2 gold loan uptick.
