# Manappuram Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by strong 60.1% YoY growth in consolidated AUM, a sharp surge in PAT to Rs 701 crore, and robust momentum in the core gold loan business.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained gold loan AUM growth guidance of 25%-30% for FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains positive on gold loans with strong customer additions, though management noted emerging behavioral shifts and Stage 2 spikes in newly introduced short-term/EMI products, prompting a calibrated shift back toward traditional bullet loans.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Detailed discussion on behavioral Stage 2 asset quality spikes in newer short-term/EMI gold loan products, and management overlays taken as a prudent buffer.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Branch expansion target of ~500 new branches by March 2027 is reiterated, with nearly 100 opened so far and the rest in full swing.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated AUM: Rs 73,303 crore (up 60.1% YoY, 5.3% QoQ)
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 3,356 crore (up 10.2% QoQ)
- Consolidated PAT (before OCI and minority interest): Rs 701 crore (up 223% YoY, 19.9% QoQ)
- Standalone AUM: Rs 63,733 crore (up 68.5% YoY, 4.5% QoQ)
- Standalone Gold Loan AUM: Rs 58,097 crore (up 92.2% YoY, 6.3% QoQ)
- Standalone PAT: Rs 665 crore (up 77% YoY, 20% QoQ)
- Standalone GNPA: 1.79%; Net NPA: 1.24%; Provision Coverage Ratio: 31.3%
- Gold Loan GNPA: 1.04%; Stage 2 gold loans: ~5%
- Standalone Credit Cost: 1.4%
- Capital Adequacy (CRAR): 22.3% (Standalone); Asirvad CRAR: 29.2%
- Consolidated Net Worth: Rs 17,289 crore; Book Value per share: Rs 184.2
- Asirvad MFI AUM: Rs 7,919 crore; Disbursals: Rs 1,461 crore; PAT: Rs 32 crore; Net NPA: 1.1%
- Vehicle Finance AUM: Rs 2,169 crore (down 46.5% YoY, 15.3% QoQ); GNPA: 16.9%
- MSME and allied AUM: Rs 3,361 crore; Disbursements: Rs 238 crore; GNPA: 6.4%
- Home Loan AUM: Rs 1,814 crore; Disbursements: Rs 121 crore
- Average Gold Loan LTV: 64.5%
- Interim Dividend: Rs 1 per share

### Highlights
- Core gold loan business accounts for 83% of consolidated AUM, growing 92.5% YoY to Rs 60,640 crore.
- Online gold loan (OGL) book constitutes ~91% of the total gold loan book.
- Added 3.15 lakh new customers during the quarter; total customer base reached 27.2 lakh (up 11.7% YoY).
- Vehicle finance disbursements remain paused with the business in orderly wind-down/run-down mode; reassessment planned for April 2028.
- Management created prudent discretionary management overlays (accounting for ~70-80 bps of credit costs) over and above ECL provisioning.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects gold loan AUM growth of 25%-30% for FY27.
- Exit cost of borrowing expected to increase toward the 9% handle (up 25-30 bps).
- Long-term ROA targeted at 3.5%-4% and ROE at 15%-18%.
- Gold loan yields expected to settle around 17%-18% over the next three years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian economy shows underlying resilience supported by consumption, services, and public infrastructure investment.
- Domestic gold prices remain elevated compared to a year ago, supporting collateral value and customer access to secured financing despite recent volatility and moderation from peak levels.
- Borrowing demand is structural, shifting from informal lenders to regulated entities as households use gold loans for liquidity without asset sales.
- Microfinance industry shows improving collection trends and gradual recovery in disbursements, though conditions vary.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone borrowing cost decreased by 2 bps QoQ in Q2 FY27.
- Opex-to-AUM expected to trend down as OGL and technology reduce branch manpower requirements despite adding 500 branches.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned addition of ~500 new branches by March 2027 (nearly 100 added in H1, with the remaining 250+ in full swing).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity is supported by cash, liquid assets, unutilized fund lines, and diversified funding channels.
- Total auction amount for the quarter was Rs 100 crore (INR 115 crore for H1). Average gold price for auctions/evaluations cited at Rs 13,526 per gram.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Spike in Stage 2 gold loans (reaching ~5%) due to customer unfamiliarity with newly introduced short-term and EMI-based products following April regulatory changes.
- Vehicle finance GNPA elevated at 16.9% (vs 13.4% prior quarter).
- Intensifying competition from banks and NBFCs expanding reach.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gaurav Gupta (Capital Farming Consultants):** Asked about gold loan delinquencies/Stage 2 compared to peers, branch expansion status, and management attrition. *Management stated LTV is a low 65% with Stage 3 at 1% for gold, branch expansion of 500 is on track with 100 done and 250+ underway, and noted a couple of resignations (CEO and CTO) with a new CEO joining Jan 1.*
- **Rajiv Mehta (Yes Securities):** Inquired about gold loan product breakup (bullet vs monthly/EMI) and Stage 2 movement. *Management clarified that over 90% is traditional bullet loans, while Stage 2 moved up to single digits due to behavior around newer products, which customers are regularizing via contact.*
- **Shubhranshu Mishra:** Asked about yield impact of EMI products, auction quantum, and market price wars. *Management stated combined yields will hold at 17.5%-18.5%, auction was Rs 100 crore at Rs 13,526 per gram, and denied engaging in a price war while keeping rates attractive.*
- **Sanket:** Inquired about the exact Stage 2 percentage and management overlays. *Management quantified Stage 2 gold AUM at ~5% and stated overlays constitute about 70 to 80 bps of credit costs.*
- **Deep Vakil:** Asked about Income Generating Gold Loans (IGGL) vs consumption loans. *Management stated IGGL is a very small component and is being introduced gradually with cash flow underwriting, while consumption loans form the majority.*

### One-line takeaway
Manappuram posted a stellar Q2 FY27 with consolidated PAT surging 223% YoY to Rs 701 crore, driven by robust 92% growth in gold loans, even as management navigated a behavioral uptick in Stage 2 assets for newly launched short-term products.
