# Religare Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Care Health insurance delivered strong growth and profitability, and Religare Finvest resumed lending operations, but REL reported a consolidated H1 net loss and the demerger remains pending with the RBI.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (demerger remains a high-priority item; Care Health aims to bring combined ratio down; RFL targeted Rs 1,500 to Rs 1,700 crore disbursements by March 2028).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from preparation to execution as RFL officially resumed lending and new leadership/board members took charge.
- New topics or risks: RBI approved new promoter/shareholder directors (Burmans and Jimeet Modi) on the REL board. RFL restarted lending in Uttar Pradesh.
- Last quarter's promises: RFL restarted lending as promised; Care Health raised additional Tier 2 capital (Rs 300 crore in Q2 and Rs 150 crore in October).

### Highlights
- RBI approved the appointment of Dr. Anand Burman, Aditya Burman, Mohit Burman, and Jimeet Modi as promoter and shareholder directors on the REL board.
- Religare Finvest resumed lending operations, opening branches in Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow, Kanpur, Noida) and disbursing its first loan just before Diwali.
- Religare Broking's long-term rating was upgraded by CRISIL to CRISIL BBB+ Stable from CRISIL BBB Stable.
- Care Health raised Rs 300 crore in Tier 2 capital in Q2 and another Rs 150 crore in October 2026.
- REL infused Rs 100 crore into Religare Broking via a rights issue in August.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Ajay Shah) stated Care Health's combined ratio is expected to near 100% by March 2028.
- Management (Prakash Jejani) guided for RFL disbursements of Rs 1,500 to Rs 1,700 crore by March 2028.
- RFL management targets a long-term ROA of 2.5% to 2.8% and a book yield of ~15.5%.
- Management reiterated that the demerger/restructuring remains a high-priority item under engagement with the RBI.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broking market activity was subdued during Q2 with regulatory changes weighing on trading volumes.
- Care Health retail business growth remains robust with a long runway; multi-year policies range between 18% and 20%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Care Health Ind AS insurance revenue grew 29% to Rs 6,119 crore for H1; insurance service result increased >170% to Rs 129 crore.
- Care Health debt yields remain consistent in the region of 7.2% to 7.3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Religare Broking continues to invest in its trading platform, next-generation architecture, and talent.
- RFL plans to open its next phase of branches in Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- RFL reported zero net NPA and a CRAR exceeding 230%.
- Care Health investment portfolio consists of 92% bonds and G-Secs allocated to sovereign and highest-rated instruments.
- Out of Rs 1,500 crore earmarked for Care Health warrants, Rs 619 crore of proceeds have been received so far, with remaining conversions due by March 2027.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Proposed demerger scheme approval remains pending following previous RBI non-accession, requiring continued regulatory engagement.
- Care Health solvency continues to be calculated on I-GAAP numbers per current regulations, while P&L is reported under Ind AS.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rajiv (Motilal Oswal) & Hitaindra Pradhan asked about Care Health's profitability and claims on a 1/n basis.** Management explained that 1/n accounting reflects transitional years with opening/closing reserve impacts, and normal profitability on that basis should resume around October 2027, while advising focus on Ind AS numbers.
- **Manjeet (Neo Group) asked about Care Health solvency requirements and capital needs.** Management explained that the gap between I-GAAP and Ind AS net worth exceeds Rs 1,000 crore, and they await potential risk-based solvency regulations from IRDAI.
- **Yash Sedani (Integrity Ventures) asked about demerger timelines.** Management (Arjun Lamba) stated they cannot provide a timeline, but it remains a high-priority item currently under engagement with the RBI and advisors.
- **Chintan Mehta (Puniska Family Office) asked about RFL's FY28 disbursement target.** Management (Prakash Jejani) stated a target of Rs 1,500 to Rs 1,700 crore in disbursements by March 2028 as branches ramp up over 15 to 18 months.
- **Mukund (Saber Edge) asked about Care Health retail renewal loss ratios.** Management stated retail renewal loss ratios are ~75% and 3-year plus books are in the range of 79% to 80%, offset by low renewal commissions.

### One-line takeaway
Religare Enterprises marked a strategic shift from preparation to execution with RFL resuming lending and Care Health delivering strong growth, while continuing regulatory engagement on its corporate demerger.
