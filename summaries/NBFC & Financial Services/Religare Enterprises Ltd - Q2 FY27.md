# Religare Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong execution and growth in Care Health Insurance and resumption of lending at Religare Finvest, offset by consolidated losses and regulatory uncertainty on demerger.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (no formal quantitative consolidated guidance; Care Health aims to achieve ~100% combined ratio ahead of March 2028 target).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from preparation to execution as Religare Finvest resumed lending, board was strengthened with promoter directors, and Care Health sustained >35% GWP growth.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: RBI approved board appointments for Dr. Anand Burman, Aditya Burman, Mohit Burman, and Jimeet Modi. IRDAI's proposed draft regulations on distribution commission/expenses emerged as a new regulatory monitorable.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Religare Finvest delivered on its promise to restart lending, disbursing its first loan before Diwali in UP (Noida, Kanpur, Lucknow).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Total Income):** Rs 2,390 crore in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 Total Income reached Rs 4,717 crore (up 19% YoY).
- **Consolidated Loss Before Tax:** Rs 128 crore in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 Loss After Tax stood at Rs 145 crore.
- **Religare Broking (RBL):** Q2 total income grew 9% YoY to Rs 97.4 crore; PAT stood at Rs 2.5 crore. H1 PBT grew 29% YoY to Rs 13 crore. Assets under custody exceeded Rs 56,000 crore.
- **Religare Finvest (RFL):** Q2 total income rose to Rs 44.8 crore (vs Rs 20 crore in Q2 FY26); Q2 PAT stood at Rs 49 crore. Net NPA is nil; CRAR > 230%. Tangible net worth is Rs 963 crore with Rs 660 crore in cash/liquid investments.
- **Religare Housing (RHDFCL):** Q2 net loss was Rs 8 crore (vs Rs 5 crore loss in Q1 FY27). AUM stood above Rs 240 crore across 15 branches.
- **Care Health Insurance:** H1 GWP grew 35% YoY to Rs 6,897 crore (full premium basis); Retail GWP grew 44% YoY. H1 Ind AS PBT rose 42% YoY to Rs 392 crore. Ind AS Loss ratio improved by 90 bps YoY to 66.1%; Combined operating ratio improved by 70 bps YoY to 100.5%. Investment AUM reached Rs 12,900 crore.

### Highlights
- RBI approved promoter and shareholder directors Dr. Anand Burman, Mr. Aditya Burman, Mr. Mohit Burman, and Jimeet Modi onto REL's board.
- RFL resumed retail lending after 10 years, launching operations across Noida, Kanpur, and Lucknow, with Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra scheduled next.
- CRISIL upgraded Religare Broking’s long-term rating to CRISIL BBB+ Stable from CRISIL BBB Stable.
- Care Health raised Rs 300 crore in Tier-2 capital during Q2 FY27 and an additional Rs 150 crore in October 2026; a Rs 300 crore rights issue is planned for November 2026.
- Corporate offices moved to Mumbai.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Religare Finvest:** Management targets disbursement of lower than Rs 100 crore in FY27, scaling to Rs 1,500–1,700 crore in FY28, with long-term AUM target of Rs 10,000–15,000 crore in 4–5 years. Target portfolio yield is ~15.5% with target ROA of 2.5%–2.8%.
- **Religare Housing:** Housing finance scale-up is scheduled to start in January 2027.
- **Care Health:** Management expects to grow faster than the health insurance industry and reach ~100% combined ratio ahead of its original March 2028 target.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Market trading activity in Q2 was subdued due to regulatory changes weighing on trading volumes, impacting broking revenues.
- Health insurance tailwinds remain strong; Care Health's retail market share stands at 12.8% in overall industry and 24% among SAHIs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- RBL seen higher employee costs due to strategic hiring/increments and higher finance costs alongside funded book growth.
- RHDFCL operating expenses increased to ~Rs 17 crore for H1 due to depreciation and ongoing IT transformation.
- Care Health's debt investment yield remained stable between 7.2% and 7.3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- REL infused Rs 100 crore into Religare Broking via a rights issue in August 2026 to scale Margin Trade Financing.
- REL committed Rs 250 crore capital into RHDFCL to support expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- REL consolidated net worth stood at ~Rs 3,000 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Out of Rs 1,500 crore total warrant commitment, Rs 619 crore has been received (including Rs 243 crore incremental conversion over the last year), with the remaining Rs 880 crore scheduled by March 2027.
- Care Health solvency ratio stood at 1.55x. Net worth difference between I-GAAP and Ind AS stands at over Rs 1,000 crore for Care Health.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential short-term pain from proposed IRDAI distribution guidelines, which may require bringing variable services in-house and adjusting channel mix.
- Housing finance and corporate holdco operations continue to report net losses during the build-out phase.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rajiv (Motilal Oswal) asked about Care Health's profitability timing under regulatory accounting.**
  - *Management Response:* On an Ind AS basis, Care Health is highly profitable (H1 PBT Rs 392 crore). The reported 1/n accounting loss is due to transition rules implemented in Oct 2024; 1/n basis profitability will normalize from Q3 FY28 (October 2027).
- **Deepak Malhotra (CapGrow Capital) questioned why Care Health's expense ratio hasn't dropped despite 40%+ GWP growth.**
  - *Management Response:* New business grew over 50%, which carries higher upfront expenses compared to renewal business. Operating leverage resides mainly in the renewal book.
- **Manjeet (Neo Group) inquired about Care Health's need for external capital to fund growth.**
  - *Management Response:* Capital infusions are driven by I-GAAP solvency rules. Shifting to Ind AS or Risk-Based Capital (QIS) would add >Rs 1,000 crore to net worth. As multi-year business unwinds, I-GAAP profitability will naturally improve, reducing future capital needs.
- **Yash Sedani (Integrity Ventures) asked for updates on the demerger following the RBI rejection.**
  - *Management Response:* Management remains engaged with the RBI and advisors; demerger remains a high-priority item, but no concrete timeline can be provided yet.
- **Chintan Mehta (Puniska Family Office) asked why RFL's FY28 disbursement target is modest at Rs 1,500–1,700 crore given its capital base.**
  - *Management Response:* Opening new branches and training new teams takes 15–18 months to reach full productivity before accelerating scale.

### One-line takeaway
Religare Enterprises transitioned from planning to execution as RFL restarted lending and Care Health delivered 42% Ind AS PBT growth, while demerger timelines remain subject to ongoing regulatory discussions.
