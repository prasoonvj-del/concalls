# Sammaan Capital Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasizes a shift from stabilization to execution, citing strong disbursement growth, improved asset quality, and successful promoter-backed capital infusion.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained. Management reiterated full-year targets: Rs 30,100 crore disbursements and Rs 1,400 crore PAT.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "rebuilding/stabilization" to "scaling/execution." Management is now delegating day-to-day operations to focus on long-term strategy.
- **New topics:** Specifics on gold loan rollout (soft launch Q3, commercial Q4), appointment of BDO for internal audit, and the CEO's transition to a strategic role.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The company met its H1 disbursement target of Rs 10,000 crore.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 59,188 crore
- **Disbursements (Q2):** Rs 6,278 crore (62% QoQ growth)
- **PAT (Q2):** Rs 312 crore (28% QoQ growth)
- **PBT (Q2):** Rs 425 crore (30% QoQ growth)
- **Gross NPA:** 0.15%
- **Net NPA:** 0.11%
- **Cost of Funds:** 9.8% (Target 9.3%)
- **Promoter Stake:** Increased from 28.34% to 33.32% (via warrant conversion)

### Highlights
- IHC converted the first tranche of warrants (Rs 906 crore) ahead of schedule.
- Governance strengthened: BDO appointed as co-sourcing partner for internal audit; CRO and Compliance heads now report directly to board subcommittees.
- Branch network expanded to 245 (Target: 300 by year-end).
- Management noted that the company met its H1 disbursement target of Rs 10,123 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-year AUM target:** ~Rs 70,700 crore.
- **Full-year disbursement ambition:** ~Rs 30,100 crore (H2 target: ~Rs 20,000 crore).
- **Full-year PAT target:** Rs 1,400 crore.
- **Long-term goal:** Achieve AAA credit rating.
- Management expects net interest margins to expand as cost of funds declines and the fair value book is revalued.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management expects the NBFC retail credit market to grow from Rs 26 trillion (FY26) to Rs 37 trillion (FY28).
- The company is targeting a 2% market share in its core addressable segments (housing, personal, and gold loans).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin (sum of interest/fees/fair value less interest expense) was Rs 517 crore (2.9% of assets).
- Management expects spreads to expand in the near term as they pass on rate hikes while their own cost of funds continues to decline.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Branches:** 245 (Target 300).
- **Hiring:** 1,123 employees added in H1; 87% deployed at branches.
- **New Products:** Personal loans and business instalment loans are scaling; gold loans soft launch planned for Q3 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Capital Adequacy:** 20%.
- **Provisions:** Total provisions represent 10.2% of the loan book.
- **Funding Mix:** Target is 60% term loans/bonds, 20% assignment/PTC, and 20% dollar debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management acknowledged the need to manage operational complexity as the product suite and branch network expand.
- Rising interest rate environment noted, though management believes their declining cost of funds provides a competitive advantage.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aekansh Jain (Barclays) on rising rates:** Management stated that even if repo rates rise, their absolute cost of funds will likely decline, providing a competitive advantage over peers.
- **Varun (BlackRock) on reconciling high opex with PAT targets:** Management explained that branches and new products have a calibrated breakeven timeline (4-6 months for loans, 12-18 months for gold), supporting the growth trajectory.
- **Varun (BlackRock) on stock valuation:** Management believes the price-to-book multiple will improve as cost of funds drops and credit ratings improve.
- **Nayantara Ghosh (Deutsche Bank) on funding:** Management confirmed a target mix of 60% rupee debt, 20% assignment, and 20% dollar debt.
- **Sagar Pamnani (Bajaj Alt) on fee income:** Management noted that fee income fluctuates due to rate transmission but expects it to remain steady over time.
- **Abhishek Jain on further promoter stake:** Management stated the business is well-capitalized and currently does not require further capital beyond the committed warrants.

### One-line takeaway
Sammaan Capital is executing its transformation strategy under IHC ownership, delivering strong disbursement growth and improved asset quality while preparing for a multi-product retail expansion.
