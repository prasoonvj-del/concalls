# KFin Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-31 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, successful diversification into non-mutual fund businesses, and expects margin expansion from Q2 onwards.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Grew >30% YoY (consolidated); ~10% YoY (excluding Ascent).
*   **EBITDA:** Grew >7% YoY.
*   **PAT:** Marginal decline to flattish performance.
*   **International Business:** Grew >200% YoY (including Ascent); >32% YoY (organic).
*   **Margins (EBITDA):** 39.4% (excluding Ascent); 34.2% (including Ascent).
*   **Margins (PAT):** 26.6% (excluding Ascent); 21% (including Ascent).
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 687 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Diversification:** Non-domestic mutual fund business now accounts for ~40% of total revenue.
*   **Issuer Solutions:** Added 670+ clients; managed 80% of IPOs by market cap in Q1.
*   **Alternatives (XAlt):** Managed 731 funds with a 37.3% market share.
*   **Pensions:** Business broke even with 15%+ EBITDA margin; manages 2.5 million pensioners.
*   **Wealth Management:** Won 3 new mandates; mPower platform now used by 26 clients in India.
*   **ESG:** Score improved to 63 (from 54 in the previous year).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects 18% to 20% CAGR.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Management targets 40% to 45% range, maintaining a 40% threshold including Ascent by year-end.
*   **Earnings Growth:** Management expects 17% to 20% EBITDA growth and 12% to 15% PAT growth.
*   **Ascent Margins:** Management expects to reach double-digit margins within 12 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Mutual Funds:** Industry AUM grew ~15% YoY; KFin grew >16%.
*   **Corporate Actions:** Q1 was seasonally tepid; management expects tepid corporate action movement in Q2, particularly within IT services.
*   **Retail Participation:** Net new folio growth has decelerated; market improvement is currently driven by institutional investors rather than retail.
*   **Digital Assets:** Crypto market downturn impacted AUM and revenue, but management expects potential recovery.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** Driven by Ascent integration and transition costs.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Management is focusing on automation (e.g., SIP processing, FinEx platform), shifting from enterprise tech to open-source, and consolidating office space.
*   **Outlook:** Management expects sizable margin expansion from Q2 onwards as cost initiatives materialize.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Cayman Islands:** Setting up a facility to meet regulatory substance requirements.
*   **International:** Expanding presence in Malaysia, Philippines, and Thailand for mPower installations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash:** Rs 687 crore cash and cash equivalents.
*   **Dividend:** Rs 200 crore payout planned.
*   **Cash Flow:** 51% of EBITDA is converted into free cash flows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Yield Compression:** Driven by a shift from debt to liquid funds and potential contract renegotiations.
*   **Market Volatility:** Impact on mark-to-market gains.
*   **Corporate Actions:** Tepid outlook for Q2.
*   **Digital Assets:** Crypto market volatility impacting AUM.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Yield Compression:** Management attributed 30% of the 2% yield drop to a shift from debt to liquid funds, with the remainder due to provisions for pending contract negotiations.
*   **Ascent Margins:** Management clarified that double-digit margins are targeted within 12 months, not the upcoming quarter.
*   **Deal Wins:** Management confirmed ~Rs 40 crore in new deals signed in Q1, with varying delivery timelines.
*   **Philippines Contract:** Management clarified no official announcement has been made; they are currently in discussions (noting a media leak regarding their status as the highest bidder).
*   **Wealth Management Economics:** Management explained the model involves either fixed-fee contracts (implementation) or basis points on AUM.

### One-line takeaway
KFin Tech delivered 30% YoY revenue growth, with management upgrading earnings expectations and projecting margin expansion from Q2 as cost optimization initiatives take effect.
