# Muthoot Capital Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects robust retail loan growth, rating upgrade, improved collections, and disciplined asset quality.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs. 3,530 crore (25% YoY growth)
- **Balance Sheet Size:** Rs. 4,154 crore
- **Disbursements (Q2):** Rs. 677 crore (Cumulative H1 > Rs. 1,200 crore)
- **PAT:** Rs. 4.11 crore
- **NII:** Rs. 94.46 crore (including ~Rs. 14 crore upfront interest from a DA transaction; base NII Rs. 80.46 crore)
- **GNPA / NNPA:** 4.61% / 2.75% (PCR at 50%)
- **Total Borrowings:** Rs. 3,354 crore at an overall cost of 9.52%
- **Live Customer Base:** 586,441
- **Forward Flow to NPA:** Reduced to 0.50% (vs. 0.78%-0.85% last year)

### Highlights
- Received a rating upgrade to AA from Brickwork, enabling PAN India digital fixed deposit (FD) collections.
- Co-lending book reduced by 52% YoY to Rs. 382 crore; Q2 was the first in 48 months with 100% in-house sourcing and zero co-lending contribution.
- Moved two-wheeler credit scorecards and Business Rule Engines (BREs) entirely in-house via the data science team.
- Continues the pattern of steady momentum noted in our Q2 FY27 record, supported by strong retail disbursements and improving asset quality metrics.

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Exit Target (FY27):** Closer to Rs. 4,200 crore.
- **Borrowing Cost:** CFO targets an additional 10 to 30 bps reduction by March-end.
- **Segment Break-even:** Commercial vehicles (CV) expected to break even by the end of the year; used cars by Q1 of next year.
- **Long-term ROA Goal:** Reiterated target of ~4%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Vehicle finance industry grew 21% in H1; two-wheeler segment grew 16%.
- October retail sales were lower (20 lakh units vs 30 lakh units last year) due to festive shift timing (Navratri/Diwali/Dhanteras fell in same month last year; split into November this year).
- Management expects Rs. 400+ crore in fresh business for November.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Impairment expense for Q2 was Rs. 21.44 crore.
- Two-wheeler standalone yield sustained around 21.5% to 22%.
- Overall borrowing costs decreased; bank loans down by 0.61%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Digital FD platform scaled up; public deposits grew over 100% (total deposit book at Rs. 155 crore, up ~250% YoY).
- Focus on deepening presence in existing states rather than new geographic expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Incremental borrowing of Rs. 1,003 crore in Q2 (Bank loans Rs. 200 crore, NCDs Rs. 400 crore, PTC/Securitization Rs. 353 crore, FDs Rs. 85 crore).
- ALM and structural liquidity statements show no cumulative mismatch under any bucket; liquidity maintained at 148%.
- Cash/electronic mode collections at 83% of total instalments (NACH 41%, Bank/Electronic 42%).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Used car and CV segments are currently in the investment/cash-burning phase and do not yet stand-alone profitable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harshil (Finn Optical) on yield and growth drivers:** Management attributed yield expansion to running off lower-yielding co-lending books and implementing in-house origination scorecards that raised approval rates from ~45% to ~75%.
2. **Bhavesh Jain on public deposits:** Management explained that the AA rating upgrade for PAN India access and the online video-KYC FD platform drove the 100%+ growth in public deposits.
3. **Amit on NII growth vs. loan book growth:** CFO clarified that NII growth lags headline AUM growth due to ongoing repayments on older short-tenure books and runoff of low-yielding co-lending assets.
4. **Kshitij Verma on ROA target and Account Aggregators:** Management confirmed the 4% ROA goal, noting that new segments (CV/Used Cars) are temporarily dragging ROA during the investment phase. They also use Account Aggregators for 40-45% of two-wheeler and >65% of car/CV applications.
5. **Rahul Sahu on ARC recoveries:** Management stated that recoveries from the Phoenix ARC pool are stronger than expected and anticipate significant chunks of write-backs or collections starting next year as pools mature.

### One-line takeaway
Muthoot Capital delivered a steady Q2 FY27 with 25% AUM growth to Rs. 3,530 crore, an AA rating upgrade enabling PAN India deposit mobilization, and 100% in-house sourcing following the wind-down of co-lending.
