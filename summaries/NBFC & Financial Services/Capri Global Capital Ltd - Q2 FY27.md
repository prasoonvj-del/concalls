# Capri Global Capital Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong AUM growth, highest-ever quarterly profit of Rs. 418 crores (up 77% YoY), and improved operating leverage, despite temporary technical spikes in gold loan delinquencies.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated AUM: Rs. 43,878 crore (up 62% YoY, 9% QoQ)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs. 418 crore (up 77% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT: Rs. 772 crore
- Net Interest Income (NII): Rs. 812 crore (up 69% YoY)
- Non-interest income: Rs. 273 crore (up 34% YoY)
- Consolidated Gross Stage-3 (GNPA): 1.2% (up 22 bps QoQ); Net Stage-3: 0.8%
- Return on Average Equity (RoAE): 21.6% (Q2 FY27); H1 FY27 ROE at 20.4%
- Return on Average Assets (RoAA): 4.4% (Q2 FY27)
- Cost-to-income ratio: 41% in Q2 FY27 (improved from 44% in Q1 FY27 and 49% in Q2 FY26)
- Total branch network: 1,609 branches (net addition of 176 branches in Q2 FY27, including 1,154 dedicated gold loan branches)
- Gold loan AUM: Rs. 21,000 crore (up 102% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- MSME AUM: Rs. 7,016 crore (up 25% YoY); Disbursements: Rs. 560 crore (up 36% YoY)
- Housing AUM: Rs. 8,325 crore (up 39% YoY)
- Construction finance AUM: Rs. 7,503 crore (up 51% YoY)
- Co-lending AUM: Rs. 9,083 crore (up 60% YoY, 12% QoQ), accounting for 21% of total AUM
- Cost of borrowings: 9.2%
- Standalone Capital Adequacy Ratio: 22.7% for Capri Global Capital, 27.8% for Capri Global Housing Finance
- Total cash, bank balances, investments, and undrawn credit lines: Rs. 6,057 crore

### Highlights
- Raised $300 million via maiden U.S. dollar senior secured bond issue under GMTN program in H1 FY27.
- Added 176 branches during Q2 FY27; collateral weight in custody for gold loans reached 21 tonnes (up 28% YoY).
- Brand campaigns with actor Nayanthara helped achieve over 141 million reach and a 173%+ surge in gold loans.
- S&P Global ESG score reached 70 (ranked 7th in Asia Pacific; Industry Mover Award); CRISIL ESG score at 72; NSE ESG rating at 73.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 AUM guidance at Rs. 50,000 crore and FY27 PAT guidance at approximately Rs. 1,600 crore.
- Management reiterated medium-term targets: AUM of Rs. 65,000 crore by FY28, sustainable RoAE of ~20% to 21% (or 19% to 21%), and RoAA between 4.2% to 4.7% by FY28.
- Credit costs expected to be maintained in the range of 0.6% to 0.9% on a sustainable basis.
- Planned branch addition: targeting around 1,500 total branches by the end of the financial year (with ~500 new branches in gold loans alone for the year).
- Management expects new IRDAI draft guidelines (if implemented) to potentially reduce consolidated RoAE for FY28 and FY29 by about 1%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall operating environment was soft due to global energy shock, monetary tightening, and domestic inflation.
- Introduction of shorter-tenure repayment structures (1, 2, 3 months) in gold loans to comply with RBI guidelines created customer repayment friction, leading to technical delinquencies in Stage-2. These schemes have been discontinued.
- More than half of co-lending partner banks have transitioned to new Central Bank/RBI guidelines (CLA guidelines), causing near-term moderation offset by rising direct assignment arrangements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended yields and spreads on net advances increased to 17.5% and 8.3% respectively.
- Cost-to-income ratio improved to 42.6% in H1 FY27 from 48.1% due to operating leverage.
- Cost of borrowings increased only marginally QoQ to 9.2% due to liability diversification and repricing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 176 branches in Q2 FY27 (154 gold loan, 22 non-gold); total branches stand at 1,609.
- Employee base expanded with 1,477 net additions during Q2 FY27. Employee productivity in gold loans improved to Rs. 3.4 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings increased by 82% YoY with incremental sanction limits of around Rs. 4,428 crore across 40+ lender relationships.
- Leverage ratio is low at 3.8x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gross Stage-2 assets spiked by Rs. 1,266 crore entirely attributable to gold loans due to short-term tenure product shifts; management stated this is temporary and expected to normalize next quarter.
- Drought conditions in certain semi-urban and rural areas showed initial signs of stress in gold loans; management intends to tighten credit screens, raise FOIR, and reduce LTVs if needed.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ishank Gupta (Choice Institutional Equities) / Varun (Share India Securities) on Gold Loan Yields and Ticket Size:** Management explained that yields improved to 19% by focusing on granular ticket sizes below Rs. 3 lakhs and avoiding low-yield large ticket segments. Promotional campaigns like Vyapar Mela use introductory rates selectively for acquisition.
2. **Varun (Share India Securities) on Branch Productivity:** Average AUM per branch dropped from Rs. 19.2 crore to Rs. 18.2 crore. Management clarified this is a numerator-denominator effect from rapid new branch additions, while 18-month-old branches average around Rs. 20 crore.
3. **Ishank Gupta on Cost of Funds:** Analyst asked if a 50 bps repo hike could raise cost of funds by 25-30 bps due to MCLR resets. Management noted that MCLR resets happen with a lag (6-12 months) and will be partially offset by lower lending spreads negotiated with banks and cheaper short-term money market borrowings (CPs/NCDs).
4. **Nivedita Choudhary (ICICI Securities) on Guidance:** Management maintained FY27 AUM guidance at Rs. 50,000 crore and FY27 PAT guidance at Rs. 1,600 crore.
5. **Sagar Shah (Spark Private Wealth Management) on Co-lending Guidelines:** Management clarified that new guidelines prevent upfronting of co-lending income (now booked over the lifetime), but capital is released via downselling while the company retains the spread, making it a capital-light model.
6. **Soumya R (Nirva Securities) on Gold Loan Live Accounts vs AUM:** Management clarified that live accounts per customer declined (fewer multiple LANs per customer), but unique customer count grew 8% to 7,13,000.

### One-line takeaway
Capri Global delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with a 77% YoY jump in PAT to Rs. 418 crores and 62% AUM growth, absorbing a temporary Stage-2 gold loan spike through strong operating leverage and disciplined retail expansion.
