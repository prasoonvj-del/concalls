# NBFC & Financial Services - MSME & business lending: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is characterized by strong growth momentum, with several players reporting record-breaking disbursements and profitability. The overarching sentiment is positive, driven by robust demand in core MSME and retail segments. However, a clear bifurcation is emerging: while growth remains the priority, management teams are increasingly focused on operational efficiency (operating leverage) and navigating localized macro headwinds (drought, interest rate cycles, and regulatory shifts).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is broad agreement that credit demand remains resilient across MSME, retail, and wholesale segments.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Regional Stress:** A significant number of companies—**Aye Finance**, **Capri Global**, **Paisalo Digital**, **SBFC Finance**, and **MAS Financial**—flagged localized stress due to drought conditions or uneven monsoons in states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, MP, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana. While most are managing this via tightened underwriting, it remains a key watch item.
    *   **Segment-Specific Caution:** **CSL Finance** is actively consolidating its SME retail business due to over-leveraged borrower profiles and high competitive intensity, diverging from the aggressive growth seen in its wholesale segment. Conversely, **Indostar** is aggressively transitioning its portfolio toward higher-quality retail assets.
    *   **Gold Loans:** **Capri Global** and **SBFC Finance** noted temporary "technical" spikes in delinquencies, not due to credit quality, but due to customer friction following RBI-led shifts in loan tenure structures.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Interest Rate Environment:** Management teams are universally bracing for a higher-for-longer interest rate environment.
*   **Margin Management:**
    *   **Passing on Costs:** **SBFC Finance** increased its PLR by 25 bps in October to protect spreads. **Indostar** intends to pass on rate hikes to borrowers.
    *   **Efficiency Offsets:** **Aye Finance** and **Five-Star Business Finance** are relying on operating leverage and improved efficiency to offset margin compression. **Regency Fincorp** is targeting a reduction in borrowing costs to 10.75%–11.25% by FY27 end.
    *   **Yields:** **Five-Star** NIMs remained stable at 19.87%, while **Paisalo** expects NIMs to close at ~6%. **SG Finserve** maintained yields at ~13% but noted that interest costs as a percentage of income rose due to higher leverage.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Physical Expansion:** The "branch-heavy" model persists. **Capri Global** added 176 branches, **Aye Finance** plans 30+ new branches, **Five-Star** added 19, and **Regency Fincorp** is expanding via a hub-and-spoke model.
*   **Digital Transformation:** There is a clear push toward digital-first operations to drive efficiency. **Paisalo** is launching a consumer-facing app, **SG Finserve** is investing in an in-house AI/tech stack to reduce vendor dependency, and **SBFC** is utilizing AI voice bots for collections.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Most companies (**Aye Finance**, **Capri Global**, **Five-Star**, **MAS Financial**) maintained their full-year guidance, signaling stability.
*   **Regulatory/Technical Shifts:** The most notable change is the "technical" delinquency spike in gold loans reported by **Capri Global** and **SBFC Finance**, attributed to the transition to new repayment structures.
*   **Strategic Shifts:** **CSL Finance** hired a new CEO to oversee the SME retail vertical. **Indostar** released Rs 49 crore in management overlays, citing improved portfolio performance. **Regency Fincorp** announced a significant Rs 312 crore preferential issue to fund digital and gold loan acquisitions.

## Company one-liners
*   **ARCIL:** Delivered a 54% YoY PAT surge with a conservative 0.37x debt-to-equity ratio.
*   **Aye Finance:** Maintained full-year guidance with improving efficiency, despite localized drought risks.
*   **CSL Finance:** Achieved record disbursements while consolidating its SME retail business.
*   **Capri Global:** Absorbed a temporary gold loan delinquency spike with 77% YoY PAT growth.
*   **Five-Star Business Finance:** Reported historical best disbursements and stable NIMs of 19.87%.
*   **Indostar Capital:** Successfully transitioning to a high-quality retail portfolio with improved profitability.
*   **MAS Financial:** Crossed the Rs 17,000 crore AUM milestone while maintaining a self-propelling capital model.
*   **Paisalo Digital:** Scaled distribution to 6,415 touchpoints with 33% AUM growth.
*   **Piramal Finance:** Strong growth momentum with 67% YoY PAT growth and a successful capital raise.
*   **Regency Fincorp:** Reported a 3x YoY AUM jump and announced a Rs 312 crore fundraise for digital/gold loan expansion.
*   **SBFC Finance:** Achieved 15% ROE with stable asset quality and improving operating efficiency.
*   **SG Finserve:** Aggressively scaling supply chain financing with all-time high PAT.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Gold Loan Normalization:** Will the "technical" delinquencies at **Capri Global** and **SBFC Finance** normalize as customers adjust to new repayment structures?
*   **Rate Hike Impact:** How quickly will NIMs compress, and which companies successfully pass on costs versus those that suffer margin erosion?
*   **Regional Stress:** Monitoring if the drought-affected pockets (Maharashtra, Karnataka, MP) show signs of deeper asset quality deterioration.
*   **Digital Adoption:** Early performance metrics from **Paisalo’s** new app and **SG Finserve’s** tech stack investments.
