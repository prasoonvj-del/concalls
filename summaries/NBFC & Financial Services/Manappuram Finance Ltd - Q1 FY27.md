# Manappuram Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by strong 57% YoY growth in consolidated AUM, robust PAT growth, and improving gold loan yields.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated AUM: INR69,635 crores (up 9% QoQ, 57% YoY)
- Consolidated Gold Loan AUM: INR57,006 crores (82% of consolidated AUM)
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: INR3,033 crores (up 16% QoQ, 34% YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR585 crores (up 45% QoQ, 47% YoY)
- Standalone Gold Loan AUM: INR54,655 crores (up 12% QoQ, 97% YoY)
- Standalone GNPA: 1.56% (vs 1.8% QoQ); Credit cost: 1%
- Asirvad AUM: INR7,188 crores (up 5.8% QoQ, 7.2% YoY); PAT: INR21 crores
- Vehicle Finance AUM: INR2,562 crores (down 14.3% QoQ, 43% YoY); GNPA: 13.3%
- MSME and allied AUM: INR3,303 crores; GNPA: 5.9%
- Home Loan AUM: INR1,847 crores (flat QoQ)
- Consolidated Net Worth: INR16,552 crores; CRAR: 21.29%; Book Value per share: INR176.20

### Highlights
- Consolidated AUM crossed INR69,635 crores driven by the core gold loan business.
- Gold loan yield improved by 59 bps during the quarter due to pricing actions.
- Asirvad reported a turnaround PAT of INR21 crores (vs loss of INR269 crores in Q1 FY26).
- Board declared an interim dividend of INR1 per share.
- Appointed Mr. Ashish Singh as MD and CEO (joining by January 1, 2027).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects gold loan AUM growth of 25%-30% for FY27.
- Gold loan yields expected to hover around 18% (+/- 25 bps).
- Incremental cost of funds expected around the 8.8%-9% handle.
- Long-term consolidated microfinance share targeted below 10%.
- Target ROA of 3.5%-4% and ROE of 15%-18% over the next three years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Credit demand across banking and NBFC sectors remains healthy with resilient asset quality in secured portfolios.
- Customer profile in gold loans is gradually shifting toward the business class/MSME segment due to policy encouragement and IGA products.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone borrowing cost increased by ~10 bps in Q1 FY27.
- Attrition at the branch/gold loan level remains stable at around 2%-2.5% per month.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned expansion of ~500 new branches for Manappuram Finance following the removal of RBI prior approval requirements (~60% in South/Central India, ~25% in East).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Average gold loan LTV at 65.6% (impacted by denominator effect from gold price movement).
- Online gold loan accounts for ~86% of the total gold loan book.
- Asirvad CRAR at 31% with Net NPA at 1.4%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Vehicle finance GNPA remains elevated at 13.3% (vs 10.4% prior quarter); fresh vehicle disbursements paused for the year.
- Industry-wide stress in microfinance over past years, though Asirvad is stabilizing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shreepal Doshi (Equirus):** Asked about RBI LTV framework implementation and income-generating loans. *Management stated LTV goes up to 85% for EMI-based income-generating loans backed by cash flow assessments, priced at 14%-16%.*
- **Rajiv Mehta (Yes Securities):** Inquired about the 60 bps yield improvement. *Management attributed it to corrective pricing actions taken to align with peers, expecting yields to sustain around 18%.*
- **Bhaskar Basu (Jefferies):** Asked about branch expansion plans. *Management confirmed a target of 500 new gold loan branches for the year leveraging the relaxed RBI rules.*
- **Kushan Parikh (Morgan Stanley):** Sought FY27 gold loan growth guidance. *Management guided for 25%-30% growth for the year.*
- **Anuj Jain (ValueQuest Capital):** Asked about the long-term MFI strategy. *Management stated the intent to cap microfinance below 10% of consolidated AUM.*

### One-line takeaway
Manappuram reported a robust Q1 FY27 with consolidated AUM up 57% YoY and PAT up 47% YoY, driven by strong gold loan momentum and yield improvements, while non-gold segments remain under calibrated consolidation.
