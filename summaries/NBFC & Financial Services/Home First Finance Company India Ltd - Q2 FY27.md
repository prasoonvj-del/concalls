# Home First Finance Company India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong AUM and PAT growth, improved operating efficiency, and stable asset quality despite macro concerns.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 25% AUM growth target (Rs 20,000 crore by Mar 2027). Maintained 5.0-5.2% spread guidance. Maintained 10 bps annual reduction target for Opex-to-assets.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "high competitive intensity" to "good demand across the board" and "positivity has come back" after addressing leadership and tariff-related issues.
- **New topics or risks:** New: Discussion on insurance distribution fee draft circular (potential ~35 bps ROA impact; mitigation via LAP/assignment/pricing). Old: CFO transition (Nutan Gaba Patwari) is resolved with the appointment of Rinkoo Somani.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on branch expansion (6 added vs 4 in Q1). Delivered on team stability (Tamil Nadu returned to growth).

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 18,010 crore (+27% YoY, +6.3% QoQ).
- **Disbursements:** Rs 1,705 crore (+32.2% YoY, +4.7% QoQ).
- **PAT:** Rs 170 crore (+29.1% YoY, +6.5% QoQ).
- **NII Growth:** 31.9% YoY.
- **ROA:** 4.2%.
- **ROE:** 14.9% (+40 bps QoQ).
- **Gross Stage 3:** 1.7% (-10 bps QoQ).
- **Credit Cost:** 40 bps.
- **Cost-to-Income:** 30.8% (vs 32.7% in Q1).
- **Opex-to-assets:** 2.6% (vs 2.8% in Q1).

### Highlights
- **Network:** 181 branches and 379 touch points (added 6 branches).
- **Product Mix:** 84% individual housing loans.
- **Technology:** 4 in-house AI capabilities live (Cue, ClariFi, BankLens, DocIQ).
- **Co-lending:** Portfolio at Rs 593 crore (3.3% of AUM).
- **ESG:** Certified 100 homes in Green Homes initiative (cumulative 650).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth:** Management targets 25% AUM growth (Rs 20,000 crore by Mar 2027).
- **ROE:** Targeting 15% exit for FY27 and 16% exit for FY28.
- **Spreads:** Committed to 5.0% to 5.2%.
- **Opex:** Targeting 10 bps reduction in Opex-to-assets annually.
- **Branch expansion:** 20-25 branches per year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is "good across the board."
- Southern markets (Tamil Nadu) returned to growth (+12% in Q2).
- No significant impact from drought in MP/Gujarat as customers are in manufacturing/services, not agriculture.
- Ticket sizes: Rs 15-16 lakhs (expect gradual increase).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Yields:** 13% (origination and portfolio, excluding co-lending).
- **Cost of borrowing:** ~7.8% (incremental 7.5%).
- **Spread:** 5.2%.
- **NIM:** 6.0%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 6 branches in Q2.
- Plan to add 10-15 branches in the next 2 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **CRAR:** 39.8% (Tier 1: 39.4%).
- **Net worth:** Rs 4,665 crore.
- **Book Value per share:** Rs 446.
- **Funding:** 58% banks, 14% NHB, 20% assignment/co-lending, balance NCDs/ECB/NBFCs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Insurance distribution fee draft circular:** Potential ~35 bps impact on ROA (mitigation planned via LAP/assignment/pricing).
- **Co-lending:** Slowdown in Q2 due to new RBI guidelines implementation; management expects an uptick in Q3.
- **Rising interest rate cycle:** Potential transmission to customers if borrowing costs rise significantly.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Q: Why did co-lending drop to Rs 1.8 crore?* A: Management cited time taken to implement new RBI guidelines; expects uptick in Q3.
- *Q: Impact of insurance fee circular?* A: ~35 bps impact; management plans to offset 10 bps via concessions and 20-25 bps via LAP/assignment/pricing.
- *Q: Why is employee cost down QoQ?* A: Seasonal cycle; Q2/Q3 have lower hiring compared to Q1/Q4 campus cycles.
- *Q: Will you use LAP to boost margins?* A: It's a lever (headroom exists), but not currently needed as housing loan growth is strong.
- *Q: Any drought impact?* A: No, customer base is in manufacturing/services, not agriculture.

### One-line takeaway
HFFCIL delivered strong growth and improved profitability in Q2 FY27, leveraging tech-led efficiencies and stable asset quality while successfully navigating regulatory changes.
