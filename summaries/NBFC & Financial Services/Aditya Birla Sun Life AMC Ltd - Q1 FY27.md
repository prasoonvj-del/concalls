# Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-24 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong AUM growth, successful mandate wins (EPFO/ESIC), and improved investment performance, despite some volatility in SIP flows.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Revenue:** ₹625 crore (up 11% YoY).
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** ₹406 crore (up 9% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** ₹309 crore (up 12% YoY).
*   **Total AUM (Closing):** Crossed ₹10 lakh crore milestone as of June 30, 2026.
*   **Quarterly Average AUM (Mutual Fund):** ₹4.28 lakh crore (up 6% YoY).
*   **Equity Mutual Fund Quarterly Average AUM:** ~₹1.99 lakh crore (up 10% YoY).
*   **SIP Contribution (June 2026):** ₹1,085 crore.
*   **SIP AUM:** ~₹87,000 crore.
*   **Employee Count:** 1,638.
*   **Yields:** Equity (~63-64 bps), Debt (~24-25 bps), Liquid (~12-13 bps), ETF (~8 bps).

### Highlights
*   **Mandate Wins:** Surpassed ₹6 lakh crore in total average AUM (including Alternate assets), driven by the addition of the EPFO mandate (approx. ₹6.08 lakh crore) and ESIC mandate.
*   **Product Strategy:** Launched ABSL Select Sector Fund and several SIFs (Structured Investment Funds).
*   **Distribution:** Products added to recommendation lists of major banks including HDFC Bank, Kotak, and Standard Chartered.
*   **Digital:** Launched Gen AI-powered chatbot, new distributor/investor app, and WhatsApp-enabled servicing.
*   **Passive Business:** Appointed Hemen Bhatia as Head of Passives to drive growth; ETF quarterly average AUM grew 47% YoY.
*   **GIFT City:** Granted retail license; planning to launch retail products (emerging market equity, India growth fund) in the upcoming quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Yields:** Management expects yields to remain in the current range, barring minor fluctuations and the impact of telescoping pricing based on AUM growth.
*   **OPEX:** Employee costs are expected to remain in the current range (factoring in ~₹10 crore/quarter for new ESOPs). Other OPEX is expected to remain in line with inflation.
*   **Growth Strategy:** Management intends to focus on core flagship funds (Flexi Cap, Balanced Advantage, Multi-Asset, Small Cap) to drive flows.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Trends:** Industry quarterly average AUM stood at ₹83.14 lakh crore (up 15% YoY). SIP contribution for the industry was ₹31,780 crore (up 17% YoY).
*   **Market Sentiment:** Indian equities remain resilient despite volatility. Management noted a marginal slowdown in overall industry SIP flows.
*   **Fixed Income:** Witnessed some volatility in May 2026, impacting average assets, but flows returned by June/July.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Commission Structure:** Management confirmed the commission structure was restructured effective April 1, 2026, to be "win-win" and neutral for both the AMC and distributors.
*   **Employee Cost:** Increased due to the new ESOP plan rolled out in Q1 (effective Jan 2026), adding ~₹10 crore per quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Alternate Business:** AUM (PMS/AIF) increased to nearly ₹2 lakh crore. Management aims to build size in AIF Performing Credit and Real Estate Credit funds, targeting ₹5,000–₹7,000 crore per fund.
*   **Seed Capital:** AMC committed to providing seed capital up to 10% of fund size for new AIFs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Risks:** Geopolitical tensions, potential inflation rise, and monsoon uncertainty (El Niño).
*   **SIP Flows:** Experienced a marginal reduction in the SIP book this quarter, partly attributed to higher cancellation rates in ELSS schemes across the industry.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Yields:** Management clarified that current yields reflect the new TER regulation and are expected to remain stable.
*   **On SIP Decline:** Management explained the SIP dip was partly due to industry-wide ELSS cancellations and volatility in fixed income in May, but emphasized that core flagship schemes are seeing improved flows.
*   **On Alternate Business Revenue:** Management noted that PMS/AIF revenue contributes ~7% to gross revenue and ~4% to net revenue.
*   **On EPFO/ESIC Revenue:** Management stated these mandates provide marginal revenue but serve as a strategic entry point to capture further flows.
*   **On Passive vs. Active:** Management believes active funds will remain the dominant asset class for growth, while passive funds will be used for scale and asset allocation.

### One-line takeaway
Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC achieved a significant AUM milestone of ₹10 lakh crore, bolstered by large institutional mandates (EPFO/ESIC), while management maintains a stable outlook on yields and costs despite industry-wide SIP volatility.
