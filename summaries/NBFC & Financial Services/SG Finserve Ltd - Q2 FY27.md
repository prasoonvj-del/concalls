# SG Finserve Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved all-time high PAT and loan book, with successful execution of the "widening and deepening" business strategy.

### Key numbers
*   **PAT:** Rs 59 crore (all-time high).
*   **Loan Book (AUM):** Rs 5,700 crore.
*   **H1 PBT:** Rs 150 crore (doubled YoY).
*   **H1 Disbursements:** >Rs 16,000 crore (annualized Rs 32,000 crore; +46% YoY).
*   **ROA:** >4.5%.
*   **Cost-to-Income:** <15%.
*   **ROE (Quarterly run rate):** 15%.
*   **Net Worth:** ~Rs 1,600 crore.
*   **Collection Efficiency:** 99.5%.
*   **Yield:** ~13%.

### Highlights
*   **Strategy:** Successfully implemented widening and deepening strategy; reduced dependency on group ecosystem to 25% of the book.
*   **Product Mix:** Supply chain financing contributes ~70% of AUM. Factoring (started in Q1) now accounts for 15% of the book.
*   **Digital Lending:** Disbursed ~10,000 loans (book size Rs 121 crore).
*   **Operations:** Powering 60+ brands; team strength >100 with per-employee PAT of >Rs 2 crore.
*   **Ratings:** CRISIL rated AA- (long-term) and A1+ (short-term).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Targeting 30%-35% CAGR for AUM and 35%-40% CAGR for profitability.
*   **AUM Target:** Aiming for Rs 10,000 crore – Rs 11,000 crore in 2-3 years.
*   **Digital Lending:** Expected to contribute ~10% of overall AUM.
*   **Anchor Onboarding:** Targeting 2 new corporate anchors per month.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Position:** Management describes a "coexistence model" where they capture the "untied" portion of borrower requirements that banks/large NBFCs do not cover.
*   **Sector Exposure:** Steel and building products (35%) and Auto (20%) are the primary sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Interest Costs:** Interest cost as a percentage of income increased due to higher leverage (asset-to-equity moved from 2.8x to 3.7x).
*   **Yields:** Maintained at ~13%; management stated they can pass on cost increases to customers if necessary.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **GIFT City:** Setting up a wholly-owned subsidiary for overseas supply chain financing (long gestation, ~1 year to operationalize).
*   **AIF:** Filed application with SEBI for a Category III equity-based AIF; intended as a fee-generating business.
*   **Technology:** Investing in an in-house AI/digital stack (15% stake in tech entity) to reduce dependency on external SaaS vendors.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Leverage:** Board approved revising debt-to-equity cap to 4x (from 3x) to support growth.
*   **Borrowing:** Sourced from 19 banks and NBFCs; cost of borrowing is ~8%.
*   **Liquidity:** Asset-liability mismatch is positive.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Regarding RBI draft guidelines on revolving credit, management stated they are compliant as they perform term-based, not OD-based, lending.
*   **Project Finance:** 12 accounts currently have no commercial operations; projects expected to start in 6-9 months.
*   **Asset Quality:** Maintained nil NPA.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Leverage:** Management clarified they are revising the debt-to-equity cap to 4x because growth has been "front-loaded" and they have confidence in performance.
*   **Digital/AI:** Management explained the 15% stake in a tech entity is for data security and to avoid dependency on external vendors.
*   **RBI Drafts:** Management emphasized they do not do revolving/OD products, only term-based financing, so they do not anticipate disruption.
*   **Factoring:** Clarified that factoring is just a product within the supply chain ecosystem, not a separate business line.
*   **AIF:** Clarified that the AIF is a fee-generating avenue and they are not deploying their own equity into it.

### One-line takeaway
SG Finserve is aggressively scaling its supply chain-focused model by increasing leverage and diversifying into digital lending and factoring, while maintaining strict asset quality and cost discipline.
