# Muthoot Microfin Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance driven by record disbursements, margin expansion, and improved asset quality despite macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursement:** Rs 2,900 crore (highest ever, +28% YoY).
*   **AUM:** Rs 15,323 crore (+22% YoY).
*   **Total Income:** Rs 733 crore (+27% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 110.8 crore.
*   **NIM:** 12.5% (up from 12%).
*   **GNPA:** 3.38% (improved 32 bps QoQ).
*   **Credit Cost:** 2.6% (within guidance of 2.5%-3%).
*   **Cost of Borrowing:** 9.93% (first time in single digits).
*   **Cost-to-Income Ratio:** 48.1%.
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 25.5%.

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Diversification:** Non-JLG portfolio increased to 31% of AUM (from 17% in March). Individual loan portfolio reached Rs 4,164 crore.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Rationalized 80 underperforming branches; OPEX to GLP improved to 6.1% (from 7% YoY).
*   **Digital Adoption:** Digital collection reached 47%; management expects to hit 75% target ahead of 2030.
*   **Asset Quality:** Collection efficiency at 98.1% for the quarter, improving to 99.2% in October.
*   **Record Consistency:** The company continues to deliver strong profit growth, consistent with the 300% YoY growth noted in our records.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains FY27 guidance: 20% AUM growth, profit guidance, and ROA/ROE targets.
*   Expects to beat ROA guidance (upper bracket 3.3%).
*   No capital raise required in the near term.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management notes industry momentum is strong with a calibrated approach to lending; no over-leveraging observed.
*   Exposure to drought-affected districts (Maharashtra/Karnataka) is limited to 3.6% of the portfolio.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Cost of borrowing reduced to 9.93%; incremental cost of borrowing at 9.69%.
*   Management expects interest rates to remain stable or decline further via NCDs and ECB drawdowns.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Network: 1,675 branches.
*   Expanding in Assam, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana; entering Jammu & Kashmir.
*   Individual loans and gold loans are serviced through existing infrastructure, keeping incremental costs low.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Liquidity: Rs 1,835 crore on balance sheet; Rs 4,442 crore of unavailed sanctions.
*   Purchased Rs 200 crore of gold loan assets from Muthoot Fincorp to bring referral-originated loans onto the books.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macroeconomic environment:** Uneven monsoons and drought-like conditions in 34 districts. Management notes 81% of the affected portfolio has NATCAT insurance cover.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Varun Dubey (Share India Securities):** Asked about drought exposure and IRDAI draft. Management: Exposure is limited (3.6%), NATCAT covers 81% of it; IRDAI draft impact is minimal as insurance is a pass-through.
*   **Aslesh Sonje:** Asked about Maharashtra collection efficiency. Management: Stable at 98% in X bucket; no deterioration.
*   **Neeraj Khandelwal:** Asked about the Rs 200 crore loan asset purchase from Muthoot Fincorp. Management: This is a buyback of gold loans originated via referral to bring them onto the books.
*   **Ajay Dubey:** Asked about ECL release. Management: Driven by portfolio improvement; maintaining 70% provision cover and Rs 24 crore contingency buffer.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational performance with record disbursements and margin expansion, supported by a diversified product mix and improved cost of funds.
