# 5paisa Capital Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-23 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong operational performance, a strengthened balance sheet post-rights issue, and a focus on product revamps and AI integration to drive future growth.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Revenue:** Rs 88.4 crore (up 14% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 11.6 crore.
*   **Customer Base:** 52.6 lakh.
*   **Average Client Funding Book (MTF + T+5):** Rs 422 crore (up 3% QoQ).
*   **Mutual Fund AUM:** Rs 2,073 crore (up 18% QoQ).
*   **Capital Raised:** Rs 468 crore (via rights issue in April).
*   **Net Worth:** North of Rs 1,100 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Product Upgrades:** Launched "AlgoSpace" for algo trading, expanded T+5 offering to 2,500 scrips (interest rates from 0.045% per day), and extended MTF to 1,500 scrips with limits up to Rs 25 crore.
*   **AI Integration:** Launched AI insights on the app for portfolio and stock analysis; building on previous MCP (conversational interface) integration.
*   **Strategic Focus:** Shifting focus from pure customer acquisition to "quality" acquisition, prioritizing Revenue Per Customer (RPC) and long-term value (LTV).
*   **Capital Deployment:** Rs 227 crore for exchange margin requirements (due to RBI regulations on intraday facilities), Rs 150 crore for debt repayment, and Rs 88 crore for general corporate purposes (tech, product, marketing).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects growth to accelerate from Q2 onwards, driven by product revamps and growth marketing.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management aims to achieve operating leverage quickly by scaling the platform, where incremental revenue incurs minimal incremental costs.
*   **Product Pipeline:** Multiple platform upgrades are moving to production in the coming months; management expects these to make a "significant difference" to market standing.
*   **Third-Party Distribution:** No plans for third-party distribution in the near term; focus remains on core platform and adjacent products like MTF and potentially LAS (Loan Against Securities).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Environment:** Acknowledged volatility and "choppy" conditions over the past year, with a moderation in volumes across the industry following F&O changes in October 2024.
*   **Long-term View:** Management remains bullish on the Indian capital markets, citing early innings of financialization and a robust, growing retail segment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Unit Economics:** Management is focused on improving unit economics to boost margins and overall PAT.
*   **Marketing:** Intends to refine channel mix (organic vs. paid) based on quarterly CAC and RPC metrics.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Management continues to selectively evaluate inorganic opportunities to accelerate growth and acquire talent.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Repaid Rs 150 crore to banks and CPs using rights issue proceeds.
*   **Liquidity:** Strengthened balance sheet with Rs 468 crore capital raise; net worth now exceeds Rs 1,100 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Volume Moderation:** Acknowledged a slight dip in F&O volumes and overall moderation in Q1 due to market volatility.
*   **Regulatory Impact:** RBI regulations effective July 1st regarding intraday facilities necessitated a Rs 227 crore allocation for exchange margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why was Q1 growth slow compared to Q3/Q4?** Management attributed it to a "one-off" moderation due to market volatility but reiterated confidence in acceleration from Q2.
*   **Q: How will you improve ADTO growth?** Management stated ADTO is driven by the quality of the customer base and order value, not just quantity; they are focusing on retaining high-value customers.
*   **Q: Are large players taking market share?** Management noted that while there is competition, their strategy is to focus on quality acquisition and retention rather than competing on headline numbers.
*   **Q: Why no third-party distribution (TPD) like group company IIFL Securities?** Management stated they are currently focused on core platform improvements and will consider TPD in the mid-to-long term if synergies exist.

### One-line takeaway
5paisa is pivoting from aggressive customer acquisition to a quality-focused, tech-driven strategy, leveraging a strengthened balance sheet to fund product revamps and AI-led features to drive long-term operating leverage.
