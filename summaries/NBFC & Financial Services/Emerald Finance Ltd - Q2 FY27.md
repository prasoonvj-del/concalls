# Emerald Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady financial growth and platform expansion, offset by minor regulatory and market headwinds in the gold loan/distribution segment.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated total income: INR10.07 crores (up 45.81% YoY)
- Consolidated PAT: INR5.30 crores (up 47.14% YoY)
- H1 FY27 consolidated total income: INR19.51 crores (up 42.94% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR10.18 crores (up 49.80% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR8.39 crores in Q2 FY27 (up 54.75% YoY)
- MSME loan AUM: INR131 crores
- EWA active employees / base: ~5,500 transacting monthly out of 40,000–42,000 base (~12%-13% or ~14% activation rate)
- EWA monthly disbursement: ~INR14 crores (plus ~INR16 crores cross-selling monthly)
- Impairment loss: INR28.8 lakhs in Q2 FY27 (vs INR21.2 lakhs in Q1 FY27)
- Corporate onboarding: 24 new corporate partners in Q2 FY27 (totaling 270 corporates)

### Highlights
- Launched 'Emerald Ekam', an integrated financial wellness platform combining salary access, loans, investments, insurance, and employee benefits.
- Signed Unity Small Finance Bank as a lead facilitator for funding bank transfers (second pan-India partnership), with another private sector bank partnership to be announced shortly.
- Tied up with SafeGold for digital gold availability on the Emerald Ekam platform.
- On track for NSE listing eligibility after auditing the March '27 balance sheet, meeting the 3-year net worth requirement of >INR75 crores.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1, supported by festive EWA demand and activity towards the March quarter.
- MSME loan book target: Conservative target of crossing INR150 crores by the end of the year.
- EWA client target: Sourcing 120 corporate clients by the year-end (54 sourced so far).
- Gold loan syndication annual target of INR1,300 crores is expected to be achieved.
- QoQ growth target for EWA disbursement: 15% to 20%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Gold loan DSA business is experiencing stress due to regulatory requirements influencing bank appraisals, tighter credit norms by lenders, and falling gold prices.
- Partner banks have tightened credit norms, reducing funding for some NBFCs by 40% to 50%.
- EWA demand sees seasonal boosts during festivals; baseline usage depends on employee need.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage driven by technology and AI adoption has kept employee and overhead expenses low relative to top-line growth.
- Borrowing costs have not increased as of Q2; banks (State Bank and ICICI) have not hiked rates, though a potential 0.25% RBI hike might be passed on.
- Average MSME lending yields: ~18% (average ticket size ~INR40 lakhs).
- EWA IRR: 30% to 36% (ticket size up to INR1 lakh).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus on onboarding and migrating existing EWA corporate partners (270 corporates) onto the new Emerald Ekam platform.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth remains above INR75 crores for NSE eligibility criteria.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory tightening by RBI on gold loan distribution policies.
- Decreasing gold prices impacting balance transfer demand.
- Tighter credit norms and reduced funding availability from partner banks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **NSE Listing Status (Jashwanth Reddy):** Management confirmed they will be eligible after auditing the March '27 balance sheet, maintaining a net worth >INR75 crores for 3 consecutive years.
2. **Distribution Income Decline (Ketan Chheda):** Management explained that partner NBFCs faced 40%-50% cuts in funding due to tighter credit norms, and gold loans faced regulatory headwinds and falling prices.
3. **Impairment Loss & Borrowing Costs (Sejal Deshmukh):** Management clarified that impairment (INR28.8 lakhs in Q2) includes standard RBI provisioning, default provisioning, and recoveries. Existing lenders haven't hiked rates yet, but may pass on future RBI rate hikes.
4. **EBTIDA Margin Sustainability (Pratik Shah):** Management attributed margin expansion to operating leverage and AI/technology integration without heavy manpower scaling, confirming margins are sustainable.
5. **Gold Loan Syndication Volume Drop (Abhishek Malhotra):** Management noted unstable falling gold prices and difficult balance transfer cases caused a 30%-35% drop, but new bank partnerships (Unity Bank) are expected to revive volumes.

### One-line takeaway
Emerald Finance reported strong Q2 FY27 YoY PAT growth of 47.14% to INR5.30 crores, driven by technology-led operating leverage, while launching the 'Emerald Ekam' platform to expand cross-selling despite headwinds in gold loan distribution.
