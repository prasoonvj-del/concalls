# REC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Remarkable improvement in H1 performance, record disbursements, and significant reduction in NPAs.

### Key numbers
- **Loan book (AUM):** Rs 6.17 lakh crores as at September 30, 2026 (6% growth YoY)
- **Net worth:** Rs 94,325 crores (14% growth YoY)
- **Total income (H1):** Rs 29,068 crores
- **Net interest income (H1):** Rs 10,116 crores
- **Net profit (PAT) (H1):** Rs 7,541 crores
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR):** 24.04%
- **Disbursements (H1):** Rs 56,457 crores (67% growth over Q1 FY27); total disbursements for H1 mentioned as close to Rs 90,000 crores by CMD.
- **Gross credit-impaired assets (Stage 3):** 0.22%
- **Net credit-impaired assets (Stage 3):** 0.11% (down from 0.24% YoY)
- **Provision Coverage Ratio:** ~51%
- **Stage 2 assets:** Rs 3,235 crores (down from Rs 12,350 crores as at June 30, 2026)
- **Yield on loan assets:** 9.57% (vs 9.55% in Q1)
- **Cost of funds:** 7.19% (H1)
- **Spread:** 2.38%
- **NIM:** 3.22%
- **ROE:** 16.89%
- **Prepayments (H1):** Rs 19,000 crores

### Highlights
- Launched India's first pilot issuance of tokenized corporate bonds under SEBI regulatory sandbox framework in 2026.
- International ratings upgraded by Japan Credit Rating Agency to A- with a stable outlook; rated Baa3 by Moody's and BBB- by Fitch.
- Awarded 'NBFC of the Year' at the 3rd Annual Bharat NBFC & FinTech Summit & Awards 2026.
- Ranked first among 505 Indian financial sector companies in NSE ESG ratings; aiming to become the first carbon-neutral financial organization in FY27.
- Declared total dividend of Rs 7.75 per share, including Rs 3.50 for Q2. FII shareholding increased to 16.78% as of September 30, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of achieving double-digit loan book growth of around 10% to 12% for FY27.
- Target of over Rs 2 lakh crores for disbursements for the full year.
- Management expects NIMs to remain sustainable at around 3% to 3.2% and does not expect them to drop significantly further.
- Draft merger scheme with PFC is currently pending Cabinet approval; precondition is that the merged entity will remain a government company.

### Demand & industry commentary
- NITI Aayog projects $5 trillion (approx Rs 500 lakh crores) investment in India over the next 15-20 years; REC anticipates funding opportunities over Rs 100 lakh crores.
- Government announced a Rs 1.85 lakh crores project for strengthening the transmission sector and a Rs 23,000 crores biogas plan.
- Rooftop Solar Scheme (nodal agency REC) reached 55 lakh installations against a 1-crore target.
- Power sector demand remains strong, driven by renewable energy, infrastructure, logistics, and traditional power.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Borrowing cost impacted by floating term loans (~24% of borrowings); offset by floating-rate loan book (~25%).
- Management noted pressure on NIMs due to high competition and sector derisking, but expects NIMs to stabilize around 3.22%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Renewable energy portfolio reached Rs 86,660 crores (27% increase YoY), constituting solar (35%), pumped storage (14%), hybrid (18%), and hydro (13%).
- Infrastructure and logistics portfolio reached Rs 62,000 crores (10% of total loan book).
- Actively funding emerging areas including battery storage, pumped hydro, green hydrogen, nuclear, and data centers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings reached Rs 5.27 lakh crores (4% growth from March 31, 2026).
- Debt-equity ratio at 5.6x.
- Prepayments dropped significantly to Rs 19,000 crores in H1 compared to ~Rs 1 lakh crores last year.
- No new NPAs from the last 19 quarters; existing NPAs are entirely in the private sector.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Intense competition from banks flushed with liquidity and other financial institutions.
- Pressure on Net Interest Margins (NIMs) due to competitive pricing and rising borrowing costs.
- Currency volatility and forex exposure, though management noted hedging enhancements (hedged above Rs 96 per dollar).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Avinash Singh (Emkay Global):** Asked about NIM compression and conventional thermal AUM growth. Management explained that floating-rate assets cushion floating liabilities, and National Electricity Plan targets ~80 GW additional thermal capacity by 2031-32.
- **Sanket Chheda (DAM Capital):** Inquired about the confidence behind 10%-12% growth guidance. Management attributed it to peaked-out prepayments (only Rs 19,000 crores in H1) and a strong committed order book of Rs 2.5 lakh crores.
- **Abhijit Tibrewal (Motilal Oswal):** Asked about sustainability of disbursements and merger updates. Management stated that Rs 70,000 crores of documented projects are ready for disbursement, and the merger scheme is awaiting Cabinet approval while maintaining government company status.
- **Shreya Shivani (Nomura):** Inquired about the transmission sector project and smart meter rollout. Management replied that detailed guidelines for the Rs 1.85 lakh crores transmission project are awaited from the Ministry, and smart meter data analytics are successfully reducing DISCOM AT&C losses.
- **Parag Trivedi (India Capital):** Asked if thermal disbursements will drive yields and if prepayments will be lower. Management confirmed thermal yields are higher and gestation periods (5-7 years) reduce early prepayment risks.

### One-line takeaway
REC reported a strong H1 FY27 with a 6% YoY loan book growth to Rs 6.17 lakh crores, record disbursements, halved net NPAs at 0.11%, and guided for 10%-12% loan growth for the full year.
