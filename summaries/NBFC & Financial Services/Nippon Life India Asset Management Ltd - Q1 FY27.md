# Nippon Life India Asset Management Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-23 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company reported its highest-ever quarterly Profit After Tax and Operating Profit, driven by strong AUM growth and market share gains.

### Key numbers
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 5.04 bn (up 27% YoY).
*   **Operating Profit:** INR 4.94 bn (up 31% YoY).
*   **Revenue:** INR 7.67 bn (up 26% YoY, 4% QoQ).
*   **Operating Expenses:** INR 2.73 bn (up 19% YoY, 11% QoQ).
*   **Total AUM:** INR 8.62 trillion.
*   **Mutual Fund QAAUM:** INR 7.52 trillion (up 22.7% YoY, 3.7% QoQ).
*   **Market Share:** 9.04% (highest since June 2019).
*   **Equity Market Share:** 7.38% (up 34 bps YoY, 22 bps QoQ).
*   **Yields:** Equity (ex-arbitrage) at 54 bps; Debt at 25 bps; Liquid at 12 bps; ETF at 25 bps. Overall yield remained constant at 38 bps QoQ.
*   **Monthly Systematic Book:** INR 37.2 bn (as of June 2026).

### Highlights
*   NAM India was the fastest-growing AMC among the Top-10 in Q1 FY27 on both overall and equity AUM (YoY & QoQ).
*   Achieved the highest absolute AUM growth and the highest increase in AUM market share in the industry for the quarter.
*   Maintained the largest investor base in the industry with 24.1 mn unique investors.
*   Digital business contributed 78% of total new purchase transactions; digital purchase transactions and new SIP registrations rose 26% YoY.
*   AIF business raised INR 2.5 bn in commitments during the quarter; cumulative commitments reached INR 95.8 bn (up 18% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Expenses:** Management expects operating expenses (ex-ESOP and one-offs) to grow in the range of 18% to 20% for the next 6-8 quarters due to continued investments in technology, brand, and digital platforms.
*   **Yields:** Management expects a potential 1-2 bps YoY decline in blended yields on equity as AUM size increases.
*   **ESOPs:** ESOP expenses for FY27 are estimated at approximately INR 60 crores.
*   **Strategy:** Management remains optimistic about the SIF (Special Investment Fund) category and is in a "state of readiness" to launch differentiated products.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Industry QAAUM grew 15% YoY to INR 83.1 trillion.
*   Industry SIP flows were INR 939 bn (up 16% YoY).
*   Management noted that while the industry saw volatility in fixed income, they are focusing on asset allocation to de-risk portfolios.
*   The company has a well-diversified distribution mix with no single distributor accounting for more than 5% of the business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Operating expenses increased due to investments in digital infrastructure, brand activities, and technology.
*   Employee expenses saw an increase in Q1 due to annual increments and ESOPs; management expects these to remain in a similar range going forward.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   The company maintains a "de-risked" business model with no single distributor concentration exceeding 5%.
*   The investment book includes a large portion of small and midcap seed capital, which contributed to "Other Income" growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management noted that the NFO market has shrunk due to reduced euphoria, which they view as positive for the industry.
*   Gold and Silver ETF volumes decreased; combined closing AUM for these ETFs was INR 827 bn (down 2.5% QoQ).
*   The company voluntarily restricted inflows into bullion ETFs (above INR 25 crores) and gold funds (above INR 10 lakh) due to national gold import considerations; management is evaluating when to lift these restrictions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On SIF strategy:** Management clarified they are not launching "me-too" products and are waiting for the right time to launch highly differentiated strategies.
*   **On DWS JV:** Management highlighted the JV with DWS (Europe's largest asset manager) as a key strategic move to attract foreign capital into India, though it is too early to comment on specific unit economics.
*   **On Digital Channel:** Management noted that digital investors (Gen Z skew) are being educated via nudges and campaigns to focus on long-term investing rather than chasing short-term performance.
*   **On Bullion ETF Supply:** Management confirmed there have been no supply chain disruptions for physical gold backing the ETFs.

### One-line takeaway
NAM India delivered record-breaking profitability and market share gains in Q1 FY27, supported by robust systematic flows and a digital-first strategy, while signaling continued investment in technology to sustain growth.
