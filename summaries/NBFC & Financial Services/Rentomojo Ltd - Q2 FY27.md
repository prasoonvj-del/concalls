# Rentomojo Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (52.8% YoY), healthy cash flow generation (110% OCF/EBITDA), and aggressive expansion plans while maintaining profitability.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations growth:** 52.8% YoY; 12.5% QoQ.
*   **Normalized EBITDA:** Rs 58.8 crore (vs Rs 38.5 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Normalized PAT:** Rs 24 crore (vs Rs 15.8 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Purchase displacement (Q2):** Rs 447 crore.
*   **Contractual revenue:** Rs 690 crore.
*   **Net debt:** Rs 131 crore.
*   **Net debt to equity:** 0.28x.
*   **Net debt to EBITDA:** 0.65x.
*   **H1 Capex (procured):** Rs 192.3 crore.

### Highlights
*   Revenue from operations grew 52.8% YoY.
*   Normalized EBITDA grew 52.7% YoY.
*   Cash flow from operations (OCF) to EBITDA ratio is ~110%, indicating strong cash conversion.
*   Subscriber base and gross items ordered grew 42.5% and 45% YoY, respectively.
*   *Record Note:* The company continues to deliver strong growth, consistent with the positive trend noted in the 2026-11-11 investor deck.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets a PBT of Rs 90-95 crore for the year.
*   Management aims to maintain Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) above 20-25%.
*   Management intends to remain aggressive on growth, leveraging a strong balance sheet and internal accruals.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Total addressable market (TAM) estimated at ~3.8 crore apartments.
*   Industry rental penetration remains low (~1% for furniture, 1.4% for appliances).
*   Rentomojo keyword search volume grew 66% YoY.
*   Core cities (top 5) remain the primary growth drivers, contributing 90-91% of revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Marketing and advertising spend as a fraction of revenue has increased but remains within internal limits.
*   Cost of debt has decreased to ~8.65%.
*   Normalized EBITDA margins remain healthy at ~40-41%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   H1 capex procurement: Rs 192.3 crore.
*   Warehouse capacity grew 51% YoY; store count grew 43% YoY.
*   Capex is currently front-loaded in H1 due to AC seasonality.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Business model is negative working capital; internal accruals fund most capex.
*   Debt to EBITDA: 1.16x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **NCLT matter:** Hearing scheduled for December; management stated it is sub-judice.
*   **Accounting volatility:** Reported PAT is volatile due to deferred tax asset creation and one-time fire loss adjustments.
*   **Operational sensitivity:** Business is sensitive to occupancy rates and the useful life of assets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Jatin Jadhav (Sahasrar Capital):** Asked about funding capex. Management explained that internal accruals fund most capex, with debt being secondary in the waterfall.
*   **Kushagra Bhattar (Analyst):** Asked about items per user and geographic mix. Management noted 2.8 items per user is stable and core cities remain the primary focus.
*   **Kunal Sharma (Analyst):** Asked about NCLT status. Management reiterated the matter is sub-judice with a hearing in December.
*   **Amar Nath Bhakat (Analyst):** Asked about cost of debt. Management confirmed it is ~8.65% and highlighted the negative working capital nature of the business.
*   **Della Desai (Analyst):** Asked about aggressive market share vs. margins. Management stated they intend to be aggressive and have sufficient margin buffers to sustain growth.

### One-line takeaway
Rentomojo is delivering strong, profitable growth (52.8% YoY revenue) driven by high operational cash flow and a scalable subscription model, with management signaling continued aggressive expansion.
