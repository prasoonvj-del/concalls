**Quarter:** Q1 FY27 (Call date: 2026-07-22)
**Tone:** Positive – Management highlighted strong growth across all business verticals, successful operationalization of new business lines, and a "virtuous flywheel" strategy driving customer engagement.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Income (excl. dividends):** Rs 1,496 crore (+141% YoY).
*   **PPOP:** +38% YoY.
*   **PBT (excl. exceptional items):** +18% YoY.
*   **NBFC AUM:** Rs 30,667 crore (2.6x YoY).
*   **NBFC Quarterly Disbursements:** >Rs 11,000 crore (+173% YoY).
*   **Jio Payments Bank Deposits:** 1.7x YoY; 51% CASA ratio; 3.9 million customers.
*   **JioBlackRock AMC AUM:** Rs 18,412 crore.
*   **JPSL (Payments) TPV:** Rs 19,208 crore (2.5x YoY).
*   **Consolidated Shareholders' Equity:** Rs 1.37 lakh crore.
*   **Cumulative Fund Infusion (via preferential warrants):** Rs 9,890 crore (including Rs 5,934 crore in the current tranche).
*   **NBFC Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 3.9x.
*   **Average Borrowing Cost:** 7.07%.

### Highlights
*   **NBFC Portfolio Mix:** Mortgages (Home Loans/LAP) 45.4%, Corporate/SME 44.2%, Loan Against Shares 10.4%.
*   **Restructuring:** RSHL (Reliance Strategic Investments) became a 100% step-down subsidiary.
*   **Payments:** Successfully launched FASTag ANPR-based Multi-Lane Free-Flow toll operations and cross-border settlement capabilities.
*   **Technology:** Deployed AI-driven Video Personal Discussions, resulting in a 76% reduction in credit assessment time.
*   **Distribution:** Business Correspondent touchpoints scaled to 527,000 (vs 50,000 in FY26).
*   **Reinsurance:** Business operationalized and underwrote initial premiums.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Securities Broking:** Management expects to commence operations in Q2 FY27.
*   **Insurance:** General Insurance JV approval process is progressing; active non-binding agreements are in place to establish a Life Insurance JV.
*   **Strategy:** Management aims to continue leveraging technology to decouple overheads from volume growth, maintaining a "lean and agile" structure.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Strategy Shift:** Moving from a product-push model to a three-layer unified marketplace (Proprietary products, Third-party aggregation, and N=1 hyper-personalized interface).
*   **Customer Engagement:** The "flywheel" strategy focuses on four pillars: Borrow, Invest, Transact, and Protect.
*   **Market Approach:** Leveraging "late entrant advantage" to build on a cloud-native, legacy-free architecture.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **JPSL Margins:** Gross fee margins expanded by 60 bps YoY.
*   **Cost Structure:** JPSL operates on a variable cost-led, volume-linked model with low overheads.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Staff costs increased due to annual increments and bonus payouts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Physical Footprint:** Expanded physical offices in major cities to support last-mile fulfillment and localized assessment.
*   **Digital Reach:** Transactions captured across more than 19,000 PIN codes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Borrowings:** Total borrowings reached Rs 28,000 crore.
*   **Liquidity:** Raised Rs 1,500 crore via Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) against a Board-approved limit of Rs 15,000 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macroeconomic Volatility:** Management noted the need to balance the portfolio against macro volatility, maintaining stringent underwriting rules.
*   **Regulatory Adherence:** Management emphasized "Regulatory adherence in letter and spirit" as a core guiding principle.
