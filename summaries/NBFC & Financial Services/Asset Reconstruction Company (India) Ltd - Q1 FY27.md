# Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-13 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong recovery-led performance with profit more than doubling YoY, despite seasonally light acquisition activity.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 274 crore (up 128% YoY).
*   **Total Income:** Rs 276 crore (up 115% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 143 crore (up 107% YoY).
*   **AUM:** Rs 19,574 crore (up 16% YoY).
*   **Total Recoveries:** Rs 1,066 crore (up 151% YoY).
*   **Cumulative SR Redemption Ratio:** 52.5% (vs 50.8% in March 2026).
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR):** 72.5%.
*   **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.34x.

### Highlights
*   **Performance Drivers:** Growth driven by strong corporate resolutions and recovery of previously written-off assets (Rs 120 crore in Q1).
*   **Acquisition Seasonality:** Q1 is seasonally light; the company acquired Rs 353 crore of stressed assets (vs Rs 502 crore in Q1 FY26). Management expects stronger acquisitions through the year.
*   **Portfolio Mix:** AUM composition is 68% Corporate, 24% Retail, and 8% SME.
*   **Standalone vs. Consolidated:** Management emphasizes standalone financials as the "true lens" for shareholders, as consolidated results include ~220 trusts where Arcil acts as trustee, distorting fee income and expenses.
*   **Collection-as-a-Service:** Currently in the POC (Proof of Concept) stage with select NBFCs; management views this as a significant future revenue stream.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Acquisitions:** Management expects an increasing trend in acquisitions throughout the year, supported by a strong pipeline.
*   **Long-term View:** Management advises assessing performance over longer periods rather than quarter-to-quarter due to the seasonality of acquisitions and the "chunky" nature of corporate recoveries.
*   **Write-offs:** Management expects future write-offs to be "miniscule" (cumulatively less than Rs 100 crore over the next three years).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Size:** Total stressed assets across banks/NBFCs are ~Rs 24 lakh crore (including written-off loans).
*   **ARC Participation:** ARCs currently participate in <10% of the total stressed asset market.
*   **Retail vs. Corporate:** Retail stress has doubled since FY20 to ~Rs 7 lakh crore; MSME stress is ~Rs 9.2 lakh crore; Corporate pool is steady at ~Rs 7.6 lakh crore.
*   **Industry Trend:** Increased ARC participation is driven by lenders seeking specialized resolution expertise to free up management bandwidth and capital.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Finance Costs:** Rose to Rs 25 crore (from Rs 3.2 crore) due to increased borrowings in H2 FY26 to fund acquisitions.
*   **PAT Margin:** ~52% of total income.
*   **Acquisition Pricing:** Assets are acquired at ~26% of outstanding dues, providing a "margin of safety."

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Worth:** Rs 3,222 crore.
*   **Borrowings:** Reduced to Rs 1,100 crore (from Rs 1,200 crore in March 2026).
*   **Cash & Bank Balances:** Rs 252 crore.
*   **Investment in SRs:** Arcil's own investment in AUM is ~Rs 4,200 crore (roughly 22% of AUM).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Seasonality:** Q1 is historically a light quarter for acquisitions; corporate recoveries are "chunky" and event-based, making quarterly performance volatile.
*   **Write-backs:** While recurring, the recovery of previously written-off assets is event-based and lumpy; management warns against using Q1's Rs 120 crore figure as a full-year trend.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On IRR:** Management stated historical IRR across businesses is in the "higher 20s" (20-22%).
*   **On Cash vs. Structured Deals:** Management noted that while cash acquisitions reduce the acquisition price (due to time value of money), the risk assessment process remains identical to structured deals.
*   **On Retail vs. Corporate Nature:** Retail provides steady, granular cash flows (hundreds of daily repayments), whereas corporate recoveries are "chunky" and event-based.
*   **On Borrowing Decline:** Management clarified that the sequential decline in debt was due to limited Q1 acquisition activity; they expect to draw down more lines as acquisition volume picks up in H2.

### One-line takeaway
Arcil delivered a strong, recovery-led Q1 with a 107% jump in PAT, emphasizing that its standalone financials and long-term resolution track record are the best indicators of value as it scales in a growing stressed asset market.
