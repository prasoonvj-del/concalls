# IIFL Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted resilient credit demand, stable asset quality, and a return to natural operational levels, despite acknowledging capital adequacy pressures and a cautious stance on unsecured segments.

### Key numbers
*   **AUM:** INR 1,15,523 crore (up 38% YoY, 7% QoQ).
*   **Core Product AUM (Home, Gold, MSME, MFI):** INR 1,11,717 crore (up 43% YoY, 8% QoQ; 96.7% of total mix).
*   **Gold Loan AUM:** INR 58,406 crore.
*   **PAT (before non-controlling interest):** INR 713 crore (up 14% QoQ).
*   **Pre-provision Operating Profit:** INR 1,252 crore (up 50% YoY, 7% QoQ).
*   **Asset Quality:** Gross NPA 1.6%, Net NPA 0.8% (both up 9 bps QoQ).
*   **Provision Coverage Ratio:** 94%.
*   **Cost of Borrowing:** 9.13% (down 3 bps QoQ, 33 bps YoY).
*   **ROE:** 19.5% (annualized); **ROA:** 3.1%.
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** NBFC 17.1%, HFC 41.7%, Samasta 24.9%.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Shift:** The company has discontinued unsecured business loans and personal loans.
*   **Capital Management:** Board approved an enabling resolution for fresh equity (subject to AGM approval). Management is exploring multiple options, including QIP, secondary sale of subsidiaries, or listing the microfinance arm to address capital adequacy.
*   **Technology:** "Project PACE" (AI-led operating model) is moving from pilot to measurable impact in collections, fraud detection, and productivity.
*   **Funding:** Raised INR 17,183 crore via term loans, bonds, and commercial paper; USD 500 million raised through social bonds.
*   **Ratings:** Moody’s assigned Ba3 Issuer rating and (P)Ba3 GMTN program rating (stable outlook).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Expects full-year FY27 book/AUM growth of 17-18% for Home Finance, with disbursement growth over 30%.
*   **Credit Cost:** Guidance for FY27 is 1.5% to 1.7%.
*   **Margins:** Management expects margins to remain stable; benefits are anticipated from operating leverage and lower credit costs.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Opex-to-AUM is expected to remain in the 3.3%-3.4% range, with potential for downward trajectory over 2-3 years due to AI and scale.
*   **Microfinance:** Expects steady 3-4% QoQ growth and ROA of 2.5%-3% for Samasta.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro:** RBI held repo rate at 5.25%; trimmed FY27 growth outlook to 6.6% and nudged inflation to 5.1%.
*   **Gold Loans:** Industry growth slowed in Q1; company continues to grow faster than the industry. Management noted that while competition is aggressive, the market is large and unorganized, supporting their franchise.
*   **Housing:** Q1 is typically soft; management expects momentum to strengthen through the rest of the year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Yields:** Home Finance onboarding yields increased by 68-70 bps on incremental books in the last two quarters.
*   **Funding:** Cost of borrowing saw a slight uptick due to international dollar bond issuances, but management expects domestic liquidity to ease and costs to decline.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Branch Network:** Plans to set up 500 new branches in FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Cash and committed credit lines stand at INR 7,148 crore.
*   **ALM:** Positive ALM; inflows cover expected outflows across all buckets.
*   **Net Gearing:** 4.0x.
*   **SRs:** Management expects full redemption of Security Receipts (SRs) within 12-18 months, likely realizing more than book value.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Capital Adequacy:** Management acknowledged the parent company's capital adequacy is "at the edge" and requires fixing.
*   **Gold Prices:** Management identified a sharp correction in gold prices as a "real tail risk" and is maintaining LTV discipline.
*   **Asset Quality:** Acknowledged higher GNPA in housing finance due to a residual "problem book" (Micro LAP: INR 440 crore; BLC: INR 260 crore), which is expected to be cleaned up this year.
*   **Unsecured Exposure:** Maintaining a cautious stance on unsecured MSME and MFI segments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Equity Raise:** When asked about the timing of equity raises given capital adequacy, management stated they are "very conscious" of the issue and are keeping all options open (QIP, subsidiary stake sales, debt) to avoid "desperate" situations.
*   **On Assignment Income:** An analyst questioned the decline in assignment income (INR 173 crore to INR 43 crore). Management attributed this to fewer transactions during the quarter as co-lending picks up, noting that upfront income will likely become negligible in future quarters.
*   **On Gold Loan DPD:** An analyst noted an increase in 1-30/30-60 DPD buckets. Management clarified this is not concerning, as it is seasonal/marginal and historically, losses in gold loans are near zero.
*   **On AI Benefits:** When asked about the wide range of AI impact (10-40% loss prevention), management admitted AI is a "new animal" and "black box," making precise estimation difficult, but expects long-term operating leverage.
*   **On Income-Generating Gold Loans:** Management confirmed they are fully aligned with new RBI guidelines, using Udyam certificates and business proof for income-generating gold loans.

### One-line takeaway
IIFL Finance is prioritizing capital adequacy and operational efficiency via AI while pivoting toward a secured, lower-risk portfolio and scaling co-lending partnerships.
