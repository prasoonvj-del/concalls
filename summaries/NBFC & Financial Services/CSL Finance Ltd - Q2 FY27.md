# CSL Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance driven by the wholesale segment, record disbursements, and improving asset quality metrics.

### Key numbers
- AUM: Rs 1,627 crore (up 16% YoY, up 8% QoQ)
- On-book AUM: Rs 1,578 crore (up 21% YoY, 7% QoQ)
- Off-book AUM (joint lending): Rs 50 crore (up from Rs 33 crore in Q1 FY27)
- Total disbursements: Rs 385 crore in Q2 (up 31% YoY, 20% QoQ); H1 FY27 at Rs 706 crore (up 18% YoY)
- Collections: Rs 298 crore in Q2 (up 16% YoY, 5% QoQ); H1 FY27 at Rs 580 crore
- Collection efficiency: 99% in Q2 (vs 98% in preceding four quarters)
- Gross NPA: 1.13% as of Sep 30, 2026 (vs 0.95% in Q1 FY27, 0.51% in Q2 FY26)
- Net NPA: 0.71% (vs 0.60% in Q1 FY27, 0.39% in Q2 FY26)
- Gross Stage 3 Assets: Rs 17.84 crore (vs Rs 13.92 crore in Q1 FY27)
- Total ECL provision: Rs 19.67 crore (1.25% of loan book); Provision Coverage Ratio: 110.29%
- Write-offs: Rs 2.05 crore in Q2 (vs Rs 2.87 crore in Q1 FY27); Recoveries: Rs 1.44 crore
- Net Interest Income (NII): Rs 49.6 crore (up 21% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- Total Income: Rs 77.4 crore (up 21% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): Rs 35 crore (up 21% YoY, 18% QoQ)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 25 crore (up 2% YoY, 13% QoQ)
- Annualized ROE: 14.86% (vs 13.82% in Q1 FY27); Annualized ROA: 6.30% (vs 5.86% in Q1 FY27)
- Branch network: 35 branches across 7 states
- Total borrowings: Rs 962.90 crore across 35 lenders
- Undrawn sanction limits (including NCD): Rs 210 crore; Balance sheet liquidity: Rs 76.8 crore
- Leverage ratio: 1.5x; Capital adequacy ratio: 40.87%
- Credit rating: A-stable from Acuité Ratings and Research

### Highlights
- Wholesale segment remains the primary driver of AUM growth, supported by healthy disbursements, robust asset quality, and funding availability.
- SME Retail segment remains in a consolidation phase due to external factors (over-leveraged borrower profiles, stagnant MSME income growth, high competitive intensity) and internal tightening (credit policy tweaks, rationalizing non-performing branches).
- Joint lending structures expanded off-book AUM to Rs 50 crore, helping participate in larger wholesale transactions and building fee-based income.
- A new full-time CEO has been hired to oversee the SME retail vertical.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the wholesale segment to continue driving AUM growth in H2 FY27.
- Management expects better performance in SME retail disbursements in H2 FY27 as consolidation measures yield results and operating environment improves.
- Joint lending activity is expected to build up further over coming quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Wholesale pipeline stands robust at Rs 300 to Rs 400 crore of due diligence and sanctioned deals.
- Affordable housing demand remains strong, with projects oversold 5 to 10 times via lottery systems with government-fixed pricing.
- Small plotted housing projects show no slowdown, with marquee locations seeing steady sales.
- MSME ecosystem faces stagnant income growth, over-leveraged profiles, and high competitive intensity in smaller tickets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- NII and Total Income grew by 21% YoY each.
- Lower effective tax rate in the base quarter (Q2 FY26) caused a divergence between PBT growth (21% YoY) and PAT growth (2% YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus is on improving branch-level productivity and operational efficiency in SME retail rather than network expansion; rationalized non-performing branches and relocated others.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ALM remains positive across all buckets.
- Lender base expanded to 35 institutions (public/private banks, SFCs, NBFCs).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- SME retail loans below Rs 10 lakh show higher delinquency risks due to marginal borrower profiles and single rural collateral.
- Borrowers taking loans above Rs 50 lakh in the past faced higher stress and slower foreclosures; management stopped loans above Rs 50 lakh 18 months ago.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Himanshu Patel (Analyst) asked about the wholesale pipeline, sales slowdown in residential real estate, and project counts.**
   - *Management answered* that the pipeline is Rs 300-400 crore. Affordable housing and small plotted projects (~35-40% and 35% of portfolio respectively) face no sales slowdown due to escrow controls, lottery systems, and marquee locations, while conservative underwriting applies to the remaining 30%. There are 128 active projects across 80-90 builders.
2. **Himanshu Patel asked about the SME portfolio GNPA trends, branch strategy, and the new SME CEO's role.**
   - *Management answered* that legacy accounts above Rs 50 lakh originated between FY22-FY24 drove NPAs, but no NPAs have originated in the last 18 months post stopping tickets >50 lakh and reducing <10 lakh tickets. The new CEO will bring cohesiveness and daily oversight to revive SME growth post-Diwali.
3. **Himanshu Patel asked if CSL plans to expand into new product lines like gold loans or two-wheeler loans over the next 2-3 years.**
   - *Management answered* that they have no plans to launch new product lines for the next 15-18 months, as there is substantial scope to grow the existing SME LAF portfolio, and new verticals require heavy operational bandwidth and upfront investments.

### One-line takeaway
CSL Finance delivered record quarterly disbursements and 16% YoY AUM growth in Q2 FY27, anchored by a robust wholesale segment while consolidating its SME retail business.
