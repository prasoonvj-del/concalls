# Linc Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong double-digit revenue growth was offset by significant margin contraction driven by raw material volatility and aggressive sales promotion.

### Key numbers
*   **Operating Income:** ₹15,412 lakhs (up 10.8% YoY, 10.9% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** ₹1,131 lakhs.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 7.3% (vs 11.3% in Q2 FY26; 8.7% in Q1 FY27).
*   **Margin Contraction:** 393 bps YoY (190 bps due to raw material costs; 200 bps due to increased sales expenditure).
*   **PAT:** ₹419 lakhs.
*   **PAT Margin:** 2.7%.
*   **Asset Turnover:** 3.74x.
*   **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 70 days.

### Highlights
*   Achieved double-digit sales growth through focused promotional initiatives and expansion in non-writing instrument categories.
*   Expanding into lifestyle drinkware via "Tri-Tonic" (Tritan-based bottles).
*   Stackoo experience centers in Kolkata and Mumbai are supporting the broader portfolio strategy.
*   **Record Note:** While the company previously indicated the new West Bengal facility would be operational in Q3, management clarified that while infrastructure will be ready by end-December (Q3), commercial production is now scheduled for January (Q4).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects margins to improve in Q4, provided raw material prices remain stable.
*   Management declined to provide specific percentage guidance for the next 4-6 quarters due to current market volatility, noting they will provide better clarity by the end of the financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Sales promotion activities were increased to stimulate demand and gain market share while maintaining pricing structures.
*   Categories beyond writing instruments are showing healthy growth.
*   Kenya subsidiary sales momentum continues to improve.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Geopolitical uncertainties caused price increases across certain raw material inputs.
*   Management stated that their recently developed raw material mix (used over the last 1-1.5 years) has helped cushion the impact compared to standard grades like SAN, ABS, PP, and PC.
*   Internal levers for margin improvement include optimizing the raw material mix and focusing on premium-priced products.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   West Bengal manufacturing facility: Infrastructure is nearing completion; commercial production is expected to commence by January (Q4).
*   The new facility is designed for operational efficiency and future capacity expansion, rather than immediate, massive capacity increases.

### International business
*   **Uni-Linc (JV with Mitsubishi):** Operationally stable; exports account for >50% of revenue. One product launched; two more in the pipeline for the next 3-4 quarters.
*   **Turkey JV:** Operations continue to progress steadily.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Where do you see margins settling over the next 4-6 quarters?**
    *   A: Management expects margins to improve in Q4 as raw material situations appear temporary. They declined to give exact percentages due to volatility.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Uni-Linc JV products?**
    *   A: One ball pen (₹20 MRP) has been launched and is gaining volume. Two more products are in the pipeline for the next 3-4 quarters.
*   **Q: Is the West Bengal plant on schedule?**
    *   A: Infrastructure will be ready by end-December (Q3), with commercial production starting in January (Q4).
*   **Q: What are the internal levers to protect margins?**
    *   A: Management cited the optimized raw material mix (less sensitive to standard grades) and a continued focus on premium segment products.

### One-line takeaway
Linc is prioritizing market share and volume growth through aggressive promotions and new product launches, accepting near-term margin compression while awaiting raw material stabilization and the commissioning of its new West Bengal facility.
