# Anand Rathi Wealth Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-15 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted consistent, market-agnostic growth and resilience despite Q1 volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Total AUM:** Rs 1,06,300 crore (+21% YoY).
*   **Net flows:** Rs 2,743 crore.
*   **Equity mutual fund net sales:** Rs 1,900 crore.
*   **Consolidated Revenue (ex-fair value/ESOP):** Rs 336 crore (+18% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT (ex-fair value/ESOP):** Rs 116 crore (+24% YoY).
*   **Reported Revenue:** Rs 430 crore; **Reported PAT:** Rs 163 crore.
*   **PAT Margin:** 34.4% (vs 33% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Total client families:** 13,941 (added 1,600+ in last 12 months).

### Highlights
*   19th consecutive quarter of consistent performance.
*   Client attrition (AUM lost) at 0.09%.
*   Platinum client segment grew to ~230 families.
*   Digital wealth AUM: Rs 2,526 crore (+23% YoY).
*   OFA platform: 6,890 subscribers with Rs 1.66 lakh crore assets.
*   UK operations commenced.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management remains confident of achieving full-year guidance: Revenue Rs 1,415 crore and PAT Rs 460 crore.
*   Company achieved 24% of revenue and 25% of PAT guidance in Q1.
*   Long-term goal: 4% market share in Category II mutual funds (requires 22-23% AUM growth).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management views RBI circular on bank guarantees as positive for market stability by curtailing high-frequency trading volatility.
*   Management does not recommend gold, citing it already comprises 28% of Indian household savings.
*   Company focuses on intergenerational wealth management rather than investment banking transactions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Yield pressure from TER changes is minimal (1-2 bps impact).
*   Other income (Rs 110 crore) includes Rs 96 crore MTM gain on Anand Rathi Global Finance Ltd holding.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Board approved applying for an AMC license to build long-term capabilities.
*   GIFT City license: Second stage in process.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Primary MLD issuances: Rs 2,187 crore (vs Rs 1,704 crore in Q1 FY25).
*   Secondary MLD issuances: Rs 968 crore (vs Rs 755 crore in Q1 FY26).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management noted that while market volatility is a factor, their low-beta portfolio and client-centric approach mitigate risks.
*   Concentration risk in structured products (ARGFL) is viewed by management as a derisking mechanism due to credit quality control.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Platinum segment:** Management expects the client count to reach 4 digits in a few years.
*   **AUM per RM:** No cap exists; management expects this to rise as client attrition remains low.
*   **Structured products:** Management prefers internal group products (ARGFL) over third-party to manage credit risk.
*   **LRS:** Management stated this is not a current focus, preferring to concentrate on domestic markets where they have expertise.
*   **AMC License:** Management described this as a strategic "backward integration" to prepare for long-term industry challenges.

### One-line takeaway
Anand Rathi Wealth delivered consistent growth in Q1 FY27, supported by strong net flows and low client attrition, while initiating strategic moves into the AMC business and international markets.
