# Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong PAT growth (55% YoY) and stable asset quality, though management remains watchful of festive demand softness and regulatory headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained long-term targets: AUM growth (16-18%), credit cost (1.3-1.7%), and ROA (2.2-2.5%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong growth" to "cautious/watchful." Management noted the first 10 days of Navratri saw softer commerce compared to last year, particularly in the tractor segment.
- **New topics or risks:** Specific concern raised regarding a new insurance consultation paper; if implemented as is, it could impact fee-based income.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Housing finance subsidiary merger: Appointed date set for 1st April 2027, with an effective date targeted for June/July 2027.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 882 crore (55% YoY growth).
- **AUM:** Rs 1,56,000 crore (15% YoY growth).
- **Disbursement growth:** 22% YoY.
- **Asset Quality:** GS2+GS3 at 8.1% (sequential decrease of 17 bps).
- **Credit Cost:** 1.6% (within 1.3-1.7% zip code).
- **ROA:** 2.3% (H1).
- **Cost-to-income:** Under 37% (down from >40%).
- **Capital Adequacy:** 18% (Tier 1: 16.2%).
- **Subsidiary Performance:** MRHFL posted Rs 35 crore PAT.

### Highlights
- **Diversification:** Non-wheels business grew 52% and now accounts for 20% of total AUM.
- **AI Integration:** AI-orchestrated disbursements reached Rs 1,663 crore. 75% of back-office operations are now covered by AI-enabled workflows.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Cost-to-income ratio improved to under 37%.

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Growth:** Targeting 16-18% (medium to long-term).
- **Credit Cost:** Maintained guidance of 1.3-1.7%.
- **ROA:** Targeting 2.2-2.5% in the medium term.
- **Merger:** Housing finance subsidiary merger targeted for completion by June/July 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Festive Season:** Early reading of the first 10 days of Navratri shows softer commerce vs. last year; tractor segment saw more decline.
- **Rural vs. Urban:** H1 registration data indicates rural/semi-urban demand is growing at a marginally higher clip than urban/metro.
- **Drought:** 282 districts classified as drought-prone; management is monitoring potential impact on the tractor portfolio.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Cost of Funds:** Upward trajectory expected due to the interest rate environment.
- **Margins:** Q2 loan income was impacted by the high liquidity buffer and seasonal trade advances, which are non-earning assets.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Headcount:** Increased to support growth in wheels, SME, and mortgage businesses; management noted this is essential for growth plans.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** Buffer doubled from last year as a prudent measure; management may reduce this if the macro environment stabilizes.
- **Trade Advances:** Deployed at levels above normal to support festive season growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Regulatory:** Potential impact of the new insurance consultation paper on fee-based income.
- **Macro:** Drought-related stress in 282 districts and the West Asia crisis.
- **Interest Rates:** Rising cost of funds environment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: What does "AI-orchestrated" mean?** A: Management clarified it is not 100% digital but AI-enabled (e.g., top-up loans, re-KYC), resulting in lower cost of acquisition.
- **Q: Why are trade advances higher?** A: Management stated they act as "grease" for festive growth and are higher this year to support growth targets.
- **Q: Impact of insurance regulation?** A: Too early to quantify; currently engaging with regulators.
- **Q: Why is the liquidity buffer so high?** A: Prudent stance against macro uncertainty; will reduce if "clouds thin out."
- **Q: Status of co-lending?** A: Working with banks to integrate systems; "in the kitchen."

### One-line takeaway
Management delivered steady growth and stable asset quality while maintaining long-term targets, despite adopting a cautious stance on festive demand and potential regulatory headwinds in the insurance business.
