# PNB Housing Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Landmark quarter driven by strong disbursements, a credit rating upgrade to AAA by CRISIL, and improving asset quality.

### Key numbers
- **Overall Disbursements:** Rs 9,609 crore, up 60% YoY (includes pool buyout of Rs 838 crore).
- **Retail Disbursements:** Rs 9,250 crore, up 54% YoY (all-time high).
- **Loan Book:** Rs 95,079 crore as on September 30, 2026, up 19% YoY.
- **Retail Loan Portfolio:** Rs 94,391 crore, up 19% YoY.
- **Gross NPA (GNPA):** 0.91% vs 0.95% in Q1 FY27.
- **Spread:** 2.43% vs 2.12% in Q1 FY27 (adjusted for one-off: 2.24%).
- **NIM:** 3.67% vs 3.50% in Q1 FY27 (adjusted for one-off: 3.49%).
- **ROA / ROE:** 2.53% / 12.33%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 617 crore, up 6% YoY.
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 27.03% (Tier 1 at 26.62%).
- **Cost of Borrowing:** 7.31% (incremental at 7.26%).
- **Credit Cost:** Negative 19 bps due to Rs 75 crore recovery from the written-off pool.

### Highlights
- CRISIL upgraded the credit rating to AAA with a stable outlook, making PNB Housing Finance AAA-rated across all four leading domestic rating agencies (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings).
- Affordable segment disbursements grew 40% YoY to Rs 1,149 crore; emerging market disbursements grew 36% YoY to Rs 2,882 crore; prime disbursements grew 44% YoY to Rs 4,380 crore.
- Launched Micro Housing business in Q2 FY27, disbursing Rs 71 crore.
- Completed pool buyouts of Rs 986 crore in H1 FY27 (Rs 838 crore in Q2).
- Facilitated PMAY benefits for over 8,000 Urban 2.0 beneficiaries.
- Completed several Generative AI and Voice AI POCs (e.g., Voice AI for Bucket X calling, intelligent document processing).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains loan book growth guidance at 18% to 20% for FY27.
- Management maintains ROA guidance around 2.4%.
- Expects spreads and NIM to maintain a positive or stable trajectory.
- To offset the recent 25 bps repo rate hike, management is evaluating an increase in retail lending rates by 10-15 bps and corporate lending rates by 15-20 bps effective November 1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted that the 25 bps repo rate hike has not impacted market momentum, logins, sanctions, or disbursements.
- ICRA expects affordable housing finance companies to deliver AUM growth of 19%-21% in FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses increased 20% YoY to Rs 260 crore, driven by manpower additions in the affordable vertical. Opex to ATA was 1.03%.
- Normalized credit cost expected to stabilize around 10-12 bps post-normalization.
- Other expenses included a one-off GST payment of Rs 7 crore on prior years' royalty to PNB.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Distribution network stood at 404 branches (234 Roshni affordable branches, 88 emerging market branches, 81 prime branches).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Retail written-off pool reduced to approximately Rs 320 crore.
- Corporate written-off/fraud pool stands at approximately Rs 650 crore across two accounts.
- Net worth stood at Rs 20,250 crore; book value per share at Rs 776.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential impact of proposed regulations on insurance commissions, though management is evaluating offsets via higher processing fees, direct assignment (DA) income, and cost control.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhijit Tibrewal (Motilal Oswal)** asked about the nature of the one-off charges collected from a legacy corporate account. Management clarified it was overdue charges and interest received upon full account closure.
- **Renish (Analyst)** questioned the strategy of sourcing affordable loans from prime/emerging branches. Management explained it leverages existing infrastructure without incremental cost, adding over Rs 100 crore in affordable business in September.
- **Viral Shah (Nomura/Analyst)** inquired about the impact of potential insurance commission changes. Management responded that while premiums may drop 30%-40% for consumers (driving volume), the company is exploring DA sell-downs and fee adjustments to offset the impact.
- **Kunal Shah (Analyst)** asked about non-housing proportions and PBC norms. Management stated they maintain sufficient cushion and will utilize DA sell-downs and pool buyouts to manage norms.
- **Vikas (Analyst)** asked if ROA guidance of 2.4% would be upgraded given Q2 performance. Management stated they are maintaining guidance as Q2 had one-off interest recoveries.

### One-line takeaway
PNB Housing Finance delivered a landmark Q2 FY27 with a 19% YoY loan book growth, a AAA credit rating milestone across all agencies, and robust retail disbursements, while maintaining asset quality and margin resilience.
