# UTI Asset Management Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While core PAT and equity performance showed improvement, redemptions in key schemes and flat international revenues present challenges.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated core revenue from sale of services:** Rs. 398 crore (Q2 FY27) vs. Rs. 390 crore (Q2 FY26)
- **Consolidated core PAT:** Rs. 131 crore, up 22% YoY and 2% QoQ (Q2 FY27)
- **Standalone core PAT:** Rs. 136 crore, up 31% YoY and 14% QoQ (Q2 FY27)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs. 132 crore, up 17% YoY, down 55% QoQ (Q2 FY27)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs. 143 crore, down 14% YoY, down 34% QoQ (Q2 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated core revenue:** Rs. 777 crore (up 1% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated core PAT:** Rs. 263 crore (up 15% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT:** Rs. 426 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **Mutual Fund QAAUM:** Rs. 4.02 lakh crore for Q2 FY27 vs. Rs. 3.93 lakh crore in Q1 FY27 (Market share: 4.61%)
- **Total Group AUM:** Rs. 16.28 lakh crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Equity-oriented assets (active + passive):** 72% of average mutual fund AUM
- **Monthly SIP and STP inflows:** Rs. 982 crore in September 2026
- **SIP AUM:** Rs. 45,572 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Gross SIP inflows:** Rs. 2,583 crore (Q2 FY27); Rs. 5,085 crore (H1 FY27)
- **New SIPs registered:** 5.96 lakh in Q2 FY27 (87% digital)
- **Live folio base:** 1.47 crores with 3.6 lakh new investors added in Q2 FY27
- **UTI Pension Fund AUM:** Rs. 4.32 lakh crore (up 10.97% YoY), managing 24.01% of NPS industry AUM
- **UTI International AUM:** USD 1.67 billion (~Rs. 16,063 crore)
- **UTI Alternatives AUM:** Rs. 4,045 crore (up 52% YoY); SDOF IV crossed Rs. 1,131 crore
- **Process optimization savings:** Rs. 4.22 crore

### Highlights
- Equity research coverage expanded to 480 companies in H1 FY27; evaluated 36 IPOs and participated in 6.
- 51% of equity and hybrid funds ranked in quartile 1 or 2 on a 3-month basis as of September 2026.
- Launched UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund and four passive products (Nifty 500 and BSE India Sector Leaders indices).
- Onboarded 10 new Fintech partners, facilitating ~1 lakh transactions in Q2 FY27 (67% of new investor acquisitions).
- Received SEBI approval for Specialised Investment Fund (SIF) and will launch UTI Classif Hybrid Long Short Fund in Q3 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year standalone employee cost guidance at close to Rs. 95 crore per quarter and consolidated at close to Rs. 130 crore per quarter.
- Management intends to continue its dividend policy of distributing 90-95% of profit. No plans for special dividends or distributing accumulated cash.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry quarterly average AUM rose to Rs. 87.2 lakh crore in Q2 FY27 from Rs. 83.1 lakh crore in Q1 FY27.
- Industry equity-oriented schemes attracted Rs. 92,333 crore in Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 inflows: Rs. 1.83 lakh crore).
- Small and mid-cap funds accounted for 44% of September equity inflows; flexi-cap, multi-cap, large & mid-cap, and large-cap accounted for 41%.
- Monthly industry SIP contribution reached a record Rs. 32,490 crore in September 2026, with industry SIP AUM at Rs. 17.86 lakh crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Weighted average yield is flat at around 31 bps. Segment-wise yields: Equity/hybrid at 73-74 bps, ETF/index at 8 bps, cash/arbitrage at 11 bps, and fixed income at 20-21 bps.
- Passive AUM growth causes marginal yield dilution, but drops straight to the bottom line due to no incremental running costs.
- Marginal yield increase was attributed to commission repricing passed on to intermediaries following SEBI TER to BER regulatory changes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not specifically discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Holding significant cash on the standalone balance sheet, which management deems necessary to support ongoing operations and industry growth size.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Flexi-cap fund remains a challenge with large outflows, though performance has improved into the first quartile.
- Redemptions in equity schemes are primarily originating from banks and national distributors, and to a lesser extent from MFDs and direct channels.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Divij Punjabi (Banyan Tree Advisors):** Asked about equity/hybrid net outflows and employee count drop. **Management answered** that Flexi-cap saw large outflows alongside inflows, but fund performance is now in the first quartile; employee cost guidance is maintained.
2. **Anirudh Sharma (Ekaant Investments):** Inquired about SIP market share gains and AUM growth breakdown. **Management answered** that new SIP registrations improved to ~4% share with an average ticket size of Rs. 3,100; average AUM grew by ~Rs. 10,000 crore of which ~Rs. 5,720 crore came from net sales.
3. **Lalit Mohan (Equirus Securities):** Asked about SIP concentration and channel-wise redemptions. **Management answered** that Flexi-cap contributes ~20% of SIP inflows, followed by sectoral/thematic (16%), mid-cap (15%), and large & mid-cap (12.7%); redemptions are higher from banks and national distributors.
4. **Shreyas Pimple (Nomura):** Asked about the sequential increase in revenue yields. **Management answered** it was due to commission repricing passed on to intermediaries pursuant to SEBI TER-to-BER regulations.
5. **Jagannadham (Individual Investor):** Questioned the capital allocation strategy regarding high cash levels (45-50% of market cap) and suggested special dividends. **Management answered** that the board's decision is to distribute 90-95% of profits as regular dividends, but retain accumulated cash for standalone operations and growth; no special dividend is planned.

### One-line takeaway
UTI AMC reported resilient H1 FY27 performance with core PAT up 15% YoY to Rs. 263 crore, supported by a stable SIP franchise and improving fund quartiles, despite ongoing redemption pressures from bank and national distributor channels.
