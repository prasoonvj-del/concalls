# HDFC Asset Management Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong AUM growth (10% YoY) and operating profit expansion, with management highlighting the resilience of retail investors and successful execution of the alternatives strategy.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained ESOP cost guidance of INR79-80 crore for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "watchful" of market downturns to "confident" in the resilience of Indian retail investors, noting they have evolved faster than anticipated.
- **New topics:** Management confirmed the EPFO fixed income mandate has gone live.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on launching new AIFs (Category II and III).

### Key numbers
- **QAAUM:** INR9.69 trillion (up 10% YoY, 4% QoQ).
- **Equity-oriented AUM:** INR6.51 trillion (67.1% of total AUM).
- **Actively managed equity AUM:** INR6.08 trillion (up 14% YoY).
- **Revenue from operations:** INR11.7 billion (up 14% YoY).
- **Operating profit:** INR9.1 billion (up 16% YoY).
- **Operating margin:** 36 bps of AUM.
- **Profit before tax (PBT):** INR9.8 billion (up 12% YoY).
- **SIP contributions:** INR325 billion (September).
- **Systematic transactions (SIP+STP):** INR48 billion (September).
- **Unique investors:** 17.7 million (3.2 million added in 1 year).
- **ESOP cost:** INR18.6 crore (quarterly).

### Highlights
- **Alternatives:** Launched two new AIFs: a Category II equity fund (private markets) and a Category III fund (listed securities).
- **Mandates:** Commenced management of the EPFO fixed income mandate.
- **Yields:** Active equity (61 bps), Overall equity (58 bps), Debt (28-29 bps), Liquid (13 bps).
- **Market Share:** 12.7% in actively managed equity; 13% in debt.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in the SIP book and long-term industry growth.
- Plans to launch new products in the SIF (Systematic Investment Fund) category over the next few quarters.
- Continues to invest in people, technology (AI-led capabilities), and distribution expansion.
- Management does not provide specific revenue guidance for the alternatives business.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Industry flows:** Equity-oriented funds saw net inflows of INR1,161 billion; debt funds saw net outflows of INR607 billion; liquid funds saw net inflows of INR457 billion; ETFs saw inflows of INR367 billion.
- Management noted that systematic flow "softness" is driven by STP (Systematic Transfer Plan) pauses by wealth managers during choppy markets, not by SIPs.
- Management emphasized that retail investors are showing resilience, with SIPs providing predictability to flows.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margin improved to 36 bps.
- Management maintains a "tight leash" on costs while continuing to invest in future growth areas (alternatives, international, digital).
- Operating expenses are viewed as a mix of inflation-linked routine costs and upfront investments for future capacity.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanded capacity on investor servicing, operations, and sales.
- Alternative investment team now includes 20+ professionals.
- Continued investment in digital and AI-led capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Treasury investment:** ~INR8,700 crore.
- The Board continues to distribute almost all distributable post-tax cash profits as dividends.
- Balance sheet cash is utilized for seed capital in alternative platforms.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Competitive intensity from newer AMCs.
- Market volatility impacting STP flows.
- Potential for investor behavior to be tested in an extended, sustained bear market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why the improvement in yields?** A: Management attributed it to a marginal change in asset mix.
- **Q: Are alternatives margin accretive?** A: Yes, fees for AIFs sit above mutual fund equity books (80-90 bps), while government mandates (EPFO) are highly contested and run for institutional credibility.
- **Q: Is there a risk of losing market share to new entrants?** A: Management views new entrants as validation of industry potential and remains confident in their own product bouquet, distribution network, and long-term track record.
- **Q: Why the softness in systematic flows?** A: Management clarified that the softness is in STP (tactical moves by wealth managers), whereas SIP market share has actually increased.
- **Q: Why is the ESOP cost declining?** A: Management confirmed the non-cash ESOP expense is on track with the previously disclosed schedule (INR79-80 crore for FY27).

### One-line takeaway
HDFC AMC delivered steady growth and maintained cost discipline while successfully scaling its alternatives platform and navigating market volatility through resilient SIP-led inflows.
