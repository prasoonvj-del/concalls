# Max India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue growth across all verticals, substantially reduced EBITDA losses, and positive progress toward profitability.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated revenue:** Rs 95.5 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 90.4% YoY from Rs 50 crore; up 40% QoQ from Rs 68.6 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Consolidated EBITDA loss:** Rs 11.4 crore in Q2 FY27 (down 55% YoY from Rs 25.3 crore; down 54% QoQ from Rs 24.9 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Consolidated treasury assets (Max India level):** Rs 52 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Consolidated net worth:** Rs 395 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Dehradun operating revenue:** Rs 6.5 crore in Q2 (up 13.4% YoY, 5.6% QoQ).
- **Dehradun operating profit:** Rs 0.94 crore (up 25% YoY, 1.8% QoQ).
- **Noida project collections:** Rs 113.8 crore collected in Q2 (INR 163 crore collected out of INR 169 crore total).
- **Noida management fee earned:** ~Rs 10 crore in FY27 (~Rs 9.96 crore recognized in Q2).
- **Gurgaon Estate 360 collections:** Rs 28.5 crore in Q2 (YTD Rs 51 crore; ITD Rs 585 crore); DM fee earned: Rs 2.6 crore in Q2, Rs 4.72 crore in H1, ITD Rs 50.3 crore.
- **Gurgaon Estate 361 collections:** Rs 69.2 crore in Q2 (ITD Rs 177.4 crore); DM fee earned: Rs 4.9 crore in Q2, ITD Rs 12.8 crore. Total combined management fees for 360, 361, and Noida: Rs 17 crore in Q2, Rs 23 crore in H1.
- **Care Home Services revenue:** Rs 12.28 crore (up 40% YoY, 6% QoQ). Care Homes revenue increased 3x from Rs 3 crore to Rs 9 crore over the last 5-6 quarters.
- **Care Home beds & occupancy:** 485 beds across 8 care homes. Overall bed occupancy in Q2 FY27 was 29% (vs 26% in Q1); old beds occupancy moved up to 36% (vs 21% previously). Sector 41 Gurgaon occupancy: 37% (vs 29% in Q1); Chennai OMR: 21% (vs 12%); Noida: 31% (vs 27%); DLF Care Home: 45%.
- **Care Homes CM2:** Improved from negative 107% to 63% YoY.
- **ARPOB (Care Homes):** Rs 6,900 in Q2 FY27 (growing from Rs 5,800 over 6 quarters); peak ARPOB of Rs 8,400 for fresh move-ins in September 2026 exit. On an annual basis, ARPOB is Rs 22 lakh to Rs 25 lakh per bed.
- **AGEasy net revenue:** Rs 35 crore in Q2 FY27 (1.7x YoY growth, 1.9x QoQ growth). Highest monthly revenue of Rs 14.9 crore in September 2026 (exit ARR ~Rs 180 crore).
- **AGEasy ROAS:** 4.2 (note: transcript says 4.7 versus Q1 exit of 2).
- **AGEasy CM2:** Improved YoY from negative 44% to 29%, and QoQ from negative 48% to 18.5%, reaching 11% in September.

### Highlights
- Handed over possession to more than 200 residents in the Noida community (second community becoming operational out of 340 residents).
- Estate 360 sold out; Estate 361 sold 263 units out of 360 launched units (only 97 left in second lot).
- AGEasy recorded 10 patents (8 design, 2 utility; 4 granted, 6 filed including 1 international). Touched 11.7 lakh lives with 1.14 lakh repeat customers. Recognized as Service Provider of the Year (Medical Devices Equipment) at ET Healthcare Awards 2026.
- 4 Care Homes certified by NABH; Care at Home services in Chennai and NCR certified by Healthcare Sector Sales Council.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of meeting the commitment of Rs 1,800 crore gross sales value every year for the next few years.
- AGEasy is on track to achieve EBITDA breakeven in Q4 of this financial year.
- Care Homes are expected to show EBITDA breakeven by the end of H1 FY28.
- Antara Assisted Care aspires to take ARPOB closer to Rs 9,000 over the next 12 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong wait list for Dehradun units.
- AGEasy saw strong demand due to festive season and onset of winter (nebulizers, knee-caps, diapers). 75% to 80% of the surge is sustainable.
- Brand searches for AGEasy increased by over 50% and direct website traffic more than doubled between April and August 2026.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee costs impacted by Noida getting operationalized (150+ manpower added) plus new labor codes and revised PF regulations.
- Gross margins for AGEasy remained under pressure due to the Middle East geopolitical situation impacting logistics costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bengaluru diligence concluding; discussions around definitive agreements for a 6.5 lakh sq. ft. development with potential sales value of ~Rs 900 crore (pure DM arrangement).
- Pursuing another opportunity in Dehradun with estimated sales potential of Rs 850-900 crore.
- Exploring a few more projects in Delhi NCR and one more location in South India.
- Care Home capacity stands at 485 beds across 8 care homes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Treasury assets at Max India stood at Rs 52 crore with a consolidated net worth of Rs 395 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Total collections for Noida Q2: Rs 113.8 crore. Gurgaon Estate 360 ITD collection: Rs 585 crore. Gurgaon Estate 361 ITD collection: Rs 177.4 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Residential business (Antara Senior Living) is lumpy in nature and depends on construction-linked payments and collections, causing quarterly fluctuations in DM fee recognition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Harsh Kundnani (Aionios Alpha) asked about AGEasy seasonality and offline revenue bump.** Management replied that 75%-80% of the surge is sustainable through expanded distribution networks and new geographies, though some normalization will occur in October.
- **Kaustav Bubna (Kamana Holdings) asked about revenue recognition timing for management fees on Max Estates units.** Management clarified that fees are recognized in real time based on collections and quarterly reconciliations, not project completion.
- **Shashank Rastogi (Individual Investor) asked about plans to demerge AGEasy and timeline for consolidated EBITDA positivity.** Management stated restructuring thoughts are premature but being evaluated; AGEasy targets EBITDA breakeven in Q4 FY27, Care Homes by end of H1 FY28, and ASL is always EBITDA positive over a full project life cycle.
- **Nikhil Gupta (Vaayu Capital) asked about finance lease and treasury income numbers.** Management stated finance lease income was ~Rs 15 crore for the quarter, and residences vertical other income was Rs 10.7 crore (Rs 8 crore post-elimination).
- **Rajesh Shah (Ascent Capital) asked about milestones for consolidated EBITDA breakeven and Noida handover timelines.** Management reiterated segment-wise breakeven timelines and noted that Noida handovers are proceeding at 5-6 per day, expected to conclude by month-end.

### One-line takeaway
Max India reported a 90.4% YoY jump in Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue to Rs 95.5 crore alongside a 55% reduction in EBITDA losses, driven by operationalizing Noida, strong sales velocity in Gurgaon residential projects, and scaling of AGEasy and Care Homes.
