# Moneyview Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in disbursals and income, with margin expansion and profitability growth.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursals:** Rs 7,701 crore (up 7% QoQ).
*   **Managed AUM:** Rs 23,900 crore.
*   **Total Income:** Rs 1,146 crore (up 8% QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 201 crore (up 16% QoQ).
*   **Pre-tax margin:** 23% (up from 21% in previous quarter).
*   **ROE:** 27.9% (adjusted for IPO capital).
*   **Collection efficiency (September):** 98.64%.
*   **Stage 1 assets:** 94.6%.

### Highlights
*   First earnings call post-IPO.
*   Registered users: ~15 crore.
*   Repeat customers account for ~65% of AUM.
*   Lending model: 75% via Lending Service Provider (LSP) model, 25% via own NBFC.
*   IPO proceeds: Rs 750 crore raised; Rs 250 crore downstreamed to NBFC in October.
*   *Note:* The company's record shows PAT growth of 15.6% QoQ, which aligns with the 16% mentioned in the call.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects FY27 PAT to exceed Rs 800 crore.
*   Management targets top-line growth of ~40% for FY27.
*   Management expects margins to remain healthy and operating leverage to improve.
*   Management aims for ROE to be closer to 30% once IPO capital is fully deployed.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   India's personal loan market is expected to reach Rs 33-36 lakh crore by FY31.
*   Digital personal loans are projected to grow at 26%-27% annually through FY31.
*   Management sees strong festive season momentum across disbursals and collections.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Incremental borrowing cost for NBFC: 12.7%-12.8% (down from 14.5%-14.8% last year).
*   CAC as % of disbursals: 1.6% (down from 1.9%-2% a year ago).
*   Opex as % of total income: 34.5% (down from 35.9% in previous quarter).
*   Average interest rate charged to customers: 24%-25%; processing fees: 3%-4%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Not discussed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   No plans to use IPO proceeds to repay debt; funds will be leveraged to raise additional debt.
*   17 active lending partnerships; no single partner contributes >13%-14% of AUM.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management targets credit cost to remain below 5% (currently 4.9%).
*   Management noted that rating agencies are currently reviewing the company for potential upgrades following the IPO.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Viral Shah (IIFL):** Asked about FY27 outlook and asset quality. Management guided for >Rs 800 crore PAT and stable asset quality.
*   **Anirudh Sharma (Ekaant):** Asked about cost of borrowing reduction. Management explained it is due to rating upgrades and incremental borrowing at lower rates.
*   **Nischint (Kotak):** Asked about sourcing strategy. Management clarified 75% is direct sourcing (half organic, half paid) and 25% via embedded partnerships.
*   **Nidhesh Jain (Kotak):** Asked about revenue vs. AUM growth divergence. Management attributed this to margin expansion with partners and commercial negotiations.
*   **Mohit Surana (HDFC AMC):** Asked about credit cost increase. Management clarified it is a minor 2 bps movement on a quarterly basis and remains within the <5% target.

### One-line takeaway
Moneyview delivered a strong inaugural quarter post-IPO with 16% QoQ PAT growth, supported by margin expansion and a capital-light model, while guiding for >Rs 800 crore PAT in FY27.
