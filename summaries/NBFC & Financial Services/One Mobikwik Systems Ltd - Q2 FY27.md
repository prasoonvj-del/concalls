# One Mobikwik Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the fourth consecutive profitable quarter with record-high PAT and EBITDA, driven by growth in payments and lending verticals.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 302 crore (up 8% YoY, 5% QoQ).
*   **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 295 crore (up 9% YoY).
*   **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 22.7 crore (up Rs 29 crore YoY, 44% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 7.5% (up 977 bps YoY, 204 bps QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 11.8 crore (up Rs 40 crore YoY, 55% QoQ).
*   **Lending Disbursals:** Rs 1,083 crore.
*   **Contribution Profit:** Jumped 50% YoY.

### Highlights
*   **Business Performance:** Achieved fourth consecutive profitable quarter; highest-ever PAT and EBITDA.
*   **Vertical Growth:** Merchant GMV grew 76% YoY; Merchant revenue grew 150% YoY.
*   **Lending:** Onboarded 2 new lending partners, bringing the total active partner count to 11.
*   **NBFC Update:** Completed migration and slump sale of credit distribution business in August; awaiting final RBI go-ahead for Certificate of Registration (CoR).
*   **Strategy:** Management is shifting focus toward higher-quality users and GMV (transactions >Rs 2,000) to maximize potential revenue from upcoming UPI MDR.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY28 PAT:** Management targets Rs 100 crore PAT for the next financial year, assuming UPI MDR implementation.
*   **Lending:** Expects to maintain strong double-digit growth in lending disbursals.
*   **Payments Revenue:** Estimates an additional Rs 100 crore in payments revenue for FY28 post-UPI MDR implementation.
*   **Fixed Costs:** Management expects fixed costs as a percentage of total income to decline in upcoming quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **UPI Trends:** Q2 was seasonally soft for the industry; UPI value across the industry grew only 1% QoQ.
*   **Merchant Payments:** Management sees only 2-3 clear competitors in the merchant segment, unlike the highly competitive consumer UPI side.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Expects competitive intensity to increase with UPI MDR, but believes it will create a level playing field where companies compete on product/service rather than capital burn.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Take Rates:** Blended payment margin is currently 13 bps. Merchant payments net take rate is 3-4 bps; consumer payments net take rate is 16 bps+.
*   **Lending Costs:** Lending-related costs are showing a lower rate compared to previous quarters due to renegotiated contracts with partners and improved credit performance.
*   **Employee Expenses:** Increase in employee costs reflects a deliberate investment phase to support new business segments (e.g., NBFC, Merchant Payments).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **NBFC Model:** Plans to operate on an asset-light model. For a hypothetical Rs 6,000 crore lending target, aims to do 10% (Rs 600 crore) via own NBFC using a 10:90 co-lending model, requiring Rs 60 crore equity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Lending Mix:** Disbursal mix is 79% under the First Loss Default Guarantee (FLDG) model and 21% under the pure distribution model.
*   **FLDG:** Operates within the 5% DLG guidance of RBI regulations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Consumer UPI:** Experienced a soft quarter in consumer UPI; management noted this is seasonal and attributed it to a strategic shift toward higher-ticket transactions.
*   **EPS Discrepancy:** Management acknowledged a potential typo in the reported EPS (Rs 0.75) and committed to providing an updated disclosure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On UPI MDR impact:** Management clarified that of the expected Rs 100 crore revenue upside, roughly 20-25% (Rs 20-25 crore) is expected to flow to the bottom line.
*   **On Merchant vs. Consumer economics:** Management noted that while consumer side monetization is capped at ~8 bps, the merchant side offers broader cross-sell opportunities.
*   **On Lending Model:** Management stated they are agnostic between FLDG and pure distribution models, prioritizing overall disbursal growth over a fixed ratio.
*   **On Fixed Costs:** Management defended the rise in employee costs as necessary for scaling new business segments but reiterated that fixed costs as a percentage of income should trend downward.

### One-line takeaway
MobiKwik delivered its fourth consecutive profitable quarter with record earnings, while management remains focused on scaling the merchant and lending verticals ahead of the anticipated UPI MDR implementation.
