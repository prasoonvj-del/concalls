# Can Fin Homes Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-24 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported growth across all geographies and segments, successfully met disbursement targets, and expressed confidence in maintaining NIMs and asset quality despite higher-than-expected prepayments.

### Key numbers
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 2,609 crore (vs. Rs 2,500 crore projected).
*   **AUM Growth:** 10.8% (Targeting 14% for FY27).
*   **Yield:** 9.81%.
*   **Cost of Borrowing:** 6.98%.
*   **Spread:** 2.83%.
*   **NIM:** 3.81%.
*   **ROA:** 2.39%.
*   **ROE:** 18%+.
*   **Rundown (Prepayments/Closures):** Rs 1,857 crore (vs. Rs 1,730 crore in Q4 FY26).
    *   BT Out: Rs 408 crore (vs. Rs 400 crore in Q4 FY26).
    *   Part Prepayments: Rs 1,072 crore (vs. Rs 976 crore in Q4 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Growth Drivers:** Positive growth across all 6 zones; salaried segment grew 21% YoY, SENP grew 44% YoY.
*   **Segment Performance:** Housing loans grew 28% YoY; Non-housing loans (NHL) grew 32% YoY.
*   **IT Transformation:** Successfully piloted new LOS/LMS/Reporting systems in 5 branches; full implementation across 245 branches planned for Q2 FY27.
*   **Asset Quality:** Credit cost guidance maintained at 10 bps. Stage 2 and 3 delinquency absolute values decreased compared to March 2026.
*   **Customer Profile:** 82% of loans now have a CIBIL score >700.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Disbursements:** Targeting Rs 3,000 crore for Q2 FY27; full-year target remains Rs 13,000 crore, though management may push for higher if needed to offset elevated rundowns.
*   **AUM Growth:** Management maintains 14% growth target for FY27.
*   **NIM:** Confident in maintaining NIM at 3.81%+.
*   **Cost-to-Income:** Expected to hover around 19.5% for FY27; aims to return to ~18% in three years.
*   **Tax Rate:** Expected to be 21% for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   No slowdown in demand observed across geographies; project launches remain steady.
*   Added 60 new APF (Approved Project Finance) projects in Q1; total approved projects reached 331.
*   Management noted that while IT sector job losses exist, they have not seen a material impact on their loan book or delinquency rates.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Incremental bank borrowing costs have risen to 7.25%–7.5% (vs. 6.95% previously).
*   To protect margins, management increased the minimum ticket size for "special rates" from Rs 20 lakh to Rs 25 lakh.
*   IT implementation costs (AMCs, depreciation) have begun to impact opex, but no major surprises expected.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Liquidity remains comfortable with strong bank support.
*   Management is analyzing ways to retain customers making part-prepayments, including potential conversion to deposit customers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Elevated Rundowns:** Higher-than-anticipated part-prepayments (due to tenure reduction following interest rate resets) are offsetting disbursement growth.
*   **Competition:** Increased competition from large HFCs and banks; interest rate differential (vs. market leaders) has widened to >1%, making customer retention more challenging.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On IT Implementation Impact:** Management clarified that the 5-branch pilot showed no business disruption, and they expect to complete the rollout across all branches by the end of Q2 without impacting business volumes.
*   **On IT Sector Exposure:** Management stated IT sector exposure is only ~6% of customers and they have not seen a material impact on asset quality.
*   **On Write-offs:** Management confirmed cumulative write-offs since 2001 are ~Rs 20 crore, excluding fraud-related cases which are fully provided for as NPAs.
*   **On Competitive Strategy:** Management acknowledged Bajaj Housing Finance's technology advantage and stated they are "catching up" via their own IT transformation.
*   **On SENP Segment:** Management noted SENP segment is margin-accretive (yields ~0.5% higher than salaried) and they are maintaining strict underwriting (documented income/IT returns).

### One-line takeaway
Can Fin Homes delivered a resilient Q1 with strong disbursement growth, but faces margin pressure from elevated prepayments and rising borrowing costs, which it aims to mitigate through IT-led efficiency gains and tighter customer selection.
