# Anand Rathi Share And Stock Brokers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-20 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management remains confident in long-term growth and business model resilience despite regulatory headwinds and a one-time exceptional expense.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** ₹2,461 million (up 22.37% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** ₹973 million (up 30.19% YoY); **EBITDA Margin:** 39.54%.
*   **PAT (before exceptional items):** ₹391 million (up 71.2% YoY); **PAT Margin:** 16%.
*   **PAT (after exceptional items):** ₹233.51 million (up 2.35% YoY); **PAT Margin:** 9.49%.
*   **Asset Under Custody:** ₹1.13 lakh crore (up 21.44% YoY).
*   **MTF Book:** ₹13,318 million (up 55% YoY).
*   **Distribution AUM:** ₹94,791 million (up 25.82% YoY).
*   **Exceptional Expense:** ₹209.96 million (related to depository fraud).
*   **Debt-Equity Ratio:** 0.81.

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Mix:** 52% broking, 29% non-broking (MTF + distribution), 19% other income.
*   **Operational Strength:** Employee base grew to 2,263 (from 2,148 in Q1 FY26); ~57% of clients have been associated for >3 years.
*   **Credit Rating:** Upgraded to A1+ (short-term) and A+ (long-term) for bank facilities.
*   **Fraud Incident:** Recognized ₹209.96 million as an exceptional expense due to fraudulent off-market transfers in two dormant client accounts; insurance claims filed and EOW investigation ongoing.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Targeting 15% to 25% YoY.
*   **PAT Growth:** Targeting 30% to 35% YoY.
*   **MTF Book:** Target of ₹1,750 crore to ₹1,800 crore by end of FY27.
*   **Distribution AUM:** Target to scale by 40%.
*   **Strategic Goal:** Maintain a 50-50 revenue mix between broking and non-broking income.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Environment:** Navigated geopolitical tensions, crude oil volatility, and FII outflows; resilience supported by domestic liquidity and retail participation (demat base at 23 crore).
*   **Regulatory Impact:** SEBI measures on F&O (e.g., upfront margin) are curbing speculative activity; RBI’s new capital market exposure framework (effective July 1, 2026) has significantly increased working capital requirements.
*   **Trend:** Industry transitioning toward long-term investing over trading-led activities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **MTF Yield:** ~14%.
*   **Exceptional Expense:** One-time charge taken to replenish client securities as a goodwill gesture; management expects potential recovery via insurance and legal proceedings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **International:** Passing resolution to create a subsidiary in Dubai to address NRI client needs.
*   **Digital:** Focusing on AI-led insights and end-to-end digital onboarding integrated with India's digital public infrastructure (UPI, Account Aggregator, eSign).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Leverage:** Management views the 0.81 debt-equity ratio as conservative; intends to increase borrowing to support MTF book growth, noting that industry standards often reach 1.5x–2x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Fraud:** Potential for future operational risks; external consultant (EY) hired for forensic audit and process strengthening.
*   **Regulatory:** Ongoing changes in SEBI/RBI frameworks impacting trading volumes and capital requirements.
*   **Market:** Volatility and geopolitical risks impacting short-term sentiment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did MTF interest income stay flat despite loan growth?** A: Management explained the average book size was flat due to a market correction in March; the book has since resumed growth.
*   **Q: How will you prevent future fraud?** A: Management has engaged an external consultant for a forensic audit, fine-tuned internal processes, and filed insurance claims.
*   **Q: Why is the debt-equity ratio increasing?** A: Management is comfortable with higher leverage (up to 2x) to fund MTF growth; current levels are considered low.
*   **Q: What is the strategy for young investors?** A: Targeting the 25-30 age group via app-based onboarding, focusing on those transitioning from trading to wealth creation.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational growth and a clear diversification strategy remain intact despite a one-time exceptional expense related to a depository fraud incident.
