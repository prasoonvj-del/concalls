# Apollo Finvest India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Ramp-up in disbursement volumes, revenue, and profit driven by Apollo Cash.

### Key numbers
- Disbursements: 65+ crores total (scale-up of 60X in 6-7 months since February; ~20-22 crores monthly)
- Total loans disbursed: 1.6 lakh+
- Downloads: 5 lakh+
- Engineering team growth: 3X in one quarter
- Tech team composition: Close to 43% of total team

### Highlights
- Apollo Cash is showing strong product-market fit with organic traction and zero Google or Facebook ads so far.
- Key senior hire: Swaujas Chatterjee joined as Head of Marketing (from fintech space); Head of Engineering joining soon.
- 100% of engineering bandwidth is currently focused on security, platform stability, and scalability (aiming for 10x growth).
- Developing the next version of the underwriting model for better approval rates, risk rates, and smarter loan amount allocation.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management has ambitious plans and is laying the foundation to scale up significantly over coming quarters and the next year.
- Aims to double or triple disbursements without requiring external capital, with potential debt market entry or lender engagement when approaching 80-90% capital utilisation or in a couple of quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Users finding value are digitally savvy, well-versed in digital lending, and understand the ecosystem responsibilities.
- Demand resembles an FMCG product / digital credit card usage pattern where users borrow, repay before due dates, and shut/reopen facilities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Processing fees and interest from Apollo Cash are landing directly on the company's book, improving revenues and P&L.
- Focus is on marketing efficiency and unit economics rather than brand/mindshare marketing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company has provisions to borrow about 100-odd crores from the debt market if required; leverage is close to zero.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Repayment timelines for EMI loans are currently 3 months and 6 months, with exploration of 9 and 12 months. Early repayments/foreclosures are common.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cybersecurity and software infrastructure risks highlighted as requiring unprecedented caution and effort.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prajwal Ballal asked about the increase in fees and commission.** Management explained it was driven by Apollo Cash revenues landing directly on the books.
- **Sanjeev Patkar asked how to balance larger ticket sizes, better approval rates, and lower loss rates.** Management explained this is achieved via machine learning and statistically significant real-time data from Apollo Cash.
- **Sanjeev Patkar asked about balancing marketing efficiency vs mindshare.** Management stated the focus is strictly on product marketing, organic funnels, and marketing efficiency rather than vanity PR or brand ads.
- **Priyadarshan Banjan asked about capital requirements and outgrowing the balance sheet.** Management stated they have headroom to comfortably double or triple without external capital, with potential debt market access (~100 crores provisioned) in a couple of quarters.
- **Ronak Chawda asked about monthly volume targets for the financial year.** Management declined to give forward numbers, adhering to an "under-promise and over-deliver" mantra, but highlighted ambitious plans.

### One-line takeaway
Apollo Finvest's Q2 FY27 performance was bolstered by the rapid organic scale-up of Apollo Cash, with management focusing on tech security, hiring, and underwriting refinement ahead of a larger marketing and scale-up phase.
