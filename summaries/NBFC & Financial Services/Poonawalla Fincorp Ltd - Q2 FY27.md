# Poonawalla Fincorp Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-14 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Inflection point in establishing a self-sustaining earnings engine with disciplined AUM expansion, expanding NIMs, lower credit costs, and higher ROA.

### Key numbers
- AUM: Rs 74,008 crore (up 10.4% QoQ)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 375 crore (up 21.8% QoQ)
- Return on Assets (ROA): 2.18% (up 20 bps QoQ, up ~149 bps YoY)
- Gross NPA (GNPA): 1.20% (vs 1.37% in Q1 FY27, 39 bps YoY reduction)
- Net NPA (NNPA): 0.61% (vs 0.70% in Q1 FY27)
- Credit Cost: 2.19% (vs 2.40% in Q1 FY27, 2.51% in Q4 FY26)
- Net Interest Margin (NIM) + Fees: 9.26% (vs 9.10% in Q1 FY27)
- Portfolio Yield: 14.08% (up ~45 bps over Q1 FY27)
- Opex-to-AUM: 4.15%
- Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR): 18.68% (Tier 1 at 17.15%)
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR): 194% (Surplus liquidity of Rs 6,526 crore)
- Debt-Equity Ratio: 4.3x

### Highlights
- New products contributed 28% of quarterly disbursements (vs 26% in previous quarter).
- Digital Prime Personal Loans average monthly disbursements reached ~Rs 577 crore (growing 8% QoQ, contributing 42% of PL disbursements).
- Gold loan network expanded by 90 branches to 550 branches; quarterly disbursements reached Rs 1,066 crore (vs Rs 875 crore in Q1 FY27).
- Consumer durable franchise scaled to 20,000 dealers across 360 locations, with quarterly disbursements of Rs 606 crore.
- Education loans average monthly disbursements grew to Rs 218 crore (35% fully digitally processed).
- AI program expanded to 143 total projects (84 live in production, 34 delivered in Q2 FY27).
- Long-term borrowings constitute 88.92% of the debt mix; cost of borrowing stood at 7.76%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track to add a total of approximately 200 new gold loan branches in the financial year.
- Management expects credit costs to stabilize and see further structural improvements backed by calibration and collections.
- Management targets a structural operating leverage trajectory over the next 1-2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Commercial vehicles ended with an average monthly disbursement of Rs 127 crore across 1,250 channel partners and 90+ locations in 13 states.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of borrowings stood at 7.76% (vs 7.72% in Q1 FY27).
- Opex-to-AUM movement of 4.15% sits within the 10-25 bps guided range due to frontloaded investments in collection capacity, AI, and gold loan branch rollouts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gold loan branches increased from 460 to 550.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cumulative mismatch is positive across all buckets with surplus liquidity of Rs 6,526 crore.
- Provisioning coverage ratio stood at 49.14%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted general risks including global fuel price uncertainty affecting commercial vehicles, fluctuations in earnings, competition, economic conditions, and potential insurance commission regulation changes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Suraj Das (Sundaram Mutual Fund)** asked about operating leverage progress and product-level profitability. Management answered that cross-sell is in its early stages of compounding, and newer products (like gold branches breaking even in 13 months) are scaling alongside investments in AI and distribution.
- **Pranay Mehta (Investor)** asked about credit cost stabilization and product mix. Management stated that credit costs have hit a stable structural level of 2.19% supported by tight calibration and collections.
- **Abhijit Tibrewal (Analyst)** asked about the sensitivity of borrowing costs to repo rate hikes and the trade-off between growth and profitability. Management responded that one-third of borrowings are in fixed-rate NCDs, pricing power allows passing on rate hikes, and all three ROA vectors (spreads, opex, credit cost) are aligned.
- **Avinash Singh (Analyst)** questioned write-offs relative to book seasoning and potential impact of proposed insurance commission regulations. Management stated write-offs are moderating with seasoning (36 months for most products) and that core lending spreads and operating leverage will absorb potential insurance fee compressions.

### One-line takeaway
Poonawalla Fincorp delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with AUM reaching Rs 74,008 crore, PAT growing 21.8% QoQ to Rs 375 crore, and ROA expanding to 2.18%, driven by robust retail momentum, improving asset quality (GNPA at 1.20%), and enterprise-wide AI adoption.
