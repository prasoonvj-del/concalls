# Five-Star Business Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported historical best disbursements and strong collection efficiency, signaling a robust growth trajectory.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained; management remains confident of comfortably exceeding the full-year growth guidance.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "return to growth" (Q1) to "historical best" performance (Q2); management expressed high confidence in the current trajectory.
- **New topics:** Detailed explanation provided on how internal top-up loan features are inflating repayment rates; clarified that the IRDA circular on insurance commissions has zero impact on the company.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on disbursement growth (Rs 2,078 crore vs Q1’s Rs 1,496 crore).

### Key numbers
- **Disbursements:** Rs 2,078 crore (+39% QoQ, +74% YoY).
- **AUM:** Rs 14,640 crore (+6.7% QoQ, +14% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 299 crore (+10% QoQ, +5% YoY); includes Rs 19 crore one-time tax benefit.
- **Collection Efficiency (Unique Customer):** 98.2% (vs 97.7% in Q1).
- **x-bucket Collections:** 99.4% (vs 99.2% in Q1).
- **Current Bucket Proportion:** 84.78% (vs 83.3% in Q1).
- **30+ DPD:** 11.5% (vs 12.38% in Q1).
- **Slippages:** 0.68% (vs 0.70% in Q1).
- **Credit Cost:** 1.80% (vs 1.85% in Q1).
- **Cost of Funds:** 8.78% (vs 8.80% in Q1).
- **NIM:** 19.87% (vs 19.97% in Q1).
- **ROA:** 8.39% (+28 bps QoQ); **ROE:** 15.25% (+79 bps QoQ).

### Highlights
- **Operational Performance:** Achieved historical best quarterly disbursements.
- **Branch Expansion:** Added 19 branches during the quarter; 75 branches added in the last year.
- **Opex:** Management stated that Opex has fully absorbed the impact of new collection and legal infrastructure; expects operating leverage to reduce cost-to-income ratios in coming years.
- **Yields:** Incremental disbursements from October 2026 reflect a 1% yield increase to cushion against rising cost of funds.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth:** Management expects to comfortably cross the growth guidance for the year.
- **Credit Quality:** Expects flattish credit slippages and credit costs for the rest of the year.
- **Cost of Funds:** Expects an inch-up in cost of funds; incremental cost of borrowing expected to rise 20-25 bps in H2.
- **Exit FY27:** Expects cost of funds on book to be ~8.90%.
- **Opex Ratio:** Expects the Opex ratio to trend down by 50-75 bps over the next two years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Regional:** No drought impact observed in core states (Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Telangana); collections in these regions remain robust.
- **Competition:** Management does not view Mudra loans as a direct competitor due to different target borrower profiles and documentation requirements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Spreads:** NIM remained stable at 19.87%; management expects some compression due to the tightening rate cycle, to be offset by higher yields on incremental disbursements.
- **Insurance:** Zero impact from the recent IRDA circular as the company does not earn commissions on insurance products (pure pass-through).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Strategy:** Focus remains on deepening presence in core geographies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** ~Rs 1,300 crore cash on hand plus Rs 1,100 crore in unavailed sanctions.
- **Net Worth:** Rs 7,903 crore.
- **Borrowing Mix:** 69% from banks.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Interest Rates:** Hawkish RBI stance and tightening rate cycle expected to pressure margins.
- **Sticky NPAs:** Management noted that while slippages are controlled, existing NPAs are "sticky" and will take time to cure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On Repayment Rates:** Management clarified that the rise in repayment rates is due to internal top-up loan features (adjusting outstanding balances), not BT-outs.
- **On New Product:** Management confirmed interest in a new product but deferred details to the next quarter.
- **On Top-up Loans:** These represent ~10-15% of disbursements; management emphasized strict guardrails (minimum 12-18 month vintage, no DPD threshold breaches).
- **On Write-offs:** Recovery takes 18-24 months due to the lack of SARFAESI for loans <Rs 20 lakh; management expects recoveries to increase from FY28 onwards.
- **On Asset Quality:** Management confirmed that 1+ DPD customers are not eligible for top-up loans to avoid restructuring/evergreening concerns.

### One-line takeaway
Five-Star delivered a strong quarter with record disbursements and improving collection metrics, while management remains focused on navigating margin compression through yield hikes and operating leverage.
