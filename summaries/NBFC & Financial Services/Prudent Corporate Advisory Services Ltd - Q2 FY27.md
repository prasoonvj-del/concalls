# Prudent Corporate Advisory Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating business recorded its strongest quarter with robust growth in AUM, revenue, and operating profit despite a sharp market correction and treasury drag.

### Key numbers
- **Quarterly Average AUM (Q2 FY27):** INR 1.43 lakh crores (up 8% QoQ, 21% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Average AUM:** INR 1.38 lakh crores
- **Equity AUM (Sept 2026):** INR 1.37 lakh crores (up 16.2% YoY)
- **Quarterly Net Sales (Equity AUM):** INR 4,137 crores (highest in history)
- **Monthly SIP Book (Sept 2026):** INR 1,272 crores (up 17.2% YoY)
- **Market Share in Equity-Oriented AUM (ex-ETF):** 2.7% (up from 2.59% YoY)
- **Share of SIP Contribution:** 3.73% (up from 3.49% YoY)
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 736 crores for H1 FY27 (up 20% YoY); up 11.8% QoQ for Q2 FY27
- **Operating Profit:** INR 192 crores for H1 FY27 (up 37.6% YoY); up 15.4% QoQ for Q2 FY27
- **Operating Margin:** 26.4% for Q2 FY27 (expansion of 80 bps QoQ); 26.1% for H1 FY27 (up from 22.7% YoY)
- **Other Income:** INR 3.8 crores for Q2 FY27 (down 82% QoQ from INR 20.8 crores due to MTM drag on debt/hybrid portfolio)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 71.7 crores for Q2 FY27 (down 4.1% QoQ); INR 146.5 crores for H1 FY27 (up 39.1% YoY)
- **Profit Before Tax (ex-Other Income):** Up 16% QoQ and 45% YoY
- **Insurance Fresh Premium Growth (Q2 QoQ):** General Insurance up 54%, Life Insurance up 37% (YoY: GI up 62%, Life up 45%)
- **Insurance Contribution to Profit:** Roughly 12%
- **Average PMS AUM:** INR 2,020 crores (up 37% YoY)
- **SIF AUM / Certified Distributors:** INR ~900 crores / 1,792 distributors
- **New Distributors Onboarded (H1):** 3,541 (nearly 30% higher than H1 last year)
- **Health 60-Month Persistency:** 85%-86%

### Highlights
- Equity AUM grew 1.7% QoQ despite a negative MTM impact of INR 1,867 crores, driven by record quarterly net sales of INR 4,137 crores.
- MF revenue grew 9.4% QoQ (faster than the 7.9% QoQ AUM growth) due to one additional revenue day; MF yield remained stable at 88-89 bps.
- Insurance and PMS verticals saw strong sequential revenue growth of 35% and 14% respectively.
- Prutech renamed to Prudent Asset Management Private Limited; in the process of applying for an MF PMS license ahead of the April 2027 operational framework.
- Added 3,541 new distributors in H1 FY27 due to regulatory changes prompting partners to join platforms supporting compliance and tech.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a workable middle path to emerge regarding proposed insurance distribution regulations following industry representations.
- If insurance regulations are implemented as proposed, management anticipates an offset via accelerated mutual fund business as agents seek second income streams.
- Management expects PMS and bonds to become additional growth drivers over time.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Market correction in late October brought current AUM on par with the H1 average of INR 1.38 lakh crores.
- Equity AUM outperformed indices (NIFTY 50 declined 8.1%, NIFTY 500 declined 2.9% YoY) due to consistent SIP inflows and distributor alpha.
- Investor behavior is seen as more mature, with lower redemption pressure despite flat/negative 2-year returns and rising SIP cancellations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins expanded by 80 bps QoQ to 26.4%.
- Commission payout as a % of non-insurance operating revenue increased from 61.4% to 62.6% due to an additional provision of INR 1.75 crores (INR 2.5 crores total) for annual additional trails booked after observing stronger sales projections.
- Insurance business margins are stated to be in line with mutual funds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pan-India branch network, tech platform, and ~700 sales employees are utilized to support incoming distributors.
- Awaiting final clarity on FICP framework from SEBI before full-fledged online bond business launch.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Higher bond yields and hybrid portfolio reversals caused a sharp sequential drop in other income (treasury line).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Proposed insurance distribution consultation paper introduces two segments: IDA (Individual Distribution Agents) and IDE (Independent Distribution Entities like Prudent).
- Proposed rules mandate lower commissions for IDEs (e.g., 5% renewal on health insurance) compared to IDAs (10%), which management noted would make the POSP business difficult to sustain if implemented as drafted.
- IDAs would be restricted to tying up with only one general and one standalone health insurer, whereas IDEs retain multi-line optionalities.
- Other income is exposed to market volatility via debt and hybrid treasury portfolios.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal) asked about the insurance consultation paper impact and whether POSP business can survive.** Management responded that while the 12% profit contribution from insurance could face headwinds, it will not disappear overnight, and agents may migrate to IDEs for multi-insurer choices.
2. **Prayesh Jain asked about increased competition in mutual funds from new platform entrants.** Management stated that while competition increases, new entrants primarily expand the market by converting POSPs, and Prudent's long-term trail trust helps retain margins.
3. **Dipanjan Ghosh asked about the mechanics of renewal contracts with POSPs.** Management clarified that renewal commission payables to POSPs are contractual and will be adjusted downward proportionally if regulatory renewal receivables are reduced.
4. **Swarnabha Mukherjee asked about sustainable insurance margins and investor sentiment.** Management stated that while IDE/IDA parity is desired, specific survival numbers are hard to speculate on until final rules arrive. Investor sentiment remains resilient with record net sales.
5. **Sanket Godha asked whether PMS MF will cannibalize existing MFD/direct plans.** Management stated PMS MF will attract high-quality distributors and HNIs, offering a variable fee structure up to 1%, matching current yields.

### One-line takeaway
Prudent delivered its strongest operating quarter with record equity net sales and strong profit growth, while actively navigating potential regulatory headwinds in its insurance distribution segment.
