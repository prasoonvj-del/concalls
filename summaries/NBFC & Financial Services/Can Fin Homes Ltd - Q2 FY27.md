# Can Fin Homes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-17 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management remains confident in long-term growth and demand, but reported a significant, albeit temporary, disruption in disbursements due to the completion of the IT transformation project.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 disbursement target of Rs 13,000 crore and AUM growth target of 13-14%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from purely positive to cautious regarding short-term operational metrics due to IT migration impact; demand remains robust.
- **New topics:** "Project Tejas" (IT transformation) completion, Telangana "Section 22" property registration issues, PMAY 2.0, and potential regulatory changes to insurance commissions.
- **Last quarter's promises:** IT transformation rollout across all branches was completed as promised; sales team expansion is underway.

### Key numbers
- **Disbursements:** Rs 2,375 crore (net of Rs 29 crore cancellations).
- **Prepayments:** Rs 1,795 crore (vs Rs 1,857 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Credit Cost:** 9 bps (Guidance: 10 bps).
- **NHB Refinance Sanction:** Rs 1,350 crore.
- **Sales Team Contribution:** Rs 560 crore (from ~90 staff).

### Highlights
- **IT Transformation:** "Project Tejas" fully live as of Oct 1, 2026. Migration caused a 70-75% drop in disbursements in affected branches during the month of implementation.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Management plans to reallocate staff from operations to the sales team (increasing from ~90 to 150 people) due to process automation.
- **Branch Expansion:** 25 of 28 planned branches opened and operational.
- **Telangana Issue:** New "Section 22" property registration verification requirements caused temporary processing delays; process has been updated to verify before login.
- **Insurance:** Management stated proposed regulatory cuts to insurance commissions will have no meaningful impact (<1% of PBT).

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27:** Maintains Rs 13,000 crore disbursement target; expects to cover Q2 shortfall in Q3 and Q4.
- **FY28:** Targets Rs 16,000 crore disbursements and 15%+ AUM growth.
- **Margins:** Aims to protect NIM (3.75%) and spread (2.75%).
- **Digital Sourcing:** Targets 10% of business from digital channels by Q4 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Demand:** Remains robust across geographies; management views rate hikes as a "non-event" for demand.
- **PMAY 2.0:** Slower scale-up compared to PMAY-1; management expects limited impact.
- **El Niño:** Potential risk of construction slowdown due to water shortages in certain regions, though it is "too early" to project impact.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Rate Hike:** Passed on 10 bps to housing customers effective Oct 12, 2026.
- **Liability Repricing:** 65% of liabilities are floating; a 100 bps repo hike impacts the book by ~12-13 bps. Management may increase future hikes to 15 bps to match repo moves.
- **NHB Cost:** Blended cost of new refinance expected between 6.6% and 6.7%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** LCR maintained >100%.
- **Borrowing Mix:** Short-term borrowings reduced from 26-27% (Mar '23) to 12%.
- **Undrawn Sanctions:** ~Rs 2,500 crore available.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **IT Disruption:** One-time impact on Q2 disbursements.
- **Interest Rate Cycle:** Rising rates pose a risk to liability repricing if not passed on to customers.
- **Property Registration:** Regulatory changes (like Section 22 in Telangana) can create localized processing bottlenecks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On IT Impact:** Management confirmed that affected branches saw a 70-75% drop in disbursements during the migration month but recovered to 80-90% of previous levels the following month.
- **On Prepayments:** Management noted the gap between market rates and company rates is shrinking, which should help reduce prepayments.
- **On Sales Team:** Management clarified that the increase in sales staff (to 150) is being achieved by moving existing staff from operations, not by adding new headcount.
- **On Liability Repricing:** Management explained that bank borrowings reprice the month following a repo hike, while T-bill-linked loans reprice at quarterly resets.
- **On Competitive Strategy:** Management is focusing on "feet on street" and digital journeys to compete, noting that they are not under compulsion to raise high-cost NCDs.

### One-line takeaway
Can Fin Homes faced a temporary disbursement dip due to a necessary IT overhaul, but management maintains full-year guidance, citing robust underlying demand and efficiency gains from the new tech stack.
