# Paisalo Digital Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy performance across AUM growth, profitability, and asset quality, supported by scaling distribution and technology.

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 72,659 million (up 33% YoY)
- **Disbursements:** Rs 15,673 million in Q2 (up 42% YoY); Rs 32,982 million in H1 FY27 (up 77% YoY)
- **Total Income:** Rs 2,773 million in Q2 (up 24% YoY); Rs 5,376 million in H1 FY27 (up 21% YoY)
- **Net Interest Income (NII):** Rs 1,476 million in Q2 (up 17% YoY); Rs 2,923 million in H1 FY27 (up 17% YoY)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 874 million in Q2 (up 27% YoY); Rs 1,696 million in H1 FY27 (up 28% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 656 million in Q2 (up 27% YoY); Rs 1,270 million in H1 FY27 (up 29% YoY)
- **Return on Assets (RoA):** 3.3% in Q2
- **Return on Equity (RoE):** 13.4% in Q2 (improved from 11.8% YoY)
- **Gross NPA:** 0.66% (improved by 15 bps YoY)
- **Net NPA:** 0.46% (improved by 19 bps YoY)
- **Collection Efficiency:** 98.3%
- **Credit Cost:** 0.4% in Q2 (down from 1.1% in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 loan losses and provisions down 47% YoY to Rs 155 million
- **Total Borrowings:** Rs 54,808 million as of Sept 30, 2026
- **Cost of Borrowing:** 10% (declined by ~50 bps YoY)
- **Debt-to-Equity:** 2.89x
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 30.7% (Tier 1 at 25.2%)
- **Touchpoints:** 6,415 across 23 states (426 branches, 4,299 distribution points, 1,690 Business Correspondent touchpoints)
- **Customer Franchise:** ~20 million
- **Capital Raised (H1 FY27):** ~Rs 294.9 crores (public NCDs), Rs 124.47 crores (unlisted/listed private NCDs), >Rs 177 crores (commercial papers)

### Highlights
- Distribution footprint expanded to 6,415 touchpoints across 23 states.
- BC network served ~1.93 million customers across 1,690 touchpoints.
- Employee base declined 11% YoY despite 33% AUM growth, reflecting operating leverage from technology and automation.
- Raised significant funds late in the quarter, creating a temporary denominator effect that impacted reported yields.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects cost of funds to finish the full year at 10.5% (currently at 10%).
- NIMs expected to be range-bound, closing at ~6% on an annualized basis.
- Long-term ROE target: 15% to 18% range; ROA target: 4% to 4.5%.
- Aiming for 40% to 50% of customer acquisitions to come directly from the consumer-facing app by the end of the 3-year strategy.

### Demand & industry commentary
- RBI revised FY27 real GDP growth estimate to 7.1%; recent 25 bps repo rate increase reflects a calibrated approach to inflation.
- Uneven monsoons and drought-affected pockets (Maharashtra, parts of Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana) impacted rural sentiment; company tightened underwriting and moderated rural disbursements in these districts.
- Bihar experienced heavy rainfall/flooding, but low exposure keeps the impact manageable.
- Healthy credit demand observed in mobility, machinery, and MSME/SME working capital across UP, Haryana, Rajasthan, and Delhi/NCR.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Finance cost growth outpaced revenue growth due to late deployment of capital raised toward the end of Q2, causing surplus treasury cash. Management expects this to normalize in Q3 and Q4.
- Yields expected to normalize in subsequent quarters as deployment scales up.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Consumer-facing app targeted to go live in Q3 (around Christmas/New Year), with metrics shared starting Q1 of next fiscal year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings at Rs 54,808 million.
- ALM position remains comfortable with a positive gap across buckets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Patchy monsoon and drought declarations in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana require monitoring and tightened guardrails.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sandy Mehta (Evaluate Research) on RBI rate hikes and impact on yields/NIMs:** Management stated a 25-30 bps increase is priced in, full-year cost of funds guidance is maintained at 10.5%, and NIMs are expected to close at ~6%.
2. **Sandy Mehta on head count reduction vs. operational efficiency:** Management reiterated that workforce reduction is a result of mature distribution networks and asset-light expansion, though headcount additions may normalize during the festive season.
3. **Manav (Antique Stock Broking) on yield moderation causes:** Management clarified it was a transient denominator effect caused by capital/debt raised late in Q2; underlying yields remain unchanged.
4. **Dhruvi Shah (Altermount Advisors) on AI impact on cost-to-income and efficiency:** Management explained that AI helps absorb operational load (e.g., 1.90 lakh applications processed, 3 lakh daily outbound calls via 25 bots), but short-term costs remain elevated due to maintaining a maker-checker framework alongside AI. Full operating leverage impact is expected over a 3-year cycle.
5. **Ramesh Raizada (Apical Capital) on finance cost growth exceeding revenue growth:** Management attributed this to late deployment of funds raised toward the end of Q1/Q2, which normalizes in Q3 and Q4.
6. **Sudha K (TNA Advisors) on app launch and digital acquisition:** Management stated the app launches at the end of Q3, with 40-50% of acquisitions targeted through it by the end of the 3-year strategy.

### One-line takeaway
Paisalo Digital delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 33% AUM growth and 27% PAT growth, driven by distribution scaling and early operating leverage from AI, while managing rural monsoon headwinds.
