# CreditAccess Grameen Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly profit and strong H1 performance, citing robust growth, improved asset quality, and successful execution of their retail finance strategy.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised profitability guidance (ROA: 5.2%–5.8% vs 4%–4.8%; ROE: 21%–24% vs 16%–20%) and NIM guidance (14.4%–14.7% vs 12.8%–13.2%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "no visible impact" (Q1) to acknowledging temporary stress in specific geographies (Maharashtra drought, UP/Bihar/MP floods) while maintaining confidence in overall performance.
- **New topics:** Drought-related stress in Maharashtra (6 districts) and the potential impact of insurance regulation reforms (estimated 25-30 bps impact on ROA).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the potential 50 bps price cut (implemented in Q3); continued to expand retail finance (now 23.3% of AUM vs 20.6% in Q1).

### Key numbers
- **PAT:** Rs 502 crore (highest quarterly profit).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 995 crore (vs Rs 778 crore in H1 FY26).
- **AUM:** Rs 30,946 crore (+19.5% YoY, +2.1% QoQ).
- **Disbursements:** Rs 6,355 crore (+19.4% YoY, +4.1% QoQ).
- **NIM:** 15.1% (vs 14.4% in Q1 FY27).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.79%; **Net NPA:** 0.59%; **PAR 90+:** 1.11%.
- **Credit Cost:** Rs 299 crore (0.99% non-annualized); H1 annualized credit cost: 3.43%.
- **ROA:** 5.9% (H1); **ROE:** 23.8% (H1).
- **Capital Adequacy:** 26.9% (Tier 1: 26.2%).
- **Cost-to-Income:** 28.2%.

### Highlights
- **Retail Finance:** AUM grew to Rs 7,200 crore (23.3% of total AUM). Secured book (Mortgage, 2-wheeler, Secured Business Loans) grew 60.9% YoY to Rs 695 crore.
- **Borrower Base:** Added 2.3 lakh new borrowers (35% new-to-credit); total base ~45 lakh.
- **Network:** Added 60 branches (Total: 2,326 across 460 districts).
- **Liability:** Signed USD 175 million ECB in October; mobilized Rs 940 crore domestic NCDs in H1.
- **Digital:** Digital collections rose to 26.3% (vs 18.1% a year ago).

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Growth:** Retained 20%–25% guidance.
- **Credit Cost:** Maintained 3%–4% guidance; expects Q3 credit cost to "inch up" slightly due to drought conditions.
- **NIM:** Expects 14.4%–14.7% range.
- **Cost-to-Income:** Expected range 29%–30%.
- **Pricing:** Management stated they have flexibility to stop planned future rate cuts if credit costs exceed expectations.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Industry:** Banks have significantly pulled back on microfinance; NBFCs and MFIs are gaining market share.
- **Drought/Floods:** Temporary stress in 6 districts of Central Maharashtra (drought) and floods in UP, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, and MP (September). Management expects flood impact to be temporary.
- **Competition:** No large player pulling back, but liquidity constraints for smaller NBFCs are impacting industry growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Borrowing Cost:** Marginal cost of borrowing 9.1%; expects blended borrowing cost to stay in 9.2%–9.3% range until March.
- **Yields:** Management noted yields have peaked in Q2; 50 bps rate cut will take 4–6 quarters to fully flow through.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Network:** Expanding mortgage presence (now 128 Retail Finance branches) and 2-wheeler finance (950+ Group Loan branches).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **ECL Provisioning:** Strengthened to 3.31% (includes 45 bps macroprudential buffer of Rs 137 crore).
- **Liability Mix:** 30% fixed, 40% MCLR-linked, 30% repo-linked.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Drought:** Emerging stress in Central Maharashtra; borrowers requesting restructuring.
- **Insurance Regulation:** Potential regulatory changes could impact ROA by 25–30 bps.
- **Credit Cost:** Potential for Q3/Q4 credit costs to inch up due to seasonal factors and drought.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Renish (ICICI) on lending rate cuts vs. rising borrowing costs:** Management explained that current NIMs (15.1%) are at peak levels, providing a buffer to absorb costs while passing on the 50 bps cut.
- **Renish (ICICI) on credit cost trajectory:** Management clarified that even if credit costs inch up in H2, they remain confident of staying within the 3%–4% annual guidance.
- **Shreya Shivani on drought operational changes:** Management is pausing new customer sourcing in affected Maharashtra districts and deploying additional collection resources.
- **Rajiv Mehta on promoter transaction:** Management confirmed promoters are in active discussions for a strategic investor at the HoldCo level, with progress expected in the next couple of quarters.
- **Piran Engineer on ROA/Credit Cost interplay:** Management stated they can protect 4.5%–5% ROA as long as credit costs remain below 5%.
- **Anand Dama on borrower growth:** Management attributed the slow borrower growth to heavy write-offs (3 lakh customers in 12 months) and expects acceleration in Q3/Q4.

### One-line takeaway
CreditAccess Grameen delivered record profitability and raised FY27 guidance, successfully navigating localized drought and flood stress while maintaining a strong balance sheet and competitive pricing.
