# Ugro Capital Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong execution on the strategic pivot, record disbursements, and scaling of core annuity-led businesses.

### Key numbers
- **Net Disbursements:** INR3,159 crores (up 77% YoY, 24% QoQ)
- **Assets Under Management (AUM):** INR15,019 crores (up 23% YoY)
- **Focused Businesses AUM:** INR7,929 crores (53% of total AUM)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** INR68.3 crores (up 12% YoY, 11% QoQ)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR50.7 crores (up 17% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT at INR118.6 crores (up 53% YoY)
- **Portfolio Yield:** 18.7% (up 108 bps YoY, 53 bps QoQ)
- **Quarterly Operating Cost:** INR127.2 crores (down 7% YoY)
- **Gross NPA:** 2.7%; Net NPA: 2.0%
- **Collection Efficiency:** 97%
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 18.8% standalone (~22% on a pro forma merged basis)

### Highlights
- Dispersed over INR1,000 crores in every single month of the quarter (INR1,057 crores in July, INR1,010 crores in August, and INR1,092 crores in September).
- Profectus Capital merger approved by shareholders and creditors on 22nd September; final NCLT hearing scheduled for 20th November.
- *Record continuation:* Call continues the pattern from earlier records regarding the successful execution of the February 2026 pivot, with focused businesses now reaching 53% of total AUM.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided annual growth of 20% to 30% for Emerging Market LAP and 35% to 40% for GROx.
- Management targeted a steady-state ROA of 3% to 3.5% by FY29.
- Management guided FY27 operating costs at INR490 crores and above.
- Branch productivity target set at INR80 lakhs to INR85 lakhs per month per branch over the next few months (Management).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains strong for small ticket loans against property (LAP) in tier 2 towns and beyond.
- Embedded merchant finance (GROx) demand driven by small retailers via partner apps like PhonePe, Paytm, and BharatPay.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of borrowings stood at 10.19% on total debt of INR10,973 crores.
- Credit cost rose to INR86.9 crores (3.6% of average on-book loans vs. 2.8% previous quarter) due to seasoning of newer Emerging Market and GROx portfolios.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Physical network stands at 317 branches across 13 states with 1,800+ sales executives and 500+ collection officers.
- Management stated branch build-out is complete and no new branches are needed to grow.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at INR10,973 crores with leverage at 3.6x.
- Company closed the quarter with liquidity of INR1,500 crores.
- Pro forma net worth post-merger estimated at INR2,454 crores (~INR158 per share).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Monitoring credit costs closely as newer Emerging Market and GROx portfolios season.
- Potential impact of recent bank rate hikes on floating-rate borrowings (estimated at 15 bps to 20 bps impact).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shailendra Soodh (Individual Investor) on provision coverage:** Management stated the 43%-45% PCR is adequate as GROx is short-term and unsecured with quick write-off mechanics, while Emerging Market LAP is secured by property.
- **Parth (Zen Wealth Management) on shareholder overhang:** Management noted key institutional shareholders (IFU, ADV) are on the board/insiders and not selling; the stock price drop was driven by the business model reset and market conditions.
- **Parth (Zen Wealth Management) on floating borrowings:** Management stated ~50% of borrowings are floating (linked to MCLR with 1-year resets), expecting a 15-20 bps impact from rate hikes.
- **Piyush Bothra (GMO PG) on revenue policy change:** Management clarified that shifting from contractual to behavioral tenure for co-lending/assignment income changes excess interest spread accounting via reserves, with no change to core interest income.

### One-line takeaway
UGRO Capital delivered record quarterly disbursements and stable profitability in Q2 FY27 as its strategic pivot toward annuity-led MSME lending scaled rapidly.
