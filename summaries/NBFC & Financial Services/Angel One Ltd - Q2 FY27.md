# Angel One Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong earnings growth (PAT +58.2% YoY) and margin expansion despite regulatory headwinds and market volume moderation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 45% standalone OPM guidance for the year.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "resilience during moderation" to "strong performance" as the platform held up and volumes rebounded in September.
- **New topics or risks:** SEBI circular on Closing Auction Session (CAS) viewed as a tailwind; U.S. stocks pilot launched; IRDAI consultation paper on insurance commissions.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on margin efficiency (standalone OPM 48.9% vs 40-45% guidance); continued investment in new businesses (burn remains 3.5-4%).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 14.3 billion (+18.9% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 3.35 billion (+58.2% YoY).
- **Standalone OPM:** 48.9%.
- **Consolidated OPM:** 45.1%.
- **Client Funding Book:** Rs 74.5 billion (average), Rs 82.1 billion (closing).
- **Ionic Wealth AUM:** Rs 160 billion (+19.1% QoQ).
- **AMC AUM:** Rs 6 billion.
- **Credit Distribution Run Rate:** Rs 33 billion (annualized).

### Highlights
- **Client Base:** 40 million total clients; 1.5 million added in Q2 (+11.6% QoQ).
- **Orders:** Average daily orders rebounded to 5.8 million in September (+12% vs August).
- **Market Share:** Equity retail market share (option premium turnover) at 19.7%; Derivatives retail turnover share at 22.1% (22.5% in September).
- **New Initiatives:** Received authorization as Global Access Provider in IFSC; U.S. stocks pilot launched.
- **Dividend:** Board approved second interim dividend of Rs 1.4 per share.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Margins:** Management aims for ~45% standalone OPM annually.
- **Costs:** Employee cost guidance maintained at ~Rs 1,100 crore for FY27 (flat YoY).
- **New Businesses:** Wealth and AMC businesses expected to reach breakeven in 3-4 years; currently causing 3.5-4% drag on EBITDA margins.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Regulatory changes impacted market volumes, but the ecosystem is adjusting.
- SEBI circular on Closing Auction Session (CAS) expected to provide tailwinds.
- Commodities market expanded; Angel One turnover doubled YoY, though market share moderated to 44% due to new participants.
- Credit distribution seeing highest-ever momentum.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated OPM expanded >1,000 bps YoY to 45.1%.
- Normalized consolidated OPM: 43.8% (vs 43.6% in Q1).
- Employee cost management: Reduced headcount/backfills; focus on increasing talent density and productivity.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Wealth:** Focusing on annuity-based assets; AUM Rs 160 billion.
- **AMC:** Evaluating comprehensive play; seeking new leadership to scale.
- **U.S. Stocks:** Pilot launched via GIFT City.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Worth:** Rs 67.1 billion.
- **Borrowings:** Rs 75.5 billion (driven by client funding portfolio).
- **Asset Quality:** 84% of funded clients have exposure <Rs 100,000; 82% of book <30 days old.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory environment (though management views recent changes as progressive).
- Market volume volatility.
- Competitive intensity in brokerage pricing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Margin Guidance:** Analysts asked if 45% standalone OPM includes IPL costs. Management confirmed yes.
- **Client Acquisition:** Analysts questioned slower acquisition vs industry. Management stated focus is on "active" clients and revenue generation rather than just KYC numbers.
- **Credit Business:** Analysts asked if volatility is structural. Management stated it's driven by lender risk appetite and is now seeing record momentum.
- **Wealth AUM:** Analysts questioned yield/revenue conversion. Management clarified AUM is built over time and yields are in line with industry standards.
- **Employee Costs:** Analysts asked about the drop in employee costs. Management attributed it to attrition and reduced backfills, focusing on productivity.

### One-line takeaway
Angel One delivered strong profitability with 58.2% YoY PAT growth and 48.9% standalone OPM, successfully navigating regulatory shifts while scaling credit and wealth businesses.
