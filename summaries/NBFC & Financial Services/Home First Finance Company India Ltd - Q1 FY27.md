# Home First Finance Company India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in AUM and disbursements, stable asset quality, and improved profitability metrics.

### Key numbers
*   **AUM:** Rs 16,938 crore (+25.7% YoY, +6.7% QoQ).
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 1,628 crore (+31.0% YoY, +3.6% QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 160 crore (+34.5% YoY, +7.0% QoQ).
*   **NII Growth:** 38.2% YoY.
*   **ROA:** 4.2% (+10 bps QoQ).
*   **ROE:** 14.5% (+50 bps QoQ).
*   **Gross Stage 3:** 1.8% (flat).
*   **NIM:** 6.0% (up from 5.9% in previous quarter).
*   **Cost of Borrowing:** 7.8% (-10 bps QoQ).

### Highlights
*   **Network:** 175 branches and 373 touch points (added 4 branches in Q1).
*   **Headcount:** 1,988 (net addition of 133 employees).
*   **Product Mix:** Individual housing loans account for 83% of the portfolio.
*   **Technology:** Operationalized 'Cue' (AI-orchestrated customer communication platform); deployed bureau analyzer and bank statement analysis models.
*   **CFO Transition:** Nutan Gaba Patwari to step down effective August 31, 2026; Board is evaluating candidates.
*   **Co-lending:** Book at Rs 617 crore (3.6% of AUM).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management remains confident of delivering 25% AUM growth.
*   **Opex:** Management expects Opex-to-assets ratio to remain range-bound between 2.6% and 2.7%.
*   **Spreads:** Management maintains long-term spread guidance of 5% to 5.25%.
*   **Expansion:** Management is targeting aggressive growth in UP (medium-term) and Southern states (Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Telangana).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains healthy across markets.
*   Ticket sizes are rising due to higher customer incomes and aspirations, not a change in customer segment.
*   Competitive intensity remains high, particularly regarding BT (Balance Transfer) outs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Origination yield:** 13.0%.
*   **Portfolio yield (ex-co-lending):** 13.1%.
*   **Incremental borrowing cost:** 7.6%.
*   **Cost-to-income ratio:** 32.7% (+70 bps QoQ), attributed to salary increments and fresh hiring.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Added 4 branches (Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Madhya Pradesh).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Capital Adequacy Ratio:** 42.6% (Tier 1: 42.2%).
*   **Net worth:** Rs 4,483 crore.
*   **Book Value per Share:** Rs 429.
*   **Funding mix:** 57% banks, 14% NHB, 21% assignment/co-lending, balance NCDs/ECB/NBFC.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **BT-out rate:** Management noted 4.5% was an "extraordinarily good" quarter and difficult to sustain; expects a 5% ballpark range.
*   **Asset Quality:** 1+ DPD (4.7%) and 30+ DPD (3.2%) remained flat; management noted Q1 is seasonally difficult.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Sustainability of 4.5% BT-out rate?* A: Management stated this was an exceptionally good quarter; expects to control it in the 5% range.
*   *Q: Reason for Opex increase?* A: Management attributed it to salary increments and fresh hiring (133 net additions).
*   *Q: Why is co-lending momentum weak?* A: Management cited process/policy changes causing temporary hiccups, which are being addressed.
*   *Q: Strategy for higher ticket size customers?* A: Management clarified they are targeting the same customer segment; ticket sizes are rising due to income growth, not a shift to prime/bank-eligible customers.
*   *Q: Tech differentiation?* A: Management pointed to Opex-to-AUM, delinquencies, and customer experience (NPS of 79) as key metrics.

### One-line takeaway
HFFCIL delivered strong Q1 FY27 growth with stable asset quality and improved profitability, while maintaining a focus on tech-led operational efficiency despite an upcoming CFO transition.
