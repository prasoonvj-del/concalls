# PB Fintech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong core business performance offset by regulatory uncertainties following IRDAI's consultation paper on lower distribution commission payouts and dark patterns.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained execution targets for PB Health (~₹500 Cr annual revenue run rate by March 2027); set a new long-term focus timeframe of "1,000 days" post final regulatory framework.
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone shifted from highly optimistic to cautious/prudent. Demand turned tough in Q2 FY27 (requiring higher marketing spend), whereas GST-led demand tailwinds from last year faded.
- **New topics or risks:** IRDAI consultation paper proposing lower distribution commission payouts, potential restructuring of POSP architecture, and regulatory scrutiny on dark patterns/data collection.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Hiring was dramatically slowed in October 2026; PB Health rollout remains on track to add properties without changes to the original plan.

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Operating Revenue:** ₹2,178 Cr (up 35% YoY).
* **Consolidated PAT:** ₹192 Cr (up 43% YoY); PAT Margin expanded to 9% (vs 8% YoY).
* **Total Insurance Premium:** ₹9,385 Cr (up 38% YoY).
* **Core Business Growth:** Core insurance revenue up 42% YoY; core credit revenue up 19% YoY.
* **Core Online New Health & Term:** Grew 42% YoY; core new health grew 46% YoY.
* **Renewal & Trail Revenue:** ~₹1,100 Cr on a rolling 12-month basis (up 42% YoY).
* **Core Credit Disbursals (Paisabazaar):** Grew 22% YoY.
* **New Initiatives (PB Partners, UAE, PB for Business):** Revenue of ₹826 Cr (up 40% YoY), close to break-even. PB Partners standalone revenue was ~₹700 Cr. UAE Premium grew 36% YoY.

### Highlights
* **Customer Retention & Claims Record:** Health claim settlement rate stands at 97% for Policybazaar vs 87% industry average; 11,156 rejected claims worth ₹185 Cr reopened/paid last year. 61st-month life policy persistency reached 71% (vs 51–66% benchmark). Health renewal rate stands at 89% (rising to 91% from 2nd year onwards).
* **Market Sourcing Penetration:** Policybazaar sources ~40% of India's retail new health customers, >25% of pure term premium, and 17.8% of total retail health premium. Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities account for 74% of term policies and 64% of health policies.
* **AI Integration:** Over the last 18 months, AI implementations improved advisor lead capacity/conversion by 15–20% and enhanced risk modeling, customer service, and training efficiency.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Premium Runway:** On track to cross ₹40,000 Cr in total insurance premium for FY27.
* **1,000-Day Strategy:** Management committed to maintaining customer proposition, growth focus, cost discipline, and strict regulatory alignment over the next 1,000 days post-final IRDAI regulations.
* **PB Health Target:** Reaffirmed target run rate of ₹40–45 Cr per month (~₹500 Cr annualized) near breakeven by March 2027.
* **Capital Allocation:** Management confirmed no current plans to expand international footprint beyond existing UAE operations or acquire equity stakes in insurance manufacturers.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Demand in Q2 FY27 was tough across the industry, contrasting with the high-demand base of last year following the 18% GST reduction benefit (which has now normalized).
* Industry growth in retail health insurance acquisition could face flat YoY momentum in Q4 FY27 due to base effects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Selling & Marketing Spend:** Increased QoQ and YoY due to weak organic market demand requiring higher spend to generate leads.
* **Cost Realignment:** Paused aggressive hiring in October 2026. Management plans to realign variable acquisition costs to match lower net present values (NPVs) if final commission structures are reduced.
* **POSP Costs:** Acknowledged the current POSP model incurs losses and could require structural repositioning (e.g., transition to a services framework) under proposed open vs. closed architecture payout caps.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **CapEx Surge:** CapEx expanded (~4x YoY) in recent quarters to support office network expansion and staff onboarding; future CapEx pace will align with restricted hiring.
* **PB Health Expansion:** Currently adding 3 to 5 managed hospital properties on asset-light, profit-generating/O&M structures. Third-party hospital software/O&M expansion expected in ~18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Net cash position provides operational runway and stability while regulatory parameters are finalized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **IRDAI Consultation Paper:** Draft proposals seeking lower distribution commissions will directly hit insurance distribution revenue and NPV per policy.
* **POSP Viability:** Proposed lower payouts for open architecture models create working capital and cash-flow timing risks (as POSPs are paid ahead of insurer receipts).
* **Dark Patterns & Data Collection:** Regulatory scrutiny on phone number/lead collection processes could impact customer conversion rates.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA):** *Why did selling and marketing expenses rise QoQ/YoY, and will discretionary costs be cut due to regulatory overhang?*
  * **Management:** Spend rose due to tough market demand relative to last year's strong base. Management will balance cost and growth prudently without cutting customer-facing propositions.
* **Nidhesh Jain (Investec):** *Are there levers to cut costs, and could the company return to cash burn?*
  * **Management:** AI drives 15–20% advisor productivity gains. Management will adjust variable costs to match lower policy NPVs, repurpose existing staff into non-insurance/ecosystem ventures, and does not intend to return to cash burn.
* **Jayant Kharote (Axis):** *Will Policybazaar consider taking minority stakes in insurance manufacturers or adopting combined operating ratio (COR) models?*
  * **Management:** Ruled out taking minority equity stakes. However, discussions center on COR-based compensation models (ensuring 30–40% return on equity for insurers while reflecting Policybazaar's lower claims ratios and underwriting support).
* **Dipanjan Ghosh (GS):** *What are the top non-insurance growth drivers being explored?*
  * **Management:** Accelerating ecosystem initiatives including PB Pay, PB Care+, tech/software support for international governments (e.g., Bahrain financial platform), and cross-selling Paisabazaar wealth products (mutual funds, bonds, FDs) to Policybazaar customers.

### One-line takeaway
PB Fintech delivered strong Q2 operating growth (+35% revenue, +43% PAT), but near-term earnings parameters remain subject to final regulatory commission structure adjustments.
