# PB Fintech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management addressed significant headwinds from the IRDAI consultation paper on commission structures while maintaining growth momentum and cost discipline.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management did not provide explicit quantitative guidance updates for the full year, shifting focus to a "1000-day strategy" and waiting for final IRDAI regulations. (Old: baseline fresh business growth >30% YoY, Paisabazaar EBITDA ~₹50 Cr).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from aggressively optimistic/growth-focused to more cautious due to a tough demand environment and regulatory uncertainty regarding commission payouts and distribution margins.
- New topics or risks: Intensive discussion around the IRDAI consultation paper, potential lower distribution payouts, dark patterns, and evaluating non-insurance revenue options. Old promises like aggressive expansion/hiring are being reined in with a sharp reduction in hiring (e.g., October hiring was a fraction of past levels).
- Last quarter's promises: PB Health remains on track for a ₹40-45 Cr/month run rate (~₹500 Cr annual) and breakeven by March 2027.

### Key numbers
- **Total Insurance Premium:** ₹9,385 Cr (up 38% YoY).
- **Consolidated Operating Revenue:** ₹2,178 Cr (up 35% YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT:** ₹192 Cr (up 43% YoY); PAT Margin improved from 8% to 9%.
- **Core Insurance Revenue:** Up 42% YoY.
- **Core Credit Revenue:** Up 19% YoY.
- **Core Online New Health and Term Growth:** Up 42% YoY (New health up 46% YoY).
- **Renewals & Trail Revenue:** Nearly ₹1,100 Cr on a rolling 12-month basis (up 42% YoY).
- **Credit Disbursals (Core Paisabazaar):** Grew 22% (up for 5 consecutive quarters).
- **Insurance-led New Initiatives (PB Partners, UAE Business, PB for Business) Revenue:** ₹826 Cr (up 40%).
- **UAE Premium Growth:** Up 36% YoY.
- **PB Partners Revenue:** ₹700 Cr for the quarter.

### Highlights
- Core new premium (excluding savings) has grown above 34% for 14 consecutive quarters.
- Management addressed the IRDAI consultation paper, supporting its goals of lower mis-selling, better claims experience, lower prices, and deeper penetration, noting they are engaging constructively with the regulator.
- PB Health remains on track to hit a run rate of ₹40-45 Cr per month by March (around ₹500 Cr for the year) with near breakeven.
- GenAI initiatives over the last 18 months are helping advisors handle 15-20% more leads and improving conversions and training speed.

### Guidance & outlook
- Set up well for over ₹40,000 Cr in total insurance premium for the year.
- Management set a "1000-day strategy" focused on customer service, cost discipline, and continued investment in underpenetrated markets.
- Evaluating non-insurance revenue expansion and accelerated ecosystem offerings depending on final regulatory permissions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Market demand was described as "tough" in the near term, requiring higher marketing spend compared to the robust demand period last year.
- Health and term insurance remain deeply under-penetrated in India.
- Industry health claims settlement is at 87% vs Policybazaar's 97%; Policybazaar processed 1.25 lakh health claims and 80,000 motor claims in H1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Selling, marketing, and other expenses increased QoQ and YoY due to tougher market demand requiring higher spend to secure leads.
- Management committed to stricter cost discipline and balancing growth with cost, aligning variable costs with new Net Present Values (NPVs) once final regulations emerge.
- October hiring dropped to a very small fraction of past levels as a shift in hiring has already begun.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CapEx grew substantially (noted as ~4x YoY) driven by building capacity, expanding offices, and supporting staff.
- PB Health is on track executing new hospitals; intends to maintain its current stake without expanding or reducing it.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash position is viewed as a position of capability to assist through regulatory transitions.
- POSP business faces cash flow concerns as Policybazaar largely pays POSPs sooner than getting paid by insurance companies.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- IRDAI consultation paper proposing lower distribution payouts, which will reduce insurance distribution revenue.
- Open architecture POSP business under question due to proposed lower payouts for open vs. closed architecture.
- Tough near-term demand environment and potential short-term impact on visible growth numbers from past GST price elasticity wearing off.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA):** Asked about the spike in marketing expenses and cost control amidst regulatory overhang. *Management stated demand was tougher this year forcing more spend, and while growth remains critical, they will be more conscious of the balance between growth and cost.*
- **Nidhesh Jain (Investec):** Asked about cost levers and whether the company could return to a cash burn. *Management stated they are waiting for final regulations to assess NPV impact, noted GenAI as a major efficiency lever, and clarified the intent is not cash burn.*
- **Jayant Kharote (Axis):** Asked about the viability of the POSP business and taking minority stakes in insurers. *Management stated open architecture POSP lacks scope under current drafts, ruled out minority stakes in insurers, and emphasized their focus on responsible distribution and Combined Operating Ratio (COR).*
- **Shobhit Sharma (Elara):** Asked about Paisabazaar disbursal trends and marketing campaigns. *Management explained Paisabazaar was not aggressive on marketing in Q2 as they onboarded new suppliers/banks to strengthen distribution ahead of Q3.*
- **Dipanjan Ghosh (Goldman Sachs):** Asked about line-item cost rationalization (ESOPs, brand spend, PB Pay) and non-insurance product expansion. *Management declined to specify brand cost cuts, noted PB Pay is scaling as a payment solution for insurance, and stated non-insurance product expansion depends on final regulations.*
- **Shreya Shivani (Nomura):** Asked about October trends and lending partner tightening on Paisabazaar. *Management noted no change in lending partner behavior from August to October and highlighted that industry health insurance growth is normalizing post the historical GST price-cut bump.*

### One-line takeaway
PB Fintech delivered strong Q2 FY27 growth (revenue up 35% YoY, PAT up 43% YoY) but struck a cautious note as it prepares to navigate potential regulatory headwinds from proposed IRDAI commission revisions through cost discipline and ecosystem expansion.
