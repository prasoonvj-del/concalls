# Network People Services Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY 2027
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong YoY growth, successful international expansion, and scaling of new verticals like RegTech and AI-based risk intelligence despite ongoing investments.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 61.42 crore (up 75% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Growth of 66% YoY
- **PAT:** Rs 11.4 crore (up by Rs 53 crore [note: as stated in transcript, likely referring to absolute or scale vs prior periods])
- **International Revenue Contribution:** 10% to 12% of consolidated numbers

### Highlights
- Delivered strong YoY revenue growth of 75% and EBITDA growth of 66% in Q1.
- Brought down contribution from Payment Platform as a Service (PPaaS) to about 5% to derisk the organization from regulatory changes.
- Secured a global account in international business during Q1, validating the scalability of the bank-in-a-box model.
- Received an order from a large PSU in the RegTech segment and won a digital transformation and payment order from a large telecom provider for its super app.
- Formed a separate vertical for AI-based risk intelligence/RegTech, which processed approx 650 million transactions with 98% accuracy.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 60% to 70%.
- Management reiterated full-year FY27 EBITDA margin guidance of around 30%.
- Management targets international business to reach around 30% of revenue in the next 2 years (FY28-FY29) and potentially ~50% or more in 2-3 years.
- Management targets long-term EBITDA margins to improve towards 35% or more in the next 2 years.
- Management targets a CAGR of 60% to 70% for the next 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Anticipates positive revenue impact from the potential implementation of MDR on UPI once official guidelines are released by regulators, banks, and NPCI.
- High-ticket UPI transactions (impacting large merchants, representing top 80-90 acquiring banks/merchants) are expected to drive MDR revenue.
- Growing demand for regulatory technology, digital payments, and AI-led compliance solutions across banking and telecom sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- International business commands higher margins of 35% to 40% compared to 15% to 20% in India.
- RegTech and AI-based products carry higher margins.
- Employee costs did not increase proportionally with revenue due to internal efficiency measures and AI adoption.
- "Other expenses" included a grouping adjustment where purchase costs for turnkey project hardware (amounting to approx Rs 17 crore) were merged.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- International market expansion is a key strategic pillar, with implementation cycles ranging from 4 to 9 months.
- IPO funds deployment: Management has zeroed in on 3 opportunities across RegTech, AI solutions, and payment infrastructure, with deployment expected to start in the next 2 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or working capital figures discussed beyond IPO fund deployment plans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Dependence on regulatory guidelines and clarity from NPCI/RBI regarding MDR on UPI.
- Implementation cycles for international and domestic orders range from 4 to 9 months, causing quarter-on-quarter lumpy revenue recognition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Akshay (AK Investments) asked about the sequential revenue dip and guidance vs Q4:** Management clarified that quarterly sequential comparisons are misleading due to the reduction in PPaaS contribution (tapered to 5%) and milestone-based execution; they reaffirmed the 60%-70% annual growth guidance.
2. **Nishant Joshi (Equisense Advisors) asked about international margin differentials and contribution:** Management stated international contribution is currently 10%-12% and targets ~30% in 2 years, with gross margins of 35%+.
3. **Ankit Kanodia (Zen Nivesh) asked about the direct vs indirect benefits of UPI MDR:** Management explained that direct benefits come from acquiring platform revenue shares with banks, while indirect benefits arise from increased technology spending by banks.
4. **Hardik Gandhi (HPMG) asked about the timeline for IPO fund deployment:** Management stated that funds will start deploying in products and market expansion over the next two quarters.
5. **Ashish Soni (Family Office) asked about the spike in other expenses and employee cost trends:** Management clarified that the INR 17 crore increase in other expenses was a grouping/merger of hardware purchase costs for turnkey projects, and employee costs lagged revenue growth due to internal AI-driven efficiencies.

### One-line takeaway
NPST delivered a 75% YoY revenue jump in Q1 FY27 while pivoting away from pure PPaaS toward higher-margin RegTech and international SaaS models, keeping its full-year 60%-70% growth and 30% EBITDA margin guidance intact.
