# CreditAccess Grameen Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-29 · NBFC & Financial Services*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the strongest Q1 in company history, citing robust portfolio growth, normalized asset quality, and strong profitability.

### Key numbers
*   **AUM:** Rs 30,319 crore (+16.4% YoY, +2.5% QoQ).
*   **Disbursements:** Rs 6,107 crore (+11.9% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 493 crore (+720% YoY).
*   **NIM:** 14.4%.
*   **PPOP:** Rs 873 crore (+33.6% YoY).
*   **ROA:** 5.9%; **ROE:** 24.4%.
*   **Asset Quality:** Gross NPA 2.18%; Net NPA 0.76%; PAR 90 1.46%.
*   **Credit Cost:** Rs 212 crore (0.72% non-annualized).
*   **Liquidity:** Cash and equivalents of Rs 3,536 crore (10.4% of total assets).
*   **Capital Adequacy:** CRAR 24.9%.

### Highlights
*   **Borrower Growth:** Added 2.5 lakh new borrowers (35% new-to-credit); expects to add ~1 lakh borrowers per month.
*   **Retail Finance:** Constitutes 20.6% of AUM (up 250 bps QoQ).
*   **Digital Adoption:** Digital collections reached 24.2% of total collections; Grameen Mahi app has 15.4 lakh active users (34.5% of borrower base).
*   **Network:** Opened 42 new branches (Total: 2,276); employee base grew 3% YoY to 21,981.
*   **Attrition:** Moderated to 20.6% (vs 25.8% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Funding:** Completed private NCD issuance of Rs 425 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **AUM Target:** Reaffirmed medium-term guidance of Rs 50,000 crore AUM by calendar year 2028.
*   **Pricing:** Management indicated a potential 50 bps price cut in H2 FY27 if asset quality remains stable, following the board-approved pricing policy.
*   **Review:** Management will monitor credit cost trends for one more quarter before revisiting FY27 guidance.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro Environment:** Management stated there is currently no visible impact from the West Asia crisis or El Nino on business or rural cash flows.
*   **Up-cycle:** Management believes the current microfinance up-cycle is structurally strong, supported by MFIN guardrails and internal Business Rule Engine (BRE) controls.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Borrowing Cost:** Currently 9.2%; management expects it to end the year around 9.3%–9.4%.
*   **Cost-to-Income:** 29.3%.
*   **Yields:** Management expects yields to settle at current levels, noting that any future pricing benefits passed to customers will be linked to credit cost trends.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Network:** Total network stands at 2,276 branches across 457 districts.
*   **Strategy:** Expansion is budgeted and planned; no additional capital expenditure required for growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity Buffer:** Includes Rs 3,536 crore cash, Rs 2,993 crore undrawn funding lines, and Rs 9,440 crore funding pipeline.
*   **Liability Mix:** Foreign borrowings account for 24% of the mix.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Volatility:** Potential impact of the West Asia crisis on fuel/macro conditions and El Nino on rural cash flows.
*   **Credit Cost:** Management identified credit cost behavior as the key determinant for future profitability and pricing decisions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Renish (ICICI) on guidance risks:** Asked if El Nino/War warrants guidance changes. Management stated they see no negative indicators on the ground and will wait one quarter before re-evaluating.
*   **Renish (ICICI) on retail finance profitability:** Asked about the drag of new products. Management clarified that all retail products are profitable (except 2-wheeler, which is a small book); mortgage needs ~Rs 1,000 crore AUM to reach full breakeven.
*   **Rajiv Mehta (YES Securities) on pricing cuts:** Asked about the disconnect between Q1 ROE and full-year guidance. Management noted they may implement a 50 bps price cut in Q3 and another 50 bps in Q4 if credit costs remain low.
*   **Nidhesh (Investec) on ROA capping:** Asked if management is capping ROA. Management clarified they are not capping ROA but will pass benefits to customers if high performance sustains.
*   **Abhishek (HSBC) on overlay provisions:** Asked about using excess profitability for overlays. Management noted they already have a Rs 41 crore overlay for the West Asia crisis and maintain the highest Stage 1 provisioning in the industry.

### One-line takeaway
CreditAccess Grameen delivered a record-strong Q1 with 24.4% ROE, and management remains confident in their growth trajectory while signaling potential pricing cuts in H2 FY27 if credit costs remain benign.
