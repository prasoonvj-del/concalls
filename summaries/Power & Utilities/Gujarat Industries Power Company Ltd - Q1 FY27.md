# Gujarat Industries Power Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-15 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q4 and FY25-26
**Tone:** Positive - Strong thermal PLFs, scaling up of Khavda solar project, and committed regular investor interactions.

### Key numbers
- SLPP 1 PLF: crossed 75%
- SLPP 2 PLF: crossed 80%
- Khavda 600 MW solar CUF: 33.24% (expected 30%+ annually)
- Khavda 600 MW expected annual revenue: Rs 420 crore
- Khavda 600 MW expected EBITDA: Rs 350 to 360 crore
- FY26 Solar division EBITDA: Rs 332 crore; PBT: Rs 62 crore
- Total borrowings (non-current liabilities) as of 31st March 2026: Rs 3,250 crore (or Rs 3,247 crore)
- Average coupon rate on borrowings: 7.8% (floating)
- Capital work in progress: Rs 2,065 crore
- Expected peak debt: Rs 6,000 to 6,500 crore (including thermal expansion)

### Highlights
- Thermal stations (SLPP 1 & 2) operated above threshold limits (75% and 80% PLF) and are top in merit order with heavy demand in South Gujarat.
- 600 MW Khavda solar project fully allocated in November 2025; operating smoothly with 33.24% CUF.
- Remaining 500 MW at Khavda at an advanced stage of commissioning; expected to be operational in phases post-monsoon / by end of FY27.
- Total allotted Khavda park capacity is 2,375 MW (GIPCL developing 1,100 MW on its own; rest given to third parties like Coal India, SJVN, SAEL). GIPCL expects net O&M park revenue of Rs 20 to 25 crore annually for 25 years.
- In-principle approval received from GUVNL for a 750 MW (3 x 250 MW) lignite-based thermal expansion under Section 62 cost-plus basis; target commissioning by FY32/FY33.
- Baroda gas station land/infrastructure to be utilized for Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS); GUVNL approved a 20/120 MW BESS first phase, with a second phase of 30/160 MW planned.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects blended EBITDA to reach Rs 940 to 950 crore once the 500 MW Khavda project runs for a full operational year (FY28).
- FY27 interest cost estimated around Rs 250 to 260 crore; depreciation around Rs 425 to 450 crore. Post 500 MW full commissioning (FY28), interest projected at Rs 400 to 450 crore and depreciation at Rs 550 to 600 crore.
- Thermal capex (750 MW) estimated around Rs 6,000 crore spread over 4 to 5 years (2027 to 2032/2033).
- Management confirmed willingness to hold earnings conference calls every quarter going forward.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Heavy power demand in South Gujarat with no evacuation issues for thermal assets.
- Khavda region faced temporary evacuation delays due to KPS 2 readiness, but GIPCL's 600 MW is fully evacuating power.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Available lignite in the region is sufficient to cater to the 750 MW thermal expansion plus 500 MW for the entire PPA life period.
- Tax is under MAT; no tax expense at division levels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 500 MW Khavda solar expansion underway; expected to commission in phases ending around late 2026.
- Additional 200 MW planned at Khavda using optimized land pockets (capex estimated at Rs 800 to 1,000 crore).
- Baroda BESS pilot capex estimated at Rs 250 to 300 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Thermal stations and existing RE assets (262 MW, 112 MW solar, wind) are more or less debt-free.
- Khavda 600 MW debt repayment starts next quarter with a 1-year moratorium ending June 2026. Repayment is structured as a step-up over time.
- Funding for the Rs 6,000 crore thermal capex likely planned at an 80:20 debt-equity ratio (Rs 4,800 crore debt, Rs 1,200 crore equity via internal accruals, promoter infusion, or equity dilution).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gas-based stations remain stranded (geopolitical situation and high gas prices make revival unlikely; management considering hiving them off in the next couple of years).
- Initial teething problems and lower initial profitability expected during the phased commissioning of the 500 MW solar project.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prit Nagersheth (Wealth Finvisor) asked about Khavda PLF/CUF and revenue sizing:** Management stated 33.24% CUF and approx. Rs 420 crore revenue for 600 MW.
- **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company) asked about debt trajectory post-expansion:** Management clarified that closing debt would reach around Rs 4,500 crore upon 500 MW commissioning, and peak near Rs 6,000 to 6,500 crore including thermal expansion.
- **Falguni Dutta (Mansarovar Financials) asked about other income breakup:** Management cited Rs 17 crore income tax refund, insurance claims, and government grant amortizations.
- **Vipul Shah (Subhmangal Investments) asked about park developer net earnings:** Management guided for net revenue of Rs 20 to 25 crore per annum from park O&M services over 25 years.
- **Ramakrishnan (Equity Intelligence) questioned PBT coverage against rising interest and depreciation:** Management explained that FY27 will remain stable, while FY28 PBT will normalize once the 500 MW plant fully operates for the full year.

### One-line takeaway
GIPCL is transitioning into a major renewable player with its 1,100 MW Khavda solar park scaling up, supported by steady thermal cash flows, while planning a 750 MW lignite thermal expansion and BESS initiatives.
