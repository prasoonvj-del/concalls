# Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While domestic O&M and international order wins grew, overall revenues declined YoY due to delays in international project transitions and domestic PPA/NTP issues.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,527 crore in Q2 FY27; Rs 3,118 crore in H1 FY27 (down ~11% YoY).
- **Domestic EPC Revenue:** Rose ~13% YoY to Rs 2,768 crore in H1 FY27 (Rs 1,366 crore in Q2).
- **International EPC Revenue:** Rs 62 crore in Q2 FY27; Rs 165 crore in H1 FY27 (compared to Rs 929 crore in H1 FY26).
- **O&M Revenue:** Rs 99 crore in Q2 FY27 (up ~55% YoY); Rs 183 crore in H1 FY27 (up 48% YoY). Expects full-year O&M revenue >Rs 400 crore.
- **Gross Profit & Margin:** Rs 140 crore in Q2 FY27; gross margin improved to 9.2% (vs 8.9% in Q2 FY26) and 9.5% in H1.
- **Operational EBITDA:** Rs 56 crore in Q2 FY27 (3.7% margin) vs Rs 62 crore (3.5%) in Q2 FY26. H1 operational EBITDA was Rs 134 crore.
- **PBT / PAT:** Rs 17 crore in Q2 FY27. H1 PAT stood at Rs 70 crore (vs loss of Rs 439 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Order Book:** Rs 12,481 crore as of Sept 30, 2026 (up 34% YoY and from Rs 11,813 crore in March 2026). 56% India (Rs 6,943 crore), 44% International (Rs 5,538 crore).
- **Order Wins:** Rs 3,626 crore in H1 FY27 (nearly 90% international). Q2 wins over Rs 985 crore (including taxes) across Rajasthan (534 MW BOS) and South Africa (616 MW BESS).
- **O&M Portfolio:** Reached 18.9 gigawatts (up from 13.5 gigawatts in March). Expects 22-25 gigawatts by year-end.
- **Working Capital:** Net working capital at negative Rs 301 crore as of Sept 30, 2026. Receivables days 176; payable days 164.
- **Debt:** Gross borrowings reduced by Rs 244 crore in H1 to Rs 920 crore. Cash and bank balances at Rs 319 crore. Net debt at Rs 601 crore.
- **Credit Lines:** Secured over INR 3,000 crores of new credit lines in the current year (INR 4,600 crores over last year and current year combined), plus INR 700+ crores from surety bonds. Utilization at ~70%.

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 was a transition period as older international projects completed and new ones awaited LOA/NTP.
- 8 projects (4 in India, 4 overseas) are in the order book yet to start execution, expected to commence in H2.
- O&M business expanded strongly to 18.9 gigawatts, providing steady recurring income.
- Expects to receive ~Rs 156 crore under indemnity from promoters in the current year.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Management maintained guidance of up to 10% revenue growth for FY27, expecting Q3 to be better and Q4 to be heavily loaded.
- **Margins:** Expects EPC gross margins of 8% to 10% and O&M gross margins around 20% (with minimal overheads, converting closely to EBITDA).
- **Order Book:** Expects order book to remain above Rs 10,000 crore at the end of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's total renewable and hydro capacity reached ~308 GW (~53% of generating capacity) as of Sept 2026; solar capacity at ~171 GW. FY26/Calendar 2026 solar additions expected around 50 GW DC.
- Near-term awards slowed due to three factors: shift to domestic cells via ALMM-II (raising costs 15-17%), delays in PPAs/transmission constraints (40-50 GW awaiting PPAs, curtailment in Rajasthan/Gujarat), and aggressive bidding by new players.
- Government initiatives: Green Energy Corridor Phase-III approved on Sept 30, 2026 (outlay of ~Rs 1.86 lakh crore); Oct 6, 2026 Ministry of Power rules for market-rate land payments for transmission lines.
- BESS: Rapid growth; 8.2 GWh added in H1 2026. CRISIL expects 45-50 GWh commissioned in FY27-FY28. Battery prices have risen in 2026.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Recurring overheads remained flat at Rs 79–84 crore per quarter (Rs 84 crore in Q2). Expected to settle around 4.5% of revenue for the full year.
- Domestic margins face pressure due to low entry barriers and aggressive bidding by new entrants; management avoided low-margin bids.
- International margins are higher and more stable, yielding greater absolute profits on turnkey projects.
- Finance costs increased due to new credit lines and longer LC periods.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Work with promoter Reliance Group on their integrated renewable energy hub in Kutch, Gujarat is in final planning/technological design stages post-monsoon readiness.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross borrowings down by Rs 244 crore in H1 to Rs 920 crore. Net debt stable at Rs 601 crore.
- Shareholders' equity rose to Rs 719 crore from Rs 651 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in NTPs and PPA signings for PSU and domestic projects pushing execution timelines.
- Aggressive, unsustainable bidding by non-serious/new competitors in domestic reverse auctions.
- Battery price increases and tight domestic cell manufacturing capacity (only ~35 GW cell capacity vs 200 GW module capacity).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sameer Dalal (Investor) asked about the reason for execution delays and why new projects haven't started.** Management explained that Coal India and NTPC projects faced delays in PPA signings and NTP issuance, while international projects faced financing delays; however, execution will ramp up in H3/H4.
2. **Disha (Sapphire Capital) asked about execution visibility for the 10% growth target.** Management stated Q3 will improve and Q4 will be heavily loaded, driven by international projects like West Minya in Egypt and execution of the 8 pending projects.
3. **Faisal Hawa (H.G. Hawa & Co.) asked about banking/BG limits and Reliance/Adani order pipelines.** Management replied they added Rs 4,600 crore in credit lines over the last two years with 70% utilization, received an RFQ for the next 1.2 GW under the Adani framework, and expect Reliance orders soon.
4. **Jayesh Shroff (Cask Capital) questioned whether "up to 10%" was a downgrade from the previous 10-15% range and asked about rising finance costs.** Management clarified it is around 10%, not zero, and explained that finance costs rose due to new credit lines and longer LC tenures.
5. **Nilesh Doshi (Prospero Tree AMC) asked about O&M contract structures and labor intensity.** Management clarified that O&M is ~50% tied to own EPC and ~50% third-party, operates on a per-MW peak fee model, is labor-intensive, and carries 20% gross margins with minimal overheads.

### One-line takeaway
Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy reported a subdued H1 FY27 due to a transition in international projects and domestic PPA delays, but management maintains a 10% revenue growth guidance for FY27 backed by a strong Rs 12,481 crore order book and second-half execution pickup.
