# Tata Power Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported highest-ever quarterly PAT driven by operational growth across business clusters and strong domestic power demand.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 RE capacity addition target of 2.5 GW and full-year capex target of Rs 25,000 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive; power demand growth accelerated to 10%–11% YoY in Q2 (and ~12% in October) due to delayed/weak monsoons, heatwaves, and higher cooling/agricultural consumption.
- **New topics or risks:** Appeal rejected in Singapore International Commercial Court regarding the Kleros arbitration (case moved to Supreme Court of Singapore); potential Chinese technology export controls being monitored for ingot/wafer expansion; higher RE curtailment at 11% in Q2 vs 8.8% in Q1.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Board approval for the 1,800 MW Shirwata PSP project was delivered in Q2 FY27 with a gross capex of Rs 11,871 crore.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 19,886 crore, up 26% YoY (vs Rs 15,769 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Up 5% YoY.
- **PAT:** Rs 1,652 crore, up 33% YoY (vs Rs 1,245 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 38,784 crore (up 17% YoY).
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 8,494 crore (up 7% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 3,053 crore (up 22% YoY).
- **H1 Capex Spent:** Rs 10,000 crore (Rs 6,000 crore spent in Renewables).

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly PAT of Rs 1,652 crore.
- Signed SPPA for Mundra plant with Gujarat, which is currently reserved for CERC order; billing across all 5 DISCOMs is happening under SPPA. Advanced discussions ongoing with two other states to conclude before December 2026.
- Approved 1,800 MW Shirwata pumped hydro storage project (PSP) with an investment of Rs 11,871 crore (75% debt / 25% equity).
- Coal JV profitability produced ~Rs 400 crore profit in Q2, which management expects to sustain for the next two quarters.
- Took a remaining non-cash impairment of Rs 96 crore for the Georgia asset.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 performance to exceed H1 as monsoon-related project delays subside and new capacities commission.
- Target to add 2.5 GW of renewable capacity in FY27 remains intact despite missing Q1/Q2 targets due to evacuation line delays.
- Management expects RE Genco annual run-rate EBITDA to exceed Rs 4,500 crore as newly added plants operate for full periods.
- Subsidy for rooftop solar is expected to continue past March in a revised structure.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Weak monsoon and heatwaves drove national power demand up 10%–11% in Q2, extending to ~12% YoY growth in October 2026.
- Inadequate rainfall affected hydro plant reservoirs nationwide, which management notes will force thermal and other generation plants to operate at full capacity during winter months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industry-wide grid transmission constraints pushed RE curtailment to 11% in Q2 (up from 8.8% in Q1 FY27). Management expects curtailment to decline as connectivity lines and BESS assets commission.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **FY27 Capex:** On track to execute full-year target of Rs 25,000 crore.
- **Pumped Hydro Storage:** Bhivpuri PSP (1,000 MW) expected to commission in H1 CY2029; Shirwata PSP (1,800 MW) targeted for CY2031.
- **Ingot & Wafer Plant:** All approvals secured; construction to start in November 2026 with target operational date of March 2028. Orders for equipment examine potential Chinese technology export controls.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Distribution regulatory assets reached Rs 83.1 billion (Rs 8,310 crore) across Delhi and Odisha due to delayed tariff/true-up orders owing to lack of regulatory quorums. Amortization is expected to accelerate in FY28 per Supreme Court timeline guidelines (by 2031 for Delhi).
- Cash balance on books stood at Rs 11,165 crore (including ~Rs 1,400 crore in the RE business).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High curtailment levels (11% in Q2) due to regional transmission and evacuation line delays.
- Kleros Singapore legal dispute: Singapore International Commercial Court rejected the appeal; case is now listed in the Supreme Court of Singapore for January 19. Opposing party is attempting to attach shares of two Singapore subsidiaries (pledged with consortium lenders).
- Potential impact of proposed Chinese technology export controls on solar ingot/wafer equipment sourcing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** *Asked about the 28% YoY increase in distribution regulatory assets to Rs 83.1 billion.*  
  - **Management:** Attributed to the lack of full regulatory quorums in Delhi and Odisha. Quorums are restored, true-up orders are expected, and amortization will pick up pace starting FY28 (guided by Supreme Court's 6-year amortization timeline till 2031 for Delhi).
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** *Asked about high RE curtailment (11% in Q2 vs 8.8% in Q1).*  
  - **Management:** Confirmed curtailment was 11% due to transmission constraints, but expects it to ease in coming quarters as evacuation connectivity completes and BESS installations commission.
- **Satyadeep Jain (Ambit):** *Asked why capex for Shirwata PSP jumped to Rs 11,871 crore from earlier Rs 8,000 crore estimates.*  
  - **Management:** Stated there was no cost jump; earlier market numbers may have been net of incentives. Tata Power reports capex on a gross, conservative basis.
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** *Asked about plans to convert solar cell/module lines from mono PERC to TOPCon and ingot/wafer status.*  
  - **Management:** Work on ingot/wafer plant starts in November 2026 for March 2028 operationalization. Retaining mono PERC for cell/module as product demand remains strong, though a small TOPCon line is operational.
- **Subhadip Mitra (Nuvama):** *Asked about potential risks in the intervening period before the January Supreme Court hearing for the Kleros case.*  
  - **Management:** Shares are pledged to lender consortiums who will legally block attachment notices; expects total legal resolution within 3–6 months without provisioning as they maintain a strong case.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Power delivered record quarterly profits driven by robust demand and power generation, while advancing its Rs 25,000 crore annual capex plan across utility renewables, distribution, and pumped hydro storage.
