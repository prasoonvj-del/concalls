# NTPC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational and financial performance driven by robust power demand and efficiency gains, despite temporary transmission constraints in renewables.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 45,752 crore, up 17% YoY (from Rs 39,167 crore).
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 5,320 crore, up 14% YoY (from Rs 4,653 crore).
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 89,584 crore, up 10% YoY.
- **Standalone PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 10,662 crore, up 13% YoY; Adjusted PAT at Rs 10,469 crore (up 17% YoY).
- **Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 105,857 crore, up 14% YoY.
- **Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 14,247 crore, up 26% YoY.
- **NGEL H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 2,045 crore (up 58% YoY); Operating EBITDA at Rs 1,818 crore (up 60% YoY).
- **Group Capex (H1 FY27):** Rs 23,932 crore; Standalone Capex: Rs 14,398 crore.

### Highlights
- NTPC Group installed capacity reached 91,506 MW as of H1 FY27, with 2,398 MW added in H1 FY27 (NTPC: 196 MW, NGEL: 1,136 MW, other group companies: 1,066 MW) plus 162 MW in October 2026.
- NTPC Group generation stood at 236 billion units in H1 FY27, up 10.4% YoY. NTPC coal stations achieved a PLF of 75.11% vs Rest of India average of 68.35%.
- Board recommended a first interim dividend of Rs 3 per share for FY26-27.
- Weighted average interest rate on borrowings improved to 5.72% in H1 FY27 compared to 5.98% in FY26.
- Awarded Lara Stage 3 (1,600 MW) in Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised NTPC Group renewable capacity addition guidance for FY27 downwards to ~8 gigawatts (from 9.5 gigawatts) due to transmission constraints.
- Management expects transmission constraints affecting NGEL to be surmounted by next year mid-July.
- FY28 group capacity addition target is ~9.6 gigawatts (Thermal: 1,460 MW, Hydro: 444 MW, RE: 7,735 MW).
- FY29 group capacity addition target is 10,877 MW (Thermal: 3,070 MW, RE: 7,807 MW).
- Management plans to tender ~4.8 gigawatts of additional thermal capacity across Q1 and Q2 of next fiscal.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India recorded YoY growth of ~11% in power generation and ~15% in power demand in H1 FY27; October growth reached 21%.
- Peak demand grew ~14.5% and non-peak demand ~9.5% on average monthly data for H1. Maximum demand met reached 271 GW (peak) and 253 GW (non-peak).
- Growth driven by digital services, urban transport, industries, changing weather patterns, and data centers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- NGEL EBITDA margin sustained at 89% in H1 FY27.
- Disincentives/fixed cost under-recoveries reduced by half to Rs 332 crore compared to Rs 625 crore YoY.
- Group captive coal dispatches grew 21% YoY to 26.13 MMT in H1 FY27. Current station coal stock stands at 5.2 MMT (7 days requirement). Tenders issued to procure 13 MMT of coal for H2 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capacity under construction exceeds 37 gigawatts, including ~17.32 gigawatts of thermal, ~3.6 gigawatts of hydro, and ~16 gigawatts of renewables.
- NGEL is planning to implement 40 gigawatt-hours of BESS via direct international competitive bidding (ICB) following relaxation of procurement rules.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross property, plant, and equipment at the group level increased 11% YoY to Rs 4,85,444 crore as of 30 September 2026.
- Consolidated regulated equity stood at Rs 1,21,786 crore; standalone regulated equity stood at Rs 92,926 crore (reduction due to transfer of coal mining business to NTPC Mining Limited).
- Executed a USD 750 million loan agreement with a consortium from IFSC Gift City and availed RBI concessional USD-INR swap facility for USD 450 million drawn.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Transmission constraints (particularly in Gujarat and Rajasthan/Khavda region) causing renewable power curtailment and delaying commercial operations date (COD).
- Coal supply constraints from CIL and Singareni leading to critical coal stock levels at several stations nationally.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) asked about thermal pipeline additions and RE capacity target revisions.** Management stated they plan to tender ~4.8 GW of thermal capacity next fiscal and revised FY27 RE capacity addition guidance to ~8 GW due to transmission delays.
2. **Mohit Pandey (ICICI Securities) asked about the reasons for the drop in standalone regulated equity and other income.** Management explained regulated equity dropped due to the transfer of coal mines to a subsidiary, while other income fell by Rs 432 crore due to lower dividend receipts from JVs/subsidiaries and a 28% drop in late payment surcharges (LPS) from better collections.
3. **Apoorva Bahadur asked for details on C&I opportunities and BESS timelines.** Management stated an EOI for C&I power and heat closes in November, and utility-scale BESS deployments take 12 to 18 months.
4. **Atul Tiwari asked about Ratnagiri gas costs and direct battery sourcing from China.** Management stated imported LNG fuel cost averages under $28-$29 (translating to ~Rs 26/kWh), and confirmed they can now directly tender 40 GWh of BESS via ICB following clearance under GFR rules.
5. **Rajesh Majumdar asked about the growth in BRBCL's profitability.** Management attributed BRBCL's profit increase to reduced finance costs from loan refinancing and scheduled repayments, alongside fly ash transportation billing.

### One-line takeaway
NTPC posted strong financial and operational growth in Q2 FY27 driven by soaring power demand and efficiency gains, even as renewable additions faced temporary transmission-led delays.
