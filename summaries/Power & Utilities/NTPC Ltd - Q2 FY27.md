# NTPC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial growth across standalone and consolidated entities, driven by higher power demand, generation, and operational efficiencies despite RE transmission constraints.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 45,752 crore, up 17% YoY (vs Rs 39,167 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 5,320 crore, up 14% YoY (vs Rs 4,653 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated Adjusted PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 7,152 crore (vs Rs 5,000 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,05,857 crore, up 14% YoY (vs Rs 93,083 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 14,247 crore, up 26% YoY (vs Rs 11,334 crore in H1 FY26).
- **NGEL Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 938.16 crore, up 53.22% YoY; Operating EBITDA stood at Rs 829.69 crore, up 56.67% YoY.
- **Interim Dividend:** Declared first interim dividend of Rs 3 per share.

### Highlights
- NTPC Group generation grew 10.4% YoY to 236 BU in H1 FY27 (vs 214 BU in H1 FY26), crossing 200 BU in a record 156 days.
- NTPC coal stations achieved a PLF of 75.11% in H1 FY27 compared to the national average of 68.35%.
- Installed capacity reached 91,506 MW as of H1 FY27, adding 2,398 MW during the period (196 MW NTPC, 1,136 MW NGEL Group, 1,066 MW other group entities).
- NVVN traded 31 BU in H1 FY27 (+25% YoY) and commissioned a 250 MW / 500 MWh BESS project.
- Standalone regulated equity stood at Rs 92,926 crore (reduced YoY due to transfer of coal mining business to NTPC Mining Ltd); consolidated regulated equity stood at Rs 1,21,786 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Capacity Addition:** Scaled down total FY27 group capacity addition guidance from 9.5 GW to ~8 GW (all renewable), driven by RE evacuation transmission delays. Target for FY28 is 9,639 MW (1,460 MW thermal, 444 MW hydro, 7,735 MW RE) and for FY29 is 10,877 MW (3,070 MW thermal, 7,807 MW RE).
- Management expects Khavda-region RE transmission delays to be fully resolved by mid-July 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India recorded ~11% YoY growth in power generation and ~15% YoY growth in power demand during H1 FY27. October generation grew 21% YoY.
- Peak national demand reached 271 GW while non-peak demand reached 253 GW in H1, driven by data centers, industrial activity, digital services, urban transport, and weather patterns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Coal dispatches from captive mines rose nearly 21% YoY to 26.13 MMT in H1 FY27. 
- Coal stock stands at 5.2 MMT (7 days of requirement). Tenders issued to procure 13 MMT of coal from alternative domestic sources for H2 FY27, plus 4 MMT under award.
- Biomass co-firing increased 65% YoY to 0.83 MMT in H1 FY27.
- Under-recovery disincentive dropped to Rs 332 crore in Q2 FY27 from Rs 625 crore in Q2 FY26.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Group Capex (H1 FY27):** Incurred Rs 23,932 crore (vs Rs 23,115 crore in H1 FY26). Standalone capex was Rs 14,398 crore.
- Under-construction pipeline totals over 37 GW (17.32 GW thermal, 3.6 GW hydro, ~16 GW renewables).
- Planning to tender an additional 4.8 GW thermal capacity in Q1/Q2 FY28 across Jhabua Stage 2 (800 MW), Talcher Stage 4 (800 MW), Talcher Kaniha Stage 3 (1,600 MW), Bhilai (800 MW), and BRBCL Stage 2 (800 MW), with an estimated capex of Rs 67,000 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Weighted average borrowing interest rate reduced to 5.72% in H1 FY27 (vs 5.98% in FY26).
- Executed USD 750 million loan facility from GIFT City banks; drawn USD 450 million under RBI concessional USD-INR swap facility.
- Gross Property, Plant, and Equipment grew 11% YoY to Rs 4,85,444 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Transmission bottlenecks in Gujarat and Rajasthan (Khavda belt) causing RE power curtailment and pushing commercial operation dates (CODs) into late 2027.
- Tight domestic coal stock levels across power plants in the country.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** *Curious about RE curtailment impact on NGEL financials.*
  - Management stated that transmission constraints will be resolved by mid-July 2027. While short-term book revenue is impacted via T-GNA (which covers O&M and interest costs but not depreciation), total PPA terms get extended, preventing permanent revenue loss.
- **Mohit Pandey (ICICI Securities):** *Why did standalone profit grow strongly despite lower regulated equity and lower other income?*
  - Management cited O&M operational gains, higher plant efficiencies, and reduced fixed cost under-recoveries (disincentives dropped from Rs 625 crore to Rs 332 crore).
- **Apoorva Bahadur:** *Details on C&I (Commercial & Industrial) strategy.*
  - Management issued an Expression of Interest (closing late November) to partner in C&I for power generation and heat across solar, hydro, thermal, and hybrid arrangements.
- **Atul Tiwari:** *Updates on BESS direct procurement from Chinese suppliers.*
  - Management clarified that restrictions under Rule 144 of GFR previously limited direct sourcing, but after actively pursuing with the government, NTPC is issuing an International Competitive Bidding (ICB) tender for a 40 GWh battery system to source directly from primary global/Chinese manufacturers.

### One-line takeaway
NTPC demonstrated strong operational efficiency and 14% standalone PAT growth, while near-term renewable capacity additions face temporary delays due to grid transmission bottlenecks.
