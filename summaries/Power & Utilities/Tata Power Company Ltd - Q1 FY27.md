# Tata Power Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-31 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted consistent performance with the 27th consecutive quarter of growth in PAT and EBITDA, strong power demand, and robust progress across new businesses and renewable capacity additions.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 1,401 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 4,249 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **Power Demand Growth:** Nearly 8.5% in Q1 (May at ~11%, June at 9.8%)
- **Mumbai Transmission Pipeline Investment:** ~Rs 10,000 crore over next 5 years
- **Renewable Cluster Growth (CAGR basis shared):** Revenue up 22%, EBITDA up 23%, PAT up 37%
- **Rooftop Business Growth:** Revenue up nearly 100% YoY (CAGR 4-year Q1 revenue 58%, EBITDA 68%, PAT 67%)
- **Module Production:** Crossed 1,000 MW in Q1
- **Capex in Q1:** Rs 5,300 crore
- **Annual Capex Guidance:** Rs 25,000 crore for FY27
- **Net Debt to Underlying EBITDA:** 3.41
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 1.25
- **Coal Prices Increase:** Up about 5% to 7%

### Highlights
- Marked the 27th consecutive quarter of growth in PAT and EBITDA, driven by foundational operations and new business additions.
- Transmission business reported 45% CAGR revenue, 19% EBITDA, and 27% PAT over the last 4 years. Mumbai Transmission reported 7% CAGR revenue, 11% EBITDA, and 21% PAT.
- TBCB projects progressing: Jalpura-Khurja line to complete in a few days; Bikaner-Neemrana line to commission by October.
- TP Solar cell and module plants operating; module production crossed 1,000 MW in Q1 for the first time.
- Mundra SPPA concluded with Gujarat; advanced stages of approval from 3 other states expected in August, with the 4th in September. Plant running under Section 11 for scheduling.
- Pumped hydro and Bhutan hydro projects making good progress; Khorlochhu project (600 MW) PPA signed with UPPCL.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q1 performance to multiply by 4.5x to 5x by the end of the year.
- Capex of Rs 25,000 crore lined up for the year is expected to be fully implemented, with 50% allocated to renewable projects.
- Rooftop business plans to grow by about 60% to 70% this year.
- Pumped hydro project (first unit) expected to be commissioned at the beginning of calendar year 2029, with subsequent units at 2-month intervals. Khorlochhu project expected operational in calendar year 2030.
- Coal prices expected to increase by up to 5% over the next 9 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Power demand growth is strong (8.5% in Q1; July consistently in the range of 260 to 270 GW) driven by delayed, inadequate rains leading to cooling requirements and picked-up industrial growth.
- State DISCOMs are showing a growing preference for conducting their own bidding rather than relying on central agencies, and are seeking FDRE or RTC bids.
- Industry-wide renewable curtailment was noted at roughly about 5% in Q1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Mundra supplementary PPA is based on actual cost of coal and actual parameters (including heat rate), operating as cost reimbursement/cost-reflective without ROE.
- TP Solar margins benefited from higher efficiency cells and yield improvements.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total Q1 capex was Rs 5,300 crore; full-year capex target is Rs 25,000 crore (50% for renewables, balance for FGD, transmission, and distribution).
- Renewable capacity expected to cross 9 GW by the end of the financial year by adding 2,500 MW to 2,700 MW.
- Construction on the 1,800 MW Shirwata PSP expected to start later this year subject to approvals and Board sign-off.
- Financial closure for the 1,125 MW Dorjilung project expected in the next 2 months with PFC, IFC, and other lenders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to underlying EBITDA stands at 3.41; net debt to equity is 1.25.
- Odisha DISCOM collections were temporarily delayed due to delayed government payments (Panchayati Raj departments) and heatwave non-disconnection mandates up to June 15, expected to recover in Q2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Industry-wide curtailment averaging around 5% was cited as a Q1 pain point.
- Temporary collection delays in Odisha DISCOMs due to government payment schedules and heatwave billing cycles.
- Tata Projects continues to face legacy project losses, though ~90% of legacy projects have concluded.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) on Odisha DISCOM muted growth and Tata Projects losses:** Management explained Odisha issues were timing-related (delayed government drinking water/street light payments and heatwave billing cycles) and expected recovery in Q2. Tata Projects losses are due to the final 10% of legacy projects, which will normalize in coming quarters.
2. **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital) on TBCB EBITDA margins looking low (13%):** Management clarified that TBCB projects are currently under construction, so revenues/PAT are booked via lease accounting rather than operational EBITDA sharing; operational commissioning starts from Q2 onwards.
3. **Apoorva Bahadur (IIFL Capital) on rooftop realization mismatch between billing and order inflows:** Management stated the variance is driven by a product mix impact (different module types and government ULA schemes).
4. **Satyadeep Jain (Ambit Capital) on the massive growth trajectory of rooftop solar and potential cannibalization:** Management highlighted huge market potential (only 40 lakh consumers out of 25 crore in India) and stated they command a dominant market share and strong supply chain, noting that market nature will evolve.
5. **Bharani (Avendus Spark) on Mundra supplementary PPA tariff details:** Management confirmed the supplementary PPA covers actual cost of coal and cost-reflective fixed costs, but does not include a return on equity.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Power reported a robust Q1 FY27 with an 11% rise in PAT to Rs 1,401 crore—marking its 27th consecutive quarter of profit growth—underpinned by strong power demand, surging rooftop solar additions, and accelerated execution across renewable and transmission pipelines.
