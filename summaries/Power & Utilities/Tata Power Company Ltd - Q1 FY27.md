# Tata Power Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-31 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted the 27th consecutive quarter of YoY growth in PAT and EBITDA, strong structural power demand, expanding rooftop and cell/module manufacturing operations, and execution of a large capex program within defined leverage guardrails.

### Key numbers
- **PAT:** Rs 1,401 crore (up 11% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 4,249 crore (up 8% YoY).
- **Q1 Capex Spent:** Rs 5,300 crore.
- **Net Debt to Underlying EBITDA:** 3.41x.
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 1.25x.
- **Solar Module Production:** Crossed 1,000 MW in Q1 FY27 (record quarterly volume).
- **Renewable Capacity:** 6.7 GW current operational capacity; targeting to cross 9 GW by the end of FY27 (adding 2,500 MW–2,700 MW in FY27).

### Highlights
- Marked the 27th consecutive quarter of YoY PAT and EBITDA growth.
- Module manufacturing plant reached peak quarterly production (>1,000 MW); cell plant yield and efficiency improvements ongoing.
- Concluded Supplementary Power Purchase Agreement (SPPA) with Gujarat for the Mundra plant. The plant is scheduled under Section 11 with tariffs billed per SPPA; approvals from 3 additional states expected in August 2026 and the 4th in September 2026.
- Signed PPA with UPPCL for the 600 MW Khorlochhu hydro project in Bhutan (regulatory approval received; operational target CY2030).
- Secured SECI PPA for 1 unit (330 MW) of the 1,000 MW pumped storage hydro project (PSP) on an annuity basis; construction progressing toward commissioning starting early CY2029.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-Year Financial Trend:** Management expects FY27 annual profitability to follow historical patterns where full-year PAT reaches 4.5x to 5x of Q1 PAT.
- **Capacity Additions:** Target to commission 2,500 MW to 2,700 MW of renewable capacity in FY27 (226 MW commissioned in Q1, ~500 MW line of sight in early Q2).
- **Rooftop Solar:** Business targeted to grow 60%–70% YoY in FY27. Management expects cumulative rooftop revenue to cross Rs 30,000 crore by 2029 (ahead of the earlier 2030 guidance).
- **Pumped Hydro Expansion:** Work on the 1,800 MW Shirwata PSP project to begin in late FY27 post-board/regulatory approvals.

### Demand & industry commentary
- National power demand grew ~8.5% YoY in Q1 FY27 (May up ~11%, June up 9.8%), driven by delayed rains, intense heat waves, and rising industrial power consumption. July demand ranged consistently between 260 GW and 270 GW.
- Renewable auction volumes from central agencies (SECI, NTPC, NHPC) have slowed as state DISCOMs shift toward direct state-level bidding for customized FDRE (Firm & Dispatchable RE) and RTC (Round-The-Clock) solutions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industry-wide renewable curtailment averaged ~5% during Q1 FY27 due to grid transmission bottlenecks.
- Indonesian coal prices increased 5%–7% YoY; management expects prices to stay within a +/- 5% range over the next 9 months.
- TP Solar (manufacturing) margins remained healthy at ~25%–26% due to internal process efficiencies, yield management, and opportunistic external cell sales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **FY27 Capex Target:** Planned at Rs 25,000 crore (50% allocated to renewables; balance across T&D, FGD, and PSP). Q2 FY27 capex expected between Rs 6,000 crore and Rs 6,500 crore.
- **Transmission:** Mumbai Transmission business to invest ~Rs 10,000 crore over the next 5 years on a regulated equity basis. Jalpura-Khurja TBCB transmission line completing shortly; Bikaner-Neemrana line scheduled for October 2026 commissioning.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to underlying EBITDA stood at 3.41x and net debt to equity at 1.25x, remaining within internal target guardrails.
- Working capital collections in Odisha and Delhi DISCOMs experienced temporary seasonal shifts due to summer billing lag and delayed government scheme payouts, which management expects to normalize in Q2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution delays on state transmission infrastructure impacted timely grid connection of completed renewable capacity in Q1.
- Industry-wide curtailment losses (~5%) due to transmission grid congestion.
- Legacy third-party loss-making EPC contracts continue to drag profitability, with full wrap-up expected over the next quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** *Asked about lower central RE auction volumes and PSP commercial structures.*  
  - **Management:** DISCOMs prefer customized state-level RTC/FDRE bids over central auctions. PSP commercialization will be a mix of annuity bids (330 MW tied with SECI) and direct bilateral PPAs with C&I clients like Tata Steel.
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** *Asked about muted Q1 earnings in Odisha DISCOMs and EPC business losses.*  
  - **Management:** Odisha collections were deferred due to government non-disconnection mandates during summer heatwaves and delayed state payouts, but operational sales volume grew 10.4%. EPC losses stem from final phaseout of legacy third-party contracts (90% completed), which will wrap up in ~1 quarter.
- **Apoorva Bahadur (IIFL Capital):** *Asked about ALMM-II implementation shifts and rooftop realization metrics.*  
  - **Management:** ALMM-II remains intact for utility and rooftop projects; exemptions affect <10% of the market (behind-the-meter/limited open access). Rooftop realization variations are due to module specification/capacity mix across government schemes (ULA) and commercial installations.
- **Anuj Upadhyay (Investec):** *Asked about solar EPC segment losses and future focus.*  
  - **Management:** The company has stopped taking third-party EPC orders to repurpose all EPC resources to execute its internal 5.5 GW captive renewable pipeline.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Power delivers steady earnings growth supported by scale in rooftop renewables, utility-scale expansion, stable regulated T&D returns, and disciplined execution of its Rs 25,000 crore capex program.
