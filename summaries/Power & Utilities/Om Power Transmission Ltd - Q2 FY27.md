# Om Power Transmission Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, margin expansion, and a sharp acceleration in order wins post-quarter close providing clear medium-term visibility.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from Operations: Rs 128.29 crore (Up 56.4% YoY for Q2); Rs 249.93 crore (Up 46.3% YoY for H1)
- EBITDA: Rs 19.56 crore (Up 111.3% YoY for Q2); Rs 34.34 crore (Up 61.4% YoY for H1)
- EBITDA Margin: 15.25% (vs 11.29% in Q2 FY26 and 12.15% in Q1 FY27); 13.74% for H1 (vs 12.45% in H1 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 13.85 crore (Up 116.7% YoY for Q2); Rs 24.37 crore (Up 65.3% YoY for H1)
- PAT Margin: 10.7% (vs 7.7% in Q2 FY26 and 8.6% in Q1 FY27); 9.7% for H1 (vs 8.6% in H1 FY26)
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Rs 4.04 (vs Rs 2.54 in Q2 FY26); Rs 7.24 for H1 (vs Rs 5.92 in H1 FY26)
- Order Book: Rs 565.99 crore as of September 30, 2026; Rs 273.21 crore won in October 2026, taking total order book to ~Rs 839 crore (before adjusting for October execution)
- Bid Pipeline: Rs 1,600 crore
- Net Worth: Rs 283.43 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 136.94 crore at March 2026)
- Gross Borrowings: Rs 24.06 crore (vs Rs 47.67 crore at March 2026)
- Cash, Bank Balance & Liquid Investments: Rs 38.34 crore (Net cash positive position)
- Debt-to-Equity: 0.08x (vs 0.35x at March 2026)
- Trade Receivables: Rs 237.06 crore (vs Rs 221.42 crore at March 2026); retention money constitutes 45% of total receivables

### Highlights
- Monsoon execution remained healthy, driving 56% YoY revenue growth in Q2.
- Underground cabling order book recovered significantly with wins from PGVCL (Bhavnagar) and GETCO (66 kV) during the quarter, followed by UGVCL (Ambaji) in October, contributing to Gujarat’s Wire-Free City Mission.
- Commissioned the 400 kV GIS substation at Dholera (one of the largest projects to date); associated 400 kV transmission lines are in final stages.
- IPO proceeds utilization: Out of Rs 119.32 crore net proceeds, Rs 108.56 crore utilized as of September 30, 2026 (Rs 25 crore for debt prepayment, Rs 55 crore for long-term working capital, Rs 28.11 crore for general corporate purposes, and Rs 0.46 crore for capex). Balance Rs 10.75 crore earmarked for machinery/equipment capex.
- Credit rating improved to CRISIL BBB+.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 50% YoY.
- Steady-state annual EBITDA margin guidance is maintained at 12% to 13%, and PAT margin between 8% to 9%, subject to project and segment mix.
- Opening order book target by the end of FY27 is in the range of Rs 1,000 crore to Rs 1,200 crore.
- FY30 Vision: Targeting revenue of around Rs 2,500 crore by FY30, driven by scale-up in 765 kV transmission networks, Power Grid and GETCO tenders, GIS substations, and geographic expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Transmission and distribution capex cycle in Gujarat and across India remains strong, driven by network strengthening, renewable energy evacuation, and urban underground cabling conversion.
- Gujarat Wire-Free City Mission aims to convert overhead distribution networks in all municipal corporations and municipalities into underground cabling by 2030 (initial phase provision of Rs 500 crore).
- Right of Way (RoV) for PSU tenders (e.g., GETCO) remains in the utility's scope.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin (15.25%) was elevated due to a favorable business segment and project mix (79% revenue from higher-margin transmission line vertical, 17% from substations, and execution of fast-track specialized Dholera project).
- Employee benefit cost reduced as a percentage due to lower revenue contribution from the O&M vertical (3% of H1 revenue).
- Finance cost includes bank guarantee commission (10% performance bank guarantee required for contracts), which scales with top-line growth. Borrowing cost expected to decline from 9%-10% to around 8% following the CRISIL credit rating upgrade.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure will be deployed in line with project requirements using remaining IPO proceeds (Rs 10.75 crore).
- Expanding capabilities toward complex projects: 765 kV transmission networks, GIS substations, pile foundations, river crossings, and HVDC.
- Bidding actively for projects outside Gujarat (e.g., Delhi, Madhya Pradesh) and participating in Power Grid tenders alongside GETCO. Company stated it is individually qualified to bid for upcoming 765 kV GETCO tenders without a JV.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is in a net cash-rich position with gross debt reduced to Rs 24.06 crore and cash/liquid investments at Rs 38.34 crore.
- Trade receivables increased to Rs 237.06 crore due to accumulation of retentions (45% of total receivables) at the advanced execution stage of projects, which will normalize upon project closures. 84% of debtors are in the less than 6 months aging bucket.
- Net cash used in operating activities for H1 was negative Rs 38.20 crore, primarily reflecting a reduction in trade payables (from Rs 123.55 crore to Rs 88.11 crore) utilizing IPO proceeds to avail cash discounts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Margins are subject to quarterly volatility depending on the project mix and stage of execution.
- Intense competition in the O&M segment is putting pressure on margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rounak Bajpai (Wealthmine Advisory) asked about the margin sustainability and timeline for 765 kV projects.** Management stated 765 kV execution takes 24 to 36 months, and while Q2 margins were elevated (15.25%) due to mix, steady-state annual EBITDA margins will range between 12% and 13%.
2. **Tejas Khandelwal questioned the spike in trade receivables and rising finance costs.** Management explained that receivables spiked temporarily due to retention money accumulation (45% of total receivables) at advanced project stages, and finance costs rose due to higher bank guarantee commissions required for top-line growth.
3. **Yashvi Gandhi asked about margins and RoV for underground cabling orders.** Management replied that cabling margins are lower than EHV transmission lines but sufficient to maintain profitability, and RoV is entirely in the utility's (GETCO) scope.
4. **Arjun Vinay Tambe inquired about the FY30 vision and revenue targets.** Management stated they are targeting around Rs 2,500 crore in revenue by FY30 by moving up the value chain to 765 kV, GIS, and non-Gujarat geographies.
5. **Gayneshwar asked about the lack of non-Gujarat tender wins despite geographic expansion efforts.** Management replied they have bid for tenders in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh, and wins in other states will be announced soon.

### One-line takeaway
Om Power Transmission delivered robust H1 FY27 revenue growth of 46% and margin expansion, supported by a net cash-rich balance sheet, a surge in post-quarter order wins to ~Rs 839 crore, and clear strategic visibility toward its FY30 targets.
