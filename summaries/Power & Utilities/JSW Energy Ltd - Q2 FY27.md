### Highlights
- Commissioned 1.4 GW of organic renewable capacity since April (nearly half of the FY27 3 GW target) and added 513 MW in Q2 (301 MW solar, 212 MW hybrid).
- Completed acquisition of Maruti Clean Coal & Power (259 MW total, 195 MW net tied under long-term PPA with Rajasthan discoms) on August 6th.
- Received NCLT sanction on October 1st for the acquisition of GE Power India's boiler business (including Durgapur manufacturing facility); placed entire Salboni boiler order with Durgapur.
- Placed Phase II turbine orders for Salboni with Toshiba JSW in September (total 3,200 MW across four 800 MW units ordered).
- Halol wind blade facility commenced production in June; Chitradurga facility on track for Q3 FY27 completion.
- ICRA and India Ratings reaffirmed long-term credit rating at AA with a Stable outlook.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of achieving the 3 GW commissioning target for FY27, supported by ₹20,000 crore planned capex.
- Expects to commission ~1 GWh of energy storage projects in FY27.
- Group C&I portfolio projected to grow to over 8.5 GW by 2030 (Group companies contributing a large share).
- Group RTC power requirement for JSW Group expected to more than double to ~7 GW by 2032.
- Salboni Phase I first unit expected by 2030.
- KSK Unit 4 (600 MW) on track for FY28 commissioning, and balance 1,200 MW by FY29.
- Guided for annual RE capacity additions of 2.5 GW to 3 GW beyond FY27.
- Conservative project debt cost assumption of 9% maintained for full project lives (25 years).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India real GDP grew 7.8% YoY in Q1 FY27; RBI expects FY27 growth at ~7.1%.
- Index of Industrial Production (IIP) grew 8% YoY in August; September PMIs at 55.1 (manufacturing) and 55.2 (services).
- Q2 FY27 power demand grew ~11.1% YoY; peak demand touched 270 GW; October month-to-date growth at 21.2%.
- Long-term power demand CAGR view of 5–6% may prove conservative based on recent quarters.
- Procurement shifting decisively towards firm and dispatchable capacity (FDRE, solar-plus-storage, standalone batteries, and long-term thermal). States awarded ~17 GW of long-term thermal in FY26 at ₹5.4–₹6.3/unit, with another 18 GW under bidding.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Transition to domestic coal and job-work arrangements at imported-coal plants shielded JSW Energy from fuel-price volatility.
- Maruti plant fully domestically fuelled via long-term FSA with SECL and SHAKTI scheme linkage.
- Debt cost reduced by 51 bps YoY to 8.28%. ~25% of term debt is at fixed rates; ~90% of floating portion linked to internal benchmarks (MCLR) which lag repo rate hikes. Refinancing levers remain available.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total operational capacity stands above 15.1 GW.
- Salboni thermal project: Turbine generator orders placed for all four 800 MW units; boilers ordered from in-house Durgapur facility.
- KSK 1,200 MW expansion moved to "under construction"; equipment orders placed locally.
- Pumped storage pipeline stands at ~26.4 GWh.
- Bhutan 920 MW Punatsangchhu-III hydro project in partnership with Druk Green Power Corporation is at DPR stage; JV finalization and DPR expected by June 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net Debt to TTM EBITDA at ~4.94x (excluding CWIP debt).
- Cash and cash equivalents at ~₹15,700 crore.
- Expected cash accruals of ~₹6,500 crore plus ₹7,150 crore from recent fundraising/steel share transactions provide ample equity funding visibility.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Coal stocks at power plants fell to multi-year lows in October (~96 plants critical with 6–7 days of stock vs. 19–20 normative days).
- Industry-wide curtailment averages ~5.4% nationally (JSW portfolio at 4.8% in H1; Q2 curtailment caused ~225 MUs loss and ₹59 crore revenue impact). ~60% of curtailed capacity on temporary GNA to move to permanent GNA by end of Q3 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** Asked about PPA visibility and strategy for Mahanadi (KSK) expansion. Management stated they are exploring DISCOM PPAs given firm power demand, but have the flexibility to keep part of the capacity merchant/short-term due to low fuel costs near the mine.
2. **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** Inquired about BESS strategy and time-shift arbitrage compared to peers. Management clarified that their curtailment is temporary, solar capacities are tied to PPAs, and they will selectively add standalone BESS for evening peak arbitrage over and above 3.2 GWh.
3. **Atul Tiwari (JPM):** Asked about merchant sales volume and sustainability of Q3 pricing. Management stated Q3 is fully booked at pricing equal to or better than Q2's ₹6.9/unit.
4. **Dhruv Muchhal (HDFC AMC):** Questioned the 3-year timeline for KSK's 1,200 MW brownfield expansion. Management explained that common infrastructure (balance of plant, coal handling, railway, transmission) is already built for 3.6 GW, giving high execution confidence.
5. **Abhineet Anand (BNP Paribas):** Asked about the sensitivity of debt costs to RBI rate hikes. Management expects only ~30 bps transmission on a 75 bps repo rate hike due to MCLR lagging, capping average debt cost around 8.50% with refinancing support.

### One-line takeaway
JSW Energy delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 24% revenue and 23% EBITDA growth, backed by robust power demand, 1.7 GW of capacity additions since April, and strategic insulation from fuel and interest rate volatility.
