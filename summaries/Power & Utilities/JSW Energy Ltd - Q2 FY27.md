# JSW Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong power demand, steady execution (1.7 GW capacity additions since April), strong revenue/PAT growth, and insulated balance sheet.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 6,658 crore (+24% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 3,907 crore (+23% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 1,141 crore (+38% YoY); PAT attributable to shareholders: Rs 1,056 crore (+50% YoY)
- **Net Generation:** 17.5 billion units (+17% YoY)
- **Depreciation:** Rs 943 crore (+17% YoY)
- **Finance Costs:** Rs 1,615 crore (+14% YoY)
- **Weighted Average Cost of Debt:** 8.28% (down 51 bps YoY from 8.79%)
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** ~Rs 15,700 crore
- **Net Debt to TTM EBITDA (ex-CWIP):** 4.94x (vs ~5.2x in FY26)

### Highlights
- Commissioned 1.4 GW of organic renewable capacity since April 2026; total capacity additions stand at 1.7 GW (including 300 MW Maruti thermal acquisition), taking installed capacity above 15.1 GW (~61% renewables).
- Maruti Clean Coal & Power acquired in August for an enterprise value of Rs 1,410 crore; generated Rs 45 crore EBITDA in 55 days post-acquisition in Q2.
- Utkal plant EBITDA more than tripled YoY to Rs 368 crore in Q2 FY27 due to long-term PPAs with Karnataka and Assam and improved plant availability.
- Merchant power sales stood at 1.1 billion units (7% of total generation volume, ~4% of portfolio capacity) at an average realization of ~Rs 6.9 per unit (~20% premium over DAM average of Rs 5.7 per unit).
- ICRA and India Ratings reaffirmed long-term credit rating at AA (Stable).

### Guidance & outlook
- Target to commission 3 GW of renewable capacity in FY27 remains fully on track, alongside ~1 GWh of BESS projects.
- Management plans capacity additions of 2.5 GW to 3.0 GW annually toward its 30 GW target by 2030.
- Group captive/C&I portfolio target expected to expand from 3.7 GW currently to over 8.5 GW by 2030, driven by JSW Group’s RTC power requirements doubling to ~7 GW by 2032.
- Long-term power demand CAGR estimated at 5–6%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- All-India power demand grew 11.1% YoY in Q2 FY27 (peak demand at 270 GW), with October MTD demand up 21.2%.
- National renewable capacity addition reached ~23.9 GW in H1 FY27, crossing 300 GW total non-fossil capacity.
- Domestic coal stocks dropped to multi-year lows in October, with ~96 power plants operating under critical stock levels (6–7 days of inventory vs 19–20 days norm).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- API4 benchmark coal prices averaged ~$115 per tonne in Q2 FY27 (+28% YoY from $90/tonne).
- Company mitigated imported fuel cost spikes via transition to domestic coal and job-work arrangements at imported coal plants.
- Maruti plant operates on long-term linkage under SECL FSA and SHAKTI scheme.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex for FY27 stands at ~Rs 20,000 crore.
- Salboni thermal project: Turbine generator orders placed for all 4 x 800 MW units (3,200 MW total); first unit targeted for 2030. Boiler orders placed with newly acquired Durgapur facility (GE Power India boiler business).
- KSK Mahanadi: Unit 4 (600 MW) on track for FY28; balance 1,200 MW targeted for FY29 at a 25–30% lower capex per MW than industry benchmark.
- In-house supply chain integration: Halol wind blade facility operational since June; Chitradurga facility on track for Q3 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ~25% of term debt is fixed rate (tenor 5–6 years), and ~90% of floating rate debt is linked to internal bank benchmarks (MCLR).
- Equity funding visibility supported by ~Rs 6,500 crore internal cash accruals and Rs 7,150 crore capital raised/unlocked.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Renewable curtailment stood at ~4.8% of generation in H1 FY27, impacting Q2 revenue by Rs 59 crore (~225 MUs curtailed). Management expects ~60% of curtailed capacity to transition to permanent GNA by end of Q3 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** *Asked about PPAs for upcoming Mahanadi expansion capacity and drivers for Utkal’s QoQ EBITDA jump.*  
  **Management:** Stated Mahanadi has low fuel cost given mine proximity, providing flexibility for long-term PPAs or merchant exposure; Utkal improved due to higher plant availability (82% PLF) allowing incremental merchant sales alongside state PPAs.
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** *Inquired about transmission/land readiness for 30 GW by 2030 and BESS strategy.*  
  **Management:** Confirmed capacity expansion is split between off-grid group captive, STU intra-state networks, and RE-plus-BESS to bypass grid curtailments.
- **Atul Tiwari (JPMorgan):** *Asked for details on merchant volume and sustainability of merchant pricing.*  
  **Management:** Confirmed 1.1 BUs sold in Q2; Q3 capacity is fully booked at pricing equal to or better than Q2's Rs 6.9/kWh.
- **Dhruv Muchhal (HDFC AMC):** *Questioned the feasibility of a 3-year commissioning timeline for the 1,200 MW KSK expansion.*  
  **Management:** Equipment orders are placed and common infrastructure (coal handling, railway, transmission) for 3.6 GW is already in place from the acquisition.
- **Abhineet Anand (BNP Paribas):** *Asked about interest rate hike sensitivity and impact on project IRRs.*  
  **Management:** Expected interest rate transmission to MCLR to add at most ~30 bps to overall cost of debt (up to ~8.50%); project underwriting models conservatively assume a 9.0% cost of debt over 25 years with benchmark mid-teen return IRRs intact.

### One-line takeaway
JSW Energy demonstrated strong Q2 FY27 performance driven by thermal PPA conversions, solid operational generation, and accelerating RE capacity additions while remaining fully funded for its Rs 20,000 crore FY27 capex plan.
