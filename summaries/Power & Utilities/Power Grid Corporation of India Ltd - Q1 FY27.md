# Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27

**Tone:** Mixed - Strong operational execution and a robust order pipeline are currently masked by regulatory accounting adjustments impacting reported PAT.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (Consolidated):** Rs 11,697 crore (vs Rs 11,444 crore in the previous quarter).
*   **Transmission Charges:** Rs 10,905 crore (vs Rs 10,620 crore).
*   **PAT:** Rs 3,598 crore (vs Rs 3,631 crore).
*   **Capitalization (Q1):** Rs 5,277 crore.
*   **Gross Fixed Assets:** Rs 3.25 lakh crore.
*   **System Availability:** 99.8%.
*   **Receivable Days:** 12 days (improved from 19.41).

### Highlights
*   Commissioned 1,635 circuit km of transmission lines and 10,500 MVA of transformation capacity in Q1.
*   Secured 6 new transmission projects (inter-state and intra-state) with a total gross annual tariff of Rs 2,200 crore.
*   Forayed into a new asset class with the country's first Synchronous Condenser project at Fatehgarh II.
*   Collection efficiency stood at 104% (Rs 11,404 crore realized against Rs 10,963 crore billed).
*   Signed a green loan agreement of JPY 80 billion with JBIC.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Capex Guidance:** Rs 37,000 crore (maintained).
*   **Capitalization Guidance:** Rs 30,000 crore (maintained).
*   **Works in Hand:** Rs 1.75 lakh crore (TBCB: Rs 1.46 lakh crore, RTM: Rs 25,000 crore, Others: Rs 4,200 crore).
*   **Bidding Pipeline:** Rs 1.19 lakh crore (Rs 73,875 crore under bidding, Rs 45,000 crore to be floated).
*   Management expects the Rs 7.9 lakh crore sector investment plan (by 2035-36) to be spread across the next 3-4 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Growth drivers include the National Electricity Plan (900+ GW by 2035-36), hydro potential in the Brahmaputra Basin, data centers (71 GW), and green hydrogen/ammonia.
*   "One Sun, One World, One Grid" concept is gaining traction, with management noting the need for cross-border grid integration.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Regulatory Drag:** Reported PAT was impacted by a Rs 560 crore regulatory drag, comprising Rs 330 crore in depreciation and Rs 230 crore in interest on the differential between filed petitions and CERC orders.
*   Equipment supply constraints are easing due to increased vendor capacity and the company's bulk procurement strategy.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Capital work in progress (CWIP) stands at Rs 50,419 crore.
*   TBCB equity investments are funded entirely through internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net worth improved to Rs 1,04,028 crore.
*   Receivable days reduced significantly to 12 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory Accounting:** Timing differences between CERC orders and tariff filings create volatility in reported quarterly earnings.
*   **Execution:** Right of Way (ROW) challenges persist, though policy improvements are helping.
*   **Land Costs:** Uncertainty remains regarding the impact of new land compensation guidelines on project costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Apoorva Bahadur (IIFL):** Asked about the PAT drag. Management clarified it is a regulatory accounting issue (interest on differential tariff and depreciation) and not an operational failure.
*   **Bharanidhar (Avendus Spark):** Asked about the timeline for the Rs 7.9 lakh crore capex. Management stated it will be spread over the next 3-4 years, dependent on generation and load growth.
*   **Sumit Kishore:** Requested better disclosure on TBCB consolidated performance. Management agreed to examine better disclosure practices to help analysts evaluate the company.
*   **Dhruv (HDFC MF):** Asked about lease accounting impact on TBCB. Management confirmed lease accounting is used, which affects depreciation reporting but is PAT neutral.

### One-line takeaway
Power Grid continues to deliver strong operational growth and maintains a healthy order pipeline, with short-term earnings volatility driven by regulatory accounting mechanics rather than operational performance.
