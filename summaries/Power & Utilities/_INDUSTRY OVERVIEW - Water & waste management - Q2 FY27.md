## Power & Utilities - Water & waste management: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the water and waste management sector report a generally positive demand environment driven by robust municipal order books and expanding industrial capital expenditure. However, execution has been hampered by localized headwinds, including Middle Eastern logistical bottlenecks affecting international shipments (Concord Enviro Systems, Ion Exchange) and unexpected cost overruns in legacy treatment projects (Ion Exchange). While asset-heavy players like Antony Waste and VA Tech Wabag highlight record order books and infrastructure scaling, companies with high working capital cycles or exposure to export markets face temporary margin pressures and cash flow constraints.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Traditionally, Indian water and waste management firms operated primarily as EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contractors relying heavily on lumpy municipal tenders and public sector outlays. Over the past five years, the industry has shifted toward asset-light consultancy, long-term O&M (Operations & Maintenance) contracts that provide recurring revenue annuities, and integrated build-own-operate models for industrial zero liquid discharge (ZLD) and waste-to-energy projects. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **Expansion into industrial ZLD and solar manufacturing water solutions:** Concord Enviro reported robust domestic demand with H1 domestic order inflows up 55% YoY, including orders from solar photovoltaic module manufacturers like a recent INR14.25 crore order [Concord Enviro concall Q2 FY27].
- **Diversification into high-value environmental testing and climate tech consultancy:** Techknowgreen secured NABL approval for its TRL9 environmental testing center, validating high-end consultancy services that support its technology and knowledge segments [Techknowgreen concall H1 FY27].
- **Scaling up municipal waste-to-energy and recycling infrastructure:** Antony Waste Handling Cell is advancing its AP waste-to-energy projects with a planned capex of Rs 670–680 crore, targeting commercial operations by Q1 FY29 [Antony Waste concall Q2 FY27].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** All companies agree that underlying structural demand from urban municipal bodies and industrial clients (such as steel, pharma, and solar manufacturing) remains robust, supported by strong order pipelines.
* **Divergence:** While EMS Ltd (targeting 30-35% QoQ growth in H2) and VA Tech Wabag (order book exceeding Rs 20,000 crore) see aggressive order conversion and acceleration, Ion Exchange remains cautious near-term due to legacy project write-downs, and Concord Enviro notes lumpy execution cycles in large projects.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreements:** Managements across the board note that fixed overheads and geopolitical trade frictions have created short-term pressures. Middle East logistical disruptions have impacted international dispatches and input costs for players like Ion Exchange and Concord Enviro.
* **Disagreements:** Antony Waste's margins were depressed by a temporary Rs 27 crore expected credit loss (ECL) accounting provision, whereas Ion Exchange suffered massive operating losses due to cost overruns in legacy Treatment Solutions projects. Conversely, Concord Enviro expanded gross margins to 57.5% (vs 50.5% YoY) through better product mix despite forex losses.

## Capex & capacity
* Antony Waste is undertaking major capex of Rs 670-680 crore for its AP waste-to-energy projects [Antony Waste concall Q2 FY27].
* Concord Enviro is spending INR30-35 crores in FY27 (including INR15 crores for Vasai facility debottlenecking) and guiding INR50-60 crores for FY28 [Concord Enviro concall Q2 FY27].
* Ion Exchange has Rs 240 crores in capital work-in-progress (CWIP) directed toward Roha facility backward integration, Ankleshwar modular expansion, and Goa membrane facility expansion [Ion Exchange concall Q2 FY27].
* EMS Ltd has no company-level major capex planned for FY27 apart from routine machinery [EMS concall Q2 FY27].

## What changed since last quarter
- **Antony Waste Handling Cell:** Tone shifted from "Mixed" to "Positive" on record revenue, though the Moshi WtE plant restart was delayed from October 2026 to December 2026 due to shipping logistics. Guidance maintained at 20% revenue CAGR.
- **Concord Enviro Systems:** Tone shifted from cautious to confident as operational execution normalized. Maintained 15-20% revenue growth guidance for FY27.
- **EMS Ltd:** Shifted from "recovery phase" to "growth phase" with targeted H2 QoQ growth of 30-35%. Promoter pledge reduced from Rs 200 crore to Rs 60 crore.
- **Ion Exchange India:** Tone turned negative due to legacy project write-downs and West Asia supply chain disruptions, though backward integration at Roha continues into the end of the fiscal year.
- **Techknowgreen Solutions:** Maintained positive momentum, reporting 46% YoY revenue growth and closing a Rs 3 crore corporate loan using internal cash.
- **VA Tech Wabag:** Maintained positive medium-term guidance, reporting a lifetime high order book exceeding Rs 20,000 crore and net cash over Rs 1,000 crore.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Antony Waste:** Progress on the Moshi WtE plant restoration ahead of the December 2026 deadline and working capital/DSO normalization.
* **Concord Enviro:** Execution progress on large projects (>INR50 crores) and scaling of the Vasai facility.
* **EMS Ltd:** Delivery on projected 30-35% QoQ revenue growth for H2 and further promoter pledge reduction toward zero.
* **Ion Exchange:** Stabilization of Treatment Solutions margins and progress on the Roha backward integration.
* **Techknowgreen Solutions:** Operating cash flow conversion following October collections and execution of Deendayal Port orders.
* **VA Tech Wabag:** Order book conversion and mitigation of Middle Eastern logistical headwinds.
