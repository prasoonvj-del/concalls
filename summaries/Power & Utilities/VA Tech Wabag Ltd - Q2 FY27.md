# VA Tech Wabag Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record order book and net cash milestones, with strong revenue and profit growth, despite acknowledging logistical headwinds in the Middle East.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (15-20% revenue CAGR; 13-15% EBITDA margin).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive, though management acknowledged a slight slowdown in new project starts in the Middle East due to shifting investment strategies.
- **New topics:** Succession planning (grooming internal talent and lateral hires), AI-based knowledge management, and specific updates on the Indosol project land status.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on order book (>INR 20,000 crore) and net cash (>INR 1,000 crore) milestones.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1):** INR 1,887 crore (up 20.3% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA (H1):** INR 261 crore (up 21% YoY; margin ~14%).
- **Consolidated PAT (H1):** INR 195 crore (up 30% YoY; margin 10.3%).
- **Standalone Revenue (H1):** INR 1,587 crore (up 19% YoY).
- **Order Book:** INR 20,207 crore (lifetime high).
- **Order Intake (H1):** INR 5,200 crore.
- **Net Cash:** >INR 1,000 crore (record level).
- **Net Working Capital:** 101 days (improved from 104 days in March 2026).
- **ROE:** 16.3% (vs 15.7% in March 2026).
- **ROCE:** 20.5% (vs 19.5% in March 2026).

### Highlights
- Secured maiden project in Kuwait (60 MIGD SWRO desalination) and entered UAE (Ajman Wastewater Treatment).
- Secured major orders from Reliance Industries (Jamnagar desalination, remineralization, and effluent treatment).
- Achieved "Wriddhi 1.0" strategic targets; "Wriddhi 2.0" (next 5 years) to be unveiled in May 2027.
- O&M backlog reached INR 6,500 crore.
- 15th consecutive quarter of positive net cash.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated medium-term objectives: 15-20% revenue CAGR, 13-15% EBITDA margin, ROCE >20%, and ROE >15%.
- Management expects the next 12-18 months to be a "bumper" period for order intake.
- O&M revenue targeted to reach 20% of total revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Middle East:** Existing projects are moving; new project starts are slower as governments re-evaluate investment baskets.
- **India:** Strong traction in Tamil Nadu, Bangalore, Delhi, MP, and Rajasthan across municipal and industrial segments.
- **Sunrise sectors:** Management identified data centers, solar, semiconductors, and green hydrogen as modular, high-tech, and high-growth opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margins:** Management stated current margins are structural, not one-off.
- **Commodity inflation:** Indian contracts have pass-through escalation formulas; international projects are hedged.
- **Employee expenses:** Increased due to annual increments and Wage Code changes (PF/gratuity).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company maintains an asset-light model; no capex required for growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash (excluding HAM) reached >INR 1,100 crore.
- Net working capital days improved to 101 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Middle East:** Supply chain delays in shipping equipment; management is using air freight or alternative routes to mitigate.
- **Bangladesh:** Some project delays due to political disturbances, though payment security remains intact via World Bank funding.
- **Indosol:** 90% of land acquired; 100% expected within a quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) on Middle East execution:** Management confirmed shipping delays due to regional disturbances but noted that project sites remain active and work is progressing.
2. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial) on bid pipeline:** Management stated they are at a peak in bidding activity and expect the next 12-18 months to be a "bumper" period for order intake.
3. **Sailesh Raja (360 ONE) on competitive landscape:** Management noted they are well-positioned to participate in PPP models as an EPC partner, leveraging their strong balance sheet and local presence.
4. **Rajagopalan Ranganathan (Investor) on succession:** Management confirmed a focus on grooming internal talent and lateral hires, alongside implementing AI-based knowledge management systems.
5. **Vinit M. (Institutional Equities) on sunrise sectors:** Management clarified that sectors like data centers and semiconductors offer smaller, modular, high-tech orders rather than massive ticket sizes, requiring specialized teams.

### One-line takeaway
VA Tech Wabag achieved record order book and net cash milestones in H1 FY27, maintaining strong growth guidance despite logistical challenges in the Middle East.
