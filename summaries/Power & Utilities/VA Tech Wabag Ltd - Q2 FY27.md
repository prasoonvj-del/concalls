# VA Tech Wabag Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Reported lifetime high order book exceeding Rs 20,000 crore, record net cash over Rs 1,000 crore, and highest-ever Q2 revenue and net profit.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained medium-term targets (15%-20% revenue CAGR, 13%-15% EBITDA margin, ROCE >20%, ROE >15%, O&M share targeting 20%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained strongly positive; order intake reached Rs 5,200 crore in H1 FY27. European market saw a revival with two contract wins, while Saudi Arabia showed slight execution/bidding slowness due to Middle East geopolitical developments.
- **New topics or risks:** Supply chain shipments experienced severe delays in the Middle East due to regional conflicts, mitigated via alternative shipping routes and air freight. Indosol project faced temporary land reallocation delays in Andhra Pradesh.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on order book expansion (>Rs 20,000 crore) and cash position (>Rs 1,000 crore net cash).

---

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Crossed Rs 1,000 crore, up 20% YoY.
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 145 crore; EBITDA margin at 14.5%.
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated PAT:** Crossed Rs 100 crore, up 23% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,887 crore, up 20.3% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 261 crore, up 21% YoY; EBITDA margin at ~14% (13.8%).
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT:** Rs 195 crore, up ~30% YoY; PAT margin expanded to 10.3% (vs 9.6% in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Financials:** Revenue Rs 1,587 crore (up 19% YoY), EBITDA Rs 227 crore (up 20% YoY), PAT Rs 169 crore (up 29% YoY).
- **Order Book:** Lifetime high of Rs 20,207 crore (H1 order intake over Rs 5,200 crore).
- **Revenue Mix (H1 FY27):** International 54%, India 46%; EPC 82%, O&M 18%.

---

### Highlights
- Crossed double lifetime milestones: Order book exceeded Rs 20,000 crore and Net Cash exceeded Rs 1,000 crore.
- Signed and commenced 60 MIGD (~272 MLD) SWRO desalination plant in Kuwait with HEISCO, including 5-year O&M.
- Secured Mega desalination order in Jamnagar, India from Reliance Industries Limited (~201 MLD, expandable to ~302 MLD, plus ~72 MLD remineralization plant) and a repeat Medium order for an ETP at the Dhirubhai Ambani Green Energy Giga Complex.
- Expansion into UAE via Ajman Wastewater Treatment Project and signed O&M contract with Adani group.
- Concreting for 400 MLD Perur Desalination plant in Chennai reached 90% completion (~1 lakh cubic meters poured).
- Contrary to earlier media reports of cancellation, management confirmed that basic engineering for the Yanbu desalination plant is substantially ready and long-lead equipment procurement has been initiated.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue CAGR:** 15% to 20% in the medium term (management guidance).
- **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained in the 13% to 15% band (management guidance).
- **Return Metrics:** ROCE target >20% (achieved 20.5% in H1), ROE target >15% (achieved 16.3% in H1).
- **Wriddhi 2.0:** Long-term 5-year strategic plan to be formally unveiled at the annual investor meet in May 2027.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust order pipeline across municipal and industrial segments in India (Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Maharashtra, AP, MP, Rajasthan, Delhi, Bengaluru).
- High inquiry levels in sunrise sectors: semiconductor, solar manufacturing, data centers (converting air-cooled systems to water-cooled), and green hydrogen.
- European cluster showing increased activity after a subdued period.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity pricing and geopolitical disturbances created supply chain and shipping cost pressures, but execution timelines and margins remained resilient.
- Standard escalation clauses in Indian contracts pass commodity inflation to clients; international orders are hedged shortly after contract award.
- Employee expenses rose to Rs 99 crore in Q2 due to annual increments and full implementation of Wage Code adjustments.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operates an asset-light business model requiring minimal capital expenditure for business expansion.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Cash (excluding HAM):** All-time high of over Rs 1,100 crore (vs Rs 950 crore at FY26-end); 15th consecutive quarter of positive net cash.
- **Net Cash (including HAM):** Rs 1,036 crore.
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Consolidated OCF stood at Rs 225 crore (>1.1x H1 PAT).
- **Net Working Capital:** Improved to 101 days as of September 2026 (vs 104 days as of March 2026).

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe shipment and transit delays in the MEA region requiring rerouting or air-freighting critical components.
- Indosol Solar project delayed by land reallocation following a change in state government in Andhra Pradesh (900 out of 1,000 acres secured; balance 100 acres expected in a quarter).

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** *How are geopolitical headwinds in the Middle East impacting execution and margins?*  
   **Management:** Projects have not suffered material margin or timeline impact as major work is already at sites. Main challenge is delayed ocean shipments, addressed via alternate longer routes or air freight.
2. **Sailesh Raja (360 ONE):** *What is the status of civil works at the Al Haer project in Saudi Arabia?*  
   **Management:** Civil works are over 80%-85% complete, and mechanical equipment erection has commenced.
3. **Mudit Bhandari:** *What caused the land issue at the Indosol Solar project?*  
   **Management:** Government change in AP led to land reallocation. Indosol secured 900 out of 1,000 acres and expects the remaining 100 acres within a quarter, after which the mega phase will kick off.
4. **Vinit M. (Systematix):** *What is the opportunity size in high-tech sectors like data centers and semiconductors?*  
   **Management:** These are modular, high-technology, smaller-ticket orders (12-15 month execution) offering repeat order opportunities as client capacity expands.
5. **Rajagopalan Ranganathan:** *What is the promoter succession plan?*  
   **Management:** CMD's son, Rohan Mittal, has been in the business for 9 years managing GCC, Middle East, and Africa operations, and is being groomed for leadership alongside homegrown and lateral talent.

---

### One-line takeaway
VA Tech Wabag delivered strong H1 FY27 performance with revenue up 20.3%, net cash exceeding Rs 1,100 crore, and a record Rs 20,207 crore order book while navigating Middle East supply chain logistics.
