# Adani Power Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong volume growth, higher realizations, strategic PPA tie-ups, and reported PAT growth of over 140%.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (Vision of reaching 45 GW capacity by FY32 maintained; long-term PPA tie-up for upcoming thermal pipeline increased from 56% to 62%).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains strongly positive; demand outlook expanded to highlight upcoming power demand from data centers as a key driver for baseload thermal power.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Entry into hydro generation via acquisition of GVK Energy (330 MW Alaknanda plant) under CIRP; joint development of 770 MW hydro project in Bhutan with Druk Green Power; derecognition/reversal of Rs 1,970 crore alternate coal compensation revenue from Haryana DISCOM.
- Last quarter's promises: Korba Phase-II 660 MW Unit-1 achieved grid synchronization (commercial commissioning expected shortly in Q3 FY27, on track with timeline); credit rating upgraded from AA to AA+.

### Key numbers
* **Sales Volume:** 26.4 billion units (up 11.5% YoY).
* **Plant Load Factor (PLF):** 70.1% (vs 62.8% in Q2 FY26).
* **Continuing Revenue from Operations:** Rs 17,014 crore (up 29.8% YoY).
* **Total Continuing Revenue:** Rs 17,528 crore (up 28.5% YoY).
* **Prior Period Revenue (One-time):** Rs 2,422 crore (vs Rs 669 crore in Q2 FY26).
* **Total Reported Revenue:** Rs 19,951 crore.
* **Continuing EBITDA:** Rs 6,321 crore (up 18.5% YoY).
* **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 8,743 crore (up 45.7% YoY).
* **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 7,019 crore (up 141.5% YoY).
* **Fuel Cost:** Rs 10,229 crore (up 41.8% YoY).
* **Finance Cost:** Rs 760 crore (down 9.7% YoY).
* **Gross Debt:** Rs 61,860 crore as of Sept 30, 2026 (vs Rs 53,556 crore as of March 2026).
* **Net Debt:** Rs 45,846 crore as of Sept 30, 2026 (vs Rs 45,022 crore as of March 2026).

### Highlights
* Received Letter of Award for a 25-year PPA for 1,320 MW from Uttarakhand Power Corporation Limited (to be supplied from Korba Phase-II) at a fixed charge of Rs 4.30.
* Secured 62% long-term PPAs for the upcoming 24 GW thermal pipeline.
* Credit rating upgraded from AA to AA+ by CARE Ratings and India Ratings.
* Declared successful resolution applicant for GVK Energy Limited under CIRP, entering domestic hydro via the operational 330 MW Alaknanda plant.
* Signed shareholders' agreement with Druk Green Power for a 49:51 JV to develop a 770 MW hydro project in Bhutan (second JV after 570 MW Wangchhu).

### Guidance & outlook
* **Peak Debt:** Expected to peak at around Rs 1,000,000 crore by FY30 or FY31.
* **Hydro Capacity Target:** Targeting 5,000 MW hydro capacity internationally (Bhutan) as per group strategy.
* **PLF Expectation:** Normal expected steady-state thermal PLF going forward is ~65%.
* **PPA Bids:** Participated/participating in long-term power bids in UP, West Bengal, and Rajasthan.

### Demand & industry commentary
* All-India Q2 power demand rose 11.5% YoY to 502 billion units, with average thermal PLF increasing to 68% (from 62%).
* Thermal stepped up to meet 73% of incremental generation needed due to lower hydro output.
* Data centers are expected to become major power guzzlers, requiring 24/7 baseload thermal power.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Fuel cost increased 41.8% YoY due to higher volumes and a ~50% increase in the landed cost of imported coal.
* Lower coal quality received during the monsoon season slightly raised domestic fuel consumption per unit.
* Short-term merchant tariffs surged 33% per unit, while long-term/medium-term PPA realization improved 12.7%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Korba Phase-II:** First 660 MW unit synchronized; commercial commissioning expected in Q3 FY27. Second 660 MW unit planned for Q4 FY27.
* **Mahan Phase-II (1,600 MW):** Targeted for commercial operations in early FY28.
* **Raipur Phase-III:** Adding 1,600 MW ultra-supercritical plant, expanding total site capacity to 4,570 MW.
* Execution ongoing for Raipur Phase-II, Raigarh Phase-II, Mirzapur, and Anuppur projects.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Capex is being funded predominantly through internal cash accruals.
* Gross debt increased due to timing of funds drawn near quarter-end reflected as cash, while Net Debt remained flat at Rs 45,846 crore.
* Received ~Rs 6,000 crore from GUVNL toward energy charge revisions/prior period dues; all past period recoveries from Gujarat DISCOM are now complete.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Derecognized Rs 1,970 crore of alternate coal compensation claims previously recognized for Haryana DISCOM to adopt conservative accounting principles while discussions are ongoing.
* Landed cost of imported coal rose ~50% YoY; tariff pass-through indexation for Gujarat PPAs reflects coal price changes with a time lag.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Q on alternate coal compensation derecognition for Haryana DISCOM:** Management explained that despite having received 50% of the claim amount, they reversed ~Rs 1,970 crore on auditor advice to remain conservative until full settlement is reached.
* **Q on drivers of continuing EBITDA expansion despite stable capacity:** Management attributed the jump to full utilization of newly acquired capacities, signing of medium-term PPAs at better tariffs, higher pass-through realizations on imported coal, and lowering fuel costs at Tuticorin plant by blending ~35–40% domestic coal.
* **Q on location restrictions for upcoming state thermal bids:** Management clarified that UP and Gujarat bids allow projects anywhere in India; Rajasthan bids require in-state location; West Bengal bids are site-specific.
* **Q on peak debt and EBITDA targets:** Management stated peak debt will reach ~Rs 1,000,000 crore by FY30-FY31 (roughly 2x current levels), but declined to provide specific peak EBITDA guidance.

### One-line takeaway
Adani Power delivered stellar Q2 results with reported PAT up 141% on strong demand, operational execution, credit rating upgrade to AA+, and steady progress toward its 45 GW capacity vision.
