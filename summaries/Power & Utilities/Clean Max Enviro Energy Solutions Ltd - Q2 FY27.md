# Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong 56% YoY EBITDA growth in H1, corporate credit rating upgrade to AA stable by CRISIL, and robust demand across segments.

### Key numbers
- **Total RE contracted power sales capacity:** 6.3 gigawatts (3.6 gigawatts operational, 2.6 gigawatts contracted and under execution).
- **H1 Capacity additions:** ~600 megawatts commissioned in RE power sales (80% in Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu).
- **H1 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 1,047 crore vs Rs 670 crore YoY (56% YoY growth).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 553 crore vs Rs 386 crore YoY.
- **H1 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 120 crore vs Rs 19 crore YoY (six-fold increase off a low base).
- **Weighted average cost of debt:** Fell to ~8.3% overall (8.2% at project level).
- **Gross Block (as of 30 Sept 2026):** Rs 15,182 crore.
- **Capital Work-in-Progress / CapWIP (as of 30 Sept 2026):** ~Rs 6,700 crore.
- **Net Debt (as of 30 Sept 2026):** Rs 13,838 crore.
- **Credit Rating:** AA stable by CRISIL.
- **Average PPA tenure:** 23-year weighted average.
- **Credit profile of clients:** 83% AA, AAA, or multinationals; 12% A-rated (95% A-rated or above).
- **Repeat business share:** 85% in H1 FY27 (vs 75% for past two financial years).
- **Bikaner grid curtailment:** Fell from 70% in Q1 to 26% in October; cost ~5% of H1 EBITDA.
- **Green Bond:** Rs 2,500 crore raised in September at a weighted average cost of ~8.6% (fixed rate across 2 to 10 years).
- **Fixed vs. Floating Debt:** 53.7% fixed and 46% floating.
- **Payable days:** ~135 days.

### Highlights
- Secured AA stable corporate credit rating from CRISIL, bringing the company on par with JSW Energy and Adani Green.
- Completed the company's first green bond issuance of Rs 2,500 crore in September, locking in 53.7% of debt at fixed rates.
- Data and AI segment continues to be a strong theme, accounting for 41%-42% of overall volume and ~27%-28% of capacity with hyperscalers.
- Maintained strong contracting discipline with a 23-year weighted average PPA tenure and low receivable days.
- Successfully built projects within budgets (95% to 97% of budgeted cost) despite rising commodity prices and supply chain uncertainties.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Capacity addition guidance:** Management reiterated at least 1,500 megawatts of new operational capacity addition during the current fiscal (FY27).
- **EBITDA guidance:** Management reiterated at least Rs 3,000 crore minimum reported EBITDA for FY28.
- **Net debt guidance:** At a 5x net debt-to-EBITDA basis, projected net debt of Rs 15,000 crore to Rs 16,000 crore against the FY28 EBITDA target.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management expects a rise in AI data centers/compute factories moving to India due to building bottlenecks in the US, Europe, and geopolitical issues in the Middle East.
- Hyperscalers prefer CTU-connected wind-solar hybrid projects in South India to address curtailment risks and proximity to data centers.
- Hyperscaler contracts typically retain flexibility at their sole choice to use power directly into data centers or for green attributes.
- Repeat business share rose to 85% in H1 FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Weighted average cost of debt declined to ~8.3% overall and 8.2% at the project level.
- RE power sales segment EBITDA margin stands at 84% (up from 83.5% in March 2026) due to lower SG&A as a percentage of revenue.
- Tariff for capacity under execution is around Rs 4 for 50% solar and 50% wind.
- Future capex increases due to DCR/ALCM module prices have been factored into pricing with no impact on IRR requirements.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Annual capital expenditure exceeds Rs 10,000 crore.
- Aggregate portfolio size reached 13,200 megawatts as of September 30, 2026 (up from 7.8 gigawatts 18 months ago).
- Evacuation received stands at 3,200 megawatts; evacuation applied for stands at 2,800 megawatts.
- Present in 10 states (7 operational, 3 under execution).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 13,838 crore as of September 30, 2026, driven by ongoing capex funding.
- Debt against fully operational assets (>1 year) is Rs 6,075 crores (generating Rs 717 crore EBITDA in H1, <5x net debt-to-EBITDA).
- Under-construction asset debt and holding company debt stand at Rs 5,100 crore, corresponding to Rs 6,700 crore of CapWIP.
- Operating cash flow impacted negatively by about Rs 2 billion in H1 due to a lumpy vendor payment for wind turbine deliveries. Payable days normalized to ~135 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Grid curtailment:** Bikaner grid congestion cost ~5% of H1 EBITDA, though curtailment dropped to 26% in October.
- **Regulatory risk:** Theoretical retrospective application of day-solar night-restriction regulation carries a maximum risk of only 2% of fleet EBITDA, which management noted can be mitigated by reallocating power.
- **Interest rate macro environment:** Potential hardening of interest rates offset by having >50% of debt at fixed rates.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Puneet (HSBC) on Bikaner grid curtailment and timeline:** Management stated that Bikaner curtailment dropped to 26% in October as the grid upgraded substations, and noted that transmission timelines are outside their control. *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital) on operating cash flow and lower payables:** Management explained that operating cash flow dropped due to a bulky vendor payment for wind turbine deliveries funded via loan drawdown. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Bharani on group captive STU vs. CTU models and right to win:** Management explained that CTU group captive is used for massive industrial loads like smelters, but CleanMax reserved most CTU capacity for hyperscalers, where they hold a 35% market share in India. *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Siddhant on holding company tax liabilities:** Management clarified that the holding company pays cash tax due to investment and EPC income, while subsidiaries hold tax depreciation benefits. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Nikhil Poptani on repo rate hikes and project IRRs:** Management answered that a 100 bps repo rate hike only translates to a ~50 bps impact on overall borrowing cost because >50% of debt is fixed, keeping borrowing costs within the 8.75% underwritten in financial models. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
CleanMax delivered robust H1 FY27 results with 56% YoY EBITDA growth, secured an AA stable rating from CRISIL, and maintains strong momentum in its RE power sales and hyperscaler data center pipelines despite localized grid curtailment headwinds.
