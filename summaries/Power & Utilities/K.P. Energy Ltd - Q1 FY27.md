# K.P. Energy Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth and execution scale were overshadowed by significant margin contraction due to geopolitical and operational headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 520.97 crore (vs Rs 220.6 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 519.46 crore (vs Rs 219.54 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Infrastructure Development Revenue:** Rs 504.75 crore (vs Rs 208.25 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Gross Margin:** 20% (vs 28% in Q4 FY26).
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 37.44 crore (vs Rs 34.75 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 26.08 crore (vs Rs 25.42 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** 2.16 GW, valued at Rs 2,250 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Leadership Changes:** Appointed Prof. Sunil Maheshwari as Vice Chairman and Mr. Kapil Kriplani as incoming Group CFO.
*   **Execution:** Commissioned the 50.4 MW Vanki Wind Project in Kutch in July 2026.
*   **Operational Scale:** Quarterly revenue crossed Rs 500 crore, equivalent to ~1/3 of total FY26 revenue.
*   **IPP Portfolio:** Currently at 48.5 MW (11.5 MW solar, 37 MW wind). Management expects to reach 100 MW by end of FY27 and 248.5 MW over the next two years.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management guided for 30%–40% YoY growth for FY27, citing caution due to on-ground execution challenges.
*   **Margins:** Management declined to provide specific margin guidance, noting that Q1 is not necessarily representative of the future, but they do not envisage immediate further declines.
*   **Group Target:** The KP Group is targeting 10 GW by 2030; revised company-specific targets are expected to be published soon.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management views the renewable sector opportunity as "extremely strong" but noted that grid/transmission infrastructure development is a "timing challenge."
*   Industry faced an unusual combination of geopolitical disruptions (Strait of Hormuz), supply chain constraints, fuel/logistics pressures, and labor availability issues.
*   Right-of-Way (ROW) costs for transmission infrastructure have escalated, impacting project economics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margin pressure in Q1 was attributed to:
    *   Geopolitical disruption (West Asia) impacting fuel, logistics, and labor mobility.
    *   Increased ROW compensation costs for transmission corridors.
    *   Fixed project overheads due to prolonged execution cycles.
*   Management clarified that contracts are generally fixed-price, not cost-plus, though they are exploring force majeure clauses where applicable.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Company is exploring expansion into Karnataka; currently in advanced stages of discussion for land and grid connectivity.
*   Focus is on BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) to mitigate grid curtailment issues.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management noted that the promoter invested in share warrants last year and remains committed to increasing their stake.
*   Related party transactions are conducted at "arm's length."

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Margin Erosion:** Analysts questioned the sharp dip in EBITDA margins (from ~22% to ~12%). Management explained it was due to external cost pressures and project mix, not operational failure, and emphasized that they are optimizing efficiency to protect margins.
*   **Order Book Discrepancy:** An analyst noted the order book value dropped from ~Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 2,250 crore. Management explained this is due to execution (revenue booking) and a conservative decision to de-scope or re-evaluate certain orders.
*   **Promoter Presence:** Investors expressed concern over the absence of Dr. Faruk Patel in recent calls. Dr. Patel joined the call briefly to reassure investors of his commitment and the company's long-term growth prospects.
*   **Related Party Transactions:** Management defended the 50% related-party order book mix as a "win-win" for both entities, ensuring execution quality for the IPP business.
*   **Guidance vs. Performance:** Analysts questioned why the company is guiding for 30-40% growth when Q1 already showed >100% growth. Management reiterated caution due to potential on-ground execution hurdles like ROW protests.

### One-line takeaway
While KP Energy delivered massive revenue scaling in Q1, profitability was significantly squeezed by external geopolitical and logistical headwinds, leading management to adopt a cautious outlook for the remainder of FY27.
