# GE Vernova T&D India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, robust order intake, and EBITDA margin at the high end of guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** INR 20.3 billion (up 32% YoY).
*   **Order Intake:** INR 24.4 billion (up 52% YoY).
*   **Order Backlog:** INR 214.4 billion.
*   **EBITDA:** INR 5.5 billion (27.1% of revenue).
*   **PBT:** INR 5.8 billion (up 1.4x YoY).
*   **PAT:** INR 4.3 billion (21.3% of revenue).
*   **Cash Balance:** INR 32.5 billion.
*   **Gross Margin:** 44.5%.

### Highlights
*   **Execution:** Commissioned 400 kV substation in Nepal, 400 kV GIS for GETCO, 765 kV reactors for PGCIL and Resonia, and 765 kV generator transformer for NUPPL.
*   **Order Mix:** Export orders accounted for 21% of Q2 order intake and 32% of Q2 revenue.
*   **Financial Position:** Maintained net zero debt position.
*   **Related Party Transactions (RPT):** One RPT approval (INR 31 billion) lapsed due to customer project delays; another ($151 million) remains active and is expected to conclude in H2.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management maintained the mid-20s EBITDA margin guidance for the full year.
*   **HVDC:** Management expects 2-3 HVDC projects to come next year.
*   **STATCOM:** Management expects sizable ordering in Q4.
*   **Growth:** Management expects FY27 to be a stronger year than the previous year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **TBCB Pipeline:** Strong pipeline for TBCB projects expected to continue for the next 4-5 months.
*   **Data Centers:** Indian data center market is currently small (1.5 GW) but expected to grow to 8-10 GW by 2030; currently <2% of backlog.
*   **Competition:** Management noted slight pressure on prices but no significant impact from new entrants or Chinese players yet.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Gross margin was 44.5%; EBITDA margin was 27.1%.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management highlighted that significant operating leverage is expected to drive earnings growth as revenue scales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Progressing with a ~INR 10 billion capacity expansion program; ~20% has been incurred.
*   **Utilization:** Capacity utilization is "pretty high" (>75%) across most factories.
*   **Localization:** Focus remains on localizing transformers, valves, and controls; management noted that 765 kV bushing lead times are 15-18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash:** Generated INR 10.1 billion in cash during H1.
*   **Debt:** Net zero debt position maintained.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Supply Chain:** Management identified supply chain constraints (global demand and geopolitical disturbances) as a primary risk to operations.
*   **Lead Times:** High lead times for critical components (e.g., 765 kV bushings) impact delivery timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Sameer Thakur (Ambit):** Asked about the Trench acquisition. Management stated it is too early to comment but expects synergies in supply chain control.
*   **Mohit Kumar (ICICI):** Asked about $900 million global orders. Management confirmed some orders will flow to India and they have taken INR 1,300 crore RPT approval.
*   **Parikshit Kandpal (HDFC):** Asked about competitive intensity. Management noted no significant impact from Chinese players yet and that export orders generally carry higher margins than domestic.
*   **Rahul Gajare (Macquarie):** Asked about localization timelines. Management explained localization is a component-by-component journey, not an overnight shift.
*   **Amit Anwani (PL Capital):** Asked about data center mix. Management confirmed it is less than 2% of the current backlog.

### One-line takeaway
GE Vernova T&D India delivered a strong quarter with 32% revenue growth and maintained margin guidance, supported by a robust order backlog and a positive outlook for the TBCB and HVDC pipeline.
