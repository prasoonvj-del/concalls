# Inox Green Energy Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in the pivot to equipment supply, robust order book visibility, and long-term group synergies despite quarter-specific execution challenges.

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Revenue (Inox Wind):** INR 872 crore
* **Adjusted EBITDA (Inox Wind):** INR 237 crore
* **PBT (Inox Wind):** INR 95 crore
* **PAT (Inox Wind):** INR 64 crore
* **Cash Profit (Inox Wind):** INR 153 crore
* **Order Book / Backlog (Inox Wind):** ~4.4 gigawatt (as of July 2026); ~59% equipment supply and 41% turnkey (third-party).
* **Total Installed Wind Capacity in India:** 57.4 gigawatt (as of June 2026); 1.4 gigawatt commissioned in Q1 FY27.
* **O&M Portfolio (Inox Green):** 13.3 gigawatt peak (as of June 2026), comprising ~10.5 gigawatt of Wind operating assets and balance solar (includes ~6.5 gigawatt of acquired assets/investments).
* **Total Income (Inox Green):** INR 101 crore (up 17% YoY)
* **EBITDA (Inox Green):** INR 57 crore (up 19% YoY)
* **PBT (Inox Green):** INR 54 crore (up 74% YoY)
* **PAT (Inox Green):** INR 41 crore (up 86% YoY)
* **Cash PAT (Inox Green):** INR 55 crore (up 25% YoY)
* **Machine Availability (Inox Green):** ~96.3%
* **Wind World India FY26 Revenue:** ~INR 580 crore (~4.5 gigawatt O&M portfolio).

### Highlights
* Inox Wind pivoted its order mix toward equipment supply (59% equipment, 41% turnkey for third parties as of July '26) to ensure a healthier balance sheet and financial robustness.
* Signed an MOU for 1.5 gigawatt with Inox Clean Energy in June (firm orders for 500 megawatts signed to date; balance 1 gigawatt in due course).
* Received an LOA for 200 megawatts from NLC India in July.
* Demerger of the power evacuation infrastructure business from Inox Green into Inox Renewable Solutions Limited (IRSL) completed on August 1, 2026 (record date); IRSL to be automatically listed post regulatory approvals.
* Received NCLT Ahmedabad approval for the acquisition of Wind World India Limited; completion expected in Q2 FY27.
* 4X Wind Turbine model foundation work completed; first prototype installation on track for August, commercial launch by end of FY26.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management maintains full-year guidance for Inox Wind: ~75% revenue growth over the previous year and consolidated EBITDA margins of 20% to 22%.
* Inox Green maintains its annualized EBITDA guidance of INR 600 crore post-consolidation.
* Anticipates strong annual wind capacity additions of 8 to 10 gigawatts over the next few years in India.

### Demand & industry commentary
* India's Q1 FY27 wind capacity commissioning stood at 1.4 gigawatts.
* Out of 9.34 gigawatts of renewable capacity awarded through tenders in Q1, 2.35 gigawatts (25%) comprised stand-alone wind tenders.
* Power demand in the first 4 months of FY27 has been the highest in the last 4 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Inox Green O&M business EBITDA margin guidance stands at ~50%.
* 80% to 90% of components for Inox Wind's turbines are indigenized; expects to be nearly 100% indigenized before the end of the calendar year for both 3X and 4X models.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* IRSL Jaipur transformer factory expanding to manufacture 4.9 MVA transformers for the 4X series, medium-sized trafos (8 to 20 MVA), and large transformers (100 MVA plus).
* Own crane fleet currently has 4 operational units, with more to be added during the financial year.
* Planning commercial launch of Unit Substations (USS) in FY27, alongside inverters and energy capacitor systems.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Working capital cycle showing improvement; trade receivables expected to drop significantly over the next 3 to 4 quarters following the shift to equipment supply.
* Inox Green operates as an asset-light O&M player post-demerger of power evacuation infrastructure into IRSL, improving ROE and ROCE metrics.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Legacy EPC disruptions include Right of Way (ROW) issues, equipment transit challenges, customer site readiness, and adverse weather.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Shubham Burari (ICICI Securities) asked about Q1 execution megawatt numbers and FY27 targets.** Management stated they no longer guide on megawatt volumes due to the pivot to equipment supply, sticking instead to revenue and margin guidance.
* **Prit (Wealth Finvisor) inquired about blended per-megawatt realization for INOX Green.** Management stated the organic portfolio realization is INR 9 lakh to INR 10 lakh per megawatt (excluding GST), while acquired portfolios will have substantially higher realizations.
* **Baahubali (Retail Investor) questioned the achievability of the guidance given H2 heavy skew and requested a 30% Q2 growth confirmation.** Management declined quarterly quantification but reaffirmed full-year annualized guidance, noting that H2 typically captures 70% to 75% of business.
* **Shubham Shukla (Voyager Capital) asked about trade receivable levels and EPC consolidation.** Management explained that receivables under Ind AS 115 reflect risk transfer and pre-commissioning status, but the shift to equipment supply will drastically reduce receivables in coming quarters.
* **Akhilesh B. (North Star) asked about disruptions in shifting from turnkey to equipment supply and group-entity pricing terms.** Management stated turnkey disruptions include ROW and site delays, and confirmed that transactions with Inox Clean are strictly at arm's length.
* **Rahul Kumar (Vaikarya) asked for a breakdown of Inox Green's other income and legacy asset profitability.** Management noted that other income primarily consists of operational income from acquired assets/value-added services and treasury income, with O&M margins guided at ~50%.

### One-line takeaway
Inox Wind and Inox Green are prioritizing asset-light equipment supply and high-margin O&M expansion, leaning on group synergies with Inox Clean to drive substantial H2-weighted growth.
