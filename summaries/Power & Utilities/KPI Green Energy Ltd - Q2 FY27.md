# KPI Green Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Operating profit and portfolio scale grew, but PAT declined YoY due to higher depreciation and finance costs; investors raised governance and valuation concerns.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Lowered/Specific PAT guidance provided at ~Rs 500 crore for FY27 (vs prior broad expectations of absolute PAT growth and 30%-40% revenue growth); reiterated long-term 25% CAGR target.
- Tone and demand view: Focus shifted strongly toward large-scale M&A (Alfanar/Netra) and battery storage (BESS), while retail investors pushed back on promoter royalty and stock performance.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Investor backlash regarding the 2% promoter royalty and related-party orders to KP Energy emerged; geopolitical headwinds mentioned in Q1 were not reiterated.
- Last quarter's promises: Formalized the appointment of BDO as statutory auditor as promised in Q1; aggressive IPP energization continued.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 741 crore (up 16% YoY from Rs 641 crore).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 283 crore (up 22% YoY from Rs 232 crore).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 38% (up ~180 bps YoY from ~36.2%).
*   **PAT:** Rs 92 crore (down 21.4% YoY from Rs 117 crore).
*   **Cash Profit:** Rs 182 crore.
*   **H1 FY27 Totals:** Total income Rs 1,451 crore, EBITDA Rs 545 crore, PAT Rs 187 crore, cash profit Rs 355 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Growth:** Total renewable portfolio expanded to ~8.33 GW (3.02 GW installed, 5.3 GW under execution), breaking the earlier record of 6.94 GW. IPP accounts for 3.22 GW and EPC for 5.11 GW.
*   **Order Book:** Current order book stands at ~Rs 5,700 crore; secured an EPC order of ~Rs 2,000 crore for 500 MW solar from NACOF Oorja in Rajasthan.
*   **M&A:** Entered into a binding offer to acquire 100% of Alfanar Energy and Netra Wind (507.9 MW operating wind assets in Kutch, Gujarat with SECI PPAs) at an enterprise value of ~Rs 2,410 crore.
*   **H1 Energizations:** Added 317 MW under 677 MW GUVNL hybrid IPP (reaching 511 MW), 50 MW under 350 MW GUVNL solar IPP (reaching 301 MW), 269 MW solar for Coal India (Khavda), and 128 MW solar for MAHAGENCO.
*   **Governance:** Formally transitioned statutory audit to BDO.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management guided for ~Rs 500 crore PAT in FY27, noting H2 is historically stronger than H1. FY28 guidance will be shared in Q4.
*   Targeting an installed IPP capacity exit run-rate of ~2.1 GW by March 2027.
*   Reiterated long-term sustainable CAGR target of 25%.
*   Alfanar/Netra wind acquisition targeted to close by March 2027 (expected to generate Rs 350–400 crore in revenue).
*   Subsidiary Sun Drops Energia targeted to list via IPO within 18 months (pre-IPO round underway).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   India crossed 300 GW non-fossil capacity, tracking toward 500 GW by 2030; market shifting toward hybrid, RTC, FDRE, and storage solutions.
*   Storage is critical to absorb surplus daytime solar for evening peak demand.
*   Regulatory tailwinds include RPO, ISTS waivers, and PM Surya Sarovar Yojana.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin stood at 38%.
*   PAT contracted YoY due to elevated depreciation and finance costs from newly energized IPP assets ahead of revenue stabilization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   BESS portfolio reached 565 MW / 1,130 MWh under executed purchase agreements.
*   Sun Drops Energia is setting up an integrated storage campus in Gujarat (BESS assembly followed by phased cell manufacturing).
*   Secured 8,807 acres of land bank and ~5.2 GW of power evacuation infrastructure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   BESS manufacturing and IPP projects funded via project debt (closures achieved for some) and equity infusions/pre-IPO rounds at Sun Drops Energia.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Interim drag on net earnings as debt-funded IPP assets incur immediate interest and depreciation before revenues ramp up.
*   Shareholder scrutiny regarding promoter royalty payouts and related-party contract flows.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Analyst (Chandrashekar) questioned the justification of paying a 2% royalty to the promoter.* Management defended the payment as an approved brand/trademark royalty supported by 75% of non-promoter shareholders, noting an announced cap would be taken to the Board when comfortable.
*   *Analyst (Vansh Munoth) asked why the IPP business is not demerged or spun into an InvIT given peer valuations.* Management stated an InvIT or demerger requires operating scale of 2–3 GW, which they are working toward; exiting at 2.1 GW installed IPP by March 2027 will help achieve that scale.
*   *Analyst (Ajay Assam) asked about awarding wind EPC contracts to group company KP Energy.* Management confirmed hybrid wind scopes are given to KP Energy on a BOM basis to avoid execution risk, but asserted there are no reciprocal solar orders from KP Energy back to KPI Green.
*   *Analyst (Aditya Kumar) questioned how KPI can turn around Alfanar's low revenue to reach the Rs 350–400 crore guidance.* Management explained the operational assets were acquired at an attractive valuation (~Rs 2,400 crore vs normal IPP build cost of Rs 3,750–4,000 crore) and KPI will resolve local operational bottlenecks given promoter expertise in Gujarat wind.

### One-line takeaway
KPI Green is rapidly scaling toward 2.1 GW of installed IPP via acquisitions and execution, accepting short-term PAT dilution from financing costs while managing investor concerns on governance and royalty.
