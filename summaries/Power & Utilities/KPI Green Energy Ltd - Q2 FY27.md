# KPI Green Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue and EBITDA grew YoY, but PAT declined due to higher depreciation and finance costs from asset capitalization, alongside shareholder concerns regarding promoter royalty.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained PAT guidance at around Rs 500 crore for FY27 (previous quarter: expected ~Rs 500 crore); FY28 guidance deferred to Q4.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted toward aggressive inorganic expansion via M&A (Alfanar/Netra wind acquisition) and scaling BESS capabilities, while addressing market valuation and retail investor grievances.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Promoter royalty payments and minority shareholder pushback became major discussion points; geopolitical supply chain headwinds mentioned last quarter were not discussed.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Progressed on IPP expansion targeting 2.1 GW by March 2027 and announced binding offer for Alfanar/Netra.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 741 crore (up 16% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 283 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 38%
- **PAT:** Rs 92 crore (vs Rs 117 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Cash Profit:** Rs 182 crore
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 1,451 crore
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 545 crore
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 187 crore
- **H1 FY27 Cash Profit:** Rs 355 crore

### Highlights
- Total renewable portfolio stands at ~8.33 GW (3.02 GW installed, 5.3 GW under execution); IPP is 3.22 GW and EPC is 5.11 GW.
- Energized 317 MW under the 677 MW GUVNL hybrid IPP project (cumulative 511 MW) and 50 MW under the 350 MW GUVNL solar IPP project (cumulative 301 MW).
- Commissioned 269 MW solar for Coal India's Khavda project and 128 MW for MAHAGENCO in Maharashtra.
- Secured EPC order of ~Rs 2,000 crore for 500 MW solar from NACOF Oorja Private Limited in Rajasthan; order book stands at ~Rs 5,700 crore.
- Entered a binding offer to acquire 100% of Alfanar Energy Private Limited and Netra Wind Private Limited at an enterprise value of ~Rs 2,410 crore, adding 507.9 MW of operational wind assets in Gujarat Kutch.
- Changed statutory auditor to BDO.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 PAT to be around Rs 500 crore.
- Management expects to exit March 2027 with an installed IPP capacity of around 2.1 GW.
- Management expects Sun Drops Energia IPO within the next 18-24 months.
- FY28 guidance will be provided in Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's power sector is in a structural growth phase supported by rising demand, electrification, e-mobility, data centers, and energy security targets (500 GW by 2030).
- Storage is becoming critical to shift surplus daytime power to peak hours; BESS portfolio stands at 565 MW (1,130 MWh) with executed BESPA.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin stood at 38%.
- PAT impacted by higher energization of IPP assets, increasing depreciation and finance costs temporarily until revenue stabilizes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Land bank stands at 8,807 acres with evacuation access at ~5.2 GW.
- Sun Drops Energia is developing an integrated energy storage campus in Gujarat with a BESS assembly plant followed by cell manufacturing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Financial closure achieved for some BESS IPP projects; equity to be raised via pre-IPO rounds and promoter infusion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher depreciation and finance costs from rapid IPP capitalization temporarily suppressing PAT.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Promoter Royalty:** An investor questioned the 2% promoter royalty and high capping, arguing it hurts minority shareholders. Management responded that royalty is standard practice for brand creation, approved by 75% of non-promoter shareholders, and that the promoter intends to reduce it via future board discussions.
- **Valuation Disconnect:** An investor asked why KPI trades at a discount compared to peers valued at Rs 8,000-10,000 crore per gigawatt. Management stated that the company is in a growth phase, expanding to 2.1 GW by March 2027, and the market will eventually recognize the value.
- **Alfanar Acquisition Revenue:** An analyst asked about Alfanar's low historical revenue relative to capacity. Management answered that KP Group's execution expertise in Gujarat will help extract better performance, targeting Rs 350-400 crore in revenue.
- **KP Energy Synergy:** An analyst asked if wind bids go to KP Energy for EPC. Management confirmed KP Energy will execute wind projects on a BOM basis to avoid execution risk, but stated no cross-orders for solar will be given between the companies.
- **Sun Drops Listing:** An analyst asked about the IPO timeline for Sun Drops. Management reiterated a target of 18 months, noting recent completion of a small pre-IPO round.

### One-line takeaway
KPI Green expanded its renewable portfolio to 8.33 GW and pursued a major wind acquisition, while PAT was temporarily compressed by higher depreciation and finance costs from asset capitalization.
