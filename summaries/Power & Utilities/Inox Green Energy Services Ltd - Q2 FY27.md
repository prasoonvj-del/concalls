# Inox Green Energy Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reported sequential operational improvements and completed major strategic acquisitions, but refrained from providing FY27 revenue guidance due to near-term trade-offs from realigning toward equipment supply.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Withdrawn / not provided for FY27 (old guidance was ~75% revenue growth and 20% to 22% EBITDA margins); FY28 revenue target of ~INR 7,500 crores introduced.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from confident re-affirmation of FY27 targets to acknowledging near-term trade-offs and recalibrating short-term growth trajectory. Demand view remains highly positive for wind energy.
- New topics or risks: West Asia war causing temporary commodity and raw material price inflation/cost headwinds; Microsoft Outlook outage delaying financial results uploading. Old risks of legacy EPC disruptions and debtor collections persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Completed the payment and transfer process for Wind World India's ~4.5 GW O&M portfolio in Q2 FY27 via NCLT (delayed from Q1 expectations into Q2, with P&L consolidation starting Q3 FY27). IRSL information memorandum filed, awaiting final exchange approval. 4.X prototype installed with commercial launch targeted by end of FY27.

### Highlights
- Completed the NCLT-approved acquisition and payment transfer of Wind World India's ~4.5 GW O&M portfolio via subsidiary Vibhav Energy Private Limited; line-by-line P&L consolidation starts Q3 FY27.
- Mr. Saibaba Vutukuri joined as Managing Director (Wind Business); Mr. Sanjeev Agarwal (CEO, Inox Wind) resigned for personal reasons, staying as an advisor till December end.
- Inox Green successfully raised INR 300 crores via QIP in October 2026.
- Information Memorandum for the demerged power evacuation infrastructure business (IRSL) filed with stock exchanges, awaiting final listing approvals.
- Strategic realignment underway to increase equipment supply share to ~75% of the third-party order book.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue guidance withdrawn; management expects momentum from Q2 to continue through H2 and into FY28.
- FY28 revenue target set at approximately INR 7,500 crores.
- Long-term consolidated EBITDA margin objective remains ~20%; Inox Green O&M margins expected to be upwards of 45% post-integration.
- MNRE expects ~7.5 GW to 8 GW of wind capacity additions in India in FY27; medium-term annual additions expected to reach 8 GW to 10 GW.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India commissioned 1.8 GW of wind capacity in Q2 FY27 (3.2 GW in H1 FY27 vs 6.05 GW in entire FY26).
- Power demand registered a 15% YoY rise in Q2 FY27, marking the highest H1 power demand in at least four years.
- Renewable tendering in Q2 reached 5.8 GW (standalone wind tenders 36% / 2.1 GW; FDRE RTC tenders 1 GW).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated EBITDA margins suppressed in Q2 due to commodity and raw material price inflation caused by the West Asia war, and because Wind World O&M income was not captured in the P&L prior to consolidation date. Had Wind World income been recognized, consolidated EBITDA margins would have been ~20%.
- Wind World O&M EBITDA margins currently at 25%-30%, targeted to reach upwards of 45% through salary and cost rationalization and value-added services.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rohika facility (Gujarat) fully geared to produce blades for the 4.X platform.
- Inox Renewable Solutions Limited (IRSL) expanding transformer manufacturing capacity into medium-sized (8-20 MVA) and larger transformers, alongside planned high-value-added products like inverters, unit substations, and electronic control systems.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inox Wind debtor levels remained broadly stable compared to March 2026; debtor days targeted to fall below 150 days by the end of FY28.
- Working capital days elevated in H1 due to H2 seasonality (H2 contributes ~70% of annual revenue) and inventory buildup for H2 deliveries, which is expected to moderate by March 2027.
- Inox Green remains virtually asset-light with negligible debt and financial costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity and raw material price inflation driven by geopolitical tensions (West Asia war) creating temporary cost headwinds.
- Near-term trade-offs and forgone revenue opportunities resulting from the strategic shift to prioritize equipment supply and balance sheet quality over order volume.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanchita Sood (RoboCapital) asked about FY28 order inflow targets and reconciliation of FY27 guidance miss.** Management stated they do not target a specific order book size given 2.5–3 years of execution visibility, and confirmed they will not reach prior FY27 guidance due to strategic realignment and letting go of lower-quality orders.
2. **Prit Nagersheth (Prit Nagersheth) asked if Wind World will add ~INR 150 crores to top line in Q3 and inquired about organic wind AUM stagnation.** Management clarified that due to mid-October transaction closure, an additional ~INR 120 crores is expected in Q3, and noted that solar assets were consciously shed to focus on high-margin wind portfolios.
3. **Abhishek Nigam (Institutional Equities) asked for a breakdown of the margin miss and drop in other income.** Management declined exact breakdown specifics, reiterated that annualized margin targets remain intact, and explained that other income was lower because Wind World income was not recognized in Q2 P&L.
4. **Darshil Jhaveri asked how to reconcile the dropping of FY27 revenue growth guidance despite previous affirmations.** Management reiterated that the strategic pivot requires cleaning up the balance sheet and avoiding bad receivables, prioritizing long-term quality over chasing near-term growth.
5. **Rahul Kumar asked about Wind World's current operating margins and post-acquisition equity stake.** Management stated current margins are 25%-30% and targeted to reach 40%+ within 3-4 quarters, and confirmed Inox Green owns 75% of Wind World.

### One-line takeaway
Inox Wind and Inox Green are aggressively prioritizing balance sheet health, asset-light equipment supply, and high-margin O&M expansion—highlighted by the integration of the 4.5 GW Wind World portfolio—despite a near-term revenue growth recalibration in FY27.
