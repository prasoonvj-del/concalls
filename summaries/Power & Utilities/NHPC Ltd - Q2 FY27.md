# NHPC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with revenue up 33% YoY and PAT up 52% YoY, supported by new project commissioning and capacity restoration.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 4,482 Crore (+33% YoY from Rs. 3,365 Crore).
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs. 8,291 Crore (+26% YoY from Rs. 6,579 Crore).
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 1,549 Crore (+52% YoY from Rs. 1,021 Crore).
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs. 2,645 Crore (+27% YoY from Rs. 2,086 Crore).
- **Adjusted PAT (Q2 FY27):** ~Rs. 1,780 Crore (adding back a Rs. 231 Crore provision for Teesta-IV).
- **Generation:** 13,935 MUs in Q2 FY27 (+17% YoY) and 23,741 MUs in H1 FY27 (+14% YoY).
- **Plant Availability Factor (PAF):** 92.24% in Q2 FY27 (vs 78.58% in Q2 FY26) and 86.67% in H1 FY27 (vs 81.10% in H1 FY26).
- **Total Incentives (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 59 Crore (vs Rs. 253 Crore in Q2 FY26).

### Highlights
- Commissioned Unit 5 (250 MW) of Subansiri Lower (2,000 MW) on October 23, 2026, bringing total commissioned capacity to 1,250 MW across 5 units.
- Teesta-V Power Station resumed commercial operations in July 2026 following post-GLOF flood restoration, contributing 859 MUs generation and Rs. 99 Crore PBT in Q2 FY27.
- Parbati-II contributed Rs. 215 Crore PBT in Q2 FY27 (vs a loss of Rs. 164 Crore in Q2 FY26).
- Achieved daylighting of HRT-I at Pakal Dul HEP (1,000 MW) on June 10, 2026; physical progress reached 89%.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Subansiri Lower:** Targeting 2 more units commissioned during FY27 and the final unit by June 2027. Anticipated annual capacity charges (AFC) of ~Rs. 5,500 Crore on full commissioning.
- **Capacity Addition Targets:** Expecting 1,744 MW additions in FY27 (Subansiri, Kiru, Rangit-IV), 1,250 MW in FY28 (Subansiri balance, Pakal Dul), 1,390 MW in FY29, and 1,000 MW in FY30.
- Management expects group-level EBITDA to reach ~Rs. 10,000 Crore post-commissioning of major ongoing projects.
- Annual PAT guided to reach Rs. 6,000 Crore to Rs. 6,400 Crore level by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted shifts in energy consumption patterns over the past year, leading to excess solar power during daytime hours and supply shortfalls during morning and evening peak hours.
- State utilities are re-evaluating long-term 25-year PPAs for vanilla solar energy, leading NHPC to evaluate hybrid renewable configurations (solar + wind + BESS) and Pumped Storage Projects (PSPs).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Generation Expenses:** Reduced to Rs. 277 Crore in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs. 302 Crore in Q2 FY26) due to lower water cess in J&K and a favorable High Court verdict eliminating water cess in Uttarakhand.
- **Finance Cost:** Increased to Rs. 556 Crore in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs. 278 Crore in Q2 FY26) due to interest capitalization ending for commissioned units of Subansiri Lower.
- **Depreciation:** Rose to Rs. 610 Crore in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs. 441 Crore in Q2 FY26) due to Subansiri Lower part-commissioning.
- **Other Expenses:** Included a one-time provision of Rs. 231 Crore related to the Teesta-IV Project.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **FY27 Capex Target:** Rs. 14,300 Crore on a consolidated basis (~Rs. 6,500 Crore achieved till call date; H1 FY27 capex stood at Rs. 5,232 Crore).
- **Run-rate Capex:** Guided at Rs. 14,000 Crore to Rs. 15,000 Crore annually on a consolidated basis over the next few years. 10-year capex outlay estimated at ~Rs. 1,11,000 Crore (equity requirement of ~Rs. 40,000 Crore).
- **Project Progress Updates:**
  - *Dibang (2,880 MW):* Estimated cost Rs. 31,876 Crore; Rs. 5,504 Crore spent till Sep 2026; scheduled completion Feb 2032.
  - *Teesta-VI (500 MW):* 74% physical progress; spent Rs. 5,650 Crore out of Rs. 9,167 Crore; expected completion Sep 2029.
  - *Rangit-IV (120 MW):* 98% physical progress; expected commissioning Dec 2026.
  - *Kiru (624 MW):* 92% physical progress; spent Rs. 4,105 Crore of Rs. 5,409 Crore; targeting reservoir filling in Nov 2026 and full commissioning by Mar 2027.
  - *Pakal Dul (1,000 MW):* 89% physical progress; targeting two units by Mar 2027 and full commissioning by June 2027.
  - *Kwar (540 MW) & Ratle (850 MW):* Physical progress at 42% (completion May 2028) and 33% (completion Nov 2028), respectively. Dam concreting for Ratle started Nov 2, 2026.
  - *Sawalkot (1,800 MW) & Etalin (3,097 MW):* Awaiting CCEA approval; tendering in progress.
  - *Pumped Storage Projects (PSPs):* 18 GW in DPR/PFR stage across Maharashtra, Odisha, MP, Gujarat, and AP. Indira Sagar - Omkareshwar PSP (640 MW, estimated cost ~Rs. 5,200 Crore) tendering initiated.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Consolidated Debt:** ~Rs. 59,000 Crore (Rs. 29,000 Crore in operational projects and Rs. 20,000 Crore in under-construction projects).
- **Trade Receivables:** Stood at Rs. 4,283 Crore as of September 30, 2026 (including Rs. 1,573 Crore unbilled debtors). Net overdue >45 days stood at Rs. 290 Crore as of Sep 30, 2026, which further normalized to Rs. 6 Crore as of November 3, 2026 (total receivables Rs. 1,159 Crore).
- **Liquidity & Equity Funding:** Management expects annual operating cash generation (~Rs. 10,000 Crore by FY28) to fully fund annual debt servicing (~Rs. 3,000 Crore), dividends (~Rs. 1,800 Crore), and internal equity capex requirements (~Rs. 4,000 Crore) without requiring fresh equity issuances.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Solar connectivity/evacuation constraints delaying RE project commissioning (e.g., Gujarat 600 MW delayed to Nov 2027).
- Un-tied RE capacity: NHPC awarded 23,120 MW bids as REIA, but PPAs have been signed for only 9,000 MW due to state reluctance to commit to long-term vanilla solar power.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** *What is the tariff and financial potential of Parbati-II and Subansiri?*  
  **A:** Management stated Parbati-II has design energy of 3,125 MUs, expected annual revenue of ~Rs. 2,400 Crore (at ~Rs. 8.50/unit), and PAT potential of ~Rs. 680 Crore based on 17% ROE on ~Rs. 4,000 Crore regulated equity. Subansiri Lower has regulated equity of ~Rs. 9,000 Crore, generating ~Rs. 5,500 Crore annual AFC and Rs. 1,500–1,600 Crore PAT once fully commissioned.
- **Q:** *Why did Subansiri generate higher profitability in H1 FY27?*  
  **A:** High water availability per unit for fewer commissioned units allowed generation of 300 MUs above proportionate design energy, contributing Rs. 132 Crore PBT in Q2 FY27 and ~Rs. 170 Crore PBT in H1 FY27.
- **Q:** *What is the payment status for the Etalin project transfer from SJVN?*  
  **A:** The ~Rs. 800 Crore consideration has not been paid yet; it will be paid to SJVN once Public Investment Board (PIB) approval is formally received.
- **Q:** *Is transmission connectivity a risk for upcoming mega hydro projects in the Northeast?*  
  **A:** Management confirmed the master evacuation plan by CTU is ready, with lines being awarded via tariff-based competitive bidding aligned to project commissioning timelines.

### One-line takeaway
NHPC demonstrated robust Q2 earnings led by Teesta-V restoration and Parbati-II, with long-term earnings set to expand as 1,744 MW of hydro capacity comes online by FY27.
