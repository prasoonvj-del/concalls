# Powerica Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue growth remains robust, and profitability improved sequentially due to implemented price revisions, despite fresh commodity inflation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained double-digit revenue growth guidance for FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from mixed to more positive on sequential margin recovery and strong high-horsepower/data center traction, despite lingering geopolitical project delays.
- New topics or risks: Fresh round of inflation in key raw materials (steel, copper, aluminum) emerging in Q3 FY27; old risks from Middle East conflict continuing to delay MSLG project execution.
- Last quarter's promises: Progress reported on scaling wind capacity toward 638 MW (received LOI for 50 MW GUVNL project, total 250 MW won in reverse bidding).

### Highlights
- Sequential margin improvement driven by price revisions undertaken earlier in the year.
- Received a letter of intent from GUVNL for a 50 MW wind power project at a tariff of INR 3.51 per KWH under a 25-year PPA.
- Participated in Wind Energy Hamburg 2026.
- MSLG and EPC segments had moderate contributions due to project-driven execution timelines impacted by Middle East crisis.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects double-digit revenue growth through FY27.
- Long-term potential for margin improvement as operating leverage builds, product mix improves, and engineering-led solutions contribute.
- Target to reach 638 MW of wind power capacity and expand up to 1 GW over the long term, adding 100-150 MW annually.
- New wind projects expected to yield 82%-85% EBITDA margins, bringing blended wind margins to 72%-73% over time.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Data center segment witnessing strong demand with operators planning capacities earlier and taking realistic execution timelines.
- Medium and high horsepower (MHP and HHP) segments see strong traction from data centers, manufacturing, real estate, and rental; low horsepower (LHP) remains price-sensitive and competitive.
- Infrastructure demand showing some moderation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fresh round of inflation emerged in Q3 FY27 in steel, copper, and aluminum; earlier price increases have not fully offset the sharp rise in input costs.
- Planning further calibrated price hikes in the current quarter.
- Steady-state margins: Genset consolidated ~9%, wind IPP blended ~62-65% (new projects ~82-85%), EPC ~10%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Secured 250 MW total across recent auctions (100+ MW GUVNL, 50+ MW GUVNL, 100 MW SECI); machines already arriving for the 100 MW GUVNL project.
- Plan to leverage upcoming wind projects with 70% debt.
- Continuing discussions for joint development with GE Vernova.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Practically at zero debt post-IPO for the 330 MW wind business.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility and fresh inflation in raw materials (steel, copper, aluminum) impacting margins with time lags in price pass-through.
- Middle East geopolitical crisis delaying project execution in the MSLG segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) asked about operating margin pressures and H2 outlook.** Management stated Cummins genset business is growing steadily at 14% YoY in Q2, and backed the double-digit growth guidance.
2. **Devanshi Shah (HUF Capital) asked about wind power growth outlook.** Management outlined the path from 330 MW operational to 638 MW using 50 MW under construction and 250 MW won via reverse bidding.
3. **Ankur Periwal asked if current pricing fully covers RM inflation and execution timelines.** Management noted current pricing covers current costs, but constant volatility may require further agile revisions; order execution ranges from 2 days to 6 months for non-data center and 12-18 months for data centers.
4. **Kartik Kohli (Kotak Securities) asked about ex-data center demand and competitive intensity.** Management confirmed manufacturing, real estate, and rental are growing robustly, and competition remains consistent with past years.
5. **Shubham Borade asked about L1 status in MSLG.** Management stated execution is delayed due to attacks and situations in Saudi Arabia/Middle East, but expects movement in 1-2 months.
6. **Amitoj Singh (360 ONE Capital) asked about long-term capital allocation and wind contribution.** Management detailed the roadmap to 1 GW, targeting 100-150 MW additions yearly, leveraging projects at 70% debt, and partnering with GE Vernova.

### One-line takeaway
Powerica delivered a healthy Q2 FY27 with revenue of INR 847 crore and sequential margin recovery, backed by a strong INR 1,950 crore DG set order book and scaling renewable capacity.
