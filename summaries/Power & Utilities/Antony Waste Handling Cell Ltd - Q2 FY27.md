# Antony Waste Handling Cell Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue with strong growth in Collection & Transportation (C&T), though reported margins remain suppressed by temporary accounting provisions and operational downtime.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (20% revenue CAGR over 3-5 years).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "Mixed" to "Positive" due to record revenue and operational scaling.
- **New topics or risks:** New focus on PET bottle collection/baling for FMCG clients; Moshi WtE restoration timeline delayed from October 2026 to December 2026 due to shipping logistics.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The promise to restart the Moshi WtE plant by October 2026 was missed; management now cites December 2026.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 321 crore (+25% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 582 crore (+15% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 58 crore (17.6% margin).
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 103 crore (17.2% margin).
- **C&T Revenue (H1):** Rs 394 crore (+26% YoY).
- **C&T Volumes (Q2):** 0.6 million tonnes (+12% YoY).
- **Total Waste Handled (Q2):** 1.28 million tonnes (+1% YoY).
- **Exceptional Expense:** Rs 20 crore (impairment, restoration, and compensation).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 386 crore (Gross debt Rs 449 crore; Cash Rs 63 crore).
- **DSO:** 114 days.

### Highlights
- Reported highest-ever quarterly revenue, driven by Mumbai operations ramp-up, higher volumes, and cost escalation recognition.
- Moshi WtE facility: Power generation remains suspended; MRF and composting operations have resumed. Restoration expected by December 2026.
- C&D recycling (Mumbai): Volumes rose ~70% YoY with a 96% recycling rate.
- RDF sales: Grew 23% YoY to 49,000 tonnes.
- *Record Check:* The Moshi plant restart date (previously promised for October 2026) has been pushed to December 2026 due to shipping delays.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reiterated 20% revenue CAGR guidance over the next 3-5 years.
- AP waste-to-energy projects: Revenue contribution expected from Q1 FY29.
- Management suggests Rs 290-300 crore as a sustainable quarterly revenue base.

### Demand & industry commentary
- C&T remains the primary growth engine.
- Industry trend: Increasing private sector participation in waste processing.
- New business focus: Expanding into pest management, deep cleaning, and PET bottle collection/baling for FMCG companies.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins impacted by: 1) Expected Credit Loss (ECL) provision of Rs 27 crore on past escalation claims (accounting entry); 2) Absence of high-margin power sales from Moshi.
- Management expects core EBITDA margins to return to historical levels (22-23%) by Q1 FY28.
- Other operating expenses rose 27% due to RDF disposal costs and ECL provisioning.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- AP waste-to-energy projects: Total capex planned at Rs 670-680 crore.
- Mumbai C&T contract: Currently ramping up.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity ratio: 0.4x.
- Weighted average cost of debt: ~10%.
- Working capital: DSO at 114 days; management noted Rs 108 crore collected post-September 30.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Operational risk: Ongoing suspension of power generation at the Moshi WtE facility.
- Accounting volatility: ECL provisions on past claims impacting reported margins.
- Working capital: High DSO and reliance on municipal payment cycles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rajiv Shah (Ascend Capital):** Asked about C&D segment outlook. Management expects full-year revenue of Rs 34-38 crore for this segment.
- **Akshit Shetty:** Questioned high receivables/DSO. Management clarified that Rs 108 crore was collected post-quarter end and DSOs historically range between 90-115 days.
- **Ketan Chheda:** Asked about the Moshi plant delay. Management attributed the delay to Hormuz Strait shipping embargoes affecting parts delivery; now confirmed for December.
- **Nitesh (Retail Investor):** Asked about market cap erosion and buybacks. Management highlighted the maiden dividend payment and focus on long-term value creation through technical partnerships (MIT, JFE).

### One-line takeaway
Record revenue growth driven by C&T and new projects, though reported margins remain suppressed by temporary accounting provisions and the ongoing suspension of the Moshi WtE plant.
