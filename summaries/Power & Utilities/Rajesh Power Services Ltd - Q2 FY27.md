# Rajesh Power Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-08 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 performance with healthy growth in revenue and profitability, supported by execution capabilities and order inflows.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 929 crore (up 46% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 112 crore (up 33% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 12%
- **PAT:** Rs 79 crore (up 35% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 8.5%
- **H1FY27 Order Inflows:** Rs 1,250 crore
- **Unexecuted Order Book (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 3,647 crore
- **October Order Inflows:** Rs 450 crore
- **Total Pipeline / Bid Pipeline:** Rs 3,500 to 4,000 crore
- **Cumulative Installations (H1FY27):** 17,000+ Ring Main Units, 6,500+ Distribution Transformers, 11,400 km LT/HT/11kV cables, 35,000 km medium-voltage covered conductor, 104 substations, 2,700+ km extra-high-voltage cables

### Highlights
- Management (Utsav Panchal) highlighted growth driven by distribution execution, expansion into higher voltage transmission, and entry into the BESS segment.
- Achieved positive cash flow from operating activities during H1 FY27.
- Net worth grew more than 8x from Rs 59 crore in FY23 to Rs 484 crore in H1 FY27.
- Secured an EPC order of Rs 212 crore from Odisha Power Transmission Corporation Limited (OPTCL) in June 2026, marking entry into Eastern India.
- Established a 100% wholly owned subsidiary in New Zealand to explore international electrical infra EPC opportunities.
- Strategic investment in HKRP Innovations Limited for smart grid, IoT, and SCADA-based digital energy management solutions.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 40%.
- Management maintained full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 11% to 12% and PAT margin guidance of 8% to 9%.
- Management targets closing order book of around Rs 5,000 crore for FY27.
- Long-term 40% growth guidance remains unchanged.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's power sector entering a multi-year investment cycle with demand rising across industries, infrastructure, and data centres.
- Green Energy Corridor Phase 3 (GEC-III) approved with an outlay of Rs 1.86 lakh crore (Rs 1.36 lakh crore for intrastate transmission and Rs 50,000 crore for 50 GW BESS).
- National Electricity Policy 2047 targets 100% undergrounding of cables in towns with population >10 lakh; Gujarat Wire-Free City Mission targets 46,000 ckm of undergrounding by 2030.
- India's BESS requirement projected to reach ~47 GW by 2032.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin maintained at 12% due to improved operating efficiency and effective cost management.
- Working capital management improved through extended trade terms from traders and suppliers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Virpur, Gujarat: 65 MW / 130 MWh standalone BESS project represents entry into utility-scale battery storage.
- New Zealand subsidiary established to evaluate electrical infra EPC opportunities.
- Targeting entry into 765 kV voltage level substations after commissioning ongoing 400 kV projects.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Achieved positive cash flow from operating activities.
- Other financial assets under current assets increased due to current retention money.
- Trade credit from suppliers increased, aiding working capital management.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management, though volatility in import prices was noted by analysts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vishwendra Singh (Prudent Equity) on HKRP share of profit and subsidiary:** Management stated HKRP will be H2-heavy and achieve targeted margins by year-end; order book contains no HKRP orders. For NZ subsidiary, it is a 100% WOS evaluating EPC opportunities.
2. **Atul Kumar (Individual Investor) on working capital items and HKRP standalone targets:** Management clarified that current financial assets include retention money and other current assets include unbilled revenue (~Rs 38 crore). HKRP FY27 standalone revenue target is ~Rs 150 crore with 20% PAT margin.
3. **Maitri Shah (Sapphire Capital) on data centre and BESS opportunities:** Management stated data centre opportunities are on an RFQ basis (connecting grid to data centres and internal DC solutions). BESS bidding is purely on an EPC model, not BOT.
4. **Nikhil Kanodia (Sunidhi Securities) on key order wins and margins:** Management highlighted major wins from PGVCL (Rs 800-900 crore), RVPNL, and OPTCL (Rs 212 crore), reiterating 11-12% EBITDA guidance.
5. **Rishab Agarwal (Suraag Capital) on win rate and order book execution timeline:** Management stated win rate is 50-60% across geographies and execution timeline for unexecuted order book is 18-24 months.
6. **Bijal Shah (RTL Investments) on receivables vs revenue growth:** Management explained that execution momentum increases post-monsoon and higher execution near period-end boosted trade receivables, alongside wins in trade credit from suppliers.

### One-lake takeaway
Rajesh Power delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 46% revenue growth and positive operating cash flow, maintaining its 40% growth guidance backed by a robust order book and expansion into transmission, BESS, and smart grids.
