# EMS Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reports sequential recovery in revenue and profits, citing strong order book growth and the resolution of previous execution bottlenecks.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained; management continues to target matching FY25 revenue levels in FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "recovery phase" to "growth phase," with specific targets of 30-35% QoQ revenue growth for H2.
- **New topics:** Pledge reduction (from Rs 200 crore to Rs 60 crore).
- **Old topics:** Monsoon impact is now described as "mitigated" by shifting focus to campus-based work, whereas it was a primary concern in Q1.

### Key numbers
- **Operating Income:** Rs 160.89 crore.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 35.82 crore (Margin: 22.26%).
- **PBT:** Rs 30.72 crore.
- **PAT:** Rs 22.92 crore (Margin: 14.25%).
- **Order Book:** Rs 3,152 crore (as of 30th Sept 2026).
- **Order Book Composition:** Rs 2,619 crore CAPEX; Rs 530 crore O&M.
- **New Order Inflow (Q2):** ~Rs 900 crore.

### Highlights
- Management reported a 30% increase in operating income and 50% increase in EBITDA and PAT sequentially.
- Secured Rs 900 crore in new orders from Delhi Jal Board, PHED Rajasthan, and UP Jal Nigam (Urban).
- Pledge reduction: Promoter pledge reduced from Rs 200 crore to Rs 60 crore; management aims for zero pledge by end of FY27.
- Order book distribution: 85% Water/Wastewater, 10% Building construction, 2% Electricity, 1.49% Others.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to match FY25 revenue levels in FY27.
- Targets 30-35% QoQ revenue growth for Q3 and Q4.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to reach ~25% by Q4.
- Targeting a consistent order book of Rs 3,000 crore to support Rs 1,200 crore annual revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry opportunity estimated at Rs 1 lakh crore per annum for the next 10-15 years in urban water/sewage.
- Actively bidding in Karnataka and other states; company remains "choosy" to maintain better margins than peers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin for the quarter was 22.26%.
- Fixed costs (manpower/machinery) are stable; management expects margins to expand as revenue scales.
- "Other expenses" (consultancy, soil exploration) spiked due to new project wins but are project-specific, not recurring.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No company-level CAPEX planned for FY27, excluding machinery.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables: Rs 450 crore (includes unbilled debtors).
- Uttarakhand payment delays (due to portal changes/pro-rata funding) are now streamlined.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution risks: Heavy rainfall remains a risk, though mitigated by focusing on campus-based works (STP/WTP) rather than network laying during monsoon.
- Payment delays: Occasional delays in state-funded projects due to portal/funding changes, though management noted UP and Uttarakhand are now normalized.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ishita Lodha (Collections):** Asked about UP/Uttarakhand payment status. Management confirmed UP is normalized and Uttarakhand is streamlined after portal changes.
2. **Rahul Kumar (Order book execution):** Asked about execution timeline. Management stated a 40% conversion factor (order book to annual revenue) and noted new orders take 5-6 months to start.
3. **Dhruv Hingorani (Revenue gap vs guidance):** Questioned the 23.3% revenue growth vs previous guidance. Management clarified that investors should look at standalone figures (which grew 30%) rather than consolidated, and reiterated H2 growth targets.
4. **Sunil Shukla (Pledge):** Asked about pledge status. Management confirmed reduction from Rs 200 crore to Rs 60 crore, targeting zero by year-end.

### One-line takeaway
EMS Ltd is in a growth phase, leveraging a Rs 3,152 crore order book to target 30-35% QoQ revenue growth in H2, supported by normalized collections and reduced promoter pledging.
