# Advait Energy Transitions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong revenue and EBITDA growth, robust order book visibility, and steady progress on upcoming manufacturing expansions.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations for Q2 FY27: Rs 221 crore (up 40.8% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 32 crore (up 84.4% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin: 14.5% (vs 11% in Q2 FY26)
- Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 19 crore (up 88.5% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 PAT margin: 8.7% (vs 6.5% in Q2 FY26)
- Consolidated revenue from operations for H1 FY27: Rs 400 crore (up 45.4% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 57 crore (up 82.6% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 14.2% (vs 11.3% in H1 FY26)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 34 crore (up 77.9% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT margin: 8.5% (vs 6.9% in H1 FY26)
- Consolidated order book (including L1 positions) as of September 30, 2026: Rs 1,575 crore (up 47% YoY)
- H1 FY27 order execution value: Rs 400 crore
- Consolidated net worth: Rs 326 crore
- Gross borrowings: Rs 280 crore
- Debt-to-equity ratio: 0.86x (vs 0.42x as of March 2026)
- Incremental capex in H1 FY27: Rs 101 crore
- Anticipated H2 FY27 capex: Rs 140 crore (with Rs 100 crore funded through debt)

### Highlights
- Gangad (Gujarat) Phase 1 integrated manufacturing facility is on track for operationalization by Q4 FY27.
- Final plant and machinery orders placed for the 2.5 GWh BESS manufacturing facility (expandable up to 5 GWh); operational target by Q4 FY27.
- Secured DGVCL distribution-related orders of about Rs 52 crore, multiple orders from PGVCL, and milestone-specific orders for ERS, OPGW, and stringing tools.
- Secured a new order for a 150 MW / 300 MWh standalone BESS system in Junagadh, Gujarat.
- Conducted live testing of the 5 MW electrolyser program, engaging with over 13 Indian green hydrogen players.
- Entered into a battery cell off-take agreement with HiTHIUM for the BESS order pipeline.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a long-term top-line target of Rs 5,000 crore over 5 years.
- Expects H2 FY27 revenue growth of 55% to 60% compared to last year, aiming to cross Rs 1,000 crore for the full year.
- Targets group EBITDA margin of 15% once the manufacturing setup is established, with annual improvements of 1% to 1.5%.
- Order book mix projected to shift to a 50:50 split between PTS and NRE over the next 2 to 3 years.
- Manufacturing share of total operations is targeted at 70% to 75% by FY28-FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's transmission infrastructure investments, grid modernization, and renewable energy integration are driving demand across PTS product segments.
- Green hydrogen unit economics are gradually improving, with green ammonia prices expected to come at par with gray ammonia around 2027-2028.
- BESS market demand is accelerating, driven by public tenders and developer inquiries.
- International export markets (Japan, Europe) and domestic public tenders have mandates excluding equipment from China.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Core product (manufacturing and EPC) EBITDA margins stand between 17% and 18% on a standalone basis.
- NRE segment EBITDA margins are currently in the 7% to 8% range, expected to build up by 30% to 50% over the next 2 years.
- Inflation impacts have been mitigated through advance raw material and resource bookings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total H1 FY27 capex stood at Rs 101 crore; expected H2 FY27 capex is Rs 140 crore.
- Gangad Phase 1 and 2.5 GWh BESS facilities scheduled to be operational by Q4 FY27.
- Electrolyser capacity expansion planned for 150 MW to be operational in Q4 FY27 (out of total 300 MW Phase 2), with potential to scale to 1 GW by FY28-FY29.
- Peak annual revenue potential for 150 MW electrolyser capacity is estimated between Rs 600 crore to Rs 1,000 crore.
- Peak annual revenue potential for BESS is estimated at Rs 1,000 crore to Rs 1,100 crore per GWh.
- Fully running PTS manufacturing capacity is estimated to yield sales of over Rs 2,500 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Public sector clients comprise roughly 60%-65% of the order book, and private clients make up 40%-35%.
- Long-term steady-state debt-to-equity ratio for manufacturing and solutions business is projected around 0.5x to 0.7x.
- Company is considering raising equity funds in Q3 or Q4 via a capital raise to ease working capital and cash flow requirements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic factors beyond industry control can impact sector growth and targets.
- High initial capex for fuel cells compared to DG sets creates adoption timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Monish Nawani asked about the conviction behind the Rs 5,000 crore 5-year top-line target.** Management stated that historical 50%-60% CAGRs, qualification for larger project sizes, and strong investor/funding support underpin this vision.
2. **Naman Parmar inquired about the shift from EPC to manufacturing in the order book mix.** Management clarified that current execution is ~60%-62% EPC and ~38% manufacturing, but post-expansion (by FY28-FY29), manufacturing will constitute 70% to 75%.
3. **Garvit Goyal questioned the sustainability of 14% EBITDA margins amid inflation.** Management noted they are looking forward to similar numbers as raw material price risks are pre-booked.
4. **Ankit Gupta asked about current capacity utilization and revenue expectations for the 30 MW electrolyser facility.** Management explained the 30 MW line acts as a demonstration facility, while commercial scale-up is aligned with the 150 MW operational plan for Q4 FY27.
5. **Shashank Jha asked about BESS capacity utilization and revenue potential.** Management stated they expect 40% to 60% capacity utilization in the first year of the BESS plant.

### One-line takeaway
Advait Energy Transitions delivered strong H1 FY27 financial growth (revenue up 45% YoY) and is scaling up its manufacturing infrastructure across PTS, BESS, and Green Hydrogen to transition towards a product-led model.
