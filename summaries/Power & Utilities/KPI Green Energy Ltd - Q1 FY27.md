# KPI Green Energy Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue and EBITDA grew, but PAT declined due to higher depreciation and interest costs; management cited geopolitical headwinds impacting EPC margins.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 710 crore (up 16% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 262 crore (up 21% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 37% (up from 35%).
*   **PAT:** Rs 95 crore (vs Rs 111 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Cash Profit:** Rs 176 crore (up 6% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Scale:** Total portfolio reached ~6.94 GW (1.87 GW installed, 5.07 GW WIP).
*   **Operational Growth:** IPP portfolio generation grew ~4x YoY.
*   **Leadership Changes:** Appointed Prof. Sunil Maheshwari (Vice Chairman), Rajesh Shrivastava (Whole-Time Director), and Kapil Kriplani (incoming Group CFO).
*   **Subsidiary Performance:** Sun Drops Energia reported Rs 150-155 crore revenue and Rs 26 crore PAT.
*   **Governance:** Changed auditors to BDO.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects full earnings contribution from newly commissioned assets to build through the remainder of FY27.
*   Management expects to maintain 30%-40% YoY growth, though noted potential for 50%-60% if geopolitical conditions settle.
*   Management expects absolute PAT to grow YoY for FY27, though margins may be lower than previous guidance due to seasonality and interest/depreciation costs.
*   Management expects IPP segment revenue to exceed Rs 1,000 crore annually at full run rate.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong demand for "double engine" IPP + CPP business model.
*   Data centers identified as an "opportunistic horizon" in the RE sector.
*   Geopolitical conditions impacting supply chain (cables, steel, logistics, ROW issues), affecting EPC margins.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin improved to 37% (from 35%).
*   PAT impacted by higher depreciation and finance costs on a rapidly growing asset base.
*   Geopolitical tensions causing cost pressures on EPC business (cables, MMS structures, logistics).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity:** Commissioned 0.85 GW in the past year; booked 2.88 GW fresh orders.
*   **Assets:** Strategic land bank of 8,657 acres; power evacuation capacity of 5.10 GW.
*   **International:** Signed MOU for 5 GW in Botswana (500 MW phase planning underway); executing solar + BESS project in UAE.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Debt-to-equity expected to remain within 3:1 (long-term debt to net worth).
*   Successfully listed green bond of Rs 670 crore (5-year, 8.50% coupon).
*   Inventory levels have decreased from March 2026 levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical conditions causing supply chain bottlenecks and cost inflation (cables, steel, logistics).
*   Seasonality (monsoon) impacting renewable generation in Q2.
*   High depreciation and interest costs on new IPP assets temporarily impacting PAT.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Analyst (Shrenik Mehta) questioned the strain on the balance sheet due to IPP expansion and negative EPS growth.* Management responded that IPP provides long-term, sustainable 85%-90% EBITDA margins and that the EPS dip is a temporary phase of the project life cycle.
*   *Analyst (Aman Soni) asked about the slowdown in execution vs. guidance.* Management attributed it to billing delays for utility-scale projects (Coal India, Adani, Aditya Birla) and a conservative outlook due to geopolitical factors.
*   *Analyst (Garvit Goyal) questioned the math on IPP profitability vs. interest/depreciation.* Management clarified that depreciation on 25-year plants is lower (5%) than the 15% assumed by the analyst, and revenue will scale up.
*   *Analyst (Nishant) asked about the disconnect between business growth and share price.* Management highlighted the appointment of BDO as auditor and promoter stake increases as confidence-building measures.

### One-line takeaway
KPI Green is aggressively scaling its IPP portfolio, which is temporarily suppressing PAT due to high interest and depreciation, while EPC margins face headwinds from geopolitical supply chain issues.
