# Power & Utilities: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the sector report **strong demand growth (10–15% YoY)** driven by industrial activity, data centres, EVs and cooling loads, with all-India peak demand touching 271 GW. Renewable capacity additions are accelerating (national non-fossil capacity crossed 300 GW), but **transmission evacuation delays** (especially in Khavda, Rajasthan) remain the primary bottleneck, curtailing solar output and pushing CODs. Battery energy storage (BESS) is moving from pilot to **mainstream procurement** – discoms are shifting from RTC thermal tenders to peak-power BESS contracts on DEEP, and SECI’s 4 GW/16 GWh VGF tender signals policy support. Thermal players (Adani Power, NTPC, JSW Energy) continue to expand baseload capacity citing data-centre needs, while renewable IPPs (ACME, Adani Green, Juniper, CleanMax) are locking long-term FDRE/BESS PPAs. EPC/manufacturing order books are at record highs (Transrail ₹14.2k cr, Waaree ₹5.5k cr, Rajesh Power ₹3.6k cr), but margin pressure persists from commodity inflation, land/ROW costs and fixed-price contracts.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian utilities historically made money by owning thermal/hydro generation assets and selling power under 25-year PPAs to discoms. Over the last ~5 years the model has shifted toward **renewable asset ownership (solar/wind), hybrid FDRE contracts, group-captive/C&I offtake, and integrated EPC+OEM manufacturing**. Storage (BESS/pumped hydro) and data-centre-driven demand are the newest revenue levers. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **FDRE + BESS becoming the standard product** – Discoms and corporates now demand firm, dispatchable renewable power with storage. ACME Solar has >12 GWh BESS targeted for FY27 and 50 GWh by 2030; Adani Green operates 6.6 GWh ( >50% of India’s operational BESS) targeting 10 GWh FY27; Juniper Green guiding 1.7 GWh BESS FY27; CleanMax reports 41–42% volumes from hyperscalers with CTU hybrid projects; NTPC floating 40 GWh ICB tender. **Stage: scaling/mainstream** – matters because it unlocks higher tariffs, reduces curtailment risk and creates long-term contracted revenue visibility. [ACME Solar concall Q2 FY27; Adani Green concall Q2 FY27; Juniper Green concall Q2 FY27; CleanMax concall Q2 FY27; NTPC concall Q2 FY27]
- **Transmission build-out as enabler and investment theme** – Green Energy Corridor Phase-III (₹1.36 lakh cr intrastate + ₹50k cr BESS) and 22 planned HVDC projects (127 GW) are driving massive TBCB opportunity. Power Grid’s order book ₹2.2 lakh cr (TBCB ₹1.95 lakh cr); Transrail Lighting cites ₹9.15 lakh cr transmission opportunity to 2032; Rajesh Power and Suzlon reference GEC-III. **Stage: scaling** – matters because evacuation readiness determines RE commissioning timelines and creates multi-year EPC/equipment revenue. [Power Grid concall Q2 FY27; Transrail Lighting concall Q2 FY27; Rajesh Power concall Q2 FY27; Suzlon concall Q2 FY27]
- **C&I / Group-captive / Hyperscaler demand driving contracting** – Data centres and industrial consumers are signing long-term PPAs for 100% renewable power. CleanMax: 85% repeat business, 27–28% capacity from hyperscalers; JSW Energy targets group-captive portfolio 8.5 GW by 2030 (from 3.7 GW); ACME Solar 92% of 8.37 GW contracted; Adani Green highlights FDRE/RTC demand from data centres/AI. **Stage: mainstream** – matters because it diversifies off-taker risk away from stressed discoms and supports premium pricing. [CleanMax concall Q2 FY27; JSW Energy concall Q2 FY27; ACME Solar concall Q2 FY27; Adani Green concall Q2 FY27]
- **Manufacturing localisation & backward integration** – PLI and DCR/ALMM policies are driving domestic module, cell, blade, conductor and BESS manufacturing. Suzlon adding 3 AI-enabled blade factories (4.5 GW capacity); Waaree expanding T&D manufacturing to 1 lakh MT; KPI Green’s Sun Drops targeting 5 GWh BESS assembly by FY28 and 10 GWh cell by FY31; Advait building 2.5 GWh BESS plant; Transrail producing carbon-core HTLS conductors with Epsilon. **Stage: early/scaling** – matters because it captures margin across value chain, reduces import dependence and qualifies for domestic content tenders. [Suzlon concall Q2 FY27; Waaree concall Q2 FY27; KPI Green concall Q2 FY27; Advait concall Q2 FY27; Transrail Lighting concall Q2 FY27]
- **Hydro / Pumped storage revival for peaking and storage** – NHPC targeting 1,744 MW FY27 additions (Subansiri, Kiru, Rangit-IV) and 18 GW PSP pipeline; Adani Power acquired 330 MW GVK hydro + 770 MW Bhutan JV, targeting 5 GW hydro; Tata Power approved 1,800 MW Shirwata PSP (₹11.8k cr) and 1,000 MW Bhivpuri; NTPC 444 MW hydro FY28. **Stage: early** – matters because hydro/PSP provides long-duration storage and grid stability, complementing intermittent RE and BESS. [NHPC concall Q2 FY27; Adani Power concall Q2 FY27; Tata Power concall Q2 FY27; NTPC concall Q2 FY27]
- **Thermal capacity still growing for baseload** – Despite RE push, Adani Power building 24 GW thermal pipeline (45 GW by FY32); NTPC has 17.32 GW thermal under construction + 4.8 GW new tenders; JSW Energy executing Salboni 3.2 GW and KSK 1.8 GW. **Stage: mainstream but contested** – matters because thermal provides firm power for data centres and winter peaks, but faces financing and carbon-transition risks. [Adani Power concall Q2 FY27; NTPC concall Q2 FY27; JSW Energy concall Q2 FY27]

**Government push**
- **Green Energy Corridor Phase-III** – ₹1.36 lakh cr intrastate transmission + ₹50k cr for 50 GWh BESS by FY2032-33. Rewards transmission EPC, equipment makers, RE developers with evacuation certainty. Beneficiaries: Power Grid, Transrail, Rajesh Power, Suzlon, Waaree. [Govt: MoP, Aug 2026]
- **PM Surya Sarovar Yojana** – 5 GW floating solar with 2-hour co-located storage. Rewards floating solar EPC and BESS integration. Beneficiaries: ACME Solar, Waaree, KPI Green. [Govt: MNRE, Nov 2026]
- **SECI 4 GW / 16 GWh VGF tender** – ₹15 lakh/MWh VGF, 12-month commissioning. Rewards BESS developers with upfront capital support. Beneficiaries: ACME Solar, Adani Green, Juniper Green, NTPC. [Govt: SECI, Nov 2026]
- **CTUIL non-ROFR BESS connectivity** – Allows standalone BESS to connect without right-of-first-refusal. Rewards merchant/peak-power BESS models. Beneficiaries: ACME Solar, Juniper Green. [Govt: MoP, Nov 2026]
- **Maharashtra 4,000 MWh BESS tariffs** – Approved tariffs for 2 GW/4 GWh procurement. Rewards storage developers in Maharashtra. Beneficiaries: Tata Power, Adani Green, JSW Energy. [Govt: Maharashtra, Nov 2026]
- **UP/MP joint 500 MW pumped storage procurement** – Rewards hydro/PSP developers. Beneficiaries: NHPC, Adani Power, Tata Power. [Govt: UP/MP, Nov 2026]
- **ISTS-renewable shift to intra-state connectivity** – MoP allows ISTS projects to use intra-state lines. Rewards developers facing CTU delays. Beneficiaries: ACME Solar, JSW Energy, CleanMax. [Govt: MoP, Nov 2026]
- **Rajasthan 80% EV infra cost support** – Rewards charging infrastructure and distribution utilities. Beneficiaries: Power Grid, Transrail, Rajesh Power. [Govt: Rajasthan, Nov 2026]

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: All-India power demand grew **10–15% YoY in H1 FY27** (Adani Power: 11.5%, JSW: 11.1%, NTPC: 10.2%, Inox Wind: 15%). Peak demand hit 271 GW (NTPC). Key drivers: **data centres/AI hyperscalers** (Adani Green, ACME Solar, CleanMax, JSW Energy, Tata Power), industrial growth, EV adoption, cooling/agricultural load from weak monsoon. **Discoms shifting procurement on DEEP portal from RTC thermal/gas to short/medium-term peak-power BESS contracts** (ACME Solar). Hyperscalers prefer CTU-connected wind-solar hybrids in South India (CleanMax).

**Divergence**: 
- **Thermal vs FDRE+BESS for baseload** – Adani Power, NTPC, JSW Energy argue thermal essential for 24/7 data-centre load; ACME, Adani Green, Juniper, CleanMax say FDRE+BESS can replace thermal RTC.
- **Curtailment severity** – Tata Power reports 11% RE curtailment in Q2 (up from 8.8% Q1); Adani Green cites Khavda curtailment lowering solar CUF to 23%; Juniper Green 2.5% revenue impact; NHPC notes solar connectivity constraints. ACME Solar says regulatory update now allows curtailed solar to charge BESS for merchant sale.
- **Discom payment health** – PTC India notes lower net surcharge income due to improved discom liquidity; Orient Green says collections improved significantly; but Antony Waste DSO 114 days, Rajesh Power notes trade receivables buildup.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Solar modules**: ACME Solar – DCR ALMM 2 quotes <₹17/unit for mid-2027 delivery; Juniper Green – non-DCR ₹12, DCR ~₹18 for large utility; Waaree – margin compression from T&D consolidation (consolidated EBITDA 15.1%).
- **Battery costs**: ACME Solar – lithium carbonate prices down, 6% China export tax from Jan 2027, copper up; net BESS capex sub-₹1 cr/MWh (brownfield ₹90L–₹1 cr). Juniper Green – lithium carbonate 170k→100k yuan/ton, expects 1–2% net softening post China incentive removal.
- **Commodity inflation**: Powerica – fresh steel/copper/aluminum inflation in Q3 FY27, planning calibrated price hikes. Transrail – domestic jobs fully hedged, international with price variation. K.P. Energy – volatile labour, idling expenses, higher ROW compensation squeezing margins to ~12%. Suzlon – non-pass-through copper/aluminum/carbon, FX/commodity ~50 bps impact.
- **Margins**: Renewable generation EBITDA margins high (ACME 86.7%, Adani Green 92%, Juniper 91%, NHPC hydro). EPC/manufacturing lower: Transrail 11.6%, Suzlon 14.8%, Advait 14.5%, Rajesh Power 12%, Waaree 15.1%. Thermal: Adani Power fuel cost +41.8% YoY (imported coal ~50% higher), continuing EBITDA +18.5%. NTPC coal stock 5.2 MMT (7 days), captive dispatch +21% YoY.
