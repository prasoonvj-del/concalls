# Power & Utilities: Q2 FY27 Sector Overview

The Q2 FY27 earnings season reveals a sector in the midst of a structural transition. While demand remains robust, driven by industrial growth and the burgeoning data center economy, the primary narrative has shifted from "capacity addition" to "grid integration" and "dispatchable power." Managements are increasingly focused on BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) to solve intermittency, though transmission bottlenecks remain the single largest operational headwind.

## Big picture
The sector is pivoting toward firm, dispatchable renewable energy. **ACME Solar** and **Juniper Green Energy** are aggressively scaling BESS as a "cash engine" to replace traditional thermal procurement. **Adani Green Energy (AGEL)** and **JSW Energy** are successfully integrating thermal and renewable portfolios to lower costs and improve reliability. Meanwhile, **Power Grid** and **NTPC** are managing the massive infrastructure backbone required to support this transition, though grid connectivity delays are forcing some players to recalibrate their short-term commissioning targets.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is universal agreement on strong demand. **IEX** reported a 11.5% YoY growth in power demand for Q2, while **JSW Energy** noted that long-term demand CAGR of 5–6% may prove conservative.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Adani Power** has shifted its narrative from "summer heatwave" demand to "structural baseload demand," specifically highlighting data centers as "significant power guzzlers."
    *   **IEX** noted that while demand is high, high prices led to a temporary shift toward bilateral contracts over exchange volumes, though this is correcting.
    *   **ACME Solar** observes a distinct shift in state procurement: DISCOMs are moving away from expensive, inflexible RTC (Round-the-Clock) thermal procurement toward BESS-based peak power.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Margins:** Generally robust. **AGEL** maintained industry-leading EBITDA margins of 92%, and **Juniper Green Energy** reported 91.4%. **Waaree Renewable** saw margin compression following the consolidation of its T&D subsidiary, which operates at lower margins (9-10%).
*   **Fuel & Logistics:** **Adani Power** flagged a time lag in imported coal price pass-throughs, which could impact margins. **Concord Enviro** continues to face logistical uncertainties and sea-shipping delays out of the Middle East.
*   **Interest Rates:** Most players are successfully managing debt. **JSW Energy** reduced debt costs by 51 bps YoY to 8.28%, and **Juniper Green Energy** refinanced debt at <8%. **CleanMax** raised green bonds at ~8.6% to lock in fixed rates.

## Capex & capacity
*   **The Grid Bottleneck:** This is the sector's primary friction point. **NTPC** revised its FY27 renewable capacity addition guidance downward to ~8 GW (from 9.5 GW) specifically due to transmission constraints. **AGEL** and **Juniper Green Energy** also reported curtailment issues due to grid delays.
*   **Expansion:** **Power Grid** raised its capex guidance to Rs 40,000 crore, citing massive integration needs. **ACME Solar** raised its 2030 renewable target to 15 GW. **JSW Energy** added 1.7 GW of capacity since April and is moving forward with its 3.2 GW Salboni thermal project.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Shifts:** **ACME Solar** raised its FY27 BESS commissioning target (>12 GWh). Conversely, **NTPC** cut its RE capacity guidance due to transmission delays.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Adani Power** moved from a "heatwave" focus to a "data center/baseload" focus. **Concord Enviro** shifted from "cautious" to "confident" as execution normalized.
*   **Delivery:** **Power Grid** delivered on its promise of data center readiness (Manesar facility). **Concord Enviro** delivered on its promise of execution normalization.

## What to watch next quarter
1.  **Transmission/Grid Connectivity:** Will NTPC, AGEL, and Juniper see relief in grid availability?
2.  **BESS Execution:** Monitoring the actual commissioning of BESS projects vs. targets (ACME, Juniper, Waaree).
3.  **Data Center Demand:** Tracking if the "data center" narrative translates into concrete PPA signings for Adani Power and Power Grid.
4.  **Market Coupling:** Any regulatory movement on market coupling for IEX.
5.  **PFS Monetization:** Progress on the PTC India stake sale in PFS.
