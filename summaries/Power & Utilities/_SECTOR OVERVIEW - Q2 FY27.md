# Power & Utilities: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is characterized by a "growth-at-all-costs" mindset, fueled by structural power demand and a massive pivot toward Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). However, a clear divergence has emerged: while demand is robust, the physical grid infrastructure is failing to keep pace. Managements are increasingly vocal about transmission and GNA (General Network Access) delays acting as the primary bottleneck for commissioning. The "BESS-as-a-cash-engine" narrative is now mainstream, with companies like ACME Solar, Waaree, and GK Energy aggressively integrating storage into their business models.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is universal agreement that power demand is surging. **Adani Power** and **NTPC** cite structural baseload demand, while **IEX** reports a 10.2% YoY growth in H1 electricity demand.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Data Centers:** **Adani Power** and **Clean Max** identify data centers and AI compute factories as the next "significant power guzzlers," with Clean Max noting that hyperscalers are actively seeking CTU-connected hybrid projects.
    *   **Market Volatility:** **IEX** highlights that high demand has led to significant price volatility, with average Day-Ahead Market (DAM) prices rising 46% YoY. Conversely, **PTC India** warns that gas-based power is becoming prohibitively expensive (costing ~Rs 30/unit) due to the Middle East crisis, forcing a reliance on other sources.
    *   **Scheme Dependency:** **GK Energy** remains optimistic about PM KUSUM, expecting a 35-40% growth even if the scheme is delayed, but acknowledges that technical discussions are currently slowing rollout schedules.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Commodity & Supply Chain:** **Suzlon** flagged that the West Asia crisis is driving FX and commodity volatility (copper, aluminum, carbon) that cannot be fully passed through, impacting margins. **ACME Solar** and **Juniper Green** are monitoring the 6% Chinese export tax on batteries, though they remain confident in pricing.
*   **Battery Economics:** **Waaree** is navigating the US Section 232 framework, noting that local US manufacturing yields ~8-9 cents/W EBITDA, significantly higher than export margins. **NTPC** is managing coal supply constraints, with stock levels at critical 3-day levels at several stations.
*   **Interest Rates:** **Clean Max** noted that a 100 bps repo rate hike only impacts borrowing costs by ~50 bps due to their fixed-rate debt strategy.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Aggressive Expansion:** The scale of ambition is massive. **Adani Power** is targeting 45 GW by 2032; **ACME Solar** raised its 2030 target to 15 GW (with 50 GWh BESS).
*   **The Grid Bottleneck:** This is the sector's "elephant in the room." **NTPC** explicitly revised its FY27 renewable capacity addition guidance *downward* (from 9.5 GW to ~8 GW) due to transmission constraints. **Juniper Green** and **Clean Max** also reported revenue impacts due to transmission line delays (e.g., Bikaner grid congestion).

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Shifts:** **ACME Solar** raised its FY27 BESS commissioning target (from 10 GWh to >12 GWh). Conversely, **NTPC** lowered its RE capacity addition guidance due to grid delays.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Adani Power** shifted its narrative from "summer heatwave" (Q1) to "structural baseload demand" (Q2). **ACME Solar** adopted a more aggressive growth stance, labeling BESS a "cash engine."
*   **Accounting:** **Adani Power** adopted a conservative stance, reversing Rs 1,970 crore in revenue claims related to Haryana DISCOMs.
*   **Execution:** **Suzlon** reorganized project execution by appointing regional chief project officers to accelerate commissioning.

*   **IEX:** Steady volume growth; focusing on technology and market coupling.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Grid Resolution:** Will the transmission delays cited by **NTPC**, **Juniper Green**, and **Clean Max** show signs of easing?
*   **BESS Scaling:** Execution speed of BESS commissioning for **ACME Solar**, **Waaree**, and **GK Energy**.
*   **Regulatory/Strategic:** Progress on the **PTC India** PFS divestment and any updates on **IEX** market coupling regulations.
