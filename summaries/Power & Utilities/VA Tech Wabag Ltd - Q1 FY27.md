# VA Tech Wabag Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reporting record results, healthy execution, strong order intake, and continued financial discipline with net cash positive for the 14th consecutive quarter.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: INR 887 crore, up 20.8% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA: INR 116 crore, up 21.7% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA Margin: over 13%
- Consolidated PAT: INR 90 crore, up almost 37% YoY
- Consolidated PAT Margin: 10.2%
- Standalone Revenue from Operations: INR 739 crore, up over 15% YoY
- Standalone EBITDA: INR 105 crore, up over 21% YoY
- Standalone PAT: INR 79 crore, up over 30% YoY
- Segment contribution: EPC 82%, O&M 18%
- Geographical mix: India 48%, Overseas markets 52%
- Net cash balance (excluding HAM): INR 965 crores
- Net working capital days: 108 days
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 19.6%
- Return on Equity (ROE): 16%
- Order Intake: Over INR 3,400 crore (INR 34 billion); EPC INR 29.6 billion, O&M INR 4.7 billion. International markets accounted for 77%, India 23%.
- Order Book: INR 19,400 crore (INR 194 billion / ~$1.8 billion); 66% EPC and 34% O&M. Middle East accounts for about 35% to 40%.

### Highlights
- Secured maiden project in Kuwait: 60 MIGD SWRO desalination plant from the Ministry of Electricity Water and Renewable Energy in partnership with HEISCO.
- Awarded third phase of the 60 MLD Ajman Sewage Treatment Plant in UAE (sewage biorefinery).
- Strengthened Indian municipal portfolio with repeat orders in Bengaluru (BWS&SB at Byramangala and Bellandur) and Delhi (Mitraon project from DJB).
- Secured a technologically advanced project in Vienna, Austria (Vienna Water Works).
- Perur desalination project in Chennai crossed a milestone with 1 lakh cubic meters of concreting completed (75%+ complete).
- Ras Tanura Industrial Wastewater Treatment project entered the decommissioning phase; Al Haer wastewater treatment project has engineering and procurement largely complete with installation underway.
- Two framework orders worth INR 600 crore were prudently removed from the order book as they were waiting for effectiveness, leaving the current INR 19,400 crore order book fully effective.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a 15% to 20% revenue growth.
- Medium-term EBITDA margin outlook maintained at 13% to 15% (management expects 13% to 14% band for the current year, improving towards 15% over time).
- Management is currently working on a long-term strategy rehash called "Wriddhi 2.0" to be presented in May 2027.
- Targeting to take the industrial order mix to around 20% to 25% over time.
- Order backlog multiple targeted to remain beyond 3x (currently over 4x).

### Demand & industry commentary
- MEA region remains a strong growth engine driven by water security needs across desalination, wastewater treatment, and reuse.
- Geopolitical developments in the Middle East have had no material impact on projects, operations, or underlying demand.
- Saudi Arabia announced 2027 as the "Year of Water"; robust bid pipeline and inquiry levels across GCC.
- Future energy solutions (data centers, solar, semiconductors, green hydrogen, CBG) represent decadal growth opportunities for industrial water.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross and contribution margins vary based on project mix (EP vs. EPC and type of treatment).
- Other expenses include standard accounting provisions for delays or anticipated defaults based on stringent internal policies.
- Forex gains/losses are operational in nature, arising from working capital transactions, and are not non-operational.
- Company maintains a natural currency hedge via third-country imports (Europe, Japan, US) and PCFC lines.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company operates on an asset-light model; no capex is required for taking orders or driving growth.
- Continued selective evaluation of developed markets (Europe) and maintaining a cautious watch on South America.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash positive for the 14th consecutive quarter.
- Net working capital days maintained in the 100 to 110 days range.
- TSGENCO arbitration case is sub-judice, with a favorable ruling from the Supreme Court.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East managed via force majeure clauses, contractual letters to clients for price increases/time delays, and natural project insulation (social/water infrastructure away from strike zones).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial) on margins and provisions:** Management clarified that gross margins fluctuate based on the EP vs. EPC project mix, and higher "other expenses" stem from stringent accounting provisions for delays/defaults, which are reversible upon collection.
2. **Kishore Kumar on Middle East execution risks:** Management stated that Middle East projects are far from strike zones, social in nature, and managed smoothly despite geopolitical events, with formal force majeure communications sent where necessary.
3. **Dhananjay Mishra on Industrial segment mix:** Management explained that industrial orders fluctuate across capex cycles (historically ~30% of revenue) and targeted an eventual industrial mix of 20% to 25%, driven by oil & gas and emerging tech sectors.
4. **Sailesh Raja on Kodungaiyur project status:** Management confirmed the agreement is signed, project report submitted to PE, and refurbishment is set to start anytime soon as part of a 20-year project.
5. **Mihir Vyas on operational margins excluding forex:** Management firmly disagreed with excluding forex, stating that forex gains/losses are fully operational and tied to international working capital invoices and payments.
6. **Parth Thakkar on bid pipeline size:** Management stated the active bid pipeline across Middle East, Africa, and India stands between $3 billion to $4.5 billion (INR 20,000 crore to INR 25,000 crore) tracked over an 18-month horizon.

### One-line takeaway
VA Tech Wabag reported a strong Q1 FY27 with 20.8% revenue growth, 21.7% EBITDA growth, a record order book of INR 19,400 crore, and a 14-quarter net cash positive balance sheet, driven by robust international execution in the MEA region.
