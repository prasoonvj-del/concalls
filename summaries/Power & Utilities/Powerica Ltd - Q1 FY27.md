# Powerica Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth offset by commodity inflation impacting margins in the DG set business.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 780 crores (up 26.7% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 106 crores
- **EBITDA Margin:** 13.6%
- **PAT:** INR 64 crores
- **PAT Margin:** 8.3%
- **DG Set Revenue Growth:** 26.2% YoY
- **Wind Power Revenue Growth:** 28.8% YoY
- **DG Set Business EBITDA Margin:** 5.6%
- **Wind Power Business EBITDA Margin:** 48.6%
- **DG Set Order Book (as of July 31, 2026):** INR 1,700 crores (15% to 19% YoY growth)
- **Data Center Order Book (as of July 31, 2026):** INR 900 crores (reached INR 1,100 crores as of August 7, 2026)
- **IPP Portfolio Roadmap:** 633.55 MW (or 638.35 MW) from current 330 MW
- **RECD Revenue (Q1 FY27):** INR 16-17 crores
- **Taxation Rate:** ~25.168%

### Highlights
- Revenue performance driven by strong execution in the DG set business and higher wind installation (51.3 MW in February 2026) coupled with better wind conditions.
- DG sets powered by Cummins contributed 72% to total Q1 FY27 revenue; allied business contributed 9%; wind power contributed 18.6% (IPP 11.5%, EPC/O&M for BOP 7.1%).
- Manufacturing remains the top market segment, followed by real estate, with the rental segment being the second-fastest growing.
- Powerica received the "Excellence in Power Backup Solutions for Data Centers" award at the 25th Data Center Summit and Awards 2026.
- Powerica Renewable Infra Private Limited won the "Developer of the Year - Solar & Wind Award" at MahaUrja Expo 2026.
- Incorporated two wholly-owned subsidiaries (Windfusion Renewable Private Limited and Whisperwind Renewable Private Limited) and acquired a 49% stake in Fuji-Kailash Energy Private Limited.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated double-digit revenue growth guidance for FY27.
- Margins are expected to improve from Q3 onwards due to operating leverage, product mix improvements, engineering-led solutions, and newly implemented price hikes.
- Wind IPP capacity addition timeline: 50 MW in the current financial year (FY27), 150 MW in FY28, and 100 MW in the subsequent financial year.
- EPC balance of plant (BOP) business expected to execute 250 MW to 300 MW annually, generating approximately INR 400 crores in top-line per year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Data center demand is robust; data center-specific orders stand at INR 1,100 crores as of August 7, 2026.
- International inquiries for MSLG (Medium Speed Large Gensets) and second-phase PSU orders are under technical discussion following delays due to geopolitical situations.
- RECD (Retrofit Emission Control Devices) demand was temporarily postponed due to the West Asia crisis and GRAP implementation delays in the National Capital Region.
- No orders are being lost due to supply constraints, though rapid data center demand growth is felt across all engine makers and DG suppliers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross profit margins and EBITDA margins were impacted by elevated commodity prices and West Asia geopolitical crises, exacerbated by a time lag in passing on costs.
- Price hikes implemented in a two-phase approach (mid-Q1 and start of Q2) at the genset level to mitigate input cost pressures.
- Wind IPP new projects yield ~82-83% EBITDA on revenue, old 330 MW projects yield ~62-63%, and EPC business yields 10-11% EBITDA margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- DG set capacity utilization ranges between 75% and 80% on a single-shift basis, with adequate capacity currently available.
- Expanding wind IPP portfolio from 330 MW to 633.55/638.35 MW via recent wins: 100 MW GUVNL (PPA signed, under construction), 50 MW GUVNL (LOA awaited), and 100 MW SECI (LOA received).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position stands at approximately INR 193 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and energy prices impact input costs and create time lags in passing price hikes.
- Fixed-price legacy orders executed during Q1 weighed on profitability without immediate escalation mechanisms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) asked about the margin trajectory and fixed-price nature of old orders in the genset business.** Management (Ritesh Agrawal) replied that Q1 and part of Q2 margins are impacted by legacy orders and the West Asia crisis, but new orders incorporate revised pricing, expecting improvement from Q3 onwards.
2. **Divyam Surekha asked about the execution timeline and demand acceptance for price hikes.** Management (Jai Ram Oberoi) stated data center execution takes 12-18 months, and price hikes are being absorbed smoothly, drawing a parallel to a 33% price pass-through achieved during CPCB IV+ implementation over 9 months.
3. **Hetvi Sanghvi asked about the revenue and margin mix between owned wind assets and third-party BOP work.** Management (Pradeep Gupta) clarified that owned IPP capacity is scaling from 330 MW to 633 MW, while EPC BOP execution remains steady at 250-300 MW annually generating ~INR 400 crores top-line.
4. **Saif Gujar asked about the growth of the data center order book and single inquiry sizes.** Management (Ritesh Oberoi/Ritesh Agrawal) confirmed data center orders jumped from INR 900 crores (July 31) to INR 1,100 crores (August 7) due to a single large order exceeding INR 200 crores, noting order sizes are increasing.
5. **Nagendra Maurya asked about capacity utilization and RECD business performance.** Management (Ritesh Agrawal) stated DG capacity utilization is 75-80% on single shift, and RECD revenue was restricted to INR 16-17 crores for the quarter due to project postponements from the crisis and GRAP implementation.

### One-line takeaway
Powerica delivered strong 26.7% YoY revenue growth to INR 780 crores in Q1 FY27, though profitability faced headwinds from commodity inflation and fixed-price legacy orders, with management anticipating margin recovery by Q3 alongside robust data center and wind expansion visibility.
