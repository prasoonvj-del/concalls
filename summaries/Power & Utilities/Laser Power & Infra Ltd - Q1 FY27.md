# Laser Power & Infra Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue and EBITDA growth driven by strong EPC execution and stable manufacturing profitability.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 5,215 million (Up ~15% YoY from Rs 4,541 million in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 659 million (Up ~26% YoY from Rs 524 million in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 12.6% (vs 11.5% in Q1 FY26)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 286 million (Up ~27% YoY from Rs 222 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 211 million
- **PAT Margin:** ~4.1%
- **Manufacturing Revenue:** Rs 3,824 million (vs Rs 3,932 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Manufacturing EBITDA:** Rs 388 million
- **EPC Revenue:** Rs 1,391 million (Up ~129% YoY from Rs 609 million in Q1 FY26)
- **EPC EBITDA:** Rs 276 million (vs Rs 93 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Finance Cost:** Rs 362 million (vs Rs 296 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Total Order Book (as of June 2026):** Rs 27,884 million (Manufacturing: Rs 14,327 million; EPC: Rs 13,557 million)
- **HTLS / Re-conductoring tenders participated in:** Approximately Rs 1,250 crores (out of ~Rs 3,500 crores floated by utilities over the last 1 year)
- **HT Cable Revenue Share:** Grew from 9% to 29% of total revenue over the last nine quarters
- **Debt Reduction via IPO proceeds:** Approximately Rs 4,900 million utilized to repay loans
- **Gross Debt:** Approximately Rs 3,600 million
- **Fixed Deposits (Margin Money):** Approximately Rs 2,400 million
- **Working Capital Cycle:** Typically 100 to 120 days

### Highlights
- Company completed a successful IPO and listed in July 2026.
- Substantial portion of IPO proceeds (~Rs 4,900 million) utilized to repay outstanding loans, bringing net debt down to a negligible level after adjusting for Rs 2,400 million in fixed deposits.
- Integrated manufacturing and EPC model provides quality control, material availability, and delivery schedule coordination.
- Manufacturing operates across 3 units with an aggregate installed capacity of ~85,000 metric tonnes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue to continue growing at a steady historical CAGR of around 15% to 16%.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to remain stable and operating performance to improve as high-value products and debt de-leveraging benefits kick in.
- Full benefits of interest cost savings from IPO debt repayment will accrue over subsequent quarters in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's power generation capacity is planned to increase from ~500 GW to 900 GW in the next 5 to 7 years.
- CRISIL data indicates India's conductor market grew from ~Rs 102 billion in FY20 to ~Rs 185 billion in FY25, and is expected to reach Rs 230 to 250 billion by FY30.
- Right-of-Way (RoW) challenges are increasing, driving utilities to adopt advanced, high-capacity conductors (HTLS/AECC) to uprate existing corridors instead of building new transmission lines.
- Addressable market for HTLS conductors and re-conductoring saw tenders worth ~Rs 3,500 crores floated over the last year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Finance cost absorbed ~55% of quarterly EBITDA (Rs 362 million in Q1 FY27), moderating the conversion of operating profitability to PAT.
- High-voltage (HT) cables fetch ~2% to 3% higher EBITDA margins compared to normal cables.
- Conventional conductors are commoditized with the lowest margins in the portfolio; specialized HTLS conductors are expected to fetch higher margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Immediate focus is on improving utilization, increasing productivity, and growing the contribution of higher value-added products.
- Future capex will be undertaken in a phased manner aligned with customer demand, supported by available land within the manufacturing footprint.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stands at ~Rs 3,600 million post-IPO debt repayment of ~Rs 4,900 million.
- Company maintains fixed deposits of ~Rs 2,400 million as margin money, making net debt negligible.
- Working capital increased in Q1 due to project execution and mobilization of ~Rs 8,000 million worth of projects commenced in Q4 FY26, leading to a temporary spike in inventory (FG and WIP up by Rs 90 crores). Working capital is expected to moderate as projects progress.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- EPC profitability and working capital requirements fluctuate depending on project stages, contract milestones, and customer certifications, making quarterly run-rates variable.
- Finance costs weighed heavily on Q1 bottom-line conversion (though to be mitigated by post-quarter debt repayment).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bala Murali Krishna (Investment Advisors) asked why standalone numbers were presented instead of consolidated.**
   - *Management answered:* The subsidiary is currently an SPV with nil revenue for the year, and the bottom-line difference between standalone and consolidated was minuscule (excluding an extraordinary one-time profit of Rs 32 crores in the previous year's consolidated numbers). Consolidated numbers will be presented once the SPV starts generating revenue next year.
2. **Vidit Trivedi (Asian Market Securities) asked about the capacity utilization and revenue split between cables and conductors.**
   - *Management answered:* The current product split in the manufacturing division is ~90% cables and ~10% conventional conductors. Utilization depends on product mix and complexity, and capacity is expanded proactively before hitting 75%-80% utilization.
3. **Raman KV asked about quarterly interest savings post-debt repayment.**
   - *Management answered:* With Rs 490 crores of debt repaid at a ~9% cost of capital, annual interest savings will be ~Rs 40 crores at the PBT level, flowing across quarters.
4. **Pranav Jain (Financiers) asked about competitive advantage and bidding eligibility in re-conductoring tenders using TS Conductors tech.**
   - *Management answered:* The company tied up with TS Conductors in March 2025 and spent ~1.5 years on product development, type-testing, prototype testing, and erection approvals, enabling them to participate in ~Rs 1,250 crores of tenders. Tenders do not mandate specific tech, but all previous technology partners are eligible to bid.
5. **Krupa Desai (Electrum Capital) asked why actual production volume remained flat despite capacity increases over 3 years.**
   - *Management answered:* The company is shifting mix toward specialized, value-added products which have lower volume but contribute higher to top-line and bottom-line.

### One-line takeaway
Laser Power & Infra delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 15% revenue growth and 26% EBITDA growth, backed by robust EPC execution, while post-IPO debt repayment of Rs 490 crores sets the stage for significant finance cost reductions and improved PAT conversion ahead.
