# Emmvee Photovoltaic Power Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with a 45% YoY revenue growth in H1, robust EBITDA margins of ~35%, and healthy order book expansion.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 3,132 crore (up 45% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 1,105 crore (up 47% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 762 crore (up 79% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 1,576 crore (up 39% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 557 crore (up 39% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** ~35.3%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 382 crore (up 61% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** ~24%
- **Q2 FY27 Finance Cost:** ~INR 9.5 crore
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow:** ~INR 410 crore (up 239% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Solar Module Production:** 952 MW (up 40% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Solar Cell Production:** 513 MW (up 61% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Cell Capacity Utilization:** ~89%
- **Q2 FY27 Module Capacity Utilization:** ~44%
- **Order Book (as of Sept 30, 2026):** ~10.4 GW (with ~1.5 GW new inflows in Q2)
- **Net Debt to Equity Ratio (as of Sept 30, 2026):** ~0.12x
- **Total Inventory:** INR 1,884 crore ( Finished goods: INR 860 crore)
- **Silver Consumption:** Reduced from ~220 milligrams to 60-65 milligrams.

### Highlights
- Delivered strongest half-yearly financial performance to date.
- Maintained EBITDA margins at ~35%.
- Started G12R cell manufacturing in Q2.
- Net debt reduced following IPO proceeds deployment towards debt repayment; finance costs dropped significantly YoY (e.g., INR 9.5 crore in Q2 FY27 vs INR 55.2 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Received state government capital subsidies (25% for earlier projects; similar subsidies expected for upcoming projects).
- 100% of power utilized in the last quarter came from renewable energy via open-access power.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of achieving the upper end of its projections/guidance based on H1 numbers (INR 2,200 crore to INR 2,400 crore EBITDA).
- Expects utilization rates close to 90% for cell manufacturing for the remaining two quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- H1 of CY2027 witnessed India's highest-ever solar installation for a 6-month period.
- Domestic module demand running at a pace translating to ~64% YoY growth.
- Growing adoption of hybrid, round-the-clock, and firm renewable energy projects increasing AC-DC ratios and module demand.
- Non-tendered business has grown substantially (1.5 GW to 2 GW demand per month).
- Order book split: ~70% DCR and ~30% non-DCR; ~25% retail/KUSUM and ~75% institutional (C&I and utility).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Silver consumption reduced to 60-65 milligrams from 220 milligrams.
- Transition from M10R to G12R increased single-format cell wattage to ~9.5W.
- Credit days reduced from 64 days in FY26 to 25 days in H1 FY27 due to vendor negotiations and procurement cost optimization.
- Forex gains and power cost optimization via open-access renewables reduced other expenses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 6 GW integrated TOPCon cell and module manufacturing project on track: module facility commissioning by December 2026, cell facility by March 2027.
- Post-completion total installed capacity: ~16.3 GW of modules and ~8.9 GW of cells.
- Total capex cost for 6 GW cell and module: INR 5,500 crore (including hard cost of ~INR 4,600 crore, working capital margin money, IDC, and contingencies).
- Backward integration into ingot and wafer manufacturing planned at 9 GW (5 GW + 4 GW phases), subject to regulatory developments (ALMM List-III), market conditions, and economic viability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt draw down of INR 828 crore in last quarter was for the new expansion project; interest cost is currently being capitalized until commissioning.
- Total inventory at INR 1,884 crore (finished goods INR 860 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as major threats; management noted ALMM-II deferment is extremely unlikely.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Apoorva Bahadur (IIFL Capital) asked about the divergence in capex per gigawatt (INR 5,500 cr for 6 GW) versus industry standards (INR 5-6 cr/GW).** Management clarified that the INR 5,500 crore includes both cell and modules (6 GW each), hard costs of INR 4,600 crore, plus working capital margin money, IDC, contingencies, and soft costs.
2. **Deepak Krishnan asked about inventory and guidance.** Management stated finished goods are INR 860 crore, and confirmed they can achieve the upper part of their guidance based on H1 performance.
3. **Parth Majithia asked about the reduction in creditor days.** Management explained it results from dynamic vendor negotiations to optimize procurement costs.
4. **Sudhanshu (JM Financial) asked about levers to maintain margins amid falling module prices.** Management highlighted reduction in silver usage (down to 60-65 mg), transition to G12R cells, and manufacturing/operational efficiencies.
5. **Sahil Sheth asked about the revenue concentration of the largest customer rising to 44% in Q2.** Management responded that delivery timelines cause quarter-on-quarter variations in customer share, and transactions are secured via substantial down payments or LCs.

### One-line takeaway
Emmvee posted its strongest H1 performance with a 45% YoY revenue growth and 79% PAT growth, backed by robust cell utilization, expanding integrated capacities, and strict operational discipline.
