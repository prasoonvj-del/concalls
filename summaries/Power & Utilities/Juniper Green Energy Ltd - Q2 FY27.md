# Juniper Green Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly total income and EBITDA driven by strong wind generation and commissioning milestones.

### Key numbers
- Total income: Rs 372 crore (up 2.3x YoY, up 15% QoQ)
- Operating income: Rs 345 crore (up 2.4x YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 340 crore (up 2.6x YoY, up 15% QoQ) at an EBITDA margin of 91.4%
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 313 crore (up 2.7x YoY) at an operating margin of 90.7%
- Adjusted Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 54 crore (compared to loss of Rs 18 crore in Q2 FY26 and adjusted loss of Rs 53 crore in Q1 FY27)
- Cash PAT (adjusted PAT + depreciation): Rs 153 crore (up 3.9x YoY from Rs 40 crore)
- H1 FY27 Total Income: Rs 696 crore (up 104% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 634 crore (margin 91.1%); PAT: Rs 106 crore; Cash PAT: Rs 280 crore
- Generation (Q2 FY27): 1,130 million units (up 127% YoY); H1 FY27: 2,075 million units (up 98% YoY)
- Fleet-wide CUF: Q2 FY27 at 27% (vs 21% in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 at 28.5% (vs 24.1% in H1 FY26)
- Wind Portfolio Weighted Average CUF: 50% (up from 41.3% YoY)
- Plant availability: 98.7% for H1; Grid availability: 99.5% for H1
- Capex incurred: Rs 2,600 crore in Q2; Rs 3,800 crore in H1; cumulative capex close to Rs 19,000 crore (Gross block: Rs 12,250 crore; CWIP + advances: Rs 6,600 crore)
- Total debt: Rs 13,700 crore; Net debt: Rs 11,600 crore; Operational net debt: Rs 9,000 crore
- Net worth: Rs 5,200 crore post Rs 1,800 crore primary IPO proceeds; Net debt to net worth: 2.22x
- Weighted average cost of debt: 8.46% (down from 8.58% last quarter)
- Days receivable outstanding: 25 days for H1 FY27

### Highlights
- Commissioned ~316 MW peak of renewable capacity and ~100 MWh of BESS in Q2.
- Commissioned >1 GW peak in the first 7 months of FY27 (~494 MWp solar, ~542 MW wind, ~500 MWh BESS across 11 sites in 3 states).
- Overall operational capacity stands at 2.8 GW peak + 600 MWh BESS; total portfolio at 11.2 GW peak + 9 GWh BESS (6.1 GW peak operational and PPA-signed).
- Commissioned 51 units of India's largest wind turbine by rotor diameter, the Envision E-182 (5 MW, 181-meter rotor diameter, 130-meter hub height).
- ICRA upgraded credit rating to AA- stable; 7 project-level rating upgrades received (3 SPVs now in AA category).
- Refinanced >Rs 2,500 crore across 5 projects in FY27 at weighted average interest rate <8% p.a.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track for full-year commissioning guidance of 2 GW peak of generation capacity and 1.7 GWh of BESS.
- Expected FY27 capex of additional Rs 5,000 crore in the balance 6 months, taking total capex to Rs 24,000 crore by financial year end.
- Expected EBITDA run rate for March '27 capacity (3.9 GW peak): Rs 2,750 crore (current run rate for 2.8 GW is Rs 1,900 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's installed renewable capacity reached close to 300 GW as of September 30, 2026 (~25 GW added in H1 FY27).
- Power demand H1 growth strong at 10.2%.
- Evening peak hours driving demand for BESS to shift daytime solar power to peak hours.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Lithium carbonate prices dropped from 170,000 yuan to 100,000 yuan per ton; expected 1-2% net softening on battery prices post removal of China's 6% export incentive.
- Non-DCR solar modules available at Rs 12 or lower; DCR procurement expected around Rs 18 or below for large utility projects over time.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Surplus connectivity of >5 GW for future bids, >20 GWh of BESS connectivity, >16,000 acres of land secured, and >100 identified wind locations.
- In-house EPC team executing in parallel across multiple sites/states at STU and CTU levels.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- 68% of borrowings from financial institutions (PFC, IREDA, NaBFID, IIFCL) providing long-tenor stable funding.
- 86% of portfolio contracted with central agencies (SJVN, NHPC, NTPC, SECI) and GUVNL; 98% off-takers rated A or above; 98% secured through long-term (25-year) PPAs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Curtailment of ~2.5% of overall revenue experienced in Q2 due to transmission line delays (specifically Juniper Green Stellar/Fatehgarh 3 - Bhadla 3 line), which management noted has since been absorbed by batteries and expects the line to be completed by December.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Puneet (HSBC Bank) on transmission delays for Juniper Green Stellar and Beta:** Management stated Stellar is running on T-GNA with the pending line expected in December (built by Apraava), and Beta is expected by March '27 with merchant batteries covering any potential interim curtailment.
2. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) on PPA conversion for LOAs and thermal mimic:** Management noted that of 4 GW of earlier LOAs, 1 to 1.3 GW peak is expected to be contracted via PSAs/PPAs over the next 1 to 1.5 months, while thermal mimic PPAs are taking longer as DISCOMs understand the delivery model.
3. **Sudhanshu Bansal (JM Financial) on standalone BESS arbitrage sustainability:** Management expressed confidence that arbitrage will continue due to lack of operational standalone BESS capacity from aggressive bidders, while noting they retain long-term integration plans for these batteries.
4. **Mahesh Patil (ICICI Securities) on bidding strategy and return thresholds:** Management stated they look for mid-to-high teen returns and skip overly aggressive bids (such as recent Rajasthan solar park tenders), focusing on FDRE/hybrid tenders with batteries.
5. **Sahil Seth (Anand Rathi) on thermal mimicking tender details and returns:** Management outlined the 230 MW capacity win comprises 530 MW solar (790 MW DC), 75 MW wind, and 2.2 GWh BESS, delivering mid-to-high teen returns.

### One-line takeaway
Juniper Green Energy delivered a record Q2 FY27 with revenue and EBITDA scaling 2.3x and 2.6x YoY respectively, backed by robust wind CUF of 50%, strong operational execution, and continued balance sheet strengthening.
