# Gujarat Energy Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Excellent performance across key business segments driven by integrated model synergies and strong gas trading profitability.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 9,670 crore (vs Rs 5,924 crore YoY, +63%)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 1,482 crore (vs Rs 896 crore YoY, +65%)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 998 crore (vs Rs 561 crore YoY, +78%)
- **Overall Sales Volume:** 15.66 mmscmd
- **Gas Trading Sales Volume:** 12.22 mmscmd (Intercompany allocation to CGD: 8.9 mmscmd; External sales: 3.32 mmscmd)
- **CGD Sales Volume:** 12.34 mmscmd
- **CNG Volume:** 3.76 mmscmd (vs 3.33 mmscmd YoY, +13%)
- **PNG Industrial Volume:** 7.17 mmscmd (vs 4.71 mmscmd YoY, +64%)
- **Morbi Cluster Average Volume:** 5.67 mmscmd (increased 181% QoQ compared to Q4 FY26, peaking around 8 mmscmd during May-June 2026)
- **Non-Morbi Industrial Volume:** 2.04 mmscmd
- **PNG Domestic Customer Additions:** ~59,000 new customers; cumulative base >24.77 lakh households
- **CNG Stations Added:** 6 new stations added and 9 upgraded; total stations 844
- **Gas Trading Segment Earnings Before Tax:** Rs 726 crore (vs Rs 237 crore YoY, +206%)
- **CGD Infrastructure Capex (Q1):** Rs 127 crore
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** ~Rs 7,200 crore
- **Gross Block:** ~Rs 12,000 crore

### Highlights
- Completed the listing and trading of additional equity shares allotted pursuant to the merger scheme.
- Sourced 10 LNG cargoes despite global disruptions and Middle East conflict.
- CGD segment volumes underpinned by a balanced mix: CNG grew 13% (Gujarat +19% outside, +12% in Gujarat), and PNG industrial grew 64%.
- Supported Morbi ceramic industry during propane supply constraints, scaling gas supplies from ~0.4 mmscmd in April 2026 to ~8 mmscmd in May-June 2026.
- PNG domestic connections saw a strong push under government initiatives amid constrained LPG supplies; connected ~91,000 domestic and >1,000 commercial connections from January to June 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Gas Trading Profit Guidance:** Management maintains a conservative full-year EBT guidance of ~Rs 1,100 crore for the gas trading business despite strong Q1 outperformance.
- **CGD EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 5.5 to 6.5 per SCM (Q1 was Rs 5.18 per SCM).
- **CNG Station Expansion:** Planning to add >75 new CNG stations and upgrade ~70 CNG stations in FY27.
- **Capex Guidance:** Maintained at ~Rs 1,000 crore for the CGD business for the year.
- **Long-term Sourcing:** Expects term contract volumes to increase from ~2 million tonnes LNG equivalent (28%) currently to ~4 million tonnes by 2030.
- **Non-Morbi Industrial Volume Outlook:** Expected to reach ~3 mmscmd in 1.5 to 2 years as pipeline infrastructure in regions like Ahmedabad rural, Thane, Kutch, and Dahej matures.
- **GSPL Transmission Listing:** Expected around early September, pending regulatory/SEBI exemptions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global energy markets remain uncertain due to geopolitical developments.
- Propane availability for Morbi has improved post-July via non-Middle East sourcing (U.S., Venezuela), driving Morbi gas run-rate down to ~3 mmscmd (with ~5.3-5.4 mmscmd equivalent as propane). Current pricing in Morbi: Gas at ~Rs 78/scm vs Propane at ~Rs 65/scm.
- Morbi minimum floor demand of ~1.8 to 2 mmscmd expected to persist due to smaller unit sizes or lack of space for propane infrastructure.
- Government mandate/push is active for domestic and commercial PNG (switching from LPG), but industrial segments face open competition from alternate fuels like propane, steamhouse, briquettes, and solid fuels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sourcing split for CGD: Term contracts ~20% (including ~20% Henry Hub linked), Domestic gas (APM/HPHT) ~12.5% (margins fixed), New Well Gas ~6%, GAIL pool gas ~2%, and short-term/spot/inter-segment ~67%.
- Gas trading margins benefit from timed sourcing and dated Brent aging advantages on long-term agreements.
- Trading margin target remains ~4% to 5% percentage margin over cycles.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pipeline network spans ~45,900 km across 6 states and 1 union territory.
- Planning to set up an in-house propane import and storage facility/terminal near the Morbi market (detailed project report in early stages).
- Evaluating data center "behind-the-meter" power supply and other revival strategies for gas-based power plants (GSEG and Pipavav).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stands at ~Rs 7,200 crore.
- Comprehensive cash deployment strategy and dividend review plans to be presented to investors by Q3.
- Unabsorbed tax losses/merger refund claims of ~Rs 900 crore: revised tax returns are being filed, and refunds are expected to take at least a year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility (Middle East conflicts) impacting spot LNG prices, LNG cargo availability, and shifting timelines for low-cost global LNG waves.
- Lower PLFs for gas-based power plants (GSEG operating at ~6%, Pipavav at ~1%) due to high spot gas costs outside peak summer demands.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities) / Bineet Banka (Nomura):** Asked why trading guidance is maintained at Rs 1,100 crore despite a massive Q1 beat and whether Q2 will see negative reversals. *Management answered that guidance is kept conservative, and no negative reversals are expected.*
2. **Amit Murarka (Axis Capital):** Asked about the drop in Morbi gas volumes to 3 mmscmd and propane competition. *Management explained that pricing (~Rs 78 for gas vs ~Rs 65 for propane) drove substitution, but ~1.8-2 mmscmd will stay on gas due to lack of customer propane infrastructure.*
3. **Mayank Maheshwari (Morgan Stanley):** Inquired why long-term gas sourcing has been delayed despite multiple shocks. *Management clarified it is not due to a lack of confidence in demand, but rather geopolitical shocks shifting the timeline of reasonable-term pricing; they have deals with Total, Uniper, Qatar, and are scouting for 2028 supplies.*
4. **Bineet Banka (Nomura):** Asked about the timeline for GSPL Transmission listing. *Management stated it is expected in early September due to regulatory exemption procedures.*
5. **Deepak Malhotra (CapGrow Capital):** Asked about the utilization of Rs 7,200 crore cash on the balance sheet. *Management noted a concrete cash deployment and return strategy will be announced by Q3.*

### One-line takeaway
Gujarat Energy Limited reported a stellar Q1 FY27 with a 78% YoY jump in PAT to Rs 998 crore, propelled by robust gas trading profitability and strong integrated CGD volume growth, while maintaining conservative full-year guidance amid ongoing geopolitical and pricing volatilities.
