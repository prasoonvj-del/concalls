# Adani Green Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial growth with Q2 power supply revenue up 45% YoY and rapid deployment of energy storage to mitigate transmission bottlenecks.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Power Supply:** Rs 4,019 crore (+45% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA from Power Supply:** Rs 3,835 crore (+51% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 92%.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Power Supply:** Rs 8,299 crore (+36% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA from Power Supply:** Rs 7,957 crore (+41% YoY); H1 EBITDA margin stood at 93%. Total H1 EBITDA reached Rs 8,276 crore (management noted it would be close to Rs 9,000 crore excluding grid curtailments).
- **H1 FY27 Energy Sales:** 25.7 billion units (+32% YoY).
- **Operational Capacity:** Reached 20.8 GW (+24% YoY greenfield additions).
- **Storage Capacity:** Commissioned 3 GWh BESS at Khavda in Q2, taking total operational energy storage to 6.6 GWh.
- **Average Interest Rate:** 8.9% as of Q2 FY27 (flat compared to ~8.95% in March 2026).
- **H1 Solar CUF:** 23% (down from 24.8% in H1 FY26 due to grid curtailments).
- **Merchant Realization (Infirm Power):** Rs 2.4 per unit in Q2 FY27 vs Rs 2.1 per unit in Q2 FY26.

### Highlights
- Crossed 20.8 GW operational capacity, supplying clean power equivalent to 4.4 million homes and avoiding 17.5 million tonnes of CO2 emissions in H1 FY27.
- Operates 6.6 GWh of total operational BESS, representing >50% of India's operational battery energy storage.
- Operationalized 10.8 GW of solar, wind, and hybrid capacity at Khavda.
- SPV AGE23L (~15% of AGEL EBITDA) received a 7-notch rating upgrade over 5 years to AAA status, bringing its interest rate down from 10%–11% to 8.4%.
- Retained #1 global ranking in the alternative electricity subsector by FTSE Russell for the second consecutive year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to add 4.5 to 5 GW of capacity in FY27, provided Phase 3 transmission evacuation lines are commissioned by Q4 FY27.
- Confident of surpassing the target of 10 GWh operational BESS capacity by the end of FY27.
- Maiden 500 MW Chitravathi pump storage project on track for completion by end of the year.
- Management targets 45 GW RE capacity, 5 GW pump storage, and 40–50 GWh BESS by FY30.
- Maintains strict capital allocation threshold of 13%–14% ROA (EBITDA / Project Cost) with AA+ or higher counterparties under 25-year contracts.

### Demand & industry commentary
- High electricity demand driven by industrial growth, EV adoption, data centers, AI hyperscalers, and overall economy electrification.
- Strong emerging demand for Firm and Dispatchable Renewable Energy (FDRE), RTC power, and integrated RE plus storage solutions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Delivered Q2 EBITDA margin of 92% and H1 EBITDA margin of 93%.
- O&M and employee costs were rationalized through digitization and technology investments.
- Secured long-term 25-year PPA tariffs at Rs 2.7/kWh for solar (with AESL) and Rs 3.93/kWh for wind.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex program of Rs 2.4 lakh crore through FY30.
- Rapid deployment of BESS provides "virtual transmission" capability to store peak solar power and insulate AGEL from transmission delays.
- Balance of System (BOS) and civil infrastructure work are kept ready ahead of time to allow fast module installation once transmission lines are clear.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ~72% to 73% of project debt is on fixed interest rates for 3–5 year terms.
- Debt mix: 70% Rupee-denominated and 30% USD-denominated (USD debt is 100% hedged for both principal and interest).
- Net leverage ratio stands at ~5.6x; management expects leverage to drop to 4.5x–5.0x post-FY28/29 and move below 4.0x towards FY30 as operational assets scale up.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Right-of-Way (ROW) issues causing transmission corridor delays at Khavda. Phase 3 evacuation timeline shifted from Q3 FY27 to Q4 FY27.
- Temporary grid curtailment impact on solar CUF and energy generation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Kumar (Emkay):** What is the status of transmission evacuation and FY27 commissioning targets?
  - *Management:* ROW issues are being addressed by state/central authorities. Phase 3 evacuation is now expected in Q4 FY27. If completed by Q4 FY27, AGEL will commission 4.5 to 5 GW. BOS is fully prepared for fast module installation.
- **Nirav Shah (Geecee Holdings):** How will rising domestic/global yields impact overall borrowing costs?
  - *Management:* 72% of debt is fixed-rate for 3–5 years. Ratings upgrades on operational assets (e.g., AGE23L reaching AAA) lower interest rates by 20–25 bps per notch. Average interest rate has remained flat at 8.9%.
- **Aniket Mittal (SBI Mutual Fund):** How much of the 10 GWh BESS capacity is tied up in long-term contracts?
  - *Management:* 3.5 GWh is already tied up under long-term contracts; the remaining capacity commissioned at Q2-end is currently in the process of being tied up. Input power (solar/wind) is fully available.
- **Amit Bhinde (Axis Capital):** Who absorbs grid curtailment costs under private 25-year PPAs?
  - *Management:* PPAs operate on take-or-pay deemed generation terms. Power that is scheduled but backed down by grid/procurer is paid for. Unscheduled power due to lack of grid capacity is not paid.
- **Dhruv Muchhal (HDFC AMC):** Why did Q2 EBITDA grow 51% YoY while generation grew 33% YoY?
  - *Management:* Attributable to higher merchant realisations for infirm power (Rs 2.4 vs Rs 2.1/kWh), BESS capacity additions, better wind availability, and O&M cost efficiencies.
- **Swetha Rakhecha (Cantor Fitzgerald):** Will accelerated BESS deployment alter capex or net leverage targets?
  - *Management:* Total capex remains on track. Leverage will remain ~5.6x until FY28–29 and will naturally taper down to 4.5x–5.0x and below 4.0x by FY30 as operational cash flows increase.

### One-line takeaway
Adani Green Energy continues to deliver strong 50%+ YoY EBITDA growth while utilizing accelerated battery storage deployment to derisk transmission constraints at Khavda.
