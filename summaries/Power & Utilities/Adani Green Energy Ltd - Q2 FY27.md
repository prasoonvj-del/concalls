# Adani Green Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust financial and operating performance, driven by capacity additions, higher realizations, and operational efficiencies, despite transmission curtailment challenges.

### Key numbers
- Operational capacity: Crossed 20 GW milestone, ending at 20.8 GW.
- Energy sales (H1 FY27): 25.7 billion units, up 32% YoY.
- Greenfield capacity additions (H1 FY27): Up 24% YoY.
- Revenue from power supply (H1 FY27): INR 8,299 crore, up 36% YoY.
- EBITDA from power supply (H1 FY27): INR 7,957 crore, up 41% YoY.
- Total EBITDA (H1 FY27): INR 8,276 crore (would have been close to INR 9,000 crore without curtailment).
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 93%.
- Revenue from power supply (Q2 FY27): INR 4,019 crore, up 45% YoY.
- EBITDA from power supply (Q2 FY27): INR 3,835 crore, up 51% YoY.
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin: 92%.
- Installed BESS capacity: 6.6 GWh (3 GWh commissioned in Q2 at Khavda).
- Khavda commissioned capacity: 10.8 GW of solar, wind, and hybrid capacity.
- Solar CUF (H1 FY27): Dipped to 23% vs 24.8% YoY primarily due to curtailment.
- Interest cost / Average cost of borrowing: AGEL level at 8.9%.
- Merchant/infirm solar power realization: INR 2.4 (vs INR 2.1 last year); PPA solar tariff with AESL: INR 2.7.
- Tied-up BESS capacity: 3.5 GWh out of 6.6 GWh.
- Debt profile: ~72% to 73% of project loans are fixed rate for a medium-to-long term (3 to 5 years).

### Highlights
- Crossed 20 GW operational capacity, supplying clean power to over 4.4 million homes and avoiding 17.5 million tons of CO2 emissions in H1 FY27.
- Commissioned 3 GWh of BESS at Khavda during the quarter, representing over 50% of India's operational BESS.
- Maiden 500 MW pumped storage project at Chitravathi progressing towards year-end completion.
- AGEL retained its #1 global ranking in the alternative electricity subsector by FTSE Russell for the second consecutive year.
- One key SPV (AGE23L), representing nearly 15% of AGEL's overall EBITDA, upgraded to AAA rating, reducing interest rate to 8.4%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains firmly on track to exceed 10 GWh of BESS capacity during the fiscal year.
- Targeting 4.5 to 5 GW of capacity commissioning in FY27, depending on transmission corridor availability (Phase 3 evacuation expected by next quarter/Q4).
- Committed to disciplined execution of the INR 2.4 lakh crore capex program through FY30.
- Committed to maintaining a 13% to 14% ROA threshold by tying up capacities with AA+ (or better) counterparties.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's non-fossil fuel capacity reached ~308 GW (over 54% of total installed power capacity), progressing toward the 500 GW target by 2030.
- Peak electricity demand continues to reach new highs, driven by industrial growth, electric mobility, data centers, and electrification.
- Significant opportunities emerging from demand for firm/dispatchable RE, power needs from AI/data centers/hyperscalers, and integrated renewables plus storage solutions for C&I customers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industry-leading EBITDA margins maintained at 93% in H1 FY27 and 92% in Q2 FY27.
- Operating costs well-controlled via ongoing investments in digitization, technology, and analytics.
- Average cost of borrowing remains stable at 8.9%, supported by rating upgrades over the past several years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- INR 2.4 lakh crore total capex program through FY30 is progressing as planned.
- Accelerated BESS development to insulate against transmission network delays.
- Khavda plant expanded to 10.8 GW of solar, wind, and hybrid capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ~72%-73% of project loans are fixed rate for 3 to 5 years, insulating against near-term rate hikes.
- Foreign currency (USD) debt principal and interest are fully hedged (RG2 hedged till FY30, RG1 till FY28).
- Leverage profile expected to remain in the current range through FY28/FY29 and taper toward 4.5x and below 4x post-FY30 as operating assets increase and loans amortize.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Right of Way (ROW) and transmission infrastructure availability issues at Khavda causing power curtailment and pushing back Phase 3 evacuation lines to Q4.
- Lower solar CUF in H1 (23% vs 24.8% YoY) due to grid curtailment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Kumar (Emkay Global) asked about transmission commissioning at Khavda and mitigation of curtailment.** Management stated that PGCIL/AESL face ROW issues, and while Phase 3 evacuation is expected by Q4, AGEL is accelerating BESS development to mitigate curtailment.
- **Nirav Shah (Geecee Holdings) asked about interest rate risks.** Management clarified that 72-73% of loans are fixed for 3-5 years, 25-year PPAs insulate returns, and rating upgrades (like AGE23L moving to AAA) continue to lower borrowing costs.
- **Aniket Mittal (SBI Mutual Fund) asked about solar CUF dip and BESS tie-ups.** Management confirmed the CUF dip was primarily curtailment-related and noted 3.5 GWh of BESS is currently tied up out of 6.6 GWh.
- **Amit Bhinde (Axis Capital) questioned payment mechanics during curtailment.** Management clarified that for scheduled power (and grid-backed-down scheduled power), AGEL is paid on a take-or-pay basis by the procurer (e.g., AESL).
- **Swetha Rakhecha (Cantor Fitzgerald) asked about leverage and capex allocation.** Management stated leverage will remain stable near-term and decline below 4.5x-4x post-2029/2030 as operating cash flows amortize project debt.

### One-line takeaway
AGEL delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 36% revenue and 41% EBITDA growth, crossing 20 GW operational capacity while aggressively scaling BESS to mitigate Khavda transmission curtailment and secure 13-14% ROA.
