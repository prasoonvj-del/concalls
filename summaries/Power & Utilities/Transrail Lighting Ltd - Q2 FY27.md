# Transrail Lighting Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever revenue and EBITDA delivered alongside strong H1 performance and robust cash flows.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): INR2,056 crores (up 32% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): INR238 crores (up 28% YoY)
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 11.6%
- PBT (Q2 FY27): INR158 crores (up 26% YoY)
- PAT (Q2 FY27): INR104 crores (up 12% YoY)
- H1 FY27 revenue: INR3,792 crores
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR441 crores (11.6% margin)
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR212 crores
- Operating cash flow (H1 FY27): INR435 crores (vs INR29 crores in H1 FY26)
- Net working capital days (H1 FY27): 82 days
- Net debt: INR143 crores as of September 30, 2026
- Tower manufacturing capacity: 1,84,400 metric tons per annum (scaling to 2,00,000 tons by end of year)
- Conductor manufacturing capacity (Phase 1 complete): 40,800 kilometers per annum (scaling to 49,500 km by end of year)
- Order book: INR14,218 crores as of September 30, 2026
- Post-quarter order wins declared: INR1,747 crores

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA despite Middle East/West Asia supply chain and logistics disruptions.
- Board approved acquiring a 49% stake in a Project SPV for a ~990 MW AC solar project in Maharashtra (total project cost ~INR3,900 crores, requiring ~INR500 crores equity over the next year funded via internal cash accruals).
- Secured 5 conductor jobs in India using in-house manufactured carbon core HTLS conductors (partnered with France's Epsilon).
- Secured greenfield GIS project in Jordan and 765kV/400kV transmission lines in India.
- Promoter share pledge stands at 2% to 3% for short-term borrowing, expected to be removed in 6 to 9 months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of achieving annual growth guidance and expects 20% revenue growth for at least the next 3 years.
- Management guided for 11%+ EBITDA margins and normalized PAT margins returning to 5.5% to 6%.
- Annual order intake guidance maintained at INR10,000+ crores.
- Tax rate expected to normalize to 25%-26% going forward (after a one-off tax scenario in Q2).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural opportunity in Indian power T&D remains strong with multi-decadal growth drivers up to 2032.
- Green Energy Corridor Phase III (GEC-III) envisages an outlay of ~INR1,86,000 crores (INR1.36 lakh crores for intra-state transmission and INR50,000 crores for BESS).
- Total Indian transmission opportunity estimated up to INR9,15,000 crores till 2030-2032.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Domestic jobs fully hedged against commodity price fluctuations (aluminum, etc.).
- International jobs largely carry price variation clauses.
- Higher depreciation and interest costs on an absolute basis due to capex and growing operations, but interest cost scaling down as a percentage of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Tower capacity expanding to 2,00,000 metric tons per annum by end of the year.
- Conductor capacity expanding to 49,500 kilometers per annum by end of the year.
- Solar IPP equity commitment of ~INR500 crores over the next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- CFO for H1 FY27 reached INR435 crores.
- Net working capital cycle sustained at 82 days.
- Balance sheet remains under-leveraged with net debt at INR143 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued impact on supply chain and logistics due to the Middle East/West Asia crisis.
- Increase in trade receivables outstanding for more than 6 months (management stated focus on reduction in ensuing quarters).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Harshit on Peak Revenue & Growth:** Management stated current expanded capacities support a ~INR16,000 crores order book and 20% growth for the next 3 years, reaching INR12,000 crores topline by FY29.
- **Deepak Purswani on HTLS Reconductoring:** Management clarified that Epsilon (France) supplies the carbon core, and the 12-month market potential is >INR10,000 crores with better-than-average margins.
- **Yash Kabra on PAT vs Revenue Growth:** Management explained that higher depreciation from capex, rising absolute interest costs, and a one-off tax provision impacted Q2 PAT growth; tax and PAT margins (5.5%-6%) will normalize.
- **Balamurali Krishna on Solar IPP Funding:** Management stated the ~INR3,900 crore solar project is debt-funded with ~INR500 crore equity funded via internal accruals over the next year.
- **Rahul on Share Pledging:** Management stated that 2% to 3% promoter shares are pledged for short-term borrowings, which will be released in 6 to 9 months.
- **Balamurali Krishna on Receivables >6 months:** Management acknowledged an increase and stated focused efforts to reduce them in the upcoming quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Transrail Lighting reported a strong Q2 FY27 with 32% revenue growth and 28% EBITDA growth, backed by robust execution, strong cash flows, and strategic expansion into solar IPP and advanced HTLS reconductoring.
