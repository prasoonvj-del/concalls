# Suzlon Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong sequential financial growth, record Q2 deliveries, and rating upgrades were offset by year-on-year EBITDA margin compression due to customer mix, non-recurring opex, and commodity/FX headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 4,869 crore in Q2 FY27, up 27% QoQ (H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 8,688 crore, up 24% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 720 crore in Q2 FY27, up 21% QoQ (H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 1,315 crore).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14.8% in Q2 FY27 vs 18.8% in Q2 FY26.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 394 crore in Q2 FY27, up 29% QoQ (H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 699 crore).
- **Deliveries:** 710 MW in Q2 FY27, up 40% QoQ (H1 FY27 Deliveries: 1,217 MW, up 21% YoY).
- **Commissioning:** 454 MW in Q2 FY27, up 69% YoY (H1 FY27 Commissioning: 723 MW, up 168% YoY).
- **Order Book:** 6 GW backlog providing 18 months of execution coverage (1.5 GW orders secured in H1 FY27).
- **Net Cash:** Rs 2,048 crore (Net worth crossed Rs 10,277 crore as of September 2026).

### Highlights
- Commissioning volume grew 69% YoY to 454 MW in Q2 FY27, with 1.25 GW of erected turbines in the pipeline awaiting future commissioning.
- CRISIL upgraded long-term credit ratings to AA-/Stable and short-term ratings to A1+.
- DevCo business model gained customer traction, booking 900 MW of orders in H1 FY27.
- Suzlon’s RE AMS (services) business maintains an installed base of over 16.5 GW with EBITDA margins above 40%.
- Foundry and forging segment generated Rs 167 crore revenue and Rs 50 crore EBITDA in Q2 FY27.
- Successfully commissioned the prototype for the first S175 5 MW turbine and bagged an initial order for 21 turbines (105 MW).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 WTG contribution margins to remain in the 22%–23% range.
- Full-year EBITDA margin is guided at 15% plus ~1% operating leverage upside in H2 FY27 (H2 volumes typically 1.5x–1.65x of H1).
- Management reaffirmed long-term Suzlon 2.0 targets by FY31: 40% domestic market share, 15 GW order book, 10 GW annual sales, 3 GW export order book, 1 GW export sales, and a 25% revenue CAGR trajectory.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India crossed 59 GW of installed wind capacity during the quarter; India's execution pipeline stands at >40 GW, with annual manufacturing capacity >20 GW.
- Approved Green Energy Corridor Phase-III will enable intrastate transmission and evacuation of up to 135 GW of RE capacity.
- Bidding momentum remains high, with 8–9 GW of wind RTC/FDRE/wind-dominated tenders awarded in H1 FY27.
- Annual domestic wind installations are expected to cross 10 GW in the near term and hit 15 GW by FY31.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 FY27 WTG contribution margin fell to 21.2% (vs 26.9% in Q2 FY26) due to customer/scope mix changes, segment mix shifts (~50 bps), FX/commodity volatility (~50 bps impact), and one-off costs.
- Non-recurring opex of ~Rs 50 crore for Suzlon 2.0 branding, travel, and SG&A reduced Q2 EBITDA margin by ~100 bps (expected to taper off in subsequent quarters).
- Non-pass-through raw materials include copper, aluminium, and carbon; steel price changes are largely pass-through.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fully operational manufacturing capacity stands at 4.5 GW (based on 3.3 MW turbines), with expansion underway via three AI-enabled smart blade factories.
- Total capital allocation target for the DevCo business model is capped at Rs 500 crore (current net deployment stands at Rs 125 crore–Rs 130 crore as assets are developed and monetized).
- Solar and BESS integrated offerings currently operate on an asset-light model; capex will begin after leadership teams and partnership terms are finalized in coming quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net cash position stood at Rs 2,048 crore (cash balance of ~Rs 2,300 crore; encumbered portion is roughly 40%–50%).
- Net working capital expanded due to seasonality and inventory buildup to support higher execution targets in H2 FY27.
- Finance costs were higher due to increased delivery volumes (+21% YoY) and working capital deployment for growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange volatility and rising commodity costs driven by ongoing West Asia geopolitics.
- Stretched working capital receivables linked partially to execution timelines and milestone-based commissioning pay-outs (5%–10% holdback per contract).
- Year-on-year margin volatility caused by client scope variations and execution site availability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** *Why did WTG contribution margins drop to 21.2% and what is the margin guidance?*
  **A:** Management explained the margin reduction was due to customer mix (150-180 bps), segment mix (50 bps), commodity/FX volatility (50 bps), and non-recurring branding/SG&A costs (100 bps). Standardized full-year EBITDA margin is expected at 15% with a potential +1% operating leverage benefit in H2.
- **Q:** *What is the progress and capital commitment in the DevCo, BESS, and solar models?*
  **A:** Total DevCo capital allocation is capped at Rs 500 crore (net commitment currently Rs 125–130 crore). DevCo yielded 900 MW in H1 orders. Solar and BESS are following an asset-light entry strategy without immediate capex.
- **Q:** *Why did financial performance at acquired entity Renom see profit declines despite top-line growth?*
  **A:** Management clarified Renom is a long-term ISP growth play. It added 178 MW in H1 FY27 and expects multi-gigawatt scaling as unserved market capacity expands.
- **Q:** *Is increased competition from Adani and JSW impacting market share?*
  **A:** Management noted Suzlon maintained ~28%-29% order market share and ~23% delivery share in H1 FY27. Competitor capacities are significantly allocated to internal platform consumption, and domestic market expansion to 15 GW annually provides ample room.
- **Q:** *Why did receivable days increase quarter-on-quarter?*
  **A:** Management attributed receivable increases to supply timing and billing retention tied to final commissioning stages (5% to 10%), which will convert to cash as 1.25 GW erected turbines are commissioned.

### One-line takeaway
Suzlon delivered strong sequential revenue and commissioning growth backed by a 6 GW order book and net cash balance sheet, though near-term margins remain constrained by raw material inflation and customer mix.
