# Suzlon Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sequential growth in revenue, deliveries, and commissioning, backed by a robust industry environment and a 6 GW order book.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 4,869 crore (up 27% QoQ from Rs 3,819 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 720 crore (up 21% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 394 crore (up 29% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 WTG Deliveries:** 710 MW (up 40% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 Commissioning:** 454 MW (up more than 69% from 269 MW in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 Deliveries:** 1,217 MW (up 21% YoY from 1,009 MW in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Commissioning:** 723 MW (up 168% YoY from 270 MW)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 8,688 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 1,315 crore
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 699 crore
- **Order Book:** 6 GW (1.5 GW secured in H1 FY27; provides ~18 months coverage)
- **Consolidated Net Worth:** Rs 10,277 crore
- **Net Cash Position:** Rs 2,048 crore (management also cited Rs 2,300 crore later in the call)
- **Foundry and Forging Q2 Revenue:** Rs 167 crore (EBITDA of Rs 50 crore)
- **Renom H1 Additions:** 178 MW

### Highlights
- Highest-ever second-quarter India deliveries (710 MW) and strong commissioning jump despite seasonal monsoon impact.
- Consolidated net worth crossed the Rs 10,000 crore mark.
- CRISIL upgraded long-term rating to AA-/Stable and short-term rating to A1+.
- Reorganized project execution by appointing 3 regional chief project officers with independent teams.
- RE Tech successfully commissioned the prototype of the first S175 5 MW turbine and secured a customer order for 21 turbines (105 MW).
- DevCo model secured 900 MW of orders booked in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be 1.5x to 1.65x of H1 in terms of business activity/deliveries.
- WTG contribution margins are expected to remain in the 22% to 23% range for FY27 (subject to externality normalization).
- Full-year EBITDA margin expected around 15% plus upside of 1%.
- Suzlon 2.0 targets a 5-year journey: aiming for 40% India market share by FY31, 15 GW order book exit by FY31, and a 25% CAGR trajectory.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India crossed 59 GW of installed wind capacity; annual installations expected to cross 10 GW in the near term and reach 15 GW by FY31.
- Green Energy Corridor Phase-III enables evacuation up to 135 GW of RE capacity.
- H1 FY27 saw 8–9 GW of wind RTC, FDRE, and wind-dominated tenders awarded.
- Industry wind equipment exports exceeded Rs 12,000 crore in FY26.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin impacted YoY by lower WTG contribution margins (declined from 26.9% to 21.2%) due to customer and scope mix changes, forex, and commodity volatility.
- Non-recurring opex (Suzlon 2.0 branding, travel, SG&A) increased operating expenses by ~Rs 80 crore in Q2 (including ~Rs 50 crore in specific costs), having a ~1% adverse impact on EBITDA margin.
- Commodity and FX impact accounted for ~50 bps impact on margins.
- Steel is largely a pass-through, but copper, aluminum, and carbon must be absorbed; COGS improvement programs are underway.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity of 4.5 GW is fully operational.
- Expanding footprint with 3 AI-enabled smart blade factories.
- DevCo capital allocation capped at Rs 500 crore (net commitment currently around Rs 125–130 crore).
- Solar and BESS expansion will follow an asset-light model with team building currently underway; capex will commence in subsequent quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash stands at Rs 2,048 crore to Rs 2,300 crore.
- Net working capital is higher due to seasonality and inventory buildup gearing up for higher H2 volumes.
- Adequate working capital limits tied up for execution.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia crisis driving high FX and commodity volatility (copper, aluminium, carbon) which cannot be fully passed through.
- Seasonal lean period during monsoon impacting execution pace.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital) asked about the depressed 21.2% contribution margin and fixed expenses.** Management explained that customer/scope mix (150-180 bps), segment mix (50 bps), non-recurring opex (100 bps), and commodity/FX (50 bps) drove the decline. Non-recurring costs will not repeat, and operating leverage is expected in H2. *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Shakuntala Agarwala (Individual Investor) questioned the competitiveness against Adani/JSW and Renom's low profitability relative to its acquisition cost.** Management replied that the market is expanding fast (8 GW+ demand in H1), Suzlon maintains a ~28–29% market share, and Renom is a long-term play to be scaled to multi-gigawatts. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Raj Shah (ENAM AMC) asked why margins fell to 14.8% despite prior guidance of ~17%.** Management clarified that the 15.1% H1 margin plus leverage effect points to ~15% plus upside for the full year, subject to commodity stabilization. *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Sweta Jain (Anand Rathi) asked about realization dips and working capital linkage to DevCo.** Management stated ASP was ~Rs 5.9 crore/MW based on contracted mix and project activity, clarifying that working capital is not driven by DevCo but by inventory buildup for H2. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Satpal Singh Khanuja (Ishaan Ventures) asked when installations will exceed deliveries and suggested bringing back detailed stage-wise installation slides.** Management stated an 18-month turnaround for sharper commissioning on the ground, and took the presentation suggestion under evaluation. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
Suzlon reported record Q2 deliveries and commissioning with a 6 GW order book and a robust net cash balance sheet, though margins faced near-term headwinds from commodity volatility and non-recurring branding expenses.
