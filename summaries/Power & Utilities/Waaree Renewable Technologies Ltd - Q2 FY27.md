# Waaree Renewable Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew YoY, but execution slowed down in the quarter due to land handover delays at the developer end, and margins compressed following the full consolidation of the T&D subsidiary (Associated Power Structures Pvt Ltd - APSPL), which also brought higher finance costs and depreciation due to acquisition financing.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY2027):** INR 1,057.84 crores (up 36.53% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY2027):** INR 159.98 crores
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY2027):** 15.12%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY2027):** INR 81.48 crores
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY2027):** INR 1,982.09 crores (up 43.84% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY2027):** INR 333.46 crores
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY2027):** 16.82%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (H1 FY2027):** INR 200.44 crores
- **Solar EPC Execution:** 456 MWp in Q2 FY2027; 1,345 MWp total for H1 FY2027
- **New Orders Won (Q2 FY2027):** 1.7 GWp
- **Consolidated Unexecuted Order Book (as of Sept 30, 2026):** > INR 5,500 crores (> INR 3,300 crores from Solar EPC and BESS; > INR 2,200 crores from T&D vertical)
- **Operational IPP Portfolio:** 82.82 MWp
- **O&M Portfolio:** 1.5 GWp
- **Azamnagar T&D Manufacturing Capacity:** 1 lakh metric tons
- **Finance Cost (Q2 FY2027):** INR 36.83 crores (increased from INR 9.59 crores in Q1)
- **Depreciation & Amortization (Q2 FY2027):** INR 26.97 crores (increased from INR 5.78 crores in Q1)

### Highlights
- Executed 456 MWp of solar EPC projects in Q2, taking H1 execution to 1,345 MWp.
- Secured 1.7 GWp of new orders during Q2.
- Transitioning from a pure solar EPC player into a broader energy transition EPC platform spanning solar, BESS, T&D, IPP, and O&M.
- APSPL acquisition fully consolidated, bringing a T&D order book of > INR 2,200 crores and an active order pipeline of > INR 20,000 crores.
- Finance costs and depreciation increased sharply due to debt-funded acquisition financing for APSPL (approx. 75% of the INR 1,225 crore acquisition funded through debt).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains positive on renewable energy, BESS, and T&D sectors backed by favorable policy support.
- Management expects consolidated EBITDA margin to be maintained at more than 15%.
- Existing unexecuted order book of INR 3,300 crores plus new inflows are expected to be converted into revenue over the next 3 to 4 quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's installed renewable energy capacity reached ~299 GW as of Sept 30, 2026 (Solar: ~171 GW, comprising >125 GW ground-mounted, ~33 GW grid-connected rooftop, ~12 GW hybrid/off-grid).
- Government approved PM Surya Sarovar Yojana (5 GW floating solar with 2 hours co-located storage).
- CEA estimates India's BESS requirement at ~208 GWh by 2030; BESS projects >1 MWh stand at ~144 GWh as of May 2026 (7.5 GWh commissioned, 42 GWh under construction, 39 GWh awarded, 55 GW in tender stage).
- Union Cabinet approved Green Energy Corridor Phase 3 scheme with an outlay of ~INR 1.86 lakh crores for intrastate transmission infrastructure (evacuation up to 135 GW RE and 50 GWh BESS by FY2032-33).
- Following domestic solar pipeline of ~25 GW and international pipeline of ~9 GW.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- T&D business operates at lower EBITDA margins (9% to 10%) compared to solar EPC, but management expects improvement through scale, operational excellence, and synergies.
- Depreciation and finance costs rose due to acquisition financing for APSPL; management stated interest costs will be optimized/reduced where possible in coming quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding T&D manufacturing capabilities via an additional facility at Azamnagar with a capacity of 1 lakh metric tons.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt levels increased due to acquisition financing for APSPL (~75% of INR 1,225 crores funded through debt).
- Management plans to evaluate ways to reduce debt in upcoming quarters.
- Non-fund-based bank limits and working capital facilities remain adequate with no constraints flagged by banks.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution slowdown in Q2 was attributed to land handover and connectivity delays at the developer's end.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ninad Sarpotdar (Desvelado Advisory) asked about the sharp drop in solar execution in Q2 (456 MW vs ~889 MW in Q1) and reasons for slow execution.**
   *Management answered that delays were primarily on the developer's end regarding land handover and connectivity, but noted that orders remain in line with expectations to be executed over the next 3 to 4 quarters.*
2. **Saksham Tyagi (Desvelado Advisory) asked about margin compression and the impact of APSPL acquisition financing on finance costs and depreciation.**
   *Management acknowledged that depreciation and finance costs rose due to the debt-funded acquisition, but stated that future operational contributions from APSPL will cover these obligations and that consolidated EBITDA margins are expected to be maintained above 15%.*
3. **Khushi Agarwal (JRK Stock Broking) asked about the relative growth and profitability contribution of solar EPC vs T&D going forward.**
   *Management responded that solar EPC will remain the core earnings driver, further supported by the T&D vertical.*
4. **Makarand Bhosekar (Nirmiti Investment Advisors) asked if the solar EPC business has plateaued given the 40% YoY/QoQ revenue decline.**
   *Management denied that the business has plateaued, reiterating that the decline was due to specific project-level land handover delays which will convert to revenue in subsequent quarters.*
5. **Soumya Raghuvanshi (Nirva Securities) asked about the competitive intensity and pricing discipline in utility-scale EPC bidding.**
   *Management stated that utility-scale is a growing market, order inflow and pipeline remain strong, and the company focuses on maintaining threshold margins through operational efficiency.*
6. **Yohan Khinvasara (Asian Broking) asked if the company plans to stay leveraged at current levels due to the acquisition debt.**
   *Management answered that while the debt was taken for the acquisition, they will assess ways to reduce it through group initiatives in upcoming quarters.*

### One-line takeaway
Waaree Renewable Technologies reported 36.5% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 to INR 1,057.84 crores, though solar execution slowed due to developer-end land delays and profitability faced pressure from debt-funded acquisition financing and consolidation of the lower-margin T&D business.
