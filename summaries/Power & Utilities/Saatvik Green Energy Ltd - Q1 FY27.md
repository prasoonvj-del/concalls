# Saatvik Green Energy Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed – Operational performance and profits were impacted by global headwinds and delayed customer procurement, but long-term integration plans and order book remain strong.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 511.0 crore (INR 5,110 million), down 44.19% YoY from Rs 915.7 crore (INR 9,157 million) in Q1 FY26 and down 68.21% QoQ from Rs 1,607.7 crore in Q4 FY26.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 42.5 crore (INR 425 million), down 76.08% YoY from Rs 177.7 crore in Q1 FY26; EBITDA margin stood at 8.33% (up 108 bps QoQ from 7.25% in Q4 FY26, but down 1,108 bps YoY from 19.41%).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 5.4 crore (INR 54 million), down 95.37% YoY from Rs 116.6 crore in Q1 FY26 and down 91.06% QoQ from Rs 60.4 crore.
- **Production Volume:** 408 MW vs 935 MW in Q4 FY26 and 685 MW in Q1 FY26.
- **Sales Volume:** 334 MW vs 1,050 MW in Q4 FY26 and 579 MW in Q1 FY26.
- **Confirmed Order Book:** ~6.35 GW, valued at approximately Rs 8,200 crore (~132% of current operational module capacity of 4.8 GW).
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.99.

### Highlights
- Secured a domestic solar PV module order worth Rs 138 crore in July 2026 (execution by December 2026) and orders aggregating ~Rs 400 crore on August 11, 2026 (execution by March 2027).
- Expansion into B2C segments, solar kits (Saatvik SuryaConnect), hybrid inverters (UDAY Plus), and transformer manufacturing via the acquisition of Melcon.
- Target to increase non-solar module business from 4%–5% to 7%–10% of revenue in FY27 and 15% by FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Full-Year Guidance:** Reaffirmed guidance of 3.5 to 4.0 GW module sales, revenue of ~Rs 6,000 crore, EBITDA margin of ~12%, and PAT margin of 6% to 7%.
- **Cell Manufacturing Ramp-up:** Full ramp-up expected by Q4 FY27 to achieve ~80% capacity utilization.
- Expects significant EBITDA margin expansion in H2 FY27 once captive cell lines are operational.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ALMM-2 implementation was extended from July 1, 2026 to January 1, 2027 due to a domestic cell supply deficit.
- C&I and utility customers adopted a wait-and-watch approach in Q1 due to geopolitical tensions (Iran war starting Feb 20), high commodity prices, freight hikes, and uncertainty over ALMM timelines.
- Order book mix: ~70% utility and ~30% C&I / open access. ~30% of total order book is Domestic Cell Requirement (DCR) compliant.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins in Q1 FY27 were affected by raw material price volatility, elevated freight/logistics costs, foreign exchange fluctuations, and a lack of cell-level backward integration.
- Management adopted a selective order execution strategy to avoid low-margin/unprofitable volumes where cost pass-through was not feasible.
- Captive cell manufacturing is expected to yield 18%–20% margins on cell components.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Odisha Project Phase 1 (57 acres):** Includes 2.4 GW cell manufacturing capacity and 4.0 GW module manufacturing capacity (total capex ~Rs 1,850 crore). ALMM-2 inspection scheduled for September 2026.
- **Odisha Project Phase 2 (27 acres):** 3.6 GW cell capacity expansion (capex ~Rs 1,600–1,700 crore), expanding total cell capacity to 6.0 GW. Target completion by end of FY28.
- **Odisha Project Phase 3:** Planning initiated for 6.0 GW ingot and wafer manufacturing capacity, targeted for completion in FY29 to align with ALMM-3.
- Expanding encapsulant manufacturing capacity from 2.0 GW to 5.0 GW.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total capex incurred to date: ~Rs 1,000 crore out of the Phase 1 & Phase 2 total planned capex of ~Rs 3,500 crore.
- Existing total debt stands at ~Rs 1,250 crore (primarily project debt for Odisha).
- Peak net debt is expected to reach ~Rs 2,200 crore to Rs 2,400 crore as phase expansions progress.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued global geopolitical instability impacting raw material prices (polysilicon/cells) and freight rates.
- Slower-than-expected commercial commissioning of Odisha Phase 1 cell capacity or delays in ALMM-2 approvals.
- High proportion of fixed-price contracts (~60%–70% of utility orders and 30% of C&I orders) exposing margins to unhedged raw material cost increases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Prakhar Porwal, Ambit):** Where do you see total debt peaking and what is the capex breakdown?  
  **A (Management):** Current debt is ~Rs 1,250 crore with ~Rs 1,000 crore capex incurred to date. Total Phase 1 capex is ~Rs 1,850 crore and Phase 2 is ~Rs 1,600–1,700 crore. Net debt is expected to peak at around Rs 2,200 to Rs 2,400 crore.
- **Q (Prakhar Porwal, Ambit):** What is the breakdown of fixed vs. variable contracts in the order book?  
  **A (Management):** For modules, C&I orders (~30% of total) are fixed price. For utility orders, ~30%–40% are variable price contracts, while the remainder are fixed price contracts.
- **Q (Mahesh Kumar):** Why did EBITDA margins compress in Q1 and when will cell integration benefit margins?  
  **A (Management):** Module-only manufacturing has become crowded, and input costs surged due to geopolitics and currency movements without immediate pass-through. Captive cell production starting in H2 FY27 will expand margins into high double-digits.
- **Q (Maria Mittal):** What share will non-solar module/ancillary businesses contribute to revenue?  
  **A (Management):** Currently at 4%–5%, targeted to reach 7%–10% in FY27 and 15% in FY28 as offerings expand into transformers, inverters, and battery storage.
- **Q (Nimish Pandya):** What is the scale and target for the newly acquired Melcon transformer business?  
  **A (Management):** Addressing a Rs 30,000 crore market (growing to Rs 55,000 crore by 2031), the company aims to achieve 8%–10% market share and build a Rs 1,000–1,500 crore revenue business over the next 3–4 years.

### One-line takeaway
Saatvik's Q1 FY27 performance saw volume and margin contraction due to raw material and execution delays, but management expects a strong recovery in H2 FY27 driven by its upcoming 2.4 GW cell integration and a Rs 8,200 crore order book.
