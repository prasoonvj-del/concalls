# Power & Utilities - Renewable power producers: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season highlights aggressive capacity scaling across solar, wind, and hybrid portfolios, paired with an accelerated push into Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) to solve grid balancing and transmission bottlenecks. While top-line growth and order books remain robust across most players, managements faced headwinds from localized grid curtailments, Right-of-Way (ROW) delays, and margin compression stemming from customer mix, labor volatility, and debt-funded acquisitions.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the sector agree that power demand is surging, driven by industrial electrification, C&I clients meeting ESG goals, and rapidly expanding data centers and AI hyperscalers. ACME Solar, Adani Green, CleanMax, and Advait Energy specifically noted strong traction for integrated Firm and Dispatchable Renewable Energy (FDRE) and RTC alternatives.
* **Divergence:** While most players view solar and solar-hybrid as primary drivers, Orient Green Power diverged by highlighting surplus solar capacity and afternoon grid absorption issues in Tamil Nadu, prompting a strategic shift toward wind-focused organic growth.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreements:** Companies noted that module prices remain manageable (e.g., non-DCR modules below Rs 12/watt noted by Juniper Green; future modules negotiated at <Rs 17/watt by ACME Solar). Borrowing costs are generally stabilizing, with weighted average debt costs hovering between 8.3% and 9.1% (e.g., CleanMax at 8.3%, Adani Green at 8.9%, Juniper Green at 8.46%, Orient Green at 9.09%).
* **Disagreements & Pressures:** Margin pressures varied by business model. EPC and construction-heavy players like K.P. Energy and Waaree Renewable Technologies cited margin compression due to labor volatility, ROW costs, and acquisition-related finance/depreciation charges. Meanwhile, Suzlon Energy and Inox Green pointed to customer/scope mix and geopolitical commodity/FX volatility impacting short-term margins.

## Capex & capacity
Capital expenditures remain massive as companies race to build out generation and storage infrastructure. Adani Green outlined a Rs 2.4 lakh crore capex plan through FY30. CleanMax is deploying over Rs 10,000 crore annually (CapWIP at Rs 6,700 crore). Juniper Green incurred Rs 3,800 crore in H1 FY27 (cumulative capex near Rs 19,000 crore). Simultaneously, BESS deployment is moving from pilot phases to core operations, with ACME Solar commissioning 5.2 GWh, Adani Green reaching 6.6 GWh, and Juniper Green adding 500 MWh.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Revisions:** ACME Solar raised its FY27 BESS target from 10 GWh to >12 GWh and its 2030 target from 10 GW to 15 GW. Conversely, Inox Green *withdrew* its FY27 revenue guidance due to a strategic realignment prioritizing equipment supply over short-term volume. KPI Green and CleanMax maintained their trajectory.
* **Tone Shifts:** ACME Solar adopted a more aggressive stance, framing BESS as a "cash engine." K.P. Energy shifted to a more cautious tone, accepting compressed margins as the "new normal" for EPC operations. Waaree Renewables acknowledged a slower Q2 execution pace due to developer-end land handover delays.

## What we were watching
* 🟢 **MoP allows ISTS-renewable projects to shift to intra-state transmission connectivity:** ✅ showed up — ACME Solar highlighted benefits from Green Energy Corridor Phase III and grid connectivity frameworks.
* 🟢 **CERC Connectivity and General Network Access regulations:** ✅ showed up — ACME Solar and Juniper Green discussed GNA timelines and transmission dependencies.
* ⚪ **Supreme Court 70:30 GST formula rulings:** — not discussed.
* ⚪ **SECI annulled floating solar and BESS tender:** — not discussed.
* ⚪ **Cabinet VGF scheme for grid storage & transmission corridors:** 🟡 partly — referenced generally via sector-wide BESS momentum, though specific VGF scheme mechanics were not core discussion items.

## What to watch next quarter
* Resolution of Right-of-Way (ROW) issues and transmission line commissioning timelines (such as Khavda Phase 3 for Adani Green and transmission line delays for Juniper Green and CleanMax).
* Execution progress on newly acquired assets (e.g., Alfanar/Netra integration by KPI Green; Wind World portfolio consolidation by Inox Green).
* Impact of battery supply chain dynamics and potential Chinese export taxes on BESS deployment costs.
