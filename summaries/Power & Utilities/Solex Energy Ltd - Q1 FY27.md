# Solex Energy Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew slightly YoY, but profitability declined due to seasonally softer volumes, higher operating expenses, and the full-quarter impact of depreciation and interest on expanded capacity.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 265.6 crore (vs Rs 261.0 crore in Q1 FY26, +1.8% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 33.8 crore (vs Rs 42.7 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin: 12.7% (vs 16.4% in Q1 FY26)
- Depreciation and Amortization: Rs 10.2 crore (vs Rs 4.3 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Finance Cost: Rs 12.5 crore (vs Rs 5.4 crore in Q1 FY26)
- PBT: Rs 11.1 crore
- PAT: Rs 8.3 crore
- PAT Margin: 3.1%
- EPS: Rs 7.39
- Executable order pipeline: Rs 845.84 crore targeted for execution by December 31, 2026 (includes a Rs 42.47 crore August 2026 order and an LOI for a further Rs 175 crore order)
- Total order book visibility: Approximately Rs 3,400 crore
- Average capacity utilization assumption for FY27: Around 55%

### Highlights
- Completed listing of existing shares on BSE main board (no fresh capital raised); now traded on both NSE and BSE with over 11,000+ shareholders.
- Securing new orders: Rs 42.47 crore order from a domestic private limited company in August 2026 for N-type TOPCon 620W peak glass-to-glass modules; LOI received for an additional Rs 175 crore order.
- Industry disruption from ALMM-2 (ALCM) on cells: Uncertainty regarding cell manufacturing timelines until clarification on May 25, 2026, resulted in temporary project holds and customer-driven delivery reschedules.
- Cell manufacturing project (5 GW total planned, first phase 2.2 GW N-type TOPCon+): On track for commissioning by end of calendar year 2027; debt component (~Rs 700 crore) at advanced stage, equity component (~Rs 350 crore via NCDs/CCDs) progressing through due diligence.
- Land procured for cell project; electricity connection application for 30 MW submitted to Gujarat government with verbal approval received.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reaffirmed FY27 revenue guidance and PAT margin in the range of 5% to 6%.
- Expects a seasonally stronger H2 (H1-H2 curve typically follows a 1:3 pattern).

### Demand & industry commentary
- H1 is seasonally softer for utility-scale solar project execution in India, while H2 sees the bulk of module dispatches and revenue conversion.
- Grid stability and load curtailment issues in Gujarat/Western India are temporary and expected to ease with National Green Grid integration and BESS adoption.
- Standalone module manufacturers face no major survival threat due to rapidly growing captive, rooftop, and C&I markets, despite higher domestic cell costs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed costs, depreciation, and interest for the 4 GW capacity were absorbed over a smaller revenue base in the seasonally weak quarter.
- Working capital deployment was higher, with a larger share met through fund-based limits rather than non-fund-based facilities.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 4 GW module manufacturing capacity at Tadkeshwar, Gujarat is fully operational; no rigid capex planned for additional module capacity due to market availability.
- Cell project cost revised to ~Rs 1,050 crore (funded by ~Rs 700 crore principal debt and ~Rs 350 crore equity/margin).
- Onboarding an experienced TOPCon cell manufacturer on a KPI-linked basis for design, commissioning, and operations.
- BESS manufacturing evaluation ongoing, planned to be housed in a separate subsidiary under Vision 2030 (which targets 10 GW module, 10 GW cell, 10 GW BESS, and 2 GW wafer/ingot capacity).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory levels remain elevated due to seasonal factors (monsoons delaying installations) and are expected to clear out after monsoons.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Customer-driven rescheduling of module deliveries due to policy confusion surrounding ALMM/ALCM deadlines in May/June 2026 (though management emphasized these are timing shifts, not cancellations).
- Execution and operational complexities associated with TOPCon cell manufacturing in India (addressed via technical partnerships and expert onboarding).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rishi (Investo/Individual) asked about capex plan changes from Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 1,050 crore:** Vipul Shah explained that additional module capacity plans were dropped as market capacity is readily available, reducing capex by ~Rs 200 crore.
- **Manan Shah (Moneybee) asked about the weak Q1 performance despite capacity availability:** Vipul Shah cited higher depreciation/interest, lower government EPC revenue, and customer-driven deferrals due to ALMM confusion.
- **Manan Shah asked about ALMM-2 vulnerability in the order book:** Chetan Shah stated that grandfathered projects and only ~20% of other orders had vulnerability, which is now resolved via government extensions.
- **Ashish Golechha (Bee Ventures) asked how Solex plans to handle cell manufacturing execution risks:** Chetan Shah explained they are mitigating manpower and recipe challenges by partnering with ISC Konstanz and onboarding professional cell manufacturing operators.
- **Krunal Patel asked if domestic cell prices will match imported Chinese cells:** Chetan Shah stated domestic cell costs will initially be higher than imported ones.

### One-line takeaway
Solex Energy reported a muted Q1 FY27 due to seasonal softness and policy-driven delivery delays, but management remains confident in a stronger H2 execution pipeline and a medium-term earnings inflection from upcoming backward integration into cell manufacturing.
