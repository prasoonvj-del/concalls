# Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust order book expansion, raised capex guidance, and imminent commissioning of new digital infrastructure offset regulatory accounting impacts.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Capex guidance raised from Rs 37,000 crore to Rs 40,000 crore; capitalization guidance set at Rs 25,000 crore to Rs 30,000 crore.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more optimistic with clear commercial readiness in new verticals (data centres) and massive renewable energy integration pipelines (GEC-III).
- New topics or risks: Pang evacuation scheme shifted from HVDC to an AC solution (~Rs 31,000 crore) due to high-altitude cosmic/UV challenges; SPV rationalization merged 28 TBCB SPVs into 2. ROW and land compensation remain persistent operational factors.
- Last quarter's promises: Data centre commissioning promised as "imminent" in Q1 was delivered, with the Manesar facility ready for commercial operation within weeks.

### Key numbers
- **Fixed Assets:** Rs 3.29 lakh crore (consolidated), Rs 2.80 lakh crore (standalone).
- **Network Stats:** 1.87 lakh circuit kilometres, 6.5 lakh MVA transformation capacity.
- **System Availability:** 99.81% (Transmission availability).
- **Tripping per line:** 0.17 (Srinagar-Dras line).
- **Q2 Elements Commissioned:** 981 circuit km and 14,600 MVA transformation capacity.
- **H1 Elements Commissioned:** 2,616 circuit km and 25,100 MVA capacity.
- **H1 TBCB Annual Tariff Wins:** Interstate Rs 7,664 crore (9 projects), Intrastate Rs 443 crore (2 projects), Total Rs 8,107 crore.
- **Works in Hand (Order Book):** Rs 2.20 lakh crore (TBCB Rs 1.95 lakh crore, RTM Rs 0.25 lakh crore).
- **Bidding Pipeline:** Rs 67,233 crore (Interstate and Intrastate under bidding + to be floated).
- **Receivable Dues:** 10.54 days.
- **Financial Growth:** Operating income growth of 7.7% YoY; PAT growth of 8% YoY.
- **Borrowings Tied Up:** JBIC green loan JPY 80 billion, SBI term loan Rs 10,000 crore, UBI Rs 5,000 crore, bonds Rs 2,000 crore.

### Highlights
- Completed major 765 kV lines: Koppal-II PS – Raichur, Bidar PS – Maheshwaram, and Sikar-II – Khetri.
- Completed SPV rationalization by merging 28 TBCB SPVs into 2 SPVs for improved governance.
- Revised Pang evacuation scheme from HVDC to an AC solution (~Rs 31,000 crore) due to extreme high-altitude technical complexities.
- First commercial data centre in Manesar (1,000 racks, 14 MW, Tier III certified, sovereign cloud) is fully ready and set to commence operations in a few weeks.
- Upgraded National Transmission Asset Management Centre and operationalized I3SOC cybersecurity operations.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Capex Guidance:** Raised to Rs 40,000 crore (from Rs 37,000 crore).
- **Capitalization Guidance:** Rs 25,000 crore to Rs 30,000 crore.
- **Future Pipeline:** Green Energy Corridor-III (Intrastate) of Rs 1.36 lakh crore and Rs 50,000 crore for BESS.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Driven by electricity-intensive growth across industry, agriculture, transport, and digitalization, leading to massive renewable energy integration needs.
- Anticipates significant future tender flow for ~22 HVDC projects (~127 GW) in Brahmaputra basin, Khavda, and Rajasthan.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Regulatory Drag:** Impacted by -Rs 354 crore on account of regulatory tariff trajectory and -Rs 193 crore due to differential tariff orders from CERC.
- Equipment supply constraints (transformers) have eased through bulk procurement strategies.
- Price variation clauses exist for commodities like copper and aluminum, with negative price movements acting as a windfall for the company.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Khavda-Nagpur HVDC project execution is progressing on schedule.
- Market-based land compensation guidelines are expected to drive up absolute capex outlays as states adopt them.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-equity ratio stands at 59.41 (headroom available up to 1:1.5).
- Outstanding receivables remain exceptionally low at 10.54 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Right of Way (ROW) and land owner identification challenges persist across states, though mitigated by state adoption of market-based compensation rules.
- High-altitude environmental challenges (UV rays, cosmic rays) restrict advanced technology deployment like HVDC in remote regions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** Asked about capitalization guidance and ROW issues. Management responded that market-rate land compensation adoption by states is easing ROW, and bulk procurement has eliminated equipment shortages.
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** Asked about TBCB IRR profiles and HVDC pipeline visibility. Management confirmed IRRs remain encouraging and highlighted ~22 HVDC projects (~127 GW) in the planning stage.
- **Namit Arora (IndGrowth Capital):** Asked about data centre and BESS expansion plans. Management stated the Manesar pilot data centre commissioning is imminent, land is secured for a second southern facility, and Section 62 RTM routes are ready for BESS.
- **Atul Tiwari (JP Morgan):** Asked whether ROW issues would impact FY28 capitalization. Management clarified that projects are not stalled, state machineries take time to realign, and gestation periods naturally span across quarters.
- **Mahesh Patil (ICICI Securities):** Asked about competitive intensity from non-transmission players and Khavda-Nagpur progress. Management welcomed competition, affirmed return profiles remain protected, and stated Khavda works are on schedule.

### One-line takeaway
Power Grid delivered steady operational expansion backed by a Rs 2.20 lakh crore order book, raised its capex guidance to Rs 40,000 crore, and prepared to commercialize its maiden sovereign data centre.
