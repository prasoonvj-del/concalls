# Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong operational growth in transmission income (+7.7% YoY), expanding capex guidance, robust TBCB project wins, and a Rs 2.20 lakh crore order book.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Capex guidance raised from Rs 37,000 crore to Rs 37,000–40,000 crore (targeting ~Rs 40,000 crore); Capitalization guidance revised to Rs 25,000–30,000 crore (was Rs 30,000 crore).
- **Tone and demand view:** Demand outlook remains strongly positive, propelled by electricity-intensive growth, GEC-III Intrastate projects (Rs 1.36 lakh crore), BESS (Rs 50,000 crore), and 22 planned HVDC projects across the country.
- **New topics or risks:** Introduction of Ministry of Power's market-based land compensation guidelines requires state-by-state adoption, creating temporary procedural delays in clear land acquisition/ROW; commercial pilot data centre (14 MW, 1,000 racks) set to commission within weeks.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered complete commissioning of TBCB projects (Ramgarh-II, Bidar) and major 765 kV / 400 kV lines (Koppal-II PS links).

### Key numbers
- **Fixed Assets (Consolidated):** ~Rs 3.29 lakh crore (Standalone Gross Fixed Assets: Rs 2.8 lakh crore).
- **Consolidated PAT Growth:** 8% YoY (Standalone PAT growth: 7% YoY).
- **Transmission Operating Income Growth:** +7.7% YoY (+Rs 887 crore).
- **Receivable Days:** 10.54 days.
- **System Availability:** 99.81% (Tripping per line: 0.17).
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 59.41 : 40.59.
- **Q2 Additions:** 981 circuit km transmission lines, 14,600 MVA transformation capacity.
- **H1 Additions:** 2,616 circuit km transmission lines, 25,100 MVA transformation capacity.
- **H1 TBCB Wins:** 9 ISTS projects (Rs 7,664 crore annual tariff) + 2 Intrastate projects (Rs 443 crore annual tariff); total annual tariff won: Rs 8,107 crore.
- **Order Book (Works in Hand):** Rs 2.20 lakh crore (TBCB: Rs 1.95 lakh crore, RTM: Rs 0.25 lakh crore, Data centre/storage/smart meters: Rs 0.04 lakh crore).
- **Bidding Pipeline:** Rs 67,233 crore (Rs 63,086 crore active bidding + Rs 4,147 crore to be floated).
- **Segment Revenues:** Telecom Rs 283 crore, Consultancy ~Rs 400 crore.

### Highlights
- Secured major projects including the Barmer HVDC project and the first series of 8 synchronous condensers.
- Converted the Pang evacuation scheme from an HVDC topology to an AC solution (~Rs 31,000 crore) due to altitude and cosmic ray complexities.
- Merged 28 TBCB SPVs into 2 SPVs to streamline governance.
- Commercial 14 MW, 1,000-rack Tier-III certified sovereign cloud data centre in Manesar to be commissioned within a few weeks.
- Operationalized I3SOC (Security Operations Centre) and upgraded the National Transmission Asset Management Centre.
- Developed an indigenous 100 kg payload material drone and deployed a 220 kV mobile GIS.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Capex:** Targeted at Rs 37,000 crore to Rs 40,000 crore for FY27; management expects annual capex to remain well above Rs 40,000 crore in FY28/FY29.
- **Capitalization:** Guided at Rs 25,000 crore to Rs 30,000 crore for FY27.
- Management expects ~22 HVDC projects totaling 127 GW capacity (including Brahmaputra basin, Khavda, and Rajasthan routes) to be tendered over time.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Electricity-intensive growth across industry, transport, agriculture, and digitalization is driving generation and transmission demand.
- GEC-III presents a Rs 1.36 lakh crore Intrastate opportunity, and BESS presents a Rs 50,000 crore opportunity (largely under TBCB route), facilitating 135 GW of RE integration.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Transmission operating income grew 7.7% YoY, but reported income was offset by regulatory asset adjustments (-Rs 354 crore trajectory shift and -Rs 193 crore differential CERC order timing).
- Equipment contracts feature predefined price variation formulas tied to market indices for commodities like copper and aluminum; positive price movements are accommodated in cost cushions, while negative movements benefit Power Grid.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Khavda-Nagpur HVDC project is progressing on schedule on its 4-year timeline, with transformers being delivered and firewall construction underway.
- Pursuing Section 62 (RTM route) regulatory petitions for integrated battery energy storage systems (BESS) for grid stability in Northern and Western regions.
- Secured land for a second data centre location in Southern India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Tied up fresh long-term financing: JBIC Green Loan (JPY 80 billion), SBI term loan (Rs 10,000 crore), Union Bank of India (Rs 5,000 crore), and bonds (Rs 2,000 crore).
- Receivable days maintained at 10.54 days with minimal outstanding dues beyond 45 days.
- Interest income from subsidiaries increased YoY to Rs 1,201 crore (from Rs 794 crore); JV dividends rose to Rs 60 crore (from Rs 24 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Right of Way (ROW):** Implementation of new Ministry of Power land compensation guidelines relies on individual state government notifications and local land valuation processes, causing temporary execution friction.
- **Execution Delays during Festive Season:** Project work experiences temporary pushbacks during major national holiday periods.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** *Are capitalization delays due to supply chain or ROW issues?*
  **Management:** Transformer supply constraints are completely resolved via bulk procurement. The Rs 25,000–30,000 crore capitalization target reflects state-level administrative timelines in deploying new market-rate land compensation guidelines.
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital):** *What are the upcoming HVDC project opportunities?*
  **Management:** CEA/CTUIL have planned ~22 HVDC projects (127 GW capacity) across Brahmaputra basin, Khavda, and Rajasthan corridors; bidding for two projects in FY28 is likely.
- **Atul Tiwari (JP Morgan):** *Will ROW issues spill over to affect FY28 capitalization?*
  **Management:** No projects are stalled. Transmission commissioning timelines are governed by TSAs rather than strict financial year quarters; delayed completions naturally roll over into immediate subsequent quarters.
- **Mahesh Patil (ICICI Securities):** *Does entry of road EPC players in TBCB bidding impact Power Grid's return profile?*
  **Management:** Returns will not be dictated by new entrants. Power Grid maintains strong return profiles while winning and executing competitive bids.
- **Mohit Pandey (Citi):** *How are project returns protected against commodity inflation like copper?*
  **Management:** Pre-bid equipment supplier tie-ups and price variation clauses with fixed index weightages cushion against commodity inflation; price declines act as a windfall gain.

### One-line takeaway
Power Grid is expanding its capex trajectory to ~Rs 40,000 crore backed by a Rs 2.20 lakh crore order book, robust TBCB win rates, and new growth vectors in storage and data centres.
