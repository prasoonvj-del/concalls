# ACME Solar Holdings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY top-line and net profit growth, paired with upgraded operational targets for FY27 and FY30.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** 
  - FY27 BESS commissioning guidance raised from >10 GWh to >12 GWh.
  - 2030 targets raised from 10 GW RE with 25 GWh BESS to 15 GW RE with 50 GWh BESS.
  - Capex spend: H1 FY27 spend stood at Rs 6,500 crore; H2 FY27 spend expected to exceed H1 spend.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained strongly positive; demand view highlighted a structural pivot by discoms from RTC (thermal/gas) to short-term and medium-term peak power BESS contracts.
- **New topics or risks:** 
  - New topics: 6% Chinese export tax effective January 2027; government policy initiatives (Green Energy Corridor Phase III, Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana, CTUIL non-ROFR BESS connectivity, SECI 4 GW / 16 GWh VGF tender).
  - Disappeared: Discussion on ACME Suryodaya incident.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Achieved target of commissioning >5 GWh BESS in H1 FY27 (reached 5.2 GWh till date).

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** Rs 1,007 crore (+67.5% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 874 crore (+63.6% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 86.7% (Renewable generation: 90.8%; BESS operations: 78.3%)
- **PAT:** Rs 269 crore (+133.5% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 26.7%
- **Asset Base:** Rs 28,462 crore (up Rs 11,466 crore YoY)
- **CUF:** 27.8% (vs 24.1% YoY)
- **Power Generation:** 1,840 million units (+19.6% YoY)
- **BESS Short-term Power Sold:** 324 million units
- **Commissioned BESS Capacity:** ~5.2 GWh till date

### Highlights
- Signed 430 MW FDRE PPAs during Q2 and an additional 400 MW FDRE PPA with NTPC post-quarter, bringing signed PPAs to 92% of the 8,370 MW contracted portfolio.
- CRISIL upgraded ACME Solar Holdings' credit rating outlook to AA- Positive from AA- Stable.
- Locked in short-term BESS contracted revenues of >Rs 1,600 crore for FY27 (partial sale) and >Rs 1,500 crore for FY28 (partial sale).
- Revised 400 MW NTPC PPA tariff down by Rs 0.33/unit (Rs 0.11 GST/change-in-law, Rs 0.22 voluntary) while extending battery storage duration from 4 to 4.5 hours.

### Guidance & outlook
- **BESS Commissioning:** Target raised to >12 GWh for FY27.
- **2030 Roadmap:** Raised targets to 15 GW RE and 50 GWh BESS storage.
- **RE Capacity Additions:** Expecting complete commissioning of 1.5 GW in FY27 (dependent on GNA readiness) and 2.5–3 GW in FY28.
- **Contracted BESS Volume:** FY27 BESS capacity 90–95% tied up; FY28 BESS capacity 50–60% tied up.
- Management expects to win an incremental 5 GW of PPA bids over the next 12 to 18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Shift in state discom demand on the DEEP portal away from RTC thermal/gas contracts to BESS peak power procurement.
- SECI launched a 4 GW / 16 GWh (4-hour) peak power tender offering VGF of Rs 15 lakh/MWh with a 12-month commissioning timeline.
- Central regulatory updates now allow curtailed solar power (ahead of GNA line commissioning) to charge BESS for merchant/short-term sale.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- BESS capex for brownfield operational substations sits between Rs 90 lakh to Rs 1 crore per MWh (saving ~Rs 20 lakh/MWh vs greenfield due to existing AC infrastructure).
- DCR ALMM 2 module price quotes for mid-2027 delivery are below Rs 17/unit.
- Lower lithium carbonate prices offset higher copper costs and the upcoming 6% Chinese export tax; management expects overall battery capex to continue trending downward.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Spent Rs 6,500 crore on capex in H1 FY27 (Rs 3,500 crore capitalized, Rs 3,000 crore CWIP); H2 FY27 spend is expected to be higher.
- Possesses ~7 GWh of batteries on-site with LCs opened for an additional 5 GWh yet to be delivered.
- Secured/applied for ~10 GW of RE connectivity inventory and granted >5 GW of BESS connectivity.
- Cumulative contracted battery supply stands at ~19 GWh across CATL, Hithium, Ganfeng, Trina, and CLOU (4 GWh added in Q2).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stood at ~Rs 26,500 crore; Gross block stood at ~Rs 28,500 crore.
- Cash balance stood at ~Rs 10,000 crore at quarter-end following late-quarter disbursements.
- Weighted average cost of debt for operational projects is ~8.4%; ~65% of borrowings are from long-term institutional lenders (PFC, REC, IREDA, NaBFID).
- Under-construction project debt carrying ~9%+ interest will be refinanced sub-9% upon commissioning. Debt book has 0% foreign currency exposure.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Transmission GNA delays impacting the full commissioning timelines of hybrid/FDRE plants.
- Chinese 6% battery export tax starting January 2027 and potential cell supply chain friction.
- Merchant exposure to spot market (IEX) power prices for BESS charging prior to co-located solar completion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Revision in capacity additions and capex given transmission delays?**  
  *A:* Full FDRE commissioning relies on GNA availability (targeting 1.5 GW in FY27, which may shift by a quarter), while partial element commissioning will hit 2.5–3 GW. H1 capex spend was Rs 6,500 crore, and H2 spend will exceed H1.
- **Q: What drove the tariff reduction on the 400 MW NTPC FDRE PPA?**  
  *A:* Total tariff dropped by Rs 0.33/unit (Rs 0.11 GST impact reduction from 12% to 5%, and Rs 0.22 voluntary reduction), while battery duration was expanded to 4.5 hours.
- **Q: How realistic is the 15 GW 2030 target given grid connectivity constraints?**  
  *A:* Targeted substations have planned commissioning dates between 2028 and early 2029. With >10 GW connectivity inventory secured, contracted capacity can reach 12–15 GW by late 2027.
- **Q: Does the 15 GW target assume power off-take by parent company ACME Cleantech?**  
  *A:* No, 15 GW factors in only AAA utility counterparties (SECI, NTPC, states). Any demand from ACME Cleantech for green ammonia or data centers would be additive.
- **Q: How will BESS spreads and capex behave over the next 2–3 years?**  
  *A:* BESS capex remains sub-Rs 1 crore/MWh. Dropping lithium carbonate prices balance higher copper costs and the 6% Chinese tax. Peak power spreads remain robust due to severe discom peak deficits.

### One-line takeaway
ACME Solar delivered robust Q2 growth backed by scaling BESS revenues and raised its 2030 targets to 15 GW RE and 50 GWh BESS, anchored by long-term PPAs and state peak power demand.
