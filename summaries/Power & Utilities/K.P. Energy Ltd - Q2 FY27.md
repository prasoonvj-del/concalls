# K.P. Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited persistent geopolitical headwinds, labor volatility, and Right-of-Way (ROW) cost pressures squeezing operating margins despite strong revenue scaling.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (management guided that margins will stay at current levels for the next 2 to 4 quarters).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from acknowledging headwinds to accepting compressed margins as the "new normal" for EPC operations amid industry saturation.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Change in statutory auditors to MSKC & Associates LLP; ongoing discussions on the Saudi-based Raz Holding Group LOI mentioned.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: IPP portfolio currently stands at 48.5 MW operational with 202 MW under development.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 540 crore (up ~78% YoY from Rs 303 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 539 crore (vs Rs 301 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Infrastructure Development Revenue:** Rs 527 crore (up ~82% YoY from Rs 289 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **O&M Revenue:** Rs 3.2 crore (vs Rs 1.72 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Sale of Power Revenue:** Rs 8.41 crore (vs Rs 10.01 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Gross Margin:** Approximately 19% (broadly in line with Q1 FY27)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 38.86 crore (vs Rs 37.44 crore in Q1 FY27; Rs 88.72 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 26.92 crore (vs Rs 26.08 crore in Q1 FY27; Rs 53.18 crore in H1 FY27 vs Rs 61.37 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Depreciation:** Rs 9.27 crore
- **Finance Costs:** Rs 17.11 crore
- **Order Book:** ~1.64 GW valued at ~Rs 1,940 crores as of September 30, 2026
- **IPP Portfolio:** 48.5 MW operational with 202 MW under development
- **IPP Generation:** 2.75 crore units in Q2 FY27; 5.92 crore units in H1 FY27
- **Inventory:** Rs 1,067 crore (down from Rs 1,330 crore)

### Highlights
- Consolidated H1 FY27 total income reached Rs 1,062 crore (71% of total FY26 income).
- Transitioning into KP 3.0, focusing on full wind EPC capabilities rather than large IPP expansion.
- Appointed MSKC & Associates LLP as statutory auditors effective Q2 FY27, succeeding MAAK & Associates.
- Related-party transactions constituted roughly 50% of the quarter's revenue.
- Bidding for approximately 1 gigawatt of tenders (estimated value Rs 7,000 crore to Rs 8,000 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated that EBITDA margins will stay at current levels for the next 2 to 4 quarters.
- Management guided that debt-to-equity will not exceed 2 in the future.
- India's wind capacity additions are projected by MNRE at 7.5 to 8 gigawatts in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India added 3 gigawatts of wind capacity in H1 FY27; cumulative installed capacity crossed 59 gigawatts.
- Government targets 100 gigawatts by 2030 and 136 gigawatts by 2036.
- Indian wind turbine manufacturing capacity doubled to 24 gigawatts with 70% to 80% localization.
- Gujarat leads the country with over 17 gigawatts of installed wind capacity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin pressure driven by volatile labor costs, idling expenses due to site delays, and higher Right-of-Way (ROW) compensation costs paid to landowners/farmers.
- Fixed-price contracts limit the ability to fully pass on unexpected cost spikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No large IPP plans for KP Energy beyond the 202 MW under development, keeping future capex requirements constrained.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory reduced to Rs 1,067 crore from Rs 1,330 crore.
- Debt-to-equity ceiling set at 2 under KP 3.0.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution vulnerabilities from unpredicted Right-of-Way (ROW) delays, labor availability, and crane/equipment shortages.
- Prolonged geopolitical disruptions affecting fuel, logistics, and material costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nishant (Individual Investor) on Profit Decline:** Asked why profits dropped YoY. Management responded that margins have compressed due to increased scale and external market/geopolitical realities.
- **Nitin Sethi (Individual Investor) on Margin Sustainability:** Asked if 12% margins are the new normal. Management confirmed margins will stay at this level for the next 2 to 4 quarters as industry competition tapers pricing.
- **Parth Kotak (Plus91 Asset Management) on Capex & Debt:** Asked about capex and leverage. Management stated capex will remain low as no large IPP expansion is planned beyond 202 MW, and debt-to-equity will not exceed 2.
- **Ritesh Bhagwati (Alpha Plus Capital) on Group Contracts:** Asked about margins on group orders. Management clarified all group entity contracts are at arm's length and priced at prevailing market rates.
- **Darshil Pandya (Finterest Capital) on Cost Pressures:** Questioned what drove the sharp margin drop from previous years. Management reiterated that human-driven project execution, labor idling, and higher ROW costs impacted profitability.
- **Darshan Chandra (Individual Investor) on Guidance Accuracy:** Frustrated that prior margin guidance of 18%-20% dropped to 12%. Management defended their stance, stating they cannot mislead investors and must reflect reality.

### One-line takeaway
While K.P. Energy achieved massive top-line scaling, persistent cost pressures, labor volatility, and higher Right-of-Way expenses compressed margins, leading management to guide for stable, lower-tier profitability over the next 2 to 4 quarters.
