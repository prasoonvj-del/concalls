# Ion Exchange India Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth across all segments was offset by significant profitability headwinds due to legacy project execution, geopolitical factors, and stabilization costs at the new Roha facility.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 701 crore (+20% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 32 crore (-49% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 4.54%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 3 crore.
*   **PAT Margin:** 0.44%.
*   **Order Book:** Rs 2,473 crore (as of June 2026).
*   **Bid Pipeline:** Rs 9,777 crore.
*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   **Treatment Solutions:** Revenue Rs 210 crore (+14% YoY); EBIT loss Rs 17 crore.
    *   **Industrial Products:** Revenue Rs 105 crore (+14% YoY); EBIT Rs 13 crore (11.89% margin).
    *   **Lifecycle Services:** Revenue Rs 72 crore (+28% YoY); EBIT Rs 7 crore (9.92% margin).
    *   **Specialty Chemicals:** Revenue Rs 230 crore (+21% YoY); EBIT Rs 22 crore (-52% YoY).
    *   **Consumer Products:** Revenue Rs 112 crore (+33% YoY); Loss Rs 34 lakhs.

### Highlights
*   **Segment Reclassification:** The company split the former "Engineering" segment into three distinct reporting segments: Treatment Solutions, Industrial Products, and Lifecycle Services to improve transparency.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Roha resin facility is commissioned and undergoing stabilization; pharma resin capacity expansion (6x) at Ankleshwar is in progress.
*   **New Wins:** Secured a ~$52 million contract from Hyundai (announced post-quarter).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Profitability:** Management aims to restore double-digit profitability over time but noted it will take time.
*   **Consumer Products:** Management targets breaking even in FY27.
*   **Roha Utilization:** Management maintains the 25% utilization guidance for the first year despite a soft start.
*   **Legacy Projects:** Management expects significant progress on large legacy projects by the end of FY27, though the UP project will likely spill into the next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Growth Drivers:** Strong demand anticipated in semiconductor, data centers, and green hydrogen (electrolyzers).
*   **Strategy:** Focus on "India and beyond" with a pivot toward high-tech, advanced, and emerging solutions (e.g., resource recovery, PFAS destruction) to improve margin mix.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Compression:** Specialty Chemicals EBIT declined 52% YoY, impacted by ~6% due to Roha facility costs and 3-4% due to the absence of FX gains seen in Q1 FY26.
*   **Input Costs:** Management noted some moderation in crude prices but stated the impact on input costs has not yet fully materialized.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Roha Facility:** Designed for 5x total resin capacity expansion (Phase I doubled capacity; Phase II + debottlenecking to reach 5x).
*   **Membranes:** Second manufacturing plant in Goa is under construction to expand UF/MBR range.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Legacy Constraints:** The UP project (government-funded) continues to constrain cash flow; unexecuted portion is ~11% of the total order backlog.
*   **Asset-Light Focus:** Management intends to remain asset-light for BOOT/BOO projects.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Legacy Projects:** Management admitted they are "not out of the woods" regarding legacy project execution, which continues to drag on profitability.
*   **Geopolitical Uncertainty:** Ongoing crises are affecting invoicing and supply chain stability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the reclassification?** Management stated it was to provide transparency into the growing products, chemicals, and services businesses, which now comprise ~60% of the company, versus the 40% contribution from Treatment Solutions.
*   **Q: Is the worst over for Treatment Solutions?** Management clarified that while a significant part of the legacy projects is complete, a sizable portion remains; they are mitigating this by adding more profitable, selective projects like the Hyundai contract.
*   **Q: Can you quantify the Roha 5x capacity?** Management explained this is a global aspiration; Phase I doubled capacity, and total output will reach 5x the original Ankleshwar baseline after Phase II and debottlenecking.
*   **Q: Are you pursuing semiconductor opportunities?** Yes, the company is actively bidding for ultra-pure water projects in the semiconductor and solar sectors, leveraging past experience and new technology partnerships.
*   **Q: Will the UP project close this year?** Management stated it is premature to say it will close this year; it is dependent on government fund flow and will likely spill into the next financial year.

### One-line takeaway
Ion Exchange is pivoting toward a more transparent, product-and-service-led business model, but near-term earnings remain pressured by legacy project overhangs and stabilization costs at the new Roha facility.
