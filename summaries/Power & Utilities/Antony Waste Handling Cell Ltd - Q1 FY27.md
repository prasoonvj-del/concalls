# Antony Waste Handling Cell Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth was offset by significant margin compression, a tragic operational incident, and one-time extraordinary expenses.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 269 crore (+6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 45 crore (-27% YoY, -33% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 16.8% (vs 24.4% in Q1 FY26 and 22.8% in Q4 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 0.7 crore (vs Rs 23 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Total Tonnage:** 1.4 million tons (+5% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 324 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Tragic Incident:** A legacy waste mound collapse at the PCMC Waste-to-Energy (WtE) facility resulted in nine fatalities. The company is providing financial and medical support to affected families.
*   **Refinancing:** Successfully refinanced the Antony Lara Renewable Energy term loan, reducing interest rates from 10.25% to 8.25%.
*   **New Contract:** Secured a Rs 243 crore project from Greater Noida Industrial Development Authority for electrical/mechanical road sweeping (5-year term, starts Q3 FY27).
*   **Project Completion:** The CIDCO bio-mining project was completed during the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **WtE Recovery:** Management expects the WtE plant to resume operations by the first week of October.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Revenue growth in FY27 is expected to be driven by the BMC contract (starting Q3) and the Atkoli project (operational by Q4).
*   **Margins:** Management expects margin profiles to improve from Q1 levels, targeting a return to historical trends (22-24%) by FY28 as new contracts ramp up and cost escalations are passed through.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management aims to shift the portfolio mix toward a 50-50 split between Collection & Transportation (C&T) and Processing/WtE.
*   Industry is facing inflationary pressures in fuel, repairs, maintenance, and additives.
*   Management stated that existing portfolio growth is 6-9% (via tipping fee escalations), with new business adding 10-15%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Compression Drivers:** 1) Higher operating expenses (vehicle hiring/transportation at CIDCO); 2) Employee costs rose 18% YoY (now 34% of revenue); 3) Deferral of Q4 FY26 waste disposal activities added Rs 10 crore in incremental expenses.
*   **Finance Costs:** Increased 35% YoY due to a higher debt base prior to refinancing.
*   **Exceptional Items:** Rs 7 crore one-time prepayment charge for loan refinancing; expected impairment charge of Rs 22-24 crore for WtE facility damage (to be treated as an extraordinary item).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Andhra Projects:** Kadapa and Kurnool projects are on schedule; civil designs are complete, and contractors have mobilized.
*   **Atkoli Project:** Expected to be operational by Q4 FY27, adding 600-800 tons per day capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Gross debt at Rs 435 crore; cash and bank balances at Rs 111 crore.
*   **Leverage:** Net debt-to-equity ratio at 0.4x.
*   **Working Capital:** DSOs remained stable at 114 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Operational Risk:** Ongoing regulatory review of the WtE plant following the accident.
*   **Margin Volatility:** Management noted that quarterly performance is subject to volatility due to labour-centric costs and fuel price fluctuations, which are not always immediately offset by contractual escalations.
*   **Force Majeure:** Damage to WtE project assets due to extreme monsoon conditions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Ronak Shah (Equirus):** Asked about WtE revenue impact. Management confirmed revenue will be impacted until the plant restarts in October, with fixed costs of Rs 2.5-3 crore per month.
*   **Manish Agarwal (Tradeswift):** Questioned the Rs 10 crore incremental transportation cost. Management clarified it was a one-time closure cost for the completed CIDCO bio-mining project and will not recur.
*   **Nitesh (Retail Investor):** Challenged the recurring "surprises" and margin misses. Management defended the business as utility-like and advised investors to view performance over a longer time horizon rather than quarterly.
*   **Ketan Chheda (Retail Investor):** Asked about potential for Compressed Biogas (CBG) projects. Management confirmed they are evaluating CBG as part of larger integrated waste projects.

### One-line takeaway
Q1 FY27 was a transitional quarter impacted by a tragic operational incident, one-time closure costs, and margin compression, though management remains confident in FY27 growth driven by new contract ramp-ups.
