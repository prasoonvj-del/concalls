# GK Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong H1 revenue growth, successful expansion into rooftop solar and BESS segments, robust institutional order wins, and significant post-quarter collections.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 907.8 crore (up 38.9% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 160.5 crore (up 21.6% YoY) at a margin of 17.7%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 109 crore (up 30.7% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 402.7 crore (up 12.3% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 74.4 crore at a margin of 18.5%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 49.4 crore (up 6.3% YoY) at a margin of 12.3%
- **Trade Receivables (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 1,012.38 crore (204 days outstanding)
- **Post-Q2 Collections (Oct 1 to Oct 28, 2026):** Rs 377.5 crore (bringing outstanding down to ~128 days)
- **Q2 Installations:** 19,391 systems (up 41.8% YoY); Capacity Commissioned: 84 MW (up 33.3% YoY)
- **H1 Installations:** 43,509 systems (up 77.6% YoY); Capacity Commissioned: 193 MW (up 70.8% YoY)
- **Cumulative Base:** Over 1,84,000 systems and over 800 MW across India
- **Order Book (as of Sept 30 + Oct onwards):** Rs 788 crore
- **Cumulative FY Orders Received (excluding BESS):** Rs 1,422.29 crore (including GST)
- **BESS Order:** 150 MW / 300 MWh utility-scale installation

### Highlights
- Built a broader renewable energy platform across retail rooftop, residential/commercial B2C, agriculture pumps, and utility BESS.
- Received institutional orders for 1 lakh rooftop solar systems and 14,000 off-grid DC Solar PV water pumping systems in Maharashtra (October 2026).
- Entered the Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) segment with a 150 MW / 300 MWh utility-scale order; aims to expand to 2 GW in the midterm.
- Operates on an asset-light, controlled manufacturing model with partners across 8 states.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management intends to maintain EBITDA margins of 17% to 18% for FY27.
- Management expects a 35% to 40% growth even if PM KUSUM is delayed, but remains on track for potential doubling of FY26 numbers if PM KUSUM rolls out well in Q3/Q4.
- 2030 Ambition: 1 million installed systems and USD 1 billion business.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Out of 11.8+ crore farmers in India, only 3 crore have irrigation; 7.8 million farmers lack pumping solutions.
- India's grid-connected rooftop capacity reached 33.4 GW (rooftop additions grew 69% in FY26 to 8.7 GW).
- CEA/MNRE projections cite India's battery storage requirement rising from ~35 GWh in FY27 to ~236 GWh by FY32.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Asset-light, controlled manufacturing model avoids heavy fixed asset capex and lowers transportation costs.
- Rooftop segment cash-in cash-out cycle takes around 45 to 60 days.
- Secured confirmatory agreements for 875 MW of solar cells/modules for the current year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocation focuses on execution, brand, technology, and customer reach rather than fixed asset manufacturing plants.
- Present in 6 to 7 states, focusing on markets with maximum market share rather than aggressive pan-India expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables stood at 204 days as of September 30, 2026, which reduced to ~128 days after collecting Rs 377.5 crore in October. Year-end receivables expected around 120 to 150 days.
- Management has no intent to dilute equity at current price bands; existing debt and generated profits are sufficient for funding targets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Monsoon slows field installations in Q2, making it the seasonally weakest quarter.
- Potential technical discussions and product upgradation thoughts causing rollout schedules for government schemes like PM KUSUM.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishita Jain (Sapphire Capital) on revenue visibility vs. order book:** Management clarified that the Rs 1,800 crore figure mentioned in media interviews represents total revenue visibility/pipeline for the year (including executed orders), whereas the Rs 788 crore is the active order book.
2. **Nishita Jain on margin sustainability:** Management affirmed confidence in maintaining 17%-18% EBITDA margins for FY27 due to proper planning and controlled manufacturing.
3. **Sweta Jain on solar pump debtor days:** Management explained that while H1 closing showed 204 days, post-quarter collections of Rs 377.5 crore reduced outstanding days to ~128 days, aligned with a normal 120-day cycle.
4. **Jainam Vora on PM KUSUM 2.0 delay:** Management stated they do not view the scheme as delayed, having consistently guided for Q3 arrival with financial impact in Q4.
5. **Jainam Vora on BESS business model:** Management clarified they are setting up their own BESS plants (capex model) to procure power from DISCOMs and sell it back, securing a fixed revenue model for 15 years.
6. **Aditya Srinivasan on equity dilution:** Management stated they have no plans to dilute equity at current price bands, as promoter holdings are around 79% and must eventually come down to 75% within two years.

### One-line takeaway
GK Energy delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 38.9% revenue growth, expanded into rooftop solar and BESS utility storage, and significantly improved working capital through post-quarter collections.
