# Triveni Power Transmission Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Resilient operating and financial performance with strong order booking, growing aftermarket and defence segments, and positive free cash flow.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Growth of 6% YoY and 60% sequentially.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Growth of 18% YoY; total H1 revenue stood at Rs 189 crore.
- **EBITDA Margins:** 36.1% in Q2 FY27 and 35.7% in H1 FY27 (moderated YoY due to base effect and product mix, but ahead sequentially).
- **Aftermarket Revenue Growth:** Up ~41% YoY in Q2 FY27; up 29% in H1 FY27, contributing 27% to total revenue.
- **Order Booking:** Rs 126 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 48% YoY); Rs 330 crore in H1 FY27 (up 95% YoY). Cumulative order booking for H1: Product Rs 132 crore, Aftermarket Rs 64 crore, Defence Rs 135 crore.
- **Export Product Orders:** Rs 12 crore in Q1 FY27; Rs 36 crore in Q2 FY27 (3x increase).
- **Closing Order Position (as of September 30, 2026):** Rs 626 crore, up 57% YoY from Rs 398 crore. (Breakdown: Rs 156 crore for gears in FY27 and Rs 81 crore for Defence; closing gear order book up 15% QoQ).
- **Free Cash Flow:** Positive free cash flow after capex of approximately Rs 13 crore in H1 FY27.
- **Core Working Capital Days:** 188 days as of September end vs 193 days in March.
- **Capex:** Total H1 FY27 capex was Rs 41 crore (capitalisation of Rs 10 crore and CWIP of Rs 31 crore). Total long-term capex program of Rs 340 crore (evenly split between gears and defence) is largely concluded.

### Highlights
- First earnings conference call following the listing of TPTL.
- Portfolio becoming more diversified with strong growth in aftermarket and defence, balancing product business modulation.
- Significant new orders secured from Middle Eastern (including ADNOC) and African customers.
- Defence revenue scaled meaningfully from a low base, supported by the inauguration and production at a new defence facility.
- Strategic focus expanding into new industrial areas: space ventures (gearbox qualifications) and data centres.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide specific quarterly or annual revenue/margin forecasts, but anticipates a very positive H2 FY27 across product, aftermarket, and defence segments.
- Medium-term growth ambition (over next 5 to 7 years) is to deliver a sustained CAGR of over 25% YoY.
- Long-term EBITDA margin target is maintained at 35%+.
- Incremental capex in the near and medium term will primarily be balancing/routine capex and small capacity enhancements. Next major capex cycle trigger will be around 80%-85% capacity utilisation.

### Demand & industry commentary
- External research estimates the combined OEM and aftermarket ecosystem for specialized global high-speed industrial gearboxes at ~$1.1 billion in 2026, growing at 6%-7% through 2033.
- India's FY27 Ministry of Defence allocation is Rs 7.85 lakh crore, with Rs 2.19 lakh crore capital outlay (21.8% above FY26 BE) and Rs 1.39 lakh crore earmarked for domestic procurement.
- MENA region capex shows some delivery delays due to regional turbulence, but customer demand and steady stream orders (ADNOC, etc.) remain intact.
- Global high-speed gearbox market has 7-9 major competitors (mostly European); largest player holds ~15% market share. TPTL's global market share is in low single digits.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- YoY margin moderation driven by product mix leading to higher material cost percentage and continued investments in people and manufacturing capabilities.
- People/HR cost additions will continue but at a small scale.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Long-term capex program of Rs 340 crore is almost complete. Combined capex creates a total peak revenue potential of ~Rs 700 crores for gearboxes plus significant defence revenue.
- Manufacturing speed advantages driven by inventory investments, digital transformation, supply chain relationships, and flexible 3-shift/weekend work capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet allows funding of growth primarily through internal accruals.
- Working capital improved due to realization of overdue receivables.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Engineered-to-order nature of the business and long/lumpy deployment, approval, and procurement cycles in defence can cause quarter-to-quarter variability in revenue recognition and order placement.
- Global geopolitical and energy price risks create short-term uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Srinivasareddy (Yellow Investments) on order book delivery schedule:** Management stated gear product orders have a 6-8 month delivery cycle, aftermarket 3-6 months, and defence varies (some 12+ months). Rs 156 crore is slated for FY27 gears and Rs 81 crore for defence, but exact quarterly splits are not provided.
2. **Gaurav Shukla on medium-term growth guidance:** Management stated the 5-7 year growth ambition is >25% CAGR YoY, supported by order book visibility and international expansion.
3. **Ashish Golechha on competition from RENK/Elecon and naval position:** Management noted that international competitors in South India have been present for years without impacting TPTL's market share, and highlighted that TPTL is the sole Indian company with complete ab-initio technology for marine gearboxes.
4. **Sanjay Manyal on defence margin dilution:** Management clarified that in the medium-to-long term, defence will not be margin dilutive and the company aims to maintain EBITDA margins above 35%.
5. **Divij Punjabi on defence capacity and import substitution:** Management noted the facility is multimodal with no immediate further capex needed, and highlighted that procurement is shifting from 100% imports (historically) to "Make in India."
6. **Neil Bahal on capacity utilisation triggers:** Management stated that the next phase of expansion capex will be triggered when utilisation reaches 80%-85%, though they are not at that threshold yet.

### One-line takeaway
Triveni Power Transmission delivered a resilient H1 FY27 with 18% revenue growth, strong order booking (up 57% YoY), and positive free cash flow, while expanding its global footprint, aftermarket, and defence verticals.
