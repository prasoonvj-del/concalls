# Concord Enviro Systems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue and profitability were impacted by near-term supply chain disruptions due to the Middle East conflict.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR853 million (vs INR1,024 million in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** Negative INR149 million (vs negative INR9 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Net Loss:** INR176 million (vs PAT of INR41 million in Q1 FY26)
- **Order Book:** INR699 crores
- **Trading segment revenue loss:** INR15 odd crores
- **Manufacturing orders down:** INR42 crores to INR43 crores
- **Overall revenue impact:** INR50 crores to INR55 crores
- **WaHa investment stake:** Less than 2% for USD575,000
- **European ZLD order size:** Roughly EUR600,000

### Highlights
- Order book increased to INR699 crores, driven by S&P order book.
- Secured first ZLD order from Europe (metal slag value recovery in the European market) worth EUR600,000, with delivery expected in Q3.
- Partnered with California-based WaHa for atmospheric water generation and humidity control, investing for a sub-2% stake (USD575,000) and securing exclusive rights for India and UAE alongside global manufacturing partnership.
- Successfully delivered first project with new raw effluent (REM) membranes in partnership with NALA Membranes for the textile and paper industry.
- Appointed Shleshank Laheri as Group CFO.
- H-Xtreme heat exchanger gaining market traction, offering fuel savings and up to 90% efficiency.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects execution to normalize by the end of Q2 FY27 and growth momentum to strengthen in H2 FY27.
- Targeting overall order intake of about INR1,000 crores for the financial year.
- Expects 15% to 20% growth possibilities over the next two years, with EBITDA margins stabilizing around 14% to 16% (or 12% to 16% over the next two-three years) once revenue crosses INR850 crores.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Witnessing healthy order inflows across core segments (industrial water treatment, ZLD, recycling, process separation).
- Steel sector driving strong demand for ZLD due to water scarcity for production targets; waste pickle liquor treatment solution demonstrated at one of India's largest steel manufacturers.
- Solar sector showing strong traction with larger players adopting innovative solutions for efficiency.
- Carbon capture expected to become a very large segment in 3 to 5 years (potentially larger than water), with Concord focusing on the biological route.
- Exploring data center and battery manufacturing opportunities via dehumidification and water recycling (supported by the WaHa partnership).
- Targeting semiconductor industry membrane replacement market via US partners working with Micron.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Temporary negative impact on EBITDA margins due to investments in talent and execution for larger projects.
- Freight rates and spare parts costs started coming down in July after supply chain disruptions caused by the Middle East conflict.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested USD575,000 for a minor stake (<2%) in WaHa for technology access and global manufacturing rights.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Roserve Water as a Service platform scaling up, building recurring/annuity-style revenues.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain disruptions due to the Middle East conflict impacted Q1 execution.
- H2 execution depends on stable operating conditions over the upcoming six weeks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Disha (Sapphire Capital) on revenue loss quantification:** Management stated trading segment lost ~INR15 crores and manufacturing orders down were ~INR42-43 crores, totaling INR50-55 crores.
2. **Disha on annual growth and order intake targets:** Management noted an order intake target of ~INR1,000 crores for the year, expecting to exceed and deliver growth in H2 based on the INR699 crore order book.
3. **Nikhil Gupta on WaHa partnership financials:** Management clarified the stake is <2% for a cash infusion of USD575,000, securing exclusive India/UAE rights and a licensing arrangement.
4. **Subrata Sarkar on semiconductor strategy:** Management explained they are working through a US partner collaborating with Micron to first target the membrane replacement market before project segments.
5. **Subrata Sarkar on carbon capture timeline:** Management stated carbon capture will take 3 to 5 years to become a large segment, focusing on the biological route.
6. **Nikhil Tiwari on ZLD competitors:** Management named Ion Exchange, Thermax, Arvind Envisol, Praj Engineering, Permionics, and Hyper Filtration as key competitors.

### One-line takeaway
Concord Enviro's Q1 FY27 performance was dented by Middle East supply chain disruptions causing a net loss, but management anticipates a strong H2 rebound backed by a robust INR699 crore order book and strategic global partnerships.
