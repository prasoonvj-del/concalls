# Indian Energy Exchange Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth in electricity and gas segments, coupled with management confidence in technology integration and market leadership despite regulatory headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 203 crore (up 10.7% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 139 crore (up 12.9% YoY).
*   **Electricity trading volume:** 39.7 billion units (up 12.7% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 electricity volume:** 77 billion units (up 14.2% YoY).
*   **IGX Q2 PAT:** Rs 23.4 crore (up 144% YoY).
*   **IGX Q2 gas volume:** 42 million MMBtu (up 160% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **RTM Dominance:** Real-Time Market (RTM) share reached ~42% of total electricity volume, surpassing the Day-Ahead Market (DAM) for the second consecutive quarter.
*   **RTM Growth:** Volumes reached 16.5 billion units (up 10.5% YoY).
*   **REC Segment:** Traded 15.3 lakh certificates, significantly lower than 44 lakh in Q2 FY26 due to new buyout regulations.
*   **ICX Performance:** Revenue stood at Rs 2.3 crore (up 20.8% YoY); issued 52 lakh I-RECs.
*   **Coal Exchange:** Incorporated Indian Coal Exchange Ltd as a wholly-owned subsidiary; license application submitted to Coal Controller Organisation.
*   **IGX IPO:** Process remains on track to reduce IEX holding from 47.3% to 25% by December 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Management expects 15% growth in electricity volume for the full year.
*   **IGX Outlook:** Management expects 40%-50% YoY volume growth.
*   **ICX Outlook:** Management expects 20%-25% revenue growth.
*   **Industry:** Management believes power demand growth will drive exchange volumes; thermal capacity addition target revised to 115 GW by 2035.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Power Demand:** H1 FY27 demand grew 10.2% YoY to 897 billion units; Q2 FY27 demand grew 11.5% YoY to 502 billion units.
*   **Price Volatility:** Weak monsoon drove DAM buy bids up 120% YoY; average DAM prices rose 46% YoY to Rs 5.72/unit; average RTM prices rose 49% YoY to Rs 5.24/unit.
*   **Coal Supply:** Inventory at ~8 days as of Oct 22, 2026; ~100 plants at critical 3-day level.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Treasury income returns are currently in the 15%-18% range.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Technology:** Focus on AI bidding assistants for DISCOMs and automated solutions for industrial users.
*   **Integration:** API integration with major customers completed for both front-end (bidding) and back-office (reconciliation/settlement).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Investments:** Standalone investments stood at Rs 2,170 crore against shareholder equity of ~Rs 1,388 crore.
*   **Liabilities:** Other financial liabilities (settlement/margin) were ~Rs 1,051 crore.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Management stated they are evaluating all options, including share buybacks, to create value for shareholders; dividend payout remains ~60-65% of profits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Market Coupling:** Management noted implementation is "challenging," requires significant preparation (software/hardware/mock trials), and expects it will take 1.5-2 years post-regulation.
*   **REC Volumes:** Impacted by new buyout regulations; designated consumers are currently "sitting on the fence."
*   **Regulatory Uncertainty:** CERC draft regulations on market coupling and RTM scheduling (reducing gate closure from 75 to 50 minutes).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is there no movement on market coupling?** A: Management stated they are in no hurry and emphasized that implementation requires extensive preparation (1.5-2 years) post-regulation.
*   **Q: Why are DAM volumes sluggish vs TAM?** A: High demand/shortage led to bilateral contracts; prices have since corrected, and DAM/RTM volumes are now increasing.
*   **Q: Why not do a share buyback given the stock price?** A: Management is evaluating all options to create shareholder value and will consider buybacks.
*   **Q: Are you worried about DISCOMs migrating to other exchanges?** A: No, management emphasized that IEX is tightly integrated via APIs (front and back-office) with major customers.
*   **Q: Impact of coupling on IEX?** A: Management believes they have strengthened customer relationships and technology to retain market position.

### One-line takeaway
IEX delivered steady growth in electricity and gas volumes, with management focusing on technology integration and product diversification while navigating regulatory uncertainty regarding market coupling.
