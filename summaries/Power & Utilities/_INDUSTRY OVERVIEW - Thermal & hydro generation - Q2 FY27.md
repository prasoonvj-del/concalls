# Power & Utilities - Thermal & hydro generation: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Management commentary across the Power & Utilities sector reflects robust momentum, driven by strong double-digit growth in all-India power demand (+10% to +15% YoY in H1/Q2 FY27, with October demand surging up to 21% YoY). Severe heatwaves, delayed monsoons, and lower hydro reservoir levels forced thermal generation to step up sharply—meeting 73% of incremental energy demand (Adani Power). 

While long-term thermal additions are accelerating to meet baseload requirements (especially from data centers), key short-term operational headwinds include low domestic coal stockpiles (~96 plants operating under critical levels) and grid transmission evacuation bottlenecks causing renewable energy curtailment (8.8% to 11%).

---

## Where the industry is heading

**Business model so far**  
Historically, power utilities operated on utility-scale long-term Cost-Plus Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) with state DISCOMs, earning fixed regulated returns on equity. Over the last five years, the business model has rapidly expanded into competitive bidding for hybrid renewables, merchant/short-term power sales, and backward integration into equipment manufacturing and captive C&I supply arrangements. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
* **Data center baseload demand driving thermal expansion**: *scaling* — Data centers are emerging as critical 24/7 power consumers requiring steady thermal baseload power, prompting developers to expand thermal pipelines alongside renewables [Adani Power concall Q2 FY27; Powerica concall Q2 FY27].
* **Shift toward Pumped Storage Projects (PSP) & BESS**: *scaling* — State DISCOMs are moving away from plain vanilla solar PPAs due to daytime peak gluts, pushing generators into large-scale pumped hydro storage and battery energy storage [NHPC concall Q2 FY27; Tata Power concall Q2 FY27; NTPC concall Q2 FY27].
* **Evacuation bottlenecks driving high RE curtailment**: *scaling* — Transmission delays in major solar/wind belts (Khavda) elevated curtailments up to 11%, forcing utilities to shift connectivity to intra-state networks or await permanent General Network Access (GNA) completion [Tata Power concall Q2 FY27; JSW Energy concall Q2 FY27; NTPC concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
* **Connectivity and General Network Access regulations**: Rewards generators by standardizing open transmission access; JSW Energy expects 60% of curtailed RE capacity to transition to permanent GNA by end-Q3 FY27 [Govt: CERC Connectivity and General Network Access regulations, Nov 2026].
* **Grid Storage & Viability Gap Funding (VGF)**: Supports large-scale BESS and storage initiatives across major utilities [Govt: Cabinet clears Rs 14,500 crore viability gap funding scheme for grid storage, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
All managements report strong power demand growth across India. Q2 all-India demand grew 10%–11.5% YoY, and October MTD demand expanded 12%–21% YoY. Industrial activity, weather conditions, urban transport, and data center buildouts are cited as key drivers.

### Divergence
* **Merchant Tariffs & Realizations**: JSW Energy achieved merchant realizations of ~Rs 6.9/unit (a 20% premium over DAM average) and reported its Q3 merchant capacity is fully booked at equal or higher rates. Adani Power noted short-term merchant tariffs jumped 33% YoY. Conversely, regulated utilities like NTPC and NHPC remain predominantly anchored to long-term regulated cost-plus tariffs.
* **Vanilla Solar Contracting**: NHPC highlighted that state utilities are hesitant to execute 25-year long-term PPAs for plain vanilla solar power due to midday power gluts, forcing a pivot to hybrid RE, PSPs, and RTC power, whereas JSW Energy reported strong group-captive demand doubling to ~7 GW by 2032.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

* **Fuel Costs & Domestic Coal Stocks**: Domestic coal stock levels hit multi-year lows in October (~96 plants on critical stocks with 6–7 days inventory vs 19–20 days norm). API4 benchmark imported coal averaged ~$115/tonne (+28% YoY), causing Adani Power's fuel costs to rise 41.8% YoY. JSW Energy mitigated imported coal costs via domestic linkage blending under the SHAKTI scheme and job-work arrangements.
* **Equipment & Raw Material Inflation**: Powerica flagged a fresh round of Q3 FY27 price inflation in steel, copper, and aluminum, prompting calibrated price hikes for diesel gensets. Tata Power is monitoring potential Chinese technology export controls for its solar ingot/wafer equipment sourcing.
* **RE Curtailment Financial Impact**: Grid bottlenecks drove RE curtailment to 11% at Tata Power (up from 8.8% in Q1) and cost JSW Energy Rs 59 crore in Q2 lost revenue (225 MUs curtailed).

## Capex & capacity

* **Adani Power**: Secured 62% long-term PPAs for its upcoming 24 GW thermal pipeline; targeting 45 GW total capacity by FY32. Synchronized 660 MW Korba Phase-II Unit-1 (COD Q3 FY27). Entered hydro via 330 MW GVK Alaknanda acquisition and 770 MW Bhutan JV.
* **JSW Energy**: On track for Rs 20,000 crore capex in FY27. Added 1.7 GW capacity since April 2026 (taking operational capacity above 15.1 GW). Placed TG orders for 3,200 MW Salboni thermal plant; targeting 30 GW total capacity by 2030.
* **NHPC**: Target capex of Rs 14,300 crore in FY27. Commissioned 250 MW Subansiri Lower Unit-5 in October 2026. Target capacity additions: 1,744 MW in FY27 and 1,250 MW in FY28. Developing 18 GW of Pumped Storage Projects (PSPs).
* **NTPC**: Group H1 capex reached Rs 23,932 crore with an under-construction pipeline of >37 GW (17.32 GW thermal, 3.6 GW hydro, ~16 GW RE). Scaled down FY27 addition guidance from 9.5 GW to ~8 GW due to RE transmission bottlenecks. Planning to tender 4.8 GW thermal capacity in Q1/Q2 FY28 (capex ~Rs 67,000 crore).
* **Tata Power**: Executing Rs 25,000 crore FY27 capex plan; targeting 2.5 GW RE additions in FY27. Approved 1,800 MW Shirwata PSP (gross capex Rs 11,871 crore). Ingot/wafer manufacturing facility construction starting November 2026 for March 2028 COD.
* **Powerica**: Order book at Rs 1,950 crore (Rs 1,200 crore from data centers). Expanding wind capacity from 330 MW toward 638 MW ( targeting up to 1 GW long-term).

## What changed since last quarter

* **NTPC**: Scaled down FY27 group capacity addition guidance from 9.5 GW to ~8 GW (entirely renewables) due to RE grid evacuation line delays in Khavda, expecting resolution by mid-July 2027.
* **Adani Power**: Maintained 45 GW FY32 capacity vision; increased PPA tie-ups for its upcoming thermal pipeline from 56% to 62%. Upgraded credit rating to AA+. Derecognized/reversed Rs 1,970 crore alternate coal compensation revenue for Haryana DISCOM on conservative accounting advice.
* **Tata Power**: Maintained FY27 RE addition target (2.5 GW) and Rs 25,000 crore capex guidance despite missing Q1/Q2 installation targets due to evacuation delays. Formally approved the Rs 11,871 crore 1,800 MW Shirwata PSP project.
* **Powerica**: Re-established sequential margin recovery (EBITDA margin 15.2% vs 13.6% in Q1) following earlier product price hikes, while maintaining double-digit full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance.
