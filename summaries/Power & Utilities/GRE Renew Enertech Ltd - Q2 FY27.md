# GRE Renew Enertech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue, absolute profit, and order book growth, with management noting that margin contraction is temporary due to strategic credentials-building.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 139 crores (up ~217% YoY from Rs. 44 crores in H1 FY26; up 75% HoH vs H2 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 13 crores (up 132% YoY from Rs. 6 crores in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.52% (vs 13.01% in H1 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 9 crores (up ~128% YoY from Rs. 4 crores in H1 FY26).
- **PAT Margin:** 6.56% (vs 9.13% in H1 FY26).
- **Order Book (as of September 30, 2026):** Rs. 229 crores.
- **Large Utility-Scale Project:** 50 MW AC / 65 MW DC (Project cost: Rs. 175 crores, including Rs. 5 crores O&M for 3 years).
- **IPP/RESCO Portfolio:** ~25-26 MW (running and ongoing).
- **Trade Receivables:** Rs. 80 crores (106 receivable days).
- **Fund Raising / Debt Actions:** Raised Rs. 8.50 crores via unsecured loans, enhanced cash credit limit to Rs. 5 crores, and secured a new term loan of Rs. 2.58 crores for the Rashtrapati Bhawan project.
- **IPO Proceeds Utilized:** Rs. 39.56 crores fully utilized as of September 30, 2026 (including Rs. 29.85 crores for the Himmat Nagar solar project).

### Highlights
- Consolidated revenue reached Rs. 139 crores, driven primarily by the solar EPC segment (contributing Rs. 129 crores or ~93% of revenue).
- Undertook a large 50 MW AC / 65 MW DC project (Mahagenco tender via fourth-layer sub-contracting) at a lower margin specifically to establish utility-scale execution credentials for future bidding.
- Securing and executing a 1 MW rooftop solar project at India's Rashtrapati Bhawan.
- Total completed and ongoing portfolio stands at 197 MW.
- *Note vs Record:* The earnings call continues the rapid scaling of the solar EPC and RESCO portfolio noted in earlier corporate updates, while explicitly detailing the margin trade-off made for utility-scale credentials.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects **70% to 80%** conversion of the current order book in H2 FY27.
- Management declined to provide specific futuristic capacity guidance figures for FY28 and FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand observed for industrial rooftop and ground-mount solar parks, particularly leveraging their specialized tracker technology (yielding 19 to 19.5 lakh units vs 15 to 16 lakh units for fixed panels).
- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) represent a major future opportunity in India; management stated they are backend-ready to enter BESS as soon as Gujarat's draft policy is finalized.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin declined to 9.52% and PAT margin to 6.56% due to the execution of the large 50 MW AC / 65 MW DC project at a lower margin. Management stated this margin pressure is temporary until project completion in H2 FY27 and H1 FY28.
- Uses branded raw materials (modules from Waaree, Rayzon, Vikram Solar, Adani Solar, Goldi; inverters from Hitachi, Sungrow; cables from Polycab, RR Cable).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- RESCO/IPP projects are typically funded at a 25:75 equity/accruals to debt ratio.
- Total IPP portfolio (running + WIP of 10-11 MW) is approximately 25-26 MW.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables increased to Rs. 80 crores (106 days), which management stated is offset by increasing trade payables as part of working capital arrangements for the 65 MW project execution.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary margin compression due to low-margin, credentials-building utility-scale EPC projects.
- Expansion of trade receivables and payables driven by project-specific working capital needs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tahir Hyderabadwala (Grobis Fund)** asked about FY28/FY29 RESCO targets and the increase in trade receivables. **Management answered** that futuristic capacity figures cannot be provided right now, and explained that trade receivables are balanced by trade payables as a working capital mechanism for the 65 MW project.
2. **Rahul Singhania (Investor)** asked about the EBITDA margin trajectory and RESCO economics (rooftop vs ground mount). **Management answered** that margins will recover after the 50 MW project concludes in H2 FY27/H1 FY28, and ground-mount offers superior generation and unit economics due to their tracker technology despite higher project costs.
3. **Sampreeti (Investor)** asked about RESCO funding requirements and evaluation of BESS/hybrid solutions. **Management answered** that RESCO funding follows a 25:75 internal accrual-to-debt model, and they are fully prepared to enter BESS once Gujarat's policy is finalized, initially deploying it on their own existing assets.
4. **Devansh Shah (Investor)** asked about the "others" segment composition and O&M scope. **Management answered** that "others" includes a US subsidiary and LED sales (~Rs. 1.5 crores), and clarified that O&M is performed strictly in-house for their own customers to ensure quality and customer confidence.
5. **Vishal Singh (Investor)** asked about the response to geographic expansion outside Gujarat. **Management answered** that response has been very strong due to their unique tracker technology, resulting in a healthy pipeline of future opportunities.

### One-line takeaway
GRE Renew Enertech delivered triple-digit YoY revenue and profit growth in H1 FY27, accepting near-term margin dilution on a large utility-scale project to secure vital credentials for future tender biddings.
