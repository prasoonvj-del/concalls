# Orient Green Power Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-29 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management highlights operational stability and new capacity additions, but notes headwinds from delayed monsoon, lower wind availability, and capital constraints.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs. 81.43 crore (down 7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs. 60.01 crore (down 9% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs. 23.94 crore (down 16% YoY).
*   **Interest Cost:** ~Rs. 14 crore per quarter.
*   **Blended Interest Rate:** 9.1%.

### Highlights
*   **Capacity Additions:** Commissioned 9.9 MW of incremental wind capacity (3.3 MW in Q1, 6.6 MW in previous quarter) and 7 MW of solar capacity.
*   **Projects in Progress:** 17.6 MW solar and 7.8 MW wind repowering projects are underway, with management expecting commissioning by September 2026.
*   **Operational Performance:** Q1 performance was impacted by a delayed monsoon and muted wind availability.
*   **Subsidiary Restructuring:** Liquidating an intermediate 100% subsidiary in the Netherlands; the underlying 10.5 MW asset remains with the company.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Performance:** Management expects full-year revenue and EBITDA to be equal to or better than the previous year, contingent on wind availability.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting 1 GW capacity long-term, though management noted the timeline is flexible and dependent on capital availability.
*   **Repowering:** Planning 15-20 MW of repowered assets for the next fiscal year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **C&I Demand:** Strong demand from industrial customers for 100% renewable power.
*   **Technology Preference:** Wind is currently preferred over solar for C&I projects due to the high cost and regulatory uncertainty surrounding battery storage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Management expects to sustain margins as O&M costs are largely fixed.
*   **Interest:** The company is actively working to reduce borrowing costs; currently at 9.1%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Additions:** 17.5 MW solar and 7.8 MW wind repowering.
*   **Future Strategy:** Evaluating battery storage for existing solar projects, pending regulatory clarity in Tamil Nadu.
*   **Capital Constraints:** Management stated that expansion beyond 20-25 MW requires equity infusion, as debt-only expansion is not feasible.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Position:** Projected debt of Rs. 535 crore by year-end (an increase of Rs. 70 crore YoY).
*   **Debt Repayment:** Planning to repay Rs. 100 crore of debt in the current year.
*   **Andhra Pradesh Dispute:** Provided Rs. 6.5 crore (approx. 30% of Rs. 20 crore exposure) as a prudent measure regarding interest on delayed payments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Wind Variability:** High dependency on monsoon and wind patterns; Q1 was significantly worse than the previous year.
*   **Capital Raising:** Management acknowledged that the current share price makes equity raising difficult.
*   **Regulatory Delays:** Approvals in Tamil Nadu were delayed due to government changes, though management noted this is now resolving.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is the status of the 1 GW target?** A: Management stated they are working on it via organic and inorganic routes, but capital is the primary hurdle.
*   **Q: Why is the guidance conservative?** A: Management cited the unpredictability of wind; while capacity is higher, revenue depends on wind availability.
*   **Q: When will promoter pledges be released?** A: Management expects to release the pledge by the end of the year, as loan repayments are ahead of schedule.
*   **Q: Why not use hybrid solar-wind?** A: Management noted that while technically superior, the economics depend on land costs and battery storage viability.

### One-line takeaway
While the company is expanding capacity and maintaining operational stability, growth remains constrained by capital availability and the inherent unpredictability of wind generation.
