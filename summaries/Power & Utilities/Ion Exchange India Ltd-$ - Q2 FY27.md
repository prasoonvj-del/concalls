# Ion Exchange India Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY2027
**Tone:** Negative - Financial performance was significantly impacted by cost overruns in legacy Treatment Solutions projects, resulting in substantial operating losses and net losses.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management did not provide specific numeric revisions, but noted expectations for improved turnover and margins in H2 based on order backlog.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more cautious regarding near-term profitability due to legacy project write-downs, though underlying demand and order pipeline remain steady.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Middle East crisis impacts (deferred GCC dispatches and input cost inflation) became a prominent near-term headwind.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Roha facility stabilization and backward integration continue through H2, slightly delayed into the end of the fiscal year.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 6747 million (-8% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Loss of Rs 848 million
- **PAT:** Loss of Rs 828 million
- **Order Book:** Total Rs 29,690 million (Engineering project order book Rs 26,960 million; Sri Lanka Rs 100 million; UP Jal Jeevan Rs 2,630 million)
- **Bid Pipeline:** Rs 93,700 million
- **Order Inflow:** Rs 8,130 million (driven by medium-sized opportunities and a USD 52.83 million contract from Hyundai Engineering and Construction)
- **Treatment Solutions Revenue:** Rs 1498 million (vs Rs 2958 million in Q2 FY26); EBIT loss of Rs 1215 million
- **Industrial Products Revenue:** Rs 1,360 million (+35% YoY); EBIT Rs 86 million
- **Lifecycle Services Revenue:** Rs 706 million; EBIT Rs 59 million
- **Specialty Chemicals Revenue:** Rs 2,546 million (+17% YoY); EBIT Rs 225 million
- **Consumer Products Revenue:** Rs 1,005 million (+30% YoY); EBIT loss of Rs 41 million

### Highlights
- Treatment Solutions segment severely impacted by changes in estimated costs to complete legacy projects; management stated they have reached "the bottom of the barrel" regarding legacy cost overruns.
- Secured a prestigious international contract from Hyundai Engineering and Construction valued at USD 52.83 million for filtration units in the Middle East.
- Consumer Products segment delivered strong volume growth (30% YoY) and is scaling up operations.
- Advanced digital solutions offerings are receiving an encouraging response.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects an improvement in turnover and a better margin profile during the second half of the year.
- Consumer Products division targets reaching break-even during this financial year.
- Backward integration for the Roha plant is expected to be completed within this financial year.
- Treatment Solutions margins are expected to return to higher single digits in the balance part of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Bid pipeline of Rs 93,700 million is driven largely by the industrial side (refining, power, and steel) in domestic and international markets.
- Shipping lines to Europe and Africa remain unpredictable and congested due to geopolitical challenges.
- Jal Jeevan Mission funds are trickling into UP projects, with stronger traction expected in H2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Specialty Chemicals margins under pressure due to input cost inflation from the West Asia crisis (impact ~7-8%) and facility costs at the Roha plant (impact ~7%).
- Price increases are being progressively accepted in domestic markets, but international markets are slower.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital work-in-progress (CWIP) of Rs 240 crores is largely directed toward the Roha facility backward integration, modular expansion at Ankleshwar, and membrane UF facility expansion in Goa.
- Mann and Hummel technology tie-up for membrane manufacturing in Goa is progressing, with production expected from next financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Other current liabilities rose due to unearned revenue (around Rs 250 crores) and customer advances (just under Rs 300 crores).
- UP Jal Jeevan receivables account for around 10% of total receivables; exposure levels remain similar to March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical crises in West Asia causing input cost inflation and shipping/logistics congestion.
- Execution risks and legacy cost overruns in Treatment Solutions (though management believes the worst is accounted for).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pratik Kothari (Unique PMS) asked if the Treatment Solutions issues were just delayed dispatches and if incremental losses are finished.** Management confirmed legacy cost overruns were fully accounted for in this quarter (contributing to ~90% of negative PBT), representing the bottom, and remaining execution should not see material adverse impacts.
- **Deepak (Sundaram Mutual Fund) asked about the revenue contribution from the Hyundai order in FY27.** Management stated a decent amount of revenue is expected in this financial year, but declined to give specific single-contract guidance.
- **Homiar Irani (Investor) asked about Roha utilization and backward integration delays.** Management explained the plant has been operational for a year, but West Asia input cost inflation and certification/stabilization phases impacted offtake; backward integration finishes this fiscal year.
- **Homiar Irani asked when Consumer Products will break even.** Management reiterated the plan is to reach break-even during this financial year.
- **Ankit Maheshwari (Oxmox Capital) asked about the rise in other current liabilities.** Management attributed it to increases in unearned revenue (around Rs 250 crores) and customer advances (near Rs 300 crores).

### One-line takeaway
Ion Exchange reported a severe quarterly loss driven by legacy project cost overruns and input inflation, but management expects a recovery in H2 supported by a strong order backlog and new contract executions.
