# Concord Enviro Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Power & Utilities*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Execution improved and the business returned to year-on-year revenue growth after Q1 supply chain disruptions.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (15% to 20% revenue growth for FY27).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to confident/positive as operational execution normalized in domestic markets.
- New topics or risks: Mention of Mexican peso forex losses; solar photovoltaic orders received post-Q2 (INR14.25 crores); shifting non-core Sharjah operations back to India. Old risks: Middle East sea freight logistics challenges remain, though air dispatches normalized.
- Last quarter's promises: Promised execution normalization by end of Q2 and H2 growth — delivered on Q2 execution recovery.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR1,344.55 million (up 7.7% YoY from INR1,248.4 million in Q2 FY26).
- **Gross Profit:** INR772.67 million (vs INR629.93 million in Q2 FY26).
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 57.5% (vs 50.5% in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** INR77.79 million (up 1.6% YoY from INR76.57 million in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 5.8% (vs 6.1% in Q2 FY26).
- **Order Book:** INR7,164 million / INR714 crores (up 34% YoY sequentially).
- **Domestic Order Inflows (H1 FY27):** Up 55% YoY compared to H1 last year.
- **Solar PV Order Received Post-Q2 (Oct 1, 2027):** INR14.25 crores.
- **Sharjah Facility Capacity Utilization:** 50% to 60%.

### Highlights
- Execution improved during Q2, driving a return to YoY revenue growth, led by domestic markets (68% of Q2 revenue).
- Secured a significant order from a leading global alco-bev player for its Mexico plant.
- Subsequent to Q2 (October 1st, 2027), Rochem Separation Systems received orders aggregating INR14.25 crores from a solar photovoltaic module manufacturer for its Hyderabad facility.
- New initiatives like the H-Xtreme heat exchanger and industrial dehumidification (WaHa partnership) progressed into customer engagement and order execution.
- Management shifted non-core assembly activities from Sharjah back to India to manage costs, keeping Sharjah focused on membrane manufacturing.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue growth in the range of 15% to 20% for FY27.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to reach low double-digits in FY27, returning to historical 14% to 16% levels by FY28.
- Management targets a long-term annual growth rate of 20% to 25%.
- Capex guidance: INR30 crores to INR35 crores for FY27 (including INR15 crores for the Vasai facility); INR50 crores to INR60 crores for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broader demand for environmental, industrial water/wastewater treatment, ZLD, desalination, and membrane solutions remains encouraging across steel, solar manufacturing, chemicals, pharma, F&B, and industrial recycling.
- Solar photovoltaic manufacturing is emerging as a key growth vertical.
- International revenue accounted for approximately 32% of Q2 revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin was marginally impacted by forex losses due to Mexican peso vs dollar movements.
- Fixed costs remain elevated due to investments in talent and execution capabilities for larger projects.
- O&M blended EBITDA margins are in the low 20s%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sharjah facility is operating at 50% to 60% capacity; management plans to review scale-up investments in Q4.
- Vasai facility debottlenecking and expansion capex of ~INR15 crores is underway in FY27, funded largely via IPO proceeds.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debtor days range between 110 days and 120 days; average working capital cycle is maintained at 140 days to 150 days.
- Non-fund-based limits (bank guarantees and letters of credit) requirements are higher due to the larger order book, while cash credit facilities remain stable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing logistical uncertainties and sea-shipping delays out of the UAE/GCC region due to Middle East geopolitical issues.
- Quarterly revenue and margin volatility due to lumpy execution cycles of large projects (>INR50 crores).
- Forex volatility risk (specifically Mexican peso).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shaishav Vora (Prudent Investments) on quarterly volatility:** Management explained that H2 is stronger (H3/Q4 peak) and large lumpy orders (>INR50 crores) cause QoQ variations.
2. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) on EBITDA margins and 15-20% growth confidence:** Management stated that operating leverage and large steel sector deliveries scheduled for Q4 provide confidence in meeting revenue targets and achieving low double-digit EBITDA margins.
3. **Henil Bagadia (Equicorp) on Sharjah facility utilization:** Management noted 50%-60% utilization, explaining that non-core project assembly was shifted back to India while membrane production remains based in Sharjah.
4. **Giriraj Daga (Visaria Family Office) on capex and working capital:** Management guided INR30-35 crores capex for FY27 and INR50-60 crores for FY28, stating working capital cycles are expected to stabilize around 140-150 days.
5. **Dilip Sahu on long-term growth levers:** Management highlighted CETP ZLD upgrades, industrial dehumidification, and new export markets (Europe and North America) as key future levers.

### One-line takeaway
Concord Enviro rebounded in Q2 FY27 with 7.7% YoY revenue growth, supported by robust domestic execution and a INR714 crore order book, despite lingering Middle East sea-freight headwinds.
