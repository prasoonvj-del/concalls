# PCBL Chemical Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong financial performance (revenue/PAT growth) and pricing discipline offset by volume headwinds due to geopolitical volatility and cautious customer inventory management.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 2,474 crore (+14% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 310 crore (+12% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 74 crore (+20% YoY).
*   **Carbon Black Sales Volume:** 134,180 tonnes (Domestic: 92,843 tonnes; Export: 41,337 tonnes).
*   **Carbon Black Segment Mix:** Tyre grades (77,914 tonnes), Performance Chemicals (39,698 tonnes), Specialty Grades (16,568 tonnes).
*   **Power Generation:** 186 million units (108 million sold externally at Rs 5.34/unit).
*   **Aquapharm Performance:** Revenue Rs 433 crore; EBITDA Rs 42 crore; Sales volume 24,253 metric tonnes.
*   **EBITDA per tonne (Carbon Black):** ~Rs 17,500 (excluding Rs 2,300/tonne tariff refund).

### Highlights
*   **Pricing Discipline:** Management prioritized margins over volume, intentionally letting go of low-margin spot orders in Southeast Asia and domestic markets.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** "Project Alchemy" contributed Rs 10-15 crore to cost savings; management expects acceleration in bottom-line realization from these initiatives in coming quarters.
*   **Aquapharm:** Green Chelates performing well; PBTC ramping up. First commercial order for synthetic thickeners secured.
*   **Battery Materials:** Pilot project in Palej (Nanovace JV) producing samples for internal qualification; shifting focus to silicon-carbon composite solutions.
*   **Infrastructure:** Commissioned Super Conductive Black facility; Acetylene Black project progressing with ECMS approvals.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **EBITDA:** Reiterated guidance of double-digit growth in average EBITDA per tonne over FY26 levels.
*   **Volume:** Management noted the initial ~7% volume growth guidance is "challenging" due to raw material availability and competitive market conditions; volume may be lower than initially projected.
*   **Aquapharm:** Maintained commitment to double-digit revenue growth for FY27.
*   **Carbon Black:** Expects buying patterns to improve gradually in H2 as customers gain price clarity.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Geopolitics:** Conflict in West Asia and Russia-Ukraine continues to disrupt trade routes, elevate freight costs, and impact feedstock (CBFS) availability.
*   **Feedstock:** Russian refinery throughput at multi-decade lows, tightening global CBFS supply. Coal tar prices in China have firmed up.
*   **Customer Behavior:** Tyre manufacturers are maintaining lean inventories and adopting a "wait-and-watch" approach, impacting quarterly volumes.
*   **Exports:** Management expects volume growth in international markets (US/Europe) as infrastructure (e.g., US railcars) and supply chain flexibility improve.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Freight/Logistics:** Costs have risen sharply due to vessel shortages and longer transit times.
*   **Raw Materials:** Diversifying feedstock sourcing beyond the Gulf to include Southeast Asia (Korea, Malaysia, Thailand) to de-risk supply.
*   **Power:** PCBL is self-sufficient; Aquapharm incurred ~Rs 10 crore higher power costs during the quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Rs 300 crore planned for FY27.
*   **Coal Distillation:** Plan is in the final stage of approval; announcement expected shortly.
*   **Specialty Carbon Black:** Capacity is ~75,000 tonnes; management is dialing up volumes as raw material availability improves.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   *Not explicitly discussed in detail, other than mention of inventory management.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Tariff Uncertainty:** US trade tariff volatility remains a concern for large customers, impacting long-term volume commitments.
*   **Inventory:** Customers are destocking, which negatively impacted Q2 volumes.
*   **Competition:** Some competitors are aggressively chasing volume in spot markets, though management believes low-price strategies are unsustainable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the volume decline and if it was due to demand or supply. **Management:** Clarified it was a deliberate margin decision to exit low-contribution spot markets, particularly in Southeast Asia and domestic spot segments.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the sustainability of the cost-saving program. **Management:** Confirmed "Project Alchemy" initiatives are proven; horizontal deployment is underway, with acceleration expected in coming quarters.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the impact of US tariff refunds. **Management:** Confirmed receipt of Rs 33 crore, which was fully reflected in the quarter's EBITDA per tonne.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the battery material strategy. **Management:** Clarified they are focusing on silicon-carbon composites rather than graphite, aiming to move from ingredient supply to integrated battery solutions.

### One-line takeaway
PCBL delivered strong profitability growth despite volume headwinds by prioritizing margin-accretive business and exercising strict pricing discipline amidst geopolitical and feedstock volatility.
