# Regaal Resources Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Record PAT, strong volume growth, capacity expansion on track despite temporary turbine disruption.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: cut (value-added share for FY27 reduced from 20-22% to 10-11%; volume and interest cost guidance maintained)
- Tone and demand view: remains positive but more cautious on margin sustainability; demand strong with 150% order book vs target
- New topics: turbine disruption and recommissioning; GST recovery from distributor (60% done, full by Dec); land acquisition for future expansion (7-8 acres); new products DMH/DAH by early 2027-28; methane gas power plant (1.6 MW); export share 10%
- Old topics resolved: turbine fixed; trading sales eliminated
- Last quarter promises: capacity commissioned and operational (1,500-1,575 TPD); value-added ramp-up started but slower than guided; H2 volume target 240k-250k tonnes; interest cost ~INR 40 crore maintained

### Key numbers
- Operating income: Rs 316.6 crore (INR 3,166 million)
- Core manufacturing revenue: Rs 312.7 crore (INR 3,127 million), up 64.4% YoY
- Value-added revenue: Rs 129 crore (INR 1,290 million), up 64.3% YoY, margin 40.8%
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 54.2 crore (INR 542 million), up 54.7% YoY, margin 17.1%
- PAT: Rs 23.2 crore (INR 232 million), up 38.7% YoY, margin 7.3%
- ROCE: 10.2%; ROE: 14.2% (up from 10.8% in Q1)
- Net debt: Rs 880.8 crore (INR 8,808 million) as of Sep 30, 2026 (term loan Rs 535.7 cr, working capital Rs 350.6 cr)
- Cash conversion cycle: 123 days (improved from 130 days in Q1)
- Maize crushing volume: 90,489 tonnes, up 32% YoY
- H2 FY27 volume target: 240,000-250,000 tonnes; FY27 full year target: 400,000 tonnes
- FY28 volume target: 525,000-550,000 tonnes
- Interest cost guidance FY27: ~INR 40-42 crore
- EBITDA per tonne: Q2 INR 6,000; H1 INR 5,300; sustainable steady-state INR 4,500 +/- 5%
- Value-added share (excl. LG): currently ~7%; FY27 target 10-11%; FY28 target 22-23%; long-term 30-35% of starch
- Capacity: 1,650 TPD, currently running 1,500-1,575 TPD
- Capex: total project INR 665 crore, INR 275 crore remaining to capitalize
- GST recovery: 60% recovered, expect full by December
- Trading sales: Q2 FY26 Rs 127 crore; Q2 FY27 negligible
- Average realization (all products): INR 35/kg in Q2

### Highlights
- Record quarterly PAT of Rs 23.2 crore with 7.3% margin
- Maize crushing volume up 32% YoY despite 1-month turbine disruption at new 825 TPD facility (turbine recommissioned)
- 90% of H2 maize inventory secured at lower prices, lasting till mid-March; Rabi crop arrival expected mid-March
- Export contribution risen to 10% of fiber and starch; tapioca starch at USD 700/tonne supporting Indian starch demand
- Land agreements signed for 7-8 acres adjacent to existing 57-acre site for future expansion
- Trading sales eliminated (down from Rs 127 crore in Q2 FY26 to negligible)
- EBITDA per tonne at INR 6,000 in Q2, well above sustainable INR 4,500 baseline

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 maize crushing target maintained at 400,000 tonnes; H2 target 240,000-250,000 tonnes
- FY28 target 525,000-550,000 tonnes
- Value-added share (excl. LG) guidance cut to 10-11% for FY27 (from 20-22% earlier), 22-23% for FY28, long-term 30-35% of starch
- Net interest cost FY27 guided at INR 40-42 crore (maintained)
- Working capital debt to halve by March 2027 as inventory consumed
- DMH/DAH (dextrose) commissioning by early 2027-28; 1.6 MW methane gas power plant and LG drum unit underway
- No major capex beyond INR 275 crore remaining; focus on stabilization and efficiency

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand strong: order book at 150% of target; starch prices raised post-meeting
- Maize prices up 50%+ from Q1 to Q2 (INR 10 to 15/kg); further increase in October
- 80-85% of finished product prices rose with maize; value-addition delta expanded from 10% to 15-20%
- Tapioca starch at USD 700/tonne keeps Indian maize starch competitive globally
- Maize availability concern due to ethanol diversion and kharif crop shortfall (El Nino), but Bihar is net exporter; Rabi crop arrival mid-March
- Customer acceptance of higher prices confirmed; no shift to alternatives observed

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Maize procurement strategy: 90% of H2 inventory in stock at lower prices; direct farmer procurement in Bihar (1.75-2.5 lakh tonnes loyalty)
- Maize cost up 50%+ YoY; starch realizations up correspondingly; value-addition per tonne increased
- Sustainable EBITDA per tonne guided at INR 4,500 +/- 5%; current run-rate INR 5,300-6,000
- Power cost reduction via 1.6 MW methane gas plant from effluent treatment
- Cash conversion cycle improved to 123 days from 130 days; expected to improve further

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current capacity 1,650 TPD, operating at 1,500-1,575 TPD; can exceed 1,650 TPD historically
- Remaining capex INR 275 crore to be capitalized (total project INR 665 crore)
- New projects: DMH/DAH by early 2027-28; 1.6 MW methane gas power plant; LG drum manufacturing; modified starches (cationic, carboxy methyl, pre-gel, spray)
- Land secured for future expansion (7-8 acres adjacent)
- No major new capex planned for 1-2 years; focus on stabilization and efficiency

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt Rs 880.8 crore (term loan Rs 535.7 cr, working capital Rs 350.6 cr)
- Working capital debt peaked in H1 due to advance maize procurement; expected to drop >50% by March 2027
- Term loan stable as new loans offset repayments; interest subvention under Bihar policy
- Inventory covers till mid-March; Rabi crop arrival to replenish
- GST recovery: 60% received, balance expected by December

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Margin sustainability uncertain due to commodity nature; management avoids percentage margin guidance
- Maize price volatility: kharif crop shortfall, ethanol diversion, government sugar capping affecting ethanol
- Value-added ramp-up slower than previously guided (10-11% vs 20-22% for FY27)
- Dependence on Rabi crop arrival in March for raw material security
- Customer approval timelines for value-added products (15 days to 2.5 months)

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Diwakar Rana (Prudent Equity) on debt reduction roadmap:** Management confirmed working capital debt will halve by March 2027 as inventory consumed; term loan stable; interest cost maintained at INR 40-42 crore.
2. **Rohit Sinha on inventory and pricing:** Confirmed 90% H2 inventory in stock at lower prices; maize prices up 50%+; starch prices up 80-85%; value-addition delta expanded.
3. **Gulit Singh on EBITDA margin sustainability:** Management refused percentage guidance; gave per-tonne metric: sustainable INR 4,500 +/-5%; current Q2 INR 6,000; value-added share to reach 30-35% of starch long-term.
4. **Pratik Kothari on exports and maize availability:** Exports at 10% of fiber/starch; tapioca starch at USD 700 supports demand; maize availability risk from ethanol diversion and crop shortfall, but Bihar net exporter with strong farmer network.
5. **Naman Maheshwari on FY28 margins after low-cost inventory exhaustion:** Management said INR 4,500/tonne sustainable in FY28; current run-rate higher due to low-cost inventory and high starch prices; cannot promise beyond INR 4,500.
6. **Rajesh Jain on capacity and value-added split:** Of 1,100 tonnes starch output, 20% to glucose, 20% to value-added (excl. LG); long-term 30-35% of starch to value-added; land available for future expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Regaal Resources delivered record profitability in Q2 FY27 on strong volume growth and margin expansion, but lowered FY27 value-added share guidance while maintaining volume and debt targets, with H2 set to benefit from low-cost inventory and expanded capacity.
