# Kiri Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management describes an "inflection point" driven by the new copper/fertilizer project, but current performance is pressured by high input costs, non-cash treasury losses, and a time lag in passing on price hikes.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 288 crore (+48% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 7 crore (Margin 2.4%).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Loss of Rs 23 crore.
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 299 crore (+40% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 2 crore.
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Loss of Rs 39 crore (before share of profit from associates).
*   **Share of profit from associates (Lonsen Kiri):** Rs 16 crore.
*   **Material Margin:** 29.1% (vs 26.4% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Pivot:** The integrated copper and fertilizer project remains the core growth strategy. Procurement of major plant/equipment is underway, with civil and structural works progressing.
*   **Mining Acquisitions:** Acquired 40% stake in Makilala Mining (Philippines) and incorporated Andamina SPA (Chile). A new management team, including a CEO with experience at Carmen Copper, has been appointed at Makilala.
*   **Dyes Business:** Market prices for key intermediates (H-Acid) improved (Rs 1,600-1,800 range), but performance was impacted by the execution of older, lower-priced orders.
*   *Record Check:* The call confirms the strategic pivot to copper/fertilizer and the ongoing legal/arbitration process regarding the mining stake, consistent with the provided record.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Project Timeline:** Phase one (downstream facilities) remains on track for H1 FY28; smelter/fertilizer complex expected for FY29.
*   **Dyes Business:** Management targets 8-10% EBITDA margins for the dye business going forward.
*   **Production Targets:** Once fully operational, the project targets 0.5 million tonnes of copper and 1.1 million tonnes of fertilizer production annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **H-Acid Supply:** Chinese production is disrupted due to new regulations requiring continuous nitration; current Chinese output is <50% of capacity.
*   **Price Pass-through:** Dyes prices have increased in tandem with intermediates, but with a 2-3 month lag due to existing order books and inventory transit times.
*   **Copper Market:** Domestic refining capacity lags consumption growth, providing opportunities for domestic players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Operating Costs:** Profitability was weighed down by higher fuel, power, and effluent treatment expenses.
*   **Treasury Impact:** Consolidated profitability was impacted by an unrealized non-cash fair value loss of Rs 57 crore on treasury investments.
*   **Copper Project CAPEX:** Total project CAPEX is ~USD 280 million; Kiri's equity investment is expected to be >USD 40 million.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Intermediate Capacity:** Utilization is currently <60%; management is working to increase production.
*   **Copper Sourcing:** Makilala Mining is expected to produce 140,000 tonnes of copper concentrate, with 100,000 tonnes earmarked for Kiri’s India facility.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Capital Raising:** Preferential allotment of shares at Rs 475 per share is ongoing.
*   **Liquidity:** Management stated the balance sheet and liquidity position are sufficient to support project execution.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Legal/Arbitration:** Celsius (partner in Makilala) has appealed court awards; Kiri will continue to represent itself in the appeals court.
*   **Execution Risk:** Management acknowledged that project risk remains until milestones are achieved.
*   **Market Volatility:** Geopolitical developments and supply chain disruptions continue to affect global commodity markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why are EBITDA margins low despite price hikes?* A: Management explained a 2-3 month lag in passing on costs due to existing order books and inventory transit times.
*   *Q: Why not a rights issue instead of preferential allotment?* A: Management acknowledged the point but stated the preferential allotment process is ongoing.
*   *Q: Will copper price volatility hurt the project?* A: Management stated the business will be 100% hedged (purchase/sale) to maintain constant spreads, regardless of LME price fluctuations.
*   *Q: Status of Makilala Mining management?* A: A new management team (CEO/CFO) has been appointed; Kiri was involved in the selection process.

### One-line takeaway
Kiri is in a transition phase, prioritizing the long-term copper/fertilizer pivot while managing a legacy dyes business currently hampered by high costs and a time-lag in price realization.
