# Platinum Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 23.3% YoY, but profitability was impacted by surging ocean freight costs, product mix shifts, and delayed capacity ramp-ups.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 121.3 crore (vs Rs 98.4 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 10.8 crore (vs Rs 13.6 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8.9% (vs 13.8% in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 7.6 crore (vs Rs 11 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 230.20 crore (vs Rs 213.8 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 24.3 crore (vs Rs 28.7 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 18.7 crore (vs Rs 24.1 crore in H1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Egypt Facility:** Capacity increased from 30,000 MTPA to 60,000 MTPA. Partial production (stabilizers) expected by 31st December 2026; remaining capacity by 31st March 2027.
*   **New Segments:** Oleochemicals sales reached Rs 11.4 crore in Q2 FY27.
*   **Rivadu Lifesciences:** Entered an exclusive agreement with South Korea-based CAREGEN Co., Ltd for "Korglutide" (weight management product) in India.
*   **Management Hires:** Onboarded new leadership for CPVC division, R&D, and marketing, including a Global R&D Head based in Germany.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management maintains a 3-year CAGR target of 35%.
*   **Egypt Contribution:** Expected to contribute Rs 25-30 crore in revenue in FY27 (Jan-Mar 2027).
*   **Margins:** Management expects to return to the 13-15% EBITDA margin range in FY28.
*   **Pharma/Rivadu:** Management expects growth from this segment to materialize from Q1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **PVC Pipe Industry:** Experienced significant degrowth (18-20%) between April and September 2026, though sales picked up from mid-September.
*   **Logistics:** Management does not anticipate shipping line costs to decrease in the next 3-4 months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** Attributed to the transition/ramp-up phase of new facilities, a temporary lower share of high-margin products (lead-free PVC), and surging ocean freight costs.
*   **Freight Impact:** Export logistics costs doubled to Rs 5 crore in Q2 FY27 (on Rs 24 crore revenue) compared to Rs 2.5 crore in Q1 FY27 (on Rs 22 crore revenue).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Palghar Facility:** Current utilization is 30-35%; management aims to cross 50% utilization in the next 2 years.
*   **Non-current Assets:** Increased to Rs 28.96 crore (from Rs 1.94 crore in March 2026) primarily due to capital advances for the Egypt subsidiary.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Management stated the company remains debt-free.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   High shipping line costs impacting export profitability.
*   Industry-wide demand weakness in the PVC pipe sector during H1.
*   Delay in Egypt facility commissioning resulted in missing 3-4 months of projected revenue (approx. Rs 30-35 crore).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did revenue growth miss expectations?** A: Management explained that the Egypt operation was expected to start earlier; the delay caused a shortfall of Rs 30-35 crore in revenue.
*   **Q: Why the sharp margin decline?** A: Management cited doubled shipping costs for exports and a shift in product mix (lower share of high-margin lead-free stabilizers).
*   **Q: What is the strategy for Rivadu Lifesciences?** A: Management is targeting the weight management market with Korglutide, positioning it 10-20% cheaper than competitors like Mounjaro/Ozempic, with plans for local manufacturing if sales scale.
*   **Q: Will Egypt exports replace Indian exports?** A: Yes, India will only export to East Africa, Bangladesh, and neighboring countries; the rest will be handled by the Egypt facility.
*   **Q: Is the finance cost increasing?** A: Management clarified that finance costs have not increased as the company is debt-free.

### One-line takeaway
While Q2 performance was hampered by high logistics costs and delayed capacity commissioning, management remains confident in long-term growth via the Egypt expansion and new product initiatives in oleochemicals and pharma.
