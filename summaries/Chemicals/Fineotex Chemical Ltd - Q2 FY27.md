# Fineotex Chemical Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong YoY growth in revenue, operating EBITDA, and PAT, alongside expansion in international oil & gas and new industrial water treatment verticals.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (management reiterated progress toward the $200 million revenue target for the U.S. segment by calendar year 2028).
- Tone and demand view: Expanded focus into advanced water and wastewater solutions (Fineotex America Inc.) targeting data centers and semiconductors, alongside sustained oil & gas momentum.
- New topics or risks: Establishment of Fineotex Holdings Inc. and Fineotex America Inc.; ICRA long-term rating upgrade to ICRA AA- stable. Geopolitical transit delays from Asia to the U.S. continued to be monitored.
- Last quarter's promises: The 148,000 MTPA Texas manufacturing capacity and acquired U.S. operations continue to scale successfully as previously targeted.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 778.13 crore (up 166.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 118.74 crore (up 111.2% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 92.75 crore (up 81.5% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Business Volumes:** Increased by approximately 132.3% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated ROIC:** Approximately 36.12%
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated Total Income:** Approximately Rs 391 crore (up 169% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Approximately Rs 60 crore (up 92% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Approximately Rs 45 crore (up 71% YoY)
- **U.S. Manufacturing Capacity:** Approximately 148,000 metric tonnes stated annual capacity
- **Segment Revenue Contribution (Q2):** Oil & gas ~65%, Textile specialty ~25%, Cleaning & hygiene ~7-8%

### Highlights
- ICRA upgraded the company's long-term credit rating to ICRA AA- stable and reaffirmed the ICRA A1+ short-term rating.
- Formed new subsidiaries Fineotex Holdings Inc. and Fineotex America Inc. to build an integrated solutions platform for industrial water and wastewater management.
- Oil & gas operations in the U.S. (CrudeChem) and India expanded customer engagements with major players like Aramco and ONGC.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Sanjay Tibrewala) targets the U.S. oil and gas business to reach $200 million in revenue by calendar year 2028.
- Management projects the company could grow 3x to 4x over the next 6 to 7 years.
- Management expects the blended tax rate to normalize around 17% to 20% going forward.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Textile specialty chemicals demand is improving, supported by favorable zero-tax/tariff dynamics in the U.K. and EU and recovery in key customer segments.
- Oil & gas demand shows strong tailwinds, with Middle Eastern and U.S. operators seeking responsive technical solutions and reliable last-mile delivery.
- Water treatment demand is accelerating due to massive data center and semiconductor facility investments in Texas and globally.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- COGS increased by 6% while other expenses dropped by 6%, attributed by management to stabilizing technical consultancy onboarding costs and inventory transit timing lags.
- Raw material price fluctuations are passed through to customers within 1 to 2 months via spot pricing and war surcharges.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- U.S. operations maintain a manufacturing platform with approximately 148,000 metric tonnes of stated annual capacity across Brookshire and Midland, Texas.
- Investments and focus are prioritized toward scaling oil & gas and water treatment platforms.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a strong cash position and disciplined working capital management.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments causing freight rate fluctuations and increased transit times from Asia to the U.S.
- Weather disruptions (such as storms or lightning) causing temporary short-term halts in oilfield activities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhruvi Shah (NVS Brokerage):** Asked about PAT margin recovery toward historical 16%-21% levels. *Management stated absolute PAT has doubled, operational efficiencies are improving post-acquisition, and blended margins will trend higher.*
2. **Samarth Goel (Choice Institutional Equities):** Inquired about the drop in other expenses and COGS increase. *Management explained other expenses reflect settling technical onboarding costs, and price pass-through occurs on 1-2 month cycles.*
3. **Nalin Shah (NVS Brokerage):** Asked about water treatment revenue timelines and 3-year targets. *Management stated water treatment orders could begin in Q4 this year, and projected 3x-4x growth over 6-7 years.*
4. **Prateek Giri (Subh Labh Research):** Questioned the sequential revenue flatness in CrudeChem despite rising rig counts. *Management explained oilfield revenues fluctuate based on well-specific variables and weather, and should be viewed on an annual rather than quarterly basis.*
5. **Mansi Rathod (Hathway Investments):** Asked about the tax rate drop and segment revenue breakdown. *Management cited the 115BAB tax rate (15%) for the new Indian plant and lower U.S. rates, and provided segment splits (Oil ~65%, Textile ~25%, Hygiene ~7-8%).*

### One-line takeaway
Fineotex delivered a strong H1 FY27 with consolidated total income surging 166.8% YoY to Rs 778.13 crore, driven by robust international oil & gas scaling and strategic expansion into industrial water treatment solutions.
