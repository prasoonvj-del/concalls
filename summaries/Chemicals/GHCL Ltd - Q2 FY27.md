# GHCL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Global oversupply, elevated energy and freight costs, and soft realizations persist, though domestic demand outlook remains positive.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 821 crore (Up 11% YoY, Up 3% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 202 crore (Up 16% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 24.7% (compared to 23.7% in Q2 FY26 and 29% in Q1 FY27)
- **PBT before exceptional items:** Rs 172 crore (Up 19% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 127 crore (compared to Rs 107 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,619 crore (Up 4% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 435 crore (Up 9% YoY); Margin at 26.9%
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 278 crore
- **Net Cash Surplus:** > Rs 1,070 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **H1 Total Cash Generation:** Rs 360 crore (Capex: Rs 68 crore, Dividend: Rs 110 crore, Debt Repayment: Rs 13 crore, Working Capital increase: Rs 217 crore, Net cash outflow: Rs 48 crore)

### Highlights
- Successfully commenced commercial production of vacuum salt (capitalized in September) and bromine (subsequent to quarter close).
- Board approved a capex of Rs 88 crore for developing an in-house railway siding infrastructure.
- Salt works capacity expansion and Zara Zumara salt works development ongoing with an investment of ~Rs 450 crore across phases.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 overall numbers to be similar to or slightly better than FY26, aided by incremental EBITDA from vacuum salt and bromine.
- Q3 FY27 numbers expected to be lower than Q2 FY27 due to annual maintenance shutdown and tapering of low-cost raw material inventory benefits, but higher YoY.
- Management expects realizations to remain soft over the next few quarters due to global oversupply.
- Greenfield project implementation timeline: ~3 to 3.5 years from the 'Zero date', followed by ~1 year for stabilization. Phase 1 is 0.5 million tonnes (out of 1.1 million tonnes total project phase). Estimated cost for 0.5 million tonnes is ~Rs 4,200 crore; total 1.1 million tonnes cost is ~Rs 6,800 to Rs 7,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global soda ash market remains oversupplied with elevated inventories in certain regions; synthetic Solvay-based plants in China and globally are operating below cost.
- Lithium sector continues to be a clear bright spot with strong demand momentum.
- In India, Q1-Q2 FY27 demand growth moderated to ~1%+ (compared to 9% last year), impacted by geopolitical situations and delayed solar glass capacity execution.
- Solar glass capacity in India projected to grow from 15 GW (as of Sep 2026) to 35 GW in the next 6 months, driving soda ash demand in FY27-FY28.
- Government of India imposed minimum import price on clear float glass for 1 year, supporting domestic soda ash demand from glass segments.
- Indian imports have stabilized at ~4 lakh tonnes per half-year; imports from Iran, Russia and others have tapered down, consolidating into U.S., Turkey, and China.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Energy and freight costs remain elevated; Middle East conflict disrupts material flows and adds cost pressure.
- Bromine segment margin projected at 40%+.
- Treasury income run rate expected around Rs 10 to 12 crore quarterly.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Vacuum salt plant expected to reach full intended capacity by end of FY27; bromine plant to reach full capacity by next financial year.
- Salt field productivity enhancement underway to add ~3 lakh tonnes of extra salt production over 2 years. Captive salt requirement currently covers ~40% of total requirements (~18-20 lakh tonnes).
- Infrastructure pipeline project for existing facility expected to be capitalized by year-end.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital increased by ~Rs 217 crore in H1 due to higher inventory and trade receivables amid supply chain disruptions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global oversupply driven by China and soft realizations due to import pressure.
- Geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East affecting material flows and cost.
- Delays in government approvals and land acquisition for the greenfield project.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ansh (Capital One) on greenfield project timelines:** Management stated it takes 3.5 years from Zero date plus 1 year to stabilize once land approvals are fully in place.
2. **Rohit Sinha (Sunidhi Securities) on pricing and margin gap:** Management noted that while historical 10-year average EBITDA margin was 29-30%, cost optimizations keep current margins at 25%, which will rebound when global oversupply rebalances. A small price increase of 1.5-2% was taken in Q3.
3. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.) on capex returns and cash return to shareholders:** Management explained that vacuum salt asset turnover reflects high underlying salt costs, but delivers a 20%+ ROCE. On cash returns, management highlighted past buybacks and dividends, stating they do not intend to sit on idle cash and will judiciously allocate capital.
4. **Gagan Dixit (Emkay Global) on greenfield phasing:** Management clarified Phase 1 is 0.5 million tonnes (costing ~Rs 4,200 crore) out of a total 1.1 million tonnes plan, and given annual Indian demand growth of 2.5 lakh tonnes, offtake tying is not a concern.

### One-line takeaway
GHCL reported steady Q2 FY27 performance with 16% YoY EBITDA growth and commercialized its vacuum salt and bromine projects, while navigating a soft global pricing environment through strict cost discipline.
