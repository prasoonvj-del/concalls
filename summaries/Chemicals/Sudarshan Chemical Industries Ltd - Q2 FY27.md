# Sudarshan Chemical Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong execution and debt-reduction via asset monetization, offset by macro demand softness in Europe/US and operational challenges at RIECO.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained for the Acquired Group at EUR 700 million turnover and EUR 35 million EBITDA (incorporating the loss of VP4 business EBITDA).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from positive to cautious. Management noted weak US housing and European construction, volatile masterbatch demand, and ongoing geopolitical and logistics headwinds, though demand upticks are expected towards Q4.
- **New topics or risks:** Rs 74 crore employee restructuring provision in Germany; internal supply chain bottlenecks and cost overruns at RIECO resulting in an EBITDA loss; definitive agreement signed to acquire Clariant’s toll-manufacturing aluminum dyes business in Switzerland.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Closed the VP4 plant raw material business divestment on November 2, 2026 for EUR 75.8 million; quantified European employee restructuring cost at Rs 74 crore; went live on Wave 1 of the single SAP integration (Legacy Sudarshan and Heubach India).

### Key numbers
- **Legacy Sudarshan Pigment Revenue:** Rs 821 crore vs Rs 730 crore in Q1 FY27 and Rs 651 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **Legacy Sudarshan EBITDA:** Rs 128 crore; EBITDA margin at 15.6%.
- **Acquired Group Revenue:** Rs 1,962 crore; Business EBITDA at Rs 112 crore (Reported EBITDA at Rs 134 crore including Rs 22 crore inventorized overhead).
- **Total Pigment Global Business Revenue:** Rs 2,695 crore vs Rs 2,327 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **Total Pigment Global Business EBITDA:** Rs 242 crore vs Rs 90 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **RIECO Performance:** Revenue at Rs 47 crore; EBITDA loss of Rs 3 crore.
- **ONE Sudarshan Total Revenue (including RIECO):** Rs 2,830 crore (Rs 868 crore Legacy + RIECO; Rs 1,962 crore Acquired Group).
- **H1 EPS (before exceptional items):** Rs 26.2.
- **H1 ROCE:** 22.6%.
- **Net Debt (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 580 crore; Net Debt to Equity at 0.2 (excludes EUR 75.8 million received on Nov 2, 2026).
- **Net Working Capital:** 25.3%.

### Highlights
- Closed sale of VP4 raw material business on November 2, 2026 for EUR 75.8 million; all funds received and earmarked for debt reduction.
- Signed definitive agreements to acquire Clariant’s aluminum dyes manufacturing business (previously under toll manufacturing agreement), expected to close early next year.
- Restructuring in Germany involves Rs 74 crore provision for support-function headcount reduction; annual savings of a similar or slightly higher amount expected over the next 1.5 years.
- Went live on Wave 1 of IT/SAP integration; overall system integration targeted for completion by mid-next year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reaffirmed FY27 guidance for the Acquired Group: EUR 700 million revenue and EUR 35 million EBITDA (factoring in the VP4 divestment).
- Expects signs of demand uptake towards Q4 FY27.
- FY29 vision targets EBITDA margin reaching ~12%, driven predominantly by internal cost reduction/value capture (two-thirds) and revenue growth/recapturing lost market share (one-third).

### Demand & industry commentary
- US housing and European construction markets remain weak.
- Masterbatch demand remains volatile due to fluctuations in crude oil and polymer prices.
- European manufacturing index showed positive movement, and certain raw material prices have stabilized.
- Primary demand traction observed in coatings and digital inks.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices stabilizing, but lead times for raw materials and finished goods deliveries remain elevated due to geopolitical uncertainty.
- European employee restructuring program covers non-manufacturing support roles, aiming for cost rationalization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Backward integration for European pigment IP into India will require new capital deployment ("metal on the ground") rather than asset relocation.
- No major volume capex planned immediately; board is assessing long-term cash deployment options following VP4 proceeds.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 580 crore at Q2-end; will reduce substantially following receipt of EUR 75.8 million (~Rs 680-700 crore equivalent) on Nov 2, 2026.
- Working capital days saw seasonal and supply-chain related increases, though H1 inventory days improved YoY. Management is prioritizing net working capital efficiency in coming quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- RIECO business suffered internal supply chain bottlenecks and cost overruns on two late-stage projects, resulting in an EBITDA loss of Rs 3 crore in Q2.
- Ongoing geopolitical uncertainties impacting shipping logistics, lead times, and end-market demand.
- Upcoming operational risk from Phase 2/3 single-SAP system transition targeted by mid-next year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nitesh Dhoot (360 ONE):** *How much revenue growth is volume vs price?* Management answered that Legacy Sudarshan had some pre-buying in September due to SAP go-live in October; adjusting for that, legacy growth was split half-and-half between price and volume. Acquired Group growth was mostly price increases with muted volume growth.
- **Viraj Mahadevia:** *Details on the Rs 74 crore employee restructuring in Germany?* Management clarified it pertains to European support functions (not plant labor). Annual savings will match or exceed this amount, phasing in over 1.5 years as employees depart by fiscal year-end.
- **Viraj Mahadevia:** *Will board consider share buybacks given net cash position post-VP4 proceeds?* Management stated the board is actively evaluating capital allocation options to maximize shareholder returns and will present a detailed plan within a year.
- **Archit:** *What levers bridge Acquired Group margins to target levels?* Management stated levers include org structure rationalization, yield/utility improvements, global procurement, IT integration, AI usage, and future backward integration in India.
- **Nitesh Dhoot:** *Why acquire a Swiss manufacturing site for aluminum dyes instead of shifting to low-cost regions?* Management explained that product approvals are strictly tied to specific production sites; acquiring the site was critical for business continuity, though cost-optimization efforts will be applied.

### One-line takeaway
Sudarshan Chemical maintained its Acquired Group FY27 EBITDA guidance of EUR 35 million while strengthening its balance sheet through the EUR 75.8 million VP4 sale, despite near-term headwinds in European demand and execution challenges at RIECO.
