# Aarti Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue and EBITDA, driven by robust volume growth and strong operational performance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs. 3,887 crore (up 73% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 418 crore (up 44% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Over Rs. 802 crore (up 58% YoY from Rs. 507 crore)
- **PAT:** Rs. 169 crore (up 60% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 324 crore (up 117% YoY from Rs. 149 crore)
- **Net Debt:** Over Rs. 4,650 crore
- **FY27 Capex Guidance:** Rs. 700 crore to Rs. 800 crore
- **Zone IV Bottom-line Aspiration (first 18 months):** Rs. 250 crore to Rs. 350 crore
- **Long-term Contract Revenue Potential:** ~Rs. 1,500 crore (10+ year agreement announced Oct 13, 2026; US$ 25 million structured capex)
- **Augene Chemicals JV Revenue Potential:** Rs. 300-400 crore (steady-state)
- **Fuel Additives Capacity:** Expanded to 360 KTPA from 290 KTPA in Q1 2026
- **Working Capital:** Optimized to 55 to 60 days
- **Trading Volume (H1):** ~Rs. 500 crore

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue and EBITDA; quarterly EBITDA surpassed Rs. 400 crore.
- Middle East geopolitical tensions caused crude oil volatility, soaring raw material prices (Sulphur up 5-6x), and elevated freight costs, stretching working capital and net debt.
- Board approved raising up to Rs. 1,000 crore via an enabling resolution (approved by shareholders on Oct 31, 2026) to deleverage the balance sheet.
- Zone IV (Jhagadia, Gujarat) Phase I commissioned on Sep 7, 2026 (Calcium Chloride unit, PEDA plant, and section of Multipurpose Plant operational). Management plans 5-10 products in FY27, expanding to 20-25 products across 5 chemistry blocks by FY28.
- Entered a 10+ year manufacturing and supply agreement with a global specialty chemical major on Oct 13, 2026 (~Rs. 1,500 crore revenue potential, commercial operations in FY29).
- Augene Chemicals JV facility ready with trial production underway; order book gaining traction. Aarti Circularity (Plastic Recycling JV) project execution on track.
- Executed over 50 GenAI use cases in the workspace last quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the current strong momentum to continue, with appropriate guidance for next year to be provided closer to the end of the financial year.
- Aspiration is for Zone IV to be value-accretive and EBITDA-additive in FY28.
- Mid-term expectation is for the Energy segment to settle at 30% to 40% of the overall portfolio.
- Estimated tax rate for FY27/FY28: 15% to 20%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Dyes & Pigments: Steady growth driven by domestic NCB chain demand despite sulfur inflation.
- Agrochemicals: Stable volume growth, but mixed pricing trends; H2 might reflect weaker domestic monsoon impact. Quarterly contracts create a lag in price pass-through.
- Pharma & Fluoro Products: Pricing and volume gains in PNCB; robust paracetamol building block demand due to Chinese capacity rationalization. Fluoro product margins remain under pressure.
- Polymers & Additives: Robust demand in high-end engineering, EV, and defense. PDA chain softness created a short-term Q2 drag, with traction expected to resume in Q3 underpinned by DCB demand.
- Energy-Linked Portfolio: Favorable blending dynamics from crude prices and gasoline-naphtha cracks. Transitioning towards a solutions-based approach over the next 12-24 months.
- Tailwinds from China's capacity rationalization / anti-involution strategy and India's trade agreements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Absolute EBITDA / contribution margins are protected for ~2/3rd of the portfolio operating on pass-through, though percentage margins appear lower due to top-line inflation.
- Acid value chain (sulfur inflation) and select AgChem intermediates faced difficult price pass-through and margin compression.
- Inventory in waters: Rs. 800 crore to Rs. 1,000 crore.
- Provision of Rs. 40 crore taken between consol and standalone mainly for goods in transit.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY27 capex guidance maintained at Rs. 700 crore to Rs. 800 crore.
- ~Rs. 1,800 crore to Rs. 1,900 crore of CWIP (mostly Zone IV) expected to be capitalized by March 31, 2027.
- Fuel Additives capacity utilization remained strong at 360 KTPA. DCB debottlenecking aligned to 140 KTPA. NCB expansion under evaluation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased to over Rs. 4,650 crore due to stretched working capital from elevated RM prices.
- Enabling resolution passed to raise up to Rs. 1,000 crore for deleveraging and long-term flexibility.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Crude oil volatility, potential H2 softening of refining cracks and gasoline-naphtha cracks, and potential inventory losses if global pricing reverses.
- Fixed costs from Zone IV coming into the P&L around Q4.
- Import supply chain gaps for raw materials like Aniline/Methanol.
- Chinese overcapacity and dumping in the PDA chain creating short-term pricing pressure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Arun Prasath (Equities / 360 ONE):** Asked if H2 strength and current pricing could prompt a guidance upgrade from Rs. 1,800 crore EBITDA. Management stated momentum is strong, but offset by Zone IV fixed costs and potential crude corrections; guidance will be reviewed at year-end.
2. **Abhijit Akella (Equities):** Inquired about Zone IV peak revenue/bottom-line potential and CWIP capitalization. Management guided for Rs. 250-350 crore bottom line in the first 18 months and capitalization of Rs. 1,800-1,900 crore CWIP by March 2027.
3. **Aditya Khaitan (Investor):** Asked which segment faced RM pressure. Management highlighted the acid value chain (sulfur) and select AgChem intermediates due to quarterly lag.
4. **Darshita Shah (Analyst):** Asked about differences between NCB and PDA chains regarding Chinese competition. Management noted China's anti-involution (VAT rebate removal) helped NCB, whereas PDA suffers from Chinese overcapacity/dumping without similar policy shifts.
5. **Rohit Sinha (Analyst):** Inquired about trading volume inclusion in top line. Management stated H1 trading volume was ~Rs. 500 crore to build market for expanded capacities.

### One-line takeaway
Aarti Industries delivered record Q2 revenue and EBITDA driven by robust volume growth and pass-through pricing, while moving to deleverage its balance sheet via a Rs. 1,000 crore fund-raise and ramping up its Zone IV facility.
