# Aether Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Solid quarter with disciplined execution, ramp-up in CRAMS/CEM businesses, and strong new developments in oil and gas, semiconductors, and agrochemicals.

### Key numbers
- **CRAMS and CEM contribution:** >65% of sales.
- **Oil and gas contribution:** 33% of sales.
- **New customers added in H1:** 16 customers.
- **Customer and certification audits in H1:** >20 audits.
- **Agrochemical CEM contract volume:** 200 to 250 tonnes per year initially, projected to ~600 tonnes per year over time.
- **Baker Hughes potential market size:** USD2 billion to USD2.5 billion for new products; total Baker Hughes opportunity around INR750 crore to INR800 crore.
- **Target business with Baker Hughes:** >USD100 million (INR1,000 crore).
- **Semiconductor revenue target at maturity:** INR300 crore to INR400 crore in the next 2 to 2.5 years (by 2029).
- **Semiconductor sales volume target:** 300 to 400 tonnes by FY29.
- **Semiconductor average market price:** USD45 to USD50 per kg.
- **Working capital cycle:** ~165 days.
- **Capex guidance for FY27:** INR350 crore to INR370 crore.
- **CWIP on balance sheet:** ~INR700 crore (INR400 crore expected to be capitalized by the end of this financial year).
- **R&D projects:** >65 CRAMS projects.

### Highlights
- Commercialized a new set of oilfield industrial chemicals for Baker Hughes in Q2 FY27 after making minor modifications to a production stream at the Strata site (Site 4).
- Started first commercial shipments to semiconductor customers from existing production facilities; new production block at Magnum site on track for Q4 FY27 commercial production.
- Initiated a multiyear research program with Dow to develop advanced silicones manufacturing technology in India; immediate goal is to design, build, and operate a pilot unit.
- Moved R&D operations from 24/6 to 24/7 during the quarter.
- Signed a new CEM contract with a leading global agrochemical innovator for a new chemical entity, with production starting in Q4 FY27 at the fourth production block of the Magnum site.
- Added new CRAMS engagements with an automotive ancillary major, a global mining company, and an oil and gas major.
- Line of sight established on 14 of the 16 production blocks at the Magnum site (2 earmarked for LSM, 12 for CEM contracts).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects oil and gas and material science sectors to grow much faster than pharma and agro sectors.
- Capex guidance for FY27 maintained at INR350 crore to INR370 crore.
- Target to reach INR300 crore to INR400 crore top line from semiconductors in the next 2 to 2.5 years (by 2029).
- Target to grow Baker Hughes business to >USD100 million (INR1,000 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Macro environment remains volatile, but demand for CRAMS and CEM remains strong.
- Strong momentum in Europe with large-scale manufacturers and small/midsized specialty chemical companies looking to Aether as a manufacturing partner.
- Oil and gas sector expected to attract significant investments over coming years due to energy security focus.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins expected to improve as the portfolio moves more towards CEM and CRAMS.
- Management remains conservative on EBITDA margin guidance (conservatively maintaining ~30% view despite potential for higher margins).
- Inventory levels increased due to safety stock for new sites (Milliken and Magnum) and lead times for raw materials imported from the Far East.
- Price increases in raw materials are pass-through under contract manufacturing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Strata site (Site 4) modified for new Baker Hughes products without needing a new block.
- Magnum site (Site 5) construction progressing ahead of original anticipation; civil work for several blocks completed or above ground.
- ~INR400 crore of the ~INR700 crore CWIP expected to be capitalized by the end of FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital cycle at ~165 days (3 days higher than March 31 due to inventory build-up for new sites).
- INR200 crore of loans already taken, with interest being capitalized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile macro environment.
- Potential impact of war situations or pricing effects kept in mind via conservative margin assumptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sajal Kapoor (Antifragile Thinking) asked about R&D scaling, key person dependencies, and capacity selectivity.** Management stated the organization is flat with 12 long-tenured group leaders, and technology relies on a combined multidisciplinary flywheel (chemistry + chemical engineering), mitigating individual dependency. They noted they say "no" or "maybe" to RFQs within 2 days based on core competencies and safety.
2. **Farokh Pandole (Avestha Fund Management) asked about the revenue potential of the second series of Baker Hughes products.** Management stated the market size is USD2 billion to USD2.5 billion, and they target >USD100 million (INR1,000 crore) from Baker Hughes.
3. **Arun Prasath (Avendus Spark) asked if the new Baker Hughes products require additional capex.** Management clarified that the expansion is covered by the original capex at the Strata site, with asset turns expected to reach 3x to 3.5x at maturity.
4. **Abhijit Akella (KIE Limited) asked about weighted average interest rates and capitalization.** Management declined to disclose open-platform interest rates on the call and stated ~INR400 crore of the ~INR700 crore CWIP will be capitalized by the end of the financial year.
5. **Bhavika (Niveshaay) asked about segment revenue growth and inventory.** Management explained that oil and gas and material science are growing faster than pharma/agro, and inventory increases are strategic for new site ramp-ups.

### One-line takeaway
Aether Industries reported a solid Q2 FY27 driven by expanding CRAMS/CEM contracts, notably scaling partnerships with Baker Hughes and advancing commercialization across semiconductors, agrochemicals, and the Magnum site.
