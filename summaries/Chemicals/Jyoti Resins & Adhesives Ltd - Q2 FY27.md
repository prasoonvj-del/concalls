# Jyoti Resins & Adhesives Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by healthy volume growth, improved realisations, and recovery in EBITDA margins.

### Key numbers
- Volume growth: ~15% YoY in Q2 FY27; targeting 15% to 17% for the full year.
- EBITDA margin: Recovered to 23% in Q2 FY27 from 14.4% in Q1 FY27.
- Gross margin: ~63% in Q2 FY27.
- Manufacturing capacity: Brownfield expansion completed, increasing capacity from 2,000 to 3,500 tonnes per month (42,000 tonnes annually).
- Capacity utilisation: ~70% to 75%.
- Dealer network: Expanded to 14,000 dealers across 16 states (added Odisha).
- Current investments/cash: Rs 65 crore (up from Rs 3.6 crore in March).
- FY28 revenue target: Rs 450 crore to Rs 500 crore (aiming for Rs 375 crore to Rs 400 crore in FY27).

### Highlights
- Completed brownfield expansion to 3,500 TPM; greenfield facility in Gujarat planned with 1,500 TPM capacity and an investment of Rs 45 crore to Rs 50,000 (stated as Rs 45 crore to Rs 50 crore).
- Stepped up marketing investments to ~7% of revenue in H1 FY27 (compared to 3.5% last year), associating with brand ambassador Pankaj Tripathi and movie promotions.
- Onboarded 150 sales personnel in the last four months, taking the total sales team to 650.
- Continued pattern: Successfully completed brownfield capacity expansion to 3,500 TPM as noted in previous records.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 EBITDA margins to improve towards 24% to 25%, subject to no sharp raw material price increases.
- Aiming for Rs 500 crore top line by FY29 and progressively towards Rs 1,000 crore.
- Targeting 20% annual volume growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q3 and Q4 are seasonally stronger for building construction and adhesive products.
- Demand supported by deeper penetration into tier-2/tier-3 cities and hardware trades.
- OEM segment focus expanding with a longer-term target of 15% of total revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- VAM (Vinyl Acetate Monomer) prices averaged around $1,000/tonne, having briefly touched $1,100–$1,150/tonne.
- Implemented 6% to 7% price increases in May, which were accepted by the market.
- Maintained 60- to 90-day raw material procurement contracts to buffer against VAM and crude volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Brownfield CapEx of Rs 6 crore to Rs 7 crore is completed.
- Next phase includes a greenfield facility in Gujarat (1,500 TPM, estimated at Rs 45 crore to Rs 50 crore).
- Total active branches increased by 7 during the quarter to reach 61 branches.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free.
- Debtor days remain slightly elevated due to easing terms in newer geographies, with an overall cycle around 150-160 days (mature markets around 75 to 100 days; newer markets 120 to 150 days).
- Exploring channel financing solutions for smaller markets over the next 1-2 quarters to aid collections.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and fluctuations in crude and VAM prices.
- Slower real estate site execution observed during Q1.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rahul Jain asked about the achievability of Rs 500 crore revenue by FY28.** Management clarified that while FY29 is the original target, FY28 could see close to Rs 450 crore - Rs 500 crore if 20% volume growth is sustained.
- **Mahak Singhvi asked about the sharp increase in current investments/cash flow.** Management attributed it to repeat business cycles, profitability, and lower immediate CapEx post-brownfield completion.
- **Mudit Minocha suggested a share buyback given the cash on the balance sheet.** Management stated they prefer to deploy funds into sales growth, geographical expansion, and aggressive market capture rather than a buyback currently.
- **Harsh Chaurasia inquired about the high debtor days in new vs. mature markets.** Management explained that newer markets require longer credit terms (120-150 days) to build dealer relationships, while mature markets operate at 75-100 days.

### One-line takeaway
Jyoti Resins delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with ~15% volume growth and an EBITDA margin recovery to 23%, backed by aggressive distribution expansion and completed brownfield capacity.
