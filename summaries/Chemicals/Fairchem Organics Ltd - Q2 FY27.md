# Fairchem Organics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted improved price realizations and reduced raw material costs, though they remain cautious regarding global geopolitical headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 167 crore (+49.7% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 343 crore (+41.4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 10.5% (Q2); 10.3% (H1).
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 10.2 crore (Q2); Rs 21.2 crore (H1).
*   **Realization:** Rs 135/kg (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 130/kg (Q1 FY27) vs Rs 106/kg (Q2 FY26).
*   **Volumes:** 12,200 tonnes processed / 12,300 tonnes sold (Q2); 24,600 tonnes processed / 25,800 tonnes sold (H1).

### Highlights
*   Improved performance driven by better price realization and lower raw material costs.
*   Domestic market remains supportive; lower dimer acid imports helped operating margins.
*   Dumping from China has decreased, which management attributes to a reduction in Chinese export incentives.
*   Company purchased land worth Rs 12 crore from promoters; management noted this land was already in use for operations and a worker colony.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management is "cautiously optimistic" for the remainder of the year.
*   Management aspires to reach 15-18% EBITDA margins, contingent on capacity utilization improvements.
*   Targeting a 5% improvement in capacity utilization per quarter.
*   New product launch (novel technology) is delayed from Q2 to Q3 FY27 due to R&D modifications.
*   US exports are expected to commence once freight rates stabilize.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Domestic market environment is supportive.
*   Cosmetic industry demand in Europe is slowing due to geopolitical strife.
*   China has significant vegetable oil production, providing them with a raw material cost advantage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Import duty on Crude Soybean Oil reduced from 16.5% to 11%, which management expects will improve raw material availability and lower costs.
*   Fuel, salary, and manufacturing costs remained stable despite inflation.
*   Working capital cycle is ~100 days.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Current capacity utilization is 60-65%.
*   No immediate expansion plans; management is waiting for the success of the new product launch before committing to further capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Trade receivables stand at ~Rs 100 crore.
*   No current plans to reduce debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Uncertainties from the Middle East crisis and broader global macroeconomic environment.
*   High freight costs, particularly for exports to the US.
*   Potential competition from smaller oleochemical players for raw materials.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Can margins reach 14-15%?* A: Management stated they aspire to 15-18% but emphasized this is dependent on capacity utilization.
*   *Q: Why did the government previously increase import duty?* A: Management speculated it was to curb consumption and promote MSP for farmers; they do not expect further increases due to current inflation.
*   *Q: Status of new product?* A: Delayed to Q3; management is upgrading the plant based on R&D suggestions.
*   *Q: Why buy land from promoters?* A: It was already in use for operations/colony; the transaction was formalized in the current period.

### One-line takeaway
Fairchem is benefiting from lower raw material costs and reduced Chinese dumping, with management focused on scaling capacity utilization by 5% per quarter and launching a new, novel-technology product in Q3.
