# Indigo Paints Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth and healthy volume trends, offset by a severe, one-off compression in gross and EBITDA margins due to high-cost raw material inventory.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 340.5 crore, up 14.1% YoY (gross revenue growth of 18.8%).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 30.3 crore, down 33.8% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 8.9%, down from 15.3% YoY (a 640 bps decline).
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 13.8 crore, down 46.3% YoY (margin at 4.0% vs 8.5% YoY).
- **Gross Margin (Standalone):** 38.5%, down 660 bps YoY from 45.1%.
- **H1 Standalone Revenue:** Rs 690.5 crore, up 16.4% YoY.
- **H1 Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 92.3 crore, up 3.2% YoY (margin at 13.4% vs 15.1% YoY).
- **H1 Standalone PAT:** Rs 56.2 crore, up 8.3% YoY (margin at 8.0% vs 8.6% YoY).
- **A&P Spends (Standalone Q2):** Rs 18.3 crore (5.4% of revenue) vs Rs 15.7 crore YoY.
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2):** Rs 358.7 crore, up 15% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 31 crore, margin 8.6%.
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2):** Rs 13.4 crore, margin 3.7%.
- **Apple Chemie Revenue (Q2):** Rs 18.4 crore, up 35.3% YoY (H1 at Rs 38.1 crore, up 37.8% YoY).
- **Active Dealers:** 19,730 as of September 30, 2026.
- **Tinting Machines:** >12,700.
- **Depots:** 56 across 28 states (added Bikaner in Q2).
- **Indigo Color Canvas Stores:** 19.

### Highlights
- Primers, distempers and others led category value growth at 29.4% (9.4% volume growth).
- Enamels and wood coatings grew 17.8% in value and 8.2% in volume.
- Emulsions grew 13.3% in value and 10.4% in volume; Putty and cement paints grew 12.6% in value with flat volumes (-0.2%).
- Jodhpur new water-based plant (90,000 kiloliters/annum capacity) commenced trial production in September 2026 and expected to be in full production by end of November 2026.
- Installed solar capacity stands at 350 kW (Pune/Cochin), with an additional 1,200 kW underway at Jodhpur (completion by December 2026).
- Social initiatives: Indigo Seva Utsav repainted over 260 government schools; painter health benefit program covers >30,000 families; SkillUp trained >2,300 professionals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects gross margins to improve substantially from Q3 onwards, reclaiming leading industry positions during seasonally strong Q3 and Q4.
- EBITDA margins expected to normalize in H2.
- Management stated no further substantive capex is expected for the next two to three years (minor additions of Rs 15-20 crore annually possible).
- Priority remains to grow significantly faster than the market, deepen underpenetrated geographies, and drive portfolio premiumization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- October sales recorded highest-ever monthly sales and highest YoY monthly growth in 3-4 years.
- Management noted paint demand is largely inelastic to price increases historically.
- Industry implemented two sets of price hikes in Q3 (one implemented in late October/early November, another scheduled for November 21).
- No visible adverse impact on volumes due to price hikes so far.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Key raw material prices almost doubled in March and April due to the Iran war, leading to high-cost inventory consumption in Q2 P&L.
- Unfavorable product mix and higher discounts also impacted gross margins in Q2.
- Pass-through of higher-priced raw material inventory was largely over by August; September margins returned to near normal.
- A&P expenses held at 5.4% of revenue in Q2 to support brand building and influencer engagement.
- Treasury income was lower due to adverse bond yield movements.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Jodhpur water-based plant (90,000 kiloliters/annum) trial production underway; statutory clearances in place; commercial/full production expected by December 2026.
- Current year-round average capacity utilization is ~60%, peaking at ~75% in high-demand months (October, March).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Major capex cycle (such as the ~Rs 275 crore Jodhpur expansion) is largely complete for the next 2-3 years.
- Potential deployment of free cash flow (dividends, buybacks, or M&A) will be decided by the Board at an appropriate time.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical instability and potential crude oil volatility (e.g., Middle East tensions) impacting raw material availability and pricing.
- Unpredictability of COGS timing through the ERP system during extreme raw material price swings.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) asked about top-line growth expectations relative to the industry and market share dynamics.** Management responded that while H1 growth was in line, they aim for 1.5x to 2x industry growth, driven by influencer engagement and aggressive top-line strategies rather than heavy discounting.
- **Prakash Kapadia (Entrust) asked for a breakdown of the gross margin drop and a path to sustainable performance.** Management clarified that ~2-2.5% was product mix and the rest was high-cost raw material inventory expensing, reiterating confidence in H2 recovery.
- **Deepak Juneja (Individual Investor) questioned the status of the Jodhpur water-based plant and future inventory shock risks.** Management stated trial runs are ongoing with full production by December, and raw material cost shocks are not expected unless geopolitical conflicts restart.
- **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) asked if current price hikes fully cover inflation and whether demand is hurt by labor/paint inflation.** Management stated price hikes more or less cover inflation in totality, and no volume deceleration has been observed.
- **Akhilesh B (Northstar) inquired about volume elasticity and future capital allocation like buybacks.** Management noted paint demand is price-inelastic, and regarding buybacks, stated it is an agenda for the Board once a larger cash chest is built or if meaningful.

### One-line takeaway
Indigo Paints' Q2 FY27 profitability suffered a one-off shock from high-cost raw material inventory and unfavorable product mix, but management asserts the issue is resolved and expects strong margin recovery and volume momentum in H2.
