# Chemplast Sanmar Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth and specialty segment performance are offset by structural commodity headwinds and persistent net losses.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,323 crore (+28% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 111 crore (+158% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8% (vs 4% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Net Loss:** Rs 5 crore.
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 2,448 crore (+15% YoY).
*   **H1 Net Loss:** Rs 181 crore.
*   **Gross Debt:** Rs 1,748 crore (as of September 30, 2026).

### Highlights
*   **Paste PVC:** Strong performance with 23% sequential volume growth; demand peaked in September, driven by automotive and footwear sectors.
*   **Regulatory:** Madras High Court interim order for provisional assessment of paste PVC imports from EU and Japan remains in effect; government appeal was not entertained by the division bench.
*   **Restructuring:** Management stated a committee has completed significant work regarding the restructuring of specialty and commodity businesses and expects to provide an update soon.
*   **Value-Added Chemicals:** Performance remained subdued; caustic soda volumes impacted by Karaikal shutdown and chlorine disposal challenges at Mettur.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **CMCD:** Management expects Q4 to be a strong quarter due to dispatch weighting.
*   **Refrigerant Gases:** Commercial production of R32 from the new plant is expected to commence by the end of FY27.
*   **Profitability:** Management declined to provide specific bottom-line guidance, noting that profitability depends on VCM price evolution.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Suspension PVC:** Structural oversupply continues to weigh on industry profitability; management noted that while near-term spreads are volatile, they are moving in a positive direction.
*   **Caustic Soda:** International prices face pressure from excess supply from China and weak demand.
*   **Paste PVC:** Domestic demand remains healthy; limited domestic availability and constrained imports supported market prices.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Logistics:** Geopolitical developments in the Middle East and shipping route disruptions increased input and logistics costs.
*   **Spreads:** Management stated an aspirational goal of $200/ton spread in PVC to achieve PBT positivity; Q2 spread was ~$199/ton (factoring in NRV reversal).
*   **Raw Materials:** VCM prices are volatile; management noted a 12%-14% increase in VCM prices in the last month.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Refrigerant Gases (R32):** Capacity debottlenecked to 20 kt (from 14 kt); capex envelope remains Rs 250 crore.
*   **Maintenance:** Normal maintenance capex guided at Rs 60-70 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Gross debt stood at Rs 1,748 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Commodity Headwinds:** Structural oversupply from China in PVC and caustic soda continues to pressure margins.
*   **Operational:** Caustic soda production constrained by Karaikal shutdown and resulting chlorine disposal issues.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you elaborate on the CMCD pipeline and why it is bucking the AgChem slowdown?**
    *   *A:* Management noted traction in non-AgChem (pharma/specialty) and that inquiries from the AgChem space are starting to pick up.
*   **Q: What is the moat in the PVC business given it is a commodity?**
    *   *A:* Management cited established infrastructure and a large demand-supply gap in India (4.2 million tons demand vs 1.5 million tons capacity).
*   **Q: Is there any update on the restructuring committee?**
    *   *A:* Management confirmed significant work has been done with advisors and an update is expected at the earliest.
*   **Q: Will the paste PVC product development require additional capex?**
    *   *A:* No, these are recipe and process changes using existing infrastructure.
*   **Q: Is there a risk of inventory loss due to falling crude prices?**
    *   *A:* Not currently, as VCM pricing is linked to caustic soda production/chlorine availability, which provides a buffer.

### One-line takeaway
Strong growth in specialty chemicals and paste PVC is being offset by structural commodity headwinds, keeping the company near break-even.
