### Demand & industry commentary
- Helium demand exponential growth, 2-3 year supply shortfall outlook
- R32 India demand ~20,000 MT vs 80,000 MT planned swing-plant capacity
- Quota years FY24-26 ending Dec 2026 driving maximal import/sales pressure
- Post-2028: developed nations 15% HFC quota cut → accelerated HFO adoption
- Quota system GWP-based; swing plants can switch products (e.g., 125 vs 32)
- No export stress: domestic need ~5,000 MT, Mambattu 2,000-2,500 MT, leaving only ~2,500 MT exportable
