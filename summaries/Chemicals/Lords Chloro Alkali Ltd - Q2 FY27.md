# Lords Chloro Alkali Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong operational performance and cost efficiencies despite lower product realizations, with a clear roadmap for capacity expansion and diversification.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 115 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 100 crore (Q2 FY26), a 14% YoY growth.
*   **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 222 crore vs Rs 201 crore (H1 FY26).
*   **Caustic Soda Volumes:** 23,000 tonnes (Q2 FY27) vs 21,000 tonnes (Q2 FY26).
*   **Power & Fuel Expenses:** Rs 37.79 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 42 crore (Q1 FY27).
*   **H1 FY27 EBITDA Growth:** 10.36% YoY.
*   **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 28 crore (flat YoY).
*   **Renewable Energy:** 47 MW DC (40% of total energy needs).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Achieved 40% of power consumption from renewable sources, significantly reducing grid power reliance.
*   **Volume Growth:** Caustic soda volumes grew >9% YoY; Chlorinated Paraffin Wax (CPW) volumes grew >40% YoY.
*   **Cost Management:** Power and fuel costs declined by Rs 5 crore QoQ despite higher production volumes.
*   **Remuneration Policy:** Management stated they have capped executive remuneration at 4% for the next financial year to address investor concerns regarding optics.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Target:** Management expects at least Rs 550 crore revenue in FY28.
*   **Free Cash Flow:** Anticipates ~Rs 80 crore annual free cash flow post-July 2027.
*   **Renewable Target:** Aims to reach 70%-80% renewable energy consumption in the next 2-3 years (subject to Rajasthan state policy).
*   **Strategic Direction:** Plans to diversify into specialty chemicals to reduce reliance on commodity chemical cycles.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Caustic Soda:** India is a net exporter; management expects domestic demand growth to absorb upcoming capacity additions from Reliance and Adani.
*   **Chlorine:** Realizations in the Alwar region are between negative Rs 1,000 and 0, which management noted is better than the Gujarat region (approx. negative Rs 2,000).
*   **Market Dynamics:** Global caustic soda market is growing at ~5% annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Power Costs:** Grid power costs ~Rs 8.3/unit, captive solar ~Rs 2/unit, and group captive power ~Rs 4.50/unit.
*   **Raw Materials:** Cost of raw material consumed increased by Rs 6 crore QoQ, primarily due to higher prices of paraffin oil and sulfuric acid.
*   **CPW Margins:** EBITDA margins for CPW fluctuate between 0% and 20% depending on paraffin oil acquisition costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Ongoing Capex:** Total plan of Rs 315 crore. Rs 150 crore completed last year; remaining Rs 165 crore to be completed by July 2027.
*   **Capacity:** Current caustic soda capacity is 300 tonnes/day, expected to reach 360 tonnes/day by July 2027.
*   **Future Expansion:** Potential to reach 500 tonnes/day; requires an additional Rs 140-150 crore investment (timeline not committed).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Management described debt-to-equity and debt-to-EBITDA levels as "strong" and "well serviceable."
*   **Funding:** Open to using a mix of internal accruals, debt, and equity for future acquisitions or expansions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Commodity Cycles:** High sensitivity to caustic soda and raw material price fluctuations.
*   **Regulatory Risk:** Future renewable energy targets are dependent on Rajasthan state policy remaining unchanged.
*   **Execution:** Future expansion timelines (beyond July 2027) are not yet committed.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How will the company manage chlorine realizations?** A: Management noted that as large players (Reliance/Adani) enter the market and produce PVC, chlorine will be consumed internally, likely improving market dynamics.
*   **Q: Why did profitability decline QoQ despite lower power costs?** A: Management attributed this to a Rs 6 crore increase in raw material costs (paraffin oil/sulfuric acid) and higher employee benefit expenses (incentives).
*   **Q: Are you looking at inorganic growth?** A: Yes, management is researching potential acquisitions in the specialty chemicals space, to be pursued after the current capex cycle ends in July 2027.
*   **Q: What is the role of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)?** A: Management stated BESS is currently insignificant (1.45 MW) and only implemented to meet state mandates; they do not plan heavy investment here until a significant use case emerges.

### One-line takeaway
Lords Chloro Alkali is leveraging significant renewable energy cost advantages to maintain profitability amidst commodity price volatility, while preparing for a strategic pivot toward specialty chemicals post-July 2027.
