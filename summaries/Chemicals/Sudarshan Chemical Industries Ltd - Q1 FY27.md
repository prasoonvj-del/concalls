# Sudarshan Chemical Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Robust financial and operational performance driven by value capture, cost reduction, and strong profitability despite geopolitical and logistic challenges.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 2642 crore (One Sudarshan)
- **Acquired group revenue:** ~EUR 1 billion annual run-rate base; Q1 acquired group EBITDA at Rs. 146 crore (reported) and Rs. 128 crore (business EBITDA).
- **Business EBITDA (One Sudarshan):** Rs. 247 crore (operating profit from actual sales without inventory change impacts).
- **Reported EBITDA (One Sudarshan):** Rs. 266 crore (~60%+ YoY growth).
- **Legacy Sudarshan sales growth:** Performing in the 12% to 13% range.
- **Acquired group sales growth:** 5% YoY.
- **RIECO revenue:** Rs. 38 crore.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Rs. 12.3 per share (not annualized).
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** 22.7% (annualized).
- **Net Debt:** Reduced to Rs. 531 crore (down from a peak of Rs. 922 crore 18 months ago); debt-to-equity leverage at 0.2.
- **Net Working Capital:** 23.6% (annualized).
- **Guidance:** EUR 700 million turnover and EUR 35 million EBITDA for the acquired group (maintained, currently unrevised due to geopolitical watch-and-wait).

### Highlights
- Completed ~18 months since merging Sudarshan with Clariant and Heubach; footprint spans 19 manufacturing sites across 11 countries, 5 continents, 1,600+ products, and 4,000+ global customers. 55%-60% of assets are based in Asia.
- Opened a second global headquarters in Frankfurt.
- Set up a global capability center in Pune with centers of excellence.
- Progressing on "Project Integra" (One SAP project) to replace 4 different SAP systems and 130 applications, targeting go-live within this financial year.
- Acquired group debt reduced by 60% from peak levels to Rs. 531 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains previous guidance of EUR 700 million turnover and EUR 35 million EBITDA for the acquired group; will review/reconsider after Q2 given geopolitical uncertainties.
- Aspiration to reach 7x the EBITDA of 2023 and achieve a debt-free level.
- Long-term projection on the investor deck targets Rs. 12,000 crore+ revenue by FY2029.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Geopolitical issues in the Middle East caused energy cost spikes in Europe and India, logistics cost increases, and extended shipping/logistic cycles by two weeks, prompting customer purchase delays and hand-to-mouth inventory holding.
- **Coatings:** US and European decorative (house paints) and automotive markets subdued.
- **Plastics:** Short-term cycles due to polymer price increases and hand-to-mouth supply chains.
- **Printing Inks:** Volume-driven traditional ink markets declining due to digitalization; specialty packaging inks seeing better traction.
- **Special Applications:** Agro, digital inks, etc., showing very good growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin maintained at 50% plus levels (reported 2% movement this quarter attributed to cost reductions in yields, utilities, and production).
- Energy, raw material, and logistics costs increased due to Middle East crisis; safety stocks maintained to ensure business continuity.
- Price increases taken strictly to pass on input cost inflations, not to exploit supply-demand dynamics; will be reversed if raw material/oil prices soften.
- Subsidiary subsidiary restructuring: Wholly owned subsidiary signed an agreement with an employee representative body for an employee restructuring program post-June 30, 2026 (quantification and financial impact expected by end of Q2).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No new volume capex needed; existing capacities are sufficient to support projected growth.
- Moderate capex may be undertaken for special initiatives or backward integration based strictly on attractive ROIs.
- Restructuring internal holding: 70% stake in Sudarshan Colorants to be held directly by Sudarshan Chemical from overseas subsidiaries as part of pre-planned group structure rationalization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands at Rs. 531 crore with a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.2.
- Acquisition debt prepayment is accelerating alongside net debt reduction.
- RIECO business undergoing transformation; faced execution challenges in Q1 due to customer readiness delays and subcontractor labor availability, but management expects normalization and positive numbers by fiscal year-end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions (Middle East), energy cost spikes in Europe and India, and logistic cycle delays.
- Subdued decorative and automotive coatings demand in the US and Europe.
- Volume decline in traditional printing inks due to digitalization.
- Potential restructuring charges in European subsidiaries (quantification pending for Q2).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) on underlying volume growth vs. value/currency gains:** Management clarified that volume hasn't necessarily declined given the broad product mix (€1 to €140), 6% growth was achieved despite headwinds, and growth will be driven by recapturing lost business and continuous value capture.
2. **Ankur Periwal (Axis Capital) on debt repayment and RIECO status:** Management stated acquisition debt prepayment will accelerate as net debt drops, and RIECO execution challenges are expected to normalize starting current quarter.
3. **Rohit Nagraj on sustainability of recent price hikes:** Management confirmed price hikes were strictly to pass on cost inflation and would be reversed if raw material/oil prices soften.
4. **Nitesh Dhoot on the employee restructuring program note:** Management stated the financial quantification of the European works council restructuring agreement is fluid and will be clarified by the end of Q2.
5. **Rashmi Gohil on guidance revision given strong Q1:** Management answered they are maintaining guidance and adopting a wait-and-watch approach through Q2 due to geopolitical uncertainties.

### One-line takeaway
Sudarshan Chemical delivered a robust Q1 FY27 with a One Sudarshan business EBITDA of Rs. 247 crore and continued debt reduction, while management maintained its full-year guidance amid ongoing geopolitical and logistics headwinds.
