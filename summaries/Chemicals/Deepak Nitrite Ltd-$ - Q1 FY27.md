# Deepak Nitrite Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by record revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, alongside successful stabilization of integration projects and strong domestic demand.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs 2,592 crore (up 35% YoY, 22% QoQ)
- EBITDA: Rs 554 crore (up 159% YoY, 45% QoQ)
- EBITDA margin: 21% (compared to 11% in Q1 FY26 and 18% in Q4)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): Rs 468 crore (up 202% YoY, 55% QoQ)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 345 crore (up 207% YoY, 57% QoQ)
- Phenolics Revenue: Rs 1,775 crore (up 36% YoY, 24% QoQ)
- Phenolics EBIT: Rs 418 crore (up 254% YoY, 46% QoQ); EBIT margin at 24%
- Advanced Intermediates (AI) Revenue: Rs 804 crore (up 33% YoY, 14% QoQ)
- Advanced Intermediates EBIT: Rs 67 crore (up 89% YoY and QoQ); EBIT margin at 8%
- Domestic to export revenue mix: 85:15 (Consolidated) / AI mix at roughly 50:50
- Consolidated Net Worth: Rs 6,214 crore
- Debt-to-Equity ratio: 0.27x
- Total Capex spent so far on propylene and polycarbonate project: Rs 1,200 crore

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly performance across Revenue, EBITDA, PBT, and PAT.
- Completed and stabilized the ammonia-to-amines integration chain, transitioning from a nitration company buying nitric acid to a "nitrogen company that nitrates."
- Advanced Intermediates performance driven by improving domestic demand, better product mix, and backward integration synergies (nitrogen and hydrogenation facilities).
- Pre-commissioning runs of MIBK and MIBC demonstrated meeting or exceeding designed utility/consumption norms and capacity.
- Made major inroads into seed marketing of polycarbonate compounds with marquee customers.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in delivering growth profitably while continuing to invest in the future.
- MIBK, MIBC, and acetophenone projects to be commissioned in August 2026; remaining multi-purpose agrochemical intermediates facility projects within Q2.
- Polycarbonate commissioning targeted for H2 FY28-29, with Bisphenol A (BPA) commissioned a couple of months after.
- Total capex spend targeted at INR 1,000 crore to INR 1,500 crore for the current financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment gradually becoming more favorable for integrated and diversified manufacturers.
- Domestic market position remains strong; domestic demand is improving across product categories.
- Import duty on phenol was temporarily removed during Q1 by the government and has since been reimposed.
- No new phenol capacities coming up globally/in China currently; Indian demand is strong with limited domestic supply creating a balanced-to-short supply scenario.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion supported by higher operating leverage, favorable product mix, backward integration benefits, improved manufacturing efficiencies, and cost discipline.
- Raw material volatility (propylene, benzene, ammonia, toluene, hydrogen) successfully navigated through innovative procurement and engagement.
- Advanced Process Controls (APC) implemented in Phenolics to improve efficiency and maintain product quality.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phenolics capacity reached a run rate close to 1 lakh tonnes per quarter during periods in Q1; aiming to debottleneck existing assets to touch 4 lakh tonnes annualized phenol capacity with an incremental investment of ~INR 70-80 crore.
- Total announced propylene and polycarbonate project outlay of INR 11,500 crore funded by a 60:40 debt-equity ratio; debt fully tied up.
- Peak debt for the INR 11,500 crore project expected around INR 8,000 crore to INR 8,500 crore, maintaining a comfortable debt-to-equity ratio below 1.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains robust with a net worth of Rs 6,214 crore and debt-to-equity at 0.27x.
- Working capital efficiency improved via tighter inventory management discipline and receivables collections.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Q1 faced disruptions including unavailability of contractual manpower and a natural gas shortage impacting pre-commissioning activities.
- Global challenges include price volatility, evolving geopolitical dynamics, uneven demand recovery, and escalating freight and insurance rates.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nirav Jimudia (IIFL Capital) on Phenol run-rate and capacity:** Management clarified that they achieved 1 lakh tonnes run-rate during parts of Q1 and plan to debottleneck to reach 4 lakh tonnes annualized capacity. Management also stated MIBK/MIBC met design consumption norms.
2. **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) on Phenolics debottlenecking:** Asked what changed to push capacity towards 4 lakh tonnes. Management attributed it to confidence and investments in Advanced Process Controls (APC) requiring ~INR 70-80 crore, preserving quality.
3. **Archit Joshi (Nuvama Wealth) on Phenol/Polycarbonate timelines and merchant sales:** Management explained that Chemtech subsidiary capacity will integrate downstream (BPA to polycarbonate) while participating opportunistically in merchant markets; polycarbonate targeted for H2 FY28-29.
4. **Sajal Kapoor (Antifragile Thinking) on R&D translation to gross margins:** Management explained that legacy chemistries faced intense "red ocean" competition from players globally, but consolidation, stricter compliance laws, and flow chemistry adoption are structurally improving margins.
5. **Tushar Raghatate (Omega Portfolio Advisors) on peak debt for mega project:** Management answered that peak borrowing for the INR 11,500 crore project will be around INR 8,000 crore to INR 8,500 crore with a debt-to-equity ratio well below 1.

### One-line takeaway
Deepak Nitrite delivered record-breaking Q1 FY27 financial performance, underpinned by strong Phenolics operations, successful backward integration, and a clear roadmap for large-scale downstream expansion.
