# Premier Explosives Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating performance recovered strongly with 92% YoY revenue growth as Q1 execution delays eased.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (Targeting INR 500 crore to INR 600 crore revenue for full year / H2).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to positive as supply chain bottlenecks improved over the past two months.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Delays in obtaining export licenses for the April 2026 INR 350 crore export order; AP government issued a new defense policy offering four options for land acquisition.
- Last quarter's promises and delivery: Katepally RDX/HMX water trials started in October (slightly delayed from September target), with commercial production planned by mid-to-end December 2026; Apollo Micro Systems open offer (26%) successfully completed with board reconstitution expected in December.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR 145.28 crore (+92% YoY, +42% QoQ)
- EBITDA: INR 17.23 crore (+160.8% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 11.9% (+320 bps YoY, vs 5.7% in Q1 FY27)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 11.97 crore (-33% YoY, +295.1% QoQ)
- PAT margin: 8.2% (vs 23.6% in Q2 FY26)
- Total Order Book: INR 1,642 crore as of October 2026 (~96% Defense)
- H1 FY27 Performance: Revenue INR 247.84 crore (+13.8% YoY); EBITDA INR 23.11 crore (-15.9% YoY); PAT INR 15.0 crore (-54.8% YoY)

### Highlights
- Operating performance recovered post Q1 supply delays and global logistics headwinds.
- The 26% open offer by Apollo Micro Systems completed successfully, while the acquisition of the 41.33% promoter stake is in progress.
- Chaffs and flares expected to contribute ~INR 200 crore in revenue in FY27.
- Nipun land mine sample modifications accepted by DRDO/ARDE; bulk supply set to restart in FY27.
- *Pattern check*: Commercial explosives margins remain thin or negative due to long-term contract price escalation clauses lagging cost inflation.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets INR 500 crore to INR 600 crore revenue in FY27/H2.
- Remaining INR 270 crore balance from the October 2025 countermeasures order targeted for completion by March 2027 (pending delivery period extension).
- Targeting overall average margins of 20% to 25% long-term across the product mix.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Defense indigenization drives strong demand across rocket motors, high energy materials, countermeasures, and warheads.
- Global demand for high energy materials (RDX/HMX) remains high with short supply, though pricing remains fluid ($60–$70/kg).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins impacted in recent periods by stopping in-house flare component production (due to last year's accident) and substituting with costlier imported components.
- Service contracts (10–11 year tenure) face margin erosion because cost increases exceed escalation clauses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Katepally plant expansion increasing RDX/HMX capacity from 100 tonnes to 600 tonnes per annum.
- H1 FY27 capex incurred was INR 11 crore; management expects to complete the planned FY27 capex of INR 32 crore by year-end.
- Selected one of four options offered under Andhra Pradesh government’s defense policy for land acquisition.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company holds an order book of INR 1,642 crore providing revenue visibility (96% defense, balance bulk explosives and services).
- Singareni Collieries extended its existing bulk explosives supply order.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in obtaining government export licenses for multi-order defense shipments.
- Pending delivery period extensions for major domestic countermeasure orders (INR 75 crore balance from July 2023 order and INR 270 crore balance from October 2025 order).
- Heavy reliance on external/MIL components for 40mm grenade ammunition halting progress.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital):** Status of delivery extension and LD recovery for the July 2023 order?
   - *Management:* Extension applied and expected this quarter; supplies are ongoing and the remaining INR 75 crore order will complete in FY27. LD recovery application follows order completion.
2. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital):** Timeline for Katepally RDX/HMX commercial production and FY28 volume target?
   - *Management:* Water trials are ongoing; commercial production starts mid-to-late December 2026. Expecting ~30 tonnes production in Q4 FY27 (~INR 20 crore revenue) and targeting ~300 tonnes in FY28 (50-60% internal consumption), subject to export licensing.
3. **Keyurkumar Vadaliya (Niveshaay):** Reasons for gross margin decline over recent years?
   - *Management:* Bulk/industrial explosives are at negative margins; long-term service contracts have inadequate escalation clauses; plant accident forced reliance on higher-cost imported flare components instead of in-house manufacturing.
4. **Ayush (Nirmal Bang):** Revenue contribution expected from chaffs and flares in FY27?
   - *Management:* Expecting around INR 200 crore revenue from chaffs and flares based on orders in hand.
5. **Ronak Singhvi (NAFA Asset Managers):** Plans or facility for 155mm artillery shells or TNT explosives?
   - *Management:* No existing facility or plans for 155mm shells; company does not manufacture TNT explosives.

### One-line takeaway
Premier Explosives executed a strong Q2 operational turnaround with 92% YoY revenue growth, while advancing its 600 TPA Katepally expansion and Apollo Micro Systems acquisition integration.
