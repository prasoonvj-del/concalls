# Premier Explosives Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sequential recovery in revenue (+42% QoQ) and EBITDA margins (11.9% vs 5.7% in Q1) driven by improved execution and supply chain normalization.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (implied by management's focus on H2 execution and full-year targets).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from Cautious to Positive due to resolved supply chain bottlenecks and improved execution momentum.
- **New topics/risks:** Specifics on Apollo Micro Systems acquisition (open offer complete, promoter stake acquisition in progress).
- **Old topics:** Supply chain issues (reported as improved); Katepally trials (started).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Katepally trials (delivered/started); Apollo acquisition (progressing).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 145.28 crore (up 92% YoY, 42% QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 17.23 crore (up 160.8% YoY, 193.2% QoQ).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.9% (vs 8.7% in Q2 FY26, 5.7% in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT:** Rs 11.97 crore (down 33% YoY, up 295.1% QoQ).
- **Order Book:** Rs 1,642 crore (as of October 2026).

### Highlights
- **Defense Segment:** Accounts for ~96% of the total order book.
- **Apollo Micro Systems:** 26% open offer successfully completed; acquisition of 41.33% promoter stake is in progress.
- **Katepally RDX/HMX Plant:** Water trials have started; commercial production expected by mid-to-end December.
- **Nipun Mine Order:** Production restarted; bulk supplies expected within this financial year.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Management targets Rs 500-600 crore for H2 FY27.
- **Production:** Targeting 30 tonnes of RDX/HMX production in Q4 FY27; targeting 300 tonnes for FY28 (dependent on export licenses).
- **Margins:** Management targets 20-25% gross margins for defense products.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Defense:** Demand remains strong due to indigenization and global geopolitical conditions.
- **Bulk Explosives:** Singareni Collieries order continues; no new major orders announced.
- **Chaffs and Flares:** Expect ~Rs 200 crore revenue in FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margins:** Impacted by imported components (due to flare plant accident last year) and fixed-price long-term service contracts.
- **Outlook:** Management expects gross margins to improve to the 20-25% range.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex:** H1 FY27 capex was Rs 11 crore; total FY27 target is Rs 32 crore (to be completed by year-end).
- **Andhra Pradesh Land:** Government formulated a defense policy; company selected an option and replied.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Export Licenses:** Delays in obtaining licenses (order-to-order basis, 3-4 month processing time) remain a bottleneck.
- **Raw Materials:** Dependency on imported raw materials for certain defense products (e.g., 40mm ammunition).
- **Operational:** Hazardous nature of RDX/HMX production requires slow, step-by-step scaling.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital)** asked about export license delays. Management stated the process is government-dependent and not in the company's hands.
2. **Keyurkumar Vadaliya (Niveshaay)** asked about gross margin decline. Management attributed it to imported components and fixed-price long-term contracts.
3. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital)** asked about RDX/HMX revenue potential. Management is targeting 300 tonnes in FY28, contingent on export licenses.
4. **Ayush (Nirmal Bang)** asked about chaff/flare revenue. Management expects Rs 200 crore in FY27.
5. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital)** asked about 40mm ammunition. Management noted no progress due to lack of raw materials/primers from MIL.

### One-line takeaway
Premier Explosives delivered a strong recovery in Q2 FY27 with 92% YoY revenue growth and improved margins, supported by normalized supply chains and progress in defense execution, while awaiting export license clearances for further growth.
