# Indo Borax & Chemicals Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-08 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating revenues grew 31% YoY and net profits rose 59% YoY, supported by improved capacity utilization, realizations, and operational efficiencies.

### Key numbers
- Operating revenue: Rs. 70.36 crore (up 31.34% YoY from Rs. 53.57 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs. 19.80 crore (up 62.16% YoY from Rs. 12.21 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 28.1%
- Net profit (PAT): Rs. 16.25 crore (up 59.31% YoY from Rs. 10.20 crore in Q1 FY26)
- PAT margin: 22.3% (up 430 bps YoY)
- EPS: Rs. 5.07
- FY27 revenue guidance: Rs. 250 crore to Rs. 260 crore (vs. Rs. 215 crore last year) with ~20% EBITDA margin
- DOT volume target (FY27): 1,500 tonnes (contribution ~9% of total profile)
- Kronox acquisition stake: 64.26% equity share; total consideration ~Rs. 250 crore
- Kronox cash balance: ~Rs. 80 crore on balance sheet
- Kronox planned Capex: ~Rs. 110 crore (phased)
- Indo Borax planned Capex (next 2-3 years): ~Rs. 50 crore (Rs. 20 crore for Boron Oxide, Rs. 20-25 crore for Boric Acid, remainder for Zinc Borate)
- Funding for Kronox acquisition: ~Rs. 134-135 crore internal accruals, ~Rs. 48 crore from selling two non-core Mumbai commercial office spaces, rest via term loans and promoter commitment of Rs. 25 crore.

### Highlights
- New leadership team took charge in Q4 FY26 following Zenrock Chemicals emerging as the new promoter.
- Board approved the amalgamation of wholly-owned subsidiary Indo Borax Infrastructure Private Limited with the parent company (July).
- Board approved the acquisition of a 64.26% stake in Vadodara-headquartered Kronox Lab Sciences (August).
- Practical capacity utilization for Boric Acid expected at 96%-98% (targeting ~18,000 tonnes production vs. 20,000 tonnes nameplate capacity).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year FY27 revenue guidance at Rs. 250 crore to Rs. 260 crore with an EBITDA margin of ~20%.
- EBITDA absolute number projected to grow at 11% to 12%.
- Next financial year expected to show continued growth driven by new capacities in Boron Oxide and Boric Acid.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India Boric Acid market estimated at 40,000 tonnes per annum, projected to grow at 8% CAGR to over 53,000 tonnes by 2030.
- Steel and refractory segment accounts for 55%-60% of Boric Acid demand.
- Q2 is typically a low-volume monsoon quarter for the industry.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw materials (Boron ore, sulphuric acid, etc.) have become expensive due to ongoing wars, and passing on full costs is limited.
- Boric Acid spot pricing adjusted down slightly in Q2 due to monsoon-led weak demand.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Indo Borax plans ~Rs. 50 crore Capex over the next 2-3 years, primarily for Boron Oxide (~Rs. 20 crore), Boric Acid (~Rs. 20-25 crore), and Zinc Borate.
- Kronox has a planned Capex of ~Rs. 110 crore to be executed in phases over 18-24 months, starting once the deal is consummated; expected to be fully functional by FY30-FY31 (delivering 3x to 3.5x revenue).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Indo Borax maintains a strong balance sheet and healthy internal accruals.
- Kronox holds ~Rs. 80 crore in cash on its balance sheet.
- Acquisition funding of ~Rs. 400+ crore covered via internal accruals, asset sales, and term loans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing war conditions keeping raw materials expensive with limited pass-through capability.
- Q2 seasonality (monsoon quarter leading to lower volumes).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dhruv Bajaj (GrowthSphere Ventures) asked about the rationale for Kronox's promoter exit and Dahej Capex timeline.** Management stated they focus on business synergies (Boron chemistry, global reach, excipients/nutraceuticals) rather than previous promoters' reasons; Dahej Capex will take 18-24 months in phases after deal completion. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital) asked about margin sustainability for FY27.** Management guided for a soft Q2 due to monsoons and high raw material costs, reiterating a full-year EBITDA margin target of ~20%. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Mehul Savla (RW Equity) inquired about the funding structure for the Kronox acquisition and open offer.** Management detailed a breakdown of internal accruals, Mumbai office asset sales (~Rs. 48 crore), term loans, and a Rs. 25 crore promoter backstop totaling Rs. 432 crore available. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Saloni Arya (Molecule Ventures PMS) questioned how Zenrock (as an SPV) services its NCD debt without relying solely on Indo Borax dividends.** Management stated adequate funds and alternative mechanisms exist outside of IBCL dividends. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Harihar Koirala (Individual Investor) asked about ultra-high purity segments like electronic/solar grade.** Management stated they will evaluate opportunity pipelines after taking over the company. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
Indo Borax delivered a strong Q1 FY27 (31% revenue growth, 59% PAT growth) and is pursuing strategic inorganic expansion via the acquisition of Kronox Lab Sciences alongside internal specialty chemical capacity expansions.
