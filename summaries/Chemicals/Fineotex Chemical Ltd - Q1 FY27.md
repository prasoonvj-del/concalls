# Fineotex Chemical Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong YoY revenue and profit growth, successful integration and expansion of U.S. operations (CrudeChem), and robust demand.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 386.72 crore (up 165% YoY from Rs 146.62 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 133.40 crore (compared to Rs 45.96 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Gross Margin:** 35.42%.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 59.14 crore (up 134.7% YoY from Rs 25.20 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 15.70%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 48.21 crore (up 92.67% YoY from Rs 25.03 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC):** 33.06%.
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** 25.56%.
- **Return on Equity (ROE):** ~20%.
- **Working Capital Cycle:** 72 days.
- **Texas Facility Capacity:** Increased to ~148,000 metric tonnes per year (after adding 69,000 MTPA).
- **Total Company Manufacturing Capacity:** 2,68,000 metric tonnes per year.
- **Texas Capacity Utilization:** ~63%.
- **Oil & Gas Revenue Contribution:** ~65% of total revenue (~Rs 250 crore in the quarter); ~55% in volume.
- **Guidance Reiterated:** USD 100 million for FY27 and USD 200 million for FY28 from the oil and gas/U.S. segment (management statement).

### Highlights
- Commissioned a major capacity expansion at the Texas manufacturing facility (CrudeChem Technologies Group), increasing capacity to ~148,000 MTPA.
- Oil and gas operations under U.S. management achieved meaningful improvements in operational efficiency, execution capabilities, and scalability, leading to significant EBITDA margin expansion.
- Successfully passed on higher raw material costs stemming from Middle East geopolitical developments, preserving healthy blended margins.
- Indian new plant operations are housed under 100% subsidiary FSPL Specialties Private Limited (benefiting from tax planning under 115BAB at 15%).
- Expanded dealer network for Fineotex India from 103 to 115.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains guidance of USD 100 million in FY27 and USD 200 million in FY28 for the U.S. oil and gas segment, based on order books and customer expectations rather than crude oil price fluctuations.
- Expects capacity utilization at the Texas plant to scale up over the coming quarters based on strong order books and industry tailwinds.
- Aspires to continually improve EBITDA margins and scale the global specialty chemical portfolio through organic growth, strategic collaborations, and disciplined M&A.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Textile specialty chemicals business delivered a resilient performance despite a dynamic global operating environment, supported by improving exports, healthy domestic consumption, and value-added textile solutions.
- Oil and customer demand remains encouraging across production chemicals, water treatment solutions, flow assurance products, and specialty formulations.
- Sustainability and green chemistry are driving strong customer demand and better profitability across both textile and oil & gas segments.
- Oil and gas activities in the U.S. are stable and not directly disrupted by short-term crude price fluctuations since upstream/downstream investments are planned years ahead.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended gross margin improved to 35.42% (compared to ~29%-30% in the previous quarter and 33% last year).
- Consolidated EBITDA margin stood at 15.70% (up ~150 bps QoQ from 13.93%).
- Raw material volatility due to Middle East geopolitical issues was successfully mitigated by passing on price increases and utilizing war surcharges common in the U.S. market.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Texas facility capacity expanded by 69,000 MTPA to reach 148,000 MTPA; total capacity stands at 268,000 MTPA.
- Current 63% capacity utilization in Texas is based on a single shift; additional shifts or minimal incremental capex (USD 1–2 million) can support further scaling without heavy capital outlays.
- Actively evaluating disciplined, synergistic inorganic growth opportunities in specialty chemicals, led by M&A head Yusuf K Contractor.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a healthy balance sheet with strong cash reserves.
- Working capital cycle maintained at a disciplined 72 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term uncertainties from geopolitical developments and raw material volatility.
- Weather disruptions (storms, lightning, rainfall, snowfall in the U.S.) can cause temporary short-term halts in oilfield activities.
- High export mix and global operations expose the company to freight cost fluctuations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kriti Tripathi (NVS Brokerage):** Asked about expected revenue/bottom-line growth from the Texas expansion and status of inorganic opportunities. *Management answered that utilization is currently at 63% on a single shift with headroom for 30-40% growth; M&A is continuously evaluated based on strategic synergy and cash discipline.*
2. **Parth Modi (Equirus Securities):** Inquired if the USD 100M/200M guidance is contingent on crude prices staying above a certain level (e.g., USD 60). *Management clarified that guidance is based on order books and customer demand, not crude prices, as extraction activities continue regardless of short-term price movements.*
3. **Sunil Jain (Individual Investor):** Asked about standalone Indian revenue dip and CrudeChem margins. *Management explained the Indian revenue dip is optical due to accounting segregation of the new subsidiary FSPL, and stated that CrudeChem's EBITDA margins have turned positive and track well with consolidated levels.*
4. **Prateek Giri (Equirus Securities):** Questioned whether achieving high capacity utilization quickly implies the need for substantial future capex to hit USD 200 million. *Management stated that current utilization is based on one shift, and any minor capacity addition would require minimal capex (USD 1-2 million), making it non-consequential given their cash position.*
5. **Rushil Shah (Individual Investor):** Asked how Fineotex will achieve USD 200 million by FY28 given normal industry growth of 5-7%. *Management highlighted tailwinds from sustainability demand, supplier tie-ups, competitive pricing, and a snowball effect from major service operators adopting their solutions.*

### One-line takeaway
Fineotex reported a robust Q1 FY27 with 165% YoY revenue growth and 134% EBITDA growth, propelled by the successful integration and major capacity expansion of its U.S. subsidiary CrudeChem alongside resilient textile operations.
