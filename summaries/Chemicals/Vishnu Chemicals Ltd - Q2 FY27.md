# Vishnu Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Steady financial growth and resilient domestic demand, offset by geopolitical shipping disruptions, elevated logistics costs, and temporary volume drops in export markets.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated operating revenue: INR 429.53 cr (up 7.1% YoY)
- H1 FY27 operating revenue: INR 862.30 cr (up 15.4% YoY)
- Gross profit: INR 207.79 cr (up 20.3% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA: INR 64.00 cr (up 10.0% YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR 129.49 cr (up 13.7% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 14.9% (vs 14.5% YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 15.00%
- Profit after tax (PAT): INR 35.57 cr (up 8.2% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT: INR 75.21 cr (up 15.5% YoY)
- PAT margin: 8.3%; H1 FY27 PAT margin: 8.7%
- Shipping and forwarding expenses: 8.8% of operating revenue in Q2 (vs 5.9% in Q1)
- Export logistics cost: 20.9% of export sales in Q2 (vs 10.7% in Q1)
- Debt-to-equity: 0.67x as of September 30, 2026
- Debt-to-EBITDA: 2.8x as of September 30, 2026
- Total debt (including working capital): INR 717.00 cr
- Total capex incurred so far: INR 190.00 cr; Total planned capex outlay: ~INR 524.00 cr (excluding chrome metal JV); Next 12 months capex spend: ~INR 300.00 cr

### Highlights
- ICRA upgraded the company's long-term credit rating to A-Stable (from CARE A-minus).
- Entered a joint venture agreement with DCX Chrome, France, to establish a 6,000 metric tonnes/annum high-purity chrome metal manufacturing facility in Visakhapatnam (target production: Q4 FY28).
- Vizag plant was temporarily shut from October 20 due to cyclones and maintenance, with no business loss due to advance inventory; management stated it restarts "day after tomorrow" (consistent with record of Parwada plant resuming operations on November 9).
- Revenue mix: Domestic sales contributed 58%, exports 42%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets 18% to 19% (potentially up to 20%) EBITDA margins for next financial year (FY28), driven by backward/forward integration and product mix changes.
- Management expects export volumes and the US market to improve by Q4.
- Peak debt is expected to reach INR 800.00 cr to INR 820.00 cr over the next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic market remains resilient, supported by outperforming infrastructure and automotive sectors.
- Base cargo for the leather business was impacted by high freight costs (representing 20%-25% of selling price), causing a value loss of INR 25.00 cr to INR 30.00 cr per quarter.
- India's chrome metal demand is estimated at ~2,000 tons, growing at 12% to 13% YoY, currently met mostly via imports.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins sustained despite elevated logistics costs driven by West Asian geopolitical issues affecting international shipping.
- Volatility observed in raw materials like sulfur, sulfuric acid, and energy costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex outlay stands at ~INR 524.00 cr (~INR 250.00 cr for DMSO, ~INR 220.00 cr for oxide expansion); INR 190.00 cr spent to date.
- Dimethyl Sulfoxide (DMSO) 10,000 tonnes/annum plant expected to commence operations in Q1 FY28.
- Strontium capacity is at 12,000 tonnes/annum, targeting 70% utilization next year.
- Chrome capacity expansions include ~20,000 tonnes of incremental chrome oxide green and 5,000 to 6,000 tonnes of chromic acid. Chrome and barium capacity utilizations are over 75% and ~70% respectively.
- South African chrome ore mining operations have started; beneficiation plant commissioning is in the final phase, with shipments to India targeted from Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory days increased due to macro environment and logistics disruptions.
- Receivable days moved up to 72-73 days (compared to 62 days YoY).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia impacting international shipping and freight rates.
- Value loss of INR 25.00 cr to INR 30.00 cr per quarter in leather base cargo due to high freight.
- Volatility in key raw materials (sulfuric acid, sulfur) and energy costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sagar Jethwani (PhillipCapital PMS)** asked about export sales drop and lost sales. Management quantified the value loss at INR 25.00 cr to INR 30.00 cr per quarter in leather base cargo and expects recovery by Q4.
- **Nirali Gopani (Unique PMS)** asked about strontium profitability and utilization. Management stated utilization is targeted at 70% next year but did not provide specific EBITDA margin guidance.
- **Kaushal Sharma (Equinox Capital)** asked about chrome metal JV unit economics. Management stated EBITDA margins are expected upwards of 20% given product value, targeting 60%-70% exports.
- **Sheel Shah (Sameeksha Capital)** asked about inventory and receivable days. Management attributed inventory increases to the macro environment and noted receivables moved to 72-73 days.
- **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital)** asked about H2 outlook and FY28 EBITDA guidance. Management reiterated an 18%-19% EBITDA margin target for next year, driven by backward and forward integration.

### One-line takeaway
Vishnu Chemicals delivered steady Q2 FY27 performance with 7% revenue growth despite freight headwinds, and is expanding via strategic JVs, forward integration, and backward integration.
