# Chemicals: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian chemicals sector reflected a fragmented operating environment. While companies with strong CDMO exposure, specialized product pivots, or volume expansions (such as Aarti Industries, Acutaas Chemicals, and IOL Chemicals) reported robust revenue and profit growth, others faced severe margin compression. Managements universally highlighted cost headwinds stemming from Middle East geopolitical tensions—specifically soaring crude-linked raw materials, sulfur inflation (Aarti noted sulfur up 5–6x; Meghmani noted sulfuric acid at 7–8x), higher coal/utility prices, and surging freight rates. China remains a double-edged sword: companies benefited from anti-dumping duties (ADD) or anti-involution policies in select chains (like PVC and NCB), but suffered from aggressive Chinese overcapacity and dumping in others (such as PDA and crop protection).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Domestic agricultural and Kharif demand was broadly tepid or mixed due to uneven monsoons and delayed planting, as noted by Aarti Industries, Archean Chemical, Best Agrolife, and NOCIL. Export logistics and container availability via the Strait of Hormuz acted as a shared bottleneck for export-oriented players like Devson Catalyst and Meghmani Organics.
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   *Pharma and CDMO:* Acutaas Chemicals reported booming demand, achieving its Rs 1,000 crore CDMO target a year early, and IOL Chemicals saw non-ibuprofen APIs surge 70% YoY. Conversely, Hikal faced headwinds due to U.S. FDA remediation caps limiting new product approvals.
    *   *Agrochemicals:* Best Agrolife intentionally shed generic products (-30%) to focus on patented formulations (+20%), whereas Aarti Industries and Meghmani Organics struggled with lag in passing on raw material price hikes in generic AgChem intermediates.
    *   *Consumer/Building:* Asian Paints noted rural markets outperforming urban, with B2B buffering urban retail slowdowns. GHCL highlighted that global soda ash remains oversupplied with soft realizations, though domestic solar glass demand is rising.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Almost all companies reported margin pressures from escalating crude-derived inputs, coal, and sea freight inflation during Q2. 
*   **Disagreements & Strategies:** Companies diverged significantly in their pricing power. Clean Science chose to *absorb* high raw material costs rather than raise prices, prioritizing market share defense against Chinese pricing (which is 10–15% lower). In contrast, Asian Paints implemented calibrated price hikes (~7% in Q1, ~5% in Q2, and ~3.5% for November) to balance inflation with demand elasticity. Aarti Industries protected absolute contribution margins on two-thirds of its portfolio via pass-through mechanisms, even as percentage margins compressed due to top-line inflation.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Aarti Industries:** Maintained FY27 capex guidance at Rs. 700–800 crore; commissioned Zone IV Phase 1 in September 2026.
*   **Acutaas Chemicals:** Guided FY27 capex at ~Rs. 3,000 million; inaugurated its South Korea semiconductor plant ahead of schedule.
*   **Archean Chemical:** Invested ~INR 463 crore YTD in the SiCSem semiconductor project (total $240 million) which commenced civil works; progressing on bromine debottlenecking.
*   **Asian Paints:** Commissioned its VAE facility in September; full Phase 1 capacity expected within a year.
*   **Best Agrolife:** Capex remains deferred until profitability and business stability are fully secured.
*   **Clean Science:** Subsidiary CFCL completed trials for advanced HALS grades; expanding HALS capacity to 12,500 tonnes.
*   **DCW Ltd:** Initiated a Rs. 250 crore investment program for SIOP expansion and power infrastructure.
*   **Devson Catalyst:** Adding 5,088 MTPA to reach 11,293 MTPA capacity, operational by Q1 FY28.
*   **GHCL:** Approved Rs. 88 crore for in-house railway siding; commercialized vacuum salt and bromine projects.
*   **Hikal:** Incurred H1 capex of INR 81 crore; Panoli multipurpose facility commenced commercial production.
*   **IOL Chemicals:** Planned Rs. 250 crore capex for FY27; announced ~Rs. 500 crore across three new manufacturing projects (Ibuprofen expansion to 18,000 MTPA, CDMO facility, and specialty chemicals tolling).
*   **NOCIL:** Board sanctioned Rs. 130 crore capex for H2 and next H1; Dahej TDQ brownfield expansion progressing toward customer approvals.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Acutaas Chemicals:** Raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to ~30% (from 25%) and EBITDA margin guidance to 38%–40%; accelerated its Rs 1,000 crore CDMO target by one year to FY27.
*   **Asian Paints:** Adjusted decorative volume growth guidance to 8–9% (from 8–10% for FY27) and flagged the new Anti-Dumping Duty on TiO₂ as an active headwind.
*   **Best Agrolife:** Management admitted it is "hard to say" if the previously stated 10–15% revenue growth target is achievable, though profitability targets remain intact.

## Company one-liners
*   **Aarti Industries:** Delivered record Q2 revenue and EBITDA while preparing a Rs. 1,000 crore fundraise to deleverage.
*   **Acutaas Chemicals:** Accelerated its CDMO targets and raised full-year guidance on the back of stellar Pharma Intermediate growth.
*   **Archean Chemical:** Advanced bromine debottlenecking and semiconductor civil works despite severe cost inflation and logistics friction.
*   **Asian Paints:** Relied on industrial JVs, new product innovations, and VAE commissioning to buffer an inflationary decorative market.
*   **Best Agrolife:** Sacrificed generic top-line revenue to successfully expand margins through a high-margin patented product mix.
*   **Clean Science:** Achieved record revenue led by HALS volume growth, but absorbed raw material inflation to protect market share.
*   **DCW Ltd:** Benefited from basic chemical spread normalization while navigating input cost friction in specialty silicates.
*   **Devson Catalyst:** Delivered margin expansion despite export disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **GHCL Ltd:** Commenced vacuum salt and bromine production while managing through a globally oversupplied soda ash market.
*   **Hikal Ltd:** Suffered net losses due to FDA compliance remediation and raw material spikes, but anticipates an H2 recovery.
*   **IOL Chemicals:** Posted stellar H1 profit growth supported by high asset utilization and announced Rs. 500 crore in expansion projects.
*   **Meghmani Organics:** Grew revenues 16% YoY but saw margins squeezed by surging energy, logistics, and sulfuric acid costs.
*   **NOCIL Ltd:** Grew top-line via realization pricing despite a minor volume contraction, maintaining guidance toward the upper end of its range.

## What to watch next quarter
*   The trajectory of Middle East geopolitical tensions and their direct impact on crude-linked raw materials, sulfur prices, and ocean freight rates.
*   Ramp-up progress and commercial customer approvals for major new capacities: Aarti's Zone IV products, Clean Science's advanced HALS grades, DCW's SIOP expansion, and Devson Catalyst's upcoming facility.
*   Regulatory developments, specifically the implementation and impact of Anti-Dumping Duties on TiO₂ (Asian Paints/Meghmani) and PVC (DCW).
*   Progress on U.S. FDA reinspection timelines for Hikal's Bengaluru facility.
