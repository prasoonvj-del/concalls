# Chemicals: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian chemical sector in Q2 FY27 presented a stark dual dynamic: robust volume growth and expanding opportunities in specialized downstream niches vs. intense commodity oversupply from China and ongoing global logistics friction. 

Managements across the board highlighted strong domestic demand in pharmaceutical intermediates, CDMO/CRAMS, automotive, infrastructure, battery materials, and defense applications. Companies with higher exposure to custom synthesis and high-barrier chemistries (such as **Acutaas Chemicals**, **Aether Industries**, **Navin Fluorine**, **Neogen Chemicals**, **Jubilant Ingrevia**, and **Solar Industries**) reported buoyant order pipelines and expanding margin profiles.

Conversely, bulk commodities—particularly chlor-alkali, suspension PVC, soda ash, and select dye/agrochemical intermediates—faced structural margin compression. Global market oversupply driven by Chinese capacity additions (flagged by **Tata Chemicals**, **GHCL**, **Chemplast Sanmar**, **Clean Science**, and **Hikal**), paired with volatile crude/petrochemical feedstocks and elevated container freight rates due to West Asian supply chain disruptions, weighed on realization pass-through. In response, managements are accelerating structural cost-mitigation projects, primarily backward integration and captive renewable energy (solar/wind) adoption (**20 Microns**, **Epigral**, **J.G. Chemicals**, **Lords Chloro Alkali**, **Navin Fluorine**).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Broad Consensus Pockets
* **Pharma Intermediates & CDMO/CRAMS:** Strong global customer traction, anti-China sourcing shifts, and contract expansions driven by innovators (**Acutaas Chemicals**, **Aether Industries**, **Navin Fluorine**, **Jubilant Ingrevia**, **Hikal**).
* **Automotive & Infrastructure / Construction:** Strong volume pulls for zinc chemicals (**J.G. Chemicals**), rubber chemicals (**NOCIL**), performance additives (**20 Microns**), and construction polymers (**Apcotex Industries**).
* **Advanced Energy & Defense:** Explosives and energetic materials (**Solar Industries**), battery chemicals/salts (**Neogen Chemicals**, **Himadri Speciality Chemical**, **Acutaas Chemicals**), and semiconductor chemicals (**Aether Industries**, **Archean Chemical**).

### Divergence & Mixed Outlooks
* **Agrochemicals & Crop Protection:** Managements disagree on the timing of recovery. **Excel Industries**, **Hikal**, **Jubilant Ingrevia**, and **Meghmani Organics** report persistent weakness, low pricing power, and Chinese dumping. In contrast, **Acutaas Chemicals** and **Aether Industries** report resilient custom synthesis/NCE contract demand.
* **Commodity Plastics vs. Specialty Polymers:** **Chemplast Sanmar** and **DCW** struggle with low suspension PVC spreads due to Chinese oversupply, whereas **Chemplast Sanmar** saw 23% sequential volume growth in paste PVC. **Styrenix Performance Materials** and **Supreme Petrochem** note healthy domestic appliance demand for ABS/EPS, but highlight volatility in imported styrene monomer.
* **Dyes, Intermediates & Pigments:** **Bodal Chemicals** highlighted structural Chinese capacity discipline lifting H-acid and vinyl sulfone realizations to allow direct exports to China. Conversely, **Kiri Industries** faced margin pressure due to executing older low-priced orders and elevated utility costs, while **Meghmani Organics** noted depressed pigment demand in Europe despite US resilience.
* **Soda Ash:** **GHCL** and **Tata Chemicals** see persistent global oversupply and elevated inventories in China keeping global prices soft, even as domestic demand remains supported by rapid Indian solar glass capacity expansion.
* **Aroma & Flavor Ingredients:** **Oriental Aromatics** and **S H Kelkar** report strong domestic FMCG and flavor demand, whereas **Camlin Fine Sciences** faced severe vanillin pricing pressure due to cheap Chinese material routed through Southeast Asia.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

### Key Raw Material Inflation
* **Petrochemical Feedstocks & Solvents:** Phenol, acetone, methanol, aniline, VCM, and styrene monomer saw elevated volatility (**Clean Science**, **Epigral**, **Styrenix**, **Supreme Petrochem**, **Camlin Fine Sciences**).
* **Acids & Sulfur:** Sulfur and sulfuric acid prices jumped sharply due to Middle East supply shortages, creating margin drag for **Aarti Industries**, **Meghmani Organics**, **Archean Chemical**, and **TANFAC Industries** (where sulfur rose up to 5x).
* **Energy Costs:** High coal, lignite, gas, and furnace oil prices inflated utility costs for **20 Microns**, **Epigral**, **DCW**, and **Tata Chemicals UK**.

### Freight & Supply Chain Friction
Geopolitical conflicts in West Asia (Red Sea, Strait of Hormuz) inflated container shipping costs—in some cases by 2x to 10x—and extended transit times, impacting export-heavy businesses including **Devson Catalyst**, **Platinum Industries**, **NOCIL**, **Vishnu Chemicals**, **Yasho Industries**, and **Vinati Organics**.

### Pass-Through Delays & Structural Mitigation
* **Pass-through Lag:** B2B spot suppliers experienced 30-to-90-day lags in passing on freight and raw material increases (**20 Microns**, **TANFAC**, **Vinati Organics**, **Yasho Industries**, **Kiri Industries**), temporary eroding gross/EBITDA percentage margins.
* **Captive Energy & Sourcing:** Managements are countering energy inflation by commissioning captive solar/wind plants (**20 Microns** targeting 40% power coverage; **Lords Chloro** at 40%; **Epigral** targeting 30-35%; **J.G. Chemicals** solar phase 1) and acquiring captive raw material mines (**20 Microns**, **Archean**).

## Capex & capacity

* **Active High-Scale Programs:** 
  * **Aarti Industries:** Maintaining FY27 capex at Rs 700–800 crore; Zone IV Phase I commissioned.
  * **Aether Industries:** FY27 capex guided at Rs 350–370 crore; Magnum Site 5 construction progressing ahead of plan with line of sight on 14 production blocks.
  * **Navin Fluorine:** Annual capex frame of Rs 700–1,000 crore; debottlenecked Dahej MPP, expanding R32, and executing CDMO cGMP-4 Phase 2 (Rs 125 crore).
  * **Neogen Chemicals:** Doubling Telangana Organolithium capacity to 600 MTPA (Rs 45 crore); executing battery chemicals salt plant at Pakhajan post-Rs 600 crore QIP.
  * **TANFAC Industries:** HFC-32 project 90% complete; expanding AHF to 45,000 MTPA and solar grade DHF to 50,000 MTPA.
  * **Privi Speciality Chemicals:** Phase 1 debottlenecking completed (54,000 MTPA); advancing Phase 2 and 3 capex outlays (~Rs 1,000 crore total).
  * **Solar Industries:** Scaling defense manufacturing capacity to support a Rs 17,568 crore defense order book.
  * **Vinati Organics:** Announced Rs 500 crore capex for new specialty platforms in personal care, water treatment, and oilfields.
* **Pauses, Delays & Asset Closures:**
  * **Apcotex Industries:** Nitrile latex plant expansion call remains paused pending margin consistency.
  * **Meghmani Organics:** Titanium dioxide plant operations remain suspended due to unviable 7-8x higher sulfuric acid costs.
  * **Camlin Fine Sciences:** Diphenol plant kept closed since May 2026 due to uncompetitive local production vs. cheap Chinese imports.
  * **Tata Chemicals:** Kenya mining operations suspended due to a regulatory notice from the Ministry of Mining.

## What changed since last quarter

* **Acutaas Chemicals:** Raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to ~30% (from 25% in Q1) and EBITDA margin guidance to 38%–40% (from "stable"). Accelerated its Rs 1,000 crore CDMO target to FY27 (one year ahead of schedule).
* **TANFAC Industries:** Revised FY27 revenue growth guidance upward to 35%–40% (from 30% previously) following strong solar grade DHF demand.
* **Camlin Fine Sciences:** Cut full-year expectations, stating it will not achieve its earlier guidance of Rs 200 crore EBITDA at a 10% margin due to severe H1 liquidity and raw material headwinds.
* **Solar Industries:** Raised tone to highly confident on surpassing its full-year revenue guidance of Rs 14,000 crore.
* **NOCIL:** Clarified full-year FY27 revenue will close towards the upper end of its Rs 1,400–1,600 crore guided range.
