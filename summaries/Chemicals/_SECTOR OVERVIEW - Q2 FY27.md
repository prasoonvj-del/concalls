# Chemicals: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector delivered **strong top-line growth** in Q2 FY27 — Aarti Industries (+73% YoY), Navin Fluorine (+57%), Solar Industries (+69%), J.G. Chemicals (+64%), Asahi Songwon (+66.5%), Neogen (+42%), Yasho (+89.5%) — but **profitability diverged sharply**. Specialty/CDMO names (Navin Fluorine 34.2% EBITDA margin, Privi 27.5%, Clean Science standalone 36%, Aether >65% CRAMS/CEM) held margins; commodity-heavy or logistics-exposed players saw compression (Vinati Organics -27% EBITDA YoY, Camlin Fine Sciences 2.5% consolidated EBITDA margin, Hikal negative PAT, Meghmani 11% consolidated EBITDA margin, Tata Chemicals consolidated loss). **Geopolitical freight/energy inflation** (Middle East, Red Sea) and **China overcapacity/dumping** (PDA chain, pigments, carbon black feedstock, TDI, sulfonamides) were the most cited cross-cutting headwinds. Capex intensity remains high across specialty, fluorochemicals, battery materials and defence.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian chemicals has historically made money through cost-competitive commodity/basic chemical manufacturing and export arbitrage. Over the last ~5 years the model has shifted toward **specialty chemicals, CDMO, import substitution (China+1), backward integration into raw materials, and higher value-added downstream products** with global partnerships. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **Specialty/CDMO scaling** – *scaling* – Aarti Industries (Zone IV 5-10 products FY27, 20-25 by FY28; 10+ yr Rs 1,500 cr contract), Navin Fluorine (CDMO FY27 USD 100M order book visibility, cGMP-4 Phase 2 Rs 125 cr), Jubilant Ingrevia (25+ confirmed CDMO molecules, peak Rs 1,500-2,000 cr), Hikal (new agro CDMO contract Rs 50-75 cr/yr peak), Aether Industries (14 of 16 Magnum blocks line-of-sight, Baker Hughes >USD 100M target), Privi Speciality (Phase 2/3 Rs 1,000 cr capex, Rs 1,700-1,800 cr incremental revenue), Clean Science (HALS 2x YoY volume, Performance Chemical 2 Q4 FY27), Rossari Biotech (HPPC 20% YoY, export 31% of revenue).  
- **Battery materials / EV supply chain entry** – *early to scaling* – Himadri Speciality (LFP 2,000 TPA Q3 FY27, 40,000 TPA next year; anode engineering phase; CNT 200 TPA Q4 FY27), Neogen Chemicals (battery chemicals Rs 37 cr Q2 vs Rs 5 cr YoY; FY28 >Rs 1,000 cr target; Organolithium doubling to 600 TPA), TANFAC Industries (HFC-32 10,000 MTPA Q3 FY27 commissioning), Stallion India Fluorochemicals (Bhilwara R32 10,000 MTPA trials Mar 2027, Mambattu HFO blending Q1 FY28), India Glycols (bio-glycols, performance chemicals 36% YoY), Gulshan Polyols (6 MW green hydrogen BOO), Archean Chemical (SiCSem semiconductor $240M, EC received Oct 2026).  
- **Backward integration & captive raw materials** – *scaling* – 20 Microns (captive talc/quartz mines Dec 2026, 9 MW solar ~40% power, Rs 8 cr annual EBITDA benefit), Clean Science (ethoxylation facility for HALS precursor, 5-7% margin uplift), Vinati Organics (Rs 500 cr capex in adjacent chemistries, pilot-tested), PCBL Chemical (coal distillation approval imminent), GHCL (salt field productivity +3 lakh tonnes/2yr, captive salt 40% of needs), Archean Chemical (brine field 24,000 ha planned, Phase 1 4,000 ha CWIP ~Rs 80 cr).  
- **Global footprint expansion** – *scaling* – Fineotex Chemical (Fineotex America Inc. for industrial water/wastewater, Texas 148,000 MTPA), Rossari Biotech (Saudi Arabia greenfield land lease, 8-12 months for blueprint), J.G. Chemicals (BDJ Materials Dubai subsidiary for sourcing/sales), Yasho Industries (global lubricant additive players localizing in India), Aether Industries (Dow silicones pilot, Baker Hughes multi-year), Solar Industries (defence exports, Pinaka ~Rs 9,000 cr family order value).  
- **Debt-funded capex with balance sheet management** – *mainstream* – Aarti Industries (Rs 1,000 cr enabling resolution for deleveraging), Neogen Chemicals (Rs 600 cr QIP 6.5x oversubscribed), TANFAC Industries (Rs 350 cr QIP + preferential), Anupam Rasayan (Bliss GVS Rs 300 cr term loan + Rs 1,450 cr quasi-equity), Camlin Fine Sciences (Rs 250 cr rights issue for working capital), Vinati Organics (Rs 500 cr + potential Rs 500 cr from internal accruals), Ester Industries (Elite JV Rs 1,200-1,400 cr at JV level), Gulshan Polyols (Rs 100-120 cr Muzaffarnagar expansion), India Glycols (Rs 114 cr CWIP across 14-15 projects).

**Government push**

- **PLI schemes** – 20 Microns bid submitted for rare-earth permanent magnets (REPM) [Background].  
- **India-EU FTA** – Prospective, expected to drive German investments and chemical exports [ET Chemicals analysis, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus:** Domestic demand resilient across construction, auto, pharma, agro, solar glass, defence. Export demand mixed — logistics/freight volatility and China competition weigh.  
**Divergence:**  
- **Agrochemicals:** Aarti Industries (stable volume growth, quarterly contract lag), Jubilant Ingrevia (muted pricing, excess China capacity), Hikal (elevated RM/energy, China pricing pressure, channel inventory depleted), PCBL Chemical (tyre grades 77,914 T, performance chemicals 39,698 T, specialty 16,568 T; customer destocking), NOCIL (domestic volumes single-digit growth aided by GST 2.0, export single-digit; weaker monsoon/inflation near-term headwind).  
- **Pharma/CDMO:** Navin Fluorine (healthy growth, strong order visibility), Hikal (Swissmedic GMP, 28 customer audits, US FDA reinspection FY27), Jubilant Ingrevia (steady volumes, sustained demand), Aether Industries (CRAMS/CEM >65% sales, 16 new customers H1).  
- **Specialty chemicals:** Privi Speciality (broad-based domestic/export, African market growing), Clean Science (HALS 2x YoY, resilient across performance/pharma/FMCG), Rossari Biotech (HPPC 20% YoY, textile 24%, AHN 19%), Yasho Industries (industrial chemicals ~90% revenue, 62% volume YoY, 152+ products).  
- **Battery/EV materials:** Himadri Speciality (robust demand, 11% volume YoY), Neogen Chemicals (US 45x tax credits driving non-FEOC sourcing, Indian ACC ~60 GWh by Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28), TANFAC (solar grade DHF robust, Indian solar cell 100 GW in 3 yrs), Stallion India (helium exponential demand, 2-3 yr supply shortfall).  
- **Commodity chemicals:** Tata Chemicals (global soda ash oversupply, China inventory 1.89 MT Sept, India healthy on solar glass/infra), GNFC (TDI 70k TPA vs Wanhua 360k new plant, fierce China competition), PCBL Chemical (CBFS tightness, Russian refinery multi-decade lows, coal tar firming in China), GHCL (global oversupply, Solvay plants below cost, India demand moderated to 1%+).  
- **Pigments/Colorants:** Sudarshan Chemical (US housing/EU construction weak, masterbatch volatile, coatings/digital inks traction), Meghmani Organics (pigment utilisation 51%, Europe challenging, US good), Asahi Songwon (AZO/API pricing pressure, China Plus One not playing out, Phthalocyanine structural efficiencies).  
- **Fluorochemicals:** Navin Fluorine (HFC firm pricing, HFO 10% global ref gas demand), TANFAC (HFC-32 steady, domestic competitive), Stallion India (R32 India demand ~20k MT vs 80k planned swing capacity), Vinati Organics (ATBS destocking H1, H2 recovery; antioxidants/IBB strong).  
- **Defence/Explosives:** Solar Industries (defence Rs 1,002 cr Q2, 29% mix; order book Rs 17,568 cr defence), Premier Explosives (order book Rs 1,642 cr, 96% defence; chaffs/flares ~Rs 200 cr FY27), Fineotex Chemical (oil & gas ~65% segment, CrudeChem scaling).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Raw material inflation:** Sulfur 5-6x (Aarti Industries), 5x (TANFAC), 7-8x normal (Meghmani Organics sulfuric acid); furnace oil +33% (20 Microns Rs 1.34 cr impact), gas +77% (20 Microns Rs 0.62 cr), coal/lignite +60-70% (Epigral, DCW), phenol doubled in 6 months (Camlin Fine Sciences), fluorspar inventory built to 6-7 months (TANFAC), propylene oxide/lauryl alcohol/phenol spikes (India Glycols).  
**Freight/logistics:** Export freight +11% (20 Microns Rs 2.92 cr), ocean freight doubled/tripled (Rossari Biotech), vessel shortages/longer transit (PCBL Chemical), Red Sea/Middle East disruptions (Yasho Industries Rs 10→20 cr Q1→Q2, Vishnu Chemicals export logistics 20.9% of export sales Q2 vs 10.7% Q1, Stallion India freight +20%).  
**Pass-through mechanisms:** Aarti Industries (~2/3 portfolio pass-through, % margins appear lower due to top-line inflation), Navin Fluorine (pricing actions, forex tailwinds, operating leverage), Vinati Organics (RM passed, logistics/energy not fully), Yasho Industries (could not pass spot cost increases on accepted POs in Q2, expects pass-through from Q3), Meghmani Organics (45-day lag), TANFAC Industries (45-day lag), NOCIL (conversion cost +Rs 10-20/kg), PCBL Chemical (diversifying feedstock beyond Gulf to SE Asia).  
**Margin outcomes:** Navin Fluorine 34.2% EBITDA (H1 +367 bps YoY), Privi Speciality 27.5% (vs 26.1%), Clean Science standalone 36% EBITDA, Aether Industries (conservative ~30% view), 20 Microns gross 47.7% (engineered mix), Vinati Organics standalone 21% Q2 (ATBS shift + unpassed logistics), Camlin Fine Sciences consolidated 2.5% (liquidity, sub-scale), Hikal 10.3% (RM +100% solvents, US FDA remediation Rs 20 cr/yr), Meghmani consolidated 11% (input/energy/logistics), Tata Chemicals consolidated loss (UK energy, Kenya suspension), GHCL 24.7% (energy/freight elevated), GACL 15.74% (RE 55% power, avg rate Rs 7.09/unit H1 vs Rs 7.6), Lords Chloro Alkali (RE 40% power, grid Rs 8.3 vs captive solar Rs 2).  
**China pricing pressure:** Aarti Industries (PDA chain dumping), NOCIL (import dumping, ADD on sulfonamides helped but antioxidants 60% business), PCBL Chemical (competitors chasing low-margin spot volume), Sudarshan Chemical (European competition, VP4 divested), Meghmani Organics (pigment overcapacity), Asahi Songwon (AZO/API tough pricing), Styrenix Performance Materials (Thailand structural challenges).
