# Supreme Petrochem Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY growth in revenue and PAT, with management highlighting robust domestic demand despite geopolitical headwinds and supply chain volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Operating Income:** Rs 1,957 crore (vs Rs 1,100 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **H1 FY27 Operating Income:** Rs 3,649 crore.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 160 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8.12%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 95 crore (almost doubled YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 331 crore (9.08% margin).
*   **Sales Volume (Q2):** 95,242 MT (+24% YoY, +35% QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 Sales Volume:** 1,66,085 MT (-3% YoY).
*   **Interim Dividend:** Rs 2.5 per equity share (125%).

### Highlights
*   **Volume Drivers:** Revenue growth driven by increased volumes and higher selling prices (linked to raw material costs).
*   **Operational Challenges:** Navigated volatile styrene prices, supply chain disruptions, elevated ocean freight rates, and subdued export volumes.
*   **Competitive Pressure:** Duty-free imports from ASEAN/China intensified domestic price competition during the first two months of Q2; imports normalized after duty exemption withdrawal in July.
*   **Xmold:** Operations streamlined with improved margins and product profile; numbers doubled YoY.
*   **Product Mix:** Speciality products now account for ~40% of the mix.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Demand:** Domestic demand remains good, supported by the festive season.
*   **Future Outlook:** Management expects to outperform last year's volumes if global conditions stabilize post-US midterm elections.
*   **ABS Capacity:** Phase 1 (70,000 tons) utilization is currently 85% of truncated capacity (due to equipment issues). Full capacity utilization for the 80,000-ton compounding facility is expected by FY29-30.
*   **EPS Demand:** FY27 total demand in India estimated at 1,65,000–1,70,000 tons.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Segments:** Demand is balanced 50-50 between OEM and non-OEM segments.
*   **Appliances:** Strong demand from major appliance manufacturers setting up new units under PLI schemes.
*   **Exports:** Currently difficult to execute due to high freight rates and volatility; company continues to engage with customers for future recovery.
*   **Imports:** Styrene monomer landed price has crossed $1,600.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Materials:** Styrene monomer prices have risen from $900–$950 to $1,600–$1,650.
*   **Pass-through:** Raw material price increases are passed through to customers, though a time lag exists.
*   **Spreads:** GPPS spreads are ~$200–$250; HIPS spreads are ~$300–$350.
*   **Inventory:** Increased inventory levels are primarily due to higher raw material prices and material in transit.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Projects:** Wide-width insulation board line (1,50,000 cubic metres) and compounding capacity expansion (50,000 to 80,000 tons) remain on track.
*   **Timeline:** Insulation board commercial production expected in Q1 FY28; compounding expansion by Q3 FY28.
*   **Funding:** Capex is funded entirely through internal cash accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Status:** Company remains debt-free.
*   **Cash Position:** Investable surplus of Rs 886 crore as of September 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** War clouds and supply chain disruptions from the Gulf region impacting raw material availability.
*   **Freight:** High ocean freight rates are shrinking deltas and making exports challenging.
*   **Volatility:** Management noted difficulty in predicting future trends due to global uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On ABS Capacity Utilization:** Management clarified they are operating at 85% of *truncated* capacity (due to damaged equipment not yet fully repaired), not rated capacity.
*   **On Inventory Loss:** Management stated there is no "inventory loss" per se; the SAP system uses moving average valuation, and price volatility is "part of the game."
*   **On Export Opportunities:** Management explained that the time gap between order receipt and shipment makes it difficult to lock in prices/freight in the current volatile environment.
*   **On EPS Market Share:** Management clarified their market share in EPS is lower than 70%.
*   **On Inorganic Growth:** Management confirmed no plans for inorganic growth at this time.

### One-line takeaway
Supreme Petrochem delivered strong YoY profit growth and volume recovery despite significant raw material cost inflation and supply chain headwinds, maintaining a debt-free balance sheet while continuing its strategic capacity expansions.
