# S H Kelkar and Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong growth in the Flavour segment and ongoing momentum in international markets despite geopolitical and inflationary headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 636 crore (up 15% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue at Rs 1,299 crore (up 14% YoY).
*   **Fragrance Segment H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,088 crore (up 11.9% YoY).
*   **Flavour Segment H1 Revenue:** Rs 184 crore (up 47% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (H1):** Rs 168 crore (up 33% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (H1):** 13%.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 911 crore as of September 30, 2026.
*   **Capex Guidance:** Rs 30-35 crore for FY27; Rs 75-80 crore for FY28.

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance:** Fragrance growth was broad-based across geographies. Flavour segment growth was driven by domestic and international demand, though management noted Q2 revenue of Rs 72 crore was below the typical run rate compared to a strong Q1.
*   **Strategic Focus:** Management is consolidating gains from recent investments in global creative, technical, and manufacturing capabilities in Europe and America.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** The company intentionally exited low-margin categories (approx. Rs 25 crore revenue impact) to improve profitability.
*   **Insurance Claim:** Expected to be closed by March 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management expects 12-13% YoY revenue growth over the next 2-3 years.
*   **EBITDA Growth:** Targeting 13-14% EBITDA growth YoY in the next 2-3 years.
*   **Margin Targets:** Management aims to reach 15% EBITDA margins within 12-18 months, depending on inflation stabilization.
*   **Debt Reduction:** Expects September debt levels to be the peak for the year, with reduction anticipated by March 2027 through inventory optimization.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Dynamics:** Management remains optimistic about demand in the second half of the year, expecting supply chain issues and Middle East tensions to stabilize.
*   **Regional Trends:** U.S. and European markets are seeing initial sales generation from new Creative Development Centers (CDCs).
*   **Flavour Segment:** Management views 20% YoY growth as sustainable for the Flavour business, noting that the 47% H1 growth included some one-off inventory stocking by clients.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Management believes there is a "wide open space" for mid-sized players in Europe and America due to industry consolidation, allowing the company to compete with large global players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Gross margins are expected to remain around 43%. Management noted potential for 0.5% improvement if raw material inflation eases.
*   **Inflation:** Management observed high base inflation in the last six months due to geopolitical disturbances.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Fixed costs for CDCs are largely in place; management expects operating leverage to improve EBITDA margins as sales scale up.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Europe:** Factory is fully operational since May 2026.
*   **India:** Fragrance factory renewal expected to complete by end of FY27 (Q4). A second factory is expected in FY28.
*   **CDCs:** All centers in Germany, Manchester, and the U.S. are manned and operational.
*   **Maintenance Capex:** Estimated at Rs 15 crore annually.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Inventory:** High inventory levels were maintained in H1 to ensure supply continuity. Management plans to reduce inventory intensity over the next six months.
*   **Interest/Depreciation:** Interest costs expected to remain in the Rs 16-17 crore per quarter range for the second half. Depreciation/amortization is expected to be Rs 145-150 crore on an annualized basis post-capitalization of new facilities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On ROCE:** Management explained that ROCE remains subdued due to heavy, disproportionate investments in new markets (Europe/USA) and domestic recovery capex post-fire. They expect returns to normalize as these investments scale over 7-8 years.
*   **On Growth Drivers:** Management clarified that 12-13% growth is a composite of mature businesses (growing at 5-6%) and new products/categories (growing at 25%).
*   **On Standalone Margins:** Management clarified that standalone numbers are not reflective of actual business performance because operations moved to subsidiaries (KFG/KFL) following the fire incident; they expect restoration next year.
*   **On Competition:** When asked about higher growth/margins of smaller peers (e.g., Privi), management attributed it to specific tailwinds in the ingredient business and currency dynamics, rather than a direct comparison of their core fragrance/flavour model.

### One-line takeaway
S H Kelkar is in a transition phase, prioritizing long-term global expansion and capacity building over short-term ROCE, while targeting a return to 15% EBITDA margins within 12-18 months.
