# Rossari Biotech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA, broad-based growth across divisions, and improved working capital.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs. 697.6 crore (up 19% YoY) in Q2 FY27
- **Gross Profit:** Rs. 220.3 crore (up 19% YoY) in Q2 FY27
- **Gross Margin:** 31.6% in Q2 FY27 (up 200 bps QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 87 crore (up 21% YoY) in Q2 FY27
- **EBITDA Margin:** 12.5% in Q2 FY27 (up 91 bps QoQ)
- **PAT:** Rs. 38.9 crore (up 5.5% YoY, 11% QoQ) in Q2 FY27
- **Diluted EPS:** Rs. 7.02 in Q2 FY27
- **Division Revenue Growth (H1 FY27 YoY):** HPPC up 20% to Rs. 1,095.1 crore (Q2 up 18% to Rs. 541.9 crore); Textile Specialty Chemicals up 24% to Rs. 226.9 crore (Q2 up 26% to Rs. 120.8 crore); Animal Health and Nutrition (AHN) up 19% to Rs. 72.8 crore (Q2 up 11% to Rs. 34.8 crore).
- **Core B2B Revenue:** Rs. 619.9 crore (up 21% YoY) with EBITDA of Rs. 87.2 crore (margin 14.1%) in Q2 FY27.
- **Institutional & B2C Revenue:** Rs. 77.7 crore (up 3% YoY) with EBITDA of Rs. 0.2 crore in Q2 FY27.
- **Net Debt:** Rs. 221 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs. Rs. 248 crore in June 2026 and Rs. 281 crore in March 2026). Gross borrowings: Rs. 361.8 crore.
- **Working Capital Days:** 86 days in Q2 FY27 (vs. 97 days in FY26).
- **Export Growth:** HPPC export revenue grew ~34% YoY / ~35% QoQ; overall H1 exports at 27%, reaching 31% in Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA.
- Institutional & B2C business turned EBITDA positive (Rs. 0.2 crore) due to portfolio rationalization and cost discipline.
- Board approved leasing land for a greenfield manufacturing facility in Saudi Arabia (KSA) through wholly-owned subsidiary Rossari International Limited Company.
- Thailand blending facility ramping up steadily, aiding regional textile exports.
- Unitop plant received EXCiPACT registration, aiding pharma surfactant/excipient exports.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 EBITDA margin to remain around 12% (+/-) for the full year, with margin improvements anticipated next year.
- Management expects mid-teen to high-teen revenue growth for the full year.
- Management aims to bring exports above 30% of overall revenue by the end of the year.
- Management targets becoming debt-free on working capital debt by mid-next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Environment remains dynamic with volatile raw material prices, Middle East geopolitical pressures affecting freight/logistics, and cautious customer buying behavior.
- HPPC growth driven by value-added products in personal care, agro surfactants, adjuvants, and oil and gas solutions.
- Textile growth driven by export markets (Bangladesh, Southeast Asia, Turkey, Latin America), the Thailand facility, new fiber chemicals, and silicone-based/sustainable auxiliaries.
- AHN Q2 is traditionally the weakest season; peaks in Q3 and Q4.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Freight costs doubled/tripled compared to H1/Q2 last year, impacting EBITDA margins as customers are unwilling to absorb the entire hike.
- Core raw materials (like phenol) prices increased YoY; company passed on most increases.
- Margin focus enhanced via "tail cutting" (discontinuing bottom 5% lowest-margin products) and portfolio mix optimization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dahej ethoxylation capacity utilization is at ~30%; peak utilization expected ~2 years post-commissioning (end of FY28) with a 3x asset turnover target.
- Blended capacity utilization across facilities is close to 60%.
- Remaining capex for H2 FY27 estimated at Rs. 50 crore to Rs. 75 crore, focused on pharma, aroma chemicals, hydrogenation, and biosurfactants. Indian capex will be significantly lower going forward.
- Saudi Arabia (KSA) project is in land-leasing phase; detailed blueprint, capex, and timelines to be finalized and brought to the Board over the next 8-12 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross borrowings reduced by ~Rs. 46 crore in H1 to Rs. 361.8 crore. Net debt-to-equity stands at 0.16.
- Net working capital improved to 86 days, targeting 87-90 days range.
- Positive operating cash flow of Rs. 131 crore generated in H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical turmoil in the Middle East impacting shipping routes (Bab el-Mandeb, Hormuz), container/ISO tank availability, and driving up insurance and freight costs.
- Constrained ethylene oxide (EO) availability from supplier Reliance limited ethoxylate volumes in Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Maitri Shah (Sapphire Capital) on Export Margins & B2C Plans:** Management noted exports reached 31% of revenue in Q2, targeting 30%+ for the full year. B2C turned positive and is targeted to be EBITDA positive in H2; the plan to exit B2C at the right time/value remains unchanged.
2. **Gagan Dixit (Emkay Global) on EO Supply & Capex:** Management stated EO constraints from Reliance limited Q2 volumes, but allocation should ease next year when Reliance's expansion kicks in. Free cash flows will primarily target debt reduction before heavy KSA spending.
3. **Nitesh Dhoot (Anand Rathi) on Core B2B Margins:** Management explained that Y-o-Y core margin compression (14.1% vs 14.8%) was driven by surging freight costs and higher raw material prices (phenol) that were not fully absorbed by customers.
4. **Hemkesh Khattar (Green Portfolio) on KSA Timelines:** Management clarified that KSA is currently in land leasing and preliminary boundary wall phases; detailed capex and timelines will be finalized over the next 8-12 months.
5. **Madhur Rathi (Sequent Investments) on ROCE and Gross Margins:** Management stated ROCE will improve as recent capex ramps up utilization. Unitop's lower legacy gross margins and higher advance raw material working capital cycles impacted consolidated gross margin comparisons.

### One-line takeaway
Rossari delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA in Q2 FY27, driven by strong export growth and a turnaround in the B2C segment, while navigating raw material volatility and freight headwinds.
