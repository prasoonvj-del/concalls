# Chemicals - Specialty chemicals: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Management tone across the specialty chemicals sector in Q2 FY27 is mixed to cautiously optimistic. Companies heavily focused on contract manufacturing (CRAMS/CDMO), high-tech applications (semiconductors, battery materials, oilfield chemicals), and active pharmaceutical intermediates (Acutaas Chemicals, Aether Industries, Neogen Chemicals, Fineotex Chemical, J.G.Chemicals) reported strong volume-driven revenue growth and expanding margins. Conversely, commodity-linked, agrochemical intermediate, and polymer players (Camplin Fine Sciences, Chemplast Sanmar, NOCIL, Clean Science, Vinati Organics) faced profitability headwinds due to volatile feedstock costs (particularly crude derivatives, phenol, and sulfur spikes), unpassed logistics/freight inflation from West Asian geopolitical tensions, and persistent Chinese dumping. 

Balance sheet management has come into sharp focus, with leading companies initiating fund-raising and deleveraging exercises (Aarti Industries board approving up to Rs. 1,000 crore, Neogen raising Rs. 600 crore via QIP, Camlin Fine Sciences announcing a Rs. 250 crore rights issue, and Ganesh Benzoplast divesting its liquid tank farm business for Rs. 1,154 crore).

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far**
Historically, Indian specialty chemical producers operated as low-cost intermediate manufacturers driven by regional commodity export opportunities and asset-heavy capacity expansion. Over the past five years, the industry has rapidly shifted toward asset-light contract development and manufacturing (CRAMS/CDMO), custom synthesis, and integrated solution platforms tied to long-term global supply contracts. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **Expansion into Battery Materials & EV Value Chain** | *Scaling*
  Indian chemical majors are aggressively expanding into battery chemicals, electrolyte salts, and active cathode materials driven by localized battery gigafactory rollouts in India and non-FEOC tax credit mandates in the US. Neogen Chemicals completed a Rs 600 crore QIP and consolidated its electrolyte salt undertaking under subsidiary NML for Rs 245 crore, reporting battery chemical revenue of Rs 37 crore in Q2 FY27 and guiding for Rs 250–300 crore in FY27 and over Rs 1,000 crore in FY28 [Neogen Chemicals Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Himadri Speciality Chemical is commissioning a 2,000 TPA LFP Cathode plant in Q3 FY27, targeting a 40,000 TPA expansion alongside Carbon Nanotubes (200 TPA) in Q4 FY27 [Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Acutaas Chemicals commercialized its 3rd battery product and is validating its 4th additive [Acutaas Chemicals Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. This shift elevates asset turnover and long-term operating margins.
- **Entry into Semiconductor & High-Tech Electronic Chemicals** | *Early*
  Specialty players are diversifying into electronic-grade solvents, semiconductor processing chemicals, and data center applications. Aether Industries started commercial shipments to semiconductor customers with a dedicated target of Rs 300–400 crore top line by FY29 [Aether Industries Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Acutaas Chemicals inaugurated its South Korea semiconductor plant ahead of schedule [Acutaas Chemicals Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Fineotex Chemical established US subsidiaries Fineotex Holdings and Fineotex America to provide industrial water management solutions targeting data centers and semiconductor fabs [Fineotex Chemical Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Jubilant Ingrevia acquired a 40% stake in Zettaone Technologies for Rs 189 crore to enter the electronics design management system (EDMS) space [Jubilant Ingrevia Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. This provides structural diversification away from traditional agro/pharma cycles.
- **Scale-up of Long-Term CRAMS / CDMO Platforms** | *Mainstream*
  Chemical manufacturers are securing multi-year take-or-pay supply contracts with global innovators to insulate revenues from spot volatility. Aarti Industries executed a 10+ year agreement with ~Rs 1,500 crore revenue potential [Aarti Industries Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Anupam Rasayan signed a 6-year contract with a US specialty metal firm expecting Rs 100–150 crore H2 FY27 revenue [Anupam Rasayan India Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Acutaas Chemicals reached its Rs 1,000 crore CDMO target one year early in FY27 and set a new target of Rs 2,000 crore by FY30 [Acutaas Chemicals Ltd Concall Q2 FY27]. Hikal signed a multiyear agro CDMO contract with a European global player worth Rs 50–75 crore annually at peak realization [Hikal Ltd Concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **Sulfonamides & Anti-Dumping Duties:** DGTR/Ministry of Finance actions on anti-dumping duties on Titanium Dioxide imports from China [Meghmani Organics Ltd Concall Q2 FY27] and recommended ADD on PVC/sulfonamides [NOCIL Ltd Concall Q2 FY27] provide protection for domestic producers against Chinese overcapacity.
- **Madhya Pradesh Govt Park Approvals:** Approval of agro, chemical, and textile parks to boost regional ecosystem investments [Govt: MP govt approves agro, chemical, textile parks, Nov 2026].
- **Gujarat AAR Biodegradable GST Clarification:** Clarified 5% GST rate on biodegradable bags subject to valid biodegradability claims [Govt: Gujarat AAR 5% GST rate on biodegradable bags, Nov 2026].
- **Coal Gasification Initiative:** Pending Rs 37,500-crore initiative by the Coal Ministry to support synthetic natural gas and methanol feedstocks [Govt: Coal Ministry Rs 37,500-crore coal gasification initiative, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
- **Consensus on CRAMS, Energy & High-End Industrial Demand:**
  - **CRAMS/CDMO & High-Tech:** Robust demand across custom synthesis, semiconductors, electronics, and oilfield applications. Aether Industries highlighted CRAMS/CEM contributing >65% of sales, driven by Baker Hughes oilfield contracts and semiconductor qualification. Acutaas Chemicals accelerated its Rs 1,000 crore CDMO goal to FY27.
  - **Automotive & Infrastructure:** Domestic auto momentum supported strong volumes in zinc oxide and rubber chemicals. J.G.Chemicals reported record revenue driven by PV and 2-wheeler growth, while Apcotex cited strong performance in construction and waterproofing.
- **Divergence on Agrochemicals, Agroscience & Commodity Polymers:**
  - **Agrochemicals:** Agrochemical intermediates remain mixed. Excel Industries noted DETC quarterly sales hit multi-year lows due to suboptimal monsoon (87% of normal) in India and LATAM. Hikal noted agro customers bought strictly for immediate requirements amid Chinese dumping. Conversely, Anupam Rasayan and Aether reported resilient agro CRAMS/CEM traction due to long-term innovator agreements.
  - **Specialty Polymers & Rubber Chemicals:** Ester Industries saw severe demand erosion (-49% YoY) in a marquee high-margin specialty polymer due to a customer-side technology switch. NOCIL experienced a 3% YoY drop in Q2 volumes due to logistics disruptions and temporary domestic tire/agri demand softness. Clean Science put Barbituric acid on hold due to 3x higher domestic raw material costs.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Raw Material Inflation & Feedstock Volatility:**
  - Sulfur prices surged 5–6x, straining raw material value chains for Aarti Industries and DMCC. Phenol prices doubled in six months, causing Camlin Fine Sciences to keep its Diphenol plant shut since May 2026. Methanol (Rs 61–62/kg vs normal Rs 26–27) and Ammonia (Rs 85–90/kg vs normal Rs 45–50) remained elevated for Balaji Amines.
  - Open-market maize prices escalated sharply to Rs 30–32/kg due to El Niño, impacting Gulshan Polyols' near-term Q3 margins.
- **Freight, Shipping & Pass-Through Lags:**
  - Middle East geopolitical tensions and Strait of Hormuz/Red Sea route disruptions caused container shipping rates to skyrocket (Devson Catalyst noted 20ft container rates rose 10x from $600–800 to $6,000–8,000; Platinum Industries export logistics doubled to Rs 5 crore).
  - Pass-through mechanisms insulated contractual players (Aether, Aarti, Fineotex, Anupam), but spot and short-cycle sellers (Clean Science, Vinati Organics, Meghmani Organics, Yasho Industries) suffered margin compression as spot inflation could not be instantly passed to customers on locked purchase orders.

## Capex & capacity
- **Aggressive Commercialization & Ramping:**
  - **Aarti Industries:** Guided FY27 capex at Rs 700–800 crore; capitalization of Rs 1,800–1,900 crore CWIP at Zone IV by March 2027.
  - **Aether Industries:** Capitalizing ~Rs 400 crore of CWIP by year-end; executing FY27 capex of Rs 350–370 crore.
  - **Neogen Chemicals:** Doubling Organolithium capacity at Patancheru (300 to 600 MTPA for Rs 45 crore) and setting up Pakhajan salt facility by Feb/March 2027.
  - **Amtech Esters:** Tripled UPR resin capacity via the commercialization of its Asoda plant (4,800 MTPA added) in November 2026.
  - **Vinati Organics:** Announced Rs 500 crore internal-accrual-funded capex plan for new specialty chemicals with an additional Rs 500 crore under consideration.
  - **Chemplast Sanmar:** R-32 refrigerant gas plant debottlenecked to 20 kt, coming on stream by end of FY27.
  - **J.G.Chemicals:** Dahej Phase 1 (15,000 MTPA zinc oxide) trial runs set for December 2026.

## What changed since last quarter
- **Guidance Revisions & Upgrades:**
  - **Acutaas Chemicals:** Raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to ~30% (from 25% in Q1) and EBITDA margins to 38%–40% (from "stable"). Advanced Rs 1,000 crore CDMO target from FY28 to FY27.
  - **Camlin Fine Sciences:** Lowered full-year expectation; acknowledged it cannot meet the earlier FY27 target of Rs 200 crore EBITDA due to H1 headwinds, targeting Q4 turnaround.
  - **Gulshan Polyols:** Cautioned on Q3 margin compression due to maize prices, while introducing FY28 revenue guidance of Rs 2,800–3,000 crore.
- **Tone Shifts & Delivery on Promises:**
  - **Aarti Industries:** Delivered on record top line/EBITDA (>Rs 400 crore EBITDA) and initiated balance sheet deleveraging via shareholder approval for a Rs 1,000 crore capital raise.
  - **Fineotex Chemical:** Sustained strong growth post-Texas expansion, reiterated $200M US oilfield target by CY28, and launched water management subsidiaries.
  - **Ganesh Benzoplast:** Executed landmark strategic shift, divesting its liquid tank farm assets for Rs 1,154 crore to focus on chemicals and EPC.

## What we were watching
- ✅ **showed up** · **MP govt approves agro, chemical, textile parks (Nov 2026):** Highlighted as expanding sector manufacturing tailwinds and regional infrastructure support across domestic coverage.
- ✅ **showed up** · **Gujarat AAR 5% GST rate on biodegradable bags (Nov 2026):** Confirmed tax clarification providing demand clarity for eco-friendly polymer and packaging specialty applications.

## What to watch next quarter
- **Ramp-up of Greenfield/Brownfield Facilities:** Commercialization and order-book intake at Aarti's Zone IV Phase 1, Neogen's Pakhajan battery salt plant, J.G.Chemicals' Dahej facility, and Chemplast Sanmar's R-32 refrigerant gas plant.
- **Freight & Logistics Stabilization:** Watching whether ocean freight rates and container availability normalize across West Asian shipping lanes to relieve margin pressures on exporters (Platinum, Devson Catalyst, NOCIL, Yasho).
- **Deleveraging and Capital Raising Progress:** Completion and fund utilization of Camlin Fine Sciences' Rs 250 crore rights issue, Aarti's Rs 1,000 crore fund raise, and Ganesh Benzoplast's first Rs 500 crore divestment tranche inflow.
- **Raw Material Pass-Through Realizations:** Monitoring Q3 margin recovery in Vinati Organics (ATBS sales shift), Clean Science (HALS backward integration benefits), and Gulshan Polyols (feedstock shift to rice/damaged grains).
