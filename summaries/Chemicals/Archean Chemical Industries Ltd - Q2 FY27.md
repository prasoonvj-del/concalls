# Archean Chemical Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong export orders and revenue growth in bromine and derivatives, but offset by cost inflation, logistics challenges, and tepid domestic demand/power cuts.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 305.4 crore (up 32% YoY from Rs 231.8 crore)
- **Consolidated Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 301.6 crore (up 26% YoY from Rs 240 crore)
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 89.3 crore (up 17.5% YoY, up 6.4% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 74.5 crore (up 8% YoY, up 2.2% QoQ)
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 43.2 crore (up 12.2% YoY, up 6.7% QoQ)
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 31.3 crore (up 8% YoY, up 3% QoQ)
- **Industrial Salt Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 161 crore (up 9% YoY); Volumes down 9% to ~812,000 tonnes
- **Bromine Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 127 crore (up 76% YoY); Volumes up 29% to ~4,100 tonnes
- **Derivatives (Acume) Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 36 crore (up 81% YoY); Finished volumes up 56% YoY to 2,115 tonnes (EBITDA loss of ~Rs 1 crore)
- **SOP Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 2.18 crore; Volume at 444 tonnes
- **H1 FY27 Total Income (Standalone):** Rs 637.4 crore (up 21.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Total Income (Consolidated):** Rs 634.4 crore (up 17.4% YoY)
- **Industrial Salt Volume Guidance:** High confidence on 4.2 to 4.3 million tonnes, targeting 4.5 million tonnes.
- **Bromine Volume Guidance:** Expecting to hit the lower end of earlier guidance.
- **SOP Volume Guidance:** Downgraded to 3,500 - 4,000 tonnes for FY27.
- **Semiconductor Capex (SiCSem):** $240 million total; ~INR 463 crore invested YTD.

### Highlights
- Industrial salt shipments impacted by order deferrals from PVC/caustic soda players; new orders secured in Vietnam and Oman.
- Hajipir-to-port road construction completed; operational from early October, expected to mitigate 15%-30% distance/cost inflation.
- Commercialized 4 new derivatives products (including NPBr and IPBr); 8 new molecules under development.
- SiCSem semiconductor project in Odisha commenced civil works; received Environmental Clearance (EC) in mid-October.
- SOP pilot plant trials fully completed; plant-scale trials successful for KTMS to Schoenite conversion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident about the second half of the year and the full year (MD).
- Bromine full-year average yield expected to remain at a minimum of INR 300/kg (MD).
- SOP volume guidance for FY27 revised down to 3,500 - 4,000 tonnes due to feedstock limitations and trial delays (MD).
- Core capital spend (excluding flame retardant project) to remain within depreciation curve; debt-to-equity target at 0.6x to 0.7x over a 3-year window (MD).
- SiCSem project timeline is 24 months end-to-end (MD).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Chlor-alkali industry marked by price volatility for caustic soda and chlorine; spike in Chinese ethylene prices ($1,140-$1,200/MT) and weaker PVC demand pressured operating rates and salt lifting schedules.
- Agriculture sector demand tepid due to monsoons, impacting agrochemicals and brominated derivatives.
- Oil and gas sector resilient; steady inquiry levels for clear brine fluids despite Middle East geopolitical conditions.
- Semiconductor chemical specialty demand remains strong.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industrial HSD prices rose sharply from ~INR 87/liter in Q2 FY26 to ~INR 136/liter in Q2 FY27.
- Coal and propanol prices increased ~40% YoY; sulfur and SO2 prices more than doubled due to Middle East supply shortages; hydrated lime impacted by pet coke price rise.
- Logistics costs elevated due to seasonal shutdown of Jakhau Port and road diversions adding 15%-30% to trip distances (mitigated post-October via new road).
- Bromine margins supported by consumption efficiency improvements offsetting sulfur/lime inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bromine debottlenecking on schedule to reach 25,000 tonnes run-rate next year.
- Brine field expansion ongoing: 24,000 hectares total planned; Phase 1 of 4,000 hectares completing capitalization in CWIP (~INR 80 crore chemical CWIP, ~INR 40 crore SiCSem CWIP).
- SiCSem semiconductor project requires an estimated total investment of INR 1,000 - 1,100 crore beyond ISM incentives, largely to be funded via debt on Archean's balance sheet.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone chemical business ROCE improved from 15.5% in H1 last year to 16.5% in H1 this year (overall ROCE 12.2%).
- Total capital invested in Neun and SiCSem YTD is INR 463 crore.
- Land lease extension for the Hajipir site is under discussion with the Gujarat government; no operational impact currently.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Winter weather conditions could impact brine recovery and operational stability.
- Upward drift in fuel/WTI/Brent prices could erode logistics savings.
- Uncertainty in oil and gas order commitments affecting Oren Hydrocarbon.
- Domestic pricing pressure in bromine due to competitive intensity and lagging agro/pharma demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) vs Rampraveen Swaminathan (MD):** Asked if FY27 volume guidance for salt (4.5 million tonnes) and bromine can be met. *Management stated high confidence in 4.2-4.3 million tonnes for salt and reaching the lower end of bromine guidance, though PVC market unpredictability remains a variable.*
2. **Aditya Khetan (SMIFS Institutional Equities) vs Rampraveen Swaminathan (MD):** Inquired about Oren Hydrocarbon customer traction and revenue visibility. *Management stated plants are operating for starches/solvents, but oil & gas market uncertainty has limited offtake to campaign orders; expects aggressive ramp-up by Q1 next year.*
3. **Vinay Nadkarni (Hathway Investments) vs Rampraveen Swaminathan (MD):** Asked for SOP volume clarity. *Management admitted a downgrade to 3,500 - 4,000 tonnes for FY27 due to a 1-quarter trial delay and lower KTMS feedstock production.*
4. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.) vs Ramamurthy Natarajan (CFO) / MD:** Asked about CWIP breakup. *Management clarified that out of INR 120 crore CWIP, ~INR 80 crore relates to chemicals (brine field expansion and SOP modification) and ~INR 40 crore relates to the SiCSem semiconductor project.*
5. **Rishabh Jain (Bajaj Finance) vs Rampraveen Swaminathan (MD):** Queried about SiCSem capex funding and debt. *Management stated INR 1,000 - 1,100 crore will be front-loaded over the next 2 years and funded largely through debt.*

### One-line takeaway
Archean delivered steady revenue and EBITDA growth in Q2 FY27 despite severe cost inflation and logistics hurdles, with execution advancing on bromine debottlenecking, brine fields, and the SiCSem semiconductor project.
