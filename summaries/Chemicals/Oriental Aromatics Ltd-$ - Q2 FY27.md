# Oriental Aromatics Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, improving operating margins, and steady volume growth despite input cost challenges.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 318.19 crore (up ~17% YoY, 22% QoQ)
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** Rs. 578 crore (up 16.3% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 25.26 crore
- **EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 7.94% (up 32 bps QoQ, 158 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA margin (H1 FY27):** 7.80% (vs 7.11% in H1 FY26); standalone H1 EBITDA margin at 8.6%
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 5.70 crore (up ~130% QoQ)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs. 8.3 crore (vs Rs. 1.2 crore in H1 FY26)
- **PAT margin (Q2 FY27):** 1.82%
- **Cash profit (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 13.58 crore (vs Rs. 10.23 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Cash profit (H1 FY27):** Rs. 23.8 crore (vs Rs. 16.7 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Volume growth (Q2 FY27):** Production up 7% QoQ; Sales volume up 4% QoQ
- **Volume growth (H1 FY27):** Sales volume up 7% YoY; Production volume up 1% YoY
- **Ambernath plant (Q2 FY27):** Production up 14% QoQ; Sales volume up 20% QoQ
- **Baroda plant (Q2 FY27):** Production up 8% QoQ; Sales volumes up 12% QoQ and 8% YoY (H1 sales volume up 10% YoY)
- **Bareilly plant (Q2 FY27):** Production up 4% QoQ; Sales volumes down 5% QoQ and 8% YoY (H1 production up 8% YoY, sales volume up 11% YoY)
- **Mahad plant (Q2 FY27):** Production up 51% QoQ; Sales volume up 74% QoQ; capacity utilization at ~50% - 55%
- **Net debt-to-equity ratio (as on Sep 30, 2026):** 0.64 (vs 0.56 in previous quarter)

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs. 318.19 crore in Q2 FY27.
- Entered into a technical, strategic, and manufacturing arrangement with Unilever Industries Private Limited.
- Continued internal aroma ingredient programs to strengthen fragrance creation capabilities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a steady top-line CAGR growth of 10% to 12% (per Q&A).
- Management aims to reach a 10% EBITDA margin target going forward (per Q&A).
- Full-year guidance acknowledged by management (in response to an analyst) targeting Rs. 1,000 crore turnover and Rs. 50 crore net profit (per Q&A).
- Mahad plant expected to reach optimum volume utilization (75%-80% threshold / bridging gap) within the next two to three quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian FMCG industry demonstrated encouraging growth in Q2, particularly in home care, personal care, and beauty categories (double-digit volume growth in home care and beauty/well-being).
- Global specialty aroma ingredients market remains competitive with substantial manufacturing capacity across Asia, creating pricing pressure.
- Camphor and Terpene Chemicals division faces structural overcapacity and competitive pricing in domestic markets.
- Raw material prices for alpha-pinene (key for Camphor) and petrochemical-derived inputs remain elevated and volatile, impacting off-take in price-sensitive segments like pooja.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Elevated and volatile prices of terpene-based and petrochemical-derived raw materials (especially alpha-pinene).
- Competitive market conditions restrict full pass-through of raw material cost increases to customers.
- Margin improvement driven by better sales realization, product mix, and ongoing operational efficiency programs.
- Standalone H1 EBITDA margin (8.6%) reflects the drag of the Mahad subsidiary on consolidated EBITDA margins (7.8%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company has been in a consolidation phase over the past 1-1.5 years with no substantial new capex, focusing instead on asset utilization and R&D.
- Intends to announce further expansion plans (brownfield or greenfield) in coming quarters.
- Mahad facility operating at 50% - 55% capacity utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity ratio increased to 0.64 as of September 30, 2026, due to higher inventory levels, increased inventory purchase costs, and higher receivables from sales occurring at the end of the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated raw material costs and volatility in petrochemical feedstocks and natural raw materials.
- Structural overcapacity and competitive pricing in the domestic Camphor market.
- Geopolitical uncertainty and currency movements impacting procurement and manufacturing costs.
- Mahad facility currently operating below optimal utilization levels, acting as a drag on consolidated profitability.

### One-line takeaway
Oriental Aromatics delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs. 318.19 crore in Q2 FY27 with expanding EBITDA margins (7.94%), supported by strong FMCG demand and a strategic Unilever partnership, even as raw material inflation and structural Camphor overcapacity persisted.
