# India Glycols Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, supported by premiumization in spirits and margin recovery in chemicals.

### Key numbers
*   **Net Revenue:** Rs 1,130 crore (up 9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 170 crore (up 13% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Up 32% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.0% (vs 14.3% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Finance Costs:** Rs 25 crore (down from Rs 45 crore in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Restructuring:** NCLT approved the scheme of arrangement on July 17, 2026; the business will segregate into three entities: India Glycols Limited (Chemicals), IGL Spirits Limited (Spirits & Bio-Fuel), and Ennature Bio Pharma.
*   **Spirits:** Revenue Rs 361 crore (up 5.3% YoY), EBITDA growth 14.2%. IMFL volumes grew 55% YoY to 1.4 million cases.
*   **Chemicals:** Revenue Rs 362 crore (up 20.6% YoY).
*   **Bio-Fuel:** Revenue Rs 323 crore, EBIT Rs 27 crore (up 19% YoY), EBIT margin 8.4%.
*   **Ennature Bio Pharma:** Revenue up 65% YoY, EBITDA up 188% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Spirits:** Management aspires to deliver EBITDA >Rs 500 crore in FY27 and >Rs 1,000 crore in 4-5 years.
*   **Chemicals:** Management aspires to reach revenue of ~Rs 2,500 crore and EBITDA of ~Rs 400 crore in 4-5 years.
*   **Ennature Bio Pharma:** Management aspires to reach EBITDA of Rs 130-150 crore in 4-5 years.
*   **Debt:** Management targets becoming a debt-free company from FY28 onwards.
*   *Note: Management clarified these figures are "aspirations," not formal projections.*

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Crude Oil:** The spike in crude prices provided a tailwind for bio-based Ethylene Oxide (EO) price competitiveness but created headwinds regarding raw material availability (propylene oxide) and logistics.
*   **Logistics:** Freight costs for westbound cargo increased 5-20x, impacting chemical exports.
*   **Spirits:** Premiumization is driving growth; the company is focusing on the "Prestige & Above" segment, which saw volumes double YoY to 0.5 million cases.
*   **Ennature:** Nicotine sales grew 2x QoQ, driven by customer conversions in Europe.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Finance costs declined due to debt reduction.
*   Chemical margins were impacted by supply chain disruptions and prohibitively expensive raw materials (e.g., propylene oxide).
*   Management expects to improve chemical gross margins to ~30% in 5-6 years through product mix and innovation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Management expects low capex for the current year, estimated at Rs 5-20 crore.
*   **Capacity:** Nicotine crude processing operations expanded at the Kashipur plant.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Debt levels reduced during the quarter; debt-to-equity ratio improved.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical conflict impacting freight costs and supply chains for exports to the Middle East and US.
*   Volatility in raw material availability and pricing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Ragini Ramkumar (Negen Capital):** Asked for Spirits guidance. Management reiterated the >Rs 500 crore EBITDA target for FY27 and plans to double volumes.
*   **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor Company):** Asked about JV performance. Management noted JV revenue up 21% QoQ and EBITDA up in high double digits, attributing success to improved product mix and reduced EO price disadvantage.
*   **Vignesh Iyer (Sequent Investments):** Asked about Prestige & Above segment. Management confirmed 0.5 million cases, doubling YoY.
*   **Aman (Individual Investor):** Asked about EBITDA margin volatility. Management clarified the Q4 FY26 spike was due to one-time dividend income from Clariant.
*   **Pragyam Laddha (Omnee Management):** Asked about volume vs. revenue growth mismatch. Management attributed this to brand/state mix and expects revenue growth to exceed volume growth as premiumization takes hold.

### One-line takeaway
India Glycols delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with broad-based growth and margin expansion, while setting ambitious long-term EBITDA targets across its restructured business segments.
