# Camlin Fine Sciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Earnings and EBITDA were negatively impacted by liquidity issues, high input prices, channel financing costs, and sub-scale plant utilization, though management expects a turnaround driven by an upcoming rights issue.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Total specialty ingredient revenue was Rs 4,089 million (up from Rs 4,013 million last quarter); Aroma revenue reduced from Rs 774 million to Rs 472 million (entirely ethyl vanillin sales).
- **EBITDA Margin:** Consolidated EBITDA margin was 2.5% in Q2 (down from 3.4% in the previous quarter), while Specialty Ingredients EBITDA margin improved from 6.35% to 8.04%. 
- **Vanillin Volume & Realization:** Sold 334 metric tons of ethyl vanillin in Q2 (compared to ~580 metric tons of methyl and ethyl combined in the previous quarter). Net realization is about $12 per kg ($13.7 blended with ethyl and methyl).
- **Net Debt:** Net debt increased slightly from Rs 5,260 million to Rs 5,380 million, largely due to foreign exchange updation.
- **Interest Cost:** Interest cost without forex was Rs 163 million (vs Rs 169 million).
- **Rights Issue:** Board approved a rights issue of approximately Rs 250 crores (promoters backing their share at 48%).
- **Diphenol Maintenance Cash Burn:** Reduced to Rs 5 crores for the quarter.

### Highlights
- The Board approved a rights issue of around Rs 250 crores to stabilize working capital, unwind channel financing, clear overdue creditors, and drive future growth.
- Diphenol plant remains closed since May 2026 due to exorbitant input prices and basic raw material phenol costs more than doubling in six months, making local production economically untenable compared to cheaper imports from China.
- Aroma production was impacted due to the switch to ethyl vanillin one month before the quarter end and plant underutilization.
- Operating expenses were largely controlled despite freight costs almost doubling during the quarter due to war situations and other factors.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 to remain challenging with only a slight margin improvement, noting they cannot go below Q2 performance.
- Management expects a turnaround in Q4 FY27 with liquidity support, anticipating top line to cross Rs 600 crores and EBITDA margin at ~10%.
- Management does not expect to reach the earlier guidance of Rs 200 crores EBITDA at 10% for full year FY27 due to H1 headwinds.
- With the rights issue funds, management expects to add Rs 50 crores in quarterly revenue to blends and target 1,000 metric tons per quarter of vanillin sales at $12 realisation.

### Demand & industry commentary
- U.S. demand visibility for vanillin is 2,500 to 2,700 tons for the next calendar year; Europe visibility is 1,300 to 1,500 tons.
- Certain large U.S. distributors previously bought Chinese vanillin routed through Thailand and Malaysia, but have discontinued due to legal risks and Syensqo's anti-dumping duty actions, leading them to approach Camlin.
- Chinese material is flowing through Asia (Thailand, Malaysia) into the U.S. and Europe, keeping realizations slightly muted at $12-$12.5 net.
- In natural products, there is no real increase in pricing, and petro-downstream pass-through takes time.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin dropped by ~2% due to high prices, USD appreciation, and liquidity issues leading to expensive channel financing and long outstanding payables.
- Channel financing and long outstanding payables cost the company roughly 3% to 4% of overall costs (around Rs 190-200 million per quarter) in interest.
- Vanillin cost of production is ~$10 per kg at 70% capacity utilization. Estimated EBITDA per kg at 70% utilization is ~$2.
- Diphenol plant maintenance cash burn is targeted to be reduced to Rs 25 million per quarter by the end of Q4 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- The Rs 250 crores rights issue proceeds will be dedicated to augmenting working capital and stabilizing the working capital cycle; no project finance is involved.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 5,380 million (up from Rs 5,260 million due to forex).
- Working capital cycles are currently strained with high reliance on channel financing on both purchase and sales sides and long-outstanding accounts payable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inability to pass on raw material price increases to customers due to local competition and pricing pressures.
- Elevated input prices (phenol doubled in six months; TBHQ and BHA raw materials up 15-20%).
- Geopolitical tensions and crude volatility impacting freight and operational costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pratyush Kamal (InCred Equities) asked about the counterfactual impact if the rights issue had occurred a quarter earlier.** Management stated sales would have been higher by about Rs 100 crores and EBITDA better by about Rs 60 crores.
- **Rehan (Coheron Wealth) questioned the shift back to B2B distributor models for vanillin given past credit cycle and working capital stress.** Management explained that large actual users (F&F companies) demand 90-120 days credit, whereas distributors shorten the cycle to 30 days, preserving liquidity.
- **Rehan asked about vanillin tonnage and current costs.** Management clarified they sold ~334 metric tons of ethyl vanillin in Q2, hold 175-200 tons inventory, and production cost is ~$10 at 70% utilization.
- **Niraj (White Pine Investment Management) inquired about the mismatch between Aroma segment's -23% EBITDA margin and the stated $2 per ton EBITDA.** Management explained that $2 applies at 70% capacity utilization, whereas low utilization in Q2 caused heavy unabsorbed fixed overheads (fixed costs are ~Rs 20 crores/quarter).
- **Surya Narayan Patra (Phillip Capital) asked about the future of the diphenol plant given massive Chinese capacity additions.** Management stated there is no visibility to restart the plant as Chinese prices are way below production costs.
- **Meet Gada (Sanghavi Family Office) asked if further capital will be required beyond the Rs 250 crores rights issue.** Management stated the Rs 250 crores will resolve current mismatches and fund growth, requiring no additional borrowings if plans proceed without lag.

### One-line takeaway
Camlin Fine Sciences reported depressed Q2 margins and cash flows due to liquidity constraints, high raw material prices, and sub-scale plant operations, but expects a structural margin recovery and top-line growth to 10% EBITDA in Q4 backed by an upcoming Rs 250 crore rights issue.
