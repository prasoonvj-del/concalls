# J.G.Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered best-ever quarterly performance with record revenue, EBITDA, and PAT driven by healthy demand and favorable product mix.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 361.01 crore (up 64% YoY, 14% QoQ); H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 676.66 crore (up 54% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 38.65 crore (up 77% YoY, 7% QoQ); EBITDA margin: 10.71%; H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 74.9 crore (up 66% YoY), margin: 11.07%.
- **PAT:** Rs 27.95 crore (up 86% YoY, 7% QoQ); PAT margin: 7.74%; H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 54.07 crore (up 72% YoY), margin: 7.99%.
- **Capacity Utilization:** Exceeding 80% of achievable capacity.
- **Net Cash Surplus:** Rs 63.3 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Non-rubber revenue contribution:** Over 18% of sales in H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly performance surpassing Q1, driven by healthy demand, favorable product mix, and focused execution.
- Launched *LabPure* (analytical grade zinc oxide) on e-commerce platforms and introduced *JDZRA* for non-tire rubber applications; applied for a rubber curing activator patent.
- Commissioned Phase 1 of the Naidupeta solar project (supplying 25-30% of energy requirements with a 4-year payback).
- Incorporated BDJ Materials and Metals Trading FZCO in Dubai as a step-down wholly owned subsidiary to diversify sourcing and boost global sales.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident in surpassing previous numbers in Q3 FY27, noting October production was the highest ever.
- Management targets a consolidated EBITDA margin of 14% to 15% in 3 years.
- Internal target is to double revenues every 3 years (targeting well over Rs 1,200 crore in FY27 from ~Rs 600 crore in FY24).
- Post completion of expansions, installed capacity will exceed 115,000 tonnes of zinc chemicals, placing JGC among the top 3 globally.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Auto industry maintained strong momentum in Q2 FY27: PV volumes grew ~30% YoY, 2-wheelers ~15%, commercial vehicles ~29%, and 3-wheelers ~23%.
- Indian tire majors are progressing with ~Rs 25,000 crore in announced capex despite West Asia disruptions.
- Industry is undergoing a gradual shift from fragmented regional suppliers to organized players capable of consistent quality and scale; JGC is well-positioned as India's largest player (77,000 MTPA capacity).
- Non-rubber applications (pharma, chemicals, agriculture, ceramics) account for over 18% of H1 sales; ceramics approval cycles are short (1-3 months).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin experienced a lead-lag inventory effect and cost pressures, with management focusing on EBITDA/per-tonne margins rather than percentage gross margins.
- Working capital intensive business with ~90 to 100 working capital days.
- Raw material sourcing network of over 100 global suppliers maintained uninterrupted supply despite West Asia and Ukraine conflicts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Dahej Greenfield (Gujarat):** Phase 1 (~15,000 MTPA of zinc oxide within a 40,000+ MTPA facility) is nearing completion with trial runs scheduled for next month (December 2026); Phase 2 planned to start around mid-to-late 2028 calendar year. Peak revenue potential is higher than the previously guided Rs 900 crore, expected to be achieved by FY29-FY30.
- **Naidupeta Brownfield (Andhra Pradesh):** Adds ~5,000 MTPA capacity; commissioning pushed slightly to mid-to-end January (Q4 FY27).
- Acquired ~16.74 acres of freehold land at Naidupeta via subsidiary BDJ Oxides Pvt Ltd for long-term expansion.
- Total capacity post-expansions expected to reach ~79,900 tonnes per annum (and over 115,000 tonnes of zinc chemicals ultimately).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash surplus stood at Rs 63.3 crore as of September 30, 2026; expected to exceed Rs 100 crore by the end of FY27.
- Inventory increased from March 2026 levels due to post-West Asia crisis normalization (March inventory was artificially low due to transit delays). Management stated no additional borrowings are needed even if revenue doubles.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia crisis and geopolitical conflicts caused global supply chain and transit lead-time disruptions.
- Higher cost material sourced domestically in rising LME environments hit plants ahead of delayed lower-cost imports.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shreyans Jain (3A Capital Services) asked about volume growth and sequential gross margin drop:** Management stated volume growth was in the mid-teens, and gross margin fluctuations are due to inventory lead-lag effects. They reiterated a target of 14%-15% EBITDA margins over 3 years.
2. **Dhruvin Kadakia (Monarch Networth Capital) inquired about employee cost leverage and Naidupeta timeline:** Management explained that employee costs scaled efficiently because teams were built ahead of revenue growth, and Naidupeta commissioning is targeted for mid-January (Q4).
3. **Akshit Shetty (Family Office) asked about working capital and inventory buildup:** Management clarified that March 2026 inventory was low due to transit disruptions from the West Asia crisis; current inventory levels provide supply security, and operations are comfortably funded without debt.
4. **Param Shah (Carnelian Capital) asked about Dahej revenue potential:** Management stated the Rs 900 crore peak revenue potential from Dahej will likely be achieved by FY29-FY30, with actual potential even higher.
5. **Virag Shah (Elios Financial Services) asked about sensitivity to falling zinc prices:** Management clarified they are LME-agnostic because pricing is passed through on a monthly average basis, insulating margins from price crashes while volume growth continues.

### One-line takeaway
J.G. Chemicals reported its best-ever quarterly performance driven by robust volume growth and auto demand, while advancing major greenfield (Dahej) and brownfield (Naidupeta) expansions to capture industry consolidation and drive non-rubber diversification.
