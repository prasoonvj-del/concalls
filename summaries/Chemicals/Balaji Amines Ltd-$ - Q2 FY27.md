# Balaji Amines Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Meaningful improvement in revenue, EBITDA, and profitability driven by product mix, operating efficiencies, and cost management despite volatile raw material conditions and challenging sales volumes.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 472 crore (up ~36% YoY from Rs 348 crore; up sequentially from Rs 461 crore in Q1 FY27).
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 106 crore (up ~58% YoY from Rs 67 crore; down sequentially from Rs 121 crore in Q1 FY27).
- EBITDA Margin: 22% (compared to 19% in Q2 FY26 and 26% in Q1 FY27).
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 67 crore (up ~82% YoY from Rs 37 crore).
- Diluted EPS: Rs 20.76 per equity share (compared to Rs 10.67 in Q2 FY26).
- Consolidated Sales Volumes: 24,491 metric tonnes (down ~6% YoY from 26,165 metric tonnes; up ~13.5% QoQ from 21,587 metric tonnes).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue: Rs 933 crore (up ~31% YoY from Rs 715 crore).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 227 crore (up ~74% YoY from Rs 131 crore, margin at 24% vs 18%).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT: Rs 146 crore (up ~98% YoY from Rs 74 crore).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Sales Volumes: 46,078 metric tonnes (vs 53,735 metric tonnes in H1 FY26).
- Standalone Q2 FY27: Revenue Rs 434 crore, EBITDA Rs 103 crore, PAT Rs 69 crore. Zero debt position at standalone level.
- Consolidated Net Worth: Rs 2,083.02 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Consolidated Debt: Rs 161.15 crore; Cash and cash equivalents: Rs 52.62 crore.
- H1 FY27 Cash flow from operating activities: Rs 54.46 crore; Capex/expansion cash outflow: Rs 99.38 crore.
- DMF basic prices: more than Rs 110-115; DMF capacity utilization: >50% (Unit-4 at 15-20%).
- Acetonitrile expanded capacity: 1,440 tonnes per month (current prices around Rs 230-240 level).
- DME pricing: Rs 90 to Rs 100 per kg.

### Highlights
- Commenced commercial production at the expanded Acetonitrile facility and commissioned the DME plant.
- Received PESO Nagpur office approval for Dimethyl Ether (DME) road tanker valves after testing.
- Balaji Specialty Chemicals Limited (BSCL) continuing expansion: Unit-1 brownfield expansion expected to be commissioned towards the end of FY27; Unit-2 greenfield expansion targeted for commissioning in Q4 FY27.
- Improved manufacturing process for Di Isopropylamine, reinforcing process optimization.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects top line to reach between Rs 3,500 crore to Rs 4,000 crore by FY28-29.
- Management expects all plants/expansions to complete commercial production by the end of the current financial year (FY27), shifting focus to capacity utilization (80%-90%) starting from Q1 of next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- End-user industries include pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, paints, resins, solvents, rubber chemicals, and other industrial applications.
- Global raw material prices are volatile due to geopolitical situations (wars, sanctions), leading to higher spot prices.
- Antidumping duty on EDA was recommended by the Ministry of Commerce, but not implemented by the Finance Ministry; management is preparing a writ petition in the High Court.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (such as methanol and ammonia) are volatile and elevated due to geopolitical conflicts.
- Methanol prices currently at Rs 61-62 (normal historical range seen around Rs 26-27, max Rs 30).
- Ammonia prices currently at Rs 85-90 (normal international range seen around Rs 45-50).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acetonitrile expanded capacity increased to 1,440 tonnes per month.
- BSCL Unit-1 brownfield expansion (EDA-based derivatives: DETA, TETA, Piperazine, AEEA, AEP) expected by end of FY27.
- BSCL Unit-2 greenfield expansion (hydrogen cyanide, sodium cyanide, EDTA, EDTA-2Na) at MIDC Chincholi targeted for Q4 FY27 commissioning.
- N-Methyl Morpholine (NMM) project progressing for commercialization during FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone level maintains a zero debt position.
- Consolidated debt stands at Rs 161.15 crore; cash and cash equivalents at Rs 52.62 crore.
- Consolidated net worth at Rs 2,083.02 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile raw material prices and geopolitical situations impacting predictability.
- Intermediary behaviour leading to sudden price spikes despite lower cost inventories.
- Bureaucratic mismatch between the Ministry of Commerce recommending antidumping duties and the Finance Ministry not implementing them, forcing legal recourse.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Akshay Stija (Alpha Invesco) asked about DMF plant utilization and pricing.** Management replied that DMF prices improved to over Rs 110-115, and capacity utilization increased to more than 50% (Unit-4 at 15-20%).
2. **Akshay Satija asked about DMC progress with the battery electrolyte client.** Management stated the client's order size increased from 20-60 tonnes to 500-1,000 tonnes, deliverable starting next month.
3. **Aditya Iyer (HDFC Securities) asked about DME approvals for LPG blending.** Management stated valve approvals from PESO are complete, road tanker approvals are expected in 3-4 weeks, and bulk supplies for LPG blending await BPCL's government approval, while ISO tank exports for aerosol are active.
4. **Kumar Saurabh (Scientific Investing) asked about the Rs 3,000-4,000 crore medium-term guidance assumption.** Management confirmed the target stands for FY28-29 based on upcoming full-capacity operations of all new expansions, even if current inflated raw material prices decline.
5. **Rajiv Rupani (Private Investor) questioned the repeated delays in BSCL Unit-1 brownfield completion dates.** Management explained they are running the plant for 15 days and doing expansion/modifications for 15 days to capture market demand instead of completely shutting down the revenue-generating plant.

### One-line takeaway
Balaji Amines delivered strong YoY growth in H1 FY27 profitability despite lower aggregate sales volumes, while advancing major capacity expansions in Acetonitrile, DME, and BSCL subsidiaries toward commercialization by the end of FY27.
