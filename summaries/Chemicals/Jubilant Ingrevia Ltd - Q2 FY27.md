# Jubilant Ingrevia Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue and EBITDA growth, disciplined cost execution, and strategic initiatives.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,394 crore, up 24% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs 221 crore, up 51% YoY
- PAT: Rs 111 crore, up 60% YoY
- H1 Revenue growth: 25% YoY
- H1 EBITDA growth: 44% YoY
- Specialty Chemicals revenue: Rs 560 crore, up 16% YoY and 5% QoQ (EBITDA: Rs 140 crore, Margin: 25%)
- Nutrition and Health Solutions revenue: Rs 270 crore, up 50% YoY and 11% QoQ (EBITDA: Rs 39 crore, up 87% YoY, Margin: 15%)
- Chemical Intermediates revenue: Rs 563 crore, up 24% YoY and 8% QoQ (EBITDA: Rs 64 crore, up 26% YoY)
- Zettaone Technologies acquisition stake: 40% stake for an investment of Rs 189 crore
- H1 Capex: INR 120 odd crore

### Highlights
- Quarterly EBITDA has more than doubled over the past 3 years.
- Acquired a 40% stake in Zettaone Technologies Limited for Rs 189 crore, entering the electronics design management system (EDMS) space.
- Consumer/personal care segment reached 7% of the overall portfolio, driven by vitamin B3 personal grade and other products.
- New multipurpose plant in Gajraula remains on track for commissioning by the year-end.
- Secured 25+ confirmed molecules in the CDMO pipeline with a peak potential revenue of over Rs 1,500 crore (excluding the large contract). Total potential with incremental opportunities is close to Rs 2,000 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is sticking to its annual guidance and expects to be closer to the upper end of the guidance (INR 750 crore - 800 crore [implied EBITDA/revenue context as stated by analyst/management]).
- Management anticipates higher volumes in Acetyls supported by planned closures in Europe.
- Zettaone expected to grow by at least 60% to 70% this year.
- Multi-year average capex guidance maintained at Rs 400 crore to Rs 500 crore annually.
- Specialty Chemicals portfolio aspiration is to grow at 20% to 25% YoY on a multi-year CAGR basis.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Acetyls: Volumes remain stable while pharma crude prices supported improved realization; input/utility costs partially offset pricing benefits.
- Pharmaceuticals: Growing, supported by steady volumes and sustained demand for intermediates.
- Agro-chemicals: Under pressure with stable volumes but muted pricing due to excess capacity in China.
- Personal care: Strong volume growth with increasing traction from new outsourcing customers.
- Nutrition: Maintained steady volumes despite softening in vitamin B3 pricing in the animal feed segment.
- Semiconductors & electronics: Strong growth in new molecule pipeline with customer traction, particularly in Japan. Zettaone market growing at 35%-40% annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Elevated crude prices led to higher input costs in Chemical Intermediates, managed via improved realizations.
- Vitamin B3 pricing in animal feed softened over past few weeks; potential lingering impact into Q3.
- Base pyridine and vitamin B3 facing normal seasonal pricing pressures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gajraula multipurpose plant nearing completion for year-end commissioning.
- Assets built in the last 3–3.5 years expected to reach average 70%+ utilization levels by the end of the fiscal year.
- Zettaone is establishing a new manufacturing facility near Bangalore.
- Greater Noida R&D and cleanroom facility supporting semiconductor expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail aside from capex outlays.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Excess capacity in China keeping agro-chemicals under pressure.
- Softening of vitamin B3 pricing in animal feed.
- Geopolitical uncertainties.

### One-line takeaway
Jubilant Ingrevia delivered strong Q2 FY27 results with 24% revenue and 51% EBITDA growth YoY, backed by robust execution in Specialty Chemicals and Nutrition, and expanded into the electronics ecosystem via a 40% stake in Zettaone.
