# Excel Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust financial performance driven by diversification, cost discipline, and raw material procurement, offsetting a cyclical downturn in agrochemicals.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 274.6 crores, up 1.6% YoY (from INR 270.2 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR 568.3 crores, down 2% YoY (from INR 579.7 crores in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 36.6 crores, up 21.5% YoY (from INR 30.1 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 79 crores.
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 13.3%, up from 11.1% in Q2 FY26.
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 13.9%, up from 12.5% in H1 FY26.
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 26.7 crores, up 42.8% YoY (from INR 18.7 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 55.8 crores (margin of 9.8%).
- **Exports (Q2 FY27):** INR 81 crores, up from INR 47 crores YoY.
- **Agro vs Non-Agro Mix (H1 FY27):** Agro at 35%, Non-agro at 65%.
- **Standalone Inventory (Sept 2026):** INR 262 crores compared to INR 129 crores in March.

### Highlights
- Dedicated capacity for the 5-year long-term supply arrangement (announced November 2025) successfully completed operations on 23rd July 2026.
- Awarded silver rating in EcoVadis Sustainability Assessment (93rd percentile globally).
- Received 2 trophies at Maharashtra Safety Awards 2025 for Lote site; Safe-Tech Award 2026 for Roha site.
- *Note on Record:* Planned ₹200–300 crore capex over 3 years is reaffirmed by management during Q&A for focus areas.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the agrochemical market to remain subdued in the near term, with demand weakness persisting in Q3 FY27.
- Raw material price volatility, supply availability, and shipping disruptions may continue to create cost pressure during H2.
- Guidance status: maintained structural focus on diversified growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Agrochemical industry impacted by monsoon at approximately 87% of normal levels, creating weak demand in India and LATAM (primary geographies for DETC).
- DETC quarterly sales reached the lowest level in the last 7/11 years.
- Non-agro businesses (Performance Solutions, Yellow Phosphorus (YP) derivatives, Contract Manufacturing) offset the agrochemical downturn.
- Chinese shipping difficulties restrict competition primarily to domestic players, though full-fledged recovery in agro intermediates will take longer due to large capacity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion supported by improved product mix, better realizations, cost discipline, and proactive raw material procurement.
- Prudent inventory build-up of critical raw materials (inventory rose to INR 262 crores in September from INR 129 crores in March) helped control raw material costs and improve gross margins.
- Raw material price volatility and pricing pressures expected to continue into Q3 and Q4.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned ₹200–300 crore capex over 3 years targeting YP derivatives, Performance Solutions, and Contract Manufacturing (approximately 50% / ~INR 150 crores committed).
- Multi-purpose plant (MPP) coming up at one of the sites to support growth opportunities.
- Capital deployment benchmarks: 1.5x to 2x fixed asset turnover ratio and a blended ROCE target of 15% to 20% (closer to 20%).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains exceptionally strong with a healthy net cash position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Suboptimal monsoon (87% of normal) and El Nino adversely impacting agrochemical demand.
- Raw material price volatility, supply availability concerns, and logistics/shipping disruptions creating H2 cost pressures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Siddhesh (PL Capital) on DETC market share and sales drop:** Management stated they maintained market share in agrochemicals/DETC despite sales hitting multi-year lows due to El Nino and poor weather in India and LATAM.
- **Samar Singh on margin sustainability vs. low-cost inventory:** Management clarified that improvements stem from a structural shift toward high-margin focus areas (Contract Manufacturing, Performance Solutions) combined with proactive procurement.
- **Varun Mishra on gross vs EBITDA margin divergence:** Management explained that low-cost inventory is getting replaced by higher-cost inventory, alongside adverse product mix changes from lower agro intermediates sales.
- **Prashant (Retail Investor) on monetizing Silox India investment:** Management noted that both the board and management are aware of the holding and review it periodically, and will communicate board decisions at the appropriate time.

### One-line takeaway
Excel Industries delivered robust Q2 profitability growth despite a cyclical agrochemical downturn, driven by structural diversification into performance solutions and contract manufacturing.
