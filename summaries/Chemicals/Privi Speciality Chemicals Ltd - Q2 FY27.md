# Privi Speciality Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly sales and profitability achieved amidst adverse global headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Total income / revenue: INR773.16 crore (up 13.9% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR212.49 crore (up 16.66% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 27.48% (vs 26.12% in FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR117.03 crore (up 24.61% YoY); H1 PAT INR201 crore (up ~29% YoY)
- Raw material cost (RMC) as % of sales: 52% (vs 54% in Q1, 51% in September '25)
- Net debt-to-EBITDA: 1.07x; Net debt-to-equity: 0.51x
- Net debt (absolute): INR814.51 crore as of September 30, 2026
- ROCE: 24.30% (up 216 bps YoY); ROE: 26.34% (up 134 bps YoY)
- Working capital cycle: Less than 100 days (down from 124 days YoY)
- Cash generated from operations (H1): INR445 crore
- Installed capacity expansion: Phase 1 completed, adding 6,000 MTPA to take total capacity from 48,000 MTPA to 54,000 MTPA.
- PRIGIV JV performance (Q2): Sales INR28 crore, positive PBT close to INR2 crore. H1 sales INR47 crore, negligible loss of INR0.02 lakh.

### Highlights
- Declared the company’s first-ever interim dividend of INR5 per share (face value INR10) for FY26–FY27.
- State incentives received stood at INR1.62 crore in H1 (vs INR9 crore in the previous year); balance INR12 crore to INR15 crore expected in the next 2 quarters.
- Asset turnover is at 1.7x on the fixed asset base.
- PRIGIV JV achieved a profitable quarter in Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains an overall annual revenue growth guidance of 20% for FY27.
- Management expects to approach INR3,750 crore to INR4,000 crore revenue and maintain EBITDA margins near current levels.
- Confident of achieving the "5K:1K plus" vision (INR5,000 crore revenue) by 2030, driven by Phase 2 and Phase 3 capex.
- Phase 2 capex (maltol, ethyl maltol, furfural, cyclopentanone, etc.) expected to be completed between June and September 2027, with revenue contribution starting from October 2027 and full capacity potential of ~INR1,000 crore.
- Phase 3 capex planned at INR400 crore to INR425 crore, giving an additional revenue potential of INR450 crore to INR500 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broad-based demand across domestic and export markets; African market growing well.
- Customers continue to place strong emphasis on dependable supply, consistent quality, and diversified sourcing.
- European/U.S. labor and energy crises create opportunities for compliant, quality-conscious Indian manufacturers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Expenses under control at 9.72% of sales due to fixed cost amortization over a larger revenue base.
- 2/3rd of revenue (~70% of raw materials) comes from non-petroleum sources, insulating the company from West Asian war/feedstock volatility.
- Freight costs managed via quarterly pricing mechanisms with customers; no major supply chain disruptions noted.
- Other income increase primarily driven by favorable foreign exchange gains due to high exports.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phase 1 debottlenecking completed (54,000 MTPA); volume impact to reflect significantly in Q4 as annual contracts commence.
- Total remaining capex for Phase 2 and Phase 3 estimated at ~INR1,000 crore. Combined revenue potential of all 3 phases estimated at INR1,700 crore to INR1,800 crore.
- Privi Fine Sciences (6,000 MTPA capacity, potential revenue INR300-350 crore) awaiting final Pollution Control Board and regulatory approvals, expected before the end of the financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term annual working capital cycle expected to stabilize around 105 to 110 days.
- Receivables declined due to timely collections from customers.
- Deleveraging process for PRIGIV JV debt into an advance is currently in process with multinational partners.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory approval delays affecting the scaling and capacity utilization of Privi Fine Sciences / Privi Biotechnology assets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Krish Salot (Veer Growth AIF) asked about the capacity utilization and ramp-up timeline for the Phase 1 debottlenecking.** Management answered that expanded capacity utilization will hit 85% to 90% in Q4 when new annual contracts kick in. Management did not dodge.
- **Ram Arvind (iThoughtPMS) questioned how management plans to achieve 20% annual growth given H1 grew at 15%.** Management reiterated confidence in 20% growth, noting that H2 is historically stronger than H1 due to annual calendar-year contracts and new products. Management did not dodge.
- **Sudhir Bheda (Bheda Family Office) asked about the balance outstanding state incentives.** Management quantified it at INR12 crore to INR15 crore expected over the next 2 quarters. Management did not dodge.
- **Krish Salot asked about the losses and utilization of Privi Fine Sciences.** Management explained that full capacity cannot be realized until pending regulatory/Pollution Control Board permissions are received, which are expected by the end of the fiscal year. Management did not dodge.
- **Anirudh Sharma asked for the total remaining capex and revenue potential of Phases 2 and 3.** Management stated remaining capex is ~INR1,000 crore, targeting INR1,900 crore total incremental revenue across all three phases by 2030. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
Privi Speciality Chemicals reported its highest-ever quarterly revenue and profitability in Q2 FY27, maintaining a 20% annual growth guidance backed by expanding capacities and strong operational efficiencies.
