# Epigral Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Challenging operating environment with volatile raw material prices and squeezed margins, though volume demand and realizations showed recovery.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 752 crore, up 28% YoY (compared to Rs 589 crore in Q2 FY26).
- EBITDA: Rs 136 crore, up 3% YoY (compared to Rs 132 crore in Q2 FY26).
- EBITDA Margin: 18% in Q2 FY27 (compared to 18% in Q2 FY26, impacted by high raw material costs).
- PAT: Rs 64 crore, up 26% YoY (compared to Rs 51 crore in Q2 FY26).
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 1,461 crore, up 21% YoY.
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 315 crore (Margin: 22%).
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 164 crore (Margin: 11%).
- ROCE: 15% as of 30 September 2026 (18% if excluding Capital Work-in-Progress).
- Net Debt: Rs 514 crore as of 30 September 2026 (vs Rs 496 crore as of 30 September 2025).
- Net Debt to EBITDA: 0.9 as of 30 September 2026 (vs 0.8 as of 30 September 2025).
- Capex spent in H1 FY27: Rs 88 crore.
- Derivatives and specialty business revenue contribution: 57% in Q2 FY27.
- Chlorotoluene expected revenue generation for the year: close to Rs 100 crore (potential peak utilization revenue of Rs 300 crore to Rs 350 crore).
- Capacity utilizations (Q2 FY27): ECH at 75% to 80%, Hydrogen Peroxide at 95%, Caustic Soda at 75%, CPVC at 60% to 65%.
- Maintenance capex: 1.5% of gross block annually.

### Highlights
- Revenue growth driven by a 6% increase in sales volume value and improved realizations for majority products.
- CPVC and Epichlorohydrin (ECH) capacity expansions are in final stages with trial runs underway; commissioning expected within a quarter.
- Pilot plants for epoxy resin and multipurpose plant (MPP) are ready; customer approval process to start soon to minimize qualification timelines.
- Hydrogen peroxide for solar applications targeted for commercialization from Q4 onwards.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a positive outlook for H2 FY27, noting that H2 historically outperforms H1.
- Reaffirmed long-term targets: reaching gross block of Rs 4,100 crore to Rs 4,200 crore to fetch Rs 5,000 crore to Rs 5,100 crore revenue by FY31.
- Epoxy resin plant targeted for commissioning by March 2028, driving revenue from FY29 onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Chemical industry faced a complex, volatile, and challenging operating environment with headwinds expected to persist.
- India's demand for CPVC is growing at a CAGR of 13% annually.
- Chlorine prices have improved, moving out of the negative zone due to domestic demand growth and new downstream plants.
- Caustic soda global prices expected to increase in Q3 and Q4 due to high energy costs in Europe during winter.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins under pressure due to a time lag in passing through volatile feedstock prices and normalization of previous inventory lead days.
- External pressures include the West Asia crisis, extended lead times, increasing global freight rates, and rising domestic transportation costs.
- Coal prices increased by 60% to 70% over 3 to 4 months, impacting coal-based chlor-alkali plants. Energy cost increases are being passed on via chlorine pricing.
- Company plans to reduce energy costs over the next 2 to 3 years by sourcing 30% to 35% of total demand from solar and wind hybrid projects (investing 26% equity in the SPV).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gross block currently stands at around Rs 3,000 crore, expected to rise by Rs 500-600 crore upon commissioning CPVC and ECH plants.
- Additional Rs 600 crore capex allocated for MPP and epoxy resin projects.
- Post-expansion, approximately 50% of ECH production is expected to be consumed captively for epoxy resin, and 50% sold in the market.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt to EBITDA is at a comfortable level of 0.9.
- Sizable capital work-in-progress is yet to contribute to P&L, impacting reported ROCE.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile feedstock prices and West Asia crisis creating raw material price pressures.
- Lag in passing on price increases and normalization of inventory lead days impacting margins.
- Rising global freight rates and domestic transportation costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nirav Jimudia (Anvil Wealth) on Chlor-alkali & Chlorine:** Asked if caustic soda prices increased commensurately with coal prices, and drivers of chlorine price recovery. *Management answered that caustic prices haven't fully moved globally, so cost recovery was shifted to chlorine, ending negative chlorine pricing norms.*
2. **Nirav Jimudia (Anvil Wealth) on ECU Realizations:** Asked for Q2 ECU and chlorine realizations. *Management stated Q2 ECU realization was around Rs 31,000-Rs 32,000, and chlorine was around zero.*
3. **Abhinav Mandowara (Aequitas Investments) on CPVC:** Asked about CPVC spreads, realizations, and contract nature. *Management stated realizations are around Rs 120-Rs 125/kg moving with raw material prices, operating at 60-65% utilization, with quarterly/monthly contracts depending on customer size.*
4. **Nirav Jimudia (Anvil Wealth) on Sequential EBITDA Drop:** Asked about product-wise contributions to the sequential drop in operating profit. *Management attributed the margin pressure primarily to the chlor-alkali segment due to higher coal and energy prices.*
5. **Gagan Dixit (Emkay Global) on FY31 Guidance & ECH Utilization:** Asked about revenue split for FY31 and ECH export/captive split. *Management stated that post-expansion, ~50% of ECH will be consumed captively for epoxy (commissioning target March 2028) and 50% sold externally, supporting the Rs 5,000+ crore FY31 revenue goal.*

### One-line takeaway
Epigral reported 28% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by volumes and realizations, but EBITDA margins were constrained by volatile raw material costs and energy inflation, even as key derivatives expansions near completion.
