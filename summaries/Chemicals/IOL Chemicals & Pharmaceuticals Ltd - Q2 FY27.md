# IOL Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy growth across businesses, improved profitability, and strong demand momentum.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 795 crore (~40% YoY)
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,551 crore (~39% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 115 crore (~80% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 14.4% (+321 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 227 crore (~70% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 14.5% (vs 11.8% in H1 FY26)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 65 crore (~117% YoY)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 130 crore (more than doubled YoY)
- **Exports:** ~28% of H1 FY27 revenue
- **Capacity Utilization:** 90% to 95%+ for major products
- **Capex Guidance:** Rs 250 crore for FY27 (~half spent in H1)
- **Short-term Working Capital Borrowings:** Rs 200 crore as of 30th September 2026

### Highlights
- Non-ibuprofen API portfolio grew ~70% YoY, contributing 45% of pharmaceutical revenue (vs 38% in Q2 FY26).
- Chemicals business EBIT contribution increased to ~7% in Q2 (vs ~1% in Q2 FY26).
- Received CEP approval from EDQM for Fenofibrate-Process II (taking total CEP approvals to 22).
- Received GMP certification for formulation CDMO facility and secured EU-REACH registrations for triacetin.
- Announced three manufacturing projects totaling ~Rs 500 crore funded through internal accruals: Ibuprofen expansion (12,000 MTPA to 18,000 MTPA), a formulation CDMO facility (1,500 million tablets), and a dedicated specialty chemicals facility under a long-term tolling arrangement.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth of 25%+ with an EBITDA margin of 14% to 15%.
- Sustainable long-term blended EBITDA margin targeted at 15% to 17%.
- CDMO and specialty chemical projects are expected to commence commercial operations in FY27 (with real contributions ramping up later), while the Ibuprofen expansion is targeted for commissioning in FY28 (impact mainly in FY28-29).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global ibuprofen prices have been stable over past quarters; management expects them to remain near current levels for the next 2-3 quarters.
- API order book averages 4 to 6 months for major customers.
- CDMO target is primarily the European market.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Finance costs increased due to higher working capital utilization supporting increased business operations and volumes.
- Other expenses increased due to higher power and fuel costs (seasonal summer spike) and logistics/freight costs.
- Raw material imports account for ~62% to 63% overall (~60-70% for chemicals like acetic acid, ~20-22% for API).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total FY27 capex planned at Rs 250 crore (half spent in H1).
- Ibuprofen capacity to increase from 12,000 MTPA to 18,000 MTPA via backward integrated facility (commissioning FY28).
- CDMO facility asset turnover projected around 1:1 initially, generating ~Rs 125 crore revenue on a ~Rs 110 crore investment. Blended company asset turnover expected around 1:1.75.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital limits are optimized; management stated no additional long-term borrowings are planned as operating cash flows and existing cash/limits are sufficient. Peak short-term borrowings at Rs 200 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in raw material prices (e.g., acetic acid) and shipping/freight costs.
- Geopolitical uncertainties and global supply chain disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bhavika Singhvi (Niveshaay) on Ibuprofen realization trends and sustainability:** Management stated prices have been stable for the last few quarters and expect them to remain flat for the next 2-3 quarters; the expansion is a long-term strategic move, not a short-term reaction.
2. **Ahmed Madha on breakdown of incremental Ibuprofen capacity:** Management clarified that roughly 50% will go to new API merchant customers and 50% will feed internal CMO/formulation supply.
3. **Vignesh Iyer on working capital and borrowings:** Management confirmed that the Rs 200 crore short-term working capital borrowing as of 30th Sept 2026 represents the peak requirement for the year and no further borrowings are needed.
4. **Yogesh Patil on CDMO financial metrics:** Management stated they expect a 1:1 asset turnover for the CDMO business (generating ~Rs 125 crore revenue on Rs 110 crore capex) with better margins than traditional API/chemicals.
5. **Varun Sanghavi on specialty chemicals tolling pricing model:** Management disclosed that the dedicated specialty chemicals tolling arrangement operates on a **cost-plus model**.

### One-line takeaway
IOL reported a strong H1 FY27 with revenue up ~39% YoY and PAT more than doubling, driven by high capacity utilization, a growing non-ibuprofen API mix, and improved chemical realization, backed by Rs 500 crore in announced expansion projects.
