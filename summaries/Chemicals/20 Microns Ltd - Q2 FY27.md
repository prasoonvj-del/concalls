# 20 Microns Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth returned and gross margins expanded, but EBITDA and net profit declined due to higher energy, freight, and employee costs.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 238.16 crore (up 3.2% YoY from Rs 230.78 crore in Q2 FY26; compared to Rs 244.72 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 47.7% in Q2 FY27 (vs 47.6% in Q2 FY26). H1 gross margin rose by about 80 bps YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 27.73 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 31.85 crore in Q2 FY26 and Rs 35.87 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.6% in Q2 FY27.
- **PBT:** Rs 19.20 crore.
- **PAT:** Rs 13.64 crore.
- **Finance Cost:** Rs 4.20 crore (down 5.6% YoY from Rs 4.45 crore in Q2 FY26; vs Rs 3.91 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **CapEx deployed:** ~30% of the Rs 100 crore capacity programme deployed so far (including Rs 12 crore on solar and Rs 7 crore on mining acquisitions). Further Rs 17 crore planned in Q3 (all on solar).
- **Cost Headwinds (H1):** Furnace oil rose from Rs 42 to Rs 56 per kg (~33% increase, impact of Rs 1.34 crore); Gas rose from Rs 69 to Rs 122 per kg (~77% increase, impact of Rs 0.62 crore); Freight per metric tonne rose from Rs 2,108 to Rs 2,346 (11% increase, impact of Rs 2.92 crore). Total cost impact was Rs 4.78 crore (~200 bps of consolidated EBITDA).

### Highlights
- Functional additives (over 250 specialized grades) contribute about one quarter of revenue.
- Non-paint markets (polymers, cables, rubber, inks, ceramics, agrochemicals, construction chemicals) now account for more than half of sales.
- Acquired captive mines in Rajasthan and Andhra Pradesh; captive talc and quartz/feldspar production to start in December 2026.
- 9 MW captive solar plant (5.5 MW in Rajasthan, 3.5 MW in Gujarat) totaling ~Rs 29 crore investment is targeted for commissioning in Q4 FY27, expected to meet ~40% of power needs with an estimated annual EBITDA benefit of ~Rs 8 crore (~80 bps of EBITDA margin).
- 20 Microns Nano grew in double digits this quarter with increased profitability.
- Submitted a bid under the Government of India's PLI scheme for rare-earth permanent magnets (REPM).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue and operating performance in H2 to be better than H1.
- Management guided to an 18% CAGR over the next 2 to 3 years, supported by the ongoing CapEx and mining reserve acquisitions (noted as management estimates).
- Full-year benefits from the solar project and new mines will accrue starting FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- B2B demand environment is unpredictable with subdued demand in the paint segment, while polymer, rubber, and construction-chemical customers grew in double digits during the quarter.
- Q4 is typically one of the strongest quarters for the company and client industries.
- Imports account for ~40% of total raw materials, sourced mainly from China, Malaysia, and Vietnam.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins supported by a richer, engineered product mix rather than price hikes alone.
- Energy and freight inflation weighed on H1 margins (reducing EBITDA margin by ~200 bps).
- Captive sourcing and the upcoming solar plant are aimed at structurally mitigating raw material and power cost pressures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rs 100 crore capacity expansion programme is under way over 24 months, funded through internal accruals and selective debt.
- Malaysia project ongoing: quarry capacity expansion from 4,000 MT to 8,000 MT expected by FY29, with plant commencement by FY30.
- Expanding capacities in calcium carbonate, specialized/engineered kaolins, talc, and nano-related functional additives.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Funded via internal accruals and selective debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility and sharp increases in energy (furnace oil and gas) and export freight costs.
- Supply-chain disruptions, geopolitical conflicts, and general macroeconomic unpredictability making specific quarterly margin projections difficult.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harshit Khadka (Robo Capital) & Janish Shah (Individual Investor) on FY27/H2 outlook and 18% CAGR:** Management stated that specific numerical guidance is difficult due to market unpredictability, but H2 will be better than H1, and the 18% CAGR is targeted over the next 2-3 years backed by CapEx and mines. *(Management did not dodge, but caveated with market unpredictability).*
2. **Kavya Varma (Apricus Wealth) on divergence between customer growth and 20 Microns' growth:** Management explained that end customers show double-digit growth due to retail price hikes, whereas 20 Microns is a B2B supplier unable to pass on equivalent price increases, and segment-wise demand varies (e.g., paints subdued, polymers/rubber grew). *(Direct answer).*
3. **Kavya Varma (Apricus Wealth) on buyback/promoter stake:** Management stated no buyback plans currently, while promoter stake increase is discussed regularly at the board and will be pursued step by step. *(Direct answer).*
4. **Amit Bhaiya (Individual Investor) on quantification of H1 energy/freight impact and solar economics:** CFO Nihad Baluch quantified the exact cost impacts (furnace oil +Rs 1.34 cr, gas +Rs 0.62 cr, freight +Rs 2.92 cr; total ~Rs 4.78 cr or 200 bps) and stated the solar grid tariff reduction will yield ~Rs 8 crore annual benefit. *(Direct answer with precise numbers).*
5. **Shubham Sahani (RSPN Ventures) on revenue split and import/local mix:** Management stated imports constitute 40% and local sourcing 60%, and declined to provide an immediate segment-wise revenue split on the call, directing the analyst to the IR team. *(Partially dodged/deferred to offline).*

### One-line takeaway
While Q2 FY27 revenue grew 3.2% YoY and gross margins held steady at 47.7%, EBITDA margins moderated to 11.6% due to energy and freight inflation, prompting structural investments in captive solar power and mining to restore profitability.
