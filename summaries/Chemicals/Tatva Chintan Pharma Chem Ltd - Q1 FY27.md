# Tatva Chintan Pharma Chem Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-21 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating revenue grew 43% YoY and EBITDA grew 86% YoY, driven by volume growth and improving demand visibility.

### Key numbers
- **Operating Revenue:** INR1,671 million (up 43% YoY, 25% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** INR323 million (up 86% YoY, 15% QoQ)
- **Phase Transfer Catalysts (PTC) Revenue:** INR428 million (up 47% YoY, 38% QoQ)
- **Electrolyte Salts Revenue:** INR63 million (up 76% YoY, down 52% QoQ)
- **Pharma & Agro Intermediates and Specialty Chemicals (PASC) Revenue:** INR584 million (up 25% YoY, 63% QoQ)
- **Structure Directing Agents (SDA) Revenue:** INR578 million (up 47% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- **Capex Guidance:** Approximately INR200 crores approved for a new greenfield facility.

### Highlights
- Commercial production of a pharma intermediate commenced in Q1 FY27; management expects INR70-80 crores of revenue from new pharma molecules in FY27, scaling to around INR200 crores at full utilization.
- Delivered the first plant-scale batch of a semiconductor chemical, which was successfully qualified by the customer.
- Board approved a greenfield manufacturing facility with an investment of approximately INR200 crores (groundbreaking on 20 July 2026), targeting an asset turnover of 1.2x to 1.5x at peak utilization (INR300 crores revenue).
- Celebrating 30 years of Tatva Chintan.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 25%-30% YoY.
- Management maintains EBITDA margin guidance of 20%-22%.
- Electrolyte Salts revenue guidance maintained in the range of INR40 crores to INR60 crores.
- Management expects a compounded revenue growth of 20%-25% YoY over the next 3 to 4 years.
- Target ROIC of 20%-22%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Customer procurement patterns have become significantly more predictable.
- PTC demand is expanding due to widening acceptability across the chemical industry, though forward integration into SDA and Electrolyte Salts consumes internal volumes.
- SDA demand is strengthening with the geographical implementation of Euro 7 standards.
- Electrolyte Salts demand for energy storage devices is rapidly increasing; severe short supply of key raw materials due to the Middle East crisis caused temporary production delays in Q1.
- Hybrid battery customer commercialization program is advancing towards commercial supply (full-scale ramp-up expected in late 2027).
- Semiconductor product serves as a key starting raw material, etching agent, and cleaning agent.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Growth is primarily volume-driven, with marginal pricing impact. Price pass-on for increased raw material costs started 40-50 days prior to the call.
- Severe raw material shortages caused by the Middle East crisis led to temporary idle time and production delays in the Electrolyte Salts segment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dahej facility is nearing saturation mode in terms of available space; small debottlenecking is possible, but major growth requires the new site.
- Greenfield manufacturing facility at Jolva/new site scheduled to be operational within 21 months (internal target 18 months).
- MONOGLYME continuous flow equipment was converted to produce raw material for supercapacitor battery electrolytes due to aggressive Chinese price drops.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments and evolving trade dynamics globally.
- Semiconductor qualification is rigorous and will take time; commercialization in large volumes is not foreseen before Q4 of 2028.
- Flame retardant products remain connected to the polymer industry, where market reversal has not occurred, delaying commercialization.

### One-line takeaway
Tatva Chintan delivered strong Q1 FY27 growth (revenue up 43% YoY), announced a INR200 crore greenfield capex to alleviate Dahej capacity constraints, and achieved key commercial milestones in pharma and semiconductor chemistry.
