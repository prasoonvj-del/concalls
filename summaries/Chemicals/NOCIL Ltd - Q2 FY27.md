# NOCIL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited global uncertainty, supply-side constraints, geopolitical logistical challenges, and import dumping, alongside near-term demand headwinds from weaker monsoon and inflation.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs.830 crore (26% YoY growth)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs.427 crore (33% YoY growth, 6% QoQ growth)
- **H1 FY27 Volume Growth:** 3% YoY
- **Q2 FY27 Volume Growth:** -3% YoY, -7% QoQ
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs.36 crore (61% YoY growth, -20% QoQ growth)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 8.4%
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs.81 crore (54% YoY growth)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 9.8%
- **Q2 FY27 PBT:** Rs.28 crore (52% YoY growth, -24% QoQ growth)
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs.65 crore (57% YoY growth)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs.24 crore (98% YoY growth, -13% QoQ growth)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs.52 crore (76% YoY growth)
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Rs.1,400 crore to Rs.1,600 crore (towards the upper end)
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Around 10%
- **Annual Depreciation (TDQ Plant):** ~Rs.1 crore per month / ~Rs.3 crore per quarter on ~Rs.220 crore base
- **Total Capex Sanctioned (Board):** Rs.130 crore (to be spent across H2 and next year's H1, plus routine maintenance capex)

### Highlights
- Domestic volumes supported by single-digit growth following GST 2.0 implementation and sustained demand.
- Export volumes recorded single-digit growth from international market traction and customer conversion.
- Q2 revenue growth was primarily driven by higher average selling prices, while volumes were impacted by temporary supply-side and geopolitical logistical challenges.
- Supply-side constraints have been addressed, and affected volumes are expected to recover in H2.
- Actively exploring inorganic growth opportunities in the chemical space to build cyclic resilience and utilize cash.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of closing FY27 towards the upper end of the Rs.1,400 crore to Rs.1,600 crore revenue range with EBITDA margins of around 10% (V.S. Anand).
- Expects 8% to 10% volume growth for FY27 and a fair assumption of 10% volume growth for FY28 (V.S. Anand).
- Expects H2 volume indexation around 160 to 165 (V.S. Anand).
- Captive power boilers and full turbine utilization benefits expected to kick in during H2 (V.S. Anand, P. Srinivasan).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic tire demand benefited from GST 2.0 consumption tailwinds.
- Industry faced higher raw material costs due to rising crude oil prices amid geopolitical tensions.
- Weaker monsoon and inflationary pressures could create near-term demand headwinds.
- Underlying industry fundamentals remain supported by healthy replacement demand, OEM volume growth, and long-term drivers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Conversion costs impacted by significant utility cost increases (coal and gas) and spiraling domestic and sea freight costs.
- Lack of full volume utilization restricted operating leverage benefits in H1.
- Antidumping duty (ADD) on sulfonamides had some portion of benefit in Q2, though antioxidants comprise 60% of the business.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Brownfield TDQ expansion at Dahej progressing towards customer approvals expected over coming months; capitalization expected in Q3 upon approval.
- Integrated specialty rubber chemicals project progressing as planned.
- Rs.130 crore board-sanctioned capex to be spent across H2 and next year's H1, alongside routine maintenance capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital brought down from 32%+ in March '25 to around 25% of sales.
- Inventory run rate managed around 40 to 60 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global uncertainty, supply-side disruptions, competitive pressures, and import dumping.
- Weaker monsoon and inflationary pressures creating near-term demand headwinds.
- Geopolitical tensions causing higher crude, fuel (coal/gas), and freight costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nirav Jimudia (Anvil Wealth Management) asked about the feasibility of achieving 165 volume indexation in H2 and conservatism in guidance.** Management stated they are positive on reaching 8%-10% volume growth, indexation of 160-165, and admitted to some conservatism while aiming to achieve more.
- **Aditya Khetan (SMIFS Institutional Equity) questioned the drivers of optimism amid moderation in tire growth and asked about antidumping duty impact on 4ADPA.** Management clarified that PILFLEX-13 ADD was not notified/accepted, but 4ADPA ADD (if notified) could increase competitor costs; ADD on sulfonamides has a minor impact as antioxidants form 60% of the business.
- **Nitesh Dhoot (Anand Rathi Institutional Equity) asked about realization jumps versus gross profit spreads and inventory reversal risks.** Management stated inventory is rolling (40-60 days) with no significant short-term negative effect, and working capital is optimized at ~25% of sales.
- **Ravi Mehta (OneUp) inquired if gross profit per tonne sustainability was driven by ADD or inventory benefits.** Management confirmed that while a small portion of ADD is present in Q2, overall market conditions have improved compared to last year's base.

### One-line takeaway
NOCIL reported 33% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by realizations despite volume drops, and management maintains its FY27 top-line guidance toward the upper end of Rs.1,400-1,600 crore with ~10% EBITDA margins.
