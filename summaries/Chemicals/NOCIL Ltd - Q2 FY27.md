# NOCIL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-26 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited global uncertainty, supply-side constraints, geopolitical logistical challenges, and import dumping, alongside near-term demand headwinds from weaker monsoon and inflation.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs. 830 crore (up 26% YoY / 36% YoY as per CFO)
- Q2 FY27 Revenue: Rs. 427 crore (up 33% YoY, 6% QoQ)
- H1 FY27 Volume growth: 3% YoY
- Q2 FY27 Volume decline: 3% YoY, 7% QoQ
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs. 36 crore (up 61% YoY, down 20% QoQ)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin: 8.4%
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs. 81 crore (up 54% YoY); Margin: 9.8%
- Q2 FY27 PBT: Rs. 28 crore (up 52% YoY, down 24% QoQ); H1 FY27 PBT: Rs. 65 crore (up 57% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs. 24 crore (up 98% YoY, down 13% QoQ); H1 FY27 PAT: Rs. 52 crore (up 76% YoY)
- Working Capital: Inventory brought down to around 25% of sales (from 32%+ in March '25)
- Annual depreciation charge increase from TDQ plant: ~Rs. 1 crore a month or ~Rs. 3 crore per quarter on a base of typically Rs. 220 crores
- Sanctioned Capex: Rs. 130 crore sanctioned by the Board to be spent across H2 and next year's H1

### Highlights
- Domestic volumes saw single-digit growth aided by GST 2.0 consumption tailwinds; export volumes recorded single-digit growth.
- Q2 volumes impacted by temporary supply-side constraints and logistical challenges from geopolitical situations, which management states have now been addressed.
- Brownfield TDQ expansion at Dahej awaits customer approvals, expected in Q3, after which capitalization will occur.
- Management is actively exploring inorganic growth opportunities in the chemical space.
- *Continues pattern from earlier record:* Capex of Rs. 130 crore sanctioned by the board across H2 and next year's H1 remains on track.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 Revenue Outlook: Management remains confident of closing towards the upper end of the Rs. 1,400 crore to Rs. 1,600 crore range, with EBITDA margins around 10%.
- Volume Growth Guidance: Management expects to stay in the 8% to 10% volume growth range for FY27, targeting an index range of 160 to 165 in H2 (base Q1 FY20 = 100), and projects a similar 10% volume growth assumption for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic tire demand benefited from consumption tailwinds following GST 2.0.
- Weaker monsoon and inflationary pressures could create near-term demand headwinds.
- Long-term industry fundamentals remain supportive due to healthy replacement demand, OEM volume growth, and long-term drivers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher raw material costs driven by rising crude oil prices amid geopolitical tensions; utility costs (coal and gas) and domestic/sea freight costs increased significantly, raising conversion costs by Rs. 10 to Rs. 20 per kg.
- Antidumping duty (ADD) on sulfonamides provided some benefit in Q2, though not quantified as a major portion since antioxidants comprise 60% of the business.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Brownfield TDQ expansion at Dahej is progressing towards customer approvals expected in Q3.
- Integrated specialty rubber chemicals project is progressing as planned.
- Total board-sanctioned capex is Rs. 130 crore, to be spent across H2 and next year's H1, alongside routine maintenance capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital improved, with inventory brought down to around 25% of sales from 32%+ in March '25.
- Captive power boilers/turbines full utilization expected to kick in during H2 as volumes increase.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global uncertainty, supply-side constraints, geopolitical logistical challenges, and import dumping.
- Weaker monsoon and inflationary pressures creating near-term demand headwinds.
- Higher coal, gas, and sea freight costs impacting conversion margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nirav Jimudia (Anvil Wealth Management) asked about the feasibility of achieving ~165 index volume growth in H2 and whether FY28 volume growth assumption of 10% holds.** Management (V.S. Anand) confirmed confidence in hitting the 8%-10% volume growth range for FY27 and stated 10% is a fair assumption for FY28.
- **Aditya Khetan (SMIFS Institutional Equity) asked about the upside potential on margins if ADD is imposed on 4ADPA.** Management (P. Srinivasan) clarified that 4ADPA is an intermediate for PILFLEX-13, and since ADD on PILFLEX-13 was not accepted by the central government, an ADD on 4ADPA would only raise competitors' production costs without necessarily driving up PILFLEX-13 market prices.
- **Nitesh Dhoot (Anand Rathi) asked how much of the realization increase is driven by ADD versus cost-pass-through.** Management (P. Srinivasan) answered that ADD on sulfonamides is not significant because 60% of the business is antioxidants, and price increases largely reflect higher input costs and improved spreads.
- **Ravi Mehta (OneUp) asked if gross profit per tonne sustainability in Q2 relied on ADD.** Management confirmed that while some portion of ADD was present in Q2, improved market conditions and better run-rates compared to last year also contributed.
- **Pavan Kaware (Nayan M Vala Securities) asked about revenue expectation and capex splits.** Management reiterated the Rs. 1,400-1,600 crore range (leaning to the upper end) and confirmed the Rs. 130 crore capex is split across H2 and next year's H1.

### One-line takeaway
NOCIL reported 33% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 despite a 3% volume decline, driven by higher realizations and cost-pass-throughs, and management maintains its FY27 top-line guidance toward the upper end of Rs. 1,400–1,600 crores.
