# DCW Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth and Basic Chemicals recovery were offset by margin pressure in Specialty Chemicals due to input cost volatility and high freight rates.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 663 crore (+23% YoY, +22% QoQ).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,205 crore (+19% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 55 crore (-13% YoY, +32% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8.2%.
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 96 crore (-20% YoY).
*   **Finance Costs:** Rs 13.6 crore (Q2, -8% QoQ, -12.5% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 26 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Basic Chemicals:** Segment EBITDA swung to Rs 20 crore (from a Rs 14 crore loss in Q1) driven by PVC-VCM spread normalization and Synthetic Rutile volume recovery.
*   **Specialty Chemicals:** Segment faced margin pressure as CPVC input costs rose faster than selling prices; high ocean freight impacted SIOP exports to North America.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Board approved a share buyback of up to Rs 40 crore.
*   **Corporate:** Unveiled a refreshed company identity.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 FY27 to be "significantly stronger" than H1.
*   Management is confident of achieving double-digit margins in H2.
*   Expects full-year finance costs to be 9-10% lower than last year.
*   Management estimates revenue potential at full capacity/current prices to be ~Rs 2,500 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **PVC:** VCM-PVC spread normalized to >$100 (currently ~$120); current PVC prices are $940-$960.
*   **Regulatory:** DGTR recommended countervailing duty on Chinese PVC imports (pending Ministry of Finance approval).
*   **Demand:** Management reports good demand for CPVC (plumbing/infrastructure) and SIOP (paints/coatings/construction).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Power:** 75% captive, 25% imported (mostly renewable). Solar CAPEX provides savings, but absolute power costs remain high due to rising coal/lignite prices.
*   **SIOP Margins:** Maintained at ~40%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Investment Program:** Rs 250 crore project initiated (SIOP expansion + power infrastructure optimization).
*   **SIOP:** Rated capacity is 30,000 tonnes; Phase-1 (7 KT) and power efficiency project underway. Next phase (8 KT) will take capacity to 45 KT.
*   **CPVC:** Capacity is 50,000 tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 297 crore.
*   **Gross Debt:** Rs 323 crore.
*   **Net Debt/EBITDA:** 0.12x.
*   **Current Ratio:** 1.17x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** West Asia crisis impacting feedstock costs and freight rates.
*   **Operational:** Dhrangadhra facility floods in Q2 caused temporary plant stoppage.
*   **Market:** Volatile PVC prices and Chinese import dumping.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Buyback Size:** Analyst questioned the "minuscule" buyback size; Management clarified that SEBI regulations (25% of 10-day avg volume) limit the buyback to Rs 40 crore.
*   **Margins:** Analyst asked if double-digit margins are achievable in H2; Management confirmed confidence in reaching this target.
*   **Power Costs:** Analyst asked why power costs are rising despite solar investment; Management explained that coal/lignite price hikes have offset the savings from solar.
*   **SIOP Ramp-up:** Analyst asked about timing; Management expects capacity to be on board in Q4 of next financial year, with a 6-month ramp-up period.
*   **CPVC Spreads:** Analyst asked about contract nature; Management noted contracts are short-term/monthly, leading to lags in passing on price hikes during volatile periods.

### One-line takeaway
DCW delivered a mixed quarter with strong revenue growth and a recovery in Basic Chemicals, while management remains optimistic for a stronger H2 driven by normalized spreads and ongoing capacity expansion.
