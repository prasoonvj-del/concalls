# Chemicals - Dyes & pigments: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian dyes, pigments, and specialty chemicals sector reflects a bifurcated reality. Companies with strong export exposure or integrated domestic operations (such as Asahi Songwon, Bodal Chemicals, and PCBL Chemical) reported robust YoY growth in revenues and profitability, aided by structural supply disruptions and tighter environmental regulations in China. Conversely, players with heavy exposure to Western markets (such as Sudarshan Chemical and Kiri Industries) faced macro headwinds, soft construction demand in Europe and the US, and high operational costs. Deleveraging and asset monetization remained key themes, with multiple firms utilizing proceeds to strengthen balance sheets.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Historically, Indian dye and pigment players relied on low-cost mass production of basic intermediates and commoditized pigments, serving as an alternative manufacturing hub with minimal focus on integration or high-end technology. Over the past five years, the model has shifted toward backward integration, moving up the value chain into specialty pigments, green energy adoption, and aggressive global acquisitions or captive raw material sourcing to insulate against geopolitical supply shocks. [Background]

**Shifts underway** - 
*   **Backward integration and captive raw material sourcing**: Scaling up; Kiri Industries acquired a 40% stake in Makilala Mining and incorporated Andamina SPA to secure captive copper concentrate for its upcoming integrated facility [Kiri Industries concall Q2 FY27], while PCBL Chemical progressed on its coal distillation plant approvals [PCBL Chemical concall Q2 FY27]. This shift mitigates third-party supply chain volatility and protects margins.
*   **Asset rationalization and aggressive deleveraging**: Mainstream; Asahi Songwon reduced gross borrowings by ~Rs 49 crore in H1 FY27 [Asahi Songwon concall Q2 FY27], and Sudarshan Chemical closed the sale of its non-core Frankfurt VP4 plant for EUR 75.8 million to pay down debt [Sudarshan Chemical concall Q2 FY27]. These moves enhance balance sheet resilience amidst volatile global demand.
*   **Geographic and product portfolio expansion via M&A**: Scaling; Sudarshan Chemical signed definitive agreements to acquire Clariant’s Swiss aluminum dyes manufacturing business [Sudarshan Chemical concall Q2 FY27], and Kiri Industries advanced its USD 280 million copper and fertilizer pivot [Kiri Industries concall Q2 FY27]. This reduces reliance on legacy cyclical dye portfolios.

**Government push** - 
*   MP government approval for new agro, chemical, and textile parks rewards regional infrastructure development, benefiting chemical manufacturers expanding local footprints [Govt: MP govt approves parks, Nov 2026].
*   Gujarat AAR clarification on 5% GST rates for biodegradable bags supports packaging and specialized chemical inputs tied to eco-friendly compliance [Govt: Gujarat AAR, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board noted that global export destinations (particularly Europe and the US) are experiencing seasonal inventory tightening, geopolitical supply chain friction, and cautious customer buying patterns. Furthermore, a 2-to-3-month price-transmission lag exists when passing intermediate cost increases onto downstream dyestuff customers.
*   **Divergence:** Bodal Chemicals and Asahi Songwon highlighted positive structural tailwinds from China (stricter environmental enforcement and initial exports of H acid, vinyl sulfone, and dyestuff to China), whereas Sudarshan Chemical and Kiri Industries reported persistent demand softness in Western construction and housing markets. Orient Bell noted a shift from Q1 "demand chasing supply" to Q2 "supply chasing demand" with restored capacities.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   Raw material and intermediate prices (such as sulfur, aniline, and coal tar derivatives) experienced upward pressure due to geopolitical tensions and war, though certain inputs like sulfur softened slightly from peak levels (Bodal noting a drop from ~Rs 100/kg to ~Rs 90/kg).
*   Freight and logistics costs rose due to vessel shortages and longer transit times (highlighted by PCBL Chemical).
*   Firms deployed strict cost disciplines: Asahi Songwon maintained raw material inventories at ~2 weeks via just-in-time practices, PCBL advanced its "Project Alchemy" cost-savings program, and Sudarshan Chemical undertook a Rs 74 crore European employee restructuring program to realize structural overhead savings.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Asahi Songwon:** Operating AZO pigments at 70%-75% and Chhatral Greenfield at ~40%; a meaningful AZO line addition requires Rs 10-15 crore and will trigger at ~85% utilization [Asahi Songwon concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Bodal Chemicals:** Saykha benzene downstream plant reached ~85% utilization in October; pausing immediate brownfield dye intermediate expansions until Chinese structural shifts stabilize [Bodal Chemicals concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Kiri Industries:** Advancing phase one downstream facilities for its copper/fertilizer project toward H1 FY28, with total project capex estimated at ~USD 280 million [Kiri Industries concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Orient Bell:** Completed 1.2 million square meters of ceramic-to-GVT capacity conversion in October 2026; total FY27 capex projected around Rs 20 crore [Orient Bell concall Q2 FY27].
*   **PCBL Chemical:** Planned FY27 capex of Rs 300 crore, focusing on specialty carbon black volumes and advanced battery material pilots [PCBL Chemical concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Sudarshan Chemical:** Evaluating long-term capital deployment post-VP4 proceeds while integrating European operations and pursuing IT/SAP harmonization [Sudarshan Chemical concall Q2 FY27].

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance & Tone Shifts:** Sudarshan Chemical shifted from a positive tone to a cautious outlook regarding US housing and European construction demand. PCBL Chemical flagged that its initial ~7% volume growth target has become challenging due to raw material and competitive pressures. Bodal Chemicals and Asahi Songwon maintained an upbeat tone on the back of record H1 performances.
*   **Delivery on Promises:** Orient Bell successfully completed its planned conversion of 1.2 million square meters of ceramic capacity into GVT in October 2026 [Orient Bell concall Q2 FY27]. Sudarshan Chemical successfully closed the divestment of its Frankfurt VP4 plant on November 2, 2026, for EUR 75.8 million [Sudarshan Chemical concall Q2 FY27].

## What to watch next quarter
*   Sustainability of high intermediate realizations (such as H-acid pricing settling around ~Rs 1,650) and whether order books reflect prolonged demand absorption in China.
*   Pacing of debt reduction and capital allocation announcements following asset monetization proceeds (Sudarshan Chemical's EUR 75.8 million VP4 sale and Asahi Songwon's debt reduction).
*   Execution milestones on large-scale strategic diversification projects, specifically Kiri Industries' copper/fertilizer pivot and PCBL Chemical's battery material and coal distillation initiatives.
