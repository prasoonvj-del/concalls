# Amtech Esters Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 revenue/PAT growth driven by new capacity additions, operationalization of Asoda plant, and clear expansion roadmap.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 27.5 crore, up 45.7% YoY (67.5% of FY26 full-year revenue).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 2.3 crore, up 78% YoY.
- **PAT margin:** 8.5% vs 6.9% YoY (up 160 bps).
- **EPS:** Rs 2.64, up 30% YoY from Rs 2.03.
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 14.1 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Borrowings:** Rs 0.6 crore.

### Highlights
- Listed on the BSE SME platform in September 2026.
- New Asoda plant started commercial production on November 2, 2026 (following trial runs from October 3, 2026), expanding UPR resin capacity by 4,800 MTPA to 7,760 MTPA (2.6x increase).
- Invested Rs 8.81 crore of IPO proceeds into 100% subsidiary Croda Pigments to expand pigment paste capacity from 382 MTPA to 973 MTPA.
- Transitioned to bulk delivery in 25-tonne container tankers at Asoda from 225–250 kg barrels, opening up larger supply visibility with quartz segment customers.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue:** Conservative guidance of Rs 70 crore (management internal target is Rs 80–85 crore).
- **FY27 PAT:** Rs 8–9 crore (~10–11% PAT margin).
- **FY27 EBITDA margin:** 15%+.
- **FY28 Revenue:** Targeting Rs 100–120 crore (~Rs 10 crore average monthly run-rate).
- **FY28 Margins:** EBITDA margins expected to expand by 100–150 bps YoY due to operational efficiencies at Asoda; PAT margin targeted at 12–13%.
- **Croda Pigments (Subsidiary):** Expecting ~Rs 15 crore revenue in FY27 (50% YoY growth) and Rs 23–25 crore in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Resin demand spans buttons, DMC/MCB switchgear, fiberglass roofing, automotive dashboard/bumpers, marine, statues, and quartz stone.
- Button grade resins currently generate 60–70% of parent revenue.
- Revenue mix is expected to diversify to ~50% quartz stone and 30–35% buttons in FY28.
- Monsoon season (July–September) is traditionally a lean period for application; H2 is significantly stronger.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw materials include maleic acid, phthalic acid, glycols (MEG, DEG, PG, TEG), and styrene.
- Recent crude price fluctuations caused short-term volatility in pricing and raw material costs.
- Bulk shipping via pipelines and tankers at Asoda reduces manual handling overheads and container costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Bahadurgarh plant:** 2,960 MTPA resin capacity (ran at 88% usable capacity in FY26).
- **Asoda plant:** Adds 4,800 MTPA; built entirely through internal accruals with no fresh term debt. Expecting 30–40% utilization in November 2026 and 60–70% by December 2026.
- Asoda plant has scope to add an additional 250 MT/month (3,000 MTPA) capacity in the future.
- Planning a third resin plant by late FY28 with 100–125 MT daily (2,500 MTPA) capacity focusing on the quartz market.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stands at Rs 37 crore; net cash position of Rs 13.5 crore.
- Trade credit terms in the market range between 70–75 days.
- Allocation of ~Rs 18 crore IPO funds: Rs 2.4–2.5 crore for issue expenses, Rs 8.81 crore to Croda Pigments (Rs 4.5 crore working capital, Rs 3.41 crore capex), ~Rs 4.5 crore debt/liability repayment, and ~Rs 4.4–4.5 crore for parent working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Crude oil price volatility directly impacts raw material costs and product realizations.
- Commercial launch of Asoda plant was delayed to November 2026 from the original target of pre-IPO (September 2026).
- Anti-dumping duty on Titanium dioxide (pigment raw material) is currently under government review.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS):** Why is H2 expected to be significantly larger than H1 (Rs 27.5 cr)?  
  *Management:* H1 is seasonally lean due to monsoon impact on resin applications. With Asoda operationalized, capacity is tripled and direct 25-tonne tanker shipping enables higher volume delivery to bulk quartz buyers.
- **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS):** What are the EBITDA margin expectations given past volatility?  
  *Management:* Margins will remain at 15%+ for FY27. Automation and centralized administrative overheads at Asoda will yield a 100–200 bps margin expansion starting FY28.
- **Nitin (Individual Investor):** What is the peak revenue potential of current capacity?  
  *Management:* At full capacity utilization, current setup across plants can generate up to Rs 150 crore in peak annual revenue.
- **Jignesh V. (Vedarth Investments):** Promoter stake dropped to 45% post-listing; any plans to increase or raise capital?  
  *Management:* Total friendly and family holdings are above 50%. Management will consult merchant bankers on increasing promoter stake if needed, and intends to remain term loan-free for near-term expansions.

### One-line takeaway
Amtech Esters is scaling up rapidly with its new Asoda plant tripling resin capacity, enabling bulk quartz supply, and driving guidance toward Rs 70 crore revenue in FY27 and Rs 100+ crore in FY28.
