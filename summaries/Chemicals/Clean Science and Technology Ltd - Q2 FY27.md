# Clean Science and Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Record revenue and strong HALS volume growth were offset by margin compression due to high raw material costs and competitive pricing pressures.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 295 crore (up 23% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 226 crore (up 11% QoQ, 10% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 92 crore.
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 56 crore.
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 36%.
*   **Standalone PAT Margin:** 24%.
*   **HALS Segment Revenue:** Grew 2x YoY.
*   **Raw Material Cost (Consolidated):** 43.3% of revenue (vs 39.7% previously).

### Highlights
*   Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue in company history.
*   HALS segment: H1 FY27 volumes reached 67% of the total volumes achieved in the entire previous financial year.
*   Export sales (Standalone): Increased to 73% of business (vs 64% in the previous quarter).
*   CFCL (subsidiary) completed trials for advanced HALS grade; commercial launch expected in H2 FY27.
*   Barbituric acid production is on hold due to unfavorable economics, as domestic raw material costs increased 3x.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Performance Chemical 2:** Commercial production expected in Q4 FY27 (Jan/Feb); targeting 20-25% capacity utilization in the first year.
*   **HALS Capacity:** Currently 11,500 tonnes; management plans to reach 12,500 tonnes via de-bottlenecking and new lines.
*   **HALS Utilization:** Expected to reach 55-60% in coming quarters (currently 30-40%).
*   Management expects margins to improve quarter-on-quarter as higher-grade HALS sales increase and backward integration benefits materialize.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains resilient across Performance, Pharma, and FMCG segments.
*   Input costs (phenol, acetone, methanol) are 50-100% higher YoY due to the Middle East conflict.
*   Chinese prices are 10-15% lower than Indian prices; management stated they are absorbing costs to maintain market share rather than passing on price hikes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Standalone raw material costs rose to 41% (vs 35% in Q1 FY27).
*   Backward integration: Started one product (ethoxylation facility) as a precursor for HALS; another intermediate will be produced in-house next quarter.
*   Backward integration is expected to improve margins for specific HALS segments by 5-7%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Total investment in subsidiary stands at Rs 900 crore (Rs 50 crore infused in Q2, Rs 150 crore in H1 FY27).
*   Clean Antioxidant 962: Currently producing ~500 tonnes within existing assets; no specific capex deployed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   High raw material costs (crude-linked) and inability to pass through price hikes due to competitive pressure from China.
*   Uncertainty in margin trajectory due to macroeconomic factors and crude oil prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities):** Asked about margin compression. Management confirmed they are absorbing costs to maintain market share rather than passing them on.
*   **Sanjesh Jain:** Asked about Barbituric acid. Management confirmed it is on hold because domestic raw material costs became 3x, making production unviable.
*   **Arun Prasath (Avendus Spark):** Asked about the "breaking point" for price hikes. Management stated they will not be the first to raise prices to avoid losing market share.
*   **Abhijit Akella (KIE):** Asked about HALS margin impact from backward integration. Management expects a 5-7% improvement for those specific segments.

### One-line takeaway
Clean Science delivered record revenue driven by HALS volume growth, but profitability remains pressured by high raw material costs and competitive pricing constraints.
