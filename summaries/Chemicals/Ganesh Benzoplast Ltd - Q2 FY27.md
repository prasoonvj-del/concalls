# Ganesh Benzoplast Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-13 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights value unlocking via the divestment of the liquid tank farm business and robust growth in chemicals and EPC.

### Key numbers
- **Divestment proceeds:** INR1,154 crores (valuation for JNPA, Goa, Cochin, and JNPA-linked rail business).
- **Payment tranche schedule:** ~INR500 crores before January 2027; ~INR300 crores before March 2027; balance INR354 crores in tranches over the next 12 to 18 months.
- **Use of first INR500 crores:** ~INR190 crores to INR200 crores for taxes and bank limit closures; ~INR200 crores combined for a share buyback and special dividend; ~INR100 crores for chemical business expansion and EPC book increase.
- **Chemical business performance (Q1 FY27):** Turnover of ~INR60 crores; profit of INR6 crores.
- **Chemical business profitability target:** Increase from ~INR18 crores (current year) to INR60 crores to INR70 crores over the next 4 to 5 years (5x increase).
- **Chemical capacity & utilization:** Capacity is ~1,000 tonnes a month per plant (2 plants total). Utilization is 70% to 80%.
- **Chemical export orders:** Over 600 tonnes received, expected to grow to 2,000 tonnes per year.
- **EPC order book & targets:** Current year EPC book close to INR500 crores (including a ~INR300 crores order from KKR). Yearly EPC revenue expected to cross ~INR250 crores per annum next year. Long-term EPC PAT target of ~INR150 crores.
- **Combined PAT target:** ~INR210 crores of PAT in the next 3 to 5 years (chemicals ~INR60 crores + EPC ~INR150 crores).
- **Consolidated YoY growth target:** 15% to 20% year-over-year growth by FY28.

### Highlights
- Divested liquid tank farm (LST) business at JNPA, Goa, and Cochin, along with the JNPA-linked rail business connecting Daund, for INR1,154 crores.
- Promoters will not participate in the share buyback.
- Achieved REACH certification for key products (benzoic acid, sodium benzoate) in the UK and Europe.
- Executed notable EPC projects including a 22 km SS pipeline for GACL/NALCO JV, tank farms for ANA Oleo and Allana, fuel tanks for the Mumbai airport, gas holders for Adani Mundra Petrochemicals (MPL), chemical tank farm for Jindals, and structural work for Reliance at Hazira.
- Retained a small, insignificant shareholding in a terminal in Kandla.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects 15% to 20% year-over-year growth by FY28.
- Chemical PAT targeted at INR60 crores to INR70 crores in 4 to 5 years.
- EPC PAT targeted at ~INR150 crores in the long term.
- Total consolidated PAT targeted at ~INR210 crores in 3 to 5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Chemical business has been on an upward trajectory with profitability up nearly 1,000% over the last 2 years.
- Petroleum-related EPC sector projected at ~INR70,000 crores over the next 5 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management aims to sustain and improve chemical EBITDA margins (historical chemical EBITDA margins around 11% to 12%).
- Raw materials are petroleum-based, and crude fluctuations impact costs, but management states they try to pass on fluctuations to customers.
- EPC PAT margins considered conservatively at 7% to 8%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Stage 1 capex for chemicals and EPC not expected to exceed ~INR150 crores.
- Doubling capacity of the chemical plant with REACH certification, expected to be commissioned by July 2027.
- Advanced discussions with technology/license providers for niche agro and downstream product derivatives.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ~INR190 crores to INR200 crores from the first tranche to be used to close existing bank limits.
- Future debt to be kept to a bare minimum; working capital for EPC to be serviced via incoming tranches and necessary banking lines/bank guarantees.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Crude oil price volatility affecting petroleum-based raw material costs in the chemical division.
- Milestone and port transfer approvals required from port authorities for deal closure tranches.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepesh Sancheti (Maanya Finance) asked about the timeline for tranches and working capital for EPC.** Management clarified the 12 to 18-month spread for the balance 354 crores and stated that existing/reinstated bank limits and incoming funds will service EPC working capital.
2. **Romil Savaliya (Family Office) asked about the rationale for flat top-line growth in chemicals vs. profitability.** Management responded that profitability (growing from INR5 crores to INR15–20 crores) is prioritized over top-line revenue.
3. **Nishita (Individual Investor) questioned the rationale for divesting the higher-margin LST business.** Management explained that evaluation of future costs, market movements, and the attractive valuation offered by buyers made it a fiduciary duty to present the deal to shareholders.
4. **Bhavesh Patel asked if special dividends/buybacks would apply to subsequent tranches.** Management stated the initial buyback/dividend applies to the first tranche, and future capital deployment decisions will be taken with the Board depending on expansion needs and cash availability.
5. **Ketan Chheda asked why the company didn't retain the LST cash cow to fund other divisions.** Management stated that while LST could fund its own growth (like 43 new tanks under construction at JNPA), keeping it would have stagnated the growth of the chemical and EPC divisions.

### One-line takeaway
Ganesh Benzoplast is unlocking substantial value by divesting its LST business for INR1,154 crores—returning ~INR200 crores via buyback/dividend from the first tranche—while pivoting to high-growth chemical and EPC verticals.
