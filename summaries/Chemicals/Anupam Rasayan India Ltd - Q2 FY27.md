# Anupam Rasayan India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong consolidated performance, margin improvement, and strategic acquisitions like Bliss GVS Pharma.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Total Income (Q2 FY27): Rs 821 crore, up 11% YoY from Rs 739 crore.
- Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 202 crore, up 41% YoY from Rs 144 crore.
- Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): ~25% compared to 19.4% in Q2 FY26.
- Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 65 crore, up 14% YoY from Rs 57 crore.
- PAT Margin (Q2 FY27): ~8%.
- Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27): Rs 1,489 crore, up 21% YoY from Rs 1,230 crore.
- Consolidated EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 377 crore, up 38% YoY from Rs 273 crore.
- Consolidated EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27): 25.5% compared to 22.2% in H1 FY26.
- Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 116 crore, up 10% YoY from Rs 106 crore.
- Cumulative portfolio of LOIs and long-term contracts: ~Rs 17,496 crore.
- Bliss GVS acquisition stake: ~50.07% acquired as of September 28, 2026.
- Bliss acquisition funding: Rs 300 crore term loan and Rs 1,450 crore via non-controlling/non-voting instruments.
- Standalone working capital cycle: Reduced slightly from 276 days to 271 days (excluding Bliss balance sheet).
- Operating cash flow (H1 FY27): Rs 328 crore.

### Highlights
- Completed the acquisition of a 50.07% stake in Bliss GVS Pharma Limited on September 28, 2026, integrating finished dosage formulations with existing intermediates and custom synthesis.
- Signed a six-year long-term supply agreement with a leading U.S.-based global specialty metal manufacturer, marking entry into the geological resources sector.
- Commercialized ETFA using continuous flow chemistry successfully at commercial scale.
- Standalone revenue decline in Q2 FY27 compared to Q2 FY26 was attributed to a one-time sale of ~Rs 180 crore in Q2 FY26.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects consolidated revenue for H2 FY27 to be around Rs 2,400 crore to Rs 2,500 crore, translating to an annualized run rate of ~Rs 4,800 crore.
- Management guides for an organic growth of ~20% to 25% over a near-term 3 to 5-year time period.
- Management expects Bliss GVS steady-state EBITDA margins to be around 22% to 24%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Customer engagement remains strong across polymers, electronic chemicals, and fluorinated products.
- Management notes that cyclicality is expected to reduce over the next 4 to 8 quarters as Pharma and Performance Materials share increases.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins improved due to higher contributions from the Performance Materials segment and a favorable product mix.
- High inventory levels are intentionally maintained as shock absorbers to insulate customers from raw material price volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bliss GVS plant capacity utilization is currently running in the 40s.
- Multipurpose plants and kept-free capacity enabled rapid 4-month commercialization of the new U.S. metal manufacturer contract.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Bliss GVS balance sheet was consolidated as of September 2026, while its revenue contribution for Q2 FY27 is limited to 3 days (from September 28, 2026).
- Bliss acquisition was funded via a Rs 300 crore term loan (single-digit interest, housed in a wholly-owned subsidiary) and Rs 1,450 crore quasi-equity financing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as negative risks, other than standard cautious guidance on margins and integration timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nitish Rege (ChrysCapital) asked about API acquisition targets and Bliss margin guidance.** Management stated they are actively evaluating API targets with nothing public yet, and guided steady-state Bliss EBITDA margins at 22%-24%.
2. **Tanya Chowdhary asked about sustainability of standalone margins and Bliss synergies timeline.** Management attributed standalone margins to product mix and urged caution on guidance, noting Bliss synergies across CDMO and supply should materialize in 3 to 4 quarters.
3. **Meet Vora asked about H2 revenue expectations and the new specialty metal contract potential.** Management guided H2 consolidated revenue at Rs 2,400–2,500 crore, and stated the new metal contract should contribute Rs 100–150 crore or more this year.
4. **Hardik asked about the Bliss acquisition funding structure and Elementium LOI progress.** Management detailed the Rs 300 crore term loan and Rs 1,450 crore quasi-equity funding, and stated Elementium contract discussions are ongoing with small monthly supply volumes already started.
5. **Burhanuddin Solanki asked about margin improvement initiatives at Jayhawk.** Management cited process optimization, sourcing cost reductions via Indian scale, and effluent treatment cost improvements as immediate levers.

### One-line takeaway
Anupam Rasayan delivered strong H1 FY27 performance with 21% revenue growth and 38% EBITDA growth, anchored by strategic inorganic expansions into Bliss GVS Pharma and Jayhawk Fine Chemicals.
