# Anupam Rasayan India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong EBITDA growth driven by product mix and strategic acquisition milestones, including acquiring a controlling stake in Bliss GVS Pharma and entering the geological resources sector.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 821 crore (+11% YoY compared to Rs 739 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 202 crore (+41% YoY compared to Rs 144 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** ~25% (vs 19.4% in Q2 FY26). Standalone EBITDA margin stood at 34%.
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 65 crore (+14% YoY compared to Rs 57 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** ~8% (vs ~7.7% in Q2 FY26).
- **Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,489 crore (+21% YoY).
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 377 crore (+38% YoY); EBITDA margin at 25.5% (vs 22.2% in H1 FY26).
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 116 crore (+10% YoY).
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Rs 328 crore (vs Rs 334 crore for full year FY26).
- **Revenue Mix (Q2 FY27):** Life science-related specialty chemicals 42%, Performance Materials 28%, Other fluorinated products 29%.

### Highlights
- Completed acquisition of 50.07% equity capital in Bliss GVS Pharma Limited as of September 28, 2026; balance sheet consolidated in Q2 FY27, with only 3 days of revenue/operating contribution included.
- Acquisition funded via Rs 300 crore term loan and Rs 1,450 crore quasi-equity (non-controlling, non-voting instrument led by Bain Capital, alongside Trust Group and Investec).
- Signed a six-year long-term supply agreement with a U.S.-based global specialty metal manufacturer, marking entry into the geological resources sector.
- Commercialized ETFA using continuous flow chemistry.
- Appointed industry veteran Rahul Adakmol (ex-DRL, Bharat Serums) as CEO of Bliss GVS.
- Achieved "Strong" CRISIL ESG rating of 62 (up from 54 "Adequate" previously) and Core ESG rating of 63.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guides H2 FY27 consolidated revenue at Rs 2,400 crore to Rs 2,500 crore, translating to an annualized run-rate of Rs 4,800 crore to Rs 5,000 crore across Anupam, Jayhawk, Tanfac, and Bliss.
- Management expects near-term 3-to-5-year organic growth of 20% to 25%.
- Maintained average consolidated EBITDA margin guidance at 22% to 24%, cautioning against over-extrapolating Q2 standalone margins (34%).
- Management expects revenue cyclicality to reduce over the next 4 to 8 quarters as Pharma and Performance Materials contribution grows.
- The new U.S. specialty metal contract is expected to contribute Rs 100 crore to Rs 150 crore in the remainder of FY27, with revenue potential doubling over 12–18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Seeing strong demand traction across high-value businesses including polymers, electronic chemicals, EV, semiconductor, defense, and fluorinated products.
- Global customers are increasingly demanding integrated, full value-chain offerings with dual manufacturing bases (onshore U.S. and offshore/nearshore India/Africa/Japan).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin improvement in Q2 FY27 driven by a higher contribution from Performance Materials and favorable product mix.
- Raw material price volatility (e.g., sulfuric acid moving from Rs 7 to Rs 45/kg, consumed at 250 metric tonnes per day) is passed through to customers without impacting EBITDA margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commercialized the new U.S. specialty metal product within 4 months utilizing existing multipurpose plant (MPP) capacities without additional capex.
- Bliss GVS plant capacity utilization is currently in the 40s%.
- Actively searching for API asset targets to complete the pharma value chain.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents on consolidated balance sheet stood at ~Rs 750 crore, retained across entities for working capital and minor capex.
- Bliss GVS acquisition debt taken at wholly-owned subsidiary level carries a single-digit interest rate without initial-year servicing pressure on target entities.
- Consolidated working capital cycle (excluding Bliss GVS balance sheet) improved from 276 days to 271 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Prior year base contained a one-time standalone sale of ~Rs 180 crore in Q2 FY26.
- Operational integration of recent M&A acquisitions (Jayhawk and Bliss GVS) across reporting, supply chain, and financials remains ongoing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Nitish Rege, ChrysCapital):** Are you exploring API acquisitions, and what is the steady-state margin for Bliss GVS?
  - **A:** Management confirmed active evaluation of API targets (no public announcements yet). Steady-state consolidated EBITDA margin for Bliss and Anupam is guided at 22%-24%, despite Bliss delivering higher margins recently due to high-margin product mix.
- **Q (Tanya Chowdhary):** Why maintain margin guidance at 24-26% when standalone is 34%, and what is the rationale behind recent aggressive M&A?
  - **A:** High Q2 standalone margin was due to product mix variations, requiring caution. M&A strategy focuses on building an integrated platform across chemistries (HF/KF to polymers/formulations) and geographies (India/U.S.).
- **Q (Meet Vora):** What driven the 6-year specialty metal contract win and what is the revenue outlook?
  - **A:** Technical speed (4-month turnaround) and available MPP capacity won the contract against peers. Expected to generate Rs 100-150 crore in the balance of FY27 and double in run-rate over 1-1.5 years.
- **Q (Hardik):** What is the Bliss acquisition debt structure and Elementium LOI progress?
  - **A:** Acquisition funded via Rs 1,450 crore quasi-equity (long-term buyout option) and Rs 300 crore single-digit interest term loan at subsidiary level. Elementium contract terms are being finalized; monthly schedule supplies have commenced.
- **Q (Burhanuddin Solanki):** What specific margin-improvement initiatives are being implemented at Jayhawk?
  - **A:** Three key interventions: raw material process optimization (solvents/acids), sourcing leverage from India via Anupam's scale, and effluent treatment cost reductions.

### One-line takeaway
Anupam Rasayan is transforming into a multi-geography integrated specialty chemical and pharma platform, targeting H2 FY27 consolidated revenues of Rs 2,400–2,500 crore following its acquisition of Bliss GVS.
