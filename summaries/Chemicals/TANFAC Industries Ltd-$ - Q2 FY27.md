# TANFAC Industries Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly revenue and raised full-year growth guidance, citing strong demand in solar grade DHF and upcoming commissioning of the HFC-32 project.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 230 crore (Q2), up 37% YoY; Rs 418 crore (H1), up 21% YoY.
*   **PAT:** Rs 19 crore (Q2), up 11% YoY; Rs 36 crore (H1), flat YoY.
*   **Gross Margin:** 32.5% (Q2) vs 39% (Q2 FY26).
*   **Fundraise:** Rs 350 crore raised in H1 FY27 (Rs 250 crore QIP + Rs 100 crore preferential issue).
*   **Capex Incurred:** Rs 360 crore (H1 FY27).

### Highlights
*   Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 230 crore.
*   Increased solar grade DHF capacity from 20,000 MTPA to 25,000 MTPA via debottlenecking.
*   HFC-32 refrigerant gas project is 90% complete; commissioning targeted for Q3 FY27.
*   Management expects H2 FY27 to outperform H1 due to new product scale-up and HFC-32 contribution.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Revised FY27 guidance to 35-40% (previously 30%). FY28 growth projected at 55-60% over FY27.
*   **Margins:** EBITDA margin expected to normalize to 15-16% in Q3, 18% in H2, and 24-26% in FY28.
*   **Capex:** Total FY27 capex estimated at Rs 440-450 crore. FY28 capex guided at minimum Rs 250 crore for AHF and solar grade expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Solar grade DHF demand remains robust, driven by the expansion of Indian solar cell manufacturing (expected to reach 100 GW capacity in 3 years).
*   HFC-32 demand is steady; management sees no significant threat from imports as domestic prices are competitive.
*   Management does not anticipate immediate competition in solar grade DHF due to high capital requirements and technical barriers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Gross margin percentage compression attributed to higher product realizations (increasing the revenue base) and a 45-day lag in passing on raw material cost inflation (sulphur prices rose 5x).
*   Fluorspar inventory increased to 6-7 months (from 3 months) as a precautionary measure against geopolitical supply chain risks.
*   Sulphur prices are showing signs of moderation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Near-term:** Commissioning HFC-32 in Q3 FY27.
*   **Medium-term:** Plans to expand AHF capacity from 30,000 MTPA to 45,000 MTPA and solar grade DHF capacity from 25,000 MTPA to 50,000 MTPA.
*   **Long-term:** Evaluating greenfield projects for electronic chemicals, fluoropolymers, and next-generation gases.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Working capital days increased from 91 to 104, primarily due to higher inventory (fluorspar) and timing of receivables (70% of increased receivables collected in October).
*   Management expects working capital to normalize in coming quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia impacting international supply chains and transit times.
*   Lag in passing on raw material price volatility to customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did margins compress?** Management clarified that absolute margins remain intact; the percentage decline is due to a higher revenue base (denominator effect) and a 45-day lag in cost pass-through.
*   **Q: What is the status of R-32 utilization?** Management expects 20% utilization in Q4 FY27 and 95-100% in FY28.
*   **Q: How will you fund future capex?** Management stated they have Rs 157 crore unutilized from recent fundraises and will use internal accruals/debt for future projects.
*   **Q: Any competition in solar grade?** Management stated no immediate competition is expected due to the Rs 90-100 crore capex requirement and 1-year gestation period.
*   **Q: How is the R-32 contract mix?** Management indicated a 65-35% mix of formula-based contracts and spot sales.

### One-line takeaway
TANFAC delivered record revenue and raised growth guidance, banking on solar grade DHF demand and the imminent commissioning of its HFC-32 project to drive significant margin expansion in FY28.
