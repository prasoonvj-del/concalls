# India Glycols Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong top-line and EBITDA growth supported by scaling specialty materials and performance chemicals post-demerger.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained long-term aspirations (~INR 2,000 crore revenue and ~INR 400 crore EBITDA in 4-5 years for the chemicals business; 10x in sales and EBITDA in 10 years).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to acknowledge short-term margin headwinds in performance chemicals due to high raw material costs (propylene oxide, lauryl alcohol, phenol) and geopolitical/war impacts, though top-line volume growth remains robust.
- New topics or risks: Detailed post-demerger reporting structure (Bio-based Specialty Materials and Sustainable Performance Chemicals); explicit discussion of Middle East war disruptions and U.S. ethanol-linked bio-glycol pricing pressures.
- Last quarter's promises: The 3-way demerger was completed, and this call marks the first investor meeting for the new pure-play India Glycols Limited (Chemicals).

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Revenue: INR 358 crore (up 30% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: INR 713 crore (up 26% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: INR 52 crore (up 26% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR 113 crore (up 25% YoY)
- Bio-based Specialty Materials H1 Revenue: INR 382 crore (up 43% YoY), EBITDA INR 62 crore (up 15% YoY)
- Performance Chemicals Q2 Revenue: INR 167 crore (up 36% YoY), H1 Revenue INR 313 crore (up 29% YoY)
- Performance Chemicals Q2 EBITDA: INR 24 crore (up 49% YoY), H1 EBITDA INR 51 crore (up 40% YoY)
- Performance Chemicals Q2 EBITDA Margin: 14.2% (vs 13.0% YoY); H1 margin at 16.3% (vs 15.0% YoY)
- Volume growth: Q2 volume up ~10% YoY, up ~3% QoQ
- Long-term aspirations: INR 2,000 crore revenue and INR 400 crore EBITDA in 4 to 5 years

### Highlights
- Completed the 3-way corporate demerger into IGL Spirits Limited, Ennature Biopharma, and India Glycols Limited (Chemicals).
- Chemicals business restructured into two reporting segments: Bio-based Specialty Materials and Sustainable Performance Chemicals.
- Bio-based specialty materials led growth driven by glycols and glycol ethers; performance chemicals saw top-line volume scale up significantly despite cost pressures.
- Logistically advantaged site with direct rail connectivity and an on-site ICD, aiding cost-effective domestic and export movements.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aspires to scale up to 10x in sales and EBITDA in 10 years.
- Management aims for about INR 2,000 crore revenue and about INR 400 crore EBITDA in the next 4 to 5 years.
- Specialty Materials expected to deliver steady near-, mid-, and long-term growth.
- Performance Chemicals to act as an important mid- to long-term growth engine with a robust new product and technology pipeline.

### Demand & industry commentary
- End markets (packaging, textiles, oil & gas, coatings, personal care, agro, pharma, electronics, food & beverage, construction, polymers) are growing faster than India's GDP.
- Crude oil price volatility and Middle East supply disruptions created headwinds for raw material availability and export freight, but created domestic tailwinds for glycol ethers as imports became expensive.
- U.S. crude/ethanol price linkage compressed bio-glycol margins temporarily when crude rose.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Single biggest raw material is ethanol, followed by coal for energy. Other key raw materials include propylene oxide, lauryl alcohol, and phenol, which saw high price spikes.
- Employee costs reduced due to lower salary/commission allocation for the Chairman.
- Bio-glycol margins faced pressure due to pricing tied to U.S.-imported ethanol amidst rising input costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital work-in-progress (CWIP) closing balance stands at INR 114 crore across 14-15 ongoing projects.
- Capacity enhancement projects (including amine manufacturing and asset modifications) are largely on track for completion between December 2026 and June 2027.
- Small debottlenecking investments ongoing to support high-demand areas in new performance chemicals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total long-term debt allocated to India Glycols is INR 450 crore, with annual repayments of about INR 125 crore.
- Working capital cash credit (CC) limit allocated at INR 300 crore, alongside non-fund-based limits for bank guarantees and LCs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts and war causing sharp crude volatility and supply disruptions from the Middle East.
- High price spikes in raw materials like propylene oxide, lauryl alcohol, and phenol impacting product viability.
- High export freight costs affecting international order execution and customer switching ability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Balasubramanian (Arihant Capital) on Performance Chemicals margins vs headwinds:** Management clarified that despite a tough operating environment and lower percentage margins, they aggressively chased top-line volume to fill new capacities, expecting margin enrichment as corrections happen.
2. **Balasubramanian (Arihant Capital) on ethanol sourcing from IGL Spirits:** Management stated that the two entities operate at arm's length; IGL Chemicals chooses the best-priced sourcing option (imported vs. domestic/sister company) without added risk.
3. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.) on employee cost drop:** Management explained the drop was due to lower salary and commission allocation for the Chairman, establishing INR 17-17.5 crore as the normalized quarterly run-rate.
4. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.) on CWIP and capex:** Management noted INR 114 crore CWIP comprises 14-15 projects (like amine capacities) slated for completion between December 2026 and June 2027.
5. **Sarthak Jain (Desvelado Advisory) on new specialty unit utilization:** Management stated the unit runs on multiple flexible equipment sets rather than fixed capacity utilization, with certain areas running full and requiring debottlenecking.

### One-line takeaway
India Glycols successfully completed its restructuring into a pure-play chemical entity, delivering robust 30% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 despite margin pressures from raw material volatility.
