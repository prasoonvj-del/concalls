# Transpek Industry Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-25 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management acknowledges near-term pricing pressures and revenue/EBITDA decline but expresses confidence in long-term growth through new product development and capacity expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 155.1 crore (down 6.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 24.1 crore (down 32.4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.6%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 8.9 crore.
*   **Revenue Mix:** Polymers (48.7%), Specialty Chemicals (22.5%), Pharma (10.11%), Others (18.7%).
*   **International Revenue:** 84.4% (North America >52%).

### Highlights
*   Company remains net debt-free with strong cash reserves.
*   DuPont contract successfully assigned to Arclin; business continues with no changes to terms.
*   Renewed Responsible Care certification (valid July 2026–July 2029).
*   Increased revenue contribution from specialty chemicals, organic peroxides, dyes, and surfactants.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects 15-20% revenue growth for FY27 YoY.
*   Aspiration to double business size in the next 5 years.
*   Targeting 15-20% EBITDA margins.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Global environment remains complex due to geopolitical tensions, energy price volatility, and trade uncertainties.
*   Indian chemical industry showing resilience; global customers are increasingly seeking diversified sourcing.
*   Pricing pressures from Indian and global competitors are impacting the industry.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins impacted by aggressive pricing, oversupply, and subdued demand in export markets.
*   DuPont/Arclin contract is formula-driven (pass-through for raw materials).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Odisha Expansion:** Planning a new manufacturing site; feasibility report in progress.
*   **Pilot Plant:** Setting up a multi-purpose pilot plant (4 streams) at Ekalbara, expected to be ready in ~6 months.
*   **R&D:** Doubling R&D team and infrastructure to accelerate product development.
*   **Capex Guidance:** ~Rs 250 crore over 5-6 years.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Currently ~70-75% (due to product mix/permission constraints), though operating at full permission levels.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Company is net debt-free.
*   Silox investment (~Rs 300 crore) is illiquid; management is attempting to monetize but faces constraints due to the shareholders' agreement.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions (West Asia) impacting logistics and global demand.
*   Pricing pressure from competitors in acid chloride and other segments.
*   Regulatory hurdles for expansion at existing Ekalbara site.
*   Uncertainty regarding long-term contract renewal (though management sees no reason for non-renewal).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS):** Questioned lack of growth since FY19 and suggested a buyback. Management defended the strategy, citing industry cycles and the need to invest in growth rather than buybacks.
*   **Sunil Kothari:** Asked about capacity utilization and expansion. Management explained that capacity is product-specific and limited by permissions, necessitating new sites like Odisha.
*   **Samarth Singh:** Questioned the return on past capex. Management clarified that past capex was largely for safety, replacement, and captive thionyl chloride production, not growth.
*   **Prashant:** Suggested better disclosure in presentations. Management agreed to improve transparency while maintaining confidentiality.

### One-line takeaway
Transpek is navigating a volatile chemical market by pivoting toward high-value, complex chemistries and greenfield expansion in Odisha, while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet.
