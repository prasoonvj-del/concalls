# Gulshan Polyols Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong Q2/H1 profitability and cash flow, but management cautioned on near-term margin pressure in Q3 due to record open-market maize prices.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 targets (Consolidated revenue ~Rs 2,600 crore, EBITDA margin ~10%, PAT margin ~5%). Initiated FY28 revenue guidance of Rs 2,800 crore to Rs 3,000 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Near-term margin caution expressed for Q3 due to sharp spikes in maize prices; H1 profitability buffer provides comfort to achieve full-year commitments.
- **New topics or risks:** Sharp escalation in open-market maize prices (Rs 30–32/kg due to El Niño impact); shift in feedstock strategy towards FCI rice and damaged food grains (DFG); new Rs 100–120 crore expansion in Muzaffarnagar for Sorbitol and MDP/NDC; 6 MW green hydrogen BOO partnership.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Trident PCC plant timeline refined to commissioning in April 2027 (generating revenue starting FY28).

---

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 614 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 13% YoY); H1 FY27 Total Income at Rs 1,260 crore (up 11% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 69 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 63% YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA at Rs 159 crore (up 97% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.2% in Q2 FY27 (up 338 bps YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA margin at 12.7% (up 558 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 39 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 151% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT at Rs 92 crore (up 222% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 6.3% in Q2 FY27 (up 347 bps YoY); H1 FY27 PAT margin at 7.3% (up 481 bps YoY).
- **Segment Revenue (Q2 FY27):**
  - Ethanol: Rs 427 crore; EBITDA Rs 62.5 crore; EBITDA margin 14.6%.
  - Grain Processing: Rs 166 crore (up 24% YoY); EBITDA Rs 2.45 crore; EBITDA margin 1.5%.
  - Mineral Chemical: Rs 22.7 crore; EBITDA Rs 4.6 crore; EBITDA margin 20.2%.

---

### Highlights
- Secured an OMC ethanol allocation of 19.7 crore liters, generating an ethanol order book of Rs 1,300 crore plus ~Rs 300 crore from byproducts (total ~Rs 1,600 crore).
- Country liquor order received of ~Rs 100 crore for FY27.
- Received a capital subsidy of Rs 5 crore from Assam under the NEIDS policy in Q2, which was reduced from capital assets in the balance sheet (no P&L impact).

---

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Consolidated Guidance:** Revenue ~Rs 2,600 crore (Ethanol: Rs 1,700–1,800 crore; Grain Processing: ~Rs 800 crore; Mineral Processing: ~Rs 100 crore); EBITDA margin 10%; PAT margin ~5%.
- **FY28 Consolidated Target:** Revenue of Rs 2,800 crore to Rs 3,000 crore.
- **Target Ethanol Deliveries:** Expecting to execute ~22 crore liters in ESY 2026–27, with plans to debottleneck capacities to reach 24–25 crore liters.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural opportunity in Indian ethanol remains intact driven by the Ethanol Blending Program and energy security focus.
- Maize prices are at an all-time high in India (Rs 30 to Rs 32/kg) due to agricultural crop impact from El Niño. Industry bodies have represented to the government to reduce import duty on maize.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Current OMC ethanol feedstock allocation mix: 25% FCI rice, 10% damaged food grains (DFG), and 65% maize.
- Company is recalibrating feedstock bidding away from high-cost open-market maize towards DFG and FCI rice.
- Open-market maize prices above Rs 26–27/kg make standalone maize-ethanol unviable, but high DDGS realizations provide partial cushion.
- Q3 EBITDA margins expect downward pressure (guided around 9%–11%), but H1 cushion will help sustain full-year margins.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Muzaffarnagar Expansion:** Investing Rs 100–120 crore to set up a 30,000 MTPA Sorbitol plant (taking total capacity to 1,10,000 MTPA) and expanding Maltodextrin Powder (MDP) / Non-Dairy Creamer (NDC) capacity 6-fold from 5,000 MTPA to 30,000 MTPA.
  - Target commissioning: May 2027.
  - Peak revenue potential: Rs 200–250 crore at ~10% EBITDA margin (initial year potential: Rs 120–130 crore; 2-3 years to reach optimal utilization).
- **Green Hydrogen Plant:** Partnered with Zero Footprint Industry Limited under a Build-Own-Operate (BOO) model for a 6 MW green hydrogen plant at Muzaffarnagar under a 10-year contract valued at Rs 300 crore. Expected to reduce hydrogen sourcing costs by ~20%.
- **Trident PCC Plant:** On-site Precipitated Calcium Carbonate (PCC) slurry plant (22,000 MTPA) at Trident Ltd in Barnala, Punjab is on track for April 2027 commissioning. Expected annual revenue: ~Rs 20 crore (~Rs 200 crore over 10 years).
- Capital work-in-progress (CWIP) stands at ~Rs 15 crore across Muzaffarnagar, Bharuch, and Borgaon.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt reduced to ~Rs 233 crore as of Q2 FY27 (down from Rs 312 crore at end-FY26).
- Long-term term debt currently at ~Rs 123 crore. Term debt with HSBC (balance two installments of Rs 10.2 crore) will be fully repaid by February 2027, leaving only ~Rs 90 crore debt with SBI.
- Planned capex of Rs 120 crore for Muzaffarnagar will be funded via Rs 100 crore long-term debt and Rs 20 crore internal accruals.
- Cash flow from operations in H1 FY27 stood at Rs 119 crore, mainly used for debt reduction.
- Inventory increased by ~Rs 60 crore in Q2 due to advance procurement of grain feedstock to hedge against rising market prices.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unprecedented high maize prices (Rs 30–32/kg) creating near-term pressure on ethanol operating margins in Q3/Q4.
- Ongoing overcapacity in domestic starch processing leading to weak realizations/margins in the standalone starch business.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Het Pradhan (Damani Family Office) on raw material procurement strategy:**  
   *Management Answer:* Current allocation is 25% FCI rice, 10% DFG, and balance maize. Company can request OMCs to switch allocation from maize to DFG or FCI rice to avoid unviable maize prices. (Did not dodge)
2. **Chinmay Madgulkar (Ageas Federal Life Insurance) on grain processing segment expansion:**  
   *Management Answer:* New expansion at Muzaffarnagar replaces low-margin starch with Sorbitol, MDP, and Non-Dairy Creamer (NDC), targeting Rs 200–250 crore peak revenue with 10% EBITDA margins. Funded via Rs 100 crore debt and Rs 20 crore internal accruals. (Did not dodge)
3. **Dhruv Patel (Vertex Capital) on maize price sensitivity:**  
   *Management Answer:* Maize price beyond Rs 26–27/kg becomes unviable for ethanol; current high DDGS prices provide essential cushion. (Did not dodge)
4. **Nitin Awasthi (InCred Equities) on green hydrogen plant details:**  
   *Management Answer:* Settled on an on-site BOO satellite plant with Zero Footprint Industry under a 10-year Rs 300 crore contract, saving ~20% vs buying from open market, as Muzaffarnagar lacks nearby caustic soda pipelines unlike Bharuch. (Did not dodge)

---

### One-line takeaway
Gulshan Polyols delivered strong Q2/H1 FY27 operational growth and debt reduction, maintaining its full-year guidance while proactively shifting ethanol feedstock away from record-high maize prices and adding downstream starch derivative capacity.
