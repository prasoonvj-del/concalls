# Gulshan Polyols Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 performance with 97% EBITDA growth and 222% PAT growth, despite near-term margin pressure from record-high maize prices.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Revenue ~Rs 2,600 cr, EBITDA margin 10%, PAT margin ~5%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "favorable operating conditions" (Q1) to "near-term pressure on operating economics" due to record-high maize prices.
- **New topics:** Strategic partnership with Zero Footprint Industry Ltd for a 6 MW green hydrogen plant; specific expansion details for Muzaffarnagar (Sorbitol + MDP/NDC); Trident PCC plant commissioning timeline.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The company is executing on its strategy to shift from starch to higher-value derivatives (Sorbitol/MDP/NDC) and is actively managing feedstock mix to mitigate raw material volatility.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27:** Revenue Rs 1,255 cr (+11% YoY), EBITDA Rs 159 cr (+97% YoY), PAT Rs 92 cr (+222% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27:** Revenue Rs 614 cr (+13% YoY), EBITDA Rs 69 cr (+63% YoY), PAT Rs 39 cr (+151% YoY).
- **Ethanol Order Book:** Rs 1,600 cr (Rs 1,300 cr allocation + Rs 300 cr byproducts).
- **Debt:** Rs 233 cr (down from Rs 312 cr in FY26).

### Highlights
- Secured 19.7 crore liters ethanol allocation.
- Initiated Muzaffarnagar expansion: Sorbitol (+30,000 MTPA) and MDP/NDC (+25,000 MTPA to 30,000 MTPA total).
- Strategic partnership with Zero Footprint Industry Ltd for a 6 MW green hydrogen plant (10-year contract, Rs 300 cr value).
- PCC facility at Trident (Barnala) on track for April 2027 commissioning.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27:** Revenue target ~Rs 2,600 cr (Ethanol: Rs 1,700-1,800 cr; Grain: ~Rs 800 cr; Mineral: ~Rs 100 cr).
- **FY27 Margins:** EBITDA 10%, PAT ~5%.
- **FY28 Revenue Target:** Rs 2,800 cr - Rs 3,000 cr.
- **New Capacity Potential:** Rs 200-250 cr annual revenue at optimal levels (2-3 year ramp-up).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Ethanol remains the primary growth engine.
- Maize prices at historic highs (Rs 30-32/kg); management noted prices >Rs 26-27/kg make ethanol production unviable.
- Industry seeking import duty relief on maize.
- Focus shifting to FCI rice and damaged food grains (DFG) to mitigate maize price impact.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin 11.2% (up 338 bps YoY).
- Inventory increased by ~Rs 60 cr due to strategic stocking ahead of price spikes.
- Current feedstock mix: 25% FCI rice, 10% DFG, balance maize.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Muzaffarnagar expansion: Rs 100-120 cr investment (funded by internal accruals and debt), commissioning May 2027.
- Debottlenecking ethanol plants to reach 24-25 crore liters capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt reduced to Rs 233 cr; targeting ~Rs 125 cr by March 2027.
- Operating cash flow H1: Rs 119 cr.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High maize prices impacting near-term margins.
- Commodity price sensitivity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Het Pradhan (Damani Family Office):** Asked about feedstock mix. Management confirmed 25% FCI rice, 10% DFG, balance maize, with flexibility to switch.
- **Chinmay Madgulkar (Ageas Federal):** Asked about grain processing profitability. Management noted starch overcapacity is the issue; new expansion targets higher-value Sorbitol/NDC.
- **Dhruv Patel (Vertex Capital):** Asked about maize price sensitivity. Management confirmed >Rs 26-27/kg is unviable.
- **Nitin Awasthi (InCred Equities):** Asked about debt/capex. Management confirmed debt reduction and new Rs 100 cr loan for expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Gulshan Polyols delivered strong H1 growth and maintained FY27 guidance, pivoting to higher-value derivatives and green hydrogen to mitigate record-high maize costs.
