# Stallion India Fluorochemicals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company started FY27 on a positive note with strong YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by better product mix, logistics execution, and inventory planning.

### Key numbers
- Total revenue: Rs 124.68 crore (up 12.78% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 25.27 crore (up 75.85% YoY)
- PAT: Rs 18.57 crore (up 79.15% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: ~17% (Q1 FY27)
- R32 current prices: ~Rs 800 per kg (peak was Rs 900 per kg)
- Helium current prices: ~Rs 4,000 per kg
- Helium plant installed capacity: 1,200 metric tonnes per annum
- R32 manufacturing facility capacity (Bhilwara): 10,000 metric tonnes

### Highlights
- Completed preparations for the high-purity helium processing plant at the Khalapur facility; commercial operations expected to begin in the coming quarter.
- Mambattu facility (Andhra Pradesh) progress shifted to year-end completion as engineering teams were moved to prioritize the Bhilwara plant.
- Bhilwara R32 manufacturing facility completion targeted for the end of December 2026 (delayed from original timelines due to a late funding start following a shift from a preferential issue to a rights issue).
- Advanced stages of negotiation for an ISRO tender (tenders typically around 40,000-50,000 cubic meters of gas).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains revenue CAGR guidance of 30-35% over the next three years.
- EBITDA margins expected to improve by 3% to 4% over the medium term as backward integration and higher-value specialty gases increase.
- Peak revenue potential exceeding Rs 1,100 crore with a minimum ~15% PAT margin once Khalapur, R32, and Mambattu are operational (around FY28).
- Helium plant expected to contribute ~12% in its first year, starting with about 5 containers in the current year, increasing next year to 12, then 24.
- Core business PAT margin expected to be a shade lower than 10% for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Helium demand is projected to see exponential growth with a 2-3 year outlook of shortfall and higher pricing.
- Total Indian demand for R32 is ~20,000 tonnes against upcoming domestic manufacturing capacities of 70,000-90,000 tonnes, necessitating an export drive, blends, and tie-ups.
- China's GWP quota reductions starting January 1, 2028, are expected to globally impact the market positively.
- Many R32 plants are swing plants that can switch production to other high-GWP products (like 134a or 404a) based on pricing dynamics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 gross and EBITDA margins benefited 50:50 from better product mix (higher HFO percentage), earlier planning, logistics strain from the Gulf crisis, and historical high stock-keeping (inventory gains). Management noted these record Q1 margins are not fully sustainable every quarter.
- AHF and MDC are raw materials for backward integration; management expects a glut of AHF supply globally by 2028.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Khalapur high purity helium processing plant (1,200 MTPA): Commercial operations expected in Q2.
- Mambattu facility: Redesigned from a 5-tank to a 12-tank structure with hydrocarbon, semiconductor, and helium capabilities. Target completion by year-end 2026.
- Bhilwara R32 plant: Target completion by end of December 2026; estimated capex for subsequent HFO plant around Rs 350-400 crore (10,000 tonnes capacity).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management is evaluating potential capital raising (debt and/or equity dilution) to fund faster execution of future growth projects (like the HFO plant) rather than relying solely on internal accruals and pacing growth over a longer timeframe.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Logistics and import strains due to the Gulf crisis affecting pricing.
- R32 prices currently on a downswing (around Rs 800/kg down from a peak of Rs 900/kg).
- Potential shipping blocks from the Middle East for helium mitigated via strategic swapping mechanisms with partners like Sharjah Oxygen.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Disha asked about the sustainability of Q1 margins and inventory gains.** Management stated it was a 50:50 mix of product mix/better planning and inventory/stock-keeping gains, and while margins will improve over the medium term due to manufacturing, the specific Q1 spike may not repeat continually.
- **Paras Chheda asked if the R32 revenue contribution of Rs 250 crore for FY27 remains intact given the December delay.** Management answered that it would change slightly, with revenue contribution shifting mainly to Q4.
- **Arindam Dutta asked about working capital and fundraising plans.** Management noted they initially desired no dilution, but to maintain a "scorching pace" of growth and fund upcoming projects like HFO without extending timelines to 6 years, they may evaluate equity dilution alongside debt.
- **Bhavika Singhvi asked about pre-sold capacities and customer contracts for the R32 plant.** Management stated they do not pre-sell via loose MOUs because it limits upside in rising markets and lacks enforceability if prices fall, choosing instead to rely on internal captive use (Mambattu facility) and open market demand.
- **Dhwanil Shah asked about helium capacity utilization targets for next year.** Management clarified that while capacity is 1,200 metric tonnes, utilization will ramp up incrementally (targeting around 20% next year) rather than hitting full capacity immediately.

### One-line takeaway
Stallion India delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with a 75.8% surge in EBITDA, while advancing key capacity expansions in helium, R32, and HFO blending to drive medium-term growth.
