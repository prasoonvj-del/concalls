# Pidilite Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance driven by double-digit revenue and PAT growth, robust underlying volume growth, and stable domestic demand.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 4,541 crore, up 21.3% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** Improved by 120 bps YoY.
- **Consolidated PAT Growth:** 30.3% YoY.
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 4,237 crore, up 22.2% YoY.
- **Standalone Underlying Volume Growth (UVG):** 11.3% overall (Consumer & Bazaar: 12.2%, B2B: 7.3%).
- **Standalone B2B Exports UVG:** -8.4% YoY.
- **Standalone Gross Margin:** 52.5%, down 90 bps YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 26.4%, improved by 80 bps QoQ.
- **Standalone PAT Growth:** 27.7% YoY.
- **Domestic Subsidiaries Revenue Growth:** 11.5% YoY (Consumer & Bazaar up 17%, B2B up 3.5%).
- **International Subsidiaries Revenue Growth:** 12% YoY.
- **VAM Consumption Cost:** $1,370/tonne in Q1 FY27 vs $924/tonne YoY and ~$800/tonne in Q4 FY26.
- **Dividend:** Rs 11.50 per share approved by shareholders.

### Highlights
- Double-digit top-line growth across both domestic and international subsidiaries (notably Bangladesh, Egypt, and Kenya).
- Total costs below gross margin grew 14.5% YoY, slower than revenue growth, providing operating leverage.
- Launched core technological innovations: Fevicol X-PER with anti-bending technology for woodworking and M-Seal Advanced (lower VOC, low odor, washable).
- NioPro premium tile adhesive range seeing increased traction, expanding manufacturing from 1 plant to 4 plants.
- UnoFin architectural coatings JV seeing architectural acceptance in commercial and high-end residential projects.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its long-term EBITDA margin guidance corridor of 20% to 24%.
- Management expects margins in full-year FY27 to potentially sit in the middle-to-higher end of the 20–24% target band if raw material/crude volatility remains stable.
- Management aims to deliver double-digit underlying volume growth over the medium term (1x–2x real GDP growth for core categories and 2x–4x for growth categories).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic consumer demand remains steady with positive trends and no immediate signs of elasticity concerns post-price hikes.
- Tile adhesives penetration in India remains low at 25%–30%, providing substantial growth runway despite increasing competition from tile and cement companies.
- Waterproofing category (Dr. Fixit) accelerated into mid-teens plus volume growth momentum in Q1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- VAM input costs experienced high volatility, swinging from $800–$900 up to near $2,000/tonne before receding.
- Proactive price hikes implemented across categories covered raw material cost increases, though management absorbed a portion of margin hit.
- Management utilizes dynamic trade rebates in response to short-term raw material price declines to maintain channel fairness.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Production capacity for the NioPro joint venture product range is being expanded from 1 plant to 4 plants to increase geographic coverage.
- Continued investments in automated plant network to ensure product quality consistency and reduce total delivered cost.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Dividend of Rs 11.50 per share approved at the AGM.
- Detailed debt and working capital figures were not specified during the call.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions in key overseas markets resulted in a degrowth in B2B export volumes (-8.4%).
- High volatility in key raw materials, including VAM and crude oil derivatives.
- Rising competitive intensity in tile adhesives and waterproofing segments from regional and cement players.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama):** *How does management react to sharp volatility in VAM prices after taking price hikes?*  
  **Management:** Pricing was taken proactively based on replacement margins. Dynamic trade rebates are provided as input costs soften. Competitors closely mirror Pidilite's pricing and rebate strategy.
- **Jay Doshi (Kotak Securities):** *Is there any demand moderation in Consumer & Bazaar, and how is management countering new cement competition in tile adhesives?*  
  **Management:** Demand remains strong. Pidilite counters competition in tile adhesives through a wide plant network, consistent quality, total delivered cost optimization, and expanding NioPro across 4 plants.
- **Arnab Mitra (Goldman Sachs):** *Did low-cost inventory artificially boost Q1 gross margins, and will margins drop in Q2?*  
  **Management:** Q1 benefited from replacement pricing, carryover lower-cost inventory, and scheme moderation. Some of the lower-cost inventory benefit will unravel in Q2 as higher-priced material is consumed, but normalized H1 margins remain sound.
- **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital):** *With a 26.4% EBITDA margin in Q1, will full-year margins trend near the upper end of guidance?*  
  **Management:** If crude stays in the mid-80s and volatility remains low, margins could easily end up in the middle-to-higher end of the 20%–24% guidance band.

### One-line takeaway
Pidilite delivered strong Q1 FY27 performance with 21.3% consolidated revenue growth and high volume momentum, maintaining its 20–24% EBITDA margin target band.
