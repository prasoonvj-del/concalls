# Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong YoY growth in revenue and PAT, robust demand, and progress in high-value material platforms despite temporary margin fluctuations due to freight costs and raw material timing.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,691 crore (vs Rs 1,071 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 303 crore (up 27% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 231 crore (up 31% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 3,123 crore.
*   **H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 616 crore.
*   **H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT:** Rs 460 crore.
*   **EBITDA per metric tonne:** Rs 21,000 (vs Rs 17,900 in Q2 FY26).
*   **Carbon Black Capacity Utilisation:** 88%.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Expansion:** Incorporated Ardent Impex FZCO (Dubai) to enhance global sales, marketing, and procurement.
*   **Birla Tyres:** Commercial production resumed May 2025; Board proposed demerger of tyre business from Dalmia Bharat Refractories into Himadri. Q2 revenue from this segment was Rs 140 crore with Rs 1 crore EBITDA.
*   **Mining:** Environmental clearance received in October 2026; operations expected to commence in Q3 FY27.
*   **Sicona/IBC:** Continued investment in Sicona Battery Technologies (which acquired Advano) and International Battery Company (IBC) to strengthen the lithium-ion battery value chain.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **PAT Target:** Management maintained guidance of Rs 1,100 crore+ PAT by FY28.
*   **EBITDA per tonne:** Management expects this to hover between Rs 20,000 and Rs 24,000.
*   **Capex:** Total spend for FY27 expected to be Rs 800 crore to Rs 900 crore.
*   **LFP Ramp-up:** Targeting 50%+ capacity utilisation in Year 1, moving to 90%+ in subsequent years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Demand:** Robust demand across segments; volume growth of 11% YoY.
*   **Geopolitics:** Management noted that while geopolitical tensions impacted freight rates (temporary phenomenon), the business model remains resilient.
*   **Export Mix:** Exports increased to 33% of total sales (vs 26% in Q2 FY26).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** EBITDA per tonne improved YoY due to focus on high-value-added products.
*   **Sequential Dip:** EBITDA per tonne dipped slightly from Rs 22,700-22,800 (sequential) to Rs 21,000 due to timing of raw material price pass-throughs and higher freight costs.
*   **Inventory:** Moderated to 9-10 days of raw material to manage volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Carbon Nanotubes (200 TPA):** Targeted for commissioning in Q4 FY27.
*   **Super Speciality Carbon Black (6,000 TPA):** Targeted for Q4 FY28.
*   **LFP Cathode (2,000 TPA):** Targeted for Q3 FY27 commissioning; 40,000 TPA expansion planned for next year.
*   **Anthraquinone & Carbazole (5,300 TPA):** Both phases to be commissioned together in Q4 FY27 to avoid shutdown impact.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Funding:** Management stated intent to fund capex through internal accruals, not debt.
*   **Working Capital:** Net working capital as % of sales was 37% in Q2 FY27 (down from 41% in Q1 FY27).
*   **Receivables:** 65 days (vs 63 days in Q1 FY27).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Freight Costs:** Skyrocketing container freight rates impacted margins in the current quarter.
*   **Raw Material Pass-through:** Timing mismatch between raw material price increases and customer price adjustments caused temporary margin compression.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the sequential dip in margins?** Management attributed it to higher freight costs and the timing of raw material price pass-throughs, emphasizing that YoY EBITDA per tonne is up.
*   **Q: Status of Anode technology?** Management stated the product quality (capacity per milliampere per gram) is "bang on target" and superior to benchmarks; they are currently in the engineering phase for full-scale capex.
*   **Q: Why defer the 40,000 TPA LFP plant?** Management explained it allows time for customer qualification and approvals so the plant can start with commercial capacity rather than committing capex ahead of the curve.
*   **Q: Will LFP/Anode pricing be in line with Chinese suppliers?** Management confirmed pricing must be in line with global/Chinese competitors.
*   **Q: Mining segment revenue potential?** Management expects ~Rs 500 crore top line from the sand mining business.

### One-line takeaway
Himadri is aggressively scaling its high-value battery material and speciality chemical portfolio using internal accruals, maintaining a long-term PAT target of Rs 1,100 crore+ by FY28 despite short-term margin volatility from freight and raw material timing.
