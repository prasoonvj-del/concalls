# Sigachi Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited steady operational improvements, better sales mix toward special/co-processed grades, and growing demand across segments.

### Key numbers
- Total Operating Income (Revenue): Rs 130.45 crore
- EBITDA: Rs 19.20 crore
- EBITDA Margin: 14.72%
- Net Profit (PAT): Rs 8.78 crore
- Net Profit Margin: 6.82%
- Segment Revenues: MCC Rs 91.22 crore, O&M Rs 13.93 crore, API Rs 16.67 crore
- Production/Sales Volume (MCC & allied): 7,128 metric tonnes (H1 FY27)
- Capacity Utilization: Dahej unit ~80%, Jhagadia unit ~79% (H1 FY27 average)
- Insurance Claim Receivable: ~Rs 49 crore total receivable (expecting 5-10% discount/settlement before December)
- CapEx Spent so far (MCC 12,000 MT + CCS facility): ~Rs 29-30 crore
- Total CapEx Required for upcoming projects: ~Rs 200 crore (MCC: ~Rs 100 crore, CCS: ~Rs 90 crore)

### Highlights
- MCC capacity expansion of 12,000 metric tonnes per annum at Dahej SEZ and the CCS facility at Dahej are both expected to be commissioned in Q2 FY28, taking total MCC capacity to ~30,000 metric tonnes per annum.
- Launched four new PureCoat coating portfolio offerings: PureCoat Flavor, PureCoat TiO2 Free, PureCoat Pearl, and PureCoat NP (using pullulan).
- Promoters cancelled the previously announced Rs 200 crore preferential issue following feedback from investors and well-wishers regarding dilution at the SEBI-calculated price of Rs 26.40, especially as the stock price subsequently rose to ~Rs 39.
- Lower capacity utilization during H1 (~80%) was attributed to planned shutdowns for installing advanced safety equipment in FBD and dryers following the Hyderabad plant accident.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised FY27 revenue guidance from ~Rs 650 crore to ~Rs 600 crore due to a slight deferment of new project commissioning (MCC expansion deferred to Q2 FY28).
- Management expects H2 FY27 EBITDA margins to improve to ~18% (balancing out to an overall lower full-year margin) driven by no increase in fixed overheads, higher revenue, and an improved sales mix toward special and co-processed grades.
- Management expects FY28 revenue to cross Rs 800 crore, aided by the new capacity coming online in Q2 FY28 adding over Rs 200 crore in top-line potential.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains higher than production; supply is currently the only constraint.
- No pricing pressure from over-competitiveness; company maintains high market competitiveness.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average price realization for MCC improved: Q1 FY27 at Rs 241.36, Q2 FY27 at Rs 262.83, and H1 average at Rs 252.16 (vs H1 FY26 average of Rs 216.42).
- Higher margin profile anticipated in H2 due to a shift towards high-margin co-processed variants and special grades (co-processed pricing is 3-4 times higher than normal grades).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dahej MCC 12,000 MTPA expansion and CCS facility expected in Q2 FY28.
- Total estimated remaining CapEx of ~Rs 200 crore to be funded via debt, equity, or internal accruals (not yet finalized; options open).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt currently stands at low/meager levels (reference to FY26 debt of ~Rs 140 crore). Bankers are supportive for future debt raising if needed.
- Insurance reimbursement of ~Rs 49 crore expected to be realized by November/December 2026 after a anticipated 5-10% discount.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Deferment of project commissioning by a few months (MCC expansion pushed to Q2 FY28).
- Uncertainty and time consumed in finalizing insurance claim payouts (discussions ongoing with a 5-10% discount indicated).
- Temporary lower capacity utilization due to mandatory safety upgrades and audits.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital) asked about the full-year revenue and margin guidance cut.** Management clarified that revenue guidance is trimmed from Rs 650 crore to ~Rs 600 crore due to project deferment, but H2 margins are expected to improve to ~18% due to fixed overhead absorption and better sales mix.
2. **Vansh Solanki (RSPN Ventures) questioned how H2 revenue can grow 40% given capacity constraints and lower H1 API revenue.** Management explained that Q2 dispatches were deferred to Q3 on customer request, existing plant capacity utilization will increase post-safety installations by mid-November, and new API products will scale up.
3. **Rupesh Tatiya (Long Equity Partners) asked why Sigachi's margins are moderate compared to peers and about API capacity/order book.** Management noted that realizations and margins are improving sequentially every quarter due to sales mix, and API has a 100 kL capacity capable of generating Rs 100-150 crore.
4. **Ankur Savariya asked about the status of the insurance claim and how CapEx will be funded post-cancellation of the preferential issue.** Management stated the insurance claim of ~Rs 49 crore (net of 10% discount) is expected by November/December, and future CapEx funding (debt/equity) will be announced at an appropriate time.
5. **Raghav asked why the preferential allotment of Rs 200 crore was cancelled.** Management explained that after announcing the issue at the SEBI-mandated price of Rs 26.40, the stock price rose to ~Rs 39, leading investors and well-wishers to question the heavy dilution at that price; the board called it off in the best interest of shareholders.
6. **Vansh Solanki asked why capacity utilization is stuck at 80% despite high demand.** Management explained that priority was given to safety audits and retrofitting advanced safety equipment into dryers and FBDs following the Hyderabad incident, which required staggered shutdowns.

### One-line takeaway
Sigachi Industries trimmed its FY27 revenue guidance to ~Rs 600 crore due to project deferments, but expects H2 margin recovery to ~18% driven by a richer sales mix and higher utilization once safety upgrades are fully completed in November.
