# Gujarat Alkalies and Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the highest quarterly and half-yearly sales in two decades, driven by improved realizations and operational efficiency.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,358 crore (vs Rs 1,083 crore YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 222 crore (vs Rs 133 crore YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.74% (vs 11.6% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 79 crore (vs Rs 16 crore YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 2,602 crore (vs Rs 2,188 crore YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 451 crore (vs Rs 258 crore YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 133 crore (vs Rs 24 crore YoY).
*   **ROCE (H1 FY27):** 8.52% (vs 1.87% in FY26).

### Highlights
*   Domestic sales grew 29% YoY; exports grew 14% YoY.
*   Renewable energy (RE) accounted for 55% of power consumption in Q2 (vs 38% YoY).
*   Joint Venture (GNAL) performance improved significantly: H1 PBT turned to a profit of Rs 8 crore (vs loss of Rs 36 crore YoY).
*   Management stated that European markets are becoming import-dependent, creating export opportunities for Indian producers.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Medium-term aspiration:** Management aims for >Rs 10,000 crore revenue and >20% EBITDA margin by FY 2031 (not near-term guidance).
*   **Strategy:** Focus on operational reliability, RE integration, cost management, and value-led market growth.
*   **Current Trend:** Management noted that market realizations post-September quarter are "relatively better" than Q2.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Pricing improvements seen in chlorotoluene, anhydrous aluminum chloride, phosphoric acid, hydrogen peroxide, and caustic soda.
*   Chlorine realization improved significantly during the quarter.
*   Management expects increased consumption of methylene chloride due to new refrigerant capacities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Average H1 power rate reduced to Rs 7.09/unit (vs Rs 7.6/unit YoY).
*   Targeting RE share of 80% and power cost <Rs 6/unit by FY30.
*   "Project Ahvaan" (strategic transformation) is focused on efficiency, procurement, and power cost reduction.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current capacity utilization:** ~92%.
*   **Upcoming commissioning:** 200 TPD caustic soda flaking (Dec 2026), 120 TPD HCl synthesis (Jan 2027), Chlorotoluene downstream (Mar 2027).
*   **Future pipeline:** Electronic-grade H2O2 (Jan 2028), KOH expansion/boilers (FY29).
*   **Chlorine integration:** Target to reach 75% integration in 2-3 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Invested Rs 32.33 crore in an SPV with Clean Max Sphere Energy for 62.7 MW renewable projects.
*   Increase in "other financial liabilities" attributed to dividend payments and capital goods purchases.
*   Funding for future capex will be a mix of internal accruals and borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Chemical business is cyclical; commodity prices, energy costs, and geopolitical developments impact performance.
*   Grid disturbances and plant shutdowns impacted some operations.
*   Large caustic soda capacities coming up in the industry.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How will the company handle large upcoming industry capacities?** A: Management stated they are focusing on cost optimization, market penetration, and diversifying product shifts to maintain competitiveness.
*   **Q: Can you clarify the power mix?** A: 43% is grid/captive (112 MW), 28% is short-term RE (52 MW), and 29% is own RE assets (53 MW).
*   **Q: What is the status of Hydrazine Hydrate?** A: It is a strategic product; management is focused on market development and ramp-up, preferring to evaluate performance over a longer horizon.
*   **Q: Are you installing batteries for RE?** A: Currently investigating battery-operated RE; details will be provided upon approval.
*   **Q: Why did profitability dip sequentially (Q1 vs Q2)?** A: Attributed to challenging market scenarios, geopolitical developments, and a low-wind period affecting RE generation.

### One-line takeaway
GACL delivered strong H1 growth driven by improved realizations and higher RE usage, while maintaining a clear, long-term strategic focus on chlorine integration and downstream value-added products.
