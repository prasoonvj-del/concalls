# Berger Paints India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - gross margins were compressed by double-digit raw material inflation and high-cost inventory liquidation, though strong decorative value growth and robust industrial demand provided support.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone revenue growth (YoY):** 11.9% (Q2), 12.3% (H1)
- **Consolidated revenue growth (YoY):** 13.2% (Q2), 12.5% (H1)
- **Consolidated PBDIT growth (YoY):** 5.2% (Q2), 11.1% (H1)
- **Standalone volume growth:** 3% (Q2), 5.6% (H1)
- **Decorative value growth:** 10%, driven by price growth of ~8%
- **Standalone gross margin:** 36.6% (Q2), 38.1% (H1)
- **Consolidated gross margin:** 39.3% (Q2), 40.1% (H1)
- **Standalone EBITDA margin:** 10.8% (Q2), 14.4% (H1)
- **Consolidated EBITDA margin:** 11.6% (Q2), 14.4% (H1)
- **Standalone PAT growth (YoY):** -7.3% (Q2), 13.2% (H1)
- **Consolidated PAT growth (YoY):** 5.2% (Q2), 19.3% (H1)
- **A&P campaign extra investment:** Rs 22 crore for "Rang Banara Rahe" campaign
- **Retail store network:** 2,000+ stores added as of H1 FY27
- **Tinting machine additions:** 4,800 machines added in H1 (2,700 added in Q2)
- **Automotive coatings growth:** ~31% (driven by two-wheeler demand)
- **BNPA revenue growth:** Over 40%

### Highlights
- Decorative business registered double-digit value growth driven by pricing; differentiated products (home seal, roof coolant seal, dam shield, wood coatings) outperformed.
- Eastern markets were impacted by extended rain; volume growth moderated due to festive demand shifting to Q3 (delayed Diwali) and a conscious mix improvement away from price-sensitive categories (putty, textures, enamel de-grew).
- New product launches include waterproof tile adhesive, decorative panels (imported technology from Bolix), long life parapet coat, and dam shield primer.
- Subsidiaries and JVs performed well: Bolix SA, BP Nepal, STP, Berger Rock, and Berger Becker registered strong revenue and profitability growth (though SBL and BNPA faced profit compression due to RM inflation).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to be close to the guided 15% to 17% EBITDA margin band in Q3 and Q4 through calibrated price increases, cost-saving initiatives, and softening crude prices.
- October saw the highest sales growth in the last 25-30 months due to festive demand; Q3 revenue growth is expected to be robust.
- Net cash balance sheet to be maintained.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industrial business delivered robust high double-digit volume and value growth across business lines (automotive, protective coatings, general industrial).
- New entrant has raised prices by 5% to match industry levels in DPN, but is rebating heavily through dealers, keeping competitive intensity high.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins impacted by double-digit raw material inflation, liquidation of high-cost inventory, and delayed price increases in the industrial segment.
- Operating margin compression was cushioned despite Rs 22 crore spent on the "Rang Banara Rahe" campaign.
- Industrial business faced a gross margin drop of ~5.5%; price hikes implemented in October and planned for November/December to recover 90%+ of cost increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Completed expansion at the Hindupur plant.
- West Bengal government announced a new industrial incentive policy; management will evaluate it to decide the investment scale for the Panagarh plant. Orissa plant also planned to meet future demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory and receivables saw a buildup driven by peak RM prices and planned inventory ahead of the large October/Diwali season.
- Company continues to remain cash positive on a consolidated basis.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile raw material and crude-linked prices affecting gross margins.
- Intense competition with high dealer rebating from new entrants.
- Extended rains impacting eastern markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rishi Mody (on inventory/receivables buildup and marketing spend):** Management explained the inventory buildup was planned for the large October festive season at peak RM prices. On marketing, management noted that brand building requires sustained spends with initial launch bursts rather than on-off switches.
- **Aniruddha Joshi (on urban strategy and cash deployment):** Management stated the urban "MEGA" experiment is yielding positive results and will scale in April. Surplus cash will fund upcoming factories in Panagarh (West Bengal) and Orissa without borrowing.
- **Aniruddha Joshi (on competitive intensity):** Management noted the new entrant matched industry pricing but uses higher dealer rebates, and Berger's market share remains stable at ~20.2%.
- **Pratik Gothi (on gross margins and industrial price hikes):** Management clarified that deco gross margin drop was lower than industrial, and industrial price recovery is ongoing with an aim to recover ~90% by end-November.
- **Sushil Chavan (on Tier 1 urban shift to panels/decoratives):** Management stated Berger focuses on exteriors and waterproofing (much larger segments) where opportunities remain robust, rather than high-end interior panels.

### One-line takeaway
Berger Paints reported a margin-compressed Q2 due to double-digit RM inflation and ad spends, but expects a strong Q3 recovery driven by festive demand and price hikes.
