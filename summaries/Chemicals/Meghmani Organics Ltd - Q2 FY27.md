# Meghmani Organics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Near-term margin pressure due to sharp increases in input, utility, and logistics costs, despite healthy YoY revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 658 crore, up 18% YoY (up 26% QoQ)
- **Standalone Net Profit:** Rs 45 crore, up 6% YoY
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 70.9 crore, flat YoY
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** Impacted by elevated input and logistics costs (segment-wise: Crop Protection at 11%, Pigments at 10%)
- **Crop Protection Segment:** Production 10,000 metric tonnes; capacity utilization 72%; Revenue Rs 496 crore; EBITDA Rs 54 crore
- **Pigment Segment:** Production 4,252 metric tonnes; capacity utilization 51%; Revenue Rs 161 crore; EBITDA Rs 16 crore
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,181 crore, up 3% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Net Profit:** Rs 103 crore, up 23% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 164 crore, up 9% YoY
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 666 crore, up 16% YoY
- **Consolidated Net Profit (Q2 FY27):** Rs 33.9 crore, up 193% YoY
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 73 crore, up 40% YoY; Margin at 11% (vs 9% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,209 crore, up 2% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Net Profit:** Rs 82.1 crore, up 239% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 171 crore, up 44% YoY; Margin at 14.1% (vs 10% YoY)
- **Standalone Total Debt:** Rs 497 crore (Short-term: Rs 435 crore, Long-term: Rs 62 crore); Debt-to-Equity: 0.27x
- **Consolidated Total Debt:** Rs 661 crore (Short-term: Rs 438 crore, Long-term: Rs 223 crore); Debt-to-Equity: 0.41x
- **Debt Repayment (up to Sept 30):** Approximately Rs 64 crore
- **Trade Receivables:** Days increased from 100-110 days to 125+ days.

### Highlights
- Received NCLT approval for the amalgamation of wholly-owned subsidiaries (Kilburn Chemicals Limited and Meghmani Crop Nutrition Limited) into Meghmani Organics Limited.
- Commenced production of Nano fertilizer products (Nano DAP, Nano NPK, Nano Zinc) at the Sanand facility under the Crop Nutrition segment.
- Titanium dioxide operations remain suspended due to commercial unviability (elevated sulfuric acid prices at 7-8x normal levels and weaker realizations post anti-dumping duty withdrawal), despite the Ministry of Finance re-imposing anti-dumping duty on imports from China on October 24, 2026 for 5 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects double-digit top-line growth for the Crop Protection segment over the next 2 to 3 years, with a long-term EBITDA margin target of 15% to 17%.
- Pigment segment top-line is expected to remain in the range of Rs 600 crore to Rs 650 crore, with an EBITDA margin target of 10% to 11%.
- Crop Nutrition segment expected to grow in double digits over the next 3 years with EBITDA margins in the range of 20% to 22%.
- Consolidated EBITDA margin expected to be in the range of 13% to 14%.
- Too early to provide guidance on the Titanium Dioxide segment until raw material scenarios improve.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Agrochemical demand supply is stable globally, but high raw material and utility volatility created margin pressure on manufacturers as price pass-through takes time.
- Europe is passing through a challenging time with impacted demand and high costs; the U.S. market maintains good demand and margins.
- Crop Nutrition (Nano fertilizers) adoption is increasing gradually through field trials and correct dosage protocols, overcoming initial market misinformation regarding total urea replacement.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins impacted by sharp increases in input costs, energy/utility costs, coal prices, and logistics costs.
- Sulfuric acid prices remain significantly elevated (7-8x normal levels), preventing the restart of the titanium dioxide plant.
- Management expects logistics costs to normalize in a few months and raw material prices to stabilize if Middle East tensions subside.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Crop Protection capacity utilization stood at 72%; Pigment capacity utilization at 51%.
- Ongoing new product registrations in international markets (Latin America, North America, Africa, Asia) mapped up to 2031.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone total debt at Rs 497 crore; Consolidated total debt at Rs 661 crore.
- Total debt repayment of ~Rs 64 crore made up to September 30.
- Trade receivables credit period extended to 125+ days (historically 100-110 days) to support customers in a challenging market; expected to normalize to 110-115 days eventually.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated input, energy, and logistics costs pressuring profitability.
- Volatility in raw material prices linked to crude oil and geopolitical conflicts.
- Challenging market demand in Europe due to high costs.
- High trade receivables and extended credit days.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ayush J (Individual Investor) on trade receivables spike:** Management explained that increased credit was extended to support customers and sustain sales in a challenging market, pushing receivables from 100-110 days to 125+ days.
2. **Yash Somani (Somani Family Office) on Crop Protection margin drop to 11%:** Management cited a sharp increase in raw material, energy, coal, and logistics costs in Q2, whereas Q1 had the benefit of low-priced inventory.
3. **Khushi Solanki (Agrawal & Co) on pigment demand in Europe and US:** Management stated Europe is very challenging with impacted demand, while the U.S. continues to show good demand and margins without tariff issues so far.
4. **Shayan (Make Money Organic) on Nano urea outlook and potential dividends:** Management defended the product's field performance against negative media reports and stated that dividend payouts will be considered once cash flows and market conditions improve.

### One-line takeaway
Meghmani Organics delivered 16% YoY consolidated revenue growth in Q2 FY27, but profitability faced headwinds from elevated input, energy, and logistics costs.
