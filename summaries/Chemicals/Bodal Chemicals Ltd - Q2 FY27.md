# Bodal Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - driven by strong revenue and EBITDA growth, higher realisations, and improved capacity utilisation in benzene downstream.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 884 crore (up 77% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 128 crore (up 197% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 14.4%
- **Consolidated Net Profit (Q2 FY27):** Rs 81 crore (up 1,259% YoY)
- **Consolidated Total Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,599 crore (up 67% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 203 crore (up 114% YoY)
- **Consolidated Net Profit after tax (H1 FY27):** Rs 112 crore (vs Rs 16 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Standalone Net Profit (H1 FY27):** Rs 109 crore
- **Segment Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Dye Intermediates Rs 345 crore (up 88% YoY); Dyestuff Rs 212 crore (up 60% YoY); Basic Chemicals Rs 99 crore (up 199% YoY); Chlor-Alkali Rs 91 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **Production Volumes (H1 FY27 Standalone):** Dyestuff 9,056 tonnes; Dye Intermediates 16,956 tonnes; Basic Chemicals 113,312 tonnes; Chlor-Alkali 38,886 tonnes

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 recorded all-time high consolidated total turnover and highest absolute EBITDA.
- Stricter environmental enforcement and no significant new capacity additions in China supported better price realization for dye intermediates globally and in India.
- Exports of H acid, vinyl sulfone, dyestuff, and benzene downstream products to China have commenced.
- Saykha benzene downstream plant improved capacity utilization significantly in H1 and reached around 85% in October.
- Other income included one-off items: Rs 6 crore profit from land sale at the Punjab unit and Rs 8.5 crore from Gujarat government subsidy.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets Rs 3,000+ crore revenue for FY27 with around 12% EBITDA margins (Mayur Padhya).
- Management expects EBITDA percentage margins to be sustained in H2 FY27.
- FY28 volume growth is expected from higher capacity utilization in benzene derivatives, TCCA plant ramp-up, and dyestuff, though top-line revenue may adjust if crude/raw material prices moderate.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global structural changes are observed in China, with H acid and vinyl sulfone exported to China frequently, and initial exports of dyestuff and benzene derivatives.
- H acid pricing reached ~Rs 1,800, corrected to ~Rs 1,600 levels, and is currently settled comfortably around ~Rs 1,650 levels with forward demand visibility up to January.
- Dyestuff demand follows a time lag of 1 to 3 months to absorb intermediate price increases.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material pressures (sulfur, aniline) were effectively passed on through realizations. Sulfur prices reached ~Rs 100/kg and have softened to ~Rs 90/kg.
- Standalone gross margins remained stable at around 40%.
- Tax rate is expected to normalize at a flat 25% going forward.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dye intermediate capacities are at optimum levels; management is not planning immediate brownfield expansions until structural shifts in China are fully verified.
- TCCA plant equipment sourcing is shifting from China to domestic procurement to expand capacity locally within the next few months.
- Potential capex or inorganic acquisitions in the Rs 100 crore to Rs 200 crore range are being considered to keep debt under control.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt remained flat compared to March 2026 levels as term debt reduced, but working capital (inventory and receivables) increased due to higher turnover.
- Future cash generation will primarily be deployed toward debt reduction.
- Accrued electricity duty subsidy of ~Rs 50 crore from the Punjab government is expected to be received gradually.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global uncertainties and geopolitical issues persist.
- TCCA business operates around break-even levels due to restricted equipment imports and low initial utilization.
- Benzene downstream plant faced technical issues in Q2, muting its bottom-line contribution during the quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Garvit Goyal (Serene Alpha)** asked about H acid sustainability post-festive season and working capital rise. *Management answered that demand commitments extend to January, and inventory/receivables rose strictly in tandem with the 70%-80% jump in turnover.*
- **Bhavika Singhvi (Niveshaay)** asked about TCCA competition and anti-dumping duty impact. *Management explained that despite anti-dumping duties, prices (~Rs 150-160) and margins remain modest due to low utilization and alternative import routes, deterring new entrants.*
- **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital)** enquired about FY27 revenue targets and one-off other income. *Management guided for Rs 3,000+ crore revenue, confirmed Rs 14.5 crore in one-off other income for Q2, and projected another Rs 15 crore from land sales in H2.*
- **Raj (TPR Capital)** asked about Chinese price quotes and order mix. *Management clarified that shipments involve a 30-60 day cycle, meaning average realizations in Q2 blended older lower-priced orders with newer higher-priced orders.*
- **Aman Soni (Seven Alpha)** questioned the conservatism regarding H acid capacity expansion given structural shifts in China. *Management stated they prefer to verify the longevity of the trend over the next quarter before committing to expansion, which can be executed in under 12 months.*

### One-line takeaway
Bodal Chemicals delivered record H1 FY27 performance (revenue up 67% YoY, EBITDA up 114% YoY) fueled by structural tailwinds in China, higher intermediate realizations, and a sharp recovery in benzene derivative capacity utilization.
