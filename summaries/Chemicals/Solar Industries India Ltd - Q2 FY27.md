# Solar Industries India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong broad-based performance across domestic, international, and defense segments, with management confident of surpassing annual guidance.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 3,519 crore (up 69% YoY) for Q2; Rs 7,187 crore (up 70% YoY) for H1.
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 1,021 crore (margin of 29.02%) for Q2; Rs 2,045 crore for H1.
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 650 crore (up from Rs 361 crore YoY) for Q2; Rs 1,316 crore (up from Rs 714 crore YoY) for H1.
- Consolidated PBT: Rs 877 crore for Q2; Rs 1,789 crore for H1.
- Defense Revenue: Rs 1,002 crore for Q2 (29% of customer basket); Rs 1,935 crore for H1 (27% of customer basket).
- Customer Basket Mix (Q2): Domestic explosives 27%, International explosives 43%, Defense 29%.
- Customer Basket Mix (H1): Domestic explosives 32%, International explosives 40%, Defense 27%.
- Raw Material Consumption: Rs 1,676 crore for Q2; Rs 3,533 crore for H1.
- Employee Cost: Rs 266 crore for Q2; Rs 522 crore for H1.
- Other Expenses: Rs 571 crore for Q2; Rs 1,111 crore for H1.
- Interest and Finance Cost: Rs 55 crore for Q2; Rs 97 crore for H1.
- Depreciation: Rs 88 crore for Q2; Rs 168 crore for H1.
- Total Order Book: Rs 20,178 crore, of which Defense order book stands at Rs 17,568 crore.
- Guided Pinaka Expected Order Size: Around Rs 3,000 crore (total Pinaka family potential around Rs 9,000 crore).

### Highlights
- Performance supported by the domestic market, international business, and defense.
- Domestic explosive performance remained strong despite the monsoon period, aided by demand from mining and infrastructure sectors, customer engagement, and expanded manufacturing footprint.
- International explosives business maintained momentum via deeper customer relationships, improved penetration, and sustained demand.
- Defense growth was driven by existing order book execution and an expanding indigenously developed product portfolio.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to surpass the stated annual top-line guidance of Rs 14,000 crores given at the start of FY27.
- Management expects full-year EBITDA margin to cross 29% on an average.
- Defense revenue is expected to cross the Rs 4,500 crore guidance.
- Management refrained from revising numerical guidance further due to geopolitical issues, supply chain disruptions, and commodity price fluctuations.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic mining demand was strong due to below-average monsoon conditions in most mining markets.
- Coal India reported record offtake.
- Industry volume growth for domestic commercial explosives is expected to be around 6% to 7%, while Solar targets 10% to 15% volume growth.
- Inquiries for defense and explosives products continue to come from global nations allowed under Government of India notifications.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material, employee costs, and other expenses increased in line with business scale-up.
- EBITDA margin for Q2 stood at 29.02%, with H1 margin at 28.45%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company continues to expand its manufacturing footprint via greenfield expansions and acquisitions (Omnia acquisition status: awaiting approvals).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not specifically discussed in detail, aside from finance cost figures (Rs 55 crore for Q2).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues, supply chain disruptions, and fluctuating commodity prices create uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs)** asked about non-defense volume growth and Guided Pinaka status. Management stated domestic growth momentum will remain robust in H2, and Guided Pinaka negotiations (~Rs 3,000 crore) are in the final stage.
- **Amit Anwani (PL Capital)** asked about 155 mm shell scaling and Omnia agri-business value accretion. Management stated 155 mm products are being established in international markets, but declined to provide further updates on Omnia prior to acquisition completion.
- **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities)** asked about the impact of missile technology opening to private sectors and MALE UAV updates. Management viewed it as a positive alignment with Solar's existing capabilities in energetic materials and propellants, and noted MALE UAV is being developed indigenously at its own pace.
- **Tanmay (Aditya Birla Sun Life)** asked if the company will substantially beat the Rs 14,000 crore guidance given H1 performance. Management affirmed confidence in surpassing the annual guidance.
- **Pinakin (Shri/HSBC)** asked about potential order quantum in negotiation. Management highlighted the ~Rs 3,000 crore Guided Pinaka order expected before fiscal year-end, and noted new missile program orders would take around 2 years post-trial completion.

### One-line takeaway
Solar Industries delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with revenue up 69% YoY and EBITDA margin at 29.02%, driving management to express high confidence in surpassing its annual guidance of Rs 14,000 crores.
