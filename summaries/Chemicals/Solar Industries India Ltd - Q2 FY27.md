# Solar Industries India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 69% YoY supported by broad-based growth across international explosives and defense, leading management to state they will surpass full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 3,519 crore, up 69% YoY from Rs 2,082 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 1,021 crore, up 75.43% YoY from Rs 582 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 29.02%, up 107 bps YoY compared to 27.95% in Q2 FY26.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 650 crore, up 80.05% YoY from Rs 361 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **Total Order Book:** Rs 20,178 crore; Defense order book stands at Rs 17,568 crore.
- **H1 FY27 Financials:** Revenue at Rs 7,187 crore (up 70% YoY), EBITDA at Rs 2,045 crore, PAT at Rs 1,316 crore.
- **Revenue Mix (Q2 FY27):** International Explosives at 43%, Defense at 29% (Rs 1,002 crore), and Domestic Explosives at 27%.

### Highlights
- Defense revenue reached Rs 1,002 crore in Q2 FY27 (up from 24% of mix in Q2 FY26) and Rs 1,935 crore in H1 FY27.
- International business maintained growth momentum across operating geographies.
- Most of the Rs 17,568 crore defense order book originates from international markets.
- Final price bids submitted for Guided Pinaka rockets; contract size expected at ~Rs 3,000 crore, bringing the total Pinaka family order value to ~Rs 9,000 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to surpass its stated full-year revenue guidance of Rs 14,000 crore for FY27.
- Defense revenue expected to exceed the annual guidance of Rs 4,500 crore.
- Full-year EBITDA margin expected to average above 29% (compared to initial annual guidance of ~28%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic explosives demand was supported by lower/below-average monsoons in mining regions, along with housing, infrastructure, and coal mining demand.
- Long-term domestic industry growth targeted at 6% to 7%, with company volume growth targeted at 10% to 15%.
- Strong export and domestic demand for defense products, including rockets, ammunitions, and tactical missiles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material cost for Q2 FY27: Rs 1,676 crore (vs. Rs 988 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Employee cost: Rs 266 crore (vs. Rs 195 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Other expenses: Rs 571 crore (vs. Rs 347 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Interest and finance cost: Rs 55 crore (vs. Rs 31 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Depreciation: Rs 88 crore (vs. Rs 61 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Management cited volatility in commodity prices, geopolitical issues, and supply chain disruptions as potential uncertainties.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company continues to expand its manufacturing footprint, greenfield expansions, and global presence.
- Broadened capabilities across energetic materials, propellants, warheads, rocket integration, 155 mm shells, and missile systems.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Specific balance sheet figures, cash balances, working capital days, and debt levels were not discussed on the call.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues, supply chain disruptions, and fluctuating commodity prices.
- Defense procurement conversion cycles involve long trial-to-contract durations taking up to a few years.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs):** *Status of Bhargavastra order timeline?*
  - **Management:** Internal and field trials expected to conclude within the calendar year, after which commercialization will follow; conversion from trials to commercial order takes time.
- **Amit Anwani (PL Capital):** *Updates on 155 mm shells and Omnia agri-business acquisition?*
  - **Management:** Facility established for 155 mm shells; targeting international orders. No new updates provided on Omnia acquisition beyond previous disclosures.
- **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities):** *Impact of missile technology access opening up to private sector?*
  - **Management:** Well aligned with Solar's established capabilities across propellants, warheads, and rocket integration; provides additional expansion opportunities.
- **Pinakin (HSBC):** *Timeline for missile RFPs post trials?*
  - **Management:** Even after trials complete over next 6 to 12 months, RFP and contract finalization take another 1 to 1.5 years; order flow expected in ~2 years.
- **Kavish Parekh (360 ONE Capital):** *Expected domestic volume growth timeline for commercial explosives?*
  - **Management:** Domestic volume growth targeted at 10% to 15% annually against an industry growth rate of 6% to 7%.

### One-line takeaway
Solar Industries delivered robust revenue and margin growth in Q2 FY27 driven by international market expansion and defense execution, leading management to raise full-year revenue and margin expectations.
