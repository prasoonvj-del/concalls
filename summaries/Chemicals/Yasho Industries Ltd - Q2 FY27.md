# Yasho Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY growth in revenue and EBITDA, though management remains cautious regarding margin volatility due to external logistics and energy cost pressures.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 347.92 crore (up 89.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 72.92 crore (up 118.2% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 33.68 crore (vs Rs 4.86 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Volume Growth:** 62% YoY; 7% QoQ.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** 66% in Q2 FY27.
*   **Exports:** 70% of revenue.
*   **Net Working Capital Cycle:** 149 days (as of H1 FY27).
*   **Total Debt:** Rs 615 crore (Short-term: Rs 329 crore; Long-term: Rs 286 crore).
*   **Debt-to-EBITDA:** 2.09x (vs 3.74x in FY26).
*   **ROE:** 27.3% (H1 FY27).
*   **ROCE:** 19.1% (H1 FY27).

### Highlights
*   Industrial chemicals business contributed ~90% of revenue.
*   Product portfolio expanded to 152+ products.
*   Supply agreement (signed last year) has received Rs 120 crore to date; trial production expected in Q4 FY27, with commercialization in Q1 FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management aspires to maintain EBITDA margins of 20%+.
*   **Revenue:** Targeting >Rs 1,600 crore revenue by FY28 (based on pre-war prices).
*   **Utilization:** Expects 75% utilization in FY28 for Phase 1; 50-60% for Phase 2 in FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains strong with no slowdown observed.
*   Seeing inquiries from global lubricant additive players looking to localize production in India.
*   Total addressable market is ~$15 billion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Freight:** Costs increased from Rs 10 crore (Q1) to Rs 20 crore (Q2).
*   **Energy:** Gas price increases added Rs 1.8 crore to expenditure.
*   **Raw Materials:** Prices increased 10-15% (March to September).
*   **Pass-through:** Management could not pass on spot cost increases for accepted purchase orders in Q2 but expects to pass on costs from this quarter onwards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex:** Rs 250 crore (funded by Rs 100 crore internal accruals and Rs 150 crore term loans).
*   **Expansion:** Building two new manufacturing buildings at Pakhajan site; commercialization expected by Q4 FY28 or Q1 FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Debt-to-EBITDA improved to 2.09x from 3.74x in FY26.
*   Capex is being deployed in line with project milestones.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Logistics:** Delays of 30-60 days (vs 25-30 days) and rising freight costs.
*   **Geopolitical:** Potential for further raw material price volatility and fuel price hikes.
*   **Market:** Some customer resistance to high prices, leading to potential volume hold-backs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why did margins decline 300 bps QoQ?* A: Management attributed 200 bps to freight/energy costs and 100 bps to product mix.
*   *Q: Is the Rs 250 crore capex tied to customers?* A: Yes, there is a soft commitment covering ~50% of utilization.
*   *Q: Will you disclose new contracts?* A: Only if there is a financial transaction involved (e.g., advances).
*   *Q: Are you shifting to customized solutions?* A: Yes, R&D is working on various industrial applications beyond lubricants.
*   *Q: Is the Rs 1,600 crore revenue target conservative?* A: Management prefers to be conservative in the current volatile environment.

### One-line takeaway
Yasho Industries delivered strong YoY growth driven by volume expansion, but faces short-term margin pressure from elevated freight and energy costs, which management expects to mitigate through better cost pass-throughs and operational leverage.
