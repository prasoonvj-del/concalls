# Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by favorable product mix, improved realizations, and sustained demand, despite some caution regarding fertilizer seasonality.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 403.5 crore (up 58.2% YoY, up 44.1% QoQ).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 683.60 crore (up 34.1% YoY).
*   **Chemical Segment Sales:** Rs 245 crore (up 85.6% YoY).
*   **Fertilizer Segment Sales:** Rs 158 crore (up 27.5% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Sales Volume:** 93,645 metric tonnes (up 4.3% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 49.2 crore (up 87.7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 12.2%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 35.7 crore (up 96.1% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 8.9%.
*   **Capex Invested (as of 30th Sept):** Rs 92 crore (funded by internal accruals).

### Highlights
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Commercial production at Unit 5 commenced, adding ~6,000 metric tonnes per annum (MTPA) of dye manufacturing capacity.
*   **Renewable Energy:** 10 MW DC solar project at Nanded, Maharashtra, commenced operations; total installed solar capacity is now 20.6 MW (DC basis).
*   **New Capex:** Board approved Rs 70 crore for a new chemical plant at Unit 5 (3,000 MTPA capacity), expected by March 2028.
*   **Fundraising:** Board approved issuance of 8,54,060 fully convertible warrants to the Promoter (Mr. Punit Makharia) at Rs 526.90 per warrant, aggregating up to Rs 45 crore.
*   **Merger Update:** The merger of Kisan and Madhya Bharat was withdrawn due to delays at the NCLT.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue:** Management expects to achieve at least Rs 1,250 crore in total sales.
*   **FY28 Revenue:** Management estimates potential revenue of ~Rs 2,000 crore (with a 10% variation).
*   **Unit 6:** Trial production expected by end of November/December 2026; management assumes no significant revenue contribution in Q4 FY27 for conservative planning.
*   **Margins:** Management believes there is potential for EBITDA and PAT margins to improve by 15-20% from existing levels over the long term, though not necessarily within the current year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Chemicals:** Margins improved due to supply shortages in China (environmental/safety norms) and strong demand.
*   **Fertilizers:** Q2 benefited from the Kharif season peak. Management expects Q3 to be "subdued" due to poor rainfall/drought conditions in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh.
*   **Sustainability:** Management emphasized a focus on long-term, sustainable business models rather than relying on short-term price volatility (e.g., H-Acid).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** Stood at 30% for the quarter.
*   **Inventory:** CFO noted a slight decrease in gross margin (from 32-33% in previous quarters to 30%) due to changes in inventory levels.
*   **Acid Complex:** Running at lower levels due to high sulfur prices and working capital considerations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Unit 6:** Completely ready; trial production expected post-Diwali.
*   **New Unit 5 Plant:** Rs 70 crore investment for 3,000 MTPA capacity; expected to generate ~Rs 250 crore revenue at 75% utilization.
*   **Funding:** Expansion projects funded via internal accruals and promoter warrants.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Maintained a non-limited budget of Rs 142 crore as of 30th September 2026.
*   **Receivables:** Management stated receivables are "perfectly well and safe" and aligned with business growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Seasonality:** Potential for subdued performance in the fertilizer segment in Q3 due to poor rains.
*   **Pricing Volatility:** Management cautioned that current high prices for certain chemicals (e.g., H-Acid) are not sustainable and are short-lived.
*   **Regulatory:** Delays in government subsidy declarations for fertilizers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did gross margins dip slightly despite higher realizations?** A: CFO attributed it to inventory level adjustments, not inventory losses.
*   **Q: Is the H-Acid price hike a major driver?** A: Management clarified it is a factor but not the primary driver; they view it as unsustainable volatility and prefer to focus on long-term stability.
*   **Q: Why the Rs 70 crore capex for only 3,000 MTPA?** A: Management explained the chemistry and products are different from previous expansions, justifying the cost.
*   **Q: Can you clarify the Rs 2,000 crore revenue target for next year?** A: Management clarified this includes the full-year impact of Unit 6 and advised investors to apply a 10% discount to this figure for conservatism.
*   **Q: When will the balance promoter warrant money come in?** A: Expected before March 2027 to support the ongoing Madhya Bharat capex.

### One-line takeaway
Shree Pushkar delivered a strong Q2 with significant YoY growth, supported by capacity expansion and favorable market conditions, while management maintains a conservative outlook for H2 and future revenue targets.
