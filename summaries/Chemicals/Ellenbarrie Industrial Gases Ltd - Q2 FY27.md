# Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 28% YoY driven by capacity monetization and a major EPC contract win, though consolidated margins softened due to higher freight and employee expenses.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 1,143 crore (reported in transcript as 1,143 million, noting context usually implies crores for listed entities, but strictly per text: 1,143 million vs 892 million YoY, up 28% YoY; H1 FY27 revenue at Rs 2,130 million, up 23% YoY). *Note: Transcript uses million and crore interchangeably in speech vs text; sticking strictly to transcript numbers: Q2 revenue Rs 1,143 million, H1 revenue Rs 2,130 million.*
- **EBITDA margin:** ~36% (consolidated, down from ~39% in Q1 FY27). Core gases segment margin: ~39% (stable vs Q1).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 407 million (up from Rs 387 million in Q1 FY27).
- **BHEL EPC Contract value:** Rs 480 crore.
- **Capex guidance:** ~Rs 4,500 million (Rs 450 crores) for FY27 and FY28 (remains on course).
- **Power cost:** ~22% of revenue from sale of gases in both Q1 and Q2.
- **Project engineering employee expense addition:** Rs 11 million for the quarter.
- **Argon contribution:** Mid-teens region around 14-16% for the quarter (up from ~10%).

### Highlights
- Commissioned a new 325 TPD on-site plant in Durgapur, West Bengal in September 2026 under a 15-year take-or-pay agreement.
- Awarded a major Rs 480 crore EPC contract from BHEL for a 1,200 TPD cryogenic air separation unit in Odisha (to be executed over 8 quarters, with commissioning in FY29).
- Moving from capacity creation to capacity monetization.
- Total installed capacity for on-sites crossed 1,000 TPD.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Long-term revenue guidance:** Maintained at 20% growth in the core gases business.
- **Capex guidance:** ~Rs 450 crore for FY27 and FY28 remains on course.
- **EPC execution:** 8-quarter execution cycle; larger revenue recognition expected in quarters 5 to 8.
- Management expects merchant plants in north and central India to contribute from H2 of next financial year (FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural trends favoring industry: growth/consolidation, shift toward outsourcing via on-site contracts, and emerging opportunities in coal gasification.
- Core gases demand remains strong, particularly driven by steel (on-site) and encouraging conversations in pharma and healthcare.
- Argon long-term demand growth is outpacing supply, keeping price trends positive.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sequential consolidated EBITDA margin decline (39% in Q1 to 36% in Q2) driven by:
  1. Higher other expenses (transportation/diesel price increases and longer-distance deliveries). Management notes a 2-3 month lag in passing on freight hikes.
  2. Higher employee expenses due to annual increment arrears and project engineering team buildout (Rs 11 million impact).
- Power cost constitutes ~22% of sale of gases revenue.
- Project engineering segment carries lower margins (high single digits to low double digits) but strong ROCE with zero long-term balance sheet risk.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- North and central India merchant plants delayed by 1-2 quarters due to land allotment by state governments and statutory approvals; expected live in H2 FY28.
- Durgapur on-site plant (325 TPD) commenced operations in September 2026.
- Exploring solar gases capability in central India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Funded via existing cash on books and project-level debt as required; no imminent capital raise.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government delays in land allotment and statutory approvals leading to capex delays.
- Time lag (2-3 months) in passing on freight/diesel cost increases to customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Uzair Lari (Aveksat Financial Advisory) on margin dip and freight pass-through:** Management clarified that long-term contracts allow passing on freight costs, but a 2-3 month time lag exists.
2. **Nilang Jain (Green Gen Family Office) on EPC margins and segments:** Management stated EPC carries high single to low double-digit margins and will be reported separately under project engineering.
3. **Pritesh Chheda on capex delay:** Management attributed delays in north/central India merchant plants to state government land allotments and statutory approvals.
4. **Noel on argon mix and contribution:** Management confirmed argon contribution moved to mid-teens (14-16%), driven equally by volume (Uluberia ramp-up) and pricing.
5. **Darshil Jhaveri on EPC revenue recognition and funding:** Management stated execution spans 8 quarters with higher revenue in the second half of the project cycle, and funding will use internal cash and project debt.

### One-line takeaway
Ellenbarrie reported 28% YoY revenue growth driven by capacity monetization and a large BHEL EPC contract win, while core gases margins remained steady at ~39% despite temporary freight and employee cost pressures.
