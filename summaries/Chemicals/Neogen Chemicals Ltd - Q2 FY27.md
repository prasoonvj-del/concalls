# Neogen Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-08 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - robust all-round performance with consolidated revenue up 42% YoY and EBITDA up 51% YoY, supported by scaling battery chemicals and core specialty segments.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 296 crore (+42% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 45 crore (+51% YoY)
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 54 crore (+54% YoY) with a margin of 18.6%
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 8 crore (+144% YoY)
- Standalone PAT: Rs 21 crore (+127% YoY)
- Battery Chemicals Revenue (Neogen Ionics): Rs 37 crore (vs Rs 5 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Organic Chemicals Revenue: Rs 238 crore (+29% YoY)
- Inorganic Chemicals Revenue: Rs 58 crore (+140% YoY)
- Consolidated Net Debt: Rs 1,095 crore (Standalone Net Debt: Rs 378 crore)
- Liquidity (Liquid investments/Fixed deposits): Rs 465 crore
- Insurance Claims: Cumulative recoveries of Rs 164 crore (Rs 155 crore on-account + Rs 9 crore salvage); additional accepted claim of Rs 152 crore (total received and receivable Rs 316 crore)

### Highlights
- Board approved doubling Organolithium capacity at Patancheru, Telangana from 300 metric tonnes per annum to 600 metric tonnes per annum with an outlay of up to Rs 45 crore.
- Completed maiden QIP raising Rs 600 crore, oversubscribed 6.5x.
- CRISIL upgraded Neogen's long-term bank facility outlook to CRISIL A- stable from negative (short-term rating reaffirmed at CRISIL A2+).
- Neogen Ionics executed a business transfer agreement to consolidate the lithium-ion electrolyte salt undertaking under step-down subsidiary Neogen Morita New Materials Limited (NML) for a cash consideration of Rs 245 crore.
- Trial runs of the rebuilt Dahej plant are underway, with commercial production expected within the current financial year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects consolidated annual revenue to be close to Rs 1,350 crore to Rs 1,400 crore or slightly better for FY27 (base business Rs 1,100 crore plus, battery business Rs 250 crore to Rs 300 crore).
- Management expects FY28 consolidated net debt in the range of Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,500 crore.
- Management projects FY28 base business revenue between Rs 1,200 crore and Rs 1,300 crore, and battery chemicals revenue crossing Rs 1,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Organolithium demand is expanding into high-growth applications including pharma, agro, semiconductors, and specialty polymers.
- U.S. electrolyte manufacturers are accelerating sourcing shifts towards non-FEOC suppliers in India to qualify for Section 45x tax credits.
- Indian advanced chemistry cell manufacturing capacity is slated to reach ~60 gigawatt hours by Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28 (including players like Ola, Exide, Tata, Reliance, Amara Raja, and Waaree).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin stood at 18.6%; management guides base Neogen margins at 18% to 20%.
- Consolidated profitability was temporarily impacted by initial fixed cost absorption at Neogen Ionics, Dahej rebuild overheads, interim tolling, and elevated input costs.
- Lithium feedstock pricing is fully pass-through with quarterly resets for long-term customers and monthly resets/fixed periods for others.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Patancheru Organolithium expansion (300 to 600 metric tonnes per annum) targeted for completion by H1 next year, unlocking Rs 150 crore to Rs 200 crore in revenue potential.
- Pakhajan salt facility trial production expected to begin by December 2026, with commercial production in February/March 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt stood at Rs 1,095 crore and standalone net debt at Rs 378 crore against liquidity of Rs 465 crore.
- Inventory days are targeted around 180 days by the end of FY27, improving to 140–160 days in FY28.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Complex macro environment marked by Middle East geopolitical conflicts, supply chain friction, feedstock cost inflation, and temporary overheads from the Dahej facility rebuild.
- Execution uncertainty regarding the exact stabilization and ramp-up timelines of Indian cell manufacturers (ranging from 6 months to 2 years).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nilesh Ghuge (HDFC Securities)** asked about Organolithium timelines and FY28 net debt. Management stated the capacity expansion will complete by H1 next year (contributing Rs 50–75 crore next year and full potential thereafter) and guided FY28 net debt to be Rs 1,300–1,500 crore.
- **Jason Soans** asked about battery chemicals guidance and captive vs. external salt mix. Management confirmed FY27 battery guidance at Rs 250–300 crore and FY28 at over Rs 1,000 crore, noting ~80% of salt capacity is expected to be sold externally.
- **Abhijit Akella** asked about the standalone vs. consolidated revenue gap and inventory increase. Management explained the gap is due to inter-unit raw material transfers, and inventory is elevated due to ongoing Dahej rebuilding and outsourced tolling.
- **Archit Joshi (Nuvama)** asked about U.S. non-Chinese demand mechanics. Management detailed how U.S. 45x tax credits require non-FEOC suppliers, driving strong demand for Neogen's salts.
- **Tejas Arun Sonawane** asked for a breakdown of Organolithium revenues. Management declined to disclose specific figures for a single molecule due to competitive reasons.

### One-line takeaway
Neogen delivered a strong quarter with 42% YoY revenue growth, propelled by robust core specialty chemicals, rapid scaling in battery chemicals, a Rs 600 crore QIP fundraise, and strategic capacity expansions.
