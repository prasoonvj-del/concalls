# Premier Explosives Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue and margins declined due to temporary dispatch delays, supply chain disruptions, and elevated raw material costs, though management maintains full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 102.6 crores, down 28% YoY
- EBIT: INR 4.8 crores, down 80% YoY (EBIT margin: 4.7%)
- Net Profit (PAT): INR 3.0 crores, down 80% YoY (PAT margin: 3.0%)
- Order Book: INR 1,393 crores (Defense: INR 1,309 crores / 94%, Explosives: INR 42 crores / 3%, Services: INR 42 crores / 3%)
- Q1 FY27 execution from the Oct 2025 order: INR 21 crores

### Highlights
- Revenue and profitability impacted by global headwinds, maritime supply chain disruptions, and delayed export licenses.
- Recently received several export licenses over the past week and started moving material from factories.
- Apollo Micro Systems' acquisition of promoter stake is expected to bring synergies in defense electronics, mission-critical systems, and naval opportunities; details to be shared by December.
- Developing payloads in partnership with drone manufacturing industries rather than standalone drone tech.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 revenue guidance of around INR 600 crores.
- Management targets full-year EBITDA margin of 15% to 20%.
- Expects to complete the INR 430 crores order from October 2025 before the end of FY27.
- Expects INR 150 crores to INR 200 crores in export dispatches in Q2 FY27.
- Expects another INR 200 crores to INR 300 crores in order inflows for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying demand for defense products, RDX, and HMX remains robust due to global war scenarios.
- Bulk explosives margins remain very thin (low single-digit) or negative; Coal India orders withdrawn due to low prices, while servicing Singareni Collieries (balance INR 25 crores to INR 28 crores to be executed in FY27).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Elevated raw material costs and product mix created temporary pressure on gross margins and EBITDA margins.
- Other expenses dropped to INR 11 crores in Q1 due to base effects from previous expected credit loss provisions and forex variations; sustainable quarterly run rate is around INR 9 crores to INR 10 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Katepally expansion: RDX and HMX production plant erection is almost complete; water trials expected in September, followed by production. Mixing plant (2.5 tonnes planetary mixer) delayed by maritime logistics, now being shipped by air; plant ready for dummy trials by end of September.
- Andhra Pradesh land parcel expansion is currently stalled due to pricing disagreements with the government; management is requesting a reasonable price for the large land area required.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global maritime supply chain disruptions and delays in obtaining export/import licenses impacting delivery timelines.
- Heavy reliance on imported chaff material components, leading to external dependencies.
- Intense competition in industrial explosives with over 40 players.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Paras Kulkarni (Ignite Capital)** asked about the sustainability of other expenses and gross margin recovery. Management stated INR 9-10 crores is the normalized run rate and margins will improve sequentially with better product dispatches and execution.
2. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital)** questioned whether the INR 600 crores revenue guidance is intact despite a weak Q1. Management confirmed they retain the INR 600 crores guidance, citing internal capacity for flares and upcoming dispatches.
3. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital)** asked about the status of Katepally capex. Management stated RDX/HMX water trials start in September and mixing plant dummy trials by end of September.
4. **Deepak (Individual Investor)** inquired about the reasons for the EBITDA margin drop. Management reiterated it was due to product mix and raw material input costs, maintaining the 15-20% annual target.
5. **Paras Kulkarni (Ignite Capital)** asked about naval order traction post-Apollo acquisition. Management answered that total strategic potential and details will be known by December.

### One-line takeaway
Premier Explosives reported a weak Q1 FY27 with a 28% revenue decline and 80% drop in PAT due to supply chain and export license delays, but management remains confident in achieving its INR 600 crores revenue guidance and 15-20% EBITDA margin target for the full year.
