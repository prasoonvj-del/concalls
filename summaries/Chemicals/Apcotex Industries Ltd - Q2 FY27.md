# Apcotex Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and profit growth despite macro headwinds, inventory losses, and geopolitical supply chain disruptions.

### Key numbers
- **Operating Revenue:** Rs. 499 crore (up 48% YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 58 crore (up 44% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.69% (vs. 12.06% in Q2 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 35 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 6.98%
- **Sales Volume Growth:** 7% YoY (export volumes also recovered)
- **H1 FY27 Operating Revenue:** Rs. 1,025 crore (up 44% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 175 crore (up 121% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 17.12% (expanded by 599 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 114 crore (up 156% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 11.11% (vs. 6.25% YoY)
- **Capex / Expansion Projects:** Rs. 210 crore at Valia facility (Rs. 65 crore spent, Rs. 155 crore cash outflow remaining)

### Highlights
- Recorded the strongest-ever half-yearly financial performance (record EBITDA, PBT, and PAT).
- Export volumes recovered, reversing Q1 FY27 declines despite tight MENA region conditions and high ocean freight.
- Working capital requirements remained elevated due to higher input costs, increasing inventory values and receivables.
- Maintained a net cash positive position as of 30th September 2026.
- Inventory losses of close to 1.5% were incurred during the quarter due to falling raw material prices in the first two months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects long-term volume growth to be 20-30% over GDP, averaging about 10% CAGR over the next 3 to 5 years.
- NBR expansion project completion and commercialization on track for April 2027.
- SBR latex plant in Valia expected to come on stream in Q2 of next financial year.
- Nitrile latex expansion decision remains paused; management is waiting to see a couple more quarters of consistent margins before investing.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Construction is the strongest performing segment (including repairs, waterproofing, and oil drilling applications). Paper also performed well; textiles were decent, and tires were okay.
- Carpet sector remains a laggard due to US tariffs, geopolitical conflict, and high ocean freight rates, though Q2 showed some recovery versus Q1.
- Long-term demand for NBR in India is expected to grow well over 4-5 years across automotive, industrial applications, and LPG hoses.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses rose due to higher freight costs from geopolitical supply chain disruptions.
- Approximately 1.5% inventory losses impacted gross and EBITDA margins during the quarter.
- Management prioritizes volume growth, EBITDA per ton, and ROCE over a rigid percentage EBITDA margin, though an ideal EBITDA margin is closer to 15%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rs. 210 crore Valia capex project is on track; Rs. 65 crore spent so far and Rs. 155 crore remaining.
- Solid NBR project to come on stream in April 2027; ramp-up expected over 2 to 3 years.
- SBR latex plant at Valia expected in Q2 of the next financial year.
- Nitrile latex plant running at close to full capacity utilization; no final investment call made yet.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a net cash positive position as of 30th September 2026.
- Working capital requirements remained elevated due to higher input costs, driving up inventory and receivables.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical supply chain disruptions leading to higher freight costs and tight container availability (particularly in the MENA region).
- Overcapacity in the global nitrile latex market (lingering post-COVID capacity) and overcapacity in the domestic paper industry.
- Price volatility in raw materials (petrochemicals/oil) leading to inventory losses.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Khetan (SMIFS Institutional Equities) on gross margin dip and export volume recovery:** Management explained that inventory losses (approx. 1.5%) and high selling/raw material prices compressed percentage gross margins, while export volumes improved due to slightly better ship availability (e.g., to Saudi Arabia) and aggressive market positioning.
2. **Aditya Khetan on nitrile latex cycle and expansion plans:** Management stated they are running near full capacity with improved margins compared to the last 3 years, but are waiting for a couple more quarters of consistent margins before deciding on further reactor additions.
3. **Farokh Pandole on NBR capex outflow and timelines:** Management clarified that out of Rs. 220 crore total project outlays, Rs. 65 crore is spent, leaving Rs. 155 crore to be outflown. NBR startup is targeted for April 2027.
4. **Jasdeep Ahluwalia on synthetic latex volume growth outlook:** Management guided for a 10% CAGR volume growth over the next 3 to 5 years (20-30% over GDP).
5. **Suchit M (Retail Analyst) on sequential Q2-Q3 trends:** Management acknowledged the observation regarding historical seasonal dips (monsoon impacts in Q2) and stated they will analyze the historical quarterly trend data provided by the analyst, reiterating their focus on annual performance over quarter-on-quarter volatility.

### One-line takeaway
Apcotex delivered a record H1 FY27 performance driven by strong volume growth and pricing actions, while managing supply chain headwinds, inventory losses, and maintaining a net cash balance sheet ahead of major capacity expansions.
