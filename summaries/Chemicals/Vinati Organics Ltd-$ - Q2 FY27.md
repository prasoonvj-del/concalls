# Vinati Organics Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume and product performance offset by margin pressure due to unpassed logistics/energy costs and ATBS destocking, though management notes the worst is behind.

### Key numbers
- Standalone Net Income (incl. other income):
  - Q2 FY2027: Rs 707 crore (up 21% YoY from Rs 562 crore; down 4% QoQ from Rs 701/707 crore)
  - H1 FY2027: Rs 1,387 crore (up 25% YoY from Rs 1,112 crore)
- Standalone EBITDA:
  - Q2 FY2027: Rs 142 crore (down 27% YoY from Rs 195 crore)
  - H1 FY2027: Rs 333 crore (down 10% YoY from Rs 368 crore)
- Standalone PAT:
  - Q2 FY2027: Rs 87 crore (down 33% YoY from Rs 129 crore)
  - H1 FY2027: Rs 210 crore (down 15% YoY from Rs 242 crore)
- Consolidated Net Income (incl. other income):
  - Q2 FY2027: Rs 693 crore (up 23% YoY from Rs 566 crore; down 2% QoQ from Rs 704 crore)
  - H1 FY2027: Rs 1,398 crore (up 25% YoY from Rs 1,115 crore)
- Consolidated EBITDA:
  - Q2 FY2027: Rs 138 crore (down 24% YoY from Rs 183 crore)
  - H1 FY2027: Rs 318 crore (down 9% YoY from Rs 350 crore)
- Consolidated PAT:
  - Q2 FY2027: Rs 187 crore for H1 (down 15% YoY from Rs 219 crore in H1 FY2026; Q2 individual PAT not separately stated)
- H1 FY2027 Product Mix (approximate contribution):
  - ATBS: 27%-28%
  - Butyl Phenols: 16%-20% (also cited as 17%-18%)
  - Antioxidants (AO): 15%-17%
  - IBB: 12%-15%
- FY2027 Top-line Guidance: 20%-25% improvement over previous year.
- FY2027 EBITDA Margin Guidance: Around 24%-25%.
- New Capex: Rs 500 crore announced + potential additional Rs 500 crore under consideration.
- Asset Turnover for new capex: 1:1 to 1.5x.
- ROI on new capex: 15%-20%.
- Average realization for new products: ~$3 per kg (range $2-$4.5).
- Export share of new products: 65%-70%.
- VOPL revenue for FY2027 expected: Rs 75 crore - Rs 100 crore.

### Highlights
- ATBS demand impacted in H1 due to customer destocking, but volume recovery anticipated in H2 with strong Q3 uptick.
- Antioxidant (AO), IB, and IBB businesses delivered strong performance in H1; expected to maintain momentum in H2.
- Butyl Phenols delivered stable performance in H1 with better demand expected in H2.
- VOPL (Veeral Organics) is undergoing process re-engineering; commercial sales for expanded MEHQ expected starting April. PTAP and other isoamylene-based product revenues expected to start from December/January.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to end FY2027 with a 20%-25% top-line growth.
- EBITDA margin expected around 24%-25% for FY2027, recovering to 25%-26% in FY2028.
- VOPL to reach optimum capacity in about one year following April commercialization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Middle East war led to rising raw material, energy, and logistics costs.
- Logistics rates started cooling in Q3 with further reductions expected in Q4, positively impacting profitability.
- ATBS demand recovery backed by oil and gas application improvements in H2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price increases were mostly passed on, but logistics and energy cost spikes could not be fully passed through, hurting bottom-line margins in Q2.
- Standalone EBITDA margin dropped to 21% in Q2 due to ATBS sales shift to Q3 and unpassed logistics/energy costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Announced a Rs 500 crore capex plan for new specialty chemicals (fragrances, personal care, water treatment, oil fields, resins, etc.), funded entirely through internal accruals, to be commercialized in two years.
- Planning an additional Rs 500 crore capex, currently scouting for land; clarity expected in the next few months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Both capex phases will be entirely funded through internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions (Middle East war) impacting raw material, energy, and logistics volatility.
- Customer destocking temporarily delaying ATBS targets.
- Internal production/re-engineering delays at VOPL.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rohit Nagraj (360 ONE Capital)** asked about the chemistry platforms and revenue scale for the Rs 500 crore capex. Management replied that products are related/adjacent to current chemistries, tested via pilot facilities over the last year, targeting a 1:1 to 1.5x asset turnover and 15%-20% ROI.
- **Nilesh Ghuge (unspecified firm)** inquired about ATBS growth and VOPL commissioning. Management stated Q3/H2 will show recovery, and VOPL commercial sales will begin around April, reaching optimum capacity in a year.
- **Naushad Chaudhary (unspecified firm)** questioned execution confidence and past teething issues. Management noted they are working with more reliable suppliers, process engineers, and technology this time, keeping pilot trials and land acquisition in check.
- **Abhijit Akella (unspecified firm)** asked about the competitive landscape and realization for new products. Management stated they will be sole or minor producers domestically, targeting 65%-70% exports at an average realization of ~$3/kg.
- **Surya Narayan Patra (India Private Limited)** probed the lowest-ever margin performance and MEHQ competition. Management reiterated that the margin dip was strictly due to the ATBS sales shift and unpassed logistics costs, and that MEHQ remains a high gross-margin product despite temporary internal scale-up hurdles.

### One-line takeaway
Vinati Organics reported a sharp Q2 EBITDA/PAT decline due to ATBS sales shifting to H2 and unpassed logistics/energy inflation, but management guides for a strong H2 rebound, 20%-25% top-line growth for FY27, and announced Rs 500 crore+ in internal-accrual-funded specialty capex.
