# Asahi Songwon Colors Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved the strongest half-year in the company's history with record revenue, a 142% YoY surge in Q2 EBITDA, and aggressive debt reduction.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 201 crore (up 66.5% YoY from Rs 120 crore; up 6.6% QoQ).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 26.96 crore (up 142% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 13.25% (up from 9% in Q2 FY26; 7.18% in Q1 FY27).
- **Q2 FY27 Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 14.89 crore (up 610% YoY from Rs 2 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 390 crore (up 44% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 59.56 crore (up 157% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 15.15% (up from 8.45% in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 33.92 crore (up 623% YoY).
- **Capacity Utilisation:** AZO pigments at 70%-75%; API Odhav facility at ~95%, Chhatral Greenfield facility at ~40%.
- **Debt Reduction:** Gross borrowings reduced by ~Rs 49 crore in H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 generated more EBITDA (Rs 59.56 crore) than the entire FY26 full year (Rs 56 crore), while H1 net profit is nearly 2x FY26 full-year profit.
- Phthalocyanine (Blue) pigment business drove performance via supply chain efficiencies and cost disciplines.
- AZO and API businesses turned EBITDA and cash positive.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 FY27 to be significantly better than Q3 of the previous year, despite typical seasonal inventory tightening by global export customers.
- Management expects H2 FY27 performance to be better than H1 FY27 for the AZO and API subsidiaries.
- Management targets reaching the Rs 900 crore to Rs 1,000 crore revenue mark over the next few years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global demand is slightly lower currently in AZO and API segments.
- The China Plus One strategy has not played out as expected in AZO pigments due to Chinese pricing and compliance shifts, but recent price hikes by Chinese suppliers offer testing opportunities.
- Q3 globally sees softness as export customers tighten inventories towards calendar year-end.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Phthalocyanine business is confident of sustaining long-term EBITDA margins of 13% to 15%.
- Raw material prices are on an uptrend due to geopolitical uncertainties and war.
- The company maintains strict inventory controls, keeping raw material inventory at around 2 weeks via just-in-time practices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- A meaningful AZO line addition would require Rs 10 crore to Rs 15 crore and will be triggered around 85% capacity utilisation.
- The company is evaluating capital allocation options (Greenfield projects, acquisitions, or high ROCE opportunities) for the next phase of growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross borrowings reduced by ~Rs 49 crore in H1 FY27.
- Total debt (long and short-term combined) is projected to drop below 1x EBITDA by the end of '27, with net worth-to-debt estimated at 0.15x to 0.20x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Tough pricing pressure and intense competition continue to weigh on AZO and API realizations.
- Global demand remains subdued.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yug Jain asked about AZO capacity utilisation and pricing:** Management stated utilisation is at 70%-75% and confirmed PBT breakeven for AZO remains the goal for FY27 despite tough pricing.
- **Preet Nagarsheth asked about sustainable blended EBITDA margins:** Management stated a group-level sustainable EBITDA aim of around 13% (10%-11% on a consolidated floor basis).
- **Aditya Baid asked about API realizations and CEP status:** Management confirmed Pregabalin CEP approval dossier is filed and expects approval by March 2027 (or earlier).
- **Shikha asked about capital allocation for the Rs 1,000 crore revenue goal:** Management clarified that reaching Rs 1,000 crore will not require significant capital expenditure due to low capacity utilisation at Chhatral API (at ~40%) and Dahej AZO plants.
- **Pajal Vats asked about the rationale of the API business:** Management stated API provides a large growth runway in pharma generics, though it involves longer lead times and regulatory testing than pigments.

### One-line takeaway
Asahi Songwon delivered a record H1 FY27 powered by structural cost efficiencies and aggressive deleveraging, even as AZO and API segments navigate ongoing price pressures.
