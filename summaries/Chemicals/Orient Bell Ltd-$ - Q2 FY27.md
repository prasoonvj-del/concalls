# Orient Bell Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained revenue growth, sequential margin expansion, and improved operational efficiency driven by FMCG-type secondary sales focus.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained policy of not giving future guidance.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from Q1's "demand chasing supply" (due to Morbi shutdowns) to Q2's "supply chasing demand" with 100% restored supply and near-term market volatility persisting.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Microsoft ERP outage mentioned during the call; export/freight issues not discussed this quarter.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Ceramic to GVT capacity conversion of 1.2 million square meters completed in October '26 as planned.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 227.6 crore, up 38.8% YoY
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 430.5 crore, up 40.6% YoY
- **Volume growth (Q2 FY27):** 14.6% YoY
- **Volume growth (H1 FY27):** 18.1% YoY
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 22.4 crore
- **EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 9.8%, expansion of 380 bps YoY
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 40 crore
- **EBITDA margin (H1 FY27):** 9.3%, expansion of 430 bps YoY
- **PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 16.4 crore, growth of 326% YoY
- **PBT (H1 FY27):** Rs 27.6 crore, growth of 741% YoY
- **Working capital/DSO:** 43 days (vs 48 days in Q4 FY26); working capital cycle at 18 days (vs 20 days in March '26)
- **Average selling price (ASP):** Rs 333 in Q2 vs Rs 320-321 in Q1
- **Tile adhesive (Masterbond) revenue:** Rs 3.2 crore in Q2 (vs Rs 2.4 crore in Q1)
- **Capacity utilization:** 75% in Q2 (Dora facility at 88%-89%)

### Highlights
- Delivered industry-leading volume and value growth, outperforming the broader industry (industry volume growth ex-OBL was ~1% in H1).
- Completed conversion of 1.2 million square meters of ceramic capacity into GVT in October '26.
- Invested Rs 1.1 crore for 1 MW renewable energy addition at the Dora facility, making all manufacturing facilities powered by green energy.
- Debtor levels declined by 5% despite 41% revenue growth in H1 FY27.
- Secondary sales contribution to retail increased from ~30% last year to 45% in Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects near-term market volatility to persist.
- Management remains confident in long-term industry and OBL prospects, noting cement and steel volume growth as positive leading indicators.
- Double-digit EBITDA margin is considered well in grasp for H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Retail to projects ratio remains constant at 80-20.
- Geographic revenue split: North and Central 49%, East 24%, South 19%, West 7%.
- Morbi players reduced prices in July/August after Q1 spikes, but gas costs (propane) rose to ~Rs 83 in November (up ~20% from Rs 67 in July/August), leading to expectations of continued closures (40%-50%) among Morbi units post-Diwali.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended gas cost for OBL remained stable around Rs 60-odd.
- Gross margin for own manufacturing is around 38%-39%.
- ASP increased by about 20% YoY in Q2, while volume contributed about 15%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex for the current financial year expected around Rs 20 crore (including maintenance capex).
- Next financial year EOP activity to be initiated in the next 2-odd months.
- Future capacity additions likely to be greenfield or brownfield rather than ceramic-to-GVT conversions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a debt-free position with healthy cash balance and liquid investments.
- Working capital days improved to 18 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term market volatility in the tile industry.
- Potential supply disruptions and cost pressures depending on Morbi factory reopenings and propane price trends.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Saiguhan Elancheran (Individual Investor) on South India presence:** Management noted efforts to increase brand awareness via TV ads in Southern languages and ground presence through shop-in-shop concepts.
- **Apurva Anil Sharma (Raas Capital) on gas prices and EBITDA:** Management stated November propane is at ~Rs 83, expects stable costs for the quarter, and targets double-digit EBITDA in H2.
- **Love Gupta (Counter Cyclical Investments) on GVT salience:** Management confirmed GVT salience reached 45% (up from 20% in FY22/23) and expects further increases.
- **Tanmoy Roy (Individual Investor) on Morbi pricing impact:** Management explained that Q2 growth was achieved despite Morbi price cuts, and rising propane costs (~Rs 83) limit further price reductions by Morbi.
- **Ashvath Rajan (Arihant Capital) on capex plans:** Management stated FY27 capex will be ~Rs 20 crore including maintenance, with FY28 plans to be finalized after EOP reviews.

### One-line takeaway
Orient Bell posted robust Q2 FY27 revenue (Rs 227.6 crore, +38.8% YoY) and EBITDA margin expansion to 9.8%, driven by secondary sales execution and stable operating efficiencies.
