# Navin Fluorine International Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance across all business verticals, robust revenue and EBITDA growth, and solid execution on strategic priorities and capex.

### Key numbers
- **Operating Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,191 crore, up 57% YoY
- **Operating EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 407 crore, up 66% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 34.2%
- **Operating PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 336 crore, up 87% YoY
- **Profit After Tax / PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 269 crore, up 81% YoY
- **Operating Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 2,236 crore, up 51% YoY
- **Operating EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 765 crore, up 69% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 34.2%, expanding 367 bps YoY
- **Operating PBT (H1 FY27):** Rs 619 crore, up 93% YoY
- **Profit After Tax / PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 513 crore, up 93% YoY
- **EPS (H1 FY27):** Rs 100
- **Net Operating Working Capital Days:** 72 days
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 8 per equity share (face value Rs 2)
- **Solstice HFO Contract Extension:** Extended by 4 years until December 2032 with additional revenue potential of approx USD 180 million
- **CDMO Phase 2 cGMP-4 Capex:** Approx Rs 125 crore

### Highlights
- PAT crossed Rs 500 crore in H1 FY27, backed by disciplined execution and capabilities.
- HPP business witnessed healthy revenue growth supported by higher volumes and improved realizations; AHF facility utilization improved and R32 capex is on track.
- Specialty Chemicals saw healthy growth with strong order visibility for H2 FY27; MPP capacity at Dahej was debottlenecked in September 2026.
- CDMO momentum remains strong, with the FY27 USD 100 million order book visibility target well within reach; engagement deepening with European CDMO major.
- Advanced Materials platform focusing on specialized applications for data centers, electronics, semiconductors, and defense.
- Investing in renewable energy across plants to meet over 60% of energy requirements.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H1 FY27 EBITDA margin range to be maintained for the full year FY27.
- Capex frame is targeted at Rs 700 crore to Rs 1,000 crore annually (approx Rs 700 crore estimated for FY27).
- Advanced Materials is targeted to form roughly 10% to 15% of the expanded pie by 2030.
- Nectar specialty plant expected to run at around 60%-65% of PAR in FY27, with balance in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pricing environment for HFC remains firm with sequential improvement in realizations.
- Strong customer interest observed for upcoming capacities in HPP.
- CDMO expanding presence across oncology, cardiovascular, neurology, and animal health with global innovators.
- Global HFO capacity constitutes about 10% of global ref gas demand in tonnage terms; HFO products serve greener/cleaner applications and command premium pricing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sourcing impacts and raw material inflation (sulfur, gas, fluorspar) offset by forex tailwinds, pricing actions, operating leverage, and operational efficiencies.
- Operating EBITDA margin expanded to 34.2% in Q2 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Dahej MPP:** Debottlenecking commissioned in September 2026.
- **R32 / HFC Capacity:** Expansion progressing as planned, on track for commissioning shortly.
- **Chemours Project:** Progressing towards commissioning in Q3 FY27.
- **Advanced Material Adoption Capacity:** Expected to commission by Q2 V-FY28.
- **CDMO cGMP-4 Phase 2:** Approx Rs 125 crore capex progressing as planned; operational by end of Q4 FY27.
- **TDF Project:** Indigenous specialty material development with DRDO progressing on schedule.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net operating working capital days stood at 72 days.
- Strong balance sheet and capital frame supporting 60%-65% EBITDA-to-cash conversion on a sustainable basis.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted volatile geopolitical environment and mentioned they do not have full visibility on future long-term EBITDA margins beyond near-term guidance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) asked about the drivers of sequential revenue growth across segments and how gross margins were maintained amidst inflation.**
   * *Answer:* Growth was driven by pricing/volumes in HPP (including AHF and Solstice HFO), higher campaign executions/asset extraction in Specialty MPP plants, and growth in CDMO base business/campaigns. Gross/EBITDA margins were protected via forex tailwinds, pricing actions, operating leverage, and efficiency improvements.
2. **Rohit Nagraj (360 ONE Capital) asked about the implied lower annual run rate for the Solstice HFO contract extension and its impact on own HFO commercialization.**
   * *Answer:* Management clarified that pricing operates on a pass-through mechanism and historical 2020/2022 pricing environments differ, but the extension guarantees larger volumes and extends the asset lifecycle to 11 years. They also noted that having this contract does not restrict them from developing other non-conflicting varieties of HFOs.
3. **Ankur Periwal (Axis Capital) asked about cGMP-4 Phase 2 utilization and potential triggers for Phase 5.**
   * *Answer:* Both Phase 1 and Phase 2 are dedicated to Fermion. Once utilization of GMP plants approaches 70%-80%, Phase 5 will be triggered.
4. **Arun Prasath (Avendus Spark) asked about capital allocation strategy and sustainable OCF/capex outlook.**
   * *Answer:* Management stated their capex frame is Rs 700 crore to Rs 1,000 crore annually, with Rs 700 crore expected for FY27. Growth up to FY28 is largely baked in, and further capex will be announced as new projects clear stage gates.
5. **Jason Soans (IDBI Capital) asked about the status of the 10 CDMO molecules and pipeline readouts.**
   * *Answer:* Management reiterated that 2 of the molecules have very encouraging near-term progress, including a Chinese regulatory approval and fast-track U.S. FDA path for one, and darolutamide label expansion for another.

### One-line takeaway
Navin Fluorine delivered a strong Q2 & H1 FY27 with revenue up 57% YoY and EBITDA margins at 34.2%, driven by broad-based growth across HPP, Specialty Chemicals, and CDMO verticals alongside disciplined operational execution.
