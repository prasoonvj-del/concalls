# Prasol Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly revenue and strong YoY growth, with management reiterating full-year guidance despite geopolitical headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 413 crore (up 38.6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 58 crore (up 86.1% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 38 crore (up 122.2% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 846 crore (up 37.1% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 148 crore (up 106% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 99 crore (up 138.9% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 17.5%.

### Highlights
*   Achieved record quarterly revenue; H1 profit before tax has already surpassed the previous full year's profit.
*   Growth driven by higher realizations and single-digit volume growth in the domestic business.
*   Diversified raw material sourcing base to mitigate volatility, though this resulted in shorter credit periods.
*   Net working capital cycle increased to 76 days (from 63 days in Q1).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 1,550 crore to Rs 1,650 crore.
*   **FY27 EBITDA Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 240 crore to Rs 250 crore (excluding Q1 inventory/forex gains).
*   **Long-term Goal:** Targeting Rs 2,800 crore to Rs 3,000 crore revenue in 5 years.
*   **Exports:** Targeting >30% of total revenue (currently ~25%).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand from domestic and international customers remains healthy; order book is strong.
*   Last month registered the highest sales ever.
*   **Agrochemicals:** Experienced 150% growth in October due to weather-related demand shifts; management is not investing further in this segment.
*   **Mining Chemicals:** Currently in the 2-2.5 year trial/approval cycle.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Raw material prices remain elevated.
*   Shipping/freight costs are volatile; management expects performance to improve as conditions normalize.
*   Power cost at Mahad plant is Rs 9-9.50/unit; expects savings of Rs 1.25-1.50/unit after laying a dedicated power cable and potential solar integration.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Phase 1 Capex:** Rs 250-300 crore total; Rs 75 crore invested in H1, with Rs 75-100 crore planned for the remainder of the year.
*   **Capacity:** Phase 1 adds 60,000 tonnes; total capacity to reach 150,000-160,000 tonnes over 12-24 months.
*   **Phase 2 Capex:** Rs 250-300 crore planned for R&D-led products.
*   Expansion projects are phased; 25-30% of Phase 1 products expected to go live by June/July 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Post-IPO, utilized Rs 60 crore for debt repayment.
*   Working capital cycle increased to 76 days due to shorter credit terms from new suppliers; management aims to bring this back to 60-70 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical volatility affecting raw material availability and shipping times.
*   Potential inventory losses if raw material prices drop (mitigated by formula-based pricing with top 10 customers).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why did working capital days increase?* A: Management cited shorter credit periods from new suppliers and inventory buildup to avoid stock-outs amid shipping delays.
*   *Q: How to mitigate inventory loss risk?* A: Management uses formula-based pricing with major customers to pass on price fluctuations.
*   *Q: Status of mining chemicals?* A: Currently in the 2-2.5 year approval cycle; equipment has dual-use capability for lubricants.
*   *Q: Why no further agrochem investment?* A: Management stated they are "light on agro" and focusing investments on other product segments.

### One-line takeaway
Prasol delivered record Q2 performance with strong YoY growth and reiterated FY27 guidance, focusing on capacity expansion and supply chain diversification despite working capital headwinds.
