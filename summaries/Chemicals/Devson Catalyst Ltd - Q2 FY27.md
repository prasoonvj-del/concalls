# Devson Catalyst Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported growth in PAT and EBITDA margins despite export headwinds caused by geopolitical supply chain disruptions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 31.69 crore (vs Rs 31.36 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Revenue Growth (YoY):** 1.14%.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 10.25 crore (up 9.84% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 31.80% (up 250 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 7.5 crore (up 13.12% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 23.29% (up 246 bps YoY).
*   **Net Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 6.37 crore.
*   **Order Book:** Rs 25-28 crore.

### Highlights
*   **IPO:** Successfully listed on BSE SME platform in July 2026, raising Rs 39.39 crore.
*   **Product Mix Shift:** Catalyst revenue grew to Rs 25.31 crore (from Rs 13.26 crore in H1 FY26), while ceramic balls (Rs 3.81 crore vs Rs 13.45 crore) and adsorbents (Rs 2.49 crore vs Rs 4.28 crore) declined.
*   **Export Impact:** Export revenue contribution fell to 6.72% (from 42.95% in H1 FY26) due to supply chain disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **Customer Base:** ~79.33% of FY26 revenue generated from repeat customers.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects revenue to increase in H2 if the Strait of Hormuz situation normalizes.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Targeting 50% utilization of the new facility by the end of FY28.
*   **Growth:** Management aims to maintain historical growth rates.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Exports:** International demand remains, but shipping/logistics is the primary bottleneck.
*   **Freight Costs:** Shipping costs for 20ft containers have increased 10x (from $600-800 to $6,000-8,000).
*   **Entry Barriers:** High barriers exist due to the technical nature of products and the requirement for "proven performance" and experience in refinery/petrochemical processes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Materials:** 97% of raw materials are sourced indigenously; availability is stable.
*   **Pricing:** Natural gas price increases are passed through to customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity:** Existing capacity is 6,205 MTPA.
*   **Expansion:** Adding 5,088 MTPA (total 11,293 MTPA); new facility expected to be operational in Q1 FY28.
*   **Future Plans:** Land acquired for a potential third facility; expansion will be considered once the second facility reaches 70-80% utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Shareholders' Fund:** Rs 76.09 crore (vs Rs 33.50 crore as of March 31, 2026).
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 41.9 crore.
*   **IPO Proceeds:** Rs 12 crore allocated to long-term working capital; Rs 4.14 crore utilized as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Supply chain disruption in the Strait of Hormuz impacting export volumes.
*   **Logistics:** High freight costs affecting export competitiveness.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Rahil Bohra:** Asked about capacity expansion timeline (Q1 FY28) and current order book (Rs 25-28 crore). Management confirmed the timeline and order book status.
*   **Manav Kothari:** Asked about utilization targets (50% for new plant in FY28) and product mix. Management confirmed the 50% target and noted the shift toward higher-margin catalysts.
*   **Varun Sanghvi:** Asked about export concentration (70-80% Middle East) and diversification. Management noted plans to expand into Russia, Africa, and South Korea.
*   **Samruddhi:** Asked about entry barriers. Management highlighted the need for technical certification and proven performance in refineries.
*   **Venkatesh K:** Asked about the reason for muted revenue growth. Management attributed it to the decline in exports due to the Strait of Hormuz situation.

### One-line takeaway
Devson Catalyst delivered double-digit PAT growth and margin expansion despite significant export headwinds, with future growth contingent on the normalization of Middle East shipping routes and the commissioning of new capacity in Q1 FY28.
