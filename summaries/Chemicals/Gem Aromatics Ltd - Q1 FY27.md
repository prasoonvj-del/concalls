# Gem Aromatics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management highlighted a seasonally soft quarter, supply chain disruptions in Madagascar, and a transition phase for new product verticals, though they remain optimistic about long-term scaling.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 83 crore (vs Rs 76 crore in Q1 FY26)
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 99 crore (vs Rs 88 crore in Q1 FY26)
*   **Standalone Gross Profit:** Rs 14.7 crore (17.7% margin)
*   **Consolidated Gross Profit:** Rs 16.5 crore (16.7% margin)
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 8.5 crore (10.3% margin)
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 3.3 crore (3.3% margin)
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 7.3 crore
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Loss of Rs 7.9 crore
*   **Consolidated Cash PAT:** Rs 1.3 crore
*   **Depreciation:** Rs 9.1 crore (impacted by Dahej facility capitalization)
*   **Total Plant Capex (Dahej):** ~Rs 270 crore (Rs 265 crore incurred/capitalized)

### Highlights
*   **New Verticals:** Commercial production commenced for Safranal and cooling agents (Gemcool 3, 5, 23). Customer audits for cooling agents are complete, with initial orders secured.
*   **Phenol Derivatives:** Trial production expected by end of Q2 FY27; commercial production targeted for Q3 FY27.
*   **International Expansion:** Approved incorporation of a Brazil subsidiary to expand distribution in Latin America.
*   **Strategic Shift:** Focus on reducing dependence on the traditional mint portfolio and building a balanced mix of non-mint, higher-value specialty products.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27:** Management described this as a "ramp-up year" for new verticals.
*   **FY28:** Expects meaningful revenue contribution and significant volume ramp-up across new product lines.
*   **Krystal Ingredients:** Management expects this unit to contribute >50% of overall revenue by FY28.
*   **No specific margin/revenue guidance provided for FY27.**

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Core Business:** Demand remains strong and back to normal levels, though Q1 is seasonally soft.
*   **Export Markets:** Seeing ramp-up in demand from the Western Hemisphere (U.S. and Latin America).
*   **Customer Engagement:** Large MNCs in oral care, confectionery, and FMCG are currently in the trial/approval phase for new products, which involves smaller initial batches before scaling.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** Gross and EBITDA margins impacted by product mix and higher raw material costs in the clove business.
*   **Operating Leverage:** New facility carries a higher operating cost base (depreciation, interest, manpower) while new verticals are still in the early commercialization cycle.
*   **Clove Sourcing:** Floods in Madagascar caused a 30-day port shutdown, delaying raw material shipments; supply has resumed since May.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Dahej Facility:** Substantially capitalized; focus is now on improving capacity utilization and customer qualification.
*   **Multipurpose Platform:** The Dahej plant provides flexibility to respond to evolving customer requirements across different specialty chemistries.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Management expects the working capital cycle for Krystal (synthetic products) to be lower than the traditional Gem business.
*   **Factoring:** Company is utilizing factoring services to reduce debtor days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Supply Chain:** Shipping challenges (especially to the Western Hemisphere) remain a concern.
*   **Seasonality:** Q1 is historically a softer quarter due to the mint harvest cycle (May-July).
*   **Timing Mismatch:** High fixed costs (depreciation/interest) from the new facility have kicked in before the revenue from new products has fully materialized.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Eugenol/Clove supply:** Management clarified that while Madagascar floods caused a backlog, they are diversified in sourcing (East Africa/other Madagascar regions) and do not foresee long-term structural availability issues.
*   **On capacity utilization:** When asked if they expect 25% utilization on 500-tonne capacity for cooling agents, management indicated they should be "fairly close" to those numbers by Q3/Q4.
*   **On order values:** Management declined to disclose specific order values or customer names for the new cooling agents.
*   **On profitability path:** Management explained the consolidated loss is a function of the "timing mismatch" between high new-facility costs and the gradual ramp-up of new product revenues.
*   **On currency impact:** Management stated that currency depreciation is neither a tactical advantage nor disadvantage, as they hedge foreign contracts and import/export across various segments.

### One-line takeaway
Gem Aromatics is in a transition phase, absorbing high depreciation and operating costs from its new Dahej facility while awaiting the commercial scale-up of new specialty verticals to drive future profitability.
