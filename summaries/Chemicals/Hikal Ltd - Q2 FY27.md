# Hikal Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Chemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue and volume grew YoY, but profitability was impacted by higher raw material prices and fixed remediation costs.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** INR 434 crore
- **Q2 EBITDA:** INR 45 crore (Margin: 10.3%)
- **Q2 PAT:** Negative INR 6 crore
- **H1 Revenue:** INR 837 crore
- **H1 EBITDA:** INR 82 crore (Margin: 9.8%)
- **H1 PAT:** Negative INR 14 crore
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.52 (as of September 30, 2026)
- **H1 Capital Expenditure:** INR 81 crore
- **Pharma Q2 Revenue:** INR 278 crore (EBIT: INR 13 crore, Margin: 4.6%)
- **Pharma H1 Revenue:** INR 511 crore (EBIT: INR 20 crore, Margin: 4%)
- **Crop Protection Q2 Revenue:** INR 156 crore (EBIT: Negative INR 2 crore, Margin: -1.5%)
- **Crop Protection H1 Revenue:** INR 326 crore (EBIT: Negative INR 8 crore, Margin: -2.5%)
- **Animal Health Target:** INR 400 crore by FY30
- **Remediation Cost Impact:** Approximately INR 20 crore annually (fixed cost)
- **New Agrochemical CDMO Contract Potential:** INR 50 crore to INR 75 crore per year at peak realization

### Highlights
- Received a Certificate of GMP Compliance from the Swissmedic Agency for the Bengaluru facility.
- Commenced commercial production of the new multipurpose facility at Panoli for Specialty Ingredients.
- Signed a multiyear CDMO contract with a European-based global agrochemical company; commercial launch expected towards the end of FY27.
- Secured regulatory approvals in China and Brazil for two established pharmaceutical products.
- Successfully completed 28 customer audits in the pharma division during the quarter.
- Animal Health business delivered YoY growth with contribution margins improving QoQ.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated full-year guidance for mid-teens growth in revenue and a substantial improvement in profitability.
- Management stated confidence that H2 performance will be significantly better than H1.
- Management expects U.S. FDA reinspection of the Bengaluru facility towards the end of the current financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Crop protection division faced elevated raw material/energy prices and pricing pressure from Chinese competition; customers bought strictly for immediate requirements.
- Management stated that the worst of the crop protection industry is behind as excess channel inventory has depleted and underlying demand is returning.
- Personal care demand is shifting towards next-generation UV filters and complex skincare products.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins were impacted by higher raw material prices, with key solvents and certain commodities increasing sharply by over 100%.
- U.S. FDA remediation expenses added approximately INR 20 crore annually to fixed costs.
- Pharma capacity utilization ranged between 60% and 70% as production was consciously slowed for quality compliance.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure of INR 81 crore incurred toward debottlenecking, inventory, regulatory upgrades, and selective capacity creation.
- Panoli multipurpose facility commenced commercial production; customer validations are underway.
- Phase 2 repurposing for human and animal health CDMO projects is ongoing for completion by the end of next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Growth initiatives continue to be financed through internal accruals and external debt.
- Debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.52 as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated raw material and energy prices impacting margins.
- Pricing pressure from Chinese competition in crop protection.
- Geopolitical tensions (war situations and Middle East crisis) weighing on margins.
- Ongoing U.S. FDA warning letter restricting new product approvals in Bengaluru and capping capacity utilization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aman Vora (Premier Capital) asked about the ~900 bps drop in Pharma margins compared to Q2 FY25.** Management explained it was due to lower contribution margins from raw material price pressures/product mix and higher fixed costs from U.S. FDA remediation.
2. **Rohit Sinha (Sunidhi Securities) inquired about potential revenue additions once the U.S. FDA warning letter is lifted.** Management stated an expected positive impact of around INR 150 crore on an annualized basis (like-to-like) post-clearance, plus pending CDMO contracts.
3. **Henil Bagadia (Equicorp) asked about the capacity utilization and peak revenue potential of the new agrochemical CDMO contract.** Management stated peak realization is expected to reach INR 50 crore to INR 75 crore per year by calendar year '29 to early 2030 with high margins.
4. **Harshil Parekh (BTH Capital) asked if severe price erosion impacted Gabapentin realizations.** Management clarified that while contribution margins are lower than other molecules, high volumes maintain gross margins close to 35%, though the revenue share of Gabapentin is dropping YoY.
5. **Raghuram Kuchi (Bestpals Research) asked about the confidence level in clearing the upcoming U.S. FDA reinspection.** Management expressed high confidence, citing successful completion of 28 customer audits, a positive face-to-face meeting with the FDA in June, and approvals from ANVISA, PMDA, and Swissmedic.

### One-line takeaway
Hikal reported subdued profitability in Q2 FY27 due to raw material inflation and U.S. FDA remediation costs, but management remains confident in a strong H2 recovery backed by new CDMO contracts and ongoing regulatory resolution.
