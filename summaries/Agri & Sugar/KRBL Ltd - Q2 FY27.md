# KRBL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue driven by strong domestic demand and accelerating consumer pack volumes, despite continued West Asian export headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained ~10% domestic volume growth for FY27; updated full-year EBITDA margin guidance to 17%-19% (compared to 17%-18% in Q1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted more positive as general trade and bulk pack domestic volumes recovered strongly in Q2; export revenues grew 22% QoQ despite ongoing West Asian logistics challenges.
- **New topics or risks:** Unseasonal rain in Punjab in mid-October flattened standing crops and wet harvested paddy in mandis; tighter US sanctions on Iran in August further complicated payment/banking channels. Old topics like Strait of Hormuz freight disruptions persisted.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Domestic volume acceleration was delivered (consumer pack volume growth accelerated from 7% YoY in Q1 to 18% YoY in Q2).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Total Income / Revenue):** Rs 1,727 crore (up 14% YoY, up 15% QoQ); Total Income stood at Rs 1,768 crore (up 15% YoY).
- **Domestic Revenue (ex-power):** Rs 1,390 crore (up 33% YoY); H1 domestic revenue at Rs 2,612 crore (up 24% YoY).
- **Export Revenue:** H1 export revenue at Rs 541 crore (down 41% YoY); Q2 export revenue at Rs 297 crore (up 22% QoQ, down 31% YoY).
- **Gross Margin:** 34.5% (vs 29.2% in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 21.1% (vs 16.6% in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT (Profit After Tax):** Rs 159 crore (margin 14.6%) vs Rs 172 crore (margin 11.2%) in Q2 FY26.
- **Inventory (as of September 30, 2026):** Total inventory at Rs 2,203 crore, comprising Rs 146 crore / 31,000 tonnes of paddy and Rs 1,900 crore / 278,000 tonnes of rice.
- **Cash and Bank Balance (including treasury investments):** Rs 2,895 crore (vs Rs 2,157 crore YoY).
- **Domestic Branded Rice Sales:** Rs 1,134 crore (up 23% YoY).
- **Masala Portfolio H1 Sales:** ~Rs 4.5 crore (targeting an exit run rate of ~Rs 25 crore for FY27).

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 1,727 crore.
- Consumer pack volume growth accelerated to 18% YoY in Q2 (vs 7% in Q1); modern trade and e-commerce consumer pack volumes grew 31% YoY, while general trade consumer pack volumes returned to growth at 10% YoY.
- Regional rice volumes grew 42% YoY in Q2, taking H1 regional rice growth to 32% YoY.
- Maintained market leadership positions: General trade volume share at ~36%, modern trade at 39.6%, and e-commerce exit share at 41.1%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects ~10% overall domestic rice volume growth for FY27.
- Full-year EBITDA margin expected between 17% and 19%.
- Full-year exports expected to match or exceed last year's ~Rs 1,550 crore if Gulf shipping routes stabilize.
- Planning to procure around 750,000 tonnes of paddy in FY27.
- Targeting an annualized revenue run rate of ~Rs 25 crore for the masala portfolio by the end of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- USDA projects world rice production at 537 million tons and consumption at 539 million tons for 2026-27, leading to stock drawdowns. India's production is estimated at 147 million metric tons due to a weaker monsoon.
- The 2026 monsoon ended 13% below normal nationally, but the northwest basmati belt finished only 6% below normal; irrigation sustained the crop, putting the basmati crop modestly above last year.
- Basmati paddy prices surged to Rs 4,500–Rs 5,200 per quintal due to zero carryover stock, high export realizations, and Middle East inventory restocking demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded YoY due to higher realizations, better product mix, and higher other income.
- EBITDA margin increased YoY, partially impacted by higher proportionate freight and advertisement costs.
- Domestic rice realizations averaged ~Rs 80 per kg in Q1 and Q2 (up ~12% YoY). Export realizations averaged ~Rs 1.32 lakh per tonne (~Rs 132 per kg).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company is expanding distribution reach (present in ~3.3 lakh outlets, reaching ~1.2 crore urban households) and rolling out order-to-cash integration.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total inventory stood at Rs 2,203 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Cash and bank balance including treasury investments stood strong at Rs 2,895 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia, intermittent Strait of Hormuz transit, elevated freight/war risk premiums, and tighter US sanctions on Iran.
- Unseasonal October rains in parts of Punjab causing localized damage and flattened standing crops.
- Sharply higher paddy procurement prices (up 20% YoY) putting pressure on gross margins for new crop sales.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash Dantewadia (Dante Equity) asked about export growth and Q3 margins.** Management stated exports could double if US-Iran agreement occurs; Q3 EBITDA margin is guided between 16% and 20% (nearing 20%).
- **Hoshang Hinduja (Individual Investor) asked about bulk cargo freight agreements and basmati pricing.** Management explained bulk shipments work for Iraq (20k-25k tonnes) but not containerized markets like Saudi Arabia, and basmati prices will remain firm/high due to zero carryover stock.
- **Amit Agarwal (Leeway Investments) asked about export country breakup and Uplife oil availability.** Management noted 75% of exports go to the Middle East, and Uplife oil is currently moving slower on e-commerce due to high platform investment requirements.
- **Bhavi Chauhan (Care PMS) asked about Q-o-Q margin dip and cash utilization.** Management stated the Q-o-Q dip was due to lower other income and higher freight/ad expenses, and committed to evaluating shareholder payouts beyond the 15% dividend policy.
- **Chirag Singhal (Water Fund) asked about full-year export recovery and volume growth.** Management confirmed H2 exports could match last year (~Rs 1,500+ crore) if Gulf conditions normalize, and reiterated 10% domestic volume growth for the year.

### One-line takeaway
KRBL reported record Q2 revenue of Rs 1,727 crore (up 14% YoY) fueled by 33% domestic growth and accelerating consumer pack demand, while export recovery awaits West Asian shipping normalization.
