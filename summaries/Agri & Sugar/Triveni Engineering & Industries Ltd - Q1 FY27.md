# Triveni Engineering & Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Improved profitability in sugar and alcohol alongside lower debt and cost of funds, offset by lower sugarcane yields/crush, lower alcohol offtake, and slower execution in the water business.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** ₹1,581 crore (up 2% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Up 6% YoY
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** ₹5 crore (vs loss of ₹9 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Standalone Gross Debt:** ₹1,238 crore (comprising term loans of ₹376 crore and working capital of ₹862 crore) as of June 30, 2026, down from ₹1,603 crore YoY.
- **Average Cost of Funds:** 6.8% (down 75 bps YoY from 7.5%)
- **Sugar Segment Revenue:** ₹1,235 crore (up 6% YoY); PBIT ₹14 crore (up 82% YoY). Domestic dispatches up 7%; average realisation up 3%.
- **Sugar Inventory:** 3.59 lakh metric tonnes valued at ₹38.41 per kg as of June 30, 2026. Current sugar prices at ~₹4,600/quintal for refined and ~₹4,525/quintal for sulphitation.
- **Alcohol & Distillery Revenue:** ₹373 crore (down 13% YoY). Production: 57,488 kilolitres (down 12% YoY); sales volume: 50,483 kilolitres (down 19% YoY). PBIT: ₹31 crore (up 32% YoY). Country liquor volumes: ~15.5 lakh cases.
- **Water Business Revenue:** ₹43 crore (down 21% YoY); PBIT ₹2 crore. Order book: ₹1,472 crore (including ₹1,065 crore of O&M contracts). Orders received: ₹9 crore.
- **TPTL Share of Profit:** ₹4.35 crore accounted for in consolidated accounts.

### Highlights
- The composite scheme of arrangement became effective May 19, 2026 (power transmission business demerged to Triveni Power Transmission Limited [TPTL] from April 1, 2026); Q1 FY27 numbers reflect continuing operations with restated prior periods.
- Sugar cane crush for SS 2025-26 stood at 8.25 million metric tonnes (down 9% YoY) due to poorer yields and increased diversion in Western UP. Gross recovery improved 26 bps to 11.1%.
- TPTL share allotment completed on July 22, 2026; listing application expected to be completed in 4-6 weeks subject to regulatory approvals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management anticipates national closing sugar stocks for SS 2025-26 at ~4 million tonnes, the lowest since September 2017.
- Management expects a substantial shift towards grain-based ethanol next year in a broad ratio of 3:1 (grain to sugary feedstocks).
- Management expects a massive rebound in sugarcane availability, recoveries, and lower cost of production for the Shamli (Sir Shadi Lal) unit in the upcoming season.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India achieved 20% ethanol blending in ESY 2025-26 with ~717 crore litres procured.
- Government issued a directive effective August 1 to November 30, 2026, limiting dealer sugar stock to 30 days and setting a stock limit of 4,000 quintals.
- Supreme Court ordered a status quo on the OMC ethanol allocation framework; AG sought permission to allocate 100 crore litres.
- Estimated domestic sugar consumption is placed at 28.1 to 28.3 million metric tonnes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Distillery margins and PBIT supported by lower maize procurement prices, better DDGS realisations, and ongoing cost optimisation.
- Maize prices saw a small single-digit increase due to poorer rainfall in UP and Bihar, which management stated was offset by higher DDGS realisations and advance procurement.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure incurred at sugar plants to improve cost efficiencies and lower production costs for the upcoming season.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone gross debt reduced to ₹1,238 crore as of June 30, 2026, down from ₹1,603 crore YoY.
- Consolidated average cost of funds reduced by 70 bps YoY to 6.8%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Lower sugarcane yields and higher sugarcane costs; past pest/disease incidences (top borers) in Western UP.
- UP excise quota allocation policy increased monetary penalties 6x for retailers failing the 75% volume restriction, causing short-term market confusion.
- Slower execution in water business EPC jobs (Prayagraj and Vadodara).
- Negative mainstream media press regarding ethanol blending.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shubhi Gupta (Trinetra Asset Managers) on yield challenges:** Management explained that last year's drop was due to heavy rainfall and top borers; current crop health is good with low pest incidence, though the next six weeks of grand growth are critical.
- **Aman Kumar Sonthalia (Securities) on Maharashtra/Karnataka monsoon and sugar diversion:** Management expects flattish production in MH/KA and stated that total sugar diversion to ethanol will likely be between zero and 3 million tonnes, not reaching 3 million due to lower cane crops.
- **Sanjay Manyal on distillery capacity utilisation and maize pricing:** Management noted that interest moratorium expirations for standalone distilleries next year could strain their 20-50% capacity utilisations, while maize price increases were minimal and covered through advance procurement and DDGS realisations.
- **Rajesh Majumdar on Sir Shadi Lal numbers and capital allocation:** Management stated Sir Shadi Lal numbers are amalgamated, reported a crush of 82 lakh quintals with 10.4% recovery for FY26, and noted the board is actively examining long-term capital allocation for cash flows.
- **Neil Bahal on branded alcoholic beverages vision:** Management highlighted that 95-96% of the volume is country liquor (top 5 in UP), while branded spirits are growing slowly with cautious, performance-linked capital allocation.

### One-line takeaway
Triveni Engineering delivered improved Q1 FY27 profitability and reduced debt/cost of funds despite lower cane yields and alcohol offtake, while progressing the TPTL demerger and listing.
