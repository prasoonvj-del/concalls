# Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd_DVR - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, improved profitability, and significant progress in working capital reduction despite a challenging monsoon.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,576 crore (Q2); Rs 3,100 crore (H1, +4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 207 crore (Q2); Rs 372 crore (H1).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 13.2% (Q2); 12.1% (H1).
*   **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 40 crore (Q2); Rs 43 crore (H1).
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** 179 days (improved from 205 days in Sept 2025).

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance:** Hi-Tech Micro-irrigation (MI) margins at 18.8%; Food segment at 12%; Plastics segment at 9%.
*   **Cash Flow:** Converted Rs 206 crore of EBITDA into cash in Q2.
*   **Debt Repayment:** Successfully repaid Rs 230 crore of NCDs.
*   **Operational Discipline:** Management stated they intentionally let go of Rs 200-250 crore of business in H1 to prioritize cash flow and working capital discipline.
*   **Project Business:** Reduced active projects to 7-8; expects project business to reach zero by next fiscal year.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **H2 Outlook:** Management expects "flattish" growth for H2 due to drought and raw material uncertainty, though they remain watchful for potential demand boosters.
*   **Receivables:** Management expects a net reduction of Rs 300 crore+ in receivables by March 2027.
*   **Liquidity:** Management anticipates Rs 100 crore from Maharashtra incentives and Rs 100-150 crore from land monetization in H2.
*   **FY28:** Management expects a stronger balance sheet and improved P&L as legacy issues are resolved.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Drip Irrigation:** Domestic retail business grew ~18%.
*   **Piping:** Recovered to last year's levels after a weak Q1; management expects 8-10% growth for the full year driven by non-agricultural applications.
*   **Macro Headwinds:** Management cited the "worst monsoon in 35-50 years" impacting West and South India (80-85% of business) and high polymer prices due to the West Asian war.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Strong demand for large-diameter pipes (infrastructure), solar water pumps, and food/beverage processing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Strategy:** Management aims for 12% margins in the Plastics segment and 18-19% in Hi-Tech MI.
*   **Pricing:** Management stated there will be no margin hit when polymer prices drop, as pricing is based on average inventory costs rather than spot prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Tomato JV:** Expected growth capex of Rs 30-40 crore (subject to asset acquisition closing in Dec).
*   **Beverage Business:** Phase 2 investment of ~Rs 100 crore planned between March and June.
*   **Biochar:** Growth capex planned for the next fiscal year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Receivables:** Total ~Rs 1,860 crore (Rs 800 crore project-linked, Rs 400 crore MIP-linked).
*   **Interest Savings:** Management estimates ~Rs 20 crore annual interest savings in FY28 due to debt repayment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Monsoon:** Deficient rainfall in key states (Maharashtra, West, South) is limiting agricultural activity and irrigation demand.
*   **Raw Materials:** Unpredictable polymer supply and costs due to geopolitical tensions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why is the piping division growth lagging?** Management explained that while rural demand is weak, they are pivoting to non-agri applications and expect 8-10% growth for the full year.
*   **Q: How much of the Rs 800 crore project receivables will be collected by March?** Management expects a net reduction of ~40% (approx. Rs 320 crore) by March 2027.
*   **Q: Will there be a margin hit if polymer prices fall?** Management clarified that pricing is based on average inventory costs, so they do not anticipate a negative impact.
*   **Q: What is the status of the coffee MOU?** Management noted it is a positive development but currently small in scale compared to banana and pomegranate tissue culture.

### One-line takeaway
Jain Irrigation delivered a profitable quarter with strong cash conversion and working capital improvement, prioritizing debt reduction and liquidity over top-line growth in a challenging drought-affected environment.
