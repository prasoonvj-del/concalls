# Agri & Sugar: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Agri & Sugar sector in Q2 FY27 exhibits a dual dynamic: strong domestic realization and volume demand in traditional sugar, basmati rice, and bio-chemicals, juxtaposed against severe feedstock cost inflation and policy headwinds in biofuels, along with weather-induced disruptions in agri-inputs.

1. **Sugar & Biofuels Pivot:** Domestic sugar inventory balances remain tight (~3.5–3.6 million tonnes closing stock), keeping sugar prices firm and driving strong segment profitability for integrated millers. However, standalone and grain-based distilleries face severe margin compression due to stagnant ethanol procurement prices, government restrictions on diverting sugarcane juice/B-heavy molasses, and surging maize costs.
2. **Agricultural Inputs & Weather Friction:** Performance across seed and crop care segments was hit by severe weather anomalies—notably a 40% rainfall deficit in June, extreme summer heatwaves, and depleted reservoir levels threatening the upcoming Rabi season. Illegal Bt cotton seed sales also impacted legal seed market share.
3. **Consumer Pack Acceleration vs. Export Logistics:** Domestic consumer demand for packaged staples (basmati rice, packaged foods) expanded strongly. However, international logistics routes—specifically Red Sea/Bab el-Mandeb transit delays and West Asian geopolitics—led to working capital bloat, elevated freight/insurance expenses, and delayed export shipments.

---

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
* **Domestic Sugar & ENA:** Managements across the sugar ecosystem (**Balrampur Chini**, **Triveni Engineering**, **BCL Industries**, **Godavari Biorefineries**) agree that domestic sugar prices will remain firm supported by low opening stocks (~3.5 million tonnes) and disciplined supply. ENA demand is similarly strong due to peak seasonal consumption and reduced sugar diversion.
* **Branded Staples & Consumer Demand:** **KRBL** and **LT Foods** reported robust domestic basmati rice volume growth, with consumer pack demand accelerating in both general trade and modern trade/e-commerce.

### Divergence
* **Ethanol Tender Economics & Viability:** **TruAlt Bioenergy** and **BCL Industries** view current OMC tender conditions as a "survival of the fittest" environment due to lower maize ethanol pricing and the ban on juice/B-heavy molasses. Conversely, integrated play **Balrampur Chini** views firm domestic sugar prices as an effective buffer against compressed distillery margins.
* **Agri-Input & Seed Recovery:** **Kaveri Seed** expects a strong recovery in maize seeds next season due to low channel inventories, whereas **Godrej Agrovet** remains cautious regarding crop care and agri-input demand during the Rabi season due to depleted reservoir levels, despite recent cyclone rainfall in Andhra Pradesh.
* **Export Routes vs. Domestic Sourcing:** **LT Foods** and **KRBL** maintain a positive view on underlying global basmati demand, but disagree with optimistic timeline expectations regarding international shipping costs, expecting friction in West Asian routes to persist into H2.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

* **Maize Feedstock Inflation:** Maize prices surged to Rs 26–30 per kg (up 25%–30%), severely eroding standalone grain-based ethanol profitability (**BCL Industries**, **TruAlt Bioenergy**, **Triveni Engineering**, **Godavari Biorefineries**).
* **Sugarcane State Advised Price (SAP) & Recovery:** Haryana increased SAP by Rs 15 to Rs 430/quintal for SS 2026-27. **Balrampur Chini** noted that every Rs 10/quintal increase in sugarcane SAP adds Rs 100 crore in raw material costs. Furthermore, late October rains in UP reduced initial cane sugar recovery by 50–70 bps (**Triveni Engineering**).
* **Paddy Sourcing Costs:** New crop paddy prices opened 20% to 25% higher YoY (**KRBL**, **LT Foods**), with paddy inventory rates reaching Rs 43/kg and finished rice rising to Rs 60/kg, necessitating proportional price revisions in finished basmati products.
* **Freight and Supply Chain Costs:** Transit times from African origins extended from 35–45 days to 110–112 days due to Red Sea conflicts (**Aelea Commodities**), inflating working capital carrying costs, ocean freight rates, and insurance surcharges (**LT Foods**, **Pajson Agro**).

## Capex & capacity

* **PLA & Specialty Chemicals:** **Balrampur Chini** has spent Rs 2,400 crore on its 80,000-tonne PLA (polylactic acid) project, targeting operational start in November/December 2026. **Godavari Biorefineries** committed Rs 25 crore toward debottlenecking bio-based chemical operations to reach a Rs 240 crore/quarter revenue run-rate by Q3 FY28.
* **Compressed Biogas (CBG) Pivot:** Companies are pivoting capex toward CBG to hedge ethanol volatility. **TruAlt Bioenergy** commissioned its first 20 TPD plant under its Sumitomo JV (targeting 72 TPD by quarter-end across 4–5 plants costing ~Rs 300 crore). **BCL Industries** is setting up a 20 TPD CBG plant in Fatehabad (~Rs 70 crore revenue potential).
* **Global Sourcing & Processing Expansion:** **Pajson Agro** announced a share-swap acquisition of Pacific Global FZCO (60,000 MT facility in Cote d'Ivoire), taking total processing capacity to 113,000 MT. **Aelea Commodities** approved a Rs 114 crore capex plan for a 60 MT shelling expansion and Cardanol unit.
* **Palms & Seeds Facilities:** **Godrej Agrovet** commissioned phase one of its integrated oil palm complex at Khammam, Telangana (mill, CPKO refinery, biogas plant). **Kaveri Seed** holds Rs 120–130 crore in CWIP while incorporating 8 international subsidiaries across Africa and Asia.

## What changed since last quarter

* **BCL Industries:** Shifted tone from "cautiously optimistic" to flagging major margin pressure from surging maize prices (Rs 29–30/kg) and government tender rules. Fully restored operations at its 200 KLPD Bathinda plant on August 28 following the June fire.
* **KRBL Ltd:** Shifted more positive as domestic consumer pack volume growth accelerated from 7% YoY in Q1 to 18% YoY in Q2. Full-year EBITDA margin guidance was raised slightly to 17%–19% (from 17%–18%).
* **Balrampur Chini Mills:** Confirmed tight supply-demand dynamics requiring ~3 million tonnes of domestic sugar imports/TRQ balance, while securing its first institutional order for PLA products.
* **Godavari Biorefineries:** Returned to positive consolidated EBITDA (Rs 10.9 crore vs negative Rs 4.4 crore YoY), driven by a 38.8% YoY surge in bio-based specialty chemicals.
* **Godrej Agrovet:** Turned more cautious regarding Rabi season prospects due to reservoir depletion; successfully commissioned the Khammam CPKO refinery phase one on September 1 as promised.

2. **State Advised Price (SAP) & UP Crushing Recovery:** UP government announcement of the sugarcane SAP and recovery levels following late-October unseasonal rains (**Triveni Engineering**, **Balrampur Chini**).
3. **OMC Ethanol Pricing & Allocations:** Final allocations and potential price relief for grain-based ethanol in the ESY 2026-27 tender (**TruAlt Bioenergy**, **BCL Industries**).
4. **Rabi Sowing & Water Reservoir Levels:** Sowing updates for maize and winter crops given dry soil conditions and crashed reservoir levels (**Godrej Agrovet**, **Kaveri Seed**).
5. **Red Sea Freight & Transit Times:** Normalization of logistics routes and working capital release for cashew and basmati exporters (**Aelea Commodities**, **LT Foods**, **KRBL**, **Pajson Agro**).
