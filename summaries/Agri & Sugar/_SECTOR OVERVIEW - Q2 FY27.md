# Agri & Sugar: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is splitting along clear lines. **Sugar & ethanol** players report firm domestic sugar prices and low inventories (Balrampur Chini, Triveni Engineering, EID Parry, Godavari Biorefineries) but face a **policy squeeze**: the new ESY 2026-27 ethanol tender excludes sugarcane juice/B-heavy molasses, cuts maize-ethanol prices by ~Rs 3/litre, and caps FCI rice at 30% [Triveni Engineering; EID Parry; Godavari Biorefineries; Trualt Bioenergy]. Maize costs have surged 25-30% (Rs 26-30/kg), eroding grain-distillery margins [Trualt Bioenergy; BCL Industries; Triveni Engineering]. **Basmati rice** companies enjoy record domestic demand and pricing power (KRBL, LT Foods, Chaman Lal Setia) but flag “catastrophic” ocean freight (up 6-10x) and Iran payment risks [Chaman Lal Setia; KRBL; Aelea Commodities]. **Shrimp/seafood** volumes are growing (Apex Frozen, Avanti Feeds) but profitability is hit by antidumping duties, elevated farm-gate prices, and cyclone-related supply disruptions [Apex Frozen; Avanti Feeds]. **Animal protein** (poultry, buffalo meat, fresh meat) sees strong realizations and volume growth [Venkys; HMA Agro; Godrej Agrovet; DSM Fresh Foods], while **seeds** battle El Niño rain deficits and illegal Bt cotton [Kaveri Seed; Nath Bio-Genes]. **Cashew** players navigate Red Sea logistics delays (transit 35-45 → 110-112 days) and working-capital bloat [Aelea Commodities; Pajson Agro]. **Coffee** (CCL Products) delivers steady double-digit volume growth with deleveraging. Across the board, managements are **diversifying downstream** (PLA, bio-chemicals, CBG, branded foods, IMFL) to escape commodity cyclicality.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian agri-businesses have historically made money by processing seasonal commodities (sugar, rice, shrimp, cottonseed) at scale, selling largely unbranded into wholesale/export channels, and riding government price-support cycles (FRP/SAP for cane, MSP for grains, ethanol blending mandates). Over the last ~5 years, the model has shifted toward **integrated value chains** (cane development, contract farming, multi-feedstock distilleries), **branded consumer plays** (packaged rice, ready-to-eat, dairy, pet food), and **specialty chemical/bio-plastic diversification** (PLA, bio-based chemicals, CBG, SAF) to capture higher, less cyclical margins [Background].

**Shifts underway**
*   **Sugar → Bio-plastics & Specialty Chemicals** – *Scaling*. Balrampur Chini has spent Rs 2,400 cr on an 80,000 TPA PLA plant targeting Nov/Dec 2026 commissioning with first institutional order secured [Balrampur Chini]. Godavari Biorefineries’ bio-based chemicals hit Rs 204 cr revenue in Q2 (38% YoY), 52% of chemical segment, with Rs 25 cr debottlenecking starting Apr 2027 targeting Rs 240 cr/quarter by Q3 FY28 [Godavari Biorefineries]. EID Parry focuses on “substrate monetisation” (green grow media, bagasse) over new biochemical capex [EID Parry].
*   **Grain Ethanol → CBG/SAF/DME Multi-product Biorefineries** – *Scaling*. Trualt Bioenergy commissioned first 20 TPD CBG plant (Sumitomo JV), building 4-5 more (~Rs 300 cr) targeting 72 TPD by quarter-end and 250 TPD across 20-24 plants in 2 years; SAF front-end engineering underway pending policy [Trualt Bioenergy]. BCL Industries tendering 20 TPD CBG plant at Fatehabad (~Rs 70 cr, 40-45% EBITDA margin) [BCL Industries]. Godavari Biorefineries running CO₂-to-DME pilot with ICT Mumbai (completion Mar 2027) and TNBC drug candidate (CDSCO trial permission Q3 FY27) [Godavari Biorefineries].
*   **Commodity Export → Branded Domestic & Diversified International** – *Mainstream*. KRBL domestic branded revenue +23% YoY (Rs 1,134 cr), consumer pack volumes +18% YoY, masala portfolio targeting Rs 25 cr exit run-rate [KRBL]. LT Foods India branded +8% vs category 1.3%, household reach 67.5 lakh, Project Vistara expanding direct distribution; Europe secured mainstream private label [LT Foods]. Chaman Lal Setia prioritising domestic over volume-chasing, Saudi repeat order 200 tonnes [Chaman Lal Setia]. Godrej Agrovet’s Godrej Foods B2C +50% YoY (38% salience), quick-commerce driven [Godrej Agrovet]. CCL Products domestic branded ~Rs 140 cr (+25%), US brand launches planned, Nepal launched [CCL Products]. DSM Fresh Foods launched Meevaa heat-and-eat brand (5,000+ orders in 48 hrs), pivoting from Middle East exports to domestic quick-commerce [DSM Fresh Foods]. HMA Agro diversifying into rice, seafood, fruits [HMA Agro].
*   **Backward Integration & Multi-origin Sourcing** – *Scaling*. Godrej Agrovet added 13,000 ha oil palm plantations in H1, commissioned Khammam integrated complex (mill, CPKO refinery, biogas) Sep 1 [Godrej Agrovet]. Trualt Bioenergy setting up warehouses in Bihar, MP, Punjab to diversify maize/rice sourcing [Trualt Bioenergy]. Pajson Agro acquiring Pacific Global FZCO (60,000 MT Cote d’Ivoire) via share swap for farmgate sourcing [Pajson Agro]. Aelea Commodities shifting to direct African sourcing via UAE/Singapore/Europe merchants [Aelea Commodities]. LT Foods consolidating Nature Bio Foods manufacturing via new MP facility (Phase 1 FY27) [LT Foods]. DSM Fresh Foods advanced Rs 70 cr to vendors for contract farming [DSM Fresh Foods].
*   **Seed & Crop Protection → Global Registration Platforms** – *Early*. Kaveri Seed board approved wholly-owned subsidiaries in 8 countries (Tanzania, Kenya, Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Zambia, Peru) for 3-5 year registration cycles [Kaveri Seed]. Nath Bio-Genes Uzbekistan JV (Nath Bio-Genes Central Asia) targeting Bt-GT local varieties soft launch by 2028 [Nath Bio-Genes].

**Government push**
*   **Ethanol Blending / ESY Tenders** – Rewards supply volume but new tender terms (juice/B-heavy ban, maize price cut, FCI rice cap) pressure margins. Benefits: Triveni Engineering, EID Parry, Godavari Biorefineries, Trualt Bioenergy, BCL Industries [Govt: OMCs/Cabinet, Nov 2026 – 20% blending achieved; damaged grain allocation tripled; SC barred fresh allocations].
*   **Sugar Trade Policy** – Duty-free raw sugar imports extended to Mar 2027 (10 lakh tonnes); 8,606 tonnes raw cane sugar export quota allocated. Manages domestic surplus/deficit. Benefits: Balrampur Chini, Triveni Engineering, EID Parry, Godavari Biorefineries [Govt: Finance Ministry, Nov 2026].
*   **Oil Palm Mission (NMEO-OP)** – Supports plantation expansion & processing. Benefits: Godrej Agrovet (Khammam complex, 13k ha added) [Background].
*   **Fertilizer Subsidy** – Cabinet approved 6 brownfield urea projects (Rs 54,000 cr); FY27 subsidy estimated Rs 2.5-2.76 lakh cr. Indirectly supports seed/agri-input demand [Govt: Cabinet, Nov 2026].
*   **Agri-Derivatives Margin Tweak** – Lower margin requirements for agri-commodity derivatives. Benefits commodity hedgers/traders: Aelea Commodities, Pajson Agro, Chaman Lal Setia, KRBL, LT Foods [Govt: Finance Ministry, Nov 2026].
*   **Front-of-Pack Nutrition Labelling** – SC mandates implementation within 4 months. Impacts branded food players: LT Foods, KRBL, CCL Products, DSM Fresh Foods, Venkys, Godrej Foods [Govt: Supreme Court, Nov 2026].
*   **PLI Scheme for Food Processing** – Supports capacity/brand building in ready-to-eat, value-added. Benefits: LT Foods, DSM Fresh Foods, Venkys, HMA Agro, CCL Products, KRBL [Background].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**
*   **Sugar**: Domestic prices firm; India needs 1-3 million tonnes imports (Balrampur: ~3M; Triveni: 1-2M) given low opening stocks (~3.5M tonnes) and ~27.5M tonnes net production estimate [Balrampur Chini; Triveni Engineering; EID Parry].
*   **Ethanol**: Structural demand strong (OMC allocations ~40-42 cr litres for Trualt; 1,100 cr litres national tender) but feedstock restrictions create supply gap (~300 cr litres from sugar-based plants unable to participate) [Trualt Bioenergy; Triveni Engineering; Godavari Biorefineries].
*   **Basmati Rice**: Domestic demand robust (KRBL: +33% YoY domestic revenue; LT Foods: India +24% YoY; Chaman Lal: record PBT). Global consumption > production (USDA: 539 vs 537 MMT) supports prices [KRBL; LT Foods; Chaman Lal Setia].
*   **Animal Protein**: Poultry/buffalo meat consumption growing 4-5% long-term (Venkys); 70-75% non-veg population, chicken primary organized protein [Venkys; HMA Agro; Godrej Agrovet]. Fresh meat/ready-to-eat premiumization accelerating [DSM Fresh Foods; LT Foods; Godrej Agrovet].
*   **Coffee**: Premiumization, freeze-dried > spray-dried; green coffee range-bound $3,400-3,800/tonne [CCL Products].

**Divergence**
*   **Shrimp Exports**: Apex Frozen sees US demand improving (tariff stabilization, +59% YoY US volumes) and EU logistics recovering (+46% QoQ); Avanti Feeds calls US market “not lucrative” (21% total duty: 5.53% ADD, 5.77% CVD, 10% Sec 301) and cuts export guidance 19,000→17,500 MT [Apex Frozen; Avanti Feeds].
*   **Seed Weather Dependency**: Kaveri Seed reports maize recovery in Q2 (new hybrids, higher acreage) but cotton volumes -21% (illegal Bt cotton, El Niño); Nath Bio-Genes cites uneven rainfall (June -35% per IMD) hurting cotton, paddy offsetting at 37% revenue share [Kaveri Seed; Nath Bio-Genes].
*   **Cashew Logistics**: Aelea Commodities reports transit times 110-112 days (vs 35-45) bloating inventory/borrowing (Rs 232 cr vs Rs 111 cr); Pajson Agro runs ~95% utilization, notes global cashew CAGR 9-10% but flags geopolitical/forex pressure [Aelea Commodities; Pajson Agro].

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Sugarcane**: Balrampur: every Rs 10/quintal SAP hike = Rs 100 cr incremental cost [Balrampur Chini]. EID Parry: Karnataka cane landed cost Rs 3,798/MT (vs Rs 3,620), farmer agitations forcing ~Rs 3,800 vs FRP Rs 3,300-3,400 [EID Parry]. Triveni: Haryana SAP +Rs 15 to Rs 430/quintal (SS 26-27), SS 27-28 announced at Rs 450 [Triveni Engineering].
*   **Maize**: Trualt: 25-30% surge in Q2, Rs 26-27/kg, “completely erodes standalone maize-ethanol profitability” [Trualt Bioenergy; Triveni Engineering]. BCL: Rs 29-30/kg, “squeezing ethanol viability” [BCL Industries]. Godavari: higher maize prices pressuring grain distillery margins [Godavari Biorefineries].
*   **Fish Meal / Soy Meal**: Avanti Feeds: Fish meal Rs 210/kg, Soy meal Rs 80/kg, Wheat flour Rs 36/kg – “lowest margins in business cycle” [Avanti Feeds].
*   **Paddy**: LT Foods: new crop ‘26 paddy +22-25% YoY (paddy Rs 43/kg vs Rs 36 Mar ’26; rice Rs 60/kg vs Rs 54) [LT Foods]. KRBL: basmati paddy Rs 4,500-5,200/quintal (zero carryover, high export realizations) [KRBL]. Chaman Lal: high paddy prices driven by exporters with Iran exposure [Chaman Lal Setia].
*   **Logistics/Freight**: Aelea: Red Sea conflict transit 110-112 days. Chaman Lal: ocean freight $200-250 → $5,500-6,000/container (“catastrophic”). HMA Agro: shipping 6-10x at peak, stabilized last 2 months. Apex Frozen: Cape of Good Hope routing extends EU sailing times [Aelea Commodities; Chaman Lal Setia; HMA Agro; Apex Frozen].
*   **Green Coffee**: CCL: $3,400-3,800/tonne range-bound, pre-harvest prices steady [CCL Products].
*   **Margins**: Balrampur sugar PBIT +166% YoY; distillery margins compressed (stagnant ethanol prices) [Balrampur Chini]. Triveni sugar segment PBIT Rs 113 cr H1 (vs -Rs 5 cr); alcohol PBIT +44% H1 [Triveni Engineering]. Godavari bio-chemicals EBITDA margin 10.8% (+60 bps); sugar/co-gen loss Rs 19.3 cr [Godavari Biorefineries]. Trualt H1 EBITDA margin 18.2%, Q2 14.03% (maize impact) [Trualt Bioenergy]. KRBL EBITDA margin 21.1% (vs 16.6%), guiding 17-19% FY27 [KRBL]. LT Foods consolidated EBITDA margin 11% (vs 11.4%), targeting 13-14% medium-term [LT Foods]. CCL EBITDA/kg Rs 135-140 stable [CCL Products]. Venkys broiler cost Rs 104-105/kg (vs Rs 90 Sep ’25), current ~Rs 110 [Venkys].
