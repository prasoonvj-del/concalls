# Agri & Sugar: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Agri & Sugar sector was characterized by strong top-line delivery across most players (AWL revenue +26%, Jain +10%, TruAlt +606%), but this was heavily tempered by severe weather headwinds. Managements across the board highlighted the adverse impacts of El Nino, deficient rainfall, and the worst monsoon in 35-50 years across key agricultural belts, leading to input cost inflation and rural demand sluggishness. 

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements universally cited weather vagaries—specifically rain deficits and El Nino—as major drags on rural demand and crop sowing (affecting maize, cotton, rice, and sugarcane). 
* **Divergence:** 
  * **AWL** noted a clear split where urban demand outperformed rural demand, with rural areas showing sluggishness.
  * **Jain** faced severe drought conditions in West and South India (representing 80-85% of its business), forcing the company to intentionally let go of Rs 200–250 crore of project and solar pump business due to tight state liquidity.
  * **Kaveri Seed** experienced a stark divergence between segments: while cotton volumes dropped significantly due to illegal Bt cotton and rain deficits, non-cotton revenues grew 29% in H1, and maize recovered in Q2.
  * **TruAlt Bioenergy** saw strong structural demand supported by OMC ethanol allocations, despite lower raw material availability.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreements:** Inflation in raw materials was a recurring theme. Surging prices of maize (up 25–30%, noted by TruAlt and Kaveri) and polymer volatility (Jain) squeezed quarterly margins. AWL also battled supply chain disruptions in sunflower, leading to a temporary inventory buildup.
* **Margin metrics:** 
  * **TruAlt:** EBITDA margin moderated to 14.03% in Q2 due to maize inflation, down from its H1 average of 18.2%.
  * **AWL:** Edible Oil EBITDA stood at Rs 4,100/MT, and Industry Essentials at Rs 4,300/MT.
  * **Jain:** Maintained robust margins in Hi-Tech Micro-Irrigation at 18.8%, with Food at ~12% and Plastics at ~9%.
  * **Kaveri:** H1 EBITDA margin stood at 30.48%.

## Capex & capacity
* **AWL:** Focused on expanding its acquired Tops (GD Foods) brand in North India before scaling to East and Central India over the next 1–1.5 years.
* **Jain:** Planned capex includes Rs 30–40 crore for a Tomato JV, ~Rs 100 crore for Phase 2 of the beverage business, and growth capex for biochar next fiscal year.
* **Kaveri Seed:** CWIP stands at Rs 120–130 crore (largely for machinery and agricultural land acquisition); expanding internationally by incorporating wholly-owned subsidiaries in 8 countries.
* **TruAlt:** 1G ethanol capex is complete; active capital allocation is now directed toward Compressed Biogas (CBG)—building 4–5 new plants (~Rs 300 crore total) to reach 72 MT/day, alongside front-end engineering for a Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) plant.

## What changed since last quarter
* **AWL Agri Business Ltd:** Management's tone shifted from "resilient" last quarter to explicitly noting rural demand pressure and rainfall deficit risks. Guidance for Edible Oil and Industry Essentials was maintained, projecting ~Rs 85,000 crore revenue for FY27.
* **TruAlt Bioenergy Ltd:** Received an additional 10 crore litres allocation from OMCs post an August Supreme Court judgment (plus another 5 crore litres), and announced a planned slump sale of the Badami plant for Rs 171 crore to repay debt.

## Bullish vs cautious

| Company | Tone | Key reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AWL Agri Business Ltd** | Mixed | Strong volume (+14%) and revenue (+26%) growth, but PAT dipped slightly and OCF turned negative due to a Rs 6,000 crore inventory buildup. |
| **Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd** | Positive | Delivered double-digit revenue growth, improved profitability, and strong cash conversion despite severe drought. |
| **Kaveri Seed Co Ltd** | Mixed | Robust H1 PAT growth (+26%) and non-cotton momentum, offset by cotton volume declines from illegal Bt seed proliferation. |
| **TruAlt Bioenergy Ltd** | Mixed | Massive YoY revenue surge (+606%) and profit turnaround, but Q2 margins were squeezed by a 25–30% jump in maize prices. |

## Company one-liners
* **AWL Agri Business Ltd:** Delivered strong volume-led revenue growth offset by inventory-driven working capital pressure.
* **Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd:** Posted a profitable quarter with strong cash conversion and working capital improvement, prioritizing debt reduction amidst drought headwinds.
* **Kaveri Seed Company Ltd:** Achieved robust H1 PAT growth of 26% driven by non-cotton segments, despite El Nino and illegal Bt cotton hurdles.
* **TruAlt Bioenergy Ltd:** Recorded explosive revenue growth and an H1 profit turnaround while balancing raw material inflation in ethanol and aggressively expanding its CBG footprint.

## What to watch next quarter
* **AWL:** Normalization of the Rs 6,000 crore sunflower inventory buildup and cash flow recovery during the OND quarter.
* **Jain Irrigation:** Further progress on receivables reduction (targeting Rs 300 crore+ reduction by March 2027) and the wind-down of project business.
* **Kaveri Seed:** Liquidation and utilization of seed inventory in Rabi sowing, alongside updates on international subsidiary rollouts.
* **TruAlt Bioenergy:** Ramp-up of capacity utilization toward the 75–80% target, execution of new CBG plants, and ESY 2026–2027 allocations.

*Based on: AWL Agri Business Ltd, Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd, Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd_DVR, Kaveri Seed Company Ltd, Trualt Bioenergy Ltd*
