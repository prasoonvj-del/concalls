# Godavari Biorefineries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth and margin expansion in the bio-based chemicals business drove a return to positive consolidated EBITDA, overcoming seasonal headwinds in the sugar and ethanol segment.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained the bio-based chemicals revenue target of Rs 240 crore per quarter by Q3 FY28, backed by an additional Rs 25 crore debottlenecking investment.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to a more optimistic view on bio-based chemicals due to strong customer demand and higher fossil fuel costs creating a favorable pricing gap, despite ongoing drought and policy hurdles in sugar/ethanol.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Mention of supply chain disruptions from the Hormuz area impacting product pricing; severe drought conditions in Maharashtra and Karnataka highlighted as a fresh immediate risk.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): The grain-based distillery commissioned at the end of Q1 is now operating to meet OMC orders; CDSCO application for TNBC trials is expected to receive permissions in the current quarter.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 549.2 crore (up 27.5% YoY); Total income: Rs 549.8 crore (up 26.6% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 10.9 crore (vs negative Rs 4.4 crore in Q2 FY26); EBITDA margin: 2% (vs -1% YoY)
- **Consolidated Net Loss:** Rs 15.2 crore (vs Rs 41.6 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Bio-based Chemicals Revenue:** Rs 204.2 crore (up 38.8% YoY); Segment EBITDA: Rs 22.1 crore (up 47% YoY); EBITDA margin: 10.8% (vs 10.2% YoY)
- **Integrated Sugar & Co-gen Revenue:** Rs 334.2 crore combined with ethanol (vs Rs 275.7 crore last year); Sugar/Co-gen EBITDA loss: Rs 19.3 crore (vs Rs 23.1 crore loss in Q2 FY26)
- **Ethanol EBITDA:** Rs 10.6 crore (vs Rs 4.7 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 1,109.7 crore (up 14.6% YoY); H1 EBITDA: Rs 13.4 crore (vs Rs 2.1 crore YoY); H1 Net Loss: Rs 34.6 crore (vs Rs 57.6 crore YoY)
- **Total Distillery Capacity:** 800 kL per day

### Highlights
- Secured international patents for anti-cancer pharmaceutical compositions, bio-based rubber enforcement technology, and antiviral compounds across Europe, China, Mexico, and the US.
- Recognized with the Manufacturing Process Innovator of the Year Award at the FICCI Chemicals and Petrochemicals Award 2026.
- Continued progress on the CO₂-to-DME pilot plant with ICT Mumbai, targeted for completion by March 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the target to reach Rs 240 crore per quarter in bio-based chemicals by Q3 FY28, supported by a Rs 25 crore debottlenecking investment starting April 2027.
- Expects stronger sugar prices going forward owing to lower production from drought conditions and restricted juice/B-heavy molasses diversion.
- Anticipates profitable operations in Q3 and Q4 driven by the seasonal start of the sugar and ethanol operations.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global geopolitical and climate volatility have increased the scarcity of fossil resources, driving demand for sustainable bio-based alternatives and reliable supply chains.
- India's E20 milestone reinforces the integrated biorefinery model, though recent government restrictions on sugarcane juice and B-heavy molasses diversion for ethanol have tightened feedstock dynamics.
- Rising maize prices have increased margin pressures on grain-based ethanol production.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Bio-based chemical margins expanded due to debottlenecking benefits and improved operating efficiencies.
- Fossil fuel price increases helped bridge the cost gap between fossil and green alternatives for customers.
- Maize price inflation created near-term margin pressures for grain-based ethanol.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total distillery capacity stands at 800 kL per day, including the newly operational grain-based facility.
- Commenced a Rs 25 crore investment in further debottlenecking of bio-based chemistry to drive future specialty chemical growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory levels showed seasonal contraction compared to March peaks.
- Focus remains on optimizing working capital and maintaining financial discipline.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe drought conditions in Maharashtra and Karnataka affecting the farm economy and cane availability.
- Government restrictions on sugarcane juice and B-heavy molasses diversion impacting ethanol production volumes.
- High maize prices creating margin headwinds for grain-based ethanol.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vivek Gupta (Star Investments) asked about the revenue drivers for bio-based chemicals and the bridge to Rs 240 crore:** Management explained that Q2 outperformance (Rs 204 crores vs Rs 190 crores targeted) stemmed from debottlenecking and temporary price increases due to Hormuz supply issues; the Rs 240 crore target by Q3 FY28 will be driven by the Rs 25 crore debottlenecking investments.
- **Kranti asked about timelines for profitability and the DME/drug discovery projects:** Management noted H2 is seasonally profitable, the DME pilot completes by March 2027, and CDSCO permission for TNBC trials is expected this quarter with preliminary results in 6-9 months.
- **Nivesh Verma asked about funding for the TNBC drug candidate:** Management stated preliminary efficacy trials will cost Rs 15-20 crores max, after which they plan to out-license the molecule to pharma companies.
- **Hitesh Agarwal asked about inventory reduction and cash flow:** Management clarified that inventory reduction reflects normal seasonal variations where March sugar stocks are highest and September stocks are lower.
- **Varun Mishra asked about the sensitivity of ethanol economics and sugar recovery:** Management noted that restricted cane diversion will increase sugar production, and stronger sugar prices are expected to offset ethanol pressures.

### One-line takeaway
Godavari Biorefineries reported a return to positive consolidated EBITDA in Q2 FY27, underpinned by robust growth in bio-based specialty chemicals despite seasonal agricultural and policy headwinds in sugar and ethanol.
