# BCL Industries Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong margin expansion and country liquor growth, offset by a temporary fire-related plant shutdown, lower revenues from exiting the edible oil business, and competitive pressure on ENA/ethanol realizations.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 623 crore (vs Rs 820 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 66 crore, up 17% YoY
- EBITDA Margin: 10.5%, up 370 bps YoY (from 6.8% in Q1 FY26)
- PAT: Rs 36 crore, up 6% YoY
- PAT Margin: 5.7%, up from 4.1% in Q1 FY26
- Distillery Segment EBITDA Margin: 12.41% (vs 11.8% in Q4 FY26 and 10% in Q1 FY26)
- ENA Volumes: 19,376 KL (vs 7,960 KL in Q1 FY26)
- Ethanol Volumes: 37,787 KL
- Country Liquor Volumes: 6,37,993 boxes, up 42% QoQ and 46% YoY
- ENA Realizations: Declined to Rs 58 per liter (from Rs 70 per liter in Q1 FY26)
- Maize Procurement Cost (previous quarter): Rs 22 to Rs 23 per kg (current price around Rs 25 per kg)
- DDGS Realizations: Rs 24 to Rs 25 per kg
- Profit on sale of fixed assets (dismantled unit): Rs 199.47 lakh
- Total Debt: Approximately Rs 360 crore (including working capital utilization of around Rs 60 crore)

### Highlights
- Experienced a fire incident on June 19, 2026, at an ethanol storage tank in Bathinda, Punjab, resulting in a temporary shutdown of the 200 KLPD plant; no injuries reported. Full recovery of losses and P&L impact expected via insurance.
- Completed the acquisition of the remaining 25% stake in Svaksha Distillery Limited on June 30, 2026, making the 350 KLPD Kharagpur (West Bengal) plant a wholly-owned subsidiary.
- Commercial trials for the newly added 150 KLPD unit at Bathinda began in late June 2026 and were successfully commissioned in early July, helping mitigate the revenue impact of the temporary shutdown.
- Completed exit from the packaged oil business, including formal closure of the Oil & Vanaspati Unit at Bathinda. Commissioned a maize oil extraction unit at Svaksha for backward integration.
- Launched Jamun Vodka in July 2026, building on the success of Punjab Raspberry launched in Q4.
- Total debt reduced; working capital limits being further reduced by Rs 50 crore in August; SBICAP released the pledge on 75 lakh shares.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects grain ethanol demand to improve slightly next year due to likely restrictions on sugarcane-based ethanol.
- Long-term growth drivers anticipated from isobutanol policy, sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), flex fuel engines, and ethanol as a clean cooking fuel.
- Management aims to bring working capital utilization down to zero over time.
- Steady-state EBITDA margins for the company are projected to remain around 10% to 12%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ENA and private ethanol realizations remain under pressure due to industry oversupply, with private ethanol sold to Reliance averaging Rs 58 ex-factory.
- Country liquor market in Punjab is close to 1.25 crore cases per annum; BCL targets 30 lakh cases this year.
- Flex fuel vehicle adoption is currently minimal (only one model for sale), so demand creation from E85/E100 will take time.
- Biodiesel is currently unremunerative due to low prices and imported raw materials, leading management to hold the Svaksha biodiesel project on hold.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 115 TPH paddy straw boiler meets 100% of distillery steam and power requirements.
- Maize prices have risen to around Rs 25 per kg (from Rs 22-23 per kg previously).
- Average EBITDA margin across maize-based ethanol and ENA is about 12% (yielding roughly Rs 9 to Rs 10 per liter), whereas government FCI rice-based ethanol operates almost at cost.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 250 KLPD grain-based plant at Fatehabad is currently on hold while management evaluates industry headwinds and policy roadmap.
- Malt unit and biodiesel projects are on hold/under conceptualization with no set timelines.
- 200 KLPD Bathinda plant expected to resume operations in approximately 15 days following storage tank repairs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt stands around Rs 360 crore (inclusive of ~Rs 60 crore working capital utilization).
- Pledge on 75 lakh shares previously held with SBICAP fully released due to improved financial health.
- Working capital limit being reduced by another Rs 50 crore in August 2026.
- Real estate market in Bathinda is slow, so there is no immediate timeline for land sales from the old edible oil plant.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Fire incident temporarily shut down the 200 KLPD Bathinda ethanol plant.
- Over-supply and competitive pressure depressing ENA and private ethanol realizations.
- Rising maize and fuel raw material prices.
- Slow real estate market delaying land monetization in Bathinda.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepesh Sancheti (Maanya Finance) asked about unit economics and margins for maize-based ethanol/ENA.** Management replied that maize-based ethanol and ENA yield an average EBITDA margin of about 12% (roughly Rs 9-10 per liter), while FCI rice-based ethanol is sold almost at cost. *(Revealing regarding product-wise profitability)*
2. **Abhishek Kale (Individual Investor) questioned the viability of biodiesel given raw material sourcing.** Management explained that biodiesel projects are on hold because finished product rates are low and raw materials are often imported, whereas government policy prioritizes indigenous farm-income drivers like isobutanol. *(Clarified strategic pivot away from biodiesel)*
3. **Bhavesh (Individual Investor) asked about the status of the Fatehabad expansion and debt reduction.** Management stated the Fatehabad project is on hold due to recent social media backlash and policy uncertainty, but debt and working capital are actively being paid down. *(Showed cautious capex stance)*
4. **Bhavesh asked about the Supreme Court order regarding OMC procurement.** Management confirmed BCL is one of the biggest gainers, securing an order of around 4.5 crore liters lasting through November. *(Highlighted near-term order book visibility)*
5. **Navneet Bhaiya (Individual Investor) inquired about debt levels and buyback plans.** Management clarified current debt is around Rs 360 crore, working toward minimizing working capital to zero, and stated a share buyback is not on the agenda. *(Gave clarity on capital allocation priorities)*

### One-line takeaway
BCL Industries reported a 17% YoY rise in Q1 FY27 EBITDA and margin expansion to 10.5% despite a temporary plant fire and falling ENA realizations, supported by robust country liquor volumes and backward integration.
