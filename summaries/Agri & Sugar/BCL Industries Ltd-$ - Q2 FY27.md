# BCL Industries Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Margins under pressure due to rising maize prices and regulatory changes in the ethanol tender, despite full operational recovery and volume growth.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Guidance not explicitly stated as raised or cut, but management noted margins will come under pressure due to rising raw material costs.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from "cautiously optimistic / ethanol demand improving" to recognizing major headwinds from oversupply, surging maize prices (hovering around Rs 29-30 per kg), and tighter government tender rules, making it a "survival of the fittest."
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New focus on Compressed Biogas (CBG) with a 20-tonne-per-day plant planned in Fatehabad; new discussion on FCI procuring rice with only up to 10% broken rice; old fire incident concern disappeared as operations fully resumed.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The 200 KLPD Bathinda plant successfully resumed operations on August 28, 2026, as promised.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 695 crore (H1 FY27: Rs 1,319 crore)
- EBITDA: Rs 66 crore (H1 FY27: Rs 132 crore, up 5.9% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 9.5% for Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27: 10%, up 191 bps YoY)
- PAT: Rs 29 crore
- PAT Margin: 4.2%
- Distillery Segment EBITDA Margin: 11.9% (vs 11% in Q2 FY26, up 90 bps YoY)
- Oil Refinery Revenue: Rs 188 crore
- Oil Refinery EBITDA Margin: 3.7%
- ENA Volumes: 22,500 KL (vs 12,130 KL in Q2 FY26)
- Ethanol Volumes: 39,500 KL
- Country Liquor Sales: 6.42 lakh cases (up 41.4% YoY)
- ESY 2025-26 Total Order Book: 1.5 lakh KL, up 41% (including additional allocation of 4.37 crore liters for Q4)
- ENA Realizations: Rs 68 to Rs 70 per liter

### Highlights
- Temporary disruption at the 200 KLPD Bathinda plant from the June 19 fire was fully resolved, with operations resuming on August 28 after statutory approvals; insurance claim expected by Q3 end.
- Entire installed distillery capacity of 900 KLPD is now operational (550 KLPD at Sangat, 350 KLPD at Svaksha).
- Continued exit from packaged oil, focusing entirely on soft oil refining (400 tonnes per day each at Bathinda and Kharagpur) and maize oil extraction.
- One-time gain of Rs 2 crore from the sale of assets of the former oil and vanaspati unit at Bathinda.
- *Continues pattern from record:* Moving ahead with diversification into CBG and planning a malt plant in Bathinda for IMFL launch in FY28-FY29, continuing the strategic pivot toward consumer segments.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects margins to face pressure due to major burdens shifting to grain distilleries and increased demand for maize.
- Peak revenue potential for the company with added capacity estimated around Rs 2,900 crore.
- Company plans to reinvest cash flows into CBG, malt, and potentially Goyal Distillery, rather than increasing dividends.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Ethanol blending targets are not at risk due to industry overcapacity, but high raw material prices make it "survival of the fittest."
- ENA demand is strong, reinforced by peak demand season and sugar companies diverting less toward ENA.
- Biodiesel is currently unviable at OMC tender rates (Rs 95 per liter) given high byproduct prices.
- Denatured alcohol demand from API companies is strong, but management avoids forward contracts due to grain price volatility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Maize prices increased significantly, hovering around Rs 29 to Rs 30 per kg.
- Distillery margins improved by 90 bps YoY to 11.9% despite higher maize prices, aided by shared infrastructure and operational efficiencies.
- DDGS prices increased slightly.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned 20-tonne-per-day CBG plant at Fatehabad: tender for plant and machinery to float post-Diwali, targeting ~Rs 70 crore revenue at 90-95% utilization and 40-45% EBITDA margins.
- Goyal Distillery file moved to get environmental clearance for ENA and ethanol flexibility (9-10 months process).
- Malt plant application in Punjab to be moved post-elections, targeting IMFL launch in FY28-FY29.
- No immediate plans to start bottling in West Bengal.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital utilization continues to decrease with cash generation; debt reduction ongoing per repayment schedules.
- Land bank: Apartment stock mostly finished; remaining stock plots valued around Rs 10 crore; old oil mill land to be monetized next year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Pressure on ethanol margins due to surging maize prices and government policy changes placing the burden on grain distilleries.
- Volatility in grain prices preventing forward contracting in denatured alcohol.
- Lower viability of biodiesel and ethanol at current raw material costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Udit Sehgal (PinPoint X Capital) asked about ethanol tender expectations and margins.** Management stated allocations should be similar to last year, but margins will face pressure due to rising maize prices, mitigated partly by passing costs in the ENA business. *(Revealing regarding margin headwinds)*
2. **Udit Sehgal asked about the CBG plant potential.** Management stated the 20 TPD Fatehabad plant expects ~Rs 70 crore revenue at 90-95% utilization and 40-45% EBITDA margins. *(Gave specific unit economics for CBG)*
3. **Imran Khan (Longbow India) asked about capital allocation and cash deployment.** Management stated cash will be reinvested into CBG, malt, and plant expansions rather than increasing dividends in the coming years. *(Clarified dividend and capital allocation policy)*
4. **Deepesh J. Sancheti (Maanya Finance) asked about maize price levels.** Management corrected that maize prices are hovering around Rs 29 to Rs 30 per kg, squeezing ethanol viability. *(Highlighted raw material cost inflation)*
5. **Bhavesh (Individual Investor) asked about peak revenue potential.** Management stated peak consolidated revenue with added capacity is estimated around Rs 2,900 crore. *(Provided top-line guidance)*
6. **Bhavesh asked about timelines for IMFL and Goyal Distillery.** Management replied IMFL is targeted for FY28-FY29, and Goyal Distillery EC approval will take 9 to 10 months. *(Gave project timelines)*

### One-line takeaway
BCL Industries reported stable Q2 FY27 performance with revenue of Rs 695 crore and EBITDA of Rs 66 crore, but warned of margin pressures ahead from surging maize prices despite full operational recovery and strong ENA/country liquor volumes.
