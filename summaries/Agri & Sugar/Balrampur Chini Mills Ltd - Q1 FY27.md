# Balrampur Chini Mills Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stable operations, firming domestic sugar prices, and good progress on the PLA project.

### Key numbers
- Sugar inventory as of June 30: 45.67 lakh quintals at an average carrying cost of INR 37.19 per kg.
- PLA project capex spent by the end of July: INR 2,180 crore.
- PLA capacity: 80,000 tonnes.
- PLA expected average capacity utilization in the first year (Jan-Mar): around 40%.
- Brazil sugar production estimate: around 40 million tonnes (per management).

### Highlights
- Q1 FY27 revenues improved across Sugar and Distillery segments, supported by higher sugar realizations and distillery volumes.
- Sugarcane crushing and production increased due to improved cane availability.
- Maizapur grain distillery and sugar plant are now ready for operations.
- Lactic acid commissioning is on track for October, and PLA commissioning is on track for December.
- Product development, customer trials, and market engagement for PLA are progressing well.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a positive net impact overall, offsetting potential higher sugarcane costs and restrictions on distillery diversion with higher sugar prices.
- Lactic acid plant commissioning targeted for October; PLA plant commissioning targeted for December.
- Management expects around 40% average capacity utilization for the PLA plant from January to March.
- First meaningful clarity on the 2026-27 sugar season production is expected around the end of September.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Season 2025-26 turned out tighter than anticipated with lower production, healthy domestic consumption, and ethanol diversion resulting in inventory drawdown.
- Domestic sugar prices have firmed up due to the tighter demand-supply balance.
- Brazil is expected to produce around 40 million tonnes, with ethanol being more profitable, keeping diversion towards sugar lower. EU sugar production is seeing some contraction, with an overall global deficit of 1 to 2 million tonnes anticipated.
- In the PLA segment, regulatory movements towards sustainable packaging (e.g., for pan masala and gutka) present a large addressable opportunity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher sugar realizations provided support against increased cane costs during the quarter.
- Distillery margins were resilient in Q1 as operations used B-heavy and maize-based ethanol largely, with negligible juice-based ethanol sales.
- Management assumes potential sugarcane price hikes (SAP) and a likely ban on B-heavy and juice diversion for the upcoming season, leaving only C-heavy allowed.
- Grain-based distillation relies on broken rice and maize; government availability measures for broken rice are anticipated.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex spent on the 80,000-tonne PLA plant reached INR 2,180 crore by the end of July.
- Maizapur plant is fully equipped to manufacture sugar and run on grain/C-heavy.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Carrying cost of sugar inventory: INR 37.19 per kg for 45.67 lakh quintals as of June 30.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility and speculation regarding sugar production estimates and closing stocks from various sources.
- Potential restrictions or bans on B-heavy and juice diversion for ethanol in the upcoming season.
- Uncertainty regarding potential state advised price (SAP) hikes for sugarcane ahead of state elections.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjay Manyal (DAM Capital):** Asked about distillery volumes and C-heavy assumptions if B-heavy/juice diversion is banned. *Management answered that a ban on B-heavy and juice is a reasonable assumption, C-heavy and grain targets will be maintained, and net profitability will be supported by higher sugar prices.*
2. **Pankaj Tibrewal (Ikigai Asset Manager):** Inquired about the ban on plastics for gutka/pan masala and the PLA opportunity size. *Management stated they are in active trials, the opportunity is very large, and guided to around 40% capacity utilization for Jan-March.*
3. **Prashant Biyani (Elara Capital):** Asked about cane crop quality given below-normal rainfall. *Management stated rainfall in East UP has been ideal for cane with no negative impact from El Niño.*
4. **Shailesh Kanani (Asian Market Securities):** Asked about distillery margin sustainability and transfer pricing. *Management clarified that quarterly margins vary based on feedstock, and Maizapur off-season expenses were retained in the sugar division.*
5. **Vikram Suryavanshi (Phillip Capital):** Asked for India sugar balance sheet numbers and international output. *Management declined to give specific balance sheet figures due to market confusion and estimated Brazil production at ~40 million tonnes.*

### One-line takeaway
Balrampur Chini Mills enters the upcoming season with tight industry inventory and firm sugar realizations expected to offset cane cost pressures, while its large-scale PLA project remains on track for commissioning by December.
