# Pajson Agro India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady revenue growth, high plant utilization, and a strategic acquisition announced to expand global capacity.

### Key numbers
- Total income: INR 145 crores (up 23% YoY)
- Revenue from operations: Approximately INR 143 crores
- EBITDA: Approximately INR 20 crores (up 21% HoH)
- EBITDA margin: 14% (up 190 bps HoH)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Approximately INR 12.6 crores (standalone PAT approximately INR 12.5 crores; up 19% HoH)
- Plant capacity utilization: Around 95% in H1 FY27 (vs 86% in FY26)
- Institutional business contribution: 37.5% of revenue in H1 FY27 (vs 34% in FY26)
- Royal Meva revenue contribution: 4.6% in H1 FY27 (vs 1.36% in H1 FY26)
- Combined processing capacity post-acquisition: 113,000 metric tonnes (current India capacity: 18,000 metric tonnes; Vizianagaram greenfield addition: 35,000 metric tonnes; Pacific Global facility: 60,000 metric tonnes)

### Highlights
- Andhra Pradesh plant operated near full capacity (~95% utilization).
- Appointed Mr. Vu Thai Son as strategic advisor.
- Board approved 100% stake acquisition of Pacific Global FZCO (operating a 60,000 metric tonne facility in Cote d'Ivoire) via a share swap deal without cash consideration.
- Board approved Pajson Agro Employee Stock Option Scheme 2026.
- Institutional customers grew with large recurring orders from leading food and snack confectionery companies, supported by 178 distributors across 20+ states.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27-FY28 to be a transformational period.
- Vizianagaram facility commercial production expected to begin in Q4 FY27 (delayed by a couple of months due to three cyclones in Andhra Pradesh).
- FY28 utilization guidance: 60% to 70% for the new greenfield plant while maintaining near 90% at the current plant.
- FY29 target: Combined production utilization rates at 85% to 90%.
- Pacific Global acquisition and consolidation expected to complete in FY27 (2 to 3 months). Pacific Global plant utilization expected at 50% in FY27 and full utilization next financial year.
- Long-term goal for Royal Meva: Target 15% of total revenue in the next 3 to 5 years.
- 5 to 7-year vision: Achieve 5% to 8% of global market share and a leadership position in the world cashew industry.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Kernel demand remained healthy.
- Global cashew market grows at a CAGR of 9% to 10%.
- India represents about 30% to 35% of the global cashew market standalone, with the remaining in the rest of the world.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs remained high; management chose to protect volumes by gradually adjusting prices and maintaining higher raw nut inventory.
- Higher raw nut inventory added to working capital and interest costs in the near term.
- Pressures on margins in H1 FY27 driven by geopolitical reasons, forex movements, and increased shipping line surcharges/freight costs.
- Africa processing offers higher EBITDA margins than India (5% to 7% higher / 15% to 20% group OPM margins expected).
- Cote d'Ivoire offers tax holidays and subsidized tax rates for the next decade.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Vizianagaram facility will add 35,000 metric tonnes, taking India capacity to 53,000 metric tonnes; trials expected by February. IPO proceeds being deployed here.
- Pacific Global acquisition adds 60,000 metric tonnes in Cote d'Ivoire, taking total combined capacity to 113,000 metric tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital requirements will increase as volumes scale and inventory expands.
- Long-term debt in the new acquisition is nil. Working capital lines being discussed with international institutions.
- Working capital typically runs at around 25% to 30% of turnover.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disturbances and forex volatility putting pressure on earnings.
- Transit delays and freight issues impacting factory material receipts.
- Operational efficiencies, team controls, and internal governance across multiple countries as scale increases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhishek Malhotra asked about the Vizianagaram delay and rationale for Pacific Global acquisition:** Management cited three cyclones in Andhra Pradesh for a 2-month delay (trials now in February). The acquisition brings farmgate sourcing, processing, and customer relationships under one listed entity via a total share swap at NAV without promoter premiums.
- **Mayur Parekh asked about minority shareholder protection in the share swap:** Management emphasized that the transaction is strongly EPS-accretive, executed at independent valuation (NAV), and adheres to strict corporate governance without charging promoter premiums.
- **Purva Shah asked about the valuation and revenue of Pacific Global:** Management clarified that Pacific Global is a pre-revenue/recently started greenfield project in Cote d'Ivoire (subsidiary of a UAE entity), which explains the valuation difference relative to market cap.
- **Aryan Bhatia asked about African procurement and distribution strategy:** Management stated Indian plants will serve India while the Ivory Coast plant serves the rest of the world, reducing reliance on merchant traders via direct farm-gate relationships.
- **Kumar Vivek asked about debt and tax benefits in Africa:** Management confirmed no long-term debt in the acquired entity, parity with Indian interest rates in Cote d'Ivoire, and a decade of tax holidays/subsidies provided by the local government.

### One-line takeaway
Pajson Agro reported steady H1 FY27 revenue of INR 145 crores and announced a transformational 60,000 MT capacity acquisition in Cote d'Ivoire via a share swap, expanding its global footprint to 113,000 MT.
