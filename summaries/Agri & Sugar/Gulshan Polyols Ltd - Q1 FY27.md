# Gulshan Polyols Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong start with record revenue, significant YoY growth in EBITDA and PAT, and favorable operating conditions.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 646 crore (up 8% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 91 crore (up 135% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 14.2% (compared to 6.5% YoY)
- PAT: Rs 54 crore (up 307% YoY)
- Ethanol segment revenue: Rs 426 crore; EBITDA: Rs 81 crore (margin: 18%)
- Grain processing segment revenue: Rs 170 crore; EBITDA: Rs 8 crore
- Mineral chemical segment revenue: Rs 24 crore; EBITDA: Rs 5 crore (margin: 23%)
- Ethanol order book: ~19 crore litres
- Raw material prices (maize average): Rs 23 to Rs 25 across 4 plants
- Export share: ~5% to 6% of turnover (~Rs 18 crore)

### Highlights
- Highest ever quarterly revenue in company history at Rs 646 crore.
- Transitioning from investment phase to execution, optimization, and cash generation.
- Ethanol business remains the primary growth engine.
- On-site plant at Trident expected to be operational by the end of FY27.
- MP state government capital subsidy of Rs 5 crore received and adjusted against capital spend.

### Guidance & outlook
- Full year FY27 consolidated revenue target: ~Rs 2,600 crore (Ethanol: Rs 1,700-1,800 crore from ~22 crore litres supply; Grain processing: ~Rs 800 crore; Mineral processing: ~Rs 100 crore).
- Consolidated EBITDA margin guidance for FY27: 10% to 11%.
- Consolidated PAT margin guidance for FY27: 5% to 6%.
- Grain processing segment EBITDA margin expected to be ~5% for the year.
- Ethanol capacity utilization target by FY28: 100% to 110% via debottlenecking.
- Next major capex planned for FY28 in specialty and import-substitute chemicals.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India achieved E20 blending milestone ahead of schedule; E30 targeted by 2030.
- Ethanol blending increase beyond 20% may see a temporary delay of 6 months to 1 year due to recent social media noise/protests, but E20 is permanent.
- Talks are underway regarding opening ethanol exports to neighboring countries (like Japan, Indonesia), which management noted could happen soon.
- Worst of the industry downcycle for grain processing is behind us; correction in maize prices improved export competitiveness and domestic realizations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 margins benefited from conducive raw material prices and all-time high DDGS prices.
- Q2 is seasonally under pressure due to higher grain prices ahead of the Kharif harvest.
- Raw material holding capped at 30-40 (or 40-45) days due to large volumes (~3,000 tonnes of grain processed daily across 4 plants).
- Ethanol raw material mix: 40% FCI rice (mandatory), ~50% maize, 10% DDGS/broken rice.
- Availability of FCI rice since March 2025 has brought significant relief to the industry.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total installed ethanol capacity: ~26 crore litres per annum.
- Minor debottlenecking planned to take capacity utilization to 100%-110% by FY28.
- Specialty chemicals expansion planned for FY28; products currently under R&D/evaluation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Focus in FY27 on improving capacity utilization, reducing working capital intensity, strengthening cash flows, and maintaining prudent leverage.
- Natural hedge used for foreign exchange exposure (matching exports and imports); no forward contracts/hedging used.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity-linked business causes quarterly margin fluctuations based on raw material prices.
- Temporary social media noise/protests regarding ethanol blending.
- Uttar Pradesh location for starch/grain processing makes exports unviable due to high transport costs to ports.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Darshil Jhaveri (Crown Capital) on Q1 margin vs full-year guidance:** Management explained Q1 was exceptional due to favorable raw materials and high DDGS prices, whereas Q2 faces seasonal pressure before the Kharif cycle, keeping full-year guidance conservative at 10%-11%. (Did not dodge)
2. **Darshil Jhaveri on ethanol pushback/E30 timeline:** Management stated E20 is permanent (saving Rs 40,000 crore in forex), though higher blending targets might face a 6-month to 1-year delay due to social noise. (Did not dodge)
3. **Pushkar Jain (Mili Capital) on grain export viability:** Management clarified that while competitors near ports export starch, UP-based plants like Muzaffarnagar face high logistics costs to ports, making direct exports unviable. (Did not dodge)
4. **Nitin Awasthi (InCred Equities) on accounting of subsidies:** CFO clarified that grants are accounted strictly on a receipt basis and no P&L subsidy income was booked in Q1, though Rs 5 crore capital subsidy from MP was adjusted against plant capex. (Did not dodge)

### One-line takeaway
Gulshan Polyols delivered a record-breaking Q1 FY27 driven by ethanol growth, maintaining its full-year consolidated revenue target of ~Rs 2,600 crore and 10%-11% EBITDA margins despite expected seasonal raw material cost pressures.
