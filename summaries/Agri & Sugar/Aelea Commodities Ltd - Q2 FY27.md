# Aelea Commodities Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 42% YoY, but PAT remained flat due to logistics-led working capital bloat and project delays.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 246 crore (up 42% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 23 crore.
*   **PAT:** Rs 8.63 crore (vs Rs 8.70 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Borrowing:** Rs 232 crore (as of H1 FY27) vs Rs 111 crore (as of H2 FY26).
*   **Finance Cost:** Rs 8.39 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 8.36 crore (H2 FY26).
*   **Capacity Utilization:** 95-97%.

### Highlights
*   **Logistics Impact:** Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb conflict increased transit times from 35-45 days to 110-112 days.
*   **Inventory:** Higher inventory levels maintained due to extended transit times and a shift in Diwali timing.
*   **Sourcing:** Company is moving toward direct sourcing from African origins via merchant traders in UAE, Singapore, and Europe.
*   **Operational Status:** FSSC 22,000 certified; 140 metric tonnes (MT) installed capacity.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Expansion:** Board approved (Nov 2, 2026) a 60 MT expansion in shelling capacity (to 200 MT total) and a Cardanol unit.
*   **Capex:** Total project cost is Rs 114 crore (Rs 100 crore for manufacturing, Rs 14 crore for staff housing).
*   **Timeline:** 30-month project span.
*   **CNSL/Solar:** Management expects completion by mid-to-end December 2026, with positive impacts expected in H2.
*   **Long-term:** Management stated the 1,000 MT capacity goal by 2037 remains achievable.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Share:** India is the largest consumer (30% share), followed by Europe (25%), US (140,000 tonnes), and China (110,000 tonnes).
*   **Export Strategy:** Targeting Middle East markets to leverage lower logistics costs and demand for premium quality.
*   **Price Sensitivity:** Management noted demand destruction for premium grades (180s/240s) in India due to recent price hikes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Dynamics:** Management clarified that EBITDA % declines as raw cashew prices (denominator) rise, but absolute EBITDA per kg remains stable.
*   **Efficiency:** Finance costs remained stable despite higher borrowing due to efficient management.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **New Capacity:** Expansion will be funded by internal accruals and debt.
*   **Constraint:** Existing unit cannot expand further without the new project; future growth requires new land/units.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Borrowing increased significantly due to higher raw material prices and longer transit cycles (110-112 days).
*   **Debt:** Management confirmed they will utilize long-term debt for the new fixed capital project.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Ongoing conflicts impacting logistics and transit times.
*   **Execution:** Management admitted to delays in solar and CNSL projects and stated they are learning to avoid providing internal timelines to the market.
*   **Working Capital:** High inventory carrying costs due to supply chain disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did margins not improve despite deeper value chain?** A: Management cited exceptional logistics delays and market conditions; expects normalization long-term.
*   **Q: Why are project timelines slipping?** A: Management admitted delays (solar/CNSL) and stated they are learning not to give internal timelines to the market.
*   **Q: How to finance Rs 114 crore capex?** A: Internal accruals and debt.
*   **Q: Why not buy directly from African farms?** A: Regulatory/licensing hurdles; port-based buying is more efficient at current scale.

### One-line takeaway
Management is prioritizing capacity expansion and absolute EBITDA per kg while navigating significant logistics-driven working capital pressure and project execution delays.
