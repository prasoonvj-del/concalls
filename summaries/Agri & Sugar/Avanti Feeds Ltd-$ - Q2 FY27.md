# Avanti Feeds Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Negative - Profitability significantly impacted by soaring raw material costs (fish meal, soybean meal) and adverse export duty structures, despite stable demand.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Cut. FY27 export volume guidance reduced from 19,000 MT to 17,500 MT; feed sales volume guidance reduced from 5.85 lakh MT to 5.80 lakh MT.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious" to negative due to El Nino impacts on raw materials and persistent export duty headwinds.
- **New topics or risks:** El Nino identified as the primary driver of raw material inflation; "lukewarm" government response to raw material import requests.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Pet food land conversion and government approvals completed (Delivered); construction to commence.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Gross Income:** Rs 1,803 crore (down 8.29% QoQ; up 8.69% YoY).
- **Consolidated PBT:** Rs 96 crore (down 38.85% QoQ; down 57.71% YoY).
- **Standalone Feed Division Gross Income:** Rs 1,420 crore (down Rs 195 crore QoQ; up Rs 220 crore YoY).
- **Standalone Feed Division PBT:** Rs 86 crore (down 24.56% QoQ; down 52.22% YoY).
- **Feed Sales Volume:** 1,52,888 MT (vs 1,93,852 MT in Q1 FY27).
- **Shrimp Processing Gross Income:** Rs 387 crore (up 11% QoQ; down 16% YoY).
- **Shrimp Processing PBT:** Rs 36 crore (vs Rs 45 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Pet Food Sales:** Rs 202 lakh (vs Rs 180 lakh in Q1 FY27).

### Highlights
- Feed division margins compressed due to raw material inflation (fish meal, soybean meal, wheat flour).
- Export division impacted by higher ocean freight and increased anti-dumping duty (1.35-5.53% range).
- Management confirmed an additional ~8% feed price hike in October 2026.
- Farmers are restocking in November, which management expects to support Q4 feed sales.
- Fish feed project remains on hold due to intense low-cost competition.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 feed sales around 5,80,000 MT.
- Management expects FY27 shrimp exports around 17,500 MT.
- Management anticipates Q4 FY27 performance to improve compared to historical norms due to current restocking activity.
- Management expects El Nino effects to persist through March/April 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global shrimp demand remains strong, but Indian exports face headwinds from tariffs (US) and geopolitical factors.
- US market is not considered "lucrative" due to high duty structures (21% total: 5.53% ADD, 5.77% CVD, 10% Sec 301).
- Industry is lobbying the government for raw material import permissions and quota controls on fish meal exports.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Current raw material prices: Fish meal Rs 210/kg, Soya bean meal Rs 80/kg, Wheat flour Rs 36/kg.
- Feed PBT margin: 6.03% (vs 7.03% in Q1 FY27; 15.05% in Q2 FY26).
- Management describes current margins as the "lowest" in the business cycle.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pet food: Land conversion and government approvals completed; construction to commence.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is utilizing cash reserves for working capital due to high inventory holding requirements.
- US tariff refunds: Supreme Court ruled in favor, but funds are not yet liquidated/received.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- El Nino-driven raw material inflation (fish meal, soybean meal).
- Cyclones and disease outbreaks impacting shrimp production/harvest.
- High export duties (21% total for US market).
- Government response to raw material import requests described as "lukewarm."

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ronak Shah (Equirus):** Asked about feed price hikes. Management confirmed ~8% hike in Oct.
- **Ronak Shah:** Asked about US duty structure. Management confirmed 21% total impact.
- **Nitin Awasthi (InCred):** Asked about EBITDA/kg recovery. Management noted it depends on raw material stabilization and Q4 restocking.
- **Vivek Singh (Investor):** Asked if margins are at the bottom. Management agreed they are at the lowest.
- **Vivek Singh:** Asked about US tariff refunds. Management confirmed no timeline for liquidation.

### One-line takeaway
Avanti Feeds is navigating a cyclical trough characterized by severe raw material inflation and export duty headwinds, with management pinning hopes on Q4 restocking and potential government intervention to stabilize input costs.
