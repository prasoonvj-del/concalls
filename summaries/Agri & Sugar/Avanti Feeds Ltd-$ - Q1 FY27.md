# Avanti Feeds Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-28 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Rising raw material prices and cost pressures are impacting profitability despite strong volume growth.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Gross Income:** Rs 1,966 crore (up 30% QoQ from Rs 1,516 crore; up 19% YoY from Rs 1,657 crore)
- **Consolidated PBT:** Rs 157 crore (down 15% QoQ from Rs 184 crore; down 37% YoY from Rs 249 crore)
- **Standalone Feed Division Gross Income:** Rs 1,615 crore (up 51% QoQ from Rs 1,069 crore; up 27% YoY from Rs 1,279 crore)
- **Standalone Feed Division PBT:** Rs 114 crore (down 18% QoQ from Rs 139 crore; down 45% YoY from Rs 224 crore)
- **Feed Sales Volume:** 1,93,852 MT (vs 1,23,725 MT in Q4 FY26; 1,65,564 MT in Q1 FY26)
- **Feed Raw Material Consumption Prices (Q1 FY27):** Fish meal at Rs 153 per kg (vs Rs 123 in Q4 FY26, Rs 93 in Q1 FY26); Soya bean meal at Rs 58 per kg (vs Rs 49 in Q4 FY26, Rs 40 in Q1 FY26); Wheat flour at Rs 28 per kg (vs Rs 31 in Q4 and Q1 FY26)
- **Current Raw Material Purchase Prices:** Fish meal at Rs 225 per kg; Soya bean meal at Rs 71 per kg; Wheat flour at Rs 33 per kg
- **Shrimp Processing & Export Division Gross Income:** Rs 350 crore (down 22% QoQ from Rs 446 crore; down 7% YoY from Rs 378 crore)
- **Shrimp Processing & Export PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 45 crore (vs Rs 48 crore in Q4 FY26; Rs 25 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Pet Food Sales:** Rs 180 lakh (vs Rs 151 lakh in Q4 FY25/26)
- **Estimated FY27 Feed Sales:** Around 5,85,000 MT
- **Estimated FY27 Shrimp Exports:** Around 19,000 MT
- **Pet Food Project Planned Capex:** Around Rs 175 crore (Land purchased for around Rs 25 crore)

### Highlights
- Consolidated PBT declined YoY due to increased raw material prices in the feed division.
- Feed division saw a 51% QoQ jump in gross income driven by a 70,126 MT increase in sales volume.
- Shrimp export value decreased by only 7% YoY despite a 16% volume decline, aided by better average selling price realization.
- Pet care brand 'Avant Furst' continued growth in cat and dog food segments, expanding to Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 markets, and e-commerce platforms.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY26/27 to be a challenging season for the aquaculture industry regarding shrimp production due to steep feed raw material prices, exports, and global demand.
- Estimated calendar year 2026 shrimp production in India at 8-9 lakh MT, with feed consumption at 11-12 lakh MT.
- Management estimates FY27 feed sales at around 5,85,000 MT and shrimp exports at around 19,000 MT.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Aquaculture activity is progressing very smoothly with favorable climate and attractive farm-gate prices, though cost remains a key concern.
- Global shrimp exports from India grew in FY25/26 (US is largest importer at 33%, followed by China at 21%, EU at 17%).
- Industry is engaging with state and central governments for policy support, monitoring raw material availability/prices, and viewing the shrimp industry as an integrated value chain.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- PBT margin on revenue for the feed division stood at 7.06% in Q1 FY27 compared to ~13% in Q4 FY26 and 17% in Q1 FY26.
- Steep price spikes in key raw materials (fish meal and soybean meal) put pressure on margins.
- A price hike of around 10% was implemented in the feed division on June 19, 2026.
- Management is optimizing formulations to mitigate raw material volatility without compromising feed quality and FCR.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Land purchased near Hyderabad for around Rs 25 crore for a state-of-the-art pet food manufacturing facility; application for Consent for Establishment submitted, construction to begin upon approval. Total estimated capex is around Rs 175 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Steep and unprecedented increase in shrimp feed raw material prices (fish meal, soybean meal, wheat flour).
- Supply-side constraints, climate changes, diseases, and global economic stress affecting aquaculture.
- Farm-gate price volatility impacting farmer viability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arjun Khanna (Kotak Mutual Fund) asked about farm-gate price trends and currency benefits:** Management explained farm-gate prices balance global prices and processor needs, keeping farmer sustainability in mind.
- **Arjun Khanna asked about further feed price hikes given rising input costs:** Management noted price setting requires balancing farmer affordability, sustainability, and statutory/government discussions via committees.
- **Arjun Khanna asked about U.S. reciprocal tariff refunds:** Management stated entries are under ADD/CVD review and suspension status, and Customs and Border Protection (CBP) is not processing them currently.
- **Ronak Shah (Equirus Securities) asked about volume guidance vs Q1 momentum and price hikes:** Management confirmed a ~10% price hike was taken on June 19, 2026, and noted Q2 might remain a pinpoint before price hikes and government interventions play out.
- **Ronak Shah asked about pet food capex status:** Management reiterated an estimated Rs 175 crore total investment, with ~Rs 25 crore spent on land so far.

### One-line takeaway
Avanti Feeds reported a decline in profitability for Q1 FY27 due to a steep surge in raw material costs for its feed division, even as sales volumes and processing efficiencies remained resilient.
