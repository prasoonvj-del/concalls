# DSM Fresh Foods Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong top-line growth and scaling of new business lines, despite initial operating deleverage from new subsidiary investments.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 165.5 crore, up 72.6% YoY (from Rs 95.9 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Gross profit:** Rs 47.7 crore, up 48.4% YoY.
- **Gross margin:** 28.9% (compared to 33.6% in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 17.6 crore, up 18% YoY.
- **EBITDA margin:** ~11%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 7.3 crore, up 4% YoY.
- **Channel mix:** B2B and HoReCa 84%; B2C 16% (compared to 68% B2B/HoReCa and 32% B2C at end of FY26).
- **Category mix (H1 FY27):** Chicken 55%, goat meat 20%, fish and seafood 25%; vegetarian 7%, non-vegetarian 93%.
- **Meevaa Foods revenue contribution:** ~Rs 12 crore (~7% of consolidated revenue).
- **Partner retail network:** Expanded to 200 stores (from ~100 stores at end of FY26).
- **Receivable days:** Increased to 73 days (from 60 days in FY26).
- **Short-term loans and advances:** Rs 93 crore total (~Rs 22 crore advanced to subsidiary Avyom for acquisition; ~Rs 70 crore advanced to vendors/suppliers for contract farming).

### Highlights
- Revenue grew ~73% YoY to Rs 165 crore, driven by stronger institutional relationships, market penetration, and the launch of Meevaa Foods.
- Transitioning into an omnichannel fresh protein and diversified food platform, adding heat-and-eat brand Meevaa Foods (domestic range of 12 vegetarian products).
- Meevaa received >5,000 orders within 48 hours of launch; distribution expanded to Amazon, Amazon Now, and Blinkit.
- Aquaculture platform spans ~270 acres for backward integration in seafood.
- Neighbourhood meat retailer partnership programme grew from ~100 stores (end of FY26) to 200 stores.
- *Comparison with record:* Net profit of ~Rs 7 crore aligns with the earlier H1/Q2 result record, showing flat PAT growth (+0% to +4%) despite strong top-line expansion due to fixed costs in new subsidiaries.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-year FY27 growth aspiration:** 70-80% revenue growth (reaffirmed as broadly aligned with H1 performance, dependent on H2 execution).
- **Meevaa guidance:** Aiming for the brand to break even in FY27 (within 8-9 months of operations); medium-term revenue potential of Rs 300-400 crore for Meevaa over the next 3-4 years.
- **Long-term channel mix target:** Management targets a balanced 50:50 split between B2B and B2C.
- **Cash flow:** Management expects cash flow to turn positive in approximately one year.
- **Capital raising:** Management stated sufficient liquidity is available and no immediate equity or long-term debt raising is planned.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural shift in India's food consumption landscape towards protein awareness, hygienic/traceable products, premiumisation, and convenience.
- Winter season typically supports stronger fresh meat consumption (chicken and mutton).
- Export demand for Meevaa to the Middle East faced disruptions due to container/shipping shortages, shifting immediate focus towards domestic quick-commerce and e-commerce scaling.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin moderated from 33.6% to 28.9% YoY due to an increasing contribution from the lower-margin institutional (B2B/HoReCa) business.
- EBITDA margin declined to ~11% (from ~16% in H1 FY26) due to fixed costs associated with launching and scaling Meevaa Foods and subsidiary Avyom.
- Standalone Zappfresh PAT margin maintained at 6%, while consolidated margins dropped to 4% due to initial operating losses/fixed costs in subsidiary Avyom.
- Advertising cost per order for Meevaa declined by ~60% between July and August/September.
- Annual pricing reviews are conducted; no immediate price hikes planned despite macroeconomic/war-related commodity cost pressures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fixed assets increased to ~Rs 34 crore on the consolidated balance sheet due to the Meevaa Foods manufacturing facility acquisition in Chandigarh.
- Chandigarh facility peak revenue potential: Rs 120-140 crore in the immediate coming financial year, and Rs 300-400 crore over 3-4 years (with 5 acres of land, 1 acre built up currently, expandable via automation).
- Retail partner stores target: 300-400 stores (currently at 200).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total short-term loans and advances stand at Rs 93 crore (includes Rs 22 crore to subsidiary Avyom and Rs 70 crore in vendor/supplier advances for contract farming/price lock-ins).
- Blended receivable days increased to 73 days (from 60 days in FY26) driven by a higher B2B revenue mix offering 60 to 90 days credit terms.
- Company operates across four entities (standalone Zappfresh, Avyom, Varuna, and a UAE subsidiary), adding reporting and compliance bandwidth pressure.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chains and procurement impacted by macroeconomic conflicts/wars, increasing commodity prices and supplier advances.
- Shipping/container shortages disrupted export channels for Meevaa to the Middle East.
- Initial operating deleverage and fixed costs from new subsidiaries (Avyom/Meevaa) weighing on consolidated EBITDA margins and cash flows.
- Working capital stretch reflected in rising receivable days (73 days) and higher supplier advances (Rs 93 crore total).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Lohia questioned the ballooning debt, rising receivables, high supplier advances, and weaker consolidated profitability versus standalone.** Management clarified that advances include Rs 22 crore to subsidiary Avyom and Rs 70 crore in contract-farming vendor advances to lock in prices. Receivable days rose to 73 due to higher B2B mix. Consolidated profitability dropped because subsidiary Avyom incurs fixed costs ahead of revenue ramp-up. *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Akash Chaudhary asked why B2C share is declining and dragging down EBITDA.** Management explained that B2B was scaled for volume and economies of scale, while marketing was capped due to supply chain stress, but the long-term target remains a 50:50 B2B/B2C split. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Mohit Bansal asked about the timeline for cash flow break-even.** Management stated that approximately one year is a reasonable timeline. *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Raghav asked about the nature of other expenses hitting margins and when Avyom's operating deleverage will clear.** Management reiterated that standalone margins are stable (6% PAT), and the drop is purely subsidiary fixed costs; Meevaa is expected to break even in FY27 within 8-9 months of operations. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Akash Chaudhary asked about Meevaa export status given shipping issues.** Management admitted exports took a hit due to container shortages, prompting a pivot to scale domestic quick-commerce and e-commerce channels where margins are comparable. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
DSM Fresh Foods reported 73% YoY revenue growth to Rs 165 crore in H1 FY27, but consolidated margins moderated as investments in new subsidiaries (Meevaa Foods and Avyom) and a higher B2B mix introduced short-term operating deleverage and working capital stretching.
