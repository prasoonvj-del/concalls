# Piccadily Agro Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever Q1 volumes and revenue, driven by strong growth in the branded alco-bev portfolio.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 270.5 crore (up 18.1% YoY)
- **Distillery revenue:** Rs 205.7 crore (up 26.3% YoY; 76% of total revenue)
- **Branded alco-bev revenue (premium, super-premium, luxury):** Rs 82.3 crore (up 47.3% YoY; 43.5% of distillery revenue)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 47.2 crore (up 21% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 18.5% (up 30 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 22 crore (up 15.4% YoY)
- **EPS:** Rs 2.21 (up 10.5% YoY)
- **Malt stock under maturation:** 87,000 barrels (minimum size 200 liters each)

### Highlights
- Recorded the highest-ever Q1 volumes and revenue in the company's history.
- Premiumization remains the core driver; contribution of the premium/luxury portfolio rose to 43.5% of distillery revenue (vs 37.8% in Q1 FY26).
- Chhattisgarh plant commissioned, contributing approximately Rs 5 crore in its first 15 days of operations.
- Scheme for the demerger of the sugar division was filed with stock exchanges on April 28, 2026.
- Malt capacity utilization is running consistently at 80%; Indri distillery expansion running at 60%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year FY27 guidance: branded alco-bev business growth of approximately 60% to 70%, and company-level revenue growth of around 60%.
- Company-level EBITDA margin target of 23% to 24% for FY27, with H2 expected to be the primary growth driver (accounting for 60% to 65% of annual branded alco-bev revenue).
- Chhattisgarh facility revenue target of Rs 300 crore to Rs 400 crore for the year, with 50% capacity utilization expected by the end of the year.
- Aiming for Indri to reach top-5 global single malt status in 4-5 years, translating to ~500,000 cases and ~Rs 1,200 crore top-line on Indri alone at current prices.
- Filled barrel target increased to 115,000–120,000 barrels by March 2027 (crossing initial guidance of 100,000).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian alco-bev market is experiencing structural premiumization as consumers trade upwards for quality, authenticity, and provenance.
- Single malts globally grow at low single digits (2%-3%), whereas Indri is growing at high double digits (18%-20% YoY).
- International expansion is gaining momentum, with North America as the primary export market and ongoing additions to international airports and duty-frees. Long-term export target is 70% of sales.
- Younger consumers increasingly prefer quality over quantity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin performance impacted by cost pressures from grain and fuel prices due to geopolitical conflicts, alongside upfront investments in distribution, manpower, and brand marketing.
- Ethanol margins across the industry generally hover around 10%; country liquor margins range between 15% to 18%.
- Advertising and promotion (A&P) expenses and employee costs as a percentage of revenue are expected to decline in H2 as top-line scales up.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No large capex planned for FY27; heavy capex has largely been executed, leaving only ordinary maintenance capex and ongoing barrel purchases.
- Malt capacity stands at 30,000 liters/day, supporting ~800,000 to 850,000 cases of Indri if fully utilized.
- Chhattisgarh capacity at 210 KLPD, expected to reach ~50% utilization by the end of the financial year.
- Portavadie distillery in Scotland is intended as a global beachhead distillery for blending and selling Scotch brands globally.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt reduced by approximately Rs 10 crore during the quarter; no increase in debt is planned for the year, with operational cash flows reinvested into growth and liquid inventory.
- Branded business accounts receivable stand at around Rs 170 crore (including excise recovery), with receivable days currently at approximately 100 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Court orders in Chennai impacting ethanol plant operations temporarily.
- External volatility in grain and fuel prices creating cost pressures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ruchika Bhatia (Alchemy Capital) on Chhattisgarh impact from ethanol disruptions:** Management stated they factored in the disruptions and will pivot product mixes without altering guidance.
2. **Rahil Dasani on sustainability of Indri volume growth vs. barrel capacity:** Management clarified they hold 87,000 filled barrels (1.6 crore liters maturing) and do not foresee supply constraints for 18%-20% annual Indri growth.
3. **Himanshu Bisani on employee/other expense spikes:** Management explained it reflects senior hires and brand investments ahead of H2 scaling, which will normalize percentages over the full year.
4. **Varun Arora on the definition and milestone for Camikara and Cashmir success:** Management stated these represent new category creations (pure cane juice rum, luxury vodka) targeting triple-digit growth off small bases, tracking market share percentages over time.
5. **Aman Vij on Scotland FTA impact:** Management clarified that since they mature products domestically and are not net bulk scotch importers, the FTA mainly aids global ambitions via Portavadie rather than domestic input costs.

### One-line takeaway
Piccadily Agro delivered its highest-ever Q1 revenue and volumes, driven by a 47.3% surge in branded alco-bev sales, and remains confident in achieving its 60%-70% branded growth and 23%-24% EBITDA margin guidance for FY27.
