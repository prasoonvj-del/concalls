# Kaveri Seed Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong non-cotton and maize growth, but cotton volumes and revenues impacted by El Nino, delayed monsoon, and illegal Bt cotton.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): Rs. 286.68 crore vs Rs. 268.09 crore YoY (growth of 6.9%).
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs. 940.44 crore vs Rs. 725.44 crore YoY (growth of 29.84%).
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs. 87.41 crore vs Rs. 90.53 crore YoY (decline of 3.4%).
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs. 286.68 crore vs Rs. 301.45 crore YoY (decline of 4.7%).
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 30.48% vs 33.79% YoY (approximate calculation based on stated numbers).
- EBITDA margin (H1 FY27): 30.48% vs 41.55% YoY (as stated in transcript for H1).
- Net Profit / PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs. 178 crore (Cash on books stood at Rs. 718 crore).
- Net Profit / PAT (H1 FY27): Rs. 224 crore vs Rs. 178 crore YoY (growth of 26%).
- Interim Dividend: Rs. 5 per share (250% on face value of Rs. 2).

### Highlights
- Non-cotton revenues grew 29% in H1; cotton revenue declined 1.7%.
- Maize was the largest drag in H1 with a ~30% drop in sowing due to ~40% rain deficit in Karnataka and parts of Maharashtra, but saw a recovery in Q2 with new hybrids and higher acreage.
- Cotton volumes were down 21.4% to 37.7% in H1, affected by illegal Bt cotton and reduced acreage.
- Export business grew significantly, with the board approving incorporation of wholly-owned subsidiaries in Tanzania, Kenya, Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Zambia, and Peru.
- Board declared an interim dividend of Rs. 5 per share (face value Rs. 2).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the maize business to recover and grow next year based on current sowing sentiment and low inventory levels.
- Cotton portfolio transition: Management expects 80%-90% of incremental growth in cotton over the next 3-4 years to come from new hybrids rather than older ones.

### Demand & industry commentary
- El Nino and delayed monsoon heavily impacted sowing across major sectors in H1.
- Illegal Bt cotton sale is increasing year-on-year, impacting market share for legal players, though the government is taking steps to control it.
- Export markets (Africa, Southeast Asia) require long-term brand building and regulatory approvals taking 3-5 years per country.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inventory levels are higher (e.g., rice and cotton inventory buildup), but management does not anticipate major write-offs and expects inventory to be utilized in Rabi and next year's Kharif.
- Average life of seed inventory: 1.5 to 3 years for cotton and maize, up to 3-5 years for rice; germination is tested quarterly.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital work in progress (CWIP) stands at around Rs. 120-Rs. 130 crore, pertaining to machinery and agricultural land acquisition (50%-60% for agricultural land).
- Expanding international footprint through subsidiaries for product registration, research, and distribution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash on books stood at Rs. 718 crore as of H1 FY27 (vs Rs. 817 crore YoY).
- Payables and inventory levels are high due to seed production cycles and delayed sales realization in H1, expected to normalize post-returns and liquidation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Proliferation of illegal Bt cotton and competitive intensity from MNCs.
- Weather vagaries (delayed monsoon, rain deficits in key states like Karnataka and Maharashtra affecting maize and cotton).
- Long gestation periods (3-5 years) and strict regulatory requirements for overseas expansion and hybrid registrations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rushabh Shah (Buglerock PMS) on export strategy:** Asked about the shift from distributor models to own-brand international subsidiaries. Management explained that initial exports were through local distributors, but building a sustainable brand requires local incorporation, registrations, and 4-5 years of investment.
- **Viraj Kacharia (SIMPL) on cotton growth outlook:** Asked if cotton growth can double from current levels. Management stated that growth will be driven by new hybrids replacing older ones, targeting Rs. 25-Rs. 50 crore incremental contribution over 3-4 years.
- **Dhruv Saraf (Bowhead India Fund) on inventory buildup:** Inquired about the jump in inventory and risk of write-offs. Management clarified that inventory is higher due to lower-than-anticipated H1 sales from weather issues, but it will be carried forward to Rabi and next Kharif without major write-offs.
- **Sonal Kohli (Bowhead India Fund) on H1 vs H2 margins and standalone vs consolidated numbers:** Asked about EBITDA margin trends. Management explained that subsidiary expenses and seasonal sales mix create variances, but core operational margins remain stable.

### One-line takeaway
Despite H1 headwinds from El Nino, rain deficits, and illegal Bt cotton, Kaveri Seeds reported strong H1 PAT growth of 26% (Rs. 224 crore) driven by non-cotton segments and robust cash reserves of Rs. 718 crore.
