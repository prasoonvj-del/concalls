### Highlights
- Revenue growth led by maize (+36.3% YoY in Q2) and new product ramp: new cotton hybrids 37.7% of portfolio (up from 21.4%), new single-cross maize hybrids 21.8% (up from 21.4%).  
- Cotton volumes down 36% YoY due to acreage decline in Karnataka; illegal BT cotton sales rising (15–16% of market vs 10–12% earlier).  
- Export momentum: 71.6% growth in non-cotton crops; subsidiaries approved in 8 countries (Tanzania, Kenya, Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Zambia, Peru) for own-brand sales.  
- Interim dividend declared at Rs 5/share.  
- Inventory buildup >Rs 150 cr YoY from higher production and lower-than-expected sales; management says seed properly stored (life 1.5–3 yr maize, 3–5 yr cotton, 5+ yr rice), no major write-offs expected.  
- Subsidiaries Aditya Agritech and Genomics: analyst cited ~Rs 100 cr revenue and ~Rs 80 cr PAT each (not confirmed by management).  
- CWIP of Rs 120–130 cr for agricultural land (50–60%) and machinery/office.

### Guidance & outlook
- Maize acreage expected to grow 9–10% next year, supporting seed demand.  
- Cotton portfolio transition: 85–90% of sales from new hybrids in 3–4 years; older hybrids fading.  
- Export own-brand sales in 3–4 years after registration; currently selling via distributors.  
- EBITDA margins: standalone H1 FY27 29.85% vs 30.8% in H1 FY26; consolidated similar. Expect stability/improvement with new hybrid scale.  
- No explicit FY27 revenue/EBITDA guidance.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Maize: strong demand, prices high (~Rs 32/kg), acreage growth anticipated.  
- Cotton: illegal BT cotton share rising; government action slow. Kaveri maintaining volumes via new hybrids.  
- Rice: hybrid variety 468 dominant; competition from MNCs but strong dealer network.  
- Vegetables: high competition from MNCs/domestic; minimal traction.  
- Weather: El Nino, delayed monsoon, and cyclone in Andhra/Telangana impacted H1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated H1 EBITDA margin 30.48% vs 13.32% YoY (large jump attributed to low base/mix).  
- Standalone H1 EBITDA margin 29.85% vs 30.8% (down ~95 bps).  
- No major inventory write-offs yet; seed vigor tested quarterly.  
- No specific raw material cost commentary.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CWIP Rs 120–130 cr: agricultural land (50–60%, registration pending 6–8 months) and machinery/office.  
- New overseas subsidiaries incorporation approved; initial investment minimal (registration, entity setup).  
- No major domestic capacity expansion disclosed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash Rs 178 cr (end 2Q FY27) vs Rs 267 cr (end 1Q FY27).  
- Inventory up >Rs 150 cr YoY.  
- Payables to seed producers largely cleared; cash decline due to inventory buildup and production payments.  
- No debt figures disclosed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Illegal BT cotton sales eroding legal hybrid market share.  
- High inventory (>Rs 150 cr increase) carries write-off risk if not sold next season.  
- Cyclone/erratic rainfall hitting production/sales in Andhra, Telangana, Maharashtra.  
- Vegetable segment: intense competition, low success.  
- Export registration timeline 3–4 years delays own-brand revenue.  
- High dependency on few hybrids (e.g., rice 468).
