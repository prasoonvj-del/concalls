# Godrej Agrovet Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Double-digit revenue growth and strong animal nutrition offset weather-led crop care headwinds and cost inflation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained intent to chase double-digit growth despite a tougher environment; no explicit full-year numerical PBT guidance updated.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more cautious due to a super El Niño impact, dry June, and severe reservoir level crashes impacting the upcoming Rabi season.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Twin cyclones in Andhra Pradesh (acting as a soil water relief), U.S. election volatility impacting palm prices, and GTM AM/PM distributor model pilots in dairy.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Khammam CPKO refinery first phase successfully commissioned on September 1st as promised.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 2,995 crore (up 17% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 222 crore (flat YoY), margin at 7.4%
- Profit before tax (PBT): Rs 131 crore (up 5% YoY)
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 103 crore (up 22% YoY)
- H1 FY27 consolidated revenue: Rs 5,847 crore (up 13% YoY); H1 PAT: Rs 231 crore
- Animal nutrition revenue growth: 14% YoY; segment profit growth: 32% YoY
- Oil palm revenue growth: 28% YoY; segment profit growth: 4% YoY
- Dairy revenue growth: 6% YoY; value-added products salience: 49%
- Godrej Foods B2C growth: 50%; salience: 38%
- Net working capital: improved to 21 days (from 32 days last year); absolute working capital: Rs 655 crore
- Renewable energy share: 79.3%; Scope 1 and 2 emissions reduced by 11.1%

### Highlights
- Animal Nutrition delivered strong volume gains and realizations; exited the shrimp feed business to focus on profitable growth segments.
- Oil Palm revenue grew 28% with a 14% growth in Fresh Fruit Bunch (FFB) volumes; added 13,000 hectares of plantations in H1 and inaugurated India's first integrated oil palm complex in Khammam, Telangana (commissioning a mill, CPKO refinery, and biogas plant on Sept 1).
- Crop Care severely impacted by El Niño demand weakness and unfavourable product mix, though maize herbicide Ashitaka crossed Rs 100 crore in its first-year launch.
- Astec LifeSciences faced weak enterprise demand and lower realizations, though CDMO inquiry-to-R&D conversion reached 90% with 15 products in the pipeline.
- Dairy revenue grew 6% led by calibrated pricing and value-added products; profitability remained under pressure due to milk procurement, packaging, and logistics costs.
- Godrej Foods B2C business grew 50%, driven by quick commerce expansion and new launches (frozen chicken, frozen momos, crispy nugget bites).
- Bangladesh JV (ACI Godrej Agrovet) delivered robust volume, revenue, and profit growth.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management intends to continue chasing a double-digit growth trajectory despite a tougher operating environment.
- Management expects the newly commissioned PKO derivatives plant to add roughly 2% to the overall EBITDA margin of the oil palm business over a midterm horizon of 2 to 3 years.
- Management targets a healthy double-digit CAGR for the oil palm business for the year and midterm.
- Management expects agchem industry impact to persist into the Rabi season due to crashed reservoir levels.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India experienced one of the driest Junes in 100 years (40% rain deficit), followed by extreme heat waves in July and August in southern states, impacting cattle productivity and oil palm extraction ratios (OER).
- Two recent cyclones in the Bay of Bengal dumped heavy rainwater in coastal Andhra Pradesh, helping replenish soil water levels for oil palm.
- Milk pricing faces both temporary El Niño-led productivity drops and structural uplifts from rising value-added product salience.
- Palm oil prices are expected to remain range-bound in the short term (INR4,250 to INR4,400) before potentially creeping up in February/March depending on Indonesian/Malaysian El Niño effects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs faced continuous pressure from inflation across milk, feed, and packaging.
- Animal nutrition margins improved through a favourable product mix and operating efficiencies.
- Inventory holding levels were proactively increased to hedge against inflation, impacting short-term debt levels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned the first phase of the CPKO refinery and mill at the Khammam integrated complex in Telangana on September 1; full hydrogenation and fractionation rollout scheduled for December.
- Added 13,000 hectares of oil palm plantations in H1.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity stands at approximately 0.8; debt increased by Rs 200-300 crore vs March levels due to proactive inventory stocking to counter inflation.
- Net working capital improved to 21 days; absolute working capital reduced to Rs 655 crore.
- Dividend of Rs 50 crore received from the Bangladesh JV during the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Weather uncertainties, including extreme heat waves, super El Niño effects, and low reservoir levels threatening the Rabi season.
- Geopolitical volatility and elevated raw material, milk procurement, and packaging inflation.
- Slower enterprise demand and lower realizations in the agrochemical and Astec LifeSciences segments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhijit Akella (Kotak Securities) asked about the health of oil palm plantations and FFB arrivals following the drought and recent cyclones.** Sunil Kataria clarified that trees suffered no widespread damage, cyclones brought beneficial soil water, and FFB volumes grew in double digits despite 3 months of heat stress. Management did not dodge.
- **Abhijit Akella asked about the financial contribution and margin impact of the new PKO derivatives plant.** Sunil Kataria stated that meaningful financial impact will start in Q4 and scale in FY28, adding approximately 2% to segment EBITDA margins over the midterm. Management did not dodge.
- **Abhijit Akella asked about the drivers behind the increase in debt levels.** CFO Ravishankar explained it resulted from proactively increasing inventory holding levels against inflation, which will normalize in H2 as inventory is consumed. Management did not dodge.
- **Arun Kumar (Analyst) asked about sustainability of animal feed volume growth and broiler feed strategy.** Sunil Kataria explained that cattle feed and layer feed grew at high single/double digits while broiler feed was consciously let go due to lack of strategic fit, keeping overall volume growth at 3%. Management did not dodge.
- **Probal Sen (ICICI Securities) asked about the dairy GTM revamp and margin trends in value foods.** Sunil Kataria detailed the new AM/PM distributor model pilot launched in Chennai and explained that foods margins are being protected with EBITDA plus working media expanding by 20 bps despite hyperinflation. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
Godrej Agrovet delivered a resilient 17% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, as strong performance in Animal Nutrition and Oil Palm cushioned severe weather and inflation headwinds in Crop Care and Dairy.
