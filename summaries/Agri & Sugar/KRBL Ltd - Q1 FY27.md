# KRBL Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong profitability and domestic growth, offset by Middle East logistics disruptions impacting exports.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 1,560 crore (down 3% YoY)
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 1,496 crore (down 6% YoY, down 2% QoQ)
- **Export Revenue:** Rs 244 crore (down 50% YoY); non-Middle East exports grew 37% YoY
- **Domestic Revenue (ex-power):** Rs 1,221 crore (up 14% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 36.3% (vs 25.7% in Q1 FY26); 33.51% excluding other income (vs 24.15% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 372 crore
- **EBITDA Margin:** 23.8% (vs 13.9% in Q1 FY26)
- **PAT (Profit After Tax):** Rs 261 crore (up 73% YoY from Rs 151 crore); PAT margin at 16.7% (vs 9.3% YoY)
- **Other Income:** Rs 64 crore (vs Rs 32 crore YoY), including Rs 18 crore higher gain on sale/realization of investments, Rs 9.5 crore forex gain, and Rs 4.4 crore higher interest income
- **Inventory (as of June 30, 2026):** Paddy at Rs 286 crore (71,000 tonnes); Rice at Rs 2,481 crore (389,000 tonnes)
- **Cash + Investments (as of June 30, 2026):** Rs 1,841 crore (vs Rs 1,281 crore YoY)
- **Masala Portfolio Annualized Run Rate:** Rs 9 crore (targeting Rs 25 crore by end of FY27)
- **Regional Rice Growth:** 25% YoY in Q1

### Highlights
- Delivered highest quarterly profitability in company history, driven by strong domestic performance, price realizations, and other income.
- Middle East conflict (Strait of Hormuz disruption) caused a volume shock for exports; export realizations were ~20% higher YoY and 13% higher QoQ.
- Domestic branded rice realizations rose ~11% YoY; consumer pack and regional rice performed well while bulk pack sales saw temporary postponement due to price hikes.
- E-commerce primary sales grew ~50% YoY, exiting June with ~41% market share (a ~10 percentage point lead over the next competitor).
- Launched India Gate Light and Fluffy Poha across 22 cities in North India.
- Masala portfolio grew 74% value YoY.
- Gangavathi regional rice facility expected to be operational by end of Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year guidance of meaningful export growth, expecting exports to recover progressively from Q2 as shipping routes stabilize.
- Management is confident of delivering ~10% domestic volume growth for FY27 and sustaining it over the next 2-3 years.
- Full-year EBITDA margin guidance: 17% to 18%.
- Full-year gross margin guidance: ~30%.
- Targeting an annualized revenue run rate of ~Rs 25 crore for the masala portfolio by the end of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- USDA projects global rice production at ~537 million metric tonnes and consumption at ~543 million metric tonnes for '26-'27; ending stocks expected to draw down.
- USDA projects India's '26-'27 rice production at ~150 million metric tonnes and exports at ~25 million metric tonnes.
- India's monsoon cumulative rainfall was running ~11% below long-period average as of August 5; IMD revised seasonal forecast down to ~90% of normal. Basmati belt is canal and tube well irrigated, minimizing acreage loss but carrying yield risk for longer-duration varieties.
- 2025 Basmati crop saw floods in Punjab causing a 20%-25% output decline in the state; quality was sharply segmented. Old crop paddy availability ran out by May with no material replacement until October.
- Pakistan's 2025 Basmati exports fell 26% in volume and 32% in value due to trading at a ~$100/tonne premium over Indian material.
- Middle East importers bought hand-to-mouth and deferred forward commitments due to Strait of Hormuz disruptions; destination inventories have been drawn down.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Container freight rates from India's west coast to the Middle East rose more than tenfold from ~$500 to ~$5,000 per container at peak disruption.
- Branded business realizations vis-a-vis Q4 were higher by ~9% in Q1.
- Q1 EBITDA margin of 23.8% is deemed unsustainable by management due to high prices and MTM investment gains during the quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New facility at Gangavathi expected to be operational by the end of Q3 to support regional rice growth.
- Direct Saudi entity setup deferred; management is actively searching for a long-term distributor while maintaining interim wholesale distribution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total inventory as of June 30, 2026 stood at Rs 2,944 crore (71,000 tonnes of paddy; 389,000 tonnes of rice).
- Total cash and investments at Rs 1,841 crore, driven by lower net working capital requirements and higher internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia and partial closures/delays at the Strait of Hormuz impacting export shipping volumes and container availability.
- Deficient monsoon and reservoir shortages (~27% below last year) posing yield risks for longer-duration paddy varieties.
- High freight rates and potential price resistance if rice prices increase further.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Balaji Vaidyanath (NAFA Asset Managers) asked about export outlook for Q2 and sustainability of high prices with high freight.** Management stated that benchmark prices in the Middle East have increased, allowing them to pass on freight costs, and Q2 export numbers will be much better as bulk business resumes and routes partially open.
- **Chirag Singhal (Water Fund) asked about margin sustainability and why KRBL isn't building a broader non-basmati basket more aggressively on quick commerce.** Management clarified that Q1 margins (23.8% EBITDA) are not sustainable and guided for 17%–18% EBITDA margins for the full year. On quick commerce, management noted they are actively working with platforms like Blinkit and Zepto to enter more regional rice varieties.
- **Amit Aggarwal (Leeway Investments) asked why the company didn't buy more inventory given the cash on books.** Management answered that cash on books does not mean buying blindly; market conditions must be assessed, and current stock levels (389,000 tonnes of rice) are comfortable.
- **Yash Dantewadia (Dante Equity) asked about regional rice targets and the impact of the supply gap on basmati prices.** Management stated regional rice is targeted for 25% growth, and confirmed that once peace processes complete and markets fully open, prices will likely shoot up further, constrained only by staple affordability and the upcoming new crop.

### One-line takeaway
KRBL delivered record quarterly profitability (EBITDA margin 23.8%, PAT up 73% YoY) in Q1 FY27, driven by strong domestic realization and 14% domestic revenue growth, despite a 50% drop in Middle East exports due to Red Sea/Strait of Hormuz shipping disruptions.
