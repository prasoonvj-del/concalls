# Agri & Sugar - Sugar & ethanol: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian Agri, Sugar & Ethanol sector in Q2 FY27 is navigating a structural divergence between sugar and ethanol operations. Sugarcane availability is expected to decline 2–10% across key states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, and UP) due to El Niño and unseasonal late October rains. This crop tightness, combined with strict government restrictions banning sugarcane juice and B-heavy molasses for ethanol in the ESY 2026-27 tender, is keeping domestic sugar realisations firm and driving potential import requirements of 1 to 3 million metric tonnes. 

Conversely, the ethanol segment faces significant margin compression. Feedstock restrictions have forced reliance on grain (maize/rice) and C-heavy molasses. However, maize prices have surged 25–30% to ₹26–30/kg, while OMC tender terms have lowered maize ethanol procurement prices by ~₹3/litre, creating a "survival of the fittest" environment for grain-based distilleries. Companies are aggressively diversifying into Compressed Biogas (CBG), specialty bio-chemicals, bioplastics (PLA), and consumer liquor/CPG to protect overall profitability.

## Where the industry is heading

**Business model so far**  
Historically, Indian sugar mills operated as cyclical commodity processors dependent on seasonal sugarcane crushing, regulated sugar sales quotas, and volatile domestic prices. Over the last five to seven years, government blending mandates transformed them into integrated bio-refineries, driving revenues through ethanol blending programs and co-generation power [Background].

**Shifts underway**  
*   **Expansion into Compressed Biogas (CBG) and Bio-CNG:**  
    *Stage: Scaling*  
    Driven by government guaranteed gas prices and capital subsidies under GOBARdhan 2.0, ethanol and sugar players are rapidly deploying CBG infrastructure. TruAlt Bioenergy commissioned its first 20 TPD CBG plant under a JV with Sumitomo, targeted reaching 72 TPD capacity by quarter-end, and has a 250 TPD buildout planned over 2 years [Trualt Bioenergy concall Q2 FY27]. BCL Industries is floating tenders for a 20 TPD CBG plant at Fatehabad (targeting ₹70 crore revenue, 40-45% EBITDA margin) [BCL Industries concall Q2 FY27].  
*   **Diversification into Bio-Plastics and Specialty Bio-Chemicals:**  
    *Stage: Scaling*  
    To de-link from cyclical sugar and ethanol pricing regulations, leading players are investing in high-value bio-materials. Balrampur Chini spent ₹2,400 crore of its total ₹3,080 crore gross capex on an 80,000 TPA PLA (bioplastics) project, securing its first institutional order and passing pan-masala packaging tests [Balrampur Chini concall Q2 FY27]. Godavari Biorefineries delivered a 38.8% YoY growth in bio-based chemicals (₹204.2 crore revenue), adding ₹25 crore in debottlenecking capex [Godavari Biorefineries concall Q2 FY27].  
*   **Downstream Pivot to Consumer FMCG, Liquor, and Nutraceuticals:**  
    *Stage: Early to Scaling*  
    Mills are moving up the value chain into branded consumer goods and IMFL. BCL Industries is planning a malt plant in Bathinda for an IMFL launch by FY28-FY29 while scaling country liquor sales (+41.4% YoY) [BCL Industries concall Q2 FY27]. EID Parry is focusing on high-margin CPG (targeting ₹500 crore revenue) and expanding its US nutraceuticals business [EID Parry concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**  
*   **GOBARdhan 2.0 / CBG Subsidies:** Assured CBG gas pricing at ₹104+/kg for 10 years with take-or-pay terms, alongside doubled capital subsidies of ₹2 crore per TPD (capped at ₹30 crore per plant) [Trualt Bioenergy concall Q2 FY27]. Benefits TruAlt Bioenergy and BCL Industries.
*   **Feedstock Bans & Allocation Changes:** Policy restricting ethanol production from sugarcane juice and B-heavy molasses in the ESY 2026-27 tender forces utilization of grain and C-heavy molasses [Triveni Engineering concall Q2 FY27; Trualt Bioenergy concall Q2 FY27].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

*   **Consensus:**  
    *   **Sugar Prices & Tightness:** Managements across Balrampur Chini, Triveni Engineering, EID Parry, and Godavari Biorefineries agree that domestic sugar prices will remain firm through FY27. Opening stocks are low (~3.5 to 3.7 MMT), and domestic production is curtailed by weather disruptions.
    *   **Sugar Imports:** Balrampur Chini, EID Parry, and Triveni Engineering all highlight that India will likely need to import sugar (estimates range between 1 and 3 MMT) in SY 2026-27 to stabilize domestic balance sheets.
*   **Divergence:**  
    *   **Ethanol Feedstock Strategy:** TruAlt Bioenergy and BCL Industries are actively procuring grain and stocking up on maize/rice to meet OMC targets despite high costs. Conversely, Triveni Engineering notes that current maize prices (~₹26-27/kg) coupled with a ₹3/kg reduction in maize-ethanol tender prices render standalone grain ethanol unviable, relying instead on integrated operations and forward-procured grain cushions.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

*   **Sugarcane Costs:** Rising significantly. EID Parry noted its landed cane cost reached ₹3,798/MT (+5% YoY) and highlighted farmer agitations in Karnataka pushing actual payouts to ~₹3,800/MT vs state FRP of ₹3,300–3,400/MT. Balrampur Chini warned that every ₹10/quintal hike in State Advised Price (SAP) adds ₹100 crore to raw material costs. Triveni noted SAP increases in Haryana (+₹15 to ₹430/quintal).
*   **Maize Inflation:** Surged 25% to 30% across the board. BCL Industries reported maize prices hovering around ₹29–30/kg; Triveni and TruAlt cited ₹26–30/kg.
*   **Ethanol Margins:** Broadly compressed. Distilleries face stagnant OMC ethanol procurement prices (unchanged for 3 years per Balrampur Chini) alongside lower prices for maize ethanol in the ESY 26-27 tender. TruAlt expects a 1-2% drop in ethanol EBITDA margins, while BCL warned of continuous margin squeeze.

## Capex & capacity

*   **Bioplastics (PLA):** Balrampur Chini has spent ₹2,400 crore of its total project cost on its 80,000 TPA PLA plant, targeting commissioning in November/December 2026.
*   **Compressed Biogas (CBG):** TruAlt Bioenergy is investing ~₹300 crore across 4-5 new plants (aiming for 250 TPD total footprint over 2 years). BCL Industries is deploying ~₹70 crore for a 20 TPD CBG plant in Fatehabad.
*   **Bio-Chemicals:** Godavari Biorefineries is executing a ₹25 crore debottlenecking program to take quarterly chemical revenues to ₹240 crore by Q3 FY28.
*   **Distilleries & Asset Realignment:** TruAlt is executing a slump sale of its Badami unit (Unit 5) for ₹171 crore to repay debt. BCL Industries has fully commissioned its 900 KLPD total distillery network.
