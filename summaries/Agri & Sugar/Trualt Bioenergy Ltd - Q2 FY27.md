# Trualt Bioenergy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth and profit turnaround, but raw material price inflation squeezed margins compared to Q1.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 811 crore (up 606% YoY from Rs 115 crore; up from Rs 627 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,438 crore (up 243% YoY from Rs 419 crore)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 114 crore 
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 14.03% (down from H1 average due to 25%-30% surge in maize prices)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 261 crore
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 18.2%
- **PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 47 crore (compared to loss before tax of Rs 49 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 35 crore (compared to loss of Rs 38 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 94 crore (compared to loss of Rs 33 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Ethanol Sales Volume (H1 FY27):** 19.07 crore litres (Q1: 8.29 crore litres, Q2: 10.78 crore litres)
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Rs 192 crore
- **Capex Invested (H1 FY27):** Rs 180 crore
- **Total Equity (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 1,687 crore (vs Rs 1,578 crore in March 2026)
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.55x
- **Inventory (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 206 crore (reduced from Rs 528 crore in March 2026)
- **Trade Receivables (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 367 crore (reduced from Rs 406 crore in March 2026)
- **Finance Costs (H1 FY27):** Rs 87 crore
- **Depreciation and Amortization (H1 FY27):** Rs 49 crore

### Highlights
- Received an additional 10 crore litres allocation from OMCs following an August Supreme Court judgment, plus another 5 crore litres, taking the total supply allocation for the previous ethanol supply year to nearly 40 crore litres.
- Average capacity utilization for H1 stood at 62%–65%, with management targeting 75%–80% going forward.
- Commissioned the first 20 TPD CBG plant under the Sumitomo Corporation joint venture.
- Received a viability gap fund incentive of Rs 150 crore under the PM JI-VAN Yojana for the proposed Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) plant.
- Planned slump sale of the Badami plant (Unit 5) for Rs 171 crore, which will be used to repay Rs 130+ crore of term debt and allocate Rs 40+ crore to working capital, saving approximately Rs 22 crore annually in interest and employee expenses.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects capacity utilization to increase to 75%–80% going forward.
- For Q3 FY27, management has 8.5 crore litres of pending orders in hand from the previous ethanol supply year.
- Management expects the new ethanol supply year (2026–2027) allocations to be around 40 to 42 crore litres (with a bid eligibility calculated up to 66 crore litres based on OMC formulas).
- Management expects CBG segment revenue of Rs 20 crore from 2 to 3 plants in the coming 2 quarters.
- Management expects EBITDA margins for ethanol to potentially see a 1% to 2% drop on average due to current tender pricing, but expects margins to improve from Q3/Q4 onwards compared to Q2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- The tender for Ethanol Supply Year (ESY) 2026-2027 has a ban on sugarcane juice and B-heavy molasses for ethanol production, placing pressure on grains and C-heavy molasses.
- Out of ~500 ethanol plants in India, ~230 mono-feed sugar-based plants cannot participate continuously, creating a shortage of ~300 crore litres from sugar-based plants, leaving ~1,600 crore litres expected from the rest of the industry.
- El Nino impact is expected to cause a 35% drop in maize availability, 10%–12% drop in rice availability, and 10%–12% drop in sugarcane availability.
- Under GOBARdhan 2.0, CBG capital subsidies have doubled to Rs 2 crore per TPD (capped at Rs 30 crore per plant), gas prices are assured for 10 years at Rs 104+/kg with a take-or-pay clause, boosting industry prospects.
- Potable alcohol demand stands at ~218 crore litres with a 3% YoY compound growth; ENA prices are seeing long-term contracts at Rs 62–Rs 63 per litre. Industrial chemical alcohol demand is at 250–300 crore litres.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margins moderated to 14.03% due to a 25% to 30% surge in maize prices and muted seasonal agrarian availability during summer.
- DDGS (byproduct) prices are averaging Rs 28–Rs 30 per kg, providing additional margin support without overhead costs.
- Raw material contribution to cost is expected to remain sub-70%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 1G ethanol capex is completed; future capex is primarily directed toward CBG.
- Building 4 to 5 new CBG plants with a project cost of ~Rs 65 crore per plant (~Rs 300 crore total), funded by Rs 70–80 crore in debt and capital subsidies. Total installed CBG capacity expected to reach 72 metric tonnes/day by the end of the quarter.
- Total CBG build-out with GAIL and Sumitomo is envisioned around 250 TPD across ~20–24 plants over the next 2 years.
- SAF plant front-end engineering is ongoing; capex will commence once the government announces the policy roadmap.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total equity at Rs 1,687 crore; debt-to-equity ratio at 0.55x.
- Inventory reduced significantly from Rs 528 crore (March 2026) to Rs 206 crore (September 2026).
- Trade receivables reduced from Rs 406 crore to Rs 367 crore.
- CBG plants require minimal working capital (estimated at Rs 25–30 crore for a 100 TPD operation).
- Eligible for 25% capital subsidy upfront on CBG plants upon obtaining consent to operate, which will directly reduce term debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- El Nino impact causing significant drops in domestic crop acreage (maize down 35%, rice/sugarcane down 10%–12%), elevating raw material costs and squeezing margins.
- Government reduced maize-based ethanol prices by ~Rs 3 per litre in the new tender despite rising input costs, which management noted lacks logic and has been contested by the industry.
- Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) roadmap and incentive rollout from the Government of India has been delayed due to a need for further clarity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sucrit D. Patil (Eyesight Fintrade):** Asked about forward volume guidance and execution risks for the SAF plant. Management replied they have 8.5 crore litres of pending orders for Oct-Nov and expect 40–42 crore litres allocation for the new ESY.
2. **Disha (Analyst):** Inquired if the raw material cost increases can be passed on. Management explained that due to high sugar prices, sugar players won't divert syrup/B-heavy anyway, and TruAlt is mitigating grain cost pressures by stocking up maize/rice at cheaper locations and leveraging dual-feed flexibility.
3. **Prathamesh Sawant (Analyst):** Asked about El Nino impact on southern procurement. Management stated they are diversifying sourcing across Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, and Punjab by setting up warehouses.
4. **Udit Sehgal (Analyst):** Asked about promoter share pledges. Management clarified that pledges are for future personal purchases, and promoter holding is near 71% with room up to 75%.
5. **Bhavya Gandhi (Bajaj Alternate Investments):** Asked about ethanol realization and EBITDA metrics for CBG. Management noted blended ethanol realization is expected around Rs 64–Rs 65, and CBG EBITDA margin is expected around 50%–55%.
6. **Nitin Awasthi (Analyst):** Asked about the new OMC tender allocation rules based on 81-83 working days and capacity. Management confirmed the bid eligibility calculation allows TruAlt to bid for approximately 66 crore litres.

### One-line takeaway
TruAlt Bioenergy delivered a massive 606% YoY revenue surge and profitable H1 turnaround in H1 FY27, but faced a sequential margin squeeze in Q2 due to a 25%–30% jump in maize prices, while aggressively expanding its high-margin CBG footprint.
