# EID Parry India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While sugar prices and international markets are improving, Tamil Nadu cane availability issues, CPG revenue decline, and restructuring/closure costs at PSRIPL weigh on performance.

### Key numbers
- **Global Sugar Surplus (ISO 2025-26):** 2.24 MMT (vs 3.2 MMT deficit prior year)
- **Global Sugar Production:** 182 MMT
- **London White Sugar Prices:** Recovered to ~$471/ton by July 2026 (from ~$404/ton early 2026)
- **Raw Sugar Prices:** 14–15 cents per pound
- **India SY 2025-26 Estimates:** Gross production 31 MMT, ethanol diversion >3 MMT (2.9 MMT mentioned later), domestic consumption 28 MMT, exports 0.8 MMT, closing stocks 4.2 MMT, net production 28 MMT (up from 26 MMT)
- **Domestic Sugar Prices:** Well north of Rs. 45–46 per kg currently
- **TN Units Operation (Q1):** Operated for 54 days (vs 37 days YoY)
- **TN Cane Crushed (Q1):** 1.47 LMT (vs 2.12 LMT YoY)
- **Gross Recovery (TN, Q1):** 7.95% (vs 8.02% YoY)
- **Sugar Production (Q1):** 0.12 LMT (vs 0.17 LMT YoY)
- **Cane Landed Cost:** Rs. 4,031 per MT (vs Rs. 3,844 per MT YoY); FRP at Rs. 3,550
- **Sugar Sales Volume (Q1):** 0.89 LMT (vs 0.56 LMT YoY)
- **Average Sugar Selling Price:** Rs. 40.02 (vs Rs. 40.97 YoY)
- **Sugar Closing Stock:** 1.1 LMT valued at Rs. 42 (later stated as 1.16 LMT valued at Rs. 41.50) (vs 1.2 LMT YoY)
- **Sugar Segment Revenue (Q1):** Rs. 410 crore (vs Rs. 347 crore YoY)
- **Co-gen Generation (Q1):** 180 lakh units (LU) (vs 221 LU YoY)
- **Power Exports (Q1):** 89 LU (vs 122 LU YoY)
- **Power Tariff Realization:** Rs. 4.89 per unit (vs Rs. 3.67 per unit YoY)
- **Power Segment Revenue (Q1):** Rs. 6.6 crore (vs Rs. 7.53 crore YoY)
- **Distillery Production (Q1):** 351 lakh liters (LL) (vs 412 LL YoY)
- **Distillery Sales (Q1):** 380 LL (vs 413 LL YoY; comprising 138 LL ENA and 242 LL ethanol)
- **Distillery Price Realization:** Rs. 63.49 per liter (vs Rs. 67.59 per liter YoY)
- **Nutraceuticals Revenue (Standalone, Q1):** Rs. 6.22 crore (vs Rs. 6 crore YoY)
- **Nutraceuticals Revenue (Consolidated, Q1):** Rs. 61 crore (vs Rs. 27 crore YoY)
- **Consumer Product Group (CPG) Revenue (Q1):** Rs. 94 crore (vs Rs. 188 crore YoY)
- **PSRIPL Infusion / Debt Settlement:** Rs. 610 crore infused to settle bank obligations; additional Rs. 55 crore loan given (total Rs. 665 crore infused in Q1)
- **Standalone Debt (as of June 30, 2026):** Short-term debt Rs. 980 crore; Long-term debt Rs. 150 crore
- **Distillery Capacity:** 582 KLPD total (approx. 18 crore liters), out of which 120 KLPD is grain capacity.

### Highlights
- Global sugar markets transitioning from surplus-driven bearish phase to balanced outlook; Indian sugar prices moved above Rs. 45–46/kg due to El Nino conditions, tight inventories, and steady demand.
- PSRIPL (refinery) operations ceased as of March 31, 2026; all bank liabilities fully settled. SEZ unit exit in-process, expected to close by September 30, 2026; liquidation of plant and machinery is underway.
- CPG revenue fell due to an intentional recalibration of the business model toward margin-accretive products, though absolute contribution margin pool grew.
- Nutraceuticals growth driven largely by the Valensa business in the US and new product launches (derm, hair, and skin health).
- Employee costs increased due to Voluntary Retirement Schemes (VSS) offered at legacy plants to bring down fixed costs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects CPG to achieve a quarterly breakeven in another 4 to 5 quarters.
- Management expects Nutra to deliver its highest-ever revenue in FY27 with healthy EBITDA.
- Management expects to execute monetization of non-core land assets in FY27.
- Cane crushing in TN and AP expected to be flat or see about a 5% drop in the current year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global sugar consumption stable; Indian domestic consumption stable at 28 MMT.
- E20 blend levels expected to remain at 20% for the foreseeable future.
- Sugarcane availability in TN and AP remains a macro concern as farmers shift to more lucrative crops (like paddy).
- State support announced in Tamil Nadu for sugarcane via direct benefit transfer to farmers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cane landed cost increased to Rs. 4,031 per MT, driven by higher FRP (Rs. 3,550).
- CPG margin pool improved via shift to value-added products (jaggery, brown sugar).
- Nutraceuticals steady-state EBITDA margin targeted between 12% and 15%.
- Jaggery margins are substantially better than white sugar, akin to food product gross margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New jaggery plant in Karnataka will be commissioned in 6 months, more than doubling current jaggery capacity and targeting ~Rs. 100 crore turnover combined.
- No capacity additions planned for the India Nutra business.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Ruthless focus on working capital and cost reduction programs across all businesses.
- Standalone debt as of June 30, 2026: Short-term Rs. 980 crore, Long-term Rs. 150 crore (compared to March 31 short-term debt of Rs. 1,250 crore).
- Infused Rs. 610 crore plus Rs. 55 crore (total Rs. 665 crore) into PSRIPL subsidiary to clear all bank liabilities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cane availability and yields in TN and AP are under pressure; back-half rains in August and September are critical for Karnataka cane yields.
- Fixed feedstock availability in TN required insourcing feedstock at higher costs.
- Potential penalties from OMCs if committed ethanol supply volumes are not met.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjay Shah (KSA Securities) on CPG breakeven and model:** Management explained revenue dropped intentionally due to model recalibration, focusing on margin-accretive products; quarterly breakeven targeted in 4–5 quarters. Organic/inorganic expansion into ethnic snacks/culinary convenience is being explored.
2. **Rajesh Majumdar on PSRIPL accounting and cash outflow:** Clarified that the actual cash outgo was Rs. 610 crore for debt settlement plus a Rs. 55 crore loan (total Rs. 665 crore in Q1), while impairment and guarantee reversals were accounting adjustments.
3. **Rajesh Majumdar on TN sugar continuity:** Management noted they are running a tight ship on costs and evaluating operational measures amid structural cane shifts to paddy.
4. **Rajakumar Vaidyanathan on sugar vs. ethanol economics:** Management stated that given current high sugar prices, producing more sugar makes sense, but they must balance this against contractual OMC ethanol supply commitments to avoid penalties.
5. **Gautam Dedhia on non-core asset monetization:** Management confirmed land parcels are being prospected for sale with an expectation to execute in FY27.

### One-line takeaway
EID Parry reported a mixed Q1 FY27 characterized by lower cane crushing and revenue drops in CPG and the closed refinery, offset by higher sugar realizations, disciplined working capital management, and strong traction in the US Nutraceuticals business.
