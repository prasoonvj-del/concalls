# Balrampur Chini Mills Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sugar segment performance (166% PBIT growth) and on-track PLA project commissioning, despite distillery margin pressure.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained PLA commissioning timeline (November/December).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "anticipating" to "confirming" a tight supply-demand balance; management now explicitly projects a ~3 million tonne import requirement for India.
- **New topics:** Secured first institutional order for PLA-based products; FSSAI/pan masala regulatory opportunity; specific mention of cane variety 20016 as a promising replacement for 238.
- **Last quarter's promises:** PLA commissioning remains on track for November/December; Maizapur distillery is operational.

### Key numbers
- **Sugar segment PBIT:** Rs 140 crore (+166% YoY).
- **PLA capex spent:** Rs 2,400 crore (as of 15th October).
- **Cane availability:** Expected 2% lower YoY.
- **Ethanol production target:** 18-20 crore litres (total for the season).
- **Dividend:** Rs 4 per equity share.

### Highlights
- Sugar segment PBIT grew 166% YoY due to improved realizations and firm domestic prices.
- PLA project is on track; construction is in full swing with operations expected to commence in November 2026.
- Management confirmed the first institutional order for PLA-based products and successful testing protocols for pan masala applications.
- Company expects 2% lower cane availability for the upcoming season due to unseasonal rains.
- *Record check:* The PLA project timeline remains consistent with the previous H2 FY27 target.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects domestic sugar prices to remain firm.
- India likely requires ~3 million tonnes of imports to maintain comfortable inventory levels.
- PLA business: Management requested patience for 6 months to stabilize production and marketing; expects a "bright tomorrow" post-stabilization.
- Cane price sensitivity: Every Rs 10/quintal increase in cane price results in Rs 100 crore of incremental raw material cost.

### Demand & industry commentary
- 2025-26 season closing stock: 3.6 million tonnes.
- 2026-27 production expected to decline (Maharashtra/Karnataka impacted by El Niño; U.P. stable).
- Government has allowed 10 lakh tonnes of Tariff Rate Quota (TRQ) imports.
- Ethanol: Procurement prices have not been revised for 3 years despite a 16-20% increase in Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Distillery margins compressed due to stagnant ethanol procurement prices.
- Fixed overheads (depreciation, maintenance) were charged to the P&L in this non-crushing quarter, impacting reported numbers.
- Maizapur distillery expected to run on grain (maize/rice) with a target of 8-9 crore litres.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- PLA project: 80,000 tonnes capacity; Rs 2,400 crore spent by 15th October.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management highlighted a strong financial position but provided no specific debt figures.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Non-revision of ethanol procurement prices despite rising feedstock costs.
- Uncertainty regarding U.P. State Advised Price (SAP) for sugarcane.
- Execution risks associated with the new PLA business (production yields, chemical consumption, and marketing).
- Potential volatility in sugar production estimates.

### One-line takeaway
Strong sugar segment performance and on-track PLA commissioning drive the outlook, though distillery margins remain pressured by stagnant ethanol pricing.
