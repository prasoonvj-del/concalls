# Prime Fresh Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-24 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by higher volume growth, recovery of old pending bills in the service business, and inventory gains from rising fruit and vegetable prices.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 81.71 crore (up 15.7% YoY from Rs 53.34 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 6.00 crore (up 51% YoY from Rs 4.02 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.83% (vs 7.53% in Q1 FY26)
- **PAT:** Rs 4.35 crore (up 51% YoY from Rs 2.89 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Working capital/debt limits:** Bank of Baroda cash credit limit hiked from Rs 7.8 crore to Rs 20 crore at 8.5% interest; additional Rs 20 crore sanctioned for the Nashik CDP project.
- **Sinar, Nashik project (IA component):** Total investment of Rs 75 crore (excluding Rs 5 crore to Rs 6 crore pre-operative expenditure), of which Rs 45 crore to Rs 50 crore is spent on infrastructure. Eligible for up to 40%-50% subsidy/grant (approx Rs 24 crore grant). Peak term loan utilization expected at roughly Rs 35 crore, settling around Rs 18 crore to Rs 20 crore.

### Highlights
- Operating across 19 states with a network of over 1,30,000 farmers, 2,400+ suppliers, 90+ HCE relationships, and 100+ FPOs.
- Current product focus is on 9 to 12 F&V products, with plans to add 3 to 4 more categories in the next 1 year.
- Nashik Cluster Development Programme (CDP) under the National Horticulture Board of India is likely to be awarded in the current month (August 2026).
- Acquired a 51% stake in a company focused on value-added products and the ready-to-eat segment.
- Subsidiary Florens Farming Limited accounts for about 10% of the F&V business.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guides for a sustainable EBITDA margin range of 7% to 7.5% and a net profit margin range of 5% to 5.5% due to higher investments and corporate overheads in the current year.
- Volume growth target: 15% to 20% minimum.
- Value growth target: 25% to 30%.
- Internal aspirational goals: EBITDA margins of 9.5% to 11% in the next 2 to 2.5 years (by FY29), and a vision to reach Rs 2,000 crore revenue by 2031.
- Internal rolling supply target: INR 10 crore to INR 15 crore supply on credit basis by FY28 to drive incremental sales without additional capital.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic market is preferred over exports due to volatile freight rates and environment.
- Onion and pomegranate prices are shooting up, driving growth; pomegranate volume is strong, though prices may fall, impacting value growth.
- B2C business is minimal (1% to 2%); management does not intend to heavily invest in B2C to avoid defocusing from the core B2B model.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 EBITDA margin (9.83%) may not be sustainable because the jump was aided by old recoveries in the service business, strong volume growth, and inventory gains from rising F&V prices.
- Direct farmer sourcing provides a long-term advantage of 5% to 8% over supplier purchases.
- Price pass-through lag to customers is typically 4 to 8 days for most business; longer (1 to 3 months) for less than 4% of total business (HoReCa/tenders).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sinar, Nashik project ("Prime Fresh Agro Park") capex execution to begin between end-October and end-November, spread over 18 to 20 months in phases.
- Sourcing capacity stands at close to 2.5 lakh tonnes (against FY sales of 65,000 tonnes). Nashik CDP aims to add 15,000 tonnes sourcing capability in Year 1 and up to 2 lakh tonnes over 6 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone debtors reduced by around INR 12 crores in Q1. Internal target is to maintain debtors below 25% of sales (88 to 94 days).
- Company holds INR 7 crore to INR 8 crore as slow-moving or non-moving debtors from 2022-2025 errors.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile freight rates and environment impacting exports.
- Substantial drop in pomegranate prices may cause value growth to lag volume growth.
- Past errors in credit extension resulted in slow/non-moving debtors of INR 7 crore to INR 8 crore.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prateek Giri (Subhlabh Research) on working capital and receivables:** Management acknowledged historical errors leading to INR 7-8 crore in slow-moving debtors, but highlighted debtor reduction in Q1, improved credit monitoring, and new Bank of Baroda credit limits (INR 20 crore at 8.5%), stating equity dilution will not be needed for working capital.
2. **Prateek Giri on trade payables:** Management stated that due to built credibility and past prompt payments, they are targeting a rolling INR 10-15 crore supply on credit by FY28 to generate incremental sales without capital investment.
3. **Tanmay Jhaveri (Fininterest Capital) on the Nashik/Sinnar project details and capex:** Management explained the dual component (farmer subsidy and Implementing Agency capex of INR 75 crore with ~INR 24 crore grant), phasing of capex over 18-20 months, and peak term loan utilization of ~INR 35 crore.
4. **Tanmay Jhaveri on price pass-through lag:** Management clarified that procurement price increases are passed to customers within 4 to 8 days (except for <4% of business like HoReCa/tenders which take 1-3 months).
5. **Sonu Vasan (Individual Investor) on B2C expansion:** Management stated they spent INR 55-75 lakhs in the past on B2C research but chose to stay away because B2C requires dedicated long-term capital commitment and resources, preferring to stick to core B2B.

### One-line takeaway
Prime Fresh reported a strong Q1 FY27 led by inventory gains and service recoveries, and is scaling up via the Nashik Cluster Development Programme while focusing on working capital discipline and B2B growth.
