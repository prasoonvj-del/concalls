# Venkys (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Agri & Sugar*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong poultry realizations and Animal Health performance were offset by oilseed profitability pressure due to volatile soya prices.

### Key numbers
- **Cash and cash equivalents:** Rs 112.69 crore (as of 30th September 2026) vs Rs 117.53 crore (as of September 2025).
- **Q2 FY27 average realizations:** Broiler chicks Rs 41.18 (vs Rs 21.42 in September 2025); layer chicks Rs 47.89 (vs Rs 45 in September 2025); commercial broiler bird Rs 108.57 (vs Rs 71.27 in September 2025); broiler hatching eggs Rs 33.85 (vs Rs 18.96 in September 2025); layer hatching eggs Rs 13.22 (vs Rs 12.44).
- **H1 FY27 average realizations:** Broiler chicks Rs 34.29 (vs Rs 26.48 in H1 FY25-26); layer chicks Rs 47.84 (vs Rs 45.39); SPF eggs Rs 91.48 (vs Rs 78.23); contract broiler farming bird Rs 102.46 (vs Rs 80.56); broiler hatching eggs Rs 29.64 (vs Rs 17.02); layer hatching eggs Rs 13.24 (vs Rs 12.46); DOC Rs 54,021 per tonne (vs Rs 33,571); refined oil Rs 1,46,722 per tonne (vs Rs 1,22,462); maize Rs 24,539 per tonne (vs Rs 25,334); soya Rs 56,520 per tonne (vs Rs 36,163).
- **Cost of production (broiler bird):** Rs 104-105 per kg in Q2 (vs Rs 90 per kg in September 2025); current cost of production around Rs 110 per kg.
- **Q2 FY27 sales volumes (Oilseed):** DOC cake 50,716 tonnes (vs 56,000 tonnes in September 2025); oil 11,035 tonnes (vs 11,514 tonnes).
- **Capacity utilization:** Oilseed H1 capacity utilization at 53% (Q2 at ~50%); SPF eggs capacity utilization at 37% in H1 (vs 55% last year); Animal Health new unit at Kesurdi at 35%-40%, old unit at Osade at ~70%.
- **Animal Health guidance:** FY revenue expected between Rs 435 crore to Rs 450 crore (vs Rs 391 crore last year).

### Highlights
- Q2 is typically a weak quarter for the Poultry and Poultry Products segment due to fasting periods and religious rituals, but performed well this year due to better realizations.
- Oilseed profitability was impacted by a sharp increase in soya seed prices in July and August followed by a sudden drop in September, leading to lower realizations.
- Animal Health outside sales grew 20% to 21% YoY.
- *vs Record:* The call notes ongoing biosecurity issues and lower buyer requirements in SPF eggs, breaking the pattern of smooth capacity ramp-ups noted in earlier decks.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a long-term average 4% to 5% growth in poultry consumption.
- Animal Health revenue is guided at Rs 435 crore to Rs 450 crore for the full year, with a minimum 15%+ top-line growth speed.
- Oilseed capacity utilization is expected to improve to 60%-65% minimum in the remaining period.
- Layer segment capex of approximately Rs 60 crore is planned for FY27-28, with land acquisition currently underway in Uttar Pradesh.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Non-vegetarian consumption accounts for 70% to 75% of India, with chicken remaining the primary organized, cost-effective non-veg alternative.
- Input costs (protein and energy sources like maize and soya) have increased by 35% to 40%; management stated that chicken and egg prices are bound to increase in tandem with inflation over the long term.
- Maize prices reached Rs 33-34 per kg; the company is optimizing costs by substituting a portion of maize with bajra (trading Rs 3-4 lower).
- Proposed imports of DDGS (Distillers Dried Grains with Solubles) could act as a partial substitute for soya (replacing up to 10% in broiler rations), reducing pressure on soya prices without threatening domestic crushers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Oilseed margins suffered in September as end-product DOC prices dropped from Rs 60,000/tonne in July to Rs 48,000/tonne.
- Animal Health stock-in-trade purchases (INR 104.6 crore referenced in annual report) represent bulk raw material inputs for feed safety, gut health, and anticoccidials sourced globally from China and Europe.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Cumulative land holding of the company is approximately 1,000 acres, primarily used for breeding operations and restricted for biosecurity reasons. Rooftop solar is utilized across units for power cost-effectiveness.
- SPF total capacity stands at 1,83,00,000 eggs (production was 33.74 lakh eggs in Q2). Expansion is ongoing and will take over 12 months to yield production.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 112.69 crore as of 30th September 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unpredictable global commodity price movements (maize and soya) and uneven monsoon rains affecting crop yields and seed availability.
- Biosecurity issues constraining SPF egg production and export opportunities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS)** asked about the margin impact of permanently higher maize prices due to ethanol blending. Management responded that input costs have risen 35%-40% across commodities, and poultry/egg prices will adjust upward over the long term as consumers accept the new normal.
2. **Rajas Shah (BTH Capital)** asked about the reason for low SPF egg capacity utilization (37%). Management cited biosecurity issues and lower buyer requirements.
3. **Akshay (Alpha Invesco)** inquired about oilseed division recovery and EBIT margins. Management explained that September prices crashed from Rs 60,000 to Rs 48,000/tonne for DOC, but trends are improving for H2.
4. **Ankit Kapoor (Individual Investor)** asked about the benefit of DDGS import quotas. Management stated that imported DDGS will serve as a partial protein substitute for soya, helping keep the cost of production in check.
5. **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS)** suggested executing a Rs 100-200 crore share buyback given the removal of buyback tax and demerging the Animal Healthcare division. Management noted the suggestions and stated the Board will consider them.

### One-lake takeaway
Venky's delivered a good Q2 performance driven by strong poultry realizations, offsetting volatility in the oilseed segment and lower SPF egg utilization.
