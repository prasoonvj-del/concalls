# Kansai Nerolac Paints Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Paints & Coatings*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Mixed demand across segments with geopolitical and commodity cost headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Standalone: Net revenue growth 10.2% YoY, PBDIT growth 7.7% YoY, PBT growth 5.1% YoY.
- Consolidated: Net revenue growth 9.8% YoY, PBDIT growth 8.3% YoY, PBT growth 5.8% YoY.
- Decorative growth: Value grew at high single-digit; volume grew at low single-digit.
- Industrial segments: Automotive, Performance Coating Liquids, and Powder Coatings witnessed strong/robust growth; Auto Refinish growth was flat.
- Network expansion: Added 1,700 dealers in Q1.
- Capex outlay: INR 601 crores planned over 2+ years for capacity expansions at Sayakha, Bawal, and Hosur (66,000 KL/year paint/coatings and ~10,000 metric tonnes/year resin capacity).
- Industrial price hikes: Approximately 5% realized so far.

### Highlights
- Management launched employee value proposition ("where our passion takes color") to aid talent attraction and retention.
- Decorative 6-pronged strategy drives retail, project business (double-digit growth), construction chemicals (double-digit growth), wood finish (high single-digit growth), branding/media, and network expansion.
- New product launches include Excel Everlast 20 (exterior paint with 20-year warranty), Excel Total Floor Coat, and Perma NoDamp NXT.
- Won Golden Peacock Award for Energy Efficiency at Hosur Plant and an ABBY 2026 award (1 Grand Prix, 1 Gold, 6 Silver, 3 Bronze).
- Maintained a bronze medal in EcoVadis 2026 (top 18% of companies assessed) and strong CRISIL ESG rating (16 out of 548 in the chemical industry).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to maintain EBITDA margins around 13% to 14% for the year, subject to geopolitical stability and crude oil volatility.
- Medium-term target is to reach the higher end of 14%+ margins through premiumization and fixed-cost leverage over the next 2-3 years.
- Annual baseline capex is normally INR 150-200 crores; the INR 601 crore capex will be spread across 2 to 2.5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Competitive intensity remains high with freebies and aggressive dealer schemes continuing, though the market has reached a steady-state equilibrium.
- Kansai Nerolac is consciously prioritizing premium mix over low-margin, high-volume segments.
- Festive demand shift: Diwali is in November, leaving October fully available for painting cycles in Q3.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Faced significant headwinds from oil price increases, sharp rupee depreciation, and West Asia geopolitical crises affecting raw materials (especially solvents).
- Industrial price hikes lag by 1 to 2 quarters; further price increases of ~3% in decorative and 3%-5% in industrial are anticipated in Q2.
- Some peak June raw material inflation saw slight downward corrections.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- INR 601 crore capex outlay over 2-2.5 years adds 66,000 KL/year and 10,000 MT/year of resin capacity across Sayakha, Bawal, and Hosur.
- Resin capacity addition serves as backward integration for automotive paints.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not explicitly discussed in detail beyond normal capex outlays.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts causing supply chain disruptions.
- High commodity prices tied to crude oil mix and raw material availability uncertainty.
- Import cost surges driven by rupee depreciation.
- Potential impact on consumer sentiment from the inflationary environment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on volume growth vs. peers:** Management clarified that lower volume growth (low single-digit vs. high single-digit value growth) is intentional to prioritize premium mix over low-margin items. Management added that competitive intensity remains high.
- **Avi Mehta (Macquarie) on capex timeline:** Management clarified that the INR 601 crore capex will be spread over 2 to 2.5 years, resulting in a manageable annual capex increase.
- **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) on industrial price hikes:** Management stated that industrial price hikes average around 5% so far, with further negotiations ongoing for Q2.
- **Mihir Shah (Nomura) on Q2 margin trajectory:** Management noted that while high-cost inventory will impact Q2, anticipated price increases of 3% in deco and 3%-5% in industrial, alongside internal cost optimizations, will help protect margins.
- **Percy Panthaki (IIFL) on decorative volume vs. value:** Management reiterated that sacrificing low-margin volume to over-index on emulsions is the deliberate reason for the volume-value growth divergence.

### One-line takeaway
Kansai Nerolac reported a steady Q1 with 9.8% consolidated revenue growth, intentionally sacrificing low-margin volume for premium mix while navigating raw material inflation and high competitive intensity.
