# JSW Dulux Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Paints & Coatings*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong volume growth of 25% (18% to 19% adjusted for price) and revenue of Rs 965 crore under tough transition circumstances.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 965 crore (up 18.8% YoY like-to-like from Rs 812 crore)
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 360.8 crore (up 2% YoY from Rs 353.6 crore)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 115.1 crore (up 14.7% YoY from Rs 100.4 crore)
- **PAT:** Rs 135.5 crore (vs Rs 67.2 crore YoY; includes exceptional items: Rs 55.9 crore dividend income from ICI R&T and Rs 21.5 crore interest on IT fund refund)
- **Volume Growth:** 25% blended (18% to 19% adjusted for price)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.9%
- **Gross Margin:** 37.4% (underlying gross margin closer to 39.5% to 40%)
- **Headcount addition:** Added approximately 160 people in R&D and regional areas (e.g., Hyderabad)

### Highlights
- Relocated Delhi headquarters to JSW Center in Mumbai between April and May and June.
- Carved out powder coating business, IRC business, and a few customers retained by AkzoNobel in its unlisted entity; JSW Dulux like-to-like houses all remaining brands and businesses.
- Reclassified promotional spends (painter reclass) to gross revenue in line with industry practice (impacted gross margins by ~2.5 points).
- Launched "Project Akshaya" integration program with JSW Paints covering cross-manufacturing (e.g., JSW Paints made in Gwalior, JSW Dulux in JSW Paints plants), supply chain redesign, ERP migration by year-end, and functional alignment.
- Realized savings of Rs 2.4 crore in Q1 from Project Akshaya initial initiatives.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to return to the 13% to 15% range.
- Management maintains the goal to achieve double-digit growth in both volume and value.
- Vision 2030-2031: Aiming to become a #2 player in decorative and #1 in industrial coatings (combined with JSW Paints).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Decorative business grew high double digits; industrial businesses grew upwards of 25% (automotive and industrial coatings grew in the 20s).
- Growth was led by decorative premium (45% of portfolio) and adjacencies (waterproofing, woodcare), while mass market and economy segments grew in early double digits.
- Growth was driven by an increase in value per outlet from existing towns and secular contributions across outlet types.
- Competitive intensity remains high with new entrants pursuing discounting strategies; management benchmarks pricing against the market leader.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended inventory was ~95 days (decorative at ~60 days, industrial over 100 days), leading to an impact on gross margins due to buying high-priced stocks at the end of March.
- Crude challenges and high-priced inventory impacted gross margins by approximately 2 points.
- Opex increased due to hiring ~160 people in R&D and regional expansion and associated travel costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Active town presence to be expanded from ~3,400-3,500 towns to ~4,500 towns this year, focusing on populations greater than 20,000.
- Top 20 high-potential/high-share markets to transition to a direct/hybrid model alongside the existing distributor model.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Received Rs 108 crore income tax refund for assessment year 2013-14 (including Rs 21.5 crore interest).
- Received Rs 55.9 crore dividend income from ICI R&T from real estate monetization transactions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Turbulent external environment and significant input/raw material price increases due to crude challenges.
- Short-term margin dilution from transitioning inventories, painter promotional reclassifications, and reinvestments in headcount/R&D.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pratik Gothi (HSBC) on ground execution and deco changes:** Management explained the micro-market strategy, sharpening focus on top categories (premium, adjacencies, mid-market), digitalizing painter and architect programs, and shifting select top towns to direct/hybrid models.
2. **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) on strategic targets and timelines:** Management clarified the 2030-2031 vision to reach a #2 position in decorative and #1 in industrial (combined entity size currently ~Rs 6,000 crore), while detailing the digitalized customer lifecycle management for painters.
3. **Abhishek Mathur on drivers of relative volume outperformance vs peers:** Management noted that decorative growth was broad-based (led by premium and value per outlet), supported by early strategic planning initiated around the time of the acquisition announcement and enhanced digital lead management.
4. **Pratik Gothi (HSBC) on discounts/rebates:** Management clarified that higher rebate ratios in annual reports include project business rates and distributor commissions (4.5%–4.7%), and they do not match new entrant discounting, benchmarking instead against the industry market leader.

### One-line takeaway
JSW Dulux delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 25% volume growth and 18.8% revenue growth, while aggressively investing in headcount, digital systems, and integration synergies under Project Akshaya to drive its 2030 market leadership vision.
