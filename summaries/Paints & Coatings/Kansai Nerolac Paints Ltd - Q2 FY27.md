# Kansai Nerolac Paints Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Paints & Coatings*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth driven by automotive and projects, but margins pressured by volatile crude and currency fluctuations.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (targeting EBITDA margin recovery towards 13%-14% by Q4 FY27).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to more optimistic on demand, citing healthy macro indicators and buoyant auto/project segments, though input cost volatility remains a concern.
- New topics or risks: Mention of Nerofix acquisition integration and related VAM inventory pricing impacts; specific mention of Bangladesh subsidiary issues. Old risks like West Asia tensions and crude volatility persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Added 3,000 dealers in H1 (on track with the 10%-15% annual network expansion target).

### Key numbers
- Standalone net revenue: up 18.9% YoY (H1: up 14.2%)
- Standalone PBDIT: up 4.9% YoY (H1: up 5.8%)
- Standalone PBT: up 4.3% YoY (H1: up 4%)
- Consolidated net revenue: up 18.6% YoY (H1: up 14%)
- Consolidated PBDIT: up 4.1% YoY (H1: up 6.6%)
- Consolidated PBT: up 3.6% YoY (H1: up 4.9%)
- Decorative value growth: nearing double-digit
- Project and institutional business growth: double-digit
- Construction chemicals and waterproofing growth: waterproofing upward of 30%, construction chemicals 60%-70%
- Nepal subsidiary growth: close to 20%
- Dealer network addition: 3,000+ dealers added in H1 (75%-80% machine dealers)
- NXTGEN stores: 190+, shop-in-shop: 270+, paint zone stores: 490+ at end of H1
- Industrial price hikes: 7%-8% achieved retrospectively (April 1 / June 1)

### Highlights
- Decorative segment performance aided by PaaS (Paint as a Service) in 250+ cities, AID Connect in 45+ cities, and Pragati program covering 90,000+ painters in H1.
- New product launches include Excel Everlast 20 (20-year warranty exterior paint), Excel Total Floor Coat, Perma NoDamp NXT, and Nova 2-in-1 primer.
- Won Golden Peacock Award for Energy Efficiency for the Hosur plant, e4m Real-Time Programmatic Awards 2026, and multiple ABBY Awards 2026.
- Retained EcoVadis bronze medal (top 18%) and CRISIL ESG rating strong category.
- Nerofix integration complete; reported double-digit revenue growth but near-term profitability hit due to long VAM inventory.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to touch 13%-14% EBITDA margins by Q4 FY27 as further price hike settlements flow through.
- Management pegs safe crude range at $80-$85 for margin stability.
- Expects good growth in Q3 based on current project pipelines and auto demand.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Elevated volatile crude oil prices, extreme rupee volatility, and geopolitical challenges continued.
- Automotive demand was very good across passenger vehicles, two/three-wheelers, and commercial vehicles.
- Competitive intensity remains intense across regions with heavy investments by all stakeholders.
- Intentional mix correction in decorative (sacrificing low-end products) led to value growth nearing double-digit.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inflation was upwards of 25%, while price increases achieved were 7%-8%.
- Industrial price hikes settled retrospectively (effective April 1 or June 1); further price hikes announced at end of September to flow into Q3.
- VAM prices settled around $1,200.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Distribution expansion focused on low-presence towns and optimizing underperforming dealers (targeting 10%-15% numerical expansion annually).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Accounts restated following Nerofix merger, impacting profitability by INR 4 crores to INR 5 crores due to inventory pricing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued West Asia tensions leading to supply chain disruptions and market uncertainty.
- Sustained high crude oil and derivative prices coupled with currency volatility.
- Bangladesh subsidiary facing top-line and bottom-line issues.
- Potential impact on consumer sentiment from inflationary environment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya Bhartia (Investec) on industrial price hikes and margins:** Management stated 7%-8% price hikes were secured retrospectively, but against 25% inflation, further discussions are ongoing; expects sequential margin improvement aiming for 13%-14% by Q4.
- **Pratik on decorative volume/value and competition:** Management noted decorative value growth is nearing double-digit due to mix correction, and competitive intensity remains high across regions.
- **Aniket on industrial growth:** Management noted industrial profitability depends on mix and expects Q3 growth to be good based on pipeline.
- **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) on subsidiaries, waterproofing, and network:** Management reported Nepal grew ~20% with healthy profits, Bangladesh is struggling, waterproofing grew >30%, construction chemicals grew 60%-70%, and 3,000 dealers were added in H1.

### One-line takeaway
Kansai Nerolac delivered 18.6% consolidated revenue growth in Q2 FY27, though profitability was constrained by raw material inflation and lag in full industrial price pass-through.
