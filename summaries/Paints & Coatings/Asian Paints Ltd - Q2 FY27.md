# Asian Paints Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Paints & Coatings*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line value growth and volume resilience offset by gross margin compression from input cost inflation, B2B price realization lag, and high competitive intensity.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained full-year EBITDA margin guidance band of 18% to 20%; narrowed volume growth expectations to 8% to 9% going forward (vs 8% to 10% earlier). 
- **Pricing:** Reversed previous stance of avoiding further price hikes by announcing a ~3.5% price hike for November 2026 (in addition to ~5% taken in Q2 and ~7% in Q1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Volume growth moderated to 6.4% in Q2 (vs 9% in Q1) as Q2 absorbed the full impact of dealer inventory pre-filling from Q1 price hikes alongside seasonal product mix shifts.
- **New topics & risks:** Imposition of Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on TiO₂ effective October 24, 2026, adding a 2% to 3% cost pressure.
- **Delivery on promises:** Commissioned Phase 1 of the VAE manufacturing plant in September 2026 (target was August 2026).

---

### Key numbers
* **Decorative Volume Growth:** 6.4% YoY (on base of ~11% last year); H1 volume growth at ~8% YoY.
* **Decorative Value Growth:** 14.5% YoY; H1 value growth ~16% YoY.
* **Deco + Industrial Volume Growth:** 6.8% YoY; Value Growth: 16.4% YoY.
* **Standalone Net Sales Growth:** 14.8% YoY; PBDIT Margin: 17.1% (vs 18.5% YoY); PAT Growth: 15% YoY.
* **Consolidated Net Sales Growth:** ~17% YoY; PBDIT Margin: 15.6% (down 202 bps YoY); PAT Growth: 7% YoY.
* **H1 Consolidated Performance:** PBDIT Margin at 18.2%; PAT Growth at 24% YoY.
* **New Product Contribution:** 18% of total revenues.
* **Industrial JVs Performance:** PPGAP (Auto OE/Refinishes) revenue grew 34% YoY, PBT margin at 14.2% (down 309 bps YoY); APPPG (General Industrial) revenue grew 28% YoY.
* **International Business:** Revenue grew ~30% YoY; PBT margin compressed to 4.8%.
* **Interim Dividend:** Rs. 5.75 per share (payout ratio 21.4% for H1 FY27).

---

### Highlights
* **Regionalization & Brand Integration:** Tied up with 5 regional *Bigg Boss* formats across regional markets and launched region-specific shade guides.
* **Product Innovations:** Launched *Apcolite* premium satin emulsion with polyurethane washability technology, a universal wood & metal anti-corrosion product with a 4-year warranty, and *Chromacosm* shade system (5,000+ colors).
* **Decor & Category Performance:** Bath, Kitchen, and Weatherseal categories delivered strong growth; White Teak growth was slower.
* **Backward Integration:** Commissioned Phase 1 of the GenNext VAE green technology facility in September 2026.

---

### Guidance & outlook
* **Volume Growth:** Targeted at 8% to 9% going forward, supported by the upcoming festive (longer Diwali in November) and wedding seasons.
* **Margins:** Reconfirmed full-year PBDIT margin guidance band of 18% to 20%; normalized gross margin expected in the 41% to 43% band.
* **VAE Benefits:** Management expects Phase 1 (150 KT capacity) to reach full utilization within next year, yielding 300 bps to 500 bps cost/margin benefits on relevant products.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Geography Trends:** Rural markets continue to outpace urban markets; urban markets supported by strong B2B demand in T1/T2 cities.
* **Competitive Intensity:** Remains high across all segments, accompanied by elevated network-level promotional spending across the industry.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Inflation:** Q2 experienced ~3% sequential raw material inflation, outpacing calibrated price increases.
* **Pricing Actions:** Took a ~7% price hike in Q1, ~5% in Q2, and announced an additional ~3.5% hike for November 2026.
* **Gross Margin Compression:** Lower gross margin caused by unrecovered cost inflation and a seasonally subdued product mix.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **VAE Facility CapEx:** Total CapEx of ~Rs. 3,250 crore for the VAM/VAE chemistry project. Phase 1 operationalized in September 2026.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Maintained H1 dividend payout ratio at 21.4% with an interim dividend of Rs. 5.75 per share.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD):** Imposition of ADD on TiO₂ from October 24, 2026, creating an estimated 2% to 3% cost impact.
* **Raw Material Volatility:** Sustained volatility in crude derivatives and key raw materials.
* **Geopolitical & Foreign Exchange Pressures:** Regional conflicts and currency devaluation impacting Middle East and Africa operations.
* **Monsoon Deficit:** Potential risk to sustained rural demand recovery.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Mihir Shah (Nomura):** *On Q2 volume normalization and gross margin trajectory.*  
  *Management:* Q2 absorbed the full effect of Q1 inventory pre-fill; gross margins should normalize in the 41% to 43% band following the November price increase.
* **Avi Mehta (Macquarie):** *On risks to the 18-20% EBITDA margin guidance band.*  
  *Management:* Confident in maintaining the 18% to 20% band for FY27 based on H1 performance (18.2% consolidated), upcoming price hikes, cost excellence programs, and VAE benefits.
* **Aditya Bhartiya (Investec):** *On TiO₂ Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) impact and B2B pricing lag.*  
  *Management:* ADD adds 2-3% to costs, which will be counteracted via TiO₂ usage rationalization, alternative grades, and calibrated price hikes. B2B and international price realization lags will be recovered in Q3/Q4.
* **Manoj Menon (ICICI Securities):** *On why full inflation is not passed on directly to consumers.*  
  *Management:* Decorative paint is a discretionary category; aggressive price hikes risk demand deferral or consumer down-gradation. Pricing is calibrated to avoid disrupting consumer demand.

---

### One-line takeaway
Asian Paints delivered resilient 6.4% volume and 17% consolidated revenue growth, taking further price hikes and commissioning its VAE plant to protect its 18–20% EBITDA margin target amid RM inflation and TiO₂ Anti-Dumping Duties.
