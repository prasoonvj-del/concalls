# Paints & Coatings: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Paints & Coatings sector reveals a recurring theme of **resilient volume and top-line growth offset by margin compression**. Managements across the board cited challenges from persistent raw material inflation (particularly crude derivatives and titanium dioxide), geopolitical tensions (such as Middle East conflicts and the Iran war impacting raw material costs), and high-cost inventory liquidation. Despite these headwinds, companies reported robust festive demand heading into October, with several noting record-high monthly sales. Industrial and B2B segments generally outperformed or provided a healthy buffer against urban decorative slowdowns, while rural markets showed leadership in recovery.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** All four companies reported strong underlying volume momentum and highlighted buoyant demand in October, aided by the festive season (Diwali) and wedding season. Paint demand was generally characterized as historically inelastic to price increases.
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   **Asian Paints** noted that rural markets continue to lead over urban, with B2B providing a key buffer in urban areas.
    *   **Berger Paints** pointed to extended rains impacting eastern markets and a conscious mix improvement away from price-sensitive categories (with putty, textures, and enamel de-growing).
    *   **JSW Dulux** achieved the highest volume growth in the sector (21.8% YoY), driven partly by low base effects and strong undercoat/adjacent category performance.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
Managements universally reported gross and EBITDA margin pressures driven by double-digit raw material inflation (crude and TiO₂ spikes) and the liquidation of high-cost inventory. 
*   **Asian Paints** experienced ~3% inflation in Q2, faces a 2-3% cost impact from the new Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on TiO₂, and targets a gross margin band of 41-43%.
*   **Berger Paints** saw gross margins compressed by double-digit RM inflation and high-cost inventory, with industrial gross margins dropping ~5.5%.
*   **Indigo Paints** suffered a severe, one-off gross margin drop (down 660 bps YoY to 38.5%) due to high-cost RM inventory from March/April crude spikes, but noted normalization by September.
*   **JSW Dulux** faced a 400 bps gross margin drop, specifically driven by industrial raw material COGS increasing 30%-35% against a lag in price recovery.

To combat inflation, companies implemented multiple rounds of calibrated price increases across Q2 and heading into Q3 (ranging from 3% to over 7% depending on the segment).

## Capex & capacity
*   **Asian Paints:** Commissioned Phase 1 of its VAE facility in September (total capex Rs 3,250 crore), expecting full capacity of Phase 1 within a year and a 300-500 bps margin benefit on relevant products.
*   **Berger Paints:** Completed expansion at the Hindupur plant and is evaluating the West Bengal incentive policy for the Panagarh plant, alongside plans for an Orissa plant.
*   **Indigo Paints:** Trial production at the new Jodhpur water-based plant (90,000 kiloliters/annum) commenced in September, with full production expected by end-November/December 2026. Management noted major capex is largely complete for the next 2-3 years.
*   **JSW Dulux:** Leveraging cross-manufacturing across 5 JSW Dulux and 3 JSW Paints plants; selective capacity investments ongoing with industrial utilization nearing 75%.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Asian Paints:** Shifted tone from "cautious" to acknowledging a "turbulent environment"; volume growth guidance for the remainder of the year adjusted to 8-9% (previously 8-10%); flagged the new Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on TiO₂. Successfully delivered on the VAE facility commissioning timeline.
*   **JSW Dulux:** Maintained intent to approach ~13% EBITDA margin while turning more cautious on input-cost inflation ($105-$115 crude); delivered Rs 7.3 crore in synergies via Project Akshaya (vs Rs 2.4 crore in Q1).

## Bullish vs cautious

| Company | Tone | Key reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Asian Paints** | Mixed | Strong volume/value growth and VAE commissioning balanced against persistent inflation, margin pressure, and new TiO₂ ADD headwinds. |
| **Berger Paints** | Cautious | Gross margins compressed by double-digit RM inflation and ad spends, though offset by strong decorative value growth and robust industrial demand. |
| **Indigo Paints** | Mixed | Strong top-line growth and volume trends offset by a severe, one-off Q2 gross/EBITDA margin compression from high-cost RM inventory. |
| **JSW Dulux** | Mixed | Strong volume (21.8%) and revenue growth offset by margin headwinds from raw material inflation and lagging industrial price recovery. |

## Company one-liners
*   **Asian Paints:** Asian Paints delivered resilient H1 growth despite a turbulent inflationary environment, relying on strong industrial performance, new product innovation, and the commissioning of its VAE facility to protect margins.
*   **Berger Paints:** Berger Paints reported a margin-compressed Q2 due to double-digit RM inflation and ad spends, but expects a strong Q3 recovery driven by festive demand and price hikes.
*   **Indigo Paints:** Indigo Paints' Q2 FY27 profitability suffered a one-off shock from high-cost raw material inventory and unfavorable product mix, but management asserts the issue is resolved and expects strong margin recovery and volume momentum in H2.
*   **JSW Dulux:** JSW Dulux posted strong 21.8% volume growth and 21.3% revenue growth, though margins faced a 400 bps compression due to severe raw material inflation in the industrial segment.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Absorption and consumer reaction to cumulative price hikes (November/December tranches) implemented across the sector.
*   Pacing of gross and EBITDA margin recovery in H2 as high-cost raw material inventories fully clear out.
*   Ramp-up and utilization milestones for new capacity additions (Asian Paints' VAE plant, Indigo Paints' Jodhpur plant, and Berger's regional expansions).
*   Synergy realizations from JSW Dulux's Project Akshaya and the ongoing impact of competitive intensity and dealer rebating by new market entrants.

*Based on: Asian Paints Ltd, Berger Paints India Ltd, Indigo Paints Ltd, JSW Dulux Ltd*
