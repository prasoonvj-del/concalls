# JSW Dulux Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Paints & Coatings*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume and revenue growth, but gross and EBITDA margins faced headwinds due to raw material inflation and lagging industrial price recovery.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained intent to approach ~13% EBITDA margin, though noting it is tough to predict fully due to elevated crude ($105-$115) and forex volatility.
- Tone and demand view: Remained upbeat on volume momentum and demand buoyancy in October, but sounded more cautious on input-cost inflation impacts and the need to monitor how price hikes are absorbed.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New focus on antidumping duty (additional 2%) and specific mention of elevated crude levels reaching $114-$115; old discussion around initial headquarters relocation and specific headcounts disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Project Akshaya delivered Rs 7.3 crore in synergies in Q2 (vs Rs 2.4 crore in Q1); ERP migration is on track for December.

### Key numbers
- **Volume Growth:** 21.8% YoY
- **Revenue Growth:** 21.3% YoY
- **EBITDA:** Rs 110.9 crore (up 4.4% YoY like-to-like from Rs 106.3 crore)
- **PAT (excluding exceptional items):** Rs 71.2 crore (up 2% YoY from Rs 69.8 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.5%
- **Project Akshaya Synergies Realized:** Rs 7.3 crore in Q2

### Highlights
- Industrial business led revenue growth with over 32% growth; decorative saw mid-double-digit growth.
- Implemented price increases in 3 tranches (Sept 30, Oct 14, Oct 26) totaling ~3.2% to 3.5% additional in decorative, plus another ~4% on the industrial side.
- Project Akshaya unlocked Rs 7.3 crore in Q2 driven by supply chain efficiencies, cross-manufacturing (e.g., Mohali, Hyderabad, Vijayanagar), and unified projects business; ERP migration scheduled for December.
- Effective tax rate lowered to ~19% due to a favorable one-off income tax order for Assessment Year 2013-14.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to bring EBITDA margins closer to 13% despite elevated crude ($105-$115) and forex rates (~94.5-96).
- Expects Project Akshaya to deliver roughly 8% of overall EBITDA in full-year savings.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Decorative premium segment grew high single-digit in volume and double-digit in value; flagship brands (Velvet Touch, Weathershield) and adjacencies (waterproof, wood care) performed well.
- Industrial verticals (Marine & Protective, coil coating, packaging coatings) experienced high double-digit volume growth and market share gains.
- Demand remains buoyant in retail and projects with no slowdown witnessed in October.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended price increase averaged 7.9% for the quarter with an exit rate of 10.4% in September; mix was adverse by 8.4%.
- Gross margin dropped 400 bps, largely driven by the industrial business where raw material COGS increased 30%-35% against only 6%-7% price recovery.
- Working capital inventory elevated temporarily due to pre-buying titanium dioxide as a group.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Cross-manufacturing active across 5 JSW Dulux plants and 3 JSW Paints plants.
- Industrial capacity utilization reached close to 75% in certain verticals, driving selective capacity investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Lower investment income limited PAT growth to 2%.
- Working capital impacted by elevated inventory (titanium dioxide pre-buying) and higher industrial market credit.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated crude oil prices ($105-$115) and volatile forex (INR 96 peak).
- Lag in industrial price recovery relative to steep raw material inflation (COGS up 30-35%).
- Potential impact of cumulative price increases on demand if macroeconomic headwinds persist.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) on industrial coatings profitability:** Management explained that Marine & Protective, coil, and packaging coatings saw COGS rise 30%-35% with only a 6%-7% price increase, creating a margin lag. They are pursuing additional 4% price hikes and trimming low-margin accounts.
2. **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) on volume growth drivers and market share:** Management stated decorative volume grew ~20% (or 8% excluding lower-margin putty/texture), driven by undercoat recovery, premium segment growth, and adjacencies, while industrial gained share in vehicle refinish, coil, and packaging.
3. **Aniket Kamble (ICICI Securities) on rainfall deficiency impact:** Management noted it is too early to assess direct impacts as aggregate national deficiency is within range, and October demand remains buoyant.
4. **Umang Shah (Avendus PMS) on Project Akshaya savings trajectory:** Management stated full-year savings from Project Akshaya are targeted at roughly 8% of overall EBITDA, driven by cross-manufacturing, procurement synergies, and functional alignment.
5. **Abhishek Mathur (Systematix Group) on relative volume outperformance vs peers:** Management attributed the 21% volume growth to low base effects from price corrections last year, strong undercoat sales, and expansion in premium and adjacent categories.

### One-line takeaway
JSW Dulux posted strong 21.8% volume growth and 21.3% revenue growth, though margins faced a 400 bps compression due to severe raw material inflation in the industrial segment.
