# Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly revenue crossing Rs 1,000 crores, strong order inflows, and successful exit from NCLT.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 1,032 crore, up 135% YoY and 50% QoQ.
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (including other income):** Rs 133 crore, up 189% YoY.
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 12.9%, up 240 bps YoY.
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 108 crore against Rs 28 crore a year ago (3.9x).
- **Q2 FY27 EPS:** Rs 1.87.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,722 crore.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 218 crore.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 166 crore.
- **Order Inflow (H1 FY27):** Rs 2,217 crore (Book-to-bill of 1.29x).
- **Firm Order Book:** Rs 3,735 crore at quoted value (approx. Rs 4,300 crore at current market prices).
- **Net Worth:** Positive Rs 764 crore as of September 30, 2026 (compared to negative Rs 604 crore as of March 31, 2026).
- **Net Debt:** Net cash of Rs 572 crore as of September 30, 2026 (compared to net debt of Rs 527 crore).
- **Carry-forward Tax Losses:** Rs 1,432 crore as of March 31, 2026.

### Highlights
- Delivered Diamond Power's first Rs 1,000+ crore revenue quarter, driven by medium/extra-high voltage (MV/EHV) cables, AL59 conductors, and data centre cables.
- Newly commissioned copper cable line for data centre cables contributed Rs 240 crore (23% of Q2 revenue) in a single month (September).
- Successfully prepaid the resolution plan for Rs 501 crore cash consideration ahead of the September 2027 deadline, fully exiting the NCLT process.
- QIP funds raised Rs 1,614 crore in July; promoter loans completely repaid.
- Secured an 800 kV HVDC conductor order worth Rs 974 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Management stands by its revenue guidance of Rs 4,300 crore to Rs 4,500 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Expected to remain in the 11% to 13% band, expanding to 14% to 16% post polymer backward integration commissioning by December 2027.
- **Tax Outflow Guidance:** Management expects normal corporate tax payments to start from Q1 of FY29 due to carry-forward losses.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India is in its largest-ever transmission investment cycle.
- Renewable evacuation demand is strong, particularly from parks like Khavda; solar cables order book stands at Rs 221 crore.
- Underground cabling demand is expanding (e.g., Adani Electricity, PGVCL coastal underground schemes, GETCO Jamnagar 66 kV project).
- Data centre cable demand is robust across 66 cities with over 230 developers.
- Management is not bullish on HTLS re-conductoring, estimating it will not exceed 5% of top line, and does not manufacture ACSR conductors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee and other expenses declined to 5.7% of revenue from 8.2% a year ago.
- Other income stood at Rs 18 crore, comprising treasury income on QIP funds in fixed deposits.
- Metal and polymer costs vary by cable construction (metal/polymer account for ~35%-40% each in MV/EHV cables).
- Price variation clauses protect absolute contribution per ton/kilometer against LME metal price fluctuations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total planned capex of approx. Rs 366 crore across 6-7 projects focused on margin enhancement and backward integration.
- **Polymer Complex (Backward Integration):** Rs 170 crore investment for an 84,500 tons capacity polymer compound project. Phase 1 (6 compounding lines costing Rs 78 crore) to be commissioned progressively between June and December 2027. Phase 2 (Swiss reciprocating line for super-clean EHV XLPE compounds up to 400 kV, capacity 18,000 tons) to commission in early 2028.
- Copper cable capacity live at 1,500 tons/month, scaling to 2,500 tons/month.
- LV cable project to be commissioned before April 2027.
- Single-core cable project for solar infrastructure already on stream; planning a 1,000 km/month single-core 3.3 kV cable plant over next 18 months.
- Ordered a 6th CCV line for delivery within a year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth turned positive to Rs 764 crore from negative Rs 604 crore.
- Shifted from net debt of Rs 527 crore to net cash of Rs 572 crore.
- Finance cost of Rs 12 crore in Q2 is primarily LC discounting and bank charges on trade volumes.
- Legacy receivables of Rs 957 crore: management is positive on recovering Rs 300 crore over 24 months, with Rs 116 crore already confirmed by customers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Exposure to government/infrastructure sector spending cycles and geopolitical/crude price dynamics affecting new energy adoption.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishant Bagrecha (InCred Capital) on Data Centre sustainability:** Management stated that FY27 expects Rs 750 crore from data centres, and they are tracking opportunities across 66 cities and 230 developers.
2. **Vidit Trivedi (Asian Markets Securities) on Tax Shield:** Management clarified that carry-forward losses stood at Rs 1,432 crore as of March 31, 2026, and normal corporate tax will likely start in Q1 FY29.
3. **Achal Lohade (Nuvama Institutional Equities) on Margin/Revenue lag:** Management explained that price escalations operate with a 30-day time lag prior to dispatch, impacting percentage margins during metal price drops while absolute contribution per ton remains protected.
4. **Kriti Tripathi (NVS Brokerage) on Capacity Utilization:** Management stated capacity utilization is approximately 45% of what it will be post operationalization of balancing equipment.
5. **Ajit Sethi (Eiko Quantum Solutions) on HTLS vs. MV/EHV:** Management stated they are not bullish on HTLS (estimating max 5% of top line) and prefer underground MV/EHV cables.
6. **Sanjaya Satapathy (Ampersand Capital) on Gross Margins vs. Peers:** Management defended their 11%-13% EBITDA margin guidance, noting they do not spend 2%-3% on B2C advertising/branding, focusing instead on quality and competitive B2B pricing.

### One-line takeaway
Diamond Power reported its first Rs 1,000+ crore revenue quarter, successfully exited NCLT with a net cash balance sheet, and is scaling MV/EHV cables alongside backward integration into polymer compounding.
