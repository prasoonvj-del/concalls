# Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved first Rs 1,000+ crore revenue quarter with sharp PAT expansion, operational turn to net cash, and successful NCLT exit.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 1,032 crore (up 135% YoY, up 50% QoQ).
- **EBITDA (incl. Other Income):** Rs 133 crore (up 189% YoY); margin at 12.9% (up 240 bps YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 108 crore vs Rs 28 crore in Q2 FY26 (3.9x YoY).
- **EPS:** Rs 1.87 for Q2 FY27.
- **H1 FY27 Financials:** Revenue at Rs 1,722 crore; EBITDA at Rs 218 crore; PAT at Rs 166 crore.
- **Order Book:** Rs 3,735 crore at quoted value (approx. Rs 4,300 crore at current market prices under price variation clause) as of September 30, 2026.
- **Order Inflow:** Rs 2,217 crore in H1 FY27 (Book-to-bill of 1.29x).

### Highlights
- Passed the milestone of delivering 90% of last year’s full-year revenue and exceeding FY26 full-year PAT in H1 FY27 alone.
- Data centre cables from the newly commissioned copper line generated Rs 240 crore (23% of Q2 revenue) in a single month (September 2026).
- Product revenue split in Q2: Cables accounted for 40%, Conductors 31%, and Data Centre Cables 23%.
- Pass-through of inflated metal prices accounted for ~1/3rd of top-line growth, while real volume growth in dispatches was 60% to 70% YoY.
- Order book mix: 46% conductors (includes Rs 974 crore AL59 order for 800 kV HVDC line), 36% medium voltage (MV) cables, and balance in LT/data centre cables.
- Adani Group entities represented less than 30% of top-line in Q2 (43% of current order book), moving toward a target cap of 20% to 25%.
- Served ~670 customers in H1 FY27; established export teams and appointed a major distributor in the UAE/Middle East.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reiterated FY27 revenue guidance of Rs 4,300 crore to Rs 4,500 crore (implying Rs 2,580 crore in H2 FY27).
- Expects EBITDA margins to stay in the 11% to 13% band until backward-integrated polymer compounding comes stream by December 2027, after which margins are targeted at 14% to 16%.
- Target data centre segment sales of Rs 750 crore in FY27.
- Management expects export contributions to scale meaningfully in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by transmission grid buildout, renewable evacuation (e.g., Khavda park), disaster management, underground cabling, data centres, and industrial capex.
- Live inquiry pipeline stands at Rs 32,000 crore across medium voltage (MV) and extra high voltage (EHV) cables to be finalized across the industry over the next 90 days.
- Management views HTLS re-conductoring as a niche market (2% to 5% of company top-line target) and favors AL59 eco-conductors and underground MV/EHV cables over traditional ACSR conductors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Polymer and metal represent raw material costs: for MV/EHV cables, metal is 35-40% and polymer is 35%; for LV cables, metal is 45-50% and polymer is 20%.
- Operating leverage reduced employee and other expenses to 5.7% of revenue vs 8.2% YoY.
- Contribution margin moved to 16.9% from 18.7% due to higher LME metal price pass-through inflating the revenue base, while absolute contribution per ton remained protected.
- Received Rs 18 crore in other income from treasury yield on QIP FDs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total planned margin-enhancing capex of Rs 366 crore across 6–7 projects.
- Setting up a Rs 170 crore, 84,500-ton integrated polymer compounding plant (Phase 1: 66,500 tons costing Rs 78 crore commissioning progressively between June–Dec 2027; Phase 2: 18,000 tons super-clean peroxide XLPE line commissioning in early 2028). Management expects captive consumption to yield an additional Rs 120 crore in EBITDA (~3.5% margin boost).
- Scaling copper capacity from 1,500 tons/month toward 2,500 tons/month.
- Ordered 6th CCV line with delivery within one year.
- Setting up a 1,000 km/month single-core 3.3 kV solar cable plant to commission over the next 18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth improved to positive Rs 764 crore at Sep 30, 2026 (from negative Rs 604 crore at Mar 31, 2026).
- Shifted from net debt of Rs 527 crore to net cash of Rs 572 crore, aided by Rs 1,614 crore QIP proceeds in August 2026.
- Prepaid full NCLT resolution plan amount of Rs 501 crore in September 2026 (a year ahead of deadline), realizing a Rs 9.5 crore prepayment discount and completing NCLT exit.
- Finance cost of Rs 12 crore in Q2 mainly comprises LC discounting and trade bank charges.
- Accumulated carry-forward tax losses stand at Rs 1,432 crore as of March 31, 2026; normal corporate tax outgo is expected to start only by Q1 FY29.
- Target recovery of Rs 300 crore out of Rs 957 crore legacy receivables over a 24-month horizon (Rs 116 crore already confirmed by customers).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Standard geopolitical and macroeconomic risks associated with government-dominated power/infrastructure spend.
- Pass-through lag (30-day window) under price variation clauses causes short-term EBITDA margin percentage fluctuations of +/-1% during sharp commodity price shifts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Nishant Bagrecha (InCred Capital):** How do you address intensifying competition from established players in MV/EHV?  
  **A (Management):** First-mover advantage with 11 MV lines and upcoming captive EHV polymer compounding (adding 10% additional margin on polymer cost) will provide cost competitiveness that peers taking 2–3 years to build capacity cannot match easily.
- **Q: Vidit Trivedi (Asian Markets Securities):** What is the margin differential between data centre/conventional cables and tax outgo expectations?  
  **A (Management):** Data centre cables command ~2% extra margin due to strict pre-qualification entry barriers. Carry-forward losses of Rs 1,432 crore shield the company from income tax until Q1 FY29.
- **Q: Natasha Jain (PhillipCapital):** What is the plan for export pre-qualifications?  
  **A (Management):** Applications for UL approvals (US market) and type-testing samples (Europe market) are submitted. Dedicated teams expect to clear major export pre-qualifications before March 2027.
- **Q: Achal Lohade (Nuvama):** Why did EBITDA margin improve only 70 bps QoQ despite 135% revenue growth?  
  **A (Management):** Margins are protected on absolute rupee/ton basis under cost-plus price variation clauses. High metal prices inflate the revenue denominator, suppressing percentage margins temporarily.
- **Q: Ajit Sethi (Eiko Quantum):** What is the market size and outlook for HTLS re-conductoring?  
  **A (Management):** HTLS does not have a strong long-term future in India and will be limited to 2–5% of company top-line; management favors AL59 eco-conductors and underground MV/EHV cables.

### One-line takeaway
Diamond Power has completed its operational turnaround—exiting NCLT to become net cash with Rs 1,000+ crore quarterly revenue—and is scaling high-margin MV/EHV cables, data centre lines, and captive polymer compounding.
