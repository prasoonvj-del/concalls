# HPL Electric & Power Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue crossed Rs 600 crores, driven by strong Consumer & Industrial growth, though margins remain impacted by input cost volatility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained comfortable outlook; C&I segment expected to comfortably cross Rs 1,000 crores (already at Rs 615 crores in H1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted slightly more bullish on C&I volume growth and retail expansion, while acknowledging persistent supply chain and raw material cost pressures.
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared:** Board's in-principle approval for company restructuring/segregation was newly introduced. Wage hike mentions disappeared; geopolitical supply chain disruptions and freight costs remained.
- **Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered:** Cairo switches launched; retail expansion to 100,000 outlets targeted for March is on track (>90,000 currently achieved).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 608 crore, up 40% YoY (highest-ever quarterly revenue).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,124 crore, up 37% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 71 crore, up 8% YoY (H1 EBITDA at Rs 135 crore, up 9% YoY).
- **EBITDA margin:** 11.74% (vs 12.26% in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT:** Rs 23 crore.
- **Consumer and Industrial (C&I) revenue:** Rs 337 crore, up 64% YoY (~55% of quarterly revenue; H1 revenue at Rs 615 crore, up 60% YoY).
- **Wire and cables revenue:** Rs 178 crore, up 159% YoY (H1 revenue at Rs 324 crore).
- **Lighting revenue:** Up 59% YoY.
- **Domestic switchgear revenue:** Up 43% YoY.
- **Industrial switchgear revenue:** Up 29% QoQ.
- **Metering and systems revenue:** Rs 271 crore, up 18% YoY and 14% QoQ (H1 revenue at Rs 509 crore, up 17% YoY).
- **Order book:** Rs 2,700 crore as of November 2, 2026 (>96% from Metering and Systems).
- **Distribution network:** >900 authorized dealers and >90,000 retailers.
- **Capacity utilization:** Metering at ~70%; Wires and cables flexible manufacturing at ~80%.

### Highlights
- Crossed the Rs 600 crore quarterly revenue milestone for the first time.
- Board granted in-principle approval to evaluate company restructuring/segregation of businesses to unlock shareholder value.
- Added 7-8 new machines in wires and cables over the last 6 months with staggered orders following.
- Received first major export order from Africa with advance payment.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be better than H1 across both consumer and smart metering businesses.
- C&I revenue is guided to comfortably exceed Rs 1,000 crores for the full year.
- EBITDA margins expected to stabilize around 12% to 12.5% as price hikes are fully absorbed by Q3.
- Retail outlet count targeted to reach 100,000 by March.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Smart meter installation momentum ongoing; 7 crore meters installed nationally as of September, out of a 22.5 crore total target with 17 crore allocated to AMISPs.
- Adani's acquisition of IntelliSmart viewed positively as a major AMISP consolidation supporting preferred vendor HPL.
- Low-cost aggressive bidding by fringe players (e.g., Iskraemeco entity in Kerala tender) viewed by management as unsustainable over a 10-year horizon given technology and warranty risks.
- Data center and specialized cables R&D underway, with commercial production expected around end of Q1 (June).
- Water meter pilots ongoing, with meaningful numbers expected next year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost inflation driven by geopolitical disruptions in West Asia affecting metals, industrial plastics, and electronics.
- Metering contracts are fixed-price, preventing direct cost pass-through; R&D design and manufacturing changes used to offset costs.
- C&I price increases implemented in Q2 with further post-Diwali hikes lined up to offset lagging margin compression.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Metering capacity utilization at ~70%; cable capacity utilization at ~80% with 7-8 new machines added recently.
- Future capex plans for wire and cables product expansion over the next 3 years are under evaluation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Focus on tightening stock, debtors, and overall working capital ratios in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility affecting raw material prices (metals, plastics) and supply chain lead times.
- Time lag in passing on price hikes in C&I and inability to pass on costs in fixed-price smart meter contracts.
- Utility installation delays and pace of AMISP deployment schedules affecting order execution pacing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sahil Patani asked about margin normalization and data center cable R&D timelines.**
   - *Management reply:* Margins should stabilize around 12-12.5% as remaining price hikes take effect post-Diwali. Specialized cables for data centers, EVs, and infra will see commercial production around June, contributing meaningfully in FY28/FY29.
2. **Pranjal Mukhija asked about the impact of Adani acquiring IntelliSmart and sustainability of low-cost bidding (e.g., Kerala tender).**
   - *Management reply:* Adani acquiring IntelliSmart is positive as HPL is a preferred vendor. Low-cost disruptive pricing by new entrants is unsustainable given 10-year meter warranty requirements and high technical standards.
3. **Sahil Patani asked for details on the board's restructuring proposal.**
   - *Management reply:* It is an enabling provision allowing the board and teams to evaluate demerger options to bring focus and unlock value across distinct B2B and B2C platforms.

### One-line takeaway
HPL Electric posted its highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 608 crore (up 40% YoY) driven by robust 64% growth in Consumer & Industrial, though operating margins remained pressured by raw material inflation.
