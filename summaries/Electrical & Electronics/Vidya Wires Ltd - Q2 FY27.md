# Vidya Wires Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and profits grew YoY, ALCU ramp-up is progressing well, and focus is shifting to sales and execution.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated H1 Revenue:** Rs. 11,306.5 million (up 42.63% YoY from Rs. 7,927 million)
- **Consolidated Q2 Revenue:** Rs. 5,809.4 million (up 52.5% YoY from Rs. 3,809.4 million; up 5.68% QoQ)
- **Consolidated H1 EBITDA:** Rs. 491.8 million (against Rs. 382.64 million in H1 FY2026)
- **Consolidated Q2 EBITDA:** Rs. 239.55 million
- **Consolidated H1 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 319.7 million (against Rs. 225.3 million in H1 FY2026)
- **Consolidated Q2 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 148.4 million
- **Consolidated H1 EBITDA per kg:** Rs. 59 (up ~34% YoY from Rs. 44 per kg in H1 FY2026)
- **ALCU Revenue Contribution:** Rs. 60 crores in Q1, Rs. 110 crores in Q2 (approx. 18% to 20% of Q2 revenue)
- **ALCU Installed Capacity:** Around 10,000 to 11,000 metric tonnes currently installed out of the targeted 18,000 metric tonnes.

### Highlights
- Copper lines at the ALCU plant started production in February (within 8 months running at a healthy share of capacity with output rising monthly).
- Aluminium line testing ongoing, customer approvals in process; volumes expected to build up from the end of Q3.
- New ALCU products launched in February (EV, round ripple, aluminium paper-covered) are being supplied on regular orders; product range includes aluminium enameled wires/strips, paper-covered aluminium strips, PV ribbon for solar modules, and copper busbars and foils.
- Started exports from ALCU to Israel and Nepal; aiming for exports to make up 25% of revenue once expansion is complete.
- CTC (Continuously Transposed Conductor) plant is close to completion; trial production expected to begin by mid-December, contributing to revenue from Q4.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects capex to be largely complete by the end of Q3.
- Management expects EBITDA and EBITDA per kg to increase in absolute terms and per kg going forward (targeting around Rs. 60-65 per kg).
- FY2028 capacity will be 100% operational, and management expects better volume growth.
- Guidance status: Management declined to share specific percentage/tonnage numerical guidance but expressed a positive and optimistic outlook.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Renewable energy order scenario is very bullish; solar module growth is driving PV ribbon demand.
- Power segment (transformers) is doing well, driven by massive capacity expansions in the transformer industry.
- EV adoption is showing good growth, increasing demand for electricity and charging stations.
- Started receiving initial orders from data center applicators for copper busbars.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed cost expenses for the new plant have kicked in while volumes are still building, placing some pressure on Q2 EBITDA per tonne (softened to Rs. 48,000-49,000 per tonne from Rs. 52,000-53,000 in Q1).
- H1 consolidated EBITDA per kg stood at Rs. 59.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- IPO funds set aside for ALCU capex are being used as planned, with unspent amounts kept in fixed deposits.
- Out of 18,000 MTPA targeted ALCU capacity, 10,000 to 11,000 MT is installed; balance capacity will be added by January / mid-December.
- CTC plant completion expected by December-end (mid-December trial production).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Tax notice assessment is over, and management has filed for the next stage; the initial high demand has dropped significantly, and management is confident it will reduce to zero.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Teething operational issues at the new plant.
- Raw material price rises (copper prices) and Middle East geopolitical issues impacting operations and margins.
- A temporary slowdown/stagnancy in renewable energy installations previously due to transmission execution delays.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Adityapal Jaggi (MSA Capital Partners) asked about the divergence between rising EBITDA per kg (H1 at Rs. 59) and the P&L impact, and requested volume data in presentations.** Management explained that fixed costs kicked in before full volume ramp-up, and agreed to include volume data in future investor presentations.
- **Pranav Jain (Prabhudas Lilladher) asked about Q2 volume estimates (~4,300-4,500 tonnes) and the drop in EBITDA per ton QoQ.** Management stated that teething issues and fixed costs caused the dip, but expected margins and volumes to improve as sales take precedence.
- **Pranav Jain asked about CTC volume expectations and HVDC certification timelines.** Management replied that production starts mid-December for mid-size power transformers, and HVDC empanelment will take another 6 to 9 months based on performance and customer confidence.
- **Pragyam Laddha asked for volume guidance or capacity utilization numbers for FY2028.** Management declined to give explicit numbers but reiterated optimism for better performance as capacity becomes fully operational.
- **Achyut Bapat asked about the status of the Rs. 50 crore tax notice.** Management stated that the assessment is complete, the amount has dropped significantly, and they are confident of a favorable outcome.

### One-line takeaway
Vidya Wires is transitioning from installation to sales management at its ALCU facility, with improving EBITDA per kg and upcoming CTC production expected to drive better performance in H2 FY27 and FY28.
