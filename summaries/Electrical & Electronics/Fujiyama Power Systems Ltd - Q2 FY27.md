# Fujiyama Power Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-14 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and profits doubled YoY, driven by robust retail demand and capacity expansion, though management opted to keep full-year guidance unchanged pending further policy clarity.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained - management declined to revise full-year guidance upward this quarter despite strong H1 performance, preferring to wait for Q3 results and clarity on PM Surya Ghar 2.0 and ALMM List-II implementation.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to a more cautious/conservative stance on guidance due to regulatory and policy variables (ALMM List-II, PM Surya Ghar 2.0), while underlying retail demand remains strong.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New focus on compliance with MNRE (August 2026) and CEA cybersecurity regulations requiring Indian-hosted servers and data sovereignty for inverters; Bawal fire insurance claim expected to resolve this financial year.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The 2 GW power electronics facility at Ratlam was commissioned in August 2026 as promised; lithium-ion capacity upsized from 2 GWh to 3 GWh with commissioning expected next quarter.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 1,307 crore, up 130% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 2,652 crore (similar to full year FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 223 crore, up 116% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 478 crore, up 129% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17.1% in Q2 FY27 (vs 18.9% in Q1 FY27 and 18.1% in Q2 FY26); 18% for H1 FY27.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 133 crore, up 111% YoY; PAT margin of 10.2%.
- **H1 FY27 Normalized PAT (ex-fire provision):** Rs. 298 crore, up 128% YoY (margin of 11.2%).
- **Distribution Reach:** >10,200 channel partners across >25 states/UTs (added >100 distributors and >1,000 dealers in H1).
- **Service Footprint:** >1,000 on-roll service engineers (up from ~650 in March 2026).
- **Working Capital & Inventory:** Net working capital days fell to 59 days in H1 FY27 from 83 days in FY26; inventory days fell to 99 from 123; receivable days at 10; inventory at Rs. 1,230 crore (vs Rs. 910 crore in March).
- **Balance Sheet:** Total assets Rs. 2,907 crore; total equity Rs. 1,466 crore; total debt Rs. 851 crore; cash and cash equivalents Rs. 52 crore; net debt Rs. 800 crore; net debt-to-equity ratio 0.55x.

### Highlights
- Operating as a consumer household energy company rather than a pure solar module player, with >85% revenue from retail household and small businesses.
- Commissioned 2 GW power electronics facility at Ratlam in August 2026.
- Raised stake in Zayo Energy and Zayo Cables to 50% in Q1; backward integration planned for frames, ribbons, junction boxes, cables, and EVA sheets next fiscal year.
- Fully compliant with MNRE (August 2026) and CEA cybersecurity regulations mandating Indian servers and data sovereignty for inverter apps and data loggers.

### Guidance & outlook
- Full-year guidance maintained; management will review and provide updates during Q3 results once clarity emerges on PM Surya Ghar 2.0 and ALMM List-II.
- EBITDA margin expected to remain sustainable around 18% to 19%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana has covered >60 lakh households, installed >19 GW, and released >Rs. 36,000 crore in subsidies as of October 31, 2026 (~5 lakh households remain in current phase ending March 2027).
- PM Surya Ghar 2.0 under government consideration, with proposed support for battery storage (BESS), generation-linked incentives, and shared rooftop solar.
- India rooftop solar capacity grew from ~12 GW in March 2024 to ~26 GW in March 2026 (against estimated 67 GW potential, <5% penetration).
- Non-DCR solar module market is oversupplied, but DCR market faces cell shortages; company's panels are consumed captively or through its own retail channel.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- QoQ EBITDA margin decline (18.9% in Q1 to 17.1% in Q2) driven by seasonal softness, overheads from running Ratlam ahead of full volume, increased selling/distribution expenses, and higher freight due to geopolitical inflation.
- Margin impacted temporarily by external sourcing of tubular batteries post Bawal fire until Hathras plant commissions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Ratlam Facility:** Power electronics and panels running; lithium-ion capacity upsized from 2 GWh to 3 GWh (online next quarter, taking total Li-ion capacity to 3.5 GWh).
- **Hathras (UP):** New 1 GW tubular battery plant replacing Bawal fire capacity expected to commission next quarter.
- **TOPCon Cell:** 1.2 GW TOPCon solar cell plant at Ratlam on track for Q1 FY28 (pushing for earlier launch), taking total cell capacity to 2.2 GW (alongside 1 GW PERC).
- Evaluating ingot wafers ahead of ALMM List-III (June 2028), advanced chemistry batteries, and expanding BESS via 5 MW Dadri and 10 MW Ratlam pilots.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt increased to Rs. 851 crore (net debt Rs. 800 crore) primarily to fund working capital and Ratlam capex.
- Insurance claim filed for full carrying value of Bawal fire assets (Rs. 143 crore pre-tax); realization expected this financial year to provide cash inflow.
- Board considering QIP resolution for potential debt repayment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary margin dilution from running new unoptimized capacities (Ratlam) and external outsourcing of tubular batteries.
- High inventory buildup (Rs. 1,230 crore) due to business doubling and positioning for H2 and domestic cell transition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prithvi Raj (Unifi Capital):** Asked if H2 revenue growth will match H1 and whether full-year guidance will be beaten.  
  *Management:* Declined to revise guidance, citing variable factors like ALMM List-II implementation and PM Surya Ghar 2.0 clarity; will review at Q3 results.
- **Himanshu Bisani (Capital):** Asked why EBITDA margins (17%-19%) are lower than pure cell manufacturers (>30%) and how integration impacts this.  
  *Management:* Explained that B2C margins are more sustainable and less volatile than B2B; product mix includes lower-margin batteries and inverters alongside panels.
- **Shwetha:** Asked about the proportion of in-house vs. bought-in DCR cells used in H1.  
  *Management:* Around 70% to 80% was in-house, and 20% to 30% was sourced from outside.
- **Sagar Shah (Investor):** Asked about the sharp rise in other expenses and plans to reduce rising debt and inventory.  
  *Management:* Stated that other expenses doubled due to fixed overheads at Ratlam, brand marketing in Tier 2/3 cities, and higher freight; debt increased for Ratlam capex and working capital, which will be addressed via insurance recovery and potential QIP approval.

### One-line takeaway
Fujiyama doubled its revenue and profits YoY in H1 FY27 on the back of aggressive retail expansion and backward integration, but maintained its full-year guidance pending policy clarity.
