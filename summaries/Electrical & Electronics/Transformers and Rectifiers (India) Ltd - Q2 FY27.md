# Transformers and Rectifiers (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects better execution, improved capacity utilization, operating leverage, and a 32% YoY growth in the unexecuted order book.

### Key numbers
- **Unexecuted Order Book:** Rs 7,237 crore as of 30th September 2026 (up 32% YoY), executable over 18 to 24 months.
- **Order Inflows (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,169 crore (97% YoY growth).
- **Revenue from Operations (Standalone, Q2 FY27):** Rs 709 crore (66% YoY and 27% QoQ growth).
- **EBITDA (Standalone, Q2 FY27):** Rs 114 crore (203% YoY and 53% QoQ growth).
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 16.1% (vs 8.8% in Q2 FY26 and 13.3% in Q1 FY27).
- **Revenue from Operations (Standalone, H1 FY27):** Rs 1,268 crore (35% YoY growth).
- **EBITDA (Standalone, H1 FY27):** Rs 185 crore (47% YoY growth).
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 14.6% (vs 13.4% in H1 FY26).
- **Total Debt (Standalone, as of 30th Sep 2026):** Rs 575 crore (Long-term: Rs 125 crore, Short-term: Rs 449 crore).
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.35x.
- **Standalone Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 77 crore.
- **Standalone Inventory:** Rs 654 crore.
- **Trade Receivables:** Rs 1,053 crore (Current: Rs 825 crore).
- **Net Working Capital Days:** 154 days (Inventory days: ~93, Receivable days: ~117, Payable days: ~56).
- **Retention Money:** ~Rs 200 crore (5% to 10% range).
- **Changodar Expansion Capacity & Capex:** 35,000 MVA capacity; ~Rs 150 crore capex.
- **Average Realization:** ~Rs 7 lakh per MVA.
- **Export Contribution (Q2 FY27):** 8% of total revenue; ~15% of order book.
- **Production (Q2 FY27):** ~9,500 MVA.

### Highlights
- Secured first-ever nuclear power sector order from NPCIL for Kaiga Units 5 and 6 (generator transformers, 400 kV plus).
- Received large transformer orders from Megha Engineering and Infrastructures Limited, APTRANSCO, Damodar Valley Corporation, and GETCO.
- Successfully completed dynamic short-circuit testing on three transformers (400 kV and 220 kV auto-transformers and power transformers).
- Established a subsidiary in the UK in September 2026 to support and expand in UK and European markets.
- Inquiries under negotiation stand at Rs 21,000 crore (80% domestic, 20% export).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of achieving FY27 guidance and full-year growth plans (targeting 25% and beyond revenue growth, and 9% to 10% PAT margin).
- Management targets reducing working capital cycle days to below 120 days (aiming for 110 to 120 days) by the end of the financial year.
- USD 1 billion revenue target maintained for the late part of FY29 or early 2030.
- Changodar expansion commercial production expected to commence around Diwali.
- CTC backward integration plant expected to be commissioned around Diwali, with commercial manufacturing by Q4 and external sales from Q1 next year; full backward integration margin expansion estimated at 200 to 300 bps by FY28/FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying demand remains supportive due to investments in power generation, transmission infrastructure, renewable energy integration, and grid modernization.
- Emerging demand drivers include data centers, railway electrification, EV infrastructure, and energy transition projects.
- Company is registered as a regular supplier to large data centers in India and abroad.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (transformer oil, CRGO, copper) remain on the higher side, but price variation clauses in majority orders protect margins.
- Price rises in Q2 FY27 contributed roughly 7% to 8% to revenue growth.
- Other expenses remain in a similar range as a percentage of sales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Changodar manufacturing capacity is being expanded to 35,000 MVA.
- Moraiya plant capacity will increase by 30%+ once the insulation/pressboard plant goes live, followed by EHV transformer capacity additions.
- No additional funding or capex required beyond planned projects.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Leverage remains comfortable with a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35x.
- Unutilized QIP proceeds are earmarked for backward integration initiatives.
- Working capital days remain elevated at 154 days due to long manufacturing cycles, milestone-based billing, and testing requirements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Timely availability of certain critical raw materials remains an issue, managed through maintaining higher inventory levels until backward integration is complete.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harshit Sachdeva (Columbus Capital) on Kaiga order size and nuclear greenfield value:** Management stated greenfield nuclear transformer component cost is 15% to 18% of the plant cost; orders are repetitive and 400 kV plus.
2. **Mihir Vyas on free cash flow and working capital targets:** Management stated positive cash flows will emerge as working capital days are reduced to 110–120 days by the end of the financial year.
3. **Deepak Poddar on backward integration margin impact:** Management confirmed 200 to 300 bps margin expansion driven by backward integration, fully realized by FY29.
4. **Jainam on PGCIL order timeline and capacity utilization:** Management stated execution timeline for the PGCIL order is 24 to 28 months, starting manufacturing in December next year, and aiming for ~90% capacity utilization.
5. **Abhijeet Singh on HVDC converter transformer qualification:** Management stated the PGCIL repair process will take 4 to 5 months, followed by 6 to 7 months of running/testing before commercial qualification (1.5 years away).

### One-line takeaway
TARIL delivered strong Q2 FY27 results with a 66% YoY revenue jump and 203% EBITDA growth, backed by a robust Rs 7,237 crore order book and upcoming capacity expansions.
