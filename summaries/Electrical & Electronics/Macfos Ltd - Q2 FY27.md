# Macfos Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in sustaining growth momentum, citing strong demand in the electronics ecosystem and successful execution of both distribution (1.0) and in-house product (2.0) strategies.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 181.96 crore (+41% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 21.91 crore (+34% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 13.18 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **Return/Replacement cost:** Rs 1.7 crore (H1).

### Highlights
*   Growth driven by increased traction from corporate clients and healthy repeat purchases.
*   Robu 2.0 (in-house brands) gaining momentum, particularly in the drone ecosystem.
*   Product portfolio expansion: SmartElex (310 SKUs), Pro-Range (thousands of SKUs), and SimplyFly (14 drone frames).
*   Management stated that return/replacement costs were inflated by an operational backlog of Rs 35-40 lakh, which is expected to normalize next quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to continue the current growth path for the next 4-5 years, supported by the "Make in India" initiative and a growing startup culture.
*   No immediate plans for equity dilution; management intends to continue funding growth via bank debt.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains encouraging across both corporate and hobbyist segments.
*   Management believes the market is growing due to the evolution of new technologies (e.g., drones, 3D printers) rather than just taking market share from competitors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Inventory days are currently ~120 days; management expects this to decrease in the near future.
*   Shift toward importing via sea to save costs has contributed to higher inventory days.
*   Management targets PAT margins of 7-8%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Continued investment in warehousing, IT infrastructure, and marketing to support growth.
*   Management declined to share specific expansion plans or location strategies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Working capital requirements are funded through existing bank relationships.
*   Management continues to negotiate for better credit terms and limits with suppliers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Inventory days are on the higher side (120 days).
*   Return/replacement costs increased due to an operational backlog.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why are inventory days high?** A: Management attributed it to strategic decisions (importing via sea vs. air) and noted it should normalize.
*   **Q: Will you dilute equity?** A: Management stated equity is the "costliest money" and they prefer bank debt for growth.
*   **Q: Are you taking market share?** A: Management believes the overall electronics market is expanding, creating new demand rather than just shifting share.
*   **Q: What is the revenue contribution of Robu 2.0?** A: Management declined to share specific numbers, citing a desire to focus on product quality and market adoption first.
*   **Q: Do you have return clauses for corporate clients?** A: No, corporate clients are generally bound to keep products unless they are faulty.

### One-line takeaway
Macfos delivered strong H1 growth and remains focused on scaling its dual-engine model (distribution + in-house manufacturing) using internal cash flows and debt, while maintaining a positive long-term outlook.
