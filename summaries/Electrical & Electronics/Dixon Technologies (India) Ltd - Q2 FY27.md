# Dixon Technologies (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth (~47% YoY) driven by IT hardware expansion and memory price pass-through, offset by margin percentage compression due to mobile PLI 1 expiration, raw material price hikes, and pricing pass-through lags in ODM appliances.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 21,806 crore (up ~47% YoY)
- **EBITDA (ex-fair value gain):** Rs 609 crore
- **PAT (post-minority interest, ex-fair value gain):** Rs 293 crore
- **Mobile & EMS Segment:** Revenue Rs 20,093 crore; Operating Profit Rs 509 crore
- **IT Hardware Segment:** Revenue Rs 2,160 crore
- **Telecom & Networking Segment:** Revenue Rs 1,837 crore
- **Home Appliances (Washing Machines):** Revenue Rs 586 crore; Operating Profit Rs 48 crore
- **Consumer Electronics (LED TVs & Refrigerators):** Revenue Rs 1,115 crore; Operating Profit Rs 40 crore

### Highlights
- Mobile manufacturing volumes remained stable YoY at ~9.7 million units in Q2 FY27 (~17.2 million units in H1 FY27), despite a 26% YoY contraction in India's overall smartphone market.
- IT Hardware revenue scaled to Rs 2,160 crore, leading Dixon to transition from the hybrid category (Rs 250 crore capex) to the global category (Rs 500 crore capex commitment) under IT PLI 2.
- Approved under the ECMS scheme, Dixon established a JV with Gemtek (Taiwan) to manufacture optical transceivers (SFPs) and BOSAs, with trials commencing in Q4 FY27.
- Washing machine volume grew 27% YoY in semi-automatic (6.3 lakh units) and 24% YoY in fully automatic top-loading (1.3 lakh units). Dixon’s annual washing machine capacity stands at 3.9 million units (~33% of total domestic market demand).
- Q Tech (51% subsidiary) expanded camera module capacity from 80 million to 120 million units annually, with further expansion planned to 180–190 million units by next fiscal.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Mobile Volumes:** Full-year FY27 volume targeted at 33–34 million units (ex-Vivo). Vivo JV expected to add ~18–20 million units in FY28.
- **Exports:** Motorola export volumes to ramp up meaningfully from Q4 FY27; Transsion feature phone and smartphone exports to start in FY28.
- **IT Hardware:** Expected to deliver Rs 6,000–6,500 crore in revenue in FY27 (1.3–1.4 million units) and scale to Rs 15,000–20,000 crore (~3.5–4 million units) over the next two years.
- **Display Modules (HKC JV):** Auto and notebook display trials in Q3 FY27, mobile display trials in Q4 FY27, and commercial mass production starting in FY28.
- **Quarterly Volumes:** Q3 FY27 mobile volume expected at 7–7.5 million units; Q4 FY27 expected at ~9.5 million units.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic smartphone demand shrank 26% YoY in Q2 FY27; Dixon maintained volume run-rates via market share gains and customer additions.
- Rapid global expansion of AI infrastructure elevated memory component prices, increasing mobile and IT hardware selling prices by 35–40% YoY.
- Low-to-mid range LED TV demand remains soft due to input inflation, while demand for large screens (50"+), QLED, and OLED models remains steady.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins were impacted by the expiration of the Mobile PLI 1 scheme, elevated global memory prices, polymer cost inflation, FX volatility, and higher freight costs.
- Cost pass-through is automatic and immediate in pure EMS segments (Mobile, IT Hardware, Telecom), but has a time lag in ODM products (Appliances, Lighting, TV).
- Washing Machine EBITDA margin compressed from 10.5%–11.0% to 8.3% due to polymer inflation and pricing pass-through lag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex:** Incurred ~Rs 700 crore in H1 FY27; full-year FY27 capex guided at ~Rs 1,200 crore. FY28 capex guided at Rs 1,200–1,300 crore.
- Construction of a 1 million sq. ft. facility in Noida for Vivo manufacturing is nearly complete, with operations starting in Q3 FY27.
- Total planned investment in display module manufacturing is ~Rs 1,000 crore over 3 years (Rs 300–350 crore in FY27).
- Refrigerator capacity is expanding by 375,000 sq. ft. to manufacture deep freezers, visi-coolers, and multi-door frost-free refrigerators.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash burn stood at Rs 280 crore in H1 FY27, driven by strategic inventory stocking of memory components and pending PLI receivables.
- Management expects 80–85% of the blocked working capital to be released by March 2027.
- Ongoing capex programs are entirely funded via internal cash accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Persistent elevated memory prices diluting percentage margins across EMS verticals.
- Time lag in passing through raw material and freight cost increases in ODM appliances.
- Soft demand environment in mass-market LED TVs and direct-cool refrigerators.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Siddhartha Bera (Nomura):** *What is the mobile volume outlook for H2 and the progress on exports?*
  **Management:** Expecting ~33–34 million units for FY27 (ex-Vivo). Motorola exports ramp up from Q4 FY27; Transsion feature phone exports start next fiscal. Vivo JV is expected to contribute 20–21 million units in FY28.
- **Sameet Sinha (Macquarie):** *What caused the margin compression and how does cost pass-through work?*
  **Management:** EMS businesses have automatic pass-through with zero lag. ODM businesses (Appliances, Lighting) have a lag, causing Washing Machine EBITDA margins to compress from ~11% to 8.3%.
- **Renu (IIFL):** *What is the execution roadmap for IT Hardware under the upgraded PLI commitment?*
  **Management:** Upgraded capex commitment from Rs 250 crore (hybrid) to Rs 500 crore (global). FY27 revenue will reach Rs 6,000–6,500 crore and target Rs 15,000–20,000 crore in 2 years based on order books from 4 of the top 5 global brands.
- **Vipraw Srivastava (PhillipCapital):** *Why did Q Tech margins decline and what drove the H1 working capital burn?*
  **Management:** Q Tech margins dropped due to FX pass-through issues (now resolved with clients) and rapid capacity expansion; margins will normalize to 6–7%. H1 cash burn of Rs 280 crore was due to strategic memory inventory and PLI receivables, 80–85% of which will unlock by March 2027.
- **Keyur Pandya (ICICI Pru):** *What is the potential revenue and capex scale for display modules?*
  **Management:** Total capex of ~Rs 1,000 crore over 3 years can generate Rs 6,000–7,000 crore in peak annual revenue, largely serving captive component demand for laptops, auto, and mobile.

### One-line takeaway
Dixon is aggressively expanding into IT hardware, display modules, and optical components to deepen localization and drive top-line scale, while navigating temporary input-cost margin dilution.
