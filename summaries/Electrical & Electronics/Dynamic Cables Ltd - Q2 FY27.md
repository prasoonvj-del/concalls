# Dynamic Cables Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 revenue growth, record-low receivable days, and a healthy order book supported by robust power T&D demand, though tempered by near-term export headwinds and commodity volatility.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 712 crore (up 31% YoY from INR 543 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Operating Profit (EBITDA):** INR 74.3 crore (up 29% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 10.44%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 47.1 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **Customer-wise Revenue Mix (H1 FY27):** Government 23%, Private 69%, Export 8%
- **Receivable Days:** Less than 60 days on cum GST sales as of September 30, 2026
- **Order Book:** Approximately INR 829 crore
- **Q2 FY27 Volume Growth:** Around 6% YoY
- **New Facility Investment:** INR 40–45 crore with a revenue potential of INR 240–250 crore at optimal utilization (6x-7x asset turn)

### Highlights
- Installed plant and machinery at the new manufacturing facility, with commercial contribution expected from H2 FY27.
- Receivable days reached a record low of under 60 days on cum GST sales.
- Long-term revenue growth guidance maintained at around 20% CAGR.
- Operating margins (EBITDA %) guided to remain in the 10% to 11% range.
- Product sales mix in Q2 FY27: High tension cables 65%, Low tension cables 28%, Conductors and other products 7%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a long-term overall sales growth guidance of around 20% CAGR.
- Management maintains EBITDA margin guidance of 10% to 11%.
- Finance cost is guided to remain below 15% of EBITDA.
- New facility is expected to ramp up over the next 3 to 4 quarters, delivering an estimated INR 240–250 crore at optimal utilization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Power infrastructure sector demand remains strong and encouraging, driven by T&D investments, renewable energy, and industrial development.
- The market is value-driven rather than volume-driven, with unlimited latent demand in power T&D infrastructure.
- Volume demand typically offsets price volatility (higher prices mute volume demand; lower prices increase it).
- Export markets offer huge long-term potential, though near-term momentum is impacted by transient geopolitical and logistical headwinds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 80% to 90% of contracts feature price variation clauses, protecting margins against commodity volatility; fixed-price contracts represent less than 20%.
- Gross margins fluctuated due to changes in product/revenue mix and sharp commodity price volatility.
- Aluminum is the primary metal used (not copper); recycled aluminum is not used in cable manufacturing.
- Finance costs are levied on average working capital utilization and vendor financing, targeted to stay below 15% of EBITDA.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total investment in the new plant is INR 40–45 crore; expected to begin contributing in H2 FY27.
- Long-term asset turnover averages 6x-7x capex investment. Gross block as of March 2026 was approximately INR 184 crore, putting total capacity potential at INR 1,400–1,500 crore before the new plant.
- Expansion (brownfield/greenfield) will remain an ongoing, inevitable requirement.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains a long-term debt-free / term-debt-free company; borrowings are restricted to working capital.
- H1/September inventory levels are seasonally higher to prepare for H2-heavy sales, compounded by higher raw material prices.
- Top 10 customers contribute 40%–45% of sales, with 70%–80% of sales coming from repeat customers annually.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions, high sea freights, lack of insurance covers, high insurance premiums, and tariff fears impacting export shipments (especially to the US and Middle East).
- Short-term commodity price volatility affecting quarterly gross margins and order booking momentum.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vidit Trivedi (Asian Market Securities) on finance cost increase despite lower debt:** Management clarified that finance cost depends on average working capital utilization and vendor financing levies, reiterating the target to keep it below 15% of EBITDA.
2. **Sangeeta Purushottam (Fund Manager) on volume vs. price growth and gross margin drop:** Management explained that the cable business is value-driven, absolute growth should be looked at via EBITDA percentage (10%-11%), and gross margins vary due to changing product/revenue mixes.
3. **Kaustav Bubna (Individual/Fund Manager) on gross margin decline from 20%+ to 16%-17%:** Management stated the drop was driven by revenue mix variations across orders executed in the quarter and minor fixed-price contract impacts.
4. **Nikhil Purohit (Fident Asset Management) on product mix and EBITDA margin sustainability:** Management confirmed Q2 product mix was 65% HT, 28% LT, 7% conductors, and expressed confidence in maintaining the 10%-11% EBITDA margin band barring deep macro instability.
5. **Hoshang (Analyst) on inventory increase and new product segments (solar/data center cables):** Management stated inventory is higher due to H2 seasonality and higher raw material prices; solar cable sales engagement has begun, while data center cables remain under product development.

### One-line takeaway
Dynamic Cables reported strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 31% YoY to INR 712 crore and launched its new manufacturing facility, maintaining its long-term 20% growth and 10%-11% EBITDA margin guidance despite near-term export headwinds.
