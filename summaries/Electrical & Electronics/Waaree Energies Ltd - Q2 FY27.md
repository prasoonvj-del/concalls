# Waaree Energies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27

**Tone:** Positive - Management reaffirmed strong growth momentum, robust retail/US execution, and maintained its full-year EBITDA guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs. 7,517.7 crore, up 23.9% YoY.
- **Q2 Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 1,335.2 crore; margin at 17.8%.
- **Q2 PAT:** Rs. 753.6 crore.
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs. 15,449.5 crore, up 47.3% YoY.
- **H1 Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 2,775.1 crore, up 15.5% YoY; margin at 18%.
- **H1 PAT:** Rs. 1,645.5 crore compared to Rs. 1,651.1 crore in H1 FY26.
- **Order Book:** ~Rs. 59,200 crore (25.6 GW module order book) as of October 26, 2026.
- **ROCE / ROE:** ROCE stood at 28.7% and ROE at 24.2%.

### Highlights
- Retail business revenue reached Rs. 5,753 crore in H1 FY27 (37% of H1 total revenue), surpassing the total retail revenue of Rs. 5,515 crore for full FY26.
- Retail channel footprint expands to 775+ exclusive retail centers and 3,000+ distribution points.
- Domestic US manufacturing recorded H1 FY27 revenue growth of 113% YoY, reaching Rs. 1,300 crore in Q2 FY27 compared to Rs. 732 crore in Q2 FY26.
- India export revenue in Q2 dropped to Rs. 89 crore (vs. Rs. 2,143 crore last year) due to US Section 232 implementation.
- Sales mix in Q2: Retail 46.1%, Utility/IPP/C&I 22.9%, Overseas 18.5%, EPC/O&M/T&D 12.6%.
- H1 module production stood at 6.2 GW; cell production was 1.9 GW.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management affirmed operating EBITDA guidance of Rs. 7,000 crore to Rs. 7,700 crore for FY27.
- Expects H2 FY27 to contribute 55% to 60% of total FY27 EBITDA guidance.
- Retail segment revenue projected to reach Rs. 10,000 crore to Rs. 11,000 crore in FY27.
- Targets Rs. 90,000 crore to Rs. 1,000,000 crore top-line in 4–5 years, with ~50% expected from non-core module segments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India installed 35.3 GW solar in the first 9 months of CY26 and 20.8 GW in H1 FY27 (vs 38 GW in CY25). US installed 19.2 GW in H1 CY26 (vs 43 GW in CY25).
- Annual India demand run-rate is around 55–60 GW, with rooftop solar seeing acceleration via PM Surya Ghar Yojana and government mandates.
- BESS, data centers, green hydrogen, and green ammonia are emerging as primary new demand drivers.
- Section 232 implementation sets predictable pricing baselines in the US market: module MIP at 38 cents + 15% duty; cells at 22 cents + 15% duty.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 margin included Rs. 11 crore tariff refund adjustment (compared to Rs. 349 crore refund recognized in Q1 FY27).
- Operational EBITDA per watt peak for US local manufacturing ranges between 8–9 cents (including 7 cents IRA benefit) versus 3–4 cents for India exports.
- DCR spot prices in India range from 19–21 cents (utility) to 22–24 cents (retail); non-DCR utility prices stand at 12–12.5 cents.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex program is ~Rs. 30,000 crore (35% deployed, 28% under execution, 37% in planning).
- **Module Capacity:** Current installed capacity of 25.8 GW (including 1.6 GW in US). Adding 3.2 GW in the US (4.8 GW total US capacity planned); trial production for 2 lines by March 2027 (1.6 GW operational in Q3 FY27).
- **Cell Capacity:** Current installed capacity of 5.4 GW. Adding 10 GW at Unn facility in FY27 (5 GW in Q3 FY27, 5 GW in Q4 FY27), increasing cell capacity to 15.4 GW.
- **Ingot & Wafer:** 10 GW Nagpur facility civil work progressing; commercial production targeted by September 2027 (Capex: Rs. 5,500 crore).
- **BESS:** Phase 1 commissioning of 5.1 GWh pack/containerization in Q3 FY27 and 3.5 GWh battery cell pilot in Q4 FY27.
- **Inverters, Transformers & Electrolyzers:** Commenced 4 GW inverter production and existing transformer lines; Rs. 1,200 crore capex allocated across these three verticals with target revenue potential of Rs. 7,000–8,000 crore.
- **Solar Glass Facility:** Spent Rs. 360 crore of planned Rs. 3,900 crore capex on land near Navsari, Gujarat; live date expected in FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Remains net debt-free with net cash of ~Rs. 3,224 crore and net debt-to-equity ratio of -0.2x.
- Cash generated from operations surpassed 5x YoY in H1 FY27.
- Substantial increase in advances from customers (reflected under other current liabilities) for both Indian and US utility orders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- US Section 232 policy led to ~3 GW of export orders being de-risked and removed from reported order book figures pending contract renegotiations to MIP terms.
- Temporary drop in domestic module capacity utilization (<50%) in Q2 FY27 due to supply chain realignment following ALMM II policy timeline extensions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** What caused the order book export component to drop from 24% to 7.8%?  
  **A:** Management preemptively removed ~3 GW of India-to-US export orders from reported figures due to Section 232 implementation, renegotiating them under the new Minimum Import Price rules.
- **Q:** What is the margin profile difference between US exports and US local manufacturing?  
  **A:** Exporting modules from India yields 3–4 cents/watt peak EBITDA. Manufacturing in the US generates 2–3 cents operating margin plus 7 cents IRA cash incentive, totaling 8–9 cents/watt peak.
- **Q:** Are there supply disruption risks for cell and wafer machinery from China policy restrictions?  
  **A:** Management confirmed equipment contracts for cells, battery, and ingot-wafer facilities are secured, avoiding timeline disruptions.
- **Q:** How will the Data Center strategy impact capital allocation?  
  **A:** Management stated Data Center renewable infrastructure falls within the existing committed Rs. 3,250 crore RE power infrastructure budget, serving as a demand pipeline generator without building hold-to-maturity assets.

### One-line takeaway
Waaree Energies is leveraging aggressive backward integration (cells, wafers) and rapid US local expansion to offset export policy shifts while expanding high-margin domestic retail channels.
