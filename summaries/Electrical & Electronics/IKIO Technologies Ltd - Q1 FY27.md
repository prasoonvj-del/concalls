# IKIO Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and EBITDA grew strongly YoY, driven by diversification into new verticals and international markets, despite geopolitical and supply chain headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 169 crore, up 41% YoY.
- **Other Business Revenue:** Rs. 124 crore, up 53% YoY (54% CAGR over FY23–FY26).
- **Home Lighting ODM Revenue:** Rs. 45 crore, up 16% YoY and 18% QoQ.
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 22 crore, up 94% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 13%, up from 9.4% in Q1 of last year.
- **PAT:** Rs. 11 crore, up from Rs. 2 crore YoY.
- **Hearable & Wearable Segment Contribution:** Approximately 15% to 18% of the topline.

### Highlights
- Revenue mix continues to shift successfully away from traditional home lighting ODM towards a diversified portfolio (Lighting, Hearables & Wearables, Energy Solutions, Electronic Components, Automotive Lighting).
- Global footprint expanded to over 20 countries, reducing single-geography dependence.
- Block 1 (approx. 2 lakh sq. ft.) is fully operational; Block 2 has been partially commercialized in Q2 FY27; Block 3 construction is progressing.
- Company holds over 3,000 SKUs supported by an in-house R&D and product design team.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY revenue growth guidance at 18% to 20% due to ongoing market volatility and geopolitical issues, noting they will review it by the end of Q2 or Q3.
- Full-year home lighting ODM revenue is expected to be broadly around Rs. 170 crore (give or take an uptick of Rs. 10–15 crore).
- Expected steady-state EBITDA margin at peak utilization is 17% to 18%.
- Target to reach full capacity utilization within the next 3 to 3.5 years if global conditions remain stable.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 is seasonally the leanest quarter for the company.
- Global trade tensions and war-led supply chain disruptions caused increased lead times and raw material price volatility, leading to an estimated revenue miss of Rs. 10–15 crore in the Middle East/Gulf region.
- Hearable and wearable segment has transitioned from basic job work to 50% ODM products.
- Automotive lighting has started production (May/June) focusing on tier-1 aftermarket brands, with OEM onboarding planned for Phase-2 by FY28.
- Honeywell association is expanding; SKUs produced are expected to multiply 3x to 4x by the end of the year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined to 40%–41% (from 44%–45%) due to war-led supply chain disruptions, rising metal prices (MS, aluminum, copper), and higher semiconductor prices/lead times.
- Employee expenses increased due to revisions in minimum labor wages.
- ODM EBITDA margin stood at 8.4% for the quarter; Other businesses delivered approximately 15% EBITDA margin.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Remaining capex for the year is estimated around Rs. 20–25 crore.
- Tower 2 has been partially commercialized in Q2 FY27, with two floors utilized for the hearables and wearables segment.
- Tower 3 construction is progressing as planned.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts and war scenarios causing severe raw material volatility (metals, semiconductors) and extended lead times (up to 5x–6x).
- Spot buying of electronic components required to maintain deliveries, impacting gross margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mayank Agarwal (Scientific Investing) asked about the margin trajectory and peak margins.** Management stated that practical peak EBITDA margins are targeted at 17%–18% due to a diversified product mix.
2. **Harsh Pingle asked about the dip in gross margins.** Management explained that war-induced raw material spikes (metals and semiconductors) and long lead times forced spot buying, impacting gross margins.
3. **Ankur Gulati asked about segment-wise EBITDA margins.** Management clarified that ODM EBITDA margin was 8.4% for the quarter, while the other businesses segment delivered around 15%.
4. **Ankur Gulati asked about production visibility for Hearables & Wearables.** Management answered that it constitutes 15%–18% of the topline with 3- to 6-month visibility based on customer schedules.
5. **Ankur Gulati asked about automotive lighting strategy.** Management explained that current production is for tier-1 aftermarket brands, with OEM entry targeted for FY28.

### One-line takeaway
IKIO Technologies is successfully executing its revenue diversification strategy across new verticals and geographies, though near-term profitability faces headwinds from geopolitical supply chain disruptions and input cost inflation.
