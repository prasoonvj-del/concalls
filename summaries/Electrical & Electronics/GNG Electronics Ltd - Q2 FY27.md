# GNG Electronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the "best ever quarter" with strong revenue growth and margin expansion, leading to an upward revision of full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 624.8 crore (up 42% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,037 crore (up 38% YoY).
*   **Q2 Gross Margin:** 25.1% (up 515 bps YoY).
*   **H1 Gross Margin:** 24.9% (up 438 bps YoY).
*   **Q2 Volume:** 2,25,000 units.
*   **H1 Volume:** 3,74,000 units.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 427 crore.

### Highlights
*   Management stated the growth is broad-based across India (45% YoY) and international markets (40% YoY).
*   The company is de-commoditizing the refurbished space by selling under the "Electronics Bazaar" brand.
*   Operational reach: Present in 55 countries (up from 49 in Q1), with 5,542 customer touchpoints and 820 suppliers.
*   Headcount increased to 2,811 employees.
*   Marketing campaign launched with influencer "Technical Guruji."

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management raised FY27 growth guidance to 40% (previously 30%).
*   **Profitability:** Management raised PAT growth guidance to 150 bps expansion over the previous year (previously 100 bps).
*   **Expansion:** Management plans to enter the Asia Pacific region, including Singapore, Vietnam, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and ASEAN countries.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management cited IDC data showing a 20.1% YoY decline in global PC shipments for the September quarter.
*   Management noted that new laptop prices have risen significantly (from Rs 35,000–40,000 to Rs 65,000–72,000), increasing the value proposition for refurbished devices.
*   Management expects memory and storage supply constraints to persist through 2027–2028, citing commentary from Micron and SK Hynix.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Gross margin expansion is attributed to brand positioning and a wider price gap between new and refurbished products.
*   Inventory gains contributed 125–150 bps to margin expansion in Q2.
*   Employee costs are rising due to strategic hiring in engineering, sales, and leadership roles to support future growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Capacity utilization is currently in the 40%–50% range.
*   Capacity has been expanded in India and UAE (8 facilities).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net debt increased to Rs 427 crore (from Rs 406 crore in Q1). Management expects debt to remain at similar levels for the full year.
*   Receivables are in the 40–45 day range.
*   Inventory remains elevated due to strategic bulk procurement; management expects normalization to 90 days of sales in approximately 2 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Higher tax rate: Management expects an effective tax rate of 14%–16% for FY27 (vs 10.6% last year).
*   Supply constraints: Potential for continued volatility in component availability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Management stated margins are sustainable and will grow because the price reset in the industry is permanent, and the brand is gaining pricing power.
*   **Inventory Strategy:** Management clarified that elevated inventory is a strategic decision to capitalize on supply shortages and will likely continue for the next 2 quarters.
*   **Debt Levels:** Management indicated that current debt levels are comfortable and will be funded primarily through accruals.
*   **Employee Cost:** Management explained that hiring is "ahead of time" to build leadership and engineering capabilities for future growth.
*   **Impact of Memory Price Drop:** Management argued that even if memory prices crash, the business has a "cushion" because they buy ahead of the curve and have significant room to adjust pricing relative to new laptops.

### One-line takeaway
GNG Electronics delivered a strong performance driven by structural demand for refurbished PCs and is aggressively scaling its brand and global footprint, supported by raised full-year guidance.
