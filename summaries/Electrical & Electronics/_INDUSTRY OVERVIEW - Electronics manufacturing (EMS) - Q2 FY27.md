# Electrical & Electronics - Electronics manufacturing (EMS): what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The EMS sector is experiencing a period of aggressive top-line expansion, driven by structural tailwinds in AI infrastructure, IT hardware, and localization (PLI schemes). However, the narrative is bifurcated: while companies like **Netweb Technologies** and **Aditya Infotech** are reporting record-breaking growth and raising guidance, others are navigating margin headwinds caused by input cost inflation (specifically memory and SOC components) and the temporary margin dilution associated with strategic investments or product mix shifts. A common theme is the transition from pure-play assembly to higher-value-add manufacturing (PCB, OSAT, display modules).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board report robust demand. **Netweb Technologies** (AI/HPC), **Syrma SGS** (Auto/Industrial), and **Dixon Technologies** (IT Hardware) highlight strong order books. **Highness Microelectronics** and **Virtuoso Optoelectronics** note that demand for specialized displays and appliances remains strong, with early seasonal kick-offs.
*   **Divergence:** There is a clear divergence in strategy regarding specific segments. **Kaynes Technology** has intentionally cut smart meter sales by 80% to prioritize balance sheet health and de-risk receivables, contrasting with the aggressive growth strategies seen in AI and IT hardware segments. **GNG Electronics** is capitalizing on a specific niche—refurbished PCs—arguing that rising new laptop prices are driving structural demand for their offerings.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Input Inflation:** A consensus exists regarding the sharp escalation in semiconductor component prices (SOC, DDR, flash memory). **Aditya Infotech**, **Dixon**, **GNG**, and **Netweb** all flagged elevated memory prices. **Syrma SGS** and **Kaynes** noted that supply chain constraints and lead-time extensions are creating cost-push effects.
*   **Margin Pressures:** Managements are dealing with margin compression from three distinct sources:
    *   **Pass-through lags:** **Dixon** noted that while EMS segments have automatic pass-through, ODM products (Appliances) suffer from time lags.
    *   **Strategic Investments:** **Cyient DLM** and **Kaynes** are absorbing upfront costs for new facilities (OSAT/PCB) and sales capabilities, which are depressing near-term margins.
    *   **PLI/Mix Shifts:** **Dixon** cited the expiration of Mobile PLI 1 as a factor in margin dilution.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is in a massive capital deployment phase to deepen localization.
*   **PCB/OSAT:** **Kaynes** operationalized OSAT/PCB units in Q2; **Syrma SGS** is investing Rs 800–900 crore in PCB projects.
*   **Display/Components:** **Dixon** is investing Rs 1,000 crore over 3 years in display modules; **Virtuoso** is expanding compressor capacity to 6 million units.
*   **General Expansion:** **Aditya Infotech** is executing a Rs 650 crore plan to double capacity; **Highness Microelectronics** is fast-tracking a Goa facility to meet PLI requirements.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance:** **Aditya Infotech** raised FY27 revenue and PAT guidance. **GNG Electronics** raised revenue growth guidance to 40% (from 30%) and PAT growth guidance. **Syrma SGS** upgraded annual revenue growth guidance to 40%–45%.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Kaynes Technology** shifted tone toward balance sheet discipline and cash preservation, moving away from the aggressive smart meter growth seen previously. **Virtuoso Optoelectronics** reported that its Star Eltech facility is now on hold due to lessor issues.
*   **Financial Reporting:** **Kaynes Technology** restated prior period financials under Ind AS 8 following a change in auditors.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Supply Chain Normalization:** **Syrma SGS** expects supply chain constraints to ease by Q1 FY28; monitoring this will be key for margin recovery.
*   **Smart Meter Restructuring:** **Kaynes Technology** plans to hive off installation and O&M services to a third party by next quarter.
*   **Margin Recovery:** Investors will watch if **Dixon** and **Kaynes** can recover margins as cost pass-throughs catch up and new high-margin facilities (PCB/OSAT) ramp up.
*   **Inventory Unwinding:** **Kaynes** plans to unwind Rs 200–250 crore of strategic inventory starting December 2026.

## Company one-liners
*   **Aditya Infotech:** Delivered exceptional H1 performance, wiped out debt post-QIP, and raised FY27 top-line guidance.
*   **Cyient DLM:** Reported robust H1 revenue growth of 30.2% while absorbing upfront investments that depressed near-term margins.
*   **Dixon Technologies:** Aggressively expanding into IT hardware and display modules to drive scale, while navigating temporary input-cost margin dilution.
*   **GNG Electronics:** Delivered strong performance driven by structural demand for refurbished PCs and raised full-year guidance.
*   **Highness Microelectronics:** Achieved 63.4% revenue growth and 32.1% EBITDA margins, fast-tracking capacity to capture PLI demand.
*   **Kaynes Technology:** Sacrificed short-term smart meter top-line to improve balance sheet health while locking in long-term OSAT/PCB execution via Vishay JV.
*   **Macfos:** Delivered strong H1 growth and remains focused on scaling its dual-engine model using internal cash flows.
*   **Netweb Technologies:** Delivered record-breaking quarter with 4x YoY growth, supported by AI infrastructure demand and zero net debt.
*   **Syrma SGS:** Reported 71% YoY H1 revenue growth and upgraded full-year revenue guidance to ~INR7,000 crores.
*   **Virtuoso Optoelectronics:** Delivered a strong H1 with 78% standalone revenue growth and is scaling capacities to sustain 30%+ long-term CAGR.
