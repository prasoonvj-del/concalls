# Vikram Solar Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Volumes and revenues declined sequentially due to project delays from IPP customers and delayed ALMM List-II clarity for C&I, though unit economics and order inflows improved.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (implied higher H2 volumes and holding of EBITDA per watt peak, but no numerical full-year guidance given).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from strong sequential growth in Q1 to a tough Q2 with temporary volume drops, but management remains confident on H2 recovery.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: U.S. Section 232 circular introducing minimum import price and 15% ad valorem duty rendered 1 GW of U.S. export orders unviable (removed from order book).
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Gangaikondan 9 GW cell facility remains on schedule for first cell out in December and COD in March 2027.

### Highlights
- Q2 volume (591 MW) and revenue (Rs 1,077 crore) fell sequentially due to IPP customer deferrals (land and evacuation constraints) and delayed clarity on ALMM List-II for C&I.
- Distribution network expanded to 133 distributors (up from 119) and over 750 dealers/system integrators; distribution volume reached 44% of total volume.
- 1 GW of U.S. export orders removed from the order book due to the U.S. Section 232 circular (minimum import price and 15% ad valorem duty).
- 9 GW TOPCon Plus cell facility on schedule: first cell out in December, sequential commissioning over three months, and project COD in March 2027.
- BESS Phase 1 (7.5 GWh assembly plant in Chennai): equipment undergoing factory acceptance tests, shipment in Q2, first container rollout next quarter, commercial operations targeted for Q1 next fiscal year. VION low-voltage brand established over 300 distributors in 9 months.
- Safeguard duty INR150 crores and Rs 50 crores receivables withheld by customers remain under follow-up.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 volumes to be substantially higher than H1, anticipating a return to around 1 GW of volume in upcoming quarters as the 8.2 GW order book converts.
- Management expects EBITDA per watt peak to hold around current levels in H2.
- Captive cells coming online in FY28 are expected to nearly double DCR margins and lift overall margins meaningfully.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CEA expects peak power demand to rise from 289 GW to 459 GW by FY36, requiring solar capacity to triple to 509 GW.
- PM Surya Ghar added 23.5 lakh systems (~7 GW) in H1 FY27. Utility-scale solar tendering rose 61% QoQ.
- Data centers projected to grow 4x to 6 GW by 2029, driving new long-term C&I demand (1 GW data center load requires 4-5 GW solar + storage).
- Union Cabinet approved Rs 1.86 lakh crore Green Energy Corridor Phase-III to evacuate up to 135 GW of renewable energy.
- India added 8.9 GW of solar capacity in Q2 (down 25% from 12 GW in Q1); ground-mounted additions fell 31% from 6.4 GW to 4.4 GW.
- Crude oil sub-90 levels expected to lower logistics and freight costs going forward.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA per watt peak rose 37% QoQ to Rs 1.71 due to a richer channel mix (distribution at 44% of volume) and higher-efficiency products.
- Finance cost fell 13% QoQ to Rs 43 crore.
- Other expenses fell 25% due to lower volumes and cost optimization/value engineering.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gangaikondan project (6 GW modules, 9 GW cells) total cost is Rs 5,800 crore, funded 1/3 equity and 2/3 debt; Rs 1,700 crore spent to date, with another Rs 3,800 crore planned in H2 (mostly for cells).
- Wafer ingot and BESS spending are paced to keep capital concentrated mainly on the cell project in FY27. Wafer ingot commissioning targeted for mid-FY29 matching ALMM List-III.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt at Rs 632 crore, net debt at Rs 547 crore; net debt-to-equity ratio maintained at 0.17.
- Other current assets increased from ~Rs 360 crore to ~Rs 620 crore due to advances for ongoing capex vendors and DCR suppliers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- IPP customer delays due to land availability, monsoon, and evacuation constraints.
- Regulatory changes in the U.S. (Section 232 circular) making 1 GW of U.S. export orders unviable.
- Over-capacity in module manufacturing and narrowing non-DCR market causing price competition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shubham Burade (ICICI Securities) asked about DCR share in the 8.2 GW order book and execution timeline.** Management stated the order book is primarily non-DCR, and 60%-70% will be executed over the next 12-18 months.
2. **Sahil Sheth (Anand Rathi) asked about the BESS tech export curbs impact.** Management answered that assembly has no export control issues, and cell-related controls are years away with mitigation plans.
3. **Deepak Purswani (Prabhudas Lilladher) asked if volume will return to ~1 GW next quarter.** Management confirmed it is a fair estimate as demand normalizes.
4. **Vaibhav Shah asked about the increase in other current assets and EOM receivables.** Management explained it is due to capex and DCR vendor advances, which will convert in Q3/Q4, and safeguard duty/withheld receivables are being pursued with advisors.
5. **Vinod Bansal asked if EBITDA margin guidance will see an uptick from Q1 FY28 onwards.** Management confirmed that once captive cells flow into modules, cell margins will be captured, leading to an uptick.

### One-line takeaway
Despite a temporary Q2 volume and revenue drop caused by IPP project delays and U.S. tariff hurdles, Vikram Solar improved unit economics, expanded distribution, and stayed on track to commission its 9 GW cell facility by March 2027.
