# Virtuoso Optoelectronics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue growth (+85%) and doubling of PAT (+103%) despite raw material price pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 376.6 crore, up ~85% YoY and 18.7% QoQ.
- **PAT:** Rs 9.0 crore, up 103% YoY from Rs 6.4 crore in Q1 FY26.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.3% (similar to Q4 FY26 levels).
- **Working Capital Days:** ~85 days as of Q1 FY27 end.
- **EBITDA Margin Target:** 9% to 10% for FY27; Net PAT margin target of 2.5% to 3.0%.

### Highlights
- Strong seasonal execution in room air conditioners (RAC) and refrigeration.
- Compressor vertical ran at >50% capacity utilization, roughly 3 months ahead of schedule.
- Onboarded a marquee commercial refrigeration customer and entered the medicinal refrigerator segment (targeting Vizi coolers next).
- Segment revenue mix expected for FY27: Room AC 55–60%, Compressors 15%, EMS 10%, Commercial Refrigeration 10%, Components/Washing Machines 5%.
- Customer concentration for top AC client reduced from >60% in FY26 to expected 25%–35% in FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 35% to 40% YoY; target of 35%–40% CAGR over the next 3–5 years (guidance revision to be reviewed at end of Q3).
- **Peak Revenue Potential:** Ongoing expansions to enable Rs 3,500 crore to Rs 4,000 crore peak revenue potential at optimal utilization levels over the next 12 months.
- Management expects RAC industry growth of 8% to 10% in the upcoming season.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 demand was healthy overall, though commercial refrigeration faced temporary deferrals in B2B purchasing due to pricing increases.
- Raw material availability and global logistics created cost pressures; Indian captive component capacities and local sourcing are coming online.
- RAC channel/market inventory levels are currently at normal levels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins faced pressure from price spikes in imported raw materials (copper, aluminum) and dollar fluctuations.
- Margins expected to stabilize around current 9.3% levels in FY27 with potential expansion into FY28 as ODM mix grows and RM prices stabilize.
- PLI scheme impact is ~1% on AC EBITDA; management is negotiating win-win pricing with customers over the next 12 months to offset PLI expiration in 2027.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Total FY27 CapEx:** Rs 80 crore to Rs 100 crore planned for Virtuoso Optoelectronics.
- **Commercial Refrigeration CapEx:** Rs 20 crore to Rs 25 crore in FY27 to scale capacity from 1.5 lakh to 2.5 lakh units (and eventually 4.0 lakh units next FY).
- **EMS Capacity:** Expanding from 4 lakh CPH to 12 lakh CPH (Phase 1 of 8 lakh CPH live by end of September / Q3).
- **RAC Capacity:** Expanding from 1.0 million to 1.8 million units over 12–15 months (aiming for 50%–60% utilization in FY28).
- **Compressors Capacity:** Moving from 2.8 million to 6.0 million units by Dec 2026 / Jan 2027. Acquired 22 acres of land for long-term consolidation of the compressor business.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated higher interest cost in Q1 FY27 was primarily driven by OCD (Optionally Convertible Debentures) borrowings carrying mid-teen interest rates.
- Raised Rs 150 crore debt via OCDs to fund compressor phase-1 expansion and backward integration (shell manufacturing and motor assembly).
- Management intends to maintain a stable debt-to-equity ratio over the next 12 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in imported raw materials (copper, aluminum, chips) and currency fluctuations.
- Near-term margin absorption lag when passing raw material cost increases to clients.
- Phasing out of PLI benefits starting 2027.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Are you expanding into AC compressors along with refrigeration compressors?**  
  **A:** Management confirmed they are strictly focusing on refrigeration compressors for now and have not finalized any plans for AC compressors.
- **Q: How will compressor margins evolve?**  
  **A:** Current compressor EBITDA margin is 5%–7%. Margins are expected to improve by 1%–2% in FY28 once in-house backward integration (motors, shells) is fully localized.
- **Q: How much captive consumption exists for component plants?**  
  **A:** ~15% of VPPL component output and 2%–3% of compressor output is consumed captive by VOEPL; remaining 85%+ is sold to third-party customers.
- **Q: How do you plan to handle the loss of PLI incentives after 2027?**  
  **A:** The EBITDA impact is ~1% in RAC. Discussions are ongoing with brand customers to adjust pricing models over the next 12 months.
- **Q: What is the timeline and peak utilization expectation for the RAC expansion to 1.8M units?**  
  **A:** Entire capacity will be live before the FY28 season, expecting 50%–60% utilization driven by both existing customers and new client additions.

### One-line takeaway
Virtuoso Optoelectronics is executing rapid backward integration and capacity scaling in RAC, compressors, and EMS to drive a 35%–40% multi-year revenue CAGR with resilient 9%–10% EBITDA margins.
