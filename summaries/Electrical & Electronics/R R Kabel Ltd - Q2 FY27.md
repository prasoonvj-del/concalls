# R R Kabel Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record H1 revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, though management slightly fine-tuned full-year volume guidance amid market volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 3,284 crore (up 52% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 6,452 crore (up 53% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 286 crore (up 63% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8.7% (up 60 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 181 crore (up 55% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 386 crore (up 87% YoY).
*   **Interim Dividend:** Rs 6.50 per equity share.

### Highlights
*   **Wires & Cables (W&C):** Revenue grew 53% in Q2 and 55% in H1. Segment profit margin was 9.4% in Q2 and 9.6% in H1.
*   **FMEG:** Revenue grew 42% in Q2 and 35% in H1. Segment reported a loss of Rs 12 crore in Q2; H1 loss margin narrowed to 2.2% (from 4.5% YoY).
*   **Acquisition:** Entered a business transfer agreement to acquire UM Cables (optical fibre business) for ~Rs 77 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume Growth:** Management expects 14-15% volume growth for the full year (fine-tuned from previous 16-18% guidance).
*   **Margins:** Targeting 9.5% EBIT margin for W&C in FY27, rising to 10.5% by FY28.
*   **FMEG:** Management maintains the objective of achieving full-year breakeven at the segment profit level in FY27.
*   **Exports:** Management expects export share to remain in the 30-35% range for the second half.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Drivers:** Sustained focus on infrastructure, power, electrification, housing, and industrial investments.
*   **Volume Trends:** Overall volume growth was high single-digit in Q2 (Wires grew double-digit; Cables grew single-digit).
*   **Exports:** Export growth outpaced domestic growth in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **W&C Margins:** Improvement driven by disciplined commodity management, operating leverage, and cost absorption.
*   **Interest Costs:** Management noted higher interest costs due to high raw material prices and increased LC-backed trade facilities.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 550-600 crore for FY27 (excluding land and UM Cables acquisition).
*   **New Investment:** Approved Rs 200 crore for land acquisition in Rajasthan to support future expansion.
*   **UM Cables:** Acquisition cost of ~Rs 77 crore is separate from the base capex guidance.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Net working capital cycle improved to 46 days (from 49 days in March and 50 days in June).
*   **Debt:** Management expects debt to increase to fund capex and working capital requirements, noting that current leverage remains healthy.
*   **Trade Acceptances:** LC-backed trade facilities stand at approximately Rs 1,290 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Competition:** Management acknowledged increased competition in the domestic market.
*   **Volatility:** Management cited market volatility as a factor for fine-tuning volume guidance.
*   **FMEG Losses:** Segment remains loss-making in Q2 due to seasonality (fan-heavy product mix).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Volume Guidance:** When asked about the trim in volume guidance, management clarified it is "fine-tuning" due to market volatility and not a fundamental change in growth drivers.
*   **Capex/Debt:** Management confirmed debt will increase to fund the Rs 600 crore capex, Rs 200 crore land, and UM Cables acquisition, but emphasized that the company is not "over-debt."
*   **UM Cables:** Management stated it is too early to provide a detailed business plan for the new acquisition; they will provide updates in the next quarter.
*   **FMEG Seasonality:** Management explained that Q2 is a low season for fans, which impacted profitability, but they remain confident in full-year breakeven.
*   **Export Mix:** Management noted that the export mix is shifting slightly in favor of cables due to new capacity additions.

### One-line takeaway
R R Kabel delivered record H1 financial performance, and while management slightly moderated full-year volume guidance due to market volatility, they continue to pursue aggressive capex and strategic acquisitions to drive long-term growth.
