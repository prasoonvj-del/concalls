# Orient Cables (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly performance driven by strong demand, improved product mix, and better capacity utilization.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,018 crore in H1 FY27 (up 103% YoY); Rs 528 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 107% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 119.2 crore in H1 FY27 (up 155% YoY) with 11.7% margin; Rs 63.6 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 158% YoY) with 12.0% margin.
- **EBITDA Margin Change:** +240 bps YoY for H1 FY27; +230 bps YoY for Q2 FY27.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 73.7 crore in H1 FY27 (up 194% YoY); Rs 40.1 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 180% YoY).
- **Volume vs. Realization Growth (H1 FY27):** ~60% from volume growth, rest from realization growth.
- **Capacity Utilization:** ~72% as of September 30, 2026; Bengaluru plant running at 60%+ utilization.
- **Installed Cable Manufacturing Capacity:** ~9.63 lakh kilometers per annum.
- **Exports:** ~8% of revenue in H1 FY27; exports reached ~Rs 79.3 crore in H1 FY27 vs. ~Rs 50.4 crore in H1 FY26.
- **Return Ratios (Annualized):** ROCE and ROE at 30% plus.
- **Gross Fixed Asset Turnover:** 8x plus.

### Highlights
- Maiden earnings conference call following the BSE and NSE listing in October 2026.
- Bengaluru facility commenced commercial operations in May 2026 with an initial capacity of ~1,09,320 km/annum.
- Entire IPO proceeds of Rs 155.5 crore earmarked for debt repayment have been fully utilized.
- Subsidiary OCL Greentech started supplying EV charging gun and cable assemblies to Tier-1 OEMs in Q2.
- Continues past pattern of capacity expansion and debt reduction via IPO proceeds, as noted in recent disclosures.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects absolute performance in H2 FY27 to be broadly in line with H1 FY27, noting Q3 is typically seasonally softer while Q4 picks up.
- Peak revenue potential with current capacity estimated at Rs 2,800 crore to Rs 2,900 crore.
- New Bhiwadi plant expansion expected to push potential peak revenue to Rs 4,500 crore plus from FY29 onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global wires and cables market projected to reach $304.2 billion by CY30 at a 5.6% CAGR (1Lattice report). Indian market expected to grow at a 13.4% CAGR to INR 2,103 billion by FY31.
- Networking, optical fiber, and E-beam cable markets in India projected to grow at CAGRs of 18.7%, 17.3%, and 37.8% from FY26 to FY31.
- Fixed broadband penetration in India remains below 5%; data center capacity projected to rise from 1.7 GW in FY26 to 10-12 GW by FY31.
- Top 10 customers associated with the company for an average of over 9 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expansion supported by a favourable product mix, operating leverage, and improved operational efficiencies.
- Management states blended margins are expected to remain in the current range with slight potential improvements over the years from R&D and value-added products.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acquired ~42,530 square meters (11 acres) of additional land in Bhiwadi for a new manufacturing facility; civil construction underway and expected to complete in H1 FY28, with commercial production from beginning of FY29.
- New Bhiwadi facility will include high-voltage and medium-voltage cable manufacturing.
- JV Corelink Cable Technologies (50:50 with Aditya Infotech) installation underway, with commercial production expected in Q4 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds of Rs 155.5 crore fully utilized for debt repayment, strengthening the balance sheet and financial flexibility.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management, though Q3 was noted as a seasonally softer quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Disha (Trinetra Asset Managers):** Asked for volume vs. realization bifurcation. *Management stated ~60% growth came from volume and the rest from realization.*
2. **Achuth:** Asked about data center vs. telecom customer mix for OFC and future capex. *Management noted majority is telecom currently, but they are building a vertical for direct data center pitches via OEMs; confirmed FY28 capex via the Bhiwadi plant.*
3. **Jigar Shah:** Inquired about structural margin improvements from new products. *Management expects blended margins to remain stable with a slight upward bias over time.*
4. **Vanshi Shah:** Asked about key growth drivers and return ratios. *Management cited capacity utilization, Bengaluru ramp-up, and the upcoming JV; highlighted 30%+ ROCE/ROE and 8x+ asset turnover.*
5. **Raj Mehta:** Asked about networking cable contribution share going forward. *Management stated networking share will slightly reduce as other segments grow faster.*

### One-line takeaway
Orient Cables delivered a robust H1 FY27 with triple-digit revenue and profit growth, backed by high capacity utilization, new plant expansions, and successful debt reduction from IPO proceeds.
