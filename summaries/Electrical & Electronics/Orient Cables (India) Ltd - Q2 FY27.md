# Orient Cables (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Maiden earnings call post-October 2026 listing reporting record quarterly performance with revenue and PAT growing over 100% and 180% YoY, respectively.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Standalone):** Rs 528 crore, +107% YoY (H1 FY27: Rs 1,018 crore, +103% YoY).
- **Growth split (H1 FY27):** ~60% from volumes, balance from better realizations.
- **EBITDA (Standalone):** Rs 63.6 crore, +158% YoY (H1 FY27: Rs 119.2 crore, +155% YoY).
- **EBITDA margin (Standalone):** 12.0%, +230 bps YoY (H1 FY27: 11.7%, +240 bps YoY).
- **PAT (Standalone):** Rs 40.1 crore, +180% YoY (H1 FY27: Rs 73.7 crore, +194% YoY).
- **Exports:** Rs 79.3 crore in H1 FY27 vs Rs 50.4 crore in H1 FY26 (contributed ~8% of revenue).

### Highlights
- Company completed its IPO and listed on NSE and BSE in October 2026.
- In H1 FY27, revenue nearly equalled and EBITDA/PAT exceeded full-year FY26 levels.
- Top 10 customers have an average association of >9 years; market share in Indian networking cables expanded from ~16% in FY22 to 22.9% in FY26.
- Started supplying EV charging gun and cable assemblies to Tier-1 OEMs in Q2 FY27.
- Standalone operations drove reported financials; contributions from OCL Greentech (51% subsidiary) and CoreLink Cable Technology (50:50 JV with Aditya Infotech) were relatively insignificant.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 absolute performance to be broadly in line with H1 FY27 (noting Q3 is seasonally softer, with momentum picking up in Q4).
- CoreLink JV commercial production is expected to commence in Q4 FY27.
- Solar junction boxes commercial production expected to begin in Q4 FY27.
- Blended EBITDA margins expected to remain around current levels or slightly improve over the years.
- Current total capacity peak revenue potential estimated at Rs 2,800 to Rs 2,900 crore; expected to be fully utilized by end of FY27 or early FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India wires and cables market projected to grow from ~Rs 1,122 billion in FY26 to Rs 2,103 billion by FY31 (13.4% CAGR). Indian networking, OFC, and E-beam cables projected at 18.7%, 17.3%, and 37.8% CAGRs, respectively.
- Fixed broadband penetration in India remains <5%; data center capacity projected to rise from 1.7 GW in FY26 to 10–12 GW by FY31.
- Demand in OFC and specialty power is primarily driven by telcos, ISPs, and telecom equipment manufacturers; data center opportunities are currently served via OEMs, with direct vertical build-out planned.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by operating leverage, improved operational efficiencies, and a favorable product mix.
- Management indicated power cables carry slightly lower margins than networking cables, but have relatively lower manufacturing costs, keeping overall impact stable.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total installed cable capacity: ~9.63 lakh km/annum across 3 plants (2 in Bhiwadi, 1 in Bengaluru). Overall capacity utilization stood at 72% as of September 30, 2026.
- Bengaluru facility (started May 2026, initial capacity ~109,320 km/annum) achieved >60% capacity utilization in Q2.
- Bhiwadi 11-acre expansion (civil work underway, completion targeted in H1 FY28) will add high-voltage and medium-voltage cable manufacturing; operations targeted from early FY29, expanding peak revenue potential to Rs 4,500+ crore.
- EV charging gun and cable capacity ramping up in Q3 FY27, expected to be fully utilized by Q4 FY27.
- Annualized ROE and ROCE were at 30%+ in H1 FY27; gross fixed asset turnover ratio at 8x+.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds: Entire Rs 155.5 crore earmarked for debt repayment has been fully utilized. Balance proceeds to fund plant/machinery capex and general corporate purposes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Middle East conflict caused minor hiccups in procurement during the prior quarter, but management noted corrective actions were taken with no material impact.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Volume vs. Realization Growth:** Growth of 103% in H1 was ~60% volume-driven, with the remaining ~40% coming from better realizations.
- **Data Center Exposure:** Majority of OFC sales currently go to telecom operators (Airtel, Jio); data centers are addressed through OEM partners, but a direct vertical is being built out.
- **Product Margin and Realization Breakdown:** Management declined to provide specific product-level realizations or standalone margins for networking, OFC, or specialty cables.
- **Capacity Runway:** Current capacity supports peak revenues of Rs 2,800–2,900 crore. With the new Bhiwadi plant (FY29), revenue potential increases to Rs 4,500+ crore.

### One-line takeaway
Orient Cables delivered strong >100% revenue growth with debt wiped out via IPO proceeds, while expanding capacity toward Rs 4,500+ crore peak revenue potential.
