# Genus Power Infrastructures Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted robust H1 execution, strong operating cash flow generation ahead of schedule, and stable margins despite macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Standalone H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 2,497 crore (up 19.4% YoY from Rs 2,091 crore)
- Standalone H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 485 crore (up 9.2% YoY from Rs 444 crore)
- Standalone H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 296 crore (up 7% YoY from Rs 277 crore)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin: 19.4%
- H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow: Positive Rs 127 crore (vs outflow of Rs 158 crore in H1 FY26)
- Net Debt (as of Sept 30, 2026): Rs 1,746 crore (vs Rs 1,560 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- Q2 FY27 Standalone Revenue: Rs 1,133 crore (broadly in line with Rs 1,149 crore in Q2 FY26; down 17% QoQ)
- Q2 FY27 Gross Margin: 38.3% (up 127 bps QoQ)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 225 crore; EBITDA Margin: 19.8% (up 77 bps QoQ)
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 133 crore; PAT Margin: 11.8%
- Cumulative Order Book (as of Sept 30, 2026, excluding taxes): Rs 22,634 crore
- Cumulative AMISP Awarded Meters: ~3.6 crore; Installed: ~1.7 crore
- H1 FY27 Meter Installations: ~4.1 million; Q2 FY27 Installations: ~1.8 million
- H1 O&M Revenue: ~Rs 150 crore; expected to exceed Rs 300 crore in FY27
- Export Order Book: Close to Rs 100 crore; target of Rs 500 crore over next 3-4 years
- India Smart Gas Metering Opportunity: Rs 35,000 crore to Rs 36,000 crore over next few years; Genus market share target: 20%

### Highlights
- Management expressed deep condolences on the passing of Chairman and Executive Director Mr. Ishwar Chand Agarwal on November 4, 2026.
- Achieved operating cash flow positivity in H1 FY27 (Rs 127 crore) well ahead of the previously guided timeline (end of FY27/start of FY28), driven by higher operating profit and lower working capital requirements (inventory reduced by over Rs 100 crore).
- All 26 project SPVs are now operational, improving the debtor cycle and speeding up SAT (Subscriber Acceptance Test/System Acceptance Test) processes (over 1.45 crore meters have undergone SAT out of 1.7 crore installed).
- Q2 execution was impacted by a long-drawn monsoon, local body elections, slower pace in Uttar Pradesh, and the meter mix.
- *Check against record:* The call did not mention the CRISIL rating outlook revision to positive, but confirmed the completion of the share buyback (discussed as an alternative to dividends, utilizing ~Rs 148-150 crore to benefit smaller shareholders without promoter participation).

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 Revenue Guidance: Maintained at Rs 6,000 crore to Rs 6,500 crore.
- FY27 EBITDA Margin Guidance: Maintained conservatively at 18%.
- FY27 Installation Target: ~1 crore smart meters.
- FY28 Revenue Guidance: ~Rs 7,000 crore.
- Net Debt Peak: Management reiterated net debt will not cross Rs 2,000 crore and expects it to reduce from FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Estimated national smart meter requirement is 31 to 32 crore meters, with ~15 crore still to be tendered.
- Live tenders currently stand at ~2 crore meters (primarily Tamil Nadu); expect ~2 crore additional meters from West Bengal (10-15 million) and Haryana (7 million) in the next 30-60 days.
- Tamil Nadu's earlier 3-crore tender was scrapped by the new government and replaced with a new 2-crore tender (submission in February).
- Adani's takeover of IntelliSmart is viewed positively by management as it strengthens industry execution and confidence.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- YoY margin moderation was attributed to elevated raw material costs from West Asia supply disruptions, currency movements on imported components, and higher depreciation/finance costs as operations scaled.
- Q2 gross and EBITDA margins expanded QoQ due to better supplier terms and improved logistics efficiency.
- Finance cost for the quarter was ~Rs 50 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity exceeds 1.8 crore meters annually.
- GIC platform can support 3 to 5 crore additional meters without fresh capital.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased to Rs 1,746 crore as of September 30, 2026 (from Rs 1,560 crore in March 2026), staying within the peak Rs 2,000 crore ceiling.
- Receivables reflect quarter-end billing timing and are expected to decline progressively from Q3 as SAT clearances convert into payments. Working capital days expected to improve by 50 to 75 days in FY27 YoY.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Seasonal monsoon headwinds, local body elections, and slower execution in Uttar Pradesh (expected to normalize after UP elections around April).
- Elevated raw material input costs and currency volatility impacting imported components.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mahesh Patil (ICICI Securities)** asked about the reasons for the sequential revenue dip in Q2. *Management answered* that installation pace slowed due to an extended monsoon and state-specific delays (e.g., UP), but reiterated annual guidance.
2. **Neeraj Shajel (Individual Investor)** questioned the declining order book trend over the last 18 months and the impact of Adani acquiring IntelliSmart. *Management answered* that they are selective and return-focused in bidding (skipping low-margin tenders) and welcomed Adani's consolidation as positive for the industry.
3. **Saket Saurabh (Wealth Finvisor)** asked why the FY28 revenue guidance of Rs 7,000 crore appears conservative given gas and export traction. *Management answered* that they prefer to guide numbers they are 100% confident of and may upgrade guidance later if conditions warrant.
4. **Aditya Singh (Multibagger Stocks)** questioned the rationale of a ~Rs 150 crore buyback when net debt is rising and GIC JV requires capital. *Management answered* that the buyback was executed instead of dividends to benefit smaller shareholders without increasing promoter payout ratios.
5. **Kushal Maheshwari (AK Capital)** asked about the outlook for UP installations and finance cost peaks. *Management answered* that UP installations will normalize post-elections in April, and net debt will remain below Rs 2,000 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Genus Power delivered resilient H1 FY27 performance with 19.4% revenue growth and achieved operating cash flow positivity ahead of schedule, driven by scaling operational projects and improving working capital cycles.
