# Fujiyama Power Systems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance driven by 125.3% YoY revenue growth, expanding operating margins, and an upward revision in full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 13,457 million (Rs. 1,345.7 crore), up 125.3% YoY from Rs. 5,973 million in Q1 FY26.
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 2,548 million (Rs. 254.8 crore), up 140.6% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 18.9%, expanding 120 bps YoY from 17.7% in Q1 FY26.
- **Exceptional Loss:** Provisional provision of Rs. 1,436 million (Rs. 143.6 crore) net carrying value due to a fire incident at the Bawal facility (post-tax impact of Rs. 1,074 million).
- **Reported PAT:** Rs. 578 million (Rs. 57.8 crore) with a PAT margin of 4.3%.
- **Normalized PAT (ex-fire provision):** Rs. 1,652 million (Rs. 165.2 crore), up 144.5% YoY; Normalized PAT margin expanded to 12.3% from 11.3% in Q1 FY26.
- **Distribution Reach:** Added >80 distributors, >1,000 dealers, and >30 exclusive Shoppes, bringing total channel partners to >10,100.

### Highlights
- Commissioned a 2 GW solar panel manufacturing plant at Ratlam during the quarter, taking total panel capacity to 3.5 GW.
- Commissioned a 2 GW power electronics facility at Ratlam in August 2026, taking total power electronics capacity to 4 GW.
- Increased equity stake from 19% to 50% in Zayo Energy Private Limited and Zayo Cable Private Limited to ensure backward integration for solar module components (frames, ribbons, cables, EVA sheets, junction boxes).
- Bawal fire damage covered by insurance; assessment is at an advanced stage, and full recovery of the Rs. 1,436 million net carrying asset value is expected.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance to 70% YoY (up from 50% YoY previously), with potential to revise upwards in coming quarters.
- Re-iterated EBITDA margin guidance to remain "sustainable to improve" from Q1 FY27 levels of 18.9%.
- 2 GW lithium-ion battery capacity at Ratlam on track for commissioning by Q2 FY27.
- Target to reach over 15,000 total distribution network partners by the end of FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India rooftop solar potential estimated at >630 GW, with the government target set at 90–100 GW of rooftop solar by 2030 (out of 300 GW total solar target).
- Installed rooftop solar capacity stands at ~30 GW (~15 GW contributed by PM Surya Ghar Yojana covering 50 lakh of the 1 crore target households).
- Current rooftop installation run-rate under PM Surya Ghar is ~1 GW per month.
- Total system realizations (BOM billed to distributor including panels, inverter, and battery) range from Rs. 26 to Rs. 30 per watt.
- Government actively working on PM Surya Ghar 2.0 policy, expected to incorporate battery storage (BESS) provisions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage and manufacturing scale drove margin expansion, allowing partial efficiency gains to be passed to customers via competitive pricing.
- In-house DCR (Domestic Content Requirement) solar cell plant operating at >80% utilization, protecting margin profile against falling non-DCR market prices.
- Bawal fire led to temporary loss of internal lead-acid tubular battery capacity, impacting overall gross margin by less than 0.5% due to external vendor sourcing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gross block (including CWIP) expected to reach ~Rs. 1,300 crore by the end of FY27 (up from ~Rs. 800 crore at the start of the year).
- Total FY27 incremental capex of ~Rs. 500 crore to be funded via Rs. 200 crore debt, Rs. 100 crore unutilized IPO proceeds, and Rs. 200 crore internal cash flows; no equity dilution.
- Zayo Energy/Zayo Cable capex estimated at Rs. 180–200 crore (Fujiyama's equity contribution ~Rs. 50 crore); production expected to start next financial year.
- 1.2 GW TOPCon solar cell capacity expansion targeted for Q4 FY27 commissioning (~7.5 to 9 months timeline).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Funded Rs. 500 crore capex through Rs. 200 crore debt and internal accruals/IPO funds without equity dilution.
- Complete settlement of the Rs. 143.6 crore insurance claim for Bawal fire expected by the end of FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Fire incident at Bawal lead-acid battery plant disrupted operations, requiring temporary leasing of an operational third-party facility while insurance processes clear.
- Industry-wide supply constraints in DCR solar panels causing minor adoption delays under government schemes.
- BIS regulatory inspection identified minor non-compliance in 15 out of 500 SKUs (valued under 1% of inventory), which management subsequently addressed via testing and certification.

  *Management:* Revised full-year revenue growth guidance upward from 50% to 70% YoY based on strong demand and Ratlam capacity readiness.

- **Abhi Jain (AJ Capital):** How will the ~Rs. 500 crore FY27 capex be funded?  
  *Management:* No equity dilution will take place; funding comprises Rs. 200 crore debt, Rs. 100 crore IPO proceeds, and Rs. 200 crore internal accruals.

### One-line takeaway
Fujiyama delivered stellar 125.3% YoY revenue growth in Q1 FY27 led by rapid capacity additions and strong rooftop solar demand, raising its full-year revenue growth guidance to 70% YoY.
