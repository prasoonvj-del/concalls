# G V Electricals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by 11.6% revenue growth, a 55% surge in PAT, margin expansion, and a strong order book following its BSE SME listing.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Operations):** Rs 81.97 crore (up 11.6% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 9.69 crore (up 29.4% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.8% (expanded 160 bps YoY from 10.2%)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 6.04 crore (up 55% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 7.4% (improved 210 bps YoY from 5.3%)
- **Executable Order Book:** Rs 555.84 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Interest Savings:** Rs 0.90 crore from debt repayment
- **Core Network Revenue Share:** 85% of operating revenue (up 19.8% YoY)

### Highlights
- Marked successful listing on the BSE SME platform during the half-year period.
- Balance sheet is net debt-free, with IPO proceeds utilized for debt repayment and working capital requirements (EMD, BG).
- *Record Check:* The call's reported 55% PAT growth confirms the earlier investor deck statement for H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for approximately 30% organic top-line growth for FY27 based on the existing order book and pipeline.
- Management expects the data center sector to account for 50% to 60% of business/pipeline terms in FY28 and FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth supported by DISCOM privatization, outsourcing, transmission and distribution loss reduction, metering, and electrical infrastructure for data centers (including clients like Hiranandani/Nidar, Yotta Noida, Vertiv, and Schneider).
- Geographical expansion underway with bids submitted in Uttar Pradesh, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, and Rajasthan.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expanded 160 bps YoY to 11.8%; PAT margin improved 210 bps YoY to 7.4%.
- Management noted that new project execution initially incurs higher capex/opex due to learning curves and varying terrain, but these fluctuations are eventually recovered.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- IPO funds are actively supporting working capital requirements (EMD, BG, and project cycles). No specific capex outlay number was provided.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company is net debt-free. 
- Debt repayment generated Rs 0.90 crore in interest savings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Terrain differences and initial learning environments can cause temporary capex/opex fluctuations during new project execution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Praful Agarwal (RM Ventures) asked about core centers and geographical expansion:** Management responded that focus remains on EPC and O&M/maintenance, expanding into Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, and Rajasthan as DISCOMs outsource.
- **Analyst asked if EBITDA margin expansion is sustainable:** Management stated margins will mostly sustain, though initial terrain and learning costs cause temporary short-term fluctuations.
- **Analyst asked about the execution timeline for the Rs 555.84 crore order book:** Management explained that ~70% is EPC and ~30% is MMG/AMC work, with execution periods ranging from 3 to 18 months depending on the job.
- **Individual investor asked about data center client concentration and contribution:** Management declined to explicitly name specific clients (such as Vertiv or Schneider) due to competitive sensitivity, but stated data centers could drive 50% to 60% of business in FY28/FY29.

### One-line takeaway
G V Electricals delivered a strong H1 FY27 with a 55% surge in PAT, an expanded order book of Rs 555.84 crore, and robust opportunities stemming from power sector privatization and data center outsourcing.
