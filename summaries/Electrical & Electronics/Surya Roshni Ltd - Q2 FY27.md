# Surya Roshni Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited record-breaking Q2 performance with robust revenue and volume growth across both steel and lighting segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 targets and promises.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positively, noting Q2 became the strongest-ever second quarter despite seasonality, with strong execution visibility for H2.
- New topics or risks: Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligation impact mentioned for lighting (~Rs 3-3.5 crore in H1). Old risks regarding freight volatility persisted, while Middle East concerns eased with strong US and export pickups.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on ramping up new DFT mills, export traction (especially US), and strong sequential volume growth.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,448 crore, up 33% YoY and 20% QoQ
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 143 crore, up 19% QoQ
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 77 crore, up 29% QoQ
- **Lighting & Consumer Durables Revenue:** Rs 507 crore, up 17% YoY and 11% QoQ
- **Lighting & Consumer Durables EBITDA:** Rs 40 crore (margin 7.8%)
- **Lighting & Consumer Durables PBT:** Rs 28 crore
- **Steel Pipes & Strips Revenue:** Rs 1,941 crore, up 38% YoY
- **Steel Pipes & Strips Volume:** 2.67 lakh tonnes, up 23% YoY and 17% QoQ
- **Steel Pipes & Strips EBITDA:** Rs 103 crore
- **Steel EBITDA per metric tonne:** Rs 4,105 (vs Rs 4,006 in Q1 FY27 and Rs 5,013 in Q2 FY26)
- **Net Working Capital Cycle:** 63 days
- **ROCE:** 15.74%
- **ROE:** 11.35%
- **Net Cash Surplus:** Rs 75 crore (zero-debt company as of September 30, 2026)
- **Professional Lighting Order Book:** Over Rs 200 crore
- **Steel Order Book:** About Rs 1,200 crore (including ~1 lakh tonnes of export orders worth Rs 850 crore, of which ~80,000 tonnes are API pipes)
- **Wire & Cable Sales:** Rs 95-100 crore in H1 (targeting Rs 250 crore by March)

### Highlights
- Delivered best-ever second quarter for both Lighting & Consumer Durables and Steel Pipes & Strips.
- Steel export volumes rose 64% YoY to ~65,000 tonnes, with US volumes more than doubling sequentially.
- Value-added products contributed ~48% of total steel volumes; hollow sections grew 35% as rising steel prices led distributors to restock.
- Net cash surplus stood at Rs 75 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track for FY27 lighting guidance of 22%-23% value growth and ~25% volume growth (~Rs 2,200 crore revenue and ~Rs 200 crore EBITDA).
- Targeting FY28 value growth of ~20% and ~15% CAGR through FY30 in lighting, with EBITDA margins of ~9% to 9.5%.
- Steel volume target for full year FY27: crossed 11 lakh tonnes (with H2 expected to deliver ~6 lakh tonnes).
- Steel EBITDA per tonne expected to improve to Rs 4,800-Rs 4,900 in Q3 and exceed Rs 5,000 in Q4, averaging ~Rs 4,600-Rs 4,650 for the full year.
- Steel volume target for FY28: ~14 lakh tonnes at an EBITDA per tonne of Rs 4,600 to Rs 4,800, scaling to ~2 million tonnes by FY2030.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic oil and gas tendering revived after a prolonged lull; domestic API order book grew to ~20,000 metric tonnes.
- Section square pipe demand grew 18-20% across India, though facing pricing pressure.
- Lighting industry experiencing price erosion and conventional-to-LED transitions, but company distribution reach and in-house manufacturing provided an advantage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel EBITDA per ton impacted by an increase of ~Rs 1,700 per ton in sea freight and fixed-price export/API contracts; price increases will reflect in Q3.
- Lighting margins impacted by higher input costs across copper, aluminium, steel, and petroleum-based materials, plus a Rs 3-3.5 crore EPR liability and Rs 4-5 crore higher publicity expenses for wires.
- In-house manufacturing (80% for lighting) and advance material pre-bookings helped absorb cost pressures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY27 total capex planned at Rs 150-155 crore (Rs 50 crore incurred by October, including DFT mill commissioning in Bhuj, Bahadurgarh, and Gwalior).
- Hindupur Phase 2 investment of Rs 50-55 crore to be completed by March 2027, with an additional Rs 75 crore planned next year.
- Greenfield plan of Rs 250-300 crore (including land) to add 3 to 3.5 lakh tonnes over 2 years, taking total capacity to over 2 million tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained zero-debt status with a cash surplus of Rs 75 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Net working capital cycle was 63 days.
- Paid Rs 60 crore in advance tax during H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global macroeconomic turmoil and turmoil across the world.
- Price sensitivity and pricing pressure in sections and wire/cable segments.
- Unorganized sector pressure in smaller pockets for lighting.
- Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) recurring annual liability of Rs 5-6 crore.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Keshav Garg asked about sustainable steel EBITDA per ton and share buybacks.** Management stated the normalized run-rate is ~Rs 5,000 per ton, and confirmed that a share buyback proposal will be taken to the Board for approval soon.
- **Saket Kapoor asked about the demerger status.** Management stated they respect shareholder sentiments and will discuss it with the Board before the next meeting.
- **Saket Kapoor asked about capex details for greenfield expansions.** Management clarified that an investment of Rs 250-300 crore (inclusive of land) will add 3.5 lakh tonnes of capacity.
- **Resham Jain asked about API pipe demand and margins.** Management noted that domestic tenders are reviving, and while coating material costs squeezed API EBITDA per ton down to ~Rs 8,000, it should settle in 2-3 months.
- **Shasank asked about the 70% increase in other expenses.** Management explained it was primarily driven by higher freight and publicity expenses.
- **Keshav Garg asked about the rationale behind expanding consumer durable SKUs (like coolers, kitchenware).** Management explained that products are chosen based on existing trade distribution overlap and that none of the products are loss-making.

### One-line takeaway
Surya Roshni delivered a record Q2 FY27 with consolidated revenue up 33% YoY and robust volume growth across steel and lighting, maintaining a zero-debt balance sheet and strong H2 execution visibility.
