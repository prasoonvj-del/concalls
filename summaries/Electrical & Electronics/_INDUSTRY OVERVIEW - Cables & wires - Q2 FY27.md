# Electrical & Electronics - Cables & wires: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Cables & Wires sector reflects strong underlying growth driven by an unprecedented domestic power transmission investment cycle, renewable energy evacuation, and data center demand. Most companies reported robust double-digit YoY revenue growth (ranging from 10% to over 100%), supported by healthy order books and capacity additions. A recurring theme across multiple players (such as **Apar Industries**, **Laser Power**, and **Lumino Industries**) is a strategic pivot toward high-value, specialized products—such as HTLS/AL-59 conductors, medium/extra-high-voltage (MV/EHV) cables, and data center wiring—which offset temporary volume or tonnage headwinds. Several companies (e.g., **Diamond Power**, **G V Electricals**, **Orient Cables**, and **Lumino Industries**) highlighted successful recent BSE/NSE listings, IPO-led debt reduction, and turnarounds from legacy distress. 

---

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
* **Power T&D & Renewables:** Universally strong demand across the board, fueled by grid modernization, DISCOM outsourcing, and massive renewable energy evacuation (solar parks like Khavda, Green Energy Corridors).
* **Data Centers & Specialized Infrastructure:** Emerging as a major structural growth driver, with companies reporting growing order inflows from hyperscalers and data center developers for specialized power and communication cables.

### Divergence
* **HTLS Reconductoring:** Views are split on the near-term prominence of High-Temperature Low-Sag (HTLS) conductors. **Diamond Power** stated it is not bullish on HTLS, estimating it will not exceed 5% of its top line and preferring underground MV/EHV cables. Conversely, **Apar Industries**, **Laser Power**, and **Lumino Industries** see surging demand for HTLS and AL-59 driven by Right-of-Way (RoW) constraints and grid modernization.
* **Exports vs. Domestic Momentum:** While **Finolex Cables**, **KEI Industries**, and **KSH International** report robust overseas demand (particularly in the US, Middle East, and Australia), others like **Dynamic Cables** and **Skipper Ltd** flagged near-term export headwinds, high sea freights, and transient shipment deferrals due to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.

---

## Costs, raw materials & margins

* **Commodity Volatility & Pass-Through:** Sharp volatility in copper, aluminum, and base oils kept gross margins dynamic. Most B2B players rely on price variation clauses (PVC) and inventory alignment (3–4 months) to protect absolute contribution per ton, though time lags occasionally squeeze percentage margins during sharp price drops.
* **Operating Leverage & Product Mix:** Margin expansion (where achieved) was driven primarily by operating leverage and richer product mixes favoring high-margin specialized cables over commodity wires.
* **EPC Drag:** Companies with legacy EPC businesses (**KEI Industries**, **Laser Power**) experienced margin pressures or small EBIT losses as they wind down or execute lower-margin EPC segments.

---

## Capex & capacity
* **Aggressive Capacity Expansion:** Capital expenditure remains robust across the sector. **KEI Industries** reported Rs 333 crore incurred in H1 for its Sanand facility; **Apar Industries** planned Rs 1,500 crore over 12–15 months (Rs 500 crore spent in H1); **KSH International** approved Phase-3 expansion to 72,200 MT by Q1 FY29 (outlay up to Rs 185 crore); and **Orient Cables** is expanding via a new 11-acre Bhiwadi facility.
* **Backward Integration:** Several firms are investing in backward integration to secure supply chains and enhance margins—notably **Diamond Power** investing Rs 170 crore in a polymer compounding complex and **Finolex Cables** expanding preform/fiber draw tower capacities.

---

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Upgrades:** **KEI Industries** upgraded its FY27 revenue growth guidance from 20% to 25%+.
* **Fine-Tuning:** **R R Kabel** fine-tuned its full-year volume guidance to 14–15% (from 16–18%) due to market volatility. **Skipper Ltd** noted a potential 1–2% downside to its 15% revenue target due to export shipment deferrals.
* **Execution Headwinds:** **Laser Power** highlighted heavy monsoon rains causing execution delays and deferring ~Rs 100 crore of EPC revenue.
* **New Watchpoints:** **Finolex Cables** explicitly flagged the entry of new deep-pocketed competitors like UltraTech into the market as a watchpoint for future pricing pressure.

---

## What to watch next quarter
* Ramp-up and commercial utilization of major capacity additions (e.g., Sanand Phase 2 for **KEI Industries**, ALCU and CTC plants for **Vidya Wires**, and Ranihati for **Lumino Industries**).
* Normalization of working capital and inventory levels following H1 inventory buildups ahead of H2 execution.
* Resolution of geopolitical and freight rate bottlenecks affecting export shipping timelines (**Skipper**, **Dynamic Cables**).
* Progress on FMEG breakeven targets (**R R Kabel**).

## Company one-liners
* **Apar Industries Ltd:** Strong order book and margin expansion driven by premiumization offset temporary volume headwinds, with management expecting a catch-up in H2 execution.
* **Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd:** Diamond Power reported its first Rs 1,000+ crore revenue quarter, successfully exited NCLT with a net cash balance sheet, and is scaling MV/EHV cables alongside backward integration into polymer compounding.
* **Dynamic Cables Ltd:** Dynamic Cables reported strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 31% YoY to INR 712 crore and launched its new manufacturing facility, maintaining its long-term 20% growth and 10%-11% EBITDA margin guidance despite near-term export headwinds.
* **Finolex Cables Ltd:** Finolex Cables posted a stellar Q2 with a 65% jump in revenue and doubled EBITDA, propelled by robust electrical cable demand and exceptional realization and volume growth in communication cables.
* **G V Electricals Ltd:** G V Electricals delivered a strong H1 FY27 with a 55% surge in PAT, an expanded order book of Rs 555.84 crore, and robust opportunities stemming from power sector privatization and data center outsourcing.
* **KEI Industries Ltd:** KEI Industries delivered strong top-line growth of 38.5% YoY in Q2 FY27, upgraded its FY27 growth guidance to 25%+, and remains well-positioned to expand capacity via its debt-free balance sheet.
* **KSH International Ltd:** KSH International delivered stellar Q2 FY27 results with record PAT, 26% volume growth, and a strong export surge, prompting a Phase-3 expansion to 72,200 metric tons by FY29 while guiding to a long-term sustainable EBITDA of Rs. 85,000/ton.
* **Laser Power & Infra Ltd:** Laser Power & Infra entered the high-voltage transmission market with maiden HTLS and 220 kV underground orders while weathering seasonal execution delays, setting up H2 for cash flow recovery and debt-led interest cost savings.
* **Lumino Industries Ltd:** Lumino Industries delivered a strong Q2 FY27 post-listing with 30% revenue growth, stable ~13% EBITDA margins, positive operating cash flow, and a robust order book of Rs 3,093 crore, supported by debt reduction from IPO proceeds and capacity expansion plans.
* **Orient Cables (India) Ltd:** Orient Cables delivered a robust H1 FY27 with triple-digit revenue and profit growth, backed by high capacity utilization, new plant expansions, and successful debt reduction from IPO proceeds.
* **R R Kabel Ltd:** R R Kabel delivered record H1 financial performance, and while management slightly moderated full-year volume guidance due to market volatility, they continue to pursue aggressive capex and strategic acquisitions to drive long-term growth.
* **Skipper Ltd:** Skipper is leveraging a strong balance sheet and diversified export order book to navigate near-term shipping headwinds while maintaining a robust long-term growth trajectory in the power transmission sector.
* **Vidya Wires Ltd:** Vidya Wires is transitioning from installation to sales management at its ALCU facility, with improving EBITDA per kg and upcoming CTC production expected to drive better performance in H2 FY27 and FY28.
