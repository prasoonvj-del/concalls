# Prostarm Info Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management acknowledged margin compression due to elevated freight/input costs and a shift in product mix, despite strong revenue growth and new capacity commissioning.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Cut EBITDA margin guidance; previously 12-13% for FY27, now management expects "above 11%".
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "seasonal moderation" to acknowledging "headwinds" in the utility-scale BESS segment; now prioritizing C&I (Commercial & Industrial) over utility-scale.
- **New topics/risks:** Freight costs have "almost doubled" YoY; forex volatility impacted bottom line; active discussions to "down sell" (divest) the two major BESS developer projects.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on commissioning the Jhajjar (BESS) and Bakrol (UPS) facilities (Sept 25, 2026).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 120 crore (82% YoY growth).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 196 crore (62% YoY growth).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 9 crore (17% YoY decline).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.83%.
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 16 crore (5% YoY growth).
- **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 8.06%.
- **PAT:** Rs 6 crore (27% YoY decline).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 11 crore (6% YoY growth).
- **H1 PAT Margin:** 5.46%.
- **Order Book:** Rs 1,121 crore (78% government, 22% private).
- **Working Capital Cycle:** 192 days (vs 185 days in FY26).

### Highlights
- **Capacity:** Commenced operations at 1.2 GWh BESS facility (Jhajjar, Haryana) and UPS assembly facility (Bakrol, Gujarat) on Sept 25, 2026.
- **Strategy:** Pivoting away from utility-scale BESS bidding toward selective C&I and special application tenders to improve margins.
- **Divestment:** Actively negotiating to "down sell" the two major BESS developer projects (total order value Rs 854 crore) to free up working capital.
- **Digital:** Successfully implemented SAP and Salesforce during the quarter.
- **Export:** Exported a sample home energy storage system to an international customer in Oct 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Targeting ~Rs 500 crore for FY27.
- **Margins:** Expects FY27 EBITDA margin >11%; FY28 EBITDA margin to normalize to 12-13%.
- **Growth:** Targeting minimum 25% revenue growth for FY28.
- **PAT Margin:** Expects 8.5-9% for FY28.
- **Capacity Utilization:** BESS facility utilization expected at 25-30% in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **BESS:** Utility-scale segment facing "headwinds" and margin pressure due to aggressive bidding and high project costs; C&I segment viewed as the primary growth driver.
- **Data Centers:** India's capacity expected to grow from ~1,150 MW (Dec 2024) to ~3,200 MW by FY30, driving UPS demand.
- **Policy:** Government focus on local manufacturing (VGF scheme, PLI) is expected to benefit domestic players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margin Pressure:** Impacted by higher contribution of low-margin third-party products, elevated freight costs (doubled YoY), forex fluctuations, and initial overheads from new facilities.
- **Inventory:** Deliberately increased inventory to Rs 120 crore (vs Rs 76-77 crore previously) to support BESS execution and mitigate supply chain lead times.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Jhajjar (BESS):** 1.2 GWh capacity (single shift), scalable to 3 GWh with three shifts.
- **Bakrol (UPS):** Capacity of 3,000 units/day; potential revenue of Rs 500-600 crore/year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Receivables:** Dropped from Rs 257 crore (H1) to Rs 201 crore in Oct 2026 following collections.
- **Debt:** Total debt expected to increase to support inventory and BESS facility working capital.
- **Cash:** Net debt position remains comfortable with Rs 97 crore in FDs against debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Margin Volatility:** Management admitted that the "new normal" for input costs is higher than previous years.
- **Execution Risk:** High working capital blockage due to inventory buildup and GST input credit (Rs 18.5-19 crore).
- **Project Viability:** Admitted margin depletion on BESS developer projects due to lithium/DC block cost increases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why the margin decline?** Management cited doubled freight costs, forex impact, and high overheads from new plants; expects improvement in H2.
- **Q: Status of BESS developer projects?** Management is in talks with 6-7 players to divest these projects to reduce leverage and focus on manufacturing.
- **Q: Is the margin decline structural?** Management termed it temporary, driven by the current phase of settling new facilities and market conditions.
- **Q: Can you maintain 25% growth?** Management reiterated 25% as the minimum growth target for FY28, driven by the BESS segment.
- **Q: Why is operating cash flow negative?** Attributed to deliberate inventory buildup and capex; expects improvement by year-end.

### One-line takeaway
Prostarm is prioritizing manufacturing scale-up and C&I market penetration to offset utility-scale BESS headwinds, while actively seeking to divest capital-heavy developer projects to stabilize margins and cash flows.
