# Aditya Infotech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Recorded strongest-ever H1 performance with 89.9% YoY Q2 revenue growth, turned debt-free post-QIP, and raised FY27 full-year revenue and PAT guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised FY27 revenue guidance to Rs 6,500 crore – Rs 6,800 crore (from Rs 6,000 crore – Rs 6,500 crore) and PAT margin to 9% – 10% (from 8.5% – 9.5%); maintained EBITDA margin guidance at 14% – 15%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains aggressive on acquiring market share (guiding 43%–45% by year-end vs 43.3% in FY26); highlighted that H1 YoY growth (89.7%) was helped by a soft base from STQC backlogs last year, and growth will normalize in H2 on a standard base.
- **New topics or risks:** Suffered a short-circuit fire incident at the Kadapa plant in Sept 2026 leading to a Rs 34.4 crore exceptional loss (fully covered by insurance); raised Rs 1,500 crore via QIP in Sept 2026; repaid all debt in Oct 2026; agreed to acquire a 30% stake in BLUE KGraph AI Solutions for Rs 45.4 crore.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Completed the QIP (raised Rs 1,500 crore); acquired 22 acres for Kadapa Phase 3 and secured land in Greater Noida; housing/enclosure plant trials scheduled for Q3 FY27 with commercial production in Q4 FY27 (slight shift from Q3 operational expectation).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,746 crore (up 89.9% YoY, up 24.5% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 307 crore (up 177% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17.6% (vs 14.8% in Q1 FY27)
- **Gross Margin:** 30% (flat YoY, vs 30.8% in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT (pre-exceptional):** Rs 220 crore (up 214% YoY)
- **Reported PAT:** Rs 185.5 crore (after Rs 34.4 crore exceptional loss)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 3,148 crore (up 89.7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 515 crore (margin 16.3%)
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted PAT:** Rs 362 crore (up 252% YoY)
- **Market Share:** ~42% (for 12 months to Sept 2026, up from 30% at IPO)
- **CP PLUS Segment Share:** Contributed 88% of Q2 revenue (vs 86% YoY); IP products reached 79% of CP PLUS sales (vs 71% YoY)
- **Cash & Debt:** Cash & cash equivalents of Rs 1,434 crore vs borrowings of Rs 363 crore as of Sept 30, 2026 (all borrowings repaid in Oct 2026)

### Highlights
- Completed a Rs 1,500 crore QIP in September 2026 and paid off all debt in October 2026 to become net debt-free.
- Signed agreements to invest Rs 45.4 crore for a 30% stake in BLUE KGraph AI Solutions to integrate video compression technology that cuts storage/bandwidth needs by up to 90%.
- Brand portfolio expansion: NEXIVUE introduced in April 2026; EYRA launching in late November/December 2026; CP PLUS PRO series launching in Q4 FY27.
- Signed Bollywood actor Ranveer Singh as brand ambassador for a national campaign alongside cricket and airport branding.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Raised to Rs 6,500 crore – Rs 6,800 crore (54% to 61% YoY growth).
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Maintained at 14% – 15%.
- **FY27 PAT Margin Guidance:** Raised to 9% – 10%.
- **Market Share Guidance:** Expected to reach 43% – 45% by FY27 end.
- Management expects H2 FY27 EBITDA margins to drop below Q2's 17.6% level as lower-cost inventory depletes and raw material cost increases are passed on gradually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Video surveillance market unit CAGR projected at 18% – 20% over a 3-to-5-year horizon.
- Strictly enforced government cybersecurity standards (STQC) continue to favor compliant Indian players and restrict land-border supply chains. Over 40+ brands are currently STQC certified.
- Shift towards AI-driven, intelligent security systems, cloud storage, and solutions across enterprise and consumer segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Prices for critical semiconductor inputs—SOC, DDR, and flash memory—have escalated sharply across the industry.
- Strategic pre-buying of SOCs and memory protected H1 gross margins (30.4% in H1, up 374 bps YoY) and secured supply continuity.
- Input price increases are being passed through gradually to avoid channel disruption and to gain market share.
- Localizing 4 component categories: lenses (2 million/month plant planned in Greater Noida), housing enclosures (Kadapa trials Q3, commercial production Q4), cables (live in Q4), and IoT modules.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex plan of ~Rs 650 crore over 18–24 months to double total capacity by late FY29 to early FY30.
- Kadapa Phase 3 expansion underway following acquisition of an additional 22 acres of land.
- Greater Noida greenfield site land acquired; civil construction starts in Q4 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables improved to 87 days in Q2 FY27 (vs 93 days in Q1 FY27 and 105 days YoY).
- Inventory remains elevated due to deliberate strategic stocking of critical memory and SOC components.
- Finance costs fell 41% YoY in H1 FY27; debt repayment in October will eliminate interest expenses in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued cost inflation in semiconductor components (SOC, DDR, flash memory).
- Potential margin compression in H2 FY27 due to delayed pass-through of raw material cost hikes.
- Exceptional loss of Rs 34.4 crore due to a fire caused by a short circuit at the Kadapa plant in September 2026.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhruv Jain (Ambit Capital) asked why H2 EBITDA margins calculate to ~13% despite backward integration benefits.**  
   Management responded that backward integration benefits (cables, housing) will only start in FY28 as plants go live in Q4 FY27. Maintained full-year EBITDA guidance at 14%–15% to retain a margin cushion for component cost volatility and market share push.
2. **Anuj Kashyap asked if land-border/Chinese competitors have applied for STQC certification.**  
   Management stated that no land-border company or product has received live STQC certification under current government trusted-supply-chain guidelines.
3. **Nikhil asked if price hikes led to market pushback or lower demand.**  
   Management explained that price hikes are deliberately staggered to prevent channel shock and execute a competition-crushing market share expansion strategy.
4. **Rahul Agarwal asked about medium-term unit growth, capex layout, and localization benefits.**  
   Management expects 18%–20% unit CAGR over 3–5 years, confirmed a Rs 650 crore capex commitment to double capacity by late FY29/early FY30, and confirmed localization will structurally improve EBITDA margins over time.

### One-line takeaway
Aditya Infotech delivered exceptional H1 performance, wiped out debt post a Rs 1,500 crore QIP, and raised FY27 top-line guidance while keeping H2 margin cushions to aggressively expand market share.
