# Saatvik Green Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sequential execution caught up with the order book, and commercial production commenced at the new Odisha facility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 revenue guidance of ~Rs 6,000 crores with an EBITDA margin of ~12% (noted as FY27 during call, previously stated for FY27).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to positive as sequential execution improved and commercial production at Odisha commenced.
- New topics or risks: EcoVadis Bronze medal, Golden Peacock and Prithvi Awards 2026; UDAY Plus hybrid inverter, lithium batteries, and e-commerce channel expansion; China export control announcements on cell/wafer technology transfer.
- Last quarter's promises: Odisha Phase 1 module capacity commercial production commenced on November 2, 2026 (target met). Cell line 2.4 GW moved to trial runs (on track for December 2026 ramp-up).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 902.6 crore (INR 9,026 million), up 76.83% QoQ from Rs 511.0 crore (INR 5,110 million) in Q1 FY27 and up 17.53% YoY from Rs 768.0 crore (INR 7,680 million) in Q2 FY26.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 69.6 crore (INR 696 million), up 63.76% QoQ from Rs 42.5 crore (INR 425 million) in Q1 FY27, and down 44.89% YoY from Rs 126.3 crore (INR 1,263 million) in Q2 FY26.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.7%, down 60 bps QoQ from 8.3% in Q1 FY27 and down 875 bps YoY from 16.45% in Q2 FY26.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 20.2 crore (INR 202 million), up 274.07% QoQ from Rs 5.4 crore (INR 54 million) in Q1 FY27, and down 76.15% YoY from Rs 84.7 crore (INR 847 million) in Q2 FY26.
- **Production Volume:** 658 MW vs 408 MW in Q1 FY27 and 792 MW in Q2 FY26.
- **Sales Volume:** 640 MW vs 334 MW in Q1 FY27 and 538 MW in Q2 FY26.
- **Average Realization:** INR 13.49 per watt-peak in Q2 FY27 and INR 13.89 per watt-peak for H1 FY27.
- **Effective Capacity Utilization:** 58.5% in Q2 FY27 and 47.4% for H1 FY27.
- **Confirmed Order Book:** 6.68 GW as of September 30, 2026 (~110% of installed module capacity of 6.1 GW).
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 1.37x as of September 30, 2026, vs 0.99x in June 2026 and 0.44x a year ago.
- **Capital Expenditure:** Rs 500.1 crore (INR 5,001 million) in H1 FY27.
- **Capital Work in Progress:** Rs 884.2 crore (INR 8,842 million) as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
- Commercial production at the 1.3 GW module manufacturing facility in Gopalpur (Odisha) commenced successfully on November 2, 2026; full 4 GW capacity expected by December 2026, taking total operational module capacity to 8.8 GW.
- The 2.4 GW cell manufacturing line moved to trial runs; production ramp-up targeted by December 2026. ALMM List-II inspection called for and planned within November 2026.
- Phase 2 cell expansion (3.6 GW) initiated, targeting a total cell capacity of 6 GW by FY28. Phase 3 planning initiated for 6 GW ingot and wafer capacity by FY29.
- Added 970 MW of new orders in Q2 FY27, taking the confirmed order book to 6.68 GW (breakup: ~60% DCR, ~40% non-DCR; revenue breakup: ~52%-55% utility, ~28% C&I, ~12% retail, ~3% exports, ~4% other).
- Received EcoVadis Bronze medal (79th percentile globally), Golden Peacock Occupational Health and Safety Award 2026, and Prithvi Award 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reaffirmed FY27 guidance: Revenue of ~Rs 6,000 crores with an EBITDA margin of ~12%.
- Expects cell integration benefits to reflect partially in Q3 and more fully in Q4 FY27, with full-year optimization next year.
- Cell capacity utilization target: Above 90%. Module utilization expected to return to the 80s next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India added ~20.8 GW of solar capacity in H1 FY27 after 44.6 GW in FY26 (+87% YoY); non-fossil capacity at ~238 GW in March 2026 against 500 GW by 2030 target.
- Annual solar demand projected to rise from ~50 GW in FY27 to ~85 GW by FY30.
- PM Surya Ghar crossed 50 lakh households; PM-KUSUM projects working toward a March 2027 completion deadline; C&I open access opportunity estimated at 20 GW - 25 GW.
- ALMM List-II for solar cells came into force on June 1, 2026 (with limited exemptions up to December 31, 2026). ALMM List-III for ingots/wafers applies from June 2028.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sequential revenue growth (+77%) drove higher EBITDA and PAT compared to Q1 FY27.
- Sourcing wafers for the Odisha cell facility via long-term contracts with tier-one suppliers in China.
- Non-solar panel/ancillary businesses contributed INR 40.8 crore in Q2 FY27 (INR 63.4 crore in H1 FY27), targeted to reach 15% of revenue over the next two years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Odisha Phase 1 (57 acres): 2.4 GW cell + 4.0 GW module manufacturing. 1.3 GW module live, full 4 GW by December 2026; 2.4 GW cell trial runs underway.
- Odisha Phase 2 (27 acres): 3.6 GW additional cell capacity (total 6 GW) targeted for FY28.
- Odisha Phase 3: 6 GW ingot and wafer manufacturing targeted for FY29.
- Encapsulants capacity: 2 GW live, expanding by 3 GW to reach 5 GW by FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventories stood at Rs 907.4 crore (INR 9,074 million) as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 701.6 crore in March 2026) due to stock build-up ahead of Odisha operations.
- Receivables decreased to Rs 485.5 crore (INR 4,855 million) from Rs 698.8 crore in March 2026. Trade payables were Rs 470.5 crore (INR 4,705 million).
- Leverage increased due to the Odisha capital build-out phase.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- China's announcement regarding export controls on cell and wafer technology transfer (management noted it is being watched closely, alternative equipment sources exist in Germany, and it should not derail projects).
- Rising debt-to-equity ratio (1.37x) during the capex build phase.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Abhishek, Motilal Oswal):** What are current price realizations for DCR and non-DCR across segments?  
  **A (Management):** TOPCon utility prices are around INR 20–INR 21 per watt-peak. Non-DCR prices are around INR 12 to INR 13 per watt-peak.
- **Q (Abhishek, Motilal Oswal):** Does the China export control on cell/wafer technology transfer impact Phase 2?  
  **A (Management):** Not worried; alternative equipment sources exist outside China (e.g., Germany), and it should not derail plans as Phase 1 is ready and Phase 2 tie-ups are in place.
- **Q (Jainam Jain, IIFL Capital):** Can you elaborate on the SuryaConnect kit business dynamics, dealer network, and competition?  
  **A (Management):** Launched as a turnkey B2C solution for residential rooftop customers under PM Surya Ghar. Backed by a growing distribution network of 85–90 distributors/resellers across India, targeting district- and village-level penetration.
- **Q (Myra Mittal, Individual Investor):** What is the strategy and revenue/profitability contribution expected from non-module businesses (EPC, pumps, BESS)?  
  **A (Management):** Allied products like inverters and transformers increase project share from 50%-60% to 70%-80%. Non-module share is currently ~4% and targeted to reach 15% of revenue in two years.
- **Q (Nimish Pandya, NP Investments):** What level of margin improvement is expected from backward integration in Odisha, and when will it reflect?  
  **A (Management):** Benefits will start reflecting very partially in Q3, with real benefits from Q4 FY27 and full yield optimization next year. Margins will be in line with peers already in cell manufacturing.
- **Q (Nimish Pandya, NP Investments):** What is the current sourcing arrangement for wafers at the Odisha facility?  
  **A (Management):** Working on long-term contracts with tier-one suppliers in China for wafers, ahead of setting up internal 6 GW ingot/wafer manufacturing by June 2028.

### One-line takeaway
Saatvik's execution scaled up significantly in Q2 FY27 with a 77% sequential revenue jump and the commercial launch of its Odisha module facility, laying the groundwork for margin expansion via backward cell integration.
