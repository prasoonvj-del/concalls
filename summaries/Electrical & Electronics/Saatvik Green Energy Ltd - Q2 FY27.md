- Recent order wins: INR 476 cr (Vikran Engineering, Aug), ~INR 190 cr (Aug), INR 297.5 cr (Sep), INR 1,041 cr (SECI, Sep), INR 100 cr (MSEDCL, Oct).

- Sustainability: EcoVadis Bronze (79th percentile), Golden Peacock OH&S Award, Prithvi Award, IGBC Green Factory Silver, Brand of the Year.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY28 revenue ~INR 6,000 cr, EBITDA ~12% (corrected from initial FY27 mention).
- Cell utilisation target >90% in FY28; module utilisation target back to 80s% in FY28.
- EBITDA benefits from cell integration: partial in Q3, meaningful from Q4, full from FY28 Q1.
- Order book execution priority: 6.68 GW confirmed pipeline.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India added ~20.8 GW solar in H1 FY27 (vs 44.6 GW full FY26, +87% YoY). Non‑fossil capacity 238 GW vs 500 GW 2030 target.
- Annual solar demand expected 50 GW (FY27) to 85 GW (FY30).
- PM Surya Ghar: 57 lakh households solarized; targeting 75 lakh by Dec‑2026. PM‑KUSUM deadline Mar‑2027; next phase with agri‑PV being finalized.
- C&I open access opportunity 20‑25 GW.
- ALMM List‑II (cells) effective Jun‑2026; limited exemption till Dec‑2026; no blanket extension. List‑III (ingots/wafers) from Jun‑2028.
- Domestic cell capacity ~37 GW vs 200+ GW module capacity – integration becoming key differentiator.
- US Section 232 tariffs on polysilicon products from Dec‑4; India duties on cells/modules progressing. Exports only ~3% of revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin 7.7% (down from 16.4% YoY) due to module‑only manufacturing, commodity/FX volatility, and lack of cell integration.
- Captive cell production expected to bring margins in line with integrated peers (high‑double‑digit EBITDA on cell component).
- Wafer sourcing: long‑term contracts with Chinese tier‑1 suppliers; own 6 GW ingot/wafer by FY29.
- Encapsulant supply chain: 2 GW captive, expanding to 5 GW.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Odisha Phase‑1 (57 acres): 2.4 GW cell + 4 GW module (total capex ~INR 1,850 cr). Module 1.3 GW live; full 4 GW by Dec‑2026. Cell ramp‑up Dec‑2026.
- Phase‑2 (27 acres): 3.6 GW cell (capex ~INR 1,600‑1,700 cr); total cell 6 GW by FY28.
- Phase‑3: 6 GW ingot/wafer; completion FY29.
- Encapsulant: 2 GW → 5 GW by FY28.
- Module capacity: 4.8 GW (Ambala) + 4 GW (Odisha) = 8.8 GW by Dec‑2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt‑to‑equity 1.37x (Sep‑26) – up from 0.99x (Jun‑26) due to Odisha build‑out.
- Inventories INR 9,074 million (vs INR 7,016 million Mar‑26) – stocking for Odisha and order execution.
- Receivables INR 4,855 million (vs INR 6,988 million Mar‑26).
- Trade payables INR 4,705 million.
- Focus: commission on schedule, improve utilisation, manage balance sheet as new capacity generates cash.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- China export controls on cell/wafer tech (management assesses low impact).
- ALMM List‑II inspection delays could defer cell revenue.
- Fixed‑price contracts (~60% DCR, 40% non‑DCR) expose margins to raw material/FX swings.
- Leverage elevated during build phase; peak net debt expected INR 2,200‑2,400 cr (per prior call).
- Module price corrections and commodity volatility.
