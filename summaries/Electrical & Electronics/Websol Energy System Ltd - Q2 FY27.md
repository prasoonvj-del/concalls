# Websol Energy System Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever half-yearly revenue and operating profit supported by strong capacity utilization and becoming debt-free.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 361 crore (up 114.6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 734 crore (up 89.6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 254 crore (up 44.4% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 128 crore (up 77.3% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 80 crore (up 73.2% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Net Profit (PAT) growth:** 39.2% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Net Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 212 crore (vs Rs 132 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Q2 Cell Capacity Utilization:** 91% (producing >255 MW)
- **Q2 Module Capacity Utilization:** 68%
- **Outstanding Order Book:** Rs 1,280 crore as of end of Q2 FY27
- **Greenfield Phase 1 (2 GW) Project Cost:** Estimated at Rs 1,350 crore
- **TOPCon Upgrade Capex:** Approximately Rs 270 crore

### Highlights
- Became a debt-free company following the repayment of IREDA debt in August 2026.
- Received allotment of 54.20 acres at Falta Industrial Park for a proposed 4 GW integrated facility in September 2026 (broken pattern from past record: land allotment for the 4 GW plant is now officially secured and detailed in this call).
- Board approved entry into new verticals: EPC, solar independent power production (IPP), solar parks, and energy trading (including a 40% subscription to Bright Earth Cell Private Limited).
- Appointed Vinay as CEO of Websol Renewables to lead TOPCon manufacturing and power development projects.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to stabilize around 30% for the current financial year due to some moderation.
- TOPCon upgrade (converting 600 MW Mono PERC to 750 MW TOPCon) is targeted for commercialization by the end of FY27 (Q4 FY27).
- Greenfield 2 GW integrated facility trial runs expected to start by Q3 of FY27-28 (December 2027).
- Greenfield Phase 1 (Rs 1,350 crore) funding plan: Rs 400-500 crore from internal accruals and the rest via debt (targeting a 70:30 debt-to-equity sanction to preserve cash for subsequent phases and backward integration).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India added ~30.8 GW of solar capacity in the first 6 months of FY27; H1 CY27 saw India's highest-ever solar installation for a 6-month period.
- Demand driven by large solar parks, C&I users, rooftops, government programs, and storage/hybrid projects.
- Significant gap remains between cell and module capacities in India, benefiting domestic cell manufacturers. ALMM List II extension indicates domestic cell capacity is still catching up.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Moderation in silver prices and optimization of silver consumption supported cost management and blended margins.
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin stood at 35%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current capacity: 1.2 GW Mono PERC cell capacity and 550 MW module capacity.
- Post-upgrade (end of FY27): Cell capacity will reach 1.35 GW (600 MW Mono PERC + 750 MW TOPCon); module capacity remains 550 MW.
- Greenfield 4 GW integrated facility planned in two phases of 2 GW each. Wafer and ingot manufacturing will be housed at a separate facility (with timing linked to ALMM List III clarity, working towards June 2028).
- Equipment for TOPCon upgrade has been ordered from non-Chinese suppliers (German vendor Centrotherm/outside China) and is in transit; unaffected by Chinese technology export restrictions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stands at Rs 780 crore with zero net debt as of September 2026.
- H1 FY27 cash generated from operations was Rs 244 crore (net operating cash flow after tax at Rs 212 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Monitoring potential delays in ALMM List III mandate deadlines before committing capital to ingot-wafer backward integration.
- Margin moderation expected as new capacities ramp up and industry pricing evolves.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Virendra Verma (Implania Services) asked about the timeline and funding of the 4 GW West Bengal expansion.**
   - *Management answered* that Phase 1 (2 GW) will cost Rs 1,350 crore, funded via internal accruals and debt, with trial runs targeted for Q3 FY28 (December 2027).
2. **Jasmine Surana asked about capacity details and ALMM III / ingot-wafer plans.**
   - *Management answered* that current cell capacity is 1.2 GW (Mono PERC), moving to 1.35 GW (TOPCon/Mono PERC mix) by year-end, while ingot-wafer plans await clarity on ALMM List III deadlines (targeted around June 2028).
3. **Rahul Joshi asked about BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) plans.**
   - *Management answered* that they find BESS a good complement and are evaluating the opportunity, but have no concrete plans to share yet.
4. **Geetarth Tandon asked about land allocation usage for ingots/wafers.**
   - *Management clarified* that the 54.2-acre land is strictly for the integrated TOPCon facility, and wafers/ingots will be set up at a different facility.
5. **Gaurav Singh asked about Chinese restrictions on TOPCon technology transfer.**
   - *Management answered* that they are not using turnkey Chinese lines and their equipment suppliers (such as for furnaces) are outside China (e.g., German suppliers), so there is no impact.
6. **Amit Mishra asked about machinery procurement and pricing realization for TOPCon vs. Mono PERC.**
   - *Management answered* that advances are paid, equipment is shipped, civil work is ongoing, and TOPCon cell realizations are higher than Mono PERC by roughly 1 cent.

### One-line takeaway
Websol Energy delivered a record H1 FY27, turning debt-free with robust cash flows while aggressively advancing its 750 MW TOPCon upgrade and planning a phased 4 GW greenfield expansion.
