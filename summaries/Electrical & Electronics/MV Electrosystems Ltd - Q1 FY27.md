# MV Electrosystems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-01 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by a strong order book of over Rs 1,000 crore, a successful IPO for working capital, and upcoming production scale-up.

### Key numbers
- Order book: Rs 1,000+ crore (including ~Rs 989 crore for locomotive propulsion systems [564 units] and an Rs 86 crore PO for EMU propulsion).
- Target revenue for FY27: Rs 400 crore.
- Production capacity/ramp-up target: 10 propulsion systems in September 2026, scaling up to 25/month in November, and 40/month by January 2027 (maximum capacity up to 55/month).
- Expected PAT margin at design run-rate: 10% plus.
- Annual R&D expenses: ~Rs 9 crore (3% to 4% of total revenues).
- Business cycle / payment cycle: Total cycle from procurement to payment received is within 85 days based on recent dispatches (prospectus mentions 105 days; raw material procurement cycle is ~45 days).
- Ticket sizes: 12-coach EMU train electrics roughly Rs 16 crore; 16-coach Vande Bharat train propulsion and electrics roughly Rs 40 crore; locomotive propulsion system value Rs 1.67 crore (excluding AMC).
- OHE fault locator pricing: Sectioning point automatic fault locator at Rs 12 lakh, sectioning point automatic fault locator at Rs 24 lakh, substation transformer automatic fault locator at Rs 36 lakh (~Rs 1 crore total per substation section).

### Highlights
- First investor meeting following the company's IPO and listing.
- R&D center recognized by the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR) for indigenous IPR of propulsion systems.
- Unit 2 setup is complete, with box fabrication and assembly commencing the week of the call.
- Tied up with South Korean company PNC Technologies for automatic fault locators (AFL) and Hansung Motors for traction motors.
- Decided not to participate in the InnoTrans exhibition in Germany due to domestic scale-up burdens.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a revenue of Rs 400 crore for FY27.
- Expects to reach a stable production run-rate of 40 propulsion systems per month by January 2027.
- Expects 10% plus PAT margins once the scale-up and stabilization are achieved.
- Anticipates continuous order inflows from upcoming tenders by CLW, BLW, and PLW.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways manufactures ~1,600 locomotives per year alongside a total population of ~15,000 locomotives requiring rehabilitation.
- Growing demand for EMUs, MEMUs, Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat trains, Metros, and RRTS corridors.
- Long-term growth drivers include the modernization and doubling of rail electrification (1x25 kV to 2x25 kV lines).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material procurement lead times vary from 7 days to 6 months (average financial calculation of 45 days).
- Bulk purchasing (e.g., 200-300 sets) helps manage global price changes.
- Initial months will see constrained margins due to low production scale, but margins will improve month-over-month.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No further capex is required for machinery or production infrastructure.
- Additional capex is limited to testing machines/setups (three ordered, installation in 45-60 days).
- Manufacturing facilities can operate up to two shifts.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds were primarily raised for working capital to scale up operations and manufacturing.
- Payments from Indian Railways have historically been received within 15 days of dispatch.
- Working capital requirement is estimated at ~Rs 200 crore to support a Rs 1,000 crore turnover (at 40 units/month).
- R&D expenditure is expensed out in the P&L and not capitalized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Initial scale-up phase may result in thin or negative margins in the first few months.
- Complex supply chain with thousands of part numbers and varying import lead times.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinay Chaudhary (Invexa Capital) on supply chain and raw materials:** Management clarified that raw material procurement lead times average 45 days, with the total financial cycle much shorter than the prospectus estimate of 105 days based on recent 85-day completions.
2. **Ayush Agarwal (MAPL Value Investing Fund) on execution deadlines and margins:** Management stated that deliveries span into next year, production will scale to 40 units/month by January, and 10%+ PAT margins are expected at full run-rate.
3. **Pritesh Jain on MEMU developmental order (Rs 86 crore):** Management stated the delivery timeline is 24 months, with internal design and development targeted within 8 to 10 months ahead of RDSO approval.
4. **Vinay Maheshwari (IGEs Family Office) on capacity constraints beyond FY29:** Management noted capacity is built for 55 units/month, but they will operate at a stable 40 units/month to match consistent demand, utilizing extra capacity for EMU propulsion.
5. **Tushar Bohra on OHE fault locator potential:** Management broke down the pricing per piece (Rs 12 lakh to Rs 36 lakh) and noted a total revenue potential of ~Rs 1 crore per substation section over a 5 to 7-year rollout.

### One-line takeaway
MV Electrosystems is rapidly scaling up its indigenous rail propulsion and electrification manufacturing to execute its Rs 1,000+ crore order book, targeting Rs 400 crore revenue in FY27 with 10% plus PAT margins at full run-rate.
