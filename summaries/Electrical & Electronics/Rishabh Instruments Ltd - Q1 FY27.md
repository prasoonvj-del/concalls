# Rishabh Instruments Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance in the EEI segment and robust order bookings offset the planned decline in the Alucast segment.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 1,983 million (+4.2% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 333 million (+17.3% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 16.8%.
*   **Consolidated PAT:** INR 194 million (-1.4% YoY).
*   **EEI Segment Revenue Growth:** 34% YoY.
*   **EEI Segment EBITDA Margin:** ~24%.
*   **Lumel S.A. Revenue:** INR 639 million (+39% YoY).
*   **Lumel Alucast Revenue:** INR 443 million (-41.2% YoY).
*   **Net Cash:** INR 1,606 million.

### Highlights
*   **EEI Segment:** Remains the primary growth engine, with adjusted EBITDA margins expanding 520 bps YoY to 24.8%.
*   **Lumel S.A.:** Delivered strong growth despite a subdued European industrial environment, supported by product diversification and new customer wins.
*   **Solar Inverters:** Achieved operational profitability; launched 3-phase iNEO models (up to 12 kW) and developing hybrid solutions.
*   **Lumel Alucast:** Currently at operating breakeven; management expects full-year adjusted EBITDA breakeven by end of FY27.
*   **Infrastructure:** Partially commissioned the new Nashik manufacturing facility to support future capacity expansion.
*   **Market Expansion:** Opened the first Rishabh TMI Experience Center; plans to open centers in Mumbai and Delhi by year-end.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **EEI Segment:** Management maintains guidance of ~20% top-line growth and 20-22% EBITDA margin for the year.
*   **U.S. Market:** Targeting INR 100 crore revenue within 2-3 years.
*   **Solar Inverters:** Targeting INR 250-300 crore revenue next year.
*   **Lumel Alucast:** Management expects to restore double-digit EBITDA margins over the medium term.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Data Centers/AI:** Strong traction in the domestic market; won 3-4 data center orders with 10 more in the pipeline.
*   **Current Transformers:** Global demand rising due to AI and energy meter requirements; capacity being doubled.
*   **Europe:** Growth driven by energy grid upgrades (Germany) and increased security needs (radiation gates).
*   **Domestic:** Order bookings up 20% YoY.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Includes a INR 20 million provision for ESOPs and stock appreciation rights.
*   **PAT Impact:** Consolidated PAT declined 1.4% YoY, primarily due to a INR 20 million increase in depreciation.
*   **Lumel Alucast:** Adjusted EBITDA margin at -6.4% (negative INR 28 million).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Manufacturing:** New Nashik facility aims to increase production capacity by >2.5x over 4-5 years.
*   **Current Transformers:** Capacity being expanded from 5,000-6,000 units/day to 8,000-10,000 units/day.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Management is exploring acquisition opportunities in the INR 50-200 crore range, though no specific targets are finalized.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Cash:** The company remains net debt-free with INR 1,606 million in cash and cash equivalents.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro/Geopolitical:** Ongoing risks from geopolitical uncertainties, tariff developments, and supply chain volatility.
*   **Seasonality:** Management noted potential impact from holiday seasons in Europe (July/August/December/January) and India (Diwali).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital):** Asked about EEI growth sustainability. Management attributed it to "multiple engines" (data centers, solar, global grid upgrades) and confirmed guidance remains conservative.
*   **Dhwanil Desai:** Asked about U.S. scaling. Management confirmed an organic target of INR 100 crore in 2-3 years and stated they are open to inorganic opportunities.
*   **Zaki Nasser (Investor):** Asked about AI/Data center traction. Management confirmed winning 3-4 data center orders with 10 more in the pipeline.
*   **Kiran (TableTree Capital):** Asked about Lumel S.A. order concentration. Management clarified the EUR 3 million order is ongoing and contributes ~20-25% of sales, but is diversified by other revenue streams.
*   **Mulesh Savla (Shah & Savla):** Asked about PCB/CT capacity. Management confirmed doubling CT capacity and utilizing SMT lines for high-end PCBA.

### One-line takeaway
Strong EEI performance and global diversification offset the planned decline in Lumel Alucast, with management maintaining conservative guidance despite a robust start to the year.
