# HPL Electric & Power Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and business momentum, though margins were impacted by input costs and geopolitical disruptions.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 515 crore, up 35% YoY (highest-ever first-quarter revenue).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 63 crore, up 9% YoY.
- **EBITDA margin:** 12.26% (moderated due to input cost volatility and revenue mix).
- **PAT:** Rs 19 crore.
- **Cash profit:** Increased by 21% YoY.
- **Consumer and Industrial (C&I) revenue:** Rs 278 crore, up 55% YoY (contributing ~54% of revenue).
- **Wire and cable revenue:** Rs 146 crore, up 79% YoY.
- **Lighting revenue:** Rs 56 crore, up 78% YoY.
- **Industrial switch gear revenue:** Up 19% YoY.
- **Metering and systems revenue:** Rs 234 crore, up ~17% YoY.
- **Order book:** Rs 3,200 crore as of August 7, 2026 (metering and systems account for >96%).
- **Dealer/Retailer reach:** >900 authorized dealers and >85,000 retailers.
- **Minimum wage hike impact:** Haryana minimum wages increased by ~40% in May.

### Highlights
- C&I segment achieved its highest-ever quarterly revenue, driven by broader product participation across wires and cables, lighting, and switch gears.
- Launched a new generation switch range called "Cairo."
- Metering business entered a stable, execution-led phase.
- Maintained flat other expenses despite a 35% increase in the top line.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the next two quarters to be good for the C&I segment with continued growth and channel expansion.
- Management remains optimistic about smart metering growth for the next 1-2 years.
- Management expects C&I margins to potentially improve by Q3 as price hikes are passed on.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand for wires and cables is diversified across builders, industrial OEMs, solar, telecom, retail, and institutional customers.
- Smart metering market is transitioning into a stable execution-led phase with consistent deployment.
- Roughly 7 crore smart meters have been installed nationwide to date out of the initial 25 crore target.
- Data centers are viewed as a major long-term growth segment (5-10 years); HPL plans to introduce DECA/international-certified cables for data centers by May-June next year.
- Exploring water meters (already launched, awaiting approvals/stabilization) and potentially gas meters in the future.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins and EBITDA margins compressed due to raw material inflation (metals and industrial plastics driven by crude/West Asia geopolitical disruptions starting February).
- Metering margins dropped ~50% this quarter due to sudden price spikes in industrial plastics and high metals (copper, aluminum).
- Minimum wage hikes (e.g., ~40% in Haryana in May) increased manpower costs during Q1.
- Mitigation actions: Initiated pricing actions, product mix changes, design changes, and use of alternate materials. Price hikes implemented in wires and cables and select lighting products.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Higher depreciation reflects increased investments in capacity over the past 2-3 years.
- Commissioned 7-8 new high-level automation machines (e.g., MCB machine doing the work of 44 workers, producing 18,000 MCBs) with an ROI of 3-4 years.
- Future metering capex expected to be primarily maintenance-focused (tools, dies).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Finance costs declined in absolute terms over the last three quarters.
- Other expenses remained flat despite a 35% top-line growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts in West Asia causing volatility in metals and industrial plastics.
- Time lag in passing on raw material price increases to fixed-price smart meter contracts.
- Execution speed of AMISPs can impact smart metering sales pacing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Viraj Mahadevia (Marquee equity investor / analyst) asked about gross margin compression from 38% to 30% and if hedging is used.**
   - *Management reply:* Stated that raw material inflation from geopolitical issues hit margins, but critical active/passive components (like semiconductors) are booked in advance. Price pass-throughs in meters happen with a lag in new orders.
2. **Kunal Dubey asked about data center cable demand and margin outlook.**
   - *Management reply:* Confirmed plans to launch DECA-certified data center cables by May-June next year. On margins, stated Q1 levels act as a baseline, and they expect improvements by Q3.
3. **Chandresh Chamalpani asked about the impact of Adani acquiring Intellisensor/Intellispart and a meter manufacturing OEM.**
   - *Management reply:* Welcomed the Intellispert acquisition as a consolidation of HPL's position as an approved/preferred vendor. Declined to comment on speculative OEM acquisition news.
4. **Viraj Mahadevia asked about the runway for smart metering (Smart Meter 2.0 and exports).**
   - *Management reply:* Viewed smart metering as a 10-15 year long-term story, followed by 2.0 replacement cycles. Noted recent approval from a Middle East utility for HPL meters, opening international export opportunities.
5. **Dia asked if margins can return to 16-17% if the Middle East situation eases.**
   - *Management reply:* Confirmed that if geopolitical disruptions and crude prices ease, margins can recover and grow, aided by C&I volume growth and automation offsetting wage inflation.

### One-line takeaway
HPL Electric delivered its highest-ever Q1 revenue (up 35% YoY) driven by robust C&I and steady metering growth, though profitability was temporarily compressed by geopolitical raw material inflation and wage hikes.
