# Rishabh Instruments Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance and margin expansion in the core EEI segment offset the planned contraction in the aluminum die-casting business.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained EEI top-line growth guidance at 20% to 25% and HPDC EBITDA breakeven target.
- Tone and demand view: Maintained a positive stance on core EEI and international markets, while noting emerging supply lead times and commodity price volatility.
- New topics or risks: Highlighted component availability issues, rising lead times, and volatility in commodity prices (polycarbonate, copper, silver) due to geopolitics.
- Last quarter's promises: Nashik facility continues its disciplined ramp-up and utilization following partial commissioning.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2128 crore (Q2 FY27, up 8.4% YoY from Rs 1963 crore); Rs 4111 crore (H1 FY27, up 6.3% YoY from Rs 3867 crore).
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 15.2% (Q2 FY27); 16% (H1 FY27).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 181 crore (Q2 FY27, down 18% YoY).
- **EEI Segment Revenue:** Rs 3255 crore (H1 FY27, up 29.3% YoY); EEI Q2 growth was 24.9% YoY.
- **EEI Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 750 crore (H1 FY27, up 29.9% YoY); Q2 adjusted EBITDA margin was ~21.1%.
- **Lumel SA Revenue:** Rs 1338 crore (H1 FY27, up 24.8% YoY) with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 21.9%.
- **Lumel Alucast Revenue:** Rs 855 crore (H1 FY27, down 36.6% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA negative at Rs 32 crore.
- **Net Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 1698 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
- **EEI Segment Driver:** Contributed 79.2% of consolidated revenue in H1 FY27, remaining the core growth and profitability engine.
- **International Growth:** U.S. revenue doubled in H1, while U.K. and China grew by 38% and 25% respectively.
- **Solar Inverters:** Single-phase iUNO range (1 to 5 kW) achieved operational profitability; commissioned a new production line of 400 inverter units/day (~12,000/month). Developed 3-phase iNEO models up to 12 kW, with 50 kW and hybrid solutions under development.
- **Lumel Alucast (HPDC):** Revenue contraction was deliberate as part of the transformation plan to prioritize profitable business over volume; expected PAT level loss of Rs 10 crore to Rs 15 crore by year-end due to depreciation and withholding tax provisions.

### Guidance & outlook
- **EEI Segment:** Management guides for 20% to 25% top-line growth.
- **EEI Full Year Guidance:** Rs 140 crore to Rs 150 crore adjusted EBITDA and Rs 95 crore to Rs 100 crore PAT.
- **Lumel Alucast:** Expected to remain at operating EBITDA breakeven (+/- 1% to 2%) for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's economic momentum remains robust with infrastructure, manufacturing, and digitalization driving structural growth.
- AI and digital infrastructure expansion are driving demand for data centers, creating strong opportunities for electrical measurement, low-voltage current transformers, power quality, and energy monitoring.
- European industrial demand remains uneven, but energy efficiency, automation, and grid modernization continue to create opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins were pressured by geopolitical tensions in West Asia impacting energy, freight, insurance costs, and input materials (polycarbonate, copper, CRGO steel, PCB material, silver).
- Q2 consolidated PAT was impacted by a Rs 13 million increase in depreciation (Nashik building capitalization), lower other income, an ESOP/SAR provision of Rs 16 million, and a withholding tax provision of Rs 25 million at Lumel Alucast.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Nashik facility capacity expansion is underway with a near-term focus on disciplined ramp-up, higher utilization, and process improvements.
- Incremental capital allocation for SMT lines and building utilization is expected around Rs 10 crore to Rs 20 crore annually.
- Actively looking for strategic inorganic growth opportunities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company remains net debt-free with net cash and cash equivalents of Rs 1698 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility affecting energy, freight, insurance, and input material availability and pricing.
- Increasing electronic component lead times and supply constraints.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rahul Jain (Credence Wealth):** Asked about EEI segment growth sustainability and margin dips. Management stated that growth remains strong and reiterated the 20%-25% guidance due to external uncertainties, explaining that margin compression in standalone EEI was driven by product mix (solar and EMS scaling).
- **Rahul Jain:** Asked about HPDC profitability drivers. Management explained that operating breakeven is supported by pruning legacy contracts and pursuing non-automotive RFQs, though gestation periods remain 3 to 6 months.
- **Ambrish Shah (Choice):** Asked about COGS increases. Management cited product mix shifts toward solar and EMS, alongside rising commodity costs (copper, polycarbonate) that cannot be passed on immediately.
- **Ambrish Shah:** Asked about capital allocation priorities. Management noted incremental capex of Rs 10 crore to Rs 20 crore annually and openness to strategic inorganic opportunities.
- **Zaki Nasser (Nasser Investments):** Asked about the rationale for holding Lumel Alucast. Management stated the priority is to restore its profitability to historical levels (18-20% EBITDA) before evaluating strategic alternatives.
- **Zaki Nasser:** Asked about promoter stake sale rumors. Management dismissed them as speculative market chatter and promised direct disclosure if any such event occurs.

### One-line takeaway
Rishabh Instruments delivered a robust H1 FY27 led by 29.3% growth in its core EEI segment and international expansion, effectively absorbing commodity cost pressures and a deliberate contraction in its HPDC business while maintaining a strong net-cash balance sheet.
