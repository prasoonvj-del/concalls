# Finolex Cables Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profitability growth driven by robust electrical and communication cable segments.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,013 crore (vs Rs 1,396 crore YoY, up ~44%)
- **PBT:** Rs 277 crore (vs Rs 171 crore YoY, up ~62%)
- **PAT:** Rs 221 crore (vs Rs 139 crore YoY, up ~59%)
- **PBT Margin:** 13.4% (vs 11.8% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 10.7% (vs 9.6% YoY)
- **Electrical Cables Revenue:** Rs 1,767 crore (vs Rs 1,206 crore YoY, up ~47%)
- **Communication Cables Revenue:** Rs 176 crore (vs Rs 109 crore YoY, up ~62%; margins close to 30%)
- **Copper Rod Revenue:** Rs 8 crore (vs Rs 403 crore YoY)
- **Exports:** ~Rs 50 crore total (Communication cable exports: Rs 35–40 crore)
- **JV (Finolex Plastech / as stated):** Revenue of ~Rs 87 crore with a profit of ~Rs 7 crore.

### Highlights
- Automotive cables, battery cables, flexible wires, solar, and agricultural applications posted high double-digit volume growth.
- Building wire volumes saw a low single-digit growth due to channel destocking.
- Copper rod plant was shut during the quarter due to continued restrictions on commercial LPG availability.
- Preform production has commenced; stabilization is expected in the next couple of months. Management decided to expand draw tower capacity directly from 4 million to 8 million fiber kilometers in one go.
- New CFO Sachin Naik introduced, effective June 1, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the overall capex guidance for the year at approximately Rs 300 crore.
- Management expects communication cable margins to normalize from the current ~30% as older lower-cost raw materials are fully consumed, but anticipates they will remain in the double-digits (low double digits).
- FMEG revenue target of Rs 5 billion by FY28 remains unchanged.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global fiber prices surged from USD 5-6 in December to USD 17-18, currently settling between USD 11-13 per km (with premium fibers higher).
- Helium and other input costs have seen wild fluctuations.
- Data center and AI-driven requirements have boomed globally, increasing demand for high fiber-count cables (running into thousands of fibers).
- Domestic telecom demand remains steady due to 5G rollouts and expansions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Supply chain issues post-March regarding fuel and PVC availability handled at a higher cost; mostly passed on to end consumers.
- Communication cable margins expanded due to consumption of lower-cost raw material inventory sourced in Nov-Dec last year.
- Commercial LPG shortages impacted device/fan manufacturing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Draw tower capacity expansion to 8 million kilometers will be completed by the end of Q2.
- Cabling capacity expansion planned to go up to 10 million (taking a little longer).
- Preform facility (100 metric ton capacity, equivalent to ~4 million km of fiber) is intended 100% for captive use.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Channel inventory, especially on building wires, was low at the end of the quarter due to destocking.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in raw material input costs (helium, PVC, fuel, germanium tetrachloride).
- Commercial LPG restrictions causing shutdowns in the copper rod plant and constraints for FMEG/fan manufacturers.
- Geopolitical disturbances in the Middle East impacting input cost stability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vidit Trivedi (Asian Market Securities) on sustainability of EBITDA/segment margins:** Management stated electrical cable margins are sustainable, while communication cable margins will normalize from 30% once old RM is depleted but stay in double digits.
2. **Sonali on fiber pricing and preform captive usage:** Management noted standard fiber settled at USD 11-13/km; 100% of the 100-tonne preform capacity is intended for captive consumption.
3. **Achal Lohade on potential revenue at peak capacity:** Management stated 8 million fiber km sold purely as fiber at USD 11/km yields ~USD 88 million, with 25-30% higher value addition possible depending on cable designs.
4. **Balasubramanian on data center capabilities:** Management confirmed R&D and capex are underway to supply high-fiber-count cables (up to thousands of fibers) in the near future.
5. **Tej Patel on preform sourcing and germanium issues:** Management acknowledged germanium tetrachloride is a restricted item requiring daily follow-ups, though they have enough for the calendar year.

### One-line takeaway
Finolex Cables delivered a strong quarter with a 44% revenue surge and 59% PAT growth, propelled by robust volume growth in electrical cables and margin expansion in communication cables driven by global AI/data-center fiber demand.
