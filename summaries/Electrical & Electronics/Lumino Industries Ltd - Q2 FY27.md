# Lumino Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, margin stability, positive cash flow, and successful debt reduction from IPO proceeds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 661 crore (up 30% YoY from Rs 509 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 84 crore (Margin: 12.8%)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 53 crore (Margin: 7.9%)
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,183 crore (up 25% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 155 crore (Margin: 13.1%)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 93 crore (Margin: 7.8%)
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Positive Rs 56 crore (includes Rs 68 crore trade payable/acceptance repayment from IPO proceeds)
- **Order Book:** Rs 2,885 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Manufacturing: Rs 922 crore, EPC: Rs 1,963 crore); Rs 3,093 crore total as of October including October order inflows of Rs 386 crore; L1 in tenders worth Rs 555 crore.
- **Manufacturing Tonnage (H1 FY27):** 13,781 metric tonnes (vs. 15,800 metric tonnes in H1 FY26)
- **Gross Debt:** Down to Rs 128 crore post-IPO

### Highlights
- First quarterly earnings call since the company's listing in September 2026.
- Revenue contribution stands at 70% from products and 30% from EPC; management aims to maintain manufacturing at a minimum 70% of total revenue.
- Received UL certification to support exports to the US and Europe; exports span 17 countries as of September 26.
- Received Rs 138 crore out of ~Rs 140 crore previously stuck with the West Bengal Electricity Board.
- Working capital days improved to 66 days as of September 2026 (down from 73 days in March). EPC working capital cycle improved to 111 days (from 120 days), and manufacturing to 46 days (from 50 days).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects manufacturing EBITDA to be in the range of 12% to 13.5% and EPC EBITDA in the range of 13% to 14%.
- Finance cost expected to reduce by Rs 15 crore to Rs 16 crore in H2, and by Rs 20 crore to Rs 25 crore on an annual basis.
- Management expects H2 operating cash flows to improve further due to realization and recovery of retention money.
- Total Addressable Market (TAM) for HTLS conductors expected to be around Rs 5,000 crore next financial year, with management targeting 10% to 15% conversion into orders.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand supported by renewable capacity additions, data center expansion, power infrastructure investments, Green Corridor Phase 3, and Pradhan Mantri Gati Shakti.
- Growing demand for specialized products like STLS (High Temperature Low Sag) conductors and AL-59 for grid modernization and renewable evacuation.
- Traditional conventional conductor demand is gradually reducing/phasing out in favor of AL-59.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- HT/LT cable EBITDA margins expected to be 1% to 2% higher than normal cables once production scales.
- Lower tonnage in H1 FY27 (13,781 metric tonnes vs 15,800 metric tonnes in H1 FY26) was attributed to a conscious shift toward higher-value, specialized products like HTLS and AL-59, which consume more production capacity (1.5x).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total current installed aluminum capacity: 40,000 metric tonnes per annum across two manufacturing facilities in Howrah, West Bengal (H1 capacity utilization at ~60%-62% physical, effectively ~80% on a value-added product mix).
- New manufacturing facility at Ranihati, Howrah will add 35,000 metric tonnes in phases, taking total capacity to 75,000 metric tonnes by FY28-FY29 (Phase 1 adds 10,000 metric tonnes).
- Commercial production for medium-voltage cables (3.3 kV to 33 kV) from the new facility is slated to start by December or January first week.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds of Rs 500 crore utilized partly to repay debt (Rs 264 crore repaid by September, remaining balance of Rs 75 crore due by November 15).
- Debt repayment from IPO proceeds has reduced gross debt to Rs 128 crore and is expected to lower debt service costs.
- Debtors over 6 months reduced significantly; out of Rs 230 crore as of March, Rs 215 crore has been realized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Penetrating the new 11 kV and 33 kV cable market will involve price competition initially, impacting margin leverage for the first 12 months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ajit Sethi (Eiko Quantum Solutions) on volume decline vs value mix:** Management clarified that lower tonnage in H1 was due to a shift to value-added items like AL-59 and HTLS which take 1.5x capacity, resulting in flat effective utilization of ~80%.
2. **Retail Shareholder on working capital and overdue receivables:** Management stated that working capital improved to 66 days, and out of Rs 230 crore of debtors older than 6 months as of March, Rs 215 crore has been realized.
3. **Rahil Shah (Investor) on West Bengal Electricity Board dues and EHV substation eligibility:** Management confirmed receiving Rs 138 crore out of Rs 140 crore from the board, leaving only Rs 2 crore outstanding. They expect to complete ongoing substations by March 2027 to become eligible to bid for 765 kV tenders next year.
4. **Garvita Jain (Advisors) on finance cost savings:** Management quantified finance cost savings at Rs 15-16 crore for H2 and Rs 20-25 crore annually following debt repayments from IPO proceeds.
5. **Viraj Jain (Investor) on labor shortages:** Management stated they do not face labor shortages because they operate primarily in EHV substations with dedicated labor gangs rather than transmission lines where shortages are prevalent.

### One-line takeaway
Lumino Industries delivered a strong Q2 FY27 post-listing with 30% revenue growth, stable ~13% EBITDA margins, positive operating cash flow, and a robust order book of Rs 3,093 crore, supported by debt reduction from IPO proceeds and capacity expansion plans.
