# Schneider Electric Infrastructure Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reported record order intake and a strong backlog, but acknowledged margin pressure due to commodity inflation, currency devaluation, and legacy fixed-price contracts.

### Key numbers
*   **Order Intake:** Rs 915 crore (highest ever for a quarter).
*   **Order Growth:** 0.5% YoY; double-digit sequential growth.
*   **Sales Growth:** ~5% YoY.
*   **Backlog:** Rs 2,100 crore+ (up 33% YoY).
*   **EBIT:** Rs 32 crore.
*   **Export Revenue:** 10-12% of total revenue.
*   **Import Content:** 10-15% of COGS.

### Highlights
*   Record order intake of Rs 915 crore.
*   Kolkata plant is now operational.
*   Strong focus on emerging segments: Data centers, semiconductors, and renewables.
*   Management stated that 1/5th of the order bank is from emerging segments.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects the remaining 3 quarters of FY27 to be positive, citing a healthy pipeline.
*   Management declined to provide specific numerical revenue or margin guidance.
*   Pricing actions have been initiated to mitigate commodity inflation.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Macro outlook remains strong, driven by electrification, data center/AI expansion, urbanization (EVs, Vande Bharat trains), and "Make in India" initiatives.
*   Management noted healthy inquiry pipelines in Metals & Mining.
*   Management is selective in bidding for DISCOM tenders, focusing on projects where they can add value (e.g., grid modernization) rather than civil works.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins were impacted by commodity inflation (copper, aluminum, steel) and an ~8% YTD depreciation of the rupee.
*   Legacy orders (booked before December) are fixed-price, limiting the ability to pass on cost increases.
*   Q1 experienced negative operating leverage due to soft sales growth and annual salary increments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Capex of ~Rs 500 crore invested over the last 3 years across 3 plants.
*   Expansion programs (Baroda MV factory, transformer factory, Kolkata plant) are on track with staggered completion timelines.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   No exceptional loans reported.
*   Interest costs normalized compared to the previous year (which included a one-time accounting gain).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Commodity price volatility and FX devaluation impacting import costs.
*   Inability to enforce price variation clauses in certain government/utility tenders.
*   Execution risks associated with legacy fixed-price contracts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Legacy Orders:** Management clarified that orders booked before December are fixed-price and cannot be revised, impacting current margins. Newer contracts now include mandatory price variation clauses.
*   **Margins:** Management attributed the Q1 margin dip to negative operating leverage and external commodity/FX headwinds, rather than operational inefficiency.
*   **Export Strategy:** The new Kolkata plant is export-focused; management expects absolute export value to increase, though percentage mix will fluctuate.
*   **TAM/DISCOMs:** Management clarified that not all DISCOM spending is addressable; they avoid civil works and focus on equipment and digital solutions.
*   **Price Hikes:** Management stated they acted promptly on price hikes for standard products as soon as cost increases were quantified.

### One-line takeaway
While the company is seeing record order intake and strong demand in emerging segments, near-term profitability remains constrained by legacy fixed-price contracts and commodity/FX headwinds.
