# RIR Power Electronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady financial growth, ongoing expansion in Odisha, and strong demand momentum across infrastructure sectors.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs. 33.98 crores (Up 25.1% QoQ from Rs. 27.16 crores in Q1 FY27; Up 32.5% YoY from Rs. 25.64 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Adjusted Operational EBITDA:** Rs. 5.66 crores (Up 29.8% YoY from Rs. 4.36 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Adjusted Operational EBITDA Margin:** 16.6% (vs. 17.0% in Q2 FY26; down slightly due to a 2.8% increase in raw material costs from geopolitical issues and expenses on events like SEMICON India and Transrail)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 4.45 crores (Up 41.2% YoY from Rs. 3.15 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **PAT Margin:** 12.5% (Up 53 bps YoY from 11.97% in Q2 FY26)
- **EPS:** Rs. 0.56 (vs. Rs. 0.47 in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** Rs. 61.14 crores (Up 31.1% YoY from Rs. 46.66 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted Operational EBITDA:** Rs. 10.36 crores (Up 42.8% YoY from Rs. 7.25 crores)
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted Operational EBITDA Margin:** 16.9% (Up 140 bps YoY from 15.5%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 7.59 crores (Up 55.2% YoY from Rs. 4.89 crores)
- **Order Backlog:** Rs. 39 crores as of September 30, 2026 (Up 38.9% from March 31, 2026)
- **Estimated Epitaxy Revenue (Next 4-6 months):** Rs. 12 crores to Rs. 15 crores

### Highlights
- Completed erection and installation of silicon carbide (SiC) epitaxial wafer manufacturing reactors at the upcoming Odisha facility.
- Odisha facility is India's first vertically integrated SiC semiconductor facility; commercial production is expected to commence upon receiving final statutory and regulatory (building and fire) clearances from the Odisha government (anticipated in November or December).
- Phase 1 of the Odisha project consists of epitaxy (Phase 1A) and packaging (Phase 1B); Phase 2 is fabrication.
- Term loan for Phase 1B (packaging) is at the closing stage with the bank, backed by promoter immovable properties and FDs, carrying a 5% government interest subsidy.
- Order backlog increased by nearly double over previous periods, shifting operational challenges from market demand to plant productivity.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain a healthy growth and profitability trajectory, viewing the current quarterly revenue run-rate as a sustainable base.
- Management declined to provide formal forward-looking revenue or EBITDA guidance numbers, but expressed optimism about outperforming past numbers.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand momentum is observed across industrial, power, railways, and infrastructure segments.
- Railway business is witnessing 70% to 80% growth over last year in quantity and value for existing products, driven by Vande Bharat train sets and freight locomotives.
- Defense sector: Completed field and test demonstrations for an electromagnetic aircraft launcher over the last 2.5 years; RFPs and first rail/defense orders are anticipated anytime.
- Export orders from the US have been executed up to ~70%.
- Management highlighted that RIR is uniquely positioned in India as the highest-value-chain player in medium and high-power devices (up to 9000 volts, 6000 amps), facing competition globally from only 4-5 players (ABB, Siemens, Hitachi, Toshiba) and none locally in SiC vertical integration.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Adjusted operational EBITDA margin dropped slightly YoY by 40 bps to 16.6% due to a 2.8% increase in raw material costs driven by geopolitical issues and event expenses (SEMICON India, Transrail).
- H1 EBITDA margin improved by 140 bps YoY to 16.9% despite higher other expenses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Odisha Phase 1A (epitaxy) reactors are installed; awaiting building and fire clearances to start commercial production.
- Odisha Phase 1B (packaging) will take 9 to 12 months to complete once bank financing is secured. No additional equity participation is planned for Phase 1B.
- Phase 2 (fabrication) will be executed as a greenfield project.
- Existing facility bottleneck operations are running near 90% capacity and can be doubled by adding specific machines. Assembly-based lines (LPD and equipment) can be scaled by adding benches and personnel.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity remains comfortable and the balance sheet is strengthening.
- Phase 1B debt will be secured via promoter immovable properties and FDs, supplemented by a 5% government interest subsidy.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in receiving state government clearances (building and fire clearances from the Odisha government) holding up commercial production of the epitaxy plant.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Charchit Maloo (Genuity Capital) asked about the operational timeline of the epitaxy plant and consolidated revenue/EBITDA outlook.**
   - *Management replied* that internal testing is done and they are awaiting statutory building and fire clearances from the Odisha government, hoping to start this quarter (November/December). They declined to provide forward-looking statements but expect to maintain or improve upon current performance.
2. **Prateek Giri (Investor) asked whether Rs. 34 crores quarterly revenue can be taken as a base case run-rate and inquired about Phase 1B packaging status and bank loan securities.**
   - *Management confirmed* Rs. 34 crores is a sustainable operating base. For Phase 1B, debt is at the final approval stage with the bank, backed by promoter immovable properties and fixed deposits, and benefits from a 5% interest subsidy. No additional equity is required for Phase 1B.
3. **Somnath Paul (Investor) asked about differentiation against heavily funded big semiconductor players entering India.**
   - *Management clarified* that large players are focused on the final 5% of the value chain (packaging and outsourced testing), whereas RIR is vertically integrated from material (SiC/silicon) to high-power systems. Competitors cannot easily buy the technology or replicate medium/high-power capabilities without starting from scratch.
4. **Keshav (Investor) asked about the capacity, wafer realization, and revenue potential of the epitaxy reactors.**
   - *Management stated* they do not track a fixed per-wafer realization due to varying voltage and thickness specifications, but estimate epitaxy revenue of Rs. 12 crores to Rs. 15 crores for the next 4 to 6 months of the financial year. Around 90% of capacity has expressions of interest/Letters of Intent (LOI).
5. **Vijendra (Investor) asked about participation in the Ministry of Power/MITY initiative for indigenous HVDC systems.**
   - *Management confirmed* RIR has submitted its interest and plans to submit its development plan within a month, noting they are better positioned than peers because they already possess domestic SiC epitaxy capabilities.

### One-line takeaway
RIR Power Electronics reported strong Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 32.5% YoY and is on the verge of commencing commercial production at India's first vertically integrated silicon carbide epitaxy facility in Odisha, while awaiting final state government clearances.
