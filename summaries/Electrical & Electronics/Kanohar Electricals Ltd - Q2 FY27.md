# Kanohar Electricals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (112% YoY) and robust order book (Rs 2,711.2 crore) driven by high-voltage transformer demand.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 208.6 crore (+112% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 345.7 crore (+109% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 35% (Q2), 32.3% (H1).
*   **PAT Margin:** 25.8% (Q2), 23.5% (H1).
*   **PAT Growth:** +180% YoY (Q2), +165% YoY (H1).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 2,711.2 crore (+34% QoQ).
*   **MVA Sold:** ~2,130 MVA (Q2).

### Highlights
*   Revenue growth driven by higher manufacturing volumes and a better business mix.
*   Transformer business contributed 88% of H1 revenue.
*   400 kV products accounted for 56% of H1 revenue; products 220 kV and above accounted for 78%.
*   Shunt reactors contributed 22% of H1 revenue (negligible in H1 FY26).
*   *Note:* The company continues to maintain its growth trajectory and capacity expansion plans as discussed in the Q1 FY27 call.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains FY27 revenue guidance of ~Rs 950 crore.
*   Management expects full-year EBITDA margin profile to be broadly similar to FY26 (despite current 32-35% run rate).
*   Expects similar YoY growth in FY28 as in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Power transmission sector outlook remains encouraging due to infrastructure investments, renewable integration, and grid modernization.
*   Railways offer a ~Rs 20,000 crore market opportunity over 5-10 years for Scott-connected transformers.
*   Entry barriers remain high due to short-circuit testing requirements and track record.
*   No current plans for exports; focus remains on the domestic market.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Stable margin environment reported; no pressure from increased competition or raw material costs.
*   Management notes quarterly/order-based variations in margins.
*   Raw material availability is managed; no production impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Brownfield expansion at Gangol facility on track; expected completion by beginning of FY29.
*   Current capacity: 19,200 MVA (annualized).
*   Capacity utilization (annualized) for Q2 was ~41%.
*   Targeting 80% utilization before planning further capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Total borrowings reduced to Rs 28.1 crore (from Rs 39 crore as of March 31, 2026).
*   Trade receivables reduced to Rs 141.6 crore (from Rs 210.8 crore).
*   Inventory stood at Rs 196.8 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Management noted that achieving the same H1/H2 revenue proportion as last year is not guaranteed.
*   Margin volatility exists between quarters and orders.
*   Entry into 765 kV segment requires a 3-4 year timeframe to build track record for regular tenders.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Analysts questioned why guidance is conservative (28% range) vs. current 32-35% run rate. Management cited quarterly/order variations and prudence.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Analysts questioned low utilization (10% quarterly/41% annualized). Management clarified it's a trajectory-based ramp-up and they need lead time for FY29 expansion.
*   **765 kV Entry:** Analysts asked about the strategy. Management confirmed they are technically ready but need 3-4 years to build a track record.
*   **Export Strategy:** Analysts asked about US/export opportunities. Management stated focus is currently on the domestic market.
*   **Revenue Guidance:** Analysts questioned if Rs 950 crore is conservative. Management reiterated it is based on current production plans.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational performance and order book growth support the company's expansion plans, though management remains conservative on full-year margin guidance due to potential quarterly volatility.
