# Havells India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-24 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue (+35% YoY) and EBITDA (+30% YoY) growth across categories, partially offset by commodity-led margin softness in cables and off-season absorption in Lloyd.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue Growth:** 35% YoY overall.
* **EBITDA Growth:** Approximately 30% YoY, driven by operating leverage.
* **Volume Growth:** Close to double digits in Cables & Wires as well as Switchgear.
* **Segment Margins:** 
  * **Switchgear:** Contribution margins restored to 38%–39%.
  * **Cables & Wires:** Q2 contribution margin declined 380 bps YoY due to lower aluminium prices on a high base; H1 margin retained at ~15%.
* **Segment Share & Run Rate:**
  * **Exports:** ~15% of Switchgear revenues.
  * **Renewables:** Clocking ~Rs 300 crore per quarter.
  * **Lloyd Revenue:** ~Rs 615 crore in Q2 (vs ~Rs 1,460 crore in Q1 FY27). Room Air Conditioners (RAC) account for ~75% of Lloyd business.
* **ROCE:** Currently at ~23%.
* **Capex Guidance:** Retained at Rs 1,400 crore (INR 14 billion) for FY27.

### Highlights
* Category-wide top-line growth supported by established divisions (cables, switchgear) and emerging renewables.
* Implementation of a pull-led distribution model over the past 6–9 months (reducing scheme-driven push selling into trade channels) has stabilized.
* Renewables division is scaling via integrated product kits distributed through existing brand and channel infrastructure, with manufacturing outsourced to Goldi Solar.
* Refrigerator plant commissioned in March is ramping up well to increase non-RAC category share in Lloyd (targeting non-RAC to reach ~40% over time).

### Guidance & outlook
* Management refrains from giving explicit annual revenue or timeline guidance for Lloyd profitability.
* Positive outlook on demand and margins heading into the festive season.
* ROCE expected to return above historical 25%+ levels in the next couple of years via asset sweating and margin stability.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Consumer demand remains steady despite an elevated inflationary environment.
* High inflation over recent years led to a temporary increase in unorganized market share in wires, but new organized entrants are expected to take share predominantly from unorganized players.
* Pass-through of raw material prices is structurally faster in cables and wires compared to switchgear and appliances due to consumer and channel behavior.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Undertook calibrated price hikes of 10% to 25% across categories over the past 12 months (over 30% in cables & wires).
* Planning further price increases of 5% to 7% in Q3 FY27 to cover elevated input costs that were unabsorbed in Q2.
* Cables margin impacted by aluminium price softness in Q2, though the business remains 80%–90% domestic and heavily reliant on aluminium cables. Wires remain entirely copper.
* Lloyd margins impacted by raw material inflation and seasonal fixed-cost under-absorption during the AC off-season.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* FY27 capex maintained at Rs 1,400 crore, primarily directed toward Cables & Wires capacity expansions and an R&D center.
* Ongoing cable expansion is focused on Medium Voltage (MV) and Extra High Voltage (EHV) underground cables, with full capacity expected on stream within 18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Trade channel inventory levels normalized by the end of Q2 following peak-season drawdowns.
* ROCE stands at ~23%, supported by existing asset cash flows funding ongoing expansions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Commodity price volatility and elevated raw material costs across copper and aluminium.
* Seasonal under-absorption of fixed manufacturing overheads in Lloyd during off-peak quarters.
* Potential short-term margin or market share pressures from new competition entering the consumer wires segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Q: How is Havells strategizing against new competitors launching wire businesses?**  
  *Management:* organized entrants primarily gain share from the unorganized market. Long-term investments in technology/factories will build the industry, while Havells will leverage deeper trade relationships, brand power, and influencer management to protect market share.
* **Q: What is Havells' positioning and edge in the renewables segment vs larger utility players?**  
  *Management:* Havells is not competing with utility-scale players (like Adani or Waaree). Focus is on selling branded integrated solar kits to residential and industrial clients via existing dealer/system-integrator networks, with contract manufacturing handled by Goldi Solar.
* **Q: Why did cable contribution margins drop 380 bps YoY, and what is the outlook?**  
  *Management:* The decline was due to falling aluminium prices against a high base last year. H1 margins at ~15% align with historical averages (15%–15.5%), and margins should normalize as new MV/EHV capacity ramps up.
* **Q: Why did Lloyd sales drop sharply QoQ to Rs 615 crore alongside margin weakness?**  
  *Management:* RAC sales follow strict seasonality where Q1 is the largest quarter and Q2 is the lowest. Off-season fixed-cost under-absorption and unpassed raw material inflation led to subdued margins, which will recover as the seasonal ramp-up begins in Q3/Q4.
* **Q: When will ROCE return to historical 25%+ levels?**  
  *Management:* ROCE is currently ~23%. Newer capex takes time to ramp up, but sweating existing brand/distribution assets alongside margin management will drive ROCE above 25% over the next couple of years.

### One-line takeaway
Havells delivered robust 35% YoY revenue growth backed by healthy volumes across categories, leveraging price hikes and channel strength to navigate commodity volatility and seasonal margin softness in Lloyd.
