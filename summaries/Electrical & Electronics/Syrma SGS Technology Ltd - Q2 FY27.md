# Syrma SGS Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong 71% YoY growth in H1, robust operating cash flow generation, and upgraded annual revenue guidance while maintaining fiscal discipline and working capital improvements.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR2,000 crores for Q2 FY27 (up 73% YoY, 25% QoQ); INR3,603 crores for H1 FY27 (up 71% YoY).
- **Operating EBITDA:** INR193 crores for Q2 FY27 (up 68% YoY); INR355 crores for H1 FY27 (up 68% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.9% for H1 FY27 (vs 10.1% in H1 FY26).
- **PBT:** INR165 crores for Q2 FY27; INR306 crores for H1 FY27 (up 95% YoY).
- **PAT:** INR124 crores for Q2 FY27 (up 87% YoY); INR230 crores for H1 FY27 (up 98% YoY).
- **PBT Margin:** 8.5% for H1 FY27 (vs 7.4% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 6.4% for H1 FY27 (vs 5.5% YoY).
- **Export Revenue:** INR514 crores for Q2 FY27 (up 91% YoY); INR901 crores for H1 FY27 (up 80% YoY). Expected to cross INR1,700 crores for FY27.
- **ODM Revenue:** INR322 crores for Q2 FY27; INR591 crores for H1 FY27 (up 117% YoY). Expected to cross INR1,000 crores to INR1,100 crores for FY27.
- **Open Order Book:** INR7,420 crores as of September end (Auto 30%, Consumer/Industrial 24.5%-25% each, Healthcare 8%, IT & Railways 12%+).
- **Net Working Capital Days:** 63 days as of September '26 (down from 71 days in Q1 FY27 and 73 days in September '25).
- **Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** INR120 crores for H1 FY27 (OCF-to-EBITDA conversion of ~34%).
- **Net Debt / Net Cash:** Net cash position of INR258 crores (treasury balance of ~INR1,000 crores, gross debt of INR746 crores) as of September '26.
- **Capex Incurred:** INR200 crores in Q2 FY27 (~INR140 crores toward the PCB project, balance for brownfield expansions). Total PCB project spend expected to reach INR400 crores–INR450 crores by March '27 and INR800 crores–INR900 crores in total.
- **ROCE:** 21.4% (adjusted annualised for H1 FY27).
- **Customer Concentration (H1):** Top 5: 38%, Top 10: 50%, Top 20: 66%.
- **New Customers Onboarded:** 18 customers in H1 FY27 (32 customers in FY26).
- **Acquired Entities Contribution (H1):** Elcome contributed INR135 crores; Syrma Johari MedTech (SJML) contributed INR140 crores.

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 performance surpassed full-year FY25 figures across revenue (INR3,578 crores vs INR3,787 crores), EBITDA (INR355 crores vs INR324 crores), PAT (INR230 crores vs INR184 crores), exports (INR901 crores vs INR854 crores), and ODM (INR591 crores vs INR453 crores).
- Broad-based revenue growth across verticals in H1 YoY: Automotive up 80%+ (contribution ~26%), Healthcare up 78%, Industrial up 58%, IT up 100%+, Railways up 45%, Consumer up 62%.
- Commissioned high-grade medical plastic facility in Jodhpur (Syrma Johari) for molding, extrusions, and blow molding.
- Implemented ARC systems across plants, showing initial 5% to 6% OEE improvement on SMT lines.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Upgraded annual revenue growth guidance to 40% to 45%, translating to ~INR7,000 crores for FY27 (management statement).
- **FY28 Outlook:** Confident of crossing the $1 billion revenue mark in FY28.
- **EBITDA Margin Outlook:** Expects FY27 close at 10% to 10.5%, and 10.5% to 11%+ in FY28-29 driven by high-margin accretive projects (PCB, etc.).
- **PCB Project Guidance:** Commercial production/series production expected by September '27 (trial production by March/April '27). Expected annual revenue potential of INR900 crores to INR1,000 crores at 70%-80% capacity utilization (INR1,200 crores to INR1,350 crores at full capacity in FY29) with steady-state EBITDA margins of 15% to 18% (excluding PLI/incentives).
- **JV Revenues (FY28):** Elemaster JV expected to deliver INR150 crores to INR200 crores; Kaga JV expected to deliver INR100 crores to INR150 crores.
- **Acquired Entities Guidance:** Elcome targeted at INR300 crores to INR350 crores for FY27; Syrma Johari targeted at ~INR300 crores for FY27 (45%-50% growth).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand side remains very bullish across all verticals, particularly automotive, industrial, and consumer.
- Supply chain constraints (especially in electronic components) are persisting and expected to ease out around April to June of 2027 (Q1 FY28).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Supply chain constraints create a cost-push effect (estimated at 3% to 5%), which dilutes percentage margin profile due to pass-through mechanisms not yielding value-adds on higher cost bases, though absolute figures are maintained.
- Natural forex hedge exists due to sizeable export revenues offsetting imports. Domestic business includes forex pass-through mechanisms.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total PCB capex estimated at INR800 crores to INR900 crores by March '28 (INR400 crores–INR450 crores by March '27).
- Normal greenfield/brownfield capex estimated at INR100 crores to INR150 crores per year for the next 2 years.
- Elemaster JV capex: INR40 crores to INR50 crores; Camera module initial capex: ~INR30 crores; Kaga JV capex: INR30 crores to INR50 crores.
- Applied for 5-6 projects under the ECMS scheme (PCB, CCL, HDI Flex, Camera modules for automotive, and Magnetics). Planning CCL plant execution in FY28 to get ready by December '28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position of INR258 crores, supported by treasury of ~INR1,000 crores and gross debt of INR746 crores.
- Net working capital days improved to 63 days as of September '26 (from 71 days in Q1 FY27). Target to maintain at 60 to 65 days.
- Inventory increased to INR1,570 crores (71 days), including strategic inventories of critical components with longer lead times.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing supply chain constraints and lead time extensions (up to 36 weeks for critical active components, 4-16 weeks for passives) could impact shipment matching between intermediaries and customers.
- Geopolitical conditions and macroeconomic unpredictability (midterm elections, Middle East) cited as external variables.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sameet Sinha (Macquarie) asked about ODM expansion and guidance conservatism:** Management stated ODM expands into medtech, defense, RFID, power electronics, and industrial segments; the tempered H2 growth guidance reflects supply chain caution rather than demand slowdown.
- **Aditya Bhartia (Investec) inquired about the $250 million framework agreement:** Management (Jaidit Singh Brar / J.S. Gujral) noted NPIs/early runs are underway, with full revenue impact expected in FY28 and beyond (estimated annualized INR500 crores to INR1,500 crores).
- **Santosh Seshadri (Avendus Spark) asked about ISM scheme participation:** Management clarified they will enter the semiconductor ecosystem under ISM only with a credible partner and hope for something concrete in 6 months.
- **Kishore Kumar (Unifi Capital) asked about PCB layer mix and pricing impact:** Management stated bulk consumption is sub-8 layer (predominantly 2 to 6 layer); cost-push inflation contributed ~3% to 5% to the quarter's revenue growth, with the rest driven by volume.
- **Indrajit Agarwal (CLSA) asked about PCB profitability and backward integration into CCL:** Management stated they do not expect a PAT loss in the PCB business during initial ramp-up, and plan to execute a CCL plant post-PCB stabilization in FY28.

### One-line takeaway
Syrma SGS reported a robust H2/Q2 FY27 with 71% YoY H1 revenue growth, positive operating cash flow, and upgraded full-year revenue guidance to ~INR7,000 crores, backed by secular demand across verticals and disciplined working capital management.
