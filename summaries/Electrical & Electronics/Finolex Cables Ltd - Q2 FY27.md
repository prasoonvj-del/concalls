# Finolex Cables Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong year-on-year revenue growth of 65% and significant EBITDA margin expansion driven by communication and electrical segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None provided; capex guidance was not explicitly updated.
- Tone and demand view: Maintained a positive stance; communication cables nearly doubled YoY (116% growth), and management noted strong export inquiries and demand, though reiterating that 30% communication cable margins will eventually normalize to mid-teens.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: UltraTech's entry into the market was explicitly discussed as a new watchpoint. Commercial LPG shortages and copper rod plant shutdowns mentioned last quarter were absent.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Draw tower expansion to 8 million kilometers is progressing toward completion by the end of Q2/early Q3; preform stabilization remains ongoing.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,269 crore (up 65% YoY from Rs 1,376 crore; up 13% QoQ from Rs 2,013 crore)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 4,282 crore (up 55% YoY from Rs 2,771 crore)
- **EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 309 crore (up from Rs 143 crore YoY and Rs 248 crore QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 13.6% (up 322 bps YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 557 crore (up from Rs 274 crore YoY; margin 13%, up 312 bps YoY)
- **PBT:** Rs 375 crore (up 57% YoY from Rs 239 crore)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 286 crore (up 53% YoY from Rs 187 crore; up 29% QoQ from Rs 221 crore)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 273 crore (up 68% YoY from Rs 163 crore)
- **H1 Consolidated PAT:** Rs 522 crore (up from Rs 325 crore YoY)
- **Electrical Cables Revenue:** Rs 1,960 crore (86% of quarterly revenue, up 65% YoY; segment EBIT Rs 217 crore, up 68% YoY, margins steady at 11%)
- **Communication Cables Revenue:** Rs 230 crore (up 116% YoY from Rs 106 crore; up 30% QoQ; segment EBIT jumped to Rs 69 crore from Rs 1.2 crore YoY)
- **Other Segment Revenue (FMEG & Trading):** Rs 73 crore (up 12% YoY; EBIT improved 40% YoY)
- **Cash and Liquid Investments:** Close to Rs 2,000 crore with a consolidated net worth of Rs 6,484 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **H1 Net Cash Flow from Operating Activities:** Negative Rs 106 crore due to working capital adjustments (inventory buildup).
- **Inventory and Working Capital Metrics:** Inventory buildup of Rs 460 crore during H1; inventory days slightly over 70 days (historically 60-62 days); receivable days at 18 days (historically 15-18 days).

### Highlights
- Building wire volume growth was in low single digits, but agri and solar volumes more than doubled (from a smaller base), auto improved ~70%, and industrials grew ~25%.
- Communication cables fiber and optic fiber cables grew by ~25% in volume.
- Preform plant stabilization is ongoing (taking 3 to 6 months; effective usage around 50%); expected to stabilize by the end of the year or a month later.
- Expanded fiber draw capacity of 8 million kilometers is likely to come into play towards the end of the current quarter or early next quarter.
- Solar cable capacity is being doubled (from 5,000 km/month to 10,000 km/month); equipment on order for delivery in early January, targeting commercial operation from April onwards.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects communication cable segment margins to eventually normalize to mid-teens over time, though timing depends on global supply chains and demand.
- Solar cable capacity will double from April onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Hyperscalers have not yet started acquiring or ordering cables in large numbers in India, but demand exists in US and European markets.
- Standard fiber prices have slightly tapered from ~Rs 1,200 per km to ~Rs 1,150 per km.
- Global major optical fiber lines are booked until the end of 2027, sustaining overseas demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins held firm at about 20%.
- Operating leverage and better communication cable margins drove EBITDA margin expansion.
- Input costs like gases and helium remain volatile depending on the Middle East situation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fiber draw expanded capacity target: 8 million kilometers/year; preform capacity equivalent to 4 million kilometers; cabling capacity targeted between 12 million and 15 million kilometers in 7 to 8 months.
- Most expansion funds have already been spent.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net zero debt balance sheet with a consolidated net worth of Rs 6,484 crore.
- Negative operating cash flow in H1 driven by a conscious inventory buildup of Rs 460 crore in September to secure supplies; expected to normalize in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in commodity prices and input costs (such as gases and helium).
- Increased competition and potential market impact from new entrants like UltraTech with large financial backing.
- Risk of inventory margin losses if commodity prices drop significantly.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sumit Ambekar (Parami Financial Services) on hyperscaler demand for OFC:** Management stated hyperscalers have not started ordering in large numbers in India yet, and high fiber-count data center cables (up to 13,000 fibers) are currently limited to US and European markets.
2. **Achal Lohade (Nuvama Institutional Equities) on OFC capacity utilization and realization:** Management noted standard fiber prices dipped slightly from ~Rs 1,200 to ~Rs 1,150/km, and preform plant capacity utilization is currently around 50% during the stabilization period.
3. **Achal Lohade on UltraTech's market entry impact:** Management noted UltraTech entered near the end of September, so Q2 had virtually no impact, and they are watchful of future moves given the competitor's deep pockets.
4. **Sushil Choksey (IndSec Securities) on capacity enhancement roadmap:** Management outlined that fiber capacity will reach 8 million km, preform equivalent to 4 million km, and cabling capacity 12–15 million km in 7–8 months.
5. **Umesh Gupta (Emergent Capital) on communication cable margin sustainability:** Management reiterated that 30% segment margins are not permanent and will eventually normalize to mid-teens depending on global supply chain normalization.
6. **Ankit Soni on export pipeline:** Management confirmed strong overseas demand, with lines booked globally till end-2027, and noted exports to Malaysia, Europe, and the US, contributing Rs 25–28 crore within communication cable revenue.

### One-line takeaway
Finolex Cables posted a stellar Q2 with a 65% jump in revenue and doubled EBITDA, propelled by robust electrical cable demand and exceptional realization and volume growth in communication cables.
