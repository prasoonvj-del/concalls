# Yash Highvoltage Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue growth of 95% YoY, margin expansion, and a robust order book.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 194.5 crore (vs INR 99.6 crore in H1 FY26, up 95% YoY)
- **Domestic / Export Mix:** Domestic 95%, Exports 5%
- **Product Mix (Value):** RIP bushings 86%, OIP and high-current bushings 14%
- **Volume:** ~4,000 total bushings in H1 FY27 vs 2,950 in H1 FY26 (~40% YoY growth); ~1,500 OIP and ~2,200 RIP units produced.
- **Gross Margin:** 48.3% (vs 42.4% in H1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** INR 55 crore (vs INR 23 crore in H1 FY26, up 139% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 28.1% (vs 23.3% in H1 FY26)
- **PAT:** INR 36 crore (vs INR 14 crore in H1 FY26, up 157% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 18.6% (vs 13.7% in H1 FY26)
- **Order Book:** INR 760 crore as of 30th September 2026 (up from INR 400 crore at the start of the fiscal year), to be executed over the next 15 to 18 months. Export order book is INR 40-50 crore (~5% to 7%).
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 63 days as of 30th September 2026 (vs 81 days in FY26)
- **Net Cash Position:** INR 95 crore as of 30th September 2026
- **Preferential Allotment:** Raised approx. INR 151 crore total from marquee investors (INR 90.99 crore via equity shares, INR 15 crore upfront against warrants; balance payable within 18 months).
- **Depreciation:** INR 4.4 crore (vs INR 2.6 crore in H1 FY26)

### Highlights
- Entered representation agreements with Ensales Electrical Associates, Gateway Power Sales, and EHV Solutions LLC in the US, extending the footprint to ~27 states.
- Completed seismic qualification testing for IEEE-compliant 138 and 230 kV OIP condenser transformer bushings (first Indian OEM to achieve this).
- Successfully tested the first 245 kV wall-through transformer bushing in-house and supplied it to a leading PSU.
- Migrated globally to SAP S/4HANA successfully.
- Acquired Sukrut (subsidiary/JV taken over in December), which has returned to profitability within 9 months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain or improve EBITDA margins in H2 and FY28.
- Greenfield facility commercial production and invoicing has begun for the assembly model; localized RIP core trial runs are ongoing, with type tests expected soon. Brownfield expansion completion targeted for Q3.
- Combined peak revenue potential of Unit 1 (Halol) and Unit 2 (Jarod greenfield) estimated at INR 900–1,000 crore by 2030–31.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural demand drivers include grid modernization, replacement of aging infrastructure, renewable integration, data centers, and industrial electrification.
- The company holds ~1% to 1.2% of the global market demand and aims for ~5% market share in the next 4 to 5 years.
- Component players have historically experienced negligible impact from Chinese imports compared to capital equipment manufacturers.
- Management views the current environment as a multi-year seller's market given specialized entry barriers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by product mix, a ~40% volume increase, better price realization, and operational efficiencies.
- Import duty and freight savings from localized core manufacturing estimated at 18% to 20%, though pricing strategy will depend on competition.
- Non-current other assets increased to INR 40 crore due to advance payments made to international core vendors to secure capacity slots.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total planned capex for the greenfield project (Jarod) is INR 220 crore, of which INR 106 crore has been spent up to 30th September 2026; the balance will be deployed over the next 12 months.
- Halol existing facility capacity utilization is close to 90%.
- Localized RIP bushing capabilities up to 550 kV are on track (245 kV localized production by Q4; 420 kV/550 kV localized by Q4 FY28 or mid-CY28).
- Company will maintain a combination of local and imported cores for the next couple of years as internal capacity will not fully meet rising global/domestic demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash of INR 95 crore as of 30th September 2026.
- Receivable collection period remains stable at 47 days (in line with H1 FY26).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as adverse surprises; management noted execution and supply chains are currently stable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjay A (Elara Capital) / Management:** Asked about market share targets. *Answer:* Company holds ~1% globally, aims for ~5% in 4-5 years, and seeks to maintain Indian market leadership.
2. **Vaibhav Mishra (Research) / Management:** Inquired if 28% EBITDA margins are sustainable. *Answer:* Management stated efforts are directed to maintain or improve margins going forward.
3. **Abhisar Jain / Management:** Asked about initial experience with localized core development and import balancing. *Answer:* Initial results are very encouraging; type tests are expected in a couple of weeks, and imports will continue alongside local sourcing to meet massive demand.
4. **Ambrish Shah (Consultancy) / Management:** Questioned the impact of higher depreciation on PAT margins. *Answer:* Bulk of greenfield investment was put to use in September; despite depreciation rising 2.5x to 3x, management aims to maintain profit margins.
5. **Utkarsh Somaiya (Quantum Solutions) / Management:** Asked about the timeline for peak revenue and future capex. *Answer:* INR 1,000 crore peak revenue targeted by FY30-31; current capex is sufficient, with any future needs funded via internal accruals.

### One-line takeaway
Yash Highvoltage reported a stellar H1 FY27 with revenue up 95% YoY to INR 194.5 crore and EBITDA margins expanding to 28.1%, underpinned by robust order inflows of INR 760 crore and progressive execution of its Jarod greenfield expansion.
