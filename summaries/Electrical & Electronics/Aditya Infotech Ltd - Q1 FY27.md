# Aditya Infotech Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 89.5% YoY, margin expansion, and a robust balance sheet.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 1,402 crore, up 89.5% YoY
- **Gross Margin:** 30.8%, up 810 bps YoY
- **EBITDA:** INR 208 crore, up 20% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14.8%, up 604 bps YoY
- **Adjusted PAT:** INR 142.2 crore, up 332.5% YoY
- **Market Share:** 43.3% in the Indian video surveillance industry in FY2026 (Frost & Sullivan)
- **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.07
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 64 days
- **Capacity:** ~2.5 million units per month
- **Capacity Utilization:** 85% to 90%
- **Price Hikes Taken:** 10% to 20% so far (estimated 15% to 20% by end of H1)

### Highlights
- CP PLUS brand contribution reached 87% of overall revenue; IP products accounted for 79% of the CP PLUS portfolio.
- Finance costs reduced by 59% YoY.
- Inaugurated a new corporate office and R&D center in Noida on August 2, 2026 (bringing total operational R&D centers to Noida, Ahmedabad, and Taiwan; Bengaluru center opening shortly).
- Incorporated Corelink Cable Technology Private Limited (JV with Orient Cables) for manufacturing network LAN and CCTV coaxial cables in Rajasthan, with commercial production expected by the end of the financial year.
- Manufacturing capacity expanded to 42 FA lines, 4 MI lines, and 12 SMT lines by the end of August.
- Transitioned to a top-down demand generation model for enterprise, government, and AI-driven opportunities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 14% to 15%.
- Management expects potential price increases of around 25% for the full year to counter cost escalations.
- Export opportunities are targeted as a growth lever in the next 18 to 24 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CCTV is a priority sector for the government, and management sees no deviation from policy regarding import restrictions.
- STQC norms and cyber-security testing have eliminated old/Chinese inventory, leaving 40+ certified brands in the market.
- Demand for AI-powered and higher-value intelligent surveillance solutions is increasing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins moderated slightly from Q4 due to the exhaustion of lower-cost inventory.
- Cost hikes in DDR and SOC have been phenomenal across all product categories.
- Semiconductor-based solutions comprise ~35% of BOM (sourced from Taiwan); passive electronics comprise 15%-20%. Overall import solution is 55%-60%, moving toward 40% domestic upon localization.
- Forex exposure is hedged on a weekly basis, maintaining a cover between 85% to 100% (currently at 90%+ levels).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Housing and enclosure expansion is on track to become operational by Q3 FY2027.
- Land acquisition for the Kadapa greenfield expansion is in the final stages.
- A second manufacturing cluster in Greater Noida is under application for land allocation.
- Exploring new categories: machine vision cameras, drone camera modules, industrial autonomous mobile robots, home IoT (video door phones, door locks, smart doorbells), CP PLUS Pro series, and Nexivue brand rollout.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity reduced sharply to 0.07.
- Working capital metrics improved with a reduction in inventory and debtor days, leading to a cash conversion cycle of 64 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cost escalations in DDR and SOC components globally.
- Potential time delay in passing on inflationary cost increases fully to consumers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhruv Jain (Ambit Institutional Equities) asked about price hikes and the exhaustion of low-cost inventory.** Management stated price hikes have varied between 10% to 20% and are being passed on gradually to avoid consumption shocks.
2. **Naushad Chaudhary (Birla Sun Life Insurance) asked about the TAM expansion from new categories.** Management highlighted that Pro series, Home IoT, and industrial automation/drones represent large future adjacencies and TAMs, though early-stage exact numbers were not quantified.
3. **Saurabh Shah (AUM Fund Advisors) inquired about BOM sourcing and forex hedging.** Management clarified that ~35% of BOM (semiconductors) comes from Taiwan, and forex exposure is tightly hedged at 90%+ levels weekly.
4. **Shubham Thorat (Capital Advisors) asked about current capacity utilization and R&D spend.** Management stated capacity utilization is at 85%-90%, and R&D is treated as a strategic moat/investment rather than budgeted as a fixed percentage of revenue.

### One-line takeaway
Aditya Infotech delivered strong Q1 FY27 results with revenue up 89.5% YoY and EBITDA margin at 14.8%, driven by the CP PLUS brand, margin expansion, and robust execution despite rising component costs.
