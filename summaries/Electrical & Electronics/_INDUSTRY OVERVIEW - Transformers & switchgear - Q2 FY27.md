# Electrical & Electronics - Transformers & switchgear: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the transformer and switchgear sector reflects a continuation of robust structural demand driven by power transmission and distribution (T&D) investments, renewable energy integration, and data centers. However, operational results were a mixed bag. While several companies (such as Kanohar, TARIL, and Yash Highvoltage) posted stellar revenue and margin expansions, others faced headwinds from rising commodity costs (copper, steel, aluminum, and transformer oil), temporary weather disruptions, and execution delays. 

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements universally agree that domestic demand is in a multi-year upcycle. Key structural drivers cited across the board include renewable energy integration, grid modernization, industrial electrification, and rapidly expanding data center and semiconductor infrastructure. 
* **Divergence (Exports vs. Domestic):** Companies diverge sharply on their geographic focus. Players like **Atlanta Electricals**, **Shilchar Technologies**, **Yash Highvoltage**, and **Transformers and Rectifiers (TARIL)** are actively scaling up exports, targeting markets in North America, Europe, and the UK. Conversely, **Kanohar Electricals** stated it has no current plans for exports, maintaining a strict focus on the high-growth domestic market.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreements:** Most companies acknowledged persistent inflationary pressures on key raw materials—specifically copper, aluminum, steel, and transformer oil (with Raksan noting transformer oil prices more than doubled). 
* **Disagreements/Variations in Protection:** Companies with strong pricing power or robust Price Variation (PV) clauses, such as **TARIL**, **Kanohar**, and **Shilchar**, managed to insulate their margins. However, companies burdened by fixed-price or legacy contracts—such as **Schneider Electric Infrastructure** (where only 20–25% of projects have PV protection), **Atlanta Electricals**, **Salzer Electronics**, and **Raksan Transformers**—reported margin compression due to a time lag in passing on raw material cost increases.

## Capex & capacity
Capacity expansion is widespread across the sector to capture surging demand:
* **Atlanta Electricals:** Commissioning its Inverter Duty Transformer (IDT) plant in Q3 FY27 and undertaking a ~INR 193 crore backward integration capex for tanks and radiators.
* **Kanohar Electricals:** Brownfield expansion at its Gangol facility is on track for early FY29.
* **Raksan Transformers:** Setting up a new facility in Liwaspur, Sonipat (~INR 72.58 crore investment) to scale capacity up to 3,250–4,000 MVA.
* **Salzer Electronics:** Planning modest capex in FY27 to add 20%–25% balancing capacity in its Switchgear division.
* **Schneider Electric Infrastructure:** Expanded Vacuum Interrupters capacity to 3.75 lakh units/year.
* **Shilchar Technologies:** Phase 3 expansion adding 6,500 MVA (including 220 kV class) is slated for April 2027 commissioning.
* **Transformers and Rectifiers (TARIL):** Expanding Changodar manufacturing capacity to 35,000 MVA alongside a CTC backward integration plant.
* **Yash Highvoltage:** Advancing its Jarod greenfield expansion (INR 220 crore total capex) to localize RIP bushing production.

## What changed since last quarter
*(Based on companies providing explicit sequential comparisons)*
* **Salzer Electronics:** Maintained its FY27 revenue guidance of INR 2,100 crore, but shaved its EBITDA margin guidance down to "around 8%" (previously 8%–8.5%). Its tone shifted to be more optimistic on demand, though it reported further delays in its Saudi Arabia plant (pushed to April 2027 due to regional conflict) and Bengaluru street light project.
* **Schneider Electric Infrastructure:** Maintained guidance but shifted its tone from "record order intake" in Q1 to "sustained demand" in Q2, while continuing capacity capitalization as promised.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Commodity Price Trends & Margin Recovery:** Whether companies facing margin compression (Salzer, Raksan, Schneider, Atlanta) can successfully realize price hikes and navigate raw material volatility.
* **Execution of Delayed Dispatches:** Monitoring Atlanta Electricals to ensure smooth delivery of the ~INR 180 crores of weather-deferred Q2 orders (70% already dispatched in October).
* **Capex Milestones:** Progress on greenfield and brownfield facility commissioning (e.g., TARIL's Changodar plant, Shilchar's Phase 3, Yash Highvoltage's Jarod plant, and Raksan's Liwaspur facility).
* **Working Capital Cycle:** Efforts by companies like TARIL to bring down working capital days closer to 110–120 days.

## Company one-liners
* **Atlanta Electricals:** Reported a 35.5% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27 despite Q2 weather disruptions, and remains confident in its 40% CAGR growth and 16-18% EBITDA margin guidance.
* **GE Vernova T&D India:** Delivered a strong quarter characterized by robust order intake, revenue expansion, and high-end EBITDA margins.
* **Kanohar Electricals:** Strong operational performance and order book growth support expansion plans, though management remains conservative on full-year margin guidance due to potential quarterly volatility.
* **Raksan Transformers:** Reported a muted H1 FY27 due to a lower-value B2G product mix and raw material cost inflation, but remains confident of scaling up significantly once its new Sonipat facility becomes operational.
* **Salzer Electronics:** Reported strong top-line growth but continues to face margin compression due to commodity price lags, guiding for progressive margin recovery in H2 FY27.
* **Schneider Electric Infrastructure:** While benefiting from strong demand in emerging segments like data centers and renewables, profitability remains constrained by commodity inflation and fixed-price government contracts.
* **Shilchar Technologies:** Rebounded strongly in Q2 FY27 with 43% QoQ revenue growth and recovering North American exports, keeping it firmly on track for its annual targets.
* **Transformers and Rectifiers (TARIL):** Delivered strong Q2 FY27 results with a 66% YoY revenue jump and 203% EBITDA growth, backed by a robust Rs 7,237 crore order book.
* **Yash Highvoltage:** Reported a stellar H1 FY27 with revenue up 95% YoY and EBITDA margins expanding to 28.1%, underpinned by robust order inflows and progressive greenfield execution.
