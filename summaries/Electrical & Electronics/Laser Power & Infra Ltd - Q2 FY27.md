# Laser Power & Infra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sequential recovery in operational performance, lower finance costs post-IPO debt repayment, and strategic entry into high-voltage transmission with maiden HTLS and 220 kV underground project wins.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 12.5%–13.0% (historical H1 revenue seasonality of 40%–42% maintained).
- Tone and demand view: Stronger focus on high-voltage transmission opportunities (HTLS conductors and 220 kV underground cabling) driven by land and Right-of-Way (RoW) constraints in urban corridors.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Heavy rains caused temporary execution delays in EPC projects, deferring ~Rs 100 crore of EPC revenue and temporarily inflating inventory to Rs 946 crore; obtained provisional LOI for ~40 acres of land lease in Kharagpur.
- Last quarter's promises: Utilized ~Rs 490 crore from IPO proceeds to repay debt in Q2 FY27, initiating interest cost reductions (Q2 finance cost reduced sequentially to Rs 33 crore from Rs 36 crore in Q1).

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 628 crore, up 2.1% YoY (sequential growth of 20.5%).
  - Manufacturing Revenue: Rs 524 crore, up 29.9% YoY.
  - EPC Revenue: Rs 104 crore, down 51% YoY (impacted by heavy rains).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 76 crore, down ~5% YoY (sequential growth of 15.2%).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 12.1% (vs 13% in Q2 FY26).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 41 crore (vs Rs 29 crore in Q1 FY27 and Rs 45 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 31 crore, down 3.1% YoY (up 44.8% QoQ).
- **PAT Margin:** 4.9% (vs 4.1% in Q1 FY27).
- **Order Book:** ~Rs 2,543 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Manufacturing: ~Rs 1,303 crore; EPC: ~Rs 1,240 crore).
- **Order Inflow:** ~Rs 357 crore in Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 order inflow: ~Rs 449 crore).
- **Total Borrowings:** ~Rs 499 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
- Secured maiden commercial HTLS conductor order worth Rs 72.76 crore from Power Grid Corporation of India for supply and reconductoring, utilizing technology licensed from TS Conductors USA.
- Secured first 220 kV underground cabling EPC order worth Rs 44 crore from CESC Limited (1-year execution timeline).
- Dispatched manufacturing orders steadily, while deferred EPC execution of ~Rs 100 crore due to heavy rains is expected to recover in H2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Full-year EBITDA margin expected to stabilize between 12.5% and 13.0%.
- Full-year operating cash flow targeted to turn positive by year-end.
- Management expects working capital days to reduce by 25 to 40 days by Q4 FY27 from Q2 levels (166 days).
- H1 historically accounts for 40%–42% of full-year revenue, with the remaining 58%–60% expected in H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Urban grid expansion faces severe land acquisition and Right-of-Way (RoW) constraints, driving utility demand for HTLS conductors (higher power carrying capacity) and extra-high-voltage (EHV) underground cabling.
- Inter-state transmission remains overhead-dominated, but reconductoring of existing lines with specialized technologies (like TS Conductors) is gaining traction.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (primarily aluminum) remained stable during the quarter, aligning volume growth closely with revenue growth.
- Specialized conductors fetch higher margins than conventional conductors; management is actively reducing the share of commoditized conventional conductors.
- Manufacturing EBITDA margin typically ranges between 10%–11%, while EPC projects fetch 14%–16%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Received provisional Letter of Intent (LOI) and in-principle approval from West Bengal Industrial Development Corporation Limited for ~40 acres of land on long-term lease at Vidyasagar Industrial Park in Kharagpur (adjacent to current plant).
- Detailed capex and capacity addition plans for Kharagpur will be formulated and disclosed in upcoming quarters.
- Manufacturing capacity utilization is maintained around 70%–75% on a full-year basis.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt repayment of ~Rs 490 crore completed in Q2 FY27 using IPO proceeds; full benefit of interest savings to reflect in H2 FY27.
- Working capital expanded by ~Rs 100 crore QoQ to Rs 1,159 crore, primarily due to elevated finished goods/WIP inventory (Rs 946 crore vs Rs 710 crore in Q1) from rain-affected EPC installations.
- Trade receivables reduced to Rs 1,382 crore from Rs 1,416 crore in Q1 FY27.
- Working capital cycle improved sequentially to 166 days from 183 days in Q1 FY27 due to a higher revenue base.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Weather-related disruptions: Heavy monsoon rains severely delayed EPC execution, deferring revenue and stretching inventory.
- Working capital intensity: Inventory accumulation temporarily strained operating cash flows in H1 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vidit Trivedi (Asian Markets Securities) asked about the margin structure of specialized products and structural move to 13%–14% EBITDA margin.**
  - *Management answered:* Higher-margin specialized conductors and EHV cables will improve overall P&L margins progressively as their product mix increases.
- **Divyesh Sheth (Samitti Consultant) asked about tech differences and price competition in HTLS conductors.**
  - *Management answered:* TS Conductor technology represents a second-generation advanced platform providing lower overall cost structure and technical advantages, allowing competitive execution against older technologies even during tender pricing pressure.
- **Nishita asked about the reasons for the 51% YoY dip in EPC revenue and the target revenue mix.**
  - *Management answered:* The dip was caused by rain-led erection delays deferring ~Rs 100 crore revenue; optimal target revenue mix between manufacturing and EPC remains 75:25.
- **Rahul Chacko asked about the timeline for normalizing working capital days.**
  - *Management answered:* Working capital peaked in September and is expected to reduce by 25 to 40 days by Q4 FY27 as inventory and receivables convert into cash.
- **Divyesh Sheth (Samitti Consultant) asked for the product mix within the manufacturing order book.**
  - *Management answered:* Manufacturing order book of Rs 1,300 crore comprises ~85% cables and ~15% conductors.

### One-line takeaway
Laser Power & Infra expanded into high-voltage transmission with key HTLS and 220 kV wins, while post-IPO debt repayment positions H2 FY27 for lower finance costs and cash flow recovery.
