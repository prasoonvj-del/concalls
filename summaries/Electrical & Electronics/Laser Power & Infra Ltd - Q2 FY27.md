# Laser Power & Infra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Maiden entry into high-voltage transmission (HTLS and 220 kV underground orders) and sequential revenue/profitability recovery despite weather-related execution headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained EBITDA margin guidance of 12.5% to 13%. (No old numeric guidance range was provided last quarter, but management noted stable margins).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted toward tangible commercial validation in transmission, with management emphasizing the transition into specialized high-voltage products and underground cabling due to rising grid modernization and Right-of-Way constraints.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Heavy rains causing execution delays and deferred EPC revenue (~Rs 100 crores) emerged as a new operational headwind. Kharagpur land expansion progressed with a provisional LOI received.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Debt repayment via IPO proceeds (~Rs 490 crores) was completed as previously planned, with partial benefits reflecting in Q2 finance costs.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue from Operations:** Rs 628 crore (Up 2.1% YoY, up 20.5% QoQ)
- **Manufacturing Revenue:** Rs 524 crore (Up 29.9% YoY)
- **EPC Revenue:** Rs 104 crore (Down from Rs 212 crore YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,150 crore (Up 7.5% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 76 crore (Down ~5% YoY from Rs 80 crore, up 15.2% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 12.1% (vs 13% in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 140 crore (Margin at 12.3%)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 41 crore (vs Rs 45 crore in Q2 FY26 and Rs 29 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 31 crore (Broadly stable YoY vs Rs 32 crore; up 44.8% QoQ)
- **PAT Margin:** 4.9% (vs 4.1% in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 52 crore (Up 7.2% YoY)
- **Finance Cost:** Rs 33 crore (vs Rs 34 crore in Q2 FY26 and Rs 36 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Total Borrowings:** Rs 499 crore as of September 30, 2026 (post utilization of ~Rs 490 crore from IPO proceeds)
- **Working Capital:** Rs 1,159 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 1,059 crore in Q1 FY27); working capital days reduced to 166 days from 183 days
- **Inventory:** Rs 946 crore (vs Rs 710 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Trade Receivables:** Rs 1,382 crore (vs Rs 1,416 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Total Order Book:** Rs 2,543 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Manufacturing: Rs 1,303 crore; EPC: Rs 1,240 crore)
- **New Order Inflows:** Rs 357 crore in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 inflow at Rs 449 crore

### Highlights
- Secured first commercial HTLS conductor order worth Rs 72.76 crore from Power Grid for supply and reconductoring.
- Received first 220 kV underground transmission EPC order worth Rs 44 crore from CESC Limited, with an execution timeline of one year.
- Received provisional letter of intent and in-principle approval from West Bengal Industrial Development Corporation Limited for ~40 acres of land at Vidyasagar Industrial Park in Kharagpur on a long-term lease.
- Utilized ~Rs 490 crore of IPO proceeds to repay identified borrowings during Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margin to stabilize in the range of 12.5% to 13%.
- Management expects interest cost savings from IPO debt repayment to reflect more fully in H2, supporting overall PAT margin improvement.
- Management expects operating cash flow to turn positive for FY27 as manufacturing orders are dispatched and EPC inventory/receivables convert into cash.
- Management targets an improvement of approximately 25 to 40 working capital days by Q4 FY27.
- Typically, the company achieves 40% to 42% of full-year revenue in the first half, with the balance in the second half.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's power sector is experiencing rising electricity demand, rapid renewable energy additions, and transmission network expansion/grid modernization.
- Transmission corridors are increasingly constrained by land availability, right-of-way (RoW) approvals, and long implementation timelines, driving demand for high-capacity conductors (HTLS) and specialized underground execution capabilities in dense urban areas.
- Utilities are gradually adopting underground cabling over overhead distribution lines to improve network reliability against natural calamities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Manufacturing segment EBITDA margins typically range between 10% and 11%.
- EPC projects typically provide EBITDA margins in the range of 14% to 16%.
- Aluminium metal prices remained stable during the quarter.
- Finance costs stood at Rs 33 crore, capturing only partial-period benefits of the IPO debt repayment completed during Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity utilization is maintained at roughly 70% on a full-year basis.
- The Kharagpur land allotment is intended for an integrated power cable and conductor manufacturing facility to expand capabilities into higher-value transmission products.
- Management stated that the timing and scale of capital expenditure will be phased and disciplined, aligned with demand visibility, utilization, product strategy, and expected returns.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at approximately Rs 499 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Absolute working capital increased by ~Rs 100 crore to Rs 1,159 crore due to a temporary elevation in inventory (rising to Rs 946 crore) caused by execution delays from heavy rains.
- Trade receivables reduced sequentially from Rs 1,416 crore to Rs 1,382 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Heavy rains caused execution delays in EPC projects, deferring approximately Rs 100 crore of EPC revenue and impacting quarterly EBITDA.
- Elevated inventory levels temporarily affected operating cash flow during the period.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vidit Trivedi (Asian Markets Securities) asked about potential margin improvement over 2-3 years with specialized products and whether EBITDA margin can move to 13%-14%:** Management stated that specialized products carry higher margins, and as the product mix improves, it will positively impact the P&L.
2. **Divyesh Sheth (Consultant) asked about capacity utilization, volume growth, and segment mix:** Management noted that full-year capacity utilization is kept around 70%, volume growth tracked revenue growth due to stable aluminum prices, and the manufacturing mix will remain dominated by cables (80%-90%) while conductors represent 10%-15% (with a shift toward specialized variants).
3. **Nishita (Analyst) asked about the reasons for the drop in EPC revenues and quantify deferred revenue:** Management explained that heavy rains stalled project erection despite supplies, deferring roughly Rs 100 crore of EPC revenue which is expected to be recovered in subsequent quarters.
4. **Rahul Chacko (Analyst) asked about working capital normalization and order inflows:** Management stated that working capital peaked in September and is already normalizing, guiding for a 25 to 40 days reduction by year-end and positive operating cash flow for FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Laser Power & Infra entered the high-voltage transmission market with maiden HTLS and 220 kV underground orders while weathering seasonal execution delays, setting up H2 for cash flow recovery and debt-led interest cost savings.
