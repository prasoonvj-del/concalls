# Kanohar Electricals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-14 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by high-voltage transformers and healthy demand tailwinds.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 137.1 crore (up 103.5% YoY)
- Gross profit: Rs 57.5 crore (up 97.4% YoY)
- Gross margin: 41.9% (vs 43.2% in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 38.8 crore (up 152.2% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 28.3% (expansion of 550 bps YoY)
- PAT: Rs 27.3 crore (up 140% YoY)
- PAT margin: 19.9% (expansion of 300 bps YoY)
- Order book: Rs 2,026 odd crore as of June 30, 2026 (up 11% from Rs 1,818.3 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- Incremental order inflows: Rs 332.2 crore in Q1; Rs 867.3 crore in Q2 (including Rs 250+ crore from a Central PSU)
- Manufacturing volume: 1,725 MVA executed in Q1
- Total manufacturing capacity: ~19,200 MVA across Rithani and Gangol (Meerut)

### Highlights
- Celebrated maiden earnings call following a strong response to the company's IPO.
- Transformer business contributed 84.9% of revenue and EPC contributed 14.81% in Q1 FY27.
- Product mix shifted heavily towards premium ratings: 400 kV transformers accounted for 70.16% of Q1 revenue (up from 10.3% in FY24 and 52% in FY26).
- Backward integration covers transformer tanks, control panels, radiators, and insulation components for captive use to control quality and delivery.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets FY27 revenue of approximately Rs 950 crore with a broadly similar EBITDA margin profile as FY26.
- H2 is expected to remain the key execution period for the company.
- Tendering currently underway is largely for execution in FY29 and beyond.
- Existing capacity is sufficient through FY28; brownfield expansion of 18,000 MVA at Gangol is planned to be in place for FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural growth cycle driven by power demand, data centers, e-mobility, renewables evacuation, and intra-state transmission (e.g., Green Energy Corridor III with Rs 1.85 lakh crore outlay).
- Indian railway network electrification moving to 2x25 kV AC systems, creating demand for Scott-connected transformers (Railway market estimated at Rs 20,000 crore over next 5-10 years for traction transformers).
- High entry barriers due to mandatory field performance history and government lab short-circuit testing (taking 18 to 24 months for a 500 MVA unit). Kanohar has completed 200+ successful short-circuit tests.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Most contracts include IEEMA Price Variation Clauses to mitigate raw material volatility.
- Power transformers, Scott-connected transformers, and shunt reactors carry a broadly similar gross and EBITDA margin profile.
- Net working capital cycle anticipated to remain around 110-120 days (receivables ~120 days, inventory ~80 days, offset by payables of 70-80 days).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Brownfield expansion of 18,000 MVA planned at Gangol plant, funded partly via IPO proceeds, targeted for completion by end of FY28 for FY29 operations.
- Solar power plant of ~2,500 kW planned for power cost savings and sustainability.
- 765 kV technology developed in-house/indigenously; requires 3 to 4 years of field performance after first supply to become eligible for regular tenders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or cash balance numbers stated in the call transcript.
- Customers are primarily central/state transmission utilities and well-funded projects; no delinquencies reported.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain management identified as a key operational risk.
- Potential short-term supply-demand mismatch and margin pressure beyond the next 3-4 years as new players get qualified.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sharan (Unifi Capital) on raw material inflation and margins:** Management clarified that most contracts have IEEMA Price Variation Clauses protecting against raw material volatility.
- **Lovish (Burman Capital) on GEC-III impact:** Management stated that intra-state transmission bulk is in 400 kV, acting as a multi-decadal structural tailwind.
- **Mihir Manohar on industry supply and margins:** Management noted that while new entrants are expected and required to meet growing demand, the company's current order book maintains stable margins comparable to FY26.
- **Jinesh Karia on order book sustainability:** Management stated they do not guide for specific order book closing figures, but current activity will adequately cover projected revenues for FY28 and FY29.
- **Rosita Fernandes on capacity utilization and ramp-up:** Management stated that existing capacity is sufficient through FY28, with the 18,000 MVA brownfield expansion coming up by end of FY28 for FY29.
- **Vedant on 765 kV readiness:** Management confirmed the technology is developed indigenously without external tie-ups and the upcoming capacity will be suitable for 765 kV.

### One-line takeaway
Kanohar Electricals delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 103.5% revenue growth and 152.2% EBITDA growth, backed by high-share 400 kV transformer execution, robust order inflows, and long-term infrastructure tailwinds.
