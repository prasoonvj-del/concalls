# Solex Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management noted temporary regulatory turbulence and supply chain disruptions over the past three months that impacted margins and utilization, but operations have returned to 24/7 and order books remain intact.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: FY27 revenue guidance maintained; PAT margin expected to be impacted/lower than earlier due to Q2 challenges. FY29 cell line guidance initiated (EBITDA margin 23% to 25%, PAT margin 12% to 14%, revenue ~Rs 4,000 crore).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from Q1's policy uncertainty to normalization, with operations back to 24/7 and expectations to cover targets in the next 4.5 months.
- New topics or risks: Introduction of Tapi RQ quarter cell modules; unexpected surge in shipping costs from $900-$1,000 to $3,500 per container; conversion of non-fund-based limits to fund-based increasing cost of funds; Rs 50 crore CCD raised in subsidiary for cell line. Old risks of ALMM policy friction persisted through the quarter.
- Last quarter's promises: Land and environmental clearance issues for the cell line were cleared as promised, with ground breaking soon and execution still targeted for December 2027.

### Key numbers
- Cell line capex: Rs 500 crore to Rs 600 crore per gigawatt
- BESS manufacturing total capex: Rs 250 crore
- Subsidiary funding: Rs 50 crore CCD proposed/raised for cell line
- Shipping costs: Surged from $900–$1,000 to $3,500 per container
- FY29 guidance: Revenue ~Rs 4,000 crore, EBITDA margin 23% to 25%, PAT margin 12% to 14%

### Highlights
- Operations returned to 24/7 capacity utilization following a 3-month disruption caused by policy and supply chain challenges.
- Introduced Tapi RQ (Quarter Cell Module) technology, working on certification and production line conversion.
- Cleared land and environmental clearance challenges for the upcoming cell line project; ground breaking expected soon.
- Signed a term sheet and raised a Rs 50 crore CCD in the subsidiary for the cell line project.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to cover revenue and margin targets within the next 4.5 months.
- Cell line commercial production targeted for December 2027; FY29 guidance projects revenue of ~Rs 4,000 crore, EBITDA margin of 23% to 25%, and PAT margin of 12% to 14%.
- BESS manufacturing line targeted to start by September 2027.
- Evaluating potential manufacturing opportunities in the US market.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ALMM implementation initially caused industry struggles due to a lack of domestic cell capacity, leading to project extensions.
- Long-term Indian solar demand is expected to increase by three times, driven by the national green grid and BESS adoption.
- Industry nameplate capacity vs. actual utilization averages around 50%; management believes actual active TOPCon capacity closely matches current demand, avoiding a true overcapacity scenario.
- Majority of current supply remains non-DCR due to grandfathered import provisions and domestic cell shortages.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed costs remained flat against lower capacity utilization, impacting profitability during the challenging quarter.
- Conversion of non-fund-based limits to fund-based limits increased the cost of funds.
- Management absorbed unexpected shipping cost increases ($900-$1,000 to $3,500 per container) to honor customer commitments rather than passing costs or canceling orders.
- PAT margin for H1 was impacted by these operational and logistics headwinds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Cell line capex is budgeted at Rs 500 crore to Rs 480/600 crore per gigawatt.
- BESS assembly line capex is estimated at Rs 250 crore, with consultants appointed for documentation.
- Long-term roadmap includes a 5 GW solar cell and 10 GW BESS assembly under a Rs 4,000–4,500 crore MOU with the Gujarat government.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Raised Rs 50 crore via Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) in the subsidiary for the cell line.
- Shifted certain non-fund-based limits to fund-based limits, raising funding costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical supply chain disruptions affecting raw material availability.
- Sudden spikes in shipping and freight costs.
- Shortage, high costs, and quality variability of domestic DCR cells.
- Timing mismatches in policy implementation (ALMM-2/ALCM) affecting project execution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vinita F (Credence Investments) asked about module warranty provisions:** Management stated zero warranty claims to date, with 30-year warranties backed by Munich Re insurance premiums.
- **Manan Dalal (Dalal Family Office) asked about sustainability of lower other expenses and handling cell price volatility:** Management explained fixed costs weighed on a lower revenue base and non-fund limits converted to fund-based; larger orders have currency/cell price immunity clauses.
- **Gunesh Wagh (Aavya Advisors) asked about industry overcapacity:** Management clarified that nameplate capacity (230 GW) differs drastically from actual utilization (~50%) and usable TOPCon capacity, mitigating overcapacity fears.
- **Dhanraj Tolani (Kuber Advisors) asked about cell line capex and funding:** Management stated Rs 500–600 crore/GW capex and noted a Rs 50 crore CCD was raised in the subsidiary for funding.
- **Mahender Manral asked how management will fund the large multi-thousand-crore capex roadmap without massive dilution:** Management responded they will execute in phased pieces and use structured debt initially before equity conversion to protect shareholder value.

### One-line takeaway
Despite recent regulatory friction and logistics cost spikes that compressed margins, Solex has restored 24/7 operations and remains firmly on track with its backward integration timelines for cell and BESS manufacturing.
