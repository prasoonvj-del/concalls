# Virtuoso Optoelectronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue growth (78% standalone, 83% consolidated) and significant PAT expansion despite Q2 being a seasonally lean period.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained long-term CAGR guidance of 30+%; no specific numerical revenue/growth guidance given for FY27/FY28.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to reflect early onset of the season in H2 and a strong order book across segments, though Q2 remained lean as expected.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Star Eltech (Chennai) facility reported as currently on hold due to lessors (not in management's control).
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Compressor and EMS expansions remain on track for commissioning by end of FY27/December.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 534.00 crore, up 78% YoY from Rs 300.49 crore.
- **Standalone Absolute EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 51.16 crore, up 60% YoY from Rs 31.90 crore.
- **Standalone Absolute PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 10.22 crore, up 213% YoY from Rs 3.27 crore.
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 544.80 crore, up 83% YoY from Rs 302.56 crore.
- **Consolidated Absolute EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 56.00 crore, up 77% YoY from Rs 31.70 crore.
- **Consolidated Absolute PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 11.74 crore, up 213% YoY from Rs 2.54 crore (approx. from Rs 2.54 cr to Rs 11.74 cr).
- **Compressor Capacity & Utilization:** Current capacity at 2.8 million units (operating at 70%–75% utilization); expanding to 6.0 million units by Q4 FY27.
- **Compressor ASP:** Rs 1,400 to Rs 1,500.
- **Debt Structure:** Subsidiary VCPL issued Rs 150 crore in Optionally Convertible Debentures (OCDs) held by True North as a bridge arrangement; average cost of bank borrowings between 8.25% and 8.4%.

### Highlights
- Q2 is seasonally a lean period, but the company posted strong H1 performance.
- EMS capacity addition is under commissioning, expected to go live by December (primarily for captive controller manufacturing initially).
- AC indoor unit (IDU) expansion is 70% complete, with the remaining 30% expected by December end; outdoor unit (ODU) expansion will follow.
- Deep freezer capacity expansion from 1.5 lakh to 2.5 lakh units planned ahead of next season (June/July).
- Star Eltech (Chennai) manufacturing facility is currently on hold due to lessor issues.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a consistent long-term CAGR of 30%+ over a 5-year block.
- Anticipates strong H2 (Q3 and Q4) driven by encouraging order books and early season kick-offs.
- Compressor capacity utilization expected at 40%–60% for FY28 on the expanded 6 million unit capacity.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Outsourcing mix for the industry is maintained at 40% to 60%.
- AC season kicked in slightly earlier than anticipated.
- Commercial refrigeration shows a strong order book with optimal capacity utilization expected in the next 6 months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins expected to remain range-bound between 9% and 10%.
- Compressor margins are currently lower due to competition with imported prices, but expected to improve with backward integration and reduced imports starting Q4.
- Cost pass-through to brands is an ongoing process across products, with effects visible in Q3.
- PLI inflows: Expected Rs 20 crore in Q4 for the current year, and another Rs 20 crore next year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Compressor Phase 2 expansion (taking capacity to 6 million units) expected to go live by mid-to-end of Q4 FY27.
- EMS capacity expandable up to 3 lines; going live by December.
- Deep freezer capacity moving from 1.5 lakh to 2.5 lakh units by June/July.
- Backward integration in IDUs is at 85%–90%, ODUs at 60%, and compressors targeted to scale from 10%–25% up to 60% over the next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity endeavour is to keep it below 1.
- Rs 150 crore of total debt is in the form of OCDs issued by subsidiary VCPL, which management plans to replace with bank debt over the next 15–16 months.
- Inventory increased substantially in H1 to prepare for the upcoming season and manage expiring import allowances; expected to normalize in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Star Eltech facility in Chennai is on hold due to lessor-related issues outside management's control.
- Margin pressure on compressors due to competition with imported Chinese prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Piyush Jain asked about compressor margins and utilization:** Management stated compressor margins are currently lower than other segments due to imported price competition, but will improve via backward integration starting Q4. FY28 capacity utilization is expected at 40%–60%.
- **Kunal Tokas asked about the contribution of anchor clients in ACs:** Management replied that the anchor client will continue to account for 40% to 50% of overall AC volumes.
- **Hemant Soni asked about revenue contribution from Nashik Phase 2:** Management refrained from giving a specific H2 revenue estimate, reiterating the 30%–35% multi-year CAGR target.
- **Siddharth Jain asked about the Rs 150 crore debentures in VCPL:** Management clarified that True North holds the debentures as a bridge arrangement, which will be converted into bank debt over 15–16 months (not equity).
- **Rishabh Agrawal asked about EMS captive vs external usage:** Management clarified that the newly added EMS capacity is initially for captive controller consumption (representing ~INR 3,000 of a INR 20,000 AC BOM), with scalability for external sales.

### One-line takeaway
Virtuoso Optoelectronics delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 78% standalone revenue growth and is scaling capacities in compressors, EMS, and ACs to sustain a 30%+ long-term CAGR.
