# Solarworld Energy Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth offset by margin compression due to sudden raw material price volatility and delayed LOAs, though management expects a significant recovery in FY28.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 244 crore (up 77% YoY from Rs 137.9 crore)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 15.2 crore (vs Rs 18.1 crore in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 5.9%
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 4.2 crore (vs Rs 9.3 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 412 crore (up 100% YoY from Rs 206 crore)
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 35.8 crore; EBITDA margin: 8.3%
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 13.7 crore
- Consolidated order book (as of October 31, 2026): Rs 3,856 crore (Solar EPC & O&M: Rs 2,718 crore; BESS EPC: Rs 1,138 crore or ~30%)
- BESS orders: 582 MW AC and ~1.3 GWh DC
- First equity tranche for Narmadapuram solar cell JV: Rs 26.8 crore (made on October 30, 2026)
- Net worth (as of September 30, 2026): Rs 862 crore
- Debt-to-equity ratio (as of September 30, 2026): 0.46x
- FY27 guidance: Revenue of Rs 1,600 crore to Rs 1,700 crore; EBITDA margin of 6% to 7%; PAT margin of 5% to 6% (implying Rs 80 crore to Rs 90 crore PAT)
- FY28 target: Revenue of ~Rs 3,000 crore; PAT of Rs 200 crore to Rs 220 crore

### Highlights
- Fully automated BESS manufacturing facility commissioned with KUKA robotics; ~1 GW of its manufacturing capacity is covered by orders in hand.
- 1.5 GW module manufacturing facility in Roorkee is operational and ALMM listed; 5 GW junction box manufacturing line has commenced operations.
- 2.4 GW solar cell JV facility in Narmadapuram, Madhya Pradesh is targeted for commercial production in June 2027.
- *Note on Record:* Unlike past aggressive backward integration pacing where execution timelines slipped, management stated the Roorkee module plant is operational and the solar cell JV is progressing toward June 2027 commissioning.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY28 to be a "bumper year" with significantly improved margins due to easing raw material costs and higher-priced order execution.
- BESS line is projected to contribute almost half of top-line and profitability next year.
- Long-term target over the next 2 to 3 years is to achieve a top-line of ~Rs 5,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's renewable transition is moving toward an ecosystem generating, storing, and delivering power reliably.
- Utility-scale BESS market in India is projected to be around 350 GWh over the next 5 to 6 years; total addressable market (TAM) is very large.
- Solar EPC activity is facing a temporary slowdown (expected to last another 12 months) driven by grid connectivity issues and the need for BESS to address the duck curve.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Copper and aluminum prices have started to come down, which management expects will boost margins.
- Most PSU contracts (NTPC, SJVN, SECI) are fixed-price with no pass-through mechanism, leading to margin dips during metal super-cycles.
- BESS raw material (battery-grade lithium carbonate) prices dropped from ~200,000 Yuan/tonne 6 months ago to ~90,000 Yuan/tonne.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Evaluating a 2 GWh lithium-ion cell manufacturing facility in India (withdrawn PLI application due to overcrowding); decision expected by mid-December 2026.
- Evaluating a data center EPC joint venture with an overseas player for India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Focused on cash flow management, working capital efficiency, and capital allocation.
- Board is evaluating short-term actions (including potential buybacks) to address shareholder wealth erosion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sudden and sharp volatility in raw material prices (metals) between bid submission and delayed LOA issuance by PSUs (e.g., 4-5 month delays from NTPC) severely impacted current-year margins.
- Pre-approval requirements and restrictions on exporting cell manufacturing equipment from China pose execution monitoring risks.
- *Note on Record:* Margin guidance was lowered again (from earlier 10.5% to 6-7%), continuing the pattern of guidance adjustments noted in past disclosures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Atul Mehra (Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund) on profitability roadmap and shareholder value:** Management guided FY27 PAT at Rs 80-90 crore and FY28 PAT at Rs 200-220 crore. On shareholder value loss, management noted the board takes feedback seriously and is evaluating short-term actions (like a buyback) to be communicated during the quarter.
2. **Rahul Chacko (Analyst) on fixed pricing and hedging:** Management clarified that PSU contracts are fixed-price without pass-throughs, while private contracts do have pass-throughs. Direct hedging for processed metals (copper, steel, aluminum) is not feasible.
3. **Udit Sehgal (Analyst) on BESS market and cell manufacturing:** Management stated BESS revenue next year should reach Rs 1,500-1,800 crore. Regarding the 2 GWh cell plant, they withdrew the PLI application due to 23 bidders but are assessing Chinese equipment export restrictions.
4. **Parag Prakash Hemdev (Investor) on disclosure discrepancies and guidance cuts:** Challenged management on annual report risk disclosures versus actual margin collapse and repeated guidance cuts. Management explained that unexpected outlier metal price spikes combined with 4-5 month LOA delays by PSUs prevented effective pre-procurement hedging.
5. **Parveen Malhotra (Analyst) on solar cell JV technology and asset-light model:** Management stated the Narmadapuram plant uses TOPCon technology with low obsolescence risk for 4-5 years in India, and 70-80% will be consumed internally. They confirmed Solarworld remains an asset-light EPC player, using backward integration strictly to support EPC.

### One-line takeaway
Solarworld faced severe margin compression in H1 FY27 due to fixed-price PSU contracts and metal price volatility, but management expects a sharp recovery in FY28 driven by scaling BESS contributions, easing raw material costs, and operational backward integration.
