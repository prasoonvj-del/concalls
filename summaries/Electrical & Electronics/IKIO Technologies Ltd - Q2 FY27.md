# IKIO Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and margin expansion driven by successful diversification into non-lighting segments and international markets, with management signaling confidence in exceeding initial growth targets.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management indicated full-year growth will likely exceed initial expectations (previously 18–20%), now targeting 35%–50% growth in the "other business" segment.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious due to geopolitical headwinds" to "confident" as the company successfully navigated supply chain pressures and achieved strong growth.
- **New topics:** Automotive lighting (first quarter of supply, Rs 5 crore revenue), plans to add dashcams, and EV chargers (R&D complete, trial phase).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Block 2 partially commercialized in Q2 (delivered).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 209 crore (up 27% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 378 crore (up 33% YoY).
- **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 28 crore (up 52% YoY).
- **Q2 EBITDA Margin:** 13.4% (up 220 bps YoY).
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 51 crore (up 71% YoY).
- **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 13.4% (vs 10.4% in H1 FY26).
- **Q2 PAT:** Rs 13 crore (up 18% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 24 crore (up 80% YoY).
- **Other business segment revenue (H1):** Rs 289 crore (up 47% YoY).
- **Automotive lighting revenue (Q2):** Rs 5 crore.

### Highlights
- Successfully transitioning from traditional lighting to an integrated technology-driven platform.
- Export revenue reached Rs 57 crore in H1, accounting for 50% of total revenue mix.
- Automotive lighting segment launched; currently serving aftermarket, with OEM entry planned.
- *Record Check:* The call confirms the ongoing capacity expansion (Block 2 partial, Block 3 on track), consistent with the record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year growth to be better than initially anticipated.
- "Other business" segment growth guidance: 35%–50% for the year.
- Long-term consolidated EBITDA margin target: 16%–18% once new facilities/verticals reach steady-state efficiency.
- EV chargers: Trial phase ongoing; results expected in 1-2 quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Automotive lighting: Seeing strong traction; transitioning from conventional to LED.
- Middle East: Export growth of 70%–80% QoQ despite geopolitical tensions.
- Home lighting: Steady state; focusing on diversifying the customer base beyond the single anchor client.
- B2B focus: Management reiterated they will remain in B2B and not enter B2C.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins expanding due to operational efficiencies in newer verticals.
- Raw materials: Electronic components, aluminum, and plastic.
- Inventory strategy: Use of shared inventory for components (e.g., drivers) helps manage lead times and price volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Block 2: Partially commercialized in Q2 (approx. 2 lakh sq. ft.); generated Rs 20–22 crore revenue in Q2.
- Block 3: Construction on track; expected completion by Q3 FY27; capacity to exceed 1 lakh sq. ft. due to revised norms.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital for non-lighting business: 75–90 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and policy/duty changes remain a monitoring point.
- Operational challenges during the transition of orders to new facilities (expected to be sorted by Q3).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankur Gulati (Genuity Capital) asked about subsidiary margins.** Management noted 16.2% for subsidiaries and reiterated 16–18% long-term consolidated target.
- **Rohit (MAPL) asked about Block 2 revenue.** Management stated Rs 20–22 crore in Q2, with potential for Rs 150–200 crore at full utilization.
- **Harsh (Suraag Capital) asked about EMS business.** Management clarified 20–25% is captive, rest is for external customers.
- **Ankur Gulati asked about automotive lighting.** Management confirmed they are the first in India to manufacture the LED source in-house.
- **Rohit (MAPL) asked about Honeywell.** Management noted a strategic pipeline exists but cannot disclose details due to NDA.

### One-line takeaway
IKIO is successfully scaling its diversified non-lighting verticals and international footprint, with management signaling confidence in exceeding initial growth targets despite geopolitical volatility.
