# Atlanta Electricals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Healthy H1 revenue and profit growth offset by temporary Q2 execution headwinds and gross margin moderation due to heavy rainfall and elevated commodity prices.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** INR390.1 crores, up 23.1% YoY (from INR317 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Profit:** INR103.6 crores, up 6.1% YoY (from INR97.8 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Profit Margin:** 26.6% (compared to 30.9% in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR59.6 crores, up 8.8% YoY (from INR54.8 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 15.3%, contracting by ~201 bps YoY (from 17.3% in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR32.1 crores, up 28% YoY (from INR25.1 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 8.2% (compared to 7.9% in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 Finance Costs:** INR8.5 crores (down from INR13.2 crores YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 Basic & Diluted EPS:** INR4.18 (compared to INR3.50 in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** INR856.4 crores, up 35.5% YoY (from INR632.1 crores in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** INR230.9 crores, up 28.6% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR136.7 crores, up 32% YoY (from INR103.6 crores in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 16.0% (compared to 16.4% in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR79.0 crores, up 40.4% YoY (from INR56.2 crores in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 9.2% (compared to 8.9% in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Depreciation & Amortization:** INR20.1 crores (compared to INR7.9 crores in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 MVA Produced:** 8,664 MVA.
- **Order Book (as of Sept 30, 2026):** INR3,196.97 crores, up 54.5% YoY (compared to INR3,116.63 crores as of June 26th and ~INR2,069 crores a year ago). Includes INR184 crores of 400 kV class orders.
- **Inquiry Pipeline:** ~INR19,464 crores.
- **Q2 FY27 Order Inflow:** ~INR421 crores.
- **Deferred Deliveries (Q2 end):** ~INR180 crores (~70% already dispatched in October).
- **Inventory (as of Sept 30, 2026):** INR632.9 crores (compared to INR364.1 crores as of March 31, 2026).

### Highlights
- Unusual heavy rainfall and waterlogging in Gujarat caused precautionary manufacturing shutdowns and customer site disruptions in Q2, resulting in ~INR180 crores of finished orders being deferred (not cancelled). ~70% of these were dispatched in October.
- Gross margins moderated due to elevated commodity/raw material prices (transformer oil, copper, steel) and execution of firm price orders booked in the previous year (accounting for ~15% of quarterly revenue).
- Added 12 new customers in Q2, including the first US export customer, new state utilities, and renewable energy players.
- Secured a LoI worth INR193.92 crores from APTRANSCO for 12 units of 160 MVA, 220 kV class autotransformers.
- Plant capacity utilizations (as stated by CFO): Unit 1 (Anand) at 60%, Unit 2 at 40%, Bangalore plant at 90%, Vadod facility at 30%, and Jambusar (765 kV plant) at 7% to 8%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 and FY28 EBITDA margin guidance at 16% to 18%.
- Management re-affirms its 3-year revenue growth aspiration of ~40% CAGR from the time of listing, expecting to maintain it over the next 2 years (FY27 and FY28).
- Medium-term aspiration for exports to contribute ~15% of overall revenue within the next 2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Healthy underlying demand across transmission and distribution, renewable energy, battery energy storage systems (BESS, with active inquiries of ~3,400 MVA worth INR184 crores), data center infrastructure, and industrial applications.
- India's power infrastructure sector represents a multi-year growth opportunity driven by T&D investments and renewable integration.
- U.S. market traction shows active inquiries of ~120 transformers aggregating to 9,300 MVA.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity price volatility (transformer oil, copper, steel) impacted gross margins (26.6% vs 30.9% YoY).
- Firm price contracts accounted for ~10% to 12% of the current order book; management stated that remaining firm price orders will be substantially executed in Q3 with price revisions successfully negotiated with customers.
- Majority of orders carry price variation clauses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **IDT Plant (Inverter Duty Transformer):** Civil construction complete, machinery installation underway, commissioning expected before end of Q3 FY27. Planned capex: INR65 crores. Adds ~5,000 MVA capacity, releasing Unit 2 capacity for 66 and 132 kV classes.
- **Tank & Radiator Backward Integration (AE components):** Layout and machinery finalized, ground breaking completed. Planned capex: ~INR193 crores, targeted commissioning by October 2027, funded primarily through internal accruals with bank term loans tied up as backup.
- **Higher Voltage Initiatives:** Vadod facility received multiple customer approvals; manufacturing of first 315 MVA, 400 kV class transformer commenced October 26th, targeting commercial contribution in Q4. For 765 kV, in-house design started (international technology partnership dropped due to sanction constraints), targeting production start in Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased to INR632.9 crores due to finished goods awaiting dispatch/acceptance and raw material procurement.
- Capex is planned to be funded through internal accruals; long-term debt tied up with banks as a backup but unavailed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Weather disruptions (heavy rainfall/typhoons) affecting manufacturing and customer site readiness.
- Commodity price volatility (copper, steel, transformer oil).
- Supply chain disruptions caused by typhoons in China/Japan and Middle East war impacting container availability and critical components.
- International sanction-related constraints forcing a pivot away from an international technology partner for 765 kV development.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Teena Virmani (Motilal Oswal) on 765 kV plant status:** Management stated that while the international tech partner talks were dropped due to sanctions, they are confident in internal design capabilities, have started design/material ordering, and will follow CEA-standardized designs.
2. **Kunal Mehta on order inflow slowdown (INR421 crores in Q2):** Management clarified it was a conscious management decision to cautiously book 220 kV and below orders given existing visibility, while shifting focus to 400+ kV orders going forward. Firm price orders comprise only 3% to 4% (or 10-12% as per CFO clarification) of the order book and have been re-priced.
3. **Rohan on firm price contract impact on Q3:** Management stated they re-approached customers due to Middle East war impacts and successfully secured price revisions on remaining firm price orders, so Q3 margins will not be negatively impacted.
4. **Jainam on EBITDA margin expectations for H2:** Management reiterated confidence in achieving 16%-18% EBITDA margins for FY27 and FY28, supported by operating leverage, higher H2 execution, and improved capacity utilization.
5. **Hemant Soni on 40% CAGR timeline:** Management confirmed the 40% CAGR commitment applies to the 3-year period from listing, with 2 years remaining (FY27 and FY28).

### One-line takeaway
Atlanta Electricals reported a 35.5% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27 despite Q2 weather disruptions and raw material headwinds, and management remains confident in achieving its 40% CAGR growth and 16-18% EBITDA margin guidance backed by a robust order book and upcoming high-voltage capacity expansions.
