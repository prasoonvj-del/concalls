# Tempsens Instruments (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-22 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and export growth, with management investing ahead of time for future capacity and organizational expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 118 crore, up 33% YoY from Rs 89 crore.
- **Temperature Sensing Revenue:** Rs 60 crore, up 42% YoY (51% of total product revenue).
- **Electrical Heating Revenue:** Rs 25 crore, up 148.8% YoY.
- **Specialized Cables Revenue:** Rs 32.5 crore, down 8.8% YoY.
- **Domestic Revenue:** Rs 78 crore, up 18.5% YoY.
- **Export Revenue:** Rs 39.8 crore, up 78.3% YoY (34% of total revenue vs. 25% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 26.6 crore, up 15.7% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22%, down 330 bps YoY from 25.3%.
- **Gross Material Margin:** 47.4% (vs. 49.3% in Q1 last year, 46.9% in FY25, and 46% in FY26).
- **Employee Cost:** Rs 20.4 crore, up 45% YoY (taking ~140 bps impact).
- **ESOP Charge:** Rs 57 lakh in Q1 FY27 with no comparable charge in the previous year.
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 21.4 crore, up 16.2% YoY.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 16.3 crore, up 15.6% YoY.
- **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 17.3 crore, up ~15% YoY.
- **Total Borrowings (as of March 2026):** Rs 78 crore.
- **Inventory:** Approximately Rs 110 crore to Rs 120 crore (65% to 70% raw materials, balance WIP and finished goods).
- **Global Market Size (TAM estimated by F&S for 2025):** Temperature sensors ~$4.3 billion, Heating solutions ~$13.2 billion, Specialized cables ~$33 billion (Total over $50 billion, growing 7% to 8% to $75 billion by 2030).
- **Aggregate Peak Revenue Potential of 4 new expansion projects (commissioning Q4 FY27 to Q3 FY28):** ~Rs 500 crore at full utilization against FY26 revenue base of Rs 445 crore.

### Highlights
- Cross-sale reached nearly Rs 267 crore in FY26 (60% of revenue, >30% growth YoY).
- Top 10 customers accounted for 19% of FY26 revenue; no end market exceeds 22% of revenue. Two-third of revenue comes from projects and OEMs, one-third from recurring replacement business.
- Utilized Rs 55 crore of IPO fresh issue proceeds (total Rs 95 crore) to repay borrowings.
- Acquired land for ~Rs 25 crore for specialized cable expansion from internal accruals.
- Acquired an 80,000 sq. ft. facility with an existing shed for the Victura Technologies JV (production expected to commence Q4 FY27; peak revenue potential ~Rs 120 crore).
- New EDGE series pyrometers co-developed with Micro-Epsilon slated for dispatches in Q3 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue growth for the year to be in line with the blended growth over the last 3 years.
- Management expects margins to remain broadly similar to last year's margin profile as seeded projects take off and Q1 investments begin to pay back.
- Profit growth is expected to be in line with revenue growth by the end of the year.
- Management expects OEM part and new product contributions to exceed 25% of revenue starting FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Four major tailwinds: Electrification of process heat, alternative energy (fuel cells, green hydrogen), China-plus-one factor, and continued investments in oil and gas and steel.
- Export markets expanded into Mexico, Poland, and South Korea, alongside existing growth in Southeast Asia and North America.
- Medium-voltage heaters (up to 15 megawatts) targeting carbon capture, battery storage, and green hydrogen are tested up to 7.2 kilowatts; explosion-proof certification targeted by Q4 FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 330 bps EBITDA margin contraction driven by high base gross margins in Q1 last year (49.3%), 140 bps impact from employee cost increases/headcount additions/ESOPs, and lower revenue contribution from recently acquired businesses during their integration phase.
- Raw materials are booked at the time of receiving orders, passing price fluctuations through to customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Unit 1 (Brownfield):** ~50,000 sq. ft. for OEM contact sensors; expected commissioning Q3 FY28; peak revenue potential ~Rs 120 crore.
- **Unit 6:** ~50,000 sq. ft. plant for medium-voltage heaters; expected completion Q1 FY28; peak revenue potential ~Rs 200 crore.
- **Unit 8 (Greenfield):** Specialized cables plant construction started; expected completion Q2 FY28; peak revenue potential ~Rs 60 crore.
- **Heating custom-built furnaces expansion:** ~25,000 sq. ft. addition planned this year.
- **Victura JV facility:** 80,000 sq. ft.; production by Q4 FY27; peak revenue potential ~Rs 120 crore.
- Existing capacity utilization is running at about 80%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO fresh issue proceeds of Rs 55 crore used to reduce borrowings from the Rs 78 crore level reported in March 2026.
- Land acquisition of ~Rs 25 crore funded via internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as material risks by management, though geopolitical situations were discussed with management stating they view them mid-term as a positive catalyst for electrification.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pratik Dharmshi (Union Mutual Fund) asked about the addressable market and evolution of newer segments like Micro-Epsilon pyrometers and fuel cells/semiconductors.**
   - *Answer:* Aryan Rathi stated the Micro-Epsilon market is a ~Rs 3,000 crore market where Tempsens addresses <1%. Medium-voltage heaters face limited competition. Overall OEM and new product contributions are expected to exceed 25% of revenue starting FY28. Margins across newer products are expected to remain broadly similar.
2. **Sahil (S R Investment) asked about order trajectory and run-rates for fuel cells.**
   - *Answer:* Vinay Rathi and Aryan Rathi noted they are in advanced stages of field trials and run-ups, focusing on executing at scale, and will disclose exact figures as projects are executed.
3. **Aman (Advisory) asked about competitive intensity, industry moats, and customer switching costs.**
   - *Answer:* Vinay Rathi explained that products represent <1% of a plant's cost but are critical safety/efficiency items. Vendor approvals take 3 to 5 years initially due to strict certifications (e.g., explosion-proof, ASME U Stamp), creating high entry barriers, with 75% to 90% of revenue coming from critical segments.
4. **Naman Parmar asked about replacement cycle frequency and segment margin profiles.**
   - *Answer:* Vinay Rathi stated replacement cycles vary widely from a few hours (molten aluminium) to 20 years (nuclear plants), making up 30% of business. Blended margins are shared, while temperature sensors and electrical heaters carry slightly higher margins than specialized cables. Priyanka Menaria added inventory stands at ~Rs 110-120 crore, with price fluctuations passed to customers.
5. **Jenish Karia asked about short-term/long-term headwinds from political situations and order book visibility for new-age industries.**
   - *Answer:* Vinay Rathi stated no negative revenue impact is expected in upcoming quarters; mid-term, it should positively drive process electrification. Heavy industry order books run at 4 to 4.5 months, while OEM players (like Victura and long-term furnace contracts) offer 9 to 12 months or multi-year visibility.

### One-line takeaway
Tempsens reported a strong Q1 FY27 with 33% revenue growth and aggressive capacity/OEM investments across thermal loops, positioning itself for multi-year scaling ahead of four key industrial tailwinds.
