# Cello World Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Subdued consumer demand, inflationary pressures, and input cost headwinds impacted volumes, though management remains hopeful for demand normalization.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs. 526.7 crore (or Rs. 527 crores)
- EBITDA margin: 22.2%
- PAT: Rs. 73.4 crore
- PAT margin: 13.9%
- Gross margin (Company level): 52.4%
- Writing Instrument YoY growth: 52%
- Molded Furniture and Allied products revenue: Rs. 80 crore
- E-commerce revenue contribution: 16.3%
- General trade revenue contribution: 71.1%
- Export revenue contribution: 7.3%
- Modern trade revenue contribution: 5.3%
- Consumer Ware gross margin: 55%
- Writing Instrument gross margin: 53.8%
- Molded Furniture gross margin: 39.5%
- Glassware capacity utilization: ~60%
- Opalware capacity utilization: 80%-85%
- Price hikes taken: 7% to 20% across product categories (12% to 13% average at the consumer level)

### Highlights
- Implemented price increases across most product categories to counter rising input costs.
- Writing Instrument division delivered a healthy performance driven by the Cello brand.
- In-house steel bottle manufacturing commenced at the Rajasthan facility with 8 operational lines, though yet to reach optimal utilization.
- E-commerce momentum remained strong, reaching 16.3% of overall revenues with healthy profitability.
- Wim Plast merger share allotment is delayed due to technical glitches, expected to be completed in a few weeks.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide specific annual guidance for FY27 due to the tough and dynamic environment, preferring to reassess next quarter.
- Expects performance to steadily improve as demand conditions normalize.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer demand remained subdued due to inflationary pressures and macroeconomic uncertainties impacting discretionary spending.
- Glassware scale-up was slower than anticipated due to continued dumping from China.
- Weaker export demand witnessed for most products during the quarter.
- Channel inventory has dropped as primary sales slowed and partners liquidated older stock; secondary sales have improved, signaling a better festive season ahead.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gas prices for the glass business surged ~80% compared to March levels, impacting margins as the increase could not be fully passed on during the quarter.
- Crude oil volatility impacted polymer and PVC raw material prices, prompting price hikes of 7% to 20%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major capex planned for FY27 apart from maintenance capex and small additions of a few lines in the steel segment.
- Steel facility has 8 operational lines (targeting 50-55 active SKUs out of 150 historical SKUs) with a peak revenue potential of ~Rs. 300 crore.
- Glassware facility peak revenue potential is estimated at Rs. 250 to 275 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash is being preserved for potential inorganic growth opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued dumping from China impacting Glassware scale-up.
- Soaring gas and polymer/PVC raw material costs due to geopolitical conflicts and crude volatility.
- Subdued discretionary consumer demand causing volume drop post-price hikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital) asked about the Glassware unit ramp-up and China dumping:** Management replied that capacity utilization is 60% due to the clearance of older stock, and revenues are up 30-35% YoY with profitability near. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Utkarsh Nopany asked about gross margin sustainability and price hike impact:** Management stated gross margins are expected to stay in the 54%-55% band and improve, with 90% of price hike impacts already visible in Q1. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Akhil Parekh asked about the peak potential of glass and steel facilities:** Management quantified peak glass plant revenue at Rs. 250-275 crore and current steel plant capacity at ~Rs. 300 crore. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Percy Panthaki asked about promoter stake sale rumors:** Management pointed to the already issued exchange clarification and declined further comment. *(Management deflected/avoided details).*

### One-line takeaway
Cello World reported a muted Q1 FY27 with flat-to-soft revenues as aggressive price hikes and steel product stock-outs impacted volumes, though management anticipates a strong recovery led by channel inventory normalization and scaling in-house steel and glassware operations.
