# Merritronix Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-27 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial growth with revenue up 91.36% YoY and net profit more than tripling.

### Key numbers
- Total Income: INR 107.21 crores, up 91.36% YoY (vs INR 56.03 crores in H1 FY26).
- EBITDA: INR 15.99 crores, up 94.76% YoY.
- EBITDA Margin: 14.91%, up 26 bps YoY.
- Net Profit: INR 12.09 crores, up 223.72% YoY (vs INR 3.73 crores in H1 FY26).
- Net Profit Margin: 11.27%, up 461 bps YoY.
- Order Book: INR 101.64 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- Capacity & Utilization: 7.65 lakh SMD boards, 6 lakh THT boards, 4.20 lakh product assembly units per year at ~65% utilization.
- Employee Strength: Total 100 (55-56 on rolls, ~45 on contract).

### Highlights
- First earnings call since listing on BSE SME platform on June 8, 2026.
- Signed MOUs with Analinear Design Technologies and Stuam Technologies to offer integrated design and manufacturing solutions.
- Continuing record: Order book stands at INR 101.64 crores, maintaining steady growth momentum above earlier expectations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 momentum to continue, targeting close to INR 220 crores revenue for FY27.
- Guidance remains on track; NADCAP certification will not impact annual guidance.
- Build-to-spec (BTS) and product solutions expected to contribute 20%-25% by FY29, obsolescence management at 15%-20%, with the rest in build-to-print (BTP).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Seeing repeat and new requirements from defense, aerospace, telecom, and industrial electronics customers.
- Top 5 customers account for 90%-92% of revenue; top client accounts for ~60%. Deconcentration expected within a year.
- Exploring CERN bids worth close to INR 100 crores coming up in January.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins on CERN orders are at least 22%; outsourcing/obsolescence segment gross margins are 30%-40%, with EBITDA margins around 25%-30%.
- Working capital cash flows currently negative due to advance payments to suppliers and waiting for materials.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- IPO funds being deployed for committed capex; installation and commissioning of secondary end-to-end assembly line expected to complete by end of calendar year 2026.
- Second SMT line optimized for low-volume, high-mix requirements. Full system manufacturing foray may require ~INR 100 crores investment if current work yields results.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term loans and advances include supplier advances of INR 29 crores, capital goods advances of INR 14 crores, and government authorities/prepaid balances.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- NADCAP certification audit delayed from December to January end due to vacation schedules.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Varun Sheth (investor) asked about NADCAP certification delay and impact on growth.** Management stated they are 100% confident of clearing the audit in January, noting it enables forward integration into commercial aerospace, though existing guidance is independent of it.
- **Arnab Bhattacharjee (Arnab Bhattacharjee) asked about obsolescence revenue decline and CERN bids.** Management noted obsolescence did ~INR 10-11 crores with INR 5 crores in the pipeline, and explained CERN margins are at least 22% with upcoming INR 100 crore bids in January.
- **Vibhor asked about FY27 revenue potential based on H2 trends.** Management guided to close to INR 220 crores for FY27.
- **Akshi Gupta asked about client concentration.** Management stated top 5 clients represent 90%-92% and the top client represents ~60%, but expects deconcentration in the next year.
- **Darshit Shah asked about SMT line capacity correlations to revenue.** Management clarified that sales correlate with hardware bill of materials rather than unit capacity, and existing infrastructure is sufficient to reach INR 500 crores.

### One-line takeaway
Merritronix delivered stellar H1 FY27 results with over 90% revenue growth and tripled profits, while laying the groundwork for higher-value build-to-spec and strategic defense partnerships.
