# Schneider Electric Infrastructure Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong order intake and revenue growth were offset by margin compression due to persistent commodity inflation and the inability to pass on costs in fixed-price contracts.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Management declined to provide specific numerical revenue or margin guidance).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "record order intake" (Q1) to "sustained demand" (Q2).
- **New topics:** Launch of a new advanced, fully digitalized breaker in the Kolkata plant; further capacity expansion for KMVC (to 3.75 lakh units/year).
- **Last quarter's promises:** KMVC capacity expansion capitalization has commenced as planned.

### Key numbers
- **Order Intake:** Rs 1,265 crore (H1 FY27: Rs 2,180 crore).
- **Revenue:** Rs 780 crore (20% YoY growth).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,400 crore (12.5% YoY growth).
- **EBIT Margin:** 7.2% (Q2), 6.2% (H1).
- **PAT:** Rs 30 crore (43% YoY decline).

### Highlights
- **Product Launch:** Unveiled a new medium-voltage breaker in the Kolkata plant; features include full digitalization, 2x operational life, and 40% lower maintenance costs.
- **Awards:** Received Golden Peacock Award for corporate governance and Indian Chamber of Commerce award for sustainability manufacturing.
- **Emerging Segments:** Continued focus on data centers, semiconductors, and renewables; supplied skid-mounted stations for data centers and powertrain solutions for semiconductor setups.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be better for leveraging operations and revenue.
- Management stated the company is "hopeful to continue" on the current growth path but noted uncertainties regarding commodity volatility.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Data Centers:** 1.6 GW currently operational; industry expects capacity to reach 8 GW by 2030. Management noted 2.5 GW–3 GW of projects are currently in financial closure or under construction.
- **Macro:** Sustained demand driven by energy transition, AI/digitalization, urbanization, and "Make in India" initiatives.
- **Manufacturing:** Sector contribution to GDP expected to rise from 15% to 25% over the next four years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Material Costs:** Continued pressure from rising prices of copper, transformer oil, and steel.
- **Employee Costs:** Increased due to inflation and higher headcount for engineering/group support.
- **Finance Costs:** Increased due to RBI rate changes (from 6.1% to 6.7%).
- **Other Expenses:** Impacted by FX devaluation and timing-related accounting provisions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **KMVC Expansion:** Further capacity expansion for Vacuum Interrupters announced, taking capacity to 3.75 lakh units/year (up from 2.5 lakh units previously).
- **Kolkata Plant:** Capitalization of the KMVC plant has started; shifting of existing capacity to the new facility is planned for coming quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No incremental borrowing reported.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Commodity Volatility:** Management noted that copper, aluminum, and steel prices have increased double-digits.
- **Contractual Risks:** Inability to enforce price variation clauses in certain PSU and large-scale tenders, forcing the company to absorb cost escalations.
- **Legacy Backlog:** Approximately 20-25% of the order backlog has a duration of more than one year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Price Variation:** Management clarified that only 20-25% of projects are protected by price variation clauses; they are often unable to include these clauses in PSU tenders without becoming ineligible.
- **Margin Recovery:** Management stated that if commodity price impacts are carved out, material margins are "well on track," and they aim to leverage operating costs in H2.
- **Export Mix:** Exports currently account for 12-13% of revenue; management expects this to grow alongside domestic expansion.
- **Order Sustainability:** Management declined to call the current order intake a "one-off," noting the business is cyclical and dependent on the country's capex cycle.
- **Capacity Expansion Rationale:** Management explained the rapid expansion of KMVC capacity (from 1.8 lakh to 3.75 lakh units) is driven by anticipated global demand.

### One-line takeaway
While Schneider Electric Infrastructure is benefiting from strong demand in emerging segments like data centers and renewables, profitability remains constrained by commodity inflation and the inability to pass on costs in fixed-price government contracts.
