# Insolation Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, major backward integration projects nearing commissioning, and expectations of margin recovery in H2.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: INR1,582.09 crores (up 100.56% YoY from INR788 crores in H1 FY26).
- Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations: INR836.69 crores (up 96.45% YoY from INR425.90 crores in Q2 FY26).
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR140.31 crores (up 23.3% / 23% YoY from INR113.80 crores).
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: INR63.44 crores (up 13.32% YoY from INR55.99 crores).
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 8.87% (compared to 14.43% in H1 FY26).
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin: 7.58%.
- H1 FY27 Net Profit (PAT): INR63.08 crores (vs INR80.07 crores in H1 FY26).
- Q2 FY27 Net Profit (PAT): INR25.06 crores (vs INR36.95 crores in Q2 FY26).
- H1 FY27 PAT margin: 4% (Q2 FY27 PAT margin: 3%).
- Order book: ~2.2 gigawatt total; 1.3 gigawatt scheduled for delivery by March '27.
- Capacity utilization: Over 60%.
- Single-day dispatch record: ~16 megawatt (26,660 modules across 40 trailers) on 4th November '26.
- Channel network: >1,000 channel partners, >40,000 customers.
- H1 module dispatches: ~1 gigawatt (approx. 20% DCR, 80% non-DCR).
- RIPS 2022 incentive: Turnover-linked benefit of up to ~INR224 crores over 9 years for Sawarda facility (Phase 1 investment INR179.12 crores); 100% electricity duty exemption yielding >INR1 crore annual savings.
- Aluminum frame capex: ~INR100 crores (funded via internal accruals).
- Aluminum frame savings: INR50 to INR60 per frame set.
- Cell prices: INR13.25 to INR13.75 per watt.
- DCR module prices: INR21 to INR23; Non-DCR module prices: INR12.25 to INR12.75.
- IPP target: 400 megawatt plus by FY27 (~70 megawatt executed in H1 FY27).

### Highlights
- Aluminum frame manufacturing plant trial runs underway; commercial operations targeted from Q3 FY27.
- 4.5 gigawatt TOPCon solar cell facility at Narmadapuram, MP progressing toward commercial operations targeted for Q4 FY27 in one go.
- Entered a 5-year strategic partnership with Caelux Corporation, USA to develop 3 gigawatt hybrid tandem modules targeting up to 29% efficiency by end of 2029 at the Jaipur Sawarda facility.
- Received entitlement certificate under RIPS 2022 for the Sawarda facility.
- Achieved highest-ever single-day dispatch of ~16 megawatt on 4th November '26.
- *Vs Record:* The record noted general scaling via vertical integration; the call confirms exact commercial timelines (Q3 for frames, Q4 for cells) and specifies the Caelux partnership details which break prior patterns of relying solely on standard TOPCon technology.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to recover over the next 2 quarters, with full-year FY27 EBITDA and margins returning toward FY26 levels (though management noted it might not fully reach FY26's 14% due to international disturbances).
- Management confirmed H2 FY27 will be better than H1 FY27.
- Fully operational backward integration is expected to push EBITDA up to ~20%.
- FY28 revenue guidance of INR8,000 crores remains intact (stated by management/Sumit Jain).
- IPP portfolio targeting 400 megawatt plus by FY27.
- Ingot/wafer capex planned before ALMM Part 3 implementation on 1st June 2028; total plant project cost guided at INR450 crores to INR500 crores per gigawatt with utilities (or INR650-700 crores total for the project, correcting an earlier presentation typo).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand supported by government-led programs, utility-scale projects, EPC, C&I distributed solar, and B2C residential rooftop segment (including 40,000 homes solarized under PM Surya Ghar Yojana in Rajasthan).
- Global supply chain disruptions and input pricing are gradually easing.
- Indian solar industry is moving toward greater domestic value addition and integrated manufacturing ecosystems.
- Long-term energy demand in India is robust; management expects strong sector growth for the next 10 years despite temporary raw material price fluctuations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins moderated due to prevailing industry pricing dynamics, elevated input costs, dollar prices, and international freight.
- Higher depreciation and finance costs impacted PAT margins (accounting for a ~3% impact).
- Aluminum frame integration expected to save INR50 to INR60 per frame set.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 4.5 gigawatt TOPCon cell facility at Narmadapuram (MP): civil/PEB work done, machinery installation underway, targeted for Q4 FY27. Approx 2.5 gigawatt proposed across strategic MoUs/partnerships under internal evaluation.
- Aluminum frame plant: 18,000 metric tons per annum, civil/PEB completed, trial runs underway, Q3 FY27 commercial operation target. Designed for 1:1 integration (initially 65% captive, 35% open market).
- Ingot and wafer plant capex planned ahead of June 1, 2028 (ALMM Part 3); estimated at INR450-500 crores per gigawatt with utilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Temporary debt-equity ratio may stretch up to 2:1 or 2.5:1 during expansion, but longer-term target is well within 2:1 or lower, supported by internal accruals.
- Contingent liabilities (surety bonds, LCBG for tenders like rooftop RREC, NTPC, NLC) are normal operational tender requirements and close automatically upon project conclusion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- International market disturbances, dollar price fluctuations, ferrous/non-ferrous and international freight volatility impacted H1 margins.
- Temporary stretch in debt-equity ratios expected during the expansion phase.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Laxmikant Bharadia (Third Eye Investments)** asked about closing FY27 margins at 14% and contingent liabilities. Management stated 14% may not be fully achieved due to past disturbances, but H2 will be better than H1; contingent liabilities were clarified as routine tender surety bonds/LCBGs that close automatically.
2. **Rajesh Varma** asked about savings from aluminum frames and cell backward integration. Management stated frames save INR50-60 per set, and fully operational backward integration can push EBITDA up to ~20%.
3. **Sourav Khara** asked about cell realization, IPP revenue contribution, and FY28 guidance. Management stated cell prices are INR13.25-13.75/watt, DCR modules INR21-23/watt, non-DCR INR12.25-12.75/watt, and re-affirmed the INR8,000 crore FY28 revenue guidance.
4. **Sachin Rajguru** asked about Chinese policy risks on cell manufacturing equipment. Management answered that machinery is already more than 60-70% constructed, advances paid, FAT team is in China, and dispatches start this month without anticipated risk.
5. **Nikhil Sanghavi & Narendra Jain** questioned the presentation slide stating INR650-700 crores per gigawatt for ingot/wafer lines. Management clarified it was a typo for total project cost, and actual machinery cost with utilities is expected at INR450-500 crores per gigawatt.

### One-line takeaway
Insolation Energy reported a 100.56% YoY surge in H1 FY27 revenue to INR1,582.09 crores despite margin pressures, and remains firmly on track with its backward integration milestones into solar cells, aluminum frames, and tandem modules.
