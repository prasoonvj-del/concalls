# Susan Electricals India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 and HY1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Marked by improved profitability, a 190% YoY surge in revenue, strong execution, and a growing order book.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall direction; management noted a personal target of doubling scale, but provided no explicit numerical full-year revenue guidance.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more confidently towards specification-driven products (medium-voltage and high-tension cables) backed by newly arrived mandatory dry-curing (CCV) lines.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Discussion on land parcels/factory shifting to save costs and TReDS non-cash discounting facilities were new; raw material price volatility concerns remained from last quarter.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: CCV lines for high-tension cables have arrived at the factory and are in the installation stage.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Up 190% YoY (absolute numbers not stated, but HY1 revenue rose significantly)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 13.15 crore, up 191% YoY (compared to Rs 4.53 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA / Operating Profit (Q2 FY27):** Rs 23.78 crore, up 160% YoY
- **Blended EBITDA margin:** ~11.5% to 12% (normalized; reported ~11.77% to 13.13% in specific segments)
- **Unexecuted Order Book:** Rs 484.77 crore as of September 30, 2026 (almost doubled, up 109% YoY)
- **Active Order Pipeline:** ~Rs 800 crore to Rs 1,200 crore
- **Volumes Sold (Q2):** Conductors: 1,498 tons (937,238 kg); Winding wires: 3,641 tons
- **Capacity:** Current manufacturing capacity at 7,500 tons, expanding to 12,000 tons (adding 4,200 to 5,500 tons targeted across FY27-FY28)
- **Cash & Bank Balances / FDs:** Rs 20,000 lakh (including Rs 5.62 crore pledged in mutual funds)

### Highlights
- First half-year post-listing demonstrated strong operational execution and scalability.
- Arrived CCV (dry-curing) lines at the factory make it mandatory to participate in complex, high-tension projects.
- Acquired/identifying land parcels (UPSIDC industrial area) to shift and expand factories for cost savings.
- Continued participation in railways (RVNL, Coalfields) and exploring defense sectors.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to eventually achieve a product mix target of roughly one-third each across LT, HT, and winding wires, with HT/MVCC scaling up significantly.
- No absolute revenue guidance provided; management stated a personal target of doubling scale.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Power sector demand remains robust, driven by transmission and distribution (T&D), RDSS, and replacement cycles for failing transformers.
- Tenders won through L1 to L6 positioning; reverse auctions decide final rates with high strike rates in execution.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Normalized blended EBITDA margins sustainable around 11.5% to 12%.
- HT/MVCC margins expected to improve as volume scales and operating leverage kicks in.
- Raw material prices (aluminum, copper) managed via contract price variations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity target of 7,500 tons scaling up to 12,000 tons through phased expansion in FY28-FY29.
- Utilizing internal cash flows and working capital lines to fund expansion without heavy debt reliance.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flows improving significantly with aggressive collections.
- Working capital managed via Cash Credit (CC) limits secured by Fixed Deposits (FDs) and TReDS non-cash bill discounting facilities.
- Receivables showing healthy collections with a portion under 30 days old.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material price fluctuations (metals).
- Timelines in tender issuances and execution delays due to holidays.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankur Gulati (Genuity Capital) on Margins:** Management explained that normalized EBITDA margins stay between 11.5% and 12%, excluding one-time metal price gains.
2. **Sunrise Growth Fund on Capex & Funding:** Management stated expansion and land acquisition will be funded via internal cash generation and existing CC limits, minimizing incremental debt.
3. **Miri Family Office on Export Plans:** Management stated exports are not an immediate priority as domestic demand for high-margin cables in India is robust with fewer competing players.
4. **Dhaval on Working Capital & Receivables:** Management clarified that collections are robust, cash flows are positive, and TReDS is used purely as a non-cash bill discounting facility to manage MSME payables.
5. **Individual Investor on Realizations & Volumes:** Management provided volume data (1,498 tons of conductors, 3,641 tons of winding wires) and noted realization tracking will be examined for future disclosures.

### One-line takeaway
Susan Electricals India delivered a stellar Q2 FY27 with a 190% jump in revenue and 191% growth in PAT, underpinned by a surging order book and strategic capability upgrades in high-tension cables.
