# Cyient DLM Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Record order book (INR 2,810 crore), strong revenue growth (26.5% YoY), robust pipeline (>$1B), but near-term margins pressured by deliberate investments in sales and product development.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 3,931 million (Rs 393.1 crore), up 26.5% YoY; H1 FY27: INR 7,669 million (Rs 766.9 crore), up 30.2% YoY.
- Order book: INR 28.1 billion (Rs 2,810 crore), up ~INR 2.1 billion sequentially; book-to-bill 1.6x for 6 consecutive quarters.
- Pipeline: >$1 billion (unweighted).
- EBITDA: INR 342 million (Rs 34.2 crore), up 9.5% YoY; margin 8.7% (down 135 bps YoY). H1 EBITDA margin 9.6% (stable).
- Normalized PAT: INR 158 million (Rs 15.8 crore), up 25.8% YoY; margin 4%. H1 normalized PAT up 60.4% YoY, margin 4.2% (+79 bps).
- Reported PAT: INR 340 million (Rs 34 crore) includes one-time earn-out reversal of INR 182 million.
- Net working capital: 153 days (improved from 161 days in Q1); inventory 162 days (stable), DSO 73 days (from 77), DPO 68 days (from 63).
- Operating free cash flow: INR 172 million (Rs 17.2 crore).
- Finance costs: down 10.4% YoY to INR 61 million.

### Revenue mix
- **By industry:** Aerospace 47% (YoY +62%), Industrial 31% (+29%), Medtech 14% (+14%), Defense 7% (+13%).
- **By product:** PCBA 48% (+19%), Box build 27% (+85%).
- **By geography:** Rest of world 93%, India 7%.

### Highlights
- 6 consecutive quarters of order book growth; backlog at all-time high.
- Box build revenue surged 86% YoY, signaling shift to higher-value integration.
- Added 3 new logos in Q2.
- Completed Altek Electronics integration; renamed Cyient DLM Electronics, Inc., expanding US footprint.
- Strategic roadmap: Strengthen (FY27, margin 9-11%), Expand (FY27-29, margin 11-13% into robotics, AI data center, semi-cap equipment), Transform (FY30+, margin 13-18% via electronic products/platforms).
- Build-to-spec (electronic products) programs: 4 major programs worth ~$1B over 7-9 years; contracts signed, production expected FY29-30.
- Investments in sales/marketing (Chief Growth Officer, bid teams) and product development fully expensed (not capitalized).

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 EBITDA margin: high single-digit, ~9% (9-10% range).
- FY28 onwards: double-digit EBITDA margins.
- Revenue growth: H2 FY27 expected >30%; full year 30-35% YoY.
- Order book execution timeline: 18-24 months.
- Semiconductor capital equipment: already generating revenue, ramp-up continuing.
- AI data center & robotics: early exploration; update by end of FY27.
- Pipeline win ratio: ~40%; expected order intake $400-500M from existing pipeline.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global EMS market: ~$650B to ~$1.1T; high-reliability segment ~$230B.
- Three structural tailwinds: AI/data center infrastructure ($58B addressable for EMS), semiconductor capital equipment, robotics.
- Rising electronic content, supply chain diversification, outsourcing trends favor high-reliability EMS.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin compression (8.7% vs 10% YoY) driven by: (1) sales/marketing investments (~100-150 bps impact), (2) product development costs expensed, (3) revenue mix variability.
- Gross/contribution margins higher in FY27 YTD vs FY26.
- Employee costs up due to sales hires; no further sales/marketing additions until quarterly revenue doubles.
- Product development costs expensed as policy (conservative); no capitalization.
- Finance costs down 10.4% YoY (lower working capital borrowings).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Altek integration completed; US entity (Cyient DLM Electronics, Inc.) operational with local sales team.
- No specific capex guidance or capacity utilization disclosed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital improved to 153 days (from 161 in Q1).
- Inventory days stable at 162; DSO improved to 73 (from 77); DPO increased to 68 (from 63).
- Customer advances 13 days (from 15).
- Operating free cash flow INR 172M in Q2.
- Finance costs down 21% YoY in H1 due to lower working capital borrowings.
- Net debt figure not disclosed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain shortages, longer lead times, geopolitical uncertainties (memory market).
- Material availability delays revenue conversion (18-24 month order book execution).
- Near-term margin pressure from expensed investments.
- High-mix, low-volume business leads to revenue mix variability.
- Product development costs not capitalized – impacts near-term P&L.

### One-line takeaway
Cyient DLM delivers strong top-line momentum with record backlog and pipeline, but near-term margins are suppressed by deliberate, expensed investments in sales and electronic products that management expects to drive margin expansion from FY28 onward.
