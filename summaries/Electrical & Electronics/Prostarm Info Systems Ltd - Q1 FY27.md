# Prostarm Info Systems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong YoY growth, a healthy order book, and strategic pivots to higher-margin C&I segments despite seasonal revenue moderation.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 76 crore (38% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 7 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8.55% (up 126 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 5 crore (156% YoY growth).
*   **PAT Margin:** 6.05%.
*   **Total Order Book:** Rs 1,090 crore (including L1 status).
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** 168 days (improved from 185 days in Q4 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Performance:** Revenue moderated sequentially due to the seasonal nature of the business (Q1 typically sees lower activity).
*   **Strategic Wins:** Secured a battery energy storage project (Rs 11 crore) and a solar EPC order (Rs 165 crore) from Solarium Green Energy.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Implementation of SAP and Salesforce is nearing completion, expected to be operational by the end of H1 FY27.
*   **Order Book Composition:** Excluding developer projects (Rs 857 crore), the remaining order book stands at Rs 236 crore, plus approximately Rs 70 crore from dealer business.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management targets a minimum of 25% growth for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management maintains guidance of 12%–13% EBITDA margin for the full year, noting that H2 is typically stronger.
*   **Working Capital:** Targets 120–150 days by March 2027.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Jhajjar facility utilization expected at 20%–25% in FY27, moving to 40%–50% in FY28.
*   **Profitability:** Management expects PAT margins of 8.5%–9% for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **BESS Market:** Management noted "pricing stress" and margin erosion in the utility-scale BESS segment due to aggressive bidding and geopolitical issues affecting imports.
*   **Strategic Pivot:** The company is shifting focus from utility-scale to Commercial & Industrial (C&I) BESS applications, which offer better margins.
*   **Industry Outlook:** Management expects the BESS sector to stabilize and improve in the next financial year as the market matures and "serious players" remain.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Profitability improvement in Q1 was driven by higher revenues and operating leverage.
*   **Cost Pressures:** Management noted rising logistics costs and import challenges for battery components.
*   **Operating Costs:** Employee costs are rising due to the hiring of senior leadership and operational expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Jhajjar Facility (BESS):** 1.2 GWh facility is in the final stage of commissioning; expected to be operational by end of H1 FY27.
*   **Gujarat Facility (UPS):** Progressing as planned; expected to commence commercial operations in Q2 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow improved from negative Rs 49 crore in FY26 to negative Rs 15 crore in Q1 FY27; management expects to turn positive by year-end.
*   **Debt:** Bihar project debt is being tied up with State Bank of India at the SPV level; management stated there will be no personal or corporate guarantees from the flagship company.
*   **Equity:** Management stated no further equity dilution is planned for the next 12 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution Timing:** Some orders (e.g., South Eastern Railway) are experiencing delays due to site clearance issues, spilling over into Q2/Q3.
*   **Geopolitical/Supply Chain:** Restrictions on battery exports from China and a 2% tax increase on batteries effective September 1st are creating challenges.
*   **Concentration Risk:** A significant portion of trade receivables is tied to a few large government projects, though management emphasized these are government-funded and carry no realization risk.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the sequential revenue decline?** Management explained it is seasonal; if the Rs 20 crore in deferred billing from Q4 had been recognized in Q1, revenue would have been Rs 96 crore.
*   **Q: Status of developer projects (Bihar/KPTCL)?** Management clarified that no rental revenue will be recognized in FY27; only EPC billing will hit the books as the projects are completed.
*   **Q: Why the delay in Jhajjar commissioning?** Management stated they intentionally held production to manage costs amid rising logistics/component prices and to pivot strategy toward C&I.
*   **Q: Will you sell the Karnataka project?** Management confirmed selling the project is the first priority as it is "very remunerative."
*   **Q: Are you worried about trade receivables?** Management noted receivables dropped from Rs 231 crore in March to lower levels by August, with significant realization from the Pune CCTNS project.

### One-line takeaway
Prostarm is navigating short-term seasonal softness and BESS utility-sector headwinds by pivoting to higher-margin C&I projects and expanding manufacturing capacity, with management confident in achieving 25% growth and improved cash flows by year-end.
