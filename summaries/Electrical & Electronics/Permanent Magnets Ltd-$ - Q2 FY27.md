# Permanent Magnets Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong EV demand pickup, robust revenue growth, and significant margin expansion YoY.

### Key numbers
- Stand-alone revenue from operations: Rs 72.5 crores (up 49% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 17.8 crores (up 159% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 25% (vs 14% in Q2 FY26 and 19% in Q1 FY27)
- Export vs Domestic split (Q2): 49% export, 51% domestic
- Automotive sales: Rs 24 crores in Q2 (vs Rs 15 crores in Q1 FY27 and Rs 10.5 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Alloys revenue (H1): About Rs 12 crores; expected Rs 40-50 crores for the full year FY27
- Relays gross margins: Expected 15% to 20%
- Quantum Magnetics Phase 2 capacity potential: Rs 40 to 60 crores at optimal utilization
- New facility capex: Building cost estimated around Rs 30 crores; land cost was Rs 25 crores (shift expected in 2 years)

### Highlights
- Growth driven by good demand in the EV segment where supplies picked up, along with a pick-up in the domestic smart meter business.
- Margin improvement attributed to a better product mix from higher EV demand and positive operating leverage.
- Alloys division saw significant YoY revenue growth in H1, though full capacity of the new furnace could not be utilized due to teething issues (now resolved).
- Relays project to make pilot supplies in H2, with commercial ramp-up moved to FY28.
- Quantum Magnetics Phase 2 capex (block cutting, machining, surface treatment) was commissioned in October in line with the Q3 timeline; approved under the ECMS PLI scheme (25% capex incentive).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EV demand to remain at similar levels for the rest of the financial year (H2).
- Alloys division full-year revenue guided at Rs 40-50 crores for FY27, scaling to Rs 70-80 crores in FY28.
- Overall full-year revenue growth expected to be around 20% to 30%.
- Full-year EBITDA margin expected to be in the range of 20% to 25%.
- Quantum Magnetics revenue for FY27 may get pushed to FY28 due to ongoing international customer approval processes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- EV demand uptick came from existing customers (export market) expanding requirements and platform adoption.
- Domestic smart meter demand depends on utility installations; currently expected to remain at similar levels.
- Alloys division received orders from oil and gas, industrial applications, and small orders from aerospace.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Export margins are better; domestic margins are much lower.
- Alloys division incurred higher initial costs due to teething issues and delay penalties paid to customers; virgin alloy gross margins expected at 10-15%, and returns business at 15-25%.
- Quantum Magnetics currently plans to import blocks, with eventual plans for a full domestic supply chain.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Quantum Magnetics Phase 2 commissioned in October; product approval process starting. Applied for PLI under ECMS scheme (25% capex incentive).
- Plant consolidation into a single facility expected in about 2 years, saving close to Rs 5 crores per year in rent; building capex estimated at ~Rs 30 crores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Land cost for new facility was ~Rs 25 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Teething issues and technical breakdowns in the new alloys furnace caused sales delays and penalty costs.
- Relays commercial sales delayed due to iterative testing and customization required by customers.
- Quantum Magnetics revenue dependent on international customer plant visits and approvals, creating timing uncertainty.
- Geopolitical uncertainties and Chinese EV domination in Western markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankit Gupta (Bamboo Capital) on EV demand sustainability:** Management stated demand is driven by existing export customers and expects it to hold steady in H2, though specific FY28 forecasts are not yet provided.
2. **Dhwanil Desai (Turtler Capital) on margin sustainability:** Management noted margins depend on product mix (exports vs domestic), and if EV demand holds, margins can stay close to the Q2 level.
3. **Rahul Rao (Individual Investor) on alloys teething issues:** Management explained technical breakdowns with the new furnace caused delays and customer penalties, but orders are pushed back, not cancelled.
4. **Sanjay Kumar (Investor) on Quantum Magnetics PLI/ECMS eligibility:** Management clarified they qualified for the ECMS PLI scheme (25% capex incentive) but did not qualify for the other MeitY scheme due to net worth requirements.
5. **Ankit Gupta on Relay timelines and margins:** Management stated commercial ramp-up is pushed to FY28 due to ongoing design iterations with customers, with targeted gross margins of 15-20%.

### One-line takeaway
Permanent Magnets delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 49% revenue growth and 25% EBITDA margins, propelled by an EV export demand revival, while new divisions (alloys, relays, Quantum Magnetics) ramp up toward FY28.
