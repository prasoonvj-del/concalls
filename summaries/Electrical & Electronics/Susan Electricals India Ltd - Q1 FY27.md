# Susan Electricals India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-22 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with significant year-on-year revenue growth, turnaround to net profit, and improving product mix.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 95.36 crore, up 279% YoY
- **Operating profit (EBITDA):** Rs 11.04 crore, up 980% YoY
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 6.39 crore (compared to a loss of Rs 0.42 crore YoY)
- **Blended operating margin:** ~11.9% in Q1 FY27
- **Segment EBITDA margins (Q1 FY27):** LT cables and conductors ~6.5%; HT cables ~7%; Winding wires ~24.39%
- **Unexecuted order book:** Rs 142.39 crore as of June 30, 2026
- **Active order pipeline:** Rs 150 crore (Total order visibility: Rs 292 crore)
- **FY27 Capex planned:** Rs 15 crore to Rs 20 crore
- **Capacity expansion:** Existing 7,500 Km p.a. to 12,000 Km p.a. (+60%, adding 4,500 Km p.a.)

### Highlights
- Core focus remains on strengthening manufacturing operations and increasing contribution from higher-value products (LT, HT, and MVCC cables).
- Unexecuted order book of Rs 142.39 crore expected to be executed over the next ~3 months.
- Regularly bidding for Rs 800-1,200 crore of tenders with an expected conversion rate of 15% to 20%; many pipeline items already L1/L2 awaiting LOI.
- Exploring opportunities in railways, defence, and empanelment with leading power sector companies.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects growth momentum to continue, supported by strong power sector demand.
- Target to increase HT & MVCC contribution from ~5-10% currently toward ~50% by year-end (also mentioned as ~15% by year-end in earlier remarks) and higher thereafter.
- Expected FY27 revenue mix roughly balanced at ~30-35% each across HT/MVCC, LT/conductors, and winding wires.
- No absolute revenue guidance provided for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Backed by strong industry tailwinds including T&D expansion, RDSS, reconductoring, 24-hr supply push, and replacement demand.
- Power sector environment expected to remain favourable for the next several years.
- Orders are primarily tender-based from central/state utilities and EPC contractors (e.g., Monte Carlo, RVNL).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- HT and MVCC cables have a product-level EBITDA/net margin potential of approximately 20% (or 20-25%) at scale; winding wires and LT/conductors typically yield 10-15%.
- Raw material price fluctuations (specifically aluminium rods) are managed through appropriate pricing mechanisms and operational measures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commercial operations for the expanded capacity on track for February 2027, adding 4,500 Km p.a. to reach 12,000 Km p.a.
- Capex of Rs 15 crore to Rs 20 crore in FY27 is primarily towards machinery and expansion-related requirements.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is currently focusing on strengthening operations and expanding manufacturing capabilities; funding requirements will be evaluated based on growth plans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in raw material prices (particularly aluminium rods) due to geopolitical developments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) on Segment-wise Margins:** Management clarified Q1 FY27 EBITDA margins were ~6.5% for LT cables and conductors, ~7% for HT cables, and ~24.39% for winding wires.
2. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) on Margin Potential:** Management explained that HT/MVCC margins are currently lower due to early-stage scale, but carry a product-level potential of ~20% as volumes scale and operating leverage kicks in.
3. **Divyansh Thakur (Finterest Capital) on Product Mix:** Management stated that increasing HT and MVCC contribution from 10-15% towards ~50% by year-end will drive operating profitability.
4. **Chandan Jain (Capital Market Analyst) on Raw Material Volatility:** Management noted that aluminium rod price fluctuations are closely monitored and managed via appropriate pricing mechanisms.
5. **Ankur Gulati (Genuity Capital) on Debt:** Management stated current focus is on operations and expansion, and additional debt/funding will be evaluated as needed based on growth.

### One-line takeaway
Susan Electricals India delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with a 279% jump in revenue and a return to profitability, driven by better product mix and robust demand in the power sector.
