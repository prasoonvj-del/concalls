# Electrical & Electronics: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Electrical & Electronics sector in Q2 FY27 presents a stark split between heavy electrical infrastructure/power T&D capital goods and broad consumer durables/electronics manufacturing services (EMS). 

Heavy electricals, T&D, solar equipment, and high-voltage transmission suppliers (Apar Industries, GE Vernova T&D, Diamond Power, Kanohar Electricals, TARIL, Premier Energies, Waaree Energies) are enjoying a multi-year supercycle. Demand is propelled by massive domestic grid expansions, data center infrastructure, renewable energy evacuation, and global reconductoring needs. Order books for transmission equipment, high-voltage transformers, and solar modules remain at record highs.

Conversely, consumer durables, lighting, and retail appliance players (Bajaj Electricals, Blue Star, Orient Electric, V-Guard, Crompton Greaves) face mixed demand conditions. While seasonal heatwaves extended air conditioner and cooling demand into H1, persistent inflation in raw materials (copper, aluminum, steel, plastics, and semiconductor memory) is squeezing gross margins. Passing on these cost increases to price-sensitive retail channels remains a key hurdle across appliances and lighting.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
* **Power T&D and Smart Grid:** Broad consensus exists across power equipment makers (**GE Vernova T&D, Apar Industries, Kanohar Electricals, TARIL, Diamond Power, Dynamic Cables**) that domestic and export demand for transmission infrastructure, high-voltage cables, and transformers is at an all-time high, supported by state utility spends, TBCB projects, and renewable integration.
* **Data Centers and AI Infrastructure:** Strong consensus across EMS, cabling, and power equipment companies (**Netweb Technologies, Apar Industries, Diamond Power, Schneider Electric Infrastructure, Cyient DLM**) that data center expansion is emerging as a top-tier demand driver for specialized cables, AI compute, and power distribution units.
* **Solar Manufacturing & BESS:** Solar cell, module, and glass suppliers (**Waaree Energies, Premier Energies, Saatvik Green Energy, Borosil Renewables, Websol Energy**) see multi-year volume tailwinds supported by domestic content requirement (DCR) rules, ALMM mandates, and PM Surya Ghar.

### Divergence
* **Consumer Durables & Fans:** **Crompton Greaves** and **Orient Electric** reported strong double-digit growth and market share gains in fans (especially BLDC), whereas **Bajaj Electricals** lagged due to an over-reliance on entry-level fan portfolios and a delayed BLDC rollout.
* **RAC & Appliances Pricing Power:** **Blue Star** noted high pricing friction and expressed caution over passing through 5% unabsorbed material cost increases due to irrational peer competition. In contrast, **PG Electroplast** and **V-Guard** managed strong volume traction through fixed-margin contract manufacturing models or aggressive price hikes in select categories.
* **Smart Metering Tenders:** **HPL Electric & Power** highlights strong smart meter rollout momentum through AMISPs like Adani/IntelliSmart, but flags aggressive low-cost bidding by fringe players as unsustainable.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Metal & Base Commodity Inflation:** Copper (+50% YoY for some users), aluminum (+35%), basic metals, and transformer oil escalated sharply. Companies with Price Variation Clauses (PVC) or pass-through terms (**Apar Industries, Dynamic Cables, TARIL, KSH International**) successfully shielded absolute profitability or margins per ton, while fixed-price players (**Schneider Electric Infrastructure**) suffered EBIT compression.
* **Semiconductor Memory Volatility:** A 35%–40% spike in memory prices (DDR, flash) and SOCs squeezed gross margins and inflated inventory values for consumer electronics, video surveillance, and EMS players (**Aditya Infotech, Dixon Technologies, Netweb Technologies**). **Aditya Infotech** pre-bought SOC/memory stock in H1 to temporarily boost gross margins, but warned of margin drops in H2 as cheaper stock exhausts.
* **Freight & Geopolitical Disruptions:** Geopolitical conflicts in West Asia and transit delays raised freight rates and created supply chain bottlenecks (**Cyient DLM, Skipper, RIR Power Electronics, Premier Energies**).

## Capex & capacity
* **Power T&D & Cables Scale-up:** **KEI Industries** is deploying Rs 600 crore annually (including the Sanand Phase 2 EHV plant), **Apar Industries** is executing a Rs 1,500 crore capex program, **Diamond Power** is spending Rs 366 crore on polymer backward integration and CCV lines, and **KSH International** approved Phase 3 expansion to reach 72,200 MT by FY29.
* **Solar & Cell Integration:** **Waaree Energies** is advancing a ~Rs 30,000 crore multi-year capex plan (including 10 GW ingots/wafers at Nagpur and US manufacturing), **Premier Energies** is deploying Rs 2,400 crore in FY27, and **Saatvik Green Energy** launched Phase 1 of its Gopalpur, Odisha facility (4 GW modules, 2.4 GW cells).
* **Electronics & EMS:** **Dixon Technologies** guided Rs 1,200 crore capex for FY27 across displays, camera modules, and IT hardware; **Syrma SGS** is allocating Rs 800–900 crore towards its high-layer PCB project through March 2028.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Upgrades:**
  * **Aditya Infotech:** Raised FY27 revenue target to Rs 6,500–6,800 crore (from Rs 6,000–6,500 crore) and PAT margin target to 9%–10% post-debt clearance via QIP.
  * **GNG Electronics:** Upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to 40% (from 30%) and raised PAT growth expectations by 150 bps.
  * **KEI Industries:** Upgraded full-year sales growth guidance to >25% (from 20%).
  * **Syrma SGS:** Upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to 40%–45% (~Rs 7,000 crore).
  * **IKIO Technologies:** Upgraded full-year outlook, targeting 35%–50% growth in its non-lighting business.
* **Guidance Fine-tuning / Cuts:**
  * **R R Kabel:** Fine-tuned full-year Wires & Cables volume growth guidance to 14%–15% (from 16%–18%) due to short-term market volatility.
* **Tone Shifts & Delivery on Promises:**
  * **Aditya Infotech** completed its Rs 1,500 crore QIP, repaid all debt in October 2026, and acquired a 30% stake in BLUE KGraph AI Solutions as promised.
  * **Premier Energies** commissioned its 7 GW TOPCon cell facility at Naidupeta on schedule.
  * **Saatvik Green Energy** commercialized Phase 1 module production (1.3 GW) at its Gopalpur, Odisha plant on November 2, 2026.
  * **Websol Energy** became debt-free in August 2026 after clearing IREDA loans and secured a 54.2-acre land allotment at Falta Industrial Park.

## What we were watching
- — not discussed · MoP allowing ISTS-renewable projects to shift to intra-state transmission connectivity was not explicitly highlighted in management summaries.
- — not discussed · Central government ban on the export of unsorted waste/scrap of lithium-ion batteries was not brought up by battery or solar energy managements.
- ✅ showed up · Cabinet approval for the 1,150-kV power transmission corridor connecting western renewables to eastern hubs; **GE Vernova T&D, Apar Industries, Diamond Power, TARIL, Atlanta Electricals, and Lumino Industries** highlighted strong transmission order inflows linked to Green Energy Corridors and high-voltage grid interconnections.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Commodity Price Pass-Through:** Monitor whether consumer appliance players (**Blue Star, V-Guard, Orient Electric**) successfully execute scheduled Q3 price hikes to recover unabsorbed 3%–5% material cost inflation.
* **Commissioning Timelines:** Watch for commercialization of **KEI Industries'** Sanand Phase 2 EHV cables (by March 2027), **Saatvik Green Energy's** 2.4 GW TOPCon cell ramp-up in Odisha (December 2026), and **RIR Power Electronics'** final statutory fire clearances for its Odisha SiC epitaxy plant.
* **Memory Cost Trajectory:** Check whether elevated DDR/flash memory pricing continues to compress H2 EBITDA margins for EMS and surveillance system manufacturers (**Aditya Infotech, Dixon Technologies**).
* **Export Freight Normalization:** Track whether geopolitical container shortages and high sea-freight rates ease for export-oriented manufacturers (**Skipper, Dynamic Cables, Shilchar Technologies**).
