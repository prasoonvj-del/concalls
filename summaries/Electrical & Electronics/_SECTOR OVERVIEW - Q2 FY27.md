# Electrical & Electronics: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Electrical & Electronics sector reflects robust top-line momentum across most companies, powered by structural tailwinds in power transmission and distribution (T&D), renewables evacuation, data centers, railways, and exports (particularly to the US). However, sector participants faced varying degrees of headwinds, including unseasonal heavy rainfall/waterlogging (Atlanta Electricals), geopolitical logistics disruptions (Apar Industries, RIR Power), and supply chain/raw material cost pressures (Syrma SGS, Vidya Wires). Despite localized execution pauses and commodity volatility, managements remain structurally bullish, raising or maintaining aggressive growth guidance backed by bulging order books and active capacity expansions.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** All ten managements report exceptionally strong underlying demand. Key drivers unanimously cited include grid modernization, renewable energy integration (solar/wind and BESS), data center expansion, and massive capital outlays by domestic utilities and global markets (especially the US).
*   **Divergence (Exports vs. Domestic):** 
    *   **Apar Industries, KEI Industries, R R Kabel, and Shilchar Technologies** highlighted outpaced growth or surging demand from international markets, with the US serving as a major growth engine (e.g., Apar’s US SPARK program traction; KEI noting the US accounts for ~50% of its export order book; Shilchar seeing strong North American rebound).
    *   Conversely, players like **Yash Highvoltage** (95% domestic) and **Atlanta Electricals** remain predominantly focused on the domestic market while selectively building out global distribution footprints.
    *   **Syrma SGS** noted broad-based secular demand across all EMS verticals, led by automotive (up 80%+) and healthcare.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Managements across the board acknowledged acute commodity price volatility—particularly in copper, aluminum, steel, and transformer oil—alongside freight and logistics pressures driven by geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East. 
*   **Disagreements/Handling:** 
    *   *Pass-through & Hedging:* **KEI Industries** and **Shilchar Technologies** noted natural hedges or disciplined raw material pass-through mechanisms (e.g., retail price revisions twice a month, customer-backed 180-day LCs). **Atlanta Electricals** faced short-term gross margin moderation (~26.6% vs 30.9% YoY) due to legacy firm-price orders, which they have successfully renegotiated for H2.
    *   *Fixed-Cost Dilution:* **Vidya Wires** and **Permanent Magnets** flagged temporary margin pressure or teething issues as new manufacturing facilities/furnaces incurred fixed operating costs ahead of full volume ramp-ups. 
    *   *Margin Expansion:* Premiumization and high-margin product mixes allowed **Apar Industries**, **Permanent Magnets**, and **Yash Highvoltage** to expand EBITDA margins significantly YoY (Apar at 11.4%, Permanent Magnets at 25%, Yash at 28.1%).

## Capex & capacity
Managements across the sector are aggressively deploying capital to capture multi-year demand:
*   **Apar Industries:** Spending Rs 1,500 crore over 12-15 months (Rs 500 crore spent in H1).
*   **KEI Industries:** Progressing on Sanand plant capex (H1 payment of Rs 402 crore), guiding for ~Rs 600 crore annual capex over the next 3-4 years.
*   **Atlanta Electricals:** Constructing an IDT plant (Rs 65 crore) and initiating backward integration for tanks/radiators (~Rs 193 crore).
*   **R R Kabel:** Maintaining FY27 capex at Rs 550-600 crore, plus Rs 200 crore for Rajasthan land and ~Rs 77 crore for the UM Cables acquisition.
*   **RIR Power Electronics:** Installed SiC epitaxial wafer reactors in Odisha, awaiting statutory approvals for commercial production.
*   **Shilchar Technologies:** Advancing Phase 3 expansion (6,500 MVA addition) targeting commissioning by April 2027.
*   **Syrma SGS:** Incurred Rs 200 crore in Q2 (primarily toward a mega PCB project); total PCB spend expected to reach Rs 800-900 crore.
*   **Vidya Wires:** Near-completion of its ALCU facility and CTC plant, with capex wrapping up by end of Q3.
*   **Yash Highvoltage:** Progressing on its Jarod greenfield expansion (total planned capex of Rs 220 crore).

## What changed since last quarter
*   **R R Kabel:** Fine-tuned full-year volume growth guidance down slightly to 14-15% (from 16-18%) due to market volatility and competitive intensity.
*   **Syrma SGS:** Upgraded annual revenue growth guidance to 40-45% (~INR 7,000 crores for FY27) following a stellar H1 performance.
*   **KEI Industries:** Upgraded full-year growth guidance to over 25%, backed by strong order visibility.
*   **Atlanta Electricals:** Experienced temporary Q2 delivery deferrals (~Rs 180 crore) due to heavy rainfall and waterlogged sites in Gujarat, though ~70% was dispatched in October.

## Company one-liners
*   **Apar Industries:** Strong order books and margin expansion offset temporary volume headwinds, with execution poised to catch up in H2.
*   **Atlanta Electricals:** Weather disruptions dampened Q2, but a robust order book and capacity expansions keep Atlanta anchored to its multi-year CAGR goals.
*   **KEI Industries:** Robust revenue growth and stable margins underscore KEI's confident path toward scaling its Sanand manufacturing footprint.
*   **Permanent Magnets:** An EV export revival drove exceptional Q2 profitability while new alloy, relay, and magnet divisions scale toward FY28.
*   **R R Kabel:** Record H1 results and strategic acquisitions offset a slight fine-tuning of volume growth guidance amid market volatility.
*   **RIR Power Electronics:** Awaiting final Odisha statutory clearances to launch India's first vertically integrated silicon carbide epitaxy facility.
*   **Shilchar Technologies:** A strong Q2 bounce-back backed by domestic power demand and recovering US exports keeps annual targets secure.
*   **Syrma SGS Technology:** Upgraded annual guidance and robust H1 cash flows highlight Syrma's aggressive execution across EMS and ODM verticals.
*   **Vidya Wires:** Transitioning from plant installation to commercial sales execution at its ALCU facility to drive H2 performance.
*   **Yash Highvoltage:** Stellar H1 growth and expanding margins reflect surging high-voltage bushing demand and smooth greenfield progress.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Execution Catch-up:** Whether Apar Industries and Atlanta Electricals successfully recover deferred volumes and weather-impacted dispatches in H2.
*   **Regulatory & Statutory Clearances:** Commercial production start dates for RIR Power's epitaxy plant and Vidya Wires' CTC plant pending state and technical approvals.
*   **Margin Trajectory & Commodity Pass-through:** How well companies like R R Kabel, Vidya Wires, and Atlanta Electricals manage volatile copper/aluminum prices and absorb fixed overheads from newly commercialized plants.
*   **FMEG Turnaround:** Progress on R R Kabel's FMEG segment achieving full-year profitability/breakeven.
*   **Capex Milestone Progress:** Execution updates on Syrma SGS's mega PCB project and Yash Highvoltage's Jarod greenfield facility.
