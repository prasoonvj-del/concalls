# Electrical & Electronics: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian Electrical & Electronics sector demonstrated high top-line momentum in Q2 FY27, backed by massive power Transmission & Distribution (T&D) grid expansion, rapid renewable energy integration (solar and BESS), data center power requirements, and expanding Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS). 

However, performance across the sector reflected a sharp divergence in bottom-line execution. Heavy industrial equipment and power component makers—such as Apar Industries, Diamond Power, KEI Industries, KSH International, Kanohar Electricals, and Transformers and Rectifiers (India) Ltd (TARIL)—reported robust order books and solid execution, supported by strong demand from state utilities, renewable evacuation (e.g., Khavda), and industrial infrastructure. 

Conversely, consumer-facing appliances, wires, and solar module manufacturers faced margin pressure. Raw material cost spikes in copper, aluminum, polymer, steel, and transformer oil, combined with elevated memory and SOC semiconductor component prices, eroded gross margins. Companies operating under fixed-price contracts or facing pass-through lags (such as Amber Enterprises, Blue Star, Prostarm Info Systems, and Solarworld Energy) experienced margin compression, whereas players with price-variation clauses (PVC) or quick pricing power fared far better.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Traditionally, Indian electrical and electronics players focused on component assembly, commoditized cable/wire distribution, and low-margin build-to-print contract manufacturing, relying on imported critical raw materials and semiconductors. Over the last 5 years, the sector has shifted toward backward integration (in-house cells, frames, polymer compounding), B2B/B2C direct channel models, and specialized high-voltage infrastructure. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **Backward integration into critical components & cell manufacturing**: Companies are investing heavily to localize component manufacturing, moving away from low-margin assembly to capturing integrated margins.
  - *Stage*: Scaling
  - *Evidence*: Aditya Infotech is localizing 4 component categories (lenses, housing enclosures, cables, IoT modules) [Aditya Infotech concall Q2 FY27]. Diamond Power is setting up a Rs 170 crore polymer compounding plant to boost margins by ~3.5% [Diamond Power concall Q2 FY27]. Solar players like Premier Energies commissioned a 7 GW cell line [Premier Energies concall Q2 FY27], Insolation Energy is setting up a 4.5 GW TOPCon cell plant and aluminum frame unit [Insolation Energy concall Q2 FY27], and Waaree Energies is adding 10 GW cell capacity and a 10 GW ingot/wafer plant [Waaree Energies concall Q2 FY27]. Kaynes Technology operationalized its OSAT and PCB units [Kaynes Technology concall Q2 FY27].
  - *Why it matters*: Enhances operational self-reliance, protects against global supply-chain disruptions, and structurally raises EBITDA margins by 200–500 bps.
- **Pivot to high-margin Data Center and AI infrastructure**: Electrical equipment and cable manufacturers are shifting capacity to meet the specialized power density and optical cabling requirements of data centers.
  - *Stage*: Scaling
  - *Evidence*: Diamond Power generated Rs 240 crore (23% of Q2 revenue) from data center cables in September 2026 [Diamond Power concall Q2 FY27; Livemint Nov 2024]. Apar Industries secured major cable orders from Google (US) and Amazon (India) [Apar Industries concall Q2 FY27]. Netweb Technologies reported AI systems revenue of Rs 773 crore (+1,068% YoY) [Netweb concall Q2 FY27]. Blue Star expects its data center order book to double from Rs 2,000 crore to Rs 4,000 crore in 3 years [Blue Star concall Q2 FY27].
  - *Why it matters*: Data center applications offer ~2% higher gross margins and command multi-year order visibility.
- **Divestment of capital-intensive/loss-making assets to focus on core markets**: Companies are shedding legacy EPC divisions or loss-making overseas subsidiaries to de-risk balance sheets.
  - *Stage*: Scaling
  - *Evidence*: Exicom Tele-Systems approved the 100% divestment of its loss-making US/global subsidiary Tritium for $85 million [Exicom concall Q2 FY27]. JD Cables announced a complete exit from its EPC business to focus purely on core manufacturing [JD Cables concall Q2 FY27]. Prostarm Info Systems is negotiating to down-sell Rs 854 crore of utility-scale BESS developer projects [Prostarm concall Q2 FY27].
  - *Why it matters*: Unlocks blocked working capital, eliminates operating losses, and boosts return on capital employed (ROCE).

**Government push**
- **PM Surya Ghar & PM-KUSUM Schemes**: Accelerates domestic content requirement (DCR) solar module, inverter, and pump demand [Fujiyama concall Q2 FY27; Premier Energies concall Q2 FY27; Waaree Energies concall Q2 FY27].
- **ALMM Mandates (List-I, List-II, List-III)**: Enforces local sourcing of modules and cells, restricting non-compliant land-border imports [Saatvik concall Q2 FY27; Websol concall Q2 FY27].
- **STQC Cybersecurity Standards**: Strictly enforced trusted-supply-chain guidelines in video surveillance, favoring certified domestic manufacturers over Chinese imports [Aditya Infotech concall Q2 FY27].
- **State EV Policies & Battery Storage Tariffs**: Maharashtra's approval of 4000 MWh battery storage tariffs and state EV subsidies drive BESS assembly and power equipment orders [Govt: Maharashtra govt approved 4000 MWh battery storage tariffs, Nov 2026; GK Energy concall Q2 FY27; Waaree Energies concall Q2 FY27].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
- **Power T&D and Grid Modernization**: Unanimous optimism across power equipment, cable, transformer, and switchgear makers (Apar, Atlanta Electricals, Dynamic Cables, GE Vernova T&D, G V Electricals, Kanohar, KEI Industries, KSH International, Laser Power, Lumino Industries, TARIL, Yash Highvoltage). Demand is driven by Green Energy Corridors, state DISCOM grid revamps, and inter-state transmission lines.
- **Solar & Storage (BESS)**: Strong underlying structural demand driven by rooftop solar (PM Surya Ghar) and utility storage mandates. Solar cell and module capacity additions are accelerating (Emmvee, Insolation Energy, Premier Energies, Saatvik, Waaree, Websol).

### Divergence
- **Consumer Electronics & Appliances**: 
  - *Bullish*: Crompton Greaves (+18.2% YoY revenue growth), V-Guard (+38.2% YoY revenue growth), and Orient Electric (+25.2% YoY growth) reported strong demand across fans, appliances, and lighting, supported by market share gains and premiumization.
  - *Cautious/Subdued*: Bajaj Electricals posted modest 1.5% YoY revenue growth, dragged down by entry-level fans; Dixon Technologies noted soft mass-market LED TV and direct-cool refrigerator demand due to input inflation.
- **Solar Modules & EPC Execution**:
  - *Strong execution*: Insolation Energy (+96.5% Q2 revenue), Premier Energies (+38.2% YoY revenue), Solarium Green Energy (+165% H1 revenue), and Waaree Energies (+23.9% Q2 revenue) reported surging dispatches.
  - *Headwinds*: Vikram Solar experienced a sequential volume and revenue dip due to IPP land/evacuation constraints and US Section 232 tariff changes (removing 1 GW export orders). Solarworld Energy cited PSU contract LOA delays, while Solex Energy faced temporary ALMM regulatory friction.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

- **Commodity Price Volatility**: Direct raw material prices surged YoY—Copper (+50%), Aluminum (+35%), Base oils (+100%), Zinc (+36%), transformer oil (more than doubled from Rs 68/L to Rs 148/L), polymer resins, and steel.
- **Semiconductors & Electronics Costs**: Rapid AI infrastructure buildout globally drove memory (DDR, flash) and SOC component costs up by 35%–40% YoY, diluting percentage margins across EMS players (Dixon, Cyient DLM, Kaynes).
- **Pass-through Mechanisms**:
  - *Protected*: B2B equipment makers (Dynamic Cables, KEI Industries, KSH International, TARIL, Laser Power) have 80%–90% of order books covered under Price Variation Clauses (PVC) or quarterly lag formulas. High commodity prices inflate revenue denominators, causing minor temporary percentage margin suppression while absolute rupees/ton remain protected.
  - *Compressed*: Consumer durables (Blue Star, Cello World, Bajaj Electricals) and fixed-price contract holders (Amber's legacy railway contracts, Prostarm's BESS developer contracts, HPL's fixed smart meter orders, Schneider's PSU tenders, Solarworld's NTPC contracts) suffered severe margin contraction due to pricing lag or non-pass-through clauses.
- **Freight & Logistics**: Geopolitical disturbances in West Asia raised ocean freight rates and transit times, doubling container shipping costs for export-oriented players (Solex, Prostarm, Dynamic Cables).

## Capex & capacity

The sector is undergoing one of its largest capex cycles, funded by operational cash flows, IPO/QIP proceeds, and bank debt:

- **Solar Cells, Modules & Glass**:
  - *Premier Energies*: Commissioned 7 GW TOPCon cell facility at Naidupeta; FY27 capex guided at Rs 2,400 crore.
  - *Waaree Energies*: Total capex plan of ~Rs 30,000 crore; expanding cell capacity to 15.4 GW, adding 10 GW ingot/wafer plant, 4.8 GW US module plant, and solar glass facility in Navsari.
  - *Saatvik Green Energy*: Commissioned 1.3 GW module plant in Odisha (scaling to 4 GW); 2.4 GW cell line under trial runs.
  - *Insolation Energy*: 4.5 GW TOPCon cell plant in MP commissioning in Q4 FY27; Rs 100 crore aluminum frame plant.
  - *Borosil Renewables*: Rs 1,100 crore capex for 600 TPD solar glass expansion commissioning in Q4 FY27.
  - *Websol Energy*: Rs 270 crore TOPCon cell upgrade (750 MW) + Rs 1,350 crore 2 GW greenfield cell plant.
- **Power T&D, Cables & Wires**:
  - *KEI Industries*: Annual capex of ~Rs 600 crore; Sanand facility (EHV/HT cables) capitalizing by March 2027.
  - *Diamond Power*: Rs 366 crore capex across projects, including an 84,500-ton integrated polymer compounding plant.
  - *Apar Industries*: Rs 1,500 crore capex program over 12–15 months (Rs 500 crore spent in H1).
  - *Laser Power & Infra*: Acquired ~40 acres land lease at Kharagpur for expansion post-IPO debt payoff.
  - *R R Kabel*: Rs 550–600 crore base capex + Rs 200 crore land acquisition in Rajasthan + Rs 77 crore acquisition of UM Cables.
- **EMS, Electronics & Appliances**:
  - *Dixon Technologies*: Guided FY27 capex at ~Rs 1,200 crore (IT hardware, display modules JV with HKC, components).
  - *Kaynes Technology*: Expanding OSAT (Semicon Unit 2) and PCB manufacturing lines in Chennai; Vishay acquiring 49% stake in Semicon subsidiary.
  - *PG Electroplast*: Guided FY27 capex at ~Rs 400 crore (refrigerator plant in Sri City, compressor plant in Supa).

- **Guidance Revisions**:
  - *Raised*: **Aditya Infotech** (Revenue raised to Rs 6,500–6,800 cr; PAT margin to 9–10%), **GNG Electronics** (Revenue growth raised to 40%), **KEI Industries** (FY27 revenue growth raised to 25%+), **Syrma SGS** (Annual revenue guidance upgraded to 40–45% / ~Rs 7,000 cr), **IKIO Technologies** (Targeting 35–50% growth in non-lighting).
  - *Cut / Moderated*: **Amber Enterprises** (Cut Railway revenue growth to 15–20% and Railway EBITDA margin to 8–10%), **Prostarm Info Systems** (Cut EBITDA margin guidance to >11% from 12–13%), **R R Kabel** (Fine-tuned volume growth to 14–15% from 16–18%), **Solarworld Energy** (Lowered EBITDA margin guidance to 6–7% from 10.5%).
- **Tone Shifts**:
  - **Exicom Tele-Systems**: Shifted from waiting for Tritium turnaround to announcing complete 100% divestment ($85m valuation).
  - **Kaynes Technology**: Intentionally reduced smart meter sales by 80% YoY to de-risk receivables and balance sheet.
  - **Cyient DLM**: Near-term margin compression accepted as product development and sales headcount expenses are fully loaded upfront.
  - **RIR Power Electronics**: Shifted focus from order booking to plant productivity as order backlog doubled.
- **Balance Sheet Restructuring**: 
  - **Debt Payoffs**: Aditya Infotech (turned net debt-free post Rs 1,500 cr QIP), Diamond Power (prepaid Rs 501 cr NCLT resolution plan amount to turn net cash), Laser Power & Infra (repaid Rs 490 cr borrowings via IPO proceeds), Lumino Industries (repaid Rs 264 cr debt post-IPO), Orient Cables (wiped out Rs 155.5 cr debt post-IPO), Websol Energy (turned debt-free post IREDA repayment).

## What we were watching

- ✖ **went the other way** · Nov 2026: SECI annulled floating solar and BESS tender / Cancelled Agri-PV tenders  
  → SECI cancelled tenders, but utility BESS and solar additions continued scaling aggressively via private C&I and state utility orders. Premier Energies, Saatvik, Waaree Energies, and GK Energy reported record order books.
- ✅ **showed up** · Nov 2026: Maharashtra govt approved 4000 MWh battery storage tariffs  
  → BESS execution scaled sharply; Captain Polyplast bagged repeat MSEDCL solar pump orders under PM-KUSUM, while Prostarm, GK Energy, and Solarworld reported massive BESS order books.
- ✅ **showed up** · Nov 2026: Supreme Court 70:30 GST formula not retrospective before Jan 2019  
  → Solarium Green Energy noted GST on solar components was cut to 5% effective Sept 2025, and tax/legal clarity benefited solar EPC project execution.
- ◐ **partly** · Nov 2026: UP, MP, TN, and Rajasthan state government decisions (storage, 21-day clearances, EV incentives)  
  → Solarium Green Energy, Atlanta Electricals, and GK Energy won large contracts in AP, Maharashtra, and Rajasthan. However, Raksan Transformers noted GRAP construction restrictions in Delhi-NCR temporarily delayed Sonipat plant completion.
- ⏳ **not yet** · Nov 2026: Proposed US Tariffs (25%/17%) on Indian goods  
  → Pending proposed tariffs did not impact Q2 dispatches directly, but US policy changes (Section 232 circular introducing MIP and 15% ad valorem duty) caused Vikram Solar to drop 1 GW of US export orders and Waaree Energies to de-risk 3 GW of export orders into local US manufacturing.
