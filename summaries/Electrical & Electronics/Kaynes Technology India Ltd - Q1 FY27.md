# Kaynes Technology India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth (40% YoY) and robust order book, but tempered by margin pressure from supply chain disruptions, negative operating cash flow, and a strategic decision to scale down the smart metering business to manage working capital.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 946 crore (40% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 147.6 crore (31% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.6%.
*   **Order Book:** ~Rs 9,000 crore.
*   **Operating Cash Flow (CFO):** Negative Rs 259 crore.
*   **Capex (Q1):** Rs 180 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** ~Rs 800 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Business Mix:** EMS business grew 48% YoY; Smart Metering business (GridCrest) saw a 12% decline YoY as management consciously restricted production/supplies to prioritize collections and improve balance sheet health.
*   **New Wins:** Added India’s second-largest 2-wheeler EV manufacturer; onboarded global brands from Germany and France; secured a marquee automotive opportunity for PCB.
*   **Strategic Initiatives:** Completed 1 year of August Electronics acquisition, which is contributing to North American market access.
*   **Space Tech:** First 3U satellite in prototype phase (expected launch mid-next year); entered titanium gas bottle manufacturing for ISRO/DRDO.
*   **Sustainability:** Planting 10,000 trees; solar installation in progress to increase renewable energy consumption.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **OSAT & PCB:** Commercial revenue booking expected to start in Q3/Q4 FY27. Management targets Rs 450-500 crore revenue for these segments combined for FY27.
*   **Cash Flow:** Management remains confident in turning cash positive by the end of the financial year.
*   **Growth:** Management targets 2x market growth; expects a "difficult year" due to supply chain/cost pressures.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Supply Chain:** Global disruption in West Asia affecting equipment imports and component logistics; lead times for some categories are 6-8 months.
*   **Pricing:** Component prices have risen 30-35% (other components) and PCB prices have tripled; management notes this is a global issue.
*   **Demand:** Demand remains strong across all segments (railways, automotive, aerospace, defense) despite global inflation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margins impacted by cost escalations (energy, crude, commodities, forex) and higher depreciation from new investments.
*   **Inventory:** Strategic decision to increase inventory (up Rs 156 crore) to mitigate supply chain volatility and ensure business continuity.
*   **Pass-through:** Pricing is generally pass-through, but there is a lag in adjustment (quarter-on-quarter).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **OSAT/PCB:** Current capex goal is Rs 300 crore each for OSAT and PCB; EMS capex planned at Rs 250 crore.
*   **Operational Status:** OSAT Unit 2 and Circuit Chennai facility remain on track to be operational by Q3 FY27.
*   **Funding:** Management stated cash is not a constraint; modular investments will continue based on customer demand and subsidy inflows.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** 12-month rolling working capital days at 163 days.
*   **Receivables:** Significant improvement in collections from a leading EV 2-wheeler customer (receivables now <Rs 100 crore).
*   **Metering Receivables:** High receivables in the metering business (Rs 1,311 crore) prompted the decision to restrict production.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Supply Chain:** Global component shortages and logistics disruptions are causing timing slippage in OSAT/PCB ramp-up.
*   **Profitability:** Management expects profitability to take a "couple of quarters" to normalize.
*   **Metering Business:** Ongoing challenges with receivables and long-term service models; management is evaluating options to de-risk or divest the service portion of this business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the negative operating cash flow?** Management explained it was driven by a strategic decision to increase inventory (Rs 156 crore) and high receivables in the metering business (Rs 1,311 crore).
*   **Q: Any plans to divest the smart metering business?** Management acknowledged the business model is challenging and is exploring options to de-risk the receivables/service portion; a strategy update is promised by February.
*   **Q: Are you seeing margin compression due to component costs?** Management admitted it is a "difficult year" and confirmed that while pricing is pass-through, there is a lag, which will pressure the bottom line in the near term.
*   **Q: What is the status of OSAT/PCB validation?** Management confirmed OSAT validation is starting and PCB validation is ongoing with a global player; commercial billing is on track for Q3/Q4.

### One-line takeaway
Kaynes is prioritizing balance sheet discipline and strategic inventory building over short-term metering growth, while aggressively scaling its high-potential OSAT and PCB segments for H2 FY27.
