# Exicom Tele-Systems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights a record order book, successful commissioning of the Hyderabad plant, and a clear path to consolidated EBITDA breakeven within two quarters.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 236.8 crore (+57% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 331.1 crore (+61% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 20.9 crore (8.8% margin).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Loss of Rs 21.9 crore (6.6% negative margin).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Loss of Rs 73.6 crore.
*   **Order Book:** Rs 1,400 crore+ (Consolidated); Rs 1,000 crore (Critical Power).
*   **Tritium Performance:** USD 10 million revenue; USD 21 million bookings.

### Highlights
*   **Hyderabad Plant:** Fully operational; provides 3x production capability.
*   **Critical Power:** Revenue grew 73% YoY (consolidated). Secured large orders from Indian telcos and BharatNet project.
*   **EV Segment:** Revenue grew 50% YoY (consolidated). Secured 100% share of business for a leading international brand's wallbox chargers.
*   **Tritium:** Bookings doubled to USD 21 million; management reports a "momentum shift" in demand.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Breakeven:** Management expects consolidated EBITDA breakeven within the next two quarters and Tritium EBITDA breakeven by Q4 FY27.
*   **Growth:** Management targets 3x revenue growth for Tritium compared to the previous year.
*   **Exports:** Targeting to increase export sales to 15% of Critical Power revenue in FY27 (from 8% in Q1).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Critical Power:** Driven by 5G rollout, BharatNet fiber connectivity, and BSNL-led infrastructure programs in border regions.
*   **EV:** Passenger car registrations are at record highs, driving demand for AC chargers.
*   **Tritium:** New products (TRI-FLEX, DC-FLEX, GRID-FLEX) are in mid-to-advanced trial stages with Fortune 100 companies.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Standalone gross margin at 29.1% (up 2% QoQ, down 3.6% YoY).
*   **Fixed Costs:** Increased by Rs 8.7 crore due to parallel operations at Gurgaon and Hyderabad plants.
*   **Tritium Margins:** Impacted by the exhaustion of low-cost inventory acquired previously.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity Utilization:** DC charging ~65%; AC charging ~100%; DC power systems ~90-100%; PCBA lines ~100%.
*   **Expansion:** Management plans to increase AC charger monthly production run rate by 50% over the next 3 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Consolidated debt stood at Rs 370 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** Inventory is temporarily elevated due to parallel plant operations; receivables are elevated due to sharp revenue growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Supply Chain:** Geopolitical disruptions affecting semiconductors, plastic, and copper availability.
*   **Commodity Prices:** Rising input costs impacting margins.
*   **Tritium Turnaround:** Management acknowledged the turnaround has taken longer than expected.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did consolidated EBITDA move to a loss despite 61% growth?** Management attributed this to the exhaustion of low-cost inventory at Tritium and the Rs 8.7 crore fixed cost impact of running two plants in parallel.
*   **Q: When will Tritium orders convert to revenue?** Management stated that large strategic opportunities are expected to convert to commercial contracts in CY26, with execution beginning in Q1 CY27.
*   **Q: Is the 222,000 AC charger capacity figure accurate?** Management clarified this capacity is based on double-shift operations.
*   **Q: What is the current capacity utilization in the US (Tritium)?** Management noted current revenue is roughly 1/4th of the facility's potential capacity.
*   **Q: Why was standalone EV growth only 15%?** Management explained this was a timing issue; Q4 FY26 saw high DC charger demand due to pre-ordering, which impacted the sequential growth comparison for Q1.

### One-line takeaway
Exicom is navigating a transition phase characterized by high growth in order books and capacity expansion, with management signaling a return to consolidated profitability within two quarters as parallel plant costs phase out.
