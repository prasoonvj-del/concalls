# Premier Energies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY revenue and profit growth, commissioning of the 7 GW cell line, and record operational cell capacity utilization.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained margin expectations; full-year FY27 capex guided to ~Rs 2,400 crore (moderating to ~Rs 700 crore over H2 FY27).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained upbeat; project commissioning expected to accelerate in H2, driving annual India solar additions to ~50 GW AC.
- **New topics or risks:** Draft Chinese export controls on wafer/ingot manufacturing tech emerged; commodity inflation/delays from Middle East war flagged; management explicitly dismissed market rumors of GEF promoter block sales.
- **Last quarter promises:** Delivered commissioning of the 7 GW Naidupeta TOPCon cell line (trial runs on; ALMM inspection scheduled). BESS partnership with RCT finalized.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,656 crore (+38.2% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 760 crore (+17.8% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 462 crore (+30.8% YoY)
- **Solar manufacturing EBITDA margin:** 29.3%
- **Solar manufacturing PAT margin:** 17.9%
- **Transcon subsidiary:** Revenue Rs 122 crore, PAT Rs 18 crore, EBITDA margin 15%, PAT margin 8%
- **Order inflows:** Rs 4,001 crore during the quarter
- **Total order book:** Rs 16,260 crore (cells & modules) + Rs 330 crore (Transcon transformers)

### Highlights
- Commissioned 7 GW TOPCon cell line at Naidupeta; trial runs underway with commercial production expected in late November 2026.
- Total order book reached Rs 16,260 crore, continuing the steady order expansion pattern recorded over recent quarters.
- Strong order inflow of Rs 4,001 crore during the quarter.
- Management firmly denied market rumors regarding GEF or promoter block sales and clarified that the QIP resolution is purely enabling with no capital raise planned.

### Guidance & outlook
- **7 GW Cell Ramp-up:** Guided to reach 25%-30% CUF in December 2026, scaling to ~70% CUF by end of March 2027.
- **Depreciation:** Full-year FY27 depreciation expected around Rs 600 crore.
- **Transformer Business:** Expected to treble revenues over 3 years, with normalized EBITDA margins of ~15%.
- **US Cell Plant:** Evaluating location with a JV partner; targeting finalization/operationalization within 12-18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's full-year solar installations expected to reach ~50 GW AC, with PM Surya Ghar Yojana compensating for utility-scale weakness.
- Forthcoming Surya Ghar 2.0 and KUSUM 2.0 schemes expected to improve the product mix toward higher-margin DCR modules.
- Standalone, unintegrated module assemblers face severe pressure, whereas vertically integrated scale players remain well-positioned.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- DCR module prices: Retail at Rs 23-24/watt; KUSUM-C/C&I at Rs 21-21.5/watt; IPP segment at Rs 20-20.5/watt (blended average ~Rs 21.5-22/watt).
- Cell pricing: Mono PERC at $0.12-$0.125/watt; TOPCon at $0.14-$0.145/watt.
- Non-DCR modules priced at Rs 12.75-13/watt (single-digit EBITDA margins).
- Commodity inflation (aluminum, copper, polymer films) following Middle East war increased costs and stretched delivery timelines; silver prices softened.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **FY27 Capex:** Guided at ~Rs 2,400 crore (~Rs 700 crore expected across H2 FY27).
- **Ingot & Wafer:** 169 acres acquired in Naidupeta with approvals; Phase 1 targeted by Dec 2027 and Phase 2 (+5 GW, reaching 10 GW) by June 2028.
- **BESS:** Operationalization expected in Q2 2027; partnership with RCT (Germany) provides access to European/US markets and Indian-made EMS to achieve 40% local DCR requirement without cell-level manufacturing capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Capital work-in-progress (CWIP) stood at ~Rs 2,900 crore as of September 30, 2026 (primarily the Naidupeta cell plant).
- Working capital: Temporary inventory buildup driven by pre-commissioning buffer stock for Seetharampur and Naidupeta and shipping disruptions; expected to normalize by Q4 FY27.
- Credit rating stands at A+.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential export controls by China on advanced wafer/ingot manufacturing equipment.
- Raw material price inflation and supply chain bottlenecks linked to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.
- Aggressive pricing by competitors in utility and non-DCR segments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sumit Kishore (Axis Capital) on China technology export controls:** Management noted equipment for the 7 GW cell line is already delivered. Ingot/wafer draft restrictions apply primarily to ultra-thin slicing machinery, and pre-existing wafer supply pact deposits are registered with Chinese authorities.
- **Kunal Shah (DAM Capital) on block sale and QIP rumors:** Management refuted rumors of GEF/promoter block deals and stated operating cash flows are fully adequate for capex, ruling out near-term QIP issuance.
- **Sahil Sheth (Anand Rathi) on declining cell order book:** Management explained no cell orders were canceled; rather, cell order backlogs fell because new cell output is increasingly reserved for captive use in the new 5.6 GW module line.
- **Praveen Sahay (PL Capital) on low aggressive bids (e.g. SECI at ~Rs 17-18/watt):** Management clarified this represented introductory discounting by a new entrant to win market credentials and does not reflect prevailing market pricing (~Rs 20-20.5/watt for IPP DCR).
- **Apoorva Bahadur (IIFL Capital) on inventory build-up:** Management attributed the rise in inventory to raw material buffering ahead of large-scale ramp-ups and shipping delays, projecting normalization by March 2027.

### One-line takeaway
Premier Energies maintained strong earnings growth with ~29% solar EBITDA margins, while successfully commissioning its 7 GW cell line to support captive integration and future scale.
