# Salzer Electronics Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue growth remains strong at 38.4% YoY, and management expects progressive margin recovery in H2 FY27 as pricing actions take effect.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained revenue guidance of Rs 2,100 crore for FY27. EBITDA margin guidance for FY27 is now "around 8%" (previously 8%–8.5%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "mixed" to more optimistic regarding demand, though acknowledging continued geopolitical/commodity headwinds.
- **New topics/risks:** Saudi Arabia plant delayed further to April 2027 (previously Sept/Oct 2026). Bengaluru street light project revenue start delayed to Feb 2027.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Did not deliver on the Saudi plant start date (delayed due to regional conflict).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 579.77 crore (up 38.4% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,077.79 crore (up 25.32% YoY).
- **EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 39.95 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 6.89% (vs 8.73% in Q2 FY26; 6.29% in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT:** Rs 14.87 crore (vs Rs 13.39 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT Margin:** 2.56% (vs 3.2% in Q2 FY26).
- **Export Contribution:** 23.6% of revenue in Q2 FY27.
- **Working Capital:** Net working capital days reduced to 135 days (from 153 days in March 2026).

### Highlights
- **Segment Performance:** Industrial Switchgear (56.39% of revenue, +23.79% YoY growth); Wires & Cable (36.18% of revenue, +57.48% YoY growth); Building Products (7.43% of revenue, +103.53% YoY growth).
- **Kaycee Industries:** Revenue Rs 17.95 crore (+20.4% YoY); EBITDA margin 14.37%.
- **Smart Metering:** Revenue Rs 2.87 crore for H1 FY27; management noted execution remains challenging.
- **Project Delays:** Saudi Arabia plant operations rescheduled to April 2027; Bengaluru street light project revenue start delayed to Feb 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Maintained FY27 revenue target of Rs 2,100 crore.
- **Margins:** Expects EBITDA margins to normalize progressively in Q3 and Q4; full-year FY27 EBITDA margin target is "around 8%".
- **FY28 Outlook:** Expects EBITDA margin to be around 9%–10%.
- **Saudi Arabia:** Expects first-year revenue of Rs 20 crore, growing to Rs 100 crore over 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Sector Tailwinds:** Strong demand from power infrastructure, renewable energy (PM-DHARA program), industrial automation, and data centers.
- **Pricing:** Management has implemented price increases over the past several months to offset commodity inflation; notes a time lag between announcement and realization in revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Input Costs:** Commodity price volatility (copper, silver, aluminum, plastics) continues to impact margins.
- **Pass-through:** Wire & Cable division operates on a cost-plus basis (stable margins); Switchgear division faces a lag in price pass-through.
- **Hedging:** Company does not currently hedge commodities.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex:** Modest capex planned for FY27; focused on balancing capacity in the Switchgear division (expected to add 20%–25% capacity).
- **Capacity Utilization:** Switchgear at ~80%; Wires & Cable at ~65%.
- **Saudi Arabia:** Total project cost estimated at Rs 15 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Working Capital:** Objective is to keep working capital within ~25% of revenue.
- **Finance Cost:** Finance cost as a percentage of sales is currently 2.3%–2.4%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Geopolitical:** War in the Middle East causing delays in Saudi plant execution.
- **Execution:** Delays in Bengaluru street light project due to internal restructuring of the municipal body (BBMP).
- **Margin Pressure:** Continued impact of raw material price volatility on Switchgear margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Analyst:** Asked about the "drag" of the Smart Meter business. **Management:** Stated the drag on EBITDA/PAT is 0.7%–1% max; they are exploring exports to Far East/Middle East/Africa.
- **Analyst:** Asked about the Saudi plant delay. **Management:** Confirmed delay due to regional conflict; installation of machinery expected by January 2027.
- **Analyst:** Asked if revenue guidance could be raised given H1 growth. **Management:** Declined, sticking to the Rs 2,100 crore target.
- **Analyst:** Asked about hedging. **Management:** Confirmed they have explored it but currently do not hedge.
- **Analyst:** Asked about the Bengaluru project revenue. **Management:** Confirmed annuity revenue of ~Rs 2.25 crore/month for 84 months.

### One-line takeaway
Salzer reported strong top-line growth but continues to face margin compression due to commodity price lags, with management guiding for progressive margin recovery in H2 FY27 and a full-year EBITDA margin of ~8%.
