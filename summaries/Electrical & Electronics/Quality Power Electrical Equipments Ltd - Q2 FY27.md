# Quality Power Electrical Equipments Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue and profit after tax grew strongly, driven by business mix, operating efficiencies, and cost management.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 285.45 crore
- Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 63.33 crore (up 80.10% YoY)
- Consolidated Order Book: Crossed Rs 2,000 crore (excluding Win-Win Specialty Insulators)
- Win-Win Specialty Insulators Order Intake: Averaging ~Rs 35 crore/month (approx. Rs 75 crore in last 2 months)
- Hyperinflation Impact (Ind AS 29): Rs 8 crore debit in Q1, net credit of Rs 4 crore in Q2 (net loss impact)

### Highlights
- Sangli global coil factory commenced manufacturing operations on 20th October 2026; first round of ISO audit completed, final customer audits to start end of November.
- New PCS factory for BESS applications in Nigde, Turkey, commenced manufacturing operations; billing expected in the current quarter subject to contractual milestones.
- Win-Win (WS) Insulators successfully completed type tests for porcelain insulators up to 400 kV and secured orders for 800 kV HVDC, 765 kV AC, and 400 kV AC from major Indian utilities including Power Grid.
- Proposed QIP targeted for November, subject to regulatory approvals and market conditions.
- Consolidated order book crossed Rs 2,000 crore for the first time.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated it is too early to celebrate and guidance remains unchanged; normal practice is to review guidance at the end of Q3.
- Q3 at Quality Power may be subdued due to transitions between factories and people.
- Endoks expected to have a blockbuster year in revenue from the PCS factory; management plans to revise guidelines at the end of Q3 if everything goes as planned.
- Management guided for at least ~50% growth for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Transmission infrastructure, HVDC, FACTS, renewable integration, grid stability, and energy storage continue to present opportunities.
- Global supply chain for specialized electrical equipment remains constrained due to geopolitical developments, changing trade conditions, logistics disruptions, and extended procurement lead times.
- Insulator demand significantly exceeds supply globally; delivery lead times for 765 kV switchgear are 18 months primarily due to porcelain shortages.
- US enacted a new rule banning Chinese equipment after 69 kV in transmission, benefiting compliant non-Chinese players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone material cost increased from 35% to 45% in Q2 due to normalization of previous quarter's vendor drawbacks and significant rises in aluminum, resin, and paint prices.
- Contracts are largely fixed-price (approx. 99%), with buffers factored in based on copper/aluminum price movements at delivery timelines.
- Target group stable EBITDA/operating margins are guided around 25%. Quality Power standalone margins >25%, Mehru ~22%-23%, Insulators booked at ~25% EBIT (would be >30% without SEZ duty), and PCS at ~10%-12%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- QIP proceeds of ~Rs 700 crore planned utilization: ~Rs 175 crore for repaying debt and WS promoters; Rs 50-70 crore for expanding porcelain/composite insulator capacity at WS; ~Rs 300 crore for working capital; balance for US office/Class B level manufacturing entry and future acquisitions.
- Endoks PCS factory capacity is 4 GW (peak revenue potential ~Rs 1,500-2,000 crore for PCS/BESS).
- Sangli coil factory peak revenue potential is north of Rs 1,500 crore. WS current capacity is ~Rs 300 crore, expanding to slightly north of Rs 500 crore with new capex.
- Mehru looking at renting a facility near its current factory for immediate order stabilization, followed by a new factory in ~18 months to double capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt is close to zero.
- Working capital requirements are elongating due to reverse integration into cable manufacturing (HVDC CTC cables) which adds 4 months of manufacturing and credit timelines.
- Inventory increased due to stocking of chipsets, IGBTs, and memory cards for Endoks (~Rs 20 crore in Turkey) and long lead-time items like insulators/bushings for Quality Power and Mehru.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elongated working capital cycles resulting from reverse integration into cable manufacturing and rising order book execution.
- Global supply chain constraints, logistics disruptions, and extended procurement lead times.
- Transition friction and lower workforce productivity during factory relocations and parallel customer audits (approx. 60 customer audits required for Sangli).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Manav Singhal (Individual Investor) asked about the specific quantum utilization of the proposed Rs 700 crore QIP.** 
   *Management answered that ~Rs 175 crore goes to debt/WS promoter repayment, Rs 50-70 crore for WS capacity expansion, ~Rs 300 crore for working capital (due to elongated manufacturing/credit cycles from cable backward integration), and the rest for US market entry and future M&A.*
2. **Viraj asked about the impact of GE Vernova acquiring Trench Group for $9 billion.**
   *Management replied that Trench is their largest competitor. While GE may initially source from its own acquired entity, Quality Power expects to gain considerable leverage and increased sourcing volumes from Hitachi and Siemens, who control 70%-75% of the HVDC/FACTS market.*
3. **Pritesh Chheda asked about peak revenue potentials and capacities for Mehru, Win-Win, and Endoks.**
   *Management stated WS peak capacity will exceed Rs 500 crore post-capex; Endoks PCS capacity of 4 GW has a potential of ~Rs 1,500-2,000 crore; Sangli coil factory potential is north of Rs 1,500 crore; Mehru requires a new factory in 18 months to double capacity.*
4. **Abhijit Singh asked about Win-Win's commercial ramp-up, 400 kV type tests, and gas supply.**
   *Management noted that a new PNG pipeline via GAIL starting next Monday will lower energy costs and allow full-scale production. They guide for ~Rs 200 crore revenue next year for WS, quoting 6-9 months delivery against an industry standard of 18 months.*
5. **Chirag asked about standalone material costs rising from 35% to 45% and minority stakes in subsidiaries.**
   *Management explained that Q1 margins were not sustainable due to vendor drawbacks, and aluminum, resin, and petrochemical prices rose. Regarding 49% minority stakes in Mehru and Endoks, management stated buying them out would be a capital misallocation since they already access technologies via a 1% royalty.*

### One-line takeaway
Quality Power reported a strong Q2 FY27 with PAT up 80.1% YoY to Rs 63.33 crore and an order book crossing Rs 2,000 crore, while scaling new manufacturing facilities in Sangli and Turkey and advancing a Rs 700 crore QIP to fund working capital and expansion.
