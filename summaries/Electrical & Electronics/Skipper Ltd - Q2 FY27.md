# Skipper Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record revenue and order inflows, with strong balance sheet improvement, despite acknowledging some shipment deferrals in exports.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,384 crore (up 10% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 2,694 crore (up 7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 145 crore (up 11% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 10.5%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 57.5 crore (up 56% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 4.2% (up 130 bps YoY).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 10,066 crore.
*   **Order Inflows (Q2):** Rs 2,111 crore (up 70% YoY).
*   **Order Inflows (H1):** Rs 3,786 crore (up 18% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 964 crore (including interest-bearing acceptances, down Rs 358 crore YoY).
*   **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.38x.

### Highlights
*   **Financial Position:** Strengthened by a Rs 433.5 crore preferential equity raise and a CRISIL rating upgrade to A+/stable.
*   **Export Diversification:** Middle East exposure reduced to <18% of export order book; North America (44%) and Australia (18-19%) are key growth drivers.
*   **Acquisition:** Announced acquisition of a small manufacturing company in South India for Rs 15-16 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** Net working capital days stable at 90-100 days.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains 15% FY27 growth target, though noted a potential downside of 1-2% due to shipment deferrals.
*   **Order Inflow:** Maintained FY27 target of Rs 7,500 crore.
*   **Margins:** Aspirational long-term EBITDA margin target is 12%; management expects to maintain 10-11% range in the interim.
*   **Export Inflow:** Target of Rs 1,500 crore for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Transmission Sector:** Outlook remains robust in India and globally, driven by grid expansion, renewable integration, and AI-led data center power requirements.
*   **Domestic Market:** Described as "vibrant" with robust order inflows in H1.
*   **Export Market:** Facing temporary shipment deferrals due to elevated global freight rates; management expects shipping rates to have peaked and anticipates reductions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Employee Cost:** Q2 spike due to arrears payment; expected to normalize to ~5.5% of revenue in Q3.
*   **Margin Pressures:** Facing cost pressures from fuel and commodity price increases, which tempered potential EBITDA margin expansion.
*   **Contracts:** Majority of orders are fixed-price; management stated risk to margins is "nil" due to sufficient pricing buffers and down payments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current Expansion:** 75,000 MTPA capacity expansion (taking total to 450,000 MTPA) is on track for commissioning in H2 FY27.
*   **Long-term Roadmap:** Aiming for 600,000 MTPA capacity by FY29 via greenfield expansion.
*   **Capex Intensity:** Annual capex range is Rs 250-300 crore; management expects to fund this via internal accruals.
*   **Greenfield Plans:** Currently identifying land; first phase of new unit targeted to start by end of next financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Finance Cost:** Declined to 3.2% of revenue (from 4.2% YoY).
*   **Cash Flow:** Management expects operating cash flow to be at least 75% of total cash flow this year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical/Macro:** Continued geopolitical uncertainties and elevated shipping freight rates causing customer shipment deferrals.
*   **Middle East:** Region remains a challenge; management is not optimistic about near-term revival.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Analyst asked about the feasibility of 15% revenue growth given H1 performance.* Management admitted at worst, growth might be 13-14% due to shipment deferrals.
*   *Analyst questioned margin stability if Infra (lower margin) drives growth.* Management stated the quality of contracts is improving in both Engineering and Infra, supporting margin stability.
*   *Analyst asked about export working capital.* Management clarified that export collections are often faster (upon shipment), though inventory cycles are longer, making them comparable to domestic business.
*   *Analyst asked about capacity utilization.* Management noted ~80% utilization, with 90% being "doable" but currently constrained by export shipment deferrals.

### One-line takeaway
Skipper is leveraging a strong balance sheet and diversified export order book to navigate near-term shipping headwinds while maintaining a robust long-term growth trajectory in the power transmission sector.
