# Electrical & Electronics - Consumer electricals: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The consumer electricals and electronics sector delivered robust revenue growth in Q2 FY27, bolstered by festive demand shifts, strong volume execution in appliances like Room ACs and washing machines, and scaling new verticals (such as electronics and renewables). However, profitability across many players faced headwinds from persistent raw material inflation (metals, polymers, and energy) and foreign exchange volatility. While companies with strong pricing power successfully defended or expanded margins via price hikes and cost-efficiency programs, contract manufacturers and those tied to fixed-price legacy contracts experienced margin compression.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board noted that underlying consumer demand remains resilient. Strong traction was reported in high-drain alkaline batteries, BLDC fans, solar products, and kitchen appliances. Festive season demand (Dhanteras/Diwali) was viewed with optimism following low channel inventories.
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   *Regional/Weather Impact:* **V-Guard Industries** highlighted strong regional disparities, with South India growing 51% due to an extended summer compared to 23% in the North/East. Conversely, **Aditya Vision** cited local headwinds including floods/cyclones in Bihar and slow October sales.
    *   *Segment Divergence:* **Amber Enterprises** flagged slow offtake and delays in Railway Subsystems, whereas its consumer durables and electronics divisions thrived. **Blue Star** noted a temporary pause in quick commerce infrastructure impacting commercial refrigeration. **Bajaj Electricals** lagged in entry-level fans while its BLDC fans and switchgears surged.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Almost all managements (including **Amber Enterprises**, **Cello World**, **Crompton Greaves**, **Eveready Industries**, **Havells India**, **Orient Electric**, **PG Electroplast**, and **Surya Roshni**) reported sharp inflationary pressures across key raw materials—specifically copper, aluminum, zinc, steel, polymers, and natural gas (with **Cello World** noting gas prices doubled to ~INR 53-54 SCM).
*   **Disagreements & Strategies:** 
    *   *Price Pass-Through:* Brand-focused players like **Havells India** (10% to 25% cumulative hikes), **Crompton Greaves** (11% to 15%), and **Orient Electric** (3% to 6%) actively passed on costs through calibrated price increases and internal cost-saving programs (e.g., Crompton's *Unnati* project, Orient's *Project Sanchay*).
    *   *Contract Manufacturing Constraint:* Contract manufacturers like **PG Electroplast** and **Amber Enterprises** noted that percentage gross margins naturally soften in high-commodity environments because their business models operate on fixed absolute per-unit margins or suffer from a 1-quarter lag in cost pass-throughs. Blue Star reported an unpassed 5% gap on RAC material cost escalations.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Amber Enterprises:** Guided full-year FY27 capex at Rs 1,800–2,000 crore.
*   **Havells India:** Maintained FY27 capex guidance at Rs 1,400 crore, focusing on Cables & Wires and R&D.
*   **PG Electroplast:** On track for ~Rs 400 crore in FY27 capex, with the Sri City refrigerator plant and Supa compressor project progressing toward commercial production.
*   **Surya Roshni:** Planned Rs 150–155 crore capex for FY27, including DFT mill and greenfield expansions.
*   **Blue Star:** Full-year capex guided at ~Rs 300 crore.
*   **V-Guard Industries:** Full-year capex guided at ~Rs 150 crore.
*   **Aditya Vision:** Adding ~Rs 90 lakhs per store, targeting 230–235 stores by FY27 end.
*   **IKIO Technologies:** Block 2 partially commercialized; Block 3 construction on track for completion by Q3 FY27.
*   **Susan Electricals:** Expanding manufacturing capacity from 7,500 tons to 12,000 tons across FY28–FY29.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Amber Enterprises:** Cut FY27 revenue growth guidance for Railway Subsystems & Defence to 15%–20% (down from 30%–35%) and margins to 8%–10% due to legacy fixed-price contracts. Restructured its mobile business into a dedicated subsidiary, Amber Digital Technologies.
*   **Cello World:** Shifted from a cautious tone to greater optimism following October festive demand normalization, maintaining a ~10% full-year growth outlook.
*   **IKIO Technologies:** Raised growth expectations for its "other business" segment to 35%–50%, shifting its tone from cautious to confident.

## What to watch next quarter
*   The actual translation and retail sell-through of the festive season (Diwali/Dhanteras) into primary order replenishment.
*   The ability of companies like **Blue Star**, **Eveready Industries**, **V-Guard**, and **Orient Electric** to fully absorb planned price hikes and recover gross margins.
*   Progress on key capacity ramp-ups, including **PG Electroplast's** Sri City refrigerator plant and Supa compressor facility, and **Susan Electricals'** new high-tension CCV lines.
*   Execution updates on **Amber Enterprises'** mobile manufacturing scale-up and railway order offtake.

## Company one-liners
*   **Aditya Vision:** Delivered a record-breaking Q2 with 28% revenue growth while expanding its retail footprint into three new states.
*   **Amber Enterprises:** Scaling its electronics and mobile verticals rapidly while navigating temporary margin pressure from fixed-price railway contracts and commodity lags.
*   **Bajaj Electricals:** Prioritizing a turnaround in its fan portfolio and scaling new categories like wires/switches alongside a leadership transition.
*   **Blue Star:** Experiencing strong RAC volume growth and data center wins, tempered by margin pressure from unpassed raw material inflation.
*   **Cello World:** Revenue grew 7.8% despite festive shifts, though margins faced heavy compression from surging energy and polymer costs.
*   **Crompton Greaves:** Delivered an 18.2% revenue jump backed by normalized supply chains, strong rural demand, and disciplined price pass-throughs.
*   **Eveready Industries:** Achieved its 8th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth on strong alkaline/rechargeable traction while managing raw material inflation.
*   **Havells India:** Posted robust 35% revenue growth across established and emerging categories, utilizing price hikes to manage commodity volatility.
*   **IKIO Technologies:** Successfully scaling its diversified non-lighting verticals and international footprint to exceed initial growth guidance.
*   **Orient Electric:** Reported 25.2% revenue growth driven by premiumization and market share gains in fans, even as inflation impacted gross margins.
*   **PG Electroplast:** Crossed Rs. 2,950 crore in H1 revenues driven by aggressive capacity scaling in ACs, washing machines, and backward integration.
*   **Surya Roshni:** Posted a record Q2 with 33% revenue growth, a zero-debt balance sheet, and strong export momentum in steel pipes.
*   **Susan Electricals:** Delivered a stellar 190% revenue surge and doubled its order book, supported by new high-tension cable capabilities.
*   **V-Guard Industries:** Reported 38.2% revenue growth and a 71% PAT jump, powered by extended summer weather and robust electronics demand.
