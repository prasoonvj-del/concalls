# KEI Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to 25%+ backed by robust demand, strong order books, and ongoing capacity expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Net Sales:** Rs 3,776 crore, up 38.52% YoY (Wire & Cable segment sales grew 39.5% YoY).
- **Operating Margin:** 9.97% vs 9.88% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 401 crore, up 28.69% YoY (EBITDA margin at 10.62%).
- **PAT:** Rs 262.45 crore, up 28.96% YoY (PAT margin at 6.95% vs 7.46% YoY).
- **Domestic Wire & Cable Sales:** Rs 3,184 crore, up 46% YoY.
- **Export Sales:** Rs 493 crore vs Rs 472 crore YoY.
- **Extra High Voltage (EHV) Cable Sales:** Rs 217 crore vs Rs 172 crore YoY.
- **EPC Sales:** Rs 46 crore vs Rs 47 crore YoY.
- **Stainless Steel Wire Sales:** Rs 61 crore vs Rs 53 crore YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Net Sales:** Rs 6,961 crore, up ~31% YoY; PAT at Rs 536.59 crore, up 34.4% YoY; H1 EBITDA margin at 11.73%.
- **Order Book:** Rs 4,927 crore in Wires & Cables (includes Rs 991 crore exports) and Rs 258 crore in EPC.

### Highlights
- Sales mix in Q2 FY27: B2C / distribution network contributed 59%, domestic B2B / institutional 26%, exports 13%, EPC and stainless steel wire 2%.
- Active working dealer count stood at 2,146 as of September 30, 2026. The company reaches 25,000+ retailers and over 2 lakh electricians.
- Book value per equity share rose to Rs 753 as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 697 as of March 31, 2026).
- CARE and ICRA long-term rating stands at AA+ and short-term rating at A1+.
- Q2 included Rs 8 crore in foreign exchange gains (vs less than Rs 1 crore in Q1 FY27).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to 25%+ YoY (up from 20% earlier) and expects a 20% CAGR over the next 3 to 4 years.
- Annual EBITDA margins are guided at 11% for FY27, with plans to move toward 11.5%–12% as Sanand capacity ramps up.
- PAT is expected to grow 3% to 4% faster than top-line growth (aiming for 24%–25% PAT growth over the medium term).
- Management expects ROCE and ROE to return to historical levels of 23%–24% and 19%–20%, respectively, post full commissioning of Sanand.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains strong across renewable energy (solar, wind), power T&D, thermal projects, data centers, EV infrastructure, railways/metros, and urban infrastructure.
- Strong housing and commercial space activity is driving domestic wire demand.
- Export demand is robust, especially from the USA (which constitutes ~50% of the export order book), as well as the Middle East and Australia.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 margin dynamics were impacted by raw material price volatility and inventory adjustments, but management emphasizes evaluating margins on an annualized basis.
- Raw material price changes (copper/aluminum) are naturally hedged through 3–4 months of inventory and order book alignment for B2B, while retail prices are revised twice a month.
- EPC division reported small EBIT losses (Rs 8 crore in Q2; Rs 5 crore in Q1) due to winding-down costs; full-year EPC EBIT loss is expected at around Rs 20 crore before complete closure.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total metal-consuming capacity currently stands at ~183,000 metric tonnes per annum.
- H1 FY27 capacity utilization: Cable 77%, House Wire 64%, Stainless Steel Wire 91%, Communication Cable 43%.
- Sanand Capex: Incurred Rs 333 crore in H1 FY27 (total Rs 1,876 crore up to September 30, 2026). Another ~Rs 200 crore will be spent in H2 FY27.
- Phase 2 of EHV and HT cable production at Sanand is planned for commercial production by end-March 2027. Once fully operational, Sanand is expected to deliver asset turnover >3x and peak revenue in FY28–FY29.
- Total annual capex guidance is ~Rs 600 crore for the next 3 to 4 years (including Rs 100–150 crore maintenance capex). New capex will focus on the Salarpur facility expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company remains debt-free with cash and bank balances of Rs 698 crore (including Rs 169 crore QIP unutilized funds) as of September 30, 2026.
- CWIP stood at ~Rs 1,300 crore as of September 30, 2026, driven by the Sanand expansion, which will capitalize around March 2027.
- Inventory increased by ~Rs 1,200 crore in H1 FY27 due to raw material valuation and factory filling at Sanand (~Rs 600–700 crore). Inventory holding is expected to normalize to ~90 days of COGS by March 2027.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Short-term margin volatility caused by copper price swings and inventory revaluation.
- Residual wind-down losses in the legacy EPC business over the next 1–2 quarters.
- Execution and approval timelines for the Sanand Phase 2 EHV expansion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Natasha Jain (PhillipCapital):** Asked about the YoY gross margin decline and entry of new market competitors.  
  *Management Answer:* Clarified that Q1 margins had inventory revaluation impacts that normalized in Q2; annualized EBITDA margins remain on track for 11%, and pricing power remains intact despite competition.
- **Aditya Bhartia (Investec):** Asked if strong H1 growth implies a potential revenue slowdown in H2 based on the 25% guidance.  
  *Management Answer:* Guidance of 25%+ is cautious to account for uncertainties, but the company will capture max available market demand supported by adequate capacity and net cash position.
- **Rahul Agarwal (IKIGAI Asset):** Asked about the loss in EPC and EHV potential post-Sanand.  
  *Management Answer:* EPC will see Rs 5–7 crore loss per quarter as it winds down completely. Sanand will add Rs 1,500–1,700 crore of EHV capacity over the existing Rs 700 crore capacity.
- **Kevin Shah (Aditya Birla Capital):** Asked about volume growth guidance of 10% vs earlier 16%–17%.  
  *Management Answer:* Higher copper prices increase value terms significantly; since capital and budgeting operate in value terms, overall revenue growth of 25%+ value growth reflects true business expansion.
- **Bharat Shah (Bharat Shah Capital Ideas):** Asked about long-term profit growth expectations and focus on margins versus absolute profit/cash flows.  
  *Management Answer:* Agreed that top-line value and cash flow generation drive value creation; profit after tax is expected to grow at 24%–25% (3%–4% faster than 20% top-line CAGR).

### One-line takeaway
KEI Industries delivered strong top-line growth of 38.5% YoY in Q2 FY27, upgraded its FY27 growth guidance to 25%+, and remains well-positioned to expand capacity via its debt-free balance sheet.
