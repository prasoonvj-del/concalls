# KEI Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust demand visibility across domestic and overseas markets, strong order books, and upgraded full-year growth guidance.

### Key numbers
- Net Sales (Q2 FY27): Rs 3,776 crore (up 38.52% YoY)
- Total Wire & Cable Sales Growth (Q2 FY27): 39.5% YoY
- Operating Margin (Q2 FY27): 9.97% vs 9.88% YoY
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 401 crore (up 28.69% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin over Net Sales (Q2 FY27): 10.62% vs 11.46% in H1 FY27
- Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27): Rs 262.45 crore (up 28.96% YoY)
- PAT Margin / Net Sales (Q2 FY27): 6.95% vs 7.46% YoY
- Domestic Wire & Cable Sales: Rs 3,184 crore (up 46% YoY)
- Export Sales (Q2 FY27): Rs 493 crore vs Rs 472 crore YoY
- Extra High Voltage (EHV) Sales (Q2 FY27): Rs 217 crore vs Rs 172 crore YoY
- EPC Sales (Q2 FY27): Rs 46 crore vs Rs 47 crore YoY
- Stainless Steel Wire Sales (Q2 FY27): Rs 61 crore vs Rs 53 crore YoY
- H1 FY27 Net Sales: Rs 6,961 crore (up ~31% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: 11.73% vs 11.46% YoY (Operating Margin: 11.09%)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 536.59 crore (up 34.4% YoY)
- Total Order Book: Wire and Cable Rs 4,927 crore; EPC Rs 258 crore; Export orders out of this: Rs 991 crore
- Cash and bank balances (as on September 30, 2026): Rs 698 crore (including QIP balance of Rs 169 crore)
- H1 FY27 Capex Payment: Rs 402 crore (Sanand capex: Rs 333 crore; Total Sanand capex till date: Rs 1,876 crore)
- Metal Consuming Capacity: ~1,83,000 metric tonnes per annum (Copper and Aluminum combined)
- H1 FY27 Capacity Utilisation: Cable division ~77%, House Wire division ~64%, Stainless Steel Wire division ~91%, Communication Cable capacity ~43%

### Highlights
- Sales contribution: B2B / Domestic institutional 26%, B2C / Distribution network 59%, Exports 13%, Domestic EPC and Stainless Steel Wire 2%.
- Active working dealers stood at 2,146 as of September 30, 2026.
- External ratings: Long-term rating from CARE, India Ratings, and ICRA is AA+; short-term rating is A1+.
- Book value per equity share: Rs 753 vs Rs 697 as of March 31, 2026.
- EPC business is being closed down, leaving minor closure-related EBIT losses (reported Rs 8 crore loss in Q2).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to grow the current year (FY27) by **more than 25%** and achieve a **20% CAGR over the next 3 to 4 years**.
- EBITDA margin guidance is maintained at **11%** for the current year, moving toward **11% to 12%** as Sanand capacity ramps up.
- Planned commercial production of Phase 2 EHV and HT cable in Sanand will commence by the **end of March 2027**. Peak revenue from Sanand is expected by **FY28-29** with an asset turnover of between 3x and 4x.
- Capital expenditure is expected to be approximately **Rs 600 crore annually for the next 3 to 4 years** (comprising Rs 100-150 crore maintenance capex and Rs 450-500 crore growth capex). An additional ~Rs 200 crore will be spent on Sanand in the balance 6 months of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand observed in data centers, power, T&D, renewable energy (solar and wind), thermal power projects, EV infrastructure, railway electrification, metros, urban infrastructure, and domestic/global manufacturing projects.
- Housing and commercial space wire demand remains strong.
- U.S. demand is very strong across industry and energy sectors, forming ~50% of the current export order book.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margin and EBITDA margin remain stable on an annualized basis, though quarter-on-quarter variations occur due to inventory valuation impacts and metal price fluctuations.
- Retail prices are revised twice a month based on metal price movements; institutional orders operate under a natural hedge (3-4 months order book backed by 3-3.5 months inventory).
- Foreign exchange gain for Q2 FY27 was Rs 8 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CWIP increased due to the Sanand project. Sanand Phase 2 EHV and HT cable will commence commercial production by March 2027, capitalizing most CWIP.
- Sanand monthly billing is targeted at Rs 150-200 crore in Q2 and Rs 250-300 crore in Q3.
- House wire production capacity is sufficient for the next 2 to 3 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company remains debt-free with a net cash position.
- Inventory increased significantly in H1 (by Rs 1,200+ crore) primarily due to initial stocking/filling of the new Sanand plant and metal valuation; management expects inventory days to normalize to ~90 days of COGS by March 2027.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations and metal price (copper) volatility could impact near-term dynamics, though management maintains volume/value demand resilience.
- Ongoing small operating losses (Rs 5-8 crore per quarter) in the winding-down EPC segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Natasha Jain (PhillipCapital) on Gross Margin Y-o-Y Decline:** Management explained that Q1 margins were elevated due to inventory valuation impacts, which naturally adjusted in Q2; annualized margins remain stable near 11%. *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Aditya Bhartia (Investec) on Revenue Growth Guidance vs. H1 Performance:** Asked if revenue growth could exceed the guided 25% given net cash and capacity. Management replied they guide cautiously at 25%+ to account for variables, but actual results will reflect in the balance sheet. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Saumil Mehta (Kotak AMC) on Working Capital & Inventory:** Asked if inventory days have peaked. Management clarified that Sanand's initial stocking drove inventory up, but days will normalize downwards by the end of H2. *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Umang Mehta (Asian Markets Securities) on Export Order Book:** Asked about the U.S. share in the export order book. Management stated that the U.S. accounts for almost 50% of the Rs 991 crore export order book. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Rahul Agarwal (IKIGAI Asset) on EPC Segment Losses:** Asked about expected full-year losses for EPC. Management stated losses of Rs 5-7 crore per quarter will persist for 1-2 quarters as the segment is shut down. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
KEI Industries reported robust Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 38.52% YoY and EBITDA of Rs 401 crore, maintaining its full-year guidance of 25%+ top-line growth and 11% EBITDA margins backed by strong domestic/overseas demand and scaling Sanand capacities.
