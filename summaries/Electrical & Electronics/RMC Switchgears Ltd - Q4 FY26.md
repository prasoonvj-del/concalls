# RMC Switchgears Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-15 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q4 FY26 / FY26 (Earnings Call held on June 11, 2026)
**Tone:** Mixed - Consolidated revenue grew healthily, but profitability and cash flows were impacted by project execution delays, input cost pressures, and product development investments, though Q4 showed a strong recovery.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue for FY26: Rs. 401.59 crore (up 26.4% YoY).
- Q3 FY26 net loss: Rs. 7.07 crore.
- Q4 FY26 net profit: Rs. 9.3 crore.
- Current unexecuted order book: Rs. 850 crore plus.
- Tender pipeline: Rs. 1,500 crore plus.
- Trade receivables: Increased from Rs. 148 crore to Rs. 205 crore.
- Estimated addressable market for PulseBox: ~75 lakh urban/critical transformers; potential market size of Rs. 50,000 crore plus.
- Wholesale Price Index (WPI) inflation in March 2026: 3.88%.

### Highlights
- FY26 consolidated revenue reached Rs. 401.59 crore, driven by execution across infrastructure and EPC businesses.
- Profitability was lower than expectations due to investments in "PulseBox" (IoT-enabled distribution monitoring solution), execution delays, and input cost pressures.
- Company returned to profitability in Q4 FY26 with a net profit of Rs. 9.3 crore (recovering from a net loss of Rs. 7.07 crore in Q3) supported by execution catch-up and tighter operating discipline.
- Developed "PulseBox", completing proof of concept and pilot deployments, with discussions progressing toward technical evaluation and tender specification with utilities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide specific revenue guidance for FY27.
- Management reaffirmed its long-term aspiration to build a Rs. 5,000 crore enterprise value/scale by 2030 (10x-13x growth).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Government of India announced transmission infrastructure investments of around Rs. 9 lakh crore till 2032 due to solar power integration bottlenecks.
- Phase 2 of RDSS modernization is focusing on digitalizing electricity infrastructure.
- Government announced a requirement for 102 GW of floating solar power plant possibilities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins were affected by sharp price movements in solar-linked inputs such as aluminum and silver, as well as increases in steel, copper, polymer, plastic, fuel, freight, and logistics.
- Wholesale Price Index (WPI) inflation stood at 3.88% in March 2026.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- B2B segment expansion is planned through human resource additions rather than major capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables increased from Rs. 148 crore to Rs. 205 crore, driven by heavy billing/deliveries concentrated in March due to delayed execution earlier in H2.
- Operating cash flows turned negative due to delayed collections and milestone timing.
- Retention money on projects is generally 10%; receivables overdue beyond 6 months (excluding retention) are estimated at 3% to 5%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Project execution delays caused by extended rainfall (particularly in Maharashtra) shifting execution into late Q4.
- China-linked solar supply chain disruptions, safeguard duty-related cost movements, and higher solar input costs leading to project cancellations, rework, or deferrals.
- Delayed billing realizations and payment processes when dealing with government utilities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gunit Singh (Gunit Singh Associates / Investor) asked about the unexecuted order book, tender pipeline, and negative operating cash flows / rising receivables.** Management stated the unexecuted order book is Rs. 850 crore+ and tender pipeline is Rs. 1,500 crore+, explaining that receivables rose because a large portion of billings happened in March.
- **Kanishk Gupta (Investor) asked about the revenue split between B2B and B2G, and delayed collections.** Management replied that ~90-95% of revenue is B2G (government), but they aim to increase B2B over time; government receivables lack fixed due dates and remain in work-in-progress (WIP) until bill acceptance.
- **Ravi Kumar (Investor) asked about FY27 guidance and PulseBox status.** Management declined to give revenue guidance for FY27, and noted that PulseBox is in advanced discussions for tender specification with at least one utility following a successful proof of concept.
- **Shreyans J (Investor) asked about the 13x growth target by 2030 given the stock price dip.** Management affirmed that the 10x-13x growth aspiration remains intact, stating that larger scale enables bidding for bigger tenders, while noting they have no control over market share prices.

### One-line takeaway
RMC Switchgears reported a 26.4% increase in FY26 revenue to Rs. 401.59 crore, but faced profitability and working capital pressures from execution delays and cost inflation, while pivoting toward technology-led utility solutions like PulseBox.
