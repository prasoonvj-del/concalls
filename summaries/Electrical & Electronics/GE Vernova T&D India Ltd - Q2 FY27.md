# GE Vernova T&D India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (Quarter ended September 30, 2026)  
**Tone:** Positive – Strong order intake, revenue expansion, healthy backlog, and robust EBITDA margins at the higher end of management guidance.

---

### Key numbers
* **Order Intake (Q2 FY27):** Rs 2,440 crore (INR 24.4 billion), up 52% YoY compared to Rs 1,610 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **Order Backlog:** Rs 21,440 crore (INR 214.4 billion) as of September 2026 (>3 years of FY26 revenue).
* **Revenue:** Rs 2,030 crore (INR 20.3 billion), up 32% YoY compared to Rs 1,540 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **Gross Margin:** 44.5%.
* **EBITDA:** Rs 550 crore (INR 5.5 billion); EBITDA margin at 27.1%.
* **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 580 crore (INR 5.8 billion), growing 1.4x YoY from ~Rs 400 crore.
* **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 430 crore (INR 4.3 billion), representing 21.3% of revenue.
* **H1 Cash Generation:** Rs 1,010 crore (INR 10.1 billion).
* **Cash & Cash Equivalents Balance:** Rs 3,250 crore (INR 32.5 billion) as of September 30, 2026.
* **Export Share (Q2):** 21% of orders and 32% of revenues.

---

### Highlights
* Key Q2 wins include 400 kV generator transformer and 420 kV GIS packages for a 1,500 MW pumped storage hydro project in Maharashtra, 800 kV / 420 kV GIS packages in Andhra Pradesh, and TBCB packages in Himachal Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh.
* Company declared L1 position for Power Grid's Barmer HVDC project.
* Shareholder approval for related party export orders worth ~Rs 3,100 crore (granted in 2025) lapsed due to operational delays on the customer side; however, a $151 million US-related party approval remains active through May 2027 (discussions ongoing).

---

### Guidance & outlook
* Management maintained full-year EBITDA margin guidance in the **mid-20s** range.
* Expects domestic order pipeline for TBCB projects to remain strong over the next 4–5 months.
* Confident in executing the Rs 21,440 crore backlog profitably over coming years.
* Expects 2 to 3 new HVDC projects to come up for bidding next year (FY28).

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* Transmission sector is driven by renewable grid integration, long-distance HVDC/AC corridors, grid automation, and smart substations.
* High-voltage equipment lead times (e.g., 765 kV bushings) remain extended globally at 15–18 months.
* Data center demand in India is currently in early stages (1.5 GW installed, expected 8–10 GW by 2030); major high-voltage demand traction expected from Q4/next year.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* EBITDA margins were supported by cost management and execution mix.
* Slight pricing pressure noted in high-voltage equipment, though not significant.
* Employee cost growth remains lower than the revenue growth rate.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Ongoing capacity expansion program of ~Rs 1,000 crore to Rs 1,100 crore (approx. 20% incurred to date), primarily across existing plant locations.
* Factory capacity utilization across key lines is above 75%.
* GE Vernova parent acquired Trench Group; management expects positive supply chain synergies for HVDC, STATCOM, bushings, and coils in India long-term.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Maintains a net zero-debt balance sheet with cash balance of Rs 3,250 crore.
* Management continues evaluating capital allocation options for excess cash reserves to optimize shareholder returns.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Global supply chain constraints and geopolitical disturbances remain key operational risks.
* Customer delay on large international projects caused a Rs 3,100 crore related-party export approval to lapse.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** *Are we in contention for the $900M global orders booked by GE Vernova Global?*  
  **Management:** Yes, orders will flow to India; related-party approval of Rs 1,300 crore has been taken and finalization is ongoing.

* **Parikshit Kandpal (HDFC Securities):** *How is competitive intensity evolving with Chinese/foreign tie-ups?*  
  **Management:** No new HVDC tenders have taken place post-Barmer to observe Chinese tie-up strategies. On regular high-voltage transformers, there is slight price pressure, but it is not significant.

* **Shirom Kapur (Jefferies):** *How long does it take for TBCB projects to turn into equipment orders?*  
  **Management:** TBCB project completion schedules extended from 18–21 months to 24–30 months, giving developers a float; equipment ordering generally occurs 3 to 5 months post developer award.

* **Amitoj Singh (360 ONE Capital):** *Will VSC-based HVDC projects (like South Olpad) have lower gross margins due to sourcing IGBTs from third parties?*  
  **Management:** Sourcing components like IGBTs (from suppliers like Mitsubishi) or CRGO externally is standard practice; complete valve design and assembly are done in-house and sourcing does not negatively impact gross margins.

---

### One-line takeaway
GE Vernova T&D India delivered a strong quarter with 32% YoY revenue growth and 27.1% EBITDA margin, backed by a robust Rs 21,440 crore order backlog and a debt-free Rs 3,250 crore cash balance.
