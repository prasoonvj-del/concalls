# Salzer Electronics Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management acknowledged margin pressure due to raw material volatility and West Asia conflict, but remains confident in long-term demand and structural growth in the electrical sector.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 498 crore (up 13% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 31 crore (vs Rs 42 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 6% (vs 9.2% target previously mentioned).
*   **PAT:** Rs 8 crore (vs Rs 17 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT Margin:** 2%.
*   **Export Contribution:** ~19% of revenue.
*   **Segment Revenue:** Industrial Switchgear (54%), Wire & Cable (40%), Building Products (6%).
*   **Segment Growth:** Industrial Switchgear (+10% YoY), Wire & Cable (+11% YoY), Building Products (+48% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margin contracted due to sharp increases in copper, silver, and plastic costs, coupled with a lag in passing these costs to customers.
*   **Pricing Actions:** Management has implemented price increases in Feb, March, May, and June, with further hikes planned for August (effective September).
*   **Subsidiary Performance:** Kaycee Industries revenue grew 8% YoY; EBITDA at Rs 2 crore, PAT at Rs 1 crore.
*   **Strategic Investments:** Invested an additional Rs 13 lakhs in Salzer EV Infra (total Rs 93 lakhs) and Rs 1.68 crore in Effilume (total Rs 4.2 crore, 47% stake).
*   **Volume Growth:** Reported 7-8% volume growth for the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Management revised guidance to 8%–8.5% for the full year (down from the previously mentioned 10%).
*   **Margin Normalization:** Expects Q2 to remain pressured, with normalization to 9%–9.5% in Q3 and Q4.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Confident in achieving 23%–25% growth for the year.
*   **Saudi Arabia:** Operations expected to commence in September/October; expects ~Rs 25 crore additional revenue from this plant in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Sector Tailwinds:** Strong demand driven by power infrastructure expansion, renewable energy, data centers, and industrial automation.
*   **Global Environment:** Operating environment remains mixed/volatile due to West Asia conflict, trade policy uncertainties, and commodity price volatility.
*   **Smart Meters:** No major updates; currently a "drag on the balance sheet." Management is evaluating the future of this investment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Material Impact:** Commodity price volatility (copper, silver, aluminum) caused a 3.2% elevation in raw material consumption costs.
*   **Pass-through Mechanism:** Wire & Cable business operates on a cost-plus model (stable margins). Switchgear business has a price variation clause but faces a lag in implementation (currently trying to shorten pass-through time to under 2 months).
*   **Hedging Policy:** Company does not hedge commodities, relying on stock holdings as a natural hedge.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Guidance:** FY27 capex is expected to be minor (Rs 15–16 crore) for maintenance and balancing, plus expansion at the Hosur plant and Saudi facility.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Switchgear at 80%–85%; Wire & Cable at 65%.
*   **Future Expansion:** No major capex planned for Wire & Cable or Smart Meters/EV charging this year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Management noted that working capital remains a strain but claimed the number of days has improved compared to the previous quarter.
*   **Finance Costs:** Reported a 300 bps reduction in finance costs as a percentage of revenue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Margin Volatility:** Significant contraction in switchgear margins due to raw material price spikes.
*   **Geopolitical Risks:** West Asia conflict delaying the Saudi plant commissioning and impacting supply chains.
*   **Investor Relations:** Discrepancy between investor presentation (which cited 10% EBITDA target) and management's current guidance (8%–8.5%) was flagged by analysts; management committed to correcting the presentation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Questioned the discrepancy between the 10% EBITDA target in the presentation and the 8% guidance on the call. **Management:** Acknowledged the error and committed to correcting the presentation.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the "drag" of the Smart Meter business. **Management:** Admitted it is a drag and stated they are evaluating the investment, with no decision made yet.
*   **Analyst:** Asked if the company can use a cost-plus model for switchgear. **Management:** Declined, stating it would require opening cost sheets to customers, which they avoid to maintain competitive advantage in normal times.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the Saudi plant potential. **Management:** Stated it will start with wire ducts and terminal connectors; expects ~Rs 25 crore revenue in FY28.

### One-line takeaway
Salzer Electronics is navigating a challenging margin environment due to commodity price volatility and a lag in cost pass-through, but management expects a recovery in profitability by Q3 FY27 while maintaining strong revenue growth momentum.
