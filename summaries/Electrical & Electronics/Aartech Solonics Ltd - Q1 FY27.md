# Aartech Solonics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record numbers and strong order execution, expressing confidence in healthy revenue and margin growth for the remainder of the year.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 728 lakhs (vs Rs 433 lakhs YoY).
*   **Total expenses:** Rs 7.2 Cr (vs 469 lakhs YoY).
*   **Profit before tax (PBT):** Rs 119 lakhs (vs 73 lakhs YoY).
*   **Material consumed:** Rs 3.36 Cr.
*   **Employee benefit expenses:** Rs 119 lakhs.
*   **Order book:** Rs 7 Cr (executed in Q1).

### Highlights
*   Revenue grew approximately 1.5x YoY, driven by a strong order book and execution.
*   Management noted that Q1 is typically a subdued quarter, making the "record numbers" significant.
*   Segment revenue breakdown: Trading (Rs 181 lakhs), Project business (Rs 156 lakhs), and others (Rs 40 lakhs).
*   No exceptional items reported for the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects the next three quarters to be healthy in terms of both revenue and margins.
*   Management expects to reach near-about the Rs 100 Cr inquiry pipeline number by this financial year or the next.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Bus Transfer System (BTS):** Seeing traction in refineries, process industries, and cement industries.
*   **Data Centers:** Identified as a new area of focus, particularly for energy storage applications.
*   **Defense:** Key focus area for specialized energy storage devices (ultracapacitors/energy storage).
*   **Oil & Gas:** Cited as a "sunrise horizon" for the BTS product line.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Material costs increased marginally YoY; management attributed this to disciplined procurement, rate contracts, and savings efforts.
*   Employee expenses rose due to annual increments and performance bonuses.
*   Control and Relay Panels (CRP) currently offer margins of ~10% plus; management aims to move to higher ratings (415 KV to 750 KV) to improve margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Strategy focuses on capitalizing R&D expenses rather than expensing them to build long-term assets.
*   Management maintains a "frugal" approach to R&D overheads.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Balance sheet remains "very healthy" with negligible debt.
*   The company continues to be cash-rich.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Project Gestation:** Projects often have long gestation periods (18-24 months for technical evaluation of new products).
*   **Supply Chain:** Stagnation in supply chain deliveries noted.
*   **External Factors:** Policy changes and ecosystem shifts may impact project timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How does the company plan to scale ultracapacitors?** A: Management stated the technology is proprietary and currently undergoing technical evaluation (18-24 months). They focus on solving specific customer pain points rather than commoditization.
*   **Q: Why are R&D expenses low despite critical product development?** A: Management explained they follow a frugal model, focusing on prototyping and validation, and capitalize R&D expenses as assets rather than expensing them.
*   **Q: What is the roadmap for CRP margins?** A: Management aims to collaborate with relay manufacturers to move into higher KV ratings (beyond 220 KV), which offer better margins than the current 10%+ range.
*   **Q: Is the stock price reflecting performance?** A: Management expressed belief that the market will eventually incorporate the improved financial performance into the stock price.

### One-line takeaway
Aartech Solonics delivered strong Q1 growth driven by order execution and disciplined cost management, with a strategic focus on high-margin segments and long-term R&D in defense and energy storage.
