# Centum Electronics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in achieving full-year growth targets, supported by a robust order book and the successful completion of overseas restructuring.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 205 crore (+11% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 204 crore (+14% YoY).
*   **Standalone Order Book:** ~Rs 1,800 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **Standalone Order Inflow:** ~Rs 360 crore (+70% YoY).
*   **BTS Order Inflow:** Rs 120 crore (+150% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 23 crore (11.28% margin).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 24 crore (11.56% margin).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 106 crore (includes Rs 94 crore one-time gain from deconsolidation).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 14 crore (6.59% margin).

### Highlights
*   **Overseas Restructuring:** Successfully deconsolidated overseas subsidiaries (Centum T&S Group SA) effective June 4, 2026. The French court has initiated liquidation proceedings for remaining entities; management expects no further liabilities.
*   **Strategic Focus:** The company is sharpening focus on its core India ESDM (Electronics System Design & Manufacturing) platform.
*   **Business Segments:**
    *   **BTS (Build-to-Spec):** Strong demand in space, radar, electronic warfare, and air navigation.
    *   **EMS (Electronic Manufacturing Services):** Growth driven by semiconductor equipment (box builds/PCBA).
*   **New Wins:** Received a strategic partner award from a global industrial and energy conglomerate.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains a target of 25%+ revenue growth for the India business for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Aiming for EBITDA margins >13% (from 12.5% in the previous year).
*   **Semiconductor Equipment:** Revenue contribution expected to reach USD 25 million to USD 30 million in the next 1-2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Semiconductor Equipment:** The company is a qualified partner for global OEMs; demand is recurring with long product life cycles.
*   **Space:** The company is moving up the value chain from components to payloads and satellite integration.
*   **Localization:** Seeing increased opportunities as global customers implement localization strategies in India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **EMS Margins:** Typically 10-11% (cost-plus model).
*   **BTS Margins:** Targeting 20%+; export-oriented BTS is expected to have higher margins than domestic tender-based business.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **New Facility:** Design for the new facility at KIADB Aerospace Park is complete; construction is starting soon.
*   **Capex Guidance:** Estimated at Rs 50 crore to Rs 70 crore, with spending likely to commence toward the end of the next fiscal year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Advances:** Total advances stand at ~Rs 210 crore, with approximately two-thirds derived from the BTS segment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution:** Management noted quarterly variations in project execution and revenue phasing, particularly in the BTS business, which can lead to "lumpy" quarterly performance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Semiconductor Competition:** Management stated there are no direct Indian competitors for their EMS semiconductor business; primary competition is based in Malaysia.
*   **Export BTS vs. Domestic:** Management highlighted that export BTS opportunities are preferred as they are not strictly tender-based (L1) and offer better margin profiles.
*   **Direct Armed Forces Engagement:** Management confirmed they are in discussions and answering RFIs for full-system opportunities directly with the armed forces.
*   **Competitive Positioning:** When asked about peers like Astra and Data Patterns, management claimed parity in capability, noting they were L1 in a recent 10-bidder tender for the UHM program.

### One-line takeaway
Centum Electronics has successfully exited its loss-making overseas operations and is now pivoting toward a high-growth, India-centric ESDM strategy, with strong visibility in semiconductor and defense sectors.
