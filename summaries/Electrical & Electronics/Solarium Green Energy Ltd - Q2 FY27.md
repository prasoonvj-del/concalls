# Solarium Green Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Rapid revenue growth exceeding the entire previous full-year FY25 in just 6 months, driven by integrated manufacturing and large EPC execution.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 310.5 crores, up 165% YoY (vs Rs. 116.9 crores in H1 FY26). Exceeded FY25 full-year revenue of Rs. 230.1 crores.
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 25.6 crores, up 57% YoY (vs Rs. 16.4 crores).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 10.5 crores, up 14% YoY (vs Rs. 9.2 crores).
- **EBITDA margin:** 8.3% (vs 14.0% in H1 FY26).
- **Gross margin:** 21.6% (vs 44.4% in H1 FY26).
- **Order book:** Rs. 720.9 crores (EPC: Rs. 461.1 crores, Module supply: Rs. 259.8 crores).
- **Total borrowings:** Rs. 189.1 crores (Long-term: Rs. 73.6 crores, Short-term: Rs. 115.5 crores). Cash and bank balances: Rs. 35.6 crores.
- **Trade receivables:** Rs. 281.9 crores (up from Rs. 152 crores in March 2026).

### Highlights
- All four business segments (Manufacturing, Large EPC, Residential installations, and Solar Kits) operated together for the first half.
- The 1.23 GW automated module facility at Miroli, Ahmedabad was commissioned in March 2026 and enlisted on ALMM-I in July 2026.
- Won two 50 MW AC single-site ground-mount contracts valued at Rs. 188.5 crores and Rs. 186.5 crores, providing ~130 MW DC of captive module off-take.
- *Continues the strategic pivot toward large single-site EPC contracts and away from distributed rooftop tenders, as noted in past company records.*

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to recover to the 9%-10% range in H2 FY27 as plant utilization normalizes.
- Management expects PAT margins to target the 5%-6% range in H2 FY27.
- Management expects 60%-70% of the Rs. 720.9 crore order book to be executed within FY27.
- Management stated that no equity fundraise is currently on the cards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PM Surya Ghar scheme has benefited 50 lakh households with 14.8 GW of rooftop capacity; Union Budget allocated Rs. 22,000 crores for FY27.
- GST on modules, inverters, and balance of systems was cut from 12% to 5% effective September 2025.
- DCR cell supply capacity increased from 20 GW to 42 GW in the last 6 months, and ALMM-II implementation timeline was extended to December 31, 2026.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined to 21.6% (from 44.4%) due to a mix shift toward Large EPC and module sales, which carry lower gross margins than residential installations.
- Segment gross margins: Residential at 35%-37%, C&I/Government at 25%, Manufacturing at 14%-15%.
- Plant carried 6 months of fixed costs against ~2.5 months of manufacturing revenue due to delayed ALMM enlistment (impact of Rs. 4.5-5 crores).
- Finance cost rose to Rs. 8.3 crores (vs Rs. 4.4 crores YoY) due to plant term loans and working capital deployment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 1.23 GW module manufacturing facility operational; plant utilization reached 75%-80% in September 2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at Rs. 189.1 crores against cash and bank balances of Rs. 35.6 crores (Net Debt: Rs. 153.5 crores).
- Trade receivables increased to Rs. 281.9 crores from Rs. 152 crores in March 2026.
- Inventory decreased to Rs. 40.7 crores from Rs. 99.7 crores as modules were dispatched into EPC and external orders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Fixed cost overhead and unabsorbed plant costs during the initial unutilized months prior to ALMM enlistment.
- Elevated trade receivables (Rs. 281.9 crores).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** Who are the clients and payment terms for the two large 50 MW EPC contracts? **A:** Mahagenco (Maharashtra State Government DISCOM); payments are milestone-linked (10-12 milestones) and realized within 15-30 days of billing.
- **Q:** How do you reconcile the high receivables (Rs. 282 crores) with short payment cycles? **A:** Older AR has been largely recovered; the current AR primarily pertains to module dispatches in August-September following ALMM enlistment.
- **Q:** What is the structure of the subcontracting for the two 50 MW contracts? **A:** Subcontracted to other listed EPC players to limit working capital exposure while securing spreads and mandatory in-house module supply.
- **Q:** Why did the residential run-rate drop in H1? **A:** The company shifted strategy away from end-to-end execution (which incurred heavy warehouse/city fixed costs) toward Solar Kits and exclusive city distributors to preserve EBITDA margins without heavy cash burn.
- **Q:** What is the full-year margin and revenue outlook? **A:** Management refrained from hard revenue guidance but expects H2 EBITDA margins to return to 9%-10% and PAT margins to 5%-6% as plant utilization scales.

### One-line takeaway
Solarium Green Energy delivered rapid H1 FY27 revenue growth driven by its integrated manufacturing and large EPC pivot, though margins compressed due to fixed costs during delayed ALMM enlistment and a product mix shift.
