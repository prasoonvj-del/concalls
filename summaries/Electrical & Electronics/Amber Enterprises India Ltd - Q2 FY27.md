# Amber Enterprises India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong consolidated revenue growth (+60% YoY) driven by Electronics, but bottom line suffered from commodity inflation, forex losses, wage hikes, fixed-price contracts in Railways, and PPA inventory adjustments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Cut for Railway Subsystems & Defence revenue growth to 15%-20% (down from 30%-35% guided in Q1 FY27) and Railway EBITDA margins to 8%-10% (down from 15%-16%). Maintained CD industry growth target (20%-22%) and capex guidance (Rs 1,800-2,000 crore).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from broad-based optimism to cautious on Railways due to customer offtake delays and legacy fixed-price contracts; CD demand remains stable.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Restructuring mobile business into dedicated wholly owned subsidiary Amber Digital Technologies; PPA inventory fair-value adjustment impact of Rs 28.8 crore in H1 FY27 (Rs 13.5 crore in Q2) with Rs 32 crore remaining for H2; DRHP filing by subsidiary IL JIN Electronics restricting detailed management disclosures on the segment.
- Last quarter's promises: Operations at the fire-affected IL JIN plant resumed (delivered); Sidwal greenfield facility in Faridabad became operational and is scaling up (delivered).

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,627 crore (+60% YoY vs Rs 1,647 crore in Q2 FY26).
* **Consolidated Operating EBITDA:** Rs 127 crore (+30% YoY vs Rs 98 crore in Q2 FY26), after Rs 13.5 crore PPA inventory adjustment.
* **Consolidated Adjusted Loss After Tax:** Rs 57 crore (before exceptional loss of Rs 9.5 crore). Total PAT impacted by Rs 9.5 crore exceptional loss (Rs 3.2 crore Ascent share purchase + Rs 6.3 crore fire loss receivable difference).
* **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 6,515 crore (+28% YoY vs Rs 5,096 crore in H1 FY26).
* **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 464 crore (+29% YoY vs Rs 361 crore in H1 FY26).
* **H1 FY27 Adjusted PAT:** Rs 69 crore (before exceptional loss of Rs 132.2 crore).
* **Divisional Q2 Revenue & Operating EBITDA:**
  * **Consumer Durables (CD):** Revenue Rs 1,227 crore (+41% YoY); Operating EBITDA Rs 4 crore.
  * **Electronics:** Revenue Rs 1,248 crore (+94% YoY); Operating EBITDA Rs 116 crore (+195% YoY).
  * **Railway Subsystems & Defence:** Revenue Rs 152 crore (+15% YoY); Operating EBITDA Rs 7 crore.

### Highlights
* **Corporate Restructuring:** Housed mobile business (including production for OPPO, OnePlus, and Realme) under a dedicated wholly owned subsidiary, Amber Digital Technologies.
* **Subsidiary Consolidation:** IL JIN acquired the remaining 40% stake in Power-One and 1.5% stake in Ascent Circuits via CCPS swap arrangement, making both 100% subsidiaries of IL JIN.
* **Leadership Transition:** Sanjay Arora appointed President of New Businesses & Group Evolution (spearheading Mobile Division). Satendra Singh appointed CEO & Whole-Time Director of IL JIN Electronics.
* **Fire Operations Update:** Operations at the fire-affected IL JIN facility have resumed and are currently in the scale-up phase.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Railway Division:** Management reduced FY27 revenue growth guidance to 15%-20% (due to delayed project offtakes) and EBITDA margins to 8%-10%. Margins expected to rebound to normalized levels (14%-16%) from Q2 FY28 onwards as legacy fixed-price contracts expire. Target to double top-line in two years remains intact.
* **Consumer Durables:** Management maintained full-year RAC industry growth outlook of 20%-22% and expects to grow in line. Margin impact guided at 50-100 bps if H1 commodity levels persist.
* **PPA Inventory Adjustment:** Remaining non-operational raw material consumption impact of ~Rs 32 crore will be fully absorbed over Q3 and Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
* **RAC / CD Industry:** H1 FY27 RAC industry growth was 18%. Growth was supported by commercial AC additions, star-rating changes, and price increases.
* **PCB Shortages:** Copper Clad Laminate (CCL) supply shortages are currently being addressed via industry capacity expansions; supply chain normalization expected over the next 2-3 quarters.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Bill of Materials (BOM) Exposure:** Direct commodity exposure (copper, aluminum, steel, resin) accounts for 40% of RAC BOM, expanding to 52% when including components like compressors.
* **Pass-Through Mechanism:** Price changes passed on to customers on a 1-quarter lag basis. Took an 8% YoY price hike in RAC in Q2 FY27 (offsetting a 12% gross commodity hike with 4% supplier cost improvements).
* **CD Margin Drivers:** CD EBITDA was compressed by minimum wage hikes in Haryana (Rs 22-25 crore H1 impact for Amber standalone; Rs 20-30 crore for CD division), non-operational costs (Rs 32-35 crore in H1), and initial operating costs of Sri City greenfield plant.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Full-Year Capex:** Guided at Rs 1,800-2,000 crore for FY27; expected to remain in a similar range in FY28.
* **Facility Expansion Timelines:** Ascent Hosur expansion expected to start commercial production in H1 CY27; Korea Circuit facility expected to commence in CY28.
* **Capacity Utilization:** Sri City greenfield facility currently operating at 40% utilization, expected to normalize by Q4 FY27.
* **Subsidies/Incentives:** Cash inflows from government incentives are expected to begin in FY29 post commercial production.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **Net Debt:** Stood at Rs 2,582 crore as of Q2 FY27. Gross debt at Rs 4,100-4,200 crore. Management noted net debt peaks in Q2/Q3 due to business seasonality and declines in Q4/Q1.
* **Net Interest Cost:** Stood at Rs 63-64 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 56 crore in Q1 FY27), expected to stay around similar levels in Q3 and Q4.
* **Working Capital:** Net working capital days stood at 53 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **Fixed-Price Contracts:** Delayed execution of legacy fixed-price contracts in the Railway division without price variation clauses is hurting near-term margins.
* **Commodity & Currency Volatility:** Spikes in copper, aluminum, steel, resin, or USD/INR exchange rates could suppress percentage margins despite quarterly lag pass-through clauses.
* **IL JIN Fire Loss:** Additional exceptional loss of Rs 6.3 crore recognized due to discrepancy between fire loss amount and expected insurance receivable submitted to date.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Achal Lohade:** What drove the 41% YoY growth in Consumer Durables?
  * **Management:** Driven by higher contribution from commercial ACs (added new products and customers), price increases due to commodity and star-rating changes, and volume growth.
* **Dhruv Jain:** How will Railway/Sidwal margins trend over FY28-FY29 and does doubling top-line guidance hold?
  * **Management:** Doubling top-line target holds based on order book. FY27 EBITDA margin will be 8%-10% due to legacy contracts, gradually recovering to normalized 14%-16% levels by FY29 with price variation clauses in new contracts.
* **Keshav Lahoti:** Why is the PPA inventory fair-value adjustment taking a full year to absorb?
  * **Management:** Inventory consists of finished and semi-finished goods from acquired entities (Unitronics, Shogini, Power-One). Model mix and sales liquidation speed spread the absorption over four quarters.
* **Rabindra Nath Nayak:** Will Amber retain control of IL JIN post-IPO dilution?
  * **Management:** DRHP reflects maximum potential dilution (<40%). Management expects shareholding to stay above 51% and board control will remain with Amber.
* **Naveen Kumar Dubey:** Can you quantify the H1 cost headwinds impacting CD standalone/Amber?
  * **Management:** H1 included Rs 32-35 crore in non-operating expenses (forex, ESOPs), Rs 20-30 crore in minimum wage revisions, and Rs 15-20 crore in initial Sri City plant costs.

### One-line takeaway
Amber is aggressively scaling its Electronics and Mobile verticals while enduring temporary margin pressure from fixed-price railway contracts and commodity pass-through lags.
