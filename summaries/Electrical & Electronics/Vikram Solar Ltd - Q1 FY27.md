# Vikram Solar Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - While volume and revenue grew YoY, margins contracted due to cost pressures and policy uncertainties.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,563 crore (up 38% YoY, up 8% QoQ)
- **Volume Dispatched:** 1,006 MW (up 32% YoY)
- **Production Volume:** 1,085 MW
- **EBITDA:** Rs 126 crore
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.06%
- **PAT:** Rs 19.78 crore
- **Realisation:** Rs 15.02 per watt peak (up 8% QoQ)
- **DCR Modules Sold:** 76 MW
- **Order Book:** 7.9 GW (Large accounts non-DCR order book is 7.1 GW)
- **Capex Deployed (Q1 FY27):** Approx. Rs 500 crore (80% module facility, balance cell plant)
- **Total Capex Plan (FY27):** Approx. Rs 5,000 crore
- **Imported Cell Spot Price:** 4 cents landed + 27.5% BCD

### Highlights
- Highest ever quarterly volume of 1,006 MW and 38% YoY revenue growth.
- First module rolled out from the Gangaikondan facility on June 29, 2026.
- Transitioned module portfolio from M10R to G12R across the platform; manpower deployment down by ~40% with improved cycle times on new automated lines.
- Received Ecovadis Platinum rating for the second consecutive year (top 1% globally).
- Distribution network expanded to 119+ distributors and 757+ dealers across 24 states and 500 districts; monthly distribution run rate doubled.
- VSL PowerHive (BESS business) ordered equipment for its 7.5 GWh Chennai assembly plant (delivery planned November 2026, commercial operations target March 2027) and executed its first 20 MWh utility-scale solution order; finalised partner and shortlisted land for the 7.5 GWh LFP cell plant targeting Q4 FY29 COD.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Ranjan Jindal, Sameer Nagpal) deferred providing a formal full-year FY27 outlook update until the H1 results, citing ongoing policy and pricing stabilization.
- Expected DCR volume growth of 2x to 2.5x every quarter.
- Cell plant utilization targeted at 40% to 50% in its first year (accounting for a 6-month ramp-up).

### Demand & industry commentary
- ALMM 2 enforcement remained unclear before being implemented and then deferred to December 2026 on July 18, 2026, creating temporary uncertainty and holding back C&I buying decisions.
- India added ~12 GW of solar in Q1 FY27, driven by rooftop (PM Surya Ghar) and storage.
- C&I demand estimated at 15 GW a year; total country consumption estimated at 45-50 GW AC.
- Grandfathered non-DCR projects estimated around 80 GW, supplemented by the ALMM 2 extension.
- Emerging demand drivers include data center policies (UP, Gujarat, Rajasthan, AP) targeting 25-26 GW over five years, plus green hydrogen.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin compressed to 8.06% (delivering Rs 1.25 per watt peak) due to COGS inflation, which management stated was not fully passable.
- Gulf conflict caused war-related inflation in base metals (aluminum and copper up sharply, impacting frames and ribbons comprising ~35% of balance raw materials) and crude oil (impacting EVA encapsulant, comprising ~12% of balance raw materials).
- Chinese cell prices spiked in the previous quarter, hitting Q1 inventory.
- Non-DCR MSAs have cell pass-through clauses, but no pass-through for Balance of Raw Materials (BORM).
- Other expenses per watt fell 6% QoQ; finance cost per watt fell over 11% QoQ.
- Mid-market sales force doubled, carrying a ~Rs 0.50/watt higher peak price realization. Distribution segment provides Rs 1 to Rs 1.5 better price realization over key accounts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Integrated Gangaikondan site structured in 3 stages: 6 GW module facility (fully funded), 9 GW cell plant (on track for Q4 FY27 cell out), and 9 GW wafer/ingot facility (board approved increase from 6 GW to 9 GW; ground-breaking expected next month, target COD FY29).
- Total estimated cost for 9 GW wafer-ingot project: Rs 5,600 crore.
- Remaining capex deployment of roughly Rs 4,700 crore planned for the rest of the year. Funding to utilize debt at a target debt-equity of 70:30 (75:25 marginal).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains effectively debt-free with no long-term debt and negligible net debt-to-equity.
- Sanctioned capex facilities not yet drawn; major debt disbursements to begin at the end of the current month.
- Working capital utilization decreased over the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Policy uncertainty and volatility regarding ALMM 2 timelines affecting customer purchasing behavior.
- High competitive intensity and oversupply in the module market preventing full cost pass-through of raw material inflation.
- Rising geopolitical tensions in the Gulf impacting raw material and freight costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Purswani (Prabhudas Lilladher) asked why cost escalations weren't passed on given MSA clauses.** Management (Ranjan Jindal) explained that pass-throughs apply only to cells, not BORM (aluminum, copper, EVA), and oversupply prevented full pricing compensation.
2. **Pravin Sahay asked about the margin delta between DCR and non-DCR products.** Management stated DCR delivers higher margins than non-DCR, but refrained from giving specific spread numbers until volumes scale up beyond Q1's 75 MW.
3. **Bala Murali Krishna questioned why Vikram Solar's margins dropped ~50% while peers dropped less.** Management clarified that the drop is an outcome of not having captive cell manufacturing yet, which will correct once the cell plant is commissioned.
4. **Karan Gupta asked about imported Chinese cell landed costs and domestic cell manufacturing cost.** Management (Rinal Shah / Sameer Nagpal) replied that imported Chinese cell spot prices are 4 cents landed plus 27.5% BCD.
5. **Sahil Sheth asked about debt funding ratios for the capex.** Management (Ranjan Jindal) stated debt-equity for the project phase will target 70:30.

### One-line takeaway
Despite record quarterly volumes of 1,006 MW, Vikram Solar's Q1 FY27 profitability was severely compressed by raw material inflation and policy-driven demand pauses, prompting a strategic pivot toward higher-margin distribution channels and aggressive backward integration.
