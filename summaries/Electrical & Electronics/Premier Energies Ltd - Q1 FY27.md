# Premier Energies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reported record revenue and profit growth, strong order inflows, and robust solar sector demand.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** INR2,508 crore (up 34% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR759 crore (up 27% YoY)
- **PAT:** INR472 crore (up 53% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 30.3%
- **PAT Margin:** 18.8%
- **Transcon (51% stake):** Revenue of INR110 crore, PAT of INR18 crore, EBITDA margin of 27%, PAT margin of 17%
- **Order Inflow:** INR3,011 crore
- **Total Order Book:** INR15,000 crore (including transformers)
- **Cell Capacity Utilisation:** 92% (Telangana operational plants)
- **Total Capex (Q1):** INR1,500 crore (INR1,250 crore in solar, INR250 crore in Transcon)

### Highlights
- The 5.6 GW fully automated solar module plant at Seetharampur became operational.
- The 7 GW TOPCon cell line at Naidupeta is in advanced stages of commissioning, with trial runs starting later in the month.
- Transformer capacity is targeted to grow from 4 GVA to 16.25 GVA by FY28.
- Management clarified that enabling resolutions are routine for hyper-growth flexibility, and there are no concrete plans to raise primary capital in the near future.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided that EBITDA margins will be maintained at 29% to 30% (plus-minus 100-150 bps), supported by operating leverage, backward integration, and scale.
- Transformer business revenue is expected to treble over the next three years.
- Management stated concrete plans to set up cell manufacturing in the US, expecting output in 24 to 30 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's Q1 solar capacity addition reached nearly 12 GW, driven by momentum in PM Surya Ghar Yojana and KUSUM schemes.
- Rooftop installations are expected to average 1.2 to 1.3 GW over the next 9 months, while KUSUM installations are expected between 6 to 8+ GW AC.
- Government extension of ALMM deadlines up to December triggered an influx of non-DCR module orders. DCR modules are completely sold out for FY28.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Non-DCR module business is currently not profitable due to a vast oversupply (~250 GW capacity vs ~60 GW demand in India).
- DCR module realisations remained stable (Surya Ghar sold at 24-24.5 cents, KUSUM at ~22 cents). Silver prices dropped, and foreign exchange impact was neutral due to dollar-based pricing.
- Employee and other expenses increased due to advance hiring and operational costs post-capitalization for Seetharampur and Naidupeta, though per-unit manpower costs on new scaled lines are estimated to be ~40% lower.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total Q1 capex stood at INR1,500 crore (INR1,250 crore for solar expansion at Seetharampur and Naidupeta, INR250 crore for Transcon).
- BESS Phase 1 (6 GW, out of 12 GW total plan) land and construction have started; a technology partner will be finalized in 2 to 3 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management noted that the finance expense increase from new lines will be relatively small since capacity expansion was largely funded by equity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Grid congestion and duck curve pressures arising from rapid solar capacity addition, though mitigated by rapid BESS and pump storage additions.
- Oversupply and low profitability in the non-DCR module market.
- Talent attraction and retention challenges in the cell industry, managed via continuous skill training, Great Place to Work recognition, and ESOPs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Apoorva Bahadur (IIFL Capital) on cell vs. module mix:** Management stated that cell sales will gradually decline as in-house DCR module consumption scales up to match cell capacity.
2. **Kunal Shah (ICICI Securities) on non-DCR influx and margins:** Management clarified that non-DCR is low-margin/unprofitable and run only to utilise capacity, but robust DCR order books will sustain blended margins.
3. **Nitin Arora (Nuvama) on transformer revenue and margins:** Management expects transformer revenue to treble over three years; EBITDA margins historically run around 15% and PAT at 8%-10%, with expectations to land slightly ahead.
4. **Anupam Goswami on Q3/Q4 DCR demand:** Management noted no slowness in DCR demand, anticipating heavy activity heading into March deadlines for KUSUM and Surya Ghar.
5. **Raman KV (Sequent Investments) on industry consolidation:** Management affirmed that industry consolidation is inevitable, favouring scaled, vertically integrated players over smaller unintegrated manufacturers.

### One-line takeaway
Premier Energies posted record Q1 FY27 financial performance with 34% revenue growth and steady ~30% EBITDA margins, underpinned by strong domestic solar demand and large-scale capacity expansions.
