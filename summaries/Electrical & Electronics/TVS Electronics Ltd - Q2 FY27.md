# TVS Electronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue rebounded sequentially and customer support services turned profitable, but margins remained under pressure due to elevated material costs and net loss continued.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 116 crore (sequential growth of 10%)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 3 crore
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 2.93% (expansion of 519 bps sequentially)
- **Q2 FY27 Net Loss:** Rs 1 crore
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 222 crores (broadly stable YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 1 crore
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** ~1%
- **H1 FY27 Net Loss:** Rs 8 crores
- **Products and Solutions Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 79 crore (growth of 10% QoQ); H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 152 crores (degrowth of 4% YoY)
- **Customer Support Services Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 37 crore (growth of 10% QoQ); H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 70 crore (growth of 5% YoY)
- **EMS Assembly Line Utilization:** 40% to 45% currently (improved from 30% to 35%)
- **Solar O&M Portfolio:** Approximately 4 gigawatts

### Highlights
- Entity revenue rebounded sequentially driven by improved performance across both business verticals.
- Customer Support Services (CSS) turned profitable this quarter, supported by volume increases across all verticals, discontinuation of non-performing customers, and Total Cost Management (TCM) initiatives.
- EMS business completed new product development (NPD) and testing for trials, and secured firm orders from new clients.
- Management noted that our record shows Q2 FY27 revenue at Rs 116 crore (-9% YoY), continuing a weak results pattern noted on November 14, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a normalized EBITDA margin of 5% to 8% on a consolidated basis under a normalized non-inflationary environment.
- Management expects material prices to stabilize, though some volatility may persist subject to market conditions and geopolitical disturbances.
- Full-year revenue reflection from solar O&M installations is expected in the next financial year.
- Potential second SMT assembly line expansion may happen in the next 2 to 4 or 3 quarters if utilization exceeds 70%-80%.
- Guidance status: none.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Solar adoption is strong across India, overcoming an initial grid-related slowdown in the first two quarters.
- Demand in Products and Solutions faced delays in RFQ closures by corporate customers due to price hikes caused by higher material costs and rupee depreciation.
- AIDC product offerings received good response at expos, and sales plans are underway to convert interest into firm orders.
- Warehousing and logistics are growing sectors presenting market opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins remain under pressure due to elevated material costs, delayed RFQ closures, and ongoing investments in new business initiatives and capability building.
- Total Cost Management (TCM) continues via digitization, AI adoption, discretionary spend controls, and field support efficiency improvements.
- Procurement costs negotiated and improved; rupee and material prices showing stabilization signs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- EMS assembly line utilization currently at 40% to 45%.
- Second SMT line planned when utilization crosses 70%-80% (expected in 2 to 4 quarters).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or working capital numbers discussed beyond ongoing cost management and operating leverage.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Material cost volatility due to war and forex fluctuations.
- Delays by corporate customers in finalizing RFQs.
- Negligible market share currently in the AIDC segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tushar Khurana (Peace Wealth) asked about CSS profitability drivers and EMS scaling:** Management stated CSS improved due to volume growth across verticals, dropping non-performing customers, and TCM initiatives; EMS completed prototyping/testing for new clients and secured firm orders with utilization at 40%-45%.
- **Tushar Khurana asked about AIDC market share and order delays:** Management confirmed AIDC market share is negligible, and past order delays were driven by price increases, though inquiry conversion is improving.
- **Abhijit Periwal (APSEC) asked about the solar O&M revenue reflection timeline:** Management clarified that current onboarding will translate to full-year revenue potential (ARR) starting next financial year.
- **Abhijit Periwal asked about the trigger for a second SMT line:** Management stated it will be triggered when capacity utilization exceeds 70%-80%, potentially in 2 to 3 quarters.
- **Tushar Khurana asked about normalized consolidated EBITDA margins:** Management guided to an expected 5% to 8% EBITDA margin under normalized conditions.

### One-line takeaway
TVS Electronics posted a sequential revenue and EBITDA recovery in Q2 FY27 alongside a CSS segment turnaround, though profitability remains constrained by material cost pressures and growth investments.
