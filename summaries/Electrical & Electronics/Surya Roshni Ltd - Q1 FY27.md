# Surya Roshni Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited strong revenue and PAT growth, highest-ever Q1 lighting and steel volumes, and reiterated full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,046 crore, up 28% YoY
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 120 crore, up 46% YoY
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 60 crore, up 77% YoY
- **Lighting & Consumer Durables Revenue:** Rs 456 crore, up 15% YoY
- **Lighting & Consumer Durables EBITDA:** Rs 36 crore, up 17% YoY (margin 7.9%)
- **Lighting & Consumer Durables PBT:** Rs 24 crore, up 12% YoY
- **Steel Pipes & Strips Revenue:** Rs 1,590 crore, up 32% YoY
- **Steel Pipes & Strips Volume:** 2.28 lakh tonnes, up 21% YoY
- **Steel Pipes & Strips EBITDA:** Rs 84 crore, up 63% YoY
- **Steel Pipes & Strips PBT:** Rs 57 crore, up 134% YoY
- **Steel EBITDA per metric tonne:** Rs 4,006 vs Rs 2,922 YoY
- **Net Working Capital Cycle:** 72 days
- **ROCE:** 12.69%
- **ROE:** 8.95%
- **Net Cash Surplus:** Rs 154 crore (zero-debt company as of June 30, 2026)
- **Professional Lighting Order Book:** Approx Rs 150 crore
- **Steel Order Book:** Rs 800 crore (including 78,000 tonnes of export API orders for the US)
- **Wire & Cable Enrollments:** 36,000 electricians via DBT program

### Highlights
- Delivered strongest-ever first quarter for Lighting & Consumer Durables, led by LED bulbs, battens, downlighters, appliances, and professional lighting.
- Steel pipes achieved highest-ever Q1 sales volume, driven by section pipes, ERW API pipes (supported by US market entry), spiral, cold rolling, and GP pipes.
- Passed on input cost increases of ~7% in lighting with minimal profitability impact.
- Wire business achieved close to its FY26 sales volume within Q1 FY27; FY26 revenue target remains Rs 250 crore, on track with 3-year guidance of Rs 500 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains fully on track to deliver FY27 guidance of 22% to 23% value growth and ~25% volume growth in lighting.
- Full-year FY27 steel EBITDA per tonne guidance maintained at Rs 4,600 to Rs 4,700 (anticipating Rs 4,400-Rs 4,500 in Q2 and higher in H2).
- Full-year FY27 company-wide revenue targeted at Rs 9,400 crore to Rs 9,500 crore and total EBITDA at Rs 670 crore to Rs 680 crore (with lighting EBITDA expected around Rs 200 crore on Rs 2,200 crore turnover).
- Lighting value growth targeted at 19% to 20%; steel volume growth at ~22% and value growth at 30%+.
- Export contribution in steel targeted to increase from 20% to ~25% (with US share at 8% to 10%).
- Long-term capacity target: ~16 million (16 lakh) tonnes in FY27 and ~2 million tonnes by FY28-FY29.
- Long-term 5-year CAGR target of 15% to 18%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Galvanized pipes remained soft due to continued delays in government fund releases.
- Middle East orders were lower by 8,000 to 10,000 tonnes due to extended regional situations.
- Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia, and other new markets are in the pipeline for steel exports.
- API pipe tenders are picking up in India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel EBITDA per tonne of Rs 4,006 impacted by about Rs 700 per tonne due to higher ocean freight on older order bookings (impact of nearly Rs 3,800/tonne on exports) and an increase in coating material, gas, and ORM costs (~Rs 200/tonne impact).
- Fresh export orders are being booked at increased freight rates, expected to positively impact Q2 and H2.
- Initiatives in automation, energy efficiency, and replacing old plants target a per-tonne cost reduction of about Rs 1,100 in steel.
- Spiral segment API EBITDA per tonne was Rs 3,420 in Q1 due to coating material impact, expected to improve to Rs 5,000 for the full year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Three new DFT mills are being commissioned across Gujarat, Malanpur, and Bahadurgarh plants between August and December 2026, taking total capacity to 16 lakh tonnes by the end of the year.
- Adding ~3 lakh tonnes of capacity in South India (Hindupur) via a ~Rs 60 crore investment (first mill commissioning in January 2027); total capex for current capacity addition is ~Rs 100 crore funded via internal accruals.
- Total capacity additions planned at 2 lakh to 3 lakh tonnes every year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is zero-debt with a net cash surplus of Rs 154 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- Working capital increased slightly due to steel price ups and downs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and ocean freight rate volatility.
- Delays in government fund releases impacting galvanized pipe demand.
- Middle East volume disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Viraj Mehta (Enigma Investment Partners) asked how management will achieve the Rs 4,700-4,800 full-year EBITDA per tonne guidance given Q1 was at Rs 4,000.** Management explained that Q1 was impacted by high ocean freight on old bookings and lower volume due to vessel/Middle East issues; fresh orders with higher freight pricing and incoming lower-priced imports will bridge the gap in H2.
- **Kiran asked about the composition of US exports.** Management clarified that US exports are primarily ERW API pipes (not spiral), a new market contributing an expected 8% to 10% of volume.
- **Love Gupta asked about buyback and demerger updates.** Management stated the team is working on a buyback following the removal of double taxation, and that the board will evaluate both buyback and demerger options based on macroeconomic stability.
- **Pranav asked why macro/micro conditions delay the demerger.** Management agreed there is no theoretical/regulatory stop and that both divisions are strong standalone, promising to convey shareholder sentiment to the board.
- **Raj Mehta questioned lagging volume/margin performance compared to peers.** Management defended the company's 21% volume growth and 32% value growth in steel, noting distinct product mixes and lower base effects from previous SAP issues.
- **Saket Kapoor asked about lighting manufacturing mix.** Management stated that 80% of lighting products are manufactured in-house and 20% are outsourced under strict quality and exclusive production controls.

### One-line takeaway
Surya Roshni reported a robust Q1 FY27 with 28% revenue and 77% PAT growth, maintaining its full-year guidance backed by capacity expansions, a shift toward value-added products, and a net cash surplus balance sheet.
