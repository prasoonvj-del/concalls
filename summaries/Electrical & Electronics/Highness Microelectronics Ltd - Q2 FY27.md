# Highness Microelectronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27 (ended September 30, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and profitability growth with robust demand and capability expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 12.01 crore (up 63.4% YoY from INR 7.35 crore)
- **Total Income:** INR 12.45 crore (vs INR 7.37 crore YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA:** INR 3.85 crore (vs INR 1.77 crore YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 32.1% (vs 24.1% YoY)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** INR 3.29 crore (up 173.9% YoY from INR 1.20 crore)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 2.47 crore (up 167% YoY from INR 0.92 crore)
- **PAT Margin:** 20.6% (vs 12.5% YoY)
- **Total Borrowings:** INR 7.08 crore (Long-term: INR 2.70 crore, Short-term: INR 4.38 crore)
- **Total Assets:** INR 43.62 crore
- **Inventories:** INR 8.20 crore
- **Trade Receivables:** INR 13.73 crore
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** INR 1.68 crore
- **Net Operating Cash Flow:** Negative INR 14.58 crore
- **Order Book Visibility:** ~INR 30 crore to be executed in the next 12 months

### Highlights
- Delivered strong YoY growth in H1 FY27 driven by specialized display applications.
- Management noted that H1 is historically slower, with H2 expected to be much stronger.
- Progressing on "Goa Fast Track" project—setting up a manufacturing line on leased space to meet urgent customer PLI requirements ahead of the main Goa facility.
- Medical-grade surgical monitors hold 30-40% margins; company is listed as the sole manufacturer of medical-grade surgical monitors in India with CDSCO MDR 14 and ISO 13485 certifications.
- Received strong response at US Rail event in Chicago following MOU with Axiom Manufacturing in Texas.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to top up projections and sees potential to grow 2x-3x with the new energy sector MOU opportunity.
- Main Goa facility commissioning is targeted within 9 to 12 months.
- Confident of maintaining overall operating margins due to in-house design and end-to-end capabilities.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Focus remains strictly on specialized displays (Defense & Aerospace, Railways & Transportation, Medical & Healthcare, Industrial Automation) and excludes commercial items (TVs, cell phones, laptops).
- Energy sector customers facing PLI local content obligations are driving urgent demand for localized display sourcing.
- Engaged with Delhi Metro (supplying ~1 train every 40-45 days), Chennai Metro Phase 2, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kochi, Pune, Kolkata, and Patna metros; targeting all 30 cities building metros in India.
- Strong export interest from Australia (Oceania) via Sydney partner and participation in AusRAIL.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) increased by ~3% to INR 2.70 crore against a 63.4% revenue increase.
- Employee benefit expenses rose to INR 2.60 crore (21.7% of revenue vs 18.3% YoY) due to team building.
- Other expenses stood at INR 2.85 crore.
- Finance costs were INR 0.48 crore and depreciation was INR 0.52 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Goa Fast Track project capex is INR 12 crore (INR 9 crore capex + INR 3 crore working capital).
- Main Goa facility land has government approvals; excavation begins next week.
- Installed glass cutting line at Rabale facility to aid "Make in India" localization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Negative operating cash flow of INR 14.58 crore caused by supplier advance payments, reduction in trade payables (INR 3.47 crore decrease), and increases in trade receivables, short-term loans/advances, and other current assets.
- Old receivables from UAE and Australia reduced by 50%; remaining higher receivable balance driven by September sales realized in October.
- Standard MSME collection cycle is maintained within 45 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange exposure, imported input dependencies, and working capital requirements for rapid growth.
- Weather cycles (heavy rains in Goa) previously delayed site work.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Atharva (Individual Investor) asked about the objective of the recent fundraise:** Management explained it funds the "Goa Fast Track" leased line to meet urgent local sourcing/PLI demands from energy sector customers.
- **Siddarth on receivables and payables movement:** Management clarified that old receivables from UAE/Australia are down 50%, and higher total receivables are due to late September billing realized in October; negative operating cash flow resulted from creditor advances and lower payables.
- **Ashish Soni on sustainability of the 2x-3x energy sector opportunity:** Management confirmed an MOU for at least 3 years, making it an ongoing, sustainable revenue stream.
- **Venkatesh on capex and timeline for the new Goa facility:** Management stated Goa Fast Track capex is INR 12 crore, and the main facility is targeted to go live in 9 to 12 months.
- **Rajat on margin sustainability:** Management expressed high confidence in maintaining margins due to in-house design controls and end-to-end solution offerings.

### One-line takeaway
Highness Microelectronics delivered strong H1 FY27 performance with 63.4% revenue growth and 32.1% EBITDA margins, and is fast-tracking capacity expansion to capture surging domestic PLI and export demand.
