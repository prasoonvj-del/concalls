# Orient Electric Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Electrical & Electronics*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Broad-based revenue growth of 25.2% YoY, supported by market share gains and premiumization, despite headwinds from persistent commodity inflation.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 879 crore (up 25.2% YoY)
- H1 Revenue: INR 1,829 crore (up 24.3% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 57 crore (up 49.7% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 6.5% (expanded 106 bps YoY)
- H1 EBITDA Margin: 6.7% (expanded 104 bps YoY)
- PAT: INR 27 crore (more than doubled YoY); H1 PAT: INR 59 crore
- Gross Margin: 29% for Q2; H1 Gross Margin: 29.4%
- Working Capital: 34 days
- Lighting & Switchgear Revenue: INR 329 crore (up 25.7% YoY)
- ECD Revenue: INR 550 crore (up 24.9% YoY)
- Project Sanchay Savings: INR 13 crore in Q2; INR 23 crore for H1

### Highlights
- Delivered broad-based growth across all categories; fans outperformed the industry, resulting in market share gains.
- Lighting segment: Consumer lighting saw double-digit growth; high-value looms remained consistent at 66%; professional lighting delivered high-single-digit growth. Wires more than doubled YoY; switchgear and switches delivered high-double-digit growth.
- ECD segment: BLDC fans grew ~26% YoY (representing ~30% of ceiling fan mix, compared to industry average of 17%-18%). Premium products accounted for ~40% of the fan mix.
- New products (NPD) contributed >30% of fans revenue during the quarter, with a success rate of >90%.
- Appliances grew in double digits, led by water heaters and garment care.
- Digital channels: E-commerce grew by high double-digits; quick commerce contributed ~15% of digital revenue and grew ~100% YoY.
- Direct-to-Market (DTM) model expanded across Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, and Jharkhand; took over service for Punjab and Nagpur.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aspires to trend towards a double-digit EBITDA margin over the long term, supported by mix improvement, productivity, and sustained cost optimization.
- Management expects H2 EBITDA percentages to be better than Q2, with margins trending closer to double-digits over the next 4 to 6 quarters.
- Management guided that full-year CAGR will likely be >15%.
- Working capital days expected to return to ~20 days over the next 4 to 6 quarters via channel financing and other measures.
- Long-term gross margin target stays at ~32%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Persistent commodity inflation (copper, aluminum, crude-linked movements) and geopolitical uncertainties continue to affect supply chain visibility.
- Festive season demand and H2 outlook are viewed with confidence due to brand preference, market share gains, and refreshed product portfolios.
- Unorganized segment is growing at the bottom of the pyramid due to cumulative price hikes, but Orient's premiumization strategy has helped soften the impact.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins impacted by input cost pressures; prices increased 3% to 6% across categories in Q2 and another ~3% in October in some categories.
- Project Sanchay generated savings of ~INR 13 crore in Q2 (INR 23 crore for H1), with higher accruals expected in H2.
- R&D spend stands at ~0.8% to 0.9% of turnover.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Hyderabad facility operating at ~40% capacity utilization on a single shift (~75% PPW, 20%-25% ceiling fans). Production being optimized for South India to save logistics/warehousing costs. Expected to add ~20-30 bps to margins at steady-state utilization.
- Added 3,000 to 4,000 more outlets on a base of ~1 lakh+ direct/indirect distribution for fans.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Closed the quarter with a working capital of 34 days (up from earlier periods due to DTM transition). 

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Persistent inflation in key raw materials (steel, copper, aluminum, plastics) outpaces price hikes, impacting gross margins.
- Geopolitical uncertainties affecting supply chain visibility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities):** Asked about trade up-stocking ahead of October price hikes and BLDC trajectory. Management stated price hikes did not cause abnormal primary up-stocking, and BLDC continues strong momentum at 30% of ceiling fan mix.
- **Madhur Rathi (Investments):** Questioned the timeline for double-digit EBITDA margins and increasing working capital days. Management stated H2 will see better margins, targeting double-digits in 4-6 quarters, and working capital will normalize to ~20 days.
- **Naushad Chaudhary (Aditya Birla Sun Life):** Inquired about full-year revenue growth guidance and gross margin recovery. Management raised the CAGR expectation to >15% and reiterated a long-term gross margin target of 32%.
- **Tanay Shah (DAM Capital):** Asked about cumulative price hikes and DTM margin impact. Management detailed category-wise price hikes (80%-100% inflation recovery across categories) and confirmed DTM is margin-accretive with higher NPD contribution.
- **Lakshminarayanan (Investments):** Asked about B2B mix, distribution expansion, and commodity BOM exposure. Management noted B2B is double-digit (25% in lighting) and steel/plastic/copper inflation is secular across the industry.
- **Nirransh Jain:** Questioned the 250 bps gross margin drop despite price hikes and premium mix. Management clarified that severe and rapid inflation outpaced price hikes, though pricing indexation vs. peers has improved.

### One-line takeaway
Orient Electric reported a strong 25.2% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by premiumization, new product launches, and market share gains, even as persistent commodity inflation kept gross margins under pressure.
