# Shakti Pumps India Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong execution in solar pump installations, export growth, and momentum in business diversification despite raw material and tender headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Solar pump installations in Q2: 23,865 units (~7% YoY growth)
- Q2 FY27 Solar EPC revenue: Rs 543 crore (23,865 units)
- Q2 FY27 Export sales: Rs 95 crore
- Q2 FY27 Domestic, industrial, and other sales: Rs approximately 40 crore
- H1 FY27 Solar EPC revenue: Rs 1,230 crore
- H1 FY27 Export sales: Rs 177 crore (including Uganda project sales)
- Capacity utilization for pumps and motors: ~68% in H1 FY27
- Rooftop business revenue growth: 58% YoY in Q2
- Total planned capex: Rs 1,700 crore (Rs 200 crore spent so far on 2.2 GW solar cell plant)
- Outstanding receivables as of board meeting date: Rs 1,750 crore
- Debt: Rs 830 crore at Q2 end (due to delayed collections received in October); currently reduced to below Rs 500 crore
- Average interest cost: ~7%

### Highlights
- Installed 23,865 solar pumps in Q2 FY27, achieving ~7% YoY growth.
- Recorded ~70% growth across rooftop, solar domestic, and industrial businesses.
- Commenced trial production at the 500 MW solar panel facility; commercial production expected within 1 to 1.5 months.
- First panel from the 0.5 GW line recently revealed; 2.2 GW solar cell facility scheduled for completion in FY28.
- Pump and motor facility development under QIP 1 is underway; VFD and structure facilities are completed.
- Advanced in-house power electronics capabilities maintained, manufacturing both controllers and VFDs internally.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects PM-KUSUM 2.0 scheme approval in the ongoing quarter (Q3 FY27), with positive execution results in the subsequent quarter.
- Management expects debt to further decline by March end.
- Strong revenue visibility backed by existing order book that covers the current quarter, plus upcoming execution from PM-KUSUM 2.0 and Magel Tyala schemes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PM-KUSUM 2.0 central scheme awaits central government clearance and revisions.
- Current solar business execution is concentrated in a single state under Maharashtra's Magel Tyala scheme with only one active tender.
- Pricing flexibility remains limited in the solar business due to concentrated tender activity.
- Submersible pumps act as lifelines in drought conditions (e.g., Karnataka) as they draw water from deep underground; company achieved ~40% market share in Karnataka scheme.
- Export growth depends on how the geopolitical situation (Middle East conflict, Strait of Hormuz) evolves over the next six months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs increased by ~3% QoQ due to conflict around the Strait of Hormuz impacting MS, steel, and copper prices.
- Cost optimization (advertising expense optimization, Kaizen initiatives, tighter cost management) reduced costs by 2%, limiting net EBITDA impact to 1%.
- Sales realization remained almost flat with a ~1% impact due to sales mix.
- Average borrowing interest cost is around 7% supported by AA- credit rating.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex plan stands at Rs 1,700 crore, with ~Rs 200 crore spent on the 2.2 GW solar cell plant so far.
- 500 MW solar panel facility started trial runs; commercial production expected in 1 to 1.5 months.
- 2.2 GW solar cell facility targeted for completion in FY28, with strong revenue contributions after March 2028.
- Pump and motor facility development ongoing; VFD and structure facilities completed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Q2 receivables/collections of ~Rs 400 crore from Asian Development Bank financing for Maharashtra Government were delayed and received in October instead of closing within Q2.
- Total debt stood at Rs 830 crore at Q2 end and has subsequently reduced to below Rs 500 crore after October collections.
- Outstanding receivables stood at Rs 1,750 crore as of the board meeting date.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts (Strait of Hormuz / Middle East) causing logistics constraints and rising raw material prices (MS, steel, copper).
- Delay in PM-KUSUM 2.0 scheme rollout by the central government.
- Asian Development Bank condition capping any single bidder's share at 25% in Maharashtra market-mode tenders restricted allocation despite being L1.

### One-line takeaway
Shakti Pumps delivered steady Q2 FY27 performance driven by strong solar installations and export growth, while awaiting PM-KUSUM 2.0 rollout and ramping up in-house solar panel and cell manufacturing capacities.
