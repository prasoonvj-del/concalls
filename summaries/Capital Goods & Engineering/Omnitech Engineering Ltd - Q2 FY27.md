# Omnitech Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, a healthy order book, and successful diversification into new segments like defense and data centers, despite some margin compression due to operational leverage.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 175.56 crore (+40.5% YoY).
*   **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 42.22 crore (Note: Transcript states this figure, though it appears inconsistent with Q2 data; reporting as stated).
*   **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 51.25 crore (+17.7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 101.87 crore (+45.4% YoY).
*   **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 30.57 crore (+35.5% YoY).
*   **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 80.01 crore (+117% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 3,178 crore (as of Oct 31, 2026).
*   **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.37x (vs 0.34x in FY26).
*   **Capacity:** 3.34 million hours (annualized, +26.2% YoY).
*   **Capacity Utilization:** 68% to 70%.

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Mix:** Energy (53.5%), Motion Control & Automation (22.9%), Industrial Equipment Systems (19.4%), Other (4.2%).
*   **Geography:** Exports account for ~89% of revenue (North America 65.5%, Asia 20.9%, India 10.8%, Europe/UK 2.8%).
*   **New Segments:** Commenced commercial orders in data center infrastructure; secured first article development orders in aerospace and defense.
*   **Operational:** NABL accreditation received for in-house metallurgical lab.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects 30% to 35% YoY revenue growth for the next two years.
*   **Margins:** Management aims to sustain EBITDA margins in the 30%–32% range.
*   **Peak Revenue:** Management estimates peak revenue potential of Rs 1,100–1,200 crore upon completion of current capacity expansions.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Oil & Gas:** Demand remains steady; company focuses on operation and maintenance (O&M) rather than pure capex, providing recurring revenue.
*   **Defense:** Currently working with major Indian defense players; focusing on machine components and subsystems.
*   **Data Centers:** Qualification time for components is 8–14 months; margins are expected to be in line with current company levels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Compression:** EBITDA margins saw pressure due to operational leverage from entering new segments (defense, data centers) and increased tooling/consumable costs.
*   **Raw Materials:** Pricing is largely a pass-through, with clauses reviewed quarterly or half-yearly (fluctuation absorption typically 5–8%).
*   **Finance Cost:** Average borrowing cost is 8% to 8.25%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Expansion:** Chhapara, Rajkot facility expansion is underway, expected to be commissioned in 10–11 months.
*   **Funding:** Capex funded via IPO proceeds, internal accruals, and debt.
*   **Utilization:** Current utilization is 68–70%; peak sustainable utilization is 80–85%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Net working capital days improved to 259 days (from 294 days in FY26), driven by improvements in inventory (-24 days) and receivables (-13 days).
*   **IPO Proceeds:** Rs 418 crore total; Rs 50 crore used for debt repayment, Rs 208 crore utilized for projects, Rs 210 crore unutilized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution:** Management emphasized that executing the large order book is more critical than securing it.
*   **Timeline:** Capacity expansion is running 1–2 months behind schedule due to IPO timing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the sequential slowdown in growth?** Management reiterated the focus on long-term sustainable growth (30-35% CAGR) rather than quarter-on-quarter volatility.
*   **Q: Are you supplying blades for gas turbines?** Management clarified they focus on other gas turbine components, not blades.
*   **Q: Will you set up dedicated facilities for customers?** Management stated it is premature to discuss; they prefer the current model but are open to future discussions.
*   **Q: Is the order book executable at Rs 600 crore/year?** Management noted it is a gradual ramp-up over 5 years, not linear, with significant acceleration expected in FY28/FY29.
*   **Q: Why did other expenses increase?** Management attributed this to higher tooling/consumable usage and job work (heat treatment) for new capacity ramp-ups.

### One-line takeaway
Omnitech is successfully diversifying into defense and data centers while maintaining a 30-35% growth trajectory, supported by a Rs 3,178 crore order book and ongoing capacity expansion.
