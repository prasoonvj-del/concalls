# Omnitech Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong top-line growth, a growing order book, and expansion into defense and data centers, despite minor capex delays.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 175.56 crore, up 40.5% YoY.
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 51.25 crore, up 17.7% YoY.
- **PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 40.33 crore, up 37.8% YoY.
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 30.57 crore, up 35.5% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Performance:** Revenue Rs 42.22 crore (up 50% YoY), EBITDA Rs 101.87 crore (up 45.4% YoY), PBT Rs 80.01 crore (up 117.3% YoY), PAT Rs 60.29 crore (up 117% YoY).
- **Order Book:** Rs 3,178 crore as of October 31, 2026.
- **Capacity & Utilization:** Annualized machine capacity reached 3.34 million hours (up 26.2% YoY). Utilization stands at 68%–70% (peak capacity 80%–85%).
- **Return Ratios (Annualized):** ROE at 16.4% (vs 11.7% in FY26); ROCE at 16.7% (vs 13.7% in FY26).
- **Working Capital:** Net working capital days improved to 259 days from 294 days in FY26 (inventory improved by 24 days; receivables by 13 days).

### Highlights
- **Segment Revenue Share (Q2 FY27):** Energy 53.5%, Motion Control & Automation 22.9%, Industrial Equipment Systems 19.4%, Diversified/Other 4.2%.
- **Geographic Mix (Q2 FY27):** North America 65.5%, Asia 20.9%, India 10.8%, Europe & UK 2.8%. Exports accounted for 89% of total revenue.
- **Order Book Mix:** Energy 76%, Motion Control & Automation ~12%, Industrial Equipment Systems ~11.5%.
- **New Business Progress:** Commenced receiving commercial orders in data center infrastructure (cooling system components/assemblies) following first article approvals. Received commercial production orders in aerospace and defense.
- **Certifications:** ISO 17025 NABL accreditation received for in-house metallurgical lab; NADCAP certification program is on schedule.
- Breaks pattern in record: Execution of large multi-year orders like Weatherford is being phased gradually over 5 years rather than ramping immediately in year one.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Management targets full-year FY27 revenue growth of 30%–35% YoY, maintaining a 30%–35% CAGR over the next 2 years.
- **Margin Guidance:** Sustainable EBITDA margins expected between 30%–32% (or 30%–33%) for H2 FY27 and full year.
- **Capacity Peak Revenue:** Management projects total potential peak revenue of Rs 1,100–1,200 crore once the Chhapara facility is commissioned.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- YoY EBITDA margin expansion lagged revenue growth due to operational leverage costs associated with scaling defense, aerospace, and data center lines, along with ongoing new product developments.
- Q2 other expenses rose due to higher consumption of tooling, consumables, and subcontracting (heat treatment) for new capacity ramp-ups.
- Raw material price and currency movements are contractual pass-throughs with standard threshold bands (5%–8%), adjusted quarterly, half-yearly, or annually.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **IPO Proceeds Utilization:** Out of Rs 418 crore raised, Rs 50 crore used for debt repayment, Rs 158 crore utilized for project execution (total Rs 208 crore utilized), leaving Rs 210 crore unutilized to be deployed as scheduled.
- **Chhapara Expansion:** Delayed by 1–2 months due to timing of IPO proceeds; expected to complete within the next 10–11 months.
- Future capex beyond IPO proceeds will be funded through bank debt, internal accruals, and benefits under the Gujarat Viksit Scheme.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.37x vs 0.34x at FY26 end.
- **Borrowing Cost:** Average borrowing cost stands at 8.00%–8.25%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution delays of 1–2 months in Chhapara facility construction.
- Long qualification lead times of 8 to 14 months for high-precision components in data centers and aerospace.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arham (Arham Investments):** Asked about defense program specifics (Sukhoi, BrahMos) and gas turbine products.  
  *Management:* Confirmed engagement with all major Indian defense players without naming specific platforms; noted gas turbine focus is on structural components, excluding blades.
- **Saurabh (Sameeksha Capital):** Inquired about data center component specs, qualification cycles, and margins.  
  *Management:* Stated qualification takes 8–14 months, margins are equivalent to energy products, and supply involves machined parts and cooling system assemblies.
- **Pratham Doshi:** Asked for order book cancellation terms and unutilized IPO funds.  
  *Management:* Clarified orders do not carry cancellation risk, raw material costs are pass-through, and Rs 210 crore of IPO funds remain unutilized.
- **Arham (Arham Investments):** Asked if FY27 top-line growth could exceed 40% given H1's 50% YoY growth.  
  *Management:* Reaffirmed a conservative guidance range of 30%–35% growth for FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Omnitech demonstrated strong 40.5% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 while expanding into defense and data centers, backed by a robust Rs 3,178 crore order book.
