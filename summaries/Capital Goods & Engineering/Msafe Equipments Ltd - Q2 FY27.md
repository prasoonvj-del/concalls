# Msafe Equipments Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 and HY1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported healthy growth, strong profitability, and progress in their rental-led model, having surpassed previously provided guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Operations Revenue:** Rs 49 crore (up 53% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 15.61 crore (margin at 38.65%)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 8.49 crore (up 55% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operations Revenue:** Rs 47 crore [Note: Transcript states this as stated in call, alongside noting rentals contributed 51.5%] (up 47% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 15.76 crore
- **Rental vs Sale Contribution (H1 FY27):** Rentals contributed 51.5% (scaffolding rental yields at 60% to 38% [Note: range mentioned as 32% to 38% and 60% to 66% for yields])
- **Capacity/Target Metrics:** Scaffolding capacity increased from 3,200 tonnes to 9,000 tonnes, with a revised target of 15,000 tonnes per annum. Formwork initial capacity target is 1,500 to 2,500 tonnes.
- **Exports:** Contributing 7% to 8% of business.

### Highlights
- Rental income contributed 51.5% in H1 FY27, strengthening recurring income.
- Expanded warehouse network to 9 locations, improving pan-India reach.
- Integrated facility in Mathura is under progress and scheduled to become operational in May 2027.
- Trial runs for formwork have commenced, targeting commercialisation in December 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the annual guidance of ~70% top-line growth and similar bottom-line growth for FY27.
- Formwork segment is expected to contribute at least 15% to the business from Q3/Q4 onwards.
- Management stated they have surpassed the commitments provided earlier.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is supported by high-rise construction, mass housing, infrastructure projects, data centres, semiconductor plants, and major airports (Navi Mumbai International Airport, Bhogapuram Airport).
- Formwork offers faster execution and helps reduce project costs.
- The company intends to cross-sell to existing customers, particularly infrastructure and civil contractors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin stood at 38.65% (compared to 39.67% YoY), with the minor dip primarily driven by product mix (increase in product sales which carry lower margins of 5% to 6%).
- Depreciation is calculated using the written-down value method.
- Scaffolding valuation is Rs 65,000 to Rs 70,000 per tonne; formwork is valued at Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 2 lakh per tonne.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ongoing capex includes completing the factory in Mathura (operational May 2027) and meeting working capital requirements.
- Scaffolding capacity target scaled up to 15,000 tonnes per annum. Formwork target set at 1,500 to 2,500 tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt is being utilized for factory completion and inventory/working capital. Management indicated taking on debt of around Rs 35 crore [Note: specific debt number referenced in Q&A context as "taking 35 crore? Yes"].
- Cash flow from operations was positive for the first half, and management's basic objective is to remain cash-flow positive for the full year.
- Trade receivables days-outstanding improved compared to last year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Enquiry-to-order conversion and execution cycle for formwork is relatively long.
- Becoming debt-free in the near term is difficult due to ongoing asset-heavy and inventory-heavy reinvestment requirements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Surya Kalagarla (Phillip Capital) on leasing breakdown:** Management clarified that rental contribution remains stable around 51%–52%.
2. **S NAFA Asset Managers on formwork competition and guidance:** Management stated that while new players are entering, market opportunity is vast due to a shift towards high-rise construction, and formwork will contribute at least 15% to business going forward.
3. **Singh (JM Financial Asset Management) on capacity and utilisation:** Management noted capacity was scaled up from 3,200 to 9,000 and now targeted at 15,000 tonnes, with scaffolding asset utilisation near 80%.
4. **Sagar (Tapprs Ventures) on scaffolding vs formwork economics:** Management explained formwork generates slightly lower yields but has a significantly larger addressable market and longer useful durations.
5. **Samruddhi Shinde (Phillip Capital) on margin dip:** Management clarified the minor contraction from 39.67% to 38.65% was due to a product mix change with higher outright product sales.

### One-line takeaway
Msafe Equipments delivered strong Q2 FY27 growth with 53% YoY revenue increase and 55% YoY PAT growth, driven by expansion in its rental model and scaling capacities in scaffolding and formwork.
