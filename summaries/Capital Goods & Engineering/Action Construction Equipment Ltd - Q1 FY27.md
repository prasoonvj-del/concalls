# Action Construction Equipment Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-24 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Recorded best-ever Q1 performance in terms of revenues and profits despite cost volatility and supply chain uncertainty.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** INR 836 crores, up ~19% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** INR 170.58 crores, up 19.66% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.40%, expansion of 12 bps YoY.
- **PBT:** INR 156.79 crores, up 23.81% YoY (margin at 18.75%, up 73 bps).
- **PAT:** INR 118.59 crores, up 22.47% YoY (margin at 14.18%, up 41 bps).
- **Cranes, Construction Equipment & Material Handling Segment Revenue:** INR 738.37 crores, up ~22% YoY (volumes up 17.25% YoY; margins at INR 134.09 crores, up 24.35% YoY).
- **Agri Equipment Division Revenue:** INR 42.67 crores (margins at INR 4.34 crores).
- **Segment Contribution:** Exports ~3%, Defense ~5% (full-year expected: Exports 6-7%, Defense 5-6%).
- **Price Increases Taken:** Cumulative ~10% across the last 6 months (1-1.5% in January, 3-4% in March, 5-6% in June under implementation).
- **Capex Guidance:** INR 200 crores to INR 250 crores for the full year (includes ~INR 130-140 crores for land contracted earlier, INR 40-50 crores for plant 9, and INR 50-60 crores for robotics/automation/maintenance).

### Highlights
- Registered best-ever Q1 performance driven by operational excellence, cost optimization, and calibrated pricing actions.
- Strategic joint venture with KATO Works Limited to commence operations by the end of July (meaningful revenues from FY28 onwards).
- Defense business progressing; started manufacturing rough terrain forklifts in the current quarter.
- Plant 9 capacity being built for defense manufacturing and special machines with a turnover capacity of ~INR 500 crores, requiring a capex of INR 40-50 crores.
- Expanded tower crane capacity to about 1,000 cranes via minor arrangements and a rented nearby facility.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management refrained from giving a specific full-year percentage growth guidance due to geopolitical and unpredictable macroeconomic times, preferring to provide a full-year target around the end of September.
- Reiterated historical seasonality: ~40-45% of revenue in H1 and 55-60% in H2.
- Full-year EBITDA margin aim is to maintain and sustain around 15-16%.
- Defense revenue expected to be over INR 200 crores in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry demand remained stable during Q1 following H2 FY26 normalization.
- Hydra type of cranes market recovering after being subdued last year due to price increases and technological shifts; Hydra-to-NG mix expected to normalize to 60:40 this year and 50:50 over the next 1-3 years.
- Structural growth drivers remain intact via government infra spending (roads, railways, urban infra, manufacturing, logistics, data centers).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin contraction of ~140 bps attributed to commodity cost headwinds (steel prices up ~20%, tires, belts, rubber, plastics, oil derivatives, freight, and currency movement).
- Overall raw material inflation expected to be around 11-12%, fully impacting by July-August.
- Price hikes taken are intended to cover costs rather than expand margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total full-year capex estimated at INR 200-250 crores.
- Existing complex land will be mostly exhausted after plant 9 (dedicated to defense/special machines, ~100 acres total).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Healthy balance sheet with available funds noted, though specific net debt/cash figures were not explicitly provided in crore terms.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Escalating geopolitical tensions (West Asia/Iran war, shipping/export issues to Middle East/Saudi Arabia), volatile energy markets, firm steel prices, elevated freight costs, and inflation impacting manufacturing supply chains (engine and casting supplier issues faced in the last month).
- Price resistance from customers amid a lean monsoon season following recent price hikes.
- Unfavorable Finance Ministry decision regarding the non-implementation of anti-dumping duties on imported/Chinese cranes despite DGTR recommendations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Garvit Goyal (Serene Alpha):** Asked about demand/supply chain and full-year growth. Management noted strong demand till May, temporary engine/casting supply constraints, and deferred guidance to September end due to recent price hikes and rains.
- **Aditya (Old Bridge Mutual Fund):** Asked about Hydra vs. New Generation (NG) mix and gross margins. Management stated Hydra share is returning to 60:40 with higher tonnages lifting realizations; gross margin drop is purely commodity-driven.
- **Preet (InCred AMC):** Inquired about raw material inflation and margin sustainability. Management guided to 11-12% total inflation impact and reiterated the goal to maintain 15-16% operating EBITDA margins.
- **Aniket Madhwani (Steptrade Capital):** Asked about defense segment revenue and plant 9 capacity. Management confirmed expectations of >INR 200 crores in defense (5-6% mix) and ~INR 500 crores peak turnover potential from Plant 9.
- **Lakshminarayanan (Tunga Advisors):** Asked about demand planning evolution and KATO localization. Management stated they carry 10-20% extra semi-finished/finished inventory to handle upside; KATO export models targeting 50-60% localization in 2 years.
- **Divyam Jain (360 One Capital):** Asked about KATO JV capital structure and royalty. Management clarified KATO is contributing INR 100 crores in cash for a total INR 200 crores investment, with ACE contributing in-kind tech/infrastructure; 3% royalty applies only to KATO-specific design export models built by the JV.

### One-line takeaway
ACE delivered its best-ever Q1 performance with 19% revenue growth and stable profitability, while managing commodity cost inflation through calibrated price hikes and advancing its strategic KATO JV and defense initiatives.
