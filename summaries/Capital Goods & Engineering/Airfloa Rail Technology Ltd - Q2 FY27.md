# Airfloa Rail Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong top-line and bottom-line growth, backed by strategic acquisitions, expanded margins, and new high-value engineering contracts.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 220.3 crore (+143% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 48.4 crore (+120% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 21.9%
- **PAT:** Rs 25.1 crore (+108% YoY)
- **PAT margin:** 11.4%
- **Order book:** Rs 610 crore as of November 15, 2026 (includes ~Rs 180 crore acquired order book from KIN)
- **ROCE:** 32.1%
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.5x

### Highlights
- Acquired the business of KIN Railway Equipment Private Limited (Coimbatore) for Rs 41 crore cash consideration plus GST, adding an order book of ~Rs 180 crore and a manufacturing facility of >2 lakh sq ft.
- Secured a design contract (as lead member of consortium with Italy-based BLUE Engineering S.r.l.) worth Rs 34.85 crore (incl. GST) from ICF Chennai for Vande Bharat sleeper trainset interior design (12-month execution). Management noted Airfloa is the first Indian company to secure an engineering contract from Indian Railways.
- Signed an MoU with KTK Group Company Limited to jointly pursue platform screen door projects in India.
- Commenced re-tendering participation at revised prices to offset input cost increases (aluminum).

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 500 crore and above.
- **FY27 Balance Execution:** Plans to execute Rs 280 crore in H2 FY27.
- **FY27 Order Inflows Target:** Pursuing fresh inflows of Rs 450 crore in H2 FY27 to close FY27 with an unexecuted order book of Rs 750 crore to Rs 800 crore.
- **FY28 Revenue Guidance:** Initiated at Rs 750 crore (~90% from railways, ~10% from defense/aerospace).
- **PAT Margin Guidance:** Maintained within 10% to 12% baseline range, with aspirations for higher margins.
- **EBITDA Margin Target:** Targeted between 20% to 30%, expecting expansion over the next 6 to 12 months as high-tech projects scale.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust demand across Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat, metro rail systems, EMUs, MEMUs, refurbishment programs, and railway infrastructure.
- Management estimated the platform screen doors market in India at Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 2,000 crore annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity price volatility (aluminum) impacted earlier project economics, prompting disciplined bidding and re-tendering at revised pricing.
- EBITDA margin reached 21.9% in H1 FY27; technology-heavy orders (automatic sliding doors, vacuum toilets, metro interiors, platform screen doors) expected to drive further margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added >2 lakh sq ft manufacturing facility in Coimbatore via the KIN acquisition, rebranded as the international business facility to target global OEMs (Alstom, Hitachi, Siemens) and exports.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash and bank balances:** Rs 17.3 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Inventory days:** Reduced by 19 days compared to March 2026.
- **Receivables:** Total debtors stood at ~Rs 263 crore (approx. 78.2% under 180 days). Management targets reducing debtor days within 6 months.
- **Borrowings:** ~Rs 100 crore short-term/working capital debt (total debt ~Rs 140 crore including KIN acquisition funding).
- **Cost of borrowing:** 8.25% to 8.5%. Management expects an additional Rs 100 crore borrowing requirement over the next 2 years.
- **Operating cash flows:** Targeted to turn positive in FY28.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High receivable build-up (~Rs 263 crore) due to long conversion timelines on new turnkey train contracts (Amrit Bharat, EMUs, MEMUs).
- Long qualification and development cycles in defense and aerospace programs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On low H1 FY27 order inflows vs H2 target:** Management stated order selection is strictly disciplined to protect profitability rather than pursuing growth at any cost; confident of adding Rs 450 crore inflows in H2 FY27.
- **On high receivables & peak debt:** Management explained ~78.2% of receivables are under 180 days. Current borrowing levels are sufficient to execute the Rs 750 crore FY28 target; fresh equity will only be considered if large inorganic defense contracts materialize.
- **On defense JV status with Big Bang Boom:** Management clarified defense has long qualification timelines; working on 3 projects targeting FY28 revenue realization. Strategic JV announcements are expected in the near term.
- **On product focus in defense/aerospace:** Management stated focus areas are unmanned systems (drones, anti-drones, autonomous tech), precision-guided munitions, and space satellite shock/vibration testing for ISRO.

### One-line takeaway
Airfloa is executing strongly on railway expansion via the KIN acquisition and high-margin engineering wins, while laying structural groundwork for aerospace and defense growth by FY28.
