# HD Fire Protect Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects strong financial growth, robust demand, and expanding international presence in its first earnings call as a listed company.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 127 crore (Up 29% YoY)
- Operating EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 37.5 crore (vs Rs 29 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27): Rs 28.9 crore (vs Rs 20.4 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 236.3 crore (Up 23% YoY)
- Operating EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 67.2 crore (vs Rs 57.6 crore in H1 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT) (H1 FY27): Rs 52.9 crore (vs Rs 47.1 crore in H1 FY26)
- Net cash flow generated from operating activities (as of Sep 30, 2026): Rs 71 crore
- Order book: Rs 79 crore
- Export revenue contribution (H1 FY27): 35.63%
- Repeat customer contribution to revenue (FY26): 85.2%
- Capacity utilization: 60% to 70% (single-shift operation)
- UL listed certifications: 21 (as of Sep 2026)
- FM approved certifications: 87 (as of Sep 2026)
- Global fire protection equipment market CAGR expectation (CY25 to CY30): 6.5% to 7.5% ($85 billion to $90 billion)
- Indian fire protection equipment market CAGR (FY20 to FY26): 12% (reached INR 109 billion)
- Indian fire protection equipment market expected CAGR (FY26 to FY31): 10% to 12% (INR 175 billion to INR 195 billion)

### Highlights
- First earnings conference call as a listed company.
- Industrial projects account for around 75% of revenue (oil & gas, power, data centers, offshore, warehouses, chemical, pharma), with the balance from building commercial segments.
- Contract manufactured outsourced goods account for around 35% of total revenue.
- Note on record: MPCB rejected the approval application for two new sheds (K-99 and 298/1) at the Jalgaon facility due to procedural/technical aspects; management stated this does not impact existing business/production and new applications are in process.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is positive and confident of reaching its revenue targets, expecting exports to have slightly higher growth (5%-6% higher contribution compared to last year).
- Anticipates similar revenue seasonality, with 55% to 60% of revenue coming in H2, and Q4 being the strongest quarter.
- Expects similar historical EBITDA and PAT margins going forward as raw material prices stabilize and pricing is adjusted.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global market expanding at 6.5%-7.5% CAGR; Indian market expanding at 10%-12% CAGR driven by urbanization, infrastructure development, safety awareness, and stricter regulations.
- Strong demand seen in oil & gas and petrochemical sectors from the Middle East.
- Growing demand for pre-action water-based systems and gas suppression in data centers.
- International expansion targets include Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines), South America (Brazil, Colombia, Mexico), and Europe (Spain, Italy), alongside initial supplies to the US.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 margins impacted by fluctuations in raw material prices (bronze and steel), supply chain constraints, and elevated shipment costs.
- Freight costs for exports are fully passed on to end users; raw material import freight is factored into input costs and managed via price list revisions.
- Volatility has stabilized, and new price lists incorporate these costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing facilities located in Jalgaon and Thane (Maharashtra) spanning approximately 8.5 acres.
- Jalgaon facility expanded with one unit for manufacturing and another for an industrial warehouse and R&D center.
- New production facility and warehouse at Wagle Estate, Thane, expected to be completed by end of November 2026.
- Saudi Arabia expansion: Phase 1 involves forming a company and starting trading/stocking; Phase 2 involves acquiring lease land for local assembly of ~5 products.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash generated from operating activities stood at Rs 71 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Procedural/technical refusal from MPCB for commissioning new sheds at Jalgaon (management is re-applying).
- Geopolitical situations affecting logistics and raw material volatility.
- Stringent and time-consuming qualification/field-testing protocols (e.g., 1-year field testing for Saudi Aramco monitors) act as barriers to immediate revenue realization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aakash Raval (Sanghvi Family Office) on order book and contract manufacturing:** Management stated the order book is ~Rs 79 crore and contract manufacturing makes up ~35% of revenue, offering an extended product line under company branding and certifications.
- **Gaurav Uttarani (Indiv/Managers) on H2 execution and Saudi expansion:** Management confirmed historical seasonality (55-60% in H2, strongest Q4) holds, and detailed the two-phase Saudi strategy starting with trading and moving to local assembly.
- **Mohit (IIFL) on MPCB order impact:** Management clarified that the refusal applies only to new sheds under commissioning and has zero impact on existing operations or current-year targets; new applications have been submitted.
- **Jay Negandhi (Indiv/Managers) on Saudi Aramco approvals and TAM:** Management noted they hold 2 categories (sprinklers/valves, deluge valves), have applied for monitors (pending 1-year field test), and are discussing foam suppression systems.
- **Prathamesh Dahake (Oswal) on competitive landscape for certifications:** Management stated that domestic peers cannot easily accelerate UL/FM approvals due to stringent testing timelines, and emphasized the company's established Proven Track Record (PTR) as a key moat.

### One-line takeaway
HD Fire Protect delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 29% revenue growth and 42% PAT growth, backed by robust industrial demand, expanding international footprints, and established global quality certifications.
