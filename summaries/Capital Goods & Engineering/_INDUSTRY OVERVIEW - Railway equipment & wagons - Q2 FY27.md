# Capital Goods & Engineering - Railway equipment & wagons: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the capital goods and railway equipment sector report robust underlying demand driven by Indian Railways' aggressive capital expenditure—highlighted by Acme India Industries noting a record Rs 2.93 lakh crore railway capex outlay. Order books remain strong across the board, ranging from Rs 610 crore at Airfloa Rail Technology to Rs 5,850 crore at Jupiter Wagons and ~Rs 8,000 crore in freight rolling stock at Texmaco Rail & Engineering. However, companies are actively navigating a transition away from heavy reliance on government railway orders by diversifying into private clients, export markets, passenger rail, and green energy (such as BESS), while executing heavy capital expenditure for backward integration.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** All managements agree that structural tailwinds in Indian Railways modernization, freight wagon replacement demand, and safety upgrades (such as Kavach and smart wagons) provide multi-year visibility. 
* **Divergence:** Companies diverge sharply on their reliance on Indian Railways. Texmaco Rail reported a 31% YoY decline in revenue due to lower Indian Railways orders, prompting a strategic pivot where 85% of its order book is now anchored in private and export markets. Conversely, Acme India (63% market share in toilet modernization), Oriental Rail Infrastructure (scaling wagon production to 600 units/quarter), and Concord Control Systems (focusing on Kavach and locomotive intelligence) continue to target and secure substantial government and public-sector-driven orders.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
Raw material volatility (particularly steel and aluminum) is largely mitigated through price variation formulas in government tenders or pass-through models (as noted by Oriental Rail and Concord Control). Margins vary significantly based on product complexity:
* **Concord Control Systems** leads the sector in profitability, posting an EBITDA margin of 29.30%, which management attributes to in-house technology development and scale.
* **Airfloa Rail Technology** follows with an H1 EBITDA margin of 21.9%, expecting further expansion from tech-heavy orders.
* **Acme India** guides for 17% to 18% EBITDA margins for FY27.
* **Oriental Rail** reported an EBITDA margin of 16.2%.
* **Jupiter Wagons** posted a 12% EBITDA margin in Q2, expecting recovery toward 14%-15% in FY28 as wheelset production scales.
* **Texmaco Rail** defended its EBITDA margin at 10.2%. Working capital pressures, specifically high receivable/debtor days, remain a common operational challenge for companies like Airfloa and Acme.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is witnessing aggressive capital expenditure for backward integration and capacity enhancement:
* **Jupiter Wagons** is executing a Rs 2,500 crore total wheel manufacturing project (with Rs 900 crore equity infused and Rs 1,100 crore debt utilized).
* **Cosmic CRF** expanded galvanizing capacity to 12,000 MT, is adding a forging unit (11,000 MT capacity), and is integrating the Amzen asset.
* **Concord Control Systems** has installed >2 lakh square meters of PCB manufacturing capacity via its Fusion acquisition.
* **Airfloa Rail Technology** added a >2 lakh sq ft manufacturing facility in Coimbatore via the KIN acquisition.
* **Oriental Rail** and **Acme India** are utilizing minor capex or facility modifications to debottleneck capacities, with Oriental targeting a run rate of 600 wagons per quarter.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Jupiter Wagons:** Management's tone shifted from a cautious prototype/transition phase to strong demand revival and accelerating order inflows. They formalized an MoU with Poland’s PESA for passenger rail entry and advanced their battery cell manufacturing plans.
* **Texmaco Rail & Engineering:** Management shifted from discussing short-term execution priorities to declaring an "inflection point" in their business transformation, heavily emphasizing their pivot to private and export markets (now 85% of the order book) and an initial foray into the defense sector.

## What to watch next quarter
* Execution run rates and scaling targets, specifically Oriental Rail's target of 600 wagons/quarter and Jupiter Wagons' ramp-up to 600–800 wagons/month.
* Commissioning milestones and trial updates, including Concord Control's Kavach trials, Cosmic CRF's Amzen plant overhaul, and the progress on Jupiter's axle and wheel manufacturing lines.
* Order inflows in private and export markets, particularly for Texmaco and Airfloa.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Historically, railway equipment and wagon manufacturers relied heavily on low-margin, high-volume turnkey supply contracts directly tied to Indian Railways budgets and government tender cycles. Over the past 5 years, the model has shifted toward backward integration, technological upgrading (safety systems, electronics, advanced bogies), diversifying into private/export markets, and moving into asset-light or higher-value engineering and energy storage (BESS) segments [Background].

**Shifts underway** -
* **Pivot to private and export markets to de-risk dependence on Indian Railways:** Scaling. Texmaco shifted its order book to 85% private and export markets [Texmaco concall Q2 FY27], while Airfloa is targeting global OEMs like Alstom, Hitachi, and Siemens via its new international facility [Airfloa concall H1 FY27]. This shift matters because it insulates earnings from government budget cyclicality and improves pricing power.
* **Backward integration and advanced technological manufacturing (PCBs, wheelsets, forgings, electronics):** Scaling. Concord acquired Fusion to manufacture rigid and flexible PCBs [Concord concall H1 FY27], Cosmic CRF expanded galvanizing capacity and set up an 11,000 MT forging unit [Cosmic concall H1 FY27], and Jupiter Wagons invested heavily in its Rs 2,500 crore railwheel facility [Jupiter concall Q2 FY27]. This matters for protecting operating margins and capturing higher value-add locally.
* **Diversification into green energy and passenger rail/urban transport:** Scaling. Jupiter Wagons expanded its BESS order book past 500 MWh (targeting Rs 1,000 crore by FY27) and partnered with Poland's PESA for passenger rail [Jupiter concall Q2 FY27]. This matters as it expands the addressable market beyond cyclical freight wagons.

**Government push** - Cabinet clearance for the Noida Metro Aqua Line extension (Railways +, Construction +) directly supports urban transit and rolling stock demand [Govt: Cabinet, Nov 2026].
