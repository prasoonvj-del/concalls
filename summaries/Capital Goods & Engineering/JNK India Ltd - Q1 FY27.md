# JNK India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and profit growth, healthy order book, and intact annual guidance.

### Key numbers
- Order book (as on June 30, 2026): Rs 1,801 crore
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 186 crore, up 80.6% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 21.9 crore, up 3.1x YoY (margin: 11.8% vs 7.0% YoY)
- Standalone EBITDA margin (JNK India): 14.0%
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 9.6 crore, up 8.5x YoY (margin: 5.2% vs 1.1% YoY)
- JNK Chemdist JV revenue contribution: 8.8% to group revenue; operating loss of Rs 3.6 crore
- Unbilled revenue: Rs 200 crore to Rs 221 crore
- Opportunity pipeline: More than Rs 6,000 crore (50:50 mix between international and domestic)
- Non-heating segment medium-term revenue target: Around 40% of revenue in 3 to 5 years

### Highlights
- Seasonality: Q1 typically contributes 10% to 15% of full-year revenue, H1 contributes 30% to 35%, and H2 accounts for 60% to 70%.
- A large export order received on June 8, 2026, was cancelled solely due to end-user licensing and approval requirements not materializing in time; management stated this happened at an early stage with no cost incurred or cash loss.
- Board approved registering a branch office in Iraq to explore oil, gas, refining, and petchem opportunities.
- JNK Chemdist JV is expanding into green hydrogen, sustainable fuels, and chemicals; green hydrogen is produced as a byproduct from ethanol (making ethyl acetate).
- Management noted that standalone financial statement operating expense / PAT margin rows had arithmetic errors requiring a revised filing.

### Guidance & outlook
- Revenue growth guidance: Maintained at around 20% to 25% YoY.
- EBITDA margin guidance: Maintained at about 12% to 14%.
- Order inflow hit rate expectation: Historically 20% to 25% for heating equipment; expected around 10% to 12% for newer diversified sectors initially.
- JNK Chemdist revenue expectation: 10% to 15% of JNK India revenue in FY27/next couple of years; expected to turn green (profitable) by the financial year-end.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall opportunity pipeline exceeds Rs 6,000 crore (~Rs 3,000 crore export heating equipment, ~Rs 3,000+ crore domestic non-heating equipment/technology-led EPC/renewable energy/metals & minerals).
- Heating equipment comprises ~60% of the current opportunity pipeline; process plants and adjacent tech-led EPC comprise ~40%.
- Export traction: Africa (Nigeria, Ethiopia) planning investments in refining, petrochemicals, and fertilizers; Middle East also showing opportunities.
- Diversification avenues: Offshore, metals and minerals, and renewable energy to reduce dependence on large capex refining/petchem orders.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity price fluctuations on ongoing projects are managed as orders are placed with sub-vendors on a fixed-price basis, having no major impact on ongoing margins.
- JNK Chemdist carries a high fixed-cost base causing operating losses in Q1, but operating leverage is expected to improve as revenue scales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major capex or fund-raising plans highlighted; non-fund-based limits (bank guarantees) may require enhancement for new contracts.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Payment terms from clients like Reliance and private companies (and BPCL Bina) are manageable with limited negative cash flow.
- Back-to-back arrangement with JNK Global for certain export/domestic projects aids working capital as the parent submits bank guarantees and receives payments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Project licensing and regulatory approvals are strict entry barriers; delays/failure in securing them can result in rare order cancellations (as experienced with the June 2026 export order).
- High fixed costs in the Chemdist JV led to Q1 operating losses.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kamlesh Bagmar (Lotus Asset Managers) on diversification targets:** Management stated the goal is to shift non-heating segments to 40% of revenue in 3 to 5 years.
2. **Deepak Purswani (SVAN Investments) on margin compression:** Management clarified that standalone JNK India EBITDA margin was 14%, but consolidated margin fell to 11.8% due to a Rs 3.6 crore operating loss in the Chemdist JV.
3. **Nikhil Kanodia (Sunidhi Securities) on TAM and right to win in new sectors:** India TAM is $300m-$500m in offshore and $500m-$1b in metals and minerals; JNK targets projects of $30m-$50m where large players (L&T, Technip) typically do not bid. Hit ratio is expected at 10%-12% initially.
4. **Rupesh Tatiya (Longequity Partners) on JNK Global dispute:** Management declined to comment on sub judice matters at the parent level, reiterating that JNK India is fully capable of executing projects independently.
5. **Suyash Jaywant (Mangaldas Venichand Trades) on Chemdist hydrogen cost advantage:** Management explained that green hydrogen is a byproduct of ethyl acetate production from ethanol, lowering relative production costs.

### One-line takeaway
JNK India delivered strong Q1 YoY revenue and profit growth, maintained its 20%-25% revenue growth and 12%-14% margin guidance, and is strategically pushing into non-heating adjacencies despite an isolated early-stage order cancellation.
