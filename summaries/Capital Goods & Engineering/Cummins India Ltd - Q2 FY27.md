# Cummins India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew year-on-year across domestic markets, but gross margins and profits contracted sharply due to input commodity inflation and corporate cost true-ups.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (management reiterated a focus on phased pricing, operational efficiencies, and cost control without issuing formal numbers).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted toward cautious on profitability due to severe raw material cost pressures and global geopolitical uncertainty, despite resilient end-market demand.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: A one-off corporate management cross-charge true-up in other expenses to support data center growth in India.
- Last quarter's promises: Price increases taken at the start of Q2 FY27 lagged raw material cost surges due to execution of prior contracted order backlogs.

### Highlights
- Launched QSK60-powered G34 and G37 gensets at bauma CONEXPO INDIA 2026 and showcased CPCB IV+ compliant 125 kVA generator set.
- Data center segment contributed 30% to 40% of domestic power generation sales.
- Non-data center domestic power generation showed positive growth in realty, manufacturing, and residential sectors.
- Continued evaluation of QSK95 indigenization and manufacturing network with no immediate structural changes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects demand across key end markets to remain resilient.
- Management anticipates persistent geopolitical uncertainty and inflationary input commodity pressures in the near term.
- Management aims to mitigate margin pressures over time via phased price hikes, supplier cost management, and operational efficiency.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Data center inquiry pipelines and order books remain robust globally and in India, driven by AI expansion.
- Industrial segment performance was constrained in Q2 FY27 by coach factory inventory build-up in Rail, project execution lumpiness in Marine, and supply chain constraints in Compressor.
- Diesel gensets are expected to remain the primary backup power technology in India due to fuel availability and infrastructure requirements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined by ~400 bps QoQ, primarily driven by raw material and commodity cost inflation.
- Other expenses were elevated due to a one-off corporate management cross-charge true-up covering increased corporate support for Indian operations (data centers).
- Pricing realizations lag instant commodity cost spikes as current deliveries are fulfilled under previously contracted order backlog prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocation philosophy remains unchanged, focusing on facility maintenance, safety, and growth.
- Management and supplier networks are expanding high-horsepower capacity in response to data center demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at Rs 3,800 crore to Rs 4,000 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Extended lead times and supplier capacity constraints for high-horsepower engines (QSK60) relative to market demand.
- Profitability lag caused by contracted backlogs preventing immediate pass-through of commodity cost inflation.
- Global geopolitical instability impacting export market consistency quarter-on-quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: How much of the 400 bps gross margin contraction was due to inflation versus product mix?**  
  *A:* Management answered that the vast majority was caused by commodity cost inflation, with product mix playing only a small role.
- **Q: Are capacity bottlenecks causing risk of market share loss in high-horsepower engines?**  
  *A:* Management stated that AI-driven data center demand is outpacing capacity industry-wide; Cummins is scaling up supplier capacity sustainably while aligning delivery schedules with customer project execution timelines.
- **Q: What caused the rise in other expenses during the quarter?**  
  *A:* CFO Soma Ghosh clarified that the increase was due to a one-off corporate cost true-up for management cross-charges supporting Indian business growth.
- **Q: Why have price increases been slow to restore margins?**  
  *A:* Management explained that orders currently being shipped were contracted at prior price points; price revisions affect new orders and take time to reflect in revenues.
- **Q: Are natural gas or fuel cell solutions threatening diesel genset demand?**  
  *A:* Management noted alternative fuels require proper infrastructure and economics; diesel gensets will continue as the dominant primary backup solution in India.

### One-line takeaway
Cummins India faces near-term margin pressure from commodity inflation and lagged backlog pricing, but long-term structural demand in data centers and domestic infrastructure remains strong.
