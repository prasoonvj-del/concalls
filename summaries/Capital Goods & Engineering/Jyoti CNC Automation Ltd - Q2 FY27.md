# Jyoti CNC Automation Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited highest-ever quarterly dispatches, robust order intake, and confidence in achieving guided margins and revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 602 crore (up 18.5% YoY from Rs 508 crore)
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 588 crore (up 31% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 131 crore (margin: 21.8%)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 164 crore (margin: 28%, up 320 bps YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 73 crore (margin: 12%)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 115 crore (margin: 19.6%, up 46% YoY)
- **Order Book:** Rs 5,082 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Order intake of Rs 800 crore during the quarter)
- **Dispatches:** ~1,900 machines (1,911 machines sold in Q2 FY27 vs 1,315 in Q2 FY26)
- **Capex:** Rs 183 crore incurred in H1 FY27; projected total outlay of Rs 450 crore to Rs 500 crore for FY27

### Highlights
- Dispatched approximately 1,900 machines, marking the highest quarterly dispatches in company history.
- Received approval for a capital investment of over Rs 1,020 crores over 5 years under the Government PLI (ECMS) scheme, featuring a 25% capital incentive (approx. Rs 255 crore central subsidy), with matching state grants expected.
- Developed the MTX 100, a compact 5-axis multitasking turn-mill center targeting aerospace, defence, automotive/EV, and oil & gas sectors.
- Huron added new manufacturing capacity in November 2025; dispatches from expanded capacity are expected to commence in Q3 and Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 25% to 30% combined (Jyoti and Huron).
- Management maintains EBITDA margin guidance of 25% for the full year.
- Expects to cross 8,000 machines in total dispatches for FY27.
- Huron revenue target set close to Rs 300 crore for the second half, with expected dispatches of Rs 80 crore to Rs 100 crore in Q3.
- Operating cash flow (OCF) target maintained at 50% of EBITDA.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's CNC machine demand is currently met ~60% through imports, presenting a significant import-substitution opportunity.
- Order book mix: 36% aerospace and defense, 22% automotive and auto components, 21% general engineering, 12% electronics manufacturing services (EMS), and 4% die and molds.
- Revenue mix: 36% automotive and auto components, 28% general engineering, 21% aerospace and defense, 7% electronics and manufacturing services, and 1% die and molds.
- Robust demand from the auto sector driven by capacity additions in the EV space.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management stated that despite higher shares of entry-level machines, the company's business model ensures margins will not fall below 25%.
- Global supply chain bottlenecks and increased lead times for CNC controllers reported due to high worldwide demand from AI and data center sectors.
- Employee count increased by ~950 people over the last 6 months; employee cost run rate in H2 expected to remain similar to H1.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Foundry operations expected to commence in the coming days, scaling capacity from 1,500 tonnes per month to 5,000 tonnes per month (capable of single-piece casting up to 15 tonnes using lost foam vacuum casting).
- Phase 1 capex for electronics component manufacturing (PLI/ECMS) planned at Rs 400 crore to Rs 450 crore over the next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory days improved to ~190 days from 203 days in March, with further reduction targeted by year-end.
- Operating cash flow is running at close to 47% of EBITDA.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing Middle East conflict causing higher energy/commodity prices, shipping disruptions, and increased freight/logistic costs.
- Extended lead times and supply chain constraints for CNC controllers globally.
- French investigation status remains unchanged (status quo) following responses submitted in April.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Harshit Patel (Equirus Securities):** Asked about order intake sustainability and Huron revenue. Management confirmed strong visibility and reiterated the Rs 300 crore target for Huron in H2.
- **Bajrang Bafna (Sunidhi Securities):** Inquired about margin sustainability given product mix and R&D expenses. Management noted that operational leverage and backward integration will fund future mechatronics/robotics R&D while maintaining 25% margins.
- **Ajay Surya (Niveshaay):** Asked about Huron investigation and controller development capabilities. Management stated status quo on Huron inquiry and highlighted a dedicated team of 150+ engineers working on controllers.
- **Abhishek Jain (Kriis PMS):** Questioned deferred Huron revenues and consolidated margins. Management confirmed deferred revenues will flow in H2, improving consolidated EBITDA margins by 3% to 4%.
- **Rabindra Nath Nayak (Nirmal Bang):** Asked about PLI capex funding and foundry capacity. Management clarified Phase 1 capex of Rs 400-450 crore will be partially funded by subsidies, and foundry capacity will reach 5,000 tonnes/month.

### One-line takeaway
Jyoti CNC posted robust Q2 FY27 performance with record dispatches and a strong order book of Rs 5,082 crore, while securing a major PLI scheme approval to drive import substitution in CNC controllers.
