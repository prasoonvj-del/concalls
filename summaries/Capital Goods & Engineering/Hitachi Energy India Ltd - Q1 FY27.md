# Hitachi Energy India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company delivered robust YoY growth in revenues, order inflows, and profitability backed by execution discipline and strong demand.

### Key numbers
- **Order Inflow:** Rs 5,096.5 crore (ex-HVDC up 26.1% YoY and 39.7% QoQ).
- **Order Backlog:** Rs 32,222.1 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 2,493.7 crore, up 68.6% YoY.
- **Operational EBITDA:** Rs 399.9 crore, up 135.0% YoY (includes unrealized FX loss of Rs 36.37 crore).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Up 120.2% YoY; PBT margin stood at 15.6%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) Margin:** 11.8%.
- **Other Income:** ~Rs 57 crore (primarily interest income on deposits).
- **Export Share in Revenue:** ~25%.

### Highlights
- Secured landmark orders including a ~Rs 1,700 crore order for 3 transmission links (supporting a 2 GW wind power project) in Europe.
- Won the company's first Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project (165 MW / 330 MWh) in Andhra Pradesh.
- Bagged orders for 56 bays of 400 kV GIS and 12.8 km of 400 kV GIB infrastructure for a 100 GW solar park in western India.
- Received multiple data center orders from hyperscalers, including a 40 × 2,500 kVA order in Hyderabad.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects strong revenue visibility driven by an order backlog of Rs 32,222.1 crore and a healthy bidding pipeline.
- Expects a greenfield HVDC project to be awarded within the next 6 months.
- Railways/metro orders are expected to pick up momentum starting in H2 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth is supported by non-fossil energy integration, data centers, transmission projects, grid modernization, and distribution infrastructure upgrades.
- BESS demand is being propelled by domestic mandates requiring energy storage for renewable energy projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 60%–65% of orders contain price pass-through clauses, insulating the company from commodity volatility.
- Gross margin experienced a slight YoY contraction due to project/product execution mix, but improved QoQ.
- FX/commodity volatility caused an unrealized FX loss of Rs 36.37 crore during the quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Groundbreaking commenced in June 2026 for Hitachi Energy India's 20th manufacturing plant in Karjan, Vadodara (smart transformer plant), targeted for commissioning by December 2028.
- Depreciation increased due to ongoing capex towards capacity expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company earned ~Rs 57 crore in other income, largely from interest on bank deposits.
- Finance costs remained broadly stable during the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange volatility resulted in an unrealized loss of Rs 36.37 crore in Q1 FY27.
- Temporary slowdown in order timing from railway and metro authorities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Anwani (PL Capital):** What is the value of the European offshore wind order, and what is the pipeline for BESS?
  - **Management:** The European transmission links order is around Rs 1,700 crore. BESS pipeline in India is strong due to RE integration mandates; current focus is domestic deployment before scaling exports.
- **Shirom Kapur (Jefferies):** What drove the YoY contraction in gross margin, and what is the offering scope in BESS?
  - **Management:** Gross margin variation was driven by product execution mix. In BESS, the company offers modular/scalable digital, grid integration, and management solutions excluding battery cell supply.
- **Parikshit Kandpal (HDFC Securities):** How much revenue did HVDC contribute this quarter?
  - **Management:** Full HVDC revenue execution picks up in years 2 and 3. Khavda project execution is picking up and Bhadla is starting, so Q1 had minimal HVDC revenue contribution.
- **Jason Soans (IDBI Capital):** Does the government allowing Chinese players into GIS/transformers pose a pricing threat?
  - **Management:** Competitors were already operating in some form; management does not see any material threat or impact on margin ambitions as long as there is a level playing field.
- **Umesh Raut (Nomura):** Is the drop in utility/transmission order share temporary?
  - **Management:** Yes, transmission project timing is temporary. Railway order flows are expected to recover in H2 FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Hitachi Energy India delivered robust 68.6% YoY revenue growth and expanded PBT margins to 15.6%, underpinned by strong execution and diversification into BESS and data center infrastructure.
