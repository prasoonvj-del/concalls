# Escorts Kubota Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume growth and market share gains in Construction Equipment and tractors, offset by severe margin pressure from commodity inflation.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Operating Revenue (Continuing Operations):** INR 3,231.4 crore, up 16.3% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** INR 292.4 crore (EBITDA margin at 9.0% vs 13.1% YoY and 11.2% QoQ).
- **PBT (Continuing Operations):** INR 344 crore vs INR 431.1 crore YoY.
- **Net Profit (PAT):** INR 255 crore vs INR 321.2 crore YoY.
- **EPS:** INR 23.18 vs INR 29.19 YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 6,410.3 crore, up 21.9% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 647.8 crore (margin at 10.1%).
- **Domestic Tractor Volume:** 32,628 tractors vs 32,329 YoY (marginal growth; industry down ~3% to 2.66 lakh units). Market share improved by 48 bps.
- **Export Tractor Volume:** 1,389 tractors vs 1,548 YoY (industry up ~16% to 29.6k units).
- **Construction Equipment (CE) Volume:** 1,764 machines, up nearly 54% YoY (industry up ~20%).
- **CE Segment Revenue:** INR 536.2 crore, up 58.6% YoY.
- **CE EBIT Margin:** 7.9% vs 3.8% YoY and 5.4% QoQ.
- **Agri Machinery Segment Revenue:** INR 2,690.1 crore, up 10.6% YoY.
- **Agri Machinery EBIT Margin:** 7.8% vs 12.8% YoY and 10.8% QoQ.

### Highlights
- Domestic tractor market share improved by 48 bps in Q2, driven by a refreshed product portfolio (Shaurya, Promaxx, Digitrac range, NeoStar) and retail execution.
- Non-tractor revenue (Agri Solutions, engines, spares/service) constituted 22% of Agri Machinery segment revenue vs 17% YoY.
- CE segment saw a 411 bps YoY jump in EBIT margin to 7.9%, driven by operating leverage and timely price increases.
- Showcased new products including BLX75K Backhoe Loader, F-25 fighter, and Hydra 16 Plus pick-and-carry cranes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a marginal single-digit degrowth for the tractor industry in FY27 due to a high base and second-half moderation.
- CE industry growth expected to moderate to high-teens for FY27.
- Commodity inflation expected to keep Q3 margins under pressure, with recovery starting from Q4 onwards following price hikes.
- Normal annual capex guided at ~INR 350 crore for FY27. Greenfield project costing estimated at ~INR 2,000 crore spread over 3 to 4 years (land acquisition of INR 400-450 crore already done).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic tractor industry witnessed moderation in Q2 due to monsoon-related disruptions, deficit rainfall (13% deficit), and high base effect from last year's GST cuts.
- Regional divergence noted: West and South saw degrowth (West down 21% in Q2 due to reservoir levels at 70%), while North and East saw marginal growth.
- Export industry concentrated in higher HP segments where EKL has limited presence, alongside market corrections in key export regions.
- CE demand supported by infrastructure development and government project execution.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity inflation reached 8-9% by the end of H2 (exit of Q2), compounded by manpower and conversion cost increases.
- EKL implemented a 1.5% price hike in April and 2-2.5% in August; a further 3-3.5% price hike is effective post-festive season (November), taking cumulative price hikes to 6-7%.
- Operating costs impacted by a 35% jump in contractual manpower cost in Haryana effective April 1st.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Greenfield land acquisition completed for INR 400-450 crores; construction expected to start next year, phasing out over 3-4 years starting with the tractor factory.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Production levels were higher than sales in Q2 to build inventory for the festive season, leading to a temporary inventory benefit reflected in costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity cost inflation (steel, rubber) building up further pressure into Q3 (additional 2-3% inflation hit).
- Subdued rural sentiment, deficit rainfall in West/South, and depleted reservoir levels impacting H2 demand.
- Geopolitical headwinds and lag in passing on price hikes impacting margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gunjan Prithyani (Bank of America):** Asked about the sharp margin drop despite price hikes. Management explained that 8-9% inflation outpaced the 3.5% cumulative price hikes taken so far, with another hike coming in November, and recovery expected only by Q4.
2. **Gunjan Prithyani (Bank of America):** Inquired about regional trends. Management confirmed severe degrowth in the West and South due to 13% deficit rainfall, while the North remained flat-to-marginally positive.
3. **Ashwani Kumar (3P Investment Managers):** Asked about 4-wheel drive adoption. Management stated 4-3W/4WD volumes grew 3x over the last 6-7 months following new product launches.
4. **Rishi Vora (Kotak Securities):** Asked about export market outlook. Management indicated exports would likely remain flat this year, with recovery expected next fiscal year.
5. **Mitul Shah (DAM Capital Advisors Limited):** Questioned inventory correction risks in H2. Management acknowledged that H2 will see double-digit industry degrowth, leading to a low single-digit degrowth for the full fiscal year.

### One-line takeaway
Escorts Kubota delivered 16.3% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by strong Construction Equipment performance and market share gains, but margins compressed significantly to 9.0% due to severe commodity inflation outstripping price actions.
