# Diffusion Engineers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 50.7% YoY and a robust order book, despite temporary margin pressure from input cost inflation.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 125.93 crore (up 50.7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 16.64 crore (up 34.51% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 13.21% (vs 14.80% in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 12.75 crore (up 25.46% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 226.79 crore (as of Sept 30, 2026), up 30.3% since March 31, 2026.
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 236.04 crore (up 43.73% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 29.43 crore (up 31.21% YoY).

### Highlights
*   Growth driven by healthy demand across heavy engineering, wear plates, wear parts, and welding consumables.
*   Management noted that 30% of revenue comes from new project builds, while 70% is derived from servicing the existing industrial installed base.
*   New product initiatives (shredders, rotors, rotary air feeders) are ready for sales; vertical roller mills have been successfully supplied to a leading cement manufacturer.
*   Company received qualification to manufacture loco shells for Indian Railways.
*   Export market expansion continues, with new breakthroughs in Ghana and Nigeria; US order expected pending tariff resolution.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects 20-25% YoY revenue growth for the next 3-5 years.
*   New product initiatives are expected to contribute 10-15% of revenue in the next 2-3 years.
*   Management expects to return to previous margin levels by Q4 FY27, provided raw material prices remain stable.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Core industries (cement, steel, power, mining) show strong momentum; 9 core industries grew 7.9% in September 2026.
*   No slowdown observed in end-user industries; management emphasized that the large existing installed base provides a buffer against potential new capacity expansion slowdowns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins moderated due to increased raw material costs (specialty ferroalloys/metals), energy costs, and logistics.
*   Management stated that price increases are being passed on to customers with a lag of 1-1.5 quarters.
*   Raw material mix is approximately 85-90% domestic and 10-15% imported.
*   Some raw material prices (e.g., tungsten) have shown rationalization after initial spikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Major capital expenditure is nearing completion; new facilities are being operationalized in a phased manner.
*   Expanded capacities are expected to improve operational efficiency and allow for larger, more complex orders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed in detail.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disruptions causing input cost volatility.
*   Fixed-price contracts (shorter lead times) limit the ability to immediately pass on cost increases.
*   JV profitability can be lumpy due to seasonal cycles (e.g., August/December/April lull periods in Europe/UK).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Margin recovery:** Management clarified that price hikes are being accepted by customers and they expect to return to previous margin levels by Q4 FY27.
*   **JV performance:** Management explained the negative contribution in Q2 was due to seasonal lulls in Europe/UK and expects the JV to finish the calendar year on a high.
*   **Order book mix:** Management confirmed growth across all three segments (heavy engineering, wear plates, welding consumables), noting that heavy engineering contributes the most to the order book due to larger size and longer lead times.
*   **New product contribution:** Management stated new products currently contribute 1-2% of revenue but are targeted to reach 10-15% in 2-3 years.
*   **Railways opportunity:** Management highlighted the loco shell qualification as a strategic entry point into the Indian Railways expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Diffusion Engineers delivered strong top-line growth and order book expansion, with management confident in margin recovery by Q4 FY27 as they pass on input costs and ramp up new capacities.
