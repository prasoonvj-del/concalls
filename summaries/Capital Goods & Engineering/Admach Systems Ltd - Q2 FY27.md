# Admach Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth driven by specialized execution, in-house component manufacturing, and a solid order book.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 42.2 crores (up 48.34% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 9.95 crores (up 47.62% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 6.53 crores (up 45.91% YoY)
- **Order Book:** Rs 77.42 crores as of the latest reporting period
- **Nuclear Order:** Rs 10.62 crores from Nuclear Fuel Complex
- **Capex Spend (FY27 guided):** Rs 15 crores to Rs 16 crores
- **Execution Target:** Rs 65 crores to Rs 70 crores of current order book to be executed before the end of FY27
- **Full Year Guidance:** Management is confident of crossing Rs 100 crores revenue in FY27

### Highlights
- Growth reflects execution capabilities and demand for specialized engineering solutions in defense, nuclear, steel processing, and advanced testing.
- Shift to in-house manufacturing of machine components (previously offloaded to vendors) saved time, transportation, and payouts, improving EBITDA margins.
- Developing two new game-changing import-substitute products (one in steel, one in nuclear) to be launched in the next 3 to 3.5 months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to comfortably cross Rs 100 crores revenue in FY27.
- FY28 growth trajectory is projected to remain constant with historical growth patterns, with volume and lines increasing.
- EBITDA margins expected to remain sustainable in the 20%-plus range for H2 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's focus on defense indigenization and nuclear capacity expansion (targeting 100 GW by 2047 from 8.8 GW currently) provides a favorable long-term environment.
- European partners are increasingly inclined to shift work to India due to energy crises, high costs, and Chinese competition inside Europe.
- Steel segment will account for roughly 50% of turnover, with non-steel (NDT and nuclear) making up the other 50%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin was ~23%.
- Margin expansion is attributed to manufacturing machine components in-house rather than offloading to vendors.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY27 capex estimated at Rs 15 crores to Rs 16 crores.
- Capex allocation: ~60% toward plant and machinery, and ~40% toward factory buildings, infrastructure improvements, and testing facilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- CWIP stands at Rs 15 crores, comprising new shed construction and plant/machinery.
- Disciplined working capital management is a key focus.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government orders and processes involve lengthy scrutinies, documentation, and agreements, taking about 2 quarters to commercialize.
- Contractual obligations restrict detailed disclosures on strategic defense and nuclear development projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ashish Soni (MM Capital Ltd):** Asked if new nuclear opportunities are similar to the executed Rs 10 crore order. *Management answered that some pipeline projects are similar while others involve different areas of the nuclear complex, with some being bigger in value, taking about 2 quarters to materialize.*
2. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital Ltd):** Inquired if the 23% EBITDA margin is sustainable. *Management confirmed it is sustainable and will be maintained or slightly improved in H2.*
3. **Aryan Bhatia (Analyst):** Asked about the breakdown of the Rs 15 crore CWIP. *Management clarified that 60% is for plant and machinery and 40% is for factory building/shed improvements.*
4. **Udit Sharma (InVed Research):** Questioned the ratio of steel vs. non-steel revenue for FY27. *Management stated steel will be around 50% and NDT/nuclear the other 50%.*
5. **Atharva Kolvankar (Individual Investor):** Asked what percentage of the current order book belongs to nuclear and defense. *Management stated it is currently 4% to 5%, but expected to increase soon.*

### One-line takeaway
Admach Systems delivered robust H1 FY27 growth backed by in-house manufacturing efficiencies, a healthy order book of Rs 77.42 crores, and rising demand in high-barrier defense, nuclear, and NDT sectors.
