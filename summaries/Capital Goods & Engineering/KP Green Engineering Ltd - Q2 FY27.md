# KP Green Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue and order book growth, but PAT declined due to higher depreciation, interest costs, and geopolitical pressures on raw material prices.

### Key numbers
- Total Income: Rs 682 crore, up 27% YoY (from Rs 536 crore in H1 FY26)
- Revenue from Operations: Rs 679 crore
- EBITDA: Rs 119 crore, up 17% YoY (from Rs 102 crore in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin: 17.4%
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 32 crore (compared to Rs 58 crore in FY26)
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Rs 6.9
- Cash Profit: Rs 75 crore, up 11% YoY
- Order Book: Rs 1,892 crore as of September 2026
- New Orders Announced in H1: ~Rs 770 crore (Rs 240 crore in July, Rs 258 crore in August, Rs 270 crore in October)
- Total Manufacturing Capacity: ~4 lakh tonnes
- Galvanizing Capacity: 90,000 tonnes per annum (Matar facility)
- Net Worth: Rs 491 crore (vs Rs 382 crore YoY)
- Fixed Assets: Rs 695 crore (vs Rs 330 crore YoY)
- Total Debt: Rs 447 crore (vs Rs 196 crore YoY)
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio: 0.91x (vs 0.5x YoY)
- Capacity Utilization: 40% to 50% overall (Galvanizing plant above 50%)

### Highlights
- Changed statutory auditor to BDO Network.
- Promoter confirmed equity infusion of Rs 100 crore (Rs 25 crore in 30-60 days, remaining Rs 75 crore over 18 months).
- *Record Check:* Unlike past periods where related-party orders constituted ~50% of sales, H1 FY27 saw only ~17% of sales from the KP Group, driven by a deliberate outward-focused strategy and large external orders like BSNL.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 Revenue Guidance: ~Rs 1,700 crore to Rs 1,800 crore.
- PAT Margin Guidance: 5% to 7% range for the next 2 quarters.
- Order Book Target: Upwards of Rs 2,500 crore by next midyear.
- Long-term Guidance: 25% CAGR for the next 5 years (under KP 3.0).
- Capacity Utilization Target: ~70% next year.
- Capex Guidance: ~Rs 300 crore over the next 18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India targeting 500 GW of non-fossil capacity by 2030 (driving demand for module mounting structures, torque tubes, lattice towers).
- National Electricity Plan envisages Rs 9 lakh crore transmission investment between 2023–2032.
- Telecom: ~8 lakh towers currently, 12 lakh planned for 5G rollout; company executing an Rs 819 crore BSNL order across ~1,280 sites.
- Railways and Roads FY27 capital outlay at Rs 3 lakh crore each; company supplies bridges, coverage towers, and crash barriers (first in India to clear all 3 NATRAX crash tests in first attempt).
- Pre-engineered building (PEB) market expected to grow from Rs 21,000 crore (2025) to Rs 38,900 crore (2030); data center capacity expected to grow from 1.5 GW to 6.5 GW by 2030.
- International: Received LOIs from Africa and Saudi Arabia (pending advance payments before exchange disclosure).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin impacted by geopolitical issues causing sharp increases in gas and zinc prices, though price variation formulas under IEEMA helped recover costs to a large extent.
- Lower PAT caused by higher depreciation and interest costs stemming from recent capex.
- Working capital cycle stretched due to higher capex, sales growth, and retention payments on orders like BSNL; operating cash flows expected to turn positive after March.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Galvanizing plant utilization currently above 50%, expected to reach up to 70% by the end of the year.
- Planned future expansions into adjacent metal products: hot rolling mills, onshore-offshore tubular towers, container manufacturing, cables and conductors, and aluminum extrusion. Land already purchased for tubular towers, with capex starting next financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt increased to Rs 447 crore; debt-to-equity rose to 0.91x.
- Working capital under temporary stress due to scaling operations and project retention money.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility leading to fluctuating raw material prices (zinc, gas).
- Stretched working capital cycle and higher receivable days due to project execution terms (e.g., BSNL retentions).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Hasmukh (Individual Investor) on related-party sales and order book run rate:** Management clarified that related-party sales dropped to 17% because they are pursuing larger external orders (like BSNL) to be more outward-driven. The order book provides 9–10 months of revenue visibility and is targeted to cross Rs 2,500 crore by next midyear.
2. **CA Ashish Jindal on working capital deterioration and trade payables:** Management stated that working capital is stretched due to higher capex and BSNL payment terms/retentions, but expect operating cash flows to turn positive after March.
3. **Aniket Panda on lower PAT and capacity utilization:** Management explained that capacity utilization is at 40%–50% due to rapid scaling over 3 years and seasonality, expecting 70% utilization next year and maintaining a 25% 5-year CAGR target.
4. **Rohan (Individual Investor) on margin compression and guidance:** Management defended the 5%–7% PAT margin guidance as realistic given ongoing geopolitical cost pressures, and guided for Rs 1,700–1,800 crore full-year revenue.
5. **Saravanan B. on future product timelines and capex funding:** Management stated hot-rolling mills and tubular tower capex will begin next financial year, funded through a mix of debt and a Rs 100 crore promoter equity infusion.

### One-line takeaway
KP Green Engineering reported robust 27% revenue and 17% EBITDA growth in H1 FY27 alongside a strong order book of Rs 1,892 crore, though PAT contracted due to higher depreciation, interest expenses, and geopolitical cost pressures.
