# Batliboi Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust order inflows, strong macro momentum, strategic acquisitions, and an improved year-on-year performance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 125 crore (up 80% YoY in Q1 FY26 as stated)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 4% (stable YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 49 lakh (compared to a loss of Rs 2.4 crore YoY)
- **Order Inflow:** Rs 283 crore total; Machine Tools Rs 59 crore; Air Engineering Rs 21 crore; Textile Machinery Rs 79 crore; Environmental Engineering Rs 76 crore
- **Order Backlog:** Rs 618 crore total; Machine Tools Rs 183 crore (30% of overall, including Quickmill); Textile Machinery Rs 201 crore; Environmental Engineering Rs 134 crore
- **Quickmill Turnover:** Rs 45 crore in Q1 FY27
- **Air Engineering Revenue:** Rs 21 crore in Q1 FY27
- **Textile Machinery Revenue:** Rs 12 crore in Q1 FY27
- **Environmental Engineering Revenue:** Rs 27 crore in Q1 FY27
- **Penta Automation Revenue (FY26):** Rs 25 crore
- **Environmental Engineering Landmark Order:** Rs 52 crore (SAEL Industries)
- **Electricity Cost:** Approx Rs 10 per unit

### Highlights
- Acquired Penta Automation Systems Private Limited, specializing in customized industrial automation and robotics integration.
- Environmental Engineering division secured a landmark order worth ~Rs 52 crore from SAEL Industries Limited for a pollution control system at a solar cell manufacturing facility in Jewar, Uttar Pradesh (execution in 6-8 months).
- Machine tool division installed 88 manufacturing division machines and 1 trading division machine; manufacturing capacity increased by ~30% over the last year due to capex investments.
- Quickmill (Canadian subsidiary) expanding into Gulf, Mexico, Egypt, and South America, securing large orders in Saudi Arabia.
- Bioconserve Renewable Envirotech Private Limited operating in ZLD and effluent treatment for the textile space.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to deliver around 10% top-line growth over the last year.
- Management aims to improve EBITDA margins to 7%-8% over the next 1 to 2 years.
- Penta Automation expects 25%-30% growth for the current fiscal and the next 2-3 years.
- Environmental Engineering division expects improved performance for the full year with major pipelines expected to fructify in the next 2-3 quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's macro momentum is driven by policy support for Atmanirbharta, global supply chain realignments, and FTAs with the UK and EU.
- Structural shift toward smarter, more complex machinery and CNC solutions due to a shortage of skilled manpower and manufacturing growth.
- Renewable energy investments (solar, storage, EVs) driving demand; solar cell manufacturing space growing significantly.
- Textile industry expected to benefit from new textile incentive policies and upcoming FTAs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material cost increases (copper, steel) are being addressed by absorbing part of the impact, passing increases to customers, and driving productivity improvements.
- Solar power currently covers 65%-70% of factory requirements; management plans to augment capacity by fiscal end to make power costs revenue-neutral.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex includes investments in the machine tool space and installing an additional solar plant in the factory.
- Upgrading existing manufacturing range of machines.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific figures provided other than order backlogs and inflows. Surat factory land bank monetization is actively pursued with potential buyers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential adverse impacts from a prolonged conflict in the Middle East and tariff-related headwinds.
- Shortage of skilled manpower driving the need for automation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shaishav Vora (Prudent Investments) on Penta Automation integration:** Management explained that automation is integrated across divisions (like robot-assisted cell manufacturing in machine tools) to counter skilled labor shortages. Penta is guided for 25-30% growth.
- **Prashantkumar Uttamlal (Investor) on low EBITDA margins (4%):** Management acknowledged the low margin and stated the target is to reach 7-8% EBITDA over 1-2 years through operational efficiencies and new acquisitions.
- **Kunal (Investor) on order backlog discrepancies:** Management clarified calculations regarding Quickmill integration and the bifurcation between manufacturing and trading segments.
- **Kunal on Environmental Engineering/ZLD positioning:** Management clarified that the SAEL order is for air pollution control (fumes in solar manufacturing), while Bioconserve handles water ZLD, primarily targeting the textile sector initially before expanding to pharma and chemicals.
- **Sarveswara (Investor) on land bank:** Management stated they are looking for a buyer for the Surat factory land bank if an attractive deal arises.

### One-line takeaway
Batliboi reported a strong 80% YoY revenue jump to Rs 125 crore and positive PAT in Q1 FY27, backed by a robust Rs 618 crore order backlog and strategic thrusts into automation and renewable-linked environmental engineering.
