# Praj Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While the company reports improved performance YoY and sees growth in international markets and new segments (GenX, CBG), domestic Greenfield projects face funding and regulatory headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (Q2):** Rs 849 crore (vs Rs 842 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **PBT (Q2):** Rs 36.1 crore (vs Rs 29.6 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT (Q2):** Rs 23.1 crore (vs Rs 19.2 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,560 crore (vs Rs 1,480 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 57.1 crore (vs Rs 39.2 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 34.7 crore (vs Rs 24.62 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Order Intake (Q2):** Rs 715 crore.
*   **Order Backlog (Sept 2026):** Rs 4,460 crore.
*   **Cash in hand:** Rs 533 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Mix:** 69% Bioenergy, 21% Engineering, 10% PHS.
*   **Export Revenue:** 38% of total revenue.
*   **Praj GenX:** Mangaluru facility approved by 18 international customers; 4 framework agreements operationalized.
*   **CBG Business:** Board approved up to Rs 100 crore investment for a JV with BPCL.
*   **Bio-IBA:** Construction of India's first bio-IBA plant expected to be completed by end of 2026.
*   **Lifecycle Services:** Continued steady growth quarter-over-quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Order Bookings:** Management expects better order bookings in H2 FY27, driven by CBG, international markets, and GenX.
*   **Long-term Vision:** Management maintains the vision of reaching Rs 10,000 crore revenue by 2030, though some inflection points are delayed.
*   **GenX Breakeven:** Management expects PBT breakeven within 12 months, targeting Rs 500-600 crore revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Domestic Market:** Greenfield projects face funding challenges and ethanol supply-demand imbalances. Customers are shifting focus to Brownfield solutions and feedstock diversification (grain modules).
*   **International Market:** Positive development in USA, Brazil, Argentina, SE Asia, and Africa; opportunity pipeline of ~Rs 1,000 crore.
*   **CBG:** New GOBARdhan policy is driving interest in Napier grass, rice straw, and press mud-based plants.
*   **Data Centers:** Precision fabrication for data centers is emerging as a growth opportunity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **EBITDA Margin:** ~6.2% for the quarter. Management believes margins have bottomed out.
*   **GenX Costs:** Operating expenses are ~Rs 25-30 crore/quarter; including interest and depreciation, the total is ~Rs 45 crore/quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **CBG JV:** Praj to invest Rs 100 crore (matched by BPCL). Potential for 8-10 plants over 3 years.
*   **GenX Facility:** 7 sheds total; 2 currently equipped. Additional sheds to be activated as volumes increase.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Position:** Rs 533 crore cash in hand as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** OMCs excluded sugarcane juice, sugar syrup, and B-Heavy molasses from recent ethanol procurement tenders.
*   **Execution:** Project funding challenges for domestic Greenfield projects are impacting backlog conversion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Atul Tiwari (JPMorgan):** Asked if EBITDA margins have bottomed out. Management confirmed they have and that GenX breakeven is expected within 12 months.
*   **Shailesh Kanani:** Asked about GenX order inflow guidance. Management reiterated they are on track for the envisaged order booking for FY27.
*   **Aditya Mongia (Kotak):** Asked about CBG JV potential. Management indicated 8-10 plants are possible with the Rs 100 crore investment, with a 12-month execution cycle per project.
*   **Manish Goyal:** Asked about GenX breakeven revenue. Management clarified that ~Rs 500-600 crore revenue is required for PBT breakeven.
*   **P. Jha:** Asked why BPCL is entering JVs with others. Management noted that OMCs want to execute multiple projects in parallel and one JV may not be sufficient for their scale.

### One-line takeaway
Praj is navigating domestic Greenfield headwinds by pivoting toward Brownfield, international markets, and new growth engines like CBG and GenX, with management signaling that margins have bottomed out.
