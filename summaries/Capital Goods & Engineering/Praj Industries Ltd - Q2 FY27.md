# Praj Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Sequential improvement amid challenging environment; strong pipeline visibility in CBG, GenX, and international bioenergy; management confirms margin bottom and GenX breakeven within 12 months.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 849 crore (Rs 8.49 billion) vs Rs 842 crore YoY (+0.8%)
- PBT: Rs 36.1 crore (Rs 361 million) vs Rs 29.6 crore YoY
- PAT: Rs 23.1 crore (Rs 231 million) vs Rs 19.2 crore YoY (+20.3%)
- H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 1,560 crore (Rs 15.6 billion) vs Rs 1,480 crore YoY
- Export share: 38% of revenue
- Segment mix: Bioenergy 69%, Engineering 21%, PHS 10%
- Q2 order intake: Rs 715 crore (62% domestic, 38% international)
- Order backlog (Sep 2026): Rs 4,460 crore (63% domestic)
- Cash on hand: Rs 533 crore (Rs 5.33 billion)
- GenX quarterly opex: ~Rs 25-30 crore (excl. interest & depreciation); ~Rs 44-45 crore incl.
- GenX breakeven target: Rs 500-600 crore revenue over next 12 months
- CBG JV investment: Up to Rs 100 crore (matched by BPCL) for 8-10 plants
- International bioenergy pipeline: ~Rs 1,000 crore over next few quarters

### Highlights
- Domestic 1G ethanol orders: mostly Brownfield (efficiency, co-products, feedstock diversification via grain modules)
- MoRTH amended rules to recognise E100 as fuel for certified flex-fuel vehicles
- OMCs excluded sugarcane juice, syrup, B-Heavy molasses from 2026-27 ethanol tenders; only C-Heavy molasses and approved grains permitted
- Greenfield domestic projects face funding delays; clarity expected by end of FY27
- International ethanol markets (USA, Brazil, Argentina, Southeast Asia, Africa) developing positively
- Bio-IBA plant (India’s first) construction to complete by end-2026; engine testing underway
- Lifecycle services growing steadily QoQ
- GOBARdhan Policy driving CBG inquiry pipeline (Napier grass, rice straw, press mud)
- Board approved in-principle Rs 100 crore investment via 50:50 JV with BPCL for CBG plants
- SAF: Detailed engineering for ethanol-to-SAF plant progressing; FID expected Q1 FY28
- GenX: Mangaluru facility approved by 18 international customers; 4 framework agreements operationalised
- GenX expanding into precision fabrication for data centers (server frames, cooling piping)
- ZLD/PHS steady; new opportunities in batteries, semiconductors, solar cells

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects better order bookings in H2 FY27 driven by CBG, international bioenergy, GenX
- GenX orders to trickle from Q3 FY27; revenue impact from Q4 FY27 onwards
- GenX PBT breakeven within 12 months (from Q3 FY27) at Rs 500-600 crore revenue
- CBG JV incorporation in 2-3 months; first plant commercial production after 15 months; 8-10 plants over 2-3 years
- Bio-IBA commercialisation demonstration by early next year
- SAF project FID expected Q1 FY28
- INR 10,000 crore revenue by 2030 vision maintained (some inflection points delayed)
- EBITDA margins bottomed at ~6.2% in Q2; improvement expected from GenX ramp-up and higher revenue

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic ethanol: Feedstock restrictions increase need for multi-feed flexibility
- E100 recognition paves way for long-term ethanol capacity creation
- Global ethanol blending mandates rising (energy self-sufficiency driver)
- CBG: GOBARdhan Policy provides pricing clarity, offtake guarantee, feedstock specification
- Data center demand growing for modular precision fabrication
- SAF: Global opportunities pursued
- ZLD/PHS: Ultra-high purity water and ZLD mandated for battery, semiconductor, solar cell manufacturing

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin ~6.2% (confirmed bottomed out)
- GenX investment continuing: ~Rs 25-30 crore/quarter operating expenses; ~Rs 44-45 crore/quarter incl. interest & depreciation
- Margin improvement hinges on GenX revenue ramp-up and overall revenue growth
- No specific raw material cost commentary

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- GenX: 7 sheds at Mangaluru; 2 equipped; additional sheds activatable with minimal capex as volumes grow
- CBG JV: Up to Rs 100 crore equity from Praj; total project capex ~Rs 800-1,000 crore for 8-10 plants over time
- Bio-IBA plant construction completing end-2026
- No other major capex disclosed

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash: Rs 533 crore (Sep 30, 2026)
- Order backlog: Rs 4,460 crore
- GenX lease: 10-year term (3 years elapsed); rental split into interest/depreciation per Ind-AS for 7 more years
- No net debt or detailed debt figures disclosed

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Domestic Greenfield ethanol projects stalled by funding challenges
- Ethanol supply-demand imbalance slowing backlog conversion
- Projects based on sugary feedstocks (juice, syrup, B-Heavy) challenged by OMC tender exclusions
- CBG: Round-the-year feedstock availability critical bottleneck
- Bio-IBA: Policy/blending mandate not controlled by Praj; dependent on government test results
- GenX: Long revenue conversion cycles on some orders
- International pipeline conversion uncertainty

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Atul Tiwari (JPMorgan) – Order inflow & GenX timing**: Management cited three H2 drivers – CBG, international bioenergy, GenX. GenX orders from Q3, revenue from Q4.
2. **Atul Tiwari – Margin trajectory**: Confirmed EBITDA margin bottomed at ~6.2%; improvement from GenX revenue and higher topline.
3. **Amit Anwani – CBG JV margins & data center scope**: Stressed known partner (BPCL), technology credibility, offtake assurance, multi-product valorisation (bio-manure, bio-bitumen). Data center opportunity significant with existing hyperscaler; engaging new customers.
4. **Shailesh Kanani – GenX guidance & CBG strategy**: Reaffirmed FY27 GenX order booking guidance (Rs 800-1,000 crore) and breakeven. CBG JV driven by policy clarity, proven tech (5,000 tonnes CBG in 500 days), multi-feedstock design. TAM in thousands of crores over 5-10 years.
5. **Aditya Mongia (Kotak) – CBG JV order potential & international pipeline**: JV active in 2-3 months; 8-10 projects identified; Rs 500-600 crore ordering opportunity in year one. International pipeline ~Rs 1,000 crore (bioenergy only, GenX additional). GenX PBT breakeven within 12 months.
6. **Manish Goyal – GenX breakeven math**: Clarified annual cost ~Rs 140-150 crore (not Rs 180 crore); breakeven revenue Rs 500-600 crore over next 12 months. Additional sheds need minimal capex. Lease runs 7 more years.

### One-line takeaway
Praj delivers sequential resilience with a clear line of sight to margin recovery and GenX breakeven, backed by a diversifying order pipeline across CBG, international bioenergy, and data center fabrication, though domestic Greenfield ethanol headwinds persist.
