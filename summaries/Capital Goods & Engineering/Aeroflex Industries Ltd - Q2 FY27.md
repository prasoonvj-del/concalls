# Aeroflex Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Quarterly revenue crossed the Rs 200 crore milestone for the first time, supported by strong demand across verticals and scaling of the liquid cooling business.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 207.68 crore (up 87% YoY)
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 353.66 crore (up 81% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 50.34 crore (up 94% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 24.27%
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 83.83 crore (up 103% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 23.77% (vs 21.19% in H1 FY26)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 36.56 crore (up 157% YoY)
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 17.6%
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 55.35 crore
- **PAT Margin (H1 FY27):** 15.65%
- **Metal Flexible Hose Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** 40% YoY
- **Assemblies & Value-Added Products Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** 44% YoY
- **Liquid Cooling Vertical Contribution (H1 FY27):** 24% of revenue
- **Metal Bellows Revenue:** Rs 6 crore in Q2 FY27 (Rs 9 crore in H1 FY27)
- **Hyd-Air Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 11.5 crore
- **Depreciation Impact:** Change from WDV to SLM method effective April 1, 2026, reduced depreciation expense by Rs 8.41 crore for both Q2 and H1.

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 200 crore quarterly revenue milestone for the first time.
- Liquid cooling vertical contributed 24% to H1 FY27 revenue, marking an evolution into an integrated advanced flow control and liquid cooling solutions provider.
- Announced the merger of wholly-owned subsidiary Hyd-Air Engineering into Aeroflex to streamline operations, optimize costs, and leverage infrastructure.
- Granted a patent for automating the generation of 3D CAD models for braided hose assemblies.
- SS flexible flow capacity completed up to 20 million meters per annum.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 overall sales growth of almost 60%, closing between Rs 715 crore to Rs 725 crore at the least.
- Skid assemblies targeted to achieve Rs 150 crore to Rs 200 crore for FY27.
- FY30 top-line target upgraded to close to Rs 1,500 crore, with EBITDA margins maintained at around 25% and slightly upwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rapid advancement of AI and high-performance computing is accelerating the shift from air cooling to liquid cooling in data centers globally and in India.
- Global liquid cooling market is upwards of $4 billion to $5 billion, growing at >30% YoY; Vertiv Energy is the biggest global and Indian player.
- Ports and terminals, firefighting, and data centers are seeing significant demand for hose assemblies.
- Aeroflex works exclusively with one large US multinational for the India liquid cooling market; international expansion discussions are underway.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Freight and forwarding expenses increased significantly due to international shipping cost rises.
- Change in depreciation policy from WDV to SLM boosted reported profits.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Announced a total capex of Rs 202 crore:
  - Stainless steel flexible flow solutions: Expansion of 10 million meters per annum (mix of brownfield at Taloja and a new hose assemblies facility) taking total capacity to 30 million meters per annum by March 2028, with an investment of approximately Rs 124 crore. Peak revenue potential at peak utilization is Rs 850 crore to Rs 900 crore (assuming 70-75% for assemblies).
  - Skid assemblies: Raising capacity from 15,000 to 30,000 units per annum by March 2028, with an additional investment of Rs 78 crore. (Current capacity expansion to 15,000 units expected to complete by Dec 2026, with 12,000 already commissioned).
- Peak capacity utilization of the total Rs 202 crore capex expected to generate incremental revenue of Rs 500 crore to Rs 550 crore per year.
- Funding via internal accruals, bridge loan financing, and equity fundraise if required.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet has negligible leverage. Funding for capex prioritizes internal accruals and bridge/long-term debt before equity.
- Trade receivables grew slower than sales, and working capital receivable days reduced in H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- International shipping and freight costs have increased significantly.
- Strict international regulations, checks, and audits mean international supply timelines are difficult to predict.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raman KV (Sequent Investments) asked about Hyd-Air cost savings and skid order book/pipeline.** Management stated exact margin savings numbers are difficult to quantify pre-merger but expect a couple of percentage increase; order visibility is secured for a minimum 3-month horizon under confidentiality.
2. **Nikhil Chaudhary (Toro Wealth) asked about international liquid cooling opportunities and capacity constraints.** Management confirmed talks with international players are progressing well, and the 30,000-unit skid capacity expansion accounts for both domestic and international markets.
3. **Tej Patel (Niveshaay) asked if new skid capacities are exclusively for the existing domestic customer.** Management clarified exclusivity remains for the Indian market, while certain capacities are earmarked for international markets where no exclusivity applies.
4. **Alisha Mahawla (Trust MF) asked about skid capacity utilization and international realizations.** Management expects 40-50% utilization for skids in FY27 and noted international realizations are expected to be higher than domestic once finalized.
5. **Shreyansh Talesara (Equentis) asked about upgraded FY29/FY30 guidance.** Management upgraded the FY30 top line target to ~Rs 1,500 crore with ~25% EBITDA margins.
6. **Jainam Doshi (KRIIS PMS) asked about compatibility with Vertiv's 800 VDC data centers and skid average realization fluctuations.** Management explained designs are collaborative with Vertiv, and average realizations per skid fluctuate (ranging from Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 5 lakh) based on project complexity mix in a given quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Aeroflex delivered a record Q2 FY27 with revenue crossing Rs 200 crore, driven by strong core hose demand and surging traction in data center liquid cooling skids, prompting an upgrade to its FY30 outlook and a Rs 202 crore capex announcement.
