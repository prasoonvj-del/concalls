# Engineers India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by an all-time high order book and a favorable shift in revenue mix toward higher-margin consultancy services.

### Key numbers
*   **Order Book:** Rs 20,421 crore (as of Sept 30, 2026).
*   **Order Inflow (H1 FY27):** Rs 7,428 crore (Consultancy: Rs 5,762 crore; LSTK: Rs 1,666 crore).
*   **Order Inflow (Current/As of call date):** Rs 8,100 crore.
*   **Turnover (Q2):** Rs 802 crore (Consultancy: Rs 594 crore; Turnkey: Rs 208 crore).
*   **PBT (Q2):** Rs 229 crore (vs Rs 145 crore in Q1).
*   **PAT (Q2):** Rs 180 crore (vs Rs 109 crore in Q1).
*   **EPS (Q2):** Rs 3.20.
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q2):** 27% (vs 19% in Q1).

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Mix Shift:** In H1 FY27, the revenue mix shifted to 68% Consultancy and 32% Turnkey, compared to 47% and 53% respectively in H1 FY26.
*   **Consultancy Performance:** Segment profit margin reached 30% in Q2; management stated this was due to the business mix shift and not one-time items.
*   **Turnkey Performance:** Segment margins were 4-5% in Q2; management expects margins to improve as projects gain momentum.
*   **Other Income:** Rs 65 crore, including dividends from NRL (Rs 21 crore) and CEIL (Rs 11 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects Rs 4,200–4,300 crore for FY27, with ~10% YoY growth thereafter.
*   **Order Inflow:** Management expects to cross Rs 8,000 crore for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management aims to maintain a 25% annual margin for the Consultancy segment and 15-16% for EBITDA.
*   **Long-term:** Management expects a healthier bottom line in coming years due to the Rs 20,000+ crore order book.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Middle East:** Situation remains unstable due to geopolitical issues; management is bidding for jobs but expects a wait of several months for stabilization.
*   **Domestic:** Strong pipeline in AP refinery, Paradip Phase 2, strategic storage, and coal gasification.
*   **Diversification:** Infrastructure contributes 20-25% of order inflow; company is also expanding into data centers, non-ferrous, and fertilizer sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Consultancy segment margins are structurally higher than Turnkey.
*   Turnkey margins are currently lower (4-5%) as new projects are in early stages.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Company is implementing a Compressed Biogas (CBG) plant with its own investment.
*   Focusing on training manpower for new segments like nuclear energy.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical instability in the Middle East and Africa affecting project timelines and tender flow.
*   Project execution delays (e.g., land acquisition for Paradip Phase 2).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is the 30% consultancy margin sustainable?** Management clarified there were no one-offs; while 30% was the quarterly figure, they conservatively guide for 25% annually.
*   **Q: Will you revise order inflow guidance?** Management confirmed they will cross the Rs 8,000 crore target and may revise guidance next quarter.
*   **Q: Status of Dangote project?** Management stated work is progressing well with teams deployed on engineering activities.
*   **Q: Opportunities in nuclear?** Management noted it is in early study/tendering phases and will be a significant opportunity in the next financial year.
*   **Q: Why is the Middle East order book limited?** Management stated the region is unstable, but they are bidding for contracts and will update in a quarter or two.

### One-line takeaway
Engineers India has achieved a record order book and strong margin expansion by pivoting toward high-margin consultancy, with management expressing confidence in crossing Rs 8,000 crore in order inflows for FY27 despite geopolitical headwinds.
