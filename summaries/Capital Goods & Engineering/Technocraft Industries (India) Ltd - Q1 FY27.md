# Technocraft Industries (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by robust US scaffolding demand, record drum closure margins, and strong engineering services growth.

### Key numbers
- Steel Scaffolding revenue: Rs. 240 crore (consolidated for Q1)
- Mach One (Aluminum Formwork) revenue: Rs. 165 crore (consolidated for Q1)
- Drum Closure EBIT margin: ~43%
- Defence order book: Rs. 20-21 crore (including ~Rs. 10 crore for missile canisters)
- Plastic Drum Closure sales: Rs. 14.5 crore (previous quarter)
- Fabric division machinery asset sale: Rs. 25-30 crore; working capital release: Rs. 15-20 crore
- Unallocated income MTM gains: Rs. 27 crore

### Highlights
- Aluminum extrusion plant operated at 100% capacity (over 95% utilization).
- Drum closure segment achieved its highest-ever revenue, absolute EBIT, and EBIT margin (~43%).
- Textile fabric division was shut down, and equipment was sold.
- Defence JT Coolers received export orders from Israel worth ~Rs. 20 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided sustainable EBIT margins of upwards of 15% for Scaffolding and Engineering Services, and upwards of 30% for Drum Closures.
- Management stated no significant capex is planned for the year besides regular maintenance; Phase 2 of the CSN plant is eyed for next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Scaffolding demand in the US remained strong, driven by AI chip manufacturing, semicon plants, and conventional energy installations.
- Middle East scaffolding sales declined due to war and shipping difficulties (accounting for 2-3% of total sales).
- Mach One demand in India is strong across residential, commercial, GCCs, and data centers in 7 major cities.
- Engineering outsourcing demand in the US is buoyant, supported by AI transformation and manufacturing automation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Rupee depreciation against the dollar boosted absolute export realizations for Drum Closures.
- Aluminum prices increased during the quarter; in-house backward integration helped mitigate the impact.
- US Section 232 tariff on scaffolding stands at 50% for all countries except China (which faces an additional 25% under Section 301, totaling 75%). Management expects a scaffolding tariff refund of ~$3 million.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No significant new capacity capex planned for the year; maintenance capex only.
- Extrusion plant utilization is >95%, Mach One is at 75-80%, and Scaffolding is at ~95%.
- Management stated capacity can be expanded within 3 months using existing infrastructure in Mumbai and China if required.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Shutdown of the Fabric division released Rs. 15-20 crore in working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global geopolitical turbulence, war, shipping disruptions, freight costs, and tariffs.
- Slow construction activity in Europe due to the Russia-Ukraine war and Germany slowdown.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prateek Bhandari asked for Scaffolding and Formwork revenue split:** Management reported Steel Scaffolding at Rs. 240 crore and Mach One at Rs. 165 crore.
- **Siva asked about Drum Closure 43% margin sustainability:** Management stated 43% is not the new normal due to external volatility, maintaining a sustainable target of upwards of 30%.
- **Rahul Kumar asked about US scaffolding capacity constraints:** Management noted they can expand capacity within 3 months using existing floor space in Mumbai and China.
- **Ajay asked about Defence JT Cooler approval status:** Management confirmed the product is fully developed, approved by DRDO and an Israeli partner, but noted quantum is hard to estimate given defence sales dynamics.
- **Prolin Nandu asked about US tariff differentials vs China:** Management stated they face a 50% tariff while China faces 75%, giving them a 25% tariff advantage over Chinese manufacturers, though Chinese steel cost advantage narrows the final landed cost gap.

### One-line takeaway
Technocraft posted a strong Q1 FY27 underpinned by robust US infrastructure demand, record profitability in drum closures, and full capacity utilization in its aluminum extrusion business.
