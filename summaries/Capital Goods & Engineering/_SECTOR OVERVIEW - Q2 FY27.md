# Capital Goods & Engineering: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings commentary across the Capital Goods & Engineering sector reflects broad operational resilience, strong order book building, and robust second-half (H2) execution expectations. A majority of sector managements report healthy order backlogs anchored in domestic public infrastructure spending, power transmission, railways modernization, space, and defense indigenization. 

However, clear cross-currents exist. Top-line execution and margin trajectories are constrained by two primary friction points: persistent commodity cost inflation (steel, copper, aluminum, resins) and global logistical/geopolitical disruptions (Middle East conflict, Red Sea/Hormuz shipping bottlenecks, elevated ocean freight rates, and customer-side project pushouts). 

Seasonally, H2 is expected to contribute between 55% and 65% of full-year revenues across equipment builders, fabricators, and turnkey solution providers. Managements are relying on price hike realizations, operating leverage, and backward integration to restore margins in H2.

---

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus & Areas of Demand Strength
- **Power Transmission & Grid Expansion:** Managements report high global and domestic momentum. **Karamtara Engineering** points to a decade-long power transmission supercycle driven by renewable energy evacuation and grid upgrades (765 kV). **Quality Power Electrical Equipments** highlights severe global supply shortages in specialized electrical equipment and insulators, while **Kirloskar Brothers** sees elevated demand across power and utility sectors.
- **Space, Defense & High-Tech Indigenization:** Space and defense allocations are creating record order backlogs. **INOX India** achieved an all-time high order backlog of INR 2,268 crore, with space orders exceeding INR 1,100 crore (driven by U.S. aerospace customers). **Admach Systems** (nuclear/defense), **Technocrats Plasma Systems** (defense optics/lasers), **Shree Refrigerations** (naval HVAC), **Triveni Power Transmission** (naval gearboxes), and **Kirloskar Advanced Systems** report strong pipeline traction.
- **Railways Modernization & Safety:** Railway infrastructure and rolling stock modernization remain key growth drivers. **Concord Control Systems** is capitalizing on Kavach safety deployments and intelligent loco systems with an INR 846 crore order book. **Cosmic CRF**, **Oriental Rail Infrastructure**, and **Diffusion Engineers** (loco shell qualification) report accelerating railway execution.
- **Data Centers & High-Density Power/Cooling:** Engineering components for data centers are emerging as a major growth adjacency. **Standard Engineering Technology** (GScale), **Omnitech Engineering**, **Carborundum Universal**, and **Sanghvi Movers** report increasing order inflows from data center infrastructure builds.

### Divergence & Sector-Specific Headwinds
- **State-Level Infrastructure Delays & Cash Flows:** While central capex remains healthy, state-level government spending and contractor cash flows in states like Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Bihar have faced delays, dragging down near-term execution for **AJAX Engineering**, **Cosmic CRF**, and **WPIL Ltd**.
- **Chemical & Agrochemical Softness:** Capital spending in traditional global chemical and agrochemical sectors remains subdued, pressuring equipment providers such as **GMM Pfaudler** and **HLE Glascoat** in Europe, though partially offset by domestic pharmaceutical/peptide demand.
- **Textile Machinery & Export Friction:** **LMW Ltd** notes cautious sentiment in textile machinery due to financial constraints and lulls in export destinations like Bangladesh and Turkey, alongside electronics availability bottlenecks.
- **Domestic Greenfield Bioenergy Funding:** **Praj Industries** flags funding and regulatory hurdles for domestic Greenfield ethanol projects (due to OMC feedstock allocation shifts), prompting a pivot toward Brownfield modules, international markets, and Compressed Biogas (CBG).

## Costs, raw materials & margins

- **Input Cost Pressures:** Broad-based inflation in raw materials—primarily steel, copper, aluminum, resins, and electronics/PCBs—weighed on gross margins during Q2 (**Ador Welding**, **AJAX Engineering**, **Greaves Cotton**, **Carborundum Universal**, **Kirloskar Oil Engines**, **LMW Ltd**). 
- **Freight & Shipping Constraints:** Export-oriented firms faced significant margin hits and transit delays from soaring sea freight costs and geopolitical shipping disruptions in the Red Sea and Middle East (**AIA Engineering** saw export freight reach ~10% of sales; **Tega Industries**, **Shakti Pumps**, **Sanghvi Movers**, **JNK India**, and **WPIL Ltd** reported delayed customer acceptances/dispatches).
- **Pass-Through Mechanics & Lagged Recovery:** Companies with fixed-price contracts absorbed short-term margin contraction but are implementing calibrated price hikes. Managements at **Hy-Tech Engineers**, **Diffusion Engineers**, **Ador Welding**, and **Greaves Cotton** express confidence that margin recovery will take full effect in Q3 and Q4 as cost pass-through lag fades.
- **Margin Protection via Backward Integration:** Companies leveraging in-house component manufacturing report expanding margin profiles despite input cost inflation (**Admach Systems** achieved ~23% EBITDA margin by in-housing machine parts; **Concord Control Systems**, **Oriental Rail Infrastructure**, and **Technocrats Plasma** also cite backward integration benefits).

## Capex & capacity

Managements are executing targeted capital expenditure plans to support order execution:
- **Quality Power Electrical Equipments** is launching a ~INR 700 crore QIP to fund cable backward integration, insulator expansion (WS), and debt repayment.
- **ISGEC Heavy Engineering** is executing a planned INR 502 crore capex program to yield INR 1,200 crore in incremental annual revenue by FY29.
- **Carborundum Universal** maintained its consolidated capex guidance at INR 400 crore, focusing on semiconductor equipment components and thin-wheel production.
- **Shakti Pumps** is advancing an INR 1,700 crore total capex framework, including a 500 MW solar panel plant (trial runs ongoing) and a 2.2 GW solar cell facility planned for FY28.
- **Jyoti CNC Automation** secured approval for over INR 1,020 crore of capital investment over 5 years under the Government PLI (ECMS) scheme.
- **Standard Engineering Technology** is expanding operational space by 3-4 lakh sq ft in core business and building a 1.5-1.6 million sq ft greenfield facility.
- **Technocrats Plasma Systems** is scaling manufacturing space 5x by moving to a 20,000 sq ft facility in December 2026.
- **Gabion Technologies** is expanding total group capacity to 18,000 MTPA by late January 2027.

---

## What changed since last quarter

- **Guidance Revisions:** **Tega Industries** revised its equipment business revenue growth guidance downward from 25% to 15% due to customer project pushouts, while maintaining Molycop's 5% volume growth target. Most other companies (**Hy-Tech Engineers**, **Elgi Equipments**, **JNK India**, **Tempsens Instruments**, **Cosmic CRF**, **Carborundum Universal**, **Technocrats Plasma Systems**) reaffirmed their full-year top-line and margin guidance.
- **Tone Shifts & Delivery:** 
  - **Hy-Tech Engineers** shifted from reporting a "temporary cost lag" in Q1 to confirming "fully finalized" price revisions with OEMs and opening its New Jersey US warehouse.
  - **Elgi Equipments** recognized INR 40 crore in tariff refunds (50-60% credited to material costs in Q2) and reached 35% geography coverage for its "Vayu" compressor launch.
  - **Tega Industries** confirmed that one-time acquisition transaction costs for Molycop have meaningfully tapered off.
  - **Tempsens Instruments** received ETL and CE approvals for its standard laboratory furnaces, with bulk dispatches starting in Q3 FY27.

## What to watch next quarter

1. **PM-KUSUM 2.0 Policy Approval:** Rollout timing and revised tender pricing for solar pump execution (**Shakti Pumps**).
2. **Kavach & Defense Approvals:** Trial completion and vendor clearance timelines for Kavach safety systems (**Concord Control Systems**) and loco shell/nuclear qualifications (**Diffusion Engineers**, **Admach Systems**).
3. **Capacity Commercialization:** Commissioning of new facilities in Q3/Q4, including **Standard Engineering's** GScale data center infrastructure plant (Dec 15 launch), **Technocrats Plasma's** 20,000 sq ft plant, and trial production at **Karamtara's** Saudi Arabia facility.
4. **Margin Catch-up:** Realization of price hikes pushing through lagged input cost inflation across welding, auto components, pumps, and heavy engines (**Ador Welding**, **Diffusion Engineers**, **Greaves Cotton**, **Kirloskar Oil Engines**).
5. **Overseas Subsidiaries Restructuring:** Execution of European entity turnarounds and closures (**GMM Pfaudler** UK/Germany restructuring, **Carborundum Universal** AWUKO asset sale completion and Foskor resolution).
