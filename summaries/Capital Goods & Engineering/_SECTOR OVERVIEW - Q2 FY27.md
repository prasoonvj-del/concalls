# Capital Goods & Engineering: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Capital Goods and Engineering sector in Q2 FY27 presents a sharp contrast between resilient top-line growth driven by heavy industrial capex (power transmission, data centers, railways, and defense) and severe margin friction caused by short-term cost inflation. Managements report record order books across several sub-segments—such as **Engineers India** (Rs 20,421 crore), **INOX India** (Rs 2,268 crore), **ISGEC Heavy Engineering** (Rs 10,273 crore consolidated), **HLE Glascoat** (Rs 1,087 crore), and **Zetwerk** (Rs 14,500 crore)—yet profitability was frequently squeezed by surging input costs (steel, copper, aluminum, zinc, resins) and high export freight rates.

Operationally, companies with exposure to data center infrastructure (**Cummins India**, **INOX India**, **Omnitech**, **Standard Engineering**, **Kilburn Engineering**, **TD Power Systems**), domestic power transmission supercycles (**Hitachi Energy India**, **Karamtara Engineering**, **Quality Power**), and defense/nuclear indigenization (**Admach Systems**, **Airfloa Rail**, **GEE Ltd**, **Poojaa Precision**, **Shree Refrigerations**) reported intense demand. However, domestic infrastructure execution faced temporary state-level payment/tendering slowdowns in regions like Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Bihar (**AJAX Engineering**, **Cosmic CRF**, **Dee Development**, **WPIL Ltd**), alongside geopolitical shipping disruptions in the Middle East.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus Drivers
*   **Data Centers & AI Infrastructure:** Broad agreement on explosive structural demand. **Cummins India** reported data centers contributed 30%–40% of domestic power generation sales. **TD Power Systems** noted global OEMs are booking production slots beyond 2030. **Standard Engineering** and **Kilburn Engineering** are expanding dedicated capacities for data center equipment and enclosures, while **INOX India** and **Omnitech** highlighted new component qualification pipelines.
*   **Power Transmission & Grid Expansion:** Strong consensus on a multi-year transmission supercycle driven by renewable energy evacuation and grid upgrades. **Hitachi Energy India** highlighted government approvals for 1150 kV AC transmission systems. **Karamtara Engineering** cited a decade-long supercycle targeting 1.4 million tons capacity. **Quality Power** noted global supply constraints for specialized electrical equipment and high demand for HVDC/FACTS.
*   **Railways & Defense Indigenization:** **Airfloa Rail** (Vande Bharat sleeper design win), **Concord Control** (Kavach safety systems), **Jupiter Wagons** (10,000 wagon order book and passenger coach expansion with Poland's PESA), and **Diffusion Engineers** (loco shell qualification) highlighted sustained Indian Railways capex. **Admach Systems** and **Shree Refrigerations** saw rising order visibility in nuclear and naval defense import substitution.

### Divergences & Headwinds
*   **State-Level Construction & Infrastructure:** **AJAX Engineering** noted near-term execution delays due to below-budgeted state government spending and delayed contractor payments in Maharashtra, MP, Rajasthan, and Bihar, though volume growth remained strong in Gujarat, UP, Telangana, and AP. **Action Construction Equipment (ACE)**, by contrast, reported robust post-September equipment demand, initiating FY27 revenue growth guidance of 18%–20%.
*   **Thermal & Renewable Project Finalizations:** **Dee Development Engineers** noted extremely slow tender/finalization timelines at state/PSU developers (BHEL, L&T), prompting a pivot to private developers like JSW and Adani. **Praj Industries** reported domestic greenfield bioenergy projects faced funding and regulatory delays, forcing a focus on brownfield retrofits.
*   **Wind & Solar Equipment:** **Inox Wind** withdrew specific FY27 revenue and margin targets due to strategic realignments and commodity inflation. **Oswal Pumps** lowered full-year revenue growth guidance to 10%–15% (from 20%–25%) due to delays in PM-KUSUM 2.0 rollout, whereas **Shakti Pumps** reported 7% YoY growth in Q2 solar pump dispatches while awaiting PM-KUSUM 2.0 clearance.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

*   **Steel & Base Metal Inflation:** Managements across the sector flagged sharp cost inflation in steel (rising to Rs 66–68/kg from Rs 48–50/kg earlier in the year), copper, aluminum, zinc, and resins during Q2. **Action Construction Equipment** implemented 7%–8% price hikes by September to counter steel costs, with further hikes planned for Q3/Q4. **Ador Welding** absorbed gross margin compression (dipping to 34.2%) before lag-adjusted price hikes took effect. **Diffusion Engineers** expects margin recovery by Q4 FY27 after passing on costs with a 1 to 1.5 quarter lag.
*   **Lagged Backlog Realizations:** Companies with fixed-price backlogs saw temporary margin squeezes. **Cummins India** suffered a ~400 bps QoQ gross margin contraction as deliveries were fulfilled under previously contracted prices while input costs surged. **KSB Ltd** noted fixed-price contracts pressuring margins, with standard price pass-through taking 6 to 9 months.
*   **Logistics & Freight Pressures:** **AIA Engineering** reported export freight costs surged to ~10% of sales (vs normal 7%–8%), suppressing EBITDA. **Fabtech Technologies**, **JNK India**, and **Jash Engineering** flagged geopolitical friction and shipping delays in the Red Sea and GCC belts, leaving finished goods in transit and delaying revenue recognition.
*   **Margin Expansion via Mix & Localization:** High-margin services and internal integration provided relief. **Engineers India** expanded Q2 EBITDA margin to 27% as revenue mix shifted to 68% consultancy. **Dee Development** expanded EBITDA margins 380 bps YoY to 20.1% via captive seamless pipe sourcing and higher job-work mix. **Zetwerk** expanded manufacturing segment gross margin to a historical high of 25.5%.

## Capex & capacity

*   **Aggressive Capacity Ramp-ups:**
    *   **Adroit Industries:** Expanding Pithampur machining capacity from 900,000 to 1,800,000 units (Q4) and Dewas forging capacity from 3,000 to 5,500 tonnes (Q1 FY28).
    *   **Bonfiglioli Transmissions:** Investing Rs 1,500 million in CY27 to build a heavy-duty gearbox plant in Talegaon, Pune.
    *   **ISGEC Heavy Engineering:** Executing a Rs 502 crore capex program across five facilities to yield Rs 1,200 crore in incremental annual revenue by FY29.
    *   **Jyoti CNC Automation:** Commencing a Rs 1,020 crore 5-year PLI (ECMS) investment, expanding foundry capacity from 1,500 to 5,000 tonnes/month.
    *   **TD Power Systems:** Constructing Plant 4 (Siemens Energy 2-pole generators) targeted for Q1 CY28 completion.
*   **Backward Integration & Tech Transfers:**
    *   **Oswal Pumps:** Proposed a Rs 455.85 crore 1.2 GW TOPCon solar cell plant at Karnal for internal consumption.
    *   **Shakti Pumps:** Trial production commenced on its 500 MW solar panel facility, with a 2.2 GW solar cell plant slated for FY28.
    *   **Quality Power Electrical:** Sangli global coil factory and Turkey PCS factory commenced operations; planning a Rs 700 crore QIP for insulator capacity and working capital.
    *   **Tempsens Instruments:** Five expansion projects underway with peak revenue potential of ~Rs 500 crore.

## What changed since last quarter

*   **Guidance Upgrades:**
    *   **Action Construction Equipment (ACE):** Initiated numerical FY27 revenue growth guidance of 18%–20% (up from previous wait-and-see stance).
    *   **TD Power Systems:** Raised FY27 revenue guidance to INR 2,900–3,000 crore (from INR 2,600 crore) and initiated FY28 guidance at INR 3,400–3,500 crore.
    *   **Carborundum Universal:** Revised Ceramics segment sales growth guidance upward to 25%–28% (from 23%–25%).
*   **Guidance Cuts / Withdrawals:**
    *   **Inox Wind:** Withdrew specific FY27 revenue (75% YoY) and EBITDA margin (20%–22%) targets; introduced FY28 revenue guidance of ~Rs 7,500 crore.
    *   **Kilburn Engineering:** Lowered FY27 revenue guidance to Rs 600–650 crore (from ~Rs 700 crore) due to project decision delays.
    *   **Oswal Pumps:** Lowered full-year revenue growth guidance to 10%–15% (from 20%–25%) and backed off margin targets due to PM-KUSUM 2.0 delays.
    *   **Tega Industries:** Reduced equipment business revenue growth guidance to 15% (from 25%) due to customer project pushouts.
*   **Tone Shifts & Execution Deliveries:**
    *   **Dee Development:** Fully utilized Rs 225 crore preferential issue proceeds to repay debt and cleared Q1 Middle East dispatch spillovers.
    *   **Hy-Tech Engineers:** Finalized OEM price revisions, shifting tone from Q1 cost-lag caution to full recovery in H2.
    *   **Jupiter Wagons:** Shifted from Q1 transition-cautious to high confidence, ramping execution to 600–800 wagons/month.
    *   **GE Power India:** Received NCLT approval for the Durgapur manufacturing business demerger to JSW Energy.

## What to watch next quarter

1. **Pass-Through of Raw Material Price Hikes:** Watch whether lagged price revisions in **Cummins India**, **Ador Welding**, **Diffusion Engineers**, **Greaves Cotton**, and **KSB Ltd** successfully restore EBITDA margins in Q3/Q4.
2. **PM-KUSUM 2.0 Scheme Clearances:** Track central government tender releases and state allocations for **Oswal Pumps** and **Shakti Pumps**, which are critical for H2 volume acceleration.
3. **Commissioning of Major Expanded Capacities:**
    *   **Hy-Tech Engineers:** Commissioning of Pithampur machining expansion (Q4).
    *   **Technocrats Plasma:** Commercial production start at its 5x larger 20,000 sq ft facility in December 2026.
    *   **Diffusion Engineers:** Final operationalization of expanded heavy engineering lines by Q4.
    *   **Msafe Equipments:** Commercialization of formwork segment in December 2026.
4. **Resolution of Geopolitical Shipping Friction:** Monitor container transit times and ocean freight rates affecting export deliveries for **AIA Engineering**, **Fabtech Technologies**, **Jash Engineering**, **Texmaco Rail**, and **WPIL Ltd**.
5. **Defense & Nuclear Tender Conversions:** Track GEM portal and railway tender outcomes for **Behari Lal Engineering** (NABL lab integration), **Airfloa Rail**, and **Engineers India** (nuclear study conversions).
