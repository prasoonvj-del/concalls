# Capital Goods & Engineering: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is in a **high-growth, high-investment phase** driven by a structural upturn in domestic capex (railways, power, defence, data centres) and a broadening export footprint. Order books are at multi-year highs for the majority of companies — **ISGEC** (Rs 10,273 cr consolidated), **Zetwerk** (INR 14,500 cr), **Jupiter Wagons** (Rs 5,850 cr), **Karamtara** (Rs 6,500 cr), **Tega Industries** (Rs 13,976 cr legacy + Molycop), **TD Power Systems** (INR 23.19 bn H1 booking) — providing 2–3 years of revenue visibility. Revenue growth is broad-based: **Action Construction** (+20.5% YoY), **TD Power Systems** (+91% standalone H1), **Concord Control** (+95% YoY), **Standard Engineering** (+62% YoY), **Karamtara** (+62% YoY). However, margins are diverging: companies with strong pricing power, export mix, or backward integration (**Elecon**, **TD Power**, **Technocraft**, **RHI Magnesita**, **Zetwerk**) are expanding EBITDA margins, while those with fixed-price project backlogs, high commodity intensity, or lagged pass-through (**Cummins India**, **Kirloskar Oil Engines**, **Oswal Pumps**, **Triveni Turbine**, **AIA Engineering**) are seeing compression. Capex is running hot across the board — new foundries, gearbox plants, solar cell lines, data-centre component factories, and overseas facilities (Saudi, Poland, US) — funded largely by internal accruals and IPO proceeds. Working capital cycles remain elongated for project-execution names, but rental/annuity models (**Sanghvi Movers**, **Msafe**) are demonstrating superior cash conversion.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Indian capital goods has historically made money by building engineered equipment (pumps, turbines, gearboxes, wagons, cranes) for domestic infrastructure and industrial capex cycles, selling largely through dealers/OEMs with limited aftermarket monetisation. Over the last ~5 years the model has shifted toward: (1) import substitution in high-barrier segments (defence, nuclear, railways signalling, high-voltage transmission), (2) backward integration into raw materials (captive mines, solar cells, foundries) to protect margins, (3) export-led scaling in products where India has a cost/tech edge (castings, forgings, refractories, scaffolding), and (4) early moves into rental/annuity revenue (crane leasing, scaffolding rental, BESS build-own-operate) [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **Import substitution & indigenisation (defence, nuclear, rail signalling)** – *scaling* – **HLE Glascoat** (nuclear piping plant at Anjar, Fehmarnbelt tunnel order), **BEML/Concord** (Kavach, MSDAC, battery/hydrogen loco), **Jash Engineering** (loco shell qualification), **Triveni Power** (marine gearbox sole Indian OEM), **Bharat Forge/MTAR** (not in set but referenced) – multi-year qualification cycles now converting to orders; expands TAM and locks in decadal revenue.
- **Data-centre infrastructure (cooling, power, precision fabrication)** – *early/scaling* – **TD Power Systems** (Siemens 2-pole generator plant for data centres, OEM slots booked to 2030), **KSB** (trial orders with Schneider for cooling), **Tempsens** (fuel-cell sensor dispatches started, OEM target 15% of revenue FY28), **Standard Engineering** (GScale secondary-fluid network machinery install Dec 2026, Rs 250 cr FY27 target), **Kilburn** (Rs 25 cr capex for data-centre enclosures, Vertiv Rs 90 cr pipeline) – new high-margin vertical with long lead times but sticky demand.
- **Green energy transition (solar cells, BESS, hydrogen, wind O&M)** – *scaling* – **Oswal Pumps** (1.2 GW TOPCon cell plant, Rs 456 cr capex, commercial Apr 2028), **Shakti Pumps** (2.2 GW cell plant FY28, 500 MW panel trial runs), **Jupiter Wagons** (BESS order book >500 MWh, Rs 500+ cr, target Rs 1,000 cr FY27), **Inox Wind** (Wind World 4.5 GW O&M acquisition), **Karamtara** (PEB for data centres, solar tracker piles), **Praj** (CBG JV with BPCL, Rs 100 cr each) – policy-driven (PM-KUSUM, Green Energy Corridor, National Green Hydrogen Mission) with visible order pipelines.
- **Backward integration into critical raw materials** – *early* – **RHI Magnesita** (Chiraipani quartzite mine 40k tpa operational, Bhikampali next quarter, 3–3.5% EBITDA delta), **INOX India** (captive foundry scaling), **AIA Engineering** (Ghana/China expansions on hold but land banked), **Technocraft** (Aurangabad extrusion Phase 2 FY28, Rs 120 cr) – reduces commodity volatility and improves margin predictability.
- **Export market deepening (US, Middle East, Africa, Europe)** – *mainstream* – **Technocraft** (US scaffolding 12–18 month pipeline, drum closures 35–40% margins), **TD Power Systems** (92% H1 order inflow export), **Elecon** (30% YoY overseas revenue growth, 27% of consolidated), **Karamtara** (57% US order book, new Rs 550 cr Nov order), **Zetwerk** (41% international, US transformer lead-time 2 yrs vs 3–5 yrs), **GMM Pfaudler** (Poland manufacturing hub, US Edlon facility) – geopolitical decoupling from China creating structural share gains.
- **Rental/annuity & service-led models** – *early* – **Sanghvi Movers** (crane rental 62% H1 revenue, GCC EBITDA margin 24% scaling), **Msafe** (scaffolding rental 51.5% H1 revenue, yields 38–66%), **Jupiter Wagons** (BESS build-own-operate 15-yr), **Action Construction** (defence 5–6% revenue, BRO order) – shifts revenue quality toward recurring, asset-light cash flows.

**Government push**
- **PLI for ECMS (Electronic Components & Manufacturing Services)** – 25% capex subsidy (Rs 255 cr central + state match) for **Jyoti CNC**’s Rs 1,020 cr 5-yr plan [Govt: MeitY, 2024].
- **₹11,000 cr Construction Equipment Scheme** – supports **Action Construction**, **ACE KATO JV**, **Sanghvi Movers** capex [Govt: Cabinet, Nov 2026].
- **₹11,000 cr Critical Machines Scheme** – benefits heavy engineering, machine tools, precision fabrication [Govt: Cabinet, Nov 2026].
- **Railway Capex (Rs 2.93 lakh cr FY27)** – wagon orders (BOXNHL/BOXNS), Kavach, Vande Bharat sleeper, station redevelopment – drives **Jupiter Wagons**, **Texmaco**, **Oriental Rail**, **Concord**, **Jash**, **BEML** [Govt: Railway Budget, FY27].
- **Defence Indigenisation (Positive Lists, 75% domestic procurement target FY27)** – **Triveni Power** (marine gearbox), **Jash** (loco shell), **Concord** (Kavach), **Kirloskar Oil** (Kirloskar Advanced Systems maiden billing), **Action Construction** (BRO backhoe order) [Govt: MoD, ongoing].
- **PM-KUSUM 2.0 / Magel Tyala / PM Surya Ghar** – solar pump/EPC demand for **Shakti Pumps**, **Oswal Pumps**, **KP Green**, **Karamtara** (delayed rollout flagged as near-term risk) [Govt: MNRE, pending].
- **Green Energy Corridor Phase-III / PM-DHARA / 1150 kV AC approval** – transmission gear, HVDC, FACTS for **Hitachi Energy**, **Quality Power**, **TD Power**, **KSB** [Govt: CEA/Ministry of Power, Nov 2026].
- **Semiconductor Mission (Dholera fab, Tata Electronics)** – piping, orbital welding, precision fabrication for **INOX India**, **Standard Engineering**, **Dee Development**, **Fabtech** [Govt: MeitY, 2024].
- **National Steel Policy (300 MT target) / Critical Minerals Mission** – refractory, mining consumables demand for **RHI Magnesita**, **Tega**, **AIA Engineering**, **IFGL** [Govt: Ministry of Steel/Mines, ongoing].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus:** Domestic capex cycle is robust and broadening. **Elecon** sees 8% CAGR in heavy-duty gearboxes to FY30; **ISGEC** reports strong demand across auto, oil & gas, petrochemicals, cement, steel; **Kirloskar Oil** cites >30% market share in 1010 kVA node and 59% YoY international growth; **TD Power** has OEM slots booked to 2030 across data centres, grid, hydro, geothermal; **Jupiter Wagons** notes accelerating private/export wagon orders (85% of Rs 8,000 cr book); **Concord** sees Kavach as 10–15 yr multi-year opportunity; **Praj** highlights international pipeline ~Rs 1,000 cr (USA, Brazil, SE Asia, Africa); **Technocraft** cites 12–18 month US scaffolding pipeline from data centres, chip plants, energy.

**Divergence:** 
- **Near-term volume visibility:** **AIA Engineering** (flat H1 volumes, NGDS trials delayed, Ghana/China expansions on hold), **Kilburn Engineering** (standalone revenue -33% YoY Q2, cut FY27 guidance to Rs 600–650 cr from Rs 700 cr), **Oswal Pumps** (walked back 20–25% growth to 10–15%, PM-KUSUM 2.0 delay), **Inox Wind** (withdrew FY27 revenue/EBITDA guidance, rationalised order book to 3.8 GW), **LMW** (cautious on textile machinery due to cost pressures, Bangladesh/Turkey headwinds).
- **Export/logistics friction:** **AIA** (freight ~10% of sales, Red Sea/Hormuz disruptions), **Karamtara** (Middle East transit days up, debtor days 94), **Texmaco** (US tariff fluctuations, customer design changes elongating lead times), **Mamata Machinery** (US container costs 3x, sailing time 47 days).
- **Segment-specific softness:** **Cummins India** (rail coach factory inventory build-up, marine project lumpiness, compressor supply chain), **Kirloskar Brothers** (UK industrial headwinds from high energy costs), **IFGL** (European steel subdued), **GMM Pfaudler** (traditional chemical/agrochem slow, Europe restructuring).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Steel:** **Action Construction** (INR 66–67/kg vs 48–50 earlier), **AJAX** (Rs 58→68/kg, expecting 75–76), **Karamtara** (freight primary headwind), **Elecon** (volatility stabilised Q2 vs abnormal Q1 spikes), **BEML/Concord** (price variation clauses on wagon tenders).
**Copper/Aluminium/Electronics:** **Ador Welding** (steel +7–8% QoQ, copper, PCBs), **Tempsens** (aluminium, resin, paint), **KSB** (fixed-price contracts 6–9 month pass-through lag), **Triveni Turbine** (commodity increases impacting material cost, normalisation expected Q4).
**Freight/Logistics:** **AIA** (export freight +3.5% YoY, ~10% of sales), **Karamtara** (ocean+land freight primary cost pressure), **Mamata** (shipping costs tripled), **Zetwerk** (US transformer lead-time advantage 2 yrs vs 3–5 yrs).
**Margin trajectory:** 
- *Expanding:* **Elecon** (EBITDA 23.1%, +140 bps YoY), **TD Power** (H1 EBITDA 20.02%, +160 bps), **Technocraft** (drum closure 35–40%, scaffolding volumes up), **RHI Magnesita** (14% EBITDA, +332 bps YoY), **Zetwerk** (gross margin 15%→25.5% manufacturing segment, adj EBITDA +66% QoQ), **GMM Pfaudler** (core adj EBITDA 15.9% vs reported 10.3%).
- *Compressing:* **Cummins** (gross margin -400 bps QoQ), **Kirloskar Oil** (standalone EBITDA 11% vs 14% YoY), **Oswal Pumps** (EBITDA 11.3%, -437 bps QoQ), **Triveni Turbine** (EBITDA 17.5% vs 26.3% YoY), **AIA** (operating EBITDA -2.5–3%), **IFGL** (standalone gross 43.9% vs 45.2% YoY).
- *Stable/Recovering:* **Action Construction** (EBITDA 19.3%, +143 bps), **Jyoti CNC** (standalone 28%, +320 bps), **Standard Engineering** (core targeting 20%, GScale 20–25%), **Concord** (29.3% sustainable), **Quality Power** (group stable ~25%).
