# Capital Goods & Engineering: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Capital Goods & Engineering sector reflects a generally robust demand environment domestically—led by infrastructure, data centers, cleanrooms, and renewable energy—offset by severe headwinds from soaring global freight costs, raw material inflation (metals, resins, oil derivatives), and geopolitical friction in the Middle East and Africa. While companies with high-value technical capabilities or exposure to high-growth niches (such as liquid cooling, advanced ceramics, and high-margin consultancy) delivered stellar top-line and margin expansions, others faced margin compression as input and shipping costs temporarily outpaced price hikes. 

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus / Agreements:** Managements across multiple segments highlighted strong secular demand in data centers, cleanrooms/pharma upgrades, and renewable energy. Fabtech Cleanrooms, Fabtech Technologies, and Aeroflex Industries all emphasized surging requirements for precision air/liquid cooling and cleanroom spaces driven by AI, semiconductor missions, and global pharma compliance (Schedule M). International geopolitical instability—particularly in the Middle East (Red Sea, Strait of Hormuz) and Africa—was widely cited by Engineers India, AIA Engineering, Fabtech Technologies, and WPIL as a bottleneck creating friction points, delayed tenders, and logistical uncertainties.
*   **Divergence:** Divergence was stark in rural and agricultural-linked segments. Escorts Kubota noted a subdued rural sentiment and tractor industry degrowth in the West and South due to deficit rainfall (13% deficit) and high bases. Conversely, AIA Engineering reported that mining industry demand (specifically copper in South America and gold) remains "exciting and stable." Meanwhile, Shakti Pumps experienced localized execution concentration under single-state schemes (like Maharashtra's Magel Tyala) while awaiting central PM-KUSUM 2.0 rollouts.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Almost all manufacturing and export-heavy players flagged escalating international shipping freight rates and raw material volatility (steel, MS, copper, aluminium, and oil-derived resins) as key margin pressures during the quarter. 
*   **Divergence in Impact & Action:** 
    *   *AIA Engineering* reported export freight reaching ~10% of sales (normal range 7-8%), dragging operating EBITDA down by 2.5% to 3%.
    *   *Escorts Kubota* faced 8–9% commodity inflation, which outpaced its cumulative 6–7% price hikes, compressing EBITDA margins down to 9.0%.
    *   *Carborundum Universal* absorbed fuel and resin cost spikes in its Abrasives segment (where margins hit a bottom and are targeted back to a 15% normal trajectory), but successfully expanded margins in Ceramics.
    *   *Fabtech Cleanrooms* prioritized market share on reference projects despite raw material spikes (e.g., aluminium doubling from Rs 220 to Rs 440 over two months), squeezing early project margins.
    *   *Shakti Pumps* offset raw material inflation by activating internal cost optimizations (Kaizen, advertising cuts) that saved ~2% and limited net EBITDA impact to 1%.

## Capex & capacity
Managements are actively expanding capacities to capture long-term structural tailwinds:
*   **Aeroflex Industries:** Announced a total capex of Rs 202 crore to scale stainless steel flexible flow solutions (to 30 million meters/annum by March 2028) and skid assemblies (to 30,000 units/annum).
*   **Escorts Kubota:** Completed greenfield land acquisition for INR 400–450 crore, with a total project estimate of ~INR 2,000 crore phasing out over 3–4 years.
*   **Shakti Pumps:** Continuing its total Rs 1,700 crore capex plan (~Rs 200 crore spent so far), with its 500 MW solar panel facility entering trial runs and a 2.2 GW solar cell plant targeted for completion in FY28.
*   **Carborundum Universal:** Maintained its full-year consolidated capex guidance at Rs 400 crore (H1 spent Rs 127 crore), focusing on semiconductor equipment components.
*   **AIA Engineering:** Incurred H1 capex of INR 86 crore and guided an additional ~INR 250 crore for H2 (inclusive of land completion and normal/incremental capex).

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Aeroflex Industries:** Upgraded its FY30 top-line target to close to Rs 1,500 crore, maintaining ~25% EBITDA margins.
*   **Carborundum Universal:** Revised its consolidated Ceramics sales guidance upward from 23%-25% to 25%-28% (and margins from 20.5%-21% to 22.5%-23.5%), while trimming Abrasives margin guidance due to input cost pressures.
*   **Fabtech Cleanrooms:** Revised its full-year FY27 growth expectations drastically upward to 70%–80% (from a prior conservative 20%–30%).
*   **Escorts Kubota:** Adjusted tractor industry growth expectations down to a marginal single-digit degrowth for FY27 due to H2 rural moderation.
*   **Engineers India:** Indicated that it will likely cross its Rs 8,000 crore annual order inflow target early and may formally revise guidance next quarter.

## Company one-liners
*   **AIA Engineering:** Reported flat H1 volumes and margin pressure from soaring freight, but remains focused on FY28–FY29 volume growth plans and NGDS trials.
*   **Aeroflex Industries:** Delivered a record quarter crossing Rs 200 crore in revenue, prompting an upgrade to its FY30 outlook and a Rs 202 crore capex rollout.
*   **Carborundum Universal:** Achieved robust H1 consolidated top-line growth of ~15% led by Ceramics and Electrominerals, while cleaning up European operations.
*   **Engineers India:** Reached a record-high order book and margin expansion by pivoting to consultancy, aiming to exceed Rs 8,000 crore in FY27 inflows.
*   **Escorts Kubota:** Grew revenue by 16.3% on strong construction equipment demand, but saw margins compress to 9.0% due to surging commodity costs.
*   **Fabtech Cleanrooms:** Posted a 75% YoY revenue jump and doubled its order book, absorbing raw material volatility to capture reference project share.
*   **Fabtech Technologies:** Doubled its EBITDA sequentially in Q2 with a >Rs 900 crore order book, maintaining full-year margin and growth targets.
*   **Shakti Pumps:** Delivered steady solar execution and export growth while ramping up backward integration into solar cells and panels.
*   **WPIL Ltd:** Suffered near-term domestic revenue lumpiness and execution delays, but anticipates a strong H2 recovery backed by its Rs 5,000+ crore total backlog.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **AIA Engineering:** Progress on NGDS trials in Peru/Ghana and potential reactivation of China/Ghana geographical expansions.
*   **Aeroflex Industries:** Execution progress on the Rs 202 crore capex and progression of international liquid cooling partnership talks.
*   **Carborundum Universal:** Complete exit and resolution of Foskor Zirconia and AWUKO, alongside working capital reversal in H2.
*   **Engineers India:** Tendering updates from the Middle East and early-stage nuclear energy opportunities.
*   **Escorts Kubota:** Impact of November price hikes (3.0-3.5%) in mitigating commodity inflation and rural demand stabilization.
*   **Fabtech Cleanrooms & Technologies:** Conversion speed of the massive hot pipelines (Rs 600 cr / Rs 3,600+ cr respectively) and working capital/receivable collections.
*   **Shakti Pumps:** Official central approval and rollout of the PM-KUSUM 2.0 scheme and commercial production commencement of the 500 MW solar panel facility.
*   **WPIL Ltd:** Actual release of West Bengal government funds and realization of H2 project commissioning milestones.
