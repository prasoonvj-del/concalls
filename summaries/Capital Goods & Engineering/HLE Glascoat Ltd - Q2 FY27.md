# HLE Glascoat Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a record consolidated order book crossing Rs 1,000 crores, sequential revenue and EBITDA recovery, and strong demand in API and specialty chemicals.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 346.7 crores (up 15.3% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 39 crores (up 72.3% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 11.2%
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 16 crores (up 680% QoQ)
- **Consolidated PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 4.6%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 647.4 crores (up 2% YoY from Rs 634.7 crores)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 61.6 crores (down 23% YoY from Rs 80 crores)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 9.5%
- **Consolidated Order Book (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 1,087.3 crores (up 51% YoY and 64% QoQ from Rs 662 crores as of June 30, 2026)
- **Filtration, Drying and Other Equipment Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 102 crores (up 5.4% QoQ from Rs 96.8 crores)
- **Glass Lined Products Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 195.4 crores (up 24.2% YoY from Rs 157.3 crores)
- **Heat Exchanger Equipment Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 48.8 crores (down 12.3% YoY, up 362.3% QoQ)
- **Net Working Capital Days:** 102 days (increased from 80 days in FY26)
- **THALETEC Germany H1 Revenue:** Rs 192 crores (EBITDA margin a little over 11.5%)
- **Omeras (HLE Surface Technologies) H1 Revenue:** Little in excess of EUR8.5 million (achieved EBITDA breakeven)

### Highlights
- Consolidated order book crossed Rs 1,000 crores for the first time, reaching Rs 1,087.3 crores.
- Step-down subsidiary HLE Surface Technologies GmbH (Omeras) secured a landmark order worth approximately EUR20.56 million for vitreous enamel cladding panels for the Fehmarnbelt tunnel connecting Germany and Denmark.
- THALETEC product range contribution reached approximately 22% of overall glass-lined product business in India during Q2.
- Omeras achieved EBITDA breakeven in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its ambition of reaching Rs 2,000 crores revenue by FY28, driven by existing products, new products, and the upcoming Omeras manufacturing plant in India.
- Management remains confident in achieving 15% EBITDA margins by FY28.
- For FY27, management expects revenue for the Indian glass-lined equipment business to be between Rs 260 crores and Rs 280 crores.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand in India remains optimistic, particularly in the API and specialty chemical sectors.
- European demand is slightly subdued, while demand from the U.S. is quite high.
- Previously deferred projects in pharma and specialty chemicals are now progressing towards finalization.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Europe is facing cost pressures, which the company mitigates by leveraging its Indian manufacturing base for strategic sourcing.
- Fixed costs in the Indian glass-lined business are high, but management expects a substantial flow to the bottom line once revenue crosses certain thresholds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- A new plant is being set up near Anand (at Adas) focused on heavy equipment exceeding 100–150 tonnes, targeting the energy, oil & gas, petrochemical, and renewable energy sectors.
- Omeras India business is likely to be commissioned in the next 2 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital days increased to 102 days in line with order book expansion, up from 80 days in FY26.
- Total debt is reported as very manageable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Customer delivery acceptances were occasionally delayed due to geopolitical disturbances, impacting revenue dispatch timing.
- Initial period for heavy engineering and large-scale orders (like Kinam's oil & gas orders) is lumpy until a regular track record is established.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankit Gupta (Bamboo Capital) asked about the discrepancy between strong pharma demand and the muted performance of the filtration & drying segment.**
   - *Management answered* that execution cycles take 4 to 7 months, and revenue is only accounted upon dispatch, causing a delayed P&L impact. Furthermore, some dispatches were postponed due to customer delivery delays.
2. **Uttam Purohit (VVD Asset Managers) asked about Omeras' quarterly run rate and yearly turnover expectations.**
   - *Management answered* that H1 turnover was over EUR8.5 million with EBITDA breakeven, aiming for serious profitability past EUR20 million and double-digit margins between EUR25 million and EUR30 million.
3. **Ajay Surya (NIVESHAAY) asked about competitive intensity in Indian glass lining and F&D.**
   - *Management answered* that competitive intensity driven by capacity-filling has subsided as market demand picked up; HLE is successfully positioning itself as a technology solution provider with premium THALETEC and F&D offerings.
4. **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital) asked about the path to reach historical peak revenues and 16-18% margins in the Indian GLE business.**
   - *Management answered* that higher realizations from THALETEC products and cost discipline will drive margins back to 16-18% at around Rs 300 crores revenue.
5. **Ajit Sethi (Eiko Quantum Solutions) asked if the Rs 2,000 crores revenue and 15% EBITDA margin guidance for FY28 remains intact.**
   - *Management answered* affirmatively, stating they are on track, supported by existing/new products and the upcoming Omeras India plant.

### One-line takeaway
HLE Glascoat reported a record consolidated order book of Rs 1,087.3 crores and sequential financial recovery, keeping its FY28 targets of Rs 2,000 crores revenue and 15% EBITDA margin intact.
