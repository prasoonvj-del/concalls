# Kilburn Engineering Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue performance fell below targets due to project deferments, but management maintained full-year guidance and highlighted a strong inquiry pipeline.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 117 crore.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 24.2 crore.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 20.1%.
*   **Order Inflows (YTD):** ~Rs 190 crore.
*   **Order Inflow (Q1 only):** Rs 134 crore.
*   **Order Book:** Rs 485 crore.
*   **Inquiry Pipeline:** Rs 4,000 crore.

### Highlights
*   Revenue was impacted by the timing of customer deliveries and deferment of project executions.
*   Management expects FY27 to be significantly second-half (H2) weighted.
*   The company is now net debt-free following the completion of equity fundraising.
*   Management confirmed no further fundraising is required for the next two years to achieve the Rs 1,000 crore revenue aspiration.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue:** Maintained guidance of ~Rs 700 crore.
*   **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Maintained guidance of 20%.
*   **Order Inflow Target:** Targeting Rs 800 crore for FY27.
*   **Medium-term Goal:** Investments are designed to support an annual revenue capacity of Rs 1,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong traction noted in fertilizer and nuclear sectors.
*   Data center infrastructure is identified as a key growth area for Monga Strayfield.
*   Steel sector expansion is driving inquiries for M.E. Energy.
*   Geopolitical tensions have led to longer decision-making cycles for customers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Maintained 20.1% EBITDA margin despite lower revenue, attributed to execution discipline.
*   Company books 80% of raw materials within 48-72 hours of order receipt to mitigate price risk.
*   Management is attempting to negotiate compensation for delays caused by customers on fixed-price contracts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Kilburn Engineering and M.E. Energy capacity expansions are at an advanced stage, with completion expected by end-October 2026.
*   Monga Strayfield is expanding metal sheet fabrication capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Balance sheet is net debt-free.
*   Working capital position is reported as better than the March quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical instability is causing project delays and deferments.
*   Customer-side delays in approving drawings and quality inspections are extending execution cycles.
*   Certain projects (e.g., carbon black sector) are currently on hold due to pending environmental and land clearances.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Sagar Shah (Spark Capital):** Asked about fund utilization. Management stated funds are for capex and growth; no further fundraising is needed for the next two years.
*   **Bhavya Nahar (Tamohara Investments):** Asked about the status of delayed O&G/WHR orders. Management noted strong inquiries in steel and ferrous alloy sectors as alternatives.
*   **Sameer Chheda (Prince Polyplast):** Asked if cost escalations impact margins. Management clarified they use fixed-price contracts but try to seek compensation for customer-led delays.
*   **Andrey Purushottam (Cogito Advisors):** Suggested that sharing the "inquiry pipeline" is misleading and requested the company focus on the order book. Management acknowledged the suggestion.

### One-line takeaway
While Q1 revenue was soft due to project deferments and geopolitical headwinds, management remains confident in a strong H2, supported by a robust inquiry pipeline and a net debt-free balance sheet.
