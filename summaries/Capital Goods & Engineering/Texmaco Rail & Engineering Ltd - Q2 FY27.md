# Texmaco Rail & Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 15% QoQ, but declined 31% YoY due to lower Indian Railway orders; management claims the company is at an "inflection point" with a strategic shift to private and export markets.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained; management remains committed to the "Texmaco 2.0" roadmap.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "execution of short-term priorities" to an "inflection point" narrative; management is more vocal about the "tipping point" of the business transformation.
- **New topics:** Foray into the defense sector announced.
- **Last quarter's promises:** South Africa facility remains under evaluation/planning; no new capacity addition in core business (sweating existing assets).

### Key numbers
- **Standalone revenue:** Rs 863 crore (up 15% QoQ; down ~31% YoY from Rs 1,250 crore).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 88 crore (margin 10.2%).
- **PAT:** Rs 36 crore.
- **Operational metrics:** 1,106 wagons delivered; Foundry production at 6,468 metric tonnes.
- **Order book:** ~Rs 8,000 crore (freight rolling stock) + Rs 3,000–3,500 crore (infra/other).
- **Export order book share:** 54% (vs 9% YoY).
- **Revenue mix:** Freight cars (62.3%), Infra/Electrical (27.8%).

### Highlights
- **Strategic Shift:** Successfully pivoted to private and export markets (85% of order book) to reduce dependence on Indian Railways (15% of order book).
- **Business Performance:** EPC business turned profitable; electrical contracting business is "flourishing."
- **Ratings:** CRISIL long-term rating upgraded to A+; ESG rating improved to 51–59.
- **Defense:** Initiated foray into the defense sector, citing similar manufacturing DNA requirements.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Management targets a core base of Rs 4,000–5,000 crore plus Rs 5,000–6,000 crore from new businesses.
- **Execution:** Management expects "much, much better output and rebound" in the second half of the year.
- **South Africa:** Execution of wagon and locomotive orders expected to commence in FY28 and the following quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways demand is expected to continue due to infrastructure and coal transport requirements.
- Management noted that the government is simplifying procurement policies to welcome private investment and designs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margins:** EBITDA margin defended at 10.2%; management stated adjusted EBITDA (excluding non-repeatable items) is 70 bps higher QoQ.
- **Cost Control:** Finance costs reduced; initiatives in power, fuel, and freight negotiations are ongoing to protect margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Foundry:** Capacity is 60,000 metric tonnes; management stated no further capacity expansion is needed in the core business.
- **Capex:** ~40% of planned capex for the year has been executed.
- **South Africa:** Facility planned for maintenance and localization of exports.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt is retiring; management stated no need for new debt in the short term as operational surpluses are sufficient.
- Significant portion of contracts are secured via advances and milestones, mitigating working capital risk.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Geopolitical/Tariff:** Fluctuations in tariff situations (specifically US export market) remain a risk factor.
- **Supply Chain:** Management noted that while supply chain stress exists, alternative arrangements are in place.
- **Order Delays:** Management acknowledged that new designs and supply chains for private/export orders take time to stabilize.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Q: Why is the YoY revenue down 31%?** A: Management attributed this to lower Indian Railway orders, which are being offset by the shift to private/export markets.
2. **Q: What is the Rs 700 crore provision?** A: Management stated it was a risk mitigation measure for geopolitical uncertainty and declined to provide further granular details.
3. **Q: When will results be consistently good?** A: Management claimed the company has reached an "inflection point" and expects better results from the next quarter onwards.
4. **Q: Status of South Africa facility?** A: Management confirmed a facility will be set up for maintenance and to capture a larger share of the business.
5. **Q: Why was the government tender share lower?** A: Management explained that the government exercised option clauses based on specific contract history, not capacity constraints.

### One-line takeaway
Texmaco is navigating a transition phase, sacrificing YoY revenue growth to pivot toward a higher-margin, private/export-led order book, while maintaining a 10.2% EBITDA margin.
