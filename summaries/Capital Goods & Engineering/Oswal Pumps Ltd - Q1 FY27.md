# Oswal Pumps Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Margins moderated due to competitive bidding and geopolitical cost pressures, though management remains confident in achieving annual revenue guidance through diversification.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 474 crore (Down 7.9% YoY, down 7.1% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 82 crore (Margin: 17.1%)
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 74 crore (Margin: 15.7%, down 747 bps QoQ)
- **Gross Margin Sequential Decline:** 548 bps
- **PAT:** Rs 54 crore (Margin: 11.2%)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 266 crore (as of June 30, 2026)
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.15x
- **Net Debt to Operating EBITDA:** 0.90x
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 244 days (vs 172 days as of March 31, 2026)
- **Receivable Days:** 229 days (vs 155 days as of March 31, 2026); un-due receivables at Rs 305 crore
- **Pumps Supplied:** 43,000 units in Q1 FY27 (vs 40,400 units in Q4 FY26; 56,000 units in Q1 FY26)
- **Pump Order Book:** 22,025 pumps
- **Near-term Pipeline:** ~12,500 pumps (Direct PM KUSUM, Magel Tyala, indirect PM KUSUM, exports)
- **Solar Rooftop/Utility/C&I Order Book:** ~72 megawatt (with a wider pipeline of 359 megawatt)
- **FY27 Guidance:** Revenue growth of 20%-25% over FY26; Operating EBITDA margin of 15%-17%; PAT margin of 11%-13%
- **Medium-term Target:** Sustained growth momentum of 30% to 40%

### Highlights
- Revenue decline and margin compression attributed to three main factors: industry-wide competitive bidding under the Magel Tyala scheme (driving a 9% reduction in realization), diversification into solar module sales through channels, and elevated input costs from geopolitical conditions.
- Impact was partially offset by internal cost and value engineering initiatives.
- Created a dedicated PM Surya Ghar vertical with a dedicated business head.
- Target to complete ~2,00,000 solar installations under PM Surya Ghar in the first year, generating expected revenue of Rs 800 crore to Rs 1,000 crore.
- Introducing in-house wires and cables into channel offerings (expected revenue of Rs 70 crore to Rs 100 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 guidance of 20%-25% overall revenue growth with a back-ended profile.
- Expects Q2 FY27 revenue growth of more than 10%-15% YoY.
- Operating EBITDA margin guided at 15%-17% and PAT margin at 11%-13% for FY27.
- Medium-term (FY28-FY29) growth projected at 30% to 40% as execution across multiple fronts accelerates.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PM KUSUM 2.0 has faced delays, pushing focus and industry bidders toward state government projects like Maharashtra's Magel Tyala scheme, resulting in aggressive price bidding.
- Management expects PM KUSUM 2.0 to be rolled out during Q2 FY27 (based on inputs from the Ministry).
- Current aggressive bidding prices across the industry are viewed by management as unsustainable.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin dropped by 747 bps QoQ due to lower gross margins, employee benefit expenses (annual increments and senior hires), and operating leverage (impact of 1%-1.5%).
- Bifurcation of margin impact provided by management: ~8%-9% from aggressive price bidding in Magel Tyala, ~3%-3.5% from raw material cost inflation due to geopolitics, offset by value engineering.
- PM Surya Ghar margins are lower than PM KUSUM, expected around 12%-15%, blended down to the 15%-17% overall company EBITDA margin guidance.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pump and motor plant capacity expansion and automation on track for completion by Q3 FY27.
- Solar module plant (first phase of 1 GW capacity) expected to complete commercial production by early/mid-September (Q2 FY27).
- In-house inverter production expected within 6 months (field tests in 2 months).
- Total capex aligned with IPO proceeds.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 266 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- Cash conversion cycle stretched to 244 days due to receivable days increasing to 229 days, caused by delayed payments from state nodal agencies.
- Receivables are fully secure from government/government-backed counterparties; Rs 305 crore was not due yet as of June 30, 2026. Management expects payment cycles to normalize over the medium term.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delay in the rollout of PM KUSUM 2.0.
- Intense competitive bidding and pricing pressure under state tenders (Magel Tyala).
- Geopolitical uncertainties driving volatility in raw material (e.g., copper) costs.
- Extended government receivable collection cycles stretching working capital.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Manish Gadia (Individual Investor) asked about weak stock performance post-IPO and execution missing guidance.** Management replied that fundamentals and capacity expansion are on track, and setbacks are temporary, driven purely by external factors like PM KUSUM delays and Magel Tyala price competition.
2. **Disha (Sapphire Capital) asked about cost steps to win tenders and sustainability of the 20%-25% growth.** Management stated they are utilizing value engineering, diversifying into PM Surya Ghar (targeting 2 lakh homes), and introducing wires and cables to protect margins.
3. **Karan (Choice Institutional Equities) asked for a breakdown of the margin drop from 24%-25% to 15%.** Management attributed 8%-9% to aggressive price bidding, 3%-3.5% to geopolitical raw material cost inflation, and 1%-1.5% to operating leverage.
4. **Prakhar asked if the 15%-17% EBITDA margin guidance factors in KUSUM renewal.** Management clarified that guidance is based strictly on current visibility and bids in hand without assuming KUSUM 2.0 renewal benefits.
5. **Dheeraj Ram (360 ONE Capital) asked about revenue potential and margins from solar rooftops.** Management estimated Rs 800 crore to Rs 1,000 crore in revenue from 2,000,000 homes, with project margins around 12%-15% contributing to the overall 15%-17% company average.
6. **Pawan Kumar asked for clarification on the disconnect between total revenue guidance and Surya Ghar revenue targets.** Management explained that Surya Ghar revenues will scale from Q2 to Q4, which alongside pump orders and new channel items will meet the 20%-25% overall growth target.

### One-line takeaway
Oswal Pumps reported a revenue and margin contraction in Q1 FY27 due to delayed PM KUSUM schemes and aggressive state tender bidding, but management remains confident in achieving 20%-25% revenue growth for FY27 driven by aggressive diversification into PM Surya Ghar and channel sales.
