# Capital Goods & Engineering - Industrial machinery: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Capital Goods & Engineering – Industrial machinery sector reflects a generally positive operational environment driven by domestic infrastructure spending, defence indigenization, and strong order books. However, performance was offset by input cost inflations (steel, copper, electronics), soaring export freight rates, and international geopolitical frictions. While several players reported strong double-digit top-line growth and margin expansions, others faced near-term headwinds from raw material pricing lags and loss-making international subsidiaries.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board highlight robust domestic demand underpinned by government capital expenditure, infrastructure build-outs, and heavy industries (steel, cement, power, mining). Defence, aerospace, and data center infrastructure are emerging as key structural growth vectors. 
*   **Divergence:** While domestic demand remains largely insulated and buoyant, international markets show stark divergence. Companies exposed to Western Europe and certain export hubs (like Turkey and Bangladesh) reported headwinds, unabsorbed fixed costs, or subdued growth (e.g., LMW, Carborundum Universal, Control Print). Conversely, firms targeting the Americas, the Middle East, and high-barrier specialized engineering (such as Adroit Industries, Elecon, and Sanghvi Movers) are seeing healthy export traction.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Cost Pressures:** Input cost inflation—particularly in steel, copper, aluminum, electronics, and PCBs—pressured gross margins for several players during the quarter (e.g., Ador Welding, Carborundum Universal, Greaves Cotton, Diffusion Engineers). Additionally, AIA Engineering suffered a 2.5% to 3% hit to operating EBITDA due to export freight costs soaring to ~10% of sales.
*   **Mitigation:** Most managements are deploying price hikes (often operating with a lag of 1 to 1.5 quarters) and internal Value Analysis and Value Engineering (VAVE). Companies with strong pricing power or pass-through mechanisms (such as Poojaa Precision and Adroit Industries) noted insulation from raw material volatility. Backward integration initiatives (e.g., Gabion Technologies, Jyoti CNC) are also being prioritized to safeguard long-term margins.

## Capex & capacity
Capital expenditure remains aggressive across the sector as companies scale up to meet robust order books and execute import-substitution strategies:
*   **Jyoti CNC** is outlaying Rs 450 crore to Rs 500 crore in FY27, backed by major PLI (ECMS) scheme approvals.
*   **Carborundum Universal** maintains its consolidated capex guidance at Rs 400 crore.
*   **Elecon Engineering** is progressing smoothly with its 3-year capex program.
*   **Ador Welding** is stepping up annual capex to Rs 60 crore–Rs 70 crore for FY27 and FY28.
*   **Poojaa Precision** is aggressively scaling melting and casting capacities by FY28 to support its Rs 1,000 crore revenue target by FY30.
*   **Sanghvi Movers** revised its FY27 capex ceiling upwards to Rs 583 crore to fund crane fleets and Middle East expansion.

## Company one-liners
*   **AIA Engineering Ltd:** Reported flat H1 volumes and margin pressure from soaring freight costs, but remains internally focused on achieving 25,000–30,000 tonnes of annual growth in FY28–FY29.
*   **Admach Systems Ltd:** Delivered robust H1 FY27 growth backed by in-house manufacturing efficiencies, a healthy order book of Rs 77.42 crores, and rising demand in high-barrier defense, nuclear, and NDT sectors.
*   **Ador Welding Ltd:** Delivered a strong 23% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by robust core demand, while management addresses short-term steel inflation pressures through price hikes.
*   **Adroit Industries (India) Ltd:** Delivered strong Q2 FY27 financial growth driven by robust export demand and repeat customers, while on track with capacity expansions.
*   **Carborundum Universal Ltd:** Reported robust H1 top-line growth of ~15% consolidated driven by Ceramics and Electrominerals, even as it absorbed AWUKO closure costs and near-term margin pressure in Abrasives.
*   **Control Print Ltd-$:** Core coding and marking business remains steady and profitable, while management continues to absorb deliberate long-term technology investments and subsidiary losses.
*   **Diffusion Engineers Ltd:** Delivered strong top-line growth and order book expansion, with management confident in margin recovery by Q4 FY27.
*   **Elecon Engineering Company Ltd:** Delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with 10.5% revenue growth and margin expansion, underpinned by robust international execution and a strong order book.
*   **Gabion Technologies India Ltd:** Scaling rapidly, leveraging its integrated design-to-installation model and new capacity to capitalize on infrastructure spending.
*   **Greaves Cotton Ltd:** Scaling its EV and core businesses while navigating input cost pressures, with management prioritizing margin recovery in H2.
*   **Jyoti CNC Automation Ltd:** Posted robust Q2 FY27 performance with record dispatches and a strong order book of Rs 5,082 crore, alongside securing a major PLI scheme approval.
*   **LMW Ltd:** Reported a robust 30% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by strong momentum in Machine Tools and ATC, amid cautious margin stances in the textile division.
*   **Msafe Equipments Ltd:** Delivered strong Q2 FY27 growth with 53% YoY revenue increase, driven by expansion in its rental model and scaling capacities in scaffolding and formwork.
*   **Poojaa Precision Engg. Ltd:** Aggressively scaling capacity and diversifying into non-auto sectors to achieve a Rs 1,000 crore revenue target by FY30.
*   **Sanghvi Movers Ltd:** Delivered a strong H1 with 36% revenue growth and reaffirmed full-year guidance, leveraging a robust order book and renewable E&C growth.
*   **Shree Refrigerations Ltd:** Delivered robust H1 FY27 top-line growth with margin expansion, backed by naval indigenization leadership, reaffirming its 40% CAGR guidance.
*   **Standard Engineering Technology Ltd:** Aggressively scaling its data center infrastructure business (GScale) while maintaining strong core business growth, reiterating its Rs 1,450 crore revenue target.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Margin Recovery & Pass-Through Lags:** Tracking whether companies like Ador Welding, Greaves Cotton, and Diffusion Engineers successfully recover gross margins by Q4 through realized price hikes.
*   **Freight & Logistics Volatility:** Monitoring whether soaring export freight costs stabilize for export-heavy players like AIA Engineering.
*   **Execution of New Tech & Subsidiaries:** Progress on turnaround efforts for loss-making international subsidiaries (Control Print, Carborundum Universal's AWUKO/Foskor exits, LMW's overseas entities) and commercialization timelines for new ventures like Standard Engineering's GScale data center operations.
*   **Order Book Conversion:** Actual execution pace and spillover risks on large order books across Elecon, Jyoti CNC, and Sanghvi Movers.
