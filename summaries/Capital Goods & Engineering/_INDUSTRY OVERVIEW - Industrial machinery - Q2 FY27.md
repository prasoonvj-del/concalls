# Capital Goods & Engineering - Industrial machinery: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian industrial machinery and capital goods sector demonstrated robust revenue expansion in Q2 FY27, backed by multi-year order books, strong domestic infrastructure capex, and accelerated global supply-chain diversification (China+1). However, the quarter highlighted an operational divergence: top-line momentum remained healthy across heavy engineering, data center infrastructure, defense indigenization, and precision components, but profitability was temporarily squeezed by raw material price surges (primarily domestic steel and copper), elevated ocean freight rates, and lag-driven pricing models. 

Export-led players faced extended shipping transit times and regional logistical bottlenecks, while domestic project execution saw temporary disruptions due to extended monsoon delays and slower disbursement of state-level contractor payments. To safeguard returns, companies are aggressively deploying backward integration, accelerating high-margin non-auto and aftermarket expansion, and pricing in cost increases for H2 FY27.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Historically, Indian industrial machinery OEMs operated on standard catalog equipment sales and low-margin sub-assembly subcontracts routed through domestic dealer networks. Over the past five years, the model has fundamentally pivoted toward end-to-end turnkey engineering solutions, high-yield asset leasing/rental models, localized global warehousing, and direct customer engagement driven by proprietary technological IP [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **Data Center & AI Infrastructure Expansion**: *Scaling*. Machinery manufacturers are reallocating capacity and executing specialized capex to supply precision cooling components, structural enclosures, power platforms, and backup drive turbines for data center builds globally and in India. Standard Engineering Technology acquired a 33.55% stake in GScale Energy to target Rs 250 crore in data center revenue for FY27 [Standard Engineering Technology concall Q2 FY27]. Kilburn Engineering approved Rs 25 crore capex at Monga Strayfield for data center enclosures to meet Rs 90 crore demand from clients like Vertiv [Kilburn Engineering concall Q2 FY27]. Omnitech Engineering commenced commercial dispatches for cooling system assemblies [Omnitech Engineering concall Q2 FY27], and Triveni Turbine secured marquee gas turbine orders for data center applications [Triveni Turbine concall Q2 FY27].
- **Defense & Aerospace Indigenization**: *Scaling*. Driven by domestic content mandates, capital goods OEMs are securing technical qualifications and commercial orders for specialized defense components, naval HVAC, nuclear assemblies, and locomotive structures. Omnitech Engineering secured commercial production orders for defense and aerospace platforms [Omnitech Engineering concall Q2 FY27], Kirloskar Oil Engines completed maiden commercial billing via Kirloskar Advanced Systems [Kirloskar Oil Engines concall Q2 FY27], and Action Construction Equipment commenced defense deliveries, securing large backhoe loader orders from the Border Roads Organisation [Action Construction Equipment concall Q2 FY27]. Shree Refrigerations secured submarine AC indigenization awards [Shree Refrigerations concall Q2 FY27], while Diffusion Engineers qualified for Indian Railways loco shell production [Diffusion Engineers concall Q2 FY27].
- **Deep Backward Integration into Foundries & Mining**: *Scaling*. Operating margins are being insulated against vendor delays and raw material inflation through internal foundries, in-house component machining, and captive mineral sourcing. Jyoti CNC is scaling foundry capacity from 1,500 to 5,000 tonnes/month using lost-foam vacuum casting [Jyoti CNC Automation concall Q2 FY27]. RHI Magnesita India operationalized the Chiraipani quartzite mine (40,000 tonnes/year) to yield a 3%–3.5% EBITDA margin delta [RHI Magnesita India concall Q2 FY27]. Admach Systems expanded in-house component manufacturing to save vendor payouts and lift EBITDA margins to ~23% [Admach Systems concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **PLI Scheme for Electronics Components & Manufacturing (ECMS)**: Rewards local capital investment and component manufacturing in high-precision technology sectors. Jyoti CNC received capital approval for over Rs 1,020 crore investment featuring a 25% central capital subsidy (~Rs 255 crore) [Govt: PLI ECMS scheme launch 2021; Jyoti CNC Automation concall Q2 FY27].
- **Certificates of Origin Authorization**: Streamlines export verification and lowers trade friction for engineering dispatches into key trade agreement nations [Govt: Commerce Ministry, Nov 2026]. Benefits export-focused engineering OEMs including Action Construction Equipment, Adroit Industries, and LMW Ltd.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
- **Infrastructure & Power Momentum**: Managements broadly agree that domestic infrastructure, power transmission, steel, mining, and heavy engineering remain primary growth engines (ACE, Greaves Cotton, Kirloskar Oil Engines, Systematic Industries, Diffusion Engineers).
- **Global Supply Chain Realignment**: Strong order pipelines from North America, the Middle East, and ROW indicate that global buyers continue shifting sourcing to India under "China+1" strategies (Adroit Industries, Bonfiglioli Transmissions, LMW Ltd, Omnitech Engineering).
- **H2 Seasonality Acceleration**: Sector players consistently note that H2 delivers 55%–65% of full-year revenue due to post-monsoon execution pick-ups and year-end customer budget clearing (ACE, AJAX Engineering, JNK India, Mamata Machinery, Systematic Industries, Triveni Turbine).

### Divergence
- **State Govt Spending & Contractor Cashflows**: AJAX Engineering flagged infrastructure execution delays and strained contractor liquidity in states like Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh, whereas Gabion Technologies and Action Construction Equipment experienced uninterrupted order conversion across central economic corridors and BRO projects.
- **Export Shipping Logistics vs Local Production**: Standard capital equipment exporters (AIA Engineering, Jash Engineering, Mamata Machinery) suffered dispatch deferments and elevated ocean freight expenses. Conversely, companies with overseas local warehousing (Adroit Industries' Memphis setup) or international manufacturing platforms (Bonfiglioli Transmissions, Tega Industries' Molycop unit) decoupled successfully from shipping bottlenecks.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Steel & Alloy Spikes**: Domestic steel prices rallied sharply to Rs 66–68/kg (up 10%–12% in October from Rs 48–50/kg earlier in the year), pressuring gross margins for fabrication-heavy capital equipment (ACE, AJAX Engineering, Ador Welding, Diffusion Engineers, Kirloskar Oil Engines).
- **Freights & Supply Chain Friction**: Global container shipping rates tripled to USD 11,000 on select long-haul lanes with transit times doubling to 47 days, heavily hitting AIA Engineering (freight reached ~10% of sales) and Mamata Machinery.
- **Pass-through Mechanics & Lag**: Fixed-price contracts created a temporary 1-to-2 quarter margin lag across B2B OEMs (Greaves Cotton, Diffusion Engineers, Behari Lal Engineering, Triveni Turbine). Companies relying on formulaic price escalations (IEEMA indexation at KP Green Engineering; pass-through bands at Omnitech, Adroit, and Poojaa Precision) successfully protected baseline unit margins.

## Capex & capacity
- **Foundry & Heavy Manufacturing Additions**: Large-scale capacity additions are underway to clear record order backlogs. Jyoti CNC is investing in Phase 1 PLI expansion (Rs 400–450 crore) and scaling foundry capacity. Behari Lal Engineering is constructing Unit 3 for heavy centrifugal casting. Msafe Equipments is expanding scaffolding/formwork capacity up to 15,000 tonnes/year alongside a new Mathura plant.
- **Inorganic Scale-Up**: Cash-rich engineering firms are deploying balance sheets for global acquisitions. Tega Industries completed the integration of Molycop, Systematic Industries acquired 72,000 MTPA GI wire capacity across Chhattisgarh and West Bengal, while AJAX Engineering is evaluating acquisitions backed by Rs 1,151 crore cash reserves.

## What changed since last quarter
- **Guidance Revisions**:
  - *Raised / Initiated*: Action Construction Equipment formally initiated FY27 revenue growth guidance at 18%–20%. Omnitech Engineering maintained a robust 30%–35% growth view. Carborundum Universal raised Ceramics segment growth guidance upward to 25%–28%.
  - *Cut / Moderate*: Kilburn Engineering cut FY27 revenue guidance from ~Rs 700 crore to Rs 600–650 crore due to standalone customer deferments. Tega Industries lowered Equipment division revenue growth guidance from 25% to 15%. Glen Industries trimmed FY27 revenue expectations to Rs 225–230 crore following election/monsoon project delays. Mamata Machinery shifted target from 15% growth to flat YoY.
- **Tone Shifts**: Action Construction Equipment shifted from "wait-and-see" to "constructive and disciplined" post-September sales pick-up. Hy-Tech Engineers transitioned from "cost lag" to "fully finalized price revisions." Conversely, RHI Magnesita adopted a selective volume stance to prioritize "value over volume."

## What to watch next quarter
- **Steel & Raw Material Pass-through Realization**: Track whether price hikes announced for late Q3/Q4 by Action Construction Equipment, Ador Welding, AJAX Engineering, and Greaves Cotton effectively restore EBITDA margins back to ~12%–20% target bands.
- **H2 Seasonality & Order Conversion**: Monitor H2 revenue execution heavyweights (JNK India, Mamata Machinery, Triveni Turbine, Kilburn Engineering) to verify if deferred H1 dispatches convert into cash flows.
- **Data Center & Defense Commissioning**: Watch for initial revenue flows from newly commissioned data center lines at Monga Strayfield (Kilburn Engineering), GScale Energy (Standard Engineering), and initial defense deliveries from Hy-Tech Engineers, Kirloskar Advanced Systems, and Technocrats Plasma Systems.
- **Freight & Transit Normalization**: Check if ocean freight rates and transit delays moderate for export-heavy players (AIA Engineering, Jash Engineering, Mamata Machinery).
