# GE Power India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-16 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27 (Investor Meet held on 10 July 2026, filed 16 July 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a successful multi-year turnaround, improved financial metrics, a credit rating upgrade to ICRA BBB+ stable, and a value-accretive demerger of the Durgapur business.

### Key numbers
- Net Worth: Increased from INR 57 crores in March 2024 to INR 483 crores by March 2026.
- Liquidity Position / Bank Balance: Shifted from a deficit of INR 66 crores in 2023 to INR 880 crores by March 2026.
- Bank Guarantees Exposure: Shed INR 1,364 crores over two years.
- EBITDA: Improved from a loss of INR 251 crores in FY23 to INR 277 crores in FY26.
- Core Services Order Bookings: Grew from INR 299 crores in FY22 to INR 734 crores in FY25-26 (CAGR of ~25%); delivered ~34% YoY growth in FY25-26.
- Other oOEM Segment Orders: Increased from ~INR 162 crores to ~INR 322 crores.
- Durgapur Facility Losses: Underutilization resulted in an average loss of ~INR 27 crores per year (2023-2025) booked in GEPIL.
- Durgapur Demerger Entitlement Ratio: 10 fully paid equity shares of JSW Energy for every 139 fully paid equity shares of GE Power India Limited.
- Credit Rating: ICRA long-term rating upgraded to BBB+ with a stable outlook as of June 2026.

### Highlights
- Focused strategy since 2024 on high-margin, cash-accretive opportunities with faster cash conversion cycles, freezing commercial strategy around services, and expanding in third-party fleets (other oOEM segment) and international markets (Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Australia, UAE, Malaysia, Indonesia, Morocco).
- Completed portfolio actions including hydro and gas slump sale, BHEL settlement, and the proposed demerger of the Durgapur business.
- Declared a dividend in 2026, reflecting restored financial stability.
- Proposed demerger of the Durgapur business unit (comprising 661 acres of leasehold land, industrial factory, and residential township) to JSW Energy Limited on a going-concern "as-is-where-is" basis retrospectively effective 1st July 2025.
- Established a 5-year manufacturing services agreement with JSW Energy to secure reserved capacity at pre-agreed schedules and pricing, alongside efforts to establish an independent supply chain.
- Board of Directors unanimously approved the demerger resolution.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects no disruption to manufacturing and fabrication support for the core services business due to the 5-year manufacturing services agreement and parallel development of an independent supply chain.
- Full supply chain independence is targeted to be achieved soon.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted a broader positive demand outlook for the Indian power industry supporting better utilization of the Durgapur facility under JSW Energy.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Shifted focus from chasing volume for revenue to chasing value for profitability, improving EBITDA from negative levels to INR 277 crores in FY26.
- Durgapur facility incurred an average loss of INR 27 crores per year from 2023 to 2025 due to underutilization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Durgapur facility factory utilization in recent years became limited largely to service work and non-coal activities, with significant underutilization from 2023 to 2025.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Substantial improvement in liquidity (INR 880 crores by March 2026) and reduction in outstanding bond/bank guarantee exposures (shed INR 1,364 crores).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged in the transcript other than historical underutilization and losses at the Durgapur facility.

### One-line takeaway
GE Power India Limited has completed a multi-year turnaround characterized by a surge in net worth to INR 483 crores, positive EBITDA of INR 277 crores, and a strategic demerger of its loss-making Durgapur facility to JSW Energy to sharpen its focus on core services.
