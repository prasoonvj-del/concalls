# Technocrats Plasma Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong revenue and profit growth driven by robust demand across end-markets, alongside expansion initiatives and new technology partnerships.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 10,355.1 lakh (up 97.4% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,703.9 lakh (up 78% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 16.5%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,194.8 lakh (up 58% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 11.5%
- **Capacity Utilisation:** ~75%
- **Current Order Book:** ~Rs 60 crore (expected to be executed in H2 FY27)
- **IPO Proceeds Raised:** Rs 60.98 crore (Utilisation: Rs 21.2 lakh towards capex, Rs 14.74 crore towards working capital; balance Rs ~34 crore unutilised)
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.13x
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs 62.9 lakh
- **Revenue Guidance (FY27):** 45% to 50% growth over FY26 (minimum assured baseline)

### Highlights
- Completed SME IPO raising Rs 60.98 crore, listed on BSE SME platform.
- Rented a new 20,000 sq ft facility (5x larger than the old 4,000 sq ft facility, which will be surrendered); commercial production starts in December 2026.
- Progressing on technology transfer initiatives with RRCAT (indigenous fibre laser source for defence) and BARC (plasma gasification for e-waste, municipal solid waste, and coal gasification).
- Participated in Weldex (Moscow) and ISET 2026 exhibitions.
- Served 95 customers in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a minimum revenue growth of 45% to 50% in FY27 compared to FY26.
- Expects FY27 turnover to be around minimum Rs 200 crore.
- Expects small sample orders for plasma gasification worth Rs 5 to Rs 6 crore in FY27, with large export/commercial orders anticipated from next year onwards.
- Defence-related indigenous laser business expected to start generating commercial business after 2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand driven by infrastructure development, shipbuilding, automotive, power, railways, defence, ONGC, oil & gas, and heavy engineering.
- Shipbuilding is highlighted as a booming industry with order books full for the next 5-6 years, driving demand for automation, robotics, and collaborative robots (cobots).
- High demand for specialized machines like pipe profile cutting machines from nuclear, naval, heavy engineering, and steel plants.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- In-house factory production yields higher profit margins (40% to 50%) compared to outside site jobs (15% to 20%).
- Plasma gasification and specialized robotics/laser automation are expected to command much higher margins (above 40%-45% EBIT margins).
- Material costs increased due to global input cost increases (impact of war) and advance purchases for future projects.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Shifting manufacturing from a 4,000 sq ft rented facility to a new 20,000 sq ft rented facility in December 2026 to bring more project execution in-house.
- Long-term plans include consolidating a significant majority of manufacturing under one roof (land identification underway).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables increased from Rs 21 crore to Rs 68 crore and inventories increased from Rs 33 crore to Rs 53 crore due to outside site project execution cycles taking 4 to 5 months for realisation.
- Operating cash flow was negative by ~Rs 50 crore in H1 FY27.
- Moving site execution in-house is expected to shorten collection cycles to around 60 days with milestone/advance payments.
- Low debt-to-equity ratio of 0.13x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Outside site project execution leads to elongated working capital cycles (4 to 5 months for money realisation) and negative operating cash flows.
- Input cost inflation driven by geopolitical factors impacting material costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital) asked if the 45-50% guidance is conservative given H1 growth of 100%.**
   - *Management replied* that 45-50% is the minimum baseline growth they are certain of, translating to a turnover of around Rs 200 crore.
2. **Sanskar (Analyst) questioned the sharp rise in trade receivables (to Rs 68 crore) and negative operating cash flow of ~Rs 50 crore.**
   - *Management explained* that outside site projects take 4-5 months to realise, but shifting work in-house via the new facility will resolve this working capital stretch.
3. **Nilesh Shah (Analyst) asked about the stage of the RRCAT technology transfer project.**
   - *Management stated* that detailing and paperwork are done, a PhD electronics engineer has been hired to head it, and main assembly/engineering starts next month.
4. **Rahil Mehta (Analyst) inquired about expected order sizes and margins for plasma gasification.**
   - *Management stated* initial sample orders of Rs 5-6 crore are expected in FY27, followed by large orders (Rs 50-500 crore range) next year, with EBIT margins expected above 40%-45%.
5. **Moni Shah (Analyst) asked about business development in the defence vertical and robotics.**
   - *Management highlighted* supplying robots/cobots for heavy/light fabrication (targeting shipbuilding) and developing specialized laser applications with RRCAT for defence.

### One-line takeaway
Technocrats Plasma Systems reported a strong H1 FY27 with 97.4% revenue growth and is scaling up in-house manufacturing space 5x to improve operating margins and cash flows while expanding into niche tech areas like robotics, fibre lasers, and plasma gasification.
