# Behari Lal Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 21.7% YoY and PAT grew 27.7% YoY, driven by a higher-value product mix, despite a slight QoQ margin dip due to rising raw material and power costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management did not provide specific numerical guidance for the second half of the year (maintained the stance of focusing on completing Unit 3 and mix upgrades).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains positive and confident on demand, noting firming input costs and temporary geographical/power disruptions that caused a minor dip in melt shop utilization compared to Q1.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Added discussion on NABL accredited laboratory certification enabling participation in GEM portal tenders for defense/aerospace, and rooftop/grounded solar plans for carbon reporting and cost reduction.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines, etc.) and whether they were delivered: Unit 3 construction is progressing on schedule for commissioning next year; Rs 30.4 crores out of Rs 93 crores fresh issue proceeds utilized as of September 30, 2026.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 154.2 crore (up 21.7% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 305.8 crore (up 19.9% YoY)
- EBITDA (including other income) for Q2: Rs 27.6 crore (up 26.9% YoY, margin 17.9%, up 74 bps YoY; down from 19.6% in Q1); H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 57.4 crore (up 25.1% YoY, margin 18.8%, up 75 bps YoY)
- Profit before tax (PBT): Rs 23.4 crore; Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 17.4 crore (up 27.7% YoY, net margin 11.3%); H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 36.6 crore (up 26% YoY)
- Earnings per share (EPS): Rs 4.26 for Q2; Rs 9.18 for H1 FY27
- Effective tax rate: 25.6%
- High-value product share: 62.9% of revenue in Q2 (vs 57.8% for full year FY26 and 60.4% in Q1 FY27)
- Order book: Rs 179.4 crore as of September 30, 2026 (execution tenure of 3 to 4 months)
- Net worth: Rs 427.3 crores as of September 30, 2026
- Cash and bank balances: Rs 114.6 crores as of September 30, 2026
- Capital expenditure: Rs 12.1 crores in H1, with a further Rs 4.1 crores paid as capital advances
- Fresh issue proceeds utilization: Rs 30.4 crores utilized out of Rs 93 crores fresh issue as of September 30, 2026; Rs 62.6 crores unutilized (held in bank deposits)
- Operating cash flow: Rs 16.9 crores in H1 FY27 (impacted by a deliberate inventory buildup of Rs 23.6 crores)
- Volumes: 1,365 tonnes sold in Q2 for crusher parts (vs 1,324 tonnes in Q1)

### Highlights
- Single cast piece size increased from 20 metric tons to 25 metric tons.
- Successfully set up an in-house NABL accredited laboratory to shorten qualification cycles and participate in GEM portal tenders.
- Unit 3 project is under construction and progressing on schedule.
- Planning rooftop and ground-mounted solar capacities for cost reduction and meeting carbon intensity reporting requirements for export customers.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management gave no specific numerical guidance for the second half of the year.
- Focus areas for the rest of the year: complete Unit 3, keep the product mix moving upward, convert defense and aerospace qualifications, and maintain a conservative balance sheet.
- Commercial production and meaningful contributions from new specialized rolls (ICDP/HSS) and defense/aerospace are expected starting next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remained firm across all three product lines throughout the quarter.
- Indian steel industry growth is driving multifold demand for ICDP (Indefinite Chilled Double Poured) and HSS (High-Speed Steel) rolls, which are currently largely imported.
- Engineering castings export order book remains steady (exports comprise 5% to 10% of the total order book; 90% of exports are engineering castings).
- Customer concentration is low: top 10 customers contribute less than 50% of revenue, with no single customer contributing more than 10%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Scrap and alloy input prices firmed up through the quarter; selling prices were reset with a 2 to 3-month lag.
- Inventory increased by Rs 23.6 crores to cover the order book against raw material price firming; inventory is expected to normalize next quarter.
- Power accounts for approximately 7% of total expenditure; planned solar installations are expected to reduce power costs via net metering.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Unit 3 construction is ongoing, targeting commissioning by next year (first half of next financial year).
- Expanding single cast piece size from 20 metric tons to 25 metric tons.
- Adding forward integration such as CNC machining and finishing facilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Term loans are fully paid off; net debt is negative (net cash).
- Cash and bank balances stood at Rs 114.6 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- Working capital inventory increased due to deliberate raw material stocking for confirmed orders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material and fuel/power costs firmed up during the quarter, creating a short-term pricing lag.
- Geographical and power supply disruptions in recent months caused a minor moderation in melt shop utilization.
- Defense and aerospace qualifications involve long-term, stage-wise inspection cycles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shweta (Systematix) on IPO proceeds utilization and Unit 3 timeline:** Management clarified that Rs 30.4 crores out of Rs 93 crores of fresh issue proceeds was utilized by September 30, 2026, and stated that Unit 3 construction is on track for commissioning next year. Management did not dodge.
2. **Rinku Gupta on QoQ profit dip:** Management explained that profits dipped slightly QoQ due to a 2 to 3-month lag in passing on rising raw material and power costs, but grew 26%-27% YoY. Management did not dodge.
3. **Rohan Sanghvi on differentiation and trials for ICDP/HSS rolls:** Management highlighted import substitution, made-to-order engineering, fourth-generation legacy relationships, and a first-mover advantage, stating trial lots will be sent immediately upon plant commissioning. Management did not dodge.
4. **Darshan on SMS/boundary utilization moderation in Q2:** Management attributed the dip to regional power and geographical disruptions rather than planned maintenance, noting it did not impact product output. Management did not dodge.
5. **Manas Shah on defense/aerospace timeline and NABL lab:** Management noted that obtaining the NABL lab certification is a major breakthrough that allows participation in GEM portal tenders, with meaningful revenue contribution expected by next year. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
Behari Lal Engineering delivered 21.7% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by a richer high-value product mix, even as temporary cost inflation and pricing lags caused a slight sequential margin compression.
