# Elgi Equipments Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 17% YoY with EBITDA up 28% YoY, despite ongoing commodity cost pressures and geopolitical headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Europe to be marginally profitable; Australia reorganization on track for Q3/Q4 recovery).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from broad optimism to acknowledging specific headwinds (geopolitical conflicts, commodity inflation) while maintaining confidence in mitigation.
- **New topics or risks:** Tariff refund (Rs 40 crore) recognized in P&L; "Vayu" product launch progress updated (35% geography coverage achieved).
- **Last quarter's promises:** MK2 campus phase 1 (global support center) is now on stream; Vayu launch on track.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue Growth:** 17% YoY (4% driven by foreign exchange).
- **EBITDA Growth:** 28% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 16.3% (up from 14.9% in the previous quarter).
- **PBT:** 15%.
- **Net Cash Addition:** Rs 200 million (excluding dividend).
- **Cash Profit (Apr-Sep):** Rs 337 crore.

### Highlights
- **Regional Performance:** India outperformed Rest of World (ROW) markets (52% vs 48% revenue mix).
- **Product Launch:** "Vayu" (low-cost compressor) achieved 35% geography coverage; full coverage expected by Jan/Feb 2027.
- **MK2 Campus:** Phase 1 (global support center) is on stream; move of diesel/portable compressor manufacturing to the new facility expected by Q1 2028.
- **Tariff Refund:** Rs 40 crore received; 50-60% recognized in Q2 P&L as a credit to material cost.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Europe:** Management expects to be marginally profitable in FY27 (break-even as worst-case).
- **Australia:** Reorganization expected to yield returns in Q3/Q4.
- **Cost Mitigation:** Management expects to mitigate commodity cost increases via internal cost-reduction initiatives over the next 6-9 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **India:** Strong demand driven by "Demand=Match" technology, go-to-market project, and economic buoyancy.
- **Europe:** Industrial business grew YoY, but portables (Italy) muted due to economic downturn.
- **US:** Industrial business remains strong; Pattons (distribution) slowly improving.
- **Middle East/Africa:** Africa performed well; Middle East impacted by conflict.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Material Costs:** Continued to increase; Q2 price corrections did not fully offset Q2 increases (impact expected in Q3).
- **Margin Drivers:** EBITDA margin improvement (14.9% to 16.3%) driven by volume and tariff refund.
- **Initiatives:** Management is implementing projects to reduce material, variable, and fixed costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **MK2 Program:** Ongoing; Rs 600 crore earmarked over 4-5 years.
- **IT/Infrastructure:** Shifted hardware to a leasing model; increased investment in cybersecurity and CRM.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** Net cash continues to grow.
- **Working Capital:** Investment primarily in inventory.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Commodity Inflation:** Rising metal commodity prices.
- **Geopolitical:** Conflicts impacting Europe and Middle East operations.
- **Macro:** Economic downturn in Europe.
- **Operational:** Cybersecurity (recurring IT cost).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Status of price corrections?** Management: Price increases up to July/August were pushed through; subsequent increases are not yet reflected in margins.
- **Q: Vayu margins?** Management: Lower than Elgi range at gross level, but EBITDA gap will narrow as aftermarket kicks in.
- **Q: Why are IT/Consulting costs high?** Management: IT costs rose due to cybersecurity, CRM implementation, and hardware leasing; legal/professional spend is stable.
- **Q: Tariff refund accounting?** Management: 50-60% recognized in Q2 as material cost credit; balance remains in inventory.
- **Q: Why is standalone other income higher than consolidated?** Management: Standalone includes dividends from subsidiaries, which are eliminated in consolidated results.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational performance and successful new product rollout (Vayu) offset by persistent commodity inflation and geopolitical headwinds.
