# Jash Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and profits grew YoY, supported by a strong consolidated order book and management confidence in hitting annual guidance.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 195 crore (+22% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 27.3 crore (+23% YoY)
- Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 14.3 crore (+30% YoY)
- Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 351 crore (+20% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 42 crore (+75% YoY)
- Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 239% higher YoY (exact base/number not specified)
- Consolidated Order Book: Rs 955 crore (International: Rs 625 crore, Domestic: Rs 330 crore)
- Standalone Revenue (Jash Engineering): Rs 227 crore for the year (growth of 9%, PAT up 18%)
- Rodney Hunt Revenue growth: 17% (PAT slightly negative)
- Waterfront Revenue: Rs 23 crore (+8% YoY, PAT positive)
- Jash Process Equipment Revenue: Rs 21 crore (vs Rs 25 crore last year, PAT slightly negative)
- Subsidiary Order Books: Jash Engineering Rs 561 crore, Rodney Hunt Rs 391 crore, Mahr Rs 27 crore, Waterfront Rs 31 crore, Jash Process Equipment Rs 32 crore
- Tariff refunds received: USD 0.54 million (USD 0.7 million pending)
- Rodney Hunt order book: USD 40 million (approx. Rs 391 crore)
- Waterfront U.K. target revenue: GBP 10-12 million (longer term)
- US business revenue target: USD 50 million

### Highlights
- Consolidated order book stands at Rs 955 crore after inter-company deductions.
- Orders worth approx Rs 46 crore negotiated and expected this month; Rs 129 crore under negotiation for the current quarter.
- WesTech India renamed to Jash Process Equipment; integration and SOP alignment underway, with certain products now manufactured in Indore.
- Destination-based delivery terms led to approx Rs 46 crore of dispatches remaining in-transit at September closing, impacting Q2 revenue recognition.
- Note on Record: Unlike past calls where data-center vessels were pursued more actively, management clarified that data-center orders are now treated as non-core and picked selectively due to high competition and capacity constraints.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY revenue guidance of Rs 875 crore (Jash Engineering: Rs 530 crore, Rodney Hunt: Rs 330 crore, Mahr: Rs 15 crore, Waterfront: Rs 60 crore, Jash Process Equipment: Rs 40 crore).
- Expects to achieve USD 35-36 million turnover in the US this year.
- Management targets reaching a consolidated order book of Rs 1,000 - 1,200 crore and crossing Rs 1,150 crore in the near term.
- Expects EBITDA margins to move above 20% over the next 1-2 years as international businesses scale.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Order inflows for Jash Process Equipment have improved, providing better visibility.
- UK market seeing firm inquiries and frame agreement discussions; expects 3-4 years down the line to reach GBP 10-12 million.
- Saudi/Middle East manufacturing plant plans temporarily parked due to geopolitical/war situations in the Gulf; will revisit next month.
- Data-center vessel segment is highly competitive and treated as job-work; management targets 20-25 such vessels annually depending on spare capacity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Historical gross margins range between 55-60%.
- Employee cost historically runs at around 18% of revenue on a full-year basis (though higher in H1 due to lower H1 revenue share).
- USD 0.54 million tariff refund booked during the quarter under consumption items.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Appointed a leading AMC contractor in the USA to evaluate proposals for a new US facility; construction expected to start around December and take 12 months to commission (operations expected to fully contribute from FY28-29).
- Orange facility in the US being enhanced with minor investments to cater to production requirements for the next 1.5 years.
- Existing global capacities can support up to Rs 1,200 crore in revenue.
- Saudi subsidiary set up as 100% owned; initial strategy involves component dispatch, assembly, and testing before full manufacturing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail beyond order book and revenue metrics.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delivery timeline and transit time challenges for exports from India to international markets.
- Labour availability challenges at existing US locations.
- Geopolitical and war situations in the Gulf delaying the Saudi plant execution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanju Marwha on Medium-term Margins & Onshoring:** Asked how management maintains confidence in 13-14% PAT / 21-23% EBITDA targets given onshoring and operating deleverage from new capacities. *Management answered that fixed costs will not grow at the same pace as revenue, and scaling US/UK businesses toward target volumes will drive operating leverage.*
2. **Dilip Kumar Sahu on Gross Margins & WesTech Integration:** Inquired about gross margin sustainability and WesTech (Jash Process Equipment) integration. *Management stated gross margins remain within the historical 55-60% band, and WesTech is being integrated via Jash SOPs, local manufacturing in Indore, and new product developments.*
3. **Vinayak on Q2 India Subdued Growth & Rodney Hunt:** Asked why India performance was muted while Rodney Hunt grew. *Management explained that destination-based delivery terms left Rs 46 crore of invoiced goods in transit at quarter-end, delaying revenue recognition.*
4. **Vinayak on US Plant Timeline & Capacity:** Asked if US plant commissioning is delayed to Q4 FY28 and if existing capacity is a bottleneck. *Management clarified existing capacity can handle up to Rs 1,200 crore, and the new US plant will come online by late 2027/early 2028.*
5. **Salil Desai on Tariff Refunds:** Asked about the tariff refund amount booked in Q2 and its line item. *Management stated USD 0.54 million was booked under consumption items.*
6. **Vinayak on Data-Center Vessels and M&A:** Asked about data-center vessel traction and acquisitions. *Management stated data-center vessels are taken selectively based on capacity (targeting 20-25 vessels/year), and M&A discussions are ongoing though premature to disclose.*

### One-line takeaway
Jash Engineering posted a 22% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 and maintained its full-year revenue guidance of Rs 875 crore, driven by strong international order books and scaling overseas subsidiaries despite transit headwinds.
