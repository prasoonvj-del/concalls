# KSH International Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong volume growth, rising export contributions, record PAT, and improved working capital and debt reduction.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 1,320 crore (up 85% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 90 crore (up from Rs. 46 crore YoY and Rs. 74 crore QoQ)
- **PAT:** Rs. 48 crore (up 62% YoY and from Rs. 42 crore QoQ)
- **EBITDA per ton:** ~Rs. 1,01,400 per metric ton (vs ~Rs. 65,500 YoY and ~Rs. 93,000 QoQ)
- **Sales volume:** 8,842 metric tons (up 26% YoY and 11% QoQ)
- **Installed capacity:** 57,845 metric tons as of September 30, 2026
- **Capacity utilization:** 76% in Q2 FY27 (vs 73% in Q1 FY27)
- **Export revenue growth:** Up 108% YoY; 32% of total revenue in Q2 (up from 26% in Q1)
- **Specialized Magnet Winding Wires growth:** 87% YoY
- **Standard wire growth:** 119% YoY
- **Operating cash flow (H1 FY27):** ~Rs. 43 crore positive
- **Total outstanding debt:** Reduced to Rs. 341 crore as of September 30, 2026 (down from Rs. 481 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Working capital days:** Improved to 52 days (vs 60 days in Q1 and ~70 days YoY)
- **Phase-3 expansion capital outlay:** Up to Rs. 185 crore (Rs. 1,850 million) over the next 18 months

### Highlights
- Completed 96% of Phase-1 and Phase-2 Supa capacity expansion as of September 9, 2026, roughly 6 months ahead of schedule.
- Power Grid approved the Supa facility to supply magnet winding wires up to 765 kV transformers and reactors.
- Board approved Phase-3 expansion to add specialized magnet winding wire capacity, targeting ~72,200 metric tons by Q1 FY29.
- Upcast recycle plant and rooftop solar plant are now operational, providing incremental benefits.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects ~26% volume growth for the full year to be sustainable, supported by expanded capacity in H2.
- Management expects a long-term sustainable EBITDA per ton of ~Rs. 85,000 post Phase-3 expansion (though individual quarters may vary based on mix and currency).
- Management aims to return exports to historical averages of ~40% of total revenue over the next few years.
- Optimum capacity utilization for new capacity is typically ~85%; management expects to reach 85% utilization on the 72,200 metric ton capacity between FY30 and FY31.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Sustained growth seen across power generation, transmission, renewables, railways, and data centers.
- CTC demand in India is expected to reach ~120,000 to 130,000 tons by FY30 (excluding exports).
- Received a trial order to supply specialized wires for 2 HVDC transformers overseas.
- Middle East transit times and transport costs increased due to disturbances, but no significant drop in business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- LME copper price and exchange rate are a direct pass-through (make-to-order model).
- High EBITDA per ton driven by elevated CTC contribution, higher export volume, weaker rupee, and operational recycling/solar benefits.
- Finance costs rose due to bank charges and finance costs charged by suppliers (trade payables backed by supply credit).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phase-3 expansion will add specialized magnet winding wire capacity, taking total capacity to ~72,200 metric tons by Q1 FY29.
- Capex of up to Rs. 185 crore includes purchasing 10 acres of land in Supa, buildings, and plant & machinery, funded by internal accruals and long-term debt.
- The 10-acre land parcel provides scope for an additional ~20,000 tons of capacity if required beyond Phase-3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt reduced by ~Rs. 140 crore from Q1 FY27 to Rs. 341 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Leverage ratio (Debt/Equity or similar) stood at 0.38x (down from 0.51x in Q1).
- Target ROCE is 20% inching closer to 25%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Increased transit times and transport costs in the Middle East due to regional disturbances.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shubham (ICICI Securities) asked about the sustainability of high EBITDA per ton and Phase-3 capex drivers.** Management clarified that elevated CTC and exports skewed Q2, and guided to a long-term sustainable EBITDA of Rs. 85,000/ton.
- **Abhi asked why finance costs rose despite debt reduction.** Management confirmed that supplier-charged finance costs on trade payables contributed to the increase.
- **Abhi asked if conversion charges per ton are rising with contract renewals.** Management stated that value-addition from higher-complexity products drives profitability more than like-for-like price increases.
- **Pratik Dharamshi asked about long-term customer agreements (e.g., Hitachi).** Management stated that customers increasingly seek long-term supply visibility, and discussions on framework agreements are ongoing.
- **Mayank Chaturvedi asked about the European HVDC trial order.** Management confirmed it is a trial order for 2 transformers that will open future possibilities once qualified.

### One-line takeaway
KSH International delivered stellar Q2 FY27 results with record PAT, 26% volume growth, and a strong export surge, prompting a Phase-3 expansion to 72,200 metric tons by FY29 while guiding to a long-term sustainable EBITDA of Rs. 85,000/ton.
