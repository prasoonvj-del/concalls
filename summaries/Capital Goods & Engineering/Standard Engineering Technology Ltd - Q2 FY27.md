# Standard Engineering Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong core business growth, successful execution, and the upcoming commercialization of the GScale data center infrastructure business.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 296.6 crore (up 62.3% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 51.4 crore (up 79.7% YoY).
*   **PBT:** Rs 39.2 crore (up 41.4% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 28.2 crore (up 37.9% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 544.3 crore (up 53% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 91 crore (up 56.5% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 54.9 crore (up 32.2% YoY).
*   **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 61 crore (vs Rs 45 crore in full FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Core Business Transformation:** Shifting from equipment manufacturing to an integrated engineering solutions provider (concept to turnkey projects) for pharma, chemical, and food industries.
*   **Strategic Investments:** Acquired 19.19% stake in GL Hakko (Japan) to enhance glass lining technology and R&D.
*   **GScale Update:** Secondary fluid network machinery installation is underway; revenue generation targeted from December 15, 2026. Power products production targeted from January 15, 2027.
*   **Stake Increase:** Management plans to increase stake in GScale to 51% via a share swap, pending stock exchange approval.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue:** Management guided for Rs 1,450 crore (comprising ~50% growth in core business and Rs 250 crore from GScale).
*   **Export Target:** Management aims for 20-30% of revenue from exports in the next 2-3 years.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management expects GScale EBITDA margins between 20-25%; core business targeting 20% in coming years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Pharma/CDMO:** Strong order visibility and pipeline due to capacity expansion in the sector.
*   **Exports:** Increased inquiries from US and Europe; current export contribution is 7-8%.
*   **Data Centers:** Management cited reports suggesting India's data center capacity could grow to 15-50 GW in the next 10-15 years, driven by sovereign data mandates.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** EBITDA growth supported by operating leverage and product portfolio expansion.
*   **Profitability Impact:** PBT/PAT growth lagged EBITDA growth due to reduced interest income, as cash reserves were deployed for acquisitions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Core Business:** Current capacity is 8 lakh sq ft; planning to add 3-4 lakh sq ft. Potential production capacity post-expansion is Rs 3,500-4,000 crore.
*   **GScale:** 4 lakh sq ft total; 2.9 lakh sq ft operational from January 15, 2027; additional 1 lakh sq ft in 3-4 months.
*   **Greenfield:** Building a 1.5-1.6 million sq ft facility on a 36-acre site.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Cash reserves previously held in fixed deposits were deployed for GScale and GL Hakko acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution Risk:** GScale revenue targets are subject to timely production readiness and project execution.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Global data center market slowdown concerns, though management remains bullish on the Indian market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Rajat Agarwal (GScale stake):** Asked about the plan to increase stake. Management confirmed they are waiting for exchange approval to move to 51% ownership via share swap.
*   **Rohit Taparia (PAT decline):** Asked why profit declined. Management clarified it was due to lower interest income following the deployment of cash into acquisitions, not operational issues.
*   **Viraj Mahadevia (Export strategy):** Asked how they are capitalizing on global opportunities. Management noted tie-ups with resellers (IPP, API Pharma, Biocon Solutions) and direct sales in Japan via GL Hakko.
*   **Ishaan Bhave (GScale revenue):** Asked if the Rs 250 crore target is feasible. Management confirmed 10+ inquiries and that the factory is on track for a December 15 launch.
*   **Maitri Shah (Data center slowdown):** Asked about global slowdown concerns. Management stated India is insulated due to sovereign data mandates and the need for local capacity.

### One-line takeaway
Standard Engineering is aggressively scaling its data center infrastructure business (GScale) while maintaining strong core business growth, with management reiterating a consolidated revenue target of Rs 1,450 crore for FY27.
