# Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Headline numbers are humming with strong gross profit expansion, record order backlog, and improving adjusted EBITDA.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** 12% QoQ growth (Q2 FY27); H1 revenue grew 33% YoY.
- **Gross Margins:** Expanded to ~15% in Q2 FY27 (compared to 13% YoY and 12% in prior quarter); segment gross margin for manufacturing hit a historical high of 25.5%.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 171 crore in Q2 FY27 (66% higher QoQ); H1 adjusted EBITDA grew 70% YoY. Expected full-year adjusted EBITDA of INR 650 crore to INR 700 crore (growing 60%-70% YoY from FY26 base of INR 420 crore).
- **Order Backlog:** INR 14,500 crore (all-time high, up 67% YoY).
- **Working Capital:** Around 30-31 days.
- **Full Year Guidance:** Revenue growth of 25% to 30%; adjusted EBITDA growth of 60% to 70%.

### Highlights
- First post-listing earnings call.
- Operating leverage kicking in, resulting in gross margins and EBITDA growing faster than revenue.
- Manufacturing segment H1 revenue at INR 4,448 crore (20% higher YoY) with segment EBITDA at 8.3% in Q2 vs 6.6% in the previous quarter.
- Ecosystem segment H1 revenue grew 50% YoY, with Q2 revenue around INR 2,000 crore (-7% QoQ due to commodity prices); operates at 2%-3% gross margin and 1% EBITDA.
- 80% of revenue comes from repeat customers. Revenue coverage stands at roughly three years.
- *Note vs Record:* The call confirms the robust H1 FY27 financial performance and all-time high order backlog noted in the investor deck record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for full-year revenue growth of 25% to 30% and adjusted EBITDA growth of 60% to 70%.
- Management expects to be PBT positive in H2 FY27.
- Management expects to be OCF neutral to positive on a full-year basis, and OCF positive starting next year (FY28).
- Management expects full-year gross margins to be closer to 21%, with potential 100-200 bps annual improvements toward the 25% target by FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Revenue breakdown for manufacturing H1: Energy 64%, Precision 22%, Capital Goods 14%. Geographic split: 59% India, 41% International.
- Fulfillment split H1: 60% captive factories, 40% marketplace model.
- Strong demand in the U.S. and India driven by renewables, electrification, thermal power, and data center/AI capex cycles (exposure to U.S. AI capex at 10%-20% of order book).
- U.S. power transformer lead times are 3-5 years; Zetwerk is offering 2 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Upfront costs are primarily people costs (software engineering, product/design engineering).
- 3M margin drivers: Mix (higher-margin products like aerospace/defense), Model (captive vs. marketplace), and Market (international).
- Discontinued operations provisioned with no further losses/provisions expected; operationally breakeven in Q2.
- ESOP charges were higher in Q2 (INR-related vesting); expected to normalize in H2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Primary proceeds from the IPO (completed October 23, 2026) are being used to retire debt, reducing finance costs in H2 and FY28.
- Long-term category expansion ongoing into new areas like space, robotics, and battery energy storage (BESS).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital remains under control at ~30-31 days.
- TTM operating cash flow (OCF) was negative INR 97 crore, but improved significantly (67% QoQ improvement in Q2, ~50% YoY improvement in H1).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Defense industry noted as notoriously cyclical.
- Supply-constrained business: order backlog is growing faster than revenue, requiring ongoing capacity ramp-up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Varun Gandhi (Finavenue Growth Fund) on pricing power:** Management stated they do not charge a premium on standard marketplace pricing, but for high-demand categories like transformers where they offer a compressed lead time of 2 years vs the industry's 3-4 years, they price at the market rate for that expedited service.
2. **Tejas Mehta (Sierra Capital) on OCF and working capital:** Management confirmed the company will be neutral to positive on OCF for the full year and OCF positive next year, with working capital at ~30-31 days.
3. **Gaurav Jawalkar (JM Financial) on PBT and discontinued operations:** Management stated they expect to be PBT positive in H2 FY27, noting that Q2 ESOP charges were elevated due to IPO vesting and will normalize. Discontinued operations will see no further provisions or losses.
4. **Tejas Mehta on U.S. AI capex exposure:** Management acknowledged 10%-20% exposure to the U.S. AI capex cycle via the order book, but noted they are not seeing any slowdown in customer behavior or RFQs.
5. **Tejas Mehta on European expansion:** Management stated they are doubling down on the U.S. and India because both offer strong GDP growth and supply-side decoupling from China, whereas Europe has not yet decoupled.

### One-line takeaway
Zetwerk reported a strong Q2/H1 FY27 post-listing, highlighted by a 67% YoY surge in order backlog to INR 14,500 crore, expanding gross margins to ~15%, and significant operating leverage driving a 66% QoQ jump in adjusted EBITDA.
