# Elgi Equipments Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and EBITDA growth across geographies, with management expressing confidence in sustaining margins and future performance.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue Growth:** 23% YoY (includes 7% exchange-related impact).
*   **EBITDA Growth:** 28% YoY.
*   **PAT Margin:** 9.7% (similar to Q1 FY26).
*   **Aftermarket Revenue Contribution:** ~28-30% in India; ~15-16% in rest of the world.
*   **Warranty Cost:** <1% of revenue.
*   **Tariff Refund:** ~$4 million approved; ~$1.6-1.8 million received.

### Highlights
*   **Regional Performance:** India standalone grew 28%, North America 37%, Europe 21%, and Australia 17%.
*   **Technology:** "Demand=Match" technology launched last September; now standard across Tier 1, 2, and 3 compressors. Global rollout expected within this year.
*   **New Projects:** Tier 4 segment (bottom of the pyramid) launch scheduled for September in Hyderabad; products validated with initial orders received.
*   **Operational Changes:** Reorganization underway in Australia, Europe, and the US to improve efficiency.
*   **Vacuum Business:** Local production and sales have commenced with "phenomenal" percentage growth in Q1, though from a small base.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to continue improving. Long-term target is 18% by 2031.
*   **Pricing:** Price corrections implemented to offset 5-9% raw material cost increases; impact expected to be fully realized by Q3.
*   **Growth:** Expects continued growth in India, though growth rates may moderate compared to Q1 due to a higher base in subsequent quarters of the previous year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Drivers:** Growth in India driven by EV ecosystem, renewable energy, and semiconductor sectors.
*   **Inquiries:** Demand remains strong, though gestation periods for finalization are longer than normal.
*   **Competition:** Management claims no technological gap with global peers (Atlas Copco, Ingersoll Rand) and cites superior energy efficiency and lower lifecycle costs as key differentiators.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Materials:** Faced 5-9% cost increases; mitigated via cost reduction and price corrections.
*   **Employee Costs:** Increase attributed to global increments.
*   **Other Expenses:** 12% increase due to new rental premises for the motor plant and US facilities.
*   **Efficiency:** In-sourcing of motors and ongoing cost-reduction initiatives are key margin drivers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Primary focus is on the "MK2" program (shifting to a new campus) and land acquisition in Italy (contracted six years ago).
*   **Distribution:** Strategy focuses on reorganizing networks for deeper penetration rather than just increasing distributor count.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Net cash position remains "very strong."

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical/Macro:** Potential impact of future US tariffs (if changed by administration) and commodity price volatility.
*   **Operational:** Challenges in service business in Australia and North America; restructuring costs expected to continue.
*   **Market:** Southeast Asia remains a challenging market for an Indian brand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How does Elgi compete with global giants?** Management emphasized that 80% of the market prioritizes energy efficiency and maintenance over price, where Elgi claims to be world-class.
*   **Q: Is the 28% India growth volume or price-led?** Management clarified it was primarily volume-driven, with only marginal price corrections taken.
*   **Q: Why is the aftermarket contribution lower globally vs. India?** Management noted that in India, service is a smaller part of the revenue mix compared to the US, where service is a significant, albeit lumpy, revenue stream.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Tier 4 product launch?** Management confirmed the launch is on track for September, with distributors trained and internal organization in place.
*   **Q: Will the cost trajectory continue to rise?** Management stated costs will rationalize as top-line grows and through a new "process project" aimed at standardizing operations globally.

### One-line takeaway
Elgi delivered a strong Q1 with broad-based growth and margin resilience, supported by successful technology adoption and a clear strategy to scale through operational efficiency and new product launches.
