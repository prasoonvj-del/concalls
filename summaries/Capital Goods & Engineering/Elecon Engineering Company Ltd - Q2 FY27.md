# Elecon Engineering Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - 10.5% YoY revenue growth, expanding EBITDA margins, and a robust order book providing strong H2 visibility.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 639 crore, up 10.5% YoY (from Rs 578 crore in Q2 FY26) and up 22.7% QoQ (from Rs 521 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Up 11.1% YoY (adjusted for Rs 25 crore onetime arbitration settlement in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 148 crore, up 17.7% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 23.1%, expanding 140 bps YoY.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 257 crore, up 11.4% YoY on an adjusted basis; margin at 22.2%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 96 crore, up 9.8% YoY; PAT margin at 15.1%.
- **Gear Division Q2 Revenue:** Rs 488 crore, up 10.6% YoY (76% of consolidated revenue).
- **Gear Division Overseas Revenue:** Up 30.3% YoY.
- **Gear Division EBIT & Margin:** EBIT rose 27.2% to Rs 108 crore; EBIT margin expanded 290 bps to 22.1%.
- **Gear Division Order Intake:** Rs 493 crore in Q2; H1 order intake up 8.8% to Rs 1,063 crore.
- **Gear Division Open Order Book:** Rs 1,056 crore as of September 30, 2026, up 37% YoY.
- **MHE Division Q2 Revenue:** Rs 151 crore, up 10.4% YoY.
- **MHE Division Order Intake:** Rs 253 crore in Q2 (up 32.5% YoY), including overseas orders of Rs 68 crore. H1 order intake rose 34.8% to Rs 438 crore.
- **MHE Division Open Order Book:** Rs 579 crore as of September 30, 2026, up 27.3% YoY.
- **Overseas Business:** Q2 overseas revenue increased 30.6% YoY to Rs 159 crore. H1 overseas revenue reached Rs 309 crore (up 26.2% YoY), contributing 27% of consolidated revenue. International order inflow in Q2 was Rs 230 crore; international outstanding order book stood at Rs 326 crore.
- **Working Capital Cycle:** Normalized to 89 days from 98 days in FY26.
- **Net Cash Position:** Approximately Rs 700 crore.
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 0.50 per equity share (face value Rs 1).

### Highlights
- Long-term credit rating outlook upgraded from AA Stable to AA Positive; short-term rating reaffirmed at A1+.
- Lead banker released collateral security for fund-based and non-fund-based working capital limits, removing hypothecation on movable and immovable properties (including plant and machinery).
- MHE division secured international orders worth approximately USD 9 million during H1.
- Catalog vs. engineered product mix for gears stands at approximately 53% catalog and 47% engineered.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects mid-double-digit consolidated revenue growth (around 15%) for the remaining part of the year.
- Management guides for a minimum EBITDA margin percentage at last year's level (FY26).
- Gear division EBIT margin guided to be maintained in the 20% to 22% range.
- MHE division EBIT margin guided to stay in the 22% to 24% range.
- Management maintains the aspirational target of reaching Rs 5,000 crore top line and 50% overseas revenue mix by FY30, supported by organic growth and potential inorganic acquisitions abroad.
- Defence P-17 Bravo orders expected to be awarded at the beginning of FY29, with execution taking 2 to 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian heavy-duty industrial gearbox market estimated to grow at approximately 8% CAGR through FY30.
- Domestic demand remained stable, though experiencing near-term softness/lethargy due to monsoon impacts and delayed project execution clearances; momentum is expected to pick up post-Diwali.
- Strong demand drivers include public capex, infrastructure, steel capacity expansion, cement, power, fertilizers, ports, and data center cooling tower applications.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material steel price volatility stabilized in Q2 compared to abnormal spikes in Q1.
- Margin expansion driven by price hikes, favorable product mix, and higher contributions from high-margin overseas export orders.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 3-year capital expenditure program remains on track.
- De-bottlenecking ongoing; gear division operating at around 60% capacity utilization with targeted machine additions to relieve constraints.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Shareholders' funds stood at Rs 2,509 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- No conventional borrowings on the balance sheet; reported debt primarily represents lease liabilities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic uncertainty, geopolitical developments, raw material price volatility, rupee depreciation, and high inflation rates.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Garvit Goyal (Analyst) asked about domestic market softness and guidance clarity.** Management clarified that monsoon disruptions and steel price volatility caused temporary project sluggishness, but firm order backlogs and post-Diwali momentum support a ~15% H2 revenue growth target.
2. **Raj Shah (Enam AMC) asked about defence order timelines for P-17 Bravo.** Management stated RFPs were issued, budgetary offers submitted, and final order awards from shipyards are expected in Q1 FY29 as a single-bidder follow-on to P-17 Alpha.
3. **Pathanjali Srinivasan (Sundaram Mutual Fund) inquired about the drivers behind Gear division margin expansion.** Management attributed it to price hikes, improved engineered product mix, and a higher share of high-margin export orders executed in Q2.
4. **Gaurav Nigam (Tunga Investments) asked about the sustainability of export growth.** Management explained that overseas demand is shifting from standard gearboxes to customized application-driven solutions, data centers, and oil and gas applications, especially in the US and Middle East.
5. **Het (Quantum AMC) asked about the path to the Rs 5,000 crore FY30 target.** Management stated growth will come from organic expansion, marine defence orders, heavy-duty steel gearboxes, MHE exports, and potential inorganic acquisitions in the US and Europe.

### One-line takeaway
Elecon delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with 10.5% revenue growth and a 140 bps expansion in EBITDA margin, underpinned by robust international execution and a strong order book providing positive visibility for H2.
