# Bonfiglioli Transmissions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q3 CY26
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly revenue and robust profit growth across all three business verticals, driven by strong domestic and export demand.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 6,602 million (up 30.2% YoY)
- **Domestic Revenue Growth:** 23.8% YoY
- **Export Revenue Growth:** 46.2% YoY (Exports constituted 30.6% of 9M CY26 revenue)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 1,289 million (up 38% YoY), EBITDA margin at 19.5%
- **PAT (Net Profit):** Rs 871 million (up 38.2% YoY), PAT margin at 13.1%
- **9M CY26 Revenue:** Rs 17,920 million (up 22.8% YoY), EBITDA margin at 19.7%, PAT margin at 13.1%
- **Segment Q3 YoY Growth:** Industrial Automation +30.5%, Off-highway Mobility +54.9%, Wind +8%
- **Capex:** Rs 1,000 million planned for CY26 (Rs 70-80 crores invested up to Sept), Rs 1,500 million planned for CY27

### Highlights
- Maiden quarterly earnings conference call post-listing.
- All three business verticals (Industrial Automation, Off-highway Mobility, and Wind) recorded their highest-ever quarterly revenues.
- Annualized fixed asset turnover stood at 8.06x as of September 30, 2026.
- *Record check:* Performance aligns with the post-IPO investor deck record of record financial results.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year CY26 revenue growth to be around 20%.
- Management aims for a revenue CAGR of 15% over CY26-28.
- Management targets an EBITDA margin in the vicinity of 18.5% for the CY26-28 period as a general trend.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broad-based recovery in Indian manufacturing, with strong demand in material handling, mining, food & beverage, factory automation, biogas, and wastewater processing.
- Off-highway mobility growth was supported by new program wins and maturing customer relationships.
- Wind segment faced a 30% YoY decline from its top customer (GE) in 9M CY26, which was offset by 22% growth from other customers and global wins; GE recovery is expected in H1 CY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by operational efficiencies, localization, VAVE initiatives, pricing actions, and favorable forex.
- Management noted that commodity cost increases are not yet fully reflected in material costs and forex benefits may not repeat fully, justifying a conservative margin outlook of 18.5%.
- Effective royalty payment is 4.4% to 4.5% of sales (under a fixed 6% royalty rate on specified products).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CY27 capex of Rs 1,500 million will cover regular investments and the initiation of a heavy-duty gearbox manufacturing plant in Pune (Talegaon).
- Heavy-duty plant construction will start in H1 CY27 with production contributing meaningfully post-CY28.
- Mannur plant lease renewed for 3 years with a 6-month notice period; operations will be shifted to Talegaon, Pune in H2 CY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is a net cash company; entire capex will be funded through internal accruals with no debt borrowing planned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange benefits and temporary lags in raw material cost pass-through may not repeat in future quarters.
- Demand volatility from key customers in the wind segment (e.g., GE).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dhimant Shah (ITI Mutual Fund)** asked about growth tailwinds and heavy-duty segment plans. Management confirmed plans to build a heavy-duty plant in Pune starting H1 CY27, contributing post-CY28.
- **Raj Shah (Analyst)** asked why full-year revenue growth is guided at 20% when 9M growth is 23%. Management explained that customer schedules vary by quarter and Q4 CY25 had a high base.
- **Mohit Kumar (Analyst)** asked if the CY26-28 margin guidance of 18.5% is overly conservative given 9M margins of 19.7%. Management stated that forex gains and unabsorbed commodity cost increases may normalize.
- **Ridhi Agarwal (Analyst)** asked about the heavy-duty plant location vs. previous mentions of Cheyyar. Management clarified that the plant will be in Talegaon, Pune (adjacent to existing land) for operational synergies, calling prior news of Cheyyar misquoted.

### One-line takeaway
Bonfiglioli Transmissions posted a robust Q3 CY26 with record revenues across all verticals, supported by strong domestic demand and exports, while laying the groundwork for heavy-duty expansion.
