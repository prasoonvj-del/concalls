# GE Power India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY 2026-27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong order intake growth (38% YoY), revenue expansion, and strategic execution including international wins and NCLT approval for the Durgapur demerger.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Not explicitly updated or discussed in numeric terms; maintained strategic focus on high-margin core services.
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, bolstered by robust domestic power demand and international expansion (Shoaiba project).
- New topics or risks: Shoaiba Fuel Conversion Project details in Saudi Arabia (~INR 561 crores total value); cash pool lending increased to INR 741 crores with enhanced shareholder limit of INR 1,000 crores.
- Last quarter's promises: Durgapur demerger received NCLT approval on 13 October 2026, advancing the process initiated last quarter.

### Key numbers
- Order intake (Q2 FY27): INR 331 crores (up 38% YoY from INR 240 crores).
- Core orders (Q2 FY27): INR 232 crores (up 43% YoY).
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): INR 311 crores (up 11% YoY from INR 281 crores).
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): INR 620 crores (up from INR 567 crores).
- Profit before tax and exceptional items from continuing operations (Q2 FY27): INR 53 crores (vs INR 46 crores YoY).
- Profit before tax and exceptional items from continuing operations (H1 FY27): INR 122 crores (vs INR 90 crores YoY).
- Order backlog (as of 30 September 2026): INR 1,527 crores.
- Cash pool lending (as of 30 September 2026): INR 741 crores (earning 5.65%-6.25% interest).
- Trade receivables (net): INR 756 crores.
- Shoaiba Fuel Conversion Project order value: ~INR 561 crores total (~INR 76 crores booked in Q2, remaining ~INR 485 crores scheduled for Q3).
- Non-GE fleet contribution to orders: 15%-17%.

### Highlights
- Secured two Notices of Award from Dar Al Balad for the Shoaiba Fuel Conversion Project in Saudi Arabia (~INR 561 crores cumulative).
- Received NCLT Mumbai Bench sanction for the demerger of the Durgapur manufacturing business to JSW Energy Limited (certified copy received 13 October 2026). Entered into a 5-year manufacturing services agreement with JSW Energy.
- Total Addressable Market (TAM) assessed at ~INR 6,500 crores and Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) at INR 3,700 crores, with GEPIL addressing ~21% market share.
- Shareholders approved an enhanced cash pool lending limit of up to INR 1,000 crores in August 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains committed to driving sustainable growth in core services, focusing on consistent profits and cash generation.
- Continued focus on short-cycle, high-margin, cash-accretive opportunities (core payback in 12-15 months, upgrades in 24 months) rather than long gestation EPC projects.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's peak electricity demand reached ~270 GW in Q2 FY27; total electricity generation increased 11% YoY to ~536 billion units.
- Thermal power remains vital for base-load requirements and grid stability, driving plant operators to focus on availability, reliability, efficiency, and life-cycle cost optimization.
- CEA has a plan for non-decommissioning of units till 2030 with ~66 GW of refurbishment plans; GEPIL reported winning 100% of RFQs issued in this space so far.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Strategic pivot away from volume-driven revenue chasing toward higher-margin, shorter-cycle, cash-accretive opportunities continues to expand margins.
- Cash pool funds earn interest between 5.65% and 6.25%, above normal fixed deposit rates, backed by a parent corporate guarantee from GE Vernova Inc.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Announced a repair facility expansion in Vadodara to the tune of INR 18 crores in May 2026.
- International expansion anchored by the Shoaiba Fuel Conversion Project in Saudi Arabia (boiler fuel conversion for 9 out of 14 units over a 2.5-year execution window).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net receivables stood at INR 756 crores (including legacy FGD EPC receivables), noted by management as all good with nothing sticky or doubtful.
- Cash pool deployment with LM Wind Power Blades India Private Limited increased to INR 741 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as major business risks by management, other than navigating market-driven timelines for CEA-mandated modernization projects and managing legacy FGD backlog execution (~20% of current backlog).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tushar Bhavsar (Cognizance 4D MPL) asked about the counterparty, terms, and rationale for the INR 741 crores cash pool lending.** Management replied that funds are deployed with LM Wind Power Blades India Private Limited at 5.65%-6.25% interest, repayable on demand, and backed by a GE Vernova Inc. corporate guarantee.
- **Murthy Vallura asked when order books will reach four digits based on CEA modernization mandates.** Management stated that while they have won 100% of RFQs so far and view this as a major focus area, exact timing is market-driven and futuristic.
- **Mehul Savla asked about losses in subsidiary NGSL and tax rate outlook.** Management clarified that NGSL contributed a ~INR 5 crores operational loss at the consolidated level, and tax outgo currently applies only to interest earned on cash pools/FDs until accumulated losses are fully absorbed (approx. 2 years).
- **Sanjay Kohli asked if the Shoaiba project is margin-accretive and whether quarterly numbers will smooth out.** Management affirmed the project follows their high-margin, short-cycle strategy, while reiterating focus on core execution.
- **Arjun Goyal asked whether the Shoaiba project aligns with the new services strategy and the FGD proportion in current revenue.** Management confirmed Shoaiba is a boiler upgrade service project, and stated that FGD backlog accounts for ~20% of total backlog and 14%-15% of current quarterly sales.

### One-line takeaway
GE Power India Limited delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 38% YoY order growth, driven by core services momentum and an international win in Saudi Arabia, alongside regulatory advancement on the Durgapur demerger.
