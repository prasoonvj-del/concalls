# Dee Development Engineers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by strong revenue growth, margin expansion, and a robust order book following the completion of the major growth capex cycle.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 294.5 crore, up 31.6% YoY.
- Operating EBITDA: Rs. 49.7 crore, up 38.7% YoY.
- EBITDA margin: 16.9%, compared to 16.0% in Q1 FY26.
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs. 16.1 crore, up 22.4% YoY.
- Order book: Rs. 2,428 crore as of 30 June 2026.
- Net debt: Rs. 718 crore as of Q1 FY27 closing, down from Rs. 733 crore at the end of FY25-26.
- Biomass pellet plant capacity: 72,000 MT per annum.

### Highlights
- Q1 FY27 is the first quarter reflecting early operational results from the completed growth capex cycle, including the Anjar pipe fabrication facility and the seamless pipe plant.
- Around Rs. 25 crore of dispatches scheduled for Q1 got pushed into Q2 (primarily oil and gas export side) due to export customers deferring take-offs amid the Middle East situation; material is ready.
- Received a major domestic purchase order of Rs. 386.82 crore from Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) for piping manufacturing and supply.
- Commissioned a biomass pellet plant co-located with the Malwa Power Plant at Muktsar in Q1 FY27; revised Malwa tariff stands at Rs. 5.44 per kWh for the full year.
- Preferential issue of Rs. 300 crore was allotted on 8 July 2026 (net proceeds approx. Rs. 293 crore), with ~Rs. 225 crore earmarked for debt repayment/prepayment and the balance for general corporate purposes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains fully committed to a top-line of Rs. 1,500 crore plus and an EBITDA margin of more than 19% for FY27.
- Management targets fresh order inflows of more than Rs. 2,000 crore for FY27.
- Net debt by the close of FY27 is expected to be no more than Rs. 400-425 crore.
- Anjar facility peak revenue potential of around Rs. 1,500 crore targeted by FY28-29, with 60-65% capacity utilization expected in FY27 and 100% by the end of next year.
- Thailand facility target revenue is Rs. 170 crore to Rs. 200 crore; capacity is 100% booked for the next three years with no expansion planned.
- Non-core segment targeting a combined revenue of around Rs. 80 crore for FY26 (Note: stated as FY26 in transcript).
- Aiming to reach Rs. 2,500 crore top-line by FY29 instead of FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's capex cycle is building momentum; overseas markets are seeing a clear pickup across energy, process industries, and infrastructure.
- Power and oil and gas sectors continue to anchor execution and the order book.
- Exploring new sectors like data centers and planning to enter the nuclear sector within two years (advanced partner discussions underway to close in Q2).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin improvement is driven by better capacity utilization, operating leverage, and initial backward integration contribution from the seamless pipe plant.
- Gross margins may fluctuate/fall slightly due to a higher material cost mix in power sector jobs compared to oil and gas/job work, but conversion costs are lower, supporting EBITDA margin guidance of >19%.
- Effective tax rate for consolidated hovers between 20% to 21% (standalone at 25.17%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Major growth capex cycle completed in FY26 (Anjar facility and seamless pipe plant). No new major capex or capacity expansion planned for Thailand.
- Seamless pipe plant focused on high wall thickness and P-92 pipes for 800 MW/660 MW coal-fired boilers; expects ~20% EBITDA on seamless pipe manufacturing. Captive vs. outright sale utilization planned at 50-50.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced to Rs. 718 crore in Q1 FY27 from Rs. 733 crore; preferential issue proceeds of ~Rs. 225 crore utilized for debt/working capital reduction. No new debt planned for the year.
- Working capital metrics in Q1 FY27: Inventory days at 174 (down from 243 in Q1 FY26), debtor days at 123 (up from 99), creditor days at 34 (down from 66 due to intentional cash/low credit payments to leverage purchase prices), total working capital days at 263 (down from 276). Target is to bring working capital days to 180-200 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Export customer take-offs deferred in Q1 due to the Middle East situation, delaying ~Rs. 25 crore of dispatches to Q2.
- BHEL's execution pace is slower than expected, though order inflow and tender preparation remain ongoing.
- GE HRSG order is delayed, though management noted it will not impact top-line guidance due to traction from other clients like Nooter Eriksen and Siemens.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anirudh Agarwal (Value Quest):** Asked about HRSG and GT piping progress with GE and Siemens. Management stated GE GT orders are on track, HRSG order is delayed but offset by Nooter Eriksen (filling Thailand facility 100%), and a Memorandum of Understanding is signed with Siemens starting next year (10 units in year one, scaling up).
2. **Pranay Roop Chatterjee (Prabhudas Lilladher):** Inquired about BHEL domestic power orders and power PPA EBIT drop. Management noted BHEL is slow but active, and PPA EBIT dropped due to temporary diversion of fuel from the power plant to run the newly commissioned pellet plant.
3. **Riken Gopani (Capri Global):** Asked about inflow outlook and fertilizer projects. Management guided for Rs. 2,000+ crore inflows for the year and stated domestic fertilizer projects (like Assam) are being eyed for future years.
4. **Vinit (Institutional Investor):** Asked about P-91/P-92 seamless pipe usage in HRSG versus coal-fired boilers. Management clarified these are for high-thickness coal boilers (not HRSG), expecting ~20% EBITDA on seamless pipes with a 50-50 captive/external sales split.
5. **Nishant Bhatt (Marquee Equity/Emkay):** Asked about gross margin depression and asset turnover. Management reiterated that higher material content in power jobs impacts gross margins, but consolidated asset turnover is 1.39 and EBITDA margin guidance of >19% remains intact.

### One-line takeaway
DEE Development Engineers delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with a 31.6% revenue growth and 38.7% EBITDA growth, backed by completed capex, robust order inflows, and strong operational leverage, keeping management firmly confident of hitting its >19% EBITDA margin and Rs. 1,500+ crore revenue guidance.
