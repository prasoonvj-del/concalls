# Carborundum Universal Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance with double-digit segment growth across all three segments, despite exceptional items from European subsidiary closures.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 911 crore (up 28% YoY from Rs 712 crore; up 7.7% QoQ). H1 FY27 standalone sales at Rs 1,757 crore (up 24.6% YoY).
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,455 crore (up 13.1% YoY from Rs 1,287 crore; up 3.1% QoQ). H1 FY27 consolidated sales at Rs 2,865 crore (up 14.9% YoY).
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 72 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 12.4% YoY from Rs 64 crore; down from Rs 88 crore QoQ due to subsidiary dividend seasonality).
- **Consolidated PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 138 crore (up 22.9% YoY from Rs 113 crore; up 16% QoQ).
- **Consolidated PAT (after non-controlling interest):** Rs 12 crore in Q2 FY27 compared to Rs 75 crore in Q2 FY26, impacted by an exceptional item of Rs 18.3 crore for AWUKO. H1 FY27 consolidated PAT after NCI at Rs 89 crore.
- **Standalone Segment Results (PBIT):** Rs 134 crore (up ~20% YoY from Rs 112 crore). Ceramics PBIT Rs 67 crore (up 48.2% YoY), Electrominerals PBIT Rs 29 crore (up 49.1% YoY), Abrasives PBIT Rs 37 crore (down 19.9% YoY).
- **Consolidated Segment Results (PBIT):** Rs 163 crore (up 26.3% YoY from Rs 129 crore; up 22.4% QoQ). Ceramics PBIT Rs 101 crore (up 62.1% YoY).
- **Capex:** Rs 127 crore spent in H1 FY27. Full-year consolidated capex guidance maintained at Rs 400 crore.
- **Balance Sheet:** Consolidated debt at Rs 362 crore, cash at Rs 251 crore (excluding VAW cash), resulting in net debt of Rs 111 crore and debt-to-equity ratio of 0.09.
- **Subsidiary Performance:** 
  - VAW Russia sales RUB 1.55 billion (flat YoY), PAT RUB 98.7 million.
  - Foskor Zirconia sales ZAR 53 million, loss of ZAR 24 million.
  - Rhodius sales EUR 14.5 million (down 16.8% YoY), net loss of EUR 0.3 million.
  - AWUKO sales EUR 1.1 million; entered into asset sale agreements for Rs 5.34 crore and social plan with employees, recognizing a Rs 18.3 crore exceptional item.

### Highlights
- Standalone sales marked the highest-ever quarterly sales reported.
- All three standalone segments recorded double-digit H1 growth: Ceramics up 25.9%, Electrominerals up 32.1%, and Abrasives up 15.3%.
- AWUKO closure process is near completion with agreements signed in October 2026 for the sale of property, plant, equipment, and inventory for Rs 5.34 crore, followed by final liquidation and de-registration.
- Foskor Zirconia is operating at low levels and management realistically expects to complete its exit/resolution in Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Consolidated Sales Growth:** Maintained comparable growth guidance of 11% to 12% (excluding Foskor and AWUKO). 
- **Segment Sales Growth Guidance:** 
  - Abrasives (ex-AWUKO): Maintained at 11% to 12%.
  - Ceramics: Revised upward from 23%-25% to 25% to 28%.
  - Electrominerals (ex-Foskor): Maintained at 9% to 10%.
- **Margin Guidance:** 
  - Consolidated Abrasives (ex-AWUKO): Revised from 9.5%-10% to 8% to 8.5% due to H1 standalone margin drop and cost pressures.
  - Consolidated Ceramics: Revised upward from 20.5%-21% to 22.5% to 23.5%.
  - Consolidated Electrominerals: Maintained at 9% to 9.5%.
- **Capex:** Maintained consolidated capex guidance of Rs 400 crore.
- **Rhodius Outlook:** Sales expected to be flat compared to last year, with losses lower than last year; aiming for a break-even by Q1 of next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Abrasives growth driven by volumes, industrial activities, construction, infrastructure-led demand, new geographies, and new products.
- Ceramics growth driven by industrial ceramics (metallized cylinders, engineered ceramics, Bloom Energy-related products, SOFCs) and refractories across India, the Americas, and Australia.
- Electrominerals demand supported by strong refractory and abrasive customers domestically and abroad.
- Chinese import pressure in Electrominerals continues, though offset partially by a strong dollar and weak rupee making imports expensive; export duty withdrawal on Chinese competitors is viewed as a welcome step.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Abrasives manufacturing costs rose due to higher fuel prices and oil-derived input costs (phenol, resins). Management noted a timing lag in passing on these cost hikes, resulting in margin contraction. Management views Abrasives margins as having bottomed out and targets a normal trajectory of 15%.
- Electrominerals standalone margin drop on a QoQ basis attributed to product and industry customer mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex was Rs 127 crore.
- Semiconductor equipment components investment progressing toward peak revenue potential of Rs 300-350 crore by FY30/31.
- Thin wheel facility established in Hosur (replacing Uttarakhand operations) with qualification underway with 6-7 international customers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt stood at Rs 111 crore (Debt of Rs 362 crore less cash of Rs 251 crore).
- Operating cash flow absorbed funds due to a surge in receivables (from September sales collected in Oct/Nov) and higher inventory build-up for H2 growth; management expects a reversal in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued operation under constraints at VAW Russia.
- Inability to immediately pass on sharp input cost increases (phenol, resins) in Abrasives due to market competition.
- Ongoing challenges from Chinese competition in Abrasives and Electrominerals.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harshit Patel (Equirus Securities):** Asked about peak revenue and capex for semiconductor equipment components. *Management stated peak happens around FY30/31 with top-line potential of Rs 300-350 crore.*
2. **Shabbir Taher (DAM Capital):** Inquired if Abrasives margins have bottomed out and the status of the Uttarakhand thin wheel relocation. *Management confirmed margins have bottomed out and are targeting a 15% normal trajectory; stated Hosur thin wheel facility is undergoing customer qualifications.*
3. **Sajal Kapoor (Antifragile Thinking):** Asked about potential further cash outflows or write-offs for the German exit (AWUKO) and working capital reversal. *Management stated no further losses are expected, asset sales are agreed with cash received, and working capital will reverse in H2.*
4. **Mohit Surana (Monarch Network Capital):** Asked about Rhodius sustainable margins and price/volume mix. *Management noted step one is breaking even by Q1 next year, and growth is predominantly volume-driven.*
5. **Akshay Thakur (Helios Capital):** Asked about the strategy behind AMB/DBC substrates and zirconia sourcing post-Foskor. *Management highlighted global diversification trends, value-added substrate focus, and confirmed Zirconia sourcing has independent alternative suppliers.*

### One-line takeaway
Carborundum Universal reported robust H1 top-line growth of ~15% consolidated (and ~25% standalone) driven by strong Ceramics and Electrominerals performance, even as it absorbed closure costs for AWUKO and near-term margin pressure in Abrasives.
