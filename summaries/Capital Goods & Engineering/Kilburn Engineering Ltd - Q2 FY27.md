# Kilburn Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Cautious - Management lowered FY27 revenue expectations due to standalone project deferments and export delays, while reiterating medium-term CAGR guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Cut. Full-year FY27 revenue guidance lowered from ~Rs 700 crore to Rs 600–650 crore; 20–25% CAGR guidance through FY28 maintained over FY26 base (Rs 620 crore).
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone shifted to more cautious regarding near-term standalone execution due to deferred customer decisions in export/capital goods markets, while subsidiary demand remains strong.
- **New topics or risks:** Added a new board-approved capex of Rs 25 crore for data center enclosures at Monga Strayfield; disclosed promoter pledge of 5 lakh shares (<1% of promoter holding).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Expansion at Kilburn’s Saravali plant and ME Energy’s Pune plant delayed slightly from end-October 2026 to Q3/early Q4 FY27 (by December 2026).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 153.1 crore, down 0.33% YoY (vs Rs 153.6 crore) and up 30.9% QoQ.
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 270.1 crore, down 4.5% YoY.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 16.5 crore for Q2; Rs 29.6 crore for H1.
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 69.5 crore in Q2 (vs Rs 114.8 crore in Q2 FY26); PAT of Rs 5.7 crore (vs Rs 18 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Subsidiary Performance:** H1 revenue doubled to ~Rs 141.7 crore (vs Rs 73.4 crore in H1 FY26), contributing over 50% of group revenue and 21% profit contribution growth.
- **Order Book:** Consolidated order book stood at Rs 471 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 485 crore at start of quarter).
- **New Order Intake:** Rs 136 crore in Q2; over Rs 300 crore YTD.

### Highlights
- Execution slowdown in standalone entity was due to delayed customer decision-making and postponed dispatch schedules linked to global geopolitical uncertainty.
- Entry into the global fertilizer EPC vendor ecosystem generated multiple inquiries.
- ME Energy entered the ferro-alloy sector with an order in Bhutan for waste heat recovery systems and qualified to bid for a waste heat recovery boiler system for a cement plant.
- Monga Strayfield expanded its RF Dryers business into European and North American food processing markets.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 consolidated revenue of Rs 600–650 crore (transition/consolidation year).
- Reintegrated commitment to achieve 20% to 25% CAGR from FY26 base (Rs 620 crore) by FY28, targeting Rs 900–1,000 crore revenue.
- Expects order finalizations worth Rs 200–250 crore in the next 45–60 days (including Middle East, Far East, and domestic nuclear orders).
- Targeted full-year FY27 order intake remains close to Rs 800 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Order wins led by fertilizers, carbon black, and AI data centers.
- Long-standing customer Vertiv indicated a requirement of Rs 90 crore in FY27 for power/data center enclosures.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Target long-term average blended EBITDA margin maintained at ~20%.
- Raw material price risk is mitigated by booking key raw materials within days of order receipt; contracts generally do not contain price escalation clauses.
- Profitability in Q2 was impacted by higher employee costs and raw material consumption percentage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Kilburn Standalone:** Rs 25 crore brownfield expansion at Saravali plant on track for completion by December 2026; supports standalone revenue potential of Rs 750–800 crore.
- **ME Energy:** Phase 2 Pune plant expansion (civil work mostly complete) will take revenue capacity beyond Rs 350–400 crore.
- **Monga Strayfield:** Board approved Rs 25 crore capex over 6–9 months primarily at Pune facility for data center enclosures, battery storage, and transformer cabinets. Expected segment revenue of Rs 50–60 crore in FY27, ramping to ~Rs 100 crore in FY28 (expanded capacity potential of Rs 120–130 crore).
- Total combined subsidiary capacity expected to exceed Rs 500 crore in revenue potential for FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flow from operations will remain negative at year-end due to sales concentration in H2 driving higher debtors and unbilled revenue.
- Receivables extending beyond 180 days exist due to milestone billing linked to customer project commissioning.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Customer delays in dispatch finalizations and project completions causing deferred revenue recognition.
- Persistent negative cash flows from operations due to unbilled revenue and extended debtor collections.
- Pledge created on 5 lakh promoter shares (<1% of promoter holding) for group entity requirements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tejas Thakkar (T&J Investment):** Asked about full-year revenue outlook given soft H1.  
  *Management:* Stated FY27 revenue would hover between Rs 600–650 crore due to timing of order conversions, but subsidiary growth will help maintain overall revenue close to last year's level.
- **Harshit Sachdeva (Columbus Capital):** Inquired about the timeline and margin profile for the data center enclosure vertical.  
  *Management:* Capex will be completed in 6–9 months, enabling precision engineering revenues to scale past Rs 100 crore in FY28, while keeping blended group EBITDA margins around 20%.
- **Sagar Shah (Spark PWM):** Asked why EBITDA margin moderated despite sequential revenue growth.  
  *Management:* Attributed the moderation to product mix changes, higher employee expenses, and elevated raw material value as a percentage of total contract value.
- **Dhirendra Kumar Patro (Spark PMS):** Asked about price escalation clauses in the order book.  
  *Management:* Clarified that contracts do not contain price escalation clauses; margins are locked by procuring raw materials immediately after order receipt.

### One-line takeaway
Kilburn faced standalone revenue drag from delayed customer approvals, but strong growth in subsidiaries and a new Rs 25 crore data center capex support its long-term target of 20–25% CAGR through FY28.
