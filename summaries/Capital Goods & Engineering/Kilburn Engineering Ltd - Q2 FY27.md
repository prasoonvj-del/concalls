# Kilburn Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong subsidiary performance and new data center vertical growth are offsetting standalone revenue weakness caused by geopolitical project delays.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Cut. Revenue guidance for FY27 lowered to Rs 600-650 crore from the previous ~Rs 700 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong inquiry pipeline" focus to describing the current year as a "transition year" and "year of consolidation."
- **New topics:** Specific focus on the data center enclosure business (Vertiv relationship) and the formation of a new JV in the Middle East.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Capex for Kilburn Saravali and ME Energy Pune Phase 2 remains on track for completion by Q3/early Q4.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2):** Rs 153.1 crore (vs Rs 153.6 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1):** Rs 270.1 crore (vs Rs 282.8 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2):** Rs 16.5 crore.
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2):** Rs 69.5 crore (vs Rs 114.8 crore YoY).
- **Order Inflow (Q2):** Rs 136 crore.
- **Order Book:** Rs 471 crore.

### Highlights
- Subsidiaries (ME Energy and Monga Strayfield) now contribute >50% of group revenue; revenue from subsidiaries doubled in H1 to Rs 141.7 crore.
- Standalone revenue decline attributed to slower customer decision-making and geopolitical uncertainty, not structural issues.
- Board approved Rs 25 crore capex for Monga Strayfield to expand capacity for data center enclosures.
- Group revenue grew 30.9% QoQ, indicating execution is picking up.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue:** Expected to be Rs 600-650 crore.
- **Long-term:** Management maintains 20-25% CAGR growth target (FY26-FY28).
- **Capacity:** Group infrastructure being built to support >Rs 1,000 crore annual revenue capacity.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Key growth sectors: Fertilizers, carbon black, AI data centers, and nuclear power.
- New entry: Ferrous alloy space (waste heat recovery); first order from Bhutan.
- Data center: Enclosure business expected to reach Rs 100 crore annual revenue in FY28; current demand exceeds supply.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **EBITDA Margin:** Management maintains 20% long-term average target.
- **Contracts:** No price escalation clauses; raw materials are booked immediately upon order receipt to mitigate price risk.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Kilburn Saravali & ME Energy (Pune Phase 2):** Expansion underway; completion expected by Q3/early Q4.
- **Monga Strayfield:** Rs 25 crore capex for data center enclosures (6-9 month implementation).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Promoter Pledge:** <1% of promoter holding (for corporate requirements).
- **Receivables:** Some outstanding >180 days, linked to project commissioning schedules.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainty causing project deferments and delays in finalization/dispatch.
- Customer-side delays in approving drawings and quality inspections.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tejas Thakkar (T&J Investment):** Asked about data center competition/defense exposure. Management: Limited to power/data centers; no defense/aerospace exposure.
- **Tejas Thakkar:** Asked about promoter pledge. Management: <1% of holding, used for corporate requirements.
- **Sameer Chheda (Prince Polyplast):** Asked about order book targets. Management: Confirmed Rs 800 crore intake target for FY27.
- **Sagar Shah (Spark PWM):** Asked about margin dip. Management: Attributed to product mix and higher employee/raw material costs as a percentage of order value.

### One-line takeaway
A transition year where subsidiary growth and new data center verticals are buffering standalone project delays, with management maintaining long-term growth targets.
