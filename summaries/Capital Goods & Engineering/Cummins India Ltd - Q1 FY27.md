# Cummins India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong domestic top-line growth driven by power generation and data centers, offset by severe commodity inflation and margin pressure.

### Key numbers
- **Sales:** Rs 3,375 crore (+18% YoY, +14% QoQ)
- **Domestic Sales:** Rs 2,854 crore (+22% YoY, +14% QoQ)
- **Exports:** Rs 521 crore (Flat YoY, +16% QoQ)
- **Profit Before Tax (before exceptional items):** Rs 721 crore (-0.7% YoY, -12% QoQ)
- **Power Generation (Domestic):** Rs 1,424 crore (+35% YoY, +10% QoQ)
- **Distribution Business:** Rs 886 crore (+14% YoY, +16% QoQ)
- **Industrial Business (Domestic):** Rs 458 crore (+10% YoY, +20% QoQ)
- **High-Horsepower Exports:** Rs 296 crore (+16% YoY, +37% QoQ)
- **Low-Horsepower Exports:** Rs 180 crore (-20% YoY, +2% QoQ)

### Highlights
- Data center segment contributed 40% of overall domestic power generation revenue in Q1 FY27 (up from 23% in Q1 FY26).
- High demand in high-horsepower (HHP) engines is outpacing supply across the industry.
- Price increases were taken at the beginning of Q2 FY27 to counter raw material inflation; Q1 FY27 included no price hikes.
- Distribution business reach stands at 3,500 trained engineers and 450 touchpoints with 2-hour (critical) and 4-hour (non-critical) service response guarantees.
- Royalty and support services rates were standardized to 4% (previously ranged between 1% to 8%).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects strong domestic demand across key end markets to sustain, led by power generation, data centers, railways, and mining.
- Commodity cost inflation, freight costs, and supply chain bottlenecks are expected to persist in the near term.
- Management remains focused on operational efficiency, cost management, and supply chain resilience to protect and improve margins.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Power Generation:** High demand across manufacturing, data centers, residential, and commercial realty. Data center customers are asking to prepone deliveries due to tight lead times.
- **Industrial Segment:** Railways continues strong order execution (power cars, diesel-electric tower cars, hotel load converters). Mining tenders (coal) are picking up. Construction was flattish YoY due to a lack of new national highway tenders.
- **Exports:** Mixed performance; Middle East exports were impacted by regional geopolitical conflicts, while Europe and Asia-Pacific markets performed decently.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Unprecedented commodity inflation in steel, pig iron, aluminum, and copper, alongside higher freight and labor shortages, pressured Q1 FY27 margins.
- Pig iron prices increased 14% to 20% YoY.
- Employee costs rose due to annual merit increments effective April 1, 2026, true-up of variable compensation, and an actuarial credit baseline in Q4 FY26.
- Full commodity cost recovery via pricing hikes is not immediate due to backlog order realizations taking about a quarter to reflect.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capacity utilization rate is currently between 70% to 75%.
- Capital investment continues in line with historical spending levels in existing facilities.
- Domestic power generation demand is currently driven by the QSK60 node; local capacity expansion for 78-liter and 95-liter engines remains under evaluation as market demand evolves.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Related party transaction (RPT) approval limits for export to the UK subsidiary were raised for FY27 as a governance measure to retain flexibility for export opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Persistent inflation in major input commodities (steel, pig iron, copper, aluminum) and elevated freight costs.
- Ongoing geopolitical instability in West Asia/Middle East affecting export deliveries.
- Extended lead times and supply constraints for high-horsepower engines relative to high market demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Are capacity constraints causing lost market share or orders in HHP nodes like QSK60/95?**  
  *A:* Management answered that demand is outpacing supply across the entire industry. Losing orders is a function of customer relationships and preferences, where Cummins remains strongly positioned via its distribution and reliability.
- **Q: Will gross margins return to historic 36%-37% levels post Q2 FY27 price hikes?**  
  *A:* Management noted commodity inflation and freight hikes are unprecedented. While cost controls and price adjustments will improve margins, management declined to confirm a return to historic peak margins.
- **Q: What drove the sharp jump in employee costs despite flat headcount?**  
  *A:* Management explained it was due to annual merit increments (effective April 1, 2026), true-up of variable compensation, and a lower base in Q4 FY26 due to an actuarial credit.
- **Q: Did data center customers ask to delay deliveries due to slower end-user execution?**  
  *A:* Management stated they have seen the opposite—data center clients are requesting preponed deliveries.
- **Q: What is the distribution business breakdown for data centers?**  
  *A:* Comprehensive service contract revenues for data centers materialize after the standard 2-year warranty period expires.

### One-line takeaway
Cummins India delivered record top-line growth driven by 40% data center contribution in power gen, but margin recovery remains subject to passing on severe commodity inflation over coming quarters.
