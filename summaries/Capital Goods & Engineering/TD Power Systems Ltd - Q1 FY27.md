# TD Power Systems Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Buoyant market conditions, strong order inflows, and robust financial growth across standalone and consolidated operations.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Total Income:** INR6.3 billion vs INR3.63 billion (up 74% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 19.34% (including other income, excluding exceptional and treasury income) vs 18.7% YoY
- **Standalone PAT:** INR853 million vs INR471 million (up 81% YoY)
- **Consolidated Sales:** INR6.43 billion vs INR3.76 billion (up 71% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR860 million vs INR500 million (up 72% YoY)
- **Manufacturing Order Book:** INR22.08 billion (Generator and motor: INR19.29 billion, Railway: INR2.11 billion, Spares and aftermarket: INR0.22 billion, Turkey business: INR0.46 billion)
- **Order Inflow (Q1):** INR7.34 billion (up 87% QoQ); Direct and deemed exports: INR6.84 billion (93% of inflows)
- **Cash Position:** INR2.4 billion
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Revised to INR2,600 crores
- **Capex Guidance:** INR50 crores for FY27
- **Turkey Business Execution:** EUR3 million to EUR3.5 million for the year

### Highlights
- Strong growth driven by AI data centres, grid stabilization, basic power generation, and renewables (geothermal, hydro, waste-to-energy).
- Export and deemed exports (excluding railway orders) stood at 57% of the order book.
- Railway segment: Management is not taking fresh orders, will review sustainability at year-end, and will repurpose manufacturing capacity for generators and motors.
- Turkey facility to execute EUR3 million to EUR3.5 million in orders this year and act as a service backup/insurance for the European market.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised FY27 guidance to INR2,600 crores, with a potential to cross this number.
- Expected quarterly order inflow of around INR700 crores (~INR2,800+ crores for the full year).
- Aiming for capacity of around INR32 billion for FY28 with INR50 crores capex.
- Looking to expand capacity to INR40 billion+ for FY29 and FY30 (details to be announced in the next earnings call).
- Management is close to signing agreements in the large generator segment (above 100 megawatt), with announcements expected in August.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Tremendous shortage of power generation equipment globally, specifically in gas turbines, gas engines, transformers, and generators.
- Steam turbine market growing at 10% to 12% in captive power, biomass, and waste-to-heat recovery.
- Gas engines and gas turbines seeing massive, unpaused growth with large volume orders.
- Hydro segment experiencing one of its highest activity years, including refurbishment business in India and abroad.
- Domestic power generation demand remains subdued (~10%-12% growth), with no explosive demand or hyperscaler data centre activity due to lack of baseload gas and water.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Price variation clauses have been implemented in customer contracts.
- Gross contribution margins maintained within a tolerable range via pricing, cost reductions, product mix, and exchange rate gains.
- Employee expenses increased due to scaling up employee count from 1,750 to 2,600.
- Service business remains around 5% to 6% of overall sales; electric generators typically require negligible service in the first 10 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ongoing debottlenecking capex of INR50 crores in FY27 to support FY28 capacity of around INR32 billion.
- Planning further capacity enhancements for FY29 and FY30 to take capacity beyond INR40 billion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash position maintained at INR2.4 billion.
- Working capital requirements and trade receivables are growing in line with business expansion; customer advances contributing to other current liabilities.
- Management stated payment terms with customers cannot be altered significantly to avoid losing business.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential execution delays on-ground for projects, though current customer shipments remain unaffected as products go to multiple distributed sites.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Surana (Monarch Networth Capital) asked about peak revenue potential without growth capex and large generator TAM.** Management stated they are targeting ~INR32 billion capacity for FY28 with debottlenecking and will share details on large generators (above 100 MW) in August.
2. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) asked about full-year order inflow and capex plans.** Management guided to ~INR700 crores per quarter (~INR2,800 crores annually) and INR50 crores capex for FY27.
3. **Alisha Mahawla (TRUST Mutual Fund) asked if supply chain issues or shipping delays are causing execution slowdowns.** Management clarified that products are shipped directly to multiple global customers/sites, and they see no disruption in order inflow or customer acceptance.
4. **Kunal (360 ONE Capital) asked if data centre shifts to combined cycles improve TD Power's TAM.** Management confirmed it will improve opportunity size over time as projects transition from open cycle.
5. **Kushal Goenka (Mangal Keshav Financial Services) asked about efforts to improve working capital/receivables and fundraise via debt.** Management noted payment terms cannot be altered significantly without risking business, and declined to discuss fundraise details prior to the upcoming Board meeting.

### One-line takeaway
TD Power Systems delivered a stellar quarter with 71%+ revenue growth, revised its FY27 guidance to INR2,600 crores, and is scaling capacity toward INR32 billion for FY28 amid buoyant global demand for power generation equipment.
