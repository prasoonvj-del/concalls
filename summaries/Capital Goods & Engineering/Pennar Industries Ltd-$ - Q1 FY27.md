# Pennar Industries Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating discipline, improved product mix, and strong order backlogs drove profitability despite moderate revenue growth, with management expecting strong double-digit growth in Q2.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR884.55 crores (up 3.58% YoY) / INR870.4 crores (up 2.9% YoY as stated by CFO)
- **Profit before tax (PBT):** INR46.8 crores (up 16.04% YoY)
- **PBT margin:** 5.38% (up from 4.77% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR106.8 crores (up 13.3% YoY from INR94.3 crores)
- **Customized Design Building Solutions (PEB) revenue:** Increased from INR411 crores to INR507 crores
- **ROCE:** Around 20%
- **ROE:** Around 12%
- **Interest cost:** 4.18% of revenue
- **Employee expenses:** INR107.32 crores (up 16% YoY), driven by U.S. operations (up 37% or INR16.24 crores)
- **Gross margins:** Expanded from 42.56% to 43.85% at a consolidated level
- **Contribution margins:** Increased from 27.14% to 28.51% (from INR231.81 crores to INR252.12 crores)
- **Order books:** PEB India at INR1,008 crores; PEB U.S. (Ascent) crossed USD100 million; Boiler division at over INR150.75 crores; Hydraulics order backlog at INR30 crores

### Highlights
- Profitability growth significantly exceeded revenue growth due to strong operating leverage and efficiency benefits.
- PEB U.S. revenue ran ahead of plan, and order backlog crossed USD100 million.
- Structured Engineering (Engineering Services) grew 26.3% YoY; annual revenue run rate is about INR70 crores.
- Achieved first industrial power boiler order and first orders in the pharmaceutical sector.
- Commissioned the Hyundai plant for the Body-in-Shop (BIW) business.
- Promoters committed INR50 crores via warrants (INR20 crores already brought in).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects strong double-digit revenue and profitability growth starting from Q2 FY27 across PEB India, PEB U.S., and overall company level.
- Stated long-term guidance for finance cost is around 4% of revenue.
- Target debt-to-equity ratio around 0.7 by the end of the year.
- Stated ROCE target is 25% (range of 25% to 30%).
- Stated long-term PBT margin target of 7% over the next 2-3 years.
- Stated annual PAT growth target of at least 20%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PEB India and PEB U.S. demand drivers remain strong with record-high order backlogs and available drawing clearances.
- Hydraulics demand in the U.S. slowed down due to tariff uncertainties, putting the business in a wait-and-watch mode.
- Tech Pennar faced a weak spot with a thin order backlog and customer execution flips; a new sales team is in place.
- Legacy businesses (steel special grades, tubing, railways) contributing about 25% of revenue are in steady state with no new capital deployed.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel price increase pass-through experienced a 1-2 month lag, causing a momentary blip in PEB operating margins, which management expects to recover in Q2.
- Employee expenses rose due to U.S. capability investments and scaling manpower for order backlogs.
- Deliberately stocking slightly more raw material in anticipation of revenue growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capacity utilization and asset capacities are ready to take on additional revenue from order backlogs.
- Solar capabilities previously housed under Pennar are part of a 2 gigawatt capacity JV with Zetwerk (INR4,000 crores top-line potential; Pennar holds 45%).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital allocation is elevated due to investments supporting revenue growth and higher order books; management priority for Q2 is to accelerate collections and optimize current assets.
- Term loan costs attributable to the Telco acquisition.
- Advance percentage in PEB India reduced to 25% (from 5% to 10% historically).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Operational challenges in PEB India execution impacted project delivery in Q1.
- Tariff uncertainty in the U.S. impacting Hydraulics order bookings.
- Tech Pennar experienced execution flips at some customers and a thin order book.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nitin Jain (Fairvalue Equity Advisors) on PEB EBIT margin drop:** Management explained it was a temporary 1-2 month lag in passing through steel price hikes and expects margins to recover from Q2.
2. **Bhashit Parikh (Retail Investor) on moving goalposts for PAT margins:** Management defended the long-term margin trajectory, attributing past variances to temporary commodity pricing effects and emphasizing continuous ROCE and operating margin improvements.
3. **Nileshlal Narendra Shah (Investments) on high employee costs and holding company discount:** Management stated salary increases support scaling U.S. operations and engineering teams to service record order books, and defended the warrant issuance as a measure of promoter confidence.
4. **Venkatasubramanian R. on AI disruption in Engineering Services:** Management stated that automation only yields 30-35% productivity gains, and the robust 26% growth and 3-shift operations indicate no threat to the business model.
5. **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital) on legacy vs. core growth:** Management reiterated that legacy segments (steel, railways, aerospace machining) are in steady-state with no new capex, while prioritized growth vectors (PEB India/U.S., Boilers, BIW, Engineering Services) will drive growth.

### One-line takeaway
Pennar Industries reported moderate Q1 revenue growth of 3.58% YoY but strong 16.04% PBT growth, backed by record-high order books and management confidence in double-digit sequential growth from Q2.
