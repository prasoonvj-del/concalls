# Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by record-high performance, strong demand, and strategic expansion into Copper Clad Laminates (CCL).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 387 crore, growing 35.46% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 42.13 crore, up 38.85% YoY.
- **PAT:** Rs 24.07 crore, up 55.97% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10.82%.
- **PAT Margin:** 6.22%.
- **Return on Equity (RoE):** 9.66%.
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** 13.31%.
- **Base Business Revenue (FY26):** Rs 1,080 crore (FY27 projected target at Rs 1,315 crore).

### Highlights
- Recorded historically highest performance for Q2 and H1 FY27.
- Net worth increased by 156%.
- CCL project civil work and construction are ~75% complete; machinery expected by mid-November 2026, installation in 45 days, and trial runs targeted between December 20th, 2026, and January 20th, 2027.
- Appointed a Chinese national CTO with 30 years of experience, alongside 22 engineers, for CCL machine installation and quality control.
- Over 50% cash subsidy and interest subsidies/ECMS scheme support available for the CCL project.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated previous guidance was a Rs 750 crore top line from all five CCL lines (20% EBITDA, 13% PAT) and a consolidated Rs 2,500 crore top line by FY28 (14% EBITDA, 11% PAT).
- Management announced that due to >300% increases in CCL market prices, a revision of guidance will be announced very soon, which could drastically improve bottom-line margins.
- Base business top-line target is Rs 1,315 crore for FY27 and Rs 1,800 crore up to FY28.
- Target to reach 85% capacity utilisation on the first CCL line by April 2027. Five total lines to be installed by April 2028.
- Long-term vision to capture 7–8% market share in CCL over the next five years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industrial fasteners market expected to grow to USD 115.67 billion by 2032; stainless steel market to USD 353 billion by 2034; tubes and pipes market to USD 60.14 billion by 2034.
- Indian PCB market expected to reach Rs 1,76,790 crore by 2032; Indian CCL market to reach nearly Rs 30,000 crore by 2031.
- Massive shortage of CCL in India with zero current domestic manufacturers, leading to high pricing power and starved demand from PCB manufacturers.
- Company visited 57 key PCB customers; order books and long-term contracts in discussion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices for CCL have increased by 40% to 50%, while finished CCL prices have surged by over 300%.
- Gross margin dropped marginally from Q1 (10.33%) to Q2 (9.44%) due to expenses booked for new products ahead of sales commencement.
- Effective tax rate expected to be around 15% or near last year's level.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total planned investment for CCL is Rs 338 crore over 2–3 years, plus Rs 86 crore in a captive solar plant.
- Phase 2 CapEx for fasteners, tubes, pipes, sheet metal, and clips is ongoing with ~Rs 58 crore being spent this year.
- Five CCL lines planned: Line 1 starts trial in Dec 2026; Line 2 and 3 orders to be placed Dec-Jan, starting June 2027 and Sept 2027; Lines 4 and 5 starting Jan 2028 and April 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term credit rating is A- (Stable).
- Successfully completed a rights issue, providing sufficient liquidity for working capital.
- Five lines funded through term loans and internal cash flows; no equity dilution or new equity fundraising planned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Initial teething challenges or synchronization delays of 1–2 weeks possible during CCL plant commissioning.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Srishti Jain asked about the margin expansion to 13% and volume numbers.** Management replied that PAT can move towards 13% due to high-margin CCL products, though a conservative guidance is being maintained until official revision.
- **Priyanshu Jain asked about CCL utilisation ramp-up and Chinese engineering support.** Management outlined a ramp-up target to 85% by April 2027 for the first line and confirmed 22 engineers plus a 30-year experienced CTO will arrive for setup.
- **Meet Mehta asked about Tg grades and anti-dumping duties.** Management stated they produce 140 Tg to 180 Tg for diverse sectors (auto, EVs, drones, aerospace) and noted anti-dumping duty does not apply since there are no other domestic producers.
- **Mayank Agarwal asked about competition and China restrictions.** Management stated there is zero competition currently due to 60-70% industry growth and no export restrictions from China under the ECMS/PLI scheme.
- **Abhay Singh asked about working capital and potential delays.** Management confirmed rights issue proceeds cover working capital, and the project remains strictly on schedule with no delays identified.
- **Vineet Khatri asked about pricing power and export/domestic customer strategy.** Management stated they can price at par or slightly below Chinese imports while offering advantages on logistics and wait times, enabling a single line to potentially generate Rs 500 crore in revenue.

### One-line takeaway
Ratnaveer reported record-high Q2 FY27 performance (revenue up 35.5% YoY, PAT up 56% YoY) and is on track to commission India's first Copper Clad Laminate (CCL) plant by December 2026, with management indicating potential upward guidance revisions due to surging industry pricing.
