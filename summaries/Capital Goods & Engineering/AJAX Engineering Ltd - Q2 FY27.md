# AJAX Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-14 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While revenue and market share expanded and non-SLCM grew strongly, industry headwinds in certain states, delayed contractor payments, and rising commodity/steel costs created margin pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Total Revenue:** Rs 487 crores (up 9.5% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 SLCM Revenue:** Rs 395.8 crores (up 5.9% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Non-SLCM Revenue:** Rs 55.8 crores (up 64.7% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Spares and Services Revenue:** Rs 35.4 crores (down 4.7% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 51.5 crores (up 13.7% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 50.9 crores (up 30.4% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Total Revenue:** Rs 961.5 crores (up 5.5% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 SLCM Revenue:** Rs 783.5 crores (up 3.3% YoY, supported by 2.5% volume growth)
- **H1 FY27 Non-SLCM Revenue:** Rs 103.5 crores (up 31.5% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Spares and Services Revenue:** Rs 74.6 crores (largely stable YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 110.6 crores (up 3.7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 11.5% (vs 11.7% in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 106.5 crores (up 16% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Export Revenue Growth:** 47% YoY
- **H1 FY27 SLCM Retail Market Share:** 75.5% (vs 70.7% in H1 FY26 and 73.5% in FY26 average)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Nearly Rs 1,151 crores (as of September 26)
- **Working Capital Cycle:** Maintained at 22 days
- **H1 FY27 ROCE / ROE:** 19% / 14% respectively
- **Price Increases:** ~2% in Q4 FY26, ~1% in September, ~1% in November, with another ~2% planned for Q4 FY27.

### Highlights
- Management noted that 60%-65% of annual revenue is typically generated in H2.
- SLCM application mix shifted: roads dropped to ~40% YTD in FY27 (from 70% in FY25), while buildings, urban infra, and factories rose to ~39% YTD (from 13% in FY25).
- Export revenue share in Q2 FY27 stood at 7.4% of total revenue.
- Organizational restructuring placed all manufacturing under a single leader, moved the Obadenahalli plant head to procurement/supply chain, and aligned services/spares with strategy.
- *Note vs record:* The transcript does not explicitly mention the new manufacturing facility expected to become operational in H2 FY27, though it discusses new product rollouts like ARGO 4000.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects customer sentiment and demand momentum to improve in H2.
- Management targets ending the year with close to double-digit growth.
- Management plans another 2% price increase in Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Near-term headwinds and project execution delays persist due to below-budgeted government spending (especially by state governments) and delayed contractor payments in Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and Bihar.
- Good volume growth was seen in Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh.
- UDAAN series expanded to 20 states (6 added in H1); H1 volumes reached 216 units (vs 49 units in H1 FY26 base).
- ARGO 4000 has been launched to address gaps in the SLCM portfolio.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs increased by close to mid-single digits during H1 FY27 due to rising steel prices (moving from Rs 58/kg to ~Rs 68/kg, with expectations of reaching Rs 75-76/kg) and crude-linked commodities.
- Q2 gross margins improved marginally by ~20 bps YoY supported by price hikes.
- Q2 other expenses included a one-time SAP implementation cost of Rs 16 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- The company is evaluating inorganic opportunities utilizing its strong cash position of nearly Rs 1,150 crores.
- Export expansion targets South Africa as a beachhead and non-Brazil LATAM markets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash position of nearly Rs 1,151 crores as of September 26.
- Healthy working capital cycle maintained at 22 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term infrastructure slowdown, delayed state government approvals, and strained contractor cash flows in key states like Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh.
- Volatility in commodity cycles (steel, aluminum), forex fluctuations, and geopolitical risks impacting margins.
- Drop in machine running hours by ~25% YoY, leading to softness in spares and services revenue.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vaibhav Shah (JM Financial) asked about demand green shoots and margin expectations for H2.** Management stated growth is visible in specific states (Gujarat, Karnataka, UP) while Maharashtra and MP remain challenged; margin trajectory depends on commodity prices and geopolitical factors.
- **Raghunandhan NL (Nuvama) asked about the private sector mix shift and non-SLCM drivers.** Management noted private sector share (buildings, urban infra, factories) grew to ~39% YTD FY27, and non-SLCM growth was led by simplified products like concrete pumps sold via dealers (62 out of 68 dealers selling pumps).
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) asked about the weakness in spares and services.** Management explained that machine running hours dropped by 25% YoY, directly impacting spare parts consumption, but supply chain and dealer accountability are being strengthened.
- **Lakshminarayanan K G (ithought PMS) asked about transitioning to turnkey concreting solutions.** Management responded that while the company focuses on product quality, mix, and select private applications, deep consulting is often handled internally by large contractors, requiring careful selection of where to play.
- **Sankaranarayanan S (ithought PMS) asked about UDAAN scalability in urban vs rural markets.** Management stated UDAAN is primarily deployed in rural roads, narrow village lanes, and small precast/brick applications, while higher-end urban projects utilize standard SLCMs and batching plants.

### One-line takeaway
AJAX Engineering delivered a moderate performance in H1 FY27 with 5.5% revenue growth and steady market share gains, offsetting state-level infrastructure slowdowns and raw material inflation through price hikes and non-SLCM expansion.
