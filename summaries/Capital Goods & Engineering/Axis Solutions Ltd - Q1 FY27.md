# Axis Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and EBITDA growth, supported by robust product demand and registrations.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 48.98 crore (up 78%+ YoY from Rs 27.48 crore)
- Total Income: Rs 49.06 crore (vs Rs 27.56 crore YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 6.21 crore / Rs 6.22 crore (up 122% YoY from Rs 2.79 crore)
- EBITDA Margin: 12.70% (up 252 bps YoY from 10.18%)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): Rs 3.46 crore (vs Rs 1.75 crore YoY)
- Tax Provision: Rs 37 lakh (vs nil YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 3.09 crore (up 77% YoY from Rs 1.74 crore)
- PAT Margin: 6.32% (down 4 bps YoY from 6.36%)
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Rs 0.66 (vs Rs 0.37 YoY)
- Open Order Book: Rs 365 crore

### Highlights
- Incorporated AxisSol Arabia in Saudi Arabia for local market capture and registrations; set up subsidiaries in Germany, Singapore, Dubai, UK, and India (Axiot 60% stake).
- Launched MAG200 electromagnetic flow meter and an in-house hydrogen solid storage technology (demonstrated end-to-end at Automation Expo Asia 2026).
- Implemented an in-house CRM platform for sales tracking and reporting.
- MD Dr. Bijal Sanghvi received the CEO Excellence Award and Founders Award.
- Segregated offerings into three brands: Axociate (channel partners), Axcellence (solutions/global brands), and Axcelerator (IP-led/new developments).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Ninad Vora) stated growth and profits will be "very, very good as compared to last year's growth," but declined to provide specific numerical guidance due to compliance.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong steady demand from heavy process industries (oil & gas, petrochemicals, power), chemical sector, and government initiatives like Digital India, National Green Hydrogen Mission, and Indian Railways' Kavach safety programme.
- International presence expanding in Southeast Asia, Middle East, and Europe, aided by ATEX and IECEx certifications and import substitution.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- R&D investment policy targets ~10% of annual profit.
- MRO/warranty/recurring revenue currently constitutes ~5% to 6% of total revenue and is expected to grow over the next 1–2 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operating across five purpose-built facilities in Ahmedabad totaling ~1,40,000 square feet.
- Current capacity can support up to double the current turnover without requiring new facilities in the near term.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Public shareholding is 12.62%, promoters hold 85.64%, and DIIs hold 1.74%; management noted plans to comply with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) requirements in due course.
- Receivables ageing at year/quarter-end reflects heavy closing in February and March; normal payment terms are 30 to 60 days or via letters of credit.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Safety hazards associated with hydrogen storage and hazardous areas managed via specialized engineering (HydroSafe).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dipen Parikh on order book execution cycle:** Management stated that while some orders extend into next year, majorly the open orders of Rs 365 crore will be closed during the current year.
2. **Pratik Bagadia on hydrogen and Kavach revenue timeline:** Management (Ninad Vora & Dr. Bijal Sanghvi) declined to give specific revenue timelines, stating that commercialisation activities and POCs are ongoing and updates will be shared via BSE.
3. **Pratik Bagadia on low profit margins despite 13 patents:** Management clarified that hydrogen products have not yet been commercialized or sold, so no hydrogen margins are reflected in current financials.
4. **Dhiraj Khadilkar on company scope (manufacturing vs software):** Management (Sanjeev Chakraborty) explained that Axis Solutions is a design, engineering, hardware manufacturing, and system integration company combining electronic/electrical products with software solutions.
5. **Pratik Bagadia on factory capacity utilisation:** Management (Ninad Vora) stated they do not work on batch processing, but capacity can handle double the current turnover before needing new facilities.

### One-line takeaway
Axis Solutions delivered strong Q1 FY27 performance with 78% YoY revenue growth and a 252 bps expansion in EBITDA margins, driven by robust industrial demand and core product execution.
