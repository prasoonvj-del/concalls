# KSB Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q3 CY '26 (9M ended September 2026)
**Tone:** Mixed - Robust order intake and strong non-nuclear demand were offset by margin compression from fixed-price contracts, delayed nuclear testing, and softer valve segment margins.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** EBITDA margin target maintained at 13% to 14% (management explicitly noted they are not targeting 15% to 16% due to strategic market entries).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted toward stronger non-nuclear order drivers (Dangote, ONGC, thermal power) compensating for slower nuclear progress.
- **New topics or risks:** S/4HANA go-live scheduled for February; nuclear tender qualification criteria diluted for new players; control valve and cable failures delaying nuclear testing.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Shirwal plant expansion completed previously, but nuclear pump testing completion slipped from this fiscal year to early next year.

### Key numbers
- **9-Month Revenue:** Rs 2,064.7 crore (INR 20,647 million)
- **Orders on Hand (YTD September):** Rs 2,859.9 crore (INR 28,599 million)
- **Dangote Fertilizer Order:** Rs 118 crore (INR 1,180 million)
- **ONGC Offshore Pumps Order:** Rs 85 crore (INR 850 million)
- **Valves Segment EBIT Margin:** 2.9%
- **Renewable Electricity Share:** 59% of total power needs

### Highlights
- Secured major high-value orders including Dangote Fertilizers (Rs 118 crore) and ONGC offshore pumps (Rs 85 crore).
- Foundry gas supply and availability issues largely resolved, returning to 85% to 90% operational stability.
- Orders on hand remain robust, supported by strong non-nuclear demand across energy and industrial sectors.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to maintain a long-term EBITDA margin band of 13% to 14%.
- Export revenue targeted at 15% to 20% of total revenue.
- Water/wastewater and commercial building services segments targeted to grow at a minimum of 15% to 20% YoY.
- KUSUM 2.0 solar scheme expected to revive early next year following MNRE specification updates.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Thermal power demand remains strong, with supercritical projects and boiler feed/circulation pump orders (JSW Salboni, L&T projects) in progress.
- Data center cooling infrastructure demand is growing, with trial orders initiated with OEMs like Schneider.
- Export opportunities expanding, particularly in the U.S. market via EPC contractors like Kiewit.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed-price project contracts continue to pressure margins amid commodity inflation; standard business price pass-through takes 6 to 9 months.
- Valves segment margins impacted by an unfavorable product mix (more project orders than standard business) and commodity cost pressures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- SAP S/4HANA implementation on track for a February go-live.
- Shirwal facility operations supporting increased volume demands.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Solar and government receivables remain elevated (above Rs 100 crore); management is capping aggressive exposure to solar projects to manage working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Teething issues during nuclear pump testing (control valve failures and cable damage) pushing final testing completion to early next year.
- Intense pricing competition across both nuclear and standard industrial segments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Surana (Monarch Networth Capital) on nuclear pump testing delays:** Management explained that unexpected teething issues (control valve and cable failures) will push the completion of the 500-hour test into early next year.
2. **Dhawan (AlfAccurate Advisors) on fixed-price contract margin impact:** Management stated that low-margin fixed-price contracts and raw material lag effects are expected to persist up to the first quarter of next year.
3. **Raj Shah (Enam AMC) on peer comparison and margins:** Management clarified that lower margins stem from strategic entries into new market segments to gain market share, which will be offset by aftermarket and export growth.
4. **Analyst 3 on foundry issues and SAP rollout:** Management confirmed that foundry gas supply issues are 85% to 90% resolved and SAP S/4HANA is scheduled to go live in February.

### One-line takeaway
KSB Ltd continues to register robust order inflows from non-nuclear segments, though near-term profitability remains constrained by fixed-price contract lags and nuclear testing delays.
