# Kirloskar Oil Engines Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong top-line growth across all divisions and market share gains in key segments, despite temporary margin compression from input cost inflation.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,257 crore (+17% YoY, +14% QoQ).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,722 crore (+19% YoY, +18% QoQ).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 191 crore (-6% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 11% (vs 14% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 115 crore (-14% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 130 crore (-18% YoY).
*   **Arka Fincap AUM:** Rs 7,781 crore.
*   **Arka Fincap Net NPA:** 0.5%.
*   **Net Cash Position:** Rs 309 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Record Sales:** Achieved highest-ever standalone quarterly sales of Rs 1,722 crore.
*   **Segment Performance:** PowerGen revenue grew 17% YoY to Rs 792 crore; Distribution & Aftermarket (DABU) grew 29% YoY to Rs 293 crore; Fluid Dynamics grew 28% YoY to Rs 331 crore.
*   **International Expansion:** International business surged 59% YoY to Rs 413 crore (consolidated), now contributing ~20% of total sales. PowerGen is now the primary driver of international growth (45% of international mix).
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Formalized a flexible service agreement with DEUTZ for global distribution of the 1.6L engine platform.
*   **New Subsidiary:** Kirloskar Advanced Systems completed maiden commercial billing, marking entry into specialized defense and aerospace systems.
*   *Note:* The company is continuing its stated strategy of expanding international presence and high-horsepower (HHP) nodes, consistent with previous records.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth Ambition:** Management reiterated the goal of reaching $2 billion in revenue by FY30.
*   **International Target:** Aiming for international business to reach 30% of total revenue by FY30.
*   **Margin Recovery:** Management expects to see operating leverage gains starting from H2 or FY28 as incremental revenue improves absorption.
*   **Seasonality:** Q4 is typically the strongest quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **PowerGen:** Strong demand in High Horse Power (HHP) applications; market share in the 1010 kVA node is now >30%.
*   **Data Centers:** Optiprime range seeing strong traction; first systems dispatched to China.
*   **Real Estate/Infrastructure:** Seeing volume growth across LHP and MHP segments, though management declined to isolate specific real estate demand.
*   **Order Book:** Underlying demand is stronger than reported revenue; growth was achieved despite container shortages and shipping delays.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Headwinds:** Sharp rise in crude oil, copper, aluminum, and pig iron prices caused margin compression.
*   **Mitigation:** Deployed a three-tiered framework: targeted price increases, improved power density/engineering, and higher volume absorption.
*   **Pricing:** Price hikes are implemented based on specific business/customer needs; management noted that inflation is a recurring phenomenon and they will continue to endeavor to pass it on.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Guidance:** Maintained full-year capex guidance of Rs 2,100 crore.
*   **Timeline:** Work on new capacity has started; production expected to commence by FY28/29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Position:** Healthy net cash position of Rs 309 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** Cash conversion cycle stable at 31 days; net inventory days improved by 1 day to 63 days.
*   **Arka Fincap:** Tier 1 capital >21%; leverage <3.8x. Currently in an investment phase (distribution/tech) for retail expansion, with returns expected from next fiscal.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Margin Pressure:** Input cost inflation remains a challenge; management admitted they are not "done" with price hikes.
*   **Project Delays:** A specific data center order faced delays at the customer's end.
*   **Financial Services:** PBT for Arka dropped due to planned investments in retail distribution and technology.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you break down PowerGen growth into volume vs. price?** A: Management declined to provide this split.
*   **Q: Is the 2% growth in Industrial segment a sign of weakness?** A: Management clarified it was an execution challenge, not a demand issue.
*   **Q: Are the current margins the bottom?** A: Management stated that if commodity inflation plateaus, this could potentially be the bottom, with directional improvement expected.
*   **Q: Will Arka Fincap seek external capital?** A: Management is evaluating private and public capital-raising avenues but has no definitive update.
*   **Q: Are you seeing delivery timeline elongation?** A: No, customers want units as soon as possible.

### One-line takeaway
Kirloskar Oil Engines delivered record top-line growth driven by strong PowerGen and international demand, while management remains focused on navigating commodity headwinds through pricing and operational discipline.
