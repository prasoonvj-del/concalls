# Karbonsteel Engineering Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-16 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H2 and FY26
**Tone:** Cautious - Management highlighted operational headwinds including LPG supply disruptions, labor migration, and cost escalations, despite crossing Rs 300 crores in revenue.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Crossed Rs 300 crores (compared to Rs 273 crores in FY24).
- EBITDA Margin: 10.86%.
- PAT: Rs 10.51 crores (Normalized PAT estimated at Rs 16.56 crores after adding back one-time costs).
- Order Book: Approximately Rs 350 crores as of May.
- Production volume: 22% higher than the previous year.
- Production capacity expansion target: From 30,000 tons to 54,000 tons.

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 300 crores in revenue for the year.
- Consolidated operations by exiting the Khopoli facility (2.5 acres) and shifting resources to the Umargam facility (over 20 acres).
- Incurred one-time expenses including a Rs 1.65 crore bad debt write-off from an NCLT case, Rs 2.4 crores in rental costs due to capex delays, and Khopoli shifting charges.
- Reduced long-term borrowings by Rs 5 crores and short-term borrowings by Rs 10 crores.
- Inventory days improved from 150 days to 130 days.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects normalized EBITDA margins in the range of 12-13%.
- Management expects production of about 40,000 tons in FY26 at a sales price of about Rs 100 per kg.
- Management guides for PAT margins of 4-5% for FY26, moving above 5% for FY27, with a long-term aspiration of 7-8%.
- Management expects to reach the full 54,000-ton capacity in the year following FY26, and targets long-term revenue of Rs 800-1,000 crores post-FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Order book stands at approximately Rs 350 crores as of May.
- 90-95% of orders are repeat business due to precision custom heavy fabrication capabilities.
- Completed audits for two of India's largest business houses and a major power plant company; signed an MOU for 15,000 tons for data centers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin maintained at 10.86% despite cost pressures.
- Faced a force majeure from sole LPG supplier starting early March, impacting cutting and cooking operations.
- Steel prices rose nearly 25% (from Rs 50-55 per kg to Rs 65-70 per kg); steel price increases were passed through via price variation (PV) clauses.
- Conversion margin is around Rs 30 to Rs 45 per kg depending on job complexity.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Umargam facility capacity expansion from 30,000 tons to 54,000 tons is underway with a revised completion timeline of October 2025.
- Capex includes Rs 21 crores in work-in-progress and an expected investment of another Rs 10-15 crores in automation.
- Installing a 1-megawatt solar plant in two phases at a cost of Rs 3-3.5 crores to reduce power bills by over 50%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term borrowings reduced by Rs 5 crores; short-term borrowings reduced by Rs 10 crores.
- Inventory holding improved to 130 days (internal goal is 90-100 days).
- Gross block stands at Rs 61 crores including CWIP; net worth is Rs 115 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Operational challenges caused by a force majeure on LPG supply from the sole supplier starting in March.
- Labor migration and unrest affecting project execution timelines.
- NCLT ruling resulted in a Rs 1.65 crore bad debt write-off.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sudhir Bheda (Bheda Family Office)** asked about normalized margins; management stated normalized EBITDA margins are expected in the 12-13% range.
- **Marmik Khandelwal (Aarth Growth Fund)** asked about solar capex; management responded it will cost Rs 3-3.5 crores and reduce power bills by over 50%.
- **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical)** asked why realizations are lower than PEB players; management clarified that Karbonsteel is solely a manufacturer, whereas PEB players are EPC contractors.
- **Sandeep Bandari (Individual Investor)** asked if inventory can be reduced; management explained that inventory remains slightly higher due to custom fabrication requirements.
- **Gunit Singh (Counter Cyclical PMS)** asked about high finance costs; management stated finance costs are expected to normalize around 4% of turnover as high-interest unsecured loans are paid off.

### One-line takeaway
Karbonsteel crossed Rs 300 crores in revenue with a 10.86% EBITDA margin, consolidating operations at Umargam and expanding capacity to 54,000 tons by October 2025 despite LPG and labor headwinds.
