# IFGL Refractories Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line and domestic demand offset by margin compression due to rising raw material and fuel costs.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 297 crore (8% YoY)
- **Standalone Domestic Revenue:** Growth of 7% YoY
- **Standalone Export Revenue:** Growth of 9% YoY
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 31 crore (-17% YoY)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 16 crore (7% YoY)
- **Standalone Gross Margin:** 43% vs 47% in Q1 FY26
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 515 crore (13% YoY)
- **Consolidated Gross Margin:** 48% (stable)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 40 crore (2% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 17 crore (58% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT Margin:** 3%
- **R&D Spend (mentioned by management):** Around Rs 20 crore

### Highlights
- Consolidated revenue grew 13% YoY driven by India and overseas operations.
- India steel demand forecast to grow 7.4% in '26 and 9.2% in '27.
- British Steel moved into public ownership; former Liberty Steel Aldwarke (Specialty Steel) melt shops looking to restart in November-December 2026.
- Sheffield Refractories plastic ramming mass production commenced at IFGL Vizag facility.
- Monocon and Sheffield Refractories revenue grew in double digits YoY despite headwinds.
- U.S. business delivered double-digit YoY revenue growth.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of growing ahead of the underlying market over the medium term.
- Management targets double-digit revenue growth for the domestic business for the full year.
- Management aims to bring loss-making overseas subsidiaries back to black and achieve breakeven for Hofmann Ceramic by the end of FY27.
- Management targets a return to double-digit consolidated EBITDA margins depending on geopolitical conditions and overseas demand.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India remains the strongest major steel market; steel demand forecast to grow 7.4% in '26 and 9.2% in '27.
- U.S. steel production robust, growing 6% YoY.
- Europe steel demand bottoming out, with growth expected to revive in '26 and '27.
- British Steel production was minimal in Q1 due to blast furnace issues, expected to rectify in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA contraction (-17% YoY) driven by higher raw material costs (geopolitical uncertainties, supply chain disruptions) and elevated LPG/fuel costs.
- Price increases implemented across customers and categories, with a time lag in passing on cost increases.
- Standalone employee cost at ~10% of revenue (Rs 28.7 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Addition of mag carbon brick and casting flux lines expected to add Rs 150 crore to Rs 200 crore at peak capacity.
- Chinese JV land acquired in Gujarat; project currently on hold awaiting government approval for a location change.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Old dues from Liberty Steel are unsecured and expected to be dealt with by the administrator.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disruptions impacting raw material availability and pricing.
- Elevated ocean freight rates impacting operations in U.K. and China.
- Blast furnace issues at British Steel temporarily curtailing offtake.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Krishna (Prathamesh Invest) asked about overseas subsidiary margin outlook and Monocon performance:** Management stated momentum will be maintained and aggressive steps are taken to turn loss-making units around; British Steel public ownership brings better visibility.
2. **Amit Ahuja (CJ Capital) asked about reasons for EBITDA margin dip:** Management explained that surging raw material and fuel (LPG) costs outpaced the price hikes obtained in the near term.
3. **Sahil Sanghvi (Monarch Networth) asked about Europe business/Monocon losses and breakeven timeline:** Management clarified that Europe includes Sheffield Refractories (hit by low British Steel offtake) alongside Monocon, and expects normalization in Q2 with continued progress toward breakeven.
4. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company) asked about factors leading to double-digit EBITDA margin recovery:** Management cited turning Monocon U.K. into black and maintaining double-digit margins in the U.S.
5. **Saket Kapoor asked about the status of the Chinese JV:** Management stated the project is delayed as authorities advised a location change, requiring a fresh approval application.

### One-line takeaway
IFGL delivered 13% YoY consolidated revenue growth in Q1 FY27, but profitability was compressed by surging raw material and fuel costs, though management expects headwinds to ease as overseas operational bottlenecks resolve.
