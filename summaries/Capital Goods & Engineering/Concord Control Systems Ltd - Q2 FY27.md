# Concord Control Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported significant YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and profits, alongside improved working capital efficiency and a strong order book.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** ~INR 159 crore (~95% YoY growth)
- **EBITDA:** ~INR 46.7 crore (~110% YoY growth)
- **PAT:** ~INR 31.2 crore (~95% YoY growth)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 29.30%
- **Opening Order Book (FY27):** ~INR 697 crore
- **New Orders Received (H1):** ~INR 308 crore
- **Unexecuted Order Book (as on September 30, 2026):** ~INR 846 crore
- **Debtor Days:** Reduced from 210 to 178 days
- **Inventory Days:** Reduced from 313 to 259 days
- **Fusion Capacity:** >2 lakh square meters of installed capacities for flexible and rigid PCBs; expects ~INR 400 crore revenue at full scale.
- **Targeted Revenue CAGR:** 40% to 50% over the next 3 to 5 years.

### Highlights
- Revenue grew ~95% YoY to ~INR 159 crore; EBITDA grew ~110% YoY to ~INR 46.7 crore.
- Working capital metrics improved: debtor days down to 178 from 210, and inventory days down to 259 from 313.
- Unexecuted order book stands at ~INR 846 crore (nearly 4x FY26 revenue).
- Business is anchored on 4 technology platforms: Green mobility, Smart locomotive intelligence, Sensing & AI-driven diagnostics, and Railway safety (Kavach, MSDAC).
- Successfully deployed India's first battery-locomotive propulsion system; hydrogen propulsion system under development for early next year deployment.
- Acquired Fusion, enabling backward integration of PCBs and PCBA/EMS. Exhibited at InnoTrans Germany and planning participation at Electronica, Germany.
- Main board migration application will be filed soon as eligibility criteria are met.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a revenue CAGR of 40% to 50% over the next 3 to 5 years.
- Expects international markets to contribute one-fourth to one-half of revenue over the next 3 to 5 years.
- Fusion capacity expected to reach ~INR 400 crore in revenue at full scale.
- H2 traditionally performs better than H1 (typically a 60-40 mix).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways is strongly focused on safety and speed, driving demand for intelligent locomotives, Kavach, and connected control systems.
- Kavach opportunity is large, with only ~10% deployed currently; management views it as a multi-year opportunity spanning the next 10 to 15 years.
- Green mobility and alternative fuel propulsion systems are nascent but offer significant domestic and global opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin stood at 29.30%; management believes sustainable margins of 29%-30% are achievable due to in-house technology development and economies of scale.
- Fusion component price increases are handled via a complete pass-through model for external clients.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fusion offers >2 lakh square meters of installed capacity for flexible and rigid PCBs. Internal consumption will utilize double-digit percentages for initial capacities, while PCBA capability utilization will be 30% to 40%.
- R&D team consists of over 100 engineers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash conversion cycle improved marginally.
- Debt-to-equity ratio remains healthy; no fundraising is foreseen in the near future for OpEx/working capital based on the current order book.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Long execution cycles, inventory management, customer inspections, and milestone-based payments are inherent to railway projects.
- Geopolitical issues impact component pricing, though pass-through is used where possible.
- Safety-critical product approvals (like Kavach trials and MSDAC) involve long gestation and trial periods.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shashank Jha asked about the timeline for Kavach approved vendor status and revenue recognition via Progota.** Management stated trials are ongoing and progressing successfully, but declined to provide a specific date. They clarified that Progota is an associate company (46.5% stake), so only profits are consolidated, not line-by-line revenues.
2. **Chitresh Lunawat inquired about the execution timeline for Kavach orders and trial completion.** Management noted that orders have 12-month delivery periods and they are hopeful of meeting timelines while undergoing safety-critical trials.
3. **Pritesh Chheda asked about granularity of Advanced Rail vs. legacy business in revenues and backlog.** Management stated Advanced Rail is fully integrated, with locomotive subsystems and green mobility representing over 80% of current revenues and future order books (ex-Kavach).
4. **Mudit Jain questioned the sustainability of 29%-30% margins.** Management affirmed these are sustainable due to in-house developed technologies reducing costs over time and economies of scale.
5. **Shubham Agarwal asked about international conversion timelines from InnoTrans Germany.** Management stated international opportunities are long-term, targeting 25% to 50% of revenue from international markets over the next 3 to 5 years.
6. **Puneet Kaur asked about addressable opportunities and FY30 revenue expectations.** Management confirmed that a 40%-50% CAGR naturally points toward ~INR 1,000 crore revenue by FY30, though declined to break down individual platform targets.

### One-line takeaway
Concord Control Systems delivered robust H1 FY27 performance with ~95% revenue growth and ~110% EBITDA growth, backed by an order book of INR 846 crore and a 40%-50% CAGR growth target driven by railways modernization, safety systems, and global expansion.
