# Systematic Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and profits grew YoY alongside capacity expansion and order wins in the new-age business.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs. 3,113.6 million (up 22.7% YoY from Rs. 2,536.8 million in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs. 224 million (up 11.8% YoY from Rs. 200.5 million in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.2%.
- **Profit Before Tax (H1 FY27):** Rs. 170 million (up 36.9% YoY from Rs. 124.2 million in H1 FY26).
- **Installed Steel Wire Capacity:** 1,72,000 metric tonnes per annum across 6 facilities.
- **Installed OPGW Capacity:** 12,000 km per annum.
- **Installed OFC Capacity:** 48,000 km per annum.
- **Steel Wire Sales Mix:** Power transmission 50%, Infrastructure 24%, Data transmission 10%, General engineering 10%, Agriculture 6%.
- **Order Wins:** Approximately Rs. 100 crores from Power Grid for OPGW, plus an order worth approximately Rs. 30 crores from another power utility and railway approvals/orders.

### Highlights
- Maiden earnings conference call as a listed company (listed in September 2025).
- Acquired an operating GI wire manufacturing facility at Siltara, Chhattisgarh (48,000 metric tonnes per annum capacity, spread over 10 acres).
- Completed the acquisition of Wire Brigade Industries Private Limited at Howrah, West Bengal (24,000 metric tonnes per annum capacity), effective November 6, 2026.
- Board approved a potential capital raise aggregating approximately Rs. 33.94 crores to support expansion and the Wire Brigade acquisition.
- *Note vs Record:* The record shows successful transition to net debt-free status in Q4 FY26; the current call highlights continued capital raising (Rs. 33.94 crores) for acquisitions.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the legacy steel wire business to grow at an annual rate of 20%-25% over coming years.
- Management estimates annual revenue potential of Rs. 200-Rs. 250 crores for OPGW and around Rs. 50 crores for OFC at optimum capacity utilization, targeting optimum utilization over the next 18-24 months.
- Combined annual revenue with newly acquired units is projected around Rs. 700-Rs. 750 crores, with peak potential reaching approximately Rs. 1,000 crores at full capacity.
- Steel wire margins are expected to remain broadly stable over the next 1-2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- H1 traditionally accounts for ~40% of annual business due to monsoon seasonality, with ~60% in H2.
- Central Electricity Authority's National Electricity Plan foresees ~Rs. 9.15 lakh crores in transmission infrastructure investments up to 2031-32.
- Expanding broadband, Bharatnet, and telecom infrastructure are driving demand for OPGW and OFC.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins are targeted at ~25% or higher for OPGW and ~18% or higher for OFC.
- Management stated that cost increases can be fully passed on to customers; supply chain availability for optical fiber is noted as a global constraint.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total combined installed steel wire manufacturing capacity stands at 1,72,000 metric tonnes per annum.
- Siltara (Chhattisgarh) and Howrah (West Bengal) facilities are being integrated and debottlenecked.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone financials reported for H1; West Bengal facility financials will be included on a consolidated basis starting H2 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global supply constraints exist for optical fiber, impacting the raw material supply chain.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankur Gulati (Genuity Capital):** Asked about combined revenue run-rate and OPGW execution timelines. *Management answered that combined revenue should reach Rs. 700-750 crores, and the Rs. 82 crore Power Grid OPGW order has a 24-month execution window.*
- **Taher (Grobiz Fund):** Inquired about export approvals and non-Power Grid OPGW order book. *Management stated they have ~Rs. 100-125 crores of total OPGW orders in hand and are focusing primarily on the strong domestic market first.*
- **B. R. Nahar (Mili Capital):** Asked if optical fiber supply is secured and if margins will rise. *Management confirmed optical fiber supply is a global constraint, but high OPGW demand supports higher profitability.*
- **Mahesh Attal (Dhairya Investment Advisors):** Asked about the capacity utilization and revenue contribution of the new-age business. *Management explained that new-age technical products require design approval and pilot projects before scaling up.*
- **Kapil Advani (Aarth Growth Fund):** Inquired about volume vs realization growth and product use cases. *Management noted they do not perform legacy benchmarking due to market volatility and that OPGW is primarily used for power transmission and data transmission combined.*

### One-line takeaway
Systematic Industries reported 22.7% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27, expanded its manufacturing footprint via acquisitions in Chhattisgarh and West Bengal, and secured major OPGW orders to scale its high-margin new-age business.
