# IFGL Refractories Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue and PAT grew significantly, cash flows improved, and European operations turned profitable.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained consolidated EBITDA margin guidance of 9% to 10% for FY27 (same as previous quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from mixed/cautious to more positive, backed by a strong recovery in exports and European operations returning to profitability.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Termination of the Marvels International JV and impairment of investment (Rs 1.69 crore); voluntary liquidation of Hofmann Ceramic in Czech Republic completed effective July 1st, 2026.
- Last quarter's promises: Sheffield Refractories technology transfer entered phase 2 (equipment ordered for delivery in Dec/Feb-Mar); Chinese tap hole clay plant commenced commercial operations as announced.

### Key numbers
- Standalone Total Income (Q2 FY27): Rs 325 crore (13% YoY)
- Standalone Total Income (H1 FY27): Rs 624 crore (10% YoY)
- Standalone Gross Margin (Q2 FY27): 43.9% vs 45.2% YoY
- Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 35 crore vs Rs 37 crore YoY (EBITDA margin: 10.8%)
- Standalone H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 66 crore
- Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 18 crore (23% YoY)
- Standalone H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 34 crore (15% YoY)
- Consolidated Total Income (Q2 FY27): Rs 555 crore (13% YoY)
- Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27): Rs 1,071 crore (13% YoY)
- Consolidated Gross Margin (Q2 FY27): 48.9% vs 48.6% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 51 crore (28% YoY) (EBITDA margin: 9.2%)
- Consolidated H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 91 crore (15% YoY)
- Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 26 crore (103% YoY)
- Consolidated H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 43 crore (82% YoY)
- Standalone Domestic Revenue Growth (Q2 FY27): 5% YoY
- Standalone Export Revenue Growth (Q2 FY27): 40% YoY
- Consolidated Cash Flow Improvement (H1 FY27): Increased by Rs 68 crore vs Rs 8 crore YoY
- US Steel Production (YTD October '26): 75 million metric tonnes (6% YoY growth)
- Tianjin Monocon Tap Hole Clay Plant Capacity: 2,000 metric tonnes per annum (revenue potential of Rs 20 crore to Rs 25 crore at full utilization)
- Capex Guidance: Around Rs 40 crore to Rs 50 crore for the year

### Highlights
- Consolidated performance showed strong revenue growth alongside a 28% YoY increase in EBITDA and 103% YoY increase in PAT.
- European business returned to a positive segment result compared to a loss in the corresponding quarter last year.
- Voluntary liquidation of Hofmann Ceramic (Czech Republic) completed effective July 1st, 2026.
- Tianjin Monocon Aluminous Refractories (China step-down subsidiary) inaugurated and commenced commercial operations of its new tap hole clay manufacturing plant on 16/11/2026.
- Joint venture with Marvels International Group and Marvel Refractories (Anshan) was mutually terminated effective October 14th, 2026; company impaired its investment by Rs 1.69 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 to remain encouraging and balanced despite seasonal Christmas holidays due to geographical diversification.
- Management aims to maintain standalone double-digit EBITDA margins and consolidated EBITDA margins of 9% to 10% for FY27.
- Growth in India is expected to track rising crude steel production.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian steel industry demonstrated resilience with healthy crude steel and finished steel consumption growth.
- US steel production grew robustly by 6% YoY to 75 million metric tonnes YTD October '26.
- European steel production remained subdued, though European operations improved.
- Middle East, Turkey, and Asia showing good traction for exports from India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone gross margins contracted (43.9% vs 45.2% YoY) due to higher raw material and input costs (alumina, resin-bonded materials, magnesia).
- Standalone employee costs decreased by 50 bps sequentially in percentage terms.
- Price increases are being actively negotiated and supported by customers, though with a time lag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital work-in-progress stands at Rs 39 crore at the consolidated level.
- Planned capex for the year is around Rs 40 crore to Rs 50 crore, with debottlenecking underway for select product lines.
- Khurda project timelines remain under exploratory review due to geopolitical shifts.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows improved significantly by Rs 68 crore in H1 FY27, driven by better receivables and inventory control.
- Land from the terminated Marvels JV remains with the company as an asset.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global supply chain disruptions, rising freight costs, and increased lead times impacting operations.
- Ongoing raw material cost pressures from geopolitical conflicts affecting inputs like alumina and magnesia.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company) asked about CWIP, pricing mechanism, and Marvel JV termination:** Management explained CWIP includes India/US operational and Khurda capex, detailed that bespoke contracts are periodically negotiated with customer support, and confirmed the Marvel JV was terminated due to financial issues though land remains an asset.
2. **Saket Kapoor asked about Europe outlook during Christmas and margin guidance:** Management stated Christmas impact is offset by broader geographic spread, guided for 9-10% consolidated EBITDA margin for FY27, and noted capacity utilization in India is 60% to 70%.
3. **Sahil Sanghvi (Monarch Networth) asked about export geographies and Sheffield localization:** Management noted exports are distributed across the Middle East, Turkey, and Asia; phase 2 of Sheffield technology involves ordered equipment arriving between December and February/March, with growth expected in FY27-28.
4. **Sahil Sanghvi asked about Khurda project status and capex guidance:** Management stated Khurda is under exploratory review, debottlenecking is underway for next year's output, and full-year capex is guided at Rs 40 crore to Rs 50 crore.
5. **Kushal Maheshwari (AK Capital) asked about the Middle East subsidiary market share:** Management highlighted strong growth in Saudi Arabia and GCC but avoided specific numbers due to day-to-day geopolitical uncertainties.
6. **Saket Kapoor asked about Chinese subsidiary details and raw material trends:** Management clarified Tianjin tap hole clay plant has Rs 25 crore peak revenue potential with a long gestation period, and noted raw material prices (alumina, resin, magnesia) are facing upward pressures from wars.

### One-line takeaway
IFGL delivered robust Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue and a 103% YoY surge in PAT, supported by strong export recovery and turnaround in European operations despite input cost headwinds.
