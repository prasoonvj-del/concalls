# Lohia Corp Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 42% YoY and a record order book, despite slight EBITDA margin moderation due to commodity price inflation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained; management projects 10-15% growth for FY28 and expects to reach Rs 2,500-2,600 crore revenue next year.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong" to "heavy traction" in the domestic market after 2-3 years of low investment.
- **New topics:** Secured first order from South Korea; executed specialized stretching line for the defense sector (bulletproof vest components); recycling segment gaining traction (ReclaMax 120, ReclaPro D150).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Capex of Rs 75 crore confirmed for maintenance and new capacity (recycling/conversion/coating).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 594 crore (up 42% YoY, 18% QoQ).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,097 crore (up 50% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 116 crore (up 33% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 19.4% (vs 20.7% in Q2 FY26; 19.9% in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT:** Rs 77 crore (up 48% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 13%.
- **Order Book:** ~Rs 2,000 crore (up 12% QoQ, 93% YoY).
- **Net Cash Position:** Rs 123 crore (Total borrowing Rs 115 crore vs Cash/investments Rs 238 crore).
- **Net Debt to Equity:** -0.18x.
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** -0.57x.

### Highlights
- Record order book of ~Rs 2,000 crore.
- First order secured from South Korea.
- Executed 1,000mm specialized stretching line for the defense sector (ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene).
- Recycling segment: Supplied ReclaMax 120 (500 kg/hr capacity) on Aug 26; advancing ReclaPro D150 (mother-baby extruder).
- TTRC led BIS working group on polylactic acid (bioplastic) standards.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth of ~30-35%.
- Management projects FY28 growth at 10-15%.
- Internal EBITDA margin target remains 20% (range 19-21%).
- Order book expected to remain in Rs 2,000-2,200 crore range by March end.
- H2 revenue expected to be Rs 1,200-1,300 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic market seeing "heavy traction" after 2-3 years of low investment; expects this to continue for 5-6 months.
- Export order book at all-time high (>Rs 500 crore).
- Growth drivers: Non-bag applications (wrapping fabric, specialty fabric), cement bag sector (shift to block bottom bags), and BOPP bags.
- Recycling segment: Currently executing 1-2 machines/month; expects revenue of Rs 30-35 crore this year, doubling next year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin moderation (19.4%) attributed to higher commodity prices.
- Contracts are for deferred delivery (6-9 months); price hikes implemented in Feb and July 2026.
- Operating leverage helped offset cost increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current capacity supports up to Rs 2,500 crore revenue.
- FY27 capex: ~Rs 75 crore (maintenance + new capacity for recycling/conversion/coating).
- Future plans: Building a new shed for assembly capacity.
- Vendor ecosystem: Adding new vendors and augmenting existing capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital cycle: 92 days (vs 84 days at end of FY26).
- Customer advances: ~20% of order book.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations impacting commodity prices.
- Potential for order execution delays (site readiness, bank finance, LC issues).
- Expects some tapering in order book growth next year due to high base.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rushil Selarka:** Asked about price pass-through. *Management:* Contracts are 6-9 months; price lists revised in Feb/July; discounts tightened to manage short-term inflation.
2. **Viraj Mehta:** Asked about capex for next year. *Management:* Rs 75 crore spent this year; planning new shed for assembly capacity.
3. **Nishita:** Asked about recycling revenue. *Management:* ~Rs 30-35 crore this year; confident of doubling next year.
4. **Pankaj Saraf:** Asked about domestic growth drivers. *Management:* New segments (conversion machinery, block bottom bags) and non-bag applications.
5. **Saket Kapoor:** Asked about "other current liabilities" (Rs 478 crore). *Management:* Primarily customer advances.

### One-line takeaway
Lohia Corp delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with 42% revenue growth and a record Rs 2,000 crore order book, while expanding into specialized defense and recycling machinery.
