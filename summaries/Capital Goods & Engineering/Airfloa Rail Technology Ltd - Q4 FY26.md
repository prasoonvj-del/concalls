# Airfloa Rail Technology Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-08 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H2 FY2026 & FY2026
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 66% YoY, solid order book visibility, and ambitious FY2027 guidance supported by capacity expansion and new segment entries.

### Key numbers
- **FY2026 Revenue:** Rs. 319.6 crore (up 66% YoY)
- **H2 FY2026 Revenue:** Rs. 229.1 crore (up 114% YoY vs H2 FY2025)
- **FY2026 EBITDA:** Rs. 64.2 crore (up 33% YoY)
- **FY2026 EBITDA Margin:** 20.1%
- **FY2026 PAT:** Rs. 39.1 crore (up 52% YoY)
- **FY2026 PAT Margin:** 12.2%
- **ROE (FY2026):** 22.4%
- **ROCE (FY2026):** 24.9%
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.2x as of March 31, 2026
- **Unexecuted Order Book:** Approx. Rs. 486.9 crore as of May 2026 (Govt: Rs. 415-420 crore; Non-Govt: Rs. 50-55 crore; Defence: Rs. 29 crore)
- **Active Bid Pipeline:** Approx. Rs. 1,200 crore (~70% Indian Railways, Rs. 120 crore metro, Rs. 60-70 crore HAL/defence)
- **Receivables (March 31, 2026):** Rs. 214 crore (~20% collected by May end; ~70% expected by June end)

### Highlights
- Evolving from a component-focused railway supplier into an integrated engineering and manufacturing platform (interiors, assemblies, code systems, turnkey projects).
- Proposed Joint Venture with Big Bang Boom Solutions expected to be formally incorporated by June 15, 2026, targeting air defence systems, autonomous drones, and high-power microwave laser systems.
- Implemented a channel partner model across regional zonal railways to execute refurbishment contracts without heavy direct infrastructure overheads.
- Secured in-principle/formal sanctions for Rs. 120 crore in debt funding (Rs. 60 crore sanctioned, another Rs. 60 crore expected between June 15 and June 30, 2026) at an interest rate of 8.25%.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY2027 Revenue Guidance:** Targeted at approximately Rs. 500 crore.
- **FY2027 PAT Margin Guidance:** Maintained between 12% and 13%.
- **Medium-term Outlook:** Timeline toward the Rs. 1,000 crore revenue milestone is extended modestly due to selective bidding and commodity volatility, but long-term fundamentals remain intact. Management noted potential to target Rs. 1,000 crore revenue and Rs. 150 crore PAT by FY2028 if conditions remain favorable.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Key railway growth drivers include Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat, metro rail projects, EMUs, refurbishment programs, and border modernization.
- Refurbishment segment holds a Rs. 26,000 crore sanctioned outlay (initial allotment of 25,000 coaches over 3-4 years); management expects to bag around Rs. 100 crore in orders this year.
- Defence segment includes approved AMCA simulator prototypes from HAL with volume orders expected.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin contracted from 25% to 20.1% due to steep commodity inflation (aluminium prices up >80%, stainless steel up 60-65% YoY). Price variation clauses only covered a portion of raw material cost increases.
- Other expenses increased due to higher CSR expenditure, bill discounting charges, and forex-related costs.
- Finance costs declined despite business scaling due to lower processing costs, debt repayments, and reduced delayed payment charges.
- R&D spend is targeted to increase to 8%-9% of sales in FY2027 (from ~4% previously) to fund advanced product development and technology transfers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capacity utilization is currently at ~90%, managed through a two-shift mechanism introduced late last year.
- Capex outlay for FY2027 is approximately Rs. 30 crore to Rs. 35 crore, earmarked for a 14-acre facility development (50,000 to 1,00,000 sq. ft. shed initially) to replace high-rent discrete units.
- Procurement of high-precision Chinese machinery is delayed due to new Indian end-user certificate requirements by the Chinese government; management is evaluating alternative machinery relevant to lightweight rail interiors.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital efficiency improved: receivable days improved by 24 days and inventory days by 48 days.
- Trade receivables of Rs. 214 crore are elevated due to heavy March execution; ~20% collected by May end, with 70% targeted by June end.
- Additional debt of Rs. 120 crore being raised (8.25% interest rate) to support working capital and growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Steep commodity price volatility (aluminium and steel) and incomplete pass-through via price variation clauses.
- Delays in procuring high-precision machinery from China due to regulatory/end-user certificate hurdles.
- Working capital intensity and negative operating cash flows requiring debt funding.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Paras Chheda (Purpleone Vertex Ventures) on Working Capital & Debt:** Management clarified that receivables will normalize in 3-6 months (target cycle 60-70 days), and Rs. 120 crore in debt (Rs. 60 crore sanctioned, Rs. 60 crore expected by June 30 at 8.25% interest) will fund growth without equity dilution in FY2027.
2. **Archit Agrawal on EBITDA Margin Contraction:** Management explained that an 80% spike in aluminium and 60-65% surge in stainless steel prices compressed margins, as price variation clauses only cover part of the escalation.
3. **Sahil Garg on R&D and Cost Burdens:** Management defended the increase in R&D spend to 8%-9% and additional debt interest, stating it is necessary for high-value defence systems (Big Bang Boom JV) and that margins will be protected via selective bidding and higher-margin orders.
4. **Rudraksh Raheja on the Channel Partner Model:** Management explained that zonal railway contracts (especially refurbishment) are executed via regional channel partners to save on EMD, bank guarantees, and infrastructure overheads while sharing limited margins.
5. **Khush Banthia on Machinery Imports from China:** Management stated that the delay in importing Chinese machinery will not impact FY2027 revenue targets since two primary machines are already secured, two-shift operations are active, and channel partners will aid execution.
6. **Subhajit Das on Lumpy Railway Orders:** Management clarified that lumpy sales trends are inherent to Indian Railways due to customer spending patterns, whereas private customers like Siemens and Alstom follow structured monthly schedules.

### One-line takeaway
Airfloa Rail Technology delivered 66% revenue growth in FY2026 with a 20.1% EBITDA margin, guiding for Rs. 500 crore revenue in FY2027 backed by an order book of Rs. 486.9 crore, channel partner execution models, and expansion into defence and refurbishment.
