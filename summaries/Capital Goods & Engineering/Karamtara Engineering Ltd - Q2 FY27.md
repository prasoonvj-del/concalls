# Karamtara Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - strong revenue growth (62% YoY), robust order book (Rs 6,500 cr), maintained FY27 guidance, and multiple growth engines advancing on schedule.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,647 cr (+62% YoY); international 73%, renewable products 73%
- EBITDA: Rs 196 cr, margin 11.9% (+46% YoY, +~100 bps QoQ)
- PAT: Rs 94.6 cr, margin 5.7% (+37% YoY)
- H1 FY27: Revenue +67% YoY (renewable 77%, international 70%); EBITDA Rs 370 cr (11.5% margin); PAT Rs 182 cr (5.6% margin)
- Order book (Sep 30): Rs 6,500 cr (Jun 30: Rs 6,000 cr); fresh intake Q2: Rs 2,100-2,200 cr; ~60% (Rs ~4,000 cr) to execute in FY27
- Working capital cycle: 37 days (H1); debtor days 94 (down from 100 QoQ)
- Capex H1: Rs 370 cr; gross block Rs 2,411 cr; net worth Rs 2,083 cr
- Installed capacity: 8,79,800 tpa (excl. rolling/galvanizing)
- US order book contribution: 57%; new Rs 550 cr order received Nov 5

### Highlights
- Three growth engines articulated: (1) Transmission towers – Bhachau facility commissioned, targeting 1200 kV capability; (2) Saudi Arabia multiproduct facility – trial production Dec 2026, galvanizing Mar 2027, meaningful revenue FY28, large FY29; (3) PEB for data centers – 100k tpa at Kutch, trials Mar-Apr 2027.
- FY27 guidance maintained: revenue growth 60-65% YoY, broad-based across solar, wind, transmission, fasteners.
- Capacity utilisation target 60-70% by FY27 end (optimum 85-90%); combined sellable capacity ~1.4 mtpa once Saudi and Kutch online.
- H2 FY27 capex ~Rs 250 cr (Saudi, Gujarat profile unit, PEB, Tower Testing facility); funded via internal accruals, some debt; Rs 200 cr IPO proceeds still in FD.
- Two independent directors appointed Nov 5 (Ajoy Mehta, Sanjib Chaudhuri).
- Defense/aerospace subsidiary formed, team building, product strategy under deliberation – no revenue guidance yet.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue growth 60-65% YoY maintained.
- Margin expansion expected in H2 as freight costs pass through (Q2 already +100 bps QoQ).
- Saudi revenue: initial contribution FY28, full ramp FY29-30.
- PEB facility revenue contribution from FY28.
- Historical H2 revenue share ~55-57% (seasonal post-monsoon pickup).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global renewable capacity target 11,200 GW by 2030 ($7.2 tn investment); India 500 GW non-fossil by 2030 ($400 bn).
- Power transmission in “supercycle” driven by renewable evacuation, grid upgrades (220/400→765 kV), cross-border connectivity.
- US demand “very robust” led by data centers/chips, aging grids; Europe and Australia growing (anti-dumping on Chinese products).
- Middle East crisis increasing transit days and freight costs; local content rules mandate Saudi manufacturing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin 11.9% (Q2), up ~100 bps QoQ; freight (ocean + land) primary cost pressure.
- Raw material risk mitigated via back-to-back booking; currency hedged naturally (import raw material for export orders).
- US contracts FOB – freight and tariff pass-through; Middle East partly pass-through, partly absorbed.
- Expect further margin improvement in Q3/Q4 as freight passes through and Middle East logistics ease.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bhachau transmission facility recently commissioned.
- Saudi: trial production Dec 2026, galvanizing Mar 2027; 12-18 month ramp.
- Kutch (Gujarat): new rolling mill (transmission/solar tracker piles) + dedicated PEB facility (100k tpa) – trials Mar-Apr 2027.
- Tower Testing facility in H2 FY27.
- Target combined sellable capacity ~1.4 mtpa (excl. captive rolling/galvanizing).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital cycle 37 days (H1); debtor days 94 (vs 100 Q1, up YoY due to 73% international mix vs 45%).
- Gross block Rs 2,411 cr; net worth Rs 2,083 cr.
- IPO funds Rs 200 cr parked in FD; capex funded by internal accruals, term debt if needed.
- Order book Rs 6,500 cr provides visibility.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Middle East crisis elevating transit days, freight costs, and debtor cycles.
- Freight at peak – sustained high rates could compress margins if not fully passed through.
- Geopolitical turmoil (US tariffs, Middle East) – management “hopeful” but acknowledges execution risk.
- High international revenue share (73%) lengthens working capital cycle.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Transmission share trajectory** – Mgmt: 20% in FY27, improves FY28+ from India and Saudi.
2. **Debtor days increase** – Mgmt: due to product/geographic mix (73% international) and Middle East transit delays; expects improvement as volumes grow and crisis eases.
3. **Margin drivers** – Mgmt: freight main headwind; ~100 bps QoQ recovery in Q2; further pass-through in Q3/Q4.
4. **Saudi progress** – Mgmt: plant nearly constructed, machines arriving, trial production Dec, galvanizing Mar; bullish on Expo 2030/World Cup 2034 demand.
5. **US demand post-election** – Mgmt: demand robust (data centers, grid upgrade); tariffs pass-through; 30% price advantage vs US producers remains; new Rs 550 cr order received.
6. **Defense subsidiary** – Mgmt: subsidiary formed, team building, evaluating product alignment (fasteners, stampings, forgings); no timeline/guidance yet.

### One-line takeaway
Karamtara delivers 62% YoY revenue growth with expanding margins, a Rs 6,500 cr order book, and three visible growth engines (transmission, Saudi, data-center PEB) on track, maintaining 60-65% FY27 guidance despite freight/geopolitical headwinds.
