# Karamtara Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, robust order book, and successful capacity expansion, maintaining guidance despite geopolitical headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,647 crore (up 62% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 196 crore (up 46% YoY); Margin: 11.9%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 94.6 crore (up 37% YoY); Margin: 5.7%.
*   **H1 Revenue Growth:** 67% YoY.
*   **Order Book:** Rs 6,500 crore (vs Rs 6,000 crore as of June 30, 2026).
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** 37 days.
*   **H1 Capex:** Rs 370 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance:** Renewable products contributed 73% of Q2 revenue (77% in H1). International revenue stood at 73% for Q2 (70% in H1).
*   **Order Execution:** Management expects to execute ~Rs 4,000 crore of the current order book in the current financial year.
*   **New Board Appointments:** Ajoy Mehta and Sanjeeb Chaudhuri appointed as Additional Independent Directors effective Nov 5, 2026.
*   **Record:** The company confirmed the ongoing expansion in Bhachau and Saudi Arabia, consistent with the record of recent infrastructure scaling.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Guidance:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth target of 60% to 65% over FY26.
*   **Seasonality:** Management reiterated that H2 is historically stronger (55-57% of annual revenue) compared to H1 (42-45%).
*   **Long-term Growth:** Management targets a 30-35% CAGR, noting the base is expanding.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Transmission Supercycle:** Management sees a decade-long supercycle in power transmission driven by renewable energy evacuation, grid modernization (moving to 765 kV), and cross-border connectivity.
*   **US Market:** Demand is "very robust," driven by data centers and AI-related power requirements. The company recently secured a large Rs 550 crore order in the US.
*   **Saudi Arabia:** Local manufacturing is mandatory for energy infrastructure projects; the facility is expected to start trial production in December (transmission/solar) and galvanizing by March.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** EBITDA margin improved by ~100 bps sequentially. Management expects further expansion in Q3/Q4.
*   **Freight Impact:** Geopolitical issues (Middle East) increased freight costs and transit times, impacting margins. Management noted these are largely pass-through in contracts, but some Middle East contracts required partial absorption.
*   **Commodity Risk:** Mitigated via back-to-back procurement and contractual safeguards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current Capacity:** 8,79,800 metric tons per annum (excluding captive facilities).
*   **Future Capacity:** Targeting 1.4 million tons sellable capacity upon completion of Saudi and Kutch expansions.
*   **PEB/Data Centers:** New facility for Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) targeting data centers is expected to be ready by the beginning of the next financial year.
*   **Tower Testing:** Planning to add in-house tower testing facilities.
*   **Capex Guidance:** ~Rs 250 crore planned for H2 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Debtor days increased slightly due to higher international sales mix (longer cycle) and Middle East transit delays, but improved sequentially from 100 to 94 days.
*   **Liquidity:** Sufficient cash flow; IPO proceeds remain largely unutilized (Rs 200 crore parked in FDs).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical/Logistics:** Middle East crisis impacting transit times and freight costs.
*   **Execution:** Potential delays in project deliveries due to logistics bottlenecks.
*   **Tariffs:** US tariffs are viewed as a pass-through cost; management does not anticipate a material impact on demand or pricing power.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On US Tariff Risk:** Management clarified that tariffs are a pass-through; even if tariffs drop, the company maintains a 30% price advantage over local US manufacturers.
*   **On Defense/Aerospace:** Management confirmed the formation of a subsidiary and is currently evaluating product alignment (fasteners, stamping) but provided no specific revenue guidance yet.
*   **On Saudi Revenue:** No specific revenue number provided; expects meaningful contribution starting next year, with full utilization by FY29-30.
*   **On Competition:** Management noted that Chinese competition is limited in the US/Europe due to national security concerns and quality requirements (welding/specs).
*   **On Order Book:** Management confirmed fresh order intake of Rs 2,100-2,200 crore during the quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Karamtara is aggressively scaling capacity and expanding its global footprint, leveraging a strong transmission supercycle and data center demand to maintain a 60-65% growth trajectory despite logistics-related margin headwinds.
