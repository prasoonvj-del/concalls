# INOX India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a record-high order backlog, robust H1 financial performance, and growing traction in sunrise sectors like space, LNG, and semiconductors, despite temporary margin headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Total income (Q2 FY27): Rs 467 crore (up 26.6% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 100 crore (up 10.8% YoY)
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 21.3%
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 66 crore (up 7.8% YoY)
- PAT margin (Q2 FY27): 14.2%
- Total income (H1 FY27): Rs 849 crore
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 189 crore; EBITDA margin: 22.3%
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 127 crore; PAT margin: 14.9%
- Order backlog: Rs 2,268 crore (72% from exports)
- Total fund availability / net cash: Rs 384 crore
- Beverage keg sales growth (Q2 FY27): ~446% YoY
- Beverage keg order book growth (Q2 FY27): ~155% YoY
- Liquid cylinder dispatches (H1 FY27): >1,500 cylinders worth >Rs 25 crore

### Highlights
- Order backlog crossed Rs 2,200 crore, reaching an all-time high of Rs 2,268 crore. Space sector orders comprise ~Rs 1,100 crore (driven by a major U.S. aerospace customer).
- Secured a mega order for 16 cryogenic subcoolers (12 LOX, 4 methane) from a U.S. space company, to be manufactured at the Savli facility.
- Entered into a 3-year global supply agreement with a leading global brewing company (requirement >4 lakh kegs across 22 countries for 2027); secured 4 out of 5 major global beverage customer approvals.
- Commenced execution for initial piping scope at Tata Electronics' semiconductor facility in Dholera; inaugurated the 'SemiOrbit' Orbital Welding Skill Excellence Center.
- Secured an order worth ~Rs 35 crore for specialized cryogenic equipment for the LIGO-India gravitational-wave observatory project in Maharashtra.
- Dispatched major equipment for the Bahamas project and the first 2 tanks for the U.S. aerospace customer during September.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H1 EBITDA margin of 22.3% to remain within the expected range going forward, noting that Q2 margin dips were due to a mix of rate and strategic pricing and are not structural.
- Company is growing at 18% to 20% minimum, with potential for faster growth if larger opportunities materialize (CEO).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global supply chains and logistics face complexity due to the West Asia conflict, but underlying demand across industrial gases, LNG, aerospace, space, scientific research, and semiconductors remains encouraging.
- Indian Railways' launch of LNG trains brought 4 related orders (2 dispatched).
- Private Indian space startup ecosystem showing strong potential with pipelines estimated at Rs 2,000 crore to Rs 3,000 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 margin decline was driven by a mix of rate and strategic pricing for competitive market share, geopolitical uncertainties, and logistic costs.
- For the U.S. space order, freight is in the customer's scope (Ex-works delivery), mitigating freight cost risks. Custom-built/non-standard products carry better margins than standard equipment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Savli facility has ~50% available space for expansion.
- Land acquired near Kandla port; construction underway to finish by March 2027.
- Funded entirely through internal accruals; debt-free status maintained.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total fund availability stood at Rs 384 crore as of Q2 FY27.
- Company remains debt-free with net cash.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Availability of skilled manpower is a near-term challenge, addressed through internal training centers (e.g., SemiOrbit).
- Geopolitical uncertainties and credit issues ramped up in the last quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Surana (Monarch Networth) on margins:** Asked if the Q2 margin dip is the new normal. Management clarified it is not structural, pointing to H1 EBITDA margins of 22.3%.
- **Eshwar (Unnamed firm) on U.S. space order execution:** Inquired about execution timelines. Management stated 10% to 15% will be completed by March 2027, and 80% to 90% in FY28.
- **Vinil Shah (Dalal & Broacha) on pricing strategy:** Asked why aggressive pricing is needed given specialized manufacturing. Management explained it applies to standard equipment for market share, while custom-built non-standard products command better margins.
- **Bhavika Singhvi (Niveshaay) on subcooler order contribution:** Asked about subcooler order size. Management stated subcoolers and associated tanks for the U.S. space customer make up 60% to 70% of the space order backlog (>Rs 1,000 crore).
- **Jay Negandhi (Ambit Capital) on semiconductor opportunity size:** Asked about potential order values. Management stated exact opportunity is hard to quantify, but initial Dholera fab piping scope serves as a foundational entry into a massive addressable market.

### One-loke takeaway
INOX India reported record H1 performance and an all-time high order backlog of Rs 2,268 crore, underpinned by major international space and industrial cryogenic wins, even as margins experienced temporary headwinds.
