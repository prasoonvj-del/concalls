# Tempsens Instruments (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 reported)
**Tone:** Positive - H1 revenue grew 33.7% YoY driven by strong exports, successful debt reduction post-IPO, and robust pipeline execution in new OEM segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (revenue growth for the year expected to be in line with the 3-year blended growth rate).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, with management anticipating a strong second half as new OEM supplies and capacity expansions ramp up.
- New topics or risks: Added a sales office in Ajman (UAE) and discussed prototype discussions for AdBlue heaters with an automotive manufacturer; no new risks introduced.
- Last quarter's promises: Laboratory furnaces received ETL and CE approvals with bulk dispatches starting in Q3 FY27 as promised; 5 capacity expansion projects remain on their announced timelines.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 255 crore, up 33.7% YoY.
- **H1 Export Revenue:** Rs 94 crore, more than doubled YoY (37% of product revenue vs 25% YoY).
- **H1 Temperature Sensing Revenue:** Rs 133 crore, up 57% YoY (53% of product revenue; organic growth ~25%).
- **H1 Electrical Heating Revenue:** Rs 47 crore, up 44% YoY.
- **H1 Specialized Cable Revenue:** Rs 74 crore.
- **H1 Domestic Revenue:** Rs 159 crore, up 12% YoY.
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 54.9 crore, up 11.9% YoY (margin at 21.2% of total income vs 25.2% YoY).
- **H1 Gross Margin:** 45.7%.
- **H1 Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 44.5 crore, up 11.7% YoY.
- **H1 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 35.7 crore, up 18.1% YoY (Adjusted PAT up 17% to Rs 38 crore).
- **Total Borrowings:** Rs 40.7 crore as of September 30, 2026 (down from Rs 77.9 crore as of March 31, 2026).
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.06x (down from 0.15x).
- **Capacity Utilization:** Approximately 80%.

### Highlights
- Utilized Rs 55 crore of IPO fresh issue proceeds (total Rs 95 crore) to repay debt.
- Export revenue more than doubled, aided by Tempsens Germany and Tempsens Polska consolidation and sustained organic growth (organic export growth ~45%).
- Standard laboratory furnaces received ETL and CE approvals; bulk dispatches starting in Q3 FY27.
- Temperature sensor supplies for fuel cells commenced in the last 2 months; cable harness approvals are underway.
- Added a new sales office in Ajman, UAE to serve the Gulf region.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year revenue growth to be in line with the blended growth over the last 3 years.
- Management expects profitability to progressively improve in the second half as new product supplies (harnesses, furnaces, OEM sensors) ramp up.
- Management expects OEM opportunities (fuel cells, emission control, life sciences) to account for ~15% of revenue next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Traditional heavy industries (power, oil & gas, petrochemicals, glass, cement) remain core growth drivers.
- China Plus One and supply chain diversification are driving increased global demand for Indian engineered products.
- Traditional heavy industry order books stand at 3 to 4 months, while OEM business visibility extends to 1 to 2 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 gross margin stood at 45.7%, broadly in line with FY26 full-year level of 46%.
- Employee cost increased 41% YoY to Rs 42.1 crore due to team building for new capacities, corporate structure investments, newly consolidated entities, and ESOP charges of Rs 1.1 crore.
- Other expenses grew 37% YoY to Rs 25.6 crore.
- Finance cost for H1 was Rs 2.5 crore; full-year debt reduction impact will reflect in subsequent quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 5 capacity expansion projects are on track for commissioning between Q4 FY27 and Q3 FY28, with an aggregate peak revenue potential of ~Rs 500 crore at full utilization.
- Unit 1 brownfield plant (50,000 sq. ft.) for OEM contact sensors on track for Q3 FY28 (peak revenue potential Rs 120 crore).
- Unit 6 plant (50,000 sq. ft.) for mid-voltage heaters on track for Q1 FY28 (peak revenue potential Rs 200 crore).
- Electrical heating custom-built furnace capacity adding 25,000 sq. ft.
- Unit 8 specialized cable plant on track for Q2 FY28 (peak revenue potential Rs 60 crore).
- Victura JV facility (80,000 sq. ft.) expected to commence operations by Q4 FY27 (peak revenue potential Rs 120 crore).
- H1 capex was Rs 36.7 crore, including Rs 26 crore for leasehold land for cables.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings reduced to Rs 40.7 crore from Rs 77.9 crore in March 2026.
- Inventory increased by ~Rs 28 crore in H1 to Rs 139 crore to support business growth.
- Receivables decreased slightly to Rs 83.1 crore.
- Working capital days expected to remain constant.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management flagged no specific material risks; geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East are viewed mid-term as a positive catalyst for process electrification.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Naman Parmar (Niveshaay Investments) asked about gross margin headwinds and fuel cell supplies.**
   - *Management reply:* Gross margins are expected to sustain near FY26 levels, and fuel cell temperature sensor dispatches have officially commenced with ongoing customer ramp-up.
2. **Abhinav asked about employee expense run-rate and absorption timeline.**
   - *Management reply:* Manpower costs will be absorbed against scaling revenues in the second half and fully absorbed next year.
3. **Pratik asked about the fuel cell business scale and margin recovery.**
   - *Management reply:* OEM business from fuel cells and emission control is expected to reach ~15% of revenue next year, and consolidated margins will normalize toward historical levels as new lines ramp up.
4. **Arvind Arora asked about capacity utilization and order book visibility.**
   - *Management reply:* Current capacity utilization is running at ~80%; traditional order books provide 3 to 4 months of visibility, while OEM contracts offer 1 to 2 years.
5. **Neel Mehta asked how automotive commercial vehicle heating differs from radiator manufacturing.**
   - *Management reply:* The offering involves specialized heating cables and sensors specifically designed for exhaust emission compliance under newer emission norms.

### One-line takeaway
Tempsens delivered a robust H1 FY27 with 33.7% revenue growth, doubled exports, and a substantially deleveraged balance sheet post-IPO, positioning itself for multi-year scaling across new OEM and industrial verticals.
