# JNK India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 revenue growth, robust order book, and maintained annual guidance despite temporary Q2 execution hurdles.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (Revenue growth: 20%-25%, EBITDA margin: 12%-14%).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive; acknowledged temporary execution headwinds (monsoon and logistics) in Q2 but reiterated confidence in H2 acceleration.
- New topics or risks: Detailed discussion on Dangote's Kenya refinery timeline and broader non-heater bid pipeline (~Rs 2,500 crore).
- Last quarter's promises: H2 execution-heavy trend reiterated; order inflows progressing as anticipated with a major Rs 500 crore export order win from JNK Global.

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 operating revenue: Rs 187.5 crore, up 3.4% YoY
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 20.8 crore, up 6.6% YoY
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin: 11.1%
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 13.3 crore (margin: 7.1%)
- H1 FY27 operating revenue: Rs 370.2 crore, up 30.4% YoY
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 39.4 crore, up 50% YoY (margin: 10.6%)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 22.9 crore (margin: 6.2%)
- Order book (as of September 30, 2026): Rs 1,662 crore
- Export order win (Nigeria): Approximately Rs 500 crore from JNK Global
- Domestic order wins (flare packages): Aggregating Rs 30 crore
- Bidding pipeline: Rs 5,000 crore (excluding recent orders)
- Deferred revenue in Q2 due to logistics/monsoon: Rs 30 crore to Rs 40 crore
- JNK Chemdist order book: Rs 20 crore

### Highlights
- Secured the largest export order in JNK India's history (approx. Rs 500 crore from JNK Global for a Nigeria project heater package, 18-month delivery).
- Secured two domestic flare package orders worth Rs 30 crore with a 16-month execution timeline.
- H2 traditionally contributes 60%-70% of annual revenue; management expects strong recovery in Q3 and Q4.
- JNK Chemdist is focused on R&D, green fuel, and green hydrogen initiatives, with commercial scale-up expected in about two years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 guidance of 20%-25% revenue growth and 12%-14% EBITDA margin on operational revenue.
- Expects Kenya refinery inquiries in 2-3 months and order finalization in 4-5 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Bidding pipeline stands at ~Rs 5,000 crore (50% domestic, 50% international; 50% combustion/heaters, 50% non-combustion/non-heaters).
- International focus includes Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, Ethiopia).
- Domestic bidding spans refining, petrochemicals, fertilizers, metals, minerals, and green energy adjacencies.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin impacted by project mix and execution delays; H1 EBITDA margin stood at 10.6%.
- Commodity price fluctuation risk for copper, nickel, and aluminum is managed via back-to-back arrangements with sub-vendors or hedging, while some PSU contracts include steel price variation clauses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major capex highlights; focus remains on execution capacity and technological expansion via JNK Chemdist.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management is confident of funding working capital through customer payment terms, internal reserves, and existing bank limits without raising additional debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical logistical challenges and severe monsoon disruptions impacted Q2 fabrication and site erection progress.
- Commodity price volatility remains a general business risk in project execution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shubham Borade (ICICI Securities) on growth levers for guidance:** Management explained that H1 historically contributes 30%-35% of revenue while H2 contributes 60%-65%, supporting the maintained 20%-25% growth guidance.
2. **Deepak Purswani (SVAN Investments) on Dangote project scope:** Management clarified that the current Rs 500 crore order is primarily for supply, with potential for additional scope (erection/commissioning) under discussion.
3. **Kamlesh Jain (Lotus Asset Managers) on deferred revenue:** Management quantified Q2 delayed dispatches due to logistics and rains at Rs 30 crore to Rs 40 crore.
4. **Rupesh Tatiya (Long Equity Partners) on BPCL Vizag refinery timeline:** Management noted tenders are likely in 6-8 months and ordering in 12 months, with potential opportunity size around Rs 5,000 crore (not yet in the Rs 5,000 crore bid pipeline).
5. **Ram Modi on bid-to-win ratio and funding:** Management stated the historical bid-to-win ratio is ~30% for heaters and estimated 10%-15% for newer non-heater segments, confirming internal accruals and bank limits are sufficient for working capital.

### One-line takeaway
JNK India reported strong H1 revenue growth of 30.4% YoY and secured a major Rs 500 crore export order, keeping its annual guidance intact despite temporary Q2 execution hurdles.
