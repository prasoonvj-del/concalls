# TIL Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted healthy YoY growth, successful integration of Tulip Compression, and a clear focus on execution and aftermarket expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 117.1 crore (+86% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 78.6 crore (+25% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 7.3 crore (vs Rs 1.1 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 6.2% (vs 1.5% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 3.4 crore (+223% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 4.3% (vs 1.5% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Negative Rs 5.5 crore.
*   **Standalone PAT:** Negative Rs 7.2 crore.
*   **Order Book:** Rs 211 crore (TIL core) + Rs 328 crore (Tulip).
*   **Order Pipeline:** Rs 373 crore (TIL core) + Rs 117 crore (Tulip).

### Highlights
*   Acquired Tulip Compression (TCPL) in May 2026; consolidated from May 8, 2026.
*   Delivered nine ReachStackers in Q1; ReachStacker market share is 38-40%.
*   TIL is now classified as an MSME company.
*   Showcased indigenous products at Excon: CarryKing 515, 85-ton truck crane, and rough terrain applications.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects a "significant jump" in turnover this year.
*   Management aims to triple turnover in 5-7 years.
*   Targeting 15-16% EBITDA margin in the medium-to-long term (standalone).
*   Tulip expected to provide 14-15% EBITDA margin in the medium-to-long term.
*   Management aims to increase stake in Tulip to 74% over the next few years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Seeing activity across ports, logistics, infrastructure, construction, and industrial sectors.
*   Defense sector is a long-cycle business aligned with "Atmanirbhar Bharat."
*   City Gas Distribution (CGD) sector is a key growth area for Tulip.
*   ReachStacker applications are expanding beyond ports to mining and steel industries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Focusing on optimizing material costs and localizing supply chains (aiming for 90%+ localization).
*   Defense sector supply chain optimization is time-lagged due to approval requirements.
*   Aftermarket revenue is expected to become 40-45% of total revenue long-term.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   No significant capex planned; only "bridging capex" of Rs 5-10 crore.
*   Kharagpur plant is currently 30-35% utilized.
*   Board approved up to Rs 50 crore equity infusion into Tulip.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Working capital cycle is currently stressed; management targeting 60-75 debtor days.
*   High receivables in FY26 attributed to a large missile crane order delivered in late March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical and logistics challenges impacted turnover last year.
*   Competition from Chinese players (pricing and credit terms).
*   Defense sector approvals delay supply chain optimization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did EBITDA margins decline QoQ (9.6% in Q4 vs 6.2% in Q1)?** A: Management explained that product mix varies significantly by quarter, and margins depend on the specific products delivered.
*   **Q: Can you challenge the dominant market share in Pick and Carry cranes?** A: Management believes their "CarryKing 515" is a unique, integrated solution (lifting + carrying) that will disrupt the market.
*   **Q: What is the synergy between TIL and Gainwell?** A: Management noted potential for TIL to provide fabrication for Gainwell and shared engineering/aftermarket expertise.
*   **Q: Why is the working capital cycle stressed?** A: Management cited a large defense order delivered in late March; they are now an MSME, which should enforce stricter payment terms.

### One-line takeaway
TIL is in the early stages of a turnaround, focusing on execution, aftermarket growth, and clean energy integration via Tulip, with a goal to triple revenue in 5-7 years.
