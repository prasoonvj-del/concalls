# Inox Wind Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in achieving its full-year guidance of 75% YoY revenue growth and 20–22% EBITDA margin, supported by a 4.4 GW order book and a strategic pivot toward equipment supply.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Consolidated):** Rs 872 crore
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 237 crore (Margin: ~27%)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 95 crore
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 64 crore
- **Cash Profit:** Rs 153 crore
- **Order Book:** 4.4 GW as of July 2026 (provides 24–36 months of execution visibility)
- **Order Mix:** ~59% equipment supply and 41% turnkey (excluding group entities)
- **INOX Green Q1 Performance:** Revenue Rs 101 crore (+17% YoY), EBITDA Rs 57 crore (+19% YoY), PAT Rs 41 crore (+86% YoY), Cash PAT Rs 55 crore (+25% YoY)
- **O&M Portfolio:** 13.3 GW peak as of June 2026 (10.5 GW wind, balance solar; includes 6.5 GW acquired operational O&M assets)

### Highlights
- Pivoted strategy to increase the share of equipment supply in the order mix to improve working capital and balance sheet robustness.
- Signed an MOU for 1.5 GW with INOX Clean Energy, with firm orders signed for 500 MW to date.
- Received a repeat LOA for 200 MW from NLC India in July 2026.
- Demerger of the power evacuation infrastructure business from INOX Green into INOX Renewable Solutions Limited (IRSL) completed on August 1, 2026 (record date).
- Honorable NCLT Ahmedabad approved the acquisition of Wind World India Limited (4.5 GW O&M portfolio, FY26 revenue ~Rs 580 crore); completion expected in Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Growth:** Maintained target of 75% YoY growth over FY26 on a consolidated basis.
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Maintained guidance of 20% to 22% on a consolidated basis.
- **Seasonality:** H2 is expected to contribute 70% to 75% of annual business performance.
- **INOX Green EBITDA:** Guided to an annualized run-rate of Rs 600 crore post-consolidation of acquisitions starting Q3/Q4 FY27.
- **INOX Clean Pipeline:** Group company INOX Clean plans to add 3 GW+ IPP capacity annually, driving recurring orders for INOX Wind, INOX Green, and IRSL.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India commissioned 1.4 GW of wind capacity in Q1 FY27, taking installed wind capacity to 57.4 GW as of June 2026.
- Annual industry wind capacity additions expected to range between 8 to 10 GW over the next few years, driven by RTC, FDRE, and hybrid tenders.
- India's installed wind capacity projected to increase 7x over the next two decades.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Currently 80% to 90% of wind turbine components are indigenized; management expects to reach nearly 100% indigenization before the end of the calendar year for both 3X and 4X models.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **4X WTG Model:** Prototype installation scheduled for August 2026, with commercial launch expected by end-FY26.
- **IRSL Manufacturing Expansion:** Upgrading Jaipur transformer factory to manufacture 4.9 MVA transformers for the 4X series; planning capacity for 8–20 MVA and 100 MVA+ transformers.
- **Power Electronics:** Expanding into inverters, unit substations (commercial launch in FY27), and energy capacitor systems.
- **Equipment Assets:** Currently operating a fleet of 4 cranes, with additional cranes being added in FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Shifting execution focus toward equipment supply to shorten working capital cycles and lower trade receivables.
- Management expects meaningful reductions in receivable days and working capital metrics starting Q2/Q3 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution risks inherent in turnkey projects, including Right of Way (ROW) issues, customer site readiness, and adverse weather conditions.
- Macroeconomic force majeure events (e.g., Middle East geopolitical tensions).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why raise funds/dilute equity in INOX Green when it generates healthy cash flow?**  
  *Management:* Stated that these were enabling resolutions and declined further comment as they were in a silent period.
- **Q: What was the Q1 execution volume in MW?**  
  *Management:* Clarified that they no longer disclose MW volume execution and instead guide on consolidated annual revenue and margins.
- **Q: How will the company achieve the implied ask-rate of ~Rs 6,500 crore revenue in Q2–Q4 FY27?**  
  *Management:* Explained that equipment supply allows faster client allocation and turnover without being blocked by site-readiness delays, reiterating that revenues are heavily H2-skewed.
- **Q: What is the breakdown of INOX Green’s Rs 57.9 crore other income?**  
  *Management:* Over Rs 50 crore is operational income (from acquired operational assets and value-added services like overhauls/life extension), with the balance being treasury income.
- **Q: How are transactions priced between INOX Wind and group entity INOX Clean?**  
  *Management:* Confirmed all inter-group transactions are conducted strictly on an arm's-length basis with no preferential pricing.

### One-line takeaway
Inox Wind is leveraging strong group synergies and a shift toward equipment supply to drive full-year 75% YoY revenue growth and maintain 20-22% EBITDA margins.
