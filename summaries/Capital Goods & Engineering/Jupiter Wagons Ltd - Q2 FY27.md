# Jupiter Wagons Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by sequential financial recovery, a strong revival in order bookings (total order book at ~INR 5,850 crore), and strategic expansions into passenger rail and energy storage.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: FY30 revenue target of INR 8,000-10,000 crore reiterated (clarified timeline is FY30); BESS order book target maintained at ~INR 1,000 crore by end of FY27; JEM expected to turn EBITDA positive in Q3 FY27 and profitable by FY28.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from transition-cautious in Q1 to highly confident, projecting a sharp rise in execution to 600-800 wagons per month.
- New topics or risks: Signed MoU with Poland's PESA for passenger rail; secured 100 MW BESS project in West Bengal (INR 400 crore); clarified defense business is a standalone holding structure unrelated to Jupiter Wagons.
- Last quarter's promises: Lucchini RS and SIMEST equity infusion (~INR 290 crore) for 25% stake in JTRWF confirmed; axle line erection underway for mid-next year commissioning.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: INR 772 crore (vs INR 671 crore in Q1 FY27)
- EBITDA: INR 94 crore
- EBITDA margin: 12%
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 46 crore
- Overall order book: ~INR 5,850 crore
- Wagon order book: ~INR 3,600 crore (~10,000 wagons)
- Ministry of Railways order: INR 1,140 crore (2,583 BOXNHL and BOXNS wagons)
- Private sector orders: INR 450 crore (GATX India, Chetak International, Balmer Lawrie for ACT2 automotive carriers and BFNS 22.9-tonne payload wagons)
- BESS order book: >500 megawatts valued at INR 500 crore+
- JTRWF financing: Total equity infusion ~INR 900 crore; total bank limit INR 1,600 crore (INR 1,100 crore utilized); Lucchini and SIMEST invested ~INR 290 crore for 25% stake.
- West Bengal BESS project: 100 MW standalone, ~INR 400 crore value under 15-year build-own-operate model.

### Highlights
- Secured INR 1,140 crore order from Ministry of Railways for 2,583 wagons and INR 450 crore from private customers.
- Signed an MoU in October with PESA (Poland's largest train and metro manufacturer) for passenger rail vehicle development and manufacturing.
- Received formal LOA for 100 MW standalone BESS project in West Bengal from WBSEDCL (~INR 400 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY30 revenue target of INR 8,000-10,000 crore to be met.
- Wagon execution expected to ramp up to 600-800 wagons per month in coming quarters.
- FY28 expected to see a return to historical EBITDA margins of 14%-15% as new order books and the wheel line commence production.
- JEM expected to turn EBITDA positive in Q3 FY27 and profitable by FY28.
- Stone India brake production expected to reach 700-800 systems/month by the beginning of next year; DAKO JV revenues to accrue from Q1 next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand picking up across government and private segments; tender for 5,800 BOBRN wagons strengthens industrial opportunity.
- High-speed and semi-high-speed rail segments currently rely entirely on imported wheelsets; domestic production via JTRWF offers significant import substitution and export potential.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins improved sequentially to 12% in Q2 FY27.
- Strategic focus shifting towards higher-margin high-speed rail components and specialized freight.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Schuler team has commenced erecting the axle line at the Odisha facility, with commissioning slated for the middle of next year (FY28); wheel line commissioning expected by the end of next financial year.
- Planning a new manufacturing facility for passenger coaches (capex and timeline details to be announced shortly).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt limit for JTRWF stands at INR 1,600 crore, with INR 1,100 crore utilized. Total equity infusion at INR 900 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global restrictions on the transfer of battery cell technology (though management noted they identified the right partner).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sahil Patani (Strokes Capital) on axle facility and margins:** Asked if the axle facility is on track for Q4/mid-next year and when EBITDA margins return to 14%-15%. Management confirmed the axle line is on track for mid-next year and margins will recover toward historical levels in FY28.
- **Koundinya Nimmagadda (Jefferies) on execution and passenger coach capex:** Asked about run-rates and passenger facility capex. Management guided to 600-800 wagons/month and stated passenger plant capex figures will be announced shortly.
- **Mohit Yadav (Investor) on BESS targets and defense:** Asked about BESS market share and Purulia defense land. Management stated BESS target is INR 1,000 crore by FY27 and clarified the defense business is a separate holding structure, not part of Jupiter Wagons.
- **Atharva Kolvankar (Spangle Advisors) on wheelset scaling and battery cells:** Asked about execution challenges and battery cell risks. Management expressed full confidence due to Lucchini's technology partnership and stated they are finalizing a technology transfer partner for high-voltage cells.

### One-line takeaway
Jupiter Wagons posted a sequential financial and operational recovery in Q2 FY27 with revenue of INR 772 crore and a 12% EBITDA margin, supported by a growing order book of INR 5,850 crore and strategic expansions into passenger rail and energy storage.
