# Jupiter Wagons Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by sequential revenue recovery, major order wins in both government and private wagon sectors, expanding BESS order book, and strategic partnerships entering the passenger rail segment.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY30 revenue guidance of Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 10,000 crore (clarified timeline vs analyst perception of FY29).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from cautious wagon transition/ prototype approval phase to strong demand revival and accelerating order inflows across government and private sectors.
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter:** MoU with Poland's PESA in October 2026 for passenger rail mobility (metros, semi-high speed, high-speed rail); upcoming technology transfer for in-house high-voltage/capacity battery cell manufacturing targeting FY29.
- **Last quarter's promises and delivery:** Lucchini RS and SIMEST stake allotment (25% for ~Rs 290 crore) in JTRWF completed; passenger systems tie-up announced as promised; wagon monthly production preparing to scale back to peak levels of 600-800 wagons/month.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 772 crore (up sequentially from Rs 671 crore in Q1 FY27).
- EBITDA: Rs 94 crore.
- EBITDA margin: 12%.
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 46 crore.
- Total order book: ~Rs 5,850 crore (Wagon order book: ~Rs 3,600 crore / ~10,000 wagons).
- Recent order wins: Rs 1,140 crore from Ministry of Railways (2,583 BOXNHL and BOXNS wagons); Rs 450 crore from private sector (GATX India, Chetak International, Balmer Lawrie).
- Energy storage order book: Exceeds 500 MW valued at Rs 500+ crore (secured 100 MW standalone BESS project in West Bengal for ~Rs 400 crore).

### Highlights
- Signed an MoU in October 2026 with Poland’s PESA to enter the passenger rail vehicle segment (intercity, metro, semi-high speed, and high-speed rail).
- Finalized allotment of a combined 25% stake in Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory Limited (JTRWF) to Lucchini RS and SIMEST for ~Rs 290 crore.
- Stone India freight brake facility is fully operational, targeting 700–800 brake systems per month by early next year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Target BESS order book of ~Rs 1,000 crore by the end of FY27.
- Jupiter Electric Mobility (JEM) expected to turn EBITDA positive in Q3 FY27 and profitable by FY28.
- Monthly wagon execution targeted to scale back to peak levels of 600–800 wagons per month over the coming quarters.
- Re-affirmed long-term target of reaching Rs 8,000 crore–Rs 10,000 crore revenue by FY30.
- EBITDA margins expected to return toward historical levels (14%–15%) in FY28 as wheelset production and larger order execution scale.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Wagon demand is accelerating across both Indian Railways and private sector customers (e.g., automotive carrier wagons ACT2 and BFNS wagons).
- Semi-high-speed and high-speed passenger train wheelsets currently face 100% import dependence in India, creating a strong import substitution opportunity for JTRWF.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins in Q2 stood at 12%, with management focusing selectively on higher-margin BESS and specialized rail orders.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total wheel manufacturing project capex: Rs 2,500 crore (Equity: Rs 900 crore fully infused; Bank debt limit: Rs 1,600 crore, of which Rs 1,100 crore utilized).
- Axle manufacturing line (Schuler equipment) commissioning expected by mid-FY28 (middle of next year); wheel manufacturing line targeted for completion by end of FY28.
- Setting up a new dedicated manufacturing facility for the passenger rail segment via majority-owned JVs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Funded JTRWF project through Rs 900 crore equity and Rs 1,100 crore drawn out of Rs 1,600 crore sanctioned debt facilities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Transition and ramp-up timeline delays in technology transfer agreements for localized battery cell manufacturing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Axle line timeline and operational tracking:** *Sahil Patani (Strokes Capital)* asked if the Q4 2027 axle facility is on track. Management confirmed Schuler is erecting the axle line, targeting commissioning by mid-next year.
- **Margin recovery trajectory:** *Sahil Patani* asked when EBITDA margins will return to 14%-15%. Management answered that margin expansion will pick up over upcoming quarters, returning to FY26 levels soon, with strong EBITDA margin recovery expected in FY28 supported by the wheel line.
- **Wagon execution drop:** *Koundinya Nimmagadda (Jefferies)* questioned why execution was ~400 wagons/month despite a strong starting order book. Management explained they were previously circumspect due to order book uncertainty, but with renewed order momentum, execution will rise sharply to 600-800 wagons/month.
- **Passenger rail pre-qualification criteria:** *Koundinya Nimmagadda* asked how JWL will qualify for passenger coach tenders. Management explained they will bid through JVs where JWL holds a majority stake, utilizing technology transfer and references from foreign partners like PESA.
- **Defense business involvement:** *Mohit Yadav* asked about Purulia land and defense business plans. Management clarified that the defense business is outside Jupiter Wagons under a separate holding structure with zero JWL capital investment.

### One-line takeaway
Jupiter Wagons demonstrated operational recovery with a Rs 5,850 crore order book, preparing for high-margin growth in FY28 driven by axle/wheelset commissioning and expansion into passenger rail and energy storage.
