# Tega Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 performance with record legacy revenue, successful full-quarter consolidation of Molycop, expanding margins, and initiation of cross-selling synergies.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Equipment business revenue growth guidance was reduced from 25% to 15% due to customer project delays. Molycop volume growth guidance maintained at 5%.
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains positive, with acquisition transaction costs tapering and clear progress on cross-selling (first trial order of 500 tonnes secured).
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Detailed discussion on accounting presentation of non-controlling interest (Apollo JV liability of Rs 710 crore) and land stamp duty in Australia. Old supply chain/freight container risks were absent.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Chile plant on track for January 2027 soft commissioning and March 2027 commercial production (reconfirmed). One-time transaction costs tapered as promised.

### Highlights
- H1 FY27 marked the first full quarter of Molycop consolidation (effective June 1, 2026).
- Tega legacy consumables achieved highest-ever first-half revenue, driven by a 20% growth in legacy revenue.
- Achieved early cross-selling success: secured a trial supply of 500 tonnes of Molycop grinding media to an existing Tega customer, pointing to potential annualized supplies of over 30,000 tonnes.
- Tega prepaid Rs 1,000 million of term loans from preferential issue proceeds in October 2026.
- One-time transaction costs have meaningfully tapered from Q1 to Q2, with management stating acquisition-related one-off costs are largely behind.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the long-term Molycop volume growth guidance at ~5%.
- Equipment business revenue growth guidance revised downward from 25% to 15% due to customer-led project pushouts.
- Consolidated EBITDA margins expected to trend upward from the current 14% level, targeting 17%-18% over the medium term.
- Deleveraging via internal accruals and non-core asset monetization remains the top financial priority over the next 12-18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Copper prices trading strong around $14,500 per metric ton (historical highs over $14,800), driven by electrification, grid modernization, renewable energy, and AI infrastructure.
- Gold prices range-bound between $4,000–$4,100 per ounce, supported by bullion prices, central bank buying, and safe-haven demand.
- Declining ore grades and constrained mine supply support elevated production and higher comminution consumable demand.
- Freeport Indonesia's Grasberg Block Cave milling throughput averaged ~67% of normalized run rates during Q2, expecting ~80% by mid-2027 and near full capacity by end of calendar year 2027.
- Cobre Panamá processing authorized stockpiled ore (sufficient until mid-2027), while the Interministerial Commission recommended assessing a new agreement for orderly, self-financed closure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Legacy gross margins remained healthy at 60% in Q2 (61% for H1), aided by favorable product mix and pricing discipline.
- CRUspi steel index averaged 216 in Q2 FY27 (up 9% YoY); AMM HMS Scrap Index averaged $387 (up 13% YoY). Index movements impact top line with a contractual lag.
- Molycop EBITDA margins stood at 14% (or ~$155 per ton).
- Tax expense elevated in H1 due to regional disallowances; expected to normalize going forward.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Chile plant construction remains on track for soft commissioning in January 2027 and commercial production by March 2027. The new capacity is intended for sustenance over the next 4-5 years rather than immediate incremental revenue guidance.
- Molycop Q2 capital expenditures stood at $5 million (trailing 12-month capex at $28.9 million).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total consolidated assets expanded to Rs 227,066 million as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 43,145 million in March 2026), with Molycop adding Rs 204,042 million of assets and Rs 167,895 million of liabilities.
- Provisional purchase price allocation (PPA) recognized identifiable intangibles of Rs 34,718 million and goodwill of Rs 50,571 million.
- Trade receivables for Tega legacy down 15% since March 2026 due to improved collection efforts.
- Credit rating: CRISIL A+ with stable outlook (long-term) and CRISIL A1+ (short-term).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Equipment business revenue recovery delayed due to customer approval and clearance timelines for shipping project-intensive orders.
- Elevated finance costs (consolidated finance cost at Rs 4,086 million for H1, including preference share dividends under IndAS) pending debt reduction via asset monetization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Tushar Khurana (Peace Wealth) asked about Molycop volume growth and finance cost outlook.** Management clarified Molycop 4-month volume growth was 2.2% (flat for the 3-month quarter YoY) and stated finance costs will moderate as non-core asset monetization and debt repayment progress over 12-18 months. *(Direct answer)*
- **Chirag Muchhala (Centrum Broking) asked about the contingent liabilities status for Molycop mines and equipment business underperformance.** Management stated Cobre Panamá and Grasberg are not yet triggering contingent earn-outs due to lack of full permits/ramp-up, and equipment delays stem from customer delivery approvals rather than order book issues. *(Direct answer)*
- **Varun Jain asked about remaining acquisition expenses and the Apollo minority interest derivative liability (Rs 710 crore).** Management explained acquisition expenses are nearly complete (remaining around $6-7M stamp duty paid in Q2) and clarified the Rs 710 crore liability is a non-cash IndAS reclassification of non-controlling interest with no immediate cash outflow. *(Direct answer)*
- **Raman KV asked about medium-term margin trajectories and tax rate normalization.** Management guided that consolidated margins will trend up from 14% toward 17-18% over time via operational synergies (GCC, lower cost HQ, SG&A rationalization), and high tax rates will taper off. *(Direct answer)*
- **Sagar Shetty asked about cross-selling synergy updates and margin expansion drivers.** Management highlighted the initial 500-tonne trial order and reiterated four cost-optimization drivers (GCC, HQ relocation, SG&A reduction, and yield efficiencies) targeting 100 bps margin expansion. *(Direct answer)*
- **Varinder Bansal asked about the debt sustainability and capital raising plans.** Management confirmed no further equity/capital raising is needed, and debt will be serviced through operational cash flows and non-core asset sales. *(Direct answer)*

### One-line takeaway
Tega Industries delivered a record H1 FY27 supported by strong legacy consumable growth and the initial consolidation of Molycop, while prioritizing operational integration and debt reduction.
