# Dee Development Engineers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by solid margin expansion, strong debt reduction from preferential issue proceeds, high order book visibility, and maintained full-year guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 guidance of Rs 1,500 crore revenue and >19% EBITDA margin. Reiterated FY28 revenue guidance at Rs 1,800 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains strong on power and export orders; however, management shifted immediate conversion focus to private players (JSW, Adani) due to slow tender/finalization timelines at BHEL and L&T.
- **New topics or risks:** Signed a framework agreement on 16th October 2026 for domestic nuclear projects; talks for a global nuclear piping JV are at an advanced stage (expected closure by end-FY27); dedicated nuclear piping plant under construction at Anjar.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The Rs 20-25 crore dispatch spillover from Q1 due to Middle East logistics was fully executed in Q2. Preferential proceeds of Rs 225 crore were fully utilized to repay debt.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 293 crore (+8.5% YoY).
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 58.9 crore (+33.7% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.1% (up 380 bps YoY from 16.3%).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 23.5 crore (+31.8% YoY); margin at 8%.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 587.4 crore (+19.0% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 108.6 crore (+35.9% YoY); margin at 18.5%.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 39.5 crore (+27.8% YoY).
- **Order Book:** Rs 2,531 crore as of September 30, 2026 (+94% YoY); stood at Rs 2,586 crore as of October 31, 2026.
- **Order Inflow:** Rs 1,179 crore in H1 FY27 (+97% YoY), representing ~60% of FY27 inflow guidance.
- **Finance Cost:** Decreased sequentially from Rs 17.2 crore in Q1 FY27 to Rs 15.3 crore in Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
- High-margin job work (where clients supply raw material) formed 45% of H1 FY27 execution, holding back reported top-line, but with-material execution will increase to >70% in H2 FY27.
- Won a Rs 46 crore order in October 2026 from a US-based EPC player for modules and off-skid piping for delivery by July 2027.
- Non-core segment delivered near-full quarter operations at the 72,000 MTPA Muktsar biomass pellet plant; non-core full-year revenue target maintained at ~Rs 80 crore supported by the Malwa Power tariff of Rs 5.44 per kWh (under appeal at APTEL).
- Our record notes Q2 FY27 net profit at Rs 23 crore (+32% YoY) and October 31 order book at Rs 2,586.43 crore, which aligns with management disclosures.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Target:** Confident of meeting guidance of Rs 1,500 crore (requires ~Rs 900 crore gross execution in H2 FY27; net execution target ~Rs 750 crore after adjusting WIP/RFP of Rs 100-125 crore).
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin Target:** Reiterated at >19%.
- **FY27 Order Inflow Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 2,000 crore.
- **FY28 Revenue Target:** Reaffirmed at Rs 1,800 crore.
- **Nuclear Segment Guidance:** Expects Rs 50-60 crore initial revenue in FY28, scaling up to ~Rs 200+ crore in FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic capex momentum across thermal power, refining, and process industries remains strong; power sector orders represent over 60% of the piping order book (Rs 1,476 crore).
- Export market momentum remains strong with exports expected to stay above 50-60% of revenues, supported by heavy traction in gas turbine (GT) piping from GE, Siemens, and HRSG work from Nooter Eriksen and John Cockerill.
- Advanced discussions underway for large prospective orders with private power developers like JSW and Adani.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed costs are largely stable; management expects strong operating leverage in H2 FY27 as top-line scales up.
- Captive supply of seamless pipes from the Anjar plant generates 10-15% cost savings on pipe procurement inputs.
- Job work contracts carry 5-10% higher EBITDA margins than with-material contracts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Anjar 7,000-tonne seamless pipe plant is now fully operational; expected to generate ~Rs 300 crore annual revenue at full capacity in FY28 (50% internal consumption, 50% external sales).
- Dedicated nuclear piping plant is currently under construction at Anjar, Gujarat, with machinery orders placed and receiving underway.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Repaid Rs 225 crore of debt out of net proceeds of ~Rs 293 crore raised via preferential issue.
- Inventory days reduced to 188 days in H1 FY27 (down from 223 days in H1 FY26); full-year working capital days expected at 225-240 days (targeting ~200 days medium term).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Official purchase orders from GE for HRSG business remain delayed (only LOI received); execution pushed to FY28.
- Order finalization and tendering processes at BHEL and L&T remain extremely slow.
- Conversion timelines in the nuclear segment are long due to slow regulatory and testing procedures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankit Soni (Mirae Asset):** *Asked how achievable the ~Rs 900 crore H2 revenue required for FY27 guidance is.*  
  **Management:** Stated net execution required is ~Rs 750 crore after adjusting Rs 100-125 crore WIP/RFP, expressing 100% confidence in reaching the Rs 1,500 crore target due to ramping shop floor dispatches.
- **Ankit Gupta (BAMPL):** *Inquired why order inflows from BHEL and L&T are lagging despite large sector awards.*  
  **Management:** Explained BHEL processes are slow and L&T price finalization is tough; company is redirecting focus toward private players like JSW and Adani for large orders expected by Dec 2026–Jan 2027.
- **Chandresh Malpani:** *Asked about the delay in GE HRSG order execution.*  
  **Management:** Clarified that GE PO is pending (LOI held), but capacity is fully occupied with HRSG orders for Nooter Eriksen, John Cockerill, and Siemens, making the GE delay non-impactful for revenue targets.
- **Prateek Srivastava:** *Asked why job work was high at 45% in H1 and what H2 mix will look like.*  
  **Management:** Noted that priority completion of legacy BHEL/L&T/Dow orders drove H1 job work share; H2 mix will shift heavily toward with-material orders (>70%).

### One-line takeaway
DEE Development Engineers delivered a solid Q2 FY27 with 33.7% EBITDA growth and significant debt reduction, keeping it firmly on track to hit its full-year Rs 1,500 crore revenue and >19% EBITDA margin targets.
