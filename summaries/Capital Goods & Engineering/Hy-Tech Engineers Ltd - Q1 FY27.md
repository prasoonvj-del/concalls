# Hy-Tech Engineers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-25 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust demand, strong execution, and margin expansion expected ahead despite a temporary cost lag.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 43 crore (INR 430 million), up 13% YoY (from Rs 38 crore / INR 380 million in Q1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 8.4 crore (INR 84 million), compared to Rs 8.2 crore (INR 82 million) in Q1 FY26.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 19.6%, down from 21.5% in Q1 FY26.
- **PBT:** Rs 6.1 crore (INR 61 million), up 9% YoY.
- **PAT:** Rs 4.6 crore (INR 46 million), up 11% YoY (from Rs 4.1 crore / INR 41 million in Q1 FY26).
- **PAT Margin:** 10.7%.
- **IPO Proceeds:** Rs 135.7 crore raised total (including Rs 60 crore fresh issue).

### Highlights
- Successfully listed on NSE and BSE on September 1, 2026.
- End-to-end integrated manufacturing (forging to plating) with a portfolio of 11,000 SKUs (80% standard, 20% OEM-specific).
- Secured IRIS railway certification, DRDO defense registration, and registrations with Cochin Shipyard and Mazagon Dock.
- Board approved acquiring 6.5 acres of land for future expansion and setting up a 100% subsidiary and warehouse in the US.
- Participating in bauma led to new OEM customer additions including Tata Hitachi, Sany, and Fluidconnecto.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a minimum 20% YoY revenue growth for FY27.
- Management expects PAT to grow by 25% to 30% in absolute terms.
- EBITDA margin targeted at 24% to 25%, scaling up by 0.5% to 1% annually over the next 2-3 years.
- Long-term 5-year plan targets a 50:50 export-to-domestic revenue mix (currently ~30% export).
- Capex of Rs 30 crore from IPO proceeds expected to add ~Rs 100 crore to business capacity over the next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global market size for fittings is ~Rs 40,000 crore (USA: Rs 20,000 crore, Europe: Rs 10,000 crore, India: Rs 2,000 crore, Other: Rs 8,000 crore).
- Domestic market growing at 7%-8%, but Hy-Tech expects to grow at ~20% via higher share of business (SOB) and new OEMs.
- Chinese equipment makers in India (e.g., Sany) are seeking local import substitutes due to indigenization mandates.
- Exploring data center and oil & gas opportunities for stainless steel fittings by FY28-FY29.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 EBITDA margins were impacted by rising input costs due to geopolitical issues in Feb-Apr; price revisions were agreed with OEMs effective April 1 but will reflect in Q2 earnings.
- Raw material is procured directly from mills domestically; no material is imported.
- Avoids brass fittings due to copper volatility and pilferage risks.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current manufacturing output is 3.5 million fittings/month; IPO proceeds will double capacity to 7 million fittings/month over 3 years.
- Existing plant infrastructure operates at 60%-70% utilization, maintaining 20%-25% excess capacity for new customer onboarding.
- Procured rotary transfer machines (churning out 70,000 pieces/month each with 1 operator); 4 more planned.
- Board approved 6.5 acres of additional land; plant expected to be ready in 2 years with higher automation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Fresh issue proceeds of Rs 60 crore from IPO utilized for capex, debt repayment, and general corporate purposes.
- Continues to reinvest ~50% of profits into capex annually.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical risks: Management intentionally caps US business exposure at ~25% to mitigate dependency on shifting trade/political relations (e.g., under Trump).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Srinivasareddy (Yellow Investments) on Q1 margin dip and FY27 outlook:** Management explained that input cost hikes in Q1 were offset by price revisions effective April 1, which will flow into Q2, maintaining EBITDA margins at 24%-25% for the year.
- **Rahil Dasani on US and export strategy:** Management reiterated that the US accounts for ~25% of sales, and while they are setting up a US subsidiary/warehouse for John Deere and others, they will diversify remaining exports across Europe, Australia, New Zealand, and Dubai to avoid single-country dependency.
- **Rajiv Mehta on revenue visibility from capex:** Management stated that the Rs 30 crore IPO capex will add ~Rs 100 crore to revenue, and continuous capex supports a 25%-30% growth trajectory for the next 10 years.
- **Arvind Arora on data center opportunities:** Management confirmed they have developed stainless steel fittings for data centers (and oil & gas) over the past two years, with commercial traction expected by FY28-FY29.
- **Subhanu Bangal on fixed assets and capacity:** Management clarified that forging and fitting capacities will expand by 1.5x in line with production targets, and PPE levels reflect depreciation and government subsidies in D-zones.
- **Paras Chheda on 5-year targets:** Management targeted reaching ~Rs 500 crore in revenue over 5 years based on a 20%-25% CAGR.

### One-line takeaway
Hy-Tech Engineers reported a steady Q1 FY27 with 13% revenue growth and 11% PAT growth, backed by strong import-substitution demand, upcoming capacity doubling via IPO funds, and margin recovery anticipated in Q2 following OEM price revisions.
