# Inox Wind Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Management withdrew specific FY27 revenue growth guidance amid strategic realignment, margin pressure from Middle East war commodity inflation, and CEO resignation, though sequential revenue grew 58%.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Cut/Withdrawn. Management withdrew specific FY27 revenue growth (earlier 75% YoY) and EBITDA margin (earlier 20–22%) targets; introduced an FY28 revenue guidance of ~Rs 7,500 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone shifted from aggressive full-year guidance to prioritizing quality of business, balance sheet discipline, and free cash flows over revenue quantum. Demand outlook for wind remains structurally strong (8–10 GW annual industry additions).
- **New topics or risks:** CEO Sanjeev Agarwal resigned (continuing as advisor through December 2026); Middle East/West Asia war created raw material and commodity price inflation; temporary margin compression due to Wind World O&M revenue not being recognized in the Q2 P&L.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Prototype 4X WTG installation (promised for August 2026) is complete; commissioning is expected shortly with commercial launch targeted by end-FY27. Payment for Wind World India’s 4.5 GW portfolio was completed during Q2 as planned.

---

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,378 crore (+58% QoQ)
- **Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 238 crore (flat QoQ)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 103 crore (+8% QoQ)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 69 crore (+8% QoQ)
- **Cash Profit:** Rs 165 crore (+8% QoQ)
- **Q2 Order Inflows:** ~350 MW; H1 FY27 inflows: ~1.85 GW
- **Order Book:** ~3.8 GW as of October 2026 (~61% equipment supply, 39% turnkey in 3rd-party order book)
- **Inox Green Revenue:** Rs 113 crore (+12% QoQ)
- **Inox Green EBITDA:** Rs 62.5 crore (+10% QoQ)
- **Inox Green PAT:** Rs 44 crore (+8% QoQ)
- **Inox Green Capital Raise:** Raised Rs 300 crore via QIP in October 2026
- **Inox Green O&M Portfolio:** ~12.5 GWp as of September 2026 (~10.4 GW wind, 2.1 GWp solar)

---

### Highlights
- CEO Sanjeev Agarwal resigned due to personal reasons; Mr. Saibaba Vutukuri joined as Managing Director, Wind Business.
- Strategic decision to pivot to equipment supply (target 75% of order book) resulted in order book rationalization/discontinuance to ~3.8 GW.
- Completed full payment for Wind World India’s 4.5 GW O&M portfolio via QIP proceeds; line-by-line P&L consolidation starts Q3 FY27.
- Management noted consolidated Q2 EBITDA margin would have been ~20% if Wind World O&M earnings were recognized in P&L instead of being routed through the balance sheet.
- Inox Renewable Solutions Limited (IRSL) filed its Information Memorandum with stock exchanges for independent listing following the power evacuation infrastructure demerger.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Guidance:** Refrained from giving specific FY27 revenue guidance.
- **FY28 Revenue Target:** Guided to ~Rs 7,500 crore (to be reviewed/reaffirmed toward end-Q4 FY27).
- **EBITDA Margin:** Medium-term target maintained at ~20%.
- **Receivables:** Target to reduce trade debtor days to below 150 days by end-FY28.
- **Inox Green:** Wind World EBITDA margins targeted to increase from 25–30% to upwards of 40–45% within 3–4 quarters.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- India installed 1.8 GW wind capacity in Q2 FY27, bringing H1 FY27 additions to 3.2 GW (compared to 6.05 GW in FY26).
- MNRE projects 7.5–8.0 GW wind additions in FY27; industry expected to enter an 8–10 GW annual addition phase in the near-to-medium term.
- Standalone wind tenders formed 36% (2.1 GW) of total RE capacity awarded in Q2 FY27 (5.8 GW).
- National power demand increased 15% YoY in Q2 FY27.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Temporary margin compression in Q2 driven by raw material and commodity inflation triggered by the West Asia war.
- Dynamic pricing implemented on fresh contracts and incremental bids to pass through commodity cost fluctuations.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 4.X platform prototype installed; commercial launch targeted by end-FY27.
- Rohika (Gujarat) blade manufacturing facility fully prepared for the 4.X platform.
- IRSL expanding into 8–20 MVA and larger transformers, inverters, unit substations, and electronic control systems.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables remained broadly stable vs March 2026 levels despite H1 revenue growth.
- Balance sheet has minuscule net debt (0.1–0.2 D/E ratio).
- H1 working capital elevated due to seasonal inventory build-up for H2 (which historically contributes ~70% of annual revenue).

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Middle East/West Asia geopolitical conflicts impacting commodity costs and global supply chains.
- Cash-flow lockup and delays in third-party turnkey EPC projects.
- Temporary disruptions associated with senior management transitions.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Are we abandoning the earlier guidance of 75% YoY revenue growth and 20–22% EBITDA margin for FY27?**  
  *Management:* Confirmed they will not meet those guided FY27 numbers as they chose to cancel poor-quality orders and forgo revenue to protect balance sheet quality and working capital.
- **Q: What is the expected top-line impact of Wind World consolidation on Inox Green in Q3?**  
  *Management:* Stated that with completion in mid-October, Q3 will see ~Rs 120 crore of additional Wind World revenue, effectively doubling Inox Green's quarterly revenue.
- **Q: Why did management shift revenue expectations despite reiterating in Q1 that H2 delivers 70% of sales?**  
  *Management:* Explained that executing bad turnkey sites delays cash collection. Choosing balance sheet strength and debt-free status over bloated receivables caused the recalibration.
- **Q: How will Inox Green achieve 45%+ EBITDA margins in Wind World?**  
  *Management:* Through rationalizing excessive employee costs, eliminating administrative overheads, and scaling value-added services (life extensions, overhauling, repowering).
- **Q: Are you witnessing pricing pressure in the wind market from new entrants like Adani Wind?**  
  *Management:* Stated no pricing pressure is visible as supply is constrained relative to massive demand, and wind tariffs remain very competitive compared to solar with battery storage.

---

### One-line takeaway
Inox Wind withdrew its FY27 revenue growth guidance to shift toward high-quality equipment orders, while Inox Green's Wind World consolidation doubles its operational revenue scale from Q3 FY27.
