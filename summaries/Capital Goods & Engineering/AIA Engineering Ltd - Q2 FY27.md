# AIA Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Volumes remain flat and margins were impacted by steep freight cost increases and geopolitical uncertainties, though management anticipates returning to growth in FY28 and FY29.

### Key numbers
- **Sales volume (Q2):** 61,500 metric tonnes (production: 63,600 metric tonnes)
- **Sales volume (H1):** 126,000 metric tonnes
- **Top line / Revenue (Q2):** INR 1,093 crore
- **Other operating income (Q2):** INR 19.5 crore
- **Top line / Revenue (H1):** INR 2,246 crore
- **Other operating income (H1):** INR 34 crore
- **Other non-operating income (Q2):** INR 71.7 crore (down from INR 98 crore YoY)
- **Other non-operating income (H1):** INR 182 crore (down from INR 207 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2):** INR 361 crore
- **EBITDA (H1):** INR 786 crore
- **PAT (Q2):** INR 249 crore
- **PAT (H1):** INR ~550 crore
- **Export freight cost increase:** Grown by over 3.5% in Q2 and H1 YoY (reaching close to 10% of sales in Q2).
- **Realization per kg increase:** Increased by about INR 178 (largely attributable to freight pass-through).
- **Net working capital cycle:** ~129 days (comprising 41 days raw material stock, 79 days WIP and finished goods, and 87 days receivables).
- **Capex incurred (H1):** INR 86 crore
- **Capex guidance (H2):** Additional ~INR 250 crore (largely due to corporate house land completion transaction, normal maintenance, and incremental capex of INR 50 crore to INR 75 crore).
- **Mining sales (Q2):** 39,700 tonnes (H1: ~79,000 tonnes)

### Highlights
- Total mining sales showed a slight increase on a YoY basis for H1 and Q2.
- Export benefits were reported as very high/highest ever linked to dispatches, though representing shipments from India rather than end-customer billings.
- Order backlog stood at INR 10.7 billion, supported by large orders received in the last month of Q2.
- Expansion projects in Ghana and China are on hold for at least one more quarter, though management will reconsider reactivating geographical expansion by the end of next quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is working internally on a volume growth plan of 25,000 to 30,000 tonnes each year for FY28 and FY29 (not formally provided as a rigid volume guidance yet).
- Second half of the current fiscal is expected to bring some possible upside as NGDS plus liner-based successes start becoming visible.
- Mexico tariff impact is considered to have reached its absolute bottom.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Mining industry demand, especially copper in South America and gold, remains exciting and stable.
- Non-mining (cement/thermal) volumes are not predictable, not scalable beyond a point, and not needle-moving, though cement/sigma demand remains normal.
- Mexico implemented a 50% duty tariff on imports from various countries as part of NAFTA obligations, affecting under 7,000 to 8,000 tonnes max per year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA declined by 2.5% to 3% (reported EBITDA declined by ~5%) due to a steep increase in export freight costs.
- Export freight reached ~10% of sales in Q2 (normal range expected at 7% to 8%), driven by a combination of high sea freight and air freight components.
- Gross margins remained generally comparable directionally.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex was INR 86 crore.
- H2 capex projected at ~INR 250 crore (corporate land transaction + INR 50-75 crore normal/incremental capex).
- Ghana and China capacity expansions remain on hold for another quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital cycle maintained at 129 days (41 days raw material, 79 days WIP/finished goods, 87 days receivables).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High and volatile global shipping freight costs and logistical uncertainties (Red Sea, Hormuz, etc.) creating friction points for conversion trials and delivery lead times.
- Geopolitical trade actions and import duties (e.g., Mexico's 50% tariff).
- Elongated trial timelines at certain mining sites.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital) on volume drivers and Mexico impact:** Management clarified that Mexico volume impact was small (under 7,000-8,000 tonnes max) due to a 50% duty, which they consider the absolute bottom. *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Ankur Periwal (Axis Capital) on 25,000-30,000 ton growth composition:** Management stated that NGDS acts as a game-changing structural shift and potential top-up, but deferred detailed color on the exact growth split to future quarters. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Umesh Raut (Nomura) on freight cost impact and logistics:** Management explained that freight hit ~10% of sales due to abnormal shipping dynamics and air freight, acting as a geopolitical friction point rather than just a cost issue. *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Bajrang (Sunidhi Securities) on currency competitiveness and U.S./global normalization:** Management noted they are agnostic to US politics and do not compete directly with China, focusing instead on cross-currency stability and resolution of Middle East shipping routes. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital) on Peru and Ghana NGDS trials:** Management stated trials at one mine are delayed and the other went in for a redesign/reiteration, declining to provide an immediate timeline for outcomes next quarter. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
AIA Engineering reported flat H1 volumes and margin pressure from soaring freight costs, but remains internally focused on achieving 25,000–30,000 tonnes of annual growth in FY28–FY29 alongside ongoing NGDS trials.
