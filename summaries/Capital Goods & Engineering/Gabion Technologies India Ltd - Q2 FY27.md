# Gabion Technologies India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth, capacity expansion nearing completion, and a robust order book pipeline.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 71.53 crore (up 76.2% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 10.27 crore (up 78.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 14.4% (vs 14.2% in H1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 5.42 crore (up 124.1% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 7.6% (vs 6% in H1 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** Above Rs 120 crore.
*   **Total Live Work Contracts:** Rs 179.9 crore (Rs 69.4 crore billed, Rs 110.5 crore to be billed).
*   **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.86 as of 30th September 2026.

### Highlights
*   **Execution:** Strong performance on economic corridors (Raipur-Visakhapatnam, Bengaluru-Vijayawada, Chennai-Tirupati) and BRO projects in Uttarakhand.
*   **Order Wins:** Secured 14 new work orders worth Rs 53.4 crore between April and September.
*   **Order Book Composition:** 79% from private/EPC clients; 21% from direct government work.
*   **Design Recognition:** Received standalone design orders from Hindalco, NCC, and D.H. Desai.
*   **Capacity:** Assam unit (5,000 MTPA) is operational. Paonta Sahib expansion is underway.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Guidance:** Management targets Rs 180 crore – Rs 200 crore for FY27; expects to cross Rs 300 crore in FY28 (range Rs 300 crore – Rs 350 crore).
*   **Growth:** Management expects 60% growth from FY27 to FY28.
*   **Margins:** Management expects 1% to 2% improvement in PAT margin for FY28.
*   **Order Pipeline:** Expects Rs 150 crore of new orders in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Drivers:** 12.6% of India's land area is landslide-prone; slope protection is shifting from post-failure repair to original project design.
*   **Sector Tailwinds:** Union Budget FY27 capital expenditure at Rs 12.22 lakh crore; significant railway projects in the Northeast (Rs 77,000 crore).
*   **Mining/Industrial:** New vertical for ash ponds and mine works has a potential of Rs 200 crore – Rs 300 crore over the next 2-3 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Optimization:** Employee costs reduced from 14% to 10%.
*   **Backward Integration:** PVC compound integration planned for Q1 next year (expected to add 1% to PAT margin). Future plans include GI wire integration (expected to add another 1.1% to PAT margin).
*   **Seasonality:** H2 is expected to be stronger than H1 due to the rainy season impacting H1 construction.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity:** Group capacity to reach 18,000 MTPA (from 9,108 tons in FY26) once Paonta Sahib expansion is commissioned.
*   **Timeline:** Full 18,000 MTPA capacity expected to be operational from late January 2027.
*   **Revenue Potential:** Manufacturing capacity potential is ~Rs 150 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow was an outflow of Rs 5.69 crore due to inventory buildup for order execution.
*   **Receivables/Inventory:** Trade receivables at Rs 43.91 crore; inventories at Rs 46.76 crore.
*   **Debt:** Management expects debt to increase by Rs 10 crore – Rs 15 crore by year-end but expects the debt-to-equity ratio to remain under 1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Working Capital:** High working capital intensity; management plans to tighten collections and expects a 10% improvement by year-end.
*   **Execution:** Reliance on peak season (H2) for revenue targets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the sharp increase in interest expense?** Management clarified that finance costs actually decreased YoY (Rs 2.09 crore vs Rs 2.52 crore) and they are working to optimize finance costs.
*   **Q: What is the margin differential between project and product business?** Management stated the solutions (project) business has a 5% higher PAT margin than the supply (product) business.
*   **Q: How will you grow direct government work?** Management noted that post-IPO net worth allows for higher bid capacity, and a dedicated bid desk is now pursuing these tenders.
*   **Q: Status of European certification?** Management expects the first product certification this financial year and full certification by mid-next financial year to aid exports.

### One-line takeaway
Gabion Technologies is scaling rapidly, leveraging its integrated design-to-installation model and new capacity to capitalize on infrastructure spending, with a clear strategy to improve margins through backward integration and government order wins.
