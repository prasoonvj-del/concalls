# GMM Pfaudler Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong order inflow (highest ever, crossing Rs 1,200 crore) and sequential revenue growth, offset by restructuring in loss-making European entities.

### Key numbers
- **Order Inflow (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,200 crore (India: Rs 483 crore, more than double sequentially).
- **Consolidated Order Inflow (H1 FY27):** Rs 2,204 crore, up 17% YoY.
- **Consolidated Order Backlog:** Rs 2,446 crore, up 14% YoY.
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,065 crore, up 18% YoY and 15% QoQ.
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,989 crore, up 17% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 110 crore, up 18% QoQ.
- **Reported EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 10.3%.
- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin (excluding loss-making entities, Q2 FY27):** 15.9% (Rs 139 crore).
- **Reported EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 204 crore (Margin: 10.2%).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (excluding loss-making entities, H1 FY27):** Rs 247 crore (Margin: 15.2%).
- **European Loss-Making Entities EBITDA Loss (H1 FY27):** Approximately Rs 43 crore.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT, Q2 FY27):** Rs 35 crore (vs Rs 38 crore YoY, and up 59% QoQ).
- **Reported PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 57 crore, up 20% YoY (Margin: 2.9%).
- **Net Debt Reduction (H1 FY27):** Approximately Rs 116 crore repaid.
- **CRISIL ESG Score:** 61 for FY26 (up from 50 in FY25).

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly order intake crossing Rs 1,000 crores for consecutive quarters.
- Non-traditional industries contributed 44% of the quarter's orders.
- Heavy Engineering Technology (HET) order intake up 254% YoY to Rs 201 crore; booked out till Q2 next year.
- Peptide orders from Indian customers reached close to Rs 150 crores in the last six months.
- Restructuring progress: U.K. facility closed, German workforce reduced by 40+ employees, and transition of manufacturing activities to Poland initiated.
- Top management changes: Departure of Thomas Kehl (CEO International) and Alexander Poempner (Group CFO); Ankit Nayyar appointed as Group CFO.
- CRISIL credit rating maintained at AA- with a stable outlook.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY 2027 Revenue Growth:** Double-digit growth expected.
- **FY 2027 Reported EBITDA Margin:** About 10% - 11% (including loss-making entities).
- **FY 2027 Adjusted EBITDA Margin (Core Business):** 14% - 15% (representing 80% of the business).
- Management expects benefits of the transformation/restructuring program to become increasingly visible towards the end of FY 2027, with fuller impact from FY 2028 onwards.
- Tax rate target: Endeavour to reach 30% or lower.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pharmaceuticals (especially CDMO and peptides) driving strong demand in CRT and PPT divisions.
- Heavy engineering growth supported by nuclear sector (Nuclear Power Corporation of India, Megha Engineering) and thermal power (LP and HP heaters).
- Defense sector seeing demand in Europe for acid recovery/concentration plants for TNT and nitrocellulose (NATO-sponsored projects).
- US semiconductor industry driving high-purity systems demand for Edlon.
- Metals and minerals sector investing in rare earths for data centers and AI.
- Traditional chemical and agrochemical sectors remain subdued/slow.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins impacted by loss-making European entities, but EBITDA margins improved due to operating leverage on top-line growth.
- 80% of the business operates at core adjusted EBITDA margins of 14% - 15%.
- Finance costs expected to decrease due to refinancing and debt reduction.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding the manufacturing facility in Poland to serve as a low-cost manufacturing hub alongside India.
- Heavy engineering factory in India is fully booked out till Q2 of next year.
- New facility opened in the U.S. for Edlon to cater to existing and future demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Repaid approximately Rs 116 crore of net debt during H1 FY27.
- Focus on group-wide refinancing, debt reduction, and legal entity/tax structure simplification.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External environment remains challenging in certain markets, notably Europe.
- European chemical industry reset with plant closures and low investments impacting high-cost European operations.
- Loss-making European entities reported an EBITDA loss of approximately Rs 43 crore in H1 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sameer Thakur (Ambit Private Limited) asked about European restructuring cost targets and savings.** Management replied that major costs (UK shutdown and German headcount reduction) are already accounted for in past numbers, and no large additional restructuring costs are expected.
2. **Kunal Mehta (InCred Equities) asked about the execution timeline and end markets for HET and peptide orders.** Management stated HET is booked out till Q2 next year, peptides have brought in ~Rs 150 crores in orders, and demand is driven by GLP-1/peptide drugs and nuclear/thermal power sectors.
3. **Sagar Shah (Spark PWM) inquired about the tax rate and debt reduction trajectory for FY27-28.** Management stated the tax rate target is 30% or lower, and finance costs will decrease following further debt repayments and refinancing.
4. **Nikhil Kanodia (Sunidhi Securities) asked for granularity on EBITDA-to-PAT levers and FY28 margin trajectory.** Management noted ETR reduction from 49% to 30% and removal of notional forex/intercompany loan losses will drive PAT expansion, with detailed numbers to be shared at an upcoming Investor Day.
5. **Anil Shah (Insightful Investment) pushed for specific margin numbers for next year given the European restructuring.** Management advised waiting for the upcoming Investor Day for granular breakdowns, but affirmed that 15% core EBITDA margins are achievable.
6. **Aviral Sharma (Finavenue Growth Fund) asked about the scale of the peptide and defense opportunities.** Management highlighted that peptides are a once-in-a-generation drug category with massive global shortages, and defense involves high-margin acid recovery plants for NATO/Europe.

### One-line takeaway
GMM Pfaudler delivered record order inflows and steady revenue growth in Q2 FY27, backed by strong momentum in pharma/peptides and heavy engineering, while aggressively restructuring its loss-making European operations to improve future profitability.
