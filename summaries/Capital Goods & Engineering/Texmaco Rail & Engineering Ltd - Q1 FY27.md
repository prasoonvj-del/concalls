# Texmaco Rail & Engineering Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted successful execution of short-term priorities, improved EBITDA/margins, a turnaround in the Rail Infra business, and strong private/export order diversification.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone revenue from operations:** Rs 753 crore
- **Bright Power revenue:** Rs 175 crore (up 76.8% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 81 crore (margin of 10.8%)
- **Rail Infra & Green EBIT margin:** 1.4% (turned positive from an EBIT loss YoY)
- **Bright Power EBIT margin:** 10.8% (expansion of 150 bps YoY)
- **Finance costs:** Declined 18.2% YoY and 17% sequentially
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 44 crore (up 4.8% YoY; margin at 5.9%, up 123 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 52 crore (up 85.9% YoY; margin at 6.9%, up 381 bps YoY)
- **Operational metrics:** 1,054 freight cars delivered; Foundry Division production at 5,148 tonnes
- **Order book:** Rs 9,923 crore as of June 30, 2026
- **Freight Car Division order book share (Private & export):** 96.4% in Q1 FY27 (vs. 79% in FY26 and 21% in FY25)
- **Wagon leasing:** ~35 rakes currently operating; ready to invest in ~100 additional rakes in the near term
- **Outstanding wagon order book:** >6,000 wagons (includes South African orders)
- **South Africa order value:** ~Rs 4,100 crore (wagon pricing and long-term maintenance; excludes locomotive pricing which is under process)

### Highlights
- Short-term priorities of improving EBITDA, turning around the Rail Infra business, strengthening Bright Power, and prudent financial management yielded visible results.
- Structural shift in the Freight Car Division towards private and export orders (96.4%).
- Strategic developments in Joint Ventures: TrinityRail entered the Texmaco Touax leasing JV (reducing Texmaco's stake to 34%); Wabtec JV advancing in air brake equipment, predictive maintenance, and new design bogies/IP; Saira Asia preparing for semi-high-speed and high-speed passenger rolling stock qualification; Texmaco Nymwag positioning tank rolling stock solutions for India.
- South African footprint established via major export orders, including wagon supply, long-term maintenance, and an upcoming locomotive mandate.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is working on a 2030 roadmap ("Texmaco 2.0") aiming to double top-line revenue (targeting Rs 5,000 crore to Rs 6,000 crore core base plus new businesses) while driving bottom-line margins towards mid-teens.
- Management expects annual top-line growth of 20% to 50% from new businesses and an overall revenue growth of 15% to 20% in 1–2 years.
- Core business EBITDA margin targeted to improve by 1.2% to 3% over the next 1 to 3 years.
- Around 50% to 60% of the South African wagon order is expected to be revenue-accrued in the next financial year (FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways' National Rail Plan targets increasing rail transport modal share from ~27% to 45% by 2030, which management expects will drive progressive demand for wagons, tracks, signaling, and safety equipment.
- Geopolitical and macroeconomic factors favor rail transport for inflation control and lower oil import dependency.
- Private sector participation is expanding due to Ministry of Railways reforms and policy enablers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Focus on cost optimization, product mix, higher-margin geographies, and AI-enabled efficiencies supported margins despite lower top-line execution.
- Supply chain stress experienced in Q1 due to high oil and gas prices impacting critical component partners, but alternative arrangements have been made and output is improving.
- Increase in "other expenses" was attributed mainly to freight charges paid for export wagon dispatches (revenue is recognized under total revenue).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocation for defense and Kavach will be measured and controlled to manage risk.
- Considering setting up a facility in South Africa (investment numbers under evaluation) for localization of exports (wagons and locomotives).
- Wagon manufacturing capacity is elastic (15,000–16,000 commodity wagons), but focus has shifted to higher-margin, specialized wagons rather than pure volume.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Disciplined debt payment and borrowing cost optimization drove an 18.2% YoY reduction in finance costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- General supply chain stress and volatility in oil and gas prices impacted component availability in Q1.
- Management exercises caution regarding metro and passenger train segments to avoid margin erosion, taking calibrated steps instead of rushing in.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Balasubramanian (Arihant Capital) on capital allocation for new areas (Kavach, defense, renewables, leasing):** Management stated that capital allocation for defense and Kavach will be controlled and not massive. For leasing, Texmaco's stake in the JV is 34% post-Trinity entry, targeting a 50% share of the leasing market.
2. **Parvez Qazi (Nuvama Group) on South African orders and localization:** Management confirmed the Rs 4,100 crore order value (wagons + maintenance) and stated that setting up a facility in South Africa is aimed at building long-term sustainable footprint and localization.
3. **Deepak Poddar (Private Investor) on FCD execution and wheelset availability:** Management noted supply chain headwinds in Q1 but stated alternative arrangements are in place. Wheelset supply from Rail Wheel Factory is currently sufficient for private and export orders.
4. **Rajesh Bhandari (Individual Investor) on 2030 vision and passenger segments:** Management detailed the "Texmaco 2.0" vision to double top-line size by 2030. On metros and Vande Bharat, management stated they are evaluating profitable entry strategies rather than jumping in blindly.
5. **Saumil Shah (Individual Investor) on order execution timelines and guidance:** Management clarified that 50% to 60% of the South African wagon order will execute in the next financial year, with maintenance spread over 15 years, and guided for 15%–20% revenue growth over 1–2 years.
6. **Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities) on South African locomotive order breakdown:** Management clarified that the disclosed order value covers wagons and maintenance, while the locomotive portion and its revenue timelines will be finalized upon tying up with a suitable partner.

### One-line takeaway
Texmaco Rail & Engineering delivered a profit-focused Q1 FY27 with an 85.9% surge in PAT, driven by margin expansion, a 96.4% private/export order mix, and strategic global partnerships.
