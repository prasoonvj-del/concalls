# Fabtech Cleanrooms Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasized strong top-line growth, a record-high order book, and strategic market penetration despite volatile raw material costs.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 133.23 crore (up 75% YoY)
- Standalone Revenue: Rs 107 crore (more than double YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 10.28 crore (up 75% YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA Margin: 7.67% (slightly ahead of the same half last year)
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 5.15 crore (up 55% YoY)
- EPS: Rs 4.18
- Order Book: Rs 468 crore as of 30th September (more than double from March; consolidated order book at Rs 517 crore)
- Sector-wise Order Book: Pharma and life sciences at Rs 217 crore; Renewable energy at Rs 195 crore; Data centers, semiconductors, and other industries at Rs 56 crore
- Receivables: Rs 129 crore as of 30th September (collected Rs 23 crore in October)
- Borrowings: Rs 40 crore against a net worth of Rs 112 crore (D/E of 0.37x)
- Manufacturing Capacity: Hyderabad plant adds ~50,000 square meters across 2 acres near Genome Valley

### Highlights
- Standalone business grew faster than consolidated, with revenues more than doubling.
- Order book more than doubled to Rs 468 crore (standalone), driven by large ticket sizes in life sciences and microelectronics.
- Hyderabad manufacturing plant commenced operations, featuring automated roll forming machines, CNC laser cutting, and dedicated PUFF/Rockwool panel lines with ISO, FM 4880, FM 4882, and ROHS certifications.
- Group entities (Kelvin, Advantech, Aart, Fabtech) are being integrated to offer a one-stop-shop model.
- Management highlighted a hot pipeline of Rs 600 crore out of total offers worth Rs 1,250 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 PAT to exceed H1 fairly substantially and full-year FY27 growth to reach 70%-80% (up from a conservative 20%-30% estimate) with similar percentage growth in margins.
- Management aims to become among the top three cleanroom players in India by the end of FY27 and number one by 2030, targeting a 25%-30% market share by 2030.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pharma demand is driven by revised Schedule M and international rejections, forcing facility upgrades.
- Renewable energy demand is supported by the mandate for domestic cells from June onwards.
- Data center demand is rising with AI servers requiring precision cooling and clean air (Kelvin has delivered for NSE, Nxtra, CtrlS, and Qualcomm).
- Semiconductor demand is bolstered by 12 approved projects under the India Semiconductor Mission, including a 75,000 square meter Class 1000 cleanroom project for Fabtech.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs (GI sheets, Rockwool, aluminium, powder coating) experienced high volatility and geopolitical pressure (e.g., aluminium moving from Rs 220 to Rs 440 in two months).
- Management prioritized customer commitments over immediate margins on reference projects.
- Margins in H2 are expected to improve due to billing milestones, rising volumes, settling input costs, and better commercial terms on newer orders.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added the Hyderabad plant enhancing capacity by 50,000 square meters. Upgraded automation at Umbergaon.
- Open to strategic investments or minority dilution (5-10%) if multinational partnerships arise, along with potential acquisitions of smaller companies and accessory makers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital stretched due to milestone-linked payments and longer terms accepted on reference projects.
- Management expects working capital to ease and cash flows to improve by March/April 2027.
- Banks have enhanced working capital credit lines.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unpredictable geopolitical scenarios and volatile metal and oil prices.
- Stretched cash flows and working capital pressures resulting from competitive terms offered on early reference projects.
- Customer project delays and building readiness affecting billing schedules despite completed manufacturing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Darshil Jhaveri (Retail Investor):** Asked about order execution timelines and hedging mechanisms. Management stated execution can happen in 3 months if clients are ready, and acknowledged learnings around raw material hedging for future cycles.
- **Ujjwal (Retail Investor):** Questioned the gap between exchange order book updates and H1 revenue. Management explained that while goods are manufactured, billing is delayed if customer sites/buildings are not ready to accept deliveries.
- **Sanjay Shah (Retail Investor):** Inquired about the glass ceiling for unit order sizes and semiconductor opportunities. Management stated there is no glass ceiling, pointing to potential semiconductor tickets ranging from Rs 200 crore to Rs 1000 crore.
- **Amit Jain (Retail Investor):** Followed up on order book math vs. actual dispatches. Management reiterated that client design changes and delayed site readiness account for the dispatch lag.
- **Piyush Bisani (Retail Investor):** Pointed out order book reconciliation discrepancies between standalone and consolidated figures. Management clarified the math and confirmed that raw material spikes weighed on H1 margins.

### One-line takeaway
Fabtech Cleanrooms reported a 75% YoY jump in H1 FY27 consolidated revenue and a doubled order book of Rs 468 crore, absorbing raw material volatility to prioritize strategic market penetration and reference project wins.
