# TD Power Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust financial growth, strong export-led order inflows, and raised full-year guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised FY27 revenue guidance from INR2,600 crores to INR2,900 crores – INR3,000 crores; initiated initial conservative FY28 guidance at INR3,400 crores – INR3,500 crores.
- **Tone and demand view:** Maintained a highly buoyant view, emphasizing clear project visibility up to 2030 and strong demand across multiple sectors beyond just data centers.
- **New topics or risks:** Discussed the Siemens Energy 2-pole generator project (Bhoomi Puja scheduled for November 19, 2026) and addressed potential impacts of the U.S. political landscape on data center investments.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Debottlenecking capex and large generator plant expansion plans are proceeding on schedule, with plant four targeted for completion by calendar Q1 2028.

### Key numbers
- **H1 Standalone Total Income:** INR14.55 billion (up 91% YoY)
- **H1 Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 20.02% (including other income, excluding exceptional and treasury income) vs 18.42% YoY
- **H1 Standalone PAT:** INR2.074 billion (up 110% YoY)
- **H1 Consolidated Total Income:** INR14.86 billion (up 78% YoY)
- **H1 Consolidated PAT:** INR2.096 billion (up 89% YoY)
- **H1 Manufacturing Order Booking:** INR23.19 billion (Generator business: INR20.88 billion, Railway business: INR1.7 billion, Spares and aftermarket: INR0.16 billion, Turkey business: INR0.45 billion)
- **Q2 Order Inflow:** INR8.24 billion (up 57% QoQ, 70% YoY)
- **H1 Export Order Inflow (Direct & Deemed):** INR14.06 billion (92% of H1 inflows)
- **Cash Position:** INR4.19 billion (including customer advances and preferential share issue money)
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** INR2,900 crores – INR3,000 crores (H2 distribution: INR725 crores – INR750 crores per quarter)
- **FY28 Initial Guidance:** INR3,400 crores – INR3,500 crores

### Highlights
- Strong order inflow momentum, with exports accounting for 92% of H1 inflows.
- Construction for the Siemens Energy 2-pole generator plant (Plant 4) is in full swing, with Bhoomi Puja scheduled for November 19, 2026.
- Continued brand acceptance and expanded market access globally across diversified segments.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised FY27 revenue guidance to INR2,900 crores – INR3,000 crores.
- H2 sales expected to be distributed equally between Q3 and Q4 at INR725 crores – INR750 crores per quarter.
- Initial conservative FY28 guidance set at INR3,400 crores – INR3,500 crores, with a high likelihood of upward revision.
- Medium-to-long term potential for a $1 billion top line.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by data centers, grid stabilization, basic power generation, and renewables (geothermal, hydro, waste-to-energy).
- OEM customers report strong production slot bookings beyond 2030, with clear real project visibility up to 2030.
- Hydro segment experiencing strong demand, particularly from markets like Nepal and Vietnam.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 EBITDA margin stood at 20.02% (including other income).
- Pricing remains stable; contracts feature price variation clauses linked to raw material costs to protect margins.
- Sustainable near-term margin range expected around 19%, with potential upside past 20% once Plant 4 achieves 50%-60% utilization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Plant 4 (Siemens Energy project) targeting readiness in calendar Q1 2028 (with June 2028 as the outer limit).
- By FY30, Plant 4 is expected to reach 70%-80% utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash position of INR4.19 billion maintained, inclusive of customer advances and preferential share issue proceeds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as material threats; management addressed media concerns regarding data center sustainability by noting diverse OEM customer exposure and robust forward bookings.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) asked about executable order book and Siemens Energy 2-pole generator orders.** Management stated most of the current order book is executable over the next 12 months, and confirmed orders are being taken from Siemens Energy's gas turbine business.
2. **Nitin Arora asked about long-term revenue potential and pricing dynamics.** Management stated a $1 billion top line is possible in the medium term, and confirmed pricing is stable with raw material price variation formulas in place.
3. **Mihir Manohar asked about U.S. political landscape risks for data centers and waiting periods.** Management noted that economic benefits ensure data center investments will proceed, and waiting periods for OEM capacities remain extended into 2028/2029.
4. **Mohit Surana (Monarch Networth Capital) asked about asset sweating and Plant 4 timelines.** Management stated Plant 4 is targeted for completion in calendar Q1 2028 (outer limit June 2028) and aims for 70%-80% utilization by FY30.
5. **Parikshit Kandpal asked about new demand drivers beyond traditional ones.** Management attributed growth to increased brand acceptance and market access globally over the past decade, alongside broad-based power generation demand.

### One-line takeaway
TD Power Systems reported stellar H1 FY27 performance with standalone revenue up 91% YoY, raised its full-year revenue guidance to INR2,900-3,000 crores, and initiated FY28 guidance at INR3,400-3,500 crores.
