# Acme India Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a robust order book of Rs 727 crore, strong sector tailwinds from Indian Railways capex, and confidence in achieving FY27 revenue of ~Rs 380 crore with improved H2 profitability.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 73.56 crore (tripled YoY).
- **Order Book:** Rs 727 crore (2.8x of FY26 revenue).
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** ~Rs 380 crore (approx. 44% growth YoY).
- **H2 FY27 Revenue Target:** Rs 310 crore to Rs 325 crore.
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** 17% to 18% (minimum 18% stated earlier).
- **FY27 PAT Margin Guidance:** 11% to 11.5%.
- **IPO Fund Utilization:** Rs 41 crore used for debt repayment, Rs 38 crore for working capital/vendor payments, and ~Rs 16 crore for IPO expenses out of Rs 106 crore raised.

### Highlights
- First earnings call since the company's listing on the BSE SME platform.
- Continuing the pattern from the company's earlier investor disclosures of end-to-end railway solutions, management emphasized a 360-degree presence across new coach furnishing, toilet modernization, refurbishment, and maintenance.
- H1 traditionally bears full fixed costs on low billing (10%-20% of annual revenue), resulting in a small loss, while H2 captures the majority of billing.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided FY27 revenue to ~Rs 380 crore.
- Management guided H2 FY27 revenue to Rs 310 crore to Rs 325 crore.
- Management guided an EBITDA margin range of 17% to 18% and PAT margins of 11% to 11.5% for FY27.
- Management aims to secure Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,500 crore in new orders over the next 12-15 months.
- Management provided a conservative FY28 revenue target of Rs 525 crore to Rs 550 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways' record capex stands at Rs 2.93 lakh crore for the year.
- Indian Railways runs over 100,000 passenger coaches, adding ~7,000 coaches annually (7% CAGR).
- Government announced the upgrading of 40,000 existing coaches to Vande Bharat standards.
- Addressable market across new coach furnishing, refurbishment, and maintenance is over Rs 32,000 crore annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- FY26 EBITDA margin improved to 16.2% from 14.1% in FY24.
- H1 FY27 finance cost rose to Rs 8 crore due to MSME interest provisions and borrowings for prototype delays; full-year finance cost is expected to normalize to Rs 5.5 crore to Rs 6 crore.
- Operating leverage from higher H2 billing is expected to expand margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Existing facility can support Rs 1,250 crore to Rs 1,500 crore in turnover with minor capex and modifications (currently operating on a single shift).
- Out of Rs 106 crore raised via the IPO fresh issue, Rs 79 crore has been utilized for debt repayment and working capital as of November 1, 2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term debt is negligible; short-term debt and high-cost unsecured loans were partially paid down using IPO proceeds.
- High receivable days historically driven by completion-linked milestones; management expects regular monthly billing starting April to improve cash cycles.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High seasonality: H1 incurs fixed overheads against low billing volumes, resulting in interim losses.
- Extended working capital cycles and milestone-linked collection delays in turnkey projects.
- Accounting provisions required for MSME interest and prototype testing delays.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Hardik Gandhi (HPMG) on order win visibility and competition:** Management explained they compete in ~40 out of 50 products, hold a 63% market share in toilet modernization via open tenders, and expect Rs 1,200-1,500 crore in new orders over 12 months backed by Rs 800 crore in upcoming bids.
2. **Daksh Prajapati (Hem Securities) on payment terms and margins:** Management stated 50%-60% of payments are received on material supply and the balance upon coach completion; guided for 17-18% sustainable EBITDA margins.
3. **Vijay Singh (Anand Rathi Wealth) on passenger coach maintenance credentials:** Management stated they qualify via past execution and technical JVs with Korean and Russian firms possessing train-set maintenance experience.
4. **Sachin (1729 Capital) on finance cost reduction:** Management clarified that H1 interest costs (Rs 8 crore) included MSME provisions and temporary borrowings, guiding for a normalized full-year interest cost of Rs 5.5-6 crore.
5. **Vinay Pandey (Vibhata Solutions) on ensuring L1 wins while maintaining profits:** Management pointed to early-bird advantages, stringent technical qualification criteria, and established supply chains, while dodging specific pricing trade secrets.

### One-line takeaway
Acme India Industries reported an H1 FY27 revenue of Rs 73.56 crore, underpinned by a robust Rs 727 crore order book, and guided for FY27 revenue of ~Rs 380 crore with 17-18% EBITDA margins, supported by structural tailwinds in railway capex and toilet modernization.
