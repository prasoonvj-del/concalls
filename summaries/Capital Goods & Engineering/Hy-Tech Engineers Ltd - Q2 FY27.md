# Hy-Tech Engineers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sequential growth in revenue (41%) and EBITDA (72%), with management confirming price revisions are finalized and second-half outlook remains robust.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance at ~20% and PAT growth at 24-25%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "temporary cost lag" in Q1 to "fully recovered/finalized" price revisions in Q2.
- **New topics or risks:** US subsidiary/warehouse in New Jersey is now established (was just approved in Q1). Stainless steel double ferrule fittings are now generating Rs 8-10 crore business (was just a development project in Q1).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Price revisions were successfully implemented as promised in Q1; H2 will bill at these revised prices.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 60.9 crore (up 22% YoY, 41% QoQ).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 14.5 crore (up 21% YoY, 72% QoQ).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 23.8% (vs 24% in Q2 FY26; 19.6% in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT:** Rs 8.5 crore (up 23% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 14% (vs 13.8% in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 103.9 crore (up 18% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 22.9 crore (up 14% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 13.1 crore (up 19% YoY).

### Highlights
- Established a wholly-owned subsidiary and warehouse in New Jersey to support US growth.
- Stainless steel double ferrule fittings business is currently generating Rs 8-10 crore; targeting oil & gas, shipping, and defense.
- Management confirmed price revisions with OEMs are finalized; H2 will bill at revised prices.
- Maintains a policy of keeping 20-30% excess capacity to accommodate new customer requirements.
- John Deere business currently accounts for ~12% of total turnover.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth of ~20-22% and PAT growth of 24-25%.
- Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1 (typically 53-54% of annual turnover).
- Target to double revenue within 3 years.
- Long-term target: Rs 500 crore revenue milestone.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic tractor market seeing a slight downfall, but balanced by growth in the excavator market.
- US market is primarily aftermarket (refurbishment-driven), unlike the domestic OEM-focused market.
- Management notes that MNC competitors (e.g., Parker) are 2x more expensive, providing a competitive advantage.
- Sany and Tata Hitachi are long-term contributors; ramp-up is gradual.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin improved to 23.8% in Q2 from 19.6% in Q1 due to better operating performance and cost absorption.
- Management stated they do not want to "unnecessarily charge" customers more, preferring to maintain 24-25% EBITDA margins through operational efficiency.
- Stainless steel fittings offer ~5% higher margins than carbon steel fittings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- IPO proceeds of Rs 30 crore to be invested over 1.5 years; expected to generate ~Rs 100 crore in revenue.
- Current capacity utilization is ~70%; management intentionally keeps 20-30% free for new business.
- New land at Shirwal is earmarked for growth beyond the Rs 500 crore revenue mark.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management indicated that future capex will be funded by internal accruals and generated revenue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- US midterm election results and tariff situations noted as potential variables for US business growth.
- Management noted that new sector entries (defense, railways) take a long time (1+ years) to materialize.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Paras Chheda (LLP) on growth drivers:** Management clarified growth is driven by gaining market share (SOB increase) in existing OEMs rather than just industry growth.
- **Richa Agrawal on stainless steel fittings:** Management confirmed this is a high-margin (5% premium) product opening new TAM (oil & gas, defense) with only one other player in India.
- **Rajesh (NB Investments) on competition:** Management stated MNC competitors are 2x more expensive, giving Hy-Tech a price advantage.
- **Himanshu Bisani on John Deere:** Management clarified John Deere is ~12% of revenue; business ramp-up is gradual (FY28 expected for full impact).
- **Uttam Purohit on seasonality:** Management reiterated H2 is historically stronger (53-54% of turnover) vs H1 (45-46%).

### One-line takeaway
Hy-Tech delivered a strong Q2 with significant sequential margin recovery and confirmed price hikes, while successfully operationalizing its US subsidiary and scaling high-margin stainless steel fittings.
