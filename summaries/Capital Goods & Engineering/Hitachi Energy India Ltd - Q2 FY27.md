# Hitachi Energy India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Hitachi Energy India delivered strong revenue execution (+63.5% YoY), robust ex-HVDC order growth (+85.9% YoY), and expanded operational EBITDA margins (16.5%).

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained general focus on execution and capacity expansion; no quantitative revenue/margin guidance provided.
- Tone and demand view: Remained strongly positive; demand outlook expanded with government approvals for 1150 kV AC transmission systems and PM-DHARA/Green Energy Corridor Phase-III.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared:
  - New topics/risks: Sharp dip in H1 operating cash flow (Rs 91 crore vs Rs 874 crore in H1 FY26) due to contract asset build-up in HVDC projects; unrealized notional FX loss rose to Rs 88.1 crore; approval of first-ever 1150 kV AC system in India.
  - Resolved/shifted: Revenue recognition on both large HVDC contracts (Bhadla and Khavda) officially commenced.
- Last quarter's promises: Execution on HVDC projects began as planned; capex execution timeline targets (2026–2028) remain on track.

---

### Key numbers
- **Order Inflow:** Rs 4,120.8 crore in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 order intake reached Rs 9,217.3 crore. Excluding HVDC, Q2 order intake grew 85.9% YoY and 21.8% QoQ (H1 ex-HVDC grew 53.1% YoY).
- **Order Backlog:** Rs 33,335.4 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 2,996.5 crore, up 63.5% YoY and 20.2% QoQ (H1 FY27 revenue stood at Rs 5,490.2 crore, up 65.8% YoY).
- **Operational EBITDA:** Rs 494.4 crore, up 69.5% YoY and 23.6% QoQ (H1 FY27 operational EBITDA reached Rs 894.3 crore).
- **Operational EBITDA Margin:** 16.5% in Q2 FY27.
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 430.4 crore, up 22.0% YoY and 10.5% QoQ (PBT margin 14.4%). Includes Rs 88.1 crore notional FX loss.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 318.5 crore, up 20.5% YoY (PAT margin 10.6%). H1 FY27 PAT stood at Rs 612.0 crore (+54.7% YoY).
- **Other Income:** Rs 56.8 crore (derived from interest on bank deposits).
- **Cost Structure (% of revenue):** Material cost at 61.7%, Personnel expenses at 6.2%, Operational/other expenses at 16.6%.
- **Segmental Growth:** Service order intake grew 77.1% YoY; Export orders grew 41.0% YoY in Q2 FY27.

---

### Highlights
- Commissioned India's first 400 kV, 2x 550 MVAR grid-forming STATCOM project in Fatehgarh.
- Inaugurated grid automation project for India’s largest power utility covering >180,000 circuit kilometers of transmission lines.
- Q2 order intake composition: Products 60%, Projects 34%, Services 7%. Sector mix: Utilities 52%, Industries 24%, Transport & Infra 23%.
- Cumulative HVDC revenue execution reached ~10% for the Bhadla project and ~4–5% for the Khavda project.
- *Pattern check*: Continuous execution momentum across grid modernization, data centers, and high-voltage transmission aligns with past records.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects multi-year structural investment opportunities driven by long-term grid visibility extending up to 2035.
- Expects 4 to 5 HVDC projects to be tendered/approved over the next 12 to 24 months.
- Company priorities for FY27 include strengthening core Utilities/HVDC, expanding Mobility, scaling Services, pursuing battery energy storage system (BESS) and data centers, and driving exports.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Approved cabinet initiatives (PM-DHARA, Green Energy Corridor Phase-III) represent ~Rs 1.86 lakh crore of investments, including 50 GWh of battery energy storage.
- Government approval of the 1150 kV AC transmission system opens new high-voltage market opportunities.
- Data center operational capacity in India is projected by market reports to reach 5–8 GW by 2030 (up from 1.57 GW in August 2026), with total power demand reaching 15–17 GW by 2032.
- Management observes strong on-the-ground project execution and no slowdown in data center investments across target markets (India, Southeast Asia, APAC).

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material cost fluctuations (61.7% in Q2) were driven primarily by quarterly product execution mix. Management expects gross margins to normalize as export and service revenues increase.
- PBT and PAT reflect an Rs 88.1 crore unrealized notional foreign exchange loss due to mark-to-market accounting of open purchase orders.
- Export orders carry an overall margin premium of 300 to 500 bps compared to domestic projects.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Strategic capex across transformers, high-voltage products, and grid automation facilities is targeted for completion mostly by the end of 2027.
- Groundwork for the new large power transformer facility is underway, with completion targeted for 2028.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net operating cash flow for H1 FY27 decreased to Rs 91 crore (compared to Rs 874 crore in H1 FY26) due to working capital accumulation in contract assets for large HVDC projects. Management expects cash to be recouped as milestones are billed.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Working capital lock-up in contract assets during the early stages of HVDC project execution.
- Foreign exchange volatility creating quarterly P&L mark-to-market operational adjustments (Rs 56.1 crore total exchange/commodity loss in P&L).
- Supply-chain tightness and raw material price inflation.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities):** What is the execution progress on the Bhadla and Khavda HVDC contracts?  
  - **Management:** Bhadla has booked ~10% of revenue cumulatively, while Khavda has booked ~4–5%.
- **Rahul Gajare:** How much of the newly approved 1150 kV AC system is localized?  
  - **Management:** Key components—1150 kV transformers, hybrid switchgear, and automation—are 100% localized and tested in India.
- **Sumit Kishore:** Why did net operating cash flow drop to Rs 91 crore in H1 FY27?  
  - **Management:** The decrease is attributable to working capital tied up in contract assets for major HVDC projects, which will normalize as invoicing progresses.
- **Jason Soans (IDBI Capital):** Is there any slowdown in data center order momentum?  
  - **Management:** No slowdown is being observed in target markets (India, APAC); customers have firm timelines and commissioning schedules.
- **Amit Anwani:** What is the company's addressable opportunity in the ~Rs 75,000 crore transmission projects recently highlighted by NCT?  
  - **Management:** Scope depends on project formatting, but company holds ~50% historical market share in HVDC and strong localization in ultra-high voltage AC systems.

---

### One-line takeaway
Hitachi Energy India delivered robust 63.5% YoY revenue growth and 16.5% operational EBITDA margins, supported by broad-based ex-HVDC order execution and technological readiness for multi-year grid expansion cycles.
