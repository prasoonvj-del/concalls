# Cosmic CRF Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in order execution, margin expansion, capacity ramp-up, and recovery in railway and infrastructure demand despite recent macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 151 crore (vs Rs 161 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Order Book:** Rs 612 crore (up ~39% to 40% YoY from Rs 414–441 crore in H1 FY26); 78% infrastructure and 22% railways.
- **Installed Capacity (Current Total):** 1,33,600 MT per annum (Singur standalone: 55,000 MT; NS Engineering Projects: 65,000 MT; Cosmic Springs & Engineers: 13,600 MT).
- **H1 FY27 Production Volume:** Singur standalone produced 21,110 MT; NS Engineering produced 22,424 MT.
- **Capacity Utilization:** 78.6% across operating units.
- **Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Consolidated operational cash flow turned positive at over Rs 18 crore (vs negative Rs 78 crore previously); Standalone operational cash flow crossed Rs 9 to 10 crore.
- **Amzen Transaction Payments:** Rs 32 crore paid upfront and Rs 28 crore paid as security deposit (Total Rs 60 crore committed).

### Highlights
- Railway wagon component business saw order execution deferrals due to West Asian geopolitical issues, government exchequer fund delays, and pending BCN wagon orders; recent allocation of ~9,000 BOXNHL wagon order conversions has restarted momentum.
- Order book mix deliberately pivoted to 78% infrastructure and 22% railways due to faster execution timelines in infrastructure.
- Galvanizing capacity was expanded to 12,000 MT, with an air-wiping facility being commissioned by end of November 2026.
- Operationalized over 3,000 SKUs across rolling, fabrication, galvanizing, springs, sheet piles, and crash barriers.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Target:** Reaffirmed guidance of 20% to 25% top-line growth over FY26 (FY26 consolidated sales were Rs 717 crore).
- **Long-term Outlook (FY30 Base Plan without Amzen):** Target total installed capacity of 1,74,600 MT generating Rs 1,178 crore to Rs 1,300 crore revenue at 80% utilization with 12% to 14% PAT margins.
- **Amzen Acquisition Ramp-up:** Expects to operationalize 25% capacity in FY28 (~Rs 400 crore revenue contribution), scaling to 70–75% utilization by FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management estimates Indian Railway's long-term requirement at 45,000 to 60,000 wagons per annum, whereas total domestic manufacturing capacity is ~36,000 wagons/year (effective output 32,000–33,000 units/year).
- Out of 4,12,000 total wagons in India, ~2,00,000 wagons require continuous repair and refurbishments.
- Cabinet approval of the Rs 10,000 crore container manufacturing support scheme is expected to expand indigenous container demand from 1 lakh to 5 lakh units.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone legal expenses increased to ~Rs 7 crore in H1 FY27 (vs Rs 6.5 crore) due to Supreme Court hearing expenses; legal costs are expected to drop significantly in H2 FY27.
- Raw material (steel) prices are near multi-year lows, which management expects will expand operating leverage as volumes scale.
- Springs unit yields ~25% EBITDA margin and ~14-15% PAT margin (COGS ~Rs 18,000/set, operational cost ~Rs 4,500/set, realizations ~Rs 30,000/set).
- Forging unit expected to yield 14-15% PAT margins when catering to RDSO/DRDO specifications.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Forging Unit:** Setup delayed by 2 to 2.5 months due to unseasonal rains; total CapEx of Rs 45–50 crore for 11,000 MT capacity. Commercial trial run expected in April–May 2027.
- **Springs Division:** Capacity to increase from 13,600 MT to 24,000–25,000 MT by end of FY27.
- **Amzen Asset:** Plant houses 392 machines with an initial RDSO-assessed capacity of 3,600 wagons (expandable to 7,200 wagons per annum). On-site refurbishment analysis ongoing; commercial production targeted for April–May 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Mainboard migration process is underway; share count post-warrant conversions and equity swaps will reach 1.02 crore shares (meeting the 1 crore minimum share requirement).
- Management reaffirmed no equity dilution for raising growth capital until FY28.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in NCLT court proceedings for final approval of the Amzen resolution plan.
- Government policy changes or spending slowdowns in railway wagon procurement.
- Extended lead times for mandatory product field trials and approvals (RDSO, BIS, DRDO).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arnab Bhattacharjee:** Query on high legal expenses in H1.
  *Management Response:* Attributed to Supreme Court legal team representation costs incurred during Q1 FY27; confirmed legal costs will reduce significantly in H2 FY27.
- **Hanu Rao:** Query on mainboard migration status and Amzen production timeline.
  *Management Response:* Mainboard listing criteria requires 1.02 crore shares post-warrant/swap completion, targeted by Q4 FY27. Amzen plant overhaul will wrap up by December 2026, with initial production starting in April–May 2027.
- **Akshat Agarwal:** Query on economics and capabilities of the springs and forging divisions.
  *Management Response:* Springs unit generates ~25% EBITDA margin and 14-15% PAT margin. Forging unit (11,000 MT) is set up for high-margin RDSO wagon components, infrastructure items, and defense R&D.
- **Akshat Anjana:** Query on Amzen asset utilization strategy and execution risks.
  *Management Response:* Target 25% capacity utilization (~37,000-38,000 MT / ~Rs 400 crore top-line) in FY28, ramping up to 70–75% by FY30 across wagons, repairs, loco shells, sea freight containers, and bridge girders.
- **Kunal Shah:** Query on full-year FY27 revenue targets after flat H1 performance.
  *Management Response:* Reaffirmed expectation of 20-25% revenue growth over FY26, supported by high inventory clearance, execution of converted wagon orders, and stronger second-half seasonality.

### One-line takeaway
Cosmic CRF expects H2 FY27 to drive full-year top-line growth of 20-25%, backed by a strong Rs 612 crore order book and upcoming high-margin forging/Amzen plant commissioning in early FY28.
