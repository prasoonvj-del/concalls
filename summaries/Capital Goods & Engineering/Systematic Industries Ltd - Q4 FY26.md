### Highlights
- Recorded revenue of Rs 5563 million (up 24% YoY), EBITDA of Rs 405 million, and PAT of Rs 205 million.
- Achieved net debt-free status as of March 31, 2026.
- Strategic shift towards high-growth "New Age" segments: Optical Ground Wire (OPGW), Optical Fiber Cables (OFC), and Aluminum Clad Steel (ACS) wire.
- Secured first EPC contract from Power Grid of India for OPGW (Agra-Kumher section).
- Secured order over Rs 100 million from Indian Railways for 24/48-fibre armoured OFC.
- High-end and import-substitute products constitute 20% of steel wire volume.
- Exports reach over 30 countries.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the "New Age" segment to contribute significantly to the bottom line in FY27 and FY28 with double-digit profitability.
- Target to expand high-end and import-substitute products from 20% to 30-35% of steel wire volume.
- Target to increase export contribution to 15% of total revenue.
- Management has not yet earmarked specific figures for FY27 Capital Expenditures.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rising demand for OFC driven by the proliferation of data centers and the government’s National Broadband Mission.
- SIL is the only integrated OPGW manufacturer in India controlling the entire value chain from steel processing to cable manufacturing.
