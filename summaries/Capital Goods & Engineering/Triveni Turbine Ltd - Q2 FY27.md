# Triveni Turbine Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strongest-ever quarterly order booking and robust export momentum offset by lower near-term revenue and margin compression due to an unfavorable product/geographic mix.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained expectation of growth in revenue and profitability for full-year FY27 over FY26, with performance heavily back-ended to Q3 and Q4.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from cautious to more confident regarding order conversions, backed by a record Q2 order book and easing logistics/freight constraints in the Middle East.
- **New topics or risks:** Secured a marquee combined cycle gas turbine order (data centre end-use) and a boiler feedwater pump drive turbine order for thermal plants; entered long-term gas turbine refurbishment contracts in South Africa ($80 million to $90 million total potential over 4-5 years).
- **Last quarter's promises:** The NTPC CO2-based energy storage project commissioning was pushed to Q4 FY27 (previously expected in Q2 FY27).

- **Domestic revenue:** Rs 340 crore (up 51.8% YoY)

- **Profit before tax (PBT):** Rs 75.7 crore, down 39.2% YoY (margin of 15.4% vs 24.6% YoY)
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 53.3 crore, down 41.7% YoY (margin of 10.4%)
- **H1 Revenue from operations growth:** 6.4% YoY
- **H1 Domestic sales growth:** >40% YoY
- **H1 Exports decline:** 24% YoY

- **Product order booking:** Rs 601 crore (up 32.5% YoY)

- **Domestic order booking:** Rs 379 crore (marginally lower by 2.5% YoY)

- **NTPC order remaining billing:** Approximately 15% of order value, split equally between Q3 and Q4.

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly order booking of Rs 764 crore in Q2 FY27.
- Made strategic inroads into the combined cycle gas turbine segment and boiler feedwater pump drives for thermal power plants.
- Secured unique long-term gas turbine refurbishment contracts in the SADC region (South Africa) with a total potential of $80 million to $90 million over 4 to 5 years.
- NTPC strategic order commissioning is now scheduled within Q4 FY27 (postponed from Q2 FY27 estimate); remaining billing (~15%) will not impact margins negatively.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management anticipates a strong reversion to mean margins in Q3 and Q4 (with Q4 stronger than Q3), driven by a higher mix of exports and aftermarket billings.
- Full-year FY27 revenue and profitability are expected to register growth over FY26, supported by robust execution of the existing order book.
- FY28 visibility remains strong, bolstered by healthy order booking trends.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Electrification demand is extremely robust globally, driven by data centers, electric vehicles, transportation, and industrial power requirements.
- International enquiry books show strong traction in North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, and Turkey; Central/South American inquiries have plateaued due to political/election deferments.
- Domestic enquiry levels remain stable compared to Q1, with growth led by the metal segment, despite some market share moderation in larger megawatt turbines.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Reported lower EBITDA (17.5%) and PAT (10.4%) margins due to an unfavorable mix—higher domestic share and lower aftermarket proportion during the quarter.
- Commodity price increases impacted material costs, but management expects normalization by Q4.
- Conservative accounting principles are maintained for warranty and contingencies; potential tax refunds are not yet accounted for in the P&L.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- R&D and personnel investments remain robust, focusing on new product platforms and advanced technology developments (including small gas turbines for thrust applications and Brayton cycle applications).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory levels on the balance sheet support execution in upcoming quarters. (Specific debt/working capital figures were not quantified in the call).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Lumpy enquiry books and potential deferment of orders due to political considerations/elections in regions like Central South America.
- Competitive intensity in domestic larger-megawatt turbine segments (40-50 MW).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Anwani (PL Capital) asked about domestic order growth drivers and full-year margin expectations.** Management stated domestic growth is driven by metals, and confirmed that H2 margins will expand due to higher export/aftermarket mix, with full-year profitability expected to grow over FY26.
2. **Harshit Patel (Equirus Securities) asked about export dispatch deferments and U.S. enquiry pipelines.** Management confirmed deferred dispatches are moving to Q3/Q4 as transportation eases, and noted U.S. enquiries are rising in data centers, carbon capture, and energy storage.
3. **Chirag Muchhala (Centrum Broking) asked about the U.S. combined cycle order and domestic boiler feed pump scope.** Management clarified the 50 MW steam turbine order is a combined cycle add-on (150 MW equivalent plant) from the U.S., and explained that boiler feed pump packages vary by order scope and EPC requirements.
4. **Mohit Surana (Monarch Networth) asked about domestic market share pressures and larger megawatt turbine strategies.** Management noted domestic share fluctuations depend on industry-specific demand (e.g., steel) and competition from global OEMs, while large-scale utility turbine manufacturing is assessed opportunistically.
5. **Salil Desai (Investment Managers) asked about aftermarket order-to-execution cycles.** Management explained spares take 3-4 months, services take about a quarter, and complex refurbishment contracts take 5-12 months.
6. **Prolin Nandu (Edelweiss) asked about the U.S. pipeline and combined cycle data center strategy.** Management stated data center end-use is often confidential, but electrification and energy storage themes are driving strong, diversified U.S. demand.

### One-line takeaway
Despite near-term margin compression from an unfavorable domestic-heavy revenue mix, Triveni Turbine delivered record order inflows and expects a sharp margin and revenue rebound in H2 FY27.
