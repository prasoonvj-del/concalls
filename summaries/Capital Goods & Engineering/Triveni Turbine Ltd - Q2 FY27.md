### Highlights
- Record quarterly order booking of ₹7.64 billion driven by exports (+49%) and product (+32.5%)
- Inroads into combined cycle gas turbine segment (50MW steam turbine for 150MW gas plant in US) and boiler feedwater pump drives for domestic supercritical thermal plants
- Secured long-term gas turbine refurbishment contracts via South African subsidiary (SADC region) with European partner; potential $80-90mn over 4-5 years
- Strategy to expand into gas turbine services, parts, and small gas turbine development (Brayton cycle) with academic/industrial partners
- Aftermarket closing order book more than doubled to ₹6.39 billion (26% of total) on contract wins across thermal, renewable, combined cycle, utility, geothermal
- Domestic market share declined in 40-50MW segment due to competitor's historical strength in steel; no new competitors
- US enquiry pipeline up substantially: data center combined cycle, energy storage, carbon capture, industrial cogeneration
- New advanced product platforms rolling out, expected to improve cost structure and margins in coming quarters

### Guidance & outlook
- Q3 and Q4 substantially better than H1, Q4 more than Q3 (management)
- Full-year FY27: some revenue growth over FY26, profitability higher but "not significant" (management)
- H2 margins "significantly healthier", reversion to higher margin levels based on product mix (management)
- Consolidated EBITDA roughly consistent with FY26 (management)
- FY28 provides "greater avenue for growth" (management)
- Order booking growth expected in coming quarters; visibility for FY28 order booking gives confidence (management)
- Gas turbine services/parts to grow in short term; small gas turbine development multi-year (management)
- US subsidiary: aftermarket focus, potential packaging if tariffs arise, currently servicing via India (management)

### Demand & industry commentary
- Electrification demand robust: EVs, transportation, data centers, industrial power; high commodity prices driving demand
- Domestic enquiry flat QoQ; Central South America plateauing due to politics/elections
- US enquiry up substantially: data center combined cycle, energy storage, carbon capture, industrial
- Southeast Asia flat, Europe flat, India flat
- Middle East: enquiry pipeline to increase with war threat reducing; power generation and refurb opportunities
- Drive turbines: strong Indian thermal pipeline (80GW addition) for boiler feedwater drives; Middle East drive turbine enquiries dip but recovering
- Combined cycle market: tough entry (<5% of enquiry book), gas turbine OEMs have stronghold, but opens doors
- Aftermarket: spares 3-4 month cycle, service ~1 quarter, refurbishment 5-12 months (high value add 3-4 quarters)

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Anwani (PL Capital) on domestic order trends, gas turbine contract details, small gas turbine timelines, full-year growth/margins, NTPC loss**  
   Mgmt: Domestic share loss in 40-50MW (steel segment), market growing. Gas turbine contract: South Africa, parts upgrade/replacement with European partner, $80-90mn potential over 4-5 years. Small gas turbine: thrust application, multi-year, OEM ambition. FY27: some growth, profitability up but not significant. H2 margins healthier. NTPC: marginally loss, claims later.

2. **Harshit Patel on export deferrals from Q1, product enquiry book update**  
   Mgmt: Deferrals to Q3/Q4, H2 export % higher. Enquiry book flat overall; US up substantially (data center, energy storage, carbon capture), Central SA down, SE Asia flat, Europe flat, India flat.

3. **Chirag Muchhala (Centrum) on combined cycle order (data center?), boiler feedwater pump drive scope**  
   Mgmt: 50MW steam turbine for 150MW gas plant (3:1), combined cycle conversion, direct to client, lucrative export pricing. Boiler feed pump drive: turbine + pump package; Triveni not pump manufacturer; scope varies (turbine only, or packaged at Triveni's PO or by pump manufacturer). Large domestic thermal market.

4. **Mohit Surana (Networth) on domestic share loss reasons, >100MW strategy, Middle East drive turbines**  
   Mgmt: Competitor (largest global) has historical share in steel; intensity unchanged. >100MW: opportunistic manufacturing for global OEMs, no Board strategy to become OEM; value in industrial/customized. Middle East: enquiry pipeline increasing for power generation/refurb; drive turbines more in India (boiler feedwater).

5. **Salil Desai on aftermarket order-to-execution cycle**  
   Mgmt: Spares 3-4 months, service ~1 quarter, refurbishment 5-12 months (high value add 3-4 quarters). ~80% of opening order book executed in FY. Book-and-bill: Q1 for product/spares to Q3, refurb to Q2.

6. **Prolin Nandu (Edelweiss) on US combined cycle order modus operandi, US pipeline drivers (energy storage, API, refurb)**  
   Mgmt: Normal sales process (agents, personnel, relationships). Data center not large part of enquiry. Drivers: energy storage, industrial, geothermal, electrification IPPs. Refurbishment visibility limited but hopeful. API not significant in US.

### One-line takeaway
Q2 disappointing as expected due to adverse revenue mix, but record order booking, strong export/aftermarket pipeline, and gas turbine refurbishment foray provide confidence for H2 margin recovery and FY28 growth.
