# Kirloskar Brothers Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong revenue growth and robust order inflows across domestic and international segments, despite margin pressure in the international business.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 12,767 crore (up 24% YoY).
*   **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 23,816 crore (up 19% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 1,432 crore (up 16% YoY); Margin: 11.2%.
*   **H1 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 2,738 crore (up 9% YoY); Margin: 11.5%.
*   **Consolidated Order Intake:** Rs 20,472 crore (up 68% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 7,991 crore (up 23% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 937 crore (up 37% YoY).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 544 crore (up 45% YoY).
*   **Domestic Order Book:** Rs 29,264 crore (up 29% YoY).
*   **International Order Book:** Rs 17,013 crore (up 32% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Domestic Performance:** Strong growth driven by capital goods sectors (oil & gas, fertilizer, power) and retail pumps. Small pumps segment grew 12% (50% volume, 50% value).
*   **International Performance:** Growth driven by SPP U.K., SPP USA, and the Dutch Group. Oil & gas sector is a key contributor, particularly in the U.S. (Louisiana gas fields), Guyana, and Qatar.
*   **Nuclear Power:** Received orders for primary circuit and canned motor pumps (approx. Rs 60-70 crore). Management confirmed technical qualification for primary coolant pumps and participation in upcoming tenders.
*   **Working Capital:** Inventory increased due to long-lead capital goods orders (8-12 month execution cycles) currently in WIP.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management remains confident of delivering double-digit revenue growth in FY27 over FY26 for standalone businesses.
*   **Margins:** Management expects margins to improve in the second half of the year.
*   **International Strategy:** Targeting 40% of total business from the services segment at the KBI BV level over a 3-year cycle.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Domestic:** Strong demand from thermal power, nuclear, oil & gas, and infrastructure/water projects. Data centers are an emerging opportunity in India.
*   **International:** U.K. industrial sector is facing headwinds due to high energy costs; growth is shifting toward warehousing (fire pumps) and AMP8 (water). U.S. and Guyana markets are seeing strong oil & gas activity.
*   **Commoditization:** Fire pump market is becoming commoditized due to low-cost competition meeting basic accreditation standards.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** Consolidated margins impacted by raw material price increases (copper, gun metal, steel) and a shift in product mix (lower service revenue contribution in the international business).
*   **Raw Materials:** Management noted an 8% increase in material costs in the U.K. business. Price hikes have been implemented to pass on costs.
*   **International Margins:** U.K. margins were impacted by lower service revenue and higher employee-related expenses for oil & gas project execution.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Not explicitly discussed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Working capital increased by ~Rs 225 crore (standalone) and ~Rs 250 crore (consolidated) in H1, primarily due to higher inventory for long-lead capital goods orders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **International Margins:** Oil & gas projects carry lower margins than the services business.
*   **Execution Delays:** Large orders are lumpy and dependent on customer readiness; service contract kick-offs have faced delays.
*   **U.K. Industrial Environment:** High electricity costs are negatively impacting the industrial/petrochemical base in the U.K.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the margin decline despite strong growth?** Management attributed it to raw material inflation and a temporary shift in product mix (lower service revenue).
*   **Q: Status of nuclear power tenders?** Management confirmed technical approval for primary coolant pumps and participation in upcoming NPCIL tenders.
*   **Q: Outlook for U.K. margins?** Management expects recovery in H2 but noted that margins may not reach FY25 levels due to the current mix of oil & gas projects.
*   **Q: Data center opportunity?** Management is negotiating a framework agreement with a large U.S. operator to become a primary supplier.
*   **Q: Impact of drought?** Management expects retail pump demand to increase but noted that large state-managed water projects take time to materialize.

### One-line takeaway
KBL is delivering strong top-line growth and order intake, but profitability is currently constrained by raw material costs and a temporary product mix shift toward lower-margin capital goods, with management expecting margin recovery in H2.
