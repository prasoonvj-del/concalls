# John Cockerill India Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q3 CY26 (referred to in the call as Q3 fiscal year '26 / nine months ended September 30, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasized strong YoY revenue growth, global consolidation under Project Vulcan, and a robust project pipeline, viewing near-term margin pressure as a transitional phase of upfront project mobilization costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained long-term target of EUR 1 billion / INR 8,000 crores to INR 10,000 crores by 2030 (unchanged from prior communications).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to highlight active execution and scaling of the integrated international platform, with increased hiring in India and China.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: The proposed acquisition of the U.S. entity was explicitly deferred to early 2028 (previously expected sooner); an MoU was signed with the Government of Maharashtra for green iron/steel in Gadchiroli.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: CCPS allotment to John Cockerill SA and BSE/RBI approvals for Project Vulcan were completed post-quarter; U.S. integration was pushed out.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q3):** Rs 174.4 crore (+80% YoY)
- **Standalone Revenue (9M):** Rs 523.6 crore (more than double YoY from Rs 255.5 crore)
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q3):** Loss of Rs 12 crore
- **Exceptional Income (Q3):** Rs 53.6 crore pre-tax (Rs 47.1 crore after tax) from Andheri land and building sale
- **Standalone Net Profit (Q3):** Rs 32.2 crore (including exceptional item)
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q3):** Rs 316 crore (+22% YoY from Rs 259 crore)
- **Consolidated Revenue (9M):** Rs 959 crore (+31% YoY from Rs 734 crore)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q3):** Loss of Rs 35.7 crore
- **Consolidated Net Profit (Q3):** Rs 11.1 crore
- **Deferred Consideration Payable (as of Sep 30):** Rs 218.7 crore
- **Headcount Additions:** >100 in India since September '25; >30 in China

### Highlights
- **Project Vulcan & Restructuring:** Received BSE and RBI approvals, allotting 35,185 CCPS to John Cockerill SA post-quarter. U.S. entity acquisition deferred to early 2028 for balance sheet readiness. Registered a workshop in Zhangjiagang, China.
- **Green Steel MoU:** Signed an MoU with the Government of Maharashtra on September 2, 2026, to explore green iron and green steel technology (specifically in Gadchiroli).
- **Board Changes:** Accepted resignation of Vivek Bhide; appointed Praveen Kadle, Shekhar Kashalikar (Non-Independent), and Jan Lueder (Independent) to the board.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated its long-term target of reaching EUR 1 billion (approx. INR 8,000 to 10,000 crores) in revenue by 2030 via organic growth, Volteron, and acquisitions.
- Management expects revenue recognition and margin contributions from recently acquired projects to ramp up in FY27 and FY28 as they move past the engineering and procurement stages.
- Target to achieve around 6% EBITDA margin over time as execution scales.

### Demand & industry commentary
- World Steel forecasts global steel demand growth of approximately 8% in both '26 and '27 in India.
- Increasing customer investments in capacity expansion, advanced processing, electrical steels (E-Si), energy efficiency, and low-carbon production.
- Volteron technology highlighted as a key future solution for green iron/steel production by directly electrolyzing iron ore without generating hydrogen.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q3 EBITDA losses (standalone Rs 12 crore, consolidated Rs 35.7 crore) driven by upfront engineering, mobilization, procurement, and manpower costs for newly secured projects before revenue recognition kicks in.
- Headcount expansion in India (>100 people) and China (>30 people) added to the current cost base ahead of future revenue generation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Registered a new workshop in Zhangjiagang near Shanghai to strengthen metal business and equipment-related activities.
- U.S. entity integration deferred to early 2028 due to balance sheet preparation requirements.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Deferred consideration payable stood at Rs 218.7 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Project-driven business model causes quarter-to-quarter financial variations due to execution stages and project mix.
- U.S. entity deferred due to balance sheet readiness.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rahul Kumar Paliwal (Shefa Family Office) asked about the post-2030 structure, U.S. exclusion, and Volteron IP.** Management answered that Volteron currently sits with the parent company (John Cockerill SA) to shield JCIL from costly R&D, but will be integrated into JCIL in due time; hydrogen remains outside JCIL; and the U.S. entity was deferred to 2028 to allow time for balance sheet readiness.
- **Rahul Kumar Paliwal asked about fundraising plans and M&A focus.** Management stated they are investigating all options to raise funds and equity for acquisitions in steel technologies where they are currently absent or weak.
- **Tushar Khurana asked about JVD and Volteron ownership and commercialization timelines.** Management stated JVD IP belongs to ArcelorMittal with JCIL expanding its licensing (noting the VAMA phase III project in China), while Volteron aims for its first pilot in 2027.
- **Tushar Khurana asked when JCIL will reach the scale of global players like SMS, Danieli, or Primetals.** Management stated those players took 50 years, while JCIL aims to close the gap between 2035 and 2040 leveraging Volteron and reaching the 2030 top-line targets.
- **Dishit Sanghvi asked about the rationale for omitting the North American subsidiary and its revenue contribution.** Management explained it was a balance sheet readiness issue on the promoter side; the U.S. entity contributes modest high-margin revenue (around 10% add-on potential).

### One-line takeaway
John Cockerill India reported strong YoY revenue growth for Q3 CY26, but incurred EBITDA losses due to upfront project mobilization and manpower expansion, while advancing its global integration (Project Vulcan) and green steel initiatives.
