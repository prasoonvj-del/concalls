# GEE Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY revenue and profit growth, secured strategic approvals, and outlined clear organic and inorganic growth plans.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 103 crore (up 30% YoY from Rs 79 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 8 crore (up 77% YoY from Rs 4.5 crore)
- EBITDA Margin: 7.8% (up 204 bps YoY from 5.7%)
- PBT: Rs 5.5 crore (up 318% YoY from Rs 1 crore)
- PBT Margin: 5.3% (up 365 bps YoY from 1.6%)
- Adjusted PAT: Rs 3.2 crore (up 223% YoY from Rs 1 crore)
- Adjusted PAT Margin: 3.1% (up 183 bps YoY from 1.2%)
- Interest cost / debt reduction: Interest cost ratio down from 2.4% to 1.8% YoY; working capital limits around Rs 100 crore with headroom.
- Guidance: Revenue target of Rs 1,000 crore by FY29-30.

### Highlights
- Secured a strategic approval from Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL) in the regulated nuclear sector.
- Supplied welding consumables for the commissioning of three Indian Navy warships (*INS Dunagiri*, *INS Agray*, and *INS Sanshodhak*).
- Commercial production for SAW wires and SAW flux started in Q1 FY27; flux-cored wire capacity set up, with commercial production starting in Q2 FY27.
- Signed a Joint Development (JD) agreement with a developer in the last financial year to monetize the large Thane land parcel (Wagle Industrial Estate) into commercial real estate, expecting cash flows of approximately Rs 400 crore over the next five years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to reach a turnover of Rs 1,000 crore by FY2029-30 organically and through product additions.
- Target to take EBITDA margins to a sustainable double-digit level (10% to 11% this year, eventually going up to 12%-13%).
- Total capex of Rs 30 crore to Rs 40 crore over the next 3-4 years to cover wire capacity expansion, new verticals (SAW wires, fluxes, flux-cored wire), and facility shifting. FY27 capex is estimated at Rs 5 crore to Rs 10 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry size is around Rs 12,000 crore to Rs 13,000 crore, with a significant unorganized sector share shifting toward organized players.
- Strong tailwinds from infrastructure boom, per capita steel consumption growth, railways (Vande Bharat, bullet trains, freight corridors), defence, and energy sectors.
- Exports are targeting Vietnam, Saudi Arabia, Russia (pending NAKS approval), and Middle Eastern countries (Bahrain, Muscat, Dubai). Zero duty expected in Europe next year under trade agreements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin improvement expected via economies of scale, better sourcing strategies, R&D formulation optimization, and strict operating cost control.
- Niche/specialised products contribute ~27% to 30% of the top line.
- India is competitive in stainless steel wire due to domestic availability of ores and steel mill wire rods.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current total capacity is 59,000 metric tonnes (electrode capacity partly unutilized; MIG wire capacity exhausted).
- Incremental capacity additions include SAW wires, SAW fluxes, and flux-cored wire (adds 1,000 metric tonnes by FY29, contributing >Rs 150 crore turnover).
- Electrode capacity expansion not planned until existing unutilized capacity reaches 90%-95% utilization (expected post-2029-30).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No existing term loans; operations use working capital limits of ~Rs 100 crore with comfortable headroom.
- Promoter share pledge is for a personal loan of Rs 40 crore taken for promoter/business settlement, which management expects to free up over the next 3 to 4 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Seasonality in the industry due to monsoons affecting Q1 and Q2 construction activity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Darshil Pandya asked about the confidence behind the Rs 1,000 crore revenue target given past stagnation.** Management (Umesh Agarwal) replied that past challenges are overcome, and the company has the capacity, capability, and R&D backing to achieve these figures.
2. **Praneeth Bommisetti inquired about wire vs. electrode capacity utilization and export feasibility.** Management clarified that electrode capacity is unutilized while wire capacity is fully utilized, and export orders are active in Vietnam, Saudi Arabia, and Russia.
3. **Nishita Sanklesha asked for a breakdown of the Rs 30-40 crore capex and peak revenue.** Management stated that peak revenue from the existing 71,000 MT capacity is ~Rs 850 crore, and the remaining target to reach Rs 1,000 crore will come from new verticals like SAW and flux-cored wires.
4. **Harshad T. asked about the percentage of revenue from shipbuilding and nuclear.** Management stated nuclear could reach at least 10% of business in coming years, and commercial shipbuilding is expected to account for 3% to 5% of revenue.
5. **Parimal Mithani asked about the promoter share pledge.** Management confirmed a pledge exists for a personal loan of Rs 40 crore towards business/promoter settlement, which will be released over the next 3 to 4 years.

### One-line takeaway
GEE Ltd. delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 30% revenue and 77% EBITDA growth, backed by strategic nuclear/defence approvals and an asset-monetization roadmap targeting Rs 1,000 crore revenue by FY30.
