# Kranti Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Meaningful sequential recovery in operating performance, revenue, gross margins, and profitability.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 25.16 crore (+16.4% YoY vs Rs 21.60 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 13.67 crore (+33.8% YoY); Gross margin: 54.3% (vs 47.2% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 3.80 crore (vs Rs 4.00 crore YoY; +32.2% QoQ from Rs 2.87 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 15.1% (vs 11.3% in Q1 FY27, +382 bps QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 PBDT:** Rs 290 lakhs (+56.5% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 PBT:** Rs 83 lakhs
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 56 lakhs (vs loss of Rs 5 lakhs in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 50.60 crore (+21.4% YoY vs Rs 41.00 crore)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 26.28 crore (+38.2% YoY); Gross margin: 51.9% (vs 45.6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 6.68 crore (vs Rs 7.23 crore YoY); EBITDA margin: 13.2% (vs 17.3% YoY)

### Highlights
- Entered the Defence category this financial year, adding to product and category diversification.
- Jaipur plant capacity utilization stands at ~65%, expected to reach break-even (>75%) by the end of Q3/Q4 FY27, contributing Rs 9-10 crore topline in FY27.
- *Note vs Record:* The transcript provides the exact financial breakdown (Rs 50.6 crore revenue, 21.4% YoY growth) supporting the steady H1 performance noted in earlier records.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects consistent two-digit revenue growth for the next 2-3 years, split roughly 50-50 between existing and new customers/segments.
- Management expects blended EBITDA margins to stabilize at around 17.5%-18% by the end of FY27 or early FY28.
- Management targets EV segment revenue contribution at 15%-16% for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Outlook remains encouraging across India's automotive, agricultural, and construction equipment sectors.
- Tractor industry volume growth projected to moderate to 1%-4% in FY27 following strong FY26 expansion.
- Construction and mining equipment industry volume growth projected at 8%-10% in FY27 by ICRA.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Overall profitability impacted by fixed cost structure, finance costs, and increasing depreciation costs.
- Gross margins expanded due to cost optimization, better inventory management, and operational efficiency.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY27 expansion plan is largely completed; a few remaining ordered machines will be added this fiscal year. Next 2-year expansion plan to be released by the end of the quarter.
- Capacity utilization: Pune plant at >75%, Jaipur plant at ~55%, resulting in a blended utilization of ~65%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory is high at over 141 business days; management targets a reduction of 25-30 days to bring it down to 121 or <100 days by the end of FY27.
- Debtors and creditors stated to be comfortably placed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Fixed cost overheads, finance costs, and increasing depreciation costs pressuring overall profitability.
- Elevated inventory levels (>141 business days).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhishek Verma (Cloud9 Family Offices) on international markets:** Management refrained from commenting on current international explorations, stating they are working on some areas.
- **Abhishek Verma on new categories and products:** Management noted entering the Defence sector this year and adding over 100 new products across two new categories in the last 2 years.
- **Abhishek Verma on Jaipur plant financials:** Management guided for a topline contribution of Rs 9-10 crore in FY27 and Rs 15-17 crore in FY28, with utilization expected to exceed 75% break-even soon.
- **Mayank Vivek on EV positioning:** Management explained that focusing on transmission, gearboxes, and hydraulic systems makes their product line resilient to EV/hybrid/hydrogen transitions, having entered the EV space in 2016.
- **Sachin Gupta on working capital:** Management acknowledged high inventory (>141 days) and committed to reducing it to below 100-121 days by the end of FY27 to free up cash flow.

### One-line takeaway
Kranti Industries delivered a sequential operating recovery in Q2 FY27 with improved margins and profitability, while aggressively targeting inventory reduction and sustainable double-digit growth.
