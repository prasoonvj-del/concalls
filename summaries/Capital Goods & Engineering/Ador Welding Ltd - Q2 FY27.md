# Ador Welding Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 23% YoY supported by robust demand across core end markets, though margins faced short-term raw material cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 345 crore (up ~23% YoY); H1 Revenue at Rs 654 crore (up 23% YoY).
- **EBITDA Growth:** Up 9% YoY in Q2; H1 EBITDA margin at 11.2%.
- **Gross Margin:** 34.2% in Q2.
- **Export Share:** 15% to 20% in H1.
- **Short-term Bank Borrowings:** Around Rs 28 crore as of 30th September.

### Highlights
- Company completed 75 years of operations; Board declared an interim dividend of Rs 7.5 per equity share.
- Growth driven by strong capital goods activity in infrastructure, power, oil & gas, railways, shipbuilding, defense, and heavy fabrication.
- Strategic focus on automation, expanding international reach (Middle East, Europe, U.S., Africa), improving product mix, and deepening direct customer engagement.
- Partnership with Liburdi Dimetrics brings orbital welding technology to power, nuclear, oil & gas, and shipbuilding sectors.
- Continuing to invest in a new consumables line as per previous capex plans.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of recovering gross margins and achieving long-term target EBITDA margins of 12% to 13%.
- Aspired to reach Rs 2,000 crore revenue by FY29; management states they are fairly on track.
- Expects double-digit volume growth (+10% and mid-teens) to continue into H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remains robust, driven by steel consumption across core sectors.
- Unorganized sector presence in consumables is noted, but the company focuses on engineered products requiring high-end approvals (oil & gas, shipbuilding).
- Rising labor costs and skilled welder shortages are driving customers to invest in automation and productivity solutions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin dipped to 34.2% in Q2 due to severe input cost inflation (primarily steel, copper, and electronics/PCBs) and product mix impact.
- Steel prices increased by 7% to 8% QoQ in Q2.
- Calibrated price increases are being implemented to pass on cost inflation, with normalization expected from Q3 onwards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Normal annual capex averages Rs 35 crore to Rs 40 crore.
- Capex is expected to step up to Rs 60 crore - Rs 65 crore to Rs 70 crore annually for FY27 and FY28 to support growth, before returning to normal levels.
- Expansion into international markets like Europe is underway, supplementing the main Dubai hub in the Middle East.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains strong; management does not anticipate equity dilution or major debt requirements for upcoming capex.
- Higher inventory levels and fluctuations in payables are attributed to order backlog and strategic steel purchasing amid availability concerns.
- Short-term borrowings include LCs and BGs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rapid and sporadic input cost inflation (steel and incoming logistics) impacting quarterly margins before lag-adjusted price hikes take effect.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rudraksh Raheja (ithought Financial Consulting):** Asked about the gross margin dip and sustainable EBITDA levels. Management attributed ~80% of the dip to severe cost inflation and stated the long-term EBITDA margin goal is 12% to 13%, with recovery expected in H2.
2. **Suraj Malu (Catamaran Ventures):** Inquired about volume growth and raw material breakdown. Management cited mid-teen volume growth and noted broad-based inflation across steel, copper, and electronics.
3. **Bhavya Gandhi (Bajaj Alternate Investment Management):** Asked about digital initiatives (WelDoS) and acquisition plans. Management clarified WelDoS provides welder data and that they are exploring small automation-focused acquisitions.
4. **Prasenjit Paul (Paul Asset):** Questioned the visibility of reaching the Rs 2,000 crore revenue target by FY29 and capex funding. Management confirmed they are on track and that internal accruals/strong balance sheet will fund the Rs 60-70 crore annual capex for the next 2 years without equity dilution.
5. **Dharmendra Grover (Helios Capital):** Inquired if steel price increases are temporary and pass-through status. Management stated pass-through is now catching up after a lag in Q2.

### One-line takeaway
Ador Welding delivered a strong 23% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by robust core demand, while management is actively addressing short-term steel inflation pressures through price hikes to steer margins back toward the 12%–13% EBITDA band.
