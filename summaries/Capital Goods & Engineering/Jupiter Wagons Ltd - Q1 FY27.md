# Jupiter Wagons Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Marked by strong revenue growth, strategic partnerships, and robust order inflows across segments despite a temporary transition dip in wagon production.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: INR 671 crore (+46% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 65 crore (+9% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: ~10%
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 26 crore
- PAT margin: 4%
- Fresh orders secured during quarter: INR 264 crore (JSW Rail Logistics and Central Warehousing Corporation) + INR 211 crore (JSW Port Logistics and Orissa Alloy Steel Private Limited)
- Order book segments: Railway wagons ~INR 3,000 crore; Wheelset business ~INR 700 crore; Commercial vehicle segment ~INR 500 crore; BESS ~INR 500 crore (Total order book around INR 4,53 crore / ~INR 4,000-4,500+ crore)
- BESS order book target: ~INR 1,000 crore by FY27; ~INR 5,000 crore over next 3 years
- Wheelset facility rated capacity: 100,000 wheelsets; Project capex: INR 2,600 crore (Jupiter's infusion reduced from INR 900 crore to INR 600 crore post INR 300 crore infusion by Lucchini RS and SIMEST)
- BESS project IRR: 15%+ (West Bengal State Electricity Board 15-year build-own-operate model for 100 MW and 400 MW projects)

### Highlights
- Acquired remaining 1.94% stake in Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory (JTRWF), bringing ownership to 100%.
- Entered a strategic partnership with Lucchini RS (Italy) and SIMEST (Italian government financial institute), where they will acquire a combined 25% stake in JTRWF for ~INR 290 crore.
- Stone India received RDSO approval for freight brake systems; commercial production commenced in July 2026.
- Jupiter Electric Mobility (JEM) secured 110 MW of BESS orders for FY27 via MoUs and won 2 standalone BESS projects in West Bengal (100 MW and 400 MW) valued at ~INR 400 crore under a 15-year build-own-operate model.
- ~80% of the current wagon order book consists of private wagons.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects 60%-70% of the current order book to be executed in FY27 itself.
- Stone India expected to turn EBITDA positive from Q3 FY27.
- JEM business expected to turn EBITDA positive from FY28 onwards.
- All joint ventures expected to report positive EBITDAs before the end of Q3.
- Odisha railway facility (JTRWF): Axle line commissioning targeted for FY27, wheel line commissioning for FY28; target revenue of INR 2,500 crore to INR 3,000 crore and EBITDA margin of at least 15% once fully operational (50% internal/domestic, 50% export).
- Passenger systems partnership expected to be announced before the end of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Railways maintains a loading target of 3.5 lakh million tonnes; demand for freight wagons remains strong.
- Private sector wagon ownership and leasing are growing opportunities.
- In the wheelset segment, India currently imports all passenger train wheelsets (Metro, Vande Bharat; 10,000 to 20,000 annually); total domestic market is 300,000 to 400,000 wheelsets annually.
- As speeds and loading capacities increase, the market is transitioning from cast wheels (limited to 100 km/h) to forged wheels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Wagon realization improved from an average of ~INR 38,00,000 in the same quarter last year to INR 41,50,000 in Q1 FY27.
- Non-railway side of the business has grown, carrying better margins.
- Mature wheelset and BESS businesses target 15%+ EBITDA margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Odisha wheelset facility rated capacity is 100,000 wheelsets with a total project capex of INR 2,600 crore. Majority of capex expected to be done by Q1 of next year.
- Lucchini RS and SIMEST equity infusion into JTRWF expected before the middle of next month (~INR 290-300 crore).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Pending wagons order book stands at close to 7,000 units.
- Funding for BESS projects (debt vs internal accruals) has not yet been firmed up.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Q1 FY27 saw a sequential dip in wagon production (1,141 units vs 1,347 units in Q4) due to a transition period and prototype approvals for new private wagon designs.
- Stone India may report slightly borderline negative numbers in Q2 FY27 before turning positive in Q3.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Segment-wise order book breakdown and execution:** Rehan Saiyyed (Sysatix Group / participant) asked for order book details. Management broke down the INR ~4,500 crore order book (Wagons INR 3,000 cr, Wheelset INR 700 cr, CV bodies INR 500 cr, BESS INR 500 cr) and guided to 60-70% execution in FY27.
- **Sequential dip in wagon production:** Rehan Saiyyed and Daksh Prashar asked about the QoQ revenue/production decline. Management explained it was due to a transition to new private wagon designs requiring prototype approvals.
- **Subsidiary EBITDA losses:** Koundinya Nimmagadda (Jefferies) questioned why subsidiaries reported losses dragging consolidated EBITDA below standalone. Management clarified Stone India turns profitable in Q3 post RDSO approval ramp-up, while JEM turns positive in FY28.
- **Odisha facility capacity and funding:** Balasubramanian A. (Arihant Capital) asked about capex and funding. Management confirmed Jupiter's equity is infused and foreign partner equity (~INR 300 crore) is incoming by mid-next month; rated capacity is 100,000 wheelsets.
- **Competition in wheelset segment:** Navin Sahadeo (ICICI Securities) asked about upcoming domestic competition. Management noted huge headroom, specialized technical/homologation requirements for passenger segments, and their partnership with Lucchini RS as a competitive moat.

### One-line takeaway
Jupiter Wagons reported a steady Q1 FY27 with a 46% YoY jump in revenue, driven by strong private wagon and diversified clean energy/wheelset momentum, while laying the groundwork for high-margin backward integration through its Odisha wheelset project and Stone India.
