# Rhi Magnesita India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust profitability outperformance, stable revenues above Rs 1,000 crore, and strong margin expansion driven by value-over-volume strategy.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Volume growth guidance of 7%–9% was left unquantified/withdrawn as management chose to balance value over volume; EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 13% (beaten at 14%).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more cautious on external volume growth due to competitive intensity and selective shedding of low-margin orders, but highly confident on value realization and flow control mix.
- New topics or risks: Mention of largest-ever coke oven order booked for silica production lines; initiation of Chiraipani quartzite mine operations; leadership transition with Abhishek Bajaj taking over as CFO.
- Last quarter's promises: MINPRO JV started first invoicing (delivering on Q4 FY27 commercial production expectation early); Chiraipani mine commenced operations as promised.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 1,030 crore (stable QoQ)
- **Revenue growth YoY:** 2% for H1 FY27 (Rs 2,044 crore)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 144 crore
- **EBITDA margin:** 14% (expanded by 332 bps YoY)
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 62 crore (up 62% YoY)
- **Cash and cash equivalents:** Rs 456 crore as of September 2026
- **Net debt to EBITDA:** ~0.25x (net cash)
- **Working capital intensity:** 38%
- **Operating cash flow:** Rs 99 crore in H1 FY27

### Highlights
- Secured the largest coke oven order in company history, booking silica production lines for more than 18 months.
- Commenced operations at the Chiraipani quartzite mine (40,000 tonnes annual capacity); Bhikampali mine expected to start next quarter.
- MINPRO JV raised its first invoicing, scaling up recycling and raw material resilience.
- Management transition: Azim Syed handed over the CFO role to Abhishek Bajaj.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its annual EBITDA margin guidance of 13%.
- Expects volume growth through a healthy pipeline of project and coke oven orders in H2.
- Aims to fully transition to captive quartzite mine resources by the next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment remains challenging with elevated freight, energy costs, and FX volatility.
- Crude steel production in India grew 2.8% in H1, while steel demand grew 7.2%.
- Capacity overhang in the refractory industry persists due to rapid scaling up against the 300 million tonne National Steel Policy target.
- Cement market demand remains challenging; management selectively avoided low-margin orders.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost pressures noted in fused magnesia, DBM, bauxite, resins, and ocean freight.
- Captive quartzite mining expected to provide a 3%–3.5% delta in EBITDA margins annually once fully ramped up.
- Improved product mix (higher flow control share sold in pieces) masked raw material cost percentages in the P&L.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex stood at Rs 34 crore, focused on equipment reliability, compliance, and technology transfers from the global parent.
- Rajgangpur plant capacity utilization will be boosted by the large coke oven silica orders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital intensity increased to 38% due to inventory build-up for raw material resilience and upcoming silica orders.
- Strong cash position with Rs 456 crore in cash and cash equivalents and positive operating cash flow of Rs 99 crore in H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties, ocean freight inflation, and foreign exchange volatility.
- Intense competition from domestic and Chinese players causing pricing pressure.
- Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) making European exports uncompetitive.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sahil Sanghvi (Monarch Networth Capital) asked about quartzite mine cost benefits:** Management stated it will yield a 3%–3.5% EBITDA delta and transition to fully captive by next fiscal year. (Management did not dodge).
- **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital) asked about volume growth guidance and capex:** Management explained the shift toward a value-over-volume balance and detailed capex priorities on technology transfers and compliance. (Management did not dodge).
- **Kunal Mehta (InCred Equities) asked about inventory build-up reasons:** Management attributed it to raw material cost inflation, preparations for large silica orders, and supply chain resilience. (Management did not dodge).
- **Rajesh Majumdar (360 ONE Capital) asked about flattish standalone revenues and market share:** Management explained the deliberate choice to step away from low-margin cement orders to preserve value. (Management did not dodge).
- **Nikhil Chandak (Shanghvi Family Office) asked about export potential:** Management noted North America and Europe are uncompetitive due to costs and CBAM, focusing medium-term potential on Middle East/Africa. (Management did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
RHI Magnesita India delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with a 14% EBITDA margin and 62% PAT growth, driven by a value-over-volume strategy, flow control mix enrichment, and early backward integration into captive mining.
