# Tega Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance across legacy Tega and Molycop businesses, driven by volume growth, margin expansion, and successful Molycop integration.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: INR 17.2 billion
- Consolidated adjusted EBITDA (before one-time expenses): INR 2.6 billion (margin: 15%)
- One-time acquisition and integration expenses: INR 1.9 billion
- Tega legacy revenue: INR 4.3 billion (up 21% YoY)
- Tega legacy EBITDA: INR 1 billion (up 42% YoY, margin: 22.1% vs 19.1% YoY)
- Tega Consumables revenue: INR 4 billion (up 36% YoY)
- Tega Consumables EBITDA: INR 1 billion (up 58% YoY, margin: 24.1% vs 20.9% YoY, up 320 bps)
- Tega Equipment revenue: INR 358 million (vs INR 643 million YoY); EBITDA broadly breakeven
- Molycop revenue (1 month, June 2026): INR 12.9 billion
- Molycop adjusted EBITDA (1 month): INR 1.6 billion (USD 17.2 million, margin: ~13%)
- Molycop one-time acquisition/integration costs: INR 1.85 billion (USD 19.5 million)
- Total order book (Consumable & Equipment): INR 12.3 billion (executable within one year: ~INR 9.6 billion)
- Total group debt: INR 112 billion (includes redeemable preference shares of ~INR 26 billion)
- Molycop net debt as of June 30, 2026: INR 63.66 billion (USD 672.5 million), down from INR 95.8 billion (USD 1.0 billion) as of March 31.
- Molycop 12-month historical EBITDA (ended June 2026): USD 191 million (up 11% YoY)
- Molycop 12-month historical volume (ended June 2026): 1.204 million tons (vs 1.223 million tons YoY)

### Highlights
- Completed the acquisition of Molycop on June 1, 2026; Q1 includes one month of Molycop's financial consolidation.
- Provisional purchase price accounting under IND-AS 103 resulted in goodwill of ~INR 50 billion (~USD 528 million).
- Chile plant soft commissioning targeted around January 2027, with commercial production starting March 2027 (subject to regulatory approvals).
- Molycop debt reduced by ~INR 22.25 billion (USD 235 million) at closing; total net debt declined by ~INR 32.18 billion (USD 340 million) during the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains long-term revenue growth guidance of ~15% CAGR for the Tega consumables business.
- Consolidated EBITDA margins expected to remain in the ~15% range for the full year.
- Molycop expected volume growth of ~5% and EBITDA growth of ~4% on a 10-month comparable basis.
- Molycop capex guidance: ~USD 28 million for the 10-month period; normalized capital expenditure to remain in the low-to-mid USD 30 million range for the next two years.
- Tega standalone capex (excluding Molycop) expected at ~USD 40 million for FY27 (including Chile).
- Full-year FY27 finance cost guidance for Tega: INR 110 crore to INR 120 crore; Molycop 10-month interest and principal payments expected at ~USD 70 million.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global gold market expected to grow at ~2.2% CAGR through FY30; copper demand projected at ~4.8% CAGR (blended ~3% CAGR).
- UNCTAD expects global copper demand to increase by >40% by 2040, requiring ~USD 250 billion of investment and ~18 new mines.
- Growth supported by high-performance hybrid liner solutions, throughput improvements, declining ore grades, and increased ore processing requirements.
- Freeport Indonesia's Grasberg Block Cave operation progressing through phased recovery; full capacity targeted by end of calendar year 2027.
- Panama's Cobre Panama mine approved to process stockpiled ore while long-term operating structure discussions continue (First Quantum reported 2.1 million tons of ore processed and ~3,200 tons of copper produced in Q1).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Tega legacy gross margins held at ~62% (vs 59% YoY) despite raw material volatility.
- Molycop gross margins at ~36%; ~85% of Molycop contracts are tied to steel indices, protecting gross margins against steel price volatility. Molycop internally focuses on profit per ton.
- Freight costs have risen due to container availability and shipping challenges, but both Tega and Molycop pass through cost increases (Tega with a ~1-quarter lag; Molycop through contract mechanisms and freight hedging).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Chile plant construction on track; soft commissioning in January 2027, commercial production by March 2027.
- Molycop sustaining/normalization capex expected in the low-to-mid USD 30 million range annually.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Group total debt stands at INR 112 billion (including INR 26 billion of redeemable preference shares).
- Molycop net debt closed the quarter at USD 672.5 million (INR 63.66 billion).
- Tega evaluating non-core assets (non-productive land parcels) to divest and use proceeds for debt reduction.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Equipment business faced revenue de-growth due to customer clearance delays and lower volumes impacting operating leverage.
- Potential local regulatory clearance dependencies for the Chile plant commissioning.
- Logistics challenges regarding container availability and mother vessel frequencies, though mitigated by pass-through mechanisms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankur Periwal (Axis Capital) asked about cross-selling revenue synergies between Tega and Molycop.** Management stated it is early days, teams are strategizing, and revenue ramp-up is expected from Q3/Q4 FY27 onwards. *(Management did not dodge; gave a realistic timeline).*
- **Vikas Gupta asked about debt levels at Tega and Molycop.** Management detailed group debt at INR 112 billion (including INR 26 billion preference shares) and Molycop net debt at USD 672.5 million. *(Direct answer).*
- **Varun Jain asked about Molycop volume numbers and Chinese competition in LatAm.** Management reported 12-month volume of 1.204 million tons (ended June 2026) and stated Molycop competes on shorter lead times, strong technical support, and reliability rather than solely on price. *(Direct answer).*
- **Chirag (Centrum Broking) asked if consumable annual EBITDA margins of 20% to 23% are sustainable.** Management confirmed they pass through cost inflation successfully with about a one-quarter lag, maintaining gross margins around 62%. *(Direct answer).*
- **Rushabh Doshi asked about non-core asset divestment for debt reduction.** Management confirmed they are evaluating non-productive land parcels to monetize and pay down debt. *(Direct answer).*

### One-line takeaway
Tega Industries delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with consolidated revenue of INR 17.2 billion, bolstered by robust legacy consumable growth and the initial consolidation of Molycop, while executing deleveraging and integration synergies.
