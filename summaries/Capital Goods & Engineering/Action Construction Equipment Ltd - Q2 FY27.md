# Action Construction Equipment Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved best-ever Q2 performance with 20.5% revenue growth and expanded EBITDA margins despite inflationary headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Initiated FY27 revenue growth guidance of 18%-20% (previously refrained from giving specific percentage growth).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious/wait-and-see" to "constructive and disciplined" with robust demand post-September.
- **New topics:** Steel prices reached multi-year highs in October, necessitating potential price actions in Q3/early Q4.
- **Last quarter's promises:** KATO JV commenced operations (as planned); defense deliveries commenced (as planned).

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** INR 932 crore (up 20.5% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** INR 180 crore (up 30.2% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 19.31% (expanded 143 bps YoY).
- **PBT (before exceptional items):** INR 167 crore (up 35% YoY).
- **PAT:** INR 164.3 crore (up 597 bps margin expansion to 17.62%, includes INR 50 crore exceptional gain from slump sale to ACE KATO).
- **Segment Revenue (Cranes/Material Handling/Construction):** INR 788 crore (up 13.54% YoY).
- **Agri Segment Revenue:** INR 47.49 crore (~6% of total).
- **Volumes (Cranes/Material Handling/Construction):** 2,812 units (up 20% YoY).
- **Net Debt:** Not explicitly stated as a single figure; management noted borrowing cost ~7% and alpha generation of 3-4% on investments.

### Highlights
- **Defense:** Secured large order for backhoe loaders from Border Roads Organisation; defense business (RTFLT, telehandlers, heavy recovery cranes) contributing 5-6% of revenue.
- **KATO JV:** Operational; targeting INR 150 crore revenue in FY28 from upgraded machines, with potential for export-specific models by FY29/FY30.
- **Pricing:** Implemented 7%-8% price increase across equipment portfolio by end of September to offset raw material costs.
- **Inventory:** Bloated inventory (INR 832 crore) attributed to defense order requirements (inspections/batches); expected to normalize by Q4.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Anticipates 18%-20% growth for FY27.
- **Margins:** Expects to sustain EBITDA margins via cost efficiencies and product mix improvements.
- **Pricing:** Planning further price actions in late Q3/early Q4 to counter recent steel price spikes.
- **Seasonality:** Reiterated that 55%-60% of annual revenue typically occurs in H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains robust post-September; October sales were "very good."
- Government-led capex, logistics modernization, and "Make in India" remain primary growth drivers.
- Expects backhoe loader segment to show stronger performance in Q3.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs (steel) rose 10%-12% in October; steel prices reached INR 66-67/kg (up from INR 48-50/kg earlier in the year).
- Management aims to maintain ~15% EBITDA margin at the JV level.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Current Utilization:** ~55% in cranes/construction; ~25%-30% in agri.
- **Capex Guidance:** ~INR 130 crore for FY27 (includes ~INR 40-50 crore for new plant, ~INR 30-40 crore for automation, and ~INR 130 crore for land registry/payments).
- **Future Capex:** Potential ~INR 400 crore for tower crane capacity expansion (decision pending for Q4 or Q1 FY28).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash/investments: ~INR 1,570 crore as of September 2026.
- 70%-80% of investment portfolio is liquid.
- Working capital: Short-term borrowings increased due to seasonal inventory buildup; expected to normalize by December/March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Inflation:** Recent rally in domestic steel prices (multi-year highs) exerting pressure on input costs.
- **Regulatory:** Anti-dumping duty status remains uncertain; management is waiting for judicial outcomes in the next 1-2 months.
- **Execution:** Price hikes are "painful" and "unsettling" for repeat customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya (Old Bridge):** Questioned realization dip YoY. Management clarified it was due to product mix and noted 7%-8% price increase implemented by September.
- **Preet Patani (InCred):** Asked about "Others" segment. Management confirmed it is primarily exports (approx. INR 40-45 crore).
- **Richa Agarwal:** Asked about KATO JV revenue potential. Management guided to INR 100 crore in FY28, growing to INR 300-400 crore medium-term.
- **Akshit Sethi:** Questioned inventory/borrowing increase. Management explained it is due to defense order batch requirements and seasonal buildup, expected to normalize by Q4.
- **Kaushik Poddar:** Asked about export potential. Management sees potential for INR 200-600 crore from exports by FY30.

### One-line takeaway
ACE delivered a strong Q2 with 20% revenue growth and margin expansion, while navigating steel price volatility and scaling defense and KATO JV initiatives.
