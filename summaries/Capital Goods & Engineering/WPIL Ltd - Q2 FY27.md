# WPIL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue decline in domestic operations and project lumpiness offset by management's confidence in a strong H2 recovery and robust order book.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue (Q2):** Rs 354 crore (-17% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue (Q2):** Rs 118 crore (-33% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Revenue (H1):** Rs 855 crore (+6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (H1):** Rs 123 crore (-5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (H1):** 14.33%.
*   **PAT (H1):** Rs 93 crore (+20% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin (H1):** 10.83%.
*   **Domestic Order Book:** Rs 2,423 crore.
*   **International Order Book:** Rs 2,686 crore.
*   **Project Order Backlog:** Rs 4,079 crore.
*   **Product Order Backlog:** ~Rs 1,000 crore.

### Highlights
*   Domestic project division revenue declined to Rs 57 crore in Q2 (vs Rs 90 crore YoY).
*   International business H1 revenue grew 38% YoY to Rs 626 crore.
*   Product division revenue was Rs 62 crore in Q2 (vs Rs 86 crore YoY).
*   Management stated that domestic product dispatches were deferred due to heavy monsoon in September, which is expected to even out in H2.
*   West Bengal government funds (approx Rs 300 crore+) are expected to be released starting the week following the call.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects a strong H2 performance driven by project commissioning, commencement of O&M activities, and recovery in the water infrastructure business.
*   Target EBITDA margin band remains 15-20%.
*   Management expects to exceed last year's domestic product business numbers.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Domestic thermal power sector is identified as a key growth segment with significant order potential.
*   Middle East (MENA) geopolitical flux is impacting new contract finalizations and dispatches.
*   Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) focus has shifted to project commissioning; fund releases are now linked to commissioning milestones.
*   South African water infrastructure market remains a key focus area.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Management maintains that gross margins are strong and fluctuations are due to revenue seasonality rather than structural issues.
*   Projects include cost pass-through/variation clauses to mitigate raw material price volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Management is actively looking for M&A opportunities, specifically in the U.S.
*   Company is restructuring and increasing stake in African subsidiaries (path to 100% in PCI Africa over 3 years).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   International working capital cycles are described as "very good" and are offsetting domestic cash flow challenges.
*   Management noted that funds for smaller vendors in West Bengal were disbursed last month, with expectations for company-specific funds to follow.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical flux in the Middle East affecting project timelines.
*   Execution delays in West Bengal (JJM) due to payment issues.
*   Lumpiness in project execution cycles (4-year project tenures).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Jainam Doshi (Kriis PMS) on African execution dip:** Management clarified that project execution is lumpy due to 4-year project cycles; H2 and next year are expected to see significant traction.
*   **Deepak (Svan Investment) on JJM payments:** Management confirmed that West Bengal funds are expected to be released starting the following week, which will trigger accelerated execution.
*   **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co) on margin restoration:** Management expressed confidence in returning to the 17-18% EBITDA margin band in H2.
*   **Abhishek Taparia (Emkay Global) on capacity constraints:** Management stated there are no capacity constraints, but growth is dependent on the recovery of the MENA region and domestic project execution.

### One-line takeaway
WPIL faces near-term domestic execution headwinds and revenue lumpiness, but management maintains a positive outlook for H2, driven by a strong order book and expected fund releases in West Bengal.
