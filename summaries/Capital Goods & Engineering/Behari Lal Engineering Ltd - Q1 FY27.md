# Behari Lal Engineering Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-11 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue, volumes, margins, and PAT grew YoY, driven by a higher-value product mix and strong demand.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 151.7 crore (up 18% YoY)
- Total volume: 22,095 tonnes (up 13.4% YoY, down 2% QoQ; own manufactured volume up 12% QoQ to 15,847 tonnes)
- Gross margin: 48.9% (up 146 bps YoY)
- EBITDA (including other income): Rs 29.7 crore (up 23.4% YoY, margin 19.6%, up 85 bps YoY)
- Operating EBITDA (excluding other income): Rs 27.6 crore (up 20.6% YoY, margin 18.2%, up 39 bps YoY)
- PAT: Rs 19.2 crore (up 24.5% YoY, margin 12.7%, up 65 bps YoY)
- EBITDA per tonne: ~Rs 13,457 (up 8.8% YoY)
- High-value product share: 60.4% of revenue (against 55.7% YoY and 57.8% in FY26)
- Order book: Rs 162 crore / 13,138 tonnes as of June 30, 2026
- Capacity utilization: 90.5% overall (94% melt shop, 87.5% rolling mill)
- Net worth: Rs 325 crore as of June 30, 2026 (pre-IPO)
- Cash and bank balances: Rs 52 crore as of June 30, 2026
- Gross borrowings: Rs 11 crore (working capital lines); Net debt: Negative (Net cash)
- Total planned Capex for FY27: Rs 80 crore (Rs 5 crore spent in Q1)

### Highlights
- First earnings call as a listed company following an IPO of Rs 301 crore at Rs 285 per share (listed August 19, 2026), featuring Rs 93 crore of fresh capital.
- Unit 3 is under construction; expected to commence in Q1 of next financial year.
- High-value product mix improved to 60.4%, driven by a reduction in low-value job work and commodity alloy bars.
- Secured initial orders and approvals from major defense/PSUs (e.g., BDL, NPCIL).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets reaching ~70% high-value product mix in the near future.
- Operating EBITDA margins expected to grow over the next 2-3 years.
- Defense sector revenue expected to scale up nicely starting next financial year (FY28).
- Unit 3 commercial production targeted for Q1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Steel industry demand is robust, with India targeting 300 million tonnes by 2030.
- Thermal power sector customers have provided order visibility until 2035; thermal power revenue contribution is 10.5%.
- ICDP (indefinite chilled double poured rolls) and HSS (high-speed steel) rolls are almost entirely imported in India (approx 3.5 lakh in FY26), presenting a large import-substitution opportunity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Scrap prices remained range-bound during the quarter.
- Power and fuel costs declined to Rs 8.9 crore (5.9% of revenue vs 6.3% YoY and 6.6% in FY26), aided by mix and solar initiatives.
- Working capital days increased slightly to 94 days due to higher inventory (WIP in rolls/castings and scrap stock).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total FY27 capex targeted at Rs 80 crore (funded via IPO proceeds, internal accruals, and ground solar power plant).
- Unit 3 under construction (foundry division focusing on centrifugal casting for ICDP and HSS rolls).
- Adding CNC machining and finishing facilities for forward integration.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Pre-IPO net worth at Rs 325 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- Gross debt stands at Rs 11 crore (working capital lines); debt-to-equity at 0.03x.
- Cash and bank balances at Rs 52 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Defense and specialized import-substitution sectors involve a very long customer approval and prototype cycle before mass production.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital) on growth drivers with capacity utilization already at 90-91%:** Management answered that growth will be driven by product mix optimization (shifting to high-value products) in FY27 and the addition of Unit 3 melting capacity next year.
2. **Deepak Poddar on Unit 3 capex and timeline:** Management stated exact capex details are restricted for exchange filings, but total target capex for FY27 is Rs 80 crore, with Unit 3 expected to commence in Q1 FY28.
3. **Deepak Poddar on defense sector entry:** Management noted initial orders and prototyping have started with BDL and NPCIL, with meaningful commercial revenues targeted for next year (FY28).
4. **Vatsal Mehta on centrifugal casting update:** Management confirmed orders for vertical and horizontal centrifugal casting equipment for Unit 3 are placed to manufacture ICDP and HSS rolls.
5. **Shweta Dikshit (Systematix Group) on entry barriers for ICDP/HSS rolls:** Management stated that the combination of century-old family trade links, existing customer relationships, and internal metallurgical/foundry capabilities act as strong entry barriers.

### One-line takeaway
Behari Lal Engineering reported steady Q1 FY27 growth backed by a richer high-value product mix, near-debt-free financials, and ongoing expansion into import-substituted rolls and defense segments.
