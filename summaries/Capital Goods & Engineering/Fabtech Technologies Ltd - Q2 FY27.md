# Fabtech Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a strong sequential recovery in Q2, with EBITDA more than doubling and a robust order book.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 92.09 crore (up 22.8% QoQ)
- **Q2 Total Income:** Rs 975.4 million / Rs 97.54 crore (up 24.8% QoQ)
- **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 15.57 crore / Rs 155.7 million (up 110% QoQ)
- **Q2 EBITDA Margin:** 16% (expanded 647 bps QoQ from 9.5%)
- **Q2 Reported PAT:** Rs 150.2 million / Rs 15.02 crore (includes net-of-tax exceptional item of Rs 65.9 million)
- **Q2 Adjusted PAT:** Rs 84.5 million / Rs 8.45 crore (up nearly 100% QoQ)
- **Q2 Adjusted PAT Margin:** 9.2% (expanded 354 bps QoQ from 5.6%)
- **Order Book:** Over Rs 900 crore
- **H1 Operating Cash Flow:** Approx. Rs 41 crore
- **Gross Receivables (September end):** Approx. Rs 235 crore (Net receivables: Rs 226 crore after ECL provisioning; Overdue: Rs 81 crore including Rs 25 crore retention)
- **Land Purchase (Khalapur):** Rs 18.6 crore

### Highlights
- Q2 performance reflected improved project execution, higher operating leverage, and better absorption of the cost base.
- Explored and entered new markets with a new turnkey design and build project in the CIS region.
- Saudi Arabia revenue reached Rs 462 million (up 18.5% YoY); Africa (Morocco, Kenya, Algeria) contributed ~Rs 470 million combined; India home base stands at 16% of revenue.
- Faced geopolitical uncertainty and freight volatility across GCC markets in H1; UAE team kept all sites running with enhanced security measures added to installation costs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of an adjusted PAT margin of 8% to 9% for the full year FY27.
- Targeting 25% to 30% year-on-year revenue growth over the next 3 to 5 years.
- Expecting to close the European (Italy) acquisition within the next six months or Q1 of next year (subject to due diligence and approvals).
- Expecting JV operational details and formal announcements in Saudi Arabia regarding non-pharmaceutical sectors within the next quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global turnkey pharma and biotech projects represent an ~US$180 billion market, growing at close to 8% CAGR. Serviceable markets in GCC, Africa, and MENA are US$28 billion to US$30 billion.
- Saudi Arabia plans over US$15 billion of pharma manufacturing investments (Vision 2030); Africa plans over US$60 billion until 2040.
- Hot pipeline stands between Rs 3,600 crore to Rs 4,000 crore, with an expected conversion of Rs 800 crore to Rs 850 crore over the next 12 months across the Middle East and Africa.
- Expanding into adjacencies: cell and gene therapy, high containment, data centres, veterinary, medical devices, food and beverage, electrical industries, lens manufacturing, and diaper manufacturing. Data centre contracts offer Fabtech an addressable scope of ~30%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices and freight volatility impacted H1 costs; enhanced security measures for GCC sites also added to installation costs.
- Alternative sourcing and localization strategies are targeted to add 2% to 3% to margins over the next 2 to 3 years.
- Certain reference projects are taken at lower margins to enter new geographies and build proof references.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- IPO proceeds utilization (working capital, general corporate purpose, inorganic activity) expected to complete by the end of FY27.
- Khalapur land (Rs 18.6 crore) will be utilized for the Fabtech ecosystem build; clear visibility expected by H2 results.
- Working on operational opportunities in Europe targeted for Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- International projects are backed by lines of credit and letters of credit (LCs), securing receivables.
- Other current assets increased largely due to vendor advances (approx. Rs 38 crore increase) toward H2 execution.
- Total gross receivables at ~Rs 235 crore include Rs 145 crore not due, Rs 81 crore overdue (including Rs 25 crore retention), and ~Rs 50 crore being actively pursued for collection in Q3 and Q4.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions and freight volatility in the GCC market.
- Slowness on collections in certain areas due to geopolitical uncertainty, though largely mitigated by LC backing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Milan Deep Jain (Jai Securities) asked about the implied H2 growth required to meet annual guidance and status of the Italy acquisition.** Management responded that seasonality and execution weighting toward H2 support the target of 25%–30% YoY growth, and the Italian acquisition is on track to close in the next 6 months or Q1 next year.
2. **Milan Deep Jain asked about related-party sales commissions paid to G7 Universal LLC.** Management clarified that these are conversion fees paid for valuable international project introductions and partner influence, and such payments will continue.
3. **Maitri Shah asked about gross/EBITDA margin scaling and localization benefits.** Management stated that localization and alternative sourcing are targeted to incrementally add 2%–3% to margins over the next 2 to 3 years.
4. **Niraj Chhajer asked about the composition and commercial clearance of the Rs 900 crore order book.** Management stated that approximately Rs 700 crore out of the Rs 900 crore order book are commercially cleared and active on the floor for detailed engineering.
5. **Amit Mehendale asked about competitive intensity and competitors.** Management noted main competitors include Telstar, Jacobs, and Exyte Engineering, but highlighted Fabtech's integrated in-house capabilities (process, air, water, and validation under one roof) as a key differentiator.
6. **Amit Mehendale asked about long-term ROE targets.** Arihant Dhariwal stated that current ROE is ~9%, and management targets reaching 30% or more over 2 to 3 years as the company scales.

### One-line takeaway
Fabtech Technologies reported a strong Q2 recovery with EBITDA more than doubling QoQ and a robust order book exceeding Rs 900 crore, maintaining its full-year margin and growth targets despite regional geopolitical headwinds in H1.
