# Oswal Pumps Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Margins compressed sequentially due to higher raw material costs, employee additions for EPC scale-up, and provisions, though sequential revenue growth was achieved.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Not explicitly updated with new hard percentage numbers for the full year, but management stated revenue will grow 10%-15% over last year (vs previous 20%-25% guidance) and explicitly backed off previous full-year margin targets.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more cautious due to continued delays in PM-KUSUM 2.0 and execution challenges in new rooftop/EPC state markets.
- New topics or risks: Proposed setting up a 1.2 GW TOPCon solar cell manufacturing facility via unutilized IPO proceeds reallocation and debt; increased debtor provisioning under accounting standards. Old risks around Magel Tyala pricing and delayed collections persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Q1 promises of 20%-25% revenue growth were walked back as H1 targets were missed; execution in new states took longer than expected.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 506.3 crore (Up 6.9% QoQ)
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 57.5 crore (Margin: 11.3%, down 437 bps QoQ)
- **PAT:** Rs 39.6 crore (Margin: 7.7%, down 343 bps QoQ)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 531 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.30x
- **Net Debt-to-Operating EBITDA:** 2.02x (annualized)
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 257 days (vs 244 days as of June 30, 2026)
- **Receivable Days:** 229 days
- **Inventory Days:** 56 days (vs 43 days in Q1 FY27)
- **Solar Pump Order Book:** 9,487 pumps
- **Solar EPC Order Book:** ~143 megawatt (with a pipeline of 168 megawatt)
- **Solar Cell Facility Capex:** Rs 455.85 crore total (Rs 159.85 crore via IPO reallocation, Rs 296 crore via term debt)

### Highlights
- Proposed setting up a 1.2 GW TOPCon solar cell manufacturing facility at Karnal through subsidiary Oswal Solar Energy Private Limited, expected to commence commercial operations in April 2028.
- Reprioritized capex: deferred the remaining 500 MW module expansion, dropped 300 MW of the 1,500 MW EVA encapsulant expansion, and dropped aluminium frame manufacturing.
- Received Rs 208 crore against outstanding receivables from the Maharashtra government in early October 2026.
- Diversifying into rooftop solar, utility-scale solar, and C&I solar EPC across states like Bihar, Telangana, and Rajasthan.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue to grow 10%-15% over last year's Rs 2,000 crore base.
- Operating EBITDA margins in Q2 are considered the bottom; management expects sequential improvement in Q3 and Q4.
- PM-KUSUM 2.0 expected to launch within the current calendar year (by December).
- Long-term vision of Rs 4,000 crore to Rs 4,500 crore revenue by 2030 remains intact.
- Steady-state consolidated EBITDA margins expected between 20% and 25% after the solar cell plant becomes operational.
- Non-government business targeted to reach 25% of total business in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PM-KUSUM 2.0 rollout delayed slightly due to government implementation of unique product requirements; industry expects rollout within calendar year 2026.
- Magel Tyala scheme coming to an end.
- Rooftop solar payment cycles expected to range between 90 and 120 days.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin fell 437 bps QoQ to 11.3%.
- ~210 bps decline driven by higher raw material costs (aluminium frames, glass, copper, zinc, steel) and subsidiary Walso Solar Solutions fulfilling older orders at lower input costs.
- ~227 bps decline driven by higher employee costs for solar EPC workforce scale-up, project-related opex, and higher debtor provisioning.
- In-house solar cell manufacturing is expected to meet 75% of internal requirements and drive an absolute EBITDA margin improvement of 6% to 7% once operational.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total solar cell facility capex: Rs 455.85 crore (Rs 159.85 crore reallocated from unutilized IPO proceeds; Rs 296 crore via in-principle sanctioned term debt).
- Existing solar module capacity stands at ~570 MW with an additional 1,000 MW under implementation.
- Planned solar cell commercial production start date: April 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 531 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Working capital stretched: cash conversion cycle rose to 257 days; inventory days increased to 56 days; receivable days stable at 229 days.
- Management expects debtor days to improve from ~225-226 days down to 150-160 days by the end of Q4 FY27, supported by government pressure to clear old dues before new schemes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in the rollout of PM-KUSUM 2.0.
- Elevated raw material price volatility (copper, aluminium, glass, zinc, steel).
- Extended government receivable collection cycles stretching working capital.
- Execution risks and lower initial margins in newly entered state rooftop/EPC markets (e.g., Bihar).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pranshu Bansal (Individual Investor) asked why the company missed its 10%-15% QoQ revenue growth guidance for Q2.** Management explained that execution in new states (Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana) takes time due to caution around collections, pushing revenues forward.
2. **Karan Kamdar (Choice Institutional Equities) asked about revenue benefits and EBITDA margin expansion from the new solar cell plant.** Management clarified there is no direct top-line revenue as it is for internal consumption, but it will drive an absolute 6% to 7% EBITDA margin improvement by bypassing external supplier markups.
3. **Sanyam Dhoka asked about the collection status of the Rs 208 crore received in October and expected receivable normalization.** Management confirmed the entire Rs 208 crore came from Maharashtra, and debtor days are expected to improve to 150–160 days by Q4 FY27.
4. **Arjun Tambe asked if the 2030 revenue target of Rs 4,000-4,500 crore and steady-state 20%-25% EBITDA margins are intact.** Management affirmed the targets, stating consolidated EBITDA margins will hit 20%-25% once the solar cell plant is operational.
5. **Kunal Bhalgat asked about export market developments.** Management stated the company has historically not focused heavily on exports but is now increasing focus and will provide visibility in 3-4 months.
6. **Ramesh asked for FY27 turnover and profit expectations after a challenging H1.** Management stated revenue will grow 10%-15% over last year's ~Rs 2,000 crore base, and Q2 margins represent the bottom with sequential improvements expected in Q3 and Q4.

### One-line takeaway
Oswal Pumps faced a difficult H1 with margin compression and delayed government schemes, but management is pivoting capital toward backward integration (solar cell manufacturing) and EPC diversification to recover profitability in H2.
