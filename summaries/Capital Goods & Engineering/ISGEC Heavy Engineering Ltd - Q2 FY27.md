# ISGEC Heavy Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, all-time high order books, and improved margins across segments despite minor commodity volatility.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,457 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **Standalone Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,521 crore
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 3,010 crore (up 35% YoY)
- **Standalone Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 3,106 crore
- **Standalone PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 154 crore (up 39% YoY)
- **Standalone Profit from Business Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 124 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **Standalone PBT (H1 FY27):** Rs 277 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **Standalone Profit from Business Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 244 crore (up 44% YoY)
- **Standalone Order Booking (H1 FY27):** Rs 4,844 crore
- **Standalone Orders in Hand (as of September 30, 2026):** Rs 8,757 crore (all-time high)
- **Consolidated Orders in Hand (as of September 30, 2026):** Rs 10,273 crore (all-time high)
- **Consolidated Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,860 crore (up 10% YoY)
- **Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 3,853 crore (up 25% YoY)
- **Consolidated PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 102 crore (up 100% YoY restated)
- **Consolidated PBT (H1 FY27):** Rs 155 crore (up 62% YoY restated)
- **Export Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 172 crore (11% of total income)
- **Export Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 557 crore (18% of total income)
- **Manufacturing EBIT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 13.7%
- **Project Business EBIT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 5.7%
- **Standalone Net Borrowings:** Rs 206 crore (down from Rs 381 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- **Consolidated Net Borrowings:** Rs 156 crore (down from Rs 476 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- **Cumulative Capex (H1 FY27):** Rs 199 crore out of total planned Rs 502 crore
- **Isgec Hitachi Zosen Order Book:** Rs 1,161 crore (including Rs 800 crore export orders)
- **Philippines Ethanol Segment Loss (H1 FY27):** Rs 164 crore (including interest, depreciation, and forex loss)
- **Philippines Quarterly Depreciation:** Rs 8 crore

### Highlights
- Execution proceeded well in both industrial projects and manufacturing segments.
- Manufacturing EBIT margin (13.7%) outperformed the guided 12%–13% range; project EBIT margin (5.7%) neared the 6% target.
- Standalone fund position improved by Rs 175 crore in H1 FY27, funding the Rs 199 crore capex out of internal accruals.
- Philippines ethanol plant operated at 65–70% capacity on molasses feedstock (after sugarcane crushing ended April 20, 2026); currently undergoing seasonal maintenance.
- Saraswati Sugar Mills faced lower revenues due to lower cane availability last year, but benefits from firm sugar realizations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 standalone revenue to grow by about 15% to 17%.
- Manufacturing margins are expected to stay between 12% and 13%; project margins are expected to improve to around 6% by year-end.
- Ongoing Rs 502 crore capex program is expected to yield incremental annual revenues of about Rs 1,200 crore (major portion starting next year and following years, fully materializing by FY29). Management is evaluating potential top-ups to this capex.
- Isgec Hitachi Zosen full-year turnover and profit are expected to be 7-8% and 5-7% higher than last year, respectively.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic market is strong across all product lines; healthy export inquiries from Latin America and Africa.
- Weaker Indian rupee is supporting export competitiveness.
- Demand is robust across diverse sectors including automobiles, oil and gas, petrochemicals, cement, and steel.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity prices (steel, copper, aluminium, nickel) fluctuated up to 10% due to Middle East conflicts, causing a small adverse impact on profitability, though prices eased slightly in October.
- Shipping and logistics saw delays, reduced container availability, and higher freight rates/surcharges, absorbed largely through normal contingency provisions without significant supply disruptions.
- Risk management includes back-to-back supplier offers and rate contracts covering 50%–60% raw material costs in manufactured products.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total planned capex of Rs 502 crore across presses and machine building (Bhartoli), skids and modules (Dahej SEZ), casting facilities, tube/pipe shops (Rattangarh), and standard mechanical presses (Bawal) is largely on schedule and being funded via internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone net borrowings reduced to Rs 206 crore (vs Rs 381 crore on March 31, 2026).
- Consolidated net borrowings reduced to Rs 156 crore (vs Rs 476 crore on March 31, 2026).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing Middle East conflicts caused up to 10% commodity price fluctuations and higher logistics/shipping rates.
- Philippines ethanol subsidiary incurred a Rs 164 crore loss in H1 FY27 (driven by interest, depreciation, and a Rs 41 crore forex loss).
- Lower sugarcane availability in Haryana/Punjab expected for the upcoming season.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Manish Goyal (Thinqwise Wealth Managers) asked about guidance conservatism given H1 growth:** Management clarified that the higher H1 base (Rs 3,000 crore vs Rs 2,200 crore last year) means H2 implies a steady run-rate increase towards Rs 6,200 crore annual run rate based on current orders.
- **Manish Goyal asked about Philippines depreciation and Q3/Q4 outlook:** Management stated depreciation is normalized at ~Rs 8 crore per quarter; Q3 will be weak due to maintenance, but Q4 anticipates ~90% capacity utilization with sugarcane crushing.
- **Digant Haria (Systematix) asked if short-cycle, technology-oriented project strategy is normalized:** Management confirmed they will avoid long-duration or civil-heavy projects, focusing on technology-linked, shorter-cycle projects to reduce risk and improve cash flows.
- **Rabindra Nath Nayak (SBICap Securities) asked for product-wise revenue breakup:** Management declined to provide product-wise percentages as they vary annually and quarterly.
- **Sandeep Baid asked about Philippines forex losses:** Management specified the forex loss was Rs 41 crore for the quarter out of the total Rs 60 crore PBIT loss.

### One-line takeaway
ISGEC reported robust H1 FY27 performance with standalone revenue up 35%, all-time high order books crossing Rs 10,273 crore consolidated, and halved net debt, though profitability was weighed down by forex and operational losses in its Philippines ethanol subsidiary.
