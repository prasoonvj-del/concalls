# Sealmatic India Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-12 · Capital Goods & Engineering*

**Quarter:** FY25-26 (Yearly Earnings Call, June 10, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited resilience, stable demand, growing order books, and expected margin recovery despite geopolitical headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 103 crore (vs Rs 101 crore in FY25, up 2% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 18.38 crore
- EBITDA margin: 17.36% (vs 24% in the previous year)
- Profit before tax (PBT): Rs 14 crore (approx 14% of total revenue)
- Export contribution: 54.36% of revenue (INR 40.29 crore via distributors, INR 14.5 crore via OEMs, INR 1 crore via end users)
- Domestic contribution: 45.64% of revenue (INR 36.25 crore via OEMs, INR 11.26 crore via end users)
- Exhibition costs: INR 5 crore for 14 exhibitions in FY26
- API seal project investments: INR 8 crore (supplying below cost of raw material for installed base)
- Critical API seals secured/executed: 916 seals in UAE, Saudi, Oman, Kuwait, Iraq (686 supplied, 230 under execution)
- Mongol project: 118 seals supplied, 53 under execution

### Highlights
- Achieved ISO 3834-2 certification for welding processes (total 16 quality certifications now).
- Secured major orders for Kalvari-class submarines, critical power projects (660 and 800 MW), and oil & gas in India and the Middle East.
- Participated in 14 international exhibitions in FY26; plans to reduce participation to 5 exhibitions in FY27 to save costs.
- Expanding manufacturing unit 3.
- Inventory rose to INR 62 crore due to delayed execution from geopolitical situations and rare earth material injunctions from China.

### Guidance & outlook
- Revenue growth guidance: Expect to grow by 15% in FY27.
- EBITDA margin guidance: Expect to achieve 23% to 24% in FY27 due to lower exhibition participation and tapering of subsidized API seal spending.
- Replacement aftermarket revenue from supplied API seals expected to start kicking in from FY27 (specifically noted April 2027 onwards for earlier batches, though Middle East commissioning is deferred by 6 to 9 months).
- Order book: Better on a period-to-period comparison (June 2026 vs June 2025).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global demand for mechanical seals is stable, driven by India, Europe, Middle East, and North America.
- Middle East projects (UAE, Saudi, Oman, Kuwait, Iraq) experiencing a 7-month delay in execution due to regional wars/geopolitical tensions, but new project envisaging and long-term investments remain unaffected.
- Russia continues to be an incredible market due to Western sanctions creating space for non-Western players.
- Nuclear energy sector ("Chandragupta Maurya period") progressing; actively quoting for Kudankulam expansion and liaising with BHEL.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins fell to 17.36% (from 24%) due to high exhibition costs (INR 5 crore) and heavy subsidization of API seals below raw material cost (INR 8 crore).
- Post-fit seal spare part business gross margins expected to be around 80%.
- Dollar appreciation helps realization on exports, though it impacts imports. No currency hedging is practiced.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investing in the expansion of the third manufacturing unit.
- Establishing seals and service centers across India, Middle East, and partners in Europe, USA, and South America.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow remained negative for the year.
- Inventory increased to INR 62 crore due to delayed shipments and strategic stocking over China rare earth material concerns.
- Management noted additional capital infusion (debt or other means) might be needed if aggressive API seal subsidization continues at a high scale.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical instability and war in the Middle East causing commissioning delays of 6 to 9 months.
- Supply chain and material availability risks highlighted by China's rare earth material injunctions.
- Heavy subsidization of initial API seals erodes short-term margins and impacts cash flows.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **P. Srinivasa Reddy (Srinivasa Neo Markets) on API commissioning & replacement revenue:** Management clarified that out of 686 supplied Middle East seals, about 20% are in commissioning, while 70% await settlement of regional conditions. Replacement spare margins remain around 80%.
2. **Parth on Middle East project delays & repeat API orders:** Management stated projects are delayed by 7 months, but new project pipelines remain robust. They target an additional 300 API seals for FY27.
3. **Deepak Poddar on EBITDA margin recovery:** Management guided for 23%-24% EBITDA margins in FY27, citing a reduction in exhibition count from 14 to 5 (saving ~INR 3.5-4 crore) and tapering of below-cost API seal deployment.
4. **P. Srinivasa Reddy on negative operating cash flow and rising inventory:** Management attributed inventory build-up to delayed execution and China's rare earth restrictions; operating cash flows expected to improve by FY28.
5. **Rajesh Iyer on capital infusion:** Management stated that if they aggressively scale up subsidized API seal infusions, they may need additional capital via debt or other means.

### One-line takeaway
Sealmatic delivered muted revenue growth (2%) and lower EBITDA margins (17.36%) in FY26 due to heavy upfront investments in exhibitions and subsidized API seals, but guides for a 15% revenue growth and margin recovery to 23-24% in FY27 as costs taper and aftermarket revenues loom.
