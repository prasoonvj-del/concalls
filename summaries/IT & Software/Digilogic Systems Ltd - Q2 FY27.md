# Digilogic Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth in revenue and profits, with clear visibility on the order pipeline and ongoing infrastructure expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 34.96 crore (up 91% YoY from Rs 18 crore).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 7.81 crore (up 126% YoY from Rs 3.45 crore).
*   **PAT:** Rs 4.76 crore (up 151% YoY from Rs 1.9 crore).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 20 crore (as of October 2026).
*   **Order Pipeline:** Rs 54 crore.

### Highlights
*   Executed 36 projects in H1 FY27 valued at Rs 33.18 crore.
*   Management confirmed that all previous order spillover from H1 has been billed.
*   Inventory levels increased in H1 due to pre-ordering components for projects scheduled for execution in H2.
*   New products, including High Precision GIMBAL System and custom-build single-board computers, are expected to be marketable by mid-2027, with an initial revenue potential of Rs 3-4 crore annually.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintained guidance for 25-30% top-line growth and 30-35% EBITDA and PAT growth for FY27.
*   Management expects to bill the current order book (Rs 20 crore) and the identified pipeline (Rs 54 crore) by mid-February 2027.
*   Management stated that the core EBITDA growth is 30-35%, clarifying that previous guidance of 40-45% included other income (interest on FDs).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Defense sector tailwinds remain strong, specifically for guided weapons, missiles, and drones.
*   60% of the Rs 54 crore order pipeline consists of repeat orders.
*   Management noted that delays in large defense programs (e.g., QRSAM, P-75I) have not impacted the company, as it is diversified across multiple programs like LRSAM and MRSAM.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Fixed costs remained steady during the period.
*   Margins were supported by interest income on fixed deposits.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Project Udaan:** Civil construction is underway and expected to be completed by March 2027.
*   Management expects the facility to be ready for inspection and certification by Q1 FY28, with commercialization targeted for H2 FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Inventory increased due to strategic pre-ordering of components with long lead times to meet H2 delivery schedules.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Dependency on government and DRDO procedures, which can impact the timing of order finalization.
*   The requirement for government certification for new facilities and products, which involves a multi-stage process.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Guidance Clarification:** When asked about the change in EBITDA guidance, management clarified that the 30-35% figure represents core growth, whereas the previous 40-45% figure included other income.
*   **Order Book Visibility:** Management clarified that the total visibility is Rs 74 crore (Rs 20 crore in hand + Rs 54 crore in pipeline), all of which is targeted for billing by mid-February 2027.
*   **Competitive Moat:** Management stated that the moat is protected by the necessity of R&QA certifications from government agencies for defense software/hardware, which creates a 2-3 year barrier for new entrants to replicate.
*   **Project Udaan Timeline:** Management confirmed that after civil construction finishes in March 2027, a 3-month certification process is required, targeting operational readiness by Q1 FY28.
*   **Impact of Program Delays:** Management reiterated that they are not impacted by specific program delays because they are present in multiple programs (e.g., LRSAM, MRSAM) that have already moved into production.

### One-line takeaway
Digilogic delivered strong H1 growth and maintains a confident outlook for FY27, supported by a Rs 74 crore order visibility and a strategic focus on expanding capacity via Project Udaan.
