# IZMO Ltd-$ - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-08 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q4 FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the highest quarterly revenue in company history, strong growth in izmoMicro, and robust demand across automotive and AI-driven segments.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q4 FY26):** INR 109.16 crores (up 82.5% YoY, 84.7% QoQ)
- **Consolidated Revenue (FY26):** INR 284.88 crores (up 26.8% YoY from INR 224.61 crores in FY25)
- **Consolidated Net Profit (Q4 FY26):** INR 17.3 crores
- **Consolidated Net Profit (FY26):** INR 47.56 crores
- **izmoMicro Revenue (FY26):** ~INR 18-19 crores (Q4 FY26: ~INR 9.2 crores)
- **izmoMicro FY27 Revenue Guidance:** INR 45-50 crores
- **izmoMicro Order Book:** INR 40 crores (pipeline over INR 100 crores)
- **Gross Revenue Retention Rate (FrogData):** >93%
- **Defense Budget & Industry Metrics:** India defense budget at INR 7.85 lakh crores; government ISM 1 and 2 schemes allocated INR 1 lakh crores.

### Highlights
- Q4 FY26 marked the highest quarterly revenue in IZMO's history, driven by izmoMicro, automotive software expansion, and FrogData in the U.S.
- izmoMicro achieved a breakthrough in silicon photonics packaging (32-channel high-density platform with insertion loss <2 dB and performance up to 70 GHz).
- Named photonic IC packaging partner for the MeitY-supported silicon photonics initiative at IIT Madras.
- Established a new subsidiary in Germany and joined Silicon Saxony cluster.
- Integrated an "AI factory" and LLM infrastructure internally for software development.
- Partnered with Ford to offer solutions through FordDirect's "The Shop" e-commerce marketplace.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Growth:** Management expects overall growth of 20-25%.
- **izmoMicro Revenue (FY27):** INR 45-50 crores; 5-year target of INR 1,200 to INR 1,500 crores.
- **EBITDA Margins:** Expected to normalize and improve to 20-25% in FY27, and 30%+ in FY28 as izmoMicro scales.
- **Capex Guidance:** Planning to raise ~INR 150 crores (INR 125 crores for capex, INR 25 crores for working capital) over the next 30-45 days.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Massive demand expected for optical interconnects driven by AI data infrastructure and hyperscaler investments exceeding INR 3 lakh crores in India.
- Transitioning from copper to optical photonics to match high computing speeds (e.g., NVIDIA chips).
- Strong inquiry pipeline from U.S., Europe, and India across defense, space, telecom, and AI hyperscalers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q4 other expenses jumped due to a special project with outsourced services (pass-through revenue); management expects this to normalize/decline next quarter.
- Packaging costs represent 50-60% of gross margins in advanced packaging and even higher in silicon photonics.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current izmoMicro capacity can deliver ~INR 150 crores top line. Planned expansion aims to scale addressable top line capacity to ~INR 1,200 crores (new facility targeted to start contributing in Q2/Q3 of FY28).
- Fundraise of INR 150 crores via equity-cum-debt is currently in process and expected to be finalized in 30-45 days.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables stand at 100-110 days (INR 129 crores), normal for large automotive clients like Hertz, Stellantis, and Avis who pay in 4-5 months. Company remains cash flow positive from operations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High complexity in silicon photonics packaging makes scaling difficult and customized.
- Scale of current operations is still considered small by some large global players, necessitating the planned capacity expansion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raman Kerti (Sequent Investments) on margin decline:** Management explained that Q4 prioritized top-line execution and client campaigns over margins, and costs are non-linear; margins will expand as izmoMicro scales.
2. **Prasenjit Paul (Paul Asset) on funding/scale against large players:** Management clarified they compete in packaging/complement big players rather than designing chips from scratch, hence requiring INR 100-150 crores rather than billions.
3. **Ansh Gupta (Retail Investor) on order execution timeline:** Management stated the current INR 40 crore order book will take 9 months to execute, with project timelines ranging from 3 months to 2 years (e.g., ISRO space payload).
4. **Jainam Ranka (Retail Investor) on competition in India:** Management noted no direct Indian competition in silicon photonics packaging, highlighting entry barriers via process technology and material science rather than just capital.
5. **Paras Chheda (Retail Investor) on subsidiaries audit:** Management clarified that U.S. subsidiaries undergo tax filing and review rather than expensive full audits, while U.K. and European entities are audited by KPMG.

### One-line takeaway
IZMO delivered record Q4 FY26 performance led by automotive software and a surging high-margin semiconductor/silicon photonics packaging business (izmoMicro), backed by plans for a major INR 150 crore capex expansion.
