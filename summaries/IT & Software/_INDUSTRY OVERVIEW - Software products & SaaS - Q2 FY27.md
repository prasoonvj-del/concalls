## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for Indian software products and SaaS companies reflects robust top-line momentum, aggressive AI integration, and disciplined cost structures, though performance diverges across consumer and enterprise segments. Companies like Capillary Technologies, IRIS RegTech, and Unicommerce delivered strong revenue growth exceeding 15% to 50% YoY, driven by enterprise SaaS traction, new module adoption, and international expansion. Conversely, legacy-reliant or consumer-facing players like Quick Heal faced continued YoY revenue contraction in their consumer antivirus segments, forcing strategic pivots toward enterprise and compliance markets. Profitability trends varied: companies scaling new acquisitions or frontloading marketing investments (such as IRIS RegTech and Unicommerce) experienced near-term margin stabilization, while firms with operating leverage (like Capillary and Subex) reported significant EBITDA expansions. Geopolitical uncertainties, particularly in the Middle East, caused temporary order deferrals for players like IRIS RegTech, Newgen Software, Softtech Engineers, and Subex, but underlying deal pipelines remained intact.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Indian software products and SaaS players historically relied on upfront perpetual software licenses, system integration services, and monolithic on-premise implementations. Over the last five years, the industry has undergone a structural shift toward cloud-based subscription models (SaaS), recurring Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), multi-tenant architectures, and modular utility-based pricing [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **Shift from monolithic platforms to modular AI-driven solutions**: This shift is currently scaling, enabling faster deployment cycles and targeted monetization. Subex launched its modular "Architecture of Autonomy" (AOA) and "FraudZap" AI agents, reducing implementation timelines by 15-20% and securing deals within 90-day sprints [Subex concall Q2 FY27]. Newgen Software released its NewgenONE AI Studio, recording 10 AI-based deal bookings in H1 with deal sizes 20-40% higher than non-AI deals [Newgen Software concall Q2 FY27]. Capillary Technologies expanded its aiRA platform into native ad channels, driving higher enterprise win rates [Capillary Technologies concall Q2 FY27]. This shift matters because embedded AI and modular solutions command premium pricing and shorten time-to-value, boosting margins.
- **Strategic pivot from consumer security/legacy systems to enterprise compliance and data privacy**: This shift is in the scaling stage, offsetting secular declines in traditional consumer segments. Quick Heal is actively transitioning its portfolio toward data privacy and exposure management, targeting an enterprise revenue contribution exceeding 80% within three years [Quick Heal concall Q2 FY27; Concall Q2 FY27]. IRIS RegTech is expanding its IRIS CARBON and SupTech compliance suites, pushing enterprise client counts past 50 [IRIS RegTech concall Q2 FY27]. This matters because enterprise contracts offer higher stickiness, larger ticket sizes, and predictable recurring ARR compared to volatile B2C markets.
- **Transition from system integration to proprietary SaaS products in niche verticals**: This shift is in the early stage, capturing public sector and defense opportunities. Praruh Technologies is pivoting from traditional system integration to proprietary software and SaaS-based offerings, targeting margin expansions from 13% to 20-30% as products launch in early 2027 [Praruh Technologies concall Q2 FY27]. Softtech Engineers is scaling its proprietary digital twin solutions, securing a major Rs 92.95 crore order from JNPA [Softtech Engineers concall Q2 FY27]. This matters because owning proprietary IP transforms low-margin project execution into high-margin software licensing.

**Government push**
- Atmanirbhar Bharat / Make in India policies: Rewards indigenous software and hardware products, creating favorable tender eligibility criteria for domestic firms in defense, railways, and public sector undertakings; benefits Praruh Technologies [Govt: Atmanirbhar Bharat public procurement norms, Nov 2020].
- Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act: Mandates strict data privacy and compliance for enterprises operating in India, directly driving demand for data privacy solutions; benefits Quick Heal Technologies [Govt: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, Aug 2023].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
- **Consensus**: Managements across Capillary Technologies, IRIS RegTech, Unicommerce, and Subex reported strong, sustained demand for enterprise SaaS, multi-module adoption, and automated compliance solutions. Global enterprise clients are increasingly willing to commit to multi-year SaaS contracts when clear productivity or regulatory ROI is demonstrated.
- **Divergence (Consumer vs. Enterprise)**: Quick Heal highlighted a persistent structural decline in global and domestic consumer antivirus demand due to operating system default security policies (e.g., Microsoft), contrasting sharply with its growing enterprise cybersecurity and data privacy demand.
- **Divergence (Geographic execution)**: Newgen Software experienced double-digit growth in EMEA and the US while facing temporary contractions in India and APAC. Similarly, IRIS RegTech, Newgen Software, Softtech Engineers, and Subex noted that geopolitical instability and budget delays in the Middle East temporarily impacted new order intake for SupTech and select regional projects, while domestic and Western markets remained resilient.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Operating Leverage & Margins**: Capillary Technologies demonstrated strong operating leverage, with non-COGS costs growing 34% YoY against 47% revenue growth, driving adjusted EBITDA to Rs 44.8 crore (17% margin). Subex reported EBITDA margin expansion to 20.8% despite absorbing annual wage hikes in Q2. Softtech Engineers' standalone EBITDA margin expanded to 35.1% (vs 29.6% YoY).
- **Investments and Frontloading**: Unicommerce and IRIS RegTech absorbed frontloaded operating investments in product development, talent, and US/European sales and marketing, which temporarily moderated EBITDA margins relative to peak historical levels. Quick Heal maintained flat total expenditures (excl. depreciation) at Rs 63 crore by optimizing employee costs, which dropped by Rs 17 crore YoY.
- **Gross Margins**: Capillary maintained stable gross margins at 67% despite the dilutive mix of the newly acquired Session M (operating at early 50s gross margins). Unicommerce reported Uniware gross margins around 80% and Shipway gross margins around 20%.

## Capex & capacity
- **Capillary Technologies**: Capitalization for internal tech investments reduced from Rs 36 crore last year to a run-rate of about Rs 20 crore. Management targets a 50% reduction in Session M server costs by March/April by migrating off single-instance Windows servers to multi-tenant Linux setups.
- **IRIS RegTech & Unicommerce**: Outlays are focused on SaaS sales and marketing expansions (particularly U.S. markets for IRIS CARBON) and strategic M&A scouting (Unicommerce). Unicommerce successfully scaled infrastructure to handle a peak single-day processing volume of 60 lakh transactions this Diwali season.
- **Softtech Engineers & Praruh**: Softtech and Praruh continue to invest heavily in platform development and localized data sets/building codes for international expansion (e.g., Germany for Softtech).

## What changed since last quarter
- **Guidance & Tone Shifts**: Capillary Technologies confirmed it will hit or exceed initial annual guidance, maintaining organic growth guidance at 20%. IRIS RegTech surpassed its annual 35% SaaS growth target halfway through the year. Unicommerce guided for revenue growth to accelerate to >15% and Shipway to >20% from Q4 FY27 onwards, alongside Shipway turning EBITDA profitable in Q3.
- **Delivery on Promises**: Quick Heal achieved a strong sequential recovery in revenue (up 44% QoQ) and returned to profitability (PAT of Rs 5.8 crore) following aggressive credit and cost controls initiated in previous quarters. Subex marked its fifth consecutive quarter of QoQ revenue growth.

## What to watch next quarter
- **Session M Migration & Margin Trajectory (Capillary)**: Progress on reducing Session M server costs by 50% and managing the 30-month migration churn.
- **Shipway Profitability Inflection (Unicommerce)**: Execution of guided EBITDA profitability for Shipway in Q3 FY27 and PAT profitability in Q4 FY27.
- **Middle East Order Realization (IRIS RegTech, Newgen, Softtech)**: Unfreezing of deferred orders and conversion of active RFPs in the Middle East as geopolitical conditions stabilize.
- **Enterprise ARR Scaling (Quick Heal & IRIS RegTech)**: Continuation of enterprise revenue mix expansion toward Quick Heal's 80% target and the integration of large enterprise wins for IRIS CARBON.
