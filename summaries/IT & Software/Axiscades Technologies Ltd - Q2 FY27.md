# Axiscades Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong core retained revenue growth and divestment milestone overshadowed by large impairment write-offs, margin dilution from retained corporate costs, and Phase 2 divestment delay.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained/Altered context. Management reaffirmed targeting ~Rs 600–720 crore retained revenue execution in H2 (reaching ~Rs 1,000–1,150 crore base organically/with closed M&A), but backed away from giving explicit annual PAT margin guidance due to facility transitions, interest, and depreciation. Post-ESOP EBITDA margin is aimed at ~15% in Q3 and ~20% in Q4.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from strongly positive to mixed/pragmatic. Emphasized massive ongoing "tectonic transformation" costs, non-cash impairments, and significant supply chain lead times (up to 12–18 months).
- New topics or risks: Phase 2 Akkodis divestment delayed by 3 months to February 2027 due to global corporate entity formations and MoD approvals; solvent liquidation of ADD Solutions and complete goodwill write-offs (Rs 58.3 crore total impairments across ADD Solutions and Epcogen); net debt spiked to Rs 556 crore due to acquisition debt.
- Last quarter's promises: Phase 1 Akkodis closed (delivered, closed Sept 30); Cloud Wave acquisition completed and consolidated from Sept 10 (delivered); Phase 2 closing by November 30 missed (delayed to on or before February 2027).

---

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 359.1 crore, up 20% YoY, 3.6% QoQ.
- Retained (continuing) revenue: Rs 195.6 crore, up 30% YoY, 7% QoQ (H1 retained revenue: Rs 378.9 crore, up 55% YoY).
- Discontinued operations revenue: Rs 163.5 crore.
- Consolidated reported EBITDA: Rs 11.4 crore; Pre-ESOP EBITDA: Rs 23.4 crore.
- Consolidated reported PAT: Rs 47.3 crore (vs loss of Rs 14.8 crore in Q1 FY27 and profit of Rs 23.1 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Continuing operations PAT loss: Rs 48.1 crore (impacted by Rs 38.4 crore impairment).
- Discontinued operations PAT: Rs 95.4 crore (includes Rs 129.3 crore pre-tax exceptional gain net of goodwill).
- Defence segment: H1 revenue Rs 252.7 crore (up 46% YoY); Q2 EBITDA Rs 52.5 crore (17.6% margin).
- XiDA segment: H1 revenue Rs 97.9 crore (up 55% YoY, 27.6% EBITDA margin); Q2 revenue up 47% YoY.
- Total exceptional write-offs: Rs 58.3 crore (Rs 38.4 crore continuing on ADD Solutions; Rs 19.9 crore discontinued on Epcogen).
- ESOP charge: Rs 12.0 crore for H1 (absorbed in Q2 EBITDA).
- Gross debt: Rs 624 crore; Net debt: Rs 556 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
- Closed Phase 1 of the US$ 237 million Akkodis divestment on September 30, 2026; Phase 2 classified as held for sale.
- Total guaranteed post-tax divestment cash consideration is Rs 1,395 crore: received Rs 121 crore in October 2026; expecting Rs 32 crore in November 2026, Rs 504 crore in February 2027, Rs 56 crore in July 2027, and Rs 682 crore in December 2028 (earn-outs up to ~Rs 589 crore are extra, based on FY28 EBITDA).
- Management initiated solvent liquidation of loss-making ADD Solutions and wrote off goodwill for ADD Solutions and Epcogen.
- Consolidated aerospace manufacturing company Cloud Wave from September 10, 2026 (contributed Rs 16.5 crore revenue in Q2).
- Announced acquisition of ITSJ under XiDA, giving customer access to a top US hyperscaler (Google) and a top semiconductor equipment OEM (AMAT).
- Group CFO Shashidhar S.K. superannuating in December 2026; Uttam Kumar Bhageria appointed as CFO Designate.
- Company added to the MSCI India Domestic Small Cap Index effective November 30, 2026.
- Assured Forecast Visibility (AFV) in defence expanded from ~Rs 4,500 crore to close to Rs 5,000 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Retained business revenue targets: Q3 execution expected at Rs 285–300 crore; Q4 execution expected at Rs 375–405 crore (H2 execution target of Rs 600–720 crore).
- Segment Q3 / Q4 revenue targets: DRDO Rs 120 cr / Rs 150 cr; direct MoD Rs 45 cr / Rs 60 cr; XiDA Rs 75–81 cr / Rs 90–105 cr; Aerospace Rs 51 cr / Rs 75 cr.
- EBITDA margin targets (post-ESOP): Aiming for 15–16% in Q3 FY27, scaling to 20%+ in Q4 FY27. Core verticals (Defence, XiDA, Aerospace) planned to operate at ~20% EBITDA margin individually from Q3.
- Expected exceptional gain of ~Rs 1,100 crore in Q4 FY27 upon Phase 2 Akkodis closure.
- Space business expected to generate revenue in FY28 and become EBITDA positive thereafter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust order intake from DRDO and defence PSUs; OEM programs remain intact.
- High traction in Physical AI/humanoids, data center chillers, and AI-related semiconductor equipment via XiDA. Received initial order from US-based Humanoid Inc.
- Global aerospace and strategic electronics demand strong, but severely restricted by global electronics supply chain bottlenecks.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Overheads: Corporate costs in ACTL retained business were ~Rs 15 crore in Q2, expected to reset to ~Rs 12 crore per quarter in H2.
- Legal and professional costs of Rs 14.5 crore in Q2 to normalize to Rs 4–5 crore per quarter.
- ESOP charge expected to drop from Rs 12 crore in Q2 to ~Rs 6 crore in Q3, declining to a single-digit percentage of EBITDA by Q4.
- Corporate relocation to Devanahalli initially inflated employee conveyance and canteen costs (e.g. conveyance reduced from Rs 27,000 to Rs 22,000 per employee).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Land for Missile Atmanirbhar Complex (MAC): 8+ acres registered in Hyderabad; facility expected to take 1 to 1.5 years to build and 6 months for certification. Bangalore facility handling missile subsystems integration interimly.
- Setup of laser technology testing, integration, and manufacturing facilities underway following DRDO ToT.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 556 crore as of September 30, 2026 (gross debt Rs 624 crore), including Rs 200 crore borrowed for Cloud Wave acquisition.
- Repaid Rs 85 crore debt in October 2026 utilizing Phase 1 divestment proceeds. Further debt reduction planned using divestment cash inflows.
- Expected guaranteed inflows: Rs 32 crore in November 2026, ~Rs 504 crore in February 2027.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Phase 2 divestment delay:** Timeline pushed by 3 months to February 2027 due to foreign entity formations and regulatory/MoD approvals.
- **Supply chain disruptions:** Severe delivery lead times of 12–18 months on strategic electronics, rare components, and PCBs risking XiDA and defence delivery timelines.
- **Short-term margin overhang:** Elevated corporate overheads and transaction costs depressing enterprise-level margins ahead of revenue scale-up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q3/Q4 Margin Trajectory:** Management clarified that the 15–16% (Q3) and 20% (Q4) margin expectations are post-ESOP targets, aided by corporate cost normalization and halved quarterly ESOP charges.
- **Phase 2 Akkodis Closing Risk:** Management reiterated 100% confidence in closing Phase 2 on or before February 2027, citing the buyer's eagerness to consolidate joint contracts, with remaining transaction expenses estimated at ~Rs 20 crore.
- **Akkodis Divestment Valuation & Earn-outs:** In response to margin drop in the divested unit in Q2, management affirmed that 74% (Rs 1,686 crore gross / Rs 1,395 crore net post-tax) is fully guaranteed with zero price-cut risk; the ~Rs 589 crore earn-out is proportional to FY28 EBITDA ($18 million target) and unaffected by FY27 performance.
- **H2 Retained Revenue Feasibility:** Management reiterated order visibility of Rs 600–720 crore in H2 from existing businesses and closed M&A, noting that prospective new M&A (2–3 targets by Q4) would provide additional prorated revenue.
- **Full Year PAT Margin Guidance:** Management dodged giving a definitive full-year PAT margin figure, explaining that while confident in EBITDA, PAT is subject to moving facility depreciation, relocation, and financing variables.

---

### One-line takeaway
Axiscades delivered record revenue but saw reported margins diluted by legacy write-offs and transition overheads, pinning its financial normalization on closing Phase 2 of its Akkodis divestment by February 2027 and scaling its high-margin defence and XiDA units.
