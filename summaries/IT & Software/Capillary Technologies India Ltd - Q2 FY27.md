# Capillary Technologies India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, margin expansion, adjusted EBITDA more than doubled, and successful integration of the Session M acquisition breaking even.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 271 crore (51% YoY growth, 43% on constant currency).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 44.8 crore (17% margin, more than doubled YoY).
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 22 crore (8% margin).
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 528 crore (47% YoY growth, 38% constant currency).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 89 crore (17% margin).
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 12 crore (2% margin).
- **Organic Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 318 crore (23% growth from Rs 257 crore).
- **Inorganic Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 210 crore (more than doubled from Rs 101 crore).
- **Organic NRR (Q2):** 116% (Constant currency NRR: 109%).
- **ARR:** Rs 1085 crore (51% growth YoY).
- **New ACV (TTM ended Q2 FY27):** Rs 93 crore (vs Rs 52 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Cash balance:** Rs 573 crore.
- **Short-term working capital borrowings:** Rs 45 crore (zero long-term debt).
- **Operating cash flow to adjusted EBITDA:** 128%.
- **Free cash flow to PAT:** 506%.
- **ROCE:** 7% (improved from 5% previous quarter).
- **Cash return on invested capital:** 29% (improved from 22%).

### Highlights
- Session M (acquired in Q1) has broken even in both Q1 and Q2.
- Experience Plus (rebranded from Customer Glue IP) has secured 3 existing customer adoptions with 9 proposals pending.
- aiRA (AI platform) has been expanded into native Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, and Amazon ads channels for acquisition analytics.
- New ACV grew ~79% YoY on a constant currency basis (excluding one exceptionally large healthcare deal).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they will hit or do a little better than the guidance given at the start of the year.
- Management maintained organic growth guidance at 20% for the next couple of quarters before re-evaluating.

### Demand & industry commentary
- AI capabilities are acting as a major differentiator, driving higher new ACV and win rates in enterprise deals.
- Enterprise customers increasingly prefer fixed licensing models (like teams/professional licenses at $60,000 to $100,000) over pure usage-based token models for AI.
- Agents (AI shopping agents) are expected to make consumer purchasing behavior more rational, increasing the importance of robust software platforms like Capillary over traditional agency-heavy competitors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins remained stable at 67% in Q2, despite the lower-gross-margin Session M revenue (early 50s/51-52%) diluting the mix.
- Non-cogs costs for H1 grew 34% YoY against a 47% revenue growth, demonstrating operating leverage.
- Every Rs 100 of new organic revenue generates Rs 44 of adjusted EBITDA.
- Depreciation increased by Rs 4 crore QoQ due to Session M amortization (IP depreciated over 3 years, customer contracts over duration).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capitalization for internal tech investments has reduced from Rs 36 crore last year to a run-rate of about Rs 20 crore.
- Server costs for Session M are targeted to be reduced by 50% by March/April by migrating off single-instance Windows servers to multi-tenant Linux setups.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash positive with Rs 573 crore in cash and Rs 45 crore in short-term working capital borrowings (zero long-term debt).
- Net cash generated from operating activities stood at Rs 113 crore for H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Session M migration takes a 30-month full cycle, with an expected revenue retention post-migration of roughly 66% to 70% (partly impacted by historical customer bankruptcies like Children's Place and Express).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vinay Menon (Unidentified firm) asked about aiRA's new ad channels capability and pricing:** Management explained aiRA pulls historical campaign data across Google, Meta, and TikTok to provide cross-channel intelligence in plain English, and noted a shift towards fixed team/professional licenses ($60k-$100k) rather than purely usage-based tokens.
- **Divyansh Gupta (Unidentified firm) asked why customers don't always migrate and about Session M server costs:** Management clarified that M&A migration churn is around 20% (including bankruptcies) and that Session M's single-instance server costs will be reduced by 50% by March through architectural optimization.
- **Rishabh Mehra (Unidentified firm) asked about gross margin splits:** Management stated core Capillary customers are at >70% gross margin, Session M is at early 50s (~51-52%), blending to an overall 67%.
- **Shaurya Yadav (Unidentified firm) asked about deal sizes and Optum comparison:** Management noted Optum was the largest SaaS deal in Asia and hard to replicate, though multi-million dollar deals ($20mn+) continue to be won.
- **Siva Prakash Balaji (Ithought PMS) asked about migration mechanics vs organic onboarding:** Management explained organic onboarding requires 7-8 separate integrations by the client's CIO, whereas M&A migration uses capillary-built API gateways requiring only a 2-3 week UAT from the customer.

### One-line takeaway
Capillary Technologies delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 51% YoY revenue growth, a doubling of adjusted EBITDA to Rs 44.8 crore, and successful break-even of the Session M acquisition alongside strong AI-driven new business velocity.
