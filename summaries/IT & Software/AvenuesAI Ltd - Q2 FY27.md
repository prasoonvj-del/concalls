# AvenuesAI Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth offset by compressed margins and negative operating cash flows.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** INR2,879 crores (+47% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** INR5,560 crores (+71% YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA (excluding other income, Q2 FY27):** INR98.9 crores (vs INR96.2 crores YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA (excluding other income, H1 FY27):** INR199 crores (+19% YoY)
- **Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT, Q2 FY27):** INR71.70 crores (vs INR67.6 crores YoY, +6%)
- **Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT, H1 FY27):** INR156 crores (+24% YoY)
- **Total Payments Processing Volume (TPV, Q2 FY27):** INR1.77 lakh crores (+19% QoQ)
- **Net Take Rate (Q2 FY27):** 5.06 basis points
- **Total Equity:** INR5,145 crores (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** INR546.5 crores (as of September 30, 2026)
- **First Half Operating Cash Flow:** Negative INR192 crores
- **Earnings Per Share (H1 FY27):** ~INR4.5 per share

### Highlights
- Company is strategically transitioning from a payments technology company into an "AI-first fintech infrastructure company" combining payments, enterprise digital platforms, and transaction intelligence (TISco).
- Total payments processing volume (TPV) reached INR1.77 lakh crores in Q2 FY27, up 19% sequentially.
- Rediff annualized revenue run rate is upwards of INR200 crores, generating about INR100 crores in annual profit. RediffPay onboarding permissions received from NPCI.
- International expansion is active in UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Oman; targeting the US market next.
- *Vs Our Record:* The call confirms the Q2 FY27 reported numbers (revenue INR2,879 cr, net profit INR72 cr) and the strategic pivot toward AI-native transaction infrastructure as noted in the investor deck.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its FY2027 consolidated revenue guidance range of INR11,000 crores to INR13,000 crores.
- Management maintained its EPS guidance range of INR8.75 to INR9.5 per share for FY2027 (achieved ~INR4.5 per share in H1).
- Internal estimate targets INR50,000 crores to INR1,000,000 crores of incremental TPV in the next 24 months via new partnerships.

### Demand & industry commentary
- UPI transactions are on an upswing during the festival season; approximately 38%–40% of current UPI transactions are processed free of charge, with ~30% expected to become chargeable under a potential MDR regime.
- Offline POS penetration in India stands at ~30%, compared to ~90% in Brazil and China, indicating significant growth headroom.
- ResAvenue (hospitality SaaS) processed 1.2 million room nights in the last 90 days across 3,000+ hotels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Net take rate reduced sequentially by ~4% to 5.06 bps in Q2 FY27 due to a conscious pricing strategy to capture scale and festival volumes.
- Operating costs are front-loaded due to investments in AI initiatives, product integrations, and platform scaling.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Out of the recent rights issue proceeds, ~INR294 crores allocated for advanced AI software technology solutions has not yet been significantly deployed as management assesses rapid industry breakthroughs.
- International expansion is progressing in the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman), with plans to enter the US market next quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow for H1 FY27 was negative INR192 crores, driven by working capital movements primarily resulting from accelerated merchant settlement cycles.
- Total equity stood at INR5,145 crores and cash & cash equivalents at INR546.5 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- No promoter selling; promoters participated in the primary rights issue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Negative operating cash flows (negative INR192 crores in H1 FY27) due to faster merchant settlements creating timing-related working capital outflows.
- Compression in net take rates (down 4% QoQ to 5.06 bps) due to competitive pricing pressures.
- Geopolitical instability in international markets could cause minor disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pramukh (MyInvestBuddy) asked why revenue and profit growth slowed this quarter.** Management answered that it was a conscious decision to reduce net take rates by ~4% sequentially to 5.06 bps to capture higher volume and scale ahead of the festival season, treating data as a long-term moat for AI.
2. **Saurabh Bansal asked about Rediff's financials and monetization timeline.** Management stated Rediff's annualized revenue run rate is upwards of INR200 crores with ~INR100 crores in annual profit, while synergies will take a few quarters to fully play out.
3. **Rahul asked about the impact of potential UPI MDR.** Management stated that if implemented (potentially January or earlier), ~30% of currently free UPI transactions will become chargeable, providing a positive revenue boost to the ecosystem.
4. **Dinesh Kumar asked about the deployment of rights issue proceeds (Quantum Edge AI data centers).** Management clarified that the ~INR294 crores earmarked for advanced AI software technology has not yet been fully deployed as they monitor rapid technological changes.
5. **James questioned dividend policy and the impact of the Rediff restructuring on retail shareholders.** Management explained that the board prioritizes reinvesting retained earnings for long-term growth over dividends, and noted that Rediff is already consolidated at ~82% ownership with no additional transfer impact.

### One-line takeaway
AvenuesAI delivered solid top-line growth (+47% YoY) in Q2 FY27, sacrificing near-term take rates and operating cash flow (-INR192 crores in H1) to drive transaction scale and fund its strategic pivot toward AI-first fintech infrastructure.
