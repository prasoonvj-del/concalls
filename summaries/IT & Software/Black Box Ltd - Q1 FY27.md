# Black Box Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly revenue, strong order book growth, and margin expansion driven by robust hyperscale demand.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 1,719 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 160 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.3% (up 90 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 56 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **Order Bookings:** US$ 339 million
- **Order Backlog:** US$ ~950 million (up 83% YoY)
- **Acquisition Contribution:** 2S (Brazil) contributed INR 60 crore to revenue (2 months of consolidation)

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue, supported by improved order backlog execution and the 2S acquisition in Brazil.
- Added a new US global hyperscaler through a US$131 million (~INR 1,240 crore) order, while deepening existing hyperscaler relationships.
- Claimed to be the only India-origin digital infrastructure solutions company executing gigawatt-scale data center programs.
- Rationalized low-value long-tail customer accounts to focus on ~300 strategic accounts.
- Reaffirmed aspiration to build an INR 18,000 crore (~US$ 2 billion) revenue company by FY30 (INR 12,000 crore organic, INR 6,000 crore inorganic).

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Guidance:**
  - Order Backlog (as of March 31, 2027): US$ 1.3 billion to US$ 1.4 billion (65% to 75% growth).
  - Order Bookings: US$ 1.3 billion to US$ 1.4 billion (32% to 45% growth).
  - Revenue: INR 7,800 crore to INR 8,000 crore (23% to 27% growth).
  - EBITDA: INR 725 crore to INR 750 crore (27% to 32% growth).
  - EBITDA Margin: 9.3% to 9.4% (up 30 to 40 bps).
  - Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 300 crore to INR 325 crore (38% to 50% growth).
- Management expects stronger revenue conversion and operating leverage in H2 FY27.
- Effective tax rate expected to be 10% to 15% for FY27 and FY28, regularizing to ~20% thereafter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global enterprise technology spending estimated by Gartner to exceed US$ 6.4 trillion (up 14.2% YoY), with US$ 822 billion on data center systems (up 62.5% YoY).
- Major hyperscalers (e.g., Google) significantly scaling up budgets.
- Black Box participates in a US$ 250 billion to US$ 300 billion addressable market, with 60% to 70% of spending in the United States.
- Supply chain conditions for cables and fiber have improved, supporting faster backlog conversion.
- Non-hyperscale enterprise business is expected to grow at a modest double-digit rate (~10%), following a lag effect from data center build-outs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expanded by 90 bps YoY to 9.3% due to operating leverage, better business mix, and disciplined execution.
- Medium-term objective is to operate consistently at or above 10% EBITDA margin.
- Investments continue in talent, sales, and engineering, with plans to onboard nearly 2,000 professionals.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Brazil acquisition (2S) adds ~US$ 50 million in annual revenue and expands capabilities in networking, cybersecurity, and managed services in Latin America.
- Plans to hire/train nearly 2,000 professionals to support scaling.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow to EBITDA conversion expected to improve meaningfully over FY26 through disciplined working capital management.
- Balance sheet described as healthy and stronger.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution is dependent on customer timelines; large gigawatt-plus sites require extensive deployment and groundwork that can cause execution to spill into subsequent quarters (e.g., FY28).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deep Shah (360 ONE Capital)** asked about organic revenue growth and cash flow improvement drivers. **Management** clarified that 2S contributed INR 60 crore, making the rest organic, and stated that talent investments are largely done, allowing operating cash flow to improve with growth.
2. **Deep Shah (360 ONE Capital)** asked if guidance was conservative given strong non-project backlog growth. **Management** answered that execution of large orders will scale significantly in Q3/Q4 and spill over into FY28, making current guidance appropriate.
3. **Vivek Choraria (Individual Investor)** questioned the increasing EBIT losses in the Products (TPS) business. **Management** stated the segment is expected to grow ~20% this year, focusing on mission-critical KVM products, and will become accretive with scale.
4. **Nandan Arekal (JM Financial)** asked about the path to 10% EBITDA margins given current guidance of 9.3%-9.4%. **Management** explained that ongoing investments in talent and large-scale project training warrant a cautious guidance, but 10%+ remains the at-scale target.
5. **Keshav Bharadia (Wallfort Financial)** asked about the risk of order cancellations if hyperscaler capex moderates. **Management** stated that the overall scale of spend (US$ 1.6 trillion over 4 years) is gigantic, and they are capturing a small slice, seeing acceleration rather than moderation in project announcements.

### One-line takeaway
Black Box reported its highest-ever quarterly revenue and a record order backlog of ~US$ 950 million, remaining confident in achieving its FY27 guidance and long-term US$ 2 billion revenue goal by FY30.
