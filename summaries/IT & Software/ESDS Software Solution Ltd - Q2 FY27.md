# ESDS Software Solution Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects successful transition into an integrated AI-enabled sovereign technology ecosystem, strong H1 financial growth, and steady progress on the Sharon AI cluster deployment.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 1,383.4 million (up 3.5% QoQ from Rs 1,336.6 million in Q1)
- Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 2,719.0 million (up 24.6% YoY from Rs 2,183.7 million in H1 FY26)
- Total Income (H1 FY27): Rs 2,788.0 million, including Rs 68.0 million of other income (up 25.6% YoY from Rs 2,219.0 million in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27): Rs 1,137.5 million (up 12.3% YoY from Rs 1,012.7 million in H1 FY26)
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 603.7 million (up 26.3% YoY from Rs 477.9 million in H1 FY26)
- Total IPO Proceeds Raised: Rs 7,200.0 million (utilised: Rs 469.13 million; unutilized: Rs 6,730.87 million, with ~Rs 6,500.0 million in fixed deposits)
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: Rs 16,368.31 million (as of September 30, 2026)
- Other Bank Balances: Rs 3,419.80 million (as of September 30, 2026)
- Total Assets: Rs 41,095.0 million (as of September 30, 2026)
- Reserves: Rs 12,822.76 million (as of September 30, 2026)
- Sharon AI Deal Contract Value: USD 1,250.0 million (7-year price lock; 5-year commitment)
- Sharon AI Projected Revenue: Rs 288.0 crores monthly revenue; Rs 50.0 crores to Rs 60.0 crores monthly PAT once 8,200 GPUs fire up
- India AI Factory Capex: Rs 1,500.0 crores (funded via IPO proceeds and customer advances, targeted to go live by June next year)
- Domestic AI Factory Revenue Target: Rs 1,000.0 crores per year (targeted by June-July next year with ~1,150 to 1,200 GPUs added initially)
- India Data Center Capacity Utilisation: Close to 85%

### Highlights
- Company became a listed company on September 4, 2026 (BSE Scrip Code: 544898).
- Business integrated across three segments: Infrastructure as a Service, Managed Services, and Software as a Service (including Swaraj Cloud and AI products).
- Sharon AI project (8,200 GPUs, 16-megawatt power) is in final NVIDIA cluster testing stages; ready-for-service (RFS) expected between December 1 and December 17, 2026, allowing 1 month of revenue/profit booking in Q3.
- Internal virtual subsidiary created to streamline operations and transfer smaller customers, focusing ESDS energy on top 400-500 customers.
- Management structure streamlined: CIO and CTO roles rendered redundant following the introduction of the Chief AI Officer (CAIO) and Chief Product Officer (CPO) roles.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the Sharon AI cluster to generate monthly revenue of Rs 288.0 crores and monthly PAT of Rs 50.0 crores to Rs 60.0 crores once fully utilised.
- Domestic AI factory capex of Rs 1,500.0 crores is expected to go live by June next year, targeting an additional Rs 1,000.0 crores in annual revenue.
- Management aspires to triple or quadruple domestic revenue from the current Rs 500.0 crores baseline over the next 1 to 2 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand for AI compute and factories is primarily driven by AI labs (connected to top 17-18 labs globally) and banks.
- Global data center expansion is severely bottlenecked by power shortages in the US and Europe; India has a projected 40-50 gigawatt surplus for AI factories over the next 4-5 years.
- DPDP implementation next year is anticipated to create a surge in demand as users potentially migrate from foreign platforms to sovereign domestic clouds like Swaraj Cloud.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Domestic AI factory PAT margins expected in the range of 26%-27%; AI factory PAT margins expected around 16%-18%.
- NVIDIA's CUDA platform enhances GPU performance over time rather than causing software obsolescence, supporting long-term asset residual value and strong secondary market demand for H100/H200.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of Rs 1,500.0 crores for India AI factory using IPO proceeds and customer advances, targeting go-live by June next year.
- Active pursuit of powered land (50-100 megawatts) globally and domestically to support future AI factory pipelines.
- Initial addition of 1,150 to 1,200 GPUs in India by June-July next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total assets stand at Rs 41,095.0 million; reserves at Rs 12,822.76 million.
- Cash and cash equivalents at Rs 16,368.31 million; other bank balances at Rs 3,419.80 million.
- Unutilized IPO proceeds of Rs 6,730.87 million, with ~Rs 6,500.0 million parked in fixed deposits and the remainder in a monitoring account.
- Advance of USD 140 million paid to Sharon AI, replicated symmetrically in current/non-current asset and liability schedules alongside customer advances.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Quarterly performance can vary based on project execution and revenue recognition.
- Legal negotiations for large multi-thousand GPU Master Service Agreements (MSAs) can drag on for roughly two months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar asked about the status of Sharon AI GPUs and expected revenue/PAT contributions.** Piyush Somani stated NVIDIA testing is in final stages; RFS is expected by December 17 at latest, with monthly revenue of Rs 288.0 crores and monthly PAT of Rs 50.0 crores to Rs 60.0 crores once fully operational.
2. **Amit Chandra inquired about Sharon AI delivery delays, financial health, and CTO resignation impact.** Piyush Somani clarified that Sharon AI has $2 billion in cash equivalents against a $1.5 billion market cap, locking rates at $3.50/hour. He added that the CTO/CIO roles became redundant due to the integration of CAIO and CPO functions driven by AI adoption.
3. **Prateek Srivastava asked about contract terms with Sharon AI and the drop in customer counts.** Piyush Somani explained the Sharon AI contract has a 5-year commitment with a 7-year price lock on a take-or-pay basis, and noted that smaller customers were shifted to an internal virtual subsidiary to focus on the top 400-500 accounts.
4. **Deepak Pandey questioned the balance sheet non-current/current liabilities.** Saurabh Daga explained it pertains to the USD 140 million advance paid to Sharon AI and matching customer advances sitting symmetrically in assets and liabilities.
5. **Akash Jain asked about GPU technology obsolescence risk.** Piyush Somani answered that NVIDIA’s CUDA platform continuously improves hardware performance over time, preserving long-term asset value.

### One-line takeaway
ESDS reported strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 24.6% YoY and is poised for a major step-up in scale, awaiting imminent go-live of its 8,200-GPU Sharon AI cluster while advancing a Rs 1,500 crore domestic AI factory expansion.
