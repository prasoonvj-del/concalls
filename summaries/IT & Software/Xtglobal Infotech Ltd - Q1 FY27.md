# Xtglobal Infotech Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady operating performance with improved profitability margins and expanded client engagements despite a cautious demand environment.

### Key numbers
- Standalone Revenue: Rs 19.19 crore (up 8% YoY, up 1.8% QoQ)
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 2.82 crore (up 76.3% YoY)
- Standalone EBITDA Margin: 14.7% (up 571 bps YoY)
- Standalone EBIT: Rs 3.08 crore (up 75.6% YoY); Margin: 16% (up 618 bps YoY)
- Standalone PAT: Rs 1.82 crore (up 19.8% YoY); Margin: 9.5% (up 474 bps YoY)
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 93.30 crore (up 1.1% YoY, up 2% QoQ from Rs 89.52 crore in Q4 FY26)
- Consolidated EBITDA (Earnings before interest and depreciation): Rs 7.06 crore (up 7.8% YoY); Margin: 7.6% (vs 4.8% in Q4 FY26 and 7.1% in Q1 FY26)
- Consolidated EBIT: Rs 6.15 crore (up 25.2% YoY, up 2.1% QoQ); Margin: 6.6%
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 3.89 crore (up 4.3% YoY vs Rs 3.73 crore in Q1 FY26); Margin: 4.2% (stable)

### Highlights
- Added 7 new finance and accounting services engagements across Australia, the United States, and Ireland.
- Entered the Irish market with a first finance and accounting outsourcing engagement, establishing an offshore finance support function from India delivery centers.
- IT services business added clients in India and the United States.
- Internal Zoho paperless transformation completed 90% across 13 products and modules during the quarter.
- Admitted into general bidding for a couple of U.S. state/public sector government contracts.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects focus on Australia and Ireland to be high next year, projecting revenues in those regions to reach local currency million-dollar levels over the next year (representing less than 10% of total revenue for the next couple of years).
- Management expects growth in the U.S. public sector due to recent entry into government contracts.
- Dividend consideration may be looked at next quarter after declaring results (Management statement).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global technology services environment in Q1 FY27 remained measured; businesses assessed technology spending against macroeconomic uncertainty, geopolitical developments, and changing priorities.
- Clients remained selective on discretionary tech spending with longer decision-making cycles.
- Enterprises are prioritizing productivity, cost optimization, and measurable outcomes.
- AI focus is moving from experimentation toward practical deployment in enterprise processes and applications.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Revenue growth reflects a shift from onsite to offshore delivery, where billing rates are lower (e.g., $70 onsite vs $35 in India), resulting in modest top-line movement but higher profit margins.
- Offshore margins are higher due to cheaper resources compared to onsite.
- Products contribute 25% to revenue, and finance and accounting services contribute around 14%; both offer high margins and are recurring in nature.
- Circulus (automation product) provides sustainable high profit margins.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic uncertainty, geopolitical developments, and cautious client demand with selective discretionary spending.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rishabh Sharma (VP Capital) on PaaS growth engine and international markets:** Management (Rama) stated focus is on Australia and Ireland to reach million-dollar levels next year, contributing less than 10% of total revenue over the next couple of years.
2. **Rishabh Sharma on consolidated revenue growth of only 1.1% YoY:** Management (Raghuram) explained this is due to a strategic shift from onsite to offshore delivery (lower billing rates in India vs onsite), while the actual client base and real volume are growing.
3. **Rishabh Sharma on sustainability of consolidated EBITDA margin (7.6%):** Management confirmed it is sustainable because expenses are drastically reduced and offshore delivery yields higher profit margins.
4. **Omkar More (Individual Investor) on building scale in U.S. and Europe:** Management highlighted new traction in the U.S. public sector (a new segment for them) and the new Ireland client entry as drivers for scale.
5. **Sahil Gupta (Individual Investor) on GCC engagements:** Management stated they target mid-market GCC customers starting with small teams (3 to 5 members scaling up to 50) in the U.S. market.

### One-line takeaway
XTGlobal Infotech reported modest consolidated revenue growth of 1.1% YoY to Rs 93.30 crore in Q1 FY27, driven by an intentional shift to offshore delivery, which successfully expanded consolidated EBITDA margins to 7.6% and standalone PAT by 19.8% YoY.
