# Tech Mahindra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported broad-based YoY growth, deal wins exceeding $1 billion, 12th consecutive quarter of margin expansion, and announced a 1:1 bonus issue on its 40th anniversary.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue:** US$1.693 billion (+6.8% YoY reported, +7.2% YoY CC, +2.4% QoQ CC); Rs 16,180 crore (+15.6% YoY, +3% QoQ). Organic CC revenue grew 7.0% YoY and 2.3% QoQ.
* **EBIT & Margin:** EBIT of US$250 million; EBIT margin at 14.8% (+260 bps YoY, +40 bps QoQ). In INR terms, operating profit was Rs 2,387 crore (+40.5% YoY).
* **Net Profit (PAT):** US$166 million (+23.2% YoY); Rs 1,586 crore (PAT margin of 9.8%, up 130 bps YoY).
* **Deal Wins:** US$1.023 billion in Q2 FY27; LTM deal wins stood at US$4.270 billion (+34.8% YoY).
* **Cash Flow & Capital Allocation:** Free Cash Flow of US$135 million for Q2. Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) at 31.2% (up 680 bps YoY, up 290 bps QoQ). Interim dividend declared at Rs 15 per share.
* **Effective Tax Rate (ETR):** 29.9% (26.6% excluding a one-time prior-period tax adjustment).
* **Working Capital:** DSO stood at 88 days (up 4 days QoQ, down 6 days YoY).

### Highlights
* Board recommended a 1:1 bonus share issue in celebration of the company's 40th anniversary.
* Vertical YoY growth (Constant Currency): Communications (+9.5%), Retail, Travel & Logistics (+9.2%), Manufacturing (+7.0%), Hi-Tech (+6.7%), Healthcare (+6.2%), and BFSI (+4.6%).
* Top 20 million-plus revenue accounts grew 3.1% QoQ and 11.7% YoY.
* Launched *Zero-Gravity Telco Architecture* to optimize enterprise architecture for AI deployment in telecom operators.
* Announced an expanded partnership with ServiceNow and an AI-driven Security Service Edge solution with Cisco; partnered with SpaceX.ai.
* Partnered with IHub Quantum Technology Foundation to build middleware for India’s 20-qubit trapped-ion quantum computer by December 2026.
* Upskilled >24,000 associates on AI productivity tools and >4,500 on agent platforms; ~48,000 associates hold AI certifications.
* Introduced plans to disclose AI revenue reporting starting in H2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management reiterated its commitment to hit the full-year FY27 EBIT margin target of 15.0%.
* Target to convert 90% to 100% of PAT to Free Cash Flow on a full-year basis remains intact.
* Management expects normal seasonality in H2 FY27 without unusual furloughs or seasonal weakness, outside of a minor tail-end impact in European auto manufacturing.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Enterprise adoption is shifting rapidly from AI experimentation to scale execution; clients are diversifying AI workloads towards cheaper open-source and open-weight models alongside frontier models.
* Competition, rather than pure budget diversion into AI models, remains the primary driver of industry pricing dynamics.
* Growth momentum remains strong across Digital Enterprise Applications (+13% YoY) and BPS (+6.5% YoY), while experience design and mid-market consulting remain soft during strategic repositioning.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Margin expansion in Q2 was driven by volume growth and savings under *Project Fortius*, partially offset by wage hikes.
* Management highlighted gross margin leverage through AI toolchains releasing headcount from fixed-price engagements, alongside unexercised pyramid rationalization upside (historically a 3%–4% margin gap vs. peers).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Established the Toronto Innovation Hub and an Engineering Experience Center during the quarter to drive co-creation and product commercialization.
* Launched *Indus-Terra*, a 30-billion-parameter domain model in partnership with NVIDIA and AWS as part of the IndiaAI Mission.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Hedge book stood at US$0.4 billion as of September 30, 2026 (all under 12-month tenure). MTM hedge gain of US$13.83 million recorded (US$8.7 million in P&L, US$5 million in reserves).
* Capital allocation policy remains committed to returning 85%+ of overall FCF to shareholders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* European automotive manufacturing vertical declined 3.9% QoQ due to high prior-quarter execution base; a minor sequential revenue headwind of ~0.5% is expected to spill into Q3 FY27.
* Cash flow conversion in Q2 was temporarily impacted by working capital requirements from ramping up rebadged large deals, which management expects to normalize in Q3.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Kumar Rakesh (BNP Paribas):** *Query on gross margin expansion limits following headcount additions.*  
  *Management Answer:* Confirmed gross margin opportunities are not tapped out. High utilization will be offset by AI toolchains freeing up talent from fixed-price projects, plus ongoing room for long-term pyramid optimization.
* **Sudheer Guntupalli (AMC):** *Query on whether AI coding tools like Claude Code indicate deflationary risks or if FY28 could see double-digit growth.*  
  *Management Answer:* Refuted the deflation narrative, emphasizing that AI expands project scope and business outcomes rather than just reducing hours. Declined to give formal growth targets for the next 3-year cycle, but stated the operational strategy is explicitly pivoting toward growth outperformance.
* **Abhishek Pathak (ICICI Securities):** *Query on trade-off between margin expansion and growth, and execution in BFSI.*  
  *Management Answer:* Reaching close to the 15% EBIT margin target allows TechM to focus heavily on profitable top-line growth. Admitted BFSI acceleration was a slower burn than initially expected, but leadership adjustments and market scale position it to return to >8% growth rates.
* **Rod B:** *Query on source of growth and long-term commercial upside from Quantum technology.*  
  *Management Answer:* Growth is led by strategic $20M+ accounts (+11.7% YoY). Quantum is seeing early adoption in network optimization/energy reduction for telcos and post-quantum cryptography; expects commercial monetization beyond the next 12 months as qubit counts rise.
* **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley):** *Query on outcome-based non-linear commercial models for AI.*  
  *Management Answer:* Adopted joint human-plus-agent pricing models incorporating token economics in processes like F&A and Telecom operations, gradually shifting client contracts away from classic T&M/Fixed Price setups.

### One-line takeaway
Tech Mahindra delivered strong Q2 FY27 organic growth (7% YoY CC), expanded EBIT margin to 14.8%, announced a 1:1 bonus issue, and reaffirmed its 15.0% full-year FY27 EBIT margin target.
