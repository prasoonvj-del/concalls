# Tech Mahindra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Broad-based revenue growth, 12th consecutive quarter of margin expansion, and strong deal wins exceeding $1 billion.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** US$1,693 million (up 6.8% YoY reported, 7.2% YoY in constant currency, 2.4% QoQ in constant currency); INR 16,180 crore (up 15.6% YoY, 3% QoQ).
- **Organic Revenue Growth (Constant Currency):** 7% YoY, 2.3% QoQ.
- **Deal Wins:** US$1,023 million for the quarter; last-12-month deal wins at US$4,270 million (up 34.8% YoY).
- **EBIT Margin:** 14.8% (up 40 bps QoQ, 260 bps YoY); EBIT stood at US$250 million (INR 2,387 crore).
- **PAT:** US$166 million (up 23.2% YoY); INR 1,586 crore with a PAT margin of 9.8% (up 130 bps YoY).
- **Free Cash Flow:** US$135 million generated during the quarter.
- **DSO:** 88 days (up 4 days QoQ, down 6 days YoY).
- **ROCE:** 31.2% (up 290 bps QoQ, 680 bps YoY).
- **Hedge Book:** US$0.4 billion (less than 12 months).
- **Interim Dividend:** INR 15 per share.
- **Bonus Share Issue:** Recommended in the ratio of 1:1.

### Highlights
- Celebrated the company's 40th anniversary.
- **Vertical Performance (YoY Constant Currency / reported highlights):** Communications grew 9.5% YoY (supported by Orange deal ramp-up); Retail, Travel and Logistics grew 9.2% YoY; Manufacturing grew 7% YoY; Hi-Tech grew 6.7% YoY; Healthcare grew 6.2% YoY; BFSI grew 4.6% YoY.
- **TechM Helix (AI initiative):** Scaled AI toolchains across service lines and industry verticals; launched 25 prioritized industry/horizontal AI use cases with over 100 vertical use cases in progress.
- Launched Zero-Gravity Telco Architecture and Consumer Agentic Experience Studio.
- Established Toronto Innovation Hub and Engineering Experience Center; launched Indus-Terra (30-billion-parameter model with NVIDIA and AWS).
- Workforce enablement: Over 24,000 IT associates upskilled on AI productivity tools, 4,500+ on agent platforms, and ~48,000 hold an AI certification.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated that they remain on track to achieve the full-year FY27 EBIT margin target of 15%.
- Expects normal seasonality in the second half of the year without unreasonable furloughs in Q3 or Q4.
- Plans to introduce AI revenue reporting as part of H2 FY27 disclosures.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Seeing broad-based growth across verticals and geographies.
- Digital enterprise applications (ServiceNow, Salesforce, SAP, Oracle) grew nearly 13% YoY.
- Accounts with >US$20 million in revenue grew 11.7% YoY, outpacing the rest of the company.
- Transitioning from AI experimentation to enterprise-scale adoption, focusing on agentic business processes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin improvement driven by strong volume growth and Project Fortius savings, partially offset by wage hikes.
- Effective tax rate was 29.9% due to a one-time prior-period tax adjustment (26.6% excluding it).
- Utilization remained high, but management expects AI toolchains to release headcount from fixed-price projects to fuel further growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Free cash flow to PAT target maintained at 90% to 100% on a full-year basis.
- Capital allocation policy reaffirmed to return 85% and above of free cash flow to shareholders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flow impacted this quarter by the ramp-up of new rebadged deals, expected to normalize next quarter.
- Hedge book mark-to-market movement was US$13.83 million ($8.7 million in P&L, $5 million in reserves).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued sequential revenue headwind expected in Q3 in the Manufacturing vertical (European automotive program) to a much smaller extent following accelerated execution in Q1.
- ADMS business seeing productivity expectations on large contract renewals, making it slightly muted.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kumar Rakesh (BNP Paribas) on service capabilities and margins:** Asked how TechM Orion drives share gains and if gross margin expansion is near peak. *Management stated that AI toolchains across service lines will continue to drive productivity and margin opportunities, and structural skill advantages have not been fully tapped.*
2. **Sudheer Guntupalli (AMC) on AI deflation and growth milestones:** Asked if AI deflationary impact has peaked and how to think about double-digit growth. *Management noted that the AI opportunity is about expanding scope and outcomes rather than just efficiency, and emphasized that the company's focus is firmly on growth while declining to give a 3-year plan commitment prematurely.*
3. **Abhishek Pathak (Emkay) on margin-growth trade-off and BFSI:** Asked if margins would be sacrificed for growth and questioned BFSI growth interventions. *Management responded that AI toolchain productivity allows both growth and margin expansion, and noted that while BFSI has been a slower burn than expected, leadership changes and focus remain strong.*
4. **Rod B (Analyst) on revenue concentration and quantum technology:** Asked whether growth stems from wallet share in existing accounts and the 3-5 year outlook for quantum. *Management highlighted that >$20M accounts grew 11.7% YoY, and stated that quantum tech (like network optimization and post-quantum crypto) will become commercially viable as qubit counts rise beyond the next 12 months.*
5. **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley) on Comms outlook and pricing models:** Inquired about Comms pipeline visibility and outcome-based agent pricing adoption. *Management confirmed strong Comms visibility and noted growing enterprise adoption of human-and-agent collaborative pricing models (Vector Squad).*

### One-line takeaway
Tech Mahindra delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 7.2% YoY constant currency revenue growth, a 260 bps YoY expansion in EBIT margins to 14.8%, and strong momentum driven by Project Helix and broad-based large deal wins.
