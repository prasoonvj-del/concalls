# Mold-Tek Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported fabulous results, with standalone H1 PAT doubling last full year's PAT and robust growth across divisions.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 46.64 crores, up 25% YoY
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 10.16 crores, up from Rs 3.15 crores YoY (more than tripled)
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Up by 32.5% YoY
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Up by 39% to Rs 92 crores
- **Standalone PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 20.19 crores (4.4x growth)
- **Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 18.62 crores (375% / 4.7x jump YoY)
- **Forex gain/loss (Q2 FY27):** Forex loss of around Rs 70 lakhs (compared to Rs 1.5 crores gain in Q1)
- **Beryl Q2 Net Loss:** $25,000 (improved from $70,000 to $75,000 loss in the previous quarter)
- **Per capita revenue contribution:** Increased from 4,500 to 5,220 (over 15-16% YoY improvement)
- **Guidance:** 18-20% top-line growth in consolidated and around 20-25% in standalone for the next 12 months.

### Highlights
- Standalone H1 PAT matched the last full year's PAT of Rs 10 crores, reaching Rs 20 crores.
- Improved product mix, diversification, operational excellence, and entry into high value-added engineering services (poles, towers, and structural steel detailing/design) drove performance.
- Loss-making BIW (Body in White) segment successfully downsized from 150-160 resources to 40-50 resources.
- Acquired company Beryl received major orders (including a ~$1,000,000 Hillsborough County order) and is operational from October onwards; expected to reach breakeven in Q3.
- Added a new domain in civil and land development in the US through an MOU with a client, with operations scaling from November/January.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects at least 18-20% top-line growth in consolidated and 20-25% in standalone for the next 12 months, subject to dollar fluctuations.
- Management is confident that company performance will remain on a positive track for the next 1 to 2 years.
- Beryl is expected to break even in Q3 and contribute handsomely from next financial year (FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural steel detailing constitutes about 75-80% of revenue.
- Power distribution services (poles and towers) are experiencing massive demand due to data center growth in the US; team size is being ramped up from 70-75 towards 120 people by March.
- US clients have no choice but to outsource core engineering (civil, mechanical) due to a rapid dimunition of internal engineering talent.
- Residential engineering, civil, and architectural services represent a market 40-50 times larger than high-rise commercial steel detailing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operational efficiencies drove per-person revenues up by nearly 15-16% YoY.
- Incentives, retraining, multi-skilling, and automation are improving manpower utilization without requiring large recruitment drives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Beryl's design head (an L-1 visa holder) and a supporting team of 5 in Hyderabad have been established to shift Beryl's focus from pure inspection to design.
- Actively evaluating structural engineering acquisition targets in the US that possess scale-up capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not specifically discussed in detail, other than the elimination of loss-making segments and improving profitability.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential risks include unpredictable US construction demand patterns or a slowdown in AI/data center infrastructure spends, though mitigated by broader service expansion into residential and land development.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kaustav Bubna (BMSBL Capital) asked about margin sustainability and FY28 guidance.** Management stated uncertainty from EV/automotive and BIW segments has been eliminated, assuring a positive trajectory for 1-1.5 years with 18-25% top-line growth.
2. **Rupesh Tatia (Long Equity Partners) asked about Beryl's breakeven and division breakups.** Management noted Beryl's loss narrowed to $25,000 in Q2 and expects breakeven in Q3, while steel detailing accounts for ~75-80% of standalone revenue.
3. **Manish (Individual Investor) inquired about forex impacts.** Management noted a Rs 70 lakh forex loss in Q2 compared to a Rs 1.5 crore gain in Q1, expecting neutral-to-positive forex if the rupee stabilizes at 95 against the dollar.
4. **Riya Shah (Aarti Securities) asked about data center repeatability and manpower needs.** Management confirmed ongoing data center projects and stated that internal productivity gains (up 15%) mean massive new hiring is unnecessary.
5. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked about employee utilization.** Management explained that further efficiency gains of ~15% are targeted via automation and incentivization without increasing headcount.

### One-line takeaway
Mold-Tek Technologies delivered a stellar Q2/H1 FY27, more than doubling standalone profits driven by operational efficiencies, the downsizing of loss-making segments, and scaling high-value US engineering verticals.
