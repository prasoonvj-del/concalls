# Tata Consultancy Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-14 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained growth momentum, healthy order book, and AI revenue crossing the $3.1 billion annualized milestone, despite selective and uneven demand.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 73,188 crore (up 1.3% QoQ and 11.2% YoY)
- Revenue in USD: $7,642 million (up 20 bps QoQ and 2.4% YoY)
- Constant currency revenue growth: 0.5% QoQ and 2.8% YoY (International revenue grew 1.2% QoQ in constant currency)
- Operating Margin (EBIT): 24%
- Net Margin: 19%
- TCV: US$9.6 billion (excluding Porsche and Best Buy deals)
- Annualized AI Revenue: Reached $3.1 billion (crossed 10% of overall revenue)
- LTM IT Services Attrition: 13.3% (down 30 bps QoQ)
- Cash Conversion: 102.2% of net income
- Funds Invested: $5.98 billion
- DSO: 74 days (constant sequentially in dollar terms)
- Total Workforce: 598,056 associates

### Highlights
- Growth broadened across core portfolios: BFSI grew 2.5% QoQ, Manufacturing grew 3.1% QoQ, and Technology & Services grew 3.1% QoQ in constant currency.
- International markets grew sequentially led by UK (+3.5%), North America (+0.4%), and Continental Europe (+0.4%).
- Strategic partnerships and M&A: Partnership with Porsche and proposed acquisition of MHP (subject to regulatory approvals) strengthen AI-powered mobility and engineering; Best Buy GCC engagement highlights capability to build AI-native global capability centers.
- Launched TCS ADD AgentHub for drug discovery, domain-specific agentic control planes, and Physical AI capabilities (Autonomous Engineering Lab with NVIDIA in Bengaluru and an AI-first lights-out factory lab in Pune).
- Learning hours increased 17% QoQ to 17.1 million hours; onboarded 10,000 university graduates globally this quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide specific revenue or earnings guidance.
- Management remains confident that growth momentum will continue on a medium-term basis, supported by TCV, pipeline, and client conversations.
- Management maintains its long-term operating margin aspiration of 26% to 28%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand environment has not materially changed; discretionary programs without near-term value remain under scrutiny.
- Three distinct AI demand patterns: (1) AI-native solutions with business outcomes, (2) AI-led transformation of enterprise systems, and (3) Autonomous GBS.
- BFSI: Strongest large vertical, growing 2.5% QoQ and 3.9% YoY in constant currency, driven by banking, capital markets, and insurance.
- Manufacturing: Grew 3.1% QoQ and 4.2% YoY, driven by factory automation, engineering productivity, and industrial AI.
- Technology and Services: Grew 3.1% QoQ and 4.8% YoY, driven by enterprise software, cloud modernization, and agentic AI adoption.
- Consumer Business: Declined 0.7% QoQ and 1% YoY due to sensitivity to inflation and selective consumer spending.
- Life Sciences and Healthcare: Grew 0.3% QoQ with stable demand in compliance and clinical platforms.
- Communications and Media: Broadly flat, with digital platform growth offset by telecom pressure.
- Energy, Resources, and Utilities: Declined 0.5% QoQ, though grid modernization and asset optimization remain relevant.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins remained stable at 24% sequentially, impacted by investments in strategic partnerships, M&A initiatives, niche talent hiring, higher bench, and increased subcontractor costs.
- Subcontractor costs increased across India and overseas to meet near-term demand-supply skill mismatches and visa fee impacts.
- Performance-linked bonus outlay was lower this quarter due to performance against internal targets.
- Near-term margin headwinds for H2 include MHP acquisition-related dilution (approx. 50 bps), normal furlough seasonality, and continued ecosystem investments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocation policy remains unchanged to return substantial free cash flows while judiciously investing in long-term growth.
- Data center expansion capacity will be planned once contracts are secured, with an 18 to 24 month timeline for revenue generation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash conversion at 102.2% of net income.
- Funds invested at the end of the period stood at $5.98 billion.
- DSO stood at 74 days in constant dollar terms.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Discretionary programs without near-term value remain under scrutiny.
- Regional markets (such as India) experience lumpiness and project deferrals, leading to volatility.
- Margin headwinds from MHP acquisition dilution and upcoming furloughs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sudheer Guntupalli (Kotak Mahindra Asset Management) vs. K Krithivasan:** Asked about AI deflation versus incrementality. Management noted that while deflation exists due to productivity, AI is also used for modernization and vendor consolidation, creating new opportunities.
2. **Ravi Menon (Macquarie) vs. Aarthi Subramanian:** Asked whether AI revenues act as an inflection point similar to digital past trends. Management stated that growth is coming from traditional services (via vendor consolidation and AI-powered engineering) alongside rapid AI growth.
3. **Ruchi Mukhija (ICICI Securities) vs. Samir Seksaria:** Asked about gross margin declines and H2 margin commitments. Management quantified the MHP dilution impact at approx. 50 bps and stated they will endeavor to inch up margins while prioritizing investments.
4. **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama Equities) vs. K Krithivasan / Aarthi Subramanian:** Asked about client adoption of LLM/SLM application layers and agentic control planes. Management confirmed strong traction in agentic control planes, particularly in BFSI, Manufacturing, and Life Sciences.
5. **Rishi Jhunjhunwala (IIFL Institutional Equities) vs. Samir Seksaria:** Inquired about the sharp increase in external consultant, equipment, and project delivery costs. Management clarified these reflect upfront capacity and partnership investments that will sweat out over time, alongside traditional margin levers.

### One-line takeaway
TCS delivered steady constant currency revenue growth of 0.5% QoQ and 2.8% YoY with operating margins at 24%, driven by strong traction in AI revenues ($3.1 billion annualized) and broad-based vertical performance despite near-term investment headwinds.
