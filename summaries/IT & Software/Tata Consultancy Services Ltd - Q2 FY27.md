# Tata Consultancy Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-14 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Steady international growth and expanding AI revenue offset near-term margin headwinds from elevated subcon costs and project deferrals in India.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** ₹73,188 crore (up 1.3% QoQ, 11.2% YoY; in USD: $7,642 million, up 0.2% QoQ, 2.4% YoY; CC growth: 0.5% QoQ, 2.8% YoY).
- **International Revenue Growth:** 1.2% QoQ in constant currency.
- **Operating Margin (EBIT):** 24.0% (stable QoQ).
- **Net Margin:** 19.0%.
- **Order Book (TCV):** $9.6 billion (excludes Porsche and Best Buy engagements).
- **Annualized AI Revenue:** $3.1 billion (exceeded 10% of total revenue).
- **DSO:** 74 days (flat QoQ in USD terms).
- **Cash Conversion:** 102.2% of net income; invested funds stood at $5.98 billion.
- **Headcount:** 598,056 associates; LTM IT attrition at 13.3% (down 30 bps QoQ).

### Highlights
- Announced partnership with Porsche and proposed acquisition of MHP (subject to regulatory approvals) to create an AI Centre of Excellence for mobility.
- Selected by Best Buy to transform and build AI-native Global Capability Centers (GCCs).
- Launched TCS ADD AgentHub for drug discovery, an Autonomous Engineering Lab with NVIDIA in Bengaluru, and an AI-first lights-out factory lab in Pune.
- Ranked #1 Salesforce Services partner by leading analyst firms.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated that TCS does not provide specific revenue or earnings guidance.
- Management expects medium-term growth momentum to continue supported by strong deal TCV and client pipeline.
- MHP acquisition consolidation is expected to dilute operating margins by ~50 bps depending on transaction closure timing.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall demand environment remains selective; non-near-term discretionary projects face continued scrutiny.
- **BFSI:** Grew 2.5% QoQ CC (3.9% YoY CC), led by UK, Europe, and APAC across banking, capital markets, and insurance.
- **Manufacturing:** Grew 3.1% QoQ CC (4.2% YoY CC), driven by factory automation, supply chain resilience, and industrial AI.
- **Technology & Services:** Grew 3.1% QoQ CC (4.8% YoY CC), driven by enterprise software and digital transformation.
- **Consumer Business:** Declined 0.7% QoQ CC (1.0% YoY CC), impacted by inflation and insourcing pressure.
- **Life Sciences & Healthcare:** Grew 0.3% QoQ CC.
- **Energy, Resources & Utilities:** Declined 0.5% QoQ CC.
- **Geographies (QoQ CC):** UK (+3.5%), Continental Europe (+0.4%), North America (+0.4%). India declined ~10% QoQ (up 6% YoY) due to project deferrals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margin was unchanged QoQ at 24.0%, supported by currency gains and operating leverage.
- Headwinds included increased subcontracting costs, strategic ecosystem investments, higher bench, and M&A initiatives.
- Variable pay outlay was lower during the quarter based on internal target achievement.
- Long-term margin beacon remains at 26%–28%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Onboarded 10,000 campus graduates in Q2 (totaling 24,000 in H1 FY27).
- Planned data center services capacity expansion will be tied to client contract wins, taking 18–24 months before generating revenue.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash conversion remained healthy at 102.2% of net income.
- Total funds invested stood at $5.98 billion at quarter-end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in regional and India domestic revenues due to project deferrals and non-annuity contract delivery.
- Upcoming H2 headwinds including seasonal client furloughs and ~50 bps margin dilution from the MHP acquisition.
- Elevated subcontractor and external consultant fees weighing on gross margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sudheer Guntupalli (Kotak Mahindra AMC):** Asked if increased subcontractor cost was driven by visa fee hikes.  
  *Management response:* Confirmed increase spans India and overseas to address near-term skill mismatches; visa fees were only a partial factor.
- **Ravi Menon (Macquarie):** Inquired about the 10% QoQ revenue drop in India and lower $100M client counts.  
  *Management response:* India drop was due to volatile project deferrals (expected to resume later); the $100M client drop was strictly due to foreign currency fluctuations.
- **Ruchi Mukhija (ICICI Securities):** Asked if margin targets face pressure from MHP acquisition and investments.  
  *Management response:* Acknowledged ~50 bps dilution from MHP and H2 seasonality, but stated management will continue working to "inch up" margins while prioritizing growth investments.
- **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama Equities):** Clarified if gross margin pressure stems from pricing headwinds.  
  *Management response:* Confirmed portfolio pricing remains broadly stable, with AI-led services commanding higher margins.
- **Rishi Jhunjhunwala (IIFL):** Questioned the steep YoY increase in external consultant fees and delivery project expenses.  
  *Management response:* Costs reflect direct delivery fulfillment, partnership ecosystem commitments, and front-loaded capacity creation for incoming demand.

### One-line takeaway
TCS sustained steady international revenue growth and crossed $3.1 billion in annualized AI revenue while absorbing near-term margin drag from capacity investments and regional market deferrals.
