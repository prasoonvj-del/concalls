# Dev Information Technology Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-12 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q4 FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strategic global partnerships, achievement of all Microsoft solution partner designations, and a focus on expanding enterprise-led capabilities and profitability.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated total income: Rs 56 crore (up 8.1% YoY) for Q4 FY26; Rs 193.50 crore for FY26.
- Q4 FY26 EBITDA: Rs 5.04 crore (up 68.5% YoY).
- Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin: 8.99% (up 322 bps YoY from 5.77%).
- Q4 FY26 Net profit: Rs 8.96 crore (vs Rs 1.13 crore in Q4 FY25).
- FY26 Net profit: Rs 75.60 crore (includes an exceptional unutilized gain from the reclassification of Dev Accelerator investment post-IPO).
- Diluted EPS for FY26: Rs 13.25.
- FY26 Export business revenue: Around Rs 45 crore.
- Cloud revenue: Rs 25 crore.
- Cybersecurity revenue: Rs 5 crore to Rs 8 crore.
- India government/corporate business revenue: Around Rs 110 crore.
- Current order book: Rs 50 crore to Rs 60 crore.
- Employee count: 1,000 to 1,100 people.
- XDuce partnership revenue expectations: $1 million to $2 million for the current year; $3 million to $5 million for FY27, growing 15% to 20% YoY thereafter.

### Highlights
- XDuce Infotech acquired a 25% stake in Dev IT from promoters via market value.
- Management signed a shareholding agreement with UCI New York (25% stake acquisition) to unlock over $3 million in U.S. Microsoft Dynamics business.
- Achieved all 6 Microsoft Solution Partner Designations (SPDs).
- Board approved the transfer of Talligence and product businesses to Technosys Private Limited for a cash consideration of approx. Rs 11.90 crores.
- Signed an exclusive master distribution agreement with A21 Technologies for the Talligence AI platform across India and MENA.
- Participated in a joint venture (Scaleax) to provide GCC-as-a-Service in GIFT City, Gujarat.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue target: Around Rs 200 crore.
- FY28 revenue growth target: 15% to 20% growth over the current year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural tailwinds remain strong in cloud adoption, regional transformation, cybersecurity, AI, automation, and data modernization.
- India's domestic market is growing faster than the export business, providing visibility and stability against geopolitical disruptions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- FY26 EBITDA stood at Rs 7.23 crore with an EBITDA margin of Rs 3.74 crore [as stated in transcript].
- Margin pressures in FY26 were attributed to a conscious focus on the lower-margin India market for existence/stability, delays in strategic integrations requiring third-party outsourcing, and investments in ABCD talent.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Established physical presence alignment in North America via XDuce and New York via UCI.
- Continuing heavy investments in skill development and enterprise solutions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a focus on cash flows to ensure deal execution does not negatively impact liquidity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations and global market conditions could impact business continuity and global blockchain adoption.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shamit Thakur (SKP Securities) on XDuce partnership benefits and revenue split:** Management explained XDuce brings on-site enterprise presence in the US/UK while Dev IT acts as the offshore delivery center. Revenue split for non-India deals is structured around 80% for Dev IT and 20% for XDuce.
- **Rupen Mehta (Individual Investor) on Microsoft SPDs:** Management noted that achieving all 6 designations places them in the top ~5% of partners globally, directly helping close enterprise deals like a recent win in Texas.
- **Vinod Shah (Individual Investor) on revenue growth holding back:** Management reiterated a strategic pause/focus on the domestic Indian market last year to hedge against geopolitical risks and build base stability.
- **Parag Dave (Dave Ventures) on EBITDA margin decline:** Management cited lower-margin domestic orders, lack of large exceptional gains compared to previous periods, and outsourced delivery costs due to delayed internal readiness.
- **Sanika Deshmukh (Finix Capital) on product transfer to Technosys:** Management clarified that separating products (Talligence/ByteSIGNER) from the core service business allows better valuation, attracts strategic product investors, and improves Dev IT's service-focused EBITDA.

### One-line takeaway
Dev IT delivered steady revenue growth and margin expansion in Q4 FY26 while pivoting toward North American enterprise markets through strategic equity alignments with XDuce and UCI.
