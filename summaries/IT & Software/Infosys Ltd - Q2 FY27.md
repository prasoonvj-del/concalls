# Infosys Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong AI growth ($10.5% of revenue$) and robust mega deal wins ($5.2 bn TCV) were offset by softer volumes, cautious discretionary spend, and lower pricing expansion leading to a guidance cut.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** $5,145 mn, up 1.5% YoY and 1.2% QoQ in constant currency (CC) terms (acquisitions contributed ~0.4% QoQ).
- **AI Revenue:** 10.5% of total revenue (up from 8.2% in Q1 FY27), growing 29% QoQ.
- **Large Deals TCV:** $5.2 bn across 19 deals (62% net new), including a $2 bn+ 7-year net-new deal and a $1 bn renewal. H1 TCV reached $8.8 bn (up 28% YoY).
- **Operating Margin:** Stable at 21.0% (down 10 bps QoQ; H1 at 21.0%).
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** ₹19.32, up 8.7% YoY (H1 EPS: ₹38.51, up 11.7% YoY).
- **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** $928 mn (113% of net profit).
- **Interim Dividend:** ₹26 per share, up 13% YoY.
- **Workforce & Utilization:** Net headcount increased by ~4,400 employees; 8,000 freshers hired in Q2 (~13,000 in H1). Utilization stood at 85.4% (incl. trainees) and 81.9% (excl. trainees). Attrition declined to 12.7% (from 13.0% QoQ).

### Highlights
- Signed a net-new $2 bn+ 7-year deal with an international financial services client, expected to start transitioning in late Q4 FY27 and contribute >1% revenue growth in FY28.
- Renewed a $1 bn mega deal with an existing financial services client.
- Topaz Fabric ecosystem integrates 15 foundation models (open-weight and closed models) for token cost optimization and enterprise deployment.
- Capital allocation policy remains intact to return 85% of FCF over 5 years via dividends and buybacks.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 CC Revenue Growth:** Guidance revised down to 1% to 2% YoY (from 1.5% to 3.0% previously). 
  - Management noted the lower end assumes further macro deterioration while the upper end assumes improvement.
  - Offshoring shifts and the earlier decision not to pursue a low-return European manufacturing deal continue to impact full-year growth by ~1.8% to 2%.
  - Financial Services (FS) and Energy, Utilities, Resources & Services (EURS) are expected to grow above company average.
- **FY27 Operating Margin:** Guidance maintained at 20% to 22%. Management expects H2 margins to face headwinds from compensation hikes (effective October 2026 and January 2027), lower revenue seasonality, deal ramp-ups, and productivity sharing, offset by Project Maximus savings.
- **Effective Tax Rate:** Guidance maintained at 29% to 30% for FY27.
- **Hiring:** Target of hiring ~20,000 college graduates in FY27 remains unchanged.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprise spending remains cautious due to geopolitical tensions, high interest rate yields, tariffs, and budget constraints.
- **Financial Services:** Robust growth with $3.5 bn+ in deal wins in Q2 across global/regional banks, payments, and capital markets; mortgage sector faces potential slowdown from rate expectations.
- **Manufacturing:** Near-term pressures, client-specific revenue decline, high competitive intensity around AI productivity, and deferred discretionary spend.
- **EURS:** Stable demand focused on targeted high-impact programs, cost take-outs, vendor consolidation, and core modernization.
- **Retail:** Softness due to lower third-party revenues, subdued consumer demand, and supply chain disruptions affecting consumer tech/logistics.
- **Hi-Tech:** Weakness driven by lower third-party revenues and budget cuts/ramp-downs at a major US client.
- **Communications:** Challenging environment with tight control on discretionary spend and pricing pressure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margin Walk (QoQ):**
  - *Headwinds:* -70 bps from sequential change in post-sales customer support, -20 bps from acquisition amortization.
  - *Tailwinds:* +40 bps from Rupee depreciation, +20 bps from Project Maximus, +20 bps from calendar/working days.
- **Technical Subcontracting:** Stable at 8.9% of revenue (management targets 8%–9% band).
- **Onsite Mix:** Reduced by 70 bps in H1 to 22.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No specific capex guidance or capacity figures were disclosed.
- Freshers intake planned at ~20,000 for FY27, with >50% completed in H1.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stood at $4.7 bn with a debt-free balance sheet.
- DSO stood at 64 days (down 7 days YoY); DSO including unbilled (net of unearned) was 77 days (down 10 days YoY).
- Yield on cash investments stood at ~6.9% in Q2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Competitive pricing intensity and aggressive AI-driven productivity pass-through asks compressing traditional billable work.
- Slower-than-anticipated discretionary spend recovery in Retail and Hi-Tech.
- H2 margin headwinds from two-phase wage increases and client furloughs.
- Revenue impact in Q4 FY27 from a previously called-out European manufacturing client program exit.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kumar Rakesh (BNP Paribas):** *Is the organic business setup improving for next year given the $2 bn mega deal?*  
  *Management Response:* Salil Parekh confirmed confidence that organic growth will be in good shape next year, supported by 29% QoQ AI growth, the $2 bn deal (>1% revenue contribution in FY28), and a strong mega deal pipeline.
- **Bryan Bergin (TD Cowen):** *What caused the guidance revision midpoint cut?*  
  *Management Response:* Jayesh Sanghrajka explained that expected pricing increases did not materialize due to competitive intensity, volume growth in Q2 was softer than expected, and discretionary spend slowed in Retail and Hi-Tech.
- **Yogesh Aggarwal (HSBC):** *Is Hi-Tech weakness spread across clients or isolated?*  
  *Management Response:* Salil Parekh clarified that Hi-Tech weakness was primarily concentrated in one large US client, while Banking, EURS, Healthcare, and parts of Manufacturing show strong broad-based traction.
- **Jonathan Lee (Guggenheim):** *How long can Project Maximus offset H2 margin headwinds from compensation hikes and amortization?*  
  *Management Response:* Jayesh Sanghrajka emphasized historical margin discipline (maintaining 21% despite 50 bps acquisition headwinds and tech investments) and stated management remains confident in achieving the 20%–22% full-year margin band.
- **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama):** *Is AI revenue genuine net-new work or reclassified legacy revenue?*  
  *Management Response:* Management confirmed AI revenues (10.5% of total) represent net-new transformation and modernization work. Salil Parekh added that while legacy deflation is a finite process, AI revenue growth is a long-term secular trend.

### One-line takeaway
Infosys scaled its AI revenues to 10.5% and secured $5.2 bn in large deals, but cautious client spend and pricing pressure forced a cut in FY27 revenue growth guidance to 1%–2%.
