# Infosys Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong AI growth and record large deal wins are being offset by revenue guidance cuts, pricing pressure, and softness in discretionary spending verticals.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue Growth:** 1.5% YoY, 1.2% QoQ (constant currency).
*   **AI Revenue:** 10.5% of total revenue (up from 8.2% in Q1), growing 29% QoQ.
*   **Large Deals:** $5.2 bn TCV (62% net new).
*   **Operating Margin:** 21%.
*   **Free Cash Flow:** $928 mn.
*   **EPS:** Rs 19.32 (up 8.7% YoY).
*   **Attrition:** 12.7%.
*   **Utilization:** 85.4% (including trainees).

### Highlights
*   **Mega Deals:** Signed two mega deals: one >$2 bn (net new, 7-year, expected to contribute >1% revenue growth in FY28) and one $1 bn (renewal).
*   **Dividend:** Board announced an interim dividend of Rs 26 per share.
*   **Hiring:** Net headcount increased by 4,400; 13,000 freshers hired in H1 (target ~20,000 for FY27).
*   **Project Maximus:** Management continues to utilize this program to support margins.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Revised to 1% to 2% YoY (constant currency) from previous 1.5% to 3%.
*   **Operating Margin:** Maintained at 20% to 22%.
*   **Outlook:** Management expects H2 margins to face headwinds from compensation hikes, seasonality, and large deal ramp-ups, partially offset by Project Maximus.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Strong:** Financial Services, Energy & Utilities, and Manufacturing.
*   **Weak:** Retail (impacted by lower third-party revenues/consumer demand) and Hi-Tech (impacted by client-specific ramp-downs/budget cuts).
*   **Pricing:** Management noted competitive intensity and pricing pressure due to client expectations for AI-led productivity commitments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Walk:** Margins remained stable at 21%.
    *   **Headwinds:** Post-sales customer support (-70 bps), acquisition impact (-20 bps).
    *   **Tailwinds:** Rupee depreciation (+40 bps), Project Maximus (+20 bps), higher working days (+20 bps).
*   **Subcontracting:** Costs remain in the 8-9% range of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Utilization:** 85.4% (including trainees).
*   **Offshoring:** Management noted an increase in offshoring as part of cost-reduction efforts for clients.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash:** $4.7 bn in cash and cash equivalents.
*   **Debt:** Debt-free balance sheet.
*   **DSO:** 64 days (down 7 days YoY).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro:** Geopolitical tensions, interest rates, and tariffs constraining discretionary spend.
*   **Pricing:** Competitive intensity and AI productivity commitments are putting pressure on pricing models.
*   **Guidance Risk:** Lower end of guidance assumes further macro deterioration; upper end assumes improvement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why narrow guidance despite healthy TCV?** A: Management cited softer H1 performance, pricing pressure, and discretionary spend weakness in Retail/Hi-Tech.
*   **Q: Is AI revenue cannibalizing traditional services?** A: Management stated AI is driving new work (e.g., legacy modernization) and is a secular growth trend, though it creates finite productivity deflation in legacy portfolios.
*   **Q: Why are subcontractor costs high?** A: Management explained it is for immediate skill needs and geographical requirements; they aim to keep it in the 8-9% range.
*   **Q: Impact of US visa/PERM changes?** A: Management sees no material impact on attrition or costs as they rely heavily on local US hires.
*   **Q: Are AI projects higher margin?** A: Management declined to share specific AI margins but noted they are competitive and comfortable with overall company margin guidance.

### One-line takeaway
Infosys is successfully scaling AI and securing mega deals, but faces near-term revenue headwinds from discretionary spending softness and pricing pressure, leading to a downward revision in full-year growth guidance.
