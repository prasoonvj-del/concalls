# Pine Labs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Good execution across all business lines, market share gains in offline, issuing, and online segments, and 24% YoY revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth YoY: 24%
- Adjusted EBITDA growth YoY: 27%
- Flow-based income growth YoY: 25% (volumes up ~50%)
- Mid-market terminals deployed (trailing 12 months): ~100,000
- NCMC Bharat Yatra prepaid cards distributed: 25,000 to 30,000 per month (~1,000 daily), largely via Blinkit and Zepto
- UAE daily transactions processed: 1 lakh
- Malaysia POS deployed: ~1,00,000
- Philippines POS deployed: 50,000
- Total employee count: ~5,200 (net addition of ~675-680 people, mostly in sales and field ops)

### Highlights
- Enterprise accounts holding at 100% retention; added new brands like Tanishq across all stores.
- Mid-market segment (apparel, coffee shops, restaurants, duty-frees, airports) gaining market share due to integrated ERP/billing solutions and value-added services.
- Flow-based income expanding with new revenue streams from data services, marketing-related products, and distribution.
- Issuing business growing via prepaid/gift cards (including gaming brands) and mass transit programs (NCMC Bharat Yatra).
- Online payment gateway powering top 4 Q-commerce companies and enabled for Flipkart's Big Billion Days. Signed up one of the largest consumer billers, going live in Q3.
- International expansion scaling significantly in UAE (Emirates NBD, Wio Bank partnership), Malaysia, and Philippines.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue growth to come in higher than the 23%-23.5% talked about at the start of the year (Amrish Rau).
- Full-year EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 21% (Amrish Rau).
- H2 EBITDA expected to be at a much higher position than H1 (Amrish Rau).
- Employee tax benefit card launch scheduled for late November / early December.
- Scaling gaming distribution for gift cards and expanding affordability beyond electronics expected in H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Navratri and Dussehra periods were extremely strong; Diwali performance will be clear in two weeks.
- UPI transactions account for 75% to 77% of total transactions on Pine Labs POS machines (only 25% card-based), compounding at ~68% for the last two years.
- Organized retailers are moving to device-based UPI reconciliation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cloud and data costs remain variable at 8.5% to 9% of top-line, optimized for AI and tokenization.
- Contribution (CM) margins guided in the range of 52% to 56% (Sameer Kamath).
- Working capital guided to land between 13% to 16% of top-line for the full year (Sameer Kamath).
- Capex elevated due to delayed procurements/chip shortages from Q4 slipping into H1 and 80% advance payments required by vendors to lock in prices (Sameer Kamath).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned deployment of 10 lakh (1 million) soundboxes in the market to capture missing UPI transactions at merchant locations.
- International sales force being expanded to support UAE, Malaysia, Philippines, and other global markets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital and cash flows cyclical inter-quarter, but expected to land within the 13%-16% full-year guidance range (Sameer Kamath).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sales team expansions take up to 9 months to become productive and remunerative, impacting near-term cost absorption.
- Global e-commerce retailer exit from Singapore impacted card distribution revenue in Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vijit Jain (Citi) on UPI growth/GTV:** Asked about 2028 projections and soundbox aspirations. Management noted organized retail moving to device-based UPI and plans to deploy 10 lakh soundboxes to capture 70% growth.
- **Pranav Kshatriya (Axis Capital) on Capex & Indirect Costs:** Asked why H1 capex rose to ~Rs 240 crore. Management explained it was an overflow of ~Rs 35-40 crore of chip-related procurement slips from Q4 plus 80% vendor advances.
- **Nitin Aggarwal (Motilal Oswal) on International Revenue Dip:** Asked about flat Q-o-Q international revenue. Management cited the exit of a large global retailer from Singapore affecting gift card distribution, though EBITDA impact was immaterial.
- **Jayant Kharote (Kotak) on EBITDA Margins and Sales Investment:** Questioned whether incremental growth is coming at dilutive margins and if EBITDA guidance would be cut. Management strongly denied lowering guidance, reiterating a 21% full-year expectation and explaining that sales investments take 9 months to pay off.
- **Prakhar Sharma (Jefferies) on Soundbox Commercials:** Asked if merchants would pay rent plus MDR. Management stated merchants cannot afford both, so Pine Labs will prioritize transaction monetization (MDR) over base rentals.

### One-line takeaway
Pine Labs delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with 24% revenue growth and 27% adjusted EBITDA growth, driven by market share gains across offline, online, and issuing segments, and plans aggressive investments in sales and 1 million soundboxes to capture the UPI monetization wave.
