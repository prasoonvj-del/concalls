# CSM Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-31 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q4 & FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy financial performance with significant margin expansion, robust profit growth, and a strong order book driven by GovTech demand.

### Key numbers
- **FY26 Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 226 crore (up 12% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA:** Rs. 48 crore (up 57% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA Margin:** 21% (compared to 15% in FY25)
- **FY26 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 24 crore (up 70% YoY)
- **FY26 PAT Margin:** 10.5% (compared to 7% in FY25)
- **Q4 FY26 Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 60 crore
- **Q4 FY26 EBITDA:** Rs. 16 crore (up 3% YoY)
- **Q4 FY26 EBITDA Margin:** 26.6% (compared to 17.5% in Q4 FY25)
- **Q4 FY26 PAT:** Rs. 9 crore (up 7% YoY)
- **Q4 FY26 PAT Margin:** 15.1% (compared to 8.7% in Q4 FY25)
- **Order Book (as on 31st March 2026):** Rs. 357.63 crore
- **Export/International Revenue Share (FY26):** 9% export revenue (5% international revenue stated separately by management)

### Highlights
- First earnings call post-listing on stock exchanges.
- Recognized a one-time exceptional charge of Rs. 2.7 crore towards statutory impact of new Labor Codes.
- Board recommended a final dividend of Rs. 0.5 per equity share for FY26.
- Capital deployment included Rs. 10 crore in assets under development (AI wrapper/layer), Rs. 3.17 crore in property, plant, and equipment, and Rs. 1.17 crore in working capital WIP.
- Added two new geographies in FY26: Malawi and Cabo Verde.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expressed strong confidence in long-term opportunities, expecting order bookings to be better than last year due to buoyancy in government spending on digital public infrastructure.
- Anticipates a high volume of deal executions by the end of Quarter 2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operates in the niche "GovTech" domain across 14 countries, 20 states/union territories in India, employing ~1,300 people with over 180 active projects.
- 90% to 95% of revenue historically comes from repeat customers with long-standing relationships (15–20 years).
- Mining is one of the largest revenue contributors (e.g., i3MS in Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Rajasthan, Kenya).
- Other key focus areas include trade facilitation (single window portals), agriculture, education, healthcare, and digital public infrastructure.
- International growth heavily anchored in Africa (Nairobi delivery center, East Africa, Malawi, Kenya, Uganda, Ethiopia) alongside multilateral agencies like World Bank, ADB, and UN.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA and PAT margins expanded due to improved operating leverage, disciplined cost management, and modernization of old GovTech systems.
- International project margins are higher than domestic ones, though associated expenses are also higher.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continued expansion in domestic and international markets, particularly Africa (added Malawi and Cabo Verde).
- Heavy investments made in proprietary products, AI/ML wrappers, cloud infrastructure, and R&D tools.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Days sales outstanding (DSO) increased from 58 days to 129 days; management noted this is due to normal government approval lags, but liquidity and WIP are aligned to manage the delay.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slow government approval processes and billing lags leading to extended working capital cycles.
- Inherent risks in forward-looking statements including execution risks, wage inflation, talent retention, and political instability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shivam Gupta (Trinetra Asset Managers) on revenue concentration and industry contribution:** Management stated mining is the largest contributor and sees massive global opportunities in mineral extraction and compliance modernization.
2. **Vishal on revenue contribution from Odisha and high DSO (129 days):** Management noted geographic expansion via new wins (Chhattisgarh, NAFED, Rajasthan, Adani in Assam) and acknowledged government billing lags while affirming that liquidity is managed accordingly.
3. **Devyanshu Varma (Beacon Capital) on Q3 to Q4 revenue drop (Rs. 64 cr to Rs. 60 cr):** Management explained that government business is traditionally skewed towards the second half of the year, with Q4 historically being the strongest, and Q2 of the prior year had an exceptional order booking.
4. **Khushi Hawaldar (Individual Investor) on AI differentiation and margins:** Management explained that AI serves as an internal efficiency driver and an external solution for deterministic government use cases (like land approvals and exams) built using proprietary LLMs trained on historical data.

### One-line takeaway
CSM Technologies delivered robust profit and margin expansion in FY26 driven by its niche GovTech focus, and management remains highly optimistic about future growth backed by a healthy Rs. 357.63 crore order book.
