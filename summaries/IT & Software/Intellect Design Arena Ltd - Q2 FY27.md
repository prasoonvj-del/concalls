# Intellect Design Arena Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong trailing-twelve-month income growth of 17%, successful early deliveries, and strategic advancements in AI-led transformations with the introduction of MSOCK.

### Key numbers
- **Trailing-twelve-month (LTM) total income:** Rs 3,388 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **LTM License-linked income:** Rs 1,768 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **LTM Platform income:** Rs 1,497 crore (up 51% YoY)
- **LTM Gross Margin:** 56.7% of total income
- **LTM EBITDA Margin:** 21.7%
- **Pipeline:** Rs 13,125 crore (up 9% YoY)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs 1,216 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up 31% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Margin:** 57.2%
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 22.7%
- **R&D Expense (LTM):** Rs 42-43 crore expensed per quarter in P&L, with a portion capitalised.
- **Expected Q3 SaaS Churn impact:** USD 300,000 to 400,000.

### Highlights
- Introduced **MSOCK** (Multidimensional Multilayer System Connected Knowledge), which makes enterprise knowledge computable and closes the gap for mission-critical systems in AI implementation.
- Trailing-twelve-month income grew 17% YoY, marking growth across seven consecutive quarters.
- Secured 21 strategic wins during the quarter, with 50% of deal signings coming from the Americas and Europe.
- Multiple major programs went live ahead of plan: a wealth management program in Asia (15-month program), a Middle East powerhouse (11-month program), and a lending transformation in Europe advanced by three months.
- Appointed Suresh Eshwara Prabhala as an additional director designated as an independent director, subject to shareholder approval.
- Vijay Anand joined as Senior Executive Vice President and Head of Operating Wholesale Banking.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reaffirmed its confidence in achieving the Rs 4,000 crore revenue milestone by FY28.
- Management considers FY27 an inflection point driven by proprietary AI innovations (MSOCK) and expanding market access.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is increasingly driven by AI-led transformations, modernization, and mainframe-to-cloud migrations.
- Competitors in the AI/knowledge-parsing space include C3.AI, Core AI, Palantir (in defense), and CAST.
- Mainframe-to-cloud migration deals are expected to range in double-digit millions (up to the upper side of double-digit millions).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage is expected to play out as revenue scales on a relatively flat cost base.
- Gross margin stands at 56.7% on an LTM basis and 57.2% for Q2 FY27.
- EBITDA margin stood at 21.7% on an LTM basis (22.7% for Q2 FY27) despite sustained investments in AI.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- R&D investments continue, with Rs 42-43 crore expensed quarterly in the P&L.
- Evaluating potential acquisitions of a certain size in Latin American, European, and Eastern European markets to gain client access and shorten sales cycles.
- Exploring strategic alternatives for MSOCK, including a separate subsidiary or independent go-to-market strategies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 1,216 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) remains a continuous focus for capital efficiency and receivable monitoring.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Natural expiration and progression of a legacy SaaS contract (Central 1 acquisition legacy) resulting in anticipated churn of USD 300,000 to 400,000 in Q3.
- Decision-making cycles in traditional banking modernization can occasionally be slow.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Adi Jhawar (AK Investment) asked about revenue recognition for customisation and licence sales.** Arun Jain and Vasudha Subramaniam explained that customisation sits under implementation revenue (classified under A2.1/A2.2) and is recognised typically on a percentage-completion basis before transitioning to stable AMC (A3).
- **Dev Mehta (Unique PMS) asked about R&D capitalisation and if FY27 is an inflection point.** Arun Jain confirmed FY27 is an inflection point due to the creation of MSOCK, improved delivery speeds, and operational leverage.
- **Vivek Taruga asked about the SaaS customer loss mentioned by management.** Arun Jain clarified it was a natural expiration of an acquired contract's term where roughly 80% was retained as forecasted.
- **Prolin Nandu (Edelweiss Public Alternatives) questioned why profit growth has remained relatively flat despite revenue scaling.** Arun Jain responded that profits have been intentionally reinvested into building world-class proprietary AI capabilities (MSOCK and eMACH.ai) without diluting equity, which will yield operating leverage moving forward.
- **Paresh Sanghvi (Club Millionaire) asked if the company would consider share buybacks given strong cash generation.** Arun Jain stated that the option has been discussed at the Board level, but capital is currently being prioritised for potential strategic acquisitions and business investments.
- **Ravi Mehta (OneUp) asked about internal adoption and go-to-market strategy for MSOCK.** Manish Maakan stated that 141 internal implementations are utilizing MSOCK, and Arun Jain added that MSOCK will be taken to market via top-down and bottom-up strategies, potentially as a separate subsidiary or product line.

### One-line takeaway
Intellect Design Arena reported a 17% YoY increase in LTM income to Rs 3,388 crore and introduced its computable enterprise knowledge platform, MSOCK, positioning FY28 for the Rs 4,000 crore revenue milestone.
