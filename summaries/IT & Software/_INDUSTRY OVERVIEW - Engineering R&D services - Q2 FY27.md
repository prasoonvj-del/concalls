# IT & Software - Engineering R&D services: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Management commentary across Engineering R&D (ER&D) services in Q2 FY27 reflects a bifurcated demand environment: solid traction in North America, India, and emerging verticals (aerospace, off-highway vehicles, AI infrastructure) offset by structural softness, restructuring, and platform delays among European automotive OEMs. Companies with high exposure to digital engineering, products, and AI-native service delivery (such as L&T Technology Services, Mold-Tek, Onward Technologies, and Tata Technologies) delivered strong sequential revenue and profit growth, while those impacted by specific client program cancellations or facility transitions (such as KPIT Technologies and Axiscades) experienced temporary top-line pressure or margin headwinds.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Traditionally, Indian ER&D service providers operated as offshore staff-augmentation and project-delivery subcontractors, billing on time-and-material rates for mechanical, electrical, and embedded software tasks. Over the past five years, the model shifted toward fixed-price outcome-based contracts, turnkey full-vehicle/product development, proprietary software platforms, and high-value domain-led solutions, reducing dependency on linear headcount scaling. [Background]

**Shifts underway** - 
*   **AI-Native Service Delivery and Infrastructure Engineering:** Scaling from experimental GenAI proofs-of-concept into mainstream enterprise adoption, with firms deploying proprietary internal frameworks (e.g., KPIT's "Beacon" logging over 5 million transactions, LTTS's $120 million annualized AI-led revenue) and setting up dedicated Data Center Engineering business lines for AI hardware and servers [KPIT Technologies concall Q2 FY27; L&T Technology Services concall Q2 FY27]. This shift matters because it drives non-linear revenue growth and margin expansion through automated productivity.
*   **Diversification into Software-Defined Machines and Aerospace/Energy:** Moving beyond passenger cars into off-highway vehicles, commercial trucks, aerospace (eVTOLs), and energytech (power T&D, data center cooling) [KPIT Technologies concall Q2 FY27; Mold-Tek Technologies concall Q2 FY27; Tata Elxsi concall Q2 FY27; Onward Technologies concall Q2 FY27]. This is in the scaling stage, evidenced by KPIT's 27% YoY growth in Off-Highway & CVs, Mold-Tek scaling power distribution services to 120 people, and Tata Elxsi partnering with Sarla Aviation for India's first eVTOL. This cushions ER&D providers against cyclical slumps in European automotive.

**Government push** - 
*   Commerce Ministry authorization of 17 government agencies to issue Certificates of Origin for exports to New Zealand (rewarding engineering exports and market diversification) [Govt: Commerce Ministry, Nov 2026].
*   Standard long-running export promotion and software technology park schemes [Background].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that North American and Indian markets remain resilient and robust, driven by digital engineering, infrastructure upgrades, and strong domestic manufacturing demand. Conversely, European passenger automotive demand remains soft, muted, and subject to customer restructuring and platform delays (noted by KPIT Technologies, LTTS, Tata Elxsi, and Tata Technologies).
*   **Divergence:** While companies heavily levered to digital engineering and cross-industry diversification (such as Onward Technologies upgrading guidance to 15–18%, LTTS achieving mid-16% EBIT margins early, and Mold-Tek doubling standalone H1 PAT) reported stellar growth, automotive-heavy players faced headwinds. KPIT Technologies absorbed a 13% revenue hit from two major client program cancellations in Japan and Europe, whereas Tata Technologies secured a large US$138 million full-vehicle engineering win despite regional European softness.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Wage Hikes and Headwinds:** Annual salary increments exerted downward pressure on margins across companies (e.g., a 100 bps headwind at Tata Technologies, wage hikes implemented in Q2 at Tata Elxsi, and a projected 50–80 bps impact from Q3 wage hikes at LTTS). 
*   **Margin Expansion Levers:** Companies offset wage inflation through higher offshore mix (LTTS at 55.9%), rising fixed-price contract shares (KPIT at 70%, LTTS at 35.6%), improved employee utilization (Tata Elxsi up to 78%, Mold-Tek driving a 15–16% YoY increase in per capita revenue via automation), and operational leverage.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Companies are aggressively expanding physical footprints and delivery centers. Onward Technologies opened its 4th design center in Pune; KPIT established a joint delivery center in Vietnam; Mold-Tek added a Hyderabad design team for Beryl and expanded its US structural engineering footprint; and LTTS set up an EI lab with NVIDIA for physical AI.
*   **M&A and Divestments:** Strategic portfolio reshaping was prominent. LTTS completed the Smart World & Communication (SWC) divestment with a Rs 165 crore one-time gain; Axiscades closed Phase 1 of the Akkodis divestment while experiencing a 3-month delay on Phase 2 alongside goodwill write-offs; and Mold-Tek integrated Beryl Project Engineering to scale US residential and permit engineering.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance & Tone Shifts:** Onward Technologies upgraded its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 15–18%. Axiscades backed away from explicit annual PAT margin guidance due to facility transitions and acquisition debt, while reaffirming H2 execution targets. KPIT expects sequential revenue growth to return in Q4 as client cancellations bottom out over the next two quarters.
*   **Delivery on Promises:** LTTS completed its SWC business divestment on schedule. Axiscades successfully closed Phase 1 of the Akkodis divestment and acquired Cloud Wave, though Phase 2 of Akkodis was delayed to February 2027.

*   **KPIT Technologies Ltd:** KPIT relies on off-highway growth, products, and AI solutions to stabilize revenue while managing structural spend cuts and platform delays across European passenger car accounts.
*   **L&T Technology Services Ltd:** LTTS delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with accelerated sequential revenue growth, early achievement of mid-16% EBIT margins, and robust traction in AI-led and Sustainability deal conversions.
*   **Mold-Tek Technologies Ltd:** Mold-Tek Technologies delivered a stellar Q2/H1 FY27, more than doubling standalone profits driven by operational efficiencies, the downsizing of loss-making segments, and scaling high-value US engineering verticals.
*   **Onward Technologies Ltd:** Onward Technologies delivered a strong quarter with record revenue and margin expansion, supported by a strategic pivot toward larger, long-term digital engineering engagements in the US and Europe.
*   **Tata Elxsi Ltd:** Tata Elxsi delivered resilient broad-based revenue growth and margin expansion in Q2 FY27, backed by US/India auto strength and Healthcare recovery, despite ongoing soft demand in European automotive.
*   **Tata Technologies Ltd:** Tata Technologies is successfully pivoting toward large, outcome-based "full vehicle" engineering programs, offsetting temporary German market softness with strong deal wins and a strategic focus on aerospace and AI-native delivery.
