# Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-10 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q4 & FY26 (Ended 31st March 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management expects strong revenue growth and margin stability backed by expanding order books and productized AI solutions.

### Key numbers
- Revenue guidance: Rs. 3,000 crores for FY27 (management)
- Long-term confirmed order book: Rs. 1,100 crores+ for the next financial year (management)
- Order book mix: Cybersecurity ~46-47%, Enterprise applications ~24-26%, Healthcare ~14%+, IT consulting remainder
- Customer segment mix: Private/Enterprise ~80%, Government/CGIS ~20% (management)
- Revenue mix: Recurring revenue ~70-75%, Project-based revenue ~26-30% (management)
- EBITDA margin: Improved to ~17-20% in Q4 (management)
- Capex budget: Rs. 150 crores - Rs. 200 crores+ initial budget for FY27 (management)
- Attrition rate: Expected below 2% to 3% (management)
- R&D cost: ~10% to 12% over the last two financial years (management)

### Highlights
- Company operates across five business units: Enterprise Smart Applications, Cybersecurity, Healthcare, Telecom infrastructure (5G/CNPN/PMN), and IT consulting.
- Flagship products include AccessGenie (AI surveillance/facial recognition), Blura SAGA (social media monitoring), BluTOR and BluHawk (cybersecurity), BluHealth (vital parameter generation via face recognition), and BioSter (space sterilization).
- Received in-principle sanctions from BSE for the acquisition of Geo Impex; data center land acquisition is the first step, with traction expected in FY27.
- Transitioning AIS Anywhere from customized deliverables to a SaaS model with source code ownership by Blue Cloud.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 revenue guidance of Rs. 3,000 crores.
- Management projects ~30% YoY growth year-on-year from FY28 onwards (management).
- First data center line expected to be ready by Q1 2027 (management).
- EBITDA margins expected to remain in the current improved range or trend better (management).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Government is driving AI adoption in India, but strong traction is also building in the private sector and public sector units (PSUs).
- Emerging markets like Ghana, Liberia, Senegal, and Mauritius showing significant interest; West African region (ECOWAS) has potential business under advanced discussion.
- Sovereign data centers and 5G telecom see high demand due to data localization requirements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross and operating margins expanded in Q4 due to completion of infrastructure setup, SaaS revenue realization, and productization of R&D investments.
- R&D spending to moderate as the company shifts focus to a Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) model.
- Cloud infrastructure SaaS costs currently take 15% to 35% of expenses, which will reduce once company-owned data centers are operational.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Initial FY27 capex planned at Rs. 150 crores - Rs. 200 crores+, with upside potential depending on telecom rollouts (CNPN/PMN) and edge data center closures.
- First phase of data centers targeted for completion by Q1 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Account receivables saw a temporary spike due to geopolitical situations affecting international client payments; company is shifting to a modular pro-rata billing model to improve collections.
- Current ratio is around 1, expected to improve next quarter following payout model changes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations caused slight delays in international client payments, impacting account receivables.
- Telecom projects are capital-intensive, which could influence depreciation depending on financing models chosen.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raman KV (Sequent Investments) asked about closing order book and segment mix:** Management stated Cybersecurity forms 46-47%, Enterprise applications 24-26%, and Healthcare ~14%+, with a confirmed order book of Rs. 1,100 crores+ for next year.
- **Gaurav Shukla (Finvestors) asked about the Rs. 3,000 crore revenue guidance versus Rs. 1,100 crore confirmed order book:** Management clarified that Rs. 1,100 crores is from existing recurring contracts, while the remainder will come from pipeline negotiations, MOUs, and inorganic additions like Geo Impex.
- **Harsh Shah asked about revenue mix between recurring and project-based:** Management stated ~70-75% is recurring (long-term contracts) and 26-30% is project-based.
- **Diya (Sapphire Capital Partners) asked about Q1 FY27 revenue expectations and capex:** Management guided for a stable run rate of around Rs. 200 crores (plus/minus) for Q1 based on existing order books, scaling up toward the annual target, with initial capex budgeted at Rs. 150-200 crores+.
- **Charchit asked about account receivables spike and AIS Anywhere revenue:** Management attributed receivable delays to geopolitical factors and noted they are moving clients to a modular billing model; AIS Anywhere is transitioning to a SaaS model to scale volume.

### One-line takeaway
Blue Cloud Softech enters FY27 with a confirmed order book of Rs. 1,100 crores+, maintaining its Rs. 3,000 crore revenue guidance on the back of its AI-first platform products, cybersecurity expansion, and planned data center rollouts.
