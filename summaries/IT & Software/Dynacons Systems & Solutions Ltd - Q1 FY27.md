# Dynacons Systems & Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong order inflows and healthy demand environment, despite temporary revenue impact from OEM lead times.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR313 crores (vs INR328 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** INR40 crores
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR19.00 crores (approximately INR20 crores / EPS INR15.54)
- **Order Book:** INR3,104 crores (as on date)
- **Bidding Pipeline:** INR6,650 crores
- **Key Order Wins:** INR750 crores from RBI (private cloud), INR267 crores from NPCI (data center augmentation), INR125 crores from Central Bank of India (AI-ready infrastructure), INR25 crores from J&K Bank (ERP implementation)
- **Depreciation:** INR8 crores (for the quarter)
- **Fixed Assets / ROU Assets:** INR158 crores (added last year for As-a-Service business)

### Highlights
- Secured marquee enterprise wins from RBI, NPCI, Central Bank of India, and J&K Bank, enhancing visibility across project-based and recurring revenue opportunities.
- Operating margins (EBITDA margin) expanded, driven by a better business mix, disciplined execution, and higher contribution from value-added infrastructure and managed services.
- Strategic priorities remain focused on data centers, cloud, AI capabilities, cyber security, and annuity-based services.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide specific revenue or margin guidance.
- Management expects deliveries to normalize progressively and is confident of converting the strong order book into revenue over coming quarters, maintaining long-term growth momentum.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying demand environment remains healthy, driven by rapid adoption of AI, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and data center modernization.
- AI infrastructure demand (high-performance servers, GPUs, memory) has increased significantly across the industry, leading to longer OEM lead times.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin improvement driven by favorable business mix, disciplined execution, and higher managed services contribution.
- Price increases from OEMs are backed by back-to-back support and loaded into finalized customer orders, insulating the company from price escalations post-order finalization.
- As-a-Service business growth has increased lease costs, depreciation, and capital employed (ROU assets), though management notes these are backed by contracted revenues.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital additions are primarily directed toward the As-a-Service business (Device-as-a-Service and Core Banking-as-a-Service).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- As-a-Service model has increased capital employed and fixed/ROU asset base, which may moderate historical ROCE levels over time.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Extended OEM-led times and supply chain delays deferred execution and revenue recognition in Q1 FY27.
- Component price volatility and shooting budgets causing customers to prioritize mission-critical applications over non-critical ones.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhi Jain (AJ Capital) on Q1 revenue dip and margins:** Management clarified that the revenue dip was purely a timing issue due to extended OEM lead times, and reiterated confidence in long-term growth and sustained higher operating margins.
- **Madhur Rathi (Investments) on EBITDA distortion from lease rentals/depreciation:** Management explained that lease costs and depreciation relate to the scaling As-a-Service (DaaS and Core Banking) business and are tied to corresponding revenue contracts.
- **Madhur Rathi on raw material price pass-throughs:** Management stated that back-to-back OEM support and pre-finalized project tenders protect the company from unexpected cost escalations.
- **Bhagya on comparison with peers like Netweb:** Management distinguished Dynacons as a pure-play System Integrator partnering with global OEMs (Dell, HPE, Cisco, NVIDIA) rather than a hardware manufacturer/OEM.
- **Gunit Singh on ROCE impact from rising depreciation and ROU assets:** Management stated that investments are backed by contracted revenue visibility and evaluated for commercial viability.

### One-line takeaway
Dynacons reported a temporary revenue dip in Q1 FY27 due to OEM lead times, but posted strong EBITDA and record order inflows of over INR3,100 crores led by large BFSI private cloud and AI infrastructure wins.
