# MSTC Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever Q1 e-commerce revenue, PBT, and PAT since listing, driven by growth in mineral and scrap sales and exit from the legacy trading segment.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 94.25 crores (up nearly 22% YoY)
- E-commerce revenue: Highest-ever Q1 at Rs. 89.49 crores (vs. Rs. 70.03 crores YoY)
- Other operating revenue: Rs. 28.51 crores (vs. Rs. 23.27 crores YoY)
- Total expenses: Rs. 36.51 crores (vs. Rs. 31.78 crores YoY)
- EBITDA percentage of total income: 69.05% (nearly 3% higher YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 81.49 crores (up ~32% YoY from Rs. 61.88 crores)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): Rs. 78.53 crores (up 31.70% YoY from Rs. 59.63 crores)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs. 58.12 crores (up 31.14% YoY from Rs. 44.32 crores)
- Consolidated PBT (including JV share): Rs. 78.63 crores
- Consolidated PAT: Rs. 58.22 crores (up 37.51% YoY from Rs. 6.01 crores EPS base/PAT basis stated)
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Rs. 8.26 (vs. Rs. 6.30 YoY); Consolidated EPS: Rs. 8.27 (vs. Rs. 6.01 YoY)
- Gross Trading Volume (GTV): Rs. 23,900 crores for the quarter

### Highlights
- Completely exited the legacy trading and marketing segment (110% BG back model) in FY26 and recovered all dues, closing the chapter in Q1.
- Electronic trading platform for EPR certificates (CPCB) is fully developed with integrations and security testing complete, awaiting government notification.
- Travel portal (`mstcsmarttravel.in`) operationalized for the B2B segment (internal use and sister PSUs); B2C rollout planned post-IATA empanelment.
- TReDS platform development at an advanced stage; awaiting RBI approval.
- Mahindra JV (MMRPL) posted a positive PAT for the first time in sequential quarters, driven by higher feedstock inflows.
- Secured new contracts: minor/major/critical mineral block auctions, Red sandalwood sale (100 tons via international competitive bidding for Tamil Nadu Forest Department), Coal India NRS linkage auctions, Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited process scrap auctions, Karnataka liquor license auctions, Telangana land parcel auctions, and charge point operator selection for KSEB.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to maintain average double-digit growth over the long run.
- Management expects to secure RBI approval and operationalize the TReDS platform within FY27.
- Outlook for the Mahindra JV is positive.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Scrap sales historically contribute 50% to 55% of e-commerce revenue; steel sector is cyclical, but average contribution remains steady.
- Mineral and critical mineral block auction demand is strong and sustainable.
- Government push on Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) norms and scrappage policies are driving End-of-Life Vehicles (ELVs) away from the grey market and into Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities (RVSFs).
- Multiple coal and mineral exchanges are planned nationally; management is evaluating feasibility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Total expenses increased by 16% (from Rs. 31.78 crores to Rs. 36.51 crores) due to normal salary/wage increases, inflationary pressure, and expanded pan-India operations including a new corporate office in New Delhi.
- Employee benefit expense: Rs. 27.50 crores (vs. Rs. 23.25 crores YoY).
- Depreciation: Rs. 2.96 crores (vs. Rs. 2.25 crores YoY).
- Other expenses: Rs. 9.01 crores (vs. Rs. 8.53 crores YoY).
- Tax expense: Rs. 20.41 crores (vs. Rs. 15.31 crores YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company remains committed to an asset-light model.
- Expanding private sector client footprint (Tata, Reliance, Vedanta, ArcelorMittal) for scrap and commodities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Legacy trading segment closed down with revenue dropping to zero; segmental reporting for trading discontinued.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Scrap and steel sectors are highly cyclical, leading to fluctuations in volumes and realizations.
- High growth rates achieved in Q1 may not be sustainable linearly every quarter.
- Regulatory approvals (RBI for TReDS, government notification for EPR) are outside management control and create timeline uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shilpa (Lotus Wealth) asked about the revenue share in EPR trading relative to CPCB fees.** Management responded that the EOI outlines details, but it is premature to speculate on numbers before operationalization.
- **Saurabh Ginodia (Equirus Securities) asked about TReDS revenue models and RBI approval timelines.** Management stated revenue will primarily come from transaction fees, and while internal target for operationalization is FY27, exact timelines depend on RBI approval.
- **Surabhi (NV Alpha) asked if CPCB committed a minimum volume for the EPR portal.** Management clarified there is no minimum volume guarantee, as pricing and volume will be market-driven.
- **Kumar Saurabh asked about margin sustainability above 60% and client stickiness.** Management cautioned that high growth and margins fluctuate cyclically, while stickiness is ensured via process transparency, integrity, and system robustness.
- **Vinayak Mohta asked about the IATA empanelment and B2C travel rollout.** Management explained that B2B for government/PSUs is operational via EaseMyTrip partnership, while B2C requires independent IATA empanelment.

### One-line takeaway
MSTC delivered its highest-ever Q1 e-commerce revenue, PBT, and PAT since listing, successfully exiting legacy trading to focus on digital platforms, scrap auctions, and new verticals like EPR and TReDS.
