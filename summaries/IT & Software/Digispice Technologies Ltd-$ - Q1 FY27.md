# Digispice Technologies Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong profitability growth, the credit business reaching break-even, and expansion in high-margin financial product distribution.

### Key numbers
- **PAT:** INR 9 crores (continuing business) / INR 6.6 crores (overall)
- **Registered Agents (Adhikaris):** 16.85 lakhs across ~2.6 lakh towns
- **Customers Served:** 27 million monthly (170 million annually)
- **AePS GTV:** INR 13,300 crores (17.93% market share in Q1, recovering to 18.3% in July)
- **BBPS GTV:** INR 1,500 crores (14.2% YoY growth)
- **Savings Accounts Opened:** 17.8 lakhs (Float balances at ~INR 320 crores, up 45% YoY)
- **Credit Disbursements:** Grahak loan disbursements at INR 167.7 crores (1.7x YoY); Credit business disbursements at INR 30.8 crores (55% QoQ, 2.8x YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 8.6 crores (approx. 6.5x QoQ)
- **Gross Margin:** INR 48.1 crores for Q1

### Highlights
- Spice Money is now the only continuing business under Digispice Technologies.
- The NCLT merger second motion was reached in July 2026; management expects completion by March 2027 to form a pure-play listed fintech.
- The new credit and UPI business engines achieved break-even at the gross margin level.
- Launched FD-backed secured credit cards and two-wheeler insurance products.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the Spice Money NCLT merger to close by March 2027.
- Management targets an exit of close to INR 500 crores in UPI cash points for Q2.
- Management expects 50% or more of margins to come from financial product distribution and credit segments over a five-year horizon.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Cash-first consumers in Tier-3, Tier-4, and Tier-5 India represent a massive underpenetrated market for formal financial services.
- CICO (Cash-in Cash-out) saw a seasonal dip due to seasonality and industry-wide regulatory changes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating efficiencies in indirect costs drove EBITDA to INR 8.6 crores and EBIT growth.
- Gross margin mix is shifting away from low-margin collections toward high-margin financial product distribution (CASA, credit, insurance).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- The company operates an asset-light, zero-debt business model relying on internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Zero-debt company with a growing float balance of approximately INR 320 crores from savings accounts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Third-party bank controls and off-Us rates for cash withdrawals remain dependent on external banking rules.
- High commoditization and price sensitivity in the traditional corporate CMS collections market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raj Vyas asked about three-year success metrics:** Management responded that transaction volume is the bedrock for data collection, driving operating leverage and profitability.
- **Prisha Shah asked about insurance sales moderation:** Rohit Sood explained that shopkeeper insurance was sold to a captive Adhikari base which saturated quickly; the company is expanding its portfolio and insurer-tech partnerships.
- **Prisha Shah asked about credit stress:** Akash Bafna stated that no stress has been seen in agent lending or open-market lending due to tight risk controls and cash-flow visibility.
- **Bhavya Agarwal asked about AePS market share dip in Q1:** Rohit Sood noted that direct benefit transfer (DBT) government disbursements in southern territories temporarily skewed market share percentages.

### One-line takeaway
Digispice is successfully transitioning into a pure-play listed fintech via the Spice Money merger, driving profitability through operating leverage and high-margin financial product cross-selling in rural Bharat.
