# Excelsoft Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue growth remains strong at 25.2% YoY, supported by robust demand and healthy order inflows, despite near-term margin pressure from strategic upfront investments in international expansion and talent.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** INR 80.9 crores (up 25.2% YoY)
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** INR 161.2 crores (up 33.9% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 13.2 crores
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 16.35% (compared to 29.3% in Q2 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27):** INR 8.3 crores (compared to INR 10.6 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Profit After Tax (H1 FY27):** INR 17.5 crores (up 6.2% YoY from INR 16.5 crores)
- **Order Pipeline:** Increased from INR 900 crores at the start of the year to INR 1,200 crores
- **Strategic Investments:** INR 20.3 crores in FY27 for sales expansion, leadership, talent, and product development
- **Cash and Bank Balances:** Approximately INR 407 crores (debt-free)
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** INR 34.7 crores
- **Top-line Guidance:** On track for INR 350 crores top-line by the end of the financial year

### Highlights
- Education technology services contributed 60.75% of Q2 revenue (growing 41.9% YoY); product business contributed the remaining 39.25%.
- Established local sales, account management, and customer engagement teams in the US, UK, Middle East, and Singapore, and is setting up a Canadian subsidiary.
- VTCT skills engagement went live in September, conducting roughly 25,000 tests in October against an expectation of 30,000 tests per month.
- Won a proctoring contract with the University of Memphis, marking an entry into the US market.
- Shipped "Assessment in a Box" palm-held server demo units for customer pilots/POCs (deployed in Africa, UK, and Scotland).
- *Record check:* Margins moderated due to strategic investments in AI, talent, and global expansion, continuing the trend noted in the November 2026 investor deck.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of achieving the targeted top-line of INR 350 crores by the end of FY27.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to reach between 22% to 25% once investments start yielding results over the next couple of quarters and expects this level to be sustainable.
- AI-specific revenues are expected to become an increased percentage of total revenues in the coming quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall demand for technology and services remains very encouraging with zero significant customer losses.
- Decision-making from large organizations (particularly government-owned trusts) has been slow over the last few months.
- Pipeline has improved by 20% compared to last year, reaching INR 1,200 crores.
- Strongest growth expected from the UK, Europe, US, and Southeast Asia; Middle East has been slower over the past year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Increase in employee costs reflects investments in international sales teams, leadership, specialized talent, recruitment, annual salary increments, and statutory labor law contributions.
- External expenses increased due to higher consultant costs supporting project delivery, US/UK expansion, and expected credit loss provisions.
- No structural change in the margin profile; pressure is solely due to upfront investments and revenue deferrals from customer-side delays (UAT, security clearances, implementation readiness).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Maintained a debt-free balance sheet with cash reserves of ~INR 407 crores; internal accruals will not be impacted for planned investments.
- Expanded physical and sales presence in the US, UK, Middle East, Singapore, and upcoming Canadian subsidiary.
- Invested in proprietary AI hardware infrastructure and LLMs/SLMs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-free company with cash and bank balances of approximately INR 407 crores.
- Half-yearly operating cash flow stood at INR 34.7 crores.
- Some revenue recognition was deferred to subsequent quarters due to customer-side dependencies (UAT, acceptance, security clearances).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slow decision-making and delayed user acceptance testing (UAT) / readiness from large government-owned trusts and clients.
- High customer concentration (top five customers comprise ~75% of revenue), though management notes these consist of multiple independent P&L divisions and a 5-year plan is in place to reduce concentration to 20-30%.
- Gestation period for enterprise sales in overseas markets can delay revenue conversion relative to upfront sales investments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mahesh Kumar (MU Investments) on EBITDA margin pressure and normalized margins:** Management explained that costs are driven by upfront strategic sales investments and training on a smaller base. Normalized EBITDA is expected to reach 22% to 25% once investments yield results.
2. **Mahesh Kumar on customer concentration risk:** Management acknowledged the top-5 concentration (~75%), clarifying that clients operate through multiple independent P&L divisions, and outlined a long-term goal to bring individual customer share below 20%.
3. **Abhishek Kamdar on FY27 revenue guidance:** Management confirmed they remain on track for a INR 350 crores top-line by the end of the financial year.
4. **Abhishek Kamdar on AQA ramp-up status:** Management reported significant progress on e-marking (98.6% accuracy on handwritten answers across thousands of scripts) with ongoing workshops and transition to the handwriting extraction phase.
5. **Divya Reddy (Sunshine Investments) on VTCT and Saras in a box progress:** Management stated VTCT is on track (~25,000 tests in October vs 30,000 monthly target) and "Saras in a box" units are currently deployed with customers in Africa, the UK, and Scotland for pilot/POC testing.

### One-line takeaway
Excelsoft delivered strong 25.2% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by its education technology services, while EBITDA margins moderated due to upfront strategic investments in global sales and AI capabilities.
