# Kellton Tech Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Macro and geopolitical uncertainties are causing delayed contract starts, though demand and order books remain stable.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 316 crore (~7% YoY growth)
- **EBITDA:** 11.1% (Rs 35 crore)
- **PAT:** 7.1% (Rs 22.3 crore)
- **EPS:** Up quarter-on-quarter

### Highlights
- Secured significant client wins including a Fortune India conglomerate (low-code/no-code workflow), a UAE group (cloud-native operating system), a Middle East energy infrastructure enterprise, an industrial services enterprise, and a pest management services leader.
- Completed deployment of Optima fields in India (Northeast terrain across 80-odd wells) in under 6 months.
- Launched Phoenix.ai (legacy modernization product accelerator) and Structi.ai (context engine for unstructured data).
- Strengthened partnership to select tier Snowflake partner.
- Received the Gold award for best HR tech implementation at the BW World Tech Future Awards 2026 for the Government of Karnataka HRMS2 program covering 2 lakh employees.
- Kumori acquisition added Rs 3 crores in revenue for the quarter, bringing ServiceNow capabilities and certifications.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide long-term or annual percentage growth guidance due to geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainties affecting contract starts.
- Management noted that order books provide visibility for the next 9 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global chaos, geopolitical tensions, and macroeconomic concerns are leading clients to worry about cash flows and delay the physical start of signed contracts.
- AI-led inquiries and smaller pure-AI projects are increasing, with AI components now embedded in every offering rather than just acting as add-ons.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Uncollectible receivables are occasionally written off (e.g., a handful of crores historically), but overall receivables as a percentage remain consistent with growth. 

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- DSO is extended due to dealing with Fortune 100 clients (90-day clock) and government contracts (such as LICs and HRMS).
- FCCB round has been delayed due to market conditions, but management expects it to happen in due course.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues and wars are causing delayed contract starts and slower execution.
- Clients are concerned about cash flows, impacting project kickoffs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sai Jitendra regarding Kumori acquisition and FCCB:** Management explained Kumori added Rs 3 crores in revenue and brought critical ServiceNow certifications. The FCCB round is delayed due to current market sentiment but will happen in time.
- **Krishnansh regarding account receivables:** Management clarified that working with Fortune 100 companies and government entities inherently involves extended collection cycles (e.g., 90 days), though write-offs remain minimal (a handful of crores).
- **Vishwanarayana regarding headwinds:** Management noted that while demand exists and contracts are signed, clients are delaying the actual start dates due to cash flow concerns amid global uncertainties.
- **Harsh regarding demand confidence:** Management stated that while starts are delayed, AI-led inquiries are robust and provide confidence once macro chaos settles.
- **Shruti regarding platform differentiators:** Management highlighted Phoenix.ai, Structi.ai, and internal KAI platforms delivering ~30% internal efficiencies and helping modernize legacy code 50% faster and cheaper than competitors.
- **Krishnansh regarding revenue volatility:** Management stated that 9-month order books are predictable, but percentage-based forward guidance cannot be given due to market volatility.

### One-line takeaway
Kellton Tech delivered steady ~7% YoY revenue growth in Q1 FY27 to reach Rs 316 crore, but management withheld forward guidance due to macro-driven delays in contract starts.
