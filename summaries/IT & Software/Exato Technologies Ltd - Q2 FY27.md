# Exato Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-26 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a remarkable quarter with strong YoY growth in revenue, profitability, and expansion in international markets and AI infrastructure.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall growth view (eying 80-90% growth for the full year on revenue and profitability). Last quarter guided for 60-70%+ revenue growth and 70-80%+ profitability.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more firmly toward scaling international operations (US, Middle East, Europe), launching AI infrastructure (GPU-as-a-service, private AI with HPE), and proprietary IP (ExaFone launched 7 months ahead of schedule).
- New topics or risks: Dropped the previous ERP acquisition target after due diligence failed; added new targets in CX and AI. Added Abhijit as Chief Technology and Growth Officer. Flagged longer deal closure times (6 to 8 months) for international contracts.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on launching proprietary IP (ExaFone launched Aug 31, 2026, ahead of March 2027 schedule); secured Claude Partner Network certification; expanded leadership team further.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 58.85 crore (up 40.23% YoY)
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 102 crore (61% of last year's revenue)
- **Q2 EBITDA Growth YoY:** 57% to 58%
- **Q2 EBITDA Margin Change:** Expanded by 210 bps YoY
- **Q2 PBT Growth YoY:** 67.27%
- **Q2 PAT Growth YoY:** 65.26%
- **H1 PAT Growth YoY:** ~80%
- **Order Book:** Rs 681 crore (Rs 280 crore executed, Rs 410 crore remaining to be executed over next 3 years)
- **Customer Retention Rate:** 97%
- **Active Customers:** 150+
- **ExaFone Pipeline:** 40 opportunities, 3 deals closed
- **International Revenue Mix:** 26.39% (vs 24% last year), Domestic: 73.61%
- **BPO/ITES Contribution to India Revenue:** 76%
- **Domestic Gross Margin:** 20% to 22%

### Highlights
- Launched proprietary cloud telephony platform, ExaFone, on August 31, 2026 (ahead of March 31, 2027 schedule); secured 3 enterprise clients and 40 opportunities in pipeline.
- Achieved Claude Partner Network certification with 15 certified professionals on Anthropic Claude.
- Upgraded to Mitel Platinum Partnership in Q1; participated as a Platinum Partner at NiCE World events in Mumbai and New Delhi with 450+ attendees.
- Appointed Abhijit as Chief Technology and Growth Officer (ex-Kore.ai, IDFC Bank, HDFC Bank, Kotak Mahindra).
- Board approved opening an office in Dubai to tap the Middle East market and serve as a gateway to UK and Europe.
- Dropped evaluation of the previously discussed ERP acquisition due to due-diligence concerns; currently evaluating two other CX/AI companies.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year guidance aiming for 80-90% growth on both revenue and profitability.
- Expects Q3 and Q4 to be stronger, historically being the largest quarters and supported by a robust pipeline of international deals.
- Infrastructure-as-a-service and AI infrastructure targeted to contribute 35-40% of revenue in three years.
- Long-term vision (by FY30) is to derive ~60% of revenue from international markets.
- Aims to scale customer base from 150+ to 500-600 within 3 to 4 years.
- Proprietary IP target is to contribute 25% to 30% of revenue in the next 3 to 4 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CX-as-a-service and AI-as-a-service markets growing at CAGRs of 8.4% and 35% respectively.
- Global industry TAM for CX/AI projected to grow from ~$15 billion currently to ~$30 billion by 2030.
- Enterprise customers are increasingly demanding outcome-based managed services and complete integration (front-office to infrastructure) rather than standalone platforms.
- High demand for private AI ("LLM in a box") and GPU-as-a-service among digital natives and enterprises seeking alternatives to expensive hyperscalers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- International deals command significantly higher gross margins compared to domestic business.
- Domestic gross margins remain steady at 20% to 22%.
- Infrastructure-as-a-service business will involve upfront investments in Year 1, but is expected to offer strong profitability over time.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding global footprint into the US, Australia, Singapore, Middle East (Dubai), and subsequently UK and Europe.
- Building out sovereign private AI and GPU-as-a-service infrastructure (partnered with HPE; launched AMD MI350P processors with 1,000 GPUs, with Nvidia H100 planned next).
- Ongoing investments into proprietary IPs (ExatoIQ and ExaFone) budgeted around $2 million.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Profitable customer acquisition model maintained while scaling operations.
- Long-term, non-cancellable 3-to-5-year customer contracts provide high revenue visibility.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- International deal closure cycles are relatively long, typically taking 6 to 8 months.
- Due diligence on potential inorganic acquisitions can fail, requiring caution and selectivity in M&A execution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bharath Reddy (Inorganic plans & ExatoIQ budget):** Management clarified they dropped the ERP acquisition due to failed due diligence but are evaluating two CX/AI targets. ExatoIQ budget is ~$2 million, with a product revenue potential of $5 million and 40-45% profitability.
2. **Devan Khanna (Competition from OpenAI/ChatGPT & recurring revenue):** Asked why enterprises would return if foundational models are direct. Management explained they own the communication layer, data context, and integration workflows, securing recurring revenue through multi-use-case deployment and managed services.
3. **Devan Khanna (Guidance & blended margins):** Asked if infrastructure services dilute margins. Management stated that capturing the full stack (AI + infrastructure) expands total deal value and delivers better overall profitability over time.
4. **Nikhil (Order book execution & infrastructure contribution):** Management stated Rs 280 crore of the Rs 681 crore order book is executed, leaving Rs 410 crore to be executed over 3 years, with substantial additions expected in Q3/Q4. Infrastructure is expected to form 35-40% of revenue in 3 years.
5. **Bharath Reddy (Guidance upgrade rationale):** Asked why guidance was raised. Management cited expanding international business, traction under Murali's leadership in AI infrastructure, and a robust US/global pipeline.
6. **Analyst on Middle East expansion:** Asked about Dubai subsidiary rationale. Management noted Abhijit is based in Dubai, enabling direct engagement with Mitel leadership, regional telcos, and acting as a bridge to the UK/Europe markets.

### One-line takeaway
Exato Technologies posted a strong Q2 FY27 with 40% YoY revenue growth and 65% PAT growth, backed by early IP commercialization, expanding international traction, and a strategic push into AI infrastructure.
