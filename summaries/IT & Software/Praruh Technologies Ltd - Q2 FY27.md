# Praruh Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth driven by project execution and a strategic pivot toward proprietary software and SaaS.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 84.06 crore (vs Rs 17.60 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Revenue Growth:** 378% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 11.34 crore (vs Rs 1.95 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 13.49% (vs 11.07% in H1 FY26; +242 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 8.18 crore (vs Rs 0.98 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **PAT Growth:** 735% YoY.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Management is transitioning from traditional system integration to proprietary software and SaaS-based offerings.
*   **New Vertical:** Establishing a dedicated vertical for Cyber Security, Defense, and Security Operations Centers (SOC), with a new lead joining in Nov/Dec 2026.
*   **Partnerships:** Signed strategic partnerships with Cisco, Splunk, IBM, Wizeverse AI, Adtran Networks, and Google (WiseBuds) to enhance portfolio and tender eligibility.
*   **Client Wins/Execution:** Executed projects for DMRC, RailTel, ONGC, Indian Navy, and Air Force.
*   **Make-in-India:** Focus on indigenous products to align with government policies and increase total addressable market (TAM).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects revenue to reach "triple figures" (exceeding Rs 100 crore) in FY27/28.
*   **Margins:** Management targets margin expansion to 20-30% as proprietary software and SaaS products scale.
*   **Product Launch:** SaaS and AI-driven product sales are scheduled to commence from February 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong demand observed in Defense, PSUs, and Cyber Security sectors.
*   Industry shifting toward AI-driven, data-centric solutions for infrastructure management.
*   Government "Atmanirbhar Bharat" policies are creating favorable eligibility criteria for indigenous software.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **R&D Investment:** Company is heavily investing in in-house IP, allocating at least 5% of revenue toward R&D.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management expects operating leverage to improve margins as the business scales.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management is prioritizing project cost discipline and monitoring receivables closely to support delivery requirements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Long-term milestones?** A: Management aims to scale beyond Rs 100 crore and transition from system integration to a proprietary software player.
*   **Q: SaaS/Product adoption?** A: Products are in the R&D/maturing phase; launch is planned for Jan/Feb 2027, targeting SMEs and BFSI clients who cannot afford outright purchases.
*   **Q: Margin improvement?** A: Margins are currently ~13%; management expects them to rise to 20-30% as the company shifts to licensing and SaaS models.
*   **Q: Partnership impact?** A: Partnerships with global giants (IBM, Splunk) are essential for bidding on large tenders and meeting certification requirements.

### One-line takeaway
Praruh Technologies delivered strong H1 growth and is aggressively pivoting toward high-margin proprietary software and SaaS, supported by strategic global partnerships and government-led demand in defense and cybersecurity.
