# Infinity Infoway Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27 (Half-year ended September 30, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue doubling, robust EBITDA/PAT growth, and healthy order book visibility.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 16.81 crore (up 106.22% YoY from Rs. 8.15 crore)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 8.52 crore (up 109.68% YoY from Rs. 4.06 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 50.68%
- **PAT:** Rs. 5.02 crore (up 79.36% YoY from Rs. 2.80 crore)
- **PAT Margin:** 29.89%
- **Team Size:** 178 employees (increased from 156 in the previous financial year)
- **Order Book:** Rs. 78 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Total Expenses (including depreciation of Rs. 1.72 crore):** Rs. 10.54 crore

### Highlights
- Evolved from a SaaS ERP software company into an AI-enabled technology company with proprietary products.
- Serving over 61 education clients (recurring revenue from 28 customers) and 35 industry clients (recurring revenue from 16 clients).
- Conducted 76 examinations through portals, serving over 18 lakh students.
- Ongoing execution of Gujarat Gyan Guru Quiz (G3Q) Season 3 with the Government of Gujarat; Season 1 recognized by the World Book of Record, London.
- Participated in AI Manthan 2.0 in Kanpur; received approval to demonstrate the "ZeroTouch" product during examinations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain the current revenue run-rate going forward, noting that H2 is historically stronger than H1.
- ZeroTouch revenue contribution is expected to reach 20%–22% of the top line in the next 2 years.
- International revenue is expected to commence in the current financial year.
- Margins are expected to remain manageable for at least the next couple of quarters/year due to recurring business models and AI cost-efficiencies.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand for automated, secure exam solutions in the Indian government sector due to volume and security requirements.
- International markets show a significant gap for school/college ERP solutions, initially targeting private clients in the Gulf, Southeast Asia, and Europe.
- ZeroTouch international expansion is deferred for at least 1 year while domestic traction and ERP client bases are established.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee costs increased due to headcount addition (from 156 to 178) to support sales capability, execution, and expansion to over 10 states.
- ZeroTouch carries an EBITDA margin of 50%–60%+ due to its proprietary nature.
- Depreciation increased due to capitalization and commencement of revenue for products under development (including ZeroTouch).
- AI implementation is helping reduce certain operating costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure included upgrading ZeroTouch devices following Q1 demonstrations and hiring GPUs/building physical servers in-house for AI/LLM models.
- Geographic presence expanded to more than 10 states in India.
- Phased international expansion plan: Gulf first, followed by Southeast Asia, then Europe, focusing initially on private schools and colleges.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not specifically discussed in detail, other than mentioning that ZeroTouch is a CapEx-heavy proprietary product.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Current conditions in the international market are described as "not that much favorable."
- Initial entry into international markets will require marketing and expansion investments, making immediate high margins difficult to sustain at the start.
- Scaling at an exponential rate in the future could temporarily make margin management challenging during initial periods.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vaibhav Parekh (Balaji Capital) on Employee Costs:** Management explained that team size increased from 156 to 178 to build sales and execution capability for expansion across 10+ states.
2. **Meet Gada on International Markets:** Management confirmed a phase-wise expansion starting with private schools/colleges in the Gulf, then Southeast Asia, and Europe.
3. **Maitri Shah on ZeroTouch Revenues and Margins:** Management stated ZeroTouch revenue is currently nominal in H1 but expected to pick up in Q3; margins are 50%–60%+ EBITDA, and it is projected to contribute 20%–22% to the top line in 2 years.
4. **Maitri Shah on Seasonality:** Management confirmed that historically (over 5 years), H2/Q3/Q4 outperform H1/Q1/Q2.
5. **Kapil Advani on Depreciation and PPE:** Management attributed the depreciation spike to commercialization/revenues starting for ZeroTouch and other previously under-development products, and PPE additions to ZeroTouch device upgrades and GPU/server investments for AI/LLM models.

### One-line takeaway
Infinity Infoway delivered a stellar H1 FY27 with revenue more than doubling and a 50%+ EBITDA margin, backed by strong execution in AI-driven education ERP, government quiz platforms, and the commercial rollout of its proprietary ZeroTouch examination solution.
