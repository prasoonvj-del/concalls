# Ivalue Infosolutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust tracked funnels, higher expected deal conversions in H2, and reiterated full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Sales:** Rs 731.6 crore (up 14% QoQ, down 17.6% YoY vs. Rs 887.8 crore in Q2 FY26 due to a high base from a ~Rs 150 crore deal last year)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Sales:** Rs 1,372.8 crore (down 8.1% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 70.2 crore (up 35% QoQ, down 2.8% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Margin:** 9.6% (normalized at 8.9% excluding a one-time high-margin deal benefit; H1 FY27 normalized gross margin at 8.6%)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 122.3 crore (up 7.8% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 31.3 crore (up 54.7% QoQ, down 29.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 51.5 crore (down 14.7% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Operating EBITDA Margin:** 4.3% (H1 FY27 at 3.8%)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 23.4 crore (up 48.7% QoQ) with a 3.2% margin on gross sales and 12.2% on net revenue
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 39.1 crore (down 2.4% YoY)
- **Annuity Sales (H1 FY27):** Rs 734.2 crore (up 16.1% YoY), representing 53.5% of gross sales (Q2 annuity at Rs 437 crore, 59% of sales)
- **Tracked Funnel:** Rs 6,900 crore (up ~12% QoQ from Rs 6,150 crore)
- **Net Working Capital:** 48 days as of September 30, 2026 (vs. 46 days in September 2025)
- **Cash Adjusted for Debt:** Rs 124 crore
- **ROCE:** 24.7% for Q2 FY27 and 22.9% for H1 FY27
- **Non-operating Income:** Included a Rs 3.7 crore gain from selling vacant land.
- **One-time Write-off:** Rs 4.4 crore bad debt provision for an uncollectible receivable from a defaulted Indian partner.

### Highlights
- H1 performance was impacted by a high base in Q2 FY26 (which included a ~Rs 150 crore large deal) and delayed enterprise customer decisions due to heavy negotiations and hardware cost inflations.
- Segment performance in H1: Cybersecurity declined 8.7% YoY; Information Lifecycle Management (ILM) dropped 48.1% YoY due to lumpiness and budget shifts; Data Center Infrastructure (DCI) grew 39.7% YoY; Application Lifecycle Management (ALM), cloud, and others grew 27.2% YoY.
- Annuity growth was driven by customers opting for renewals over costly tech refreshes/upgrades due to budget constraints.
- *Continuity note:* Unlike earlier reports of purely organic growth without defaults, this quarter included a Rs 4.4 crore write-off from a partner default.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated full-year FY27 guidance of **20% top-line growth and 20% PAT growth** over the previous year.
- Management expects H2 to account for ~60% of annual sales, supported by large deal conversions in Q3 and Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Large enterprise purchasing remains budget-led and skewed toward H2. 
- Customers are heavily negotiating post-RFP awards and grappling with AI-related security threats and DPDP regulatory compliance deadlines.
- Indian OEMs in the security/compliance space are outperforming global peers and expanding into the Middle East.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses grew 35% YoY (or 18% normalized, stripping out the Rs 4.4 crore partner write-off).
- Manpower costs grew 15% YoY in H1 due to right hiring, ASEAN international investments, and annual appraisal hikes of 8% to 10%.
- Forex M2M impact created a ~Rs 1 crore hit on P&L.
- Medium-term gross margins are expected to stay between 9% to 9.5%, with operating leverage driving EBITDA margin expansion of 20-25 bps.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continued investments in international presence, particularly in ASEAN markets (Thailand, Philippines, Vietnam, CLM region).
- Management intends to evaluate acquisition targets using its Rs 124 crore cash pile, or make a capital allocation call in Q1 next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital stands at 48 days.
- Net cash (cash adjusted for debt) is Rs 124 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Customer budget constraints leading to deferral of tech refreshes in favor of basic renewals.
- Binary nature of large deals creates quarterly lumpiness and execution dependency.
- Default risk from partners, as evidenced by the Rs 4.4 crore write-off this quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinay Menon (Monarch Networth Capital) on Q3 early indicators and guidance math:** Management stated that October billings and confirmed bookings for the next two months already exceed total Q3 billings from last year, giving confidence in a strong H2.
2. **Jatin Chawla on ILM segment decline:** Management explained that ILM deals were deferred as customer budgets temporarily shifted toward Data Center Infrastructure (DCI); recovery is expected in Q3 and Q4.
3. **Vibhav Khandelwal on the Rs 4-5 crore write-off recovery:** Management clarified that the write-off resulted from a settlement with a defaulted Indian client, and no further recovery is expected.
4. **Gokul on employee expense increases:** Management attributed the 15% YoY H1 increase to strategic headcount additions, ASEAN market investments, and 8%-10% annual salary appraisals.
5. **Bijal Shah on cybersecurity breaches acting as an industry tailwind:** Management noted that while cyber incidents are increasing, enterprise IT teams are generally well-prepared; breaches often stem from improper feature implementation rather than lack of software purchases.
6. **Vinay Menon on DPDP regulation opportunities:** Management confirmed they are ahead of the curve, have secured marquee contracts, and view the Make-in-India compliance ecosystem as a strong tailwind for the next two years.

### One-line takeaway
Despite an H1 contraction driven by a high base and deal deferrals, iValue reiterated its 20% full-year growth guidance, anchored by a robust Rs 6,900 crore tracked funnel and anticipated large deal conversions in H2.
