# Ksolves India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue declined YoY and QoQ due to cautious spending and client-specific ramp-downs, but profitability remained resilient within the guided band, supported by AI-led productivity and cost optimization.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 37.8 crores (down 4.7% YoY, 8.8% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 79.2 crores (up 2.5% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 9.7 crores (Margin: 25.7%, down from 30.4% in Q2 FY26 and 30.3% in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 22.3 crores (Margin: 28.1%)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 7.25 crores (Margin: 19.2%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 16.5 crores (up 10.9% YoY)
- **Q2 EPS:** INR 3.05 (vs INR 3.55 YoY)
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** INR 17.6 crores as of September 30, 2026
- **Second Interim Dividend:** INR 1 per share (Total H1 interim dividend: INR 5 per share, ~72% of H1 PAT)

### Highlights
- Became a Select Partner in both the Anthropic (Claude Partner Network) and OpenAI Partner Networks; 100% of engineers are Claude certified.
- Upgraded to Frappe Bronze Partner in India.
- Secured key business wins across diversified sectors, including three in banking (South America, U.K., Europe), global ports, and education in Australia.
- Recognized by Dun & Bradstreet as one of the leading SMEs for 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue to remain soft and trail last year's levels due to cautious client spending and macroeconomic/geopolitical uncertainty.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to remain within the guided range of 25% to 30% for FY27, with scope for improvement in Q4 as recent wins ramp up.
- Management expressed optimism about returning to 20%+ growth in FY28, conditional on geopolitical stability.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Clients remain selective with discretionary spending under scrutiny.
- Client discussions have shifted from headcount/technology to agentic AI business outcomes across platforms like Odoo and Salesforce.
- Initial client exploration of DIY AI has largely shifted back to outsourcing feature additions and domain expertise combined with AI.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin moderated to 25.7% due to a revenue-led dip (absorbing a revenue decline of INR 3.6 crores in the quarter), which was partially offset by cost optimization, headcount rationalization, and AI-led productivity.
- AI deployment increased engineer output by 2x to 3x per person.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Employee headcount reduced from ~550 to ~495-500 due to natural attrition and capacity adjustments, managed effectively through AI productivity gains without new hiring.
- Opened a sales office in Australia for local client contracts and bidding purposes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a debt-free balance sheet with a cash and bank balance of INR 17.6 crores.
- Continued strong shareholder returns with interim dividends totaling INR 5 per share for H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cautious client spending environment and global macroeconomic/geopolitical uncertainty.
- Client-specific ramp-downs (notably in telecom) impacted quarterly revenue, and the pipeline was not deep enough to fully offset the drop within the quarter.
- Scarcity of specialized AI and BFSI skill sets in the market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Apoorv (Individual Investor) on FY28 growth outlook:** Management stated they are optimistic about returning to 20%+ growth in FY28, provided geopolitical conditions remain stable.
- **Nikhil Lakhkar (Individual Investor) on GCC opportunities:** Management noted they do not have a dedicated sales or marketing team for the domestic Indian market, focusing primarily on North America, Europe, Australia, and the Middle East.
- **Priyanka Prashant (Individual Investor) on BFSI momentum:** Management stated they have 11-12+ BFSI clients and expect meaningful revenue contributions from recent banking wins starting in H2 FY27.
- **Apoorv (Individual Investor) on client ramp-downs:** Management confirmed a large revenue hit from two clients, though one has started scaling back up with new AI-driven requirements.
- **Dhiraj Dave (Samvad Financial) on employee reduction:** Management explained that the drop in headcount to ~500 was driven by natural attrition and capacity adjustments, which were easily absorbed because AI boosted per-employee output by 2x to 3x.

### One-line takeaway
Ksolves reported a soft quarter with revenue declining 4.7% YoY due to client-specific ramp-downs, but defended its profitability within the guided margin band through AI-driven productivity gains and disciplined cost management.
