# Capitalnumbers Infotech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Negative - Management acknowledged missing growth targets, failed Middle East expansion, and significant share price underperformance.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 54.24 crore
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 13.54 crore
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 24.96%
*   **Net Profit:** Rs 9.69 crore
*   **Net Profit Margin:** 17.87%
*   **EPS:** Rs 3.97
*   **Epitome Cloud (50-day contribution):** Revenue Rs 4.43 crore, EBITDA Rs 1.5 crore

### Highlights
*   **Acquisition:** Completed 100% acquisition of Epitome Cloud Inc (US-based Salesforce/Enterprise transformation firm) on August 10, 2026, for Rs 40 crore.
*   **Performance vs. Record:** The call confirms the decline in H1 FY27 revenue and profitability noted in our records, explicitly attributing it to failed Middle East expansion and inability to replace project-based revenue.
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Management is pivoting away from Middle East investments due to "intensely prolonged" decision-making cycles and refocusing on US/UK markets.
*   **Retention:** Reported ~90% client retention rate.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **H2 FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Rs 58 crore to Rs 60 crore.
*   **H2 FY27 EBITDA Guidance:** Rs 17 crore to Rs 18 crore.
*   **Epitome Cloud:** Management expects 10-15% YoY growth for the acquired entity.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Service Lines:** AI and Generative AI are the fastest-growing service lines; company is now a Claude-certified partner.
*   **Market Focus:** Shifting focus from pure-play offshore to offering onshore/on-site consulting in the US and Europe.
*   **Salesforce:** Epitome Cloud’s focus on CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) and CLM (Contract Lifecycle Management) is viewed as a niche, high-barrier-to-entry area.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Compression:** EBITDA margin impacted by three factors: 50% decline in other income (Rs 3.2 crore impact), lower operating revenue (Rs 1.68 crore impact), and higher group expenses (Rs 1.84 crore impact).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Cash Position:** Company holds ~Rs 150 crore in cash/investments.
*   **Expansion:** Planning to open an office in the Bay Area (US) to bolster local presence.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **M&A:** Management expressed intent for faster, more judicial deployment of remaining IPO funds for inorganic growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution Failure:** Management admitted to failing to achieve objectives in the Middle East over the last 9-10 months.
*   **Guidance Miss:** Management acknowledged missing the 15-20% organic growth guidance provided in previous calls.
*   **Revenue Replacement:** Difficulty in replacing revenue from completed projects or clients reducing spend.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Nisarg Shah (Individual Investor):** Challenged the 50% miss on guidance. Management admitted operating challenges and that the Middle East investment did not yield expected results.
*   **Mustafa (Individual Investor):** Critiqued the share price performance (trading at 1/4th of IPO price) and lack of EPS growth. Management admitted the performance has been poor and acknowledged investor frustration.
*   **Ananya Sharma:** Asked about H2 growth drivers. Management cited Salesforce/AI integration and the pivot to Western markets.
*   **Maitri Shah:** Questioned the math on Epitome's contribution. Management clarified that the Rs 4.43 crore revenue reflects only 50 days of consolidation post-acquisition.

### One-line takeaway
Management is pivoting away from failed Middle East expansion to focus on US/UK markets and Salesforce/AI integration to recover from a weak H1 performance.
