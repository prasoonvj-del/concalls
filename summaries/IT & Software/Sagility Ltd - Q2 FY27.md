# Sagility Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, highest adjusted EBITDA margin in six quarters, and raised FY27 EBITDA margin guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 20,321 million / $212.6 million, up 22.5% YoY (12.2% in constant currency).
- **Organic Revenue Growth (Q2 FY27):** 22.2% in INR terms / 11.9% in constant currency (ex-CareSeed).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 5,353 million / $56 million, up 23% YoY.
- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 26.3% (highest in six quarters).
- **Adjusted PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 3,254 million / $34 million, up 8.1% YoY (16% margin).
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR 39,956 million / $420.4 million, up 25% YoY (13.7% in constant currency).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 10,069 million / $105.9 million, up 25.2% YoY (25.2% margin).
- **Adjusted PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 5,951 million / $62.6 million, up 18.9% YoY (14.9% margin).
- **Deal ACV:** $40.2 million, up 18.2% YoY (wins across 31 existing clients and 1 new client).
- **Employee Strength:** 51,486 at the end of Q2.
- **Quarterly Voluntary Attrition:** 25.7% (improved from 28.6% in Q1 FY27 and lower YoY).
- **DSO:** 78 days (improved from 80 days in Q1).
- **Net Debt / (Cash):** Moved to a net cash position of INR 1,127 million as of September 2026 (from net debt of INR 1,962 million at the end of FY26).
- **Chirok Health TTM Revenue (up to Sept 2026):** $13 million with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 35.8% and NRR >115% (750+ employees).
- **Chirok Acquisition Cost:** $20 million up front, plus up to $12.35 million earn-out over CY27 and CY28.

### Highlights
- Won a large payment integrity engagement from a Big Ten health plan covering >10 million members ($10M+ ACV).
- Each of the top 10 client groups now exceeds $20 million in revenue on a trailing 12-month basis.
- Board approved an interim dividend of INR 0.15 (15 paise) per share for Q3.
- Closed the acquisition of Chirok Health LLC yesterday to bolster mid-cycle RCM and provider capabilities. CareSeed is now fully integrated into the clinical practice.
- Won industry awards including Everest Group Elevate Honor, Avasant Leader recognition, Stratus Award, and Golden Peacock Award for ESG.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Management maintains low double-digit organic revenue growth guidance in constant currency for FY27.
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Management raised FY27 adjusted EBITDA margin guidance from 24%–25% to 25%–26%.
- **Debt Repayment:** Management remains on track to repay outstanding debt fully during FY27.
- **AEP/OE Seasonality:** Management expects Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) and Open Enrollment (OE) revenues to be slightly lower than last year (down by ~100 bps as a percentage of total revenue).

### Demand & industry commentary
- U.S. healthcare is navigating steep overall cost increases, but national payers improved H1 2026 profitability via right-sizing membership and lower medical utilization rates.
- Payers are prioritizing immediate operational efficiencies, clinical outcomes, and AI integration.
- Buying processes for transformational deals are rigorous, involving senior stakeholders and clear business cases.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 26.3% due to operating leverage, disciplined cost management, delivery efficiencies, favorable FX movements, and a favorable geo mix, despite statutory wage revisions in Karnataka and Telangana (full quarter impact of INR 148 million in Q2 vs INR 70 million in Q1).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 fixed asset additions stood at INR 1,168 million. Operating cash flow for H1 was INR 6,343 million; free cash flow was INR 5,176 million.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company transitioned from a net debt of INR 1,962 million at the end of FY26 to a net cash position of INR 1,127 million by September 2026.
- DSO improved to 78 days in Q2 from 80 days in Q1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as major risks, though management noted that AEP/OE revenue contribution may be slightly lower YoY.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Chirag Kachhadiya (Motilal Oswal) on margin levers & provider acquisition scale:** Management replied that margins improved due to operating efficiencies, FX tailwinds, and geo mix. Chirok acquisition brings $13M in TTM revenue and complements the mid-revenue cycle.
2. **Rishi Jhunjhunwala (IIFL) on provider strategy and AEP outlook:** Management stated the provider strategy is to accelerate growth via Chirok and a focused go-to-market team; AEP revenue impact is expected to be ~100 bps lower than last year's 6%.
3. **Rohit Thorat (Axis Capital) on payer-provider conflict of interest:** Management clarified there is no inherent conflict as they help both operate efficiently, bridging friction around denials and payment delays.
4. **Vamshi Krishna (Kotak) on AI disruption in mid-cycle RCM:** Management noted that while AI impacts outpatient coding, inpatient coding relies on human expertise, making Chirok's services durable.
5. **Akshat Agarwal (Jefferies) on minimum wage updates and delivery centers:** Management confirmed minimum wage impacts are fully baked into guidance; reduction in reported delivery centers is primarily due to site consolidation/counting methodologies.
6. **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama) on ACV composition and provider deal sizes:** Management stated provider deals tend to be smaller historically, but end-to-end RCM capabilities will enable larger deals; gross margins are comparable (within 200–300 bps).

### One-line takeaway
Sagility delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 22.5% YoY revenue growth, a 6-quarter high EBITDA margin of 26.3%, a net cash balance sheet, and a raised FY27 EBITDA margin guidance of 25%–26%, further augmented by the strategic acquisition of Chirok Health.
