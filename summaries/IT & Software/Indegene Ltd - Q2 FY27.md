# Indegene Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent revenue growth across client base, strong deal wins, and expanding GenAI-enabled offerings.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR11,223 million (up 39.5% YoY, 5.6% QoQ); US$117.5 million (up 27.5% YoY, 4.4% QoQ)
- EBITDA: INR1,873 million (up 32% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 16.7%
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): INR1,474 million
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR1,123 million (up 10% YoY), 10% of revenue
- Effective Tax Rate: 23.8%
- Revenue per employee (Trailing 12-month): US$79,600 (up from ~US$69,000 YoY)
- Active Clients: 107 (31 more than a year ago)
- Clients above $1 million/year: 59
- Clients above $10 million: 13
- Tectonic run rate: $1.7 million this quarter (growing from ~$1 million/quarter)
- Net DSO (net of unearned and unbilled revenue): 69 days (up 2 days)
- Cash and investments: INR13,896 million

### Highlights
- Marked 5 consecutive quarters of >5% QoQ growth in INR terms.
- Top 5 accounts grew 9.2% sequentially in INR terms; top 20 accounts grew 6.7%.
- BioPharm completed its first year as part of Indegene; realized ~$1.5 million in annual cost synergies, reinvested by expanding go-to-market team from 3 to 9 people.
- Won 9 deals with ACV >$1 million, including 3 large deals (one $5 million+, two $3-$5 million).
- Expanded Tectonic model to Canada (3rd market after Germany and Spain) with the largest customer.
- Launched Audience Intelligence platform combining Tandem and Invisage within the Indegene Data Universe.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide formal guidance.
- Re-stated goals: Organic growth rate better than FY26, and quarterly EBITDA margins back to the historical range (19% plus) by Q4 FY27.
- Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1, and margin growth in H2 to outpace revenue growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry drivers: Capital and deal-making are back (pharma/biotech funding up ~85% YoY to >INR69 billion; M&A doubled to ~INR194 billion); record U.S. regulatory approvals (45 novel medicines); pipeline is larger and later-stage (~12,700 molecules in development, Phase II up ~18%); industry reallocating spending to flexible partners and AI.
- Shift from whether AI can work to how to scale it; focus on workflow redesign and scientific credibility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Annual wage increases impacted Q2 margins by ~150 bps, which was largely offset by improved productivity and favorable exchange rates.
- Interest income declined 44% QoQ to INR134 million due to markdown in mutual fund portfolio NAVs from rising interest rates, impacting PBT by ~90 bps and PAT by ~70 bps.
- Amortization charges are expected to reduce in Q3 and beyond.
- Investment portfolio shifting toward higher allocation to held-to-maturity instruments to reduce volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and investments stood at INR13,896 million.
- DSO net of unearned and unbilled revenue slightly higher at 69 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ramp-up for Tectonic with the largest customer remains slow due to internal change management dynamics between central teams and local/therapeutic brand teams.
- One customer slipped slightly below the $25 million-plus cohort due to temporary volume variations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vinay Menon (Monarch Capital) asked about Tectonic run rate and unbilled revenue jump.** Management stated Tectonic grew to $1.7 million/quarter (contracts crossed $2 million/quarter), and unbilled revenue timing was due to summer delays in Europe/U.S.
- **Prolin Nandu (Edelweiss Public Alternatives) asked why Tectonic ramp-up is slow while scope increases and details on Agentic AOR.** Management explained central vs. local brand team dynamics cause slow ramp-ups, and described Agentic AOR as an AI-led concepting approach that compresses agency launch timelines from months to days.
- **Renga Varshini (Wealthified) asked about competition from IT companies like TCS ADD.** Management stated they do not see IT companies in their deal segment, competing primarily with domain-focused agencies and CROs.
- **Sathish (Kotak Institutional Equities) asked about the $10 million outcome-based deal timing.** Management clarified that meaningful revenue and margin impact will show up in Q4 following calendar-year threshold true-ups in February/March.
- **Divyansh Agarwal (Ionic Asset Managers) asked about the $25 million client drop.** Management reiterated it was a temporary volume variation and the client is expected to return to the cohort in 2-3 quarters backed by new deal wins.

### One-line takeaway
Indegene delivered a strong Q2 with 39.5% YoY revenue growth, expanding high-value enterprise relationships, and growing traction in GenAI-enabled solutions like Tectonic and Agentic AOR, keeping it on track for 19%+ EBITDA margins by Q4.
