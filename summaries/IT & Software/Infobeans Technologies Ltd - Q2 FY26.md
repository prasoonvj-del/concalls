# Infobeans Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained revenue growth and strong client retention despite macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 165 crore (+28% YoY, +6% QoQ).
*   **USD Revenue Growth:** 22% YoY.
*   **Operating Margin:** 16%.
*   **Client Retention:** 94% of revenue derived from existing clients.

### Highlights
*   Achieved "Claude Select Services" partner status with Anthropic.
*   Core focus areas remain AI, Salesforce, and ServiceNow.
*   BFSI identified as a strong vertical.
*   Awarded Ecovadis bronze medal for sustainability practices.
*   Team strength stands at 1,900 employees.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management aims for 24% operating margins in the long term.
*   Management expects a sustained healthy order pipeline and demand.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Shift toward outcome-based pricing models observed.
*   Enterprise demand is increasingly focused on "agentic" AI workflows (L0/L1 automation).
*   Management reported no pricing pressure despite AI adoption.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Operating margin reported at 16%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Capitalized Rs 2 crore for intangible assets (accelerators/products like Stanza and Beantrail).
*   Actively scouting M&A targets in the $10 million range, specifically in ServiceNow and data domains.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Trade receivables increased; management attributed this to business growth and client payment cycles, expressing confidence in recovery.
*   Director loan (interest-bearing) has been repaid.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   US visa norms (H1B) and macro environment (Trump administration) noted as potential challenges, though management claims low dependency.
*   Increased trade receivables.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **M&A Strategy:** Management is scouting targets in the $10 million range but maintains strict criteria; no deal is signed yet.
*   **AI Pricing:** Management stated there is no pricing pressure; AI is primarily used to increase internal efficiency.
*   **Receivables:** Management attributed the increase to business growth and client payment cycles, expressing confidence in recovery.
*   **Visa Dependency:** Management clarified that they are offshore-heavy and not significantly dependent on H1B visas.

### One-line takeaway
InfoBeans delivered 28% YoY revenue growth with a focus on AI-led service offerings and active M&A scouting, despite minor working capital headwinds.
