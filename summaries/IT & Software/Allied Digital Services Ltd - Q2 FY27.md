# Allied Digital Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record H1 revenues and improved working capital metrics, with management signaling stabilization in government project bidding.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 265 crore (Q2), up 14% YoY; Rs 526 crore (H1), up 16% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 27 crore (Q2), 10% margin.
*   **PBT:** Rs 17 crore (Q2); Rs 34 crore (H1).
*   **PAT:** Rs 13 crore (Q2); Rs 25 crore (H1).
*   **New Orders/Renewals:** Rs 74 crore secured in Q2.
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 185 crore.
*   **Gross Borrowings:** Rs 167 crore.
*   **Trade Receivables:** Rs 264 crore (down from Rs 320 crore as of March 2026).
*   **Debtor Days:** 84 days (improved from 96 days in FY26).

### Highlights
*   Services business (annuity contracts) grew 31% YoY.
*   International revenues increased 36% YoY.
*   Secured key engagements including a U.S.-based AI-powered service desk and a system integration project for MIDC (Maharashtra).
*   Management is prioritizing direct customer engagements to increase ownership and share of technology spending, moving away from a reliance on partner-led/consortium models.
*   FY27 is characterized by management as a period of "consolidation and institutional strengthening."

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains the annual revenue milestone of Rs 1,000 crore.
*   Management targets a 20% CAGR year-over-year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Enterprises are becoming more deliberate in investment decisions, leading to extended evaluation cycles for large transformation programs.
*   Government segment activity is picking up; hardware/equipment costs have stabilized, allowing the company to return to active bidding.
*   Management noted that while large contracts take longer to conclude, small and mid-sized opportunities are converting faster.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins reflect ongoing investments in talent, leadership, and growth capabilities.
*   Management noted that equipment and hardware costs, which previously impacted commercial viability, have shown signs of stabilization.
*   Focus remains on optimizing cost structure, improving delivery productivity, and enhancing resource utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   The company maintains a positive net cash position.
*   Gross borrowings increased to Rs 167 crore to support the growing scale of operations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Competitive pricing pressures and elevated operating costs.
*   Volatility in product pricing (though currently stabilizing).
*   Extended decision-making cycles for large transformation programs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Rushil Katiyar (Choice Equities):** Asked about the decline in order book and government tender participation. Management explained they were previously selective due to price volatility but are now bidding again (e.g., Noida Safe City) as OEM pricing has stabilized.
*   **Hitesh Kadam (Arihant Capital):** Asked about the mix of government vs. enterprise business. Management stated they are striking a balance: government projects provide scale, while enterprise business provides longevity and stability.
*   **Hitesh Kadam (Arihant Capital):** Asked when AI savings will outpace revenue deflation. Management noted they are running parallel processes before reducing headcount and are focusing on automation and preventive problem-solving.
*   **Rushil Katiyar (Choice Equities):** Asked about M&A plans. Management confirmed they are looking for potential acquisitions specifically in the AI and cybersecurity spaces.

### One-line takeaway
Allied Digital delivered record H1 revenue growth and improved working capital, while pivoting toward direct enterprise relationships and leveraging AI to manage costs amidst stabilizing government project demand.
