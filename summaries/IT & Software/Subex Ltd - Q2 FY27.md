# Subex Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent QoQ revenue growth, disciplined execution, and expanding operating margins despite annual wage hikes.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 85.59 crore (up 7.7% QoQ, 24% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 20.8%
- Normalized EBITDA: Rs 17.77 crore (vs. Rs 16.87 crore QoQ)
- PAT: Rs 14.29 crore (vs. Rs 14.22 crore QoQ)
- Normalized PAT: Rs 14.3 crore (vs. Rs 16.9 crore QoQ)
- Cash and cash equivalents: Rs 198 crore (vs. Rs 184 crore at the end of the previous quarter)
- Minority investments: PrivaSapien appreciated ~5x, OnGrid appreciated ~2x relative to original values.

### Highlights
- Marked five consecutive quarters of QoQ growth.
- Operating discipline over the last three years is translating into financial performance.
- Minority investments in PrivaSapien and OnGrid showed significant appreciation (~5x and ~2x respectively).
- AI agent deal ("FraudZap agents") closed in a 90-day sprint; three technical POCs successfully closed exit criteria in the last quarter.
- HyperSense deployment timelines reduced by ~15-20% due to reusable libraries of controls.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims for high single-digit to double-digit growth directionally.
- EBITDA path and margin durability targeted within a sustainable range.
- Minority investments and inorganic growth opportunities flagged as primary uses of the cash reserve (Rs 198 crore). Buybacks are currently not an option due to negative retained earnings.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Telco industry growth remains around 2-3%; Subex is growing at 3-5x the industry rate.
- Geopolitical issues in the Middle East and US elections noted as wildcards/impacts, though partially baked into planning.
- India market traction expected to remain limited as major players like Jio build proprietary tech stacks; company continues working with Vodafone.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Annual wage increase took place in Q2, yet EBITDA and PBT grew QoQ.
- R&D expenses do not pass through the balance sheet and are not capitalized; they go entirely through the P&L with increasing intensity.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus on inorganic growth pipelines utilizing cash reserves.
- Expanding into AI-led capabilities: Architecture of Autonomy (AOA), modular point solutions, and economic integrity layers rather than relying on a platform-heavy approach.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at Rs 198 crore.
- Negative retained earnings prevent share buybacks currently; ESOP trust share purchases to be spaced out subject to UPSI constraints.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions (Middle East volatility, US elections).
- Client concentration and competitive pricing pressures in the telecom software space.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Parth Dalal (Individual Investor)** asked if AI lets operators run fraud/assurance themselves, what replaces managed services? Management answered that managed services cover maintenance, upgrades, and cross-telco insights/patterns that individual operators lack.
- **Haider Kachwalla (Individual Investor)** questioned the constant currency growth versus reported INR depreciation. Management clarified that INR depreciation benefit is around 2-3% QoQ and 8-10% YoY, and current growth significantly outperforms the 2-3% industry baseline.
- **Patrick Mathias (Retail Investor)** asked about the AI strategy (point products vs platforms). Management confirmed a modular, interoperable point-solution approach ("Architecture of Autonomy") rather than a monolithic platform stack.
- **Abhishek Kale (Individual Investor)** asked about new logo revenue contribution and implementation time. Management explained backlog recognition (70% recurring/backlog, ~40-50% of new deal recognized in year one) and noted a 15-20% reduction in HyperSense implementation timelines.
- **Mahesh Kumar (Individual Investor)** inquired about AI agent scalability and orchestration. Management confirmed agents scale to multi-agent manager/router structures and focus on economic integrity and decision governance.

### One-line takeaway
Subex delivered its fifth consecutive quarter of QoQ growth, translating long-term operational discipline into financial expansion while pivoting toward AI-driven decision integrity and modular architecture.
