# Sonata Software Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Mixed - International revenue returned to sequential growth and domestic gross contribution rose, but offset by top-account ramp-downs and margin investments.

### Key numbers
- **International Services Rupee Revenue:** Rs 797.9 crore (+2.7% QoQ, +9.3% YoY)
- **International Services USD Revenue:** USD 82.8 million (+1% QoQ, +0.9% YoY; +1.2% QoQ, +1.3% in constant currency)
- **International Services EBITDA Margin (before other income & forex):** 15.5% (vs 15.4% in Q1 FY27)
- **International Services PAT:** Rs 69.1 crore (+11.1% QoQ, YoY degrowth)
- **Domestic Business Revenue:** Rs 1,655.6 crore (-33.9% QoQ, +19% YoY)
- **Domestic Business Gross Contribution:** Rs 82 crore (+4.5% QoQ, +19.3% YoY)
- **Domestic Business PAT:** Rs 47.3 crore (+3.1% QoQ, +12.2% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,445.2 crore (+15.4% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 116.4 crore (+7.7% QoQ from Rs 118.1 crore in Q1 FY27, YoY degrowth)
- **International Order Book:** USD 98.5 million (book-to-bill of 1.18x); AI-led order book at USD 24.2 million (~25% of total)
- **International AI Pipeline:** USD 358 million
- **Total Headcount:** 6,214; Attrition: 10%
- **Utilization:** 90.5% (vs 88.5% in Q1 FY27)
- **Closing Gross Cash Balance:** Rs 426.3 crore

### Highlights
- International Services returned to sequential growth (1% QoQ USD) following previous quarters of headwinds and large-account ramp-downs.
- Added 4 new customers in International Services; top 10 clients contributed 51% revenue share.
- Domestic business signed a 5-year strategic collaboration agreement with AWS and a 3-year joint GTM initiative with Google India for Google Gemini Enterprise.
- *Comparison to Record:* The call expanded on the steady Q2 performance and 125% interim dividend noted in our record by detailing specific segment-level drivers and headwinds.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects positive growth momentum in the coming quarters despite isolated Q3 headwinds in one or two verticals.
- Management maintains a medium-term margin aspiration of 18% to 20% for International Services.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is driven by AI-led business transformation, legacy modernization, and service delivery transformation.
- Vertical performance: Healthcare & Life Sciences (HLS) growing consistently; TMT solid outside isolated account pressures; BFSI facing minor Q3 headwinds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- International EBITDA margin of 15.5% was impacted by wage hikes for junior management (~60% of workforce), severance costs in a large account (~50 bps impact), and proof-of-delivery/concept investments (~100 bps impact).
- Margins were supported by improved utilization (90.5%), pyramid optimization, fixed-price optimization, and SG&A savings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up competency teams and building platforms for the AWS and Google partnerships over the next two to three quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- International DSO stood at 52 days (aided by advanced collections; management aims to maintain ~60 days).
- Domestic DSO stood at 47 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ramp-downs and renewals pressure in a top account, with potential isolated TMT headwinds in Q3.
- Decision-making cycles for mid-sized accounts remain longer due to security and regulation concerns despite high interest in offerings.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dipesh Mehta (Emkay Global) asked about the top customer drop and data revenue weakness.** Management replied that the drop occurred during renewals and ramp-downs in specific geographies, but the pipeline remains robust.
- **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities) asked about TMT vertical stress and margin levers.** Management clarified that TMT pressure is isolated to specific accounts, and margins are expected to trend upward toward the 18-20% target.
- **Ashis Dash (Systematix) asked about deal sizing strategy.** Management explained an intentional shift toward key deals in the $2 million to $4 million range to mitigate large-deal decision delays.
- **Dipesh Mehta asked about potential impairment for the Quant acquisition.** Management stated periodic governance testing showed no impairment, and a merger of Quant with Sonata is being evaluated by the board.

### One-line takeaway
Sonata reported a return to sequential revenue growth and strong AI deal momentum, balancing margin investments and isolated top-account headwinds with new hyperscaler partnerships.
