# Info Edge (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating profit grew 30% YoY, 99acres losses reduced significantly, and AI monetization scaled up.

### Key numbers
- Standalone billings: Up 13% YoY (H1: up 14%)
- Standalone revenue: Rs.842 crore, up 13% YoY (H1: up 12%)
- Operating profit: Rs.347 crore, up 30% YoY (H1: up 32%)
- Operating PBT margin: 41%, up 541 bps YoY
- Cash generated from operations: Rs.373 crore, up 27% YoY (H1: Rs.598 crore, up 27%)
- Cash balance as of September: Rs.4,976 crore
- Recruitment billings and revenue growth: 13% YoY in Q2 (H1 billings: up 15%)
- Recruitment operating PBT margin: 59%
- 99acres billings growth: 21% YoY in Q2 (up from 17% in Q1); revenue growth: 17% YoY
- 99acres operating loss: Reduced by 93% from Rs.23 crore in Q2 FY26 to less than Rs.2 crore in Q2 FY27
- Matchmaking billings growth: 23% YoY in Q2 (Jeevansathi up 18%, Aisle up 40%)
- Matchmaking operating profit/loss: Jeevansathi reported operating profit of Rs.2 crore; Aisle reported operating loss of Rs.3 crore
- Shiksha billings decline: 27% YoY; revenue decline: 9% YoY
- AI billings annualized run rate (based on Q2): Around Rs.240 crore (Q2 AI revenue: Rs.60 crore)

### Highlights
- Hitesh Oberoi is stepping down as Managing Director and CEO effective December 2, 2026, after 27 years; Sanjeev Bikhchandani will take over as Executive Vice Chairman, Managing Director, and Group CEO starting December 3, 2026.
- Excluding Shiksha, standalone billings grew over 14% in Q2 and about 16% in H1.
- AI-led services for jobseekers (Naukri 360) grew billings by 37%; paid subscribers reached 2.7% of MAUs (vs. 1.3% in January '25). Job Hai billings grew 86%.
- 99acres app DAUs grew 25% YoY; live listings grew 23% for owners, 14% for brokers, and 15% for new projects; property enquiries grew 33% YoY.
- Investment portfolio spans ~135 companies with ~Rs.5,000 crore invested.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects 99acres to generate positive operating cash for FY27, with cash generation strengthening over time.
- Internal sales targets are set around 14% to 15% growth.
- Management aims to gain further share in H2 in both traffic and revenue for Real Estate while improving profitability.

### Demand & industry commentary
- In core India B2B recruitment, hiring activity stayed steady, led mainly by replacement demand and premium hiring.
- Q2 recruitment billings growth across sectors: IT, Tech and BPM up 3%; GCCs up 7%; other sectors up 18%; recruitment consultants up 7%.
- Premium jobseeker views on Naukri grew by 25% YoY in Q2.
- Real estate competitive intensity has decreased; Housing.com sold to Aurum, which aims to break even in 4-5 quarters, leading to lower marketing costs industry-wide.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 99acres marketing investment was about 13% lower YoY while enquiries grew.
- Job Hai burn is around Rs.45 crore a year, impacting overall Naukri margins by 2-3%.
- AI offerings carry high gross margins with minimal incremental cost post-development.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Increasing investment in AI infrastructure, talent, and tools.
- Expanding 99Shorts beyond NCR to more cities.
- Shiksha is pivoting by building domestic counseling supported by AI voice bots and expanding Study Abroad destination coverage to the UK, UAE, and continental Europe.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Ended September with a cash balance of Rs.4,976 crore.
- ~Rs.5,000 crore invested through balance sheet and AIFs across ~135 companies.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- AI-led changes in search behavior have reduced referral traffic for Shiksha, affecting delivery outcomes.
- Timing of customer renewals creates quarterly volatility in reported billings.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sachin Salgaonkar (Bank of America):** Asked about Sanjeev Bikhchandani's succession role and the 13% billing growth vs prior quarters. *Management answered that Sanjeev will take over as CEO while they evaluate leadership, and that quarterly billings fluctuate due to renewal timing, pointing to 14-15% underlying growth.*
2. **Vijit Jain (Citi):** Asked if AI billings are additive and about bundled vs disaggregated pricing. *Management clarified that AI revenue is nearly all additive (with some base in Talent Pulse and jobseeker services) and is sold via separate rate cards though negotiated alongside bundles.*
3. **Vivekanand Subbaraman (Ambit Capital):** Asked about the rationale for Hitesh's departure given vacant CBO roles and his future equity stake. *Management stated Hitesh remains financially/emotionally attached, will stay on the board, and stepped down because key strategic goals (AI strategy, 99acres leadership, profitable Jeevansathi) and institutional depth are established.*
4. **Swapnil Potdukhe (JM Financial):** Asked about flat unique customer counts in Naukri. *Management explained they revamped e-commerce offerings, making them slightly more expensive for better outcomes, reducing smaller transactional customer counts while e-commerce revenues rose.*
5. **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama):** Asked about the sustainability of IT sector growth given flat net headcounts. *Management noted that even with zero net addition, companies continually replace departing staff, using Naukri as the most efficient hiring platform.*

### One-line takeaway
Info Edge delivered a strong Q2 with 13% revenue and 30% operating profit growth, driven by accelerating momentum in 99acres, scaling AI monetization, and robust cash generation, alongside a leadership transition as Sanjeev Bikhchandani succeeds Hitesh Oberoi as CEO.
