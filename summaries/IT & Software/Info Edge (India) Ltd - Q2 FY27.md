# Info Edge (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong margin expansion and acceleration in real estate, offset by Shiksha's structural slowdown and MD/CEO leadership transition.

### Key numbers
* **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 842 crore, up 13% YoY.
* **Standalone Billings:** Grew 13% YoY (H1 billings up 14% YoY). Excluding Shiksha, standalone billings grew >14% YoY in Q2.
* **Operating Profit:** Rs 347 crore, up 30% YoY.
* **Operating PBT Margin:** 41%, expanded 541 bps YoY.
* **Cash Generated from Operations:** Rs 373 crore, up 27% YoY (H1 cash generated was Rs 598 crore, up 27% YoY).
* **Cash Balance:** Rs 4,976 crore as of September 30, 2026.
* **AI & Data Products ARR:** ~Rs 240 crore (Q2 revenue of ~Rs 60 crore).

### Highlights
* **Recruitment Segment:** Q2 revenue grew 13% YoY; billings grew 13% YoY (H1 billings up 15% YoY). Operating PBT margin was 59% with operating profit up 20% YoY.
  * Sectoral Billings Growth (Q2 YoY / H1 YoY): IT, Tech & BPM +3% / +9%; GCCs +7% / +19%; Other Sectors +18% / +16%; Recruitment Consultants +7% / +5%.
  * Naukri 360 billings grew 37% YoY; paid subscriber penetration reached 2.7% of MAUs (vs 1.3% in Jan 2025) with a 64% operating PBT margin. Job Hai billings grew 86% YoY; NaukriGulf grew 9% YoY (36% operating margin).
* **Real Estate (99acres):** Billings grew 21% YoY to accelerate from Q1 (+17%); revenue grew 17% YoY. Operating losses reduced 93% YoY to <Rs 2 crore (vs Rs 23 crore loss in Q2 FY26). Generated Rs 22 crore in operating cash vs Rs 2 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **Matchmaking (Jeevansathi & Aisle):** Segment billings grew 23% YoY (Jeevansathi +18%, Aisle +40%). Operating near breakeven (Jeevansathi operating profit Rs 2 crore, Aisle operating loss Rs 3 crore). Cash generated was Rs 3 crore vs cash loss of Rs 2 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **Education (Shiksha):** Billings declined 27% YoY; revenue fell 9% YoY. Operated near breakeven with operating cash losses of Rs 16 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management estimates underlying Recruitment billings growth at ~14% to 15% going into H2 FY27.
* Management expects 99acres to generate positive operating cash for full-year FY27 with progressive operating leverage in H2 FY27.
* Management expects Jeevansathi billing growth to accelerate in H2 FY27 and Aisle to remain on a high-growth trajectory.
* Management aims to keep Job Hai annual burn around Rs 45 crore and targets breakeven at Rs 100–125 crore topline.

### Demand & industry commentary
* IT sector hiring remains driven predominantly by replacement demand, while non-IT growth (+18% YoY in Q2) is led by BFSI, Consumer, Retail, and Manufacturing.
* Premium talent demand remains strong, with premium jobseeker views up 25% YoY in Q2 FY27.
* GCC growth is driven primarily by mid-sized and smaller GCCs expanding India operations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Marketing spends for 99acres decreased ~13% YoY despite property enquiries growing 33% YoY, demonstrating marketing efficiency.
* AI products (AI-Rex, Talent Pulse, jobseeker AI tools) carry high gross margins; clients adopting AI offerings paid 10%–15% higher ARPUs on average upon renewal.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Info Edge maintains an investment portfolio of ~135 companies across AI, deeptech, and consumer tech, with ~Rs 5,000 crore invested balance sheet and AIF capital.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Total cash balance stood at Rs 4,976 crore at the end of H1 FY27.
* Operating cash flow generation was Rs 373 crore for Q2 FY27 and Rs 598 crore for H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* AI-driven shifts in search engine behavior significantly reduced referral traffic to Shiksha, forcing a business model pivot toward direct counseling and AI voice bots.
* IT services sector volume pressure continues due to muted net headcount additions by large IT firms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **MD & CEO Transition:** Analysts questioned the sudden departure of Managing Director & CEO Hitesh Oberoi (effective Dec 2, 2026) and succession planning. Management clarified that Founder Sanjeev Bikhchandani will take over as Executive Vice Chairman & Group CEO while internal leadership depth across verticals is evaluated.
* **Recruitment Billings vs Revenue Recognition:** Analysts queried the 13% Q2 billings growth compared to 17.5% in Q1. Management explained that customer renewal timing shifted billings across quarters (H1 billings growth of 15% reflects underlying trend) and revenue recognition occurs over 12 months.
* **AI Revenue Additiveness & Pricing Strategy:** Management confirmed the Rs 240 crore ARR from AI products is almost entirely additive, priced via separate rate cards (e.g., Rs 3,500/mandate for AI-Rex for corporates), and not cannibalizing core Resdex revenues.

### One-line takeaway
Info Edge delivered solid 13% revenue growth and 30% operating profit growth led by margin expansion in 99acres and AI monetization, despite headwind in Shiksha and an upcoming CEO transition.
