# Ceinsys Tech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted eight consecutive quarters of sequential EBITDA growth, strong margin expansion, and a growing share of non-government and long-term projects.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained the policy of not giving quantitative forward-looking revenue or margin guidance.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted slightly to acknowledge subdued top-line growth in H1 due to delayed government fund disbursements, but expressed confidence in a growth pickup by Q4 as funds release.
- **New topics or risks:** Incorporated a new joint venture subsidiary named AASTRA AI India Private Limited (incorporated November 3, 2026) and a new wholly-owned subsidiary in UAE (Abu Dhabi) approved by the board. Old risks regarding unbilled revenue (UBR) and blocked Jal Jeevan Mission funds persisted, but management noted specific Maharashtra state cabinet/GR timelines and UP government collections starting.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The AI Fabric JV announced last quarter was officially incorporated as AASTRA AI India Private Limited on November 3, 2026, and technical/business due diligence is underway.

### Key numbers
- **Operational Revenue:** Rs 160 crore (for Q2 FY27); Rs 317 crore (for H1 FY27, flat YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 43 crore (up 20% YoY for Q2); Rs 81 crore (up 24% YoY for H1).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 26.8% (expansion of 506 bps YoY for Q2); 25.6% for H1.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 33 crore (up 27% YoY for Q2); Rs 64 crore (up 11% YoY for H1).
- **PAT Margin:** 20.4% (expansion of 472 bps YoY for Q2); 20% for H1.
- **Geospatial Engineering Services Revenue:** Rs 130 crore (up 73% YoY for Q2); Rs 225 crore (up 52% YoY for H1).
- **Technology Solutions Revenue:** Rs 29 crore (for Q2).
- **Non-Government Revenue Contribution:** 27% (Rs 44 crore) vs 16% YoY.
- **Order Book:** Rs 805 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Fresh Order Inflows:** Rs 231 crore since April 1, 2026.
- **Mobilized Funds for Inorganic Growth:** Around Rs 235 crores.

### Highlights
- Delivered eight consecutive quarters of sequential EBITDA growth.
- Incorporated joint venture AASTRA AI India Private Limited on November 3, 2026, with AI Fabrik Inc., USA.
- Board approved establishing a wholly-owned subsidiary in UAE (Abu Dhabi) to target smart city, water, and energy geospatial solutions.
- Secured international order from TSecond USA of ~Rs 24 crore for AI-powered change detection and asset management; received Rs 28 crore LOA from Rite Water Solutions and a ~Rs 10 crore O&M extension from MSRDC Tunnels.
- Credit rating upgraded by CARE Ratings to BBB+ with a stable outlook (from BBB with a positive outlook).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its policy of not providing quantitative future revenue or margin guidance.
- Management expects top-line growth to recover and become visible by Q4 FY27 once blocked government funds are disbursed.
- Aims to maintain an equal 50/50 yearly revenue contribution split between geospatial and technology solutions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Focus is shifting towards long-term projects (5 to 7 years) in transport, ITMS, ATMS, MLFF, satellite imagery analytics, 3D city modeling, and digital twins.
- Non-government revenue contribution rose to 27% from 16% YoY.
- Total visible market opportunity size for the transportation domain (ITMS/ATMS/EPC corridors) is estimated between Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 11,000 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by project delivery excellence, resource optimization, and efficiency gains from internal AI tools (which management estimates provide an indirect cost reduction benefit of Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Doubling workforce dedicated to advanced AI solutions and products.
- Expanding international footprint with a new UAE subsidiary incorporating by December end.
- Actively evaluating inorganic acquisition targets in the $20 million range across geospatial, AI, and transport ITMS domains.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Unbilled revenue (UBR) and total trade receivables remain elevated, with UBR at Rs 422 crores as of H1 FY27.
- Management expects ~Rs 250 crore of stuck Maharashtra government project funds (JJM/IoT) to be cleared by March 31 via a state government GR and cabinet session.
- Uttar Pradesh government has started making payments (received ~Rs 11 crore in Oct/Nov, expecting another ~Rs 14 crore).
- Target working capital cycle of 125 to 145 days once collections normalize.
- Net worth strengthened following receipt of the balance 75% of share warrants (Rs 130 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subdued revenue growth in H1 FY27 due to delayed government fund disbursements stalling project execution.
- High unbilled revenue and delayed collections from government clients (particularly Jal Jeevan Mission projects in Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Saksham Tyagi (Desvelado Advisory):** Asked about UAE subsidiary opportunities and AI acquisition timelines. Management stated UAE focus is on water/energy/smart cities incorporating by December end; AI acquisitions are being evaluated without rushing, alongside the AASTRA AI JV.
2. **Deepak Poddar (Analyst):** Inquired about order book visibility and H1 growth shortfall. Management clarified that H1 growth was subdued due to delayed government disbursements, but expects a pickup in Q4 once funds release and margins to continue improving.
3. **Keshav Garg (PMS):** Highlighted massive unbilled revenue (Rs 422 crore in H1 FY27) and non-existent operating cash flows. Management stated that ~Rs 250 crore is expected to be cleared by Maharashtra by March 31 via a cabinet GR, and UP payments have commenced; confirmed zero disputes on UBR work completion.
4. **Gunit Singh (Individual Investor):** Asked about status of pending L1 orders and transport opportunity size. Management stated two of three L1 orders are pursued for December closure; pegged total transport ITMS/ATMS opportunity size at Rs 10,000–11,000 crore.
5. **Riddhesh Ram Gandhi (Analyst):** Suggested a share buyback given the low stock price and net cash balance instead of external acquisitions. Management stated they would table the suggestion at the next board meeting, noting previous board preference was for capability-building acquisitions.

### One-line takeaway
Ceinsys Tech delivered flat H1 revenues but expanded EBITDA margins to 25.6% via operational efficiencies, while management expects unbilled revenue collections to normalize by Q4.
