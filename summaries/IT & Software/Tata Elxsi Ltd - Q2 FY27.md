# Tata Elxsi Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-19 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Broad-based revenue growth across all verticals and resilient operating margins despite wage hikes, though Europe remains soft.

### Key numbers
- Operating Revenue: Rs 1055.7 crore (grew 2.3% YoY in constant currency)
- EBITDA: Rs 231.4 crore (grew 7.1% QoQ and 19.7% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 21.9% (70 bps QoQ improvement)
- Transportation vertical QoQ growth: 2% in constant currency
- Media and communication vertical QoQ growth: 3.4% in constant currency
- Healthcare and life sciences vertical QoQ growth: 3.5% in constant currency
- Employee Utilization: 78%

### Highlights
- Secured a multi-year deal from a leading automotive OEM for a next-gen cloud-based telematics and connected vehicle ecosystem.
- Strategic collaboration with Sarla Aviation to support development of 'Shunya', India's first indigenous eVTOL aircraft, alongside a win from a global aerospace supplier for test rig development and validation.
- Secured a strategic AI engagement with a global technology leader to develop a GenAI-powered virtual assistant focused on multilingual intelligence.
- Won a deal from a global renal care leader for end-to-end product engineering of next-generation home dialysis systems.
- Implemented annual wage increases across the workforce during the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide formal guidance.
- Management expects H2 to be better than H1, anticipating sustained growth momentum and upward margin trajectories driven by operational leverage and pyramid management.

### Demand & industry commentary
- US and India geographies performed very well; Europe remains soft, particularly due to automotive revenue exposure and delayed decision-making.
- Enterprise AI spending is becoming more thoughtful and ROI-based.
- Healthcare and life sciences segment has bottomed out from previous quarters and shows good deal pipeline visibility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Wage hikes were offset by operational leverage (10-20 bps) and the fading of one-time costs, aided by a 60-70 bps cross-currency benefit.
- Management aims to improve margins over the next 2 to 3 quarters via operational leverage, higher utilization (headroom up to historical peaks of 81%-82%), and pyramid rebalancing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slowness and delayed decision-making in the European automotive market due to systemic OEM issues.
- ER&D talent retention pressures driven by GCC expansions.
- Potential impact of Q3 being a short working-day quarter and potential customer furloughs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bhavik Mehta (JP Morgan) asked about sustainability of growth and margins:** Management highlighted that growth is broad-based (US and India driving auto, recovery in healthcare, and deal ramp-ups in media), while margins will benefit from operational leverage.
- **Rishi Mody (Antique Stock Broking) asked about employee cost and utilization:** Management stated salary hikes are done for the year, utilization stands at 78% (with headroom to reach historical 81%-82% peaks), and pyramid management will be a focus.
- **Sulabh Govila (Millennium Melius) asked about deal leakage and ramp-ups:** Nitin Pai noted that large multi-year deals protect the revenue base and supplier-side leakage in auto has reduced.
- **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities) asked about transportation sub-verticals and Europe drag:** Management stated growth is driven by auto OEMs in the US and India, while Europe remains a wait-and-watch due to delayed deal closures.
- **Sankaranarayanan S asked about group customer concentration (Tata Motors and JLR):** Management noted JLR faces challenges and layoffs, but Tata Elxsi remains engaged in R&D for new programs; a lot of recent automotive growth is coming from outside group customers.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Elxsi delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with broad-based vertical growth and 70 bps sequential margin expansion despite annual wage hikes, supported by strong traction in the US and India.
