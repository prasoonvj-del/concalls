# Tata Elxsi Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-19 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported broad-based sequential growth across all key verticals, EBITDA margin expansion despite annual wage hikes, and expressed confidence in H2 FY27 outperforming H1 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Operating Revenue:** Rs 1,055.7 crore, up 2.3% QoQ in constant currency (CC).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 231.4 crore, up 7.1% QoQ and 19.7% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 21.9%, expanding 70 bps QoQ.
- **Vertical Performance (QoQ CC growth):**
  - Transportation: +2.0%
  - Media and Communication: +3.4%
  - Healthcare & Life Sciences (HLS): +3.5%
- **Employee Utilization:** 78% (up from previous quarter).

### Highlights
- **Transportation:** Secured a strategic multi-year deal from a global automotive OEM for a next-generation cloud-based telematics and connected vehicle ecosystem.
- **Aerospace & Defense:** Partnered with Sarla Aviation to develop "Shunya" (India's first indigenous eVTOL aircraft) and won a validation/test-rig engagement from a global aerospace supplier.
- **Media & Communication:** Won a GenAI strategic engagement with a global technology leader to build a multilingual virtual assistant.
- **Healthcare & Life Sciences:** Secured an end-to-end product engineering deal from a global renal care leader for next-generation home dialysis systems.
- **Regional Performance:** US and India showed strong growth; Japan was flattish; Europe remained soft due to auto concentration.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Company Policy:** Does not provide formal financial guidance.
- **H2 FY27 Revenue:** Management expects H2 FY27 revenue to be higher than H1 FY27, driven by sustained momentum in Media & Communication, Healthcare recovery, and strength in US/India auto.
- **Seasonality:** Q3 is a shorter quarter with fewer working days and potential client furloughs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Automotive:** Growth is driven by OEMs in the US and India, while Tier-1 supplier revenues have stabilized. Decision-making among European OEMs remains delayed due to systemic cost and electrification challenges.
- **AI Spend:** Enterprise spending on GenAI is becoming more pragmatic, selective, and ROI-driven rather than unconstrained experimental spending. Primary enterprise ROI targets remain focused on high-cost functions like supply chain.
- **Healthcare & Aerospace:** Healthcare bottomed out in Q1 FY27 and is seeing pipeline recovery. Aerospace is growing smartly from a small base.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margin Drivers (QoQ):** Cross-currency tailwinds contributed 60–70 bps, and operational leverage/one-off expense reversals contributed 10–20 bps, offsetting annual wage hikes implemented in Q2.
- **Margin Levers:** Management targets a gradual upward margin trajectory over H2 FY27 via operational leverage, higher utilization (headroom up to historical peak of 81–82%), and active pyramid management (moving mature programs from senior experts to junior talent).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company continues calibrating junior hiring and training lower-level talent while managing high demand for specialized ER&D talent against Global Capability Centers (GCCs).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*(Not discussed in the call)*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued uncertainty, softness, and delayed deal closures among European automotive OEMs.
- Operational impact of potential Q3 customer furloughs and lower working days.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bhavik Mehta (JP Morgan):** *Is growth momentum sustainable across H2 FY27?*  
  **Management:** Yes, US and India auto are strong, Media & Comm shows strong deal-ramp momentum, and Healthcare has bottomed out. Only European auto remains wait-and-watch.
- **Rishi Mody:** *Are high senior employee costs diluting deal margins, and how will utilization play out?*  
  **Management:** A sizable portion of senior pay is already variable. Over the next two quarters, pyramid correction and higher utilization (from 78%) will help control employee costs.
- **Sulabh Govila:** *Are existing deal wins sufficient for near-term growth?*  
  **Management:** Growth requires a mix of existing program ramp-ups and fresh deal wins to offset routine project completions/ramp-downs.
- **Ankur Pant:** *How confident is management on customer concentration risks in Healthcare?*  
  **Management:** Diversification across geographies (winning deals in Germany and Japan) and new client additions over the last 5–6 quarters have significantly reduced concentration risk.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Elxsi delivered resilient broad-based revenue growth and margin expansion in Q2 FY27, backed by US/India auto strength and Healthcare recovery, despite ongoing soft demand in European automotive.
