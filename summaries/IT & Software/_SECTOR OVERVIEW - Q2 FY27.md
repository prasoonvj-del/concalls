# IT & Software: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Management commentary across the Indian IT & Software sector reflects a bifurcated operating environment. On one hand, tier-1 and traditional IT services firms are navigating cautious enterprise spending, pricing pressure, extended deal decision cycles, and selective discretionary spending (Infosys, Hexaware, Wipro, Saksoft). On the other hand, specialized software product platforms, data center infrastructure providers, and AI-native digital engineering players are reporting robust, near-record top-line growth and record order books (Black Box, E2E Networks, ESDS, Firstsource, Mphasis, Onward Technologies, L&T Technology Services). 

A dominant, structural trend sweeping the sector is the acceleration from AI experimentation to full-scale enterprise adoption. AI capabilities—ranging from generative AI agents and autonomous coding tools to sovereign AI compute platforms—are rapidly transitioning from auxiliary offerings into core revenue drivers and operational productivity levers (Infosys, HCLTech, LTM, Firstsource, Cyient, Happiest Minds, Capillary Technologies, Fractall, Datamatics, Workmates Core2Cloud).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
* **Enterprise AI & Digital Transformation:** Broad consensus exists that enterprise clients are actively funding AI, cloud modernization, data infrastructure, and cybersecurity. AI adoption is expanding beyond IT departments into core business and operations functions (Mphasis, HCLTech, LTM, Capillary Technologies, Indegene, Persistent Systems).
* **AI-Driven Productivity & Outcome Pricing:** Managements agree that traditional Time & Material (T&M) models face deflationary pressure, driving a sector-wide pivot toward fixed-price, outcome-based, and value-engineering contracts (Happiest Minds, Mphasis, Birlasoft, Inventurus, Tech Mahindra).

### Divergence
* **Geographic Trends:** Managements diverge on regional trajectories. Americas performance remains resilient or accelerating for players like Black Box, Firstsource, Fractal, and Onward Technologies, whereas LatentView, Wipro, and Mastek report client-specific softness or election-related delays in North America. Europe displays deep division: European automotive and manufacturing spend remains soft and structurally constrained (KPIT, LTTS, Tata Elxsi, Tata Technologies, Wipro), while UK/Continental enterprise services and public sector pipelines are expanding for Firstsource, Mastek, and Tata Elxsi.
* **Vertical Headwinds vs. Tailwinds:** 
  * **BFSI:** Shows robust recovery and strong deal momentum for Mphasis, HCLTech, Wipro, Firstsource, and Persistent, but muted or selective spend according to Infosys and Sonata Software.
  * **US Healthcare & Payers:** Facing regulatory policy uncertainties, Medicare Advantage recalibrations, and payer margin pressure (Wipro, Firstsource), whereas provider analytics and health tech remain strong drivers for Sagility and Inventurus Knowledge Solutions.
  * **Telecom & Manufacturing:** High-tech and telecom spend remains constrained by cost-cutting and budget deferrals (Infosys, HCLTech, Ksolves, Wipro), while Tech Mahindra reports robust CC growth (+9.5%) in Telecom via its Zero-Gravity architecture.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

* **Wage Hikes & Talent Costs:** Salary increments implemented during the quarter created a 50 bps to 190 bps margin drag across multiple providers (Birlasoft, HCLTech, Mastek, Tata Elxsi, Tata Technologies, Quick Heal, Subex). However, utilization improvements, offshore delivery shifts, and lower subcon expenses helped absorb these headwinds (Birlasoft, L&T Technology Services, Persistent Systems).
* **AI Tooling & Subcontracting Costs:** Increased utilization of niche third-party skills, AI token costs, and partner platform integrations led to temporary margin dilution for select players (Birlasoft, Persistent, eClerx), whereas companies with internal AI agents (Happiest Minds, Ceinsys, Ksolves, Digispice) report significant cost savings and 2x–3x individual engineer productivity gains.
* **Hardware & OEM Supply Constraints:** Providers engaged in hardware, IoT, data centers, and system integration (Orient Technologies, Black Box, Allied Digital) report severe global OEM lead times (stretching up to 16–18 weeks or 12–18 months) and rising component/memory costs (Sasken, Datamatics).

## Capex & capacity

* **Data Center & GPU Compute Infrastructure:** Massive capex deployments are underway to meet AI infrastructure demand. E2E Networks, ESDS, and Black Box are aggressively expanding high-density GPU capacity (NVIDIA Blackwell/H100/H200 clusters) and sovereign AI factories (ESDS deploying Rs 1,500 crore for its domestic AI factory).
* **Delivery Centers & R&D Labs:** Companies are opening specialized labs and international hubs—LTTS launched an EI lab with NVIDIA; HCLTech invested Rs 185 crore in a Bengaluru semiconductor lab; Onward Technologies opened its 4th Pune design center; and Nanta Tech opened experience centers in Ahmedabad and Mumbai while setting up a Dubai subsidiary.

## What changed since last quarter

* **Guidance Revisions & Tone Shifts:**
  * **Raised Guidance:** Firstsource raised the lower end of FY27 CC revenue guidance to 10.5%–13% (from 10%–13%). Mphasis raised full-year FY27 CC revenue growth guidance to low double digits. Sagility raised FY27 adjusted EBITDA margin guidance to 25%–26%. Workmates Core2Cloud raised FY27 revenue guidance to Rs 202 crore. Datamatics raised FY27 revenue growth expectations to 9%–9.5%.
  * **Lowered / Cut Guidance:** Infosys cut FY27 CC revenue growth guidance to 1%–2% (from 1.5%–3.0%). eMudhra lowered FY27 total income guidance to ~Rs 800 crore (from ~Rs 825 crore) and PAT to ~Rs 125 crore (from ~Rs 137 crore) following a public TLS certificate incident. LatentView pushed out its $200M revenue timeline by 9 months to end-CY28.
  * **Outlook Withdrawn / Softened:** Hexaware withdrew its Q4 CY26 revenue outlook to tighten forecasting rigor. Axiscades backed away from giving explicit annual PAT margin guidance due to non-cash impairments and facility transitions.
* **Tone & Strategy Pivots:**
  * Aurionpro shifted from a mixed tone to strong execution momentum as peak R&D intensity tapered off.
  * Meta Infotech emphasized dropping low-margin product sales to pivot toward high-margin cybersecurity services.
  * Matrimony.com confirmed its commission-based Wedding Services model hit bottom and resumed growth.
  * Axiscades closed Phase 1 of its Akkodis divestment but delayed Phase 2 to February 2027.

## What to watch next quarter

* **Q3 Seasonal Furloughs & Working Days:** Monitoring the magnitude of standard Q3 seasonal headwinds, client furloughs, and working-day reductions across traditional IT services (Infosys, Hexaware, Wipro, Birlasoft, Tata Elxsi).
* **GPU AI Cluster Go-Lives:** Imminent commercial ready-for-service (RFS) status of ESDS’s 8,200-GPU Sharon AI cluster (expected Dec 2026) and E2E Networks’ next-tranche B200 GPU deployments.
* **Government & Enterprise Unbilled Revenue Collections:** Resolution of stuck government funds and Jal Jeevan Mission project unbilled receivables for Ceinsys Tech (~Rs 250 crore GR clearance expected by March 31) and Austere Systems.
* **M&A Integrations & Legal Closures:** Closure of Persistent’s Nagarro squeeze-out offer, Happiest Minds’ merger track with ITC Infotech, Axiscades’ Phase 2 Akkodis divestment (targeted Feb 2027), and Digispice’s Spice Money NCLT merger.
