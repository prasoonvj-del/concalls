# IT & Software: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Across the Indian IT & Software sector in Q2 FY27, management commentary reflects a split operational reality: broad macro uncertainty and delayed enterprise decision cycles for legacy contracts contrast sharply with rapid commercial scaling in AI infrastructure, sovereign clouds, and agentic AI. Tier-1 and mid-tier traditional IT service providers continue to report muted top-line growth driven by client cost-optimization, headcount rationalization, and seasonal furloughs. Conversely, companies providing GPU compute, cloud infrastructure, ad-tech, data engineering, and specialized niche platforms are reporting multi-fold growth.

A common structural theme across the sector is the shift of artificial intelligence from experimental proofs-of-concept (POCs) into revenue-generating production workflows. While this transition accelerates new deal wins, several software and services firms are facing near-term "revenue cannibalization" and pricing pressure as clients demand AI-led productivity pass-throughs.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
### Consensus
* **AI & Sovereign Infrastructure:** Demand for AI compute, data center engineering, sovereign cloud platforms, and GPU clusters is operating at historic highs. Hyperscaler demand and national AI missions are driving accelerated multi-year commitments (e.g., E2E Networks, ESDS Software Solution, L&T Technology Services).
* **Enterprise AI Adoption:** Clients across verticals are transitioning from GenAI experimentation to "Agentic AI" and compute-ready workflows, driving strong demand for platform modernization, data security, and automation.
* **Discretionary Spend Constraints:** Traditional enterprise IT spending remains cautious and outcome-focused. Decision cycles for large-scale digital transformation programs remain extended, favoring short-cycle, high-ROI projects (below $20 million).

### Divergence
* **BFSI & Financial Services:** Strong, broad-based recovery reported by Firstsource Solutions, Mphasis, and Persistent Systems (driven by regulatory compliance, debt servicing, and vendor consolidation). However, Hexaware Technologies, eClerx Services, and Sonata Software noted temporary volatility, client-specific renewals friction, or delayed compliance refresh cycles.
* **Automotive & Manufacturing:** Onward Technologies, Mold-Tek Technologies, and C.E. Info Systems reported record momentum in automotive/engineering services (driven by power distribution, navigation, and software-defined vehicles). Conversely, KPIT Technologies and L&T Technology Services highlighted continued softness and cost-cutting among European passenger car OEMs.
* **Retail & Hi-Tech:** Infosys and Latent View Analytics flagged weakness in Retail and Hi-Tech due to lower consumer demand and client budget cuts. Conversely, Happiest Minds, CarTrade Tech, and Fractal Analytics reported sequential stability or acceleration in CPG, retail, and tech-adjacent sub-segments.
* **Government & E-Governance:** Allied Digital Services noted a stabilization in OEM pricing allowing a return to active bidding. However, Newgen Software and C.E. Info Systems cited deal closure delays, collection friction, or elevated receivable days (over 120 days) in government accounts.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Margin Execution Levers:** Companies defended operating margins primarily through improved utilization (Persistent at 85.8%, Infosys at 85.4%, Sasken at 86%), fixed-price project optimization, offshore delivery mix shifts (LTTS at 55.9%), and AI-driven internal coding tools.
* **Wage Increments & Talent Costs:** Merit hikes phase across Q2-Q4, creating direct margin drag: Birlasoft (-170 bps), Indegene (-150 bps), Persistent (-180 bps net), Hexaware (-100 bps), LTTS (-50 to -80 bps expected in Q3), and Nucleus Software (higher costs for specialized AI talent).
* **Hardware & Component Volatility:** ESDS, Orient Technologies, and Sasken noted global hardware/memory supply constraints (e.g., DDR5/DDR4 pricing and OEM supply lead times stretching to 16–18 weeks). Allied Digital noted OEM product pricing has begun stabilizing.
* **Non-Linear Headcount:** Headcount additions remain decoupled from top-line revenue growth across IT services due to AI-driven output multipliers (2x-3x output per developer reported by Ksolves and Datamatics).

## Capex & capacity
* **AI Compute & GPU Expansion:** Massive capital deployments continue in high-density compute infrastructure. ESDS is deploying a USD 1.25 billion Sharon AI cluster (8,200 GPUs) alongside a Rs 1,500 crore domestic AI factory capex. E2E Networks is rolling out NVIDIA Blackwell and B200 GPU lots.
* **Physical & Lab Infrastructure:** LTTS launched an EI lab with NVIDIA for physical AI native applications. Onward Technologies opened its 4th design center in Pune, while Saksoft, Novus Loyalty, and Nanta Tech expanded international GTM subsidiaries in the US, Europe, Singapore, and Dubai.
* **M&A Capital Allocation:** Cash balances remain substantial. Cyient completed the TAO Digital acquisition ($130 million upfront). Sagility acquired Chirok Health ($20 million upfront). Persistent is raising $1.25 billion (debt/equity) to fund the Nagarro acquisition.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Upgrades:**
  * **Firstsource Solutions:** Raised full-year FY27 constant currency revenue growth guidance to 10.5%–13% (from 10%–13%).
  * **Mphasis:** Upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to low double digits (from high single digits to low double digits).
  * **Workmates Core2Cloud:** Raised FY27 revenue guidance to Rs 202 crore (from Rs 190.56 crore).
  * **Datamatics Global Services:** Upgraded FY27 growth expectation to 9%–9.5% (from high single digits).
  * **Nanta Tech:** Raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to 45%–50%.
  * **Onward Technologies:** Upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to 15%–18%.
  * **Sagility:** Raised FY27 adjusted EBITDA margin guidance to 25%–26% (from 24%–25%).
* **Guidance Cuts & Revisions:**
  * **Infosys:** Cut full-year FY27 constant currency revenue growth guidance to 1%–2% (from 1.5%–3%).
  * **eMudhra:** Lowered FY27 total income guidance to ~Rs 800 crore (from ~Rs 825 crore) and PAT to ~Rs 125 crore (from ~Rs 137 crore) due to exiting its public TLS certificate business.
  * **Latent View Analytics:** Shifted its $200 million revenue target timeline out by 9 months to end of CY28.
  * **Hexaware Technologies:** Withdrew Q4 CY26 revenue outlook to tighten forecasting rigor under new CEO Vivek Jaitley.
* **Tone Shifts & Delivery:**
  * **Aurionpro Solutions:** Shifted from "cautious" to "strong momentum" as heavy R&D intensity peaked and data center execution accelerated.
  * **Birlasoft:** Confirmed exit from unprofitable non-core accounts is complete.
  * **Info Edge:** Transitioned 99acres toward operating cash neutrality (losses dropped 93% YoY). Announced Sanjeev Bikhchandani will succeed Hitesh Oberoi as CEO effective December 3, 2026.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Seasonal Furloughs & Q3 Working Days:** Management teams across Infosys, Birlasoft, Hexaware, and Mphasis expect traditional Q3 seasonal headwinds from lower billable days and customer furloughs.
* **GPU Cluster Ready-For-Service (RFS):** ESDS Software anticipates final NVIDIA cluster testing and RFS for its Sharon AI cluster between December 1 and 17, 2026, unlocking Rs 288 crore monthly revenue run-rate potential.
* **Wage Revision Margin Hits:** Phased salary hikes will hit P&L margins in Q3/Q4 for LTTS (50-80 bps), Hexaware (100 bps), and Cyient.
* **Enterprise AI Deflation vs. Expansion:** Track whether short-cycle AI deployments continue to cannibalize legacy contract volumes (as seen in Saksoft and Tracxn) or successfully expand total addressable market through higher-margin outcome pricing.
