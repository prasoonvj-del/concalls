# IT & Software: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian IT and software sector reflects a generally robust environment driven by secular trends in artificial intelligence (AI), data center infrastructure, and digital transformation. While several firms—such as Aurionpro, Fractal, and Xtranet—hit major scale milestones (with Fractal crossing $100 million in quarterly revenue), others faced pockets of client-specific softness, deal delays, or deliberate short-term margin dilution to fund long-term capacity and sales expansions (e.g., Hexaware, Tracxn). 

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Across almost all participating companies, managements expect **H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1**, supported by healthy order books, recent project go-lives, and seasonal strength. Generative AI, cloud adoption, and sovereign data infrastructures are universally cited as key tailwinds.
* **Divergence:** While firms exposed to hyperscalers, data centers, and advanced AI (like Aurionpro, ESDS, and Xtranet) report "extraordinary" demand cycles, others faced localized headwinds. Birlasoft noted softness in the E&U vertical due to a European client ramp-down; Hexaware cited headwinds in Travel & Transportation and a GSE client; and Tracxn highlighted that global VC deal volumes remain at a 10-year low despite rising tech funding.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Wage Hikes and Talent:** Several companies absorbed annual wage increases during the quarter—such as Birlasoft (impacting margins by 170 bps) and Indegene (150 bps). Fractal noted gross margin compression due to upfront investments in scarce AI engineering talent.
* **Technology & Cloud Costs:** Cloud and data costs remain stable or variable (e.g., Pine Labs reports cloud costs at 8.5–9% of top-line). Companies are increasingly building in-house coding agents or proprietary platforms (Aurionpro, Indegene) to optimize token costs and reduce reliance on third-party models.
* **Margin Resilience:** Despite cost pressures, operational efficiencies and richer product/software mixes allowed most players to defend or expand their EBITDA margins (e.g., Birlasoft maintaining ~15%, C.E. Info Systems guiding for >35%).

## Capex & capacity
* **Data Centers and AI Infrastructure:** A prominent theme is heavy infrastructure spending. Aurionpro, ESDS, and Xtranet are expanding data center capacities. ESDS is deploying its 8,200-GPU Sharon AI cluster and earmarking a Rs 1,500 crore capex for its India AI factory. Pine Labs noted elevated capex due to vendor advance requirements and chip procurements.
* **Sales and Go-To-Market:** Firms like Pine Labs, Tracxn, and Birlasoft are aggressively expanding sales headcounts and operational infrastructure, accepting near-term cost absorption to drive future growth acceleration.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Aurionpro:** Shifted tone from "mixed/cautious" to "strong momentum," noting that R&D intensity has peaked.
* **Birlasoft:** Management confirmed that the exit from low-margin/negative accounts is now "done and dusted."
* **Hexaware:** Withdrew its Q4 CY26 outlook to improve forecasting rigor and accuracy following deal-delay challenges under new leadership (CEO Vivek Jaitley).

## Company one-liners
* **Aurionpro Solutions:** Aurionpro has successfully pivoted to an AI-native product strategy and is scaling its data center business, with management signaling that the heavy R&D phase is over.
* **Birlasoft:** Birlasoft delivered sequential growth and improved operational metrics, successfully absorbing wage hikes while pivoting toward AI-led transformation.
* **C.E. Info Systems:** C.E. Info Systems reported a strong Q2 FY27 with 15.8% revenue growth and significant EBITDA/PAT expansions across Automotive and Enterprise segments.
* **Capillary Technologies:** Capillary delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 51% YoY revenue growth, a doubling of adjusted EBITDA, and successful break-even of Session M.
* **ESDS Software Solution:** ESDS reported strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 24.6% YoY and is poised for a major step-up in scale with its Sharon AI cluster and domestic AI factory expansion.
* **Fractal Analytics:** Fractal surpassed $100 million in quarterly revenue with 20% YoY top-line growth and improved profitability, positioning itself for enterprise AI acceleration.
* **Hexaware Technologies:** New leadership is prioritizing a 90-day strategic review and improved forecasting rigor following a quarter of missed revenue expectations.
* **Indegene:** Indegene delivered a strong Q2 with 39.5% YoY revenue growth, expanding enterprise relationships, and growing traction in GenAI-enabled solutions like Tectonic.
* **Pine Labs:** Pine Labs delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with 24% revenue growth and 27% adjusted EBITDA growth, driven by market share gains and UPI monetization.
* **Praruh Technologies:** Praruh Technologies delivered strong H1 growth and is aggressively pivoting toward high-margin proprietary software and SaaS.
* **Tracxn Technologies:** Tracxn is prioritizing aggressive sales capacity expansion and AI-driven product enhancements, accepting short-term losses amid challenging global deal markets.
* **Xtranet Technologies:** Xtranet delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 42% revenue growth and doubled PAT, driven by a structural shift toward enterprise/BFSI and data centers.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Execution and Go-Lives:** The commercial operationalization and revenue recognition of major infrastructure assets, such as ESDS's Sharon AI cluster deployment and Aurionpro's large data center contracts.
* **Margin Trajectory:** Whether companies absorbing wage hikes or upfront investments (like Fractal's AI talent and Indegene's target of 19%+ EBITDA by Q4) successfully realize operating leverage.
* **Deal Velocity and Seasonality:** How companies navigate Q3 seasonal furloughs (noted by Birlasoft and Hexaware) versus those expecting accelerated H2 execution (Xtranet, Pine Labs).
