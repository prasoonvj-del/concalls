# Firstsource Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - 10th straight quarter of double-digit YoY revenue growth, 8th straight quarter of sequential margin expansion, and raising the lower end of revenue guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 28.3 billion (up 22.2% YoY, 3.7% QoQ; USD 296 million, up 11.7% YoY, 2.6% QoQ; constant currency growth of 12.3% YoY and 2% QoQ).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 55.5 billion / USD 584 million (up 12.3% YoY in constant currency).
- **EBIT Margin:** 12.5% (up 100 bps YoY, 10 bps QoQ).
- **H1 FY27 EBIT:** INR 6.9 billion (up 33.4% YoY; margin 12.4%).
- **Net Profit (PAT):** INR 2.3 billion (up 29.7% YoY on a normalized basis).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 4.5 billion (up 30.4% YoY on an adjusted basis).
- **Diluted EPS:** INR 3.31.
- **Net Debt:** INR 15.3 billion (down from INR 17.1 billion at the end of June 2026).
- **Normalized DSO:** 67 to 69 days.
- **FCF to PAT:** 150% for Q2; 126% for H1.
- **Hedge Book:** Coverage of GBP 69.2 million at an average rate of INR 116.6 per pound; USD 68.5 million at an average rate of INR 95.7 per dollar.
- **Headcount:** 39,937 Firstsourcers (net addition of 3,062 during the quarter).
- **Attrition:** Voluntary attrition for associates with >180 days tenure at 28.4% (trailing 12-month basis).

### Highlights
- Signed four large deals (ACV > USD 5 million), including two from new logos (7th straight quarter of 4+ large deals).
- Added 17 new logos (including six strategic logos).
- Exited the quarter with 51 clients generating > USD 5 million annual run rate (up by 6) and 23 clients in the > USD 10 million cohort (up by 3).
- Over 50% of revenue is non-linear in nature.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance (FY27):** Raised the lower end; now expects constant currency revenue growth of 10.5% to 13% (compared to 10% to 13% earlier).
- **Margin Guidance (FY27):** Reaffirmed EBIT margin guidance of 12.25% to 12.75%.
- Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **BFS Vertical:** Grew 19% YoY and 9% sequentially in CC; added 9 new logos. Driven by consumer debt levels, servicing complexity, and cost pressure. Focus areas include intelligent operations, customer servicing, and financial crime/compliance using the Kairos operating system.
- **Healthcare Vertical:** Grew 5% YoY but declined 2% sequentially in CC; added 4 new logos. Payer growth impacted by Medicare Advantage scope recalibration; provider side seeing tailwinds from HR1 Medicaid renewal changes (moving to six-monthly from January 2027) and rising denials/labor costs.
- **CMT Vertical:** Grew 6% YoY and flat sequentially in CC; added 2 new logos.
- **Utilities & Retail Portfolio:** Grew 25% YoY and flat sequentially in CC; added 2 new logos.
- **Geography:** North America grew 6% YoY and 1% sequentially in CC. Europe grew 27% YoY and 7% sequentially in CC. Australia grew 50% YoY.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins expanded despite absorbing the first phase of annual wage increments and onboarding ~3,000 people ahead of revenue.
- Junior to mid-level wage hikes occurred in Q2; senior-level hikes are scheduled for Q3.
- Effective tax rate for H1 was 23.2%; expected to be 22% to 24% for FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capitalizing costs directly attributable to the Kairos platform development (held under intangible assets under development, including INR 232 million).
- India and Philippines accounted for >85% of the net headcount increase.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stood at INR 4.1 billion.
- Net debt reduced to INR 15.3 billion from INR 17.1 billion in June 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as adverse risks, apart from general macro volatility and client scope recalibrations (e.g., Medicare Advantage).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya Gupta (AG Square Capital) on furloughs:** Management stated that because they run mission-critical continuous operations, they do not anticipate seeing Q3 furloughs typical of IT services.
- **Vibhor Singhal (Analyst) on healthcare bottoming out:** Management noted that payer Medicare headwinds are largely behind them, and structural shifts (HR1 Medicaid changes, provider RCM automation) position healthcare for recovery.
- **Moksh Mehta (Ambit Capital) on margin levers to reach mid-teens:** Management stated that reaching the 14% to 15% band over 2-3 years will be driven by right-shoring (80% gross hiring offshore/nearshore), automation, process excellence, account-level margin improvements, and facility optimization.
- **Ankur (Analyst) on guidance mathematics:** Management clarified that raising the lower end reflects increased line-of-sight and visibility after H1, while the upper end remains in play dependent on phased transformation program milestones.
- **Shraddha (Analyst) on AI-led deal deflation:** Management noted that while renewals may involve client productivity expectations, they offset potential deflation by taking on adjacent work to keep revenue neutral or accretive.
- **Girish Pai (Analyst) on competition from AI start-ups:** Management stated they coexist and partner with AI-native start-ups, leveraging the start-up's tech speed with Firstsource's domain depth and enterprise scale.

### One-line takeaway
Firstsource delivered its 10th consecutive quarter of double-digit YoY revenue growth and 8th straight quarter of margin expansion, prompting management to raise the lower end of its FY27 constant currency revenue growth guidance to 10.5%-13%.
