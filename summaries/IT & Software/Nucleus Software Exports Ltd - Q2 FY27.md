# Nucleus Software Exports Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth and healthy order book were offset by margin pressure due to higher employee and AI talent costs.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 227.7 crore (8.2% QoQ, 6.7% YoY)
- Dollar revenue: USD 24 million (6.9% QoQ, -1.9% YoY)
- H1 FY27 consolidated revenue: Rs 438.1 crore (1.6% YoY); USD 46.5 million (-6.9% YoY)
- Product revenue share (Q2): 84.4% (vs 83.9% QoQ); H1 product revenue: 84.2%
- EBITDA (Q2): Rs 15.4 crore (vs Rs 7.6 crore QoQ, Rs 23.1 crore YoY)
- EBITDA margin (Q2): 6.8% (vs 3.6% QoQ, 10.8% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 23 crore (vs Rs 56.8 crore YoY); EBITDA margin: 5.3% (vs 13.2% YoY)
- Other income (Q2): Rs 16 crore (-10% QoQ, -0.8% YoY)
- PAT (Q2): Rs 21.3 crore (-11% QoQ, -19.2% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 45.1 crore (-26.6% YoY)
- Order book (as of Sep 30, 2026): Rs 1,317 crore (90% product, 10% project) vs Rs 1,244 crore as of June 30, 2026
- Cash and cash equivalents: Rs 942.1 crore as of Sep 30, 2026 (vs Rs 977.9 crore on June 30, 2026)
- Receivables: Rs 220.4 crore (vs Rs 174.7 crore on June 30, 2026)
- DSO: 80 days (vs 63 days on June 30, 2026)
- Headcount: 2,066 as of Sep 30, 2026 (vs 2,059 on June 30, 2026)
- Forward covers: USD 2.5 million at an average rate of Rs 96.87 per USD

### Highlights
- Launched FinnOne Neo 9.0 and FinnAxia 9.0 in Vietnam with partner FPT.
- Recognized in Gartner's 2026 market overview for commercial banking cash management and Forrester's Cash Management Solution Landscape.
- Generative AI-powered Visual Report agent received the Most Innovative Practice Award at the CII National AI Awards 2026; recognized as Oracle's 2026 APAC ISV Partner of the year.
- Added 5 new logos in the last six months (3 in Q1, 2 in Q2).
- Appointed Sudhanshu Tripathi as Chief Marketing Officer.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they do not provide specific revenue earnings guidance.
- Management expects margin recovery in coming quarters as order book execution scales and AI investments stabilize.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Implementation cycles range from 3 to 18 months, occasionally spilling over to 2 years due to high-volume data migration and complexity.
- Geographic revenue breakdown (Q2): India 57.3%, Middle East 12.4%, Southeast Asia 11%, Europe 5.7%.
- Top 5 clients contributed 27% of revenue in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Delivery cost (including product development) in Q2 was Rs 167.9 crore (73.8% of revenue).
- Marketing and sales expenses were 11% of revenue (vs 10% QoQ, 7.3% YoY).
- Employee costs increased due to hiring AI talent at higher market rates, labor code change impacts, and the launch of RSU policies.
- Other income was impacted by lower MTM gains on net mutual funds due to rising interest rates in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Temporary increase in receivables and DSO (80 days) attributed to AMC billed late in the month and collected subsequently.
- Advanced billing/deferred revenue increased due to advance AMC billings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher competition in global geographies impacting pricing dynamics.
- Market heating up for specialized AI talent, driving up employee costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Samar Singh (TPF Capital)** asked about order book methodology and AMC inclusion. Management clarified that the methodology of including up to multi-year AMC contracts in the order book has not changed.
- **Bharat Sheth (Quest Investment)** asked about AI impact on implementation timelines and margin recovery. Management stated that end-to-end autonomous migration is not yet reached due to human-in-the-loop requirements, and margins will reflect order book execution over time.
- **Prolin N (Individual Investor)** asked about breaking the Rs 200–230 crore quarterly revenue run rate. Management noted Q2 is one of the highest revenues in recent quarters and growth will reflect as parallel implementations convert.
- **Hardik Bora (Viraiya Capital)** inquired about overseas order proportions and pricing stress. Management stated overseas wins follow historical revenue proportions and pricing remains range-bound despite competition.
- **Sanjay K (SKS Capital)** questioned rising employee costs versus peers. Management explained that unlike AI-as-a-service companies, Nucleus embeds AI into software, and costs reflect investments in AI talent, labor code changes, and RSUs.

### One-line takeaway
Nucleus Software posted steady Q2 revenue of Rs 227.7 crore and a robust order book of Rs 1,317 crore, though margins remained subdued due to ongoing investments in AI talent and product transformation.
