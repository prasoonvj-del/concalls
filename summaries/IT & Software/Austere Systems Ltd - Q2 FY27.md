# Austere Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew modestly and core operations were steady, but net profit declined due to a legal provision and higher depreciation.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 11.29 crore, up 9.12% YoY (vs Rs 10.34 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Total Income:** Rs 11.97 crore, up 13% YoY (vs Rs 10.59 crore in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 2.99 crore; EBITDA Margin at 25.01%.
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 1.77 crore; Net Profit Margin at 14.75%.
- **Other Income:** Rs 68.27 lakh (up from Rs 24.91 lakh in H1 FY26).

### Highlights
- Secured work orders worth Rs 1.87 crore from HDFC Bank for MIS projects and received 2 new work orders from HARTRON (Haryana).
- Empaneled with Indian Port Rail and Ropeway Corporation Limited (IPRCL) for digitalization/AI/security consulting and Haryana Knowledge Corporation Limited (HKCL) as a software development organization.
- Completed a ServiceNow project for Genpact.
- Strategic expansions: Incorporated 51%-owned subsidiary Austere Next Private Limited for enterprise solutions (SAP, ServiceNow, Oracle), established wholly owned subsidiary Austere Systems LLC in Delaware (USA), and partnered in Neural Nexis AI LLP.
- Client retention stands at 80%–90%, with revenue split roughly 40:60 between public and corporate sectors.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide explicit forward-looking revenue guidance or publish a consolidated order book.
- Priorities include expanding the Dial 11-UrbanSphere platform (adding GIS and AI capabilities) across municipal bodies, scaling enterprise AI/cloud offerings, and driving expansion in North America and Europe.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Urban digital governance is the primary growth driver; management identified a quantum opportunity across India's ~660 municipal corporations for its tested UrbanSphere platform.
- Demand remains strong for enterprise solutions across healthcare and education sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Created a prudent provision of Rs 44 lakh against an ongoing legal appeal, which reduced EBITDA and PAT margins.
- Depreciation expense increased due to full 6-month charges on fixed assets added late in the prior fiscal year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Currently setting up an AI lab at the Gurugram facility.
- Expanding overseas sales presence through the newly incorporated US entity (Delaware).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company is debt-free and cash-rich, funding operations and expansion via internal accruals and IPO proceeds rather than debt.
- Operating cash flows remained positive in H1 FY27 (Rs 3.51 crore generated in FY26).
- Trade receivables grew 6% YoY, lagging the 9% revenue growth due to improved collection discipline.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential administrative and payment delays in government projects due to bureaucracy shifts or officer transfers.
- Impact of the open-ended legal dispute and associated Rs 44 lakh provision.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** What caused the decline in net profit despite 9% revenue growth?  
  **A:** Management explained that a prudent Rs 44 lakh provision for a pending legal appeal, along with a full half-year depreciation charge on assets added late last year, impacted profit margins.
- **Q:** How do government payment cycles operate?  
  **A:** Government billing is milestone-based; while deliveries match milestones, administrative delays occur occasionally due to bureaucratic transfers.
- **Q:** What drove the increase in other income to Rs 68.27 lakh?  
  **A:** It was primarily interest earned on unutilized IPO proceeds placed in short-term bank deposits, which will normalize as funds are deployed.
- **Q:** What is the revenue split between products and services?  
  **A:** Management clarified that 80%–90% of revenue comes from IT services/SaaS delivery, as in-house software platforms are sold under a services/SaaS model.

### One-line takeaway
Austere Systems delivered steady 9% revenue growth and maintained debt-free operations while expanding into AI and US enterprise markets, though profitability was temporarily pinched by a legal provision.
