# Sasken Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Meaningful profitability improvement, healthy order momentum, and strong execution across software services.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs. 321 crore (up 26% YoY, down 5.4% QoQ)
- H1 consolidated revenue: Rs. 660 crore (up 24.8% YoY)
- Software services revenue: Rs. 252 crore (up 34% YoY, up 14.6% QoQ)
- Product solutions revenue: Rs. 69 crore (up 2.4% YoY, down 42% QoQ)
- Gross margin: 26.8% or Rs. 86 crore (up 64.7% YoY, up ~490 bps QoQ)
- Software services gross margin: 32.2% (up 160 bps QoQ)
- Product solution gross margin: 7% (up from 5.9% in Q1)
- EBITDA: Rs. 41 crore
- EBITDA margin: 12.9% (up 330 bps QoQ, up 700 bps YoY)
- EBIT: Rs. 29 crore
- EBIT margin: 8.9% (up 250 bps QoQ, up 660 bps YoY)
- Consolidated PAT: Rs. 26 crore (up 8.8% QoQ, up 143.8% YoY)
- H1 EBITDA margin: 11.2%
- H1 EBIT margin: 7.6%
- H1 PAT margin: 7.4%
- Total Contract Value (TCV) signed: $47 million (up 66% YoY), comprising $25 million new bookings and the rest renewals
- Annual Contract Value (ACV): ~$34 million
- Active customer base: 101 (4 new logos added in Q2)
- Top 5 customer contribution: 49% (down from 56% in Q1)
- Top 10 customer contribution: 63%
- Headcount growth: 7% QoQ
- LTM attrition: 9.3%
- Utilization: 86%
- Cash and investments: ~Rs. 399 crore
- Services order booking: $33 million

### Highlights
- Implemented annual salary revisions across levels in July.
- Launched Juniper Alta, a new device developed end-to-end and brought to shipment within 18 months.
- Notable wins: multiyear telematics engineering program with a leading European automotive group, cockpit and ADAS work for a European automotive OEM, engineering across applications, HMI, middleware, and testing for an Indian EV company, and an integrated asset tracking solution.
- CSAT stands at 4.5 out of 5.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to moderate software services growth to above industry average (single-digit growth on a sustained basis) for the foreseeable future.
- Management expects a healthy next two quarters for product solutions, having secured 90% to 95% of memory required for FY2027.
- Management targets ~10% to 12% EBITDA margins in product solutions when that business scales to approximately $12 million to $14 million per quarter (from $7 million to $8 million currently).
- Management remains on task for the $240 million revenue goal (60x4x3 plan), potentially delayed by ~12 months, leveraging organic and inorganic means.
- About 90% to 95% of H2 revenues are tied up.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Technology spending is increasingly directed at engineering-led programs connecting silicon, software, and connected devices.
- Customers are consolidating vendors and seeking partners for larger integrated scopes.
- Automotive sector shows ongoing opportunities in software-defined vehicles, connected mobility, digital cockpits, and EV architectures.
- Artificial intelligence is altering products and delivery models; complex work in chip design, firmware, and safety-critical embedded systems still depends on domain knowledge and validation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin improvement driven by favorable business mix, improved utilization, operational efficiencies, and ~0.5% exchange rate gains.
- Product solutions volume impacted by memory availability and component pricing pressures; mitigation includes direct supplier engagement, customer assistance, open market procurement, and shifting from DDR5 to DDR4 or lower memory configurations.
- Inventory increased due to memory and components backed by firm customer orders with zero risk.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Mention of capacity utilization improving to 86% and headcount growing by 7%.
- Manufacturing of product solutions utilizes third-party EMS providers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with cash and investments of ~Rs. 399 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Timing of customer decisions and program ramp-ups can vary.
- Volatility in product solutions quarter-to-quarter due to program milestones, shipment schedules, product life cycles, and memory/component availability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dev Gulwani (Care PMS)** asked about memory shortage impact on product solutions; management (Hareesh Ramanna) explained they secured 90-95% of memory for FY27 via multi-channel sourcing and config adjustments (DDR5 to DDR4).
- **Kaustav Bubna (Kamana Holdings)** asked about sustainable margins and business mix; management stated software services show continuous improvement, while product solutions require annual evaluation and need $12-14M/quarter scale for 10-12% EBITDA margins.
- **Nikhil Chowdhary (Toro AIF)** asked about product margin drivers despite volume dips; management clarified it was driven by silicon segment execution recovery and operational efficiency, with ~0.5% currency impact.
- **Krupa Desai (Electrum Capital)** asked about manufacturing and IP royalties; management confirmed they use third-party EMS for manufacturing and that IP/royalty earnings are a multiyear endeavor expected in coming years.
- **Divyam Doshi (9two3 Capital)** asked about revenue recognition for recent wins; management stated ramp-up was achieved by October 1, 2026, with revenue recognizing starting Q2/Q3.
- **Ritesh Choudhary (Molecule Ventures)** asked about automotive order book vertical split; management declined to share vertical-level splits due to potential quarterly fluctuations at their current size.

### One-line takeaway
Sasken delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 26% YoY revenue growth and a 330 bps QoQ expansion in EBITDA margin to 12.9%, driven by robust software services demand, disciplined execution, and improved utilization.
