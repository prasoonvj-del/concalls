# eMudhra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong core enterprise and international growth, offset by a certificate security incident in the TLS business leading to downwardly revised guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** INR 209 crore (~INR 2,090 million), up ~20% YoY (19.6% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 51.3 crore (~INR 513 million), up 21.6% YoY; margin at 24.5%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27):** INR 33.4 crore (~INR 334 million), up 26.3% YoY; margin at 16%
- **Total Income (H1 FY27):** INR 402 crore (~INR 4,018 million), up 23.4% YoY
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 65.4 crore (~INR 654 million), up 27% YoY; margin at 16.3%
- **Guidance revision:** FY27 total income reduced from ~INR 825 crore to ~INR 800 crore; PAT reduced from ~INR 137 crore to ~INR 125 crore (~25% PAT growth originally, now lower).
- **TLS business revenue share / impact:** TLS was ~1% of revenue (~INR 4 crore); incident estimated to impact FY27 total income by INR 20-23 crore and PAT by INR 10-12 crore.
- **Incremental planned CapEx (FY27):** ~INR 23 crore.
- **CRYPTAS Q2 run-rate revenue:** ~INR 30 crore; expected to make >$1 million EBITDA up to December.

### Highlights
- Enterprise solutions built on the 5-product platform grew 36% YoY and make up two-thirds of revenue.
- International markets contribute two-thirds of revenue.
- New market wins: National PKI for Nigeria's certifying authority regulator; first CAS win in Kazakhstan with a defense entity; added clients in Indonesia, The Philippines, Germany, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi.
- Certificate security incident on October 17: Independent researcher reported weak keys in 8 intermediate certificates in a public TLS hierarchy created in April 2025; all 8 revoked the next day with no end-customer certificates issued and root keys unaffected. Browser programs distrusted the related root.
- Management decided to exit the sale of publicly trusted TLS certificates to focus fully on the core platform.
- US customer University Federation continues working with eMudhra for CertiNext on a subcontract model for TLS.
- *Record Check:* Continues the pattern of solid top-line and PAT growth from Q2 FY27 results, but breaks previous full-year guidance due to the unexpected TLS security incident.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reduced FY27 total income guidance to ~INR 800 crore (from ~INR 825 crore) and PAT to ~INR 125 crore (from ~INR 137 crore).
- Management expects the impact of the TLS incident to last only the next two quarters, returning to business as usual thereafter.
- EBITDA margins expected to be maintained around 25%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Customers facing fixed deadlines on post-quantum migration, machine/AI agent identity, and data privacy (board-level topics in banking/regulated industries).
- Platforms already support hybrid post-quantum certificate authorities.
- Trust services (conventional digital signatures) projected to grow flat to 8-10%, while eSign/remote sign volumes are scaling rapidly (7-8 lakh eSigns per day).
- DPDP compliance driving conversations with B2C banks, NBFCs, and private sector entities; deal sizes range from INR 40-50 lakhs to INR 1-2 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Receivables grew in line with business, days remain around same levels.
- Creditors paid without delay resulting in a decline in creditors, weighing on operating cash flow in H1.
- Gross margins for Q2 FY27 stood at 55.8% (H1 at 56.5%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Incremental planned CapEx for the year is ~INR 23 crore, directed towards completing the data privacy stack, adding AI capabilities to emSigner, and ID verification for digital signature operations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow weighed down by paying creditors without delay despite revenue growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Reputation risk and customer inquiries following the TLS certificate security incident.
- Walked away from a couple of US deals directly tied to TLS.
- Declining structural viability of public TLS due to Google issuing 90-day free DV certificates and browsers phasing out EV importance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Surbhi Soni (Bellwether Capital):** Asked about US private PKI and DPDP pipeline. Management stated US private PKI pipeline is healthy and DPDP deals are materializing with NBFCs, expecting large traction next year.
- **Sagarika Chetty (Antique Stockbroking):** Inquired about Trust services sluggishness and CRYPTAS performance. Management clarified Trust token disruption was due to FIPS level 3 compliance delays (resolved in Sept), and CRYPTAS quarterly run-rate was ~INR 30 crore with >$1M EBITDA up to December.
- **Pankaj Sachdeva (Avis Capital):** Asked if TLS incidents are normal. Management clarified that the TLS incident is not a normal occurrence and is being addressed via an external review by KPMG.
- **Pranay Jain (Banyan Tree Advisors):** Asked if CertiNext bundling was affected by TLS exit. Management answered that CertiNext is a neutral platform and can integrate third-party SSL certificates seamlessly; thus, the TLS exit does not impact CertiNext sales.
- **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities):** Asked about client pushback or contract delays. Management stated there is zero termination of existing contracts and existing clients are unaffected outside of the single US university (which is continuing via subcontracting).

### One-line takeaway
eMudhra posted strong 20% revenue and 26% PAT growth in Q2 FY27, but lowered its FY27 guidance by INR 25 crore in revenue and INR 10-12 crore in PAT after exiting its 1%-revenue public TLS business following a certificate security incident.
