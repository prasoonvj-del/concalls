# Tracxn Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth remains modest (1.1% YoY) despite management's focus on operational scaling and new product launches.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 21.3 crore (up 1.1% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Negative Rs 5.9 crore.
*   **PAT:** Negative Rs 4.1 crore.
*   **Deferred revenue:** Rs 38.1 crore (up 9% YoY).
*   **Customer accounts:** 2,419 (up 13% YoY).
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Negative Rs 6.1 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Strategy:** Management is utilizing a "three-phase playbook" for business units: (1) acquisition, (2) product gap filling, and (3) improved conversion rates.
*   **Headcount Expansion:** Sales and marketing headcount increased to 30% of total staff (from 25%). The company completed a planned expansion to double closing-sales capacity by December.
*   **Product Launches:** Introduced proprietary taxonomy for sector benchmarking, "Talent Signals" (tracking founders/alumni), and AI-driven "Agentic Workflows" (MCP tools, AI Assistant) to embed Tracxn into client workflows.
*   **Geographic Split:** 48% of revenue is derived from outside India.
*   **Customer Base:** 49% private-market equity (VCs, banks, family offices), 45% corporates (M&A, innovation, strategy), and remainder in educational/government sectors.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects margins to improve as revenue accelerates, targeting a conversion of 80% to EBITDA at scale.
*   Management anticipates returning to low single-digit profitability levels once current one-time investments in headcount and expansion are complete.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Global tech funding is at a decade high, but deal volume remains at a 10-year low.
*   Management noted a rebound in global late-stage funding and M&A activity.
*   The company is seeing increased demand for "stealth" company data and founder-signal tracking from investment banks.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   86% of expenses are employee-related (in-house, no outsourced contract workforce).
*   Second-largest expense is cloud hosting, followed by rent.
*   Paid-marketing expense is minimal as the company focuses on organic traffic generation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   The company completed its planned sales capacity expansion in September.
*   No specific capex figures provided, but management noted investments in headcount and infrastructure are currently impacting short-term profitability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Cash and cash equivalents remain at a "healthy level."
*   Deferred revenue grew 9% YoY to Rs 38.1 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Revenue Mix:** The shift in customer mix toward newer accounts has resulted in slightly lower Average Selling Prices (ASP) in some geographies.
*   **Market Environment:** Global deal activity remains at a 10-year low, impacting the broader investment banking and VC landscape.
*   **Profitability:** The company is currently loss-making (negative EBITDA and PAT) due to deliberate investments in sales and product expansion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Why are revenues flat despite increasing customer accounts? **Management:** Attributed this to the global slowdown in deal activity and a change in customer mix, where newer accounts are acquired at slightly lower price points.
*   **Analyst:** How do you differentiate services for Investment Banks (IB) vs. VCs? **Management:** VCs prioritize sector-specific data (e.g., defense-tech, D2C) and early-stage signals, whereas IBs prioritize revenue size and "Live Deals" for fundraising/M&A pipelines.
*   **Analyst:** Are you targeting Fortune 500 enterprises aggressively? **Management:** Yes, it is a priority area, and they are seeing positive response signs from these departments.
*   **Analyst:** How do you handle login sharing? **Management:** They use IP tracking to detect sharing, which then acts as a trigger for the sales team to upsell "full-team packs."

### One-line takeaway
Tracxn is prioritizing aggressive sales capacity expansion and AI-driven product enhancements to drive future growth, accepting short-term losses while navigating a challenging global deal-making environment.
