# Hinduja Global Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Sequential improvement in revenue, high single-digit comparable growth in continuing business, and a strong 339 bps QoQ expansion in total EBITDA margin.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (no specific numerical revenue or margin guidance numbers given, but management reiterated the strategic focus on ramping newer client engagements, deepening existing accounts, improving margins, and building an AI-native workforce).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from "cautiously optimistic" with margin pressure absorption to encouraging, noting that the underlying business is showing healthy momentum and pipeline opportunities are shifting to larger, more strategic, multi-tower technology-led transformation programs.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Middle East expansion in Dubai (HGS MENA IT Consulting LLC) was mentioned as actively winning its first digital operations client; UK G-Cloud 15 and Canada Canoe public sector procurement frameworks highlighted. Old risks around legacy contract phase-outs continued to flow through but non-recurring transition expenses are expected to normalize in H2.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Project Ganga scaled further with 1,221 applicants trained, 40 loans approved, and 16 networks live with 200 paying customers.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 1,076.2 crores (up 2.5% QoQ, down 1.4% YoY)
- Total income: Rs. 1,214.5 crores (up 1.1% QoQ)
- Total EBITDA (including other income): Rs. 158.8 crores (up 36.5% QoQ, up 0.5% YoY)
- Total EBITDA margin: 13.1% (up 339 bps QoQ from 9.7% in Q1 FY27, and compared to 12.9% in Q2 FY26)
- Operating expenses: Rs. 1,055.7 crores (down from Rs. 1,084.9 crores in Q1)
- Other income: Rs. 138.3 crores
- Depreciation: Rs. 117.5 crores
- Interest expenses: Rs. 50.9 crores
- Loss before tax: Rs. 9.6 crores (narrowed from Rs. 52.8 crores in Q1)
- Net loss (PAT): Rs. 12.4 crores (narrowed from Rs. 66.3 crores in Q1, and Rs. 27 crores in Q2 FY26)
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs. 2,126.5 crores (down 1% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Total EBITDA: Rs. 275.1 crores (down 13.4% YoY); EBITDA margin: 11.4%
- H1 FY27 Cash from operating activities: Rs. 108.4 crores
- Cash and bank balances: Rs. 528.1 crores as of September 30, 2026
- Total borrowings: Rs. 1,406 crores as of September 30, 2026
- DSO: 68 days (compared to 61 days in Q2 FY26)
- Client concentration: Top customer 4.6%, top 5 17.1%, top 10 26.1%

### Highlights
- Continuing business delivered high single-digit growth on a comparable basis with newer engagements ramping up.
- Announced a multi-year, multi-million-dollar engagement with Rackspace Technology covering site reliability engineering, cybersecurity, and product development for sovereign AI infrastructure.
- Established Middle East presence in Dubai (UAE) and won the first digital operations client.
- Gained access to public sector frameworks including UK's G-Cloud 15 and Canada's Canoe Procurement Group (for ERP implementation and embedded consulting).
- Project Ganga (Uttar Pradesh) progressed: 1,221 applicants trained out of over 4,000 applicants, 355 loan applications filed under CM YUVA, 40 loans approved, 16 digital service providers live, and 200 paying customers acquired.
- Broadband revenue up 8% YoY in H1 with subscriber base up 6%; high-speed packs (>100 Mbps) rose to 13.5% of base, while entry-level pack dropped to 18.8%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects continuing sequential recovery and improvement in profitability as newer client engagements ramp up and the business mix evolves.
- Management expects non-recurring client transition and runoff expenses not to recur at current levels in H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprise demand is shifting from single-service CX contracts toward multi-tower, technology-led transformation programs and outcome-based mandates.
- AI strategy focuses on "automate the transaction and humanize the interaction," combining agentic AI with domain knowledge and human-in-the-loop controls.
- Digital television (DTV) globally and locally faces structural decline and subscriber churn, countered by broadband cross-selling and cost optimization.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expanded 339 bps QoQ to 13.1%, driven by delivery efficiency, lower operating expenses (down by ~Rs. 29 crores QoQ), and a shifting revenue mix.
- Q2 included some non-recurring expenses owing to client transitions and runoffs which are expected to abate in H2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Prudent capex management: depreciation is outpacing gross fixed asset additions. H1 capex stood at Rs. 81.9 crores.
- Expanding delivery presence across India, Philippines, US, UK, Canada, and the new Middle East entity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total assets stand at Rs. 11,582.5 crores; total equity is Rs. 8,447.3 crores (~73% of total assets).
- Trade receivables reduced to Rs. 800.1 crores as of September 30, 2026, from Rs. 871.8 crores in March 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Profitability pressures in the traditional digital television (DTV) media business due to global structural declines.
- Initial margin headwinds from organic talent investments and transition costs of new technology-heavy business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jimit Mehta asked if the overall business mix will become more favorable for earnings quality and profitability as HGS increases focus on technology-led engagements.** Management answered affirmatively, stating that while it takes time as they heavily invest in organic skills, they expect the mix change to improve profitability over upcoming quarters and the next financial year.
2. **Manan Dalal asked about the key business areas contributing to higher single-digit comparable growth despite portfolio transitions.** Management answered that new logos added previously (79 new logos) are starting to ramp up from smaller initial footprints into expanded growth as customer trust builds.
3. **Mandira A. asked about the significance of the 3-year strategic collaboration with Rackspace Technology.** Management answered that it enables HGS to provide site reliability engineering and managed services for dedicated physical servers running sovereign AI models, avoiding public cloud data privacy concerns and driving recurring long-term revenue.
4. **Jignesh Patel asked about key priorities for the next phase of Project Ganga implementation.** Management answered that all four phases (application, selection, training via LMS/physical center, and CM YUVA loan processing) are complete, and it is now an assembly line process targeting 50 to 100 digital service providers per month to reach 2 million households over 2 to 3 years.
5. **Arihant (minority shareholder) questioned capital allocation decisions since the 2021 healthcare sale, the cash surplus deployment, and minority shareholder value.** Management answered that cash and internal accruals are being used for organic capability building, data/analytics acquisitions (like TekLink), and cost optimization, while maintaining dividend payouts, with the ultimate goal of restoring pre-divestiture growth and EBITDA margins.

### One-line takeaway
HGS delivered a strong sequential recovery in Q2 FY27 with a 339 bps EBITDA margin expansion and high single-digit comparable growth, driven by tech-led deal wins, cost discipline, and broadband expansion.
