# Saksoft Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue remained flat sequentially, and the company is navigating short-term revenue cannibalization from AI-led efficiency gains while investing in leadership for future growth.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 249 crore (broadly flat QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 44 crore (down 4% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 17.6% (contracted 66 bps QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 32 crore (up 10% QoQ).
*   **PAT Margin:** 12.92% (expanded 114 bps QoQ).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 497 crore (down 2% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 89 crore (down 8% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 17.93% (down 107 bps YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 61 crore (down 111 bps YoY).
*   **Net Cash:** Rs 244 crore (as of 30th September 2026).
*   **Employee Utilization:** 84%.

### Highlights
*   **Leadership:** Appointed Gurmeet Chahal as CEO to strengthen U.S. and Europe markets.
*   **Revenue Mix:** U.S. (51%), Europe (28%), APAC/Others (21%).
*   **Vertical Mix:** Emerging verticals (45%), Banking/Financial Services (30%), Logistics (16%), Digital Commerce (9%).
*   **Client Metrics:** 16 clients generate >USD 1 million annually; Top 5, 10, and 20 clients contribute 40%, 58%, and 72% of revenue, respectively.
*   **Delivery:** On-site (43%) and offshore (57%) mix.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 performance to be stronger than H1.
*   Targeting ~Rs 1,100 crore revenue for FY27 if no inorganic options materialize.
*   Long-term goal remains USD 500 million revenue by 2030.
*   Management expects EBITDA margins to return to the 19-20% range over the next 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand environment remains selective with cautious client spending.
*   Clients are shifting from AI experimentation to enterprise-level implementation.
*   Management reports "revenue cannibalization" in the short term as AI-led productivity reduces team sizes for clients.
*   Some clients are in-sourcing work to captives, impacting revenue.
*   Strongest demand traction seen in engineering and managed operations (infrastructure/application support).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Sales cost as % of total is ~3%; management plans to increase this in a phased manner.
*   Short-term margin pressure expected due to investments in leadership and sales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   No specific capex guidance provided.
*   Focus on "non-linear" growth via M&A and partnership pulls (e.g., ServiceNow, Salesforce).
*   No plans for new geographic expansion; focusing on U.S., U.K., and Europe.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net cash position of Rs 244 crore as of 30th September 2026 (increased by Rs 95 crore YoY).
*   Strategy is to fund acquisitions via internal cash and structured payouts; management stated they will not dilute capital or take excessive debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Impact of AI maturity and client willingness to adopt AI.
*   Short-term revenue cannibalization from AI efficiency gains.
*   Slow conversion of pipeline in the U.S. market.
*   Geopolitical uncertainty impacting decision-making.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Kanishk Gupta (SS Family Office):* Asked about the USD 500m target and organic vs. inorganic mix. Management admitted it is a "mammoth target" but relies on non-linear growth (M&A/partnerships).
*   *Vikas S (RBC):* Asked about "push strategy" and capital. Management clarified "partnership pull" doesn't require capital, unlike M&A.
*   *Rushabh (RBSA):* Asked about risks. Management noted the industry-wide challenge of AI adoption and the need to transform proactively.
*   *Girish (Ksema Wealth):* Asked about AI deflation/rate cuts. Management clarified they face "revenue cuts" due to efficiency gains (reduced team size), not rate cuts.
*   *Anupam Bhide (Bhide Family Office):* Challenged the definition of "AI projects." Management argued 100% of their work is AI-enabled (engineering/operations).

### One-line takeaway
Saksoft is navigating a transition period characterized by short-term revenue cannibalization from AI-led efficiency gains while investing in leadership to drive future growth and long-term margin recovery.
