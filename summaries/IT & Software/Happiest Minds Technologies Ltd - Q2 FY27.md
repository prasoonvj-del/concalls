# Happiest Minds Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered healthy revenue growth, improved operating margins, and strong adoption of AI-led initiatives.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 645 crores (growing 12.5% YoY in rupee terms, 2.7% QoQ; 6.8% YoY in constant currency)
- Operating Profit (EBIT): INR 119 crores (growing 22.4% YoY, 9.6% QoQ)
- Operating Margin: 18.5% (up 100 bps QoQ from 17.5%)
- Adjusted PAT: INR 79.1 crores
- Adjusted EPS: INR 5.25
- H1 FY27 Revenue: INR 1,274 crores (growing 13.4% YoY in rupee terms)
- H1 FY27 Operating Profit: INR 228 crores
- H1 FY27 Adjusted PAT: INR 159 crores
- Active Customers: 311
- Billion-dollar corporations served: 95 (up from 92 in Q1)
- Repeat business: 95.1%
- IP-led revenues: 10.5% (up from 9.3% in Q1)
- Total headcount: 6,466 Happiest Minds
- Utilization: 81.8% (up from 81% in Q1)
- Voluntary attrition: 14.3% (down from 15.4% in Q1)
- DSO: 101 days (compared to 92 in Q1)
- Cash and cash equivalents: INR 1,716 crores
- Interim dividend: INR 3.05 per equity share (face value INR 2)

### Highlights
- Proposed combination with ITC Infotech announced on August 31; targeting US $1 billion in annual revenue by FY28 and completion by October-November next year.
- Launched "Project Reimagine" to transition from effort-based delivery to customer value engineering using AI.
- Secured a multimillion-dollar BOTAT (Build, Operate, Transform, Agentify and Transfer) engagement with a global digital asset management company.
- Generative AI Business Services unit grew approximately 135% YoY in constant currency terms in H1 FY27.
- 55% of projects delivered with AI-enabled delivery; 72% of developers and testers using advanced AI tools.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains encouraged by the structural growth opportunity across AI, digital engineering, modernization, cloud, and cybersecurity despite a mixed macro environment.
- Combined entity aspires to achieve US $1 billion in annual revenue by FY28 post-merger with ITC Infotech.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprise technology spending remains selective, prioritizing investments that deliver clear, measurable business outcomes in the near to medium term.
- AI is increasingly moving from experimentation to enterprise-scale adoption.
- BFSI contributed ~28% of revenues; Americas contributed ~56%, APAC grew ~10% sequentially to 9%, and India contributed 18%.
- Sequential momentum seen in retail & CPG, high tech, and industrial verticals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins improved by 100 bps QoQ to 18.5%, supported by improved utilization, operating efficiencies, cost discipline, and higher IP-led business.
- Net short-term borrowing cost is managed between 0.5% to 1% via PCFC/EPC mechanisms linked with forex forwards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Utilization improved to 81.8%.
- Combination with ITC Infotech progressing through necessary approvals (CCI and NCLT tracks).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Ended the quarter with cash and cash equivalents of INR 1,716 crores.
- ROCE stands at ~24%, ROE at ~14.7%.
- DSO increased to 101 days due to migration and integration delays from Aureus and PureSoftware; improving DSO is a priority.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- U.S. EdTech market continues to face challenges, impacting regional performance.
- High-tech space saw muted performance due to completed projects or customers running out of funding.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditi Patil (ICICI Securities) on U.S. market flattish run rate:** Management stated that while NN bookings are positive, drags from the challenging U.S. EdTech market and completed high-tech projects muted growth.
- **Aditi Patil on IMSS segment growth drivers:** Management (Ram Mohan) explained the sharp sequential growth was driven by annuity-based wins, Middle East/India large deals, and increasing security focus (~40% of IMSS from security).
- **Aditi Patil on unallocable corporate expenses:** Management (Venkatraman N) clarified that segment reporting splits only go up to gross margins, and all corporate costs post-gross margin are shown as unallocable, while company-level operating margin is 18.5%.
- **Individual Investor Sujit on merger transaction steps:** Management explained the two tracks: the NCLT merger process and the SPA share transfer between promoter Ashok Soota and ITC Infotech.
- **Individual Investor Rakesh Martha on short-term borrowings:** Management explained that short-term export borrowings (PCFC/EPC) are tied to forex forwards, resulting in a net cost of 0.5% to 1% or net benefit.

### One-line takeaway
Happiest Minds delivered a steady Q2 with 12.5% YoY revenue growth and a 100 bps QoQ margin expansion to 18.5%, driven by high utilization and scaling AI-led solutions, while progressing on its strategic combination with ITC Infotech.
