# Meta Infotech Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-09 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H2 & FY 2026
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited strategic investments for long-term growth and a robust order book, despite near-term profitability moderation.

### Key numbers
- **FY26 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 270 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **H2 FY26 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 60 crore (up 72% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA:** Rs 18 crore (compared to Rs 25 crore in FY25)
- **H2 FY26 EBITDA:** Rs 2 crore (compared to Rs 6 crore in H2 FY25)
- **FY26 PAT:** Rs 11 crore (compared to Rs 14 crore in FY25)
- **H2 FY26 PAT:** Rs 0.4 crore (compared to Rs 3 crore in H2 FY25)
- **FY26 ROE:** 15%
- **FY26 ROCE:** 22%
- **May 2026 Order Book:** Rs 506 crore (~1.9x FY26 revenue)
- **Employee Strength:** Increased from 265 to 309 professionals in FY26; target 350-375 by March 2027.
- **Imperva Professional Services Revenue:** Dropped to Rs 2 crore in FY26 from Rs 8.5 crore in FY25.

### Highlights
- Onboarded 12 new cybersecurity vendors during FY26 (8 focused, 4-5 non-focused), expanding capabilities into AI security, microsegmentation, patch management, OT security, and post-quantum cryptography.
- Expanded commercial presence beyond Mumbai into Delhi, with increased focus on Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, and soon Pune.
- Shifted focus toward high-margin cybersecurity services, which contributed 75% of FY26 EBIT.
- Aspirational target set by management to grow PAT by up to 4x from FY26 levels by FY29.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided an aspirational PAT growth of up to 4x by FY29 from FY26 levels.
- Services revenue contribution target is 25% to 30% over a three-year period, with a minimum 25% increase in services revenue for FY27.
- Target PAT margin increase to 10% plus over the next three years.
- Employee count targeted to reach 350 to 375 by March 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global cybersecurity demand-supply gap is about 70%.
- Indian cybersecurity market is sized at USD 6 billion to USD 7 billion, growing at 15% to 18% YoY.
- Rising threats due to AI are driving customers to invest more heavily in cybersecurity services.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses and profitability were impacted by strategic investments in leadership, talent acquisition, geographic expansion, and capability building.
- Gross profit for FY26 was maintained at Rs 49 crore, similar to FY25.
- Management decided to drop low-margin product engagements (anything under 5%-6% margin) and focus on products yielding 9% to 10% margins.
- Forex volatility on a large dollar-denominated contract impacted H2 results; future contracts include a "Spot Plus 2" forex protection clause.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanded physical offices to Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, and Hyderabad; Pune office coming up soon.
- International expansion underway in Dubai and Australia via associated companies run by the CMD's relatives, and plans to develop the US market through alliances.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory stood at ~Rs 20 crore as of March 2026, expected to be sold in April.
- Loans under assets (Rs 13.8 crore) consist of advances for property purchases, security deposits, and employee advances.
- Management stated no extra equity or debt funding is needed for expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Imperva acquisition by Thales caused disruption, leading to a drop in professional services revenue from Rs 8.5 crore to Rs 2 crore.
- High industry attrition (25% to 40%); Meta's attrition was noted at under 20%, mitigated by maintaining bench strength and structured replacements.
- Currency fluctuation losses on large USD contracts in H2 FY26.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aniket Madhwani (StepTrade Capital) on H2 revenue dip:** Management explained it is due to seasonality from a large >Rs 100 crore vendor order booked primarily in H1. *(Direct answer)*
2. **Aniket Madhwani on Imperva revenue drop:** Management explained the decline was due to Imperva being acquired by Thales, causing a transition/gestation period for the new team. *(Direct answer)*
3. **Abhishek Sharda (Hem Securities) on PAT margin targets:** Management clarified the target is to expand PAT margins to 10%+ over three years, aiming for a 4x increase in absolute PAT by FY29. *(Direct answer)*
4. **Lakshminarayan KG on cash flow conversion:** CFO Paresh Soni and the CMD clarified that negative operating cash flow was impacted by tax payments and year-end inventory timing, which has since been resolved. *(Direct answer)*
5. **Lakshminarayan KG on customer concentration:** Management noted that one major banking client contributed ~50-55% of the topline in FY26 via low-margin product pass-through revenue. *(Direct answer)*

### One-line takeaway
Meta Infotech treated FY26 as an investment year to build out its geographic presence, technical vendor partnerships, and senior leadership, setting an aspirational target of 4x PAT growth by FY29.
