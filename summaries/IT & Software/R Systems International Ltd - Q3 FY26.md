# R Systems International Ltd - Q3 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q3 FY2026
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted 7 consecutive quarters of growth, record-high EBITDA margins, and a successful pivot toward AI-led service offerings.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 615.2 crore ($64.4 million), up 23.4% YoY.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 124.4 crore (20.2% margin), up 47.4% YoY.
*   **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 66.5 crore (10.8% margin), up 53.1% YoY.
*   **Adjusted EPS:** Rs 5.6, up 53% YoY.
*   **9M Revenue:** Rs 1,791.7 crore, up 27.7% YoY.
*   **9M Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 360.9 crore (20.1% margin), up 49.7% YoY.

### Highlights
*   Company reported 7 consecutive quarters of growth.
*   Revenue mix is shifting from pure-play product development (OPD) to data readiness for AI, agentic implementations, and modernization.
*   AI-related revenue now exceeds 35% of total revenue.
*   Novigo acquisition integration is proceeding; growth is in line with company average despite geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.
*   Top 10 clients contribute 24.4% of revenue; top client contribution is in the 5.8%–6% bracket.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management aims for sustainable double-digit growth rates.
*   Targeting an enterprise revenue mix of 25%–35% within 2–3 years to derisk from the TMT sector.
*   Management expects to maintain EBITDA margins in the high 18% range.
*   No formal financial guidance provided.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   AI Product Development Life Cycle (PDLC) is replacing traditional SDLC.
*   Clients are moving from experimentation to production-grade AI deployments.
*   Private equity clients are favoring custom AI development over off-the-shelf vertical AI platforms.
*   Competition in AI-led services is intensifying, but management believes their "AI-first" strategy and engineering velocity are key differentiators.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Adjusted EBITDA margin reached an all-time high of 20.2%.
*   Currency tailwinds contributed ~Rs 7 crore to EBITDA.
*   Higher hiring costs for AI talent slightly impacted operating margins.
*   SG&A expenses decreased to Rs 113 crore due to support count optimization and internal AI adoption.
*   RSU costs declined to Rs 3.2 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Company is actively expanding AI/data teams and bench.
*   Utilization maintained above 80%.
*   Management remains open to inorganic growth/acquisitions to enhance strategic capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   DSO: 55 days (billed), 76 days (including unbilled).
*   Other financial liabilities increased to Rs 268 crore, primarily due to a second interim dividend of Rs 96 crore declared in September 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East (Novigo operations).
*   Intensifying competition in AI-led services.
*   Slower-than-anticipated deal closure times for complex AI/agentic workflows.
*   Potential for further volatility depending on US election outcomes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is organic growth accelerating?** A: Management noted organic growth is in the high single digits, aiming for double-digit growth; acknowledged deal closure times for AI projects are longer than anticipated.
*   **Q: Impact of enterprise pivot on margins?** A: Management believes there is no significant profitability gap; enterprise clients are accustomed to fixed-price/outcome-based models, which may allow for better margins.
*   **Q: Why is TTM ACV flattish?** A: Management explained that while ACV is flattish, the nature of deals has changed to be more transformative, with shorter revenue realization cycles.
*   **Q: Is the worst over for Novigo?** A: Management stated the crisis is not over due to geopolitical uncertainty, but teams are delivering on the ground.

### One-line takeaway
R Systems delivered strong Q3 results with record margins, successfully pivoting its business mix toward AI-led transformation and enterprise clients, though organic growth remains in the high single digits.
