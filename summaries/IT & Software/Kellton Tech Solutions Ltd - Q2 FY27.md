# Kellton Tech Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted steady growth, six new client wins, and successful AI-led project execution despite macroeconomic headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained the stance of not providing specific percentage-based forward guidance due to macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainties.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious" to more optimistic regarding internal execution and AI-led traction, though management acknowledged global macro conditions (e.g., US elections, Iran-Iraq war) continue to impact stock valuation and capital raising plans.
- **New topics:** FCCB (Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds) status clarified as "on hold" due to market conditions; Rights issue status confirmed as "work in progress" awaiting exchange approvals.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The company successfully executed the revamp of an Agritech client's platform (promised in Q1) and continued to secure new logos.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 321 crore (6.5% YoY growth).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 637 crore (6.6% YoY growth).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 39 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 12.1%.
- **PAT:** Rs 23 crore.
- **PAT Margin:** 7.2%.
- **EPS:** 43 paise per share.

### Highlights
- Secured six new client wins: global automotive finance company (loan integration platform), US healthcare claims processor (AI-driven enterprise management), Saudi low-cost airline (quality engineering), premium automotive logistics provider (IT infra/support), seed engineering company (application development), and global hearing aid manufacturer (R&D/firmware engineering).
- Won first prize at SEBI’s tech sprint for "Niyama," an AI-based regulatory compliance agent.
- Received "Emerging Excellence Award" from ADB (Asian Development Bank) for rapid scaling.
- Ranked 4th in SBI AURA hackathon for agentic AI solution in personalized banking.
- Completed a major technology stack upgrade for a global Agritech client without downtime.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide long-term or annual percentage growth guidance due to macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainties.
- Management believes the "bottom is done" for the stock price and expects it to appreciate when market conditions improve.

### Demand & industry commentary
- AI is being integrated into the core of all new platforms rather than as an add-on to ensure future-proofing.
- Management emphasized that product/framework-based revenue commands higher valuation multiples than pure services revenue, driving the strategic focus on IP investment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Not discussed.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Management plans to invest in Intellectual Property (IP) to improve valuation multiples.
- Funds from proposed FCCB and rights issue are intended for debt retirement and IP acquisition/development.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Goodwill:** Increased significantly due to a "commodity acquisition."
- **Debt:** Management intends to use proceeds from future capital raises (FCCB/Rights Issue) to retire debt.
- **FCCB:** Currently on hold due to global macroeconomic conditions and stock price performance; management stated there are no current plans to execute it immediately.
- **Rights Issue:** Awaiting approvals from NSE and BSE; promoters intend to participate.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Macroeconomic/Geopolitical:** US elections, Iran-Iraq war, and global climate are impacting stock valuation and the timing of capital raises.
- **Dilution:** Management acknowledged that FCCB issuance would cause dilution but emphasized it is necessary for IP investment and debt retirement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shweta (Individual Investor) on FCCB dilution:** Management stated FCCB is on hold due to market conditions and they will not dilute at current low price points.
- **Ram (Individual Investor) on Rights Issue:** Management confirmed they are awaiting exchange approvals and promoters will participate.
- **Raghu (Individual Investor) on Goodwill increase:** Management attributed the rise to a "commodity acquisition" and noted they have written off international goodwill, leaving only Indian goodwill on books.
- **Vishwan Narayan (Individual Investor) on dilution concerns:** Management clarified that the rights issue does not dilute existing holdings, whereas FCCB would, but reiterated that FCCB is not happening "anytime soon."

### One-line takeaway
Kellton Tech posted steady 6.5% YoY revenue growth and operational wins in AI, while management deferred capital-raising plans (FCCB) due to market volatility.
