# Orient Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating performance improved due to a higher contribution from the managed services business, driving margin expansion and higher profitability.

### Key numbers
- Total Income (Q2 FY27): Rs. 220.06 crores (up 7.99% QoQ from Rs. 203.77 crores in Q1 FY27)
- Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27): Rs. 219.77 crores (up 8.84% QoQ from Rs. 201.92 crores)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs. 22.45 crores (up 45.6% QoQ from Rs. 15.42 crores)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 10.20% (expanded by 263 bps QoQ from 7.57% in Q1 FY27)
- Profit Before Tax (Q2 FY27): Rs. 14.74 crores (up 124.14% QoQ from Rs. 6.58 crores)
- Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27): Rs. 11.72 crores (up 126.66% QoQ from Rs. 5.17 crores)
- EPS (Q2 FY27): Rs. 2.56 per share (up 126.55% QoQ from Rs. 1.13)
- Services Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs. 130.99 crores (up 24.95% QoQ from Rs. 88.78 crores in Q1 FY27; 59.6% contribution)
- Product Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs. 88.78 crores (compared to Rs. 97.09 crores in Q1 FY27)
- Total Income (H1 FY27): Rs. 423.83 crores
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs. 37.87 crores (Margin: 8.94%)
- Profit Before Tax (H1 FY27): Rs. 21.32 crores
- Profit After Tax (H1 FY27): Rs. 16.90 crores
- EPS (H1 FY27): Rs. 3.69 per share
- Order Book (as of Sept 30, 2026): Rs. 331.61 crores (Product: Rs. 132 crores, Services: Rs. 199 crores), expected to be billed by March 31, 2027
- Employee Strength (as of Sept 30, 2026): 1,681 employees
- Interim Dividend: Rs. 2 per share (20% of face value) approved by the Board for FY27

### Highlights
- Received a purchase order of approx. Rs. 76.20 crores (excl. GST) from NPCI in July 2026 for servers with a 7-year warranty, to be executed over 18 weeks.
- Signed a 3-year contract worth USD 3.15 million with Securonix in September 2026, becoming its exclusive value-added distributor and joint go-to-market partner in India.
- Recognized as an Amazon Web Services (AWS) Managed Service Provider.
- Revenue mix shifted toward services, which contributed 59.6% in Q2 FY27 compared to 51.92% in Q1 FY27.
- Board approved an interim dividend of Rs. 2 per share for FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated priorities for H2 FY27 remain focused on profitable growth, operational efficiency, working capital discipline, and expanding the managed services business.
- Management expects margins to be sustainable as long as the revenue mix remains around 60% services and 40% products.
- Management declined to disclose specific forward-looking revenue numbers for FY27 compared to FY26.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Infrastructure product business faces challenges due to delivery delays and rising prices, a trend expected to continue for another year.
- Most customers prefer Opex investment over Capex for infrastructure.
- Supply constraints from OEMs are severe, with delivery timelines stretching to 16-18 weeks (about 4 months), and OEM feedback suggests issues may persist through 2028 globally.
- Cybersecurity is increasingly a boardroom discussion, driving heavy investments in managed security services.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher contribution from higher-margin managed services directly drove EBITDA margin expansion.
- Infrastructure component prices are rising weekly.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planning to invest approximately USD 5 million in cybersecurity expansion, hire around 100 cybersecurity professionals, and target a 12% expansion in the cybersecurity customer base.
- NOC and SOC facility at the Turbhe office (funded via IPO proceeds) is scaling up; current capacity utilization is in the double digits and expected to reach full capacity usage by Calendar Year 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is debt-free and aims to maintain a debt-free status through March 2027.
- Exercising strict working capital and credit evaluation discipline for Device as a Service (DaaS) customers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued difficulty and delivery delays in the infrastructure products business, with skyrocketing prices.
- OEM supply constraints projected to potentially linger through 2028.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aman Patle (Private Limited) asked about drivers for strong service contribution and sustainability.** Management replied that infrastructure product challenges prompted a strategic shift toward Orient Hosted Managed Services (Data Center, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Infrastructure, Digital Transformation), leading to a 25% sequential services revenue jump.
2. **Harshil Shah asked about the 14% H1 FY27 revenue dip YoY and order book breakup.** Management explained the dip was due to difficult infrastructure market conditions and selective deal-picking, while EBITDA improved via higher-margin managed services. The Rs. 331.61 crore order book comprises Rs. 132 crores in products and Rs. 199 crores in services.
3. **Honey Nathani asked about NPCI server order execution and OEM supply availability.** Management confirmed execution is on track within the 18-week schedule for delivery next week, but noted OEM supply lead times have worsened to 16–18 weeks globally.
4. **Aditya Banerjee asked about AWS Managed Service Provider milestone impact.** Management stated it provides enterprise visibility, validates implementation/security capabilities, and helps win larger cloud managed services contracts.
5. **Chinmay Shah asked about SOC/NOC capacity utilization and DaaS pipeline.** Management stated SOC/NOC utilization is currently in low double digits and targeted to reach full capacity by Calendar Year 2027; DaaS is growing sequentially with strict credit agency approval vetting.

### One-line takeaway
Orient Technologies delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with a 45.6% QoQ surge in EBITDA and 126.6% QoQ growth in PAT, driven by a strategic pivot toward high-margin managed services that offset headwinds in the infrastructure product business.
