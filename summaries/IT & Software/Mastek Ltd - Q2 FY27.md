# Mastek Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue growth, strong cash collections, and robust momentum in AI transformation deals, despite some macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainties impacting the order backlog.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** $107.3 million (up 2.4% QoQ in constant currency; up 3.4% QoQ and 8.3% YoY in INR at Rs 1,018.7 crore).
- **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 14.4% (down 100 bps QoQ).
- **PAT:** Rs 105.7 crore (10.3% of total income).
- **12-Month Order Backlog:** $286.2 million (down 7.7% QoQ; up 2.3% YoY in USD, up 10.4% YoY in INR).
- **EPS:** Basic Rs 34.1 (up 8.3% YoY); Diluted Rs 33.9 (up 8.4% YoY).
- **DSO:** 74 days (decrease of 1 day).
- **Net Cash & Investments:** Rs 582.7 crore (net of debt).
- **Attrition (LTM):** 15.2% (down from 16.4%).
- **Headcount:** 4,810 (reduction of 87 QoQ).

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 1,000 crore quarterly revenue for the first time in INR terms.
- Closed 53 AI-related orders in the quarter, with AI deals contributing roughly 45% of total order bookings.
- Launched new offerings: "Service-as-a-software" (replacing traditional SaaS with AI-native solutions) and a strategic partnership with Innovaccer for Healthcare & Life Sciences AI transformation.
- Completed the sale of a property in Chennai, leading to an exceptional profit offset by workforce restructuring costs and old receivable write-offs.
- Collected $120 million during Q2; paid a dividend of Rs 50 crore and loan repayment of around Rs 55 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic that H2 performance will be strong and that FY27 will outperform FY26, barring unforeseen geopolitical and macroeconomic issues (such as in the Middle East).
- Management aims to improve EBITDA margins by at least 160 bps in the medium term, though the velocity depends on macro factors and localized security-cleared talent costs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- UK business performed robustly with 4.1% QoQ revenue growth; Financial Services in the UK grew by over 16%.
- Opened a new front in Energy & Utilities in the UK, landing three new frameworks.
- North America order book and total backlog improved significantly (total backlog up 47.9% YoY), driven by Healthcare & Life Sciences, retail, and manufacturing.
- Middle East demand continues to face stringency and uncertainty, impacting order books.
- Healthcare & Life Sciences saw temporary slowness due to right-shifting of project starts (transition from discovery to beta phase), but management expects a bounce back in H2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin declined 100 bps QoQ to 14.4%, primarily impacted by salary increments and ESOPs (190 bps impact) and project mix changes due to localization/security-cleared resource demands in the UK (200 bps impact), partially offset by internal AI-led efficiencies (200 bps) and working days.
- Subcontractor costs remain range-bound between 18% to 20% of revenue, with potential minor fluctuations based on UK localization demands.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total closing headcount stood at 4,810. Utilization (including trainees) decreased by 0.8% QoQ due to domain resource investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash and investments increased by Rs 36 crore during the quarter to Rs 582.7 crore.
- DSO improved by 1 day QoQ to 74 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slower consumption and delayed start of certain large wins (e.g., North American federal deal delayed past mid-term elections; UK public sector orders delayed pending new government budget announcements) led to a sequential decline in the 12-month order backlog.
- Geopolitical uncertainty and market conditions in the Middle East are creating headwinds.
- Increased local security clearance requirements in the UK are altering project margins and cost structures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities) asked about the order book softness and FY27 outlook.** Management explained that right-shifting/delays in large deal starts (like a North American federal deal delayed until mid-term elections and slower UK consumption) impacted the 12-month backlog, but reiterated that H2 and FY27 overall are expected to outpace FY26.
2. **Amit Chandra asked about the margin bridge and UK margin risks.** CFO Deepak Kedia broke down the 100 bps margin drop (190 bps wage/ESOP impact, 200 bps mix shift for security-cleared resources, offset by 200 bps AI efficiencies) and noted that UK localization demands require internal adjustments.
3. **Dipesh Mehta (Emkay Global) asked about AI deal sizes and margins.** Management stated AI transformation deals typically start between $0.5 million and $5 million and expand over time, carrying gross margins of 40% to over 50%, whereas AI productivity takeouts are more price-aggressive.
4. **Dipesh Mehta asked about order book decision-making delays.** Management noted UK public sector orders stalled temporarily due to cabinet changes and impending government budgets, alongside Middle East softness.
5. **Pulkit Chawla (360 One Capital Market) asked about the Healthcare vertical contraction and subcon costs.** Management explained Healthcare softness in the UK was a timing gap between alpha/discovery and beta phases, expected to reverse in H2; subcon costs are stable around 18-20%.

### One-line takeaway
Mastek delivered steady Q2 revenue growth of 2.4% QoQ in constant currency and robust cash collections, aggressively pivoting toward AI-native service offerings despite temporary order backlog softness driven by government and geopolitical transitions.
