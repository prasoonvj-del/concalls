# C.E. Info Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited strong year-on-year growth across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, alongside a robust funnel and open order book.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Up 15.8% YoY (Q2); H1 revenue up 18.4% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Up 60% YoY (Q2); H1 EBITDA up 20.4% YoY; H1 EBITDA margin at 37.2%.
- **PAT:** Up 90% YoY (Q2); H1 PAT up 32% YoY.
- **Map-led Segment:** Q2 revenue reached INR 76.7 crores; Q2 EBITDA margin at 41.6% (H1 EBITDA margin at 47%).
- **IoT-led Segment:** Q2 revenue reached INR 55.1 crores; Q2 EBITDA margin at 23.8% (H1 EBITDA margin at 19.2%).
- **Automotive Segment Growth:** Up 52% YoY in Q2; up 40% in H1.
- **Enterprise Segment Growth:** Up 44% YoY in Q2.
- **Receivables:** Days in the range of 120 to 122 days.

### Highlights
- Management is heavily investing in technology for future readiness and strengthening leadership across technology, product, and business.
- AI, ML, and neural networks are being embedded across Map-led and IoT-led platforms.
- Automotive segment secured a commercial vehicle localized navigation solution contract, a leading 2-wheeler OEM contract renewal, and new line-fitment and aftermarket Go-Lives.
- Enterprise segment won a large delivery/quick commerce software stack contract (workforce automation) and saw existing quick commerce customers scale volumes 5x. Also won BFSI customers (Geo-Verify and Geo-Fi) and a large telecom operator for network expansion.
- Government segment saw a one-off revenue base in the corresponding previous quarter; secured key Go-Lives in the oil and gas upstream sector (digital twin) and a first-time national disaster response win.
- Inventory was reduced by half, customer collection timelines remain steady, cash flows are strong, and supplier accounts are being settled faster.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain an EBITDA margin of 35% plus for the full year.
- Management expressed a bullish and encouraging outlook for H2, citing the open order book and recent Go-Lives (such as the oil and gas IoT subscription customer).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Seasonality exists across segments: Enterprise sees peaks in Q1 and Q4, Government peaks towards Q4, and Automotive follows a build-up.
- Government business approach is being carefully calibrated to prioritize collectibility and margins over volume.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- IoT business margins benefited from built-in operating leverage and sales synergies across the group.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory reduced to half.
- Receivables are steady at 120-122 days, with internal focus on improving government collections.
- Company is cash-rich.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government receivables remain elevated (around INR 100 crores plus), with internal efforts focused on faster collections.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anmol Garg (DAM Capital) asked about enterprise seasonality:** Management explained it stems from varied revenue models (annual licenses, per delivery, per transaction, consumption, and per asset/user per month).
2. **Anmol Garg asked about H2 visibility and 20%+ growth:** Management stated H2 should benefit from converting the substantial open order book and recent Go-Lives like the oil and gas contract.
3. **Anmol Garg asked about elevated receivable days despite lower government revenue:** Management clarified that non-government receivables are healthy (business-as-usual), while government collections remain the core area of focus.
4. **Gautam Rathi (CWC Advisors) asked if IoT hardware sales had one-offs:** Management clarified the growth is driven by broader B2B sales and marketing execution across Automotive, Enterprise, and Government rather than one-off hardware sales.
5. **Gautam Rathi asked about the path to acceleration over the next 2 years:** Management reiterated that business momentum and the order book remain strong, though future quarterly numbers cannot be predicted.

### One-line takeaway
C.E. Info Systems reported a strong Q2 FY27 with 15.8% revenue growth and significant EBITDA and PAT expansions, driven by robust momentum across Automotive and Enterprise segments.
