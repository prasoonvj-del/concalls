# Wipro Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-16 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Operating margins expanded sequentially, but constant currency revenue was flat QoQ alongside deal-signing delays and weakness in US healthcare.

### Key numbers
* **IT Services Revenue:** $2.61 billion (flat QoQ, up 0.6% YoY in constant currency).
* **Operating Margin:** 16.5% (up 0.5% / 50 bps QoQ).
* **Total Contract Value (TCV):** $2.6 billion; 9 large deals signed.
* **Net Income:** Rs 3,200 crore (INR 32 billion, down 5% QoQ).
* **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** INR 3.23 (up 0.9% QoQ, up 4.2% YoY).
* **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 5,312 crore equivalent (166% of net income).
* **Gross Cash Balance:** $4.6 billion as of September 30, 2026.
* **Net Other Income:** Declined 68% QoQ due to lower investable surplus post-buyback completion in June 2026.

### Highlights
* Launched AI Native Unit in April, now staffing over 2,000 engineers (including Forward Deployed Engineers).
* Strategic deal integrations of Mindsprint (agriculture/food supply chain via Olam deal) and HARMAN DTS (design-to-manufacture and engineering) drove new client additions.
* Europe grew 0.6% QoQ and 5.1% YoY (CC); APMEA grew 3.1% QoQ and 13.0% YoY (CC).
* Americas 1 declined 2.1% QoQ and 2.7% YoY (CC) due to client-specific issues and healthcare softness.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Q3 FY27 IT Services Revenue Guidance:** $2.571 billion to $2.625 billion.
* **Q3 FY27 Constant Currency Growth Guidance:** -1.5% to +0.5% QoQ.
* Management stated Q3 results will be sequentially impacted by seasonal furloughs and lower working days.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Demand environment remains largely unchanged with clients prioritizing cost optimization, vendor consolidation, and ROI on AI adoption.
* **BFSI:** Showed momentum (grew 1.0% QoQ CC), driven by legacy modernization, agentic AI scaling, and cloud/data foundation projects.
* **Technology & Communications:** Seeing strong demand from digital native firms in custom silicon, physical AI, and design-to-manufacture.
* **US Healthcare:** Facing weakness (-3.8% QoQ CC) due to regulatory policy changes and margin pressures on US payers, though platform solutions are attracting first-time outsourcers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Operating margin expanded by 50 bps QoQ to 16.5%, supported by operational efficiency and Rupee depreciation, offset by investments in growth.
* Salary increases (effective March 1, 2026) were rolled out earlier in the year; no new salary hikes are currently under consideration.
* Management expects operational improvements to support margin expansion over time toward its target band of 17.0%-17.5%, without offering a specific timeline.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Headcount declined by over 6,500 employees QoQ (flattish YoY) due to AI-driven productivity gains and lack of campus hiring in H1.
* Campus hiring will resume in H2 FY27 to support volume growth.
* Accelerated investments into five AI growth themes: AI data centers, AI foundry services, physical AI, AI devices, and design-to-manufacturing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Robust cash conversion with operating cash flows at 166% of net income.
* Effective tax rate stood at 23.9% for the quarter.
* Accounting yield on average investments in India was 7.1%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Policy changes and slowing membership growth in US healthcare continuing to pressure discretionary tech spend.
* Administrative delays in finalizing deal paperwork led to TCV slippages from Q2 into Q3.
* Impact of seasonal furloughs and lower working days in Q3 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Girish Pai (BOB Capital):** *Why did Q2 TCV drop QoQ?*  
  **Management:** Two confirmed deals could not be signed before quarter-end due to paperwork delays; both slipped into Q3. Pipeline for Q3 remains strong.
* **Sudheer Guntupalli (Mahindra AMC):** *Will Wipro leverage its balance sheet for large acquisitions?*  
  **Management:** Strategy will remain targeted toward inorganic opportunities that enhance consulting-led, AI-powered capabilities in priority sectors (similar to HARMAN DTS and Mindsprint).
* **Ravi Menon:** *Is BFSI growth sustainable, and will it lead overall growth?*  
  **Management:** Optimistic on BFSI recovery, noting good momentum in Capco across regions and client captives returning work; BFSI and Tech will likely compete to lead growth.
* **Ashwin Mehta:** *What drove the 6,500+ headcount drop in Q2?*  
  **Management:** H1 lacked campus hiring and productivity gains were realized from AI initiatives. Campus recruitment will resume in H2.
* **Dipesh Mehta (Emkay Global):** *Are deal slippages closed and will sequential growth resume past Q3?*  
  **Management:** Delayed deals are currently being signed. Organic sequential growth remains the endeavor, with Q4 expected to benefit from Q3 deal conversions.

### One-line takeaway
Wipro's Q2 was marked by margin resilience and AI-led operational progress, despite near-term revenue flatlining caused by deal-signing slippages and US healthcare softness.
