# Hexaware Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q3 CY26
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth was below expectations due to client-specific softness and deal delays, though management emphasized a strong underlying pipeline and a new strategic focus on AI.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** $411.9 million (up 1.6% QoQ, 4.3% YoY); INR 3,933.1 crore (up 2.3% QoQ, 12.9% YoY).
*   **Adjusted EBIT Margin:** 13.8% (17 bps improvement QoQ).
*   **Reported EBIT Margin:** 12.2%.
*   **Reported PAT:** 10.9%.
*   **Adjusted PAT:** 9.4%.
*   **Headcount:** 35,887 (net addition of 1,381).
*   **DSO:** 77 days.
*   **Cash & Investment Balance:** ~$200 million.

### Highlights
*   **Leadership:** Vivek Jaitley appointed as new CEO.
*   **Restructuring:** Netherlands operations restructured; one-off cost of ~$6 million taken in Q3; expected to improve annual operating margins by 40 bps going forward.
*   **Merit Increases:** Effective October 1, 2026, with a 100 bps impact on margins.
*   **Client Metrics:** 32 clients with >$10 million revenue (vs 30 YoY); 17 clients >$20 million (vs 15 YoY).
*   **AI Strategy:** Focused on three pillars: AI for IT (Zero Friction Enterprise), AI for Business (outcome-based interventions), and AI Foundation (Anthropic partnership).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management withdrew Q4 CY26 outlook to improve forecasting rigor and accuracy.
*   **Margins:** Full-year CY26 adjusted EBIT margin range narrowed to 13-13.5%.
*   **Seasonality:** Management expects Q4 to follow historical seasonal patterns (furloughs, fewer working days, sequential decline).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Vertical Performance:** Banking (+6.6% QoQ) and TPP (+7.2% QoQ) led growth.
*   **Headwinds:** Softness in one GSE client (cost optimization) and Travel & Transportation (Middle East conflict).
*   **Vendor Consolidation:** Management stated this is a structural industry feature; Hexaware has won most consolidation initiatives it participated in.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **One-offs:** 530 bps earnout reversal (SMC), 110 bps earnout charge (Cybersolve), 150 bps restructuring (Netherlands).
*   **Onshore Mix:** Increased to 53.1%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Utilization:** 84.9% (broadly unchanged QoQ).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Company remains debt-free.
*   **Cash Flow:** Last 12 months operating cash flow is ~110% of reported profit.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Client-Specific:** Softness in GSE clients and Travel & Transportation.
*   **Execution:** Slower-than-anticipated ramp-ups in certain large deals.
*   **Volatility:** Asset/license revenue remains volatile (contributes ~2% of total revenue).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Strategy/Incentives (Anmol Garg):** Management (Vivek) announced a 90-day strategic review; (Vikash) noted a recent shift to commission-based sales plans to increase deal velocity.
*   **Business Momentum (Gaurav Rateria):** Management clarified there is no "slacking off," but deal closures were delayed by client decision cycles.
*   **Forecasting Rigor (Prateek Maheshwari):** Management committed to tightening processes to improve forecast accuracy and credibility.
*   **Netherlands Restructuring (Dipesh Mehta):** Management confirmed no incremental charges after Q2 CY27.
*   **Competitive Intensity (Vibhor Singhal):** Management noted it is a buyer's market, but Hexaware (as a challenger) often gains share from incumbents.

### One-line takeaway
New leadership is prioritizing a 90-day strategic review and improved forecasting rigor following a quarter of missed revenue expectations due to client-specific headwinds.
