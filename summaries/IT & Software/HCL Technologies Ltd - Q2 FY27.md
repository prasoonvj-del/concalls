# HCL Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-15 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Solid overall performance driven by record H1 deal bookings, strong Advanced AI growth, and sequential EBIT margin expansion, despite near-term software software revenue softness and macro volatility.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** $3,724 million (up 2.3% QoQ CC, 2.6% YoY CC).
- **Services Revenue:** $3,442 million (up 3.0% QoQ CC, 4.0% YoY CC).
  - **IT & Business Services (ITBS):** Up 2.7% QoQ CC, 4.4% YoY CC.
  - **Engineering and R&D Services (ERS):** Up 4.1% QoQ CC, 2.1% YoY CC.
- **HCLSoftware Revenue:** $294 million (down 5.8% QoQ CC, 11.2% YoY CC); Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) reached $1.134 billion (up 7.8% YoY including acquisitions, up 2.6% YoY organically).
- **Advanced AI Revenue:** $219 million (up 28.4% QoQ, 78% YoY); annualized run-rate at $876 million.
- **Agentic AI Revenue:** Crossed $100 million for the quarter.
- **Net Deal Bookings:** $3.3 billion (up 35% QoQ, 27% YoY).
- **EBIT & Margin:** $677 million; EBIT margin at 18.2% (up 131 bps QoQ, 71 bps YoY). Adjusted for restructuring (84 bps), EBIT margin stood at 19.0%.
- **Net Income:** $538 million (14.4% of revenue).
- **Normalized Diluted EPS (LTM):** INR70.22 (up 5% QoQ, 12.2% YoY); including one-off New Labor Codes impact, Diluted EPS was INR67.56.
- **Interim Dividend:** INR12 per share (LTM payout of INR60 per share, representing 88.6% of Net Income).
- **Headcount & Attrition:** Total headcount at 230,339 (net addition of 6,450 QoQ); LTM attrition at 12.8%. Revenue per employee rose 2.4% YoY.

### Highlights
- Delivered the second-highest quarterly bookings in company history ($3.3 billion) and the strongest first half (H1) ever.
- Inorganic contributions in Q2 totaled $35.6 million (97 bps sequential growth impact), including HPE Telco Solutions ($21 million for 2 months, 62 bps impact) and Jaspersoft ($15 million for full quarter).
- Announced intent to acquire Croatia-based enterprise RPA platform Robotiq.ai to bolster AI orchestration in HCL GuNo.
- Completed acquisition of HPE’s Telco Solutions Group (adding OSS and 5G subscriber data management IP).
- Launched HCLTech Pulse, a business unit targeting mid-market enterprises ($500M to $5B revenue).
- Expanded strategic partnerships: achieved OpenAI Advanced Partner status, NVIDIA AI factory deployment validation, global Premier partnership with Anthropic (establishing a dedicated Anthropic BU and CoE), family of Gemini enterprise offerings with Google Cloud, and Cohere partnership in Canada and Germany.
- No material impact expected from the US Department of Labor's suspension of the PERM program due to small application volume in recent years and focus on local in-country hiring.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Company Constant Currency Revenue Growth:** 2.5% to 4.0% (includes 87 bps inorganic contribution).
- **FY27 Services Constant Currency Revenue Growth:** 3.0% to 4.0% (includes 62 bps inorganic contribution).
- **FY27 EBIT Margin Guidance:** Maintained at 17.5% to 18.5%.
- Midpoint of full-year organic growth guidance for both Services and Company level remains broadly unchanged.
- Management noted revenue from the mega deal won will start accruing in Q1 of next financial year (FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broad-based growth across geographies and verticals; 5 of 7 verticals grew YoY, led by Retail & CPG (+14.0% YoY CC) and Financial Services (+7.1% YoY CC).
- Regional YoY CC performance: Rest of World (+11.4%), India (+21.8%), USA (+3.5%), Europe (-0.3%).
- Clients prioritizing AI, data, and operating model transformations for efficiency and business resilience.
- Broad demand across AI factory solutions (grew 64% QoQ), semiconductor workloads, legacy/product modernization, and sovereign AI.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBIT margin expanded 131 bps QoQ due to:
  - Project Ascend & AI-driven operational efficiencies: +73 bps.
  - SG&A operating leverage: +50 bps.
  - Higher-margin Advanced AI revenue: +32 bps.
  - Forex benefits: +23 bps.
- Margin headwinds: Higher restructuring costs (-22 bps) and HPE Telco Solutions consolidation (-25 bps).
- Advanced AI services deliver 7% to 10% higher margins than traditional services, while IP/platform components carry gross margins upwards of 70%.
- Management aims for a long-term desired EBIT margin band of 18% to 19%, but will prioritize re-investing operational efficiency gains into AI capabilities and talent.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested INR185 crores to launch an advanced semiconductor lab in Bengaluru for post-silicon engineering, testing, and validation.
- Announced plans for HCLTech's first AI data center and a Global Technology Center in Bhubaneswar (Odisha).
- Opened a new Global Technology Center in GIFT City, Gujarat, focused on financial services clients.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Gross Cash:** $2.66 billion; **Net Cash:** $2.56 billion as of September 30, 2026.
- **Total DSO (including unbilled receivables):** 84 days (down 2 days QoQ).
- **LTM Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** $1.96 billion (95% conversion of Net Income).
- **LTM Free Cash Flow (FCF):** $1.78 billion (87% conversion of Net Income).
- LTM FCF conversion was impacted by two one-off items: bilateral advance pricing agreement (BAPA) tax payments and a transfer of cash to an external gratuity trust. FCF to Net Income conversion is expected to revert to 100%+ levels as these roll off.
- **ROIC (LTM):** Company ROIC at 41.2% (up 261 bps YoY); Services ROIC at 49.4% (up 411 bps YoY).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic environment remains dynamic and volatile, leading management to maintain a cautious overall outlook.
- HCLSoftware license revenue faced headwinds due to lower multi-year contract renewals falling due in Q2 compared to Q2 FY26 ($39 million upfront vs. $81 million in Q2 FY26).
- Softness and discretionary spend pressure in top telecommunications and manufacturing client accounts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhishek Pathak (Motilal Oswal):** *How is value shared with frontier AI labs, and what is driving Advanced AI revenue competition?*  
  **Management:** Value from lab partnerships (OpenAI, Anthropic) primarily comes via Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) services. Product/platform integrations (like AI Force) command non-linear pricing inclusive of token costs. Advanced AI win momentum includes single-sourced deals, boutique competitor overlap, and IP monetization.
- **Ravi Menon (Axis Capital):** *Why not raise the upper end of FY27 growth guidance given strong performance and pipeline?*  
  **Management:** While Q3 will see seasonal software step-up and services are expected slightly above the midpoint, management prefers caution given external geopolitical and macro volatility.
- **Rishi Jhunjhunwala (IIFL Capital):** *When will growth resume in top client buckets (Top 5/10/20)?*  
  **Management:** Ramp-downs in two telco clients and one manufacturing client were already factored into guidance. Upselling across the broader client base remains strong (adding 1 client in $100M+, 8 in $50M+, 10 in $20M+, and 33 in $10M+ YoY).
- **Surendra Goyal (Citi):** *What explains the gap between software ARR growth (+7.8% YoY) and reported software revenue decline (-11.2% YoY)?*  
  **Management:** Revenue is impacted by timing of multi-year contract renewals and upfront revenue recognition rules (60%-80% recognized upfront). ARR is the primary metric reflecting true business health; renewal volume is expected to bounce back in FY28/FY29.
- **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley):** *What is the medium-term philosophy around operating margins given investments?*  
  **Management:** The desired operating margin band remains 18% to 19%, but management will flexibly plow back efficiency gains into AI talent, IP, and platform capabilities rather than solely maximizing near-term margins.

### One-line takeaway
HCLTech delivered strong Q2 FY27 results with record H1 bookings and margin expansion driven by Advanced AI scaling, while keeping full-year growth guidance realistic amidst macro volatility.
