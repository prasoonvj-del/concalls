# HCL Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-15 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Solid quarter with strong Advanced AI scaling, near-record bookings, and margin expansion, tempered by macroeconomic caution.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** $3,724 million (up 2.3% QoQ and 2.6% YoY in constant currency)
- **Services Revenue:** $3,442 million (up 3.0% QoQ and 4.0% YoY)
- **IT and Business Services (ITBS):** Up 2.7% QoQ and 4.4% YoY
- **Engineering and R&D Services (ERS):** Up 4.1% QoQ and 2.1% YoY
- **HCLSoftware Revenue:** $294 million (down 5.8% QoQ and 11.2% YoY)
- **HCLSoftware ARR:** $1.134 billion (up 7.8% YoY)
- **Advanced AI Revenue:** $219 million (up 28.4% QoQ and 78% YoY; annualized at $876 million)
- **EBIT Margin:** 18.2% (up 131 bps QoQ and 71 bps YoY); normalized EBIT margin excluding restructuring was 19.0%
- **Net Income:** $538 million (14.4% of revenue)
- **New Deal Bookings:** $3.3 billion (up 27% YoY and 35% QoQ)
- **Total Headcount:** 230,339 (net addition of 6,450 QoQ)
- **LTM Attrition:** 12.8%
- **Gross Cash:** $2.66 billion; **Net Cash:** $2.56 billion (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 12 per share

### Highlights
- Advanced AI offerings crossed $100 million per quarter for agentic AI.
- Achieved OpenAI Advanced Partner status, signed a global Premier partnership with Anthropic, and expanded collaboration with NVIDIA, Google Cloud, ServiceNow, and Cohere.
- Acquired HPE's Telco Solutions Group (adding $21 million in revenue for 2 months) and Jaspersoft (full quarter contribution of $15 million).
- Announced plans for an AI data center in Bhubaneswar in partnership with Sarvam and the Government of Odisha; opened new Global Technology Centers in Bhubaneswar and GIFT City, Gujarat; launched an advanced semiconductor lab in Bengaluru with an investment of Rs 185 crores.
- Launched "HCLTech Pulse", a dedicated business unit for fast-growing enterprises with $500 million to $5 billion in annual revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Growth Guidance:** 3% to 4% for Services and 2.5% to 4% for the Company (includes inorganic contribution of 62 bps for Services and 87 bps for Company).
- **FY27 EBIT Margin Guidance:** Maintained at 17.5% to 18.5%.
- Management remains cautious due to a fluid and volatile macro environment despite strong bookings and AI demand.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is prioritized around AI, data, and operating model transformation for efficiency and innovation.
- Growth was broad-based, led by Retail and CPG (up 14% YoY) and Financial Services (up 7.1% YoY).
- Geographic growth: USA up 3.5% YoY, Europe down 0.3% YoY, Rest of World up 11.4% YoY, and India up 21.8% YoY.
- US Department of Labor PERM program suspension is expected to have no material impact on talent plans given low historical application numbers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBIT margin expanded 131 bps QoQ driven by Project Ascend / AI-driven efficiencies (+73 bps), Advanced AI superior margins (+32 bps), SG&A operating leverage (+50 bps), and forex (+23 bps), offset by higher restructuring costs (-22 bps) and HPE acquisition dilution (-25 bps).
- Advanced AI services carry margins 7% to 10% higher than traditional services; Advanced AI IP/platform components carry gross margins upwards of 70%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested Rs 185 crores in an advanced semiconductor lab in Bengaluru.
- Expanded physical footprint with new tech centers in Bhubaneswar and GIFT City.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total DSO including unbilled receivables stood at 84 days (down 2 days QoQ).
- LTM ROIC at 41.2% (up 261 bps YoY); Services ROIC at 49.4%.
- LTM Free Cash Flow to Net Income conversion was 87% (impacted by one-time BAPA tax payment and gratuity trust transfer); expected to revert to 100%+ as one-offs roll over.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macro environment remains fluid, volatile, and dynamic with pressures on discretionary spending in specific telco and manufacturing clients.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhishek Pathak (Motilal Oswal) on AI ecosystem partnerships vs. managed services:** Management stated that value for frontier labs (OpenAI/Anthropic) is largely services-led (FDE motion), with token costs included in pricing for non-linear models.
2. **Ravi Menon (Axis Capital) on Advanced AI margins:** Management clarified that Advanced AI services operate at 7% to 10% higher margins than traditional services, while IP platforms have gross margins above 70%.
3. **Rishi Jhunjhunwala (IIFL Capital) on top client buckets:** Management noted that previously flagged headwinds in 3 top-20 clients have played out, while upselling drove robust additions in $100M+, $50M+, and $20M+ client categories.
4. **Surendra Goyal (Citi) on Software revenue decline vs. ARR growth:** Management explained that reported revenue decline is due to lower multi-year license renewals in the quarter (recognition timing), while ARR (growing 7.8% YoY including Jaspersoft) is the true health metric.
5. **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley) on partnership revenue tracking:** Management noted they do not precisely track ecosystem partner revenue directly, focusing instead on services, annuity, and influenced revenue.

### One-line takeaway
HCLTech delivered a solid Q2 FY27 with 2.3% sequential revenue growth, a 131 bps expansion in EBIT margins to 18.2%, and near-record bookings driven by rapid scaling in Advanced AI services.
