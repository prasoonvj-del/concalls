# KPIT Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · IT & Software*

Here is a concise, factual summary of KPIT Technologies Ltd's Q2 FY27 earnings call.

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed – Growth faced headwinds from a 13% revenue hit across two major client cancellations, balanced by strong traction in off-highway, commercial vehicles, and products.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue Growth:** 6.5% YoY and 1.0% QoQ in INR terms; 0.2% YoY and 0.5% QoQ in constant currency (CC) terms.
* **EBITDA Margin:** 17.8%, expanding 60 bps YoY (up from 17.2%).
* **Segment Revenues:** 
  * Off-Highway & Commercial Vehicles (CV): Up 27% YoY and 17.6% QoQ.
  * Passenger Cars: Down 8% YoY and 4% QoQ (impacted by two top accounts declining over 13% of total revenue).
  * Products Revenue: Up 25% YoY and 5% QoQ.
* **Contract Mix:** Fixed Price projects reached 70% of overall business.
* **AI Revenue:** 9% of total revenues originate from "AI-first" projects.
* **Balance Sheet Assets:** Net cash stood at INR 6.2 billion (down due to INR 1.4 billion dividend, ~INR 1.0 billion acquisition payment for Cymotive, and DSO increasing from 51 to 53 days).

### Highlights
* Growth in off-highway, commercial vehicles, and product sales offset sharp revenue drops from two large clients (one in Japan, one in Europe).
* 89% of new deal wins during the quarter came from targeted focus accounts (primarily across Autonomy, Connected, and IDART).
* Deployed an internal AI product framework ("Beacon"), logging over 5 million transactions, 12,000 engineering agents built, and adoption by 9,000+ users.
* Formed a new joint delivery center in Vietnam partnering with a Chinese Tier-1 supplier to handle global programs for Chinese OEMs outside China.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management reiterated that H2 FY27 growth will be better than H1 FY27.
* Sequential revenue growth is expected to return in Q4 FY27 as client cancellations bottom out over the next 2 quarters.
* EBITDA margins are projected to expand incrementally every quarter, moving toward historical 20–21% ranges early next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Passenger Vehicle Headwinds:** European OEMs face structural margin pressures and budget cuts, delaying major next-gen platform deployments (Gen 4 software shifted from 2028 out to 2029–2030).
* **Commercial Vehicle Shift:** Off-highway and truck OEMs are entering Software-Defined Machine (SDM) cycles, replicating the transformation passenger vehicles underwent four years ago. 
* **Regional Dynamics:** US market remains resilient across all 3 verticals; India drives strong expansion within SIMA (Southeast Asia, India, Middle East).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Margin expansion (up 60 bps YoY) was driven by favorable business mix via higher product contribution.
* Management increased internal R&D expenditure by a couple of million dollars during the quarter.
* Implemented wage hikes for grades A through E, with remaining grades planned for Q3/Q4.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Expanding operational presence in Vietnam to serve as a hub for Southeast Asia, leveraging regional software talent and local OEM manufacturing presence.
* Initiating expansion into non-automotive adjacencies—specifically **Energytech** and **Deep Tech**—leveraging existing domain assets over the next 2–3 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Pre-tax EBITDA-to-operating cash flow conversion stood at 57% in H1 FY27 (impacted by DSO expansion).
* Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) increased from 51 days to 53 days QoQ.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **Client Concentration Drag:** Top 2 client cutbacks generated a 13%+ revenue hit that will take 2 more quarters to fully bottom out.
* **QORIX Delays:** Mainstream middleware market adoption delayed as OEM clients pushed Gen 4 rollout schedules from 2028 to 2029–2030.
* **Employee Attrition:** People attrition ticked up to mid-teens levels in Q2 FY27 (though management expects a sharp reduction in Q3).

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Rishabh Rathi (Goldman Sachs):** *Asked regarding the geographic breakdown and customer scale-downs.*  
  *Management Response:* Confirmed degrowth in Europe and Japan/Korea/China (JKC) was directly linked to program cancellations at two specific top accounts; expects both accounts to bottom out in 2 quarters.
* **Nitin (Analyst):** *Inquired about vendor consolidation in Europe and QORIX timeline.*  
  *Management Response:* Anticipates European vendor consolidation to conclude by Q1 next year. Confirmed QORIX program adoption timelines have been deferred out to 2029–2030 by OEMs, prompting KPIT to explore alternate non-auto adjacencies for the stack.
* **Rahul Jain (Dolat Capital):** *Asked if current fixed-cost structures require optimization given lower revenue growth.*  
  *Management Response:* Acknowledged ongoing optimization by shifting client engagement delivery mixes from high-cost onsite locations toward lower-cost offsite/India centers, driven by increased AI-led automation and customer cost pressures.
* **Vaibhav Yadav (Analyst):** *Asked whether the European recovery timeline has been delayed further.*  
  *Management Response:* Stated European passenger auto recovery will be long-drawn, but KPIT expects to gain market share via lower-cost India delivery and AI transformations starting H1 of next year.

### One-line takeaway
KPIT relies on off-highway growth, products, and AI solutions to stabilize revenue while managing structural spend cuts and platform delays across European passenger car accounts.
