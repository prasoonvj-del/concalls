# KPIT Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue growth was muted due to a sharp decline in two major accounts, though offset by growth in products, off-highway, and commercial vehicles.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth: 6.5% YoY and 1.0% QoQ in rupee terms; 0.2% YoY and 0.5% QoQ in constant currency (CC) terms.
- Off-highway and Commercial growth: 27% YoY and 17.6% QoQ.
- Products revenue growth: 25% YoY and 5% QoQ.
- EBITDA margin: Improved by 60 bps YoY from 17.2% to 17.8%.
- Net cash: INR 6.2 billion.
- DSO: Increased from 51 days to 53 days.
- Fixed price projects: Moved to about 70%.
- AI platform: More than 9,000 users, over 5 million transactions, and 12,000 engineering agents built; ~9% of revenues from AI-first projects.

### Highlights
- Two top accounts (one in Japan, one in Europe) experienced a sharp decline due to program cancellations/expense cuts, impacting total revenue by 13%.
- Off-highway and commercial vehicles, along with products, helped offset the top account drop.
- 89% of wins came from target accounts.
- Increments were done for initial grades (A to D/E) during the quarter; employee attrition was in the mid-teens but management expects it to drop substantially next quarter.
- Initiated two new adjacencies: Energytech and Deep tech.
- Expanded presence in Vietnam (center with a Chinese Tier 1 and support for an OEM) to serve as a hub for Southeast Asia and global Chinese OEM programs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated that H2 FY27 will be better than H1.
- Expects growth to resume/rebound starting Q4 FY27.
- Expects the two declining major client accounts to bottom out in the next couple of quarters.
- Supply chain consolidation in Europe is expected to meaningfully conclude by the end of Q1 next year, creating opportunities in H1 of next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- European passenger car OEMs continue to struggle and cut costs, but this creates opportunities for vendor consolidation and cost disruption via AI transformation.
- Truck and off-highway markets are seeing growth, particularly in the US.
- Software-defined vehicles (SDV) are transitioning towards AI-defined vehicles (AIDV).
- High-priority spending areas include digital cockpit, diagnostics, after-sales transformation, cybersecurity, and autonomous driving (NOA/robotaxis).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expansion (60 bps YoY) was driven largely by higher contribution from products.
- R&D investments increased by a couple of million dollars during the quarter.
- Optimization of client engagement mix underway, moving more work remotely to lower-cost centers based on client demand.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital outflows during the half-year included dividend payments (INR 1.4 billion) and an initial acquisition payment for Cymotive (INR 1 billion).
- Expanded footprint in Vietnam for manufacturing support and talent availability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash stands at INR 6.2 billion.
- DSO increased from 51 days to 53 days, impacting the working capital cycle and operating cash flow.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued headwinds and sharp declines in two major client accounts impacting passenger car revenues.
- Sluggishness and margin pressures among European passenger car OEMs.
- Qorix programs/mainstream market timelines pushed out by a couple of years to 2029-2030, delaying loss stabilization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rishabh Rathi (Goldman Sachs) on geographic trends and client declines:** Management clarified that passenger car degrowth is driven by two specific clients (Japan and Europe) which will bottom out in the next couple of quarters, while US and SIMA (Southeast Asia, India, Middle East) are growing.
2. **Ravi (...) on cash flow and product revenue disclosure:** Management attributed lower H1 OCF to increased DSOs and stated that detailed product reporting will be provided early next year.
3. **Nitin (...) on Qorix losses and Vietnam subsidiary:** Management stated Qorix product timelines have shifted to 2029-2030, while the Vietnam center supports a local OEM and a Chinese Tier 1 for global programs outside China.
4. **Ankur (...) on CV segment sustainability:** Management affirmed that the CV segment (off-highway and trucks) is seeing broad-based adoption of software-defined machines (SDM) and will contribute healthily over the medium term.
5. **Rahul Jain (...) on cost optimization:** Management noted ongoing optimization by shifting work to remote and low-cost delivery centers while protecting technology investments.

### One-line takeaway
Despite a 13% revenue drag from two major declining accounts, KPIT reported muted overall growth offset by strength in commercial vehicles and products, and management expects performance to improve through H2 and rebound by Q4.
