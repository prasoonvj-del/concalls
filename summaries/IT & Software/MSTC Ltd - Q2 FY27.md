# MSTC Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever Q2 and H1 financial performance since listing, driven by e-commerce growth and a complete exit from the legacy trading segment.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained double-digit growth aspiration (10% to 20% range).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positively regarding the Mahindra JV (MMRPL posting a positive PAT for the third sequential quarter) and receipt of RBI in-principle approval for TReDS.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New evaluation of proposed coal and mineral exchanges by the Ministries of Coal and Mines.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Received RBI in-principle approval for the TReDS platform as anticipated.

### Key numbers
- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) for H1: Rs 545.80 billion (excluding trading)
- H1 EBITDA: Rs 184.55 crores (vs. Rs 130.71 crores YoY)
- H1 PBT (Standalone): Rs 178.06 crores (vs. Rs 125.79 crores YoY)
- H1 PAT (Standalone): Rs 132.72 crores (vs. Rs 93.47 crores YoY)
- Consolidated H1 PBT: Rs 178.47 crores (vs. Rs 122.16 crores YoY)
- Consolidated H1 PAT: Rs 133.13 crores (vs. Rs 89.84 crores YoY)
- Total Revenue (Standalone H1): Rs 257.20 crores (up 31.25% YoY from Rs 195.96 crores)
- Revenue from operations (Standalone H1): Rs 204.26 crores
- Other income (Standalone H1): Rs 52.94 crores
- Employee benefits expense (Standalone H1): Rs 51.89 crores (vs. Rs 46.34 crores YoY)
- Total expenses (Standalone H1): Rs 79.14 crores (vs. Rs 70.17 crores YoY)
- JV (MMRPL) H1 PAT: Rs 0.82 crores
- Scrap rate increase: 8% to 10% QoQ

### Highlights
- Posted highest-ever Q2 and H1 numbers since listing, with marketing contribution down to zero following the complete exit from trading.
- Received in-principle approval from the RBI for the TReDS platform, subject to conditions and audits.
- Mahindra JV (MMRPL) posted positive H1 profits, marking the third sequential quarter of positive earnings.
- Executed notable auctions including liquor licenses for Karnataka (INR 125 crores), land parcels for Telangana (INR 2,863 crores), an oil rig ($5.8 million), the first coal gasification event, round 6 of the UDAN scheme, tranche 8 of critical mineral blocks, ~4 lakh metric tonnes of chromite, and 1.54 million metric tonnes of iron ore/coal.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a long-term double-digit growth aspiration (broadly 10% to 20%).
- Management expects TReDS platform operations to start upon final authorization and stabilize over the next 2 fiscals.
- Management expects to assess EPR revenue flows after operating the platform for 6 to 8 months once launched.
- Management expects to conclude evaluations on setting up coal and mineral exchanges within 3 to 4 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Scrap prices have hardened over the last 2 quarters (seeing 8% to 10% QoQ increases), and iron ore volumes have increased significantly by more than 10% to 15%.
- Evaluating opportunities in proposed coal and mineral exchanges by the Ministries of Coal and Mines, given legacy expertise.
- B2B travel portal is adding corporate clients and expanding steadily; B2C segment is targeted for rollout in 3 to 6 months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone total expenses increased by 12.78% YoY to Rs 79.14 crores, while revenue grew by 31.25%.
- Employee benefits expense accounted for roughly 12% of revenue, standing at Rs 51.89 crores.
- EBITDA percentage of total income peaked at 71.75%.
- Management notes margins fluctuate cyclically (driven by scrap prices and geopolitical factors) and average out over 1 to 1.5 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company continues to operate on an asset-light e-commerce model.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents temporarily increased due to pending creditor payments at quarter-end, but management notes it normalizes around Rs 900 crores to Rs 1,000 crores.
- Marketing contribution/trading business fully exited, leaving zero risk-free trading exposure.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory approvals (CPCB/Ministry for EPR) are outside management control and create timeline uncertainties.
- Scrap and steel sectors are highly cyclical, making margin and volume trends non-linear.
- Potential long-term impact on existing coal auction volumes if future coal exchange regulations mandate all trading through exchanges.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Naman (Shanghvi Family Office) asked about EPR regulatory delay stages and phase-wise launches.** Management stated that delays involve both regulatory and stakeholder consultations outside MSTC's scope, and 5 notified categories will expand over time.
- **Saurabh Ginodia (SMIFS Limited) asked about coal exchange operationalization timelines and competitive advantage against NSE.** Management stated they are evaluating market volumes and revenue streams with a 3 to 4-month timeline to reach a conclusion, leveraging MSTC's legacy domain expertise.
- **Shashwat Jalan (NV Alpha) asked about revenue quantification for coal volumes and cash position increases.** Management clarified that coal is commingled in the mineral segment (contributing 20-25% broadly) and cash spikes are temporary timing differences normalizing to Rs 900-1,000 crores.
- **Shantanu Basu (SMIFS) asked about TReDS market size and Mahindra JV EBITDA run rate.** Management noted TReDS will share space with 5 other platforms and scale based on MSME policies, while the JV maintains positive profitability conditional on firm scrap prices.
- **Bharat Shah (Bharat Shah Capital) challenged the episodic nature of platform transactions versus a predictable growth engine.** Management defended that MSTC is a sector-agnostic digital e-commerce platform provider leveraging strong B2B PSU moats and a large buyer base.

### One-line takeaway
MSTC delivered record H1 financial performance with zero trading revenue, supported by robust scrap/mineral auctions, positive JV contributions, and a newly secured RBI in-principle approval for TReDs.
