# Meta Infotech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasizes that the "era of negativity" is ending, citing a strengthened foundation and reiterating the 4x PAT growth target by FY29.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 89.5 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 210 crore (H1 FY26), a decline of 57% YoY.
*   **Revenue (Sequential):** Rs 89.5 crore vs Rs 59.9 crore (H2 FY26), a growth of 49% QoQ.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 7.8 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 15.8 crore (H1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8.7% (H1 FY27) vs 7.5% (H1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA (Sequential):** Rs 7.8 crore vs Rs 2.3 crore (H2 FY26); margin improved from 3.8% to 8.7%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 4.9 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 10.4 crore (H1 FY26).
*   **PAT (Sequential):** Rs 4.9 crore vs Rs 40 lakh (H2 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 92.7 crore as of September 2026.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Management is consciously discontinuing lower-margin product sales to a leading bank to prioritize higher-margin cybersecurity services.
*   **Revenue Mix:** Shifted to ~71% product / 29% services (historically 85% product / 15% services).
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Operations expanded from Mumbai to Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Delhi, and the Gulf region.
*   **Vendor/Client Traction:** Onboarded 3 new vendors and 12 new clients in H1 FY27.
*   **Awards:** Received "Best Technical Partner of the Year 2025" and "Best Deal of the Year" at the M. Tech Partner Conference.
*   **Record Check:** The strategic shift away from low-margin product sales to services, noted in our records, is confirmed as the primary driver for the current revenue decline and margin improvement.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **PAT Target:** Management reiterated the goal of 4x PAT growth from FY26 levels by FY29.
*   **Growth:** Management expects 30%+ YoY growth in PAT going forward.
*   **Recurring Revenue:** Targeting 50%-70% YoY growth in recurring business; aims for net profit to reach 50% of cost this year, 75% next year, and 100% in two years.
*   **Managed Services:** Recurring managed services revenue stood at Rs 21.5 crore as of H1; management expects to add ~Rs 7 crore annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Cybersecurity:** Demand remains robust due to rapid digitization and AI adoption.
*   **AI Security:** Management views AI security as an emerging opportunity; currently working on deals but has not closed any yet.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margin expansion is driven by the strategic shift toward higher-margin cybersecurity services and improved revenue quality.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Expanded presence to 5 locations in India and 6 in the Gulf.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Client Concentration:** Management acknowledged the risk of relying on a few large clients; strategy is to diversify to 50 top accounts in Mumbai and 15 accounts each in other regions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What are the key growth drivers for the next 2-3 years?** A: Management cited the shift to services, geographic expansion (5 locations in India, 6 in Gulf), and vendor partnerships (10+ added post-IPO).
*   **Q: How is Meta positioning for AI security?** A: Management stated AI security is an additional offering within their services portfolio, not a primary standalone focus.
*   **Q: How should investors view the revenue trajectory?** A: Management advised focusing on PAT rather than top-line, as they are intentionally sacrificing low-margin product revenue.
*   **Q: How does Meta compete with large integrators like IBM/Wipro?** A: Management claimed large competitors are failing on quality and that Meta is successfully replacing them in several customer accounts.
*   **Q: Is the 4x PAT target still on track?** A: Management confirmed they are on track, citing the recurring nature of their sustenance business.

### One-line takeaway
Management is prioritizing margin-accretive services over top-line growth, aiming for a 4x PAT expansion by FY29 through geographic and vendor diversification.
