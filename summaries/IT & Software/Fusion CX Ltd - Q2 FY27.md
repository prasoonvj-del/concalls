# Fusion CX Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and PAT growth accompanied by strategic global acquisitions and post-IPO momentum, despite minor margin contraction from upfront training costs.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 5,253 million (+22.1% YoY, +7.1% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** INR 976 million (+15.2% YoY); EBITDA Margin: 18.6% (-110 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 586 million (+31.3% YoY); PAT Margin: 11.2% (+77 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 10,158 million (+20.4% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 1,927 million (+27.7% YoY); Margin: 19%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 1,142 million (+56.3% YoY); Margin: 11.2%
- **Return Ratios (H1 FY27):** Annualized ROCE of 40.1%, ROE of 36.9%
- **DSO:** 73 days (improved from 80 days YoY)

### Highlights
- First earnings conference call post-IPO listing on October 22, 2026.
- Completed two acquisitions in H1 FY27: VAP in Australia (August 2026) and impacx in Western Germany (~EUR 12 million annual revenue) in November 2026.
- Declared an interim dividend of 75% (INR 0.75 per equity share).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management did not provide specific numerical guidance, but stated they are chasing growth and remain committed to maintaining and accelerating revenue, EBITDA, and PAT.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management views AI as a major tailwind, driving demand across omnichannel CX and the AI data infrastructure business (Annotera and Roborax).
- Nearshore and onshore contact center demand is accelerating, particularly in health plan, AI data, telecom, and BFSI verticals.
- Voice-to-non-voice mix stands at 70% voice and 30% non-voice.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin contracted 110 bps YoY to 18.6% due to manpower training costs incurred for two top-5 clients ahead of Q3 ramp-ups. Management stated margins will normalize as training concludes.
- Employee costs remain stable within historical ranges based on global delivery spread.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Approximately 40% of IPO proceeds deployed toward debt reduction and the impacx acquisition.
- Exploring opening up India delivery centers to service the newly acquired Australian market alongside existing delivery out of the Philippines.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- DSO reduced to 73 days compared to 80 days in the previous year.
- Balance IPO proceeds remain allocated for platform upgrades, overseas debt repayment, general corporate purposes, and future acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Upfront training investments for major client ramp-ups can temporarily compress quarterly EBITDA margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankur Pant (IIFL Capital)** asked about margin impact and Australian market rationale. Management explained training costs are temporary ahead of Q3 production, and Australia targets high-paying BFSI/mortgage broking segments.
- **Mayank Kumar (Prasun Exponentials)** asked about AI tailwinds and acquisition valuations. Management stated acquisitions were struck at 4x-4.5x EBITDA multiples in line with their playbook.
- **Naveen Vyas (Microsec Wealth)** asked about organic growth moderation and segment mix. Management clarified H1 organic growth was 12.7%, voice/non-voice is 70/30, and AI data infrastructure contributes ~5%.
- **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama Equities)** asked about Q3 seasonality. Management stated Q3 is seasonally stronger, driven by healthcare promotions and festive retail demand.

### One-line takeaway
Fusion CX posted robust Q2 revenue and PAT growth backed by global expansion and proprietary AI tools, with margins temporarily impacted by upfront training expenses for major client ramp-ups.
