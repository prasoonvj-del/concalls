# Mobavenue AI Tech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 41.3% YoY, stable EBITDA margins above 20%, and successful international expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 767 million (up 41.3% YoY, 5.4% QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,496 million (up 48.6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 660 million (up 47.3% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 21.1% (up 80 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 113 million (up 54.8% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 14.7% (up 130 bps YoY).
*   **Revenue per Outcome (RPO):** Increased >10% YoY.
*   **Direct Client Contribution:** 64.7% of Q2 FY27 revenue.
*   **International Revenue Contribution:** 23.8% (up from 12.3% in Q2 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Business Model:** Operates as an AI-native global ad tech and consumer growth platform using an "A3 framework" (Awareness, Acquisition, Activation).
*   **Operational Scale:** Processes ~1.4 billion signals daily; reaches ~2.7 billion devices globally; decision-making speed <15 milliseconds.
*   **Client Base:** Serves ~170 brands across 13 countries; key sectors include Quick Commerce, BFSI, Fintech, Super Apps, entertainment, and travel.
*   **Corporate Action:** Initiated merger of the wholly-owned Indian subsidiary into Mobavenue AI Tech Limited to simplify corporate structure.
*   **Technology:** Launched "Mobavenue Neural Engine" earlier this year; PiiX (AI-powered Apple ads platform) gaining traction.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Long-term Philosophy:** Management reiterated the "Rule of 50" (annual revenue growth >30% with EBITDA margins ≥20%).
*   **Confidence:** Management expressed confidence in achieving/exceeding the 30% growth benchmark for the year, citing strong H1 performance and international expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Trends:** Digital advertising is shifting from media buying to measurable business outcomes; AI is increasingly central to campaign planning and real-time decision-making.
*   **International Strategy:** Expanding in the US, UK, Singapore (regional hub for Southeast Asia), Philippines, Indonesia, and Thailand.
*   **Wallet Share:** In India, the company typically holds 3-4% of a client's overall marketing spend, or 7-10% of "alternate" (non-Google/Meta) spend.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Supply-end Data Cost:** Improved to 58.5% of revenue (vs 60.4% in Q1 FY27 and 61.5% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Employee Benefit Expenses:** Rs 99 million (up 62.3% YoY), reflecting deliberate investment in talent and global teams.
*   **Other Expenses:** Rs 57 million (up 17.9% YoY); improved to 7.2% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Asset-light Model:** Management emphasized an asset-light operating model to scale across markets with limited incremental infrastructure investment.
*   **International Setup:** Setting up a subsidiary in the US to onboard direct customers; Singapore office operational as a regional hub.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital

*   *None explicitly flagged by management.*

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Confidence in 30% growth guidance?** Management noted H1 growth of 49% and strong RPO trends, expressing confidence in meeting or exceeding the 30% target.
*   **Q: Why did direct client contribution decline?** Management clarified it is not a decline but a result of entering new international markets via agencies/resellers first, with plans to hire direct teams later.
*   **Q: How does PiiX platform work?** Management explained it targets the Apple ecosystem (iOS users), which is growing in India and dominant in developed markets; it is a utility to help advertisers scale on Apple Search Ads.
*   **Q: Competitive differentiation?** Management cited their outcome-based charging model, speed of integration (3-5 days), and proprietary AI infrastructure as key differentiators.
*   **Q: Potential for ads on chatbots?** Management confirmed they are in the process of integrating these systems to tap into new inventory pipes.

### One-line takeaway
Mobavenue delivered strong profitable growth in Q2, successfully leveraging its AI-native platform to scale internationally while maintaining EBITDA margins above 20%.
