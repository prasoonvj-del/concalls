# Affle 3I Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - 14th consecutive quarter of sequential top-line growth with highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and consumer conversions.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 7.47 billion, up 20.4% YoY and 3.1% QoQ.
- **EBITDA:** INR 1.68 billion, up 20.0% YoY and 4.0% QoQ.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22.4% (stable/flat compared to previous quarters).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 1.28 billion, up 21.7% YoY and 7.5% QoQ.
- **PAT Margin:** 16.6% (improved 60 bps QoQ).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** INR 1.58 billion, up 22.1% YoY and 6.6% QoQ.
- **India & Emerging Markets Revenue Growth:** 20.2% YoY (contributing 72.2% of revenue).
- **Developed Markets Revenue Growth:** 20.7% YoY (contributing 27.8% of revenue).

### Highlights
- 95% of revenues grew over 25% YoY after adjusting for headwinds in specific customer segments like RMG and FinTech.
- Acquired strategic assets of AdColony to enhance publisher ecosystem and audience intelligence, aiming to activate over 100,000 mobile apps and reach over 500 million connected devices in Developed Markets this year.
- Platforms won 18 awards at the Connected TV Asia Symposium 2026, recognized at e4m India Digital Marketing Awards 2026, and received the Gold Award at the Agency Reporter Programmatic Asia Awards 2026.
- Technology IP portfolio expanded to over 300 unique enforceable patent claims spanning fraud intelligence, precision targeting, and AI-native ads.
- Featured five customer-approved case studies in the earnings presentation across QSR, travel, and quick commerce verticals (including expanding in Netherlands).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is progressing on a larger inorganic acquisition with third-party due diligence advisors, aiming to close by early 2027.
- Confident in sustaining medium-term internal organic growth expectations pegged at 25%, with external analyst models around 20%.
- Management aims to achieve its 10x growth vision (targeting $1 billion revenue) in the next few years (hoping for a 5-year timeline similar to past execution).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand environment across key markets remains encouraging, especially looking ahead to the festive quarter.
- Time spent on digital content consumption is increasing globally, with no observed negative impact from slowing smartphone adoption or device replacement cycles.
- Consumer platform approach allows engagement across mobile, CTV, and autonomous/agentic intelligent connected devices (AICDs).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inventory and data costs stood at 63.2% of revenue from operations, remaining broadly in line with previous quarters.
- Employee costs increased 3.4% QoQ due to annual release of appraisals and bonuses in a few geographies, and 7.8% YoY.
- Other expenses stood at 5.6% of revenue, increasing marginally by INR 2.3 million QoQ due to business promotion for Developed Markets expansion.
- Effective tax rate for the full year is 18.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Developed Markets expansion is a primary focus area, targeting wallet share of large global ad budgets.
- AdColony asset integration is being executed organically via existing teams without humongous ongoing capital investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating Cash Flow (OCF) to PAT ratio was lower in Q1 due to upfronting of collections in March ahead of FY2026 year-end (which resulted in 110% OCF to PAT for FY2026); management expects OCF to PAT to normalize to 80%-85% by Q3.
- No material changes in collection risk.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory and macroeconomic headwinds impacted specific customer segments like RMG and FinTech.
- Currency fluctuations and cross-currency ROI adjustments impacted short-term inventory costs/take rates.
- Bobble (invested asset of INR 136 crore) entered insolvency proceedings initiated by its management; Affle's management and board are challenging this via courts/arbitration due to denied inspection rights, and auditors/board will test impairment once NCLT appeals are decided.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Karan Taurani (Elara Capital) on geopolitical/macro impacts and gross margins:** Management stated that 95% of revenues growing at 25%+ gives confidence in the underlying business, and margin expansion is expected within the fiscal year through Developed Markets scaling.
2. **Vijit Jain (Citigroup) on AdColony integration and AI cost dynamics:** Management clarified that AdColony app unlocks are part of regular organic business development using existing teams without heavy capital outlay; on AI, management emphasized strategic positioning around agentic devices and human vs. non-human data distillation.
3. **Dipak Saha (Ashika Institutional Equities) on CTV unit economics:** Management explained that Affle is a consumer-centric platform rather than a commoditized inventory seller, utilizing algorithms to show ads on mobile, CTV, or AICDs based on consumer attention.
4. **Anmol Garg (DAM Capital) on Developed Markets growth potential:** Management pushed back on the idea of DM growth slowing down for Affle, noting that Affle operates on a small base there with a differentiated model targeting 20%+ growth.
5. **Onkar Ghugardare (Shree Investments) on the upcoming large M&A and 10x timeline:** Management reiterated that any M&A will be bottom-line accretive, maintain growth velocity above 20%, and keep the organization on track for the $1 billion revenue milestone in the next few years.
6. **Sanjay Ladha (Bastion Research) on Bobble insolvency:** Management clarified that Bobble's insolvency is seen as a move by its management to avoid court orders regarding inspection rights; auditors stated impairment will be tested once NCLT proceedings conclude.

### One-line takeaway
Affle reported its 14th consecutive quarter of sequential top-line growth with a 20.4% YoY revenue increase to INR 7.47 billion and 20% EBITDA growth, while advancing on strategic Developed Market integrations and upcoming M&A.
