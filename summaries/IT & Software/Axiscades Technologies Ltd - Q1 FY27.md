# Axiscades Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest revenue in company history, strong ongoing business performance, and structured transformation via divestments and acquisitions.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenues: Rs 346 crore, up 42% YoY and 27% QoQ.
- Continuing operations revenue: Rs 183 crore, up 94% YoY (from Rs 94 crore in Q1 FY26).
- Reported EBITDA (combined): Rs 27.9 crore.
- Reported PAT loss: Rs 14.8 crore (vs PAT of Rs 20.9 crore in Q1 FY26).
- Normalized EBITDA (continuing/combined adjusted): Rs 41 crore at an 11.8% margin (up from 20.5% YoY - *note: transcript states YoY comparison as 20.5%, likely referring to absolute or prior normalized base*).
- Normalized PBT: Rs 23.1 crore; Normalized PAT: Rs 20.2 crore.
- Defense revenue: Rs 125 crore, up 112% YoY; underlying EBITDA of Rs 13 crore (up 15% YoY).
- XiDA (ESAI) revenue: Rs 49.5 crore, up 63% YoY at a 33% EBITDA margin.
- Aerospace manufacturing revenue (Q1): Rs 6.1 crore.
- ADD Solutions EBITDA loss: Rs 4.8 crore; PAT loss: Rs 6.7 crore.
- Cash on balance sheet: Rs 81 crore, up 78%.
- Property, plant and equipment increase: Rs 40 crore in one quarter.
- Akkodis divestment value: US$237 million (~Rs 2,256 crore); Phase 1 target cash Rs 190 crore (August 31st), Phase 2 target cash Rs 525 crore (November 30th). Estimated extraordinary gain of ~Rs 1,255 crore.
- Assured defense forecast visibility: Rs 4,500+ crore (specifically Rs 4,557 crore).
- FY27 pro forma continuing revenue guidance: Rs 1,377 crore; Pro forma EBITDA target: Rs 270 crore.

### Highlights
- Consolidated reported the highest revenue in the company’s history, alongside a net loss driven by transition accounting (Ind AS 105) and overlap of costs.
- Engineering Services business divested to Akkodis; classified under discontinued operations.
- ADD Solutions (European unit) designated as non-core and loss-making, with an exit action plan targeting Q4 FY27.
- XiDA headquartered out of the US to capture proximity to technology, capital, and resources; added two marquee global technology customers (semiconductor equipment manufacturer and a global AI/hyperscale technology company).
- Aerospace manufacturing capability strengthened via non-binding offer for an AS9100 certified precision manufacturing company (annualized FY27 revenue ~Rs 180 crore, EBITDA Rs 39 crore at 22% margin, expected completion in Q2).
- Infrastructure setup progressing: Devanahalli AeroLand Complex (DAL) fully functional; Devanahalli Atmanirbhar Complex (DAC) space manufacturing facility under construction; Missile Atmanirbhar Complex (MAC) in Hyderabad ground broken in July; Center for Advanced Manufacturing (CAM) in Devanahalli planned (240,000 sq. ft. on 20 acres).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reaffirmed FY27 guidance on continuing operations pro forma basis: Rs 1,377 crore revenue and Rs 270 crore EBITDA.
- Expects PAT to be broadly around 50% of EBITDA (~Rs 135 crore).
- Target to replace divested aerospace services EBITDA by Q4 FY27 via organic growth and acquisitions, aiming for a Rs 375 crore revenue and Rs 84 crore EBITDA annualized run rate in aerospace by Q4 FY27, scaling to Rs 1,000 crore by FY29.
- Defense segment expected to grow at over 75% YoY over the next several years.
- XiDA business expected to grow by over 100% this year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global OEMs are looking at India as a source for global supply chains in precision manufacturing and electronics.
- Defense pipeline exceeds Rs 24,000 crore, converging into assured forecast visibility.
- Directed energy weapons and missile systems seeing strong traction (transfer of technology for 30-kilowatt laser directed energy weapon selected/approved; development/production orders for anti-tank and BrahMos missiles).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee cost fell from 53% of revenue to 44% of revenue.
- One-off provisions totaling Rs 13.1 crore taken in EBITDA (Rs 9.62 crore receivable provisioning on aged defense transaction, Rs 3.5 crore hedge provision from unwinding positions).
- Transaction cost of Rs 21.81 crore recorded below EBITDA as an exceptional item (half of estimated Rs 45 crore).
- Finance cost: Rs 8.9 crore; Depreciation: Rs 8.8 crore in the continuing business.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CapEx deployment will match inflows from divestments, supplemented by short-term bridge financing of Rs 100-150 crore if needed.
- Facilities expanding across DAC, MAC, and CAM complexes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Divestments executed on a cash-free, debt-free basis.
- Cash rose 78% to Rs 81 crore.
- Out of Rs 142 crore deferred revenue from FY26, over Rs 60 crore recovered in Q1, with the balance planned across Q2 and Q3.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- ADD Solutions continuing to drag with a Rs 4.8 crore EBITDA loss and Rs 6.7 crore PAT loss.
- Accounting treatment and cost overlap during the transition phase impacted reported profitability.
- Inherent binary risk of M&A execution for non-binding acquisition pipeline.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar asked about the rationale behind divesting the high-EBITDA aerospace services business rather than keeping both services and manufacturing.** Management (Mukund Santhanam & KP Mohanakrishnan) explained that the strategy is to shift from services to high-margin, sticky product and solutions, funding the transition and manufacturing build-out without equity dilution.
2. **Kaushik Mohan inquired about normalized PAT expectations for the full year.** Management stated normalized PAT is expected to be around 50% of the Rs 270 crore EBITDA target (~Rs 135 crore).
3. **Praful Rai asked about the execution timeline for the Rs 4,500+ crore defense order book.** Sharadhi Babu clarified the timeline is primarily across 3 years up to FY30, with some spillover into the fourth year, alongside 75% YoY defense revenue growth in FY27.
4. **Mahek Talati asked for updates on Project Kusha and LUH Maritime orders.** Sharadhi Babu confirmed engagement on multiple modules for Kusha and stated the Rs 150-170 crore visibility for LUH Maritime is backed by confirmed orders.
5. **Mayur Parkeria asked about strategic partnerships/JVs at the subsidiary level with OEM partners.** Mukund Santhanam stated discussions are ongoing with existing and new OEM clients, though it is currently early to provide specific timelines.
6. **Balasubramanian asked about CapEx guidance and deployment.** Mukund Santhanam and Shashidhar SK explained that CapEx will align with divestment inflows, backed by a temporary bridge loan facility of Rs 100-150 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Axiscades reported its highest-ever consolidated revenue driven by a 94% YoY jump in continuing operations, while absorbing transition and one-off costs as it pivots toward a product, solutions, and manufacturing model funded by major asset divestments.
