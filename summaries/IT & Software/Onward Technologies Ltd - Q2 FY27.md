# Onward Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record revenue, expanded margins, and upgraded FY27 guidance based on strong H1 execution.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 170.2 crore (+22.4% YoY, +13.9% QoQ).
*   **Total Income:** Rs 171.2 crore.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 26.6 crore (+33.7% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.6%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 16.8 crore (+41.2% YoY).
*   **$1M+ Customers:** Increased from 16 to 18.
*   **Net Hiring (H1):** 430+ employees.

### Highlights
*   Highest ever quarterly revenue achieved.
*   Renewed a 3-year digital engineering engagement with a global construction and mining leader, valued at ~Rs 44.5 crore (contract value increased >70% vs previous term).
*   Recognized Rs 2.9 crore as R&D credit (UK-based benefit for FY26).
*   Growth driven by deeper engagement with strategic customers, improved utilization, and strong offshore execution.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue Growth:** Management upgraded guidance to 15% to 18% (previously implied lower).
*   **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Management expects 13.8% to 14.3%.
*   Management aims for $10 million in recurring annual revenue per customer (up from the $1 million base).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong demand in AI data center space, specifically in power management, cooling systems, and intelligent industrial platforms.
*   Transportation & Mobility (T&M) vertical showing recovery in automotive and growth in rail.
*   Healthcare (HCLS) vertical is growing from a small base; management expects a trajectory similar to the Industrial segment.
*   Management noted that Onward Technologies has no India-based customers; revenue is derived entirely from US and Europe.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margin expansion attributed to higher utilization of offshore engineers, improved offshore leverage, and delivery discipline.
*   Depreciation increased due to the purchase of an office in Worli, Mumbai, and hardware investments for new design centers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Opened 4th design center in Pune (expected to reach full capacity by Nov/Dec 2026).
*   Signed lease for additional seats in Pune to support capacity buildup.
*   Chennai AI lab expected to be operational by Q4 or early 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **DSO:** 80 days (management expects normalization to 65-70 days in Q3/Q4).
*   Unbilled receivables increased in H1 due to delayed POs and customer payments (now rectified).
*   Management stated the company holds "substantial cash reserves."

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Management expressed caution regarding US visa/PERM regulations impacting the on-site delivery model.
*   **Execution:** Management identified rapid scaling and hiring as the primary execution challenges.
*   **Competition:** The hiring market is becoming increasingly competitive.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the R&D credit?** A: Management explained it is a standard UK government benefit for R&D investment; it was material enough to report as a special line item.
*   **Q: Why the guidance upgrade?** A: Management cited strong H1 execution and improved visibility from existing North American customers.
*   **Q: AI impact on revenue?** A: Management stated AI is currently a productivity enabler, not a workload reducer; customers are cautious about putting IP on AI systems.
*   **Q: Hiring strategy?** A: Management clarified they do not hire freshers; they rely on lateral hiring and have 90+ internal recruiters on payroll.
*   **Q: Why the increase in DSO?** A: Management attributed the 80-day DSO to rapid growth and delayed POs, confirming the issue is rectified.

### One-line takeaway
Onward Technologies delivered a strong quarter with record revenue and margin expansion, supported by a strategic pivot toward larger, long-term digital engineering engagements in the US and Europe.
