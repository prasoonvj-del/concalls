# Millworks Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue shifted to H2 due to accounting timing and defense approval delays, leading to an operating loss, though management expects strong H2 recovery.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): INR 17.37 crores
- Gross margin: ~37%
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): INR 1.81 crores
- Net loss (H1 FY27): INR 1.83 crores
- Employee costs (H1 FY27): INR 4.46 crores
- Finance cost (H1 FY27): INR 1.81 crores
- Depreciation (H1 FY27): INR 1.46 crores
- Inventory (as of Sep 30, 2026): INR 28.34 crores (up from INR 11.47 crores in March)
- Net worth: INR 232.79 crores
- Debt-to-equity: 0.15x
- IPO proceeds utilized for working capital: INR 61.94 crores out of INR 81.50 crores
- IPO proceeds spent on machinery: INR 28.73 crores out of INR 61.03 crores
- Unutilized IPO proceeds in FDs/bank accounts: INR 59.03 crores
- Trade receivables reduction: ~INR 27.5 crores in H1
- Order book: INR 230 crores (1.5x last year's revenue), including a single export order of INR 111 crores from a defense sector company
- Total trade receivables outstanding: INR 142 crores (including INR 86.88 crores from Quick Pay outstanding for 6-8 months, and INR 55.17 crores/INR 38.11 crores due for more than 6 months)
- Advance paid to Quick Pay: INR 10.59 crores
- Stake in Quick Pay Private Limited: 8%
- Full-year revenue guidance: Crossing INR 200 crores
- FY28 growth guidance: 60% to 70% YoY growth

### Highlights
- Management stated that orders worth ~INR 30 crores were executed in September but billed in October, shifting revenue into H2 due to accounting standards (recognition upon transfer of risks and rewards).
- Indian defense orders moved to H2 due to extended customer and regulatory approval timelines; management clarified that no orders were cancelled.
- Historically, ~70% of revenue comes in H2.
- Unit 4 expansion is underway with more than 17 new machines installed and commissioned.
- Vidwan Aeronautics was made a subsidiary to strengthen aero engine capabilities.
- A 3D printing Centre of Excellence was set up with Markforged, USA; a MoU was signed with TridenTech to co-develop UAV engines.
- Process audits by major aerospace companies are ongoing to transition from Tier 2 to Tier 1 supplier status.
- *Note on Record:* Unlike past calls where capacity additions or delays were recurrent themes, this call specifically detailed the integration of Unit 4 and ongoing aerospace audit preparations for February 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a significantly stronger second half driven by deferred revenue recognition, new capacity utilization, and order execution.
- Full-year FY27 revenue is expected to cross INR 200 crores.
- FY28 YoY revenue growth is guided at 60% to 70%.
- Anodizing plant in Unit 4 to be operational in January 2027; NADCAP certification planned for July/August 2027.
- IRIS rail certification training underway, with certification targeted for May 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Defense remains the primary revenue contributor, followed by rail, aerospace, and semiconductor machinery parts.
- Management aims to move aerospace ahead of rail in the revenue mix over time.
- International export defense orders offer faster cash realization and typically include 30% advance payments.
- Semiconductor machinery parts face a delay due to the lack of a dedicated Class 10,000 clean room facility, which is expected to be ready in Q4 FY27 as new machines arrive.
- Build-to-spec mix is targeted to increase from current levels to 50%-70% over two years, commanding 3% to 5% higher margins than build-to-print.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin compressed modestly to ~37% (compared to ~38% in H2 last year) due to higher raw material prices driven by geopolitical situations and supply chain effects.
- Operating loss was driven by full fixed costs (employee, finance, depreciation) against deferred H1 revenue.
- Raw material costs account for 40% to 45% of product costing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Unit 4 expansion ongoing; 17+ new machines installed.
- Clean room facility for semiconductor machinery parts arriving in December/January, operational in Q4 FY27.
- Anodizing plant installation and trials underway in Unit 4, targeting January 2027 operations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with net worth of INR 232.79 crores and debt-to-equity of 0.15x (holding more cash than debt).
- Trade receivables reduced by INR 27.5 crores in H1, but total receivables remain high at INR 142 crores.
- Export terms tightened to 30% advance upon PO and 30-45 days from invoicing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Auditor issued a qualified opinion regarding INR 86.88 crores outstanding from Quick Pay for 6-8 months and an advance of INR 10.59 crores, citing uncertainty of defense order completion and long receivable days.
- Long gestation periods (8-12 months) and complex inspection criteria (17 inspections) for Indian defense orders strain working capital and cash flows.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Niraj Chhajer (Pransh Business Ventures) on Auditor Qualification and Receivables:** Questioned the INR 86.88 crores outstanding from Quick Pay and INR 10.59 crores advance. *Management answered* that the advance was required for specialized electronic components (like GPS) procured in Quick Pay's name due to defense license requirements, and expects to recover the money in December.
2. **Yogesh Patil (Parami Financial) on Receivable Management:** Asked how the company will control lumpy defense receivables. *Management answered* that they plan to balance Indian defense exposure by increasing focus on international defense exports, which come with advances and faster cash cycles.
3. **Ashok Agarwal (Retail Investor) on Full-Year Guidance:** Asked for FY27 and FY28 revenue guidance. *Management answered* that full-year revenue will cross INR 200 crores and guided for 60%-70% YoY growth for FY28.
4. **Atharva Kolvankar (Analyst) on Build-to-Spec vs Build-to-Print Margins:** Inquired about margin differences. *Management answered* that build-to-spec carries 3% to 5% higher margins than build-to-print, though it involves a 3-to-6-month gestation period.
5. **Chitresh (Analyst) on Auditor Qualified Opinion Details:** Pressed for clarity on why the auditor gave a qualified opinion. *Management answered* that it was due to the uncertainty of defense order completion timing and receivables crossing 180 days, but reiterated confidence in recovering funds by December.

### One-line takeaway
Millworks reported a weak H1 FY27 with an operating loss due to revenue shifting into H2 and delayed Indian defense approvals, but management remains confident of crossing INR 200 crores in revenue for FY27 backed by a INR 230 crore order book and strong export traction.
