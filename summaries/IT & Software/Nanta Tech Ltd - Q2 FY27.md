# Nanta Tech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and EBITDA growth driven by execution in the robotics and AI segment.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 42.6 crore (up 97.7% YoY from Rs 21.5 crore in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 9.9 crore (up 221.2% YoY from Rs 3.1 crore in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 23.3% (up 900 bps YoY from 14.3% in H1 FY26)
- PAT: Rs 5.1 crore (up 131.4% YoY from Rs 2.2 crore in H1 FY26)
- PAT margin: 11.9% (up 170 bps YoY from 10.2% in H1 FY26)
- Order book: Approximately Rs 60 crore at the end of H1
- Robots deployed in H1: Around 200 units (targeting minimum 1,000 units for the full year)

### Highlights
- Secured an AI automation solution worth Rs 6.6 crore for Pointer Insurance (6-month execution), marking entry into the BFSI segment.
- Signed LoI with Connplex Cinemas to deploy 100 AT10 marketing and delivery robots under a Robot-as-a-Service (RaaS) model with an indicative contract of Rs 14.4 crore over 36 months.
- Signed an exclusive MoU with Aimtron Technology Private Limited for joint design, development, and contract manufacturing of Allbotix robots with indicative orders of approximately Rs 10 crore over 15 months, retaining IP ownership.
- Expanded internationally by establishing a wholly owned subsidiary (Nanta Technology FZ-LLC) in Dubai, UAE, and signing an MoU with GM Biovalley for Southeast Asia (Malaysia, Brunei, Singapore).
- Inaugurated flagship experience centers in Ahmedabad and Mumbai (Andheri East).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year FY27 revenue growth of 45% to 50% (revised upward from earlier expectations).
- Full-year FY27 EBITDA margin is expected to be between 20% and 22%.
- Management targets deploying a minimum of 1,000 robots across client sites in FY27 (800+ robots in H2).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Seeing increasing adoption of technology-driven solutions for productivity, cost optimization, and customer enhancement.
- India installed over 10,500 industrial robots in 2025 (15% YoY growth). Global service robotics market projected at USD 107 billion by 2030; global RaaS market expected to grow from USD 2.7 billion to USD 16.54 billion by 2034 (22.3% CAGR).
- India's professional service robotics market projected to reach USD 2.7 billion by 2030 (12.2% CAGR). India expected to be the second-biggest robotics market after China.
- Strong traction in autonomous cleaning robots for factory floors and corporate offices, alongside warehousing and hospitality sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin improvement attributed to a favorable product mix shift toward high-margin robotics and AI solutions compared to the AV business.
- Capex model EBITDA margins average around 30% to 35%; RaaS realization takes around 15 to 16 months on 24 to 48 month contracts.
- AMC and software charges from the second year onwards range from 12% to 20% for hardware and software separately.
- Most components are sourced in-house or via Indian vendors, except for cheap motors and actuators which are imported from China and Taiwan (though Indian alternatives exist at higher costs for defense/government projects).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Aimtron partnership provides an asset-light manufacturing model; management is also planning an in-house robot assembly line before the end of the year.
- RaaS model expected to account for 25% to 30% of deployments this year, with the rest via outright capex sales.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- RaaS model requires higher capex on the book; management is evaluating scaling it carefully.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted that deploying physical AI into existing client ecosystems is time-taking and complex.
- High capex requirements for scaling the RaaS model aggressively.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Garvit Goyal (Serene Alpha) asked about full-year guidance revisions:** Management stated they are very comfortable and confident in achieving 45% to 50% growth for FY27.
- **Aviral Sharma (Finavenue Investments) asked about the split between outright sales and RaaS:** Management noted H1 was roughly 50-50, but H2 will see more outright sales.
- **Achuth (Duchan Capital) asked about the current order book:** Management stated the order book stands at around Rs 60 crore at the end of H1.
- **Nirvan Jain (Saransh Partners) asked about the local value addition via Aimtron:** Management explained that motors and actuators are imported from China/Taiwan, while everything else is sourced cost-effectively in India.
- **Kundan K (Individual Investor) asked about defense entry details:** Management stated they secured a Phase 1 pilot project for defense AI infrastructure and expect larger orders after prototype delivery in December.

### One-line takeaway
Nanta Tech delivered robust H1 FY27 results with 97.7% revenue growth and 221.2% EBITDA growth, underpinned by surging adoption of its Allbotix robotics and AI solutions.
