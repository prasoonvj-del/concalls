# Latent View Analytics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While the company returned to a sequential growth trajectory and saw strong momentum in financial services and industrials, it continues to face macroeconomic headwinds and consolidation/budget cuts in its large technology vertical.

### Key numbers
- **USD Revenue Growth:** 2% QoQ
- **INR Revenue Growth:** 2.5% QoQ (revenue came in at Rs 2.5% higher)
- **Financial Services Vertical Growth:** 3.5% QoQ; expected 45% to 46% YoY growth for FY27
- **Industrials Vertical Growth:** 21.4% QoQ; expected 60% to 65% YoY growth for FY27
- **CPG and Retail Vertical Growth:** 2.7% QoQ; expected to be flattish YoY for FY27
- **Technology Vertical Growth:** Expected negative growth of about 5% YoY for FY27
- **Full Year FY27 Revenue Guidance:** ~$127 million (representing >6% growth YoY over $119.5 million in FY26)
- **Utilization:** 84% (down from 85% in Q1)
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** 21% to 22% band for the medium term
- **Effective Tax Rate (ETR):** Expected to remain in the range of 26% to 28% going forward
- **Anthropic Certifications:** 153 employees certified on Claude solutions
- **Databricks Influence Revenue:** ~$30 million for the full year
- **Deal Wins:** ~10-plus wins reported in Q2 (including 3 in asset/wealth management; largest logo win at ~$850,000)

### Highlights
- Returned to positive sequential growth with broad-based contributions across financial services, industrials, and retail.
- AI continues to be a core growth driver, with significant wins in Agentic AI (e.g., supply chain project for an apparel manufacturer, enterprise data observability program for a financial services company, and legacy code rewriting for a tech company's finance controllership team which cut migration time by 95%).
- Management is constituting a new CTAIO (Chief Technology and AI Officer) office in India to unify IP development, partnerships, AI capabilities, and AI DNA.
- Won industry awards from AIM (Innovative Practice Best Industry Application, Best AI Solution Showcase Technology) and Databricks Service Partners 2026 leadership quadrant.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided to full-year FY27 revenue of $127 million (~6%+ YoY growth).
- Management adjusted the $200 million revenue target timeline; the internal plan and market target is now to reach $200 million by the end of calendar year 2028 (shifted out by about 9 months).
- An Investor Day is planned for the first week of February (post Q3 results) to unveil granular execution plans.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Technology Vertical:** Experiencing headwinds due to customer consolidation and budgetary cuts (especially in gaming and video streaming). Management believes they are close to the bottom of this consolidation.
- **Financial Services:** Wealth and asset management are showing strong momentum.
- **Industrials:** Growth led by auto and auto component manufacturers (poised to cross $4 million on a full-year basis for one large logo and $1 million for another).
- **CPG & Retail:** Retail exhibits strong growth momentum, though overall CPG & retail may be flattish due to post-earnout leadership exits.
- **Healthcare & Life Sciences:** Brought on a senior leader from HCLTech; target is for HLS to be at least 10% ($20 million) of overall revenue by 2028 through organic investments and inorganic moves.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins declined QoQ due to wage corrections (8-10% in India, 4-5% in the U.S. for L4 and above, impacting gross margins by 1% to 1.2%), carrying a sizable bench for delayed large project kickoffs (impacting margins by 0.4%), and deployment of COE engineers onto active engagements.
- PAT margins were higher QoQ due to a one-time deferred tax benefit on ESOPs from leadership hires.
- Adjusted EBITDA metric will be discontinued starting next quarter in favor of reported EBITDA.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding geographic presence into Europe, focusing on automotive, CPG, and retail verticals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific balance sheet, debt, or working capital figures disclosed outside of cash generation commentary.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic sentiment headwinds and midterm elections in the U.S.
- Ongoing consolidation and budgetary cuts in the technology sector impacting key tech accounts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dipesh Mehta (Emkay Global) asked about the visibility to reach the $127M guidance and H2 acceleration.** Management answered that $125M is already signed/locked in, with $2M coming from advanced pipeline conversations, and Q3 is expected to grow 3% to 4% QoQ.
2. **Dipesh Mehta asked about account mining and annuity business strategy.** Sonal Ramrakhiani explained that a structured program across sales and delivery has been rolled out to scale sub-$1M accounts to $5M and $5M to $10M, aiming to reduce deal-cycle dependency.
3. **Aagam Shah asked if the $200M target by FY28 is intact.** Rajan Venkatesan and Sonal Ramrakhiani clarified the timeline has shifted by 9 months to the end of calendar year 2028, with detailed plans to be shared at the February Investor Day.
4. **Srinath (Bellwether Capital) asked about Databricks traction and Anthropic partnership updates.** Rajan Venkatesan stated Databricks influence revenue is at ~$30M, and Sonal Ramrakhiani noted 153 employees have been certified on Anthropic Claude with GTM initiatives underway. Srinath also suggested a buyback, which management did not directly commit to.
5. **Pritesh Thakkar (PL Capital) asked about the margin impact from the project bench.** Rajan Venkatesan quantified the impact at 0.4%, which is expected to be recouped in coming quarters.

### One-line takeaway
LatentView reported a return to sequential growth of 2% in USD terms and guided to $127 million in revenue for FY27, while pushing out its $200 million revenue goal to calendar year 2028 amidst tech sector headwinds.
