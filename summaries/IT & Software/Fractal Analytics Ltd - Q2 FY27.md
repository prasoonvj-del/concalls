# Fractal Analytics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue crossed $100 million for the first time, with strong growth and improved profitability.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 957 crore (up 20% YoY, 5% QoQ; constant currency 10% YoY, 4% QoQ)
- Adjusted EBITDA: 16.2% margin, up 123 bps YoY (adjusted EBITDA grew 30% YoY)
- Net Income: INR 79 crore, up 139% YoY (net income margin 8.3%, up 413 bps YoY); excluding associate loss, net income is INR 102 crore (10.7% margin)
- Gross Margin: 44.0%, down 225 bps YoY
- H1 FY2027 Revenue: INR 1,869 crore (up 20% YoY; constant currency 10%)
- H1 FY2027 Net Income: INR 151 crore (up 114% YoY)
- Cash and cash equivalents: INR 1,728 crore (including IPO proceeds of INR 608 crore)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS): 76
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): 115%

### Highlights
- Revenue crossed $100 million threshold for the first quarter.
- Vertical performance: CPGR up 11% YoY; Healthcare and Life Sciences (HLS) up 79% YoY; BFSI up 30% YoY; TMT grew 6% QoQ (bottomed out); Others up 47% YoY.
- Geographic performance: Americas up 22% YoY; Europe up 20% YoY; APAC up 7% YoY.
- Product revenue reached INR 38 crore (4% of total revenue), running at over INR 150 crore annually (up 39% YoY) with remaining performance obligations (RPO) over INR 200 crore.
- Fractal Alpha segment revenue grew 40% YoY to INR 34 crore with a segment loss of INR 10 crore; closed Pricing One transaction with a European firm to give Asper.ai a foothold in Europe.
- Top 10 client concentration decreased from 53.2% to 51.7% of revenue. Clients with >$20 million revenue increased to six (up from four a year ago).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects gross margins to recover significantly in Q3 and Q4.
- Management expects CPG to grow sequentially in the next and following quarters.
- Management anticipates G&A expenses to grow at a slower rate than revenue, driving continued operating leverage.
- Management expects the Fractal Alpha segment to approach breakeven by the end of the year and break even next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprise AI floodgates have not opened yet, but the company is building capabilities for future scaling.
- Deal sizes are becoming larger due to the complexity of enterprise AI workflows.
- Strong inbound interest from frontier labs and expanding partnerships with Microsoft and AWS.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined 225 bps YoY to 44.0%, impacted by investments in scarce AI engineering talent (-279 bps) and annual merit increases (-216 bps), partially offset by a weaker rupee (+270 bps).
- SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue improved from 27% in Q2 FY2026 to 23.6% in Q2 FY2027.
- R&D investment rose 13% YoY to INR 60 crore (6.3% of revenue), of which INR 40 crore was expensed.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Utilized INR 81 crore from IPO proceeds over the quarter, mostly for R&D.
- Actively pursuing M&A pipeline targeting deep AI engineering talent and industry-specific expertise.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stood at INR 1,728 crore (including INR 608 crore IPO proceeds).
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) increased by 5 days to 76 days due to timing of collections; expected to normalize by year-end.
- H1 FY2027 cash flow from operations was negative INR 24 crore due to variable compensation payouts in Q1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inflation and consolidation in CPGR led to delayed contract signings and slightly muted growth.
- Qure.ai (associate company) share of loss was INR 23 crore for the quarter, impacted by USAID funding cuts affecting TB programs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley) asked about the vertical growth divergence and gross margin impact:** Management explained HLS is a complex, high-investment sector, while CPG faced inflation/consolidation headwinds; the gross margin drop is due to forward-deployed AI engineering investments, expected to recover in Q3/Q4.
- **Aditi Patil asked about CPG sequential decline and G&A outlook:** Management cited seasonality (strong AMJ surge) and macro headwinds for CPG, and confirmed G&A will grow slower than revenue, maintaining operating leverage.
- **Suyash Kela asked what would change if privately held with INR 1,700 crore cash:** Management noted they would lean more aggressively into R&D and FDE talent without worrying about near-term gross margin/utilization hits, but remain committed to balancing growth with public market EBITDA targets (targeting ~22%).
- **Anil Nahata asked about the product revenue hockey stick and Qure.ai volatility:** Management stated product traction and win rates are strong, supporting the long-term target, and explained Qure.ai's fluctuations stem from abrupt changes in US government funding (USAID cuts) under the DOGE program, though the underlying business is rebounding.
- **Mohit Bhalla asked for a service line revenue breakup:** Management provided a qualitative breakdown: ~60% AI transformation/growth, ~35% knowledge/context layer creation, and ~5% redesigning work.

### One-line takeaway
Fractal surpassed $100 million in quarterly revenue with 20% YoY top-line growth and improved profitability, absorbing near-term AI talent investments while positioning itself for enterprise AI acceleration.
