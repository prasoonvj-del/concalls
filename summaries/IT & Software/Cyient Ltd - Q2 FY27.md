# Cyient Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited highest EBIT in 10 quarters, strong order intake, and successful integration of recent acquisitions.

### Key numbers
- **Group Revenue:** $233.9 million (up 7.1% QoQ, 15.3% YoY in constant currency); Rs. 2,235 crore (up 7.7% QoQ, 25.5% YoY).
- **DET Revenue:** $174.2 million (up 7.5% QoQ, 6.3% YoY in constant currency); Rs. 1,664 crore (up 8.1% QoQ, 15.7% YoY). Organic DET revenue: $164.5 million (up 1.6% QoQ, 0.4% YoY in constant currency); Rs. 1,572 crore (up 2.1% QoQ, 9.3% YoY).
- **DET EBIT Margin:** 14.2% (up 107 bps QoQ, 206 bps YoY). Highest in 10 quarters. DET absolute EBIT: Rs. 237 crore (up 16.8% QoQ, 35.3% YoY).
- **Group EBIT Margin:** 10.2% (up 52 bps QoQ, 83 bps YoY).
- **Group PAT:** Rs. 121 crore; Group EPS: Rs. 11.54 (up 11.8% QoQ).
- **DET PAT:** Rs. 153 crore (up 8.7% QoQ, 11.9% YoY); DET EPS: Rs. 14.59 (up 14.2% QoQ, 17.4% YoY).
- **Order Intake:** DET highest-ever Q2 order intake of $158.4 million (up nearly 21% YoY). Technology order intake: $10.3 million (up 39.7% YoY). Semiconductor custom ASIC order intake up >50% QoQ. DLM book-to-bill >1.6x.
- **Segment Revenues:** Semiconductor revenue at $19.3 million (up 8% QoQ; Kinetic contributed $11.9 million). DLM revenue grew >26% YoY in INR. TAO Digital contributed $9.6 million (approx. 5.5% of overall revenue) for one month.
- **Free Cash Flow:** DET free cash flow at Rs. 214 crore (~140% conversion of PAT).
- **Hedges:** Hinge book close to $70 million at an average rate of 95.6.
- **Employee Count:** Group total at approximately 19,500.

### Highlights
- Board declared an interim dividend of Rs. 12 per share.
- Completed the buyback of Rs. 720 crore covering 6.4 million shares.
- Integrated TAO Digital (adding ~3,000 employees and platform software/data engineering capabilities).
- Signed a multi-year framework agreement with a global consumer product leader with potential up to €50 million annually (excluded from order intake).
- Established new business units: Intelligent Engineering Solutions and a CTO office for Horizon 2/3 technologies.
- Launched CYiNGINE (agentic AI-enabled lifecycle engineering platform) and Agentic AI MRO offering.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expressed confidence in sustaining business performance and growth in H2 on both organic and inorganic bases.
- Semiconductor business breakeven remains targeted for FY 2028 (might be deferred by a quarter or two due to supply constraints).
- Ambition for DET is to reach a 70-30 mix of sticky annuity vs. project-based revenue (currently 20-30 percentage points away).
- Effective Tax Rate (ETR) expected to remain around 28% for the rest of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Transportation and mobility grew 4.5% QoQ and 16.5% YoY in constant currency (sixth consecutive quarter of growth), driven by automotive, mobility, and commercial aviation aftermarket services.
- Semiconductor industry experiencing strong demand driven by AI, but supply is tight with foundries running at full utilization, leading to higher wafer prices and longer planning cycles.
- DLM supported by favorable structural drivers (rising electronic content, AI infrastructure demand, supply chain diversification).
- Energy and process industries seeing recovery; nuclear energy spending plans increasing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Wage hikes planned in two phases across Q3 and Q4 with equal P&L impact.
- Cost optimization program underway to offset wage increases and support margins through FY 2027.
- Semiconductor EBIT loss reflects deliberate investments in product R&D (core custom ASIC business is self-sustaining).
- Effective tax rate down 90 bps QoQ to 28.3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- TAO Digital acquisition financed through external commercial borrowings ($120 million debt on books, 5-year loan at SOFR plus spread with interest rate swap). Upfront payment was $130 million out of $218 million total acquisition price.
- Semiconductor fundraising included $20 million debt and $10 million equity (convertible preference shares).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- DET net DSO reduced by 3 days.
- Buyback of Rs. 720 crore reduced cash balance and other income, while increasing EPS.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Semiconductor supply constraints, tight foundries, and rising memory costs affecting consumer/edge AI end demand and weighing on margins in the immediate term.
- Potential headwinds in commercial aviation from aviation fuel prices and inflation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sandeep Shah (Equirus Securities):** Asked about annuity revenue proportion and organic H2 sustainability. Management stated they are 20-30 percentage points away from their 70-30 target and confirmed confidence in H2 organic growth.
2. **Dipesh Mehta (Emkay Global):** Inquired about wage hike phasing and EBIT trajectory. Management noted wage hikes will occur in Q3 and Q4 in two equal phases, offset by cost optimization.
3. **Kawaljeet Saluja (Kotak Securities):** Questioned the €50 million framework agreement inclusion and FX hedge book. Management explained framework agreements are excluded from order intake until SOWs are signed; disclosed hedge book of ~$70 million at an average rate of 95.6.
4. **Vibhor Singhal (Nirmal Bang):** Asked about TAO Digital's margin profile and debt funding. Management stated TAO's EBITDA margins are higher than Cyient DET, and acquisition was funded via $120 million debt (SOFR-linked 5-year loan with swaps).
5. **Shradha Agrawal (Equirus Securities):** Inquired about Semiconductor breakeven timeline given supply tightness. Management confirmed breakeven is still targeted for FY 2028, with a potential delay of a quarter or two.
6. **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities):** Asked about semiconductor slowdown vs. supply constraints. Management clarified there is no demand slowdown; constraints are strictly supply-side.

### One-line takeaway
Cyient delivered a strong Q2 with DET EBIT margins reaching a 10-quarter high of 14.2%, robust order intake, and successful integration of TAO Digital, supporting management's confidence in sustained H2 growth.
