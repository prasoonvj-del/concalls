# Datamatics Global Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and significant margin expansion driven by operational leverage and AI-led service adoption.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 546.6 crore (+11.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 115.5 crore (+30% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 21.1% (+301 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 73.4 crore (+16.1% YoY).
*   **Adjusted EBIT:** Rs 99.3 crore (18.2% margin), excluding TNQTech intangible asset amortization.
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,060.6 crore (+10.7% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   **Digital Operations:** Revenue Rs 314.9 crore (+15.6% YoY); EBIT margin 21.8%.
    *   **Digital Technologies:** Revenue Rs 166.6 crore (+8.8% YoY); EBIT margin 9.5%.
    *   **Digital Experiences:** Revenue Rs 65.1 crore (+0.8% YoY); EBIT margin 12%.
*   **Strategy:** Management has shifted focus from "hunting" new logos to "farming/mining" existing accounts.
*   **Geographic Mix:** US (57%), UK/Europe (24%), India (12%), ROW (7%).
*   **Client Concentration:** Top 5 (29%), Top 10 (39%), Top 20 (53%).
*   **Leadership:** Appointed Dr. Rajesh Gharpure as President and COO.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects FY27 growth to be closer to 9%-9.5% (up from previous "high single digits" guidance).
*   **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to remain steady at current levels (~20-21%).
*   **Long-term:** Management targets Rs 3,000 crore revenue in 3 years, aiming for 15% CAGR (10-12% organic, 3-4% inorganic).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   ~55% of current opportunities are heavily AI-driven.
*   Management stated there is no current impact from client in-housing or Global Capability Centers (GCCs).
*   Digital Technologies segment margins are muted due to R&D investments (Rs 40-50 crore level) and hardware costs in the Fare Collection business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin expansion is primarily attributed to operational leverage in the Digital Operations segment.
*   Annual wage hikes were implemented in Q1 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Headcount is expected to remain stable/flat (around 14,000-15,000) as AI tools drive productivity.
*   Management is in dialogue with companies for potential acquisitions but nothing has matured yet.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Cash/Investments:** Rs 557 crore (down from Rs 710.2 crore in Q1 FY27 due to final TNQTech acquisition payment).
*   **Billed DSO:** 66 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Amortization of intangible assets from the TNQTech acquisition (non-cash charge) will continue until Q3 FY28.
*   Fare Collection business margins impacted by hardware costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Strategy for Rs 3,000 crore target?* A: Management stated it will be 10-12% organic and 3-4% inorganic.
*   *Q: AI revenue contribution?* A: Management does not break this out as AI is bundled into service offerings.
*   *Q: Margin sustainability?* A: Management believes the 21.1% EBITDA margin is sustainable and does not expect significant fluctuations.
*   *Q: Client addition strategy?* A: Management confirmed a shift from hunting (previously 15-18/qtr) to mining existing accounts (6-7/qtr).
*   *Q: Headcount outlook?* A: Management does not expect headcount to increase in proportion to revenue due to AI-driven productivity.

### One-line takeaway
Datamatics delivered a strong quarter with margin expansion and healthy growth, supported by a strategic pivot toward mining existing accounts and leveraging AI for operational efficiency.
