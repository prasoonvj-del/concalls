# Softtech Engineers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasized strong standalone growth, a significant new order win from JNPA, and a robust pipeline, despite temporary consolidated headwinds from overseas investments and geopolitical order deferrals.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone H1 FY27:** Revenue Rs 64.92 crore (+26.1% YoY); EBITDA Rs 21.07 crore (+37.9% YoY); EBITDA margin 32.5% (vs 29.7%); PAT Rs 5.55 crore (+69.5% YoY).
*   **Standalone Q2 FY27:** Revenue Rs 32.70 crore (+27.2% YoY); EBITDA Rs 11.47 crore (+50.8% YoY); EBITDA margin 35.1% (vs 29.6%); PAT Rs 3.63 crore (+120.8% YoY).
*   **Consolidated H1 FY27:** Revenue Rs 64.82 crore (+20.4% YoY); EBITDA Rs 17.04 crore (+18.6% YoY); PAT Rs 1.37 crore (vs Rs 1.44 crore).
*   **Consolidated Q2 FY27:** Revenue Rs 31.53 crore (+17.6% YoY); EBITDA Rs 7.99 crore (+14.4% YoY); EBITDA margin 25.3% (vs 26.0%); PAT Rs 0.21 crore (vs Rs 0.33 crore).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 300 crore.
*   **Sales Pipeline:** Rs 625 crore.

### Highlights
*   Won a Rs 92.95 crore order from Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust (JNPA) for AI-powered digital twin solutions.
*   Recurring revenue grew 46.2% to Rs 28.21 crore in H1 FY27; contribution to total sales improved to 43.5% (vs 37.5% in H1 FY26).
*   SaaS/transaction-based revenue grew 32.7% to Rs 18 crore.
*   *Note:* The call confirms the "deferred overseas orders" mentioned in our record, identifying a ~Rs 1.8 crore Middle East order deferral due to geopolitical issues.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to deliver Rs 100-125 crore from the current order book over the next 12 months.
*   Targeting 40% conversion of the Rs 625 crore sales pipeline; expects 50-75% of that conversion to be delivered within 12 months.
*   Management does not see a downside to FY27 guidance; expects the deferred Middle East order to be executed once the situation stabilizes.
*   Management is in advanced stages of signing a permitting solution engagement in the Middle East.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strategy focuses on three interconnected pillars: Cities (CivitCITY), Ports/Infrastructure (CivitPORT), and Industrial Corridors (CivitINDUSTRY).
*   eTDR platform volumes were lower in Q2 due to a slow real estate market and regulatory framework adjustments, but management expects growth in subsequent quarters.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Consolidated EBITDA margin stood at 26.3% (vs 26.7% YoY).
*   Other expenses increased due to go-to-market investments in Germany and technical fees for project delivery.
*   Finance costs declined to Rs 1.89 crore (vs Rs 2.16 crore).
*   Depreciation and amortization increased to Rs 6.85 crore (vs Rs 5.65 crore) in Q2 due to product development investments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Significant investments made in aligning products for the German market (data sets, building codes, GIS maps).
*   Expanding business development in Germany with a new sales director.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed in detail.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical situation caused the deferral of a ~Rs 1.8 crore Middle East order.
*   Slow real estate market impacted eTDR volumes in Q2.
*   Consolidated PAT impacted by higher depreciation/amortization from product development investments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why the gap between standalone and consolidated growth?* A: Management cited investments in Germany and the deferred Middle East order.
*   *Q: Risk of cancellation for the deferred order?* A: Management stated no risk of cancellation; it is purely a deferral due to geopolitical factors.
*   *Q: Why are eTDR volumes lower?* A: Management attributed it to a slow real estate market and regulatory changes, but expects improvement.
*   *Q: When will depreciation/amortization stabilize?* A: Management expects absolute numbers to remain elevated or slightly higher as they continue to invest in platforms, but the percentage of cost to sales will decrease as they scale.

### One-line takeaway
Strong standalone performance and a significant new JNPA order win underpin growth, while management navigates temporary consolidated headwinds from overseas market investments and geopolitical order deferrals.
