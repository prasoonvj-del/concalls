# Mphasis Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management raised full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance to low double digits on the back of broad-based performance, strong short-cycle deal conversion, and a record AI-led deal pipeline.

### Key numbers
* **Net New TCV Wins:** $466 million for Q2 FY27 (TTM TCV at $1.8 billion; 66% AI-led).
* **Direct Revenue Growth (CC):** 3.5% QoQ, 11.3% YoY.
  * **BFS Direct:** 4.5% QoQ, 15.0% YoY.
  * **Insurance Direct:** 1.8% QoQ, 14.7% YoY.
  * **TMT Direct:** 10.9% QoQ, 14.5% YoY.
  * **Logistics & Transportation:** -12.0% QoQ.
* **Service Lines (Direct CC):**
  * **Enterprise Applications:** 75.6% of revenue; grew 3.7% QoQ, 14.4% YoY.
  * **BPO:** 6.6% QoQ, 18.7% YoY.
  * **ITO:** -4.9% QoQ, -21.3% YoY.
* **Geographies (Direct CC):** Americas grew 3.1% QoQ (12.4% YoY); Rest of World grew 16.4% QoQ (23.5% YoY); EMEA declined 3.3% QoQ (-9.0% YoY due to global deal contract allocation).
* **Client Portfolio Growth (TTM YoY):** Top 5 clients grew 15.1% YoY (4.1% QoQ); Top 10 grew 13.0% YoY; Top 11–20 grew 22.7% YoY.
* **EPS:** INR 29.4 (up 14.6% QoQ).
* **Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** $34 million (normalized for $39.5 million delayed remittance received in early October).
* **DSO:** 105 days (normalized; expected to return to double digits in Q3).
* **Pipeline:** Total pipeline up 4.4% QoQ and 23% YoY (71% AI-led).

### Highlights
* Raised full-year FY27 constant-currency revenue growth guidance.
* Mphasis Tria platform adoption is expanding beyond traditional IT budgets into non-IT enterprise operating/business budgets (origination, payment exceptions, claims, underwriting).
* Won 5 large deals in Q2 FY27, including a recurring revenue-share contract with a large broker-dealer to scale their advisor administration business.
* Small-deal activity (<$20 million TCV) grew 26% YoY on a TTM basis; pipeline for deals <$20 million grew 35% YoY.
* Added 2 clients in the $50M+ bucket QoQ, 3 in the $20M+ bucket YoY, and 5 in the $5M+ bucket YoY.
* *Break/Continuity with Record:* Continues the pattern of high-single/double-digit Direct revenue growth and AI-led TCV wins outlined in recent filings.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Revenue Growth:** Raised full-year FY27 constant-currency revenue growth guidance to **low double digits** (up from high single digits to low double digits).
* **EBIT Margins:** Maintained FY27 EBIT margin target range of **14.75% to 15.75%**.
* **Cash Flow Conversion:** Targeting 80% conversion of PAT to Operating Cash Flow in FY27, with further normalization to historical levels in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Enterprise AI demand is shifting from technology adoption to measurable economic outcomes; budgets are increasingly co-funded by business and operations leaders outside IT.
* Enterprise demand is concentrated in three main pools: AI engineering (harnesses/infrastructure layers), cyber estate hardening (vulnerability management, non-human identity, post-quantum cryptography), and AI-infused savings-led process transformation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Q2 EBIT margin remained flat near the lower boundary of the target band (~14.8%).
* Margins were restrained by front-loaded investments in Tria IP/platform capabilities, TAP acquisition amortization, sales coverage, and an increased onsite mix required during initial phases of AI deployments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Finalized acquisition commitments including Red Oak, with closing expected in November 2026 involving a $28 million payout commitment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Reported OCF was impacted by $39.5 million of collection remittances initiated on September 30, 2026, but credited on October 1; normalized OCF was $34 million.
* DSO spiked to 105 days due to the quarter-end collection lag but is projected to improve back to double-digit days in Q3 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Macroeconomic and geopolitical headwinds continuing to weigh on the Logistics & Transportation vertical (-12.0% QoQ).
* Onsite-heavy initial delivery mix in AI engineering engagements creating near-term gross margin pressure.
* Temporary volatility in quarterly cash conversion metrics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley):** What gives confidence to upgrade revenue guidance despite TCV remaining in the steady $400M+ range?
  * **Management:** Confidence stems from backlog execution, robust short-cycle deal mix (<$20M TCV deals in pipeline up 35% YoY; TTM short-deal TCV up 26%), faster revenue conversion of short-burst AI engineering projects, and ongoing account expansion in top client tiers.
* **Sandeep Shah (Elara Securities):** What will drive the sharp H2 cash flow conversion required to meet the 80% OCF-to-PAT target?
  * **Management:** OCF will recover as DSO normalizes from Q2’s 105-day quarterly aberration back to double digits in Q3. In addition, contract acquisition payments will be lower in FY28 compared to FY27.
* **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama):** Is Logistics undergoing a structural decline similar to older legacy businesses?
  * **Management:** No. Logistics is cyclical and affected by macro issues, but client accounts feature leading enterprise brands; Mphasis is actively reinvesting in account coverage to drive wallet-share expansion.
* **Kumar Rakesh (BNP Paribas Mutual Fund):** Why is cash flow displaying high quarterly volatility relative to stable revenue in a contractual services business?
  * **Management:** The quarter-end cutoff anomaly ($39.5M initiated Sept 30, landed Oct 1) caused the extreme variance; there are no underlying contractual structural changes, and cash conversion will normalize to historical trends by FY28.

### One-line takeaway
Mphasis upgraded its FY27 revenue growth guidance to low double digits backed by broad-based operational momentum and fast-converting short-cycle AI deals.
