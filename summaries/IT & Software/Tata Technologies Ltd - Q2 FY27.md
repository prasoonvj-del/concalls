# Tata Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-19 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in double-digit growth and margin expansion, supported by strong deal wins and a robust pipeline, despite acknowledging transient softness in the German market.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** US$178 million (up 22.2% YoY in constant currency).
*   **Services revenue:** US$142 million (up 26.2% YoY, 4.3% QoQ).
*   **Operating EBITDA margin:** 16.1% (resilient despite 100 bps headwind from salary increments).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 273 crore (up 31.5% YoY, 2.2% QoQ).
*   **Net income:** Rs 171 crore (vs Rs 166 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Net cash:** Rs 330 crore (down from Rs 880 crore in Q1, primarily due to Rs 475 crore dividend payout).
*   **Headcount:** 12,895 (net increase of 316 associates).
*   **Attrition:** 15.6% (improved 70 bps sequentially).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Wins:** Secured five large deals in Q2, including a landmark US$138 million full vehicle engineering program with a leading European luxury automotive OEM.
*   **Aerospace:** Identified as a key second growth engine; leveraging relationships with Airbus to expand into manufacturing engineering, propulsion, and MRO.
*   **AI Strategy:** Nearly 1,000 associates are deploying AI agents; transitioning to "AI-native" delivery to improve productivity and development speed.
*   **BMW JV:** BMW TechWorks India contributed Rs 19.7 crore (including deferred income), with profit share up 115% YoY.
*   **Accounting Change:** A change in accounting treatment for certain OEM-hosted SaaS licenses (Ind AS 115) reduced Technology Solutions revenue by Rs 12.6 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management reiterated confidence in maintaining strong double-digit revenue growth for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Expect sequential margin improvement in the second half of the year through disciplined execution and productivity initiatives.
*   **Long-term:** Management maintains 18-20% EBITDA margins as the company's "North Star."

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Automotive:** Seeing a structural shift where Western OEMs are becoming leaner and outsourcing more engineering/product development.
*   **Germany:** Acknowledged temporary demand softness due to customer restructuring (e.g., VW, BMW), but expects conditions to stabilize in 2027.
*   **Aerospace:** Strong demand; industry facing capacity and throughput challenges. Expects exponential scaling of opportunities, particularly with potential new aircraft programs.
*   **Technology Solutions:** Education segment faces near-term pressure due to slower infrastructure readiness at customer sites.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Headwinds:** Annual salary increments created a >100 bps margin headwind.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Increased D&A and lease-related interest costs due to new office facility in Pune; expects these to be absorbed as revenue scales.
*   **Subcontracting:** Management noted a "fine balance" between permanent employees and subcontractors to manage peaks and troughs in program execution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **DSO:** 102 days (vs 97 days in Q1).
*   **Billed DSO:** 63 days (improved from 65 days in Q1).
*   **Unbilled DSO:** 39 days (vs 32 days in Q1).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geographic/Client Concentration:** Ongoing restructuring at German automotive clients (e.g., VW, BMW) impacting near-term performance.
*   **Education Segment:** Slower infrastructure readiness impacting project execution.
*   **Execution:** Scaling large, complex programs requires managing capability, infrastructure, and AI investment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did margins not expand despite the large deal ramp-up?** Management explained that salary increments were a 100 bps headwind, but they were pleased to maintain 16.1% margins through operational discipline.
*   **Q: Is the $138 million European OEM deal net new or a renewal?** Management stated it is a turnkey program running for ~2.5 years, with potential for variant opportunities to extend beyond the initial scope.
*   **Q: Is the weakness in Germany deteriorating?** Management clarified the weakness is consistent with previous expectations and is tactical; they remain committed to the region for long-term R&D globalization trends.
*   **Q: Can the company sustain 20% growth for the next 3-4 years?** Management noted that while demand is strong, sustaining 20%+ growth becomes challenging as the company scales; the focus is on capability and AI-driven competitive moats.
*   **Q: Any impact from the Tata Group's semiconductor/chip initiatives?** Management confirmed they are already supporting Tata Electronics and view the group's consortia capabilities as a key differentiator.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Technologies is successfully pivoting toward large, outcome-based "full vehicle" engineering programs, offsetting temporary German market softness with strong deal wins and a strategic focus on aerospace and AI-native delivery.
