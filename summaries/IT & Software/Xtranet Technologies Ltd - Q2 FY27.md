# Xtranet Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue growth, expanding EBITDA margins, and robust order inflows.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 110 crores (up 63% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Operational EBITDA:** Rs. 22 crores (up 144% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 20.42%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 14 crores (up 111% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 12.49%
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 160 crores (up 42% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operational EBITDA:** Rs. 33 crores (up 123% YoY, margin at 20.49%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 20 crores (doubled YoY, margin at 12.37%)
- **Order Book:** ~Rs. 390 crores as of September 30, 2026
- **Q2 Fresh Orders:** ~Rs. 90 crores
- **Bid Pipeline:** >Rs. 2,000 crores

### Highlights
- Formed a dedicated data center subsidiary ("Xtranet Data Center") to address hosting, cloud, and managed services for enterprise and BFSI clients.
- Structural shift in customer mix: Government and PSU contribution reduced to ~30%–35%, with increasing traction from BFSI and private enterprises.
- Proprietary platforms XtraTrust and Synergy together contribute 14% of revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated an ambition to surpass original revenue guidance (previous guidance mentioned as Rs. 500 crores for FY27 by an analyst) while sustaining strong EBITDA and PAT margins.
- Management expects heavy execution loading in Q3 and Q4.
- International revenue contribution from Middle East and Africa proposals expected to start from Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian data center industry installed capacity expected to reach ~7.5 GW by March 2030, supported by >Rs. 3 lakh crores investment.
- Indian IT services revenue growth projected at 3%–5% in FY27.
- Strong demand for AI cloud adoption, cybersecurity, and enterprise digital transformation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability grew significantly faster than revenue due to a richer product mix (higher contribution from digital services, proprietary platforms, and data centers).
- Management expects to maintain EBITDA margins around 17.5%–18% going forward.
- Supply chain and procurement strengthened for hardware and software to protect margins and ensure timely execution.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Initial data center plan: 2MW capacity requiring Rs. 130–140 crores of investment (funded via internal accruals and debt), expandable to 10MW under an asset-light model.
- First block of IT park (42,000 sq. ft.) alignment is ongoing, with IPO capex allocation expected to be utilized in the current quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital days expected to improve by 15–20 days compared to FY26 due to the lower share of government business.
- Temporary increase in working capital during H1 driven by inventory/supply chain investments for accelerated H2 execution.
- Recent September-end invoice receivables expected to be collected in October and the current quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as material risks, other than standard supply chain and working capital management addressed through strategic procurement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anil Joshi (Individual Investor) on Data Center Outlay:** Management clarified that the initial 2MW data center requires Rs. 130–140 crores (debt and internal accruals), with plans to scale to 10MW via an asset-light model.
2. **Rohil (Individual Investor) on Margin Sustainability:** Management attributed margin expansion to higher-yielding digital services, proprietary platforms, and data center verticals, and guided for 17.5%–18% sustainable EBITDA margins.
3. **Yogesh Soni (Individual Investor) on Negative Operating Cash Flow & Receivables:** Management explained that working capital temporarily increased due to inventory/supply chain investments for H2 execution, and invoices raised at the end of September are being collected in October/current quarter.
4. **Prateek C on International Expansion:** Management noted active proposals in 6 African countries and the Middle East for 10-to-15-year contracts, with revenue contributions expected from Q4 FY27.
5. **Dhruv Shah on XERP Competitiveness:** Management stated that XERP targets small and mid-market organizations (SMEs/MSMEs) and 60+ banks, offering AI-enabled ERP solutions at an accessible price point compared to Oracle or SAP.

### One-line takeaway
Xtranet delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 42% revenue growth and doubled PAT, driven by a structural shift toward enterprise/BFSI clients and higher-margin proprietary platforms and data center segments.
