# Black Box Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue, record order backlog crossing $1 billion, and strong growth momentum driven by hyperscale demand.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (FY27 revenue guidance of INR 7,800 crore to INR 8,000 crore).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted even more bullish on AI/data center momentum, noting accelerating investments and zero slowdown post-US elections.
- New topics or risks: Front-loading of ~300 technical/project talent additions in the last two months with 700-800 more planned; old risks of supply chain constraints were absent.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on order backlog growth ($1.04 billion achieved vs ~$950 million) and record revenues.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 1,936 crore (up 22% YoY, 13% QoQ)
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR 3,655 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 175 crore (up 23% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 9%
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 335 crore (up 29% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 9.2%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27):** INR 73 crore (up 31% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (H1 FY27):** INR 129 crore (up 16% YoY)
- **Order Bookings (Q2 FY27):** $298 million
- **Order Bookings (H1 FY27):** $637 million
- **Order Backlog:** ~$1,044 million ($1.04 billion) as of September 2026 (up 88% YoY)
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** INR 115 crore
- **Hiring target:** ~3,000 professionals by FY30

### Highlights
- Reported highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue in Q2 and H1 FY27.
- Order backlog reached a record level of ~$1.04 billion, providing revenue visibility of 24-36 months.
- Data center contribution to Q2 revenue reached 20% (vs ~17% in FY26), expected to reach ~30% in FY27; data center order bookings in H1 crossed $250 million.
- Front-loaded investments by hiring ~300 employees over the last two months, with 700-800 more planned over the next 6-9 months to execute hyperscaler orders.
- Reiterated long-term goal of reaching $2 billion revenue by FY30.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to achieve the upper end of the FY27 revenue guidance range of INR 7,800 crore to INR 8,000 crore.
- EBITDA margin target of 10% set for Q4 FY27, with medium-term objective to consistently maintain 10% or higher.
- Expects H2 organic order bookings to significantly exceed H1, aiming closer to $1 billion to reach full-year bookings guidance.

### Demand & industry commentary
- U.S. hyperscalers collectively expected to invest $725 billion-$750 billion in 2026 and trillions over the next few years.
- Global IT spending expected to exceed $7 trillion in 2027, with data center spending at ~$1.36 trillion (Gartner October publication).
- Zero slowdown observed in AI infrastructure demand post-U.S. elections; companies like Meta and Google scaling up investments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin at 9% was impacted by front-loading investments in skilled technical and project talent.
- Improved cost absorption and operating leverage in H2 expected to drive margins to the 10% target in Q4.
- Material/equipment is largely out of scope for large hyperscale contracts (constituting only 2%-3% for last-mile delivery); primarily a services-led model.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continuing plans to recruit nearly 3,000 professionals by FY30, primarily in the U.S.
- Expanding engineering and delivery capabilities in the U.S. to support gigawatt-scale data center projects.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- H1 operating cash flow reached INR 115 crore.
- Working capital cycles for large projects face initial 90-105 day payment delays during mobilization, which normalize as execution scales in Q4.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Initial teething issues and setup times on large projects can temporarily delay cash conversion cycles before settling into regular 60-day payment terms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bajrang Bafna (Sunidhi Securities)** asked about the impact of the U.S. political shift and NVIDIA/AI investment sentiment. **Management** stated they see no slowdown, citing Meta and Google's recent massive compute announcements and strong order backlog execution.
2. **Nandan Arekal (JM Financial)** asked about order backlog tenure progression. **Management** explained average backlog tenure of 18 months will likely move to 20-22 months and plateau around 24 months as larger data center orders scale up.
3. **Vivek (Individual Investor)** questioned if the company is falling behind on acquisitions to reach the $2 billion target. **Management** responded they are actively evaluating accretive opportunities in H2 but prioritize shareholder value over chasing deals blindly.
4. **Aditya Saraf** asked about execution risks in regions like Texas and Virginia post-election. **Management** reiterated no roadblocks or bumps are visible, with customer capex plans actually increasing.
5. **Nikhil Kanodia (Sunidhi Securities)** asked about H-1B visa dependency. **Management** clarified that Black Box is a local U.S. company and is not visa-dependent, so visa regimes will not impact growth or hiring.

### One-line takeaway
Black Box delivered record Q2 revenue and a historic $1.04 billion order backlog, remaining highly confident in its FY27 revenue guidance and long-term AI-driven growth trajectory.
