# Protean eGov Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Core franchises are growing, major upfront RFP investments are largely behind, and early traction in new enterprise bundled solutions and international strategies is visible.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 271 crore, up 8% YoY
- **EBITDA:** Rs 43 crore (15% margin); normalized EBITDA (excluding Rs 10 crore upfront strategic RFP investments) at Rs 53 crore (18% margin), up 22% YoY
- **PAT:** Rs 16 crore (6% margin); normalized PAT at Rs 24 crore (8% margin)
- **Tax Services revenue:** Rs 121 crore, up 13% YoY; market share at 65.8% (+330 bps QoQ, +780 bps YoY)
- **CRA Services revenue:** Rs 80 crore, up 2% YoY; 4.7 million new subscribers onboarded (up 15% YoY)
- **Identity Services revenue:** Rs 27 crore, up 24% YoY; volume growth of 26%
- **New Initiatives revenue:** Rs 42 crore (16% of total revenue); H1 FY27 grew 56% YoY
- **Cash and marketable securities:** Approx Rs 750 crore (zero debt) as of September 30, 2026
- **ASK Centres:** 187 out of 192 live as of October 2026 (150 open till September)
- **Farmer IDs created (AgriStack):** 11 crore

### Highlights
- Two large RFP mandates (CERSAI CKYCRR 2.0 and UIDAI Aadhaar Seva Kendra) are nearing implementation completion, with major upfront investments largely behind.
- Shift from selling standalone APIs to a journey-led bundled solution approach for enterprises (BFSI), with 20+ mandates already signed and 30+ in the pipeline.
- Restructured international business to pursue "DPI in a Box" opportunities in global markets.
- **Record check:** Transcript confirms the ongoing absorption of upfront investments in strategic RFPs and steady revenue growth mentioned in the recent investor deck.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects reported margins to move closer to the underlying run rate in coming quarters as major project investments conclude.
- No formal numerical guidance provided due to nascent stage of new strategies and pending clarity on PAN 2.0 rollout timelines.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PFRDA expects NPS subscribers to reach 40 crore over the next 5 years and AUM to reach Rs 100 lakh crore by 2040.
- Pension coverage in India is below 7% compared to 70% in the US and Europe.
- Banks spend Rs 9,000 crore to Rs 11,000 crore annually on Priority Sector Lending Certificates (PSLC) due to difficulties in lending to small/marginal farmers, presenting a large TAM for AgriStack.
- RBI's account aggregator interoperability guidelines create an even playing field, benefiting Protean's AAA business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin impacted by Rs 10 crore upfront investment towards strategic RFP implementation (H1 impact: Rs 28 crore).
- Deployed AI and automation in back-office operations to optimize business operations, reduce external dependencies, and drive cost savings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Property, plant and equipment increased from Rs 63 crore in March 2026 to Rs 150 crore in September 2026 due to investments in the ASK project.
- Cash reserves (Rs 750 crore) earmarked for tech upgrades, ASK project execution, product journey development, and potential international JVs/branches or inorganic acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Zero debt position maintained.
- DSO increased from 64 days to 93 days primarily due to project-related receivables.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Lack of clarity from ITD on the exact revenue model evolution for PAN services post PAN 2.0 implementation next year.
- Quarterly lumpiness in new initiatives revenue due to project-led billing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Agrawal (Param Capital) on Tax Services and PAN 2.0:** Asked about growth sustainability and risk of degrowth in FY28. Management stated that ~70% of PAN is driven by the assisted model (not part of online PAN 2.0 mandate), but final clarity from ITD is pending.
2. **Deepak Agrawal on Capital Allocation:** Suggested share buybacks given the large cash pile. Management responded that cash is required for tech upgrades, ASK project execution, product journeys, and international expansion/JVs, though dividends will continue.
3. **Bajrang Bafna (Sunidhi Securities) on AgriStack TAM:** Asked about monetization potential. Management explained that banks spend Rs 9,000 crore to Rs 11,000 crore annually on PSLCs, and AgriStack data can convert this cost item into a lending/revenue opportunity.
4. **Vinil Shah (Dalal & Broacha) on DPI in a Box feedback:** Asked about international customer responses. Management noted strong interest from developing and developed nations for integrated stacks like CKYC, digital IDs, and pension infrastructure.
5. **Saurabh Jain (Sunidhi Securities) on NPS revenue economics:** Asked about revenue per subscriber. Management stated per-subscriber revenue ranges from Rs 100 to Rs 500 based on AUM, plus a Rs 40 account-opening fee.

### One-line takeaway
Protean is successfully transitioning from a transactional API provider to a high-margin, journey-led enterprise solution provider and global DPI exporter, with peak domestic RFP upfront investments now largely behind.
