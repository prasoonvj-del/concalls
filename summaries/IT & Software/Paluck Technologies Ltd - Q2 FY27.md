# Paluck Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY 2026-27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong organic growth, a robust order pipeline, and a shift toward higher profitability in H2.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 51.2 crore (approx. 34% YoY growth).
*   **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 5.09 crore (approx. 42% YoY growth).
*   **Revenue Guidance (FY27):** Rs 130 crore – Rs 140 crore.
*   **PAT Guidance (FY27):** Rs 17 crore – Rs 18 crore.
*   **Revenue Mix (H1):** Automobile & Engineering Services (Rs 27.15 crore); Equipment Rental (Rs 24 crore).

### Highlights
*   **Business Segments:** Operates in two verticals: (1) Telecom & Automobile Engineering Services (managing ~60,000 nodes) and (2) Construction Equipment Rental (fleet of 192 assets).
*   **Customer Base:** Serves AAA-rated clients including Tata, L&T, Megha Engineering, and various telecom majors (Ericsson, Nokia, Airtel, etc.).
*   **Recurring Revenue:** 85%–90% of business is recurring with long-term contracts (3–5 years).
*   **IPO Impact:** IPO funds received in September 2026; utilization for capex is planned for H2 FY27, with major growth impact expected in FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Outlook:** Management expects 25%+ YoY growth in revenue and PAT.
*   **FY28 Outlook:** Management targets 40% growth in top line, aiming for Rs 190 crore – Rs 200 crore revenue, driven by IPO fund utilization.
*   **Long-term:** Targeting 4x growth over the next five years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Seasonality:** H1 is typically lighter (40% of annual revenue) due to the rainy season; H2 contributes 60% of revenue and a larger share of annual PAT due to fixed costs.
*   **Entry Barriers:** High barriers due to the need for specialized capability in both telecom uptime (99.999% requirement) and construction equipment delivery.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Segment Margins:** Construction Equipment Rental (~35% gross margin); Telecom Services (~20%–23% gross margin).
*   **Blended Margins:** Blended gross margin is approximately 23%–25%.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Management noted H1 margins were impacted by higher recurrence of certain expenses, but expects annual margins to remain stable.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Construction equipment segment is capex-heavy; IPO proceeds will be utilized to purchase new equipment.
*   **Telecom:** Segment is working capital-heavy due to the need for inventory maintenance across pan-India sites.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Reduced from Rs 50 crore (3-4 years ago) to current levels of Rs 4.5 crore – Rs 5 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** H1 FY27 operating cash flow was negative (approx. -Rs 9 crore) due to inventory replenishment and working capital absorption.
*   **Receivables:** Aging is typically 90–120 days; management attributed recent 80% YoY increase in trade receivables to one-off delays and expects improvement in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Cash Flow:** Management confirmed that operating cash flow is expected to remain negative in H2 due to business growth and capex outflows.
*   **Inventory:** High inventory levels required for telecom site maintenance; management expects inventory days to reduce as business scales.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did operating profit margins decline in H1?** Management explained that H1 has a higher recurrence of certain expenses, but the full-year margin profile remains intact.
*   **Q: Why was operating cash flow negative in H1?** Management cited inventory replenishment across pan-India telecom sites as the primary driver.
*   **Q: Will operating cash flow be negative in H2?** Management confirmed it will likely remain negative due to continued business growth and capex-related outflows.
*   **Q: What is the status of trade receivables?** Management stated the 80% YoY increase is due to one-off events and expects the number of days to reduce in H2.
*   **Q: What is the earning potential of the new capex?** Management expects Rs 50 crore of additional orders to materialize in 2-3 months, fueling FY28 growth.

### One-line takeaway
Paluck Technologies is transitioning from a debt-reduction phase to an aggressive growth phase, leveraging IPO proceeds to scale its recurring-revenue business model, despite near-term cash flow pressure from working capital and capex requirements.
