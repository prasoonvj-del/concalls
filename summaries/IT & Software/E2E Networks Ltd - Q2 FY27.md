# E2E Networks Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance driven by Blackwell GPU contribution, better utilization, and higher average realizations.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,238 crore (up 411% YoY, 43% QoQ)
- EBITDA: Rs 1,715 crore (up 45% QoQ)
- EBITDA Margin: 76.6% (expanded by 141 bps QoQ)
- PBT: Rs 971 crore (up 66% QoQ)
- PAT: Rs 727 crore (up from Rs 439 crore in Q1)
- PAT Margin: 32.5% (up from 28% in Q1)
- Diluted EPS: Rs 3.48 (vs Rs 2.10 in Q1)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 3,805 crore (up 376% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 2,894 crore (margin of 76%)
- H1 FY27 PBT: Rs 1,558 crore (vs loss of Rs 222 crore in H1 FY26)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 1,166 crore (vs loss of Rs 163 crore in H1 FY26)
- India AI customers revenue mix: ~13% to 15% for the quarter
- International revenue mix: ~20% to 25% for the quarter

### Highlights
- Signed a nearly Rs 1,000 crore deal with a Sovereign AI company for the Blackwell cluster, lasting through June 2029 (converting from pay-as-you-go to a multi-year commitment).
- Strengthened management and talent density: added a new Chief Revenue Officer, a Founder's Office team, a Field CTO, and appointed Mr. Anandamoy Roychowdhary to the Strategic Advisory team.
- Continuing rapid investments in Sovereign AI platforms TIR and Jarvis to operate as a full-stack player.
- Next lot of B200 GPUs expected to go live in Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the business view as a 70%+ EBITDA margin business for the medium and long term.
- Management declines to give speculative capex guidance, future MRR exit guidance, or percentage breakdowns of contracted vs short-term revenue, preferring to announce plans after deployment plans are solidified.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand for AI infrastructure is part of a massive global TAM (addressable market near $1 trillion).
- The company is focusing on global market opportunities rather than domestic competition alone.
- Inference demand, agentic workloads, and platform soft releases (Blaze, Endpoint, Jarvis lab) are underway, though management tracks overall fleet realization rather than siloed functional metrics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin performance driven by Blackwell GPU contribution, better utilization, and higher average realization.
- Operating costs for the quarter were Rs 18 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Order-to-deployment cycle typically varies between 3 to 6 months.
- The company remains open to all forms of capital deployment, off-balance-sheet funding, and ecosystem partnerships (including with L&T on an arm's length basis).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No upper limit defined for debt as long as front-end cash flows and ability to repay support it.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Forward-looking numbers and percentage metrics (such as international or contracted mixes) are subject to wild swings and point-in-time variations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bharat Gulati (Dalal & Broacha) asked about the contribution of the Rs 1,000 crore contract to MRR.** Management stated exact contribution varies by usage and declined to predict future MRR.
- **Keshav asked when global GPU price increases will reflect in MRR.** Management explained they balance short-term gains with long-term customer commitments and platform R&D investments.
- **Harsh Shah (Seven Rivers Holding) asked about India AI Mission revenue share.** Nitin Jain (CFO) stated India AI customers contributed roughly 13% to 15% for the quarter.
- **Bhavya Gandhi asked about comfortable debt levels and international revenue mix.** Management stated comfort with debt as long as repayment ability exists, and CFO Nitin Jain noted international revenue is 20% to 25%.
- **Neil Munot asked how E2E differentiates its platform from larger scaled domestic players.** Management emphasized looking at the global $1 trillion TAM where there is a vast blue ocean and enough demand for multiple players.

### One-line takeaway
E2E Networks delivered robust Q2 FY27 growth with Rs 2,238 crore in revenue and a 76.6% EBITDA margin, underpinned by its Blackwell GPU deployment and expanding Sovereign AI platform strategy.
