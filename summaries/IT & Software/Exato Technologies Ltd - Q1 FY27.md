# Exato Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-26 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a strong start to the fiscal year with robust year-on-year growth in revenue, margins, and order book expansion, driven by international markets.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue growth YoY:** 50%
- **EBITDA growth YoY:** 82%
- **PBT growth YoY:** 102%
- **PAT growth YoY:** 104%
- **Order Book:** Rs 660 crore (up from Rs 600 crore in Q4; includes Rs 60 crore of new orders added in Q1, Rs 251 crore total executed up to Q1, and ~Rs 410 crore remaining to be executed)
- **ARR:** Increased by ~Rs 20 crore to ~Rs 140 crore (targeted at Rs 180-200 crore by end of FY27)
- **Exports contribution:** 27.2%
- **BPO and IT/ITES contribution:** >70% of revenue
- **Top 5 clients contribution:** ~60% of revenue
- **Client retention rate:** 97% to 98%
- **AI Infrastructure / Unified Communications initial margins:** 10% to 12% (expected to increase via land-and-expand)

### Highlights
- Added 60 crore of new orders from international customers, including iQor BPO (adding Rs 60-65 crore), alongside account expansions from TaskUs and Diligenta.
- Upgraded partnership status with Mitel to Platinum Partner (highest category in APAC) and Acumatica to Gold Partner.
- Forged new partnerships with HPE, AWS, Fortinet, and amplifAI.
- Key leadership additions: Dr. Milind Raman Godbole (Non-Executive, Non-Independent Director), Murali Menon (Chief AI Officer, ex-TCS CTO), Alok Bohra (from NiCE for channel alliances), Kailash Sethuraman (AI Infrastructure lead), and Paresh Sheth (Advisor for infrastructure vertical).
- Proprietary IP platform on the verge of signing initial agreements and expects revenue contribution to start as early as this month or next.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue to grow at least 60% to 70% (or even more) and profitability to grow more than 70% to 80%.
- ARR targeted to reach Rs 180 to 200 crore by the end of FY27.
- AI infrastructure vertical expected to contribute 25% to 30% of business in FY27 once it scales within a quarter.
- Expects to announce strategic international deals and proprietary platform deals in upcoming months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global CX and AI-as-a-service market is growing at a CAGR of 35% to 40%.
- Cloud ERP growing at close to 20% CAGR.
- Total Addressable Market (TAM) for Exato currently stands at $500 to $600 million, projected to be a $30 billion market by 2030.
- Agentic AI is moving beyond discussion, with customers actively exploring CX transformation and private AI/sovereign AI models.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability boosted by strong international execution and higher-margin exports and services (maintenance, implementation, consulting contributing ~30%).
- Employee costs optimized and expected to be optimized further via internal AI tool implementations.
- AI infrastructure carries a relatively lower initial margin (10-12%) but opens avenues for managed services cross-sell.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Heavy investment in expanding US and UK presence, with Chief Revenue Officer camping in the US and local presence being established.
- Expanding from 4 to 5 verticals (adding AI Infrastructure / Application as a service / Managed Services).
- Actively evaluating inorganic growth opportunities and potential mergers/acquisitions with the help of a Big Four firm.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Non-cancellable, long-term customer contracts (3-5 years) provide strong revenue visibility.
- Headcount being increased cautiously while leveraging internal automation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations and data security concerns leading companies to demand secure, sovereign, private AI infrastructure rather than relying entirely on public models.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Miten Shah (traditional IT vs. Exato differentiation):** Asked how Exato differs from large system integrators. Management explained large SIs face conflict working with BPOs, whereas Exato focuses purely on the niche CX and analytics vertical, bundling consulting, technology, and OEM products.
2. **Bharath Reddy (customer count and inorganic targets):** Inquired about the 300-500 customer addition target and inorganic plans. Management stated they are on track via organic growth, platform/IP readiness, and potential acquisitions adding to their 5 verticals.
3. **Venkata Padala (HPE partnership terms):** Asked about the commercial terms with HPE. Management clarified they own the customer relationship and consulting, utilizing HPE hardware for a "single-envelope" enterprise AI stack and appliance model.
4. **Wilroy Luis (M&A funding & margins):** Asked how M&A would be funded and about low-margin segments. Management stated M&A could be a mix of cash and equity, and while AI infrastructure has lower initial margins, the focus is on overall customer lifetime value and managed services.
5. **Miten Shah (Top client concentration & explaining CXaaS):** Asked about top client risk and a simple explanation of CXaaS. Management stated top 5 clients contribute ~60% but contracts are sticky and non-cancellable; they walked through how call recording, speech analytics, workforce management, and Agentic AI optimize customer experiences for banks and BPOs.

### One-line takeaway
Exato Technologies delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 50% YoY revenue growth and triple-digit PAT growth, driven by international expansion, a growing order book of Rs 660 crore, and strategic scaling into AI infrastructure.
