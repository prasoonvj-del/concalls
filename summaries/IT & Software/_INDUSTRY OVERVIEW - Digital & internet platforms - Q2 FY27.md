# IT & Software - Digital & internet platforms: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for digital and internet platforms reflects robust top-line momentum across most players, driven by AI integration, digital payments adoption, e-commerce, and regional expansion. Companies like **AvenuesAI** (+47% YoY revenue), **BLS E-Services** (+55% income), **C.E. Info Systems** (+15.8% revenue), **CarTrade Tech** (record income of Rs 236 cr), **MSTC Ltd** (record H1 financial performance), **Matrimony.com** (+17.6% revenue), **Mobavenue AI Tech** (+41.3% revenue), and **Infinity Infoway** (revenue more than doubled) reported strong operational execution. However, pockets of friction persist: **eMudhra** lowered full-year guidance following a security incident and exit from public TLS, **IndiaMART** faced elevated Silver-tier churn, and **Route Mobile** saw profits pressured by one-off costs.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across fintech, logistics, and digital services cite strong structural demand from Tier 3–6 Bharat and international markets (Middle East, US, Southeast Asia). Festive season upswings were highlighted by **AvenuesAI**, **CarTrade**, and **Digispice**. BFSI and non-IT sectors continue to drive robust hiring and enterprise tech adoption (**Info Edge**, **Protean eGov**).
* **Divergence:** While platform transaction volumes generally rose, **IndiaMART** noted a 10%–15% decline in unique inquiries due to defensive measures against AI/LLM web scraping and Google AI Overviews expanding. Similarly, **Tracxn** pointed out that global tech deal volumes remain at a 10-year low despite rising tech funding, contrasting with the high-growth narrative of ad-tech and platform peers. **Route Mobile** reported softness in long-distance international messaging.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Consensus on AI & Tech Investments:** Front-loading operating costs for AI research, data centers, and talent is a common theme (**AvenuesAI**, **IndiaMART**, **Infinity Infoway**, **Mobavenue**). 
* **Agreement vs Disagreement on Margins:** Companies leveraging platform scale and asset-light models reported high profitability—**Infinity Infoway** posted a 50.68% EBITDA margin, **CarTrade** hit 36%, and **MSTC** saw EBITDA peak at 71.75% of total income. Conversely, **AvenuesAI** compressed its net take rate to 5.06 bps to capture volume; **Route Mobile** suffered margin pressures from a security incident at its Colombian subsidiary (Masiv); and **Protean eGov** absorbed Rs 10 crore in upfront strategic RFP investments.

## Capex & capacity
* Most players maintain an asset-light posture (**BLS E-Services**, **MSTC**, **Mobavenue**). 
* Targeted capital allocation varies: **AvenuesAI** holds ~INR 294 crores from a rights issue earmarked for advanced AI software; **Protean eGov** increased PPE to Rs 150 crores due to rollout of Aadhaar Seva Kendras (ASK); **Infinity Infoway** invested in physical servers and GPUs for AI/LLM models; and **Novus Loyalty** and **CarTrade** are utilizing cash piles for strategic M&A and partnerships (e.g., Novus acquiring AutoPay).

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Revisions:** **eMudhra** reduced its FY27 total income guidance to ~INR 800 crore (from ~INR 825 crore) and PAT to ~INR 125 crore (from ~INR 137 crore) due to its exit from the public TLS business after a certificate security incident. Meanwhile, **AvenuesAI**, **MSTC**, and **Matrimony.com** maintained or reiterated their growth trajectories.
* **Execution Milestones:** **Digispice** filed its second motion for the NCLT merger of Spice Money; **MSTC** received RBI in-principle approval for its TReDS platform; **CarTrade** crossed Rs 1,400 crores in cash reserves.

## What to watch next quarter
* Integration and scaling of recent acquisitions (e.g., Atyati by **BLS**, AutoPay by **Novus**, Heltar by **Route Mobile**, Rediff synergies by **AvenuesAI**).
* Potential implementation of UPI MDR (noted by **AvenuesAI** as a potential revenue catalyst if ~30% of free transactions become chargeable).
* Progress on Silver subscriber churn recovery at **IndiaMART** and clarity on the PAN 2.0 revenue model for **Protean eGov**.

## Company one-liners
* **AvenuesAI Ltd:** Sacrificed near-term take rates and operating cash flow to drive transaction scale and fund its AI-first fintech infrastructure pivot.
* **BLS E-Services Ltd:** Delivered record quarterly revenue driven by broad-based growth and the successful integration of Atyati Technologies.
* **C.E. Info Systems Ltd:** Reported strong Q2 FY27 with 15.8% revenue growth and significant margin expansion across Automotive and Enterprise segments.
* **CarTrade Tech Ltd:** Achieved record income and PBT backed by 36% EBITDA margins and strong momentum in OLX and Remarketing.
* **Digispice Technologies Ltd-$:** Posted 10% QoQ revenue growth and Rs 9.5 crore PAT, driven by credit breakeven and UPI Cashpoint adoption.
* **IndiaMART InterMESH Ltd:** Delivered a stable quarter with 10% revenue growth, but faced headwinds from Silver supplier churn and AI web-scraping defensive measures.
* **Infinity Infoway Ltd:** Doubled revenue with a 50%+ EBITDA margin, backed by strong execution in AI-driven education ERP and ZeroTouch exams.
* **Info Edge (India) Ltd:** Delivered solid revenue and operating profit growth led by 99acres margin expansion and AI products, despite a Shiksha headwind and upcoming CEO transition.
* **MSTC Ltd:** Delivered record H1 financial performance with zero trading exposure, supported by robust scrap/mineral auctions and TReDs RBI approval.
* **Matrimony.com Ltd:** Posted 167% YoY PAT growth, successfully leveraging AI and new subscription tiers while pivoting Wedding Services to a commission model.
* **Mobavenue AI Tech Ltd:** Delivered strong profitable growth, successfully leveraging its AI-native platform to scale internationally above a 20% EBITDA margin.
* **Novus Loyalty Ltd:** Aggressively scaling its enterprise loyalty platform through AI integration and inorganic entry into payments via AutoPay.
* **Protean eGov Technologies Ltd:** Transitioning to a high-margin enterprise solution provider and DPI exporter with peak domestic RFP investments behind it.
* **Route Mobile Ltd:** Revenue and gross margins improved on new products, but profit remained pressured by one-off costs and a soft long-distance segment.
* **Tracxn Technologies Ltd:** Prioritizing sales capacity expansion and AI-driven products, accepting short-term losses amid a challenging global deal-making environment.
* **eMudhra Ltd:** Lowered full-year guidance after exiting its 1%-revenue public TLS business following an isolated certificate security incident.
