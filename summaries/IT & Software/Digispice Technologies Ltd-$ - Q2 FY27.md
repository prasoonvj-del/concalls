# Digispice Technologies Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported 10% QoQ revenue growth, continued PAT expansion to Rs 9.5 crore, and the credit business achieving break-even.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None specifically revised numerically; management reiterated the expected completion of the Spice Money NCLT merger by the end of this financial year.
- Tone and demand view: Maintained a positive, expansion-focused view on Tier 4-6 Bharat, noting strong momentum in newer products like UPI Cashpoint (3x QoQ growth) offsetting a declining industry AePS market.
- New topics or risks: Received authorization for the Physical Aggregator-Physical (PA-P) license in October 2026; noted a one-off provision of Rs 85 lakhs made in the quarter.
- Last quarter's promises: UPI Cashpoint scaled rapidly as guided; NCLT merger second motion petition was filed on July 24, 2026, with the next hearing scheduled for later in the quarter.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 118.6 crore (10% QoQ growth)
- **Gross Margin:** Rs 52.0 crore
- **EBIT:** Rs 13.3 crore
- **PAT (Continuing Business):** Rs 9.5 crore (5% QoQ growth; H1 FY27 PAT at Rs 18.56 crore, up 30% YoY)
- **ROCE:** ~13%
- **RoA:** ~33.7%
- **Agent Network (Adhikaris):** ~17.25 lakh agents across 2.71 lakh towns (14.4 lakh in Tier 4-6 towns); serving 2.2 crore customers monthly
- **AePS Cash Withdrawal GTV:** Rs 13,571 crore (18.45% market share, up 52 bps QoQ)
- **UPI Cashpoint GTV:** Rs 908 crore (3.3x QoQ growth)
- **BBPS GTV:** Rs 1,700 crore (12.6% QoQ growth, 17.3% YoY growth)
- **Lifetime Savings Accounts Opened:** 18.9 lakh accounts (earning ~Rs 50 lakh/quarter float income)
- **Credit Disbursements:** Grahak loans at Rs 252 crore (27% QoQ growth) / Rs 223.5 crore as per presentation (33% QoQ growth); Adhikari loan run rate at ~Rs 28 crore/quarter; Adhikari loan NPA at sub-2.5%

### Highlights
- Received authorization from the regulator for the Physical Aggregator-Physical (PA-P) license in October 2026 to launch the Spice Money Biz platform for small merchants.
- NCLT merger process for Spice Money into Digispice Technologies received shareholder approval in July 2026; second motion petition filed July 24, 2026, with hearing later in the month, targeting a pure-play listed fintech structure by the end of the fiscal year.
- The credit business has broken even, with Adhikari loan disbursements tracking toward a ~Rs 110 crore annual run rate.
- Subscription packs sold reached 1.36 lakh packs in Q2, driving user stickiness (subscribers account for 48% of GTV).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets growing AePS market share beyond 20% in upcoming quarters by expanding geographic footprint.
- Management plans to launch a micro-LAP product in the Vyapaari lending space by the end of Q3 or start of Q4 FY27.
- Management expects the NCLT merger to conclude by the end of this financial year, establishing a pure-play listed fintech.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Traditional off-us AePS cash withdrawal industry volume has declined QoQ and YoY, but Spice Money is consolidating market share through dense agent coverage (achieving 20%-25% market share in dense states).
- Cash-first markets in Tier 3-6 Bharat offer significant headroom, with 33% of merchants and 48% of consumers still new-to-UPI.
- Traditional corporate CMS collections are facing price sensitivity, prompting a strategic shift toward BBPS-led digital collections.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Total costs rose 11% QoQ to Rs 4.21 crore, driven by July annual increments (contributing ~Rs 2.25 crore), absence of a previous quarter's Rs 60 lakh reversal, and an Rs 85 lakh one-off provision for an exposure.
- Gross margins were protected despite lower AePS industry volumes due to a favorable product mix shift toward high-margin credit and financial product distribution.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company operates as an asset-light model with a 1,000-member team spanning tech, product, and field operations.
- Expansion is focused on increasing agent density in sporadic regions (West and South) and driving deeper product adoption in dense regions (North and East).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Asset-light, zero-debt balance sheet relying on internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Traditional corporate CMS collections business is increasingly price-sensitive due to new market entrants.
- Industry-wide off-us AePS market volumes are structurally declining as digital alternatives grow.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vedant Trivedi (Individual) asked about the agent's role 5 years from now given UPI's rise:** Management stated agents will evolve into micro-branches handling complex financial products, KYC, collections, and building trust for cash-first consumers.
- **Prisha Shah asked what drove flat gross margins despite lower revenue:** Management explained the mix shifted away from low-margin subsidies and CMS toward high-margin credit and CASA/card distribution.
- **Prisha Shah asked about plans to reach 20%+ AePS market share:** Management responded that states with dense agent coverage already track 20%-25% share, and the playbook relies on expanding agent density and transactions per agent.
- **Nayan G. asked why Adhikari loan ticket sizes and values dropped QoQ:** Management explained they adjusted underwriting models to test smaller-ticket loans for riskier customer cohorts at the top of the funnel, while number of loans increased.
- **Utsav Baheti asked about third-party cash deposit dynamics:** Management explained the product solves sender KYC for migrant workers sending cash home via Aadhaar-based authentication, replacing traditional domestic money transfer.

### One-line takeaway
Digispice delivered 10% QoQ revenue growth and Rs 9.5 crore PAT in Q2 FY27, driven by credit business breakeven, surging UPI Cashpoint adoption, and high-margin product mix shifts ahead of its upcoming NCLT fintech merger.
