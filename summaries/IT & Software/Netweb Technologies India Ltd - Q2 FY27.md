# Netweb Technologies India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record-breaking quarterly revenue and PAT, with strong order book growth and sustained demand for AI infrastructure.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,323 crore (up 335.7% YoY, 61.4% QoQ).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 197 crore (up 334% YoY, 63.7% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 14.9%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 137 crore (up 334.8% YoY, 60.2% QoQ).
*   **PAT Margin:** 10.2%.
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 222 crore (surpassed full-year FY26 PAT of Rs 206 crore).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 2,629 crore (plus L1 position of Rs 653 crore).
*   **Active Pipeline:** Approximately Rs 11,152 crore.
*   **Cash Position:** Net cash of Rs 14 crore; Rs 403 crore unutilized from QIP proceeds.

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance:** AI systems contributed Rs 773 crore (58.4% of revenue, up 1,068.1% YoY). HPC contributed Rs 248 crore; Private Cloud contributed Rs 245 crore.
*   **Customer Base:** Added 449 new customers in H1 FY27.
*   **Taiwan Subsidiary:** Board approved a wholly-owned subsidiary in Taiwan specifically for R&D to access local talent and speed up innovation.
*   **Capital Raise:** Successfully raised Rs 1,200 crore via QIP; Rs 767 crore utilized as of September 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Target:** Management targets Rs 4,000 crore revenue for FY27.
*   **Growth:** Management expressed confidence in maintaining 30-40% CAGR for the next 2-3 years.
*   **Vera Rubin:** Launching platforms in India; commercial availability planned for January.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Drivers:** Strong demand for AI compute, HPC, and private cloud from government, public institutions, and large enterprises.
*   **Sovereign AI:** India AI mission and National Supercomputing Mission 2.0 are key structural tailwinds.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Private enterprise demand is currently very heavy, potentially exceeding government demand in the current pipeline.
*   **Supply Chain:** Management noted a 200%+ increase in memory chip prices but stated this has not deterred demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Management maintains an EBITDA margin framework of around 14%.
*   **Employee Costs:** Increased due to annual increments, variable pay, and a headcount increase of 120+ employees compared to the previous year.
*   **Gross Margins:** Management stated gross margin is a function of deal mix and difficult to monitor on a QoQ basis.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Inventory:** Inventory days stood at 117; management described higher inventory levels as "intentional" to secure supply chain for the large order book.
*   **Execution:** Standard execution timeline for orders is 10-16 weeks.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Debt:** Zero net debt position; net cash of Rs 14 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** Cash conversion cycle was 94 days; management expects it to operate in the 90-110 day range going forward.
*   **Receivables:** Days improved to 62 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **None explicitly flagged by management:** Management stated they do not see fundamental issues with AI demand and view the sector as robust.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Taiwan Subsidiary:** Management clarified it is strictly for R&D to access local talent, not for manufacturing partnerships or becoming an ODM.
*   **On Competition:** When asked about competition in India, management responded, "We have no idea about that."
*   **On C-DAC/India AI RFP:** Management explained that C-DAC is acting on behalf of India AI; the new RFP is necessary because international GPU hourly rates have increased, making older rates unviable.
*   **On Operating Leverage:** Management noted that while revenue grew 4x, they continue to reinvest in R&D, which is necessary to remain relevant and maintain their "sweet spot" in the market.
*   **On Pipeline Conversion:** Management stated the conversion ratio of the pipeline is approximately 60%.

### One-line takeaway
Netweb Technologies delivered a record-breaking quarter with 4x YoY growth, supported by a robust order book and strong AI infrastructure demand, while maintaining a zero net debt position and clear focus on R&D-led growth.
