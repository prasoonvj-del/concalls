# Matrimony.com Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with triple-digit YoY profit growth and successful execution of AI-led operational efficiencies.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained outlook for double-digit YoY revenue growth in Q3; profit expected to be similar to Q1 or slightly less than Q2.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "cautious optimism" to "confident" regarding the new commission-based model for Wedding Services, which management claims has hit bottom and is now seeing growth.
- **New topics:** Announced acquisition of an additional 50% stake in Astro-Vision Futuretech Private Limited; secured government approval for voluntary Aadhaar authentication for matchmaking.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the promise of triple-digit YoY profit growth (167.3% YoY).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 134.8 crore (+17.6% YoY, +3.3% QoQ)
- **Consolidated Billing:** Rs 131.1 crore (+10.7% YoY, -3.6% QoQ)
- **Matchmaking Revenue:** Rs 133.9 crore (+17.9% YoY, +3.3% QoQ)
- **Matchmaking Billing:** Rs 130.3 crore (+10.9% YoY, -3.7% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 22.5% (vs 20.1% in Q1 FY27, 10.8% YoY)
- **Matchmaking EBITDA Margin:** 29.7% (vs 26.9% in Q1 FY27, 17.1% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 20.7 crore (+167.3% YoY, +8.6% QoQ)
- **Paid Subscriptions Added:** 3.02 lakh (+26.6% YoY, +11.2% QoQ)
- **Matchmaking ATV:** Declined 12.2% YoY, 13.3% QoQ
- **Marriage Services/Other EBITDA Loss:** Rs 4.3 crore (vs Rs 3.8 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **ROCE:** 39.8% (annualized)

### Highlights
- **Strategic Acquisition:** Board approved acquiring an additional 50% stake in Astro-Vision Futuretech to tap into the booming online astrology segment.
- **Aadhaar Verification:** Secured exclusive government approval for voluntary Aadhaar-based member verification to enhance platform trust.
- **Regional Expansion:** Launched regional versions of Luv.com (Premam, Priyam, Premalu) with encouraging initial traction.
- **AI Integration:** Implemented AI for photo/profile validation, chatbots, and customer call quality analysis to drive operational efficiency.
- **Volume Growth:** Paid subscription volume grew 26.6% YoY, driven by the introduction of shorter-duration subscription packages.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Q3 Outlook:** Matchmaking revenue expected to post double-digit YoY growth; billing expected to reach single-digit YoY growth (impacted by Shradh and Navratri timing).
- **Profitability:** Expects triple-digit YoY growth in PAT for Q3; profit levels likely similar to Q1 or slightly below Q2.
- **Wedding Services:** Management expects continuous growth in new initiatives beyond matchmaking.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Market Dynamics:** Management noted that competitive intensity in the North has stabilized; the company remains a dominant player in the South, West, and East.
- **Pricing Strategy:** The decline in ATV is a deliberate strategy to increase conversion volume via shorter-duration packages, which management believes is sustainable and does not impact long-term revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Marketing Spend:** Matchmaking marketing expenses were Rs 45.3 crore (vs Rs 46.5 crore in Q1 FY27). Management does not foresee a substantial increase in marketing spend.
- **Operating Leverage:** Margins (excluding marketing) in Matchmaking improved to 64% (vs 63% in Q1 FY27).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Cash Position:** Cash balance stands at Rs 345 crore.
- **Wedding Services:** Transitioned from a subscription-based model to a commission-based model; currently focused on scaling this in Tamil Nadu before national expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Tax Rate:** Effective tax rate is 24.1%.
- **Shareholder Returns:** Management stated the board will decide on future buybacks or dividends after the one-year cooling-off period for the previous buyback concludes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Wedding Services Losses:** The segment continues to report EBITDA losses (Rs 4.3 crore this quarter), though management insists the model is shifting toward profitability.
- **ATV Decline:** Investors raised concerns about the 13.3% QoQ drop in ATV; management defended this as a volume-driving tactic.
- **Execution Risk:** Continued reliance on "new initiatives" to drive future growth, which are currently at early stages.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Q:** Why is the ATV dropping significantly? **A:** Management clarified it is a deliberate strategy to introduce shorter-duration packages to specific cohorts to boost conversion volume without hurting overall revenue.
2. **Q:** Why not pay a special dividend given the Rs 345 crore cash pile? **A:** Management noted the board is evaluating all ways to reward shareholders and will decide after the buyback cooling-off period.
3. **Q:** Why are Wedding Services still losing money? **A:** Management explained they are pivoting from a subscription model to a commission-based model, which requires initial investment in tech and vendor relationships, but they are "very upbeat" about the growth trajectory.
4. **Q:** Is the 20-22% margin sustainable? **A:** Management believes margins will continue to expand due to operating leverage as the business grows.
5. **Q:** Why the vernacular focus for dating apps? **A:** Management stated regional branding (e.g., Premam, Priyam) creates a stronger consumer connection in India's diverse market compared to a generic pan-India brand.

### One-line takeaway
Matrimony.com delivered a strong quarter with 167% YoY PAT growth, successfully leveraging AI and new subscription tiers to drive volume, while pivoting its loss-making Wedding Services toward a commission-based model.
