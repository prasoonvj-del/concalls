# L&T Technology Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-25 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Accelerating sequential growth across revenue, profitability, large deals, and AI revenue, with EBIT margins achieving mid-16% aspirations ahead of schedule.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** $317.1 million (up 2.7% QoQ and 4.8% YoY in constant currency on continued operations).
- **Rupee Revenue:** Rs 3,040 crore (up 3.4% QoQ and 12.1% YoY).
- **EBIT Margin:** 16.3% (up 60 bps QoQ and 220 bps YoY).
- **Large Deal TCV:** USD 203 million (over 50% AI-led wins).
- **Annualized AI-Led Revenue:** USD 120 million for the quarter.
- **Net Income (PAT):** Rs 548 crore (up 53.7% QoQ and 66.8% YoY, includes a one-time gain of Rs 165 crore from SWC disinvestment).
- **Annualized EPS:** Rs 143.12 (up 23.5% over FY26 EPS).
- **Free Cash Flow (YTD):** Rs 790 crore (108% of net income).
- **Cash and Investments:** Rs 4,197 crore at the end of Q2 FY27.
- **DSO:** 78 days (billed 60 days, unbilled 18 days).
- **Attrition:** 14.7% range bound.
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 20 per share.

### Highlights
- Completed the divestment of the Smart World & Communication (SWC) business on schedule, generating a one-time gain of Rs 165 crore.
- Upskilled more than 16,000 engineers (~67% of the workforce) in AI.
- AI patent filings crossed 253, taking total company patents to 1,808.
- Launched a new EI lab in partnership with NVIDIA for physical AI native applications.
- Sustainability segment grew 13.4% YoY and 4.9% QoQ, contributing ~30% to revenues.
- Set up a dedicated Data Center Engineering business line servicing AI infrastructure, servers, and storage.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated long-term aspirations of 13% to 15% revenue CAGR with EBIT margins in the 16% to 17% range over the next 3 to 5 years.
- Management expects H2 revenue momentum to further strengthen compared to H1 while sustaining healthy EBIT margins in the mid-16% range.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Clients are doubling down on growth despite an unstable environment, shifting technology spending toward AI infrastructure, silicon, cloud platforms, data centers, and time-to-market.
- European Auto remains muted/cautious, but management expects a bounce back next year as companies re-baseline.
- Aerospace continues strong growth driven by production expansion and supply chain modernization.
- Plant Engineering demand is steady across oil & gas, chemicals, and LNG projects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBIT margin reached 16.3%, achieving mid-16% aspirations early.
- Wage hikes are announced for Q3, with an expected impact of 50 to 80 bps, which is factored into near-term margin expectations.
- Offshore mix improved to 55.9%; fixed-price mix improved to 35.6%.
- Effective Tax Rate (ETR) for the quarter was 26%, and is expected to remain between 26% and 26.5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Headcount marginally increased to 24,035 from 23,845 in Q1.
- Expanded partnership ecosystem to include Cognite, Anthropic, NVIDIA, and Databricks.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Overall DSO stood at stable levels of 78 days (well below the guided 80–85 days).
- Cash and investments rose to Rs 4,197 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- European automotive sector remains muted and under restructuring.
- Pure-play software world is impacted by new-age AI tool adoption.
- MedTech segment saw a specific program ramp down, with the next phase kicking in during H2, leading to a muted Q3 for the Tech vertical.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ravi Menon (Axis Capital):** Asked about volatility reduction in Sustainability and European auto headwinds. Management attributed Sustainability stability to breaking business into four categories, digital twin solutions, and AI integration, while noting European Auto should clear up in 9-12 months.
- **Vibhor Singhal:** Inquired about Oil & Gas Middle East exposure and Hi-Tech performance. Management clarified Middle East exposure is very small with impact restricted to U.S. and Europe, and Tech growth will resume in Q4 after a muted Q3 due to MedTech program transitions.
- **Bhavik Mehta (JP Morgan):** Asked what changed in client spending and margin trajectory. Management responded that clients are focused on AI capex and business growth rather than cost-cutting; margins will be maintained at mid-16% while prioritizing growth.
- **Dipesh Mehta:** Asked about the definition of AI-led revenue and wage hike impact. Management stated AI revenue involves significant deliverables created via the AI stack, and wage hikes in Q3 will have a 50-80 bps impact.
- **Karan Uppal:** Asked about deal tenures and AI Data Centre roles. Management noted tenures are slightly increasing (3 to 7 years depending on deal size) and detailed LTTS's role across the 6-7 layers of data center buildouts from physical infrastructure to agentic AI.

### One-line takeaway
LTTS delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with accelerated sequential revenue growth, early achievement of mid-16% EBIT margins, and robust traction in AI-led and Sustainability deal conversions.
