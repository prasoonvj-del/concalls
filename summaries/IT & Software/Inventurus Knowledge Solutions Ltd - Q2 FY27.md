# Inventurus Knowledge Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong consolidated performance following the TruBridge acquisition with doubled revenue and stable legacy margins.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue:** Rs 1,592 crore, up 104% YoY in INR terms (78% QoQ). Constant currency revenue stood at $169 million.
  * Legacy IKS Revenue: $101 million (up >13% YoY in USD terms).
  * Legacy TruBridge Revenue: $68 million (consolidated for 82 days; full-quarter run rate of ~$75 million).
* **EBITDA:** Rs 442 crore; EBITDA margin came in at 28%.
  * Adjusted EBITDA (ex-ESOP): Rs 459 crore (ESOP expense was Rs 17.5 crore).
  * Acquisition-related expenses included in EBITDA: ~Rs 25 crore.
* **PBT:** Rs 273 crore, up ~20% YoY.
* **PAT:** Rs 217 crore, up 20% YoY. 
  * Adjusted PAT (ex-ESOP & intangibles amortization): Rs 273 crore (17% of revenue), up 36% YoY.
* **EPS:** Rs 13, up 20% YoY.
* **Cash Flow & Balance Sheet:** Adjusted Operating Cash Flow (OCF) was Rs 393 crore (90% EBITDA conversion). Net debt stood at Rs 5,280 crore (~$550 million) with net debt/EBITDA <3x.
* **Effective Tax Rate (ETR):** 22.1%. Return on Equity (ROE) stood at 27%.

### Highlights
* Consolidated TruBridge for 82 days during the quarter post-acquisition.
* Retained 3 of TruBridge's top 5 executives (including former CEO Chris Fowler as Chief Strategy Officer and former CHRO Amaris as Global CHRO).
* Deal wins in Legacy IKS: Augusta Health (Mayo Clinic network cross-sell), White-Wilson, Evolve, and UMass Memorial. TruBridge added MISH (specialty surgical hospital).
* Approved ESOP grant of ~4% total equity to top 60–65 leaders, with 80% tied to achieving the FY30 Rs 3,000 crore EBITDA True North target.
* *Break from record:* Successfully integrated TruBridge for its first partial quarter, maintaining legacy IKS EBITDA margins at ~34.5%–35% despite deal expenses.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Revenue Ambition (No formal guidance provided):** 
  * Legacy IKS: Expects to continue outperforming the industry growth rate of 12% in constant currency.
  * TruBridge: FY27 viewed as a stabilization year (flattish revenue). Expects early net growth in FY28 from the System of Action business, scaling towards 10%–12% growth in late FY28/FY29. System of Record (EHR) revenue (~$100 million run rate) targeted to stay flattish.
* **Margins & Long-term Targets:** Targets consolidated EBITDA margins to return to early-to-mid 30s over time, reaffirming the True North target of Rs 3,000 crore ($320 million) EBITDA by FY30.
* **Balance Sheet:** Aiming to bring net debt leverage down to near-zero by FY30.
* **Interest Expense & Tax Rate:** Quarterly interest expense expected around $10 million at current rates. Combined ETR expected at 22%–23% for Q3 and Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
* The US EHR market is highly saturated; TruBridge EHR has strong stickiness across its 600+ rural and community hospital customer base, providing access to patient records for AI training.
* High customer demand within the TruBridge base for IKS's System of Action features (denial prediction/prevention, autonomous coding, and virtual clinical assistants).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Legacy IKS maintained standalone EBITDA margins at 34.5%–35%. Pre-synergy TruBridge delivered a ~$68 million annualized EBITDA run rate.
* Margin synergy levers identified for TruBridge: shifting back-office RCM/coding to global execution models, right-locating tech investments, and SG&A/leadership rationalization. Synergies expected to begin realizing from 2H Q3 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Initiated "Project Endeavor" to build a ground-up, AI-native operating system combining System of Record and System of Intelligence for rural and community hospitals.
* Integrating key IKS System of Action features into legacy TruBridge EHRs by Q1 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Gross debt assumed at transaction close was ~$611 million; net debt reduced to ~$550 million (Rs 5,280 crore).
* Debt structure: 5-year loan facility with an 18-month principal moratorium (repayments start late CY27). Interest rate is SOFR + 2.75%, stepping down to SOFR + 1.75% when leverage drops to 1.7x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Execution bandwidth stretched across a larger combined entity during consolidation.
* WWMG MSO remains loss-making (minority interest loss of Rs 4 crore in Q2), though losses are shrinking.
* Potential industry-wide price compression due to AI automation, though offset by outcome-based pricing models.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **On TruBridge cost synergy areas:** Management highlighted operating costs in financial health/RCM (leveraging global delivery and tech interventions) and SG&A streamlining as primary cost reduction vectors playing out Q3 FY27 onwards.
* **On System of Intelligence architecture:** Management explained that raw EHR data is transactional and lacks context; IKS creates task-specific knowledge graphs (e.g. prior authorizations) to turn disparate EHR entries into structured insights for AI execution.
* **On TruBridge cross-sell capabilities:** Management clarified that TruBridge EHR will not be cross-sold into legacy IKS physician groups due to market specialization, but acute RCM and the Encoder automated coding product will be cross-sold to legacy hospital clients.
* **On AI pricing pressure:** Management noted that unlike time-and-material IT service providers, IKS operates on an outcome-based pricing model, allowing it to retain most AI productivity gains to protect margins.

### One-line takeaway
Inventurus successfully executed initial TruBridge consolidation while holding core legacy margins at ~34.5%–35%, laying the groundwork for AI-driven synergy realization toward its FY30 Rs 3,000 crore EBITDA target.
