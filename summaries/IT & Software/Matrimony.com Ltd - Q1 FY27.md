# Matrimony.com Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management delivered on guidance with PAT increasing by 127% YoY and reported double-digit revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Billing: Rs. 136.0 crores (up 7.8% QoQ, 7.8% YoY)
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs. 130.5 crores (up 11.7% QoQ, 13.2% YoY)
- Matchmaking Billing: Rs. 135.3 crores (up 7.9% QoQ, 8.0% YoY)
- Matchmaking Revenue: Rs. 109.5 crores (up 11.7% QoQ, 13.6% YoY)
- Matchmaking EBITDA: Rs. 34.9 crores (26.9% margin)
- Paid Subscriptions Added: 2.72 lakhs (up 15.9% QoQ, 3.7% YoY)
- Matchmaking ATV: Dropped 6.7% QoQ, grew 4.2% YoY
- Success Stories Facilitated: Over 25,500
- Marriage Services Billing: Rs. 74 lakhs (up 0.5% QoQ, down 13.8% YoY)
- Marriage Services Revenue: Rs. 96 lakhs (up 13.1% QoQ, down 24.3% YoY)
- Marriage Services/Other Initiatives EBITDA Loss: Rs. 3.8 crores (compared to Rs. 5.7 crores in Q4)
- Matchmaking Marketing Expenses: Rs. 46.5 crores (vs Rs. 43.5 crores in Q4, Rs. 46.7 crores YoY)
- Consolidated EBITDA Margin: 20.1% (vs 12.4% in Q4, 11% YoY)
- Tax Rate: 23.8%
- PAT: Rs. 19.1 crores (up 96.2% QoQ, 127.5% YoY)
- Share of Loss from Astro-Vision: Rs. 9 lakhs
- Cash Balance: Rs. 342 crores
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 36.4%
- Bharat Ek Khoj (Startup) Investment: Around Rs. 4 crores

### Highlights
- Delivered guidance with PAT more than doubling YoY (up 127.5%).
- Matchmaking margins reached 26.9% (excluding marketing expenses, margins at 63%).
- Transitioned marriage services and wedding business model from subscription-based to a commission-led model.
- Leveraged AI extensively for profile/photo validations, customer service chatbots, and product efficiency.
- Launched regional versions of love.com, starting with Malayalee Love.
- Legal fees are elevated due to extensive trademark registrations and protections.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects double-digit YoY billing and revenue growth for Matchmaking in Q2 FY27.
- Management expects triple-digit YoY profit growth in Q2 FY27, with profit at similar levels to Q1 or slightly better.
- Marriage services billing in Q2 is expected to be higher than Q1.
- Wedding services aim to scale up to a Rs. 100 crore run rate before contributing meaningfully to profit.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Advertising market shows some softening across India, but the company plans to maintain or slightly step up marketing investments to drive growth.
- Paid subscription demand shows steady continuity, especially with serious matchmaking segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Matchmaking EBITDA margin stood at 26.9% (up from 22% in Q4 and 17.6% YoY).
- Matchmaking margin excluding marketing expenses is 63% (vs 59% in Q4).
- Marriage services quarterly EBITDA losses narrowed to Rs. 3.8 crores due to model adjustments and prior impairment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Maintained a cash balance of Rs. 342 crores, continuously evaluating M&A and investment opportunities.
- Invested Rs. 4 crores in Bharat Ek Khoj (AI astrology startup).
- Expanded love.com regionally (Malayalee Love) with plans for further regional rollouts.
- Manyjobs remains focused on Tamil Nadu and may evaluate a national rollout sometime next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at Rs. 342 crores at the end of Q1.
- ROCE reported at 36.4%.
- Deferred revenue gap between billing and revenue remains around Rs. 3 to 5 crores due to multi-quarter packages (e.g., 1-year package introduced last year).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher legal fees driven by extensive trademark registration and protection costs.
- Execution risk and ongoing losses in newer non-core verticals (marriage services and other initiatives).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhinav M (Aequitas Investments) on revenue recognition and billing gap:** Management explained that revenue is recognized over the subscription period (3 months to 1 year). The billing-to-revenue gap of ~Rs. 5 crores stems from the introduction of 1-year packages, and realization typically runs at 97% to 99% depending on the quarter.
2. **Vasudevan (Individual Investor) on sustainability of 100%+ profit growth:** Management clarified that the jump is a combination of continuous double-digit growth momentum and deferred revenue catch-up from last year's 1-year packages, and reiterated expectations for strong triple-digit YoY profit growth in Q2.
3. **Shrinivas (Individual Investor) on AI use cases:** Management detailed that AI is deployed for automated profile/photo validations, AI customer service chatbots, and product/customer experience enhancements.
4. **Mani (Individual Investor) on love.com traction:** Management stated love.com targets serious matchmaking as a 100% verified platform, is expanding regionally (Malayalee Love), and will see increased investments for profile acquisition and product growth.
5. **Pranay Shah on ATV decline:** Management noted that ATV dropped 6.7% QoQ due to pricing levers, data analytics discounts, and multiple product tiers (like Jodi and love.com), advising analysts not to read too much into quarterly ATV fluctuations.
6. **Ankur Jain on wedding services breakeven and ad spends:** Management stated the focus is on reaching a Rs. 100 crore run rate under the new commission model rather than immediate breakeven, with clarity expected in about a year; ad spends will not be reduced as they intend to invest further in growth initiatives.

### One-line takeaway
Matrimony.com delivered a stellar Q1 FY27 with a 127.5% YoY surge in PAT and robust double-digit matchmaking revenue growth, while actively scaling regional initiatives and AI-driven efficiencies.
