# Unicommerce Esolutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent revenue growth momentum, successful scaling of new products and Shipway, and a clear path toward improving profitability in H2.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 59.1 crore, up 15% YoY (vs Rs 51.4 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 110.4 crore, up 14.7% YoY (vs Rs 96.3 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 10 crore (margin at 17%), vs Rs 11.4 crore (margin at 22.2%) in Q2 FY26.
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 18.1 crore (margin at 16.4%).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT - Q2 FY27):** Rs 6.4 crore, vs Rs 5.8 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 11.1 crore.
- **Cash and Bank Balances:** Rs 104.4 crore as of end-Q2 FY27, up nearly 65% YoY.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 11.6 crore, up 20.6% YoY.
- **Shipway ARR:** Crossed Rs 100 crore in annualized revenue run rate, growing 15.7% YoY.
- **Uniware Enterprise Client Additions:** 121 clients added in Q2 FY27 (vs 103 in Q2 FY26), up 17.5% YoY.
- **New Modules (UniReco & UniCapture):** Crossed Rs 3 crore ARR (contributing ~2.5% of Uniware revenue); used by over 150 clients (UniReco attach rate 7-8%, UniCapture 5-6%).
- **International Business Contribution:** ~6% of Uniware revenue (doubled from ~3% at listing).
- **Uniware Segment Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 36% in Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
- Shipway crossed the milestone of Rs 100 crore in annualized revenue run rate.
- Launched DNA, a second-generation AI companion for Uniware, moving software from a system of record to a system of action (currently in beta with 10-15 customers).
- International business has doubled its revenue contribution over two years while turning profitable.
- Traditional enterprises and digital-first brands added this quarter include UCB, Amul Macho, Furlenco, Pushp Masale, JK Maini by Raymond, and Nish Hair.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Uniware Revenue Growth:** Management expects >15% YoY revenue growth from Q4 FY27 onwards.
- **Shipway Revenue Growth:** Management expects YoY revenue growth to accelerate to >20% from Q4 FY27 onwards.
- **Shipway Profitability:** Management expects Shipway to achieve adjusted EBITDA profitability starting Q3 FY27, followed by PAT profitability starting Q4 FY27.
- **Consolidated Profitability:** Pace of incremental investments is expected to moderate in H2 FY27, leading to improving margins and operating leverage.
- **Long-term Margins:** Steady-state adjusted EBITDA margins are planned to return to 20%+ levels.

### Demand & industry commentary
- B2B and quick commerce offerings are now utilized by 45% to 50% of the client base, resulting in a heterogeneous transaction mix where realization per transaction is lower due to bulk items.
- International markets (Middle East and Southeast Asia) are growing faster than domestic business; steady-state international contribution expected to reach early double digits.
- Brands typically mandate fulfillment partners to use Unicommerce's OMS/WMS, turning 3PLs into partners rather than competitors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 profitability reflected frontloaded planned investments in product development, talent, sales, and marketing.
- Uniware gross margins trend around 80%, while Shipway gross margins trend around 20% (industry benchmarks).
- Lower tax expense in H1 was due to recognized deferred tax on account of expected profitability in Shipway from Q3.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Peak single-day transactions handled reached 60 lakh processing volume this Diwali season with 2x growth in quick commerce peak processing.
- Actively scouting for strategic M&As that fit four criteria: synergistic for existing customers, good product/team, profitable or clear path to profitability, and reasonable valuation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with cash and bank balances at Rs 104.4 crores.
- Operating cash flows grew by 20.6% YoY to Rs 11.6 crores in Q2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management as structural risks; normal business considerations include ramp-up timelines for new enterprise clients (6 to 9 months).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Praneeth (SJ Investments) asked about falling average realization per order and employee step-up:** Management explained that the drop in realization rate is a mix-shift artifact from adding B2B and quick commerce (bulk items) rather than a price drop, and investments in AI/tech were proactive to maintain leadership.
- **Rajas Shah (BTH Capital) asked about lower tax expense in H1:** Management clarified it reflects deferred tax recognition due to Shipway turning profitable from Q3.
- **Pratik Banthia asked about revenue realization multiples when combining modules:** Management stated that combining UniReco and UniCapture takes realization to 1.5x-1.7x initially, stabilizing around 1.2x-1.3x.
- **Arvind Arora asked about the timeline for 20% EBITDA margins and M&A progress:** Management stated margins will gradually trend back to 20%+ as operating leverage kicks in, and they remain patient on M&A without rushing into deals.
- **Madhur Rathi asked about TAM getting squeezed by 3PL players:** Management clarified that brands mandate Unicommerce's tech stack to fulfillment partners, so 3PLs act as partners, not competitors.

### One-line takeaway
Unicommerce delivered 15% YoY revenue growth to Rs 59.1 crore in Q2 FY27, backed by scaling new AI and SaaS modules, while guiding for accelerated growth and profitability inflection in H2 FY27.
