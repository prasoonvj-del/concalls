# Hinduja Global Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue moderated slightly and profitability absorbed front-loaded investments and planned legacy contract phase-outs, but the company saw strong new logo additions and broadband momentum.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 1,050.4 crores (down 3.2% QoQ, down 0.6% YoY)
- Total income: Rs. 1,201.2 crores (down 4.3% QoQ, up 1.2% YoY)
- Total EBITDA: Rs. 116.3 crores
- EBITDA margin: 9.7% (compared to 15.7% in previous quarter and 13.5% YoY)
- Depreciation: Rs. 123.6 crores
- Interest cost: Rs. 45.5 crores
- Other income: Rs. 150.8 crores
- PBT before exceptional items: Negative Rs. (-52.8) crores
- Total PAT: Negative Rs. (-66.3) crores
- Operating expenses: Rs. 1,084.9 crores
- Total assets: Rs. 11,474 crores as of June '26
- Total equity: Rs. 8,402.5 crores as of June '26
- Gross treasury and cash surplus: Rs. 6,605 crores
- Total borrowings: Rs. 1,279 crores
- Net treasury and cash surplus: Rs. 5,326 crores
- New logos added: 19 new logos in CX and digital services; 8 clients in HRO and payroll processing

### Highlights
- Planned transition and phase-out of large legacy contracts occurred on schedule.
- Incorporated HGS MENA IT Consulting LLC in Dubai to build technology and consulting capabilities across the MENA region.
- Launched "Project Ganga" (Government Assisted Network for Growth and Advancement) on June 9, 2026, in Uttar Pradesh, aiming to connect over 2 million households with high-speed broadband and train 8,000 to 10,000 entrepreneurs (DSP model). Received around 2,000 applications and trained about 500 applicants as of August 4.
- CelerityX (enterprise business of OIL) secured new logos and repeat orders from corporates.
- High-speed broadband adoption (101 Mbps to >200 Mbps) rose from 11% last year to 15% this quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains cautiously optimistic, noting macro uncertainties and client prudence.
- Management expects a gradual improvement in both growth and margins through the year as volumes normalize and investments commercialize.
- Management expects the phase-out of legacy labor-priced contracts to conclude before the end of the current fiscal year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprises are accelerating the shift from AI pilots to AI execution, focusing on ROI, speed-to-value, governance, and scalability.
- Clients are increasingly seeking outcome-linked and non-headcount commercial structures alongside technology-rich and offshore-weighted delivery.
- Linear TV / digital television faces global and industry-wide headwinds, mitigated via bundling, IPTV, and cost optimization.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin declined to 9.7% due to immediate margin impact from ramp and training costs of new tech-heavy business, flat operating expenses against moderating revenue, and front-loading of investments in sales, solutions, domain hiring, and AI capability builds (including Agent X® and the 90-day proof of value model).
- "Project Ganga" is structured on a no-profit, no-loss basis for network rollout support, utilizing internal resources without negative cash flow.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Funding growth initiatives, including "Project Ganga," primarily through internal accruals.
- Expanding delivery and talent footprint across global geographies (India, US, UK, Philippines, Canada).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross cash and treasury surplus stands at Rs. 6,605 crores against borrowings of Rs. 1,279 crores, maintaining a net cash surplus of Rs. 5,326 crores.
- Collection discipline continues to hold and working capital metrics remain stable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macro uncertainties and client prudence/caution regarding AI readiness, data stories, and governance structures.
- Headwinds in the digital television (linear TV) business.
- Initial margin compression during the ramp-up and training phase of new client engagements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankit Jain (Gravita Consulting) asked about Project Ganga's margins and whether it would be a negative cash flow play.** 
   *Management answered* that it operates on a no-profit, no-loss basis for rollout support using in-house resources and existing capabilities, and it will not be a negative cash flow play.
2. **Aniruddha Sharma (Ekant Investments) asked about the timeline for new CX/digital logo revenue ramp-up.** 
   *Management answered* that it typically takes 6 to 8 months, with initial months incurring training and onboarding costs before becoming margin-attractive before the end of the year.
3. **Prisha Shah (Shah family office) asked if clients are moving from pilots to production in Agentic AI and what the timeline is.** 
   *Management answered* that while demand is high, customer readiness regarding data and governance varies; they expect deployments to scale progressively over the course of the year.
4. **Prisha Shah asked where HGS maintains a sustainable edge in AI given commoditized underlying tech.** 
   *Management answered* that commoditization benefits HGS because foundation model costs drop, allowing HGS to focus on proprietary process re-engineering and applying models to specific customer workflows.
5. **Aniruddha Sharma asked how the intelligent experience positioning has changed client conversations.** 
   *Management answered* that it is generating significant traction and excitement, leading to multi-tower deal opportunities involving data integration, cleaning, and AI-enabled operations.

### One-line takeaway
HGS reported a margin-compressed quarter due to legacy contract run-offs and front-loaded investments in AI and broadband initiatives, but maintains a strong net cash balance sheet and strong new logo momentum.
