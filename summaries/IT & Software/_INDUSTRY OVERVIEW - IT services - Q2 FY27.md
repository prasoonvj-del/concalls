# IT & Software - IT services: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Management commentaries across 49 IT and software services companies reveal a stark operational divergence. Multi-billion-dollar tier-1 IT services giants face persistent client caution, extended decision cycles, and soft discretionary spending in traditional application development, leading to muted revenue trajectories and select guidance cuts (e.g., Infosys trimming full-year guidance to 1%–2%). Conversely, specialist firms positioned in AI infrastructure compute, data center buildouts, digital healthcare, and AI-native platforms report record order books, accelerating deal conversions, and strong sequential margin expansion (e.g., E2E Networks, Black Box, Firstsource, and Sagility). 

While traditional time-and-material billings face client-enforced productivity deflation, companies adopting non-linear, outcome-based pricing models and agentic AI automation are expanding margins despite absorbing annual wage increments.

---

## Where the industry is heading

### Business model so far
Historically, Indian IT services relied heavily on effort-based time-and-material (T&M) delivery models and offshore headcount arbitrage. Over the last five years, the industry began shifting toward fixed-price digital transformation, managed service annuities, and platform-based SaaS integrations. [Background]

### Shifts underway
- **Agentic & Full-Stack AI Integration**
  - *Stage:* Scaling  
  - *Evidence:* TCS generated over $3.1 billion in annualized AI revenue; Infosys reported AI revenue at 10.5% of total spend (up 29% QoQ); HCLTech's Advanced AI revenue reached $219 million (up 78% YoY); Firstsource doubled its AI-led revenue to INR 30.5 crores; Digitide doubled AI-led revenue to INR 30.5 crores; Birlasoft generates ~33% of code via Agentic AI tools [TCS concall Q2 FY27; Infosys concall Q2 FY27; HCLTech concall Q2 FY27; Firstsource concall Q2 FY27; Digitide concall Q2 FY27; Birlasoft concall Q2 FY27].  
  - *Impact on earnings:* Unlocks higher-value transformation programs and offsets volume deflation in legacy coding.
- **GPU Compute & Sovereign AI Infrastructure**
  - *Stage:* Early  
  - *Evidence:* E2E Networks reported Q2 revenue of Rs 2,238 crore (up 411% YoY) following Blackwell GPU deployment and a ~Rs 1,000 crore Sovereign AI deal; ESDS Software is deploying an 8,200 GPU Sharon AI cluster targeting Rs 288 crore monthly revenue; Black Box's order backlog reached $1.04 billion behind $250M+ H1 data center bookings [E2E Networks concall Q2 FY27; ESDS Software concall Q2 FY27; Black Box concall Q2 FY27].  
  - *Impact on earnings:* Transforms revenue profiles toward capital-intensive, high-margin, multi-year infrastructure commitments.
- **Outcome-Based & Value-Engineering Pricing Models**
  - *Stage:* Scaling  
  - *Evidence:* Happiest Minds launched "Project Reimagine" to transition from effort-based delivery to customer value engineering using AI; Inventurus Knowledge Solutions and Digitide enforce outcome-based contracts in healthcare RCM and BPM [Happiest Minds concall Q2 FY27; Inventurus concall Q2 FY27; Digitide concall Q2 FY27].  
  - *Impact on earnings:* Protects realization rates and profit margins as AI automation reduces required billable hours.
- **Direct Enterprise Relationships over Partner-Led/Consortium Models**
  - *Stage:* Scaling  
  - *Evidence:* Allied Digital is pivoting away from partner-led/consortium models to direct engagements, expanding services annuity contracts by 31% YoY [Allied Digital concall Q2 FY27].  
  - *Impact on earnings:* Increases ownership of client tech budgets and improves working capital cycles.

### Government push
- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act:** Driving enterprise cloud migration, data sovereignty, and security audit spend ahead of compliance deadlines [Govt: DPDP Act, Nov 2026; ESDS concall Q2 FY27; iValue concall Q2 FY27].
- **IndiaAI Mission:** Catalyzing domestic compute infrastructure and sovereign language model deployments [Govt: IndiaAI Mission, Nov 2026; E2E Networks concall Q2 FY27; Tech Mahindra concall Q2 FY27].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
Enterprises globally remain disciplined with discretionary IT budgets due to macro volatility, elevated interest rates, and geopolitical uncertainty. Large transformation contracts face prolonged evaluation cycles and aggressive competitive pricing during renewals.

### Divergence
- **Weakness / Headwinds:**
  - *Retail, Hi-Tech, CPG, and Communications:* Softness flagged by Infosys, Latent View, Wipro, and Birlasoft due to client budget cuts, insourcing, and lower third-party revenues.
  - *US Healthcare Payers:* Face short-term scope recalibrations and regulatory shifts (Wipro, eClerx, Infosys).
- **Strength / Tailwinds:**
  - *AI Infrastructure & Data Centers:* Hyperscaler compute demand is surging (Black Box, E2E Networks, ESDS, Aurionpro).
  - *BFSI Recovery:* Rebounding driven by legacy modernization, agentic AI, and vendor consolidation (TCS, Mphasis, Firstsource, Tech Mahindra, Hexaware).
  - *Pharma & Healthcare Services:* Outperforming via AI-driven RCM, clinical workflows, and flexible partner engagement (Indegene, Sagility, Inventurus).

## Costs, raw materials & margins

- **Wage Increments:** Annual compensation hikes compressed margins in Q2 across several players (Birlasoft -170 bps, Indegene -150 bps, Mastek -190 bps, Latent View -100 to -120 bps), while others phased implementation across Q3/Q4 (Cyient, Infosys).
- **Talent & Tool Investments:** Front-loading technical hires and licensing AI development platforms created short-term cost drag (Black Box, Persistent -90 bps tool amortization, Latent View, Nucleus Software).
- **Margin Defenses:** Companies defended margins via record utilization (Infosys 85.4%, Sasken 86%, Persistent 85.8%), right-shoring/offshoring mix (Firstsource, Birlasoft), and internal AI efficiency tools (Ceinsys generated ~Rs 25–30 crore cost benefits; Mastek realized 200 bps AI efficiencies).

## Capex & capacity

- **Data Center & GPU Capex:** ESDS is executing a Rs 1,500 crore domestic AI factory capex; Xtranet is investing Rs 130–140 crore in a 2MW data center facility; HCLTech invested INR 185 crore in a Bengaluru semiconductor lab and planned a Odisha AI data center.
- **Headcount Expansion:** Focused hiring in specialized AI, data center, and BPM roles. Firstsource added 3,062 employees; Black Box onboarded 300 technical talent with 700–800 more planned; TCS added 10,000 campus graduates; Infosys added ~4,400 net employees.
- **Delivery Centers:** Ceinsys incorporated a UAE subsidiary; Digitide set up a Coimbatore AI COE and Bangalore lab; Nanta Tech inaugurated experience centers in Ahmedabad and Mumbai.

## What changed since last quarter

- **Guidance Revisions:**
  - *Cut:* Infosys narrowed FY27 constant currency revenue growth guidance to 1%–2% (from 1.5%–3.0%). Capitalnumbers missed H1 targets following a failed Middle East expansion. Hexaware withdrew its Q4 CY26 revenue outlook.
  - *Raised:* Firstsource raised the lower end of its revenue growth guidance to 10.5%–13% CC. Mphasis upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to low double digits. Sagility raised FY27 adjusted EBITDA margin guidance to 25%–26%. Workmates raised FY27 revenue guidance to Rs 202 crore. Nanta Tech raised full-year revenue growth guidance to 45%–50%.
- **Execution & Tone Shifts:**
  - Aurionpro shifted to "strong momentum" as peak R&D investments moderated.
  - Birlasoft confirmed its exit from non-profitable accounts is fully complete.
  - Persistent absorbed ~$19–20 million in one-time Nagarro deal expenses while maintaining 20.8% YoY CC revenue growth.

## What to watch next quarter

- **Q3 Seasonal Furloughs:** Impact on billable hours for tier-1 IT providers (Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Birlasoft, Hexaware).
- **Go-Live Milestones:** Ready-for-service testing of ESDS's 8,200-GPU Sharon AI cluster in December 2026.
- **H2 Revenue Acceleration & Collections:** Ceinsys expects ~Rs 250 crore in cleared government payments; iValue targets ~60% of annual sales in H2; Excelsoft expects EBITDA margins to recover to 22%–25%.
- **M&A Integrations:** Closing of Persistent's Nagarro acquisition financing, Mphasis's Red Oak acquisition ($28M), and Happiest Minds' merger progress with ITC Infotech.
