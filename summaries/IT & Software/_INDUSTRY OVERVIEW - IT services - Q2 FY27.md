# IT & Software - IT services: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is in the midst of a structural pivot toward "AI-native" operations. Managements are moving beyond mere experimentation, with many companies (e.g., **Aurionpro**, **Mphasis**, **Sagility**) reporting that AI is now a core driver of revenue and operational efficiency. While the long-term outlook remains anchored in AI-led transformation, the near-term environment is characterized by a "tale of two cities": companies with strong AI-led deal pipelines are seeing growth, while those exposed to discretionary spending in Hi-Tech and Retail are facing headwinds. A common theme is the deliberate rationalization of low-margin businesses to improve profitability.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is universal agreement that AI is the primary demand driver. Companies are increasingly moving from "effort-based" to "outcome-based" or "agentic" AI models.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Strong Momentum:** **Mphasis** reported its strongest sequential growth in three years, raising FY27 guidance. **Firstsource** raised the lower end of its revenue guidance, citing 10 consecutive quarters of double-digit growth. **Nanta Tech** saw 97.7% YoY revenue growth, driven by robotics and AI.
    *   **Macro/Budgetary Headwinds:** **Infosys** cut its revenue guidance, citing softness in Retail and Hi-Tech. **eClerx** noted that deal wins (ACV) softened due to client decision delays. **Latent View** and **Hexaware** highlighted consolidation and budget cuts in the technology vertical. **Ksolves** reported a revenue decline due to client-specific ramp-downs.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Wage Inflation:** Annual wage hikes were a universal margin headwind (e.g., **Birlasoft**, **Cyient**, **eClerx**, **Firstsource**, **Mastek**).
*   **AI Productivity:** Companies are increasingly using AI to offset margin pressure. **Birlasoft** noted ~33% of code is now generated by AI. **Ksolves** reported that AI deployment increased engineer output by 2x-3x. **Mastek** noted AI-led efficiencies helped offset margin pressure from salary hikes.
*   **Margin Strategy:** There is a clear focus on "right-shoring" and operational leverage. **Datamatics** and **Happiest Minds** reported margin expansion driven by operational leverage. **Digitide** and **Alldigi** are actively exiting low-margin domestic businesses to improve the overall mix.

## Capex & capacity & expansion
*   **AI Infrastructure:** A significant shift toward building AI-specific infrastructure is underway. **ESDS** is deploying an 8,200-GPU Sharon AI cluster. **Xtranet** formed a dedicated data center subsidiary. **Nanta Tech** is scaling its robotics manufacturing.
*   **"Atoms-to-Bits":** **Aurionpro** highlighted a strategy of integrating software with hardware manufacturing for data centers and transit, while **Orient Technologies** is investing in cybersecurity and managed services capacity.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Revisions:** **Infosys** cut its revenue growth guidance (1% to 2% from 1.5% to 3%). **Firstsource** raised the lower end of its revenue guidance. **Xtranet** and **Nanta Tech** indicated they expect to surpass original revenue targets.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Aurionpro** shifted from "cautious" to "strong momentum." **Birlasoft** moved from "cautious/mixed" to "confident." **Hexaware** saw new leadership initiate a 90-day strategic review following missed revenue expectations.
*   **Operational Focus:** **Datamatics** shifted focus from "hunting" new logos to "mining" existing accounts.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **H2 Seasonality:** Watch for the impact of Q3 furloughs, which **Birlasoft** and **Hexaware** flagged as a potential soft spot.
*   **AI Monetization:** Monitor whether the "AI-led" deal wins (e.g., **Mphasis**, **Mastek**) translate into sustained margin expansion or if pricing pressure persists.
*   **Guidance Credibility:** Watch if companies like **Infosys** and **eClerx** see stabilization in their respective verticals (Retail/Hi-Tech/BFSI).
*   **Cash Flow:** Keep an eye on working capital and cash conversion, as **Mphasis** and **Workmates** noted quarterly fluctuations that need to normalize.
