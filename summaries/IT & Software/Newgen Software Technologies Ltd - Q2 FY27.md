# Newgen Software Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in meeting double-digit growth targets for the full year, citing a strong pipeline and recovery in H2 despite a soft Q2 license revenue performance.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 422 crore (+5.4% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 779 crore (+8.8% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 109 crore (+6.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 25.8%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 87 crore (+6.3% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 20.6%.
*   **SaaS Revenue Growth:** 36.4% YoY.
*   **Annuity Revenue Growth:** 15% YoY.
*   **License Revenue:** Declined 19.2% YoY.
*   **Net Receivables:** Rs 642 crore.
*   **Net DSO:** 144 days.

### Highlights
*   **Regional Performance:** EMEA grew 19.4%; US grew 17.1%. India declined 12.6% and APAC declined 6.4% due to timing and execution variability.
*   **New Wins:** 9 new logo additions in Q2.
*   **Key Deals:** USD 5.5 million (3-year) deal with a US health insurance company; USD 1.8 million deal with a Qatar government body; INR 11.4 crore deal with an EMEA bank.
*   **AI Progress:** Released NewgenONE AI Studio (Agent Studio 1.0 and Agent Flow 2.0.2). Completed 10 AI-based deal bookings in H1 across banking, financial services, and government.
*   **Revenue Mix:** Subscription revenue surpassed license revenue for the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management reiterated the target of double-digit growth for FY27.
*   **H2 Expectation:** Management expects acceleration in Q3 and Q4, driven by a strong pipeline and the normalization of the base effect from the previous year.
*   **Margins:** Guidance for net margin remains 20-22% for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **AI Adoption:** Customers are shifting from AI experimentation to production, focusing on explainability, traceability, and control.
*   **Banking Sector:** Large banks are currently in the POC (Proof of Concept) stage for AI in core business areas (lending, trade finance) due to strict regulatory compliance, while adoption is faster in call centers/service requests.
*   **Deal Sizes:** AI-embedded deals are seeing 20-40% higher deal sizes compared to non-AI deals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Discipline:** Expenses have stabilized over the last 4-6 quarters.
*   **Manpower:** Management stated they have sufficient staff strength and do not foresee a need for significant headcount growth to deliver future growth, leveraging AI for operational efficiency.
*   **R&D/Marketing Spend:** ~10-11% of revenue goes to product development; ~22% to sales and marketing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Not discussed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Receivables:** Net DSO is 144 days. Management attributed high receivables to delays in collections in the Middle East region.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution/Timing:** Quarterly performance remains sensitive to the timing of large license transactions and regional conversion cycles.
*   **Collections:** High DSO and collection delays in the Middle East.
*   **India/APAC Weakness:** Subdued performance in India and APAC due to deal closure delays.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On License Revenue Decline:** Management clarified the decline was due to the timing of closures and a high base effect from Q2 FY26, not a weakening of demand.
*   **On AI Impact on Revenue:** Management noted that AI deals increase deal sizes by 20-40% and improve time-to-value by 15-20%, but they are not bundling operational costs into deal sizes.
*   **On Employee Cost/Efficiency:** Management confirmed they are not planning significant hiring, as AI engineering and operational efficiencies allow them to scale with current manpower.
*   **On Buybacks:** Management stated the Board is considering buyback proposals, though no immediate action was confirmed; they emphasized a commitment to increasing dividends.
*   **On Banking Sector Slowdown:** Management explained that banks are cautious about AI in core operations due to regulatory hurdles, leading to longer conversion cycles from POC to production.

### One-line takeaway
Newgen delivered a steady Q2 with resilient margins and strong SaaS growth, and management remains confident in achieving double-digit annual growth despite temporary headwinds in license closures and collections.
