# Ceinsys Tech Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong order inflow momentum, improved margins, and clear prospects for working capital liquidation.

### Key numbers
- **Operational Revenue:** INR 158 crore (marginal YoY growth of 1%)
- **EBITDA:** INR 39 crore (up 27% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 24.4% (expansion of 505 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 31 crore (marginal decline YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 19.6% (contraction of ~59 bps YoY)
- **Geospatial Engineering Services Revenue:** INR 94 crore (up 30% YoY)
- **Technology Solutions Revenue:** INR 63 crore (down 25% YoY)
- **Fresh Order Inflows (Q1):** INR 143 crore
- **Order Book:** INR 990 crore as of end-Q1
- **Working Capital Cycle:** 164 days

### Highlights
- Secured fresh contracts worth INR 143 crore during the quarter.
- Received a Letter of Intent (LoI) from the Director of Urban Administration and Development, Madhya Pradesh, for a manpower agency under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (approx. INR 67 crore, to be executed over 3 years).
- Received an LoI from Bhandara Municipal Council for AMR water meters under AMRUT 2.0 (order value INR 17 crore, 12-month execution).
- International mobility and US subsidiary saw meaningful improvement with new business opportunities.
- Initiated an investment of up to INR 25 crore in a JV with AI Fabric USA to create a sovereign AI cloud in India for the defense/government sector.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide forward-looking revenue or margin guidance as a matter of policy.
- Management reiterated confidence in surpassing last year's operational numbers and maintaining growth trends seen over the last 2.5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Order book execution timeline has a weighted average of 12 to 18 months, plus 2 to 5 years of O&M for certain projects.
- Non-order book run-rate businesses (such as mobility and OEM products) constitute roughly 20-25% of annual revenue.
- Government of Maharashtra issued a Government Resolution (GR) to disburse blocked funds for IoT/Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) projects, expected to clear dues by Q3/end of FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins expanded due to scaling into enterprise solutions (higher on the maturity scale) rather than mere data acquisition.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Approved an investment of up to INR 25 crore in phases (INR 5 crore initial, INR 20 crore post-due diligence) for a 50-50 JV with AI Fabric USA for GPU-as-a-service and AI services.
- Actively evaluating inorganic growth opportunities within geospatial engineering and technology solutions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital days stood at 164 days.
- Unbilled revenue (UBR) elevated largely due to JJM projects (~INR 100 crore of IoT debtors), which management expects to recover by Q3/H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Past elongation of the working capital cycle due to delayed collections in Jal Jeevan Mission IoT projects (though resolution is underway via state government GR).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments):** Asked about the reconciliation of order book movements and non-order book revenue. Management clarified that run-rate businesses (mobility, OEM) do not enter the order book, accounting for ~20-25% of turnover.
2. **Pujan Shah (Molecule Ventures):** Inquired about the AI Fabric JV business model and JJM collections. Management explained it targets sovereign defense cloud services (GPU/model-as-a-service) and expects JJM collections within 2-3 quarters.
3. **Gunit Singh (Individual Investor):** Questioned why raised funds have been lying idle and suggested a share buyback. Management responded that funds are earmarked for strategic growth/investments rather than temporary cash surplus buybacks, and deployment must be value-accretive.
4. **Keshav Garg (PMS):** Raised concerns over low operating cash conversion relative to EBITDA and high unbilled revenue (UBR). Management attributed UBR to JJM milestones and reiterated that collections will normalize by year-end.

### One-line takeaway
Ceinsys Tech reported flat top-line growth but strong EBITDA margin expansion (24.4%), backed by a healthy INR 990 crore order book and anticipated working capital recovery from stalled government dues.
