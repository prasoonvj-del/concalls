# Workmates Core2Cloud Solution Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 27.5% YoY, recurring revenue mix improved to 87%, and management raised the FY27 revenue outlook.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 95.21 crore (up 27.5% YoY).
*   **Recurring Revenue:** Rs 82.97 crore (up 43% YoY).
*   **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Rs 15.9 crore (as of September 2026).
*   **Gross Profit:** Rs 31.92 crore (up 24.8% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 33.5% (vs 34.3% in H1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 16.44 crore (up 24% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 17.3% (vs 17.7% in H1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 12.03 crore (up 32.4% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 12.6% (vs 12.2% in H1 FY26).
*   **EPS:** Rs 9.32.
*   **Customer Base:** 337 (up from 316).
*   **Enterprise Customers:** 44 (up from 38).

### Highlights
*   **Revenue Mix:** Recurring revenue now accounts for 87% of total revenue (vs 77% a year ago).
*   **Churn:** Annualized gross churn was 2.6%.
*   **AI Accelerators:** Six AI accelerators are in live production; AI and cybersecurity contribute ~5% of total revenue.
*   **Partnerships:** Signed a strategic partnership with OpenAI to enable model customization.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Headcount remained flat at 169; revenue per employee rose to Rs 56.56 crore.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** US subsidiary registered; Singapore operations are active; Middle East expansion triggered.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue Outlook:** Raised to Rs 202 crore (from previous guidance of Rs 190.56 crore).
*   **PAT Ambition:** Maintained at Rs 24 crore to Rs 25 crore.
*   **H2 Pipeline:** Weighted pipeline of Rs 110 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted strong demand for AI-ready infrastructure, FinOps, and security.
*   Financial services is the largest vertical (41% of revenue).
*   Management stated that AWS has not increased prices for services or GPUs despite capacity constraints.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Cloud consumption costs rose 72 bps to 66.5% of revenue, impacting gross margins.
*   Employee costs decreased to 10.8% of revenue (from 11.2%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Rs 2.36 crore spent in H1, primarily on AI accelerator development.
*   **Accounting:** AI accelerator costs are capitalized as intangible assets and amortized over five years.
*   **Utilization:** Billable utilization held at 84%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Company remains debt-free.
*   **Cash:** Cash and liquid investments stood at Rs 43.88 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** Trade receivables rose to Rs 39.5 crore (from Rs 28.99 crore in March); debtor days moved from 64 to 62 days.
*   **Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow for the half was negative Rs 4.7 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Working Capital:** Management identified collection as the main financial priority for H2 to convert profit into cash.
*   **Project Revenue:** Management noted project revenue is cyclical and can fluctuate quarter-to-quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On AI Monetization:** Management stated AI accelerators (e.g., VoxoRA, VerID) are being used as a "spearhead" to enter new accounts and cross-sell to existing ones.
*   **On AWS Dependency:** Management clarified they are loyal to AWS but have the capability to manage other clouds (Azure, GCP) if required by clients.
*   **On Cybersecurity:** Management confirmed cybersecurity is a major growth driver and is "bolted on" to all AI accelerators by design.
*   **On Singapore Strategy:** Management clarified that the Singapore go-to-market strategy is being "re-evaluated" to match market conditions, but no costs were written off.
*   **On Inorganic Growth:** Management is open to acquisitions in AI, cybersecurity, or analytics if the right proposition arises, but no talks are currently underway.

### One-line takeaway
Workmates delivered strong H1 growth driven by recurring revenue and AI adoption, leading to an upward revision in FY27 revenue guidance despite negative operating cash flow.
