# Dev Information Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-26 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and EBITDA grew YoY, new significant orders secured, and international partnerships are scaling.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** INR62.63 crores, up 29.11% YoY
- **Q2 FY27 Total Income:** INR63.07 crores, up 28.25% YoY
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR5.21 crores, up 36.51% YoY
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 8.28%, up 50 bps YoY
- **Q2 FY27 Profit Attributable to Shareholders:** INR2.96 crores
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** INR106.76 crores, up 16.68% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** INR107.72 crores, up 16.28% YoY
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR9.19 crores, up 17.18% YoY
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 8.53%
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow:** INR4.78 crores
- **H1 FY27 Profit Attributable to Shareholders:** INR5.03 crores
- **Consolidated Borrowings:** INR19.59 crores as of September end (down from INR29.12 crores in March)
- **Trade Receivables:** INR84.73 crores (down from INR90.95 crores in March)
- **Q3 FY27 Initial Order Wins:** INR19.39 crores total across 3 orders (Vadodara Municipal Corporation: INR15.50 crores; Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation: INR1.58 crores; Bhavnagar Municipal Corporation: INR2.31 crores)
- **XDuce Partnership Revenue:** Monthly revenue started over INR1 crore from July/August 2026; expected INR8 crores to INR10 crores for the current year.

### Highlights
- Secured multi-year orders expanding the domestic order book to over INR50 crores.
- Top 10 customers contribute approximately 60% to 70% of revenue.
- Note: Compared to records, the company continued its domestic public sector order wins with major municipal and transit contracts.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue to be around INR200 crores to INR210 crores.
- Target to increase EBITDA margins by at least 50 bps annually.
- Expects blockchain business to pick up in the next couple of quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overseas demand focuses on offshore inquiries, AI-related solutions, and hybrid onshore-offshore models.
- India domestic market remains price-sensitive, yielding lower margins (10%-15%) compared to private/export business (20%-30%), but provides volume and visibility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Focus on skilling existing personnel rather than higher-cost external hiring to manage margins.
- Domestic government order growth has a dilutive effect on overall margins due to lower pricing power.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investing cash flows into resources, new-age technologies (ABCD strategy: AI, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, Data/Data Center), and geographical expansion (UK, Europe, Australia, New Zealand).
- ScaleAX (GCC as a service startup with DevX, Savvy Infrastructure, and Awfficacy) has secured its initial 2 orders focusing on GIFT City.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings reduced by INR9.53 crores to INR19.59 crores.
- Trade receivables stand at INR84.73 crores, with about INR20 crores from government sector overdue by 3 to 4 months; remainder is typically 45 days.
- Government contracts require 10% performance bank guarantees and 1%-5% EMD.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- India market business operates on tighter margins (10%-15%), impacting consolidated margins.
- Blockchain business has been slow to pick up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mayur Parikh (VY Capital) on Q-o-Q revenue jump and margin dip:** Management explained that sequential growth stems from multi-year order execution, while margin moderation is partly due to a higher mix of price-sensitive domestic government orders and internal skilling.
2. **Keval Gala (Gala Ventures) on trade receivables aging:** Management stated that ~INR20 crores is from government clients (3-4 months old), while the rest is cleared in 45 days, seeing no red alerts.
3. **Aman Shekhawat (Family Office) on ScaleAX/GCC commercial progress:** Management noted Scaleax has started securing orders (2 in place) providing end-to-end "coffee to cloud, land to ledger" services.
4. **Vikas Raj (Shree Investment) on profitability difference between government and private segments:** Management clarified that government margins run at 10%-15% while private/enterprise margins are 20%-30%.
5. **Vipul Sharma (Individual Investor) on FY27 guidance:** Management reiterated revenue targets of around INR210 crores with steady annual margin improvements of at least 50 bps.

### One-line takeaway
Dev IT delivered robust Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 29% YoY and reduced debt, backed by expanding domestic public sector orders and scaling international partnerships.
