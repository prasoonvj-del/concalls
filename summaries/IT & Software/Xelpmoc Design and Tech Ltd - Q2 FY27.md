### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jay Krishnan (Long-term Investor) asked what fundamentally is different in strategy and what measurable achievements investors can expect regarding revenue growth, commercial traction, ARR, and a path to profitability.**
   *Management answered that value is materializing through portfolio exits and green shoots in proprietary products. While product sales growth is not yet rampant due to the sigmoid adoption curve, long POCs are showing expansion potential with repeat orders.*
2. **Shokan (Individual Investor) asked about the reason for declining promoter holding and the GTM approach for RELY and DocuXray.**
   *Management clarified that the promoter drop was due to ESOP exercises and one promoter group member selling stake for personal reasons, while key management holdings remained intact for 6 months. GTM for RELY involves direct enterprise sales to senior living operators, founder-led outreach for assisted living, and influencer/digital strategies.*
3. **Abhishek Agrawal (Gemsquest) asked about revenue expectations over 6, 12, and 24 months for RELY and DocuXray, and the long-term vision for AI opportunities.**
   *Management stated that RELY bed counts and customer numbers will increase significantly in December and March quarters. For DocuXray, pilots take time due to privacy reviews, but engagement quality has improved drastically.*
4. **Vaidy (Individual Investor) asked why revenues have remained stable/flat over 3-4 quarters despite product progress, and whether service revenue will taper off.**
   *Management explained that services currently drive the majority of revenues (~85-88%), while products account for 12-15%. As product marketability increases, a shift from services to products is expected, though existing service clients are not being dropped.*

### One-line takeaway
Xelpmoc reported a sequential revenue decline and continued net losses in Q2 FY '27 as it prioritizes enterprise POCs and commercialization for its proprietary AI platforms RELY and DocuXray.
