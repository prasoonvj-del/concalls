# Persistent Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered 26th sequential quarter of revenue growth with strong performance across key verticals despite macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** USD 487.6 million (Up 7.8% QoQ, 20% YoY; in INR terms, Rs 46,639 million, up 8.4% QoQ, 30.3% YoY; Constant Currency: up 7.8% QoQ, 20.8% YoY)
- **EBIT Margin:** 15% (down 130 bps YoY)
- **EBIT:** Rs 6,977.2 million (Up 19.5% YoY)
- **Operating PAT:** Rs 4,951.5 million, margin at 10.6% (Up 5% YoY)
- **Reported PAT:** Rs 3,515.1 million, margin at 7.5% (down 25.4% YoY; includes 3.1% impact from Nagarro acquisition one-time expenses)
- **Operating EPS:** Rs 31.6 (Up 4.2% YoY)
- **Reported EPS:** Rs 22.4 (down 26% YoY)
- **TCV Bookings:** USD 633.5 million
- **New Bookings TCV:** USD 367.1 million
- **ACV Bookings:** USD 481 million
- **New Bookings ACV:** USD 262.3 million
- **Headcount:** 29,598 (Addition of 958 employees QoQ)
- **Utilization:** 85.8% (down 70 bps QoQ)
- **Attrition (TTM):** 11.9% (vs 12.3% in Q1 FY27)
- **Cash and Investments:** Rs 29,996.7 million (~USD 315 million)
- **Billed DSO:** 50 days (vs 61 days in Q1 FY27); Unbilled DSO: 24 days (vs 25 days in Q1 FY27)
- **OCF to PAT (TTM):** 96.6% (vs 76.2% in Q1 FY27)

### Highlights
- Software, High-Tech and Emerging verticals grew 28.5% YoY; BFSI grew 16.2% YoY; Healthcare and Lifesciences grew 12% YoY.
- North America revenue grew 22.5% YoY; Europe declined 8.5% YoY due to two client-specific events; India grew 19.4% YoY.
- Secured approximately 94.04% of Nagarro share capital (excluding treasury shares) at the expiry of the additional acceptance period; intends to execute a minority squeeze-out.
- Incurred Rs 1,853.4 million (~USD 19-20 million) in Q2 towards Nagarro transaction-related expenses; expects an incremental USD 25-30 million over the next 2-3 quarters.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aspires to achieve 16% to 17% EBIT margins over time (no formal forward-looking guidance given).
- Deal closure for Nagarro is expected by Q4 CY26 or Q1 CY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- High-tech clients remain on the bleeding edge of AI adoption, driving demand for product engineering and software reinvention services.
- Vendor consolidation and data/legacy modernization remain prominent themes in BFSI and other sectors.
- Slowdown in decision-making was apparent in recent weeks due to macro/geopolitical factors (e.g., Middle East dynamics).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBIT margin declined 100 bps QoQ: Annual wage hike impacted margins by 180 bps (net 140 bps impact after lower ESOP costs); lower utilization impacted margins by 20 bps; large deal third-party tools/licenses amortization impacted margins by 90 bps.
- Tailwinds included lower ESOP SG&A expenses and favorable currency movements providing a 150 bps benefit.
- Foreign exchange loss was Rs 455.8 million.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Headcount increased by 958 employees; utilization moderated to 85.8% due to proactive hiring for large deal ramp-ups.
- To fund the Nagarro transaction, the company plans to raise USD 1.25 billion through debt and equity (including USD 450 million via equity, QIP, FCCBs, or similar instruments).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and investments stood at Rs 29,996.7 million (~USD 315 million).
- Billed DSO improved significantly to 50 days from 61 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- One-time transaction and advisory expenses for the Nagarro acquisition are impacting reported PAT and EPS.
- Macro uncertainty and geopolitical tensions (Middle East) caused slower decision-making towards the end of the quarter.
- European revenue declined 8.5% YoY due to client-specific events.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vibhor Singhal (Vasanth / Equities):** Asked about BFSI traction and token cost impact. Management noted vendor consolidation and legacy modernization are key drivers, and token costs are managed via FinOps and model routing (purpose-built vs frontier models).
- **Nitin Padmanabhan (Investec):** Asked about differences in Software Reinvention Services compared to pre-2019. Management highlighted advancements in AI-led re-architecting, outcome-based commercial constructs, and software license/cloud cost reductions.
- **Kumar Rakesh (Julius Baer):** Inquired about software license cost increases and margin recovery. Management stated software licenses relate to acquired product portfolios and internal tools, reiterating an aspirational margin range of 16% to 17%.
- **Sulabh Govila (Millennium Maste Fund):** Asked about soft QoQ TCV and Nagarro quick wins. Management clarified that TCV on a TTM basis remains robust (36.3% YoY growth) and integration synergy plans will be detailed post-deal closure.
- **Ashwin Mehta (Ambit Capital):** Asked about intangibles increase and high-tech segment margins. Management explained intangibles relate to the large product portfolio deal takeovers and noted portfolio-level margin offsets across verticals.

### One-line takeaway
Persistent Systems delivered a robust 20% YoY revenue growth supported by strong deal execution and AI offerings, even as reported profitability was dented by Nagarro transaction costs and wage hikes.
