# Persistent Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · IT & Software*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - The company delivered strong double-digit constant-currency revenue growth (20.8% YoY) and solid order inflows, while absorbing one-time Nagarro transaction expenses.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue:** USD 487.6 million (up 7.8% QoQ, 20% YoY in USD terms; up 7.8% QoQ, 20.8% YoY in constant currency). In INR terms: Rs 4,663.9 crore (up 8.4% QoQ, 30.3% YoY).
* **EBIT:** Rs 697.72 crore; EBIT margin stood at 15% (down 100 bps QoQ, down 130 bps YoY).
* **Operating PAT:** Rs 495.15 crore (10.6% margin, up 5% YoY).
* **Reported PAT:** Rs 351.51 crore (7.5% margin, down 25.4% YoY), impacted by 3.1% (Rs 185.34 crore / ~$19–20 million) due to one-time Nagarro acquisition expenses.
* **Earnings Per Share:** Reported EPS at Rs 22.4 (down 26% YoY); Operating EPS at Rs 31.6 (up 4.2% YoY).
* **Order Book:** Total Contract Value (TCV) stood at USD 633.5 million (New TCV: USD 367.1 million). Annual Contract Value (ACV) stood at USD 481 million (New ACV: USD 262.3 million). TTM TCV grew 36.3% YoY and TTM ACV grew 21.9% YoY.
* **Operational Metrics:** Headcount at 29,598 (net addition of 958 QoQ); utilization at 85.8% (down 70 bps QoQ); trailing 12-month attrition at 11.9% (vs. 12.3% in Q1 FY27).

### Highlights
* **Nagarro Acquisition Progress:** Secured 94.04% of Nagarro share capital in the open offer; intends to perform a squeeze-out of remaining minority shareholders upon close. Deal closure expected in Q4 CY26 or Q1 CY27.
* **Vertical Growth (YoY in USD):** Software, High-Tech & Emerging Industries grew 28.5%; BFSI grew 16.2%; Healthcare & Life Sciences grew 12%.
* **Geographic Performance (YoY in USD):** North America up 22.5%, India up 19.4%, Rest of World up 67%, Europe down 8.5% due to two client-specific ramp-downs (one client acquired by a larger entity, another completed a fixed-price engagement).
* **Client Bucket Growth:** Top 5 clients grew 38.9% YoY; Top 10 grew 29.9% YoY; USD 10M+ clients increased from 25 to 34 YoY.
* **New Services:** Launched Software Reinvention Services leveraging the proprietary SASVA platform to help tech/ISV clients migrate products to AI-native architectures and optimize operating costs.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management does not provide formal forward-looking revenue guidance but expressed confidence in growing across all business verticals.
* Management maintains an aspirational long-term EBIT margin target band of 16% to 17%.
* Deal closure for Nagarro is targeted for Q4 CY26 or Q1 CY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
* High-Tech/ISVs are facing disruption from AI advances, creating high demand for product modernization, cloud optimization, and AI-native software engineering.
* BFSI adoption is being driven by vendor consolidation and legacy platform/mainframe modernization unlocks via AI.
* Decision-making saw some short-term macro-induced slowdown towards the end of the quarter, though pipeline remains healthy.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **EBIT Headwinds (QoQ):** Annual wage hikes (-140 bps net of lower ESOP costs), lower utilization (-20 bps), and higher third-party tools/cloud amortization from a major product takeover deal (-90 bps / ~$4 million).
* **EBIT Tailwinds (QoQ):** Lower ESOP SG&A expenses and favorable FX movements provided +150 bps combined benefit.
* **Transaction Expenses:** Total expected one-time Nagarro acquisition expenses are ~$50 million over 3–4 quarters (Rs 185.34 crore incurred in Q2; incremental $25–30 million expected over upcoming quarters). Board may seek shareholder approval to exclude one-time deal costs from ESOP vesting criteria.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Expanded AI certifications across employees to over 58,000; hold nearly 25,000 hyperscaler partner certifications.
* Capital expenditures/intangibles under development increased due to investments in internal proprietary AI tools (SASVA, iAURA, Gen AI) and software product licenses taken over under outcome-based deals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **Cash & Investments:** Total cash and investments stood at Rs 2,999.67 crore (~USD 315 million) as of September 30, 2026 (up from Rs 2,704.4 crore in Q1 FY27).
* **DSO & Cash Flow:** Billed DSO improved to 50 days (vs. 61 days in Q1 FY27); unbilled DSO at 24 days (vs. 25 days). TTM Operating Cash Flow to PAT improved to 96.6% (vs. 76.2% in Q1).
* **Acquisition Financing:** Intends to replace the Euro 1.4 billion 18-month bridge loan for Nagarro by raising USD 1.25 billion in long-term debt and equity (including USD 450 million via QIP/FCCB/equity-linked instruments). Management expects long-term net cost of debt to be in the 5%–6% range.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* European revenue declined 8.5% YoY due to two client-specific engagements ramping down.
* Short-term margin headwind of 90 bps from third-party license and cloud tool amortization linked to large product takeover deals.
* Higher finance costs and mark-to-market treasury losses led to negative net other income of Rs 13.12 crore (vs. positive Rs 41.49 crore in Q1 FY27).

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama):** *Asked about drivers in BFSI and impact of AI token costs on client adoption.*  
  **Management:** BFSI growth was driven by vendor consolidation and legacy platform modernization. High token costs are leading clients to use purpose-built small language models and token FinOps via SASVA rather than relying solely on expensive frontier models.
* **Vibhor Singhal (Nuvama):** *Clarified if the 90 bps margin impact from license/tool amortization on the large deal will persist.*  
  **Management:** Confirmed the impact will remain in the base for the next few quarters but will gradually abate as revenue scales and spend is optimized using FinOps/AI.
* **Nitin Padmanabhan (Investec):** *Inquired about commercial structures of the Software Reinvention practice.*  
  **Management:** Deals are structured as outcome-based models with guaranteed savings and revenue-share constructs, reducing client cloud spend and third-party software licensing costs.
* **Ashwin Mehta (Ambit):** *Asked about Nagarro acquisition financing cost.*  
  **Management:** The net cost of long-term debt for the acquisition is expected to be in the range of 5% to 6%.
* **Prateek Maheshwari (Jefferies):** *Asked if the $650M TCV large deal signed in Q1 has downside risks linked to customer product usage.*  
  **Management:** Stated the announced $650M deal value represents the baseline floor over 6.5 years with no downside contingency, only potential upside.

### One-line takeaway
Persistent Systems delivered strong 20.8% YoY constant-currency revenue growth while laying the ground for its Nagarro acquisition and scaling AI-led product reinvention services.
