# Hester Biosciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Standalone revenue, PAT, and operational efficiency showed strong growth.

### Key numbers
- Standalone revenue increased by 28% in Q2 and 21% in H1 YoY.
- Standalone PAT increased by 131% in Q2 and 108% in H1 YoY.
- Gross profit margin stood at 78% in both Q2 and H1.
- EBITDA margin stood at 35% in both Q2 and H1.
- Consolidated revenue grew by 38% in Q2 and 13% in H1 YoY.
- Consolidated PAT increased by 44% in Q2 and 71% in H1 YoY.
- Poultry Healthcare revenue increased by 27% in Q2 and 37% in H1 YoY.
- Animal Healthcare revenue grew by 34% in Q2, while H1 remained 14% below the previous year.
- Capacity utilization: Approximately 65% (ballpark figure).
- Consolidated debt: Rs 102 crore (with cash balance of Rs 23 crore; net debt approx Rs 79 crore).
- Intangible assets under development: Rs 10 crore.

### Highlights
- Improved performance driven by product mix, manufacturing efficiency, and operational discipline.
- Profitability improved despite higher depreciation following the capitalization of the BSL-3 laboratory and fill/finish facility.
- H1 consolidated profitability includes an exceptional accounting gain from Q1 due to the amendment of Hester Africa's loan agreement with the Gates Foundation.
- Poultry segment benefited from institutional business, deeper market penetration, customer engagement, and initial response from the H9N2 vaccine.
- Animal Healthcare growth in Q2 was supported by increased tender execution.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to sustain poultry healthcare growth, strengthen biologicals across poultry and animal health, advance product development and regulatory submissions, and improve manufacturing efficiency and capacity utilization.
- Management wants to establish a commercially viable path for Nepal and improve financial sustainability for Africa.
- Management expects poultry to at least maintain its current base and improve further with the addition of ruminant vaccines.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Regulatory requirements in animal health care are becoming increasingly rigorous (at par with human health), extending development and approval timelines.
- Government-led immunization programs in animal healthcare follow implementation schedules, causing variations across quarters.
- In Nepal, anticipated procurement under international PPR eradication programs has not materialized at the scale originally envisaged.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins supported by favorable product mix and disciplined cost management.
- Signed a power purchase agreement for a 3-megawatt group captive solar project to improve energy cost efficiency and increase renewable energy use.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- BSL-3 laboratory and fill/finish facility recently capitalized.
- Potential need for further investments and technology tweaks to meet rigorous regulatory requirements, though no concrete capex plan was finalized.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Hester India providing a $1 million loan to Hester Africa to fortify operations.
- Consolidated debt at Rs 102 crore against cash of Rs 23 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Animal healthcare and poultry work differently with distinct seasonalities and product mixes, leading to quarterly variations.
- International PPR eradication procurement in Nepal has fallen short of original expectations.
- Evolving regulatory requirements in animal healthcare increase compliance complexity and timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankit (Zen Nivesh Advisors) on export ratio:** Management stated that the export ratio from India (around 10% in FY26) has improved in the current financial year due to new export opportunities.
2. **Ankit (Zen Nivesh Advisors) on Africa loan and capex:** Management clarified that the $1 million loan is to fortify Africa operations and administration. On capex, capacity utilization is around 65%, and future technology/compliance upgrades may require investments.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) on intangible assets and BSL-3:** Management explained that the Rs 10 crore intangible assets under development pertain to R&D and product development expenses that cannot be capitalized until commercialization. BSL-3 facility startup costs are part of overall company depreciation/balance sheet.
4. **Saurab (Paul Asset) on H9N2 contribution:** Management declined to give standalone volume/revenue figures for H9N2 individually, emphasizing overall poultry healthcare growth of 37% in H1 driven by multiple products and technical engagement.
5. **Manish Jain (GormalOne LLC) on debt numbers:** Management confirmed consolidated debt of around Rs 102 crore and cash of Rs 23 crore (net debt around Rs 80 crore).

### One-line takeaway
Hester Biosciences delivered strong Q2/H1 FY27 results with consolidated PAT growing 44% in Q2, driven by robust poultry healthcare momentum and operational efficiencies, despite headwinds in international markets like Nepal and Africa.
