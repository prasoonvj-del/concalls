# Mahamaya Lifesciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered healthy growth in revenue and profitability while progressing on manufacturing transitions and infrastructure expansion.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs. 211.63 crore (up ~30% YoY from Rs. 163.06 crore)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): Rs. 15.54 crore (up ~36% YoY from Rs. 11.41 crore)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs. 11.55 crore (up ~38% YoY from Rs. 8.37 crore)
- IPO Net Proceeds: Rs. 61.96 crore; Utilized up to September 30, 2026: Rs. 32.72 crore; Balance: Rs. 29.24 crore
- Short-term borrowings / Loans: Rs. 111 crore as of September 30, 2026 (compared to Rs. 87 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- Brand Sales (H1 FY27): Rs. 6.61 crore (nearly matching the full-year FY26 brand sales of ~Rs. 6.5 crore)
- Export Sales (H1 FY27): Close to Rs. 75 lakh
- Working Capital Cycle: ~150-155 days
- Cost of Debt: 8% to 8.75%
- Technical vs. Formulation/Brand/Export Revenue Split: Technical sales at ~55%; Formulation, brand, and export at ~45%

### Highlights
- Completed and capitalized an office building listed under CWIP valued at ~Rs. 4.5 crore in early September 2026, now used as a secondary office.
- The three-storey warehouse facility is completed and operational.
- Discussions regarding a joint venture with Atul and Sumitomo Chemicals for new products, registration, marketing, and distribution are in the final stage.
- ERP implementation is targeted for March 2027.
- Applied for four patents, with another four in the pipeline (total 7 in the pipeline; none approved yet).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets revenue growth of approximately 30% to 35% for FY27.
- Aiming to maintain or improve the H1 FY27 PAT and PBT margins for the full year.
- Long-term goal is to increase the manufacturing revenue split to around 70% to 75% in the coming few years.
- Targeted reduction in working capital cycle by around 10 days to 140-145 days in the next few quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global agrochemical industry is in transition with excess capacity in China, pricing pressures on conventional molecules, and West Asian geopolitical situations impacting business.
- El Nino impacted major states in India (Maharashtra, southern states) with low rainfall during the season, but management does not see a major impact on Mahamaya due to diverse product mix and high commodity prices getting good realizations for farmers.
- Biological products and bio-stimulants currently contribute around 1% to 2% (industry-wide contribution is not more than 3% to 5%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average gross margin is close to 16%.
- Segment gross margins: Trading sales at 10-12%, B2B formulation at 21-22%, brand sales >25%, and exports >28-29%.
- Almost 80% of imports are technicals from China; active ingredients make up 80-90% of a formulation product's cost.
- Raw material costs constitute about 82% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Formulation plant expansion at Dahej: Solid formulation capacity increasing from 1,040 to 2,000 metric tons per annum (MTPA); liquid formulation capacity increasing from 13 lakh to 50 lakh litres per annum. Target completion: March 2027 (commercial production post-December 2025/2026). Capex spent so far from IPO funds: ~Rs. 3.75 crore.
- Backward integration technical manufacturing facility at Dahej: Planned capacity of 250 MTPA, targeted for commissioning by 2028 (civil structure completion expected by March 31, 2027). Total estimated cost: ~Rs. 30 crore, with ~Rs. 4 to 5 crore expended so far.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balances stood at ~Rs. 63 crore (including Rs. 29.24 crore parked in FDs/monitoring account from IPO proceeds).
- Increase in short-term borrowings from Rs. 87 crore (March 31, 2026) to Rs. 111 crore (September 30, 2026) was due to temporary parking of customer receivables into an FD ahead of an LC payment commitment in early October.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues in West Asia, container shortages from China to India, and technical price swings.
- Dependence on monsoons, pest attacks, and regional rainfall distribution affecting the seasonal agrochemical business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kanishk Jain asked about the increase in borrowings despite having cash available.** CFO explained the Rs. 20 crore difference was due to temporarily parking customer collections in an FD ahead of a major LC payment commitment in early October, resulting in a net loan increase of only Rs. 5-6 crore.
2. **Kanishk Jain asked about the status of talks for a joint venture with Atul or Sumitomo Chemicals.** MD Prashant Krishnamurthy stated discussions regarding new-age fungicide molecules, registrations, marketing, and distribution are in the final stage, and exchanges will be intimated once finalized.
3. **Ashutosh Agarwal asked about the business split and export performance.** CFO clarified technical sales are 55% and formulation/brand/export are 45%; exports were lower at ~Rs. 75 lakh due to geopolitical reasons.
4. **Drashti Bhatt asked about biologicals revenue contribution.** Chairman stated biologicals contribute around 1% to 2%, while bio-fermented products like Emamectin and Spinosad comprise 70-80% of revenue.
5. **Drashti Bhatt asked about technical import dependency and raw material sensitivity.** MD noted 80% of imports are technicals from China, and price swings are partially passed through based on market conditions.

### One-line takeaway
Mahamaya Lifesciences delivered ~30% revenue and ~38% PAT growth in H1 FY27, advancing its transition toward an integrated manufacturing model via ongoing Dahej plant expansions and technical backward integration.
