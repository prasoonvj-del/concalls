# PI Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong domestic and biologicals growth was offset by lower AgChem exports and pricing pressures amid a difficult industry cycle.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Flattish revenue growth expectation maintained; gross margin expectation reset to 47%-49% range for the next 2-3 quarters.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from early stabilization signs in Q1 to a "difficult quarter in a difficult year" marked by faster-than-expected genericization and weak monsoon conditions.
- **New topics or risks:** Plant Healthcare (global biologicals) full-year loss quantified at $10+ million; first NCE (Pioxazon) officially launched in India.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Pioxaniliprole regulatory approval and domestic launch delivered as promised in Q1.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 17,018 million (down 9% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 49%
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14%
- **Ag Chem Exports Decline:** 18% YoY (Volume down 3%-4%; pricing/cost down 13%-14%)
- **Domestic Agri Revenue Growth:** 19% YoY
- **Domestic Biologicals Growth:** 45% YoY (accounts for 18% of Agri domestic portfolio)
- **Global Biologicals Growth:** 250%+ YoY (revenue around Rs. 60 crores)
- **Pharma Business Growth:** 20% YoY (revenue around Rs. 75 crores)
- **Plant Healthcare Expected Full-Year Loss:** $10+ million
- **Net Working Capital Reduction:** 18 days (releasing Rs. 298 crores)
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs. 9.1 billion (vs. Rs. 2.3 billion YoY)
- **Net Cash Position:** Rs. 36 billion
- **Order Book:** USD 1.25 billion
- **Contract Assets:** Around Rs. 700 crore

### Highlights
- First NCE (Pioxaniliprole, branded as Pioxazon) received registration and was launched in India for rice, corn, and Bengal gram.
- Added three new CSM molecules for commercialization.
- Global biologicals distribution network expanded with a 3x increase in width across Brazil, Mexico, Spain, and the US.
- Pharma center of excellence at Hyderabad opened, new QC lab in Lodi commissioned, and kilo lab completing by year-end; electronic and specialty chemicals kilo lab at Udaipur operational by year-end.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a flattish performance for FY27 overall due to external market dynamics.
- Gross margins expected to hold around 47%-49% over the next 2-3 quarters.
- Startup and expansion costs in biologicals, pharma, and electronic chemicals expected to normalize in 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global crop protection cycle at low points; channel stocks normalized, but uneven weather, rising input costs, tight channel credits, and generic pricing pressure impacted demand.
- Domestic season affected by the weakest monsoon in over a decade; Kharif acreage flat and cautious Rabi outlook due to lower soil moisture.
- Commodity prices firming up from lows, pointing to potentially better farm economics next season.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin moderated due to 5% higher input costs and pricing pressure from El Nino and genericization.
- Core overheads tightly controlled, but investments in new businesses increased by 6%, R&D by 2%, and energy costs by 3%. R&D spend maintained at 3%-4% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Agchem manufacturing capacity sufficient for the next 2-3 years; future capex shifting towards new growth engines (pharma, electronics, biologicals).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Healthy net cash position of Rs. 36 billion.
- Net working capital reduced by 18 days, releasing Rs. 298 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subdued crop economics, elevated input costs, faster-than-expected genericization, pricing pressures, and adverse weather/monsoon impacts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhijit Akella asked about the volume vs. pricing split in export decline.** Management stated volume was down 3%-4%, while pricing and cost accounted for 13%-14%.
2. **Ankur Periwal asked about export pricing pressures and recovery timelines.** Management noted pricing is market-led to preserve market share, with green shoots and volume pickup expected by the end of the next financial year.
3. **Sumant Kumar asked about pharma profitability and scale lag.** Management stated pharma is a long-gestation CRDMO building capabilities through customer co-creation, with deals entering the pipeline.
4. **Tarang Agrawal asked about the Plant Healthcare inflection point.** Management stated an inflection to EBITDA-positive status is expected in 2-3 years.
5. **Charul Agrawal asked for the order book.** Atul Gupta stated the order book stands at around USD 1.25 billion.

### One-line takeaway
PI Industries reported a 9% YoY revenue decline in Q2 FY27 due to AgChem export headwinds and pricing pressures, though resilient domestic growth, biological expansion, and the historic commercialization of its first NCE (Pioxazon) reinforced its long-term innovator positioning.
