# Gujarat State Fertilizers & Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Record high top-line growth offset by severe margin compression in the fertilizer segment due to surging raw material costs.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Sales Revenue (Q2): up 49% YoY
- Consolidated PBT (Q2): down 42% YoY
- Consolidated PAT (Q2): down 38% YoY
- Consolidated H1 Sales Revenue: INR 8,324 crores (up 55% YoY)
- Consolidated H1 PBT: down 26% YoY
- Consolidated H1 PAT: down 22% YoY
- Standalone Sales Revenue (Q2): INR 4,711 crores (up 50% YoY, up 32% QoQ)
- Standalone H1 Sales Revenue: INR 8,293 crores (up 56% YoY)
- Standalone Q2 PBT: INR 249 crores (up 22% QoQ)
- Standalone Q2 PAT: INR 200 crores (up 26% QoQ)
- Fertilizer Segment Q2 Sales Value Growth: 55% YoY (increase of INR 1,387 crores)
- Fertilizer Segment Q2 Sales Volume: 5.69 lakh metric tonnes (down 6% YoY from 6.08 lakh metric tonnes)
- Fertilizer Segment Q2 EBIT: INR 17 crores (compared to INR 224 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Industrial Products Segment Q2 Sales Growth: 30% YoY (increase of INR 185 crores)
- Industrial Products Segment Q2 EBIT: INR 66 crores (up from INR 54 crores)
- Caprolactam-benzene spread: USD 849 per metric tonne (up from USD 512 per metric tonne)
- Subsidy closing balance: INR 3,400 crores

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever Q2 and H1 top-line performance on both standalone and consolidated bases.
- Fertilizer segment growth driven by higher DAP realizations and the government's DAP special package, despite volume declines.
- Industrial products segment benefited from better realizations and volume growth across major products (caprolactam, nylon, Anone, TGU), offset slightly by melamine.
- Maintained a strong balance sheet with no long-term debt, healthy net worth, and adequate liquidity.
- Received P&K fertilizer subsidies up to September and urea subsidies up to the third week of October.
- C-Train modification for 1,200 metric tonnes per day APS production at the Sikka unit was commissioned on October 23, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be a very good 6-month period, potentially compensating for Q2 profit deficits if the government approves expected NPK subsidy rates.
- Cost-cutting measures based on BCG recommendations at the Sikka plant are expected to be fruitful in H2.
- Caprolactam-benzene spread is expected to moderate in Q3 FY27 as regional supply normalizes.
- Melamine and technical grade urea demand expected to remain steady; HX crystal exports may improve amid subdued domestic demand.
- Other industrial products projected to maintain steady demand.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry entering Rabi season with comfortable fertilizer inventory levels (highest in past 3 years for urea and NPK), intensifying competitive pressure.
- Subdued southwest monsoon and lower reservoir levels (especially in Southern India) likely to constrain fertilizer demand.
- Price distortion between DAP and NPK continues to influence farmers' preferences, adversely affecting NPK offtake.
- Upcoming NBS subsidy rates critical for mitigating raw material costs and price distortions.
- Possible easing of Middle East/Europe geopolitical tensions could soften international raw material prices.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sharp increases in key raw materials—sulfur up 327% and natural gas up 62%—heavily compressed fertilizer margins.
- DAP fully compensated by government via regulations, yielding a 4% margin; urea carries a 3% to 4% margin.
- Solar power arrangement expected to yield operational efficiency gains of around INR 20 crores to INR 25 crores per quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Finalized a ~INR 3,000 crore phosphoric acid (PA) and sulfuric acid (SA) project at Sikka; EPC contract recently awarded.
- Project completion timeline is 30 to 33 months.
- Captive consumption from the new PA/SA plant will meet 50% of DAP/NPK fertilizer requirements at an estimated comfortable 20% margin compared to open-market purchases.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with no long-term debt, healthy net worth, and adequate liquidity.
- Subsidy receivable closing balance stands at INR 3,400 crores, with urea accounting for only INR 200–300 crores of the total.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Heightened geopolitical uncertainty and evolving global supply chain dynamics.
- High volatility in international phosphoric acid, sulfuric acid, sulfur, and rock prices.
- Subdued monsoon, low reservoir levels in Southern India, and high channel inventory posing demand headwinds.
- Price distortion between DAP and NPK impacting NPK offtake.
- Pending urea subsidy revisions based on actual pool gas rates by FICC and GAIL since April 2026.

### One-line takeaway
GSFC delivered record H1 and Q2 top-line growth driven by realizations, but fertilizer profitability was severely compressed by surging raw material costs, leaving earnings dependent on upcoming government subsidy notifications.
