# Fertilisers & Agrochemicals: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Fertilisers & Agrochemicals sector played out against a challenging macro backdrop. Managements across the board highlighted headwinds from one of the weakest monsoon seasons in a decade (IMD reporting a ~13% rainfall deficit), resulting in delayed sowing, uneven rainfall, drought conditions in parts of Southern and Western India, and low reservoir levels. This was compounded by geopolitical friction in West Asia, which caused volatility in natural gas, sulfur, shipping, and raw material costs. 

However, company performance diverged significantly based on strategic positioning: firms heavily reliant on generic products or rain-fed regions faced acute volume and margin pressures, while those pivoting toward patented/specialty portfolios, export markets (NAFTA/LATAM), or executing backward integration managed to expand margins and deliver resilient profitability.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** All managements agreed that Q2 was severely impacted by erratic, below-normal monsoon rainfall, dry spells, and low reservoir levels, which subdued domestic demand for crop protection (especially herbicides due to mechanical weeding) and fertilizers.
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   *Weakness/Cautious Demand:* **India Pesticides** reported a 22% revenue drop due to erratic monsoons and softer overseas demand. **Best Agrolife** intentionally shrank generic revenues (-30% YoY) to focus on patented products (+20% YoY), leading to a 21% top-line decline. **Sumitomo Chemical** cited H1 as one of the most difficult in a decade, with domestic revenue flat and exports down 23% in Q2. **Rallis India** noted flat revenue and margin declines due to moisture stress.
    *   *Resilience/Growth:* **Sharda Cropchem** grew revenue 13% YoY, driven by strong NAFTA (~30%) and LATAM (>80%) performance that offset European weather headwinds. **Shree Pushkar** posted a 58% revenue surge and 87% EBITDA jump. **Prasol Chemicals** delivered a 38.6% revenue jump. **Dhanuka Agritech** grew value by 10% through a focused insecticide mix in irrigated areas and lean distributor inventory. **DCM Shriram** grew overall revenues 22% YoY supported by chemicals, vinyl, and sugar, despite agri-input headwinds.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Raw Material & Input Pressures:** Surging raw material costs—such as sulfur up 327% and natural gas up 62% for **GSFC**, alongside West Asia conflict impacts on coal and gas—compressed margins across fertilizer and chemical players. Generic agrochemical products faced severe pricing pressure due to tepid demand (**Dhanuka**, **Indogulf**). 
*   **Margin Divergence:** 
    *   **GSFC** saw fertilizer EBIT collapse from INR 224 crore to INR 17 crore due to raw material spikes. **Dharmaj Crop Guard** reported a 320 bps drop in gross margin to 18.6%. 
    *   Conversely, **Best Agrolife** expanded gross margins to 45% (vs 33% YoY) and EBITDA margins to 25% (vs 15%) through a deliberate shift to high-margin patented products. **Sharda Cropchem** expanded gross margins by 140 bps to 35.9%. **Indogulf** improved EBITDA margins to 16% (vs 13%) via product mix and cost discipline.

## Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **GSFC:** Finalized a ~INR 3,000 crore phosphoric/sulfuric acid project at Sikka (30-33 month timeline) for 50% captive consumption.
*   **DCM Shriram:** Commissioned a 100 TPD Aluminium Chloride plant at Bharuch and a 68 MW renewable power project at Kota; set up a 40 MW solar plant and debottlenecking projects.
*   **Best Agrolife:** Capex remains deferred until stability is secured.
*   **India Pesticides:** Capitalized ~Rs 65 crore at the Hamirpur subsidiary; standalone capex guided at Rs 40-45 crore, subsidiary at Rs 80-90 crore.
*   **Indogulf Cropsciences:** Reallocated unutilized IPO proceeds (Rs 8.34 crore) to a climate stress management facility at Sonipat (operational Q4 FY27).
*   **Prasol Chemicals:** Invested Rs 75 crore in H1 out of a planned Phase 1 capex of Rs 250-300 crore.
*   **Sharda Cropchem:** H1 capex stood at Rs 419 crore (driven by US data compensation settlements); full-year guidance at Rs 550-600 crore.
*   **Shree Pushkar:** Commercialized Unit 5 (~6,000 MTPA dye capacity); approved Rs 70 crore for a new Unit 5 chemical plant.
*   **Sumitomo Chemical:** Dahej greenfield project (Rs 150 crore) on track for Q2 FY28-29; Tarapur capex of Rs 20 crore ongoing.

## What changed since last quarter
*(Based on companies explicitly reporting last-quarter comparisons/guidance shifts)*
*   **Best Agrolife:** Shifted stance on the 10-15% revenue growth target, stating it is "hard to say" if it will be achieved, while reiterating profitability confidence. Shifted tone from "cautiously optimistic" to acknowledging a challenging Kharif season.
*   **India Pesticides:** Lowered full-year revenue growth expectations to matching last year (rather than low single-digit growth). Capitalized ~Rs 65 crore at Hamirpur, targeting Rs 40-50 crore revenue in H2.
*   **Indogulf Cropsciences:** Maintained guidance suspension due to uncertainty, but reallocated IPO proceeds from a DF plant to a climate stress facility.

## Company one-liners
*   **Best Agrolife:** Sacrificing generic top-line revenue to drive margin expansion and profitability through a patented-product strategy.
*   **DCM Shriram:** Delivered 22% revenue growth to Rs 3,977 crore, but PBDIT declined due to higher chemical input costs and El Niño agri headwinds.
*   **Dhanuka Agritech:** Delivered resilient Q2 growth via an insecticide focus and lean inventory, overcoming industry monsoon headwinds.
*   **Dharmaj Crop Guard:** H1 revenues and PAT grew despite the weakest monsoon in a decade, prompting a top-line guidance revision to single digits.
*   **GSFC:** Recorded highest-ever top-line growth, but fertilizer profitability was squeezed by surging raw material costs awaiting subsidy revisions.
*   **India Pesticides:** Faced a 22% revenue decline due to monsoons and export softness, but secured key international approvals to support H2.
*   **Indogulf Cropsciences:** Achieved 3% revenue growth and a 27% EBITDA jump through product mix enrichment and cost discipline.
*   **Prasol Chemicals:** Posted record Q2 performance with strong YoY growth and reiterated FY27 guidance, expanding capacity amidst working capital adjustments.
*   **Rallis India:** Faced flat revenue and lower profitability due to erratic monsoon distribution and moisture stress.
*   **Sharda Cropchem:** Delivered 13% revenue growth and 23% PAT growth, led by NAFTA and LATAM strength overcoming European weather softness.
*   **Shree Pushkar:** Achieved robust YoY revenue and profit growth supported by capacity expansion and favorable market conditions.
*   **Sumitomo Chemical:** Navigated a drought-hit H1 and margin pressure with a debt-free balance sheet, strong cash reserves, and a new transformation program.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Rabi Season Progression & Soil Moisture:** Monitoring El Niño persistence, reservoir levels (especially in Southern/Western India), and crop planting/irrigation availability.
*   **Subsidy & Regulatory Updates:** Government NBS subsidy rate announcements for fertilizers and urea subsidy pool gas price settlements (**GSFC**, **Shree Pushkar**).
*   **Working Capital & Inventory Normalization:** Clearing of elevated channel inventories and normalization of working capital cycles (**Sumitomo Chemical**, **Indogulf**, **Best Agrolife**, **Prasol**).
*   **Export Order Execution:** Delivery on international order books and regulatory approvals in NAFTA and LATAM (**India Pesticides**, **Sharda Cropchem**, **Indogulf**).
*   **Corporate Actions:** Progress on the demerger timeline for **DCM Shriram** (targeting Q1 FY28 board review).
