# Rallis India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue was flat while profitability declined due to erratic monsoon distribution, moisture stress in key states, and export logistics challenges.

---

### Key numbers
* **Revenue:** Rs 858 crore vs Rs 861 crore in Q2 FY26 (flat YoY; 5% price growth offset by 5% volume decline). H1 FY27 revenue stood at Rs 1,880 crore (+3% YoY).
* **EBITDA:** Rs 126 crore, down 18% YoY vs Rs 154 crore in Q2 FY26. H1 FY27 EBITDA stood at Rs 311 crore (+2% YoY).
* **PAT:** Rs 85 crore, down 17% YoY vs Rs 102 crore in Q2 FY26. H1 FY27 PAT stood at Rs 210 crore (+6% YoY).
* **Crop Care Segment (Q2):** Revenue Rs 788 crore (+4% YoY); EBITDA Rs 126 crore (-2% YoY); PAT Rs 88 crore (+1% YoY).
  * *Domestic B2C:* Rs 556 crore (-1% YoY; price +5%, volume -6%).
    * *Crop Protection:* Rs 457 crore (-3% YoY; volume -7%).
    * *Soil & Plant Health:* Rs 74 crore (+22% YoY; price +15%, volume +7%).
  * *Institutional B2B:* Rs 232 crore (+17% YoY; volume +8%, price +9%).
    * *Exports:* Rs 165 crore (+2.5% YoY; price +10%).
    * *CSM:* Rs 27 crore (grew ~12x from Rs 2 crore in Q2 FY26, led by PEKK sales).
    * *Domestic B2B:* Rs 39 crore (+11% YoY).
* **Seeds Segment (Q2):** Revenue Rs 70 crore (-31% YoY; volume -26%); EBITDA Rs (0) crore (vs Rs 25 crore in Q2 FY26); PAT Rs (4) crore (vs Rs 16 crore in Q2 FY26).

---

### Highlights
* Launched 4 new crop care products in Q2 FY27 (Silentra, Marcellus, Blitox Super, Trynage), bringing FY27 YTD crop care launches to 9. New products launched in FY27 contributed ~Rs 100 crore in revenue.
* Launched 6 new seed products in Q2 FY27 (total 15 in FY27 YTD across vegetables, cotton, maize, and Dhaanya brand DSR rice).
* Over 60,000 retailers registered on the Anubandh Edge digital platform as of September 2026.
* Sampark+ digital initiative reached 4.23 lakh farmers.

---

### Guidance & outlook
* **Seeds strategy:** Concentrating focus on five strategic crops: Cotton, Maize, Millet, Mustard, and Rice.
* **Liquidations/Provisions:** Management confirmed that sales returns and provisions for the Kharif season have been fully accounted for in H1 FY27, with no major lingering impact expected in H2 FY27.
* **Capacity Utilisation:** Expected to remain broadly stable, with growth driven by product mix and higher-value offerings.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Domestic Kharif:** ~43% of India received ~19% deficient rainfall, creating moisture stress across Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka, Telangana, Punjab, and Haryana. Farmers prioritized fertilizer purchases over agrochemicals.
* **Global Crop Protection:** Market estimated at ~US$70.4 billion in 2026. Global cereal production is projected to decline 2.1% in 2026.
* **Export Market:** Demand for Pendimethalin AI in Europe was soft, alongside Chinese pricing aggression and supply constraints for select active ingredients like Acephate and Hexaconazole.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Input costs and shipping margins faced pressure due to higher crude oil, energy, and freight expenses stemming from Middle East logistical/Black Sea disruptions.
* Freight delays of 2 to 4 weeks were experienced due to shipping around Africa to avoid Red Sea routes and port congestion.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Navi Mumbai R&D Centre:** Project on track; government liaison and consultant onboarding completed. Commissioning expected in April 2027.
* **Capex Spend:** H1 FY27 capex was under Rs 20 crore. FY27 full-year capex expected to be slightly higher than Rs 50 crore.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **Cash Position:** Cash and liquid balances stood at ~Rs 211 crore as of September 30, 2026.
* **Working Capital & Inventory:** Inventory levels remain elevated YoY, driven primarily by fresh seed inventory harvested in March–April 2026 and elevated crop protection raw material prices. Management expects working capital to normalize and free cash flow generation to continue by March 2027.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* **Rabi Irrigation Concerns:** Major reservoir levels stood at ~70% capacity (below the 10-year average of 76.6%), posing water availability risks for Southern India and Rabi acreage.
* **Illegal Cotton Seeds:** Illegal unapproved cotton seeds proliferated, accounting for 25%–30% (~1.5 crore packets out of ~5 crore total market pool) of cotton plantings.
* **Price Pressures:** Oversupply and aggressive Chinese pricing are driving severe price deflation in key post/pre-emergent crop segments (e.g., wheat herbicides in North India).

---

### Notable analyst Q&A

* **Rohit Nagraj (360 ONE Capital):** Have all Kharif sales returns been accounted for, and what is the channel inventory status?  
  *Management Answer:* Kharif returns have been fully accounted for and provisioned for the remainder of the market. Channel inventory has been kept lean by placing products only where water and demand visibility were clear.

* **Prashant Biyani (Elara Securities):** What explains the elevated inventory level at the end of H1 FY27?  
  *Management Answer:* It is a combination of fresh seed inventory from early 2026 and chemical raw materials procured at higher prices during geopolitical uncertainty.

* **Siddharth Gadekar (Equirus Capital):** How severe is the illegal cotton seed issue and did Rallis lose market share?  
  *Management Answer:* Legal market size dropped from 5.25 crore packets to 3.5 crore packets due to illegal seeds (~25%-30% share) and reduced North India acreage. Rallis maintained/gained share in North India despite volume drop.

* **Riju (Antique Stock Broking):** Why didn't low-cost inventory procurement from Feb–March benefit H1 margins?  
  *Management Answer:* Low-cost inventory benefits were absorbed in Q1 FY27. By Q2, average raw material costs normalized while market pricing softened, alongside a adverse product mix shift away from high-margin fungicides.

* **Abhijit Akella (Kotak Securities):** What was the full-year capex guidance and what drove the increase in other expenses?  
  *Management Answer:* Full-year FY27 capex will be slightly above Rs 50 crore. Other expenses grew due to shipping/freight costs and advisory/professional fees associated with evaluating M&A opportunities.

---

### One-line takeaway
Rallis faced a soft Q2 due to erratic monsoon impacts on seeds and fungicides alongside global logistics headwinds, but managed resilience via CSM expansion and new product launches.
