# GSP Crop Science Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew ~20% YoY driven by domestic demand and realizations, but EBITDA and PAT declined due to higher raw material procurement costs and supply-related challenges.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (no explicit numeric guidance change mentioned, but management expects Q3 to remain subdued and Q4 to normalize).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from stable/positive in Q1 to cautious due to El Niño weather patterns, below-normal rainfall, dry spells in Maharashtra/Karnataka, and delayed Rabi sowing.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: El Niño peak impact, reservoir deficits in southern India, and vessel availability/logistics constraints for exports emerged as key focus areas.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Management noted the launch of a new patented insecticide for the Rabi season, aligning with ongoing product pipeline strategies.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): INR558 crore, up 19.5% YoY
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): INR73.6 crore (vs INR93.2 crore YoY)
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 13.2%
- Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27): INR44.6 crore (vs INR58.5 crore YoY)
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): INR944 crore, up ~12% YoY
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): INR116 crore (vs INR135 crore YoY)
- EBITDA margin (H1 FY27): 12.3%
- Profit After Tax (H1 FY27): INR71 crore
- Export contribution: Around 20% of total business
- R&D investment level: Around 2.5% of revenue

### Highlights
- Growth across key products was mainly driven by increased realizations due to higher raw material prices.
- Domestic B2B business saw good growth, alongside growth in B2C.
- Other income during H1 was higher primarily due to gains from the sale of a fixed asset (land parcel) and foreign exchange gains.
- Gross profit margin declined from 40% to 34% (based on analyst question confirmed by management).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 to remain a subdued quarter, with margins normalizing in Q4 as pricing and realizations improve.
- Management aims to increase export contribution up to 25% over a 3-year period.
- Management plans to increase R&D investments starting next year to support backward integration and new products.
- Over a 2 to 3-year timeline, management expects to scale business in Brazil and Latin America after acquiring registrations.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Cumulative IMD rainfall was at ~87% of the long-period average, the lowest since 2015, impacted by El Niño.
- Prolonged dry spells in Maharashtra and Karnataka and lower reservoir levels in southern India raised concerns over crop productivity and Rabi irrigation/sowing.
- Farmers are delaying sowing by 5%–10% or swapping to less water-intensive/shorter-duration crops.
- Crop protection demand picks up once crops are 45–50 days old if farmers have confidence of survival; early-stage deficit causes reluctance.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability was impacted by higher raw material procurement costs (~50% of margin pressure), supply-related challenges/costs (20%-30%), and lower contribution from higher margin products (20%).
- Operating costs rose due to higher power/fuel prices and annual manpower cost escalations.
- Approximately 40% to 45% of total raw material requirements are imported from China.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company continues to focus on backward integration to reduce import dependence and improve margins.
- Subsidiary and administration office in Brazil are being used to build a platform for B2B scale-up over the next 1–2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Logistics challenges, particularly vessel availability and extended transit times, are affecting international shipments to Brazil.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Below-normal rainfall, El Niño peak in November, declining soil moisture, and low reservoir levels affecting Rabi sowing and acreage.
- High raw material procurement costs and power/fuel cost inflation compressing gross and EBITDA margins.
- Export logistics constraints, including vessel availability and transit times to Brazil.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mayuresh Rao on Rabi exposure and weather impact:** Management stated they are primarily a Kharif-dominated company, but new patented insecticide launches are helping in Rabi despite macro challenges.
2. **Mayuresh Rao on export growth sustainability:** Management clarified that recent 20% growth was predominantly in domestic markets, while exports face vessel availability and logistics constraints.
3. **Anirudh Sharma on export contribution:** Management reiterated that exports form ~20% of revenue and are targeted to reach 25% in 3 years, driven by Latin America and Southeast Asia.
4. **Mahesh Kumar on revenue drivers:** Management noted growth was volume- and realization-led (majorly realization) in domestic B2B and B2C, but compressed margins due to raw material costs.
5. **Shravan Modi on margin contraction:** Management attributed ~50% of margin pressure to raw material costs, 20%-30% to supply chain issues, and the rest to product mix and power/fuel/manpower cost escalations, expecting normalization by Q4.

### One-line takeaway
GSP Crop Science delivered 19.5% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, but profitability contracted due to higher raw material and power costs amid industry-wide El Niño weather headwinds.
