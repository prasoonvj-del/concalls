# Insecticides (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue and EBITDA declined due to an unusual agricultural season and delayed monsoon, but gross margins improved on premium product mix.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 612 crore (down 12% YoY from Rs 691 crore)
- Gross Profit: Rs 193.16 crore (down 4% YoY from Rs 202 crore)
- Gross Margin: 31.6% (up from 29% YoY)
- EBITDA: Degrew by 20% YoY; EBITDA margin at 11.1% (down from 12.2% YoY)
- PAT Margin: 7.2% (down from 8.4% YoY)
- Volume degrowth: ~13% YoY; value growth: ~2% YoY
- Premium products (Maharatna and Focus Maharatna) contribution to B2C: 64% (up from 58% YoY)
- Segment-wise sales: B2C 64%, B2B 34%, Exports 2%
- Segment breakdown in B2C: Herbicide 59%, Insecticide 33%, Fungicide 5%, PGR 3%
- New product sales (Granuvia and Spinoace): Rs 5.50 crore in Q1
- Products launched last year sales: Rs 2.29 crore in Q1 (vs Rs 1.67 crore YoY)
- In-licensing products revenue: ~Rs 46 crore (vs ~Rs 36 crore YoY)
- Patented products revenue: Rs 70 crore (down from Rs 97 crore YoY, due to lower Hachiman sales)
- Combination products contribution: Rs 98 crore (down from Rs 134 crore YoY)

### Highlights
- Sotanala project total investment expected around Rs 200 crore (Rs 50 crore for formulations, Rs 150 crore for technicals); Rs 70 crore invested till date.
- Formulation facility at Sotanala expected to commence around April-May next year, followed by technical production by Diwali.
- IIL Crop Solutions program to double plots to be present across 14 states and 4 crops (rice, cotton, chili, soybean).
- Kaeros Research commercialized >40 products, growing at a CAGR of ~100% with single-digit margins currently.
- Granuvia expected to touch ~Rs 20 crore and Spinoace Rs 10 crore+ in FY27 (combined net sales ~Rs 25 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects agriculture activity to gain momentum in coming months with improving rainfall and acreage; positive impact visible from Q2.
- Annual capex expected to normalize around Rs 30 crore to Rs 40 crore of maintenance capex after current investment cycle ends.
- Target to reach 70% premium product contribution in next 3 to 4 years.
- Long-term goal to double the company size in 4 to 5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Delayed and uneven monsoon coupled with high temperatures impacted sowing activity and delayed crop protection demand in Q1.
- Channel activity started early in March, but high temperatures and delayed rains led to a slow start.
- Rice sowing slightly delayed in South India due to low dam levels; reduction in cotton and maize crops, but oilseeds, pulses, groundnut, and chilli showing increased traction.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Crude and petroleum-linked raw material costs, metals, plastics, solvents, emulsifiers, and technicals faced pricing pressure and supply chain disruptions.
- Solvents saw fluctuations of 25%-30% week-to-week/day-to-day.
- Gross margin expanded due to focus on specialty/premium products and inventory mix.
- Management targets cost control via solar energy, wind energy, and solid fuel to manage power and steam expenses from Q3 onwards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dahej facility operational with 50%-60% equipment utilized for the new plant; potential to add ~Rs 20 crore in technical production. Power connection and boiler upcoming.
- Udhampur plant receiving investments to segregate herbicide and insecticide plants, completing this year.
- Sotanala phase 1 scheduled by Diwali next year; phase 2 building under erection.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- June inventory was slightly elevated due to slower season start; management focused on improving inventory turns, collections, and reducing working capital cycle.
- Aiming to reduce sales return numbers to at least half of the previous year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unfavourable weather (delayed/uneven monsoon, El Nino overhang) impacting early season demand.
- High volatility in raw material and solvent prices, along with higher freight costs and container scarcity.
- Elevated salary expenses due to new hires (e.g., ~100 people hired in Kaeros).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prashant Biyani asked about Sotanala capex breakdown:** Management clarified total investment is ~Rs 200 crore (Rs 50 crore formulations, Rs 150 crore technicals), with ~Rs 70 crore invested so far.
- **Ramesh asked about product pricing/brand segmentation:** Management explained that progressive/large farmers buy expensive products, but small farmers also adopt them if ROI is demonstrated; Focus Maharatna carries 35%+ margins, Maharatna 20%-30%, and generics 10%-15%.
- **Madhur Rathi asked about inventory gains and price hikes:** Management stated that while some older inventory helped gross margins, extraordinary price hikes could not be taken due to weak market sentiment, and price hikes had to be rolled back in some cases.
- **Kunal asked about volume degrowth and Kaeros margins:** Management stated volume degrowth was ~13% with value growth of ~2%, and Kaeros margins will remain in single digits initially due to high startup expenses.
- **Parul Sharma asked about Dahej utilization and revenue potential:** Management stated Dahej technical plant has a potential to add ~Rs 200 crore in technical production once fully operational and supported by upcoming steam/boiler infrastructure.

### One-line takeaway
Insecticides (India) Ltd reported a 12% revenue decline and 20% EBITDA drop in Q1 FY27 due to delayed monsoons and high raw material volatility, but expanded gross margins by 240 bps to 31.6% driven by an increased share of premium products (64%).
