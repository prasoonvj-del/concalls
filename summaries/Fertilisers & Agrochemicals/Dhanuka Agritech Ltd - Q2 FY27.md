# Dhanuka Agritech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company delivered resilient growth and improved profitability despite industry-wide headwinds from erratic monsoons and high raw material costs.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 657.51 crore (up 9.91% YoY from Rs 598.24 crore).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 152.97 crore (vs Rs 136.73 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 109.22 crore (vs Rs 93.97 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Volume Growth:** ~6%.
*   **Value Growth:** 10%.
*   **Royalty Income (Bayer):** Rs 6.5 crore (vs Rs 4.3 crore in Q2 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance:** Insecticides were the primary growth driver (47% of revenue), followed by Fungicides (25%), Others (20%), and Herbicides (8%).
*   **Geographic Mix:** North (31%), South (29%), West (28%), East (12%).
*   **Inventory Strategy:** Management maintained lean distributor inventory levels and avoided channel stuffing, ensuring healthy cash generation.
*   **Product Launches:** LaNevo (insecticide) performed well; biostimulants (Dhanzyme Granule, Dhanzyme Gold Granule) saw revenue of Rs 11.7 crore (vs Rs 8.4 crore in Q2 FY26).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management maintains guidance for single-digit revenue growth for the full year.
*   **Margins:** Management anticipates ~100 bps loss in gross margin and a similar or slightly higher decrease in EBITDA margin for the remainder of the year.
*   **Rabi Season:** Outlook is subdued; management is focusing on South and East zones for potential demand.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Headwinds:** The sector faced a weak quarter due to a 13% deficit in monsoon rainfall and high raw material costs.
*   **Regional Variance:** North India (irrigated) performed better than rain-dependent regions (West/Central).
*   **Farmer Sentiment:** High commodity prices (sugar, wheat, cotton, chilli) support farmer income where irrigation is available.
*   **Regulatory Impact:** Government vigilance on "tagging" (pushing crop protection products with fertilizers) has increased significantly, reducing this practice.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** Maintained due to product mix, despite GST refund impacts.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Improvement driven by tight control on "other expenses."
*   **Pricing:** Generic products face pricing pressure due to tepid demand; management is passing on costs selectively.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Dahej Plant:** Currently at ~70% utilization.
*   **Expansion:** Management plans to add 2 new fungicide products to the existing plant, targeting 4 products total by FY28.
*   **International:** Subsidiaries in Brazil and Europe are for registration purposes only, not commercial activity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   The company reported strong cash generation and disciplined working capital management.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Erratic and uneven monsoon rainfall (13% lower).
*   High raw material procurement costs.
*   Pricing pressure in generic crop protection products.
*   El Niño impact on crop cycles and demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why did growth surprise vs industry?* A: Management attributed it to a focus on insecticides (LaNevo, Miyako, Mortar), re-channeling demand to irrigated areas, and not front-loading inventory in Q1.
*   *Q: Why is Dahej EBITDA loss-making?* A: Management stated this is a short-term view; they are committed to capex and scaling up with new products.
*   *Q: Is the Fertilizer E-Token system helping?* A: Management noted it helps, but sharper government vigilance on "tagging" is the primary driver of change.
*   *Q: Why is generic pricing under pressure?* A: Management cited tepid demand; they can only pass on costs selectively.

### One-line takeaway
Dhanuka delivered resilient Q2 growth driven by a strong insecticide portfolio and disciplined inventory management, despite industry-wide monsoon and demand headwinds.
