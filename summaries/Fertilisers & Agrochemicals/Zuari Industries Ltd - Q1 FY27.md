# Zuari Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume growth in sugar and profit repatriation milestones in real estate, offset by margin pressure from higher cane costs (SAP) and lower molasses-route ethanol recovery.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Total Income:** Rs 283.9 crore (up 26% YoY).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 31.3 crore (vs Rs 36.9 crore YoY).
- **Standalone PBT (before exceptional items):** Loss of Rs 4.4 crore (vs profit of Rs 0.9 crore YoY).
- **Standalone PAT:** Loss of Rs 9.5 crore (vs loss of Rs 3.9 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 327.5 crore (up 22% YoY from Rs 267.6 crore).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 29.8 crore.
- **Consolidated PBT (before exceptional items):** Loss of Rs 5.4 crore (vs loss of Rs 0.4 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Marginal profit of Rs 0.05 crore (vs loss of Rs 0.48 crore YoY).
- **Sugar Sales Volume:** 4.7 lakh quintals (up 29% YoY).
- **Average Sugar Realizations:** Rs 4,116 per quintal (vs Rs 4,036 per quintal YoY).
- **Sugar Recovery:** 11.7%.
- **Ethanol Production:** 10,138 KL (up 1% YoY).
- **Ethanol Sales:** 10,248 KL (up 5% YoY).
- **Ethanol Average Realization:** Rs 60.70 per litre.
- **Power Sales:** 62.8 lakh units (vs 7.7 lakh units YoY).
- **Strategic Investments Value:** Rs 4,223 crore as of 30th June (up 15% QoQ).
- **External Debt (Excluding Working Capital):** Rs 1,888 crore (vs Rs 1,909 crore in Q4).
- **Average Cost of Borrowings:** 9.73% (down 56 bps YoY from 10.29%).
- **Finance Cost (Consolidated):** Rs 61.5 crore (down by Rs 1.46 crore YoY).
- **Dubai Project Repatriation:** AED 55 million (approx Rs 142.58 crore) received in the current quarter; targeted at Rs 900 crore for the full FY27.
- **Zuari Infraworld Total Income:** Rs 33.9 crore (up 25% YoY).
- **Zuari Infraworld EBITDA:** Rs 24.6 crore (vs Rs 21.7 crore YoY).
- **Simon India Total Income:** Rs 11.7 crore (vs Rs 15.9 crore YoY); execution worth Rs 30 crore during the quarter; order book under execution at ~Rs 70 crore.
- **Zuari Finserv Total Income:** Rs 4.1 crore (vs Rs 5.9 crore YoY).
- **Zuari Insurance Broker Gross Return Premium:** Rs 20.8 crore (vs Rs 33.3 crore YoY); Brokerage income Rs 3 crore; EBITDA Rs 2.2 crore.

### Highlights
- Achieved 100% completion of St. Regis Residences, Dubai; handovers and profit repatriation commenced.
- Sugar segment revenue jumped due to higher domestic quota allocation, selling an additional 1 lakh quintals bringing in ~Rs 45 crore.
- Ethanol JV (Zuari Envien Bioenergy) stabilized plant operations and secured order book until October 2026.
- Real estate development management (DM) model progressing with sales office/experience center complete for the Gangothri Tribhuja project in Hyderabad (2.8 mn sq ft saleable area) and RERA approval in place for a 14.8-acre plotted development in Bangalore; total GDV stands at ~Rs 4,900 crore against an aspiration of Rs 10,000 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to complete the repatriation of ~Rs 900 crore from the Dubai project in the current financial year FY27.
- Expects the loan of ~Rs 258 crore given to associate company Zuari Agro Chemicals Limited to be repaid in the current quarter.
- Anticipates the next round of OMC ethanol tenders to open in October 2026.
- Goa land monetization plans are currently put on hold due to a new Goa Assembly law and enhanced circle rates.
- Furniture division revival plans to be decided by the end of this financial year if talks fail.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic sugar prices have risen ~10% over the past month to record highs (Uttar Pradesh prices at Rs 4,860 to Rs 4,920 per quintal) driven by lower opening inventory (closing stocks expected to decline to ~4.3 mn metric tonnes) and tighter supply positions. Festival demand expected to support realizations.
- Global sugar prices remain supportive at ~16.6 cents per pound due to lower production in Brazil and higher cane diversion to ethanol.
- West Asia crisis has resulted in deferred capex across sectors, impacting the engineering and construction industry (Simon India).
- Ethanol demand from OMCs has not seen a real increase despite higher blend announcements, with significant overcapacity observed in recent tenders (1,800 crore litres offered against 1,050 crore litres demand).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sugar margins remained under pressure due to an 8% increase in the State Advised Price (SAP) of sugarcane in UP (from Rs 370 to Rs 400 per quintal for the last season) and lower recovery from molasses-route ethanol.
- Other expenses remain in the range of Rs 14-15 crore per quarter, driven by annual repair and maintenance for the sugar and power divisions during Q1.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sugar mill operating at 100% capacity utilization; open to inorganic acquisitions if opportunities arise.
- Distillery expansion plans (targeted at 1,000 KLPD) remain on hold due to existing industry overcapacity unless policy mandates a significant jump in blending demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- External debt (excluding working capital) reduced slightly to Rs 1,888 crore.
- Repatriation from Dubai and loan repayment from Zuari Agro Chemicals are the primary avenues for debt reduction.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential UP ethanol import-export pass fee exposure of ~Rs 507 lakhs (unprovided for, backed by legal view that retrospective taxation is not permissible).
- Geopolitical issues in West Asia causing deferred capex affecting Simon India.
- Industry-wide ethanol overcapacity constraining fresh capex.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Saumil Shah (Paras Investments) on Dubai repatriation and debt reduction:** Management confirmed receipt of AED 55 million (Rs 142.58 crore) and maintained guidance of Rs 900 crore repatriation for FY27, which will help reduce overall borrowings and save interest costs.
2. **Gunjan Modi on Zuari Agro Chemicals ICD repayment:** Management reiterated expectations of receiving the full Rs 258 crore outstanding debt from Zuari Agro Chemicals in the current quarter, dismissing concerns over filing extensions.
3. **Suhani Singh (ROS Capital) on sugar margin compression:** Management clarified that despite a 34% revenue jump from higher quota allocations, segment profitability declined due to the 8% hike in UP sugarcane SAP (from Rs 370 to Rs 400).
4. **Satish Sharma on holding company investment consolidation:** Management defended the strategy of buying shares from group entities (Zuarl International/Management Services) into Zuari Industries at market price, stating it consolidates investments and helps deleverage unlisted entities.
5. **Somya Raghuvanshi on UP ethanol pass fee exposure:** Jatin Jain explained that no provision has been made for the Rs 507 lakh exposure because taxes/fees cannot be applied retrospectively under law.
6. **Jayendra Kumar on Goa land monetization and mining:** Management stated that Goa land monetization is on hold due to unfavorable local laws and high circle rates, and explicitly clarified that Zuari Industries has no intention of entering the mining business.

### One-line takeaway
Zuari Industries delivered revenue growth supported by higher sugar quotas and Dubai real estate project handovers, but profitability faced headwinds from higher sugarcane SAP costs and static ethanol realisations, even as management focuses on debt reduction via profit repatriation.
