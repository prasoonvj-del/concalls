# GSP Crop Science Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stable financial performance with strong domestic momentum, improved gross margins, and lower interest costs.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR386 crore (vs INR377 crore in Q1 FYlast financial year)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR26.4 crore, up almost 16% YoY
- EBITDA Margin: Around 11%
- Export contribution: Around 10% (expected around 20% annually)
- Domestic B2B contribution: Roughly 45% (expected around 30%-35% in 3 years)
- Domestic B2C contribution: Around 45% (expected to go to 45%-50% in 3 years)
- Formulation vs. Technical split: Almost 75% formulations, 25% technicals
- Technical capacity utilization: 70% to 75%
- Formulation capacity utilization: 25% to 30%
- Credit Rating: ICRA upgraded rating to A+ stable for long-term and A1 for short-term facilities

### Highlights
- Revenue remained broadly stable with strong momentum in the domestic business, partly offset by temporary raw material constraints in exports.
- Gross margins improved due to a shift in product mix toward specialty and differentiated products (e.g., PCT 410, Fighter).
- Interest cost decreased due to the repayment of loans using IPO funds.
- Other income included INR5.7 crore (part of total INR8.3 crore reported) from the sale of lease land.
- Depreciation increased due to the capitalization of the backward integration plant at Dahej, Saykha.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to double the share of patented products in the B2B/B2C business over 3 years.
- Management expects to outgrow industry growth rates and increase domestic market share from 3%-3.5% to 7%-8%.
- EBITDA margins are expected to grow around 12% to 13% or 13% to 14% over the upcoming 2 to 3 years.
- Overall revenue growth expectation is around 15%.
- Technical pipeline: 1 to 2 new technical products every year; Formulation pipeline: 2 to 3 new patented formulations every year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Export demand in Brazil was delayed due to pricing volatility and logistics preferences for China, but demand is expected to pick up in September for H2.
- Kharif season progress: Monsoon picked up in July with good acreage in cotton, soybean, and chillies, while paddy is stable.
- Farmers are increasingly shifting toward preventive spraying and specialty chemicals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices saw volatility due to geopolitical situations, petrochemical linkages, and an 10%-11% depreciating rupee.
- Employee costs increased due to annual increments, wage impact, and a slight headcount increase.
- Power and fuel costs increased slightly due to higher coal rates.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Technical plant capacity utilization is at 70%-75% with a scope to swap multi-product plants for high-value, low-volume products.
- Formulation capacity utilization is at 25%-30% (seasonal design, not a bottleneck).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO proceeds were utilized for loan repayments; remaining amount is pending bank brokerage settlement (approx. 2 to 3 months).
- Liquidity and credit remain a challenge in Brazil, prompting a focus on established B2B relationships.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global geopolitical developments, climate-related risks, and currency depreciation impact raw material pricing and export logistics.
- Liquidity and credit issues noted in the Brazilian market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kareena Jain on business mix and growth trajectory:** Management stated current mix is 45% B2C, 45% B2B, and 10% exports (75% formulations, 25% technicals). Patented products are 20%-22% of B2C, aiming to double in 3 years.
2. **Aniket Redkar on export/Brazil demand:** Management explained that buying was delayed in Brazil, leading some orders to shift to China; demand is expected to revive ahead of the October season.
3. **Krisha Jain on capacity utilization:** Management noted technical utilization is at 70%-75% and formulation utilization is at 25%-30%, with headroom to swap products for higher-value technicals.
4. **Anirudh Sharma on market share:** Management stated current domestic market share is 3%-3.5% and aims to scale to 7%-8%.
5. **Ayushi on other income and Q2 outlook:** Management clarified that INR5.7 crore of the INR8.3 crore other income was a one-off land sale gain, and confirmed that material supply constraints are no longer present for Q2.

### One-line takeaway
GSP Crop Science reported a stable Q1 FY27 with a 16% YoY rise in PAT to INR26.4 crore, driven by a higher-margin patented product mix and reduced interest costs from IPO fund deployment.
