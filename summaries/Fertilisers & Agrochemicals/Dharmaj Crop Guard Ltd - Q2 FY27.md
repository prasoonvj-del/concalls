# Dharmaj Crop Guard Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Resilient formulation and export performance offset by a weak monsoon-impacted Kharif season and margin headwinds in the technical segment.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 716.7 crore (vs Rs. 714.6 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 81.6 crore (vs Rs. 82.5 crore in H1 FY26); Margin at 11.4% (vs 11.5%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 51.3 crore, up 3% YoY; Margin at 7.1% (vs 7%)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 332.6 crore, down 4% YoY
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Margin:** 18.6% (vs 21.8% in Q2 FY26), a compression of ~320 bps
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 24.3 crore, down 20% YoY; Margin at 7.3% (vs 9.2%)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 13.3 crore, down 24% YoY; Margin at 4%
- **H1 FY27 Export Revenue:** Rs. 60 crore, up 50% YoY (accounts for 8% of total revenue)
- **H1 FY27 Segment Mix:** Institutional formulation 55%, Branded formulation 19%, Export 8%, Technical 18%
- **Balance Sheet (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Inventory at Rs. 174 crore; Trade receivables at Rs. 409.9 crore; Working capital borrowing at Rs. 74 crore; Long-term borrowing at Rs. 45 crore; Gross debt at Rs. 119 crore; Gross debt-to-equity at 0.24x; Net worth at Rs. 500.5 crore; Capital work-in-progress at Rs. 11.7 crore

### Highlights
- 2026 monsoon season was one of the weakest in over a decade (IMD seasonal rainfall at 87% of LPA), creating a soft Kharif season for the agrochemical industry.
- Institutional formulation grew 2% YoY in Q2 and 3% in H1; blended formulation grew 5% in Q2 and 2% in H1.
- Export revenue was flat YoY in Q2, but H1 exports surged 50% YoY.
- Technical business revenue declined 27% YoY in Q2 and 20% in H1 due to West Asia crisis impacting input pricing and demand patterns.
- Operating expenses for Q2 FY27 were lower by 14% YoY at Rs. 37.6 crore.
- New formulation facility near Kerala GIDC Ahmedabad (dedicated to herbicide manufacturing) remains on track for commissioning.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised FY27 top-line growth expectation from 18-20% to single-digit revenue growth due to the weak monsoon and subdued technical market.
- Management reconfirmed the long-term Dharmaj 2030 vision of achieving a Rs. 2,000 crore top-line remains on track.
- Focus for H2: push higher exports, sustain domestic formulation, and restore technical margins through cost pass-through.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Weak monsoon and extended dry spells (especially in Western India) impacted farmer-level consumption in the Kharif season.
- Non-Western regions (Eastern and Northern parts) were less impacted; management focused efforts there.
- Company maintains a strict policy of "no sales return" and reported no big issues regarding inventory returns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 gross margin compressed by ~320 bps YoY to 18.6% due to higher input costs from purchases made post-the West Asia crisis flowing through COGS, alongside tepid technical margins.
- Finance costs were lower by 27% in Q2 at Rs. 3.3 crore and down 22% in H1 at Rs. 5.9 crore.
- Company is actively working to pass higher costs on to end customers in Q3.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital work-in-progress stood at Rs. 11.7 crore as of September 30, 2026, reflecting spending on the new herbicide facility.
- Plant utilization for active ingredients is being protected by aligning production with captive formulation requirements.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory reduced to Rs. 174 crore (from Rs. 207.4 crore as of March 2026) as Kharif raw material inventory was consumed.
- Trade receivables reduced to Rs. 409.9 crore (compared to Rs. 450.8 crore YoY).
- Gross debt stood at Rs. 119 crore, with gross debt-to-equity improving to 0.24x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Weakest monsoon in over a decade affecting domestic Rabi/Kharif demand and low reservoir levels.
- West Asia crisis continuing to affect input pricing and technical market demand patterns.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yogansh Jaswani (Mythical Analytics) asked about positioning for FY28 and growth drivers for formulations.** Management reiterated confidence in reaching the Rs. 2,000 crore 2030 target, citing product additions, technical modifications, and export registrations. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Yogansh Jaswani asked if there is pressure on channel inventory or sales returns given industry stress.** Management clarified that the company has a strict "no sales return" policy tied to need-based sales, and expects no major returns in Q3 or Q4. *(Management did not dodge).*
- **Yogansh Jaswani asked about technical molecule targets and geographic expansion for the Rs. 2,000 crore vision.** Management stated they added 3 new products via R&D and pilot plants, focusing on backward integration to maximize captive consumption in B2C and B2B formulation while executing small technical sales. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
Dharmaj Crop Guard delivered flat H1 revenues and 3% PAT growth despite the weakest monsoon in a decade, though margin pressures in its technical segment forced a downward revision of FY27 top-line growth guidance to single digits while maintaining its long-term Rs. 2,000 crore 2030 vision.
