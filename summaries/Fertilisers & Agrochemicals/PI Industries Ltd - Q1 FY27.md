# PI Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited positive quarterly growth, strong biologicals performance, and strategic progress despite industry headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs. 17,023 million (Q1 FY27)
- **Gross Margin:** 57% (Q1 FY27)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 3,693 million (Q1 FY27)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22% (Q1 FY27)
- **Biologicals Growth (Domestic):** 50% YoY
- **Domestic Volume Growth:** 12% YoY
- **Domestic Revenue Growth:** 3% YoY
- **Order Book:** $1.2 billion
- **Contract Assets:** Around Rs. 750 crore
- **Net Working Capital Reduction:** 19 days reduction (releasing INR 300 crore of cash)
- **Net Cash:** INR 38 billion
- **Capex Guidance (FY27):** Rs. 700 to Rs. 800 crore (Q1 capex was Rs. 250 crore)
- **Effective Tax Rate (ETR) Guidance (FY27):** Around 24%
- **New Product Contribution (CSM):** 16% to 18%

### Highlights
- Commissioned one of the world's largest flow plants for advanced flow chemistry capabilities.
- Pharma center of excellence for drug discovery established at the Hyderabad CRO facility; a QC lab at the Italy site received regulatory approval.
- Global biologicals platform supported by 500+ field trials and 1,000+ grower engagements across Brazil, Mexico, Europe, and the US.
- First NCE discovered in India, Pioxaniliprole, is awaiting regulatory approvals for domestic launch.
- Net working capital days reduced by 19 days, releasing INR 300 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 to be better than FY26, driven by recovery in exports in the second half, new product launches, and gradual scale-up of pharma and global biologicals businesses.
- Revenue growth expected in lower single digits.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global crop protection market showing early signs of demand and stabilization; geopolitical/energy disruptions moderated.
- Operating environment remains challenging due to soft commodity prices, muted recovery in crop economics, genericization pressures, and tariffs.
- Domestic market impacted initially by El Nino, heatwaves, delayed sowing, and high inventory, but sowing and monsoon have recovered to be at par with last year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin pressure exists due to high input costs, higher inventory levels, and industry contraction.
- R&D investments contribute 3% to 4% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex guidance maintained at Rs. 700 to Rs. 800 crore for FY27, allocated towards existing manufacturing assets, new verticals, and innovation-led approaches.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-free balance sheet with net cash of INR 38 billion.
- Net working capital reduced by 19 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in raw material prices, erratic weather, and climatic situations impacting crops and consumption.
- Soft commodity prices and pricing pressure from genericization and tariffs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankur Periwal asked about the 90-molecule R&D pipeline and pricing pressures.** Management stated the pipeline spans Agchem, Electronics, and Biologicals, and noted that pricing pressures stem from challenged demand cycles and high input costs, though consumption patterns are expected to improve.
- **Tejas Pradhan asked about FY27 revenue and margin trajectory.** Management maintained a lower single-digit revenue growth expectation and stated the objective is to sustain gross margins through agility and optimization.
- **Rohit Nagaraj asked about Pioxaniliprole timelines and new product contribution.** Management expects an early start for the Indian launch within the year, with other geographies following in subsequent years; new product contribution in CSM was 16% to 18%.
- **Surya Narayan Patra asked about capex drivers and export volume/value disconnect.** Management stated capex goes into three verticals (manufacturing, new verticals, and innovation) and explained that currency benefits are managed via a pass-through model and risk management to optimize margins.
- **Sanjay Kumar asked about pharma expenses and Pioxaniliprole efficacy.** Management clarified that pharma is a long-gestation CRDMO J-curve requiring upfront regulatory investments, and stated Pioxaniliprole is in the diamide class with differentiated efficacy.

### One-line takeaway
PI Industries delivered resilient Q1 performance with 12% domestic volume growth and a 50% jump in biologicals, supported by a net-cash balance sheet, while advancing its long-term strategic plays in Pharma, Electronics, and Global Biologicals.
