# India Pesticides Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Challenging quarter marked by geopolitical uncertainty, softer overseas demand, volatility in raw material prices, and erratic domestic rainfall affecting agricultural activity.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Lowered full-year revenue growth expectation; management now aims to match last year's revenue rather than achieving low single-digit growth.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from expecting demand normalization in Q3/Q4 to remaining mindful of ongoing demand uncertainties, though supported by newly received export orders.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Re-visiting the product portfolio at the Hamirpur (Shalvis) facility due to geopolitical situations and supply chain challenges was newly highlighted.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Hamirpur subsidiary capitalized blocks worth ~Rs 65 crores in September, targeting Rs 40-50 crores revenue in H2. EU TEQ approval sales commercialization started as expected around November.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 231 crore (down 22.0% YoY from Rs 295 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 487 crore (down 15.9% YoY from Rs 579 crore in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 33 crore (down from Rs 54 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14.4% (compared to 18.2% in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 73 crore (margin 14.9%) vs Rs 108 crore (margin 18.6%) in H1 FY26.
- **PAT:** Rs 18 crore (down 43.8% YoY from Rs 32 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT Margin:** 8.0%.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 41 crore (margin 8.5%) vs Rs 67 crore in H1 FY26.
- **Segment Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Technicals and API: Rs 115 crore; Formulations: Rs 111 crore.
- **Export Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 177 crore (vs Rs 229 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Net Cash:** Rs 36 crore as of 30th September 2026 (vs Rs 29 crore at March end).
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Rs 48 crore.

### Highlights
- Received technical equivalence approval for an insecticide in the United Kingdom and registration for a herbicide in Argentina (first-time entry into Argentina, anticipating Rs 20-25 crore revenue in year one).
- Hamirpur facility: 2 of 10 blocks operational; 2 additional blocks expected early next year. Capitalized ~Rs 65 crore in September in the subsidiary.
- Backward integration expanded: Started producing the intermediate for Pretilachlor in-house, stopping imports of that intermediate.
- Gross margins remained robust at ~50% due to product mix despite top-line headwinds.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to recover H1 lag and match last year's total revenue.
- Confident of maintaining Q2 EBITDA margins in the next 2-3 quarters.
- Standalone capex guidance maintained at Rs 40-45 crore; subsidiary capex expected around Rs 80-90 crore for the year.
- Subsidiary targeted for an asset turnover of 2x next year, translating to ~Rs 80 crore additional revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Monsoon stress across Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Telangana, and parts of Punjab and Haryana disrupted paddy and Kharif crops, reducing herbicide application.
- Overseas demand for herbicides remained softer in H1, but export orders received for the coming quarter (from Europe and Australia worth ~Rs 100 crore total execution basket) provide near-term support.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher employee expenses and lower forex income impacted EBITDA margins.
- Other expenses included job work charges of ~Rs 12 crore in Q2 (vs ~Rs 6 crore in Q2 FY26) due to conversion of raw materials through job work instead of direct intermediate purchases.
- More than 65% of raw materials sourced from India, ~20% from China, and the rest from other countries.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Installed capacity: Dewa Road and Sandila combined at 28,300 metric tonnes of technicals and 10,200 metric tonnes of formulations.
- Hamirpur capacity: Permitted capacity of 30,000 metric tonnes of technicals and 6,000 metric tonnes of formulations.
- Re-evaluating the product portfolio at Hamirpur due to geopolitical shifts and raw material availability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net cash increased to Rs 36 crore.
- Inventory closed on a higher side due to deferred Q2 export orders and rice herbicide stock; management expects normalization by Q4.
- Tax provision made for H1 stands at ~Rs 15 crore against Rs 9 crore paid, with shortfall to be cleared next quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainty, volatile raw material prices, freight charge spikes, and softer overseas demand.
- Deficient and erratic domestic rainfall impacting agricultural activity and herbicide demand.

1. **Vidhi Shah (C.R. Kothari & Sons) asked about raw material sourcing regions and expected EBITDA margins.**
   * *Answer:* D.K. Jain stated >65% is sourced from India, ~20% from China. Management is confident of maintaining Q2 EBITDA margins going forward. (Did not dodge).

3. **Yogansh asked about the spike in other expenses.**
   * *Answer:* S.P. Gupta explained that other expenses included ~Rs 12 crore in job work charges due to shifting from direct intermediate purchases to job-work conversion. (Did not dodge).
4. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor Company) asked about capitalization movement and subsidiary turnover targets.**
   * *Answer:* S.P. Gupta stated that ~Rs 65 crore was capitalized in the subsidiary in September, targeting Rs 40-50 crore revenue in H2, and an asset turn of 2x next year (~Rs 80 crore revenue). (Did not dodge).
5. **Giriraj Daga (Visaria Family Trust) asked about product changes at the Shalvis plant and capex guidance.**
   * *Answer:* D.K. Jain explained that product portfolios are being revisited due to geopolitical and raw material availability issues, using the plant's multipurpose flexibility. S.P. Gupta guided standalone capex at Rs 40-45 crore and subsidiary capex at Rs 80-90 crore. (Did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
India Pesticides reported a 22% decline in Q2 revenue due to erratic monsoons and softer export demand, but secured key international regulatory approvals and export orders to support H2 recovery.
