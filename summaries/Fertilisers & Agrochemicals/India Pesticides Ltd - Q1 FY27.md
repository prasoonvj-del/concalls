# India Pesticides Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Subdued domestic demand, deficient rainfall, and competitive pressure impacted near-term performance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 256 crores (down 9.2% YoY from INR 282 crores).
- **EBITDA:** INR 39 crores (down from INR 52 crores in Q1 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 15.4% (compared to 18.4% in Q1 FY26).
- **PAT:** INR 23 crores (compared to INR 35 crores in Q1 FY26).
- **PAT Margin:** 8.9%.
- **Export Revenue:** INR 89 crores (35% of total revenue; up from INR 87 crores in Q1 FY26).
- **Domestic Revenue:** INR 167 crores (down from INR 195 crores in Q1 FY26).
- **Chemicals Revenue:** INR 181 crores.
- **Formulations Revenue:** INR 72 crores.
- **Volume / Pricing Split:** Volume declined by ~13%; realizations rose by ~4%.
- **Cash Balance:** INR 59 crores as of 30th June 2026.
- **Capacity Utilization:** ~70%.

### Highlights
- Received Technical Equivalence (TEQ) approval from the European Union for a fungicide product, expected to add INR 30-40 crores in revenue annually starting around November.
- Hamirpur facility development progressing as planned with 2 out of 10 blocks currently operational; expected to generate INR 50-60 crores in revenue in FY27.
- Developed an intermediate plant using in-house R&D to support supply chain stability and reduce import dependence.
- Formulation business supported by 10,200 metric tonnes capacity, a network across 18 states, 24 depots, and over 370 sales professionals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects lower single-digit revenue growth for the full year FY27.
- Sustainable EBITDA margin guidance maintained around 18% over the long term (15.5% achieved in Q1 FY27).
- Hamirpur facility revenue potential estimated at INR 1,000 crores in 3 to 4 years.
- Sandila and Hamirpur combined capex guided at INR 70-100 crores annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic market impacted by subdued demand for key herbicide Pretilachlor, deficient rainfall, and slow/non-sowing conditions.
- Chinese imports arriving at significantly lower prices, creating a highly competitive market environment.
- Management expects demand normalization in Q3 and Q4.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin moderation driven by lower sales volume, higher employee costs, increased fuel expenses, a onetime write-off of export receivables of INR 2.5 crores, and INR 6 crores in higher job work charges incurred in anticipation of demand.
- Raw material prices and finished goods prices have moderated.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Combined capacity stands at 28,300 metric tonnes of technicals and 10,200 metric tonnes of formulations.
- Hamirpur expansion: Planned addition of 2 blocks every year (herbicide and fungicide blocks prioritized).
- Sandila expansion: Adding a new herbicide block; balancing equipment capex of INR 2-3 crores for a new import-replacement fungicide product.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company maintains a strong balance sheet with a healthy cash balance of INR 59 crores.
- Expansion to be funded entirely through internal accruals; no term loans planned.
- Inventory days increased from 170 days to ~200 days due to lower sales and buildup for anticipated demand; expected to normalize to ~170 days by Q3.
- Receivable days remain stable around 120 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Deficient and erratic monsoon affecting domestic herbicide (Pretilachlor) demand.
- Intense competition from lower-priced Chinese imports.
- Onetime write-off of export receivables worth INR 2.5 crores during the quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Yogansh Jeswani (Mittal Analytics) asked about the divergence between good gross margins and lower operating margins/other expenses.**
   * *Answer:* S.P. Gupta explained this was due to a INR 2.5 crore write-off on export receivables, INR 6 crores in higher job work charges incurred in anticipation of Q1 demand, and higher fuel costs. Management did not dodge.
2. **Yogansh Jeswani asked about the new product details and import substitution potential.**
   * *Answer:* D.K. Jain stated the company is introducing a fungicide currently imported mainly from China (4,000-5,000 tonnes total imports; IPL targets 2,000 tonnes capacity). Priced at INR 500-600/kg, it will be produced at Sandila with minimal balancing capex (INR 2-3 crores). Management did not dodge.
3. **Kaushal Sharma (Equinox Capital) asked about revenue potential and timeline for Hamirpur.**
   * *Answer:* D.K. Jain reiterated that Hamirpur should deliver INR 50-60 crores in FY27 and scale up to INR 1,000 crores over 3 to 4 years with 8 to 10 blocks. Management did not dodge.
4. **Karan Shah (GeeCee) asked about volume vs. pricing growth breakdown.**
   * *Answer:* S.P. Gupta clarified that volumes declined by ~13% while prices rose by ~4%. Management did not dodge.
5. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor Company) asked about the revenue potential and timeline for the EU TEQ approval.**
   * *Answer:* D.K. Jain stated that the approval will enable direct sales alongside existing customers, adding INR 30-40 crores annually to an existing baseline of INR 100 crores for that molecule, with sales commencing around November. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
India Pesticides reported a subdued Q1 FY27 with revenue down 9.2% YoY due to erratic monsoons and weak domestic herbicide demand, but remains confident in long-term growth driven by new product additions, EU regulatory approvals, and ongoing Hamirpur expansion funded via internal accruals.
