# Insecticides (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 8% YoY driven by premiumization and Kaeros, but volume declined 6-7% and H1 revenue registered a 2% degrowth due to adverse weather and El Nino impacts.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (management noted projects and timelines remain in line with previous plans).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from waiting for recovery in Q1 to observing an 8% revenue growth in Q2, though weather headwinds like El Nino and cyclones persisted.
- New topics or risks: Impact of recent cyclones on crops (e.g., chilli), crude oil prices touching $125 before dropping below $100, ocean freight cost pressures, and the appointment of a new Independent Director (Mr. Veera Bhadram).
- Last quarter's promises: Dahej plant partially capitalized in H1, with full capitalization now targeted by December (slightly adjusted from Q1 expectations); Sotanala timeline remains consistent (formulation by May next fiscal).

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 686 crore (up 8% YoY from Rs 637 crore)
- H1 FY27 revenue: Degrowth of 2% YoY
- Gross profit margin: 33.7% in Q2 FY27 (vs 34.5% in Q2 FY26); 32.7% in H1 FY27 (vs 31.7% in H1 FY26, an improvement of ~100 bps)
- EBITDA: Rs 92.44 crore; EBITDA margin: 13.5%
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 56.17 crore for Q2 FY27; ~Rs 100 crore for H1 FY27
- Volume growth: Negative 6% to 7% in Q2; Value growth: 4% to 5%
- Working capital days: 158 days in H1 FY27 (vs 151 days in H1 FY26)
- Net working capital: ~Rs 1,000 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 966 crore as of September 30, 2025, and Rs 828 crore as of March 31, 2026)
- Inventory: Rs 814 crore (vs Rs 744 crore YoY); Debtors: Rs 719 crore (vs Rs 643 crore YoY); Creditors: Rs 533 crore (vs Rs 420 crore YoY)
- Kaeros H1 performance: Brand turnover ~Rs 75 crore, standalone total sales ~Rs 114 crore; targeting ~Rs 125 crore brand turnover and ~Rs 200 crore standalone sales for the fiscal.

### Highlights
- Appointed Mr. Veera Bhadram as an Independent Director (40+ years experience, former whole-time Director of Coromandel International Limited).
- Kaeros platform expanded: North, East, and West fully open, with southern markets opening in Q2, achieving pan-India presence this year.
- IIL Crop Solution (ICS) plots doubled to ~70 across 4 major crops (rice, cotton, soybean, chilli); completed over 18,000 farmer meetings, 9,000 demonstrations, and reached over 2.5 lakh farmers in H1.
- Dahej project partial production ongoing, expected to be fully capitalized by December.
- Sotanala formulation facility expected to start around May next fiscal, with technical manufacturing following in the next fiscal.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be positive and aims to make some recovery from H1 losses.
- Kaeros brand turnover target of over Rs 125 crore for the fiscal (standalone ~Rs 200 crore), aiming to double brand business next year.
- International business long-term target: CAGR of 40%-50%, aiming for Rs 500 crore from international markets 5 years down the line.
- Premium product contribution target: aiming towards 70% (currently over 60%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Super El Nino and adverse weather disrupted key agriculture regions globally and in India, impacting wet crops (sugarcane, paddy) and dry crops (maize, cotton, soybean, chilli).
- Two recent cyclones had mixed impacts (water availability positive, crop damage negative).
- Agri-commodity prices showing inflationary trends and price hikes; farmers moving towards premium products and reducing the number of sprays, keeping profitability intact.
- International markets remain challenged by El Nino and currency hits.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Crude oil prices spiked to $125 before dropping below $100; high sea freight and material costs adversely impacted business.
- Inventories and debtors remain at elevated levels; management focused on improving cash flows and working capital efficiency.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dahej plant undergoing minor improvements and will operate at full swing following a shutdown/restart in December.
- Sotanala formulation setup expected by May next fiscal, and technical manufacturing in the next fiscal.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital stood at ~Rs 1,000 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Inventory elevated at Rs 814 crore and debtors at Rs 719 crore; working capital days at 158 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Adverse weather conditions (Super El Nino, erratic rains, cyclones) impacting crop yields and product volumes.
- Global uncertainties, high crude oil volatility ($125 peak), and expensive sea freight.
- Elevated inventories and debtors requiring heightened working capital focus.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bharat Gupta (Fair Value Capital) on volume growth:** Management stated volume dropped 6-7%, offset by 4-5% value growth driven by premium products.
- **Bharat Gupta on Kaeros numbers:** Management stated H1 brand turnover reached ~Rs 75 crore (total standalone ~Rs 114 crore) and targets ~Rs 125 crore brand turnover for the year.
- **Praneeth (SJ Investments) on full-year volume:** Management stated this is not a year of volume growth, but value growth and H2 recovery are expected.
- **Santosh Shetty (DD Capital) on R&D expenses:** Management stated ~Rs 12 crore is booked in financials, but ~200 personnel are directly/indirectly engaged across 4 R&D centers and the OIL joint venture.
- **Yash (Grit Securities) on international targets:** Management stated export contribution is ~5% and targets a 40%-50% CAGR to reach Rs 500 crore in 5 years.

### One-line takeaway
Insecticides (India) Ltd reported an 8% YoY revenue growth to Rs 686 crore in Q2 FY27 driven by premiumization and Kaeros expansion, despite a 6-7% volume decline caused by adverse weather and El Nino.
