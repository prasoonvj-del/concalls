# Deepak Fertilisers & Petrochemicals Corporation Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Recorded highest-ever Q2 EBITDA and PAT, with PAT growing 84% YoY on integrated value chain benefits.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Commissioning timeline for Gopalpur TAN and Dahej nitric acid projects shifted from Q2 FY27 to Q4 FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Demand view remains confident; Crop Nutrition faced short-term headwinds from uneven rains and RM inflation vs positive Kharif outlook last quarter, while TAN export ban was removed in October 2026.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: TAN export ban lifted; Chardham Chemicals acquisition progressing with Wardha plant revamp (production expected H2 FY28); elevated channel inventory in Crop Nutrition. Previous PESO portal logistics issue resolved/disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises: Targeted Q2 FY27 commissioning for Gopalpur TAN and Dahej nitric acid was missed; both projects are now ~98% complete and targeted for Q4 FY27 commissioning.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 3,334 crore, up 11% YoY.
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 750 crore, up 62% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22.5%, expanding 700 bps YoY (from 15.5%).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 394 crore, up 84% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Financials:** Revenue Rs 6,590 crore (+16% YoY); EBITDA Rs 1,595 crore (+63% YoY); PAT Rs 884 crore (+93% YoY).
- **Segment Highlights:** Nitric acid volumes grew 14% YoY; IPA volume stood at 17 KT; Croptek and specialty products contributed ~28% of Crop Nutrition revenue; B2C contributed ~16% of Mining Chemicals revenue (up from 14% YoY).

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever Q2 EBITDA and PAT, with H1 PAT (Rs 884 crore) nearly matching full-year FY26 profits.
- Gopalpur TAN and Dahej Nitric Acid expansion projects reached ~98% completion.
- Government of India lifted the export ban on Technical Ammonium Nitrate (TAN) in October 2026; market development commenced in November.
- *Pattern break vs record:* Commissioning of Gopalpur TAN and Dahej nitric acid projects delayed from Q2 FY27 to Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Dahej and Gopalpur projects to start commercial operations in Q4 FY27, ramping up to 75% utilization in weeks, 85% shortly after, and >95% in FY28.
- Crop Nutrition margins expected to normalize to ~5%-7% in H2 FY27.
- Commercial production from Chardham Chemicals expected to contribute to bottom line in H2 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Mining Chemicals demand expected to pick up in H2 post-monsoon; Eastern India and Singrauli region account for 55% of national TAN demand.
- IPA realizations remain strong due to supply tightness and pharma-grade mix; nitric acid pricing showing early recovery.
- Kharif Crop Nutrition demand was impacted by uneven rainfall, raw material inflation, and elevated channel inventory, with Rabi season liquidation underway.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by 15-year Equinor long-term LNG sourcing, high in-house ammonia integration, and product mix.
- Materials run mostly on just-in-time inventory, resulting in negligible P&L inventory gains/losses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex incurred was ~Rs 774 crore; total project execution remains within approved budgets.
- Dahej nitric acid addition: ~300,000 tonnes (more than doubling existing sales of 240,000–250,000 tonnes/year).
- Gopalpur TAN addition: ~380,000 tonnes (+60% to 70% expansion over current sales of 550,000–560,000 tonnes/year).
- No major new growth capex planned post-CCPL completion; only normal maintenance capex planned near-term.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 4,743 crore; Net Debt-to-EBITDA ratio comfortable at 1.5x.
- Project capex funded 55%–60% via term loans (~Rs 4,600 crore capitalized assets), expecting ~Rs 180–200 crore annual depreciation addition.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term headwinds in Crop Nutrition from raw material inflation and high channel inventory.
- Temporary RGP supply constraints impacted IPA production volume.
- Brief market turbulence expected as domestic competitor TAN capacity comes online.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sheel Shah (Sameeksha Capital) on LNG/ammonia cost savings quantification:** Management declined to state specific savings figures (dodged exact quantification), noting benefits are structural and margins will stay in a healthy range.
- **Parth Kotak (Plus91 Asset Management) on future capex and demerger/IPO timelines:** Management stated no major growth capex is planned post-CCPL, and internal management discussions on demerger/IPO timelines are ongoing.
- **Rohit Sinha (Sunidhi Securities) on Gopalpur plant ramp-up:** Management clarified 75% capacity utilization will be achieved within a few weeks of commercial operation start in Q4 FY27, not over the entire quarter.
- **Yash Gupta (Asit Family Office) on TAN export ban:** Management confirmed the export ban was fully lifted in October 2026 and international market development began in November.

### One-line takeaway
Deepak Fertilisers delivered historic Q2 profitability driven by structural gas and ammonia integration, with its Dahej and Gopalpur capacity expansions 98% complete for Q4 FY27 startup.
