# Sharda Cropchem Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue growth, margin expansion, and strong performance in NAFTA and LATAM offsetting European weather headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,052 crore (up 13% YoY)
- **Agrochemical revenue:** Rs 840 crore (up 5% YoY)
- **Non-agrochemical revenue:** Rs 212 crore (up 68% YoY)
- **Gross margin:** 35.9% (up 140 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 164 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 15.6% (up 70 bps YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 92 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 2,125 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 343 crore (up 22% YoY; margin at 16.1%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 180 crore
- **Cash, bank & liquid investments:** Rs 846 crore as of 30th September 2026
- **Working capital days:** 70 days as of 30th September 2026 (improved by 28 days vs March 2026)
- **ROCE / ROE (H1):** ROCE at 25.9%, ROE at 20.1%
- **Total product registrations:** 3,044 as of 30th September 2026 (Pipeline: 946 applications)
- **Q2 Revenue Drivers:** Price increase, positive forex impact, and product mix contribution of 18.3%; volume degrowth of -5.1%
- **H1 Revenue Drivers:** Price increase, positive forex impact, and product mix contribution of 14.5%; volume degrowth of -3.5%
- **Capex:** H1 capex spent at Rs 419 crore (planned FY27 capex Rs 550 crore to Rs 600 crore)

### Highlights
- NAFTA revenue grew ~30% YoY with significantly better margins; LATAM agrochemical revenue grew over 80%.
- Europe (largest market) saw moderation due to extended heat and drought conditions in Central and Eastern Europe causing distributor caution in restocking.
- Non-agrochemical growth driven by conveyor and rubber belts due to sudden pent-up demand and logistics tightness.
- Total registrations breakdown: Europe 1,701, NAFTA 333, LATAM 760, Rest of World 250 (Pipeline: Europe 548, NAFTA 103, LATAM 155, Rest of World 140).
- Q2 Regional gross margins: Europe 41%, NAFTA ~40%, LATAM 14%, Rest of World 31%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance at 10% to 15%.
- EBITDA margins expected to improve through the year, remaining between 18% to 20% for FY27, supported by better product mix and operating leverage in H2.
- Planned FY27 capex of Rs 550 crore to Rs 600 crore.
- Expects a recovery in Europe in H2 (seasonally stronger period) as weather normalizes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- European weather challenges (extended heat and drought) led to temporary caution in restocking; underlying demand remains strong.
- China product availability is good with fairly competitive prices.
- Tariffs do not affect the agrochemical business because products require specific registrations and approved sourcing with limited customer choices, allowing full pass-through of tariff hikes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins expanded by 140 bps YoY to 35.9% in Q2.
- Non-agro margins impacted by surging transportation costs for conveyor belts.
- Amortization expenses increased due to capitalization of intangible assets (product registrations) amortized over 5 years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex spend stood at Rs 419 crore, driven partly by higher-than-foreseen data compensation negotiations/arbitrations in the US.
- Asset-light registration-led business model maintained.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free with net cash/liquid investments of Rs 846 crore.
- Working capital reduced to 70 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unpredictability of registration approvals and last-minute regulatory requirements/costs from authorities.
- High unpredictability in arbitration outcomes for data compensation (specifically noted in the US).
- Freight cost inflation impacting non-agrochemical margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anubhav Mukherjee (Prescient Capital) asked about the volume vs price/forex split.** Management clarified Q2 revenue growth included an 18.3% positive impact from prices, forex, and product mix, offset by a 5.1% volume degrowth.
2. **Jatin Damania (Svan Investments) questioned the drop in pipeline registrations despite high capex.** Management explained that registrations are an ongoing process with some discontinued products, and higher H1 capex was driven by US data compensation settlements.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investment) asked about volume degrowth and inventory build-up.** Management stated H1 anomalies will normalize in H2, driven by seasonally higher demand in Q3 and Q4.
4. **Sonal Minhas (Prescient Capital) asked about rising amortization as a leading indicator for revenue.** Management confirmed that higher amortization from capitalized intangible assets will continue and will drive future revenue growth.
5. **Rajat Setiya (iThought PMS) asked if tariffs impact the agrochemical business.** Management clarified that tariffs do not impact them because products are not freely tradable and tariff increases are easily passed on to customers.

### One-line takeaway
Sharda Cropchem delivered a healthy Q2 with 13% revenue growth and 23% PAT growth, led by robust performance in NAFTA and LATAM that offset weather-led European softness, while maintaining its full-year 10-15% revenue growth and 18-20% EBITDA margin guidance.
