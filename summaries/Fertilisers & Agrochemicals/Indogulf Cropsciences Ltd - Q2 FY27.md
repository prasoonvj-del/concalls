# Indogulf Cropsciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered revenue growth and improved profitability despite a challenging domestic agrochemical backdrop.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (management declined to share forward-looking guidance numbers, similar to last quarter, citing industry uncertainty).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to slightly more resilient; while Q2 remained challenging due to 13% below-normal monsoon and drought conditions, deferred demand from Q1 and a richer product mix helped drive a sequential recovery.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Reallocation of Rs. 8.34 crore of unutilized IPO proceeds from an in-house DF plant to a specialty machinery and climate stress management facility at Barwasni (Sonipat); expansion of subsidiaries like Abhiprakash Globus into Chhattisgarh and Orissa. Old risks of China export incentives did not feature this quarter, while Middle East conflict supply chain disruptions and generic price volatility were newly emphasized.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The new climate stress management facility is expected to be operational by Q4 FY27 (reallocating IPO proceeds originally meant for the DF plant).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 255.8 crore (up 3% YoY from Rs. 248.3 crore; up ~52% QoQ from Rs. 168.5 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 424.3 crore (degrowth of 3% YoY).
- **Gross Profit (Q2):** Rs. 76.8 crore (up 5% YoY from Rs. 73.1 crore).
- **Gross Margin (Q2):** 30% (compared to 29% in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA (Q2):** Rs. 40.6 crore (up 27% YoY from Rs. 32 crore).
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2):** 16% (improved from 13% in Q2 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2):** Rs. 25.7 crore (up 24% YoY from Rs. 20.7 crore).
- **PAT Margin (Q2):** 10% (compared to 8% in Q2 FY26).
- **Finance Cost (Q2):** Rs. 4.1 crore (11% lower YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Channel Mix:** B2C 59%, B2B 35%, Exports 6%.
- **H1 FY27 Product Mix (Revenue):** Crop Protection 85%, Biologicals 6%, Plant Nutrients 5%.
- **Manufacturing Capacity Utilization:** Average 60% in H1 FY27 (70% in Q1, ~50% in Q2).
- **Inventory:** Rs. 349.6 crore as of September (compared to Rs. 343.5 crore at March '26 end).
- **Subsidiary Revenue (Abhiprakash Globus):** Rs. 33.35 crore (up 58% QoQ).

### Highlights
- Improved profitability driven by a richer product mix (higher share of biologicals and nutrients) and prudent cost management.
- Operating costs reduced by 14.2% of revenue (employee expenses down 8% YoY to Rs. 13.3 crore; other expenses down 14% to Rs. 22.9 crore).
- Strong growth in key brand products in H1 FY27: Nova King (up 25x YoY to Rs. 2.23 crore), Spine (up 76% YoY to Rs. 5 crore), Fire (up 31% YoY to Rs. 9.73 crore), and Indo Apache (up to Rs. 11.50 crore).
- Export milestones: Egypt revenue up 129% YoY; reactivated sales in Sudan; entered Taiwan, Germany (for Latin America), and Israel (plant nutrition); field trials initiated in Brazil.
- Board proposed reallocating Rs. 8.34 crore of unutilized IPO proceeds from an in-house DF plant to a dedicated climate stress management and plant growth regulators facility at Barwasni, Sonipat, expected to be operational by Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 FY27 to remain a challenging quarter due to persistent El Nino conditions through winter and high channel inventory.
- Industry analysts expect agri-input earnings to bottom out between October and December 2026, with recovery anticipated from Q1 FY28 as weather conditions normalize.
- Management remains focused on factors within their control: richer product mix, new product introductions, and disciplined cost control.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic agrochemical industry faced a challenging Q2 due to below-normal and uneven Southwest monsoons (rain season closed ~13% below long-period average, weakest since 2015).
- Drought conditions declared in parts of Southern India and Maharashtra; delayed rainfall and dry September reduced herbicide usage as farmers resorted to mechanical weeding, while weaker pest/fungal pressure subdued insecticides and fungicides.
- Generic agrochemical prices spiked 15-20% between March and May due to Middle East conflicts and freight issues, but corrected to pre-conflict levels by June; elevated channel inventory and weak demand constrained pass-through of costs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Packaging costs rose due to higher crude oil prices, but overall operating costs declined as a percentage of revenue due to lower employee and other expenses.
- Generic product prices corrected to pre-conflict levels since June.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Reallocating Rs. 8.34 crore of unutilized IPO proceeds towards specialty machinery and a dedicated climate stress management/plant growth regulators facility (9,000 sq. ft.) at Barwasni, Sonipat, replacing the previously planned DF plant line due to pricing pressure and shrinking margins.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory remained elevated at Rs. 349.6 crore at September end (vs. Rs. 343.5 crore in March '26) due to below-plan seasonal sales, leading to increased working capital and negative operating cash flow in H1 FY27.
- Management expects operating cash flow to turn positive in H2 as harvesting starts and stock liquidation/debt collections improve.
- Supplier credit lines and credit ratings remain healthy.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Below-normal and uneven Southwest monsoons and persistence of El Nino conditions through winter.
- Elevated channel inventory and cautious dealer restocking.
- Weak farm economics and uncertain Rabi soil moisture.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash Parkar (Patel Investments) asked about gross margin drivers and balancing revenue vs. profitability.** Management stated gross margins improved via a favorable product mix (higher share of nutrients/biologicals) and new product additions while discarding low-margin products.
- **Poonam Jain (Sequent Investment) asked about negative operating cash flow in H1 and when it will turn positive.** Management explained that lower rainfall increased debtors, outstanding dues, and inventory; cash flow is expected to turn positive in H2 as harvesting begins and stocks liquidate.
- **Akshara Joshi (Joshi Investment) asked about 3-5 year growth priorities and supplier payments.** Management highlighted biologicals, nutrients, and the wholly-owned subsidiary Abhiprakash Globus expanding into new states as key drivers, while confirming strong supplier credentials and credit lines.
- **Maitri Shah (Sapphire Capital) asked about B2B vs B2C targets and sustainability of 16% EBITDA margins.** Management clarified focus is on brand (B2C) business, particularly biologicals and nutrients, and declined to provide forward-looking margin guidance while affirming ongoing cost and portfolio optimization.
- **Nikita Mehta (Mehta Investments) asked about the long-term potential of biologicals and in-house vs. external product development.** Management stated biologicals/nutrients are developed in-house based on identified field gaps, whereas crop protection leverages off-patented molecules with 4-5 year windows.

### One-line takeaway
Indogulf reported a resilient Q2 FY27 with 3% revenue growth and a 27% jump in EBITDA to Rs. 40.6 crore, driven by a higher-margin product mix and cost controls despite severe monsoon headwinds.
