# Coromandel International Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-31 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Resilient performance in non-fertilizer segments and strong market share gains, offset by margin pressure in the fertilizer business due to elevated raw material costs and delayed subsidy revisions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 8,215 crore (up 15% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 761 crore (down 3% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 382 crore (down from Rs 502 crore YoY).
*   **Fertilizer Sales Volume:** 10 lakh tonnes (down 9% YoY).
*   **Crop Protection Revenue:** Rs 870 crore (up 20% YoY).
*   **Crop Protection EBIT:** Rs 159 crore (up 44% YoY).
*   **Subsidy Outstanding:** Rs 3,254 crore (as of June 30).

### Highlights
*   **Market Share:** Primary market share in fertilizers increased to 22% (vs 18% YoY).
*   **Retail Business:** Revenue grew 85% YoY; 76% of stores were profitable (vs 61% YoY).
*   **NACL Industries:** EBITDA increased 9% to Rs 41 crore.
*   **Projects:** Sulphuric acid and phosphoric acid plants commissioned and stabilized. Granulation project on track for Q4 commissioning.
*   **Sustainability:** Received Responsible Care Certification.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Capacity:** Management targets 4 million tonnes of fertilizer capacity + 1 million tonnes trading (DAP) + 1 million tonnes SSP + organic/urea, totaling 8-9 million tonnes of nutrient visibility for the next 2 years.
*   **Profitability:** Management aims for steady-state EBITDA of Rs 6,500 per metric tonne in the nutrient business once normalcy returns.
*   **Capex:** Management plans to maintain a sustainable capex of ~Rs 300 crore annually, barring new high-return opportunities.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Monsoon:** 17% deficit (as of late July); all-India reservoir levels at 34% (vs 57% last year).
*   **Crop Acreages:** Mixed; pulses, oilseed, and cotton down, while paddy is normal.
*   **Regulatory:** Government introduced QR code-enabled traceability for fertilizer sales (pilot in 10-12 states).
*   **Consumption:** Phosphatic fertilizer consumption remains stable; industry inventories are comfortable.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Materials:** Phosphoric acid prices for Q2 settled at $1,700 (vs $1,360 in Q1).
*   **Sulphur:** Prices remain elevated; management expects them to soften eventually.
*   **Subsidy:** Government announced ~10% increase in NBS rates, which management states does not fully compensate for raw material cost spikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Fertilizer:** Capacity utilization moderated to 72% (6.9 lakh tonnes) to optimize inventory.
*   **Crop Protection:** Capacity expansion for key molecules on track for September commissioning.
*   **Senegal:** Evaluating SSP facility to unlock value from phosphate resources.
*   **Other:** T-MAP plant at Kakinada and seaweed granulation plant are progressing well.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Subsidy:** Received Rs 1,392 crore in Q1; additional Rs 568 crore received in July.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Middle East crisis causing supply disruptions and raw material price surges.
*   **Regulatory:** Bans on Nano DAP/Urea in UP and Maharashtra moderating growth.
*   **Margin Pressure:** Elevated input costs (sulphur/ammonia) not fully covered by current NBS subsidy rates.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Ankur Periwal (Axis Capital):** Asked about structural shift in fertilizer demand. Management: No shift, current uptake driven by monsoon; no shortage.
*   **Somaiah (Avendus Spark):** Asked about capex priorities. Management: Focusing on cash generation from recent Rs 7,000 crore investment; capex limited to ~Rs 300 crore/year unless high-return projects arise.
*   **Tarang (Old Bridge):** Asked about pricing actions. Management: Industry has taken 25-30% price increase; further increases not doable, requires subsidy correction.
*   **Prashant (Elara Capital):** Asked about subsidy requirement. Management: Seeking 6-month average rates as per policy; current rates insufficient.
*   **Nirav (Anvil Wealth):** Asked about steady-state EBITDA. Management: Reaffirmed target of Rs 6,500 per metric tonne for nutrient business once normalcy returns.

### One-line takeaway
Coromandel delivered a resilient performance driven by non-fertilizer segments and market share gains, despite significant margin headwinds in the fertilizer business due to elevated raw material costs and delayed subsidy revisions.
