# Deepak Fertilisers & Petrochemicals Corporation Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-06 · Fertilisers & Agrochemicals*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved historic best Q1 performance with a 65% jump in operating EBITDA and doubled PAT, driven by structural integration and realizations.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 3,256 crore, up 22% YoY and 8% QoQ.
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 845 crore, up 65% YoY and 139% QoQ.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 26% (compared to 19% YoY and 12% in Q4).
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 490 crore, up 101% YoY and 252% QoQ.
- **Mining Chemical Revenue:** Rs 911 crore, up 37% YoY; volume at 130 KT. B2C revenue up 42% to Rs 151 crore (17% of segment).
- **Industrial Chemical Revenue:** Rs 490 crore.
- **Crop Nutrition Revenue:** Rs 1,367 crore, up 9% YoY; manufactured NPK sales up 4%. Specialty/Croptek contributed 43% of segment revenue.
- **Balance Sheet & Debt:** Net debt reduced to Rs 4,719 crore; Debt/EBITDA improved to 1.4x (from 2.86x).
- **Capex:** Over Rs 500 crore incurred in Q1; total spend till Q1 around Rs 3,850 crore.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever Q1 EBITDA and PAT despite geopolitical and supply chain volatility.
- Commenced long-term LNG supplies with Equinor, receiving the first cargo in May.
- Ammonia plant achieved 94% average capacity utilization; debottlenecking improved capacity by ~10%.
- Completed an explosives company acquisition in May to strengthen the mining solutions value chain.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the Gopalpur TAN project (96% complete) and Dahej nitric acid project (93% complete) to commence operations during Q2 FY27.
- Management expects a faster ramp-up for the new capacities as the chemistry and plants are familiar.
- Management expects balance sheet deleveraging to start from this year onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Crop Nutrition:** Widespread rains and the El Nino worry being behind, alongside a shift to horticulture, provide positive tailwinds.
- **Mining Chemicals:** Strong demand driven by coal, infrastructure, minerals, and metals, though Q2 typically sees a monsoon slowdown.
- **Industrial Chemicals:** India's focus on pharmaceuticals and specialty chemicals boosts demand; IPA volumes expected to recover as propylene availability improves.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Middle East war conditions created sourcing and pricing strain on phos acid, sulphur, and raw materials.
- Ammonia and finished product prices remain elevated due to the Middle East conflict; management expects elevated prices to persist for a few more quarters.
- Equinor LNG supply integration is driving cost efficiency and commercial benefits.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gopalpur TAN project is ~96% complete; Dahej nitric acid project is ~93% complete. Both remain within the approved capex envelope.
- Ammonia plant successfully debottlenecked with an ~10% capacity improvement.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 4,719 crore, with Debt/EBITDA at 1.4x despite peak investment cycle capex.
- Businesses are described as working capital efficient.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Middle East geopolitical conflict causing international commodity volatility and raw material supply strains.
- Temporary PESO portal guideline changes created outward logistics and production disruptions in the TAN business during the quarter.
- Propylene availability constraints impacted IPA volumes during Q1.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Hardik Shah (Brick Capital) on ammonia capacity and capex timelines:** Management stated average utilization was 94% (aided by a 10% debottlenecking gain) and confirmed both major projects remain on track to begin operations by the end of Q2 FY27.
- **Pritesh Chheda on TAN volume reduction YoY:** Management clarified the drop was due to a temporary few-day supply chain disruption from PESO portal changes, not demand or raw material issues.
- **Adarsh Jain on business cycles and past volatility:** Management noted structural changes—such as long-term Equinor LNG integration, upcoming new capex, and a shift to B2C/specialties—will prevent the severe volatility seen in past cycles.
- **Ranjit on explosives acquisition and market positioning:** Management explained the acquisition completes the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) outcome-driven mining model, operating differently from standard input-selling competitors.
- **Nirav Jimudia on Equinor LNG contract savings and phasing:** Management stated that Equinor savings are sizable and will eventually cover total LNG requirements, though they declined to disclose exact commercial savings numbers or grade-specific net realizations (partially dodged exact quantitative savings).

### One-line takeaway
DFPCL posted its highest-ever Q1 EBITDA and PAT, propelled by an integrated value chain, Equinor LNG cost efficiencies, and advanced commissioning of major growth projects.
