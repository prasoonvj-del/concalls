# Jain Resource Recycling Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-08 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Margins impacted by evolving product mix, West Asia crisis, and facility ramp-up, though management remains bullish on long-term growth and value addition.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations: Rs. 2,725 crore (up ~76% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 110 crore (up ~22% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: ~4% (compared to 5.8% in Q1 FY26 and 3.5% in Q4 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs. 69 crore (up ~23% YoY)
- PAT Margin: ~2.5% (compared to 3.6% in Q1 FY26)
- Return on Equity (RoE): ~22.8% (as of June 2026)
- Return on Capital Employed (RoCE): ~21.4% (as of June 2026)
- Working Capital Cycle: ~60 days (Inventory days: ~55, Debtor days: ~19, Creditor days: -14)
- Copper Anode production: ~600 metric tonnes sold in Q1 FY27

### Highlights
- Copper and copper products contributed 67% of consolidated revenue (vs 55% in FY26); lead and lead alloys contributed 29%, aluminium contributed 3%.
- Successfully commissioned the entire copper anode production line (two furnaces, 1,600 metric tonnes/month capacity) and sold ~600 tonnes.
- Unit-2 facility at Gummidipoondi suffered a furnace accident on 14th July 2026 resulting in a fatality and injuries; operations resumed on 27th July 2026 after regulatory approvals. Management stated insurance covers damages and there is no major impact on annual production.
- Ahmedabad JV (with C&Y Group Investment Incorporation) commenced trial production; Kuwait strategic investment faced shipment delays due to the West Asia war crisis.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained guidance for volume growth in FY27/FY28 driven by capacity expansions and value-added projects, but stated that exact percentage growth/number guidance is premature due to external challenges.
- Total capital expenditure outlay for FY27 is expected to be approximately Rs. 87 crore, directed toward copper value-added projects, antimony, and plastic recycling.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Favorable structural tailwinds expected from government amendments to Hazardous Waste Management Rules mandating minimum 5% recycled content in products starting in FY28, which is expected to boost domestic scrap availability and organized recycling demand.
- Global sourcing network and domestic sourcing were expanded to mitigate overseas logistics disruptions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins moderated YoY due to product mix evolution, value-added business ramp-ups, and higher local/import raw material costs caused by West Asia logistics issues.
- Copper value-added products are expected to add ~2% to existing copper margins once fully stabilized.
- Company maintains a 100% commodity hedging policy via LME to protect against price fluctuations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Copper Cathode Phase-1 (1,500 metric tonnes/month) scheduled for commissioning in Q2 FY27.
- Copper wire rod (600 metric tonnes/month) and copper busbar/profiles (1,500 metric tonnes/month) scheduled for commissioning in Q3 FY27.
- Antimony project (100 metric tonnes/month output) scheduled for Q3 FY27 commissioning.
- Dedicated plastic recycling facility (investment ~Rs. 15 crore, 6 acres) scheduled to start in Q3 FY27.
- Lead capacity expansion of 15% to 20% is nearing completion, pending final approvals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong capital base maintained post-IPO.
- Rs. 20-30 crore of raw material is currently stuck in vessels due to the West Asia crisis, but management confirmed it is fully insured against war/detention with expected recovery.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia war crisis causing shipping/logistics delays for machinery imports and raw material sourcing.
- Furnace accident at Unit-2 facility resulting in operational shutdown and safety reviews.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raj Shah (Fident AMC)** asked about lead and copper EBITDA per tonne trends. *Management explained that lower margins/per-tonne fluctuations are quarter-to-quarter phenomena due to shipping cycles and hedging, and value-added copper products will drive margin recovery.*
- **Abhishek Mehra (DAM Capital)** asked for volume guidance in lead and copper for FY27/FY28. *Management stated volume growth is expected, but giving a specific percentage guidance is premature given international disruptions.*
- **Disha Chamriya** asked about the impact of the FY28 recycling mandate. *Management confirmed it will increase domestic scrap availability and demand for recycled content, positively impacting organized players.*
- **Darshil** questioned the sustainability of fluctuating quarterly EBITDA per tonne. *Management reiterated that recycling businesses must be evaluated on multi-quarter/annual averages rather than isolated quarterly numbers due to transit and hedging cycles.*
- **Krishnan Thampi** asked if the company is still 100% hedged. *Management confirmed the 100% hedging policy remains unchanged and broker limits of >$30 million cover M2M variations without blocking cash.*

### One-line takeaway
Jain Resource Recycling reported a 76% YoY revenue growth in Q1 FY27 alongside margin moderation due to product mix and West Asia logistics headwinds, while advancing multiple high-margin copper value-addition and multi-metal capacity expansions.
