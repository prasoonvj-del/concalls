# Hi-Tech Pipes Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume and revenue growth driven by infrastructure and construction demand, alongside capacity ramp-ups, despite cost headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Sales volume:** 1,56,136 metric tons (Q1 FY27) vs 124,000 metric tons (Q1 FY26), up 26% YoY.
- **Revenue:** INR1,413 crores (Q1 FY27) vs INR791 crores (Q1 FY26), up 79% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** INR49.37 crores (Q1 FY27) vs INR41 crores (Q1 FY26), up 20% YoY.
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** INR20 crores (Q1 FY27) vs INR20.92 crores (Q1 FY26).
- **EBITDA per ton:** INR3,162 (Q1 FY27) vs INR3,148 (Q4 FY26).
- **Capex guidance:** INR200 crores for FY27; total INR650 crores for the 1 million ton expansion (with INR300-350 crores remaining over the next two financial years).
- **Volume guidance:** 6.5 lakh to 7 lakh tons for FY27; 1 million tons for FY28.

### Highlights
- Achieved strong volume and revenue growth supported by infrastructure and construction demand and newly added capacities.
- Added new sizes, particularly in Jumbo Hollow sections, focusing on value-added and differentiated products.
- Entered the international market for solar products this year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is optimistic about structural growth in the steel tubes and pipes industry.
- Reaffirmed long-term vision of achieving 2 million tons capacity by FY29, adding another 1 million tons in the next 2 to 3 years.
- Sales volume target: 6.5 lakh to 7 lakh tons for FY27; 1 million tons for FY28.
- Value-added products (VAP) share target: 45% to 50% for FY28.
- International market target: 10% of total sales volumes/revenue from international markets.
- EBITDA per ton target: Upwards of INR4,000 per ton (expected by the end of FY28 as new plants reach critical mass and introductory discounts stop).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand from infrastructure and construction remains very strong with previously on-held projects taking shape.
- Gaining share in the solar segment (solar torque tubes).
- Data centers emerging as a new green-shoot segment for large hollow sections, with an eye on 15,000 to 20,000 tons for the financial year.
- End-user application mix: 50% construction and infrastructure, 20% water and oil and gas transportation, balance from engineering, solar, and specialized segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA per ton impacted by elevated gas prices (more than doubled) and significantly higher logistics costs and ocean freights.
- Offered extra special rebates to dealers for market penetration in newly operationalized plants, which weighed on near-term margins.
- DFT and API EBITDA per ton currently around INR4,500 to INR5,000; API segment expected at INR6,000 upwards.
- Purchase of stock-in-trade includes import consignments disposed of in high seas due to geopolitical situations; expected to normalize to INR50 crores to INR100 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Direct Forming Technology (DFT) facility at Sanand Unit-II Phase-3 expected to be operational by Q3 FY27.
- API pipes facility expected to be ready by Q4 FY27.
- Hindupur (Andhra Pradesh) integrated manufacturing facility for ERW tubes, solar tubes, and specialized steel products expected to become operational by Q4 FY27.
- Additional 0.5 million tons set to start construction by the end of the financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Finance cost elevated due to the simultaneous operationalization of 3 facilities and related working capital within a 4 to 5-month span; expected to normalize as utilization reaches optimum levels.
- Total remaining capex for the 1 million ton expansion is INR300-350 crores across FY28 and FY29.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated gas prices and higher logistics/ocean freight costs impacting EBITDA.
- Geopolitical situations affecting import consignments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pallav Agarwal (Antique Stock Broking):** Asked about volume and capex targets and VAP share timeline. Management guided for 1 million tons in FY28, INR650 crore total capex (INR200 crores in FY27), and 45%-50% VAP share by FY28.
- **Lokesh Kashikar (SMIFS Institutional Equities):** Inquired about segment-wise capacity breakdown and high finance costs. Management stated DFT and coated steel form the major share, API will contribute to the bottom line, and finance costs will normalize as new plants ramp up.
- **Manan Poladia (MKP Securities):** Asked about industry breakup and EBITDA per ton timeline to INR4,000. Management cited 50% infrastructure/construction, 20% water and oil/gas, and stated EBITDA/ton will move past INR4,000 once introductory dealer discounts phase out.

### One-line takeaway
Hi-Tech Pipes reported strong volume and revenue growth in Q1 FY27, backed by infrastructure demand, while advancing its 1 million ton capacity expansion plan toward a 2 million ton FY29 target.
