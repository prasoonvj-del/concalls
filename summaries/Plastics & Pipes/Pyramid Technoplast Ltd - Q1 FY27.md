# Pyramid Technoplast Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited a strong, structurally healthier operating platform with improving unit economics and solar/recycling benefits despite temporary export demand headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 222 crore, up 36% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Up 50% YoY; EBITDA margin at 10%.
- **EBITDA per ton:** Rs 16,380 in Q1 FY27 (compared to Rs 11,252 in Q1 FY26 and Rs 15,053 in Q4 FY26).
- **PAT:** Rs 10.5 crore, up 32% YoY; PAT margin at 5%.
- **Capacity Utilization:** ~62% (total installed capacity at 20,936 metric tons per quarter; volumes at 1,292 metric tons).
- **Wada Facility Contribution:** Rs 43 crore (19% of revenue); operating above 70% utilization.
- **Solar Savings:** ~Rs 2 crore saved in Q1 FY27.
- **Government Subsidies Approved:** ~Rs 24.9 crore for Wada and ~Rs 10.5 crore for Unit 7 (Bharuch), spread over 10 years.

### Highlights
- Revenue growth driven primarily by higher realization/passing on of sharp raw material cost increases.
- HDPE polymer drum tonnage down 4% YoY; IBC volumes impacted by export slowdown due to Middle East conflict, partly offset by strong MS drums growth.
- Gross margin compressed to 23% as raw material costs rose alongside selling prices; gross margin in rupee terms for HDPE remained stable.
- Green energy initiatives: 6 MW solar plant commissioned in October 2025, followed by 5 MW in Bharuch and 2.25 MW in Maharashtra (1 MW pending). Expected annual savings of ~Rs 15 crore once fully operational.
- Recycling plant processed ~150 metric tons in Q1 FY27, generating Rs 25 lakh EBITDA (estimated annual EBITDA contribution of ~Rs 2 crore; addresses 10-12% of raw material requirements).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 objective to deliver ~15% revenue growth and EBITDA margins upwards of 10% (targeting 11-12% as polymer prices normalize).
- FY27 EBITDA targeted at ~Rs 85-86 crore.
- FY27 Capex planned at Rs 20-25 crore, primarily for Kutch expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Export slowdown in the Middle East due to geopolitical conflict impacted IBC and chemical-laden drum exports, causing a near-term volume disruption rather than a structural issue.
- Domestic demand remains solid, with volume recovery expected as freight costs and polymer prices normalize.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Polymer selling price increased from ~Rs 138/kg in March to ~Rs 193/kg in Q1 FY27. Management expects polymer prices to drop by ~Rs 20 over the next 3-4 months toward Rs 140/kg, which will increase volume and improve percentage EBITDA margins.
- Financial costs rose 179% YoY and depreciation by 61% YoY due to capacity expansions. Finance cost expected to stabilize around Rs 3 crore per quarter by year-end.
- Cash conversion cycle / debtor days: 65 to 70 days.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New facility in Kutch, Western India: Investing Rs 20-25 crore for a capacity of 10,000 IBC units per month; expected to be commissioned by March 2027 with revenue potential of Rs 50-100 crore and a 4-year payback period. Land is owned.
- Wada Phase 2 expansion planned for next financial year post-March, requiring another Rs 20-25 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Term loan repayment has started; working capital loans increased due to higher raw material purchase prices (polymer prices rising from Rs 100 to Rs 150+).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflict in the Middle East disrupting export freight and overseas sales.
- EPR portal closure by the government for 3-4 months temporarily delaying credit pass-ons.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Saket Krishna / Saket Kapoor on EBITDA per ton and margins:** Management explained that higher absolute raw material values inflated revenues while suppressing percentage margins, but absolute per-unit profitability (Rs 16,380/ton) remains strong and will convert to higher percentage margins as raw material prices normalize.
- **Ankit Kanodia on raw material volatility and pricing lag:** Management stated that local procurement and booking orders in the first week of the month largely insulate the company from severe stock/pricing losses compared to historical import lags.
- **Ganesh Nagarsekar on Kutch plant revenue and utilization:** Management clarified that Kutch will initially target ~Rs 50 crore in revenue out of a Rs 90-100 crore full potential, funded via internal accruals.
- **Dipesh Sancheti on margin expectations vs. expectations:** Management reiterated that top-line value growth masked volume stability, and solar savings will fully materialize on an annualized basis once all units run at peak capacity.

### One-line takeaway
Pyramid Technoplast reported a 36% revenue growth in Q1 FY27 driven by higher realizations, while maintaining resilient per-unit profitability and advancing green energy and regional expansion initiatives despite temporary export headwinds.
