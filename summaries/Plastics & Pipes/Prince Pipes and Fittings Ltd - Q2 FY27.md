# Prince Pipes and Fittings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by stabilization of PVC prices, robust volume growth, distribution expansion, and improving capital efficiency.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 821 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **Volume (Q2 FY27):** 52,080 tonnes (up 22% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 100 crore (up 82% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 12% (up 300 bps YoY)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 48 crore (up 220% YoY)
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 6% (up 400 bps YoY)
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,430 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **Volume (H1 FY27):** 92,809 tonnes (up 7% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 177 crore (up 86% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 12% (up 400 bps YoY)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 82 crore (margin at 6%, up 400 bps YoY)
- **Total Installed Capacity:** 4,35,000 tonnes (as of H1 FY27)
- **Working Capital Days (H1 FY27):** 55 days
- **Receivable Days (H1 FY27):** 34 days
- **Inventory Days (H1 FY27):** 74 days (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Bathware (Aquel) Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 19 crore (EBITDA of minus Rs 4 crore)

### Highlights
- Implementation of Minimum Import Price (MIP) on PVC resin and stabilization of PVC prices provided input cost certainty and improved channel partner sentiment.
- Growth supported by continuous distribution expansion, digitization of the value chain (Distributor Management Systems and Sales Force Automation), and retail billing growth.
- CPVC and PPR revenue contribution expanded to over 30% (traditionally closer to 25%).
- DECILO low-noise polypropylene piping systems installed in residential and hospitality projects across multiple states; manufacturing footprint to expand from Haridwar to Silvassa by end of March quarter.
- Set up a new product innovation and R&D function to launch value-added piping categories over the next few years.
- Sponsoring *Kon Honar Crorepati* (hosted by Madhuri Dixit) to boost brand visibility.
- Working capital efficiency improved via sales quality and stricter credit control, driving DSO reduction.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year volume growth guidance in double digits.
- Management maintains EBITDA margin guidance band of 11% to 13%.
- Full-year capex guidance remains around Rs 200 crore (Rs 70 crore incurred in H1). FY28 capex expected around Rs 100 crore to Rs 120 crore (maintenance and replacement).
- Bathware (Aquel) expected to be close to breakeven in Q3 and definitely break even by Q4 (needing Rs 24-25 crore per quarter).

### Demand & industry commentary
- End-product demand is healthy, supported by long-term structural drivers in housing and infrastructure.
- Smaller unorganized players are struggling with raw material scenarios, leading to market consolidation and organized players gaining share.
- October volume growth continued at par with Q2, aided by low base from last year's festive timing.
- Competitive intensity among large players is healthy and non-predatory due to demand tailwinds and commodity certainty.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee expenses increased due to actuarial valuation impact and an organizational headcount/sales force salary increase of ~10%.
- Gross margins and profitability benefited from product mix upgrades, cost absorption, operating leverage, and decentralization benefits (e.g., Begusarai plant in the East).
- Low-cost inventory was passed on to the channel with a slight lag to drive volume growth, resulting in no major inventory gain for Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Begusarai (Bihar) plant has seen very strong capacity utilization and demand ramp-up ahead of expectations.
- Silvassa facility expansion for DECILO polypropylene systems planned by end of Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivable days reduced significantly to 34 days; inventory stabilized at 74 days.
- Payables function on procurement strategy (shorter domestic payment terms vs. extended import credit terms).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government policy timelines (such as CVD implementation by the Ministry of Finance) are difficult to speculate on.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sneha (Nuvama Wealth):** Asked about the share of restocking vs. actual demand. *Management stated restocking occurred due to MIP/price floors, but underlying end-demand is healthy alongside ongoing market consolidation.*
2. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital):** Inquired about achieving the implied 16-21% H2 volume growth for double-digit annual targets. *Management confirmed October volumes matched Q2 growth trajectory and expressed confidence in sustainable double-digit full-year volume growth.*
3. **Keshav Lahoti (HDFC Securities):** Asked why there were no inventory gains despite rising resin prices. *Management explained that inventory days are tightly controlled at 75 days, and any gains were passed on to the channel to support volume growth.*
4. **Praneet (SJ Investments):** Asked about the structural synergies of the bathware (Aquel) business. *Management noted strong overlap in channel partners, focus on Tier 2/3 retail markets, and expansion into South/East to reach breakeven by Q4.*
5. **Hrishikesh Bhagat (Kotak MF):** Questioned the rationale for passing on low-cost inventory benefits instead of retaining margins. *Management clarified it was a minor, temporary 7-10 day breathing room extended to channel partners given steep cost increases, without altering overall margin bands.*

### One-line takeaway
Prince Pipes delivered robust Q2 FY27 volume growth of 22% YoY and EBITDA growth of 82% YoY, underpinned by value-added product mix expansion, strict working capital discipline, and favorable industry consolidation.
