# Mitsu Chem Plast Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY improvement in profitability and efficient operating performance supported by a better product mix.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained the annual revenue target of Rs. 1,000 crores by FY28.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to emphasize bottom-line sustainability and value-added product focus amid stable overall market demand.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: West Asian geopolitical tensions affecting transport/exports were explicitly questioned by analysts (management noted no major direct impact).
- Last quarter's promises: The Khalapur facility for 1,000-liter IBC containers was stated to be operational by Q3 FY2027 (still on track for Q3).

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 9,580.87 lakhs (Q2 FY27) vs Rs. 9,242.05 lakhs (Q2 FY26), up 3.67% YoY
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs. 19,095.40 lakhs, up 7.46% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs. 1,188.55 lakhs (Q2 FY27), up 101.97% YoY; H1 FY27 EBITDA at Rs. 2,738.03 lakhs, up 151.40% YoY
- EBITDA Margin: 12.41% (Q2 FY27); H1 FY27 EBITDA margin at 14.34%
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs. 610.47 lakhs (Q2 FY27), up 224.12% YoY; H1 FY27 PAT at Rs. 1,484.30 lakhs, up 364.56% YoY
- PAT Margin: 6.37% (Q2 FY27); H1 FY27 PAT margin at 7.77%
- Total borrowings: Rs. 9,648.44 lakhs as of September 2026
- Cash and bank balance: Rs. 6.07 lakhs as of September 2026
- Debt-to-equity ratio: 0.76x in H1 FY27
- Manufacturing capacity: 36,000+ metric tons per annum (58 blow molding, 22 injection molding machines across 4 facilities)
- Revenue mix (Q1 FY26-27): Industrial packaging ~81.88%, hospital furniture parts ~16.27%, infrastructure furniture and others ~1.86%
- Export share: ~1.5% of total growth in H1

### Highlights
- Core industrial packaging is supplemented by emerging segments like Furnastra hospital furniture parts and upcoming IBC containers.
- Rationalizing lower-margin portfolios and prioritizing bottom-line stability over volume growth.
- Fully automated Khalapur facility for 1,000-liter IBC containers expected to be operational by Q3 FY2027.
- Export presence spans 18 countries with approvals received in major markets like USA, Canada, and Europe.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its ambition to achieve Rs. 1,000 crores in annual revenue by FY28.
- Double-digit EBITDA margins viewed as sustainable in the current market scenario.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall market demand described as very good across most serviced industries, despite isolated weakness.
- Long approval cycles (6 months to 1 year) noted for export and healthcare segments.

### Costs, raw materials & margins
- Margins supported by manpower, machinery, and material efficiency improvements, along with product mix optimization.
- Polymers are not officially hedged in India; raw materials are primarily procured locally.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total manufacturing capacity reached 36,000+ metric tons per annum.
- Optimal utilization capacity estimated to reach around Rs. 425 crores in revenue without adding major new capex beyond ongoing IBC investments.
- Khalapur facility for 1,000-liter IBCs slated to start in Q3 FY2027, offering a capital turnover of approximately 1.5 times.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at Rs. 9,648.44 lakhs with cash and bank balance of Rs. 6.07 lakhs.
- Debt-to-equity ratio at 0.76x for H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia (though management stated no direct impact on export orders so far).
- Short-term execution and initial scaling risks associated with the new IBC product rollout.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rushabh (Innovate Financial):** Asked about export contributions and optimal utilization revenue. *Management stated exports take 6-12 months to establish with major markets in USA, Canada, and Europe, and optimal utilization capacity without further capex can reach ~Rs. 425 crores.*
2. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital):** Challenged the feasibility of reaching the Rs. 1,000 crore FY28 target given current capacity limits. *Management replied that the target relies on the upcoming IBC project and a broader product basket, details of which will be announced soon.*
3. **Saket Saurabh (Individual Investor):** Inquired about volume growth and segment performance. *Management explained that flat volume growth reflects a deliberate strategic shift toward rationalizing low-margin SKUs to protect the bottom line.*
4. **Mukesh (Individual Investor):** Asked about margin sustainability and H1 vs H2 revenue distribution. *Management noted that margins are sustainable and typically follow a 40:60 H1-to-H2 revenue ratio.*
5. **Arnav Shah (Individual Investor):** Asked about market size and differentiation for the new IBC product. *Management stated it is a large market with few players in India, and details on margins and differentiation will be shared after launch.*

### One-line takeaway
Mitsu Chem Plast delivered strong profitability growth and stable margins in Q2 FY27 by focusing on high-margin product mixes, while preparing for commercialization of its 1,000-liter IBC project in Q3 to support its long-term growth targets.
