# All Time Plastics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - geopolitical crisis in West Asia drove unprecedented polymer price inflation and supply/logistics disruptions, though underlying demand remains strong.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 161 crore, up 10.5% QoQ and 2% YoY.
- **Volume of Polymers Processed:** 6,323 metric tonnes (up 25% QoQ from 5,056 metric tonnes; 7,399 metric tonnes in Q1 FY26).
- **Sales Volume:** 6,090 metric tonnes (vs 5,831 metric tonnes in Q4 FY26).
- **Capacity Utilization:** ~65% (64.9%) on a 41,000 metric tonne capacity, up from 51.9% in Q4 FY26 (52% on 39,000 capacity).
- **Gross Margin:** 39.5%, down 240 bps QoQ from 41.9% (flat YoY vs 39.3% in Q1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** INR 23 crore; EBITDA Margin: 14.3% (vs INR 22 crore / 14.8% in Q4 FY26; INR 29 crore / 18.2% in Q1 FY26).
- **PAT:** INR 12 crore, up 28.8% QoQ from INR 9 crore; down 5.5% YoY from INR 13 crore. PAT Margin: 7.5%.
- **Net Working Capital Cycle:** 60 days (vs 57 days at close of FY26).
- **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.14x.
- **Geographic Revenue Mix:** Europe 52%, USA 19%, UK 11%, India 16%.

### Highlights
- Raw material prices spiked 40% to 50% at peak levels due to the West Asia geopolitical crisis, accompanied by port congestion, extended transit times, and container shortages.
- Spilled-over sales from Q1 due to logistics delays amounted to ~INR 5.5 crore, which will reflect in July.
- Employee expenses increased due to the operationalization of the Khatalwada plant facility.
- Bamboo subsidiary (All-Time Bamboo Private Limited) set up a 75,000 sq. ft. facility in Guwahati; machinery shipped from China, expected in mid-August with commercial operations slated for Q4 FY27. Phase 1 capacity is 3,000 cubic meters per annum (capex INR 15 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets ~75% capacity utilization for FY27 on a 41,000 metric tonne capacity (dependent on external geopolitical environment).
- Expects 15% to 20% revenue growth in FY27 assuming normalization.
- Domestic business growth target maintained at 30% to 35%.
- Sustainable EBITDA margins of 18% to 19% expected once capacity utilization reaches ~80%.
- Bamboo business Phase 1 expected to operate at ~75% utilization in Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Order book remains strong and customer forecasts have held firm with long product life cycles and repeated SKU refreshes.
- Unorganized sector facing working capital stress due to high raw material costs, presenting market entry opportunities for the company.
- US market momentum is growing (19% of revenue vs 12% in FY26 as a whole) with new project wins and inquiries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material cost compression absorbed: 240 bps QoQ decline in gross margins due to time-lagged pass-through.
- Domestic prices passed on 100%; export pass-through secured with the substantial majority of customers via rollover mechanisms, with full impact visible in Q2 FY27.
- Inventory days moved to 44 (from 42) due to higher raw material cover; receivable days moved to 50 (from 48). Payable days stable at 34.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Installed capacity is ~41,000 metric tonnes.
- Ordered 14 new injection moulding machines adding ~1,500 metric tonnes of capacity, expected to come on stream in Q4 FY27. Remaining 4,000 metric tonnes of the planned 6,000 metric tonne annual expansion will be ordered based on specific project demand.
- Split-processing model for bamboo: upstream boards in Guwahati, value-added carpentry and finishing in Gujarat (Khatalwada facility).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity stands robust at 0.14x.
- Net working capital extended slightly by 3 days to 60 days due to operating environment disruptions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued West Asia conflict causing volatility in raw material pricing, shipment availability, and transit timelines.
- Power disruption shutdowns during heavy rains in July (1.5 days at Khatalwada, 3 days at Daman).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Akshay Chheda (Canara) asked about spilled-over sales:** Management quantified delayed sales at ~INR 5.5 crore, to be realized in July.
- **Nirali (Unnamed firm) asked about volume vs. pricing growth:** Management explained sales volume grew ~5% QoQ (6,090 tonnes vs 5,813 tonnes in Q4) while raw material spikes created a pricing lag to be recovered in Q2.
- **Anant Mundra (Mytemple Capital) asked about the anchor customer's store expansion:** Management stated the largest customer announced 20–25 store openings in India, contributing an estimated INR 40–50 lakh per store per month.
- **Rajesh (Unnamed investor) asked about cannibalization from bamboo products:** Management clarified there is no cannibalization as target price points and end consumers differ.
- **Dev Mehta (Unique PMS) asked about peak margin timelines:** Management reiterated that 18%–19% EBITDA margins are sustainable upon reaching ~80% capacity utilization.

### One-line takeaway
Despite a 240 bps gross margin compression and logistics disruptions from the West Asia crisis, All Time Plastics delivered sequential growth in revenue, volume, and utilization, anticipating margin recovery and 15%-20% growth in FY27 as price hikes fully take effect.
