# Atharva Poly-Plast Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth, robust balance sheet post-IPO, and expanding engineering capabilities.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 36.69 crore (up 77.5% YoY from Rs 20.67 crore; up 8.8% HoH from H2 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 7.45 crore (up from Rs 3.88 crore in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin: 20.3% in H1 FY27 (compared to 20.7% for full-year FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 4.38 crore (up from Rs 1.75 crore in H1 FY26)
- Net Worth: Rs 46.06 crore as of September 2026 (increased from Rs 19.58 crore in March 2026 post-IPO)
- Total Borrowings Reduction: Reduced by 53%, bringing debt-to-equity ratio to 0.1x
- Capacity Utilization: Approximately 67%
- Revenue Mix (H1 FY27): Premium office furniture (53.4%), Home appliances (26.9%), Engineering solutions and other applications (19.7%)

### Highlights
- Completed BSE SME platform listing on July 26th.
- Evolved from a contract manufacturer into a design and development-led manufacturing partner.
- Recognized as a Steelcase premium supplier for Asia and MillerKnoll gold supplier in 2025.
- Secured a high-engineering micro-engineering drip irrigation project with a US-based MNC (Toro); completed pilot phase and mold approval, moving to production phase.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 results to be stronger driven by stronger premium furniture revenue, engineering services business, and deferred raw material price adjustments.
- Management plans to target 25% capacity growth and add new machines in FY28-FY29 as utilization reaches 88%.
- Engineering category revenue contribution targeted at 22% to 25% for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global OEMs are increasingly evaluating India as a manufacturing and sourcing base, driving localization projects.
- Premium office furniture customers include Herman Miller, Steelcase, Haworth, and Kokuyo.
- Home appliance resin customers include Godrej and Vasantha.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material input costs constituted approximately 75% of revenue in H1 FY27.
- A 20% increase in crude prices resulted in a 22% polymer price rise, temporarily impacting H1 margins due to a 1-month lag in customer price adjustments; recovery of Rs 1.45 crore was billed in October.
- Engineering services (in-house design, DFM, mold flow analysis) command high EBITDA margins (50% to 65%+), whereas outsourced tooling mold development operates at lower margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operates on a 5.5-acre land parcel with ~40,000 sq. ft. built-up area and 20 molding machines (100 to 1,000 tons clamping tonnage).
- Target operational efficiency/peak capacity utilization is capped at 88% due to changeovers and product mix.
- Adding a molding machine takes 45 to 60 days max; generic machines (350-ton/450-ton) are readily available.
- For the new Toro irrigation project, the customer will invest in molds and assembly machines, while Atharva will invest in plastic molding machines, testing equipment, and clean manufacturing setup.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth rose to Rs 46.06 crore from Rs 19.58 crore in March 2026.
- Total borrowings reduced by 53%, leading to a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.1x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in crude oil markets leading to fluctuations in polymer pricing, mitigated by a 1-month lag in customer price pass-through mechanisms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jiten Parmar (Aurum Capital) on margin lag recovery:** Asked if invoiced material differentials were still pending. Management clarified that the ~Rs 1.45 crore price adjustment for September polymer spikes was successfully billed in October.
- **Jiten Parmar on peak capacity utilization:** Asked about maximum limits. Management stated 88% is the target operational efficiency due to changeovers.
- **Vinod on capacity expansion timeline vs. growth outlook:** Inquired about scaling up capacities given strong customer inflows. Management clarified that FY28-FY29 refers to adding individual machines as utilization nears 88%, which takes 45-60 days.
- **Vinod on H2 margin volatility historical trend:** Asked why H2 margins dropped previously. Management explained it was due to a higher revenue mix from lower-margin home appliances and tooling versus high-margin in-house engineering services in H1.
- **Rajat on engineering target revenue contribution:** Asked about engineering segment targets. Management stated 22% to 25% of revenue is expected from the engineering category this year with higher margins.

### One-line takeaway
Atharva Polyplast delivered robust H1 FY27 revenue growth of 77.5% YoY and strengthened its balance sheet post-IPO, driven by high-value engineering capabilities and marquee global clientele in premium furniture and appliances.
