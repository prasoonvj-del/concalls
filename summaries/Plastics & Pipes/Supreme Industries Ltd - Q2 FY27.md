# Supreme Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-01 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume growth recovery in Q2 and October alongside market share gains, but guidance for plastic piping volume growth was trimmed from earlier levels due to Q1 weakness and high raw material prices impacted packaging margins.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 6,079 crore (+22% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Sales Volume:** 3,41,991 MT (+1% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Operating Profit (EBITDA):** Rs. 829 crore (+34% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Standalone PAT:** Rs. 487 crore (+31% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 Segment Performance (YoY):**
  - **Plastic Piping Systems:** Volume +24%, Value +42%.
  - **Packaging Products:** Volume +3%, Value +22%.
  - **Industrial Products:** Volume +13%, Value +49%.
  - **Consumer Products:** Volume -14%, Value +2%.
- **Q2 FY27 Value-Added Products Sales:** Rs. 1,476 crore (+38% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 cPVC Volume Growth:** 38.5% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Employee Costs:** Rs. 330 crore.

### Highlights
- Operating environment stabilized in Q2 after unusual polymer price volatility in Q1.
- Value-added products contributed Rs. 1,476 crore to Q2 turnover.
- Plastic Piping volume growth rebounded to 24% YoY in Q2 after a 13.5% YoY decline in Q1.
- First 25 days of October 2026 delivered 23% YoY volume growth in plastic piping systems.
- Minimum Import Price (MIP) on suspension-grade PVC resin and removal of customs duty exemptions helped reduce price volatility.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Overall Volume Growth:** Expected at ~10% YoY (management target).
- **FY27 Revenue Target:** Expected between Rs. 13,500 crore and Rs. 14,000 crore.
- **FY27 Operating Margin:** Expected to be between 14.0% and 14.5%.
- **FY27 Plastic Piping Volume Growth:** Revised/guided to 13% YoY (requiring ~22% YoY H2 growth).
- Management expressed strong confidence in meeting the revised 13% piping growth target, ruling out further downward revisions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall PVC industry saw an estimated 10% YoY volume de-growth in H1 FY27 per raw material suppliers; Supreme gained market share over unorganized peers.
- Housing sector remains the primary driver of plastic piping demand.
- Nal Se Jal project business remains subdued due to state government funding constraints and unpaid contractor bills from previous years.
- Packaging business faced demand resistance due to steep increases in crude-linked raw material (polythene) prices and Middle East supply disruptions.
- Consumer/Furniture business declined in volume due to high polypropylene prices, localized raw material shortages, and competition from reprocessed material chairs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Company defers passing on PVC price hikes to distributors by 10–15 days to assist channel partners in adjusting balance sheets.
- CPVC price increases of 3%–7% across products announced effective late October 2026.
- Raw material availability maintained via strategic imports despite ocean shipping uncertainties.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **FY27 Capex Guidance:** Outflow estimated between Rs. 700 crore and Rs. 750 crore.
- Ongoing expansion projects progressing as planned across new units at Bihar, Jammu, Erode, Puducherry, Malanpur, and various brownfield sites.
- Expansion funded entirely via internal accruals and cash flows.
- Gas pipe fittings now manufacturable and shippable from five regional locations across India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Financial discipline maintained; expanding operations while remaining debt-free/cash-surplus via internal accruals.
- Channel inventory levels reported to be operating at minimum cautious levels, with Supreme providing 48-hour order fulfillment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued geopolitical uncertainty in West Asia impacting crude and raw material pricing.
- High raw material prices impacting margin pass-through capabilities in packaging and consumer products.
- Persistent delays in state funding matching central grants under Nal Se Jal scheme.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital):** Can Supreme achieve 13% piping volume growth in FY27 given it requires ~22% growth in H2?  
  *Management Answer:* Confirmed confidence in the 13% target, citing 23% volume growth in the first 25 days of October, strong capacity availability, and adequate raw material inventory.
- **Keshav (HDFC Securities):** What caused the YoY margin pressure in Packaging and Q2 overall margin profile?  
  *Management Answer:* High crude-linked polythene input costs could not be immediately passed on to packaging customers. Additionally, price hikes are intentionally passed on to piping distributors with a 10-12 day lag.
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec):** Are Nal Se Jal orders providing demand tailwinds?  
  *Management Answer:* No. States are failing to provide matching funds to claim central funds, and past bills remain unpaid to contractors, limiting fresh inquiries.
- **Tejas (Citigroup):** What is driving higher realization per kg in plastic piping?  
  *Management Answer:* It is a mix effect due to higher sales of premium products such as PP silent pipes (polypropylene), PERT piping systems, and cPVC.
- **Sushant Soni (HDFC/Peer):** What was the H1 industry PVC growth rate?  
  *Management Answer:* Raw material suppliers indicate a ~10% YoY de-growth in H1 industry volumes, implying Supreme gained market share.

### One-line takeaway
Supreme Industries recovered strongly in Q2 with 24% piping volume growth and maintained a robust full-year margin target of 14%–14.5% despite macro headwinds and elevated raw material costs.
