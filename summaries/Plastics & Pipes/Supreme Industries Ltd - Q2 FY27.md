# Supreme Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-01 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in volume growth targets for FY27, supported by a strong 23% volume growth in plastic piping during the first 25 days of October.

### Key numbers
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 6,079 crore (vs Rs 5,003 crore YoY).
*   **H1 Volume:** 341,991 MT (vs 338,224 MT YoY).
*   **H1 Standalone Operating Profit:** Rs 829 crore (vs Rs 617 crore YoY).
*   **H1 Standalone PAT:** Rs 487 crore (vs Rs 371 crore YoY).
*   **Q2 Value-added products turnover:** Rs 1,476 crore (vs Rs 1,072 crore YoY).
*   **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Rs 13,500 crore – Rs 14,000 crore.
*   **FY27 Operating Margin Guidance:** 14% – 14.5%.

### Highlights
*   **Segment Performance (Q2 Volume/Value Growth YoY):**
    *   Plastic Piping: +24% / +42%.
    *   Packaging: +3% / +22%.
    *   Industrial Products: +13% / +49%.
    *   Consumer Products: -14% / +2%.
*   **October Trends:** Plastic piping volume grew 23% in the first 25 days of October.
*   **cPVC Performance:** H1 volume growth of 38.5%.
*   **Value-added products:** Achieved 38% growth in Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Management expects** company-wide volume growth of ~10% for FY27.
*   **Management expects** plastic piping volume growth of 13% for FY27.
*   **Management expects** to maintain operating margins between 14% and 14.5% for the full year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Piping:** Growth primarily driven by housing.
*   **Nal Se Jal:** Management is not optimistic about significant volume contributions this year, citing that state governments are failing to match central funds, leading to payment delays and contractor inactivity.
*   **Industry:** Raw material producers reported a 10% de-growth in the industry during H1.
*   **Consumer Products:** Segment remains under pressure due to high polymer prices and raw material availability issues (diversion of polypropylene to LPG production).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Management implements price hikes with a 10–15 day lag to assist distributors in balance sheet management.
*   **Packaging Margins:** Contraction attributed to steep polyethylene price increases and supply constraints from the Middle East.
*   **Inventory:** Management anticipates inventory gains for the full year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Guidance:** Cash outflow of Rs 700 crore – Rs 750 crore for FY27.
*   **Expansion:** Work on new units in Bihar, Jammu, Erode, Puducherry, and Malanpur is progressing as planned.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management intends to fund investments through healthy cash flows and internal accruals while maintaining financial discipline.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical uncertainty in West Asia.
*   Polymer price volatility.
*   Payment delays in government-linked projects (Nal Se Jal).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital):** Questioned the 13% piping volume guidance given H1 performance. Management stated they do not expect to revise guidance lower, citing strong October growth (23%) and comfortable raw material availability.
*   **Keshav (HDFC Securities):** Asked about packaging margin contraction. Management explained that steep polyethylene price increases were difficult to pass on immediately to customers.
*   **Sneha (Nuvama):** Asked about margin misses. Management reiterated the 14-14.5% margin guidance and explained that price hikes are delayed by 10-15 days to support distributor balance sheets.
*   **Ritesh Shah (Investec):** Asked about Nal Se Jal. Management clarified they are not receiving inquiries and do not expect significant business due to state funding issues.
*   **Rahul Agarwal:** Asked about consumer segment de-growth. Management cited high prices and raw material diversion to LPG production as the primary causes.

### One-line takeaway
Strong Q2 piping volume growth (24%) and a robust October start (23%) support management's 10% volume growth guidance for FY27, despite H1 headwinds and margin pressure in the packaging segment.
