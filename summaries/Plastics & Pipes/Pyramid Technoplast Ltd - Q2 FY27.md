# Pyramid Technoplast Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a quarter of strong volume recovery, 21% YoY volume growth, higher realizations, and expanding green energy and recycling benefits.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained ~15% revenue growth and 11–12% EBITDA margin (with subsidy).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from export headwinds and Middle East disruptions last quarter to robust domestic volume recovery and pan-India long-route demand.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New focus on long-distance transportation costs and shifting from imports to 80% local procurement due to the Hormuz issue; EPR portal closure issues from last quarter disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises: Solar and recycling capacity expansions continued scaling as promised, with H1 solar savings reaching Rs 3.89 crore.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 238 crore, up 48% YoY. Total income: Rs 238.8 crore.
- **Volume:** 14,798 metric tonnes, up 21% YoY and 14% QoQ. Installed capacity: 20,993 metric tonnes.
- **Capacity Utilization:** ~70% (vs 62% in Q1 FY27 and Q2 FY26). H1 volume: 27,789 metric tonnes at ~66% utilization.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 20.3 crore, up 61% YoY; EBITDA margin at 8.5% (vs 7.8% in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA per ton:** Rs 13,718, up 33% YoY from Rs 10,326 in Q2 FY26.
- **PAT:** Rs 9.5 crore, up 54% YoY; PAT margin at 4%.
- **Average Realization:** ~Rs 1.61 lakh per ton, up 22% YoY.
- **H1 Performance:** Revenue at Rs 460.5 crore (+42% YoY), EBITDA at Rs 41.6 crore (+55% YoY, margin 9%), PAT at Rs 20 crore (+42% YoY).
- **Solar Savings:** Rs 3.89 crore saved in H1 FY27.
- **Recycling Plant:** Generated Rs 33 lakh EBITDA in Q2 FY27 (estimated FY27 EBITDA contribution at ~Rs 2 crore).

### Highlights
- Volume growth achieved across all three product categories: polymer drums tonnage up 5% YoY (16% QoQ), IBC units up 17% YoY, and MS drums tonnage up 73% YoY. Value-added IBCs contributed 32% of revenue.
- Raw material sourcing shifted to 80% local due to the Hormuz issue, halting imports.
- Paint industry machine started commercial production in October, generating ~Rs 70 lakh revenue in its first month.
- Government subsidy approvals received for Wada (Rs 24.9 crore over 10 years) and Bharuch (Rs 10.5 crore), totaling ~Rs 35 crore. Wada sanction expected by March 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 revenue growth objective at ~15% and EBITDA margin target at 11–12% (9.5% core business + 1.5% subsidy).
- FY27 Capex planned at Rs 20 to 25 crore, primarily for Kutch expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic chemical industry volume and overall industry volumes are increasing.
- Company is actively approaching pan-India long-route customers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross profit rose 28% YoY to Rs 55.3 crore; gross margin remained steady at 23% as raw material costs rose alongside selling prices.
- Operating expenses increased due to higher long-distance transportation costs and normalization of employee strength/holidays.
- Full 14.25 MW solar capacity expected to deliver ~Rs 15 crore annual savings with a 4-year payback.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Kutch expansion progressing: Investing Rs 20–25 crore for 10,000 IBCs/month capacity, expected to be commissioned by March 2027.
- Wada facility operating in full swing, with overall utilization expected to reach ~80% in FY27; expansion planned post-March (taking 6 months).
- Final 1 MW solar installation at Bharuch pre-charged, expected to start benefits next month.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debtors and working capital increased due to higher sales volumes and local purchase conversion. Creditors stood at Rs 23 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Hormuz issue disrupting imports, requiring reliance on local procurement.
- Higher transportation costs resulting from long-distance dispatches to southern markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kumar Saurabh on margin vs EBITDA/ton:** Management advised focusing on absolute EBITDA per ton rather than percentage margins due to raw material pass-through effects.
- **Sushant Soni on other expenses:** Management clarified that the rise in other expenses per kg was driven by higher long-distance transportation costs and normalization of employee strength.
- **Alok Trivedi on gross margins and price drops:** Management stated that polymer price fluctuations are passed through by buying and selling at lower rates.
- **Saket Kapoor on cash flow and tax details:** Management agreed to check and provide specific cash flow reconciliation and advance tax details (noting Rs 3.75 crore paid in H1) offline.
- **Preeti Agarwal on conservative guidance:** Management acknowledged that guidance reflects a conservative stance despite strong H1 performance.

### One-line takeaway
Pyramid Technoplast reported strong Q2 FY27 performance with 48% YoY revenue growth and 21% volume growth, driven by solid volume recovery, rising realizations, and expanding green energy and recycling benefits.
