# Jain Resource Recycling Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 58% YoY and EBITDA/PAT improved sequentially, supported by robust copper volume growth and improved working capital efficiency.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained annual revenue growth expectation at ~30% for FY27 (same as previous expectations).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to more positive regarding operations as copper anode facilities started commercial sales, Ahmedabad subsidiary commenced operations, and working capital days improved significantly.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Mention of the 57th GST Council RCM on electrical/electronic waste scrap (management stated it won't help much in sourcing unless GST rates are reduced). Kuwait project shipment delay factors specified (specialized container availability).
- Last quarter's promises: Copper Cathode Phase-1 commissioning was targeted for Q2 FY27 and is happening as of the call date; copper wire rod and busbar facilities remain targeted for Q3 FY27 commissioning.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs. 3,337 crore (up 58% YoY, up 22% QoQ)
- Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs. 6,061.4 crore (up 55% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs. 138 crore (up 27% QoQ from Rs. 109 crore in Q1 FY27)
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs. 248 crore (vs Rs. 250 crore in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 4.2% (vs 4.0% in Q1 FY27)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin: 4.1% (vs 6.8% in H1 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27): Rs. 90 crore (up 30% QoQ from Rs. 69 crore in Q1 FY27)
- PAT Margin (Q2 FY27): 2.7% (vs 2.5% in Q1 FY27)
- Copper & Copper Ingot Volumes (Q2 FY27): 16,411 metric tonnes (up 73% YoY)
- Copper Segment EBITDA per tonne (Q2 FY27): Rs. 37,237 (vs Rs. 31,825 in Q1 FY27)
- Value-Added Copper Sales Volume (H1 FY27): 3,575 metric tonnes
- Lead & Lead Alloys Ingot Volumes (Q2 FY27): 34,851 metric tonnes (vs 32,150 metric tonnes in Q1 FY27)
- Lead EBITDA per tonne (Q2 FY27): Rs. 19,310 (vs Rs. 18,660 in Q1 FY27)
- Aluminum & Aluminum Alloys Volumes (Q2 FY27): 2,493 metric tonnes (vs 2,201 metric tonnes in Q1 FY27)
- Aluminum Segment EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs. 5.3 crore (vs Rs. 4.7 crore in Q1 FY27)
- Working Capital Days: Reduced to 49 days (from 66 days at the end of FY26)
- Net Cash Inflow from Operating Activities (H1 FY27): ~Rs. 129-130 crore
- Net Debt to Equity: Improved to 0.5x (from 0.7x as of March 2026)
- Jain CY Ahmedabad Subsidiary Revenue (Q2 FY27): ~Rs. 25.4 crore

### Highlights
- Copper anode plant (both phases of 800 metric tonnes/month each, total 1,600 metric tonnes/month installed capacity) commissioned; Phase II is in trial runs.
- Jain CY Circular Solutions (Ahmedabad subsidiary) commenced commercial operations (71,000 metric tonnes annual scrap input capacity, 25,000 metric tonnes copper products capacity).
- Copper and copper business contributed 8% of total revenue.
- Note on Record: The furnace accident at Unit-2 from Q1 FY27 was not discussed this quarter, indicating operations have normalized post-regulatory approvals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue growth of around 30% for FY27, subject to market conditions and execution.
- Management expects absolute EBITDA to grow driven by volume expansion, while EBITDA margins may moderate due to evolving product mix.
- Copper cathode Phase I commissioning at advanced stage (targeted immediately); Phase II targeted for Q4 FY27.
- Copper wire rod facility (600 metric tonnes/month) and Busbar & Profiles facility (1,500 metric tonnes/month) targeted for Q3 FY27 commissioning.
- Antimony facility (100 metric tonnes/month) and dedicated plastic recycling facility (~Rs. 15 crore capex) targeted for Q3 FY27 commissioning.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Recycled metal demand remains robust as customers place greater importance on recycled materials and source efficiency.
- War and geopolitical tensions in West Asia generate new scrap and recyclable materials, creating opportunities for recycling industries despite logistics challenges.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Domestic sourcing increased to approximately 38%–39% of overall purchases, helping reduce working capital blockage and advance payment requirements compared to imports.
- High shipping freights and West Asia logistics disruptions continue to pose challenges, offset by local sourcing and exploring new overseas regions.
- Jain Green Technologies copper anode standalone EBITDA per tonne reported at Rs. 14,434 in the initial phase, expected to improve with scale.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital outlay for the entire value-added copper project (including cathode, anode, wire rod, busbar) is approximately Rs. 120 crore for Phase I.
- Kuwait project machinery is fully manufactured; shipment delayed due to lack of specialized containers caused by West Asia conflict, now expected to ship within 30 days.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital days improved to 49 days from 66 days at end-FY26 due to internal cash generation and higher local sourcing (>50%-60% local sourcing mentioned for certain segments / ~38-39% overall).
- Net debt-to-equity improved to 0.5x from 0.7x as of March 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situation and shipping disruptions in West Asia impacting logistics, freight costs, and equipment shipments (Kuwait).
- Sourcing challenges in lead imports managed through local sourcing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shubham (3A Financial Services)** asked about capex for the copper cathode plant. *Management clarified that the Rs. 120 crore capex covers the entire value-added copper portfolio for Phase I, with Phase II requiring marginal expense (approx. 25%-30% of Phase I).*
- **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities)** asked about working capital improvement and RCM impact. *Management explained that increased local sourcing (50%-60%) reduced capital blockage since local terms require payment post-arrival versus import advances; RCM is a positive but won't heavily impact sourcing unless government reduces scrap GST.*
- **Dheeraj Ram (360 ONE Capital)** asked about domestic sourcing percentages and sustainability. *Management stated overall domestic sourcing is 38%-39% across metals and expressed confidence in having raw material secured for the next four months through March.*
- **Vinit Mehta (Ashika Group)** asked about processing loss and working capital cycles. *Management stated normal melting losses apply, 70% of cathode is consumed internally for wire rods/busbars, and working capital cycles will fluctuate within a 50-70 day range.*
- **Jigar Jani (Individual Investor)** asked about EBITDA per tonne across stages. *Management clarified that Jain Green Technologies' initial EBITDA per tonne is Rs. 14,434, regular copper ingot recycling yields Rs. 37,000-39,000/tonne, and downstream integration can potentially add 2%-3% to bottom line margins over time.*
- **Aditya Bhandari (Hedge Investment Management)** asked how JRRL differentiates from peers like Gravita and POCL. *Management stated JRRL is a diversified multi-metal recycling company where copper contributes >65% of turnover (and lead ~30%), aiming to build a broader multi-metal platform alongside healthy industry competition.*

### One-line takeaway
Jain Resource Recycling delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with a 58% YoY revenue jump and improved working capital efficiency, supported by commercial traction in copper anode and multi-metal capacity expansions.
