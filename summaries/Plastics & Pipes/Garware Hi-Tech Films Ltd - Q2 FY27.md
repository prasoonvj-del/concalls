# Garware Hi-Tech Films Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue of INR696 crores, up 22% YoY, with EBITDA and PAT both growing 42% YoY, supported by favorable product mix and operating leverage.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR696 crores (up 22% YoY, 10% QoQ); H1 FY27 Revenue: INR1,329 crores (up 25% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** INR189 crores (up 42% YoY); EBITDA margin: 27.2% (expanding 377 bps YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR381 crores (up 49% YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR129 crores (up 42% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT: INR262 crores (up 50% YoY).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** H1 FY27 PBT: INR350 crores (up 51% YoY); PBT margin: 26.30% (expanding 464 bps YoY).
- **Cash Profit:** H1 FY27 Cash Profit: INR293 crores (up 46% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue Mix:** CPD (Consumer Packaging/Products Division) 73%, IPD (Industrial Products Division) 27%; Value-added products accounted for 86%.
- **Q2 Segment Contribution (Revenue):** Sun Control Films (SCF) 51%, Paint Protection Film (PPF) 24%, IPD 25%.
- **Q2 Geographic Mix (Revenue):** USA 51%, Europe 15%, India 20%, Middle East and others 12% (Exports overall: 80%, Domestic: 20%).
- **Capacity Utilisation (Q2):** Sun Control Films: 75%, PPF: 73%.
- **Balance Sheet (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Cash of INR933 crores; Working capital: 29 days.
- **Inventory/Costs:** Raw material inventory days at ~58-60 days. Raw material costs increased by 4% in Q2; logistics costs increased by INR9 crores absolute (total headwind of INR37 crores).
- **Tariff Reversals:** Received INR31 crores to date (INR20 crores refunded to customers, INR11 crores in books); expected Q3 refund of INR30 crores (~INR16 crores to books, INR14 crores to customers).

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue and record H1 performance.
- Advanced switchable privacy film capabilities through the PDLC facility; introduced Ultra Series window film kits and digital e-warranty services with Pine Labs in car care.
- International network expanded with 2 new global application studios in the US (total 14) and 4 new global distributors added in Q2.
- Garware Home Solutions direct customer reach studios increased from 9 to 14, targeting 50 studios by December 2027. Added 3 channel partners in the architectural and graphic segment.
- Digital engagement grew: Instagram following up 25% YoY, Facebook up 10%, global LinkedIn up 400%, monthly website traffic at 1.1 lakh visitors, and annualized digital footprint at 18 crores.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management upgraded FY27 EBITDA margin guidance by 200 bps to 27% plus-minus 2% while reaffirming revenue target of >INR250 crores (transcript states >INR2,500 crores).
- Management expects a 1.5% to 2% margin expansion in FY28 from TPU backward integration, targeting 28% plus margins.
- Peak revenue potential from existing capacities combined with upcoming TPU and SCF capex is estimated at ~INR3,500 crores at full utilization.
- Home solutions D2C revenue targets: INR50 crores (year 1), INR100 crores, and INR200 crores over 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PPF penetration in India has grown from 0.1% in 2020 to ~3% currently, with significant headroom toward 10% (and 20% in USA/China).
- Architectural window film business runs at ~INR400 crores annualized (representing ~30% of window film sales), with management targeting INR1,000 crores and expanding share up to 50% of window film sales.
- Global pricing for Garware's architectural products ranges from 85% to 120% of American/European global peers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 EBITDA margin of 30.3% represents a sustainable seasonal peak level due to summer demand; Q2 EBITDA margin was 27.2%.
- Raw material cost correlation to crude is low at 10-12% for sun control and 5% to 7% for PPF; IPD business directly passes on crude price fluctuations.
- TPU external sales gross margins are targeted at ~35%. Segment EBITDA margins: top sun control products up to 40-45%, medium sun control 30-35%, PPF 20-25%, D2C adds 10-15% on top of high architectural margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) film line remains on track for commissioning in Q3 FY27; 75% for captive PPF and 25% as merchant sales (targeting INR75 crores to INR100 crores in FY28).
- INR191 crores solar control film capacity expansion (adding ~1,200 lakh square feet of fungible capacity) is progressing with commercial production expected in H1 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stood at INR933 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- Working capital increased slightly to 29 days (from 28 days in FY26). Raw material inventory was intentionally increased to 58-60 days to buffer against geopolitical and supply chain shocks.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical headwinds and a slowdown in the Middle East impacted logistics and raw material costs (total headwind of INR37 crores in Q2, including INR9 crores higher absolute logistics costs and 4% higher raw material costs).
- Received a INR25 lakh MPCB penalty for consent validation related to trials on an old boiler during modifications, which management accepted.
- Customer concentration exists, though mitigated by long-term agreements and dedicated business verticals.

### One-line takeaway
Garware Hi-Tech Films delivered a record Q2 FY27 with 22% revenue growth and a 377 bps YoY expansion in EBITDA margin to 27.2%, driven by strong premium product mix, even amidst INR37 crores of cost headwinds.
