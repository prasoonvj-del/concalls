# Apollo Pipes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Gradual improvement in market conditions with moderating polymer price volatility, driving 13% YoY volume growth and EBITDA recovery.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management lowered the long-term revenue goal to Rs. 4,000 crores by 2030 (comprising Rs. 3,000 crores from plumbing and Rs. 1,000 crores from ceramics), compared to the previous target of Rs. 5,000 crores by FY31. Capacity expansion target set to 2,88,000 tonnes in the next 2 years from 2,40,000 tonnes.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from cautious to positive, expecting H2 to be better than H1 with monsoons ending and PVC prices stabilizing.
- **New topics or risks:** Entered the ceramic tiles business through Mazzini Tiles (acquiring 76% stake) as a building material extension; announced plans to raise Rs. 200 crores via equity for the ceramic expansion. Shut down smaller unviable units in Ahmedabad, Raipur, and Bangalore.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Varanasi plant is ramping up; Kisan breakeven target set for the next 6-9 months (delayed from earlier recovery expectations due to continued industry disruption).

### Key numbers
- **Sales volume:** 24,501 tonnes, up 13% YoY
- **Revenue:** Rs. 309.6 crores, up 31% YoY
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 11.5 crores (recovered from Rs. 3 crores in Q1)
- **Consolidated EBITDA margin:** 3.7%
- **Apollo Standalone EBITDA margin (excl. Kisan):** 5.7% (or Rs. 7,000 per tonne)
- **Kisan EBITDA loss:** Rs. 3,700 per tonne loss
- **CPVC volume growth:** Double-digit YoY (representing 15% to 20% of sales)
- **UPVC sales contribution:** 45% of sales
- **PPR sales contribution:** Less than 5% of sales
- **Capacity expansion target:** 2,88,000 tonnes in the next 2 years from 2,40,000 tonnes
- **Ceramic business targets (by 2030):** Rs. 1,000 crores revenue, Rs. 225 crores EBITDA, utilizing 4-5 assets with an investment of Rs. 400 crores to Rs. 500 crores

### Highlights
- Entered the ceramic tiles market via Mazzini Tiles in Morbi, acquiring a 76% stake to shorten the learning curve and supply organized brands via contract manufacturing.
- Shut down smaller, dragging units in Ahmedabad, Raipur, and a small Bangalore unit to consolidate capacity.
- Varanasi plant is ramping up, reducing initial front-loaded costs.
- Management highlighted plans to sell 2-3 land parcels in the next 6-12 months to raise almost Rs. 130 crores to fund South India capex.
- *Note on Record:* Management confirmed the capacity expansion to 2,88,000 MTPA and the M&A entry into Mazzini Tiles as noted in the recent investor deck/record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be better than H1 as monsoon impacts end and PVC prices stabilize.
- Apollo standalone EBITDA per tonne targeted to reach Rs. 8,000 to Rs. 10,000 in H2 FY27.
- Kisan target is to reach breakeven (0 to plus Rs. 1,000 per tonne EBITDA) in the next 6 to 9 months, and Rs. 4,000 to Rs. 7,000 / Rs. 8,000 per tonne positive EBITDA in FY28/2-3 years.
- Q4 FY27 volumes expected to be 10% higher than Q3 and Q2 run rates, targeting ~30,000 tonnes for Q4, taking full-year volume to around 1,05,000 tonnes.
- Long-term (2030) consolidated guidance: Rs. 3,000 crores revenue from the plumbing business and Rs. 1,000 crores from the ceramic business, totaling Rs. 4,000 crores.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Market conditions improved gradually in Q2 as polymer price volatility moderated compared to Q1.
- Government measures, including minimum import prices on suspension grade PVC resin and withdrawal of customs duty exemptions, supported the domestic market.
- Channel inventory was soft, but recent price hikes induced some stocking up.
- Government infrastructure segment (HDPE/O-PVC) remains muted due to a lack of activity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia continued to influence raw material prices.
- Window profile business is currently in the scaling-up phase, adding extra fixed costs that will normalize as volumes grow.
- Kisan continues to experience profitability pressures due to high fixed costs and industry-wide aggressive pricing competition.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capacity stands at 2,40,000 tonnes, targeted to reach 2,88,000 tonnes over the next 2 years, funded entirely from internal cash flows.
- Green field plant planned in South India with a minimum capacity of 50,000 tonnes before 2030; land acquisition process underway.
- Ceramic business funded via a planned Rs. 200 crore equity raise (of which Rs. 200 crores remains to be deployed alongside internal cash flows) targeting 4-5 total asset acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Planned sale of smaller plant land parcels expected to generate almost Rs. 130 crores to fund South India capex.
- Expansion plans intended to be funded from internal cash flows without leveraging the balance sheet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia affecting raw material prices.
- Intense pricing competition and aggressive pricing in the industry due to idle capacity spilling over from halted government infrastructure projects.
- Slow turnaround of the Kisan acquisition in Maharashtra due to adverse industry dynamics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rishab (Systematix) on the rationale behind entering the tile business:** Anubhav Gupta explained it is a building material extension sharing the same influencer network (architects/contractors). Acquiring profitable running assets in Morbi shortens the learning curve to cater to organized brands via contract manufacturing before launching any APL Apollo branded line.
2. **Nandan (Nirvana) on margin guidance and Kisan turnaround:** Management stated Apollo standalone EBITDA/tonne is targeted at Rs. 8,000-10,000 in H2, while Kisan is targeted to reach breakeven in 6-9 months and Rs. 7,000-8,000/tonne in 2-3 years.
3. **Karan (AM Securities) on channel inventory and Q3 outlook:** Management noted October run rates are similar to Q2, with December quarter expected to be flattish QoQ due to labor shortages and Delhi-NCR construction pollution bans, followed by a strong Q4.
4. **Pranav Mehta (Equirus Securities) on tile acquisition strategy and funding:** Management stated they acquire majority stakes (e.g., 76% in Mazzini) and manage day-to-day operations themselves. Funding is managed via internal cash flows and a planned Rs. 200 crore equity raise, requiring no additional debt.
5. **Srishti (Aravya) on competitive advantage and market share:** Management highlighted that North India pricing and market share remain protected, and pan-India expansion (Varanasi ramp-up, South India greenfield, and West India turnaround) will drive long-term growth toward the Rs. 4,000 crore 2030 goal.

### One-line takeaway
Apollo Pipes reported a recovery in Q2 FY27 with 13% YoY volume growth and returning profitability, while strategically expanding into the ceramic tiles segment and outlining a Rs. 4,000 crore revenue vision for 2030.
