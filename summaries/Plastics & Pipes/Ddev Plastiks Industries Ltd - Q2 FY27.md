# Ddev Plastiks Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reported strong revenue growth and high EBITDA per ton, but acknowledged margin pressures and logistics headwinds from the Middle East conflict.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Annual revenue estimate raised from INR 3,340 crores to INR 3,800 crores (79% YoY growth) due to higher average realizations; volume growth guidance maintained.
- Tone and demand view: Remain positive on structural demand, but highlighted ongoing export logistics challenges and freight inflation due to the Middle East war.
- New topics or risks: State incentive policy for BESS announced on November 5; export freight rates spiked to USD 300–350 per ton.
- Last quarter's promises: BESS project execution shifted into next fiscal year (Q1 FY28) due to awaiting state incentive policy announcements.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 970 crore for Q2 FY27 (up 44% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue Rs 1,968 crore (up 36% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 86 crore for Q2 FY27 (up 16% YoY); EBITDA margin at 9%; H1 FY27 EBITDA Rs 187 crore (up 22% YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 52 crore for Q2 FY27 (up 10% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT Rs 116 crore (up 17% YoY).
- **Volume:** 102,000 metric tonnes for H1 FY27.
- **Export Contribution:** 29% of total sales for H1 FY27.
- **EBITDA per ton:** Rs 17,000 for Q2 FY27.
- **Capex:** Rs 79 crore incurred in H1 FY27; FY27 capex guidance at Rs 175 crore, FY28 capex at Rs 50–70 crore.
- **Balance Sheet:** Cash of >Rs 100 crore, debt of Rs 67 crore, resulting in net cash of ~Rs 35 crore. Debtors stood at >Rs 650 crore and inventory at ~Rs 400 crore.

### Highlights
- Export contribution expanded to 29% for H1 FY27, driven by new markets and customers.
- Maintained market leadership across 5 high-growth product segments (PVC, anti-pipe, XLPE, engineering plastics, and HFFR).
- Revised annual revenue guidance upwards to Rs 3,800 crore for FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised annual revenue guidance to Rs 3,800 crore (79% YoY growth) for FY27.
- Management maintained volume growth guidance for FY27.
- Management reiterated the long-term top-line ambition of Rs 5,000 crore by FY30 from the compounding segment (with BESS contributions over and above).
- Management guided a sustainable EBITDA per ton benchmark range of Rs 14,000 to Rs 17,000.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remains healthy, supported by private capex in data centers and power transmission & distribution (T&D).
- Wires & cable segment growing robustly.
- Monsoon season created a temporary lean period for domestic cable laying in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin squeezed to 9% in Q2 due to raw material volatility and high export freight rates (rising from USD 10–20 per ton to USD 300–350 per ton).
- Working capital and finance costs increased due to higher inventory and debtor values driven by higher raw material prices (average selling price rose to ~Rs 192/kg).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- HFFR capacity expansion to 20,000 tons on track to double by the end of the year.
- BESS project execution is being reviewed following the state incentive policy announcement on November 5, targeting full rollout in Q1 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debtors increased to >Rs 650 crore and inventory to ~Rs 400 crore due to higher polymer prices.
- Net cash position maintained at ~Rs 35 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East causing shipping disruptions, longer transit times, and restricted material movements.
- Spike in export freight rates (USD 300–350 per ton) impacting export margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Archana Gude (IDBI Capital) on margins:** Management clarified that EBITDA per ton reached Rs 17,000 due to temporary war-risk premiums, and guided a normalized sustainable range of Rs 14,000–17,000.
2. **Shrey Gandhi (Anand Rathi) on capacity utilization:** Management explained utilization dropped due to recent capacity additions and monsoon seasonality, expecting new plants to reach 50% utilization by fiscal year-end.
3. **Jainam Ghelani (Svan Investments) on volume guidance:** Management maintained their volume growth guidance for the full year, noting that H2 historically drives the bulk of volume and revenue.
4. **Nikunj Doshi (Individual Investor) on BESS project delay:** Management clarified that the delay was due to waiting for the state incentive policy, which was announced on November 5.
5. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.) on working capital expansion:** Management stated that the increase in debtors (>Rs 650 crore) and inventory (~Rs 400 crore) is primarily a function of higher average selling prices (~Rs 192/kg) rather than structural credit extension.

### One-line takeaway
Ddev Plastiks delivered robust Q2 FY27 revenue growth driven by strong wire & cable demand and export traction, leading to an upward revision in annual revenue guidance to Rs 3,800 crore despite logistics headwinds and freight inflation.
