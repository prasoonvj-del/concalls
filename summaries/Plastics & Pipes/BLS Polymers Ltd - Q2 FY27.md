# BLS Polymers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by manufacturing expansion, improved product mix, and robust demand in power and infrastructure.

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue: Rs. 125.56 crores (up 28.92% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs. 232.59 crores (up 35.63% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs. 33.57 crores (up 41.47% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs. 20.77 crores (up 51% YoY)
- Current Manufacturing Capacity: 44,340 metric tons (increased from 35,000+ metric tons last year)
- Export Revenue Contribution: 5% to 6% of total revenue on the manufacturing side
- Top 10 Customers Concentration: Almost 55% of total revenue
- Single Customer Manufacturing Contribution: No single customer contributes more than 8% of total revenue

### Highlights
- First earnings call following the company's listing.
- Released segment reporting for the first time; H1 trading revenue decreased to Rs. 55 crores (compared to Rs. 58 crores in H1 last year), demonstrating a sustainable shift toward manufacturing revenue growth.
- Continued execution of IPO-led capacity expansion; capacity reached 44,340 metric tons in Q2.
- Management noted that Q1 FY27 EBITDA margins had temporarily spiked to 15% due to price jumps from West Asia crisis fallout.
- *Pattern check:* The call continues the capacity expansion narrative from our prior record, moving from 35,000+ MTPA to 44,340 MTPA and targeting 87,301 total MTPA.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to increase total capacity from 44,000 tons to around 55,000 tons by the end of FY27, and complete the total projected expansion to 87,000+ tons by the end of FY28.
- Management targets a margin expansion of 100 bps to 150 bps over the next 12 to 18 months, backed by capacity expansion and economies of scale.
- Export revenue is targeted to at least double as a percentage of total revenue over the next 18 months to 24 months.
- Value-added products contribution is targeted to increase from current levels of around 5% to 15% of total revenue over time.
- Annual revenue growth is planned to be sustained around the 30% to 35% trajectory achieved in H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Power cable consumption is slated to grow by 15% to 16% over the next 5 to 7 years in the Indian subcontinent and other markets, driven by energy consumption plays.
- Core industries served include power, telecommunications, water infrastructure, oil, gas, pipelines, and City Gas Distribution.
- Q2 is typically a slow period for infrastructure outlay due to heavy rains in August and September, which also causes inventory to build up across the value chain.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average EBITDA margins are around 13.25%, and PAT margins are around 7.5% to 8%.
- Profitability improvements are attributed to product mix, value addition, and manufacturing efficiencies.
- Trading is utilized as a necessity to secure raw material pricing advantages.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity expanded to 44,340 metric tons in Q2.
- Total proposed expansion plan adds 87,301 metric tons in the coming years (reaching ~55,000 tons by end of FY27 and full expansion by end of FY28).
- New capacity typically takes 12 to 18 months to reach 70% to 75% utilization.
- Compounding industry asset turnover targeted at peak utilization is 8x to 10x.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased to nearly Rs. 100 crores at Q2 end due to seasonal monsoon slowdowns in infrastructure, expected to normalize by December and March ends.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Entering new markets and specialty compounds takes a long time to build brand value and product consistency.
- Q2 experiences seasonal slow-downs due to rains impacting infrastructure outlay.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nilesh Jain (Master Investment Management) on trading vs. manufacturing mix and XLPE capacity:** Management clarified that trading is not a mainstay and H1 trading dropped to Rs. 55 crores, proving revenue expansion is driven by manufacturing. They highlighted robust power cable demand (15-16% growth) driving XLPE expansion. *(Management answered directly)*
2. **Rahul Kumar (Individual Investor) on EBITDA margin drivers and sustainability:** Management explained that H1-to-H1 margins are stable around 13-13.5% (ignoring a Q1 spike of 15% caused by West Asia crisis price jumps) and guided for a 100-150 bps margin expansion over 12-18 months. *(Management answered directly)*
3. **Aniket Shinde (Individual Investor) on export contributions and target markets:** Management stated exports are 5-6% of manufacturing revenue, with trials underway in Tunisia, Nigeria, Poland, Romania, Algeria, and Turkey, targeting a doubling of export share in 18-24 months. *(Management answered directly)*
4. **Maitri Shah on EPC approvals and seasonality:** Management clarified they do not take EPC contracts; approvals are standard product certifications required to supply pipe and cable makers. Q2 is noted as a seasonally slow quarter. *(Management answered directly)*
5. **Disha Shah on government/institutional project visibility:** Management reiterated that they operate on a B2B model supplying compound manufacturers, avoiding direct participation in large government tenders. *(Management answered directly)*

### One-line takeaway
BLS Polymers delivered strong H1 FY27 growth (revenue +36%, PAT +51%), backed by rising manufacturing scale, stabilizing margins around 13-13.5%, and ongoing capacity expansion toward 87,301 MTPA.
