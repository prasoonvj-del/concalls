# Shaily Engineering Plastics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, crossed INR100 crores EBITDA milestone in a single quarter, and robust growth in the Healthcare segment.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR320 crores, up 24% YoY and 14% QoQ (H1 FY27: INR600 crores, up 19% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR102 crores, up 25% YoY and 22% QoQ (H1 FY27: INR185 crores, up 22% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 31.9% (up 10 bps YoY from 31.8%) (H1 FY27: 30.8%, up 60 bps YoY)
- **PAT:** INR61 crores, up 20% YoY (H1 FY27: INR109 crores, up 18% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 19.2% compared to 20.0% in Q2 FY26 (H1 FY27: 18.2% vs 18.4% in H1 FY26)
- **Healthcare Revenue:** INR175 crores, up 78% YoY (H1 FY27: INR318 crores, up 81% YoY); contributed 55% of revenue
- **Consumer Revenue:** INR116 crores, down 14% YoY and flat QoQ (H1 FY27: INR231 crores, down 19% YoY)
- **Industrial Revenue:** INR29 crores, up 23% YoY (H1 FY27: INR51 crores, up 24% YoY)
- **Exports:** 62.9% of revenue in H1 FY27 compared to 71.7% in H1 FY26
- **Machine Capacity Utilization:** 52.5% in Q2 compared to 47.8% a year ago
- **Debt to Equity:** 0.2x as of 30th September 2026
- **Fixed Asset Turnover:** 2x as of 30th September 2026
- **ROCE / ROE:** 41.1% and 29.7% respectively as of 30th September 2026
- **Healthcare Device Deliveries:** 10.4 million devices in Q2

### Highlights
- Crossed the INR100 crores EBITDA milestone in a single quarter for the first time.
- Gross margins recovered to 60%, about 70 bps higher YoY.
- Healthcare is now the primary growth engine, contributing 55% of total revenue.
- Delivered 10.4 million devices in Q2; on track to exceed full-year guidance of 36 million devices.
- Signed 2 new contracts for proprietary platforms and launched the "Toby Reusable" 2-step auto-injector for next-gen GLP-1 therapies (conservatively 70% more sustainable than a disposable 2-step auto-injector).
- Received business confirmations for new products in Consumer and Industrial segments (7 new power tool products, new knobs, and new consumer electronics components).
- CARE Ratings reaffirmed long-term bank facilities at CARE A+ (stable) and upgraded short-term facilities to CARE A1+ from CARE A1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they will exceed the full-year device guidance of 36 million pens, though quantum of excess is not yet quantified.
- Targeting roughly 15% growth for next year in Healthcare, though management noted it is not official guidance due to early-stage market launches.
- Consumer segment revenue is expected to remain flat for the current year, with growth expected next year depending on global situations.
- Abu Dhabi facility construction remains on track to be ready by the end of FY28.
- Semiconductor packaging trial production expected in Q3 FY27, with commercial shipments starting in Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global GLP-1 market entering a transition phase with patent expirations and generic competition gaining momentum (launches in India in March, Canada in April, Brazil in August).
- Consumer demand for home furnishings in Europe and the U.S. remains subdued due to geopolitical uncertainties and input cost volatility.
- Demand for pen injectors and GLP-1 programs remains very strong.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- West Asia developments impacted freight costs and polymer prices.
- Price pass-throughs for polymer inflation happen with a lag of 2 to 3-4 months depending on the customer.
- Long-term Healthcare contracts have price erosion already built in, which management expects to offset via operating leverage (current utilization at 52%) and index mechanisms for raw material inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- The second manufacturing line for pens is starting qualification next week, with commercial sales expected to start in December.
- Abu Dhabi facility is targeted for completion by the end of FY28.
- Consumer Electronics and Semiconductor Packaging scale-up opportunity is being pursued over the next 36 months, with a baseline capex of around INR100 crores anticipated.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt to equity stands at a healthy 0.2x as of 30th September 2026.
- Fixed asset turnover is at 2x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia impacting freight costs and polymer prices.
- Subdued consumer demand in Europe and the U.S.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec) asked about Toby Reusable's TAM, cannibalization risks, and product recalls.** Management answered that it will not cannibalize existing pen/auto-injector portfolios as it targets innovator pharma seeking sustainability; clinical batches for emergency use are due end of 2027 with commercialization by end of CY28. Management also clarified that recent product recalls by customers were due to incorrect labeling/SKU mismatches rather than device defects or Shaily's manufacturing.
- **Harshh Shah (JM Financial PMS) asked about innovator discussions and timelines.** Management stated they are participating in RFQs and are confident of onboarding 1 or 2 innovators by the end of CY28 (note: corrected by management later that revenue takes 3-4 years to flow post-Phase II/III entry).
- **Vinil Shah (Dalal & Broacha) asked about QoQ rise in other expenses.** Management explained it was a combination of one-off expenses and higher expenses due to a change in the revenue/business mix.
- **Akhil Parekh (360 One Capital) asked about subsidiary losses.** Management stated that revenue was not recognized in Q2 due to pending customer confirmations, but the subsidiary will not have a full-year loss.
- **Raghav Mittal (Dhamma Capital) asked about the strategy and tech partnership for semiconductor trays.** Management explained they tied up with a Korean partner for contract manufacturing (JEDEC trays, carrier tapes, wafer boxes) to supply both domestic OSAT/TSAT plants and export markets, expecting margins better than average consumer/industrial segments.

### One-line takeaway
Shaily Engineering Plastics delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue of INR320 crores and crossed INR100 crores in EBITDA, driven by a 78% YoY surge in its Healthcare segment, which now accounts for 55% of total revenue.
