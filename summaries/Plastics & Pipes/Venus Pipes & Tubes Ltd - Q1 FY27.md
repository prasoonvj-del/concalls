# Venus Pipes & Tubes Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited record quarterly revenue and EBITDA, strong demand, and new capacities coming online.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 320.5 crore (up 16% YoY from Rs 276.4 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 51.5 crore (up 14.7% YoY from Rs 44.9 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 16.1% (vs 16.2% in Q1 FY26)
- PAT: Rs 26.4 crore (up 6.5% YoY from Rs 24.8 crore in Q1 FY26)
- PAT margin: 8.2%
- Domestic revenue: Rs 227 crore (up 31% YoY)
- Export revenue: Rs 94 crore (down from Rs 103 crore in Q1 FY26; contributing nearly 30% of total revenue)
- Revenue mix: Seamless Pipes 55%, Welded Pipes 39%, Others 6%
- Seamless Pipes revenue growth: 15% YoY
- Welded Pipes revenue growth: 21% YoY
- Order book: More than Rs 600 crore (pipes and fittings), plus an LOI of Rs 185 crore for the spooling project (totaling approx Rs 800 crore)
- Capacity utilization: Welded around 60%, Seamless around 85%-90%
- Net debt: Approx Rs 325 crore as of June 30, 2026
- Capex guidance: Around Rs 100-110 crore for the year (including Rs 70 crore for spooling, fittings/machinery, Rs 15 crore for maintenance/solar plant)

### Highlights
- Commercialized fittings and value-added seamless and welded capacities in May 2026.
- Entered the pipe spooling business with a planned capex of around Rs 70 crore, backed by an LOI from a data center client; on track to commence by the end of Q3 FY27.
- Aiming to build a one-stop piping and engineering solution provider.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a revenue growth guidance of around 20% for FY27.
- Management targets EBITDA margins of 18% over the coming 2 years, expecting margins to begin inching up post Q2 FY27.
- Expects the spooling business to contribute about 5% of top line in FY27 and 10%-15% in FY28.
- Expects fittings to contribute around 5%-7% of total top line in FY27 and 8%-10% in FY28.
- Management expects the business to double by FY29 or FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural growth opportunities seen in oil & gas, chemical, petrochemical, power, pharmaceutical, process industries, and data centers.
- Data center demand is driven by cooling networks (secondary fluid network / SFN).
- Emerging sectors include solar, semiconductor, CNG station-related infrastructure, and nuclear energy.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher steel prices supported reported revenues and EBITDA per kg.
- Export margins tend to be slightly higher once established, though impacted temporarily by geopolitical tensions and freight/container issues.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rs 100-110 crore capex planned for FY27, including Rs 70 crore for the spooling facility, fittings, and other machinery, plus Rs 15 crore for maintenance and a solar plant.
- Spooling facility asset turnover is expected to be more than 3x.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at around Rs 325 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- Working capital borrowings are utilized to manage operations and growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments, supply chain disruptions, and volatility in commodity prices.
- Export environment impacted by geopolitical tensions and container issues.
- EU safeguard quota reductions for seamless steel tubes and pipes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bharat Shah (BCS Capital Ideas):** Questioned the absolute size of profits relative to turnover and high interest costs. Management replied that heavy capex over the last 4 years and working capital borrowings affected the bottom line, but moving into value-added products (fittings and spooling) will improve margins to 18%-19% within 2-3 quarters.
- **Deepak (Sundaram MF):** Asked about the revenue mismatch for data center spooling against the Rs 185 crore LOI. Management clarified that they have accelerated work and target finishing a significant portion before December 2026.
- **Nikhil Chowdhary:** Asked if EU safeguard quota reductions drove the export decline. Management attributed the decline mainly to broader geopolitics and noted Venus is diversified across the US, Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
- **Aasim:** Asked for a segment-wise growth breakdown for FY27. Management stated both welded and seamless are targeted to grow around 20%, with fittings contributing 5%.
- **Simran Kumari:** Asked for exact volume growth numbers. Management stated blended volume growth was more than 7% YoY for the quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Venus Pipes & Tubes reported record Q1 FY27 performance with 16% revenue growth and 14.7% EBITDA growth, while advancing forward integration into fittings and spooling to expand margins toward 18% over the next two years.
