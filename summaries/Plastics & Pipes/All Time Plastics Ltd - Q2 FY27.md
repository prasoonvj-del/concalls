# All Time Plastics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Negative - Management described the quarter as "among the most challenging in the company's history" due to the escalation of the West Asia conflict, which caused severe raw material inflation and logistics disruptions.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 15% to 20% revenue growth target for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "Cautious" to "Negative" regarding the operating environment; demand remains resilient, but margins were severely impacted by external shocks.
- **New topics:** Piloted a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) channel; implemented SCADA-based real-time machine monitoring at Khatalwada; received ET Now Excellence in Compliance & Sustainability Award 2026.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Bamboo commercial production was slated for mid-August 2026 in the Q1 call; the current call states commercial production commences mid-November 2026.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 174 crore (up 18% YoY, 8% QoQ).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 335 crore (up 9.7% YoY).
- **Volume (Polymers Processed):** 6,910 metric tonnes (up 9% QoQ).
- **Gross Margin:** 34.3% (vs 36.2% in Q2 FY26; 39.5% in Q1 FY27).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 13.7 crore (Margin 7.9% vs 11% in Q2 FY26; 14.3% in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT:** Rs 4.2 crore (Margin 2.4%).
- **Capacity Utilisation:** 67% (up from 65% in Q1).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 11.9 crore (Gross borrowings Rs 94.6 crore; Cash/liquid investments Rs 82.7 crore).

### Highlights
- **Logistics/Operations:** Port congestion and container shortages caused a backlog of 126 containers for the largest customer (reduced to 32 as of the call date). Silvassa facility production was suspended for 3 days due to storage constraints.
- **Domestic Business:** Revenue grew 36% QoQ and 27% YoY; share of revenue increased to 19.5% (from 15.5% in Q1).
- **Bamboo Business:** Commercial production commences mid-November 2026; sales expected in Q4 FY27.
- **US Market:** Contribution dropped to 10% (from 19% in Q1) due to shipment timing; management expects this to be the second-largest customer by end of FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue:** Management targets 15% to 20% growth.
- **Volume:** Management expects at least 7% to 8% production growth for FY27.
- **Bamboo:** Projects ~Rs 60 crore revenue for CY27.
- **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to improve in H2 as price revisions take effect and fixed costs are absorbed by higher volumes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Export demand remains resilient, though management noted "some headwinds" on specific articles due to high price points.
- Domestic demand is healthy; the company added 36 new customers in H1.
- Management noted that China currently holds a competitive advantage in freight costs (approx. $100 advantage).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **RM Inflation:** Prices rose 40% to 45% during the quarter.
- **Margin Compression:** Gross margins declined as the company absorbed part of the cost increase to protect long-term customer relationships and honored confirmed export orders at fixed prices.
- **Fixed Costs:** Increased due to labor/staff costs at new Khatalwada and Bamboo facilities ahead of full utilization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capacity:** Currently ~41,000 metric tonnes; 14 new injection molding machines (1,500 tonnes) arriving December 2026, taking capacity to 42,500 tonnes.
- **Bamboo Capex:** ~Rs 18 crore spent to date; total planned Rs 20 crore. Future doubling of capacity will require an additional Rs 10-12 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Working Capital:** Inventory rose to Rs 120.2 crore (from Rs 68.6 crore in March) due to finished goods held back by shipping delays and higher raw material buffers.
- **Receivables:** Rs 100.1 crore (from Rs 74.9 crore in March); collection days improved to 46 days (from 50 days).
- **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.15x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued geopolitical instability in West Asia impacting raw material prices and shipping timelines.
- Potential for further margin volatility if price pass-throughs are delayed.
- Management noted that while they hope the "worst is behind us," the external environment remains unpredictable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Swapnil Gupta (Investor) asked about inventory composition:** Management clarified Rs 42 crore is finished goods, of which Rs 8 crore was in-transit sales recognized in October.
- **Nirali Gopani (Unique PMS) asked about margin recovery:** Management stated they are negotiating price increases and expect margins to improve in H2, though not necessarily to historic levels immediately.
- **Akshay Chheda (Canara Robeco) asked about volume visibility:** Management confirmed strong H2 visibility and expects at least 15,000 metric tonnes production in H2.
- **Sahil Doshi (Thinqwise) asked about the "worst is behind us":** Management expressed hope for stability but declined to guarantee, citing external geopolitical factors.
- **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) asked about Bamboo expansion:** Management confirmed they will double capacity once 50% utilization is reached, requiring Rs 10-12 crore capex.

### One-line takeaway
Despite a challenging quarter marked by 40-45% raw material inflation and logistics-driven margin compression, the company maintained its FY27 growth guidance, citing strong order books and expected margin recovery in H2.
