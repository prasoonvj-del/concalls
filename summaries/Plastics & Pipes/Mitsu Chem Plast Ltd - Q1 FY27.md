# Mitsu Chem Plast Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-25 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue growth and a significant expansion in profitability driven by operating efficiencies and product mix.

### Key numbers
- Total Income: INR 9,532.78 lakhs (up 11.62% YoY from INR 8,540.39 lakhs in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: INR 1,549.48 lakhs (up 209.50% YoY from INR 500.64 lakhs implied / INR 1,422.74 lakhs in Q4 FY26)
- EBITDA Margin: 16.29% (up 1041 bps YoY from 5.87%)
- Net Profit: INR 873.83 lakhs (up 566.23% YoY from INR 131.16 lakhs in Q1 FY26; up sequentially from INR 771.73 lakhs in Q4 FY26)
- Net Profit Margin: 9.18% (vs 1.54% in Q1 FY26)
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): INR 6.44 (vs INR 0.97 in Q1 FY26)
- Capacity Addition: Proposed/online addition of 3,550 metric tonnes per annum to existing 32,450 metric tonnes per annum
- Existing Capacity Utilization: 64% in FY26
- Export Share: Approximately 2% of total revenue
- Healthcare Segment Contribution: Approximately 19.80% (including furniture parts and other parts)

### Highlights
- Delivered strong financial performance with double-digit top-line growth and multi-fold bottom-line expansion due to operational efficiencies and product mix.
- Added 30+ new customers during the quarter (over 150+ customers added last year).
- Management stated that an additional 3,550 metric tonnes per annum capacity has been successfully run and is already online.
- Actively rationalizing low-volume, low-margin SKUs (profit drainers) while introducing new higher-margin products.
- Long-term objective remains achieving INR 1,000 crores in annual revenue by FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects quarter-on-quarter growth momentum to continue and remains committed to the INR 1,000 crores annual revenue target by FY28.
- Sustainable normalized EBITDA margin is typically viewed by management as 10% to 12% (or 10% to 13%), while acknowledging Q1 reached 16.29% due to specific efficiencies and product mix.
- Capacity utilization across expanded capacity is expected to hover around 64%, 65%, or 70%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand reported across all three verticals: containers, furniture parts, and others.
- Healthcare furniture continues to be an important growth opportunity via the Furnastra brand.
- IBC (Intermediate Bulk Container) project commercial production is expected to start around Q3.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material is procured locally from Indian producers with whom the company maintains good relations; imports have been stopped for several years.
- Raw material price increases driven by geopolitical situations and crude are successfully being passed on to customers with a lag of approximately one month.
- Margin expansion was attributed by management to manufacturing efficiencies, operating efficiencies, value engineering, and product mix optimization, rather than just raw material pass-through.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total manufacturing capacity increased by 3,550 metric tonnes per annum to over 36,000 metric tonnes per annum, supported by existing infrastructure.
- Capex for the recent capacity addition was approximately INR 2 crores, funded through a mix of internal accruals and debt.
- New machinery and separate investments are underway for the upcoming IBC project targeted for Q3 commercialization.
- Fundraise via warrants is earmarked for working capital.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Funds raised via warrants are utilized for working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situation and crude price volatility create raw material price pressures (though mitigated by local sourcing and pass-through mechanisms).
- Long approval cycles noted for hospital furniture products.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinod Shah (VS Ventures):** Asked about SKU rationalization. *Management answered that they actively discontinue low-margin/profit-drainer SKUs while simultaneously introducing new products.*
2. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital):** Asked about the sustainability of the 16% EBITDA margin given geopolitical headwinds. *Management clarified that normal sustainable margins average 10%-12%, and the higher Q1 margin resulted from specific operating efficiencies and product mix.*
3. **Rajesh Gupta (Individual Investor):** Questioned the rationale for capacity expansion when utilization was 64%. *Management explained that machinery takes 6 to 9 months from order to commercialization, requiring advance planning alongside infrastructure readiness.*
4. **Praveen Sharma (Individual Investor):** Inquired about the timeline and scale of the IBC project. *Management stated commercial production is expected to start around Q3 with new machinery.*
5. **Saket Saurabh (Individual Investor):** Asked about the pricing pass-through frequency and export share. *Management stated price revisions generally happen with a one-month lag, and exports constitute approximately 2% of total revenue.*
6. **Rashi Jain (Individual Investor):** Asked about the stickiness of hospital furniture vs. packaging. *Management declined to directly compare the two, noting they are entirely separate business segments with distinct customer demands and processes.*

### One-line takeaway
Mitsu Chem Plast delivered a sharp surge in profitability and margin expansion in Q1 FY27, backed by operational efficiencies, SKU rationalization, and ongoing capacity additions aligned with its FY28 revenue goals.
