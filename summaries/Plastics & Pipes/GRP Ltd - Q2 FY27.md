# GRP Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stable operating profitability achieved despite raw material inflation and geopolitical volatility.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Total Income: INR1,727 million (up 30% YoY) for Q2 FY27; INR3,300 million (up 28% YoY) for H1 FY27.
- Consolidated EBITDA: INR182 million (up 60% YoY) for Q2 FY27; INR356 million (up 60% YoY) for H1 FY27.
- EBITDA Margin: 10.5% (up 199 bps YoY) for Q2 FY27; 10.8% for H1 FY27 (vs 8.6% in H1 FY26).
- Consolidated PAT: INR64 million (up 226% YoY) from INR20 million in Q2 FY26; INR106 million for H1 FY27 (vs INR37 million in H1 FY26).
- Gross Margin: Declined to 46% in Q2 FY27 from 51% in Q2 FY26.
- EPR Income: INR67 million in Q2 FY27 (vs INR43 million in Q2 FY26); INR115 million in H1 FY27 (including INR14.9 million from Pyrova Energy).
- Segment Revenues (Standalone H1 FY27): Domestic revenue grew 40% to INR1,678 million; Export revenue grew 19% to INR1,548 million.
- Plastics Business (Consolidated H1 FY27): Revenue grew 38% YoY; EBITDA margins improved by 11 percentage points.
- Capital Expenditure: H1 FY27 capex stood at INR246 million; cumulative Pyrova Energy capex at ~INR1,140 million (including INR110 million CWIP) as of September 30, 2026.
- Debt & Working Capital: Gross debt at INR2,191 million; Net debt at INR2,140 million; Debt-to-equity ratio at 1.13x (vs 1.08x as of March 31, 2026). Working capital cycle reduced by 11 days to 84 days as of September 30, 2026.
- ECB Drawdown: EUR7.5 million drawn out of EUR12 million from PROPARCO (unchanged from March 31, 2026).

### Highlights
- Core rubber recycling business standalone segment revenue grew 30% in H1; Reclaim Rubber volumes grew 8%.
- Pyrova Energy commenced availing EPR benefits in Q2 FY27 following registration; operations improved month-on-month.
- Plastics business achieved a complete turnaround with growth across automotive and non-automotive applications.
- Shift towards domestic markets: Standalone domestic sales now account for ~52% of standalone revenue (vs ~48% a year ago).
- Received EcoVadis Gold Rating, placing GRP in the top 5% of companies assessed worldwide.
- Renewable energy met ~48% of standalone power needs in FY26, with the Gujarat Solar project expected to cross 50% in the current year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains an aspiration of high double-digit growth for the plastic business with stable EBITDA margins of 12% to 15%, and expects ~20% growth in the plastic business for the year.
- Management targets a mid-teen EBITDA margin for the entire platform business and ~2x asset turns once rCB and expansion phases are complete.
- Pyrolysis capacity is slated to expand from 15 kilotons per annum to 45 kilotons per annum by March 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global tire demand remains mixed with subdued OE demand in major economies and more resilient replacement demand.
- Indian automobile production grew strongly in September quarter; tire manufacturers see growth across OE and replacement channels.
- Reclaimed rubber consumption in India rose 5% YoY in the June quarter.
- Reset of US tariffs on Indian goods normalized export order patterns, and export volumes recovered from last year's disruption.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins fell by 5 percentage points due to raw material inflation, escalation in crude oil, natural rubber prices reaching over a decade-high, and export freight costs.
- Efficiencies, manpower productivity, energy optimization, and new downstream technologies (providing ~2% to 3% cost savings) helped expand EBITDA margins by 199 bps YoY.
- Pyrova's current performance partly reflects elevated crude/furnace oil prices due to West Asia conflicts; management cautioned that realizations may soften if energy markets normalize.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pyrova Energy cumulative investment stood at INR1,140 million (including INR110 million CWIP) as of September 30, 2026; total facility investment at Solapur targeted at ~INR1,400 million for 45k tons pyrolysis and 15k tons rCB capacity.
- Commissioning of the rCB facility at Solapur is progressing for completion by the end of Q3.
- Expansion of pyrolysis capacity (from 15k to 45k tons) is underway through March 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Proactive cash flow planning and disciplined working capital management reduced the working capital cycle to 84 days (down from 95 days as of March 31, 2026).
- Gross debt stood at INR2,191 million and net debt at INR2,140 million as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situation in West Asia drives volatility in crude/furnace oil prices and raw material (end-of-life tire) costs.
- Tightening of waste tire imports due to freight cost increases has intensified competition and put pressure on domestic raw material prices.
- Potential softening of tire pyrolysis oil realizations if global energy markets normalize.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Narendra (Equinova) on pyrolysis procurement & regulations:** Management clarified they rely entirely on domestic waste for pyrolysis and hold no imported waste for that segment (unlike reclaim rubber which uses Advance Licenses). Raw material prices are pressured due to tighter import controls on end-of-life tires pushing demand to domestic sources.
2. **Manish (MM Capital) on rCB commercial plant and EBITDA targets:** Management clarified that the commercial rCB manufacturing plant is not yet operational (only a pilot plant exists), so specific rCB EBITDA targets were not provided, but reiterated a mid-teen platform EBITDA target.
3. **Mohit Mittal (JH Investments) on gross vs EBITDA margin divergence:** Management explained gross margin contraction was driven by Pyrova's lower conversion gross margins and export freight costs, while EBITDA expanded due to operational efficiencies, energy optimization, and newer technologies (accounting for ~35% of reclaim volumes).
4. **Rohit Kumar (ADM Advisors) on plastic business turnaround and sustainability:** Management stated the turnaround stems from product and customer mix shifts toward value-added applications, targeting 20% growth and 12-15% EBITDA margins this year.
5. **Jay Jain (JM Financial/Individual) on pyrolysis ramp-up and ROCE:** Management stated commercialization post-commissioning of the expanded 45k ton capacity (by March 2027) will take 30 to 60 days, targeting ~95% utilization, ~2x asset turns, and mid-teen EBITDA margins.

### One-line takeaway
GRP delivered a 30% YoY revenue growth and expanded EBITDA margins to ~11% in H1 FY27 by successfully absorbing severe raw material inflation through operational efficiencies, energy optimization, and scaling its integrated recycling and plastics platforms.
