# Ddev Plastiks Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong financial performance with record EBITDA crossing the INR 100 crore benchmark, driven by robust traction in cables/wires and exports.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 900+ crores (implied from context) / Revenue grew 29% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Crossed INR 100 crores for the first time (specifically INR 101 crores, up 27% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 64-odd crores, up 22% YoY.
- **Export Revenue:** INR 300+ crores.
- **Installed Capacity:** 3,16,400 metric tons per annum (as of June 2026).
- **Capacity Utilization:** Average 66-odd percent.
- **Volume Guidance (FY27):** 2,31,000 tons total volume (15% volume growth target).
- **Revenue Top-Line Ambition:** INR 5,000 crores by FY30.
- **Capex Guidance:** INR 150 crores to INR 175-odd crores for FY27.

### Highlights
- Commissioned a new 48,000 metric tonnes greenfield XLPE compound facility in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan, in April 2026, taking total XLPE capacity to 2,14,500 metric tonnes per annum.
- Exports grew at a 16% CAGR between FY22 and FY26 despite geopolitical disruptions.
- Made a strategic entry into Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).
- Maintained market share leadership: ~50% in Sioplas, >33% in XLPE compounds, and a leading presence in HFFR compounds in India.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a volume growth target of 15% for FY27 despite a soft Q1 (1% volume growth).
- Management expects the Bhiwadi facility to operate at 50% average utilization for FY27, contributing close to INR 200 crores to INR 250 crores of revenue (and eventually INR 500 crores at full scale).
- Reiterated long-term top-line ambition of INR 5,000 crores by FY30.
- BESS business is shifted from West to East (Bengal), delaying it by a couple of quarters; first phase investment expected within INR 150 crores to INR 200 crores, targeting initial EBITDA margins of 6% to 8% (scaling to 11%–15% long term).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's installed power generation capacity is expected to nearly double from ~442 GW in FY4 to ~900 GW by FY32, supported by INR 9 trillion in transmission infrastructure investments.
- Shift occurring from conventional low-voltage applications to medium, high-voltage, and extra high-voltage applications.
- Cable and wire customer industries growing at 12% to 13% organically.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA per ton improved by almost INR 3 YoY, driven largely by export realizations and war-risk/freight volatility premiums in MENA markets.
- Raw material prices (resins/polymers) and freight rates experienced high volatility due to Middle East conflicts; prices expected to take 3 to 6 months to normalize downwards.
- Finance costs rose due to higher working capital borrowings for March inventory and debtors caused by sudden raw material price spikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total committed investment for FY27 is INR 150 crores to INR 175 crores, covering Medium Voltage capacity building in the East, HFFR capacity expansion to 20,000 tons, Vapi consolidation site, and BESS.
- BESS project phase 1 expected to involve ~1 GW capacity with INR 150–200 crores capex/working capital margin.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash conversion cycle/working capital days expected to normalize to 55–60 days.
- BESS first phase funding to be deployed primarily from internal accruals, with potential working capital debt of INR 100 crores to INR 150 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions (Middle East, Hormuz strait) causing raw material price volatility and shipping/logistic uncertainty.
- Short-term demand slowdown or project delays possible if crude and project costs remain elevated.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Apurva (White Stone Financial Advisors) on Q1 export realization:** Management clarified that Q1 included ~1,000 tons of delayed shipments from Q4 FY26 due to vessel/war issues, contributing to the >INR 300 crore export revenue.
2. **Hardik Jain (White Stone Financial Advisors) on sustainability of higher EBITDA per ton:** Management noted that the extra INR 2.5–3 per kg gained from war/freight volatility premiums may not sustain indefinitely, but guided for a normalized EBITDA per ton range of INR 15 to INR 17.
3. **Bhagwat (Prosperity Wealth Management) on BESS business model:** Management stated BESS will start as a supply and mix-of-EPC model (initially avoiding BOO/BOOT), requiring INR 150–200 crores in phase 1, with an expected EBITDA margin of 6% to 8% initially, scaling to 11%–15%.
4. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.) on relocation of BESS to Bengal:** Management explained the relocation to West Bengal is to capture regional opportunities and benefit from upcoming state industrial policies, resulting in a delay of a couple of quarters.
5. **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit Asset Management) on market share gains:** Management concurred that supply chain disruptions and local manufacturing footprints (like Bhiwadi) position them well to capture market share from overseas imports.

### One-line takeaway
Ddev Plastiks delivered a record-breaking EBITDA performance in Q1 FY27 backed by export realisations and new capacity additions, while maintaining its 15% volume growth guidance and long-term INR 5,000 crore revenue goal by FY30.
