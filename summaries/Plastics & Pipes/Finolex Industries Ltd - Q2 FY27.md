# Finolex Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Plastics & Pipes*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume recovery and normalized channel inventories despite geopolitical and margin pressures.

### Key numbers
- Sales Volume (Q2 FY27): 83,407 tonnes (up 28% YoY)
- Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 1,094 crore (up 27% YoY)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT) (Q2 FY27): Rs 153 crore (vs Rs 158 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Sales Volume (H1 FY27): 151,106 tonnes (down 4% YoY)
- Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27): Rs 1,978 crore (up 4% YoY)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT) (H1 FY27): Rs 300 crore (up 6% YoY from Rs 284 crore)
- Net Free Cash / Cash on Books: Rs 2,584 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- Average PVC Price (Q2): $771
- PVC/EDC Delta (Q2): $455
- PVC/VCM Spread (Q2): $171
- Capacity Utilisation: ~68%
- Capex (H1 FY27): Rs 36-37 crore
- Full-Year Capex Guidance: Rs 150+ crore
- EBITDA Margin Guidance (Full Year): ~14% to 15% (or ~Rs 15 per kg on an average)
- CPVC Volume Share (Q2): 8%
- Fitting Volume Share (Q2): 12% (overall local volume)
- Agri to Non-Agri Mix (Q2): 60:40

### Highlights
- Q2 volume recovery driven by channel destocking easing and stabilizing PVC prices following Q1 disruptions.
- H1 performance was impacted by weak Q1 numbers, but Q2 partially offset the deficit.
- October was a relatively good month, tracking slightly ahead of previous years on a YoY volume basis.
- Strong liquidity position maintained with net free cash of Rs 2,584 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains cautiously optimistic, targeting sustainable margins alongside volume growth.
- Full-year EBITDA margin expected around 14% to 15% (or roughly Rs 15 per kg).
- Full-year capex estimated at INR 150+ crore, largely for debottlenecking and routine maintenance.
- Anticipates seasonal high-demand window from January to May.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Channel inventories have normalized, and pricing environment is steadier.
- Q2 volume recovery happened across the organized industry, driven by restocking.
- Agri demand moderates traditionally in Q2 due to monsoons, but rural indicators (such as tractor and 2-wheeler bookings) remain positive despite potential El Nino water availability concerns.
- Government's Nal Se Jal fund releases could provide an overall indirect boost to demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin pressure in Q2 despite volume growth due to a time lag in raw material consumption (higher-cost inventory procured in Q1 consumed in Q2) and market schemes/discounts.
- Minimum Import Price (MIP) introduced for suspension-grade PVC resin at CIF price of $767 per metric ton.
- EDC, VCM, and Ethylene imports remain 100% sourced internationally, with Middle East supply chains successfully re-routed. Spot prices are trending higher (EDC at $450-$500, VCM at $900-$950, Ethylene at $1,150-$1,200).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total pipe and fitting nameplate capacity stands at 520,000 tonnes (including ~95,000 tonnes fitting capacity, of which ~40,000 tonnes is in-house and ~60,000 tonnes outsourced).
- Current capacity utilization is around 68%, providing sufficient headroom for current and next year.
- Capex of INR 150+ crore in FY27 is dedicated to debottlenecking (replacing low-capacity extruders with higher-capacity ones) and routine/maintenance capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net free cash stands at Rs 2,584 crore as of September 30, 2026, invested in various instruments yielding 7.5% to 8%.
- Land monetization of idle plants was done previously; no current plans to monetize existing land parcels (Ratnagiri, Masar, Urse).
- Capital allocation/cash payout decisions remain solely with the Board, with no fixed timeline or special dividend announced yet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia impacting crude prices, supply chains, and shipping rates.
- Potential El Nino impact on water availability and farmer income in certain states.
- Margin volatility due to raw material price lags and competitive channel discounting schemes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sneha (Talreja's) on margin pressure despite volume growth:** Management explained this was due to a time-lag effect where higher-cost raw material procured in Q1 was consumed in Q2, coupled with competitive market schemes.
2. **Shravan Shah (Dolat Capital) on full-year volume and margin outlook:** Management stated October was a good month and they are slightly positive on a YoY basis, reiterating full-year EBITDA guidance of ~Rs 15 per kg (14% to 15%).
3. **Nirav on El Nino and agri demand impact:** Management acknowledged potential water constraints in certain states, but pointed to positive leading indicators like rural tractor and 2-wheeler bookings.
4. **Rishab Bothra on large cash pile (Rs 2,584 crore) and lack of special rewards:** Management reiterated that capital allocation and rewards are strictly Board decisions, while noting that healthy regular dividends are currently paid.
5. **Ritesh Shah (Investec India) on DGTR recommendation of CVD on PVC from China:** Management noted that if implemented by the Ministry of Finance, it would likely replace the current Minimum Import Price (MIP).

### One-line takeaway
Finolex Industries reported a strong 28% YoY volume recovery in Q2 FY27 driven by channel restocking and stabilized PVC pricing, though margins faced a time-lagged raw material cost squeeze.
