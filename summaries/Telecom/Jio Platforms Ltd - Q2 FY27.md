# Jio Platforms Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-01 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stellar IPO demand, industry-leading customer additions, and strong revenue and EBITDA growth.

### Key numbers
- **Customers:** 542.4 million total customers (up 36 million YoY, 9 million QoQ net adds).
- **5G Customers:** 300 million.
- **Home Broadband Customers:** 30 million (up 7.3 million YoY); almost 50% from Fixed Wireless Access (FWA).
- **Data Usage:** 44.2 GB per user per month (up 14% YoY).
- **ARPU:** Rs. 218.8 per month (up 3.5% YoY).
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 40,475 crore (up 11.4% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 21,879 crore (up 16.6% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 54.1% (up 250 bps YoY).
- **EBITDA less Capex:** Rs. 15,449 crore in Q2 (up 52.5% YoY); H1 at Rs. 32,130 crore.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 8,387 crore (up 13.7% YoY).
- **Standalone JPL Revenue:** Rs. 4,881 crore (up 23% YoY).
- **Connectivity Entity Revenue:** Rs. 35,307 crore (up 10.8% YoY); EBITDA at Rs. 20,505 crore (up 14.7% YoY), margin at 58.1%.
- **Net Debt / Leverage:** Net leverage at 0.11x as of September 30, 2026; net debt at Rs. 8,822 crore (borrowings of Rs. 59,000 crore offset by marketable cash and securities of Rs. 50,000 crore). Net debt positive post IPO proceeds of Rs. 30,188 crore.
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** 11% (80 bps improvement in one quarter).
- **IPO Demand:** Rs. 7.4 lakh crore demand (24.5x oversubscription).

### Highlights
- Completed a landmark IPO raising Rs. 30,188 crore with 24.5x oversubscription across all categories.
- Reached 300 million 5G subscribers, comparable to top US operators (Verizon and AT&T).
- Maintained leadership in FWA globally, with UBR (Unlicensed Band Radio) technology driving home broadband additions.
- Emphasized the "digital gateway" concept (awareness to adoption to utilization to monetization flywheel) rather than simple bundling, citing partnerships like Gemini.
- Operating leverage resulted in ~78% of incremental revenues flowing through to EBITDA.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects peak capex to be behind them; capex for the next 4-5 years is not expected to exceed last year's spend.
- Aiming to maximize free cash flows through operating leverage and controlled capex.
- Management indicated intentions for dividends similar to Reliance's tradition, post-evaluation as a newly listed company.
- International expansion will target 10 to 15 sizable partners globally as a managed service partner, avoiding heavy capex rollouts.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Mobility market still has room for 340 million new broadband customers (240 million from existing 2G base of other operators, 100 million organic threshold additions).
- Home broadband penetration in India is at ~20% (64 million homes out of 330 million) and is projected to reach 46% (150 million homes) by FY2031.
- Enterprise segment identified as a massive growth opportunity, expanding beyond the country's 350,000 leased lines to target small merchants and medium enterprises.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins expanded by 250 bps YoY to 54.1%.
- All 5G assets are fully capitalized, with costs successfully flowing through the P&L.
- Operating efficiencies continue to drive strong flow-through to EBITDA and net profits.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Peak capex is complete; incremental 5G and broadband expansion requires no major incremental spectrum or capex.
- International expansion will be asset-light, utilizing proprietary technology stacks (UBR and OSS/BSS) via joint ventures and partnerships.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at ~Rs. 59,000 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Marketable cash and securities stood at ~Rs. 50,000 crore, resulting in net debt of Rs. 8,822 crore (leverage of 0.1x).
- Post-IPO proceeds (Rs. 30,188 crore), the company is net debt positive.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted that technology leadership has a time domain, though they believe they maintain a 2.5 to 3-year lead over competition in UBR/FWA technologies.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) on Go-to-Market strategy for digital services, international partnerships timeline, and capital allocation:** Management replied that digital scale is driven by single-customer OS views (like home/mobile ecosystems), international expansion will target 10-15 sizable partners via asset-light managed services, and capital allocation will prioritize high-ROI tech development and maintaining a strong balance sheet.
2. **Sachin Salgaonkar (BoFA Securities) on digital ARPU impact on margins, free cash flow outlook, and international tech traction:** Management stated that peak capex is behind them, driving higher FCF, and engagement is highest on UBR and full-stack OSS/BSS.
3. **Sumangal Nevatia (Kotak Securities) on external tariff hikes and macro drivers for ARPU:** Management responded that ARPU increases are being driven organically via mix and data usage (3.5% YoY growth), and tariff hike decisions will factor in market dynamics and macro conditions at the right time.
4. **Gaurav Rateria (Morgan Stanley) on international strategy and potential conflict of interest with telco partners:** Management clarified that they will not partner with domestic Indian telcos and will manage global conflicts via established partnership models akin to their Radisys subsidiary.
5. **Kunal Vora (BNP Paribas) on AI token monetization and Gemini subscription expiry:** Management noted that the 18-month Gemini free period is still early, and AI diffusion will initially happen broadly through a telecom harness rather than direct token charging.
6. **Manish Adukia (Goldman Sachs) on long-term financial KPI targets and areas where competitors outperform:** Management emphasized focusing on providing disproportionate value to customers, tracking market share, deployment scale, and ARPU uplift rather than being bound by a single metric.

### One-line takeaway
Jio Platforms delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 11.4% revenue growth, 250 bps margin expansion, and a net-debt-positive balance sheet following its landmark Rs. 30,188 crore IPO.
