# HFCL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Best ever quarterly performance with record revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, backed by a strong order book and multi-year visibility.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,541.79 crore (up 143.62% YoY, 32.73% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 658.00 crore (up 223.55% YoY, 47.78% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 25.89% (expansion of 640 bps YoY, 264 bps QoQ)
- **Consolidated PBT:** Rs 538.60 crore (up 406.49% YoY, 62.46% QoQ)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 394.22 crore (up 448.14% YoY, 60.49% QoQ)
- **PAT Margin:** 15.51% (expansion of 862 bps YoY, 268 bps QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 870.12 crore (up 1311.62% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 639.86 crore (up 1401.31% YoY)
- **Order Book:** Rs 28,900 crore
- **Export Growth:** Approximately 493% YoY

### Highlights
- Delivered the best-ever quarterly performance in company history.
- Export performance surged by approximately 493% YoY.
- Successfully delivered the second batch of Thermal Weapon Sights for AK-203 to the Indian Defence Forces in September; acquisition of aerospace business (with ~Rs 1,600 crore unexecuted order book) expected to complete in Q2.
- Allotted a 1,000-acre land parcel by the Government of Andhra Pradesh to set up an ammunition manufacturing complex.
- Evaluating a restructuring of the EPC business via a board committee and external advisors (E&Y), with a decision expected in a couple of weeks.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue growth of at least 80% for FY27 (up from earlier aspirations of ~20%, then 40%).
- Management stated they could reach the previously envisaged milestone of Rs 10,000 crore revenue by FY29 much earlier than projected.
- Defence and Aerospace revenue guidance: Rs 500-600 crore for FY27, at least Rs 2,000 crore next year, and an aspiration of Rs 5,000 crore in 3 years.
- Planned capex of approximately Rs 1,800 crore across optical fiber, cable, and preform manufacturing.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong acceleration in demand across AI infrastructure, hyperscale data centers, and telecom networks.
- Hyperscaler orders account for roughly 70% of export orders.
- Management does not see any slowdown in data center builds due to U.S. interest rate hikes, viewing AI and data centers as absolute necessities.
- Telecom operators, who had slowed buying due to past price shocks, are returning with concrete inquiries and large order possibilities.
- Global demand expected to pick up in Europe, Asia, and Southeast Asia.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin profile is expected to improve due to a higher mix of connectivity solutions, specialized high-fiber capacity cables, and backward integration.
- Long-term contracts protect against immediate raw material (preform) cost spikes, though spot preform prices were termed "insane" due to non-availability.
- In-house preform manufacturing is slated to start construction soon, with own preform availability targeted for late 2028 (and a midterm solution starting in CY2025/first quarter of next calendar year).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Optical fiber capacity expanding from 28 million to 38 million, and subsequently to 43 million fiber km.
- Cable capacity expanding from 39 million to 56 million, and subsequently to 63 million fiber km.
- Preform manufacturing capacity expanding to 600 metric tonnes, targeting operation by October 2028.
- Setting up an ammunition manufacturing complex in Andhra Pradesh.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt reduction is expected to start in the current year, with debt substantially declining by March 2027 and March 2028.
- EPC segment reported losses due to expenses incurred on BharatNet without milestone billings and pending BSNL/Army AMC approvals; management expects profitability in the EPC segment by Q1 of next year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global geopolitical situations remain outside management's control and could impact operations.
- Delays in milestone verifications and long bureaucratic approvals for BharatNet/EPC billings cause temporary working capital mismatches and reported segment losses.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pranav Kshatriya (Emkay):** Asked about hyperscaler customer concentration in the Rs 18,500 crore OFC/data center order book. Management stated ~70% of export orders are from hyperscalers (client names under NDA) and that 80%+ revenue guidance is intentionally conservative without preform supply bottlenecks.
- **Ashutosh Kumar Jha (Balyasny):** Inquired about margin sustainability and U.S. rate hike impacts on data centers. Management reiterated confidence in expanding margins via connectivity solutions and stated AI necessity overrides interest rate concerns, supported by emerging European and Asian demand.
- **Chinmay Sapre (Schonfeld):** Asked about preform cost reset frequencies. Management noted contracts are yearly or 18-24 months, and long-term customer contracts include annual price reset clauses.
- **Riken (Capri Family Office):** Asked about the path to profitability for the EPC segment. Management explained that losses stem from unbilled BharatNet expenses and pending BSNL AMC contract approvals, and expects profitability by Q1 of next year.
- **Tej Patel (Niveshaay):** Asked about passing on raw material price hikes. Management noted that short-term contracts (4-6 months) lack reset clauses but carry higher initial pricing, whereas long-term contracts have reset clauses to balance margins.

### One-line takeaway
HFCL reported its best-ever quarterly performance driven by a 143% surge in revenue and robust demand from hyperscalers, alongside ambitious scaling in its optical connectivity and newly prioritized defense segments.
