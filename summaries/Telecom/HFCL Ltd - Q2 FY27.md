# HFCL Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly revenues and profits driven by robust international optical cable demand, hyperscaler contracts, and expanding EBITDA margins.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,541.79 crore, up 143.62% YoY and 32.73% QoQ.
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 538.60 crore, up 406.49% YoY and 62.46% QoQ.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 394.22 crore, up 448.14% YoY and 60.49% QoQ.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 658.00 crore, up 223.55% YoY and 47.78% QoQ.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 25.89%, up 640 bps YoY and 264 bps QoQ.
- **PAT Margin:** 15.51%, up 862 bps YoY and 268 bps QoQ.
- **Exports Growth:** ~493% YoY.
- **Order Book:** Rs 28,900 crore.

### Highlights
- Best-ever quarterly performance in HFCL's history across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT.
- Order book increased to Rs 28,900 crore (including Rs 18,500 crore in OFC and data center interconnect, with ~70% of export orders coming from hyperscalers).
- Delivered second batch of Thermal Weapon Sights for AK-203 to Indian Defence Forces in September.
- Connectivity solutions expected to deliver over Rs 800 crore revenue in FY27 (Rs 300 crore done in Q2 FY27; Q1 was nil) and scale to Rs 2,000 crore in FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Growth Guidance:** Raised to at least 80% YoY (previously revised to 40% after Q1 FY27, from initial 20%).
- **Long-term Target:** Management expects to achieve its Rs 10,000 crore annual revenue milestone well before the earlier target of FY29.
- **Defence & Aerospace Revenue:** Projected at Rs 500-600 crore in FY27, ~Rs 2,000 crore in FY28, and aspiring for Rs 5,000 crore in 3 years.
- **Margins:** Management expects overall margin profile to improve further from current levels (~26%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust demand across AI infrastructure, hyperscale data centers, and global telecom networks.
- North American data center demand remains the strongest driver; management expects demand expansion across Europe, Asia, and Southeast Asia to follow.
- Global telecom operators are resuming optical fiber purchases after two years of low buying.
- Hyperscaler revenue currently represents 65%-75% of optical connectivity revenue, while telcos represent 25%-35%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins benefited from a favorable mix towards high-capacity optical connectivity products, data center cables (180-micron A2 fiber priced at $25-$27/km vs telco G.657B fiber at ~$15/km), and operational leverage.
- Preform spot market prices reached unsustainable levels (~$300/kg) due to tight availability, but HFCL is protected via long-term supply contracts.
- Germanium supplies for future preform manufacturing are under active negotiation with multiple vendors; germanium costs constitute a low percentage of overall cable production costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Total Capex Program:** Planned investment of ~Rs 1,800 crore across optical fiber, cable, and preform manufacturing.
- **Fiber Capacity:** Expanding from 28 million fiber km to 38 million, and subsequently to 43 million fiber km.
- **Cable Capacity:** Expanding from 39 million fiber km to 56 million, and subsequently to 63 million fiber km.
- **Preform Facility:** Doubling planned preform capacity from 300 metric tonnes to 600 metric tonnes (commissioning target October 2028).
- **Defence Manufacturing:** Setting up an ammunition manufacturing complex on 1,000 acres allotted by the Andhra Pradesh government.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt repayments are ongoing for legacy facilities; net debt levels are expected to decline substantially by March 2027 and March 2028 despite ongoing capex.
- Turnkey EPC segment reported losses (Rs 121 crore in Q2 FY27) due to unbilled work on BharatNet pending multi-step approval milestones; management expects segment profitability by Q1 FY28.
- Army AMC O&M contract under final approval stages with BSNL, which will offset EPC overhead costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties affecting global supply chains and international trade.
- Multi-step approval processes and milestone-based billing delays in domestic EPC/turnkey contracts.
- Delay in preform machinery deliveries (2-3 months delay noted, but factored into timelines).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Is there any capacity constraint or supply challenge causing conservative H2 guidance despite the 80%+ full-year growth target?**  
  *A:* Management clarified there are no preform or supply challenges; management prefers maintaining conservative publicly stated expectations.
- **Q: How will the Defence and Aerospace segment scale up to meet the Rs 500+ crore FY27 target?**  
  *A:* Deliveries of electro-optic sights, upcoming Foliage Penetration Radar orders, and consolidation of the acquired aerospace business (holding a Rs 1,600 crore order book) in Q3 FY27 will drive the ramp-up.
- **Q: What caused the EBITDA loss in the EPC segment during Q2 FY27?**  
  *A:* Billing delays due to sequential 25-step verification processes for BharatNet milestones despite incurring expenses, alongside pending AMC contract sign-offs by BSNL. Combined segment losses over H2 FY27 are expected to drop to Rs 75-100 crore before turning profitable in Q1 FY28.
- **Q: What is the status of the strategic restructuring of the EPC business?**  
  *A:* A board committee with external advisors (E&Y) is finalizing recommendations, with a board decision expected within two weeks of the call.
- **Q: How will high market prices of preform affect profitability once global capacities ramp up?**  
  *A:* Current spot preform prices of ~$300/kg reflect temporary non-availability rather than baseline economics. HFCL's margins are protected by fixed multi-year supply contracts, and internal preform capacity (600 MT) will meet ~50% of requirements by late 2028.

### One-line takeaway
HFCL is rapidly transforming into a global high-margin data-center connectivity and defence player, raising FY27 revenue growth guidance to 80%+ with expanding EBITDA margins.
