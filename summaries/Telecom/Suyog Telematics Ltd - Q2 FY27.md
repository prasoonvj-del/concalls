# Suyog Telematics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited execution dependencies on customer rollout plans (specifically Vodafone Idea and BSNL delays), though they expect a pickup in H2.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained a target of ~2,000 site deployments / Rs 200 crore CapEx for H2, though final guidance from operators remains awaited.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to a more measured pace as BSNL billing issues persist and operator rollouts depend heavily on their internal funding tranches.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Zinc battery testing began on 4 sites (down from the 10-15 targeted last quarter); BSNL stuck CapEx quantified at ~Rs 30 crore (approx. 300 sites).
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The target to reach a Rs 100 crore quarterly revenue run-rate by Q4 has been pushed out to "by next September" (September 2027), while zinc battery deployment was limited to 4 sites for initial monitoring.

### Key numbers
- **Total Towers:** 6,263 towers as of Q2 exit
- **Total Tenancies:** 7,674 tenancies (sponsored tenancies at 4,129; 20+ government site tenancies in H1)
- **Fibre Span:** 6,510 km across India (200+ km laid in H1)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 71.7 crore (Rs 717 million) in Q2 FY27
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 43.1 crore (Rs 431 million)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 60.0% in Q2 FY27
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 15.3 crore (Rs 153 million)
- **PAT Margin:** 21.4%
- **EPS:** Rs 13.09
- **Revenue per Tower:** Touch of ~Rs 31,000 per month
- **Revenue Share by Operator (H1 FY27):** Bharti Airtel 48.5%, Vodafone Idea 30.0%, Jio 19.5%, BSNL 1.1%
- **Capex Guidance / Plan:** Rs 200 crore planned for H2 FY27 (funded via Rs 120 crore internal accruals and Rs 80+ crore ICICI Bank loan)
- **BSNL Stuck Capex:** ~Rs 30 crore across ~300 sites pending billing integration

### Highlights
- Vodafone Idea contribution to revenue increased by 3% compared to the previous quarter to reach 30%.
- BSNL revenue contribution declined from 2.5% to 1.1% as management strategically paused adding BSNL towers to active tenancy/revenue counts until billing commences.
- Tested domestic zinc batteries on 4 sites and are monitoring results for a month before scaling.
- Continuing record: Unlike past quarters of strong bullishness on BSNL, the call highlighted ongoing friction with BSNL/Tejas equipment issues, with ~300 BSNL sites remaining unbilled.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets ~2,000 site deployments (Rs 200 crore CapEx) in H2 FY27, subject to monthly and quarterly customer plans.
- Aiming to achieve an annualized quarterly revenue run-rate of Rs 100 crore by next September (September 2027).
- Vodafone Idea has planned 45,000 site rollouts over the next 3 years; Suyog targets executing around 5,000 of these sites.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Vodafone Idea is investing a Rs 45,000 crore CapEx over the next 18-24 months and is currently working with Suyog on 2,000+ additional sites across India.
- BSNL has declared a rollout of 16,000 sites by March 2027 (4,000 in Mumbai, 6,000 in Delhi, 6,000 rest of India), though Suyog has excluded them from near-term projections until billing starts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin maintained at a strong 60.0% in Q2 FY27.
- Rental expenses for unbilled BSNL sites (approx. 300 sites / Rs 30 crore CapEx) have been put on hold with landowners to protect books from cash hits.
- MSAs feature 15-20 year terms with 2.5% in-built escalations on IP fees and loading charges.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H2 FY27 CapEx budgeted at Rs 200 crore, funded via Rs 120 crore internal accruals and an Rs 80+ crore sanctioned credit facility from ICICI Bank.
- Company can execute 1,000+ sites per month given field teams and funding are in place, but execution strictly follows customer service orders (zero CapEx invested without a confirmed order).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents on balance sheet remain tight (around Rs 8-9 crore as per participant notes).
- Rs 80+ crore debt sanctioned from ICICI Bank specifically for H2 CapEx.
- Advance tax paid for the first half stands at Rs 1.5 crore (net of TDS).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Execution and billing are entirely dependent on customer financial planning and rollout schedules (e.g., Vodafone Idea funding tranches and BSNL equipment/backbone delays).
- ~300 BSNL sites (approx. Rs 30 crore CapEx) are integrated as RFI but stuck without billing due to Tejas equipment and fibre/line-of-sight issues.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kaustubh Bubna questioned the high CapEx relative to slow tenancies and BSNL/Vodafone execution risks.** Management clarified that CapEx is strictly deployed only after receiving a confirmed service order, protected by 7–10 year lock-ins and exit clauses under MSAs, though acknowledging delays in BSNL billing.
2. **Ankush Modi asked why Vodafone's 600+ June orders translated to only ~206 tenancies in Q2.** Management explained that while orders are issued in bulk, actual site delivery and RFI deployment depend on Vodafone's monthly fund planning and site prioritization to avoid delayed billing loops.
3. **Saket Kapoor questioned the timeline of reaching the Rs 100 crore quarterly revenue target.** Management clarified that the top-line target of Rs 100 crore per quarter is now expected to be achieved by next September (September 2027) as quarterly additions scale up.
4. **Neeraj Chhajer asked about the status of BSNL-stuck CapEx and rental liabilities.** Management stated that ~Rs 30 crore (approx. 300 sites) is stuck awaiting BSNL integration, and rental payouts to landowners for these specific sites have been put on hold to prevent P&L hits.
5. **Varun Ghia questioned the discrepancy between multi-quarter CapEx and lower tenancy additions.** Management reiterated that past CapEx went into Airtel site upgrades (pushing revenue per tower to ~Rs 31,000), while current CapEx goes into Vodafone rollouts and unbilled BSNL sites.

### One-line takeaway
Suyog Telematics reported stable Q2 FY27 performance with a 60% EBITDA margin and a revenue of Rs 71.7 crore, keeping its H2 growth pinned on 2,000 targeted Vodafone Idea site deployments while pausing unbilled BSNL rollouts.
