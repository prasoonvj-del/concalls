# Tejas Networks Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Improved financial scale, strong order book visibility, and commencement of BSNL 4G expansion shipments.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 406 crore (vs Rs 262 crore YoY, up 55%; vs Rs 402 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 808 crore (vs Rs 464 crore in H1 FY26, up ~74%)
- **EBITDA:** Negative Rs 108 crore (vs negative Rs 293 crore in Q2 FY26; vs negative Rs 99 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT:** Negative Rs 207 crore (vs negative Rs 307 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Order Book:** Rs 2,315 crore (vs Rs 1,529 crore in Q1 FY27); mix is 96% India, 4% international.
- **Revenue Mix:** 88% India, 12% international.
- **Net Working Capital:** Lower at Rs 4,235 crore (compared to Q1 FY27).
- **Inventory:** Rs 2,314 crore (vs Rs 2,358 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 4,384 crore (vs Rs 4,277 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Global Patents:** 748 total (266 in 5G Advanced and 6G standards), with 26 patents filed in Q2.

### Highlights
- Received LOI and subsequent POs for 18,685-plus 4G sites for BSNL 4G network expansion, initiating initial shipments in Q2.
- Commenced initial shipments of end-to-end 5G mobile network solutions to an international customer in South America.
- Received an LOI from a new System Integration (SI) partner for IP/MPLS routers to upgrade BharatNet middle mile in 3 states.
- Won a pan-India strategic OTN network order for a Government of India enterprise and secured orders for 400-gig optical equipment from a new Tier 1 pan-African telecom operator.
- Launched a new logo featuring the Tata Endorsement Mark at India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2026; inaugurated a next-generation connectivity lab; demonstrated 6G sensing technology with Qualcomm.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a significant amount of collections from BSNL in the current quarter connected to achieved milestone deliveries.
- Management expects a significant portion of high inventory to be utilized in executing the BSNL 4G order in coming quarters.
- Management projects both inventory and gross debt to come down significantly with the execution of the BSNL expansion order.
- Management stated a visible path to profitability in upcoming years, driven by steady financial performance improvements.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Wireless growth drivers include emerging market 5G deployments (extending to 2030), global 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) subscriptions tripling to ~90 million by 2030, domestic 4G expansion via BSNL, and AI driving 6G commercialization (demanding massive MIMO and AI-native basebands/cores).
- Wireline drivers include AI data centers driving generational upgrades of access/aggregation/metro backbone networks, enterprise/residential broadband growth, 5G backhaul build-outs, and utility network modernization.
- Engaging with several Tier 1 mobile network operators for custom 5G radio development (custom bands and high-power radios).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- High inventory levels are attributed to build-up for the BSNL expansion order, which will be utilized and worked down as deliveries progress.
- No recurring provisioning/losses of the kind seen previously (such as contract manufacturing process losses from the first 100,000-site order) were reported for the current quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Net debt increased to Rs 4,384 crore due to continued investments in capex per plan and funding operational costs.
- Expanding the 5G radio access network portfolio across custom international bands (700 MHz to 4.9 GHz) and developing a compact baseband unit supporting up to 5 frequency bands in 1 site.
- Investing in 5G Advanced, 6G, AI RAN, and low-latency access platforms at 200 gig and beyond.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital reduced to Rs 4,235 crore due to lower trade receivables from increased collections and non-recourse bill discounting/customer advance settlements.
- Inventory slightly decreased to Rs 2,314 crore.
- Net debt rose to Rs 4,384 crore (from Rs 4,277 crore in Q1 FY27) driven by capex and operational cost funding.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management as major risks, though operational focus remains on timely delivery of the BSNL expansion order, inventory reduction, and collections.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tushar Khurana (Peace Wealth) on BSNL collections:** Management stated milestone delivery requirements are met, and substantial collections will be realized this quarter, reducing receivables significantly. *(Answered clearly regarding milestone timelines)*
2. **Tushar Khurana on international perception vs. domestic private telco penetration:** Management explained that private Indian operators finished major 4G/5G builds earlier, so Tejas is positioning its products via POCs and trials for the next expansion phase, while targeting emerging markets and partnering with global giants (like NEC/Rakuten) internationally. *(Answered straightforwardly)*
3. **Keyurkumar Vadaliya (Niveshaay) on inventory/debt reduction timelines:** Management declined to give exact quantum numbers but reiterated that inventory and debt will reduce significantly over the next few quarters as BSNL shipments execute and collections are realized. *(Answered with directional visibility)*
4. **Tushar Khurana on DCI stack and GPU vendor onboarding:** Management clarified that they partner with system integrators building entire racks (compute/networking) rather than directly with GPU vendors, focusing largely on data center interconnect (DCI) and enterprise-to-data-center connectivity. *(Answered directly)*
5. **Tushar Khurana on the NEC partnership:** Management confirmed they licensed massive MIMO 5G radio technology from NEC, are supplying it to previous and new customers, and act as a radio development partner for future generations of NEC radios. *(Answered without commenting on NEC's revenue figures)*

### One-line takeaway
Tejas Networks reported Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs 406 crore with a net loss of Rs 207 crore, while anticipating significant inventory reduction and debt lowering through the execution of its expanded BSNL 4G order.
