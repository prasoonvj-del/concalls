# Vodafone Idea Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Continued progress in operating parameters with accelerated capex, revenue growth of 6.2% YoY, and significantly reduced prepaid churn.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained three-year capex plan of Rs. 45,000 Crore.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to an accelerated execution phase ("FY27 is the year of execution") with a material doubling of quarterly capex and sharp reduction in prepaid subscriber churn.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Launched industry-first free international roaming on key plans and India's first prepaid family plan; M2M/postpaid focus expanded into enterprise private networks (Hindalco). Supply-side geopolitical risks from Q1 did not feature as a constraint this quarter.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Capex more than doubled sequentially to Rs. 3,946 Crore in Q2 FY27 (from Rs. 1,930 Crore in Q1), meeting the step-up promise; network rollout reached nearly 9,000 new broadband towers in the quarter.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs. 11,884 Crore (up 6.2% YoY, 1.7% QoQ)
- EBITDA: Rs. 4,981 Crore (up 6.3% YoY); EBITDA margin: 41.9%
- Cash EBITDA: Rs. 2,498 Crore (up 11.2% YoY); Cash EBITDA margin: 21.0% (up over 95 bps YoY)
- Customer ARPU: Rs. 198 (up 9.7% YoY)
- Total subscriber base: 194.4 million (up from 131.0 million 4G/5G subscribers; total broadband towers close to 214,000)
- Total data usage: 93.6 Petabyte/day (up 27.1% YoY)
- Capex for Q2 FY27: Rs. 3,946 Crore (H1 FY27 capex nearly Rs. 6,000 Crore; cumulative orders placed over Rs. 13,200 Crore)
- Bank debt: Rs. 943 Crore as of September 30, 2026 (legacy bank debt fully repaid subsequently; drawdowns represent incremental funding)
- Free cash and bank balance: Rs. 5,470 Crore as of September 30, 2026
- Exceptional loss: Rs. 462 Crore on account of mark-to-market treasury assets; Net loss adjusted for exceptional items: Rs. 5,513 Crore

### Highlights
- Added nearly 9,000 new broadband towers in Q2 FY27 (highest quarterly addition post-FY24); 4G population coverage reached 88%, and 5G live in 400 towns.
- Secured over Rs. 10,500 Crore in total funding till H1 FY27, including Rs. 4,000 Crore of incremental bank funding arranged in Q2 FY27.
- Launched industry-first free international roaming on key prepaid/postpaid plans and India’s first prepaid family plan.
- Deployed a dedicated full-stack private network for Hindalco Industries at Baphlimali Bauxite Mine via Vi Business.
- Paid spectrum dues of over Rs. 4,300 Crore in October 2026 (totaling over Rs. 6,000 Crore out of ~Rs. 7,000 Crore due in FY27).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track to conclude 4G network rollout by FY28 to gain coverage parity in 17 key circles.
- Management expects the ARPU growth trend to resume from next quarter, supported by underlying customer engagement and premiumization.
- Marketing costs expected to remain at Q2 levels for the remainder of FY27 due to ongoing brand refresh campaigns.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprise segment (Vi Business) expanding into IoT, cloud, intelligent digital infrastructure, and managed security services, shifting from 'sell and build' to 'build and sell' for fixed-line connectivity.
- Sequential net subscriber churn for prepaid reduced significantly from 1.5 million in Q1 FY27 to 0.6 million in Q2 FY27. Postpaid net additions hit the highest level since FY24 (total base including M2M at 33.9 million).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cash EBITDA margin improved by over 95 bps YoY to 21.0% despite adding over 22,500 unique broadband towers over the last 12 months.
- Reported EBITDA margin impacted sequentially by phasing of personnel costs and higher marketing expenses for brand refresh initiatives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q2 FY27 capex doubled run-rate compared to Q1 FY27 to Rs. 3,946 Crore.
- Total capex orders placed exceed Rs. 13,200 Crore against the 3-year guidance of Rs. 45,000 Crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Legacy bank debt fully repaid as of the call date; outstanding bank debt of Rs. 943 Crore as of September 30, 2026, comprises new incremental drawdowns.
- Discussions ongoing with SBI-led public sector bank consortium, Indian private banks, and foreign banks for the broader Rs. 35,000 Crore facility (Rs. 25,000 Crore funded, Rs. 10,000 Crore non-funded).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Seasonality in Q2 slightly moderated organic ARPU growth compared to prior quarters.
- Multi-lender involvement in the public sector bank consortium is causing the funding process to take longer than previously anticipated.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) asked about prepaid churn sustainability and ARPU growth without tariff hikes.** Management stated prepaid churn dropped to 0.6 million and expects positive momentum; ARPU growth will be driven organically by data upgrades and premiumization.
- **Vivekanand Subbaraman (Jefferies) asked about the headroom in B2B monetization and the timeline/structure of bank funding drawdowns.** Management noted enterprise focus is shifting to fixed-line and private networks, and drawdowns will occur dynamically as non-funded limits (letters of credit) are utilized.
- **Balaji Subramanian (IIFL) asked about the risk of down-trading from the new Rs. 185 voice-only plan and spectrum renewals in December.** Management noted minimal initial disruption from the voice-only plan with some migration to Rs. 199, and confirmed ongoing engagement with DoT for spectrum renewals across 8 circles.
- **Aditya Bansal (Axis Capital) questioned network opex trends and the net impact of voice-only plans.** Management clarified network costs show an absolute increase inline with rollouts, and initial results on voice-only plans show users migrating from lower Rs. 179/189 tiers rather than downgrading from Rs. 199.

### One-line takeaway
Vodafone Idea delivered a quarter of accelerated capex (Rs. 3,946 crore), a 6.2% YoY revenue increase, and a sharp decline in prepaid churn, backed by ongoing fund-raising efforts.
