# Vodafone Idea Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong execution with doubled quarterly capex, record tower additions, and narrowing prepaid churn, offset by delayed PSU loan closure and exceptional mark-to-market treasury losses.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall 3-year capex guidance of Rs. 45,000 Crore and execution pace of 3,000–3,500 4G towers per month.
- Tone and demand view: Focus shifted to network execution intensity and operational momentum; prepaid net subscriber churn improved substantially (swing of ~0.9 million QoQ), though sequential ARPU growth moderated due to Q2 seasonality.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Introduced brand refresh ('Vi pe sab hain VIP') with Shah Rukh Khan, free international roaming on core plans, Rs. 185 voice-only plans, and a private 5G network deployment at Hindalco. Recorded a new exceptional loss of Rs. 462 Crore from mark-to-market remeasurement of treasury assets. Previous supply-side geopolitical constraints were not mentioned as an issue.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Capex execution accelerated significantly as promised, doubling run-rate QoQ to Rs. 3,946 Crore and adding nearly 9,000 broadband towers in Q2.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs. 11,884 Crore (up 6.2% YoY, up 1.7% QoQ).
- EBITDA: Rs. 4,981 Crore (up 6.3% YoY); EBITDA margin: 41.9% (stable YoY).
- Cash EBITDA: Rs. 2,498 Crore (up 11.2% YoY); Cash EBITDA margin: 21.0% (up 95 bps YoY).
- Adjusted Loss (ex-exceptional items): Rs. 5,513 Crore vs Rs. 5,562 Crore in Q2 FY26 (Exceptional item loss of Rs. 462 Crore recorded due to MTM of treasury assets).
- Customer ARPU: Rs. 198 (up 9.7% YoY).
- Total subscriber base: 194.4 million (up 1.3 million QoQ from 193.1 million).
- 4G/5G subscriber base: 131.0 million (up from 127.8 million in Q2 FY26).
- Postpaid & M2M subscriber base: 33.9 million (highest sequential human postpaid net adds since FY24).
- Total data usage: 93.6 Petabyte/day (up 27.1% YoY from 73.6 Petabyte/day).
- Capex: Rs. 3,946 Crore in Q2 FY27 (~Rs. 6,000 Crore in H1 FY27; >Rs. 13,200 Crore orders placed against Rs. 45,000 Crore 3-year target).
- Broadband towers added: Nearly 9,000 in Q2 FY27 (total unique broadband towers: ~214,000; 4G population coverage at 88%).
- Bank debt: Rs. 943 Crore as of September 30, 2026 (legacy bank debt fully repaid; balance represents new drawdowns).
- Free cash and bank balance: Rs. 5,470 Crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Spectrum payments: >Rs. 4,300 Crore paid in October 2026 (>Rs. 6,000 Crore paid out of ~Rs. 7,000 Crore FY27 obligations).

### Highlights
- Net subscriber additions reached 1.3 million QoQ, driven by M2M and postpaid growth; prepaid net subscriber churn dropped from 1.5 million in Q1 FY27 to 0.6 million in Q2 FY27.
- Unveiled refreshed brand identity moving the "Vi" dot to the top, backed by brand ambassador Shah Rukh Khan and the "Vi pe sab hain VIP" campaign.
- Launched differentiated products: integrated free international roaming into key plans, launched India's first prepaid family data-sharing plan, introduced Rs. 200 OTT pack, iD+ verified calling, and Vi Business WhatsApp.
- Enterprise (Vi Business): Deployed full-stack private 5G network at Hindalco’s Baphlimali Bauxite Mine in Odisha; expanded data center and NLD capacity; partnered with AWS, Cisco, and Meta.
- Continuing record: Net loss remained high at over Rs. 5,900 Crore reported, continuing the loss-making trajectory despite revenue and Cash EBITDA expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Target to complete 4G network rollout by FY28 to achieve coverage parity across 17 key circles, maintaining a rollout speed of 3,000–3,500 unique 4G towers/month.
- Management expects organic ARPU growth momentum to resume in Q3 FY27, targeting a ~10% ARPU increase over upcoming quarters through subscriber upgrades.
- Marketing spend is expected to stay at elevated Q2 FY27 levels for the remainder of FY27 due to ongoing brand refresh efforts.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management emphasized that the Indian telecom industry requires a structural tariff increase and confirmed its intention to participate.
- Enterprise growth is being driven by migration from traditional connectivity to IoT, cloud, intelligent digital infrastructure, and managed services.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin was sequentially subdued due to the phasing of personnel costs and higher marketing spending for the brand refresh.
- Network cost increased by Rs. 99 Crore QoQ as rollout pace accelerated.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q2 capex doubled QoQ to Rs. 3,946 Crore; 5G services expanded to 400 cities and towns.
- Enterprise strategy shifted from "sell and build" to "build and sell" for fixed-line connectivity and digital solutions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total funding secured reached >Rs. 10,500 Crore by H1 FY27, including Rs. 4,000 Crore of incremental bank funding arranged in Q2 FY27.
- The SBI-led public sector bank consortium loan process (Rs. 35,000 Crore facility) is taking longer than anticipated due to multiple lenders, but management expects closure soon.
- Borrowings rely initially on non-funded facilities (letters of credit) before converting into funded bank loan drawdowns.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in concluding the multi-lender PSU bank consortium financing.
- Ongoing earnings volatility due to quarterly mark-to-market fair value adjustments of treasury shares (Rs. 462 Crore adverse impact in Q2).
- Base churn increased 20 bps QoQ to 4.0% (though down 50 bps YoY).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities):** *How will non-M2M subscribers and ARPU grow without near-term tariff hikes?*  
  **Management:** Human SIM net churn improved by over 1 million subscribers QoQ. Organic ARPU growth will continue (~10% over coming quarters) by upgrading 2G/voice customers to data/Nonstop Hero packs and premiumizing postpaid.
- **Vivekanand Subbaraman (Ambit/BofA):** *Why doesn't the Rs. 4,000 Crore H1 bank funding show up in bank debt, and what is the PSU loan structure?*  
  **Management:** Funding initially uses non-funded facilities (letters of credit) for capex, which will convert to bank debt upon drawdown (higher drawdowns coming in Q3). PSU bank consortium funding is close to closure, and funds will be drawn down as needed rather than all upfront.
- **Balaji Subramanian (IIFL):** *Is there down-trading risk from the new Rs. 185 voice plan, and how will expiring December 2026 spectrum be handled without auctions?*  
  **Management:** Early data shows minimal disruption from the Rs. 185 plan, with some users upgrading to Rs. 199 plans. For the 8 circles with expiring spectrum (e.g., Bihar), representation was submitted to DoT to continue usage without service disruption.
- **Aditya Bansal (Prabhudas Lilladher):** *Why did below-EBITDA lease network opex decline sequentially, and will entry-level voice plans drag ARPU?*  
  **Management:** Absolute network costs grew by Rs. 99 Crore QoQ across reported and cash EBITDA metrics. Premiumization efforts are expected to comfortably outweigh any drag from entry-level voice plans.

### One-line takeaway
Vodafone Idea doubled its quarterly capex to Rs. 3,946 Crore and expanded Cash EBITDA margin to 21.0% while navigating pending PSU consortium loan approvals and treasury mark-to-market headwinds.
