# Indus Towers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong co-location additions, improving tenancy ratio, and steady operational and financial performance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None provided in either quarter (maintained management stance of strong order book visibility).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more optimistic regarding execution, driven by accelerating rollouts from a major customer and initial deployments in Africa.
- New topics or risks: Severe floods in Northeast, Bihar & Jharkhand, and UP East affecting network operations; launch of Safety Shield Awards. Old risk of West Asia conflict impacting battery supplies was not mentioned.
- Last quarter's promises: Promised Africa rollouts would commence in Q2 and scale in H2; delivered as towers were deployed in Zambia and Uganda in late Q2.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 8,520 crore (up 4% YoY, 1% QoQ)
- **Core rental revenue:** Rs 5,450 crore (up 3.9% YoY, 1.3% QoQ)
- **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 4,630 crore (up 2.5% QoQ; up 5.2% YoY excluding previous year's Rs 200 crore write-back)
- **EBITDA margin:** 54.4% (up 80 bps QoQ)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 1,790 crore (up 2.7% QoQ; up 6.4% YoY excluding previous year's write-back)
- **Free Cash Flow:** Rs 1,400 crore
- **Macro towers added:** ~2,900 (Total base: ~270,500, up 5.6% YoY)
- **Co-locations added:** ~7,900 (Total base: ~440,200, up 5.9% YoY; total co-locations including leaner sites: ~454,200)
- **Tenancy ratio:** Improved from 1.62 to 1.63
- **Energy margin:** -3.6% (vs -4.6% in Q1 and -4.8% in Q2 FY26)
- **Diesel consumption reduction:** 11% YoY
- **Solar site portfolio:** ~49,000 sites (added ~3,000 sites in Q2; total installed solar capacity: 297 MW)
- **Pre-tax ROCE (trailing 12 months):** 25.3%
- **Post-tax ROE (trailing 12 months):** 19.2%

### Highlights
- Strong growth in co-location additions driven by customer network expansion and a pickup in rollouts by a major customer.
- Frontline teams successfully restored services and ensured continuity amidst severe floods in Northeast, Bihar & Jharkhand, and UP East.
- Smart meters deployed on ~140,000 sites over the last three years, improving consumption visibility.
- Network uptime improved sequentially from 99.955% to 99.97%.
- Africa expansion commenced with initial tower deployments in Zambia and Uganda generating revenue in late Q2; deployments in Nigeria scheduled for Q3.
- CRISIL ESG rating improved from 57 to 63.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects growth momentum to continue given healthy order book visibility and accelerating rollout activity.

### Demand & industry commentary
- TRAI data indicates India's 5G subscriber base grew by 36 million in Q1 to >463 million; industry added ~30,000 5G BTSs in Q2, taking the total installed base to ~593,000.
- Total data volume increased 30% YoY in Q1 with average monthly usage per subscriber rising 18%; 5G usage grew 79% YoY, accounting for 44% of total data consumption.
- Deployment focus is shifting towards network densification as 5G networks mature.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Energy margins improved due to an 11% YoY reduction in diesel consumption, driven by solar deployments (~3,000 sites added in Q2), digital energy management, and scaling of lithium-ion batteries.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Growth capex reflects healthy order books and rising CWIP (increase of ~Rs 250 crore QoQ), along with tenancies, 5G upgrades, and solar installations.
- Africa expansion is progressing on schedule with early capex per tower remaining within plan.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Free cash flow remained robust at Rs 1,400 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe seasonal weather and floods disrupting site access and causing prolonged power outages in regional circles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sachin Salgaonkar (Bank of America):** Asked about tenancy mix and tenancy renewals. Management stated tenancy ratio will remain meaningful due to existing 1T tower base, while tenancy renewals are under discussion with no updates to share.
- **Kunal Vora (BNP Paribas):** Inquired if rental revenue growth will accelerate. Management stated revenue depends on tower/tenancy additions and full-quarter revenue recognition lags deployment.
- **Kunal Vora (BNP Paribas):** Asked about energy cost deflation and margins. Management attributed savings to structural initiatives (solar, lithium-ion batteries, AI planning) rather than margin commentary.
- **Bineet Banka (Nomura):** Questioned the discrepancy between growth capex and net tower additions. Management clarified that growth capex includes CWIP, tenancy upgrades, 5G upgrades, and solar deployments, making tower count division inaccurate.
- **Satinder Singh Bedi (Eon Infotech Limited):** Suggested optimizing capital structure via an InvIT or buyback given the net cash position and questioned long-term visibility. Management did not directly address the capital structure suggestion.

### One-line takeaway
Indus Towers delivered a steady Q2 FY27 performance marked by a jump in co-locations, an improved tenancy ratio of 1.63, lower diesel costs, and early revenue generation from its Africa expansion.
