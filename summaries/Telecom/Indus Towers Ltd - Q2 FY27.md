# Indus Towers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong co-location growth, improving tenancy ratio, energy margin improvement, Africa rollout commencement.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (qualitative outlook unchanged: strong order book visibility, Africa rollout on schedule)
- Tone and demand view: Similar positive tone; tenancy ratio improved from 1.62 to 1.63; energy margin improved significantly; Africa rollouts commenced as planned.
- New topics/risks: Tenancy renewal discussions ongoing with major customer; IBS market share gains highlighted; ESG rating improvement; flood resilience demonstrated. Battery supply constraints and LPG shortage (mentioned last quarter) not discussed.
- Last quarter's promises: Africa rollouts commenced in Zambia and Uganda (delivered); solar deployments continued (3,000 sites added); battery modernization progressing; order book visibility maintained.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 8,520 crore (85.2 billion), up 4% YoY, 1% QoQ
- Core rental revenue: Rs 5,450 crore (54.5 billion), up 3.9% YoY, 1.3% QoQ
- Reported EBITDA: Rs 4,630 crore (46.3 billion), up 2.5% QoQ; adjusted EBITDA up 5.2% YoY (excluding Rs 200 crore write-back in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 54.4%, up 80 bps QoQ
- PAT: Rs 1,790 crore (17.9 billion), up 2.7% QoQ; adjusted PAT up 6.4% YoY
- Free cash flow: Rs 1,400 crore (14 billion)
- Macro towers added: ~2,900 (total ~270,500, up 5.6% YoY)
- Co-locations added: ~7,900 (total ~440,200, up 5.9% YoY)
- Tenancy ratio: 1.63 (up from 1.62)
- Total co-locations including leaner sites: ~454,200
- Energy margin: -3.6% (vs -4.6% in Q1, -4.8% in Q2 FY26)
- Diesel consumption reduction: 11% YoY
- Solar sites added: ~3,000 (total 49,000; cumulative solar capacity 297 MW)
- Pre-tax ROCE (TTM): 25.3%
- Post-tax ROE (TTM): 19.2%
- Africa: Towers deployed in Zambia and Uganda in Q2; Nigeria deployment to start in Q3.

### Highlights
- Tenancy ratio improved to 1.63 driven by robust co-location additions (7,900) on existing tower base.
- Healthy order book visibility with accelerating rollouts from major customers; management expects growth momentum to continue.
- Energy margin improved significantly due to structural initiatives: 11% YoY diesel reduction, 49,000 solar sites, lithium-ion battery transition, digital/AI fuel optimization.
- Network uptime improved sequentially to 99.97% despite seasonal floods; frontline teams ensured service continuity.
- Africa expansion commenced with initial tower deployments in Zambia and Uganda; Nigeria rollout to begin in Q3; Airtel remains anchor tenant.
- IBS segment gaining market share for two consecutive years; deployments at India Mobile Congress and Vizag Airport.
- CRISIL ESG rating improved from 57 to 63; Safety Shield Awards launched; CSR impacted 11 million lives.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects growth momentum to continue supported by healthy order book and sustained customer rollouts.
- Africa rollouts on schedule; meaningful operational and financial metrics to be shared in subsequent quarters as scale builds.
- No specific numeric guidance for revenue, EBITDA, or capex provided.
- Tenancy renewal discussions with major customer ongoing; no incremental update.

### Demand & industry commentary
- TRAI data: India 5G subscriber base grew 36 million in Q1 FY27 to >463 million; industry added ~30,000 5G BTS in Q2, total installed base ~593,000.
- Data consumption trends: Total data volume up 30% YoY in Q1; average monthly usage per subscriber up 18% YoY; 5G usage up 79% YoY, now 44% of total data traffic (up from 32% prior year).
- Shift toward network densification to support higher traffic loads and emerging use cases.
- Favourable regulatory environment: RoW rules 2024 implementation, smart meter deployment on ~140,000 sites over three years, Green Energy Open Access framework strengthening.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Energy margin improved to -3.6% from -4.6% QoQ and -4.8% YoY.
- Power and fuel expenses up only 3% YoY vs tower growth of 6%, implying lower energy cost per tower.
- Structural cost initiatives: solar deployment (297 MW capacity), digital/AI-driven fuel planning, lithium-ion battery transition at scale.
- Escalation clauses in MSAs cover majority of inflation impact over longer horizon; capex rates adjusted per MSA where flexibility exists.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Growth capex increased QoQ; CWIP up ~Rs 250 crore QoQ reflecting healthy order book.
- Capex includes new towers, tenancy upgrades, 5G loading, solar installations, and battery modernization.
- Africa capex per tower within plan during initial deployments in Zambia and Uganda; Nigeria rollout starting Q3.
- No specific capex guidance for FY27 provided.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Free cash flow robust at Rs 1,400 crore (14 billion).
- Pre-tax ROCE (TTM) 25.3%; post-tax ROE (TTM) 19.2%.
- Net cash position maintained (analyst noted); no debt figures disclosed.
- Board committed to steady/progressive dividend distribution (per previous quarter).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Tenancy renewal negotiations with a major customer ongoing; outcome uncertain.
- Seasonal weather disruptions (floods in Northeast, Bihar, Jharkhand, UP East) impacted site access and power but managed effectively.
- Africa expansion still early stage; no material revenue contribution yet; anchor tenant concentration (Airtel).
- Inflation and currency depreciation could pressure opex/capex; escalation pass-through operates on longer horizon.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA) on tenancy growth mix:** Management clarified 5G is loading on same towers; tenancy growth driven by second tenant on existing sites; ratio depends on order book but remains healthy given large 1T tower base.
2. **Sachin Salgaonkar on tower agreement expiry and customer fundraising:** Management declined to speculate on customer funding; affirmed strong order book visibility for next quarters; tenancy renewal discussions ongoing, no update.
3. **Sachin Salgaonkar on Africa metrics:** Management stated deployments commenced late Q2 in Zambia and Uganda; not yet at scale; will share meaningful KPIs in subsequent quarters; Airtel remains primary anchor.
4. **Kunal Vora (BNP) on rental revenue growth acceleration:** Management cited timing mismatch between co-location additions and revenue recognition; one-off impact in prior year; growth driven by tower/tenancy deployments per order book.
5. **Kunal Vora on energy cost deflation:** Management attributed 11% diesel reduction to structural initiatives: solar (49k sites), digital/AI fuel planning, lithium-ion batteries; cost per unit improving sustainably.
6. **Bineet Banka (Nomura) on growth capex vs tower additions:** Management explained CWIP increase reflects order book; capex includes tenancy upgrades, 5G loading, solar, not just new towers; cost per tower varies by site type.
7. **Kunal Vora on Africa capex advantage:** Management cited engineering expertise, vendor ecosystem, and BOQ optimization from India scale as differentiators; not dependent on scale advantage.
8. **Kunal Vora on residual contract period:** Management confirmed all new contracts minimum 10-year tenure.
9. **Satinder Singh Bedi on low tower additions and leaner towers:** Management stated tower count driven by customer network plans; leaner tower deployments declining as customers shift to macro towers.
10. **Bineet Banka on IBS revenue:** Management does not disclose separately; IBS revenue very small part of rental revenue.

Indus Towers delivered strong Q2 FY27 with tenancy ratio improvement to 1.63, robust co-location additions, significant energy margin improvement, and commencement of Africa rollouts, backed by healthy order book visibility.
