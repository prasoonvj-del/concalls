# Bharti Hexacom Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong performance with steady revenue and EBITDA growth across mobile, homes, and digital businesses.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs. 2,510 crore (growing 4% QoQ)
- EBITDAaL: Rs. 1,210 crore
- EBITDAaL margin: 48.2%
- Mobile customer base: 29 million
- Net customer additions (mobile): ~210,000
- Smartphone customer additions: 344,000
- ARPU: Rs. 259
- Net adds in homes, office, and other businesses: 75,000 (revenue up 8% QoQ)
- Operating free cash flow (EBITDAaL minus capex): ~Rs. 830 crore
- Net debt excluding leases: ~Rs. 960 crore
- Net debt excluding leases to EBITDAaL ratio: 0.2

### Highlights
- Benefited from one extra day during the quarter.
- Homes, office, and other businesses maintained strong momentum with 8% sequential revenue growth.
- Strong adoption of smartphone and home broadband offerings.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management indicated capex will be directed towards 5G densification, network modernization, and growing homes and IPTV businesses.
- Management stated they are in a comfortable net debt to EBITDA position and will spend adequate money to drive competitive and profitable growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Competitive intensity in Rajasthan is extremely high, described as a competitively heated market, while Northeast has a comfortable competitive position.
- Terrains in both circles (Northeast and Rajasthan) are difficult, requiring a nuanced combination of wireless and wired line solutions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Energy costs lower on a YoY basis due to seasonal impact and solar-based tower benefits, alongside some minor one-offs.
- Diesel inventory in DG sets means full impact of diesel price changes is not immediate.
- IPTV rollout led to some increase in depreciation.
- CPE-based home business has un-leveraged initial costs until critical mass is reached; unit economics are expected to turn positive with scale.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex focused on 5G densification, fiberization, network modernization, and expanding the homes/IPTV business.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains robust with net debt excluding leases at ~Rs. 960 crore.
- Net debt excluding leases to EBITDAaL ratio improved to 0.2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Difficult terrains in Northeast and Rajasthan circles require unique operational solutions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vivekanand Subbaraman (Ambit Capital) on FWA/homes opportunity in Hexacom circles vs. parent:** Management answered that out of the 400 cities, around 15–17 fall in Hexacom circles, requiring nuanced deployment due to difficult terrains.
2. **Vivekanand Subbaraman (Ambit Capital) on competitive intensity in Rajasthan and Northeast:** Management stated Rajasthan is extremely competitive while Northeast enjoys a comfortable competitive position, but ground intensity is high across both.
3. **Aditya Bansal (Motilal Oswal) on energy costs and diesel impact:** Management explained solar generation and seasonal impact lower energy costs, with diesel inventory preventing instant full-quarter cost recognition.
4. **Sanjesh Jain on depreciation jump and home segment EBIT margins:** Management attributed the slight depreciation rise to an extra day and IPTV rollout, noting that home segment margins will turn positive once scale is reached.

### One-line takeaway
Bharti Hexacom delivered a solid Q1 FY27 with 4% QoQ revenue growth, robust cash generation, and disciplined capital allocation.
