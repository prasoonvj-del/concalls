# Sterlite Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever performance across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT for the second consecutive quarter, driven by strong data center momentum and order inflows.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,529 crore (up 145% YoY, 32% QoQ)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 4,439 crore (up 116% YoY)
- **Operational EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 685 crore (27.1% margin, up 624 bps QoQ)
- **Reported EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 846 crore (includes a one-time net gain of Rs 161 crore from a US tariff refund)
- **H1 Operational EBITDA:** Rs 1,082 crore (24.4% margin)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 517 crore
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 714 crore (up from Rs 14 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Open Order Book:** Rs 35,274 crore (up 4.6x from the start of the financial year; Rs 3,021 crore scheduled for Q3 FY27 execution)
- **Order Intake (H1 FY27):** Rs 32,000 crores (roughly 4x FY26 total)
- **Global ex-China OFC Market Share:** 9% in H1 FY27 (vs. 7% in H1 FY26)
- **Attach Rate:** All-time high of 24% in H1 FY27 (vs. 20% in H1 FY26)
- **Net Cash Balance:** Rs 703 crore (improved from Rs 483 crore in Q1)
- **Impairment / Depreciation:** Impairment of Rs 49 crore (Speedon Networks); additional depreciation of Rs 22 crore.

### Highlights
- Delivered record-breaking financial performance across revenue, operational EBITDA, and PAT for the second consecutive quarter.
- Secured a new 5-year agreement worth a potential $1.2 billion with a global hyperscaler for AI-ready optical solutions.
- Upgraded FY29 EBITDA margin target from 27% to about 30%, while maintaining the Rs 20,000 crore revenue target under the Lakshya growth roadmap.
- Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar plant transitioned to 100% green power, reducing core optical fiber carbon footprint by over 80%.
- CRISIL upgraded long-term credit rating to AA stable from AA-.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised the FY29 EBITDA margin target to about 30% (from 27% earlier) while maintaining the revenue target at Rs 20,000 crores.
- Capex commitment: ~Rs 1,000 crores per year through FY29 for expanding preform, fiber, and cable capacities by 50% and building a greenfield facility for pre-term solutions.
- Net working capital cycle targeted to remain in the range of 45 to 50 days.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CRU upgraded 2026 global optical fiber cable demand projections to 8% YoY (from 63% last quarter) and 2031 demand to 851 million fiber kilometers (7.5% CAGR).
- AI data centers driving massive fiber intensity (scale-out, scale-across, and scale-up); global data center capacity expected to grow from ~82 GW to ~219 GW by 2030 (>70% AI-specific).
- India's live DC capacity reached ~1.8 GW (with 258 MW added in H1 FY27), projected to reach 10.5 GW by 2031.
- Shift from spot orders to scheduled backlog under multiyear agreements with hyperscalers and telcos.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Underlying operational EBITDA margin reached 27.1% in Q2 FY27, aided by higher capacity utilization, a richer product/customer mix, and increased share of high-value optical connectivity.
- Raw material procurement constraints (germanium, helium) have eased, and supplier diversification has improved utilization rates.
- Finance costs impacted by penalties on prepayments of gross debt to reduce leverage; net finance costs expected to reduce from Q3 onwards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Brownfield expansion underway to increase preform, fiber, and cable capacities by 50%.
- Greenfield facility being built for pre-term related solutions, backed by an annual capex of ~Rs 1,000 crores through FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position improved to Rs 703 crore. Company remains net debt-free.
- Net working capital days expected to remain in the 45-50 day range.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Local execution challenges for data centers around land, power, water, and local opposition.
- Supply chain and payment term shifts due to current supply-constrained market conditions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Achal Lohade (Institutional Equities) asked about the drivers behind the FY29 margin upgrade and pricing/raw material trends.**
   *Management answered that the upgrade is driven by a stronger order book, improved product mix toward high-margin data centers, and a higher attach rate. They declined to comment on specific pricing, emphasizing a focus on high-end Neuralis and connectivity products.*
2. **Rabindra Nath Nayak (Nirmal Bang Securities) asked about the Rs 161 crore refund, impairment/depreciation details, finance cost increases, and receivable levels.**
   *Management stated that most refunds are received with a small Phase 3 application pending. The impairment and higher depreciation relate to subsidiary Speedon Networks (squared off in full). Finance costs rose due to prepayment penalties on gross debt, which will normalize from Q3.*
3. **Dave (Seven Rivers) asked about the sustainability of AI capex beyond FY29 if bond yields spike.**
   *Management responded that hyperscaler power contracts extend into 2031-2032, providing clarity that investments by major hyperscalers will continue well beyond 2030.*
4. **Abhay Joshi (Baroda BNP Paribas MF) asked about margin expectations for the rest of FY27 and order book mix.**
   *Management stated they aim to perform in line with or better than Q2 margins, aligned with the Lakshya roadmap. Order intake includes long-term contracts with telecom operators, neutral fiber players, and data center companies.*
5. **Aritra Banerjee (Nomura) asked about the commercialization timeline for Hollow-Core and Multi-Core fiber.**
   *Management stated that network trials are underway (e.g., UK and India), but broader mainstream industry scale-up and installation standardization will take 1.5 to 2 years.*

### One-line takeaway
STL delivered record-breaking Q2 FY27 performance with revenue up 145% YoY and operational EBITDA margins expanding to 27.1%, prompting management to raise its FY29 EBITDA margin target to 30%.
