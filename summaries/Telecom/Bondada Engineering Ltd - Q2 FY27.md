# Bondada Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported steady revenue growth, a strong order book, and strategic expansion into high-growth areas like BESS, defense, and digital infrastructure while maintaining margins.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 723.7 crore (up 10% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,415.3 crore (up 16% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 84 crore (up 8% YoY), margin at 11.6%
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 162.2 crore (up 12% YoY), margin at 11.5%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 57.4 crore (up 10% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 111.4 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **Consolidated Order Book (as of Oct 31, 2026):** Rs 10,445.8 crore
- **H1 FY27 Order Inflow:** Rs 4,663.6 crore (since April 2026)
- **Cash Deposits (as of Sep 30, 2026):** Rs 335.3 crore
- **Net Worth (as of Sep 30, 2026):** Rs 840 crore (vs Rs 730 crore as of Mar 31, 2026)
- **Consolidated Balance Sheet Size:** Close to Rs 2,500 crore

### Highlights
- Received listing approval from NSE and BSE for migration from SME platform to main boards.
- Commissioned ~335 MWp in H1 FY27 across Maharashtra and Gujarat, taking cumulative commissioned solar EPC capacity to ~1.7 GWp.
- Acquired a 60% controlling stake in Onix IPP Private Limited (225 MW AC solar project in Maharashtra under 25-year PPA with MSEDCL).
- Acquired a 75% stake in KCS Engineering Solutions (defence and aerospace engineering, design, and manufacturing).
- Incorporated PhotonicGrid Networks for fibre optic networks and allied telecom infrastructure.
- Entered into a non-binding MoU with Goa Investment Promotion and Facilitation Board for proposed investments of ~Rs 3,000 crore in floating solar and compressed biogas projects.
- CRISIL upgraded long-term credit rating to CRISIL A+/Stable (short-term affirmed at CRISIL A1); four product subsidiaries received CRISIL A ratings.
- *Record check:* Results slightly differ from initial quick-release numbers in our record (PAT reported at Rs 57.4 crore vs Rs 56 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 commissioning target set at a total of 1.5 GWp.
- Management expects H1 to account for ~35% of total targeted revenue and H2 to account for ~65%.
- Management expects another 55% to 60% top-line growth in FY27 over last year.
- Long-term revenue mix target by 2030 is 70% EPC and 30% from new technologies (data centres, defence, etc.).
- Expected debt by March 31, 2027: Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,500 crore (about 1.5x of equity), including term loans of Rs 750 crore to Rs 800 crore and working capital of Rs 480 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Total active bid pipeline stands at Rs 26,000 crore to Rs 27,000 crore (solar EPC, BESS EPC, defence, data centres, optical fibre).
- Expected order winning strike rate maintained at 20% to 22%.
- BESS order intake slowed down slightly over the past 6-7 months due to volatility in battery cell and imported battery prices, but expected to pick up.
- Exploring international markets like Saudi Arabia (GCC countries) and Africa for solar EPC.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins maintained in H1 FY27 due to advance budgeting and vendor orders placed long back.
- Management expects to maintain or improve EBITDA margins going forward, particularly with annuity-based assets capturing EPC margins as well.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 50% of land acquired for the 2 GW IPP project; execution planned in tranches of 200 MW, 300 MW, or max 500 MW at a time. Funding requirements to be clear by the end of FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased to ~Rs 360 crore as of September 30, 2026, entirely comprising working capital debt except for Rs 70-80 crore of emergency credit line term loans.
- No immediate equity fund-raising plans; existing capital and tied-up facilities sufficient for current order book.
- PSU and government customer payments received as per milestones; no major collection issues reported.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Land pooling delays for the 2 GW IPP project, a common industry bottleneck in India.
- Past volatility in battery cell and imported battery prices caused a temporary slowdown in BESS order intake.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raina Patel asked about the execution timeline of the current order book.** Management clarified that out of Rs 10,445 crore, ~Rs 2,000 crore is O&M (3-5 years) and ~Rs 1,400 crore is annuity (12 years). The remaining ~Rs 7,000 crore is to be executed over the next 24 months.
2. **Manan Dalal asked about peak debt levels and equity dilution.** Management stated total debt is expected to reach Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,500 crore by March 31, 2027, and stated equity raising is not required in the near future.
3. **Yash Parkar asked about customer concentration.** Management noted that the top 5 customers comprise 70% of the order book, with Adani at ~Rs 1,500 crore, NTPC at ~Rs 2,800 crore, and NHPC at ~Rs 1,500 crore.
4. **Siddharth Soni asked about related party transactions.** Management explained that SPVs award EPC execution to the parent or subsidiary companies based strictly on competitive L1 bidding, with full transparency and banker oversight.
5. **Moiz Khan asked about BESS margins and viability.** Management stated annuity-based BESS (850 MWh out of 1,250 MWh total) carries an IRR of 14% to 14.5%, while EPC BESS (350 MWh) yields 12% to 13% EBITDA margins.

### One-line takeaway
Bondada Engineering reported robust H1 FY27 revenue growth of 16% and guided for 55%-60% annual top-line growth, backed by a Rs 10,445 crore order book and strategic diversification into BESS, defense, and IPP assets.
