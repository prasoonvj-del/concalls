# Bondada Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (and H1 FY27)  
**Tone:** Positive - strong order book growth, diversification into high-growth segments, revenue and profit growth, credit rating upgrade

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 revenue: Rs 723.7 crore (+10% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 1,415.3 crore (+16% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 84 crore (+8% YoY), margin 11.6%; H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 162.2 crore (+12% YoY), margin 11.5%
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 57.4 crore (+10% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 111.4 crore (+18% YoY)
- Order book (Oct 31, 2026): Rs 10,445.8 crore; H1 FY27 order inflow: Rs 4,663.6 crore
- Cash deposits (Sep 30, 2026): Rs 335.3 crore; Net worth: Rs 84 crore (vs Rs 730 crore Mar 31, 2026)
- Net debt: ~Rs 360 crore (Q2); expected Rs 1,300-1,500 crore by Mar 31, 2027 (~1.5x equity)
- Commissioned solar EPC capacity: ~1.7 GW peak; FY27 target: 1.5 GW peak
- CRISIL long-term rating upgraded to A+/Stable from A/Stable

### Highlights
- Major order wins: 600 MW solar PV from NTPC Green Energy, 250 MW solar from Adani Group, 250 MW solar + 50 MW/200 MWh BESS from NTPC RE, 200 MW/400 MWh annuity BESS in Tamil Nadu
- BESS: Secured VRFB projects (100 MWh Khavda, 120 MWh GIPCL); total BESS order book ~1,250 MWh (850 MWh annuity at 14-14.5% IRR, 350 MWh EPC at 12-13% margin)
- Renewable IPP: Acquired 60% stake in Onix IPP (225 MW AC solar, 25-year PPA with MSEDCL, ~Rs 150 crore annual revenue)
- Defence: Acquired 75% stake in KCS Engineering Solutions for specialised engineering/manufacturing
- Digital infrastructure: Incorporated PhotonicGrid Networks for fibre optic networks, data centre connectivity
- Main board listing approval from NSE and BSE (migration from SME platform)
- Non-binding MoU with Goa for ~Rs 3,000 crore floating solar and compressed biogas
- Revenue mix target: 70% EPC / 30% new technologies (BESS, IPP, defence, data centres) by 2030 (currently ~90/10)
- Subsidiary revenue contribution: ~10% in H1 FY27
- Bid pipeline: Rs 26,000-27,000 crore; win rate 20-22%
- Top 5 customers = 70% of order book; NTPC ~Rs 2,800 crore, Adani ~Rs 1,500 crore, NHPC ~Rs 1,500 crore pending, Telecom ~Rs 2,000 crore
- Order book composition: ~Rs 2,000 crore O&M (3-5 yr), ~Rs 1,400 crore annuity (12 yr), ~Rs 7,000 crore EPC execution in next 24 months
- FY27 revenue growth guidance: 55-60% YoY; revenue target Rs 10,000 crore by 2030
- 2 GW IPP pipeline: 50% land acquired; first phase 200-500 MW; PPAs/contracts expected in next half year
- Call tone more positive than 'mixed' label in prior results note, emphasizing strong order book visibility and diversification execution

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue growth: 55-60% YoY (management)
- FY27 solar EPC commissioning target: 1.5 GW peak
- Revenue mix target: 70% EPC / 30% annuity & new tech by 2030
- EBITDA margins: expected to maintain or improve as annuity share grows
- Debt by Mar 31, 2027: Rs 1,300-1,500 crore (~1.5x equity)
- No near-term equity raise planned; existing capital tied up for current order book
- 2 GW IPP: first phase funding clarity by end FY27; PPAs/contracts in next half year
- BESS: order intake to pick up from Q3 as cell prices stabilize

### Demand & industry commentary
- Solar EPC TAM ~Rs 2 lakh crore/year; NTPC alone tendering 50-60 GW over 2-3 years
- Exploring international markets: Saudi Arabia/GCC, Africa
- VRFB technology for long-duration storage: addresses lithium-ion limitations (12-yr life, 2-4 hr duration); VRFB offers plant life matching solar, flexible duration
- VRFB raw material diversity vs lithium-ion single-source dependency supports better margins
- Defence: focus on import substitution of Russian legacy systems; shortlisted by DPSUs
- Data centres: definitive agreement for 50 MW hyperscale (scalable to 300 MW); working on Microsoft data centre MEP in Hyderabad
- Private customers: 2-3 payment milestones; PSU: 5-6 milestones; collections on track with minor procedural delays

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 FY27 margins maintained despite global volatility due to advance vendor ordering and budget fixing
- Existing order book margins expected to hold; new tenders priced carefully
- BESS EPC margins: 12-13%; BESS annuity IRR: 14-14.5%
- VRFB margins expected better than lithium-ion due to diversified supply chain
- Annuity EBITDA margins higher than EPC; overall margins to maintain/improve as annuity share grows
- Battery cell price volatility caused order intake slowdown in last 2 quarters; seeing stabilization

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Onix IPP: 225 MW AC solar project acquired (operational asset)
- BESS: executing 850 MWh annuity + 350 MWh EPC projects
- 2 GW IPP pipeline: 50% land acquired; first phase 200-500 MW
- KCS Engineering Solutions: 75% stake acquired for defence manufacturing
- PhotonicGrid Networks: incorporated for fibre optic/data centre infrastructure
- Goa MoU: ~Rs 3,000 crore floating solar + compressed biogas (non-binding, subject to approvals)
- Manpower scaling for execution capacity; fund pooling on track

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash deposits: Rs 335.3 crore (Sep 30, 2026)
- Net worth: Rs 84 crore (Sep 30, 2026) vs Rs 730 crore (Mar 31, 2026)
- Consolidated balance sheet: ~Rs 2,500 crore
- Current net debt: ~Rs 360 crore (working capital)
- Term loans: ~Rs 70-80 crore (emergency credit line); expected Rs 750-800 crore by Mar 31, 2027 (Onix + BESS project finance)
- Working capital: Rs 480 crore
- Total debt expected Mar 31, 2027: Rs 1,300-1,500 crore (~1.5x equity)
- CRISIL rating upgrade: long-term A+/Stable, short-term A1; four product subsidiaries rated A
- Collections: government per milestones (minor delays collectible in Q3); private per milestones, on time
- Receivables management: commercial team focus on invoice quality; mix of private/PSU orders balances cash flows

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Customer concentration: top 5 customers = 70% of order book (though diversifying via PSU tenders)
- Land acquisition delays for 2 GW IPP (50% acquired, industry-wide issue)
- Battery cell price volatility impacting BESS order intake (now stabilizing)
- Execution risk: scaling manpower and timely fund pooling critical for 55-60% revenue growth
- Related party transactions: SPV structures for Onix IPP and Tamil Nadu BESS (eliminated on consolidation); arm's length pricing via competitive bidding
- Non-binding Goa MoU (~Rs 3,000 crore) subject to evaluation/approvals
- Stock performance subject to market conditions; management focuses on execution
