# GTPL Hathway Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-19 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth driven by acquisitions and subscriber additions, but offset by lower EBITDA margins and a negative PAT due to forex losses and transponder amortization.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Total Income: Rs 11,464 million (Up 19% YoY, 12% QoQ)
- Standalone Total Revenue: Rs 8,003 million (Up 25% YoY, 15% QoQ)
- Consolidated Subscription Revenue: Rs 3,455 million (vs Rs 3,024 million YoY, Rs 2,913 million QoQ)
- Standalone Subscription Revenue: Rs 2,692 million (vs Rs 2,191 million YoY, Rs 2,127 million QoQ)
- Broadband Revenue: Rs 1,459 million (vs Rs 1,393 million YoY, Rs 1,425 million QoQ)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 1,103 million
- Reported EBITDA Margin: 9.6%
- Operational EBITDA Margin: 20%
- Consolidated PAT: Negative Rs 50 lakh (impacted by Rs 75 million total: Rs 45 million forex loss, Rs 30 million transponder amortization)
- Digital Cable TV Subscribers: 11 million total (1.5 million addition in Q2, 10.30 million paying subscribers)
- Broadband Active Subscribers: 1.07 million (10,000 additions QoQ)
- Broadband Home Pass: 6 million
- Broadband ARPU: Rs 470
- Average Monthly Data Consumption per User: 436 GB
- ACT Group Cable TV Acquisition Cash Consideration: Rs 35.55 crores (acquired September 15, 2026; ~6 lakh subscribers across AP, Telangana, Odisha, Karnataka; 1.25 million primary subscribers)
- DL GTPL Cabnet Stake Acquisition: 74% remaining stake for INR83.25 million (1.5 lakh subscribers)

### Highlights
- Completed the acquisition of the cable TV business of seven ACT Group companies on September 15, 2026.
- Appointed Mr. Vivek Raina as the new CEO for the broadband business along with other senior management appointments, rebranding broadband as TrueFiber.
- GTPL Infinity (HITS platform) launched in November 2025; over 60% of the existing network converted to HITS, with full conversion targeted by December or January.
- Network migration to GPON/FTTH completely finished.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects operational EBITDA margin to increase by 150 bps to 250 bps by the end of FY27 as synergies from acquisitions and HITS implementation are fully realized.
- Target net additions of 1.8 million to 1.9 million subscribers in the digital TV business for the full year FY27.
- 3-year targets: Reach 13 million to 14 million subscribers in CATV and 1.2 million subscribers in broadband.
- Long-term annual subscriber growth guidance: 7% to 8%.
- Expects broadband subscriber quarterly additions to return to previous levels of over 20,000 following network migration and leadership changes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Bundling entertainment (CATV) and broadband helps successfully reduce churn and improve customer retention and loyalty.
- Monthly data consumption per user increased 6% YoY to 436 GB.
- Competition from telecom operators is viewed as healthy, keeping the company on its toes and performing well in strong markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operational EBITDA margin stood at 20% for Q2 FY27 (compared to 22% in Q2 FY26 and Q1 FY27).
- ACT business currently operates at a 12% EBITDA margin, which management expects to scale to 25%-26% post-integration.
- Higher data consumption has not required additional capex/opex currently; future increases in consumption would require minimal additional capex (estimated at ~10% relative to traffic doubling).
- PAT impacted by a notional forex loss of Rs 45 million and Rs 30 million in transponder amortization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Broadband home pass reached 6 million; broadband conversion rate from home pass improved from 13%-14% to 17%-18%, with a target of 20%.
- Expansion into cable-dark and rural areas via HITS is planned to begin aggressively after completing existing site conversions around December/January.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in the call.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange fluctuations impacting profitability (notional forex loss of Rs 45 million in Q2).
- Challenging market environment with changing technologies and delivery mediums.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nimish Varma (Vexora Sec) asked about ACT acquisition ARPU and margin integration:** Management stated the acquisition is accretive, with 1.25 lakh primary subscribers at higher ARPU, and full integration synergies will be realized over the next few quarters.
2. **Suvarna (Private Limited) asked about the slowdown in broadband additions and reasons behind it:** Management explained that the slowdown was due to ground-level cleanup and migrating the entire network to GPON/FTTH over the past 1.5 years; with migration complete and new CEO Vivek Raina, additions are expected to rebound above 20,000 per quarter.
3. **Santosh Shetty asked if usage growth (436 GB consumption) requires additional capex:** Management clarified that current consumption requires no additional capex or opex due to flexible technology, and any future massive scale-up would only require ~10% incremental spend.
4. **Pahal Sharma asked about digital TV/OTT bundle effectiveness and retention:** Management noted that bundling broadband, cable TV, and OTT primarily reduces churn in both businesses rather than driving massive standalone OTT revenues.
5. **Yash Mehta asked how to track operational EBITDA margin restoration:** Management replied that investors should track active/paying subscriber growth KPIs and the projected 150 to 250 bps operational EBITDA margin expansion by the end of FY27.

### One-line takeaway
GTPL Hathway delivered solid 19% YoY revenue growth to Rs 11,464 million and expanded its subscriber base through strategic acquisitions (ACT Group, DL GTPL Cabnet) and HITS rollout, though profitability was temporarily dented by integration costs, forex losses, and transponder amortization.
