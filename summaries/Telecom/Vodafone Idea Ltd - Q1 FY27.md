# Vodafone Idea Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust financial and operating performance with positive net subscriber additions for the first time since the merger.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs. 11,689 Crore (up 6.0% YoY, 3.2% QoQ)
- Cash EBITDA: Rs. 2,475 Crore (up 13.5% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 5,034 Crore (up 9.1% YoY); EBITDA margin: 43.1% (up over 120 bps YoY)
- Customer ARPU: Rs. 195 (up 10.2% YoY, 2.6% QoQ)
- Total subscriber base: 193.1 million customers
- 4G/5G subscriber mix: 67.4% (130.1 million subscribers, up from 127.4 million in Q1 FY26)
- Data usage per 4G/5G subscriber: 21.7 GB (up 25.2% YoY); total data usage: 88.4 Petabyte/day (up nearly 28% YoY)
- Pan-India 4G coverage: 87.0% (over 15,600 new sites added over the last 12 months, nearly 3,000 unique broadband towers added in Q1 FY27)
- 5G footprint: Live in over 200 cities across 17 circles (over 16,000 5G sites)
- Capex for Q1 FY27: Rs. 1,930 Crore
- Bank debt: Rs. 211 Crore (down from Rs. 1,926 Crore as of June 30, 2025); Total debt (including NCDs): Rs. 3,489 Crore
- Free cash and bank balance: Rs. 6,558 Crore as of June 30, 2026
- Exceptional item benefit: Rs. 1,816 Crore on account of fair value adjustment of earmarked shares from CLAM

### Highlights
- Delivered first quarter of positive net subscriber additions since the merger, driven by postpaid, M2M, and improving prepaid trends.
- Churn reduced by 24 bps YoY.
- Received Rs. 1,183 Crore as part-proceeds from warrant issuance to promoter Aditya Birla Group.
- Assigned Crisil A-/Stable rating by CRISIL in May 2026 and upgraded to ICRA A- (Stable) by ICRA in June 2026 for certain long-term bank facilities.
- Raised first tranche of funding of Rs. 6,400 Crore (including warrant proceeds and debt/non-fund based facilities via ECB and Indian private banks); placed orders worth Rs. 9,000 Crore of capex.
- Launched digital offerings including 'Vi Edu+' with PhysicsWallah, 'Vi Instadata', 'Choose Your Own Plan' international roaming, and partnerships with Spotify and Meta.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its 3-year capex plan of Rs. 45,000 Crore and intends to deploy roughly 3,500 towers a month on 4G going forward.
- Management maintains its guidance of 3x Cash EBITDA over the next 3 years, backed by a targeted revenue CAGR of around 16.8%.
- Plans to complete 4G rollout over the next 18 months (55,000-57,000 sites) and 5G rollout over 3 years (86,000-90,000 sites).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Telecom industry well-positioned for growth driven by fast-growing economy, young population, rising technology adoption, and lower rural tele-density.
- Strong enterprise demand across Connectivity, Cloud, IoT, Business Communications, Mobility, and Cybersecurity in sectors like BFSI, Manufacturing, Utilities, Logistics, and Government.
- M2M business seeing strong traction, doubling net additions over the last year with a dominant position in connected cars.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Network costs managed efficiently despite adding over 15,600 broadband sites over the last 12 months, though some inflationary challenges from diesel were noted.
- Roaming access charges increased due to higher participation in the wholesale line of business, which management noted is cash-accretive.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q1 FY27 capex was muted at Rs. 1,930 Crore due to supply-side issues from geopolitical constraints.
- Total capex orders placed stand at Rs. 9,100 Crore; supplies and execution have commenced, targeting deployment over the next 2 quarters or less.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Bank debt reduced to Rs. 211 Crore; total debt stands at Rs. 3,489 Crore (including Rs. 3,300 Crore NCD borrowing).
- Engaged with a funding architecture comprising PSU banks (led by SBI with 6-7 participating banks), Indian private banks, and ECBs with foreign banks.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply-side issues arising from geopolitical constraints impacted capex deployment in Q1 FY27.
- Potential inflationary pressures on network costs (e.g., diesel prices).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) asked about subscriber transition trends by circle and ARPU potential.** Management stated that churn reduction is visible across all 17 markets and expects ARPU momentum to continue, targeting steady organic growth above the current Rs. 195 level.
- **Aditya Suresh (Macquarie) asked for clarification on Cash EBITDA targets and net debt.** Management reiterated the 3x Cash EBITDA target over 3 years and a 16.8% revenue CAGR, clarifying total debt stands at Rs. 3,489 Crore.
- **Balaji Subramanian (IIFL) questioned whether postpaid additions dipped ex-M2M due to competitor priority plans.** Management denied any structural dip or impact from competition, stating net additions in postpaid remain consistently positive.
- **Saurabh Handa (Citigroup) asked about the composition of the Rs. 5,200 Crore incremental funding.** Management clarified it is a mix of debt and non-funded facilities primarily from ECBs and Indian private banks.
- **Bavineni Srinivas (Retail Investor) expressed concern over debt and suggested equity raising.** Management responded that the company has a large equity base, low bank debt (Rs. 211 Crore), and sufficient room for debt as an infrastructure company, expressing confidence in ongoing debt-raising efforts.

### One-line takeaway
Vodafone Idea reported its first quarter of positive net subscriber additions since the merger, alongside a 6.0% YoY revenue increase and a 13.5% YoY rise in Cash EBITDA, backed by ongoing fund-raising and a Rs. 45,000 Crore 3-year capex rollout plan.
