# Suyog Telematics Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited execution dependencies on customers (Vodafone Idea and BSNL) and a stagnant period over the past 18-24 months, though they expressed confidence in future rollouts.

### Key numbers
- **Total Sites/Towers:** 6,103 towers as of Q1 exit (6,100+ sites)
- **Total Tenancies:** 7,468 tenancies
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 71.0 crore (Rs 710 million) in Q1 FY27 (up 6.1% YoY compared to Rs 66.8 crore in Q1 FY26 on an apple-to-apple basis including electricity)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 42.0 crore (Rs 420 million) with an EBITDA margin of 59.3% (revised policy adding electricity reimbursement to top-line)
- **PBT:** Rs 19.5 crore (Rs 195 million)
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 14.5 crore (Rs 145 million); Net profit margin constant around 20%
- **EPS:** Rs 12.37
- **Revenue per tower:** Constant around Rs 31,000 to Rs 31,500 per month (excluding electricity)
- **Vodafone Idea Revenue Share:** 27.0% (Airtel: 48.1%, Jio: 22.6%, BSNL: 2.3%)
- **Battery Costs:** Lithium 100H price rose from Rs 33,500 to Rs 48,000; upcoming zinc battery priced at Rs 33,000 for 100H.

### Highlights
- Changed accounting policy effective 1st April to include electricity reimbursement in top-line as per GST requirements, reducing reported EBITDA percentage from historical ~70% to 59.3%.
- Received 600+ orders from Vodafone Idea in mid-June; converted 95 towers (150 tenancies) within 13-15 days in June. 
- Fast-tracked the launch of 100% made-in-India zinc batteries (tied up with GBB Batteries in Kerala) to combat surging imported lithium battery prices and reduce CapEx; first batch for 10-15 sites expected by mid-September.
- Tenancy ratio stands at ~1.2x on an overall portfolio basis, targeting 1.8x in the next 3 to 4 years (early FY29).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets 3,0ent additional tenancies from Vodafone Idea in the current financial year (FY27), with an expectation to add another 5,000 sites in FY28.
- Aiming to reach 10,000+ total tenancies by the end of FY27.
- Targeting quarterly revenue of Rs 100 crore (annualized top-line of ~Rs 400 crore) by Q4, with PAT margins expected to sustain around 20% to 24%.
- Expects H1 to account for 35%-40% and H2 for 60% of total yearly execution.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry planning roughly 2.5 lakh towers plus 50,000 from other operators (approx. 3 lakh towers) in the next 5 years.
- Airtel and Jio are currently not doing major rollouts; primary growth drivers are Vodafone Idea (targeting 45,000 site rollout over next 18-24 months, doing 3,000-3,500 per month) and BSNL (INR 77,000 crore CapEx plan for 2 lakh sites over 5 years).
- Fiber revenue is expected to remain around 5% to 8% of total revenue in FY27 as most rural/operator sites currently rely on microwave.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Lithium battery prices doubled/surged up to 50% in the last 2.5 months (from Rs 33,500 to Rs 48,000 for 100H) due to China removing subsidies and currency fluctuations.
- New zinc batteries will cost Rs 33,000 for 100H (matching old lithium pricing), are fire-resistant, and will significantly save on CapEx and operating costs.
- IndAS 10-year long-term rental agreements create notional losses in the first five years, mutting reported EBITDA margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Funded primarily through internal accruals currently; no immediate call taken on fresh fundraising unless bulk orders expand significantly.
- Single tenancy site CapEx recovery takes 3.5 to 4 years, dropping to 24 months for 2 tenancies and 18 months for 3 tenancies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Bank funds are being repaid on a monthly basis. No major immediate fundraise is required for the targeted 3,000 tenancies.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- BSNL rollout delays persist due to equipment issues with Tejas; company has withheld BSNL execution and revenue projections until billing officially commences (186 site billings remain pending).
- Rainy season in Q2 may impact execution timelines.
- Heavy reliance on customer capital raising and rollout schedules (Vodafone Idea's pending consortium funding tranche from SBI).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Guneet Singh asked about the zinc battery tie-up and financial projections.** Management clarified they are buying from GBB Batteries (Kerala) rather than manufacturing in-house, achieving cost parity with old lithium prices (Rs 33,000 vs Rs 48,000) and eliminating import dependency.
2. **Mahek Talati questioned the sudden caution around BSNL after months of bullishness.** Management explained they remain optimistic about BSNL's 2-lakh site 5-year plan, but withheld numbers from guidance until Tejas equipment issues are resolved and billing actually starts on the ground.
3. **Saket Kapoor expressed investor disappointment over 18-24 months of stagnation.** Management acknowledged the cash flow and growth impacts caused by BSNL payment/execution delays, but reiterated confidence in achieving a Rs 100 crore quarterly revenue run-rate by Q4 and scaling past historical stagnation.
4. **Surbhi Mishra asked about tenancy ratio targets and MSA terms.** Management stated they target 1.8x tenancy by FY29 (driven by macro-site rollouts) and confirmed that MSAs act as open frameworks rather than fixed-quantity closed contracts.
5. **Varun Ghia asked why tenancy targets were adjusted/lowered.** Management clarified that the 3,000 tenancy target is a conservative baseline tied strictly to Vodafone's initial Rs 6,400 crore funding tranche, and will scale up once additional consortium funds are released.
6. **Anubhav Jain asked about the status of the 186 pending BSNL sites.** Management stated that while 186 site billings remain stuck due to Tejas issues, they expect resolution and revenue commencement in Q2.

### One-line takeaway
Suyog Telematics reported a muted Q1 FY27 with consolidated revenue of Rs 71.0 crore and maintained a 59.3% EBITDA margin, pinning its near-term growth on 3,000 new Vodafone Idea tenancies and the introduction of cost-saving domestic zinc batteries.
