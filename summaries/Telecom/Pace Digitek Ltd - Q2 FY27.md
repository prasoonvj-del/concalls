# Pace Digitek Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth across energy and telecom businesses, capacity expansion, and upward revision of FY27 revenue guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 808 crore in Q2 (up 51.5% YoY, 45.5% QoQ); Rs. 1,364 crore in H1 FY27 (up 51.4% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 159 crore in Q2 (up 68.5% YoY); Rs. 245 crore in H1 FY27 (up 40.5% YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs. 97 crore in Q2 (up 43.3% YoY); Rs. 160 crore in H1 FY27 (up 30.4% YoY).
- **Executable Order Book:** Rs. 10,735 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Energy: Rs. 8,528 crore; Telecom: Rs. 2,208 crore).
- **Order Inflows:** Rs. 2,437 crore in H1 FY27 (Energy: Rs. 2,173 crore; Telecom: Rs. 265 crore).
- **Total Borrowings:** Rs. 1,371 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up from Rs. 961 crore as of March 31, 2026).
- **Cash and Bank Balances:** Rs. 515 crore.
- **Net Debt:** Rs. 856 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Net Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.35x.
- **Return Ratios (H1):** RoE at 13.2%; ROCE at 12.6%.
- **BESS Manufacturing Capacity:** Expanded to 5 GWh in August 2026; produced 844 MWh in H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Commissioned about 875 MWh of BESS capacity at project sites in H1 FY27; total delivered 1.8 GWh and commissioned 1 GWh at own project sites.
- Secured key energy orders including BESS EPC from NLC India and DVC, BESS BOOT from BSES Rajdhani Power, and BESS supply from NGSL.
- In-house BESS container fabrication facility commissioned; commercial production scheduled for November 2026 and full-scale in January 2027 to meet 50% of container needs.
- Successfully commenced production of 261 kWh C&I cabinets and completed customer sample trials.
- Telecom business commissioned over 8,000 sites for BSNL, entering a 5-year O&M phase; secured additional telecom orders from BSNL and BharatNet OFC projects.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Revised upwards to Rs. 3,300 crore – Rs. 3,500 crore (previously Rs. 3,200 crore – Rs. 3,400 crore).
- **FY28 Revenue Guidance:** Maintained at Rs. 4,000 crore – Rs. 4,200 crore.
- Management expects blended EBITDA margins to normalize at 16% to 17% and PAT margins around 10% to 11% as the energy segment mix increases.

### Demand & industry commentary
- BESS demand has increased multifold; industry demand projected at ~30-35 GWh for the financial year and ~236 GWh by FY32.
- Bidding competition has improved, with normal participation of 6 to 8 players per bid compared to 25 to 30 earlier.
- Domestic BESS prices remain competitive with Chinese landed imports after transportation and customs duty.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Energy segment EBITDA margins range from 12% to 13%; telecom EBITDA margins are around 20% to 23% (O&M at ~25%).
- In-house container manufacturing expected to reduce container costs by ~25% and overall BESS cost by 4% to 5% (gross level), boosting EBITDA by 1.5% to 2%.
- Lithium-ion cell prices saw a ~20% increase in the previous quarter but have started coming down and stabilizing in Q2.
- China's export rebate cut of ~6% effective January 2027 is expected to be offset by falling lithium-ion cell prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity expanded from 2.5 GWh to 5 GWh in August 2026; further expansion to 10 GWh targeted by Q4 FY27.
- Build-to-own-operate (BOOT) asset stood at Rs. 1,311 crore as of September 30, 2026. Management has paused new BOOT projects unless external investments are secured.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt increased by Rs. 410 crore to Rs. 1,371 crore to fund BOOT asset expansion and working capital. Peak debt by FY27 expected around an additional Rs. 1,100 crore.
- Total receivables declined to Rs. 2,177 crore as of September 30, 2026 (from Rs. 2,442 crore as of March 31, 2026) due to improved collections and older retention realizations. Target net working capital cycle is 90 to 120 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Initial mobilization and startup expenses for new solar and balance of systems projects (MAHAGENCO, Bondada) temporarily depressed standalone EBITDA margins.
- High working capital requirements in utility-scale and BOOT projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Poddar (Sapphire Capital) asked about the drivers of Q2 EBITDA margin improvement.** Management clarified that a higher proportion of high-margin telecom service revenues drove the blended margin up, and margins will normalize to 16-17% as energy revenue contribution grows.
2. **Milandeep Jain (Green Portfolio) asked about C&I order book and revenue expectations.** Management stated that while samples are out, commercial production orders are expected by end of Q3/Q4, targeting 30% of energy revenues next year with better margins than grid-scale BESS.
3. **Sweta Jain (Anand Rathi) inquired about standalone margin dip and China policy impact.** Management explained initial project mobilization costs for MAHAGENCO/Bondada lowered margins, and noted that while China's export rebate cuts take effect in January 2027, falling lithium-ion prices will offset it.
4. **Paras Chheda (Purple One Vertex Ventures) asked about BOOT project return metrics and cash flows.** Management stated project IRR is ~10% and equity IRR is ~15%, noting operating cash flows are positive once accounting finance lease receivables are excluded.
5. **Keval Shah (Sanctum Wealth PMS) asked about BESS bidding competition and realization pressure.** Management noted competition has rationalized to 6-8 serious bidders per tender, and threshold margins (16-17% EBITDA, 10-11% PAT) are strictly maintained.

### One-line takeaway
Pace Digitek delivered strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 51.4%, raised its FY27 revenue guidance to Rs. 3,300–3,500 crore, and is scaling up its BESS manufacturing capacity to 10 GWh by Q4 FY27.
