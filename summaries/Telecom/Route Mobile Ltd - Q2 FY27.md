# Route Mobile Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed – revenue and gross‑margin improve, but PAT falls YoY and margins still pressured by one‑off costs  

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: **INR 1,180 cr** (+5.4 % YoY, +2.4 % QoQ)  
- Gross profit: **INR 264 cr** (+6.8 % YoY, +9.8 % QoQ)  
- Gross profit margin: **22.4 %** (up ~30 bps YoY)  
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: **10 %** (+50 bps QoQ)  
- Adjusted profit after tax: **INR 825 mn** (≈ **82.5 cr**, –17.8 % YoY, +20.4 % QoQ)  
- Billable transactions: **47.6 bn** in Q2 (93.4 bn H1)  
- Net revenue retention (H1): **105 %**  
- Recurring‑revenue share (12‑month window Sep 2025‑Sep 2026): **93 %** (up 89 % YoY)  
- New‑product (RCS, WhatsApp, IP‑messaging) revenue: **INR 128 cr** (+36.7 % YoY, +35.8 % QoQ)  

### Highlights
- **Heltar acquisition** (AI‑native no‑code engagement platform) completed 29 Jul 2026 for **INR 33.7 cr** (₹23.9 cr cash, ₹9.8 cr deferred). Early traction with blue‑chip enterprises in healthcare and fashion.  
- **AI‑driven firewall solutions** deployed at 12 operators; gross profit up to **70 %** on some customers, blended **≈ 50‑60 %**.  
- **Top‑5 customer concentration** fell to **30 %** of revenue (down from 33 % YoY).  
- **Customers > $1 m annual billing** rose to **80** (FY27) from **73** (FY26).  

### Guidance & outlook
- Management gave **no quantitative revenue or margin guidance** for H2 FY27.  
- Stated priorities: convert high‑margin firewall and new‑product pipeline, scale Heltar via Route Mobile & Proximus Global sales teams, and maintain strong cost discipline.  

### Demand & industry commentary
- Shift from pure messaging to a **full‑stack AI‑enabled customer‑engagement platform**.  
- Operator market shows healthy pipeline for firewall solutions and network‑API development with Proximus Global.  
- Enterprise market adopting AI‑driven, no‑code engagement (Heltar) across banking, healthcare, retail, education.  

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 gross‑margin dip linked to temporary traffic loss and a **security incident at Masiv (Colombian subsidiary)**.  
- Q2 margin recovery driven by higher‑margin new‑product traffic.  
- Operating cost increase QoQ due to **non‑recurring Masiv security incident** and a **performance‑bonus provision**; management calls these one‑offs and expects run‑rate costs to normalize.  

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Completed **Heltar acquisition** (₹33.7 cr).  
- Leadership addition: **Marko Erkic** appointed CEO of Masiv to drive Latin‑America expansion.  
- No specific capex figures disclosed.  

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash balance: **INR 13.5 bn** (≈ 50 % of market‑cap).  
- Free cash (pre‑balance): **≈ INR 1,150‑1,200 cr**.  
- No debt or net‑debt numbers disclosed.  

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Security incident** at Masiv (non‑recurring, but highlighted).  
- **Non‑recurring cost items** (security incident, bonus provision) may distort margin visibility.  
- **Long‑distance messaging softness** due to aggregator loss; reliance on direct‑enterprise contracts.  
- **RCS pricing pressure** (rates nearly doubled) could affect channel mix.  
- Execution risk on **early‑stage M&A** in Latin America, Saudi Arabia, Southeast Asia.

Revenue and gross margin are improving on a higher‑margin new‑product base, but profit remains pressured by one‑off costs and a soft long‑distance segment.
