# Indus Towers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy rollout momentum, robust order book, and progress on Africa expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Gross revenues:** Rs 84.3 billion (up 4.6% YoY, 4.1% QoQ)
- **Core rental revenues:** Rs 53.7 billion (up 5.2% YoY, 1.2% QoQ)
- **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 45.2 billion (up 3.0% YoY, 1.2% QoQ)
- **EBITDA margin:** 53.6% (lower by 1.5 percentage points YoY and 0.9 percentage points QoQ)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 17.5 billion (up 0.5% YoY, down 2.7% QoQ)
- **Free Cash Flow:** Rs 14.4 billion
- **Macro towers added:** ~3,100 (Total: ~267,600, up 6.3% YoY)
- **Colocations added:** ~4,200 (Total: ~432,300, up 5.1% YoY)
- **Tenancy ratio:** Stable at 1.62
- **Total towers including lean towers:** ~446,300
- **Energy margin:** -4.6% (vs -3.6% in Q4 and -4% in Q1 FY26)
- **Diesel consumption reduction:** 13% YoY
- **Solar access sites added:** ~3,700 (Total: ~46,000; cumulative solar capacity: 259 MW)
- **Pre-tax ROCE:** 25.4% (over the last 12 months)
- **Post-tax ROE:** 18.9% (over the last 12 months)

### Highlights
- Rollout momentum supported by customer network expansion and movement of expired tenancy portfolios.
- Received regulatory approvals and operating licenses across target African markets (Nigeria, Uganda, Zambia); secured orders from anchor customer; rollouts expected to commence next quarter (Q2).
- RoW rules 2024 implemented across India; Green Energy Open Access framework strengthened in UP, Rajasthan, and Delhi.
- Network reliability maintained at 99.95% uptime.
- Battery modernization and solar deployments continued despite supply chain constraints on batteries due to West Asia conflict.
- CFO Vikas Poddar stepped down; management expressed gratitude for his tenure.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they have a firm order book and visibility for the next 3 to 4 quarters.
- Africa rollouts to commence in Q2 and scale progressively; capex expected to be moderate initially and largely debt-funded.

### Demand & industry commentary
- TRAI data shows 5G subscription base grew by 36 million in Q4 FY26 to >427 million; 5G BTS installed base reached 563,000 (up 32,000 in Q1 FY27).
- Total data consumption and average monthly usage per user grew 31% and 20% YoY in Q4 FY26. 5G usage grew 87% YoY, accounting for 43% of total data traffic in Q4 FY26.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA grew 5.2% YoY when adjusting for Rs 0.9 billion write-backs in Q1 FY26.
- Energy margin impacted by seasonality (monsoon diesel usage) and past-period settlements.
- Tower manufacturing impacted initially in April due to West Asia conflict (LPG shortage), but issue has been resolved. Battery supplies constrained by geopolitical issues but expected to recover from August.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Maintenance capex increased due to replacement of lead-acid batteries with lithium-ion battery banks.
- Africa expansion underway with anchor tenant; single tenancy expected to cover cost of capital.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Free cash flow remained robust at Rs 14.4 billion.
- Board remains committed to steady/progressive dividend distribution, unaffected by Africa capex.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disturbances impacting battery supplies and initial tower manufacturing (LPG shortage).
- Seasonal monsoon disruptions affecting on-ground execution in Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vivekanand Subbaraman (Ambit Capital):** Asked why rental income growth mirrors colocation growth despite escalations. Management (Vikas Poddar) explained that escalation and loading growth are offset by renewal discounts and revenue equalization effects.
- **Manish Adukia (Goldman Sachs):** Inquired about Africa segment margins and returns. Management (Vikas Poddar) stated it is early stage, MSAs and rate cards are still being discussed, and returns will stabilize as operations mature.
- **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA):** Asked if order book visibility depends on customer capital raising. Management (Prachur Sah) affirmed that the order book for the next 3–4 quarters is firm irrespective of funding situations.
- **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities):** Questioned ARPT trends and battery capex ROCE impact. Management explained that leaner tower structures and rural-urban mix impact ARPT, while battery replacements shift opex to a customer-compensated capex model.
- **Kunal Vora (BNP Paribas):** Asked about pricing strategy and break-even in Africa. Management stated pricing is based on cost per tower and desired returns, ensuring cost of capital coverage even with a single tenant.

### One-line takeaway
Indus Towers delivered stable Q1 FY27 performance with 4.6% YoY revenue growth, strong free cash flow generation, a robust 3-4 quarter order book, and advancing preparations for its upcoming African market entry.
