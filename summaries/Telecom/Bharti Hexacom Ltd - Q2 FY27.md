# Bharti Hexacom Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Telecom*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong performance with sequential revenue growth, solid smartphone additions, and robust free cash flow despite weather disruptions.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None provided (management noted they do not give forward-looking guidance).
- Tone and demand view: Maintained a positive stance on core growth, though adverse weather in the Northeast emerged as a notable negative impact on customer continuity and net additions.
- New topics or risks: Severe weather events in the Northeast impacting recharges and customer continuity.
- Last quarter's promises: Continued investments in 5G expansion, network modernization, and home/IPTV growth were maintained as stated.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Over Rs. 2,580 crore (growing 2.9% QoQ)
- EBITDAaL: Over Rs. 1,250 crore
- EBITDAaL margin: 48.6%
- Mobile customer base: 29 million
- Smartphone customer additions: 48,000
- ARPU: Rs. 264
- Homes, offices, and other businesses net additions: 71,000 (revenue up 3.7% QoQ)
- Operating free cash flow (EBITDAaL minus capex): About Rs. 920 crore
- Net debt excluding leases: About Rs. 1,575 crore

### Highlights
- Delivered sequential revenue growth of 2.9% to over Rs. 2,580 crore.
- ARPU stood at Rs. 264, aided by one extra day in the quarter.
- Homes, offices, and other businesses saw net adds of 71,000, driving 3.7% QoQ revenue growth in that segment.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they do not provide forward-looking guidance or estimates.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pricing interventions caused a temporary impact on customer continuity.
- Adverse weather events in the Northeast severely impacted customer continuity and recharges during the quarter.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDAaL margin came in at 48.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continued investments in 5G expansion, network modernization, and growing homes and IPTV businesses.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt excluding leases stood at about Rs. 1,575 crore, maintaining a robust balance sheet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Adverse weather impacts in the Northeast affecting customer continuity and recharge patterns.
- Temporary churn resulting from pricing interventions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Saurabh Handa (Citi) on mobile net adds turning negative and postpaid ARPU flow-through:** Management explained that negative net adds were driven by pricing intervention seasonality and severe weather disruptions in the Northeast. For postpaid, the tariff change is broadly a Rs. 50 increase flowing through.
2. **Gaurav Malhotra (Axis Capital) on ARPU growth differences vs. parent Airtel:** Management attributed differences to a distinct customer mix, affordability profiles, and the localized Northeast weather impact on existing customer continuity.
3. **Sanjesh Jain (Ambit/Sell side) on mobile revenue growth lagging Airtel and home broadband slowdown:** Management reiterated that Northeast weather disruptions created an unusual dip, and the homes slowdown is a deliberate pivot toward high-quality fiber customers.
4. **Aditya Bansal (Motilal Oswal) on voice-only plan impact and capex differences:** Management stated the voice-only plan cohort is very small and shrinking, making minimal difference to headline ARPU; they deferred answering the exact difference between reported and cash capex.

### One-line takeaway
Bharti Hexacom delivered a steady Q2 FY27 with revenue exceeding Rs. 2,580 crore and a 48.6% EBITDAaL margin, absorbing temporary weather and pricing headwinds in its operating circles.
