# Telecom: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian telecom sector in Q2 FY27 is characterized by distinct trends across its value chain: telecom service providers are focusing on customer monetization, network fiberization, and digital enterprise services; optical infrastructure and equipment providers are experiencing explosive growth driven by global AI data center demand; and telecom engineering and EPC firms are rapidly pivoting toward Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and renewable energy infrastructure. 

While core mobile capex for top operators like Reliance Jio and Bharti Airtel has peaked, capex intensity has shifted toward fiber-to-the-home (FTTH), edge data centers, and enterprise digital solutions. Vodafone Idea is accelerating its network catch-up capex after securing funding, whereas optical component manufacturers like Sterlite Technologies and HFCL are operating at multi-year operational highs due to international hyperscaler build-outs.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Historically, Indian telecom operators generated revenues primarily through voice services and volume-based 2G/3G data, relying heavily on third-party passive tower infrastructure. Over the past five years, the industry consolidated into a three-player private market alongside state-owned BSNL, shifting toward flat-rate unlimited 4G/5G data packages, asset-light tower leasing, and aggressive premiumization through postpaid upgrades and home broadband bundles. [Background]

**Shifts underway**
- **AI-driven optical demand & hyperscaler connectivity** | **scaling** | Optical equipment manufacturers are seeing revenue shifts toward hyperscale AI data center interconnects, with high-fiber-density cables commanding higher realizations ($25–$27/km vs $15/km for traditional telco fiber). Sterlite Technologies secured a $1.2 billion 5-year hyperscaler contract, while HFCL reported that 65%–75% of its optical connectivity revenues now stem from global hyperscalers [Sterlite Technologies concall Q2 FY27; HFCL concall Q2 FY27]. This shift improves gross margins and shifts order books from spot purchases to long-term multi-year backlogs.
- **Fiberization priority over Fixed Wireless Access (FWA)** | **scaling** | Major operators are prioritizing home fiber passes over pure wireless home broadband expansion to maximize customer lifetime value and network stickiness. Bharti Airtel explicitly scaled back its FWA-led rollout to focus on fiberizing home passes, treating FWA strictly as an interim fringe solution [Bharti Airtel concall Q2 FY27; Bharti Hexacom concall Q2 FY27]. This ensures higher bandwidth stability for broadband homes, though it requires higher initial fiber laydown capex.
- **Infrastructure EPC diversification into BESS and Power** | **scaling** | Telecom engineering and tower EPC players are diversifying into Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and renewable integration to offset cyclical telecom build-outs. Pace Digitek expanded its BESS capacity from 2.5 GWh to 5 GWh with plans for 10 GWh by Q4 FY27, while Bondada Engineering incorporated PhotonicGrid Networks and secured BESS contracts to target a 30% revenue share from non-EPC technologies by 2030 [Pace Digitek concall Q2 FY27; Bondada Engineering concall Q2 FY27]. This broadens addressable market size and provides higher margin visibility.

**Government push**
- **Interconnection & spectrum regulatory relief**: TRAI's revised interconnection rules effective January 2027 and the Bombay High Court ruling quashing one-time spectrum charges beyond 6.2 MHz provide regulatory clarity and cash flow relief for cellular operators [Govt: TRAI Interconnection Rules & Bombay HC Spectrum Ruling, Nov 2026].
- **Telecom PLI 2.0**: The proposed shift in PLI focus toward design-led manufacturing and backward integration aims to benefit domestic equipment makers like HFCL and Tejas Networks [Govt: Telecom PLI 2.0, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus across operators**
- **Data consumption growth**: Subscriber data usage continues to grow strongly, with Jio reporting 44.2 GB per user per month (up 14% YoY) and Vodafone Idea reporting total data usage of 93.6 Petabytes/day (up 27.1% YoY).
- **Postpaid & Enterprise tailwinds**: Postpaid additions reached record highs across operators (Airtel added 1.2 million postpaid users; Vi saw its highest sequential human postpaid net adds since FY24), backed by expanding enterprise demand for cloud, IoT, and cybersecurity services.

**Divergence across players**
- **FWA strategy**: Jio continues to aggressively push Fixed Wireless Access via UBR technology, accounting for nearly 50% of its 30 million home broadband base [Jio Platforms concall Q2 FY27]. Conversely, Bharti Airtel has deliberately scaled back FWA deployment in favor of fiber-to-the-home expansion [Bharti Airtel concall Q2 FY27].
- **Equipment & EPC demand dependency**: Equipment suppliers face diverging growth drivers. Sterlite and HFCL are seeing massive demand surges led by export hyperscalers, whereas domestic telecom suppliers like Tejas Networks and Suyog Telematics remain tied to execution and billing timelines from BSNL and Vodafone Idea's network expansion funding.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Raw material price spikes**: Lithium-ion cell prices spiked by 20% during Q2 FY27, temporarily squeezing project margins in Pace Digitek's energy segment [Pace Digitek concall Q2 FY27]. Optical preform spot prices hovered at high levels of ~$300/kg, but HFCL and Sterlite protected operational margins via long-term supply contracts and higher utilization of high-value cabling [HFCL concall Q2 FY27; Sterlite Technologies concall Q2 FY27].
- **Energy optimization & cost management**: Indus Towers and Bharti Airtel achieved structural energy savings by installing solar panels (Indus added ~3,000 solar sites in Q2, reaching 49,000 sites) and using AI-enabled dynamic RAM sleep cycles, cutting Indus's diesel consumption by 11% YoY [Bharti Airtel concall Q2 FY27; Indus Towers concall Q2 FY27].
- **Commercial & marketing spend**: Vodafone Idea reported sub-normal short-term margin expansion due to elevated marketing spend for its "Vi pe sab hain VIP" brand refresh and incremental network operating costs of Rs 99 crore QoQ as cell additions doubled [Vodafone Idea concall Q2 FY27].

## Capex & capacity
- **Core telecom operators**: Reliance Jio noted that peak capex is behind them, expecting annual capex over the next 4-5 years not to exceed FY26 levels [Jio Platforms concall Q2 FY27]. Bharti Airtel maintained its core capex guidance but acquired a Rs 2,900 crore strategic land parcel to build 1 GW of data center capacity [Bharti Airtel concall Q2 FY27]. Vodafone Idea doubled its quarterly capex QoQ to Rs 3,946 crore, targeting 3,000–3,500 unique 4G tower additions per month under its Rs 45,000 crore 3-year outlay [Vodafone Idea concall Q2 FY27].
- **Infrastructure & BESS expansion**: Pace Digitek operating at 100% of its 5 GWh BESS capacity is expanding to 10 GWh by Q4 FY27 [Pace Digitek concall Q2 FY27]. Sterlite Technologies and HFCL are running multi-year capacity expansion programs, with Sterlite committing ~Rs 1,000 crore annually through FY29 to scale preform and fiber capacities by 50% [Sterlite Technologies concall Q2 FY27; HFCL concall Q2 FY27].

## What changed since last quarter
- **HFCL Ltd**: Raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to at least 80% YoY (up from 40% guided in Q1 FY27 and initial 20%) following record quarterly results [HFCL concall Q2 FY27].
- **Sterlite Technologies Ltd**: Upgraded its long-term FY29 operational EBITDA margin target to ~30% (from 27% previously) while keeping its Rs 20,000 crore top-line goal unchanged [Sterlite Technologies concall Q2 FY27].
- **Pace Digitek Ltd**: Revised FY27 revenue guidance upward to Rs 3,300–Rs 3,500 crore (from Rs 3,200–Rs 3,400 crore) and formally paused new unfunded BOOT project bids to conserve capital [Pace Digitek concall Q2 FY27].
- **Bharti Airtel Ltd**: Shifted strategy from aggressive FWA deployment to fiberization of home passes, while committing Rs 2,900 crore to secure land for 1 GW data center capacity [Bharti Airtel concall Q2 FY27].
- **Suyog Telematics Ltd**: Pushed back its target timeline to reach a Rs 100 crore quarterly revenue run-rate from Q4 FY27 to September 2027 due to BSNL billing integration delays [Suyog Telematics concall Q2 FY27].

## What we were watching
- ✅ **Funding: Raises additional 4,000 crore rupees** (Vodafone Idea Ltd): Management confirmed securing over Rs 4,000 crore of incremental bank funding during Q2 FY27.
- ✅ **Capacity: Rs 45,000 Crore capex for 4G/5G network rollout under the 17-5-5 play by FY29** (Vodafone Idea Ltd): Management reiterated the Rs 45,000 crore 3-year capex commitment, placing over Rs 13,200 crore worth of capex orders.
- ✅ **Funding: Raised first tranche of funding of Rs 6,400 Crore via warrant proceeds and debt facilities** (Vodafone Idea Ltd): Management confirmed total funding secured reached over Rs 10,500 crore by H1 FY27.
- ✖ **Strategy: Restored 299 prepaid plan after slow additions** (Bharti Airtel Ltd): Management explicitly cited the discontinuation of the Rs 299 pack as a pricing intervention experiment, contradicting reports of plan restoration.

## What to watch next quarter
- **Vodafone Idea PSU Loan Closure**: Finalization and drawdown of the SBI-led Rs 35,000 crore public sector bank consortium loan facility.
- **BSNL 4G Execution & Revenue Realization**: Delivery of BSNL 4G expansion site orders for Tejas Networks (18,685+ sites) and resolution of unbilled site integration (~300 sites / Rs 30 crore capex) for Suyog Telematics.
- **Pace Digitek In-House Container Rollout**: Operationalization of trial production in November 2026 and commercial production in January 2027 for BESS containers, expected to lower BESS unit costs by 4%–5%.
- **HFCL Restructuring Recommendation**: Board approval of external advisor recommendations (E&Y) regarding the strategic restructuring of HFCL's EPC segment.
