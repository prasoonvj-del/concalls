# Telecom: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian telecom sector presented a largely positive narrative, characterized by record-breaking performances, surging optical fiber and cable demand driven by AI infrastructure, and the acceleration of 5G and BSNL rollouts. Companies like HFCL, Sterlite Technologies (STL), and Jio Platforms reported stellar financial metrics and robust order books. However, pockets of caution remain, particularly around execution bottlenecks, billing delays, and working capital pressures tied to specific operator rollouts.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Across the board, managements agree that demand for data centers, 5G network densification, and optical connectivity is surging. Global demand for optical fiber and cable is expanding, with CRU upgrading 2026 global demand projections (STL). Hyperscaler demand for AI-ready infrastructure is a major growth driver (HFCL, STL). 
* **Divergence:** While infrastructure and manufacturing players like HFCL, STL, and Pace Digitek are aggressively raising guidance and scaling capacities, tower and deployment companies show mixed sentiment. Indus Towers is optimistic about accelerating rollouts and Africa expansion, whereas **Suyog Telematics** struck a cautious tone, citing execution dependencies and billing delays tied to Vodafone Idea and BSNL funding tranches. Meanwhile, **Tejas Networks** is focused on executing its large BSNL 4G order while managing high inventory levels.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **HFCL** and **STL** noted margin expansions driven by richer product mixes, specialized high-fiber capacity, and connectivity solutions. HFCL termed spot preform prices "insane" due to non-availability, though long-term contracts protect them. STL noted that germanium and helium procurement constraints have eased.
* **Indus Towers** reported improved energy margins (-3.6% vs -4.8% YoY) due to an 11% reduction in diesel consumption, driven by solar deployments and lithium-ion batteries.
* **Pace Digitek** noted that lithium-ion cell prices, which rose ~20% last quarter, are stabilizing, and in-house container manufacturing is expected to reduce BESS costs by 4% to 5%. 
* **Tejas Networks** highlighted that high inventory levels are linked to the BSNL expansion order, with no recurring manufacturing process losses reported this quarter.

## Capex & capacity
* **HFCL:** Planned capex of ~Rs 1,800 crore across optical fiber, cable, and preform manufacturing; expanding optical fiber capacity to 38-43 million fiber km.
* **STL:** Committing ~Rs 1,000 crore per year through FY29 for brownfield and greenfield expansions.
* **Jio Platforms:** Management stated that peak capex is behind them; future capex for the next 4–5 years will not exceed last year's spend.
* **Pace Digitek:** Expanded BESS manufacturing to 5 GWh, targeting 10 GWh by Q4 FY27; paused new BOOT projects unless external investments are secured.
* **Suyog Telematics:** Budgeted Rs 200 crore capex for H2 FY27, funded via internal accruals and an ICICI Bank loan.
* **Indus Towers:** Growth capex reflects healthy order books, 10-14% tenancy upgrades, 5G upgrades, solar installations, and Africa expansion.
* **Tejas Networks:** Net debt increased to Rs 4,384 crore due to ongoing capex and operational costs.

## What changed since last quarter
*(Based on explicit company comparisons provided in summaries)*
* **Indus Towers:** Management's tone shifted slightly more optimistic regarding execution, driven by accelerating rollouts from a major customer. Promised Africa deployments commenced in Zambia and Uganda.
* **Suyog Telematics:** Tone shifted to a more measured pace due to persistent BSNL billing issues and operator funding dependencies. The target to reach a Rs 100 crore quarterly revenue run-rate was pushed out from Q4 to September 2027.

## Company one-liners
* **HFCL Ltd:** HFCL reported its best-ever quarterly performance driven by a 143% surge in revenue and robust demand from hyperscalers, alongside ambitious scaling in its optical connectivity and newly prioritized defense segments.
* **Indus Towers Ltd:** Indus Towers delivered a steady Q2 FY27 performance marked by a jump in co-locations, an improved tenancy ratio of 1.63, lower diesel costs, and early revenue generation from its Africa expansion.
* **Jio Platforms Ltd:** Jio Platforms delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 11.4% revenue growth, 250 bps margin expansion, and a net-debt-positive balance sheet following its landmark Rs. 30,188 crore IPO.
* **Pace Digitek Ltd:** Pace Digitek delivered strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 51.4%, raised its FY27 revenue guidance to Rs. 3,300–3,500 crore, and is scaling up its BESS manufacturing capacity to 10 GWh by Q4 FY27.
* **Sterlite Technologies Ltd:** STL delivered record-breaking Q2 FY27 performance with revenue up 145% YoY and operational EBITDA margins expanding to 27.1%, prompting management to raise its FY29 EBITDA margin target to 30%.
* **Suyog Telematics Ltd:** Suyog Telematics reported stable Q2 FY27 performance with a 60% EBITDA margin and a revenue of Rs 71.7 crore, keeping its H2 growth pinned on 2,000 targeted Vodafone Idea site deployments while pausing unbilled BSNL rollouts.
* **Tejas Networks Ltd:** Tejas Networks reported Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs 406 crore with a net loss of Rs 207 crore, while anticipating significant inventory reduction and debt lowering through the execution of its expanded BSNL 4G order.

## What to watch next quarter
* **HFCL:** Progress on EPC business restructuring, debt reduction, and defense/aerospace revenue scale-up.
* **Indus Towers:** Execution of deployments in Nigeria and resolution of tenancy renewal discussions.
* **Jio Platforms:** Post-IPO dividend policies, international managed service partnerships, and AI token monetization traction.
* **Pace Digitek:** Commercial production of C&I cabinets and execution of the expanded BESS manufacturing capacity.
* **Sterlite Technologies:** Realization of cash balances, net working capital days sustainability, and progress on commercializing Hollow-Core/Multi-Core fibers.
* **Suyog Telematics:** Resolution of BSNL billing integration for the ~300 stuck sites and execution pace of Vodafone Idea's 2,000+ site rollouts.
* **Tejas Networks:** Actual milestone collections from BSNL and the translation of high inventory into executed BSNL 4G shipments.
