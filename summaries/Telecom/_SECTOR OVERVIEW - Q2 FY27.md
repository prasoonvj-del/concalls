# Telecom: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season highlights a diverse operational landscape across the Indian telecom sector. Infrastructure and equipment providers like **HFCL** and **Tejas Networks** are benefiting heavily from large domestic state-led orders (such as BSNL 4G) and surging global optical and data center demand. Meanwhile, telecom service and digital platform providers like **Jio Platforms** are leveraging massive subscriber bases, robust 5G monetization, and asset-light international models following landmark IPO capital raises. On the enterprise communication and cable front, players like **GTPL Hathway** and **Route Mobile** are executing strategic domestic acquisitions and product expansions (AI-native platforms and HITS), though facing near-term profitability pressures from integration costs, forex impacts, and one-off provisions.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the board see robust underlying demand for data, high-speed broadband, and next-generation connectivity driven by AI infrastructure, hyperscale data centers, and fiber network upgrades.
* **Divergence (Enterprise vs. Consumer & Domestic vs. International):** 
  * **HFCL** reports that hyperscaler orders account for roughly 70% of its exports and views AI infrastructure demand as immune to macro rate hikes. 
  * **Jio Platforms** notes that India's mobility and home broadband markets still have massive headroom (projecting home broadband penetration to reach 46% by FY2031).
  * **Route Mobile** highlights robust demand for its new-product mix (RCS, WhatsApp, IP-messaging) and AI firewall solutions, though it notes softness in traditional long-distance (ILD) messaging.
  * **Tejas Networks** is seeing strong multi-year drivers across wireless (BSNL 4G expansion, global 5G FWA) and wireline (AI data center interconnects).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **HFCL:** Long-term contracts protect against immediate preform cost spikes, though spot preform prices were termed "insane" due to non-availability; margins are expanding via specialized high-fiber capacity and backward integration.
* **GTPL Hathway:** Operational EBITDA margin stood at 20%, with acquired ACT businesses currently at 12% expected to scale to 25%–26% post-integration. Profitability was dented by a Rs 45 million forex loss and Rs 30 million transponder amortization.
* **Route Mobile:** Gross margin recovered to 22.4%, but EBITDA margins remained pressured at 10% due to non-recurring items, including a security incident at its Colombian subsidiary (Masiv) and performance-bonus provisions.
* **Jio Platforms:** EBITDA margins expanded by 250 bps YoY to 54.1%, driven by operating leverage and fully capitalized 5G assets.

## Capex & capacity
* **Jio Platforms:** Management explicitly stated that peak capex is behind them; incremental 5G and broadband expansion will not require major capital outlays, and international expansion will follow an asset-light managed service model.
* **HFCL:** Undertaking aggressive capacity expansions, scaling optical fiber capacity from 28M to 38M (and subsequently 43M) fiber km, and planning an approximately Rs 1,800 crore capex program.
* **Tejas Networks:** Net debt increased to Rs 4,384 crore due to continued investments in R&D (5G Advanced, 6G, AI RAN) and working capital build-up for the BSNL 4G order.
* **GTPL Hathway:** Completed network migration to GPON/FTTH and is scaling its broadband home passes (reaching 6 million) alongside HITS platform deployment.

## What changed since last quarter
* **HFCL:** Raised FY27 revenue growth guidance to at least 80% (up from earlier aspirations of ~20%, then 40%).
* **Jio Platforms:** Successfully completed a landmark IPO raising Rs 30,188 crore with 24.5x oversubscription, turning the balance sheet net-debt positive.
* **GTPL Hathway:** Appointed a new CEO for broadband (Vivek Raina), rebranded broadband to TrueFiber, and completed the acquisition of ACT Group’s cable TV business.
* **Route Mobile:** Completed the acquisition of Heltar (AI-native no-code platform) for INR 33.7 cr on July 29, 2026.

## Company one-liners
* **GTPL Hathway Ltd:** Delivered solid 19% YoY revenue growth and expanded via strategic acquisitions, though profitability was temporarily dented by integration costs, forex losses, and amortization.
* **HFCL Ltd:** Reported its best-ever quarterly performance driven by a 143% surge in revenue and robust demand from hyperscalers alongside ambitious optical and defense scaling.
* **Jio Platforms Ltd:** Delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 11.4% revenue growth, 250 bps margin expansion, and a net-debt-positive balance sheet following its landmark Rs 30,188 crore IPO.
* **Route Mobile Ltd:** Revenue and gross margins improved on a higher-margin new-product base, but profit remained pressured by one-off costs and a soft long-distance segment.
* **Tejas Networks Ltd:** Reported Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs 406 crore with a net loss of Rs 207 crore, while anticipating significant inventory reduction and debt lowering through BSNL 4G order execution.

## What to watch next quarter
* **GTPL Hathway:** Realization of the projected 150–250 bps operational EBITDA margin expansion and post-integration scale of the ACT cable business.
* **HFCL:** Progress on EPC business restructuring decisions, debt reduction timelines, and execution of the Rs 18,500+ crore order book.
* **Jio Platforms:** Execution of international managed-service partnerships and monetization updates on AI/digital token offerings.
* **Route Mobile:** Normalization of operating costs post-one-offs and scaling of the newly integrated Heltar platform across enterprise clients.
* **Tejas Networks:** Actual cash collections from BSNL milestone deliveries and visible reduction in inventory and gross debt.

*Based on: GTPL Hathway Ltd, HFCL Ltd, Jio Platforms Ltd, Route Mobile Ltd, Tejas Networks Ltd*
