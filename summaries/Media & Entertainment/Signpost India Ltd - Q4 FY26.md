# Signpost India Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-10 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q4 & FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered robust revenue growth, margin expansion, and a transformational shift towards direct, long-term client relationships and expanded national footprint.

### Key numbers
- **Q4 FY26 Revenue from operations:** Rs 162 crore (up 46% YoY, 14% QoQ)
- **Q4 FY26 Gross profit:** Rs 67 crore (Gross margin: 41.5%)
- **Q4 FY26 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 42 crore (Margin: 26.3%)
- **Q4 FY26 Profit before tax (PBT):** Rs 27 crore
- **Q4 FY26 Net profit (PAT):** Rs 21 crore (Net margin: 13%)
- **Q4 FY26 Earnings per share (EPS):** Rs 3.95
- **FY26 Revenue from operations:** Rs 576 crore (up 27% YoY from Rs 453 crore)
- **FY26 Gross profit:** Rs 236 crore (up 33% YoY; Gross margin: 40.9%)
- **FY26 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 147 crore (up 65% YoY; EBITDA margin: 25.5%, up ~600 bps YoY from 19.6%)
- **FY26 Net profit (PAT):** Rs 70 crores (more than doubled YoY; Net margin: 12.2% from 7.5%)
- **FY26 Earnings per share (EPS):** Rs 13.14 (vs Rs 6.34 in FY25)
- **Footprint expansion:** Grew from 4 cities at listing in 2024 to 32 cities, adding 866,000 sq. ft. in FY26.
- **Anchor client revenue contribution:** Rose to around 29%.
- **FY27 Capex guidance:** Rs 60 crore to Rs 75 crore.
- **FY27 Revenue growth guidance:** Double-digit revenue growth, similar to previous year's trajectory (management noted underlying 20%+ growth potential).
- **FY27 EBITDA margin guidance:** 25% to 27% range.
- **Remaining contract tenure:** Average remaining tenure of roughly ~14 years (over 40 years of lineage period across existing contracts with 32 authorities).

### Highlights
- Maiden earnings conference call for the company.
- Transitioned from lower-quality intermediary-led work to direct, long-term relationships with blue-chip and digital-first advertisers.
- Expanded footprint by activating 9 new cities; presence now spans 32 cities managing over 15,000 assets (corrected from 12,500), reaching over 60 million people monthly.
- Scaled digital assets (e.g., Bangalore Metro network expansion, electric premium bus fleets across Mumbai, Hyderabad, Goa).
- Implemented a qualitative shift toward transit media (metros, airports, bus queue shelters, street furniture) and digital out-of-home (DOOH).
- Introduced Captura (proprietary AI-powered media planning and CRM platform) and established a dedicated R&D budget for data layering and AI analytics.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth to be in double digits (similar to FY26's 27% growth trajectory, targeting 20%+).
- EBITDA margin expected to remain in the 25% to 27% range, supported by operating leverage and rising DOOH contribution.
- Capex for FY27 guided at Rs 60 crore to Rs 75 crore across infrastructure, capacity expansion, and technology.
- Working capital cash collection cycle expected to improve significantly by Q3 FY27 due to shifting to a milestone-based billing and compliance mandate.
- Mid-term ambition to expand footprint to 100 cities nationwide via an asset-light model.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian organized out-of-home market grew 13% in 2025 to Rs 66.9 billion (FICCI-EY estimates), projected to reach Rs 85 billion by 2028.
- DOOH revenue rose from Rs 7 billion in 2024 to Rs 12.2 billion in 2025 (accounting for 18% of total OOH revenue, expected to reach 25% by 2028).
- Programmatically traded deals doubled to 30% in 2025.
- Transit media expanded 22% in 2025 to Rs 20.2 billion, contributing ~30% of OOH revenue.
- Demand is structural, driven by urbanization, transit infrastructure spending, and multi-touchpoint brand requirements from BFSI, FMCG, education, and PSUs/fintechs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of services primarily consists of fixed minimum-guarantee license fees/lease rents towards contracts, plus production/display costs. Revenue share models account for less than double digits in the portfolio.
- Management sees an internal scope for a 6% to 7% reduction in costs in the coming year without hurting the top line.
- Assets typically require a maturity period of 5 to 6 months to attain steady-state revenue streams.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex of Rs 60 crore to Rs 75 crore planned for FY27 for infrastructure, capacity, and technology.
- Expanding into smart cities, tourism hubs, and holy places (e.g., Varanasi, Puri, Ayodhya, Tirupati).
- Introduction of an asset-light model integrating regional asset owners across 100 cities using physical survey data and data layers (e-commerce, payment gateways, travel patterns).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables increased due to multi-city campaigns requiring complex regional compliance and headquarter consolidations.
- Bill discounting was explored but dismissed to avoid added costs; management is implementing milestone-based billing to bring collection cycles down to industry norms (90–120 days) by Q3 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Multi-city campaigns create short-term friction and delays in invoice approvals and cash collections from regional corporate offices.
- Front-loaded capex and license fees on newly won projects create a short-term mismatch before utilization ramps up.
- Lack of global-style traffic quality metrics in India requires custom data layering.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Zaki Nasser (Nasser Investments) on Receivable Increase:** Management explained that multi-city campaigns delayed regional compliance approvals. They are resolving this by implementing milestone-based billing, expecting a normalization/improvement by Q3 FY27.
2. **Kiran D. on Gross Margin and License Fees:** Asked if license fees scale linearly with revenue. Management clarified that India operates primarily on fixed minimum guarantee models (not revenue share), and internal cost-saving targets of 6-7% are expected to bolster PBT.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) on Bill Discounting & Margins:** Asked why bill discounting isn't used for high receivables. Management stated it adds unnecessary cost, preferring operational milestone-based billing fixes. 
4. **Aashav Patel (Molecule Ventures PMS) on Revenue Potential and Margins:** Inquired about asset maturity lags. Management noted that assets take 5 to 6 months to mature, and internal margin targets aim to push beyond conservative guidance if cost reductions materialize.
5. **Rohan Picha (Dexter Capital) on Merger Economics:** Questioned promoter holding drop post-Pressman merger. Management stated Pressman promoters converted equity due to personal family losses and retirement age (75+).

### One-line takeaway
Signpost India delivered a strong maiden financial year with 27% revenue growth and a 600 bps EBITDA margin expansion, pivoting toward direct blue-chip relationships, transit media leadership, and data-driven scalability.
