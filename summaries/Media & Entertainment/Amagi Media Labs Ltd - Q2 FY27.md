# Amagi Media Labs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record revenue growth, more than doubled adjusted EBITDA, and significant operating leverage alongside AI product delivery.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (gross margin zipcode 67% to 69% reaffirmed; durable 20% plus constant currency growth aspiration unchanged).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from pilots to production on AI, with four new AI products launched and NEWSPULSE moving into scale.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Introduced agentic media operations and agentic NOC discussions; no new risks added.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Delivered on the promise to introduce a broader product suite of AI offerings at flagship industry conferences (launched 4 AI products in Q2).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 476 crore (up 27% YoY reported, 33% ex-base; constant currency up 15% reported, 21% ex-base).
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 89.1 crore (up 114% YoY); Margin: 18.7% (expansion of 7.6 percentage points YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 75 crore; Margin: 14.9%.
- **Cash and investments:** Rs 1,774 crore with no debt.
- **Trailing 12-month NRR:** 125%.
- **Content processed:** 1 million+ hours (up 43% YoY).
- **Delivery endpoints:** 10,787+ channels (up 29% YoY).
- **Distributor count:** 477 (up 24% YoY).
- **Monetized ad impressions:** 16 billion (up 65% YoY).
- **Customers:** 503 total customers (+22 added in H1).

### Highlights
- Record quarterly revenue of Rs 476 crore.
- IDC MarketScape named Amagi a leader in the 2026 worldwide assessment of the AI-driven business cycle in the media industry.
- Launched four AI products: Amagi STUDIO, FANPULSE, Integrated Localization system, and Agentic NOC on the Amagi Intella platform.
- Proposed Dr. Bhaskar Ghosh (former Chief Strategy and Innovation Officer at Accenture) as an Independent Director and Srividhya Srinivasan as a Director to strengthen AI transformation expertise.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated gross margins will stay in the 67% to 69% range.
- Management expects Q3 to be another great quarter with elevated margin levels supported by the U.S. advertising peak and midterm elections.
- Management noted moving new products through the scaling phase will take more than one quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- TAM is estimated at $17 billion, which AI could nearly double over time.
- Streaming is close to half of U.S. TV viewing time, and connected television (CTV) accounts for 42.5% of total U.S. TV ad spend.
- Enterprise broadcast and streaming are unifying onto single platforms (e.g., local news network of a major U.S. cable network moved 60 regional feeds across 30 markets; Middle East network replaced on-prem playout across 13 channels).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin was 68.1% (down 1.6 percentage points YoY due to base effect, up 0.8 points QoQ).
- Indirect operating expenses fell from 58.6% of revenue to 49.3%.
- ESOP cost is 5.3% of revenue.
- Current quarter margins were inflated by more than 200 bps due to timing factors (favourable FX, delayed hiring, reserve releases); underlying margin was closer to 16.5%.
- Cloud FinOps generated $228,000 in monthly run-rate savings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operating cash flow conversion for H1 was 67% of Adjusted EBITDA.
- Free cash flow for H1 was Rs 85 crore against Rs 9 crore of capex.
- Active corporate development function evaluating M&A targets in video supply chain, distribution, and live sports production.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash balance closed at Rs 1,774 crore with no debt.
- Strong collections drove a lower Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and reduced underlying working capital absorption in H1 to Rs 14 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management cited their own execution as the primary area of focus and paranoia.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Manish Adukia (Goldman Sachs):** Asked about medium-term growth outlook and whether AI is a headwind or tailwind. Management stated AI is a clear tailwind and they are bullish on R&D investments, noting no deflationary impact on gross margins.
2. **Abhisek Banerjee (Analyst):** Inquired about M&A focus areas and R&D steady-state. Management noted M&A targets video supply chain/distribution/sports, and steady-state R&D is expected in the early 20% zipcode.
3. **Omprakash (Analyst):** Asked about AI pricing strategy for new products like NEWSPULSE. Management answered that pricing is consumption-driven with fixed minimum guarantees, moving toward outcome-driven models over time.
4. **Vamshi Krishna (Analyst):** Asked about differences in engagement for social short-form content versus traditional FAST channels. Management noted social platforms are shifting from marketing tools to content consumption platforms, driving demand for vertical video formats.
5. **Bharat Gulati (Dalal & Broacha):** Asked about CPM trends and insertion-to-impression economics. Management noted CPMs have stabilized rather than declined, and structural changes ensure gross margins do not suffer large standard deviations.

### One-line takeaway
Amagi posted record Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs 476 crore up 27% YoY, driven by strong operating leverage, a more than doubling of adjusted EBITDA to Rs 89.1 crore, and accelerated rollout of AI-driven products.
