# S Chand and Company Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong school education segment performance, robust cash surplus, and growth in content licensing despite seasonal losses.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenues: Rs1,145 million (up 12% YoY)
- EBITDA loss: Rs97 million
- PAT loss: Rs187 million
- Net cash balance: Rs1,182 million (vs Rs1,160 million in Q4 FY26) after dividend distribution of Rs141 million
- Content licensing revenue in Q1: Rs91 million (total nine-month/current tracking mentioned as Rs14 crores)
- CPD Singapore revenue in Q1: ~Rs1 crore (targeting over SGD 1 million / ~Rs7.5 crores by year-end)
- Total size of potential M&A targets: Rs40 crore to Rs50 crores at max
- Annual paper requirement purchased so far: ~25% (procured 3-4 months early)

### Highlights
- Refreshed curriculum businesses (Mylestone, My Zen, SmartK, Solid Steps) saw repeat adoptions nationwide.
- New partnerships formed with Allied, Jump Maths, Penguin, Speedlabs, and IIT Madras Pravartak Technologies Foundation (via Madhubun).
- Advanced paper purchases made early due to dollar appreciation (reaching ~Rs95) and geopolitical tensions in the Middle East.
- Printing and binding facility infrastructure is in process; partial operations to start in September/October, with full completion next year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guides for operating revenue growth of 10%-15% for FY27.
- EBITDA margin band guidance maintained at 17%-19% for the year.
- Content licensing revenue target: in excess of Rs40 crores, scaling clients from 5 to 10 this year.
- Core business steady-state growth expected at 10% to 12% annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Expect full adoption of new NCERT syllabus books over the course of FY27.
- Regional board growth supported by expected new syllabus implementation in West Bengal from next year.
- International school segment (~1,000 international schools in India) being targeted via the CPD Singapore acquisition.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Marketing expenses for Q2 were expensed in Q1, impacting quarterly numbers.
- Higher tax expenses due to a one-time adjustment of the tax rate to a lower rate increased PAT losses.
- Imported paper costs impacted by USD appreciation (~Rs95 vs ~Rs85 last year) and logistics costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New state-of-the-art printing and binding facility is underway; partial operations starting September-October, fully operational next year, designed to meet requirements for the next 10-15 years.
- Evaluating M&A opportunities in the test prep and school spaces (size Rs40-50 crores max).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivable days stable; Middle East geopolitical instability delayed some collections.
- Higher raw material and finished goods inventory due to strategic early paper procurement, leading to slightly higher net working capital days YoY.
- Company remains cash surplus with a net cash balance of Rs1,182 million.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical instability in the Middle East impacting collections.
- Foreign exchange volatility and logistics issues inflating imported paper costs.
- Goodwill sitting on the balance sheet depresses return ratios, though management stated impairment is not required currently due to intrinsic value.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Chandramouli (Investor) on CPD Singapore revenue and profitability:** Management stated Q1 revenue was ~Rs1 crore, targeting SGD 1 million (~Rs7.5 crores) by year-end; currently investing in content/marketing with limited burn.
- **Niteen (Aurum Edge) on early paper procurement rationale:** Management explained that dollar appreciation (~Rs95) and Middle East tensions prompted early procurement of ~25% of annual requirement to cushion price increases.
- **Niteen on buyback plans:** Management stated they are considering a market buyback and expect to decide around October once M&A opportunities crystallize.
- **Niteen on goodwill revaluation:** Management noted goodwill can only be written down through the P&L (causing losses) unless an exceptional income event or monetization of real estate/Smartivity occurs.
- **Chandramouli on long-term growth guidance:** Management guided for 10%-12% steady-state core growth, driven by 3-4% student volume growth and 4-5% pricing growth.

### One-line takeaway
S Chand reported a 12% YoY revenue growth in Q1 FY27 with a seasonal loss, while proactively securing early paper inventory and maintaining a strong net cash surplus of Rs1,182 million.
