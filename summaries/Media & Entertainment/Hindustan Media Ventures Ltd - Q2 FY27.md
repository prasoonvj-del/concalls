# Hindustan Media Ventures Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 5% YoY with improved EBITDA margins (17% vs 15% YoY) despite the festive season shifting to Q3.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** None provided (consistent with Q1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong growth" (Q1) to "consolidated gains" (Q2) due to the 20-day shift in the Diwali festive season.
- **New topics or risks:** Discussion on Matthew Cyriac’s 10% stake and potential activism; confirmation that the OTTplay business has been stopped.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Cost rationalization programs continue to reflect in lower employee expenses (consolidated Rs 95 crore vs Rs 99 crore QoQ).

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue (Consolidated):** Rs 502 crore (up 5% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Consolidated):** Rs 88 crore (up 26% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17% (vs 15% YoY)
- **PAT (Consolidated):** Rs 39 crore
- **PAT Margin:** 8% (vs 4% YoY)
- **Net Cash:** Rs 930 crore
- **Print Operating Revenue:** Rs 386 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **English Print Revenue Growth:** 5% YoY
- **Hindi Print Revenue Growth:** 11% YoY
- **Radio Operating Revenue:** Rs 25 crore (down 23% YoY)
- **Radio Operating EBITDA:** Rs 2 crore (positive)
- **Digital Operating Revenue:** Rs 28 crore (down 30% YoY)
- **Digital Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1 crore (breakeven/positive)

### Highlights
- Festive season shift (20 days) impacted Q2 revenue; management expects this to reflect in Q3 results.
- Print remains the anchor; ad revenue growth driven by volume consolidation and government (DAVP) price increases.
- Radio business rationalized by surrendering non-viable station licenses; achieved positive EBITDA of Rs 2 crore.
- Digital business recorded modest sequential improvement; focus remains on a leaner portfolio.
- Employee expenses reduced (consolidated Rs 95 crore vs Rs 99 crore QoQ) due to ongoing cost rationalization.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management provided no forward-looking estimates.
- Management believes newsprint costs have peaked but are not seeing downward impetus yet due to currency depreciation and global freight rates.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Ad revenue growth driven by volume gains and government price increases.
- Circulation revenue is flat (Rs 54 crore consolidated; Rs 39 crore HMVL).
- Circulation copies are flat; management is not pushing copies unnecessarily due to high newsprint costs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Newsprint costs rose industry-wide, pressuring margins.
- Cost rationalization program ongoing for indirect costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position: Rs 930 crore.
- "Other financial liabilities" (sundry deposits) of Rs 555 crore explained as related to Ad-for-Equity (AFE) investments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High newsprint costs and rupee depreciation.
- Uncertainty regarding government license fee structure for Radio.
- OTTplay business did not pan out.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash R. (Analyst) on Ad Revenue:** Asked about drivers. Management attributed growth to volume consolidation and government (DAVP) price increases.
- **Mohit Kumra (Analyst) on "Sundry Deposits":** Asked about the Rs 555 crore liability. Management clarified it relates to Ad-for-Equity (AFE) investments, with the counter-entry sitting in non-current assets.
- **Mohit Kumra (Analyst) on Matthew Cyriac’s 10% stake:** Asked if the investor is pushing for change. Management stated they are in conversation with the shareholder but emphasized the Board runs the company.
- **Mehul Pathak (Analyst) on Radio Outlook:** Asked if Radio will remain profitable. Management stated they are not slipping back into the red as loss-making stations were surrendered.
- **Mehul Pathak (Analyst) on Unallocated Losses:** Asked about the Rs 12.37 crore unallocated loss. Management explained it includes corporate costs, KMP remuneration, and mark-to-market valuations.

### One-line takeaway
HMVL delivered a resilient quarter with 5% revenue growth and margin expansion, successfully navigating a festive season shift and high newsprint costs through cost rationalization and radio network optimization.
