# Entertainment Network (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Subdued advertising demand and a festive shift weighed on revenue, but profitability improved through structural cost measures and lower digital losses.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: INR117 crores (including INR3 crores from international operations)
- Domestic revenue: INR114 crores, down 16.1% YoY
- EBITDA: INR11.3 crores, up 9.5% YoY
- Non-FCT (Events) revenue: INR20 crores
- Non-FCT EBITDA margins: 30.8% (compared to 27.8% in Q2 previous year)
- Digital EBITDA loss: INR7.5 crores, down from INR9.8 crores in the corresponding quarter of the previous year (reduction of 23.3%)
- Consolidated cash balance: INR371.5 crores as of September 30, 2026 (down from INR424 crores in March '26 and INR409 crores in June '26)
- Radio FCT revenue: Down 12.6% YoY (~INR60 odd crores)
- Inventory utilization: ~70% (down about 6%)
- Market share (Radio): ~25%

### Highlights
- Revenue stream evolution: Radio contributes ~53% of domestic revenue, digital 29%, and events/other businesses balance the rest.
- Profitability improved via structural cost changes, cost rationalization, improved operating efficiencies, and lower digital losses.
- Announced a partnership with Noise to leverage complementary consumer ecosystems for subscriber acquisition on Gaana.
- Digital revenue contribution to domestic revenue rose to 29% from 23% in the corresponding quarter last year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not generally give formal guidance.
- Management noted some green shoots in the advertising environment in Q3, which they believe should continue.
- Gaana EBITDA breakeven target: Management stated it remains an ambition, though higher acquisition costs and D2C headwinds might lead to a delay of 1 or 2 quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Subdued advertising demand impacted the radio business.
- Shift in festive dates (Diwali being late) moved advertising and event revenue into Q3.
- Media industry is going through a transition phase impacted by digital and AI, but management remains optimistic about India's GDP and advertising growth potential.
- Increased competition in the events business from TV, news, and internet companies is putting pressure on margins.
- Experiential marketing/going out is a growing trend, but concert margins remain low.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Structural cost measures and cost rationalization drove EBITDA growth despite lower revenue.
- Non-FCT margins are significantly lower than traditional radio margins (approx. 1/4 or 1/5 of radio margins).
- Effective ad rates were flat in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Radio capacity utilization was ~70%.
- Capex was deployed in the digital business on the tech side during the quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a strong balance sheet with consolidated cash balance of INR371.5 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- Decrease in cash balance was attributed to vendor advances for upcoming events, dividend payments, and digital tech capex (described as cyclical/seasonal).
- Other current assets increased due to an accumulation of prepaid items.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subdued advertising environment and geopolitical situation impacting traditional radio.
- Higher customer acquisition costs in the D2C ecosystem (Gaana) driven by AI search reducing referral traffic and intense competition among OTT/music players.
- Increased competition in the events space from TV, news, and internet companies putting pressure on margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mehul Madhwal (Equirus) asked about inventory utilization and realization.** Management replied that utilization was ~70% (down 6%) and effective rates were flat.
2. **Surender Singh (My Equity Sherpa) questioned the low ROCE of the radio business and rationale for future license renewals.** Management cited industry-wide transition due to digital/AI, ongoing cost structure improvements, and positive discussions with the government regarding regulatory reforms.
3. **Jay Daniel (Entropy Advisors) questioned why digital/Gaana revenues have flatlined and if the Q4 EBITDA breakeven target is intact.** Management explained they do not chase unprofitable subscriber numbers due to high acquisition costs, and breakeven might be delayed by 1-2 quarters while unit economics remain the priority. *(Management did not directly answer the exact quarterly revenue breakdown for Gaana on the spot, offering a separate meeting instead).*
4. **Jay Daniel asked about the declining cash balance.** Management attributed it to vendor advances for events, dividend payouts, and digital tech capex.
5. **Tanushi (Individual Investor) asked about volume growth and effective rates.** Management stated volume/utilization was down 5%-6% (~70% utilization) and effective rates were flat.

### One-line takeaway
ENIL reported a mixed Q2 FY27 with a 16.1% decline in domestic revenue due to subdued radio demand and a festive shift, but expanded EBITDA by 9.5% YoY via structural cost controls and reduced digital losses.
