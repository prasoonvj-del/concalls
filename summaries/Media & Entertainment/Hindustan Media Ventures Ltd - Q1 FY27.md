# Hindustan Media Ventures Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew year-on-year and profitability improved significantly due to steady advertising revenue and disciplined cost management.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue (Consolidated):** INR 497 crore (up 15% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Consolidated):** INR 90 crore (up nearly 3x YoY)
- **PAT (Consolidated):** INR 47 crore 
- **PAT Margin:** 9%
- **Net Cash Position:** INR 922 crore
- **Print Segment Revenue:** INR 376 crore (up 16% YoY)
- **Print Advertising Revenue:** INR 295 crore (up 15% YoY)
- **Print Circulation Revenue:** INR 52 crore (virtually flat YoY)
- **Print Operating EBITDA:** INR 50 crore (margin at 13%)
- **English Print Advertising Revenue:** INR 156 crore (up 12% YoY)
- **English Print Circulation Revenue:** INR 13 crore (up 14% YoY)
- **Hindi Print Advertising Revenue:** INR 139 crore (up YoY)
- **Radio Operating Revenue:** Flat YoY; Operating EBITDA at negative INR 3 crore
- **Digital Operating Revenue:** Down 28% YoY; Operating EBITDA at negative INR 3 crore (margin at negative 12%)

### Highlights
- Consolidated revenue grew 15% YoY to INR 497 crore; EBITDA went up nearly 3x YoY to INR 90 crore.
- Print remained the anchor; ad revenue grew on yield improvements and commercial/government performance.
- Radio operating on a leaner footprint after surrendering non-viable station licenses.
- Digital portfolio deliberately reset around leaner, focused offerings for sustainable/profitable growth.
- Board approved a preferential issue (in HT Media and Digicontent) to strengthen capital structure, streamline debt, and fund general business requirements.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is hopeful of maintaining a good set of numbers going forward, though no specific forward guidance on revenue or earnings is given.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Commercial advertising revenues held up from a volume perspective alongside yield improvements.
- Government advertising revenue benefitted from rate hikes implemented by the government in November of last year.
- Print circulation copies remain steady market by market.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Newsprint prices are at a peak of USD 650 to USD 700 per metric ton post-COVID; dollar strength is adding cost pressure (double whammy). Management believes prices have peaked and should plateau.
- Employee costs reduced YoY (consolidated employee expenses down to ~INR 99 crore from INR 111 crore) due to organizational right-sizing and efficiency drives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net cash stands at a healthy INR 922 crore (parked in HMVL).
- HT Media Limited and Digicontent Limited run a net debt position, which the proposed preferential issue aims to partially retire (30% to 50% of debt).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated newsprint prices, a weaker rupee, and global supply chain uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ranga Prasad (Retail Shareholder) on Preferential Issue vs. Rights Issue:** Questioned why a preferential offer of equity shares at ~INR 24 (vs. book value ~INR 70) was chosen over a rights issue, noting dilution for public shareholders. **Management Answer:** Stated preferential issue is a faster process (2-3 months faster), offers greater certainty of fundraising without underwriting risks/quasi-preferential allotment issues, and addresses debt sitting specifically in HT Media/Digicontent (whereas cash sits in HMVL). Pricing strictly follows SEBI formulas.
2. **Mehul Pathak (Retail Shareholder) on Preferential Pricing/Valuation & Debt:** Raised concerns about valuation and ethics of issuing shares at INR 24.7 when intrinsic value/book value is higher, and questioned past statements regarding debt usage in the business. **Management Answer:** Reiterated that the preferential issue is priced according to SEBI regulations (VWAP-based) in indebted entities, and debt has accumulated over 4-5 post-COVID years primarily in HT Media's English print and radio operations.
3. **Rohan Agarwal (Wave Asset PMS) on Other Income and Newsprint Costs:** Asked about the nature of treasury/other income jumps and outlook on newsprint. **Management Answer:** Explained other income includes treasury gains from mutual funds/NAV processes and realized profits on sale of assets. Stated newsprint is at peak levels (~USD 650-700/tonne) but expected to plateau, allowing stable operating print margins around 13% barring extreme currency/commodity movements.
4. **Yash R. (Analyst) on Ad Revenue Drivers:** Asked what drove the 15% ad revenue growth during a difficult quarter. **Management Answer:** Driven by yield/pricing improvements across commercial channels and the impact of government rate revisions from November last year.
5. **Mahima (Analyst) on Separate Financial Consolidations/Con-calls:** Asked if separate con-calls for HMVL and HT Media would provide better transparency. **Management Answer:** Management preferred holding a combined con-call while publishing separate financial results.

### One-line takeaway
HMVL and HT Media delivered a strong Q1 FY27 marked by 15% revenue growth and nearly 3x EBITDA expansion driven by advertising yield gains and cost discipline, even as newsprint cost pressures persist.
