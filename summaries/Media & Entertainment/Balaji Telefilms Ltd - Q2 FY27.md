# Balaji Telefilms Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong profitability driven by the film segment and commission business on a debt-free balance sheet.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** INR 181 crore (vs INR 48 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit before tax (PBT):** INR 44 crore (vs loss of INR 6.6 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit after tax (PAT):** INR 32.5 crore post deferred tax charge of INR 12.4 crore (vs loss of INR 5 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** INR 421.4 crore (vs INR 121.6 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** INR 72.9 crore (vs loss of INR 14.4 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 54.9 crore post deferred tax charge of INR 18 crore (vs loss of INR 10.9 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 segment revenue & PBT - Films:** Revenue INR 106.3 crore, PBT INR 41.24 crore
- **Q2 FY27 segment revenue & PBT - Commission business:** Revenue INR 69.7 crore, PBT INR 7.03 crore
- **Q2 FY27 segment revenue & loss - Digital B2C:** Revenue INR 5.06 crore, segmental loss INR 3.92 crore
- **Digital B2B order book:** Exceeding INR 350 crore
- **Cash reserves:** Approximately INR 200 crore
- **YouTube subscribers:** More than 11 million (added around 6.5 lakh in a few months); combined YouTube and Meta subscriber base over 21 million
- **Film box office collections:** *Bhooth Bangla* crossed INR 240 crore worldwide; *Vvan* crossed INR 90 crore worldwide gross collections
- **Pipeline (27 to 31):** 30+ movies, 15+ television shows, 25+ OTT series, 500+ micro dramas, 10+ YouTube shows

### Highlights
- Management introduced "Balaji 3.0", an integrated studio model merging digital and Marinating Films into Balaji Telefilms to serve films, television, streaming apps, YouTube, and phones.
- *Lock Upp* season 1 became a global breakout on Netflix (number 1 non-English unscripted show globally, top 10 in 12 countries), driving new sign-ups; season 2 renewed to start in February.
- Strategic partnership signed with YouTube for 5 premium original shows and 200 episodes with an MG deal while retaining IP.
- Under the record, the recent merger of ALT Digital Media and Marinating Films into Balaji Telefilms is part of forming the integrated production engine for Balaji 3.0.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects television content to contribute around INR 125 crore to INR 175 crore each year.
- Out of the INR 350 crore digital B2B order book, management expects INR 150 crore to be recognized in the current financial year, with a conservative run-rate of INR 150 crore to INR 175 crore annually from the OTT commission segment.
- Management targets a steady slate of 4 to 6 films a year with production budgets in the INR 60 crore to INR 75 crore range.
- Management expects half-yearly PBT margins of 17% to 18% to be sustainable going forward.
- Digital B2C business is expected to take 1.5 years to scale meaningfully, with cash positivity expected by the end of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's micro drama market is projected by Lumikai to reach USD 4.5 billion by 2030 (from USD 300 million in 2025).
- Global streaming platforms in India require reliable studios that deliver on time and budget.
- YouTube has become prime time for millions of Indians, shifting consumption towards Connected TV and hybrid subscription/ad models.
- Broadcast content spend on television has cut down, causing viewers to move to Connected TV and OTT platforms, though TV remains strong.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Deferred tax charge for Q2 FY27 was INR 12.4 crore (non-cash item).
- Film presales average 80% to 90% recovery of production costs before release across the previous 6 movies, capping downside risk.
- Digital B2C segment reported losses due to ongoing investments in content initiatives.
- Content library items do not carry intangibles on the balance sheet; library monetization flows straight to EBITDA with zero P&L cost.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocated to new opportunities including AI (in-house team set up), owning music labels, and movie distribution using the INR 200 crore cash reserves.
- Pipeline of 500+ micro dramas across three fronts: *Kutingg* (own platform), *Balaji Studio* (creator ecosystem for third-party/own platforms), and library adaptations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a debt-free balance sheet with cash reserves of approximately INR 200 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Theatrical windows are limited and competitive, with A-listers and external factors like IPL, vacations, and exams restricting release slots.
- Investment in the micro drama market by industry participants remains cautious.
- Broadcast TV spend has reduced.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sanchita Sood (RoboCapital)** asked about upcoming film releases and revenue guidance for H2 FY27/FY28. Management stated *Hero Ki Horroin* and *Ragini 3* are slated for this financial year, followed by *Heer Ranjha* and *Obsessive Lover* next year, but declined to provide forward-looking revenue numbers.
- **Yash Parker (Patil Investments)** asked about factors driving Q2 PBT margin (24-25%) and sustainability. Management attributed the jump to the spillover and successful box office run of *Bhooth Bangla*, guiding a sustainable half-yearly PBT margin of 17% to 18%.
- **Chaitrika Deshpande (Capital Advisors)** inquired about the sequential decline in Q2 revenue despite higher PBT. Management explained it was due to product mix (absence of high-yield weekend TV show *Naagin* and lower top-line contribution from *Vvan* compared to *Bhooth Bangla*).
- **Vinay Jain (JM Investments)** asked about the YouTube partnership economics. Management clarified it is an MG deal covering maximum production costs, supplemented by sponsorships, brand integrations, ad revenue, and future syndication/licensing rights as Balaji retains the IP.
- **Arvind Singh (Betra Investments)** asked about managing theatrical fluctuations. Management reiterated commitment to a steady schedule of 4 to 5 movies annually to smooth out volatility.

### One-line takeaway
Balaji Telefilms reported strong Q2 profitability driven by successful film releases (*Bhooth Bangla*) and commission projects, leveraging a debt-less balance sheet and a multi-format content strategy across OTT, YouTube, and micro dramas.
