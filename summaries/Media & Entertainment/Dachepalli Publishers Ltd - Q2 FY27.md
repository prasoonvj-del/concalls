# Dachepalli Publishers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong top-line growth and highlighted strategic moves into notebook manufacturing and acquisitions, offsetting near-term margin pressures from raw material costs.

### Key numbers
- Total Income: Rs. 70.06 crores (YoY growth of 74%)
- Net Profit (PAT): Rs. 9.71 crores (YoY growth of 29%)
- EBITDA: Rs. 14.63 crores (vs. Rs. 11.71 crores YoY)
- FY27 Revenue Guidance: Rs. 150 crores (+/- 10%)
- FY28 Revenue Guidance: Rs. 225 crores to Rs. 230 crores
- FY27 PAT Margin Guidance: 14% to 16% (previously stated as 14%-15% later in call)
- Paper purchase price (90% of raw material): Rs. 78 to Rs. 81 (current paper price around Rs. 93)
- Acquisition cost for Hara Communications: Rs. 10 crores to Rs. 15 crores
- Existing debt: Rs. 14 crores to Rs. 16 crores, plus planned Rs. 7 crores to Rs. 8 crores bank loan for acquisition

### Highlights
- Entered in-house notebook manufacturing via acquisition of machinery and launched the brand "PageCraft" to control quality and supply timelines.
- Developing "Pelican EduSupply" to bundle textbooks, notebooks, and supplies for schools; on-boarded 100 schools for the coming academic year.
- Partnered with Wiki Kids as sole Indian distributor for interactive flat panel (IFT) smart classroom displays.
- In talks to acquire Hara Communications to utilize its land, assets, and software for bundled school offerings and expanded production capacity.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 revenue target of Rs. 150 crores (+/- 10%) and FY28 target of Rs. 225 crores to Rs. 230 crores.
- Management maintains full-year PAT margin guidance of 14% to 16%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Seasonal nature of the business causes H1 expenditure to outpace income, with major turnover and collections kicking in during H2 (Q3 and Q4).
- Diverse geographic presence across 13 states stabilizes revenue flows compared to peers dependent on single regions.
- Army school transfers occurring in their 4th year drove repeat orders extending through August and September.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins were impacted by war-driven fluctuations in paper prices and increased marketing and sales spending in H1.
- 90% of raw material paper requirements for the annual target have been purchased in advance at Rs. 78-81, insulating the company from current prices of Rs. 93.
- MRPs are planned to be increased by 10% as a safety measure.
- Traded notebooks previously yielded a PAT of 6-7%, whereas in-house manufactured PageCraft notebooks are expected to yield around 12% PAT.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Notebook manufacturing machinery capacity stands at Rs. 30 crores to Rs. 40 crores.
- Hara Communications acquisition (valued at asset level of Rs. 10+ crores) will be funded via existing receivables and a Rs. 7 crore to Rs. 8 crore bank loan.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventories increased due to in-house production buildup and advance raw material stocking.
- Trade receivables increased alongside top-line growth; management expects positive operating cash flow for the full year.
- Total debt expected to include existing Rs. 14-16 crores plus ~Rs. 7 crores new bank loan for the plant acquisition, serviced via EMIs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Paper prices have risen and are estimated by management to potentially reach Rs. 100.
- Hardware business (IFT panels) introduces new operational risks including service and installation challenges.
- High cyclicality in working capital and large outstanding receivables during interim periods.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhur Rathi (Investments) asked about missing margin guidance and rising receivables.** Management stated that H1 margins were squeezed by paper prices and higher marketing spends, which will average out in H2, and that receivables grew in tandem with turnover and remain cyclical.
2. **Madhur Rathi asked about inventory build-up and cash flows.** Management explained that 90% of paper was bought in advance at Rs. 79-81 to hedge against rising prices, and full-year operating cash flow is expected to turn positive.
3. **Disha (Analyst) asked about the contribution of trading revenue vs. manufacturing.** Management clarified that notebooks previously constituted 10-12% of turnover via trading, but are now transitioning to in-house manufacturing under PageCraft to boost margins from 6-7% to ~12%.
4. **Guneet Singh asked about the rationale for acquiring Hara Communications.** Management stated they are acquiring the company purely at asset value (land and software) for Rs. 10-15 crores to expand production capacity and bundle software with textbooks.
5. **Shahid Chokshi asked if raw materials are fully secured to meet guidance.** Management confirmed 90% of raw material is already secured, providing a cushioning against expected further price increases.

### One-line takeaway
Dachepalli Publishers delivered strong H1 revenue growth of 74% and secured 90% of its paper requirements at lower-than-market rates, supporting management's full-year margin and revenue guidance despite near-term working capital and raw material headwinds.
