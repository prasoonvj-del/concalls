# Bodhi Tree Multimedia Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-12 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q4 and FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial compounding, successful strategic acquisitions, and robust margin expansion driven by the transition toward IP-led content.

### Key numbers
- **FY26 Total Income:** Rs 118.45 crore (up 32% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA:** Rs 17.1 crore (up 76% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA Margin:** 14.44% (vs 10.78% YoY)
- **FY26 PAT:** Rs 7.95 crore (up 62% YoY)
- **FY26 PAT Margin:** 6.71%
- **3-Year CAGR (FY24-FY26):** Revenue 36%, EBITDA 68%, PAT 50%
- **Q4 FY26 Total Income:** Rs 36.07 crore
- **Q4 FY26 EBITDA:** Rs 5.96 crore (up 56% YoY)
- **Q4 FY26 EBITDA Margin:** 16.52% (vs 9.73% YoY)
- **Q4 FY26 PAT:** Rs 2.08 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **Revenue Mix:** 80% to 85% commissioned content, 10% to 15% IP-related revenues
- **Little Adda Company (IP):** Crossed 728,000 subscribers in 4 months with 100 million+ views

### Highlights
- Completed the acquisition of a 50.01% controlling stake in Moving Images Studios (led by Manisha Sharma), focusing on digital-first unscripted IP.
- Acquired a 20% strategic stake in Lehren Networks (vintage film library and YouTube CMS).
- Formally operationalized Bodhi AI in FY26, including 'CastMatch AI', with goals to achieve 30% to 40% time savings and 20% to 40% cost savings.
- Expanded the creator-led ecosystem with Madlabs Alpha, High Concept OTT and Film Studio (Gaurav Shukla), Guroudev Bhalla Screens, and Amit Khan Content Hub.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reaffirmed 3-year targets: Rs 250 crore in revenue, Rs 25 crore in PAT, and a 50-50 IP-to-services revenue mix.
- Target to reach an IP revenue contribution of 50% in 2 years.
- Plans to scale Bodhi Tree into 20+ international markets and roll out Bodhi AI group-wide.
- Management indicated potential future fundraising plans and intentions to significantly increase promoter holdings over the next 2 to 3 years.
- Board under active discussion regarding consolidating shares back to a Rs 10 face value.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's M&E industry stood at USD 32 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 38 billion in 2028. OTT segment expected to hit USD 24 billion by 2030.
- Digital media (USD 12 billion) has overtaken television as the largest M&E segment in India.
- India has 975 million OTT/digital viewers (66% of population); paid subscriptions at 216 million; connected TV households at 143 million (projected 191 million by 2028).
- Regional content drives approximately 56% of OTT viewership.
- Massive ownership gap identified: India creates 50,000+ hours of content annually, but less than 1% of IP is independently owned by creators.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage resulted in expanding EBITDA margins, hitting a high of 16.52% in Q4 FY26.
- Policy changes made to account for IP-led businesses involving asset/inventory capitalization and subsequent amortization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Funding for acquisitions and future development initiatives came from the previous year's rights issue proceeds and internal accruals.
- Payment cycles in the content business remain milestone-based or credit-period-based, with IP revenue cycles being longer due to gestation and royalty structures.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- IP monetization has longer cycles than commissioned content, impacting near-term cash flows until royalty accruals and recoveries begin.

### One-line takeaway
Bodhi Tree Multimedia delivered a strong FY26 with 32% revenue growth and 76% EBITDA growth, advancing its strategic transition from commissioned production toward an IP-led, multi-platform content model.
