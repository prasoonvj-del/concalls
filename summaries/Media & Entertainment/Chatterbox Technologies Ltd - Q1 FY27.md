# Chatterbox Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-03 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** FY26 (Year ended 31st March 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial growth, successful IPO, and expanding service/geographic verticals, backed by structural macro tailwinds in the creator economy.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 84.22 crore (vs Rs 59.12 crore in FY25, up ~42.5% YoY)
- Total income: Rs 84.94 crore
- EBITDA: Rs 14.32 crore (vs Rs 12.16 crore in FY25)
- EBITDA margin: 17%
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 9.20 crore (vs Rs 8.86 crore in FY25)
- PAT margin: 10.9%
- Net worth: Rs 70.06 crore as of FY26 (strengthened by IPO)
- Trade receivables: Rs 26 crore (with ~Rs 17.5 crore not due)
- Revenue mix by vertical: Chtrbox Represent (39.27%), Brand Solutions (36.18%), ChtrSocial (24.55%)
- Geographic revenue mix: Domestic (~82%), International (~18%)
- Industry growth metrics cited: India creator economy expected to exceed $1 trillion in annual spending by 2030 (BCG report); creator economy growing at a CAGR of 25% to 30% globally.

### Highlights
- Completed maiden IPO in FY26, becoming India's first publicly listed influencer marketing company.
- Diversified revenue streams across six business verticals: Chtrbox Represent, ChtrSocial, BharatBox, Youth:ink, Chtr Studios, and Chtr International.
- Expanded international footprint via a Dubai subsidiary serving as a gateway into the Middle East.
- Maintained a creator roster of over 100 exclusive creators, executed over 2,000 campaigns, and worked with over 500 brands.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is optimistic about sustaining healthy growth over the medium to long term, driven by an industry CAGR of 25% to 30%.
- Management expects to double down on revenue for the ChtrSocial business in upcoming months.
- Management targets margin expansion at the bottom line by streamlining opex costs.
- Guidance status: <none> (explicitly stated: "While we don't really provide specific financial guidance...").

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by a combination of deeper engagement with existing enterprise clients, higher campaign counts, new client wins, and international expansion.
- Union Budget 26-27 formally recognized the "orange economy," signaling government backing for the sector.
- Brands are increasingly consolidating social media management, content creation, and influencer marketing with integrated partners.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins moderated during FY26 due to investments in talent acquisition, platform capabilities, business expansion, and scaling new verticals.
- ChtrSocial and the production arm generate the highest margin contributions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Resuming development and planning the launch of the edutainment short-form video app 'AAGE' in FY27.
- International expansion focused on the Middle East (GCC) through the Dubai subsidiary, with selective evaluation of Southeast Asia, LATAM, and other markets using a lean approach.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO significantly strengthened the balance sheet, taking net worth to Rs 70.06 crore.
- Standard credit period is 60 days, with actual realization cycles around 60 to 90 days (70 to 75 days average).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Forward-looking statements involve known and unknown risks beyond management's control.
- Margin moderation experienced during FY26 due to upfront strategic investments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mayuresh Raut (Invest for Value) on growth drivers:** Management stated growth will be multi-engine, led by ChtrSocial, proprietary technology/AI capabilities, exclusive talent management (Represent), and international expansion.
2. **Shruti Malpani (Aarth AIF) on the 'AAGE' app:** Management clarified it is an edutainment short-form video app mixing learning with gamification for Gen Z/young millennials, targeted for launch in FY27.
3. **Vansh Rathore (Individual) on revenue growth composition:** Management confirmed growth came from a mix of deeper wallet share with existing enterprise clients, higher campaign execution, and international efforts.
4. **Chaitrika (Unnamed firm) on the Dubai subsidiary and international strategy:** Management stated the Middle East is the immediate priority (leveraging QYOU's existing client network), focusing on lean, strategic expansion rather than high-burn multi-market entry.
5. **Keshav (BHH Securities) on stock price performance post-listing and potential QYOU Media merger:** Management (Curt Marvis) responded that patience, perseverance, and building core assets/accretive M&A are key to long-term value, declining to comment on a specific merger structure.
6. **Vishal (Unnamed firm) on bottom-line margin expectations for FY27:** Management stated they are targeting higher bottom-line margins through opex streamlining.

### One-line takeaway
Chatterbox delivered a robust FY26 with 42.5% revenue growth following its maiden IPO, focusing on integrated full-stack creator solutions, technological capabilities, and disciplined international expansion.
