# Signpost India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management maintains FY27 growth guidance, citing strong H2 project ramp-ups and a successful strategic pivot toward high-value direct clients and experiential media despite Q2 festive-led softness.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (H1):** Rs 286.8 crore (+6% YoY).
*   **Revenue (Q2):** Rs 134.5 crore (vs Rs 134 crore YoY).
*   **PAT (H1):** Rs 34.5 crore (+11% YoY).
*   **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 61.4 crore (tripled YoY).
*   **EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 28.4 crore (Margin: 21.1%).
*   **EBITDA (H1):** Rs 62.8 crore (Margin: 21.9% vs 24.4% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 195 crore (vs Rs 196.1 crore in March).
*   **Debt-to-Equity:** 0.61x.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Digital media now accounts for 34% of revenue (up from 26% in 2026).
*   **Client Quality:** Direct client revenue (excluding agency-aligned) grew 76% YoY in H1. The company reduced active direct billers by 35% (347 to 226) while increasing average revenue per biller by ~170%.
*   **Accounting Change:** Effective April 1, 2026, the company shifted depreciation method from WDV to SLM.
*   **Rating:** CRISIL upgraded long-term rating to A- Stable.
*   *Record Check:* The company continues its pivot to direct, long-term client relationships and experiential media, consistent with previous calls.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains double-digit revenue growth guidance for FY27.
*   Expects a stronger H2 in both revenue and profitability, driven by the full festive season falling in Q3 and project ramp-ups.
*   Targeting a 120-day receivable cycle by March 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Q2 performance impacted by the festive calendar shift (Navratri moved from September to October) and cautious spending by multinational/global clients due to fuel costs and global economic scenarios.
*   Focusing on "culture-led" experiential projects (e.g., Kolkata Durga Puja, Bangalore Metro) to drive engagement and "earned" media (user-generated content).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Services:** Rose 6% to Rs 83 crore in Q2, primarily fixed fees on long-tenure media rights.
*   **Employee Costs:** Increased to Rs 13.9 crore (vs Rs 11.2 crore) due to annual increments and strategic hiring.
*   **Other Expenses:** Declined 7% to Rs 9.2 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **H1 Capex:** Rs 48 crore, funded through internal accruals.
*   **Asset Activation:** Bangalore Metro (~50% activated; targeting 75-100% by March) and Kolkata streetscape (40-45% live; targeting completion by March).
*   **Yield Enhancement:** Prioritizing upgrades of existing sites (e.g., Corporation Junction, Bangalore) to digital/tech-led formats to increase revenue per square foot without adding new concessions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Trade Receivables:** Declined by Rs 44.2 crore to Rs 273.1 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** Neutral in H1 (vs absorbing Rs 47 crore in H1 last year).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Operational Constraints:** Installation speed on operational transit infrastructure is limited to 4-hour nightly windows (12 a.m. to 4 a.m.).
*   **Receivables:** Management acknowledged that while efforts are in the right direction, the current receivable level is not yet at the desired target.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: How will you monetize the 10,000+ display points?* Management: By focusing on long-term client relationships (e.g., Amul, Hyundai) and upgrading existing assets with technology/digital formats rather than just adding new ones.
*   *Q: Status of Nagpur Airport?* Management: No major revenue shift expected in the next year; growth depends on Phase 1 completion and traffic quality, not just passenger numbers.
*   *Q: Why the lumpiness in quarterly revenue?* Management: Attributed to external factors like festive timing and global market conditions; urged investors to evaluate performance on an annual basis.
*   *Q: Is the BMRCL (Bangalore Metro) licensing fee fully in the P&L?* Management: Yes, fees are covered in Q1/Q2 regardless of the specific number of stations activated.

### One-line takeaway
Signpost is successfully pivoting to a high-value, direct-client model and experiential media, with management maintaining FY27 growth guidance despite Q2 festive-led softness.
