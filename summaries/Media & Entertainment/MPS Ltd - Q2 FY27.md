# MPS Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record half-year profitability driven by operating leverage, with management raising the EBITDA floor guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised. FY27 EBITDA floor increased from "comfortably cross Rs 300 crore" to "at least Rs 310 crore."
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted to a more confident outlook on the second half, citing new mandates and the US political environment (Democratic majority seen as favorable for research/healthcare funding).
- **New topics:** ISO 42001 certification for AI governance; specific focus on "healthcare workforce learning" as a synergy between Unbound and Liberate.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on debt repayment (zero debt now); continued margin expansion (34.3% maintained).

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 448.5 crore (+17.8% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 154 crore (+39% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 34.3%.
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 34.3%.
- **Cash Position:** Rs 163 crore (as of 30th September).
- **Debt:** Zero (repaid Unbound Medicine borrowing in August).
- **DSO:** 38 days (improved from 45 days in Q1).
- **Client Base:** 839 clients (vs 422 a year ago).
- **Top 5 Client Concentration:** 46% (down from 51%).

### Highlights
- **Operating Leverage:** EBITDA grew 53% over the last six quarters while revenue grew ~20%. EBITDA per employee rose ~15% in Q2.
- **Corporate Learning:** EBITDA grew 77% in H1 with 19% fewer people; margins rose to 25% (from ~16% in H1 FY26).
- **Education Segment:** 30.6% of group revenue (up from 27.2% YoY). H1 revenue grew 36%, EBITDA grew 14.5%.
- **Research Solutions:** Margins reached 48.2% (up >600 bps YoY). Excluding AJE, revenue grew ~22%.
- **AI Governance:** Certified to ISO 42001; >200 engineers building AI in-house.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 EBITDA:** Raised floor to at least Rs 310 crore.
- **Seasonality:** Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1, with Q4 being the strongest quarter.
- **Acquisitions:** Management expects to close the next acquisition within this financial year; pipeline is active.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Research:** Demand is strongest in research integrity and peer review due to the influx of AI-generated content.
- **Education:** Bookings led by Florida’s math adoption; California to follow.
- **Healthcare:** Unbound Medicine (subscription-based) is expanding into hospitals/health systems to address nurse churn.
- **US Macro:** Management views the Democratic majority in Congress as favorable for stability in research funding (NIH/NSF) and healthcare workforce programs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margin Strategy:** Management is not capitalizing product development expenses; all costs flow to P&L.
- **Pricing:** Customers share in productivity gains, but volume growth offsets price reductions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Acquisition Strategy:** Seeking assets with AI tailwinds, high-stakes content, low customer concentration, and recurring revenue.
- **Capital Allocation:** First goal is growth/acquisitions; distributions resume once capital is deployed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Cash:** Rs 163 crore (Zero debt).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Acquisition Timing:** Management acknowledged the delay in announcing a new acquisition post-Unbound but emphasized they are walking away from deals that are overpriced.
- **Organic Growth:** Acknowledged "flat sequential revenue growth" in Q2, attributing it to the timing of new mandate billings (starting Nov/Dec).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **On Acquisition Pipeline:** Management clarified that the Rs 1,500 crore FY28 target still includes acquisitions and they are disciplined on price, preferring to miss a deal than overpay.
- **On AJE Performance:** Management stated AJE has been a financial success with a 2-2.5 year payback period and serves as a strategic gateway for research integrity mandates.
- **On Margin Levers in Unbound:** Margins improved from 12-13% at acquisition to ~18% currently; management expects further expansion into the 20s via revenue growth.
- **On AI Impact:** Management reiterated that AI "hollows out" businesses selling hours, but benefits MPS because they sell outcomes and volume.
- **On Nurse Shortage:** Management explained the solution involves mobile apps and platforms that act as companions to new nurses to reduce churn (which costs ~$60,000 per nurse).

### One-line takeaway
MPS is successfully leveraging AI to drive operating leverage and margin expansion, while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet and awaiting the right acquisition to meet its FY28 growth targets.
