# Media & Entertainment - Digital media & gaming: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The digital media, gaming, and content publishing sectors reported robust growth in Q2 FY27, characterized by expanding operating leverage, strong international traction, and a strategic pivot toward tech-enabled and outcome-driven monetisation models. Growth was notably resilient across music publishing and streaming (Tips Music, Saregama), global cloud and SaaS solutions for media/research (Amagi, MPS), influencer marketing (Chatterbox), and gaming platform aggregations (Nazara). Across the board, managements highlighted healthy cash balances, strong EBITDA margin expansions, and strategic capital redeployment toward proprietary content, high-yielding IP, and AI-native operational toolsets.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Historically, Indian digital media, gaming, and publishing companies relied heavily on ad-supported monetisation, effort-based hourly billing, fragmented creator agency work, and third-party media placement fees. Over the last five years, players have shifted toward direct IP ownership, high-margin subscription models, outcome-based SaaS workflows, and proprietary content distribution platforms [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **AI Integration into Media Workflows and Operations**: *Scaling phase*. Amagi transitioned four AI products (NEWSPULSE, Amagi STUDIO, FANPULSE, Agentic NOC) from beta to production and achieved Cloud FinOps run-rate savings [Amagi Media Labs Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. MPS certified its AI governance under ISO 42001 with over 200 engineers building AI in-house to capture volume-driven, outcome-based billing models [MPS Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Nazara deployed Bluetile’s AI-powered marketing COEs to generate 290+ ad creatives in one month across studios [Nazara Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. This shift expands gross margins and lowers per-unit operating costs across content generation and distribution.
- **Paid Streaming Subscriptions over Ad-Supported Models**: *Scaling phase*. Saregama noted paid subscriptions in India are growing faster than ad-supported streams, with 64% of free music users willing to transition to reasonably priced paid tiers [Saregama India Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Tips Music reported digital revenue contributing 73% of total revenue, expecting paid streaming subscribers in India to scale to 150 million over 10 years to lift per-stream rates [Tips Music Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. This improves revenue visibility and yields multi-decade returns on catalog content.
- **Direct-to-Client Pivot & Experiential DOOH**: *Scaling phase*. Signpost India reduced direct billers by 35% while increasing average revenue per biller by ~170%, increasing digital media to 34% of revenue [Signpost India Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Direct client growth yields higher margins and reduces reliance on ad agencies.
- **Inorganic Scale via Global M&A**: *Scaling phase*. Nazara consolidated its $303 million Bluetile and BestPlay acquisitions to drive scale in global casual mobile gaming, generating INR 329 crore revenue in its first two months [Nazara Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. MPS is actively hunting AI/learning acquisitions with a zero-debt balance sheet [MPS Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- No specific regulatory or policy changes were cited as material headwinds or tailwinds across the earnings calls for this quarter.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
- **Consensus**: Management teams across music publishing, digital media, and influencer marketing are aligned on strong underlying consumer demand, particularly coming out of the festive season (H2 FY27). Saregama and Tips Music see significant multi-year tailwinds in streaming consumption and paid subscription adoption in India. MPS and Amagi observe strong international demand from enterprise broadcast, academic, and research platforms.
- **Divergence**: 
  - *Ad-Tech & Organic Gaming*: Nazara flagged a temporary demand dip in its AdTech arm (Datawrkz) due to an anchor client restructuring, as well as lower organic installs in Fusebox casual games [Nazara Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. 
  - *Out-of-Home Advertising*: Signpost India experienced temporary Q2 softness caused by the calendar shift of Navratri into Q3 and cautious global client spend [Signpost India Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Operating Leverage**: MPS expanded EBITDA margins to 34.3% by driving higher revenue per employee (~15% QoQ) [MPS Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Amagi's indirect operating expenses dropped from 58.6% to 49.3% of revenue, boosting Adjusted EBITDA margins to 18.7% [Amagi Media Labs Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **Content Costs & Expensing**: Tips Music expensed its full INR 26.18 crore content cost upfront in Q2, while generating a 70.5% EBITDA margin [Tips Music Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Saregama maintained content budget guidance of INR 300–350 crore for FY27, noting that catalog older than 3 years yields expanding margins as past acquisition costs fade [Saregama India Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **User Acquisition & Non-Cash Expenses**: Nazara’s Bluetile acquisition saw marketing/user acquisition costs at ~75% of revenue in August/September [Nazara Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Chatterbox faced higher non-cash depreciation and amortization (INR 2.20 crore vs INR 0.65 crore) and elevated IPO PR expenses, temporary headwinds to PAT margin [Chatterbox Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

## Capex & capacity
- **Infrastructure & Studio Investments**: Chatterbox operationalized its in-house studio, improving reel production speed and removing third-party rental costs [Chatterbox Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Signpost India incurred INR 48 crore H1 capex funded via internal accruals for digital asset activations across BMRCL (Bangalore Metro) and Kolkata streetscapes [Signpost India Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
- **Content & Catalog Capex**: Saregama expects cumulative content spend of ~INR 1,200 crore over FY28–FY30 [Saregama India Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. 
- **M&A Financing**: Nazara is raising ~$100 million in debt alongside internal accruals and equity issuance to fund its $303 million Bluetile acquisition [Nazara Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

## What changed since last quarter
- **Guidance Raised / Reaffirmed**:
  - *MPS*: Raised FY27 EBITDA floor guidance from "comfortably cross Rs 300 crore" to "at least Rs 310 crore" [MPS Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
  - *Tips Music*: Stated top-line revenue growth will comfortably exceed the earlier 20% full-year guidance [Tips Music Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
  - *Amagi, Saregama, Signpost*: Reaffirmed their revenue and margin guidance bands [Amagi, Saregama, Signpost concalls Q2 FY27].
- **Tone Shifts & Product Delivery**:
  - *Amagi*: Shifted tone from running AI pilots to full production scale across four new AI products [Amagi Media Labs Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
  - *Nazara*: Transitioned to platform integration via centralized COEs post-Bluetile consolidation [Nazara Technologies Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
  - *Saregama*: Pivoted live events strategy toward proprietary IP-based shows while stepping away from dilutive artist-led event deals [Saregama India Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

## What to watch next quarter
- **Festive Season Realization**: H2 festive tailwinds across Chatterbox, Signpost India, and Saregama.
- **Integration & Debt Raising**: Nazara’s debt closure of ~$100 million for Bluetile and restructuring recovery in Datawrkz.
- **Receivable Recoveries**: Chatterbox working toward normalizing international receivable cycles (DSO up to 122 days) and Signpost targeting 120-day DSO by March 2027.
- **M&A Execution**: MPS and Amagi closing active inorganic pipeline deals using strong cash balances.
