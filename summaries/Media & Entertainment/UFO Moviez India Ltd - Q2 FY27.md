# UFO Moviez India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy theatrical momentum, consistent profitability, and an exciting content lineup for the coming quarters.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,299 million (vs. Rs 1,113 million in Q2 FY26; Rs 1,118 million in Q1 FY27)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 213 million (vs. Rs 218 million in Q2 FY26; Rs 189 million in Q1 FY27)
- **Net Profit / PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 64 million (vs. Rs 75 million in Q2 FY26; Rs 57 million in Q1 FY27)
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 2,417 million (vs. Rs 2,203 million in H1 FY26)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 402 million (vs. Rs 411 million in H1 FY26)
- **Net Profit / PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 121 million (vs. Rs 141 million in H1 FY26)
- **Advertising Footprint:** 3,946 screens (2,606 multiplex screens, 1,340 single screens)
- **Total Movies Released (Q2 FY27):** 386 movies (vs. 462 in Q2 FY26; 399 in Q1 FY27)
- **Consolidated Cash (as of Sept 30):** Rs 1,657 million
- **Net Cash (as of Sept 30):** Rs 865 million
- **Tax Accumulated Losses:** Close to INR 119 crore (carried as deferred tax asset)

### Highlights
- Q2 featured notable sleeper hits like "Hanuman Ansh" and strong performances from "Spider-Man: Brand New Day", "Awarapan 2", and "Mirzapur: The Movie".
- Highly anticipated titles like "Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-ups" and "Batwara 1947" fell short of expectations, highlighting selective audience behavior.
- Promoter stake reduction in previous quarters was clarified as a reclassification of Apollo International to non-promoter status post-IPO depromoterization, not promoter selling.
- NCLT-approved capital reduction in past years removed Companies Act accumulated losses, enabling potential capital distribution.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic about H2 FY27 growth prospects, supported by improving audience sentiment and a strong content pipeline.
- Management plans to come back in Q3 or Q4 with a clear capital distribution policy for dividends or buybacks.
- Management refrained from giving specific quarterly revenue guidance numbers.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Theatrical audiences are increasingly selective, making content quality, audience connect, and word-of-mouth critical for success.
- Advertisers are showing willingness to look at bunches of films together rather than a film-by-film basis due to established IPs and major studio backing in upcoming quarters.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating direct costs increased by 28% YoY, primarily driven by a 66% increase in cost of goods sold (sale of equipment) from INR 19 crore to INR 32 crore, which was offset by a 66% increase in sale of product revenue (from INR 254 million to INR 422 million).
- Manpower costs included a higher absolute value of performance incentives (higher by approximately INR 1 crore YoY) due to structured allocation policies in place from the beginning of the year.
- Service gross margins: Advertisement revenue upwards of 55%; VPF plus CDC in the range of 60-65%; product sales gross margin historically in the 18-24% range.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated cash stood at Rs 1,657 million and net cash at Rs 865 million as of September 30.
- Accumulated tax losses stand at close to INR 119 crore (part carried forward perpetually, part to be realized in next 4 years). No Companies Act accumulated losses exist due to past NCLT restructuring.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Underperformance of highly anticipated releases (e.g., "Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-ups") demonstrates audience selectiveness.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ashish (Individual Investor) asked about accumulated losses and promoter stake reduction:** Management clarified that promoter stake dropped solely due to Apollo International's depromoterization, and tax losses stand at INR 119 crore while book losses were cleared via NCLT restructuring.
- **Ashish further asked about dividend/buyback plans given strong cash flows:** Management stated that board-level discussions have started and a clear capital distribution policy will likely be announced in Q3 or Q4.
- **Shailesh Naik (S CODE) questioned the sharp increase in direct operating costs and lower EBITDA margin conversion:** Management explained the rise is driven by cost of goods sold tied to product sales revenue, and manpower costs had a ~INR 1 crore YoY increase in incentive allocations.
- **Shailesh Naik asked for Q3 advertising revenue projections:** Siddharth Bhardwaj declined to provide specific revenue numbers but expressed strong confidence in H2 based on the robust content lineup.

### One-line takeaway
UFO Moviez reported steady Q2 FY27 revenues and consistent profitability backed by healthy theatrical momentum, while management signaled potential capital return policies for H2.
