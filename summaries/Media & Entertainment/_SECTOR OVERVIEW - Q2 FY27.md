# Media & Entertainment: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Media & Entertainment sector highlighted a stark divergence between digital-first/content assets and legacy traditional media. Digital gaming (Nazara Technologies) and music streaming (Tips Music) reported aggressive top-line and profit expansion, while traditional print and radio players (D. B. Corp, ENIL, Music Broadcast) faced headwinds from subdued advertising demand and a festive calendar shift (with Navaratra and Diwali moving out of Q2 into Q3). Meanwhile, theatrical ecosystem players (UFO Moviez, PVR Inox) reported steady theatrical momentum despite selective audience behavior.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus Agreement:** Managements across DB Corp, ENIL, Music Broadcast, and UFO Moviez agreed that Q2 demand was suppressed by a festive shift (Navaratra/Diwali falling later in the year) and a cautious advertising environment. However, nearly all traditional and theatrical players share an optimistic outlook for H2 FY27, expecting a strong rebound driven by the festive season and robust content pipelines.
* **Divergence:** While digital and music segments saw booming demand (Nazara's consolidated revenue surged 53% YoY, and Tips Music grew revenues by 35% YoY driven by a 30%-35% increase in industry streaming volumes), radio and traditional advertising businesses struggled. ENIL and Music Broadcast noted flat-to-declining core radio revenues and cited structural pressures from digital and AI transitions. In gaming, Nazara noted strong mobile gaming trends, whereas its Fusebox unit experienced lower organic installs due to global download declines.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Inflationary Pressures:** DB Corp faced a severe ~21% YoY increase in newsprint costs, taking average prices to INR 57,000 per tonne due to geopolitical uncertainties and higher freight. 
* **Cost Rationalization:** Legacy radio players relied heavily on cost controls to protect profitability. Music Broadcast reduced total expenses by ~20% YoY, driving an operating profit turnaround. ENIL expanded EBITDA by 9.5% YoY despite a 16.1% drop in domestic revenue, aided by structural cost measures and lower digital losses.
* **Content & Acquisition Costs:** Tips Music spent INR 71 crores in H1 on content costs (against full-year guidance of INR 90–100 crores) and maintains a policy of writing off entire content costs in the release quarter. Nazara reported user acquisition spending at ~75% of revenue for Bluetile and BestPlay, while UFO Moviez saw direct operating costs rise due to a 66% increase in equipment sales COGS (INR 32 crores).

## Capex & capacity
* **DB Corp:** No large capex is planned for the next 4 to 5 quarters as production machinery is fully in place.
* **Nazara Technologies:** Funding the $303 million Bluetile acquisition via $85.5 million paid to date, $77 million in share issuance, ~$100 million in planned debt raising, and internal accruals; also expanding Funky Monkeys stores (targeting 5 more in FY27).
* **Tips Music:** Completed an open market share buyback of INR 44.44 crores and declared an interim dividend of INR 6.5 per share, maintaining strict focus on core music without entering live events.
* **Music Broadcast & ENIL:** Exploring experiential concerts and regional events in Tier 2/3 markets (Music Broadcast) and deploying cyclical tech capex in digital (ENIL).

## What changed since last quarter
* **Nazara Technologies:** Raymond Stauffer took over as CEO, Mithun Sacheti joined as Executive Director, and Arun Agarwal joined as CFO. Bluetile and BestPlay were successfully consolidated effective August 1, 2026.
* **Music Broadcast:** Revenue moderated sequentially in Q2 to INR 41 crores (down from INR 44.5 crores in Q1 FY27) due to a softer operating environment.

## Company one-liners
* **D. B. Corp:** DB Corp's Q2 FY27 financial comparison was depressed by a festive season shift and a ~21% YoY spike in newsprint costs, but underlying business drivers and advertising momentum remain stable.
* **Entertainment Network (India):** ENIL reported a mixed Q2 FY27 with a 16.1% decline in domestic revenue due to subdued radio demand and a festive shift, but expanded EBITDA by 9.5% YoY via structural cost controls and reduced digital losses.
* **Music Broadcast:** Music Broadcast Limited delivered a significant YoY turnaround in profitability and positive PBT in Q2 FY27, underpinned by strict cost discipline and structural efficiencies despite sequential revenue moderation.
* **Nazara Technologies:** Nazara reported record Q2 FY27 revenue of INR 803 crore (up 53% YoY) and EBITDA of INR 120 crore (up 93% YoY), driven by the successful consolidation of Bluetile and BestPlay and a renewed focus on AI-driven central Centers of Excellence (COEs) for gaming.
* **PVR Inox:** Management highlighted a record-breaking quarter across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by strong theatrical footfalls, box office momentum across genres/languages, and structural margin improvements.
* **Tips Music:** Tips Music delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 35% YoY revenue growth and a 70.5% EBITDA margin, backed by front-loaded content investments and robust digital-catalog engagement.
* **UFO Moviez:** UFO Moviez reported steady Q2 FY27 revenues and consistent profitability backed by healthy theatrical momentum, while management signaled potential capital return policies for H2.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Festive Season Rebound:** Execution and advertising revenue recovery in H2 FY27 across print (DB Corp) and radio (ENIL, Music Broadcast) following the late shift of Navaratra and Diwali.
* **Digital Breakeven & Monetization:** Progress on Gaana's profitability and subscriber acquisition costs (ENIL), alongside YouTube Shorts monetization negotiations (Tips Music).
* **Capital Allocation & Debt Execution:** Finalization of capital return policies/dividends at UFO Moviez, DB Corp, and Tips Music, alongside the execution of the ~$100 million debt raise for Nazara's Bluetile acquisition.
* **Newsprint Cost Trends:** Whether projected 2% to 3% softening in newsprint prices materializes for DB Corp in H3/H4.
