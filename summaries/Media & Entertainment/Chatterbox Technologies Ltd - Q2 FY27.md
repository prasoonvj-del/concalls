# Chatterbox Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy growth in revenue and EBITDA supported by brand expansion and international markets, though PAT growth moderated due to higher depreciation and amortization.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (no explicit quantitative numbers provided, but management reaffirmed growth and long-term targets).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, with increased emphasis on international contributions (reaching 32.6% of revenue) and tech-driven platform monetization.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Higher other expenses due to IPO-related PR and professional fees, and stretched DSO/working capital due to international receivables.
- Last quarter's promises: Studio capabilities went live and contributed to operations; international expansion via Dubai continued to build traction despite regional challenges.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 53.28 crore, up 48.07% YoY (vs Rs 35.98 crore in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs 9.77 crore, up 47.34% YoY (vs Rs 6.63 crore in H1 FY26)
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): Rs 7.56 crore, up 26.48% YoY (vs Rs 5.98 crore in H1 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 5.96 crore, up 26.77% YoY (vs Rs 4.70 crore in H1 FY26)
- PAT margin: 11.18%
- Depreciation and amortization: Rs 2.20 crore (vs Rs 0.65 crore in H1 FY26)
- Payment to creators and related costs: Rs 30.68 crore (vs Rs 23.09 crore in H1 FY26)
- Employee benefit expenses: Rs 7.16 crore (vs Rs 5.19 crore in H1 FY26)
- Revenue mix by vertical: Chtr Represent (40.1%), Brand Solutions (44.2%), Chtr Social (15.7%)
- Industry vertical mix: Beauty & Lifestyle (48.9%), Entertainment (18%), Hospitality (16.9%), Technology (10.7%)
- Geographic revenue mix: Domestic (67.4%), International (32.6%)
- Creator network: ~436 creators (vs 324 in H1 FY26); Exclusive creators: 110
- Campaigns executed: Close to 1,500 (vs ~1,200 in H1 FY26)
- Brands served: ~530 (vs ~458 in H1 FY26)
- Client retention rate: ~71%

### Highlights
- Chtr Represent, Brand Solutions, and Chtr Social provided diversified revenue streams.
- New in-house studio went live, improving reel production speed, eliminating third-party rental dependency, and integrating higher-margin production capabilities.
- International expansion continued, with North America and Dubai driving overseas traction alongside natural integration with Korean beauty brands.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a significant growth boost from the upcoming festive season (second half of the year).
- Management expects other expenses to drop by 30% to 40% starting next financial year as IPO-related promotional spending normalizes.
- Management aims to bring PAT margins back toward previous levels (13% to 15%) through revenue mix improvements, tech-led efficiencies, and cost optimization.
- Guidance status: <none>

### Demand & industry commentary
- Brands are increasingly shifting from fragmented, one-off Excel-sheet campaigns to organized, data-driven, and integrated multi-service partners.
- Growing demand for measurable campaign outcomes (e.g., Cost of Acquisition / CAC, real-time analytics).
- Average client relationship tenure for top 10 clients spans 4 to 5 years, moving toward annual contract cycles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- PAT growth moderated relative to revenue/EBITDA due to higher non-cash depreciation and amortization (Rs 2.20 crore vs Rs 0.65 crore) from investments in AI, digital infrastructure, and studio capabilities.
- Other expenses increased due to IPO-related PR activities and post-listing professional fees, expected to normalize next year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested in digital infrastructure, AI capabilities, and in-house studio setup.
- Dubai subsidiary operationalizing further to capture MENA region influencer marketing spends, though initial scaling faced headwinds from regional conflicts.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stood at Rs 22 crore.
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) / debtor days stretched (reaching up to 122 days), driven partly by higher export/North American revenues which carry longer credit cycles. Management expects realization over the next six months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Stretched working capital cycles and increasing trade receivable days due to international client terms.
- Geopolitical friction impacting initial traction of the Dubai/MENA expansion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shravan Modi (Syndicate Family Office) on client duration and performance mandates:** Management stated client retention is ~71% with top 10 relationships lasting 4–5 years, and confirmed a pivot toward data-driven, measurable performance mandates.
2. **Kaustubh Saha (Investor) on debtor days and studio impact:** Management noted international contracts stretch DSO to 122 days but expect collections soon; in-house studios boost production margins and speed.
3. **Nitin (Individual Investor) on other expenses and PAT margins:** Management explained other expenses rose due to IPO PR spends and rent, guiding a 30%-40% drop in other expenses next year and a return to 13%-15% PAT margins.
4. **Hiten Shah (Investor) on cash flow, M&A, and influencer payouts:** Management stated they hold Rs 22 crore in cash to fund low-hanging M&As, and clarified that exclusive talent (110 creators) operate on fixed percentage commission contracts without annual fixed retainers.
5. **Varun Trivedi (Investor) on international contribution and Dubai scale:** Management highlighted North America, Dubai, and Korean beauty brands as key international growth drivers, noting MENA initial traction was impacted by regional wars but holds large TAM potential.

### One-line takeaway
Chatterbox delivered strong 48% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27 driven by international markets and core verticals, while absorbing higher depreciation and opex from strategic tech and studio investments.
