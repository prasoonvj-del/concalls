# Amagi Media Labs Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 32%, expanding margins, and high customer momentum supported by operating leverage.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 437 crore (up 32% YoY, 21% on constant currency, 10% QoQ).
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 50 crore (up 201% YoY); Margin: 11.5% (expansion of 650 bps YoY from 5%, and up 120 bps QoQ).
- **PAT:** Rs 34 crore; Margin: 7.5% (up 630 bps YoY from 1.1%). Excluding a one-time non-cash FX charge, PAT was Rs 40 crore.
- **Cash and investments:** Rs 1,616 crore (including IPO proceeds).
- **Trailing 12-month NRR:** 125%.
- **Content processed:** 959,000 hours (up 43% YoY).
- **Delivery endpoints:** 9,900+.
- **Distributor count:** 451 (up 21% YoY).
- **Monetized ad impressions:** 13.6 billion (up 59% YoY, 21% QoQ).

### Highlights
- Highest ever quarterly revenue in company history.
- Successfully managed mission-critical infrastructure for all 104 matches of the FIFA World Cup for a major U.S. broadcaster with 100% on-air availability.
- AI traction: 10 active pilots ongoing; a major U.S. news network selected Amagi's Newspulse to transform workflows into an AI-first newsroom.
- Broad-based global wins across the U.S., Australia, the Middle East, India, and APAC.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to introduce a broader product suite of AI offerings at flagship industry conferences in Q2.
- Gross margins are expected to be maintained in the 67% to 69% zipcode for the year.
- Historical phasing note: H1 has historically been back-half weighted for EBITDA.

### Demand & industry commentary
- TAM is estimated at ~$17 billion; AI could nearly double this opportunity over time.
- Only ~10% of playout has moved to the cloud, leaving a large modernization runway.
- Streaming is close to half of U.S. television viewing, with advertising becoming central.
- Customer Advisory Board feedback indicates strong demand for vendor consolidation (e.g., customers utilizing up to 800 tools want simplified workflows).
- Platforms are looking to outsource rather than in-source ad management due to increasing complexity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin was 67.3% (down 2.2 percentage points YoY, up 0.5 points QoQ).
- Operating leverage is playing out as revenue grew 32% while the adjusted EBITDA cost base grew ~24%.
- Sales & marketing plus customer success reduced from 27% to 23% of revenue; R&D reduced from 27% to 21% of revenue.
- Generated $282,000 of monthly run-rate savings in gross margin cloud cost optimization (FinOps), which was reinvested into AI initiatives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Organic investment remains the first priority. Inorganic focus is capability-led (evaluated 33 opportunities since January, with 10 under active evaluation).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash outflow narrowed from Rs 141 crore to Rs 65 crore; free cash outflow narrowed from Rs 143 crore to Rs 68 crore.
- DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) improved sequentially by about 8 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Q1 is seasonally softer due to annual salary increments and merit increases kicking in, creating margin and cash outflow pressure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bharat Gulati (Dalal & Broacha):** Asked about gross margin sustainability and AI impact. Management stated gross margins will stay in the 67%–69% zipcode and AI is currently on an S-curve investment phase with minimal immediate deflationary impact.
2. **Bharat Gulati (Dalal & Broacha):** Inquired about in-sourcing vs. outsourcing of ads. Management clarified that platforms are looking to outsource due to complexity and in-sourcing is not seen as an industry risk.
3. **Omprakash Kavadi (Spark Institutional Equities):** Asked about AI pricing models. Management responded that they introduced an "AI credits" model mimicking token/consumption costs, aiming for outcome-driven pricing over time.
4. **Rishi Jhunjhunwala (IIFL Capital):** Asked about constant currency NRR and base effects. Management clarified NRR is reported in currency based on TTM, and Q2 carries a ~600 bps base effect headwind from prior year rev rec timing.

### One-line takeaway
Amagi delivered record Q1 FY27 revenue of Rs 437 crore up 32% YoY, backed by robust operating leverage, expanding margins, and early traction in AI-led product adoption.
