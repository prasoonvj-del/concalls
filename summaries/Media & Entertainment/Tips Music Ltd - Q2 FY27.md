# Tips Music Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by both new releases and catalog content.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 120 crores (up 35% YoY from INR 89.2 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR 227 crores (up 28% YoY)
- **Operating EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 84.64 crores (up 25% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 70.5%
- **Profit After Tax / PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 65.5 crores (up 23% YoY)
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 54%
- **Profit After Tax (H1 FY27):** INR 109 crores (up 11% YoY)
- **Content Cost (Q2 FY27):** INR 26.18 crores
- **Content Cost (H1 FY27):** INR 71 crores
- **Digital Revenue Contribution (H1 FY27):** 73% (Q2 FY27 at 71%, Q1 FY27 at 75%)
- **Paid Subscription Share:** 10% to 15% of the digital pie
- **Music Library / Reach:** 164.5 million YouTube subscribers; over 54.3 billion total views across the content library
- **Buyback:** Completed open market buyback of shares amounting to INR 44.44 crores
- **Interim Dividend:** INR 6.5 per share declared by the Board

### Highlights
- Released 73 songs in Q2 FY27 (40 film songs, 33 non-film songs).
- Key new release performance: Punjabi film album *Ishqnama* crossed 100 million views on YouTube; recreated song *Aankhon Se Tune 2.0* crossed 66 million views; *Chunnari Chunnari - Let’s Go!* crossed 100 million views.
- Catalog track *Tera Naam Doon* crossed 1 billion views on Instagram.
- 85% of revenue comes from content acquired up to 3 years back, and 15% from content acquired within the last 3 years.
- Warner partnership continues as expected; Warner contribution is in the 25%-30% range.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year FY27 content cost guidance in the range of INR 90 crores to INR 100 crores (with H2 expected to be lighter).
- Management expects to exceed their earlier 20% top-line growth guidance for the full year.
- Plans to release at least 5-6 films a year (4-5 in Hindi, 2-3 in regional languages).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth in the quarter was primarily volume-driven across both digital and non-digital segments.
- Industry streaming volume for Tips increased by 30%-35% YoY.
- Management expects paid subscriptions to grow, targeting 150 million subscribers in India over 10 years, which will drive higher per-stream rates.
- Actively pushing OTT companies to expand into Tier 2, Tier 3, and Tier 4 rural markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Content costs are front-loaded in H1 (INR 71 crores spent out of the full-year guidance of INR 90 crores to INR 100 crores).
- Tips writes off its entire content cost in the same quarter/year it is released.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Completed buyback of INR 44.44 crores.
- Focus remains strictly on the core music business via short-term artist contracts (2-3 songs); no plans to enter artist management or live events.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt details discussed, other than normal working capital/advance classifications for Warner, SMP, and others.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as systemic risks, aside from intense competition driving up content acquisition costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kavish Parekh (Capital) on YouTube Shorts monetization and growth sustainability:** Management stated that the YouTube Shorts deal is still under negotiation and not yet concluded. Growth was driven by both new releases and catalog across digital and non-digital.
2. **Akshay Kolekar on full-year guidance vs. H1 outperformance:** Management acknowledged H1 was stronger (28% growth) and noted they prefer to remain conservative, maintaining that they will exceed the 20% guidance.
3. **Sanidhya Agarwal on the strategy of writing off content costs immediately vs. industry norms:** Management explained that writing off content costs entirely in the same period ensures a clean balance sheet with no long-term financial hangover or massive future write-offs.
4. **Ravi Naredi on revenue vintage bifurcation:** Management stated that ~85% of revenue comes from content acquired up to 3 years ago, and ~15% from content acquired within the last 3 years.
5. **Sagar Jethwani on per-stream rates and YouTube Shorts delay:** Management reiterated that per-stream base rates (e.g., INR 0.05) remain stable, and revenue growth per stream will be driven by the adoption of paid subscriptions. Negotiations for YouTube Shorts are ongoing and expected to conclude during the quarter.
6. **Harshh K Shah on Warner contribution:** Management noted that Warner's contribution to revenue is in the 25%-30% range.

### One-line takeaway
Tips Music delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 35% YoY revenue growth and a 70.5% EBITDA margin, backed by front-loaded content investments and robust digital-catalog engagement.
