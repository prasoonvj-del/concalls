# MPS Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-29 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strongest first quarter in company history with significant margin expansion and successful integration of new acquisitions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 224.24 crore (+20.4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 76.96 crore (+53.0% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 34.3% (vs 27.0% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 50.39 crore (+43.0% YoY).
*   **Basic EPS:** Rs 29.70 (vs Rs 20.78 YoY).
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 138.02 crore.
*   **Borrowings:** Rs 37.63 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Operating Leverage:** Revenue grew 20%, EBITDA grew 53%, while headcount increased less than 3%.
*   **Business Shift:** Management highlighted a strategic move toward "outcomes-based" revenue (paid for results/subscriptions) rather than effort-based (hours/pages) billing.
*   **Unbound Medicine:** First full quarter in the base; management described it as a recurring, high-renewal subscription business that lowers customer concentration.
*   **Corporate Learning:** Second consecutive quarter of growth; EBITDA grew 60.7% YoY with margins expanding to 25.3% (from <17% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Guidance:** Management reaffirmed expectation to "comfortably cross" Rs 300 crore in EBITDA, viewing this as a floor rather than a ceiling.
*   **FY28 Targets:** Management targets ~Rs 1,500 crore in revenue and ~Rs 450 crore in EBITDA.
*   **Rule of 50:** Management aims to clear the "Rule of 50" (Revenue growth + EBITDA margin > 50) for the full year, noting Q1 results were on the right side of this metric.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Research Solutions (55% of revenue):** Revenue grew 13.2% YoY; core growth (excluding AJE) was 26.3%.
*   **Education:** Revenue grew 42.2% YoY to Rs 73.41 crore.
*   **AJE (Author Solutions):** Management described this as "resetting by design," resulting in smaller revenue but higher profitability and quality.
*   **AI Strategy:** Management stated AI is a tailwind in high-stakes domains (medical/nursing) where "hallucinations" are unacceptable, creating a defensive moat.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Expansion:** EBITDA margin expanded to 34.3% from 27.0% YoY.
*   **Headcount Management:** AJE roles were offshored to India; Corporate Learning headcount was reduced by ~33% YoY through structural redundancy and delayering.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Acquisition Strategy:** Management seeks assets with >$15 million revenue, high-stakes content, and motivated sellers.
*   **Financing:** Management prefers using internal cash accruals and debt over equity for acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **DSO:** Improved to 45 days (from 51 days at the end of March).
*   **Liquidity:** Net cash position of Rs 100.39 crore (Cash Rs 138.02 crore minus Debt Rs 37.63 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Phasing:** Management cautioned that they will not promise to clear the "Rule of 50" every quarter due to business phasing.
*   **Talent:** Management noted that as the "talent machine" expands, there is a risk of making mistakes in external acquisitions.
*   **Deal Discipline:** Management walked away from an education asset during due diligence due to concerns over "shortcuts" taken by the target's management.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Management/Culture:** Management stated they use "EEE" values (Excellence, Efficiency, Empathy) and an ownership mindset to integrate global acquisitions.
*   **On Headcount/Productivity:** Management clarified that Corporate Learning headcount reductions were structural/redundancy-based and did not impact the client-facing bench.
*   **On Organic Growth:** Management acknowledged the challenge to push organic growth to "late teens" and stated they are not settling for current levels.
*   **On Unbound Medicine:** Management confirmed a monthly run rate of ~$800,000 with margins of 18-20%, aiming to reach "Rule of 40" metrics (mid-20s EBITDA margin).
*   **On AI Moat:** Management explained that their moat lies in the "last mile" of workflows where AI cannot afford to be wrong, such as medical/nursing content verification.

### One-line takeaway
MPS delivered a record Q1 with significant margin expansion driven by operating leverage and a strategic shift toward outcomes-based, AI-enabled revenue models.
