# Imagicaaworld Entertainment Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and footfall growth despite a heat wave affecting operations.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 178 crore, up 20% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 90 crore, up 24% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 50.7%, up 170 bps YoY.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 58 crore, up 30% YoY.
- **PAT Margin:** 32.4%.
- **Consolidated Park Footfalls:** >11.5 lakh visitors, up 22% YoY.
- **Consolidated Park Revenue:** Rs 161 crore, up 22% YoY.
- **Consolidated ARPU:** ~Rs 1,395 (largely stable).
- **Mumbai/Pune Catchment Revenue Growth:** 18% YoY (Footfalls up 19%).
- **Rest of Maharashtra Catchment Revenue Growth:** 33% YoY (Footfalls up 14%, ARPU up 17%).
- **Gujarat Catchment:** Footfalls up 32%, revenue up 15% YoY.
- **Central India Catchment:** Footfalls up 48%, revenue up 44% YoY.
- **Novotel Imagicaa Occupancy:** 62%.
- **Novotel Imagicaa ARR:** Rs 9,657.
- **Shanku's Water Park Acquisition:** INR 50 crore investment for a 50.002% stake in Mehsana Next Parks Private Limited (MNPPL).
- **Hello Park Unit Economics:** Capex of Rs 8-12 crore per 8,000-12,000 sq. ft. center; EBITDA margin around 24-25% (after rentals); 5-7% royalty on revenues; target ticket price Rs 800-900.

### Highlights
- Q1 FY27 revenue grew 20% YoY to Rs 178 crore driven by healthy footfalls.
- Completed the acquisition of a 50% stake in Shanku’s Water Park (Mehsana, Gujarat), making MNPPL a subsidiary; consolidation effective Q2.
- Entered indoor entertainment via an exclusive India franchise partnership with Dubai-based Hello Park; signed two locations (Hyderabad and Surat), targeting 2-3 centers per year.
- Restructured reporting into four outdoor catchments: Mumbai/Pune, Rest of Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Central India.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aspires to operate a portfolio of 12 outdoor parks by 2030 (adding ~1 park/year).
- Aiming to add 2-3 Hello Park indoor centers annually.
- Debt to EBITDA target range is 2.5x to 3x (may peak at 3-3.5x for limited periods).
- Maintenance capex is expensed via P&L (6-8% of revenues); new attraction/capex budget is kept around ~5% of top line.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Organized amusement and water park industry in India remains nascent and fragmented.
- Q1 faced an unprecedented heat wave leading to ~2 weeks of non-operational days at Khopoli Park, along with a shift in CBSE school holiday calendars.
- Consciously opted for a softer pricing strategy in ticketing to drive higher footfalls and non-ticketing realization multiplier effects.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management fees for O&M of Shanku’s Water Park range from 6% to 10%.
- Hello Park royalty payable is 5% to 7% of center revenues.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sweet spot for outdoor expansion: Mid-sized parks of 30-50 acres (capital outlay: Rs 150 crore for B-tier cities to Rs 200-450+ crore for metros).
- Active evaluation of locations like Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Goa via greenfield developments or strategic acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Expansion funded via a mix of internal accruals and moderate debt.
- Promoter group confirmed intent to convert warrants before the due date at the specified price of Rs 73.5.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unprecedented heat waves cause non-operational days and impact footfalls/hotel bookings.
- Price sensitivity observed in certain markets like Surat.
- Spiritual/devotional park expansion requires active government support/infrastructure intervention to remain viable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jinesh Joshi (PL Capital) on Like-to-Like Revenue vs FY25:** Management explained that Q1 FY27 was impacted by an unprecedented heat wave (causing Khopoli park shutdowns for ~2 weeks) and a shift in school calendars, alongside a conscious softer pricing strategy. *(Management answered directly)*
2. **Jinesh Joshi on Hello Park Margins & Capex:** Management clarified the Rs 8-10 crore capex for 10k sq. ft., 24-25% EBITDA margins post-rentals, 5% royalty, and 3-4 year payback. *(Management answered directly)*
3. **Navin (iThought PMS) on Park Capex & Ride Sourcing:** Management stated they do not intend to replicate the massive Khopoli Imagicaa scale everywhere; new parks will range from Rs 150 crore to Rs 450 crore with a mix of local and imported rides. *(Management answered directly)*
4. **Vipulkumar Shah (Investor) on Gujarat ARPU Drop:** Management explained they tested price elasticity and market penetration in Surat, accepting lower ARPU to drive a 32% footfall surge, while aiming to improve non-ticketing revenues. *(Management answered directly)*
5. **Pratik (Investor) on Seasonality & Cyclicality:** Management detailed mitigation strategies including indoor entertainment (Hello Park), off-season indoor shows, events/concerts, Magic Pass, and corporate/school pushes. *(Management answered directly)*

### One-line takeaway
Imagicaaworld delivered a solid Q1 FY27 with 20% revenue growth to Rs 178 crore, expanding its footprint through the Shanku's Water Park acquisition and a new asset-light indoor entertainment partnership (Hello Park).
