# Saregama India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and operating profit growth, driven by music vertical performance and new age IP contribution.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 263.6 crores (Up 27% YoY)
- Adjusted EBITDA: Rs. 112.4 crores (Up 69% YoY)
- Operational PBT: Rs. 70.5 crores (Up 38% YoY)
- Music vertical revenue: Rs. 230.6 crores (Up 39% YoY)
- Music vertical EBITDA: Rs. 139.8 crores (Up 36% YoY)
- Music vertical net margin: Rs. 99.6 crores (Up 31% YoY)
- Video vertical revenue: Rs. 17 crores (Down 52% YoY)
- Spend on new music content (FY27 expected): Rs. 300 crores to Rs. 350 crores
- Total catalog size: ~180,000 songs, growing at 5,000 to 6,000 new releases per year

### Highlights
- Appointed Abhishek Kapoor as the new CFO (over 25 years of finance experience).
- 60% of music revenue in FY26 came from music released post-2000 (45% post-2020).
- Released 750+ originals and premium recreations across 8 languages in Q1.
- Artiste Management represents 309 artists with >440 million combined followers/subscribers on Instagram and YouTube.
- Live Events expanded: 23 Carvaan Live shows, 22 devotional shows, and 48 standard packs produced in Q1; Ilaiyaraaja U.S. tour underway.
- Pocket Aces reached breakeven in FY26 and is moving towards profitability in FY27.
- Two AI-dedicated teams created: one for content (generating neighboring audio/video content) and one for process optimization.

### Guidance & outlook
- Music vertical revenue growth guidance maintained at 20% to 23% YoY.
- Annual music EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 60% to 65%.
- Management expects to maintain a 5-year payback period followed by 60 to 80 years of returns.
- Music investment growth rate expected to temper from FY28 onwards after the current step-function increase.
- Video segment films business to be completely wound down over the next 3 to 4 quarters as investments shift to Bhansali Productions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global recorded music in 2025 was US$ 31.7 billion; India accounts for only 1% despite 18% of the world population, representing a massive underpenetrated opportunity.
- Paid streaming penetration in India is at 3% (compared to 67% in Sweden, 57% in U.S., 18% in Brazil and China).
- An EY and IMI study states 64% of free music customers in India are ready to shift to a reasonably priced paid service if free content stops.
- Management expects India to touch 100 million paid subscriptions at ~Rs. 100/month if free supply is curtailed.
- Public performance licensing at commercial venues is noted as a large, relatively untapped revenue opportunity in India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Content bought 2 to 3 years ago (e.g., *Stree 2*, *Amaran*, *Rocky Aur Rani Kii Prem Kahaani*) is now contributing positive margins.
- Artiste management operates at lower margins (~10%) due to zero content investment costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New music content spend guided at Rs. 300 crores to Rs. 350 crores for the year, with most already committed.
- International expansion focused on the U.S. market (Ilaiyaraaja and Arjan Dhillon tours).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash reserves were diverted towards specific investments such as Bhansali Productions, leading to a reduction in other income.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management stated they will continue their fight against unlicensed exploitation of music, while supporting licensed AI innovation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on YouTube views surge & brand tie-ups:** Management explained that quarterly fluctuations happen based on album performance and advised looking at a 12-month rolling basis; brand monetization is driven by a dedicated cross-vertical team leveraging Gen Z appeal.
- **Kavish Parekh (360 ONE Capital) on AI initiatives monetization & scale:** Management stated they are currently experimenting with GenAI for low-cost music videos and podcasts, noting investments are minimal and will be absorbed within the Rs. 300–350 crore content budget.
- **Disha (Trinetra Asset Managers) on Pocket Aces integration:** Management reiterated that Pocket Aces provides a unique Gen Z marketing footprint and consumer engagement moat, achieving breakeven last year with profitability expected in FY27.
- **Lokesh on pre-2020 catalog revenue growth:** Management clarified that catalog revenue organically grows at high single digits or low double digits annually, though its percentage share appears smaller due to explosive growth in new content.
- **Yash Bajaj on Music EBITDA dip despite high revenue growth:** Management attributed the slight variance to segment mix changes, specifically the faster scaling of Artiste Management which carries lower margins and zero upfront investment.

### One-lake takeaway
Saregama delivered robust Q1 FY27 results (revenue up 27% YoY, adjusted EBITDA up 69% YoY) and remains confident in its 20–23% medium-term music growth guidance, anchored by a unique content-to-live-events flywheel, underpenetrated Indian subscription potential, and disciplined AI experimentation.
