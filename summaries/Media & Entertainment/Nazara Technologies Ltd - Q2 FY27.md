# Nazara Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record revenue, strong EBITDA growth, and successful consolidation of Bluetile and BestPlay driving global platform evolution.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: INR 803 crore, up 53% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 1,232 crore, up 20% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 120 crore, up 93% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 166 crore, up 52% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 14.9%, expanded 311 bps YoY
- Profit After Tax (PAT) from continuing operations: INR 35 crore (vs loss of INR 34 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Gaming Segment Revenue: INR 656 crore, up 122% YoY; EBITDA: INR 141 crore, up 114% YoY; EBITDA Margin: 21.5%
- Mobile Gaming Revenue (including Bluetile and BestPlay): INR 550 crore, up 167% YoY; EBITDA: INR 103 crore, up 151% YoY
- Bluetile and BestPlay Revenue (first 2 months): INR 329 crore, up 49%; EBITDA: INR 64 crore, up 64%
- Organic Gaming Growth: Revenue up 11% YoY, EBITDA up 17% YoY
- Kiddopia: Subscriber base up to 5%; ARPU at $7.94, up 8% YoY; Revenue up 21% YoY
- WildWorks: Revenue up 41% YoY, EBITDA up 39% YoY
- Fusebox: Revenue down 4% YoY; EBITDA up 7% YoY
- Curve (PC & Consoles): Revenue INR 77 crore, up 27% YoY; EBITDA INR 30 crore, up 61% YoY; EBITDA margin 38.9% (up from 30.8%)
- Offline Gaming: Revenue flat at INR 29 crore; EBITDA INR 8 crore, up 29% YoY; EBITDA margin 26.2% (up from 20.3%)
- AdTech (Datawrkz & Space & Time): Revenue INR 124 crore; EBITDA INR 0.8 crore
- Absolute Sports: Revenue INR 23 crore; Adjusted EBITDA INR 1.7 crore
- Pro Football Network: Revenue up 20% YoY; EBITDA margin 17% (vs negative 18% in Q2 FY26)
- Bluetile Acquisition Cost: $303 million total ($85.5 million paid to date, $77 million via share issuance in October, ~$100 million planned via debt, balance via internal accruals)

### Highlights
- Consolidated Bluetile and BestPlay effective 1 August 2026.
- Raymond Stauffer took over as CEO; Mithun Sacheti joined as Executive Director; Arun Agarwal joined as CFO.
- Bluetile’s core capabilities established as group Centers of Excellence (COEs) across marketing, data, products, and technology with embedded AI. Marketing COE produced over 290 ad creatives for 3 studios in one month.
- Curve launched Sovereign Tower on Steam; Wobbly Life mobile reached #3 on App Store and Play Store top charts; Human: Fall Flat surpassed 60 million players.
- Fusebox launched *The Traitors* (cohort metrics showing ~3X initial performance of *Love Island*) and a new *Big Brother* season; *Love Island* was the #1 free RPG on U.S. App Store in July.
- Funky Monkeys opened 10 new stores in the last 12 months with 5 more planned for FY27; SMAAASH 2.0 design and rebranding complete, launch slightly delayed pending regulatory approvals.
- NODWIN share of loss in Nazara books was INR 23 crore for the quarter, driven operationally by an impairment on Turkish company Publishme and shifting of IPs from Q2 to Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated an ongoing focus on qualitative growth (both organic and inorganic) rather than providing specific multi-year numeric guidance.
- Expects temporary dip in Datawrkz revenue in H2 as an anchor client restructures and pauses ad spend.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer trends and market opportunity point toward the intersection of short-form casual gaming and the U.S. market.
- Fusebox experienced lower organic installs due to a global decline in downloads of similar games.
- WildWorks' Roblox traction did not meet internal bars and the game was shelved for now.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Bluetile and BestPlay user acquisition spend was ~75% of revenue in August/September (down from ~85% in Q1 FY27 due to August seasonality).
- Fusebox improved unit economics and ARPU via proprietary web shops (reducing app store fees) and optimized content/employee costs.
- Sportskeeda operating costs were down 13% QoQ.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Funky Monkeys added 10 new stores in 12 months, targeting 5 more in FY27.
- Bluetile acquisition funding involves $77 million via share issuance in October, ~$100 million planned debt raising, and internal accruals for the balance.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management is evaluating opportunistic monetization/deconsolidation of non-core assets (AdTech, Absolute Sports, NODWIN stake) to strengthen the balance sheet, though there is no distressed sale or hard timeline.
- Planning to raise approximately $100 million in debt from multiple bankers for Bluetile acquisition tranches.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Fusebox revenue declined 4% YoY due to lower organic installs.
- Datawrkz expected to see a temporary H2 dip due to an anchor client restructuring.
- NODWIN reported an EBITDA loss and took an impairment on its Turkish investment (Publishme).
- WildWorks' *Go Slinky Go!* soft-launch retention metrics are not yet strong enough and require further iterations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Jawar (AK Investments) asked about the 1-year focus under the new CEO and 5-year targets.** Raymond Stauffer explained the focus is on the Nazara platform, scaling Bluetile COEs, and integrating AI across all studios. Management declined to give explicit 5-year numeric guidance, emphasizing qualitative growth.
2. **Kunal Bajaj (Choice Institutional Equities) asked about the COE strategy and why NODWIN numbers are no longer broken out.** Raymond explained the COE leadership change and integration of AI-ready ad production and data stacks. Anupriya Sinha Das clarified that NODWIN is treated as an associate and highlighted its upcoming pre-IPO/IPO gearing.
3. **Priyam Shah (Trinetra Asset Managers) asked about a 3-4 year organic vs inorganic revenue framework.** Raymond reiterated the focus on qualitative growth and compounding organic and inorganic levers without committing to specific multi-year guidance figures.
4. **Samarth Patel (Equirus) asked about Bluetile/BestPlay margin fluctuations and WildWorks' Roblox/new game status.** Raymond noted August seasonality impacts marketing spend, which is managed dynamically. Anupriya added that the Roblox title was shelved due to insufficient traction, while *Go Slinky Go!* is undergoing metric iterations.
5. **Naitik asked about the payment structure and funding for the Bluetile acquisition.** Arun Agarwal detailed the $303 million total cost, $85.5 million paid, $77 million via October share issuance, ~$100 million planned debt, and the rest via internal accruals.

### One-line takeaway
Nazara reported record Q2 FY27 revenue of INR 803 crore (up 53% YoY) and EBITDA of INR 120 crore (up 93% YoY), driven by the successful consolidation of Bluetile and BestPlay and a renewed focus on AI-driven central Centers of Excellence (COEs) for gaming.
