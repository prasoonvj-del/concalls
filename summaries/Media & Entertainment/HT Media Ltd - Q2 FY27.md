# HT Media Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue and profitability grew year-on-year despite the festive season shifting out by 20 days.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None provided (consistent with previous quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to acknowledge margin pressure from higher newsprint costs and rupee depreciation, though overall profitability remained positive and radio/digital turned operationally profitable.
- New topics or risks: Mention of substantial shareholder engagement (Matthew Cyriac/investor firm holding close to 10%), while preferential issue discussions from last quarter did not feature. OTTplay shutdown mentioned as past action.
- Last quarter's promises: No specific operational targets were due for delivery this quarter; radio network rationalization continued as planned.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated total revenue: Rs 502 crore (up 5% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 88 crore (up 26% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 17% (vs 15% YoY)
- PAT: Rs 39 crore 
- PAT margin: 8% (vs 4% YoY)
- Net cash (consolidated): Rs 930 crore
- Print operating revenue: Rs 386 crore (up 8% YoY)
- English Print operating revenue growth: 5% YoY
- Hindi Print operating revenue growth: 11% YoY
- Print circulation revenue: Rs 54 crore (consolidated; flat YoY), Rs 39 crore (HMVL; flat YoY)
- Radio operating revenue: Rs 25 crore (down 23% YoY and sequentially)
- Radio operating EBITDA: Positive Rs 2 crore (9% margin)
- Digital operating revenue: Rs 28 crore (down 30% YoY)
- Digital operating EBITDA: Breakeven to slight positive at Rs 1 crore
- HMVL new investments (AFE): Rs 25 crore, Rs 13.51 crore, and Rs 50 crore

### Highlights
- Consolidated revenue and profitability improved YoY despite the festive season shifting by 20 days into Q3.
- Print advertising grew across both English and Hindi titles, driven by volume consolidation and government revenue impetus (DAVP price increase).
- Radio business achieved positive operating EBITDA after many quarters following station network rationalization.
- Digital operations recorded modest sequential improvement with a focus on a leaner portfolio and quality of earnings.
- OTTplay has been stopped.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they do not provide forward-looking estimates.
- Newsprint costs have flattened at a certain level; management believes they have peaked out, though timing of a downward move depends on currency and global seafaring freight rates.
- Management does not expect the Radio business to slip back into the red following station rationalization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Ad revenue growth was driven by volume market share gains/holding and pricing impetus from government revenue.
- Circulation copies and revenues remained flat as management is not unnecessarily pushing copies amid high newsprint prices.
- Newsprint prices show no downward impetus yet, but have peaked out.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins softened due to higher newsprint costs compounded by rupee depreciation.
- Indirect costs, including employee expenses, saw savings due to an ongoing cost rationalization program (consolidated employee expenses at Rs 95 cr vs Rs 99 cr QoQ and Rs 105 cr YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net cash stands at a healthy Rs 930 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher newsprint costs and rupee depreciation amid global uncertainty putting pressure on margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash R. asked about the drivers of ad revenue growth and circulation trends.** Management explained growth was due to volume consolidation and government pricing impetus, while circulation remained flat as they avoid pushing unnecessary copies during high newsprint cost phases.
- **Mohit Kumra asked about the accounting entry for sundry deposits under other financial liabilities and its relation to AFE investments.** Management clarified that liabilities represent advances received and settlements depend on advertising burn speed, sitting on the non-current asset side under investments, though without a direct one-to-one line item correlation.
- **Mohit Kumra asked about engagement with substantial shareholder Matthew Cyriac (holding close to 10%) and capital allocation.** Management stated they are in conversation with the substantial shareholder and that the Board runs the company, declining to comment on private conversations or forward capital allocation.
- **Mehul Pathak asked about the outlook for Radio profitability and license fee structures.** Management stated step one (surrendering loss-making stations) is done and step two depends on industry lobbying for government license fee revisions under the old GOPA framework, but the business will not suck in more cash.
- **Mehul Pathak asked about unallocated losses of Rs 12.37 crore.** Management explained it includes auditor fees, corporate costs, KMP remuneration, and mark-to-market valuations on investments that cannot be directly allocated to a segment.

### One-line takeaway
HT Media delivered a 5% YoY revenue increase and higher profitability in Q2 FY27, aided by print ad growth and operational turnarounds in Radio and Digital, despite a festive season shift and newsprint cost pressures.
