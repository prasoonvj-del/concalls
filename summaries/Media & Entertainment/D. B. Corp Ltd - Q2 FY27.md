# D. B. Corp Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management stated that underlying business remains stable with positive single-digit growth in advertising revenue and EBITDA on a like-to-like basis when adjusting for the festive season shift (Navaratra falling in Q3 this year vs. Q2 last year).

### Key numbers
- Consolidated total revenue: INR614 crores (vs. INR635 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Consolidated advertising revenue: INR411 crores (vs. INR439 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Circulation revenue: INR116 crores (vs. INR121 crores in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA: INR124 crores (vs. INR115 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR69 crores (vs. INR93 crores in Q2 FY26)
- H1 FY27 consolidated total revenue: INR1,246 crores (vs. INR1,222 crores in H1 FY26)
- H1 FY27 advertising revenue: INR843 crores (vs. INR832 crores in H1 FY26)
- H1 FY27 circulation revenue: INR236 crores (vs. INR241 crores in H1 FY26)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR289 crores (vs. INR297 crores in H1 FY26)
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR169 crores (vs. INR174 crores in H1 FY26)
- Radio business revenue (Q2 FY27): INR39 crores (vs. INR43 crores in Q2 FY26); EBITDA: INR11 crores (vs. INR13 crores)
- Radio business revenue (H1 FY27): INR82 crores (stable); EBITDA: INR26 crores (vs. INR24 crores)
- Circulation base: ~38 lakh (3.8 million) copies (~37.68 lakh)
- Newsprint cost increase: ~20% to 21% YoY (average cost increased from INR47,000 per tonne to INR57,000 per tonne)
- Total cash and bank balance: ~INR1,120 crores
- Other income: INR13.39 crores booked due to the termination of the Ahmedabad registered office lease agreement (derecognition of ROU assets and lease liability as per Ind AS 116/109)

### Highlights
- Festive shift impact: Navaratra shifted from Q2 last year to Q3 this year, creating an estimated advertising revenue impact of ~INR40 crores in Q2.
- Government advertising was impacted during Q2 due to the code of conduct for panchayat elections in a couple of states.
- App subscription price was increased from INR149 to INR199 on an experimental basis.
- Total cash and bank balance stood at ~INR1,120 crores, following a first interim dividend payout of INR5 per share in Q1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic about the next two quarters (H2 FY27) supported by festive season demand and underlying country momentum (though noting monsoon and geopolitical risks).
- Management maintains an endeavor to achieve high single-digit to early double-digit revenue growth for the full year in advertising.
- Newsprint prices are expected to soften by 2% to 3% in Q3 and Q4, though still remaining ~18% to 20% higher YoY.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Real estate advertising grew in single digits (management noted it would have been double-digit absent the festive shift).
- Jewelry and healthcare witnessed strong double-digit growth.
- Banking and financial services (specifically IPO business) saw strong double-digit growth (~50%), though on a smaller base.
- FMCG advertising was down ~2% for the quarter (flat to single-digit growth when considering festive adjustments).
- Automobile advertising was flat; education saw single-digit growth; lifestyle and hypermarkets were down due to festive timing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Newsprint per tonne cost increased by ~21% YoY to INR57,000 due to geopolitical uncertainties and higher freight costs.
- Newsprint inventory is maintained for a few months for domestic supply (10 to 15 days transport) and around a quarter for imported supply.
- Personnel and other operational costs remain strictly under control.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No large capex is planned as production equipment and machinery are fully in place; no major capex is foreseen for the next 4 to 5 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total cash and bank balance stands at ~INR1,120 crores.
- Board will decide on further cash distribution/dividends to shareholders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties driving up imported newsprint and freight costs.
- Uneven monsoon progress in certain parts of India (e.g., Rajasthan).
- Uncertainty surrounding election advertising timing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhinav Mandowara (Aequitas Investments) asked about the full-year ad revenue guidance and circulation price hikes.** Management (Girish Agarwal) stated they are aiming for high single-digit to early double-digit growth in advertising and confirmed no further cover price hikes are planned to avoid accelerating circulation decline.
2. **Lohit Saini (Jay Ram Stock Brokers) asked about the app subscription price hike impact and digital revenue trends.** Management stated the INR149 to INR199 price hike is being run on an experimental basis across cohorts with decent initial results; overall digital monetization spans ads, subscriptions, and tech tie-ups in a nascent phase.
3. **Pavi Chauhan asked about the decline in Monthly Active Users (MAUs) and increase in other expenses.** Management explained MAU decline is intentional due to reduced marketing spend to test organic sustainability, and other expense increases are tied to sales-based ground events/conclaves.
4. **Himanshu Upadhyay (Steadfort Investments) questioned long-term readership and pricing strategy.** Management explained that while circulation copies are largely maintained flat, focus has shifted to maximizing advertiser impact and upgrading ticket sizes rather than aggressive cover price hikes that make newspapers unaffordable.
5. **Alok Nath (Retail Investor) asked about the INR13.39 crore other income and capital return plans.** Management clarified the income arose from the termination and derecognition of the Ahmedabad office lease under Ind AS 116/109, and noted that future cash distribution/dividends will be decided by the Board given the ~INR1,120 crore cash balance and zero major capex plans.

### One-line takeaway
DB Corp's Q2 FY27 financial comparison was depressed by a festive season shift (Navaratra moving to Q3) and a ~21% YoY spike in newsprint costs, but underlying business drivers and advertising momentum remain stable.
