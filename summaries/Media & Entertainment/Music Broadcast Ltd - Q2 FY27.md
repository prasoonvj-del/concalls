# Music Broadcast Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Improved profitability supported by disciplined cost management and an efficient operating structure.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (Q2 FY27): INR 41 crores (vs INR 37.8 crores in Q2 FY26, ~8% YoY growth; moderated sequentially from INR 44.5 crores in Q1 FY27)
- Total expenses (Q2 FY27): INR 34.8 crores (vs INR 43.3 crores in Q2 FY26, ~20% YoY reduction)
- Operating profit before tax (Q2 FY27): INR 1.5 crores (vs operating loss of INR 13.3 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Reported profit before tax (Q2 FY27): INR 8.9 crores (vs loss of INR 6.4 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Revenue (H1 FY27): INR 85.5 crores (vs INR 87.2 crores in H1 FY26, ~2% YoY decline)
- Total expenses (H1 FY27): INR 70.4 crores (vs INR 91.7 crores in H1 FY26, ~23% reduction)
- Operating profit before tax (H1 FY27): INR 5.6 crores (vs operating loss of INR 20.2 crores in H1 FY26)
- Reported profit before tax (H1 FY27): INR 21.2 crores (vs loss of INR 6.3 crores in H1 FY26)
- FCT and NFCT split: 80/20 for the quarter
- Market share: 24%
- Net cash on book: INR 283 crores

### Highlights
- Company focused on four key priorities: strengthening monetization across Radio and Radio Plus platforms, enhancing advertiser engagement through integrated offerings, delivering sustainable and profitable growth, and driving operational efficiency and cost efficiency.
- Revenue growth driven by branded content, events, sponsorships, digital solutions, and integrated advertiser offerings.
- Cost savings achieved via other expenses (saving of INR 1.6 crores vs Q1), while employee expenses increased by INR 60 lakhs, resulting in a net total cost reduction of INR 1 crore compared to Q1. Quarterly cost run-rate expected to remain around INR 39 crores to INR 40 crores.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects quarterly cost run-rate to continue around INR 39 crores to INR 40 crores.
- Management declined to provide guidance for the next 2 years due to market and geopolitical uncertainties.
- Management is hopeful that momentum will continue into the next two quarters with the onset of the festive season.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Revenue moderated sequentially in Q2 due to a softer operating environment and an earlier start of the festive season last year.
- Advertising environment requires a measured and client-focused approach.
- Strong traction seen in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets for experiential activities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Total expenses reduced by ~20% YoY in Q2 FY27 and ~23% YoY in H1 FY27 due to ongoing cost optimization and operational leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Exploring concerts and other events in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets to leverage strong regional presence.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash on books stands at INR 283 crores.
- No current plans for a buyback.
- Management cannot comment on future dividend plans at this time.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Market and geopolitical uncertainties impacting revenue visibility and medium-term guidance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Meghana Saradha (Individual Investor) asked about FCT/NFCT split and cost reduction details:** Management stated the split is 80/20, market share is 24%, and explained that a INR 1.6 crore savings in other expenses offset a INR 60 lakh increase in employee costs to achieve a INR 1 crore total cost reduction vs Q1.
- **Ronak Shah (Equirus Securities) asked about non-FCT acceleration via concerts and events:** Management stated they are focusing on experiential activities and concerts in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets as long as they are profitable, aiming to shore up the 18% to 20% non-FCT contribution.
- **Chandramouli Jagannath (Individual Investor) asked about the sustainability of cost-cutting and net cash/buyback plans:** Management confirmed quarterly costs of INR 39-40 crores should continue, noted INR 283 crores in net cash, and stated there are currently no buyback plans or comments on dividends.
- **Ronak Shah (Equirus Securities) asked for directional guidance for FY27 and the next 1-2 years:** Management declined to give specific guidance due to market and geopolitical uncertainty, but indicated readiness to capture upside if markets improve.

### One-line takeaway
Music Broadcast Limited delivered a significant YoY turnaround in profitability and positive PBT in Q2 FY27, underpinned by strict cost discipline and structural efficiencies despite sequential revenue moderation.
