# Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating revenue grew 13% YoY driven by strategic investments in sports and digital.

### Key numbers
- Operating revenue growth YoY: 13%
- ZEE5 revenue growth YoY: Doubled (grew 37% QoQ to INR6.3 billion)
- Subscription revenue growth YoY: 30%
- Linear network market share: Crossed 20% (as per latest industry recognized data)
- Digital business EBITDA: INR416 million
- Total EBITDA: INR708 million
- EBITDA margin: 3.2% (mid- to high single-digit excluding new initiatives)
- Profit after tax (PAT): INR456 million
- Cash and treasury investments (as of September 2026): INR25.7 billion (bank balance INR5.3 billion, fixed deposits/other investments INR11 billion, mutual funds INR9.4 billion)
- Promoter preferential warrant proceeds received: INR7.4 billion (first tranche)

### Highlights
- Acquired rights for FIFA World Cup (until 2034), Bundesliga, and Serie A (for the next five years).
- FIFA full tournament reach grew ~41% vs. previous FIFA World Cup, with ~83% viewership live on ZEE5 (total reach exceeded 400 million across all platforms).
- Released 34 shows and movies, including 9 original series on ZEE5.
- Developed "Trinetra AI", an end-to-end AI filmmaking and content intelligence platform for micro-dramas (Bullet).
- Acquired international distribution rights for "Drishyam 3-The Conclusion" (released in Q3).
- Received INR7.4 billion from preferential warrant allotment to promoters.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects digital growth momentum to continue in H2.
- Management expects advertising revenue momentum to sustain in H2 without further decline.
- Management anticipates an accelerated trajectory on subscription revenue growth from H2 onwards compared to normalized levels, driven by ~35% to 40% retention of FIFA subscribers and strong Q3/Q4 content line-ups.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Macroeconomic sentiment remained subdued due to high inflation, weaker monsoon season, and external factors, but positive consumer demand indicators and festive season onset are helping advertising.
- Linear TV landscape continues to remain stable.
- Advertising revenue was flat YoY (up 20% QoQ) due to FIFA and marquee shows.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Overall operating costs increased by 18% YoY due to higher advertising and publicity spends across platforms and programming costs related to FIFA and expanded content offerings.
- Digital business maintained strong underlying unit economics, delivering INR416 million EBITDA.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Increased marketing investments in sports and new digital initiatives like Bullet, KidZ, etc.
- Revamped ZEE5 platform UI/UX for mobile and connected TV with AI-led discovery and personalization engines.
- Expanded Zee Studios in the Gujarati market with the comedy drama "Tom and Cherry" and built a language-focused content slate across Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, and Kannada.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and treasury investments stood at INR25.7 billion as of September 2026.
- Increase in cash outflow was impacted by FCCB and dividend payouts during the quarter.
- Receivables increase described as cyclical with no build-up affecting cash flow.
- Slight inventory build-up of shows slated for release in coming quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile geopolitical environment and macroeconomic headwinds leading to subdued sentiment.
- Major ongoing legal case includes the ICC case listed in the notes to accounts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kavish Parekh (360 ONE Capital) on FIFA revenue contribution and subscription trends:** Management stated FIFA revenue largely sits in subscription (both linear and digital) due to limited ad airtime. True linear subscription uptick will be visible next financial year as existing deals were already in place.
2. **Kavish Parekh (360 ONE Capital) on Zee Music segmental reporting and value unlocking:** Management stated they are working towards holistic segmental reporting for the organization rather than piecemeal, but committed no timeline due to businesses being in an investment phase.
3. **Kavish Parekh (360 ONE Capital) on receivables and cash flow:** Management clarified that the cash balance impact was due to FCCB and dividend payouts during the quarter, not receivables.
4. **Sameer (IIFL) on subscription growth sustainability post-FIFA:** Management noted a 35% to 40% retention rate among FIFA users, and expects accelerated subscription growth trajectory in H2 compared to normalized levels, though not as large as 30%.
5. **Mayur Awasthi on operating expenses Note 10 and advertising:** Management stated Note 10 relates to a previous year's transaction (March 2026) with no impact on the current quarter's results, and advertising revenue is expected to sustain in H2.

### One-line takeaway
Zee Entertainment delivered a 13% YoY operating revenue growth in Q2 FY27, heavily propelled by strategic sports investments like the FIFA World Cup and robust digital performance, despite near-term margin dilution from marketing and programming costs.
