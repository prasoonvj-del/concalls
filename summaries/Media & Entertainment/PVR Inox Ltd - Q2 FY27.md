# PVR Inox Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Media & Entertainment*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a record-breaking quarter across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by strong theatrical footfalls, box office momentum across genres/languages, and structural margin improvements.

---

### Key numbers
* **Total Income (Ind AS 116 adjusted):** Rs 2,133 crore, up 17% YoY.
* **EBITDA:** Rs 475 crore, up 44% YoY; EBITDA margin expanded ~420 bps YoY to 22.3%.
* **PAT:** Rs 273 crore, more than doubled YoY (up >100%).
* **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 3,775 crore (up 15% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 705 crore (up 56% YoY); PAT: Rs 344 crore (up 233% YoY).
* **Occupancy:** 30.4% (vs. footfalls of 46.3 million guests, up 4% YoY).
* **Average Ticket Price (ATP):** Rs 298, up 14% YoY.
* **Spend Per Head (SPH):** Rs 153, up 14% YoY.
* **Free Cash Flow (H1 FY27):** Rs 604 crore.
* **Net Cash Position:** Rs 141 crore as of September 30, 2026.
* **ROCE (TTM):** 16.5% (up from 10.2% in FY26).

---

### Highlights
* Industry gross box office had a historic quarter at Rs 4,433 crore (up 23% YoY), crossing Rs 4,000 crore in a single quarter for the first time.
* English box office reached Rs 1,024 crore (+87% YoY), led by *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* (Rs 608 crore).
* Board approved a new capital allocation and dividend distribution policy targeting net debt-to-EBITDA within 0.5x, returning ~30% of annual surplus cash flow to shareholders via dividends/buybacks.
* Executed a maiden share buyback of Rs 300 crore and repaid Rs 259 crore of debt during H1 FY27.
* Shifted useful life assumptions on projectors and IT assets, lowering annual depreciation by Rs 35–40 crore.

---

### Guidance & outlook
* On track to open 90–100 new screens in FY27, with screen additions expected to accelerate to 150–175 screens annually from next fiscal year (~500 screens over 3 years).
* Full-year FY27 capex guided at Rs 300–320/330 crore; next fiscal expected to exceed Rs 350 crore.
* Strong upcoming content slate: *Ramayana: Part 1*, *King*, *Love & War*, *Golmaal 5*, *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, and *Avengers: Doomsday*.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* Theatrical movie-going has sustained structural recovery; streaming platforms have rationalized aggressive direct acquisitions and returned to standard theatrical-first windows.
* Content performance diversified beyond Hindi: Hollywood, regional blockbusters (*Toxic*, *Jana Nayagan*), and low-budget sleeper hits (*Hanuman Ansh*) are driving wide profitability.
* Hindi collection growth in pure data appears lower due to a high base in Q2 FY26 and industry reporting conventions that group Hindi-dubbed versions under Hollywood totals (e.g., ~Rs 200 crore of *Spider-Man* was Hindi dubbed).

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Popcorn and Pepsi constitute 60% of F&B sales, driving robust gross margins.
* SPH growth driven 40% by volume and 60% by pricing/mix. Approximately 85% of patrons now purchase F&B.
* Cost efficiencies achieved via supply-chain automation, shift to 4PL logistics, and reduced inventory spillage/expiry.
* Rent as a percentage of revenue declined in Q2, even as absolute rent rose 11% due to variable revenue-share triggers kicking in on higher box office.
* Other income contained a one-off Rs 7 crore item (reversals of distribution-related provisions and tax refund interest).

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* H1 FY27 capex stood at Rs 132 crore.
* Screen network stands at 1,797 screens across 114 cities (opened 24 screens, closed 25 underperforming screens in H1).
* Piloted "SMART Cinema" format in Muzaffarpur: capex and opex per screen are 25–30% lower than normal screens; targeted for 300 identified Tier 3/4 cities. Rampur and Bharatpur are under fit-out, with ~100 signed in pipeline.
* Focused on asset-light and developer-participative models while targeting payback periods of 3–4 years.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Net cash positive at Rs 141 crore at end-September 2026 (improved from Rs 81 crore in June 2026).
* Discarded previous plans to monetize company-owned cinema real estate, citing comfortable net cash balance and positive earnings contribution from these properties.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Advertising revenues have been slower to pick up due to fewer large pre-marketed tentpoles in H1; monetization of sleeper hits remains a structural challenge until a planned digital pay-per-eyeball system is built (8–10 months away).
* Southern states have ~25% lower ATPs than the national average, though offset by higher occupancy.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Hindi Content Performance:** Analysts questioned weak incremental growth in Hindi box office. Management clarified that the prior-year base was exceptionally high, and that ~Rs 200 crore of Hindi box office from *Spider-Man* was accounted for under English collections.
* **SMART Cinema Rollout & Capex:** Clarified that SMART screens cost 25–30% less to build and operate, will price tickets at ~Rs 190, and will constitute a bulk of next year’s screen additions across 300 targeted smaller cities.
* **Depreciation Accounting:** Management confirmed depreciation run-rate dropped by Rs 35–40 crore annually after engaging with auditors to increase the operational useful life of IT equipment and projectors.
* **Real Estate Asset Recycling:** Management confirmed plans to sell owned real estate are formally shelved as the company is already net cash positive.

---

### One-line takeaway
PVR INOX delivered record quarterly profitability, turned net cash positive, and outlined a scalable low-capex Tier 3/4 expansion engine backed by a strong upcoming movie pipeline.
