# ZEN Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Unsorted*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, strong execution capabilities, and a robust order book.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations for Q2 FY2027: Rs. 345.1 crores (up 98.8% YoY from Rs. 173.6 crores; more than double Q1 FY2027's Rs. 141.6 crores).
- Operational EBITDA for Q2 FY2027: Rs. 132 crores.
- Operational EBITDA margin for Q2 FY2027: 38.2% (vs 37.3% in Q2 FY2026 and 27.3% in Q1 FY2027).
- H1 FY2027 operational EBITDA margin: 35.1%.
- Profit After Tax (PAT) for Q2 FY2027: Rs. 89.5 crores (up 50.7% YoY from Rs. 59.4 crores).
- Consolidated revenue for H1 FY2027: Rs. 486.8 crores (up 46.7% YoY from Rs. 331.8 crores).
- Operational EBITDA for H1 FY2027: Rs. 170.7 crores.
- PAT for H1 FY2027: Rs. 124 crores (vs Rs. 107.2 crores in H1 FY2026).
- Consolidated order book as of September 30, 2026: Rs. 1,375.7 crores (Equipment: Rs. 995 crores; AMC: Rs. 380.7 crores).
- Order inflows during Q2 FY2027: Rs. 481.8 crores.
- Consolidated working capital cycle as of September 30, 2026: 379 days (vs 255 days at end of Q1).
- Cash and bank balances: Approximately Rs. 968 crores (debt-free).
- Hyper strike interceptor capacity planned: 15,000 units per month (180,000 units per annum at full ramp-up); assumed average price of $10,000 per piece.

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue driven by strong order book execution and operational streamlining.
- Employee benefit expenses increased due to performance incentives and R&D headcount ramp-up in AI and emerging product areas.
- Other expenses increased primarily due to warranty provisioning (higher mix of Anti-Drone Systems resulting in a Rs. 9.3 crores higher provision compared to the same period last year) and consultancy/professional fees for R&D.
- Employee and other expenses combined were 24.4% of revenue in Q2 FY2027 (vs 26.3% a year ago).
- Order inflows during the quarter (Rs. 481.8 crores) exceeded execution (Rs. 345.1 crores), expanding the order book from Rs. 1,239 crores at the start of the quarter.
- Working capital increase driven by higher debtor days (due to back-ended revenue weighting towards Q2) and supplier advances for order execution.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year operational EBITDA margin guidance in the mid-30s.
- Management is comfortable achieving revenue slightly above the earlier guidance of Rs. 1,200 crores to Rs. 1,300 crores for FY 2027.
- Regarding the previously discussed two-year Rs. 4,000 crore topline target, management stated it depends on government order finalization and timing, noting that execution will take 12-18 months once orders are received.
- Hyper strike revenue is expected to start contributing from FY 2028, with the majority of orders in FY 2029.
- Capex budgeted at around Rs. 150 crores for FY 2027, and a similar amount budgeted for FY 2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems (C-UAS) orders are expected through Fast Track Procurement (FTP) and capital acquisitions. FTP RFPs are expected to be floated soon, with deadlines to finish by March 31, 2027.
- Indian Armed Forces have provided positive feedback on C-UAS systems operating in high-altitude areas (including proven performance in Operation Sindoor).
- International markets (Middle East, CIS, Southeast Asia, Europe, and the US) are being pursued; European expansion faces local content regulations (e.g., SAFE procurement requiring 65% local content), and US expansion is undergoing compliance processes and prime collaborations.
- Government focus is shifting beyond eastern borders to peninsular/Indian Ocean security and protecting key infrastructure installations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage is normalizing EBITDA margins toward the guided 35% level.
- Warranty expenses fluctuate based on the product mix between ADS and simulators (simulators carry higher margins).
- R&D expenses were 12% of the top line in FY 2026, and management expects to remain in a similar range for FY 2027 without keeping an internal fixed percentage target.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of ~Rs. 150 crores for FY 2027 and ~Rs. 150 crores for FY 2028.
- Hyper strike manufacturing capacity is planned at up to 180,000 units per annum (15,000 units/month across 3 shifts), starting at an initial 600 units/month.
- Recent acquisitions (like Cogknit, ARI, Anawave) add critical IP in command and control, AI, and naval simulation to build "combat training nodes" and tank counter-drone systems.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company remains debt-free with ~Rs. 968 crores in cash and bank balances.
- Working capital days rose to 379 days due to increased receivables from Q2 revenue weighting and supplier advances for executing the order book.
- Operating cash flows for H1 were negative due to working capital deployment, funded entirely from internal resources.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in government ordering cycles, particularly for C-UAS and FTP tenders.
- Geopolitical conflicts (such as the Israel-Iran war) have caused minor dampening or delays in export discussions.
- Strict local content regulations in Europe (65% requirement) and stringent compliance hurdles in the US market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Dixit (GS Commercials) on R&D deployment and C-UAS ordering environment:** Management clarified that R&D targets next-gen defense tech (robotics, AI, anti-drone systems) and acquisitions serve as IP purchases. On C-UAS, management noted that FTP approvals are progressing and RFPs are expected soon, while capital acquisitions take 2-3 years. *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities) on the Rs. 4,000 crore topline guidance and M&A strategy:** Management stated that execution capability is strong (12-18 months), but meeting the two-year topline depends on government order timing. Regarding acquisitions like Cogknit, management explained they target "Pareto technologies" (e.g., command and control, tank situational awareness) that drive 80% of war outcomes. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Dipen Vakil on export traction and US/Europe markets:** Management noted that Middle East discussions have faced temporary pauses due to regional conflicts, while US and European entries require navigating strict local compliance and 65% local content regulations (SAFE procurement). *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Abhijeet Singh on Q2 execution drivers and C-UAS addressability:** Management attributed Q2 execution to extreme operational streamlining and bottleneck identification. They stated Zen addresses 70%-80% of high-end war-grade C-UAS markets. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Bhavya Gandhi on Hyper strike interceptor capacity and timeline:** Management detailed that hyper strike revenue will begin in FY 2028, scaling to a capacity of 180,000 units per annum (at $10,000/unit), with major order flow expected in FY 2029. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
Zen Technologies delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue (Rs. 345.1 crores) in Q2 FY27 with a 38.2% EBITDA margin, driven by streamlined execution and a strong order book, while positioning itself for upcoming domestic FTP tenders and future global defense markets.
