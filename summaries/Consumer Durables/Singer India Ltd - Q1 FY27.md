# Singer India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained strong growth momentum across revenue and profits despite commodity price and sourcing challenges.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs. 144.5 crore, up 57% YoY
- PBT: Rs. 4 crore, up 225% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs. 5.5 crore, up 330% YoY
- Sewing Machine category growth: 74% YoY (Trade channel grew >25%, E-commerce grew >55%)
- ZigZag machines growth: >20% YoY
- Fans business growth: ~61% YoY
- Appliances segment result impact from EPR and investments: Lower by ~Rs. 74 lakhs YoY (Absolute EPR for the quarter was ~Rs. 1.2 crore)
- Operating cash flow before working capital changes (Q1 FY27): Rs. 4.35 crore
- Closing cash and bank balance (including liquid investments): ~Rs. 58 crore
- Bhiwadi factory initial production capacity: 10,000+ units per month

### Highlights
- Sustained strong growth momentum with market share gains across all channels and categories.
- Successfully manufactured new range of Made-in-India ZigZag machines at the Jammu factory, overcoming the learning curve.
- Setting up a new factory in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan, with pilot production planned to commence from the second half of the year; initially focused on ZigZag machines, later expanding to Industrial Sewing Machines and select consumer appliances.
- Appliances segment growth (~15%) driven by trade expansion, E-commerce, and fans; reducing dependence on high-cost channels like modern trade.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to quickly get into EBIT-level breakeven in Appliances by next year.
- Pilot production at the new Bhiwadi factory is slated to commence in H2 of the year.
- Management plans to complete the Kendriya Bhandar order by the end of the quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Steep increase in commodity prices resulted in demand disruption, labor shortage, and price volatility posing sourcing challenges in the appliances industry.
- Government encouragement in garment manufacturing is driving factory expansion and demand for industrial sewing machines, though offset by commodity price pressures.
- Induction cooktops saw decent demand tailwinds, while air coolers faced headwinds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Absolute gross margin for industrial machines went up by 15%, and in percentage terms by ~5%.
- EPR load stood at ~Rs. 1.2 crore for the quarter, impacting appliances segment profitability.
- Strategic inventory buildup and advances for imports (Rs. 14.3 crore advance for ZigZag machines to Singer Sourcing LLC, Rs. 4 crore advance for industrial machines) deployed during the quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bhiwadi factory is progressing well; will follow an asset-light approach starting with assembly/manufacturing (initial capacity 10,000+ units/month) and eventually scaling up to in-house component manufacturing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total deployment of ~Rs. 32 crore in inventory buildup and advances (including import advances).
- Closing cash and bank balance at ~Rs. 58 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Steep increase in commodity prices causing demand disruption, labor shortages, and price volatility.
- Headwinds in the air cooler category.
- High EPR costs impacting appliances profitability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **BIS Standard Compliance for Sewing Machines:** Ashok asked about the implementation of IS 15449. Management confirmed the standard came into effect on August 12, import NOCs have stopped, and Singer is fully compliant and manufacturing compliant ZigZag machines in India.
2. **Kendriya Bhandar Order Status:** Ashok inquired about pending order values and potential 25% scale-up. Management stated they aim to complete the order by the end of the quarter, avoided specific numbers due to competitive sensitivity, and noted that government tender timing is unpredictable.
3. **Appliances EBIT Breakeven:** Ashok asked if EBIT breakeven in consumer durables is expected soon. Management expects to reach EBIT breakeven in appliances by next year.
4. **EPR Impact and Cash Flows:** Laksh asked about the absolute EPR amount and operating cash flow. Management stated absolute EPR was ~Rs. 1.2 crore for the quarter, operating cash flow before working capital was Rs. 4.35 crore, and closing cash was ~Rs. 58 crore.
5. **Import Advances vs. Domestic Manufacturing:** Laksh questioned why heavy import advances (Rs. 14.3 crore) were paid if India manufacturing had started. Management clarified it was a de-risking measure during the transition period and while ramping up capacity past the learning curve.

### One-line takeaway
Singer India delivered strong Q1 FY27 performance with 57% revenue growth and sharp profitability expansion, driven by robust sewing machine demand and scaling domestic manufacturing capabilities.
