# EPACK Durable Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth offset by margin compression due to commodity/forex lags, absence of PLI income, and depreciation/interest costs.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** INR 439 crore (up 106% YoY from INR 213 crore)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 7.1 crore (vs. INR 0.5 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 1.62% (vs. 0.23% in Q2 FY26); comparable EBITDA improvement (excluding PLI of INR 8 crore in Q2 FY26) was INR 14.6 crore.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 1,325 crore (up 51.3% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 RAC revenue growth:** 58%
- **Hisense H1 sales:** Close to INR 90 crore (~18,000 ACs sold till September)
- **Gross debt (Gross):** INR 770 crore to INR 775 crore
- **Epavo JV stake sale consideration:** INR 15 crore for 50% stake

### Highlights
- Delivered highest ever first half revenue of INR 1,325 crore.
- Non-RAC business contributed 63% of Q2 revenue; small domestic (SDA) and large domestic (LDA) appliances grew 57% in Q2 and 62% in H1.
- Components grew 45% in Q2, with deepened captive integration in heat exchangers, copper tubing, PCBs, cross flow fans, and moulding parts.
- Customer base expanded to 72 customers across 19 categories; top 2 customer concentration reduced to 32% (down from 72% in FY26).
- Board decided to transfer 50% stake in Epavo Electricals JV to Ram Ratna Group (RRWL) for INR 15 crore and wind up two non-operational subsidiaries (Bumjin India Audio Products and EPACK Electronic Components Private Limited) to focus on the core business.
- Epavo JV share of losses was INR 12 crore for FY26 and H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated that remaining planned capex of ~INR 100 crore will be staggered over the next 6 to 12 months.
- No significant medium-term investment plans beyond the budgeted INR 100 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Primary sales for room air conditioners (RACs) in July-September were better than last year; channel inventory is 40%–50% lower than last year's 5.5 million to 6 million units, providing a clean starting point for the season.
- Washing machines growing ahead of broader market driven by fully automatic/front-load models and rising tier-2/3 penetration.
- Small appliances (air fryers, mixer grinders, induction cooktops) seeing strong traction from consumer premiumization and sourcing shift to domestic manufacturers.
- Air coolers had a softer year with normalized channel inventories.
- AC industry expected to grow 20% to 25% for the full year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity prices (copper, aluminium, plastics) and FX fluctuations were volatile; price hikes of 15% to 20% (model-dependent) were implemented to counter inflation.
- Gross margin (ex-PLI) improved slightly from 12.4% in H1 last year to 12.8% in current H1.
- Profitability held back by depreciation and interest on new capacity, lag in passing commodity costs, absence of PLI income, and a forex loss of ~INR 10 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Out of the announced INR 470 crore capex plan, INR 370 crore has been actualized till September.
- Pilot production for Hisense front-load washing machines scheduled for end of November 2026, with commercial production starting in December.
- Total installed RAC capacity stands at 2.3 million sets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stands at INR 770 crore to INR 775 crore (net debt-to-equity roughly 0.8x).
- Reduced inventory by ~INR 125 crore and paid INR 225 crore to suppliers in Q2.
- Finance costs remained absolute-flat YoY despite doubled revenue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in commodity prices and depreciation of the INR against USD and CNY causing forex losses and margin lags.
- Expiration of the PLI scheme for EPACK Durable in the current year (though discussions are ongoing with DPIIT for an extension).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tanay (DAM Capital) on volume vs. value growth & gross margins:** Management stated RAC ASPs increased 15%–20% YoY; ex-PLI gross margins improved to 12.8% from 12.4% due to product mix (SDA/LDA growth).
2. **Shreyans Jain (3A Capital Services) on PLI impact & Hisense numbers:** Management noted PLI is in its final year for them and benefits were largely passed to customers; Hisense H1 sales were ~INR 90 crore.
3. **Pratap Maliwal (Mount Intra Finance) on debt levels & peer margin comparison:** Management explained gross debt is ~INR 770–775 crore (0.8x equity) and lower relative margins are due to unutilized capacities during off-seasons and investment cycles in backward integration, which will normalize as scale builds.
4. **Rabindra Nath Nayak (Nirmal Bang) on Epavo JV exit:** Management clarified that exiting Epavo (which had INR 12 crore losses) saves capital discipline, refocuses management on core operations, and opens options to source motors externally.
5. **Rajiv Mehta (YES Securities) on forex impact & margin levers:** Management highlighted that forex loss (~INR 10 crore) dragged margins, and recovery levers include rolling back PLI discounting and improving product mix.

### One-line takeaway
EPACK Durable delivered 106% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by non-RAC expansion, but reported profitability remained constrained by forex losses, unrecovered commodity lags, and higher interest/depreciation costs.
