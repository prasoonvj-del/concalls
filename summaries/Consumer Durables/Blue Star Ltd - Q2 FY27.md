# Blue Star Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume growth and order wins are offset by persistent margin pressure and an inability to fully pass on raw material costs.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** ~21% YoY.
*   **Operating Profit Growth:** ~10.5% YoY.
*   **Segment 1 (Electromechanical Projects & Commercial AC):** Revenue Rs 1,936 cr (+16.3% YoY); Segment Result Rs 133 cr (6.9% margin vs 8.8% YoY).
*   **Segment 2 (Unitary Products):** Revenue Rs 898 cr (+29.4% YoY); Segment Result Rs 21 cr (2.3% margin vs 6.2% YoY).
*   **Segment 3 (Professional Electronics & Industrial Systems):** Revenue Rs 97 cr (+51% YoY); Segment Result Rs 23 cr (23.7% margin vs 9.6% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 7,229 cr (vs Rs 7,120 cr YoY).
*   **Net Cash Position:** Rs 289 cr (vs net debt of Rs 417 cr YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Room AC (RAC):** Primary sales growth of ~42% in Q2.
*   **MedTech Divestment:** Sold business for Rs 35.6 cr (completed Aug 31, 2026); warranty provision write-back improved profitability.
*   **Strategic Shift:** Electromechanical projects business is defocusing from infrastructure to prioritize data centers and factories.
*   **Commercial Refrigeration:** Showing early signs of revival after a period of degrowth.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **RAC Revenue Growth:** Management expects ~25% for the full year.
*   **Segment 2 Margin:** Management targeting 6.5% for the full year.
*   **Segment 1 Margin:** Guidance maintained at 7% to 7.5%.
*   **Data Center Opportunity:** Order book expected to grow from ~Rs 2,000 cr to Rs 4,000 cr in 3 years.
*   **Capex:** Full-year guidance of ~Rs 300 cr.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **RAC Industry:** H1 growth estimated at ~30% in value terms.
*   **Commercial Refrigeration:** Expecting single-digit growth this year; long-term potential remains high but currently impacted by FMCG slowdown and pause in quick commerce infrastructure expansion.
*   **Concerns:** Monsoon shortfall (impacting Tier 3-5 demand) and interest rate hikes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** Driven by material cost escalations; total cost increase since January is ~18%, with price hikes covering ~13%, leaving a ~5% gap.
*   **Product Rejig:** Implementing "Total Competitiveness Management" (value engineering, removing features) to protect margins.
*   **Outsourcing:** Evaluating increasing outsourced RAC products from 15% to 30% to optimize costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Rs 300 cr guidance includes R&D and digital expenditure.
*   **Depreciation:** Run rate has increased due to amortization of R&D, new office/warehouse leases, and digital investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Shifted to a net cash position of Rs 289 cr from a net debt of Rs 417 cr YoY, driven by improved working capital management.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Pricing Discipline:** Management does not anticipate industry pricing discipline; expects aggressive competition to continue.
*   **Input Costs:** No softening in raw material prices observed as of October.
*   **Demand Sensitivity:** Potential impact on demand from rural income drop (monsoon) and interest rate hikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Manoj Gori (Equirus):** Asked if 6.5% margin is achievable. Management: It is a "tough task" requiring scale, product rejig, and successful price pass-throughs.
*   **Natasha Jain (Phillip Capital):** Asked about pricing discipline from peers. Management: Do not expect discipline; competition is aggressive and everyone is fighting for market share.
*   **Sameer Gupta (IIFL):** Asked about the 57% YoY spike in depreciation. Management: Attributed to amortization of R&D, new office/warehouse leases, and digital investments.
*   **Rahul Agarwal (IKIGAI):** Asked about construction contract business. Management: Defocusing from infrastructure; growth is driven by data centers, targeting Rs 4,000 cr revenue in 3 years.
*   **Achal Lohade (Nuvama):** Asked if the October price hike was absorbed. Management: Not yet fully absorbed; real effect will be visible in November.

### One-line takeaway
Strong volume growth in RAC and data center order wins are currently offset by margin pressure from persistent material cost inflation, with management prioritizing product portfolio optimization to hit 6.5% segment margins.
