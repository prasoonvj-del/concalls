# Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-24 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent double-digit revenue and profit growth with supply chain disruptions behind them, despite commodity headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs. 2,263 crore, up 18.2% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 205 crore, up 24.1% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9%, expanding 40 bps YoY.
- **PAT:** Grew 65% YoY (note: PBT excluding exceptional items grew 36.2% YoY; previous year had a Rs. 20 crore exceptional item for Baroda plant restructuring).
- **ECD segment growth:** 19.4% YoY; EBIT margins improved to 11.6% (30.7% growth YoY).
- **BLDC fans growth:** 40% YoY in general trade.
- **Lighting revenue:** Rs. 298 crore, up 14.3% YoY; EBIT margin at 12.9%.
- **Butterfly revenue growth:** 15.9% YoY (excluding in-house mixer grinder sales in base last year).
- **Exports growth:** >70% YoY (highest-ever quarterly revenues).
- **Cumulative price hikes:** 11-15% across most businesses over the last six months (Fans at the top end); covers ~80% of cost increases.

### Highlights
- Launched refreshed brand identity in August 2026 featuring the "Cephyr" monogram, a new tag, and a distinctive sonic identity.
- Supply chain disruptions from Q1 FY27 are fully resolved; inventory days normalized to levels of a year ago.
- Unnati cost savings program and operating leverage drove bottom-line growth.
- Restructuring LKA (Large Kitchen Appliances) and Rhion under Shweta Sagar (CBO of Butterfly) to leverage the Butterfly ecosystem and materially reduce costs (historical EBITDA losses of Rs. 16-25 crore).
- Wires business expanded from South India to Punjab, Delhi NCR, and Gujarat.
- Solar rooftop B2C/C&I launched in 6 states; solar pump execution continuing strongly (>50% of AP government order book executed).
- Evaluation of a Greenfield manufacturing facility is in advanced stages for fans, appliances, and wires.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains very well on track for medium-term guidance.
- Confident of continuing the double-digit revenue and profit growth trajectory in H2 FY27.
- Re-stated Crompton 2.0 ambition to double revenue (growing at ~15%) and achieve 11-12% EBITDA margins by FY29 on a higher revenue base.

### Demand & industry commentary
- General trade and rural trade both delivered double-digit growth.
- E-commerce was subdued due to deliberate repositioning of go-to-market channels to improve profitability.
- Solar pump demand saw a temporary slowdown due to the tapering of Kusum 1.1; expects ramp-up with Kusum 2.0.
- Water heaters gained market share; small domestic appliances (SDA) grew >20% led by air fryers, induction cooktops, and mixer grinders.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Facing commodity cost pressures and unpredictable geopolitical developments.
- Addressing cost increases through disciplined strategic price increases (11-15% cumulative), Unnati cost savings, and operating leverage.
- Gross margins at the Crompton level (excluding solar rooftop/pumps mix impact) have been restored by and large compared to Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Evaluating Greenfield manufacturing plant in advanced stages to raise capabilities in fans, appliances, and wires.
- Solar pumps/rooftop leverage lighting/pumps infrastructure; new adjacent businesses avoid creating heavy separate cost centres.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Days of inventory normalized to levels of a year ago.
- Export business operates entirely on cash (no credit given).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity cost pressures and unpredictable geopolitical developments impacting raw materials.
- B2B lighting business margins impacted by pre-contracted orders.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Bhartia (Investec) on RM inflation & price hikes:** Management stated 11-15% price hikes have been taken across most businesses (80% cost pass-through), with further hikes planned if commodities rise, net of Unnati savings.
2. **Sameer Gupta (Indiv. / Analyst) on Solar Rooftop / Pumps:** Management noted solar rooftop is a Rs. 26,000 crore TAM where Crompton has a right to win via trust, service capabilities, and turnkey execution.
3. **Viraj Kacharia (Securities) on Butterfly & margins:** Management clarified that core category share gains and Idea First series are key drivers; North, East, and West are showing strong disproportionate growth.
4. **Umang Mehta (Kotak Securities) on LKA restructuring:** Management quantified historical LKA/Sandbox EBITDA losses at Rs. 16-25 crore, which restructuring aims to eliminate.
5. **Nilesh Doshi (Analyst) on FY29 EBITDA margin target of 11-12%:** Management explained that building scale requires upfront investments in people, brand, and innovation, and 12% margin will be achieved on a significantly larger revenue base (Crompton 2.0).

### One-line takeaway
Crompton delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 18.2% revenue growth and 24.1% EBITDA growth, backed by normalized supply chains, strong general/rural trade demand, and disciplined price pass-throughs.
