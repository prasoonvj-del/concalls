# P N Gadgil Jewellers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong revenue and profit growth, successful QIP, and successful expansion into Northern/Central India.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 2,666 crore (+22% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 224.6 crore (+57% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 8.4% (+186 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 121 crore (+53% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 4.5%.
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 5,079 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 226.5 crore (+52% YoY).
*   **Retail Revenue:** Rs 2,061 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **SSSG:** 25.5%.
*   **Gross Margin:** 12.7% (+89 bps YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Brand Portfolio:** Rebranded 'LiteStyle' to 'YOOU by PNG' (lightweight diamond jewellery); acquired 100% of 'Silvostyle' (silver fashion jewellery).
*   **Capital Raise:** Raised Rs 700 crore via QIP (oversubscribed 1.5x).
*   **Store Network:** 80 stores (79 India, 1 US).
*   **Regional Performance:** Northern/Central India stores showing strong traction with a 17.4% stud ratio.
*   **Operational Shifts:** E-com revenue declined 84% YoY due to strategic reduction in low-margin coin sales.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Store Expansion:** On track to open 23 new stores (total ~103); 8-10 openings targeted for Q3, remainder in Q4.
*   **FY27 Revenue:** Management targets Rs 13,000 crore+.
*   **FY27 PAT Margin:** Guidance of 4% to 4.2%.
*   **FY29 Aspiration:** Management targets Rs 17,000 crore+ revenue with a 25% CAGR (FY27-FY29).
*   **Debt:** Management expects debt to reduce by 20-25% by FY29/FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Seasonality:** Q2 was seasonally subdued (Chaturmas); Navaratri festive sales (approx. Rs 200 crore) shifted to Q3.
*   **Consumer Behavior:** Elevated gold prices are driving a shift toward lightweight designs, studded jewellery, and old gold exchange.
*   **Festive Outlook:** Management remains positive on Diwali and the wedding season, citing strong booking interest.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin Impact:** Sequentially down 50 bps due to franchisee revenue share increasing to 17% (from 12% in Q1).
*   **Hedge Coverage:** Raised to ~80% to mitigate gold price volatility.
*   **Operating Expenses:** Management noted successful rationalization of marketing and promotional costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Expansion Model:** Shifting toward FOCO (Franchisee Owned Company Operated) model. Future store mix planned at 50-50 COCO/FOCO.
*   **Funding:** Management stated internal accruals are sufficient for COCO expansion; no further debt required.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Cash/FDs at Rs 1,191 crore (up from Rs 554 crore in Q1).
*   **Debt:** Net borrowing reduced by Rs 167 crore (net of QIP and increased GML loans).
*   **Inventory:** Average store inventory requirement is Rs 50-55 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Gold Price Volatility:** Management noted this as a risk, mitigated by 80% hedge coverage.
*   **Seasonal Shifts:** Impact of festive timing on quarterly revenue recognition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did gross margins dip QoQ?** A: Management attributed this to the increase in franchisee revenue share (17% vs 12%), which dilutes gross margins but improves EBITDA margins due to lower operating costs.
*   **Q: How will you fund expansion?** A: Management stated internal accruals are sufficient for COCO stores; no further debt is needed.
*   **Q: What is driving success in UP?** A: Management cited a strategic "Peshwa" positioning, brand legacy, and the right merchandise mix.
*   **Q: Is current inventory turnover sustainable?** A: Management confirmed 3.7x-4x is sustainable as new stores mature.
*   **Q: How is the QIP money being used?** A: Funds were used for working capital for festive inventory; the balance is parked in bank FDs.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational performance with 57% EBITDA growth and successful expansion into Northern/Central India, supported by a robust balance sheet and a strategic shift toward a franchisee-led model.
