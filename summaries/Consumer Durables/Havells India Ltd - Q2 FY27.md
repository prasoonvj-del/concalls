# Havells India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-24 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 35% YoY driven by steady consumer demand and contributions across all categories, despite commodity volatility and margin pressures in cables and Lloyd.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue growth:** 35% YoY
- **EBITDA growth:** ~30% YoY
- **Cables contribution margins (H1):** ~15%
- **Switchgear contribution margins:** 38% - 39%
- **Lloyd revenue growth:** 28.5% YoY
- **Lloyd RAC contribution to revenue:** Close to 75%
- **Export share (Switchgear):** ~15%
- **ROCE:** Close to 23%
- **Capex guidance (FY27):** ~INR 14 billion (Cables and wires, R&D centre)

### Highlights
- Delivered strong performance in Q2 with broad-based growth across established categories (cables, switchgear) and emerging renewables.
- Consumer demand remained steady despite an inflationary environment.
- Undertook calibrated price hikes (10% to 25% over 12 months across categories) to counter sharp raw material inflation.
- Operating leverage supported healthy EBITDA growth of ~30% YoY; PBT growth was moderate due to a high base from a one-time gain in other income last year.
- Refrigerator factory commissioned in March; non-RAC refrigeration contribution to Lloyd volume growth has been decent.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a positive outlook on demand and margins going into the festive season.
- FY27 capex estimated at ~INR 14 billion, allocated towards cables and wires and the R&D center.
- Full cable/underground cable capacity expansion to be on stream in the next 18 months.
- Management avoided giving specific revenue growth guidance, quarterly margin targets, or a timeline for Lloyd turning profitable.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer demand remains steady; switchgear volume growth close to double digits.
- Overall wires and cables volume growth close to double digits.
- Unorganized sector in wires has increased due to high inflationary trends; organized players are taking share from the unorganized sector.
- Lloyd distribution system successfully transitioned from push-led to pull-led over the last 6-9 months.
- Renewable energy scaling up fast; Havells focusing on brand, distribution, and complete integrated kits rather than competing with large manufacturers/gigawatt players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Contribution margins softer YoY primarily in cables due to a decline in aluminium prices against a strong base last year.
- Switchgear contribution margins returning to normalized levels of 38%-39%.
- Lloyd margins impacted by off-season seasonal under-absorption and raw material inflation that could not be fully passed on.
- Potential further price hikes of 5% to 7% being considered in Q3 if cost surges persist.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex for FY27 guided at ~INR 14 billion, focusing on cables and wires and the R&D center.
- Recent underground cable expansions focused more towards medium voltage (MV) and extra high voltage (EHV) to capture growth over the next 5-7 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ROCE currently close to 23%.
- Distribution shift away from pushing inventory has helped maintain channel return on capital and health.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Macroeconomic uncertainties persist and commodity prices remain elevated.
- Aluminium price volatility impacts cable contribution margins.
- Off-season seasonal under-absorption creates profitability challenges in Lloyd.
- Potential for irrational behavior from new competition in the wires segment affecting market share/contribution margins temporarily.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Natasha Jain (PhillipCapital) on Switchgear margins:** Management clarified that margins are returning to normalized 38%-40% levels as pricing actions settle, though raw material challenges remain.
2. **Sameer Gupta (Individal Investor) on Wires competition:** Management noted that new entrants like Crompton rely on brand and distribution without tech/factories, while serious investors are welcomed; Havells relies on deeper distribution and trade relationships to defend market share.
3. **Pavan Kumar (Analyst) on Lloyd profitability:** Management declined to provide a timeline for profitability, reiterating a focus on building brand and distribution in the medium term.
4. **Achal Lohade (Analyst) on Lloyd distribution rejig:** Management confirmed the transition from push to pull-led distribution (implemented in Oct last year for Lloyd) has stabilized over 6-9 months.
5. **Rahul Agarwal (Analyst) on ROCE targets:** Management stated current ROCE is close to 23% and expects return ratios to improve as new capacities ramp up and existing assets sweat further.

### One-line takeaway
Havells posted a robust 35% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by steady consumer demand and volume growth, even as commodity volatility and off-season under-absorption in Lloyd created near-term margin headwinds.
