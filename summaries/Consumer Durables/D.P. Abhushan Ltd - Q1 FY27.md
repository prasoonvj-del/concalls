# D.P. Abhushan Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a strong start to the year with 58% YoY revenue growth, expanded margins, and robust footfall conversion despite high gold prices.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** Rs 853.63 crore (up 58% YoY from Rs 541 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 93.99 crore (up 70% YoY from Rs 55 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.01% (expanded by 80 bps YoY from 10.21%)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 64 crore (up 77% YoY from Rs 36 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **PAT Margin:** 7.55% (up 82 bps YoY)
- **Category-wise Revenue:** Gold at Rs 781 crore (up 59% YoY); Silver at Rs 40 crore (up 150% YoY from Rs 16 crore); Diamond at Rs 29 crore (vs Rs 31 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Product Category Mix:** Wedding-related purchases at 62%; Festive and lightweight jewellery at 18%; Other categories/gift articles at 20%
- **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG):** 52% overall
- **Footfall-to-Conversion Ratio:** 81%
- **Average Ticket Size:** Approximately Rs 1,57,000
- **Old Gold Exchange Contribution:** Approximately 25% of total sales
- **Gold Jewellery Inventory Mix:** 22-karat at nearly 80%; 18-karat at around 15%; balance from lower-karatage products
- **Studded Mix:** 6% to 7% of business
- **Volume Growth:** 1% to 2% YoY in Q1 FY27

### Highlights
- Witnessed strong seasonal and occasion-led demand from Akshaya Tritiya, summer weddings, and regional festivities.
- Launched the "D.P. Swarna Plus Scheme" in April (minimum SIP ticket size of Rs 5,000) for price protection and gold weight accumulation.
- Finalized locations for two new showrooms: Dahod, Gujarat (approx. 3,200 sq. ft., COCO model) and Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh (approx. 3,750 sq. ft., first FOCO model).
- Launched e-commerce website and mobile app (available on Play Store and App Store) and expanded on online marketplaces.
- Undertook brand-building via strategic sponsorship of the Madhya Pradesh League – Scindia Cup 2026 and launched the "Rani Rupmati Collection" showcased in Kota.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets reaching a total of 51 stores by FY30 (primarily COCO, with 5 to 7 under the FOCO model).
- Management aims to add 5 to 6 new stores in the current financial year.
- Management targets an internal volume growth of approximately 10% for both FY27 and FY28.
- Management targets increasing the studded mix to 12% to 15% by March 2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Elevated gold prices influenced early quarter decisions, but a sharp correction in June and stability in July improved buying sentiment.
- Consumers showed higher interest in lightweight, daily-wear, minimalist, and versatile jewellery designs.
- Old-gold exchange-led purchases increased due to high gold prices and import duty hikes, supporting affordability.
- Digital discovery via online catalogues and social media is increasingly preceding in-store purchases.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating profitability improved due to higher revenue scale, better operating leverages, disciplined cost management, and a favorable sales mix.
- Hedging mechanisms include real-time inventory replenishment, exchange-based procurement, Gold Metal Loans (GML) from banks/nominated agencies, and MCX-based hedging.
- Carrying inventory value is approximately Rs 1,20,000 vs prevailing gold prices of Rs 1,51,000–Rs 1,52,000 (pure gold fresh inventory around Rs 1,45,000–Rs 1,50,000).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dahod (3,200 sq. ft.) marks the company's entry into Gujarat under the COCO model.
- Jabalpur (3,750 sq. ft.) is the company's first Franchisee-Owned Company-Operated (FOCO) model showroom.
- Break-even for new capex is generally achieved within 6 to 9 months; inventory turnover reaches 3x after 2-3 years and 4.5x to 5x after 4-5 years. Mature showrooms (6-7 years) can provide 8x to 9x inventory turnover.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory turnover ratio operates around 4.7x to 5.0x annually.
- 95% to 98% of assets consist of inventory; operating cash flows remain negative due to the high working capital intensity inherent to the industry's inventory requirements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government increase in gold import duty from 6% to 15% in May 2026 introduced near-term cost and demand sensitivity.
- Sharp spikes in gold prices lead to temporary pauses in discretionary volume demand as customers adopt a wait-and-watch approach.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Praveen Jayaraman (Avendus Spark):** Asked about the traction of the DP Swarn Plus scheme and the FOCO model structure. *Management detailed the monthly SIP structure and explained that the Jabalpur FOCO model is a pilot revenue-sharing arrangement where D.P. manages operations and SOPs entirely.*
- **Kanishk Gupta (Analyst):** Questioned the bridge to the 51-store target by FY30 given recent annual store addition guidance of 3 to 4 stores. *Management clarified they are targeting 5 to 6 store additions this year (moderated temporarily in Q1 due to May developments) and expect to accelerate to achieve 51 stores by FY30.*
- **Aanchal Maheshwari (Naredi Investment):** Asked about hedging strategies and diamond/studded ratio targets. *Management outlined real-time replenishment, old-gold exchange, GML, and MCX hedging, and stated a target to increase studded mix to 12%–15% by March 2028.*
- **Nitin Dhanawat (Analyst):** Asked about volume growth (noting 1%-2% volume growth in Q1) and negative operating cash flows. *Management responded that value growth was driven by gold prices and higher making charge realizations, while negative operating cash flows are a mathematical function of maintaining high inventory (95%-8% of assets) to drive sales.*
- **Sonu Nebhwani (Analyst):** Inquired how growth will be sustained once high gold price bases normalize and volume growth remains at 1%-2%. *Management stated they internally target ~10% volume growth for FY27 and FY28, noting that pent-up demand returns once gold prices stabilize.*

### One-line takeaway
D.P. Abhushan delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 58% YoY revenue growth and 77% PAT growth, driven by robust wedding demand, higher making charge realizations, and an 81% conversion rate, despite modest volume growth of 1%–2%.
