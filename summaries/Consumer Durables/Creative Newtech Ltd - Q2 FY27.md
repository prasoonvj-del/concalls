# Creative Newtech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 / H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong earnings growth, improved operating margins, substantial cash generation, and strategic business expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 754.80 crore (YoY growth of 14.43%)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 752.14 crore
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 40.11 crore (YoY growth of 50.70%)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 5.31% (vs 4.04% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 30.12 crore
- **Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 25.94 crore (YoY growth of 36.88%)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 3.44% (vs 2.87% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 1,231.65 crore (YoY growth of 16.55%)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 1,228.21 crore
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 65.01 crore (YoY growth of 55.78%)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 5.28% (vs 3.95% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 46.82 crore
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 39.47 crore (YoY growth of 35.49%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 3.20% (vs 2.76% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 351.24 crore
- **H1 FY27 Net Working Capital Days:** 45 days (vs 75 days in FY26)

### Highlights
- Received an advance work order from BSNL for the BharatNet Middle Mile Network Project in the Odisha Telecom Circle, valued at approximately Rs 3,194.83 crore.
- Proposed acquisition of Infinova India Private Limited is expected to be completed by November 15, 2026.
- Launched global consumer technology brand WOZOYO via Hong Kong subsidiary Secure Connection Limited in the US and India.
- Brand business share of total revenue increased to 19% in Q2 FY27 (17% in H1 FY27, up from 14% in FY26).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth of around 20% (give and take).
- Management targets first-year WOZOYO brand revenue of Rs 45 crore to Rs 50 crore.
- Management targets scaling the brand business share to 40% of total revenue mix (translating to Rs 2,400 crore of revenue) by FY29.
- Management expects the BSNL project to contribute Rs 150 crore to Rs 200 crore in revenue in FY27, and Rs 500 crore to Rs 600 crore in the next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India is entering a phase of technology-led development with growing demand for integrated digital connectivity and infrastructure services.
- The India air purifier market is valued at Rs 2,000 crore growing at a 24% CAGR, while the US air purifier market is ~Rs 28,000 crore to Rs 30,000 crore.
- The Indian surveillance market is valued at Rs 30,000 crore.
- Growing enterprise and institutional demand for local AI workstations ("AI in a box") as customers avoid cloud data exports.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Brand business gross margins stand at 40% to 42%, converting to a PAT of 10% to 11% currently.
- Management spent close to Rs 12 crore on brand building for WOZOYO so far.
- Management stated that sustainable net working capital days are expected to normalize around 52 days to 55 days (compared to 45 days reported in H1 FY27).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- BSNL project involves a capex of Rs 1,700 crore and opex of Rs 1,200 crore spread across 3.5 years.
- Infinova acquisition completion is targeted by November 15, 2026, with sales expected to reflect in financials within 3 months (January/February).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- H1 FY27 operating cash flow reached Rs 351.24 crore.
- Net working capital days improved to 45 days in H1 FY27 from 75 days in FY26.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Middle East numbers are under pressure due to war, alongside rising freight charges affecting India.
- High product return rates in the US market (though WOZOYO returns are currently low at 3% to 3.5% vs industry standard of 8% to 9%).
- Government projects involve rigorous quality control (QC) and inspection processes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rehan Saiyyed (Trineta Asset Managers) asked about WOZOYO traction/channels in the US/India and Infinova acquisition timeline.** Management stated US sales reached ~$2.2 million (~Rs 20 crore) in the first 3 months across Amazon, TikTok, and retail; Infinova acquisition is expected to be completed before November 15, 2026.
- **Yash asked about WOZOYO marketing spend and brand margin scaling.** Management stated ~Rs 12 crore has been spent on brand building, and reiterated the target to reach Rs 2,400 crore in brand revenue by FY29.
- **Sudhir Bheda (Bheda Family Office) asked about working capital sustainability and BSNL revenue timelines.** Management stated steady-state working capital days will normalize around 52 to 55 days; BSNL project will contribute Rs 150 crore to Rs 200 crore in Q4 FY27 and Rs 500 crore to Rs 600 crore in FY28.
- **Amit Mehendale (RoboCapital) asked about brand PAT margins and capital raising plans.** Management stated long-term brand PAT is estimated around 8% to 10% as marketing scales; equity capital raising will be considered if larger M&A or acquisition opportunities arise.
- **Nishita asked about brand PAT margins.** Management clarified current brand PAT is ~11% to 12% and is expected to stabilize around 8% to 10% at a larger revenue scale (~Rs 1,000 crore to Rs 1,200 crore per brand).
- **Vignesh Iyer (Sequent Investments) asked about festival shifts impacting sales and working capital.** Management noted festival shifts pushed some Q2 dispatches into Q3, and reiterated steady-state working capital days of 52 to 55 days.

### One-line takeaway
Creative Newtech delivered strong H1 FY27 performance with EBITDA surging 55.78% YoY, supported by a massive BSNL infrastructure win, successful scaling of brand businesses (WOZOYO and Honeywell), and robust operating cash generation.
