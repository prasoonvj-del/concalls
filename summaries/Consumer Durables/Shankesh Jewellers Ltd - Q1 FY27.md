# Shankesh Jewellers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-17 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong year-on-year revenue growth and significant margin expansion driven by festive demand and product mix.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 424 crore, up 55% YoY from Rs 273 crore in Q1 FY26.
- Gross profit: Rs 66 crore, up 95% YoY and 7% QoQ. Gross margin expanded by 323 bps YoY and QoQ.
- EBITDA: Rs 61 crore, up 92% YoY and 30% QoQ. EBITDA margin improved by 279 bps YoY and 504 bps QoQ.
- PAT: Rs 43 crore, up 100% YoY from Rs 22 crore in Q1 FY26, and up 36% QoQ. PAT margin improved by 230 bps YoY and 385 bps QoQ.
- Business mix (Q1 FY27): Corporate customers 66%, non-corporate customers 34%.
- Product portfolio mix (Q1 FY27): 22-karat jewellery ~80%, 18-karat jewellery ~20%.
- FY26 financials: Revenue Rs 1,631 crore (3-year CAGR ~21.5%), EBITDA Rs 158 crore, PAT Rs 107 crore, ROCE 41.6%, ROE 50.9%, Debt-to-Equity 0.8x.
- 18-karat revenue growth: Scaled from Rs 18 crore in FY24 to Rs 221 crore in FY26.

### Highlights
- Maiden earnings call following public market debut on August 25, 2026 (listed on NSE and BSE).
- Asset-light business model utilizing an established, long-term network of karigars for handcrafted jewellery.
- Corporate customer contribution to revenue increased from 55% in FY24 to 64% in FY26, and 66% in Q1 FY27.
- Pan-India geographic revenue contribution in FY26: West India 32%, East India 28%, South India 21%, North India 18%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects future growth to track a similar or better pace compared to historical performance.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand seen in bridal and festive collections (such as Akshaya Tritiya in Q1).
- Structural tailwinds from the ongoing formalization of the Indian jewellery industry.
- Bridging emotional attachment and traditional craftsmanship (bridal jewellery contributes ~60% of sales).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by intricate design collections and festive product mixes (e.g., Akshaya Tritiya).
- Wastage ranges from 4% to 14% for handcrafted jewellery.
- Hedging policy: "Buy what we sell" (immediate procurement at current market price upon sale).
- Inventory valuation follows a weighted average method.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity ratio improved from 2.1x in FY23 to 0.8x in FY26.
- Trade receivables stood at Rs 126 crore (~20 to 22 days).
- Inventory turnover is approximately 8 times.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gold price volatility.
- Hardness of gold increasing when moving down to lower carats (14-karat and 9-karat).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nishita (Retail Investor) asked about volume growth versus price increases.** Management stated that physical volume sold was approximately the same YoY, with growth driven by product mix and customer relationships.
- **Amar Nath (OIR Fund) asked about hedging and margin improvement amid falling gold prices.** Management explained that inventory uses a weighted average method and margins depend on product mix (e.g., 18-karat vs 22-karat).
- **Bijal Shah (Analyst) questioned how the company achieves exceptionally high PAT/EBITDA margins compared to peers.** Management attributed it to the handcrafted product mix, intricate designs, and being in the gold jewellery business. *(Management offered to provide granular data offline via email/separate call).*
- **Ajit Sahu (Analyst) asked about the sharp sequential decline in employee costs (from Rs 11.3 crore in Q4 to Rs 3.3 crore in Q1).** Management explained that Q4 included director ex-gratia payments which were absent in Q1.

### One-line takeaway
Shankesh Jewellers delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 55% YoY revenue growth and robust margin expansion, underpinned by its asset-light handcrafted jewellery model and expanding corporate clientele.
