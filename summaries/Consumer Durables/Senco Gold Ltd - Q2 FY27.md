# Senco Gold Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong consolidated revenue growth (34% YoY) and store additions, but Q2 EBITDA margin compressed to 4.4% due to old gold exchange, festive inventory build, discounting, and hedging impacts.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained full-year operational EBITDA margin guidance of 7.5%–7.8% and store opening pace.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from Q1 lean-season headwinds and heatwaves to robust festive (Dhanteras) footfalls and wedding season demand, though unorganized competition discounting intensified.
- **New topics or risks:** Old gold exchange surging above 51% (causing 20–40 bps margin pressure), alternative funding via Commercial Paper (CP), and substantial net hedging gains included in other income (INR 99.9 crores). GML availability concerns from Q1 disappeared as it normalized.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Annual store opening target of 18–20 stores was delivered early, with 24 stores opened by mid-November (14 in H1 + 10 post-H1).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** +34% YoY in Q2; +52% YoY in H1.
- **Retail Revenue Growth:** ~29% in Q2.
- **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG):** 19% in value in Q2; 17% during the Dhanteras period (1 Oct to 11 Nov).
- **Milestones:** H1 sales crossed INR 5,000 crores; TTM sales exceeded INR 10,000 crores.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 4.4% in Q2; H1 EBITDA margin at 7.6%.
- **PAT & PAT Margin:** Q2 PAT margin at 4.1%; H1 PAT at INR 188 crores (~22% YoY growth) with a 3.7% margin.
- **Dhanteras Period (41 days, 1 Oct–11 Nov):** Revenue growth of 25%–26%; turnover crossed INR 2,400 crores; diamond sales growth at 26%.
- **Old Gold Exchange:** >51% of total business.
- **Stud Ratio:** H1 stud ratio at ~11.6%; Dhanteras stud ratio at ~9.5%–10% (expected to return to ~12% in Dec–Feb).

### Highlights
- Opened 14 new stores in H1 and 10 more between 1 October and mid-November, totaling 24 stores YTD (surpassing the full-year target of 18–20 stores). 3–4 more stores planned in the next 3–4 months.
- Became the second jewellery company in India to raise Commercial Paper (CP) funds for future growth.
- Launched titanium jewellery integrated with gold and diamonds, following earlier men's collection initiatives.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 7.5%–7.8%.
- Expects Q3 revenue growth of at least 25% YoY and Q4 growth of at least 20% YoY, driven by the wedding season (Nov–Feb) and Valentine's Day diamond season.
- Long-term vision: INR 20,000 crores revenue, 8% EBITDA margin, and ~15% stud ratio.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Average weight range preferred by consumers is 10%–12% lower than last year due to high gold prices; demand for 9-carat and 14-carat lightweight/everyday-wear jewellery is rising.
- Unorganized segment is driving competitive pricing pressure by leveraging high import duties.
- Festive new customer acquisition grew 30% YoY; customer mix stands at 40%–45% new and 55%–60% repeat.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin declined to 4.4% due to old gold exchange (>51% share, creating a 20–40 bps melting/opportunity loss), festive channel/franchisee inventory build, aggressive discounting/schemes (Flexi Gold), and 40%–50% hedging in a falling gold price phase.
- Customs duty gain: INR 16 crores in Q1 and INR 30 crores in Q2 (total INR 46 crores in H1, contributing ~90 bps to H1 EBITDA).
- Other income surged to INR 1,221 million (from INR 177 million), primarily driven by net gains on hedging positions (one-off) taken to protect cash flows against customer offers. Regular FD/securities income is INR 35–40 crores per year.
- Hedging ratio maintained at ~50% under Board policy, with an intent to move toward 65%–70% over time.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 24 stores added YTD; 3–4 additional stores planned for the remainder of FY27. Focus remains on East, North, West, and Central India.
- Inventory increased by ~INR 200 crores versus March 2026, pertaining to 7 new COCO stores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings increased to support inventory and store expansion; Gold Metal Loans (GML) dropped to 17% of working capital borrowings in Q2 due to earlier banking/GST constraints, but are expected to normalize upward over the next 3–4 months.
- Operating cash flow optically negative due to the accounting shift from GML to cash credit borrowings.
- Customer advances (Flexi Gold, MariGold) stand at INR 700–800 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Quarterly margin volatility and transient pressures from unorganized sector price wars.
- Hedging and melting losses associated with high old gold exchange proportions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Madhavendra (Individual Investor) on margin volatility:** Questioned why Senco's margins compressed while peers delivered better margins. Management explained that East India accounts for 80% of revenue with distinct market peculiarities, and reiterated H1 EBITDA of 7.6%.
2. **Deepika (Elios Financial Services) on other income jump:** Asked about the surge in other income to INR 1,221 million. Management clarified it largely constitutes net gains from hedging positions taken to protect cash flows against customer acquisition schemes (Flexi Gold).
3. **Rajas Shah (BTH Capital) on GML drop and competition:** Inquired about GML dropping to 17% and competitive intensity. Management stated GML availability had normalization issues in Q1 that are now easing (GML ratio to rise in 3–4 months), and identified the unorganized sector as the source of discounting pressure.
4. **Maitri Shah (Sapphire Capital) on financial presentation and vision:** Asked about presentation clarity on other income/expenses and long-term 2030 targets. Management advised reviewing notes to accounts for net hedging impacts and reaffirmed expansion plans across North/West/East India.
5. **Shubham (Individual Investor) on share price and profit growth:** Criticized profit growth relative to top-line performance and share price underperformance. Management defended their strong 52% H1 revenue and 22% PAT growth, stating they do not interfere in share prices and remain focused on execution.

### One-line takeaway
Senco Gold delivered robust H1 revenue growth of 52% (Q2 up 34% YoY) and crossed INR 10,000 crores in TTM sales, though Q2 EBITDA margins compressed to 4.4% due to aggressive customer acquisition, high old gold exchange, discounting, and hedging impacts.
