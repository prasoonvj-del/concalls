# Golkunda Diamonds & Jewellery Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth alongside operational execution on domestic manufacturing and retail rollout.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (Focus for next 2-3 years remains expanding in India; new facility achieving 75-80% capacity utilization within 2 years).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains confident; emphasis shifted heavily toward domestic market execution, lab-grown diamond retail rollout (NIBHANA), and establishing Indian market presence.
- New topics or risks: Entered domestic retail via lab-grown diamond brand "NIBHANA" with 4 operational stores; flagged negative operating cash flows due to rapid capex and store rollout investments.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on the commencement of the new 5,360 sq. ft. domestic manufacturing facility in Andheri East (operational since August 2026).

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 87.35 crore (+7.92% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 9.94 crore (+84.62% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 6.78 crore (+113.23% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 172.57 crore (+14.75% YoY vs Rs 150.38 crore in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 18.58 crore (+77.18% YoY); EBITDA margin at 10.74% (+378 bps YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 11.92 crore (+88.76% YoY); PAT margin at 6.89% (+270 bps YoY).

### Highlights
- Commenced domestic jewellery manufacturing in August 2026 at a new 5,360 sq. ft. facility in Andheri East, Mumbai.
- Launched proprietary lab-grown diamond retail brand **NIBHANA** through wholly owned subsidiary Golkunda Retail India Private Limited.
- Opened 4 NIBHANA retail stores across Maharashtra (2 in Mumbai, 1 in Pune, 1 in Nashik).
- Introduced 500+ new designs monthly, expanding the design repository to over 30,000 designs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management plans to open 7 additional NIBHANA stores in the coming quarter (reaching 11 stores) and aims for ~100 stores in 3 to 4 years.
- Management expects the domestic manufacturing facility to reach 75% to 80% capacity utilization over the next 2 years.
- When fully operational, domestic manufacturing is expected to add 50% to 60% to overall revenue compared to current levels.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic jewellery sector supported by wedding/festive demand, organized retail penetration, and demand for lightweight everyday branded jewellery.
- Lab-grown diamond (LGD) prices have largely stabilized over the last 6-8 months, with some price increases observed.
- Indian market is seeing growing consumer awareness and acceptance of lab-grown diamond jewellery.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- LGD jewellery margins stand at 20% to 25%, which is better than natural diamond margins.
- Raw materials (gold and diamonds) are significantly imported; gold represents 50% to 60% of total imports.
- Price risk on gold is hedged either by locking prices with customers at order placement or through gold margin loan facilities.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure for the Andheri domestic facility was ~Rs 7 crore (excluding working capital), adding 100 to 125 kg annual jewellery capacity (+50% to 60% overall capacity).
- Typical NIBHANA store economics: Rs 40-50 lakhs establishment/interiors, Rs 1.5-2 crore inventory, Rs 40-50 lakhs security deposit (total Rs 2-2.5 crore investment per store).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows were negative due to heavy upfront investments in new factory capex and retail store rollouts.
- Balance sheet holds 8 to 10 kgs of gold inventory (sample lines and work-in-progress orders) valued at purchase cost (~$3,950/oz prevailing rate).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and sluggishness in international markets led management to pause aggressive international expansion, focusing instead on India.
- Near-term cash burn from rapid store and facility rollout.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Arya Shah (Minevra Investments): What is the current capacity utilization and ramp-up timeline for the new Mumbai facility?**  
   *Management:* The facility started in late August 2026 and is currently doing minimal sampling/orders (~Rs 1 to Rs 1.5 crore sales). Expects to hit 75% to 80% capacity in 2 years.
2. **Arya Shah: What are the store economics and expansion plans for NIBHANA?**  
   *Management:* Investment per store is Rs 2 to Rs 2.5 crore (Rs 40-50 lakh establishment, Rs 1.5-2 crore inventory, Rs 40-50 lakh deposit). 4 stores are open, 7 more scheduled next quarter, targeting 100 stores in 3-4 years.
3. **Tirth Patel: What is the reason behind negative operating cash flows?**  
   *Management:* Significant cash outflows went into heavy capital expenditure for the new manufacturing facility and setting up retail stores for long-term domestic returns.
4. **Divyesh Sheth: Who are the target B2B customers for the new facility, and what is your product specialization?**  
   *Management:* Target customers include major retail chains in South India and existing export retail clients in the Middle East, plus internal production for NIBHANA stores. Specialization is strictly diamond-studded jewellery, not plain gold or colored stones.
5. **Aman: How do lab-grown diamond margins compare to natural diamonds, and is price deflation a risk?**  
   *Management:* LGD prices have stabilized over the past 6-8 months. LGD margins are currently 20% to 25%, higher than natural diamond margins.

### One-line takeaway
Golkunda Diamonds delivered strong Q2 FY27 profitability (PAT up 113% YoY) while aggressively executing its domestic strategy through a new manufacturing plant and rolling out its "NIBHANA" lab-grown retail stores.
