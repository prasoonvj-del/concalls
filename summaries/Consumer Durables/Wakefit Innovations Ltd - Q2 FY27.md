# Wakefit Innovations Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue with strong volume and omnichannel growth, despite raw material volatility and festive shift.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: COCO store addition guidance raised; management stated they will surpass the initial target of ~80 stores for the year (having already added 68 stores in H1). Capex guidance maintained at Rs 100-120 crore.
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains positive; noted a festive shift from Q2 to Q3 due to late festive dates, but October trends were encouraging.
- New topics or risks: Detailed discussion on jumbo store economics (Capex >Rs 50 crore for the first Bangalore store, targeting Q2 FY28 launch).
- Last quarter's promises: Front-loaded store openings as promised, adding 41 stores in Q2 (68 in H1).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 452 crore (+19.9% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 revenue:** Rs 856.9 crore (+18.4% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 50 crore; EBITDA margin: 11.1%
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 106.6 crore; Margin: 12.4%
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 30.5 crore (+26% YoY); Margin: 6.7%
- **PAT:** Rs 15.9 crore (Reported); PAT margin: 3.5%
- **PAT (excl. deferred tax):** Rs 22.3 crore (+39.9% YoY); Margin: 4.9%
- **H1 FY27 PAT (excl. deferred tax):** Rs 53 crore (+49% YoY)
- **Gross profit:** Rs 243 crore (+12% YoY); Gross margin: 53.9% (vs 57.7% YoY)
- **Employee benefit expenses:** Rs 54.5 crore (+30% YoY, includes ESOP of Rs 2.5 crore)
- **A&P spend:** 5.2% of revenue from operations
- **Store count:** 205 stores across 137 cities as of September 30, 2026 (added 41 in Q2)
- **MBO count:** 2,589 outlets across 787 cities
- **Channel mix (Own channels):** 70% of Q2 revenue (+39.4% YoY)
- **Repeat customer revenue:** >32%

### Highlights
- **Category performance:** Mattresses contributed 61.8% of revenue (+21.4% YoY); Furniture contributed 30.1% (+24.9% YoY); Furnishings contributed 8.1%.
- **Festive shift:** Festive sales began in the second week of October (vs last week of September last year), shifting some demand from Q2 to Q3.
- **Retail strategy:** Front-loaded COCO store additions in H1 (68 stores added) to allow maturation ahead of H2. Shifted store mix towards mini-stores (~1,000 sq. ft.) which now number 95 out of 205 stores (vs 110 mega stores).
- **Jumbo stores:** Construction of the first jumbo store in Bengaluru is progressing towards a Q2 FY28 launch.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Store expansion:** To moderate store additions in H2 to focus on productivity, but total net additions for the year will exceed the initial target of 80 stores.
- **Capex:** Rs 100-120 crore planned for FY27 (80% for retail footprint/jumbo stores, 20% for manufacturing, automation, and upgrades).
- **Marketing:** A&P spend expected to remain in the 7-8% range for the full year.
- **Pricing:** Management does not plan further price hikes barring unforeseen crude price spikes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer discretionary spending remained moderate with ups and downs.
- Cross-selling is strong, with an average cart carrying nearly 2 products.
- Furnishing growth normalized after filling leadership vacancies.
- Furniture production and delivery issues (machine breakdown, labor shortages, LPG shortage) have been resolved.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Crude-linked raw materials (polyol, TDI) remained elevated due to West Asia geopolitical disruptions.
- Raw material prices are up 30% to 60% compared to pre-war levels.
- Calibrated price hikes (cumulative 10% to 11% on mattresses since March) matched absolute rupee increases in raw material costs, keeping absolute rupee gross margin per unit stable.
- Rent expense guided to Rs 90-92 crore for the year, optimized by smaller warehouse and store footprints.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 cash capex stood at around Rs 60-65 crore (Rs 25-30 crore for COCO stores, remainder for jumbo store, automation, and upgrades).
- Standard store payback period remains under a year. Jumbo store payback is targeted around 2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory build-up includes raw materials and finished goods to prepare for sale events (maintains 3-4 months visibility).
- Brought forward tax losses expected to be fully exhausted by FY28 (Q2 or Q3).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated crude-linked raw material prices and geopolitical disruptions affecting raw material availability and logistics.
- Moderate consumer demand and spending on discretionary products.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Siddhartha Bera (Nomura):** Asked about festive demand trends and same-store sales. Management noted robust initial waves of festive sales and mid-teens like-for-like store growth.
- **Harish Advani (Axis Capital):** Asked about volume vs value growth in mattresses. Management stated mattress volume grew in mid-teens, with value higher due to price hikes.
- **Dhiraj Kaswan (InCred Equities):** Asked about channel mix and A&P spend. Management affirmed a steady target split of ~70% own and ~30% external channels.
- **Rudraksh Raheja (ithought Financial):** Asked about low rent expenses relative to store expansion. Management explained the shift to smaller mini-stores and warehouse footprint optimization.
- **Praneeth (SJ Investments):** Asked about deployment of cash and jumbo store costs. Management stated the first jumbo store capex and construction will exceed Rs 50 crore, targeting a ~2-year payback.

### One-line takeaway
Wakefit achieved record Q2 revenue of Rs 452 crore up 19.9% YoY, driven by aggressive omnichannel store expansion and steady volume growth while absorbing raw material inflation through calibrated pricing.
