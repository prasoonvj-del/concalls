# Bajaj Electricals Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted significant improvements in gross margins, EBIT, and PBT despite modest revenue growth, with a clear focus on future top-line acceleration.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue growth:** 1.5% YoY.
*   **Gross margin:** 31.9% (maintained); 32.1% YoY (vs 31.4% last year).
*   **EBIT margin:** 4.4% (improved 140 bps from 3%).
*   **PBT:** Rs 35 crore (more than doubled from Rs 15 crore YoY).
*   **Brand investments:** 3.8% of revenues (up 150 bps YoY).
*   **Consumer Products EBIT margin:** 4% (5.6% on a like-to-like basis adjusted for brand investments).
*   **Lighting Solutions EBIT margin:** 3.1%.
*   **BLDC fan growth:** 56% YoY.

### Highlights
*   **Leadership Transition:** Chairman Shekhar Bajaj will step down from executive responsibilities effective Jan 1, 2027, becoming Chairman Emeritus. Sudarshan Sampathkumar will assume the role of Non-Executive Independent Chairman.
*   **Consumer Products:** Grew 2.4%, driven by kitchen and domestic appliances (induction cookers, water heaters, steam irons).
*   **Lighting Solutions:** Degrew 1.3% due to contraction in professional lighting; consumer lighting grew double-digits, supported by wires and switchgears.
*   **Strategic Focus:** Management is prioritizing premiumization, innovation, and channel optimization.
*   **Cost Management:** Cost efficiency programs are yielding results, helping absorb commodity inflation.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management aims to grow double-digits ahead of the market.
*   **Profitability:** Management aspires to reach double-digit profitability but noted this is dynamic and dependent on inflation and investment needs.
*   **Timeline:** Management expects to surpass the highest-ever PBT (from FY21) within the next 2-3 years.
*   **Fans:** Management expects to see acceleration in fan growth in the next 3-6 months as new BLDC capacities come online.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Fans:** The category saw a decline, which management attributed to a portfolio skewed toward entry-level/sub-economy fans and a late entry into the BLDC segment.
*   **Professional Lighting:** Experienced a double-digit decline due to lost project bids and a selective approach to order winning amidst high inflation.
*   **New Categories:** Wires and switchgears are showing good traction; switchgears grew 28% (on a small base).
*   **Channel:** E-commerce grew in double digits; exports also showed strong growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Commodity costs (metal and plastic) remain elevated.
*   **Pricing:** Management prefers productivity improvements over price hikes but has initiated price increases in lighting (October) and is evaluating hikes for Consumer Products.
*   **Strategy:** Aiming for less than half of inflation to be passed through price, with the remainder absorbed by productivity.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **BLDC Capacity:** Currently building multifold capacity for BLDC fans, expected to be operational in 3-6 months.
*   **Distribution:** Focused on 4 states for new category penetration (wires/switches) where the brand is strong.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Inventory:** Management stated they have no plans for inventory correction in Q3, unlike the previous year, as channel health has improved.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Fan Portfolio:** The company is currently underperforming in the fan category compared to industry peers.
*   **Professional Lighting:** Faced a slowdown in orders and lost project bids.
*   **Macro Environment:** Geopolitical uncertainty and persistent commodity inflation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst (Sameer Gupta) asked why Bajaj is an outlier with flat growth vs. peers like Havells/Crompton.** Management admitted the fan portfolio is the primary drag but noted that excluding fans, other categories achieved double-digit growth.
*   **Analyst (Keshav Garg) questioned the long-term underperformance and loss of market share.** Management defended the company, stating they are gaining/maintaining share in most categories except fans, and emphasized that the current management team is focused on correcting the fan portfolio.
*   **Analyst (Manoj Gori) asked about the sustainability of margins.** Management explained that while they want to reach double-digit profitability, they may prioritize growth investments over margin expansion if opportunities arise.
*   **Analyst (Rabindra Nath) asked about BLDC fan pricing.** Management indicated the market operating price for the BLDC portfolio is between Rs 2,500 and Rs 3,000.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Electricals is prioritizing a turnaround in its fan portfolio and scaling new categories like wires/switches to drive top-line growth, while management transitions to a new governance structure.
