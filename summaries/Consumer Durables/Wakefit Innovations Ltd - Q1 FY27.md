# Wakefit Innovations Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and EBITDA growth were strong despite raw material volatility, with management maintaining a balanced approach to pricing and expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs. 404.9 crore (+16.6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs. 56 crore (+25.2% YoY); Margin: 13.9%.
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 37 crore (+50% YoY); Margin: 9.1%.
*   **PAT:** Rs. 23.3 crore (+19.2% YoY).
*   **Gross Profit:** Rs. 231 crore (+19.4% YoY); Margin: 57.1% (vs 55.8% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Category Performance:** Mattress (65.9% of revenue, +27.3% YoY), Furniture (28%), Furnishing (6.3%).
*   **Retail Expansion:** Added 27 COCO (company-owned, company-operated) stores; total network 165 stores across 100 cities.
*   **Channel Mix:** Own channels contributed 72.3% of revenue (+20.5% YoY); External channels contributed 27.7% (+7.6% YoY).
*   **Omni-channel:** Online channels contributed 52.7% of revenue; Offline channels contributed 47.3%.
*   **Customer Metrics:** Average units per transaction is ~2 products; repeat customers account for 36% of revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Capex:** Management expects to incur Rs. 100-120 crore in FY27 (80% for retail/Jumbo stores, 20% for manufacturing automation).
*   **Store Expansion:** Management remains on track to add ~80 COCO stores in FY27.
*   **Furniture Growth:** Management expects mid-to-late teens growth over the next two quarters, with a step-jump of 25-30% expected once Jumbo stores open.
*   **Marketing:** A&P spends expected to remain in the 7-8% range for the full year.
*   **Corporate Overheads:** Expected to remain in the 7-8% range as a percentage of top line.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Mattress:** Growth driven by volume (two-thirds) and price increases (one-third).
*   **Furniture:** Growth slowed due to machine breakdown, workforce shortages, and a pause in adding furniture-first stores to improve unit economics.
*   **Premium Segment:** The Rs. 25,000+ mattress segment is primarily an offline play.
*   **Market Share:** Management estimates their organized market share at ~10% and aims to add 4-5 percentage points over 3-5 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Materials:** Volatility in Polyol and TDI prices driven by Middle East geopolitical developments.
*   **Impact:** Management expects the full impact of raw material cost increases to be reflected in H1 FY27.
*   **Gross Margin:** Management expects ~100 bps compression in Q2 vs Q1 due to higher input costs.
*   **ESOP Expense:** Guidance revised to Rs. 10-12 crore for FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Jumbo Stores:** First store expected June-July 2027, second in August-September 2027 (Bangalore).
*   **Payback:** New stores have a payback period of ~10-11 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical instability in the Middle East impacting raw material supply and costs.
*   Potential for competitive intensity in waves.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Siddhartha Bera (Nomura):** Asked about volume vs. value growth. Management clarified 2/3 volume, 1/3 price.
*   **Navin:** Asked about premiumization. Management noted premium segment share is stable at 15-20% of total.
*   **Ritesh Shah:** Asked about price cuts vs. cost push. Management explained a "sequencing issue" where temporary stability led to minor price cuts before renewed volatility.
*   **Dheeresh:** Asked about store payback. Management confirmed ~10-11 months for new stores.
*   **Bala Murali Krishna:** Asked about market share goals. Management aims to add 4-5 percentage points to organized market share over 3-5 years.

### One-line takeaway
Wakefit delivered strong Q1 growth driven by mattress volume and retail expansion, while navigating raw material volatility through calibrated pricing and operational efficiency.
