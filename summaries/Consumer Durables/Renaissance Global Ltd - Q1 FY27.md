# Renaissance Global Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, improved profitability, and significant progress on working capital optimization.

### Key numbers
- Revenue (excluding bullion sales): INR690 crores (up 30% YoY from INR530 crores)
- Owned brand revenue: INR89 crores (up 29% YoY from INR69 crores)
- EBITDA: INR50 crores (up 22% YoY from INR41 crores)
- Owned brand EBITDA margin: 11.5% (vs 10% in Q1 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR25.6 crores (up ~280% YoY from INR6.6 crores)
- Profit before exceptional items: INR29.7 crores (up 40% YoY)
- Working capital days: 220 days (improved by 33 days YoY from 253 days in Q1 FY26)
- Forex loss: INR13 crores

### Highlights
- Commenced FY27 with healthy revenue growth, operating leverage, and disciplined execution.
- Operating 3 Jean Dousset retail stores following the July 2026 opening of the San Francisco store; plans to add 4 more locations in FY27.
- Strategic transformation continuing toward a global, high-margin branded jewellery platform.
- Initiatives underway to deliver working capital improvements of ~INR250 crores and operating cash flow of >INR300 crores in FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects bottom line growth of >30% for FY27.
- Revenue growth may be muted (with an expected annualized reduction of INR300 crores to INR400 crores) due to exiting unprofitable customer brand lines.
- Management targets INR1,000 crores of direct-to-consumer revenue by FY29 with an operating margin of at least 15% from this segment.
- Jean Dousset store rollout plan: 7 stores by the end of FY27 and 6 more stores in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Jean Dousset positioned at the luxury end of the lab-grown diamond jewellery market.
- Three-tiered approach across brands: Licensed brands average order value (AOV) around USD400, WithClarity AOV around USD3,000, and Jean Dousset AOV around USD8,000. Customer brands span from USD50 to USD3,000.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Annualized operating expenses reduced by ~INR45 crores over the last 24 years/months.
- Gross margins for Jean Dousset and WithClarity are 60% to 65%.
- Metal price increases have positively impacted inventory values during exits, resulting in negligible/zero exit costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity reduced to align with lab-grown diamond realities.
- Jean Dousset store economics: each store adds INR25 crores to INR35 crores of sales; payback period is <1 year with profitability starting in month 2 or 3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital efficiency improved, with working capital days down to 220 days from 253 days.
- Expected working capital reduction of ~INR250 crores and operating cash flow >INR300 crores in FY27 via inventory and receivable optimization.
- Net debt expected to be meaningfully lower by the end of FY27 compared to the previous year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange losses of INR13 crores recorded due to currency appreciation on dollar-denominated working capital.
- Exiting certain low-margin, high-working-capital customer relationships creates temporary revenue drag and minor working capital exit costs in P&L.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ashok Shah (Eklavya Invesco) asked about revenue extrapolation and tariff refunds.** Management stated revenue growth may moderate due to customer exits, but PAT momentum will continue (>30% growth); tariff refund applications are in process.
2. **Manprit Aurora (Aurora Wealth Advisors) asked about working capital initiatives of INR250 crores.** Management explained they are exiting low-quality customer brand lines with high consignment/receivable days, leading to INR300-400 crores revenue reduction without hurting the bottom line.
3. **Manprit Aurora asked about licensed brand performance/margins.** Management noted rationalization of licenses, dropping NFL, Netflix, and Harry Potter to focus primarily on the House of Disney, targeting a return to 14%-15% margins.
4. **Prateek Chaudhary (Samarthya Investment Advisors) asked about forex losses and inventory losses.** Management clarified forex loss is an accounting impact from currency appreciation on dollar-denominated working capital; there were no metal inventory losses due to hedging.
5. **Prateek Chaudhary asked about Jean Dousset store expansion in FY28 and stake increase.** Management stated plans for 7 stores in FY27 and 6 more in FY28; stake increase above 65% is not currently discussed at the Board level.

### One-line takeaway
Renaissance Global is intentionally sacrificing ~INR300-400 crores of low-margin B2B revenue to optimize working capital and focus on high-margin D2C power brands (Jean Dousset, WithClarity, Disney), guiding for >30% bottom-line growth in FY27.
