# Whirlpool of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong double-digit revenue growth and successful premium product launches offset by a steep decline in profitability due to geopolitical inflation, forex depreciation, and regulatory costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained no formal numerical full-year revenue or margin guidance due to macroeconomic unpredictability.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious expectations of a challenging JAS quarter to highlighting resilient volume growth and strong traction in premium portfolios (Luxuriem, Bloomwash, ACs).
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Transition of CEO/MD (Pralhad Bhutada stepping down, Nitesh Ahuja taking over effective March 31, 2027) was a new management update. Old risks around seasonal AC lows persisted.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The Pune plant ramped up fully as promised, achieving highest-ever top-load washer production in July 2026. *Luxuriem* refrigerator and new washer lineups were successfully launched with heavy promotional backing.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,873 crore (up 13.7% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,873 crore (up 14% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 2.1% (decline of 310 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 40 crore (decline of 31% YoY)
- **PAT / Profitability:** Rs 25 crore (decline of 39% YoY)
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 4,600 crore (up 12.8% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 3.9%
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 179 crore (decline of 33% YoY)
- **H1 PBT:** Rs 173 crore (decline of 29% YoY)

### Highlights
- Achieved double-digit top-line growth (circa 14%) driven by up-pricing, mix improvement, and multi-outlet retail expansion.
- Successfully ramped up the Pune manufacturing plant, achieving highest-ever top-load washer production in July 2026.
- Launched the flagship luxury refrigerator *Luxuriem* featuring a triple evaporator system, Obsidian interiors, and 6th Sense AI ethylene gas sensors; backed by massive PR and digital ad campaigns (e.g., T20 series, JioHotstar, Prime Video).
- Expanded washing machine portfolio with high-kg (10 kg) models, new CMF options (Purple Karigari glass, lilac tea), and built-in detergent dispensers.
- Elica kitchen appliances delivered ~14% growth, supported by new launches like Slim BLDC hoods and Flexi Hob-Tops.
- Secured 30-year exclusive Brand and Technology licenses with Whirlpool Corporation ensuring uninterrupted global IP access.
- Announced leadership transition: Pralhad Bhutada to step down as CEO & MD as his tenure ends March 31, 2027; Nitesh Ahuja appointed as successor, with a handover period starting January 1, 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects margin pressure to linger through the near term due to geopolitical and commodity headwinds, but anticipates relief as anniversary effects catch up in upcoming quarters.
- No formal full-year numerical guidance provided.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Frost-free refrigerators and automatic washing machines saw healthy growth, led by premium tiers.
- Air conditioner sales grew over 60% on a smaller base despite July-September being a traditionally low season.
- Semi-automatic washing machines and direct-cool refrigerators experienced intense competitive rebating and promotional intensity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins squeezed by 310 bps YoY due to geopolitical conflicts (Middle East war) spiking crude-derived raw materials (polystyrene, MDI, polyol, polypropylene, ABS).
- Forex depreciation (USD appreciation) weighed heavily on imported components (roughly 30% of inputs).
- Regulatory compliance costs (such as e-waste norms) and new energy-efficiency changes for air conditioners impacted profitability.
- Productivity programs (P4G) and indirect procurement savings partially offset cost inflations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pune plant fully ramped up to meet demand.
- Factories operating with flexibility to scale via extra shifts; cash reserves are being evaluated for automation and potential inorganic opportunities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a cash-rich balance sheet with efficient working capital management.
- Whirlpool Corporation holding stands at 39.7%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical instability driving up crude prices and input costs.
- Foreign exchange volatility impacting imported component costs.
- Intense competitive pricing, discounting, and promotional intensity in entry-level categories (direct-cool refrigerators and semi-automatic washers).
- Regulatory cost headwinds (e.g., e-waste compliance).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) on Capex and Stake Sale:** Asked about capex plans and rumors regarding parent stake sale. Management stated capex is focused on regular enhancements, automation, and R&D, and clarified that any transaction involving the parent's 39.75% stake is handled between buyer and seller per disclosure norms without management involvement.
2. **Atul Mehra (Motilal Oswal AMC) on Capital Allocation and Buybacks:** Inquired about utilizing excess cash for a buyback. Management responded that the Board reviews capital allocation regularly and will put collective decisions on the table when finalized.
3. **Achal Lohade (Nuvama Institutional Equities) on Margin Drivers and Outsourcing:** Asked about raw material impact and third-party outsourcing risks. Management explained that unlike historical trends where falling RM costs expanded margins, current crude and forex spikes cannot be fully passed on due to intense competition; on outsourcing, management noted they utilize OEM/3PL partners judiciously while embedding unique Whirlpool technological specifications.
4. **Chheda (Vallum Capital) on Senior Management Attrition:** Raised concerns over senior-level changes and reporting structures. Management reassured analysts that attrition is normal/lower than industry averages, internal talent benches are strong, and leadership transitions (such as Nitesh Ahuja succeeding Pralhad Bhutada) are planned well in advance.

### One-line takeaway
Whirlpool of India posted 14% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by robust premiumization, but profitability dropped 39% due to severe crude-oil inflation, forex depreciation, and regulatory cost pressures.
