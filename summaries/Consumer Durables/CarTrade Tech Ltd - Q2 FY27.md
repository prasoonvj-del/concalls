# CarTrade Tech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever consolidated revenue and PBT, with 36% EBITDA margins and strong cash reserves crossing Rs 1,400 crores.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 213 crore (up 10% YoY, sequentially up from Rs 201 crore in Q1).
- **Total Income:** Rs 236 crore (highest ever in history).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 76 crore (up 20% YoY and 20% QoQ).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 36% (up from 33% in Q2 last year and 31% in Q1).
- **Adjusted EBITDA (Cash Proxy):** Exceeded Rs 106 crore for the quarter.
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 85.45 crore (highest ever in history, up 7% YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 65.95 crore (up 3% YoY).
- **Cash Reserves:** Rs 1,400+ crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **MAUs:** 76 million average for the quarter.
- **Organic Traffic:** Jumped to 96%.
- **Remarketing (SAMIL) Metrics:** 1.56 million vehicles listed annually, 2.35 lakh vehicles auctioned annualized for Q2; Remarketing PAT grew 47% YoY, revenue up 12% YoY, EBITDA margin up 30%.
- **OLX Metrics:** Revenue half-yearly growth at 28%, quarter growth at 26%; EBITDA margins up to 32%; EBITDA growth up 36%.

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever total income (Rs 236 crore), revenue (Rs 213 crore), and PBT (Rs 85 crore) in company history.
- Four-year CAGR metrics: PAT up 60%, Revenue up 24%, EBITDA up 74%, and EPS up almost 7x.
- Launched Elite Seller product for businesses/consumers, which has become a significant part of the seller revenue model.
- Launched Elite Buyer program (first buyer-initiated monetization model on OLX) with a massive uptake in daily sign-ups.
- Formed a strategic partnership with Landmark for used car retail operations, combining CarTrade's tech/customer reach with Landmark's physical operations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects OLX growth rates to surpass current levels, crossing 30%-35% in coming quarters.
- Sequential EBITDA growth from Q2 to Q3 is expected to be similar (20% QoQ).
- Management stated Q3 is likely to be the best-ever quarter in almost all businesses, with strong revenue growth over Q2.
- The company is working towards its long-term goal of achieving Rs 1,000 crore profit in the next 4-5 years.
- VAYA Live is expected to roll out for OEMs and consumers this quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Standalone consumer revenue growth moderated to 4% YoY (vs 18% in Q1) primarily due to a high base effect from the GST reduction on September 22, last year (which drove a 37% growth in Q2 last year).
- Festival season timings shifted by about 10 days, moving some demand from September into October.
- October is projected to be the best-ever month in the history of the New Auto business.
- Digital ad spends by OEMs continue to see a positive structural trend over offline spends.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee costs remained stable with an 8% growth over last year, demonstrating business leverage.
- EBITDA margins expanded to 36% for the group and 41% for the consumer group.
- Tech and license costs remain fixed in nature.
- VAYA tech/token costs are not material as the product is built on proprietary data and AI code rather than costly LLM data sources.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Physical footprint stands at 500 locations and 1.6 million vehicles auctioned per year.
- Developing an e-commerce platform for non-auto products (mobiles, white goods) and cars with escrow and logistics integration.
- Integrating used car loans with banks, expected to be significant 12 months out.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents crossed Rs 1,400 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Zero major capex and minimal working capital requirements, leading to continuous cash generation.
- Tax benefits and shelters remain active in the standalone entity, restricting immediate buyback or dividend distribution via the parent entity due to regulatory timelines.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Base effect distortions from the abnormal surge following the September 2025 GST rate cut.
- OLX PAT showed a de-growth YoY primarily due to a high base effect from a ~Rs 10 crore write-back in Q2 last year and an increase in tax expenses (tax shelters present last year vs tax paid this year).
- Lower other income this quarter compared to last year due to prior-period expense write-backs at OLX.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sanchit (Citigroup) asked about standalone revenue growth moderation and OEM ad spends.** Management clarified it was purely a base effect from last September's GST reduction and festival timing shifts, not a change in OEM approach.
- **Siddhartha Bera asked about OLX new product monetization and loan integrations.** Management highlighted the rapid adoption of Elite Buyer and Elite Seller programs, and noted loan integrations with banks are in early stages but expected to scale significantly in 12 months.
- **Swapnil asked about the 18% YoY decline in monthly unique traffic (43 million).** Management attributed it to last year's high inquiry traffic around the GST rate cut and festival calendar shifts, noting underlying conversion metrics remain stable.
- **Dhimant Shah asked about the potential use of the massive cash pile and buybacks.** Management stated cash is held for potential M&A or future shareholder distributions (buybacks/dividends), though ongoing tax benefits in the standalone entity currently restrict immediate execution under regulations.
- **Sanjay Natvarlal Shah asked about the Landmark partnership structure.** Management explained that CarTrade contributes tech, product, and customers, while Landmark handles physical operations, with working capital and margin-sharing models currently being structured.

### One-line takeaway
CarTrade Tech reported its highest-ever quarterly total income of Rs 236 crore and PBT of Rs 85 crore, backed by 36% EBITDA margins and strong momentum in its OLX and Remarketing segments, despite a high base effect impacting standalone revenue growth.
