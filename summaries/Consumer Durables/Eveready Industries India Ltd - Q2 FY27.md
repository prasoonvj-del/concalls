# Eveready Industries India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Delivered 8th consecutive quarter of YoY double-digit revenue growth, but faced margin pressures due to elevated raw material costs.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 428.2 crores (up 10.7% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 53.8 crores (up 8.9% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 12.6% (contracted by 20 bps YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 31.9 crores (compared to a loss of INR 7.9 crores YoY, which included an exceptional loss of INR 37.7 crores)
- Alkaline battery volume growth: 30% YoY
- Rechargeable flashlights growth: 33.2% YoY
- Emergency LED bulbs growth: 26% YoY
- E-commerce and quick commerce growth: >50% YoY
- Net debt: INR 153 crores as of September (excluding INR 90 crores received in October from land sale)

### Highlights
- Marked the 8th consecutive quarter of year-on-year revenue growth.
- Jammu manufacturing facility became operational, with commercial production starting in May and billing beginning in September.
- New product launches include a portable liquid mosquito vaporiser, SHOR Rechargeable Flashlight with animal alarm, Xtrabright emergency LED bulb, and electrical accessories.
- Successfully closed the deal for the second plot of Noida land on October 13, 2026, realizing INR 90 crores.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to be a debt-free company within the next 2 years, barring any major capex decisions.
- Management expects the Jammu facility to scale up gradually, improving manufacturing efficiency and cost competitiveness over time.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer consumption showed resilience despite uneven monsoons and persistent inflationary pressures. Subpar rainfall created uncertainties around rural demand.
- The dry cell market is shifting from zinc to alkaline due to rising consumer preference for high-performance batteries in high-drain devices.
- Rechargeable flashlights continue to gain traction, capturing about two-thirds of the total flashlights market.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material pressures remained elevated; zinc prices registered a 36% YoY increase per tonne on average, alongside inflation in chemicals, basic metals, and electrical equipment.
- EBITDA margin contracted by 20 bps YoY due to high material costs and product/channel mix.
- Management stated material cost pressures may not be fully recovered in Q3, but full recovery is expected by Q4 via calibrated price increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Jammu facility is operational and ramping up production to improve manufacturing localization and cost efficiencies. No major capex is currently on the cards.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at INR 153 crores as of September; an additional INR 90 crores was received in October 2026 from the Noida land sale and largely utilized for debt reduction.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated raw material costs (zinc, basic metals, chemicals).
- Ambiguity in EPR norms pricing mechanism and compliance, with active discussions ongoing with the Ministry.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Akshay Deshpande (PL Capital) on margins:** Asked about margin compression. Management attributed it to elevated material costs and channel/product mix, managed via calibrated price hikes.
- **Arnav Sakhuja (Ambit Capital) on net debt:** Asked about debt reduction timeline. Management stated they aim to be a debt-free company in the next 2 years barring major capex.
- **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit Asset Management) on new products and gross margins:** Asked about new product revenue contribution and gross margin recovery. Management stated half of H1 growth came from new/core products, and gross margin recovery is expected by Q4.
- **Vikash Shrivastav (RBC) on Jammu subsidy and land sale:** Asked about capital subsidy progress and Noida land sale proceeds. Management stated NCSS registration is awaited (potential INR 20-25 crore annual cap over 10 years once approved), and INR 90 crores from the second Noida plot was received in October.

### One-line takeaway
Eveready achieved its 8th consecutive quarter of YoY revenue growth driven by strong alkaline and rechargeable product traction, while absorbing raw material inflation through calibrated pricing.
