# Onida Electronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong brand business revenue growth and distribution expansion, offsetting input cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Total Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 198.6 crores (up 22% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Brand Business Revenue:** Rs. 150.4 crores (up 65% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Brand Business Margin:** 17.7% (up 470 bps YoY, excluding one-offs)
- **H1 FY27 Total Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 381.1 crores (up 25% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Brand Business Revenue:** Rs. 325.7 crores (up 48% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Brand Business Margin:** 17.8% (vs 15% YoY, excluding one-offs)
- **Q2 AC Business Growth:** 239% YoY
- **Q2 LED TV Business Growth:** 52% YoY
- **Q2 E-commerce Business Growth:** 169% YoY
- **Service Centers:** 425 operational

### Highlights
- Launched "Onida Rewired" strategic initiative focused on affordable premiumization, innovation, superior customer experience, and sustainable profitable growth.
- Expanded distribution by adding 57 distributors and 127 direct dealers in H1; secured an exclusive tie-up with a rural distribution partner.
- Launched 4 "Onida Smarthub" exclusive brand outlets under a franchise model.
- Introduced flagship 100-inch QD Mini LED television.
- Gained market share in ACs on e-commerce channels in September 2026.
- Completed partial VRS program at the Wada plant.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic about festive and wedding season demand in H2.
- Management aims to register growth higher than the overall market.
- Internal objective is to turn EBITDA positive in FY27.
- OEM business will be phased out over the next 8 to 12 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Premiumization remains a key structural driver with demand shifting towards larger TVs, higher-capacity washing machines, and energy-efficient ACs.
- Transition to new BEE norms in ACs is largely complete.
- Festive channel inventory build-up reflects confidence in the demand outlook.
- Long-term penetration for ACs and washing machines in India remains low.
- AC demand in North and East remains muted due to winter onset, while West and South show pickup.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs rose sharply in H1 for copper, aluminum, steel, plastics, and memory chips due to unresolved issues in West Asia.
- Competitive intensity limited the full pass-through of cost increases; unpassed impact estimated at around 200 to 400 bps in some categories.
- Margins protected via tactical sourcing, calibrated pricing, cost optimization, and improved product mix.
- Estimated EBITDA break-even level is around Rs. 250 to 260 crores in revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- AC and LED TVs remain largely on a contract manufacturing model; washing machines utilize a mix of outsourcing and in-house manufacturing at the Roorkee plant.
- Wada plant operations being reduced; land property and asset evaluated for future use.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or working capital numbers discussed outside of revenue/margins.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity and component price volatility driven by geopolitical tensions in West Asia.
- Competitive intensity restricting price hikes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditya Jhawar (Jhawar Investments) on profitability timeline:** Management stated internal projections aim for EBITDA positivity in FY27.
- **Varun Punjwani on manufacturing model:** Management clarified ACs and LEDs remain on contract manufacturing with joint product design control, while Roorkee handles washing machines.
- **Haider Kachwalla on raw material inflation:** Management noted price hikes are calibrated (leaving a 200-400 bps impact unpassed) and tactical sourcing is used to mitigate copper/commodity headwinds.
- **Kaushik on EBITDA break-even scale:** Management indicated break-even is expected around Rs. 250 to 260 crores in revenue.
- **Aditya Jhawar on OEM business wind-down:** Management confirmed OEM business will be phased out over the next 8 to 12 months to focus entirely on the brand business.

### One-line takeaway
Onida Electronics reported robust Q2 revenue and brand margin growth driven by its "Onida Rewired" strategy, while navigating input cost pressures with a target for EBITDA positivity in FY27.
