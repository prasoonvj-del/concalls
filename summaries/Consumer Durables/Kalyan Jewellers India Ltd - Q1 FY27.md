# Kalyan Jewellers India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-08 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust demand, strong revenue growth ex-bullion (38% YoY), and strategic initiatives like regional brand launch and non-GML debt reduction progressing well.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (ex-bullion):** INR 10,008 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 633 crore (vs INR 508 crore YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 349 crore (vs INR 264 crore YoY)
- **India Revenue (ex-bullion):** INR 8,503 crore (vs INR 6,142 crore YoY)
- **India EBITDA:** INR 500 crore (vs INR 434 crore YoY)
- **India PAT:** INR 321 crore (vs INR 256 crore YoY)
- **Middle East Revenue:** INR 1,320 crore (vs INR 1,026 crore YoY)
- **Middle East EBITDA:** INR 90 crore (vs INR 73 crore YoY)
- **Middle East PAT:** INR 25 crore (vs INR 22 crore YoY)
- **Candere Revenue:** INR 141 crore (vs INR 66 crore YoY)
- **Candere PAT / Loss:** Profit of INR 2.1 crore (vs loss of INR 10 crore YoY)
- **Recycled Gold Share:** Over 46% in Q1 FY27; in excess of 55% for June.
- **Non-core Real Estate Asset Sale:** INR 102 crore across 2 separate parcels of land.
- **Customs Duty Benefit (Q1):** Approximately INR 40 crore.
- **Expected Customs Duty Benefit (Q2):** In the range of INR 60 crore.

### Highlights
- Launched "Shine with India" gold recirculation campaign, increasing the share of recycled gold to over 46% in Q1 and targeting 55% to 60% going forward.
- Unveiled a new regional brand exclusively for Tamil Nadu named "Akshaya Thanga Maligai" (ATM), with the first showroom launching on August 21 in Chennai, followed by four more showrooms in coming months.
- Signed agreements to sell two parcels of non-core real estate assets for ~INR 102 crore; expected to close before the end of the current quarter.
- Non-GML debt repayment remains on track for completion by the end of September, post which second tranche of real estate collaterals will be released.
- Candere turned PAT positive in Q1, driven by higher throughput and expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains target of opening 84 Kalyan showrooms in India and 50 Candere stores for the full year.
- Management expects to maintain full-year PBT margins at least comparable to the previous year, even on a conservative basis.
- Non-GML debt expected to be fully repaid by end of September 2026.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remained robust during most of the quarter, except for a 1-month slowdown during Adhik-Maas in certain parts of the country.
- July demand on the ground continues to remain strong, similar to Q1 trends.
- Gold price volatility caused short-term pauses in consumer visits, but wedding-related demand remains resilient.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Old gold exchange is margin dilutive by around 0.2% to 0.3%.
- To offset exchange dilution, management is promoting "cash for gold," which is margin accretive as it is bought at a discount to spot prices.
- Employee costs saw an increase due to periodic better increments to motivate and retain talent (impacting Q1 by around 0.2% - 0.3%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expansion is primarily driven via the asset-light FOCO model.
- ATM brand expansion will also predominantly follow the FOCO model.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Non-GML debt repayment on track to reach zero debt by September 2026.
- Second tranche of real estate collaterals to be released post debt repayment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in gold prices can temporarily cause consumers to take a pause on discretionary purchases.
- Old gold exchange and employee cost increments have short-term dilutive impacts on margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ashish Kanodia (Citi Group) on PBT margins ex-one-offs:** Management clarified that customs duty gains, exchange dilution, and platinum/silver one-time items resulted in 5.1% PBT margins, and cash for gold plus tapered exchange promos will stabilize full-year PBT margins.
- **Vivek Gautam (Individual Investor) on debt-free timeline:** Management confirmed non-GML debt will be cleared by September.
- **Prolin Nandu (Edelweiss) on cash for gold:** Management explained that buying gold for cash is done at a discount to spot (unlike board-rate exchanges) and is scaling to double digits, helping offset exchange margin dilution.
- **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay Global) on ATM brand:** Management stated ATM is tailored specifically to Tamil Nadu preferences to compete directly with regional/local players, starting with 5 showrooms.
- **Ashish Kanodia on employee cost spike:** Management stated the ~54% standalone employee cost increase was due to periodic exceptional increments for talent retention rather than a one-time bonus.

### One-line takeaway
Kalyan Jewellers delivered a strong Q1 with 38% ex-bullion revenue growth, scaled its gold recirculation and cash-for-gold initiatives to protect margins, and is on track to become non-GML debt-free by September 2026 while expanding its regional footprint.
