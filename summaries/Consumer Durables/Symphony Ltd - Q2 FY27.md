# Symphony Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust consolidated top-line growth, EBITDA margin expansion driven by operating leverage and cost discipline, and strategic entry into new product categories.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: ₹275 crore (up 31% YoY from ₹210 crore).
- Consolidated EBITDA: ₹36 crore (up from ₹13 crore).
- Consolidated PAT: ₹24 crore (up from ₹17 crore; total PAT up from ₹5 crore to ₹17 crore before forex/treasury/exceptional items).
- Standalone revenue: ₹170 crore (vs ₹155 crore).
- Standalone EBITDA: ₹30 crore (vs ₹27 crore).
- Standalone PAT: ₹29 crore (business PAT up from ₹18 crore to ₹20 crore).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated revenue: ₹653 crore (up 17%).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA: ₹83 crore (vs ₹51 crore).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT: ₹64 crore (vs ₹59 crore).
- H1 FY27 Standalone revenue: ₹411 crore (vs ₹384 crore).
- H1 FY27 Standalone PAT: ₹57 crore (vs ₹65 crore).
- Beyond India Summer Products (BISP) consolidated top-line contribution: ₹625 crore (constituting 51% of top line); standalone BISP top-line (trailing 12 months): ₹192 crore (24%).
- Interim dividend declared: ₹2 per share (total payout ~₹13 crore); total interim dividend so far: ₹3 per share (~₹21 crore payout).
- ROCE (Core capital, TTM consolidated): 143% (vs 57%); Return on Net Worth: 20%.
- Treasury balance: ₹468 crore.
- IMPCO Mexico revenue: ₹40 crore (vs ₹17 crore).
- GSK China revenue: ₹23 crore.
- Australia and USA combined revenue: ₹46 crore (vs ₹35 crore).

### Highlights
- Consolidated overseas subsidiaries registered robust performance, accompanied by measured strategic India revenue growth.
- Despite input cost pressure reducing gross margins by ~4%, EBITDA margin expanded to 13% (vs 6.4%) due to operating leverage, cost discipline, and product mix.
- BISP includes LSV, geysers, tabletop fans, exports from India, and overseas subsidiary sales, reducing India summer seasonality; all individual product categories are EBITDA-positive with high single-digit consolidated margins and ~10% standalone margins.
- D2C channel is EBITDA and PAT positive, outperforming traditional channels in margins due to no margin leakage.
- Channel inventory normalization achieved successfully following deliberate inventory management in the June quarter; current inventory primed for summer 2027.
- Declared a second interim dividend of ₹2 per share.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects new product categories to be EBITDA accretive and profitability-led, adding to the top line and EBITDA over the long term.
- Management stated they do not target being among the top three players initially, adopting a measured approach.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand at the consumer level depends heavily on temperature; normal summer temperatures should drive good sales due to clean channel inventory.
- International growth faces headwinds from Middle East shipping/tariff uncertainties and freight costs, despite healthy underlying aspirations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated gross margins lower by ~4% due to input cost pressures.
- Standalone gross margin at 45.9% (down 2.3% YoY).
- New product categories (ACs, fans) expected to carry lower margins aligned with industry standards, while air coolers maintain historical margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Calibrated launch of three new product categories in the current quarter: room air conditioners, BLDC ceiling fans, and air purifiers.
- Asset-light, outsourced business model adopted for new categories: zero capex for ACs and BLDC fans; small capex possible down the line for air purifiers.
- No immediate dedicated headcount or heavy upfront OPEX/advertising planned for the current quarter; existing sales/marketing teams and variable-cost service franchises will be leveraged.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Treasury stands at ₹468 crore after repaying most loans and borrowings in Australia.
- Working capital discipline to be maintained for new product launches using channel finance and lean inventory.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unpredictable climate patterns (El Niño) and potential lack of sustained high temperatures.
- Middle East geopolitical situation and high freight costs acting as deterrents for international shipments.
- Competitive intensity and margin pressures in the room air conditioner (RAC) segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bhavya Gandhi (Bajaj Alternate Investment Management) asked about upfront OPEX/marketing spends for new product launches in Q3.** Management (Achal Bakeri) clarified that no specific OPEX, launch expenses, or ad spends are envisaged for the current quarter; brand investments will be measured post-summer.
2. **Haider Kachwalla (YES Securities) asked how a new entrant can scale the income statement in a competitive RAC market without top-tier scale.** Management (Achal Bakeri / Nrupesh Shah) explained the business uses a pure variable-cost outsourced model, leveraging existing trade channels and year-round consumer engagement to drive accretive growth rather than chasing market share leadership.
3. **Vinay Nadkarni (Investor) asked about pricing and working capital for residential ACs.** Management (Rajesh Mishra) stated pricing will be mid-premium based on superior specifications, avoiding the entry-level segment, and working capital will be minimized via channel financing.
4. **Rabindra Nath Nayak (Nirmal Bang Institutional Equities) asked about long-term manufacturing plans and outsourcing for Europe/global markets.** Management (Achal Bakeri) replied that while current ACs and global products are outsourced (largely from China), they are not averse to captive manufacturing in the future if scale dictates it.
5. **Adwait Javkar (EquiPoise Capital Management) asked about EBITDA margin sustainability with the launch of new products.** Management (Achal Bakeri) noted that air coolers will still form the bulk of sales and maintain historical margins, while new categories will carry industry-aligned margins, keeping blended profitability supported.

### One-line takeaway
Symphony reported robust Q2 consolidated revenue and EBITDA expansion driven by operating leverage and disciplined cost management, and announced an asset-light, margin-accretive entry into room ACs, BLDC fans, and air purifiers.
