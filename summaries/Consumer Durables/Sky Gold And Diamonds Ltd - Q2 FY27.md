# Sky Gold And Diamonds Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 61% YoY revenue growth, margin expansion, and positive operating cash flow.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,391 crore (up 61% YoY, 18.8% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 4,403 crore (up 68% YoY)
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 229 crore (up 87% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 9.6% (expanded 133 bps YoY, up 27 bps QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 189.8 crore at 7.9% margin (up 90% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 346 crore
- **Reported PAT:** Rs 129 crore (up 93% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 234 crore (up 112% YoY)
- **Operational PAT (Excluding subsidiary fraud exception):** Rs 135 crore at 5.6% margin (up 101% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 136 crore (vs outflow of Rs 100.9 crore in H1 last year)
- **Exports (Q2 FY27):** Rs 435 crore (Annualized run-rate of Rs 1,600+ crore; ~18% of business)
- **H1 FY27 Exports Contribution:** Rs 811 crore
- **Advance Gold Mix:** 16% of business in Q2 FY27
- **Studded Jewellery Mix:** ~2.6% of revenue in Q2 FY27
- **Net Working Capital Days:** 55 days (down from 59 days in March '26, 63 days in Sept '25, 71 days in March '25)
- **Inventory Days:** 39 days as of Sept '26 (vs 37 days in March '26)
- **Payable Days:** 17 days (Trade payables at Rs 437 crore as of Sept '26 vs Rs 92 crore in March '26)
- **Receivable Days:** 33 days as of Sept '26 (down from 39 days in Sept '25; vs 26 days in March '26)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 531 crore as of Sept end (down 20% from FY26 levels when factoring in land sale; vs Rs 550 crore in March '26)
- **H1 Interest Cost:** 0.89% of sales (vs 0.96% in H1 last year)
- **Exceptional Charge:** Rs 7.35 crore for subsidiary Star Mangalsutra fraud incident (Total involved: Rs 10.70 crore, recovered: Rs 3.35 crore, balance fully provided).

### Highlights
- Scaled to an annualized revenue run-rate of Rs 9,000+ crore and annualized PAT of Rs 500+ crore.
- International office in Dubai (started September 2025) serving wholesalers and corporates across the GCC region.
- Transitioned statutory auditors to MSKA & Associates LLP (BDO International) and engaged institutional internal auditors.
- Implemented modern ERP system, risk and control matrix, and standardized accounting policies across parent and subsidiaries.
- Promoters committed to taking salary only when there are operating cash flows.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated that under SEBI guidelines, forward-looking guidance is not provided in presentations or calls.
- Past margin percentage guidance of 5% to 5.25% PAT margins remains on track (per CFO).
- Any gross margin improvements will be redeployed back into the business.
- Management indicated they will revisit and update growth numbers/guidance upon the conclusion of the QIP fundraise.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural shift underway from unorganized to organized jewelry manufacturing.
- Major retailers are consolidating supplier bases and preferring large, well-capitalized, compliant manufacturers.
- Drastic shift in karatage observed: customers shifting from 22K to 18K, 14K, and 9K due to sharp gold price increases (gold jumped ~80% from base price over 1.5 years).
- Strong demand for lightweight jewelry, uncut diamond jewelry, and mangalsutras.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded due to higher advance gold mix (16%) and studded business mix.
- Operating costs rose to 1.6% of sales due to investments in senior talent (CEO, larger design team, audit, and finance functions).
- Back-to-back hedging policy followed; no speculative positions in gold, shielding profitability from gold price volatility.
- Diamond payables secured at 90 to 120 days credit (aggregating to ~Rs 225 crore). Bullion credit negotiated at around 2 days (~Rs 100 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Running 40 3D printing machines with paper casting, alongside direct casting, laser cutting, and hollow stamping.
- Design team expanded to ~180 CAD designers.
- Proposed land monetization: transaction at an advanced stage of due diligence with an external buyer; promoters will purchase the land within 20-30 days if no external commitment is finalized.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Advance gold model requires almost no working capital or capital investment as customers provide the gold.
- Export receivables have shorter cycles, reducing working capital intensity.
- Gold metal loan (GML) facility sanctioned up to ~Rs 170 crore, but currently not utilized as management finds it non-PAT accretive.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Fraud incident at subsidiary Star Mangalsutra involving Rs 10.70 crore (Rs 3.35 crore recovered, Rs 7.35 crore provided as an exceptional charge).
- Heightened volatility in gold prices.
- Upward pressure on US interest rates impacting global lending and potential GML borrowing rates.

### One-line takeaway
Sky Gold & Diamonds delivered robust Q2 FY27 performance with 61% YoY revenue growth, gross margin expansion to 9.6%, and positive operating cash flow, driven by scaling advance gold, exports, and institutionalized controls.
