# Kalyan Jewellers India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, robust festive demand, and successful execution of regional brand strategy.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Targeting 25% revenue growth for the year).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "robust" to "excellent" momentum in Q3 (Navratri/Dhanteras).
- **New topics/risks:** Non-GML debt is now fully repaid (was "on track" last quarter).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Non-GML debt repayment completed (delivered). Land sale progress: INR 87 crore sold, INR 16 crore remaining.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 10,323 crore.
- **Consolidated Adjusted Revenue:** INR 9,943 crore.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 644 crore (vs INR 497 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 593 crore.
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 427 crore (vs INR 261 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated Adjusted PAT:** INR 333 crore.
- **India Revenue:** INR 9,007 crore (vs INR 6,843 crore YoY).
- **India EBITDA:** INR 549 crore (vs INR 432 crore YoY).
- **India PAT:** INR 424 crore (vs INR 262 crore YoY).
- **Middle East Revenue:** INR 960 crore (vs INR 860 crore YoY).
- **Middle East EBITDA:** INR 71 crore (vs INR 61 crore YoY).
- **Middle East PAT:** INR 12 crore (vs INR 15 crore YoY).
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** INR 20,912 crore (38% YoY growth).
- **H1 Consolidated PAT:** INR 776 crore (48% YoY growth).

### Highlights
- Launched regional brand 'ATM' in T. Nagar, Chennai (August); management reports strong revenue traction.
- Non-GML debt is now fully repaid.
- Sold land parcel worth INR 87 crore; INR 16 crore remaining to be sold in the ongoing quarter.
- Management confirmed 50% of collateral is released; remaining 50% process initiated.
- *Record consistency:* Non-GML debt repayment target met as promised in Q1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management confident of beating targeted revenue growth.
- Targeting 25% revenue growth for the year.
- Plans to launch 4 more showrooms in the current financial year (2 in Q3, 2 in Q4).
- Management expects H2 PBT margins to be better than H1 and stable.
- No immediate plans to launch ATM brand in new states; focus remains on Tamil Nadu.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Festive season (Navratri/Dhanteras) demand described as "excellent."
- Volume growth observed in Q2 and Q3 (first 40 days).
- Management notes organic shift from unorganized to organized sector, driven by younger consumers seeking transparency and choice.
- Wedding-related demand remains resilient; consumers adjust timing slightly based on gold price volatility but do not cancel.
- Organic shift from plain gold to studded jewelry continues.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- PBT margins in Q2 were stable after adjusting for customs duty benefits and silver/platinum gains.
- Exchange program share is 42%; management is using "encash" program (margin accretive) to offset exchange dilution.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus of cash flow utilization: Strengthening existing showrooms (inventory) and flagship stores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is now non-GML debt-free.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High base effect for Q3 and Q4.
- Competitive intensity remains high (discounts/making charges).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Sachdeva (UBS) on growth trajectory vs high base:** Management stated they are confident of beating targets and maintaining the 25% revenue growth target.
- **Amit Sachdeva (UBS) on PBT margin stability:** Management expressed confidence that H2 PBT margins will be better than H1.
- **Nihal Mahesh Jham (Nuvama) on ATM metrics:** Management stated performance exceeded expectations but declined to share specific metrics due to competitive sensitivity.
- **Nihal Mahesh Jham (Nuvama) on cash utilization:** Management stated focus is on existing showrooms and flagship stores; no new state expansion for ATM currently.
- **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay) on market share:** Management confirmed market share gains are occurring, driven by the shift from unorganized to organized players.

### One-line takeaway
Kalyan Jewellers delivered strong Q2 growth, achieved non-GML debt-free status, and maintains a confident outlook for H2 despite a high base.
