# Stove Kraft Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the strongest ever first quarter performance since inception, driven by revenue growth, margin expansion, and profitability.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 480.6 crore, up 41.3% YoY (vs Rs 340.1 crore in Q1 FY26).
- Gross Profit: Rs 190.4 crore, up 46% YoY (vs Rs 130.4 crore in Q1 FY26).
- Gross Margin: Expanded by 127 bps YoY.
- EBITDA: Rs 53.8 crore, up 50.9% YoY (vs Rs 35.6 crore in Q1 FY26).
- EBITDA Margin: 11.2%, up 71 bps YoY (vs 10.5% in Q1 FY26).
- PAT: Rs 17.1 crore, up 63.5% YoY (vs Rs 10.4 crore in Q1 FY26).
- PAT Margin: 3.5%.
- ROCE: 13.9% (vs 12.6% in Q1 FY26).
- ROE: 9.3% (vs 8.3% in Q1 FY26).
- Net Working Capital: 45 days.
- Induction Cooktop (ICT) Revenue Growth: 315.9% YoY (contributing 27% of total revenues).
- Pressure Cooker Revenue Growth: 41.3% YoY.
- Non-stick Cooker Revenue Growth: 21.8% YoY (accounting for 21% of revenues).
- Small Appliances & Gas Cooktops: Contributed 27.5% of revenue mix.
- Channel Contribution: E-commerce 31.8%, General trade 28.6%, Exports 15.3%, Modern retail 13%, Retail 8.7%.
- Retail Channel Growth: 86.3% YoY.
- General Trade Growth: 56.2% YoY.

### Highlights
- Delivered strongest ever first quarter performance despite global economic uncertainty, geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions, commodity price volatility, and currency fluctuations.
- Induction cooktop growth reflects a structural shift in consumer adoption rather than a temporary spike.
- Added 17 new franchise stores during the quarter, advancing toward the target of 500 standalone Pigeon exclusive outlets by the end of calendar year 2027.
- Set up a wholly owned subsidiary / JV partner in China for Triply cookware and pressure cooker circles, expected to produce commercially by the end of December 2026.
- Strategic inventory buildup undertaken ahead of the festive season increased net working capital days to 45.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of strong performance vis-a-vis last year for Q2 and Q3, supported by festive demand and operational manufacturing facilities.
- Management guided for a year-on-year improvement of at least 1% in gross margins.
- Management expects export contribution to reach 15% in the next two years.
- Management expects to grow non-induction categories by nearly 20% and maintain a long-term CAGR of 15% to 20%.
- Management targets PAT margins to reach the 7% to 8% range in two to three years, and EBITDA margins in the range of 14% to 15% over the next two to three years.
- Management aims to stabilize retail store sales at Rs 5 lakh per store per month (currently trending at ~Rs 4.3 lakh).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 has traditionally been a seasonally softer quarter, but saw broad-based growth across key categories.
- Demand for induction cooktops remains substantially above pre-war levels.
- Premiumization is a key growth theme, with consumer preferences shifting toward higher value, feature-rich products.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded due to pricing discipline, operational excellence, and favorable product mix.
- Other expenses increased as a percentage of sales due to outsourced manufacturing on job work basis (up 1.2%), marketing and business promotion expenses (up 1.2% to ~3.5%-3.6%), higher franchise commission payouts (up ~1%, with franchise mix moving from 43% to 55%-56%), and advanced CSR spend of Rs 94 lakh (~3% of PBT).
- Finance cost is Rs 7 crore YTD, impacted by asset leases added in Q3 of last year, though fund-based working capital costs saved close to Rs 2 crore YoY.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Augmenting additional capacities for pressure cookers and small appliances.
- Machine installation underway at the China JV facility for Triply circles, targeting commercial production by the end of December 2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital increased to 45 days in Q1 FY27 due to strategic inventory buildup ahead of the festive season.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global economic uncertainty, geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions, commodity price volatility, and currency fluctuations.
- Small appliances category faced temporary supply-side moderation in Q1 as resources were reallocated to meet the sudden surge in induction cooktop demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Manoj Gori (Equirus Capital)** asked about growth sustainability and pricing power. Management stated they implemented price hikes for domestic and export markets to counter input cost pressures and are confident in current margin levels with headroom for improvement.
- **Vinod Krishna** asked about export contributions and IKEA supplies. Management stated export contribution should reach 15% over the next two years, and IKEA supplies are starting in Q2 (expected to contribute 5%-6% at full scale).
- **Anand Mundra** asked about the spike in other expenses and finance costs. Management attributed it to job work charges, marketing, franchise commissions, advanced CSR, and asset leases.
- **Resham Mehta** questioned sustainability of growth rates post-base normalization. Management responded that company-led innovations, premiumization, and category creation will drive 15%+ consistent growth.
- **Nikhat Koor** asked about South vs. non-South growth. Management stated non-South regions are growing faster on a smaller base, while South remains the largest base (~50% contribution).

### One-line takeaway
Stove Kraft delivered its strongest Q1 revenue growth of 41.3% YoY and margin expansion, driven by exceptional induction cooktop demand, broad-based category traction, and improving omnichannel distribution.
