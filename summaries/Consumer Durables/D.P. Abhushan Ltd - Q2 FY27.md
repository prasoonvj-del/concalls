# D.P. Abhushan Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue declined 21% YoY in Q2 due to a festive calendar shift, but management highlighted strong H1 growth and a robust start to Q3.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (targeting 3-4 new showrooms by March 2027).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong start" (Q1) to "seasonal shift" (Q2) with a "strong start to Q3."
- **New topics:** Announced a Rs 558 crore preferential fundraise for expansion and inventory.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on Dahod (opened Sept 2026) and Jabalpur (opened Oct 2026).

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 761 crore.
- **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 61 crore.
- **Q2 PAT:** Rs 40 crore.
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,615 crore (up 7% YoY).
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 155 crore (up 18% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 104 crore (up 19% YoY).
- **Festive Sales (11 Oct – 8 Nov):** Rs 815 crore (up 13.8% YoY).
- **Inventory:** Rs 1,250 crore (as of 30 Sept).

### Highlights
- Festive demand (Navratri/Diwali) shifted from Q2 to Q3; management views Q2 weakness as postponement, not structural loss.
- Opened Dahod (Sept 2026) and Jabalpur (Oct 2026 - first FOCO model).
- Announced fundraise of Rs 558 crore (preferential issue) to fund inventory and retail expansion.
- H1 EBITDA margin: 9.61% (vs 8.68% in H1 FY26).
- H1 PAT margin: 6.45% (vs 5.82% in H1 FY26).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1.
- Targeting 3-4 additional showrooms by March 2027 (Jodhpur, Mandsaur, and Sagar).
- Long-term focus remains on Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh, and Maharashtra.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Festive sales (11 Oct – 8 Nov) grew 13.8% YoY.
- Gold sales (festive period) grew 9.3% to Rs 724 crore; Diamond grew to Rs 38 crore (vs Rs 14 crore last year); Silver grew 29% to Rs 51 crore.
- Dhanteras sales: Rs 144 crore; Pushya Nakshatra: Rs 60 crore.
- Old gold exchange: 28-30% of business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inventory gain estimated at 18-20%; balance profit from making charges.
- Inventory mix: 90% gold, 5-6% studded, balance silver/gift.
- GML: 60-65% of banking limits available for GML.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Network: 14 showrooms (13 COCO, 1 FOCO).
- Jodhpur (COCO): ~5,200 sq. ft., expected Dec 2026.
- Mandsaur (COCO): ~3,500 sq. ft., expected end of FY27.
- Sagar (COCO): ~5,800 sq. ft., expected end of FY27.
- FOCO model: Franchisee funds inventory/capex; company shares revenue.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Fundraise: Rs 558 crore (Rs 513 crore for expansion/inventory).
- Gold Swarn Plus Scheme: Rs 50 crore contribution in last 5 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Seasonal shift caused Q2 revenue decline.
- Gold price volatility impacts fresh purchases (customers prefer exchange).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Isha Shah (Malhotra Family Office):** Asked about FOCO economics. Management: Franchisee funds inventory/capex; company shares revenue based on category.
- **Atishay Jain (Analyst):** Questioned preferential issue pricing vs market price. Management: Pricing follows SEBI formula based on 90-day average.
- **Divya Daga (Analyst):** Asked about inventory/debt. Management: 90% gold inventory; GML fungible within banking limits.
- **Sourabh Patil (Purnartha Investment):** Asked for store-wise data. Management: Will consider geographical info; store-wise difficult as network grows.
- **Vani Sharma (Analyst):** Asked about 21% YoY revenue decline vs peers. Management: H1 growth is 7%; festive shift is the primary driver.

### One-line takeaway
D.P. Abhushan reported a Q2 revenue dip due to festive calendar shifts but maintained strong H1 growth (7% YoY) and announced a Rs 558 crore fundraise to accelerate expansion.
