# Whirlpool of India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Record revenue and premiumization momentum offset by margin compression due to geopolitical headwinds, forex volatility, and regulatory cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 2,582 crore (up 11.4% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,727 crore (up 12.1% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Standalone):** 4.6%
- **PBT (Standalone):** Rs 118 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **PAT / Profitability:** Consolidated PBT grew 26% YoY to Rs 139 crore (EBITDA and PBT margins at 5.1%)
- **Distribution:** Triple-digit outlet expansion KPI achieved; same-store growth at 50%+

### Highlights
- Delivered record-breaking production at the Faridabad plant despite heatwave-led constraints in Noida.
- Launched the premium *Luxuriem* 654-liter refrigerator featuring a triple evaporator system and 6th Sense AI gas emission sensors.
- Introduced new *Bloomwash* washing machines (e.g., Juniper Green) and kitchen solutions (slim BLDC hoods, plug-and-play hobs).
- Protton NXT and Jade Marble frost-free refrigerators won Italian A' Design Awards.
- Working capital turned negative around September '25 and remains efficient (receivables collected, lean operations).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects July–September (JAS) to be challenging due to being the weakest quarter for air conditioners, high forex, and lingering geopolitical impacts.
- No formal numerical full-year revenue or margin guidance provided due to external macroeconomic unpredictability.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Urban and T2+ markets showing strong appetite for premiumization.
- Refrigerator and washer portfolios gained robust traction and filled vacant space in frost-free and top-load categories.
- Air conditioner sales face seasonal lows during JAS.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins squeezed YoY due to unprecedented geopolitical instability (Middle East war) spiking crude-derived raw material prices (polyol, MDI, EPS packaging).
- Commodity and import costs escalated as 30% of raw materials are imported and indexed to the US Dollar (USD sitting at 95-96, ~10% forex depreciation).
- Local labor inflation (Haryana minimum wage revisions) and diesel premiums for urgent component shipments added overheads.
- Regulatory compliance changes (e.g., e-waste) impacted financials.
- Productivity programs (P4G) and indirect procurement negotiations partially offset inflationary pressures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commercial production started at the Pune plant in July.
- Capex is maintained at a regular run-rate; factories operate at 70-75% capacity utilization, with sufficient room to scale up via extra shifts without immediate heavy capacity capex.
- Inorganic opportunities and automation are being evaluated to utilize strong cash reserves.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained negative working capital and strong cash reserves on the balance sheet.
- Parent company (Whirlpool Corporation) stake stands at 39.75% (operating at arm's length); no discussions on stake sales.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts causing severe volatility in crude-linked raw materials and forex rates.
- Unanticipated regulatory changes (e.g., e-waste norms) creating temporary cost headwinds.
- Risk of over-production and inventory dumping in seasonal categories like air conditioners.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Atul Mehra (Motilal Oswal AMC) on Parent Stake & Cash Allocation:** Asked about parent refinancing and cash deployment. Management clarified that the parent holds 39.75% on an arm's length basis, technology/licensing is secure, and cash will be utilized for internal automation, R&D acceleration, and evaluating inorganic opportunities.
2. **Manish Agarwal (Ikigai Asset Management) on Margin Recovery & Pricing:** Inquired if full-year revenue growth can hit 15-20% and margins return to 6-6.5%. Management stated predicting exact growth is difficult due to macro-shocks, and unilateral price hikes are avoided to protect customer volume and brand trust.
3. **Achal Lohade / Participant on Cost Splits & Forex Impact:** Asked about the breakup of margin pressures. Management detailed that forex depreciation (~10%), crude spikes (polyol, MDI), minimum wage hikes, and regulatory compliance costs were the primary contributors to under-recovery.
4. **Naved Chaudhary (Birla Sun Life AMC) on Elica Partnership:** Asked about acquiring the remaining stake in Elica. Management clarified there is no intent or agreement to buy the remaining stake, praising the promoter Pralhad Bhutada for running it successfully as a subsidiary.
5. **Manjeet Buaria (Saamya Advisors) on ESOP Structure:** Questioned the stringency of ESOP vesting conditions. Management defended the plan, stating it is benchmarked against peers, highly tied to performance ratings, and features stringent clawbacks and cliff vesting.

### One-line takeaway
Whirlpool of India delivered record Q1 revenue and strong premiumization growth, but margins faced severe headwinds from forex depreciation, crude-oil inflation, and regulatory compliance costs.
