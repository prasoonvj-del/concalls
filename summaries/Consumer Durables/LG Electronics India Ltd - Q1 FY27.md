# LG Electronics India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 15.5% YoY with margin expansion, supported by strong performance across all categories and export growth.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 7,233 crore (Rs 72.33 billion), up 15.5% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 904 crore (Rs 9.04 billion), up 26.2% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 12.5% (up 110 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 653 crore (Rs 6.53 billion), up 27.2% YoY.
*   **PAT Margin:** 8.9%.
*   **Cash & Bank Balance:** Rs 5,707 crore (Rs 57.07 billion).
*   **Working Capital:** Rs 1,256 crore (Rs 12.56 billion).
*   **Capex (Q1):** Rs 736 crore (Rs 5.88 billion deployed at Sri City).

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Performance:** Every key category delivered double-digit growth.
*   **Essential Series:** Sold >500,000 units between January and June; not margin dilutive.
*   **Exports:** Grew 30% YoY; now supplying to 65 countries.
*   **Home Entertainment:** Revenue grew 22.3% YoY; segment EBIT margin at 19%.
*   **Home Appliance & Air Solution:** Revenue grew 13.6% YoY; segment EBIT margin at 11.5%.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Management** expects healthy demand in Q2, with festive stocking already underway.
*   **Management** remains confident in a 20% CAGR for the B2B business.
*   **Sri City Plant:** Compressor production commenced in Q3 FY27; Room Air Conditioner (RAC) production scheduled for Q4 FY27.
*   **Localization:** Targeting 65% localization rate over the next 3-4 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Consumer Trends:** Shift toward premium, feature-rich, and energy-efficient products.
*   **TV Segment:** 55-inch+ screen segment grew ~53% YoY, now contributing ~50% of total TV business.
*   **B2B:** Strong momentum in government and corporate orders for Information Display; 36% market share achieved.
*   **Data Centers:** Mid-scale cooling is a live revenue stream; hyper-scale is at a preliminary stage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** Improved product mix (premiumization), calibrated price increases, and operating leverage.
*   **Import Policy:** Government's May 8th quantitative restriction on compressor imports is viewed as a positive, as it leverages LG's local manufacturing capabilities.
*   **Cost Mitigation:** Localization of components (e.g., resin) and strategic/multiple sourcing used to manage input cost inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Sri City:** Total investment outlay of Rs 5,000 crore (phased).
*   **Capacity:** 7 million units for refrigerators (Greater Noida); 1 million units for ACs (Greater Noida), with an additional 2 million capacity planned at Sri City.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Funding:** Strong cash reserves allow funding of Sri City investment via internal accruals; no external debt planned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Currency depreciation pressure.
*   Elevated raw material and component costs.
*   Geopolitical uncertainties (e.g., West Asia conflict).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Latika Chopra (JPM) asked about TV growth sustainability:** Management stated growth is structural (shift to premium/large screens) rather than seasonal.
*   **Bhavani K asked about import policy impact:** Management noted it is a positive, providing a competitive advantage due to LG's existing local manufacturing.
*   **Achal Lohade asked about B2B outlook:** Management reiterated confidence in a 20% CAGR trajectory.
*   **Vishal Goyal asked about dividend policy:** Management stated dividends will be reviewed after the Sri City investment phase, prioritizing capital expenditure currently.

### One-line takeaway
Strong Q1 performance driven by premiumization, export scaling, and operational efficiency, with a robust balance sheet supporting major capacity expansion at Sri City.
