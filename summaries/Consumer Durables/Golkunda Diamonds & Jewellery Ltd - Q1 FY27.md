# Golkunda Diamonds & Jewellery Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth, supported by margin expansion and new domestic facility expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR85.21 crores (vs INR69.44 crores in Q1 FY26), up ~23% YoY (Managing Director stated INR85.24 crores).
- **EBITDA:** INR8.64 crores, up ~69.30% YoY.
- **EBITDA margin:** 10.14% (vs 7.35% in Q1 FY26).
- **Net profit (PAT):** INR5.14 crores, up ~63.9% YoY.
- **Net profit margin:** 6.03% (vs 4.2% in Q1 FY26).
- **FY26 Exports:** Approximately INR272 crores.
- **Two-year revenue trend (FY24 to FY26):** Increased from INR230 crores to INR281 crores.
- **Two-year EBITDA trend:** Increased from INR16.9 crores to INR22.84 crores (35% increase).
- **New manufacturing facility size:** 5,360 square feet.
- **New facility capacity:** 125 kg to 150 kg of jewellery per annum (~50% to 60% overall capacity increase).
- **New facility peak revenue potential:** INR250 crores to INR300 crores.
- **New facility expected revenue for current year:** INR15 crores to INR20 crores.
- **Preferential allotment capital raising:** INR27 crores.
- **Top 10 customers concentration:** ~60% to 65% of revenue.
- **Export share from Middle East:** 85% to 90%.
- **Export share from USA:** Currently below 10% (historically 20% to 30%).
- **Lab-grown diamond share:** Less than 5% to 7% of current revenue.

### Highlights
- Company conducted its first-ever earnings conference call since being listed in 1992.
- Established a new manufacturing facility in Andheri, Mumbai (5,360 sq. ft.) to enter the Indian domestic B2B and B2C markets.
- Raised capital through preferential allotment of convertible warrants (INR27 crores) to support domestic expansion and working capital.
- Developed 500+ commercial-level designs every month ready for production.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a growth of ~15% to 20% for the current financial year, predominantly driven by exports.
- Management targets domestic business to account for ~50% of total revenue by 2030 (implying overall revenue around INR550 crores to INR600 crores based on current export base).
- Management expects total revenue to more than double in a five-year timeline.
- Management plans to launch B2C operations around the pre-Diwali / current calendar year period.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Evolving consumer preferences among younger buyers driving interest in lab-grown diamonds due to affordability and acceptance.
- Retailers shifting from 18-karat to 14-karat, 9-karat, and 10-karat gold jewellery due to rising gold prices.
- US market performance has improved after stabilizing from tariff issues faced last year.
- Middle East remains a robust and consistent core market (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins improved due to focus on operational efficiency and product mix.
- Domestic B2B/B2C margins may initially be tighter due to marketing push, but expected to align with export margins over time; B2C expected to be better in terms of margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New Andheri facility is a three-storey building; two floors are up and running, and the third is planned as a retail lounge for B2C operations.
- Incremental revenue potential from the new facility is estimated at INR250 crores to INR300 crores at peak utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gold is naturally hedged as it is imported and exported at predominantly the same price.
- Diamonds are hedged using forward contracts for receivables spanning four to six months.
- Inventory levels run at ~10 to 11 times for exports; domestic inventory expected to be similar or better.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations and flight/logistics disruptions (e.g., temporary suspension of commercial flights affecting shipping) pose occasional export risks.
- Export business faces higher credit and inventory cycle competition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhaval Pandya (Areeza India) asked about the domestic expansion strategy and competitors in lab-grown diamonds.** Management replied they are targeting major retail operators in India (some existing Middle East clients) and a B2C lab-grown segment. They stated there are no specific dominant competitors yet as the segment is nascent in India.
2. **Ramesh (Millennium Capital) asked about the revenue potential of the new facility and contribution from top customers.** Management clarified the peak potential is INR250 crores to INR300 crores, and top 10 customers contribute 60% to 65% of revenue.
3. **Ramesh asked about the impact of the Hormuz/logistics issues.** Management noted that outside of a 15-20 day disruption between late February and March where flights were stopped, business operations and revenue remained normal.
4. **Prateek Shetty (A.Square) asked about the timeline and expected revenue for the new Mumbai facility for the current year.** Management stated the facility is up and running making sample lines, expected to generate INR15 crores to INR20 crores in FY27, reaching peak capacity in three years.
5. **Prateek Shetty asked about peers and B2C margin profile.** Management noted they focus on studded jewellery compared to peers mostly in plain gold, and affirmed B2C margins should be better once established.

### One-line takeaway
Golkunda Diamonds & Jewellery reported strong Q1 FY27 growth (revenue up 23% YoY, PAT up ~64% YoY) and initiated its domestic expansion strategy with a new Mumbai facility and upcoming B2C lab-grown diamond rollout, targeting 50% domestic revenue contribution by 2030.
