# Sheela Foam Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management highlighted strong revenue growth and strategic expansion (Furlenco) but noted significant margin pressure due to raw material inflation and geopolitical disruptions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,045 crore (up 19% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 87 crore (flat YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 46 crore (up ~5x YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 2,077 crore (up 22% YoY).
*   **H1 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 200 crore (up 23% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 109 crore (up >6x YoY).

### Highlights
*   Furlenco became a subsidiary effective October 1, 2026.
*   Company crossed Rs 1,000 crore revenue in Q2 for the first time.
*   Management launched the "Great Sleepwell Festival" to build a demand pipeline.
*   International business: Spain (Interplasp) revenue grew 44% in H1; Australia (Joyce) revenue grew 16% in H1.
*   Staqo (IT subsidiary) maintains ~28% EBITDA margins.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects FY27 consolidated revenue of ~Rs 5,000 crore and EBITDA of ~Rs 600 crore (post-Furlenco consolidation).
*   Management maintains a target of 15% CAGR growth.
*   Management expects to normalize EBITDA margins to 12-15% if raw material prices stabilize.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand shifted from Q2 to Q3 due to the festive season moving from September to October.
*   Management noted high price elasticity; consumers are trading down to lower categories due to price hikes.
*   Management is introducing new technology-based products to target the sub-Rs 10,000 price point.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Raw materials (Polyol up 65%, TDI up 33% since mid-February) impacted margins.
*   Operating EBITDA margin for Q2 was 8.4%.
*   Management spent an additional Rs 15-16 crore on advertising in Q2.
*   One-off provision of Rs 7 crore taken in Australia due to a customer (A.H. Beard) bankruptcy.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Furlenco has >300 products, >30 cities, and >2 lakh subscribers.
*   Furlenco utilizes an asset-based model with >90% utilization.
*   Sheela Foam will use Furlenco for furniture design, manufacturing, and logistics support to expand its own furniture retail.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Consolidated net debt is ~Rs 600 crore.
*   Expected net debt by end of FY27 is Rs 800 crore (including Furlenco's Rs 250 crore debt).
*   Management expects Rs 150-200 crore annual debt reduction from Sheela Foam's free cash flow.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical instability (Middle East) causing supply chain/logistics disruptions and raw material inflation.
*   Potential for margin dilution if raw material prices remain elevated.
*   A.H. Beard (Australia) bankruptcy resulted in a haircut.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why consolidate Furlenco fully?** A: Management cited absolute control and the ability to consolidate line-by-line.
*   **Q: Why is EBITDA margin lower than target?** A: Management explained it is a trade-off between volume and profitability amidst temporary raw material spikes.
*   **Q: What is the Furlenco contribution to the Rs 600 crore EBITDA target?** A: Rs 200 crore from Furlenco, Rs 400-450 crore from the core business.
*   **Q: Any tax benefits from Furlenco losses?** A: No, they are consolidated, not merged; Furlenco retains its own tax benefits.

### One-line takeaway
Sheela Foam is navigating severe raw material inflation by prioritizing market share and brand investment, while the consolidation of Furlenco marks a strategic pivot toward a broader furniture-and-comfort ecosystem.
