# KDDL Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume-driven revenue and EBITDA growth across key manufacturing segments, led by Eigen and watch components.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 174.7 crore (up 37.7% YoY)
- **Standalone Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 329 crore (up 36.2% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 39.4 crore (up 36.3% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 22.6%
- **Standalone EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 75.2 crore (up 38.4% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** 22.9%
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 21.8 crore; PAT margin at 12.5%
- **Standalone PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 41.3 crore; PAT margin at 12.5%
- **Consolidated Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 698.7 crore (up 31.6% YoY)
- **Consolidated Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,345.8 crore (up 33.5% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 112.1 crore (up 29.8% YoY); Margin at 16.0%
- **Consolidated EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 220.5 crore (up 32.3% YoY); Margin at 16.4%
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 48.2 crore; Margin at 6.9%
- **Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 92.9 crore; Margin at 6.9%
- **Watch Components (Dials & Hands) Growth (H1 FY27):** ~30% YoY
- **Eigen Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** Close to 50% YoY
- **Ornapac Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** Over 30% YoY
- **Capex Guidance (FY27):** Rs 70 crore to Rs 75 crore (revised up from Rs 50 crore due to preponement)

### Highlights
- Watch components (dials and hands) saw ~30% YoY revenue growth in H1, driven by domestic and export volumes, prompting management to raise full-year growth expectations above the original 12%-14% target.
- Capacity expansion for watch components has been preponed from FY28 to the current year.
- Eigen (precision stamping/engineering) delivered ~50% revenue growth in H1, driven by robust demand from export customers (US and Europe) and new customer additions.
- Ornapac (packaging) achieved positive PBT in Q2 FY27, with the positive profitability trend expected to continue.
- *Record Check:* Unlike past calls where capacity additions followed strict multi-year schedules, Eigen and watch components capex is being preponed due to unexpected demand surges.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains Eigen's medium-term growth guidance at 20%-25% CAGR, though upside potential exists if current momentum continues.
- Full-year watch component revenue growth is expected to exceed the initial 12%-14% estimate.
- Ornapac expects the positive profitability trend to continue through remaining and future quarters.
- Estima aims for breakeven next year, supported by new capabilities.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Eigen demand is robust across EV, battery energy storage systems (BESS), electronics, aerospace, and defense from both US and European markets.
- US RFQs for Eigen slowed slightly post-tariff issues, but this was compensated by European RFQs.
- Export demand for watch bracelets was subdued due to challenges in the Swiss watch industry, but new customer additions across other geographies are expected to restore growth soon.
- Favre Leuba is operational in over 35 countries with more than 45 doors outside Ethos; management targets a Rs 1,000 crore brand potential.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated and standalone EBITDA margins remained stable (Standalone H1 at 22.9%, Consolidated H1 at 16.4%).
- Gross margins declined slightly YoY and QoQ entirely due to product mix changes (faster growth in Eigen, which has a slightly lower gross margin than watch components, and faster growth in domestic watch component sales compared to exports).
- Growth across Eigen and watch components was majorly volume-driven, with minimal currency impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total FY27 capex guidance increased to Rs 70-75 crore (up from Rs 50 crore) to account for preponed expansion.
- Eigen Unit 1 space is being expanded; a rack plating line was installed at Unit 2A; an agreement for an adjacent plot for Unit 2B has been finalized to become operational in mid-FY28.
- Watch component capacity expansion has been initiated ahead of schedule.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt has seen a marginal increase due to major capex funding and higher working capital requirements driven by business scale-up.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subdued demand environment in the Swiss watch export segment impacting the bracelet business.
- Macroeconomic volatility and ongoing conflicts impacting key luxury markets (US, UAE, China) affecting Swiss watch offtake and Estima.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Digant Haria (SIA):** Asked if Eigen's 50% growth was due to backlog. Management clarified it reflects active funnel projects fructifying sooner than expected and strong customer responsiveness, prompting faster capacity expansion.
- **Nitish Rege (ChrysCapital):** Inquired about Eigen capex quantification. Management stated total FY27 capex is Rs 70-75 crore, an increase of Rs 15-20 crore over the original Rs 50 crore plan.
- **Kunal Mehta (InCred Equities):** Asked about gross margin contraction. Management explained it was due to product mix shifts toward Eigen and higher domestic watch component sales.
- **Eshwar Arumugam (ithoughtPMS):** Questioned Favre Leuba breakeven and sustainability given past failures. Management stated Favre Leuba will achieve breakeven this year, performing better than expected with global presence.
- **Lalaram Singh (Sabre Edges):** Asked about business structure simplification/restructuring. Management responded that restructuring adds costs and current focus is on operational efficiency and growth, though the idea is evaluated periodically.

### One-In-All Takeaway
KDDL delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 33.5% consolidated revenue growth and 32.3% EBITDA growth, propelled by exceptional performance in the Eigen precision engineering division (~50% growth) and watch components (~30% growth), leading to preponed capex and raised full-year expectations.
