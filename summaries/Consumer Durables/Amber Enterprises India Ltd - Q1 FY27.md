# Amber Enterprises India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue and EBITDA grew YoY, with strong performance in the Electronics division, despite temporary margin headwinds from commodity inflation and operational disruptions.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 3,888 crore (+13% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 337 crore (+28% YoY).
*   **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 126 crore (+19% YoY).
*   **Electronics Division Revenue:** Rs 985 crore (+29% YoY).
*   **Electronics Division EBITDA:** Rs 107 crore (+117% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Mobile Foray:** Entered a manufacturing collaboration with Oppo Mobiles India (covering Oppo, OnePlus, and Realme). Trial production expected in Q4 FY27; commercial production in Q1 FY28. Target: 8 million units in Year 1, ramping to 15-16 million in Year 2.
*   **PCB Expansion:** Groundbreaking completed for HDI PCB facility at Jewar (Uttar Pradesh). Construction ongoing for multilayer PCB facility at Hosur (Tamil Nadu).
*   **Sidwal (Railway/Defense):** New Faridabad facility for HVAC, pantry, doors, and gangways is now operational.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Electronics Division:** Management maintained the 40%+ revenue growth guidance for FY27.
*   **Railway Sub-systems & Defense:** Management expects 30%-35% revenue growth for FY27, with margins in the 15%-16% range.
*   **Consumer Durables:** Management expects growth in line with the RAC industry (13%-15%).
*   **Margins:** Management expects pressure from commodity prices, currency depreciation, and wage hikes to persist through H1 FY27, normalizing thereafter.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Consumer Durables:** Revenue growth moderated due to a high base effect from the previous year.
*   **Electronics:** EMS segment growth was stunted by customer inventory levels and a shift to job work; smart meter and smart watch segments degrew.
*   **PCB:** Industry faces high import dependence; management noted strategic inventory positioning is underway.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Consumer Durables:** EBITDA grew 12% despite cost headwinds.
*   **Electronics:** Bare PCB business experienced margin compression due to a steep rise in copper clad laminate (CCL) costs.
*   **Railway/Defense:** EBITDA declined 26% due to product mix, commodity inflation (copper), currency depreciation, and a 35% minimum wage hike in Haryana.
*   **Pass-through:** Management noted a 1-quarter lag for Tier 1 (Consumer Durables) and a 2-quarter lag for Tier 2 (PCB) price pass-throughs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Approved Capex:** Rs 3,200 crore (Jewar HDI PCB), Rs 1,000 crore (Hosur PCB), and Rs 500 crore (Shogini, Pune).
*   **Mega AC Plant (UP):** Currently in planning mode; construction expected in FY28, with trial production in FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 1,225 crore as of June 30, 2026 (vs Rs 510 crore as of March 31, 2026).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **IL JIN Electronics Fire:** Production was impacted, but management stated they are adequately insured and expect to meet guidance; reconstruction permission has been received.
*   **Regulatory/Market:** New BEE tonnage labeling requirements for ACs have caused temporary market inventory confusion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Mobile/PLI:** Management stated they are in discussions with OEMs regarding exports; PLI eligibility for mobile backward integration is pending draft guidelines.
*   **PCB Pricing:** Management confirmed they are passing on costs but noted a 2-quarter lag for the Tier 2 PCB business.
*   **Electronics Growth:** Management reiterated 40% growth guidance, driven by PCBA, PCB, and Industrial/Automation segments.
*   **Minority Interest:** Management explained the negative minority interest (Rs 19 crore) was due to exceptional losses (Rs 123 crore) in the Electronics division allocated to minority shareholders.
*   **IL JIN Fire Impact:** Management noted minimal business impact due to multi-geographic locations and confirmed they are adequately insured.

### One-line takeaway
Amber is aggressively diversifying into mobile manufacturing and high-value electronics (PCB/Industrial) while managing short-term margin pressures from commodity inflation and operational disruptions.
