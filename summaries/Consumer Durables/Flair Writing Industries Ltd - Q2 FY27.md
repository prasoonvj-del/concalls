# Flair Writing Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Earnings impacted by raw material inflation, geopolitical supply disruptions, and temporary weather-related factory shutdowns.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** INR 340 crore (up 6.5% QoQ)
- **Domestic sales (Q2 FY27):** INR 292 crore (up 6% QoQ from INR 276 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Export revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR 49 crore (up 13% QoQ)
- **Domestic sales (H1 FY27):** INR 568 crore (up 11% YoY from INR 513 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** INR 659.4 crore (up 8.2% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 50.6 crore
- **EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 14.9% (down from 16.7% in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 31.7 crore (up 9.1% QoQ)
- **PAT margin (Q2 FY27):** 9.3% (up 20 bps QoQ)
- **Net working capital days (H1 FY27):** 145 days (increased from 139 days in FY26)
- **Inventory days:** 104 days (increased from 94 days)
- **Debtor days:** 84 days (increased from 78 days)
- **Estimated revenue loss from floods:** INR 25 crore
- **Interim dividend:** 10% (INR 0.50 per share)

### Highlights
- Manufacturing facilities in Valsad and Daman faced temporary disruptions due to heavy rainfall and floods, remaining non-operational for an average of ~15 days and ~7 days respectively.
- Appointed PricewaterhouseCoopers as statutory auditor.
- Management stated they are looking for potential inorganic acquisitions in the Creative segment.
- Core writing instruments segment grew by 7% during H1 FY27; market share stands at over 18%.
- Creative Products segment grew by 24% YoY during H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised FY27 annual revenue growth guidance to 12% or higher (down from previous guidance of 15%).
- Management maintained medium-term revenue growth guidance of approximately 15% YoY from FY28 onwards.
- Management expects to maintain EBITDA margins around 15% assuming crude/raw material remains at current levels, with potential to return to 16%-17% if global environment improves.
- Portfolio composition target aims for 60% pen and 40% non-pen categories over the long term; expects to end FY27 at ~68% pens and ~32% other categories.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic stationery and arts material industry supported by increasing preference for branded products, continuous product innovation, and gradual expansion of organized distribution.
- Broader Indian and global writing instrument markets expected to grow at modest low single-digit rates; Flair continues to outperform the industry.
- Export demand is slightly subdued due to West Asia geopolitical uncertainties, though seeing encouraging traction in South America, Africa, and other Asian countries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross profit and EBITDA margins impacted by elevated raw material costs driven by West Asia geopolitical situations, supply-side disruptions, and price volatility.
- Mitigating margin pressure through cost rationalization, operational efficiencies, targeted promotional schemes, and selective price increases (reducing discounts/schemes and value engineering).
- Inventory days increased due to advanced stocking undertaken during the initial part of the quarter to take advantage of lower raw material prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding manufacturing facilities for Creative products in Surat and the upcoming Valsad facility.
- Commercial operations for the 4th steel bottles manufacturing line expected to commence in Q4 FY27.
- Overall capacity utilization for Steel Bottles and Creative categories is around 75%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital days increased from 139 days in FY26 to 145 days in H1 FY27.
- Inventory days rose from 94 to 104 days due to advanced RM stocking.
- Debtor days rose from 78 to 84 days due to slower collections in the export market.
- Aiming to control/reduce working capital days by 15-20 days over the next 5-6 quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Weather disruptions (floods in Valsad and Daman) causing workforce shortages and production loss.
- Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia impacting international trade, logistics, freight costs, and export collections.
- Elevated and volatile raw material prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jinesh Joshi (PL Capital) asked about inventory quantification and price hike quantum:** Management explained that raw material mix changes constantly with production stages, and price increases are managed via reducing discounts/schemes rather than blanket price hikes.
- **Resham Mehta (Green Edge Wealth) asked about the rationale for inorganic acquisitions in Creative when organic growth is strong:** Management stated that an inorganic acquisition with a strong manufacturing base would help achieve quick growth expansion and portfolio scaling.
- **Moksh Morbia (Prospero Tree) asked why operating margins dropped more than GP margins despite sequential revenue growth:** Management clarified that unallocated fixed costs and overhead accumulation during the 15-day flood shutdown weighed on operating margins.
- **Pratik Kothari (Unique PMS) asked if export/receivable delays are persisting:** Management responded that export receivable days increased due to international market disruptions, but domestic receivables remain under control.
- **Shraddha Kapadia (SMIFS Limited) asked for volume/ASP split in Pens and capacity utilization:** Management stated volume and value contributed almost equally (volume slightly higher) and promised to provide exact ASP figures separately; stated capacity utilization for Steel Bottles and Creative is around 75%.

### One-line takeaway
Flair Writing Industries reported a muted Q2 FY27 with a 6.5% QoQ revenue growth to INR 340 crore and EBITDA margin contracting to 14.9%, impacted by ~INR 25 crore in flood-related revenue losses in Gujarat and rising raw material inflation, prompting a guidance cut to 12% for FY27.
