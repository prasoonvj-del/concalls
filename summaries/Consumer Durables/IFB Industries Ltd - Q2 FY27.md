# IFB Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 14.04% YoY, but PBT and PAT declined due to elevated material costs and adverse forex impact.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,512 crore (up 14.04% YoY from Rs 1,327 crore); YTD revenue at Rs 3,042 crore (up 15.34% YoY).
- **PBDIT:** Rs 90.92 crore (6.01% of revenue), down 11.3% YoY from Rs 102.50 crore (7.73% of revenue). YTD PBDIT at Rs 179.38 crore (5.9% of revenue).
- **PBT:** Rs 53.86 crore (3.56% of revenue) vs Rs 68.32 crore (5.16% of revenue) YoY; YTD PBT at Rs 105.40 crore (3.3% of revenue).
- **PAT:** Rs 39.56 crore (2.62% of revenue) vs Rs 49.75 crore (3.75% of revenue) YoY; YTD PAT at Rs 77.62 crore (2.5% of revenue).
- **Engineering Division Growth:** Met top-line and bottom-line budgets; growth driven by fine blanking and stampings.
- **Engineering Business Mix:** Two-wheelers ~50%, four-wheelers ~40%, electrical and three-wheelers ~10%.
- **Steel Division Capacity:** Increased furnace annealing capacity from 1,500-1,700 tonnes to 2,800 tonnes per month; customer mix is ~60/40 (internal/external).
- **Sales Promotion & CSR Expense:** Total sales promotion including CSR down to Rs 75 crore (4.96% of revenue) in Q2 vs Rs 81 crore (6.13% of revenue) YoY. YTD total down to Rs 139 crore (4.58% of revenue) vs Rs 156 crore (5.92% of revenue) YoY.
- **Associate Share of Loss:** IFB Refrigeration Limited loss impact of Rs 6.23 crores in H1 vs Rs 3.23 crores YoY.
- **Capex:** H1 standalone capex at Rs 28.58 crore (Q1: Rs 12.43 crore, Q2: Rs 16.15 crore). Gujarat EV project capex of Rs 103 crore planned in phases.

### Highlights
- Material cost increases and adverse forex impact negatively impacted gross margins and PBDIT. Bill of Materials (BOM) cost increased by about 4.3% as a percentage of net revenue.
- Staff costs decreased due to not filling ~100 positions and replacement hiring adjustments, despite annual increments.
- Other expenses increased due to higher office expenditures and lease/ROU asset additions (Gujarat plant lease rental impact).
- Engineering division set up an engineering technology excellence center in Switzerland to drive cost-efficiency and value engineering under OE margin pressures.
- Acquired land in Gujarat for a 100% EV product plant, with manufacturing expected to commence by September/October 2027. Setting up a new chain plant to substitute imports.
- Steel division expanded annealing capacity to 2,800 tonnes per month.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains an aspirational full-year appliances revenue growth target of close to 20%, noting that Q2 growth of 10.5% (H1 at 14.5%) was affected by a shift in the Diwali festival timing into Q3.
- Engineering division expects to continue its growth pattern going forward barring erratic market conditions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Front-loader washing machines category is seeing migration toward higher capacities (8 kg and above, and a emerging 12 kg segment).
- Industry-wide price hikes taken to offset commodity and forex inflation have been cautious and insufficient to fully protect gross margins due to fear of pricing out customers during peak festive periods.
- Geographically, South remains a large market for the industry, followed by West, North, and East. Focus is on weighted distribution and maximizing key retail counters.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- PBDIT decline driven by ~4.3% increase in BOM cost due to commodity price hikes and adverse forex.
- Price hikes taken by the company and the broader industry were insufficient to fully pass on the commodity increases.
- Staff costs reduced via strict headcount management (leaving ~100 positions vacant).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gujarat EV plant capex of Rs 103 crore planned across 2026-2027 and 2027-2028.
- Setting up a new chain plant domestically to replace restricted imports.
- Steel division completed annealing capacity expansion to 2,800 tonnes/month; currently stabilizing operations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company historically cash positive; policy for major projects like Gujarat generally follows a 1:1 debt-equity framework, though final funding mix (internal accruals vs debt) is evaluated dynamically.
- Capital work-in-progress (CWIP) and lease liabilities (ROU asset additions including Gujarat plant) increased lease rental cash outflows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated material/commodity prices and adverse forex impact.
- Severe margin and cost-reduction pressures from Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) in the engineering business.
- Loss-making status of associate company (IFB Refrigeration Limited) due to high oil-based material costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked about the timeline and rationale for demerging the Engineering division.** Management (Arup Das/Soumitra Goswami) responded that there are no demerger plans currently as focus remains on executing pipeline projects.
- **Madhur Rathi questioned why EBITDA margins are far from the 10% target.** Management (Sandeep Abraham) explained that a 4%+ increase in BOM cost and insufficient industry-wide price hikes prevented reaching the target despite cost-innovation initiatives.
- **Lakshminarayan K.G. (Tunga Investments) asked about the end-application mix of the Engineering division.** Management (Arup Das) clarified it is ~50% two-wheelers, ~40% four-wheelers, and ~10% electrical/three-wheelers.
- **Vivek Kumar (Geometric Securities) questioned the 20% appliances growth guidance given H1 performance.** Management (Sandeep Abraham) explained that Q2 growth of 10.5% was muted due to the shift of Diwali to Q3, keeping the 20% target aspirational and achievable with a strong Q3.
- **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company) asked about the associate company (IFB Refrigeration) losses.** Management (Soumitra Goswami) clarified that losses increased to Rs 6.23 crore for H1 due to an 8-10% increase in oil-based material costs.

### One-line takeaway
IFB Industries reported a mixed Q2 FY27 with 14% revenue growth offset by a decline in profitability due to persistent commodity inflation and adverse forex impacts, while its Engineering division continued robust performance and expansion.
