# Shree Refrigerations Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, improved EBITDA margins, and a robust order pipeline backed by indigenous defence capabilities.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** ~Rs 100 crore (up ~84% / 85% YoY for H1 FY27).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 13.5% in H1 FY27 (up from 11% in H1 FY26); guided at ~22% for full-year FY27.
- **CAGR Growth:** Consistent historical 45% CAGR; guided at 40% revenue CAGR going forward.
- **Order Book:** Rs 198.60 crore as of September (started the year at Rs 270 crore).
- **Receivable Days:** ~252 days on a trailing basis (improved from 350 days in FY25, targeted to reduce to 200 days).
- **Inventories:** Increased from Rs 54 crore to Rs 80 crore due to strategic sourcing.
- **Manufacturing Capacity:** ~70,000 square feet operational across Unit 1 and Unit 2.

### Highlights
- Won the prestigious SIDM champion award (import substitution category for SMEs) for indigenizing the submarine air conditioning system, presented by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh.
- Appointed as the authorized service partner (TASP) for Danfoss Turbocor compressors for the Indian market and nearby countries.
- Promoters' holding remains unchanged post-IPO with no share sales.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain a 40% revenue CAGR going forward.
- Full-year FY27 EBITDA margin guided at around 22%.
- All guidance figures exclude potential data centre business contributions (treated as an upside).
- Expects operating cash flow to turn positive by the end of the financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Total ship building market size is estimated between Rs 4,000 crore to Rs 5,000 crore over a period of two years (defence + non-defence/retrofit).
- Over 200 ships approved/getting built by 2032; financial approval of Rs 2.75 lakh crore received for ~230-250 ships by 2035.
- Data centre market opportunity is massive, though currently facing high-CapEx hesitancy; traction expected in 6 to 8 months to a year.
- Incorporated a wholly-owned subsidiary named 'Aquilon' to manufacture and service industrial sub-zero temperature chillers, with commercial operations starting next financial year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity inflation (copper and metals) put pressure on margins, mitigated by strategic sourcing and higher inventory holding.
- Depreciation method changed to straight-line method (SLM) this year to better reflect asset use alongside heavy CapEx.
- Maharashtra government D-class subsidy for facility creation will provide additional cash inflow not yet factored into projections.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current manufacturing capacity is sufficient to support the 40% CAGR guidance.
- Additional land parcel adjacent to Unit 2 is already leveled and ready for shed construction if required.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term borrowings and CC limit utilization increased operationally.
- Trade receivables increased to Rs 128 crore (representing ~252 days).
- Operating cash flow was negative at Rs 42 crore in H1 FY27 due to strategic inventory building and higher receivables.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity inflation and geopolitical pressure on metals/copper impacting margins.
- High working capital intensity and elevated receivable days inherent to the shipbuilding sector.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Priyanshu Jain (on revenue mix and margins):** Management stated that HVAC is slightly higher than AC plants, while Ref plants constitute a smaller percentage. Margin improvement will be driven by import substitution and operational efficiencies.
2. **Siddharth S (on high receivable days vs. peers like MDL):** Management explained that shipbuilding milestone-based payment cycles and ramped-up shipyard outflows dictate receivables, but the company’s days are among the best in the sector and targeted to drop to 200 days.
3. **Nishitha Shanklesha (on H1 vs. H2 margin skewness):** Management clarified that delivery schedules tied to ship build plans are heavily skewed toward the latter half of the financial year, alongside higher fixed overhead absorption in H2.
4. **Krishna Doshi (on upcoming naval orders like NGC and P75I):** Management confirmed they are on the starting line for the newly CCS-cleared Next Generation Corvettes (NGC) and expect firm orders to convert over FY27 and next year, while P75I is awaiting CCS sanction.
5. **Aditya Raval (on negative operating cash flow):** Management attributed the negative operating cash flow of Rs 42 crore in H1 to strategic inventory sourcing for H2 deliveries and increased receivables, assuring positive cash flow by year-end.
6. **Aditya Raval (on pending criminal cases):** Management categorically stated there are no pending criminal cases against the company, promoters, or directors.

### One-line takeaway
Shree Refrigerations delivered robust H1 FY27 top-line growth with margin expansion, backed by strong naval indigenization leadership, and reaffirmed its 40% CAGR guidance.
