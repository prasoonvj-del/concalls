# Singer India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Sustained strong growth momentum with robust revenue and profitability increases across sewing machines and appliances.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management did not specify numerical guidance numbers, maintaining an ambitious internal outlook.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more confidently towards non-government sales momentum, domestic Make-in-India scaling, and strong e-commerce appliance growth despite lingering commodity inflation.
- New topics or risks: Credit rating enhancement (BBB/Stable long term, A3+ short term) and liberalized e-commerce credit terms; air purifiers explicitly ruled out due to unpredictable sales. Old risks around severe labor shortages and air cooler headwinds were largely absent or minimized.
- Last quarter's promises: Bhiwadi factory pilot production remains on track to commence next month (subject to approvals); Kendriya Bhandar government order is now mostly completed with a very small residual tail left.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs. 185.2 crore, up 34% YoY
- PBT: Rs. 9.4 crore, up 79% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs. 10.9 crore, up 85% YoY
- Sewing Machine category revenue growth: 34% YoY
- Trade channel growth (Sewing Machine): >30% YoY
- Zig-Zag Machines growth: >25% YoY
- Fans business growth: ~240% YoY (on a small base)
- Appliances gross margin value improvement: >55% YoY
- Appliances segment result: Lower marginally by Rs. 9 lakh compared to LY Q2

### Highlights
- Zig-zag sewing machines now represent approximately one-fourth of total revenue run rate; industrial sewing machines constitute a little more than 10%.
- Introduced a new range of Make-in-India Zig-Zag machines complying with new BIS standards; management aims to scale Make-in-India share to 50% this month and close to 100% over the next two quarters.
- Setting up a new factory in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan, is progressing well; pilot production is planned to commence next month, subject to government and BIS approvals.
- Credit rating enhanced to BBB/Stable for long-term and A3+ for short-term.
- Government orders are mostly finished, leaving only a small residual tail end.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is ambitious internally about reaching the Rs. 1,000 crore top-line mark in the next 3 years, dependent on market response.
- Expects growth momentum in non-government sales to continue.
- Short-term working capital increases and fund reductions are expected to convert back into cash within the next two to three quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Household sewing machine category has low penetration globally; blackhead machines are becoming obsolete, driving conversion to modern zig-zag machines.
- Government policies like BIS, QCO, and Make-in-India mandates are expected to drive the next 3 to 5 years of growth.
- Sewing machines are evolving from utility items into crafting and hobbying categories with sewing-cum-embroidery features.
- Air purifiers evaluated and rejected due to unpredictable annual sales and high inventory buildups.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Appliances gross margins sustained despite commodity price inflation via strategic price positioning, improving GM value by >55% in Q2.
- Appliances profitability impacted by EPR costs and organizational investments for market expansion.
- Trade receivables increased due to higher business revenue and liberalized e-commerce credit terms for quick gains during Big Billion days.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bhiwadi factory setup progressing well; pilot production starting next month, focusing initially on zig-zag machines and later expanding to industrial sewing machines and consumer appliances.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables and trade advances (suppliers advance at Rs. 7 crore) increased in line with business growth and e-commerce channel expansion.
- Credit rating upgraded to BBB/Stable (long term) and A3+ (short term).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued commodity price inflation impacting appliances.
- EPR cost impacts on appliances segment profitability.
- Liberalized e-commerce credit terms temporarily increasing receivables.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harshit asked about the remaining government order status and revenue split.** Management replied that government orders are more or less over with a very small residual tail left, and clarified that zig-zag machines are ~25% of revenue while industrial machines are >10%.
2. **Zaki Nasser asked about medium-term top-line aspirations (Rs. 1,000 crore mark in 3 years) and brand positioning.** Management stated they have internal ambitions and strong fundamentals, though they did not officially guide on the specific top-line target.
3. **Laksh asked about the increase in trade receivables and inventory.** Management explained receivables rose due to higher business volume and temporary, one-time liberal credit terms for e-commerce growth during festive sales.
4. **Laksh asked whether Make-in-India sewing machines contributed to September quarter sales and future targets.** Management stated Make-in-India sales started gradually in previous quarters, will reach ~50% this month, and close to 100% in the next two quarters.
5. **Jairam Stock Brokers (via chat) asked about evaluating air purifiers.** Management stated they have no plans for air purifiers due to unpredictable sales and high inventory buildup.
6. **Laksh asked about the status of inorganic growth and equity dilution.** Management stated fund reductions are short-term, working capital will convert to cash in 2-3 quarters, and they are confident of raising funds if needed for expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Singer India delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 34% revenue growth and an 85% jump in EBITDA, driven by robust non-government sewing machine demand and scaling domestic manufacturing.
