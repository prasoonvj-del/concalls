# Campus Activewear Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered double-digit revenue and PAT growth despite inflationary headwinds, with management expressing confidence in H2 margin recovery.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained full-year FY27 EBITDA margin target at 17% (previously guided 17-19% band).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "stable" margins in Q1 to "pressure" in Q2, though management remains confident in a meaningful H2 recovery.
- **New topics:** Detailed explanation of the Rs 125 crore GST input credit accumulation; specific focus on Performance footwear (Badminton/Cricket/Running).
- **Last quarter's promises:** The franchise model transition (158 stores) is now 100% complete.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 452 crore (+16.9% YoY).
- **Volume:** 6.3 million pairs (+10.2% YoY).
- **ASP growth:** 6% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 62 crore (+12.5% YoY); Margin 13.5% (-50 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 24 crore (+21.3% YoY); Margin 5.3% (+20 bps YoY).

### Highlights
- Women and Kids categories outperformed, growing 27% and 34% respectively in H1 FY27.
- Premium segment (>Rs 2,000) volume grew >50% YoY, increasing volume contribution from ~9% to 13%.
- Franchise model transition (SOR) is now 100% complete.
- *Record Check:* The company successfully completed the franchise model transition mentioned in Q1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects meaningful profitability recovery in H2 FY27 via pricing, product mix, and operating leverage.
- Maintained full-year FY27 EBITDA margin guidance of 17%.
- Targeting mid-teen growth for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumption environment described as "relatively subdued."
- Trade distribution grew 14%, D2C online 22%, and D2C offline 13%.
- Performance footwear (Badminton launched, Cricket/Running upcoming) identified as a key future growth driver.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin at 51.4% (vs 53.9% in Q1 FY27).
- Material margins under pressure due to inflation; management noted a lag between input cost increases and pricing realization.
- Management stated that if crude oil prices remain low, further price hikes may not be necessary.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Moving to larger store formats (800-900 sq. ft. vs 600-700 sq. ft. previously).
- New retail identity launched in Roop Nagar, Delhi.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- "Balance from government authorities" (GST input credit) stands at Rs 125 crore; management expects to realize this over 12-18 months.
- Net working capital days increased due to planned inventory build-up for Q3.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inflationary pressure on raw materials.
- Accumulated GST input credit (Rs 125 crore) impacting cash flow, though management expects this to decline post-March.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay):** Asked about Kids/Performance scale. Management: Nascent, "blue ocean" opportunity; Performance is a big bet.
- **Videesha Sheth (Ambit):** Asked about channel growth/ASP. Management: ASP jump of Rs 40 (Rs 12-13 from premium, rest from mix/MRP).
- **Umang Mehta (Kotak):** Asked about promoter stake sale rumors. Management: "No plan from the promoter family in the foreseeable future."
- **Bhavesh Jain (DV Investment):** Asked about Rs 125 crore GST credit. Management: Accumulated due to service-side input credits; will unlock over 12-18 months.

### One-line takeaway
Campus delivered resilient double-digit growth in a subdued environment, with management focusing on premiumization and new category expansion to drive H2 profitability.
