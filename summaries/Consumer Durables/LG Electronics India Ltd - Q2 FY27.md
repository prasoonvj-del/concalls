# LG Electronics India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Highest quarterly growth since listing (19.5% YoY) with margin expansion across segments, broad-based volume-value growth, export scaling, and new Vehicle Solutions business announced.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained – full-year FY27 mid-teens revenue growth and early double-digit EBITDA margin unchanged.
- **Tone and demand view:** More confident; Q2 marked "highest growth quarter since listing" with momentum continuing into H2. RAC defied seasonality (Q2 > Q1), and mass segments (32" TV, single-door refrigerator) joined premium in double-digit growth.
- **New topics/risks:** Vehicle Solutions entry (automotive infotainment/telematics) announced for Sri City; Chest Freezers and built-in kitchen solutions launch; Mini LED TV introduced; data center cooling flagged as watched opportunity; geopolitical risk explicitly noted for exports.
- **Old topics disappeared:** Import restriction on compressors (Q1 positive) not discussed; dividend policy question not raised.
- **Promises delivered:** Sri City on track (compressor Q3 FY27, AC Q4 FY27 as guided); Essential Series scaling (now in 21 export countries, profitable); exports expanded 65→68 countries; B2B 20%+ CAGR ambition reiterated; localization advancing with imminent compressor production.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 7,380 crore (INR 73.8 billion), up 19.5% YoY; H1 FY27 Rs 14,613 crore, up 17.5% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 701 crore (INR 7.01 billion), up 28% YoY; margin 9.5% (+63 bps YoY); H1 EBITDA Rs 1,616 crore, margin 11.1%.
- **PAT:** Rs 489 crore (INR 4.89 billion), up 25.7% YoY; H1 PAT Rs 1,142 crore, up 26.5% YoY.
- **Home Appliance & Air Solution:** Revenue Rs 4,772 crore (+20.9% YoY); Segment EBIT Rs 416 crore (+28.4% YoY), margin 8.7% (vs 8.2%).
- **Home Entertainment:** Revenue Rs 2,608 crore (+17.2% YoY); Segment EBIT Rs 337 crore (+19.9% YoY), margin 12.9% (vs 12.6%).
- **Cash & Bank:** Rs 5,051 crore (Sept 2026) vs Rs 5,707 crore (June 2026); working capital buildup Rs 656 crore for festive stocking.
- **Capex (Q2):** Rs 870 crore (vs Rs 230 crore YoY); Sri City committed investment Rs 5,000 crore over next few years.
- **Exports:** 68 countries (from 50), 151 partners (from 112); Essential Series in 21 countries.
- **B2B:** ~10-11% of revenue; target 15%+ in 3 years; 20%+ CAGR ambition intact.

### Highlights
- All key categories (washing machines, refrigerators, RAC, TV, dishwashers) delivered double-digit growth; dishwashers "breakout quarter".
- Two-track strategy succeeding: Essential range now profit contributor, bringing first-time buyers; premium multi-door refrigerators (>50% growth) and French Door/Chest Freezers launched.
- TV structural shift: 55-inch+ >50% of TV revenue; offline value market share all-time high; Mini LED widens premium funnel.
- RAC off-season defied: Q2 sales > Q1 due to elevated temperatures; Q4 constitutes ~55% of annual RAC sales.
- Washing machines: fully automatic >60% of sales; highest growth in 8 quarters driven by semi-to-fully automatic upgrade.
- B2B Information Display: strongest segment (commercial TV, corporate/retail/hospitality/government); system AC in large projects growing.
- Vehicle Solutions entry: global telematics leader, local manufacturing at Sri City, phased rollout starting with telematics.
- Great Place To Work Award – 3rd consecutive year.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Management** maintains full-year FY27 guidance: mid-teens revenue growth, early double-digit EBITDA margin; endeavors to outperform.
- **Management** expects festive/wedding season (Q3) to drive strong sell-through; channel inventory lean and healthy.
- **Management** targets B2B revenue share 15%+ in 3 years (from 10-11%); 20%+ CAGR ambition intact.
- **Management** expects significant export YoY growth in FY27; two-track (Premium + Essential) geographic diversification mitigates geopolitical risk.
- **Management** confirms Sri City compressor commercial production Q3 FY27, AC production Q4 FY27 (Jan 2027); 2 million compressor capacity addition.
- **Management** sees data center cooling as long-term opportunity (chiller capability exists); early stage.
- **Management** plans new segments: Chest Freezers, built-in kitchen solutions; exploring other white spaces.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Management** indicates demand momentum broad-based across categories and price points (mass + premium).
- **Management** notes TV upgrade cycle structural: consumers moving to larger screens across income levels.
- **Management** highlights refrigerator mass recovery: single-door double-digit growth alongside premium multi-door >50% growth.
- **Management** sees RAC demand sustained by temperature trends; new AI ACs with in-house compressor from Sri City to support Q4.
- **Management** reports B2B government projects (smart city, infrastructure) becoming increasingly important.
- **Management** states export strategy rests on premiumization (global markets) and Essential Series (high-growth economies).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Management** attributes 63 bps EBITDA margin expansion to operating leverage (volume), richer premium mix, targeted price increases (category-specific, mass protected), and Essential range profitability.
- **Management** absorbed copper, aluminum, plastic cost rises via efficiency, localization, supplier negotiation before selective hikes.
- **Management** took two rounds of selective price increases this year; no major demand slowdown observed; premiumization supports willingness to pay.
- **Management** expects local compressor production at Sri City to permanently reduce costs (supply chain risk, forex headwinds) as volumes ramp.
- **Management** notes B2B, exports, services (AMC) carry above-average margins; growing share structurally improves profitability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Q2 capex:** Rs 870 crore (tangible assets) vs Rs 230 crore YoY.
- **Sri City plant:** Rs 5,000 crore committed; will double existing capacity; dedicated hub for domestic and export.
- **Timelines:** Compressor production Q3 FY27; AC production Q4 FY27 (Jan 2027); both lines fully operational by end FY27.
- **Vehicle Solutions:** to be manufactured at Sri City.
- **New products:** Chest Freezers, built-in kitchen solutions launched; exploring new segments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash & bank:** Rs 5,051 crore (Sept 2026) vs Rs 5,707 crore (June 2026).
- **Working capital:** Rs 656 crore buildup in Q2 for proactive festive/wedding season channel stocking.
- **Funding:** Sri City investment via internal accruals; no external debt mentioned (consistent with Q1).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated raw material costs (steel, aluminum, copper, resin) and forex fluctuation headwinds.
- Geopolitical uncertainties affecting export markets (mitigated by geographic diversification).
- Potential demand moderation from future price increases (none seen yet).
- Vehicle Solutions execution risk (new B2B vertical).
- Data center cooling opportunity at early stage.
