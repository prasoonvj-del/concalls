# LG Electronics India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest growth quarter since listing with strong double-digit growth in revenue and profitability across all categories.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (mid-teens revenue growth and early double-digit EBITDA margin).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more confident, with Q2 delivering highest growth since listing and RAC performing well even in off-season.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Announced entry into the Vehicle Solutions business in India.
- Last quarter's promises: Sri City compressor production remains on track for Q3 FY27, and RAC production for Q4 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 7,380 crore (Rs 73.8 billion), up 19.5% YoY
- **EBITDA:** Rs 701 crore (Rs 7.01 billion), up 28% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.5%, up 63 bps YoY
- **PAT:** Rs 489 crore (Rs 4.89 billion), up 25.7% YoY
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 14,613 crore (Rs 146.13 billion), up 17.5% YoY
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 1,616 crore (Rs 16.16 billion), up 27.9% YoY
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 1,142 crore (Rs 11.42 billion), up 26.5% YoY
- **Home Appliance & Air Solution Revenue:** Rs 4,772 crore (Rs 47.72 billion), up 20.9% YoY
- **Home Entertainment Revenue:** Rs 2,608 crore (Rs 26.08 billion), up 17.2% YoY
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** Rs 5,051 crore (Rs 50.51 billion) as of September 2026
- **Capex (Q2 Tangible Assets):** Rs 870 crore (Rs 8.7 billion)

### Highlights
- Marked one year since listing.
- Entered the Vehicle Solutions business in India (automotive infotainment and telematics), manufacturing locally at Sri City.
- Expanded exports from 50 to 68 countries, with Essential Series now in 21 countries.
- Dishwasher segment delivered a breakout quarter.
- Received the Great Place To Work Award for the third consecutive year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained full year FY27 guidance of mid-teens revenue growth and early double-digit EBITDA margin, but endeavors to perform better than stated guidance.
- Sri City plant commercial production for compressors begins in Q3 FY27, and air conditioner manufacturing follows in Q4 FY27 (around January 2027).
- B2B business long-term growth ambition remains at 20% plus, with revenue share targeted at 15% plus over the next three years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer demand trends show first-time home appliance buyers entering the market alongside rising premium product demand.
- Fully automatic washing machines crossed 60% of overall washing machine sales.
- Premium multi-door refrigerators (side-by-side and French Door) growing over 50%.
- RAC Q2 sales surpassed Q1 due to elevated temperatures; Q4 represents the biggest RAC sales period (roughly 55% of yearly sales).
- TV demand shifting structurally to larger screens (55-inch and above contributing over half of TV revenue), with offline value market share at an all-time high.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Navigated elevated commodity prices (copper, aluminum, plastic, resin), forex fluctuations, and go-to-market investments.
- Implemented targeted, category-by-category price increases rather than across-the-board hikes, protecting the mass segment.
- Strong operating leverage from volume growth and a richer premium mix offset cost pressures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested Rs 8.7 billion in tangible assets during Q2, driven by work on the Sri City, Andhra Pradesh facility.
- Sri City will double existing production capacities and serve as a dedicated hub for domestic and export markets, backed by a Rs 5,000 crore (Rs 50 billion) committed investment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stood at Rs 5,051 crore as of September 2026, down from Rs 5,707 crore at the start of the quarter due to a working capital buildup of Rs 656 crore for festive and wedding season channel stocking.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated memory chip prices, currency pressures, and geopolitical uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Natasha Jain (Phillip Capital) asked about category demand and RAC seasonality:** Management stated momentum continues across all categories, with Q2 RAC sales exceeding Q1 due to elevated temperatures.
- **Umang Mehta asked about EBITDA margin drivers and sustainability:** Management (CFO Dongmyung Seo) highlighted scale, localization, premiumization, and high-margin B2B/export/AMC revenue mixes as structural margin drivers.
- **Achal Lohade asked about price increases and demand risk:** Management (Gurpinderjeet Singh) stated two selective rounds of price hikes were taken without impacting demand, supported by the premiumization trend.
- **Deepak Agarwal asked about B2B segment performance and data centers:** Management (Aditya Bhasin) stated B2B is currently 10-11% of revenue (targeting 15%+ in 3 years), and data center cooling is being watched closely as an interesting future opportunity.

### One-line takeaway
Strong Q2 financial performance with record growth, ongoing premiumization, and strategic expansion into the Vehicle Solutions business supported by the Sri City facility ramp-up.
