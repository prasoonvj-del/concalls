## Consumer Durables: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

### Big picture
The sector delivered **broad-based top-line growth** in Q2 FY27 — jewellery players led with 20-70% YoY revenue gains, appliances saw 15-100% growth, and footwear posted 10-35% — but **margin trajectories diverged sharply**. Jewellery margins expanded on studded/lightweight mix and operating leverage (BlueStone, PNGS Reva, Kalyan, Titan), while appliances (Voltas, Whirlpool, IFB, EPACK) and footwear (Relaxo, Metro, Campus) absorbed **18-20% commodity/forex inflation** with lagged price hikes, compressing EBITDA 100-310 bps. **Festive calendar shifts** (Navratri/Diwali moving to Q3) flattered Q3 outlook but made Q2 comparisons noisy. **Organised retail share gains** remain the dominant structural theme across jewellery, footwear and appliances.

### Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian consumer durables historically relied on **asset-light distribution (dealers/distributors), import-dependent BOMs, and B2B/wholesale-led jewellery manufacturing**. Over the last ~5 years, the model has shifted toward **owned retail (COCO/FOCO), backward integration (compressors, motors, glass, movements), D2C/omnichannel, and organised B2C jewellery branding** [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **Jewellery: B2B → B2C retail & lightweight/studded mix** – **mainstream** – Kalyan (ATM brand, 4 stores FY27), P N Gadgil (23 stores FY27, 50:50 COCO:FOCO), Senco (24 stores YTD, target 18-20), PNGS Reva (15 EBOs planned, 6 open), RBZ (4 stores FY27, Surat doing ₹50L/day), Golkunda (NIBHANA LGD brand, 4 stores, 7 more next quarter), Titan (sub-₹1L/₹50k offerings, store revamps), BlueStone (27 stores Q2, 1M customers) [Kalyan concall Q2 FY27; P N Gadgil concall Q2 FY27; Senco concall Q2 FY27; PNGS Reva concall Q2 FY27; RBZ concall Q2 FY27; Golkunda concall Q2 FY27; Titan concall Q2 FY27; BlueStone concall Q2 FY27].
- **Appliances: Import substitution & backward integration** – **scaling** – Voltas (Atomberg JV for compressors, 75-80% in-house target), EPACK (Hisense washing machines pilot Nov, commercial Dec; heat exchangers, copper tubing, PCBs captive), LG (Sri City compressor Q3, RAC Q4, ₹5,000 cr committed), IFB (Gujarat EV plant ₹103 cr, chain plant for import substitution), Stove Kraft (China JV/WOS CNY20M for Triply circles), Symphony (asset-light AC/fan/purifier launch, outsourced) [Voltas concall Q2 FY27; EPACK concall Q2 FY27; LG concall Q2 FY27; IFB concall Q2 FY27; Stove Kraft concall Q2 FY27; Symphony concall Q2 FY27].
- **Footwear: Premiumisation, sneakerisation & D2C** – **scaling** – Campus (premium >₹2k volume +50% YoY, 13% mix, women/kids +27%/+34%), Relaxo (Sparx separate sales team, 51 reimagined stores, Kenya JV 50k pairs/day), Metro (Clarks 400+ MBOs, FILA local manufacturing), Bata (global sneaker lines H2, franchisee double-digit growth) [Campus concall Q2 FY27; Relaxo concall Q2 FY27; Metro concall Q2 FY27; Bata concall Q2 FY27].
- **Omnichannel/D2C as primary discovery** – **mainstream** – BlueStone (online-first, marketing 5.7% revenue, target 4.5%), Wakefit (70% own channels, 205 stores, 2,589 MBOs), Timex (e-com 75% YoY, ₹190 cr, 1P/3P/quick commerce), Vaibhav Global (Shopify migration done, digital target 50% B2C by Q4) [BlueStone concall Q2 FY27; Wakefit concall Q2 FY27; Timex concall Q2 FY27; Vaibhav Global concall Q2 FY27].
- **Gold recycling/scrap as margin lever** – **early** – Augmont (scrap 3%→24.5% mix, double margins, WC cycle 1.7→4 days), Shringar (exchange-driven lightweight demand), D.P. Abhushan (28-30% old gold exchange), Senco (>51% exchange, 20-40 bps melting loss) [Augmont concall Q2 FY27; Shringar concall Q2 FY27; D.P. Abhushan concall Q2 FY27; Senco concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **Customs duty cut on gold (Jul 2024)** – reduced duty to 6%, boosting organised jewellery demand; Titan notes domestic gold at discount to international, pressuring realisation [Govt: Finance Ministry, Jul 2024].
- **BIS/QCO mandatory standards** – driving import substitution in appliances (IFB, EPACK, Voltas) and footwear (Relaxo cites 90% unorganised exemption as headwind) [Govt: DPIIT, 2023-24].
- **PLI for white goods (AC/LED)** – EPACK, Voltas, LG, Whirlpool beneficiaries; EPACK notes PLI final year, discussing extension [Govt: DPIIT, 2021].
- **GST Council: rate rationalisation (pending Nov 2026)** – may move items from 5% to 3%/8% slabs, impacting consumer goods pricing [Govt: GST Council, Nov 2026].
- **Futures trading restrictions on food commodities (pending Nov 2026)** – indirect impact on agri-linked raw materials [Govt: Finance Ministry, Nov 2026].

### Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: **Festive/wedding season demand robust** – Kalyan ("excellent" Navratri/Dhanteras), Titan (footfalls recovered post gold dip), Senco (Dhanteras 25-26% growth, ₹2,400 cr turnover), D.P. Abhushan (festive 11 Oct-8 Nov +13.8% YoY), PNGS Reva (Navratri +35%, Dussehra +62%), RBZ (Surat warm response), Shanti Gold (strong festive/wedding). **Organised share gains** – Kalyan, Titan, P N Gadgil, BlueStone, Senco all cite shift from unorganised. **Lightweight/studded jewellery** – Titan (18K/14K/9K adoption), P N Gadgil (stud ratio 17.4% North/Central), Senco (stud ratio 9.5-11.6%), PNGS Reva (natural diamond shift), Golkunda (LGD retail). **Premiumisation in appliances/footwear** – LG (premium multi-door fridge +50%, fully auto WM 60%+), Voltas (RAC volume +32%, share 17.4%), Campus (premium >₹2k +50%), Relaxo (sports/sneakers double-digit).

**Divergence**: **Mass consumption stress** – Relaxo (Hawaii slippers volume dip, price sensitivity), Campus ("relatively subdued" consumption), Metro (Tier-2/3 selective, rentals pressure), Vaibhav Global (UK weak, US midterm uncertainty), Whirlpool (entry-level intense rebating). **Festive timing** – LG, Eureka Forbes, Wakefit, Stanley, RBZ all note Q2→Q3 shift; Voltas calls Q2 "traditionally lean". **Export vs domestic** – Golkunda pauses international expansion (geopolitics), Renaissance (US/EU RFQs mixed), KDDL Eigen (US slowed, EU compensated), Timex (global movement shortage), Vaibhav Global (Europe ex-UK +18%).

Footwear: Campus (13.5% -50 bps), Relaxo (12.2%), Metro (27.1% consolidated +90 bps), Bata (gross +8 bps reported, underlying +70-80 bps).
