# Consumer Durables: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Consumer Durables and Retail sector reveals a bifurcated narrative. Companies with exposure to jewelry, lifestyle, and organized retail largely reported robust top-line growth, driven by formalization tailwinds, advance gold models, and aggressive store expansions (e.g., *P N Gadgil Jewellers*, *Sky Gold And Diamonds*, *Timex Group*). Conversely, manufacturers of traditional appliances, stationery, and home comfort (such as *DOMS*, *EPACK Durable*, *IFB Industries*, *Sheela Foam*, and *Vaibhav Global*) faced severe margin pressures. These headwinds stemmed from persistent raw material inflation (polyol, TDI, copper, plastics, and precious metals), adverse forex movements, and temporary transition costs, such as platform migrations or strategic acquisitions.

---

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus Views:** 
    *   **Festive Shifts:** Across sectors—from footwear (*Bata India*, *Metro Brands*) to home comforts (*Sheela Foam*, *Symphony*) and jewelry (*BlueStone*, *P N Gadgil*)—managements highlighted that the late timing of the festive calendar (Durga Puja, Navaratri, and Diwali falling largely in October/Q3 instead of September/Q2) created a temporary sales deferment, muting Q2 reported growth by an estimated 250 to 350 bps. However, October trends showed swift normalization and robust secondary sales.
    *   **Premiumization & Downtrading Coexist:** While premium lines and casualization/sneakerization are driving Average Selling Price (ASP) growth (*Bata India*, *Eureka Forbes*), sharp inflation in gold and raw materials has simultaneously triggered downtrading at lower price points or a shift in karatage toward 18K, 14K, and 9K (*Sky Gold*, *Shringar House of Mangalsutra*).
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   **Jewelry & B2B/B2C Dynamics:** Jewelry manufacturers (*Deepa Jewellers*, *Shanti Gold*) noted that soaring gold prices are driving consumers to exchange old gold, causing B2B clients to shift orders from invoice bases to job-work models. Meanwhile, retail-focused players (*BlueStone*, *PNGS Reva*) noted that natural diamond-studded pieces continue to capture resilient demand, while lab-grown diamonds (LGD) remain confined to a negligible solitaire niche.
    *   **Appliance Demand vs. Urban/Rural Realities:** While *Symphony* and *EPACK Durable* reported clean channel inventories and strong pent-up demand heading into summer/festive seasons, *Sheela Foam* noted high consumer price elasticity, forcing them to introduce new technology products sub-Rs 10,000 to combat trade-down behavior.

---

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Commodity & Forex Inflation:** Raw material pressures were universally cited. Polyol surged 65% and TDI 33% (*Sheela Foam*); copper, aluminum, and plastics saw double-digit inflation (*DOMS*, *EPACK Durable*, *IFB Industries*). Adverse forex movements (USD/CNY/CHF volatility) further hurt margins, creating forex losses for players like *EPACK Durable* (INR ~10 crore) and *IFB Industries*.
*   **Pricing Lag vs. Volume Protection:** 
    *   Most manufacturers implemented calibrated price hikes (ranging from 2% to 20% depending on the sector), but competitive intensity and fear of pricing out customers prevented full pass-through in the short term.
    *   *DOMS* explicitly prioritized volume market share over immediate margin recovery, choosing to remain patient for a couple of quarters.
*   **Operational Efficiencies & Mix Dilution:** While operating leverage aided EBITDA margins for firms like *Eureka Forbes* and *Symphony*, others (*Advit Jewels*, *Metro Brands*, *Shanti Gold*) saw temporary margin dilution due to heavy upfront marketing, exhibition expenses, and talent/minimum-wage revisions.

---

## Capex & capacity & expansion
*   **Aggressive Store Rollouts:** Organized retail and jewelry players are aggressively expanding footprints. *P N Gadgil* is targeting 23 new stores in FY27 (reaching ~103 total); *Metro Brands* operates 1,053 stores; *BlueStone* opened 27 stores in Q2.
*   **Backward Integration & Greenfield Projects:** 
    *   *DOMS* invested >Rs 200 crore in H1 FY27 on its 50-acre greenfield project in Umbergaon.
    *   *IFB Industries* acquired land in Gujarat for a 100% EV product plant (operations slated for Sep/Oct 2027) and expanded steel annealing capacity to 2,800 tonnes/month.
    *   *Timex Group* is expanding its Baddi plant to double single-shift capacity to 12 million units.
*   **Asset-Light Strategies:** *Symphony* adopted a pure outsourced, variable-cost model for its new entries into room air conditioners, BLDC fans, and air purifiers, requiring zero initial capex.

---

## What changed since last quarter
*   *Advit Jewels*: Tone shifted from "strong bridal demand" to "encouraging shift in consumer preferences" toward handcrafted luxury ahead of the Delhi showroom launch.
*   *Vaibhav Global*: Cut FY27 revenue guidance to 7%–9% (from 9%–11%) following disruptive platform migrations (Shopify Enterprise) and U.K. warehouse consolidations.
*   *DOMS*: Integrated Reynolds sales and support teams effective July 1, 2027, creating temporary employee cost mismatches and margin headwinds.
*   *EPACK Durable*: Board decided to sell its 50% stake in the Epavo Electricals JV (which suffered INR 12 crore in losses) to focus strictly on core competencies.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Festive and Wedding Season Sell-Throughs:** Realized demand and inventory clearance post-Diwali and during the heavy Q3 wedding calendar (*Bata India*, *P N Gadgil*, *Shanti Gold*).
*   **Raw Material Price Trajectory & Price Realization:** Whether companies like *Sheela Foam*, *DOMS*, and *IFB Industries* can successfully implement stickier price hikes to claw back gross margins.
*   **Progress on New Ventures & Integrations:** Execution and initial traction of *DOMS* with Reynolds, *Sheela Foam* with Furlenco consolidation, and *Symphony*'s asset-light rollout of air conditioners and BLDC fans.
*   **Stabilization Post-Disruptions:** Resolution of platform migration teething issues at *Vaibhav Global* and supply/BIS regularization for imported footwear brands at *Metro Brands*.
