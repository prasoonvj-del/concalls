# Consumer Durables: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the consumer durables and discretionary sector reported robust volume and top-line growth in Q2 FY27, underpinned by strong festive pre-bookings, aggressive retail expansion, and structural shifts toward organized players. However, profitability across several segments was compressed due to persistent raw material inflation (metals, memory, gold, and energy costs), foreign exchange headwinds, and time lags in passing on price hikes to consumers. While electronics and select appliances (like room ACs and watches) saw stellar momentum, companies dealing in commoditized segments faced margin squeeze and had to prioritize volume-led market share or deploy value engineering.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus / Agreements:** Managements universally highlighted healthy underlying demand for premium, design-led, and lightweight products. Companies across jewelry and watches noted strong consumer traction heading into the festive and wedding seasons (Advit Jewels, BlueStone, PNG Jewellers, Timex). In appliances, small domestic appliances and modern tech categories are seeing strong upgrade cycles.
*   **Divergence:** Discrepancies emerged regarding rural and lower-tier market demand. Blue Star and Eveready flagged that monsoon shortfalls and rural income drops created uncertainties in Tier 3–5 markets. Conversely, BlueStone and V-Guard reported broad-based growth across metros and smaller towns alike (though V-Guard noted regional spikes due to extended summers in the South). In commercial cooling, Blue Star noted a pause in quick-commerce infrastructure expansion, contrasting with surging consumer-facing appliance demand elsewhere.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
Input cost inflation was a prominent theme across almost all summaries, driven by rising prices of copper, zinc, aluminum, plastics, gold, and semiconductor memory:
*   **Pricing Lags & Margin Squeeze:** Blue Star reported a ~5% unrecovered cost gap despite price hikes; V-Guard noted a 2% to 4% pricing lag; EPACK Durable and Dixon (for ODMs) experienced margin pressure due to transmission lags. Timex and Borosil faced gross margin dips from material and FX pressures before implementing price hikes.
*   **Mitigation Strategies:** Companies are deploying calibrated price increases (Eveready, Timex, Borosil, EPACK), value engineering / "Total Competitiveness Management" (Blue Star), and strategic hedging (PNG Jewellers at ~80%, Sky Gold, BlueStone at 50%). DOMS explicitly stated it is prioritizing volume market share over immediate margin protection, remaining patient before taking aggressive pricing actions.

## Capex & capacity
Companies remain committed to capacity expansion, backward integration, and manufacturing footprint scaling:
*   **Dixon Technologies** guided for a full-year capex of Rs 1,200 crore, focusing on display modules, IT hardware PLI 2, and a JV with Vivo.
*   **DOMS Industries** invested >Rs 200 crore in H1 on its 50-acre greenfield project and Reynolds integration, with another Rs 100–125 crore planned for H2.
*   **Blue Star** maintained its full-year capex guidance at ~Rs 300 crore.
*   **EPACK Durable** has actualized Rs 370 crore out of its Rs 470 crore capex plan and is staggering the remaining Rs 100 crore.
*   **Borosil** approved a Rs 50 crore expansion for its Jaipur Pressware furnace and invested in captive solar projects.
*   **Timex Group** plans to invest USD 1.2 million to double single-shift capacity at Baddi to 12 million units annually.
*   **V-Guard** guided full-year capex at around Rs 150 crore.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Advit Jewels:** Shifted tone from "strong bridal demand" to "encouraging shift in consumer preferences" toward handcrafted luxury; completed construction of the Jaipur flagship store.
*   **BlueStone:** Transitioned focus from gold price volatility to "earning" customers; secured board approval for a Rs 300 crore NCD issue to retire high-cost debt; opened 27 new stores in Q2.
*   **DOMS Industries:** Integrated the Reynolds sales team effectively July 1, 2026; incorporated a subsidiary for the Seven SpA JV; absorbed a Rs 10.4 crore exceptional loss due to Gujarat floods.

## Company one-liners
*   **Advit Jewels:** Advit Jewels is successfully pivoting toward a B2C luxury retail model, utilizing its debt-free balance sheet to fund expansion, while accepting short-term margin pressure from increased marketing and brand-building activities.
*   **Blue Star:** Strong volume growth in RAC and data center order wins are currently offset by margin pressure from persistent material cost inflation, with management prioritizing product portfolio optimization to hit 6.5% segment margins.
*   **BlueStone Jewellery:** BlueStone is leveraging its tech-first, in-house manufacturing model to scale, focusing on long-term capital productivity and repeat customer growth while dismissing external threats like lab-grown gold.
*   **Borosil:** Borosil delivered a resilient H1 FY27 with 8.8% revenue growth and a 390 bps gross margin expansion, driven by price realizations, captive solar savings, and premiumization, while awaiting final anti-dumping notifications.
*   **DOMS Industries:** DOMS delivered 17.6% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by volumes, but margins faced pressure from severe raw material inflation, Gujarat flood damages, and upfront employee costs from the Reynolds integration.
*   **Dixon Technologies:** Dixon is aggressively scaling its IT hardware and component ecosystem (displays, optical) to drive long-term growth, offsetting near-term margin pressure from memory costs and appliance demand softness.
*   **EPACK Durable:** EPACK Durable delivered 106% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by non-RAC expansion, but reported profitability remained constrained by forex losses, unrecovered commodity lags, and higher interest/depreciation costs.
*   **Eveready Industries:** Eveready achieved its 8th consecutive quarter of YoY revenue growth driven by strong alkaline and rechargeable product traction, while absorbing raw material inflation through calibrated pricing.
*   **P N Gadgil Jewellers:** Strong operational performance with 57% EBITDA growth and successful expansion into Northern/Central India, supported by a robust balance sheet and a strategic shift toward a franchisee-led model.
*   **Sky Gold and Diamonds:** Sky Gold & Diamonds delivered robust Q2 FY27 performance with 61% YoY revenue growth, gross margin expansion to 9.6%, and positive operating cash flow, driven by scaling advance gold, exports, and institutionalized controls.
*   **Timex Group India:** Timex delivered a record-breaking quarter with 47% revenue growth and 54% EBITDA growth, capitalizing on strong analog demand, e-commerce expansion, and brand elevation while maintaining an asset-light retail rollout.
*   **V-Guard Industries:** V-Guard reported a robust Q2 FY27 with 38.2% revenue growth and a 71% jump in PAT, driven by strong seasonal demand and robust performance across Electronics and Electricals, though gross margins faced temporary headwinds.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Festive and Wedding Season Execution:** Actual realization of pent-up Navratri, Diwali, and winter wedding demand across jewelry (Advit, BlueStone, PNG, Sky Gold) and watch players (Timex).
*   **Margin Recovery & Price Pass-through:** Whether companies like Blue Star, V-Guard, Eveready, and EPACK successfully bridge their 2% to 5% input cost gaps via calibrated price hikes.
*   **Regulatory Updates:** The Ministry of Finance's final notification regarding DGTR anti-dumping duties on Chinese glassware for Borosil.
*   **Ramp-up of New Ventures:** Commercial production start of Hisense front-load washing machines at EPACK (December), the launch of the Vivo JV operations for Dixon (Q4), and the rollout of the Delhi showroom for Advit Jewels.
