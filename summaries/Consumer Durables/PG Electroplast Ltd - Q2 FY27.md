# PG Electroplast Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenues crossed Rs. 2,950 crore for the first half for the first time, driven by strong order books and volume growth in Room AC and washing machines.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenues (Q2 FY27): Rs. 934 crore (up 42.5% YoY)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs. 69.14 crore (up 54.8% YoY from Rs. 44.68 crore)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 7.4%
- Net Income / PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs. 13.67 crore (vs. Rs. 2.76 crore YoY)
- First Half (H1 FY27) Consolidated Revenues: Crossed Rs. 2,950 crore
- H1 FY27 Product Business Contribution: 70.3% of sales revenue (growing 41.1% YoY)
- H1 FY27 AC Business Growth: 36.2% to Rs. 1,561 crore (Volume growth: ~22%)
- H1 FY27 Washing Machines Growth: 60.7% to crossing Rs. 500 crore
- H1 FY27 Cooler Growth: Muted at ~4.8%, contributing Rs. 19.3 crore
- H1 FY27 Electronic Business Growth: 90.3%, contributing 12% of revenues
- H1 FY27 Plastic Molding and Components: Rs. 521.6 crore (up 7.5% YoY)
- H1 FY27 JV Goodworth Electronics Revenues: Rs. 623.8 crore (EBITDA: Rs. 22.7 crore)
- H1 FY27 Subsidiary PG Technoplast Revenues: Rs. 2,130 crore
- Full-Year FY27 PLI Expectation: Rs. 70 crore plus
- State Incentives (Maharashtra): Rs. 1.55 crore booked in Q2

### Highlights
- H1 consolidated revenues crossed Rs. 2,950 crore for the first time; Room AC and washing machine verticals posted highest-ever H1 sales.
- New campus at Salarpur, Bhiwadi became operational for consolidating relocations (coolers, sanitaryware, plastic components, mold making).
- Refrigerator facility at Sri City (South India) on track for commercial production in Q4 FY27 (capacity: 1.2 million units; Phase 1: direct cool and side-by-side).
- Supa compressor project on track for trial production by end of November 2026 and mass production in Jan-Feb 2027; targeting 1.5 million+ compressors in calendar year 2027.
- Statutory auditor changed to BSR & Company across the entire company starting Q3 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a full-year volume growth close to 20% for the Room AC business (delivering 2.1-2.2 million units vs. 1.7 million last year).
- Full-year RAC realization increase expected to be close to the 15%-20% range.
- Full-year washing machine growth targeted at 45%-50%.
- Electronics business full-year revenue targeted around Rs. 550-580 crore (referred to as Rs. 550-Rs. 600 crore range).
- JV Goodworth Electronics full-year sales guided around Rs. 800-900 crore.
- Sri City refrigerator plant targeting 700k production in FY28.
- FY28 capex guided around Rs. 150 to 200 odd crore.
- Total incentives for FY28 expected between Rs. 105-110 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Secondary Room AC sales were very strong in the channel with lower channel inventory compared to last year.
- Primary sales in Q2 were muted, but H2 (December and March quarters) is expected to show much better numbers.
- AC is primarily an urban/Tier-1 to Tier-3 product; industry is likely to clock >15% growth this year.
- TV season was slightly slow due to high price increases and negative price elasticity.
- Cooler inventory levels have normalized across the channel; a decent growth season is anticipated ahead.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross contribution percentage softened YoY due to the pass-through of commodity prices (copper, aluminum, plastic) and rupee depreciation.
- Contract manufacturing margins operate on a fixed absolute amount per unit rather than a percentage basis. Bill of material for AC moved from Rs. 14,000 (2 years ago) to Rs. 18,500.
- Price hikes occurred in January, April, and October, with another round planned for January 1.
- Other operating expenses reduced YoY due to lower forex loss (Rs. 3 crore vs. Rs. 8 crore last year) and absence of Rs. 3 crore one-off GST costs from last year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY27 capex guidance of around Rs. 400 crore remains on track, with most payments already made (reflected in CWIP or advances).
- Sri City refrigerator plant Phase-1 will start with direct cool and side-by-side, adding frost-free and multi-door in Phase-2.
- Supa compressor facility targeting 2 million capacity in Phase-1; potential second line decision in April-May 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stood at Rs. 422.5 crore.
- Working capital investment remains high due to higher raw material commodity prices requiring larger inventory investments.
- Management aims to keep interest costs in H2 flat compared to H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High commodity prices and semiconductor memory price spikes inflate working capital and inventory values.
- Export pricing disadvantage against Chinese manufacturing in European and American markets makes exports challenging beyond small SARC shipments.
- POS payment machine project is stalled due to customer-end issues.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tanay Shah (DAM Capital) on gross margin compression and price pass-through:** Management clarified that percentage margins decline in high-commodity environments because contract manufacturing operates on fixed per-unit margins, compounded by high growth in lower-margin electronics (semiconductor/memory cost surges).
2. **Achal Lohade (Equities) on compressor timeline and export viability:** Management confirmed trial production by end of November, mass production by Jan-Feb 2027, and noted that exports to Western markets face heavy price disadvantages compared to China.
3. **Santosh Seshadri on other expenses and finance costs:** Management explained that lower forex losses (Rs. 3 crore vs. Rs. 8 crore) and no repeat of GST-related one-offs reduced other expenses, while higher working capital is a function of elevated raw material prices.
4. **Dhruv Jain (Ambit Capital) on electronics growth drivers and FY28 incentives:** Management broke down electronics into TV motherboards, AC controllers, BMS, and CCTV, and estimated total FY28 incentives between Rs. 105-110 crore.
5. **Keyur Pandya (ICICI Life Insurance) on Q3 volume visibility and refrigerator approval bottlenecks:** Management guided early-teens volume growth for Q3 and stated that refrigerator approvals will not be a bottleneck as models are co-developed with anchor customers.

### One-line takeaway
PG Electroplast reported a robust H2 order book and strong H1 revenues, driven by aggressive capacity scaling in ACs, washing machines, upcoming refrigerators, and backward integration into compressors, despite percentage margin compression from commodity pass-throughs.
