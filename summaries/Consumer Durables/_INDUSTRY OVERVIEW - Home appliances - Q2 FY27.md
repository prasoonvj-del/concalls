# Consumer Durables - Home appliances: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Consumer Durables and Home Appliances sector reflects a broad bifurcation: **robust volume and top-line growth** driven by strong consumer demand, low category penetration, and festive channel stocking, countered by **widespread margin compression** from severe commodity inflation (copper, aluminum, plastics, steel, polyol, and TDI), adverse foreign exchange movements, and seasonal trade price lock-ins. While companies like **LG Electronics**, **Eureka Forbes**, **Stove Kraft**, **Symphony**, **Singer India**, and **Onida** posted strong profitability or revenue milestones, players heavily exposed to OEM/component dynamics or sudden cost spikes—such as **DOMS**, **EPACK**, **IFB Industries**, **Sheela Foam**, and **Voltas**—faced temporary bottom-line pressures.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that structural drivers remain intact, supported by low household penetration in categories like room air conditioners (RACs), water purifiers, washing machines, and sewing machines. Pre-festive stocking and strong volume expansion were common themes.
*   **Divergence:** Management views diverged on festival timing and seasonal impacts. **IFB Industries**, **Sheela Foam**, and **Symphony** noted that Q2 revenue growth was temporarily muted or shifted because Diwali fell later into Q3. Conversely, **LG Electronics**, **Voltas**, and **Onida** reported surprisingly strong off-season RAC and consumer appliance demand due to elevated temperatures and extended summer conditions. Furthermore, **Sheela Foam** noted high price elasticity with consumers trading down to lower-priced categories following price hikes, whereas **LG Electronics** and **Eureka Forbes** observed aggressive premiumization (e.g., multi-door refrigerators and large-screen TVs).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Almost all companies highlighted intense input cost pressures stemming from geopolitical disruptions in West Asia, surging ocean freight, and rising prices of copper, aluminum, plastics, and chemicals (polyol up 65%, TDI up 33% per **Sheela Foam**; overall BOM cost up ~4.3% for **IFB** and ~18% for **Voltas**). 
*   **Disagreements/Divergence in response:** 
    *   *Pricing vs. Volume:* **DOMS** and **Sheela Foam** explicitly chose to prioritize volume market share and brand investments over immediate price hikes, accepting short-term margin contraction. 
    *   *Aggressive Pass-through:* **Voltas** implemented a 14% price hike alongside a planned 5%–7% Q3 increase, but suffered from long seasonal price lock-ins (4–6 months) with organized trade. **Eureka Forbes** announced a 3–4% price increase for mid-November, while **EPACK Durable** and **Onida** relied on calibrated, category-specific hikes (EPACK raising prices 15–20%).
    *   *Cost Control:* **IFB Industries** combated margin erosion by aggressively freezing ~100 vacant headcount positions and driving value engineering through a new Swiss tech center.

## Capex & capacity
The sector exhibits active capacity expansion and backward integration:
*   **DOMS:** Invested >Rs 200 crore in H1 FY27 on its 50-acre greenfield Umbergaon project and Reynolds integration.
*   **EPACK Durable:** Actualized Rs 370 crore of its Rs 470 crore capex plan, preparing for Hisense washing machine production.
*   **IFB Industries:** Acquired land in Gujarat for a Rs 103 crore EV product plant (commencing late 2027) and expanded steel annealing capacity to 2,800 tonnes/month.
*   **LG Electronics:** Invested Rs 8.7 billion in tangible assets in Q2, heavily focused on the Sri City, Andhra Pradesh facility to double capacities for compressors and ACs.
*   **Singer India:** Progressing on its new Bhiwadi factory, with pilot production starting next month.
*   **Stove Kraft:** Expanding Baddi for pressure cookers/appliances and investing CNY20 million in a China subsidiary/JV for backward integration of Triply circles.
*   **Symphony:** Adopted a lean, asset-light outsourced model for its new entries into room ACs, BLDC fans, and air purifiers with zero initial capex.

## What changed since last quarter
*(Drawn from summaries explicitly providing last-quarter comparisons)*
*   **Voltas:** Shifted Voltbek EBITDA breakeven expectations to next financial year (FY28) due to severe commodity price shocks and unexpected trade price lock-in constraints during a lean quarter.
*   **LG Electronics:** Tone shifted to higher confidence following its highest growth quarter since listing; commercial production timelines for Sri City compressors (Q3) and ACs (Q4) remain firmly on track.
*   **Singer India:** Maintained strong non-government momentum; Bhiwadi factory pilot production remains on schedule for next month, while government orders have tapered off to a minor residual tail.
*   **Onida Electronics:** Advanced its "Onida Rewired" strategy, moving closer to its internal target of turning EBITDA positive in FY27 while actively planning to phase out the OEM business over the next 8–12 months.

## What to watch next quarter
*   The actual realization and financial impact of festive season sell-outs (Q3 shift).
*   The effectiveness and consumer reception of recent and upcoming price hikes (e.g., Voltas's 5–7% hike, Eureka Forbes's 3–4% hike) in absorbing commodity inflation.
*   Progress on new manufacturing and operational milestones (e.g., LG's Sri City plant launches, Singer's Bhiwadi factory pilot, Stove Kraft's China Triply facility).
*   Debt and working capital normalization as festive inventory clears (notably for Sheela Foam, Singer, and DOMS).

## Company one-liners
*   **DOMS Industries Ltd:** DOMS delivered 17.6% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by volumes, but margins faced pressure from severe raw material inflation, Gujarat flood damages, and upfront employee costs from the Reynolds integration.
*   **EPACK Durable Ltd:** EPACK Durable delivered 106% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by non-RAC expansion, but reported profitability remained constrained by forex losses, unrecovered commodity lags, and higher interest/depreciation costs.
*   **Eureka Forbes Ltd:** Eureka Forbes delivered strong H1 growth and record quarterly PAT, leveraging operating efficiencies to offset commodity inflation while maintaining a positive outlook for H2.
*   **IFB Industries Ltd:** IFB Industries reported a mixed Q2 FY27 with 14% revenue growth offset by a decline in profitability due to persistent commodity inflation and adverse forex impacts, while its Engineering division continued robust performance and expansion.
*   **LG Electronics India Ltd:** Strong Q2 financial performance with record growth, ongoing premiumization, and strategic expansion into the Vehicle Solutions business supported by the Sri City facility ramp-up.
*   **Onida Electronics Ltd:** Onida Electronics reported robust Q2 revenue and brand margin growth driven by its "Onida Rewired" strategy, while navigating input cost pressures with a target for EBITDA positivity in FY27.
*   **Sheela Foam Ltd:** Sheela Foam is navigating severe raw material inflation by prioritizing market share and brand investment, while the consolidation of Furlenco marks a strategic pivot toward a broader furniture-and-comfort ecosystem.
*   **Singer India Ltd:** Singer India delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 34% revenue growth and an 85% jump in EBITDA, driven by robust non-government sewing machine demand and scaling domestic manufacturing.
*   **Stove Kraft Ltd:** Stove Kraft posted its highest-ever Q2 revenue and profitability, driven by surging induction cooktop demand, 120 bps gross margin expansion, and disciplined omnichannel execution.
*   **Symphony Ltd:** Symphony reported robust Q2 consolidated revenue and EBITDA expansion driven by operating leverage and disciplined cost management, and announced an asset-light, margin-accretive entry into room ACs, BLDC fans, and air purifiers.
*   **Voltas Ltd:** Voltas delivered 32% RAC volume growth and extended its market leadership to 17.4%, but margins were compressed by an unprecedented 18% cost inflation locked into seasonal trade contracts.

*Based on: DOMS Industries Ltd, EPACK Durable Ltd, Eureka Forbes Ltd, IFB Industries Ltd, LG Electronics India Ltd, Onida Electronics Ltd, Sheela Foam Ltd, Singer India Ltd, Stove Kraft Ltd, Symphony Ltd, Voltas Ltd*
