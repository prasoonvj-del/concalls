# Dixon Technologies (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management remains confident in long-term growth and strategic expansion into high-value components, despite near-term margin headwinds from memory costs and appliance demand softness.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 21,806 crore
*   **EBITDA (ex-fair value gain):** Rs 609 crore
*   **PAT (ex-fair value gain):** Rs 293 crore
*   **Mobile Volumes (H1):** 17.2 million units
*   **Washing Machine Volume Growth (Q2):** 27% (semi-automatic), 24% (fully automatic top-loading)

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Expansion:** Focus on deepening manufacturing in display modules, camera modules, and mechanicals.
*   **New Tech Initiatives:** Actively pursuing silicon photonics, optical transceivers, and advanced packaging to target AI infrastructure and data centers.
*   **Vivo JV:** Operations expected to commence in Q4 FY27.
*   **IT Hardware:** Transitioning from hybrid category (Rs 250 crore capex) to global category (Rs 500 crore capex) under IT PLI 2.
*   **Q Tech:** Capacity expanded to 120 million units; targeting 180-190 million units by next fiscal.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Mobile Volumes:** Management expects 33-34 million units for the current fiscal (excluding Vivo).
*   **Vivo JV:** Anticipates 20-21 million units for the next financial year.
*   **IT Hardware:** Targets Rs 15,000-20,000 crore revenue in the next two years.
*   **Display Business:** Potential revenue of Rs 6,000-7,000 crore over the next few years.
*   **Q3 Mobile Volume:** Guidance of 7-7.5 million units.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Smartphone Market:** Industry declined 26% YoY, but Dixon sustained volumes at stable YoY levels.
*   **Memory Prices:** Significant increase (35-40% impact on selling prices); management expects prices to remain high for some time.
*   **Consumer Electronics:** Demand for large-screen TVs (50 inches+) and premium models remains healthy; value/mid-range segment remains soft.
*   **Appliance Demand:** Impacted by input cost inflation and elevated freight costs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Compression:** Washing machine margins compressed to 8.3% (from 10.5-11%) and lighting margins impacted due to commodity/freight costs and time lag in pass-through.
*   **Pass-through:** EMS business (mobiles, IT, telecom) has automatic cost pass-through; ODM business (appliances, lighting) faces a time lag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex (H1):** Rs 700 crore.
*   **Capex (FY27 Guidance):** Rs 1,200 crore.
*   **Washing Machine Capacity:** Total capacity of 3.9 million units.
*   **Display Project:** Total capex of Rs 1,000 crore planned over three years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Surge in receivables/payables due to strategic memory inventory and pending PLI receivables.
*   **Cash Flow:** Management expects 80-85% of blocked working capital to be released before March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Elevated global memory prices impacting input costs.
*   Time lag in cost pass-through for ODM businesses (appliances/lighting).
*   Softness in consumer demand for value/mid-range products.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Siddhartha Bera (Nomura):* Asked about mobile volume outlook. Management guided 33-34 million units (ex-Vivo) and 20-21 million units for Vivo JV.
*   *Santhosh Seshadri (Avendus Spark):* Asked about capital allocation. Management highlighted IT hardware and optical/advanced packaging as key growth areas.
*   *Sameet Sinha (Macquarie):* Clarified cost pass-through mechanisms. Management confirmed automatic pass-through for EMS, lag for ODM.
*   *Renu (IIFL):* Asked about IT hardware targets. Management reiterated Rs 15,000-20,000 crore target in 2 years.
*   *Vipraw Srivastava (PhillipCapital):* Questioned Q Tech margin decline. Management cited temporary blip due to capacity expansion and FX, expects recovery to 6-7%.

### One-line takeaway
Dixon is aggressively scaling its IT hardware and component ecosystem (displays, optical) to drive long-term growth, offsetting near-term margin pressure from memory costs and appliance demand softness.
