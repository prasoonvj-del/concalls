# Campus Activewear Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered 12.2% revenue growth and 17.7% PAT growth despite inflationary pressures, supply chain volatility, and statutory minimum wage revisions.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 12.2% growth YoY (reported revenue growth context)
- **Volume growth:** 11.7% YoY
- **EBITDA margin:** 15.9% (stable YoY)
- **PAT growth:** 17.7% YoY
- **ASP increases:** MRP increases of ~8% across key categories; underlying ASP increase of ~5% in the core Stuck-On category.
- **Other expenses impact:** Rs. 5 crore for minimum wage increases, Rs. 2.5 crore for advertising/marketing (distributor meet and logo launch), and Rs. 2.5 crore indirect costs from newly commissioned plants (Paonta Sahib and Pantnagar).
- **Employee cost impact:** 15% increase, of which 10% was annualization plus increments and 5% was new hiring.
- **Store expansion target:** 90 to 100 stores by the end of FY27 (80-120 stores targeted).
- **School shoes growth:** ~50% YoY.
- **Sneaker volume contribution:** 12% to 13% in Q1; target close to 30% growth for the category in FY27.

### Highlights
- Management launched *Elan by Campus* into the neo-casual footwear segment, retailing at Rs. 1,899 to Rs. 2,599.
- Recorded highest-ever Q1 production and continued momentum into July to proactively build inventory for festive demand.
- Refreshed brand identity with a new logo unveiled in May and hosted a large distributor meet with record orders.
- Transitioned 158 franchise stores from an outright business model to an SOR (Sale or Return) model.
- Closed 4-5 million capacity of archaic DIP technology and shifted sports shoes entirely to Stuck-On.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is 100% confident of delivering full-year EBITDA margins in the 17% to 19% band.
- Aiming for mid-double-digit overall growth for the year (combination of high single-digit volume and 6% to 7% ASP growth).
- Employee expenses as a percentage of sales expected to remain proportionate to last year on a full-year basis.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was broad-based, with strong momentum in women's and kids' portfolios, sneakers, and school shoes.
- School shoes moved entirely from DIP to Stuck-On, growing nearly 50% YoY.
- Direct-to-consumer channels showed strong performance: marketplace operations grew mid-double-digits, company-owned stores saw ~20% SSG growth, and brand.com grew over 100% YoY.
- Demand was slightly tapered in the last quarter due to geopolitical factors and floods in Maharashtra and Gujarat, but overall demand reading remains positive.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material inflation and statutory minimum wage revisions created cost pressures from March/April onwards.
- Management implemented an 8% MRP increase effective 1st April, which fully absorbed RM inflation.
- Additional depreciation of ~Rs. 2.5 crores absorbed from commissioning Paonta Sahib and Pantnagar plants.
- CNG/LPG supply restrictions during the quarter created temporary production constraints for the sandals category.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned new manufacturing facilities at Paonta Sahib and Pantnagar.
- Annual assembly capacity reflects the phase-out of 4-5 million pairs of DIP capacity, reallocating focus to Stuck-On.
- Opened 18 EBU stores in Q1 (highest in 6-8 quarters), supported by the new SOR model.
- Expanding FOFO network with master franchises increasing from ~8 to ~16 partners, targeting partners with 5-6 stores each.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Franchise stores transitioned to an SOR model where partners pay a deposit on inventory, and the company controls inventory and discounting.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and global supply chain volatility.
- Inflationary pressures on key raw materials and statutory minimum wage hikes.
- Temporary volume/ASP suppressions from Walmart (Flipkart/Myntra) accounting changes (GT charges netted off) and school shoes mix dilution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vidisha Seth (Ambit Capital) on 8% price hike elasticity:** Management stated the hike was effective 1st April to offset RM and minimum wage pressures, and volume still grew without significant resistance.
2. **Avinash on lower volumes/base and ASP reporting:** Management explained that Walmart accounting reclassifications and franchise model transitions temporarily impacted reported revenue/ASP growth by ~4.5% to 5% in Q1.
3. **Umang Mehta on other expenses and margins:** Management clarified that Rs. 5 crore went to minimum wage, Rs. 2.5 crore to A&P, and Rs. 2.5 crore to new plant indirect costs; full-year margin guidance of 17-19% is reiterated.
4. **Prerna Jhunjhunwala on franchise store economics and rentals:** Management clarified that franchisees bear store rentals, while the company controls inventory/discounting under the SOR model, offering ROIs between 18% and 30%.
5. **Ajay Nandavar on Gujarat growth and channel cannibalization:** Management stated EBO expansion uses differentiated product and service lenses rather than price-cutting, resulting in positive spillover effects on general trade visibility.

### One-line takeaway
Campus Activewear delivered a resilient Q1 FY27 with 12.2% revenue growth and stable margins despite cost inflation and temporary accounting adjustments, supported by strong brand traction and strategic inventory build-up for the festive season.
