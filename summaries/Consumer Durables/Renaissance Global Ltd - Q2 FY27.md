# Renaissance Global Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 performance with 90% PAT growth, significant debt reduction, and successful execution of the D2C strategy despite intentional B2B revenue contraction.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained; management reiterated the exit of ~Rs 400 crore of low-quality customer brand revenue by year-end.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained positive; management emphasized the "white space" opportunity for Jean Dousset in luxury lab-grown diamonds.
- **New topics:** Specific explanation of forex losses (due to USD-denominated debt), confirmation of holding Rs 383 crore in cash/investments for potential acquisitions, and details on Jean Dousset store unit economics (25 appointments/week target).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on store expansion (Boston boutique opened Nov 4, 2026) and working capital improvement (cycle shortened by 54 days YoY).

### Key numbers
- **Operational Revenue (Q2):** Rs 639 crore (up 20% YoY).
- **Operational Revenue (H1):** Rs 1,329 crore (up 25% YoY).
- **Total Revenue (H1, incl. bullion):** Rs 1,474 crore (up 37% YoY).
- **EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 51 crore (up 19% YoY; margin ~8%).
- **EBITDA (H1):** Rs 101 crore (margin 7.6%).
- **PAT (Q2):** Rs 26 crore (up 28% YoY).
- **PAT (H1):** Rs 52 crore (up 92% YoY).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 199 crore (down 42% YoY).
- **Cash Flow from Operations (TTM):** Rs 280 crore.

### Highlights
- **D2C Strategy:** Jean Dousset and With Clarity recorded a CAGR of 66% since FY22; management targets Rs 375 crore revenue for own brands in FY27.
- **Retail Expansion:** 4 Jean Dousset boutiques operational (LA, NY, SF, Boston); target is 8 boutiques by end of FY27.
- **B2B Rationalization:** Management is exiting low-margin, high-working-capital customer brand accounts (approx. Rs 400 crore revenue impact); inventory is being sold down.
- **Cash Management:** Highest ever TTM operating cash flow of Rs 280 crore; company holds Rs 383 crore in cash/investments for potential acquisitions.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Expect deceleration in customer brands segment due to client exits; total customer brand revenue for FY27 expected to be Rs 1,700–1,800 crore.
- **Debt:** Target to reduce net debt to ~Rs 100 crore by 2030.
- **Long-term:** Ambition to build a Rs 1,000 crore D2C business by FY29.
- **Bullion:** Sales expected to continue for the remainder of FY27; accounting methodology change likely in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Jean Dousset:** Operates in a "white space" of luxury lab-grown diamonds; average order value (AOV) is USD 5,500.
- **With Clarity:** Digital-first brand; AOV is USD 2,500.
- **Store Metrics:** Each boutique targets Rs 25–30 crore annual sales; requires ~25 appointments/week to hit targets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Forex:** Losses attributed to revaluation of USD-denominated borrowings (Rs 550–600 crore).
- **Marketing:** JD marketing spend expected to be 17–18% of sales in FY27 (vs 25% in FY26) due to operating leverage.
- **Cost Efficiency:** Implemented a cost reduction program totaling Rs 40–50 crore in overheads.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex:** Rs 10–12 crore per boutique.
- **Manufacturing:** Currently manufacturing ~25% of Jean Dousset products in-house; target is 100% in-house over 12–18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Working Capital:** Cycle improved by 54 days YoY; target is ~180 days.
- **Debt:** Net debt at Rs 199 crore; management maintains debt to preserve strategic optionality for acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Forex Volatility:** Exposure to USD-denominated debt creates accounting losses during currency fluctuations.
- **Revenue Drag:** Intentional exit of high-working-capital clients will cause temporary revenue deceleration.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Naitik (NV Alpha Fund) on forex losses:** Management clarified that losses arise from revaluing USD-denominated borrowings (Rs 550–600 crore) when the rupee fluctuates.
2. **Anushka Vora (Vimana Capital) on JD store performance:** Management stated San Francisco is tracking well (month-over-month growth), while Boston is too new (opened 6-7 days prior).
3. **Manpreet Arora (Northern Lights Wealth) on customer brand exit:** Management confirmed the Rs 400 crore exit plan, noting they are substituting these with clients requiring 90-day working capital cycles instead of 360-day cycles.
4. **Vishal Prasad (Raj Mahal Capital) on JD store economics:** Management explained that 25 appointments/week at a 33% conversion rate and USD 7,500 AOV leads to ~Rs 30 crore annual sales per store.
5. **Naitik (NV Alpha Fund) on debt reduction:** Management explained they are holding Rs 383 crore in cash/investments to pursue strategic acquisitions rather than immediate debt repayment.

### One-line takeaway
Renaissance Global is successfully pivoting toward a high-margin D2C model by sacrificing low-quality B2B revenue, using the resulting cash flow to fund retail expansion and maintain a war chest for acquisitions.
