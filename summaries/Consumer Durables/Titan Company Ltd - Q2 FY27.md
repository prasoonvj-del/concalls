# Titan Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported healthy consolidated growth across all brands/divisions and reiterated an ~11% domestic jewellery EBIT margin target despite competitive discounting and gold price volatility.

### Key numbers
* **Customs duty gain:** Rs 490 crore in Titan Jewellery division and Rs 46 crore in CaratLane in Q2 FY27.
* **Domestic Jewellery (TMZ) EBIT Margin:** 11.1% normalized for customs duty gains; 11.7% normalized for both customs duty gains and MTM reversal.
* **MTM Impact:** Q1 MTM gain reversal impacted Q2 FY27 jewellery margin by ~60 bps.
* **Damas Acquisition Liability Fair Value Adjustment:** Reassessed 4-year liability reduced by Rs 671 crore from Rs 1,668 crore, disclosed as a separate item on the face of P&L.
* **Capital Raising:** Board approved borrowing replenishment of Rs 2,500 crore.

### Highlights
* Broad-based healthy growth registered across non-jewellery businesses, including Watches, EyeCare, Fragrances, Fashion Accessories, Taneira, TEAL, and International Jewellery (North America).
* Diamond Expertise Centres expanded to over 250 stores to support long-term natural diamond adoption.
* Customer preference shows strong adoption of lower-caratage offerings (18-karat gold daily/wedding wear, 14-karat Tanishq studded, and up to 9-carat Mia).

### Guidance & outlook
* **Jewellery EBIT Margin Target:** Maintained at ~11% for FY27; growth prioritized over margin expansion.
* **Strategy:** Drive long-term buyer growth through sub-Rs 1,00,000 and sub-Rs 50,000 offerings, store network expansion, store revamps, and the Old Gold Exchange program.

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Festive Trends:** Navratri saw positive consumer sentiment and store traffic. Gold price volatility caused a brief mid-period slowdown post-Vijayadashami, but footfalls recovered with lower gold rates leading up to Dhanteras.
* **Category Mix:** Sustained demand for core jewellery and studded/diamond products (driven by younger demographics); investment-led buying (coins and bars) remained muted across the industry in Q2, inline with World Gold Council trends.
* **Watches:** October growth accelerated over Q2, supported by strong performance in the Rs 25,000 to Rs 1,00,000 price point and Edge launches.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Gold Realization Pressure:** Customs duty increase kept domestic gold prices at a discount to international rates, pressuring gold price realization across the market.
* **CaratLane Margins:** Moderated to 6.5% in Q2 due to front-loaded investments in brand building, talent, and festive promotions; H1 EBIT margin trajectory remains on track toward 8%-9% target.
* **MTM Volatility:** Management expects ongoing MTM quarterly fluctuations of 40-50 bps due to gold price volatility and hedging positions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Network expansion and store upgrading ongoing across all retail formats to expand store sizes and enter new cities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Board-approved Rs 2,500 crore debt issuance is a replenishment of existing borrowings, not an incremental net borrowing addition.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Increasing competitive intensity and discounting by local jewellers utilizing pre-customs duty inventory gains.
* Domestic market gold price discounts driven by unorganized grey channel gold inflows.
* Potential geopolitical conflicts impacting international operations and Damas recovery trajectory.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Videesha Sheth (Ambit Capital):** *What is causing CaratLane's margin pressure from 10.2% in the base period to 6.5%?*  
  **Management:** It was a conscious choice to invest in brand building, talent, and elevated promotional offers in Q2. H1 margins remain on track for the target of inching up from 8% to 9% over time.
* **Mihir Shah (Nomura):** *Given the reduction in low-margin gold coin sales, should near-term jewellery margins trend higher than 11%?*  
  **Management:** Margins will remain anchored around 11% for FY27 as growth is the primary objective and competitive pulls/pressures persist in the market.
* **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay Global):** *Is the approved Rs 2,500 crore capital raise fresh capital or debt restructuring?*  
  **Management:** It is a replenishment of existing borrowings, not additional leverage.
* **Sameer Gupta (India Infoline):** *Is the Rs 671 crore Damas liability reduction an indicator of an asset impairment?*  
  **Management:** No, it is an Ind AS 109 accounting fair-value adjustment of the four-year put/call option liability based on current geopolitical conditions. The core long-term value of the Damas opportunity has not diminished, so no asset impairment was taken.
* **Jay Doshi (Kotak Institutional Equities):** *Is smuggled gold available at a 5%-6% discount contributing to market pressure?*  
  **Management:** Domestic market gold prices trading at a discount indicates active non-official channels. Titan navigates these tactical market conditions through regional responses while awaiting potential customs duty adjustments by the government.

### One-line takeaway
Titan delivered strong Q2 growth across all business segments while maintaining its ~11% FY27 jewellery EBIT margin target despite competitive price pressures and gold rate volatility.
