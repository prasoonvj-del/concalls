# Khazanchi Jewellers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 performance, successful main board migration, and profit growth outpacing revenue driven by higher retail contribution.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (INR 5,000 crores in revenue by 2030, 25%-30% annual growth).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from Q1's strong start to confirmed festive resilience, with Oct-Nov period sales up 14% YoY with half of November remaining.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Main board migration successfully completed on October 27, 2026; credit rating upgrade planned. Cash flow concerns from Q1 did not appear as a major topic.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Migration to the main board was delivered successfully on October 27, 2026.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 1,191 crores, up 25% YoY (vs INR 952 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 80.5 crores, up 49% YoY (vs INR 53.8 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 6.8%, up from 5.6%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 56.9 crores, up 47% YoY (vs INR 38.7 crores in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 4.78%, up from 4.06%
- **H1 FY27 EPS:** INR 22.97, up from INR 15.64
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 606 crores, up 10.39% YoY (vs INR 549 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 B2B Revenue:** INR 513 crores (vs INR 517 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 B2C Revenue:** INR 87 crores, up from INR 30 crores in Q2 FY26 (14% of business)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 40.48 crores, up 24% YoY (vs INR 32.63 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 6.68%, up from 5.94%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 29.1 crores, up 23% YoY (vs INR 23.55 crores in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 4.8%, up from 4.29%
- **Flagship Showroom Revenue (since Feb 7, 2026 opening):** Close to INR 200 crores

### Highlights
- Migrated to the main board of both BSE and NSE on October 27, 2026.
- H1 FY27 marked the strongest half-year in the company's history.
- Flagship showroom generated close to INR 200 crores since opening on February 7, 2026.
- Appointed Bhagyashri Borse as brand ambassador for retail promotions.
- Continued 50+ year history since inception in Sowcarpet, Chennai.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained guidance of INR 5,000 crores in revenue by 2030, based on 25%-30% annual growth.
- Aiming to achieve 30%-40% retail contribution over the next 3 to 4 years.
- Target to open 8 to 10 stores over the next 3 to 4 years (at least 2 stores per year).
- Expecting overall margin to improve by at least 150 bps as retail scales to 40%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Gold prices reached a peak of INR 1.458 lakhs per 10 grams in January before correcting to approximately INR 1.38 lakhs.
- Revenue between October 1 and November 15 was 14% higher than the entire October-November period last year.
- Industry jewelry spending grew 16% YoY in Q2.
- Wedding season underway following the festive season, historically the strongest period for the company.
- Strong demand for natural diamonds in South India, with Vajraa Diamonds achieving total sales matching last year's full-year sales in H1 alone.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- B2B EBITDA margin is 5%-6%; retail EBITDA margin is 10%-11%.
- Natural diamond margins are 15%-18%.
- Profit grew faster than revenue due to improving product mix, increased retail contribution, and higher-margin product introductions (lightweight and diamond jewelry).
- Inventory managed via a replacement/refilling system.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned store rollout of 8 to 10 stores over the next 3 to 4 years, combining flagship stores and premium boutiques for lightweight designs.
- Initial expansion focused on Tamil Nadu, followed by Andhra Pradesh.
- Expansion to be funded via internal accruals and debt as required; QIP may be considered based on management decision.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity ratio is almost at 0.3.
- Very low debt; short receivables cycle (average cash selling to 2 weeks max for certain B2B players).
- Company plans to opt for credit rating upgrades following main board migration.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated and volatile gold prices.
- Reliance on seasonal demand shifts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aditi Jain (Wealth Management Consultancy):** Asked about credit rating upgrades and cash utilization. Management stated they will opt for credit ratings now that they are on the main board, and cash will be used for internal expansion.
- **Riya Desai:** Asked about inventory per new store and ROE/ROCE sustainability. Management replied inventory depends on store size/location, and ROCE will be sustained via higher-margin retail shift.
- **Parakh Shah (Individual Investor):** Inquired about margin split, hedging, and receivables. Management stated retail contribution (13%-14%) and product mix drove margins, inventory uses a refilling/replacement system, and receivables are kept tight (spot to 2 weeks).
- **Viren Shah:** Asked about capex/inventory per store and trade partner conflict. Management stated expansion uses internal accruals/debt, and the dual B2B/B2C model has been successfully managed for decades.
- **Vijay Menon:** Asked about H2 demand visibility and capital requirements. Management highlighted a 14% sales increase in Oct-Nov over last year and noted QIP may be considered for future expansion if needed.

### One-line takeaway
Khazanchi Jewellers posted its strongest H1 ever with a 25% revenue and 47% PAT jump, successfully migrating to the main board while scaling its retail segment.
