# Eureka Forbes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported broad-based growth, record quarterly PAT, and successful market share gains despite commodity headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 890 crore (+15% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,590 crore (+15.1% YoY).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 120.7 crore (+18.8% YoY).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 13.6% (+43 bps YoY).
*   **Reported PAT:** Rs 73 crore (+19.1% YoY).
*   **H1 Reported PAT:** Rs 129 crore (+28.6% YoY).
*   **Net Cash Surplus:** Rs 304 crore.

### Highlights
*   Product business delivered "high teens" growth YoY.
*   Water Purifier business saw >30% QoQ growth.
*   Emerging categories (robotic vacuum cleaners, water softeners, air purifiers) grew faster than the company average.
*   Launched industry-first 4-year product range for water purifiers.
*   Opex ratio improved to 41.6% of revenue (-181 bps YoY).
*   Gross margins declined 138 bps YoY to 55.1% due to commodity inflation.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 growth to accelerate over H1.
*   Management plans a 3-4% price increase effective mid-November.
*   Management expects Q3 to have a transitionary impact on margins due to the lag in price increases; recovery is expected in Q4.
*   Management aims to maintain flat margins on a full-year basis.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Growth was broad-based across channels and categories.
*   Water purifier penetration in India remains low, offering significant headroom for category expansion.
*   Robotic vacuum cleaners are increasingly viewed as a mainstream appliance in Indian households.
*   Service business performance remained steady; management expressed confidence in demand revival.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Commodity inflation intensified, impacting gross margins.
*   Management refrained from taking pricing actions during the festive period to prioritize growth and customer acquisition.
*   Operating leverage was the primary driver of EBITDA margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Significant investment made in in-store presence (branded fixtures) and point-of-sale visibility.
*   Proactively increased inventory levels ahead of BIS norms implementation to ensure supply continuity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net cash surplus of Rs 304 crore.
*   H1 cash flow was impacted by working capital deployment (inventory build-up for festive season/BIS norms and credit terms for modern trade/e-commerce).
*   Management expects operating cash flow generation in H2, consistent with historical trends.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Commodity inflation persisting across key input categories.
*   Promoter pledge activity (Management clarified this has no impact on company operations or governance).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Siddhartha Bera (Nomura):** Asked about volume/value mix. Management confirmed volume contribution was significantly higher than pricing.
*   **Keshav Lahoti (HDFC Securities):** Asked about service growth. Management noted initiatives like "pre-filter check" and "Express Care" are driving engagement.
*   **Umang Mehta (Kotak Securities):** Asked about channel inventory/festive impact. Management confirmed inventory levels were normal and sell-outs were strong.
*   **Aashish Upganlawar (InvesQ):** Asked about promoter pledge. Management stated it has no bearing on operations or governance.

### One-line takeaway
Eureka Forbes delivered strong H1 growth and record quarterly PAT, leveraging operating efficiencies to offset commodity inflation while maintaining a positive outlook for H2.
