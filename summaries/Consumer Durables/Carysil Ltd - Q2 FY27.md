# Carysil Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited highest-ever second-quarter revenue, robust domestic demand, and strong order momentum following the US election outcome, despite macro and freight headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 275.7 crore (up 14.6% YoY, 5.2% QoQ)
- **Total income:** Rs 278 crore (up 13.9% YoY)
- **Gross profit:** Rs 149.7 crore (up 16.8% YoY)
- **Gross margin:** 53.9% (up 133 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 54.1 crore (up 9.3% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 19.4% (vs 20.3% in Q2 FY26 and 21.2% in Q1 FY27)
- **Profit before tax (PBT):** Up 10.3% YoY
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Up 5.8% YoY (Consolidated H1 PAT at Rs 61.1 crore, up 21.3% YoY)
- **EPS:** Rs 9.97 (vs Rs 9.56 in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 537.9 crore (up 15% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 110.1 crore (up 17.6% YoY, margin at 20.3%)
- **Quartz sink volumes:** 217K units (up 10% YoY; contributed 54% of revenue)
- **Stainless steel sink volumes:** 47K units (up 8% YoY)
- **UK subsidiary (Carysil Products Ltd) H1 Revenue:** GBP 6.2 million (highest ever H1)
- **UK surfaces (Carysil Surfaces) Revenue:** GBP 5.8 million (vs GBP 7 million YoY)
- **US subsidiary (United Granite) H1 Revenue:** $5.6 million (vs $5 million YoY, >10% growth)

### Highlights
- Sinks (quartz and stainless steel) together contribute 65% of revenue. Faucet volumes grew 8%, kitchen appliances 12%, and surfaces 23% for the quarter.
- Granite sink plant operated at 92% capacity utilization; stainless steel plant at 91% in Q2 FY27.
- Signed renowned actor Mr. Bobby Deol as brand ambassador for FY27 and FY28.
- Appointed PwC for leadership restructuring process (Leadership 1 and 2).
- Group CFO Anand Sharma is leaving due to personal reasons; shortlisted candidates for replacement.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 guidance of 15% revenue growth and an EBITDA margin of 18% to 20%, expecting to stay on the upper side of the margin guidance.
- Reiterated target to achieve Rs 500 crore domestic revenue over the next three years.
- Global launch of nanotechnology-based sinks planned for early 2027 (starting at the KBIS show in Las Vegas in February 2027), fetching ~20% value addition over current granite sinks.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic business grew 30% in H1; India contributed 21% of revenue (vs 19% last year).
- International partners (IKEA, Lowe's, Home Depot, Howdens, Hafele) maintain large shares; extended partnerships with Home Depot Canada/US, Hafele (Australia, New Zealand, Thailand, Indonesia), and secured first order from Amazon USA.
- US election outcome (Democrats win / political stability) expected to boost order momentum and avert tariff concerns; customers already scheduling factory visits.
- UK surfaces market remains soft due to a slowdown in new kitchen builds, though replacement markets (sinks/faucets) remain resilient.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin declined QoQ/YoY due to higher raw material costs and sea freight challenges. Freight and raw material impact estimated at 1.5% to 2%.
- Implemented a 5% price hike last month (effective fully in Q3) to combat inflation. Exports to major customers are on an FOB basis, limiting freight impact.
- Planning to allocate ~5% of revenue strictly to advertising/branding in India, funded by rolling back a 5% extra discount previously given to distributors.
- Employee costs rose due to overlapping training for upcoming expansions and hiring senior talent (CTO, project heads, engineers).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- On track to add 250,000 units of annual capacity for quartz sinks and 150,000 units for steel sinks by the end of FY27.
- Doubling faucet and kitchen appliances capacities to 100,000 units each by the end of FY27 to reduce import dependence and meet BIS norms (no imports post-March 2027).
- Appliances assembly expansion operates on an asset-light model with expected asset turnover of 5x to 6x of capex and target utilization of 80-85% next year.
- Planned investment of Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore in FY27 to expand brand experience stores from 20 to a larger footprint.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Raw material inventory booked for the next 3 to 4 months to secure pricing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unprecedented sea freight cost inflation and container availability issues during Q2.
- Softer demand environment in the UK surfaces segment.
- Potential supply chain disruptions and input cost volatility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Resha Mehta (GreenEdge Wealth) on export disruptions and Surfaces degrowth:** Management stated container availability issues were weathered with minimal slippage, while UK surfaces declined due to a soft new-kitchen market and a leadership vacancy (now filled).
2. **Vaidik Bafna (Capital) on US elections impact:** Management expressed strong optimism regarding the US outcome, noting removal of tariff uncertainty, a surge in new inquiries, and the upcoming Amazon US order ramp-up.
3. **Sanjay Ladha on rising employee and other expenses:** Management explained that employee costs rose due to hiring senior talent and pre-training workers ahead of the FY27 capacity additions, which will dilute once sales scale.
4. **Sagar Jethwani on EBITDA margin impact and India ad spends:** Management quantified freight/RM impact at 1.5%-2%, noted a 5% price hike implemented last month, and explained that a 5% ad spend will be funded by cutting distributor discounts rather than hurting margins.
5. **Nipurn Khemka on talent attraction in Bhavnagar:** Management highlighted top-tier lifestyle benefits, modern infrastructure, a sports complex, and a hybrid-friendly work culture that successfully attracts metro-based senior talent.

### One-line takeaway
Carysil reported its highest-ever Q2 revenue with 14.6% YoY growth, driven by resilient core sink volumes and robust domestic expansion, while anticipating stronger US momentum and margin recovery.
