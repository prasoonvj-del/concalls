# AJC Jewel Manufacturers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-15 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong start to the year driven by >124% YoY revenue growth, client network expansion, and proposed international UAE acquisition.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 101.38 crore (+124.69% YoY from Rs 45.12 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 4.64 crore (vs Rs 1.58 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **PAT:** Rs 2.38 crore (vs Rs 0.57 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 4.58% (vs 3.50% in Q1 FY26, up 108 bps YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 2.35% (vs 1.26% in Q1 FY26).
- **EPS:** Rs 3.94 (vs Rs 1.26 in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
- Added 12 independent, mid-to-small jewellery retailers in Kerala during Q1 FY27.
- Proposed acquisition of an 80% stake in promoter entity AJC Jewel Manufacturers FZC (Sharjah, UAE) for up to Rs 9.6 crore via a non-cash share swap (expected closing in 3–6 months with ~7–8% equity dilution).
- The UAE entity generated Rs 127.95 crore revenue in CY25 and Rs 72.46 crore in H1 CY26 (Jan–Jun 2026). Consolidations expected starting Q3 FY27.
- Upgraded the B2B digital portal and ERP platform with a design repository of ~2.5 lakh designs and established a 20-member in-house design and innovation team.
- Esthara Jewels (silver retail brand) currently has 3 operational stores in Kerala, with 2 additional stores under fit-out expected to open by next month.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Standalone Revenue Guidance:** Rs 450 crore.
- **Growth Outlook:** Targeting 50% YoY CAGR volume growth over the next 2 to 3 years.
- **Esthara Stores:** Targeting Rs 20 lakh revenue per month per store with store-level breakeven expected within 1 year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Geographic concentration remains high with 90%–95% of revenue currently coming from Kerala. Active marketing efforts are underway in Chennai to expand into rest-of-India markets.
- Demand seasonality: Q1 and Q2 are moderately steady, while Q3 and Q4 see peak volumes due to festival and wedding seasons.
- Management expects potential import duty structure amendments by the government to have no adverse impact on business demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sharjah entity delivers ~4% PAT margin and ~5% EBITDA margin, operating in a tax-exempt free zone.
- Commercialized 9-karat and 18-karat gold jewellery offerings alongside 22-karat products to capture higher margins.
- Gold price volatility risk is mitigated via supplier hedging facilities and MCX hedging platforms.
- The company does not engage in bullion trading operations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **India Capacity:** 5 kg/day (gold casting jewellery) and 8 kg/day (silver jewellery).
- **Sharjah Capacity:** 3 kg/day (gold jewellery).
- **Current Capacity Utilization:** India gold plant is at 25% utilization (~1.2 kg/day gold, 1 kg/day silver); Sharjah facility operates at ~0.8 kg/day (~27% utilization).
- **100% Utilization Potential:** ~Rs 7 crore/day revenue potential for India and ~Rs 3.6 crore/day for Sharjah at full capacity.
- **Esthara Store Capex:** ~Rs 4,000 per sq. ft. (~Rs 15–20 lakh capex per store); Opex is ~Rs 5–6 lakh per month per store.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables expanded due to onboarding larger corporate accounts that command longer credit terms.
- Currently holds no gold metal loans or advance gold loan facilities.
- Further working capital will be required gradually via internal accruals, debt, or equity fundraising to scale capacity utilization toward 100%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Negative Cash Flows:** Cash flow from operations has been negative for the past 6 consecutive years; management aims to turn this positive via scaling sales volumes.
- **High Promoter Compensation:** Promoter family remuneration reached ~10%–15% of PAT in FY26 despite negative CFO. Management stated future remuneration will be aligned with PAT.
- **Customer & Regional Concentration:** Top 10 customers account for 50% of total revenue, and 90%–95% of revenues originate from Kerala.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Aditya / Sahil): What are the financials and dilution impact of the proposed UAE transaction?**  
  *A (Management):* AJC is acquiring 80% of AJC Jewel Manufacturers FZC (Sharjah) for Rs 9.6 crore via a non-cash share swap, leading to ~7–8% equity dilution. The tax-exempt unit generates ~4% PAT margin and ~5% EBITDA margin, doing Rs 72.46 crore revenue in H1 CY26. Consolidation is expected from Q3 FY27.
- **Q (Pradyumna Singhania): What revenue can be generated at 100% capacity utilization?**  
  *A (Management):* At 100% capacity utilization (5 kg/day in India, 3 kg/day in Sharjah), daily revenue capability stands at ~Rs 7 crore/day for India and ~Rs 3.6 crore/day for Sharjah.
- **Q (Gaurav): Why is promoter remuneration at 10–15% of PAT when operating cash flows are negative?**  
  *A (Management):* Management did not cite a specific historical reason, but committed to aligning promoter remuneration directly with PAT performance going forward.
- **Q (Gaurav): Why expand capacity when current utilization is only 25% in Kerala?**  
  *A (Management):* The 5 kg/day gold and 8 kg/day silver capacity in Kerala reflects recent upgrades funded through the IPO proceeds to prepare for future volume expansion.

### One-line takeaway
AJC Jewel Manufacturers is delivering robust top-line growth by expanding its customer footprint and international operations in the UAE, though persistent negative operating cash flows and regional revenue concentration warrant close monitoring.
