# Stove Kraft Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the highest-ever second quarter revenue, EBITDA, and EBITDA margins, driven by robust backend manufacturing and strong seasonal demand.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained gross margin improvement targets (~100 bps YoY expansion achieved) and long-term EBITDA margin targets (12% to 15% range in the next 3-4 years).
- Tone and demand view: Remained confident, noting sustained momentum in induction cooktops (108% YoY growth) and strong festive demand.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Added discussion on the relaunch of the premium brand Gilma (17 stores operational) and investment in a China subsidiary (WOS and JV) for Triply circles.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: China manufacturing facility setup remains on track for commercial production by end of December or early January; retail store expansion progressed with 16 new Pigeon stores added (total 360) and 17 Gilma stores operational.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 585 crore, up 23.2% YoY (vs Rs 475 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 1,065 crore, up 30.8% YoY (vs Rs 814.5 crore in H1 FY26).
- Gross Margin (Q2 FY27): 39.6%, up 120 bps YoY (vs 38.5% in Q2 FY26).
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 75.3 crore, up 32.7% YoY (vs Rs 56.8 crore in Q2 FY26).
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 12.9%, highest ever for a second quarter, up 96 bps YoY (vs 12% in Q2 FY26).
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 29 crore, up 35.7% YoY (vs Rs 21.4 crore in Q2 FY26).
- PAT Margin (Q2 FY27): 5%, up from 4.5% in Q2 FY26.
- PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 46 crore, up 44.8% YoY (vs Rs 31.8 crore in H1 FY26).
- Induction Cooktop Growth (Q2 FY27): 108% YoY.
- Pressure Cooker Growth (Q2 FY27): 46.8% YoY.
- Small Appliances Growth (Q2 FY27): 9.1% YoY.
- Channel Growth (Q2 FY27 YoY): General trade up 52.2%, E-commerce up 24.2%, Modern retail up 11.9%.
- Channel Growth (H1 FY27 YoY): General trade 3-year CAGR at 16.3%, Modern retail up 27.3%, Own retail up 70.3%.
- Working Capital: Net working capital days improved to 32 days in Q2 FY27 (vs 45 days in Q1 FY27).
- Return Ratios (H1 FY27): ROCE at 15.6% (vs 12.6% in FY26), ROE at 10.4% (vs 8.3% in FY26).
- Store Count: Pigeon exclusive stores at 360; Gilma stores at 17.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever second quarter revenue, gross profit, EBITDA, and PAT.
- Induction cooktops and pressure cookers saw massive surge due to underlying demand and geopolitical/LPG constraint shifts.
- Relaunched premium brand Gilma in southern markets (17 stores operational, clocking ~Rs 1 crore a month).
- Planned total investment of CNY20 million (~Rs 29-30 crore) in China subsidiary (WOS and JV) for backward integration of Triply circles.
- Acquired a ready factory building in Baddi and applying for land adjacent to the Bangalore factory for medium-term expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects exports to grow for the full year and reach 15% contribution in the next 3-4 years.
- Management aims to reach a total of 500 Pigeon exclusive stores and 100 Gilma stores over the next 24 months (by FY28).
- Management targets EBITDA margins in the range of 12% to 15% in the coming 3-4 years.
- Capex for core manufacturing expected to remain below Rs 50 crore, while total cash outflow including China investment and land acquisition is expected around Rs 100 crore.
- Current manufacturing facilities can support Rs 2,500 crore to Rs 3,000 crore in top line.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Induction cooktop and infrared cooktop demand remains elevated following initial LPG constraints, creating a higher baseline of sales.
- Consumer life cycles for appliances are shortening, driving earlier replacement and upgrades.
- General trade and e-commerce remain robust growth pillars.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin improved by 120 bps YoY to 39.6% due to pricing discipline, cost optimization, and operating leverage.
- Aluminum prices have seen volatility, with historical increases of over 50% corrected by about 10%; management noted shifting toward stainless steel and tri-ply.
- Other expenses increased due to higher franchise commission payouts (store mix shift), volume-linked manufacturing costs, and job work for H1 demand.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding Baddi facility for pressure cookers and small appliances via a ready factory building acquisition.
- Investing in China step-down JV/WOS (capped at CNY20 million) for Triply circles to secure raw material supply chain and cost advantage.
- Acquiring land adjacent to Bangalore factory for medium-term needs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital improved to 32 days in Q2 FY27 from 45 days in Q1 FY27.
- Borrowings increased temporarily during the quarter due to strategic inventory buildup for the festive season and cash outflows for land/subsidiary investments; management expects positive cash flows by year-end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in raw material costs (aluminum/copper).
- Lag in export revenue due to transition out of PTFE cookware and setup of alternate product lines for existing customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Praneet (SJ Investments)** asked about export slowdown and inventory buildup. Management explained it was a transition lag from older PTFE lines to new products and a strategic decision to maintain uniform manufacturing and build inventory for Q2/Q3 festivals. Management did not dodge.
- **Rajas Joshi (Chrys Capital)** asked about manufacturing capacity utilization and capex rationale. Management stated current capacity supports up to Rs 2,500-3,000 crore, and capex/investments in China and Baddi are for capability addition, backward integration, and future growth. Management did not dodge.
- **Anand Mundra (Soar Wealth)** asked about Gilma pricing and China investment details. Management stated Gilma is positioned 30-40% higher than Pigeon with higher gross margins, and China investments are capped at CNY20 million for Triply circles. Management did not dodge.
- **Rachna Kukreja (SIMPL)** asked about aluminum price trends and competitive intensity. Management stated aluminum corrections are minor compared to past spikes, and the company's manufacturing scale insulates it from unorganized competition. Management did not dodge.
- **Haider Kachwalla (YES Securities)** asked about copper exposure and induction cooktop margins. Management reiterated strong sourcing capabilities and ability to pass on price increases. Management did not dodge.
- **Shreyansh (Svan Investment Managers)** questioned the need for capex given existing capacity. Management and Chandru Kalro clarified that investments in China represent strategic *capability* addition (backward integration) rather than just capacity expansion. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
Stove Kraft posted its highest-ever Q2 revenue and profitability, driven by surging induction cooktop demand, 120 bps gross margin expansion, and disciplined omnichannel execution.
