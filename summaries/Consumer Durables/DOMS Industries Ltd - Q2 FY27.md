# DOMS Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Margin pressure due to raw material inflation and temporary costs, despite steady volume-led revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- Operating revenue: Rs 667.8 crore (up 17.6% YoY)
- H1 FY27 operating revenue: up 18.4% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs 82.8 crore (H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 165.3 crore)
- EBITDA margin: 12.4% for Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27
- PAT: Rs 38.3 crore with 5.7% margin (H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 83.6 crore with 6.2% margin)
- Exceptional loss: Rs 10.4 crore due to heavy rainfall and flooding in Gujarat
- Volume vs Realization growth: Volume growth >80%, realization growth ~20% of the ~18% total growth
- Baby hygiene (Uniclan) revenue growth: 21.5% YoY; EBITDA margin at 6.4%
- H1 FY27 Capex: >Rs 200 crore; remaining H1/H2 capex guided at Rs 100 crore to Rs 125 crore

### Highlights
- Operating revenue grew 17.6% YoY, driven by volume expansion across domestic and export markets, new product launches, and capacity additions.
- Gujarat floods caused inventory and workforce disruptions, negatively impacting sales growth by an estimated 300 to 350 bps and resulting in a Rs 10.4 crore exceptional loss (insurance claims filed).
- Reynolds sales and support teams integrated effective July 1, 2027; temporary employee cost mismatch weighed on profitability as revenue contribution was limited during the quarter.
- Commissioned the first building (170,000 sq. ft.) at the 50-acre Umbergaon facility, dedicated to office supplies/pens (Reynolds); trial production of 'Jetter' and commercial production of 'Brite' and '045' commenced.
- Incorporated 'DOMS Seven Private Limited' as a wholly owned subsidiary for the Seven SpA joint venture (awaiting apostille and final JV agreement).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained consolidated sales growth guidance of 18% to 20% for the year.
- Long-term targeted EBITDA margin range remains 16% to 17% (baby hygiene segment at 8% to 10%), though management stated they will remain patient over the next couple of quarters before taking significant pricing actions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was broad-based across scholastic stationery, scholastic art materials, office supplies, hobby and craft, back-to-school (pencil boxes), and hygiene (diapers).
- Kits and combo grew marginally due to capacity constraints and high individual product demand.
- Trade sentiment for the Reynolds brand under DOMS manufacturing is very high.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material inflation is estimated at ~15%, with consumption impact at ~9%. Raw material prices reached peak levels late in the quarter and continued rising into October/November.
- Employee costs increased by 0.5% to 1% of sales due to the Reynolds team integration.
- Management follows a measured, granular pricing approach to protect volume market share rather than immediate price hikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested >Rs 200 crore in H1 FY27 on the 50-acre greenfield project (including land footprint increase from 45 to 50 acres, plant/machinery, and Reynolds assets).
- Guided additional capex of Rs 100 crore to Rs 125 crore for the remainder of the year.
- Future expansion in the greenfield site prioritizes writing instruments, wooden pencils, and office supplies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital cycle maintained within the guided range of 55 to 60 days.
- Net debt increased due to ongoing capacity expansions, IPO fund exhaustion, Reynolds asset purchases, higher working capital needs, and lower free cash flows from reduced margins.
- Management expects to require Rs 100 crore to Rs 120 crore of debt in the midterm to fund ongoing capex.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in raw material prices (crude-sensitive components like plastics and baby hygiene inputs).
- Temporary margin pressure due to lag in passing on inflation and unabsorbed employee overheads from the Reynolds acquisition.
- Weather disruptions (heavy rainfall/flooding in Gujarat).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sneha (Nuvama Wealth) on margin outlook and pricing:** Management stated they prioritize volume-led market share over short-term margin protection, targeting a return to 16-17% EBITDA margins over time while remaining patient for a couple of quarters.
2. **Aniruddha Joshi (ICICI Securities) on Reynolds integration and portfolio:** Management confirmed that Reynolds assets (moulds/assembly machines) were relocated to Umbergaon and cannibalization isn't an issue since capacities are aligned with growth; distribution network overlaps were restructured.
3. **Aradhana Jain (360 ONE Capital) on greenfield capex breakdown:** Management detailed that H1 capex went into common infrastructure, land acquisition (expanding to 50 acres), machinery, and Reynolds asset purchases, with multiple buildings in finishing or machinery-installation stages.
4. **Jinesh Joshi (PL Capital) on debt increase:** Management explained that debt doubled from March levels primarily to fund ongoing capacity expansion post-IPO fund utilization, Reynolds acquisition, and higher working capital requirements.
5. **Rahul Agarwal (Ikigai Asset Management) on peak debt and inflation timeline:** Management stated that an additional Rs 100-120 crore of debt will be required in the midterm for capex, and estimated that full inflation pass-through across the industry historically takes 18 to 24 months.

### One-line takeaway
DOMS delivered 17.6% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by volumes, but margins faced pressure from severe raw material inflation, Gujarat flood damages, and upfront employee costs from the Reynolds integration.
