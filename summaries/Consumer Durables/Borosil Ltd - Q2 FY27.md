# Borosil Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue growth, significant gross margin expansion, improving operating profitability, and strong momentum across categories.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 627.4 crore (up 8.8% YoY from Rs 576.8 crore in H1 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** Rs 408.9 crore (up 15.6% YoY from Rs 353.6 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit Margin:** 65.2% (up 390 bps YoY from 61.3%).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA (excl. other income):** Rs 107.7 crore (up 20.5% YoY from Rs 89.4 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 44.7 crore (up 11.5% YoY from Rs 40.1 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Segment Revenues:** 
  - Larah Opalware: Rs 210.7 crore (up 7.8% YoY from Rs 195.4 crore).
  - Glassware: Rs 152.6 crore (up 2.7% YoY from Rs 148.6 crore).
  - Non-glassware: Rs 240.2 crore (up 10.9% YoY from Rs 216.6 crore).
- **Net Debt (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 95.8 crore (Total investments, cash, and bank balances of Rs 67 crore against total debt of Rs 162.8 crore).
- **Solar Power Savings:** Annual savings of ~Rs 30 crore from Phase 1-3 implementation; additional ~Rs 12 crore per annum expected from Phase 4 starting Q4 FY27 (totaling ~Rs 42 crore).
- **Ad Spend (Q2):** 5.3% of sales (compared to 7.2% in Q2 FY27 [stated as Q2 last year]).

### Highlights
- DGTR issued final findings on Sept 24, 2026, recommending anti-dumping duty on Chinese borosilicate table and kitchenware glassware for five years (awaiting Ministry of Finance notification).
- Commissioned BIS-compliant manufacturing unit in Rajasthan (Stylenest India Limited) with two double-wall lines for vacuum stainless-steel flasks/bottles; production from a third line expected in January 2027. Hydra sales improved over 30% YoY in Q2 FY27.
- Commissioned captive ground-mounted solar power plants at Bikaner (35.8 MWp with 16.9 MWh BESS across three phases); solar now meets ~60% of overall power needs.
- Launched first exclusive Borosil brand stores in Pune and Gurugram.
- Adopted an alternative approach for the new glassware manufacturing project at Bharuch: Borosil Scientific Limited (BSL) will set up and fund the facility, while Borosil Ltd's long-term requirements will continue to be met via existing manufacturing arrangements with BSL.
- Board approved expansion of borosilicate Pressware glass furnace from 25 TPD to 32 TPD at Jaipur with an addition of a 3rd forming line (estimated capex of Rs 50 crore; expected commissioning by end of Q4 FY28).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to sustain current growth momentum and EBITDA margins.
- A Vision 2030 plan will be announced by the end of FY27, involving reasonable capex.
- Long-term target of 15% to 20% CAGR is maintained by management.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Glassware performance in Q2/H1 FY27 was impacted by the earlier calendar timing of Diwali last year vs. this year, affecting B2B/gifting timing.
- Non-glassware (small home appliances, insulated bottles/flasks, cookware) registered strong broad-based growth.
- October saw very good traction, strong momentum across categories, channels (trade, B2B, e-commerce, LFS), and good acceptance of premium product placements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins expanded due to price increases taken across categories, operational efficiencies, product mix/premiumization, and savings from captive solar power.
- Input cost inflation (fuel, raw materials, packaging) arose from the West Asia conflict, particularly impacting Opalware (where LPG/raw material/packaging cost increases were only partially recovered via price hikes) and initial ramp-up costs of the Hydra facility.
- Price hikes of 3% to 7% implemented in Opalware and other categories; further price hikes will depend on evolving geopolitical/gas price situations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Jaipur Pressware furnace expansion (BG 3): Rs 50 crore capex, expected completion by Q4 FY28.
- Solar Phase 4 under implementation: Additional 6.5 MWp captive solar plant with 5.0 MWh BESS at Bikaner for Borosil Ltd at an estimated cost of Rs 27.6 crore.
- Stylenest solar plant: 3.0 MWp ground-mounted solar plant in Bikaner under Stylenest India Ltd at an estimated cost of Rs 10.0 crore, expected to commence in Q4 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital increased due to inventory buildup (driven by Diwali timing, regulatory/BIS challenges, and earlier manufacturing ramp-ups).
- Government capital subsidy of Rs 1.2 crores received out of a total expected incentive of ~Rs 55 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and West Asia conflict driving volatility in gas, LPG, raw material, and packaging costs.
- Port congestion and supply chain challenges causing minor (~1 month) delays in machinery imports (e.g., third Hydra line shifted from December to January).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sucrit Patil (Eyesight Fintrade) asked about execution risks in moving into appliances and defending against price wars:** Management stated they do not compete on price but on quality and differentiated products, and each category is treated independently based on customer demand without cross-subsidy compromises.
- **Hitesh (Kosha Capital) asked if non-glassware growth was driven by restocking of insulated bottles due to low prior channel inventory:** Management clarified that growth is broad-based and not driven by restocking, as the full portfolio is yet to be rolled out.
- **Bhavin Rupani (Investec) questioned the sustainability of working capital and inventory bump:** Management explained that the inventory buildup was due to Diwali timing, prior BIS/regulatory stocking needs, and initial plant capacity phases, which will normalize with volume growth.
- **Resha Mehta (GreenEdge Wealth) asked why Q2 gross margins improved sequentially (diverging from historical patterns):** Management attributed this to product mix, premiumization across appliances/steel cookware, and price hikes taking effect.
- **Amrish Kacker (Karm Capital) asked about the timeline and financial impact of the DGTR anti-dumping duty notification:** Management stated the Ministry of Finance has 90 days from September 24 (until December 24) to issue notifications; the impact will primarily be a reduction of dumped Chinese inventory in the market rather than immediate price hikes.

### One-line takeaway
Borosil delivered a resilient H1 FY27 with 8.8% revenue growth and a 390 bps gross margin expansion, driven by price realizations, captive solar savings, and premiumization, while awaiting final anti-dumping notifications and scaling up in-house manufacturing.
