# Inflame Appliances Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported 21% YoY revenue growth, improved EBITDA margins, record monthly chimney production, and robust order books despite supply chain challenges.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 total income: grew ~21% YoY to ~Rs 92.3 crore.
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 12.3% (improved by 50 bps YoY from 11.8% in H1 FY26).
- H1 FY27 Profit Before Tax (PBT): grew ~35% YoY to Rs 5.5 crore.
- H1 FY27 reported PAT: grew 7% YoY to ~Rs 3.8 crore (moderated by higher tax provisions and lower MAT credit benefits compared to H1 FY26).
- H1 FY27 chimney production: 1,57,000 units (vs 134,000 units in H1 FY26).
- September 2026 peak chimney production: 40,000 units from both plants (previous record 32,000 units).
- H1 FY27 chimney revenue contribution: 89%-90% (vs ~94% in H1 FY26).
- CapEx guidance: ~Rs 10 crore.

### Highlights
- Shifting to the new premises is underway post-Diwali, incorporating online product testing and automated glass plant enhancements.
- Bent glass processing capacity increased from 800-900 pieces/day to 1,700-1,800 pieces/day; a new dark factory glass plant is targeted for completion by April-May 2028.
- A joint venture is being set up ~25 km from the Panchkula plant to manufacture BLDC motors (production starting May next year) and PCB lines (targeted for October-December next year) to reduce import dependence on China.
- First full container load of 600 hobs dispatched; other premium product categories (wine chillers, built-in ovens, dishwashers, built-in refrigerators, and built-in microwaves awaiting BIS certification) are scaling up.
- *Note on Record:* The call's emphasis on localized manufacturing and robust H1 performance continues the positive operational pattern noted in the company record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a revenue growth of ~30% to 35% for FY27.
- Management expects chimney revenue contribution to decrease to ~80% in FY28 and eventually settle at 60%-65%, with other product categories scaling to 30%-35%.
- Management targets reaching 45,000 to 50,000 chimney production units per month by the close of FY27, and an eventual capacity of 100,000 units/month over the next 2-3 years.
- Management targets PAT margin expansion to 7%-8% over the long term.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management stated festive demand is steady, with order books for December, January, and February matching July and September levels.
- The Indian kitchen chimney market has reached 2.5 million units annually and is projected to expand to 3.5-4 million units over the next decade.
- The hob market remains smaller (500k-600k units annually) with high import dependency for core components like burners and valves, making domestic manufacturing currently unviable.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material pricing pressure experienced in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 was successfully passed on to customers starting July, normalizing margins.
- Cost mitigation efforts include renegotiating Chinese supplier prices, securing better payment terms, utilizing alternate materials, and increasing production volume to cover overheads.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- A ~Rs 10 crore CapEx program is underway, funded via bank term loans for capacity enhancement.
- A new 40,000 sq. ft. automated facility is being set up for BLDC motors, PCBs, and an automated dark factory glass plant (operational by April-May 2028).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term borrowings increased from Rs 33 crore to Rs 40 crore; long-term borrowings increased from Rs 19 crore to Rs 22 crore due to term loans taken for CapEx.
- LC limits were recently enhanced by Rs 30 crore from ICICI Bank, bringing total LC limits to Rs 63-64 crore to support inventory and material purchases.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain disruptions: containers from China were delayed by 10-15 days three times in recent months, impacting production by ~10,000 chimneys.
- High import dependence: ~38%-39% of BLDC components are imported, requiring heavy inventory holding to mitigate supply chain risks.
- Bent glass rejections: high historical rejection rates of 30%-35% due to scratches/defects (being mitigated via new processing facilities).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Madhur Rathi asked about margin recovery and pricing pass-through:** Management explained that pricing stress in Q4/Q1 was successfully passed on from July onwards, supplemented by internal cost-mitigation efforts. (Directly answered)
- **Shrutika Kadam asked about plant utilization and FY27/FY28 guidance:** Management declined to provide separate plant-wise utilization figures, reiterated 30-35% revenue growth guidance for FY27, and projected chimney contribution to drop to 80% in FY28. (Directly answered)
- **Lalaram Singh asked why hobs are outsourced/assembled rather than manufactured in-house:** Management explained that the smaller market size, frequent burner/tooling design changes, and high tooling investments make hob manufacturing high-risk compared to chimneys. (Directly answered)
- **Meet Mehta questioned past guidance delivery:** Management defended the company's historical 40-45% growth and projected ~35% growth for FY27, pushing back on the premise that guidance was missed. (Defensive response)
- **Rahul Kothari asked about export strategy and new plants:** Management stated they have a presence in Dubai, plan to ramp up export focus in the next 7-8 months via trade exhibitions, and prefer expanding existing plants in Panchkula rather than setting up new regional plants. (Directly answered)

### One-line takeaway
Inflame Appliances delivered robust H1 FY27 growth driven by its core chimney business and scaling new categories, while pursuing backward integration into BLDC motors, PCBs, and glass to de-risk supply chains and expand margins.
