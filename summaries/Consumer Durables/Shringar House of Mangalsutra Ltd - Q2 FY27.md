# Shringar House of Mangalsutra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profitability growth, supported by festive/wedding demand and expanding corporate relationships.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** INR 518.7 crores (up 54.8% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Sales volume:** 577 kgs (up 10% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Gross profit:** INR 68 crores (up 53.1% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 54.9 crores (up 68.7% YoY); EBITDA margin at 6.7% (up 50 bps YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 39.5 crores (up 73.2% YoY); PAT margin at 4.8%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** INR 1,367.2 crores (up 58.7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Sales volume:** 968 kgs (up 10% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Gross profit:** INR 125 crores (up 34.5% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 103.9 crores (up 40.8% YoY); EBITDA margin at 7.6%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 73.5 crores (up 43.1% YoY); PAT margin at 5.4%
- **H1 FY25 to H1 FY27 CAGR:** Revenue grew at ~41%; PAT grew at ~49%

### Highlights
- Completed one year since stock exchange listing in September.
- Corporate client share of business reached 51% (up from 35% during IPO).
- Awarded "Mangalsutra Manufacturer of the Year" at the India International Jewellery Awards.
- Entry into the bridal jewellery segment gained encouraging traction, reaching ~10% of business volume within six months.
- Board approved setting up a wholly-owned subsidiary for broader value chain expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is positive on upcoming quarters, expecting record-breaking performance driven by the festive and wedding season and corporate pipeline programs (e.g., store expansions).
- Management targets scaling corporate business share from 51% to 65% in the coming days.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand environment remains encouraging with the festive and wedding seasons underway.
- Growing customer preference for organized and branded jewellery.
- Gold price volatility and uncertainty make consumers cautious, driving demand for lightweight jewellery, contemporary designs, and affordable products.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee costs increased 67% YoY this quarter, driven by higher headcount, hiring of designers/technicians, and scaling capacity (from 2.5 to 4 tonnes) and the bridal segment.
- Management stated that rising gold prices improve percentage-based manufacturing realizations and profitability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing capacity expanded from 2.5 to 4 tonnes.
- Board approved a wholly-owned subsidiary (initial fund INR 2-5 crores) to handle manufacturing, designing, trading, and distribution across gold, silver, diamond, and precious stones (focusing on B2B and online B2C channels for 9-karat gold, silver, platinum, and lab-grown diamonds under INR 50,000). Execution expected within 2 to 3 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flow management is a top priority; negative cash flow reduced to negligible levels in Q2.
- Maintains a hedged inventory of minimum 30% to 35% using gold metal loans, MCX hedging, and advance gold models.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility and uncertainty in gold prices make consumers cautious about spending.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Andrey Purushottam (Cogito LLP) on cash flow and B2C subsidiary:** Asked about cash flow targets and whether B2C expansion causes conflict with B2B customers. *Management answered that scaling corporate share to 65% will accelerate payments and improve rolling cycles, and the B2C subsidiary will focus on non-conflicting segments like silver, 9-karat gold, and lab-grown diamonds.* (Did not dodge)
- **Vinay Nadkami (Hathway Investments) on bridal sales and job-work:** Asked about bridal revenue contribution and job-work vs outright mix. *Management stated bridal accounts for ~10% by volume, and explained that job-work (such as with Tanishq) involves advance gold processing with no capital investment or top-line gold value inclusion.* (Did not dodge)
- **Vrudhi Vora (SAS Capital) on subsidiary timeline and repeat orders:** Asked about subsidiary timeline and repeat order percentage. *Management stated the subsidiary will be operational in 2 to 3 months, and noted that >90% of revenue comes from repeat orders via customer portal access.* (Did not dodge)
- **Vinay Nadkami on employee cost increase:** Asked why employee costs rose 67% YoY. *Management explained it was due to increased headcount and hiring of designers/artisans to support capacity expansion (2.5 to 4 tonnes) and the new bridal segment.* (Did not dodge)

### One-line takeaway
Shringar House of Mangalsutra delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 58.7% revenue growth and 43.1% PAT growth, driven by rising corporate penetration, bridal segment traction, and strategic plans for a lightweight/affordable B2C subsidiary.
