# Timex Group India Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA driven by strong performance across e-commerce and key watch brands.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 359 crore (47% YoY growth)
- **EBITDA:** INR 66 crore (54% YoY growth)
- **PBT:** INR 64 crore (57% YoY growth)
- **E-commerce channel growth:** 75% YoY
- **Trade channel growth:** 34% YoY
- **Exclusive retail store additions:** 4 stores in Q2 (total doors: 44)
- **Aston Martin point of sale additions:** 14 new doors (total doors: 100)
- **Brand growth (Timex):** 67% YoY
- **Brand growth (GUESS):** 57% YoY
- **Gross inventory:** INR 358 crore
- **Trade receivables / DSO:** DSO at 19 days

### Highlights
- Recorded the biggest quarter in the company's 35-36 year history.
- E-commerce operations span 1P (Flipkart, Myntra, Amazon), marketplaces (3P), quick commerce (all 3 portals plus Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, Zepto), and dot-coms (timex.com, helix.com, justwatches.com). E-commerce contributed INR 190 crore.
- Trade channel contributed INR 85 crore.
- Brand salience: Own brands contribute ~60%, licensed portfolio ~25%, and OEM ~15%.
- Launched two celebrity endorsements during the quarter: Tamannaah Bhatia for GUESS and Malaika Arora for Versace.
- Won awards including Watch Brand of the Year by Myntra, Best Fashion Brand by Economic Times, and Best Brand in Risk Management by CNBC-TV18.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management sees potential to reach ~20% EBITDA margin in the future, prioritizing absolute rupee EBITDA and top-line market share expansion over percentage margin targets.
- Intends to expand exclusive retail to 200+ stores over the next 3 years across Just Watches and Timex World formats (targeting ~40 stores next year, 80-100 stores in 2028, and 70-80 stores in 2029).
- Plans to construct a new manufacturing building at Baddi by May next year to double single-shift capacity from 6 million to 12 million units annually.
- Plans to launch an entry-level Swiss brand from the licensed portfolio and assemble top-selling GUESS watches in India starting Q1 next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Analog watch industry growth has accelerated to 14%-15% (historically 8%-9%), while smartwatches have stabilized at low single-digit growth.
- The addressable market of INR 2,000 to INR 10,000 is dominated by a duopoly between the market leader (Titan) and Timex.
- Watch ownership in India remains low at 15%-16%, presenting significant headroom for first-time buyers and premiumization/multi-watch trends.
- Women's watch segment (led by franchise 'Fria') is seeing high consumption growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Product gross margins dropped by ~1.1% YoY due to inflationary pressure on raw materials (movements accounting for ~30% of watch cost, copper, gold, silver), and adverse foreign exchange movements (USD peak at INR 97, Swiss franc).
- Selective price increases of 5% to 15% (average 8% to 8.5%) were implemented across all brands starting April.
- Marketing spend is maintained at 10%-11% of revenue for the Timex brand, and 8%-8.5% at the company level.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Baddi plant operates at 100% capacity utilization under single shift (~500,000 units/month), occasionally running double shifts.
- Capex for doubling Baddi single-shift capacity from 6 million to 12 million units is estimated at USD 1.2 million.
- Exclusive retail expansion will continue via the asset-light franchisee-owned, franchisee-operated (FOFO) model.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross inventory stands at INR 358 crore (including finished goods, WIP/components of ~INR 100 crore, and consignment accounts of ~INR 40-45 crore); inventory is intentionally built up ahead of festive seasons, Valentine's, and front-loaded rural wedding dates ('lagan').
- DSO improved to 19 days, the lowest ever reported.
- Business model remains asset-light with no immediate requirement for external borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential regulatory risks from the government regarding FDI e-commerce platforms (Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra) under multi-brand retail restrictions.
- Volatility in foreign exchange rates and raw material prices (gold, silver, copper, watch movements).
- Global shortage of watch movements sourced from Japan.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Inventory & Baddi Operations (Tushar Khurana, Peace Wealth):** Management explained that high Q2 inventory is a deliberate, recurring strategy to support upcoming sales momentum and avoid out-of-stock situations, especially given front-loaded wedding seasons and Valentine's demand. Baddi ran double shifts for a couple of months.
2. **Gross Margin Pressures (Aastha Jain, Peakay Advisors):** Management attributed the 1.1% YoY gross margin drop to raw material inflation, movement costs, and adverse USD/FX movements, partially offset by April price hikes.
3. **EBITDA Margin Targets (Ankush Agrawal, Surge Capital):** Clarified that while top-line growth is the priority, full-year EBITDA margins have the potential to expand toward ~20% over time through fixed cost leverage.
4. **Movement Shortages & Sourcing (Rochwin Correia, KRIIS PMS):** Addressed global movement shortages from Japanese suppliers by highlighting Timex's global scale negotiation power and initial plans for a partnership with a movement manufacturer.
5. **DSO & Receivables (Saransh Bagdi & Manoj Shah):** Management reassured investors that trade receivables growth is strictly seasonal corresponding to the record INR 359 crore quarterly revenue, with DSO reaching a best-ever 19 days.

### One-line takeaway
Timex delivered a record-breaking quarter with 47% revenue growth and 54% EBITDA growth, capitalizing on strong analog demand, e-commerce expansion, and brand elevation while maintaining an asset-light retail rollout.
