# Voltas Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance with 33% growth in Segment A, 45% YoY growth in RAC volumes, and market share leadership extended to 17.3%, despite geopolitical and inflationary headwinds.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated income: Rs 4,765 crores vs Rs 4,021 crores YoY
- Profit before tax (PBT): Rs 285 crores vs Rs 203 crores YoY
- Net profit (PAT): Rs 213 crores vs Rs 141 crores YoY
- RAC secondary market share (Q1 FY27): 17.3% (lead over nearest competitor widened to 4 percentage points)
- RAC volume growth: 45% YoY
- Segment A revenue growth: 33% YoY
- Segment B carryover order book: Rs 6,345 crores as of 30th June 2026
- Voltbek market share (YTD): 9.4% in Washing Machines, 7.4% in Refrigerators; Semi-Automatic Washing Machine market share at 15.6% (Rank 2)
- Sidra Bank guarantees canceled: QAR 167 million (equivalent to Rs 433 crores)
- Voltbek Q1 FY27 JV loss: Rs 37 crores (Voltas' share)
- Channel inventory: Around 4 weeks

### Highlights
- Sold 1 million RACs in 81 days.
- Launched AI-powered Virtis Split AC series in March 2026.
- Entered a binding term sheet with Atomberg Innovation Private Limited for a proposed 50-50 joint venture to develop and manufacture high-efficiency RAC compressors in India (capacity around 2.8 million compressors; 18-month runway to commercial production).
- Voltbek recorded its highest ever quarterly sales in both value and volume.
- Sidra Bank guarantees amounting to QAR 167 million (Rs 433 crores) were canceled following a court award.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Voltbek EBITDA breakeven to be pushed over by a few quarters due to commodity price shocks from the West Asia crisis.
- Full commercial production of the Atomberg JV compressor plant is expected in 18 months.
- Projects business is shifting focus to shorter gestation jobs and private sector projects to avoid government payment delays and fixed-price margin risks.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry secondary RAC growth reported by GFK Nielsen was 15%; primary industry growth estimated at 20% to 22% in volume and 25% to 26% in value.
- Commercial refrigeration and air coolers saw muted performance due to slower market uptake following steep price increases (Freezers up 10%, Water Coolers up 15%, Water Dispensers up 10%).
- Commercial air conditioning delivered stable performance.
- Channel inventory for RAC is currently maintained at around 4 weeks.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Overall cost inflation of 10% to 12% driven by BEE table changes (3-star up ~5%, 5-star up ~15%, weighted average 7-8%), currency depreciation, and higher ocean freight/plastic costs.
- Segment A EBIT margin was 5.3% (vs 3.7% in Q1 FY26).
- Cost increases were partially mitigated through price hikes, localization, strategic sourcing, and internal cost optimization programs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Chennai and Pantnagar manufacturing facilities operated at high utilization levels.
- Split AC manufacturing mix is 75-25 (self-manufactured vs OEM); Window ACs remain 100% outsourced.
- No major standalone capex planned; maintenance capex only. Atomberg JV capex will be funded on a 50-50 basis over FY28 and FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Exited the quarter with a strong liquidity position and well-controlled working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East causing commodity inflation, currency depreciation, and supply chain uncertainty.
- New order booking in International Projects delayed due to the Middle East conflict.
- Inability to fully pass on sharp commodity price hikes to the market impacting margin expansion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Indrajit Agarwal (CLSA) asked about the Compressor JV capex, timeline, and Voltbek EBITDA breakeven.** Management stated the JV capacity is 2.8 million units, commercial production is 18 months away, capex details are being finalized, and Voltbek's breakeven is delayed by a few quarters due to commodity inflation.
- **Achal Lohade (Nuvama) asked about industry volume growth and cost inflation/price hikes.** Management stated industry secondary growth was 15%, Voltas volume grew 44%, and cumulative cost inflation of 10-12% was largely passed through.
- **Aditya Bhartia (Investec) asked how QCO norms are managed until the JV plant is operational.** Management stated up to 30% can be imported based on FY25 numbers, capacities with Highly and GMCC in India have been blocked, and a blended sourcing approach will continue.
- **Sameer Gupta (IIFL Capital) questioned the UCP EBIT margin of 5.3% despite a strong summer and asked why a JV was chosen for compressors.** Management explained that cost inflation compressed margins despite scale, and Atomberg was partnered with due to their motor-design leadership, a capability Voltas lacked.
- **Rahul Agarwal (Ikigai Assets) asked if returning to 7% margins is difficult and about project segment margin trajectory.** Management affirmed that 7%+ margins remain an aspiration over the next 8 quarters through cost optimization, and projects are shifting to shorter-gestation private jobs to protect margins.
- **Ram (JPMorgan) asked about Voltbek losses and Electromechanical segment outlook.** Management stated Voltbek's share of loss was Rs 37 crores, and project segment execution will normalize from Q3/Q4 onwards.

### One-line takeaway
Voltas delivered strong Q1 FY27 revenue and profit growth with a 45% jump in RAC volumes and market share leadership of 17.3%, while entering a compressor JV with Atomberg to secure its supply chain.
