# Senco Gold Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sales momentum with 67% YoY consolidated revenue growth, despite headwinds in May and June.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: grew by almost 67% YoY
- Retail sales growth: >50% YoY
- Same-store sales growth (SSSG): ~39%
- EBITDA: INR 213 crores (7.0% margin)
- Consolidated PAT: INR 101 crores (3.3% margin)
- Diamond jewellery sales: +43% in value, +18% in volume
- Old gold exchange: 43% of total sales quantity (blended), >55% for owned stores
- Average gold price: +61% YoY, +1% QoQ
- ASP and ATV: ASP +40% YoY, ATV +38% YoY
- July and August sales: ~25% YoY growth, 8%-10% above May-June run rate
- Inventory days: ~152
- Store additions: 3 COCO stores, 4 franchise stores, 1 Sennes showroom in Q1; 12-15 more planned for FY27

### Highlights
- Achieved record quarterly sales crossing INR 3,000 crores.
- April contributed ~55% of quarterly sales (INR 1,500-1,600 crores) driven by strong planning for Poila Boishakh and Akshaya Tritiya, while May-June moderated to INR 500-600 crores per month due to elections, Adhik Maas, and heat waves.
- Shift in consumer preference toward lightweight, design-led, daily-wear, and lower-ticket-size jewellery due to high gold prices.
- Launched a new men's jewellery collection under 'Aham' combining titanium with gold and diamonds (priced INR 20,000 to INR 1 lakh).
- Factory subsidiary operational and enabling the launch of ~100 designs per day; Sennes brand remains in start-up phase (weighing slightly on consolidated PAT).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Suvankar Sen, Sanjay Banka) retained FY27 guidance of 20%+ value growth, expecting to cross INR 10,000 crores in revenue, and noted they will revisit guidance after Q3.
- Maintained sustainable operational EBITDA margin guidance of 7.5%-7.8%.
- Long-term target: INR 20,000 crores+ revenue and 300+ stores over the next 4-5 years, with EBITDA margin targeted toward 8%, PAT margin at 4.5%-5%, and ROE/ROCE approaching 20%.
- Q2 expected to be a seasonally softer planning and inventory-building quarter ahead of the festive season.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer demand remained resilient despite high gold prices and temporary headwinds.
- Old gold exchange driven by industry-wide efforts to support the Prime Minister's appeal to reduce gold imports.
- Grammage declined ~1% on a 24-carat-equivalent basis, but mixed-purity grammage increased.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Reported Q1 EBITDA margin at 7.0%; sustainable operational EBITDA guided at 7.5%-7.8%.
- Customs-duty gain estimated at INR 12-15 crores for Q1 (roughly 45 days of impact), with further benefit expected over the next 2-3 quarters.
- Other expenses increased from ~INR 80 crores to ~INR 195 crores due to marketing-related efforts, store renovations during the lean season, and customer schemes/offers (management stated this run rate will subside). Marketing costs specifically declined 19% to INR 35 crores.
- Finance costs rose temporarily in March-April due to constrained Gold Metal Loan (GML) availability, forcing local market procurement; GML availability has since normalised.
- Hedging maintained at ~50% under Board policy due to volatility, with long-term intent to move toward 75%-80% as conditions stabilise.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 8 stores in total during Q1 (3 COCO, 4 franchise, 1 Sennes lab-grown diamond showroom).
- 12-15 additional stores planned for the remainder of FY27, primarily via the franchise route with a focus on East and North India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory days stood at ~152; management is focused on inventory efficiency and software-driven store tracking while balancing growth requirements.
- Cash flow from operations expected to appear negative in the H1 filing due to accounting optics surrounding lower GML borrowing levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainty, gold price volatility, and fluctuating liquidity.
- Margins vulnerable to hedging levels, competitive discounting, and old gold exchange schemes.
- Sennes brand and Dubai entity currently in start-up phase, negatively impacting consolidated PAT margins relative to standalone figures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Viraj Mehta (Enigma Investment) on Growth Guidance:** Asked why guidance is conservative at 20% after strong Q1. Management responded that they historically guide conservatively and will review guidance after Q3, aiming to comfortably cross INR 10,000 crores.
2. **Abhijeet (Antique Stock Broking) on Other Expenses:** Inquired about the jump in other expenses to ~INR 229 crores. Management explained it included store renovations, customer offers, and brand-building expenses specific to the quarter, and this run rate will subside.
3. **Abhijeet on Finance Cost & GML:** Asked about GML availability issues. Management clarified that banks constrained GML in March-April due to duty changes and import curbs, forcing local gold procurement, but GML availability has now normalised.
4. **Amish (Knowise) & Vaishnavi (Anand Rathi) on Customs Duty & Margins:** Asked about the exact impact of customs duty on margins. Management estimated the Q1 gross customs-duty gain at INR 12-15 crores, which was offset by customer schemes, discounts, and gold price drops, leading to the 7% EBITDA margin.
5. **Yash (Yedyna Investments) on Hedging Strategy:** Asked if the company would move to 100% hedging like peers. Management stated they maintain a ~50% hedge ratio due to recent volatility and liquidity needs, but intend to gradually increase it toward 75%-80% as conditions stabilise.
6. **Madhvendra (Individual Investor) on Earnings Volatility:** Flagged unpredictability in quarterly margins compared to peers. Management acknowledged the feedback, reiterated their 7.5%-7.8% sustainable EBITDA guidance, and committed to improving predictability and shareholder value.

### One-line takeaway
Senco Gold delivered a robust Q1 with 67% consolidated revenue growth and record sales above INR 3,000 crores, maintaining its full-year growth outlook and 7.5%-7.8% sustainable EBITDA margin guidance despite near-term cost pressures and margin volatility.
