# PNGS Reva Diamond Jewellery Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue, gross profit, and PAT growth driven by healthy consumer demand and a shift toward natural diamond-studded jewellery.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** INR 121 crore (17% YoY growth, impacted by large gold scrap/B2B sales in Q2 FY26; jewellery-to-jewellery/ex-gold growth is 46% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue ex-gold sale:** INR 239 crore (76% YoY growth).
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Profit:** INR 37 crore (50% YoY growth); **Gross Margin:** 31.1% (vs. 24.2% in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** INR 79 crore (90% YoY growth); **Gross Margin:** 33.2% (vs. 26.7% in H1 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 29 crore (49% YoY growth); **EBITDA Margin:** 23.8% (vs. 18.7% in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 63 crore (103% YoY growth); **EBITDA Margin:** 26.3% (vs. 19.6% in H1 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 23 crore (84% YoY growth); **PAT Margin:** 19.3%.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 51 crore (151% YoY growth); **PAT Margin:** 21.2% (vs. 19.3% in H1 FY26).
- **Q3 FY27 festive sales:** Navaratri 10-day sales grew 35% YoY; Dussehra single-day sales grew 62% YoY.
- **Volume growth (YoY):** Diamond stone sale basis up ~28%; gold value basis up ~57% (including ~20% rate appreciation).
- **Average Sale Price (ASP) per invoice:** INR 1,05,000 to INR 1,07,000 (vs. INR 92,000 last year).
- **Store count:** 6 EBOs operational.

### Highlights
- Launched three new COCO stores in Q2 FY27: Amanora Mall (Pune), College Road (Nashik), and Jaipur (first outside Maharashtra). An additional store opened in Bavdhan on the call date.
- Onboarded actress Sharvari Wagh as the national brand face/ambassador to support expansion outside Maharashtra.
- Launched e-commerce platform `revadiamonds.com` for digital catalog visibility and customer education, complementing offline retail.
- CareEdge improved the company's credit rating outlook from Stable to Positive.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 performance to be in line with H1, though remaining cautious due to Maharashtra drought conditions (85%+ under drought) and potential inflation from West Asian conflicts.
- Management expects to complete the planned 15 EBOs ahead of the 24-month schedule (6 already operational/opening).
- Long-term target is to reach at least 50 EBOs over 7 to 8 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Observing a strong structural shift from plain gold jewellery to natural diamond-studded gold jewellery.
- Natural diamond jewellery composition: ~30%–40% gold, ~30% diamond, remainder making charges (higher risk-free asset component than past designs).
- Solitaires/larger diamonds account for 4%–5% of the book; 95%+ is design-led smaller star-melee natural diamonds.
- Direct threat from lab-grown diamonds (LGD) is limited to the 4%–5% solitaire segment; LGD is unsuitable for multi-stone intricate design creation, and customers prefer natural diamonds for rarity and resale value (90% buyback at company stores).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBOs exert near-term margin pressure (approx. 200 bps plus or minus) due to upfront marketing and operational costs before hitting contribution maturity in 15 to 18 months.
- Capex per EBO location is INR 2 crore to INR 2.5 crore (including 70%–80% inventory, plus infrastructure, air conditioning, and marketing). Inventory requirements range from INR 10 crore to INR 18 crore depending on store size (1,000 to 1,500 sq. ft.).
- Rentals range from INR 300 to INR 700 per sq. ft. (average ~INR 500), requiring 6 months advance rental deposit (INR 30 lakh to INR 40 lakh).
- Marketing spend of INR 2 crore per store is expensed directly through the P&L under IndAS over 12 to 18 months.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 15 total EBOs planned (5 small ~1,000 sq. ft., 5 medium ~1,200 sq. ft., 5 large ~1,500 sq. ft.).
- Expansion outside Maharashtra will focus more on malls than high streets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- IPO surplus cash not required for GCP has been transferred to outstanding loans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- 85%+ of Maharashtra is under drought conditions, which could impact post-festival consumer sentiment.
- Potential inflation risks stemming from West Asian geopolitical conflicts and struggles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital):** Asked about the job work vs. own manufacturing split and volume growth. Management clarified they are strictly B2C for jewellery (job work/custom orders are under 30%, ready stock is 70%+), with diamond volume growth at ~28% and gold value growth at ~57%.
2. **L. Sampath (Spark):** Asked about store capex and format outlook. Management detailed store sizes (1,000-1,500 sq. ft.), inventory mix (INR 10-18 crore), and rental deposits.
3. **Chirag (Keynote):** Asked about design sourcing and lab-grown diamond threat. Management stated 60% of designs come from vendors/contract manufacturers and 40% in-house, and dismissed direct LGD threat due to consumer preference for natural diamond resale/rarity in multi-stone designs.
4. **Vishal Gutka (ASK Investments):** Asked about SSG and gold price inflation impact. Management noted H2 will lack inventory gains from gold price appreciation, but targeted SSG for SIS stores at 35%–40%.
5. **Hardik Agrawal (Retail Investor):** Asked about long-term store targets and EBO ASP. Management guided for at least 50 EBOs in 7–8 years, and noted EBO ASPs mirror SIS trends targeting a 0.75x stock turn in 15–18 months scaling to 1.25x in 3–4 years.

### One-line takeaway
PNGS Reva reported robust H1 FY27 revenue growth of 76% (ex-gold) and a PAT surge of 151%, driven by strong demand for natural diamond-studded jewellery and steady retail expansion.
