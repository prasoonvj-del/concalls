# V-Guard Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth across all business segments driven by volume expansion and supportive market conditions.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Net Revenue: INR 1,853 crore (up 38.2% YoY)
- Half-year Consolidated Net Revenue: INR 3,664 crore (up 30.5% YoY)
- Electronics segment growth: 60% YoY
- Electricals segment growth: 41.2% YoY
- Consumer Durables segment growth: 20% YoY
- Sunflame revenue growth: 20.5% YoY
- Gross Margin: 34.3% (vs 37.6% YoY and 36.9% in Q1 FY27)
- EBITDA (excluding other income): INR 168 crore (up 53.7% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 9.1% (vs 8.1% YoY)
- Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 112 crore (up 71% YoY)
- A&P spend: 2.3% of revenue

### Highlights
- Electronics segment growth led by Solar Power Products, digital inverters, and batteries.
- Electricals segment growth driven by price increases in Wires (cables volume flat) and healthy volume growth in Pumps, Switches, and Switchgears.
- Consumer Durables saw sustained demand for induction cookers and broader kitchen appliances, along with strong growth in ceiling fans and water heaters.
- Functional integration of Sunflame is complete; NPD refresh is underway across the portfolio, though pricing actions in CSD impacted margins.
- Lighting category launched 3-4 months ago; currently present in a few districts (test market stage), targeting a complete portfolio by April/May.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is hopeful of delivering healthy growth in upcoming quarters.
- H2 volume growth is expected to moderate compared to H1, potentially landing in the mid-teens.
- Capex guidance for the full year is around INR 150 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- South India experienced an extremely strong, continuous, and extended summer, leading to low channel inventory and strong demand (South up 51%, North up 23%).
- Power outages in various parts of the country drove inverter and battery demand.
- Channel inventory is normalized across categories.
- General trade constitutes about 70% of revenue, while modern retail, e-commerce, and canteen stores make up 30%. E-commerce is flat to single-digit growth for their portfolio; V-Guard is under-indexed in e-commerce by choice.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin moderated due to higher input costs and product mix changes.
- Management stated there is still a 2% to 3% (or 3% to 4%) pricing gap due to input cost increases, and further price hikes of 6% to 12% have been/are being implemented.
- Sustainable EBITDA margins for Electronics are estimated at 16%-17% in a normal year, and up to 18% in a good year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Solar rooftop business focuses on 3 kW to 10 kW residential systems sold through system integrators. Manufacturing is currently outsourced.
- Innovation center primarily focuses on electronic capabilities and product reliability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail, but solar business working capital is similar to the rest of the company.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situation is being monitored for potential impacts on supplies and margins.
- Rapid and frequent commodity/input cost increases create a transmission lag, impacting gross margins temporarily.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rahul Agarwal (Ikigai Asset) on Solar ROCE/Working Capital:** Management clarified that the solar business is B2B2C (residential via system integrators), so working capital is similar to company averages.
- **Keshav Lahoti (Angel One) on regional disparities:** Management explained that the South grew 51% due to an extended summer and early solar presence, whereas the North/East (23%) had intermittent rainfall.
- **Naushad Chaudhary (Aditya Birla SL) on gross margins:** Management noted a 3% gap due to mix and pricing lags, which they plan to cover with upcoming price hikes.
- **Veenit Pasad (Axis Capital) on volume growth sustainability:** Management stated H2 volume growth will moderate to mid-teens compared to H1 due to a higher base.
- **Achal Lohade (JM Financial) on Wires competition:** Management noted the recent new competitor launch in Wires has not yet impacted retail availability or V-Guard's sales.

### One-line takeaway
V-Guard reported a robust Q2 FY27 with 38.2% revenue growth and a 71% jump in PAT, driven by strong seasonal demand and robust performance across Electronics and Electricals, though gross margins faced temporary headwinds from input cost inflation and pricing lags.
