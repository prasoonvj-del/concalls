# Stanley Lifestyles Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue decline due to geopolitical logistics issues and residential project delays, offset by management's confidence in long-term brand restructuring and consolidation.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 99.35 crore (Rs 9,935 lakh) vs Rs 108.61 crore (Rs 10,861 lakh) in Q1 FY26.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 17.22 crore (Rs 1,722 lakh).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 17.3%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 0.65 crore (Rs 65 lakh).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 68 crore (as of June 30, 2026).
*   **Gross Margin (Pre-Ind AS):** 56%-60%.
*   **EBITDA Margin (Pre-Ind AS):** 11%-13%.
*   **Corporate Cost:** ~Rs 0.90 crore per month.
*   **Rent Expense:** 10% of revenue.

### Highlights
*   **Store Network:** Opened 3 stores (all in Bangalore); closed 4 stores (3 in Bangalore, 1 in Mumbai) due to market maturity and location relevance.
*   **International:** Entered Sri Lanka via a joint venture with Singer Sri Lanka PLC.
*   **New Format:** Introduced "Stanley Superlative Living," a high-end, large-format store focusing on complete home solutions (design, build, and install).
*   **Corporate Action:** Initiated amalgamation of subsidiaries and step-down subsidiaries into Stanley Lifestyles Limited to simplify structure and improve resource deployment.
*   **Leadership:** Appointed Sudhir Iyer as Group CFO.
*   **Fraud Investigation:** Management confirmed an investigation is ongoing regarding fraudulent activity by the former Company Secretary.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Management** expects better quarters ahead once logistics issues in the Middle East are resolved.
*   **Management** targets 15%-20% YoY growth for mature stores.
*   **Management** plans to implement a "1 city, 1 mega store" strategy for the Stanley brand in 6 major metros, consolidating existing boutiques.
*   **Management** intends to convert existing Stanley Boutique stores into "Sofas & More" (value premium brand) to reduce market confusion.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **B2B:** Impacted by Middle East conflict, causing freight and logistics disruptions; orders are produced but invoicing is delayed.
*   **B2C:** Conversions are slower as 80%-85% of the customer base are new home buyers, and residential project handovers have been delayed by 12-18 months.
*   **Regulatory:** Management expects the implementation of BIS certification (effective Aug 14, 2026) to benefit domestic players by curbing imports.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Depreciation:** Management noted depreciation is front-loaded due to Ind AS accounting standards.
*   **Inventory:** Higher in Q1 due to seasonal forward loading ahead of the September season and European factory closures in August.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Manufacturing Capacity Utilization:** 68% to 70%.
*   **Capex:** ~Rs 2 crore per "Sofas & More" store; up to Rs 20 crore for large-format "Superlative" stores.
*   **Strategy:** Focusing on "design districts" in major metros for new store locations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Franchisee Model:** Operates on a cash-and-carry basis; no credit provided to franchisees.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Middle East war disrupting B2B logistics and export potential.
*   **Operational:** Fraudulent activity by former Company Secretary.
*   **Regulatory:** US tariffs impacting export opportunities.
*   **Market:** Delays in residential project handovers impacting customer conversion.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Saket Kapoor asked about depreciation:** Management clarified the high depreciation is an Ind AS impact, not IGAAP.
*   **Ashish asked about margins:** Management provided pre-Ind AS figures: 56%-60% gross margin and 11%-13% EBITDA margin.
*   **Nishant Sahu asked about the fraud:** Management confirmed fraudulent activity by the former Company Secretary and stated an investigation is ongoing.
*   **Arvind Arora asked about "Superlative Living":** Management explained it is a "design-build" format for homes with budgets of Rs 3 crore-Rs 5 crore and above, aiming to increase ticket size tenfold.
*   **Manjeet Buaria asked about store consolidation:** Management confirmed they will consolidate into one large Stanley store per metro, converting existing boutiques to "Sofas & More" to avoid brand dilution.

### One-line takeaway
Short-term revenue headwinds from geopolitical logistics issues and residential project delays are being addressed through strategic brand architecture consolidation and a shift toward complete home solutions.
