# Consumer Durables - Jewellery: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian jewellery sector is currently defined by a sharp divergence between aggressive growth and short-term margin compression. While the majority of players are reporting robust revenue growth (often exceeding 50% YoY), the underlying profitability is being tested by heavy investments in retail expansion, marketing, and platform migrations. The industry is witnessing a structural shift toward organized retail, with manufacturers and retailers alike scaling capacity and formalizing operations to capture market share from the unorganized sector.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is broad agreement on the strength of the festive and wedding season demand. Managements across the board—including **P N Gadgil**, **RBZ Jewellers**, and **Shanti Gold**—noted strong booking interest and positive consumer sentiment. A universal trend is the consumer shift toward lightweight, studded, and contemporary jewellery, driven by high gold prices.
*   **Divergence:** While most players are bullish, **Vaibhav Global** stands out with a cautious tone, citing specific macroeconomic headwinds in the U.K. (cost-of-living pressures) and U.S. midterm election uncertainty. Additionally, **PNGS Reva** flagged a localized risk regarding drought conditions in Maharashtra, which could dampen post-festival sentiment.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **The "Investment Phase" Margin Hit:** A significant number of companies, including **Advit Jewels**, **RBZ Jewellers**, **Shanti Gold**, and **Vaibhav Global**, reported margin moderation. Managements generally attribute this to "transitional" costs: marketing spend for B2C pivots, one-time store launch expenses, and platform migration costs.
*   **Margin Expansion:** Conversely, **Sky Gold** and **PNGS Reva** reported margin expansion, attributing this to a favorable product mix shift toward "Advance Gold" models and higher-margin studded jewellery.
*   **Hedging:** There is industry-wide consensus on risk management. Companies are heavily utilizing Gold Metal Loans (GML), MCX futures, and natural hedging to mitigate gold price volatility. **Deepa Jewellers** is targeting 100% hedge coverage, while **P N Gadgil** has raised coverage to 80%.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is in a massive capital expenditure cycle:
*   **Retail Expansion:** **BlueStone** (27 new stores), **P N Gadgil** (23 new stores planned), and **RBZ Jewellers** (Surat launch, Rajkot/Gandhinagar upcoming) are aggressively expanding their physical footprint. **PNGS Reva** is accelerating its EBO rollout.
*   **Manufacturing:** **Deepa Jewellers** commissioned a new Hyderabad facility, while **Shanti Gold** is scaling its Marol facility and nearing completion of its Jaipur plant. **Shringar House** has expanded capacity from 2.5 to 4 tonnes.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Cuts:** **Vaibhav Global** revised its FY27 revenue growth guidance downward to 7%–9% (from 9%–11%) due to platform migration disruptions and U.K. warehouse consolidation.
*   **Operational Shifts:** **P N Gadgil** is strategically shifting toward a 50-50 COCO/FOCO (Franchisee Owned Company Operated) model to optimize capital. **Sky Gold** has implemented significant governance upgrades, including new auditors and internal controls, following a fraud incident at its subsidiary.
*   **Promoter Commitment:** At **Deepa Jewellers**, promoters have voluntarily ceased taking salaries for 10 quarters to prioritize profitability.

## Company one-liners
*   **Advit Jewels:** Pivoting to B2C luxury with short-term margin pressure from marketing.
*   **BlueStone:** Tech-first model delivering 44% growth; dismisses lab-grown diamonds as a threat.
*   **Deepa Jewellers:** B2B powerhouse leveraging job work to drive 40-45% CAGR targets.
*   **P N Gadgil:** Strong operational performance with 57% EBITDA growth; shifting to franchisee model.
*   **PNGS Reva:** High growth (76% ex-gold) driven by natural diamond-studded jewellery.
*   **RBZ Jewellers:** 55% revenue jump; profitability impacted by one-time store launch costs.
*   **Shanti Gold:** 102% H1 revenue growth; margin moderation due to heavy investment in scale.
*   **Shringar House:** 58% revenue growth; scaling corporate business share to 65%.
*   **Sky Gold:** 61% revenue growth; scaling advance gold model despite subsidiary fraud incident.
*   **Vaibhav Global:** Near-term disruption from platform migration; guidance cut to 7-9%.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Margin Recovery:** Will the companies investing in marketing and store launches (Advit, RBZ, Shanti Gold) see the promised margin recovery?
*   **Festive Conversion:** How much of the festive demand (Navratri/Diwali) actually converted to revenue, given the calendar shifts noted by several players.
*   **Gold Price Impact:** Whether the "downtrading" to lower karatage (18K/14K/9K) noted by **Sky Gold** becomes a broader industry trend.
*   **Vaibhav Global Recovery:** Whether the platform migration issues are fully resolved and if the U.K. market stabilizes.
