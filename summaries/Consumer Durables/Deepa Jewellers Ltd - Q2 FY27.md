# Deepa Jewellers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Consumer Durables*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited strong volume growth, expansion in higher-margin segments, and successful commencement of the new manufacturing facility, supported by healthy demand from organized retail chains.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 626 crore (up 25% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 45 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 7.24%
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 37 crore (up 37% YoY)
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 5.96%
- **Revenue (1H FY27):** Rs 1057 crore (up 30% YoY)
- **EBITDA (1H FY27):** Rs 84 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (1H FY27):** 7.95%
- **PAT (1H FY27):** Rs 64 crore (up 32% YoY)
- **PAT Margin (1H FY27):** 6.07%
- **Operating Volume (Q2 FY27):** 809 kg (up 13% YoY)
- **Processing and Manufacturing Volume (Q2 FY27):** 491 kg (stable YoY)
- **Job Work Volume (Q2 FY27):** 318 kg (up 44% YoY)
- **Operating Volume (1H FY27):** 1327 kg (up 12% YoY)
- **Processing and Manufacturing Volume (1H FY27):** 783 kg (down 5% YoY)
- **Job Work Volume (1H FY27):** 544 kg (up 51% YoY)
- **Gold Hedging Ratio:** 94% as of 30th September 2026

### Highlights
- Commenced in-house manufacturing facility in Hyderabad in September 2026 (spread across ~6,700 sq. ft.).
- Planned opening of the second sales office in Chennai at the end of Q3 (December).
- Promoters Ashish Agarwal and Dev Agarwal decided not to draw any salary from the business starting October 1st (for at least 10 quarters) to encourage profitability and long-term growth.
- Customer base expanded to ~373 customers, including 47 jewellery retail chains (such as Joyalukkas, Kalyan Jewellers, Lalitha Jewellers, and Bhima Jewellers).
- Portfolio spans 16 product categories, ~110 SKUs, and >24,000 designs.
- First-half revenue contribution historically accounts for ~40% of annual revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a CAGR growth of 40%-45% for FY27, FY28, and FY29, supported by capital infusion (IPO proceeds) and seasonal demand shift into H2.
- EBITDA margins are expected to increase by 80 bps to 120 bps over the next 4-6 quarters.
- Gold hedging ratio targeted to reach close to 100% over the next 2-3 quarters.
- Studded jewellery sales mix targeted to expand to 75% over the next four quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- South India accounts for ~40% of India's gem and jewellery market.
- Shift towards organized jewellery retail chains presents growth opportunities for B2B manufacturers.
- B2C customers increasingly exchanging old gold due to higher gold prices, leading B2B clients to shift orders from invoice basis to job work basis.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins moderated in Q2 due to lower gross margins (shift in SKU mix favoring plain gold jewellery over studded jewellery) and a marginal increase in employee expenses.
- Studded jewellery sales constituted 57% and plain gold 43% of sales in 1H FY27.
- Job work conversion margin offers lower working capital requirements and no exposure to gold price fluctuations.
- Effective tax rate is 25.168%; lower tax rate in current year is due to timing differences on IPO expenses under the Income Tax Act.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New Hyderabad manufacturing facility capacity: ~1.5 tonnes annually on a single-shift basis, scaling up to 2.5 tonnes on a double-shift basis.
- Facility utilization progression: 13% of total volume processed in Q1, 21% in Q2, expected ~40% in Q3, and ~50% in Q4.
- Long-term ideal manufacturing model ratio targeted at 65% in-house and 35% outsourced.
- Planned sales office expansions: Vijayawada (completed), Chennai (December), and Bangalore (April-May).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Sum total of working capital employed (inventory days + trade receivable days) expected to remain in the range of 55-65 days.
- 94% of total gold holding hedged via Gold Metal Loans (GML at 3.25% p.a.), short positions on MCX futures, and back-to-back price fixing/unfixed purchases from bullion suppliers.
- Internal accruals, IPO proceeds, and existing debt deemed sufficient to fund working capital and growth up to FY29.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inventory days and trade receivable days increased compared to March FY26 year-end (attributed to seasonality and inventory upscaling at the Vijayawada office).
- Potential impact on gross margin from gold price fluctuations (management noted a 50% fall in gold price historically resulted in only a ~60 bps impact on gross margins).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Chintan Sheth (Girik Capital) on gross margin and realization decline:** Management explained it was driven by a SKU mix shift favoring plain gold over studded jewellery, which is expected to normalize.
2. **Riddhansh Chandak (Unified Asset Management) on hedging mechanisms:** Management detailed the 3-part hedging strategy (GML, MCX short positions, and back-to-back pricing with suppliers) covering 94% of gold holdings.
3. **Prince Chaudhary on structural EBITDA margin expansion:** Management cited shifts toward studded jewellery/vaddanams, higher job work contribution, stand-alone store sales, operational leverage, and advanced manufacturing technologies (casting/electro foaming).
4. **Hrushikesh Shah (Alchemy Capital) on job work realization drop:** Management reiterated that realization drops were due to product mix shifts (plain gold vs. studded) rather than pricing changes.
5. **Nainav Mohammed on tax rate anomaly:** CFO G.K. Srinivas clarified that the standard tax rate is 25.168%, and the lower rate in H1 was due to deferred tax timing differences on IPO expenses.

### One-line takeaway
Deepa Jewellers reported robust H1 FY27 revenue growth of 30% YoY, driven by strong organized retail demand and a surge in job work volumes, while commissioning its new in-house manufacturing facility to support a guided 40-45% CAGR through FY29.
