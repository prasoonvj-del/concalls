# Alicon Castalloy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Recorded highest-ever quarterly revenue with strong domestic volume growth, expanding market share in commercial vehicles, and fresh order wins in hybrid and non-auto segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (12% to 15% material-adjusted revenue growth maintained; looking to potentially revise upward in Q4 depending on volume trajectory).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remained highly positive; domestic automotive momentum accelerated (24% YoY growth adjusted for alloy inflation), especially in CV cylinder heads where market share increased from 13% to 23%.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Softening aluminium raw material prices expected (20-25% drop projected from Sept to Dec); entry into tractor non-auto segment with integrated cylinder head and air intake manifold.
- Last quarter's promises: Shikrapur/Pune new plant expansion remains on track for mass manufacturing by April 2027; FY27 full-year capex plan of Rs 150 crore progressing on schedule (Rs 70 crore spent in H1).

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 652 crore (up 50.4% YoY, 12.6% QoQ)
- **Material-Adjusted Revenue Growth:** 24% YoY, 11.3% QoQ
- **Volume Growth (Tonnage):** 19.3% YoY, 15% QoQ
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 65.3 crore (up 14% YoY, 18% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 10.0% (vs 9.5% in Q1 FY27; 12.1% adjusted for raw material alloy inflation)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 23.9 crore (up 26% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 17.6 crore (up 27% YoY)
- **Value Addition Growth:** 22% YoY, 13.4% QoQ
- **Executable Order Book:** ~Rs 6,700 crore (covering FY27 to FY32)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 322 crore (Net debt / annualized H1 EBITDA at 1.33x)
- **Working Capital Days:** 34 days
- **Capacity Utilization:** 82% to 84% on existing 50,000 MT annual capacity

### Highlights
- Highest-ever total quarterly income of Rs 652 crore achieved in Q2 FY27.
- Indian Commercial Vehicle (CV) cylinder head market share grew from 13% in FY26 to 23% in H1 FY27.
- Secured Q2 order wins across 8 parts from 3 customers generating ~Rs 270 crore annual peak revenue (~Rs 1,000 crore total lifetime value over 6 years), with 95% from PV and 5% non-auto.
- Secured a major hybrid vehicle order expected to double current hybrid vehicle revenue, maintaining ~100% supply share for hybrid cylinder heads in India.
- Entered a new non-auto product category supplying integrated cylinder heads and air intake manifolds for tractors.
- Machining share stood at 68% of total revenue.
- Revenue mix for H1 FY27: Two-Wheelers ~40%, Passenger Vehicles ~36-38%, Commercial Vehicles ~18%, Non-Auto ~4-6%; EV mix ~4-5%, Hybrid mix ~6-7%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reiterated FY27 material-adjusted top-line growth guidance of 12% to 15% YoY (tonnage growth expected at ~15%+ YoY).
- Target to improve EBITDA margins structurally by 1% YoY over the next 2-3 years, targeting 14% to 15% by FY30.
- Export share expected to rise from 13% in Q2 FY27 to 18-20% by end of FY27.
- Data center vertical sales targeted at Rs 3-4 crore in FY27, ramping to Rs 15-20 crore in FY28; tractor segment expected to contribute Rs 12-15 crore annually from FY28.
- Peak asset turnover of 3x to 4x expected on the new Pune plant (targeting Rs 120-150 crore revenue in its first year).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic automotive demand remains strong across PV, CV, and Two-Wheelers, with Alicon outperforming market growth rates.
- European market remains challenging due to macro and inflationary headwinds, but Slovakia plant shows sequential recovery supported by new eAxle and German OEM programs.
- Commercial vehicle OEMs are increasingly offloading casting and machining programs to external suppliers due to capital constraints.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material (aluminium) inflation is 100% passed through to OEMs, albeit with a cash flow payment lag.
- Softening aluminium raw material prices expected to decline by 20% to 25% during Q3 FY27 (Sept to Dec) due to new supply from Vietnam and Indonesia.
- Working on structural cost reductions (manpower, energy, rejection/rework rates) to offset conversion cost inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex stood at ~Rs 70 crore (including Rs 40 crore in leasing), against the full-year target of ~Rs 150 crore.
- Construction of the new Pune plant is on track for mass production starting April 2027, adding 12,000 MT of annual capacity.
- Overall annual manufacturing capacity currently stands at 50,000 MT.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables increased by Rs 88 crore and inventories by Rs 32 crore during the quarter due to top-line volume growth and higher aluminium prices; realizations expected in next 2-3 months as commodity prices decline.
- Net debt comfortable at ~Rs 322 crore; expansion fully funded via internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Drag from European subsidiary (Illichmann Castalloy) resulted in a ~Rs 3.1 crore PAT loss in Q2 FY27 due to mature program phase-outs; quarterly break-even requires Rs 30-40 crore in quarterly revenue.
- Seasonally weaker Q3 expected due to customer plant shutdowns, before a stronger Q4 ramp-up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raghunandhan NL (Nuvama Research):** *Asked for timelines on the execution of the new Rs 270 crore annual order win.*  
  **Management:** Stated that execution will hit peak volume (~70%) in FY28-FY29 due to development lead times, while 25 to 30 new part numbers across auto/EV will enter SOP over the next 12 months.
- **Kashish Shah:** *Inquired about the break-even timeline for the European operations (Illichmann Castalloy).*  
  **Management:** Clarified that FY27 remains challenging due to transition overlaps, but European operations are expected to turn EBITDA positive next year (FY28).
- **Riddhesh R. Gandhi:** *Asked about progress on the Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) EV order program.*  
  **Management:** Confirmed after visiting the JLR plant that component manufacturing has commenced, offtake has started, and end-vehicle deliveries will begin in Q4 FY27.
- **Hitesh Choudhary (Securities):** *Asked why capex should continue in Slovakia rather than shifting mass production entirely to India.*  
  **Management:** Answered that Slovakia provides strategic access to European niche OEM/racing programs that demand local high-tech production and benefit from lower wage structures compared to Western Europe.

### One-line takeaway
Alicon delivered record Q2 revenue of Rs 652 crore with expanding CV market share and strong order wins, maintaining its 12-15% adjusted growth guidance while targeting steady margin expansion toward 14-15% by FY30.
