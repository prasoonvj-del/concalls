# Rico Auto Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Negative - Management admitted it was the "worst quarter" due to significant losses from air freight costs and execution challenges, though they expressed confidence in a Q4 recovery.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 852 crore (Q2); Rs 1,607 crore (H1).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 9.3 crore (Q2); Rs 46.9 crore (H1).
*   **PAT:** Negative Rs 26.3 crore (Q2); Negative Rs 29.7 crore (H1).
*   **Export contribution:** 16% of total revenue.
*   **Segment mix:** Aluminum casting (89%), Ferrous casting (11%).

### Highlights
*   Profitability was severely impacted by Rs 40 crore in air freight costs incurred to maintain supply commitments for single-source programs (Toyota and BMW).
*   Logistics transit times increased from 5-6 weeks to 10-12 weeks due to Middle East shipping route disruptions.
*   Management stated that inventory levels have now reached desired levels; air shipments are expected to cease after November 2026.
*   The company managed 50 new product launches in a short timeframe, which contributed to operational "chaos."

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains the FY27 revenue target of >Rs 3,200 crore.
*   Management expects Q4 to be the best quarter of the year.
*   Management projects at least 10% growth for the next year.
*   Price negotiations with most customers are expected to conclude by mid-December.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Domestic demand remains resilient, with strong requirements from Maruti, Hero, and Bajaj.
*   Aluminum prices are showing a downturn, which management views positively.
*   The company is focusing on hybrid and electric vehicle (EV) segments while continuing to participate in ICE engine programs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Higher logistic costs and operating expenses weighed on margins.
*   Tooling costs increased due to higher carbide prices.
*   Raw material costs are pass-through, but other inflationary costs (labor, etc.) are currently under negotiation with customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   The company increased manpower and manufacturing footprint to machine and ship more components to mitigate reliance on air freight.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   No specific debt or working capital figures were disclosed in the call.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions (Middle East) affecting shipping routes and energy prices.
*   High reliance on single-source program commitments leading to logistical "chaos."
*   Execution risks associated with managing a high volume of new product launches.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Mukesh Mody (MODY INCAP):** Asked about the long-term strategy to mitigate freight volatility. Management explained the Rs 40 crore cost was a one-time expense due to supply chain disruptions and a commitment to maintain customer lines; they expect to normalize logistics by Q3.
*   **Bhaskara (Investor):** Expressed strong dissatisfaction with management's execution and repeated losses. Management apologized, admitted they could have been more agile, and stated they are implementing corrective measures.
*   **Rohit Kumar (ADM Advisors):** Asked about the industry outlook. Management expressed optimism for Q4 and the next year, citing lower commodity prices and strong domestic demand.

### One-line takeaway
A disastrous quarter driven by one-time air freight costs to meet customer commitments, with management promising a recovery in Q4 as logistics normalize and price negotiations conclude.
