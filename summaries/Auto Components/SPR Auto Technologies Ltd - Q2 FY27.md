# SPR Auto Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth across all business segments, successful integration of acquisitions, and robust order wins despite macroeconomic headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 16,746 crore (up 61% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 3,004 crore (up 29% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 17.9%.
*   **H1 Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 31,738 crore (up 56% YoY).
*   **H1 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 5,831 million (up 28% YoY).
*   **Standalone Total Income (Q2):** Rs 10,500 million (up 17% YoY).
*   **Finance Cost Impact:** Rs 234 million (Q2) and Rs 486 million (H1) due to Antolin acquisition debt.

### Highlights
*   **Portfolio Transformation:** Powertrain-agnostic products now contribute >35% of consolidated revenue; ~60% of business is insulated from powertrain shifts.
*   **Acquisitions:** Antolin India entities are delivering strong operational performance and successful integration.
*   **Order Wins:** Management highlighted new program wins across the group, estimating a mature year revenue potential of >Rs 1,000 crore over existing standard business volumes.
*   **Financing:** Completed a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) of Rs 10,000 million in August 2026.
*   **Sustainability:** Signed a new renewable power purchase agreement with Sunsure for Pathredi and Neemrana facilities.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects to outgrow end markets, targeting a long-term CAGR of 7-8% for the industry, with the company maintaining its historical outperformance.
*   **Margins:** Management aims to maintain margins through cost optimization, plant debottlenecking, and productivity improvements.
*   **Acquisitions:** The company is actively looking at further M&A opportunities in related adjacencies (e.g., thermal management, mechatronics).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Performance:** Weighted average industry production grew by ~17% in Q2, supported by strong demand in passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, 2-wheelers, and 3-wheelers.
*   **EV Penetration:** Management expects EV penetration to reach 15-16% across all segments by 2030, but notes that ICE engines will remain relevant due to multi-powertrain strategies (CNG, biofuels, gasoline) adopted by OEMs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressures:** EBITDA margins were temporarily impacted by elevated commodity prices, higher direct material costs (tools, oils), sales mix changes, and minimum wage revisions.
*   **RM Pass-through:** Commodity price movements are passed through to customers with a ~1-quarter lag.
*   **Energy Costs:** New renewable power agreements are expected to support energy cost optimization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Strategy:** Investing in capacity ahead of demand.
*   **Specific Projects:** Expanding in Pithampur, Pathredi, and Ghaziabad; adding capacity for precision injection molding in Neemrana; investing in TGPEL and Antolin facilities.
*   **Guidance:** Management declined to provide a specific rupee figure for annual capex, citing the dynamic nature of investment decisions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** QIP proceeds were used to repay debt; management expects interest costs to normalize from Q3 onwards.
*   **Working Capital:** Levels are described as "quite normal" despite increased manufacturing volumes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Ongoing global tensions and wars are driving up prices for direct materials (tools, dyes, oils).
*   **Execution:** Potential for lag in raw material cost recovery due to contractual timing differences.
*   **Aerospace:** Management noted a long gestation period (3-5 years) for aerospace entry, leading to a strategic focus on defense instead.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Capex Guidance:** Management refused to give a specific number, stating it changes frequently based on new business wins.
*   **On Margin Normalization:** Management clarified that the standalone EBITDA margin of ~19.9% is healthy and the recent dip is primarily mix-related, not structural.
*   **On Antolin Synergies:** Management confirmed they are "perfectly on track" with capturing synergies.
*   **On Defense/Aerospace:** Management clarified that while they supply to defense, aerospace is a long-term play with high entry barriers; they are focusing on defense applications for now.
*   **On Media Reports:** Management clarified that reports about immediate entry into thermal management/mechatronics were misquoted; these areas are on the radar but not immediate projects.

### One-line takeaway
SPR Auto Technologies is successfully executing a transition into a diversified, powertrain-agnostic mobility platform, leveraging strong order wins and a healthy balance sheet to fund growth despite temporary margin headwinds from commodity lags and acquisition-related finance costs.
