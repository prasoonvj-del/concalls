# Sandhar Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue, robust outperformance over industry growth, and improving operational metrics.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR2,999.57 crores (approx INR3,000 crores), up 27% YoY
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR1,618 crores
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR261 crores, up 24% YoY (Half-yearly EBITDA: INR261 crores)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 8.64% (Operational EBITDA margin at 10.2% vs 10.4% in Q2 last year)
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** INR123 crores, up 52% YoY (before JV profits and non-recurring income)
- **EV Business H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR18 crores (11,116 motor controller units, 19,209 battery chargers sold)
- **New Projects Investment (Slide 10):** INR540 crores, with expected revenue at 3x-4x of investment; current PBT loss of almost INR25 crores
- **Sundaram-Clayton Acquisition H1 EBT Loss:** INR14.38 crores (EBITDA loss of INR1 crore)
- **Overseas Business EBITDA Margin:** 10.02% and 10.64% in H1 FY27; EBT losses reduced to 0.8 million

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue.
- Sandhar grew at 27% vs overall industry growth of 21%; two-wheeler segment grew at 33% vs industry growth of 20%.
- Minimum wages impact caused a INR13 crores cost increase in H1 compared to last year; customer settlements are expected in Q3 and Q4.
- Management noted that trial operations and shifting for the Sundaram-Clayton acquisition are underway, with consolidation into a single facility expected by Q4 FY27.
- *Continues pattern from record:* Trial runs and mass production timelines for the new ADC facility align with the November 2026 record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated the aspiration to double revenues every 3 to 4 years.
- Full-year operational margin guidance maintained above 9% (specifically guided between 9% to 9.25% to 9.35%).
- Management expects H2 margins to improve by 30-40 bps compared to H1, aided by customer price compensation (with retrospective effect) and cost settlements.
- EV business aims to double its revenue from last year.
- Overseas business expects to cut down losses by 80%-90% compared to last year, aiming for profitability starting April 2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand across various segments with deeper customer engagement.
- Industry consolidation is benefiting larger, financially sound suppliers with engineering capabilities as OEMs reduce supplier counts and shift from unorganized players.
- Content per vehicle ranges from INR3,000–INR4,000 up to INR35,000 for a motorcycle.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins impacted by denominator effect from rising commodity prices (aluminum and steel) and time lag in customer pass-throughs.
- Employee costs impacted by a INR13 crores minimum wage increase, pending customer settlements in Q3/Q4.
- Sundaram-Clayton losses driven largely by unabsorbed finance costs and depreciation from running two facilities simultaneously.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex spent so far in FY27: INR191 crores (including carry-forward capex); additional cash outflow of INR150 crores planned for the next 5 months.
- Annual capex guidance: 5% to 7% of revenues (excluding new projects).
- Sundaram-Clayton facility requires 4 megawatts of power (1 MW released a month and a half ago, 2nd MW utilized in last 15 days); full power expected by end of December to complete shifting of low-pressure die casting and machining lines by Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management remains focused on prudent cash flow management, preserving liquidity, and disciplined capital allocation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unabsorbed finance costs and depreciation causing losses in new projects (specifically Sundaram-Clayton acquisition).
- Delay in full power allocation from the state government for the new Chennai/Sundaram-Clayton facility (though expected to resolve by Q4).
- Commodity price volatility and lag in pass-throughs affecting margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Kondawar (Complete Circle Capital) asked about EV scale-up and margin guidance.** Management stated they are on track to double EV revenue and guided operational margins to 9%–9.35%. *(Did not dodge)*
2. **Rucheeta Kadge asked about gross margin reduction and employee costs.** Management explained the impact of commodity price denominator effects and INR13 crores higher minimum wages, expecting settlements in Q3/Q4. *(Did not dodge)*
3. **Rudraksh Raheja asked about new projects (INR540 crores investment) and capex plans.** Management detailed the Sundaram-Clayton, Sanaswadi, and Oragadam projects, noting 3x-4x asset turns expected once stabilized, alongside INR150 crores pending capex for the year. *(Did not dodge)*
4. **Sangeeta Purushottam asked about the path to profitability for new projects and the Sundaram-Clayton losses.** Management stated that the INR25 crores H1 PBT loss from new projects is heavily driven by Sundaram-Clayton, which will be consolidated into a single facility by Q4, eliminating these losses next year. *(Did not dodge)*
5. **Andrey Purushottam asked about the Chennai power situation for Sundaram-Clayton.** Management confirmed requirement of 4 MW, with 2 MW now active and full resolution promised by December for Q4 stabilization. *(Did not dodge)*

### One-line takeaway
Sandhar delivered record H1 FY27 revenue driven by strong volume growth outperforming the industry, while management expects margin expansion and loss reduction in new projects as facility consolidation completes by Q4.
