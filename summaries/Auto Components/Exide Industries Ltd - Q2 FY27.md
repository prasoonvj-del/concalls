# Exide Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, successful commissioning of the lithium-ion gigafactory, and robust demand across core segments.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 5,026 crore (+20.3% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 10,331 crore (+18.9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 606 crore (+53.6% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 12.1% (vs 9.4% in Q2 FY26; +270 bps YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 12.2% (+130 bps YoY).
*   **H1 Pre-tax Profit Growth:** +46.5% YoY.
*   **Cumulative Equity Investment in EESL:** Rs 5,352 crore (including Rs 550 crore in current FY).

### Highlights
*   **Lithium-ion Milestone:** Commissioned 6 GWh lithium-ion cell plant in Bengaluru on September 23, 2026.
*   **Core Business:** Achieved record half-yearly revenue exceeding Rs 10,000 crore.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** 'One Exide' model has unlocked additional four-wheeler battery capacity, allowing simultaneous catering to OEM and aftermarket demand.
*   **Strategic Partnership:** Signed long-term cooperation agreement with ExCom Energy Solutions GmbH (Germany) to expand industrial battery/energy storage in the European Economic Area.
*   *Note:* The company has successfully commissioned the 6 GWh plant, fulfilling the milestone mentioned in the 2026-11-04 investor deck.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management aims to maintain EBITDA margins within the 12%–12.5% corridor for the next two quarters.
*   **Lithium-ion:** Expects to make the lithium-ion business cash-neutral in 2-3 years.
*   **Core Business:** Anticipates high single-digit to low double-digit growth in the second half, with aftermarket demand remaining steady.
*   **OEM Demand:** Expects OEM growth percentages to moderate in H2 compared to the high growth seen in H1.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Automotive:** OEM business grew >25% for the fourth consecutive quarter. Passenger vehicle registrations rose 27% YoY, and two-wheeler registrations rose 32% YoY.
*   **Aftermarket:** Fourth consecutive quarter of double-digit growth for two-wheeler and four-wheeler replacement segments.
*   **Other Segments:** Home inverters grew 20% YoY; solar grew 40%+; exports grew >20% YoY.
*   **Telecom/Railways:** Government tenders in railways and power sectors remained muted.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Input costs remained elevated due to geopolitical challenges (West Asia) and a weaker rupee.
*   **Pricing:** Implemented calibrated price increases (approx. 9-10% on four-wheelers, 5% on two-wheelers since Jan 2026) to offset input cost pressures.
*   **Lithium Supply Chain:** Currently importing raw materials from global sources; maintaining a 3-month inventory buffer to mitigate potential supply chain risks.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Core Business:** Continues to invest Rs 400-500 crore annually (matching depreciation) for debottlenecking and factory automation.
*   **Lithium-ion:** Phase 1 (6 GWh) capex is largely complete; focus is now on stabilizing lines, yield improvement, and working capital funding.
*   **Phase 2:** Future expansion will be triggered at ~80% utilization of the current capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Status:** Remains debt-free with healthy operating cash flows.
*   **Investments:** Reported a mark-to-market loss of ~Rs 600 crore on HDFC Life investment in H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Currency:** Weakening rupee against the USD continues to pressure input costs and could impact future capex costs.
*   **Geopolitics:** Potential supply chain disruptions for lithium raw materials; management is monitoring for any export/import restrictions.
*   **Lithium Economics:** A 5-10% cost gap exists compared to imported Chinese cells on a landed basis.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Hyundai-Kia co-investment:** Management stated discussions are in an "accelerated" phase but declined to provide details, noting a joint statement will be issued upon reaching a milestone.
*   **On Lithium-ion revenue/orders:** Management confirmed revenue generation has started for LFP cells (from October); NMC cell revenue is expected to start for major OEMs in Q4 FY27.
*   **On BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems):** Management clarified they are waiting for clarity on the PLI scheme before going "full throttle" on BESS, prioritizing 2W/3W and telecom applications first.
*   **On Price gap with China:** Management acknowledged a 5-10% cost disadvantage vs. Chinese imports but emphasized that strategic benefits (localization, second sourcing) often offset this.
*   **On Depreciation:** CFO clarified that the full impact of the new plant's depreciation will be visible in H2, as the plant was commissioned late in Q2.

### One-line takeaway
Exide has successfully commissioned its 6 GWh lithium-ion gigafactory and is delivering strong core growth, with management now focused on stabilizing lithium yields and maintaining 12-12.5% margins amidst currency headwinds.
