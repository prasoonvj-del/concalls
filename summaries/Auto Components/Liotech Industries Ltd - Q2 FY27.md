# Liotech Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management is confident in long-term growth and margin stability, supported by ongoing capacity expansion and post-IPO debt reduction.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Revenue:** Rs 33.64 crore
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 6.21 crore
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 18.46%
*   **PBT:** Rs 4.72 crore
*   **PBT Margin:** 14.04%
*   **PAT:** Rs 3.54 crore
*   **PAT Margin:** 10.51%
*   **IPO Proceeds:** Rs 36.02 crore (Fresh issue: Rs 28.89 crore; OFS: Rs 7.13 crore)
*   **IPO Utilization:** Rs 24.25 crore utilized out of Rs 24.28 crore net fresh issue proceeds.

### Highlights
*   First earnings call post-listing on BSE SME platform (June 2026).
*   Operates in 9 states/Union Territories with 150+ product SKUs.
*   Secured a domestic purchase order worth ~Rs 6.46 crore in June 2026.
*   Management confirmed no deviation in IPO fund utilization.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets a long-term EBITDA margin range of 15% to 16% and net margin of 10% to 11%.
*   Management expects H2 performance to improve over H1 as capacity expansion concludes.
*   Strategic focus on expanding into export markets (South Africa and Gulf regions) and increasing national brand presence.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Construction and housing sectors remain strong, supporting demand for architectural hardware.
*   Company is currently present in 9 out of 36 states/UTs, indicating significant room for geographic expansion.
*   Management noted that demand is robust, necessitating the current capacity expansion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Steel prices remained firm during H1; management follows a policy of passing on significant price increases (>5%) to customers while absorbing minor fluctuations.
*   EBITDA margin expansion (18.46%) attributed to reduced interest burden (post-IPO loan repayment) and recent selling price increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current Capacity:** ~8 metric tonnes/day (12-hour shift).
*   **Expansion:** Adding two floors to the Shapar facility; built-up area to double to ~12,632 sq ft.
*   **New Machinery:** CNC fibre laser cutting, bending, pipe cutting, injection moulding, and automated packaging equipment.
*   **Post-Expansion Capacity:** Expected to exceed 16 metric tonnes/day (24-hour shift).
*   **Timeline:** Construction and capex completion expected in 2-3 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Receivables: ~44 days; management stated they charge interest on delayed payments.
*   Current investment of Rs 10 crore (held in FDs) is earmarked for capex.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Short-term manufacturing disturbances due to ongoing construction and modernization.
*   Raw material price volatility (steel).
*   Receivables management (some delays noted).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Capacity:** When asked if they could just run 24-hour shifts instead of capex, management explained that modernization is required to house more labor (increasing from 8 to 20 labor quarters) and to install new machinery.
*   **Defense Tender:** Management clarified that a Rs 60 lakh investment in "licenses" on the balance sheet was for a defense tender requirement; the funds were reimbursed as they did not qualify.
*   **Employee Costs:** Management clarified that the reduction in employee expenses (Rs 80 lakhs in H1 vs Rs 1.2 crore in previous half) is due to comparing 6-month data against full-year data, not staff reduction.
*   **Margins:** Management confirmed they increased selling prices post-listing to improve margins and leverage increased inquiries.

### One-line takeaway
Liotech is in a transition phase, utilizing IPO proceeds to double capacity and modernize, with management guiding for sustained 15-16% EBITDA margins despite short-term construction-related operational friction.
