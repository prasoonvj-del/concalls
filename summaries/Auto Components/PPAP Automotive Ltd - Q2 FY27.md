# PPAP Automotive Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record quarterly revenue, improved profitability, and strong order wins despite raw material cost pressures.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall growth momentum and expectations for margin recovery as price pass-throughs settle in H2, without specific numerical revisions provided.
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, bolstered by robust industry sales and strong EV order traction.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Counterfeiting of aftermarket products emerged as a key challenge this quarter, which was addressed with countermeasures; raw material inflation and time lags in price pass-through remained consistent concerns.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The Chennai plant expansion is progressing to become fully operational in Q3 FY27 as anticipated; the slump sale for the tooling subsidiary (Meraki) and the Avinya merger remain on track for Q3 and Q4 FY27 respectively.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR177.8 crores (up 29.8% YoY) for Q2; INR334.2 crores (up 31.8% YoY) for H1 FY27
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR15.7 crores (up 23% YoY) for Q2; INR28.1 crores (up 27.4% YoY) for H1 FY27
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.8% for Q2; 8.4% for H1 FY27
- **Consolidated Gross Profit:** INR70.8 crores (up 13.4% YoY) for Q2; INR136 crores (up 18.1% YoY) for H1
- **Consolidated Gross Margin:** 39.8% for Q2; 40.7% for H1 FY27
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR2.5 crores for Q2 (vs loss of INR5 lakhs YoY); INR3.3 crores for H1 (vs loss of INR2.3 crores YoY)
- **Standalone Revenue:** INR165.1 crores (up 24.4% YoY) for Q2; INR309.3 crores (up 26.7% YoY) for H1 FY27
- **Standalone EBITDA:** INR14.9 crores (up 11.7% YoY) for Q2; INR27.0 crores (up 16.8% YoY) for H1 FY27
- **Standalone PAT:** INR3.5 crores (up 78.9% YoY) for Q2; INR5.8 crores (3.7x YoY) for H1 FY27
- **Capacity Utilisation:** Approximately 78% for Q2 (vs 73% in Q1 FY27 and 68% in Q2 FY26); Tooling business utilization at 86%; Battery business utilization at 15%
- **Order Wins:** Lifetime order wins of INR300 crores in Q2; cumulative H1 order wins of INR429 crores (including EV-related orders of INR156.5 crores)
- **Tooling Business Revenue:** INR8.3 crores (up ~60% YoY)
- **Industrial Product Revenue:** INR2.8 crores (up ~64% YoY)
- **Battery Business Revenue:** INR6.6 crores for Q2; EBITDA loss narrowed to INR30 lakhs (vs INR1.2 crores loss YoY)
- **Net Finance Costs:** INR6.12 crores for H1 (down ~28% YoY)
- **Interim Dividend:** INR1.5 per equity share

### Highlights
- Secured lifetime order wins of approximately INR300 crores in Q2, taking H1 total to INR429 crores. EV-related order wins reached INR156.5 crores in H1.
- Chennai plant investments are expected to become fully operational by Q3 FY27.
- Slump sale of the tooling business into 100% subsidiary Meraki Precision Tool Engineering Limited expected to complete by end of Q3 FY27.
- Proposed merger of Avinya Batteries Limited with PPAP expected to complete by end of Q4 FY27.
- Developed approximately 400 new SKUs entering production over the next 12 months.
- Aftermarket business launched 79 new products in Q2 (active SKUs at 1,337; 154 distributors).
- Board declared an interim dividend of INR1.5 per equity share.
- Addressed product counterfeiting in the aftermarket segment through newspaper notifications and packaging countermeasures.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects margin pressure to ease progressively in H2 as pending price revisions are approved and cost recoveries are realized.
- Contribution of new orders to top line is expected to grow from 10% to 15% in H2.
- Expects battery business to achieve a zero EBITDA situation by Q3 FY27, eliminating operating losses.
- Targets growing aftermarket contribution from 5% to approximately 10% of group sales over coming years.
- Targets scaling the Industrial Products business into a more meaningful contributor to group revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic automotive sales grew 18.2% YoY to 83.88 lakh units in Q2, supported by GST 2.0, rural/urban demand, and replacement demand.
- SIAM expects positive growth momentum to continue in H2 FY27 supported by festive demand, new launches, infrastructure development, and fleet replacement.
- Management noted potential challenges from inflationary pressures, interest rate hikes, and geopolitical uncertainties.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins moderated due to elevated raw material costs and a higher contribution from tooling and battery businesses to the revenue mix.
- Time lag exists in passing on raw material cost increases to OEMs; 68% of the part-side gross margin reduction has already been recovered, with balance negotiations taking another 6 months.
- Net finance costs reduced by ~28% YoY to INR6.12 crores in H1 due to treasury income from JV divestment proceeds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Chennai plant expected to be fully operational by Q3 FY27; further capacity expansion investments to be evaluated in Q3 based on order book and utilization.
- Expanding presence in Sambhaji Nagar.
- Planning to establish 2 additional regional warehouses (West and South regions) by Q4 FY27 for the aftermarket business.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Proceeds from JV divestment invested in short-term funds pending strategic deployment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated raw material costs and time lags in OEM cost pass-throughs.
- Macroeconomic landscape including inflationary pressures, potential interest rate hikes, and geopolitical uncertainties.
- Battery business operating environment remains competitive with cell price volatility, supplier quality consistency, pricing pressures, and short delivery timelines.
- Counterfeiting of aftermarket products in the market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mihir (MB Securities) asked about the timeline for order execution and gross margin recovery.** Management stated orders are executed over 1 to 4 years, and margin recovery is expected by Q1 of next year as price negotiations settle.
2. **Payal Shah (Billion Securities) asked if EBITDA margins will improve with higher capacity utilization and price recovery.** Management confirmed that H2 margins will benefit from higher demand, new SOPs, and price recoveries, though exact quantification is difficult due to raw material volatility.
3. **Vanshi Shah (EVNA Financial Advisory) asked about customer traction in Industrial Products and export scaling.** Management explained the focus on sealing systems for air handling units, electric panels, and data centers, alongside ongoing exports to the US and Africa.
4. **Vanshi Shah asked about battery business milestones toward breakeven.** Management stated Q2 EBITDA losses narrowed to INR30 lakhs and expects zero EBITDA in Q3 FY27.
5. **Jigar Shah (Elevate Research) asked about customer base expansion with new relationships like JSW and MG.** Management confirmed receiving orders from JSW (standalone and Windsor vehicle via JSW MG Motors) and supplying to VinFast.
6. **Jigar Shah asked about aftermarket distribution and product expansion targets.** Management highlighted the target of reaching 10% of group revenue, adding regional warehouses in the West and South, and taking countermeasures against product counterfeiting.

### One-line takeaway
PPAP Automotive delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 29.8% YoY revenue growth and a return to profitability, driven by robust order wins and execution, despite gross margin pressures from raw material inflation.
