# UNO Minda Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth, driven by volume expansion and successful capacity ramp-ups, despite inflationary pressures.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 6,655 crore (up 38% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 733 crore (up 33% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 11%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 420 crore (up 38% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 2,415 crore (vs Rs 2,179 crore as of March 31, 2026).
*   **ROCE:** 20.7% (annualized H1 FY27); 24.6% excluding non-deployed assets.

### Highlights
*   **Broad-based growth:** Revenue growth driven by switches, lighting, alloy wheels, seating, and EV systems.
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Aggregate installed capacity for four-wheeler alloy wheels reached 6.3 million wheels/annum; two-wheeler alloy wheel capacity reached 9 million wheels/annum.
*   **New Projects:** 16 projects are currently under execution or ramp-up phase.
*   **Sunroof:** Board approved additional Rs 85 crore capex for a sunroof facility at Bawal to serve incremental orders; commissioning expected by Q2 FY28.
*   **Record Performance:** Management highlighted highest-ever quarterly revenue and profit.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management maintained the full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 11% (+/- 50 bps), despite commodity price volatility.
*   **Growth:** Expects growth momentum to sustain in H2 FY27, supported by the festive season, wedding season, and new launches.
*   **Asset Turns:** Targets an asset turn of 1.8x to 2x for new capex projects, with a target ROCE of 18%-20% (pre-tax) by the third full year of production.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Trends:** Indian automotive industry saw highest-ever quarterly production in PV and two-wheeler segments. Total production crossed 109 lakh units (+18% YoY).
*   **EV Adoption:** EV penetration reached 8% in passenger vehicles and 11% in two-wheelers.
*   **Exports:** Total vehicle exports grew 23.9% YoY, though PV exports declined 5.2% due to the West Asia crisis.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Commodity Impact:** Aluminum business faced a commodity price impact of approximately Rs 260-270 crore.
*   **Pass-through:** Management reiterated that commodity price fluctuations are passed through to customers, though high prices are margin-dilutive in the short term.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Employee expenses as a percentage of sales were at a low of 11.3%, reflecting benefits of operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Strategic Capex:** In August 2026, the board approved four strategic projects totaling Rs 1,415 crore, including a greenfield aluminum die casting facility at Hosur (Rs 510 crore) and a new facility at CSN by TGSIN JV (Rs 670 crore).
*   **Kharkhoda Plant:** Phase one of the four-wheel alloy wheel facility at Kharkhoda is operational; depreciation and finance costs increased due to its capitalization.
*   **Future Capacity:** Total alloy wheel capacity (two-wheeler) is expected to reach 11.8 million wheels/annum post-expansion at Kharkhoda and Hosur.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Net debt-to-equity ratio stands at 0.31.
*   **Cash Flow:** H1 FY27 cash flow from operations was Rs 711 crore; free cash flow after sustaining capex (Rs 307 crore) was Rs 404 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Environment:** Geopolitical conflicts continue to pressure energy/commodity prices and disrupt logistics.
*   **Inflation:** RBI raised inflation projections to 5.2% and repo rate by 25 bps to 5.5%.
*   **Project Costs:** New plant commissioning (e.g., TGSIN JV) initially impacts profitability due to fixed costs, depreciation, and interest.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Margin trajectory in H2 FY27 given softening input costs?** A: Management stated they pass through commodity costs both ways; they are holding the 11% margin guidance despite the dilutive impact of high commodity prices.
*   **Q: Reason for strong growth in four-wheeler alloy wheels?** A: Driven by capacity ramp-up (Kharkhoda), increased wallet share, and favorable customer mix.
*   **Q: Update on Inovance business ramp-up?** A: Project is under execution; expected to commission by the end of the current fiscal year in Khed City.
*   **Q: Update on sunroof customers?** A: Management declined to name the customer, noting it is an established player and they will share details post-launch.
*   **Q: Update on humanoid work?** A: Management stated they have no information on this and do not know where the query originated.

### One-line takeaway
Uno Minda delivered record quarterly performance with 38% revenue growth, maintaining margin guidance despite commodity headwinds while aggressively scaling capacity across core and EV segments.
