# Emmforce Autotech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth in the core business despite external cost headwinds and subsidiary losses.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 92.5 crore (up 73% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 16 crore (up 44% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 17%
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 6 crore (up 54% YoY)
- **Standalone Revenue:** INR 84 crore (up 71% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** INR 16 crore (Margin: ~19.5%)
- **Standalone PAT:** INR 9 crore (~11% of sales)
- **Subsidiary (Emmforce Mobility) Revenue:** INR 14.5 crore (External: INR 8.6 crore)
- **Subsidiary PBT Loss:** INR 2.8 crore (H1)
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Negative INR 5.9 crore
- **Receivables:** INR 63 crore (as of September) vs. INR 34 crore (as of March)
- **Capex:** INR 3 crore in H1 FY27 vs. INR 18 crore in H1 FY26
- **Installed Capacity:** INR 350 crore
- **US Program Revenue (FY27 expected):** INR 60 crore
- **Consolidated Guidance FY27:** Revenue INR 185 crore (trying for INR 195 crore); PAT INR 14 crore
- **Standalone Guidance FY27:** Revenue INR 165 crore; PAT INR 18 crore
- **Subsidiary Guidance FY27:** Revenue INR 20 crore (reduced from INR 30 crore)
- **Guidance FY28:** Emmforce Autotech Revenue INR 195 crore; Emmforce Mobility Revenue INR 40 crore

### Highlights
- OEM business share increased from 7% in FY26 to 41% in H1 FY27, driven by the US program.
- Absorbed raw material, consumables, power, gas, and ocean freight cost spikes in H1 without immediate price hikes due to expected short-term war impact.
- Shifted strategy for Emmforce Mobility from contract manufacturing (e.g., Mahindra) to focusing on own brand ("EMMFarm" rotavators launched in Maharashtra) and private labeling.
- Lot size reduced to 600.
- **Record check:** Unlike past periods where capacity expansions were ongoing, management noted capex is mostly behind with INR 3 crore spent in H1, and future growth will use existing capacity (INR 350 crore installed capacity).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided consolidated revenue of INR 185 crore (targeting INR 195 crore) and consolidated PAT of INR 14 crore for FY27.
- Standalone guidance for FY27 maintained at INR 165 crore revenue and INR 18 crore PAT.
- FY28 outlook: Emmforce Autotech revenue around INR 195 crore; Emmforce Mobility revenue around INR 40 crore.
- Subsidiary expects no cash loss in H2 FY27 and break-plus in FY28.
- Merger of Emmforce Automotives into Emmforce Autotech to be announced upon Board approval; company eligible to move to Main Board by May 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- US program running at full speed; long-term US OEM order worth INR 10.5 crore/year awaiting production start next quarter (PPAP samples submitted).
- Two additional US customers testing parts (potential INR 10 crore/year); first orders expected January 2027.
- Hydraulic gear pumps shipments expected to start in Q4.
- Agri business facing a staggered monsoon impact, but demand for "EMMFarm" rotavators and blades is picking up through distributors.
- European market traction being built following exhibition participation; potential numbers expected by FY28.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin at 19.5% (below normal 20% to 22% range) due to absorbed cost increases (tungsten, cobalt, steel, aluminum, copper, and doubled ocean freight).
- Price revisions being sought from customers effective December 1/15, 2026, to restore standalone margins to 20% to 22%.
- Ocean freight rates have started coming down.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex stood at INR 3 crore (vs. INR 18 crore in H1 FY26).
- Installed capacity is approximately INR 350 crore (including a 1,600-ton forging press).
- 1,600-ton forging press expected to have no spare capacity in FY28 due to new orders from an associate company.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows negative by INR 5.9 crore due to working capital build-up.
- Receivables increased from INR 34 crore in March to INR 63 crore in September, driven by the US program's 6-month cash cycle (stocking in US warehouses, transit, and payment terms).
- Expected receivables by year-end: around INR 50 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asian conflict disruptions increasing raw material, consumable, and freight costs.
- TAFE (major agri customer) experienced labor unrest for 2 months, delaying blade and part supplies.
- Staggered monsoon causing a slowdown in the agri market.
- Elongated working capital cycle for the US program (6-month cycle).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankit Gupta (Bamboo Capital) asked why the base business barely grew if OEM growth was factored out.** Management replied that the base business is growing at 15% to 20%, but growth is non-linear with peaks and valleys. *(Management did not directly isolate the exact base business growth percentage).*
- **Ankit Gupta questioned the strategy shift back to the own-brand agri model after losses.** Management explained that contract manufacturing for large players like Mahindra offered negligible margins, prompting the shift to their own brand and smaller private labels.
- **Jatin Chawla (RTL Investments) asked why full-year standalone PAT guidance remains at INR 18 crore despite INR 9 crore already achieved in H1.** Management stated they are being conservative due to customer inventory normalization and learning curves.
- **Vansh Modi asked for the EBITDA impact split between cost inflation and TAFE's 2-month loss.** Management stated the numbers were not readily available and offered to discuss offline.
- **Ishit Desai (Ford’s Family Office) asked about Plan B for utilizing subsidiary capacity if the own-brand model scales slowly.** Management stated that forging orders from an associate company and utilizing the blade setup for automotive parts will cover costs.

### One-line takeaway
Emmforce Autotech delivered record H1 revenues up 73% YoY driven by US OEM orders, but absorbed cost pressures and subsidiary losses led management to temper FY27 consolidated revenue guidance to INR 185 crore while aiming to restore margins via imminent price hikes.
