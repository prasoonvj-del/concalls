# ZF Commercial Vehicle Control Systems India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-27 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance driven by robust CV industry growth, OE outperformance, and solid momentum across aftermarket and export segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained a positive outlook with industry production growth and further expected increases in Q3; no specific numerical guidance numbers provided.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious resilience (due to blue-collar labor shortages and monsoon impacts in Q1) to a more bullish stance as CV production surged 47.7% YoY in Q2 and OEM projections pointed to a 5-10% further increase.
- New topics or risks: Flagged rising CPI inflation (5.5%) and RBI repo rate hike to 5.5%; discussed Project SPARK new product rollouts (Disc Brake Rotors, DEF, etc.) and EV aftermarket potential.
- Last quarter's promises: Commissioned 7 new production lines between July and September (meeting the manufacturing expansion goal); continued execution on ESC nominations and localization.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 1,252.3 crore (up 30.7% YoY)
- **Sales of Product:** Rs 1,073.9 crore (up 37.2% YoY)
- **Service Income:** Rs 135.7 crore (up 8.6% YoY)
- **Other Operating Revenue:** Rs 6.79 crore (up 1% YoY)
- **Other Income:** Rs 42.6 crore (up 2.9% YoY)
- **OE Domestic Sales Growth:** 51.6% YoY (outperforming CV industry growth of 47.7%)
- **Aftermarket Sales Growth:** 27.0% YoY
- **Export of Goods Growth:** 20.3% YoY
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 166.4 crore (up 17.2% YoY, reported)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 127.8 crore (up 18.0% YoY)
- **Underlying PBT (Excluding FX):** Rs 170.1 crore (up 24.1% YoY)
- **H1 Total Income:** Rs 2,354.0 crore (up 17.7% YoY)
- **H1 Underlying PBT (Excluding FX):** Rs 312.1 crore (up 19.0% YoY)
- **Content Per Vehicle:** Rs 40,000 to Rs 43,000 (targeted to reach Rs 50,000 to Rs 55,000 over three years)
- **Network Additions:** 120 new branches added in Q2 (total ~550 dealers/distributors, ~270 authorized service partners)

### Highlights
- Commissioned 7 new production lines between July and September, including a crankshaft machining line for ICE compressors at Oragadam and assembly lines for brake actuators and valves at Ambattur, Jamshedpur, Lucknow, and Pantnagar.
- Received the Tata Motors Atmanirbhar Excellence Award for localizing e-compressors under "Make in India" and doubling output over the last year.
- Hemi Anechoic Chamber at the proving ground received NABL accreditation for NVH testing; ESC test track booked in advance until Q2 FY28 with ~200 vehicles queued.
- Expanded Project SPARK portfolio with new launches: Disc Brake Rotors, Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF), Wheel Bearing Grease, and Clutch Master Cylinder.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects CV demand fundamentals to remain resilient, supported by infrastructure activity, fleet utilization, and replacement demand.
- OEMs indicate a bullish market going forward with further production increases expected in the Jan-Mar quarter.
- Management expects content per vehicle to improve by approximately 30% over the next three years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CV production (>6 tonnes) grew 47.7% YoY in Q2, driven by infrastructure, construction, industrial demand, e-commerce logistics, and higher fleet utilization.
- Electric bus sales reached 2,228 units in Q2.
- Trailer industry recovered strongly with 19% YoY growth in Q2.
- U.S. vehicle production grew 17.4% and Europe grew 3.9% in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs (aluminum, forgings, castings, sheet metal) are indexed with customers on a quarterly basis, leading to a one-quarter lag in price recovery.
- One-time geopolitical risks are supported by back-to-back customer reimbursements with a one-quarter lag (retrospective, no risk).
- Operational costs controlled through COFA actions during demand dips.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focused on increasing capacity for key products like compressors and actuators.
- Commissioned an additional line for export ASP cartridges with SOP planned for early Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flow drop compared to March 2026 was attributed to customer prepayments received in anticipation of higher sales growth in the prior period.
- Management stated collection mechanisms are strong with no working capital challenges.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions, shifts in global trade policy and tariffs, and supply chain disruptions.
- Volatility in energy, commodity prices, and currency markets.
- Rising CPI inflation (5.5%) and higher interest rates (repo rate at 5.5%).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sucrit D. Patil (Eyesight Fintrade) asked how management handles demand slowdowns and raw material cost spikes:** Management stated they use COFA actions to control operational costs during dips, and raw material cost increases are fully recovered from customers with a one-quarter lag.
- **Dishant Jain (Quasar Capital) asked for export breakup between the U.S. and Europe:** Management stated U.S. sales were $106 million and EMEA/APAC were close to 170 (specific currency not verbally clarified in transcript).
- **Sabil Dabhoya (Avendus Investment Managers) asked about the sustainability of MHCV ESC market share and LCV revenue target progress:** Management stated they aim to retain around 70% market share and above, having already secured nominations from three major OEMs; on LCVs, hydraulic ESC is ramping up, while braking platforms are under development considering cost pressures.
- **Nirali Gopani (Unique PMS) asked about content per vehicle metrics and target adjustments:** Management stated current content is Rs 40,000–43,000, targeting Rs 50,000–55,000, and noted that targets were recalibrated by phasing out bleeder products and introducing a lighter ADAS regulatory-compliant solution.
- **Dishant Jain (Quasar Capital) asked about internal market share expectations for ADAS and EBS:** Management stated they expect to be majority players in EBS for electric vehicles, and for ADAS, they have 100% share with new mobility OEMs and plan to enter other OEMs with a light solution.

### One-line takeaway
ZF CVCS India delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with revenue up 30.7% YoY and OE domestic sales outpacing the CV industry, supported by robust infrastructure demand, new product ramps, and expanding safety regulation adoption.
