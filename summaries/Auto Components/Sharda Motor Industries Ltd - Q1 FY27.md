# Sharda Motor Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited supplier fire impact at a key customer, geopolitical disruptions causing premium raw material procurement costs, and global macroeconomic uncertainties, despite healthy industry growth.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Consolidated):** INR 1,011.1 crore (YoY growth of 34%)
- **Gross Profit:** INR 203.9 crore (YoY growth of 8%)
- **EBITDA:** INR 103.2 crore (YoY growth of 5%)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10.2%
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT) before exceptional items:** INR 115.0 crore (vs INR 107.7 crore in corresponding quarter last year, excluding INR 22.4 crore exceptional gains)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 86.5 crore
- **Domestic Automobile Production Growth:** Over 22% YoY to 93.6 lakh units
- **Passenger Vehicle Production:** 14.53 lakh units, up 16.8% YoY (Utility vehicles up 21.2% YoY)
- **LCV Production:** 1.91 lakh units, up 20.8% YoY
- **Addressable Market Growth:** 8% to 10%
- **Uttarakhand Facility Investment:** Approximately INR 20 crore
- **North American Engine & Genset Orders:** Combined annual value of approx US$ 10.7 million; lifetime value of approx US$ 58.5 million
- **Lightweighting Portfolio Addressable Market (5-year view):** INR 8,000 crore to INR 9,000 crore
- **Integrated Muffler Total Market Size Estimate:** INR 60 crore to INR 100 crore
- **Patent Filings:** Total 24 filed (2 added in Q1 FY27), 4 awarded

### Highlights
- Gross profit growth (8%) was impacted by production disruptions from a supplier fire at a key customer and one-time premium raw material procurement costs due to geopolitical situations.
- Chakan 3 lightweighting facility commenced Start of Production (SOP) and is ramping up.
- Uttarakhand facility investment of ~INR 20 crores progressing as per customer implementation plan for co-location.
- Filed 2 additional patents in Q1 FY27, taking total filings to 24.
- Jointly showcased cutting-edge Korean products with technology partner Donghee to key OEM customers, generating RFQs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they do not provide formal guidance.
- FY27 growth drivers include full-year benefits of previously announced lightweighting orders, ramp-up of additional lightweighting programs, temperature-controlled tube adjacencies, North American export order SOPs, and supportive domestic OEM volumes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry demand remains healthy across passenger vehicles (led by SUVs/utility vehicles), commercial vehicles, and 3-wheelers.
- Management expects simultaneous growth of ICE, CNG, hybrids, flex-fuel, and EVs rather than a single winning powertrain.
- CAFE III draft (proposed from April 1, 2027) retains a multi-technology approach, moderating super-credit for strong hybrids/flex-fuel while retaining incentives for EVs.
- BS6.3/WLTP effective April 1, 2027, increases focus on catalyst efficiency, thermal management, and durability.
- Exploring entry into the premium 2-wheeler emission market for higher CC segments domestically.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Substrate (catalyst) prices are customer-directed and passed through back-to-back.
- Direct raw materials are largely indexed; steel prices have no lag.
- Impacted by one-time premium freight and premium raw material procurement costs due to geopolitical disruptions in April and May, which management noted normalized in July.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investments remain modular and linked to confirmed programs.
- Chakan 3 plant has commenced SOP and is ramping up.
- Uttarakhand facility (~INR 20 crore investment) is being established closer to the customer's manufacturing location for JIT alignment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains strong, providing capacity to pursue suitable strategic acquisitions alongside organic growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Evolving geopolitical conditions creating trade dynamics, macroeconomic volatility, supply chain disruptions, and premium raw material/freight costs.
- Supplier fire at a key customer impacted production during the quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Preet (InCred AMC) asked about the revenue timeline and ramp-up for the North American export orders ($11.85m/$23.7m stated in context).**
   - *Management response:* SOPs are on track for Q3/Q4 FY27, but exact quarterly contribution depends on customer production schedules and ramp-up takes 1 to 2 years to reach peak volumes.
2. **Preet (InCred AMC) and Sonal Gupta (HSBC) questioned the gap between 34% revenue growth, 8% gross profit growth, and steel/substrate price pass-through lags.**
   - *Management response:* Clarified that catalyst/substrate and steel are passed through without a major lag; the divergence in gross profit growth was driven by catalyst revenue mix and minor one-time premium raw material costs due to geopolitical friction.
3. **Sonal Gupta (HSBC) asked about customer sign-ups for the Donghee TLA (Technology Licensing Agreement) products.**
   - *Management response:* Stated they jointly showcased physical products to key OEMs during the quarter, generating RFQs across multiple platforms, eyeing a mid-teen to high-teen percentage share in the INR 8,000–9,000 crore lightweighting market over 5 years.
4. **Viraj Kacharia (SiMPL) asked about content changes from BS6.3/WLTP to BS7 and the export emission pipeline.**
   - *Management response:* Explained that WLTP focuses on calibration and thermal management with limited redesign, whereas BS7 (aligned to Euro-7) will increase hot-end/aftertreatment content (GPF, high catalyst requirements).
5. **Manprit Arora (Arora Wealth Advisors) asked about exploring premium 2-wheeler emission opportunities and Purem JV status.**
   - *Management response:* Confirmed they are exploring the domestic premium 2-wheeler segment above a certain CC. For the Purem JV, a program started in Q4 last year and is ramping up in FY27; the JV provides access to advanced CV technologies above 4 liters.

### One-line takeaway
Sharda Motor reported Q1 FY27 consolidated revenue of INR 1,011.1 crore (up 34% YoY) and PAT of INR 86.5 crore, impacted by a customer supplier fire and geopolitical supply headwinds, while advancing its multi-powertrain lightweighting and emission strategies.
