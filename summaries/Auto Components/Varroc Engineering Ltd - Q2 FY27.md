# Varroc Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY 2027
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth across segments and strategic initiatives in EV and restructuring, despite temporary war-related margin pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 29,479 million (Up 33.6% YoY, 11.9% QoQ)
- **India Operations Growth:** 29.5% YoY
- **Overseas Operations Growth:** 104.1% YoY
- **H1 FY 2027 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 55,822 million (Up 31.8% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 2,611 million (EBITDA Margin: 8.9% vs 8.5% in Q1 FY 2027 and 9.1% in Q2 FY 2026)
- **H1 FY 2027 EBITDA:** Rs 4,838 million (EBITDA Margin: 8.7%)
- **PBT (before JV profits and exceptional items):** Rs 1,401 million (4.7% of revenue vs 4.3% in Q1 FY 2027 and 4.1% in Q2 FY 2026)
- **H1 FY 2027 PBT:** Rs 2,532 million (4.5% of revenue)
- **Exceptional Items (Q2 FY 2027):** Rs 63 crore (related to Italy restructuring and tax disputes)
- **Consolidated Net Debt:** Rs 5,547 million as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 4,952 million as of March 31, 2026)
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.29x; Net Debt to annualized adjusted EBITDA: 0.57x
- **Capex (H1 FY 2027):** ~Rs 290 crore (Full year guidance: Rs 500 crore - Rs 550 crore)
- **EV Revenue Contribution:** ~19% of Q2 FY 2027 consolidated revenue (Up 130% YoY; e-mobility solutions revenue at ~Rs 2,773 million vs Rs 1,941 million in Q1 FY 2027)
- **Net New Business Wins (H1 FY 2027):** Annualized peak revenue of Rs 10,127 million (>48% related to EV vehicles)
- **Unrealized Agreed Customer Cash Recoveries (H1 FY 2027):** Over Rs 1,140 million

### Highlights
- Restructuring two-wheeler lighting manufacturing operations in Italy by relocating manufacturing to Romania and Vietnam to eliminate underutilized capacity; payback on shutdown costs is targeted at three years.
- Proceeding to acquire a 45% controlling stake in Forsee Power India (with an option to scale up by 6% further) to enter the battery pack and BMS segment.
- Filed over 140 patents so far.
- *Record Check:* Continues the 33.6% YoY revenue growth trajectory reported in the investor deck filed on November 4, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets at least another Rs 1,500 crores of net new business wins in H2 FY 2027, bringing the full-year annualized peak revenue win target to Rs 2,500+ crores.
- Expects EBITDA margins in the India business to return to last year's levels by the end of the year if macroeconomic conditions do not worsen.
- Net debt is expected to remain around current levels for the rest of the year, with significant reduction anticipated by the end of next year.
- Production at the Thailand 4-wheeler lighting plant is scheduled to start in mid-2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry volumes in Q2 FY 2027 saw strong YoY growth: two-wheelers up 16.3%, three-wheelers up 23.7%, passenger vehicles up 24.9%, commercial vehicles up 33.9%, and electric two-wheelers up 115%.
- Sequential festive season growth supported volumes: two-wheelers up 11.7%, three-wheelers up 25.1%, passenger vehicles up 14.2%, and commercial vehicles up 19% over Q1 FY 2027.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity inflation driven by geopolitical uncertainties and war created near-term margin pressure due to time lags in customer recoveries.
- Under-recovery from customers impacted H1 FY 2027 EBITDA by 0.4% and adversely impacted cash flows.
- Commodity inflation contributed approximately 5% to 6% of the revenue growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Full-year FY 2027 capex guidance maintained at Rs 500 crore to Rs 550 crore (~Rs 300+ crores incurred in H1).
- Romania electronics plant (Timișoara) targets EBITDA break-even by the end of the year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased by Rs 599 million from March 31, 2026, driven by higher capex and delayed cash realizations from agreed customer price revisions (over Rs 114 crores pending collection).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- War-related input cost inflation and under-recoveries impacting cash generation and margins.
- Two ongoing legal/arbitration cases (with TYC in Singapore and OPmobility in Europe); management strives to resolve them before the end of the financial year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jyoti Singh (from a participant entity):** Asked why India margins softened QoQ despite consolidated margin improvement. *Management answered* that it was driven by war-related cost under-recoveries and business mix changes, which are short-term.
- **Naman (from a participant entity):** Inquired about the profitability lag in overseas operations and the TAM/revenue potential for Forsee Power. *Management answered* that Romania is targeting break-even by year-end, and Forsee Power aims to cross over Rs 1,000 crores in revenue over the next few years in the two- and three-wheeler segments.
- **Arvind Sharma (from a participant entity):** Clarified if war recovery mechanisms are automatic. *Management answered* that while underlying contracts exist, firm confirmations in these special times require specific customer negotiations before recognition.
- **Sagar Shetty (from a participant entity):** Asked about the OPmobility arbitration claims of $66.4 million and potential financial exposure. *Management stated* there are claims and counterclaims, and they are striving to reach a resolution before the end of the financial year without providing directional financial guidance.
- **Prince Goenka (from a participant entity):** Asked about the sustainability of overseas revenue growth and two-wheeler lighting. *Management answered* that 100%+ growth cannot be expected every quarter due to a lower base effect, but sequential growth will continue.

### One-line takeaway
Varroc reported robust Q2 FY 2027 revenue growth of 33.6% YoY led by India and overseas expansion, while taking strategic actions like the Italy restructuring and Forsee Power acquisition to bolster its EV portfolio despite temporary war-induced margin headwinds.
