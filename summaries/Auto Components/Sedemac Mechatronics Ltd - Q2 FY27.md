# Sedemac Mechatronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever Q2 and H1 revenue growth driven by strong demand and new product ramp-ups, offset partly by raw material inflation.

### Key numbers
- Control-intensive ECUs sold (TTM Q2 FY27): 4.6 million (44% YoY growth).
- Revenue growth (Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27): 40%+ YoY.
- Three-year revenue CAGR: ~38%.
- ISG+EFI shipments: >1 million in quantity within 2 years (close to 500K shipped in the last 8 months).
- EV revenue share within 2/3-wheeler segment: ~11.4% in H1.
- MF3 plant built-up area: ~3x existing main plant (MF1).
- Working capital: Rs 258 crore (~15-16% of annualized Q4 revenue).
- Cumulative Capex: Rs 450 crore (up from Rs 333 crore at March end).
- Short-term borrowings increase in H1: ~Rs 110 crore.

### Highlights
- Highest ever quarterly and half-yearly revenue reported in Q2 and H1 FY27, with 40%+ YoY growth.
- ISG ECU for export ICE three-wheelers and electric two-wheeler MCU fully ramped up.
- ISG+EFI ECUs experiencing solid adoption; 2 of 3 targeted top-10 motorcycle models fully ramped up with ISG.
- Won business for ISG ECU and electric motor (magneto kit) from a European 2/3-wheeler OEM, with production start likely in H1 FY28.
- MF3 plant commenced pilot production; expected to shift substantial production by the end of this financial year.
- Bought land in Shoolagiri, Tamil Nadu, for a larger future plant named MF5.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA to hold for H2 compared to H1 FY27, balancing ongoing raw material and PCB cost pressures with price corrections and newer generation ISG controller introductions.
- Capex in H2 is expected to be lower than H1 as the MF3 plant is nearly ready.
- Commercial vehicle after-treatment controller, EFI ECUs, and MCUs expected to enter production in the coming few quarters (H1 FY28).
- Electric power tool motor controller with sensorless control technology expected to enter production in H1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- North American home standby generator demand saw reduced EFI ECU demand due to a weaker 2025 Atlantic hurricane season, but H1 already shows signs of demand recovery.
- Customer concentration risk has improved favorably due to new launches and ramp-ups.
- Company views electrification as agnostic and positive for per-unit revenue.
- Sensorless control technology for EV MCUs represents a strong value proposition to eliminate sensor failures.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA percentage dipped primarily due to high commodity/raw material inflation and hyperinflation in certain commodities, partially offset by operating leverage.
- Additional pressure anticipated from recent significant increases in PCB costs.
- Pricing is negotiated rather than a standard auto-ancillary cost-plus model; forex risks are insulated via standard formulas.
- Advanced raw material purchases were made to secure supplies amid global uncertainties, impacting working capital.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- MF3 plant started pilot production; will operate at 65-70% utilization initially when fully populated at current revenue levels.
- MF1 plant currently runs at 90%+ utilization.
- MF5 plant planned in Shoolagiri over the next 1-2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital increased to Rs 258 crore due to revenue scaling and strategic advance inventory purchases.
- Capex investments increased from Rs 333 crore to Rs 450 crore, funded via internal cash accruals and debt.
- Management stated reducing borrowings is not a primary objective; the focus is maintaining a healthy debt-to-equity ratio while funding growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High commodity inflation and surging PCB costs putting pressure on EBITDA margins.
- Customer concentration and adoption hurdles by OEMs dependent on legacy global tier-one suppliers for EFI systems.
- Execution risks and timeline shifts inherent in new OEM program launches (e.g., commercial vehicles).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nikhil Kale (Avendus) on RM pass-through:** Management clarified there is no automatic pass-through formula; pricing is negotiated, though forex is insulated via standard formulas.
- **Krupashankar NJ (Avendus) on ISG+EFI volume split:** Management declined to give precise run-rates but noted ~500K shipments in the last 8 months as a reference.
- **Vipul Agrawal on ISG vs ISG+EFI adoption bottlenecks:** Management explained that OEMs hesitate due to reliance on established global tier-one EFI suppliers, though 1M+ deployments have mitigated proof-of-concept concerns.
- **Prasad Hase on CV product timeline delays:** Management stated program timeline shifts are natural in OEM-supplier relationships and not a major structural issue.
- **Vijay Bhatia (Retail Shareholder) on power tools market vision:** Management noted early-stage high-engagement with major players in the West, comparing the current excitement to ISG in 2017-2018, while emphasizing uncertainty around ultimate commercial success.

### One-line takeaway
Sedemac delivered record Q2/H1 FY27 performance with 40%+ revenue growth led by robust ISG and EV product ramp-ups, though margins face near-term pressure from raw material and PCB inflation.
