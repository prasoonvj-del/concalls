# Timken India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit revenue growth and higher profitability, driven by healthy demand across key end markets.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Standalone): Rs 969 crore (up 25.4% YoY, up 4.3% QoQ)
- PBT (Standalone): Rs 156 crore (up 29.5% YoY)
- PBT margin (Standalone): 16.1% (vs 15.6% in Q2 FY26, +50 bps)
- Net Profit / PAT (Standalone): Rs 116 crore (up 29.7% YoY)
- EBITDA margin (Standalone): ~19.2%
- Revenue from operations (Consolidated): Rs 986 crore (vs Rs 786 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Consolidated PBT: Rs 162 crore
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 120 crore (up ~29% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue: Rs 1,898.4 crore (up 20% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Standalone PBT: Rs 306 crore
- H1 FY27 Standalone PAT: Rs 231 crore (up 19% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue: Rs 1,929 crore
- H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT: Rs 240 crore
- Q2 Segment breakdown: Rail Rs 156.7 crore (16%), Mobile others Rs 204 crore (21%), Distribution Rs 148.8 crore (15%), Process Rs 190.9 crore (20%), Exports Rs 262.7 crore (27%), plus export incentives.
- Bharuch plant revenue for Q2: ~Rs 75 crore
- Bharuch plant capacity utilisation: SRB lines at 70%, CRB lines below 60%, overall average ~60%.

### Highlights
- Bharuch facility progress: SRB lines reached 70% utilisation for the quarter; CRB lines below 60%; overall plant utilisation near 60%.
- Rail expansion at Jamshedpur and plain bearing expansion at Bharuch are on target for upcoming commissioning and PPAPs.
- Amalgamation of Timken GGB Technology Private Limited with Timken India Limited is progressing smoothly through the regulatory process to drive synergies and simplify structure.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects momentum to continue into the next quarter, supported by solid domestic demand.
- Export momentum expected to remain pretty healthy in coming quarters, aided by North American rail market alignment.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remains robust supported by 7% GDP growth and infrastructure activity.
- Key demand drivers going forward include rail and heavy truck segments across the board, along with off-highway segments globally and in India.
- Global demand is influenced by geopolitics, trade uncertainties, and supply chain alignments.
- Wind sector in domestic market is picking up, with Chinese and other gearbox production moving to India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost pressures noted across depreciation (new vessels), fuel, gas, labour, steel, and carbides (up 200%).
- Management intends to pass on cost increases to customers; industrial customers understand and pass it on, while automotive customers may delay.
- Gross margin impact (130 bps YoY increase in RM cost percentage) attributed ~50% to unfavorable product mix (lower share of railways and distribution) and ~50% to input cost and price timing differences.
- Industrial segment margins remain better than automotive segment margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rail expansion at Jamshedpur (INR 120 crore investment) and plain bearing expansion at Bharuch on target; trial runs for the new rail line to start in November/December.
- Bharuch plain bearing (GGB) investments (FRC and metal polymer) stand at ~Rs 70 crore total; expected asset turnover of 1.5 to 2x.

- (Not discussed)

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External environment remains mixed due to geopolitical challenges, trade uncertainties, and supply chain shifts.
- Cost inflation across steel, carbides, gases, and labour remains a challenge.
- Global industry activity influenced by trade uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raghunandhan NL (Nuvama Research) asked about Bharuch capacity utilisation and cost inflation pass-through.** Management replied that Bharuch average utilisation is ~60% (SRB at 70%, CRB < 60%), revenue was ~Rs 75 crore, and cost inflation is being actively passed on to customers to protect margins.
- **Varun Jain asked about depreciation drop and railway tender status.** Management clarified that the depreciation drop was an accounting reclassification (noise of ~Rs 1.3 crores) from other expenses, and railway tenders are expected to convert into orders within 2–3 weeks following committee formation and interim orders.
- **Rishi Vora asked about wind business margins and segment mix.** Management stated industrial segments yield better margins than automotive, and industrial/export mix will provide positive effects as scale is reached.
- **Nikhil (ithoughtPMS) asked about wind turbine upsizing (4-5 MW) and GGB plain bearings.** Management explained that while initial stages use plain bearings (where GGB technology competes), carrier bearings still require taper roller bearings, and GGB will supply via a mix of imports and domestic Bharuch manufacturing.

### One-line takeaway
Timken India delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 25.4% YoY revenue growth and ~30% PAT growth, supported by robust domestic demand and expanding capacity at Bharuch, despite rising cost headwinds.
