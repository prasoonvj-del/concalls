### Highlights
*   **Market Share:** Clean Air (CV segment) market share increased to 59% (vs 58% in FY26); Advanced Ride Technologies (ART) market share at 55% (vs 48% 1.5 years ago).
*   **New Wins:** Secured three-wheeler suspension business (Indo-Korean JV), three new vehicle platforms (Indo-Chinese JV), two next-gen models (Japanese OEM), new hot end exhaust program (Indian CV OEM), CNG cold end assembly (European PV OEM), and a spark plug award (European PV OEM).
*   **Technology:** Commercialized India's first locally produced semi-active CVSAe suspension.
*   **Awards:** Received "Best Manufacturing Brand" (ET Edge) and "National Green Future Leadership Award" (National CSR Leadership Congress).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains confidence in the long-term growth trajectory, supported by a robust order book, customer wins, and capacity expansion.
*   Management stated the order book covers >100% of the FY30 revenue target.
*   Management expects export headwinds (geopolitical, tariffs, freight) to ease following recent US election outcomes.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains strong across served markets, driven by new program launches and increasing content per vehicle.
*   Western markets (Europe/US) are currently soft due to GDP contraction, Chinese OEM competition, and geopolitical uncertainty.
*   Management believes "technology equalization" and India's labor cost advantage position the company as a preferred export hub.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins were impacted by inflationary headwinds, including commodity costs (lube oil, plastic, rubber), minimum wage hikes, currency depreciation, and premium freight.
*   Management noted that "non-index" items (lube oil, plastic, rubber) are harder to pass through to OEMs compared to "index" items (steel); negotiations for recovery are ongoing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **New Capacity:** Announced a new Clean Air plant in West India (approx. Rs 450 million investment) to add 12-14% capacity for hot ends.
*   **Capex Spend:** H1 FY27 capex was ~Rs 120 crore; FY27 guidance is Rs 350-450 crore.
*   **SOP Timelines:** North India plant (March 2027), West India ART plant (July 2027), West India Clean Air plant (September 2027).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Company is debt-free and maintains a net cash position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Inflationary pressures (commodity, wages, freight).
*   Geopolitical uncertainty and US tariff threats affecting export markets.
*   Delay or cancellation of some SOPs due to global uncertainty.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Margin compression in subsidiary:** Management attributed this to non-indexed commodity costs (oil/plastic/rubber) and noted that recovery is subject to ongoing OEM negotiations.
*   **Export order book decline:** Management explained this as a temporary lull due to Western market slowdowns and geopolitical issues, emphasizing that the long-term export potential remains strong.
*   **Three-wheeler entry:** Management confirmed entry into the segment, starting with conventional suspension, with plans to migrate to DaVinci technology over time.
*   **Capacity utilization:** Management confirmed 99% utilization for hot ends, justifying the need for the new West India plant.
*   **FY30 Revenue Target:** Management confirmed the current order book covers 100% of the FY30 revenue target.

### One-line takeaway
Strong growth driven by market share gains and new technology wins, though margins face temporary pressure from non-indexed commodity inflation.
