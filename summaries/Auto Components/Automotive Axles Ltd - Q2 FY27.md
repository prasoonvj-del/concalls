# Automotive Axles Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the strongest ever Q2 revenue with solid growth across top line and bottom line driven by favorable market conditions.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Industry volume outlook raised; management now expects the overall market to cross 500,000 units (+500) for the year (a new record, surpassing the previous 2018 peak of 480,000), compared to last quarter's projection of a 5%-10% YoY dip or flat volume.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious/expecting a volume dip to highly confident, supported by strong replacement cycles, GST 2.0 momentum, and better-than-forecasted monsoons.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Commodity supply chain constraints and conversion cost increases (fuel and imported raw materials) emerged as key headwinds; prior concerns regarding monsoon vagaries and sea freight rates were not emphasized.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Phase-1 capacity investments are reaching completion as planned, helping meet the current domestic demand surge.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 6,625 million (up 28% QoQ, 43% YoY)
- Total income: Rs. 6,709 million (up 27% QoQ, 42.6% YoY)
- Metal costs: 66.2% of revenue
- EBITDA: Rs. 910 million at 13.7% margin
- EBIT: Rs. 812 million at 12.3% margin
- PBT: Rs. 807 million
- EPS: Rs. 39.8 (up ~30% QoQ, 67% YoY)
- Half-year revenue: Rs. 11,793 million
- Half-year total income: Rs. 11,981 million
- Half-year EBITDA: Rs. 1,613 million at 13.7% margin (160 bps expansion YoY)
- Half-year PAT: Rs. 1,057 million at 9% margin (143 bps expansion YoY)
- Half-year EPS: Rs. 69.9 (up 47% YoY)
- Capital work-in-progress: ~Rs. 27 crore

### Highlights
- Achieved strongest ever Q2 revenue in the company's history.
- Final phase of the Rs. 120 crore capex program is currently in its final stages of implementation, delivering a 20%-30% capacity enhancement.
- Heavy-duty tandem axle is scheduled for production ramp-up by Q4 of this year.
- Coach axle for 13.5m and 15m buses launched two quarters ago is currently in production.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the market to hold strong for at least the next couple of quarters, with full-year industry volume crossing 500,000 units.
- Pre-buys are expected to continue up to April of next year ahead of regulation changes, with visibility post-April remaining a watch item.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is heavily anchored by the replacement cycle of vehicles sold between 2018 and 2020 (BS6 pre-buys now 6 to 9 years old).
- Strong tailwinds from GDP growth, infrastructure projects, industrial production, freight activity, and better-than-expected monsoons.
- High traction in heavy-duty segments, specifically tractors, tippers, and tractor-trailers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Conversion costs increased due to fuel escalations and raw materials sourced from global markets.
- Metal costs stood at 66.2% for the quarter, managed via back-to-back pass-through arrangements with customers.
- Margin expansion supported by top-line absorption benefits and efficient cost management.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phase-1 capacity expansion is largely realized and will be fully complete by next quarter, adequately servicing current demand peaks.
- Phase-2 capacity strategy is currently being evaluated for board approval to address future growth and export opportunities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Capital work-in-progress stood close to Rs. 27 crore, representing the final stages of the previous Rs. 120 crore investment.
- Advanced income tax paid for the first half is ~Rs. 31 crore (net of refund), covering 45% of estimated annual profits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity supply chain constraints and raw material/conversion cost escalations.
- Lead times for critical components like gears range from 6 to 8 weeks, adding planning complexity.
- Volatility in OEM production forecasts causing potential inventory and material planning challenges.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Samarth (Janak Merchant Securities) on product mix, exports, and air suspensions:** Management stated that newly introduced products are scaling up, export capacities are currently stretched to meet domestic demand, and Automotive Axles does not make air suspensions (though their axles are fully compatible with air suspension vehicles globally).
2. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co.) on capacity status and bus axles:** Management clarified that current capacity expansion is handling peak demand, the bus coach axle is in production, and development of the low-floor bus regulation axle was temporarily slowed to free up lab capacity for validating alternate suppliers amid heavy demand.
3. **Lakshminarayanan on EV positioning and innovation metrics:** Management stated their mechanical axle portfolio is fully protected against EV central/remote motor architectures, and they have launched 4 to 5 new products since 2020 to stay ahead of market shifts.
4. **Lakshminarayanan on forecast volatility and metal cost impact:** Management noted that close customer engagement and back-to-back pass-through agreements neutralize raw material price fluctuation risks, though lead times remain a challenge.
5. **Akash (ADV Capital Group) on capacity readiness and margin sustainability:** Management confirmed in-house capacity will be fully ready by early next quarter to handle current demand peaks, while margin sustainability depends on H2 market performance and product mix.

### One-line takeaway
Automotive Axles delivered its highest-ever Q2 revenue and a 67% YoY surge in EPS, backed by strong replacement-led M&HCV demand and newly operational capacity expansions.
