# Belrise Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly PAT supported by 26% manufacturing revenue growth, margin recovery, and landmark order wins in consumer electronics, aerospace, and auto.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (mid-teens overall revenue growth guidance with stable EBITDA margins).
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone remains strongly positive; cost pressures have subsided as anticipated, auto OEM demand remains robust, and non-auto verticals (electronics, aerospace) are accelerating.
- **New topics or risks:** Entered consumer electronics (precision tooling/CNC fixtures for a major American smartphone OEM) and signed an exclusive tech agreement with Yamato Co. (resin intake manifolds/filters); trading margins remain under pressure.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Badve Auto Component merger application filed with NCLT (expected closure Q4 FY27–Q1 FY28); ~Rs 12,000 crore of the Rs 17,000 crore QIP proceeds remain unutilized and set for deployment over coming quarters.

---

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** Rs 27,464 million (+17% YoY)
- **Manufacturing Revenue:** Rs 23,441 million (+26% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 3,306 million (+12% YoY); Margin at 12.0%
- **Manufacturing EBITDA:** Rs 3,170 million (+19% YoY); Margin at 13.5%
- **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 1,663 million (+25% YoY); Margin at 6.1%
- **Exports Contribution:** Rs 1,117 million (4.8% of manufacturing revenue)
- **Manufacturing Segment Mix:** 2W/3W at 79.7%, CV at 9.6%, PV at 5.4%, Others at 5.3%
- **ROCE:** 15.3% (up 60 bps YoY from 14.7%), excluding ~Rs 12,000 million unutilized QIP proceeds

---

### Highlights
- Secured maiden precision tooling and CNC fixture order from the ecosystem of a major American smartphone OEM, localizing imports across titanium, aluminum, and exotic materials.
- Secured first post-acquisition order win at Chester Hall (UK aerospace) for nacelle track beams on a major global aircraft OEM platform, featuring price increases and a ~5-year tenure.
- Signed an exclusive licensing and technical assistance agreement with Yamato Co. (Japan) to manufacture and sell air/oil filters and resin air intake manifolds for auto, industrial, and aftermarket segments in India.
- Won a CV chassis assembly program at Dharwad for heavy commercial vehicles (peak annual revenue Rs 1,500–2,000 million; per-vehicle content Rs 60,000–1,000,000; ramp-up Q1 FY28).
- Won 3W complete body and chassis assembly program for a top-selling model as a second source (peak annual revenue Rs 1,000–1,200 million; incremental vehicle content ~Rs 9,000; ramp-up Q1 FY28).
- Secured maiden PV sheet metal component orders from a leading Japanese OEM (Noida) and European OEM (Pune).
- Onboarded 6th OEM customer in the proprietary suspension business.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained guidance for mid-teens overall revenue growth with stable EBITDA margins.
- Reaffirmed target of >10% manufacturing revenue from aerospace and defense over the medium term (12–18 months).
- Aiming to increase ROCE to high-teens over the next couple of years via lower capex as % of revenue, higher capacity utilization, and growth in proprietary segments.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broad-based auto presence across top OEMs (supplying top 10 2W OEMs and 12 of top 15 PV/CV OEMs in India).
- Consumer electronics fixture opportunity is estimated by management as a multi-hundred million dollar market globally for this single OEM ecosystem.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins stabilized YoY as raw material/cost pressures subsided.
- Steel prices (mild and stainless) experienced lower volatility compared to non-ferrous metals like copper and aluminum.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up a new facility in Dharwad for complete heavy CV chassis assemblies (ramp-up in Q1 FY28).
- Setting up a production line facility in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar for complete 3W body/chassis assemblies (ramp-up in Q1 FY28).
- Indian facility achieved AS9100 aerospace certification to support hub-and-spoke production with the UK.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ~Rs 12,000 million of unutilized cash remains from the total Rs 17,000 million QIP proceeds.
- Scheme of merger for Badve Auto Component business applied with NCLT; expected completion in 6 to 8 months (Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28).

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Trading business margins continue to face short-term margin pressure.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jyoti Singh (Haitong):** *What is the medium-term outlook and EBITDA impact from aerospace?*  
  **Management:** Reaffirmed target of >10% revenue contribution in the medium term; stated the segment will be EBITDA and ROCE accretive, leveraging a hub-and-spoke model (UK for R&D/marketing, India for low-cost precision manufacturing).
- **Nitij Mangal (Jefferies):** *How does the consumer electronics fixture business scale?*  
  **Management:** Fixtures are highly recurring as they change every few months with new smartphone models/variants. Initial orders are modest, but management expects rapid compounding and eventual entry into larger end-product components.
- **Nitin Agrawal (JM Financial):** *Details on the Chester Hall aerospace order extension?*  
  **Management:** The contract includes volume increases and price hikes for engine nacelle track beams over a ~5-year period. Manufacturing currently occurs in the UK, with India production ramping up post-AS9100 certification.
- **Ayush Jhunjhunwala (HSBC):** *Status of the Badve Auto merger and trading margins?*  
  **Management:** Merger applied with NCLT, targeting completion in 6–8 months (Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28). Trading business cost pressures are easing, with margin normalization expected over upcoming quarters.
- **Shubham Jain (Investec):** *What was aerospace's current revenue contribution?*  
  **Management:** Aerospace currently contributes approximately 2.5% to 3.5% of manufacturing revenue.

Belrise Industries delivered record quarterly profits of Rs 166.3 crore, expanding margins and entering high-margin consumer electronics and aerospace supply chains alongside major auto wins.
