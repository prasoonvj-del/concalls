# Precision Camshafts Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-26 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong standalone Indian business growth and new program SOPs offset by a large exceptional impairment for European subsidiary EMOSS.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None provided in either quarter.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more cautious on EMOSS, leading to a conservative impairment provision, while confidence in the core Indian ICE and new program business remained high.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: A Rs 75 crore exceptional impairment for EMOSS was newly introduced; Middle East war risks disappeared from the discussion.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Delivered on Maruti Suzuki and Mahindra program SOPs starting in July and September 2026; ongoing machine shop automation (~Rs 24 crores) nearing completion by the end of the year.

### Key numbers
- Standalone total income: INR 194.96 crores (up 12.5% QoQ, up ~30% YoY)
- Standalone net loss: INR 58.69 crores (impacted by INR 75 crores exceptional item)
- Standalone PAT (ex-exceptional): INR 17 crores
- Standalone EBITDA margin: 13.2%
- Standalone PAT margin: -30%
- Consolidated total income: INR 221 crores (up ~10% QoQ)
- Consolidated EBITDA margin: 11.6%
- Consolidated PAT: INR 12.9 crores (5.83% margin)
- MEMCO revenue: INR 15.8 crores (up 10.9% QoQ from INR 14.1 crores)
- EMOSS revenue: INR 10.8 crores (down from INR 13.8 crores in Q1)

### Highlights
- Management took a conservative approach by booking an exceptional item of INR 75 crores primarily for impairment provisions against investments and loans in EMOSS; impact partly offset by customer compensation for rate revision and business loss.
- MEMCO revenue increased, driven by higher income from Bosch, Delphi, and Endress+Hauser.
- India electric heavy commercial vehicle platform delivered its first vehicle for customer evaluation and field trials, awaiting feedback.
- Commercial production (SOP) started for Maruti Suzuki (July, ~INR 50 crores annual revenue) and Mahindra programs (September 2026, ~INR 25 crores annual revenue).
- Received new nominations for Maruti Suzuki projects with SOP planned for 2029–2030.
- Won a new business award for Hyundai (India, Korea, U.S.) for 700,000 camshafts per year via a Tier 1 supplier, with SOP starting as early as April 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in the long-term outlook for PCL's core Indian business.
- Management maintains a cautious view on EMOSS given the EV slowdown and uncertain outlook in Europe.
- Management is actively looking at acquisitions in India.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Electric commercial vehicle market in Europe remains challenging with slower customer decisions and reduced program visibility.
- Customer commitments towards ICE engines continue steadily in India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Invested nearly INR 24 crores in phased machine shop automation (expected completion by end of year) targeting manpower reduction, higher machine utilization, and improved manufacturing costs.
- Optimizing foundry bill of materials and processes to manage higher raw material, alloy, and consumable costs.
- R&D re-engineering critical machines locally to support assembled camshaft demand in India.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investing in capacity, automation, and technology.
- Actively looking at acquisition opportunities in India within and outside the current product range.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Conservative approach applied to EMOSS balances via INR 75 crore impairment against investments and outstanding loans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Challenging European EV market with slower customer decisions and reduced program visibility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Priyansh from Neo Mile AMC on expansion plans:** Asked about expansion plans outside the camshaft business. Management stated they are actively looking at opportunities and acquisitions within India in spaces related and outside of their current product range. (No dodge).

Precision Camshots delivered strong standalone Indian growth and new program execution, but reported a consolidated net loss due to a Rs 75 crore impairment on its struggling European EV subsidiary, EMOSS.
