# Alicon Castalloy Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and order wins, though impacted by input cost volatility.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 579 crore (up 37% YoY, 17% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 55 crore
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 9.5%
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 18 crore (up 45% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 12 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **Consolidated Volume Growth (adjusted for material inflation):** ~17.5% YoY
- **Standalone Volume Growth (adjusted for material inflation):** ~22% YoY
- **Consolidated Tonnage:** 9,124 tonnes in Q1 FY27
- **Executable Order Book:** Rs 8,450 crore across 2026 to 2031 (as of 30th June 2026)
- **Non-auto order book share:** 2% (approx Rs 126 crore)
- **EV and Hybrid order book share:** EV at ~16%, Hybrid at ~12%
- **Capex:** Rs 40 crore in Q1 FY27; ~Rs 150 crore planned for full year FY27 (including ~Rs 70 crore for the new Shikrapur facility)
- **New Shikrapur facility investment:** Rs 125 crore over 2-3 years, targeting ~Rs 500 crore annual revenue in 4-5 years

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 500 crore in quarterly sales for the first time in company history.
- Appointed Sumit Bhatnagar as Group CEO effective April 1, 2026, operating under the philosophy of 'Reset', 'Refocus', and 'Rebuild'.
- Gained entry into two large Indian PV and CV OEMs with ~Rs 8,500 crore visibility over the next five years (booked orders).
- Acquired new businesses across auto and non-auto segments in Q1 with >Rs 450 crore revenue potential over five years.
- Won a first-ever non-auto business for aluminium compressor housings for HVAC in global data centers (starting in India, expanding to North America and Europe).
- Won a first-ever tractor segment business for an aluminium cylinder head with the largest tractor manufacturer globally.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a true top-line growth of 12% to 15% for FY27 (neutralized for material inflation).
- Management expects at least a 1% improvement in EBITDA margins for FY27.
- Management expects European operations to stage a turnaround from Q4 FY27 as new programs (including e-Axle) ramp up.
- Aims to double revenues by 2030.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian automotive industry remains structurally strong: PVs up 11.3% (highest ever Q1 at ~1.57 million units), 2-wheelers up 21%, CVs up 19.5%.
- Hybrid segment projected to grow at ~25% to 30% CAGR over the next several years; EVs projected at ~20% to 22% CAGR over 4-5 years.
- Alicon is a single-source supplier to the largest hybrid vehicle manufacturer in India.
- European sales were softer due to mature programs reaching end-of-life before new programs ramp up.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost volatility (aluminium, gas, tooling, freight) due to Middle East geopolitical developments put pressure on margins.
- Raw material inflation (aluminium) is contractually pass-through with a timing lag; direct pass-through for aluminium became back-to-back from April 1.
- Engaging with customers to recover manufacturing and operating cost increases through price revisions.
- Structural profitability is being addressed via productivity, automation, process optimization, and a higher share of high-value-added machined/complex products.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Leased a new 1.36 lakh sq ft facility in Shikrapur (5 km from existing plant); taking possession on Sept 1, 2026, with SOP targeted by March 2027.
- Shikrapur facility to feature GDC, LPDC, and machining, starting at 3,000 tonnes capacity and scaling up to 7,000 tonnes.
- Capacity utilization across plants is close to 100% (more than 90%).
- Payback period on capex typically targets 3 to 4 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Shikrapur facility capex (Rs 125 crore) funded primarily through internal accruals and supplemented by borrowings.
- Focus on improving working capital efficiency and cash conversion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Significant volatility in input costs (aluminium, gas, logistics, tooling) impacting margins.
- Temporary gap in European operations due to legacy programs ending before new ones ramp up.
- Extended validation periods and slower initial pickup for certain complex EV programs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raghunandhan N L (Nuvama Research):** Asked about the overseas business softness and FY27/3-year outlook. Management noted European softness will last 1-2 more quarters before turning around in Q4, and guided for 12-15% true growth in FY27.
- **Avinash Nahata (Parami Financial Services):** Inquired about non-auto order book contribution. Management stated non-auto is currently 2% of the order book (approx Rs 126 crore) but is being scaled via a dedicated vertical, highlighting new wins in HVAC for data centers and tractor cylinder heads.
- **Riddhesh Gandhi (Discover Capital):** Questioned return ratios (ROCE) given full capacity utilization. Management explained that older low-value/EV transition drag is being phased out, capacity is being reallocated to higher-value PV parts, and ROCE is on an increasing trend toward ~15%.
- **Ramesh (Investor):** Asked why the current order book execution is more reliable than past unmaterialized orders. Management clarified that current orders focus on traditional ICE/hybrid components with shorter validation cycles, unlike the prolonged lead times experienced in early EV bets.
- **Ramesh (Investor):** Asked about machining percentage in PVs. Management stated 65% to 70% of PV tonnage is machined, and in-house machining investments will increase going forward.

### One-line takeaway
Alicon reported robust Q1 FY27 volume growth of ~17.5% and crossed Rs 500 crore in quarterly sales, driven by domestic momentum and new PV/CV order wins, while addressing margin pressures through operational efficiencies and a shift toward high-value products.
