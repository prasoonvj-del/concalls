# Precision Camshafts Ltd - Q1 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-31 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY26-27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong growth and positive momentum in the standalone Indian business offset by a slowdown and cautious outlook in the European EV subsidiary (EMOSS).

### Key numbers
- Standalone total income: INR173 crores (up 6.6% QoQ)
- Standalone net profit (PAT): INR14.88 crores (up 12.5% QoQ from INR13.2 crores)
- Standalone EBITDA margin: 13%
- Standalone PAT margin: 9%
- Consolidated total income: INR200.8 crores (down 2.4% QoQ)
- Consolidated EBITDA margin: ~10%
- Consolidated PAT: ~4.2%
- MEMCO revenue (Q1): INR14.1 crores
- EMOSS revenue (Q1): INR13.8 crores (down from INR29 crores in the previous quarter)
- Cumulative order book: Approximately INR1,500 crores (spread over 4 to 5 years)
- EU electric truck registration share (H1 2026): 4.8% of all new truck registrations

### Highlights
- Several new programs with key customers (Mahindra, Tata Motors, Maruti Suzuki) commenced commercial production during Q1 after moving from development and validation.
- MEMCO focused on strengthening relationships with customers including Bosch, Delphi, and Endress+Hauser.
- India e-mobility heavy commercial vehicle (EHCV) platform developed and delivered to a customer for evaluation and field trials.
- Management stated an openness to acquisition opportunities within India to expand into new products, markets, and customers.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident about the medium and long-term growth prospects of the standalone Indian business.
- Management maintains a cautious near-term outlook for European subsidiary EMOSS due to slow customer decision-making and reduced program visibility in the EV segment.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian passenger vehicle (PV) market demonstrates strong growth, reflected directly in customer programs and order pipelines.
- European electric commercial vehicle space remains challenging, impacted by insufficient enabling conditions and the pullback of subsidies, as highlighted by the European Automobile Manufacturers' Association.
- Broader European automotive industry undergoing significant restructuring, pressuring OEMs to improve competitiveness, reduce costs, and rationalize investments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin stood at 13% and PAT margin at 9% for Q1.
- Consolidated EBITDA margin was about 10% and PAT was about 4.2%.
- Management remains focused on protecting the EMOSS business by carefully managing costs and investments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company is investing ahead of customer requirements in capacity additions, automation, and technology upgrades.
- Active search for acquisition opportunities in India to grow into new products and markets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management aiming to sustain EMOSS as a standalone business without requiring support from the Indian entity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Disruptions due to the Middle East war impacted operations.
- Slowdown in the European e-mobility business (EMOSS) with reduced program visibility and slower customer decision-making.
- Challenging market conditions for electric commercial vehicles in Europe.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishit Sanghvi (Neo Mill) on business focus:** Asked if the company will focus solely on camshafts or other segments. Management stated they are looking at new opportunities, expanding MEMCO products, and actively pursuing acquisition opportunities in India for new products and markets.
2. **Navneet Kamalia (Individual Investor) on order book disclosure:** Asked for the outstanding order book size. Management disclosed a cumulative order book of approximately INR1,500 crores spread over four to five years over and above existing businesses.
3. **Navneet Kamalia (Individual Investor) on European subsidiary:** Asked if EMOSS is a candidate for winding up. Management responded that it is hard to say due to dynamic markets and unprecedented European conditions, but they are trying to sustain it as a standalone business without Indian support.
4. **Navneet Kamalia (Individual Investor) on presentation quality:** Provided feedback to improve corporate presentation details. Management acknowledged the feedback and agreed to add additional information in the next quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Precision Camshafts Limited delivered a strong Q1 driven by its expanding Indian standalone operations and new PV program launches, while navigating a challenging, cautious environment in its European e-mobility subsidiary.
