# Uniparts India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest revenue quarter in the company's history driven by construction growth and new business wins.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 379 crore (37% YoY, 9% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 726 crore (32% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 89 crore (38% YoY, flat QoQ)
- **EBITDA margin:** 23.4%
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 178 crores (67% of FY26 EBITDA)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 54 crore (37% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 111 crores (70% of FY26 PAT)
- **Operating cash flow (Q2):** Rs 34 crore
- **Trailing 12-month EPS:** Rs 43.30
- **Net working capital:** 137 days of TTM revenue as of September 30, 2026
- **Net cash position:** Rs 172 crore (after Rs 41 crore dividend payout in Q2)
- **Capex (Q2):** Rs 10 crore
- **Annual capex guidance:** Around Rs 60 crore for next year
- **New business wins:** Exceeding Rs 225 crore (concentrated in construction and large ag)
- **Mexico revenue contribution:** Single-digit million dollars for H2 FY27
- **Full year FY27 revenue growth expectation:** 24% higher than FY26

### Highlights
- Delivered highest revenue quarter in company history.
- Ludhiana finishing shop restoration on track to run by end of calendar year 2026.
- First customer dispatch from Mexico facility scheduled for November 2026 (starting with 1 of 3 customers).
- U.S. patent for lower link hook granted in August.
- *Check against record:* Record confirms Q2 FY27 highest-ever revenue powered by construction and agriculture.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue to be 24% higher than FY26.
- Management expects to comfortably deliver 20% EBITDA margins over the cycle.
- Q3 FY27 expected to be seasonally the lowest quarter, broadly in line with Q1 FY27.
- Working capital days expected to remain around 136-140 days.
- Management targets mid-teens revenue growth over cycles.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Construction segment grew 49% YoY, driven by North America (AI data centers, power generation, smart manufacturing) and Europe (infrastructure, energy transition, non-residential construction).
- Large agriculture industry volumes (tractors >100 HP and 4WD) down 17% globally; however, Uniparts' large ag revenue grew 10% YoY entirely from new business.
- Small agriculture cycle starting to turn after 3 years of continuous decline; Uniparts small ag sales grew 25% YoY.
- Aftermarket segment grew over 50% YoY, representing 16.5% of revenue in Q2 FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of materials consumed remains in the range of 34% to 37% (reported at 34.3% in Q2).
- Steel price increases are passed through to customers with a quarter lag.
- Other expenses increased primarily due to freight costs, which will also be passed on with a quarter lag.
- Gross margin typically ranges between 63% to 66%, aided by warehousing sales mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q2 capex stood at Rs 10 crore; planned capex of around Rs 60 crore for next year.
- Mexico facility operational with first dispatches in November; working with 3 customers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash of Rs 172 crore after paying Rs 41 crore dividend.
- Receivables increase attributed to higher revenue size and temporary technical payment delays from 2 U.S. OEM partners (one resolved, one resolving in November).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Purchasing in the aftermarket segment is discretionary, leading to quarterly variability.
- Potential lag in passing on steel and freight cost increases to customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Digant Shah (DAM Capital) on gross margin resilience:** Management explained that raw material cost is 34.3% and steel price increases are passed through with a lag via customer contracts.
2. **Praveen Jayaraman (Avendus Spark) on construction sustainability:** Management stated growth is driven by multiyear contracts for data centers/AI in the U.S. and infrastructure/energy transition in Europe, making cancellations unlikely.
3. **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit Asset Management) on M&A strategy:** Management noted they evaluated and walked away from recent opportunities, currently reviewing half a dozen targets in hydraulics, PTO, and large fabrication without rushing into deep turnarounds.
4. **Vinay Lakhera (Ratnatraya Capital) on FTC/USDA inquiry in U.S.:** Management declined to speculate on policy, noting customer order books and relationships remain healthy.
5. **Sunil Jain (Nirmal Bang) on other expenses:** Management confirmed the increase was due to freight costs, which will be recovered from customers with a quarter lag.
6. **Nikhil Upadhaya (SIMPL) on large ag new order share:** Management noted large ag requires rigorous 18-36 month validation cycles, and large ag now constitutes roughly 35% of new business awards.

### One-line takeaway
Uniparts delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue in Q2 FY27 with robust 37% YoY growth, driven by structural momentum in construction and new business wins offsetting a down agriculture cycle.
