# Pricol Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Strong revenue growth is being offset by severe input cost headwinds and margin pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 1,334.02 crore (up 35.03% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 11.82%
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 85.21 crore
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 6.39%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 2,417.60 crore (up 29.59% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 11.64%
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 6.30%
- **H1 FY27 EPS:** Rs. 12.49
- **P3L Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 312 crore
- **P3L EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 9.9%
- **Planned Capex (P3L over next 6 quarters):** Rs. 350 crore
- **Asset Turn (P3L capacity):** 3 to 3.25

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs. 1,000 crore in revenue for a single quarter, driven by robust domestic demand and the festive season.
- All business verticals (DIS, ACFMS, Plastics) grew at more or less the same 35% rate YoY.
- Internal operational demerger into two divisions (DICVS and ACFMS) began on October 1; complete operational separation is targeted for April 1, 2027, with final NCLT orders expected in a couple of quarters.
- *Record Check:* The call confirmed continued high capacity utilization and ongoing capacity expansion plans for the plastics business, matching prior records.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects input cost headwinds to continue for a few more quarters before settling down.
- Real growth from plastics capacity expansion will materialize from FY28 and onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Two-wheeler industry recorded its highest growth ever at 35% in Q2, heavily aiding the company's performance.
- Strong export demand continues, notably for large water pumps supplied to Caterpillar in the U.S.
- Key customers in the polymer business include TVS, Ather Energy, Royal Enfield, Autoliv, and Honda Motorcycles (starting next financial year).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined by 150 bps QoQ due to soaring input costs.
- Three major cost headwinds cited: 1) Strong USD appreciation against the rupee increasing import bill of materials; 2) Surging bare PCB costs due to the West Asia crisis; 3) Rising memory costs driven by the AI surge, alongside rising labor and power/casting costs.
- First-time cost escalations have impacted EBITDA by an estimated 1% to 2%.
- Discussions are ongoing with customers to pass on these abnormal increases, though management cautioned they may not achieve 100% recovery.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Plastic division (P3L) is currently operating at close to 100% capacity.
- Planned capex of Rs. 350 crore for P3L over the next 6 quarters to create new capacities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *(Not discussed)*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unprecedented input cost spikes (bare PCBs, memory, forex, and labor) that customers may not fully absorb.
- Capacity constraints in the plastics business persisting over the next 12 months until new capacity comes online.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rishabh Rathi (Goldman Sachs) on Gross Margin vs EBITDA:** Management explained that while top-line grew, new first-time cost escalations prevented EBITDA from scaling proportionally, and 100% recovery from customers is unlikely.
2. **Hitesh Goyal on Segment Growth:** Management clarified that all verticals (DIS, ACFMS, Plastics) grew at the same ~35% rate, and near-term margin visibility remains difficult due to rising costs.
3. **Preet Pitani on Cost Impact & Pass-through:** Management estimated the EBITDA impact of first-time cost escalations at 1% to 2% and noted that product-to-product pass-through mechanics vary.
4. **Jatin Chawla on Customer Negotiations:** Management reiterated that while regular quarterly indexation works, abnormal post-Q1 cost hikes (PCBs, memory, labor) are under active negotiation.
5. **Chandra Gupta on Demerger Timeline:** Management stated that internal divisionalization started in October, full operational separation is planned for April 1, 2027, and NCLT approval is expected in a couple of quarters.

### One-line takeaway
Pricol delivered a strong 35% revenue jump to cross Rs. 1,000 crores in Q2, but margins face severe pressure from surging bare PCB, memory, and forex costs that may not be fully passed on.
