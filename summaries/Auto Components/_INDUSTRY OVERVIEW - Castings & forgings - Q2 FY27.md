# Auto Components - Castings & Forgings: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the castings and forgings sector reflects a predominantly positive tone, driven by record-high quarterly revenues, robust domestic demand across commercial vehicles (CV) and infrastructure, and aggressive multi-year capacity expansions. Companies like **Rolex Rings**, **Steel Strips Wheels**, **Talbros Automotive**, and **MM Forgings** reported record performances. However, pockets of divergence remain: companies exposed to European industrial markets, tubes, or tractors (**Kirloskar Ferrous**, **Electrosteel Castings**, **Synergy Green**) cited localized margin pressures, elevated logistics/freight costs, and potential volume softening.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Domestic demand for commercial vehicles, passenger vehicles, earthmoving, mining, and infrastructure remains healthy. North American markets have shown a strong V-curve recovery, particularly benefitting exporters like **Happy Forgings**, **Ramkrishna Forgings**, and **Rolex Rings**.
* **Divergence:** While **Happy Forgings** and **Kross** noted strong tractor segment momentum supported by market share gains, **Kirloskar Ferrous** explicitly cautioned about potential cooling or corrections in the tractor industry ahead. Furthermore, European industrial demand was described as soft by **Rolex Rings** and **Electrosteel Castings**, contrasting sharply with booming US data center and renewable energy (wind/solar) demand cited by **Synergy Green**, **Pitti Engineering**, and **Bharat Forge**.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Headwinds:** Managements across the board highlighted margin pressures arising from a spike in ocean freight/shipping rates, coking coal price increases (**Electrosteel Castings**, **Kirloskar Ferrous**), and consumables like furan resin (**Synergy Green**, **Kross**).
* **Mitigation:** Most companies rely on a 100% or partial pass-through mechanism with a 1-to-quarter lag (**Kirloskar Ferrous**, **Kross**, **Rolex Rings**, **Synergy Green**). Margin expansion was largely achieved through richer product mixes (e.g., shifting toward high-value machining and aluminum components at **Rolex Rings** and **Steel Strips Wheels**) and internal cost-optimization programs.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is undergoing a massive capex cycle focused on heavy components, advanced machining, and greenfield facilities:
* **Bharat Forge:** Investing ~Rs 1,300 crore brownfield in Baramati across four forging and large machining facilities.
* **Happy Forgings:** Progressing on Rs 650 crore heavy component/solar capex while approving an additional Rs 500 crore capex for FY28-FY29.
* **Steel Strips Wheels:** Guided to over Rs 600 crore in FY27 capex and announced a Rs 1,400 crore greenfield facility in Aurangabad.
* **Pitti Engineering:** Cumulative planned capex stands at Rs 740 crore, including a new Rs 450 crore expansion.
* **Steelcast:** Increased greenfield capacity expansion outlay to Rs 151 crore for an 11,750-tonne capacity addition.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Revisions:** **MM Forgings** raised its FY27 revenue target to >Rs 2,000 crore. **Steelcast** raised its FY27 revenue growth guidance to ~30% YoY. **Electrosteel Castings**, conversely, pushed out its 12%-13% EBITDA margin timeline from Q3 FY27 to FY28 due to delayed Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) fund flows.
* **New Strategic Actions:** **Electrosteel** engaged Zerowatt for an AI energy platform and explored a VRS scheme; **Kalyani Forge** approved a Rs 45 crore funding plan; **Ratnaveer Precision** neared completion of India's first Copper Clad Laminate (CCL) plant.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Freight and Logistics:** Normalization of ocean freight rates and container availability for export-heavy players (**Rolex Rings**, **Ramkrishna Forgings**, **Synergy Green**).
* **Capex Execution:** Progress on large greenfield and brownfield projects (**Steel Strips Wheels**, **Bharat Forge**, **Steelcast**, **Pitti Engineering**).
* **Demand Trajectory:** Sustainability of tractor demand and European industrial order books.

## Company one-liners
* **Bharat Forge Ltd:** Driving non-auto diversification across aerospace, semiconductor, and defense, offsetting near-term overseas subsidiary drags.
* **Electrosteel Castings Ltd:** Navigated a soft domestic demand environment with cost optimization while advancing strategic diversification into valves and paints.
* **Happy Forgings Ltd:** Delivered strong Q2 growth and announced a new Rs 500 crore capex to support machining capacity.
* **INDO SMC Ltd:** Achieved strong H1 growth despite margin compression from raw material volatility, pivoting to higher-margin electrical infrastructure.
* **INDO-MIM Ltd:** Reported solid H1 performance with reduced debt and strong return ratios, tracking well toward its Rs 5,000 crore top-line guidance.
* **Kalyani Cast-Tech Ltd:** Leveraging proprietary design innovations and its integrated Shivlakha logistics hub to drive containerization and long-term growth.
* **Kalyani Forge Ltd:** Prioritizing margin sustainability and core segment growth using a new Rs 45 crore funding plan.
* **Kirloskar Ferrous Industries Ltd:** Reported steady revenue growth in basic metals, but faces execution and demand headwinds in tubes and tractor foundries.
* **Kross Ltd:** Delivered an 80% YoY revenue jump backed by robust trailer and CV demand, advancing backward integration to enhance margins.
* **MM Forgings Ltd:** Reported strong Q2 with 19.7% EBITDA margins and raised FY27 revenue guidance to >Rs 2,000 crore.
* **Maiden Forgings Ltd:** Delivered strong H1 revenue and EBITDA growth, advancing the consolidation of its Bhojpur facility for cost efficiencies.
* **Pitti Engineering Ltd:** Aggressively expanding capacity (Rs 740 crore cumulative capex) to capture structural demand in data centers and infrastructure.
* **Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd:** Delivered robust Q2 performance with 54.9% YoY revenue growth and margin expansion, supported by strong CV demand and deleveraging.
* **Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Ltd:** Reported record-high Q2 performance and is on track to commission India's first Copper Clad Laminate (CCL) plant.
* **Rolex Rings Ltd:** Delivered its strongest quarter in history with a 43% YoY revenue jump, underpinned by U.S. demand recovery and auto component mix shift.
* **Steel Strips Wheels Ltd:** Delivered record revenues and a 107% jump in EBITDA, backed by premiumization and a massive Rs 1,400 crore greenfield expansion.
* **Steelcast Ltd:** Reported strong Q2 revenue growth of 29.4% and expanded planned greenfield capacity outlay to Rs 151 crore.
* **Swastika Castal Ltd:** Delivered 24% YoY revenue growth in H1 with improved EBITDA margins and positive operating cash flow from new capacity.
* **Synergy Green Industries Ltd:** Maintained 33% FY27 revenue growth guidance driven by US wind demand, despite near-term margin pressure from inflation and logistics.
* **Talbros Automotive Components Ltd:** Delivered its strongest quarterly performance with 23% revenue growth and a 17.6% EBITDA margin.
