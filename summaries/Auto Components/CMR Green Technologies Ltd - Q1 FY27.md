# CMR Green Technologies Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume and revenue growth, robust demand, and capacity expansion despite global price volatility.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 3,122 crore, up 65% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 139 crore, up 27% YoY.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Over Rs 68 crore, up 22% YoY.
- **Sales Volume Growth:** Total volume up 25% YoY; Aluminium +32%, Billets +149%, UBC +333%.
- **EBITDA per kg:** Rs 12.40 per kg (or Rs 12,400 per ton).
- **Capex:** Rs 53 crore invested in greenfield projects in Q1.

### Highlights
- Installed recycling capacity targeted to go beyond 7 lakh tons per annum by the end of FY27.
- Added Ather Energy as a new customer in the EV two-wheeler segment.
- Liquid aluminium business continues to grow, forming over 50% of total aluminium sales.
- Cash conversion cycle improved to 65 days (down from 69 days in the previous quarter); inventory reduced to 40 days (down from 45 days).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a sustainable EBITDA guidance of Rs 12 per kg.
- Internal volume growth target set at 25% for FY27, supported by ramping up new plants and existing auto demand.
- Debt-equity ratio target remains around 0.5 (currently at 0.86 due to higher working capital from price spikes, expected to normalize as prices stabilize).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Automotive sector remains a key growth driver (PVs and 2-wheelers grew 26% and 20% YoY respectively in Q1; EV penetration reached 8.26% in FY26).
- Secondary aluminium consumption in India is growing at ~13% per annum, faster than primary at 7%-8%, currently accounting for 40% of total consumption.
- Favourable regulatory tailwinds include India's proposed EPR framework (increasing recycled content requirements from 5% in FY28 to 10%), EU CBAM, and the extrusion industry growing at a 12% CAGR through FY29.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Aluminium prices act as a pass-through; management emphasizes EBITDA per ton/kg over percentage margins as a true performance indicator.
- Margins supported by a hedging strategy, diversified scrap sourcing, and risk management policies.
- Other expenses included Rs 36 crore related to cash flow hedging accounting entries.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested Rs 53 crore in Q1 in greenfield projects at Shoolagiri and Bawal.
- Brownfield projects and technology-led initiatives are progressing at Tirupati and other locations.
- Hindalco JV plant in Odisha (48,000 tons per annum capacity) produced ~6,000 tons in the quarter and is ramping up towards full capacity utilization this fiscal year.
- Hindustan Zinc MoU remains in discussion with no construction activity initiated yet.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow was negative in Q1 due to a ~40% spike in average metal prices (from ~Rs 226-230/kg to Rs 350/kg) driving up working capital requirements.
- Debt levels expected to decrease as inventory days and working capital cycles are optimized using an AI-enabled import tracking system.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global environment remains uncertain due to geopolitical tensions, supply chain disruptions, and price volatility.
- Scrap sourcing is becoming more challenging as certain countries restrict exports (mitigated by expanding domestic sourcing and import networks).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pranav Jain (Alchemy) asked how secondary aluminium is hedged given its pricing lag to LME.** Management explained they balance customer cost-plus pricing with partial LME hedging.
- **Raj Shah asked about operational cash flow negativity and debt levels.** Management clarified that OCF is temporarily negative due to sharp price-led working capital expansion, which will reverse upon price stabilization; debt-equity is currently 0.86 against a historical 0.5 target.
- **Dheeraj Ram asked about scrap sourcing challenges.** Management acknowledged global export restrictions on scrap but stated they are mitigating this by expanding domestic sourcing and alternative import channels.
- **Bhavika Singhvi asked about the Hindalco joint venture status.** Management stated the Odisha plant produced 6,000 tons for the quarter and is on track to reach full capacity utilization within the financial year.

### One-line takeaway
CMR Green Technologies delivered a strong Q1 FY27 performance with 65% revenue growth and 25% volume growth, leveraging automotive demand and capacity expansion while managing working capital pressures through efficiency measures.
