# Tinna Rubber and Infrastructure Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent growth with strong year-on-year revenue and PAT expansion, and progress on strategic capacity expansions.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Q2 FY27:** Revenue grew 31% YoY to Rs 157 crore (up from Rs 120 crore); EBITDA grew 37% YoY to Rs 29 crore; PAT grew 45% YoY to Rs 17 crore.
- **Consolidated H1 FY27:** Revenue grew 25% to Rs 313 crore; EBITDA grew 50% to Rs 63 crore (margin 20.4%); PAT grew 60% to Rs 38 crore (margin 12.2%).
- **Segment Revenue (H1 FY27):** Infrastructure up 38% YoY to Rs 112 crore; Industrial up 37% YoY to Rs 85 crore; Consumer at Rs 21 crore (up from Rs 19 crore); Steel at Rs 57 crore (up from Rs 53 crore).
- **Polymer Composites & Masterbatch (PCMB):** Revenue more than tripled to Rs 27 crore in H1.
- **Power & Savings:** Renewable power met 40% of power needs in H1; solar power savings reached approx Rs 2.37 crore.
- **Balance Sheet:** Debt fell to Rs 111 crore from Rs 121 crore at the end of FY26; interest coverage improved to 9.91 from 7.49; net debt to equity at 0.32%.
- **Full Year FY27 Guidance:** Targeting revenue of around Rs 700 crore, EBITDA of Rs 126 crore, and PAT of Rs 70 crore.

### Highlights
- CARE Ratings upgraded the company's credit rating to CARE BBB (stable) for long-term facilities and CARE A3+ for short-term facilities.
- 3,500 tonnes per annum micronized rubber powder (MRP) expansion on track for Q3 FY27 commissioning, taking total MRP capacity to 20,000 tonnes per annum (making it the world's largest MRP producer).
- At the Varale plant, Tyre Pyrolysis Oil (TPO) commercial sales began in Q2 (expected to stabilize by Q3); recovered Carbon Black (rCB) production starts in Q3 with commercial sales in Q4.
- Completed acquisition of 40 acres of land in Andhra Pradesh for an integrated rubber and plastics recycling complex (planned investment of approx Rs 250 crore over 3 years).
- Completed Rs 45 crore of capex in H1 out of Rs 100 crore planned across FY27 and FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its FY27 guidance of around Rs 700 crore revenue, Rs 126 crore EBITDA, and Rs 70 crore PAT.
- PCMB business is expected to contribute 8% to 10% of FY27 revenue, with target top line of approx Rs 70 crore in FY27 and growing to about Rs 125 crore next year.
- Vision 2029 targets: Revenue of Rs 1,000 crore, revenue CAGR above 25%, profitability CAGR above 33%, EBITDA margins above 18%, ROCE above 30%, and expansion to 8-10 locations.
- TP Buildtech targets revenue of Rs 100 crore by FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Tyre crushing volumes grew 30% YoY in H1 to 86,000 tonnes. Production of MRP and reclaimed rubber rose by 19% on steady tyre industry demand.
- Export volumes grew by approx 15%, contributing 6% to total revenue.
- High energy, natural rubber, and synthetic rubber prices have made the use of recycled rubber products even more compelling for customers, assisting in passing on cost increases.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA margin stood at 18.8% against 18.0% YoY (PAT margin 10.9% against 9.8%). Profitability moderated from Q1 due to start-up costs, geopolitical uncertainties, and subsidiary performances.
- PCMB operates at a lower EBITDA margin than the core business due to its newness and ongoing R&D expenses.
- Recovered Carbon Black is priced at least 30%-35% cheaper than virgin carbon black.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- India tyre recycling capacity is currently at 185,000 tonnes per annum, plus 15,000 tonnes in Oman (total 200,000 tonnes).
- Andhra Pradesh 40-acre greenfield project will house tyre recycling, plastics recycling, pyrolysis, rCB, PCMB, and construction chemicals. Groundbreaking expected in November.
- Oman plant ran at 90% utilization in H1 with EBITDA margins improving to 11.5% (from 6.4% in FY26); Oman reported PAT of Rs 1.3 crore in H1.
- South Africa Phase 1 complete; 9,000 tonnes per annum crumb line (Phase 2) under commissioning (delayed by 3-4 months due to supply chain disruptions). South Africa and Saudi Arabia combined recorded a start-up loss of Rs 94 lakh.
- Saudi Arabia: Plot allotted for a 24,000 tonnes per annum tyre recycling plant; construction expected to begin in H2 FY27 subject to the geopolitical situation.
- Set up Tinna Rubber Chile SpA in Santiago to widen end-of-life tyre sourcing network.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt reduced to Rs 111 crore from Rs 121 crore in FY26.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Panipat plant land (oldest and smallest facility making crumb rubber and MRP) may be acquired by the Haryana government; management stated that compensation would exceed book value and relocation would take 3-4 months without material operational impact.
- Saudi Arabia project construction has been delayed due to the West Asia conflict.
- Imported feedstock for pyrolysis is currently not permitted in India, requiring domestic procurement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vidish Asher (Shagun Capital) on EBITDA margin fluctuations:** Management clarified that 18% plus is the sustainable target, while Q1 was an aberration driven by raw material/inventory benefits, and PCMB currently operates at lower margins due to start-up phase.
- **Ashray Sheth (Ventura Securities) on reclaim rubber in road construction:** Management clarified that crumb rubber modifier (CRM) is used in bitumen for roads (not reclaim rubber), and the process is well-established under IRC guidelines.
- **Dheeraj Ram (360 ONE Capital) on EPR income:** Management stated EPR income was Rs 4.6 crore in Q2 (vs. Rs 6.7 crore in Q1).
- **Krish Jain (NAFA Asset Managers) on capacity additions:** Management explained that the near-term capacity additions are driven by debottlenecking (10,000-15,000 tonnes) and South Africa scaling up, while larger greenfield expansions like Andhra Pradesh will reflect in FY28/FY29.
- **Aaryan Doshi (Divitiae Investments) on realization drop:** Management attributed the per-tonne revenue movement to product mix changes across diverse sectors.

### One-line takeaway
Tinna Rubber delivered strong H1 FY27 performance with 25% revenue and 60% PAT growth, maintaining an 18%+ EBITDA margin while advancing major global and domestic circular economy expansions.
