# SKF India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong YoY revenue growth and stable volume, offset by margin compression due to a timing gap in passing on raw material cost inflation.

### Key numbers
- **Sales Growth:** 16% YoY and 2% QoQ (purely volume-driven, with marginal impact from price/mix).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14.5% (improved by 155 bps YoY, but dropped by 255 bps QoQ).
- **PBT (without exceptional items):** Higher by 181 bps YoY, but dropped by 252 bps QoQ.
- **Net Working Capital:** 18.8% of sales (2.9% lower YoY and improved compared to March end).
- **Sales Mix (Q2):** OE business 63%-66% (referred to as 66% for the quarter and 63% for H1), Vehicle Aftermarket 19% (20% in H1), Exports 10% (9% in H1), SKF Industrial 5% (7% in H1).
- **Capex:** Rs 140 crore announced per SEBI requirement; overall current fiscal capex expected to be close to Rs 200 crore (part of a larger Rs 500+ crore capex plan).
- **Cash in hand:** Around Rs 100 crore at the end of the quarter.
- **Macro Indicators:** GDP growth projected at 7.35% for the last quarter; CPI at ~4.9%; Manufacturing PMI at 55.1 in September 2026.

### Highlights
- Secured a major Driveline business win from a major Indian OEM for hybrid transmission technology, featuring electric vehicle offerings like ceramic bearings and conductive bushes (production to start in Q2 FY28).
- Officially launched a new global brand identity and visual identity connecting legacy heritage with future mobility.
- Demerger-related expenses are largely over.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to revert to EBITDA margins above 15% within the current financial year.
- Expects to recover the majority of the cost inflation impact in the December quarter through ongoing customer price negotiations.
- Expects next year's volume growth to be around 15% or upward of 15%.
- Trading/imports from SKF Industrials are expected to reduce to a minimal/negligible level over the next couple of years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Passenger vehicle market currently dealing with CAFE III norms; BS-7 discussions are more relevant for commercial vehicle customers at a development stage.
- Two-wheeler revenue contributes roughly 45%-50%, passenger vehicles 25%-30%, and commercial vehicles 15%-20%.
- Vehicle aftermarket volume has stabilized following a 7% YoY degrowth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- QoQ drop in EBITDA and PBT driven by high cost of goods sold (COGS) from steel price inflation and value-addition price increases given to vendors.
- A timing gap exists: price increases were given to suppliers/vendors effective July/April, while customer price increase amendments are under negotiation/awaiting PO amendments.
- Other expenses include variable costs (stores, spares, power, fuel, royalty) which scale with volume growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New manufacturing channels starting production: one line in December Q, one channel going live in Haridwar in March '27, capacity enhancement in March '27, and a third new channel in July '27.
- New production channels will partially replace trading volumes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital stands at 18.8% of sales.
- Cash in hand at the end of the quarter is approximately Rs 100 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Hyper-competitive behavior and scattered demand in the Vehicle Aftermarket.
- Steel price inflation and vendor-side cost pressures impacting short-term margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Varun Jain (Anand Rathi / Other):** Asked about EBITDA margin drop and capex announcement. Management explained the COGS/timing gap on pricing and clarified that the Rs 140 crore disclosure is a SEBI compliance notification, while total annual capex remains around Rs 200 crore.
- **Krupashankar NJ:** Asked about export sustainability and royalty fees. Management stated exports will hover around 10% of the mix, and trademark/royalty percentages remain unchanged post-demerger.
- **Lakshminarayanan:** Inquired about system-level vs. component strategy and margin recovery. Management reiterated they play strictly as component suppliers (focusing on performance outcomes like reduced friction rather than sub-assemblies) and expect to recoup almost all margin losses by the December quarter.
- **Sachin Maniar:** Asked about related-party transactions with SKF Industrial and EV content per vehicle. Management stated rental and common service charges will phase out over 2 years as space is reallocated to internal production, and EV content per vehicle will see a marginal increase due to high-value ceramic/conductive components.

### One-line takeaway
SKF India delivered 16% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, but margins contracted QoQ due to a timing mismatch in passing on steel and vendor inflation, which management expects to recover by the December quarter.
