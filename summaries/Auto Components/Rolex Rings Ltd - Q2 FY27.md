# Rolex Rings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strongest quarter in the company's history on revenue, margins, efficiency, and profitability driven by demand recovery and higher-margin auto components.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 389 crore (up 43% YoY, 28% QoQ)
- **Component Sales (Rings + Auto Components):** Rs 330 crore (up 32% YoY)
- **Auto Components Revenue:** Rs 177 crore (up 68% YoY); contributes 54% of overall sales
- **Bearing Rings Revenue:** Rs 153 crore (up 6% YoY)
- **Exports:** Rs 167 crore (up 55% YoY); accounts for 51% of component sales
- **U.S. Component Revenue:** More than doubled YoY to Rs 92 crore
- **Gross Margin:** Increased by almost 700 bps YoY
- **EBITDA:** Rs 94 crore (up 72% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 24.2% (up nearly 400 bps YoY); normalized excluding duty refund is around 21%
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 74 crore (up 68% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 19.1% (up 280 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 694 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 163 crore (up 40% YoY; margin 23.5%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 134 crore (up 44% YoY; margin 19.4%)
- **Cash and Bank Balances / Liquid Investments:** Rs 260 crore
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 68 crore
- **Capacity Utilization:** 65% currently; expected to move towards 70%-72% by the end of FY27
- **Order Book:** Rs 120 crore - Rs 125 crore per month for H2 FY27

### Highlights
- Recorded the strongest quarter in the company's history on revenue, margins, efficiency, and profitability.
- Revenue includes a one-time benefit of approximately Rs 18 crore from U.S. tariff duty refunds recognized as revenue.
- Auto components share increased to 54% from 42% in Q2 FY of the previous fiscal, driving margins.
- Company executed a buyback of Rs 180 crore during H1 and remains completely debt-free.
- U.S. tariffs are no longer impacting the business; customers have accepted the current duty structure, and order flows have normalized.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1 driven by new programs scaling up and new customer plants commencing supplies in Q3 and early Q4.
- Confident of delivering guided revenue growth for FY27.
- FY28 revenue growth expected to move towards high teens.
- FY28 EBITDA margin expected to be 50-75 bps higher than FY27.
- Operating margins expected to move back into the guided range of 21%-22% as freight rates decline.
- Auto component segment growth expected to be a minimum of 20%-22% YoY going forward.
- Defense and aerospace revenue expected over the next 12-18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- U.S. market rebounded strongly after customers held back in FY26.
- Industrial demand in Europe remains soft, but the company is reducing dependency by developing new overseas bearing ring customers.
- End-user industry contribution in Q2 FY27: Passenger vehicles 47%, commercial and heavy vehicles 30%, industrial 17%-19%, and EV hybrid consistent.
- Domestic bearing business grew 10% in H1 despite soft patches; domestic bearing customers are increasing wallet share.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded due to a richer product mix (higher auto component share) and disciplined raw material management.
- Company has a 100% pass-through mechanism for commodity/metal price movements.
- Ocean freight ran nearly 3x normal levels, fuel gas prices rose 40%, and consumable costs increased due to the West Asia conflict, elevating other expenses. Total ocean freight cost in H1 was over Rs 25 crore compared to Rs 10 crore in H1 FY26.
- Ocean freight rates are observing a decline starting October 2026.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned annual capex of Rs 30 crore - Rs 40 crore.
- Forging and machining facilities are fungible across bearing rings and auto components.
- Evaluating inorganic options to add value-added processes in India via a global agency.
- Received initial registrations and approvals in defense and aerospace sectors; focusing on round and hot forge components.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is completely debt-free.
- Working capital increased by Rs 74 crore in H1 due to higher inventory and receivables from Q2 step-up sales and longer transit times on export shipments; expected to unwind in H2 as freight normalizes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External geopolitical and economic factors in overseas markets could impact demand.
- Time lag exists between actual raw material/commodity price changes and customer price realization updates.
- Ocean freight and consumable cost spikes impact short-term expenses before customer sharing/reimbursements are settled.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital)** asked why Rolex Rings' gross margins expanded while peers reported declines, and about auto component export sustainability. Management replied that product mix shift toward auto components and a 100% commodity pass-through mechanism drove margins, and guided for auto component revenues above Rs 550-575 crore in FY27.
2. **Garvit Goyal** asked about the sustainability of the order book and Europe's demand environment. Management stated that monthly order books stand at Rs 120-125 crore, European auto component orders are ramping up, and potential exists for 25%-30% growth by FY29 subject to no geopolitical shocks.
3. **Mihir Vora** asked for a detailed breakup of component revenues, scrap, incentives, and duty refunds. Management provided exact segment breakdowns and clarified that a recurring Rs 15-odd crore duty reimbursement from customers is linked to goods lifted from warehouses.
4. **Kishore Kumar** questioned why domestic bearing growth lagged industry peers. Management clarified that Q1 was impacted by labor constraints, but Q2 domestic bearing revenue rebounded to Rs 115 crore (a 25% QoQ increase), bringing H1 growth to 10%.
5. **Dhiraj** inquired about volume growth and capacity utilization. Management stated that Q2 volume growth was ~18% QoQ, current capacity utilization is 65% (touching 67%-68% recently), and is expected to reach 70%-72% by year-end.

### One-line takeaway
Rolex Rings delivered its strongest quarter in history (Q2 FY27) with a 43% YoY revenue jump and a 24.2% EBITDA margin, underpinned by robust U.S. demand recovery, a favorable product mix shift toward auto components, and a debt-free balance sheet.
