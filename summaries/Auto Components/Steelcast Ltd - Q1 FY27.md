# Steelcast Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-06 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust financial performance, strong volume growth visibility, and strategic capacity expansion.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR124.82 crores (up 17% YoY from INR106.69 crores)
- EBITDA: INR35.24 crores (up 17.37% YoY from INR30.02 crores)
- EBITDA margin: 28.23% (vs 28.14% in Q1 FY26)
- PAT: INR23.71 crores (up 19.26% YoY from INR19.88 crores)
- PAT margin: 19% (vs 18.64% in Q1 FY26)
- Capacity utilization: 66% for Q1 FY27 (planned at ~63% for the full year FY27)
- Order book: INR140 crores (~3 to 4 months of forward business)
- Capex announced: Greenfield Foundry with 8,500 tonnes capacity, involving an investment of approximately INR120 crores over the next 2 years

### Highlights
- Renewable energy footprint being strengthened with two power plants under implementation: a 2.4-megawatt hybrid power plant (wind and solar) and a 1.4-megawatt solar power plant, both expected to be commissioned before December 31, 2026.
- Board approved a Greenfield Foundry of 8,500 tonnes capacity (5 kg to 1,000 kg castings) with a peak revenue potential of ~INR300 crores.
- Shifted track away from U.S. railroads for now due to better opportunities in other industrial sectors and markets.
- Maintained debt-free status, intending to fund the INR120 crores capex via internal accruals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a growth trajectory of approximately 20% CAGR over the coming years.
- For FY27, management expects a volume growth of 25% to 30%.
- Commissioning target for the new Greenfield plant is set for March 31, FY28, with commercial contributions starting in FY29.
- Sequential top-line improvement expected for several quarters.
- EBITDA margins expected to improve by 150 to 200 bps (targeting around 28.5% to 29%) due to operating leverage and input cost pass-throughs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global economy resilient with 3.3% GDP growth; India estimated at 6.5% to 7% for calendar year 2026.
- Casting and forging industry benefiting from robust demand in mining, earthmoving, construction, railways, defense, and other industrial sectors.
- Ground Engaging Tools (GET) revenue contribution expected to rise from ~1% currently to 3.5% in FY27, and 4.5% to 5% by FY29 (later mentioned as reaching up to 6% by FY29).
- Derisking strategy: Mining sector contribution dropped from 84% (10 years ago) to 27% currently; earthmoving is at 43%, construction at 15%, and other sectors at 15%. Export geographies expanded to 16 countries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input prices increased from March onwards; price correction on an upward basis is effective July 1, 2027, which will fully pass on raw material and fuel increases via standard sales price variation formulas.
- Natural gas prices are about 50% to 55% higher compared to February-end prices; company is exploring a transition from natural gas to electricity over the next 2 to 3 years for cost efficiency.
- Steelcast does not use ship-breaking scrap directly; it sources cleaner scrap with low sulfur, phosphorus, and carbon from downstream rolling mills and industries near Alang.
- Currency fluctuations (rupee appreciation/depreciation) are shared with customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Greenfield Foundry capex: INR120 crores over the next 2 years on a 100,000 square meter land parcel provided by the Government of Gujarat (located 12 km from the existing facility).
- Peak revenue potential from the new facility: ~INR300 crores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains a debt-free status and intends to fund future capex through internal cash accruals.
- Temporary inventory/WIP pile-up observed during ramp-ups, which typically normalizes over a 1-to-2-month lag.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management views the defense sector as high risk because it relies heavily on a single customer, prompting them to shift focus to better-priced industrial opportunities.
- Operating above 90% capacity utilization is discouraged due to potential manufacturing bottlenecks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankur Kumar (Alpha Capital) asked if the 25% guidance is volume or volume + price.** Management clarified it is purely volume growth, and price pass-throughs are separate.
- **Ankur Kumar asked about U.S. railroad recovery.** Management stated they have changed strategy and are deprioritizing U.S. railroads for more lucrative markets.
- **Mosam Shah (Wealth Guardian) asked about the 90% capacity utilization target.** Management clarified that the 90% target applies to *existing* capacities by FY29.
- **K Manunath (Individual Investor) suggested a rights issue and bonus issue.** Management declined the rights issue since they have sufficient reserves (INR120 crores) and want to avoid equity dilution, but agreed to take the bonus issue proposal to the Board.
- **Saket Saurabh (Sagari Capital) asked about export vs domestic classifications.** Management clarified that Steelcast acts as a Tier 1 supplier for 98%–99% of its sales directly to OEMs, though OEMs may indirectly export some finished goods.

### One-line takeaway
Steelcast reported a solid Q1 FY27 with 17% YoY revenue growth and robust margins, backed by a strong multi-sector demand pipeline, a newly announced INR120 crores Greenfield expansion, and a debt-free balance sheet.
