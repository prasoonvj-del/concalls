# Auto Components - Auto electricals & electronics: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the auto electricals and electronics sector reflected robust top-line momentum, with most companies reporting 30% to 60% YoY revenue growth. Performance was propelled by strong vehicle production across two-wheelers and passenger vehicles, festive demand, and rising EV penetration. However, this revenue expansion was universally tempered by margin headwinds caused by surging commodity inflation (copper, aluminium, bare PCBs, memory chips), freight rate spikes, and minimum wage revisions. While companies aggressively expanded capex and greenfield capacities to capture future demand, profitability faced a squeeze due to time lags in passing cost increases on to original equipment manufacturers (OEMs).

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the board noted strong domestic volume growth in Q2, aided by festive season demand and favorable macroeconomic conditions. However, a near-term consensus points to a likely moderation in YoY percentage growth in H2 due to a high base effect from the previous year.
* **Divergence:** EV adoption rates and growth metrics varied significantly by portfolio. Sona BLW reported BEV revenue crossing 50% of total automotive revenue, and Dhoot reported EV revenue up 108% YoY (31% of sales). Conversely, companies with higher exposure to traditional ICE segments, like Motherson Sumi Wiring and UNO Minda, reported EV contributions closer to 8%–11%. Export demand also split: companies like ASK Automotive and Sona BLW saw surging exports, while UNO Minda noted a decline in passenger vehicle exports due to the West Asia crisis.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
Managements universally highlighted severe cost pressures during the quarter. 
* **Key Cost Pressures:** Commodity inflation in copper (noted by Dhoot, Minda Corp, MSWIL), aluminium (ASK, UNO Minda), rare bare PCBs and memory chips (Pricol, Sedemac), alongside double-digit minimum wage hikes (ASK, Minda Corp). Freight rates also doubled sequentially, according to Sona BLW.
* **Pass-Through Lags:** Most companies (Lumax, Minda Corp, MSWIL, Pricol, Varroc) noted a 1-to-3 quarter lag in recovering these costs from OEMs through price indexation or negotiation. Pricol explicitly estimated a 1% to 2% hit to EBITDA from unrecoverable cost escalations. Others, such as ASK and Sona BLW, experienced margin percentage dilution due to the "denominator effect" of high raw material pass-through pricing.

## Capex & capacity
Driven by robust demand and expanding order books, companies are aggressively committing capital:
* **ASK Automotive:** Budgeted ~Rs 700 crore for FY27; ~Rs 500 crore for FY28.
* **Dhoot Transmission:** Increased capex guidance to Rs 750 crore, planning 5 new plants over 12 months.
* **Lumax Auto Technologies:** Maintained ~Rs 300 crore annual capex, alongside a major new Mechatronics facility outlay of ~Rs 1,500 crore.
* **Minda Corp:** Investing ~Rs 200 crore in a greenfield TFT display module facility.
* **UNO Minda:** Board approved Rs 1,415 crore in strategic project capex, including aluminum die-casting and TGSIN plants.
* **Varroc Engineering:** Maintained FY27 capex guidance at Rs 500–550 crore.
* **LAPL Automotive & Permanent Magnets:** Progressing on fourth manufacturing facility and Quantum Magnetics Phase 2, respectively.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Upgrades:** Dhoot Transmission raised its annual growth guidance from 25–30% to >40%; Lumax Auto Technologies raised its revenue growth guidance to 20–25% (up from implied 12–14%); ASK Automotive raised its export revenue guidance to cross Rs 180 crore in FY27.
* **Debt Milestones:** Dhoot Transmission successfully fulfilled its Q1 promise by fully repaying IPO-related debt, achieving a net cash position of Rs 900 crore.

## What to watch next quarter
* Success and speed of customer price negotiations to recover lagging commodity, PCB, and wage inflation.
* Ramp-up execution and pilot production progress at newly commissioned greenfield plants (e.g., LAPL’s 4th facility, Lumax Mechatronics, UNO Minda’s Kharkhoda/Hosur plants, Minda Corp’s TFT facility).
* Geopolitical developments, particularly regarding West Asian conflicts affecting freight rates, component supply chains (PCBs/memory), and export stability.

## Company one-liners
* **ASK Automotive Ltd:** Delivered its 12th consecutive quarter of record-breaking financial performance in Q2 FY27, outgrowing the two-wheeler industry by leveraging strong order execution, new HPDC alloy wheel supplies, and robust internal cash generation.
* **Dhoot Transmission Ltd:** Delivered a strong Q2 with 60% YoY revenue growth and a net cash position of Rs 900 crore, prompting an upward revision in annual growth guidance to >40%.
* **LAPL Automotive Ltd:** Despite a muted H1 impacted by high capacity utilization and customer delays, LAPL Automotive expects a strong H2 rebound to deliver ~40% annual growth, supported by IPO proceeds funding its fourth manufacturing facility and new OEM order wins.
* **Lumax Auto Technologies Ltd:** Delivered robust Q2 FY 27 performance with 35% YoY revenue growth and 50% YoY PAT growth, prompting an upward revision in full-year revenue growth guidance to 20-25%.
* **Minda Corporation Ltd:** Delivered a landmark Q2 FY27 with record revenue of Rs 2,189 crores (up 43% YoY) and EBITDA of Rs 257 crores, propelled by robust EV growth, key electronics order wins, and a strategic push toward backward integration.
* **Motherson Sumi Wiring India Ltd:** MSWIL delivered a strong quarter with robust volume growth and margin recovery, successfully navigating commodity headwinds through operational efficiency and customer-led cost pass-throughs.
* **Permanent Magnets Ltd:** Permanent Magnets delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 49% revenue growth and 25% EBITDA margins, propelled by an EV export demand revival, while new divisions (alloys, relays, Quantum Magnetics) ramp up toward FY28.
* **Pricol Ltd:** Pricol delivered a strong 35% revenue jump to cross Rs. 1,000 crores in Q2, but margins face severe pressure from surging bare PCB, memory, and forex costs that may not be fully passed on.
* **Sedemac Mechatronics Ltd:** Sedemac delivered record Q2/H1 FY27 performance with 40%+ revenue growth led by robust ISG and EV product ramp-ups, though margins face near-term pressure from raw material and PCB inflation.
* **Sona BLW Precision Forgings Ltd:** Sona Comstar delivered a record quarter with 35% YoY revenue growth and BEV revenue exceeding 50% of auto sales, underpinned by strong EV demand and new product introductions.
* **UNO Minda Ltd:** Uno Minda delivered record quarterly performance with 38% revenue growth, maintaining margin guidance despite commodity headwinds while aggressively scaling capacity across core and EV segments.
* **Varroc Engineering Ltd:** Varroc reported robust Q2 FY 2027 revenue growth of 33.6% YoY led by India and overseas expansion, while taking strategic actions like the Italy restructuring and Forsee Power acquisition to bolster its EV portfolio despite temporary war-induced margin headwinds.
