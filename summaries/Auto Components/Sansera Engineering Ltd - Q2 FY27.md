# Sansera Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT driven by broad-based growth across automotive and non-automotive segments, despite cost headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 10,915 million, up 32% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 21,127 million, up 33% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** INR 2,112 million, up 48% YoY; EBITDA margin at 19.4% (up ~200 bps YoY, up 15 bps QoQ). H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR 4,073 million, margin at 19.3%.
- **PAT:** INR 1,086 million, up 52% YoY; PAT margin at 10% (H1 FY27 PAT: INR 1,960 million, up 46% YoY; normalized H1 PAT: INR 2,087 million excluding Q1 litigation settlement of INR 169 million).
- **Auto ICE Sales:** INR 6,697 million, up 18% YoY.
- **Auto-Tech Agnostic & xEV Sales:** Combined sales of INR 1,363 million (best-ever quarter).
- **Non-Automotive Sales:** Crossed INR 2,000 million, contributing 20.9% of total sales; ADS revenue at INR 1,643 million (H1 ADS revenue: INR 3,097 million).
- **Order Backlogs:** ADS cumulative unexecuted lifetime order book executable over next five years stood at INR 58.7 billion (up from INR 44.4 billion in June). Non-ADS order book (peak annual sales) stood at INR 22.4 billion (up from INR 18.5 billion in June).
- **Net Debt & Working Capital:** Net debt rose to INR 2,612 million at end-September from INR 604 million in March. Working capital rose to INR 8,544 million (19.6% of annualized Q2 revenue). Operating cash flow for H1 was INR 2,073 million.
- **Capex Guidance:** FY27 capex projection raised to INR 8,000 million.
- **Other Metrics:** Gross margin expanded 180 bps YoY to 43%. Employee costs rose 25% to INR 1,469 million (including INR 167 million Karnataka minimum wage provision). Other expenses rose 41% to INR 1,114 million. Depreciation rose 20% to INR 602 million. Finance cost rose to INR 146 million.

### Highlights
- Highest ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT achieved in Q2 FY27.
- Auto ICE accounts for about 65% of sales, aligning with IPO targets.
- Commercial production at the Sansera-Nichidai JV expected to commence towards the end of H2 FY 2027.
- Dedicated defense facility expected to begin operations towards the end of Q3 FY 2027.
- Won first business from Safran during the quarter.
- *Record continuation note:* Planned FY27 capex of INR 8,000 million is confirmed in the call, matching our earlier record.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year FY27 revenue to be between INR 4,250 crores and INR 4,300 crores.
- ADS revenue target is set around 35%-40% of overall Sansera revenue towards the end of the decade.
- Management plans to release a new 5-year vision statement and target metrics at the end of the financial year as the current 5-year IPO vision period concludes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic passenger vehicle wholesales crossed 5 lakh units in October for the first time.
- Elevated order backlogs, lean dealer inventories, and strong demand for premium technologically advanced vehicles provide strong visibility for Q3 FY 2027.
- Global customers continue to diversify supply chains, favoring Indian manufacturers with scale and process design capabilities.
- Active discussions ongoing with a North American humanoid robot OEM for complex aluminum forged and machined components.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins impacted by increased costs across cutting tools, freight, and logistics due to renewed conflict in West Asia, and a provisional 30% increase in minimum wages in Karnataka (provision of INR 167 million booked).
- Margin expansion driven by richer product mix (higher ADS and export share) and operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex investments totaled about INR 3,260 million plus INR 300 million invested in the Nichidai JV, exceeding operating cash flow and increasing net debt.
- FY27 capex projection raised to INR 8,000 million to fund faster kick-starting programs in ADS, semiconductors, energy, and xEVs (approx. 50% or INR 400 crores going toward ADS).
- Planned new ADS facility expansion includes 120,000 sq ft near existing plants, targeting operations by the end of the next calendar year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased to INR 2,612 million as of September 2026 (leverage at ~0.3x of annualized H1 EBITDA).
- Working capital increased due to higher ADS revenue share requiring inventory support and higher volumes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cost pressures from West Asia conflict (freight, logistics, cutting tools) and Karnataka minimum wage revision.
- Board is weighing additional operational risks of setting up a manufacturing plant in the U.S. due to tariff uncertainties, leading to a hold on U.S. plant plans in favor of outsourcing from India.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Siddhartha Bera (Nomura) on ADS order book and capex split:** Management clarified that 50% of the INR 8,000 crore capex goes to ADS, driven by faster program lead times and filling up the second bay in Plant 9.
2. **Chandramouli (Equities) on Rolls-Royce engine opportunity:** Management stated that development is ongoing in Q3, but current capex does not factor in potential engine manufacturer business until confirmed wins materialize.
3. **Pratik Jain on FY 2031 ADS revenue target:** Management targeted ADS to comprise 35%-40% of overall company revenue by the end of the decade.
4. **Mumuksh Mandlesha on Karnataka minimum wage provision:** Management confirmed this is not a one-off and represents a provisional ~30% base wage increase (~INR 5,500), though challenged in court.
5. **Gaurav Nagori on humanoid robot opportunities:** Management confirmed active, interesting discussions with a pioneer North American humanoid manufacturing customer for complex components.
6. **Yash Agarwal on Europe/Sweden utilization and outlook:** Management stated Sweden operates at 80%-85% efficiency with stable execution linked to its single major customer, expecting a steady-state operation.

### One-line takeaway
Sansera reported record Q2 FY27 performance with 32% revenue growth and margin resilience, backed by surging non-automotive ADS orders and a raised INR 8,000 crore capex plan for FY27.
