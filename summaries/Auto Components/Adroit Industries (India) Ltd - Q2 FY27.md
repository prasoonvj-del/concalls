# Adroit Industries (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong sequential growth in revenue and profit after tax, supported by capacity expansion and new segment entries.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 426.73 crore (up 25% sequentially)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 119.56 crore (up 13% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 85.47 crore (up 14% sequentially)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 767.87 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 225.07 crore (up 48% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 29.3% (compared to 22% in FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 160.36 crore (up 51% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EPS:** Rs 3.58 (compared to Rs 2.37 YoY)
- **Exports (H1 FY27):** 82.36% of revenue (US accounts for 59.56% of sales)
- **Repeat Customers (Q2 FY27):** 87.4% of sales
- **Order Book:** Rs 1,421 crore (78% export, 22% domestic orders)
- **Capacity Utilisation:** Dewas plant at 93%-94%; Pithampur plant at 81%
- **Working Capital Cycle:** 248-249 days

### Highlights
- Completed post-IPO listing phase and maintaining ongoing dialogue with the investment community.
- Added new customers during the quarter and recently entered industrial-shaft segments.
- Formed a wholly-owned subsidiary in Canada and the United States (registered April 2026) with a local inventory warehouse in Memphis, US.
- Defence sector currently contributes a very small share of revenue; management intends to develop it progressively through technical qualifications and approvals.

### Guidance & outlook
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Management aims to maintain EBITDA margins at 30%, plus or minus 2 percentage points.
- **Mid-term Vision:** Management has a mid-term vision for FY31, though immediate focus is on executing expansion and meeting current targets.
- **Revenue Outlook:** Management stated that if they can exceed last year's revenue by 25%, it would be a good achievement given capacity constraints before new expansions come online.
- **Peak Revenue Potential:** Expanded manufacturing platform could reach a peak of Rs 2,500 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains strong across automotive and non-automotive sectors (construction, mining, firefighting, industrial, defence, locomotive, drilling).
- Global competitors include Dana Spicer, Cummins/Arvin Meritor, and Neapco.
- Seeing strong interest in North and South American markets (US, Canada, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Chile, Venezuela, Mexico, Nicaragua, Honduras, Panama).
- Indian OEM business (such as BEML and Volvo) is growing, featuring shorter payment cycles of 45-60 days compared to exports.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Finished assemblies generate higher margins (5% to 10% higher than components) due to value addition.
- Margins supported by disciplined procurement, manufacturing efficiency, and appropriate pricing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pithampur plant: Machining and assembly capacity to increase from 900,000 units to 1,800,000 units (operational by Q4).
- Dewas plant: Forging capacity to increase from 3,000 tonnes to 5,500 tonnes (operational by Q1 FY28).
- Full ramp-up targeted by Q4 FY28 following commissioning.
- Management expects to manage funding for peak revenue potential without raising additional funds.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital cycle is currently 248-249 days due to a vast SKU range (5,250 different numbers) and extended sea transit times for exports.
- Growth in the Indian OEM business is expected to help moderate the overall working capital cycle down to the 200-230 days range over time.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Each defence and industrial application requires substantial testing and validation periods ranging from six months to over a year.
- High SKU complexity requires holding extensive inventory at various manufacturing stages and in transit.

### One-line takeaway
Adroit Industries delivered strong Q2 FY27 financial growth driven by robust export demand and repeat customers, while on track with capacity expansions to support a mid-term vision toward Rs 2,500 crore in peak revenue.
