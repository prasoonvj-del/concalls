# Shivalik Bimetal Controls Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong consolidated growth driven by a strategic shift toward high-value-added components, despite temporary inventory accumulation.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** 39% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Growth:** 48% YoY.
*   **PAT Growth:** 40% YoY.
*   **Shunt Volume:** Declined by 16% (attributed to shift from raw strip supply to finished components).
*   **Assembly Business Potential:** Rs 100-200 crore.
*   **Capacity Utilization (Contacts):** ~55% (estimated by management).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Management is transitioning from supplying raw strip materials to finished assemblies/components, which improves value-add and profitability.
*   **Inventory:** Management described the current inventory buildup as a "temporary phenomenon" driven by development cycles for new programs and supply chain stabilization.
*   **Leadership:** A new CFO is joining by the end of December; the company is actively searching for a COO and senior R&D/marketing talent to support future growth.
*   **Record Note:** The Pune facility Phase I is now fully functional (as of July), continuing the pattern of scaling value-added capacity without requiring major greenfield risk.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management expects 30-35% YoY top-line growth to be sustainable for the next 2 years.
*   **Margins:** Management anticipates a potential margin dip of 1.5-2.5% as the assembly business (15-16% EBITDA margin) scales up relative to the core business (26-27% EBITDA margin).
*   **Long-term Mix:** The company aims for a 50-50 revenue split between high-margin core products and the newer assembly business.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Drivers:** Growth is currently driven by core products; PCBA/CCS (assembly) contributions are still in early stages.
*   **EV Market:** Strong traction in the EV two-wheeler segment in India; four-wheeler adoption is slower.
*   **Emerging Opportunities:** Data centers and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are identified as future growth areas.
*   **Geographic Shift:** Some US revenue is being reclassified as Asian revenue as supply chains shift to direct delivery to end-users outside the US.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Commodities:** Silver and copper price fluctuations are largely pass-through; management stated they have minimal impact on the bottom line.
*   **Value-Add:** The shift to finished components is the primary driver of improved profitability, offsetting volume declines in raw material shipments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Focus:** Capex is directed toward downstream processes, automation, and end-of-line calibration/testing rather than large greenfield projects.
*   **Flexibility:** Management noted that downstream capacity can be enhanced quickly to meet demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Management stated that the recent dividend announcement does not impact funding availability for future projects.
*   **Reserves:** The company maintains sufficient free cash/reserves for staggered capital allocation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Inventory:** Elevated inventory levels due to development cycles and design changes.
*   **Margin Dilution:** The planned scale-up of lower-margin assembly business may compress overall EBITDA margins.
*   **Development Cycles:** New programs require long validation cycles, making exact timing of revenue realization difficult to predict.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Inventory Buildup (Bhavya Nahar):** Management clarified it is a temporary result of development work and supply chain shifts, not geopolitical stocking.
*   **Shunt Volume Decline (Mahek Talati):** Management explained that shipping fewer kilograms of material (strips) but more finished components (value-add) is a positive, not a negative.
*   **US Revenue Decline (Dhruv Jain):** Management clarified this is a reclassification; the business is still with the same customers but is now being shipped directly to their Asian operations.
*   **Leadership Gaps (Naushad Chaudhary):** Management confirmed they are hiring a CFO (joining Dec), a COO, and senior R&D/marketing staff to free up the MD/WTD for strategic expansion.
*   **Assembly Business (Nirali Gopani):** Management reiterated that assembly is an extension of the core business, not a standalone vertical, and will be treated as a separate reporting bucket.

### One-line takeaway
Shivalik is successfully transitioning from a raw material supplier to a high-value-add component manufacturer, with management guiding for sustained 30-35% growth despite expected margin dilution from the new assembly product mix.
