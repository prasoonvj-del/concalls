# Happy Forgings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume-led top-line growth, resilient ~30% EBITDA margins, and board approval of an additional Rs 500 crore capex on strong multi-year demand visibility.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 501 crore, +32.7% YoY. (H1 FY27: Rs 950 crore, +29.9% YoY).
- **Finished goods volume:** +24.8% YoY in Q2 FY27 (+24% in H1 FY27).
- **Realisation:** Rs 267 per kg, +6.3% YoY in Q2 FY27 (Rs 260 per kg, +4.8% YoY in H1 FY27).
- **Gross profit:** Rs 305 crore, +33.8% YoY; margin expanded 52 bps YoY to 60.8%.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 153 crore, +32.2% YoY; EBITDA margin at 30.6% (vs. 30.9% in H1 FY27, +127 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 98 crore, +32.8% YoY; PAT margin at 19.5% (H1 FY27: Rs 189 crore, +35.8% YoY, margin 19.9%).
- **Return ratios (H1 FY27):** ROE at 17.1%, ROCE at 19.6% (ROCE on operational assets excluding cash, CWIP, and advances was >25%).

### Highlights
- Crossed the Rs 500 crore quarterly revenue milestone in Q2 FY27.
- Machining contribution reached 90% of revenue in H1 FY27 vs. 88% in H1 FY26.
- The company continues its aggressive capacity expansion phase with the board approving an additional Rs 500 crore capex program beyond the ongoing Rs 650 crore capex.
- Segmental revenue share (H1 FY27): Commercial Vehicles 33% (up high teens YoY), Farm Equipment 30% (up >20% YoY), Industrials 16% (up >50% YoY), Off-highway 12% (up >50% YoY), Passenger Vehicles 9% (nearly doubled YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to meet full-year volume growth guidance backed by a healthy order book and ramp-ups.
- FY27 full-year profitability margins expected to remain roughly similar to FY26 levels.
- Gross margin and EBITDA per kg expected to trend upwards.
- Solar project commissioning in Q4 FY27 will add ~1.5% to EBITDA and save ~Rs 30 crore in power costs annually starting Q1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **CV:** Domestic grew in mid-20s driven by infrastructure, replacement, and transit demand; CV exports remained subdued due to longer shipment times.
- **Farm Equipment:** Gained domestic market share (from 42% to 46%), outperforming the underlying market; no revenue slowdown anticipated.
- **Industrials & Off-highway:** Benefiting heavily from backup power generators for data centers, energy/gas engines, and infrastructure construction awards.
- **Order Book:** >50% of the current order book and inquiry pipeline is tilted towards exports.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price changes are passed through; 80% of domestic OEM cost increases were passed through.
- Export ocean freight rates doubled in Q2 FY27 (peaked, now softening), exerting temporary pressure on selling costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Existing capacity:** Forging at 158,000 MT (utilization 60% in H1 FY27), Machining at 82,000 MT (utilization 77% in H1 FY27; touched 80% in Q2 and expected at ~90% by Q4).
- **Ongoing Rs 650 crore capex:** Ultra-heavy components and captive solar on track for completion/commercial operations in Q4 FY28; ~Rs 300 crore already invested, ~Rs 50 crore balance in FY27, ~Rs 250 crore in FY28. Cold trials to start in Jan-Feb 2027.
- **New Rs 500 crore capex:** Approved by board for FY28-FY29; 70% towards machining, 30% towards forging (small press lines). Land already acquired 6 months ago.
- Total combined planned capacity addition: 59,000 MT forging and 34,000 MT machining, taking total capacity to ~217,000 MT forging and ~116,000 MT machining in 2 to 2.5 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and liquid reserves stood at ~Rs 350 crore.
- Net debt to equity at a conservative 0.08x; Total outside liabilities to net worth at 0.21x.
- H1 FY27 Capex incurred: ~Rs 378 crore.
- H1 FY27 Operating cash flow stood at ~Rs 124 crore, temporarily impacted by higher receivables from top-line expansion and Rs 35 crore advance payments to raw material suppliers.
- Expected to raise up to Rs 300 crore bank debt as bridge financing for the new Rs 500 crore capex.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High ocean freights doubling selling costs for exports during the quarter.
- Subdued CV export dispatches caused by longer shipping transit times (sales in transit).
- Pending promoter dilution required to comply with SEBI minimum public shareholding norms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Capex and Demand Visibility:** Management clarified the new Rs 500 crore capex is primarily for machining (70%) where utilization will reach ~90% by Q4 FY27, backed by strong pipelines in PVs, industrial gensets, and CV exports.
- **Data Center & Heavy Engine Opportunity:** Management noted they have Rs 300 crore/year of confirmed orders through 2033 for data center applications against dedicated machining lines, with another Rs 500 crore of orders targeted for closure by end of FY27 once heavy hammer cold trials begin.
- **Domestic Tractor Market Slowdown:** Despite broader industry softness, management affirmed no volume impact for Happy Forgings due to market share gains (from 42% to 46%) and lines running at capacity shortage.
- **Promoter Stake Dilution:** On the pending promoter stake dilution timeline under SEBI norms, management stated it will strictly comply within the stipulated regulatory timeframe.

### One-line takeaway
Happy Forgings delivered ~33% revenue and PAT growth in Q2 FY27 with steady ~30% EBITDA margins and announced a new Rs 500 crore capex to support accelerating order visibility across data center industrials and passenger vehicles.
