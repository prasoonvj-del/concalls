# Happy Forgings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, margin resilience, and announcement of new capacity expansion to support future demand.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 501 crore (Q2, +33% YoY); Rs 950 crore (H1, +30% YoY).
*   **Volume:** +25% YoY (Q2); +24% YoY (H1).
*   **Realizations:** Rs 267/kg (Q2, +6% YoY); Rs 260/kg (H1, +5% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 60.8% (Q2, +52 bps YoY; H1, +161 bps YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 153 crore (Q2, +32% YoY); Rs 294 crore (H1, +36% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 30.6% (Q2); 30.9% (H1).
*   **PAT:** Rs 98 crore (Q2, +33% YoY); Rs 189 crore (H1, +36% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 19.5% (Q2); 19.9% (H1).
*   **Return Ratios:** ROE 17.1%; ROCE 19.6%.

### Highlights
*   Revenue crossed the Rs 500 crore milestone in Q2.
*   Machining contribution rose to 90% in H1 FY27 (vs 88% in H1 FY26).
*   Commissioned a new forging press in Q1 and an additional hammer in Q2.
*   **Record:** The ongoing Rs 650 crore capex for ultra-heavy component manufacturing and solar power is on track for Q4 FY28 completion.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume:** Management maintained full-year volume growth guidance.
*   **Margins:** Management expects full-year profitability margin metrics to remain at levels roughly similar to FY2026.
*   **Solar:** The 33 MW captive solar plant is expected to generate ~Rs 30 crore in annual power savings, with full generation starting Q1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Commercial Vehicle (33% of revenue):** High-teen growth; domestic demand strong, exports subdued due to transit times.
*   **Farm Equipment (30% of revenue):** >20% growth; management does not expect a dip despite OEM caution, citing market share gains (42% to 46%).
*   **Industrial (16% of revenue):** >50% growth; driven by data centers and energy sector.
*   **Off-highway (12% of revenue):** >50% growth.
*   **Passenger Vehicle (9% of revenue):** Revenue doubled YoY.
*   **Exports:** Order book >50% of total; management expects meaningful contribution from North American programs starting Q3.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Raw material prices are a complete pass-through.
*   Ocean freight costs doubled in Q2, exerting near-term pressure.
*   80% of cost increases passed through to domestic OEMs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Ongoing:** Rs 650 crore capex (heavy components + solar) is progressing; Rs 300 crore invested to date.
*   **New:** Board approved additional Rs 500 crore capex (70% machining, 30% forging) to be implemented across FY28-FY29.
*   **Target:** Combined capacity to reach 2,17,000 MT forging and 1,16,000 MT machining in 2-2.5 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Leverage:** Total outside liability to net worth ratio is 0.21x; Net debt to equity is 0.08x.
*   **Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow (H1) was Rs 124 crore, impacted by higher receivables and Rs 35 crore in strategic advance payments to suppliers.
*   **Liquidity:** Cash and liquid reserves of ~Rs 350 crore.
*   **Funding:** Management plans to raise ~Rs 300 crore debt for the new Rs 500 crore capex as a bridge loan.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Higher raw material prices and increased shipment costs may exert near-term margin pressure.
*   Working capital investment (receivables) temporarily impacted cash flow.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is the Rs 500 crore capex for data centers?** A: 70% is for machining (PV, industrial, CV exports) and 30% for forging (fungible lines).
*   **Q: Any delay in the heavy hammer line?** A: No, equipment arrived in FY27; trials in Q4, revenue recognition in FY28.
*   **Q: Impact of US elections on data center capex?** A: Management sees no impact; strong demand exists, and they have secured Rs 300 crore in orders with Rs 1,000 crore in discussion.
*   **Q: Why the margin deceleration guidance?** A: Management clarified they expect EBITDA per kg to improve; the "similar to FY26" guidance accounts for potential raw material volatility.
*   **Q: Can you double revenue in 3-4 years?** A: Management confirmed that combined initiatives (data centers, heavy engines, PV) support this ambition.

### One-line takeaway
Happy Forgings delivered strong Q2 growth and announced a new Rs 500 crore capex to support machining capacity, maintaining a positive outlook despite near-term freight cost headwinds.
