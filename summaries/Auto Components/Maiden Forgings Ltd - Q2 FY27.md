# Maiden Forgings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted robust revenue and volume growth, commencement of operations at the new Bhojpur facility, and improved operational margins.

### Key numbers
- **Sales Volumes:** 19,239 metric tons (up ~14.2% YoY)
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 133.99 crore (up 22.43% YoY from Rs 109.44 crore)
- **Total Income:** Rs 139.04 crore (up 24.86% YoY, includes Rs 5.05 crore other income with a one-time gain from land sale)
- **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 14.20 crore (up 110.81% YoY from Rs 6.74 crore)
- **Reported EBITDA Margin:** 10.21% (up ~416 bps YoY from 6.05%)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 9.09 crore (up from Rs 2.50 crore YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 6.99 crore (up 233.12% YoY from Rs 2.10 crore)
- **Reported Net Profit Margin:** 5.02% (up ~314 bps YoY from 1.88%)
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Rs 4.92 (vs Rs 1.48 YoY)
- **Total Debt (Loan):** Rs 62 crore to Rs 63 crore
- **Land Sale Proceeds (Ghaziabad):** Rs 12.90 crore (gain of Rs 4.87 crore)
- **Credit Rating:** CRISIL BBB Stable for Rs 42.50 crore rated bank facilities
- **Aggregate Installed Capacity:** ~53,000 metric tons per annum across 3 facilities

### Highlights
- Commenced operations at the new manufacturing facility in Bhojpur on July 18, 2026, completing Phase 1 of Unit II consolidation.
- Sold a vacant land parcel in Ghaziabad for Rs 12.90 crore; proceeds deployed toward expansion and capex.
- Stainless steel contributes ~15% by volume and ~35% to 40% in revenue.
- Defense/government supplies currently account for 2% to 3% of revenue, with supplies commenced to HAL, BHEL, and NTPC.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects annual revenue for FY27 to be around Rs 270 crore to Rs 280 crore, targeting ~Rs 400 crore next year.
- Anticipates monthly cost savings of at least Rs 50 lakhs post-completion of overall consolidation (up from the earlier estimate of Rs 25 lakhs due to higher construction-period power and interest costs).
- Operational EBITDA margin expected to reach above 11% to 12% post full consolidation.
- Plans to migrate from BSE SME to the main board in the beginning of next financial year.
- Total capex for the new facility consolidation and machinery is expected to close around Rs 27 crore, funded via internal accruals and land sale proceeds.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains encouraging across diverse sectors including automotive, engineering, railways, defense, power, hardware, and general manufacturing.
- Export opportunities are being built in pneumatic nails, stainless steel, and bright bars, particularly in the US and European markets.
- Government/defense sectors require stringent qualifications and offer higher margins due to tailor-made demands.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs are around 87% to 90% of sales.
- Other expenses rose due to shifting and startup costs for the new Bhojpur plant.
- Stainless steel raw material delivery takes 75 to 90 days, prompting inventory holding to maintain a 30-day customer delivery cycle.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phase 1 of Unit II consolidation completed at Bhojpur; Phase 2 and remaining consolidation stages underway.
- Total capex plan is ~Rs 27 crore (including land and machinery), of which ~Rs 20 crore to Rs 21 crore has been spent so far.
- A state-of-the-art research and innovation center is planned at the new facility to drive import substitution and high-margin specialized products.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total loan stands around Rs 62 crore to Rs 63 crore.
- Inventory is maintained at ~121 days (down by 9 days), driven primarily by stainless steel holding requirements.
- Potential realization from remaining surplus land parcels estimated at an additional Rs 15 crore to Rs 20 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Operating environment remains competitive and subject to fluctuations in steel prices, raw material costs, and customer demand.
- Procurement cycles can be longer for certain specialized stainless steel grades.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Kumar (Family Office) asked about full-year revenue targets and raw material trends.** Management stated expected FY27 revenue of Rs 270-280 crore, explained that stainless steel comprises 35-40% of revenue due to higher value realization, and clarified that inventory management aids customer retention.
2. **Amit Kumar asked about the rise in other expenses and land sale deployment.** Management explained that other expenses included shifting and startup costs for the new plant, and land sale proceeds are being deployed toward capex and growth.
3. **Keval Gala asked about normalized operating margins post-consolidation.** Management stated operational margins are expected to go above 11% to 12% due to Rs 50 lakhs in monthly cost savings.
4. **Keval Gala asked about total capex and funding sources.** Management replied that total capex will close around Rs 27 crore and is funded through internal accruals and land sales, with possible future fundraising if large defense/B2G opportunities require it.
5. **Sejal (Apex Capital) asked about customer concentration and top customers.** Management stated they serve over 450 regular customers (including Fastenal, TTK Prestige, KVB, Interarch, Everest), with top 5 customers contributing only 7-8% of total sales.
6. **Sejal asked about EU CBAM applicability.** Management stated CBAM is not applicable to their product line.

### One-line takeaway
Maiden Forgings reported strong H1 FY27 revenue growth of 22.43% and EBITDA growth of 110.81%, driven by volume expansion and richer product mix, while advancing the consolidation of its Bhojpur facility to unlock cost savings and future efficiencies.
