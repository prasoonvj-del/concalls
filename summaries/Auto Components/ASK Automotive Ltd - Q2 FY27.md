# ASK Automotive Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - 12th consecutive quarter of robust performance with highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by strong industry demand and outperformance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue Growth (Q2 FY27):** 47.5% YoY; Net revenue growth (excluding 23% alloy price pass-through and 5% wheel assembly reduction): 29.5% YoY.
- **Consolidated Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** 49.6% YoY; Net revenue growth (excluding 27.1% alloy price pass-through and 5.1% wheel assembly reduction): 27.6% YoY.
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 185 crore (up 30% YoY); EBITDA Margin: 11.8%.
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 348 crore (up 31.3% YoY); EBITDA Margin: 11.9%.
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 102 crore (up 27.3% YoY).
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 187 crore (up 28% YoY).
- **EPS (Q2 FY27):** Rs 5.15 (vs Rs 4.05 YoY).
- **EPS (H1 FY27):** Rs 9.47 (vs Rs 7.40 YoY).
- **Export Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 70 crore (up 133.3% YoY from Rs 30 crore).
- **Export Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 109 crore (up 74% YoY from Rs 63 crore).
- **Segment Revenue Growth (Q2 FY27 YoY):** Advanced braking systems +46%; Aluminium lightweighting precision solutions (ALPS) +69%; Safety control cables +33%.
- **Segment Revenue Growth (H1 FY27 YoY):** Advanced braking systems +47%; ALPS +72%; Safety control cables +27%.
- **EV Revenue Contribution (H1 FY27):** 5.9% (up from 4.8% in H1 FY26); EV segment grew 98% YoY in Q2 FY27.
- **Aftermarket Revenue Contribution:** ~11% of overall revenue.
- **Kit Value:** ICE ~Rs 950; EV ~Rs 1,250 to Rs 1,300.
- **Alloy Wheels Guidance:** ~Rs 80 crore expected in FY27; ~Rs 240 crore expected in FY28 from one Japanese customer.
- **Capex Guidance:** ~Rs 700 crore budgeted for FY27; ~Rs 500 crore planned for FY28.

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, marking the 12th consecutive quarter of robust performance since listing.
- Regular commercial supplies of HPDC alloy wheels commenced on August 3, 2026, to a leading Japanese customer under technical collaboration with Kyushu Yanagawa, Japan (first time HPDC alloy wheels introduced in two-wheelers in India).
- Second captive solar plant of 11.55 MW in Bikaner, Rajasthan, operationalized in October 2026.
- Commissioned the 19th plant for friction material/brakes expansion; 20th plant (fourth in Bangalore) to be commissioned in January 2027. Previous major plant investment of ~Rs 380 crore is running at ~90% capacity utilization.
- Passenger vehicle segment revenue grew 79% in Q2 FY27 and 67% in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of growing in "high teens" in FY27 in net revenue, despite a higher base in Q3 and Q4 post-GST 2.0.
- Export revenue is guided to cross Rs 180 crore in FY27 (up from previous INR 145 crore guidance).
- Industry growth for two-wheelers is expected to moderate to around 10% in the future after current high growth rates.
- EV adoption is projected by management to reach 20% of the total market over the next 2 financial years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- SIAM reported overall vehicle production grew 20.3% YoY in H1 FY27. Two-wheeler domestic production reached 153.7 lakh units in H1 FY27 (up 19.5% YoY) and 81 lakh units in Q2.
- Backed by the festive season, improving consumer sentiment, 8th Pay Commission expectations, new model launches, and gradual replacement cycles.
- Two-wheeler EV penetration is currently around 10%, with management noting kit values are higher in EVs (Rs 1,250-1,300) compared to ICE (Rs 950).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin percentage appears lower (11.8% in Q2, 11.9% in H1) due to the "denominator effect" from pass-through increases in aluminium/alloy prices; absolute EBITDA growth remains in line with net revenue growth.
- Adjusted for abrupt aluminium price spikes, underlying EBITDA margins are estimated around 13.5% or slightly higher.
- Other expenses increased due to a heavy 35% minimum wage hike for contractual labor in Haryana and other states.
- Trade receivables increased due to higher sales and higher aluminium prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Funded via internal accruals; debt-equity ratio maintained around 0.5x, and debt-to-EBITDA kept around 1 year of EBITDA.
- FY27 capex budgeted at ~Rs 700 crore; FY28 capex planned at ~Rs 500 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains a net cash/strong cash-generating business; debt repayment is flagged as "no question at all" given strong fiscal management.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in alloy prices affects margin percentages via the denominator effect.
- Geopolitical situations noted as potential variables for exports, though current performance remains resilient.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raghunandhan NL (Nuvama Research) asked about export sustainability and order wins.** Management confirmed confidence in crossing Rs 180 crore in exports for FY27 and detailed that HPDC alloy wheel supplies to the Japanese customer will scale to ~Rs 80 crore in FY27 and ~Rs 240 crore in FY28.
2. **Ronak Mehta (ICICI Securities) asked about alloy wheel profitability and capex/plant additions.** Management stated alloy wheel EBITDA margins will sync with company levels, and highlighted the commissioning of the 19th plant with the 20th plant launching in January 2027.
3. **Yash Agarwal (Invest First Advisors) asked about EV revenue contribution and other expenses.** Management clarified that EV revenue is 5.9% of H1 FY27, growing 98% YoY in Q2, and explained that other expenses rose due to a 35% minimum wage increase in Haryana.
4. **Basudeb Banerjee (Motilal Oswal) asked about kit value differences between EV and ICE.** Management noted overall kit value is ~Rs 950 for ICE and ~Rs 1,250-1,300 for EVs, and confirmed catering to Ola, Ather, TVS, Bajaj, and Hero.
5. **Navin Kumar Dubey (JM Financial) asked about trade receivable increases and PV strategy.** Management attributed receivables to higher sales volume and aluminium prices, and noted 79% YoY growth in passenger vehicle revenue in Q2.

### One-line takeaway
ASK Automotive delivered its 12th consecutive quarter of record-breaking financial performance in Q2 FY27, outgrowing the two-wheeler industry by leveraging strong order execution, new HPDC alloy wheel supplies, and robust internal cash generation for ongoing expansion.
