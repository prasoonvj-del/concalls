# ASK Automotive Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by strong market outperformance across all product segments.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** 47.5% YoY (Net revenue growth of 29.5% YoY excluding 23% pass-through alloy price impact and 5% strategic reduction in wheel assembly business).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 185 crore, up 30% YoY. EBITDA Margin: 11.8% (impacted by alloy price pass-through denominator effect).
- **PAT:** Rs 102 crore, up 27.3% YoY.
- **EPS:** Rs 5.15 per share (vs Rs 4.05 per share in Q2 FY26).
- **Segment Revenue Growth YoY (Q2 FY27):**
  - Advanced Braking System: 46%
  - Aluminium Lightweighting Precision Solutions (ALPS): 69%
  - Safety Control Cables: 33%
  - Passenger Vehicles segment: 79%
  - EV segment (within ALPS): 98%
- **Export Revenue:** Rs 70 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 133.3% YoY from Rs 30 crore); H1 FY27 export revenue at Rs 109 crore (up 74% YoY from Rs 63 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Financials:** Net revenue growth of 27.6% YoY; EBITDA at Rs 348 crore (up 31.3% YoY, 11.9% margin); PAT at Rs 187 crore (up 28% YoY); EPS at Rs 9.47 per share.

### Highlights
- Delivered 12th consecutive quarter of strong performance post-listing, achieving highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT.
- Commercial production and regular supplies of High-Pressure Die-Casted (HPDC) alloy wheels commenced on August 3, 2026, to a key Japanese OEM under technical collaboration with Kyushu Yanagawa, Japan.
- Operationalized second captive solar power plant of 11.55 MW in Bikaner, Rajasthan, in October 2026.
- EV share of total revenue increased to 5.9% in H1 FY27 (from 4.8% in H1 FY26).
- Commissioned 19th manufacturing plant (friction materials) and targeting commissioning of 20th plant (4th plant in Bangalore) around mid-January 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects net revenue to grow in the high teens for FY27, outperforming the underlying 2-wheeler industry despite a higher base in H2 FY27 post-GST 2.0.
- Export revenue guided to comfortably cross Rs 180 crore in FY27.
- HPDC alloy wheel supplies to the Japanese customer projected at ~Rs 80 crore in FY27 and ~Rs 240 crore in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian two-wheeler domestic production grew 19.5% YoY in H1 FY27 to 153.7 lakh units and reached 81 lakh units in Q2 FY27.
- Management expects two-wheeler industry production to grow at ~10% YoY over the medium term.
- Management projects EV penetration in Indian two-wheelers to reach 20% in the next two financial years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Denominator effect from high pass-through alloy prices suppressed reported EBITDA margin percentage (11.8%); adjusted EBITDA margins are ~13.5% or higher.
- Absolute EBITDA growth (30% YoY) matched net revenue growth (29.5% YoY).
- Other expenses were elevated primarily due to a ~35% hike in minimum wages in Haryana driving up contractual labor costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Budgeted capex for FY27 is ~Rs 700 crore, followed by ~Rs 500 crore in FY28.
- Previous Bangalore plant expansion (~Rs 380 crore investment) has reached 90% capacity utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity ratio maintained around 0.5x, with debt-to-EBITDA targeted at ~1.0x.
- Increased trade receivables attributable to higher sales volumes and elevated raw material (aluminium) prices.
- Management stated internal cash accruals fully support current and future capex plans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material (aluminium) price volatility affecting reported percentage margins.
- Unfavorable geopolitical developments potentially impacting international export growth.
- Near-term EV volume weakness at key customer (Ola), offset by client additions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** How will alloy wheel supplies ramp up and what are the margin expectations?  
  **A:** Supplies to the Japanese customer started Aug 3, 2026, delivering ~Rs 80 crore revenue in FY27 and scaling to ~Rs 240 crore in FY28. EBITDA margins will be in sync with company-level margins.  
- **Q:** What is the kit value difference between EV and ICE models?  
  **A:** Total kit value (including ALPS) is ~Rs 950 for ICE two-wheelers vs ~Rs 1,250 to Rs 1,300 for EV two-wheelers.  
- **Q:** Why did other expenses increase in Q1 and Q2?  
  **A:** Driven primarily by higher contractual labor charges following a ~35% minimum wage increase in Haryana.  
- **Q:** What is the trajectory of capex and debt repayment capabilities?  
  **A:** Capex is budgeted at Rs 700 crore in FY27 and Rs 500 crore in FY28. Debt metrics remain well-controlled (debt/equity ~0.5x, debt/EBITDA ~1x), funded via strong internal cash flow generation (H1 FY27 EBITDA of Rs 348 crore).

### One-line takeaway
ASK Automotive delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with 29.5% net revenue growth, expanding ALPS capacity and commercializing first-in-India HPDC alloy wheel supplies.
