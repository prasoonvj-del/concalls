# Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Margins moderated due to elevated raw material costs and strategic investments, despite strong revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 4,215 crores (up around 24% YoY)
- Lead-acid business revenue: ~95% of total revenue, grew by around 22% YoY
- New energy business revenue: Rs 209 crores (up more than 70% YoY)
- 4-wheeler and 2-wheeler aftermarket volumes: Grew by around 15%
- 4-wheeler and 2-wheeler OEM volume growth: Around 24% and more than 35% YoY, respectively
- Home energy business growth: More than 60% in tubular batteries and home EPS
- Standalone EBITDA margin: Around 10.1%
- Consolidated EBITDA margin: Around 9.6% (lower by around 0.5% due to additional costs at the new energy business)
- Strategic initiatives impact on margins: Around 0.9%
- Total planned capex outlay for FY27: Around Rs 1,700 crores (major outlay towards new energy business of around Rs 1,300 crores; rest towards lead-acid including recycling)
- Capex spent in Q1 FY27: Around Rs 450 crores
- BESS project capacity and initial capital outlay: 10 gigawatt-hour; INR250 to INR300 crores

### Highlights
- Tubular manufacturing plant operated at 100% capacity during Q1; trading revenue stood at around 15% of lead-acid battery revenue.
- Inaugurated customer qualification plant with multi-form and multi-chemistry capability in July 2026.
- E Positive research facility expected to commence operations in the current quarter.
- Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board revoked the closure order issued on April 30, 2021, on July 18, 2026; company withdrew the writ petition before the High Court of Andhra Pradesh.
- Launched Amaron Assist pilot project in Hyderabad during Q1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects lead-acid battery business revenue growth rates anywhere between 9% to 10% in the medium period.
- FY27 capex estimated at around Rs 1,700 crores.
- Expects to ramp up BESS utilization to around 5 gigawatt-hours within 6 months of completion.
- Additional price increases of around 2% to 3% to be rolled out in July across product segments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Sustained volume momentum in both aftermarket and OEM segments.
- Automotive international revenue degrew by around 20% due to significant volume drop in the Middle East market via alternative sea routes.
- Industrial lead-acid battery business grew by around 2% YoY; UPS batteries grew by around 10%, while telecom lead-acid batteries continued to degrow due to migration to lithium-ion.
- Lithium-ion telecom volumes grew by around 50% YoY, maintaining over 60% market share in the telecom segment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins impacted by elevated material costs (procurement costs of alloy, sulfuric acid, and poly rose substantially), increased spending on brand promotions (IPL), and strategic initiatives (Amaron Assist and Factory of the Future).
- Took a price increase of around 3% in June; further price hikes of 2% to 3% planned for July.
- Battery breaking operations for recycling are in trial production; scrap prices are currently high, making reprocessing costs equal to or slightly higher than LME lead.
- R&D expenses for cell development expected to be close to Rs 100 crores to Rs 150 crores this year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- First giga cell and BESS 10-gigawatt-hour factory are on track.
- Equipment for the 2 gigawatt-hour NMC line due for delivery in Q3.
- Total FY27 capex outlay of Rs 1,700 crores, with ~Rs 1,300 crores for new energy.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash generation post-tax and post-dividend expected at Rs 700 crores to Rs 800 crores at the minimum.
- Working capital cash conversion cycle for BESS estimated at around 90 to 100 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues affecting freight costs, fuel costs, and Middle Eastern export shipping routes.
- B2B price increases face delays due to negotiation timelines.
- High local scrap costs impacting recycling margins temporarily.
- Reliance on China for cathode material procurement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kapil Singh (Nomura):** Asked about gross margin pressures and price hikes. *Management answered that a 3% hike was taken in Q1, another 2% to 3% is planned for July, and B2B hikes face delays.*
- **Vibhav Zutshi (JPMorgan):** Asked for a split of other expenses and status of NMC line equipment. *Management stated the 0.9% hit was split between brand promotion, Amaron Assist, and Factory of the Future, and NMC equipment is due in Q3.*
- **Mumuksh Mandlesha (Anand Rathi):** Inquired about BESS ramp-up and recycling plant impact. *Management stated BESS utilization can reach 5 gigawatts in 6 months, while recycling is in trial with current scrap price pressures.*
- **Raghunandhan (Nuvama Research):** Asked about export trends and BESS asset turnover. *Management highlighted Middle East freight issues and noted economic metrics will initially mimic the pack business.*
- **Shubham (Investec):** Asked about the Gotion tie-up status and 16 gigawatt-hour timeline by FY30. *Management clarified that internal teams augmented NMC technology, and capacity timelines may adjust based on demand and product mix priorities.*

### One-line takeaway
Amara Raja reported robust 24% revenue growth in Q1 FY27, but operating margins moderated due to rising raw material costs and investments in strategic initiatives and new energy capacity.
