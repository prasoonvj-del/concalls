# Talbros Automotive Components Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strongest ever quarterly performance with record revenue, robust EBITDA growth, and solid momentum across segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained organic growth guidance of 18% to 20% YoY for FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more upbeat due to record industry volumes and operational resolutions in the forging division.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Mention of European Tier 1 companies facing leveraged balance sheets driving business to India; Lohum JV progress (samples approved by Apollo Tyres and MRF, planning an independent pyrolysis and devulcanization plant in South India).
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Capex execution is on track with Rs 41 crores invested in H1 out of Rs 103 crores planned for FY27.

### Key numbers
- Total revenue: Rs 267 crore (up 23% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 510 crore (up 19% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 47 crore (up 32.8% YoY); Margin: 17.6% (up 120 bps YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 90 crore (up 28% YoY)
- PAT: Rs 33 crore (up 43% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 63 crore (up 39% YoY)
- Gasket & Heat Shield division H1 revenue: Rs 340 crore (up 22% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 63 crore (up 35% YoY)
- Forging division H1 revenue: Rs 171 crore (up 13% YoY); Q2 revenue: Rs 92 crore; EBITDA: Rs 15 crore in Q2 (up 19% YoY)
- Marelli Talbros Chassis Systems H1 revenue: Rs 226 crore (up 49% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 36 crore (up 31% YoY)
- Talbros Marugo Rubber H1 revenue: Rs 86 crore (up 32% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 10 crore (up 37% YoY)
- Capex invested in H1 FY27: Rs 41 crore out of planned Rs 103 crore for FY27
- Export contribution: ~26.5% in Q2

### Highlights
- Delivered strongest ever quarterly revenue and profitability in Q2 FY27.
- Gasket and Heat Shield division accounts for 51% of revenues, remaining single-source for 5 key customers.
- Heat Shield H1 revenues reached ~Rs 35 crores.
- Forging division secured Rs 500 crores of new orders, including major European global OEMs.
- Marelli Talbros JV EV revenues account for almost 5% of its revenues.
- Data centre gasket revenues guided at Rs 50 crore to Rs 60 crore next year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains organic growth guidance of 18% to 20% YoY for FY27, targeting group revenue over Rs 550 crores plus next year (FY28).
- Expects EBITDA margins to be maintained or improved.
- Forging division expected to grow more than 20% for the full year, targeting ~Rs 200 crores in the next 6 months.
- Exports expected to reach around 35% plus by the end of FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic automobile sales grew 18.2% YoY to 83.88 lakh units in Q2 FY27; PV sales rose 30.7% YoY to 13.61 lakh units.
- 2-wheeler sales grew 15.2% YoY to 64.33 lakh units; CV sales rose 28.7% YoY to 3.09 lakh units.
- Electric PV sales grew 84% YoY to over 1 lakh units; electric 2-wheeler sales grew ~80% to 5.89 lakh units (penetration >11%).
- European Tier 1 companies facing leveraged balance sheets, creating structural opportunities for Indian manufacturing (China Plus One).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia led to increases in raw material and freight costs.
- Profit grew faster than revenue due to operating leverage, better product mix, and disciplined cost management.
- Margins absorbed higher steel, aluminium, labour, and freight costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested Rs 41 crores in H1 out of planned Rs 103 crores capex for FY27 across Gasket, Heat Shield, and Forging divisions.
- Forging division adding new capacity machines, with sheds under preparation.
- Lohum JV planning a 10-12 acre land acquisition in South India for an independent pyrolysis and devulcanization plant.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management stated Talbros has a hedged balance sheet and customer base.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia leading to temporary cost pressures on raw materials and freight.
- Temporary manpower cost increases and starting friction at Marelli JV.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dipen Shah (Six Senses) asked about Marelli margin flattening and new contracts:** Management explained extra manpower was deployed for standard business ramp-up, raising manpower costs temporarily, but margins will recover; JLR and Stellantis businesses are scaling.
- **Jiten Parmar (Individual) asked about Marelli bankruptcy impact and Forging recovery:** Management clarified the Marelli bankruptcy is in US courts and does not affect the Indian JV; forging operational issues are resolved and second-half growth is expected to be strong.
- **Harshil Solanki (Individual) asked about order acquisition drivers:** Management highlighted technology advantages, vendor replacement in Europe due to stretched balance sheets, and China Plus One tailwinds.
- **Jainam Madrecha (Individual) asked for revenue growth splits and forging outlook:** Management stated new part numbers drove ~8-10% in gaskets, and forging is expected to touch ~Rs 200 crores in the next 6 months.
- **Varun Agrawal (Individual) asked about FY28 growth sustainability:** Management guided for 18% to 20% growth next year, targeting above Rs 550 crores.

### One-line takeaway
Talbros delivered its strongest quarterly performance in Q2 FY27 with 23% revenue growth and a 17.6% EBITDA margin, underpinned by robust sectoral demand and strong order execution.
