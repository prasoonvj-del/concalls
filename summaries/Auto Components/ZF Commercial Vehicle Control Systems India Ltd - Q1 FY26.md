# ZF Commercial Vehicle Control Systems India Ltd - Q1 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-03 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Resilience in core business and robust aftermarket growth despite external headwinds and absence of base-quarter forex gains.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 1,101.8 crore (5.7% YoY growth)
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 1,100.6 crore (excluding FX/one-offs, up from Rs 1,001.5 crore in Q1 FY25-26, ~9.3% YoY growth)
- **CV >6T segment sales growth:** 8.6% YoY (at par/marginally ahead of industry 8.4%)
- **Aftermarket Sales:** Rs 158.4 crore (15.6% YoY growth; highest ever monthly sale of Rs 63.06 crore in June 2026)
- **Export Revenues:** Rs 271.4 crore (9.7% YoY growth)
- **Export of Services Growth:** 12.5% YoY
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 140.1 crore (reported); Rs 140.8 crore excluding FX/one-offs (16.9% YoY growth)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 104.5 crore (de-growth of 14.7% YoY due to base quarter FX/one-off impact)
- **FX Impact:** Rs 1.98 crore loss in Q1 FY27 vs Rs 39 crore gain in Q1 FY26

### Highlights
- Secured ESC (Electronic Stability Control) business nominations from three major OEMs.
- Ambattur plant received CII Gold Award (95% score) and Spotlight Award for Decarbonization and Climate Action.
- Appointed Rakesh Mishra as Chief Financial Officer (effective September 1, 2026) and Ms. C.V. Kavviya as Whole-Time Company Secretary and Compliance Officer.
- Showcased advanced technologies at Prawaas 5.0.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a positive outlook supported by resilient demand fundamentals, infrastructure investments, and fleet replacement cycles.
- Export demand outlook remains positive with a strong ramp-up anticipated in the U.S. and sustained demand in Europe.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CV >6T production grew 8.4% YoY in Q1 FY27.
- Trailer segment declined due to slower mining activity, monsoon disruptions, and higher input costs.
- EV bus sales reached 1,417 units.
- Industry impacted by an industry-wide shortage of blue-collar manpower across April and May.
- July vehicle production expected to be strong at 40,000–42,000 units.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Faced headwinds from forex volatility, key commodity spikes (aluminum price rose from ~260 to >360 per kg; moderated by ~25 in July), energy, and consumable prices.
- Implemented selective price increases effective July 2026 and engaged with OEMs for cost recovery (partially realized, balance under discussion).
- PERFORM26 initiative focuses on continuous improvements, value engineering, and material cost optimization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned new assembly lines for ASP cartridges, vacuum pumps, brake actuators, and valves at Oragadam, Jamshedpur, Lucknow, and Pantnagar.
- ESC localization is currently at 40-50%, targeting >75% by start of production (SOP).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments, inflationary pressures, commodity price volatility, and monsoon-related disruptions.
- Widespread blue-collar manpower shortage affecting production in April and May.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mumuksh Mandlesha (Anand Rathi) asked about ESC nominations and content per vehicle for 12V vs 24V systems:** Management clarified that content per vehicle remains the same, targeting intermediate commercial vehicles (12V) and heavy commercial vehicles (24V) with pneumatic ESC.
- **Mukesh Saraf (Avendus Spark) asked if AEBS is part of the ESC nomination bundle:** Management stated that while they are majority players in ABS + ESC, AEBS/ADAS has multiple standalone providers in the market, though full suites are being supplied to e-bus customers.
- **Dishant Jain (Quasar Capital) asked for the reason behind the YoY other expenses delta:** Management explained it was primarily due to the base quarter having a ~Rs 39 crore forex gain, compared to a ~Rs 2.0 crore forex loss in the current quarter.
- **Shubham Bhatra (Ambit) asked about gross margin pressures and OEM pass-through timelines:** Management noted that discussions are ongoing with a 2-quarter lag, though some recovery has already been realized in Q1.

### One-line takeaway
ZF CVCS India reported a 9.3% operational revenue growth in Q1 FY27, backed by strong aftermarket and export performance, while navigating commodity inflation and managing upcoming safety regulation transitions like ESC.
