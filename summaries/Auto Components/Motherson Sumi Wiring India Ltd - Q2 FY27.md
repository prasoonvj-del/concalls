# Motherson Sumi Wiring India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by healthy passenger vehicle demand, successful ramp-up of Greenfield plants, and premiumization.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** 47% YoY growth; 19% QoQ growth.
*   **EBITDA:** 34% YoY growth; 45% QoQ growth.
*   **PAT:** 35% YoY growth; 54% QoQ growth.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 9.2% (vs 7.6% in Q1).
*   **Copper Price Impact:** Increased ~6% QoQ and ~54% YoY.
*   **EV Revenue Contribution:** 10% in Q2; 9.3% in H1.
*   **Passenger Vehicle Industry Volume:** 25% YoY growth; 14% QoQ growth.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Performance:** Revenue growth outperformed the industry, driven by vehicle premiumization (higher content per vehicle) and new model launches across EV and ICE platforms.
*   **Greenfield Plants:** All three Greenfield plants have ramped up well and are now operating at optimal utilization levels.
*   **Cost Management:** Management emphasized ongoing austerity measures and operational efficiencies to mitigate inflationary pressures.
*   **Copper Pass-through:** Copper is a pass-through item with a typical 3-month lag. Management expects better numbers within the financial year as negotiations for cost recovery (including minimum wage revisions) progress.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Demand:** Management expects healthy industry growth to continue, though YoY growth percentages in H2 may be lower due to a high base effect from the previous year's GST reduction.
*   **Capex:** Guidance for FY27 is maintained at approximately Rs 200 crore.
*   **FY28 Outlook:** Management stated it is too early to quantify FY28 capex as customer schedules are not yet finalized.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Trends:** Passenger vehicle demand remains strong.
*   **Zonal Architecture:** Management dismissed concerns that shifting to zonal architecture in EVs would reduce wiring harness content, citing that innovation and increased vehicle features continue to drive content per vehicle.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** EBITDA margins improved sequentially due to operational efficiencies.
*   **Inflation:** Facing headwinds from higher copper prices and minimum wage revisions.
*   **Pass-through:** Negotiations are ongoing with customers for full recovery of wage inflation and copper price increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Utilization:** Greenfield plants have reached optimal utilization. The company follows a policy of expanding capacity once utilization hits ~80%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   *Not explicitly discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **High Base Effect:** H2 growth rates may appear lower compared to the previous year due to the GST-related boost in the prior period.
*   **Lag in Recovery:** There is a 3-6 month lag in recovering input cost inflation (copper/wages) from customers.
*   **Volatility:** Commodity price volatility remains a challenge.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst (Nomura):** Asked if H2 could be better than Q2 in absolute terms. **Management:** Confirmed momentum continues and they are ready for the festive period, though percentage growth may moderate.
*   **Analyst (Nomura):** Asked about gross margin pressure and pending claims. **Management:** Stated that within the financial year, they expect to report better numbers as negotiations conclude.
*   **Analyst (Nuvama):** Asked if Greenfield margins have reached company-level margins. **Management:** Declined to provide plant-wise margin data, focusing on overall company performance.
*   **Analyst (Andrey Purushottam):** Asked if the lag in copper pass-through is being reduced to one month across the industry. **Management:** Stated they are working as partners with customers to find solutions, but no specific change in lag period was confirmed.

### One-line takeaway
MSWIL delivered a strong quarter with robust volume growth and margin recovery, successfully navigating commodity headwinds through operational efficiency and customer-led cost pass-throughs.
