# Auto Components - Bearings & precision parts: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the bearings and precision parts sector reported a strong Q2 FY27, characterized by robust double-digit top-line growth (generally ranging between 18% and 39% YoY) driven by domestic infrastructure, wind energy, commercial vehicles, and strong export traction (especially US, Europe, and Japan via China/Europe Plus One tailwinds). However, earnings growth across several players faced headwinds from raw material inflation (steel, copper, tin, aluminum, and energy costs). Companies are countering margin pressures through localization, automation, process efficiencies, and aggressive pass-through negotiations.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Strong demand momentum continues in industrial applications, wind energy, and commercial vehicles. Exports remain a key pillar, backed by global supply chain diversification.
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   *Tractors & Rural:* Sundram Fasteners and Menon Bearings noted tractor segment softness or uneven monsoons impacting rural demand, though Menon mitigated this via diversified non-engine applications and exports.
    *   *Railways:* SKF Industrial reported a government investment shift from rolling stock to tracks impacting its rail segment, whereas Timken India expects rail tenders to convert into orders imminently.
    *   *EVs & New Energy:* Shivalik Bimetal highlighted strong 2-wheeler EV traction with slower 4-wheeler adoption; Sundram described EVs in a temporary "lull phase" while scaling plug-in hybrids; Gala Precision is expanding into electrolyzers and offshore wind.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
Inflation in key raw materials (steel, copper, aluminum, tin, carbides, and fuel/energy) impacted gross margins across the sector:
*   **Pass-through and Lag:** SKF India, Schaeffler India, and Sundram Fasteners highlighted a timing gap or gross margin dip (e.g., ~2.5% at Sundram) due to a lag in passing raw material price hikes to automotive customers. 
*   **Mitigation:** Menon Bearings, Timken India, and Rajratan Global Wire actively use pre-decided pricing formulas or are executing price hikes. Menon specifically offset a 7+ percentage point drop in gross margins through 25% higher productivity, yield enhancements, and process automation.

## Capex & capacity
Managements are actively investing in capacity expansions, automation, and localization:
*   **Gala Precision:** Advancing Chennai Phase 2 (target Q4 operation) and acquiring 10 acres in Wada.
*   **Harsha Engineers:** Accelerating its ~Rs 220 crore capex (Advantek and China) to complete by June 2027.
*   **Menon Bearings:** Investing in robotic automation and AI visual inspection systems funded entirely through internal accruals.
*   **NRB Bearings:** Aurangabad JV with Unitec on track for April 2027 commissioning.
*   **Rajratan Global Wire:** Expanding Chennai to 60,000 tonnes, planning a 50,000-tonne Thailand expansion, and running Pithampur steel cord trials.
*   **SKF Industrial / SKF India:** Expanding Ahmedabad (CRB lines) and Pune capacities.
*   **Schaeffler India:** Continuing construction at Shoolagiri and Savli.
*   **Sundram Fasteners & Timken India:** Investing in wind energy (Phase 3) and Bharuch/Jamshedpur expansions respectively.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **SKF India (Industrial):** Shifted tone from "mixed" to more confident, highlighting strong execution in wind/heavy industries and a step jump in exports, while noting emerging risks from Chinese steel imports.
*   **SKF India Ltd:** Experienced margin compression due to a timing gap in passing on raw material cost inflation, prompting intensive customer price negotiations expected to close in Q3.
