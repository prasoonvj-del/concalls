# Electrosteel Castings Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While domestic demand remained challenging and volumes declined, management noted structural cost optimization, strong overseas performance, and long-term visibility from enhanced government outlays.

### Key numbers
- **Sales Volume (DI pipes, fittings & CI pipes):** 1.20 lakh tonnes, down 27% YoY (Standalone total income down 21% YoY to Rs. 1,119 crore).
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs. 1,465 crore.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 139 crore (EBITDA margin: 9.5%). Standalone EBITDA (including other income) was Rs. 70.6 crore (EBITDA margin: 6.3%).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs. 48.4 crore. Standalone PAT was Rs. 5.9 crore.
- **Consolidated Debt (as of June 30, 2026):** Gross debt at Rs. 1,658 crore; Net debt at Rs. 876 crore. Term debt stands at around Rs. 340 crore.
- **T.I.S. S.p.A. Italy:** Delivered EUR 10 million in revenue (18.4% QoQ growth), with EBITDA margins in the mid-teens (~13% for Q1) and PAT around 7%.
- **Exports:** Export volume for Q1 stood at around 21,000 tonnes.
- **Pig Iron Volume:** Approximately 45,000 tonnes for the quarter.
- **Order Book:** Total executable order book of around 3 lakh tonnes (~5 months of execution), with Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) comprising close to 50%.

### Highlights
- Implemented a broad-based structural cost optimization program to strengthen operating efficiencies and create a competitive cost base.
- T.I.S. S.p.A. Italy integration expanded product portfolio to offer integrated water transmission solutions.
- Announced a diversification strategy into industrial paints and protective coatings, leveraging existing R&D and manufacturing expertise.
- Board approved the manufacture of various rubber products (railway rubber components) using the gasket plant in Andhra Pradesh, subject to railway registrations taking 4 to 6 months.
- Received approximately Rs. 98 crores so far regarding coal mines.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Volume Guidance:** Management revised DI pipe volume expectations to around 5,75,000 tonnes (DIP plus CIP) due to slower initial JJM fund releases, down from erstwhile expectations of 6,50,000 to 7,000,000 tonnes.
- **Export Target:** Expects to export around 22% to 25% of total volumes by the end of the year.
- **EBITDA Margin Outlook:** Management expects EBITDA margins to hit 12% to 13% in Q3 FY27 and Q4 FY27. Q2 FY27 is expected to be similar or conservative compared to Q1.
- **Valves Segment:** Target to double revenue from the valve segment in the next 4 years.
- **Industrial Paints Business:** Phased capital outlay of Rs. 250 crores to Rs. 300 crores to target annual revenues of Rs. 800 crores to Rs. 1,000 crores in the next 5 years (with initial Phase 1 investment of Rs. 100 crores for 17,000 kilolitres capacity). Commercial production expected post-Q1 FY28.
- **Long-term Outlook (FY30/31):** Revenue growth targeting Rs. 7,000 crore to Rs. 8,000 crore with EBITDA margins of 13% to 13.5% by FY30/FY31.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment remains challenging due to slower project execution, delayed fund disbursements, and liquidity constraints at state and municipal levels in the domestic market. Middle East tensions impacted Q1 export volumes.
- Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) 2.0 approved with an enhanced outlay of Rs. 8.69 lakh crore (Central support increased to Rs. 3.59 lakh crore from Rs. 2.08 lakh crore). Central government allocated Rs. 67,670 crore towards JJM for FY27, with ~Rs. 10,344 crore sanctioned and ~Rs. 6,000 crore released so far.
- Growth momentum is expected to pick up substantially in H2 FY27 driven by higher budgetary allocations and faster fund releases.
- Additional demand expected from AMRUT 2.0, piped irrigation, river-linking projects (Ken-Betwa at ~Rs. 44,000 crore and Parbati-Kalisindh-Chambal at ~Rs. 90,000 crore), Urban Challenge Fund (~Rs. 4 lakh crore), and MCAD scheme (~Rs. 1,300 crore outlay).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin resilience in Q1 despite 50% capacity utilization was achieved via cost-cutting initiatives, moderate inventory management, and team efforts.
- DI pipe net realisations: ~Rs. 50,500/tonne in Q4 FY26, ~Rs. 55,000/tonne in Q1 FY27 (total revenue per tonne around Rs. 62,000). Management views current pricing as rock bottom for the industry.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Term debt of Rs. 340 crore will eventually reduce to ~Rs. 230 crore via scheduled repayments.
- Expansion plans focus on brownfield capacities over the next two years to optimize capital allocation (e.g., brownfield expansion for industrial paints in West Bengal, valve plant in Bengal, and ferroalloy capacity expansion).
- Greenfield site in Odisha has been held back due to market downturns.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated gross debt stood at Rs. 1,658 crore and net debt at Rs. 876 crore as of June 30, 2026.
- Company holds around Rs. 700 crore of capital on books available for investments or tactical opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slower project execution, delayed fund disbursements, and liquidity constraints at state/municipal levels.
- Geopolitical tensions (Middle East impacting exports; Saudi Arabia imposing anti-dumping duties, though management noted impact on Electrosteel is limited to 1%-1.5% of sales which can be easily diverted).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pritish Urumkar (ICICI Securities) asked about JJM 2.0 fund translation and Saudi anti-dumping impact:** Management stated that ~Rs. 6,000 crores released out of Rs. 10,000 crores sanctioned will translate to order booking pickup in 1-2 months; Saudi duties affect only 1.5% of their sales which will be easily diverted to GCC/Africa. *(Did not dodge)*
2. **Anand Darshan asked about volume guidance revision and margin sustainability:** Management clarified that the lower volume guidance (5,75,000 tonnes) is due to a slower H1, while cost-cutting and inventory moderation protected EBITDA per tonne despite 50% capacity utilization. *(Did not dodge)*
3. **Nachiket Kale asked about the industrial paints expansion timeline and capex:** Management detailed a Rs. 100 crore initial investment (17,000 KL capacity via brownfield in West Bengal) scaling to Rs. 250-300 crore total outlay over 4-5 years targeting Rs. 800-1,000 crore revenue, with initial commercial production post-Q1 FY28. *(Did not dodge)*
4. **Sajan V asked if DI pipe realizations are bottoming out:** Management confirmed that the last financial year was the worst Ductile Iron has seen since 1994, making current prices definitely "rock bottom." *(Did not dodge)*
5. **Koushik Sekhar suggested exploring a tactical buyback given comfortable debt and low valuations:** Management acknowledged it has been on their minds, noting they will discuss it further with the larger management team. *(Did not dodge)*

### One-line takeaway
Despite a 27% YoY volume decline and a challenging domestic environment in Q1 FY27, Electrosteel Castings is banking on a strong H2 volume recovery driven by JJM 2.0, while strategically diversifying into industrial paints, valves, and railway components via disciplined brownfield expansions.
