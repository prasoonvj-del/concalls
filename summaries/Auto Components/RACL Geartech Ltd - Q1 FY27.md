# RACL Geartech Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-01 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited resilient performance despite geopolitical and energy challenges, with strong growth in both domestic and export segments.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 132.60 crore (+22% YoY).
*   **Operating Revenue:** Rs 132.35 crore (+31.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 32.19 crore (+18% YoY).
*   **PBT:** Rs 16.82 crore (+50% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 25.04%.

### Highlights
*   Exports contributed 64% of total turnover.
*   Recognized as "Best Organization to Work 2026" by ET Edge.
*   Heat treatment plant replacement (shifting from LPG to electric) is on track for trial production in Jan 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains a growth guidance of 15-20% YoY.
*   Expects continued growth trajectory into FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **KTM:** Business has bounced back to pre-COVID levels; new 2027 models showing promise.
*   **BMW:** SOP slated for Oct 2026; pilot supplies have commenced.
*   **Royal Enfield:** Current run rate is 7,500-8,000 sets/month (target 10,000 sets/month).
*   **ZF:** Traction returning; 50-60% capacity utilization; potential for new electric power steering project by end-2027/mid-2028.
*   **New focus areas:** Management is exploring aerospace (civil aviation/Airbus), actuators, and robotics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Raw material consumption: ~28%.
*   Finance costs reduced significantly due to loan payoffs.
*   Effective tax rate for the year projected at 25.62%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Total Capex guidance: Rs 77.4 crore.
*   ~Rs 40 crore allocated for heat treatment plant replacement; remainder for capacity expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Finance costs decreased due to debt repayment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical conflicts and ongoing energy crisis in India.
*   Volatility in global markets.
*   Caution regarding aggressive capacity expansion for multi-source customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **KTM/Bajaj:** Management clarified the relationship is with KTM Austria; no direct communication with Bajaj India yet.
*   **Royal Enfield:** Management confirmed they are a supplier, not doing full transmission assembly; declined to disclose specific volumes/pricing to protect competitive edge.
*   **Non-Auto Diversification:** Management is exploring aerospace, actuators, and robotics; strategy is "incubation" before scaling.
*   **BMW SOP:** Confirmed for Oct 2026; pilot supplies are underway.
*   **Capex Strategy:** Management emphasized they do not "splurge"; they incubate projects and scale only when stable.

### One-line takeaway
RACL Geartech is delivering strong growth driven by premium auto clients (KTM, BMW, Royal Enfield) and is strategically diversifying into non-auto segments like aerospace and robotics while maintaining a disciplined, "incubation-first" capex approach.
