# Kross Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth driven by commercial vehicles and trailer segments, supported by capacity expansion and improving demand.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** INR 235 crore (up 80% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 30.6 crore
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 13%
- **Profit After Tax / PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 19.69 crore (note: transcript mentions INR [197] million and INR 19.69 crores contextually)
- **Profit After Tax YoY Change:** 143% (as stated by management)
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** INR 419.4 crore (vs INR 270 crore YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 53.32 crore
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 33.16 crore
- **Trailer Segment Revenue Share (Q2 FY27):** 49%
- **Component Business Revenue Share (Q2 FY27):** 51%
- **Exports Contribution:** Approximately 8% (or 7.5%-8%) of the component business
- **Trailer Axle Volumes (Q2 FY27):** In excess of 10,500 units
- **Trailer Axle Volumes (H1 FY27):** Almost crossed 20,000 units
- **Trailer Axle Volume Growth (H1 FY27 vs H1 FY26):** More than 55% YoY
- **Debt:** Term loan at INR 80 crore; Working capital and CC limit at approximately INR 32 crore; Total debt as of September 30, 2026 comes to around INR 115 crore (including working capital).
- **Preferential Issue:** INR 63.6 crore

### Highlights
- Commercial production of extruded axle beams commenced; capacity utilization is approximately 80% of current output (producing 4,400 trailer axles per month, with 65% to 80% utilizing extruded beams). By Q3 or Q4, production will entirely shift to the extruded beam process.
- Trailer segment volumes in Q2 exceeded the earlier record levels of Q4 FY26.
- Added a new customer in Europe for the supply of propeller shaft parts (currently under validation stage).
- Preferential issue of INR 63.6 crore executed to fund capacity expansions and backward integration.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 and Q4 volumes to be stronger than Q2.
- Historically, H2 is always 20% up on H1.
- Management expects exports this year to be 45% higher than the previous year.
- Seamless tube facility production trials planned for February or March 2027.
- Management expects utilization of expanded capacities to improve progressively over the coming quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- M&HCV and commercial vehicle momentum continues with robust demand indicated by OEMs, supported by GST rationalization.
- Tractor-trailer segment performing well, driven by end of monsoons and increased market share with larger customers. Chhattisgarh region noted for strong demand.
- Agri segment demand remains consistent, supported by lower inventory levels of tractors in the market. Tractor production plans (e.g., TAFE at 13,000-14,000 tractors in Oct/Nov) support Q3, though a seasonal dip typically occurs post-Diwali.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Pressures witnessed from rising consumable, oil, gas, tooling, and commodity (steel) costs.
- Price revisions concluded with OEMs and Tier 1 customers are expected to support margins during Q3 and Q4 (including retrospective compensations).
- Backward integration into seamless tubes is expected to be 8% to 10% more competitive than existing buying prices, adding an estimated 3% to 4% margin improvement on the overall business once utilized for trailer axles.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Seamless tube facility budgeted at approximately INR 170 crore total cost, funded via INR 110 crore term loan and INR 60 crore from internal accruals/equity. Total cash flow/spent so far is around INR 120 crore (INR 70 crore from term loan + INR 50 crore own contribution). Balance capex of close to INR 50 crore expected to be done in H2.
- Further expansion envisaged through strategic increase in axle shaft production, forward integration into manufacturing Cold Drawn Tubes (approx. INR 20-25 crore capex), and enhanced investment in robotic automation for forging and machining.
- Tipping jacks capacity set at 800 numbers, targeting 50%-60% utilization by Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivable days for Q2 came down to around 81 days; overall H1 receivable days at around 90 days (improved from in excess of 110 days in H1 last year). Export receivables remain above 120 days (comprising 4%-4.5% of business). Working capital cycle expected to be below 90-95 days.
- Total debt stands at approximately INR 115 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Term loan INR 80 crore + Working capital INR 32 crore). Expected to reach INR 165-170 crore upon full drawdown for the seamless project.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Pressures from continuous increases in steel commodity prices and input consumable costs impacting gross margins sequentially.
- Seasonal dip in tractor demand post-Diwali.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anubhav Mukherjee (Prescient Capital) on higher-than-industry growth:** Management answered that the trailer segment growth was driven by post-monsoon pick-up and increased wallet/market share with larger customers, while component growth was driven by CV demand post-GST rationalization and scaling exports (7.5%-8% share).
2. **Pritesh Chheda (Lucky Investment Managers) on extruded axle utilization:** Management clarified they are making 4,400 trailer axles per month, of which 65% to 80% use extruded beams, aiming for 100% by Q3/Q4.
3. **Ankur Poddar (Svan Investment Managers) on cold drawn tubes captive vs external use:** Management stated the facility is for captive consumption in Jamshedpur to support internal seamless tube/tipping jack requirements and precise sizing/strength needs.
4. **Shubham Bhatra (Ambit Asset Management) on seamless tube capex funding:** Management clarified the total budgeted cost is INR 170 crore (INR 110 crore term loan, INR 60 crore internal accruals), with INR 120 crore already spent/committed.
5. **Rajat Aggarwal (Nilgiri Advisors) on peak debt for FY27:** Management confirmed total debt will be around INR 165-170 crore once the remaining INR 50 crore for the seamless tube project is drawn, with no further debt plans.
6. **Anubhav Mukherjee on sequential gross margin dip despite price hikes:** Management explained that steel and consumable cost increases outpaced Q1 compensations, but recent settlements with OEMs/Tier 1s will reflect positively in Q3 and Q4 with retrospective compensations.

### One-line takeaway
Kross Limited delivered an 80% YoY revenue jump in Q2 FY27, backed by robust trailer and CV demand, while advancing backward integration projects to structurally enhance margins.
