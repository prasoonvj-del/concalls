# Sona BLW Precision Forgings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, with BEV revenue crossing 50% of total automotive revenue for the first time and strong 35% YoY revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,546 crore (up 35% YoY, up 18% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 356 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 23.0% (down 230 bps YoY)
- **PAT (Profit After Tax):** Rs 223 crore (up 29% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 14.4% (down ~50 bps YoY)
- **BEV Revenue:** Grew 129% YoY, contributing >50% (51%) of automotive revenue.
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 2,857 crore (up 43% YoY)
- **H1 BEV Revenue:** Rs 1,042 crore (up 119% YoY, 48% of auto revenue)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 659 crore (up 34% YoY); Margin at 23.1%
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 404 crore (up 36% YoY); Margin at 14.1%
- **H1 Cash from Operations:** Rs 594 crore (90% of EBITDA)
- **H1 Free Cash Flow:** Rs 446 crore
- **Order Book:** Net order book at Rs 251 billion; Q2 order additions at Rs 36 billion.
- **Cash & Investments:** Rs 1,966 crore (gross); net cash estimated around Rs 1,400 to 1,500 crore.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT.
- Commercialized 3 new products in a single quarter: helical limited slip differential, air springs for railways, and rear radar sensors for two-wheelers.
- Driveline division won the 2026 Deming Prize for Total Quality Management.
- Broadened first strategic priority from electrification to EPIC mobility (Electric, Personalized, Intelligent, and Connected solutions).
- Eastern markets now contribute almost 2/3 of total revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained the EBITDA margin guidance range of 23% to 25%.
- Management's medium-term aspiration implies a ~25% CAGR.

### Demand & industry commentary
- EV demand accelerated, with the EV business growing 129% YoY driven by strong electric 2-wheeler and 3-wheeler demand in India and global EV adoption.
- Suspension motor is the fastest-growing product, driven by success in China.
- Traditional differential gears and assemblies (DA + DG) grew 37% YoY.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin pressure driven by product mix changes (traction motor demand grew ~50% QoQ, carrying lower margins) and inflation in steel, copper, oil derivatives, and freight rates (freight rates nearly doubled QoQ).
- Numerator/denominator arithmetic effect of the pass-through mechanism contributed to lower margin percentages during inflationary trends.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex spend was Rs 148 crore.
- Denso JV1 carving out is targeted for closure by end of December / January; JV2 targeted for H1 CY2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross cash and investments stood at Rs 1,966 crore; net debt is negative (net cash around Rs 1,400 to 1,500 crore).
- Working capital released Rs 44 crore in H1 despite high revenue growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued West Asia war pressures causing increases in steel, copper, oil derivatives, and sharply higher freight rates.
- Near-term predictability impacted by mix shifts towards traction motors and uncertain broader inflation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arvind Sharma (Nomura/Participant) asked about suspension motor growth drivers:** Management stated adoption follows an S-curve depending on end-customer model success, with potential to expand across other OEMs.
- **Gunjan Prithyani (UBS) asked about traction motor scale-up and 3W contribution:** Management responded that growth is driven by both wallet share expansion and underlying OEM volume growth, alongside strong 3W traction.
- **Basudeb Banerjee (ICICI Securities) asked about margin range and cash deployment:** Management maintained the 23-25% margin range and stated M&A will be highly selective based on strict filters, with no rush to deploy cash given low leverage.
- **Pramod Kumar asked about humanoid robotics timeline and R&D:** Management stated they cannot predict the exact inflection point, but the adoption curve will be super steep once it triggers.

### One-line takeaway
Sona Comstar delivered a record quarter with 35% YoY revenue growth and BEV revenue exceeding 50% of auto sales, underpinned by strong EV demand and new product introductions.
