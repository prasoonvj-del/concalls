# Pitti Engineering Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted robust demand, strong order visibility, and a clear long-term growth strategy despite near-term margin headwinds from inflation and forex.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 581 crore (vs Rs 499 crore in Q2 FY26), up 16.3% YoY.
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,110 crore (vs Rs 963 crore in H1 FY26), up ~15% YoY.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 91 crore (vs Rs 80 crore in Q2 FY26), up 13% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 15.8%.
*   **Adjusted PAT:** Rs 36 crore (vs Rs 43 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Lamination & Assembly Volumes:** 21,600 tonnes (Q2), up 26% YoY; 41,000 tonnes (H1), up 22% YoY.
*   **Raw Casting & Machine Components Volume:** 3,086 tonnes (Q2), up 6% YoY; 6,054 tonnes (H1), up 1.6% YoY.
*   **Forex Loss:** Rs 4.61 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Merger:** Pitti Industries Private Limited and Dakshin Foundry Private Limited merged into the parent entity in September 2026; financials reported on a standalone basis with restated comparables.
*   **Revenue Mix:** Traction motor/railway components (27%), Power generation (16%), Industrial/commercial (12%), Mining/oil/gas (10%), Special purpose motors (8%), Data centers (7%).
*   **Incentives:** The company deferred the Maharashtra government incentive claim (Package Scheme of Incentives) to the next financial year to maximize cash flow and potentially negotiate a 9-year recovery period instead of 7 years.
*   **Working Capital:** Trade payables increased to Rs 470 crore (from Rs 236 crore), which management intends to sustain to support cash flow during the capex cycle.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Potential:** Management targets a revenue potential of ~Rs 3,800 crore by FY31 upon full utilization of planned capex.
*   **FY27 Volume Targets:** Maintained guidance of 82,000+ tonnes for lamination and 17,000 tonnes for casting.
*   **Margins:** Management expects sustainable blended EBITDA margins in the vicinity of 17%+ at current raw material price levels.
*   **Exports:** Exports are expected to remain stable or slightly lower as a percentage of total sales, though absolute export numbers are expected to grow significantly.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Data Centers:** Identified as a strong near-term growth opportunity driven by AI infrastructure; management is "cautiously optimistic" and expects this segment to potentially reach ~9% of revenue.
*   **Structural Drivers:** Growth is supported by infrastructure/manufacturing investment, electrification/digitalization (AI data centers), and global supply chain consolidation.
*   **Railways:** Modernization of North American infrastructure and domestic rail networks are key demand drivers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Headwinds:** EBITDA margin impacted by forex loss, increased consumable/packing costs, and higher fuel/power costs.
*   **Pass-through:** Steel price inflation is a quarterly pass-through; fuel and power cost increases are expected to be passed on starting January 2027.
*   **Manpower:** Employee costs are higher due to gearing up for new capacity before full utilization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **New Capex:** Announced Rs 450 crore expansion (Rs 125 crore land, Rs 325 crore plant/equipment) to consolidate Bangalore lamination operations and increase sheet metal capacity to 160,000 tonnes and machining to 1.31 million hours by FY31.
*   **Total Capex:** Cumulative planned capex is Rs 740 crore (Rs 290 crore previous + Rs 450 crore new).
*   **Funding:** To be funded through a mix of internal accruals and debt; management aims to fund primarily through internal accruals but will use debt if demand accelerates.
*   **Phasing:** Approximately Rs 250-260 crore is committed (land/building); the remainder is modular and linked to customer demand.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Ongoing Middle East conflict causing supply chain disruptions and raw material price volatility.
*   **Execution:** Q3 volumes may face seasonal headwinds (festivals in India/Europe).
*   **Margin Sensitivity:** Inflationary pressures on consumables and power may temporarily compress percentage margins even if absolute margins remain stable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why did casting volumes drop QoQ?** Management clarified that the 3,086 tonnes figure represents "pure vanilla" machine components, and total casting production is higher when including components used in lamination assemblies.
*   **Q: Will the Bangalore consolidation cause operational disruption?** Management stated that Aurangabad will remain the largest lamination facility; the Bangalore move is to consolidate existing leased facilities and future growth into one site to improve efficiency.
*   **Q: Can margins reach 18% in the long term?** Management noted 17% is a comfortable target; 18% is possible depending on the mix of high-value-added assemblies vs. loose laminations.
*   **Q: Is the Rs 450 crore capex over and above the previous Rs 290 crore?** Yes, the cumulative capex is Rs 740 crore.
*   **Q: Why defer the Maharashtra incentive?** To maximize cash flow and attempt to extend the recovery period from 7 to 9 years, which would allow for a higher total recovery.

### One-line takeaway
Pitti Engineering is aggressively expanding capacity (Rs 740 crore cumulative capex) to capture structural demand in data centers and infrastructure, while managing short-term margin pressure through operational efficiency and strategic working capital management.
