# Talbros Automotive Components Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly revenue supported by strong performance in gaskets, joint ventures, and new order momentum despite temporary margin pressures.

### Key numbers
- Total income / revenue: Rs 242 crore (up 15% YoY from Rs 211 crore)
- EBITDA: Rs 43 crore (margin at 17.6%)
- PAT: Rs 30 crore (up 35% YoY)
- Gasket division revenue: Rs 164 crore (up 21% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 29 crore (up 32% YoY)
- Forging division revenue: Rs 78.4 crore (up 4% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 14 crore (vs Rs 13 crore YoY)
- Marelli Chassis Systems JV revenue: Rs 105 crore (up 43% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 17 crore
- Talbros Marugo (TMR) JV revenue: Rs 40 crore (up 31% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 6 crore (up 57% YoY)
- EV sales contribution: Rs 12.5 crore (3.27% of total sales, compared to Rs 9 crore / 2.9% last year and Rs 10 crore / 2.56% in Q4)
- Data centre gasket potential: Rs 30 crore to Rs 40 crore annually (~5% of gasket division)
- Planned capex for FY27: Rs 103 crore

### Highlights
- Delivered highest ever quarterly revenue, surpassing Q4 FY26.
- Gasket and heat shield division contributed 52% of total revenue (Rs 164 crore).
- Commercial production started from the chassis division for Stellantis in Q1 FY27 after 2 years of design changes.
- New growth vertical entered: data centers (supplying gaskets for generator engines via Cummins and Kirloskar Oil Company).
- Export contribution stands at ~25%, targeting 35% by FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 group revenue growth target at 18% to 20%.
- EBITDA margin guidance for FY27 maintained around 17% to 17.5%.
- Forging division revenue expected to grow 15% to 20% for the full year, with double-digit growth anticipated in Q2.
- Long-term revenue targets by FY30: Gasket division to reach Rs 850 crore to Rs 900 crore; Forging division to reach Rs 600 crore to Rs 650 crore.
- EV sales mix targeted to reach a minimum of 5% in the next 2-3 years.
- Export share targeted at 35% by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PV industry grew 26% YoY (1.27 million units); 2-wheeler grew 20% YoY (5.63 million units); CV grew 15% YoY (2.83 lakh units).
- EV passenger vehicles grew 87% YoY; electric 2-wheelers grew ~68% YoY.
- European car markets remain slow due to inflation pressures and Chinese car segment headwinds, impacting forging exports.
- Kia business resumed full pace; new orders secured from Kia, Cummins, Stellantis, JLR, and BMW.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Temporary margin pressure witnessed due to elevated commodity prices (steel and aluminium) and inflation costs such as state wage increases and LPG prices.
- Management is positive about passing on cost increases to OEMs in coming quarters.
- Operational pressures from West Asia crisis and manpower shortages have largely moderated.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of ~Rs 103 crore for the year across gaskets, forgings, and heat shields.
- Evaluating potential new plant in Gujarat for chassis in talks with Tata Motors.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not explicitly discussed in detail, aside from capex plans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- European car market slowdown and muted demand impacting forging export schedules.
- Elevated commodity prices (steel and aluminium) and higher wage/LPG costs putting temporary pressure on margins.
- Past loss of Meritor business (Rs 30 crore to Rs 40 crore per annum) due to tariff issues (though Meritor is reportedly trying to restart).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dipen Shah (Six senses) asked about the progress of global OEMs diversifying from China to India and new project updates:** Management noted BMW giving more business, potential with Volvo for gaskets, Stellantis opening doors for chassis/forgings, JLR working on EV plastic components, and Cummins America scaling up.
- **Richita (CGW Investment) asked about data center gasket contribution and peak revenues:** Management stated data center gaskets are ~5% of the gasket division (~Rs 30-40 crore annually), and guided for gasket division to reach Rs 850-900 crore and forging to Rs 600-650 crore by FY30.
- **Shikha Mehta (Time & Advisors) asked about reasons for low Q1 forging growth (4%):** Management cited European market slowness, manpower shortages, and higher LPG prices, but stated July, August, and September are showing progressive improvement with an order book of ~Rs 500 crore over 5 years.
- **Jay Jain (JJ Capital) asked about the fastest-growing segment and sustainable EBITDA margins:** Management stated Marelli is expected to grow fastest at 30%-40%, followed by forgings at 20%+, and guided for a sustainable EBITDA margin around 17%.

### One-line takeaway
Talbros posted a record Q1 FY27 with 15% revenue growth to Rs 242 crore and a 17.6% EBITDA margin, driven by strong gaskets and joint ventures, while maintaining its 18-20% annual growth guidance.
