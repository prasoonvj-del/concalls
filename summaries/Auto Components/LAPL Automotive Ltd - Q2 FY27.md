# LAPL Automotive Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue growth was muted due to customer delays and capacity constraints, and profitability declined YoY, but management expects a strong recovery in H2 driven by new capacity and order book execution.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR43.64 crores, up 4% YoY (compared with INR41.94 crores in H1 FY6 [FY26])
- **Total income:** INR43.88 crores for H1 FY27
- **EBITDA:** INR6.33 crores, broadly in line with INR6.41 crores YoY
- **EBITDA margin:** 14.42%, compared with 15.29% YoY
- **Net profit (PAT):** INR2.97 crores, against INR3.3 crores YoY
- **Net profit margin:** 6.76%
- **EPS:** INR2.97 for the half-year
- **IPO proceeds raised:** INR32.4 crores (INR19.56 crores allocated to the fourth manufacturing facility, INR4.8 crores for loan repayment, INR3.25 crores for IPO expenses, INR4.8 crores for general corporate purposes)
- **Total debt:** INR15.45 crores (INR2.10 crores long-term, remainder working capital/CC), at an average interest rate of 8.25%

### Highlights
- Completed IPO and listed on BSE SME platform on August 13, 2026, at INR135 per share (43.6% premium to issue price of INR94), subscribed 241 times.
- Performed Bhumi Pujan for the fourth manufacturing facility in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar on August 19, 2026; construction is underway.
- Added a leading global automotive component manufacturer as a customer and received three new vendor codes.
- Started EV supplies for Samarth E-Mobility's AMORE brand; received mirror development orders from Oben Electric and BGAUSS (production started October 2026).
- *Record check:* Construction of the fourth manufacturing facility was confirmed as started in August/September 2026, supporting production expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a conservative 40% growth for FY27 overall, aiming for revenue of ~INR130 crores.
- Management targets a "drastic jump" of 80% to 120% in coming years once the new core unit is operational.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to sustain around 14.5% to 15.5% in H2 and return toward FY26 levels (16.75%) once volumes catch up.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Lighting and motor divisions operated at ~91% capacity utilization in FY26, creating volume constraints.
- Strategic location in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar positions the company to benefit from local sourcing policies of new OEMs entering the area (e.g., JSW Motor, JSW Green, Ather; also in talks with Mahindra Logistics, Toyota for Tier-2 supplies).
- Transitioning ticket sizes up through advanced products (e.g., 90 mm LED/projector headlamps priced at ~INR2,000, bringing total bus headlamp ticket size up to ~INR16,000).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability declined YoY due to higher raw material cost share, team additions for new programs, and increased depreciation and finance costs from capacity investments.
- Gross margins by segment: Lighting at ~28%-30% (or 30%), Motors at ~18%-20% (or 20%), Mirrors at ~20%.
- New plant will bring tooling in-house (currently 70%-80% of development cost outsourced), reducing transportation time and adding to margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fourth manufacturing facility space will be ~8x the size of the smallest existing unit.
- Shed construction expected to take 4 months, machinery setup 3 months, and plant setup 1 month (total ~8 months to start).
- Molding machine count to expand from 14 machines (80-200 tons) to another set of 14 machines with higher tonnage, raising per-part value from INR20-INR80 up to INR200-INR250 (and up to INR800 for bumpers/dashboards).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt stands at INR15.45 crores at an 8.25% interest rate.
- Average debtor recovery is ~45 days (reported debtor days increased due to invoicing from August/September ramp-up).
- Working capital days expected around 50 to 60 days.
- Inventory days increased due to raw material procurement for H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- H1 revenue growth was muted (4%) due to customer-end mishaps and delayed order execution, alongside high capacity utilization limits.
- Profitability and margins faced temporary pressure from increased depreciation, higher raw material share, and team additions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinay Jain (KRS Growth Opportunities Fund) asked why revenue growth was muted despite robust industry sales.** Management explained that energy went into the IPO, getting vendor codes, and capacity constraints (91% utilization), but new programs and order execution will drive H2. (Did not dodge)
2. **Tejas Shirodkar (WC Securities / similar) asked for segment EBITDA margins and new plant focus.** Management stated lighting is ~30%, motors and mirrors ~20%, and the new plant focuses entirely on lighting and higher-value plastic molding. (Did not dodge)
3. **Mukesh Panjwani (WC Securities) asked why margins improved historically from FY23 to FY25 despite flat revenue.** Management attributed it to product value addition, bottleneck reduction via machinery, bulk supplier negotiation, and ERP implementation. (Did not dodge)
4. **Mukesh Panjwani asked about the sharp increase in debtor and inventory days.** Management clarified that inventory increased due to raw material procurement for H2, and debtor days look high due to August/September invoicing, but actual recovery takes maximum 45 days. (Did not dodge)
5. **Sourav Sachdeva asked how the company is confident about 40% growth given flat H1 revenue.** Management answered that customer-side mishaps delayed expected POs into coming months, and they have indented ~INR15 crores per month for H2. (Did not dodge)

### One-line takeaway
Despite a muted H1 impacted by high capacity utilization and customer delays, LAPL Automotive expects a strong H2 rebound to deliver ~40% annual growth, supported by IPO proceeds funding its fourth manufacturing facility and new OEM order wins.
