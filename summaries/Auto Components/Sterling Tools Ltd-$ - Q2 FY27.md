# Sterling Tools Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong standalone revenue and volume outperformance offset by near-term margin pressure from sharp input cost inflation and time-lagged price recoveries.

### Key numbers
- **Industry Domestic Sales (Q2 FY27):** Grew ~18% YoY to 83.88 lakh units.
- **Standalone Revenue Growth (Q2 FY27):** Increased ~32% YoY.
- **Standalone Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** Increased ~28% YoY (vs. ~20% industry growth).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR58.7 crores (up 17% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR27.7 crores (up ~8% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR13.6 crores (compared to INR19.8 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR30 crores (compared to INR30.8 crores in H1 FY26). *(Note: Q2 FY26 and H1 FY26 included an exceptional gain of INR9.5 crores).*
- **SEM Revenue (Q2 FY27):** ~INR23 crores (H1 FY27 revenue: ~INR37 crores).
- **Consolidated Capex Plan (FY27):** ~INR150 crores (Fasteners: ~INR120 crores, SEM: ~INR20 crores, STML: ~INR10 crores).
- **Fasteners Capex Incurred:** ~INR25 crores out of the revised plan.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly and half-yearly standalone revenue and highest-ever half-yearly EBITDA.
- Outperformed the broader auto industry due to strong traction in the commercial vehicle (CV) segment and marquee customer volumes.
- SEM onboard chargers and multifunction units production lines commissioned as planned in Q2.
- STML high-voltage DC contactors and relays commercial supplies commenced in Q2.
- Appointed new CFO, Mr. Amit Dhadda.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to improve over coming quarters as price increases are progressively recovered from OEMs.
- Targets a return to ~15% quarterly EBITDA margins in the fasteners business by Q4 FY27.
- SEM and STML business targets annual revenue of INR150 crores+ to achieve breakeven (expected to outperform in FY28).
- Fasteners business long-term revenue target is INR1,000 crores ASAP.
- Standalone revenue for the full year is expected to surpass last year, with a potential ~15% increase.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian auto industry recorded its highest-ever Q2 sales across major segments, supported by GST 2.0 affordability, rural/urban demand, and vehicle replacement.
- SIAM expects positive H1/H2 FY27 momentum from festive demand, new launches, infrastructure spending, and fleet replacement, though inflation, interest rates, and geopolitics are key watch factors.
- Fasteners growth is driven entirely by incumbent CV customers (Tata, Leyland, Volvo, Mahindra, Daimler Benz, etc.) ramping up volumes rather than new start-ups.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability faced near-term pressure as low-cost inventory was exhausted in Q1 and input costs surged due to the West Asia conflict.
- Exceptional inflation seen across commodities and non-commodities (e.g., tungsten carbide prices up >300% in 12 months; power, diesel, tool steel, and minimum wage-driven people costs also higher).
- Pricing arrangements with OEMs allow for pass-throughs, but recoveries involve time lags and ongoing mutual negotiations, with some retrospective impact expected.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Overall FY27 consolidated capex increased to ~INR150 crores (Fasteners: ~INR120 crores, SEM: ~INR20 crores, STML: ~INR10 crores).
- Fasteners capex was ramped up from an initial INR80 crores to ~INR120 crores, planned with a 12-to-24-month horizon.
- The additional ~INR120 crores fasteners capex is expected to increase revenue capacity by roughly INR150 crores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt increased due to a INR40 crores investment in subsidiaries (SEM and STML) in H1, higher capex funding, and increased working capital needs driven by ~30% revenue growth.
- Taken a strategic view to carry higher raw material inventory from steel mills because the market is buoyant and mills are fully booked.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term input cost inflation and time lags in recovering price increases from OEMs.
- Macro factors: inflation, potential interest rate hikes, and geopolitical uncertainties.
- Customer putting advanced engineering projects (AEM product PoC) on hold due to inflationary pressures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dev (Individual Investor) on SEM/STML losses and breakeven:** Management stated losses are largely operational and fixed-cost in nature; profitability will be achieved upon reaching an annual revenue of INR150 crores+, which they expect to outperform in FY28.
- **Mihir (MB Securities) on EBITDA lagging revenue growth:** Management explained that raw material and input costs (some commodities up 300%) rose exceptionally, and customer price negotiations involve time lags.
- **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) on competitive intensity:** Management stated they feel no pricing or volume pressure following a competitor's acquisition by an Italian company, supported by quality, customer servicing, and 4-decade relationships.
- **Ansh Khimavt (Capital One) on capex increase and rising debt:** Management detailed the breakdown of the INR150 crores capex, subsidiary funding, and increased working capital/inventory holding to support buoyant demand.

### One-line takeaway
Sterling Tools reported record standalone revenue and volume growth outperformance, though profitability faced temporary headwinds from severe input cost inflation pending time-lagged price pass-throughs.
