# DIVGI TORQTRANSFER SYSTEMS Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by the Indonesia 4x4 program, EV transmission recovery, and expanding export business.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 148.7 crore (+68% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 44 crore (+99% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 26 crore (+146% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 29.5%.
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 290.5 crore (+76% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 85.53 crore (+108% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 51 crore (+162% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Transfer Case Revenue:** Rs 80.4 crore (+88% YoY).
*   **EV Transmission Revenue:** Rs 11.4 crore (+87% YoY).
*   **Component Revenue:** Rs 34.6 crore (+25% YoY); Exports crossed Rs 23 crore quarterly run rate.
*   Received recognition from Tata Motors for execution excellence on the Indonesia expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue Target:** Management targets revenue "well over" Rs 500 crore.
*   **Long-term Aspirations:** Targeting Rs 1,000 crore by FY30 and Rs 2,000 crore beyond FY32/FY33.
*   **Baseline Growth:** Management targets a baseline growth trajectory of 15-20% CAGR.
*   **Margins:** Management remains committed to maintaining EBITDA margins above 20%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Indonesia Program:** A key growth driver; Mahindra portion expected to finish by February, while Tata portion will spill over to June.
*   **EV Transmission:** Demand improved in Q2; series production for the "Sigma" program expected in H2.
*   **Strategic Opportunities:** Tata Motors' acquisition of Iveco is viewed as a significant opportunity for manual/automatic transmissions and transfer cases.
*   **Global Trends:** Management observes Indian OEMs and Tier 1s being increasingly co-opted into global platform development.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Operating leverage is becoming visible as volumes and scale build up.
*   Management notes challenges in variable costs (power, commodity) but expects scale to improve asset utilization and better manage fixed costs and depreciation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **US Subsidiary:** Established in Greenville, South Carolina; currently focused on customer engagement and RFQs. Management stated any future manufacturing investment will be assessed against customer commitments and program economics.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Cash reserves are intended for product development and capacity expansion to meet long-term revenue targets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Status:** Virtually debt-free.
*   **Liquidity:** Cash and cash equivalents of Rs 41.6 crore; bank balances of Rs 255.7 crore.
*   **Working Capital:** Trade receivables at Rs 110.5 crore; inventory at Rs 74.5 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Cyclicality:** Automotive industry is subject to OEM production schedules, model launches, and supply chain conditions.
*   **EV Volatility:** EV demand and program schedules can fluctuate.
*   **Execution Delays:** Testing backlogs at Tata Motors have delayed some EV transmission launches.
*   **Dividends:** Management confirmed no plans for a special dividend.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Status of automatic transmission commercialization?** A: Management expects to potentially move up the launch to late FY28/early FY29; an announcement is expected in December or early next year.
*   **Q: EV transmission volume visibility?** A: Management confirmed they are the sole gearbox supplier for the Sigma program (Nexon/Curvv). While testing backlogs caused delays, Q4 is expected to see higher numbers.
*   **Q: Indonesia order execution status?** A: Mahindra portion finishes in February; Tata portion will spill over to June.
*   **Q: US manufacturing plans?** A: Currently serving via India; US manufacturing will only be pursued if program economics and customer commitments justify it.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational execution and scale-up in transfer cases and exports have driven significant margin expansion, with management now focused on securing long-term growth through automatic transmissions and global platform integration.
