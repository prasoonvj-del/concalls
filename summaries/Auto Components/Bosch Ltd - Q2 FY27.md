# Bosch Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth and subsidiary integration offset by raw material/forex cost pressures and a cautious H2 outlook.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** INR 76,551 million (Rs 7,655.1 crore), up 28.2% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 10,371 million (Rs 1,037.1 crore), up 23.2% YoY.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Grew by 18.5% YoY.
- **Exceptional Item:** Reversal of employee long-term service award provision amounting to INR 1,304 million (consolidated) and INR 1,240 million (standalone).
- **Standalone Revenue from Operations:** INR 61,641 million (Rs 6,164.1 crore), up 28.6% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA:** INR 8,028 million (Rs 802.8 crore), up 30.1% YoY.
- **Standalone PAT:** Grew by 21.7% YoY.
- **Bosch Chassis Systems (RBIC - Subsidiary):** Q2 FY27 Revenue at INR 15,062 million (+27.9% YoY); EBITDA at INR 2,344 million (+4.7% YoY).

### Highlights
- Board approved issuing 3 bonus equity shares for every 1 fully paid-up equity share (3:1 bonus issue) and a sub-division of INR 10 equity shares into INR 2 face value.
- Consolidated results include Bosch Chassis Systems India Private Limited (RBIC) for the first time effective July 1, 2026.
- Discontinued employee long-term service recognition awards for managerial staff effective January 1, 2027, resulting in an exceptional provision reversal.
- World-first validation of CNG direct injection on engine and vehicle benches for passenger cars and LCVs.
- Milestone achievements: 10+ million electronic control modules shipped in 2W & Powersports; Chennai plant surpassed 20 million power tools; Chakan plant surpassed 20 million active safety units.
- *Pattern Continuation:* Extends strong top-line momentum from Q2 FY27 results announcement with the strategic consolidation of RBIC.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects flat to modest growth in Q3 FY27 and a modest overall H2 FY27 relative to a high prior-year base due to weak monsoons, interest rate hikes, and general inflation.

### Demand & industry commentary
- RBI and IMF project India's FY27 GDP growth at 7.1% and 7% respectively, supported by INR 11.1 lakh crore infrastructure capex. RBI raised the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% in October 2026.
- Passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles (HCVs/LCVs), three-wheelers, and two-wheelers saw strong Q2 FY27 demand.
- Tractor demand weakened toward the end of Q2 FY27 due to uneven/below-normal monsoons.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins faced compression from steep raw material price increases (predominantly aluminum) and INR depreciation against the Euro.
- Price increases and forex hits are passed through to OEM customers with a contractual time lag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Spent INR 350 crore on capex in H1 FY27, primarily toward upgrading existing production lines.
- Joint venture with TACO in Nashik is scheduled to begin production mid-next year; JV with TSF in Chennai is expected to commence production toward the end of next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Recommended utilization of retained earnings for the 3:1 bonus issue and stock split.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global geopolitical conflicts, supply chain disruptions, elevated energy prices, aluminum price inflation, and currency depreciation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Margin drop at RBIC:** Management explained the margin reduction was caused by raw material (aluminum) cost spikes, adverse Euro/INR forex, and line commissioning costs, which will be recovered with a time lag.
- **AEBS implementation content per car:** Management stated AEBS is already integrated into their new-generation ESP systems, so no additional content per vehicle is required.
- **H2 FY27 demand outlook:** Management confirmed H2 will likely be modest across segments due to high base effects, monsoons, and rate hikes.
- **RBIC's higher ROCE & asset turnover:** Management cited RBIC's modular line architecture, lower initial capex intensity, and global re-use of manufacturing line concepts.
- **Global supply chain shift from China:** Management noted Bosch operates on a local-for-local strategy with 8–9% exports from India, while monitoring global relocation trends and trade agreements.

### One-line takeaway
Bosch delivered strong top-line growth supported by the first-time integration of RBIC and announced a 3:1 bonus issue and 1:5 stock split, though elevated input costs and modest H2 demand remain near-term headwinds.
