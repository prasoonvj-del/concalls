# Minda Corporation Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and robust year-on-year growth surpassing consensus estimates.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,189 crore (Up 43% YoY, 19% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 4,036 crore (Up 38% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 257 crore (Up 45% YoY, 22% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.8% (H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 11.6%, EBITDA at Rs 469 crore)
- **PAT:** Rs 143 crore (Up 69% YoY, 43% QoQ; PAT margin 6.5%)
- **H1 FY27 Normalized PAT:** Rs 243 crore (Margin 6.0%, excluding Rs 106 crore exceptional gain net of tax)
- **Flash Electronics Q2 Revenue:** Rs 650 crore (EBITDA Rs 106 crore, Margin 16.4%; PAT Rs 54 crore, Margin 8.3%)
- **Flash Electronics H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,183 crore (EBITDA Rs 188 crore, PAT Rs 89 crore)
- **EV Product Revenue Growth:** Up 113% YoY for Minda Corporation (contributing 10% of revenue; 15% at group level); Up 173% YoY for Flash Electronics (contributing 32% of Q2 revenue)
- **Credit Rating:** Upgraded from AA- to AA

### Highlights
- Crossed the Rs 2,000 crore quarterly revenue milestone for the first time.
- Secured multiple TFT instrument cluster orders (5 to 12-inch) with a cumulative lifetime value exceeding Rs 1,000 crore (SOP from FY28 onwards).
- Received Government of India approval under the ECMS initiative for a greenfield TFT display module assembly facility in the Pune/Chakan region (investments around Rs 200 crore in phases, SOP in ~18 months) to backward integrate 70%-80% of TFT requirements.
- Sunroof production commenced during the quarter; secured first power footstep order from an anchor passenger vehicle customer.
- EV products account for ~35% of new order wins.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they remain highly confident in the near-term and long-term growth trajectory driven by strategic investments, technology advancement, and customer penetration.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian auto industry recorded its highest ever Q2 sales across vehicle segments, supported by GST-led affordability, festive purchases, and infrastructure activity.
- Segment growth cited: 2-wheelers ~16.5%, Passenger vehicles ~25%, 3-wheelers ~24%, Commercial vehicles ~34%, and tractors supported by favorable agri activity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins were affected by elevated commodity pricing, increased input costs (largely pass-through with recovery expected next quarter), revision in minimum wages across several states, and higher freight expenses.
- Copper price increase impact in wiring harness is ~4% to 5%, indexed back-to-back with a one-quarter lag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operating base includes 42 manufacturing plants with over 23,000 workforce; currently building 6 to 7 more plants starting operations in next 12 to 24 months.
- Land bank: ~8.2 lakh square meters; built-up space: ~2.6 lakh square meters.
- Greenfield TFT display module facility planned in Pune/Chakan (~Rs 200 crore investment in phases).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Credit rating improved from AA- to AA.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global environment continues to present uncertainty around trade and geopolitical developments.
- Cost pressures from elevated commodity pricing, minimum wage revisions, and higher freight expenses.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raghunandhan (Nuvama Research) on TFT display module investments:** Management detailed the ~Rs 200 crore investment in phases over 18 months, targeting 3x-4x asset turnover and 70%-80% localization of TFT requirements. *(Direct answer provided)*
2. **Jay Kale (Elara Capital) on Wiring harness copper inflation impact:** Management clarified that copper inflation is a 4%-5% impact in wiring harness, addressed via back-to-back customer indexing with a one-quarter lag. *(Direct answer provided)*
3. **Sahil Sanghvi (Monarch Networth) on Renewable energy / captive power:** Management stated the goal is 80%-90% power from renewables by 2035 (currently at 30%-35%), yielding savings of Rs 2 to Rs 3 per unit with around-the-clock supply. *(Direct answer provided)*
4. **Naveen Kumar Dubey (Narnolia Financial Services) on Revenue segment mix targets:** Management outlined expected long-term splits: 2W/3W at 40%-45%, Commercial Vehicles at 20%-25%, and Passenger Vehicles moving towards 25%+. *(Direct answer provided)*

### One-line takeaway
Minda Corporation delivered a landmark Q2 FY27 with record revenue of Rs 2,189 crores (up 43% YoY) and EBITDA of Rs 257 crores, propelled by robust EV growth, key electronics order wins, and a strategic push toward backward integration.
