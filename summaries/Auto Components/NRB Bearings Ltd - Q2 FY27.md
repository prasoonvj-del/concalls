# NRB Bearings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong 18.8% YoY revenue growth, expanding EBITDA margins, and robust broad-based demand across CV, industrial, and export segments.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 386 crore, up 18.8% YoY (and 19% for H1 FY27).
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 71 crore.
- **EBITDA margin:** 18.2%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 36 crore for Q2.
- **Segment performance:** CV segment revenue grew 47% YoY; Industrial grew 48% YoY; Exports grew 16%+ YoY.
- **Mahant Tool Room (MTR) revenue:** Rs 55 lakh for the quarter.
- **Market share:** ~30% global market share in commercial vehicle bearings and ~30% in two-wheeler bearings.

### Highlights
- Growth was broad-based across automotive exports, industrial applications, two-wheelers, and commercial vehicles, supported by an improved product mix and high-value engineering solutions.
- Top 10 international OEM customers include Tier-1 players like Magna, ZF, and Daimler, through which products enter BMW and Mercedes platforms across transmission, powertrain, and steering systems.
- Strategic expansion continues into high-margin, sticky non-auto and high-tech verticals: Aerospace & Defense, Automotive Adjacencies (wheel hubs, ball/tapered bearings), EV-beyond-electrification, AGVs/Robotics/Urban Air Mobility, and Heavy-duty Off-Highway machinery.
- MTR acquisition has achieved AS9100D certification capabilities for defense and aerospace mission-critical components (such as fighter jet landing gear and door systems).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H1 top-line growth momentum (~19% YoY) to be sustained and surpassed over a 5-year horizon, with upswings visible every six months as capacities come online.
- Management targets maintaining EBITDA margin in the 17%–18%+ range on a full-year basis.
- MTR is targeted to reach a turnover of Rs 300 crore by 2031 (moving to Rs 1 crore/quarter over the next 9–12 months before stepping up significantly in years 4–5).
- EBITDA and operational EBITDA should be used to track performance rather than PAT/PBIT due to high front-loaded depreciation from ongoing growth investments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Commercial vehicles and two-wheelers continue to deliver resilient double-digit underlying demand.
- Industrial segment saw 48% YoY growth, driven strongly by industrial gearbox applications.
- Global automotive customers are moving away from transactional "build-to-print" component suppliers to co-development engineering partners with customized solutions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- COGS increased by ~1% on a peer-relative basis due to strict cost management, waste elimination, and positive product mix shift.
- Higher "Other Expenses" (up 19% YoY) were influenced by power, fuel, logistics, and product development costs, which management expects to absorb via higher throughput as capacity expands.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- The joint venture with Unitec at Aurangabad remains on track for commissioning by April 2027, primarily addressing high-traction industrial gearboxes.
- Management emphasized that the company acquires capabilities and speed rather than unutilized machine capacity, preferring organically built automated shop floors integrated with proprietary simulation tools, material science, and shop-floor IoT/AI.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and customer receivables remained healthy and stable despite accelerated top-line growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Standard quarterly PAT impact from heavy ongoing depreciation as capital investments precede revenue ramp-ups.
- Long acquisition and validation lead times (minimum 18 months for bearing nominations) in the aerospace and defense sectors.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** Can the 19% revenue growth and faster EBITDA growth trajectory be sustained in H2 FY27?  
  **A:** Management confirmed that once a threshold of growth is reached, the company intends to sustain and surpass it, maintaining EBITDA margins on an annual baseline.
- **Q:** Is the Unitec JV on track for April 2027 commissioning and will capacity be fully utilized in FY28?  
  **A:** JV commissioning is on track, but full utilization will not occur in FY28 as new business divisions ramp up gradually.
- **Q:** Why did consolidated receivables increase in the cash flow statement?  
  **A:** Receivable DSOs remain strictly stable at healthy baseline levels despite elevated top-line growth; there is no receivable strain.
- **Q:** What caused the 19% YoY rise in other expenses during Q2?  
  **A:** Input costs, logistics, power, fuel, and investment in new product development drove the rise, which will be offset through higher operating scale.
- **Q:** How is the company handling technical and leadership pipeline expansion?  
  **A:** NRB hires 20–40 candidates annually from top engineering colleges with specialized master's degrees and 3 years of industry experience, building internal leadership through 20%–30% merit-based promotion jumps rather than lateral market poaching.

### One-line takeaway
NRB Bearings delivered strong 18.8% revenue growth with 18.2% EBITDA margin in Q2 FY27, capitalizing on robust CV, industrial, and export demand while strategically scaling into high-margin aerospace, defense, and EV-agnostic applications.
