# Suprajit Engineering Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly operating revenue of Rs 1,070 crore, strong 24% YoY revenue growth, and 57% YoY EBITDA growth driven by successful global restructuring.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 1,070 crore, up 24% YoY from Rs 863 crore.
- Consolidated Operational EBITDA: Rs 129 crore, up 57% YoY from Rs 82 crore.
- Standalone Revenue: Rs 470 crore, up 20% YoY from Rs 390 crore.
- Standalone Operational EBITDA: Rs 60 crore, down 0.3% YoY from Rs 61 crore.
- Total Debt: Rs 776 crore as of June 2026.
- Surplus Cash Balance: Rs 243 crore invested in mutual funds and bonds as of June 2026.
- GCM Revenue: up ~27-28% YoY; EBITDA margin moved from 5.8% to 12.6%.
- ICM Revenue: up ~21% YoY; EBITDA margin down from ~15% to 13%.
- CBS Revenues: up 110% YoY; brake shoes and brake pads up ~80% YoY.
- PLE Revenue: up 5.4% YoY; EBITDA margin down from 2.8% to 6.7% (transcript states down from 2.8% to 6.7%, though mathematically this is an increase; management attributed it to a drop in EBITDA and margin pressure due to delayed price increases).
- SED Revenue: up 48% YoY; EBITDA up 100% YoY with margins close to double digits.

### Highlights
- Highest ever quarterly operating revenue of Rs 1,070 crore.
- Global Cables and Mechatronics (GCM) restructuring successfully completed, yielding margin expansion.
- SED won the ACMA Manufacturing Excellence Award and received awards from Mahindra Last Mile Mobility, Bajaj, and TVS Motors (Platinum award for TPM).
- STC and ICM jointly received Ather's Most Innovative Supplier Award.
- SED expanding capacity on a war footing and planning to temporarily relocate to a larger leased premises before rebuilding the current plant into a multi-story electronics facility.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated that the full-year guidance provided in the press release dated May 25, 2026, still holds and they see no concern in meeting it. 
- Consolidated double-digit growth target with operational EBITDA margins between 12% to 13.5%.
- GCM operational EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 10% to 12%.
- ICM EBITDA margin guidance around last year's level (~15%).
- PLE EBITDA margin guidance around last year's level (~12%).
- SED EBITDA margin guidance targeting around 10%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global automotive and non-automotive businesses remained muted amid Middle East conflicts, oil/commodity prices, trade restrictions, and shipping disruptions.
- Indian automotive sector had a solid quarter, growing at about 22% with solid double-digit growth in PV and 2-wheeler segments.
- GCM is experiencing strong new business wins across India, Mexico, and China.
- Phoenix Lighting (PLE) Trifa sales in the Middle East remain soft, but deliveries are ramping up to a major U.S. retailer.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone margins and ICM/PLE margins faced pressure due to sky-high raw material prices and wage inflation (particularly in the NCR region due to labor migration for elections).
- Management considers margin pressure in India a temporary timing issue as discussions are ongoing to pass on wage cost increases to customers.
- GCM gross margins benefited from tight, lean operations, top-line growth, and global cost-improvement projects.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- SED is relocating to a larger leased premises and planning to completely rebuild its current plant into a larger multi-story facility.
- STC's new building is on track for completion in Q3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt stood at Rs 776 crore as of June 2026.
- Surplus cash was Rs 243 crore in mutual funds and bonds as of June 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Wage inflation and labor migration in the NCR region impacting standalone margins.
- Softness in Trifa sales in the Middle East.
- Continued muted demand in the non-automotive cable business in the U.S.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Viraj (SiMPL) on GCM margin sustainability:** Management clarified that operational margins (12.6%) had no material one-offs, driven by lean operations and top-line growth, and reaffirmed the GCM margin guidance of 10% to 12%.
2. **Anubhav Mukherjee (Prescient Capital) on SCS segment reporting:** Management reiterated that SCS has been fully integrated into GCM post-restructuring, and separate reporting is no longer meaningful.
3. **Rakesh (Axis AMC) on EV drivetrain participation:** Management explained they supply agnostic products (braking, clusters, actuators, cables) to virtually all EV and ICE players rather than focusing on the drivetrain itself.
4. **Gokul Maheshwari (Awriga Capital) on tariff recoveries:** Management noted tariff recoveries are a pass-through and not materially significant on a net delta basis.
5. **Jinal (Awriga) on SED run-rate and order wins:** Management highlighted robust order inflows, including major global EV and OEM wins (e.g., USD 37 million lifetime EV cable contract), and expanding electronics capacity due to heavy demand.
6. **Chirag Shah (White Pine Investment Management) on wage pass-through:** Management stated that material cost pass-through is smooth, but wage increases are heavily debated with customers and will take 1-2 quarters to resolve.

### One-line takeaway
Suprajit delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 24% revenue growth and a 57% jump in operating EBITDA, led by successful global restructuring, despite localized wage inflation and raw material pressures in the domestic business.
