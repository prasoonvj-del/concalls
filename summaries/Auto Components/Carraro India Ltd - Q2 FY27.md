# Carraro India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-14 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Absolute EBITDA grew, but margins faced pressure due to rising input costs and lagging price recoveries amid a difficult global environment.

### Highlights
- Domestic revenue grew 30% in H1, driven by structural 4-wheel drive tractor adoption.
- Agricultural revenue grew 23% in H1, outpacing domestic tractor industry wholesales (high single-digit growth).
- Raw material localization recovered to ~80% in Q2 from 74% in Q1 as local supply normalized; target remains 86% to 88%.
- Construction of a new paint shop building commenced; side-drive sub-assembly line and backlash machine commissioned.
- Signed an agreement for a third authorized service center in the aftermarket business.
- *Vs Record:* The record noted robust domestic demand offsetting export headwinds; the call confirms H1 revenue growth at 15% was driven by a 30% jump in domestic sales, while exports saw a recovery in Q2 after Q1's logistics-driven dip.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year FY27 revenue growth to remain within the 10% to 15% target range (anticipating closing close to INR2,500 crores, with at least ~10% double-digit growth expected for next year).
- Management expects continued growth in absolute EBITDA for FY27, with margins improving progressively as price recoveries catch up, localization increases, and new capacity lifts utilization.
- Management maintains its long-term guidance of reaching EUR350 million to EUR400 million (INR3,500 crores) turnover by 2030.
- Capex for FY27 is expected to be INR100+ crores (with second half more capital-intensive; 70%-80% may be spent this year with carryover to FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global environment is challenging due to the West Asian conflict affecting energy, freight, insurance, and input costs (steel and castings).
- European agriculture is subdued, Turkey is volatile, and U.S. customers remain measured in volume commitments.
- Indian tractor retail demand is healthy; wholesale volumes moderated recently due to a high base, uneven monsoon, and festive timing.
- Domestic 4-wheel drive tractor adoption is structural, with penetration expected to rise toward 35%-40% over coming years (current industry estimate ~23%-24%, moving to 27%-28%); Carraro holds ~56%-58% non-captive market share.
- Tele-boom Handlers (TBH) remain a bright spot; the global OEM INR300 crores in 3 years program is on track (reaching ~55%-60% of target this year), and domestic TBH market grew to ~800 machines in H1.
- Gears business expected to be flat for the full year, supported by a secured bull-gear nomination.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined by ~200 bps YoY in H1 (25.4% vs 27.5%) due to steel, casting prices, energy, logistics, and supplier labor costs.
- Raw material price increases are indexed in customer contracts with a 3-to-6-month lag. Other costs (energy, freight, labor) require separate commercial negotiations, which have started reflecting in the P&L in H2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex incurred was INR204 million.
- Paint shop construction started; portal axle capacity and differential support capacity on the 4WD line enhanced via new commissioning.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.34x.
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at ~INR955 million.
- Net working capital maintained tightly at ~40 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing West Asian conflict driving up energy, freight, and raw material (steel and castings) costs faster than recovery mechanisms.
- Global export markets remain uneven with lingering logistics disruptions and dynamic regional shifts (Turkey, Latin America, Europe).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raghunandhan NL (Nuvama Research) asked about the quantum of price inflation in domestic H1 growth and status of customer negotiations.** Management answered that price hikes accounted for roughly 2% of H1 growth (leaving 12-13% clean volume growth), and negotiations are constructive with 30-40% still ongoing.
2. **Lakshminarayanan (Tunga Investments) asked for the domestic vs international agri split and 4WD market share details.** Management broke down H1 agri domestic at INR480 crores (+34% YoY) and export agri at INR105 crores (-12% YoY), noting non-captive 4WD market share is around 56%-58%.
3. **Priyash Kothari (Sorticude Fund Management) asked about product complexity and the risk of OEMs manufacturing in-house.** Management explained that products are co-designed customized solutions involving extensive R&D, quick time-to-market, and technical moats, making in-house substitution difficult.
4. **Het Shah (Quantum AMC) asked about TBH export sales targets and margin guidance for H2.** Management confirmed TBH exports are on track to touch ~INR300 crores over 3 years (reaching ~INR180 crores this year) and guided to double-digit revenue growth with EBITDA margins recovering progressively depending on geopolitical factors.
5. **Ashwani (3P Investment) asked about corporate service charges and professional fees in other expenses.** Management clarified that corporate charges relate to centralized IT/group functions, while professional fees include R&D expenditure to maintain/develop new projects post-buyout of IP prior to the IPO.

### One-line takeaway
Carraro India delivered 15% revenue growth in H1 FY27 driven by strong domestic 4WD adoption, but margins contracted due to rising commodity and conversion costs lagging pass-through recoveries.
