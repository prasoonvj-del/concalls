# Simplex Castings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly PAT in the company's history, driven by strong execution in niche steel and power sectors.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 revenue target of around Rs 300+ crore; FY28 target of Rs 500 crore remains unchanged.
- Tone and demand view: Maintained positive tone, though management noted that mega railway wagon orders from Indian Railways are delayed to the next quarter.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Welcomed Mr. Rao, N.V.S. Reddy / V.J. Rao (former MD of AP Metro Rail Corporation) to the Board. Green hydrogen was not mentioned.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: CWIP increased to Rs 36.77 crore; management confirmed partial commissioning in 5-6 months, keeping FY27 guidance intact.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 63.01 crore (up 13.71% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 12.75 crore (up 32.12% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 20.23% (expanded by 282 bps YoY)
- **Profit before tax:** Rs 10.24 crore (up 37.41% YoY)
- **Profit after tax:** Rs 7.64 crore (up 36.98% YoY)
- **PAT margin:** 12.13% (expanded by 206 bps YoY)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 123.96 crore (up 23.21% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 24.26 crore (up 29.05% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA margin:** 19.58% (up 89 bps)
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 14.5 crore (up 40.55% YoY)
- **H1 PAT margin:** 11.7% (up 145 bps)
- **Capital work-in-progress (CWIP):** Rs 36.77 crore (as of 30th September 2026)
- **Order book:** Rs 150 crore (pending execution)
- **Capacity utilization target:** 80% to 90% of existing capacity by next financial year

### Highlights
- Delivered the highest-ever quarterly PAT in company history at Rs 7.64 crore.
- Received approval to raise close to Rs 100 crore via preferential allotment to bring premier Indian funds onto the cap table.
- Appointed Mr. Rao, N.V.S. Reddy / V.J. Rao as a Non-Executive Independent Director (36 years in Railways, 10 years in Metro Rails).
- Secured 90%–95% market share in current coke oven battery projects across private and public steel plants globally/domestically; claimed to be the largest manufacturer of coke oven doors in the world.
- Outsourcing the manufacturing of smaller castings (child parts) to outside vendors led to a reduction in other expenses.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 revenue guidance of around Rs 300+ crore.
- Second-half revenue expected to follow a 40:60 split between Q3 and Q4, driven by stronger customer execution budgets.
- Margins for H2 expected to be similar to H1, though sustainable long-term normalized PAT margin is viewed conservatively at 8% to 10% rather than the current ~12% spike.
- Target of achieving Rs 500 crore revenue by next financial year (FY28) remains in place, supported by planned capacity additions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains strong across steel, power, and railway sectors, expected to last 3–4 to 5 years.
- Mega railway wagon orders from Indian Railways (expected between July to September) have been delayed to the next quarter, impacting immediate Casnub bogie off-take for wagon builders.
- Thermal power demand is robust with projects lined up till 2032; L&T and BHEL partnerships are active.
- Defense and shipbuilding are kept as a small focus (10%–15%), as most defense designs from Russia require heavy R&D and indigenization.
- Expecting a coke oven door order from Brazil for two batteries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins expanded due to high-value niche product mix (e.g., coke oven doors).
- Long-term orders include price variation clauses to mitigate raw material inflation.
- Other expenses reduced due to outsourcing the manufacturing of smaller castings/child parts.
- Bill discounting platforms utilized with L&T and BHEL for thermal power projects to bridge working capital gaps.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CWIP increased from Rs 12.38 crore to Rs 36.77 crore as of 30th September 2026.
- Total planned capex is Rs 25 crore (alongside Rs 25 crore for working capital from the Rs 50 crore fund allocation plan).
- Fabrication shop structural capacity expansion targets moving from 500 tons to 1,500 tons per month; equipment capacity targets moving from 200 tons to 300-350 tons per month.
- Partial commissioning of capex expected in 5–6 months (March/April), with full completion spilling over slightly into early FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Additional preferential funds (~Rs 100 crore approval) will primarily support working capital expansion to scale towards the Rs 500 crore revenue target.
- Work-in-process inventory is expected to rise alongside higher turnover scale.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Railway mega wagon orders from Indian Railways are delayed to the next quarter, creating a temporary bottleneck for bogie off-take.
- Historical normalized PAT margins are 7%–8%; current >12% margins are an anomaly driven by specific niche orders and may normalize to 8%–10% long-term.

### One-line takeaway
Simplex Castings delivered its highest-ever quarterly PAT in Q2 FY27, backed by niche product dominance in steel and power sectors, while advancing capacity expansion and managing railway order delays.
