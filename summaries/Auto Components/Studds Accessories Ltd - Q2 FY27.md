# Studds Accessories Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume and export growth offset by raw material margin pressure.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 183.1 crore (up 18.6% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 22.8 crore (down 23.7% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 12.4%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 14.7 crore (down 28% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 8.1%
- **Q2 FY27 Volumes (2-wheeler helmets & boxes):** 2.04 million units
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 352.8 crore (up 16.2% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 42.3 crore (down 29.6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 12.0%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 27.0 crore (down 33.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 7.7%
- **H1 FY27 Volumes (2-wheeler helmets & boxes):** 3.99 million units (Domestic: ~3.5 million; Exports: ~0.4 million)
- **H1 FY27 Export Revenue:** Rs 79.4 crore (up nearly 50% YoY)
- **Export Revenue History:** Rs 53.2 crore (FY24) to Rs 127.6 crore (FY26)
- **Capacity Utilisation (Q2 FY27):** Helmets & boxes: 85%, EPS: 88%, Water decals: 64%
- **Capacity Utilisation (H1 FY27):** Helmets & boxes: 83%, EPS: 87%, Water decals: 65%
- **SMK Contribution to Sales:** Increased from ~13% in Q2 FY26 to 20% in Q2 FY27
- **Capex Guidance (FY27):** Rs 58.22 crore for new plant + regular capex = Rs 84.7 crore total

### Highlights
- Commenced commercial production at new plant on October 30, 2026, adding 1.5 million units of annual helmet and boxes manufacturing capacity (total installed capacity now ~11 million units per annum).
- Incorporated a wholly-owned subsidiary in Italy to function as a warehousing and distribution hub for Europe.
- Entered adjacent rider-safety categories via in-house manufacturing of riding jackets under the SMK brand (production started September 2026; target 40,000 units next fiscal year) and upcoming Bluetooth communication system launch in November 2026.
- Participated in EICMA (Milan, Italy) and India Bike Week (Panchgani) for brand building.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains volume growth guidance of 8% to 10% for FY27.
- Revenue growth guidance maintained at high teens (15% to 19%).
- Medium-term revenue aspiration: Scale overall revenue to Rs 1,000 crore, with exports contributing 25% to 30%.
- Long-term export growth sustainable rate guided at 20% to 25% (short/medium term at 30%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remains strong with no current demand issues.
- Export demand is robust across all three geographies (US, Europe, Latin America) growing at a fast pace.
- Premium segment and mid-segments do not face pressure from price hikes, whereas entry-level mass markets see deep discounting when raw material prices soften.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins and EBITDA profitability impacted by significant increases in styrene-based raw material prices.
- Implemented net price increases of ~9% across sales channels to partly offset raw material cost inflation.
- SMK gross margins stated to be 70% to 80% higher than Studds brand margins.
- Fixed costs for the Italian subsidiary impacted Q2 and Q3; profitability expected to normalize from next year as scale builds up.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total installed capacity stands at ~11 million units per annum post new plant launch.
- Planned additional capacity of 1.5 million units over the next 18 to 24 months, taking planned installed capacity to ~12.5 million units.
- Existing capacity was operating at a high utilisation level of ~83% as of September 30, 2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not explicitly discussed in detail aside from capex outlays.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in global markets due to geopolitical and trade-related uncertainties affecting styrene input costs.
- Speed of ramp-up in foreign markets where fixed costs are high, risking temporary losses in subsidiaries.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) on Capex and New Products:** Management stated total FY27 capex is Rs 84.7 crore. Riding jackets target 40,000 units next year (Rs 15–20 crore revenue potential in year one); Bluetooth capacity is over 200,000 units/year. Margins for new products will be in line with respective brand margins.
2. **Rakesh (Nine Rivers Capital) on Margins vs. Export Growth:** Management clarified that despite strong export growth, margins dropped due to severe raw material (styrene) inflation and annual wage hikes in April. A ~9% price hike has been taken, and further hikes depend on Q4 raw material trends.
3. **Sucrit D. Patil (Eyesight Fintrade) on Execution Risks in Foreign Markets:** Management stated the biggest execution risk is the ramp-up speed in foreign markets with high fixed costs, potentially causing temporary subsidiary losses. Domestic protection relies on product experience rather than pure discounting.
4. **Nikhil Upadhyay (SiMPL Innovative Brands) on RM Cycles and Competition:** Management noted this is the second extraordinary RM cycle (similar to COVID/'22-'23 dips). They calibrated price hikes to avoid discounting later and noted entry-level competition engages in deep discounting when RM softens, unlike mid/premium segments.
5. **Kunal Sabnis (Nine Rivers Capital) on Export Outperformance:** Management stated 50% export growth is not sustainable long-term; guided for 30% short-term and 20-25% long-term export growth as the base expands.

### One-line takeaway
Studds delivered double-digit revenue and robust export growth in H1 FY27, but profitability contracted due to severe styrene raw material inflation, which management expects to address through volume scale, capacity expansion, and potential further price hikes.
