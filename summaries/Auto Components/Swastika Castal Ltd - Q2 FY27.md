# Swastika Castal Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Standalone total income grew 24% YoY, profitability outpaced revenue growth, operating cash flow turned positive, and newly expanded capacity became operational.

### Key numbers
- Standalone total income (H1 FY27): INR21.25 crores (up 24% YoY)
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): INR3.34 crores (up 27.3% YoY from INR2.62 crores in H1 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 15%
- Operating cash flow (H1 FY27): INR1.04 crores (vs outflow of INR8.72 crores in H1 FY26)
- Export share of revenue (FY26): ~41%

### Highlights
- New machinery commissioned in March 2026 is now operational, with H1 marking the first meaningful period of utilization after stabilization.
- Profitability grew faster than revenue due to higher volumes, better fixed-cost absorption, and operational scaling.
- International client additions (Trench and Kuvag) and a new large Indian customer are progressing through pilot lot approvals, with full-fledged serial production revenue expected next financial year.
- *Record comparison:* The call confirms the double-digit growth noted in the prior investor deck, validating the execution of integrated precision aluminium casting capabilities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management refrained from providing unsupported revenue or volume guidance figures, but expects momentum to continue in H2 FY27.
- Full-fledged revenue contribution from new international customers (Trench, Kuvag) is anticipated in the next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Long-term demand in India's power transmission and electrical equipment sector remains encouraging, supported by the National Electricity Plan (targeting INR9.16 crores of transmission infrastructure investments from FY23 to '32).
- Transmission line additions in FY26 were ~37% higher YoY compared to FY25.
- Expanding beyond core power transmission into railways, industrial applications, and defense.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin improved to 15%, supported by higher business volumes and better absorption of fixed manufacturing costs.
- Margins remain subject to raw materials, energy costs, product mix, and customer pricing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Major manufacturing expansion is complete; new machinery is operational, providing sustainable headroom for growth without requiring large additional capex.
- Small, selective investments in machinery, furnaces, process improvements, and pollution control equipment will continue.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow turned positive at INR1.04 crores (compared to an outflow of INR8.72 crores in H1 FY26) due to normalized inventory levels and improved procurement/dispatch coordination.
- Working capital discipline, inventory optimization, and timely collections remain priorities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical factors and prior supply chain issues previously necessitated advance raw material stocking.
- External factors like raw material prices, energy costs, and global market conditions influence margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vijay (Individual Investor) on growth drivers:** Asked about 24% top-line growth. Management cited existing customer activity, power sector demand, and new operational machinery.
- **Raghav (Analyst/Participant) on operating cash flow:** Asked about the OCF turnaround to INR1.04 crores. Management explained the shift from advance raw material stocking (due to past wars) to disciplined inventory planning and cash conversion.
- **Sejal Deshmukh on capex:** Asked about planned capex. Management clarified that major expansion is complete, and any future capex will be selective and demand-driven.
- **Abhishek Malhotra on new customers:** Asked about Trench and Kuvag progress. Management stated pilot lots are approved, first orders arrived, and full serial production revenue is expected next financial year.

### One-line takeaway
Swastika Castal delivered a 24% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27 with improved EBITDA margins and positive operating cash flow, underpinned by newly operationalized capacity and strong power sector demand.
