# SKF India (Industrial) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 28.3% YoY driven by wind, heavy industries, and successful localization, despite margin pressure from raw material inflation and one-off costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained PBT margin guidance of 14%-16% (management noted they are currently at ~13% PBT margin after adjusting for one-offs).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "mixed" to more confident; management highlighted strong execution in wind and heavy industries, and a "step jump" in exports.
- **New topics/risks:** Management highlighted the impact of Middle East conflict on steel prices (up 16%) and potential future impact from Chinese steel imports.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the new TRB line (Pune) and CRB line (Ahmedabad) capacity additions.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 1,042.9 crore (up 28.3% YoY).
- **PBT:** INR 134.4 crore.
- **PBT Margin:** 12.9% (up 42 bps YoY).
- **Net Working Capital:** 18.9% of revenue.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Negative INR 6.1 crore (due to one-off settlement of intercompany payables).
- **Exports:** 16% of OE sales (up from 12% in Q1).

### Highlights
- **Strategic Drivers:** Focused on localization (new CRB line in Ahmedabad adds 220,000 units annual capacity), innovation (solid oil bearings, DuraPro Carbonitriding), and digitalization.
- **Segment Performance:** Wind (highest revenue ever), heavy industries (material handling/off-highway), and distribution showed strong growth.
- **Rail Segment:** Management noted a decline due to government shifting investment focus from rolling stock to tracks.
- **Capacity:** New Pune plant remains on track for 2028. Ahmedabad factory to add 2 more channels and 1 roller channel in the next year.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Margins:** Management maintains a near-term PBT margin target of 14%–16%.
- **Growth:** Aspiration to grow above GDP and IIP (Industrial Production) levels.
- **Steel:** Expects potential negative impact in next 1-2 quarters from Chinese steel imports if prices do not cool.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Macro:** GDP projected at 7.2%; Industrial Production (IIP) at 7.1%.
- **Wind:** 5th consecutive quarter of strong capacity addition; India is 4th largest wind player globally.
- **Steel:** Production healthy (>42 metric tons); long-term capex plans remain in place.
- **General Machinery:** Includes pumps, compressors, escalators, elevators, and textiles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Inflation:** Steel prices up ~16%; logistics costs impacted by higher oil prices.
- **One-offs:** Q2 included INR 10 crore of demerger-related expenses (vs INR 16 crore in Q1).
- **Forex:** Rupee appreciation at end of September provided a positive impact.
- **Efficiency:** Management is driving a cost-efficiency project with a consultant to reduce manufacturing costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Pune Plant:** On track for 2028.
- **Ahmedabad:** 2 full channels and 1 roller channel planned for next year.
- **Localization:** Goal to reach 70%-75% localization in general machinery by 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Working Capital:** 18.9% of revenue, described as a healthy level.
- **Cash Flow:** Negative INR 6.1 crore due to one-off intercompany payable settlement; management expects return to positive levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Geopolitical:** Middle East conflict driving up logistics and energy costs.
- **Raw Materials:** Steel price volatility and potential cost increases from Chinese imports.
- **Rail:** Government investment shift away from rolling stock impacting segment revenue.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Varun Jain (Analyst) questioned why management guides on PBT instead of EBITDA.** Management acknowledged the feedback and stated they would review the practice of measuring performance via PBT.
- **Mukesh Saraf (Analyst) queried the increase in other expenses.** Management clarified that the increase included a write-off of old receivables (offset by credit in other income), higher logistics costs, and legal/professional fees for efficiency projects.
- **Yash Goenka (Analyst) asked about revenue potential of new lines.** Management clarified these are not for new markets but to improve competitiveness/reduce lead times, expecting ~15% higher growth in those specific channels.
- **Viraj Kacharia (Analyst) asked about market share.** Management stated they do not have updated share data until the annual exercise, but noted they are gaining share in wind and heavy industries.

### One-line takeaway
SKF India (Industrial) delivered a strong 28.3% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, successfully leveraging localization to offset inflationary pressures, though margins remain sensitive to raw material costs and one-off expenses.
