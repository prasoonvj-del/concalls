# Auto Components - Engine & drivetrain parts: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector delivered robust top-line growth in Q2 FY27, powered by strong domestic demand in commercial vehicles, passenger vehicles, and construction equipment. However, profitability across multiple players was squeezed by sharp input cost inflation (aluminum, steel, scrap, and non-indexed commodities like rubber and plastic), geopolitical disruptions in West Asia driving up energy and freight costs, and air freight spikes (notably at Rico Auto). While companies are negotiating price pass-throughs with OEMs, these operate with a time lag. Amid these cost pressures, firms with strong export traction, structural segment shifts (e.g., 4WD tractor adoption), and robust order books continue to invest heavily in capacity expansion and strategic acquisitions.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
- **Consensus:** Managements widely agreed that domestic demand in Q2 was strong, supported by infrastructure projects, industrial production, freight activity, and pre-buy trends ahead of upcoming regulations. 
- **Divergence (Tractors & Agri):** Views diverged sharply on the agricultural/tractor segment. **Automotive Axles**, **Carraro India**, and **Pritika Auto** noted healthy tractor demand or structural tailwinds in 4WD adoption. Conversely, **Bosch**, **Craftsman Automation**, and **Sterling Tools** flagged softening or contracting tractor demand toward the end of Q2 due to uneven or below-normal monsoons.
- **Export markets:** Western markets (Europe/US) were generally described as soft or subdued by companies like **Carraro** and **Tenneco**, whereas players like **Uniparts**, **Divgi TorqTransfer**, and **Sansera** successfully expanded their export footprints through new program wins and global platform integrations.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Inflationary Headwinds:** Surging prices for metals (aluminum, steel, scrap) and non-indexed items (lube oil, plastic, rubber) squeezed gross and EBITDA margins across the board (e.g., **Carraro**, **Craftsman**, **Sterling Tools**, **Endurance**, **Tenneco**, **Pritika**). Additionally, cutting tool costs, energy/gas hikes, and minimum wage revisions (notably in Karnataka, cited by **Sansera**) added to cost pressures.
- **Logistics & Freight:** Geopolitical conflicts in West Asia disrupted shipping routes, extending transit times and forcing some players to incur heavy air freight costs. **Rico Auto** took a severe hit, booking Rs 40 crore in air freight to maintain single-source commitments.
- **Pass-through Lags:** Most companies have back-to-back pass-through arrangements or are engaged in active price negotiations with OEMs, but recoveries involve a 1-to-6-month time lag.

## Capex & capacity
Managements across the sector are actively investing in capacity expansions, greenfield facilities, and localization to support future growth:
- **Sansera Engineering** raised its FY27 capex projection to INR 8,000 million to fund faster-growing segments like ADS, semiconductors, and xEVs.
- **Endurance Technologies** guided for consolidated capex of INR 1,250 crore in FY27 alongside four new greenfield facilities.
- **Sterling Tools** increased its consolidated capex plan to ~INR 150 crores to support fasteners and EV components.
- **Automotive Axles**, **Carraro**, **Uniparts**, **Tenneco**, and **Pritika** are similarly advancing plant commissioning, paint shops, and capacity enhancements to service peak demand and reduce import dependencies.

## What changed since last quarter
- **Automotive Axles:** Raised its industry volume outlook significantly, expecting the market to cross 500,000 units for the year (surpassing the 2018 peak), shifting from a cautious stance to high confidence.
- **Rico Auto:** Experienced a dramatic negative shift, labeling it the "worst quarter" due to massive air freight losses from supply chain disruptions.
- **Carraro India:** Raw material localization recovered to ~80% in Q2 from 74% in Q1 as local supply normalized.

## What we were watching
- — **Government decisions (Finance Ministry direction to prioritize EVs for staff cars):** Not discussed across any of the 13 company calls.

## What to watch next quarter
- Progress and realization of price pass-through negotiations between component makers and OEMs.
- Resolution of Middle East shipping disruptions and normalization of logistics/freight costs.
- Sustainability of pre-buys ahead of regulatory changes and demand visibility in H2 FY27 post-festive season.

## Company one-liners
- **Automotive Axles Ltd:** Delivered its highest-ever Q2 revenue and a 67% YoY surge in EPS, backed by strong replacement-led M&HCV demand and newly operational capacity expansions.
- **Bosch Ltd:** Delivered strong top-line growth supported by the first-time integration of RBIC and announced a 3:1 bonus issue and 1:5 stock split, though elevated input costs and modest H2 demand remain near-term headwinds.
- **Carraro India Ltd:** Delivered 15% revenue growth in H1 FY27 driven by strong domestic 4WD adoption, but margins contracted due to rising commodity and conversion costs lagging pass-through recoveries.
- **Craftsman Automation Ltd:** Absorbing short-term margin pressures from commodity volatility and sub-optimal new plants while banking on operational leverage, price resets, and a massive $100M stationary engine order book to drive 15% sustained growth.
- **Divgi TorqTransfer Systems Ltd:** Strong operational execution and scale-up in transfer cases and exports have driven significant margin expansion, with management now focused on securing long-term growth through automatic transmissions and global platform integration.
- **Endurance Technologies Ltd:** Delivered strong revenue growth and secured strategic European assets, with management guiding for margin recovery in H2 FY27 as commodity headwinds ease and new Greenfield facilities ramp up.
- **Pritika Auto Industries Ltd:** Posted robust Q2 FY27 revenue and volume growth exceeding 30% and 22% respectively, though margins experienced near-term pressure from raw material inflation ahead of anticipated pass-throughs and capacity scaling.
- **Rico Auto Industries Ltd:** A disastrous quarter driven by one-time air freight costs to meet customer commitments, with management promising a recovery in Q4 as logistics normalize and price negotiations conclude.
- **SPR Auto Technologies Ltd:** Successfully executing a transition into a diversified, powertrain-agnostic mobility platform, leveraging strong order wins and a healthy balance sheet to fund growth despite temporary margin headwinds from commodity lags and acquisition-related finance costs.
- **Sansera Engineering Ltd:** Reported record Q2 FY27 performance with 32% revenue growth and margin resilience, backed by surging non-automotive ADS orders and a raised INR 8,000 crore capex plan for FY27.
- **Sterling Tools Ltd:** Reported record standalone revenue and volume growth outperformance, though profitability faced temporary headwinds from severe input cost inflation pending time-lagged price pass-throughs.
- **Tenneco Clean Air India Ltd:** Strong growth driven by market share gains and new technology wins, though margins face temporary pressure from non-indexed commodity inflation.
- **Uniparts India Ltd:** Delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue in Q2 FY27 with robust 37% YoY growth, driven by structural momentum in construction and new business wins offsetting a down agriculture cycle.
