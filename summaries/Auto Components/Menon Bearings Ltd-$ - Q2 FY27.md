# Menon Bearings Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported all-time high sales, EBITDA margins, and PAT, with strong volume growth and a solid business pipeline.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income Growth (QoY/YoY):** 58.60% YoY for the quarter; total income from operations for H1 FY27 at INR 194.9 crores (47% growth YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA Growth (YoY):** 103.8% YoY for the quarter; 77% for H1.
- **Consolidated PBT Growth (YoY):** 123% YoY for the quarter; 92% for H1.
- **Consolidated PAT Growth (YoY):** 117% YoY for the quarter; 89.55% for H1.
- **EPS Growth (YoY):** 117% for the quarter.
- **Cash and Bank Balance (including mutual funds):** INR 25.69 crores-plus.
- **Raw Material Price Increases (YoY):** Tin up 72%, copper up 50%, steel up 12%.
- **Segment Margins:** Brakes at 30% to 35%; Bearing at 23%; Alkop at 18% to 19%.
- **CapEx Plans (Next 12-15 months):** Approximately INR 5 crores in Menon Alkop and INR 4 crores in Menon Bearings.
- **Additional Business Pipeline:** INR 30 crores expected for FY27; INR 131 crores for FY27-FY28; INR 157 crores for FY28-FY29 (plus INR 35 crores potential from Europe).

### Highlights
- Achieved all-time high sales, EBITDA, and net profit in the company's history.
- Productivity increased by 25% over the last period, helping absorb overheads.
- Headcount increased by only about 5% while growing revenue at around 30%.
- Marketing expenses (including export freight) increased due to higher replacement market sales and export shipments to the US and Europe.
- Implementing automation (robotic cells, AI visual inspection) to reduce manpower dependency and improve quality and time, with an ROI of 2.5 to 3.5 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects revenue growth of more than 30% for FY27 and expects a similar 30%+ growth rate for FY27-FY28 and subsequent years.
- EBITDA margins are targeted to range between 20% to 22%, with a target of about 23% for next year.
- Revenue milestone of INR 500 crores is targeted for next year (FY28) or the year after.
- EV share from the Alkop segment expected to be 8% to 10% or more for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by domestic markets, rapidly growing US exports, and newly tapped European markets (yielding a potential of >INR 35 crores converting in FY27-FY28 and FY28-FY29).
- OEM business grew by almost 50% and exports by more than 75% compared to the same quarter last year.
- China Plus One and Europe Plus One policies are providing tailwinds as European companies explore India as a reliable supplier base.
- Despite a drought-like situation in Maharashtra impacting tractors to some extent, schedules from tractor companies remain at all-time highs due to diversified applications (harvesters, seeding) and exports.
- Diversifying non-auto/non-engine applications into home automation, airport applications, oil and gas, generators (Cummins), and refrigeration/AC compressors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices (aluminium, copper, tin, lead, steel) showed volatility, but pre-decided formulas allow passing on burdens to customers or gaining when prices drop.
- Gross margins declined by over 7 percentage points YoY, but EBITDA margins expanded due to process improvements, lower input consumption, higher yields, and productivity gains.
- Packing credit limit availed from bankers at low rates (4.65% to 4.75%) leveraging government subvention (2.75%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- CapEx is funded entirely through internal accruals without new term loans in the last six months.
- Planned investments of ~INR 5 crores in Alkop and ~INR 4 crores in Bearings over the next 12-15 months for automation and AI visual inspection systems.
- Single-piece flow automation across 16 bearing lines is expected to reduce manpower by ~50 employees.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowing/interest costs remained constant despite 100% turnover growth.
- Inventory levels built up initially to support US warehouse stock requirements (three-month buffer), which have now stabilized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical and macroeconomic volatility could impact projections.
- Volatility in raw material prices and logistics/freight shipment times.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bhargav Buddhadev asked about the drivers of expanding EBITDA margins despite a 7+ percentage point decline in gross margins.** Management explained that process improvements, yield enhancements, higher productivity (25% up), and overhead absorption offset raw material volatility.
2. **Bhargav Buddhadev questioned the impact of robotic automation on employee cost and headcount.** Management stated that headcount grew only 5% against 30% revenue growth, and automation in machining and visual inspection will reduce manpower significantly with a 3 to 3.5-year ROI.
3. **Vignesh Iyer asked about EV share and Alkop export drivers.** Management confirmed EV share is on track for 8%-10%+ for FY27, backed by 54 new parts developed over two years supplied to tier-2 customers like Concentric, TACO Prestolite, and Eaton Poland.
4. **Garvit asked about demand drivers and geographical expansion.** Management cited the China/Europe Plus One policies, aggressive marketing visits to the US/Europe, and robust order books across domestic OEMs, exports, and aftermarket.
5. **Athul Joby asked for segment-wise margins.** Management stated Brakes are at 30%-35%, Alkop at 18%-19%, and Bearings at 23%.
6. **Kunal Tokas asked about working capital impact from US stocking policies.** Management explained that minimum/maximum inventory levels are pre-planned with A-list US customers who require a 3-month buffer, and packing credit at subsidized rates (4.65%-4.75%) supports working capital.

### One-line takeaway
Menon Bearings delivered record-high quarterly sales and profitability in Q2 FY27, driven by robust volume growth, productivity gains, automation, and expanding domestic and export pipelines.
