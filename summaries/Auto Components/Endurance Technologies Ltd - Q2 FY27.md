# Endurance Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and strategic acquisitions (Fondalpress, Stöferle) offset margin pressure from commodity headwinds, with management guiding for margin recovery in H2 FY27.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 4,811 crore (+33% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 584 crore (+17.3% YoY); Margin: 12.1%.
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 274 crore (+21% YoY); Margin: 5.7%.
*   **Standalone Total Income:** Rs 3,761 crore (+40% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 395 crore (+18% YoY); Margin: 10.5%.
*   **European Business Income:** Rs 1,029 crore (+13% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 183 crore (+13.7% YoY).
*   **Net Cash Position:** Rs 436 crore (as of 30th September 2026).

### Highlights
*   **Acquisitions:** Acquired remaining 32% stake in Stöferle (now 100% owned) and 100% of Fondalpress (Italy) for <€1 million.
*   **Divestment:** Divested stake in Veicoli for €2.2 million.
*   **Incentives:** Maharashtra PSI incentive limit increased to Rs 858 crore (from Rs 600 crore); Rs 75 crore booked in H1 FY27.
*   **EV Business:** India standalone EV sales grew 185% YoY to Rs 229 crore (5% of net sales).
*   **Maxwell Subsidiary:** Reported positive PAT; revenue grew 94% YoY to Rs 85 crore.
*   *Note:* The company continues its aggressive expansion strategy with four new Greenfield facilities coming on stream.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management expects margin trajectory to improve from Q3 FY27 due to price pass-throughs and reduced drag from Greenfield facilities.
*   **RMC:** Management expects a 0.6% lowering of RMC-to-sales percentage in Q3 FY27.
*   **Capex:** Consolidated capex guidance for FY27 is Rs 1,250 crore.
*   **Revenue Targets:** Proprietary 4W products (suspension, brakes, driveshaft) revenue target is Rs 1,500 crore+ by FY31; Non-auto (solar/railway) target is Rs 250 crore in FY28, scaling to Rs 650 crore by FY31.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **India:** 2W sales +18%, PV +24%, 3W +25% (SIAM data).
*   **Europe:** New car sales +5.6%; 2/3 of vehicles sold are EV or hybrid.
*   **Outlook:** Management expects stability in Europe, citing the new "Accelerator Act" and caps on Chinese hybrid imports as potential opportunities for local supply chain growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** Declined 200 bps YoY to 10.5% due to higher RMC (70% vs 66% in Q2 FY26) and unadjusted conversion costs.
*   **Price Pass-through:** Customer price increases effective Oct 1, 2026, are expected to add 0.5% to gross margin.
*   **Greenfield Drag:** New facilities had a 0.5% negative PBT margin drag in Q2; management expects positive contribution next financial year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **ABS:** Capacity to reach 1.54 million units/annum by Q4 FY27.
*   **Brakes:** Chennai plant SOP in March 2027; Waluj plant added 1 million units capacity (SOP Q2 FY27).
*   **4W Casting:** Expanding at Shendra, Chakan, Chennai, and Vallam plants.
*   **Forging:** 5th aluminum forging press SOP expected in Q4 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   The company maintains a net cash position of Rs 436 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** West Asia tensions causing volatility in energy prices and supply chain uncertainty.
*   **Commodities:** Steel prices expected to increase in Q3 FY27.
*   **European Market:** Slow auto market growth, though management noted Endurance is growing faster than the market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Arvind Sharma (Gross Margin):* Asked about recovery. Management confirmed a 0.5% positive impact from price pass-throughs and 0.5-0.6% from commodity softening.
*   *Massimo Venuti (Fondalpress Acquisition):* Explained the rationale as acquiring a competitor with 16,400 sqm of building and 16 machines to insource casting and enter the truck sector.
*   *Shubham (Europe Sustainability):* Asked about order win sustainability. Management cited EU's new cap on Chinese hybrid imports and the "Accelerator Act" as tailwinds.
*   *Nishit Jalan (4W Scale-up):* Asked about 4W revenue trajectory. Management confirmed proprietary products (driveshaft, brakes, suspension) will ramp up over the next two years.
*   *Mumuksh Mandlesha (Battery Packs):* Asked about margins. Management stated it is a high-value business with decent margins, and they expect to increase margins as they scale.

### One-line takeaway
Endurance Technologies delivered strong revenue growth and secured strategic European assets, with management guiding for margin recovery in H2 FY27 as commodity headwinds ease and new Greenfield facilities ramp up.
