# Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Supported by strong domestic demand, improving international business, and sequential top-line and profitability growth.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: Rs. 1,405 crore (up 54.9% YoY vs Rs. 908 crore in Q2 FY26; up 15.5% QoQ vs Rs. 1,217 crore in Q1 FY27).
- Consolidated EBITDA (excl. other income): Rs. 256 crore (vs Rs. 123 crore in Q2 FY26 and Rs. 218 crore in Q1 FY27).
- EBITDA margin: 18.23% (vs 13.5% in Q2 FY26 and 17.96% in Q1 FY27).
- Profit before tax (PBT): Rs. 95 crore (vs loss of Rs. 10 crore in Q2 FY26 and profit of Rs. 65 crore in Q1 FY27).
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs. 71 crore (higher by 47% QoQ).
- Total new order wins in Q2 FY27: Rs. 673 crore (48% domestic, 52% export; program life ~4 years). Breakdown: CV Rs. 304 crore, Light Vehicle (LV) Rs. 272 crore, EV Rs. 7 crore, Farm Equipment Rs. 90 crore. Casting orders within this total stood at Rs. 447 crore (including Rs. 272 crore for North American LV).
- Debt reduction in H1 FY27: Rs. 126 crore; management maintains guidance to reduce total debt by Rs. 500 crore for FY27. Gross debt expected around Rs. 1,670 crore to Rs. 1,700 crore by end of FY27 (down from Rs. 2,173 crore in March).
- Capex: H1 FY27 capex at Rs. 390 crore; full-year FY27 capex guided close to Rs. 550 crore for the group (including ~Rs. 160 crore in H2, mainly for machining). JV investment pending: Rs. 50–55 crore over the next year (with Rs. 15–20 crore more in H2 FY27).
- Geographic export breakup shift: Europe increased to 41% and North America around 58% of total export sales (compared to Europe at 28% two years ago).
- Aluminum forging and cold forging capacity utilization: More than 50% (current aluminum forging capacity is 3,000 tonnes).
- Railway wheels JV targets: 20,000 to 25,000 wheels to be supplied in this financial year; optimum utilization EBITDA margin expected at 18% to 20%.

### Highlights
- Completed a substantial part of planned manufacturing investments; focus has shifted to improving utilization, productivity, and returns.
- Casting order win of Rs. 272 crore for North American light vehicle applications opens doors for the casting export market.
- Subsidiaries (including casting and oil & gas businesses) are operating at 16% to 17% margins at an individual level.
- Railway wheels JV trial batch of 300 wheels ready and under inspection by railways; commercial production expected to start partially in December and scale up in Q4 (4,000 to 5,000 wheels/month).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains guidance of reducing total debt by Rs. 500 crore in FY27.
- Full-year FY27 capex guided at close to Rs. 550 crore.
- Management expects H2 to be even better than H1 FY27.
- Management aspires to cross previous peak margins and achieve 12% to 15% ROCE in FY27, with an aspiration of 20% ROCE next year.
- Management sees a strong market environment in both domestic and international markets for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic economy showing strong momentum: GST collections at ~Rs. 6.15 lakh crore in Q2; IIP growth at 7.4% in July and 8% in August.
- Commercial vehicle and passenger vehicle domestic sales grew by 28.7% and 30.7% respectively in Q2 FY27.
- North American market experienced a V-curve recovery after a lull last year; Europe demand remains stable with growing customer base.
- Passenger vehicles identified as the next major growth lever, expected to grow at 10%+ over the next 2 years (CY28/FY29).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Power cost reduced due to lower surcharges during the quarter.
- Other expenses elevated due to a significant rise in shipping/freight costs; net impact of shipping cost on the company was Rs. 8 crore to Rs. 9 crore in Q2. Management noted customers will not bear the entire freight increase, so some cost is shared.
- Negative carry/loss of Rs. 6 crore to Rs. 7 crore on account of raw material (steel) price increases in exports. Domestic steel prices saw hikes, which were successfully passed on. Another steel hike anticipated in India in January.
- EBITDA margin expansion expected sequentially (30-50 bps quarter-on-quarter) towards peak margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Group capex for FY27 projected at ~Rs. 550 crore (focused on machining and value-add capabilities).
- Railway wheels JV: Second phase approval taken for machining capability (additional Rs. 56 crore approval); total project cost remains intact. RKFL equity contribution limited to committed amounts (Rs. 20–30 crore remaining).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total net debt targeted to reduce to Rs. 1,500 crore by end of FY27 (from Rs. 2,000+ crore). Gross debt guided at Rs. 1,670–1,700 crore by March.
- Net working capital increased by ~Rs. 140 crore in value terms due to volume growth, though working capital days remained comparable to March levels; management aims to improve working capital days ahead.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile global macroeconomic landscape, geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, potential new tariffs, supply chain dynamics, and fluctuating currency/freight costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinil Shah (Dalal & Broacha) on export growth sustainability:** Management stated that the growth is sustainable, backed by strong international order wins and expanded capacities in North America and Europe.
2. **Aditya Kumar (Old Bridge Mutual Fund) on capex escalation:** Management clarified that capex increased to ~Rs. 550 crore due to buoyant market opportunities requiring continuous investment in machining and value-add capabilities.
3. **Abhishek Jain (KRIIS PMS) on elevated other expenses:** Management attributed this to surging shipping costs, which will moderate only when freight rates decline.
4. **Sahil Sanghvi (Monarch Networth Capital) on Railway Wheels JV timeline:** Management confirmed 300 wheels are under inspection, commercial production will start partially in December, scaling to 4,000-5,000 wheels/month in Q4, targeting 20,000-25,000 wheels for the fiscal year.
5. **Akshay Shetty (Individual Investor) on RDSO approval for Railway Wheels:** Management clarified that no RDSO approval is required for wheels; inspection is done directly by the railways.
6. **Chirag Shah (Dalal & Broacha) on whether export order wins replace existing vendors:** Management clarified they are not replacing vendors; orders are for new product launches by clients where RKFL has secured a position amid robust RFQ inflows.

### One-line takeaway
Ramkrishna Forgings delivered robust Q2 FY27 performance with 54.9% YoY revenue growth and margin expansion, supported by strong domestic demand, a V-curve recovery in North America, and aggressive deleveraging plans.
