# Triton Valves Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-28 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong organic demand and robust absolute growth across segments despite commodity inflation and geopolitical headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Group Consolidated Sales:** Rs 186.50 crore (up 38.5% YoY, 17.5% QoQ).
- **Segment-wise Sales (Group Level):** Automotive Rs 103 crore (55%), Metals Rs 79 crore (42%), Climate Control Rs 3.89 crore (2%).
- **Group EBITDA:** Rs 12.40 crore (up from Rs 8.80 crore YoY).
- **Group Consolidated PAT:** Rs 9.8 crore (reported, including ~Rs 4.75 crore tax credit from merger; without it, PAT was ~Rs 5.25 crore).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 12.41 crore.
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 7.50 crore.
- **Volume vs Value Growth:** Approximately 20% volume growth, with the remainder driven by value/realization.

### Highlights
- Completed a 3:1 bonus issue in the first week of April 2026.
- Received final NCLT orders approving the merger of Tritonvalves Climatech Private Limited into Triton Valves Limited, bringing tax shields into play.
- Implemented net reporting of brass scrap sales under IndAS (job work substance vs. sale/buyback form), optically shrinking top-line but improving ratio metrics on standalone financials.
- Received Letter of Intents (LOIs) from global TPMS players AUMOVIO and Sensata; existing customer Bosch scaling up.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets FY27 revenue to be higher than the previous year.
- Management aims to scale the EV component vertical to Rs 100 crore and the climate control vertical to Rs 100 crore over the coming quarters.
- Management maintains a long-term goal of reaching Rs 1,000 crore revenue by FY30, with a "plan B/C" in place even if climate control does not pick up.
- Capex commercialization: 50% to 60% of the Rs 15 crore annual capex is expected to commercialize in FY27, with the rest in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Automotive segment growth is primarily organic (approx. 80%), driven by vehicle production growth of 12-15% in passenger cars and two-wheelers during Q1.
- EV two-wheeler demand is outstripping supply; electrification crossed 10% in July, and management expects it to reach 20-25% in 1 to 1.5 years.
- Climate control demand remains weak due to unfavorable summer seasons and cheap Chinese dumping; management is lobbying the government for a Minimum Import Price (MIP) and Quality Control Orders (QCO).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Copper and brass prices have almost doubled over the last 12 months, expanding working capital requirements.
- Gross contributions declined by 145 bps QoQ and 181 bps YoY due to the denominator effect of commodity price pass-throughs, while absolute operating profits remained stable and higher.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Annual capex guidance is set at Rs 15 crore (Rs 10 crore for automotive/EV/TPMS, Rs 5 crore for Future Tech metals for special alloys).
- Capacity utilization in tubeless valves, TPMS, and EV components is running in excess of 85%–90%; metals capacity utilization stands at 65%–70%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth increased from Rs 128 crore to Rs 138 crore in Q1.
- Debt-to-EBITDA is hovering around 3x, with management targeting to bring it between 2.5x and 3x by year-end.
- Working capital cycles expanded due to historic high copper prices.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions (the Iran crisis in Q1 disrupted Middle East raw material shipments and logistics).
- Persistent copper and brass commodity price volatility and supply chain disruptions.
- Indiscriminate Chinese dumping in the climate control segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dolly Choudhary asked about the bifurcation of growth into realization and volume.** Management stated that roughly 20% is volume growth and the rest is value/realization growth.
2. **Sudhir asked about capacity constraints and margin recovery.** Management replied that organic growth plus new programs will sustain momentum, and percentage margins are optically lower due to commodity pass-throughs, but absolute EBITDA is expanding.
3. **Rohit asked about revenue mix and ROCE targets for the metal business.** Management stated that metals could become 60%+ of group revenue due to copper price inflation, and standalone metal ROCE is targeted at 20%–25%.
4. **Manish Kela asked about engagement with smaller EV two-wheeler players.** Management confirmed deep engagement with startups in Bengaluru like Simple Energy.

### One-line takeaway
Triton Valves reported strong Q1 FY27 performance with 38.5% YoY revenue growth, driven by robust automotive and metals demand despite commodity inflation and supply chain headwinds.
