# PPAP Automotive Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, robust order wins, and improving operational metrics, despite raw material cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR156.4 crores (up 34.1% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR12.4 crores (up 33.3% YoY)
- **Capacity Utilization:** 73% (Tooling business at 84%)
- **Lifetime Orders Secured:** INR131 crores (up 51.8% YoY), with EV programs contributing INR64 crores
- **Aftermarket Revenue Growth:** 30% YoY
- **Battery Business Revenue Growth:** 4x YoY (on a lower base)
- **Sale Proceeds from Japanese JV Exit:** INR100 crores (approx. INR8 crores paid in taxes)

### Highlights
- Unified the company and subsidiaries under the "Ajay Group" identity.
- Tooling business is being hived off into a wholly owned subsidiary named Meraki Precision Tools Engineering Limited (expected completion in Q3 FY27).
- Initiated the merger of Avinya Batteries Limited into parent PPAP (expected completion in Q4 FY27).
- Formed a technology partnership with Hutchinson for advanced body sealing systems (glass run channel focus).
- Aftermarket business launched 345 new SKUs (total 1,312 SKUs) and expanded distribution network to 155 distributors.
- Industrial products export contribution stood at approximately 30% of the segment's revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the strong Q1 growth momentum to continue into Q2 and throughout the year, with quarter-on-quarter sales increases.
- Sustainable long-term EBITDA margin targets are expected around 12% to 13%.
- Management targets the industrial product business to contribute approximately 10% of overall revenues over the medium term.
- Management targets the aftermarket business to contribute around 10% to overall revenues.
- Management targets the company to be net debt-free within the next 3 years.
- Capex for FY27 is outlined for EPDM capability development (including 1 new line in Q2 and 2 more planned), toolroom expansion, and land requirements in the west area near Sambhaji Nagar.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry delivered strong Q1 performance with record-high quarterly sales across PVs, CVs, and 3-wheelers, supported by lower GST rates, finance availability, and new launches.
- Passenger vehicle sales grew 25% YoY to 1.27 million units (utility vehicle momentum); 2-wheelers up 20.3%; CVs up 18.3%; 3-wheelers up 29.7%.
- Vehicle exports recorded their strongest first quarter.
- Upcoming festival season, lower vehicle ownership costs (GST 2.0), and easier financing are expected to sustain demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material prices increased by approximately 4%, out of which management has been able to pass on about 2% to customers; discussions for the balance 50% are ongoing and expected to settle by end of Q2/start of Q3.
- EBITDA margins benefited from fixed cost absorption and better capacity utilization, though raw material inflation impacted full margin potential.
- Battery business continues to face challenges from rising raw material costs, pricing pressures, and short customer delivery timelines.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- JV exit proceeds (~INR100 crores) utilization: ~INR8 crores for taxes, 25% retained for working capital, and the rest for strategic capex.
- EPDM segment: One new line operational in Q2, with 2 additional lines planned for FY27.
- Tooling business capacity utilization at 84%; output intended to increase by 20% this year.
- Setting up a new distribution warehouse in the West in Q2 for the aftermarket business.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Target to achieve net debt-free status in the next 3 years.
- 25% of net JV sale proceeds retained for working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Watchful of geopolitical developments, commodity price volatility, and global supply chain dynamics.
- Battery business continues to operate in a challenging environment, contributing losses, though management focuses on minimizing them.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Hardik Chheda asked about revenue expectations and details regarding the Hutchinson JV.** Management replied that they are engaging with customers on new solutions (primarily glass run channels) and expect clarity/breakthroughs by the end of the year, focusing initially on the domestic market.
2. **Dhruv Rawani asked about operating margin targets and raw material cost pass-through.** Management stated that 4% raw material inflation impacted costs, 2% has been passed on, and the balance is under negotiation for settlement by the end of Q2/early Q3.
3. **Mihir Shah asked if Q1 growth is sustainable and about EV content per vehicle.** Management confirmed that Q2 demand is strong and momentum will continue. They clarified that PPAP's products are engine agnostic, though EVs demand a bit more appearance/premiumness.
4. **Saket (individual investor) asked why historical pre-COVID margins (higher) are not visible and what sustainable margins look like.** Management answered that pre-COVID pricing dynamics differ from current competitive markets, and sustainable margins for automotive ancillaries like them are around 12% to 13%.
5. **Saket asked about the company's long-term sales growth lag compared to industry and client loss.** Management clarified no clients were lost, but noted that industry growth was heavily driven by electronics/sensors where PPAP historically had no presence, whereas PPAP focuses on sealing systems and injection molded parts.

### One-line takeaway
PPAP Automotive reported a robust Q1 FY27 with consolidated revenue up 34.1% YoY driven by strong industry volumes and new order wins, while structural realignments like the Hutchinson tech partnership and Ajay Group consolidation set the stage for sustained growth.
