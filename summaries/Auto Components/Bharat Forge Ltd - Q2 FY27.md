# Bharat Forge Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted all-time high standalone sales, a strong defence order book, aggressive expansion in non-automotive sectors (aerospace, semicon), and robust margin expectations for H2.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone:** Revenue at Rs 2,526 crore (+30% YoY); EBITDA at Rs 655 crore (+20% YoY); EBITDA margin at 25.9%.
- **Consolidated:** Revenue at Rs 4,870 crore (+21% YoY); EBITDA at Rs 778 crore (+9% YoY); EBITDA margin at ~16%.
- **Exceptional item:** Rs 20 crore towards legal/consultancy charges for CDP Europe steel business restructuring.
- **Subsidiaries:**
  - JS Auto Cast: Revenue at Rs 220 crore (+16% YoY); EBITDA up 35% YoY; EBITDA margin at 14.2%.
  - Combined JSA and K-Drive quarterly run-rate at ~Rs 600 crore; targeting >Rs 2,200–2,300 crore annual revenue.
  - Overseas Europe: Revenue Rs 931 crore; EBITDA Rs 29 crore; EBITDA margin ~3.1%.
  - Overseas US: Revenue Rs 476 crore; negative EBITDA of Rs 6.7 crore.
- **Order Inflow & Order Book:**
  - H1 FY27 new order wins: Rs 2,100 crore across India manufacturing (Forgings bagged Rs 1,000 crore).
  - Unexecuted defence order book: Rs 10,950 crore as of September (includes ATAGS and CQB).

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly sales across domestic and export markets.
- Export CV segment delivered record revenue via volume growth, market share gains, and ramp-ups.
- Received bulk production clearance for ATAGS (184 guns to be delivered over 4 years starting Q1 FY28).
- Secured new multi-year aerospace business worth USD 100–120+ million across engine and landing gear components.
- Setting up a dedicated aerospace facility (middle of next year start; ~60% pre-booked).
- Semiconductor business currently at double-digit USD million run-rate; targeted to grow 2–3x in 2 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1 FY27, driven by cost-increase recoveries, semiconductor ramp-ups, and execution of new wins.
- Excluding inflationary top-line impact (2–3%), H2 EBITDA growth expected to match or exceed top-line growth.
- India manufacturing revenue guided to exceed Rs 5,000 crore/quarter by end of next fiscal year, with a 200–250 bps margin expansion (from current ~Rs 3,000–3,400 crore run-rate at ~22% margin).
- K-Drive targeted to grow revenue 20–25% annually and expand EBITDA margins to double digits over the next 2 years.
- Defence business margins expected at 20–25%, expanding 200–300 bps as fully filled ammunition ramps up.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **US Class 8 & India Trucks:** Production levels remain stable and customers are discussing blocking capacity for the next 18–20 months despite choppy monthly order inflow announcements.
- **Domestic Automotive:** Gaining strong traction through JSA/K-Drive into SUVs, pickup trucks, and utility vehicles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability was impacted by elevated freight, energy, and input costs; pricing pass-throughs underway with a standard lag.
- Inflation caused an optical ~40 bps drag on standalone margins.
- Raw material steel for Indian manufacturing follows domestic pricing trends.
- US operations dragged by steel plant breakdowns and high raw material costs in aluminum forgings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Baramati Expansion:** Investing ~Rs 1,300 crore brownfield across four forging facilities and large machining; adding 100,000 tonnes of forging capacity.
  - Production trials: First two assets in Jan–Mar, third by mid-year, fourth by year-end.
  - Will more than double Baramati's output over 2–3 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated cash stood at ~Rs 4,158 crore post-QIP fundraise.
- Consolidated net debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.45x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued operational drag in overseas subsidiaries (US posting negative EBITDA; European margins low at 3.1%).
- CDP Europe restructuring underway (targeted completion by mid-next year).
- Customer absorption and induction rate dependencies on defence platform deliveries.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aerospace contract details:** Management clarified new contracts are with multiple customers across multiple products (not a single OEM), leading to a dedicated facility that is already ~60% sold out.
- **ATAGS ramp-up schedule:** Revenue recognition will begin in Q1 FY28, covering 184 guns over 4 years (~10% Year 1, ramping up steadily thereafter based on army absorption rates). Delivered via Bharat Forge Standalone initially.
- **US subsidiary turnaround:** Management expects a resolution for the US steel subsidiary by December; aluminum operations targeting break-even over 6–12 months via non-auto product additions.
- **Defence order book constituents:** Management confirmed the Rs 10,950 crore defence backlog includes unexecuted portions of both ATAGS and CQB orders.

### One-line takeaway
Bharat Forge is driving sustained non-auto diversification across aerospace, semiconductor, and defence, setting up strong multi-year operating leverage despite near-term drags in overseas subsidiaries.
