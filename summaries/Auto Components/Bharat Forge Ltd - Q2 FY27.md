# Bharat Forge Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly sales across export and domestic markets, strong order inflows, and expansion initiatives on track.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** INR 2,526 crore, up 30% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA:** INR 655 crore, up 20% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 25.9%.
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 4,870 crore, up 21% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 778 crore, up 9% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** ~16%.
- **JS Auto Cast Revenue:** INR 220 crore, up 16% YoY (EBITDA margin 14.2%).
- **Consolidated Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.45x.
- **Consolidated Cash:** INR 4,158 crore (post-QIP).
- **Defense Order Book:** INR 10,950 crore as of September.
- **New Orders (H1):** INR 2,100 crore across India manufacturing businesses (Forgings: INR 1,000 crore).
- **Europe Revenue (Q2):** INR 931 crore; EBITDA INR 29 crore (margin 3.1%).
- **US Revenue (Q2):** INR 476 crore; EBITDA negative INR 6.7 crore.

### Highlights
- Achieved highest quarterly sales across export and domestic segments.
- JS Auto Cast and K-Drive posted strong performance; combined quarterly run-rate grew ~50% since acquisition to over INR 600 crore.
- CDP (Europe steel business) restructuring is on track to conclude by mid-next year; Q2 included INR 20 crore exceptional item for legal/consultancy charges.
- Received bulk production clearance for ATAGS artillery guns.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1.
- India manufacturing sales expected to exceed INR 5,000 crore per quarter by the end of next year, with a 200–250 bps margin accretion (per management).
- Defense business margins expected to range between 20% to 25% depending on product mix.
- K-Drive EBITDA margin targeted toward double digits in the next 2 years with 20%-25% annual revenue growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Export CV business recorded highest revenue driven by volume growth, market share gains, and new business ramp-up.
- US Class 8 truck production levels remain stable; customers are blocking capacity for the next 18-20 months.
- Aerospace business secured significant new multi-million dollar wins across engine and landing gear segments (USD 100+ million lifecycle value).
- Semiconductor business growing 2-3x, supported by large double-digit million-dollar annual orders.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability impacted by escalating freight, energy, and input costs, with a lag in customer price recoveries.
- Inflation had an optical impact of ~0.4% on margins.
- US steel subsidiary faced one-time impacts from breakdowns; US aluminum subsidiary impacted by raw material costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up a new dedicated aerospace, semiconductor, and industrial forging and machining facility in Baramati (Brownfield expansion of ~INR 1,300 crore).
- Baramati expansion will add 100,000 tonnes of forging capacity, doubling output over 2-3 years; production trials to start in Jan-Mar.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity ratio at 0.45x.
- Consolidated cash stands at INR 4,158 crore following the QIP fundraise.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Difficult operating environment and lower utilization due to holidays in Europe.
- Breakdowns in the US steel subsidiary and raw material cost headwinds in the US aluminum subsidiary.
- Lag in passing on inflationary cost increases to customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Binay Singh (Morgan Stanley) on Aerospace/Semiconductor orders:** Management clarified that a new facility dedicated to aerospace is 60% sold out starting mid-next year, and semiconductor business is scaling up 2-3x with new forging capacity.
2. **Kapil Singh (Nomura) on US Class 8 truck outlook:** Management stated customer production levels are stable and customers are looking to block capacity for the next 18 to 20 months.
3. **Chandramouli Muthiah (Goldman Sachs) on H2 EBITDA growth convergence:** Management noted that excluding the 2%-3% inflationary top-line impact, H2 EBITDA growth will sync with or slightly exceed top-line growth.
4. **Gunjan Prithyani (Jefferies) on ATAGS execution timeline:** Management clarified that production approval was delayed (not the order), shipments start in Q1 FY28, and execution will span about 4 years across 184 guns.
5. **Amyn Pirani (Axis Capital) on Defense margins:** Management guided that defense margins will range between 20%-25% depending on mix, increasing by 200-300 bps with fully filled ammunition.

### One-line takeaway
Bharat Forge delivered record Q2 revenues driven by robust exports and domestic manufacturing growth, while gearing up for aggressive non-automotive scale-up in aerospace, defense, and semiconductors.
