# Fiem Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered best-ever quarterly sales and profit, outperforming industry growth despite input cost pressures.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Capex guidance raised to ~Rs 150 crore (from Rs 100 crore). Revenue growth guidance maintained at 15-20% (targeting top end).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive; management sees no demand weakness despite monsoon concerns.
- **New topics or risks:** Management acknowledged specific upcoming HMSI models where Fiem is not participating; Ather Konarc business has scaled up.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Capex is tracking higher due to strong demand projections; 4W segment remains slow as previously communicated.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 931.78 crore (+30.97% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 131.07 crore (+32.25% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14.07% (vs 13.93% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 84.17 crore (+31.97% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,701.68 crore (+25.08% YoY).
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 235.13 crore (+26.1% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 149.36 crore (+24.63% YoY).
- **Cash & Equivalents:** Rs 163.42 crore (post Rs 105.28 crore dividend payout).

### Highlights
- **Customer Performance:** TVS business grew ~50% (LED lighting/model refreshes); Honda business grew 20% (Rebel 300, ADV 160); Hero business nearly doubled.
- **EV Segment:** Sales to EV makers doubled YoY; Ather (Konarc), River, and Simple Energy volumes are ramping up.
- **Industry Context:** 2W production was 8.09 million units (+16.5% YoY, +11% QoQ); EV 2W segment grew >80%.
- **Order Pipeline:** >100 models in pipeline for '27-'28 and '28-'29, representing Rs 800-900 crore of additional business.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Management targets the top end of the 15-20% growth range for FY27.
- **Margins:** Management guided for ~14% EBITDA margin for the full year.
- **Capex:** Increased to ~Rs 150 crore for FY27 (from Rs 100 crore) to support new capacity lines in South India.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains healthy in both urban and rural markets.
- Management views EV adoption as a structural shift favoring Fiem due to higher LED-intensive lighting content.
- 4W segment penetration remains slow; no significant revenue contribution expected in the next 12 months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined sequentially (169 bps) due to cost escalation; however, EBITDA margin improved via operating leverage.
- Management noted that cost escalations are continuous and are managed through operating expenditure control.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capacity Utilization:** ~80%.
- **Expansion:** Adding capacity in Hosur and Mysore to support customer requirements; includes injection molding and metalizing plants.
- **Asset Turn:** Currently ~3x.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables increased due to higher sales; management expects normalization in the next quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Geopolitical:** West Asia conflict impacting energy, freight, and commodity prices.
- **Currency:** Weaker rupee increasing costs of imported parts.
- **Market Share:** Management acknowledged losing participation in certain upcoming HMSI models transitioning from conventional to LED lighting.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Are you losing market share to Lumax in HMSI?** Management confirmed they are not participating in some specific model changes (conventional to LED) but highlighted recent wins with Rebel 300 and ADV 160.
- **Q: Is Ather Konarc business fully reflected in Q2?** Management stated it has started and is in the ramp-up phase; volumes are expected to be good in coming quarters.
- **Q: Why the capex hike?** Management cited strong demand projections from customers, necessitating new capacity lines in South India.
- **Q: Any update on 4W?** Management reiterated that 4W is a small percentage of sales and will remain so for the next 12 months; no margin differentiation vs 2W.
- **Q: Is the 30% revenue growth sustainable?** Management attributed it to strong OEM performance and product mix, maintaining the 15-20% full-year guidance.

### One-line takeaway
Fiem delivered a record quarter with 31% revenue growth, driven by strong 2W industry volumes and EV/LED tailwinds, while raising capex to support robust demand.
