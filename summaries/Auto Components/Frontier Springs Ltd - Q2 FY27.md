# Frontier Springs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Business is back on track after a constrained Q1, with strong revenue and EBITDA growth.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 99.73 crores (up 20.54% YoY from Rs. 82.74 crores; up 27.11% QoQ from Rs. 78.46 crores).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs. 178.19 crores (up 12.72% YoY from Rs. 158.08 crores).
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 29.73 crores (up 34.69% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 48.97 crores (up 15.23% YoY).
- **EBITDA margin:** 29.81% (up 313 bps YoY and 529 bps QoQ).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA margin:** 27.48% (up 60 bps YoY).
- **Profit before tax (PBT):** Rs. 28.5 crores (up 35.19% YoY).
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs. 21.33 crores (up 35.79% YoY; up 77.57% QoQ).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 33.34 crores (up 9.48% YoY).
- **Finance cost:** Rs. 0.04 crores.
- **Depreciation:** Rs. 1.30 crores.
- **H1 FY27 Operating cash flow:** Rs. 63.48 crores (vs. Rs. 31.41 crores for the whole of FY26).
- **Trade receivables:** Rs. 42.97 crores (down from Rs. 60.89 crores at end of March).
- **Total borrowings:** Rs. 5.35 crores.
- **Cash and bank balances:** Rs. 32.73 crores.
- **Current order book:** Rs. 300 crores to Rs. 350 crores.
- **Coil and air spring capacity utilization:** ~80%.
- **Forging capacity utilization:** ~50% (can go up to 60%-70% with automotive job work).
- **Land purchase:** Rs. 10 crores to Rs. 12 crores.

### Highlights
- Q2 performance recovered strongly from a supply-constrained Q1 caused by geopolitical issues affecting raw material availability.
- Received RDSO approval for the FIBA (Failure Indication and Brake Application) system; a trial order of 25 coach sets is expected by December 15, running for 6 to 8 months.
- Company purchased a new piece of land worth Rs. 10 crores to Rs. 12 crores for future expansion and new railway products.
- Management noted that prior record themes (railway tailwinds) continue to play out positively.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised gross revenue guidance for FY27 to "at least Rs. 450 crores" (working towards the previously stated Rs. 500 crores) due to Q1 supply disruptions.
- Management expects a minimum 20% sales growth for FY28 over FY27 execution.
- Full-year EBITDA margin guidance maintained between 27% to 29%.
- New product project cost expected to be Rs. 15 crores to Rs. 20 crores (total capital outlay including land around Rs. 30 crores).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Steady demand across freight stock, coaching stock, and locomotive applications for coil springs; springs are fitted on the Vande Bharat Express.
- Tenders for 4,000 wagons finalized, driving next-leg growth. 6,000 wagons tender finalized for wagon builders (Titagarh and Jupiter).
- Air spring business for LHB coaches running at ~250 coaches/month; demand expected to be robust for the next 3 years.
- Forgings division growth driven entirely by railways; additional job work taken up for automotive, road construction equipment (JCB, Caterpillar, ACE), and mining sector exploration.
- Export market started for railway components.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel, oil, and gas prices are higher, which management flags could put some pressure on margins in coming quarters (potential 1% to 2% dip), but pricing is actively managed via daily tender bids.
- ContiTech resolved bellows supply delays from Q1 by switching to air freight.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Coil spring manufacturing debottlenecking and modernization (automation) will add a minimum 20% capacity.
- Plant and facility setting up for the new product on the newly purchased land will start in Q4 FY27 and complete by Q1 FY28. Funded via internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains strong with negligible debt (Rs. 5.35 crores borrowings vs. Rs. 32.73 crores cash/bank).
- Operating cash flows strong at Rs. 63.48 crores for H1 FY27. Receivables improved to Rs. 42.97 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues and war constrained raw material (steel, oil, gas) availability in Q1, causing execution delays and delivery period (DP) constraints to avoid liquidated damages.
- Rising input costs of steel, oil, and gas may create short-term margin pressure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aman Soni (Seven Alpha Investors) asked about the reduction in FY27 guidance to Rs. 450 crores:** Management clarified that orders are robust, but Q1 supply constraints and delivery period (DP) execution schedules pushed revenues into subsequent quarters.
- **Nishant Joshi (Advisors Pvt Ltd) asked about FIBA revenue potential:** Management stated current Indian Railways market is Rs. 70-80 crores; expects Rs. 10-15 crores next year post-approval, scaling to Rs. 30 crores with similar margins.
- **Mahesh Atal (Investment Advisors) asked about cash deployment and ROCE:** Management stated they bought land worth Rs. 10-12 crores for a new railway import-substitution product line, with a total project outlay of Rs. 15-20 crores, funded entirely via internal cash.
- **Aditya Srinivasan (PMS) asked about ContiTech bellows exposure:** Management confirmed ContiTech overcame the Q1 shipping delays via air freight, resolving the backlog.
- **Mahesh Atal asked about defense progress:** Management noted sample shipments have been sent to BEML for the forging division, but formal defense approval is taking time.

### One-line takeaway
Frontier Springs bounced back strongly in Q2 FY27 with a 20.5% YoY revenue growth and ~30% EBITDA margin, offsetting Q1 supply setbacks as it targets at least Rs. 450 crores in gross revenue for FY27.
