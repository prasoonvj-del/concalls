# INDO-MIM Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 / H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a solid H1 performance with improved return ratios, reduced debt, and strong demand across aerospace, IGT, and emerging high-tech segments, remaining confident on top-line guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 1,271 crore (vs Rs 1,218 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 2,490 crore (up by Rs 263 crore YoY).
- **Q2 PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 309 crore (vs Rs 325 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 32% (up 6.3% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 455 crore (Standalone PAT: Rs 443 crore).
- **Return Ratios (H1):** ROCE at 36.5% (up 5.7%); ROE at 26.3% (up 3.4%).
- **Debt Metrics:** Debt-to-equity ratio at 0.27 (down from 0.7); Debt-to-EBITDA at 0.19; Total debt at Rs 941 crore (vs Rs 1,255 crore YoY).
- **Working Capital:** Receivable days at 64; Inventory days at 441; Payable days at 88; Cash conversion cycle at 417 days.
- **Segment Contribution:** MIM & Investment Casting (60%), Casting (19%), Machining (8%), Powders (1%), Others (11%-12%).
- **Segment Growth (YoY):** Consumer (+46.5%, 16% share), Aerospace (+31.5%, 9% share), Medical (+21.5%, 19% share), Automotive (+19.4%, 25% share), DPG (+19.3%, 20% share), Electronic component manufacturing (-40%, 11% share).
- **FY27 Guidance:** Top-line target of Rs 5,000 crore and bottom-line target of Rs 850 crore.

### Highlights
- H1 delivered robust growth driven by operating leverage, lower leverage, and strong return profiles.
- Subsidiaries INDO-MIM Inc. contributed Rs 200 crore in revenue and Rs 52 crore in PAT; Maya Industries contributed Rs 150 crore in revenue.
- *Note on record:* While our record notes a net profit of Rs 2,152 cr for Q2 FY27, the transcript explicitly reports H1 PAT at Rs 455 crore and Q2 PBT at Rs 309 crore, indicating a reporting divergence in past records.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they are poised to comfortably meet the FY27 top-line target of Rs 5,000 crore and marginally meet or exceed the Rs 850 crore bottom-line target, with a formal update expected in Q3.
- Triax is targeted to reach a $32 million-$35 million top-line and achieve EBITDA neutrality by the end of the year, turning profitable next year.
- Vietnam facility for electronic component manufacturing is expected to generate revenue in fiscal 2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong, robust demand observed in aerospace, IGT (Industrial Gas Turbines), drone components, humanoid robots, and private satellite applications.
- Zero cancellations reported in the IGT segment; customers are pulling in requirements and discussing subsequent packages.
- Medical remains the highest-margin segment (lifestyle business). Defense is expected to grow significantly via small arms components in India and NATO spending overseas.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion during the period was driven entirely by component mix, with no raw material price increases or significant forex volatility.
- Heavier components resulted in lower margin percentages of sale in certain batches.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Primary processes have sufficient capacity; capex is targeted at secondary operations and machining to support ramping programs.
- Setting up an iron powder manufacturing facility with an approximate capacity of 3,000 tonnes per annum (1,500-2,000 tonnes for captive use, 1,000-1,500 tonnes for external sales).
- Evaluating inorganic acquisitions in Europe in the $20 million-$50 million range for vacuum casting, MIM, or gas turbine supply chain, with greenfield expansion as a backup plan.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt declined significantly to Rs 941 crore from Rs 1,255 crore YoY.
- Inventory days remain elevated at 441 days due to lumpy raw material procurement and WIP buildup for JIT delivery, but management targets compression by year-end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Subsidiaries other than INDO-MIM Inc. and Maya Industries are currently "draggers" marginally losing money.
- Delays noted from the customer side for the initial adoption of MIM components in the Indian smartphone/consumer segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI Securities) asked about H2 margin trends and Triax outlook:** Management explained that margins fluctuate based on component mix and part weight, while Triax is on track to reach $32 million-$35 million top-line and achieve EBITDA neutrality by year-end.
2. **Deepak Krishnan (Institutional Equities) asked about the Safran contract and Vietnam facility:** Management stated the Safran deal reflects European OEMs freeing up capacity for military/civilian aerospace, and the Vietnam facility will start generating revenue in fiscal 2028.
3. **Raj Shah (Equities) asked about smartphone MIM progress and IGT cancellations:** Management noted customer-side delays for MIM smartphone components, but confirmed zero cancellations and strong demand pulling in the IGT segment.
4. **Abhinav asked about humanoid/satellite applications and backward integration:** Management highlighted active multi-customer programs with very high potential volumes, and detailed the 3,000 tpa iron powder facility intended for supply chain security and high-value sales.
5. **Rajat Kataria (Share India Securities) asked for segment-level margin mix:** Management declined to provide detailed vertical contribution margins to maintain competitive opaqueness against rivals.

### One-line takeaway
INDO-MIM delivered a solid H1 FY27 with reduced debt and strong return ratios, remaining on track to meet its Rs 5,000 crore top-line guidance amid booming demand in aerospace, IGT, and emerging high-tech applications.
