# Lumax Auto Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY 27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and PAT growth, healthy order book expansion, and confident full-year outlook.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY 27 Revenue:** INR 1,556 crore (up 35% YoY, 14% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY 27 EBITDA:** INR 228 crore (up 34% YoY); Margin: 14.7% (broadly stable YoY)
- **Q2 FY 27 PAT:** INR 117 crore (up 50% YoY)
- **H1 FY 27 Revenue:** INR 2,919 crore (up 34% YoY)
- **H1 FY 27 EBITDA:** INR 434 crore (up 42% YoY); Margin: 14.9% (expansion of 90 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY 27 PAT:** INR 215 crore (up 64% YoY)
- **Order Book:** INR 1,879 crore at the end of Q2 (up from INR 1,600 crore in Q1 FY 27)
- **Free Cash Reserves:** INR 400 crore as of September 2026
- **Long-term Debt:** INR 442 crore (Debt-to-equity ratio: 0.26)
- **H1 FY 27 Capex:** INR 60 crore (Capitalization); Full-year capex guidance around INR 300 crore

### Highlights
- Crossed INR 1,500 crore quarterly revenue mark for the first time.
- Board approved setting up a new Mechatronics manufacturing facility in Manesar, Haryana, with a capital outlay of ~INR 1,500 crore (funded via debt and internal accruals); expected to be commissioned in 2 phases starting Q3 FY 28 with a peak annual turnover of ~INR 500 crore.
- Lumax Greenfuel Energy Solutions commenced production at its Nashik facility in Q3 FY 27, supplying ferrule-less tubes/fittings and receptacles to Mahindra & Mahindra.
- Order book composition: Advanced Plastics 44%, Mechatronics 27%, Alternate Fuels 20%, Structures & Control Systems 8%. ~85% of the order book is new business. EV and CNG together account for 29% (CNG 20%, pure EVs 9%), and new future technologies account for 22%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY 27 revenue growth to moderate to 15-20% YoY due to a higher base in FY 26.
- Full-year revenue growth guidance revised to 20-25% (reaching ~INR 6,000 crore).
- Management remains confident of delivering the absolute EBITDA target of INR 1,000 crore by FY 28.
- Long-term "20.20.20.20 North Star" aims for a 20% revenue CAGR from FY 25 to FY 31, targeting ~INR 11,000 crore in revenue by FY 31 and inching closer to a 20% EBITDA margin over 5 to 7 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry production in Q2 FY 27 grew by 19% YoY to 106 lakh units (PVs up 25% to 17 lakh, 2-Wheelers up 17% to 81 lakh, 3-Wheelers up 25% to 4.5 lakh, CVs up 34% to 3.5 lakh).
- Growth driven by GST 2.0 affordability, resilient rural/urban demand, infrastructure activity, and freight movement.
- Growth expected to moderate in H2 FY 27 as the industry moves past the lower base of H1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs (crude-linked polymers, electronic parts, freight, rupee depreciation) remain monitorables.
- Sequential QoQ margin dilution was attributed to rising input costs and a lag of about one quarter in pass-throughs, increasing revenue and denominator without incremental profitability. Management stated most inflationary costs are 100% backed/recovered from OEMs with a single-quarter lag.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Full-year capex guidance maintained at ~INR 300 crore, including greenfield expansions in Mechatronics, Advanced Plastics, and Alternate Fuels.
- Chakan IV IAC facility is in the SOP phase, with product launch slated for December/January.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Free cash reserves: INR 400 crore.
- Long-term debt: INR 442 crore.
- Debt-to-equity ratio: 0.26.
- Effective tax rate remains around 25%. Minority interest share for H1 stood at 11%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global economic environment remains challenging due to Middle East conflict impacting energy/commodity prices, shipping, transportation costs, and inflation.
- Ability of manufacturers to pass on rising input costs to customers remains important for profitability.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Hiranandani (PhillipCapital)** asked about upward revision of guidance. Management replied that full-year revenue growth is expected around 20-25% (up from earlier implied 12-14%). *(Management did not dodge).*
2. **Radha (Motilal Oswal)** asked about content per vehicle and Mechatronics progression. Management stated total content per vehicle is ~INR 70,000–75,000 for PVs and INR 20,000–22,000 for 2-Wheelers, with Mechatronics contributing ~20%. *(Management did not dodge).*
3. **Raksha Srivastava (Narnolia Financial Services)** asked if higher COGS in Q2 was due to delayed pass-throughs. Management confirmed a ~1-quarter lag in recovering raw material price increases from OEMs. *(Management did not dodge).*
4. **Deep Shah (New Vernon Capital)** asked if the BCM product development is in-house or via JVs, and if it's part of the INR 1,000 crore Mechatronics target. Management clarified BCM is standalone/tech tie-up (Japanese partner) and is part of the FY 31 target. *(Management did not dodge).*
5. **Apurva Mehta (AM Investments)** asked about Aftermarket growth steps. Management noted H1 aftermarket grew 9%, but remains optimistic on achieving 15-20% CAGR over 3 to 5 years via secondary demand and new product lines. *(Management did not dodge).*

### One-line takeaway
Lumax Auto Technologies delivered robust Q2 FY 27 performance with 35% YoY revenue growth and 50% YoY PAT growth, prompting an upward revision in full-year revenue growth guidance to 20-25%.
