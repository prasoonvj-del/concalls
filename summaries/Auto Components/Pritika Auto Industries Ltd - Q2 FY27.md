# Pritika Auto Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust volume and revenue growth supported by strong tractor demand and export wins, despite near-term margin pressure from raw material costs.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Net Revenue:** Rs 153.19 crores (vs Rs 116.45 crores in Q2 FY26, up 31.55 YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Net Revenue:** Rs 298.16 crores (vs Rs 231.06 crores, up 29.04% YoY).
- **Production Volume (Q2 FY27):** 15,665 metric tonnes (vs 12,881 metric tonnes in Q2 FY26, up ~22% YoY).
- **Production Volume (H1 FY27):** 30,000 metric tonnes (vs 25,267 metric tonnes, up ~19% YoY).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 21.21 crores (vs Rs 18.61 crores, up 13.97% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 13.85% (vs 15.99% in Q2 FY26; improved sequentially from 13.45% in Q1 FY27).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT - Q2 FY27):** Rs 7.43 crores (vs Rs 6.61 crores, up 12.41% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 14.55 crores (vs Rs 12.70 crores, up 14.55% YoY).
- **Solar Power MoU Savings:** Estimated at ~Rs 110 crores for the group over 25 years (~Rs 70 crores for Pritika Engineering and Meeta Castings combined).

### Highlights
- Production volumes grew ~22% YoY in Q2 FY27, driven by healthy OEM demand and improved throughput.
- Subsidiary Pritika Engineering Components secured an export order of ~Rs 12 crores per annum from an Italian precision engineering manufacturer.
- KION USA product received customer approval; regular production expected to commence in FY27-28.
- Signed an MoU with Spark Grid for solar power supply, projected to save ~Rs 110 crores over 25 years.
- *Continuity note:* The call aligns with the November 6 investor deck highlighting strong revenue/volume growth and European export breakthroughs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets long-term revenue growth of 15% to 20% year-on-year.
- Reiterated long-term installed capacity target of 1 lakh metric tonnes by 2028.
- Exports targeted to contribute 10% to 15% of sales in the medium term (3 to 5 years).
- EBITDA margin expected to remain around 14% to 15%, with upside potential as Lost Foam Casting (LFC) utilization scales above 50% to 60%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Tractor industry demand remains supported by agricultural mechanization, government initiatives, and rural demand.
- Commercial vehicle (CV) segment contributes ~7% of business, with plans for gradual expansion.
- Railway components development is ongoing via the RDSO route, with trials expected around January/February and commercial supplies targeted for H2 of next year (FY28).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin contracted by 214 bps YoY to 13.85% due to elevated and volatile raw material prices (scrap and pig iron).
- Cost pass-through to customers operates with a one-quarter lag.
- Other expenses increased broadly in line with sales growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current installed capacity stands at 72,000 metric tonnes per annum.
- An expansion of 650 tons/month (7,800 tons total) is nearing completion, expected to be ready by the end of November 2026 (taking capacity to 80,000 tonnes).
- Further greenfield expansion planned to reach 1 lakh metric tonnes by 2028.
- Capacity utilization is ~90% for green sand molding and ~50% for Lost Foam Casting (LFC).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt-to-equity ratio stands at 0.67.
- Future capital expenditures will be funded through a comfortable mix of debt and equity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Short-term margin volatility caused by fluctuations in key raw material prices (scrap and pig iron).
- Seasonality in the tractor industry, with Q3 typically seeing lower sales due to annual maintenance shutdowns and post-festive normalization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jimit Mehta (Mehta Investments) on export potential:** Management stated that the initial ~Rs 12 crore Italian order has the potential to scale to Rs 40–50 crores over the next 3 years.
- **Mrunal Shah (Individual Investor) on railways timeline:** Management clarified that RDSO trial components will be submitted in January/February, targeting supplies by H2 FY28.
- **Janisha (SK Investments) on capacity utilization:** Management noted green sand capacity utilization is at ~90% and LFC is at ~50%.
- **Jenil Patel (Aavya Advisors) on EBITDA margin drop:** Management explained that higher raw material costs in Q2 impacted margins due to the one-quarter lag in price pass-throughs.

### One-line takeaway
Pritika Auto Industries posted robust Q2 FY27 revenue and volume growth exceeding 30% and 22% respectively, though margins experienced near-term pressure from raw material inflation ahead of anticipated pass-throughs and capacity scaling.
