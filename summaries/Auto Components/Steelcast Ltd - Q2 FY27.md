# Steelcast Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue and volume growth supported by strong demand, alongside a further expansion of manufacturing capacity.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Raised revenue growth guidance to approximately 30% YoY for FY27 (from ~25% guided in Q1 FY27).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, driven by elevated commodity prices supporting mining, earthmoving, and construction demand; management expanded total capex plans further.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Added a discussion on deploying an electric arc furnace to increase piece weight capability up to 3.5 tons, resulting in an increased capex outlay of Rs 151 crores.
- Last quarter's promises: The renewable energy plants (2.4 MW hybrid and 1.4 MW solar) remain on track for commissioning by December 31, 2026.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 138.0 crore (up 29.4% YoY from Rs 106.7 crore)
- EBITDA (excluding other income): Rs 33.8 crore (up 11.8% YoY from Rs 30.2 crore)
- EBITDA margin: 24.5% (vs 28.3% in Q2 FY26)
- PAT: Rs 23.4 crore (up 0.6% YoY from Rs 23.2 crore)
- PAT margin: 16.9% (vs 21.8% in Q2 FY26)
- Sales volume: 4,370 tonnes (up ~28% YoY from 3,411 tonnes)
- Capacity utilization: 57% for Q2 FY27 (existing capacity of 29,000 tonnes per annum)
- Executable order book: Rs 145 crore (~3 months of sales)
- Capex: Rs 19 crore incurred in H1 FY27; estimated at ~Rs 60 crore for FY27

### Highlights
- Board approved an additional capacity of ~3,250 tonnes with an investment of Rs 31 crore, taking total planned greenfield capacity expansion to 11,750 tonnes and total investment to Rs 151 crore (up from the earlier 8,500 tonnes and Rs 120 crore).
- Total manufacturing capacity will increase from 29,000 tonnes to 40,750 tonnes, extending piece weight capabilities up to ~3.5 tons via an electric arc furnace.
- Out of 55 new parts under development, 32 new parts have been developed and 15 commercialized during FY27.
- Renewable energy portfolio of 13.3 MW includes 9.5 MW commissioned, with 2.4 MW hybrid and 1.4 MW solar plants targeted for commissioning by December 31, 2026, saving ~Rs 4.5 crore annually.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains/raises revenue growth guidance of approximately 30% YoY for FY27.
- Full year EBITDA margin expected in the broad range of 26% to 27%.
- Commissioning of the new foundry targeted around April 1, 2028.
- Existing capacity utilization expected to average around 60% during FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Earthmoving (51%), mining (21%), and construction (18%) together accounted for ~90% of H1 FY27 revenue.
- Export vs domestic revenue mix was 52% and 48% respectively in Q2 FY27 (57% and 43% in H1 FY27).
- Elevated commodity prices are driving investments and demand in mining, earthmoving, construction, and railroads.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin moderated 387 bps YoY to 24.5%, impacted by ~Rs 1 crore in product development costs, higher employee expenses for upcoming expansion manpower, and a timing mismatch on raw material pass-throughs.
- Input costs increased from the first week of September and were absorbed in Q2, with price revisions expected to reflect in Q4.
- Sales are entirely on an ex-works basis, so logistics and freight costs are handled directly by customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex outlay increased to Rs 151 crore for an 11,750 tonnes capacity addition, phased across FY27 and FY28.
- Orders worth ~Rs 35 crore placed for CNC machines with delivery between January 2027 and January 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a debt-free balance sheet for the third consecutive year.
- Free reserves stood at approximately Rs 120 crore, invested in bank fixed deposits, mutual funds, and secured bonds.
- H1 FY27 ROCE stood at 28.8% and ROE at 21.9%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Q3 margins expected to remain under pressure due to the combined effect of passing on earlier cost reductions and absorbing recent September cost spikes before Q4 price recoveries kick in.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rohit Taparia (Individual Investor) asked about revenue guidance revision.** Management clarified that revenue growth guidance is raised to 30% for FY27 compared to FY26.
- **Aniket Nikumb (ABN Capital) asked about the raw material timing impact.** Management quantified the September raw material cost increase at Rs 2 crore higher compared to Q1, impacting margins temporarily.
- **Srinath V. (Bellwether Capital) asked about changes in capex and machinery.** Management explained the addition of an electric arc furnace (6–6.5 ton capacity) enables heavier castings up to 3.5 tons and better steel refining, prompting the Rs 31 crore capex increase.
- **Saket Saurabh (Sagari Capital) asked about order book visibility vs physical purchase orders.** Management stated physical purchase orders provide 3 to 3.5 months of visibility, backed by a rolling 12-month customer forecast.
- **Manish Goyal (Thinqwise Wealth Managers) asked about GET and defense progress.** Management stated GET contributes ~1.5% of sales (targeted to reach 6% by FY29), while domestic defense is no longer a focus area.

### One-line takeaway
Steelcast reported strong Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 29.4% YoY and expanded its planned greenfield capacity outlay to Rs 151 crore, maintaining a debt-free balance sheet despite near-term margin pressures from input cost timing mismatches.
