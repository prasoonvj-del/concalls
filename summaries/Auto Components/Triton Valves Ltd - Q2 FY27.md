# Triton Valves Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth across segments, structural margin improvement via internal commodity hedge, and new program wins in TPMS, EV, and climate control, though inventory buildup and commodity volatility remain watch items.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (FY27 revenue growth target unchanged; added specific margin targets: standalone EBITDA to double digits, group EBITDA >Rs 15 cr/qtr, ROCE 14-15% by Q4, climate breakeven this fiscal)
- Tone and demand view: shifted from general optimism to quantified volume growth (auto 35%, metals 20-22%, EV 100%), export recovery confirmed, climate order book jump (Rs 2.5 cr → Rs 8-10 cr for Q3)
- New topics: inventory buildup details (commodity value + shipment delays), internal hedge explanation, aluminum automotive climate valves (Hanon, Maflow), defense/medical alloys, FTA timeline (Q4 FY27), Bosch forecast extension to 2031
- Old risks persisting: Chinese dumping in climate control, commodity volatility, shipment/logistics disruptions
- Promises delivered: merger tax shield realized (Rs 5.17 cr), TPMS LOIs converting (Bosch scaling 2-3x), capex commercialization on track (H2 Rs 10 cr vs FY27 guidance Rs 15 cr), EV engagement deepening

### Key numbers
- Group consolidated Q2 revenue: Rs 200 crore (volume growth ~20% YoY, remainder commodity-driven)
- Group Q2 EBITDA: ~Rs 14.5 crore (margin 7.2%)
- Group H1 revenue: Rs 380.6 crore; H1 EBITDA: ~Rs 27 crore
- Standalone (auto + climate) Q2 revenue: Rs 110.7 crore (up 52% YoY); EBITDA: Rs 9.5 crore (margin ~8.5%, up 260 bps YoY)
- Metals volume growth: 20-22% YoY; revenue up 77% YoY (commodity-driven)
- Climate control Q2 sales: Rs 2.5 crore; Q3 order book: Rs 8-10 crore; November order book >Rs 5 crore
- EV component growth: ~100% YoY
- Export share: ~5% of Q2 revenue
- Inventory increase: target reduction Rs 20-25 crore by end-December
- Debt serviceability: 2.36x
- Merger tax shield (one-off): Rs 5.17 crore boost to standalone PAT

### Highlights
- Structural internal hedge: metals business (Future Tech) offsets commodity lag in component businesses, driving steady 6-8 quarter EBITDA uptrend despite copper/FX volatility
- TPMS programs ramping: Bosch forecast extended to 2031 at 2-3x original volumes; LOIs from Sensata and Aumovio; global programs with higher margin profile (~10 pp above tubeless)
- Climate control inflection: Panasonic global approval secured; Q3 order book 3-4x Q2; targeting breakeven at Rs 5-6 crore/month; lobbying for MIP/QCO against Chinese dumping
- EV traction: working with River Mobility, Simple Energy, battery customers; large scale-ups expected from Q4 FY27
- Metals value-add: developing alloys for writing instruments, medical, defense; higher margins than commodity grades
- Automotive climate control: new aluminum valve programs with Hanon (Hyundai/Kia) and Maflow (VW); complete solution (body, cap, core)
- Export momentum: US recovering post-tariff uncertainty; Europe distributor development; FTA operationalization hoped by Q4 FY27

### Guidance & outlook
- Standalone EBITDA margin target: double digits (from ~8.5%)
- Group EBITDA target: sustain >Rs 15 crore/quarter
- ROCE target: 14-15% by end Q4 FY27 (up ~170 bps from March)
- Climate control breakeven expected in FY27 if order momentum continues
- TPMS large program launches: Q1 FY28
- Automotive climate control new programs: Q4 FY27
- Capex H2 FY27: ~Rs 10 crore (Rs 6-7 cr automotive, Rs 2-3 cr metals)
- Continued focus on product mix upgrade, cost optimization, capacity expansion

### Demand & industry commentary
- Automotive: strong across OEM, aftermarket, export; tubeless replacing tube, TPMS replacing tubeless; CAFE norms catalyzing TPMS adoption (penetration ~10% → 50-70% in 2-3 years)
- EV: demand outstripping supply; supply chains stable; large players well-funded
- Climate control: seasonal Q2 low; Chinese dumping indiscriminate; government engagement for level playing field
- Metals: value-added alloy inquiries rising; volume growth 20-22%
- Exports: China+1 mindset intact; tariff unpredictability easing; buyers moving from wait-and-watch to ordering

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Key commodities: copper, zinc, butyl rubber (crude derivative)
- Indexation: quarterly, 3-month average lag
- Material cost (standalone): improved from 72% to 63.2% YoY (partly due to scrap netting change)
- Internal hedge: metals business gains offset component business commodity lag
- Inventory held at favorable contracted rates; will support Q3 margins if copper falls
- Pricing locked until Dec-end; copper price drops from Oct/Nov pass through only from Jan

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H2 FY27 capex: ~Rs 10 crore (automotive Rs 6-7 cr, metals Rs 2-3 cr)
- New alloys development for defense, medical, writing instruments
- Automotive climate control: aluminum valve capacity for Hanon/Maflow programs
- TPMS/EV capacity: running high (Q1: >85-90%); expansion via capex
- Metals capacity utilization: Q1 65-70%; scaling for value-added alloys

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings increased primarily due to commodity funding (copper ~Rs 720 → Rs 1,300-1,400/kg)
- Working capital cycle deteriorated due to inventory; target correction in Q3/Q4
- Cash conversion cycle: inventory payable receivable deterioration; expect normalization by Dec
- Net worth: not disclosed for Q2 (Q1: Rs 138 crore)

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inventory buildup (commodity value + Q1 shipment delays) - target Dec normalization
- Commodity price volatility (copper, zinc, butyl rubber)
- Chinese dumping in climate control segment persisting
- Customer delivery delays end-Q2 shifting ~Rs 8-10 crore revenue to Q3
- Tariff uncertainty on exports (though improving)
- Dependence on government policy (MIP/QCO) for climate control viability
