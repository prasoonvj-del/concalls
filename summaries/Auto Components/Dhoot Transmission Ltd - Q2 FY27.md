# Dhoot Transmission Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by 60% YoY revenue growth, a 108% surge in EV revenues, and an upward revision of annual growth guidance to >40%.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised annual growth guidance from 25%-30% to >40%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "strong" to "surprised positively" by industry growth; EV demand is now described as "bullish" with customers discussing 2x volumes in the next 2-3 years.
- **New topics or risks:** Management detailed a Rs 9 crore one-off ESOP expense; noted a "hard cycle of commodities" in September; announced plans for 5 new plants in the next 12 months.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The company successfully achieved a net cash position of Rs 900 crore, fulfilling the promise to repay IPO-related debt.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue Growth:** 60% YoY.
- **Total Revenue:** Rs 1,800 crore.
- **Wiring Harness Revenue Growth:** 44.6% YoY.
- **Non-Wiring Harness Revenue Growth:** 124% YoY.
- **EV Revenue Growth:** 108% YoY (31% of total sales).
- **Multilink Contribution:** Rs 160 crore.
- **Organic Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 15.2%.
- **Reported EBITDA Margin:** 14.3% (includes 0.5% ESOP impact).
- **Pre-exceptional PAT:** Rs 162 crore (+30% YoY).
- **Net Cash/Bank/FD Balance:** Rs 900 crore.

### Highlights
- Fully repaid IPO-related debt; now holds a net cash position of Rs 900 crore.
- EV revenue contribution increased to 31% of total sales (vs 27% in Q1 FY27).
- Multilink integration is ongoing; new facility in Hosur is focusing on automation to improve margins.
- *Record check:* The company has now fully repaid IPO debt, fulfilling the promise made in Q1.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth Guidance:** Revised from 30% to >40% for FY27 (Rahul Dhoot).
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Maintained at 15%-16% (Rahul Dhoot).
- **Capex Guidance:** Increased to Rs 750 crore for the year (Rahul Dhoot).
- **Export Outlook:** Expect 10%-15% annual growth for the next 3 years (Rahul Dhoot).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Two-wheeler industry grew 16% in the last quarter.
- Domestic EV industry grew >100% YoY; ICE grew >10% YoY.
- Two-wheeler exports grew ~28% YoY.
- Customers are "bullish" on EV, with some discussing 2x volumes in the coming 2-3 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Reported EBITDA margin of 14.3% was impacted by 0.5% ESOP cost and Multilink consolidation.
- Copper price inflation impact on top line: 4%-5% (Nitin Kalani).
- Other expenses increased due to outsourcing/contractor charges for incremental volumes.
- ESOP cost was Rs 9 crore this quarter; expected to normalize to Rs 3-4 crore per quarter (Nitin Kalani).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex guidance increased to Rs 750 crore.
- Added 2 battery manufacturing plants (South and West) in H1; production commenced.
- New wiring harness plant at Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar is functional and running at full capacity.
- Planning 5 new plants in the next 12 months (Pune, Chennai, Jhajjar).
- Electronics/battery component capex for FY27: Rs 150-200 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash/bank/FD balance: Rs 900 crore.
- Receivables expected to settle around 45-55 days long-term (Nitin Kalani).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical crisis could impact EV surge (Rahul Dhoot).
- "Hard cycle of commodities" noted in September.
- Multilink margins currently 10-11%; integration to automation will take 4-5 months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nitin Arora (Axis):** Asked about Capex upsize. Management: Driven by unexpected 100% EV growth and the need to prepare for customer volume requirements.
- **Siddharth Bera (Nomura):** Asked about Multilink ramp-up. Management: New Hosur facility with automation will align margins by April 30.
- **Vijay Pandey:** Asked about EV powertrain changes (hub vs mid-motor). Management: No impact on current product supply; wiring harness content might increase slightly.
- **Rishi Vora (Kotak):** Asked about ESOP cost. Management: Rs 9 crore one-off this quarter; will normalize to Rs 3-4 crore.
- **Preet Jain:** Asked about BMS/EV content. Management: Clarified they do assembly, not BMS; EV wiring harness content is similar to ICE, but EV uses two harnesses (high/low voltage).

### One-line takeaway
Dhoot Transmission delivered a strong Q2 with 60% YoY revenue growth and a net cash position of Rs 900 crore, prompting an upward revision in annual growth guidance to >40%.
