# Dhoot Transmission Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by 60% YoY revenue growth, 108% surge in EV segment, and an upward revision of FY27 growth guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised (Full-year growth guidance revised to 40%-45% from 25%-30%; annual Capex guidance increased to ~Rs. 750 crore from unspecified).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted more bullish as domestic EV revenues surged 108% YoY (vs 79% YoY in Q1) and overall 2W demand remained strong.
- **New topics or risks:** ESOP cost impact (Rs. 9 crore / 50 bps) and one-off IPO expenses impacting reported PAT; integration timeline and lower margins (10%-11%) at Multilink flagged. Q1's risk regarding H2 base effects was not mentioned.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Turning cash surplus post-IPO debt repayment delivered (net cash, bank, and FD balance reached Rs. 900 crore after fully clearing IPO-allocated debt).

### Key numbers
- **Overall Revenue Growth:** 60% YoY (Total Revenue ~Rs. 1,800 crore).
- **Wiring Harness Revenue Growth:** 44.6% YoY.
- **Non-Wiring Harness Revenue Growth:** 124% YoY.
- **Multilink Revenue Contribution:** ~Rs. 160 crore.
- **EV Segment Revenue:** Rs. 589 crore (up 108% YoY), accounting for 31% of total sales (vs 27% in Q1 FY27 and 24% in FY26).
- **Organic Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 15.2%.
- **Reported EBITDA Margin:** 14.3% (impacted by 0.5% ESOP cost and Multilink consolidation).
- **Pre-exceptional PAT:** Rs. 162 crore (up 30% YoY).
- **Net Cash & FD Balance:** Rs. 900 crore.

### Highlights
- Revenue expanded 60% YoY, driven by 108% growth in EV components and Rs. 160 crore inorganic contribution from Multilink.
- Fully repaid debt earmarked for clearance via IPO proceeds, reaching Rs. 900 crore in net cash, bank, and FD balances.
- Operationalized two new EV battery manufacturing plants (one in South India, one in West India) and a wiring harness plant at Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar running at full capacity.
- Multilink added Hero MotoCorp as a new customer for non-wiring harness products.
- *Pattern continuation:* Management is expanding its manufacturing footprint further, committing to 5 new plants over the next 12 months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Revised full-year revenue growth guidance upward from 30% to 40%-45% (Rahul Dhoot).
- Reaffirmed full-year EBITDA margin guidance at 15%-16% (Rahul Dhoot).
- Annual Capex guidance raised to ~Rs. 750 crore for FY27 to support EV and electronics expansion (Rahul Dhoot).
- Export business guided to grow at 10%-15% annually over the next three years (Rahul Dhoot).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Two-wheeler industry volumes grew 16% YoY in Q2, with domestic EV growing >100%, ICE >10%, and 2W exports growing 28% YoY.
- EV customers are targeting a 2x volume expansion over the next 2-3 years, with top customers planning global EV rollouts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Copper price inflation contributed 4%-5% to top-line growth; pricing pass-through to customers continues on a quarterly lag.
- ESOP cost was Rs. 9 crore in Q2 (50 bps margin hit) and is expected to normalize to Rs. 3 crore - Rs. 4 crore per quarter starting Q3.
- Other expenses increased due to higher piece-rate contractor charges and conversion costs for outsourced component manufacturing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Adding 5 new plants over the next 12 months: 2 IPO-funded plants, leased plants in Pune and Chennai, and a rented facility in Jhajjar.
- ~Rs. 150 crore - Rs. 200 crore earmarked for electronics and battery pack assembly capacities.
- Multilink Hosur facility automation and line shifting will begin February 1, 2027, and complete by April 30, 2027.
- Average capacity utilization across plants stands at 75% (peak operational capacity), rising to 100% during festive peak months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash, bank, and FD balances stood at Rs. 900 crore post full IPO debt repayment.
- Receivables spiked temporarily due to strong festive exit run-rate; guided to normalize to 45-55 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Multilink EBITDA margins currently lag at 10%-11%, dragging reported margins down until automation completes in April 2027.
- High raw material/commodity price volatility in September put temporary pressure on gross margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nitin Arora (Axis MF) on Capex increase:** Management explained Capex was upsized to ~Rs. 750 crore because EV demand grew 100% YoY versus the expected 20%-25%, pushing plants to peak capacity.
- **Siddharth Bera on battery Capex & Multilink timelines:** Management allocated Rs. 150 crore - Rs. 200 crore for battery/electronics Capex in FY27, confirming Hosur automation for Multilink will complete by April 30.
- **Vijay Pandey on Hero MotoCorp orders:** Management clarified the Hero MotoCorp business addition is for non-wiring harness Multilink products, not wiring harnesses.
- **Jyoti Singh on copper inflation & margins:** Management confirmed copper cost increases are passed on quarterly with a lag and reiterated EBITDA margin guidance of 15%-16%.
- **Preet Jain on BMS & EV content multiplier:** Management clarified Dhoot does battery assembly (cells provided by OEMs) rather than BMS manufacturing, and EV wiring harness BOM cost is higher due to dual (high/low voltage) harnesses.

### One-line takeaway
Dhoot Transmission delivered 60% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 led by surging EV demand, turning net cash with Rs. 900 crore in reserves while raising full-year growth guidance to 40%-45%.
