# Auto Components - Seating, lighting & interiors: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is experiencing a period of robust growth, with almost all players reporting record or near-record quarterly revenues. Managements are largely optimistic about domestic demand, driven by strong OEM production and the structural shift toward LED and EV components. However, there is a clear divergence regarding global markets, with several players flagging softness in the U.S. and Europe. The sector is characterized by high capex intensity as companies expand capacity to meet order books, alongside a strategic focus on non-auto verticals (electronics, aerospace) to diversify revenue streams.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Domestic Consensus:** There is broad agreement that domestic automotive demand remains healthy. **Fiem Industries** and **Lumax Industries** highlighted strong 2W and PV production volumes. **Sandhar Technologies** noted it outperformed industry growth (27% vs 21%).
*   **Global Divergence:** While domestic demand is strong, global markets are a point of concern. **Mayur Uniquoters** reported a slow U.S. automotive market, leading to downward revisions in OEM projections. **SAMIL** noted global light vehicle production fell 2.3% YoY, with specific weakness in China, North America, and Europe. **Suprajit Engineering** remains upbeat on the Americas but flagged Germany/Europe as a concern.
*   **Segment Divergence:** **SJS Enterprises** noted a divergence in the 2W segment, with growth heavily favoring scooters (30.1% YoY) over motorcycles (9.8% YoY), impacting their specific product mix.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Cost Pressures:** A recurring theme across the sector is the impact of the West Asia conflict on freight, energy, and commodity prices (flagged by **Fiem, Lumax, Mayur, PPAP, Suprajit**).
*   **Pass-through Lags:** Companies are experiencing a time lag in passing on raw material cost increases to OEMs. **PPAP** noted that 68% of margin reduction has been recovered, with the balance taking 6 months. **Sandhar** and **Suprajit** also cited difficulties in quick cost pass-throughs, particularly for electronic components.
*   **Margin Resilience:** Despite these pressures, many companies are maintaining or improving margins through operating leverage and product mix optimization. **SJS** achieved a 31% EBITDA margin by exiting low-margin SKUs. **Suprajit** saw successful margin recovery in its ICM and PLE divisions.

## Capex & capacity
*   **High Capex Intensity:** The sector is in a major expansion phase. **Fiem** raised its FY27 capex guidance to Rs 150 crore. **Lumax** maintained its Rs 200-250 crore guidance. **SAMIL** is investing heavily, with Rs 6,000 crore earmarked for FY27. **NDR Auto** and **Suprajit** are both undertaking greenfield/brownfield expansions.
*   **Strategic Focus:** Capex is increasingly directed toward non-auto and high-tech segments. **SAMIL** is investing ~60% of its capex in non-automotive businesses. **SJS** is investing in a new automotive display facility.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Shifts:** **Lumax** revised its full-year revenue growth guidance upward from "20% plus" to "25% plus." **SJS** recalibrated its revenue growth outlook to 1.2x–1.5x of industry growth (from 1.5x–2x) due to macro uncertainties, but simultaneously raised its EBITDA margin guidance to 29%–30% (from 27%–28%).
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Suprajit** shifted its tone from "muted" global/domestic concerns to a "solid quarter" with a "clear road ahead for H2."
*   **Operational Milestones:** **Belrise** maintained its guidance but noted that cost pressures have subsided as anticipated. **PPAP** confirmed that the Chennai plant expansion is progressing to be fully operational in Q3 FY27.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Margin Recovery:** Investors should watch for the completion of price pass-through negotiations at **PPAP** and **Sandhar**, which are expected to improve margins in H2.
*   **Integration Progress:** The successful integration of new acquisitions at **SAMIL** (Autoelectric, Yutaka Giken) and **NDR Auto** (DYA JV) will be critical for margin expansion.
*   **Facility Stabilization:** The operationalization of new plants at **SJS** (Bengaluru expansion) and **Sandhar** (Sundaram-Clayton consolidation) will be key to reducing current unabsorbed costs.
