# Fiem Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management stated that Fiem had a strong start to FY27 supported by record first-quarter 2-wheeler industry volumes, a strong order book, and a healthy opportunity pipeline.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR769.9 crores (up 18.62% YoY from INR649.07 crores)
- EBITDA: INR104.06 crores (compared to INR87.36 crores YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 13.5% (compared to 13.46% YoY)
- PAT: INR65.19 crores (up 16.31% YoY from INR56.05 crores)
- Capex in Q1: INR41.15 crores
- LED lighting share in total automotive lighting: 63%
- 4-wheeler revenue contribution: around 2.5%
- Industry 2-wheeler volume in Q1: 7.25 million units (highest ever for a first quarter, ~22.8% growth at production level)
- EV 2-wheeler volume share: >9% in Q1 (up from ~6% a year ago)

### Highlights
- TVS iQube crossed 1 million cumulative sales; second model of Norton Manx launched for export to U.K.
- Commenced supplies for Ather's new model (Konarc), River's RX02 model, and Royal Enfield EV (Flying Flea model).
- Hero's new X440 model exported to the U.S. features Fiem front and rear lighting.
- From next quarter, Fiem will supply all Vida lamps for Hero's new Andhra Pradesh plant from the Hosur facility.
- Customer capacity expansions announced: TVS expanding 2-wheeler capacity from 6.8 million to 8.3 million units by year-end; Hero tripling Vida's capacity from 15,000 to 45,000 units/month.
- Fiem is expanding its Hosur footprint at both Kelamangalam and Thally Road plants.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for full-year revenue growth of 15% to 20%.
- Management guided for full-year EBITDA margin of around 14%.
- Full-year capex is targeted at around INR100 crores.
- 4-wheeler revenue contribution expected to remain around similar levels (~2.5%) this year, with significant increases expected from next year onwards (FY28) due to longer conversion/development cycles.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian 2-wheeler industry delivered a record Q1 (7.25 million units), supported by GST rationalization, retail financing, rural demand, and faster growth in exports and EVs.
- EV adoption is viewed as a structural shift favoring Fiem due to higher LED-intensive lighting content.
- Festive season is starting on a strong note, with increasing shifts towards premium and electric vehicles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs increased due to adverse currency movement (weaker rupee) and employee cost revisions (minimum wage hike in Haryana by ~35%, plus annual increments).
- Management stated raw material cost increases will be passed on to customers with a lag of a couple of quarters, helping average out margins to ~14% for the full year.
- Employee cost run rate is expected to remain in the same range for the next 3 quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q1 capex stood at INR41.15 crores; full-year capex guided at ~INR100 crores.
- Major portion of capex is directed towards South India (Hosur) to cater to EV capacity requirements for TVS and other OEMs, with some part at Tapukara.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at roughly around INR280 crores with effectively no debt.
- Capital allocation priorities include funding normal capex, potential 4-wheeler or electronics capex, and evaluating organic/inorganic opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts, trade/tariff tensions, global supply chain disturbances, increased input costs, and weaker rupee pressures.
- 4-wheeler customer conversion cycles and validation processes are taking longer than expected, delaying meaningful revenue scaling to FY28.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Garvit Goyal (Serene Alpha) on 4-wheeler delay:** Management clarified that initial checklists, processes, and a 3-year development cycle for 4-wheelers are taking longer than expected, pushing full revenue realization from FY27 to FY28.
- **Anubhav Mukherjee (Monarch Networth) on Mercedes prototyping:** Management stated sample testing and vehicle-level validation take 1.5 to 2 years, after which an RFQ/RFI for mass production will be issued.
- **Arun Agarwal (Kotak Securities) on LED penetration stagnation:** Management explained that while old existing models continue with halogen, new models are moving towards LED, targeting ~70% LED mix over the next 24 to 30 months.
- **Viraj Kacharia (Private Investor) on new tech products:** Management noted that hands-off detection POCs and light control modules (LCM) are being developed and presented to customers, supported by an in-house EMC/EMI lab.
- **Jinesh Chopra on cash utilization vs debt:** Management stated internal accruals are strong, but debt could be raised if required for large organic/inorganic or electronic-related activities.

### One-line takeaway
Fiem Industries delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 18.62% revenue growth and stable 13.5% margins, driven by record 2-wheeler industry volumes and rising EV/LED penetration, while 4-wheeler revenue scale-up has been pushed to FY28.
