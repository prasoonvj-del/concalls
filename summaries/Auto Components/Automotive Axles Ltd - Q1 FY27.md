# Automotive Axles Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported one of the highest EBITDA percentages and quarterly EPS in the past four years, supported by favorable product mix and robust market performance.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 5,168 million
- Other income: Rs. 103 million
- Cost of raw materials (metal cost): ~65% of revenue
- Other expenses: Rs. 813 million
- EBITDA: Rs. 702 million (13.6% margin)
- EBIT margin: ~12%
- Profit for the period (PAT): Rs. 455 million (8.7% margin; net of OCI: Rs. 452 million)
- EPS: Rs. 30
- Exports: ~13% of overall revenue for the quarter
- Industry volume forecast: Revised to a 5%-10% dip YoY (or best case, matching last year's 480,000 units), compared to an initial projection of 15%-20% lower.

### Highlights
- Achieved one of the highest EBITDA percentages and quarterly EPS in the last four years.
- Electrification of a furnace was completed, helping manage production despite lower LPG availability.
- Launched a new 160 tandem axle currently in pilot batch / production mode with a large customer.
- Defense segment contributes ~5%-10% of revenue depending on tenders.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 industry volumes to be down 5%-10% YoY, or flat in the best-case scenario.
- Q4 is expected to be a peak quarter.
- Long-term EBITDA/PAT margin target range is 7.5%-8.5% for quarters, with improvements expected towards 2030 due to scale and capacity additions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 industry volume stood at nearly 110,000 vehicles in the M&HCV segment; GDP grew ~7%.
- Demand is currently driven by the replacement cycle (vehicles sold in 2017–2018 completing 7–9 years of service).
- Shift towards higher horsepower (+300 hp) in heavy-duty applications and 4x2 tractor-trailers.
- Mining sector is not being actively pursued due to low volumes and niche requirements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material/metal costs are passed through back-to-back with customers.
- Employee benefit expenses decreased sequentially due to lower workforce deployment and moderation of long-term settlement impacts taken in Q4.
- Absorbed cost headwinds from post-war LPG increases, tooling, and consumables, and is negotiating offset mechanisms with customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Implementing Phase-1 and Phase-1(a) of capex (~Rs. 120 crore program mentioned by analysts); ~40% of investments already completed with initial production started.
- Aiming for a 25%-30% capacity improvement depending on product mix to handle peak demand and export opportunities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong return on assets (RoA) and return on capital employed (RoCE) focus maintained.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions and monsoon vagaries/delayed monsoons remain key headwinds.
- Sea freight rates are random and challenging, though exports are largely Ex-Works.
- Technological progression in aftermarket parts may extend replacement cycles from 1-1.5 years to 3-6 years over the long term.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sukrit Patel (Eyesight Fintrade) on execution priorities and upcoming regulations:** Management stated top priorities are readiness of capacity investments and increasing automation. For regulations, they see minimal impact from upcoming BS-VII, AEBS, and Endurance Braking over 4 years, but are redesigning products to meet stricter pass-by noise norms.
2. **Milan Jain on capex breakup and export allocation:** Management explained capex is for upgrading/replacing lines with fully automated equipment for a 25%-30% capacity boost. On American Axle, management stated they are competitors focusing on existing customers and CDBS global entities, declining to comment on competitor strategies.
3. **Saket Kapoor on margin expansion drivers and volume outlook:** Management clarified margins benefited from favorable product mix and one-offs. Industry volume degrowth expectation was revised from 15%-20% down to 5%-10%.
4. **Samarth on market share and competition:** Management noted product mix variations can cause quarterly optical dips, but core share of business remains steady and single-source status with key OEMs like Ashok Leyland is intact.
5. **Shikha Mehta on bus axle status and exports:** Management stated bus axle low-floor regulation confusions are resolved, but they are reworking designs to integrate pass-by noise compliance. Exports increased slightly to 13% for the quarter against the usual 8%-12% range.

### One-line takeaway
Automotive Axles delivered a robust Q1 FY27 with peak-level EBITDA margins of 13.6% and a record EPS of Rs. 30, driven by favorable product mix and steady replacement demand, even as management cautiously navigates a projected 5%-10% industry volume dip for the year.
