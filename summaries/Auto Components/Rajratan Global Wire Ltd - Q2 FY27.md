# Rajratan Global Wire Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights strong volume growth, successful capacity expansion, and market share consolidation despite inflationary cost pressures.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Volume Target (FY27):** 160,000 tonnes.
*   **Thailand Exports (H1):** 8,500 tonnes; (Q2): 5,200 tonnes.
*   **India Export Target (FY27):** 14,000–15,000 tonnes.
*   **Chennai Plant Production Target (FY27):** 37,000–38,000 tonnes.
*   **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.6.
*   **Thailand Expansion Capex:** ~Rs 100 crore.
*   **Steel Cord Project Capex:** ~Rs 80 crore (standalone).

### Highlights
*   Earnings growth outpaced revenue growth due to operational improvements and higher capacity utilization.
*   Chennai plant expansion to 60,000 tonnes is on track.
*   Announced 50,000-tonne capacity expansion in Thailand; expected to be operational within 1 year.
*   Generated positive operating cash flow in both standalone and consolidated businesses.
*   Management identified "green bead wire" (scrap-based) as a key differentiator for European markets.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects 15–20% YoY volume growth for the next 10–15 quarters.
*   Thailand expansion is projected to reach full utilization within 2 years of commissioning.
*   Steel cord project is in the trial/sampling phase; expected to be fully operational next year.
*   Management stated current EBITDA margins (12.7–13%) are "rock bottom" and expects improvement from here.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Indian tire market is growing at 8–10%.
*   "China Plus One" strategy is driving export demand for the company’s products.
*   Thailand capacity is currently running at near 100% utilization.
*   Premium customers currently account for 30–32% of sales; management targets 40%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins were pressured by inflation in freight, fuel, energy, and consumables.
*   Steel prices are rising; management plans to pass on price increases in remaining counters starting December.
*   Thailand operations benefit from scrap-based raw material sourcing, providing a competitive edge in Europe (CBAM compliance).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Chennai:** Currently at 40,000 tonnes rated capacity; expanding to 60,000 tonnes.
*   **Thailand:** New 50,000-tonne brownfield expansion planned (Rs 100 crore).
*   **Steel Cord (Pithampur):** Rs 80 crore investment; trials ongoing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Borrowings increased to support 27% revenue growth and higher working capital needs.
*   Working capital cycle: Exports (Europe) ~70–80 days; Local ~40 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical turmoil affecting global markets.
*   Volatility in freight and raw material costs.
*   Competitive intensity from players like Tata Steel and Bansal Wire.
*   US Section 232 tariffs (50% duty) on steel products.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the timeline for Thailand expansion. **Management:** Clarified it will take 1 year to build and 2 years to fully utilize the new capacity.
*   **Analyst:** Questioned why operating leverage isn't boosting EBITDA margins. **Management:** Defended current margins as "rock bottom" given the inflationary environment, noting they have maintained margins despite significant cost increases.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the "green bead wire" competitive advantage. **Management:** Explained that Thailand operations use 92–95% scrap-based raw material, which is not available in India, making them a preferred supplier for European green tire initiatives.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the steel cord project revenue. **Management:** Clarified they are not counting any steel cord revenue in current projections as it is still in the trial/sampling phase.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about export growth drivers. **Management:** Attributed growth to quality, long-term relationships with global customers, and the "China Plus One" trend.

### One-line takeaway
Management is focused on aggressive volume growth and capacity expansion, maintaining that current margin levels are the floor and expecting future improvement from Thailand and operational efficiencies.
