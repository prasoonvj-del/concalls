# MM Forgings Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported considerable improvement in markets, strong momentum from domestic and export markets, and all cells running to capability.

### Key numbers
- **Total Net Sales:** Rs 427 crore (vs Rs 369 crore YoY), 16% YoY growth.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 82 crore (Rs 75 crore net of other income); EBITDA margin at 18% excluding other income (vs 19% previously).
- **EBITDA Growth:** 16% YoY.
- **PBT Growth:** 30% YoY (excluding land sale gain).
- **Profit on Land Sale (Oragadam, Chennai):** Rs 64 crore gross, Rs 58 crore net of taxes (unrealized/one-time gain, not factored into EBITDA).
- **Sales Breakup by Geography:** India 63.5% (vs 61% YoY), U.S. 18%, Europe 14%, South America 4%, Others 1%.
- **Sales Breakup by Segment:** Commercial vehicles (CV) 71%, Agri & off-highway 14%, PAT 14%, Others 1%.
- **Machining vs Forged Mix:** 67% machined, 33% directly forged.
- **Heavy Holdings:** 42% of sales (vs 43% previous year-end).
- **Sales per Ton:** Rs 2.02 lakh (vs Rs 1.93 lakh in previous reference period).
- **Sales Volume:** 20,200 tonnes in Q1 FY27 (vs 78,000 tonnes total sales in FY26).
- **Gross Debt:** Approximately Rs 750 crore.
- **Automation Investment:** Rs 7.5 crore to Rs 10 crore so far; expected to reach Rs 30 crore to Rs 40-50 crore by the end of the fiscal year.
- **Machining Capex History:** Total ~Rs 1,100 crore, of which ~Rs 1,000 crore invested in the last 10 years, and Rs 625 crore in the last 5 years.

### Highlights
- All manufacturing and machining cells operated to the fullest of their capability from June onwards, recovering from initial labor shortages in April and May.
- Successfully sold land in the Oragadam area of Chennai, yielding a net profit of Rs 58 crore post-taxes.
- Abhinava Rizel secured its initial business and is in the initial phases of Start of Production (SOP) and ramp-up.
- Implementing AI tools to analyze data, optimize inventory, reduce WIP, and identify part numbers ready for dispatch.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Management expects ~18% growth for FY27, targeting a turnover of Rs 1,800 crore to Rs 1,900 crore (up from Rs 1,600 crore in FY26).
- **Volume Guidance:** Expects Q2 and beyond volumes to hover around 23,000 to 25,000 tonnes per quarter, aiming to cross 90,000 tonnes in FY27, with a run rate targeting 1 lakh tonnes and potentially challenging 1,10,000 tonnes. Internal target is to hit 27,000 and 30,000 tonnes per quarter.
- **Capex Guidance:** Planned capex of ~Rs 150 crore for FY27 (including Rs 30 crore to Rs 50 crore for replacement/debottlenecking). Repayment of ~Rs 170 crore expected, offset by equivalent new loan drawdowns.
- **Debt Guidance:** Gross debt to be capped at approximately Rs 750 crore to Rs 800 crore.
- **Machining Mix Guidance:** Expected to hold in the 65% to 68% range.
- **EBITDA Margin Goal:** Management stated a long-term goal to squeeze out an extra 2% to 3% to reach a "20-plus" margin.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand momentum from both domestic and export markets, particularly the U.S. (Class 8 truck CV market running strongly).
- CV, tractor, and passenger car markets in India running "pretty hot".
- Hyperscaler demand is filtering into the domestic forging and component market.
- European market remains stable with no business lost, fluctuating with customer demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins improved sequentially due to higher overall realization and a better machining mix.
- Employee costs increased steeply in Q1 due to annual increments being rolled out right from April 1st (unlike the previous year) and additions to headcount.
- Power and fuel costs rose in Q1 due to West Asian conflicts impacting freight and fuel; freight expenses jumped by ~Rs 4 crore due to routing around the Strait of Hormuz.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- A 4,000-tonne press entered production in June; a 16,500-tonne press is scheduled to start production in Q4 FY27.
- Growth capex aims to increase capabilities across a wide spectrum of machined products and heavy forging presses.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stands at ~Rs 750 crore and will be maintained around current levels.
- Proceeds from the land sale will be utilized to reduce working capital, capex borrowings, and capital borrowings.
- Working capital intensity is a focus area for reduction; management is deploying AI to dissolve stuck inventory and minimize Work-In-Progress (WIP).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Labor availability shortage across the country hampered April and May operations (though resolved by June via local and migrant hires).
- Geopolitical issues (West Asian conflict affecting the Strait of Hormuz) drove up freight and fuel expenses.
- Skill availability for forging is expected to remain difficult to man over the next few years.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mumuksh (Anand Rathi):** Asked about full-year growth and the jump in machining mix. *Management answered that 18% growth (Rs 1,800–1,900 crore) is expected, driven by past investments in machining, which will stay at 65–68%.*
2. **Naveen Vijay:** Asked about hyperscaler crankshaft orders and power/fuel cost increases. *Management confirmed strong hyperscaler demand and attributed fuel cost spikes to the West Asian conflict impacting freight.*
3. **Manas Jain (Sanjay Jain Family Office):** Asked about entering tractor/axle assembly and the target for the Rs 400 crore growth capex. *Management stated they are not entering assembly as it is margin-dilutive, and the capex will focus on steel forgings and metalworking/machining spaces, including non-auto industrial.*
4. **Subrata Sarkar (Mountain Infra Finance):** Asked about volume trajectory and capacity utilization. *Management guided for 90,000+ tonnes in FY27 with a run rate crossing 1 lakh tonnes exiting the year.*
5. **Garvit Goyal (Serene Alpha Analytics):** Asked about working capital intensity and inventory. *Management highlighted the formation of a rapid action force using AI tools to slash WIP inventory and accelerate ready-for-dispatch conversion.*

### One-line takeaway
MM Forgings reported a strong Q1 FY27 with 16% revenue growth to Rs 427 crore and 18% EBITDA margins, backed by robust domestic and U.S. CV demand, an increasing share of value-added machining (67%), and a clear volume trajectory towards 1 lakh+ tonnes.
