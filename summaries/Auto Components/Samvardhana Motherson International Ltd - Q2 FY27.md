# Samvardhana Motherson International Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by record quarterly revenue growth of 27% YoY, robust order book expansion to $106 billion, and steady balance sheet metrics despite global market headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 38,280 crore, up 27% YoY and up 9% QoQ. H1 FY27 revenue stood at Rs 73,500+ crore (up 22% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 3,353 crore, up 23% YoY. H1 FY27 EBITDA reached Rs 6,457 crore (up 25% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.8% (contracted ~20 bps YoY from 9.0% due to acquisitions, but expanded 20 bps YoY in H1 FY27).
- **Normalized PAT:** Rs 1,175 crore, up 37% YoY. H1 FY27 normalized PAT reached Rs 2,207 crore (up 45% YoY).
- **Booked Business:** Reached an all-time high of USD 106 billion (INR 10 lakh crore), up from USD 96 billion in FY26 and USD 59 billion in FY23 (13% CAGR).
- **Leverage Ratio:** 0.8x (well within internal ceiling of 1.5x and financial policy limit of 2.5x).
- **Net Debt:** Effective net debt at Rs 11,292 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up from Rs 9,757 crore at June 30, 2026).
- **Capex:** Rs 1,971 crore in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 total capex stood at Rs 3,585 crore.

### Highlights
- Consolidated two key acquisitions in Q2 FY27: Autoelectric (wiring harness, consolidated for 3 months) and Yutaka Giken (emerging businesses, consolidated for 2 months), expected to together add ~$2 billion in annual revenue.
- Autoelectric opens up global passenger vehicle wiring harness markets (supplying German OEMs like BMW, Daimler, Porsche, Audi), a space SAMIL previously lacked scale in outside India.
- Wiring harness division grew ~68% YoY, with ~1/3rd coming from Autoelectric acquisition and the rest from North American commercial vehicle surge and Indian growth.
- Non-automotive segment traction: Aerospace grew >20% YoY; announced acquisition of Rotary Connectors; consumer electronics gained revenue momentum with GF3 trial runs commenced.
- Operationalized one PCB greenfield plant for backward integration into lighting and vision systems; announced 5 additional facility expansions (17 facilities in development, 10 to open in FY27).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained full-year FY27 capex guidance at Rs 6,000 crore (±10%), with ~60% earmarked for non-automotive businesses.
- Commercial vehicle production positive momentum expected by management to continue in H2 FY27.
- Management expects acquired businesses (Autoelectric, Yutaka Giken) to improve profitability to group levels as integration operational efficiencies kick in over time.
- Consumer electronics GF3 plant full commercial production expected to begin in Q4 FY27, reaching full ramp-up within two subsequent quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global automotive backdrop remains weak: global light vehicle production fell 2.3% YoY in Q2 (China -5.5%, North America -3.8%, Europe -0.6%), while India grew +15%.
- Commercial vehicle production grew 7.6% globally (North America +43.6%, India +18.9%).
- European OEMs facing platform transition uncertainties between EV/hybrid models and competition from Chinese OEMs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost inflation pressures persist in copper, aluminium, and polymers. Net debt rose partly due to higher working capital required from elevated copper prices.
- Commodity cost pass-throughs with customers operate with a 3- to 6-month lag (mostly 3 months).
- Group-wide Vision 2030 automation initiative has a pipeline of ~940 projects (out of 1,000+ target across physical, digital, and AI-led AGVs/cobots/robots).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total investment in consumer electronics greenfield footprint is approximately Rs 7,500 crore (covering 4 plants, expanded for backward integration and additional product lines).
- All 17 ongoing expansion facilities are situated in emerging markets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity position stands at Rs 1,747 crore.
- Effective net debt rose to Rs 11,292 crore due to higher copper working capital, capex, and lease liabilities consolidated from Autoelectric and Yutaka Giken.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued volatility and cost pressures in underlying raw materials (copper, aluminium, polymers).
- Subdued global passenger vehicle demand and volume contractions in European auto production.
- Lag in passing on commodity inflation to OEMs creates short-term margin friction.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Nomura):** What are the annual revenue and margin profiles for new acquisitions Autoelectric and Yutaka Giken?  
  **A (Management):** Combined annual revenue expectation is ~$2 billion in their first full year. Current profitability is slightly lighter than group average, but expected to expand via synergies.
- **Q (J.P. Morgan):** Color on the consumer electronics backward integration and margin profiles?  
  **A (Management):** Segment operates with a target of 40% ROCE over a 5-year framework; integration into deeper processes will be margin accretive.
- **Q (Nomura/Capital):** What contributed to the 68% YoY growth in Wiring Harness?  
  **A (Management):** ~1/3rd came from the Autoelectric integration, with the remaining 2/3rds coming from 40%+ CV growth in North America and strong domestic Indian growth post-GST changes.
- **Q (Morgan Stanley/Independent):** How will AIS-184 ADAS/Drowsiness regulations evolve in India?  
  **A (Management):** Regulations evolve gradually. While OEMs currently use workarounds to minimize initial cost, mature camera/sensor tech (like SAMIL's Autocruis solution) will ultimately be adopted long-term.
- **Q (Capital/Equirus):** Will aerospace hit $1 billion revenue organically before Vision 2030?  
  **A (Management):** Target is multiples of $1 billion, which will require a mix of organic greenfields and customer-directed acquisitions.

### One-line takeaway
SAMIL delivered record revenues of Rs 38,280 crore driven by strategic wiring harness and non-auto acquisitions, holding balance sheet leverage stable at 0.8x amid global auto volume headwinds.
