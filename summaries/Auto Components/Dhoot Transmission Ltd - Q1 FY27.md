# Dhoot Transmission Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong ~50% YoY revenue growth, a robust two-wheeler industry performance, and a 79% surge in EV revenues.

### Key numbers
- **Overall Revenue Growth:** Nearly 50% YoY.
- **Wiring Harness Revenue Growth:** 44.6% YoY (Absolute: Rs. 1,090 crore vs Rs. 753 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Non-Wiring Harness Revenue Growth:** 67.7% YoY (Absolute: Rs. 358 crore vs Rs. 213 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **EV Revenue Growth:** 79% YoY; EV contribution reached 27% of total revenue (up from 24% last year).
- **Multilink Contribution:** Contributed ~3% to overall revenue growth for the quarter.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 15% (improved 110 bps compared to Q4 FY26).
- **Debt Level:** Rs. 220 crore at the end of the June quarter.
- **Full-Year Growth Guidance:** 25%-30%.
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** 15%-16%.

### Highlights
- First earnings call post-listing.
- Multilink integration progressing well, adding Hero as a customer and introducing fuel level sensors and relays.
- Collaboration with Ride Vision progressing toward a JV for two-wheeler ADAS.
- Added a second major customer in the South for EV battery pack assembly.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of delivering another strong year of 25%-30% growth (Rahul Dhoot).
- Management maintains full-year EBITDA margin guidance at 15%-16% (Nitin Kalani).
- EV revenue contribution is expected to exceed 30%-32% in the next 2-3 years (Nitin Kalani).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Two-wheeler domestic volumes grew in excess of 20% and exports in excess of 36% YoY; EV industry grew by 93% and ICE in excess of 20%.
- Electrification trend is strongly aiding growth in both wiring and non-wiring harness segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins improved 110 bps QoQ to 15%.
- Key raw materials (Copper, Brass) continued an upward trend in the first 5 months, though at a slower pace than the previous year. Copper accounts for 20%-23% of BOM cost.
- Most copper inflation has been passed on, with a pricing lag of about 3 months with customers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expansion ongoing in two plants (Jhajjar and Hosur), adding 15%-20% capacity this year.
- Capacity is maintained at ~75% year-round to support peak season (Diwali) efficiency and demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt stood at Rs. 220 crore at the end of June due to the Bain capital infusion in March and Multilink acquisition impact; expected to turn cash surplus by the end of August post-IPO fund utilization.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted a soft comparison for the next 6 months because the GST reduction fueling growth was implemented in H2 of the previous year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Siddharth Bera (Nomura) on margins:** Asked about copper inflation and margin expansion. Management stated most inflation is passed on with a lag and guided for 15%-16% margins for the full year.
- **Rishi Vora (Kotak Securities) on debt and cash:** Asked about cash/debt levels. Management confirmed June-end debt was Rs. 220 crore and expected to reach net cash surplus by August-end post-IPO.
- **Vijay Kumar Pandey on Multilink and JVs:** Asked about Multilink integration and ADAS. Management stated Multilink integration takes 4 months with margins aligning with Dhoot, and Ride Vision JV discussions are advanced for two-wheeler ADAS.
- **Ajox Frederick on segment revenues:** Asked for absolute numbers. Management provided Q1 FY27 wiring harness revenue of Rs. 1,090 crore and non-wiring harness revenue of Rs. 358 crore.
- **Preet Jain on EV content and capacity:** Asked about EV content multiplier. Management stated total EV content can be up to 4x compared to ICE if all products are supplied.

### One-line takeaway
Dhoot Transmission posted a strong Q1 FY27 with ~50% YoY revenue growth, underpinned by surging EV adoption and robust two-wheeler demand, while reiterating its 25%-30% annual growth guidance.
