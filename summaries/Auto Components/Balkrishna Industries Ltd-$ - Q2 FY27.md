# Balkrishna Industries Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong sales volume growth and strategic Pirelli partnership, but profitability impacted by raw material inflation, freight costs, and higher domestic volume mix.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 3,450 crore (up 49% YoY), includes realized forex loss on sales of Rs 41 crore.
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 6,859 crore (up 35% YoY), includes realized forex loss on sales of Rs 76 crore.
- **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 629 crore (Margin: 18.24%).
- **Standalone EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,332 crore (Margin: 19.42%).
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 291 crore.
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 723 crore.
- **Tire Sales Volume (H1 FY27):** 185,167 metric tons (On-road portfolio contributed early single digit to Q2 volume).
- **Capex Spend (H1 FY27):** Approximately Rs 1,850 crore.
- **Debt & Cash (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Gross debt at approximately Rs 5,985 crore; Cash and cash equivalents at approximately Rs 3,100 crore; Net debt at approximately Rs 2,887 crore.
- **Sea Freight Cost:** Around 6.5% of total sales value in Q2.
- **Interim Dividend:** Second interim dividend of Rs 4 per equity share recommended.
- **Euro Rate (Q2 FY27):** Around Rs 103.

### Highlights
- Announced a strategic tie-up with Pirelli for an offtake agreement to produce Pirelli-branded premium car tires at the Bhuj facility; Phase 1 sales volume targeted to ramp up to 8 lakh tires by year four (2030), with a Phase 2 envisaging a 50-50 JV and a dedicated production plant.
- India's contribution increased to about 40% of overall volumes, impacting margins slightly.
- Other expenses declined QoQ due to the booking of Indian T20 league promotion expenses in Q1.
- *Record context note:* The call highlights the new Pirelli partnership and aggressive capex execution, aligning with the recent record updates on the BKT 2.0 / Vision 2030 pivot.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management envisages a long-term average EBITDA margin range between 20% and 25%.
- Management aims to meet Vision 2030 targets (5% market share and 20% turnover from the new on-road vertical).
- Capex target of Rs 3,000 crore for the year remains on track.

### Demand & industry commentary
- End markets globally were extremely volatile due to evolving geopolitical situations.
- OHT sales volume growth was strong in Q2, though not directly comparable to Q2 last year due to low U.S. sales from tariff situations.
- Europe faced challenges from extreme weather conditions and agricultural market pressures, but BKT maintained its brand position.
- India continued to outperform with positive trends across micro markets despite erratic monsoons.
- Americas witnessed stable sales; management is focusing on sharper execution to increase market share.
- All-Steel Radial mining tire range is seeing positive response and growing acceptance globally.
- Channel inventory levels are at normal levels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability was impacted by headwinds from raw material inflation and freight costs; management expects these headwinds to continue in coming quarters.
- Price hikes of about 2%-3% were implemented mainly in the Indian market; globally, management uses a tactical mix of price and volume.
- Carbon Black raw material costs remain volatile due to higher crude prices.
- Sea freight costs are estimated to remain at elevated levels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex spend reached Rs 1,850 crore in H1 FY27 against the annual target of Rs 3,000 crore; remaining capex will be deployed over the next two years.
- Loans increased due to preponing capex investments to speed up project completion.
- Old Carbon Black capacity is fully utilized, and newer lines are ramping up to feed third-party external sales (which stand at ~10% of overall Carbon Black business).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital outflow in H1 was in line with revenue growth (35%); inventory levels temporarily rose due to supply chain disruptions and buffering for EUDR regulations.
- Gross debt stands at ~Rs 5,985 crore and cash at ~Rs 3,100 crore (net debt ~Rs 2,887 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued raw material and freight cost inflation impacting margins.
- Geopolitical volatility and supply chain disruptions affecting end markets and logistics.
- Increased India volume mix (now ~40%) exerting slight downward pressure on overall margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Raghunandhan NL (Nuvama Research):** Asked about volume sustainability above 90,000 MT and pricing actions. *Management stated it is their endeavor to hit those numbers, and they implemented a 2%-3% price hike mainly in India while playing a tactical price-volume mix globally.*
2. **Siddhartha Bera (Analyst):** Asked about the steep RM cost jump and freight cost percentage. *Management confirmed sea freight is around 6.5% of total sales and noted RM cost pressures will persist for the next couple of quarters.*
3. **Arvind Sharma (Securities Analyst):** Inquired about the BKT 2.0 structure and margin trajectory. *Management clarified that the three segments (off-road, on-road, carbon) fall under one company/balance sheet for marketing/branding, and long-term EBITDA margins are targeted between 20%-25%.*
4. **Vijay Pandey (Analyst):** Asked about working capital outflows and debt increases. *Management explained that inventory rose due to supply chain issues and EUDR preparation, while debt increased due to preponed capex execution.*
5. **Yash Agrawal (Analyst):** Asked about Carbon Black capacity utilization and on-road distribution ramp-up. *Management stated old capacity is fully utilized, new capacity targets third-party sales, and distributor networks for TBR, 2-wheeler, and upcoming PCR are fully set up.*

### One-line takeaway
Balkrishna Industries delivered strong Q2 volume growth and secured a strategic tie-up with Pirelli, but near-term profitability remains pressured by raw material inflation, elevated freight costs, and a rising domestic sales mix.
