# Suprajit Engineering Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 22% YoY in H1, with successful margin recovery in ICM and PLE divisions and GCM restructuring now behind the company.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained. Consolidated EBITDA margin guidance of 12%-13.5% remains intact.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "muted" global/domestic concerns to a "solid quarter" with a "clear road ahead for H2," despite acknowledging high base effects in the Indian auto sector.
- **New topics or risks:** New: Technology tie-up with a Japanese company for gear shifters; launch of complex TFT cluster with Ultraviolette. Risks: Electronics component cost increases and difficulty in passing them on quickly.
- **Last quarter's promises:** STC building completion remains on track for Q3; GCM restructuring is confirmed as complete.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1):** Rs 2,205 crore (up 22% YoY).
- **Consolidated Operational EBITDA (H1):** Rs 267 crore (up 40% YoY).
- **Standalone Revenue (H1):** Rs 1,034 crore (up 18% YoY).
- **Standalone Operational EBITDA (H1):** Rs 149 crore (up 3% YoY).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 371 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Q2 Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 12.2%.

### Highlights
- **GCM Division:** Revenue grew ~24%, EBITDA grew ~69%; margins improved from 7.4% to 10%+. Restructuring is complete.
- **SED (Electronics):** Revenue grew 50.4% YoY, driven by digital clusters and Electronic Throttle Controls.
- **Product Launches:** Launched complex TFT cluster with Ultraviolette (featuring dual cameras, radar, and Qualcomm chips).
- **ICM Division:** Margins recovered to 15.3% (from 12.8% in Q1).
- **PLE Division:** Margins recovered to 14.4% (from 6.7% in Q1); "last man standing" strategy benefiting from a major halogen supplier's insolvency.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 margins to be slightly better than H1.
- Consolidated EBITDA margin guidance: 12%-13.5% for the year.
- GCM margin guidance: 10%-12%.
- Capex guidance: ~Rs 200 crore for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Global:** Americas showing potential; Europe remains a concern (Germany weak); China remains an enigma but management is upbeat.
- **India:** Automotive sector grew 20%+ in H1, but H2 expected to be less exciting due to high base effect (lower GST introduced Sept last year).
- **Aftermarket:** Growth remains modest as strong OEM demand leads to lower service/replacement frequency.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- SED margins impacted by significant component cost increases and logistics/shipping costs; cost pass-through is taking longer than expected.
- Other income lower YoY due to unfavorable currency movements (rupee appreciation vs last year).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Setting up greenfield cable plant in Chennai (Suprajit Automotive).
- Expanding SED facility; construction starting on vacated space.
- STC building completion expected this quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced to Rs 371 crore.
- Payout policy: 30%-40% of cash generation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity price volatility and Middle East conflict.
- Electronics component cost increases and difficulty in passing them on quickly.
- Europe demand weakness.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gokul Maheshwari (Awriga):** Asked about capital allocation. Management: Capex ~Rs 200 crore, balance for debt reduction.
- **Viraj Kacharia (SiMPL):** Asked about GCM growth sustainability. Management: Strong double-digit growth expected; Europe growth offset by US/China.
- **Chirag Shah (White Pine):** Asked about new product ramp-up (brakes/actuators). Management: Drum brake system production starting this quarter; hydraulic brakes in advanced testing.
- **Yash Agarwal:** Asked about PLE margin sustainability. Management: 14.4% margin likely not sustainable; look at Q1+Q2 average.

### One-line takeaway
Suprajit delivered a strong H1 with 22% revenue growth and 40% EBITDA growth, successfully navigating global restructuring and margin recovery in domestic divisions, with a positive outlook for H2.
