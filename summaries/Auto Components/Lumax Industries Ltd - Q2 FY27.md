# Lumax Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY 27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 33% YoY, outpacing broader industry trends, driven by new launches and rising LED share.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Consolidated):** INR 1,342 crore (up 33% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- **Manufacturing Revenue:** INR 1,300 crore (up 33% YoY)
- **H1 FY 27 Revenue:** INR 2,565 crore (up 33% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Consolidated):** INR 110 crore (up 21% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.2% (vs 9% in Q2 FY 26)
- **PAT (Consolidated, including share of associates):** INR 52 crore (up 46% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 3.9% (up 40 bps YoY)
- **Effective Tax Rate:** 25.3%
- **LED Lighting Share (H1 FY 27):** 63% of revenue (up 200 bps YoY)
- **Order Book:** INR 2,580 crore (LED lighting accounts for ~80%, EV programs for ~11%, Passenger vehicles for 63%, 2/3-Wheelers for 37%)
- **Net Long-Term Debt:** INR 188 crore as of 30th September 2026 (vs INR 291 crore as of 31st March 2025)
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 2.2 as of 30th September 2026 (vs 3.0 as of 31st March 2025)
- **Capex Incurred (H1 FY 27):** INR ~80 crore
- **Full Year Capex Guidance:** INR 200-250 crore

### Highlights
- Key model launches in PV segment: Tata Motors Aeris (tail lamps for high and base versions); Mahindra & Mahindra Thar Refresh (headlamp, fog lamp, cornering lamps, and side repeater lamp).
- Gaining wallet share and expanding customer base with OEMs including TVS, Suzuki 2-Wheelers, Yamaha Motors, and Toyota.
- Awards received: Launch Excellence Award at Tata Motors Passenger Vehicle Annual Supplier Conference; CII National Award for Best Environment Practices (Chakan II plant); UBS Forums Award for best practices in sustainability (Bengaluru plant).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised full-year revenue growth guidance upwards from "20% plus" to "25% plus" for FY 27.
- Management maintains its full-year EBITDA margin trajectory to a double-digit number, expecting H2 margins to be in the 10.5-11% range.
- Management remains bullish on FY 28, noting that almost 60% of the current order book (close to INR 1,500 crore revenue) gets delivered in FY 28.
- Management expects export revenue to potentially come in over the next 2 to 3 years, though it will remain minuscule compared to domestic.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 FY 27 auto sector growth supported by GST 2.0 affordability, rural/urban demand, freight activity, infrastructure-led growth, and replacement cycles.
- SIAM data for Q2 FY 27: Total auto production up 19% YoY to 106 lakh units. PV production up 25% YoY to ~17 lakh units; 2-Wheeler up 17% to ~81 lakh units; 3-Wheeler up 25% to ~4.5 lakh units; CV up 34% to ~3.5 lakh units.
- Management expects growth rates to ease in H2 FY 27 as the industry moves beyond the low base of H1 and GST rate cuts are fully reflected.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Material cost impact continued due to the West Asia conflict and elevated prices of electronic components.
- Raw material price escalations and lag in customer cost pass-through caused an impact of approximately 100-120 bps in H1 FY 27.
- Bare PCB localisation is around 60-70%; SMT is 100% in-house captive; connectors are 30-35% localized.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY 27 capex guidance maintained at INR 200-250 crore. Projects progressing as planned.
- ~60% of the current order book (INR 1,500+ crore) is slated for Start of Production (SOP) by the end of this fiscal year or early next fiscal year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net long-term debt stood at INR 188 crore as of 30th September 2026.
- Net debt to EBITDA improved to 2.2 as of September 30, 2026, compared to 3.0 as of March 31, 2025.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments (West Asia conflict) and energy/crude-linked polymer prices and rupee depreciation putting pressure on input costs and margins.
- Short-term supply chain volatility on electronic imports affecting production schedules.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Garvit Goyal (Serene Alpha) on margin confidence for H2:** Management stated that unrecovered Q1 escalations and Q2 impacts will pass through in H2, and with no further anticipated base escalations alongside operational leverage, double-digit margins are achievable.
2. **Viraj Kacharia (SiMPL) on OEM wallet share increases:** Management detailed wallet share expansions over the next 2-3 years, including Maruti Suzuki (from 25-30% to 35-40%), HMSI tail lamps (from <20% to near 50%), Toyota Kirloskar front lighting (to 15-20%), Suzuki 2-Wheelers (from 15-20% to >30%), and VW/Skoda tail lamps (from 30% to ~70%).
3. **Radha (Motilal Oswal) on ADB technology and competition:** Management noted they were the first in India to introduce ADB, leverage Stanley's global technologies and local engineering, and utilize in-house Advanced Lighting Centres in Czech Republic and Taiwan.
4. **Utkarsh Somaiya (Eiko Quantum Solutions) on H2 margin math:** Management confirmed H2 margins are expected between 10.5-11% to meet the full-year double-digit EBITDA target, assuming no further West Asia crisis volatility.
5. **Aaryan Doshi (Divitiae Investments) on EV contribution:** Management stated current EV revenue contribution is 10-12% and noted lighting is powertrain-agnostic.

### One-line takeaway
Lumax Industries reported a strong 33% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY 27, raised its full-year revenue growth guidance to 25%+, and maintained its double-digit EBITDA margin target despite near-term raw material headwinds.
