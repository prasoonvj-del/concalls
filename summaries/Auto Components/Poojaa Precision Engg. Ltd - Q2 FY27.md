# Poojaa Precision Engg. Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (68% YoY), robust order book, and clear capacity expansion roadmap to support long-term targets.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 210 crore (vs Rs 125 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 40 crore (vs Rs 23 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 19%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 25 crore (vs Rs 13 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **PAT Margin:** 12%.
*   **Order Book:** Rs 1,180 crore.
*   **ROE:** 25% (H1 FY27).

### Highlights
*   **Maiden Earnings Call:** Company highlighted its transformation from a manual foundry to a precision engineering partner.
*   **Segment Mix:** Non-automotive revenue grew 194% YoY to Rs 53 crore (25% of sales); automotive contributed 63%.
*   **Customer Base:** Expanded from 39 (FY24) to 58 (H1 FY27).
*   **Product Mix:** Shifted toward larger, heavier components (50-80 kg vs previous 1-9 kg range).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management targets 50% growth for FY27 and 30-35% CAGR for the subsequent two years.
*   **Long-term Goal:** Targeting Rs 1,000 crore annual turnover by FY30.
*   **Aerospace:** Management expects a 4-year timeline to reach production/sales stage for aerospace components.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Diversification:** Aiming for a 50:50 revenue mix between automotive and non-automotive sectors.
*   **Non-Auto Drivers:** Energy/power distribution is the primary sector, followed by agriculture and healthcare; defense is being added.
*   **Auto Drivers:** Exposure to 2W, 3W, PV, CV, and EV (motor housing, battery enclosures).
*   **Macro Tailwinds:** Management cited China+1 strategy, "Make in India" policy, and industry-wide shift toward light-weighting (aluminum) as key growth drivers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Materials:** Full pass-through mechanism exists with customers; raw material price volatility does not impact P&L.
*   **Margins:** Gross margin compression (59% to 53%) attributed to product mix shift toward larger, heavier parts.
*   **Fixed Costs:** Management stated fixed costs for Unit 3 are already absorbed; future costs will be variable, potentially improving margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current Capacity:** 13,800 MT (melting) and 6,000 MT (casting/finishing).
*   **Expansion:** Targeting 28,800 MT (melting) and 12,600 MT (casting/finishing) by FY28.
*   **Utilization:** Current capacity utilization is ~82%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Debt-to-equity ratio is 0.08x. Management plans to repay the entire loan by Q2 (FY27).
*   **Working Capital:** Cycle is ~1 month (excluding dies). Die stock (Rs 20 crore) has a 6-9 month cycle.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Aerospace:** Long gestation period (4 years) for certifications and production.
*   **Energy Sector:** Business is tender-based, though management claims to have mitigated seasonality by onboarding Fortune 500 clients.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How will the company reach Rs 1,000 crore revenue?** Management cited a strong order book (Rs 1,180 crore) and an annual RFQ pipeline of Rs 2,000 crore+ with an 8-10% strike rate.
*   **Q: Why did gross margins compress?** Management explained it is due to a product mix shift toward larger, heavier components, which naturally carry different margin profiles.
*   **Q: Is the 19% EBITDA margin sustainable?** Management expects margins to improve as the share of non-auto/engineering business increases and fixed costs are fully absorbed.
*   **Q: Will aerospace be serviced by current capacity?** No, aerospace requires isolated areas and will likely be handled by a future "Unit 4."

### One-line takeaway
Poojaa Precision is aggressively scaling capacity and diversifying into non-auto sectors to achieve a Rs 1,000 crore revenue target by FY30, supported by a strong order book and a pass-through pricing model for raw materials.
