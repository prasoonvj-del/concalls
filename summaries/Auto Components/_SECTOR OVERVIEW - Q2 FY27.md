# Auto Components: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for auto component makers reflected robust top-line momentum driven by strong domestic vehicle production, infrastructure activity, and festive stocking. Every tracked company reported double-digit revenue growth—ranging from 18.6% at Studds Accessories to 58.6% in total income at Menon Bearings. However, this growth was universally accompanied by severe input cost inflation (covering natural rubber, copper, aluminum, steel, and styrene), leading to margin compression or gross margin pressure for several players unless offset by aggressive pricing actions, productivity gains, or operational efficiencies.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board note that domestic demand (passenger vehicles, two-wheelers, and infrastructure-backed commercial vehicles/steel) remained healthy through Q2. However, multiple companies (including Apollo Tyres, Lumax Auto Technologies, Lumax Industries, and Motherson Sumi Wiring [MSWIL]) expect YoY percentage growth rates to moderate in H2 FY27 due to a higher base effect from previous periods.
*   **Divergence:** While domestic and certain export channels (such as US/Europe expansion for Menon Bearings, Studds, and Timken India) are booming, regional divergences exist. Apollo Tyres reported European volume declines due to temporary supply chain disruptions and the Enschede plant closure. Meanwhile, BMW Ventures is uniquely leveraged to Eastern India/Bihar infrastructure-driven steel demand, and Schaeffler India noted strong traction in industrial sectors like wind energy, metal, and cement.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Cost Pressures:** Inflation hit multiple commodities. Apollo Tyres reported a ~25% YoY increase in its H1 raw material basket (natural rubber touching INR 300, crude derivatives up 28–35%). Menon Bearings cited tin up 72% and copper up 50%. MSWIL noted copper up 54% YoY, while Studds struggled with severe styrene inflation. Timken India pointed to rising steel, carbides (up 200%), and labor costs.
*   **Pass-Through Lags & Price Hikes:** Lumax Auto Technologies, Lumax Industries, MSWIL, and Timken India noted a 1-to-3-quarter lag in passing on raw material cost increases to OEMs. To combat this, Apollo Tyres implemented 11% to 15% cumulative price hikes in its replacement segment, and Studds took ~9% price increases. Menon Bearings relied heavily on internal process improvements, yield enhancements, and a 25% jump in productivity to offset a 7+ percentage point drop in gross margins.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Expansions & Greenfield Plants:** 
    *   Lumax Auto Technologies board approved a new Mechatronics facility in Manesar with an outlay of ~INR 1,500 crore, slated for commercialization starting Q3 FY28.
    *   Studds Accessories commenced commercial production at its new plant on October 30, 2026, adding 1.5 million units of capacity (taking total capacity to ~11 million units).
    *   Timken India progressed on rail expansion at Jamshedpur and plain bearing expansion at Bharuch.
    *   BMW Ventures is scaling up its Purnea fabrication capacity (currently at 25% utilization) and launching a welding electrode plant in November.
*   **Utilization Levels:** Apollo Tyres reported high utilization at 85%–90%; Studds at 83%; Timken's Bharuch plant at ~60%; and BMW Ventures' fabrication at ~25%. MSWIL noted its three new Greenfield plants have reached optimal utilization.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Upgrades:** Lumax Auto Technologies revised its full-year revenue growth guidance upwards to 20–25% (reaching ~INR 6,000 crore). Lumax Industries similarly raised its full-year revenue growth guidance from "20% plus" to "25% plus" for FY27.

## Company one-liners
*   **Apollo Tyres:** Delivered robust 16%+ revenue growth led by domestic demand and aggressive pricing, despite a 25% surge in raw material costs.
*   **BMW Ventures:** Posted 46% revenue growth backed by Bihar's steel demand, even as a machinery sale loss dented near-term margins.
*   **Lumax Auto Technologies:** Achieved 35% revenue growth and 50% PAT growth, prompting an upward revision in full-year guidance.
*   **Lumax Industries:** Grew revenue 33% YoY, raised full-year guidance to 25%+, and maintained double-digit EBITDA margin targets.
*   **Menon Bearings:** Reported record-high quarterly sales and profitability driven by strong volumes, automation, and productivity gains.
*   **MSWIL:** Outperformed industry volume growth with 47% revenue expansion, managing copper inflation via operational efficiencies.
*   **Schaeffler India:** Delivered strong double-digit top-line growth across mobility and industrial segments while absorbing input cost headwinds.
*   **Studds Accessories:** Recorded strong volume and export growth, though profitability contracted due to severe styrene inflation.
*   **Timken India:** Delivered 25.4% revenue growth and ~30% PAT growth supported by robust domestic and export demand.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Progression of raw material price stabilization (rubber, copper, styrene, and steel) and the speed of outstanding cost pass-through negotiations with OEMs.
*   Ramp-up speed and supply chain stabilization of European operations following Apollo Tyres' Enschede plant closure and Studds' new Italian subsidiary setup.
*   Execution and capacity utilization ramp-ups at newly commissioned facilities, such as Studds' new helmet plant, BMW's welding electrode unit, and Timken's Bharuch expansion lines.

*Based on: Apollo Tyres Ltd, BMW Ventures Ltd, Lumax Auto Technologies Ltd, Lumax Industries Ltd, Menon Bearings Ltd-$, Motherson Sumi Wiring India Ltd, Schaeffler India Ltd, Studds Accessories Ltd, Timken India Ltd*
