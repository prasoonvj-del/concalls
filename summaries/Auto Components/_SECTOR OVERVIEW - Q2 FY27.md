# Auto Components: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian auto component sector delivered robust top-line performance in Q2 FY27, backed by double-digit volume growth in domestic two-wheelers, passenger vehicles, and commercial vehicles. Multi-year highs in vehicle production and festive demand propelled double-digit revenue growth for companies across the board (e.g., Kross up 80% YoY, DIVGI up 68%, Dhoot up 60%, Minda Corp up 43%, and Rolex Rings up 43%).

However, overall industry profitability faced a sharp tug-of-war between strong operational leverage and severe input cost inflation. Conflict in West Asia, commodity spikes (copper, aluminum, rubber, tin, carbides), soaring global freight rates, and state-level minimum wage revisions pushed input costs higher. While OEMs have contractual pass-through mechanisms, a standard 1-to-3 month lag created widespread short-term margin compression. Companies insulated themselves through premiumization (LED lighting, TFT displays, higher alloy wheel content), rapid scale-up of EV-specific order books, and aggressive capital deployment into non-automotive adjacencies such as Aerospace, Defense, AI Data Centers, and Solar.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
* **Domestic OE Demand:** Broad-based strength across 2-Wheelers (up 16–19% YoY per SIAM data), Passenger Vehicles (+24–25%), and Commercial Vehicles (+33–34%).
* **Festive Acceleration:** GST 2.0 implementation, strong festive retail, rural recovery, and replacement cycles boosted Q2 domestic volumes.
* **Premiumization & Tech Agnostic Shifts:** Rising LED penetration (Lumax Industries at 63% LED mix), higher electronic control content, TFT displays, and structural lightness (ASK Automotive ALPS segment up 69%) consistently outpaced underlying vehicle production growth.

### Divergence
* **H2 Moderation vs. Acceleration:** 
  * *Moderators:* Lumax Auto Technologies, Motherson Sumi Wiring (MSWIL), Lumax Industries, Bosch, and Apollo Tyres expect YoY volume/revenue growth percentages to moderate in H2 FY27 due to a higher prior-year base post-GST 2.0.
  * *Accelerators:* Bharat Forge, Happy Forgings, Kalyani Forge, Uniparts, and Pitti Engineering expect H2 to be stronger than H1, driven by export order ramp-ups, ramp-up of new plants, and seasonal execution schedules.
* **Tractor & Agriculture Segment:** 
  * *Cautious:* Craftsman Automation, Kirloskar Ferrous, Sundram Fasteners, and Bosch cite erratic/uneven monsoons and OEM caution leading to a 1–2 quarter contraction or softening in tractors.
  * *Resilient/Growing:* Menon Bearings (all-time high schedules via exports/harvesting), Happy Forgings (gained market share from 42% to 46%), and Uniparts (small ag cycle turning +25%) report continued resilience.
* **Global Export Markets:** 
  * *Upbeat:* Dhoot Transmission (>40% guidance raise), ASK Automotive (raised export target to Rs 180+ cr), DIVGI Torqtransfer (exports >Rs 23 cr quarterly run-rate), Ramkrishna Forgings (V-curve US recovery), and Rolex Rings (US component sales doubled) report robust export traction via "China/Europe Plus One."
  * *Subdued/Cautious:* Apollo Tyres, Mayur Uniquoters (soft US car market), Schaeffler India (limited visibility beyond 90-120 days), and Tenneco Clean Air cite Western GDP contraction, supply chain re-routing, and tariff uncertainties.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

### Raw Material Inflation & Denominator Effect
* **Copper & Aluminum:** Copper prices surged ~54% YoY and ~6% QoQ (impacting MSWIL, Minda Corp, Dhoot, and Sona Comstar). Aluminum experienced sharp spikes, dragging on EBITDA margins at Bosch, ASK Automotive, and Craftsman Automation.
* **Pass-Through Dynamics:** Almost all managements maintain indexation clauses, but face a **1-to-2 quarter lag** in cost recoveries:
  * *Pass-through lags:* Minda Corp (1-quarter lag), MSWIL (3-month lag), Tenneco (non-indexed items like rubber/lube oil causing temporary dips), Pricol (bare PCB and memory cost spikes undergoing tough customer negotiations), and SKF India (vendor hikes given in July; OEM PO amendments pending).
  * *Denominator Effect:* ASK Automotive and Sona Comstar highlighted that commodity pass-through increases the revenue denominator without adding EBITDA, optically diluting percentage margins despite absolute EBITDA growth.

### Non-Commodity Headwinds
* **Freight & Freight Disruption:** Ocean freight rates nearly doubled QoQ due to the Middle East crisis and port congestion. Rico Auto suffered a catastrophic Rs 40 crore air-freight impact to meet Toyota/BMW timelines. Pitti Engineering, GRP, Rolex Rings, and Ramkrishna Forgings also reported margin compression due to elevated export logistics expenses.
* **Minimum Wage Hikes:** Significant contractual labor minimum wage increases in states like Haryana and Karnataka hit fixed cost structures at ASK Automotive, NDR Auto, Sansera Engineering, and Tenneco.

## What changed since last quarter

### Guidance Revisions
* **Dhoot Transmission:** Annual growth guidance **raised from 25–30% to >40%** following 108% YoY surge in EV revenues.
* **Lumax Industries:** Full-year revenue growth guidance **raised from "20%+ " to "25%+"**; expecting H2 EBITDA margins to hit 10.5–11%.
* **Lumax Auto Technologies:** Full-year top-line growth guidance **raised from ~12–14% to 20–25%** (~Rs 6,000 cr target).
* **ASK Automotive:** Full-year export guidance **raised from Rs 145 cr to >Rs 180 cr**.
* **S.J.S. Enterprises:** Revenue growth guidance **recalibrated downward** from 1.5x–2.0x to 1.2x–1.5x of industry growth due to geopolitical drag on consumer exports and motorcycle/scooter mix variance; **EBITDA margin guidance raised to 29–30%** (from 27–28%).

### Delivery on Promises
* **Dhoot Transmission:** Achieved net cash position of Rs 900 crore, fully repaying IPO-related debt as promised in Q1.
* **Exide Industries:** Successfully commissioned Phase 1 of its 6 GWh lithium-ion cell gigafactory in Bengaluru on September 23, 2026.
* **Gala Precision Engineering:** Delivered on ramp-up at its Chennai plant (utilization rising to 70%), driving operational leverage.
* **Shivalik Bimetal Controls:** Successfully fully operationalized Phase I of its Pune facility (as of July 2026).

## What to watch next quarter
1. **Pass-Through Realization & Margin Normalization:** Extent to which price recoveries materialize in Q3 at SKF India, Tenneco, Pricol, MSWIL, Minda Corp, and Sterling Tools.
2. **Red Sea & Geopolitical Shipping Costs:** Whether ocean freight rates continue their October/November moderation to relieve margin pressure on Pitti Engineering, Rolex Rings, Rico Auto, and Synergy Green.
3. **Commissioning & Trial Runs of Major Greenfield Facilities:**
   * *Ratnaveer Precision:* Trial run of India’s first Copper Clad Laminate (CCL) line (Dec 2026–Jan 2027).
   * *Kross Ltd:* Trial production of seamless tube plant (Feb/March 2027).
   * *Ramkrishna Forgings:* Commercial scale-up of the Railway Wheels JV (scaling to 4,000–5,000 wheels/month in Q4).
   * *Sedemac Mechatronics:* Production shift to the newly built 3x-sized MF3 plant.
4. **Integration of Cross-Border Acquisitions:** Earnings trajectory of Samvardhana Motherson’s newly consolidated acquisitions (Autoelectric and Yutaka Giken, target ~$2B revenue) and Varroc’s acquisition of Forsee Power India.
