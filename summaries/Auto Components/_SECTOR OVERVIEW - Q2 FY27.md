# Auto Components: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Across the 64 covered companies, Q2 FY27 was defined by strong domestic production volumes (+18% to +20% YoY per SIAM data), robust festive/GST 2.0-led retail demand, and expanding content per vehicle via premiumization (LED lighting, TFT displays, alloy wheels, advanced drivelines). However, severe commodity inflation (natural rubber hitting Rs 300, copper up ~54% YoY, aluminum, steel, and PCBs) alongside elevated ocean freight and West Asia logistics disruptions caused margin compression for players unable to pass through input costs without a 1-to-2 quarter contractual lag. 

While domestic two-wheelers, passenger vehicles, and heavy commercial vehicles outperformed, global export markets remained mixed—North American commercial vehicles and power generation (AI data center infrastructure) showed strong recovery, whereas European agriculture and passenger vehicle production remained soft. EV adoption accelerated rapidly across two- and three-wheelers, with EV-specific components and assemblies crossing critical mass thresholds across major suppliers.

---

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
- **Domestic Automotive Strong:** Managements broadly agree that domestic two-wheeler, PV, and M&HCV segments saw record or high-double-digit growth in Q2 FY27, bolstered by festive demand, GST 2.0 affordability, and replacement cycles (e.g., **ASK Automotive**, **Apollo Tyres**, **Automotive Axles**, **Minda Corp**, **MSWIL**, **Uno Minda**).
- **Non-Auto / Infrastructure Scaling:** Strong structural demand from AI data centers, power generation, defense, and railways provided significant tailwinds (**Bharat Forge**, **Craftsman Automation**, **Happy Forgings**, **Kalyani Forge**, **Pitti Engineering**, **Uniparts India**).
- **H2 Moderation on High Base:** A consensus view emerged that while absolute demand momentum remains intact, YoY volume growth percentages in H2 FY27 will moderate due to high prior-year base effects (**Apollo Tyres**, **Bosch**, **Exide**, **Lumax Auto Tech**, **MSWIL**).

### Divergence
- **Tractors & Farm Equipment:** **Carraro India** (+23% H1 agri growth) and **Happy Forgings** (>20% farm growth) reported strong market-share-led outperformance, whereas **Bosch**, **Craftsman Automation**, **Kirloskar Ferrous**, and **Sundram Fasteners** warned of softening tractor demand in Q3/Q4 due to uneven monsoons and high base effects.
- **Export Markets (US vs. Europe):** **MM Forgings**, **Ramkrishna Forgings**, **Rolex Rings**, and **Uniparts** noted strong recoveries in US Class 8 trucks and US construction/industrial demand. Conversely, **Balkrishna Industries**, **Carraro India**, **Harsha Engineers**, and **Schaeffler India** highlighted subdued European agriculture and industrial softness.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

- **Pass-Through Lags Compress Near-Term Margins:** Commodity price spikes (copper, aluminum, natural rubber, crude derivatives, bare PCBs, memory) hit gross margins across the sector. Contractual 3-to-6-month pass-through lags created temporary margin friction (**Apollo Tyres**, **Bosch**, **Carraro India**, **Endurance**, **Lumax Industries**, **Pricol**, **Sterling Tools**, **Varroc**).
- **Denominator Effect:** Rapid pass-through of raw material prices (especially aluminum and copper) inflated top-line revenues without adding absolute EBITDA rupees, optically diluting EBITDA margin percentages (**ASK Automotive**, **Balkrishna Industries**, **Minda Corp**, **Sona BLW**, **Uno Minda**).
- **Freight & Conversion Headwinds:** Surging ocean freight rates and logistics delays stemming from West Asia conflicts severely dragged down margins for export-heavy businesses (**Balkrishna Industries**, **Carraro India**, **Happy Forgings**, **Rico Auto**, **Rolex Rings**). **Rico Auto** posted a net loss of Rs 26.3 crore in Q2 due to Rs 40 crore in emergency air freight expenses.
- **Wage Inflation:** State-level minimum wage revisions (notably Haryana and Karnataka) created unindexed operational cost spikes (**ASK Automotive**, **Minda Corp**, **NDR Auto**, **Sansera Engineering**).

## Capex & capacity

- **Capacity Additions Accelerating:** Capex programs are running at full throttle across greenfield and brownfield sites to meet rising order books, backward integration, and non-auto opportunities.
  - **Bharat Forge:** Investing ~Rs 1,300 crore in Baramati brownfield expansion and setting up a dedicated aerospace plant.
  - **Exide:** Commissioned its 6 GWh lithium-ion gigafactory in Bengaluru on Sept 23, 2026.
  - **Happy Forgings:** Announced a new Rs 500 crore capex program (70% machining, 30% forging) over FY28–FY29 alongside its ongoing Rs 650 crore heavy-component/solar capex.
  - **Pitti Engineering:** Announced a new Rs 450 crore capex program, bringing total planned capex to Rs 740 crore.
  - **Sansera Engineering:** Raised FY27 capex projection to Rs 800 crore (~50% dedicated to Aerospace & Defense).
  - **Uno Minda:** Executing Rs 1,415 crore in strategic projects (Hosur casting, TGSIN JV) plus an additional Rs 85 crore for Bawal sunroof expansion.
  - **Steel Strips Wheels:** Approved a Rs 1,400 crore greenfield facility in Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) over 60 months.

## What changed since last quarter

- **Guidance Revisions (Upwards):**
  - **Automotive Axles:** Raised full-year industry volume outlook to >500,000 units (up from expecting a 5%-10% drop or flat volumes).
  - **Dhoot Transmission:** Revised FY27 revenue growth guidance from 25%-30% up to >40%.
  - **Fiem Industries:** Raised FY27 capex guidance to ~Rs 150 crore (from Rs 100 crore); targeting top end of 15%-20% revenue growth.
  - **Lumax Auto Technologies:** Revised full-year revenue growth guidance to 20%-25% (up from implied 12%-14%).
  - **Lumax Industries:** Revised full-year revenue growth guidance to "25% plus" (up from "20% plus").
  - **MM Forgings:** Raised FY27 revenue target to >Rs 2,000 crore (from Rs 1,800–1,900 crore).
  - **Steel Strips Wheels:** Confirmed it will exceed initial FY27 top-line growth guidance of >20%.
- **Guidance Revisions (Recalibrated / Lowered):**
  - **S.J.S. Enterprises:** Recalibrated FY27 revenue growth outlook to 1.2x–1.5x industry growth (from 1.5x–2x earlier) due to West Asia export softness and motorcycle vs. scooter divergence, while *raising* EBITDA margin guidance to 29%–30% (from 27%–28%).
- **Execution Deliveries & Progress:**
  - **Dhoot Transmission:** Delivered on its promise to become net cash (Rs 900 crore) after fully repaying IPO-related debt.
  - **Exide:** Delivered on its milestone to commission Phase 1 (6 GWh) of its lithium-ion cell plant in Bengaluru.
  - **Kalyani Cast-Tech:** Commissioned Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal at Shivlakha ahead of schedule on Sept 15, 2026.
  - **Suprajit Engineering:** Fully completed the restructuring of its overseas GCM division (margins recovered to 10%+).

## What to watch next quarter

1. **Pass-Through Recovery Realization:** Watch if Q3 FY27 margin recovery materializes as 3-to-6-month price revision lags catch up across suppliers (**Carraro**, **Endurance**, **Lumax Industries**, **Minda Corp**, **Pricol**, **SKF India**, **Sterling Tools**, **Varroc**).
2. **Freight & Supply Chain Stabilization:** Tracking whether ocean freight rates continue easing from peak levels to relieve export-oriented margin pressures (**Balkrishna Industries**, **Happy Forgings**, **Mayur Uniquoters**, **Rico Auto**, **Rolex Rings**).
3. **Lithium-Ion & EV Ramp-ups:** Monitoring commercial revenue contribution and cell yield stabilization at **Exide's** newly commissioned 6 GWh Bengaluru plant and **Dhoot Transmission's** newly added battery plants.
4. **H2 Demand Trajectory Post-Festive Season:** Assessing how domestic retail sales hold up post-festive season and post-GST 2.0 momentum across passenger cars, commercial vehicles, and two-wheelers.
5. **Execution of Mega-Capex Projects:** SOP progression on **Bharat Forge's** Baramati forging expansion, **Ramkrishna Forgings'** Railway Wheels JV, **Sansera's** aerospace plant, and **Steel Strips Wheels'** Sambhajinagar greenfield site.
