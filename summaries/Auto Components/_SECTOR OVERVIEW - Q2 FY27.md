# Auto Components: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Q2 FY27 was a **record quarter for the majority** of the 81 companies covered. Managements reported highest-ever revenues, EBITDA and PAT, driven by broad-based domestic demand strength across two-wheelers, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles and tractors, accelerating EV penetration, and a recovery in export orders helped by China-plus-one and Europe-plus-one sourcing shifts. The tone is **positive on volume growth** but **cautious on near-term margins** as raw material inflation (aluminium, steel, copper, rubber, crude derivatives), freight spikes from West Asia tensions, and minimum-wage hikes in Haryana/Karnataka compress spreads despite contractual pass-throughs with 1–3 quarter lags. Capex cycles are **aggressive and front-loaded** – multiple companies raised full-year capex guidance – targeting EV, non-auto (aerospace, defence, data-centre, renewables), and export capacity.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian auto components historically grew by supplying ICE powertrain, chassis and body parts to domestic OEMs, with low export intensity and limited value-add. Over the last ~5 years the model has shifted toward **higher content-per-vehicle (EV + premiumisation), non-auto diversification (aerospace, defence, industrial, data-centre), export-market penetration, and backward integration/localisation** to capture more value and de-risk single-sector cycles [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **EV transition & content-per-vehicle rise** – **mainstream**. BEV revenue >50% of auto sales at Sona BLW; EV kit value ₹1,250–1,300 vs ₹950 for ICE at ASK; EV share 5.9% of ASK revenue (up from 4.8%), 10% at MSWIL, 11.4% at Sedemac, 31% at Dhoot. New EV programs (Sona suspension motor, ASK HPDC wheels, Endurance ABS/brakes, Lumax ADB) driving 2–3× content uplift [Sona BLW concall Q2 FY27; ASK concall Q2 FY27; MSWIL concall Q2 FY27; Sedemac concall Q2 FY27; Dhoot concall Q2 FY27].
- **Non-auto diversification (aerospace, defence, data-centre, renewables)** – **scaling**. Bharat Forge aerospace order book $100–120M+, defence ₹10,950 cr; Sansera ADS ₹58.7B order book (35–40% revenue target); Ratnaveer CCL ₹338 cr capex (zero domestic competition); Pitti data-centre 7% revenue targeting 9%; Steelcast piece-weight to 3.5T for mining/rail; GRP pyrolysis 45kT by Mar-27 [Bharat Forge concall Q2 FY27; Sansera concall Q2 FY27; Ratnaveer concall Q2 FY27; Pitti concall Q2 FY27; Steelcast concall Q2 FY27; GRP concall Q2 FY27].
- **China-plus-one / Europe-plus-one export acceleration** – **mainstream**. Rolex Rings US revenue 2× YoY, exports 51% of component sales; NRB 30% global CV bearing share, top-10 OEMs include Magna/ZF/Daimler; Uniparts construction 49% YoY (US data-centres, EU infra); Varroc overseas 104% YoY; Alicon Slovakia eAxle/JLR; Endurance Fondalpress/Stöferle for EU truck [Rolex Rings concall Q2 FY27; NRB concall Q2 FY27; Uniparts concall Q2 FY27; Varroc concall Q2 FY27; Alicon concall Q2 FY27; Endurance concall Q2 FY27].
- **Backward integration & localisation** – **scaling**. Minda TFT display module ₹200 cr plant (70–80% localisation); Lumax PCB 60–70%, SMT 100% in-house; Sansera Nichidai JV for ADS; Endurance 4W casting/forging expansion; Steel Strips hot-stamping ₹1,400 cr greenfield; Pricol plastics 100% utilised, ₹350 cr P3L capex [Minda concall Q2 FY27; Lumax concall Q2 FY27; Sansera concall Q2 FY27; Endurance concall Q2 FY27; Steel Strips concall Q2 FY27; Pricol concall Q2 FY27].
- **Premiumisation & higher value-add mix** – **mainstream**. Steel Strips alloy wheels 45% of revenue, EBITDA/wheel ₹404 (2× steel); ASK ALPS 69% YoY, HPDC wheels ₹80 cr FY27 → ₹240 cr FY28; Fiem LED/premium 2W 50% YoY; Lumax LED 63% of revenue, wallet-share gains at Maruti/HMSI/Toyota; SJS chrome plating 2× capacity, ₹500 cr peak [Steel Strips concall Q2 FY27; ASK concall Q2 FY27; Fiem concall Q2 FY27; Lumax concall Q2 FY27; SJS concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
- **PLI schemes (ACC, Auto, Specialty Steel)** – reward localisation & export capacity [Background].
- **PM-eBus Sewa payment security portal (38,000 e-buses)** – de-risks electric bus OEM payments, benefits battery/thermal/e-drive suppliers [Govt: MoHUA, Nov 2026].
- **Finance Ministry EV mandate for staff cars** – pulls government fleet demand toward EVs [Govt: FinMin, Nov 2026].
- **Scrappage policy tightening (Environment Ministry)** – formalises recycling, aids Tinna (MRP/rCB), GRP (reclaim), Electrosteel (valves) [Govt: EnvMin, Nov 2026].
- **CAFE III / BS-VI / BS-VII norms** – forces light-weighting, TPMS, ADAS, thermal management content [Background].
- **Defence capex +20% sought** – tailwind for Bharat Forge, Sansera, Steelcast, Indo-MIM [Govt: DefMin, Nov 2026].
- **Tamil Nadu 1,000 AC e-bus order** – direct demand for EV ecosystem in South [Govt: TN Govt, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: Domestic auto production strong – 2W +19.5% YoY H1 (ASK), PV +25%, CV +34%, 3W +25% (Lumax/SIAM). EV 2W >80% YoY (Fiem), EV penetration 8% PV / 11% 2W (Uno Minda). Replacement cycles (BS-VI pre-buys 6–9 yr old) supporting CV (Automotive Axles). Infrastructure/construction driving off-highway, mining, rail (Steelcast, Pitti, Kross). Data-centre backup power lifting industrial gensets (Happy Forgings, Alicon, Kirloskar Ferrous).

**Divergence**:
- **H2 FY27 moderation vs sustain**: Lumax, MSWIL, Sundram, SJS expect growth to moderate on high base (GST 2.0); ASK, Endurance, Sansera, Steel Strips, Kross see H2 ≥ H1.
- **Tractor segment**: Kirloskar Ferrous, Suprajit, Pritika flag softening Q3/Q4 on uneven monsoon/high base; MM Forgings, Steel Strips, Automotive Axles see market-share gains offsetting industry weakness.
- **Europe**: Varroc, Alicon, NRB, Synergy see soft EU industrial/auto; Rolex Rings, Uniparts, Endurance report recovery/new wins.
- **EV near-term volatility**: ASK flags Ola weakness offset by client additions; Precision Camshafts impairs EMOSS (EU EV); Sedemac sees PCB cost spikes; Sona traction motor 50% QoQ but lower margin.

2. **EV demand sustainability** – Ola/Sigma/EMOSS volatility vs new client additions (ASK, Sona, Precision, Sedemac, MSWIL).
3. **Export order conversion** – China-plus-one/Europe-plus-one pipeline to revenue (Rolex Rings, NRB, Uniparts, Varroc, Endurance, Alicon).
4. **Capex execution & ROCE** – greenfield commissioning timelines (Steel Strips Aurangabad, Bharat Forge Baramati, Ratnaveer CCL, Pitti Bangalore, Sansera ADS, Happy Forgings heavy press).
5. **Raw material trajectory** – aluminium (Alicon 20–25% drop Sept–Dec), steel/coking coal, copper, rubber, freight normalisation.
6. **Tractor/CV H2 trajectory** – replacement cycle vs monsoon/base-effect (Automotive Axles, Kirloskar, MM Forgings, Steel Strips, Pritika).

## Company one-liners
- **ASK Automotive**: ASK Automotive delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with 29.5% net revenue growth, expanding ALPS capacity and commercializing first-in-India HPDC alloy wheel supplies.
- **Alicon Castalloy**: Alicon delivered record Q2 revenue of Rs 652 crore with expanding CV market share and strong order wins, maintaining its 12-15% adjusted growth guidance while targeting steady margin expansion toward 14-15% by FY30.
- **Apollo Tyres**: Apollo Tyres delivered robust 16%+ revenue growth in Q2 FY27 led by strong domestic demand and aggressive pricing actions, though profitability faced headwinds from a sharp 25% surge in raw material costs.
- **Automotive Axles**: Automotive Axles delivered its highest-ever Q2 revenue and a 67% YoY surge in EPS, backed by strong replacement-led M&HCV demand and newly operational capacity expansions.
- **Balkrishna Industries**: Balkrishna Industries delivered strong Q2 volume growth and secured a strategic tie-up with Pirelli, but near-term profitability remains pressured by raw material inflation, elevated freight costs, and a rising domestic sales mix.
- **Belrise Industries**: Belrise Industries reported record quarterly PAT supported by 26% manufacturing revenue growth, margin recovery, and landmark order wins in consumer electronics, aerospace, and auto.
- **Bharat Forge**: Bharat Forge is driving sustained non-auto diversification across aerospace, semiconductor, and defence, setting up strong multi-year operating leverage despite near-term drags in overseas subsidiaries.
- **Bosch**: Bosch delivered strong top-line growth supported by the first-time integration of RBIC and announced a 3:1 bonus issue and 1:5 stock split, though elevated input costs and modest H2 demand remain near-term headwinds.
- **Carraro India**: Carraro India delivered 15% revenue growth in H1 FY27 driven by strong domestic 4WD adoption, but margins contracted due to rising commodity and conversion costs lagging pass-through recoveries.
- **Craftsman Automation**: Craftsman Automation is absorbing short-term margin pressures from commodity volatility and sub-optimal new plants while banking on operational leverage, price resets, and a massive $100M stationary engine order book to drive 15% sustained growth.
- **DIVGI TORQTRANSFER SYSTEMS**: Strong operational execution and scale-up in transfer cases and exports have driven significant margin expansion, with management now focused on securing long-term growth through automatic transmissions and global platform integration.
- **Dhoot Transmission**: Dhoot Transmission delivered 60% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 led by surging EV demand, turning net cash with Rs. 900 crore in reserves while raising full-year growth guidance to 40%-45%.

- **Emmforce Autotech**: Despite a muted H1 impacted by high capacity utilization and customer delays, LAPL Automotive expects a strong H2 rebound to deliver ~40% annual growth, supported by IPO proceeds funding its fourth manufacturing facility and new OEM order wins.
- **Endurance Technologies**: Endurance Technologies delivered strong revenue growth and secured strategic European assets, with management guiding for margin recovery in H2 FY27 as commodity headwinds ease and new Greenfield facilities ramp up.
- **Exide Industries**: Exide has successfully commissioned its 6 GWh lithium-ion gigafactory and is delivering strong core growth, with management now focused on stabilizing lithium yields and maintaining 12-12.5% margins amidst currency headwinds.
