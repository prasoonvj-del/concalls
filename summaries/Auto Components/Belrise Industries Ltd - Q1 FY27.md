# Belrise Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Resilient operational performance with strong new business wins, capital raise deployment, and margin stability despite cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** INR25,465 million, up 13% YoY (from INR22,622 million)
- **Manufacturing Revenue:** INR21,979 million, up 20% YoY (from INR18,323 million)
- **EBITDA:** INR2,933 million, up 5% YoY (from INR2,805 million)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 11.5%
- **Manufacturing EBITDA:** INR2,793 million, up 10% YoY (from INR2,536 million)
- **Manufacturing EBITDA Margin:** 12.7%
- **PAT:** INR1,217 million, up 9% YoY (from INR1,117 million)
- **PAT Margin:** 4.8%
- **Exports Contribution:** 4.5% to manufacturing revenue in Q1 FY27

### Highlights
- Strengthened balance sheet via a INR17,000 million capital raise (QIP) during the quarter; intends to deploy a significant majority within the fiscal year towards inorganic and organic growth.
- Entered into an agreement to acquire the tipper business of Hyva India (part of JOST Werke) on August 4, 2026, adding three manufacturing facilities (Pune, Jamshedpur, Bangalore) and expanding heavy fabrication/Tier 0.5 capabilities; transaction expected to close in Q3 FY27.
- Concluded technology collaboration with H-One Japan, strengthening capabilities for high tensile steel applications up to 1470 megapascals.
- Secured multiple marquee new business wins across 2W/3W, 4W/CV, renewable energy, and aerospace & defense.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to remain broadly stable compared to FY26 levels.
- Guided for mid-teens revenue growth for the business as a whole with stable EBITDA margins.
- Aims to reach at least 10% of consolidated revenues from aerospace and defense in the medium term.
- Hyva India acquisition expected to be completed in Q3 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Two and three-wheeler business benefited from industry-wide volume recovery and new platform launches.
- 4W and CV business revenues grew 18% YoY driven by new business wins across an expanding customer base.
- Global OEMs are increasingly looking at India via a "China replacement strategy" due to trust in IP, know-how, capability, and governance.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs (steel and polymer) remained elevated in Q1 FY27; back-to-back pricing model allows pass-through to customers with a slight lag, with no material impact expected on margins for the fiscal year.
- Management stated that the worst is behind them regarding cost pressures across commodities, staff, and energy/transportation.
- Staff costs rose due to an annual increment cycle and hiring ahead of four upcoming facility ramps.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Embarking on a manufacturing expansion for renewable energy (sheet metal assemblies for solar trackers supporting 2.5 GW annually) with production starting in Q4 FY27 and peak annual revenue expected in excess of INR1,500 million.
- Brownfield and new facility expansions ongoing (e.g., Bangalore brownfield for 2W chassis; Maharashtra expansion for CV EV localization).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Raised INR17,000 million via QIP in Q1 FY27 to support growth initiatives and inorganic opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and oil situation impacted input availability, logistics, and trading business performance in Africa, Asia, and the Middle East.
- Minimum wage hike increased staff and labor costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vipul Agrawal (HSBC Securities) on 2W order wins:** Management clarified that the INR650 million annual revenue chassis order is on top of previous wins, bringing the cumulative run rate with that specific 2W/3W OEM to roughly INR2,000 million.
2. **Nitij Mangal on margin trajectory and cost pressures:** Management stated cost pressures have peaked and anticipated margin recovery in subsequent quarters to maintain FY26 margin levels.
3. **Divyansh Jaju on aerospace contribution:** Reaffirmed medium-term target of 10% of manufacturing revenue from aerospace and defense, highlighting advanced discussions for localizing high-volume aero engine components in India.
4. **Nitin Agarwal on trading business decline:** Explained that the 19% YoY decline in the trading business was driven by Middle East/African logistics and demand challenges, which have since calmed down.
5. **Ashwin Patil on segment-wise numbers for H-One and Mag filters:** Management declined to share specific standalone numbers, emphasizing that the business is managed as a single consolidated family.

### One-line takeaway
Belrise Industries delivered a resilient Q1 FY27 with 13% revenue growth, advanced its inorganic strategy via the Hyva India acquisition and QIP deployment, and secured major order wins while maintaining stable margin outlooks.
