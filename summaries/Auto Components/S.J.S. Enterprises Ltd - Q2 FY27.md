# S.J.S. Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record quarterly revenue, EBITDA margin of 31%, and PAT margin of 20.6% (highest since IPO), driven by premiumization, exports, and operational efficiencies.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 2,827.3 million (up 16.9% YoY)
- **H1 FY2027 Revenue:** Rs. 5,437.3 million (up 20.4% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 896.4 million (up 23.1% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 31.0% (up 144 bps YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs. 583.3 million (up 34.8% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 20.6%
- **Automotive Business Growth YoY:** 21.7%
- **Exports Growth YoY:** 23.1% (Rs. 282.5 million); H1 export growth at 45.8%
- **H1 Cash Flow from Operations:** Rs. 1,070.5 million
- **Free Cash Flow:** Rs. 1,077.3 million
- **Net Cash:** Rs. 3,318.7 million as of September 2026
- **ROCE:** 41.5%
- **ROE:** 22.8%
- **Total Capex Plan (FY26–FY28):** Rs. 2,700 million to Rs. 2,850 million (incurred Rs. 1,310.6 million so far)

### Highlights
- SJS Decoplast chrome plating facility in Pune commenced operations in August 2026 (adding 2x capacity, potential peak revenue ~Rs. 500 crore).
- Bengaluru plant expansion completed with a 20% capacity addition; commercial production starts in Q3 FY27.
- Acquired remaining 9.9% stake in Walter Pack India (WPI) for Rs. 199.22 million, making it a wholly-owned subsidiary. WPI consolidating three Ranjangaon plants into one facility (operational by May 2027).
- SJS Display Electronics setting up an automotive display facility in Pune (operational in Q2 FY28); phase 1 capex of Rs. 65 crore (Rs. 25 crore for loca bonding, Rs. 40 crore for glass).
- Monthly revenue crossed Rs. 1,000 million for the first time in September 2026.
- Added new customers including Tata Motors (chrome plating), HARMAN, TADME (Toyota Middle East), and export orders from Stellantis and Whirlpool North America.
- Sourcing 2 MW of wind power from DB Renews; joined UN Global Compact.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management recalibrated FY27 revenue growth outlook to 1.2x–1.5x of passenger vehicle and motorcycle industry growth (from 1.5x–2x earlier) due to macroeconomic uncertainties, West Asian conflict impacting consumer exports, and motorcycle vs. scooter divergence.
- Management raised EBITDA margin guidance to 29%–30% (from 27%–28% earlier) driven by cost efficiencies and product mix.
- Target to achieve 14%–15% export revenue share by FY2028.
- Display market targeted at Rs. 5,000 crore to Rs. 7,000 crore by 2030-2031, with SJS targeting at least 10% market share.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's automotive production volumes grew 17.8% YoY in Q2 FY27 (PVs up 24.9%, two-wheelers up 16.5%).
- Two-wheeler growth led heavily by scooters (30.1% YoY) over motorcycles (9.8% YoY); SJS portfolio is predominantly weighted towards motorcycles with limited scooter exposure.
- Consumer segment softened due to macro uncertainties, geopolitical conflicts, and planned rationalization of low-margin SKUs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin improved to 56.5% (Gross profit up 17.9% YoY to Rs. 1,597.3 million).
- Consciously exited low-margin products/SKUs across segments to prioritize high-profit product mix.
- Depreciation reduced due to completion of intangible amortization related to Walter Pack after three years.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex funded entirely through internal accruals.
- SJS Display asset turns expected at 4x–5x for loca bonding and 2x–3x for glass (average ~3.5x).
- SJS standalone utilization at ~60%, with optimal operations up to 80%–85% due to batch-mode printing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash of Rs. 3,318.7 million as of September 2026 (vs Rs. 2,437 million in March 2026).
- Credit rating: ICRA AA- with positive outlook.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated commodity prices and pricing pressure across the industry.
- Geopolitical conflicts (West Asia) impacting export demand for consumer products.
- Growth divergence in two-wheelers favoring scooters over motorcycles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Chandramouli Muthiah (Goldman Sachs) on upside optionality from input cost corrections:** Management stated easing commodities will improve consumer sentiment and demand, while expanded internal capacities will turbocharge domestic and export growth.
2. **Chandramouli Muthiah (Goldman Sachs) on depreciation trend:** Management clarified that Walter Pack intangibles amortization completed its 3-year period starting Q2 FY27 (reducing D&A by ~Rs. 10 million), though capitalization of Pune and Bangalore plants in Q3 will increase D&A going forward.
3. **Pritesh Chheda on chrome plating peak revenue:** Management indicated the 2x capacity addition can potentially scale up to ~Rs. 500 crore in revenue.
4. **Rakesh Jain on competitive edge in the display business:** Management highlighted their 40-year printing expertise (for printed glass/specialty coatings), local bonding/nearshoring advantage, and partnership with global leader BOE Varitronix.
5. **Nitin Agrawal on lower revenue guidance despite strong outperformance:** Management explained the guidance adjustment was a transparent communication addressing a specific consumer export business given up/impacted by West Asian conflict and motorcycle-scooter mix divergence, while absolute EBITDA/PAT commitments remain intact.

### One-line takeaway
SJS delivered record profitability and margins (31% EBITDA) in Q2 FY27 by prioritizing high-margin product mixes and expanding capacities, even as revenue guidance was prudently recalibrated to 1.2x–1.5x of industry growth due to macro headwinds and product pruning.
