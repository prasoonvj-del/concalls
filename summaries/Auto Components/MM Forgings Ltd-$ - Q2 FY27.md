# MM Forgings Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited strong volume growth, successful integration of DVS Industries, and clear revenue visibility for FY27 and FY28.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised FY27 revenue target to >Rs 2,000 crore (previously Rs 1,800–1,900 crore).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained positive; management emphasized "surging forward" and identified significant scope for cost reduction (Rs 30–100 crore).
- **New topics:** DVS Industries merger (results are now consolidated); Abhinava Rizel (ARPL) commenced sales (300–400 motors/month); management admitted to a 6–8 month delay in 16,500-ton press commissioning due to foundation/vendor issues.
- **Last quarter's promises:** The 4,000-ton press is operational; the 16,500-ton press remains scheduled for Q4 FY27 commissioning (despite the noted delay in the process).

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 551 crore.
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 978 crore.
- **Q2 Sales Volume:** 24,200 tonnes (H1 total: 44,000 tonnes).
- **Q2 EBITDA Margin:** 19.7%.
- **Sales Realization:** Rs 2.23 lakh per ton.
- **Net Debt:** Rs 1,020 crore – Rs 1,070 crore.
- **Average Cost of Debt:** 5.42% (down from 8.5% last year).
- **Capex:** Rs 65 crore spent in H1; Rs 75–80 crore planned for H2.

### Highlights
- **Merger:** DVS Industries merged into MM Forgings; results are consolidated.
- **Capacity:** Total capacity increased to 130,000 tonnes following the addition of a 4,000-ton press at the Viralimalai plant.
- **Abhinava Rizel (ARPL):** Commenced sales of 300–400 motors/month since July; targeting 3,000–4,000 motors/month in the next few years.
- **Land Sale:** H1 PAT includes a Rs 54 crore profit from a land sale.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Management identified Rs 30–100 crore potential in cost reduction through efficient buying and utilization.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27:** Target of 90,000–95,000 tonnes sales volume and >Rs 2,000 crore revenue.
- **FY28:** Target of >100,000 tonnes volume and Rs 2,400–2,500 crore revenue.
- **Exports:** Targeting Rs 600 crore annualized export revenue currently, with a goal to reach Rs 1,200–1,500 crore by the end of the decade.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **US Market:** Class 8 truck market remains strong; management noted a coincidence of increased demand and reduced tariffs.
- **Domestic:** Multi-axle vehicles are seeing increased demand; CV cycle is supported by replacement demand.
- **Non-Auto:** Represents 15% of sales with "huge scope" for improvement.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Machining Mix:** Increased to 65% (from 56% previously).
- **Interest Costs:** Reduced to Rs 19 crore (from Rs 21 crore).
- **Margin Drivers:** Management expects positive margin bias due to better utilization and cost reduction initiatives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **16,500-ton Press:** Commissioning expected in Q4 FY27; Start of Production (SOP) in Q1 FY28.
- **Machining Utilization:** Currently 75–80%.
- **Future Capex:** Management noted that beyond the Rs 2,500 crore revenue mark, additional capex will be required for new orders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Debt:** Management expects debt levels to remain neutral as internal accruals from higher volumes will offset capex spending.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Execution:** Management admitted to a 6–8 month delay in the 16,500-ton press commissioning due to vendor/foundation issues.
- **Cyclicality:** Management acknowledged the inherent cyclical nature of the CV market and the need to diversify into non-auto and other geographies.
- **Skill Availability:** Remains a challenge for the forging industry.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Lakshminarayanan (Tunga Investments):** Asked if guidance could be framed as "outperformance over industry growth" rather than absolute numbers. *Management agreed it is a valid point and will work on that angle.*
- **Nipurn Khemka (CD Research):** Questioned the slow capitalization of assets and high debt. *Management stated that the 16,500-ton press (Rs 240 crore) is a major chunk of uncapitalized assets and that the balance sheet is in good shape.*
- **Ashish:** Asked if the heavy press is a gating capability for non-auto orders. *Management confirmed it opens up a new class of non-auto segments beyond the weight range of auto parts.*
- **Dhruv Sitlani:** Asked about margin evolution. *Management highlighted a potential 4–5% margin expansion possibility through cost reduction (Rs 100 crore on a Rs 2,000 crore base).*
- **Lakshminarayanan:** Asked about manufacturing system homogenization across plants. *Management admitted there is a "long way to go" to homogenize systems.*

### One-line takeaway
MM Forgings reported a strong Q2 with 19.7% EBITDA margins and raised FY27 revenue guidance to >Rs 2,000 crore, driven by a 65% machining mix and robust CV demand, despite a 6–8 month delay in commissioning its new 16,500-ton heavy press.
