# Sharda Motor Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth in revenues and profits, with robust order wins in lightweighting, exports, and premium two-wheeler segments.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management does not provide formal guidance (maintained).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious to positive, supported by healthy industry volume growth, robust demand across segments, and successful new order wins.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Entered the premium two-wheeler segment with a major order; discussed new CAFE 3 norms and BS7 draft standards. The supplier fire and one-time premium freight/raw material issues from last quarter normalized, though non-indexed commodities impacted margins slightly.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Chakan-3 is ramping up; Uttarakhand plant received factory license and SOP remains on schedule; North American genset SOPs on track for Q3 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 1,211.2 crore (YoY growth of 54%)
- **Gross Profit:** INR 233.1 crore (YoY growth of 20%)
- **Value-Added Sales Growth:** 27% YoY
- **EBITDA:** INR 125.7 crore (YoY growth of 24%)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10.4%
- **Profit Before Tax (before exceptional items):** INR 131.2 crore (vs INR 101.9 crore YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 99.4 crore
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** INR 2,222.3 crore (YoY growth of 44%)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** INR 437.0 crore (YoY growth of 14%)
- **H1 FY27 Value-Added Sales Growth:** 18% YoY
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 229.0 crore (YoY growth of 14.8%)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 10.3%
- **H1 FY27 PBT (before exceptional items):** INR 246.3 crore (vs INR 209.6 crore YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 186.0 crore
- **New Strategic Orders (H1 FY27):** $61.5 million in combined lifetime business value
- **Control Arm Order (New Customer):** Annual business value approx $3.9 million; lifetime value $19.5 million
- **Premium Two-Wheeler Emission Order:** Annual business value approx $8.1 million; lifetime value approx $40.4 million
- **Patent Filings:** Cumulative 26 filed (2 added in Q2), 6 granted (2 added in Q2)

### Highlights
- Secured new control arm order from an emerging Indian PV manufacturer leveraging Donghee's global capabilities; SOP planned for Q4 FY28.
- Entered the premium two-wheeler segment with an emission systems order from a leading premium motorcycle manufacturer; SOP planned for Q1 FY28.
- Secured incremental global orders for engineered tubes (agriculture equipment OEM) and low-pressure tubes (construction mining equipment OEM).
- Received the factory license for the new Uttarakhand plant; SOP remains on schedule.
- Chakan-3 lightweighting facility is ramping up in line with customer schedules.
- Filed 2 additional patent applications, taking total filings to 26, and received 2 grants, taking total grants to 6.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they do not provide formal guidance.
- Management expects H2 FY27 growth to be supported by the ramp-up of existing lightweighting orders, additional control arm programs, temperature-controlled tubes, and North American export programs, while noting a high base in Q3 FY26 may moderate YoY industry growth rates.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 industry automobile production increased by 18.6% YoY to 105.6 lakh units; domestic sales grew by 18.2% to 83.9 lakh units; exports increased by 23.9% to 20.9 lakh units.
- Passenger vehicle production grew 24.9% YoY to 16.59 lakh units (utility vehicles up 28.9%); commercial vehicle production grew 33.9% to 3.54 lakh units (LCVs up 32.7% to 2.27 lakh units).
- Two-wheeler production increased 16.5% YoY to 80.95 lakh units (motorcycles up 9.8%, scooters up over 30%); three-wheeler production grew 24.2% to 4.46 lakh units.
- FADA retail data for August and September 2026 showed CNG, LPG, hybrids, and EVs together accounted for ~41.9% and 41% of PV retail sales, nearing parity with petrol (~40.8% and 41.2%).
- CAFE 3 notified in September 2026, applicable from April 1, 2027; draft standards for BS7 published (initial phase proposed for PVs from April 2029).
- WLTP testing applicable from April 1, 2027 for M1 passenger vehicles and M2 vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Value-added sales grew 27% YoY, while gross profit grew 20% YoY; the divergence was due to startup/ramp-up costs for new lightweighting programs, product mix, and non-indexed commodities (consumables, BOPs, inward freight).
- Premium raw material procurement costs and premium freight largely normalized compared to Q1.
- Catalyst and substrate costs remain customer-directed pass-through items.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investments remain modular and linked to confirmed customer programs and requirements.
- Chakan-3 facility is ramping up; Uttarakhand plant received its factory license with SOP on schedule.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management maintains a strong balance sheet focus, prioritizing financial discipline, working capital efficiency, and cash generation.
- Evaluating selective inorganic/acquisition opportunities with strict return on capital employed discipline.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global uncertainties, including trade policy changes, tariffs, and customer investment planning.
- Non-indexed commodity cost pressures impacting margins marginally.
- High base effect from Q3 FY26 potentially moderating YoY industry growth in Q3 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Preet Pitani (InCred AMC) asked about the calculation and quantum of value-added sales and the gap between gross profit growth (20%) and value-added sales growth (27%).**
   - *Management response:* Explained that value-added sales growth is higher due to core emission growth and new lightweighting SOPs, but gross profit growth lagged slightly due to startup costs of new programs, product mix, and non-indexed commodities. Specific absolute numbers for value-added sales were not disclosed.
2. **Manpreet Arora (Arora Wealth Advisors) asked about the status of North American genset manufacturer SOPs in Q3 FY27 and potential EPA norm impacts.**
   - *Management response:* Confirmed that SOPs for North American genset orders are fully on track for Q3 FY27, with no indication of delay or postponement from the customer.
3. **Anubhav Mukherjee (Prescient Capital) asked about timeline expectations for M&A targets.**
   - *Management response:* Declined to provide a timeline, stating they operate within a strict framework of value accretion, technological fit, and integration feasibility without coming under pressure.
4. **Shubham Bhatra inquired about the strategy for the newly entered premium two-wheeler emission segment and expansion plans.**
   - *Management response:* Stated immediate priority is to execute the awarded premium two-wheeler programs using existing R&D and manufacturing setup with incremental investments; commuter segment entry will be considered later.
5. **Mihir Vora (Equirus Securities) asked about export RFQ traction and Euro 7 / BS7 readiness.**
   - *Management response:* Confirmed good demand traction in exports with significant wallet share opportunities post-execution. Noted they are currently developing Euro 7 products with an OEM for exports and engaging in joint development for BS7.

### One-line takeaway
Sharda Motor delivered robust Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue of INR 1,211.2 crore (up 54% YoY) and PAT of INR 99.4 crore, driven by healthy industry demand and new strategic wins in lightweighting, exports, and premium two-wheeler emission systems.
