# NDR Auto Components Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong execution, healthy order book expansion, and new strategic joint ventures.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR247.86 crore
- EBITDA: INR27.47 crore
- PAT: INR19.78 crore
- EBITDA Margin: 11.08%
- Consolidated Order Book: INR1,190 crore (comprising INR1,150 crore from NDR Auto standalone and INR40 crore from NDR Hayashi JV)
- DYA Joint Venture Investment: INR62.96 crore (51% stake by NDR Auto)
- DYA JV Targeted Revenue (FY31): INR800 crore to INR1,000 crore
- Content per vehicle for DYA JV: INR50,000 to INR80,000 (targeting volume of 1 to 1.5 lakh)
- Share of profit from associates: INR5.2 crore

### Highlights
- Consolidated order book expanded to INR1,190 crore, driven by large order wins in seating and other auto components primarily from Maruti Suzuki, Suzuki Two-Wheeler, and new business from Toyota.
- Formed a joint venture with DYA (Korea) where NDR holds 51% with an investment of INR62.96 crore, opening business opportunities with Hyundai Motors alongside Kia. The JV will manufacture full seat assemblies including foams.
- Board approved Brownfield expansions of Gujarat and Kharkhoda facilities for seat frames, trims, and other auto ancillaries.
- Standalone entity will continue to supply seat covers and frames to the JV, which will handle final seat assembly.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains an aspiration to achieve a INR3,000 crore revenue vision by FY30 (aided by organic growth and the DYA JV executing in FY31, or earlier if initiatives accelerate).
- Management expects EBITDA margins to remain sustainable around 11% to 11.5%.
- Organic order book of INR1,150 crore will be fully executed by FY30, with FY29 and FY30 expected to be bigger years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Order wins are predominantly from Maruti Suzuki, Suzuki Two-Wheeler, and Toyota.
- Demand environment is strong with new model pipelines, though slight last-minute OEM delays can occur.
- No exports currently; sales are entirely domestic.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price increases are mostly passed on to customers immediately with no significant lag.
- Other expenses increased due to minimum wage hikes in Haryana (approx. INR2.5 crore impact) and job-work operational expenses in NDR South (approx. INR2.5 crore, offset by corresponding other income).
- Sustainable EBITDA margin targeted at 11%–11.5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Brownfield expansions approved for Gujarat and Kharkhoda facilities.
- Total capital expenditure outlined includes approximately INR60 crore for announced projects plus another INR60 crore for the DYA JV, alongside yearly maintenance capex.
- Current capacity utilization is approximately 90% across plants.
- CWIP stands at INR39 crore, which includes projects for latches, Seat Belt Reminder systems, and other operational capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not explicitly discussed in detail, aside from capex outlays.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Environmental and supply chain challenges noted generally by management.
- Minimum wage hikes in Haryana put upward pressure on other expenses.
- Increased competition in the seating space with new players entering the market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jatin Chawla (RTL Investments) asked about customers for the expanded order book and details on the DYA JV.** Management replied that orders are mainly from Maruti, Suzuki Two-Wheeler, and Toyota, and the DYA JV targets INR800–1,000 crore in FY31 for Kia/Hyundai.
- **Anubhav Mukherjee asked about the operational difference between the standalone business and the DYA JV.** Management clarified that standalone continues to supply seat covers and frames, while the JV does full seat assemblies.
- **Saket Kapoor asked about consolidated P&T differences and other income components.** Management explained that dividends from BSL are eliminated on consolidation, and other income/expenses include job-work operations at NDR South.
- **Tanish Vhora asked about raw material pass-through and other expenses.** Management stated that raw material price hikes are passed on without lag, and other expenses rose due to Haryana minimum wage increases.
- **Pritesh Chheda asked about the capital outlay to reach the INR3,000 crore target.** Management outlined roughly INR60 crore for announced projects and INR60 crore for the JV, plus maintenance capex.

### One-line takeaway
NDR Auto is expanding its manufacturing footprint and partnering with Korea's DYA to capture full-seating opportunities with Hyundai-Kia, supporting its vision of reaching INR3,000 crore in revenue by FY30–FY31.
