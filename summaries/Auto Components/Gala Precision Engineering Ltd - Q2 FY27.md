# Gala Precision Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong order book growth, successful ramp-up of the Chennai facility, and margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 94 crore, up 31% YoY
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 16 crore, up 43% YoY
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 16.76%, up 135 bps YoY
- Net profit (Q2 FY27): Rs 11 crore, up 26% YoY
- PAT margin (Q2 FY27): 11.31%, down 45 bps YoY
- EPS (Q2 FY27): Rs 8.07
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 169 crore, up 26% YoY
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 28 crore, up 35% YoY
- EBITDA margin (H1 FY27): 16.56%, up 110 bps YoY
- Net profit (H1 FY27): Rs 19 crore, up 26% YoY
- PAT margin (H1 FY27): 11.06%
- EPS (H1 FY27): Rs 40.30
- Order book: Grew by 44% YoY, crossing Rs 100 crore in a single quarter
- Chennai plant capacity utilization: Expected to go up to 70% in FY27
- Chennai Phase 1 H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 22 crore
- Solar power plant capex: Rs 6.2 crore; annual cost saving expected at ~Rs 1.35 crore (net of interest)
- Bank finance secured: Rs 58 crore (Rs 15 crore fund-based working capital, Rs 10 crore non-fund working capital, Rs 28 crore term loan)

### Highlights
- Commissioned a >1.2 MW AC open-access solar power plant at Nanded, Maharashtra on October 30, 2026.
- Successfully implemented SAP HANA across all manufacturing plants on October 7, 2026.
- Received customer approvals for critical applications in railway safety, offshore wind, and mining equipment.
- Note on Record: Chennai Phase 1 capacity utilization rising toward 70% in FY27 significantly drives revenue growth and operational leverage, as confirmed by management.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets ~25% sales growth for FY27.
- Management maintains EBITDA margin target range of 17% to 19% for coming quarters.
- Chennai Phase 2 operational target: FY28 Q4 (internally targeting part capacity operation), with new land capacity creating Rs 100 crore capacity.
- FY28 capex planned at ~Rs 40 crore to Rs 45 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Revenue mix for H1: DSS (Disc Spring Segment) ~53%, SFS (Spring & Fastener Segment) ~31%, CSS (Cylindrical Spring Segment) ~16%.
- End-user industry mix (H1): Renewable energy ~42%, Industrial ~33%, Mobility ~25%.
- Exports accounted for ~36% of revenue in H1.
- Electrolyzer order received in June progressing with ~Rs 2 crore sales targeted for FY27; advanced discussions ongoing with 2 other electrolyzer companies.
- Anticipated potential business from electrolyzers, railway safety, and offshore wind of ~Rs 40 crore to Rs 50 crore over the next 2 to 3 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin improved due to lower relative manpower and other expenses, alongside the absence of forex losses seen in H1 FY26.
- Employee cost increased in absolute terms due to additions in market development in Europe and the US, but declined as a percentage of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current annual capacity is ~Rs 450 crore; expected to reach ~Rs 550 crore with Phase 2 Chennai and line balancing at Wada.
- 10-acre land parcel acquired in Wada (MOU signed June 2026) to double capacity to Rs 900 crore in phases.
- Chennai Phase 2 mezzanine floor completion by November end 2026; phase operational by Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Secured bank finance of Rs 58 crore (including Rs 28 crore term loan). Term loan will be used for incremental capex.
- KPMG completed its diagnostic analysis on working capital; internal implementation to start early FY28, with benefits accruing over 1 to 1.5 years.
- Export working capital cycle runs around 90 to 100 days, contributing to higher receivables.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Legal details and final agreement for the 10-acre Wada land parcel experienced some delay due to legal complexities.
- Long gestation periods for aerospace approvals led management to focus on near-term opportunities instead.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Divyansh Gupta (Latent PMS) asked about the revenue contribution of held-back orders from Q1:** Management stated ~Rs 3 crore was included in Q2 revenue.
- **Divyansh Gupta asked about capacity utilization and Chennai Phase 2 timelines:** Management clarified Phase 1 runs near max (~Rs 5-6 crore/month), and Phase 2 will be operational by Q4, adding ~Rs 50 crore capacity.
- **Ahaan (Individual Investor) asked about the electrolyzer revenue potential and cash flow drivers:** Management stated ~Rs 2 crore is expected in FY27, scaling to Rs 40-50 crore over 2-3 years across emerging applications; working capital expanded due to higher export sales share.
- **Vidhi Gandhi (Individual Investor) asked about the drivers of EBITDA margin sustainability:** Management attributed improvement to reduced other expenses/manpower percentages and no forex loss, guiding for 17-19% margins.
- **Lakan Chhabra (Individual Investor) asked about wind sector demand vs 30% growth target:** Management noted Gala's low base and market share gains (5-30%), export of smart fasteners for offshore wind, and introduction of new bolt lines for towers.

### One-line takeaway
Gala Precision delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 31% revenue growth and 43% EBITDA growth, underpinned by strong renewable and industrial demand and the ongoing ramp-up of its Chennai facility.
