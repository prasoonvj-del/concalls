# Schaeffler India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q3 CY26
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit revenue growth across all segments, though margins faced temporary pressure from input cost inflation and adverse sales mix.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 3,005 crore (+27.4% YoY, +12.1% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 18.7% (vs 19.1% in preceding quarter).
*   **PAT:** Rs 370 crore (+21% YoY).
*   **Free Cash Flow:** Rs 41 crore.
*   **Capex (Q3):** Rs 182 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Segmental Performance:** Automotive Technologies revenue grew 35% YoY; Aftermarket (Vehicle Lifetime Solutions) grew 22% YoY; Bearings and Industrial Solutions grew 14.7% YoY; Exports grew 46.3% YoY.
*   **Subsidiary:** KRSV Innovative Auto Solutions reported revenue of Rs 83.7 crore with negative EBITDA.
*   **Portfolio Mix:** Automotive Technologies (37%), Bearings and Industrial Solutions (35%), Exports (17%), Vehicle Lifetime Solutions (11%).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Capex:** Management expects to realize the previously guided Rs 500 crore annual capex; future budgets are under preparation.
*   **Exports:** Management stated this is not a permanent baseline; export visibility is limited to 90-120 day frozen order books.
*   **Margin Recovery:** Management expects to recover 80-85% of input cost inflation; 50% has been recovered to date, with the remainder under negotiation.
*   **Automotive Demand:** Management expects strong demand to continue into 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro:** Indian economy grew 6.4% in Q3; IIP reached 7.7%.
*   **Auto:** Passenger vehicles (+25% growth), commercial vehicles (+21%), and tractors (+7%) showed strong performance.
*   **Industrial:** Strong traction in metal, steel, cement, and wind energy sectors.
*   **Industrial Automation:** Currently ~3% of total revenue; management views this as a strategic growth area.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margin declined QoQ due to input cost inflation (fuel/energy/FX) and adverse sales mix in the mobility segment (e-mobility development costs).
*   **Inflation:** Input price inflation impacted gross margins by ~1.1% of quarterly sales.
*   **Localization:** Traded goods are ~19-20% of revenue; management aims to reduce this by increasing localization to 85%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** 9M CY26 capex stands at Rs 357 crore.
*   **Projects:** Construction continues at Shoolagiri (Phase 2) and Savli (wind bearing expansion).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Increased to ~19% of sales due to planned localization projects and unplanned inventory buildup in the e-mobility sector; management expects correction by December.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Inflation:** Food inflation and rising fuel/energy costs (linked to West Asia crisis) remain concerns.
*   **Visibility:** Limited visibility on export demand beyond 90-120 days.
*   **Working Capital:** Unplanned inventory buildup in e-mobility impacted short-term cash flow.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Export Sustainability:** Management clarified that the 46% YoY export growth is not a permanent baseline and depends on 90-day frozen order books.
*   **Vitesco Merger:** Management confirmed there are no plans to merge Vitesco India with Schaeffler India.
*   **Promoter Stake Sale:** Management clarified that the promoter intent to monetize shares is a minor stake sale to maintain control at 67-68%; it is a governance-related disclosure.
*   **Mobility Margins:** Management explained the margin dip in Mobility Solutions was due to an adverse sales mix, specifically higher development costs for new e-mobility products.
*   **Industrial Automation:** Management noted that while they currently import actuators, they are strategizing to increase local competitiveness in this sector.

### One-line takeaway
Schaeffler India delivered robust double-digit top-line growth driven by strong domestic auto and industrial demand, while actively managing margin headwinds from input cost inflation and working capital expansion.
