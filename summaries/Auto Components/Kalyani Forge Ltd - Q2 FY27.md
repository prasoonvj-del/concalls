# Kalyani Forge Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Auto Components*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted sustained EBITDA margin expansion (4th consecutive quarter >14%) and strategic operational improvements despite temporary revenue dips due to planned maintenance.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 54.4 crore (down 18% QoQ due to planned downtime).
*   **H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 9.39 crore (up 118% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 6.64 crore (up 86% YoY).
*   **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 14.2% (up 158 bps YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 15.3% (vs 11.1% in H1 FY26).
*   **Truck Segment Revenue:** Rs 23.3 crore (up 75% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** ~Rs 60 crore remaining.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Interventions:** Revenue was impacted by the reconditioning of a 2,500-ton press and the relocation of the driveline machining plant to MCD1. Both activities are now complete.
*   **Funding Plan:** Board approved a Rs 45 crore funding plan (Rs 10 crore equity via promoter preferential allotment, Rs 20 crore debt, Rs 20 crore asset/inventory monetization) to support liquidity and order book ramp-up.
*   **Business Mix:** Core segments (Cars, Trucks, Industrial) now account for 97% of sales (up from 82% a year ago). Legacy/non-core business exposure reduced to 4% of sales.
*   **Banking Transition:** Receivables increased due to a banking changeover process; management expects normalization in H2.
*   **Record Check:** The Rs 45 crore funding plan is a new development, consistent with the company's recent strategic shift toward strengthening capital structure.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management targets 15-20% revenue growth on strategic customer accounts over the next two years.
*   **Margin Targets:** Management aims to maintain the current 15% EBITDA margin level, with a medium-term goal of 18% and eventually 20%.
*   **H2 Outlook:** Management expects to return to Q1 revenue levels, targeting 10-20% growth over H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Trucks:** Strong market pull; segment grew 75% YoY and now represents 43% of total sales.
*   **AI Data Centers:** Commenced production of large engine connecting rods for an OEM supplying AI data center power applications.
*   **EV/Driveline:** Products are common to both ICE and EV; management does not view EV-specific parts as a primary focus currently.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Optimization:** Implemented "Vriddhi Council" projects to reduce material consumption and yield losses.
*   **Power/Labor:** Working to optimize sanctioned power loads and improve labor productivity through better machine programming and fixture design.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex Plan:** Rs 30 crore total (Rs 8 crore deployed in H1).
*   **Capacity:** Machining capacity increased by 10%; new Wheel Hub line installed to 30% of planned capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Receivables:** Increased due to banking transition delays; management expects normalization in Q3.
*   **Working Capital:** Targeting a cash conversion cycle of 140-150 days, with a long-term milestone of 120 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Operational Downtime:** Planned maintenance (press reconditioning and plant relocation) caused a sequential revenue decline in Q2.
*   **Legacy Business:** Some legacy customers are slow in lifting orders, with Rs 10-15 crore of receivables tied to these accounts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you provide a roadmap for FY28/29?** A: Management is targeting 15-20% revenue growth on strategic accounts and aims to reach 20% EBITDA margins over the next 1-2 years.
*   **Q: Why did receivables increase to Rs 141 crore?** A: Primarily due to a banking changeover requiring customer system updates; management expects this to normalize in Q3.
*   **Q: Are you diversifying into defense/aerospace?** A: Yes, a product roadmap is in place, but management is being selective to avoid repeating past mistakes of over-diversification.
*   **Q: How will the Rs 45 crore be utilized?** A: ~Rs 20-30 crore for working capital to support revenue growth, with the remainder for capex and general corporate purposes.

### One-line takeaway
Kalyani Forge is prioritizing margin sustainability and core segment growth, utilizing a new Rs 45 crore funding plan to de-bottleneck operations and support a targeted 15-20% revenue growth in strategic accounts.
