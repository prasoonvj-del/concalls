# Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating revenue and EBITDA grew YoY despite headwinds in international travel, air traffic, and supply chains.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Operating Revenue:** Rs 167 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 47 crore (up 3% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 28.12%
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 172 crore (up 10% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 52 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Close to Rs 12 crore (down 14% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT Margin:** Close to 7%
- **Occupancy:** 92%
- **EM Bypass Project Sales:** 33 of 69 apartments sold; average realization ~Rs 20,633 per sq ft; expected cash flows of Rs 70-80 crore during the year (Rs 21 crore already received).
- **Flurys Outlet Count:** 111 outlets (target of 140 outlets by end of FY27, 400 outlets by FY30).
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.12
- **Net Debt-to-EBITDA:** 0.70
- **Mutual Funds Portfolio:** Close to Rs 97 crore.

### Highlights
- Retained India's leading occupancy position at 92% and maintained RevPAR leadership in the upper upscale segment.
- F&B contributed approximately 43% of total revenue.
- Flurys expanded into North India with a new standalone cafe in Gurugram in August; new openings planned in Green Park (September) and Greater Kailash 2 (October).
- Completed implementation of SAP S4 HANA for Finance.
- Pune land FSI increased from 2.5 lakh sq ft to 6.7 lakh sq ft (planned as 2.5 lakh sq ft hotel development and 4+ lakh sq ft residential development).
- Acquired a 15-room property at Vembanad Lake; acquisition of Malabar House in Fort Kochi (17 keys) in progress, expected by October-November 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to double hotels from 42 hotels (2,667 keys) to 87 hotels (6,719 keys) by 2030 through a 2x growth in own assets and 3x+ growth in asset-light models.
- FY27 hotel pipeline: 12 hotels comprising 472 keys, taking the portfolio to 3,149 keys by the end of FY27.
- Flurys targeted to reach 140 stores by end of FY27 and 400 outlets by 2030.
- Capital expenditure (net of EM Bypass contribution) estimated at around Rs 1,500 crore over the next 4–5 years. Operational capex pegged at Rs 40 crore per year, and Flurys at Rs 40 crore per annum.
- Expected ARR growth in high single digits going forward.
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) expected to head past 20% by 2030 (from current 9-10%) as projects like EM Bypass go live.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 marked by a dynamic operating environment with temporary disruptions to international travel and air connectivity due to geopolitical developments (West Asia crisis).
- Domestic travel remained resilient (leisure, domestic corporate movement, weddings, social occasions).
- Domestic passenger traffic was flat; international air traffic into India declined by almost 10% during the quarter. All-India market ADR growth was 6%.
- Upcoming catalysts: BRICS Summit in Delhi, ~40 wedding dates between November and March, Aero Show in Bangalore, and Bharat Mobility Expo.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- High energy costs and supply chain disruptions noted.
- Higher finance costs incurred to propel expansion (Zillion acquisition financing).
- Deferred tax provision of Rs 2.2 crore as the company shifts to the new income tax regime (tax rate to normalize around 30%-35% / 25% from next quarter).
- Other income expected to be around Rs 3.5 - 4 crore on a quarter-on-quarter basis.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total projected capex of ~Rs 1,140 crore across projects (Pune 200 rooms, Navi Mumbai 250 rooms, Vizag 100 rooms, EM Bypass 220 rooms, Jaipur 150 rooms) @ Rs 1.2 crore per room.
- Zillion acquisition cost: ~Rs 210 crore; Juhu renovation: ~Rs 80 crore; Kochi acquisition: ~Rs 64 crore. Total (~Rs 350 crore) funded via EM Bypass inflows (~Rs 350 crore).
- The Park Mumbai at Juhu (78 rooms) targeted for launch in October 2027; acquisition cost below Rs 40,000 per sq ft.
- Vizag 100-room hotel expected to be completed by early 2030.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Favorable debt-to-equity ratio of 0.12 and net debt-to-EBITDA of 0.70.
- Mutual funds holding stands at close to Rs 97 crore, kept liquid to fund inorganic growth and acquisition opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia crisis and oil crisis impacting international travel and air connectivity.
- Subdued domestic air passenger traffic growth during Q1.
- Higher finance and interest costs impacting PAT growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Archana Gode (IDBI Capital) on ADR growth and occupancy:** Management explained that Q1 ADR growth was muted due to West Asia headwinds and flat air traffic, but high single-digit ARR growth is expected ahead, supported by upcoming events, weddings, and stabilization of palace properties.
2. **Archana Gode (IDBI Capital) on Flurys expansion pace:** Management stated that 29 additional outlets are mapped out for the rest of the year (targeting 140 stores), alongside tie-ups with Adani Airports, Phoenix Mills, DLF, and PVR.
3. **Devansh Patel on maturity of properties and stabilization period:** Management noted that resort/new properties take 2-3 years to stabilize, but high-potential markets like Mumbai, Pune, and Kolkata stabilize faster (within year one) due to location strength.
4. **Jayanth Singh on service apartment returns at EM Bypass:** Management confirmed that mixed-use development provides robust cash flows (Rs 300-325 crore expected share), enabling the hotel component to be virtually free and helping drive ROCE past 20% by 2030.
5. **Ramesh Ravikar on PAT contraction despite revenue growth:** Management attributed the 14% PAT decline to higher finance charges from acquisition financing (Zillion) and a one-time deferred tax provision as the company shifts to the new tax regime.
6. **Sahil Mahajan on mutual funds portfolio:** Management clarified that debt funds are held in regular plans to keep capital readily available for inorganic growth opportunities, and net returns are competitive.

### One-line takeaway
Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels delivered stable Q1 performance with 8% revenue growth and industry-leading 92% occupancy, backed by aggressive asset-light expansion plans in Flurys and strong cash flows from its EM Bypass mixed-use project.
