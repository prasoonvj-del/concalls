# Juniper Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating portfolio showed strong resilience with 10% YoY growth in total income and significant improvements in profitability below the EBITDA level.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 258.1 crore (up 10% YoY)
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 249.5 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 91.4 crore (margin at ~35% on total income)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 49.4 crore (up 108% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 37.2 crore (up 121% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 510 crore
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 180 crore
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 70.4 crore (up 173% YoY)
- **Average Room Rate (ARR):** Rs 11,514 (up 8.6% YoY)
- **Occupancy:** 75%
- **Gross Borrowings:** Rs 703 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** Rs 70 crore
- **Net Debt:** Rs 633 crore (Net Debt to EBITDA at 1.4x)
- **Westin Bangalore Stabilized Revenue Target (FY28):** Rs 100 crore to Rs 125 crore at 38% to 40% margin
- **Novotel Imagicaa FY28 Revenue Target:** Rs 75 crore to Rs 80 crore

### Highlights
- Signed a binding MOU in October for the acquisition of Novotel Imagicaa (287 keys) in Khopoli for approximately Rs 248 crore (Rs 80 lakh per key); expected to contribute to top line in Q4 FY27.
- Soft opening of Westin Bangalore Phase 1 (238 keys) occurred in October; walk-ins post-Diwali, online bookings from December 15, 2026.
- Finance costs declined by 50% YoY to Rs 15.3 crore; depreciation and amortizations reduced to Rs 26.7 crore.
- Company plans to proceed with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) compliance dilution in Q3.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in achieving budget set for FY27 with double-digit growth and incremental revenues from Bangalore and Novotel in Q4.
- Corporate-level EBITDA margin target maintained at 42% for full year FY27.
- Aiming to double inventory and EBITDA by FY30-31 via five major projects (totaling Rs 2,000 crore outlay) and selective acquisitions; post-completion ROCE targeted at 19%.
- Intends not to breach a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 2.6x; plans to spend ~25% to 30% of the Rs 2,000 crore capex in the next 18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 is typically a soft quarter for the industry, but the portfolio delivered 9% YoY growth in ARR and 12% growth in RevPAR.
- Corporate travel and F&B supported hotels across Mumbai, Delhi, and Ahmedabad.
- H2 is expected to see strong industry tailwinds, increased social/MICE demand, and higher room rates.
- Mumbai demand remains very strong with rates up to Rs 13,500 - Rs 14,000; management sees no negative impact from T1 terminal operations moving to Navi Mumbai airport.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses increased from Rs 81 crore to Rs 97 crore YoY, partly driven by higher HLP (gas) costs due to the Middle East conflict (impact of Rs 1.5 - Rs 2 crore) and GST ITC credit impact from rooms sold below Rs 7,500 (impact of ~Rs 2 crore).
- Management noted corrective actions are underway to replace gas-fired equipment with electric alternatives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Westin Bangalore Phase 2:** 275 keys under construction (excavation complete, foundation in progress); operational by FY29 (total 513 keys across both phases, combined investment ~Rs 850 crore).
- **Grand Hyatt New Delhi:** ~550 keys across 700,000 sq. ft.; concept design approved, ground breaking expected in Q4 FY27. Features negligible upfront land acquisition cost and a 5-year license fee moratorium.
- **Grand Hyatt Guwahati:** 277 keys including branded residences; civil tendering underway, ground breaking expected in Q3 FY27.
- **Kaziranga Resort:** Luxury wildlife resort under development; completion expected by FY30.
- **Mumbai Commercial Tower:** Construction start/ground breaking expected in early FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total assets stood at Rs 4,343 crore and equity base at Rs 2,940 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Long-term debt maturity profile of 10 to 12 years; AA- credit rating with stable outlook.
- Undrawn credit facility available at Rs 125 crore.
- Reported ROCE is 8%, but adjusted for land fair value and Ind AS accounting adjustments, the portfolio ROCE is ~17%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Gas price volatility affecting HLP costs (mitigated by switching to electric equipment).
- GST input tax credit (ITC) restrictions on rooms priced below Rs 7,500 impacting margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Lokesh Manik (Vallum Capital Advisors) asked about subdued YoY growth at Andaz (Delhi) compared to peers.** Management clarified that Andaz grew 7% (top line from Rs 77 crore to Rs 83 crore), driven by rates rather than occupancy, and noted that peers with higher growth rely specifically on diplomatic/city-centric business.
2. **Heer asked about the timeline and stabilization of Westin Bangalore and brownfield assets.** Management stated Westin Bangalore will take walk-ins post-Diwali, online bookings start Dec 15, 2026, and estimates FY28 revenue at Rs 100-125 crore at 38-40% margins; Novotel Imagicaa consolidation happens in Q4 FY27 with FY28 revenue estimated at Rs 75-80 crore.
3. **Karan asked about synergies and pricing integration for Novotel Imagicaa with the theme park.** Management stated they acquired it as a standalone asset with multiple revenue streams; theme park visitors will be charged market rates during demand periods, though the park helps build base business during downtrends.
4. **Vaibhav asked if Terminal 1 closure in Mumbai impacts Grand Hyatt Mumbai.** Management stated there will be no negative impact as the asset relies on large conventions, BKC proximity, and F&B, and flight shifts will ultimately help Mumbai destination growth.
5. **Geetarth Tandon asked about the status of the ROFO (Right of First Offer) assets (Hyatt Regency Mumbai and Chennai).** Management declined to share specific asset details, reiterating that future acquisitions will be brought to shareholders for decision making, and emphasized that core growth is intact independently via internal pipelines.

### One-line takeaway
Juniper Hotels reported resilient Q2 FY27 performance with 10% total income growth and a 121% surge in PAT, while aggressively advancing its Rs 2,000 crore expansion pipeline across greenfield projects and the newly acquired Novotel Imagicaa.
