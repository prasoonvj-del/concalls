# Mahindra Holidays & Resorts India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Good growth across resort revenue, occupancy, upgrades, and average unit realization, despite near-term capacity constraints from renovations.

### Key numbers
- **Sales value (inc. upgrades):** INR 141 crore, up 6% YoY
- **Upgrade value:** INR 83 crore, up 26% YoY
- **Average Unit Realization (AUR, inc. upgrades):** INR 13.4 lakh, up 43% YoY
- **Organic and digital member additions:** 66% of total additions in the quarter
- **Resort network / Keys:** 5,762 keys across 106 resorts (net reduction of 103 keys in Q2 due to 6 exits)
- **Occupancy:** 75.6%, up 220 bps YoY
- **Resort revenue:** Up 15% YoY (highest in last 8 quarters)
- **TRevPAR:** INR 8,777 per room, up 6% YoY
- **H1 Capex:** INR 312 crore (vs INR 166 crore YoY)
- **HCRO (Holiday Club Resorts) revenue:** EUR 36.5 million, up 8% YoY
- **HCRO EBITDA:** EUR 1.7 million (vs EUR 1.3 million YoY)
- **HCRO Net Loss:** EUR 0.3 million
- **HCRO loss in Indian books (inc. interest & forex):** INR 18 crore (vs INR 27 crore YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 19.7 crore, up 17% YoY
- **Standalone Total Income:** INR 395 crore, up 4% YoY
- **Standalone EBITDA:** INR 124 crore (vs INR 141 crore YoY), down 12%
- **Standalone PAT:** INR 43 crore (vs INR 52 crore YoY), down 16%
- **Consolidated Total Income:** INR 819 crore, up 9% YoY
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 194.8 crore, up 5% YoY
- **Deferred revenue (September end):** INR 5,828 crore
- **Cash balance (September end):** INR 1,329 crore
- **Keys under renovation:** 442 keys

### Highlights
- Launched Keystone proposition 9 months ago; 10-year Ivory product is most preferred, followed by 5-year product (both comprise ~80% of portfolio).
- Exited 6 resorts (net reduction of 103 keys in Q2) based on guest feedback/ratings using AI-based sentiment tools. Management targets exiting ~650 keys in total this year (after exiting ~400 keys last year).
- Implementing AI for paperless check-ins, WhatsApp billing, and guest operations.
- Strategic review of HCRO is in progress.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to add about 1,000 gross keys in the next 6 months.
- On track to reach 10,000 keys by FY30 (currently 85% visibility on the funnel).
- Capital allocation: ~70% towards new resorts and ~30% towards improving existing resorts.
- Expects renovated rooms under 442 keys to return to operations in Q3 and Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Limited impact on occupancy from West Asia crisis due to higher airfares, but compensated by driving holidays.
- Non-member, corporate, and MICE segments are growing areas.
- Referrals account for 40% to 50% of new sales in any given quarter.
- 12-month member retention rate improved to ~11%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA and PAT declined due to growth-linked costs, renovation drag (442 keys non-operational), initial losses from new properties, and GST/solar subsidy changes.
- Cost of acquisition as a percentage has reduced YoY.
- Sales force headcount additions will remain flat; incremental marketing spends expected for new resort openings and Club M rebranding.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex stood at INR 312 crore, funded via internal accruals.
- 8 ongoing greenfield and brownfield projects.
- Projects coming up: Ganpatipule (Q3 end/Q4 beginning), Kumbhalgarh renovation (Q3), Poovar (opened), Jaisalmer (Q3), Gir (Q4 end/Q1 early).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash balance at INR 1,329 crore as of September end.
- Relies entirely on internal accruals for expansion and transformation.
- Forex impact on HCRO was minimal in H1 compared to adverse Euro movement last year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inflation and geopolitical uncertainties have increased project delivery costs and caused delays in openings.
- 442 keys under renovation are non-revenue generating currently.
- Challenging Finnish economic environment continues to weigh on HCRO performance (net loss of EUR 0.3 million).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Yash Mehta (Unnamed entity) asked about the Keystone portfolio mix and customer preferences.** Manoj Bhat replied that the 10-year Ivory product is the most preferred, followed by the 5-year product, together comprising ~80% of the portfolio. Upgrades grew 25-27% YoY.
2. **Soumya Raghuwanshi (Nirma Securities) asked about the positioning and differentiation of Signature Resorts.** Manoj Bhat stated it targets the luxury segment for "family and tribe" (groups of friends/families traveling together), features rich experiences/local design, and targets an ARR of INR 20,000.
3. **Dhvaneet Savla (Savla Family Office) asked if the 10,000 key target by 2030 is net and if HCRO strategic review has progressed.** Manoj Bhat confirmed the 10,000 key target is net, with 85% visibility on the funnel. He stated the HCRO strategic review is still in progress.
4. **Shreyans Gathani (SG Securities) asked if sales force capacity needs to expand proportionally with member additions.** Manoj Bhat answered that sales force headcount will remain roughly constant, with marketing spends increasing primarily for new resort openings and Club M rebranding.
5. **Varun Mishra (SK Capital) asked about standalone margin compression despite higher revenue.** Manoj Bhat explained it was due to investments in future capabilities, revenue loss from closed renovation rooms, initial unprofitability of new properties, and regulatory/subsidy changes.

### One-line takeaway
MHRIL delivered a mixed standalone financial performance with lower EBITDA and PAT due to growth-linked costs and room renovations, offset by robust 15% resort revenue growth, higher occupancy (75.6%), and strong upgrade momentum.
