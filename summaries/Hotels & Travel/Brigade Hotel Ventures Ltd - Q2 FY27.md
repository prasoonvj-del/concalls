# Brigade Hotel Ventures Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Rate-led growth, margin expansion, and doubled PAT driven by tight cost control and lower financing costs.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 142 crore (up 10% YoY from Rs 130 crore)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 52 crore (up 27% YoY from Rs 41 crore); EBITDA margin at 36.7% (up from 32%)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 22 crore (up 105% YoY from Rs 11 crore)
- **Average Daily Rate (ADR):** Rs 7,876 (up 11% YoY from Rs 7,100)
- **Occupancy:** 75.2% (flat YoY, +1% domestic demand)
- **RevPAR:** Rs 5,922 (up 10% YoY)
- **Finance Costs:** Rs 9.7 crore (down from Rs 13.5 crore YoY)
- **Net Cash Position:** Rs 29 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Capex Deployed:** Rs 484 crore till September 2026 out of Rs 3,600 crore planned (including Rs 84 crore in H1 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 Metrics:** Total Income Rs 273 crore (up 7% YoY); EBITDA Rs 98 crore (up 18% YoY, margin 35.8%); PAT Rs 39 crore (up 122% YoY); ARR Rs 7,559 (up 9%); Occupancy 75.4%; RevPAR Rs 5,702 (up 9%)

### Highlights
- Growth was rate-led; active portfolio strategy increased portfolio ADR.
- F&B revenue returned to double-digit growth (up 10% YoY), recovering from a muted Q1 impacted by the West Asia crisis and dry event calendar.
- GST 2.0 impacted EBITDA margins by 1.4% for Q2 FY27 and 1.6% for H1 FY27.
- Bangalore market showed strong performance with ARR up 8% YoY to Rs 9,400, occupancy at 81%, and RevPAR up 12% to Rs 7,600.
- Other markets saw ARR grow 13% YoY to Rs 6,300, with occupancy moderating to 70% and RevPAR up 8%.
- Courtyard by Marriott Kochi Infopark completed its first full quarter post-rebranding with ARR growing 17%.
- Renewable energy accounts to 67% of total energy needs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains mid-teens growth guidance for EBITDA and overall revenue for the full year.
- Expects to spend Rs 400 to Rs 450 crore in H2 FY27 on capex (including unidentified acquisitions) and Rs 700 crore for FY28.
- Aiming to reach 3,300 keys by FY31 (from 1,700 keys under development), increasing luxury/upper upscale mix from 14% to 31% by FY29 and 38% by FY31.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remains strong, contributing 76% of customers.
- MICE activity picked up meaningfully in Q2, aided by events like the 'Electronica' conference and 'Space Expo' in Bangalore.
- October trends and forward inquiries indicate a strong Q3 supported by festive demand, the start of the wedding season, and healthy MICE calendars.
- Industry outlook points to stronger growth in H2 FY27 as corporate travel normalizes and international arrivals gradually recover.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expanded from 32% to 37% (EBITDA margin at 36.7%) due to tight cost control where employee and operating costs grew below revenue growth.
- Finance costs declined over 28% YoY to Rs 9.7 crore due to debt repayment from IPO proceeds and refinancing unsecured debt at 10.25% to secured debt at 8.1%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total planned capex is Rs 3,600 crore, of which Rs 484 crore has been invested.
- 45-key Courtyard by Marriott Chennai at World Trade Center on track to open in H2 FY27.
- Fairfield by Marriott in BIAL and Brigade Valencia scheduled to open towards the end of FY28.
- Actively evaluating acquisition opportunities in prime business and leisure markets outside South India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position stood at Rs 29 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Rs 468 crore of IPO proceeds deployed toward debt repayment.
- Peak net debt to EBITDA in FY28/FY29 projected around 3.5 to 4, with a DSCR maintained around 2 to 2.5.
- Other non-current financial assets increased to Rs 61 crore, representing surplus cash parked in long-term fixed deposits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rebranding impact: Rebranding of Four Points to Courtyard in Kochi temporarily dropped occupancy from mid-80s to 40% (recovering to 66% in Q2), dragging portfolio averages.
- GST 2.0 continues to create a headwind, impacting margins by 1.4% in Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Karan Kamdar (Choice Institutional Equities) on occupancy drops in non-Bangalore assets:** Vinay Gupta explained it was due to price positioning for the 2-year-old ibis Styles in Mysore and rebranding/re-positioning in Kochi, which displaced low-paying accounts. Management expects stabilization over the next 2-3 quarters.
- **Karan Kamdar on Brigade Enterprises' new hotel announcements (Hyatt Regency) and BHVL participation:** Nirupa Shankar clarified that these assets (KIADB land and mixed-use township SPVs) are being developed by Brigade Enterprises/SPVs, and BHVL may acquire/bring them into its portfolio at a later stage once current pipeline and future cash flows allow.
- **Adhidev Chattopadhyay on FY27/H2 RevPAR and capex guidance:** Nirupa Shankar stated RevPAR is expected to grow in the mid-teens range in H2, and capex for the remainder of the year is revised slightly from Rs 450 crore to around Rs 400 crore due to timing.
- **Parth Mandavgane on peak net debt to EBITDA:** Ananda Natarajan guided that peak net debt to EBITDA in FY28/29 will be slightly less than 4 (around 3.5), with a DSCR of 2 to 2.5.
- **Heer Gogri on M&A updates:** Nirupa Shankar noted that while acquisitions are a top priority and they are actively evaluating opportunities outside South India, documentation and due diligence might roll the finalization into the next 1-2 quarters.

### One-line takeaway
Brigade Hotel Ventures delivered a rate-led Q2 FY27 with 10% revenue growth, a margin expansion to 36.7%, and doubled PAT, backed by strong MICE recovery, robust domestic demand, and lower finance costs.
