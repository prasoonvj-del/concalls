# Mach Travel Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong YoY revenue and profit growth driven by the successful scaling of new business verticals beyond core MICE.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 144.33 crore (up 538% YoY from Rs 22.62 crore; up 73% QoQ from Rs 83.25 crore)
- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) / Total Ticketed Volume (TTV): Approx. Rs 252 crore to Rs 259 crore for the quarter
- EBITDA: Rs 8.92 crore (up 437% YoY from Rs 1.66 crore; up from Rs 3.81 crore QoQ)
- EBITDA margin: 6.09% (compared to 7.17% in Q1 FY26 and 4.54% in Q4 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 6.17 crore (up 307% YoY from Rs 1.52 crore; up from Rs 1.80 crore QoQ)
- PAT margin: 4.22% (compared to 6.54% in Q1 FY26 and 2.15% in Q4 FY26)
- Corporate travel vertical: Onboarded 100+ corporate clients since launching in April
- MICE programs (Oceania): Worth around Rs 32 crore, covering 950-1,000 delegates
- Punjab Yatra program: Valued at around Rs 92 crore, covering approximately 1.85 lakh yatris (approx. 1,100 people traveling daily; Rs 4,950 per person for a 2-night, 3-day yatra)
- IRCTC transaction: Picked up a group travel excursion business worth Rs 75 lakh to Rs 1 crore

### Highlights
- Transformed from a 22-year-old pure MICE player into a broader travel platform adding corporate travel, B2B, leisure, government/institutional projects, and a B2C OTA platform.
- Launched corporate travel vertical in April using a self-booking tool (SBT) with maker-checker technology, automated invoicing, and MIS.
- Secured IRCTC empanelment (approx. 2-year duration, extendable) for back-end travel and tourism services (rail tours, charter trains, customized tours, holiday packages).
- Expanded operating presence across Noida, New Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Bhubaneshwar, and Ahmedabad.
- B2C OTA platform (`Machtravel.com`) is currently in the testing phase and scheduled for launch in September (pushed from August).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of achieving revenue of around Rs 500+ crore in FY27.
- Current pace of growth is expected to be sustained through the balance of the financial year.
- Aiming to cross Rs 500 crore and subsequently Rs 1,000 crore as key long-term milestones.
- Eligible to move to the main board in the next 12 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Corporate travel provides recurring, 365-days-a-year revenue visibility unlike project-based MICE.
- Q2 is generally heavier than Q1 for travel, though corporate travel can dip during festival/holiday periods like October, November, and December.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- YoY margin compression (EBITDA margin at 6.09% vs 7.17% YoY) reflects investments in new verticals, team expansion, and tech infrastructure.
- Sequential margin improvement (EBITDA margin up from 4.54% to 6.09%) demonstrates improving operating leverage as the business scales.
- Management stated they do not want to burn money to raise volumes, but aggressive market positioning may occasionally require compromising on markups to scale the size of the cake.
- Improved scale is expected to increase buying and negotiating power, thereby expanding markups over time.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opening additional offices towards South India.
- Developing and testing the B2C app (`Machtravel.com`) leveraging a captive audience of about 3,000 people traveling weekly/1.25 lakh travelers annually.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-free company with about Rs 3 crore of debt.
- Management noted working capital challenges and payment cycle issues due to rapid growth, and is evaluating taking bank credit facilities in the form of overdrafts (OD) if needed.
- Punjab Yatra payment cycle is approximately 7 days after travel, with payment expected within 15 days from travel.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Working capital pressures and payment cycle delays arising from high revenue growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sudhir Bheda (Bheda Family Office) asked about working capital management given the rapid revenue scale-up.** Management responded they currently have minimal debt (approx. Rs 3 crore) and IPO funds were utilized, but acknowledged working capital needs are rising and they may utilize bank overdraft (OD) facilities. *(Did not dodge)*
- **Urmish Shah asked about the sustainability, contract size, and repeat nature of corporate travel contracts.** Management explained that contracts involve a self-booking tool (SBT) with maker-checker approvals, standard billing cycles (1st–15th and 16th–30th), and provide recurring 365-day revenue visibility. *(Did not dodge)*
- **Nishita asked about the revenue bifurcation between core MICE and new verticals, and margin recovery timeline.** Management stated core MICE contributed roughly Rs 100 crore, while corporate travel and Punjab Yatra contributed about 10% to 15% each; margins will improve gradually as operating leverage and buying power scale up. *(Did not dodge)*
- **Mrunal Shah asked about the milestones to track over the next 12-24 months.** Management highlighted achieving the Rs 500 crore and Rs 1,000 crore revenue milestones, launching the B2C app (`Machtravel.com`), scaling IRCTC and leisure businesses, and transitioning to the main board in 12 months. *(Did not dodge)*
- **Ashok Shah asked about the unit economics and repeat visibility of the Punjab Yatra contract.** Management clarified the contract is for 1.85 lakh yatris at Rs 4,950 per person (totaling Rs 92 crore), with ~1,100 people traveling daily, and is expected to run through November. *(Did not dodge)*

### One-line takeaway
Mach Travel Solutions reported a stellar Q1 FY27 (revenue up 538% YoY to Rs 144.33 crore) driven by successful diversification beyond MICE into corporate travel, institutional projects like Punjab Yatra, and tech investments, while guiding for Rs 500+ crore revenue in FY27.
