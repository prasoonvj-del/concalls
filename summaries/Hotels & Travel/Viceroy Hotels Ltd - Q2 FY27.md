# Viceroy Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Work put into hotels over the last 2 years has started showing clearly in numbers despite the convention center closure.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained near-term EBITDA margin target above 30% and long-term benchmark of 40%.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more confident as West Asia conflict impact recovered and occupancy successfully transitioned into rate growth (combined ADR up 9% QoQ).
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: One-time marketing expense of INR66 lakh for Vayu restaurant launch; RBI repo rate hike in October prompting debt reduction.
- Last quarter's promises: Rights issue of INR105.8 crores completed successfully; Marriott convention center expansion remains on track for December completion.

- **Cash and Equivalents:** INR110.5 crores at end of H1.

### Highlights
- Completed INR105.8 crores rights issue, oversubscribed 1.19x, bringing public shareholding in line with SEBI's 75% requirement; promoters' holding changed from 84.11% to 74.03%.
- Repaid INR80.25 crores of debt in the first week of October using rights proceeds.
- Opened Vayu, a rooftop restaurant bar at Courtyard, in August.
- Marriott convention center expansion to 20,000 square feet across 3 banquet halls remains on track for December completion.
- F&B revenue declined 20% in Q2 due to the convention center closure (estimated EBITDA impact of INR2 crores per quarter).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets consolidated EBITDA margin above 30% in the near term and 40% in the long term.
- Expects Marriott convention center to reopen by end of December, with full ramp-up within 9 months.
- Reaches full complex potential expected realistically in FY29, targeting complex ADR of INR9,000 to INR9,500.
- Marriott room, restaurant, and lobby renovation (Phase 3) planned for FY28, starting from April.
- Greenfield Courtyard at Madhapur construction expected to start in next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Hyderabad September RevPAR up 19% to 20%, driven by global capability centers (GCCs) accounting for 37% of July-September leasing.
- Industry RevPAR grew around 18% YoY with double-digit room rate increases by September.
- Marriott and Courtyard complex runs at ~85% domestic and 15% foreign mix; Executive Apartments run at 70%-80% domestic and 20%-30% foreign mix.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- One-time marketing expense of INR66 lakh incurred for Vayu restaurant launch.
- Marriott Executive Apartments and Courtyard operate at EBITDA margins close to 37%-40%, while Marriott operates closer to 30% due to higher F&B mix.
- Blended interest rate on remaining debt is 9.15%, with scheduled loan tenures ranging from 10 to 12 years. Next financial year interest cost expected around INR17 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Phase 1 (Courtyard rooms) and Phase 2 (Convention center) total spend expected to be around INR110-115 crores.
- Marriott room upgrade budget remains at INR40 crores.
- Evaluating running/near-complete hotels, brownfield assets, and government land parcels for expansion with return discipline.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating profit before working capital changes more than doubled to INR24 crores in H1.
- Balance rights issue proceeds (~INR25 crores) earmarked for convention center capex and daily operations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary loss of banqueting business at Marriott during renovation (INR2 crores EBITDA impact per quarter).
- Conflict in West Asia kept international arrivals subdued in H1.
- RBI repo rate hike in October raised borrowing cost considerations, making debt reduction priority.

### One-line takeaway
Viceroy Hotels delivered a strong Q2 and H1 FY27 with a 61% surge in H1 total income and 81% growth in EBITDA, driven by Courtyard's ramp-up and strong executive apartment performance, even as Marriott's convention center remained closed for expansion.
