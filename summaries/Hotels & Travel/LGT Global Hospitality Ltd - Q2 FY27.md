# LGT Global Hospitality Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, margin improvement, geographic expansion, robust booking pipeline.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 89.68 cr (YoY +35%) [Note: Investor deck shows Rs 8,967.94 cr; likely transcription error]
- EBITDA: Rs 3.81 cr, margin 4.24% (vs 3.33% in previous half)
- PAT: Rs 1.93 cr (YoY +111%)
- Branch network expanded 50%
- 51% stake acquired in Traflix (trade fairs) and Yajat Travel Solutions (corporate travel)
- Collection Hotelstay platform: 30+ units

### Highlights
- Geographic expansion into Nagercoil, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Indore, Baroda, Delhi, Mumbai, Pune; planning Lucknow, Gujarat (Ahmedabad, Rajkot), West Bengal.
- Acquisitions: Traflix (trade fair delegation leader, e.g., CPHI Munich, ChinaPlas), Yajat Travel Solutions (30+ yr corporate travel veteran), Collection Hotelstay (luxury hotels/resorts/apartments).
- Technology: Developing omnichannel app/platform for Gen Z leisure travelers; upgrading CRMs, mid-office systems.
- Segment focus: Pharma, infrastructure, IT driving corporate travel; leisure demand strong in tier-3 cities.
- 5x growth tagline targeting Rs 500-600 cr revenue in 2-3 years (management aspiration).
- Note: Call transcript reports H1 FY27 revenue of Rs 89.68 cr vs investor deck (12-Nov-2026) of Rs 8,967.94 cr; likely transcription error.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects margin improvement in H2 FY27 as fuel costs ease with Middle East stabilization and pent-up leisure demand emerges.
- Confirmed bookings robust, pipeline strong.
- Acquired entities (Traflix, Yajat, Collection) on path to profitability at entity level.
- No specific numerical guidance for FY27/FY28 provided.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Post-COVID travel revival strong; pent-up demand especially in leisure segment.
- Pharma, infrastructure, IT segments performing well for corporate travel/MICE.
- High-end individual travel (westbound to Europe/USA) impacted by Middle East geopolitical issues but recovering.
- Tier-3 cities underserved, high aspirational travel potential.
- US market returning.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel cost pressure from Middle East situation impacted H1 margins; oil prices now stabilizing.
- EBITDA margin improved to 4.24% from 3.33% in previous half.
- Further margin enhancement expected in H2 from lower fuel costs and volume-driven operating leverage.
- Investments in talent, technology, infrastructure weighing on near-term profitability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Branch network increased 50% recently; new offices in tier-3 cities (Nagercoil, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Indore, Baroda, Lucknow planned).
- Collection brand: 30+ units (hotels, resorts, furnished apartments), growing further.
- Technology capex: app/platform development, CRM, mid-office systems.
- Acquisition model: 51% stake with experienced professionals; capital allocated from proceeds, retained earnings; not draining capital.
- No specific capex guidance in rupees.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail. Management mentions "working capital light" platform model.
- No figures disclosed for debt, net debt, or working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical instability (Middle East) affecting fuel costs and high-end outbound travel.
- Sensitivity to natural calamities and economic conditions.
- Execution risk in scaling acquisitions (Traflix, Yajat, Collection) and technology platform.
- Rising competition in travel services.
- Transcription discrepancy in reported financials vs investor deck.

### One-line takeaway
LGT Global Hospitality is pivoting from a South India-centric travel agent to a national omnichannel platform via acquisitions (trade fairs, corporate travel, luxury stays) and technology, targeting 5x growth, but near-term margins hinge on geopolitical fuel costs and integration execution.
