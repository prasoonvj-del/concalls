# LGT Global Hospitality Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong revenue and net profit growth while expanding its network and scaling digital platforms, despite fuel and geopolitical cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 89.68 crores (Note: company deck states Rs 8,967.94 crore, but transcript says Rs 89.68 crores; using transcript number as per rules: Rs 89.68 crores, registering 35% YoY growth)
- Revenue growth YoY: 35%
- EBITDA: Rs 3.81 crores
- EBITDA Margin: 4.24% (improving from 3.33% in the previous half)
- EBITDA Margin change YoY: +91 bps (calculated from 4.24% vs 3.33%)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 1.93 crores, registering 111% growth YoY (Note: deck states Rs 195.78 crore, transcript states Rs 1.93 crores)

### Highlights
- Expanded network by entering new markets: Nagercoil, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Indore, and Baroda.
- Acquired majority 51% stakes in specialized trade fair and travel platforms (including Traflix and Yajat Travel Solutions) to capture B2B and MICE segments.
- Continuing transition toward becoming a national player, expanding beyond South India into locations like Delhi, Pune, and Mumbai.
- *Note vs Record:* While the investor deck on Nov 12, 2026, reported revenue of Rs 8,967.94 crore and PAT of Rs 195.78 crore, the conference call transcript explicitly stated revenue of Rs 89.68 crores and PAT of Rs 1.93 crores.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to benefit from easing cost pressures, stabilization in the Middle East, lower fuel costs, and a robust confirmed booking pipeline.
- Management mentioned a long-term "5x growth" aspiration / tagline, aiming for revenues of Rs 500-600 crores over a 2-to-3-year period under favorable market conditions.
- Guidance status: Maintained / none explicitly revised other than long-term aspirations.

### Demand & industry commentary
- High-end individual travelers (FIT) were temporarily impacted by price escalation and Middle East fuel issues, but pent-up demand is expected to emerge.
- Pharma, infrastructure, and IT industries are performing well and driving corporate travel demand.
- Tier-3 cities are showing higher spending power and aspirational travel demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel costs and price escalations in the Middle East impacted H1 margins.
- Investments were made in people, technology infrastructure, CRMs, and mid-systems to scale platforms like Collection and Hotelstay.
- Stabilization of geopolitical issues in the Middle East is expected to lower fuel costs and support margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Scaling internal accommodation brand "Collection" (hotels, resorts, furnished apartments), targeting growth beyond 30-plus units.
- Omnichannel expansion focusing on both offline branch networks in tier-3 cities and online/app-based digital platforms.
- Branch opening costs are kept low (around 3 to 4 lakhs maximum per branch) to achieve a quick break-even.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Using a 51% stake acquisition and collaboration model rather than heavy greenfield capex to preserve capital and avoid draining cash.
- Working capital requirements are kept light through collaboration models.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Tourism and travel are sensitive to natural calamities and geopolitical/macro conditions (e.g., Middle East situation causing fuel price volatility).
- High competition in travel and hospitality requires continuous differentiation and talent retention.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Analyst (Dave) asked about future plans for recent acquisitions (Traflix and Yajat).** Management explained they acquired a 51% stake to leverage experienced professionals in trade fairs and ticketing, noting trade fairs provide steady cash flow and will contribute significantly once environments settle.
2. **Analyst (Jigar Doshi) asked about the use case for digital/internet platforms.** Management stated they are building an omnichannel platform targeting Gen Z and quick travelers for outbound leisure and holidays globally, aiming to improve accountability and reduce offline dependency.
3. **Analyst (Mahesh Bhatt) asked about challenges affecting margins and recovery signs.** Management replied that oil prices are cooling down, geopolitical situations are settling, and pent-up demand will drive volume and operating margin enhancement in H2.
4. **Analyst (Suraj) asked about 5x projections and revenue targets.** Management clarified that the "5x growth" tagline targets Rs 500–600 crores over 2-3 years, built upon existing infrastructure once fragile market conditions stabilize.
5. **Analyst asked about branch expansion metrics and break-even.** Management noted that opening a branch costs around 3-4 lakhs maximum, allowing quick break-even once rolling volumes pick up.

### One-line takeaway
LGT Global Hospitality delivered 35% revenue growth and doubled PAT to Rs 1.93 crores in H1 FY27, expanding its footprint and acquiring specialized B2B platforms while positioning for margin recovery in H2.
