# Devyani International Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong growth momentum, record operating EBITDA, and successful store expansion despite macro headwinds and inflationary pressures.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 1,519 crore (+10.4% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA (pre-Ind AS):** INR 125 crore (+34% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** INR 229 crore (+18% YoY).
*   **PBT:** INR 5.7 crore (vs. loss in previous year).
*   **H1 PBT:** INR 29 crore (vs. loss of INR 24 crore).
*   **Gross Margin:** 68.5% (+70 bps YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 8.2% (vs. 6.8% YoY).
*   **KFC Revenue:** INR 642 crore (+12.3% YoY).
*   **Pizza Hut Revenue:** INR 184 crore (nearly flat YoY).
*   **Own Brands Revenue:** INR 95 crore (+11% YoY).
*   **International Business Revenue:** INR 509 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Store Expansion:** Added 70 net new stores (68 in India). Total footprint: 1,923 stores in India; 2,325 stores globally.
*   **Brand-wise Network:** KFC (844 stores), Pizza Hut (632 stores), Own Brands (225 stores), Costa Coffee (202 stores).
*   **Merger Update:** Management stated they have received CCI approval for the Sapphire Foods merger and are awaiting SEBI no-objection before proceeding to NCLT.
*   **Operational Focus:** Management highlighted a revamped Business Development (BD) structure and a shift toward value-based pricing (e.g., INR 99 and INR 149 meal combos at KFC).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Store Additions:** Management reaffirmed full-year guidance of 200 to 220 net new store additions.
*   **Merger Timeline:** Management expects to close the Sapphire Foods merger by the end of the current financial year.
*   **SSSG:** Management targets Q3 SSSG to be slightly better than Q2.
*   **Own Brands:** Management aims for Biryani By Kilo brand contribution to reach 14%-15% in the short-to-medium term.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **SSSG Performance:** India portfolio (excluding Pizza Hut) SSSG ranged from 4% to 9%.
    *   KFC: 4.4%.
    *   Pizza Hut: -1.4%.
    *   Biryani By Kilo: 8.1%.
    *   Vaango: 6.9%.
    *   Costa Coffee: 9.9%.
*   **Macro Environment:** Management noted a "K-shaped" economy with pressure on discretionary spending due to food/fuel inflation and AI-related job losses.
*   **Consumer Sentiment:** Management observed that consumers are moving toward premiumization on a selective basis and remain price-sensitive.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Management reported steep inflation in raw materials (chicken, edible oils, dairy) and LPG prices (nearly double YoY).
*   **Wages:** Steep minimum wage hikes noted in Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra, and Telangana.
*   **Pricing:** Company took a "very small and calibrated" 1% to 2% price hike YTD.
*   **Mitigation:** Gross margin improvement driven by procurement initiatives and cost-saving measures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Management stated the revamped BD structure is "firing now" and executing well.
*   Management emphasized that KFC expansion will continue as planned, citing strong demand for chicken in India.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Heightened inspection drive from food authorities across the country disrupted store operations.
*   **Macro:** Rising interest rates and food inflation are expected to keep discretionary spending under pressure.
*   **Post-Merger:** Management anticipates a potential slowdown in store openings post-merger as new management teams settle.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Saurabh Kundan (Goldman Sachs):** Asked why KFC lagged peers. Management attributed this to seasonality (Navratri/Durga Puja timing) and emphasized that they are building for long-term sustainability rather than near-term metrics.
*   **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay Global):** Asked if inflation is fully baked in. Management stated current inflation is baked in, but noted potential for minor wage-related increases; price hikes are being taken on a staggered basis.
*   **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital):** Asked about KFC SSSG sustainability. Management stated current SSSG is sustainable and they are targeting improvement in Q3.
*   **Jignanshu Gor (Bernstein):** Asked about store expansion pace vs. headwinds. Management reaffirmed commitment to 100-110 KFC store additions for the year, citing strong chicken demand.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational execution and margin expansion despite macro headwinds, with a clear focus on long-term store expansion and the upcoming Sapphire Foods merger.
