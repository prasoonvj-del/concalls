# Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Operating EBITDA grew 11% YoY with margins expanding 100 bps, backed by 93% occupancy and an aggressive asset-light pipeline.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Long-term target updated to 100 hotels and 7,500 keys by FY31 (up from 87 hotels and 6,719 keys by 2030); Flurys FY27 target maintained at 140 outlets (with ~Rs 100 crore revenue target), while 2030 Flurys store target was stated as 250–300 outlets (vs 400 targeted earlier).
- **Tone and demand view:** Demand outlook for H2 strengthened on expected wedding demand (36 of 52 scheduled weddings in H2), MICE events (Aero Show, Bharat Mobility Expo), winter travel, and spiritual tourism.
- **New topics or risks:** Signed MoU with Andhra Pradesh government for a new 100-room Vizag luxury hotel, securing Rs 10–20+ crore in subsidies/SGST refunds over 5 years. Fire NOC received and BMC NOC expected shortly for Zillion Hotels in Juhu, Mumbai.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Completed acquisition of Purity at Vembanad Lake. Flurys opened 6 new outlets in H1 (reaching 112). SAP S/4HANA Finance and Material Management modules fully went live.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Operating Revenue:** Rs 180 crore (up 7% YoY)
- **Q2 Operating EBITDA:** ~Rs 54 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **Q2 Operating EBITDA Margin:** 30.27% (up 100 bps YoY)
- **Q2 PAT:** ~Rs 15 crore (vs Rs 16 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 Operating Revenue:** Rs 347 crore (up 7% YoY)
- **H1 Operating EBITDA:** >Rs 100 crore (up 7% YoY)
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 26 crore (vs Rs 30 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 Occupancy:** 93% across owned hotels
- **H1 ARR:** Rs 7,774; **RevPAR:** Rs 7,219
- **Flurys H1 Revenue:** Rs 42 crore (Q2 EBITDA margin 8%, H1 margin ~9%)
- **H1 Management Fee Income:** Rs 7 crore (2% share of managed hotels revenue)

### Highlights
- Operating EBITDA crossed Rs 100 crore for the first time in company history in H1.
- EM Bypass mixed-use development Phase 1 is nearly 100% sold at an average price of Rs 19,500/sq ft. Management expects Rs 325 crore as its share from apartment sales over the next 3 years (Rs 38.5 crore received so far). Phase 2 launch scheduled for Feb–Mar 2027 with a projected 15%+ price increase.
- Portfolio stands at 43 hotels with 2,729 keys as of H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Target to reach 100 hotels and 7,500 keys by FY31 (adding 57 hotels and 4,771 keys over 6 years).
- Adding 7 hotels (321 keys) in FY27 and 11 hotels (558 keys) in FY28.
- Management fee revenue share targeted to grow from 2% to 4% over the next 3 years as managed keys scale 3x to 3,627 keys.
- Flurys expected to reach 140 outlets by FY27-end (~Rs 100 crore revenue) and scale to 250–300 outlets by 2030.
- Long-term ROCE expected to expand from sub-10% to past 20% by 2030/2031 as new projects open.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ICRA projects industry hotel demand to grow 7%–9% against supply growth of 5%–6% in FY27.
- H2 expected to be strong due to MICE, winter travel, 36 remaining weddings (out of 52 total for FY27), and spiritual tourism.
- Spiritual/faith tourism in India is currently a USD 65 billion market, growing at 10%–12% annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin expanded 100 bps YoY to 30.27% in Q2.
- PAT was impacted by higher depreciation (from addition of Patiala, Chettinad Palace, and Flurys store ROU assets) and higher interest expenses from acquisition borrowings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **H2 FY27 Capex:** ~Rs 100 crore (including Juhu renovation, Vizag startup capex, Rs 15–20 crore for Flurys, and Rs 10 crore operational capex).
- **FY28 Capex:** ~Rs 150–200 crore overall (Juhu, Vizag, operational) plus Rs 30–40 crore for Flurys expansion.
- **Own properties under development (400+ keys):** ~220 keys at Kolkata (EM Bypass), ~80 keys at Juhu Mumbai, and 100 keys at Vizag.
- **Vizag project:** 100-room luxury hotel costing Rs 150 crore; construction starting in 7–10 days; targeted completion H2 2030.
- **Juhu property (Zillion Hotels):** ~90% stake acquired for Rs 188 crore; project completion expected by end of CY2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Gross Debt:** Rs 300 crore; **Mutual Fund Investments:** ~Rs 100 crore; **Net Debt:** ~Rs 200 crore.
- **Gross Debt to EBITDA:** 0.23x; **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.1x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher finance and depreciation costs dragging net profit margins during the expansion phase.
- Moderation in domestic and international air travel experienced in H1 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Archana Gude (IDBI Capital):** *Status and aggressive pace of Flurys expansion to 140 stores?*  
  Management stated a new COO and team are in place, with 20 store signings already executed and expansion entering Jaipur, Lucknow, and Bangalore alongside Delhi-NCR and Bengal.
- **Yash Solani (Kadam Family Office):** *Why did PAT drop 9.9% while EBITDA grew 11%?*  
  Management clarified PAT fell due to higher interest on debt raised for Zillion (Rs 188 crore) and Purity (Rs 21 crore) acquisitions, and higher depreciation on newly added assets; Q3/Q4 operational strength is expected to offset these costs.
- **Kaushik Poddar (KB Capital Markets):** *Flurys profitability margins and store performance in Mumbai (e.g. Santa Cruz airport outlet)?*  
  Management shared that Flurys H1 margin is ~9% (targeting 12% in H2), explaining that Santa Cruz traffic temporarily dropped due to airlines moving to Navi Mumbai airport, where Flurys is in talks with Adani Group for space.
- **Shiv (Individual Investor):** *Rising debt trends, timeline for PAT translation, and potential stock buyback?*  
  Management emphasized net debt to equity is low at 0.1x, ROCE will cross 20% by FY31, and did not address buybacks, focusing instead on underlying asset expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels delivered strong Q2 EBITDA growth and 93% occupancy while aggressively expanding its asset-light hotel network, Flurys retail footprint, and EM Bypass residential cash flows.
