# Srigee Dlm Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-13 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** H2 FY26 & FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited steady growth, improved operational efficiencies, profitability, and strong demand.

### Key numbers
- **FY26 Revenue:** INR 72.31 crore
- **FY26 Total Income:** INR 75.76 crore
- **FY26 EBITDA:** INR 9.23 crore
- **FY26 EBITDA Margin:** 12.18%
- **FY26 PAT:** INR 6.87 crore
- **FY26 PAT Margin:** 9.06%
- **H2 FY26 Total Income:** INR 54.34 crore
- **H2 FY26 EBITDA:** INR 6.89 crore
- **H2 FY26 PAT:** More than doubled to INR 5.53 crore YoY
- **Polymer Compounding Capacity:** ~50 metric tonnes per month from 1 twin-screw extruder; planned expansion to 150 metric tonnes at the new facility
- **Polymer Compounding Revenue (FY26):** INR 11.257 crore
- **Mobile Assembly Volumes:** Samsung assembling >10 lakh phones; Srigee's monthly sales increased from INR 50 lakh to INR 1 crore
- **Top Customer Concentration:** Reduced from 95% to 91% in FY26
- **New Facility Plot Size:** 10,850 square meters (Plot R11A)
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** ~INR 100 crore
- **FY28 Revenue Aspiration:** INR 200 crore to INR 250 crore
- **New Facility Peak Revenue Potential:** ~INR 350 crore
- **Planned Capex:** ~INR 24-25 crore near March '27; total project cost around INR 50 crore (including land, debt, equity, and asset sales)
- **Cost of Debt Guidance:** 8% to 9% (initial discussions with ICICI bank at 8% to 8.25%, bank quoting up to 9.50%)

### Highlights
- Evolved from plastic injection molding into an integrated design-led manufacturing partner (extrusion-based polymer compounding, precision plastic manufacturing, tool room, die manufacturing, mobile phone assembly).
- Key customers include Symphony, Havells, LG, Yamaha, Neelkamal, and Elentec.
- Placed the 2,000-square-meter Ecotech-10 facility on hold to directly focus on developing the larger 10,850-square-meter facility (Plot R11A).
- Polymer compounding business promoted under the 'Polymos' brand name.
- Top 10 customer contribution reduced to 91% from 95% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 turnover to reach around INR 100 crore.
- Management aspires to achieve INR 200 crore to INR 250 crore between April 2027 and March 2028.
- Peak turnover potential from the new R11A facility is guided at least at INR 350 crore.
- Target for commercial production/commissioning at the new facility is set after August 15, before Diwali, in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Manufacturing environment remains favorable due to domestic consumption, outsourcing, Make in India, PLI schemes, and ODM demand.
- Cooler business remains seasonal, with June, July, and August typically slower. Mobile phone demand is growing rapidly.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion driven by higher contributions from mobile assembly and other manufacturing, along with back-end integration.
- In-house polymer compounding saves 10% to 20% on raw material costs compared to outside procurement. Internal captive usage is 25% to 30%.
- Russia-Ukraine war impact: Polymer is a crude oil by-product. To mitigate price volatility and lag in quarterly customer price revisions, management increased polymer purchases in March. Future POs incorporate updated market prices (e.g., PP prices increased from INR 100 to INR 140-150).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dropped the smaller Ecotech-10 plot to concentrate funds on the 10,850-sq-m R11A plot.
- Total project cost estimated at INR 50 crore, funded by IPO equity (INR 17 crore), debt, and proceeds from selling older, smaller units.
- Planned capex of around INR 24-25 crore by March '27.
- Current capacity utilization is over 100%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- 50% of IPO funds utilized by March 31, 2026; balance to be fully deployed in H1 FY27.
- In talks with ICICI Bank for debt funding to support the new plant.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High customer concentration (top customers still contribute 91%).
- Geopolitical tensions impacting crude-oil-derived polymer raw material prices.
- Potential construction delays for the new facility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shubham Vishwakarma (SV Family Office):** Asked about revenue expectations and benefits of the Polymos polymer compounding segment. *Management answered that capacity will expand from 50 to 150 metric tonnes upon moving to the new facility, yielding a 3x sales target, and providing a double benefit of 10% captive cost savings plus trading margins.*
2. **Kunal:** Inquired about the key drivers of H2 FY26 performance and margin priorities for FY27. *Management stated that mobile assembly and other income drove PAT growth, and guided for INR 100 crore turnover in FY27 while maintaining positive margins.*
3. **Sajan:** Asked about peak revenue potential and net asset turn for the INR 25 crore capex. *Management replied that peak utilization would yield roughly INR 200 crore from that specific capex tranche, and added that older smaller units would be sold to fund the INR 50 crore overall project.*
4. **Rohan Soni:** Questioned the impact of the war on business. *Management explained it caused a spike in polymer prices, which they countered by aggressively purchasing inventory in March before quarterly price resets took effect.*
5. **Akshay:** Queried about total facility potential versus listed peers. *Management stated they are being conservative with INR 350 crore potential on 10,850 square meters compared to peers achieving INR 500 crore on 7,500 square meters.*

### One-line takeaway
Srigee DLM reported robust H2 FY26 performance and is consolidating operations into a larger 10,850 sq.m facility (Plot R11A) to alleviate 100%+ capacity constraints, targeting INR 100 crore revenue in FY27.
