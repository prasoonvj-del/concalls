# InterGlobe Aviation Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27

**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line revenue growth, yield improvement, and robust cash balance were offset by high fuel prices, currency depreciation, and a net loss of 19 billion rupees.

### Key numbers
* **Total Income:** 243 billion rupees, up 24% YoY.
* **Yield Growth:** Increased by over 34% YoY.
* **PRASK (Passenger Revenue per Available Seat Kilometer):** Grew 31% YoY.
* **Cargo Revenue Growth:** Up 37% YoY.
* **EBITDAR:** 25 billion rupees (margin of around 11%), compared to 11 billion rupees (6% margin) in Q2 FY26.
* **Net Loss:** Reported net loss of 19 billion rupees (compared to a loss of 26 billion rupees in Q2 FY26). Excluding MTM and hedging impact, net loss was around 7 billion rupees (compared to a net profit of around 1 billion rupees in Q2 FY26).
* **CASK ex-fuel ex-forex MTM:** INR 3.45, up approximately 15% YoY.
* **Load Factor:** Declined by 2 percentage points YoY.
* **Closing Fleet:** 442 aircraft as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
* **New CEO & Leadership:** Willie Walsh joined as CEO; Kiran Thadimarri joined as CFO.
* **Widebody Operations Exit:** Concluded widebody damp-lease operations (Norse wet lease) effective November 1, 2026, which management expects to be accretive to spreads.
* **Fleet Deliveries & Expansion:** Inducted 22 aircraft via GIFT City entity; redelivered 10 aircraft and 2 damp-leased aircraft during the quarter. Announced daily direct flights from Mumbai to Dar es Salaam starting November 2026.
* **Loyalty Program:** IndiGo BluChip completed 2 years in October, approaching 20 million members with over 30 partners. Ancillary revenue per passenger grew from INR 525 to INR 590 YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
* **Q3 FY27 Capacity (ASK):** Expected to grow in early single digits YoY (driven by higher domestic seasonal demand offset by international cuts).
* **Q3 FY27 PRASK:** Expected to grow by more than 25% YoY.
* **Q3 FY27 Load Factor:** Management expects load factor to be flat YoY.
* **FY27 Capacity (ASK):** Expected to grow in early single digits YoY.
* **FY27 CASK ex-fuel ex-forex MTM:** Expected to increase in high single digits to early double digits range YoY.
* **FY28 Capacity (ASK):** Growth guidance maintained in the mid-teens YoY. Steady-state non-fuel CASK inflation (ex-INR depreciation) expected in the mid-single-digit range.
* **Long-Term Target:** Management remains committed to reaching 300 billion ASKs by FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Domestic & Global Demand:** Industry seat deployment in Q2 fell ~5% YoY across the industry due to seasonal softness. Demand remains resilient into Q3 across domestic festive peak periods.
* **Fuel & Crack Spreads:** High Singapore jet fuel index (60% higher YoY in Q2) and elevated crack spreads (averaging 60%–65% vs historical 20%) continue to press operating costs globally.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Fuel CASK:** Increased 69% YoY, driven by elevated Singapore jet fuel benchmark and 9% depreciation of the Indian Rupee over 12 months.
* **Government Fuel Support:** Government 25% fuel price cap expired on June 8, 2026; post-June 8 fuel purchases returned to market pricing, moderated slightly by select state VAT reductions.
* **Non-Fuel Escalation:** Capacity rationalization led to fixed-cost under-absorption, combined with annual contractual escalations and USD/INR depreciation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Aircraft Orders & Widebody Strategy:** Committed to existing fleet orders, including A350 deliveries scheduled for 2028. Using XLR aircraft to preserve slots at international airports like Amsterdam.
* **Airport Expansion:** Shifted select operations to Navi Mumbai airport to support long-term capacity and allow redevelopment of Mumbai Airport Terminal 1.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **Total Cash Balance:** 527 billion rupees, comprising free cash of 399 billion rupees and restricted cash of 128 billion rupees.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Continued geopolitical volatility affecting flight operations, routes, and operating costs.
* Indian Rupee depreciation impacting USD-denominated operating expenditures.
* Unpredictable jet fuel prices and elevated refining crack spreads.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Binay Singh (Morgan Stanley):** Asked about widebody exit impact and Q3 load factor. Management stated widebody termination will be spread-accretive and Q3 load factors are expected to remain flat YoY.
* **Arvind Sharma (Citi):** Clarified the sequential movement in fuel CASK. CFO explained Q1 benefited from a government fuel cap (25% cap above March prices) through June 8, after which fuel was bought at market prices.
* **Krupashankar (Avendus):** Inquired if widebody long-haul strategy compromises short-haul low-cost operations. CEO emphasized short-haul core will remain low-cost and uncompromised.
* **Prateek Kumar (Jefferies):** Asked if exiting widebody damp-leases affects European airport slot retention or delivery schedules. Management confirmed XLRs will maintain the Amsterdam slot and A350 delivery schedules remain on track for 2028.
* **Jinesh Joshi (PL Capital):** Questioned achieving the FY30 target of 300 billion ASKs given slowing near-term growth. Management reiterated commitment to long-term targets via international expansion and incoming aircraft deliveries.

### One-line takeaway
IndiGo is relying on pricing power, disciplined capacity rationalization, and robust cash reserves (INR 399 billion free cash) to navigate elevated fuel crack spreads and Rupee depreciation while preserving long-haul expansion plans for 2028.
