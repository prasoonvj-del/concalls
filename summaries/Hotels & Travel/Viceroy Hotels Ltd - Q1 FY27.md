# Viceroy Hotels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-07 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and EBITDA growth driven by asset enhancement, operational improvements, and strong demand despite renovations.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** INR44.9 crores (up 77% YoY from INR25.4 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR11.8 crores (up 144% YoY from INR4.8 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 26.3% (expanded 725 bps YoY from 19%)
- **Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR1.4 crores (vs. net loss of INR3 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **Consolidated PAT Margin:** 3.2% (vs. -11.9% in Q1 FY26)
- **Combined Occupancy:** 76.25% (vs. 53.65% in Q1 FY26)
- **Marriott Occupancy:** 72.04% (vs. 59.96% in Q1 FY26)
- **Courtyard Occupancy:** 83.65% (vs. 38.31% in Q1 FY26)
- **Combined ADR:** INR6,107 (vs. INR6,952 in Q1 FY26)
- **Marriott ADR:** INR6,188
- **Courtyard ADR:** INR5,985
- **Combined RevPAR:** INR4,657 (up 24.85% YoY from INR3,730)
- **Marriott RevPAR:** INR4,457 (up 5.7% YoY)
- **Courtyard RevPAR:** INR5,006 (vs. INR2,545 in Q1 FY26)
- **Room Revenues (Marriott & Courtyard):** INR19.6 crores (up 38.9% YoY from INR14.1 crores)
- **Food & Beverage Revenues (Marriott & Courtyard):** INR11.8 crores (up 15.1% YoY from INR10.3 crores)
- **Marriott Executive Apartments (MEA) Room Revenues:** INR8.5 crores (vs. INR7.1 crores)
- **Marriott Executive Apartments F&B Revenues:** INR3.3 crores
- **Marriott Executive Apartments ADR:** INR13,342 (up 7.5% YoY)
- **Marriott Executive Apartments Occupancy:** 94% (vs. 83% in Q1 FY26)
- **Marriott Executive Apartments RevPAR:** INR12,519 (up 21.1% YoY)
- **Standalone Debt:** INR220 crores (Net Debt: INR180 crores)
- **Consolidated Debt:** INR259 crores (Net Debt: INR220 crores)
- **Blended Interest Rate:** 8.7%
- **Capital Work-in-Progress (Consolidated):** INR8 crores

### Highlights
- Courtyard Phase 1 renovation completed, bringing all 168 keys back into operation and normalizing occupancy.
- Marriott convention center taken offline in the first week of April for Phase 2 upgradation, temporarily constraining banquet capacity.
- Marriott Executive Apartments contributed for a full quarter, showing strong performance with 94% occupancy.
- Depreciation increased to INR5 crores (vs. INR3.4 crores) and finance costs rose to INR5.4 crores (vs. INR1 crore) due to Phase 1 capitalization and acquisition financing.
- Promoter shareholding remains unpledged.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of progressing towards an EBITDA margin above 30% in the near term and a long-term benchmark of 40%.
- Courtyard ADR targeted to reach late 6,800 to 8,500 levels over the next 2 financial years (potentially 9,000-9,500 by FY28).
- Occupancy across Courtyard targeted at 80% to 85% levels over the next 2 financial years.
- Phase 2 renovation (Marriott rooms and convention center) expected to be completed by December (Q3) of FY27.
- Greenfield Courtyard at Madhapur (180-200 keys, project cost INR120-130 crores) expected to start construction in Q4 and go operational by FY29-2030.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian hospitality industry fundamentals remain positive, supported by rising business travel, MICE activities, domestic tourism, and Hyderabad's emergence as a tech/commercial hub.
- Hyderabad market expected to grow ADRs at an early double-digit level of 10% to 12%.
- Long-stay extended accommodation is a high-demand product with limited supply competition.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Marriott management fee structure: 1% to 1.5% on total revenue and 6% to 7% on Gross Operating Profit (GOP).
- Room gross margins are significantly higher at 80%-85%, while F&B gross margins are in the 45% to 50% range.
- EBITDA displacement for FY27 due to Phase 2 convention center closure is estimated at close to INR10 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital work-in-progress stands at INR8 crores at a consolidated level.
- Greenfield Courtyard project at Madhapur is in the approval stage under Telangana's new tourism policy, with construction expected to start in Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Monthly debt repayments are INR3.25 crores (~INR39-40 crores per annum).
- Current debt-equity ratio is close to 1, with headroom to take another INR100 crores of debt.
- Applied to stock exchanges for a rights issue to reduce promoter shareholding below 75% as mandated by SEBI by October; proceeds will be used to repay debt and keep limits open for Greenfield/future expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical conflicts ("war") temporarily impacted foreign/dollar demand and international business mix in Q1.
- Closure of the Marriott convention center temporarily constrained banqueting and event revenue streams.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Animesh Jain (Dalal & Broacha):** Asked about the 12.15% drop in combined ADR and impact of Phase 2. Management explained that lower ADR was due to inventory normalization and convention center closure, compensated by higher occupancy from group/airline/army segments, which will reverse once the convention center reopens.
2. **Madhav Agarwal (Retail Investor):** Inquired about Courtyard ARR and occupancy targets. Management stated occupancies will be maintained at 80-85% while ADRs are targeted to rise to 8,500+ (and up to 9,000-9,500 by FY28) post-convention center reopening.
3. **Prashant Kshirsagar (Nirmal Bang):** Asked for standalone/consolidated debt details and rights issue status. Management confirmed standalone debt at INR220 crores (net debt INR180 crores), consolidated debt at INR259 crores (net debt INR220 crores), and confirmed the rights issue is aimed at complying with the 75% public float norm and reducing debt.
4. **Siya (Analyst):** Asked if distressed NCLT assets are being evaluated. Management noted that the hospitality sector currently lacks distress, making it hard to find good NCLT assets, so they are focusing on strategic integration of running or brownfield assets.
5. **Saumya Raghuvanshi (Nirva Securities):** Inquired about the drop in EBITDA margin from Q4 to Q1 and sustainability of 30%. Management reiterated that Q1/Q2 are seasonally quieter, but strong Q3/Q4 performance will easily drive margins above 30% for the full year.

### One-line takeaway
Viceroy Hotels delivered a robust Q1 FY27 with a 77% surge in revenue and a 144% jump in EBITDA, driven by strong occupancies and asset stabilization, even as Phase 2 renovations temporarily impacted ADRs and banqueting capacity.
