# Royal Orchid Hotels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth and operational expansion, but profitability impacted by higher financial costs, IndAS accounting, and GST input tax credit losses.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 107 crore (up 36% YoY).
*   **Total Revenue:** Rs 115 crore.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 33 crore (up 39% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 30.7% (vs 30% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 6.4 crore (vs Rs 10.9 crore YoY).
*   **Occupancy (JLO Hotels):** 70%.
*   **Occupancy (Managed/Franchise):** 60.8%.
*   **ADR (JLO Hotels):** Rs 6,233 (vs Rs 5,488 YoY).
*   **ADR (Managed/Franchise):** Rs 4,300 (vs Rs 4,031 YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Growth Drivers:** Revenue growth attributed to the operationalization of the new 'ICONIQA' property.
*   **Portfolio Expansion:** Added 5 hotels with 237 keys during the quarter.
*   **Asset-Light Strategy:** Management continues to focus on management contracts, franchising, and revenue-share arrangements.
*   **Brand Strategy:** 'ICONIQA' is being positioned as an upper-upscale brand to drive premiumization; 'Lakshminarayan Palace' (Vadodara) was recently added under this collection.
*   **Operational Metrics:** 20% of rooms contribute to 80% of revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Medium-Term:** Management remains confident in the hospitality sector, citing rising domestic travel and infrastructure growth.
*   **Targets:** Aiming for an ROCE of 20%+ in the next year as 'ICONIQA' stabilizes.
*   **Pipeline:** 50+ hotels signed (11,000+ keys) to be opened in the next 12–24 months.
*   **Management Fees:** Management aims to double the current fee income in as short a time as possible, though they declined to provide a specific date for hitting Rs 150 crore in fees.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Seasonality:** Q1 is historically the weakest quarter for business hotels; Q3/Q4 are typically stronger.
*   **External Factors:** Occupancy in April/May was impacted by global flight cancellations due to geopolitical conflicts, affecting inbound travel.
*   **Competition:** The 'ICONIQA' property faces a cluttered market with ~1,000 new keys added by competitors in the immediate vicinity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Profitability Drag:** PAT decline driven by higher financial costs, depreciation, IndAS impacts, and the ramp-up phase of new leased assets.
*   **GST Impact:** A change in GST regulations (5% output tax without input tax credit) resulted in a Rs 2.5 crore loss in input tax credits for the quarter.
*   **Employee Costs:** Increased to 23% of revenue, attributed to new wage codes, annual increments, and team strengthening. Management expects this to remain range-bound (20-23%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Current Capacity:** ~7,000 rooms.
*   **Strategy:** Shifting focus toward larger hotels (80-120 keys) to improve yield and operational efficiency.
*   **ICONIQA:** Management expects the property to break even at ~Rs 85 crore annual revenue; they are targeting Rs 100 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **ROCE:** Currently at 17-18%.
*   **Business Model Shift:** Moving from purely asset-light (management contracts) to revenue-share and lease models, which involves higher upfront costs and IndAS accounting impacts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **GST Regulation:** The inability to claim input tax credits on the 5% GST output slab is a significant cost burden; management is seeking mitigation solutions.
*   **ICONIQA Ramp-up:** The property is in a gestation phase; management requested patience for 2-3 quarters for it to stabilize.
*   **Geopolitical Risk:** Continued impact of global conflicts on international travel and inbound tourism.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Why is PAT declining despite strong revenue growth? **Management:** Attributed to IndAS accounting, higher financial costs, and the GST input tax credit loss. They advised investors to look at non-IndAS numbers for a true picture.
*   **Analyst:** When will the "needle move" on profitability? **Management:** Stated they are in a "churning stage" and expect profitability to improve once 'ICONIQA' stabilizes and new properties come on stream.
*   **Analyst:** Why did ICONIQA occupancy drop sequentially? **Management:** Explained that Q1 is seasonally weak for business hotels and the property was impacted by flight cancellations in April/May.
*   **Analyst:** Is the Rs 2.5 crore GST loss a recurring issue? **Management:** Confirmed it is an ITC loss that must be written off, and they are making representations to the government.

### One-line takeaway
Royal Orchid is in a transition phase, scaling its network and premiumizing its portfolio, but near-term profitability is pressured by accounting adjustments, GST regulatory changes, and the gestation period of new assets like ICONIQA.
