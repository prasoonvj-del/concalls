# Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong RevPAR outperformance (30% YoY), EBITDA margin expansion to 46%, diversified revenue growth, pipeline progress, and balance sheet strengthening.

### Key numbers
- Operating revenue: Rs 411.9 crore (Rs 4,119 million), +33% YoY  
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 187.7 crore (Rs 1,877 million), +36% YoY  
- Operating EBITDA margin: 46% (+118 bps YoY)  
- Profit after tax: Rs 94.6 crore (Rs 946 million), +27% YoY  
- H1 FY27: Operating revenue +30% YoY, Operating EBITDA +38% YoY, PAT +72% YoY to Rs 143.3 crore  
- Owned domestic hotels RevPAR: +30% YoY (ADR +30% to Rs 25,046; India headline occupancy 69%, same-store >70%)  
- Suite occupancy: +700 bps YoY (paid occupancy mid-40s)  
- Net Promoter Score (H1): 87 (13 pts above APAC luxury average)  
- RevPAR index vs luxury market: 1.46x (premium ~Rs 4,500)  
- Direct booking share (brand.com): 15.1% of room revenue (vs 10.5% H1 FY26)  
- Net debt: Rs 1,215 crore (Sep 2026); Net debt/LTM EBITDA: 1.4x (vs 1.6x Mar 2026, 3.7x Mar 2025)  
- Finance cost (Q2): Rs 38.4 crore (stable QoQ); H1 finance cost -37% YoY to Rs 77.7 crore  
- Effective tax rate H1: 16% (deferred tax asset for Coorg); FY27 expected in 20s  

### Revenue mix
- Room revenue: Rs 204.1 crore (+39% YoY)  
- F&B revenue: Rs 145.6 crore (+21% YoY) – outlet 56%, banquet/MICE 44%  
- Management fees: Rs 28.9 crore (+76% YoY) – 85-90% flow-through to EBITDA for new managed openings  
- Other operating services (wellness, retail, club, transport): Rs 33.3 crore (+25% YoY)  
- Non-room revenue share (H1): 52% of total revenue  

### Highlights
- 2nd consecutive quarter of 35%+ EBITDA growth; H1 margin expansion 246 bps  
- Broad-based demand: corporate, leisure, MICE, celebrations, events; domestic +30%, international returning (low double-digit growth ex-BRICS)  
- BRICS Summit drove exceptional performance at The Leela Palace New Delhi; structural tailwind from India’s events/MICE calendar (Bharat Mandapam, Yashobhoomi)  
- Coorg Forest Sanctuary (rebranded 8 Jul): ADR doubled vs pre-acquisition; EBITDA breakeven; wellness itineraries launched  
- Three palaces (New Delhi, Jaipur, Chennai) awarded MICHELIN Keys 2nd consecutive year; Leela ranked #2 hotel brand globally (Travel + Leisure 2026)  
- City hotels H1 occupancy 74% (vs 69% PY); resort same-store occupancy near 60%; Jaipur H1 >60% (traditionally winter-only)  
- 8 new F&B venues launched in past year; Azulian House (botanical dining) >90% covers from non-residents  
- Management portfolio ramp-up driving asset-light earnings growth  
- Over 1,000 keys under development across marquee destinations  

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains mid-to-high teens EBITDA growth for H2 FY27 (on high base), implying full-year EBITDA significantly above initial mid-teens guidance  
- Q3 on track for double-digit RevPAR growth; major events (Bloomberg convention), festive weekends, international conferencing supportive  
- Corporate rate negotiations (CY27) targeting mid-to-high double-digit growth; 60-70% of corporate rates shifted to dynamic pricing  
- FY30 aspiration: ~5,200 keys (50:50 owned:managed), EBITDA ~Rs 2,000 crore  
- Dubai JV: EBITDA breakeven; renovation start early FY28 (Apr 2028), 12-month timeline; residence sales plan (2-3 years) unchanged  
- BKC Mumbai: Lease deed executed Sep 2026; shareholder approval sought for FSI purchase (50:50 JV with Brookfield); long-term plan to divest office stake, retain 50% hotel stake  

### Demand & industry commentary
- Luxury demand broadening and deepening; supply constrained in key markets  
- India emerging as preferred venue for global summits (G20, BRICS, AI Impact Summit, Energy Week) – structural rate tailwind  
- Premiumization broad-based: suite occupancy +700 bps, higher premium category sales  
- International inbound still below pre-COVID; significant upside potential  
- Domestic leisure strong: staycations driving city hotel weekends, resorts becoming all-season destinations  
- Events/MICE underpin weekday city demand; weddings/social celebrations drive year-round resort demand  
- Meaningful occupancy headroom across portfolio allows volume growth alongside rate  

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin 46% (+118 bps YoY) driven by operating leverage, pricing discipline, cost efficiency  
- H1 flow-through ~60% excluding ESOP and Coorg ramp-up  
- Payroll increase due to: (1) new headcount for value drivers (Arq clubs, Azulian House), (2) ESOP expense recognition started this quarter  
- Excluding above, payroll growth ~10-11% for H1  
- Depreciation +24% YoY (Coorg addition, value driver investments)  
- Share of JV loss (25% Dubai Palm Jumeirah) improved QoQ; asset at high occupancy, ADR lagging on low international tourism  

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 5 contracted owned pipelines (Agra, Srinagar, Ayodhya, Ranthambore, Bandhavgarh): total capex ~Rs 1,432 crore; spent Rs 150 crore; FY27 spend ~Rs 450 crore; FY28 ~Rs 600 crore; balance thereafter  
- FY27 openings: The Leela Jaisalmer (luxury desert resort), The Leela Luxury Residences Mumbai (new vertical) – pre-opening phase, teams onboard  
- CY27 end: Srinagar, Bandhavgarh (advanced stages, mock-up rooms, civil work fast-track)  
- CY28: Agra (piling/excavation done, shell/core starting), Ayodhya (shell/core stage), Sikkim (main block/villas civil done), Ranthambore (fort wall stabilization)  
- CY30: Tadoba (survey, architect appointment, design development underway)  
- BKC Mumbai: Committed capex Rs 800 crore (Rs 400 cr equity, Rs 400 cr debt) unchanged; FSI purchase requires shareholder approval  
- Renewable energy projects advancing in Chennai and Coorg; Aujasya wellness facility in Bengaluru underway  

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt Rs 1,215 crore (Sep 2026); Net debt/LTM EBITDA 1.4x (down from 1.6x Mar 2026, 3.7x Mar 2025)  
- Debt maturity >10 years; AA-stable credit rating  
- Borrowing costs reduced 30 bps from mid-Oct 2026 via lender renegotiation  
- IPO proceeds used for debt reduction; finance cost -37% YoY in H1  
- Strong balance sheet provides capacity for growth opportunities while maintaining discipline  

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Dubai JV: ADR recovery dependent on international tourism return; geopolitical uncertainty persists  
- Coorg still ramping; not yet contributing meaningful EBITDA  
- High H2 base (strong FY26 H2) may moderate YoY growth rates  
- BKC project contingent on shareholder approval for FSI purchase and subsequent office stake divestment  
- International inbound recovery slower than domestic; Incredible India campaign effectiveness uncertain

### One-line takeaway
Leela delivers another quarter of industry-leading RevPAR growth (30%) and EBITDA margin expansion (46%), driven by structural pricing power, diversified revenue mix, and expanding pipeline, with balance sheet strength (net debt/EBITDA 1.4x) supporting FY30 target of 5,200 keys and Rs 2,000 cr EBITDA.
