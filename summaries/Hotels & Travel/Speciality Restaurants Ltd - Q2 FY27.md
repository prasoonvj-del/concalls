# Speciality Restaurants Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy YoY growth in revenue, operational EBITDA, PAT, and same-store sales, alongside profitable expansion plans.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone revenue from operations:** Rs 129.78 crore, up 18.24% YoY (vs Rs 109.76 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Operational EBITDA:** Rs 10.242 crore, up 39.44% YoY (vs Rs 7.47 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA margin:** 8.03%, up from 6.81% YoY.
- **Pre-Ind AS company level EBITDA margin:** 7.90% (vs 6.52% last year).
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 6.94 crore, up 45.75% YoY (vs Rs 4.76 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Same-store sales growth (SSSG):** 8.1% in Q2, 11.32% in Q1, and 9.8% in H1 FY27.
- **Delivery mix:** 32% in Q2 FY27 (vs 27.4% in Q2 FY26); Dine-in mix: 68% (vs 72.6% in Q2 FY26).
- **Total outlets:** 125 outlets as of September 30, 2026 (118 company-owned and operated, 7 franchise-owned company-operated including subsidiary).
- **Liquor mix:** 8% to 9% of total revenues.
- **Average daily sales per outlet (Asia Kitchen):** Rs 2 lakh to Rs 2.5 lakh per day (throughput of Rs 60 lakh to Rs 75 lakh per month).
- **Restaurant level EBITDA (Asia Kitchen):** 18% (gross margins ~76%, staff costs 20%-22%, rentals 18%, operating expenses 11%, depreciation 4%).

### Highlights
- Delivered strong growth across standalone revenue, operational EBITDA, and PAT.
- Expanded during H1 through brands like Walters, Sweet Bengal, Gong, Asia Kitchen, and Siciliana, alongside renovations and brand repositioning.
- Delivery mix increased as consumer habits shift toward a hybrid model (delivery customers order 4 times a week; aggregators report 75% newcomers and 25% loyal customers).
- Maintained a kitchen-within-kitchen strategy (delivering Mainland China, Asia Kitchen, and Haka from the same unit) to optimize costs without extra capex or staff overheads.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is very optimistic about H2 FY27, noting it is seasonally the best quarter for the hospitality/restaurant business.
- Planned openings in H2 FY27 across Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, Bangalore, Kolkata, and Dubai.
- Long-term target: Expand to 200 physical outlets by FY2030, targeting restaurant-level post-Ind AS EBITDA of 22% to 23%.
- Front-store revenue and new restaurant expansion targeted to grow at 20% to 22%.
- Annual capex spend projected around Rs 50-odd crores (Rs 4 crore to Rs 5 crore per restaurant), expected to be funded through internal cash generation to remain cash flow positive.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Hospitality sector shifting to a hybrid model where dine-in happens once a week or twice a month, while delivery allows brand reach 4 times a week.
- Delivery business acts as a secondary revenue stream alongside primary dine-in operations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- No major price hikes taken; raw material inflation managed via economies of scale, vendor support, and renegotiated terms leveraging future expansion pipelines.
- Asia Kitchen unit economics: gross margins 76%, staff costs 20%-22%, rentals 18%, operating expenses 11%, depreciation 4%, yielding an 18% restaurant-level net margin.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned H2 openings: 2 Gong outlets (Delhi and Pune), 3 Walters Burger outlets, and 1 Siciliana outlet. Total Gong count to become 4; Siciliana to become 6.
- Geographic expansion focus shifting towards Delhi, Hyderabad, and Bangalore alongside existing strongholds in West Bengal, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Pune.
- Conversions and renovations of existing Mainland China to Asia Kitchen outlets resulted in 25% to 30% business growth. Target SSSG for oriental brands is 10% to 15%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow generated is strong (approx Rs 50 crores this year), intended to be reinvested to fund the annual capex plan of ~Rs 50 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Manish Bihani (Daksham Capital) asked for pre-Ind AS EBITDA margins and unit economics of major brands:** Management stated pre-Ind AS EBITDA was 7.90% (vs 6.52% YoY) and provided detailed Asia Kitchen throughput and cost breakdowns.
- **Vikram Kotak (Ace Lansdowne) asked about the delivery mix trend and inflation management:** Management confirmed delivery is a structural shift (target steady state 30%-35%) and raw material inflation is managed via scale and vendor negotiations rather than steep price hikes.
- **Zaki Abbas Nasser (Individual Investor) asked about the brand-level breakdown and capex for the 200 outlets by 2030 target:** Management outlined the brand categories (Oriental, Siciliana, QSR) and stated restaurant-level post-Ind AS EBITDA target is 22%-23% with 20%-22% revenue growth.
- **Devesh Vora (Individual Investor) asked about per-restaurant capex costs and funding:** Management stated capex per restaurant is Rs 4 crore to Rs 5 crore, total yearly capex will be around Rs 50 crores, funded through operating cash flows to stay cash-positive.

### One-line takeaway
Speciality Restaurants reported strong Q2 FY27 performance with revenue up 18.24% YoY and PAT up 45.75% YoY, driven by healthy SSSG (8.1%), a growing hybrid delivery mix (32%), and disciplined execution of its profitable expansion roadmap toward 200 outlets by FY2030.
