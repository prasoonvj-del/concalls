# Samhi Hotels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted resilient domestic demand and strong occupancy levels despite geopolitical headwinds affecting international travel.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs. 308.3 crore (up 7.3% reported; 10.8% on a comparable basis).
*   **Same-store RevPAR growth:** 9.6% YoY to ~Rs. 5,220.
*   **Portfolio Occupancy:** 79.3% (up from 74.2% YoY).
*   **PBT:** Rs. 32.7 crore (up 26.4% YoY).
*   **Finance Costs:** Rs. 37.7 crore (down 25.5% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** ~Rs. 1,490 crore.
*   **Net Debt-to-EBITDA:** ~3.2x (trailing 12-month).

### Highlights
*   Domestic travelers comprised 82% of room nights sold (up from 78% YoY).
*   36% of days in the quarter saw occupancy exceeding 90%.
*   RARE India portfolio stands at 75 hotels with 1,046 rooms.
*   Board approved an enabling resolution for a capital raise of Rs. 750 crore to ensure financial flexibility.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains a long-term revenue growth forecast of 9%-11%.
*   Targeting cumulative cash flow of >Rs. 3,000 crore over FY2027-FY2031.
*   Upscale share of revenue is expected to increase from ~40%-41% to ~60% by FY2030.
*   Management expects RARE India to generate Rs. 100 crore-Rs. 120 crore top line and Rs. 35 crore-Rs. 40 crore EBITDA within 1.5-2 years.
*   Management expects to reach a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 2.4x-2.5x by FY2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Office absorption in core markets was ~11 million sq ft in Q1.
*   Aviation passenger traffic was flat YoY for the quarter, with a recovery seen in the latter half.
*   International business travel was impacted by the West Asia crisis, leading to softer rate growth in Q1.
*   July trends are "trending far ahead" of Q1 performance.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Reported EBITDA was down 4% YoY, but comparable EBITDA growth was ~12.1%.
*   GST regime change (12% with ITC to 5% without ITC) negatively impacted EBITDA by ~Rs. 9.2 crore.
*   F&B revenue growth was muted at 3.7% due to event cancellations and restaurant renovations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Committed pipeline includes 1,660 rooms across 7 new hotels.
*   Navi Mumbai project: Expected to hit the ground by April 1, 2027; 3-4 year completion timeline.
*   W Hyderabad and Courtyard Pune (renovation) are geared for H2 FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Effective interest rate is 7.8% (300 bps lower than at IPO).
*   Credit rating remains A+ (stable).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions (West Asia crisis) continue to create uncertainty for international inbound travel.
*   Regulatory and approval processes for large-scale projects.
*   Short-term dilution in average room rates due to the shift in business mix toward domestic travelers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Fundraising:** Management clarified that the Rs. 750 crore enabling resolution is a recurring practice to provide the Board with flexibility to act on opportunities or address unforeseen problems, not an indication of immediate dilution.
*   **RARE India:** Management expects 50%-55% ROCE for this asset-light segment; 40 hotels have agreed to join the Marriott Outdoor Collection.
*   **EBITDA/Revenue Disconnect:** Management explained the YoY EBITDA dip is due to one-time items (GIC restructuring) and the GST impact, which will normalize in H2.
*   **Navi Mumbai:** Management confirmed all prior issues are resolved and the project is proceeding with statutory approvals and design development.

### One-line takeaway
SAMHI delivered resilient operating performance with 9.6% RevPAR growth and strong occupancy, while maintaining a disciplined balance sheet and long-term growth trajectory despite geopolitical headwinds.
