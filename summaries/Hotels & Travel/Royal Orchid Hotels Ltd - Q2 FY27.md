# Royal Orchid Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and EBITDA growth, with management highlighting that the key 'ICONIQA' asset has turned EBITDA positive.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (management declined to provide specific revenue targets due to global uncertainties).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "mixed" to confident; management emphasized the company is at an "inflection point" where past investments are beginning to yield results.
- **New topics or risks:** Vadodara property pre-opening costs mentioned as a drag on current quarter EBITDA.
- **Last quarter's promises:** ICONIQA turned EBITDA positive (as targeted).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 116 crore (up 34% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 31.66 crore (up 53% YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 14.73 crore (vs Rs 4.29 crore YoY).
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 58.75 crore.
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 12.46 crore.
- **EPS:** Rs 5.25.
- **Managed Hotel PBT Margin:** ~37-38%.

### Highlights
- **ICONIQA Performance:** The Mumbai property (opened late Sept 2025) is a significant revenue contributor and achieved its first EBITDA-positive quarter.
- **Expansion:** Added 5 hotels with 426 rooms during the quarter; total portfolio now comprises 127+ hotels.
- **Exceptional Items:** Reported PAT includes an exceptional gain from the disinvestment of the Tanzania subsidiary (Multi Hotels Limited).
- **Strategy:** Focus remains on an asset-light model, premiumization, and scaling the managed hotel segment.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth:** Management expects double-digit growth for the managed hotel segment.
- **Pipeline:** 50+ upcoming properties expected to add 3,500+ rooms.
- **Near-term:** 30 hotels with ~1,800 keys expected to open in the next 12 months.
- **Management View:** Management declined to provide specific revenue targets due to global geopolitical uncertainties.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Drivers:** Strong domestic travel, corporate travel, and MICE activity.
- **Seasonality:** Management expects Q3 and Q4 to be stronger than Q1 and Q2.
- **ICONIQA Metrics:** Occupancy was 70% in Q2 (consistent with H1); ADR is ~Rs 8,000.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **GST Impact:** Disallowance of input tax credits on 5% GST output slab continues to be a "big hit," impacting the P&L by ~Rs 2 crore per quarter.
- **Pre-opening Costs:** The new Vadodara property incurred >Rs 1 crore in pre-opening costs, impacting Q2 EBITDA.
- **Other Expenses:** Expected to hover around Rs 33-35 crore per quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Strategy:** Prioritizing scalable, profitable hospitality business over pure scale; focus on converting the 50-property pipeline into sustainable growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Governance:** Management stated they maintain a strong corporate governance policy.
- **Legal/Regulatory:** SEBI initial observations were ruled out; one accounting issue is pending a final order (management expects no financial impact).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **GST Regulation:** Ongoing inability to claim input tax credits on the 5% GST output slab.
- **NCLT Proceedings:** Ongoing matter involving Ksheer Sagar Developers Limited (management claims no financial implication).
- **Seasonality:** Q1/Q2 are historically weaker periods for business hotels.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: When is the inflection point?** A: Management believes they are at an inflection point now; investments made will yield better performance in coming quarters.
- **Q: Why did EBITDA decline sequentially?** A: Attributed to seasonality (rains) and pre-opening costs of the new Vadodara hotel.
- **Q: ICONIQA occupancy/ARR?** A: Occupancy is 70%; ADR is ~Rs 8,000.
- **Q: Corporate governance/Audit qualifications?** A: Management defended transparency, noted SEBI initial observations were ruled out, and stated audit qualifications are largely outside company control (legal cases).
- **Q: Managed hotel growth trajectory?** A: Management expects faster than 10-15% growth, dependent on partner development timelines.

### One-line takeaway
Royal Orchid delivered strong YoY growth and achieved an EBITDA-positive milestone for ICONIQA, though near-term profitability remains pressured by pre-opening costs and GST regulatory headwinds.
