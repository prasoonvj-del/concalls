# Chalet Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY 27  
**Tone:** Positive – margin expansion, double‑digit revenue growth and stable balance‑sheet despite aggressive capex  

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** raised – FY 27‑29 capex guidance lifted from ~₹3,000 cr to **₹3,400‑3,600 cr** (previous ≈ ₹3,000 cr).  
- **Tone & demand view:** shifted from cautious (Q1 focus on recovery) to upbeat, citing “double‑digit” revenue growth and strong CRE cash flows.  
- **New topics/risks:** Varca (Goa) construction slip of ~2 years, Taj Delhi airport inventory delay, CRZ clearance pending, and heightened geopolitical headwinds.  
- **Old topics fading:** earlier concerns on crew‑shortage largely resolved (20 rooms/day back in service).  
- **Last‑quarter promises:**  
  - Crew‑contract recovery – partially delivered (20 rooms/day back).  
  - CRE rental run‑rate target – achieved ₹300 m/month in Sep 2026.  
  - FY 27‑29 capex increase – announced and quantified.  

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (core hospitality + CRE):** **₹5,110 m** (+11 % YoY)  
- **EBITDA:** **₹2,274 m** (+13 % YoY)  
- **EBITDA margin:** **45 %**, up **110 bps** YoY  
- **Net profit:** **₹2,163 m**  
- **Hospitality revenue:** **₹4,115 m** (+8 % YoY)  
- **Hospitality EBITDA:** **₹1,691 m** (+11 % YoY) – margin **41 %**, +110 bps YoY  
- **ADR (blended):** **+11.3 % YoY**  
- **RevPAR:** **+7.2 % YoY** (leisure assets **+34 % YoY**)  
- **Resort occupancy (blended):** **41.2 %** (vs 32.6 % last year)  
- **CRE rental run‑rate (Sep 2026):** **₹300 m/month** (up from ₹290 m in Jun 2026)  
- **CRE occupancy:** **91 %** (leasable area **95 %** after LOIs)  
- **Net debt (Sep 2026):** **₹21 bn** (stable over 7 qtrs)  
- **Effective tax rate:** **25.17 %** (concessional regime)  

### Highlights
- Development pipeline now **2,036 keys** (≈ 60 % of current portfolio); ATHIVA pipeline **≈ 900 keys** toward a **1,200‑key** target.  
- **ATHIVA Pulse (Navi Mumbai)** upgrade: **₹1 bn** for a 30,000 sq ft wellness floor.  
- CRE contribution strong: **₹901 m** revenue (+22 % YoY), **₹750 m** EBITDA (+23 % YoY), margin **83 %** (+95 bps).  
- Credit rating upgraded to **IND AA / AA**.  
- Residential Phase‑II (Koramangala) 168 units near completion; hand‑over slated **Dec 2026** → revenue to be booked H2 FY 27.  
- New crew contract restored **20 rooms/day** (partial recovery from ~100 rooms/day loss).  

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue spike** expected from residential hand‑over (Dec 2026) and new hotel openings.  
- **Project timelines:**  
  - CIGNUS 2 (Powai) – substantial completion **FY 27 end**.  
  - Taj Delhi Airport – full inventory launch **early FY 28** (subject to GRAP review).  
  - Ritz‑Carlton (Hyderabad) & Hyatt Regency (Airoli) – on schedule.  
- **Capex FY 27‑29:** **₹3,400‑3,600 cr**, funded primarily by internal accruals and CRE annuity cash flows.  
- **Occupancy target** for resort portfolio: **60‑65 %** (early‑60 % range over next few years).  
- **CRE rental revenue** projected **₹500 cr** annually once CIGNUS 2 fully leased (FY 2028).  

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Supply‑demand gap** persists; “Big‑Box” strategy in major business cities seen as advantage.  
- **Geopolitical headwinds** (West‑Asia conflict) raising fuel & air‑fare costs but margins held up.  
- **Regional traffic Q2:** Delhi up (BRICS summit), Mumbai/Bangalore/Hyderabad down YoY.  
- **Leisure assets** delivering **34 % RevPAR** growth; business hotels mixed due to construction impacts and air‑traffic contraction.  
- **MICE** lagging (4 % YoY) but expected to recover H2 as weddings and large‑group events pick up.  

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin expansion driven by higher ADR, pricing power and efficiency measures.  
- Hospitality EBITDA margin **41 %**, up **110 bps** YoY.  
- CRE margin **83 %**, up **95 bps** YoY, reflecting stable annuity income.  

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **ATHIVA Pulse wellness floor:** **₹1 bn** capex.  
- **FY 27‑29 capex:** **₹3,400‑3,600 cr** (incl. two new ATHIVA properties – Hyderabad & Pune).  
- **Keys target 5‑year:** **5,500+ keys** (≈ 75‑80 % capacity addition).  
- **CIGNUS 2 (Powai):** ~1 m sq ft to be added FY 27‑28; expected “step‑change” from FY 28 onward.  

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net debt:** **₹21 bn** (≈ ₹2,100 cr) – stable.  
- **Credit rating:** upgraded to **AA**.  
- **Average cost of finance:** **7.47 %** (Sep 2026).  
- **Liquidity:** described as “comfortable”; CRE rentals provide **₹25 bn** eligible for LRD facility.  

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical shocks (fuel, air‑fare, routing) could hit demand.  
- Construction‑related disruptions (Powai, Varca, Taj Delhi air‑quality/GRAP).  
- Regulatory risk: CRZ clearance for Varca pending; GRAP restrictions for Taj Delhi.  
- MICE recovery slower than hoped; reliance on foreign‑traveller traffic (U.S., GCC).  

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **RevPAR vs. rooms‑revenue & F&B (Dipak Saha, Ashika):** Management explained rooms‑revenue outpacing RevPAR due to inventory mix; F&B grew 6.4 % YoY, MICE only 4 % YoY, expecting improvement in H2.  
2. **FY28 revenue outlook (Dipak Saha):** Management affirmed “minimum double‑digit” revenue growth from new assets, hinting at ~+20 % possible.  
3. **FY29 pipeline timing (Dipak Saha):** Delivery of ~625 keys (≈ 18 % of owned rooms) within FY 29; quarter not disclosed.  
4. **Foreign‑traveller dependence (Sumit Sinha, Macquarie):** Management sees “green shoots” after U.S. mid‑term elections and de‑escalation in Middle‑East; confident on project execution.  
5. **Varca & Mindspace timelines (Analyst team):** Varca construction to start Q4 FY 27/28 despite 2‑year slip; Mindspace fit‑out to proceed in parallel with structural hand‑over.  

### One-line takeaway
Robust margin expansion and cash‑flow‑driven growth are offset by construction delays and geopolitical headwinds, but management’s disciplined capex and strong CRE base keep FY 27 outlook upbeat.
