# Yatra Online Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong underlying bookings and market share gains, offset by geopolitical disruptions impacting international travel and MICE top lines.

### Key numbers
- **Gross Bookings:** INR 21,007 million, up 17% YoY (or 16.5% YoY).
- **Gross Margin (Revenue less service cost):** INR 1,227 million, up 6.1% YoY.
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 1,879 million, down 10.4% YoY.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 151 million, down 39.4% YoY.
- **Adjusted EBITDA to Gross Margin Ratio:** 12.29%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 3.4 million.
- **Air Passenger Volume:** 1,264,000, up 4.8% YoY (or ~5%).
- **Gross Air Bookings:** INR 16,579 million, up 17.6% YoY.
- **Air Gross Margin:** INR 699 million, up 8% YoY (margin declined from 4.6% to 4.2%).
- **Total Room Nights (Hotels & Packages):** 548,000, up ~30% YoY.
- **Hotels & Packages Gross Bookings:** INR 3,876 million, up 13% YoY.
- **Hotels & Packages Gross Margins:** INR 386 million, up 24% YoY (margin improved from 9.05% to 9.95%).
- **Stand-alone Hotel Business YoY Growth:** Gross bookings up ~34%, revenues up 62%, room nights up ~30%.
- **Cash, Cash Equivalents, and Term Deposits:** INR 1,976.9 million as of June 30, 2026.
- **New Corporate Customer Additions:** 53 new customers with an expected annual billable potential of INR 2,223 million (30+ customers via Travel Pro representing ~INR 800 million).
- **MICE Impact:** Approximately INR 300 million lower YoY in top line; temporary margin compression impact of INR 60 million on H&P gross margin (including INR 30 million from MICE top-line drop and INR 30 million from shifting to domestic group travel).
- **International Share of Business:** Under 30%.

### Highlights
- Completed 20 years of operations.
- Added 53 new corporate customers with INR 2,223 million annual billable potential.
- Travel Pro (MSME offering) secured 30+ customers representing ~INR 800 million.
- Expense management solution (RECAP) added more than 20 customers since launch.
- Extended corporate platform internationally via a partnership with Kanoo Travel in the Middle East.
- Air passenger volume grew ~5%, approximately twice the industry growth rate.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects margins to improve in the second half of the year as airline capacity and MICE normalize.
- Management expects to be close to the 20% adjusted EBITDA margin mark in the second half of the year before scaling toward a 30% aspiration in the mid term.
- Strategy to achieve a 50-50 business mix between air and hotels over the next 2 to 3 years remains on track (currently at a ~60-40 mix at the gross margin level).
- No formal full-year guidance issued for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall India online travel market expected to grow at high-single to low-double-digit CAGR over the next several years.
- Domestic travel remained resilient; air passenger traffic in India grew ~2.3% YoY.
- West Asia conflict and rerouting of flights disrupted international air connectivity, MICE activities, and outbound sentiment, causing industry-wide inquiries for some international destinations to decline by 10% to 15%.
- Medium-term outlook for outbound travel remains robust with projected low-teens growth over the next decade.
- Q2 MICE bookings are already trending at approximately 50% higher than Q1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Annual productivity-linked bonuses (PLBs) with Middle Eastern carriers remained open/unclosed in Q1 due to disruptions, leading to lower revenue recognition and subdued air margins.
- Employee expenses as a percentage of revenue increased due to hiring, training, and infrastructure costs (including Google Cloud Platform hosting) for the Kanoo project before revenue realization started in July.
- Customer promotion expenses as a percentage of gross bookings were elevated due to a higher B2C business mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested in building out people, platforms, and go-to-market capabilities for Travel Pro, RECAP, and the global-ready infrastructure for the Kanoo Travel partnership in the Middle East ahead of revenue generation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents and term deposits stood at INR 1,976.9 million as of June 30, 2026.
- Working capital initiatives are ongoing with banks regarding corporate card platforms to balance MDR costs and with American Express (BTA), HDFC, and Citibank (CTA) to manage credit card costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions (West Asia conflict) and flight rerouting leading to elevated airfares, longer routes, and uncertainty in long-haul destinations.
- Elevated airfares and timing of airline incentive programs created near-term headwinds for corporate travel.
- Nonessential corporate travel being limited in certain industries (e.g., IT).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sagarika Chetty (Antique Stockbroking) on Air Margins:** Asked if margins would remain subdued. Management explained that delayed PLB closures with Middle Eastern carriers impacted Q1, but normalization in the second half should drive margin recovery.
- **Nitin (Surge Capital) on MICE Recovery:** Asked if the INR 300 million revenue loss would be bridged immediately. Management stated that the MICE restructuring/transformation phase is over, with Q2 volumes trending 50% higher than Q1, signaling a quick normalization.
- **Anmol Garg on Margin Guidance:** Inquired about reaching 30%+ margins. Management clarified that 30% is a mid-term aspiration, and the second half of the year should see margins return closer to the 20% mark.
- **Dhruv on Corporate Restructuring:** Asked about the Yatra India and parent company merger timeline. Management noted that work has been ongoing across multiple jurisdictions for 6 quarters, but giving an exact timeline is difficult due to regulatory processes.
- **Chirag on Employee Expenses:** Asked why employee expenses increased as a percentage of revenue. Management attributed it to pre-hiring and setup costs for the Kanoo project ahead of revenue accrual starting in July.

### One-line takeaway
Yatra delivered resilient gross booking growth of 17% YoY despite temporary geopolitical disruptions and MICE headwinds, while front-loading investments in Travel Pro, RECAP, and the Kanoo Middle East partnership that management expects to drive operating leverage in H2 FY27.
