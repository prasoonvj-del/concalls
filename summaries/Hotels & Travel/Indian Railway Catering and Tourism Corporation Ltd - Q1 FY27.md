# Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted resilient performance and strong revenue growth across core segments, despite temporary margin headwinds from product mix and one-time HR costs.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 1,370 crore (up 18.10% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 330 crore.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 386 crore (down 2.77% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 28.17%.
*   **Internet Ticketing Revenue:** Rs 361 crore (up 0.5% YoY).
*   **Catering Revenue:** Rs 732 crore (up 33.82% YoY).
*   **Rail Neer Revenue:** Rs 109 crore (up 2.83% YoY).
*   **Tourism Revenue:** Rs 168 crore (up 13.5% YoY).

### Highlights
*   Internet Ticketing: 89% of reserved railway tickets in India booked via IRCTC platform.
*   Catering: Growth driven by prepaid trains (onboard sales rose from Rs 301 crore to Rs 413 crore), license fees, and election specials (Rs 41 crore).
*   Tourism: EBITDA margins improved to 11.31% from 8.78% due to better product mix and cost realization.
*   iPay: Final application submitted to RBI; awaiting response.
*   Website Transformation: Beta version of new UI/UX launched; full version expected soon.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to return to a Rs 150 crore run rate for the non-convenience fee segment.
*   Targeting introduction of ~20 Vande Bharat sleeper trains in the next year.
*   Rail Neer capacity augmentation (Ambernath and Danapur plants) expected to be finalized this financial year.
*   New Rail Neer plants (Prayagraj, Mysore, Ranchi) planned; timeline may spill over to next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Passenger growth reported at 8% YoY.
*   E-catering volume increased to ~1.6 lakh meals/day.
*   Catering opt-out rate is approximately 25-30%.
*   Non-fare revenue (NFR) initiatives, such as train branding (e.g., Sprite Tejas), are being promoted to improve profitability.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin decline attributed to revenue mix (higher catering contribution), Rs 20 crore incremental HR cost (gratuity and post-retirement benefits), and increased direct costs in ticketing.
*   Rail Neer margins impacted (10% vs 14% previously) due to a 30% increase in resin costs caused by petroleum price hikes.
*   Internet Ticketing margins at ~80% (down from 84-85%) due to IT infrastructure investments (NGET Infra Refresh).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Rail Neer installed capacity: 17.77 lakh bottles/day (reduced from 18.4 due to one plant closure).
*   NGET Infra Refresh: ~Rs 150 crore infused into hardware/software upgrades.
*   Disaster recovery site planned at Secunderabad.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Margin pressure from revenue mix and one-time HR expenses.
*   Unauthorized vendors in Rail Neer segment.
*   Geopolitical disruptions impacting tourism.
*   Resin cost volatility affecting Rail Neer profitability.
*   IT infrastructure maintenance and upgrades impacting short-term margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Reason for Sanjay Jain's resignation?** A: Management stated it was a personal decision and emphasized that the organization remains strong and leadership transition is stable.
*   **Q: Why the dip in non-convenience fee?** A: Management explained it was due to the temporary removal of ads/marketing during the website UI/UX transformation and stricter controls on agent access; they expect to recover this revenue.
*   **Q: Why did catering margins decline?** A: Attributed to "proof of concept" (POC) trains, increased HR costs, and the GST structure on prepaid trains where input tax credit is not available.
*   **Q: Can convenience fees be increased?** A: Management acknowledged this is an area of concern but noted it is a policy-driven decision; they are attempting to circumvent this by promoting credit card/credit line payments which carry higher fees.

### One-line takeaway
IRCTC delivered robust top-line growth of 18.10% driven by catering and tourism, while temporary margin compression from one-time HR costs, IT infrastructure investments, and raw material inflation is expected to normalize.
