# Waterways Leisure Tourism Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-15 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with revenue and profit growth, outperforming the previous quarter despite geopolitical uncertainty and macroeconomic headwinds, alongside the early delivery of Cordelia Sky.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 1,329 million (up 31% YoY from Rs. 1,015 million in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 777 million
- **EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 58.45%
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 585 million
- **PAT margin (Q2 FY27):** 44.02%
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** Rs. 3,230 billion [as stated] (up 16% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs. 1,242 million
- **EBITDA margin (H1 FY27):** 38.45%
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs. 813 million
- **PAT margin (H1 FY27):** 25.16%
- **Load factor (Q2 FY27):** 75.53%
- **Load factor (H1 FY27):** 91.23% (vs 87.79% in H1 FY26)
- **Average Ticket Price (ATP - Q2 FY27):** Rs. 10,300 (vs Rs. 9,529 in Q2 FY26)
- **Average Ticket Price (ATP - H1 FY27):** Rs. 11,082 (vs Rs. 10,468 in H1 FY26)
- **Average revenue per passenger (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 13,645 (vs Rs. 10,349 in Q2 FY26)
- **Average revenue per passenger (H1 FY27):** Rs. 12,930 (vs Rs. 11,424 in H1 FY26)
- **Fuel cost per APCD (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 2,471 (vs Rs. 1,434 in Q2 FY26)
- **Fuel cost per APCD (H1 FY27):** Rs. 2,481 (vs Rs. 1,472 in H1 FY26)
- **Debt-to-equity ratio:** 0.57% [as stated]
- **Compensation received:** USD 5.2 million from Norwegian Sky Limited for early handover of vessel Sky.
- **Advances paid for new vessels:** Rs. 4,290 million (interest-bearing advance).

### Highlights
- Delivered *Cordelia Sky* on October 9, 2026 (one month ahead of schedule, arriving in Mumbai on September 10 [as stated] / October 15 [letterhead date] / October 9 call date), increasing overall capacity by approximately 126% with 2,400 guests across 1,002 staterooms.
- Received USD 5.2 million in compensation from Norwegian Sky Limited for early delivery of *Cordelia Sky*.
- Launched a new structured loyalty program called the "Chairman's Club" to drive repeat travel.
- Next vessel *SUN* (sister ship to *Sky*) remains on schedule for delivery in 11 months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects fuel prices to normalize eventually and anticipates Q3 and Q4 profit margins to benefit from lower fuel pressures or cost management.
- Management is exploring further fleet expansions beyond *SUN* (currently in initial discussion stages).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian overnight ocean and coastal cruise industry valued at approx. Rs. 8,301 millions in FY25 (vs Rs. 5,764 millions in FY20), expected to grow at 20%-25% CAGR between FY26 and FY31.
- India recorded ~4.8 lakh cruise passenger movements in CY2025, with domestic passengers accounting for ~89%.
- FY28 projected to see ~95 wedding dates (vs 65-70 typically), representing an ~40% increase, which management targets for weddings and celebrations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel cost impact for H1 was almost Rs. 28 crores. To offset this, management is charging a fuel surcharge of $15-$20 per person per day and implementing cost-shedding, itinerary changes, and speed optimization.
- Corporate and shoreside costs will be distributed across multiple ships, improving operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Cordelia Sky* increases capacity by ~126% (1,002 rooms, 2,400 guest capacity).
- *Empress* has 796 rooms / 2,005 beds; *Sky* has 1,002 rooms / 2,400 beds; *SUN* has 1,002 rooms.
- Rs. 4,290 million advanced toward the acquisition of new vessels. Financing options include hire-purchase arrangements (fixed lease rentals over 10 years) or outright purchase using internal accruals and debt.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.57% [as stated].
- Short-term borrowings increased by around Rs. 344 crores, kept as an interest-bearing advance with a subsidiary/mediator for quick deployment upon closing a vessel acquisition deal.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainty and macroeconomic headwinds impacting fuel prices and travel patterns.
- Fuel cost per APCD increased significantly (Rs. 2,471 in Q2 FY27 vs Rs. 1,434 in Q2 FY26).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vansh Jain (Individual Investor) on margin expansion and vessel financing:** Management stated global cruising margins range from 35% to 40% and they aim to achieve or beat it. Financing is pursued via hire-purchase arrangements (10-year lease) or internal accruals/debt.
2. **Anurag Yadav (Analyst) on shipbuilding/operating costs for *Sky* vs *Empress*:** Management clarified that per-day variable costs (like F&B per person) remain constant, but fixed corporate/marketing costs are spread across multiple ships, lowering unit operating costs.
3. **Vaibhav Mehta (Analyst) on short-term borrowings increase (Rs. 344 crores):** CFO explained the funds are kept ready as an interest-bearing advance with a mediator to swiftly close an identified vessel acquisition.
4. **Vinamra Hirawat (Analyst) on phase-out plans for *Empress*:** Management stated there is no plan to divest *Empress* as cruise ships undergo strict regulatory checks and internal upgrades rather than cargo end-of-life cycles.
5. **Rikesh Parikh (Analyst) on fuel price hedging and surcharge absorption:** Management noted they levy a $15-$20 per person daily fuel surcharge, optimize itineraries, and plan to explore fuel hedging with a multi-ship fleet.

### One-line takeaway
Waterways Leisure reported strong Q2 FY27 results with 31% YoY revenue growth and advanced its fleet expansion through the early delivery of *Cordelia Sky* while managing fuel cost headwinds.
