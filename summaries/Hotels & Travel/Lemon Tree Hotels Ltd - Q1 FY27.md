# Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust network expansion, strategic asset-light growth, and strong recovery and momentum heading into Q2 despite a temporary softer Q1 impact.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** Rs. 346.8 crore (up 9% YoY)
- **Net EBITDA:** Rs. 151.9 crore (up 7%)
- **Net EBITDA (Adjusted for GST impact & Stock Appreciation Rights):** Rs. 162.5 crore (up 14% YoY vs Rs. 142.1 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Net EBITDA Margin:** 43.8% (down 99 bps YoY vs 44.8%)
- **PAT:** Rs. 57.3 crore (up 19% YoY)
- **Cash Profit:** Rs. 96 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **Gross ARR:** Rs. 6,361 (up 2% YoY)
- **Occupancy:** 75.7% (up 314 bps YoY)
- **Gross Debt (as of June 30, 2026):** Rs. 1,475 crore (down 11% YoY from Rs. 1,657.9 crore)
- **Cost of Debt:** 7.48% (down 53 bps YoY)
- **Network Revenue:** Rs. 576 crore (up 16% YoY; owned hotels contributed 56%)
- **Fees Income (Third-party managed/franchised):** Rs. 22.8 crore (up 42% YoY)
- **Fees from Fleur Hotels:** Rs. 22.6 crore (up 6% YoY)
- **Total Management Fee:** Rs. 45.4 crore (up 21% YoY)
- **Keys Portfolio RevPAR:** Rs. 2,885 (up 19% YoY on 67% occupancy and ARR of Rs. 4,311)

### Highlights
- Opened 6 managed and franchised hotels with 334 rooms; signed 13 managed and franchised hotels with 1,020 rooms during Q1 FY27.
- Combined operational and pipeline inventory stands at 23,381 rooms across 279 hotels in 170+ cities (135 hotels / 11,946 rooms operational across 80+ cities).
- Renovated 300 rooms this quarter, spending ~Rs. 10 crore; Keys portfolio renovation is roughly 2/3rd complete.
- Management shared pro forma financials for Lemon Tree and Fleur Hotels reflecting the upcoming demerger scheme.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects consolidated net EBITDA margins to reach around 50% in FY28 as renovation and temporary GST/SAR impacts taper off.
- Management targets opening approximately 2,000 keys in FY27.
- Management aims for Keys portfolio to achieve Rs. 6.5 lakhs per key EBITDA (~Rs. 60 crore total EBITDA).
- Long-term Lemon Tree plan is to target an EBITDA margin of 75%-80% over the next 3-4 years on a larger base of managed hotels.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 was impacted by the West Asia conflict, leading to lower inbound traffic and second-order effects on domestic corporate travel and T&E budgets, particularly hitting high-density CBD locations like Mumbai and Gurgaon.
- The company pivoted to a retail-heavy volume strategy in Q1 to maintain occupancy at the expense of ARR.
- July showed a strong recovery, and August demand remains solid.
- Management noted that India is not currently in an "up cycle" (as industry occupancies are still in the mid-60s), which provides stability against volatility.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin drop was attributed to Provision for Stock Appreciation Rights (SAR) and the loss of input credit on GST (which increased expenses by 3.1% of total revenue).
- Renovation expenses in Q1 were ~Rs. 9.8 crore (2.25%-2.3% of revenue). Renovation expenses are expected to drop dramatically starting next year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Aurika Shimla (owned 90 rooms) finishing work and licenses are in final stages; expected to open shortly.
- Aurika Shillong (leased 165 rooms) expected opening in H2 FY28 (~Rs. 33 crore deployed as of June 30, 2026).
- Aurika Varanasi (leased 47 rooms) work has begun; expected opening in FY29.
- Aurika Nehru Place, Delhi (expected opening on/after FY2030; DDA letter of award validity extended by a year).
- Fleur is pursuing additions to reach ~2,500 Keys rooms and evaluating brownfield, operating assets, and greenfield developments utilizing Warburg's capital infusion of Rs. 960 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Group level net debt stands at approximately Rs. 1,275 crore (including cash of about Rs. 200 crore).
- Long-term plan for Fleur is to maintain debt-to-EBITDA around 2x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply injection of ~2,000 new rooms near the Mumbai airport over the last 2 years has created a temporary supply-demand mismatch.
- Geopolitical tensions (West Asia conflict) create transient uncertainties for inbound and discretionary corporate travel.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Archana Gude (IDBI Capital) on Keys performance and outlook:** Management stated Keys is tracking toward Red Fox ARRs (~Rs. 4,500), with full performance expected next year once renovations wrap up, targeting a Rs. 60 crore EBITDA.
2. **Achal Kumar (HSBC) on ARR compression vs occupancy push:** Management explained that Q1 retail volume pivot lowered ARR temporarily to protect occupancy amidst corporate slowdowns, but normalized pricing strategy is back in place for July/August.
3. **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital) on Aurika Mumbai pricing power:** Management stated Q1 was an aberration and they are actively focusing on driving Aurika's pricing higher moving forward.
4. **Sameet Sinha (Macquarie) on EBITDA margin recovery trajectory:** Management guided that normalization of renovation expenses and improved H2 pricing should push net EBITDA margins toward 50% by FY28.
5. **Jinesh Joshi (Antique Stock Broking) on fee income growth drivers:** Management clarified that the compounding effect of historical room signings (e.g., 5,000 rooms signed in FY26) will drive a major acceleration in fee income over the next 2-4 years.

### One-line takeaway
Lemon Tree reported a muted Q1 due to geopolitical and corporate headwinds but remains confident in long-term compounding from its asset-light management fee model and tapering renovation cycles.
