# EIH Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong 16% top-line growth and 19% EBITDA expansion driven by robust domestic demand, higher room rates, and event tailwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (Pipeline goals unchanged: 1,100 owned keys by 2030 and 1,893 managed keys by 2031).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious on foreign arrivals (which were hurt by West Asia geopolitical issues in Q1) to optimistic due to encouraging signs of revival in foreign bookings for H2 FY27.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: A one-time finance cost impact from an Oberoi Delhi lease settlement; Wildflower Hall bidding win involving a 47.1% revenue share to the state government; fuel/power cost pressures mentioned in Q1 were not raised this quarter.
- Last quarter's promises: Summer room renovations at Mumbai hotels were completed as scheduled and returned to inventory for H2; Oberoi Grand Kolkata restoration remains on track for September.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue Growth: 16% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA Growth: 19% YoY
- Consolidated Net PAT: Rs 153 crore (up 21% YoY from Rs 142 crore)
- Cash Reserves: More than Rs 1,100 crore as of September 30, 2026
- Overall RevPAR Growth (all domestic hotels): 18.3% YoY
- Oberoi Brand RevPAR Growth: 20.9% YoY (vs 18.2% industry growth)
- Trident Brand RevPAR Growth: 14.8% YoY (vs 10.1% industry growth)
- Occupancy (Overall Inventory): 71% (down from 72% YoY due to room renovations)
- RevPAR on Reduced Inventory: Rs 14,129 (74% occupancy); Owned hotels RevPAR on reduced inventory: Rs 16,650 (76% occupancy)
- Flight Catering (OFS) Revenue: Rs 150 crore (up ~20% YoY)
- Channel Mix: MICE share increased to 16% (vs 12% YoY); direct segment share at 45% (vs 48% YoY)

### Highlights
- RGI increased from 121% to 130% YoY; 13 out of 15 hotels ranked 1st or 2nd in STR benchmarking.
- September RevPAR got a significant boost in NCR properties due to the BRICS summit.
- Vanyavilas Ranthambore was kept open for 2 of the 3 summer shutdown months, and higher off-season pricing at Rajvilas significantly boosted Jaipur/Ranthambore performance.
- Oberoi Rajgarh Palace was recognized in global finest hotel lists for 2027 and is expected to achieve EBITDA breakeven/positivity in Q3.
- Major room renovations in Mumbai (Oberoi Mumbai: 34 keys, Trident TNP: 135 keys, Trident BKC: 80 keys) were completed and inventory restored for H2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects healthy performance momentum in Q3 and Q4 supported by foreign booking revivals and full room inventory availability.
- Management expects Oberoi Rajgarh Palace to turn EBITDA positive in Q3.
- Owned pipeline aims to add ~1,100 keys by 2030 (plus 7.63 lakh sq ft commercial space in Bangalore); managed pipeline to add 1,893 keys by 2031.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 hotel industry saw 1% to 3% occupancy growth and 8% to 10% ARR growth YoY.
- Foreign travel bookings are displaying encouraging signs of recovery heading into Q3/Q4.
- Domestic air traffic contracted due to higher airfares, but hotel demand stayed resilient.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses grew in line with revenue, enabling positive P&L drop-through.
- Finance costs spiked in Q2 due to a one-time settlement of past period lease rentals for Oberoi Delhi.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Operating cash flows fully covered H1 capex spending.
- Acquired just under 10 acres of freehold land near Hyderabad Airport for a 300-key Trident hotel.
- Construction is ongoing for Oberoi London; ground breaking for the 1.7 million sq ft Hebbal Bangalore project (two hotels, commercial, retail) is planned for early next calendar year.
- Goa hotel design is in final approval stages; Oberoi Grand Kolkata restoration is in progress.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated cash reserves remain strong at over Rs 1,100 crore, positioning the company well for expansion capex over the next 2-3 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign visitor recovery depends on broader global geopolitical stability.
- The 47.1% revenue share payable to the state government for Wildflower Hall presents profitability pressures.
- Approvals for the Goa project and earlier land realignments in Tirupati caused timeline shifts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Deepak Saha asked about the reasons for the Q2 finance cost surge.** Management answered that it was a one-time settlement for past period lease rentals at Oberoi Delhi.
- **Abhay Khaitan asked why overall revenue grew 16% while luxury RevPAR grew over 20%.** Management clarified that overall RevPAR grew 18% (the >20% figure excluded rooms under renovation) and non-hotel/commercial streams grew at a slower pace.
- **Madhav Agarwal asked about Wildflower Hall revenue accounting and profitability under a 47.1% revenue share.** Management stated revenue will be accounted from end-Q4 and acknowledged 47.1% is high, committing to focus efforts on making it profitable.
- **Rajiv Bharati asked if room renovations in Mumbai were out for the entire quarter and their operational status now.** Management confirmed they were out for most of Q2 but are fully back in inventory for H2; current room renovations are limited to Bangalore (18 keys at a time).

### One-line takeaway
EIH delivered strong Q2 growth with a 16% revenue increase and 19% EBITDA growth, set for further upside in H2 as renovated room inventory returns and foreign travel revives.
