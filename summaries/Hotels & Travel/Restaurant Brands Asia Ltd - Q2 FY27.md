# Restaurant Brands Asia Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent strong performance with 14% SSSG in India, consolidated revenue up 20% YoY, and company EBITDA rising significantly.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 844 crore (up 20% YoY from Rs 703 crore).
- **Consolidated Restaurant EBITDA:** Rs 91.8 crore (up 73% YoY from Rs 53 crore).
- **Consolidated Company-level EBITDA:** Rs 43.2 crore (up 281% YoY from Rs 11.3 crore).
- **India Business Revenue:** Crosses Rs 700 crore (up 24% YoY).
- **India SSSG (Same-Store Sales Growth):** 14% (second consecutive quarter of 14% SSSG; Q1 also strong).
- **India Average Daily Sales (ADS):** Rs 1,31,000.
- **India Store EBITDA:** Over Rs 50 crore for the quarter; 13% level.
- **India Store Count:** 607 restaurants across 144 cities (reached 619 restaurants post-quarter).
- **Indonesia Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 139 crore.
- **Indonesia ADS (Both brands):** Rs 96,000.
- **Indonesia Restaurant EBITDA:** Marginal INR 8 million (positive at restaurant level for Burger King Indonesia).
- **Indonesia Company-level EBITDA:** Loss of Rs 12 crore (improved by 5 crores or 51 million YoY).
- **Popeyes Indonesia ADS:** Sliding down, at 64,000; revenue around Rs 15 crores.
- **App Frequency:** Up 7% YoY in Q2.
- **Liquidity / Cash on Balance Sheet:** Close to Rs 1,000 crore (from primary capital raise); potential additional Rs 450 crore from warrants.

### Highlights
- India business contributes 83% of revenues (expected to exceed 90% by March).
- Added a new strategic pillar called "Families" (launching kids family program and Avengers partnership in November).
- Value strategy continues with 2 for 79 / 2 for 99 on the app, marking the 14th consecutive quarter of positive dine-in traffic growth.
- Innovation pipeline: Korean Season 2 volumes were 30% better than Season 1; Peri-Peri rollout delivered 30% better volumes than Korean Season 2.
- Indonesia launched the "Super Hemat" value campaign in late September, adding 35 to 40 additional traffic per day per restaurant.
- Indonesia asset upgrade program planned for the next 6 months (HVAC, kitchen equipment, and customer touch areas).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management sees potential to increase future store expansion pace in India from 60-70 restaurants to 80-100 restaurants per year.
- Indonesia primary capital infusion of Rs 130 crore approved by the board, to be disbursed in tranches based on milestones and asset quality improvement.
- No immediate plans to add new stores in Burger King Indonesia; focus remains on fixing existing assets and driving traffic.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth in India is entirely driven by traffic, not check sizes, continuing a 14-quarter trend.
- Navratri shift to October had minimal impact on numbers due to a balanced veg/non-veg menu and strong traffic growth offsetting dips.
- Western QSR industry in Indonesia is moving in a positive direction as boycott sentiments are far behind.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins dipped slightly by 0.4% (to around 70.4% - 70.5%) due to a sudden market-wide chicken inflation headwind.
- Supply chain efficiencies and cluster DC expansion are helping offset cost headwinds.
- Energy costs reduced through solar installations, broiler efficiencies, and energy-efficient equipment (HVAC, lighting) managed by Nitin Bhayana's team.
- Inflation impacts (food, employee, utilities) fully accounted for in Q2 numbers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 12 stores in October in India; pipeline is built 6 to 8 quarters in advance for linear growth.
- Indonesia asset upgrade underway; planned capital infusion of Rs 130 crore dedicated to fixing assets and improving quality, not new store growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Liquidity stands close to Rs 1,000 crore from primary capital raise; warrants could bring an additional Rs 450 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Popeyes Indonesia continues to be a drag with sliding ADS; management is in active discussion with brand owners for a resolution.
- Industry-wide grocery and chicken cost headwinds impacted gross margins temporarily.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhwanil Desai (Motilal Oswal / Individual):** Asked about the path to 15% EBITDA margins. Management (Rajeev Varman) stated that volume leverage from the new family and kids programs will help expand EBITDA margins beyond the 13% level over time.
2. **Viraj Mehta (Equinomics):** Inquired about gross margin recovery and lessons learned from past aggressive expansions. Management clarified that the supply chain cluster approach, DC proximity, and long-term protein strategies will aid gross margins, while store expansion will follow a structured, linear pipeline rather than a bulked-up approach.
3. **Manjeet Buaria (Individual):** Asked about capital allocation for excess cash and Indonesia funding. Management stated that primary capital of Rs 130 crore for Indonesia will be released in tranches based on performance milestones strictly for asset upgrades, while India capital allocation strategies with the new promoters will be shared in due course.
4. **Naveen Koushik (Individual):** Questioned the rationale behind reinvesting in Indonesia given past headwinds. Management (Rajeev Varman) explained that recent green shoots from the "Super Hemat" campaign and fading boycotts have driven daily traffic up by 35-40 per store, justifying a double-down to restore pre-COVID margins (15% restaurant EBITDA, 27 million IDR ADS).

### One-line takeaway
Restaurant Brands Asia reported a strong Q2 FY27 with a 14% SSSG in India and a 281% jump in company-level EBITDA, while showing early turnaround green shoots in Indonesia following value-led promotions.
