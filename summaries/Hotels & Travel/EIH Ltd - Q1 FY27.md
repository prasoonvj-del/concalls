# EIH Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong domestic demand driving revenue growth despite headwinds from the West Asia crisis impacting foreign arrivals.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 698 crore (up 15% YoY from Rs 609 crore)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 207 crore (up from Rs 195 crore)
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 120 crore 
- Flight Catering (OFS) Revenue: Rs 154 crore
- Consolidated Operating Cash Flow (Q1): Rs 183 crore
- Consolidated Capex (Q1): Rs 148 crore
- Net Funds Increase (Q1): Rs 23 crore
- Industry Occupancy: Higher by 2% to 4% YoY
- Industry ARR: Higher by 6% to 8% YoY
- EIH MPI (Market Penetration Index): 108 (vs 106 last year)
- EIH RGI (Revenue Generation Index): 125% (vs 121% last year)
- Oberoi Brand Luxury Segment RevPAR Growth (Industry): 13.2%
- Oberoi Hotels RevPAR Growth: 8.2% (11.4% excluding Oberoi Rajgarh Palace)
- Trident Brand Upper Upscale Segment RevPAR Growth (Industry): 9.2%
- Trident Hotels RevPAR Growth: 13.8%
- Trident RGI: 162 (vs 155 last year)
- Overall RevPAR (all hotels including managed): From Rs 11,352 to Rs 12,801

### Highlights
- Domestic demand remained robust, offsetting the impact of lower foreign tourist arrivals caused by the West Asia crisis/Iran-US war.
- 14 out of 15 EIH hotels ranked 1st or 2nd in their STR comp set (8 ranked 1st, 6 ranked 2nd).
- The Oberoi Rajgarh Palace (opened in November) received 5 awards during the quarter and is currently in its ramp-up/stabilization stage.
- Flight catering (OFS) business recorded healthy revenue growth driven by new flight operations and higher international airline business.
- Management highlights a commitment to reducing employee working hours to comply with working hours limits, reduce attrition, and improve service quality.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects foreign tourist arrivals to return to normalcy in Q3 and Q4.
- Management anticipates increasing ARR for the current year due to limited supply and upcoming MICE events like BRICS and the Aviation show.
- Robust expansion plan of nearly 30 new properties (owned and managed) to be in operation by 2031.
- Pipeline summary includes 7 directly owned/associate properties in the pipeline and 23 managed hotels (1,833 keys) in the pipeline over the next 5 years. One managed property was postponed beyond 2032.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 industry performance was positive across occupancy and ARR.
- Oberoi brand attracts a higher percentage of foreign visitors and was thus more impacted by the West Asia crisis.
- Trident brand saw strong performance, particularly in Mumbai (TNP and BKC).
- Large upcoming events (such as the BRICS summit, aviation show in Bangalore, and potential ripple effects in Delhi/Mumbai) expected to provide tailwinds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin growth was impacted by several factors: the ramp-up phase of Oberoi Rajgarh Palace, higher marketing expenditure to drive domestic bookings, higher IT/automation spend, write-offs due to room renovations in Mumbai (Rs 7.5 crore), and power/fuel cost increases from the Hormuz crisis/Iran-US war.
- Employee costs reflect headcount increases, increments, labour code impacts, and Rajgarh additions, alongside deliberate efforts to manage working hours.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q1 consolidated capex was Rs 148 crore.
- Expansion projects in progress include The Oberoi London (opening 2028), Hebbal Bangalore (Oberoi and Trident hotels plus 7.63 lakh sq ft retail/F&B; total development area >1.3 million sq ft), Goa owned hotel (expected opening late 2029 per annual report), and The Oberoi Grand, Kolkata (197 keys, revised opening scheduled for September 2028).
- Kolkata timeline delayed due to unforeseen structural restoration requirements of the historic building and a 2-month city-wide halt on construction following a local incident.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a healthy cash balance at the end of the quarter to support long-term growth plans over the next 3-4 years.
- Cash flow from operations was Rs 183 crore against Rs 148 crore spent on capex.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions (West Asia crisis / Iran-US war) impacting foreign tourist arrivals and international bookings.
- Power and fuel cost pressures stemming from the Hormuz crisis.
- Construction and restoration delays at heritage projects like Kolkata due to safety compliance and regulatory halts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Deepak Saha asked about the revenue/EBITDA gap and whether renovation/Rajgarh costs persist.** Management clarified that Mumbai renovations are timed for summer lulls and will complete ahead of the winter season, while leisure hotels like Rajgarh take up to 3 years to stabilize.
- **Deepak Saha asked about the reason for Kolkata Oberoi's delay to 2029/2028.** Management cited complex historic building restoration requirements for safety compliance and a city-wide construction halt in Kolkata following a tragic incident.
- **Vaibhav Mulay asked about the flight catering revenue and margin impact.** Management stated OFS revenue was Rs 154 crore with healthy growth, noting it was profitable and did not significantly impact margins negatively.
- **Amit Agarwal asked about the Grand Hotel (Kolkata) room count and timeline.** Management confirmed 197 keys with a targeted opening of September 2028, noting interior finishes take considerable time.
- **Rajeev Bharti asked for quantification of various cost headwinds.** Management noted an impact of roughly Rs 9 crore on a like-to-like basis, detailing Rs 4 crore in extra marketing, IT automation spends, renovation write-offs of Rs 7.5 crore, and power/fuel increases.

### One-line takeaway
EIH delivered a 15% revenue growth in Q1 FY27 underpinned by strong domestic demand, though EBITDA margins faced temporary headwinds from leisure hotel ramp-ups, marketing/IT investments, and room renovations.
