# Chalet Hotels Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-05 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong core performance and margin expansion despite geopolitical headwinds and temporary construction-related disruptions in the MMR portfolio.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (ex-residential):** Rs 5,140 million (+10% YoY)
*   **EBITDA (ex-residential):** Rs 2,400 million (+15% YoY)
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 46.7% (+231 bps YoY)
*   **Hospitality Revenue:** Rs 4,185 million (+9% YoY)
*   **Hospitality EBITDA:** Rs 1,784 million (+11% YoY)
*   **Hospitality EBITDA Margin:** 42.6% (+92 bps YoY)
*   **Commercial Revenue:** Rs 865 million (+18% YoY)
*   **Commercial EBITDA:** Rs 735 million (+21% YoY)
*   **Commercial EBITDA Margin:** 85% (+193 bps YoY)
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 20,405 million (slightly higher vs March '26)
*   **Average Cost of Finance:** 7.4% (vs 7.5% in March '26)

### Highlights
*   **Hospitality:** RevPAR increased 6.5% YoY, driven by an 8.5% YoY growth in ADRs. Resorts outperformed business hotels with 19% RevPAR growth.
*   **Commercial:** Monthly rental exit run rate reached Rs 290 million in June '26. Portfolio committed occupancy is ~91%.
*   **Residential:** Phase 1 of Koramangala project completed; 168 units of Phase 2 pending handover in FY27.
*   **MMR Portfolio:** Performance impacted by construction at Powai and renovation at Vashi (which is now ready for rebranding). Management expects recovery in H2.
*   **Talent:** Ranked 8th among mid-sized workplaces by Great Places to Work.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Commercial:** Expects monthly rentals to scale to Rs 300-320 million during FY27.
*   **Capex:** Planned capex of ~Rs 30 billion over FY27-FY29, to be funded largely through internal accruals.
*   **Projects:** CIGNUS II (Powai) on track for substantial completion by FY27 end. Taj Delhi Airport project to launch at least 70 rooms in Q4 FY27.
*   **Strategy:** Management maintains a target of 20% of the portfolio in the leisure segment.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro:** International business (ex-crew) remained flat YoY due to the West Asia conflict. Management expects normalization 60 days after conflict resolution.
*   **Domestic:** Strong demand driven by rising affluence and discretionary spend.
*   **Resorts:** Management sees potential for stabilized occupancy between 60% and 65%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Efficiency:** Margin expansion driven by operational efficiency measures and resilient pricing.
*   **Finance Cost:** Average cost of finance declined to 7.4% due to treasury practices and leverage mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Powai Complex:** Adding 0.9 million sq. ft. of office space in current fiscal.
*   **Udaipur:** Evaluating expansion potential; currently working on internal designs for existing building.
*   **South Goa:** Planning to start construction by end of this quarter (post-monsoon).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Comfortable position of ~Rs 4 billion as of June '26.
*   **Debt Allocation:** Rs 10,914 million of net debt is allocable to assets under construction or not yet operational.
*   **Leverage:** Management noted no increase in leverage over the last 8 quarters despite acquisitions and refurbishments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** West Asia conflict impacting inbound Foreign Tourist Arrivals (FTAs).
*   **MMR Disruption:** Ongoing construction at Powai and renovation at Vashi impacted occupancy in the MMR region.
*   **Regulatory:** Delays in approvals for expansion projects (e.g., Udaipur, South Goa).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Strategy for MMR underperformance?** Management stated the underperformance is temporary due to construction pain (porch/renovation) for future gain, and expects recovery in H2.
*   **Q: Sustainability of leisure occupancy growth?** Management confirmed they have headroom and expect the portfolio to continue on its growth trend.
*   **Q: Status of Westin Hyderabad/Deloitte contract?** Renewed for 1 year; currently viewed as a partnership.
*   **Q: Impact of distribution channel mix on ARR?** Management clarified that commission payouts are at the bottom end of the market and there has been no major change in distribution channel mix.
*   **Q: Why the decline in OTA/transient mix?** Management noted this is not a trend but dependent on micro-market dynamics and segment shifts (e.g., group bookings).

### One-line takeaway
Chalet Hotels delivered solid growth in core hospitality and commercial segments, successfully offsetting geopolitical headwinds through domestic demand, while maintaining a disciplined balance sheet despite ongoing construction-related disruptions in the MMR portfolio.
