# Yatra Online Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong core business growth (Air/Corporate) and market share gains were offset by MICE weakness, geopolitical headwinds, and elevated affiliate commission costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management stopped providing formal guidance (previously hinted at H2 margin recovery to ~20%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "resilient" to acknowledging a "challenging" environment with a 6% industry-wide domestic aviation decline.
- **New topics:** Affiliate commission accounting treatment became a primary discussion point explaining the spike in "other expenses."
- **Promises:** Last quarter's promise of MICE recovery is partially delivered (37% QoQ growth), though margins remain compressed.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 213.6 crore (-39% YoY).
- **Gross Bookings:** Rs 2,165.5 crore (+6% YoY).
- **Gross Margin (Revenue less service cost):** Rs 125.5 crore (Flat YoY).
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 15 crore (-41% YoY).
- **PAT:** Loss of Rs 0.7 crore.
- **Air Passenger Volume:** Declined 2.4% YoY (vs industry decline of 6%).
- **Corporate Gross Bookings:** +18% YoY.

### Highlights
- **Market Share:** Air business outperformed the broader domestic aviation market (Yatra -2.4% YoY vs industry -6% YoY).
- **Corporate Growth:** 177 new corporate customers added; 54% of existing customers expanded business.
- **Travel Pro (SME/Mid-market):** 30+ customer closures, representing ~Rs 82.5 crore annual potential.
- **RECAP (Expense management):** 25 customer wins (8 live, 5 in implementation).
- **Kanoo Partnership:** Generating revenue; 1.5 months of execution included in Q2.
- **MICE:** 37% QoQ growth, though YoY declined 63% due to a high base in Q2 FY26.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide formal guidance due to macro volatility.
- Expects H2 to show significant improvement over H1.
- Management expects "other expenses" to taper by Rs 3-4 crore in coming quarters.
- Expects debt to improve in Q3 and Q4 as IPO proceeds are utilized.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Domestic Aviation:** Industry volumes -6% YoY; Yatra outperformed (-2.4% YoY).
- **International:** Long-haul outbound travel under pressure due to West Asia conflict, fuel costs, and rupee depreciation.
- **MICE:** Shift from large international programs to smaller, flexible groups due to budget constraints.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Gross Margin:** Remained stable YoY at Rs 125.5 crore.
- **Air Margins:** Expanded from 3.95% to 4.45% YoY.
- **Other Expenses:** Increased due to affiliate commissions (accounting treatment); management noted this is a short-term measure to protect market share.
- **Operating Leverage:** ~60% of costs are fixed; management expects 60% of incremental revenue to flow to the bottom line.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continued investment in GCP, Kanoo, RECAP, and Travel Pro (~Rs 5.2 crore cost in Q2).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash/Equivalents/Term Deposits:** Rs 218 crore.
- **Net Cash:** Improved by Rs 33.1 crore QoQ.
- **Debt:** Increased due to lower MICE float; management expects repayment via IPO proceeds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflict in West Asia impacting MICE and international travel.
- Working capital pressure on small travel agents (squeeze-out risk).
- High airfares and constrained capacity affecting demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nitin (Investec):** Asked about sequential revenue growth and affiliate costs. Management explained affiliate commissions are accounting-heavy and temporary.
- **Sonal (Prescient):** Suggested a "walk" in presentations to bridge affiliate costs. Management agreed to implement this.
- **Chirag (Motilal Oswal):** Asked about H2 vs H1. Management expects H2 improvement.
- **Akshit (Family Office):** Asked about receivables. Management attributed the increase to corporate business growth.
- **Moksh (Aurum Edge):** Asked about U.S. Holdco. Management stated it is a complex multi-jurisdictional process with no fixed timeline.

### One-line takeaway
Yatra demonstrated resilience by outperforming the domestic aviation market and growing corporate bookings, though MICE headwinds and elevated affiliate costs impacted reported profitability.
