# Yatra Online Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Core air, hotel and corporate segments show strong growth and market share gains, but MICE collapse and affiliate-driven expense pressure mask operating leverage; net loss reported.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained – no formal FY27 guidance either quarter; medium-term framework of ~15% gross booking growth driving RLSC and EBITDA growth reiterated.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "mixed with MICE at trough" to "constructive on H2 recovery"; domestic aviation deteriorated sharply (-6% YoY, -15% QoQ in Q2 vs +2.3% YoY in Q1); MICE showed sequential improvement (+37% QoQ) vs Q1 low base.
- **New topics/risks:** Affiliate commission accounting impact on other expenses (~INR 13 crore YoY) highlighted; US Holdco restructuring still pending; corporate card partnership delayed further; industry consolidation accelerating due to working capital squeeze; Kanoo partnership began execution (1.5 months in Q2).
- **Old topics disappeared:** PLB (productivity-linked bonus) closure with Middle Eastern carriers was a Q1 headwind; not mentioned in Q2 as air margins improved.
- **Last quarter promises delivered:** Travel Pro added 30+ closures (~INR 825M potential) vs 30+ in Q1; RECAP at 25 wins/8 live/5 implementing vs 20+ in Q1; Kanoo generating early revenue with positive operating contribution; MICE volumes up ~37% QoQ (guided ~50% in Q1); H2 margin recovery to ~20% adjusted EBITDA margin not yet visible (Q2 at 11.96%).

- **Corporate Gross Bookings:** up ~18% YoY; 54% existing customers expanded, 177 new customers live in year

### Highlights
- Air business outperformed market: gross bookings +15% YoY, margins expanded 50 bps to 4.45% on stronger supplier commercials
- Corporate travel +18% YoY with broad-based growth (54% existing customers expanded, 177 new logos)
- Stand-alone hotels +20% YoY, independent of MICE weakness
- MICE sequential recovery: +37% QoQ, stronger Q3 order book; strategic pricing flexibility to rebuild volumes
- Travel Pro and RECAP scaling: SME/mid-market traction and expense management cross-sell progressing
- Kanoo partnership live: managing ~30% of Kanoo's regional corporate business, positive operating contribution
- AI/automation driving efficiency: 5% YoY headcount efficiency gain, 25% QoQ growth in AI-handled chats
- Affiliate channel drove air volume growth but increased other expenses ~INR 13 crore YoY (commission accounting)
- Continued investment in growth initiatives (~INR 52 million in Q2 for Travel Pro, RECAP, Kanoo, GCP)

### Guidance & outlook
- No formal FY27 guidance due to macro fluctuations; medium-term framework (~15% gross booking growth → RLSC/EBITDA growth) maintained
- H2 expected to improve over H1: MICE recovery continuing, air/hotel trending per plan, Q3 slight improvement over Q2, Q4 "far better"
- Operating leverage: ~60% fixed costs → ~60% incremental revenue flow-through to bottom line
- RLSC growth expectation: low-to-mid teens for full year (management agreed with analyst estimate)
- Kanoo to scale in subsequent quarters; corporate card delayed until bank credit risk appetite improves
- US Holdco restructuring ongoing across multiple jurisdictions (Cyprus, US, Singapore, Cayman, India/SEBI); no timeline

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic aviation passenger traffic -6% YoY, -15% QoQ; international -19% YoY for Indian carriers
- Domestic airfares +26% YoY, international +20% YoY; rupee depreciation adding to overseas travel cost
- West Asia conflict disrupting long-haul outbound travel, connectivity, fuel costs
- Corporate group travel/MICE pressured by elevated costs and geopolitical uncertainty; shift to smaller, flexible groups
- Forward bookings showing encouraging signs; some deferred travel returning
- International corporate travel finding direct routes to Europe/US, bypassing Middle East
- Middle East ~35-40% of international travel; critical for MICE (Dubai/Abu Dhabi convention capacity)
- Industry consolidation accelerating: smaller agents squeezed by working capital needs (ticket prices up) and tech demands

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin flat YoY at INR 1,255 million despite MICE decline, offset by air/hotel growth
- Air gross margin % improved to 4.45% from 3.95% (better supplier commercials, revenue realization)
- Hotels & Packages gross margin % declined to 7.37% from 8.86% (MICE mix shift to smaller programs)
- Adjusted EBITDA margin on gross margin: 11.96% (down from ~20.3% YoY)
- Other expenses up ~INR 13 crore YoY, largely affiliate commissions (positive contribution margin but accounted below gross margin)
- Normalized other expenses level ~INR 47-48 crore (Q1 level); expect INR 3-4 crore tapering in coming quarters
- Technology/infrastructure investments for growth initiatives: ~INR 52 million in Q2
- Fixed costs ~60% of base (employee, infrastructure, data center); 5% YoY headcount efficiency gain

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Technology infrastructure investment via Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
- International expansion through Kanoo Travel partnership in Middle East
- Scaling Travel Pro (SME/mid-market) and RECAP (expense management)
- Hotel supply expansion: deeper chain relationships, Tier 2/3 inventory, aggregation platform integration
- No specific capex guidance in crore provided

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash & equivalents + term deposits: INR 2,180 million (Sep 30, 2026)
- Net cash position improved INR 331 million QoQ via collections streamlining
- Trade receivables: INR 6,100 million (vs INR 5,350 million Mar 2026) – increase driven by business growth (corporate +18%, airline receivables); corporate DSO stable
- Debt increased over last 3 quarters due to: (1) lower MICE float (advances), (2) IPO proceeds utilization starting
- Expect material debt improvement in Q3, full benefit in Q4 as IPO proceeds free up cash for repayment
- Factoring: INR 3,060 million in H1 FY27; doubtful debt provision: INR 83 million in H1 FY27
- Corporate card discussions with banks slower due to macro credit risk environment

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia conflict continuing to disrupt international travel and MICE
- Elevated airfares and constrained airline capacity
- Rupee depreciation increasing overseas travel costs
- MICE recovery uncertain; margins pressured by competitive pricing to rebuild volumes
- Corporate travel budgets under pressure; non-essential travel limited in sectors like IT
- US Holdco restructuring delayed by multi-jurisdictional compliance
- Corporate card partnership delayed
- No formal guidance due to macro uncertainty

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nitin Padmanabhan (Investec) on Other Expenses:** Asked about ~INR 15 crore YoY increase. Management: ~INR 13 crore increase, largely affiliate commissions (positive contribution margin but accounting puts commission in other expenses). Expect INR 3-4 crore tapering; normalized level ~INR 47-48 crore.
2. **Nitin Padmanabhan on Debt Increase:** Debt up 3 quarters. Management: (1) Lower MICE float, (2) IPO proceeds utilization starting – will repay debt; material improvement Q3, full benefit Q4.
3. **Sonal (Prescient) on Gross Margin Comparability:** Requested walk to adjust variable costs (affiliate commissions) below gross margin for peer comparability. Management agreed to provide walk in investor presentations.
4. **Chirag (Motilal Oswal) on H2 Trends & Operating Leverage:** H2 improving vs H1; MICE order book stronger. Operating leverage: ~60% fixed costs → ~60% incremental revenue flow-through. RLSC low-to-mid teens growth expectation for full year.
5. **Akshit Shetty (Family Office) on Receivables & Air Margin Quality:** Receivables up due to business growth (gross ticket value); DSO stable. Air margin quality: focus on gross margin % (4.45% vs 3.95% YoY), not take rate; growth at high margin quality.
6. **Prateek (Shubh Labh) on Industry Consolidation & Travel Pro Profitability:** Smaller agents squeezed by working capital (ticket prices up) and tech demands; consolidation accelerating. Travel Pro leverages existing platform (operating leverage), better supplier deals for SMEs, faster implementation (2 weeks vs 1.5-2 months), faster monetization.

### One-line takeaway
Yatra's core air, hotel, and corporate businesses delivered strong growth and market share gains in a difficult macro, but MICE collapse and affiliate-driven expense accounting pressure masked underlying operating leverage; H2 recovery hinges on MICE normalization and macro stabilization.
