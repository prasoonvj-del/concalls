# TBO TEK Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed/Cautious - Management reported strong operating leverage and growth in Europe/APAC, but highlighted significant geopolitical headwinds and volatility impacting the Middle East and overall demand.

### Key numbers
*   **GTV Growth:** 16% YoY.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA Growth:** 27% YoY.
*   **Middle East Growth:** 10% (constant currency).
*   **Europe/APAC Growth:** >30% YoY.
*   **Exceptional Loss:** Rs 8.1 crore (due to irregularities/fraud).
*   **FX Loss:** Rs 9 crore.
*   **Air Business Take Rate:** ~2% (down from 2.3-2.4%).
*   **Organic GP Margin:** 5.5% - 5.6%.

### Highlights
*   **Operating Leverage:** Demonstrated for the second consecutive quarter; SG&A growth (constant currency) was in high single digits, outpacing top-line growth.
*   **Classic Vacations:** Completed one year post-acquisition. Management has expanded the sales team by 20 people, implemented a CRM, and integrated supply organizations.
*   **Regional Performance:** Europe is now the largest source market. Middle East remains the most impacted region due to conflict.
*   **AI Strategy:** Management is investing in AI for process automation and sales tools; they view the B2B/complex travel model as AI-resilient.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth Target:** Management aims to deliver YoY growth in both top line and bottom line despite current headwinds.
*   **Q3 Outlook:** Management expects a "tough" Q3, noting it is historically the weakest quarter and currently faces high volatility.
*   **Investment:** No new market development investments are planned for the near term.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Headwinds:** Geopolitical conflicts (Middle East) and civil unrest (Europe) are causing cancellations and reduced demand.
*   **Market Dynamics:** High airfares and regional volatility (e.g., Saudi airport attacks) are pressuring demand.
*   **Resilience:** Management believes travel demand is "depressed" rather than structurally broken and expects a quick rebound once the climate improves.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Take Rates:** Air business take rates declined to ~2% due to lower incentives and refund income; management expects GP margins to remain in the 1%–1.2% range.
*   **FX Impact:** The Rs 9 crore FX loss was attributed to unhedged exposure; the company hedges ~75% of open exposures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Strategy:** Focus is on operational efficiency and integrating Classic Vacations rather than new market expansion.
*   **Classic Integration:** Tech platform integration for TBO supply into Classic is complete; TBO platform availability for Classic is expected in late November.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** A meaningful release of working capital occurred due to timing differences and the shift to credit lines replacing advance payments to service providers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Fraud:** Rs 8.1 crore loss identified in the UAE subsidiary due to suspected employee collusion; police complaints filed and insurance claims initiated.
*   **Regulatory:** Received an adjudication order regarding FEMA; the company plans to appeal and has not made a provision, citing legal advice that the penalty may not be sustained.
*   **Geopolitics:** Ongoing conflicts in the Middle East and riots in Europe are cited as unpredictable risks to revenue.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Manish Adukia (Goldman Sachs) on Classic Vacations:** Asked if Classic is a drag on growth. Management confirmed it is a drag mathematically but emphasized it is a long-term luxury play that will drive future bottom-line growth.
*   **Karan Uppal on Demand:** Asked about H2 outlook. Management stated Q3 is seasonally weak and volatile, making it difficult to predict, but they are aiming for YoY growth.
*   **Swapnil Potdukhe on Take Rates:** Asked about the decline in air take rates. Management explained it was due to lower incentives and refund income, noting they passed >50% of the impact to agents.
*   **Vibhor Singhal on AI:** Asked if AI apps like Muse threaten the business. Management reiterated that their focus on complex, multi-destination, and luxury travel makes them AI-resilient compared to point-to-point transactional models.
*   **Manish Adukia on Fraud:** Asked about the Rs 8.1 crore loss. Management confirmed it involved unauthorized bookings in a government travel agency account and is under investigation.

### One-line takeaway
TBO Tek is navigating a volatile geopolitical environment by focusing on operational leverage and luxury segment integration (Classic Vacations), while managing specific operational risks like fraud and regulatory scrutiny.
