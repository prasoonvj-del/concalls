# Le Travenues Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Resilient growth in core business and strong ramp-up in hotels, despite challenging environment for flights.

### Key numbers
- **GTV:** INR 5,304.47 crores, up 22% YoY.
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 320.96 crores, up 13% YoY.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 23.58 crores, down 17% YoY from INR 28.48 crores.
- **Reported PAT:** INR 63.42 crores (includes exceptional gain).
- **Passenger Segments:** Over 11% to INR 3.98 crores.
- **Train Segment:** Revenue INR 149.14 crores (up 16% YoY); Contribution Margin INR 47.23 crores (up 29% YoY); Segments rose 7% YoY to INR 2.9 crores. OTA rail market share close to 64%.
- **Bus Segment:** GTV INR 752.65 crores (up 32% YoY); Revenue INR 81.04 crores (up 24% YoY); Contribution Margin INR 43.14 crores (up 27% YoY).
- **Flight Segment:** Revenue down 8% YoY to INR 82.66 crores; Contribution Margin down 10% YoY to INR 35.68 crores; Gross take rate fell from 9.4% to 7%.
- **Hotel Segment:** GTV INR 111.34 crores (up 2.6x YoY, 39% QoQ); Room nights approx 4.7 lakh (over 5,100 per day net of cancellations); Gross take rate >12%; Revenue grew >6x YoY to INR 8.1 crores; Contribution loss of INR 1.21 crores; Total direct hotels tied up >12,000 across ~1,100 towns. Segment investment estimated at slightly north of INR 11 crores (~INR 10 crores in people/tech below contribution margin).
- **International Flight GTV:** Up 59% YoY.
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** INR 1,831.71 crores (net of borrowings).
- **Operating Cash Flow:** INR 76.44 crores vs INR 91.55 crores last year.
- **Exceptional Gain:** INR 40.66 crores from partial stake sale in Freshbus on August 13, 2026.

### Highlights
- Hotels disclosed as a separate reporting segment following emerging product-market fit and scale. Brevistay consolidated.
- Food on train services moved into trains.
- ConfirmTkt achieved a 4.9 rating on Google Play Store (highest-rated travel app globally at >100M downloads scale).
- Launched "women's zone" on AbhiBus and "Smart Lock" agentic AI product for flights.
- TARA (AI) resolves 94% of chat queries without human intervention and 82% of customer calls end-to-end.
- Partial stake sale in Freshbus to focus on asset-light tech and distribution platform; Freshbus remains a commercial partner.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects hotel segment investment to remain in a similar range of INR 10 crores to INR 12 crores over the next couple of quarters, subject to ground performance.
- Aspires to remain the fastest-growing OTA in India for hotels and become the largest player serving the budget to mid-market hotel category over the next 4 to 5 years.
- Flight growth remains cautious due to high fares and capacity constraints.
- Long-term EBITDA is intended to stick around mid-single-digit levels.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Train volumes helped by advanced bookings for Dussehra and Diwali; momentum needs to sustain post-festive season.
- Buses continue to grow strongly and secularly.
- Flights faced a tougher external environment with airfares up 30% YoY on several routes and overall market volume down nearly 6% in Q2. Festive demand in Q3 is a shade lower than expected.
- Hotel TAM estimated at INR 1.57 lakh crores in FY26, reaching INR 1.94 lakh crores by FY28 (VIDEC report). Lodging represents 30% of total travel spending. 60% of hotel room nights in India sell below INR 2,000/night, and independent hotels account for 73%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Hotel contribution margin improved from -55% of revenue a year ago to -15% (contribution loss of INR 1.2 crores).
- Higher flight discounts funded due to large price delta between planes and buses/trains for NBU users.
- Train contribution margin at 32% of revenue; bus contribution margin at 53% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 2,000 more directly contracted hotels last quarter, crossing 12,000 total.
- Pursuing selective acquisitions with strategic fit, strong team, technology/distribution synergy, and reasonable pricing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total cash, cash equivalents, fixed deposits, and mutual funds net of borrowings stands at INR 1,831.71 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Capacity constraints and high oil prices/fares in the flight segment impacting volume growth.
- Softer-than-expected start to Q3 festive demand on flights.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Anmol Garg (DAM Capital) asked about flight revenue per ticket/discounts and Q3 early trends.** Management stated discounts were elevated to make flights affordable for NBU users amid 30% higher airfares; discounts won't step up further. Q3 festive demand on flights is a shade lower than expected.
- **Anmol Garg asked when hotel take rates will reach 16%-17% and when the segment will be profitable.** Management stated blended take rates are currently 12% due to mix, but direct supply take rates are higher and will edge up over 3-4 quarters. Profitability is expected in 2-3 years as they prioritize scale and growth.
- **Pankaj (BofA Securities) asked to quantify hotel investment guardrails.** Management stated total hotel investment was ~INR 11 crores this quarter and expects a similar range of INR 10-12 crores over the next couple of quarters, tied to payback periods.
- **Karan Uppal (PhillipCapital) asked about hotel room night scaling and ATV trends.** Management noted ATV will remain anchored to customer affordability (~INR 2,200) since 60% of Indian rooms sell below INR 2,000, but experience-led playbooks could bring in Tier 1 customers.
- **Swapnil (JM Financial) asked about the threat of personal AI agents (horizontals vs. verticals).** Management answered that horizontals lack deep vertical supply data, contextual awareness (e.g., train vs. flight history), and trust/distribution scale, and users ultimately gravitate toward specialists.

### One-line takeaway
Le Travenues Technology reported resilient Q2 FY27 performance (GTV up 22% YoY, Revenue up 13% YoY) driven by strong growth in buses and trains, while formally reporting a rapidly scaling hotel segment that reduced contribution losses despite ongoing investments.
