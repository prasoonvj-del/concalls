# Le Travenues Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Resilient growth in core business, strong ramp-up in hotels, and all-time high PAT aided by a stake sale, despite a challenging environment for flights.

### Key numbers
- **GTV:** INR 5,304.47 crores, up 22% YoY
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 320.96 crores, up 13% YoY
- **Passenger segments:** 3.98 crores, up over 11% YoY
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 23.58 crores, down 17% YoY from INR 28.48 crores
- **Reported PAT:** INR 63.42 crores (includes a one-time exceptional gain of INR 40.66 crores from the partial sale of stake in Freshbus)
- **Train segment:** Passenger segments up 7% YoY to 2.9 crores; Revenue up 16% to INR 149.14 crores; Contribution margin up 29% to INR 47.23 crores (32% of revenue); OTA rail market share close to 64%
- **Bus segment (AbhiBus):** GTV up 32% to INR 752.65 crores; Revenue up 24% to INR 81.04 crores; Contribution margin up 27% to INR 43.14 crores (53% of revenue)
- **Flight segment:** GTV up 37% YoY; Revenue down 8% to INR 82.66 crores; Contribution margin down 10% to INR 35.68 crores; Gross take rate fell from 9.4% to 7%
- **Hotel segment:** GTV up 2.6x YoY to INR 111.34 crores (up 39% sequentially); Room nights over 4.7 lakh (approx. 5,100 per day net of cancellations); Gross take rate >12%; Segment contribution loss of INR 1.21 crores; Total hotel investment slightly north of INR 11 crores (including INR 10 crores in people and tech below contribution margin)
- **Cash and cash equivalents (net of borrowings):** INR 1,831.71 crores
- **Operating cash flow:** INR 76.44 crores vs INR 91.55 crores last year

### Highlights
- Hotels disclosed as a separate reporting segment for the first time due to emerging product-market fit and scale; Brevistay consolidated.
- Food on train services moved into trains; Freshbus stake partially sold on August 13 resulting in an exceptional gain of INR 40.66 crores (Freshbus remains a commercial partner).
- ConfirmTkt achieved a 4.9 rating on Google Play Store (highest-rated travel app globally at >100M downloads scale).
- AI agent TARA handled 43 lakh queries, resolving 94% of chat queries without human intervention and 82% of customer calls end-to-end.
- *Continuity note:* The call continues the positive momentum from Q2 FY27 reported earlier, while formally breaking out hotels and scaling investments deliberately.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects hotel investments to remain in a similar range of INR 10 crores to INR 12 crores over the next couple of quarters, subject to incremental returns.
- Management aims to become the largest budget-to-mid-market hotel OTA on a room night basis over the next 4 to 5 years.
- Management remains cautious on the flight segment near-term due to capacity constraints and high fares.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall flight market volume down nearly 6% YoY in Q2 due to 30% higher fares and limited capacity; international flight GTV grew 59% YoY.
- Train recovery observed with advanced bookings for Dussehra and Diwali, though management wants momentum post-festive season before calling a sustained recovery.
- Bus segment continues strong secular growth, outpacing the overall OTA bus market for nearly 3 years.
- VIDEC estimates India's hotel market at INR 1.57 lakh crores in FY26, reaching INR 1.94 lakh crores by FY28; 60% of hotel room nights sell below INR 2,000/night, and independent hotels account for 73% of rooms.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Flight gross take rate compressed to 7% due to higher base fares, international mix, and increased discounts funded by ixigo to make flights affordable for NBU users.
- Hotel gross take rate exceeded 12% on a blended basis; segment contribution margin improved from minus 55% to minus 15% YoY.
- Group adjusted EBITDA impacted by deliberate step-up in people and technology investments for hotels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Direct hotel supply expanded to over 12,000 directly contracted hotels across ~1,100 towns (up from 10,000 last quarter).
- 86%+ of hotel bookings originated from existing platform captive users.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total cash, cash equivalents, mutual funds, and FDs net of borrowings stand at INR 1,831.71 crores.
- Operating cash flow for the period was INR 76.44 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High airline fares and constrained capacity continue to weigh on domestic flight volume growth and load factors.
- Festive demand for flights was a shade lower than expected due to unfavorable pricing for consumers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Anmol Garg (DAM Capital) on flight discount sustainability:** Management stated that discount levels are comfortable relative to current high fares and will not be stepped up further; they expect to benefit from latent demand if fares drop.
- **Anmol Garg on hotel take rate and profitability:** Management noted that direct supply scaling will drive blended take rates toward 16%-17% over the long term (some direct hotels already at that level), while heavy investment will continue for 2-3 years for growth.
- **Pankaj (BofA Securities) on hotel investment guardrails:** Management quantified near-term investment at INR 10-12 crores per quarter, bound by strict payback period tracking and maintaining company-level mid-single-digit EBITDA.
- **Karan Uppal (PhillipCapital) on hotel Average Transaction Value (ATV):** Management stated ATV (~INR 2,200) aligns with budget/mid-market customer affordability and 60% of Indian hotel nights priced below INR 2,000, ensuring high repeat rates through problem-solving rather than chasing premium ATV.
- **Swapnil (JM Financial) on threat from consumer AI agents:** Management argued that vertical-specific data, proprietary peace-of-mind solutions, and existing trusted distribution scale give specialized OTAs a moat over horizontal AI agents.

### One-line takeaway
Le Travenues Technology reported resilient Q2 FY27 growth led by buses and an aggressive, deliberate scaling of its newly disclosed hotel segment, even as high airfares and capacity constraints pressured the flight business.
