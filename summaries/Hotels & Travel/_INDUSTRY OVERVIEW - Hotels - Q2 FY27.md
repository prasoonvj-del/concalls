# Hotels & Travel - Hotels: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Across the board, hotel managements reported a resilient and largely positive Q2 FY27 despite the traditionally soft monsoon season. Growth was predominantly rate-led, supported by strong domestic leisure and corporate/MICE demand. While geopolitical tensions in West Asia and aviation constraints posed headwinds for international inbound traffic, robust domestic travel and structural shifts in dining and stays drove strong top-line and EBITDA performances for most players.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements universally agree that domestic demand remains exceptionally strong, and anticipate a robust H2 FY27 driven by the festive season, an expanded wedding calendar, and active MICE pipelines. 
*   **Divergence:** Views on corporate demand and international travel diverged. While Indian Hotels Company Ltd (IHCL), Samhi Hotels, and Brigade Hotel Ventures highlighted strong corporate and MICE momentum, Lemon Tree Hotels described corporate demand as "tepid" due to geopolitical uncertainty and inflation. Similarly, international tourist arrivals showed mixed trends: Leela Palaces and IHCL noted slow recovery impacted by West Asian conflicts, whereas Ventive Hospitality and Mahindra Holidays reported resilience in leisure/resort segments.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
Margins were pressured by regulatory and utility headwinds, most notably changes in GST laws (such as input tax credit reversals on rooms priced below Rs 7,500), which impacted companies like Brigade, Juniper Hotels, Kamat Hotels, Royal Orchid, Samhi Hotels, and Praveg. Rising energy/gas costs (HLP) and labor law/PF changes also added cost pressures (IHCL, Juniper, Praveg, Ventive). However, strong operating leverage, higher Average Daily Rates (ADRs), and strict cost controls enabled significant margin expansion for players like Chalet Hotels, IHCL, Kamat Hotels, Leela Palaces, Lemon Tree, Viceroy Hotels, and Wonderla Holidays.

## Capex & capacity
The sector remains in an aggressive expansion phase, balancing greenfield projects, brownfield renovations, and asset-light models:
*   **Chalet Hotels** lifted its FY27-29 capex guidance to Rs 3,400–3,600 crore.
*   **Juniper Hotels** is proceeding with a Rs 2,000 crore outlay across five major projects and the acquisition of Novotel Imagicaa.
*   **Leela Palaces** is expanding toward 5,200 keys by FY30 with a major pipeline including Mumbai BKC.
*   **Lemon Tree** and **Samhi Hotels** are scaling up through asset-light partnerships and substantial capital commitments (~Rs 3,000 crore and ~Rs 2,250 crore respectively).
*   Asset-light management contracts remain a preferred strategy for rapid scaling among players like IHCL, Kamat Hotels, and Royal Orchid.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Raised / Upgraded:** Indian Hotels Company Ltd upgraded its full-year FY27 consolidated revenue growth guidance by 300 bps to 15%-17% YoY. Chalet Hotels raised its FY27-29 capex guidance from ~Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 3,400–3,600 crore.
*   **Tone Shifts:** Chalet Hotels shifted from a cautious tone on recovery in Q1 to highly upbeat in Q2. Samhi Hotels shifted from cautious international headwinds in Q1 to strong confidence for H2. Praveg reported a mixed/negative earnings tone due to seasonal losses and GST impacts.
*   **Delivery on Promises:** Royal Orchid successfully turned its key 'ICONIQA' asset EBITDA positive as promised last quarter. Chalet Hotels partially delivered on its crew-contract recovery (returning 20 rooms/day to service).

## What to watch next quarter
*   Execution and ramp-up of newly acquired or opened assets (e.g., Juniper's integration of Novotel Imagicaa, Brigade's Kochi rebranding, Viceroy's convention center reopening in December).
*   The actual realization of H2 demand tailwinds from weddings, festivals, and corporate/MICE events.
*   Further regulatory clarity or adjustments regarding GST input tax credit rules affecting mid-scale portfolios.

## Company one-liners
*   **Brigade Hotel Ventures Ltd:** Brigade Hotel Ventures delivered a rate-led Q2 FY27 with 10% revenue growth, a margin expansion to 36.7%, and doubled PAT, backed by strong MICE recovery, robust domestic demand, and lower finance costs.
*   **Chalet Hotels Ltd:** Robust margin expansion and cash-flow-driven growth are offset by construction delays and geopolitical headwinds, but management’s disciplined capex and strong CRE base keep FY27 outlook upbeat.
*   **Indian Hotels Company Ltd:** IHCL delivered strong Q2 FY27 results with 22% top-line growth and upgraded its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance to 15%-17% driven by resilient domestic travel, MICE expansion, and asset-light scaling.
*   **Juniper Hotels Ltd:** Juniper Hotels reported resilient Q2 FY27 performance with 10% total income growth and a 121% surge in PAT, while aggressively advancing its Rs 2,000 crore expansion pipeline across greenfield projects and the newly acquired Novotel Imagicaa.
*   **Kamat Hotels (India) Ltd:** Kamat Hotels delivered a breakout Q2 FY27 with a 131% surge in EBITDA and 1300 bps margin expansion, supported by higher occupancy and a near-zero net debt balance sheet heading into the seasonally stronger H2.
*   **Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts Ltd:** The Leela reported a stellar quarter with 33% revenue and 36% EBITDA growth, backed by sustained luxury pricing power, robust non-room revenue streams, and a strong expansion pipeline.
*   **Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd:** Lemon Tree delivered record Q2 performance driven by strong RevPAR growth and operational leverage, while continuing its asset-light expansion despite tepid corporate demand.
*   **Mahindra Holidays & Resorts India Ltd:** MHRIL delivered a mixed standalone financial performance with lower EBITDA and PAT due to growth-linked costs and room renovations, offset by robust 15% resort revenue growth, higher occupancy (75.6%), and strong upgrade momentum.
*   **Praveg Ltd:** Praveg reported a net loss of Rs 20.13 crore in Q2 FY27 due to monsoon seasonality, GST law changes, and higher labor costs, but management remains optimistic on a strong recovery in H2 driven by weddings, events, and newer properties like Lakshadweep.
*   **Royal Orchid Hotels Ltd:** Royal Orchid delivered strong YoY growth and achieved an EBITDA-positive milestone for ICONIQA, though near-term profitability remains pressured by pre-opening costs and GST regulatory headwinds.
*   **Samhi Hotels Ltd:** SAMHI delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 12.6% same-store revenue growth and 78.6% occupancy, supported by robust domestic office demand, while advancing its upscale expansion pipeline and steadily deleveraging its balance sheet.
*   **Ventive Hospitality Ltd:** Ventive delivered double-digit operating growth in its seasonally softest quarter, driven by a Maldives turnaround and steady India momentum, setting up for an exceptional H2.
*   **Viceroy Hotels Ltd:** Viceroy Hotels delivered a strong Q2 and H1 FY27 with a 61% surge in H1 total income and 81% growth in EBITDA, driven by Courtyard's ramp-up and strong executive apartment performance, even as Marriott's convention center remained closed for expansion.
*   **Wonderla Holidays Ltd:** Wonderla Holidays delivered a strong Q2 and H1 FY27—marked by a 42% YoY income growth and 90% EBITDA growth—anchored by the successful ramp-up of the Chennai park, robust resort performance, and steady momentum across mature existing parks.
