# Samhi Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Same-store revenue grew by 12.6% (beating the long-term guidance of 9% to 11%) and portfolio occupancy reached 78.6%, driven by robust domestic demand and strong MICE pipelines.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (same-store revenue growth guidance at 9% to 11%; net debt-to-EBITDA long-term target at 2.5x).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from cautious regarding international headwinds/soft rates in Q1 to highly confident for H2, backed by visible MICE calendars, improved booking windows, and strong pricing power.
- **New topics or risks:** Mention of potential repo rate increases by the RBI and their minimal impact on borrowing costs; detailed breakdown of the Sriperumbudur expansion (155 rooms, ~Rs. 160 crore capex, 15%-17% ROCE). Old risk regarding the West Asia crisis impacting international travel was absent this quarter, replaced by a focus on domestic office/GCC demand.
- **Last quarter's promises:** RARE India pilot integration is on track to go live on the Marriott Bonvoy outdoor collection in December 2026; W Hyderabad remains on track for Q2 FY28 opening.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs. 342.7 crore (up 15.6% YoY)
- **Same-Store Revenue Growth:** 12.6% YoY
- **Same-Store RevPAR Growth:** 14% YoY (driven by 9% ADR growth and 3.6% occupancy growth)
- **Portfolio Occupancy:** 78.6% (up from 75% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 123.8 crore (up 12% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 36.1% (comparable EBITDA margin ~39.1% excluding the ~Rs. 10 crore GST regulatory impact)
- **Finance Cost:** Rs. 38.3 crore (down 10.3% YoY)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT) before exceptional items:** Rs. 53.4 crore (up 39.9% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs. 41.1 crore (SAMHI share Rs. 33.5 crore)
- **Net Debt:** Rs. 1,458 crore as of September 2026 (down Rs. 35 crore QoQ)
- **Net Debt to Trailing 12-Month EBITDA:** 3.1x (2.2x after adjusting for capital deployed in growth projects)
- **Effective Interest Rate:** ~7.8%
- **Free Cash Flow:** ~Rs. 53 crore for the quarter after cash interest of Rs. 35 crore

### Highlights
- 37% of room nights in the quarter had >90% occupancy (effectively sold out), up from 32% YoY.
- ~80% of room nights were sold to domestic travelers, supported by local office, GCC, and industrial park demand (Bangalore and Hyderabad net office absorption reached 7.9 million square feet in Q2).
- Recognized an exceptional gain of Rs. 3.9 crore from the sale of a non-core land parcel at Kusur.
- Published the company's first sustainability report.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains long-term same-store revenue growth guidance of 9% to 11%.
- Management expects net debt-to-EBITDA to reach a "2 handle" by the end of the fiscal year or early next year, heading toward the 2.5x long-term target.
- Upper and upper-upscale portfolio contribution to income is expected to rise from 42% to ~60% by FY30.
- W Hyderabad is expected to add ~10% to the FY27 income base on a stabilized basis, targeting an opening in Q2 FY28.
- RARE India fee stream is expected to start contributing in FY28, generating ~Rs. 15 crore to Rs. 20 crore top line in the next fiscal year.
- Cumulative net capex from FY27 to FY31 is guided at ~Rs. 2,250 crore (gross ~Rs. 2,400 crore, with Rs. 150 crore funded by GIC).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Net office absorption surged in key markets like Bangalore and Hyderabad (Hyderabad more than doubled YoY to 3.8 million square feet in Q2).
- Airline passenger traffic declined YoY due to route rationalization from higher fuel prices, which management noted compresses supply rather than signaling a demand issue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- GST regulation changes (shift from 12% with ITC to 5% without ITC) impacted EBITDA by ~Rs. 10 crore in Q2; comparable EBITDA was Rs. 133.8 crore (up 21.3% YoY). The base effect will normalize from next quarter onwards.
- Business delivered a 60% flow-through before accounting for input tax credit loss.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Secured pipeline adds ~1,670 rooms and moves 473 rooms upmarket.
- W Hyderabad is in final stages (MEP, facade, fit-outs) with a targeted Q2 FY28 opening.
- Westin and Tribute portfolio (Whitefield, Bangalore): 142 existing rooms undergoing phased renovation; Westin tower site is at basement construction/slab stage.
- Ingka Center Noida (162 rooms): Reached second-floor slab, benefiting from a long-term variable lease limiting upfront capital.
- Sriperumbudur: Expanded to a 155-room separate Marriott hotel instead of a Fairfield extension; capex expected at ~Rs. 160 crore with a 15% to 17% ROCE yield on cost.
- Navi Mumbai: Approvals and design progressing; capex guided at ~Rs. 900 crore to Rs. 1,000 crore, with ground-breaking expected in early FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt decreased by Rs. 35 crore QoQ to Rs. 1,458 crore.
- Capital work-in-progress (CWIP) increased to Rs. 219 crore from Rs. 139 crore in March due to investments in W Hyderabad and Westin Bangalore.
- Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs. 279 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential gradual increase in borrowing costs due to recent RBI repo rate hikes.
- Execution and approval timelines (e.g., local authority delays and fire inspection contingencies for new projects).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital) on Sriperumbudur & RARE:** Management clarified that the 155-room Sriperumbudur project requires ~Rs. 160 crore capex and targets a 15%-17% ROCE. On RARE India, active hotels fluctuate around 70-85, and the outdoor collection goes live in December.
- **Jinesh Joshi (PL Capital) on GST & W Hyderabad:** Management noted final GST notifications regarding input tax credit on commissions are still awaited. W Hyderabad completion is targeted for end of Q4 FY27 with built-in contingencies for approvals to ensure a Q2 FY28 opening.
- **Achal Kumar (HSBC) on Net Debt/EBITDA & Asset Recycling:** Management stated net debt-to-EBITDA will reach <3x by fiscal year-end/early next year and hit 2.5x in ~18 months, supported by incremental EBITDA from W Hyderabad and potential asset recycling (2 hotels earmarked with realization value of Rs. 200 crore to Rs. 230 crore).
- **Viraj Mahadevia (MoneyGrow) on Deferred Tax:** Management explained that deferred tax assets from accumulated losses are recognized as profits turn positive; ~Rs. 100 crore of tax assets remain to be recognized in Q4.
- **Vikas Ahuja (Antique Stock Broking) on Revenue Guidance:** Management clarified that 9%-11% is same-store guidance, but total revenue growth will return to high-teens as new inventory (W Hyderabad, RARE, etc.) comes online.

### One-line takeaway
SAMHI delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 12.6% same-store revenue growth and 78.6% occupancy, supported by robust domestic office demand, while advancing its upscale expansion pipeline and steadily deleveraging its balance sheet.
