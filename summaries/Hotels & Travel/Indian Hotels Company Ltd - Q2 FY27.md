# Indian Hotels Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - 18th consecutive quarter of record performance backed by strong domestic demand, disciplined execution, and upgraded full-year guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated operating revenue:** INR2,484 crore, up 22% YoY.
- **Operating EBITDA (Consolidated):** INR706 crore, up 24% YoY; margin at 28.4% (up 50 bps YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR349 crore, up 23% YoY.
- **Standalone operating revenue:** INR1,336 crore, up 26% YoY.
- **Standalone operating EBITDA:** INR520 crore, up 40% YoY; margin at 38.9% (up 400 bps YoY).
- **Standalone PAT margin:** 27%.
- **Gross cash reserves:** Over INR4,350 crore.
- **Management fee income:** INR156 crore, up 25% YoY.
- **Ginger consolidated revenue:** INR193 crore, up 25% YoY; EBITDA margin at 40%.
- **Taj Palace, New Delhi room revenue growth:** 132% YoY in Q2 FY27 (crossed INR100 crore in monthly revenue in September).
- **Occupancy:** Increased from 75% in Q2 last year to 82% in Q2 FY27.
- **RevPAR growth:** Double-digit across all brands; Standalone RevPAR up 28%; Consolidated domestic hotel RevPAR up 25%.
- **Portfolio & Expansion:** 47 hotels signed, 20 hotels opened in H1 FY27; total portfolio at 670 hotels (69,000+ keys), with 390 operational hotels.

### Highlights
- Delivered double-digit CAGR across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT over the past 5 years.
- Asset management and major upgrades (Taj Palace New Delhi, President Mumbai, Taj West End Bengaluru, Taj Fort Aguada Goa, Taj Lake Palace Udaipur) drove pricing power.
- Portfolio expansion remains capital-light: 94% of pipeline keys are capital-light (81% management contracts, 13% leases).
- Ginger became India’s first hotel brand to reach 100 operational hotels (targeting 150 by March 31, FY27); Qmin crossed 130 outlets; Ama Stays & Trails crossed 400 villas/bungalows; Tree of Life grew to 50+ resorts.
- Announced the strategic merger of Oriental Hotels Ltd (OHL) with IHCL via an all-stock transaction (exchange ratio 25 IHCL shares for 117 OHL shares), expected to add 6%-7% upside to standalone operating revenue in FY28 (appointed date April 1, 2027).
- Paathya (ESG++): 41% energy from renewables, 361 EV charging stations across 170 locations, 92 water bottling plants installed, 83 skilling centers across 20 states training 56,000+ youth since 2020.
- Taj recognized as World's Strongest Luxury Hotel Brand by Brand Finance; Taj Falaknuma Palace and Taj Lake Palace retained 3 Michelin keys.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised FY27 consolidated revenue growth guidance from 12%-14% to **15% to 17%** (exceeding previous guidance by 300 bps).
- Hotel segment revenue growth guidance revised to **17% to 19%**.
- Management expects to cross the 400 operational hotel milestone in November or before the end of Q3 FY27.
- Management plans to revise the "Accelerate 2030" 700-hotel target portfolio guidance upwards by mid-November.
- Management expects management fee growth to sustain at a high teens CAGR.
- Management guided to open 50 hotels in total for FY27, with 30 hotels expected to open in H2.
- Management stated FY28 will mathematically be better than FY27, aided by 50+ hotel additions, OHL merger tailwinds, and a wedding calendar of 95 nights vs normal 60-65 nights.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remained resilient despite macro headwinds (West Asia geopolitical tensions, elevated fuel prices, reduced airline capacity, and higher airfares impacting international/long-haul travel).
- Supported by large MICE events (BRICS Summit, Bloomberg New Energy event, fintech events).
- Management notes a structural shift in Indian consumer behavior favoring consumption and travel.
- Event economy (social and corporate MICE) is a structural tailwind; upcoming events include the Aero Show in Bengaluru, Vibrant Gujarat, and an extended wedding calendar in H4.
- Foreign tourist arrivals (FTA) growth has a 3.9% 10-year CAGR; management views upcoming WTTC Global Summit in India as a potential upside catalyst for inbound tourism.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margin expanded by 400 bps YoY to 38.9%, driven by operating leverage, MICE activity, and management fee inflows.
- Consolidated operating EBITDA margin expanded by 50 bps YoY to 28.4%.
- International subsidiaries (London, U.S.) faced cost pressures from higher fuel/power due to West Asia crisis and property tax waiver removal in London.
- Statutory and regulatory cost increases (new provident fund rules, code of wages) were absorbed in the numbers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex spend in H1 FY27 is 20%-30% higher than historical annual averages, driven by asset upgrades, greenfield developments, and F&B concepts.
- 34,800+ keys in the pipeline, with 94% capital-light.
- Target of opening 5,000 keys every year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross cash reserves stand at over INR4,350 crore.
- Strong balance sheet provides flexibility for brand and revenue-enhancing initiatives, upgrades, greenfield developments, and F&B concepts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical tensions in West Asia leading to elevated fuel prices, airline capacity curbs, higher airfares, and impact on TajSATS airline catering and international properties (e.g., Dubai).
- Supply chain disruptions causing temporary project/renovation delays internationally.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Binay Singh (Morgan Stanley):** Asked if H2 incremental growth is driven by room rates and about TajSATS sequential EBITDA decline. Management replied that growth is a balanced mix of occupancy (up 6%-7%) and ARR, and TajSATS was impacted by June airline schedule curtailments but is diversifying into non-flight catering.
2. **Prateek Kumar (Jefferies):** Inquired about stripping out MICE impacts and the wedding calendar. Management stated MICE is structural, and next year's wedding calendar has ~95 nights vs 60-65 normal nights, acting as a tailwind.
3. **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital):** Asked about international subsidiary St. James Court margin contraction (down 360 bps YoY in H1). Management attributed it to delayed renovation works, supply chain issues, West Asia fuel cost impacts, and the removal of a London property tax waiver, but noted October demand is rebounding strongly.
4. **Sameet Sinha (Macquarie):** Questioned the strategic rationale for maintaining loss-making/low-margin international assets. Management defended international ownership (New York, London, Dubai) as crucial marketing assets, brand builders ("Taj is world's strongest hotel brand"), and differentiators that generate positive EBITDA and drive high-profile patronage.
5. **Achal Kumar (HSBC):** Asked about Tier 2/Tier 3 supply growth vs demand balance. Management responded that supply percentages look high on small bases, but unorganized-to-branded shifts and 15%-20% local demand growth easily absorb new supply.

### One-line takeaway
IHCL posted its 18th consecutive quarter of record performance in Q2 FY27, prompting management to raise its full-year revenue growth guidance to 15%-17% on robust domestic demand, high occupancy (82%), and accelerating capital-light expansions.
