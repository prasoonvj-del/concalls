# Indian Hotels Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - IHCL delivered its 18th consecutive quarter of record performance and raised its full-year FY27 consolidated revenue growth guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Operating Revenue:** Rs 2,484 crore, up 22% YoY.
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA:** Rs 706 crore, up 24% YoY; EBITDA margin stood at 28.4% (+50 bps YoY).
- **Consolidated Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 349 crore, up 23% YoY.
- **Standalone Operating Revenue:** Rs 1,336 crore, up 26% YoY.
- **Standalone Operating EBITDA:** Rs 520 crore, up 40% YoY; EBITDA margin stood at 38.9% (+400 bps YoY).
- **Standalone PAT Margin:** 27%.
- **RevPAR:** Standalone RevPAR grew 28% YoY; Consolidated domestic hotel RevPAR grew 25% YoY.
- **Occupancy:** Consolidated domestic occupancy rose to 82% from 75% in Q2 FY26.
- **Management Fee Income:** Rs 156 crore, up 25% YoY (vs Rs 125 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Ginger (Consolidated):** Revenue of Rs 193 crore (+25% YoY) with an EBITDA margin of 40%.
- **Cash Reserves:** Gross cash reserves exceeded Rs 4,350 crore as of Q2 FY27.
- **Portfolio Size:** 670 hotels (over 69,000 keys) operational or in pipeline, including 390 operational hotels. 47 hotels signed and 20 opened in H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Upgraded FY27 full-year consolidated revenue growth guidance by 300 bps to 15%-17% YoY.
- Hotel segment revenue expected to grow 17%-19% YoY in FY27.
- Ginger reached 100 operational hotels, becoming the first hotel brand in India to do so.
- Approved strategic all-stock merger of Oriental Hotels Ltd (OHL) with IHCL (ratio of 25 IHCL shares for every 117 OHL shares), appointed date set at April 1, 2027.
- Taj Palace, New Delhi crossed Rs 100 crore in monthly revenue in September 2026, with Q2 room revenue up 132% YoY.
- Taj was rated World's Strongest Luxury Hotel Brand by Brand Finance.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** Revised upward to 15%-17% YoY consolidated growth (Hotel segment growing 17%-19% YoY).
- **Openings Target:** 50 hotel openings in FY27 (30 planned for H2 FY27).
- **Ginger:** On track to reach 150 operational hotels by March 31, 2027.
- **Management Fees:** Confident of maintaining a high-teens CAGR; management internally targets crossing Rs 1,000 crore in management fee income within 2 years.
- **OHL Merger Impact:** Expected to add 6% to 7% upside to standalone operating revenue in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong domestic demand and major corporate/governmental MICE events (e.g., BRICS Summit, Bloomberg Summit) offset geopolitical headwinds in West Asia.
- Management highlights a structural shift in domestic travel and dining out trends in India.
- Wedding calendar expected to be stronger in H2 FY27 and FY28 (around 95 wedding nights next year versus ~60-65 normally).
- India's Foreign Tourist Arrivals (FTA) grew at a 3.9% CAGR over the past 10 years (vs ~11% domestic growth), presenting additional upside potential if international inflows improve.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated EBITDA margin expanded 50 bps YoY to 28.4%, while standalone margin expanded 400 bps YoY to 38.9%.
- International margins (e.g., London and US) were impacted by elevated fuel/power costs and the removal of a London property tax waiver.
- HR cost increases (provident fund rules and code of wages) were fully absorbed during the quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 FY27 capex spend was 20% to 30% higher than average annual capex spent over preceding years.
- Pipeline stands at >34,800 keys, 94% of which are capital-light (81% management contracts, 13% capital-light leases).
- Major upgrades recently completed at marquee properties: Taj Palace New Delhi, President Mumbai, Taj West End Bengaluru, Taj Fort Aguada Goa, and Taj Lake Palace Udaipur.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a strong balance sheet supported by gross cash reserves of over Rs 4,350 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical conflict in West Asia impacted airline schedule curtailments, affecting TajSATS catering operations and international travel corridors.
- Cost pressures in European/US properties due to higher energy/utility inflation and local tax policy adjustments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Binay Singh (Morgan Stanley):** Inquired if H2 growth will be rate-driven and asked about the sequential EBITDA decline at TajSATS.  
  *Management Answer:* Growth in Q2 was balanced between occupancy (+6-7%) and ARR. TajSATS faced airline schedule curtailments starting June due to West Asia tensions, but non-flight catering expansion helped preserve top-line revenue; performance is expected to improve in H2.
- **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital):** Asked about margin pressure at St. James' Court, London.  
  *Management Answer:* Minor month-long delays in executing capital upgrades (new bar, lounges, meeting rooms) temporarily lowered revenue leverage, alongside higher utility costs and tax waiver removals. Oct performance indicates a recovery.
- **Sameet Sinha (Macquarie):** Asked whether owning lower-margin international properties makes strategic sense.  
  *Management Answer:* London, Cape Town, and Dubai generate strong profits/management fees. Crucially, flagship presence in New York (Fifth Avenue) and London acts as a major strategic brand driver for Taj globally.
- **Achal Kumar (HSBC):** Inquired if 12% supply CAGR in Tier-2/Tier-3 cities threatens ADRs.  
  *Management Answer:* Supply is spread thin across ~300 cities. Local room demand is growing 15%-20%, and organized branded hotels are successfully absorbing market share from unorganized accommodation.

### One-line takeaway
IHCL delivered strong Q2 FY27 results with 22% top-line growth and upgraded its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance to 15%-17% driven by resilient domestic travel, MICE expansion, and asset-light scaling.
