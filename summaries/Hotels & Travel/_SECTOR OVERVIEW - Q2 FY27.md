# Hotels & Travel: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Indian hospitality delivered a broadly strong Q2 FY27 – typically a soft monsoon quarter – with **double-digit RevPAR growth** across most listed hotel operators, driven by a mix of **rate hikes (8–30% ARR growth)** and **occupancy gains**. Domestic leisure, weddings, MICE and spiritual tourism offset persistent international headwinds (West Asia conflict, high airfares, visa delays). The sector is in a **heavy investment phase**: listed players collectively have **>Rs 15,000 crore of committed capex** over the next 3–5 years, overwhelmingly funded through internal accruals and asset-light pipelines. Aviation (IndiGo) showed revenue resilience but a net loss on elevated fuel crack spreads and rupee depreciation. Travel services (TBO, Yatra, Thomas Cook, Le Travenues, Dreamfolks) reported mixed core growth with geopolitical drag on international/MICE segments. Cruise (Waterways) and amusement parks (Wonderla) posted strong volume and margin expansion.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian hospitality traditionally relied on asset-heavy ownership; over the last ~5 years the model has shifted toward **asset-light management/franchise contracts, mixed-use real estate monetisation, and proprietary brand building**. Travel distribution moved from offline agents to online/omnichannel platforms with a growing B2B focus. [Background]

**Government push**
- **Regional air connectivity fund tax exemption extended through FY30** – lowers cost of flying to tier-2/3 cities, benefits hotel demand in those markets [Govt: Centre, Nov 2026]
- **Greenfield airports approved**: **Doloo, Assam (Rs 2,014 cr)** and **Silchar, Assam** – improves leisure/connectivity access [Govt: Union Cabinet, Nov 2026]
- **India-Singapore MRO MoU** – builds regional aviation repair hub, supports airline maintenance costs [Govt: Cabinet, Nov 2026]
- **Draft SAF (Sustainable Aviation Fuel) policy for public consultation** – likely to raise airline fuel bills, creating uncertainty [Govt: Civil Aviation Ministry, Nov 2026]
- **Free e-visas for ASEAN tourists (pending Cabinet)** – could boost inbound leisure arrivals [Govt: Cabinet, Nov 2026]
- **Export duty raised on diesel & aviation fuel** – negative for airline operating costs [Govt: Finance Ministry, Nov 2026]
- **GST 2.0 / input credit restrictions on rooms <Rs 7,500** – ongoing margin headwind cited by Brigade (1.4% EBITDA impact Q2), Praveg, Royal Orchid (~Rs 2 cr/qtr), Samhi (~Rs 10 cr Q2), Thomas Cook Sterling (Rs 4 cr one-off) [Background]

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: **H2 FY27 structurally stronger** than H1. Key drivers: **heavy wedding calendar** (IHCL ~95 wedding nights FY28 vs 60-65 normal; Apeejay 36 of 52 FY27 weddings in H2), **MICE/events** (BRICS Summit, Aero Show, Bharat Mobility Expo, Electronica, Space Expo), **spiritual tourism** (Apeejay: USD 65bn market growing 10-12%), **domestic corporate travel** (Samhi: Bangalore/Hyderabad net office absorption **7.9 msf in Q2**, Hyderabad **3.8 msf 2x YoY**), **leisure/resort strength** (Chalet leisure RevPAR **+34% YoY**, Leela resort occupancy near 60%, Mahindra occupancy **75.6% +220bps**, Wonderla resorts **+57% YoY**). Occupancy high across board: Apeejay **93%**, IHCL **82%**, Samhi **78.6%**, Leela **69-74%**, Lemon Tree **76.1%**, Juniper **75%**, Brigade **75.2%**, Ventive India **68%**, Viceroy **79.2%**, EIH **71%**, Mahindra **75.6%**, Kamat **58%**, Waterways load factor **75.5%**.

**Divergence**: **International recovery** – IHCL/EIH/Leela see **encouraging signs of revival for H2**; InterGlobe/Le Travenues/Yatra/TBO/Thomas Cook flag **capacity constraints, 30% higher fares, West Asia conflict, visa rejections (China)** depressing long-haul outbound. **Corporate travel** – Samhi sees **strong GCC/office demand**; Lemon Tree calls it **muted due to IT hiring slowdown**; EIH notes **domestic air traffic contracted but hotel demand resilient**. **Business vs leisure mix** – Chalet business hotels mixed (construction impacts, air-traffic contraction); Leela city hotels **weekend staycation strength**; IHCL **MICE lagging (4% YoY) but expects H2 recovery**.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
**Hotel EBITDA margins expanding** despite cost pressures: Apeejay **+100bps to 30.27%**; Brigade **+470bps to 36.7%** (tight cost control, finance cost -28% YoY); Chalet consolidated **+110bps to 45%** (hospitality 41%, CRE 83%); EIH **positive P&L drop-through**; IHCL consolidated **+50bps to 28.4%**, standalone **+400bps to 38.9%**; Juniper **~35%**; Lemon Tree **net EBITDA margin 44.6% (+157bps)**, like-to-like **47.7% (+474bps)**; Leela **46% (+118bps)**; Samhi **comparable EBITDA margin ~39.1% (ex-GST impact)**; Ventive **India operating EBITDA +8% YoY (39% margin)**, Maldives **+25% YoY**; Viceroy **26.7%**; Waterways **58.45%**.
**Cost pressures**: **Fuel** – InterGlobe **fuel CASK +69% YoY** (Singapore jet index +60%, crack spreads 60-65% vs 20% historical, INR -9%); Ventive **Maldives diesel 1.5x YoY (Q2 avg $1.1/ltr, +Rs 12 cr absolute)**; Waterways **fuel cost/APCD Rs 2,471 vs Rs 1,434 YoY (+72%)**. **GST input credit loss** on rooms <Rs 7,500 – Brigade **1.4% EBITDA impact**, Praveg **credit reversal**, Royal Orchid **~Rs 2 cr/qtr**, Samhi **~Rs 10 cr Q2**, Thomas Cook Sterling **Rs 4 cr one-off**. **Labor** – Praveg **PF rule changes**; Chalet **crew contract partially resolved (20 rooms/day back)**; Leela **ESOP expenses + new headcount**; Ventive **new wage code**. **Airfares** – Le Travenues **domestic fares +30% YoY**, industry volume -6%. **Construction inflation** – Mahindra, Juniper flag **project cost escalation/delays**.
