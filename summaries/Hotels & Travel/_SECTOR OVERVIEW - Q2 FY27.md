# Hotels & Travel: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Hotels & Travel sector reflects a resilient operating environment defined by solid domestic demand, strong MICE activity, and rigorous cost discipline. While Q2 is traditionally a seasonally soft monsoon quarter impacted by regional festivities and lean travel windows, major hospitality players and OTAs reported robust rate-led growth and margin expansions. However, the sector faced notable cross-currents: elevated raw material and food inflation (particularly chicken and dairy), minimum wage hikes, regulatory headwinds such as GST law changes (e.g., input tax credit reversals on dynamic room pricing), and geopolitical/fuel cost volatility impacting international operations and the Middle East.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that domestic travel demand remains the primary structural engine, supported by a booming event economy, corporate MICE, and an extended wedding calendar in H2. OTAs and hotels alike point to a robust H2 outlook, backed by strong festival bookings and forward inquiries.
*   **Divergence:** While domestic leisure and business travel are thriving, international inbound/outbound flows face headwinds. **Indian Hotels Company Ltd (IHCL)** and **TBO Tek Ltd** noted that geopolitical tensions in West Asia, high airfares, and airline capacity constraints have pressured international properties and long-haul travel, with TBO Tek citing a particularly volatile Middle East and Europe. In the QSR space, **Devyani International Ltd** and **Restaurant Brands Asia Ltd** reported diverging international footprints: DIL noted a K-shaped domestic recovery with pressure on discretionary spending, while RBA highlighted that boycott headwinds in Indonesia are far behind, allowing turnaround strategies to gain traction.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Managements widely cited cost pressures stemming from steep food inflation (chicken, edible oils, dairy), rising utility expenses, and minimum wage revisions across several states (noted by DIL and Praveg). 
*   **Disagreements/Varying Impacts:** Companies experienced margin impacts differently based on business models. Asset-heavy hotel players like **Brigade Hotel Ventures**, **IHCL**, and **Kamat Hotels** successfully expanded margins via operating leverage and tight fixed-cost controls, even absorbing headwinds from GST 2.0. Conversely, asset-light/experiential players like **Praveg Ltd** and **Le Travenues Technology** reported EBITDA pressure or losses due to seasonal softness, high employee costs, and elevated promotional discounts (Le Travenues increasing flight discounts to offset 30% higher airfares). In the QSR segment, gross margins dipped slightly due to chicken inflation, but companies like RBA leveraged supply chain cluster efficiencies to mitigate the impact.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Agreements:** Expansion remains a strategic priority, with a heavy emphasis on asset-light management contracts and disciplined capital allocation. 
*   **Disagreements:** While luxury hotel operators like IHCL (targeting 5,000 keys/year, 94% capital-light pipeline) and Ventive Hospitality rely on internal cash surpluses and selective acquisitions, budget hotel and experiential players are tailoring scale models to regulatory realities. **Kamat Hotels** highlighted that GST rules make smaller 40-50 room hotel leases unviable, forcing a strict focus on 80-100+ room leases or asset-light management contracts (flat 6% top-line fee). In aviation and OTA tech, **Le Travenues Technology** and **TBO Tek** are prioritizing digital platform integration and targeted segment investments (e.g., Le Travenues keeping hotel investments capped at INR 10-12 crores/quarter, TBO Tek integrating Classic Vacations) over aggressive new market expansion.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Upgrades:** **IHCL** raised its full-year consolidated revenue growth guidance from 12%-14% to 15%-17% (and hotel segment guidance to 17%-19%) on the back of accelerating performance and robust occupancy (82%).
*   **Tone Shifts:** **TBO Tek** shifted to a more cautious/mixed tone, flagging an exceptional loss from UAE subsidiary fraud, forex volatility, and heightened geopolitical headwinds in the Middle East. **Praveg Ltd** noted a mixed tone as monsoon seasonality and GST law changes pushed the company into a net loss for the quarter.
*   **Delivery on Promises:** **Devyani International** received CCI approval for the Sapphire Foods merger, moving closer to closing the transaction by the end of the fiscal year. **Brigade Hotel Ventures** successfully deployed IPO proceeds toward debt reduction, lowering finance costs by over 28% YoY.

## Company one-liners
*   **Brigade Hotel Ventures Ltd:** Delivered a rate-led Q2 with 10% revenue growth, 36.7% EBITDA margins, and doubled PAT, supported by strong MICE and lower financing costs.
*   **Devyani International Ltd:** Achieved record operating EBITDA and solid store additions despite macro headwinds, while advancing toward the Sapphire Foods merger.
*   **Indian Hotels Company Ltd:** Posted its 18th consecutive record quarter, prompting management to raise full-year revenue growth guidance to 15%-17% on robust domestic demand and high occupancy.
*   **Kamat Hotels (India) Ltd:** Delivered a breakout Q2 with a 131% EBITDA surge and 1300 bps margin expansion, heading into H2 with a near-zero net debt balance sheet.
*   **Le Travenues Technology Ltd:** Reported resilient GTV growth (up 22%) driven by buses and trains, while scaling its hotel segment and reducing contribution losses.
*   **Praveg Ltd:** Incurred a net loss in Q2 due to monsoon seasonality, GST law changes, and labor cost hikes, but anticipates a strong recovery in H2.
*   **Restaurant Brands Asia Ltd:** Recorded a strong Q2 with 14% SSSG in India and a 281% jump in company-level EBITDA, alongside early turnaround green shoots in Indonesia.
*   **Speciality Restaurants Ltd:** Reported strong revenue and PAT growth, driven by an 8.1% SSSG, a growing hybrid delivery mix (32%), and disciplined execution toward its 200-outlet target.
*   **TBO Tek Ltd:** Navigating a volatile geopolitical environment by focusing on operational leverage and luxury integration, while managing specific headwinds from forex losses and fraud.
*   **Ventive Hospitality Ltd:** Delivered double-digit operating growth in its seasonally softest quarter, driven by a Maldives turnaround and steady India momentum.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **H2 Wedding & MICE Execution:** Monitor whether the extended wedding calendar and heavy MICE pipelines translate into anticipated margin expansions for hotel operators like IHCL, Ventive, and Brigade.
*   **Indonesia & Merger Milestones:** Track the operational progress of RBA's asset upgrades in Indonesia and DIL's NCLT timeline for the Sapphire Foods merger.
*   **Geopolitical & Inflationary Pressures:** Watch for stabilization in Middle East travel routes (TBO Tek), oil/diesel price trends in the Maldives (Ventive), and the mitigation of chicken/food inflation across QSR players.
*   **Hotel Segment Scale-up:** Observe the trajectory of Le Travenues' hotel contribution losses and take-rate expansion as it targets budget-to-mid-market leadership.
