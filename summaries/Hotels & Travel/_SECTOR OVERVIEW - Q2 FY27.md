# Hotels & Travel: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Hotels & Travel sector presented a bifurcated picture. Asset-owner hospitality players (such as *IHCL*, *Leela*, *Brigade Hotel Ventures*, *Kamat Hotels*, and *Ventive Hospitality*) reported a strong quarter characterized by rate-led growth, margin expansion, and robust domestic MICE and wedding demand, despite Q2 traditionally being a seasonally soft monsoon period. Conversely, intermediaries, OTAs, and aviation players (*IndiGo*, *TBO Tek*, *DreamFolks*, *Praveg*) faced notable headwinds from severe fuel cost inflation, geopolitical conflicts in West Asia, and regulatory/GST law changes that weighed on bottom lines.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that domestic travel demand remains resilient and structural, cushioned by a robust MICE calendar, corporate travel normalization, and a significantly extended wedding calendar in H4 (featuring ~95 nights compared to the normal 60–65). 
* **Divergence:** While luxury and premium hotel operators (*IHCL*, *Leela*) report broadening demand and low inbound recovery that leaves ample runway for pricing power, aviation and ticketing platforms (*IndiGo*, *Leela Travenues/Ixigo*, *TBO Tek*) highlighted that high airfares (up 30% YoY on certain routes) and capacity constraints have suppressed volume growth and hurt lower-income (NBU) travel segments. International operations faced divergent paths: Maldives properties (*Ventive*) noted a strong rebound, whereas West Asia destination management businesses (*Thomas Cook*) and international airline routes experienced sharp headwinds.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Fuel & Energy:** Jet fuel costs and diesel prices remained elevated compared to historical norms, though diesel in the Maldives declined 31% from its April-May peak (*Ventive*). *IndiGo* reported Singapore jet fuel index 60% higher YoY, heavily squeezing margins.
* **Regulatory & Tax Headwinds:** Multiple hotel and travel operators (*Brigade*, *Kamat*, *Praveg*, *Thomas Cook*) flagged a negative impact from GST law changes (such as input tax credit reversals under dynamic pricing) and statutory labor/PF cost inflation, which temporarily compressed margins or caused one-off cost provisions.
* **Operating Leverage:** Premium hotel asset owners successfully leveraged pricing power and tight cost controls to expand EBITDA margins (e.g., *IHCL* standalone EBITDA margin up 400 bps to 38.9%; *Brigade* margin at 36.7%; *Leela* margin at 46%), whereas companies with high variable/fixed lease commitments (*Praveg*, *DreamFolks*) reported net losses.

## Capex & capacity & expansion
* **Capital-Light Preference:** Asset-heavy companies are aggressively building out pipelines while maintaining a focus on capital-light management contracts (e.g., *IHCL* noted 94% of pipeline keys are capital-light; *Kamat* targets asset-light 6% top-line fee management contracts).
* **Significant Capex Deployments:** 
  * *Brigade*: Deployed Rs 484 crore out of its Rs 3,600 crore planned capex; aiming for 3,300 keys by FY31.
  * *Leela*: Total capex commitment of Rs 1,432 crore for upcoming pipeline keys; targeting 5,200+ keys by FY30.
  * *Waterways Leisure (Cordelia)*: Took early delivery of *Cordelia Sky* (increasing capacity by ~126%) and advanced funds for future vessel acquisitions.
  * *Wonderla*: Exploring 2–3 new park locations, intending to fund capex via internal cash flows without equity dilution.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Upgrades:** *IHCL* revised its FY27 consolidated revenue growth guidance upward from 12%-14% to 15%-17% due to sustained momentum.
* **Portfolio Adjustments:** *IHCL* announced the strategic merger of Oriental Hotels Ltd (OHL). *Le Travenues (Ixigo)* formally reported its hotel segment separately following emerging scale, while *Mahindra Holidays* accelerated its resort-exit strategy (exiting 6 resorts / 103 keys in Q2) using AI sentiment tools.
* **Asset Milestones:** *Waterways Leisure* received *Cordelia Sky* one month ahead of schedule, and *Praveg* commenced operations at Praveg Atolls in Lakshadweep.

## Company one-liners
* **Brigade Hotel Ventures:** Delivered a rate-led Q2 FY27 with 10% revenue growth, margin expansion to 36.7%, and doubled PAT backed by strong MICE recovery.
* **DreamFolks Services:** Reported revenue contraction and net loss for Q2 FY27 alongside sequential loss reduction while scaling non-airport lifestyle and global lounge initiatives.
* **Gujarat Fluorochemicals:** Delivered robust Q2 FY27 led by fluorochemicals/fluoropolymers growth while battery materials progress through customer qualifications.
* **Indian Hotels Company Ltd:** Posted its 18th consecutive record quarter, raising FY27 revenue guidance to 15%-17% on robust domestic demand and high occupancy.
* **InterGlobe Aviation (IndiGo):** Reported a net loss of 19 billion rupees due to fuel cost inflation and seasonal softness, cushioned by pricing discipline and strong liquidity.
* **Kamat Hotels:** Delivered a breakout Q2 with a 131% EBITDA surge and 1300 bps margin expansion, heading into H2 with near-zero net debt.
* **Le Travenues Technology (Ixigo):** GTV grew 22% YoY driven by buses and trains, alongside a rapidly scaling hotel segment reducing contribution losses.
* **Leela Palaces Hotels & Resorts:** Posted stellar 33% revenue and 36% EBITDA growth backed by luxury pricing power and robust non-room revenue streams.
* **Mahindra Holidays & Resorts:** Experienced standalone margin compression from room renovations, offset by robust 15% resort revenue growth and higher occupancy (75.6%).
* **Praveg Ltd:** Reported a net loss of Rs 20.13 crore due to monsoon seasonality, GST changes, and labor costs, anticipating an H2 recovery.
* **TBO Tek:** Navigating a volatile geopolitical environment by focusing on operational leverage and luxury segment integration while managing specific fraud and regulatory risks.
* **Thomas Cook (India):** Delivered a resilient Q2 with steady PBT and robust Financial Services growth, offsetting geopolitical headwinds in international DMCs.
* **Ventive Hospitality:** Delivered double-digit operating growth in a seasonally soft quarter driven by a Maldives turnaround and steady India momentum.
* **Waterways Leisure Tourism:** Reported 31% YoY revenue growth and advanced fleet expansion through the early delivery of *Cordelia Sky*.
* **Wonderla Holidays:** Achieved 42% income growth and a 90% EBITDA surge anchored by the Chennai park ramp-up and resilient resort performance.

## What to watch next quarter
* **H2 Seasonal Execution:** Delivery on upgraded guidance and management expectations for robust H2 performance, particularly given the expanded wedding calendar (95 nights) and peak leisure/resort seasons.
* **Fuel & Geopolitical Trends:** Jet fuel and diesel price trajectories, along with any easing of West Asian geopolitical tensions affecting international airline routes, cruise fuel surcharges, and destination management operations (*Thomas Cook*, *IndiGo*, *Waterways*, *Ventive*).
* **Asset Integration & Capex Progress:** Execution timelines on new pipeline key openings (*Brigade*, *Leela*, *IHCL*), the operational ramp-up of *Cordelia Sky* (*Waterways*), and customer qualification updates for advanced battery/materials segments (*GFL*).
