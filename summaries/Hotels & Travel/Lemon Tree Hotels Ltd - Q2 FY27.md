# Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported best-ever Q2 results with record Q2 revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and cash profit.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: maintained (Gross opening target of ~2,000 keys for FY27 maintained; H2/H1 seasonal ratio expected at 55/45).
- Tone and demand view: RevPAR growth rebalanced to 18% YoY (8% ARR growth, +633 bps occupancy), shifting away from Q1's retail volume-heavy strategy; corporate demand remains tepid due to global macroeconomic and IT hiring slowdowns.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Signed asset-light MoU with Omaxe for ~4,000 rooms across 10 properties; acquired land in Bandra East, Mumbai for ~Rs. 120 crore; Maldives acquisition put on hold due to a new Maldivian policy requiring 40% of hotel revenue to be kept in local currency; Gurgaon lease terminated due to fire compliance issues on basements.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Aurika Shimla opening shifted to Q3 FY27 (from "shortly"); Aurika Nehru Place timeline pushed to CY31 (from FY30) due to pending DDA tree-felling and Aravalli Ridge approvals, with room count revised up to 610 keys (from 572).

### Key numbers
- **Gross ARR:** Rs. 6,751 (up 8% YoY)
- **Occupancy:** 76.1% (up 633 bps YoY)
- **RevPAR:** Rs. 5,137 (up 18% YoY)
- **Total Revenue:** Rs. 363.8 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **Net EBITDA:** Rs. 162.2 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **Net EBITDA Margin:** 44.6% (up 157 bps YoY; like-to-like margin was 47.7%, up 474 bps excluding SAR and GST impacts)
- **PAT:** Rs. 79.8 crore (up 90% YoY)
- **Cash Profit:** Rs. 97.2 crore (up 28% YoY)
- **Gross Debt:** Rs. 1,429.2 crore (down 11% YoY from Rs. 1,610.2 crore)
- **Cost of Debt:** 7.44% (down 28 bps YoY)
- **Third-Party Management & Franchise Fees:** Up 30% YoY; total Lemon Tree management fees stood at Rs. 40.8 crore (up 19% YoY)
- **Q2 Portfolio Expansion:** Opened 8 managed/franchised hotels (523 rooms); signed 11 managed/franchised hotels (825 rooms)
- **Total Portfolio:** 24,061 rooms across 285 hotels in 170+ cities (Operational: 130 hotels / 12,131 rooms across 80+ cities)

### Highlights
- Recorded best-ever Q2 operational and financial performance across revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and cash profit.
- Owned portfolio revenue grew 15% YoY to Rs. 337 crore with hotel EBITDAR margin up 112 bps to 53%.
- Keys portfolio RevPAR grew 29% YoY to Rs. 3,109, supported by a 976 bps occupancy increase to 69.7%, an 11% ARR increase to Rs. 4,460, and EBITDAR margin expanding 748 bps to 50%.
- Reported a net positive exceptional item of Rs. 22 crore in P&L (Rs. 41 crore non-cash revenue gain from lease reversal on Gurgaon property termination, offset by Rs. 16 crore property tax provision and Rs. 3 crore investment diminution).
- Demerger update: Filed scheme of arrangement with NCLT after receiving CCI, SEBI, and stock exchange approvals; management expects to list Fleur Hotels in CY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to contribute 55% of full-year revenue and 60% of EBITDA, in line with historical seasonal patterns.
- Management reaffirmed target gross additions of ~2,000 rooms in FY27, with 900+ keys scheduled to open in 2H FY27.
- Long-term asset-light goal: Target Rs. 500 crore fee income for Lemon Tree standalone with ~80% flow-through (~Rs. 400 crore EBITDA).

### Demand & industry commentary
- RevPAR growth was evenly driven by ARR (8% YoY) and occupancy expansion (+633 bps YoY).
- Corporate demand remains muted due to IT sector hiring slowdowns and discretionary T&E cost controls, but retail and non-negotiated segments showed strength.
- Management stated structural supply deficit in India remains intact; supply additions are predominantly in Tier 2/3/4 markets where Lemon Tree expands via asset-light contracts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- SAR provisions and un-creditable GST together impacted expenses by 3.2% of total revenue in Q2 FY27.
- Ongoing annual portfolio renovation costs expected to normalize back to ~1.2% of revenue starting next year (down from elevated catch-up levels), boosting margins by ~1.5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acquired land in Bandra East (adjoining BKC), Mumbai for ~Rs. 120 crore to build the ~172-room Lemon Tree Premier Bandra (expected opening FY30, projected initial ARR ~Rs. 12,000, capex ~Rs. 2 crore/key).
- Signed an MoU with Omaxe for ~4,000 rooms across 10 asset-light properties (9 greenfield, 1 brownfield) in master-planned townships and PPP transit hubs; openings slated over 3-4 years.
- Owned/Leased Pipeline: Aurika Shimla (90 rooms, owned) opening in Q3 FY27; Aurika Shillong (165 rooms, leased) opening in FY28; Aurika Varanasi (47 rooms, leased) opening in FY29; Aurika Nehru Place (610 rooms, leased) letter of award extended to August 2027, targeted opening in CY31.
- Cumulative 3.5-year capex across developments and potential acquisitions estimated at ~Rs. 3,000 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stood at Rs. 1,429.2 crore as of September 30, 2026 (down 11% YoY).
- Rs. 960 crore capital commitment from Warburg Pincus for Fleur Hotels is fully available on call for acquisitions and hotel developments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in government environmental approvals (Ridge Management Board and tree felling) extending timelines for Aurika Nehru Place.
- Sudden policy changes in foreign markets; a new Maldivian requirement to hold 40% of hotel revenue in local currency rendered potential acquisitions unviable.
- Arbitrary municipal tax demands and compliance enforcement (e.g., Gurgaon basement banquet clearance loss).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital) on Aurika Nehru Place delays & Bandra East ARR:** Management explained DDA tree-felling/Ridge approvals delayed execution (LOA extended to Aug 2027, opening CY31, 610 keys); Bandra East asset is underwritten at a starting ARR of ~Rs. 12,000 for FY30.
- **Achal Kumar (HSBC) on Bandra East land acquisition justification:** Management justified the ~Rs. 2 crore/key capex by highlighting high proximity to BKC demand and expected immediate 12%-13%+ ROCE, citing similar success at their nearby Kalina property.
- **Jinesh Joshi (PL Capital) on Gurgaon lease termination & deflaggings:** Management clarified the Gurgaon lease was terminated after authorities revoked basement banquet approvals (generating a net Rs. 22 crore exceptional P&L gain); deflaggings in managed inventory were compliance-driven with no major further deflaggings expected.
- **Vaibhav Muley (Haitong Securities) on Bandra East construction timeline:** Management confirmed hotel design is complete and excavation will begin within weeks, targeting a 33-month construction schedule to meet the FY30 opening timeline.
- **Zahra Patel (Solidarity Advisors) on Omaxe deal structure:** Management clarified Omaxe owns all land/developments, while Lemon Tree will purely manage and brand the properties; revenue flow is 3-4 years away given greenfield construction.

### One-line takeaway
Lemon Tree delivered record Q2 performance with 18% RevPAR growth and 90% PAT expansion, while aggressively expanding its asset-light pipeline and restructuring owned assets via the upcoming Fleur demerger.
