# Lemon Tree Hotels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported best-ever Q2 performance across all key metrics (ARR, revenue, EBITDA, PAT) and strong RevPAR growth, despite muted corporate demand.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (55/45 H2/H1 revenue split).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "soft Q1" to "best-ever Q2"; corporate demand remains "tepid" while retail is strong.
- **New topics or risks:** New: Omaxe partnership, Maldives project on hold, Bandra land acquisition details, Gurgaon lease termination. Old: Keys renovation (80% complete).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Keys renovation progress confirmed; demerger process ongoing (NCLT filing done).

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenue:** Rs. 363.8 crore (+18% YoY)
- **Net EBITDA:** Rs. 162.2 crore (+22% YoY)
- **Net EBITDA Margin:** 44.6% (vs 43% in Q2 last year)
- **PAT:** Rs. 79.8 crore (+90% YoY)
- **Cash Profit:** Rs. 97.2 crore (+28% YoY)
- **Gross ARR:** Rs. 6,751 (+8% YoY)
- **Occupancy:** 76.1% (+633 bps YoY)
- **RevPAR:** Rs. 5,137 (+18% YoY)
- **Gross Debt:** Rs. 1,429.2 crore (down 11% YoY)
- **Cost of Debt:** 7.44% (down 28 bps YoY)

### Highlights
- Best-ever Q2 for ARR, revenue, EBITDA, PBT, PAT, and cash profit.
- Opened 8 managed/franchised hotels (523 rooms); signed 11 (825 rooms).
- Total inventory: 24,061 rooms (285 hotels); 130 hotels (12,131 rooms) operational.
- Exceptional item: Net Rs. 22 crore positive impact (Rs. 41 crore revenue from lease termination vs Rs. 19 crore expense provisions).
- Keys portfolio: RevPAR up 29% YoY to Rs. 3,109; EBITDA margin up 748 bps to 50%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects 55/45 revenue split between H2 and H1.
- Targeting 2,000+ gross room openings in FY27.
- Expects to list Fleur Hotels in CY27.
- Renovation expense to drop to ~1.2% of revenue next year.
- Capex: ~Rs. 3,000 crore planned over next 3.5 years (subject to acquisitions).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Corporate demand remains "tepid" due to geopolitical uncertainty and inflation; retail demand is strong.
- IT sector hiring/corporate travel has not fully picked up.
- Management believes supply will not outstrip demand in high-demand markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Net EBITDA margin 44.6% (47.7% on like-to-like basis, excluding GST/SAR impact).
- GST/SAR impact increased expenses by 3.2% of total revenue.
- Renovation expenses are currently elevated but expected to normalize.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acquired land in Bandra East, Mumbai (172 rooms, FY30 opening, ~Rs. 120 crore).
- Aurika Shimla: Opening Q3 FY27.
- Aurika Shillong: Expected FY28.
- Aurika Varanasi: Expected FY29.
- Aurika Nehru Place: Expected CY31 (DDA extension to Aug 2027).
- Omaxe MoU: 10 properties (~4,000 rooms) in Tier 2/3 cities; asset-light.
- Maldives project: Currently in "cold storage" due to unfavorable currency regulations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt reduced to Rs. 1,429.2 crore.
- Warburg Pincus Rs. 960 crore investment is "on call" for acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflict and inflation impacting corporate travel.
- Regulatory/compliance issues (e.g., Gurgaon lease termination due to fire safety/banquet approval withdrawal).
- Construction delays due to external factors (e.g., GRAP in Delhi, monsoons in Mumbai).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Karan Khanna (Ambit):* Asked about FIT demand and Aurika Nehru Place delays. Management noted corporate demand is tepid; DDA approvals (tree felling) causing delays.
- *Achal Kumar (HSBC):* Questioned Bandra investment justification. Management cited 12-13% ROCE target and deep demand in BKC.
- *Vaibhav Muley (Haitong):* Asked about GST impact on ADR. Management noted they are repricing hotels above Rs. 7,500 where possible.
- *Jinesh Joshi (PL Capital):* Asked about Gurgaon lease termination. Management explained it was a force majeure event due to loss of banquet approval.
- *Dikshi Jain (InCred):* Asked about Omaxe deal. Management confirmed it is asset-light and targets Tier 2/3 cities.

### One-line takeaway
Lemon Tree delivered record Q2 performance driven by strong RevPAR growth and operational leverage, while continuing its asset-light expansion despite tepid corporate demand.
