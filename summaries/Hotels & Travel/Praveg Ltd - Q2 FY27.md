# Praveg Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew YoY, but the company reported a net loss and lower EBITDA due to seasonal softness, higher employee costs, and GST law changes.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 43.75 crore (up 16.70% YoY from Rs 37.60 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 Hospitality & Events revenue:** Rs 27.73 crore
- **Q2 FY27 Advertising revenue:** Rs 16.02 crore
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 90.46 crore (up by Rs 16.17 crore YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Hospitality & Events revenue:** Rs 57.53 crore
- **H1 FY27 Advertising revenue:** Rs 32.22 crore
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Negative Rs 2.94 crore (EBITDA margin: -6.64%)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 0.97 crore (EBITDA margin: 1.06%)
- **Q2 FY27 Net loss:** Rs 20.13 crore
- **H1 FY27 Net loss:** Rs 33.37 crore
- **H1 FY27 Room nights sold:** 55,925 (in line to exceed H1 FY26 of 44,959)
- **Total Room Inventory:** 927+ rooms across 17 resorts and 1 5-star hotel

### Highlights
- Commenced operations at Praveg Atolls on Thinnakara Island in Lakshadweep on October 21, 2026 (100 accommodations: 85 cocoon-shaped, 15 shell-shaped).
- Received a LOA from Tourism Corporation of Gujarat for a contract worth Rs 11.16 crore plus GST for the Praveg contingent in the Statue of Unity area (Oct 20 - Nov 1, 2026).
- Employ over 937+ people on direct payroll with an in-house operating model.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects top-line growth in Q3 and Q4 to be better than last year, driven by the festive season, winter holidays, weddings, and events.
- Management expects advertising business to see incremental growth similar to past years (FY25: Rs 34 crore, FY26: Rs 56 crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 is seasonally the softest quarter due to monsoon, while H2 is the strongest period.
- Majority of demand is from domestic travellers; rising ATF prices and airfares have not impacted bookings.
- Experiential, destination-led tourism is growing strongly in India.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA was impacted by changes in GST laws at the end of September (requiring input tax credit reversal under dynamic pricing for rooms under Rs 7,500) and recent changes in labor laws/Provident Fund rules increasing employee costs.
- Fixed land lease rentals are payable irrespective of occupancy, impacting margins during seasonally weak quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Asset-light semi-permanent structures cost Rs 60 lakh to Rs 75 lakh per room vs Rs 1.25 crore to Rs 1.5 crore for civil structures.
- Total capital investment for existing assets is around Rs 300 crore to Rs 400 crore.
- Upcoming projects: Meghalaya luxury cottage project (Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore outlay for 40 rooms, 30-year concession), Alibaug (2 properties), Kashid, and Tanzania (Serengeti forest land allocated).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management stated there are no additional borrowings; depreciation and interest increases are related to existing asset provisions and lease liabilities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Flight connectivity constraints in Lakshadweep acting as a teething issue.
- High seasonality causing EBITDA pressure during Q1 and Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Manan Dalal (Family Office):** Asked about reasons for the swing from positive EBITDA last Q2 to a loss this Q2. *Management cited GST law changes requiring credit reversal and higher employee costs due to labor law/PF changes.*
2. **Trisha Shah (Shah Family Office):** Asked about the Meghalaya project investment and revenue potential. *Management stated the total project outlay is estimated at Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore for 40 rooms.*
3. **Vishal (Blue Lotus Capital):** Asked about the timeline for PAT breakeven given the heavy depreciation. *Management stated that profitability will improve once newer properties like Lakshadweep resolve flight connectivity issues and ramp up occupancy/ARR.*
4. **Kaustav Bubna (Kamana Holdings):** Asked which properties are underutilized. *Management noted Tent City Narmada and Ayodhya had below-average performance due to seasonality, while Diu, Daman, and Jawai operated on good occupancy.*
5. **Anuj Panwar (Family Office):** Asked about occupancy benchmarks for breakeven. *Management stated 30%-40% occupancy reaches breakeven, 40%-55% yields 20%-25% EBITDA margin, and 65%-75% yields 40%+ EBITDA margin.*

### One-line takeaway
Praveg reported a net loss of Rs 20.13 crore in Q2 FY27 due to monsoon seasonality, GST law changes, and higher labor costs, but management remains optimistic on a strong recovery in H2 driven by weddings, events, and newer properties like Lakshadweep.
