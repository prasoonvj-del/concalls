# Ventive Hospitality Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Double-digit operating growth in a seasonally soft monsoon quarter, with Maldives returning to a strong growth path and robust H2 outlook.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 582 crore (up 5% YoY)
- **Hospitality Revenue:** INR 423 crore (up 14% YoY)
- **Annuity Revenue:** INR 131 crore (up 5% YoY, 90% margin across 3.4 million sq ft)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 242 crore (down 5% YoY due to lower other income)
- **Operating EBITDA (Excluding one-offs):** INR 211 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **India Operating Revenue:** INR 207 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **India Operating EBITDA:** INR 80 crore (up 8% YoY, 39% margin)
- **Maldives Revenue:** INR 213 crore (up 20% YoY)
- **Maldives Operating EBITDA:** INR 30 crore (up 25% YoY)
- **India ADR:** INR 12,629 (up 11% YoY)
- **India Occupancy:** 68% (up 2 points YoY)
- **India RevPAR:** INR 8,606 (up 15% YoY)
- **India TRevPAR:** Up 14% YoY
- **Maldives TRevPAR:** INR 45,000 (up 20% YoY)
- **Maldives Occupancy:** 57% (up 3 points YoY)
- **Reported PAT:** Declined 47% YoY (impacted by higher depreciation and amortization of INR 102 crore vs INR 79 crore YoY)
- **Cash Profits:** INR 190 crore (up 16% YoY)
- **Gross Debt:** INR 2,366 crore (Rupee borrowings: INR 1,588 crore at 7.4% avg cost; USD debt: USD 81 million / approx INR 778 crore at 6.1% avg cost)
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** INR 507 crore
- **Net Debt:** INR 1,859 crore
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 1.6x (increased from 1.2x post-acquisitions like Sahyadri Hills)
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV):** Approx 0.2x

### Highlights
- Aloft Whitefield closed for conversion to AC by Marriott; wings and F&B outlets at Hilton Goa closed for refurbishment.
- JW Marriott Pune hosted the 12th BRICS Communications Ministers Meeting (5,000 delegates over 11 days).
- Launched 'YA:LA', a new Mediterranean restaurant at The Ritz-Carlton Pune (expected to do INR 1.5 crore to INR 2 crore a month).
- The Ritz-Carlton Pune received its first MICHELIN Key; Soho House Mumbai retained its key for the second year.
- Solar program rollout underway across Raaya, Conrad, and Anantara in the Maldives; Raaya will run generator-free for ~17 hours a day from January 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be the strongest half of the year in both markets, driven by the wedding season and strong MICE calendar in India, and high season in the Maldives with easing diesel prices.
- India H2 occupancy estimated north of 70% and ADR north of 14,500.
- Q3 business on books is at ~67% of forecasted revenue, pointing to mid-teen revenue and EBITDA growth and 70%+ occupancy.
- AC by Marriott Whitefield and Varanasi Marriott remain on track for Q1 FY28 and Q3 FY28 openings respectively (adding ~360 keys).
- Sahyadri Hills (India's first Ritz-Carlton Reserve) FY29 launch timeline remains firmly in place.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Luxury demand and the Maldives market remain very resilient despite elevated airfares and lower tourist arrivals year-to-date.
- Air traffic is improving month-on-month.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Diesel prices in the Maldives down 31% from the April-May peak, but still 1.5x of last year's levels (Q2 average at ~$1.1/litre, adding an absolute cost of INR 12 crore).
- Operating EBITDA growth absorbed the impact of the new wage code, asset investments (closures/refurbishments), and the absence of last year's one-off benefits (export incentives at Ritz-Carlton and electricity surcharge waiver at Aloft ORR Bangalore). Adjusting for these, India EBITDA grew 11% instead of 5%.
- Other income dropped due to lower forex gains (INR 21 crore vs INR 48 crore last year), repayment of inter-corporate deposits, and absence of export incentives (~INR 4 crore last year).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained CRISIL AA stable rating.
- Financial closure attained for expansion assets (Whitefield and Varanasi), but management is prioritizing internal cash surpluses to fund projects.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical and fuel price volatility (diesel remains 1.5x of last year).
- Elevated depreciation and amortization charges impacting reported PAT due to recent acquisitions and refurbishments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Achal Kumar (HSBC) on Maldives margin trajectory:** Asked why Maldives EBITDA margin was flat-to-lower (14% in Q2 vs 15% in Q1) despite a 31% drop in diesel prices. Management clarified that Q2 diesel is still 1.5x higher YoY ($1.1/litre), and the performance is normal seasonality for low quarters in the Maldives, supported by cost discipline and solar initiatives.
- **Achal Kumar (HSBC) on rising interest costs:** Asked about higher effective interest costs. Management stated it is due to acquisition financing for the Roha project and a recent 25 bps government rate hike (of which 8-10 bps was passed on), while overall rates remain competitive.
- **Achal Kumar (HSBC) on H2/Q3 outlook:** Asked for color on Q3 business visibility. Management stated Q3 business-on-books is at ~67%, indicating mid-teen revenue and EBITDA growth with 70%+ occupancy.
- **Vinay Rawal on Other Income volatility and hedging:** Asked if hedging strategies are used to curb forex volatility in other income. Management stated no specific hedging is used, and the decline was driven by lower forex gains (INR 21 crore vs INR 48 crore), repayment of ICDs, and loss of export incentives.
- **Vinay Rawal on hotel expansion timelines:** Asked about Whitefield and Varanasi execution. Management confirmed timelines and budgets are on track for FY28 openings.

### One-line takeaway
Ventive delivered double-digit operating growth in its seasonally softest quarter, driven by a Maldives turnaround and steady India momentum, setting up for an exceptional H2.
