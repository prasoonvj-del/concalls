# Thomas Cook (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Hotels & Travel*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady performance supported by a strong recovery in the India portfolio and Financial Services, despite geopolitical headwinds impacting international operations.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 2,003 crore (5% YoY decline)
- **Consolidated PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 84.8 crore (broadly flat YoY)
- **Financial Services Gross Turnover:** Rs 3,900 crore (up 7% YoY); Revenue up 21% YoY; EBIT up 10% YoY to Rs 45.2 crore (EBIT margin at 44.1%)
- **Travel Segment Revenue:** Rs 1,568.3 crore (down 7% YoY); EBIT at Rs 45.5 crore (margin at 2.9%, up from 2.4% sequentially)
- **Sterling Holidays Revenue:** Rs 127.2 crore (up 17% YoY); EBITDA at Rs 28.9 crore; PBT at Rs 14.0 crore (H1 revenue up 19% to Rs 297.1 crore, EBITDA up 15% to Rs 91.0 crore)
- **DEI Revenue:** Rs 14.7 crore (down 22% YoY from Rs 19.5 crore); EBIT loss of Rs 1.2 crore (improved from a loss of Rs 15 crore in Q1)
- **Branch Network:** Expanded 7% YoY via franchisees to 310 branches.
- **Sterling Footprint:** Reached 83 properties and crossed 4,000 keys.

### Highlights
- Financial Services retail portfolio grew 12% YoY, led by a 33% surge in the education segment.
- AI-driven initiatives (DynaPack integration, Agent Assist, and QC bot) improved agent productivity and call conversions from 2.15% to 3.40%.
- Sterling Holidays opened a record 7 resorts in a single quarter, crossing 4,000 keys.
- **Record Note:** The call transcript notes NCLT filing for Sterling's demerger, aligning with our record of the NCLT Mumbai first motion approval on November 7, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic for H2, citing forward leisure bookings trending at a healthy 30% growth YoY for Q3 (OND).
- Expects double-digit growth in Financial Services for the foreseeable future, with EBIT margins maintained between 40% to 45%.
- Sterling anticipates strong seasonal demand in H2, maintaining a target long-term EBITDA margin band of 32% to 37%.
- DEI anticipates steady Middle East recovery and a strong Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Outbound long-haul travel faced headwinds from geopolitical tensions and high visa rejection rates (e.g., China).
- Domestic travel grew 8% YoY, led by Ladakh, Kerala, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, and Char Dham.
- RBI data (April–August 2026) showed outbound education remittances down 34% and travel forex down 1%, contrasting with Thomas Cook's 33% education forex growth.
- Corporate travel air transaction volumes grew 18% YoY and hotel volumes surged 81%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Travel segment EBIT margins improved sequentially from 2.4% to 2.9% due to cost agility.
- Sterling incurred a one-off Rs 4 crore GST input tax credit (ITC) cost impact in Q2 due to a regulatory change effective September 23, 2025, affecting YoY comparability until Q3.
- DEI improved EBIT margins through workforce optimization, direct staff reductions, restructuring support functions, and IT consolidation onto the WeC platform.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sterling added 7 resorts (6 management contracts, 1 revenue share) during the quarter, bringing total properties to 83.
- Financial Services operationalized retail counters in Angamaly, Cochin, and Hubli while closing Thiruvananthapuram branches.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Sterling remains debt-free with net cash reserves of Rs 380.5 crore.
- Consolidated gross debt stands at Rs 316 crore, with a significant portion of cash balances backed by prepaid forex card floats.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing geopolitical conflicts in West Asia negatively impacted international operations (Desert Adventures sales down 67% YoY, AlliedTPro sales down 22% YoY).
- High visa rejection rates for China and flight connectivity disruptions weighed on long-haul outbound travel.
- Kailash Mansarovar Yatra cancellations resulted in an impact of Rs 18.5 crore.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Chetan (Systematix Group) on Sterling's new resort ramp-up:** Management stated that properties typically ramp up after 90 days post-stabilization, varying by seasonality.
- **Anil Shah (Insightful Investments) on Sterling's margin compression:** Management clarified that the lower reported EBITDA growth was due to an uncomparable Rs 4 crore GST ITC impact; normalized EBITDA growth was 19%–20%.
- **Purva Zanwar (360 One) on Financial Services sustainability:** Management attributed the 21% revenue growth to retail, education, and corporate segments, guiding for continued double-digit growth.
- **Nagraj C (Banyan Capital) on Sterling member base vs. FITs:** Management explained that memberships are not growing, and existing members are dispersed across a larger network, with the guest ratio heavily favoring traditional resort paying guests.
- **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) on Forex spreads and float:** Management stated blended forex gross margins are 1.6% to 1.8%, with float yields optimized at approximately 7.5% to 8%.

### One-line takeaway
Thomas Cook delivered a resilient Q2 with steady PBT and robust growth in India travel and Financial Services, offsetting ongoing geopolitical headwinds in international destination management businesses.
