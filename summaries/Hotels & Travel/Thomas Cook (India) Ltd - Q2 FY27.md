### Highlights
- Financial Services retail segment drove growth: education forex +33% YoY (vs industry -34% per RBI), corporate +10%, travel -6%
- Digital adoption in Financial Services rose to 22.4% (vs 21.5% YoY); Blinkit forex card sales +96% vs Q3 FY26
- India travel domestic +8% YoY (Ladakh, Kerala, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Bhakti); Kailash Mansarovar cancellations INR 185 million
- AI initiatives: Agent Assist pilot shows new agents booking 85%/70%/52% higher on bookings/pax/revenue vs Q1; QC bot automates 100% call audits (>2 min) vs 3-5% manual; conversion rates improved 2.15% to 3.40%
- International DMS recovery: Desert Adventures deficit -67% vs -89% in Q1; AlliedTPro -22% vs -40%; Asian Trails +9%; Private Safari South Africa +31%, East Africa +14%
- Sterling: 7 new resorts in quarter (6 management contracts, 1 revenue share); debt-free with net cash ~INR 3,805 million; 27 consecutive profitable quarters
- DEI: Workforce optimization, cost renegotiations, IT consolidation (WeC system) driving margin improvement; 5 new partnerships signed, 20+ renewed; Warner Bros Harry Potter exhibit signed in Shanghai
- Sterling demerger: NSE/BSE/SEBI approvals received; NCLT hearing done; shareholder meeting expected Dec/Jan; final approval targeted end-Mar/early-Apr 2027

- Travel: Forward bookings +30% YoY for OND quarter (India outbound + MICE, ~51% of travel revenue); expect international DMS recovery to improve vs Q2's -24% shortfall
