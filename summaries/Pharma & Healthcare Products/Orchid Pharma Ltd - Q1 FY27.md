# Orchid Pharma Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-24 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and margins improved YoY, supported by steady operational progress and integration of Dhanuka Laboratories.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (Q1 FY27): INR 304 crore (vs. INR 263 crore in Q1 FY26, ~15% YoY growth).
- Revenue from operations (FY26 combined): INR 1,233 crore (vs. INR 1,398 crore in FY25).
- Gross margin (Q1 FY27): 33% (vs. ~30% in Q1 FY26).
- Gross margin (FY26 combined): ~32% (vs. ~36% in FY25).
- EBITDA (Q1 FY27): INR 25 crore (vs. INR 10 crore in Q1 FY26).
- Combined employee and other operating expenses (FY26): Broadly flat at INR 353 crore.
- Exblifep volume growth (Advanz Pharma/Europe): ~300% in Q3 FY26, ~170% in Q4 FY26, ~50% in Q1 FY27.
- Russia Exblifep licensing arrangement estimated 10-year value: ~USD 178 million.
- NP-NC segment sales (Q1 FY27): INR 21 crore.
- AMS division revenue (Q1 FY27): INR 5 crore; EBITDA drag: ~INR 50 lakhs.
- 7-ACA project capex: INR 750 crore.
- Cefiderocol project capex: USD 20 million to USD 25 million.

### Highlights
- Merger with Dhanuka Laboratories became effective on July 10, 2026 (appointed date: April 1, 2024); financials for FY25 and FY26 restated.
- Focused integration projects initiated to crystallize merger savings, with initial benefits expected next financial year.
- Exblifep registration completed in South Africa; coverage secured across GCC markets. Advanced discussions underway for South America, Mexico, Philippines, Thailand, Morocco, Australia, US, and China.
- Cefiderocol project remains on schedule with commissioning targeted by December 2026, validation and initial batches in January–March 2027; commercial launch subject to DCGI approval and GARDP global RFP.
- 7-ACA Jammu project commissioning and first commercial batch targeted by March 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 demand for regulated products to improve (winter season), leading to better sequential sales.
- Management considers it premature to give a full-year revenue growth number for FY27 or specific quarterly percentage guidance.
- 7-ACA plant ramp-up target: 80% to 100% by the end of the first year; long-term utilization guidance is 80% in-house and 20% third-party sales.
- Exblifep long-term lifetime sales guidance remains USD 1.1 billion to USD 2 billion.
- US formulation strategy targets launches around 2030, with ANDA filings for new molecules and generic injectables (using the Cefiderocol facility) planned post-2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry still facing overcapacities leading to cutthroat competition, particularly in non-regulated markets and Cefixime API.
- Cephalosporin business in FY26 faced volume and pricing declines of 15% to 20% across important products and markets.
- 7-ACA weighted average price has been stable over the last 10 to 12 years at ~USD 60; Chinese suppliers (concentrated among 3-4 private enterprises) operate on a profitable model rather than historical state-funded dumping.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Combined gross margin compressed by ~4% to 32% in FY26 due to product mix and pricing pressure, but recovered by 3% points YoY to 33% in Q1 FY27.
- Controllable costs maintained: FY26 employee and other operating expenses flat at INR 353 crore.
- 100% of GCLE is procured from Otsuka Chemicals; pricing and values vary with demand.
- Jammu 7-ACA facility benefits from lower GST, cheapest electricity in India, abundant water, and lower boiler steam cost from agricultural waste.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No very significant capex budgeted for next financial year beyond completing the Cefiderocol and 7-ACA projects in FY27.
- 7-ACA project is among the largest and most complex fermentation projects in India, scaling up 800x from the existing pilot plant.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Pricing and demand conditions in core markets remain competitive; recovery will not be in a straight line.
- Regional conflict in the Middle East has affected planned rollout and timing of commercial launch for Exblifep.
- Fermentation scale-up and ramp-up risks for the 7-ACA plant (scaling from pilot by 800x).
- Cefiderocol timeline subject to DCGI clinical trial waiver approval and WHO PQ (taking ~2 years after filing).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shashwat Vijay (SIC Wealth Management) asked about H2 margin recovery and 7-ACA ramp-up.** Management stated non-regulated competition continues, but H2 regulated demand is typically better. 7-ACA ramp-up targets 80%-100% by year one, with 80% in-house use and 20% third-party sales.
2. **Tarun Krishna asked about Chinese 7-ACA concentration and pricing.** Management noted supply is concentrated among 3-4 companies (Sinopharm Weiqida, Zhuhai United, Yili, Livzon) and pricing has been stable at ~$60, leaving little room for drastic price cuts.
3. **Dhwanil Desai asked about China dumping risks on 7-ACA and technology transfer.** Management explained that plan A relies on low production costs in Jammu (cheap power, water, steam) and comprehensive technical handholding from the technology partner.
4. **Rupesh Tatia asked about Exblifep/Advanz Pharma penetration and Cefiderocol trial waiver.** Management stated Advanz launched in the big five European countries, while Russia dossier submission/launch takes 1.5–2 years. Cefiderocol trial waiver depends on DCGI post-application review, leveraging their precedent with Enmetazobactam.
5. **Vishal Manchanda asked about Cefixime pricing pressure and partnering with other sterile players for Ceftriaxone.** Management confirmed Cefixime is under maximum stress, and for Ceftriaxone, they plan to supply non-sterile APIs to Indian sterile manufacturers (like Aurobindo) rather than creating competing sterile overcapacity.

### One-line takeaway
Orchid Pharma reported a revenue and margin recovery in Q1 FY27 backed by 15% YoY revenue growth, while focusing on the execution of major strategic projects like 7-ACA and Cefiderocol.
