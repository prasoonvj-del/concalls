# Alkem Laboratories Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Healthy growth in India formulations and non-U.S. international markets was offset by price erosion in the U.S., a decline in net profit, and gestation losses in new platforms (Enzene and Occlutech).

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** India full-year growth target adjusted to ~11% (from ~12%); U.S. growth guided to mid-single digits in constant currency (down from mid-to-high single digits); EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 20%–21% for FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Management turned more cautious on trade generics due to intense competition and credit tightening, while remaining upbeat on chronic segment outperformance and non-U.S. international momentum.
- **New topics or risks:** Occlutech integration details (PPA depreciation of INR 28 crore for the quarter) and Denosumab BLA analytical query delays at Enzene. Daman OAI and U.S. price erosion remained ongoing risks.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Generic Tolvaptan was launched in September 2026 as planned; Occlutech was successfully integrated starting July 16, 2026.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 4,520 crore (+13.8% YoY)
- **Biotech Sales:** INR 70 crore (-35% YoY due to lumpy CDMO)
- **MedTech Sales:** INR 133 crore (including Occlutech)
- **EBITDA:** INR 1,025 crore (+11.3% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22.5%
- **R&D Expenses:** 4.3% of revenue
- **PBT Growth:** +7.7% YoY
- **Net Profit Degrowth:** -11.7% YoY
- **Net Cash:** INR 4,900 crore+
- **International Business Growth:** +22.3% YoY (U.S. +2%, non-U.S. +58%)

### Highlights
- Appointed Dr. Amrut Naik as CEO for the pharmaceutical business.
- Integrated Occlutech into the MedTech business effective July 16, 2026 (consolidated for 2.5 months).
- Mandva API facility inspection concluded with no 483s.
- IQVIA data: Company overall growth matched IPM at 13.8% YoY. Acute grew 12.1% (IPM 12.3%); Chronic grew 22.4% (IPM 16%, outperforming by 640 bps).
- Ranked #1 in volumes for Semaglutide injectable and multivitamins (VMN).

### Guidance & outlook
- **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained at 20%–21% for FY27. Core pharma EBITDA margin expected to improve ~100 bps YoY annually starting FY28.
- **India Growth:** Projected at ~11% overall for FY27.
- **U.S. Growth:** Mid-single-digit growth in constant currency for FY27.
- **R&D Expenses:** 4%–4.5% of revenue.
- **Enzene & MedTech:** Enzene EBITDA breakeven targeted in 1.5 to 2 years (revenues to double in 4 years); MedTech EBITDA breakeven in 1 year (sales 3x in 5–6 years).
- **Non-U.S. International:** Expected to land between mid-teens to higher teens for FY27, and mid-teens in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Trade generics growth moderated due to higher market intensity (entry of major players) and tighter credit policies.
- Non-U.S. growth driven by LATAM (Colombia and Mexico as key drivers, Chile strong) and Australia.
- U.S. volume expansion exceeded 10%, but was offset by ~16% price erosion (driven largely by Sacuval) and 3% from new launches, resulting in a 3% constant currency contraction (reported +2% with currency benefits).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Occlutech integration diluted EBITDA margin by 80 bps in Q2.
- Core pharma EBITDA margin stood at ~26% in Q2.
- Depreciation included INR 28 crore PPA allocation for Occlutech.
- Minimum import price (MIP) impact on Pen G and amoxicillin/clavulanic acid was not fully felt in H1 due to old inventory, with full impact expected in H2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Enzene Capex: INR 300 crore–INR 350 crore planned over the next 3 years.
- MedTech Capex: INR 400 crore planned over the next 3 to 4 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash stood at over INR 4,900 crore.
- Occlutech acquisition added INR 1,100 crore in identifiable intangibles (to be amortized over 10 years) and INR 700 crore in goodwill.
- Borrowings from Occlutech added to finance costs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Daman facility OAI status restricts new U.S. product approvals and launches.
- Denosumab BLA delayed due to regulatory analytical queries, facing competition from 11 players.
- Severe price erosion in the U.S. (Sacuval) and slow REMS ramp-up for Tolvaptan.
- Gestation losses and slower-than-expected ramp-ups in Enzene and MedTech platforms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Saion Mukherjee (Nomura):** Asked about trade generics slowdown and U.S. price erosion. Management stated market intensity and credit tightening caused the trade generic slowdown, while a 16% U.S. price erosion was driven primarily by Sacuval.
- **Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie):** Asked if EBITDA margin guidance would be raised. Management declined, citing margin dilution from Occlutech and Enzene. Asked about Daman remediation, management noted responses are submitted and they await FDA re-audit.
- **Bino Pathiparampil (Evalueserve):** Asked if Penicillin MIP impact is in the base. Management answered that H2 will reflect the full impact as old inventory depletes.
- **Damayanti Kerai:** Asked about Enzene misses. Management cited the Denosumab BLA delay, slower U.S. CDMO ramp-up, and higher B2B biosimilar price erosion in India.
- **Rashmmi Shetty (Dolat Capital):** Asked about U.S. H2 constant currency outlook. Management guided for mid-single digits due to ongoing remediation overhang on new launches.

### One-line takeaway
Alkem matched IPM growth at 13.8% YoY driven by strong chronic portfolio execution and international momentum, while navigating Daman regulatory hurdles and gestation investments in new MedTech and biotech platforms.
