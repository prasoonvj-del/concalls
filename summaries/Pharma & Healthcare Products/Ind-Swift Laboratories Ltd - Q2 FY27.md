# Ind-Swift Laboratories Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and margin expansion driven by a successful transition to a finished dosage formulation platform and scaling CDMO partnerships.

### Key numbers
*   **Operating Revenue:** ₹234.89 crore (+50.15% YoY; +26.22% QoQ).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** ₹42.33 crore (up 4.95x YoY; +27.04% QoQ).
*   **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 18.02% (+1,256 bps YoY; +11 bps QoQ).
*   **PAT:** ₹31.03 crore (up 3.54x YoY; +26.96% QoQ).
*   **PAT Margin:** 13.21% (+761 bps YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** ₹420.97 crore (+35.79% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Business Mix:** Exports account for 82.7% of total sales; domestic sales account for 17.3%.
*   **Segment Performance:** Own brands contributed 55.75% of quarterly sales (69% of export sales); Contract manufacturing accounted for 26.99% of sales.
*   **Product Updates:** Commercial validation for Clarithromycin dry suspension completed; awaiting site variation approval for European launch.
*   **Registrations:** 900+ global product registrations across 85 countries; 330+ additional registrations expected in FY27.
*   **Strategic Stake:** Company holds a 7.80% equity stake in Synthimed Labs.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27:** Management reiterated revenue growth guidance of >50%.
*   **Medium-term:** Management targets 20% to 25% revenue CAGR to cross ₹1,500+ crore by FY30, with 200-300 bps EBITDA margin expansion.
*   **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to reach 19-20% in the next quarter and aims for 20% for the full year.
*   **FY28:** Management indicated potential upward revision of FY28 guidance.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Key Products:** Atorvastatin + Ezetimibe revenue reached ₹58 crore in Q2 (vs ₹25 crore in Q1).
*   **Ibuprofen:** Revenue was ₹15-16 crore in H1 FY27; estimated at ₹20 crore in Q3 and ₹20-25 crore in Q4.
*   **Expansion:** Active focus on registering products in Peru, Mexico, Brazil, Rwanda, Congo, Cambodia, Nigeria, Ivory Coast, and Benin.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Raw Material Expenses:** Declined to 45.15% of revenue (vs 49.38% YoY).
*   **R&D Expenses:** ~₹8 crore in Q2.
*   **Sourcing:** Majority of KSMs and APIs sourced from Synthimed (~₹100 crore in FY27).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** ₹250 crore growth capex (Samba EU-GMP upgrade, Derabassi warehouse, R&D) is on track.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** 70-75% at the Samba facility.
*   **Infrastructure:** Warehouse construction at Derabassi is underway, expected to be completed in 10-12 months.
*   **Regulatory:** ANVISA (Brazil) visit for Samba facility expected in Q4; European PIC/S approval expected next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** Company is net debt-free with robust cash reserves.
*   **Working Capital:** Average realization cycle is 75 days; target is ~60 days.
*   **Inventory:** Target is a minimum of 90 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution:** Management noted the primary task is the execution of manufacturing and delivery to meet orders in hand.
*   **Regulatory:** Dependency on site variation approvals (e.g., Clarithromycin) and facility upgrades.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you achieve 20% EBITDA for the full year?** A: Management is determined and focused on achieving this target.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Synthimed stake?** A: Management plans to tag along if IndiaRF liquidates its stake, as valuations are growing.
*   **Q: How do you differentiate from other Indian formulation companies?** A: Management stated they do not compete with innovators in CDMO and leverage established distribution networks in semi-regulated markets.
*   **Q: What is the status of the two other customers for CDMO?** A: Management is in active discussions and hopes to conclude in the last quarter.
*   **Q: How does the working capital cycle differ between CDMO and own brands?** A: Own brands have a slightly lower working capital cycle due to advance/LC terms.

### One-line takeaway
Ind-Swift is executing a successful pivot to a high-margin, export-led finished dosage formulation model, supported by a net-debt-free balance sheet and strong CDMO partnerships.
