# Pharma & Healthcare Products - API & CDMO: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The API and CDMO sector in Q2 FY27 demonstrated operational resilience, with high double-digit revenue and EBITDA expansion across major players, driven by global supply chain diversification (China Plus One) and strong execution in complex chemistry. However, performance diverged based on portfolio exposure: CDMO-focused players and specialty manufacturers reported robust growth and high utilization, while generic API manufacturers faced margin compression due to geopolitical supply chain disruptions, rising solvent/petrochemical costs, and persistent commodity pricing pressure.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Traditionally, Indian manufacturers relied heavily on low-cost generic commodity APIs (e.g., Ibuprofen, Paracetamol) and basic intermediates, competing largely on volume and cost efficiency. Over the past five years, the industry has aggressively shifted toward complex CDMO/CRDMO partnerships, specialized modalities, backward integration to de-risk China dependencies, and value-added finished dosage formulations (FDF). [Background]

**Shifts underway**
*   **Expansion into Complex Modalities (Peptides, Oligonucleotides, ADCs):** *Scaling.* Players are aggressively investing in high-barrier modalities to capture post-discovery outsourcing. Divi's Laboratories is installing multiple 3,000-litre Solid Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS) lines [Divis Laboratories concall Q2 FY27]. Laurus Labs commissioned a commercial-scale peptide block, holding >16,000 liters of SPPS capacity [Laurus Labs concall Q2 FY27]. Neuland Laboratories commissioned Peptide Module 1 at Unit I [Neuland Laboratories concall Q2 FY27], and Aarti Pharmalabs is setting up a peptide lab targeted for end-FY27 [Aarti Pharmalabs concall Q2 FY27].
*   **Forward Integration into Formulations & CDMO Scaling:** *Scaling.* Pure API players are moving down the value chain. Ind-Swift Laboratories transitioned to an export-led finished dosage platform (55.75% own brands) [Ind-Swift Laboratories concall Q2 FY27]. Anlon Healthcare is entering finished dosage formulations (tablets/capsules) via Anlon Medicos [Anlon Healthcare concall Q2 FY27]. SMS Pharmaceuticals established SMS coLab as a dedicated CDMO platform [SMS Pharmaceuticals concall Q2 FY27].
*   **De-risking Chinese Procurement via Backward Integration:** *Scaling.* Anthem Biosciences reduced Chinese procurement to 20–21% while domestic sourcing rose to 75–78% [Anthem Biosciences concall Q2 FY27]. Alivus Life Sciences is operationalizing its Solapur facility in Q3 FY27 primarily for backward integration of intermediates [Alivus Life Sciences concall Q2 FY27]. SMS Pharmaceuticals highlighted backward integration as a structural cost advantage [SMS Pharmaceuticals concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**
*   **PLI Scheme for Medical Devices & Key Inputs:** Extended until FY36 with a Rs 2,700 crore outlay, supporting domestic manufacturing capability [Govt: Medical devices PLI extended to FY36, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus on CDMO & Regulated Markets:** Strong demand momentum continues in the CDMO space driven by China Plus One and the US Biosecure Act discussions. Anthem Biosciences, Sai Life Sciences, Laurus Labs, and Aarti Pharmalabs reported robust RFP funnels.
*   **Divergence on Biotech Funding & Order Timelines:** 
    *   *Optimistic:* Sai Life Sciences noted early green shoots in biotech funding and customer inquiries. Anthem Biosciences reported an influx of queries.
    *   *Cautious:* Dishman Carbogen Amcis reported a 22% YoY revenue drop due to order deferrals and funding delays from biotech clients (citing capital diversion toward AI). Neuland Laboratories reported lumpiness in commercial CMS shipments due to client project shifts.
*   **Divergence on Commodity API Pricing:** Solara Active Pharma Sciences and Solara's base business saw sequential improvements in Ibuprofen, whereas IOL Chemicals noted Ibuprofen prices are flat/stable, and SMS Pharmaceuticals actively seeks to cap Ibuprofen revenue contribution below 35% to avoid commodity risk. Natural Capsules abandoned domestic API generic sales due to severe Chinese dumping.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Freight & Solvents Pressure:** Geopolitical conflict in West Asia elevated freight costs, shipping lead times, and prices of petrochemical-based solvents and reagents. Blue Jet Healthcare, Concord Biotech, Anlon Healthcare, Neuland Laboratories, and Sai Life Sciences explicitly flagged gross margin pressure or cost spikes driven by solvent/petrochemical inflation.
*   **Pass-Through Lag:** Companies with short order books or spot pricing (Aarti Drugs, Anlon Healthcare) successfully passed on cost increases. Companies bound by long-term fixed contracts with large regulated customers (Supriya Lifescience, Blue Jet Healthcare) suffered a lag in passing on raw material hikes.
*   **Margin Leaders:** Anthem Biosciences (50.6% EBITDA margin) and Alivus Life Sciences (41.8% EBITDA margin) maintained industry-leading margins through high-margin Custom Synthesis mix and flow chemistry operational efficiencies.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Aggressive Multi-Year Outlays:** Sector-wide capex remains high. Anthem Biosciences guided Rs 1,200 crore for Unit 4 Phase 1 [Anthem Biosciences concall Q2 FY27]. Laurus Labs guided Rs 3,000 crore combined across FY27–FY28. Blue Jet Healthcare is deploying Rs 1,000 crore for Vizag Phase I. Sai Life Sciences spent Rs 621 crore in H1 FY27 out of a Rs 1,100–1,300 crore annual plan.
*   **Capacity Recalibrations:** Sai Life Sciences deferred commissioning of its 225 KL Bidar capacity from Q4 FY27 to Q3 FY28 to prioritize specific customer projects. Jubilant Pharmova guided a sharp drop in FY28 capex to ~Rs 1,000 crore (from Rs 1,800–1,900 crore in FY27) as major expansions mature.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Upgrades:** Rubicon Research raised its full-year operating EBITDA margin guidance to 24%+ (from 23%+). SMS Pharmaceuticals noted it will exceed its initial FY27 guidance (15% revenue growth, 20% EBITDA margin).
*   **Guidance Cuts & Recalibrations:** Dishman Carbogen Amcis lowered FY27 revenue expectations from single-digit growth to no growth YoY. Supriya Lifescience cut full-year EBITDA margin guidance to 23%–25% (down from previous expectations) due to LATAM regulatory delays and unabsorbed Ambernath plant overheads. Sigachi Industries trimmed FY27 revenue guidance to Rs 600 crore (from Rs 650 crore). Natural Capsules drastically cut FY27 API revenue guidance from Rs 70–80 crore down to Rs 12–15 crore.
*   **Tone Shifts:** Neuland Laboratories shifted to a cautious tone following a softer quarter linked to shipment timing. Evonile Pharma absorbed heavy one-time transition costs (Rs 83 crore) as it pivots to a standalone commercial distribution model.

## What to watch next quarter

2.  **EU-GMP Final Approvals:** Monitor final inspection report clearance for Zim Laboratories' Nagpur facility and EU audit outcome for Supriya's Ambernath site.
3.  **Refinancing Milestones:** Track Dishman Carbogen Amcis's CHF 200 million ECB drawdown targeted before/around year-end to halve quarterly interest expense.
4.  **Commissioning & Commercialization:** Watch for commercialization of Mahad Unit 3 (Blue Jet Healthcare), Solapur Phase 1 (Alivus Life Sciences), and Adelaide OTC facility (Sai Parenterals).
