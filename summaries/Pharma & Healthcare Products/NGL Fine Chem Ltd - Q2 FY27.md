# NGL Fine Chem Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth and revenue expansion, though management noted near-term margin headwinds from the commissioning of Phase II of the Tarapur facility.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 158.71 crores (up 32% YoY, up 14% QoQ); H1 FY27 revenue at Rs. 297.87 crores (up ~33% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 28.06 crores (up 64% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17.68% (compared to 14.27% YoY and 16.73% in Q1 FY27).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs. 21.60 crores (up 67% YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 16.25 crores (up 69% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT at Rs. 34.64 crores compared to Rs. 18.87 crores YoY.
- **Other Income:** Rs. 1.20 crores (down from Rs. 7.43 crores in Q1).
- **Depreciation:** Rs. 6.55 crores (up from Rs. 5.25 crores in Q1).
- **Net Cash from Operating Activities (H1):** Rs. 35.80 crores.
- **Net Investing Outflows (H1):** Rs. 44.05 crores.
- **Inventories:** Rs. 118.38 crores as of September 30, 2026 (compared to Rs. 70.28 crores as of March 31, 2026).
- **Total Borrowings:** Approximately Rs. 122 crores (compared to Rs. 108 crores at the end of FY26).
- **Animal Health APIs Contribution:** Approximately 95% of revenue this year.
- **Top 10 Customers Contribution:** Approximately 26% of sales.
- **Top 10 Products Contribution:** Approximately 65% of sales.
- **Regulatory Filings (Europe):** Total 17 filings (9 approved: 4 CEP approvals, 5 ASMF approvals; plus 4 CEP applications and 4 ASMF applications filed).
- **Regulatory Filings (US):** 9 Veterinary Master Files (VMFs) filed.

### Highlights
- Phase II of the Tarapur greenfield facility commenced production on September 24, 2026, completing the project's commissioning; it is fully staffed and provides flexibility for existing products and development pipeline.
- Inventory increased significantly due to a strategic shift from make-to-order to make-to-stock over the last five quarters and pre-buying raw materials with long lead times to mitigate potential disruptions from Gulf region unrest.
- *Note on past record:* Management noted that customer acquisition in Europe has been slower than anticipated (taking 2-3 years post-approval), causing them to likely miss their current-year EU target, whereas the Tarapur capacity commissioning and US VMF filing targets remain aligned with previous disclosures.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects capacity utilization at the new Tarapur plant to reach 30%-40% over the next two to three quarters.
- Long-term EBITDA margin target remains in the 15%-18% range, though management expects modest downward pressure and margin absorption drag over the next two to three quarters due to incremental fixed costs, depreciation, and staffing.
- Regulated market business is expected to reach critical mass between 2028 and 2030.
- Short-term debt is expected to increase slightly to support incremental working capital for turnover, but long-term debt is not expected to increase.
- Management does not foresee API prices rising over the next three to five quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was led primarily by volumes and solid demand across existing product portfolios and markets.
- International non-regulated markets are currently growing at a healthy 10%-12%.
- The industry-wide inventory destocking cycle that occurred between 2023 and 2025 has concluded.
- Fluralaner prices have declined following patent expiration; management expects prices to stabilize over the next six months as it approaches two years post-patent.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses increased due to higher staffing for the new facility, rising fuel costs (gas used at plants), and a sharp doubling of transportation/distribution costs over the last six months due to geopolitical issues in the Gulf.
- Gross margins remained robust at around 56% for the last two quarters (within the historical range of 50%-55%).
- Regulated markets offer higher target margins of 18%-22% compared to 15%-18% in non-regulated markets.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Tarapur Phase II expansion is fully commissioned and staffed; utilization is targeted at 30%-40% over the next 2-3 quarters primarily via existing unregulated markets.
- If requirements exceed current capacity within three years, management will review further capital expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at ~Rs. 122 crores (up from Rs. 108 crores in FY26).
- Inventories rose to Rs. 118.38 crores (from Rs. 70.28 crores in March 2026) driven by the make-to-stock shift and safety stock buffers; management stated working capital discipline is a priority.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term pressure on margins due to fixed operating costs, depreciation, and finance costs incurred ahead of full revenue ramp-up from the new facility.
- Soaring logistics and transportation costs driven by the Gulf geopolitical situation.
- Slower-than-anticipated customer onboarding and conversion in European regulated markets (taking 2-3 years post-approval).
- Potential price erosion in off-patent products like Fluralaner over the next six months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital) on incremental cost flows and facility capacity:** Management clarified that 50% of the plant staffing is in place with the rest to follow, finance costs will rise by no more than 20%, and depreciation will increase by ~Rs. 3 crores. Unregulated market demand will fill 30%-40% capacity, which can accommodate both unregulated and regulated needs for the next three years.
2. **Ishan Thakkar (Wealth Manager) on Fluralaner pricing sustainability:** Management stated Fluralaner prices have declined post-patent expiry and expect any further price erosion to play out over the next six months before stabilization. *(Management answered directly without dodging).*
3. **Rahul Jain (Wealth Advisor) on inventory build-up and baseline sales:** Management explained inventory rose due to the shift to make-to-stock and strategic stockpiling for Gulf supply risks, and confirmed no one-off factors were present in recent gross margins. *(Management answered directly).*
4. **Dhwanil Desai on USFDA inspection triggers:** Management explained that US sales require a sponsor customer filing a VMF, which triggers an FDA audit; inspection timelines range from 3 to 12 months, requiring patience. *(Management answered directly).*
5. **Rohit on pricing advantage vs competitors in EU/US:** Management stated the value proposition is a mix of dual EU/US registrations, fully integrated manufacturing chains, and competitive pricing where required. *(Management answered directly).*
6. **Raghuram Kuchi (Bestpals Research) on market share and plant-wide USFDA approvals:** Management confirmed the USFDA approves the plant rather than individual product files, allowing continuous DMF filings post-plant approval, and noted it is too early to guide on specific market share. *(Management answered directly).*

### One-line takeaway
NGL Fine-Chem delivered strong Q2 FY27 volume-led growth and commissioned its Tarapur Phase II facility, but faces near-term margin absorption drag and slower-than-expected customer onboarding in regulated markets.
