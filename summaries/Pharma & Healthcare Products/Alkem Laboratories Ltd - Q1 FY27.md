# Alkem Laboratories Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Solid domestic branded outperformance and strong gross margins were offset by a tax-driven PAT decline, US FDA regulatory headwinds (Daman OAI), and gestation losses in US CDMO.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 3,740 crore (+11% YoY)
- **India Sales:** Rs 2,497 crore (+10.3% YoY)
  - Branded Generics: +12% YoY
  - Trade Generics: Flattish YoY (driven by tightening DSO terms and price hikes)
- **International Sales:** Rs 1,222 crore (+16% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.5% (EBITDA grew 3.7% YoY)
- **PAT YoY Growth:** -21.7% YoY (impacted by tax provisions/losses at non-DTA foreign subsidiaries)
- **PBT YoY Growth:** +1.8% YoY
- **R&D Expense:** 4% of total revenue
- **Cash Position:** Rs 5,700 crore

### Highlights
- **IPM Outperformance:** Grew 13.2% YoY per IQVIA vs. IPM growth of 12.2% (100 bps outperformance).
- **Segment Breakdown (IQVIA):** Acute grew 12.3% YoY (vs. IPM 10.1%); Chronic grew 17.9% YoY (vs. IPM 15.4%). Outperformed in 7 focus therapies.
- **US Market Updates:** Received 5 ANDA approvals (1 tentative) in Q1 FY27. Plans to launch generic Tolvaptan in the US during Q2 FY27.
- **Daman Facility Regulatory Status:** Received US FDA OAI (Official Action Indicated) status. Management noted approved product supplies continue without interruption.
- **India Biosimilars:** Portfolio of 7 launched biosimilars generates ~Rs 150 crore in annual sales.

### Guidance & outlook
- **India Business:** Management raised full-year FY27 growth guidance to ~12% (up by 100 bps from prior guidance).
- **US Sales:** Guided to mid-to-high single-digit YoY growth for FY27.
- **Gross Margins:** Full-year FY27 guidance maintained at 66.5%–67%.
- **Tax Rate:** Consolidated tax rate expected between 30%–32% for FY27 due to unrecognized deferred tax assets on US entity losses.
- **Occlutech (MedTech):** Target of ~Rs 400 crore sales for 8.5 months in FY27 with EBITDA breakeven; expected 7%–8% annual EBITDA margin expansion to reach targets over 3–4 years.
- **US CDMO (Enzene):** Breakeven targeted at USD 25–30 million in annualized revenue, expected in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- IPM market growth is being driven by Chronic therapy opportunities (such as generic semaglutide after patent expiry, where Alkem ranks among the top 3 players).
- Trade Generics environment has become intensely competitive with larger players entering.
- Non-US international markets (Chile, Australia, Germany) continue to demonstrate robust double-digit growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Employee Expenses:** Rose >16% YoY due to annual increments, addition of ~1,200 MRs over recent quarters, and operationalization of Enzene US CDMO.
- **Gross Margins:** Benefited in Q1 from product mix (lower Trade Generics, higher branded) and favorable USD exchange rates (+10% YoY).
- Management expects upcoming quarters to face margin pressure from rising API costs as older low-cost inventory depletes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Enzene US CDMO will require capex in future years as operations scale up, but no capex is planned for FY27.
- Management stated there are no plans for new acquisitions in orthopedics or MedTech at present.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents stand at Rs 5,700 crore.
- Management tightened market practices in Trade Generics, strictly managing days sales outstanding (DSO) to maintain ROCE discipline.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **US FDA Action:** Daman plant accounts for 45% of Alkem’s US sales; an OAI status poses risk if remediation is delayed beyond 6–12 months.
- **CDMO Gestation Drag:** Enzene US unit incurs ~Rs 60 crore per quarter in operational expenses with breakeven pushed to FY28.
- **Raw Material Inflation:** Rising API prices expected to impact gross margins in Q2–Q4 FY27.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Saion Mukherjee (Nomura):** *Why was domestic growth 10.3% despite strong IPM outperformance?*
  - **Management:** Domestic growth was dragged down by flat YoY Trade Generics sales due to DSO tightening and API-led price increases. Branded generics grew 12%.
- **Abdulkader Puranwala (ICICI Securities) & Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie):** *What is the financial drag from US CDMO and when will it break even?*
  - **Management:** US CDMO quarterly opex drag is ~Rs 60 crore. Annualized revenues of USD 25–30 million are required to break even, expected in FY28.
- **Amlan Jyoti Das (JPMorgan):** *What is the impact of the Daman plant OAI status on US revenues?*
  - **Management:** Daman contributes 45% of US revenues. Commercial supplies continue without interruption. Management expects resolution within 6 to 12 months.
- **Rashmi Shetty (Dolat Capital):** *What is the updated FY27 outlook for US sales given price erosion and Daman OAI?*
  - **Management:** US growth is guided at mid-to-high single digits for FY27, largely supported by currency benefits as price erosion has flattened out.
- **Amlan Jyoti Das (JPMorgan):** *What are the financial expectations for the Occlutech acquisition?*
  - **Management:** Acquisition closed mid-July 2026. Target is ~Rs 400 crore in sales for ~8.5 months of FY27 at EBITDA breakeven, with EBITDA margins expanding by 7–8% annually over 3–4 years.

### One-line takeaway
Alkem’s strong core domestic branded business and margin discipline are navigating temporary drags from Trade Generics tightening, US CDMO gestation losses, and Daman regulatory scrutiny.
