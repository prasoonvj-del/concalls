# Prevest Denpro Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-02 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** FY26 (Year ended March 31, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Despite global geopolitical challenges and supply chain disruptions, the company delivered double-digit revenue and profit growth, supported by strong export performance and new product commercialization.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 71.81 crore, up 13.9% YoY (from Rs 63.03 crore in FY25)
- **Total income:** Rs 76.72 crore, up 14.4% YoY
- **EBITDA:** Rs 29.62 crore, up 13.3% YoY
- **Profit before tax (PBT):** Rs 27.61 crore, up 13.9% YoY
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 20.49 crore, up 12.9% YoY
- **EBITDA margin:** 38.6% (moderated marginally YoY)
- **PBT margin:** 36.0%
- **PAT margin:** 26.7%
- **Domestic business growth:** 9.0% YoY
- **Export business growth:** 17.58% YoY
- **Q4 FY26 Revenue from operations:** Rs 18.94 crore, up 2.63% YoY
- **Q4 FY26 Total income:** Rs 20.35 crore, up 4.18% YoY
- **Q4 FY26 EBITDA:** Rs 8.32 crore, up 9.09% YoY
- **Q4 FY26 PBT:** Rs 7.81 crore, up 9.21% YoY
- **Q4 FY26 PAT:** Rs 5.82 crore, up 13.23% YoY
- **Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin:** 40.87%
- **Q4 FY26 PBT margin:** 38.38%
- **Q4 FY26 PAT margin:** 28.61%
- **US market sales growth:** 37.58% YoY
- **Traditional capacity utilization:** 67% to 68% (total installed capacity turnover potential of ~Rs 125 crore)
- **New production line capacity utilization:** 18% to 20%
- **3D printers sales growth:** 162%
- **3D resins sales growth:** 40.5%

### Highlights
- Established a wholly-owned subsidiary in the UAE; operations delayed due to Middle East conflicts but online sales expected to commence next month.
- Commercialized the Disinfectant business vertical, seeing encouraging initial customer response.
- Advanced in digital dentistry with next-generation 3D printers and resins via in-house R&D and global partnerships.
- Rotoflex endodontic file system continued to receive encouraging repeat demand.
- Conducted close to 16 educational programs for dental professionals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic and expects the UAE subsidiary to play an important role in expanding international footprint.
- Management aims to scale the Oradox Oral Care business, build the disinfectant portfolio, deepen export presence in new international markets, and invest in next-generation dental materials and digital technologies.
- Aiming for a totally indigenous 3D printer by 2028.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global business environment remained challenging due to tariff developments, geopolitical tensions, and conflicts disrupting international trade and logistics.
- Domestic demand supported by increasing oral health care awareness, rising healthcare expenditure, and expanding access to quality dental care.
- Management expects digital dentistry to account for at least 40% of all procedures done at dental offices within the next 5 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management noted that disciplined cost management and operational efficiency helped mitigate inflationary pressures and maintain healthy profitability.
- Margins moderated marginally compared to the previous year on a full-year basis, but improved sequentially in Q4 FY26 due to operational efficiency.
- Company is working on import substitution for raw materials by developing them in-house.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Traditional capacity utilization runs at 67%-68% (peak turnover capacity of ~Rs 125 crore).
- New production line (digital dentistry, resins, disinfectants, oral health) runs at 18%-20% capacity utilization.
- Expanded domestic distribution network into Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities and increased sales team size.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not explicitly discussed in detail, other than disciplined working capital utilization mentioned.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions, war-related developments, and global shipping/supply chain disruptions affected customer ordering patterns and export dispatches, particularly impacting Q4 revenue growth and operations of the UAE subsidiary.
- MoCRA (Modernization of Cosmetic Regulations Act) portal in the US and Dubai Municipality registration processes created temporary hurdles for Oradox exports, resulting in a 2% drop in Oradox sales.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rahul Sharma (Individual Investor) asked about the reasons for slower relative growth compared to initial years.** Management replied that 17.5% export growth is satisfactory given global geopolitical challenges, and a diversified reach across 90+ countries helps offset regional setbacks.
2. **Yash Modi (Investor) asked about the capacity utilization and status of the Dubai/US subsidiaries.** Management stated traditional capacity is at 67%-68% and new lines at 18%-20%; US sales grew 37.58% via Axiodent and private labeling, while the Dubai subsidiary expects to start online sales next month.
3. **Ajay Shah (VVD Asset Managers) asked about Oradox traction and the company's right to win in 3D printing against MNCs.** Management stated Oradox had a minor 2% dip due to US/Dubai regulatory/war delays but new launches are planned; in digital dentistry, everyone starts at the same level (unlike traditional MNC-dominated segments), giving the company an edge alongside lower production costs.
4. **Hussain Rangwalla (Individual Investor) asked how the company competes with major MNCs (like Ivoclar, Dentsply, 3Shape) across 90+ countries.** Management responded that India's low production costs and high import duties make them hard to beat on price, supplemented by high quality standards and OEM manufacturing for major global players (currently OEMing for 3-4 big companies, expecting more).

### One-line takeaway
Prevest Denpro delivered a resilient FY26 with 13.9% revenue growth and 38.6% EBITDA margin, propelled by strong export demand and expansion into digital dentistry and disinfectants despite geopolitical headwinds.
