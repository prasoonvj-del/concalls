# Arvaya Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive – Turned profitable on a consolidated basis with 35% QoQ revenue growth and clear asset-light bed expansion plans.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 40 crore (+35% QoQ)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 5.24 crore (+32% QoQ); EBITDA margin of 13% (vs 13.1% in Q1 FY27)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 1.37 crore (up from Rs 0.53 crore in Q1 FY27); EPS of Rs 0.25
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 70.19 crore; EBITDA of Rs 9.21 crore (13.1% margin); PAT of Rs 1.9 crore
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 8.6 crore (~89% of EBITDA)
- **Operational Beds:** 517 beds (up from 262 beds a year ago)
- **Occupancy Rate:** 38% overall (vs 48% YoY); established hospitals at 46%, newer hospitals at 27%
- **ARPOB:** Rs 23,651 (+17% YoY)
- **Patient Volume:** Q2 patient discharges grew 38% to 5,526; outpatient visits increased to 35,429 (vs 33,500 earlier)

### Highlights
- Consolidated company turned profitable in H1 FY27 following the full integration of SeCURE Hospitals (acquisition completed in March 2026).
- Completed 100% share-swap acquisition (no cash payout) of Sushodha Institute of Gastroenterology, integrated effective November 1, 2026 (~Rs 1 crore monthly revenue run rate, 15%–18% profit margin).
- *This call details full implementation of the integrated asset-light, data-driven healthcare business model outlined in the November 2026 investor deck.*

### Guidance & outlook
- Target bed capacity: 791 operational beds by December 2026 / January 2027; 1,200 beds by end of FY28; 2,400 beds by FY30.
- Management expects sustainable EBITDA margins in the 13%–14% range during ongoing expansion.
- Plans to build a concentrated, saturated regional network across 12–15 core districts in Karnataka and Maharashtra before considering expansion into other states, aiming for a long-term integrated HMO model.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian hospital market estimated at $122 billion in 2025, growing at ~10% annually.
- Over 40% of Indian healthcare spending remains out-of-pocket, creating high demand for organized hospital networks in Tier 2/3 cities.
- Government-sponsored schemes (PMJAY, Yeshasvini) account for 15%–20% of patient mix, serving to optimize capacity utilization.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Lease costs sit below EBITDA as depreciation and right-of-use asset interest under accounting standards due to the asset-light leased model.
- EBITDA margin saw slight moderation to 13% in Q2 due to initial startup costs at newer hospitals.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Asset-light model: 8 of 9 operating units are leased (except Gadag).
- Capital expenditure per 100-bed greenfield hospital on lease is Rs 22–25 crore (~Rs 24 lakh per bed), with breakeven targeted within 1.5 to 2 years.
- Mysuru-II (added August 2026) commands the highest ARPOB at Rs 31,000; Kolhapur unit achieved operational breakeven in its first month.
- Filed a rights issue of ~Rs 206 crore to fund medical equipment, unit fit-outs, and future acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Equity: Rs 63.96 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Total Borrowings: Rs 31.34 crore against cash of Rs 2.8 crore; Net Debt stood at ~Rs 28.5 crore (0.45x Net Debt/Equity).
- Borrowings primarily finance capital expenditures, equipment acquisition, lease deposits, and hospital fit-outs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Goa resort currently generates negligible revenue as it undergoes repurposing into a wellness facility; operational performance expected by Q4 FY27.
- Occupancy dropped YoY to 38% (from 48%) due to rapid capacity dilution from newly acquired, un-utilized beds.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Reason for drop in occupancy:** Management clarified footfalls and discharges grew over 20% QoQ, but overall occupancy rate dipped due to adding un-utilized acquired bed capacity (newer beds at 27% occupancy vs established at 48%–56%).
- **ARPOB growth drivers:** Management attributed the 17% ARPOB increase primarily to a richer surgical and super-specialty case mix (cardiology, neurosciences, kidney, robotics), rather than price hikes.
- **Cybersecurity & Data Compliance:** Management confirmed the tech stack (ArvayaSecure app / SecurANT.ai) is HIPAA, HL7, FHIR, and DPDP Act compliant, built on consent-based architecture.
- **Tech Spending:** Management earmarked Rs 4.5 crore from the upcoming rights issue for SecurANT.ai development, with ongoing annual tech expenditure projected at Rs 2–3 crore.
- **Diagnostic Network Plans:** Management plans to develop a network of satellite labs feeding into central hospital labs; further details promised in Q3 FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Arvaya Healthcare is scaling an asset-light, multi-specialty hospital network in Tier 2/3 cities backed by technology, targeting 1,200 beds by FY28 while sustaining 13–14% EBITDA margins.
