# Concord Biotech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY 27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong broad-based revenue growth of 25% YoY and robust performance across API and newer segments despite temporary raw material cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs. 309.1 crore (up 25% YoY) for Q2 FY 27; Rs. 566.6 crore (up 26% YoY) for H1 FY 27.
- API Revenue: Rs. 250.1 crore (up 30% YoY) for Q2 FY 27.
- Formulation Revenue: Rs. 58.9 crore (up 6% YoY) for Q2 FY 27.
- Domestic Formulation Growth: 29% YoY in Q2 FY 27.
- Gross Margin: 76.2% in Q2 FY 27 (contracted by ~400 bps YoY); 77.4% in H1 FY 27.
- EBITDA: Rs. 104.4 crore (up ~18% YoY) in Q2 FY 27; Rs. 187 crore (up 25% YoY) in H1 FY 27.
- EBITDA Margin: 33.8% in Q2 FY 27; 33.0% in H1 FY 27. (Normalized H1 EBITDA margin excluding new injectable facility and Stellon expenses: 37.8%).
- PAT: Rs. 69.6 crore (up 10.3% YoY) in Q2 FY 27; Rs. 127.3 crore (up 19% YoY) for H1 FY 27.
- PAT Margin: 22.5% for Q2 and H1 FY 27.
- Capacity Utilization: Unit I at 75%, Unit III at 55%, Unit II at 21%, Unit IV (Injectables) at 5%.
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: ~Rs. 445 crore as of September 30, 2026. Zero debt.
- CapEx Guidance: Rs. 50-60 crore each for FY 27 and next year, plus Rs. 20-30 crore maintenance CapEx annually.

### Highlights
- Growth was broad-based across all product categories and geographies (U.S., Latin America, Japan).
- Oncology portfolio nearly doubled YoY; immunosuppressants delivered high double-digit growth; anti-infectives and antifungal grew in line with company average.
- Received ANDA approvals for Tofacitinib and Mycophenolate Mofetil Oral Suspension; filed US DMF for Fidaxomicin.
- Completed USFDA inspection of Unit-II (Valthera) and received ANVISA approval for Unit-III (Limbasi).
- Launched dermatology division 'Inspira'.
- Board recommended a 1:1 bonus issue, approved by shareholders.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY 27 revenue growth to be slightly better than the historical growth of 18%.
- Expects EBITDA margins to move toward the 38%-40% historical range over the next few years as new business units ramp up.
- Effective tax rate guided at 24%-25%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Customers in emerging markets are shifting procurement strategies toward APIs instead of finished formulations, and shifting procurement to Indian subsidiaries.
- Expanding customer wallet share through competitive pricing enabled by economies of scale and fermentation expertise.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin contracted by ~400 bps YoY due to higher raw material prices (solvents) from geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disruptions, alongside product mix changes.
- Management considers raw material cost pressures temporary and intends to address them via R&D and productivity rather than price hikes.
- Power and fuel costs were partially offset by renewable energy investments made last year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned CapEx of Rs. 100-120 crore total across FY 27 and next year (Rs. 50-60 crore per year) plus maintenance CapEx.
- Actively engaging with large domestic and global customers to finalize contracts for the injectables facility (Unit-IV) in H2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Zero-debt balance sheet with cash and cash equivalents of ~Rs. 445 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disruptions leading to higher solvent and raw material prices.
- Near-term margin dilution from low capacity utilization at the new injectable facility (Unit-IV) and Stellon operations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash Tanna (I thought PMS) asked about growth drivers and sustainability of API growth.** Management stated historical 18-19% growth trajectory in API is sustainable, augmented by injectables and CDMO.
- **Abhishek Kamdar (Value Plus Advisors) asked about pricing pressure and passing on raw material costs.** Management clarified there is no pricing pressure on Concord; they win customers by undercutting competitors' high prices. RM cost increases are temporary and will be absorbed via productivity.
- **Chirag (KEYNOTE) inquired about the 'Inspira' dermatology division's addressable market and targets.** Management stated the domestic market size for their range is ~Rs. 5000 crore, and aims to build significant share over 4-5 years as a fully integrated player.
- **Siddharth Negandhi (CWC) asked about gross margin headwinds, product mix, and CDMO revenues.** Management clarified mix impact referred to API product-level mix changes, and noted that low utilization in injectables temporarily impacts gross margins due to market-price transfers of APIs. CDMO current commercial project runs at ~$2 million annually.
- **Alankar (Kotak) questioned facility utilization rates and the path to historical 40% margins.** Management provided exact facility utilization numbers (Unit I: 75%, Unit III: 55%, Unit II: 21%, Unit IV: 5%) and stated that as Stellon and injectables ramp up in H2 and beyond, margins will migrate back to 38-39%.

### One-line takeaway
Concord Biotech delivered a strong 25% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY 27 driven by broad-based traction across APIs and newer segments, though temporary raw material inflation and low utilization of new facilities impacted gross margins.
