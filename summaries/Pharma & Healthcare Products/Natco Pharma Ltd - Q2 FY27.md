# Natco Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong international formulation performance and domestic recovery offset YoY revenue decline due to lower Lenalidomide sales.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised full-year PAT guidance to ₹775–800 crore (up from ₹700–750 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Tone and demand view:** US market remains challenging with steep price erosion in multi-filer launches, while Rest of the World (Brazil, Canada, MENA) and South Africa remain steady growth drivers.
- **New topics or risks:** Crop Health Sciences demerger timeline pushed back by 4–6 months to mid-CY27 due to capital structure changes following the rights issue.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Executed fundraise via Rights Issue (10% dilution) instead of QIP; fully repaid debt taken for the Adcock acquisition.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Revenue:** ₹1,019.4 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs ₹794.0 crore in Q1 FY27 and ₹1,463.0 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA (incl. other income):** ₹310.0 crore; **EBITDA Margin:** 30.4%.
- **Consolidated PAT:** ₹202.6 crore (vs ₹206.5 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **Domestic Formulation Revenue:** ₹145.0 crore (base domestic run rate estimated at ₹130–135 crore excluding Semaglutide stocking).
- **Brazil Business Revenue:** ₹121.0 crore (66% YoY growth).
- **Adcock Ingram (Associate, 49% stake):** Revenue of ₹1,344.0 crore, PAT of ₹80.5 crore; NATCO’s consolidated share of profit stood at ₹29.2 crore after purchase price allocation (PPA) adjustments.
- **Dividend Received:** ₹78–79 crore received from associate Adcock Ingram in H1.

### Highlights
- Completed a Rights Issue with a 10% equity dilution to repay debt and fund future M&A and working capital.
- Repaid all outstanding debt; company now holds net cash of ~₹2,250 crore.
- Pomalidomide launched with shared first-to-file (FTF) status alongside 5–6 generic competitors, leading to steep price erosion and minimal earnings impact.
- Investment in eGenesis stands at just under $25 million (single-digit stake); clinical trial ongoing with one pig-to-human kidney transplant patient approaching one year without dialysis.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-Year PAT:** Raised to ₹775–800 crore for FY27 (management expects Q3 to be strong and Q4 to be softer).
- **R&D Spend:** Expected at 8%–10% of total sales for FY27 (~₹300–350 crore).
- **Domestic Business Growth:** Targeting internal compounding growth of 15%–20% for next year.
- **ROW / US FY28 Outlook:** Broad expectation of 10%–15% growth, excluding exclusive/one-off upsides.
- **Agro Business (Crop Health Sciences):** Expected full-year EBITDA loss of ~₹20 crore (embedded in PAT guidance), with revenue growth targeted at 15%–20%. Demerger expected by mid-CY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **US Market:** High competition drives low margins across generic product baskets; strategy relies on patient execution around limited-competition opportunities.
- **Domestic Semaglutide:** NATCO holds ~5%–6% volume share (3% value share); selling ₹1.5–2.0 crore/month (₹40–50 lakh from pens, ₹1.2–1.5 crore from vials). Market pricing is hyper-competitive and currently un-profitable.
- **Rest of the World (ROW):** Strong growth in MENA and Brazil (80% base growth); Canada expected to see a blowout year in CY27 backed by launches.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- R&D expenses increased QoQ due to milestone achievement; facility fees to US FDA and legal expenses also contributed to higher other expenses.
- Standalone tax rate expected at 27.0%–27.2%; consolidated tax rate expected at 29%–30% for FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Annual capex target maintained at ~₹400 crore; H1 FY27 capex reached ~₹300 crore.
- Ongoing capex projects: Upgrades at Chennai and Mekaguda API facilities, new oncology line at Nagarjuna Sagar (commenced production), and an in-house cartridge line for Semaglutide (operational next fiscal).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Cash:** ~₹2,250 crore as of post-rights issue deployment. Zero outstanding bank loans following full repayment.
- **Working Capital:** India formulation DSO stands between 90 and 120 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High price erosion in US generic launches with shared FTF status (e.g., Pomalidomide, 60 mg Carfilzomib).
- Ongoing losses in the Crop Health Sciences segment due to weather-related headwinds.
- Regulatory approval delay for US Ozempic (answering queries, no approval yet).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shekhar Jain on Pomalidomide FTF monetization:** Management answered that 5 to 6 generic competitors entered in the first wave, causing steep price erosion and minimal earnings contribution despite 180-day exclusivity.
- **Bhuvanesh Bhoomi on Agro business demerger timeline:** Management stated the file needs resubmission to SEBI following capital structure changes post-rights issue, delaying demerger to mid-CY27.
- **Subhash Gupta on near-term acquisition timelines:** Management confirmed actively negotiating one transaction seriously, subject to agreements and regulatory approvals.
- **Digvijay Patil on buying 100% of Adcock Ingram:** Management stated only the 49% stake was available for purchase from the seller, but they hold the first right of refusal for the remaining stake if put up for sale.

### One-line takeaway
NATCO raised its FY27 PAT guidance to ₹775–800 crore and turned net cash of ₹2,250 crore post-rights issue, buffering generic US pricing pressures with strong ROW growth and associate contributions.
