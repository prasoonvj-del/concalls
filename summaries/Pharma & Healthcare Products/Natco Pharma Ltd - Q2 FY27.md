# Natco Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - International formulation growth, strong performance in Brazil, and an upgraded full-year PAT guidance offset lower lenalidomide revenues.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Raised full-year PAT guidance from ~INR 750 crores to INR 775–800 crores.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positive regarding diversified base business earnings and overall resilience without lenalidomide.
- New topics or risks: Rights issue completed (used to repay all debt and retain cash for M&A); eGenesis clinical trials and monetization discussed. QIP option dropped in favor of rights issue.
- Last quarter's promises: Adcock Ingram additional 13.25% stake acquired in July 2026 as promised, reaching 49% holding.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Revenues:** INR 1,019.4 crores (vs INR 794 crores in Q1 FY27 and INR 1,463 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA (including other income):** INR 310 crores.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 30.4%.
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 202.6 crores (vs INR 206.5 crores in Q1 FY27).
- **Adcock Ingram Holdings Limited (Associate):** Revenue at INR 1,344 crores; PAT at INR 80.5 crores; NATCO's 49% share of profit stood at INR 29.2 crores.
- **Domestic Formulation Revenue:** INR 145 crores (normalized base run rate at INR 130–135 crores).
- **Brazil Revenue:** INR 121 crores (representing 66% growth).
- **Net Cash:** INR 2,250 crores post-rights issue.

### Highlights
- Completed a rights issue last month (10% dilution); proceeds used to repay all bank loans and retain cash for working capital and potential M&A.
- Acquired an additional 13.25% stake in Adcock Ingram in July 2026, bringing total holdings to 49%.
- Crop Health Sciences recorded marginal profitability (INR 10 lakh profit) for the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised full-year PAT expectation to between INR 775 crores to INR 800 crores.
- Domestic formulation business targeted to compound at 15%–20% next year.
- Crop Health Sciences revenue growth expected at 15%–20% over last year.
- Demerger of Agri-fertilizer business pushed out to mid-calendar year 2027 due to SEBI refiling requirements post-rights issue recapitalization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- US remains a tough generics market requiring patience and basket plays; limited-competition products drive strong margins while multi-competitor products face severe price erosion.
- Semaglutide in India facing heavy pricing pressure and intense competition; NATCO estimates 5%–6% volume share and 3% value share, currently operating at a loss.
- South Africa associate Adcock Ingram saw lower sequential profit due to seasonal flu fade from Q1 and one-off amortization/VRS charges.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- R&D spend expected to be 8%–10% of top line (INR 300 to INR 350 crores for the year).
- Standalone tax rate expected around 27%–27.2%; consolidated tax rate around 29%–30%.
- Crop Health business expected to lose about INR 20 crores at the EBITDA level for the full year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Annual capex run rate targeted at INR 350–INR 400 crores (spent higher in H1 for API/formulation facility upgrades at Chennai and Mekaguda, a new oncology line at Nagarjuna Sagar, and a semaglutide cartridge line).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash stands at INR 2,250 crores.
- Entire bank loan repaid post-rights issue; zero debt currently.
- India DSO range expected to remain between 90–120 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Steep price erosion in Pomalidomide due to 5 to 6 shared generic first-to-file competitors.
- High lumpiness and unpredicted quarterly spikes in R&D and milestone-driven expenses.
- Crop Health business expected to incur an EBITDA loss of ~INR 20 crores for the full year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shekhar Jain (Individual Investor) on Pomalidomide and rights issue:** Management explained shared first-to-file caused steep price erosion limiting earnings bumps, and rights proceeds are for debt reduction and M&A.
2. **Rashmi Shetty on guidance revision:** Management raised full-year PAT guidance to INR 775–800 crores, citing strong H1 and good visibility in Q3.
3. **Abdul Kedar on Semaglutide and R&D:** Management clarified domestic base is running at INR 130–135 crores and full-year R&D will be 8%–10% of revenue.
4. **Kunal Randeria on domestic growth and demerger:** Management guided 15%–20% domestic growth next year and delayed the CHS demerger to mid-CY2027 due to SEBI rules post-rights issue.
5. **Digvijay Patil on debt and dividends:** Management confirmed zero debt currently following rights issue repayments and maintained that steady dividends are independent of fundraises.

### One-line takeaway
Natco Pharma reported Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue of INR 1,019.4 crores and PAT of INR 202.6 crores, raised its full-year PAT guidance to INR 775–800 crores, and cleared all debt using rights issue proceeds.
