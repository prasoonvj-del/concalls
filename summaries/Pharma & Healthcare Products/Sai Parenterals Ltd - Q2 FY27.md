# Sai Parenterals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue rose 9% sequentially to INR196 crores, gross margin expanded significantly to 47.5%, and EBITDA grew 67% sequentially to INR41 crores.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27): INR196.1 crores (up 9% QoQ from INR179.9 crores)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: INR376 crores
- Gross Margin (Q2 FY27): 47.5% (up from 40.9% in Q1 FY27)
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): INR41.4 crores (up 67.4% QoQ from INR24.7 crores restated)
- EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 21.1% (up from 13.7% in Q1 FY27)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR66.1 crores (17.6% margin)
- Profit Before Tax (Q2 FY27): INR9.9 crores
- Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27): INR10.5 crores (up 32.7% QoQ from INR7.9 crores)
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR18.4 crores
- Total Borrowings (as of 30th Sept 2026): INR357 crores (vs ~INR320 crores at March 2026)
- Cash & Cash Equivalents (as of 30th Sept 2026): INR129 crores
- Capital Expenditure (H1 FY27): ~INR70 crores spent on projects (Adelaide facility and Unit III)

### Highlights
- Noumed Pharmaceuticals consolidated since 12th November 2025.
- IPO proceeds deployment: INR98.8 crores total for 60% stakes in Saicriti Pharma (INR83.83 crores) and Sai Prathyak Laboratories (INR15 crores, renamed from Prathyak Laboratories). Balance INR3 crores allocated to general corporate purposes.
- Saicriti Pharma site at Gummadidala layout design complete, purchase orders placed for 4 process lines; target completion July 2027. Will replace Jeedimetla Units I and II.
- Sai Prathyak Laboratories acquired 60% stake on 15th September 2026, bringing 65 personnel (28 research scientists), holding 174 molecules and 285 SKUs across critical care and oncology.
- Board approved acquisition of the balance 25% stake in SP Analytics Private Limited to make it a wholly owned subsidiary.
- Adelaide manufacturing facility: AUD46 million invested out of AUD53 million planned, supported by an AUD20 million Australian Federal Government grant. Commissioning expected in January 2027; TGA license inspection anticipated in Q2 2027.
- Contracted Australia OTC supply business renewed: EBOS Group (AUD202 million, 7.5-year exclusive contract) and Wesfarmers (AUD30 million / ~INR204 crores, 3-year contract), taking total contracted Australia OTC supply to AUD232 million (~INR1,506 crores).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided to maintain gross margins above 45% in all coming quarters.
- Full year FY27 revenue target of INR750 crores expected to be comfortably crossed; H1 already covers INR376 crores (ahead of plan, maintaining historical 45:55 H1:H2 split).
- EBITDA margin for the full year expected to comfortably exceed the initially guided ~17% (H1 stood at 17.6%).
- FY27 remains a year of building; FY28 expected to reflect results of these assets. Guidance for FY28 will be provided in the next earnings call (Jan/Feb).

### Demand & industry commentary
- CDMO contributed ~73% and branded generics ~27% of Q2 revenue (75:25 split for H1).
- Exports made up ~73% of Q2 revenue. Within exports, Australia contributed 76%, Philippines 16%, South Africa 4%, and other markets 3%.
- Private sector customers accounted for 80% and institutional customers 20% of revenue.
- Philippines business added 11 molecules and 22 SKUs in Q2 led by cardiovascular therapies under the existing USD14 million contract; anti-TB USD11 million contract validation batches done with commercials starting post-January 2027.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs fell by about 3% QoQ to INR103.0 crores despite 9% revenue growth, driven by tighter inventory management, FIFO controls, and a new ERP system implementation.
- Employee and other operating costs together rose by less than 6% QoQ.
- Depreciation and amortization rose to INR25.1 crores (from INR8.1 crores in Q1) due to Noumed asset capitalization and molecule amortization from Sai Prathyak.
- Finance costs eased to INR9.0 crores from INR9.4 crores QoQ.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Unit III (Bhongir) expansion almost completed, new block commencing December 2026; capacity to rise from 85.5 million to 155 million units/month (81% increase), ramping up from Q4 FY27.
- Saicriti Pharma facility target completion July 2027, taking injectable capacity to ~154.66 million units (47% higher than Unit I and II upgrade). Total project cost estimated at INR215 crores (Company contribution INR83.83 crores via IPO proceeds, 40% funded by promoters, remainder via project debt with 3-year moratorium and 7-year repayment).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at INR357 crores as of 30th September 2026 vs ~INR320 crores in March 2026.
- FY26/FY27 cited as peak debt years due to peak capex, with debt expected to decline from FY28 and FY29 as assets generate earnings. Saicriti project debt sits at the subsidiary level.
- Receivables increased due to bunching of sales towards the end of the quarter and lower export receipts; expected to normalize in Q3 and Q4. Export aging is <120 days, domestic branded generic aging is 120-150 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Logistics disruptions due to the Iran conflict caused a slight delay in the Adelaide facility commissioning timeline (now January 2027).
- Project integration challenges noted across subsidiaries, which management is addressing by increasing management bandwidth and KMP hiring.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Heet (Axis Capital) on margin sustainability and guidance quantification:** Management stated gross margins will stay above 45% due to inventory controls, and full-year revenue of INR750 crores and EBITDA margins above 17% will be comfortably beaten. (Directly answered)
2. **Amol Rao on the spike in receivables:** Management explained it was a timing issue due to sales bunching up in the last 1.5 months of the quarter and lower realizations from two export customers, which will normalize in Q3/Q4. (Directly answered)
3. **Murali on US FDA entry plans for injectables:** Management clarified that while the Saicriti facility is built to US FDA standards, prime focus for the next 2-3 years remains EU GMP, Southeast Asia, Latin America, and Middle East; US entry will not happen independently for at least 2-3 years unless via a CMO client. (Directly answered)
4. **Gopal Bhatt on cash flow from operations fluctuations:** Management explained the cash flow movements were driven by OFS payments (INR113.1 crores from IPO proceeds) and changes in current liabilities/trade payables. (Directly answered)
5. **Bhavika Singhvi on overall 3-year vision and integration challenges:** Management outlined the 3-year vision of becoming a technology-driven player across all dosage forms (tablets, onco, mAbs, injectables) with in-house R&D, and acknowledged integration challenges which are being handled by expanding senior management bandwidth. (Directly answered)

### One-line takeaway
Sai Parenterals delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with consolidated revenue up 9% QoQ to INR196 crores and EBITDA surging 67% QoQ to INR41.4 crores (21.1% margin) driven by strict inventory controls and gross margin expansion to 47.5%, while major expansion projects in India and Australia remain on track for FY28 contributions.
