# Manorama Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong 41.9% YoY revenue growth and 51.8% PAT growth, supported by healthy demand and a value-added product mix.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (no specific numeric guidance provided, but reiterated confidence in long-term plans).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained positive; management noted no slowdowns in demand from global customers.
- **New topics:** Leadership restructuring (new MD, Chairman, and President roles announced).
- **Old vs New:** Inventory levels (specifically early shea procurement) became a major discussion point this quarter, whereas it was not a primary focus in Q1.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 459 crore (up 41.9% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 118 crore (up 34.4% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 25.8%
- **PAT:** Rs 81 crore (up 51.8% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 17.6% (expanded 114 bps YoY)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 863 crore (up 40.8% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 224 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 159 crore (up 59.2% YoY)
- **Inventory:** Rs 1,000 crore (vs Rs 728 crore in March 2026)
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.04x (vs 0.38x in FY26)

### Highlights
- **Leadership Restructuring:** Appointed Mudit Kumar Singh as Chairman, Deep Saraf as MD, Vinita Saraf as President (Business Excellence), and Ashish Saraf as President (Strategic Planning).
- **Operational Performance:** Exports accounted for 61% of H1 revenue; company serves customers in 39+ countries.
- **Inventory:** Sharp increase in inventory attributed to early seasonal procurement of shea nuts from West Africa.
- **Other Income:** H1 other income of Rs 25 crore included Rs 15 crore forex gain and Rs 10 crore interest on FDRs.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Commissioning:** New projects (backward/forward integration) targeted for commissioning in Q3/Q4 FY28.
- **Capacity Utilization:** Expects 85%-90% utilization for existing capacity; new capacity expected to ramp up gradually over 2-3 years post-commissioning.
- **Margins:** Management considers the 25%-27% EBITDA margin band comfortable, though modest quarterly fluctuations may occur due to macro factors.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Demand:** No slowdown or order deferrals observed from global chocolate or cosmetic customers; product supply remains tight.
- **Cocoa Prices:** Management clarified there is no direct linkage between cocoa price volatility and their specialty fat products (CBE/stearin).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Freight Costs:** Higher inbound freight costs due to global logistics/shipping route issues impacted raw material costs.
- **Product Mix:** Value-added products (CBE and stearin) contribute ~75% to total sales value.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capacity:** Current capacity is 47,500 tonnes, operating at ~85% utilization.
- **Capex Status:** ~Rs 70 crore-Rs 75 crore invested in projects to date; designing and engineering phases are ongoing.
- **LATAM:** Facility is gradually ramping up; volumes are being routed from Indian plants while the LATAM unit scales.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Cash Position:** Cash and cash equivalents/bank balances stood at Rs 498 crore.
- **Working Capital:** Funded through existing banking limits and internal resources.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Logistics:** Global shipping route disruptions and freight cost inflation remain external risks.
- **Execution:** Projects are complex and require significant engineering/design work, which management is monitoring closely.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nitin (Bank of India Mutual Fund):** Asked about raw material cost elevation. Management explained it is primarily due to higher freight costs for imported shea nuts, not a change in reporting.
2. **Tanay Jain:** Asked about early shea procurement. Management clarified it is seasonal and part of the normal course of business to secure supply from West Africa.
3. **Dipali:** Asked about capex progress. Management stated they have invested Rs 70-75 crore and are in the critical design/engineering phase, with larger investments expected in H2 and next fiscal.
4. **Vanshika Gupta:** Asked about cocoa price impact on realizations. Management stated there is no direct linkage between cocoa prices and their specialty fat products.
5. **Mahima Gidwani:** Asked about receivable increase. Management stated it is a timing difference related to the last 15 days of September revenue and has since normalized.

### One-line takeaway
Manorama Industries reported strong H1 growth and a robust balance sheet, while navigating logistics-led cost pressures and focusing on the execution of its multi-year expansion projects.
