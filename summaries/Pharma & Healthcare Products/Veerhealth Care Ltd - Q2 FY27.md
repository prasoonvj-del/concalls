# Veerhealth Care Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (Note: Call dated October 28, 2026, discussing Q1 FY27 results and FY27 outlook)
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights strong revenue scaling, US FDA certification driving domestic contract manufacturing ties, and expansion plans to reach the Rs 100 crore turnover target for FY27.

### Key numbers
- FY26 turnover: Approximately Rs 30 crore (also referred to as Rs 33 crore in presentation)
- Q1 FY27 revenue: Approximately Rs 25 crore
- Q1 FY27 profit: Rs 125 lakh (Rs 1.25 crore)
- FY27 turnover target: Approximately Rs 100 crore
- FY27 PAT target: Rs 5 to 6 crore
- Current capacity utilization: 65% to 70% (also stated as 70% to 75%)
- Additional production area acquired: 30,000 square feet (increasing total from 60,000 to 90,000 square feet in October 2024)
- Q1 revenue breakup: 70% contract manufacturing, 20% export business, 10% own brand (Ayurveda/Ayuveer)

### Highlights
- Crossed the entire previous financial year's turnover within a single quarter (Q1 FY27).
- Secured US FDA plant certification under the drug OTC category (audited March 2025, approval July 2025).
- Added a plastic division (Veer Plast) for backward integration of bottles, caps, canisters, enclosures, and toothbrush manufacturing.
- Received a first trial order from an Indo-Canadian company supplying to major airlines and 5-star hotels.
- Association with a leading Indian FMCG player (top 3 on local marketplaces) for face care and skin care contract manufacturing has been a major growth driver.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to achieve a turnover of approximately Rs 100 crore for FY26-27 with a PAT of Rs 5 to 6 crore.
- Capacity utilization is expected to reach 90% to 100% by the first quarter of next financial year following expansions completing around December.
- Promoter holding increase is under consideration for coming quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- US export demand remains steady via long-term supply agreements (LTAs) with a customer supplying to the US government, despite global transit/freight issues and tariffs.
- Domestic contract manufacturing demand is surging due to partnering with established FMCG and retail pharmacy networks (over 7,000+ outlets).
- Hospitality supply chain and airline amenity markets being targeted via the new Canadian collaboration.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price volatility (such as from the Gulf war) has minimal impact as major contract manufacturing raw material pricing is on "principal scope" (negotiated and absorbed by the principal/customers).
- Margin expansion expected via automation, economies of scale, and increased export shipments (targeting 12-15 shipments/month up from 6-7).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acquired 30,000 sq. ft. of adjacent land in October 2024, expanding the Vapi facility to 90,000 sq. ft.
- Adding 3 new manufacturing lines by December, taking personal care lines to 5-6 and oral care to 3.
- Introducing a 5 metric ton manufacturing capacity shortly.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Preferential allotment process for fundraising is underway and expected to be completed within two months; no equity fundraising via preferential process outside of this is active currently.
- Working capital, receivables, and payables are supported by the principal customer through quick payment terms and operational expense support.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global freight, transit issues, and tariffs impacted US shipping previously, though operations have settled.
