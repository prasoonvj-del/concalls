# GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027  
**Tone:** Positive - strong broad-based growth across all three business pillars with margin expansion and accelerating innovation portfolio contribution

### Key numbers
- Sales: INR 924 crore, up 15% YoY
- EBITDA: up 17% YoY (2 pp ahead of sales), margin +50 bps YoY
- PAT: INR 253 crore, up 24% YoY; margin 27.6% (+200 bps YoY, includes one-off subsidiary dividend)
- EPS: INR 14.95
- Innovation portfolio: 7% of revenue (vs 4% in Q1 FY26), contributing ~4 pp to headline growth
- Underlying sustainable growth (ex-CMO base effect): 9–10%
- Volume growth: 3–3.5%; Price growth: ~6%; balance from new introductions
- Shingrix: 65% YoY growth, 45,000–50,000 doses/quarter
- Nucala: ~600 active patients, business doubling YoY
- Oncology (Zejula + Jemperli): 260–270 patients in Q1; 600–700 patients in first 6 months of CY2026
- MR productivity: INR 15–16 lakh/rep (+5–6% QoQ)
- Field force: ~2,000 MRs (Gen Med), 200 (established vaccines), 40–45 (oncology)
- S&A ratio up ~1 pp QoQ (deliberate front-loading), expected to normalize
- Full-year EBITDA margin guidance: ~34% range (historical level)

### Revenue mix
- General Medicines: lion’s share, double-digit growth, all brands EI 105–106 vs market
- Vaccines: private pediatric market leader; Shingrix 65% YoY; adult vaccination ecosystem building
- Specialty: Respiratory (Nucala, Trelegy Ellipta) + Oncology (Zejula, Jemperli) – new growth platforms
- Innovation portfolio: 7% of sales (vs 4% YoY)
- Acute segment: ~60% of Indian pharma market; GSK has significant presence

### Highlights
- Industry grew ~13.5% in Q1 with balanced volume (~2%), price, new introductions
- GSK outgrew represented market across metros, Tier 1/2, rural; specialists, super-specialists, GPs
- All therapy areas where GSK operates (anti-infectives, pain, respiratory, vitamins, derma, hormones, vaccines) grew double-digit
- Gen Med turnaround confirmed: market share gains, double-digit growth
- Vaccines: private pediatric leadership maintained; Shingrix scaling rapidly
- Specialty: Nucala new patient uptake doubled (100–120/month vs 50–60); Trelegy holds 10k–12k units/month despite generics
- Oncology launched Q2 FY26; 260–270 patients touched in Q1
- Upcoming launches: Blenrep (Belantamab, 2L refractory myeloma) in 2–3 months; Arexvy (RSV adult vaccine, marketing authorization received); Bepirovirsen (Hibsago, functional cure Hep B) in next 3 quarters
- Supply chain disruptions from CMO issue fully resolved; business continuity plans strengthened
- Super-distributor arrangement for tail-end products in mofussil areas
- Clinical trials brought to India (e.g., B-Well for Bepirovirsen) reducing launch lag

### Guidance & outlook
- No formal revenue guidance; intent to sustain double-digit momentum
- INR 8,000 crore target in 4–5 years implies 13–14% CAGR (8–10% base + innovation bolt-ons)
- EBITDA margins to normalize to ~34% range for full year
- S&A ratios to revert to historical levels after Q1 front-loading
- Blenrep launch in Q2/Q3 FY27; Arexvy imminent; Bepirovirsen within 3 quarters
- Pneumococcal 24-valent not a priority for India currently

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 (Jul–Sep) is peak acute season; June–July trends tracking well, Q2 expected in line
- Oncology fastest-growing therapy at 27% YoY vs industry 12–13%
- Adult vaccination addressable: 10–12M adults 50+ with comorbidities/disposable income
- Hepatitis B burden: 40M patients in India; functional cure category creation needed
- Endometrial cancer: 40K prevalent, 17–19K new diagnoses/year
- Multiple myeloma: 19K new cases/year, 7–8K in 2L setting

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin +150 bps YoY (pricing + product mix)
- Imported purchase costs hardened due to rupee depreciation, but net margin improved
- No immediate RM/packaging pressure from West Asia conflict (embedded global supply chains)
- EBITDA grew faster than sales despite 36% YoY opex spike (deliberate front-loading)
- PAT margin +200 bps (includes one-off dividend from Biddle Sawyer subsidiary)

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing: own facility at Nashik (MIDC) + 20 contract manufacturers
- Gen Med fully locally manufactured; local-for-local model
- No plans for new local manufacturing beyond current portfolio
- EU/UK FTA not immediately material

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash position maintained
- Focus on balance sheet optimization
- One-off dividend from 100% subsidiary Biddle Sawyer boosted PAT

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rupee depreciation impacting imported costs
- High Q1 investment run-rate not sustainable; must normalize
- Oncology portfolio still early stage; patient funnel building
- Hepatitis B commercial model complexity (functional cure vs chronic therapy economics)
- Generic competition for Trelegy (Umeclidinium generics launched)
- Category creation required for adult vaccines (Shingrix, Arexvy)
