# GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY 2027
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit revenue and PAT growth, balanced performance across portfolios, and successful acceleration of innovative assets.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 924 crore, up 15% YoY (headline growth helped by a 4%-5% favorable base effect from last year's CMO supply issues; sustainable normalized growth at 9% to 10%).
- **EBITDA:** Grew 17% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin:** Improved by 50 bps YoY.
- **PAT (Profit After Tax):** Rs 253 crore, up 24% YoY (includes a one-off dividend payout from 100% subsidiary Biddle Sawyer; ex-one-off PAT growth would be 17%).
- **PAT Margin:** 27.6%, expanded by 200 bps YoY.
- **EPS:** Rs 14.95.
- **Innovation Portfolio Contribution:** 7% to 7.5% of total revenues (up from about 4% last year); contributed 4 percentage points to headline growth.
- **Shingrix Growth:** 65% YoY, selling upwards of 45,000-50,000 doses quarterly.
- **Nucala Patient Uptake:** 100-120 new patients per month (double last year's pace); roughly 600 active patients on therapy.
- **Trelegy Ellipta:** 10,000-12,000 units monthly.
- **Oncology Portfolio (Zejula & Jemperli):** Touched 260-270 patients in Q1; ~600-700 patients for the first six months of the calendar year (~250-300 patients quarterly).
- **Blended MR Productivity:** INR 15 lakhs to INR 16 lakhs per rep (improved 5% to 6% YoY).

### Highlights
- Industry external performance grew ~13.5% in Q1 with ~2% volume growth; GSK represented markets grew better than the external market across metros, Tier 1, Tier 2, and rural areas.
- General Medicines business grew in double-digits; all Gen Med brands grew better than the market (EI of 105-106).
- Pediatric vaccines maintained private market leadership; adult vaccination ecosystem work scaling up successfully.
- Oncology portfolio (Zejula for ovarian cancer, Jemperli for endometrial cancer) continuing strong momentum.
- Local manufacturing remains centered around Nashik and 20 contract manufacturers for GenMeds (local-for-local model).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its overriding objective to sustain double-digit growth momentum and achieve the 2x revenue vision (INR 8,000 crore top line in 4 to 5 years) driven by new innovative launches as arrowheads.
- Management expects S&A / opex investment ratios to normalize to historical levels in the following quarters, with full-year EBITDA margins expected around the 34% range.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry acute segment contributes 60% of the overall pharma market in India; acute season trends from June/July tracking well in line with expectations.
- Strong demand drivers in chronic and specialty areas (anti-infectives, pain, respiratory, nutritional supplements, vitamins, dermatologicals, hormones, and vaccines all grew double-digit).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins improved by 150 bps YoY, driven by pricing and product mix.
- Hardening of costs on imported purchases due to rupee depreciation was offset by net margin improvements.
- S&A spend-to-sales ratio was up by about 1 percentage point due to a deliberate, front-loaded concentration of direct marketing spends, targeted HCP events (~3,800 HCP events), and international speaker meets ahead of the peak Q2 season.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Field force: ~2,000 MRs in GenMeds, ~200 in established vaccines, ~40-45 in oncology. Incremental hiring to focus on therapy areas with little/or no presence (e.g., solid tumors, hematology, and upcoming liver disease).
- Upcoming launches within the next 18 to 24 months / near term: Belantamab Mafodotin (Blenrep) for second-line refractory relapse multiple myeloma (launch expected in next 2-3 months), Arexvy (RSV adult vaccine), Bepirovirsen (Hibsago) for chronic hepatitis B (potential launch in next 3-4 quarters), Blenrep, Ojjaara.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a strong cash position and focused on balance sheet optimization.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Hardening of costs on imported purchases due to rupee depreciation (though mitigated by well-embedded global supply chains).
- Supply chain disruption concerns addressed; management stated the worst from previous CMO issues is behind them, and supply chain continuity plans have been strengthened.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gokul Maheshwari (Awriga Capital Advisors LLP) on global restructuring impact on India:** Management stated they are suitably structured, noting that bringing global clinical trials faster to India aligns with global strategy.
2. **Vishal Manchanda on Bepirovirsen (Hibsago) commercialization and therapy duration:** Management noted it is a functional cure breakthrough for chronic hepatitis B (40 million disease burden in India), and patient funnels/roadmaps are currently being evaluated; details to be shared in subsequent quarters.
3. **Yash Doshi on opex spike:** Management clarified that the 36% YoY opex increase was a deliberate, front-loaded investment for Q1 events/evidence generation, and ratios will normalize in upcoming quarters.
4. **Rajat Srivastava on INR 8,000 crore revenue target:** Management confirmed the target is intact, relying on the strong GenMed/vaccines foundation growing at 8%-10% and high-growth innovative specialty assets acting as growth arrowheads.
5. **Vamsi Krishna on indications and patient numbers:** Management detailed that Zejula is for first-line ovarian maintenance, Jemperli is for first-line endometrial cancer (~17,000-19,000 new patients annually), and Arexvy targets 10-12 million eligible adults 50+.

### One-line takeaway
GSK Pharmaceuticals delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 15% revenue growth and 24% PAT growth (17% ex-one-off), driven by robust General Medicines performance, a 65% surge in Shingrix, and scaling contribution from specialty innovation assets.
