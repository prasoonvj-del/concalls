# Supriya Lifescience Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 3.2% YoY, but EBITDA declined 37.5% YoY due to regulatory shifts in LATAM, ramp-up costs at Ambernath, and high raw material/solvent prices.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 206 crores (up 3.2% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 45 crores (down 37.5% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22%
- **PAT:** INR 29 crores (down from INR 50 crores YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 14.2%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 396 crores (up 14.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 93 crores (down 25.3% YoY, margin at 23.4%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 53 crores (down from INR 85 crores YoY, margin at 13.5%)
- **H1 FY27 Capex:** INR 35.4 crores (Q2 FY27 capex: INR 18.8 crores)
- **Exports Contribution:** 80% of revenue in Q2 FY27 (Europe 43%, Asia 36%, LATAM 12%)
- **Backward-Integrated Revenue Share:** ~74%

### Highlights
- Management onboarded Mr. Mangesh Vaze as Chief Operating Officer (over 30 years of experience).
- Launched 2 new anesthetic liquid inhalation products from the Ambernath facility in Q2 FY27; 2 ADHD products are in the pipeline.
- EU audit for the Ambernath facility is scheduled for the week following the call (November 2026).
- *Contradiction with Record:* Our record states that Supriya delivered *double-digit H1 FY27 profit growth*, whereas the call transcript explicitly reports a 37.5% YoY decline in Q2 EBITDA and a 25.3% YoY decline in H1 EBITDA.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue:** Management reiterated confidence in achieving the **INR 1,000 crores revenue milestone by FY27**.
- **EBITDA Margin:** Management revised EBITDA margin guidance **downward to 23% - 25%** for FY27 (guiding ~25% for the remaining part of FY27).
- **Long-term growth:** Management continues to target 20%+ revenue growth over the next 3-4 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- LATAM demand was disrupted due to sudden regulatory changes classifying several high-margin controlled products as controlled substances, requiring customer import authorizations (estimated to take 4-5 months).
- Good traction observed for new launches in semi-regulated and domestic markets, though at lower average selling prices (ASPs).
- Strong global market potential for new launches: anesthetic molecule ($400M-$500M) and ADHD API market potential ($100M-$150M).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin dip of 11%-12% YoY broken down by management: ~6% from Ambernath operating expenses (approx. INR 60 crores for the full year) with zero revenue contribution, ~3% from LATAM regulatory/product mix shift, and ~3% from higher raw material and solvent prices.
- Fully backward-integrated products account for ~74% of revenue.
- Raw material and solvent price pressures continued and increased further from Q1, creating a lag in passing hikes to customers under existing large contracts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex was driven by the Ambernath facility, maintenance, Ribo block, and formulation plant requirements.
- Ambernath facility has injection, tablet, capsule, and liquid inhalation lines; designed to generate INR 450 crores - INR 500 crores at full scale.
- Ambernath breakeven sweet spot estimated around INR 160 crores - INR 200 crores in revenue, taking an estimated 1 to 1.5 years to ramp up post-approvals.
- Module E API capacity can scale up the new anesthetic product to almost 1,100 tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company has not utilized any working capital limits for the full year, except for letters of credit and bank guarantees.
- Inventory in the system stood at approximately INR 265 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unanticipated, immediate regulatory changes in LATAM (Colombia, Peru, Uruguay) disrupting high-margin product flows.
- Continued high geopolitical tensions causing elevated raw material and solvent costs.
- Delay in EU regulatory audit timeline for Ambernath pushing commercial revenue contribution out.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aashish (InvesQ PMS) on LATAM margin impact and duration:** Dr. Saloni Wagh clarified that the dip is transient (4-5 months for customer permits) and not permanent, attributing 6% of the margin drop to Ambernath expenses, 3% to LATAM/product mix, and 3% to raw materials.
2. **Sajal Kapoor (Antifragile Thinking) on achieving INR 1,000 cr revenue despite low H1:** Management confirmed confidence based on existing inventory (INR 265 crores) and firm order books scaling up in semi-regulated markets.
3. **Dheeraj (AlphaSqr) on communication/guidance credibility:** Challenged management on recurring negative surprises after guiding stable margins in Q1. Dr. Saloni Wagh cited unforeseen LATAM regulatory changes and raw material pressure as sudden events, acknowledging room to improve communication.
4. **Priyank (Singularity Asset Management) on passing raw material costs:** Management explained that while purchase order adjustments are underway, legacy fixed contracts with large regulated-market customers create a 2-3 quarter lag.
5. **Mukesh Savla (Shah and Savla LLP) on Ambernath revenue loss:** Management noted that Ambernath's FY27 revenue contribution will be closer to zero due to pushed EU audit timelines, creating a direct ~6% expense hit (INR 60 crores total expenses).
6. **Rishabh Tripathi (Multi-Dimensional Thinking) on legal/employee support:** Management confirmed full financial salary support was provided during judicial custody, bail has been granted, and they have moved to the Supreme Court for quashing by December 15.

### One-line takeaway
Supriya Lifescience maintained its FY27 revenue target of INR 1,000 crores but downgraded its EBITDA margin guidance to 23%-25%, weighed down by an un-preempted regulatory squeeze in LATAM, delayed Ambernath regulatory approvals creating an unabsorbed INR 60 crore expense overhead, and sticky raw material costs.
