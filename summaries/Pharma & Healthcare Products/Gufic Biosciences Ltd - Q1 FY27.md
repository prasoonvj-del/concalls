# Gufic Biosciences Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong operational progress at the Indore facility, successful new product launches, and a strategic shift toward an IP-led international model.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 260.8 crore (vs Rs 226.9 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 47.2 crore (vs Rs 33.2 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 18.09% (vs 14.6% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PBT:** Rs 30.1 crore (vs Rs 16.3 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 22.46 crore (vs Rs 12.1 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT Margin:** 8.61% (vs 5.3% in Q1 FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Indore Facility:** Currently operational; depot and microsphere suite nearing completion; lipid-based antifungal setup initiated.
*   **Critical Care:** Launched monobactam and beta-lactamase inhibitor combination; focus is on deepening coverage within existing hospital groups.
*   **International:** Shifting model from distributor-led to IP-led in Europe and emerging markets (Africa, Southeast Asia, South Asia) to capture higher margins. Secured approvals in 8 countries.
*   **GLP-1 (Semaglutide):** Entered a CMO partnership with Hetero; production traction expected to increase in Q2 and Q3.
*   **Aesthetics:** Partnered with Revanesse Prollenium (Canada) for fillers; launch expected by December/January.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management maintains a target of 15-20% YoY revenue growth.
*   **Capacity:** Indore capacity utilization is expected to reach 40-45% by the end of the year.
*   **Revenue Potential:** Management estimates a peak revenue potential of Rs 1,600-1,800 crore with existing facilities and current product mix.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management expects leverage to begin kicking in by mid-FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   The Indian filler market is estimated at approximately Rs 200 crore.
*   Management is prioritizing own-manufactured brands to improve supply reliability and margins.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins are expanding due to increased capacity utilization and a shift in product mix toward higher-value complex injectables.
*   Previous margin compression (Q3/Q4 FY26) was attributed to the cessation of capitalization of Indore-related expenses, depreciation, and interest.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   No major new capex planned; focus is on adding new product lines (depot, liposomal) within existing infrastructure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Sparsh division outstanding days are within standard trade terms.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory:** Awaiting feedback from authorities regarding EU certification for the Indore facility (expected in 1-2 months).
*   **Operational:** Short-term production impacts due to plant shutdowns for new machine installations and regulatory visits.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **GLP-1 Strategy:** Management clarified they are strictly a CMO partner for Hetero and have no plans to front-end the product in India or abroad.
*   **International Model:** Management explained that moving from distributor-led to IP-led models allows for 15-20% higher margins and builds long-term intangible assets.
*   **Employee Addition:** Management noted the increase in headcount was primarily to support Indore ramp-up and transition contract staff to payroll; this is considered a one-time phenomenon.
*   **Revenue Potential:** When asked about peak revenue, management clarified that with current infrastructure and product mix, they can extract Rs 1,600-1,800 crore, but this could improve with product basket changes.

### One-line takeaway
Gufic is scaling its Indore facility and transitioning to an IP-led international model, targeting 15-20% YoY growth with margin expansion driven by a shift toward higher-value complex injectables.
