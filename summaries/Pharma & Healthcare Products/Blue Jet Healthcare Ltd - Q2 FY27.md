# Blue Jet Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA with sequential growth across core businesses, marking the fourth consecutive quarter of sequential growth.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: ~Rs 376 crore (Up 28% QoQ). H1 FY27 revenue: Rs 669 crore (Up 29% YoY).
- EBITDA: ~Rs 119 crore (Up 21% QoQ). H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 217 crore.
- EBITDA Margin: 32% (down 1% QoQ). H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 32%.
- Profit After Tax (PAT): ~Rs 103 crore. H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 181 crore.
- Gross Margin: 49% in Q2 FY27 (down 4% QoQ). H1 FY27 gross margin: 51% (down 3% YoY).
- Segment QoQ Growth (Q2 FY27): Contrast Media up 25%, PI API up 52%, Artificial Sweetener degrowth of 30%.
- Segment H1 YoY Growth: Contrast Media up 47%, PI API up 19%, Artificial Sweetener degrowth of 2%.
- Capex: Mahad Unit 3 total capex incurred to date at ~Rs 250 crore; Vizag Phase I capex envisaged at ~Rs 1,000 crore.

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly revenue (~Rs 376 crore) and EBITDA (~Rs 119 crore).
- Hyderabad R&D center became operational with ~50 scientists onboarded; aims to add ~100 fume hoods over two phases.
- Successfully delivered and validated a new contrast media intermediate (NCE) and progressed on a peptide fragment with an innovator.
- Mahad Unit 3 remains on track to go on stream in H2 FY27 for backward integration of a contrast media KSM.
- *Note vs Record:* Peptide fragment deliveries advance toward expected commercialization timelines noted in prior records.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Shiven Arora, V.K. Singh) stated they remain encouraged by order visibility and customer engagement across contrast media and PI verticals.
- Management declined to provide specific forward-looking quarterly guidance or extrapolate Q2 growth numbers.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Contrast media business maintained healthy momentum driven by established customer relationships and product portfolio.
- PI vertical supported by improving demand from existing programs and newer products (including commercialized CNS and oncology products).
- Artificial sweetener segment witnessed a 30% QoQ degrowth in Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined to 49% in Q2 due to West Asia disruptions increasing prices of raw materials, reagents, solvents, and catalysts across the board.
- Management stated that certain raw material and solvent cost increases cannot be fully passed on immediately.
- Management is reviewing price increases contract-by-contract with select customers, effective from Q3.
- H1 margins were marginally impacted by headcount additions and logistics costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Mahad Unit 3 is on track to commission in H2 FY27 (~Rs 250 crore invested to date).
- Vizag (Units 5 & 6) pre-construction activities are underway with statutory compliances (EC, Consent to Establish) in place; Phase I capex is ~Rs 1,000 crore.
- Renewable energy contribution reached ~80%; achieved an EcoVadis silver rating.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- QIP proceeds will be deployed over the next three years toward capex, working capital, and general corporate purposes, strengthening the balance sheet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia crisis causing input cost inflation (solvents, reagents, catalysts, raw materials) putting pressure on gross margins.
- Inability to pass on 100% of reagent and solvent price increases to customers.
- Quarterly revenue and margins subject to shipment schedules and product mix fluctuations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amlan Das (JP Morgan)** asked about the new contrast media intermediate and peptide fragment ramp-up. *Management answered that both are NCEs/innovator products currently in clinical phases, making it premature to quantify contribution.*
- **Ravi Purohit (Securities Investment Management)** asked about iodinated ABA HCl shipments and PI order book. *Management stated projects are intact but declined to confirm or deny specific shipping statuses.*
- **Harshal Patil (ASK Wealth Advisors)** asked if Q2 gross margin acts as a new baseline. *Management stated it depends on crude price movements, but if crude remains flat, Q2 is a reasonable estimate.*
- **Bansi Desai** asked about flat constant currency contrast media revenues over 3 years. *Management clarified that volume growth is positive, offset slightly by volume discounts across product mix.*

### One-line takeaway
Blue Jet delivered record Q2 revenue and EBITDA driven by strong momentum in contrast media and PI segments, though gross margins compressed due to West Asia-induced raw material inflation.
