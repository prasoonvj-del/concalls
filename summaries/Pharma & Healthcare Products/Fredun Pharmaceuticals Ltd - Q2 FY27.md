# Fredun Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, accompanied by margin expansion and traction across new-age verticals.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained a confident outlook, stating the company has overachieved its INR 800 crore revenue target for FY27 and expects continued momentum.
- Tone and demand view: Remained highly optimistic, highlighting that true inflected growth will materialize in the next 18 to 24 months as ecosystems mature.
- New topics or risks: Addressed CARE report technical deviations, noting plans to announce a trained CFO in a few months and the appointment of an internal audit team.
- Last quarter's promises: Wagr.in platform operations are scaling with a 45-people team; cat food and hormonal product lines (CATICO) are on track to launch in January 2027 (60-90 days).

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Total Income: INR 267.64 crores, up 84.21% YoY (vs INR 145.29 crores in Q2 FY26)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: INR 42.6 crores, up 90.74% YoY
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin: 15.92%, expanded by 55 bps YoY
- Q2 FY27 Net Profit (PAT): INR 18.83 crores, up 93.44% YoY
- Q2 FY27 Net Profit Margin: 7.03%, expanded by 34 bps YoY
- H1 FY27 Total Income: INR 495.88 crores, up 87.02% YoY
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR 75.38 crores, up 91.67% YoY
- H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin: 15.20%, expanded by 37 bps YoY
- H1 FY27 Net Profit (PAT): INR 31.99 crores, up 93.92% YoY
- H1 FY27 Net Profit Margin: 6.45%

### Highlights
- Pet Care growing at 70% to 80%, mobility at 65% to 70%, mass market cosmetics at 65% to 70%, and vintage business growing at 15% to 25% YoY.
- Product portfolio expanded to approximately 2,400 products.
- Acquired a 26% stake in Vetcare, a 4-decade-old profitable pet retail pioneer and supplier to national parks, zoos, and Vantara operating on a no-burn model.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they have overachieved their INR 800 crore revenue guidance for FY27 and anticipate the same traction in the coming two quarters (Fredun Medhora).
- Management expects the real transformational growth to hit in the next 18 to 24 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth is diversified across seven verticals with no single dependency; vintage business grows at 15% to 20% annually while new-age businesses grow at 65% to 70% YoY.
- Transitioning from a typical pharma manufacturer to a consumer product company with intrinsically higher margins.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- New-age businesses are operationally profitable with intrinsically higher margins.
- A 7 to 8-month order book provides the capability to decline low-yielding products and protect margins in the vintage business.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- CARE report technical/clerical deviations were addressed by management, who noted they are technical technicalities being addressed with an internal audit team and a soon-to-be-announced CFO.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rohit Singh (Nvest Analytics):** Asked if the INR 800 crore FY27 target will be revised. Management stated they have overachieved their targets historically and expect similar traction in H2.
2. **Abhi Jain (Investor):** Asked about financial controls regarding CARE report deviations and 3-5 year vision. Management stated a trained CFO will be announced in a few months, an internal audit team was appointed, and real growth will show in 18-24 months.
3. **Gaurav Shukla (Finvestors):** Inquired about principal growth drivers. Management noted Pet Care (70-80%), mobility (65-70%), and mass market cosmetics (65-70%) are driving growth from small bases across seven verticals.
4. **Murali (Investor):** Asked about the 26% stake in Vetcare. Management explained it is a 4-decade-old profitable pet retail and B2B institutional supplier operating on a no-burn model.
5. **Harshit Kamdar (Individual Investor):** Asked about the competitive moat against well-funded rivals. Management highlighted that time cannot be bought (15-16 years for licenses), large datasets for diagnostics, and 11 years of research on nutraceuticals.
6. **Keshav Toshniwal (Karnakala Capital):** Asked about Daulcel hormonal products and Wagr. Management stated Daulcel and hormonal products launch in 60-90 days (January 2027), and Wagr now has a 45-people team operating 365 days.

### One-line takeaway
Fredun Pharmaceuticals posted robust Q2 FY27 results with ~84% YoY revenue growth and ~93% PAT growth, driven by rapid scaling across new-age consumer verticals like pet care and mobility.
