# Pharma & Healthcare Products: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian pharmaceutical and healthcare sector delivered a strong operational performance in Q2 FY27, characterized by double-digit volume outperformance in domestic branded formulations, accelerating CDMO/CRDMO contracts driven by global "China Plus One" supply chain diversification, and strong export momentum across complex generics and specialty ingredients. Across the board, managements highlighted robust demand for specialized capabilities such as peptides, oligonucleotides, sterile injectables, and antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).

However, the sector faced pervasive external friction due to ongoing geopolitical conflict in West Asia and Red Sea shipping disruptions. Management after management reported sharp increases in ocean and air freight rates, extended transit timelines (adding 45 to 60 days to trade cycles), and price spikes in crude- and petrochemical-linked solvents and reagents. Companies with strong backward integration, proprietary manufacturing processes, or differentiated product portfolios successfully expanded EBITDA margins, while those facing fixed-price order backlogs or un-passed input inflation saw temporary gross margin compression.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
* **Domestic Branded Formulations:** Strong outperformance in chronic and sub-chronic therapies (cardiovascular, CNS, diabetes, women's health, oncology) relative to the broader Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM). Companies like **Corona Remedies** (Chronic +29%), **Mankind Pharma** (Rx volume growth 6.2%), **Zydus Lifesciences** (India Rx +19%), **Ipca Laboratories** (Chronic +20%), and **Emcure Pharmaceuticals** (India +12%) reported steady market-share gains.
* **CDMO & Contract Manufacturing:** Robust structural demand driven by global outsourcing trends, supply chain diversification, and the US BIOSECURE / COINS Acts. Companies including **Aarti Pharmalabs**, **Anthem Biosciences**, **Akums Drugs**, **Laurus Labs**, **Onesource Specialty Pharma**, **Sai Life Sciences**, **Gland Pharma**, and **Bharat Parenterals (Innoxel)** reported expanding order books, project reloading, and strong pipeline conversion.

### Divergence
* **US Generics & Differentiated Launches:** **Aurobindo Pharma** (US sales +25% YoY to $477M), **Alembic Pharmaceuticals** (US sales +72% YoY), **Gland Pharma** (US +42%), and **Rubicon Research** (+40% revenue growth) posted strong US growth. Conversely, **Lupin** saw US revenue drop 13.4% QoQ due to intense generic competition in Tolvaptan, while **Sun Pharma** noted a US slowdown in *Ilumya* alongside ongoing *lenalidomide* erosion.
* **Domestic Acute Therapies:** While chronic therapies surged, acute portfolios saw mixed results. **Indoco Remedies** suffered from an "absent monsoon" impacting seasonal acute brands (*Febrex Plus*, *ATM*), and **Ipca Laboratories** noted muted acute demand in antimalarials and anti-infectives.
* **Commodity APIs vs Specialty Realizations:** **Solara Active Pharma** reported improved realizations and volumes in Ibuprofen due to competitor exits and market dynamics, whereas **IOL Chemicals** and **SMS Pharmaceuticals** noted Ibuprofen as flat-to-cyclical, capping its portfolio contribution to manage risk. **Neuland Laboratories** experienced quarterly revenue lumpiness due to uneven commercial shipment schedules for key Custom Manufacturing Solutions (CMS) molecules.
* **Consumer Healthcare & Program-Linked Portfolios:** **Mankind Pharma** admitted dissatisfaction with its Consumer Healthcare segment due to slow regional off-take, while **Sudeep Pharma** absorbed a negative top-line impact in its Specialty Ingredients segment due to a temporary pause in the Indian Government's Fortified Rice program.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

### Raw material & solvent inflation
Geopolitical turmoil in West Asia triggered cost increases across raw materials, key starting materials (KSMs), and petrochemical solvents (accounting for 7–12% of raw material costs). **Aarti Drugs**, **Aarti Pharmalabs**, **Alivus Life Sciences**, **Amanta Healthcare**, **Anlon Healthcare**, **Blue Jet Healthcare**, **Concord Biotech**, **Granules India**, **Solara Active Pharma**, and **Strides Pharma Science** all flagged raw material price volatility as a direct headwind to gross margins.

### Freight and logistics delays
Geopolitical shifts extended shipping routes, creating container shortages and elevating freight costs. **Biocon**, **Cipla**, **Indoco Remedies**, **Ipca Laboratories** (Unichem freight costs rose by ₹31.2 crore), **Granules India**, **Onesource Specialty Pharma**, **Poly Medicure**, and **Sudeep Pharma** (absorbed an extra ₹8 crore in ocean freight) reported logistical friction and higher air-freight reliance.

### Margin defense & operational leverage
* **Margin Expansion:** Companies benefiting from operating leverage, backward integration, and higher-margin specialty/CDMO mix delivered significant margin expansion: **Alivus Life Sciences** (EBITDA margin up 880 bps YoY to 41.8%), **Anthem Biosciences** (EBITDA margin 50.6%), **Laurus Labs** (EBITDA margin 34.5%), **Aarti Pharmalabs** (EBITDA up 84% YoY), **Ind-Swift Laboratories** (EBITDA margin expanded 1,256 bps YoY to 18.02%), and **SMS Pharmaceuticals** (EBITDA up 40% YoY).
* **Margin Compression:** Companies with fixed-price contracts or raw material cost pass-through lags experienced margin pressure: **Blue Jet Healthcare** (gross margin down 4% QoQ to 49%), **Concord Biotech** (gross margin contracted ~400 bps YoY), **Neuland Laboratories** (EBITDA margin down to 22.9% vs 30.4% YoY), and **Shilpa Medicare** (gross margin down 700 bps YoY due to API mix shift).

## Capex & capacity

Managements across the sector are executing aggressive capital expenditure programs targeting high-barrier modalities:
* **Peptides & Oligonucleotides:** **Laurus Labs** (commissioned SPPS capacity >16,000 liters; acquired stakes in Galenvs and SI & C Resins), **Aarti Pharmalabs** (setting up peptide/oligo labs by end-FY27), **Neuland Laboratories** (commissioned Peptide Module 1; ~₹250 cr capex), **Divi's Laboratories** (installing multiple 3,000-liter SPPS lines), **Granules India** (₹2,000 cr organic capex planned, including Zurich/Vizag peptide infrastructure), **Bajaj Healthcare** (250 kg/year peptide facility coming in Q2 FY28), and **Shilpa Medicare** (tonnage-scale peptide capacity operational end-FY27).
* **Sterile Injectables & DDCs:** **Gland Pharma** (₹3,000 cr capex plan including Pashamylaram brownfield isolator lines and Vizag SEZ greenfield), **Onesource Specialty Pharma** (commercialized 2nd DDC line; 3rd DDC line qualifying for FY28), **Amanta Healthcare** (commissioned SteriPort Line 3; SVP expansion starting Q1 FY28), and **Sai Parenterals** (Adelaide AUD 53M facility commissioning Jan 2027; Saicriti ₹215 cr injectables facility).
* **CDMO & Large-Scale Infrastructure:** **Anthem Biosciences** (Unit 4 Phase 1 ₹1,200 cr capex intact; 30-acre facility), **Sai Life Sciences** (H1 capex ₹621 cr; FY27 guidance ₹1,100–1,300 cr; 220 KL Bidar expansion ready), **IOL Chemicals** (₹500 cr projects including Ibuprofen expansion to 18,000 MTPA and CDMO facility), **Neuland Laboratories** (acquired 134 acres in Kakinada), **Laxmi Dental** (deploying ₹48.1 cr unutilized IPO funds for land/construction of a 1.25–1.5 lakh sq ft facility).

## What changed since last quarter

### Upgraded guidance & positive tone shifts
* **Alembic Pharmaceuticals:** Raised full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance to 20%–25% (up from mid-teens) and US growth to ~40% YoY following strong Q2 performance (+36% revenue).
* **Emcure Pharmaceuticals:** Upgraded FY27 revenue growth guidance to **>20%** (from mid-teens) and EBITDA margin expansion to the upper end of 75–100 bps.
* **Rubicon Research:** Upgraded full-year operating EBITDA margin guidance to **24%+** (up from 23%+).
* **SMS Pharmaceuticals:** Stated it will exceed its initial FY27 guidance of 15% revenue growth and 20% EBITDA margin.
* **Poly Medicure:** Raised standalone FY27 EBITDA margin guidance to 26%–27.5% (from 25%–27%).
* **Cipla:** Shifted tone from Q1 margin concerns to reporting a 200 bps sequential EBITDA margin expansion (to 18.7%), confirming US exit run-rate target of $1 billion remains on track.
* **Bharat Parenterals:** Marked a commercial profit inflection as subsidiary Innoxel turned into the group's primary EBITDA driver (>60% of EBITDA).
* **Symbiotec Pharmalab:** Upgraded execution status by officially signing its US Dual-Chamber Vial (DCV) long-term agreement, recognizing ₹53 crore in milestone revenue, and filing its first ANDA.

### Guidance cuts & operational delays
* **Sigachi Industries:** Revised FY27 revenue guidance downward from ~₹650 crore to ~₹600 crore due to a slight deferment in its Dahej 12,000 MTPA MCC project commissioning (pushed to Q2 FY28).
* **Cohance Lifesciences:** Reported operational recovery in Pharma CDMO (+34.5% QoQ) but introduced a new risk regarding a closure notice received at its non-GMP Suryapet facility.
* **Sai Life Sciences:** Recalibrated commissioning of its 225 KL Bidar capacity from Q4 FY27 to Q3 FY28 to prioritize customer-specific projects.
