# Pharma & Healthcare Products: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Pharma & Healthcare Products sector was characterized by record revenues and robust year-on-year growth across multiple players, driven heavily by CDMO/CRDMO momentum, complex generics, and strong domestic chronic portfolios. Companies like Aarti Pharmalabs, Granules India, and Senores Pharmaceuticals reported highest-ever quarterly revenues or historic milestones. However, growth was managed against headwinds including geopolitical tensions in West Asia, which pressured freight, solvent, and raw material costs.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Agreement:** Managements across the board report exceptionally strong underlying demand and rising RFPs/RFQs in the CDMO and CRDMO segments (highlighted by Aarti Pharmalabs, Alivus Life Sciences, Anthem Biosciences, Akums Drugs, and Granules India). Domestic chronic therapies are also outperforming the broader Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM), noted by Corona Remedies and Cipla.
*   **Divergence:** Views on the U.S. market diverge. Advanced Enzyme Technologies reported "dullness" over the past two quarters while anticipating a year-end turnaround, whereas Cipla is aggressively ramping up toward its $1 billion U.S. exit run rate with new launches (Ventolin, Advair), and Senores and Granules continue to expand their U.S. ANDA pipelines.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreement:** Geopolitical turmoil and conflicts in West Asia have elevated input costs, key starting materials (KSMs), solvents, and logistics/freight charges. Companies like Aarti Pharmalabs, Bajaj Healthcare, and Granules India noted margin pressures or inventory buffering as a result. 
*   **Divergence:** While companies with high operating leverage and complex product mixes (such as Anthem Biosciences at 50.6% EBITDA margin, Alivus at 41.8%, and Advanced Enzyme at 34%) successfully expanded or maintained robust margins—often by passing costs or shifting sourcing (Anthem reduced China procurement to 20-21%)—others faced near-term hits. Evonile Pharma reported operating losses due to transition costs, and Akums saw domestic branded margins impacted by heavy field force expansion.

## Capex & capacity
Managements across the sector are aggressively deploying capital for complex manufacturing, biologics, and specialized facilities:
*   **Aarti Pharmalabs:** FY27 capex guided at INR350–400 crores, focusing on Atali and Xanthine expansions.
*   **Anthem Biosciences:** Maintaining its INR1,200 crore capex guidance for Unit 4 Phase 1.
*   **Granules India:** Planning INR2,000 crores in organic capex over 3–4 years for peptide CDMO and U.S. platforms.
*   **Alivus Life Sciences:** Solapur facility Phase 1 coming online in Q3 FY27.
*   **Bajaj Healthcare:** Investing Rs 65–80 crore in FY27, targeting peptide and oncology plants by Q2 FY28.
*   **Akums Drugs & Senores Pharmaceuticals:** Scaling up new facilities like Baddi and the acquired Apnar plant.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Bajaj Healthcare:** Shifted strategic focus from domestic API reliance to formulation and export momentum, alongside securing NCLT approval for Genrx and launching Cenobamate.
*   **Cipla:** Tone shifted from Q1 "margin pressure" to Q2 "sequential improvement," while reaffirming its EBITDA margin guidance (18.5%–20%) and $1 billion U.S. exit run rate.
*   **Evonile Pharma:** Absorbing transition-related revenue contraction and one-off costs as it structurally shifts from a single-customer model to an independent multi-therapy player.

## Company one-liners
*   **Aarti Pharmalabs:** Aarti Pharmalabs delivered a record Q2 FY27 driven by 47% revenue growth and an 84% surge in EBITDA, supported by scaling CDMO projects, recovery in steroid APIs, and ongoing capacity capitalization.
*   **Advanced Enzyme Technologies:** Advanced Enzyme delivered a strong Q2 with 16% YoY revenue growth and expanded margins to 34%, supported by broad-based segment performance and operational efficiencies.
*   **Akums Drugs:** Akums delivered strong Q2 results led by robust CDMO volume growth and is strategically positioning for future expansion via the Baddi facility, European market entry, and the Zambia JV.
*   **Alivus Life Sciences:** Alivus Life Sciences delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 16% revenue growth and strong margin expansion, backed by traction in non-GPL and CDMO portfolios, prompting management to reiterate its 10-12% revenue growth and 35-36% EBITDA margin guidance for the year.
*   **Anthem Biosciences:** Anthem Biosciences delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with a consolidated revenue of INR 594 crore and an EBITDA margin of 50.6%, supported by strong execution in complex CRDMO programs and margin optimization.
*   **Bajaj Healthcare:** Bajaj Healthcare is aggressively scaling its specialty portfolio (Peptides, Oncology, Formulations) and strengthening its leadership team, with management targeting Rs 900-1,000 crore revenue by FY29.
*   **Cipla:** Cipla delivered a strong quarter with margin expansion and record India revenue, while management remains confident in achieving its $1 billion US exit run rate through supply-led ramp-ups and upcoming pipeline launches.
*   **Corona Remedies:** Corona Remedies reported a robust Q2 FY27 with 22% revenue growth and 22.9% growth in its India business, comfortably outperforming the broader pharmaceutical market through strong chronic segment traction and core brand execution.
*   **Evonile Pharma:** Evonile Pharma is executing a foundational transition from a single-customer model to an independent, multi-therapy direct-to-market player, absorbing near-term one-off transition costs while targeting above-market organic growth and debt reduction by FY28.
*   **Granules India:** Granules India delivered record Q2 revenue of INR15,600 million and 39% PAT growth, backed by strong complex generic demand and a transition to a net cash balance sheet, even as it addresses 11 new U.S. FDA observations at Gagillapur and West Asia-driven freight inflation.
*   **Senores Pharmaceuticals:** Senores Pharmaceuticals delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA in Q2 FY27, backed by robust ANDA approvals and upcoming commercialization from subsidiaries Amerisyn and Zoraya, keeping full-year guidance on track.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Regulatory & Compliance Resolutions:** Progress on responses and remediation for U.S. FDA observations at Granules India (Gagillapur facility) and Cipla (Pithampur facility).
*   **CDMO/CRDMO Execution:** Ramp-up of new capacity and execution of late-stage pipelines at Aarti Pharmalabs, Anthem Biosciences, Alivus Life Sciences, and Granules India.
*   **New Plant Commercializations:** Operationalization and initial revenue generation from upcoming facilities, including Bajaj Healthcare's peptide/oncology plants and Akums' Baddi facility.
*   **Geopolitical & Supply Chain Pressures:** Whether West Asia-driven freight and raw material inflation continues to impact gross margins across the sector.
