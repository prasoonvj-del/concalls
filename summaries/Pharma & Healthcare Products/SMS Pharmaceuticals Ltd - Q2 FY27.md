# SMS Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance, margin expansion, and upward revision of FY27 guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 316 crore (+30% YoY, +53% QoQ).
*   **Gross Profit:** Rs 111 crore (+41% YoY); Gross Margin: 35%.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 68 crore (+40% YoY); EBITDA Margin: 21% (+145 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 39.6 crore (+57% YoY); PAT Margin: ~13%.
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 523 crore (+19% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 109 crore (+24% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 59.8 crore (+36% YoY).

### Highlights
*   Growth driven by higher volumes, improved price realization, and operating leverage.
*   High-value APIs increased to 49% of H1 revenue (vs 47% in FY26).
*   Therapeutic growth: ARV (+47%), Anti-inflammatory (+25%), Anti-migraine (+32%), Anti-epileptic (+44%), Anti-anginal (+45%), Anti-ED (+52%).
*   R&D: 6 filings completed in H1 + October (target 10 for FY27).
*   New molecules: 6-8 expected to start commercial production in FY28.
*   Peptides: Established SMS Peptides Pvt Ltd; R&D facility operational, pilot production targeted for Q4 FY27, first CRO revenue expected Q2 FY28.
*   CDMO: Established SMS coLab Pvt Ltd as a dedicated platform.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to exceed initial FY27 guidance of 15% revenue growth and 20% EBITDA margin.
*   Targeting EBITDA margins of 22-24% for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Ibuprofen: Currently operating at 90-95% utilization; management expects to reach 9,600 tonnes/year capacity in the coming quarters.
*   Raw material prices: Pressure expected to persist for the next 1-2 quarters.
*   Ibuprofen is viewed as a cyclical product; management aims to keep its revenue contribution below 35% to reduce single-product dependency.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Backward integration cited as a structural cost advantage rather than a temporary benefit.
*   Operating leverage contributed to EBITDA margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Total Capex program: Rs 280 crore (Rs 160 crore deployed, Rs 120 crore remaining for FY27).
*   Ibuprofen capacity: Currently 400-500 tonnes/month, expanding to 9,600 tonnes/year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Peak debt by year-end: ~Rs 400 crore (combined long-term and short-term).
*   Working capital cycle: 120-150 days.
*   Cash flow conversion: ~95% in H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Cyclicality of commodity products (Ibuprofen).
*   Ongoing pressure on raw material prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Peptide Strategy:** Management clarified they are developing generic peptides in-house (pilot Q4 FY27) and exploring CDMO opportunities, aiming for meaningful revenue in 2-3 years.
*   **Ibuprofen Utilization:** Management confirmed 90-95% utilization and stated they have sufficient customer orders to support the capacity expansion to 9,600 tonnes/year.
*   **Debt Levels:** Management guided for peak debt of Rs 400 crore by year-end, accounting for both capex and working capital needs.
*   **Asset Turnover:** Management clarified the current asset turnover (excluding CWIP) is 1.72x, with a target of 1.75x.

### One-line takeaway
Strong volume growth and backward integration are driving margin expansion, with management raising FY27 guidance and investing in new growth engines like peptides and CDMO.
