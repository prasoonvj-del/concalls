# Jubilant Pharmova Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 21% YoY, but EBITDA margins declined YoY due to unavailability of high-margin SPECT radiopharma products, negligible third-party revenues, and higher operating/remediation costs at the CMO Montreal facility.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY2027 Revenue:** Rs 2,370 crore, up 21% YoY.
- **Q2 FY2027 EBITDA:** Rs 255 crore.
- **Q2 FY2027 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 31 crore.
- **H1 FY2027 Revenue:** Rs 4,599 crore, up 19% YoY.
- **H1 FY2027 EBITDA:** Rs 523 crore.
- **H1 FY2027 PAT:** Rs 87 crore.
- **Ruby-Fill Capex:** CAD 94.5 million ($67 million), with CAD 23.8 million support from the Government of Canada.
- **Spokane Line 3 Quarterly Revenue:** Approximately $20 million in Q2.
- **Montreal H1 Losses:** Approximately Rs 157 crore.
- **Net Debt Guidance (End of FY2027):** Closer to Rs 4,000 crore.
- **FY2027 Total Capex Guidance:** Rs 1,800 to Rs 1,900 crore.
- **FY2028 Capex Guidance:** Substantially reduced to around Rs 1,000 crore.

### Highlights
- **Radiopharma:** Announced a CAD 94.5 million capex to double Ruby-Fill manufacturing capacity. Signed a distribution contract for a leading PET product for new PET radiopharmacies. Independent blinded review for MIBG is on track, with NDA filing expected in Q4 FY2027 or early next quarter.
- **CDMO Sterile Injectables:** Spokane facility received VAI (Voluntary Action Indicated) status. Signed an MSA with a large pharma customer for a major global oncology product. 13 tech transfer programs currently ongoing on Line 3. First product approval on Line 3 secured without a pre-approval inspection.
- **Proprietary Novel Drugs:** Presented clinical data for 12 patients for JBI-802 at the SOHO annual meet. Initial data presentation scheduled for the American Society of Hematology conference in December 2026.
- **Financing:** Successfully raised non-convertible debentures of Rs 800 crore.
- *Note vs Record:* Our record noted Q2 net profit down 74% YoY to Rs 31 crore due to operational headwinds, which management confirmed was driven by Montreal facility losses, remediation costs, and lack of high-margin SPECT supplies.

### Guidance & outlook
- **H2 FY2027:** Management expects revenue growth momentum to continue and margins to improve QoQ, with H2 average margins in the mid-teens.
- **Long-term Debt:** Management maintains its vision of achieving net-debt-zero by the end of FY2030, supported by lower capex cycles starting FY2028 and scaling cash flows.
- **Generics Guidance:** Reiterated long-term guidance of doubling revenue and achieving 15% to 17% EBITDA margins.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Sterile Injectables:** Strong demand for US isolator capacity driven by geopolitical uncertainties and market consolidation. Customers are prioritizing high-quality standards.
- **Drug Discovery:** RFP submissions and win rates are better than the industry; management expects a ramp-up starting H2 and significantly in FY2028.
- **API Business:** Pricing pressures continue due to geopolitical scenarios, though solvent prices show signs of improvement.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Consolidated Margins:** Declined YoY in Q2 and H1 due to unavailability of high-margin SPECT radiopharma products from Montreal, negligible third-party revenues, and incremental remediation and opex costs at CMO Montreal.
- **Montreal Losses:** Cumulative losses to date include ~Rs 200 crore prior to H1, plus Rs 157 crore lost in H1 FY2027. Remediation costs are expected to decrease in H2 as the site reaches steadier state.
- **Line 3 Margins:** Tech transfer phases incur slightly higher labor and project management costs, resulting in lower initial margins, which will improve as commercial production ramps up in FY2028/FY2029.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Ruby-Fill Expansion:** To double capacity; expected to come online in the back half of FY2029, targeting peak utilization ROCE of over 20%.
- **PET Network:** Cyclotrons deployed at 5 of the 6 new sites; first sites expected online in Q3 FY2028.
- **Spokane Line 4:** Build-out complete, major qualification completed in Q2, with tech transfers on track for Q4 FY2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Debt:** Expected to close FY2027 near Rs 4,000 crore (Debt-to-EBITDA at 2.2x); projected not to increase in FY2028 due to lower capex.
- **Fundraising:** Raised Rs 800 crore via NCDs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued pricing pressure in the API segment due to geopolitical scenarios.
- Operational disruptions and remediation expenses at the CMO Montreal facility impacting high-margin SPECT product supplies.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shrikant Akolkar (Nuvama) asked about the drivers for mid-teens margin recovery in H2.** Management (Ashish Mukkirwar) stated recovery will be driven by new generics launches, stabilization of Montreal SPECT supplies, and seasonally higher production volumes.
- **Vishal Manchanda (Nuvama) asked about the total cash burn and losses incurred at Montreal.** Management quantified H1 losses at Rs 157 crore and prior losses at ~Rs 200 crore, adding that remediation expenses are expected to decline in H2.
- **Vishal Manchanda inquired about Line 3 peak utilization and margins.** Management (Chris Preti / Ashish Mukkirwar) noted current quarterly revenue is ~$20 million (close to peak guidance of $80-$90 million); tech transfer margins are lower, but commercial production in FY2028/FY2029 will drive material margin expansion.
- **Nagraj (Banyan Capital) asked about the ROI and timeline for Ruby-Fill capex.** Management (Harsher Singh) stated the CAD 94.5 million project will come online in the back half of FY2029 and is expected to yield an ROCE of >20% at peak utilization.
- **Shashank Rastogi asked if the 2030 net-debt-zero target is maintained.** Management confirmed the target is maintained, citing peak capex ending in FY2027 and strong cash generation expected in FY2029 and FY2030.

### One-line takeaway
Jubilant Pharmova delivered 21% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY2027, but profitability (PAT down to Rs 31 crore) remained compressed due to remediation costs and lost SPECT supplies at its Montreal facility, though management expects margin normalization in H2 and substantial capex reduction starting FY2028.
