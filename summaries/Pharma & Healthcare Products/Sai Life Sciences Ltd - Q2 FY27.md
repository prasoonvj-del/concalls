# Sai Life Sciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Management reports strong growth in the integrated CRDMO model and robust pipeline execution despite minor capacity recalibrations.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue Growth:** 23% YoY (CDMO +20%, CRO +28%).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue Growth:** 17% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 28% (for Q2 and H1).
*   **Capex (H1 FY27):** Rs 621 crore (Rs 358 crore in Q2).
*   **Capex Guidance (FY27):** Rs 1,100 crore–Rs 1,300 crore.
*   **Pipeline:** 14 late-phase programs, 34 commercial molecules.
*   **Headcount:** ~4,400.

### Highlights
*   **Integrated CRDMO Model:** Successfully converted another large pharma relationship into a strategic end-to-end engagement; now working with 19 large pharma clients.
*   **Capacity Status:** Expanded small molecule manufacturing capacity (220 KL) at Bidar is ready; final quality trials are underway.
*   **Capacity Recalibration:** Commissioning of 225 KL capacity moved from Q4 FY27 to Q3 FY28 to prioritize other customer-specific projects.
*   **New Infrastructure:** Design work for the new Telangana manufacturing site is underway; groundbreaking expected in Q4 FY27.
*   **CRO Momentum:** CRO business continues to grow; management noted early green shoots in biotech funding and enquiries.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1.
*   Long-term growth and EBITDA projections remain intact.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Tailwinds:** Supply chain diversification (China+1, US COINS Act, China export controls) continues to drive interest from global innovators.
*   **AI Impact:** Management does not expect AI to reduce billable hours; believes it will lead to more molecules being tested and is a net positive for process optimization.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Pressures:** Ongoing Middle East situation has increased costs for certain solvents and chemicals.
*   **Mitigation:** Proactive supply chain management and cost recovery mechanisms with customers are in place to manage margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Management maintains a strong capex cycle for the next couple of years.
*   Funding strategy: Will utilize leverage/debt if needed; management stated they will not "starve the business of capex."

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Cash reserves: ~Rs 100 crore.
*   Leverage: Described as "fairly comfortable."

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Potential impact of Middle East instability on raw material costs.
*   **Project Prioritization:** Delay in 225 KL capacity commissioning (moved to Q3 FY28).
*   **Customer Concentration:** Management is monitoring concentration but noted that integrated engagements (multiple services/molecules per client) reduce product-specific risk.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Integrated Model:** Management clarified that "integrated" refers to offering services across the entire drug discovery life cycle (discovery to commercial), not a single program.
*   **On Capacity Commitment:** Management clarified they do not have "complete commitment" on plants; they build capacity based on projections and potential to ensure readiness for customer needs.
*   **On AI:** Management reiterated that AI will not reduce budgets; it will likely increase the number of molecules in the clinic, benefiting the development side.
*   **On Capex Funding:** Management confirmed they will use a mix of internal accruals and debt to fund expansion, prioritizing growth over maintaining low leverage.

### One-line takeaway
Sai Life Sciences is successfully scaling its integrated CRDMO model with large pharma, maintaining strong margins despite supply chain cost pressures and recalibrated capacity timelines.
