# Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong domestic volume growth and gross margin expansion were offset by US generic revenue decline from lenalidomide erosion and execution slowdown in Ilumya.

### Key numbers
- **Sales:** Rs 16,100 crore (INR 1,61,000 million), up 11.8% YoY.
- **Gross Margin:** 80.8% (up YoY due to product mix and currency tailwinds).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 4,743.9 crore (INR 47,439 million), up 4.8% YoY; EBITDA Margin at 29.3%.
- **Reported Net Profit:** Rs 1,988.5 crore (INR 19,885 million).
- **Adjusted Net Profit:** Rs 3,162.0 crore (INR 31,620 million).
- **Forex Gain:** Rs 78.5 crore (INR 785 million).
- **Effective Tax Rate:** 28.3% (versus 24.7% in Q2 FY26).
- **EPS:** Rs 8.3 per share.
- **R&D Spend:** Rs 778.4 crore (INR 7,784 million), representing 4.8% of sales (Innovative R&D formed 32.4% of total R&D spend).

### Highlights
- India formulation sales reached Rs 5,552.3 crore (INR 55,523 million), growing 17.3% YoY and accounting for 34.5% of total sales.
- Sun Pharma ranked #1 with an 8.6% market share in the Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM) as of Pharmarack MAT September 2026 (up from 8.3%).
- Global Innovative Medicines sales reached USD 356 million, up 6.9% YoY (21.1% of total sales).
- US sales declined 8.2% YoY to USD 455 million due to lenalidomide erosion (up 6.5% QoQ), accounting for 27% of total sales; 5 generic products were launched in the US during Q2.
- Emerging Markets formulations grew 7.6% YoY to USD 350 million (7.4% growth in constant currency); Rest of World (ROW) formulations reached USD 241 million.
- Ilumya received US approval for active psoriatic arthritis; soft launch in November with full launch following.
- Partnership signed with LIB Therapeutics for Lerodalcibep (PCSK9 inhibitor) expanding cardiology presence outside US and China.
- Signed MFN pricing agreement with US government/state Medicaid programs, delaying Section 232 tariffs on innovative products for over 2 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained full-year revenue growth guidance of high single-digits.
- Management expects full-year R&D spend to catch up to the guided 6% to 7% of sales range as clinical trial recruitment ramps up in H2 FY27.
- Management anticipates US Ilumya sales to return to typical growth patterns over the next few quarters.
- Organon acquisition integration remains on track for completion in Q4 FY27 (early 2027).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Sun Pharma's India business volume growth stood at 6.7% for the quarter, significantly outperforming IPM volume growth of 1.8%.
- Generic semaglutide commercialized in India, South Africa, and Brazil (via local partner).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins improved to 80.8% due to favorable product mix and currency benefits.
- EBITDA growth outpaced revenue growth when adjusted for lenalidomide sales present in Q2 FY26.
- Exceptional items in Q2 FY27 include litigation settlement provisions and Organon acquisition transaction expenses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sun Pharma voluntarily recalled 11 ophthalmic products and initiated a temporary voluntary cessation of manufacturing at its Assam plant for remedial actions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated balance sheet held a net cash position of USD 3.3 billion.
- Issued debut INR Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) at 8.2% to refinance existing domestic bank borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Exclusivity erosion in generic lenalidomide in the US market.
- Slowdown in US Ilumya sales growth due to market and execution factors.
- Tax rate increased to 28.3% compared to 24.7% in Q2 FY26.
- Acquisition-related exceptional transaction costs will continue until transaction closing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie)** asked for details on the US Ilumya slowdown and recovery timeline.  
  **A:** Richard Ascroft stated slowdown was due to execution and market factors; interventions and the psoriatic arthritis launch will return it to typical growth over the next few quarters.
- **Q: Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie)** queried whether revenue guidance would be updated given double-digit growth in H1 FY27.  
  **A:** Kirti Ganorkar confirmed management is maintaining the high single-digit revenue growth guidance given at the start of the year.
- **Q: Neha Manpuria (BofA)** asked if R&D spend will meet the 6% to 7% target given the lower spend in Q2.  
  **A:** Dilip Shanghvi clarified that R&D spending will ramp up significantly in H2 as clinical trials recruit subjects, expecting to hit full-year guidance.
- **Q: Vishal Manchanda (Systematix)** asked regarding media reports on production stoppage at the Assam plant.  
  **A:** Kirti Ganorkar confirmed Sun Pharma voluntarily recalled 11 ophthalmic products and temporarily halted manufacturing at the Assam plant to execute remedial measures while keeping Indian regulators informed.
- **Q: Ashutosh Kumar Jha (Balyasny)** asked about debt structuring and borrowing costs for the Organon acquisition versus the recent NCD issuance.  
  **A:** Jayashree Satagopan clarified that Organon financing should be viewed on Organon's weighted average cost of debt, while recent domestic NCDs were routine refinancing of rupee borrowings.

### One-line takeaway
Sun Pharma's strong volume-led India growth and expanding gross margins provide resilience as US specialty execution resets and the Organon transaction tracks toward Q4 FY27 closure.
