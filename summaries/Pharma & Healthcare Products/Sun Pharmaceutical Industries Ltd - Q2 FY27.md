# Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong growth in India formulations and stable underlying performance offset by a slowdown in US Ilumya and lenalidomide erosion.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 1,61,000 million (up 11.8% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 80.8%
- **EBITDA:** INR 47,439 million (up 4.8% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 29.3%
- **Reported Net Profit:** INR 19,885 million
- **Adjusted Net Profit:** INR 31,620 million
- **EPS:** INR 8.3 per share
- **Effective Tax Rate:** 28.3%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 3,12,836 million (up 11% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 91,616 million (up 3.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Adjusted Net Profit:** INR 62,515 million
- **Global Innovative Medicines Sales:** USD 356 million (up 6.9% YoY; 21.1% of total sales)
- **India Formulations Sales:** INR 55,523 million (up 17.3% YoY; 34.5% of total sales)
- **US Business Sales:** USD 455 million (down 8.2% YoY; up 6.5% QoQ; 27% of total sales)
- **Emerging Markets Formulation Revenue:** USD 350 million (up 7.6% YoY; constant currency up 7.4%)
- **Rest of World Formulation Revenue:** USD 241 million
- **R&D Investment:** INR 7,784 million (4.8% of sales); Innovative R&D was 32.4% of total R&D spend.
- **Net Cash:** USD 3.3 billion at the consolidated level.

### Highlights
- Ilumya approved in the US for treating adults with active psoriatic arthritis (soft launch in November, full launch in following months).
- Partnered with LIB Therapeutics for lerodalcibep (PCSK9 inhibitor) outside the US and China.
- Unloxcyt elevated to a Category 2A preferred recommendation in NCCN guidelines for advanced CSCC; long-term European data showed durable response and tolerability.
- Sun Pharma signed an agreement with the US government to extend Most Favored Nation pricing to state Medicaid programs, delaying Section 232 tariffs on innovative products for over 2 years and exempting the company from GLOBE and GUARD Medicare models.
- Sun Pharma is ranked number 1 in the Indian pharmaceutical market with an 8.6% market share (Pharmarack MAT Sept '26); volume growth in India was 6.7% vs. IPM growth of 1.8%.
- Organon acquisition remains on track to close by Q4 of FY27 (early 2027); regulatory approvals secured in a majority of regions.
- Voluntarily recalled 11 ophthalmology products and initiated temporary cessation of manufacturing at the Assam plant, informing the Indian regulator.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained high single-digit top-line growth guidance for FY27.
- Management expects R&D spend to catch up and ramp up significantly in the second half, meeting full-year guidance of 6% to 7%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- US growth was impacted by lenalidomide erosion, though it grew 6.5% QoQ driven by 5 new generic product launches.
- Ilumya experienced a US slowdown driven by a mix of execution and market factors, but management expects a recovery over the next few quarters, aided by the psoriatic arthritis indication.
- India growth is robust, driven by volume expansions and new product launches across all 12 major represented therapy areas.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded YoY due to a better product mix and favorable currency tailwinds.
- EBITDA margin was lower YoY due to lenalidomide sales base effect in Q2 FY26.
- Forex gain for the quarter was INR 785 million.
- Exceptional items included litigation settlement provisions and Organon-related acquisition costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Temporary cessation of manufacturing activities at the Assam plant for 11 ophthalmology products.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net cash stands at USD 3.3 billion.
- Debut NCD issue successfully subscribed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slowdown in US Ilumya sales.
- Erosion in US business due to lenalidomide.
- Voluntary recall and temporary cessation of manufacturing at the Assam ophthalmology plant.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie):** Asked about the drivers behind the US Ilumya slowdown and recovery timeline. *Management cited execution and market factors, noting interventions are in place and recovery is expected over the next few quarters.*
- **Neha Manpuria (BofA):** Inquired about contribution timelines for Leqselvi and Unloxcyt. *Management highlighted NCCN guideline updates and positive long-term data as catalysts already showing month-over-month share improvements.*
- **Damayanti Kerai (HSBC):** Asked about therapy expansion and marketing spend for the newly licensed PCSK9 inhibitor. *Management stated they will leverage existing cardiology field force in EM and add capabilities where needed within overall budget.*
- **Vishal Manchanda (Systematix):** Asked about media reports on the Assam plant and the Organon deal's borrowing cost. *Management confirmed a voluntary recall and temporary cessation of 11 ophthalmic products at Assam; stated Organon borrowing cost will align with Organon's weighted average cost of debt.*
- **Tara Ganatra (UTI India):** Asked about sustainability of India's 6% volume growth. *Management noted that growth is an outcome of sustained scientific promotion, field force expansion, and new launches, though specific quarter predictability is difficult.*

### One-line takeaway
Sun Pharma reported an 11.8% YoY revenue increase to INR 1,61,000 million and strong India volume growth, though offset by a US Ilumya slowdown and lenalidomide erosion, while staying on track for the Organon acquisition in Q4 FY27.
