# Mankind Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and margin expansion driven by chronic/specialty portfolio and volume outperformance, despite weakness in consumer healthcare.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 4,338 crore (+17.3% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 1,175 crore (+27.2% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 27.1% (+210 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 716 crore (+37.7% YoY).
*   **Domestic Revenue (ex-consumer):** Rs 3,428 crore (+15.9% YoY).
*   **International Revenue:** Rs 680 crore (+33% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Domestic Performance:** Outperformed the Indian Pharma Market (IPM) with 15.7% secondary sales growth (1.1x outperformance).
*   **Volume Growth:** Achieved 6.2% volume growth in Q2, continuing a trend of improvement from 0.5% in FY25.
*   **Chronic Focus:** Chronic share (ex-BSV) expanded 80 bps YoY to 37.4%; management aims to reach 50% in 4-5 years.
*   **BSV Portfolio:** Continued 20%+ growth momentum for the second consecutive quarter.
*   **Consumer Healthcare:** Revenue of Rs 229 crore; impacted by industry-wide slower offtake and regional weakness in East and South. E-commerce channel grew 55% YoY, contributing 18% of segment sales.
*   **Strategic Moves:** Acquired Armed Cell Therapeutics (CAR-T cell therapy platform).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management maintained EBITDA margin guidance of 25.5-26.5% for FY27.
*   **R&D:** Expects R&D spend to be at the upper end of 3% of revenue for FY27 (including impact of ACTx).
*   **International:** Management expects double-digit growth in international business for FY27.
*   **Growth:** Management remains confident in outperforming the IPM in the foreseeable future.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   IPM growth (excluding tirzepatide) was 14.2%.
*   Hospital segment revenue grew 23% YoY.
*   Anti-infective segment showing progressive recovery (5.4% growth in Q2 vs 3.6% in Q1).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** 72.2% (+90 bps YoY), driven by price increases, favorable sales mix, and base effect.
*   **EBITDA Margin Drivers:** 90 bps from gross margin expansion, 40 bps from lower R&D, and 80 bps from operating leverage.
*   **Finance Cost:** Declined to Rs 105 crore (vs Rs 110 crore in Q1 FY27).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Rs 214 crore spent in H1 FY27.
*   **Capex Guidance:** 6-7% of revenue for FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Debt:** Reduced to Rs 2,639 crore.
*   **Leverage:** Net debt to adjusted EBITDA ratio at 0.6x (trailing 12-month).
*   **Working Capital:** Net operating working capital increased to 51 days (vs 46 days YoY).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Consumer Healthcare:** Management admitted dissatisfaction with current performance; expects recovery in 3-6 months.
*   **Tax Litigation:** Acknowledged Section 37(1) disallowance issue, but noted a favorable order from the Commissioner of Appeals reducing exposure by Rs 300 crore.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Sustainability of India Rx growth?* Management stated they are confident in outperforming the IPM, citing historical legacy and improved volume growth trends.
*   *Q: Consumer business recovery?* Management admitted unhappiness with performance; expects recovery in 3-6 months/next year.
*   *Q: ACTx funding/strategy?* Management noted current commitment (48 Million Euros) is sufficient until FY28; open to partnerships or further funding based on clinical results.
*   *Q: Margin sustainability?* Management maintained 25.5-26.5% guidance despite Q2 strength; expects operating leverage to play a positive role.

### One-line takeaway
Mankind Pharma delivered strong Q2 results with 17% revenue growth and margin expansion, supported by chronic segment strength and volume outperformance, while management works to turnaround the underperforming consumer healthcare business.
