# Zydus Lifesciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit growth across all business segments and continued market outperformance.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 79.1 billion (Rs 7,910 crore), up 29% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 18.9 billion (Rs 1,890 crore).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 24% (higher by 150 bps adjusted for Saroglitazar US launch investments).
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 9.3 billion (Rs 930 crore).
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 0.62x (Net debt reduced to Rs 5,200 crore from Rs 5,900 crore QoQ).
- **India Branded Formulations:** Revenues grew 19% YoY (H1 FY27 up 20% YoY); secondary sales grew 20% YoY.
- **International Formulations:** Rs 10 billion, up 33% YoY.
- **North America Formulations:** Rs 33.1 billion ($347-350 million), up 21% YoY and 7% QoQ. Specialty accounted for 16% of US revenue (up ~550 bps QoQ).
- **Consumer Wellness:** Rs 11.2 billion, up 76% YoY (Domestic up 29% YoY; skin & hair care up 37%, food & nutrition up 25%).
- **Medical Devices:** Rs 2.2 billion.
- **R&D Expenses:** ~8% to 8.5% of revenues (~Rs 650 crore in Q2).

### Highlights
- Branded business revenue contribution expanded to ~57% (up 150 bps QoQ), targeting two-thirds of total revenue over the medium term.
- Chronic and sub-chronic portfolio reached 56.9% of India branded formulations (up 450 bps over 4 years).
- Filed 4 ANDAs, received 12 approvals, and launched 5 products in North America during Q2; launched generic Riociguat (Adempas) in October 2026.
- Partner Formycon's BLA for Pembrolizumab (Keytruda biosimilar) was accepted for review by USFDA—the first publicly announced Keytruda biosimilar filing accepted in the US.
- Licensing deal executed via Sentynl Therapeutics with Mereo Biopharma for Alvelestat (rare genetic respiratory disorder asset).
- *Continuation of past record:* Zydus sustained its strong Q2 FY27 top-line performance, matching the reported revenue of Rs 79.1 billion (up 29.2% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Maintained guidance at 24%+ for the full year.
- **US Business Growth:** Single-digit constant currency growth expected for FY27 on a high FY26 base.
- **India IPM Outperformance:** Expected to outgrow IPM by 200–400 bps consistently (assuming IPM grows 10–11%).
- **Saroglitazar (PBC) US Launch:** Target launch in March 2027 (Goal date: November 2026). Expecting 10–12% patient share by FY30 and 20% peak share by FY33–34, with meaningful revenue kicking in from FY29/FY30.
- **Saroglitazar Commercial Spends:** Management expects US commercialization spend to run at ~$100 million annually.
- **Vaccine Business:** Target to double revenue from current annualized run-rate of Rs 450 crore over the next 3–4 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Urban recovery and steady rural off-take are driving positive momentum in Consumer Wellness.
- High unmet demand in US transdermal patch market, where market demand currently exceeds industry supply by ~50%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Upfront investments for the Saroglitazar US launch weighed on operating margins by ~150 bps during the quarter.
- Quarter-on-quarter employee expenses degrew marginally (ex-R&D), but grew YoY due to acquisitions completed over the past year.
- Other expenses (ex-R&D and Saroglitazar commercial spend) are expected to run in the range of Rs 2,000–2,050 crore per quarter.
- Amortization increased due to Assertio acquisition and will remain at current run-rate levels until September 2027.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Transdermal patch manufacturing facilities are operating at peak capacity; plans are underway to double transdermal capacity in about a year.
- Uptick in capex cycle underway to build capabilities for 5-year growth visibility.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt decreased by Rs 700 crore QoQ to Rs 5,200 crore.
- Net debt to EBITDA stands at 0.62x. Management is comfortable maintaining leverage up to 1.0x Net Debt/EBITDA.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- USFDA inspections concluded with 5 observations at the Moraiya facility and 1 observation at SEZ-II, Ahmedabad (management responded; received transdermal approval post-inspection at Moraiya).
- Competitive pricing/erosion impact from generic Mirabegron competition in the US.
- MASH indication Phase III clinical trial is currently paused for Saroglitazar due to FDA requirements of biopsy-led trial execution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Kunal Dhamesha / Neha Manpuria):** What is the US commercial spend quantum and launch timeline for Saroglitazar?  
  *A (Management):* US commercial spend will run at ~$100 million annually. Labeling queries are being answered, with a goal date in November 2026 and an expected commercial launch in March 2027.
- **Q (Saion Mukherjee):** What patient share and timelines are expected for Saroglitazar in PBC?  
  *A (Management):* Expecting 10–12% patient share by FY30, scaling to a peak patient share of 20% by FY33–34; meaningful revenue contribution will begin in FY29/FY30.
- **Q (Niharika Agarwal):** What are the launch timelines and split for Keytruda biosimilar with Formycon?  
  *A (Management):* BLA is under review, but launch timelines depend on patent litigation. Financials are structured on a profit-sharing model.
- **Q (Nitin Agarwal):** What is the status of transdermal patch opportunities and capacity expansion?  
  *A (Management):* Operating at 100% capacity while market demand is 50% higher than available supply. Additional capacity creation will take about a year to double capability.
- **Q (Saion Mukherjee):** What is management's appetite for large inorganic acquisitions going forward?  
  *A (Management):* Management prefers bolt-on acquisitions and portfolio/asset licensing deals in specialty/differentiated spaces over large-ticket platform buyouts.

### One-line takeaway
Zydus Lifesciences delivered 29% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 behind strong domestic execution and resilient US base sales, while maintaining a 24%+ EBITDA margin guidance despite spending ~$100 million annually to commercialize Saroglitazar in the US.
