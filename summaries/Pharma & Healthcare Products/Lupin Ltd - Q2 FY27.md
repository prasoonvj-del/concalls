# Lupin Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong organic growth in India and Developed Markets helped offset US generic pricing pressures in key products.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 7,827 crore, up 11% YoY.
- **US Revenue:** USD 317 million, down 13.4% QoQ in constant currency (up 9.3% YoY).
- **India Revenue:** Rs 2,345 crore approx. (implied), up 14.7% YoY; core prescription business grew 13.8% YoY with 6% volume growth.
- **Other Developed Markets (Europe, Canada, Australia):** Rs 1,182 crore, up 46% YoY (15% of overall sales). Europe grew 45% YoY.
- **API Revenue:** Up ~20% YoY.
- **Emerging Markets Revenue:** Down 4% YoY.
- **Other Operating Income:** Rs 42 crore, down 81% YoY.
- **Gross Margin:** 74.2% vs 73.3% in Q2 FY26 (74.6% in Q1 FY27).
- **Employee Expenses:** Rs 1,424 crore (18.3% of revenue), up 29% YoY.
- **Manufacturing & Other Expenses:** Rs 2,438 crore (31.3% of revenue), up 23% YoY.
- **R&D Spend:** Rs 618 crore (7.9% of sales) vs Rs 522 crore in Q2 FY26; H1 R&D at Rs 1,226 crore (7.7% of sales).
- **EBITDA (excl. Forex/Other Income):** Rs 1,957 crore, down 8.5% YoY; EBITDA Margin at 25.1% vs 31.3% in Q2 FY26.
- **Effective Tax Rate (H1):** 28.5%.

### Highlights
- Core India prescription business chronic segment grew 19.5% in H1 FY27, taking chronic mix to ~66% of portfolio (targeting 70% in 5 years).
- Diabetes portfolio in India grew 25%+ YoY in Q2 FY27, supported by market leadership in Huminsulin and the launch of generic Semaglutide injection.
- Received regulatory approval for the first green propellant MDI in India.
- In-licensed Yuvezzi in Europe from Tenpoint Therapeutics and closed the acquisition of VISUfarma.
- Received tentative approval for 505(b)(2) Apixaban Oral Suspension in the US.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue & Growth:** Expect H2 FY27 organic revenue growth excluding US to be ~20%.
- **US Business:** FY27 US revenue guided at USD 1.1 billion to USD 1.2 billion; expects FY28 US revenue to remain at USD 1 billion+ before scaling further in FY29.
- **Margins:** Maintained FY27 EBITDA margin guidance at 24%–25%, while acknowledging near-term quarterly margin moderation. Long-term EBITDA margins targeted at 29%–30% by FY29–FY30.
- **R&D & Tax Rate:** Full-year FY27 R&D guided at ~8% of sales. Full-year ETR expected at 27%–28%.
- **India Outperformance:** Guided India formulations to grow 1.2x–1.3x IPM, backed by >80 planned product launches over coming years.
- **US Pipeline:** Plan to file >15 products in FY27 and launch >60 products over the next 3 years (including ~25 injectable/respiratory products, 5 biosimilars, and 2–3 505(b)(2) assets).

### Demand & industry commentary
- IPM market growth reached a 6-quarter high of 14.7% in Q2 FY27.
- US Tolvaptan market saw entry of 2 additional generic players, leading to market share and pricing pressure; management expects further competitor entries.
- Indian respiratory inhalation market (MDIs, DPIs, nebulizers) saw slower growth in H1 but management expects seasonal pick-up in H2.
- Emerging Markets sales dropped 4% YoY due to generic competition in Dapagliflozin in Brazil.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins held steady at 74.2% despite Tolvaptan erosion due to high-margin India mix and favorable forex forward contracts.
- Other operating income dropped 81% YoY due to reduced PLI scheme export benefits.
- Operational SG&A costs increased YoY due to field force additions and settlement-related licensing fees for Mirabegron.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Balance sheet capacity permits leverage up to 2x EBITDA (Rs 16,000–17,000 crore debt capacity + Rs 3,000–4,000 crore cash) for strategic inorganic opportunities, prioritizing global ophthalmology/specialty assets and India formulations.
- Apixaban manufacturing strategy set up at Somerset facility with sufficient capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Cash:** Stood at Rs 3,349 crore as of Sept 30, 2026 (down from Rs 4,636 crore as of March 31, 2026) following the VISUfarma acquisition closure and dividend payouts.
- **Working Capital:** Operating working capital stood at Rs 7,542 crore (87 days) vs 90 days in Q1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Accelerating price erosion in US base portfolio from additional competitors entering Tolvaptan and upcoming Mirabegron LOE post-September 2027.
- Heightened geopolitical risks and adverse macroeconomic conditions in the Middle East causing higher input costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Neha Manpuria):** *How did gross margins remain strong despite Tolvaptan price/volume erosion?*  
  **A (Management):** Benefit from favorable currency forward contracts executed in Q1 (at 89–90 levels) realized in Q2, combined with better product mix from India, offset US price declines.
- **Q (Shashank Krishnakumar):** *What is the progress on respiratory filings like Respimat and Ellipta platforms?*  
  **A (Vinita Gupta):** Exhibit batches for Spiriva Respimat are completed for filing in FY27; Breo Ellipta filing is expected late FY27 or slipping slightly into Q1 FY28. Overall 7-8 target filings remain on track.
- **Q (Bino Pathiparampil):** *Will US revenues hold up in FY28 as Tolvaptan/Mirabegron erode further?*  
  **A (Vinita Gupta):** Confident in maintaining a USD 1 billion+ US revenue floor in FY28, with product launches like Apixaban 505(b)(2), Dalbavancin, and Pegfilgrastim biosimilar offsetting competitive losses.
- **Q (Kunal Randeria):** *What is the launch timeline and exclusivity status for generic Xywav?*  
  **A (Vinita Gupta):** Xywav is a long-term opportunity beyond FY29. Management believes Lupin was the sole first-to-file applicant with exclusivity rights.
- **Q (Alankar Garude):** *What caused the increase in manufacturing and other expenses?*  
  **A (Ramesh Swaminathan):** Driven by Mirabegron settlement costs (which will reverse out), field force expansion in India, volume growth, and front-loaded investments in AI and IT platforms.

### One-line takeaway
Lupin delivered solid 11% top-line growth backed by India and Europe expansion, buffering near-term US generic price erosion as it prepares for high-value complex product launches in FY28.
