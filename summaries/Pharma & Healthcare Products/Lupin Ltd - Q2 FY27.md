# Lupin Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Steady organic growth and strong India performance were offset by U.S. pricing pressures and increased competition in key products like Tolvaptan.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (including other operating income): Rs 7,827 crore (up 11% YoY from Rs 7,048 crore in Q2 FY26).
- U.S. sales: USD 317 million (down 13.4% QoQ on a constant currency basis).
- India business growth: 14.7% YoY (core prescription business grew 13.8% YoY; H1 prescription growth at 14.4%).
- Chronic segment growth: 19.5% in H1; chronic share of portfolio at ~66%.
- Other Developed Markets growth: 46% YoY (Europe grew 45% YoY); accounted for 15% of revenues.
- Other Emerging Markets: De-grew 4% YoY.
- Gross margins: 74.2% (compared to 73.3% in Q2 FY26 and 74.6% in Q1).
- Employee benefit expenses: Rs 1,424 crore, up ~29% YoY (18.3% of sales).
- Manufacturing & other expenses: Rs 2,438 crore, up ~23% YoY (31.3% of sales).
- R&D spend: Rs 618 crore, or 7.9% of sales (H1 R&D at Rs 1,226 crore or 7.7% of sales).
- EBITDA (excluding forex and other income): Rs 1,957 crore (vs Rs 2,138 crore in Q2 FY26).
- EBITDA margin: 25.1% (vs 31.3% in Q2 FY26).
- Effective Tax Rate (ETR): 28.5% in H1.
- Operating working capital: Rs 7,542 crore as of September 30, 2026 (87 days, vs 90 days in Q1).
- Net cash: Rs 3,349 crore (down from Rs 4,636 crore as of March 31, 2026).
- Other Operating Income: Rs 42 crore (down ~81% YoY from Rs 216 crore).

### Highlights
- Organic revenue growth remained in double digits, with organic growth excluding the U.S. expected around 20% for the second half.
- Diabetes segment in India delivered 25%+ YoY growth for the third consecutive quarter, driven by Huminsulin market leadership and Semaglutide injection launch.
- Entered an exclusive licensing partnership with Tenpoint Therapeutics for Yuvezzi (once-daily, dual-agent eye drop for presbyopia) in Europe.
- Received tentative approval for novel Apixaban Oral Suspension via the 505(b)(2) pathway.
- Achieved Zero Waste to Landfill certification across eight manufacturing sites.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 organic growth excluding U.S. of around 20% in H2.
- Full-year FY27 EBITDA margins guided around 24%-25%, with moderation expected in coming quarters.
- Full-year FY27 U.S. revenue guidance maintained at USD 1.1 billion to USD 1.2 billion.
- Full-year FY27 R&D spend expected around 8% of sales.
- Full-year ETR expected around 27%-28%.
- India formulations business targeted to grow at 1.2x to 1.3x the IPM growth rate.
- FY28 U.S. revenues targeted at USD 1 billion plus.
- Long-term target to double the share of complex products in the U.S. portfolio and increase chronic share in India to 70% over five years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- IPM market growth reached a six-quarter high of 14.7% in Q2.
- Chronic therapies in India now account for ~66% of Lupin's portfolio (up from ~65% in FY26).
- Biosimilars adoption in the U.S. marketplace is increasingly active, requiring different strategies (payer incentives) compared to small molecules.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins supported by volume growth, cost initiatives, and forex tailwinds (unwinding of Q1 forward rates), offset by U.S. price erosion and Middle East geopolitical risks.
- Employee expenses rose due to annual increments and business growth.
- Manufacturing and other expenses increased due to volumes, SG&A field force expansion, and settlement-related license fee payments (Mirabegron).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital allocation threshold limits set up to twice EBITDA (~Rs 16,000 crore - Rs 17,000 crore debt capacity + Rs 3,000 crore - Rs 4,000 crore cash), keeping total headroom near Rs 20,000 crore for compelling inorganic opportunities.
- Somerset facility confirmed to have sufficient capacity to drive FY28 Apixaban launch.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash reduced to Rs 3,349 crore from Rs 4,636 crore due to the VISUfarma acquisition and dividend payouts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Intensifying competition and loss of exclusivity in U.S. products, particularly Tolvaptan (additional generic entrants expected).
- Challenging geopolitical and operating environment in the Middle East impacting input costs.
- Emerging markets revenue decline of 4% YoY due to competition in Dapagliflozin in Brazil.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhara Ganatra asked about the offsetting factors for gross margins despite Tolvaptan erosion.** Ramesh Swaminathan explained that mix changes, volume tailwinds, and forex tailwinds (from Q1 forward rates) supported gross margins.
2. **Shashank Krishnakumar asked about the progress on respiratory filings (Spiriva Respimat, Breo).** Vinita Gupta stated exhibit batches for Spiriva Respimat are done for filing this fiscal, while Breo may slip slightly into Q1 FY28, keeping the 7-8 target filings on track.
3. **Bino Pathiparampil asked if FY28 U.S. revenues can match FY27.** Vinita Gupta stated revenue should remain at USD 1 billion plus, supported by Apixaban, Dalbavancin, and biosimilars offsetting Tolvaptan pressures.
4. **Neha Manpuria asked about Tolvaptan market share and pricing impact.** Vinita Gupta noted Lupin held a 50% share as the sole generic, but additional competitors will significantly impact both share and pricing contribution.
5. **Kunal Randeria asked about the timeline and exclusivity for Xywav.** Vinita Gupta clarified it is a further-out opportunity (beyond FY29) and Lupin believes it was sole exclusive and first-to-file.
6. **Shyam Srinivasan questioned U.S. guidance relative to H1 performance.** Vinita Gupta responded that because Q1 and Q2 were above plan, management is maintaining full-year guidance of USD 1.1 billion - USD 1.2 billion and watching for upside.

### One-line takeaway
Lupin delivered a steady Q2 FY27 with 11% revenue growth and 25.1% EBITDA margins, navigating U.S. pricing pressure in Tolvaptan through strong double-digit organic growth in India and Other Developed Markets.
