# Innova Captab Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and PAT growth, despite temporary margin compression due to API price volatility.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 20% volume growth guidance.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive; management is more focused on explaining the lag in API cost pass-through in export markets.
- **New topics:** Detailed discussion on Jammu facility's asset turnover (FATR) and specific EBITDA contribution (Rs 2-2.25 crore).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Jammu facility continues to ramp up as planned; revenue reached Rs 105 crore vs Rs 107 crore in Q1.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 496.3 crore (up 30.5% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 967.2 crore (up 32.1% YoY).
- **CDMO Revenue:** Rs 345.2 crore (up 30% YoY).
- **Branded Generic Revenue:** Rs 151.1 crore (up 32% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 72.4 crore (up 29% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14.6% (H1 EBITDA margin: 15.3%).
- **PAT:** Rs 42.4 crore (up 43% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 86.5 crore (up 43% YoY).
- **Jammu Facility Revenue:** Rs 105 crore.
- **Jammu Facility EBITDA:** Rs 2-2.25 crore.
- **Jammu Facility Utilization:** 22-24% (annualized).
- **Export Contribution:** 29% of revenue (Q2), 30% (H1).

### Highlights
- CDMO business remains the primary growth driver, contributing ~70% of total revenue.
- Jammu facility ramp-up is progressing; management expects revenue to cross Rs 500 crore for FY27.
- Branded generic business continues to show healthy momentum with 32% YoY growth.
- Management reiterated that the company is well-positioned to support evolving customer requirements through automation and scalable platforms.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains 20% volume growth guidance for FY27.
- Expects Jammu facility revenue to reach Rs 125-150 crore per quarter in the next two quarters.
- Blended EBITDA margin guidance remains at +/- 2% of current levels.
- Management is evaluating both greenfield and inorganic opportunities for growth beyond FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CDMO growth is supported by client traction, new customer onboarding, and an expanding product portfolio.
- Export markets face longer purchase order (PO) cycles, leading to a lag in passing on API price increases compared to the domestic market.
- Domestic market allows for swifter cost pass-through due to shorter business cycles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin impacted by 2.8% (consolidated) due to API price increases that were not fully passed through to export customers.
- Management expects margins to normalize as API prices stabilize and contracts are renewed.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No new major capex announced; focus remains on Jammu facility ramp-up.
- Management highlighted that future growth beyond FY29 may involve capital allocation toward inorganic acquisitions or greenfield projects.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital cycle maintained at 90 days.
- Management emphasized a disciplined capital allocation strategy to maintain a comfortable leverage position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- API price volatility remains a key risk, specifically regarding the lag in cost pass-through for international contracts.
- Competitive landscape in both API and finished dosage forms.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Arpit Tapadia (I.G.E. Private Limited):** Asked about Jammu revenue ramp-up. *Management confirmed Rs 105 crore revenue this quarter and expects to cross Rs 500 crore for the full year.*
2. **Abhi Jain (AJ Capital):** Questioned the lower FATR of the Jammu facility compared to older plants. *Management explained that newer, automated, and European-equipment-heavy plants have higher capital costs, leading to a lower initial FATR, but they remain confident in the business model's ROE.*
3. **Pavithra Jaivant (Prime Investor):** Inquired about the lag in cost pass-through. *Management clarified that export contracts have longer PO cycles, causing a lag, whereas domestic pass-through is swifter.*
4. **Vansh Gupta (Prescient Capital):** Asked if growth is slowing compared to peers. *Management reiterated their 20% volume growth guidance and emphasized that they are overcoming headwinds to maintain this trajectory.*
5. **Rohit Kumar (ADM Advisor):** Asked about inorganic acquisition plans. *Management stated they are evaluating opportunities for growth beyond FY29 and will inform the street upon reaching a tangible conclusion.*

### One-line takeaway
Innova Captab delivered strong top-line growth of 30.5% YoY, with the Jammu facility contributing Rs 105 crore in revenue, though margins faced temporary pressure due to a lag in passing on API cost increases in export markets.
