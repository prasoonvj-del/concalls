# Viyash Scientific Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-16 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent financial delivery, successful integration, and significantly strengthened balance sheet.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 946 crore, up 20% (or 19.5%) YoY
- **EBITDA:** Rs 205 crore, up 59.2% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 21.6%, expanding by ~530 bps YoY (or 16.2% to 21.6%)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 79 crore, more than doubled YoY (up 115% YoY from Rs 37 crore)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 112 crore, up 132% YoY from Rs 48 crore
- **Gross Margin:** 54.1%, up ~220 bps YoY from 51.9%
- **Net Debt:** Rs 86 crore
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 0.1x (vs 0.24x last quarter, ~1x a year back)
- **Finance Costs:** Rs 12.5 crore (vs Rs 20.4 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **ESOP Cost:** Rs 25 crore total for Q1 (includes Rs 19 crore incremental expense for 1.3 crore options granted under the amalgamation scheme)
- **US Business Growth:** Up 60% (or 45% YoY in dollar terms)
- **Domestic Animal Health Growth:** Up 60%
- **EU Region Growth:** Expected closer to 18-20% on an INR basis for the full year
- **Emerging Markets Growth:** Volume grew 25% last quarter

### Highlights
- Integration of the combined entity is complete in record time.
- Balance sheet is the strongest in company history; virtually debt-free on a net basis.
- Signed Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) for the acquisition of BioForLife in Italy, aligning with companion animal health strategy.
- US formulation business benefited from moving volume products to India and achieving full vertical integration with API.
- Vizag site Albendazole capacity expansion completed; commercialization started in July.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects double-digit revenue growth (mid-teens, 13%-15% overall) for FY27, with Animal Health growing at 20%+.
- Full-year EU growth targeted around 18-20% on an INR basis.
- Total Capex guidance: Rs 250 crore to Rs 300 crore per year.
- Aspiration of reaching USD 1 billion by 2032 (~18% CAGR from FY27 numbers) via organic and inorganic routes.
- ESOP cost guidance: Rs 40 crore per quarter for the remaining 3 quarters of FY27 (total Rs 150 crore for FY27), dropping to Rs 25-30 crore in FY28.
- Minority interest expected to remain around 16%-17% of total profit, with plans to buy out minority stakes in the next 1-2 years (outer limit 2 years).
- Goodwill amortization (depreciation) of Rs 25 crore per quarter will drop to Rs 10-11 crore from next quarter onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- API revenue was flat QoQ due to temporary timing issues and raw material price volatility in June (wait-and-watch approach by customers expecting war to end), but order traction has resumed strongly for Q2.
- Animal health formulation business growing strongly across all regions.
- Companion animal health is the #1 strategic growth area for the next 5 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins expected to sustain around 20%-22%.
- High-potent formulation R&D lab completed and operational.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New tablet capacity and R&D capabilities for companion animals on track; target to complete by January-February for exhibit batches.
- BioForLife acquisition expected to close in the next few months; serves as a launchpad for Europe.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced to Rs 86 crore; net debt-to-EBITDA at 0.1x.
- Working to restructure Ireland debt and optimize interest and tax burdens further.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Long gestation periods (3 to 5 years) for complex and high-potent products, with major revenues starting post-2028/2029.
- Raw material price volatility impacts short-term API procurement timing by customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Naman Bagrecha (IIFL Capital):** Asked about flat EU growth and US margin sustainability. Management clarified EU growth is phased and on track for 18-20% annually; US margins are expected to sustain due to backward integration into Indian API manufacturing.
- **Bharat Sheth:** Inquired about high-potent/oncology capabilities. Management stated R&D and manufacturing are in place, but bulk revenues will materialize post-2029 due to patent expiry timelines.
- **Sajal Kapoor:** Asked about non-negotiable principles and capital allocation risks. Management emphasized zero tolerance on compliance and governance, disciplined M&A aligned with strategy, and modest capex relative to the mature business base.
- **Chintan Sheth:** Asked about minority interest and segment split. Management guided minority interest at 16-17% with a 1-2 year buyout horizon, and noted API contributes ~40-45% of total revenue (two-thirds human, one-third animal).
- **Kumar Saurabh:** Asked about first-to-file pipeline and 2032 aspirations. Management confirmed 18% CAGR to reach USD 1 billion by 2032 is a practical and comfortable aspiration backed by clear organic and inorganic plans.

### One-line takeaway
Viyash Scientific delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 20% revenue growth, a 530 bps expansion in EBITDA margin to 21.6%, and a near net debt-free balance sheet, driven by successful integration and structural improvements in its US and animal health segments.
