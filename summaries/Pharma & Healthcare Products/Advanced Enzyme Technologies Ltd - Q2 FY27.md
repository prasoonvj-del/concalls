# Advanced Enzyme Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust growth across all business segments and improved margins despite global economic headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 2,145 million (+16% YoY, +12% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 721 million (+20% YoY, +36% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 34% (vs 33% YoY, 28% QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 512 million (+14% YoY, +33% QoQ).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 4,062 million (+10% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Buyback:** Completed acquisition of 2.41 million equity shares at an average price of Rs 289.09 per share.
*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   **Human Healthcare:** Rs 1,285 million (+6% YoY).
    *   **Animal Healthcare:** Rs 265 million (+37% YoY).
    *   **Bio-Processing:** Rs 365 million (+43% YoY).
    *   **Specialized Manufacturing:** Rs 231 million (+25% YoY).
*   **Cash Position:** Approximately Rs 610 crore (post-buyback, as of end-September plus October adjustments).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management aims for lower double-digit growth for the year.
*   **Margins:** EBITDA margin guidance maintained at 30% to 32%.
*   **US Market:** Management expects a turnaround towards the year-end.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Global Environment:** Challenging due to geopolitical tensions, inflation, and volatile energy prices.
*   **US Market:** Experienced "dullness" over the last two quarters; management sees signs of potential turnaround based on R&D traction and recent industry events.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Driven by new customer acquisition, new product development, and geographic expansion.
*   **Competition:** Management stated that Novonesis's reported capex in Mumbai is primarily for bioethanol, a segment where AETL is not present.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Input Costs:** Power, fuel, and raw material costs remained elevated.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Focus on efficiencies helped mitigate cost pressures and improve margins.
*   **Employee Cost:** Decreased QoQ due to the reversal of provisioning and lower ESOP amortization.
*   **R&D:** Spent Rs 90 million in Q2 (4.3% of consolidated revenue).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 123 crore (includes R&D and maintenance).
*   **H1 Spend:** Rs 52 crore utilized.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Sales Deferment:** Management noted Rs 10 crore of sales were deferred from Q1; Rs 2.5 crore was realized in Q2, with Rs 7.5 crore carried forward.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Macro Risks:** Geopolitical tensions, currency fluctuations, and inflationary pressures.
*   **US Market:** Existing clients are not growing rapidly; the company is working to add new customers to offset this.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is the confidence level for a US market turnaround?** A: Management cited increased R&D paper publications, clinical studies, and positive feedback from the SupplySide West trade show.
*   **Q: What is driving the growth in SciTech?** A: Broad-based growth, increased shift working, and higher capacity utilization.
*   **Q: What is the contribution of new products?** A: Management estimated approximately 7-8% of revenue comes from new products.
*   **Q: What is the status of the WELLFA segment?** A: Sales are currently negligible; management does not expect significant revenue in the next 2-3 years.
*   **Q: Is the current employee cost sustainable?** A: Management indicated the current quarter's cost is a sustainable base, though hiring continues.

### One-line takeaway
Advanced Enzyme Technologies delivered a strong Q2 with 16% YoY revenue growth and expanded margins to 34%, supported by broad-based segment performance and operational efficiencies.
