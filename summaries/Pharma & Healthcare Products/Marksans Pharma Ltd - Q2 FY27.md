# Marksans Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record quarterly revenue and strong profitability, supported by broad-based geographic growth despite higher freight and input costs.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR940 crores (up 30.5% YoY, 11.8% QoQ)
- Total income: INR958 crores (up 29.2% YoY)
- Gross profit: INR550 crores (up 33.6% YoY); Gross margin: 58.5% (up 135 bps YoY, down from 59.1% in Q1)
- EBITDA: INR214 crores (up 48.3% YoY); EBITDA margin: 22.8% (up 274 bps YoY)
- Profit before tax (PBT): INR194 crores (up 45.1% YoY)
- Profit after tax (PAT): INR144 crores (up 44.8% YoY, down QoQ from INR159 crores in Q1); Net margin: 15%
- Geographic revenue breakdown: North America INR421 crores (44.8%), U.K. & Europe INR405 crores (43%), Australia & New Zealand INR101 crores (10.7%), Rest of the World (ROW) INR14 crores (1.5%)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: INR1,781 crores (up 32.9% YoY); EBITDA: INR427 crores (up 74.7% YoY, margin 24%); PAT: INR303 crores (up 92.5% YoY, margin 16.6%)
- Operating cash flow (H1): INR322 crores; Free cash flow (H1): INR275 crores after capex of INR46 crores
- Cash balance and current investments: INR1,124 crores; Net cash position: INR1,095 crores; Net worth: INR3,367 crores
- Return ratios (TTM): ROCE 24.7% (vs 18.7%); ROE 18.6% (vs 13.6%)
- Working capital cycle: ~128 days (down from 138 days in FY26)

### Highlights
- Q2 revenue reached INR940 crores, driven by strong momentum across Europe, U.K., North America, and Australia/New Zealand.
- QliniQ (Netherlands) contributed ~INR84 crores for H1; ABCnow and Marksans GmbH are strengthening the platform in Germany.
- Effective tax rate for Q2 was 25.8%.
- Cash conversion (operating cash flow as % of EBITDA) improved to 75.2% in H1 from 30.7% YoY.
- R&D expenditure for H1 was 2.6% of revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is working towards achieving INR5,000 crores revenue in the next 3 years and INR6,000 crores within 5 years.
- U.S. order book targeted to reach $300 million over the next 3 years (from current $225 million).
- Australia subsidiary (Nova) targeted to grow into a $100 million subsidiary over the next 3 years.
- Europe revenue target of ~INR1,000 crores in 5 years remains valid.
- Management expects next year (FY28) to comfortably hit the INR4,000 crores revenue mark, with organic/inorganic run rates trending close to INR1,000 crores/quarter.
- EBITDA margin expected to remain range-bound around 21%-22% for the year, subject to raw material and freight cost movements.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Seasonal cold season impacts Q3 positively; H2 typically sees strong volumes.
- U.S. market faces inflationary pressures where food and gas take priority over medicines, alongside single-digit price erosion in prescription products.
- ROW business impacted by geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East and Red Sea affecting shipping logistics and timing of shipments.
- Finished product prices in the base portfolio are stable; no price increases witnessed.

### Costs, raw materials & margins
- Freight costs nearly doubled, adding ~INR20 crores to sequential other expenses.
- Employee costs increased by ~INR8 crores due to team expansion and investments in newer markets.
- German business incurred initial startup costs of a couple of crores.
- API and raw material costs (e.g., paracetamol, ibuprofen) remain elevated due to crude oil and geopolitical issues.
- Accounting treatment for intangible assets changed in Q2: finite useful life of 10 years applied, increasing depreciation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capital allocation includes internal capex and M&As (particularly in Europe).
- Exploring expansion of manufacturing capabilities in India via debottlenecking, adding blocks in the Teva plant, or acquiring a plant within the next 12 to 18 months.
- Not currently pursuing backward integration into APIs or entering injectables.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position stands at INR1,095 crores as of September end.
- H1 cash outflows included ~INR95 crores for acquisitions and ~INR41 crores for dividend payment.
- Lease liabilities increased by ~INR120 crores due to a new lease taken in the U.K.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East and Red Sea causing logistics and supply chain delays for ROW shipments.
- Inflation and crude oil volatility impacting input, inventory, and freight costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ahmed Madha (Unifi Capital) asked about U.S. constant currency growth and capex plans.** Management stated growth is subject to seasonality and inflation impacts, and capex will increase to support the INR5,000-6,000 crore revenue roadmap.
- **Mihir Damania (Institutional Equities) asked about the sharp rise in depreciation.** Management explained the change in accounting treatment from impairment testing to amortizing acquired intangibles over a finite useful life of 10 years.
- **Arun (Unifi Capital) asked about API cost trends and gross margin sustainability.** Management stated gross margins are expected to be around 55%-56% and expect raw material prices to cool down in ~30 days if crude prices ease.
- **Vishal Manchanda (Systematix Institutional Equities) asked about the contribution of new launches to growth.** Management stated new launches contribute ~20% in the U.K., 50%-60% in Europe, and ~10% in the U.S.
- **Harshal (ASK Wealth Advisors) asked if EBITDA margins could exceed guidance.** Management stated it is possible only if geopolitical conflicts (wars) cease.

### One-line takeaway
Marksans Pharma delivered a record Q2 FY27 with 30.5% YoY revenue growth and a 22.8% EBITDA margin, underpinned by robust geographical diversification, strong cash generation, and a net cash balance of INR1,095 crores.
