# Wockhardt Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-11 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** FY26 (Call dated 11th June, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by the US FDA approval of Zaynich, significant improvement in EBITDA margins, and a strong growth trajectory across key verticals.

### Key numbers
- **Top line / Revenue:** INR 3,373 crore
- **EBITDA:** INR 630 crore
- **EBITDA growth:** ~51% YoY
- **Profitability before tax:** INR 238 crore
- **Cash equivalents:** INR 662 crore
- **Net debt-to-equity ratio:** 0.1
- **EBITDA margin:** 18.6% (increased from 5.4% three years ago)
- **Biosimilar and biotech business growth:** 27% YoY (human insulin production scaled up by 2x, Glargine by 1.5x)
- **Emerging market business growth:** 35% YoY (contributes 28% of turnover)
- **UK business growth:** 13% YoY (contributes 39% of total business; 18% market share in covered market)
- **Capex guidance:** Normal capex of INR 200 crore to INR 300 crore per year for the next three years (primarily for biologicals capacity).
- **Zaynich global peak sales potential (management guidance):** $1.5 billion to $2 billion per year.

### Highlights
- Received US FDA approval for Zaynich (first Indian research product approved by the US FDA), which covers enterobacterales, pseudomonas, and 95% of carbapenem-resistant cases in the US based on granted breakpoints.
- Also received CDSCO approval for Zaynich in India and submitted for European approvals.
- Exited the loss-making US generic business to focus on high-margin specialties and operational excellence.
- Implemented S/4HANA cloud version and scaling up AI-related initiatives.
- Built a strong US core leadership team for commercializing Zaynich (William McNey, Dr. Dennis Deruelle, Sandy Estrada, Leo Yasinski).
- UK benefits from a subscription model offering a fixed GBP 20 million per year for three years for novel antibiotics regardless of supply volume.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a slight negative impact or breakeven within 12 to 18 months for the Zaynich launch due to upfront costs, followed by a "hockey stick" rise in profitability.
- Management guides global peak sales for Zaynich at $1.5 billion to $2 billion per year.
- Biosimilar business is expected to double within the next 24 to 36 months, with manufacturing capacity doubling in 12 to 15 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- In the US, cUTI empirical treatment has a 40% failure rate due to multi-microbial infections and multi-mechanisms of mutation.
- India and emerging markets face a high carbapenem resistance burden (35% to 45% among gram-negative infections).
- Global antibiotic market for new novel antibiotics is around $1 billion in total across existing players, presenting a clean white space due to lack of pipelines from big pharma.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- R&D spend will continue at approximately the same percentage of sales.
- Operating cost for the US Zaynich launch will involve an upfront investment, with breakeven expected in 12 to 18 months.
- India pricing for Zaynich will be heavily discounted (75% to 80%) relative to US pricing ($1,200 to $1,500 per day) to eliminate price as a barrier.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Normal capex of INR 200 crore to INR 300 crore per year over the next three years, primarily allocated to biologicals capacity.
- US Zaynich supply will be manufactured in Europe at a US FDA-approved facility, while emerging markets and India will be supplied from India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash equivalents stand at INR 662 crore.
- Net debt-to-equity ratio is 0.1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Building a new commercial model and communicating about a new drug like Zaynich requires developing new organizational capabilities and competencies over the next 12 to 18 months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tarang Shah asked about the top execution risks over the next 12 months and mitigation plans.** Management answered that building an entirely new business model and organizational capabilities for innovative drugs is the biggest challenge, which is being mitigated by keeping intellectual capital in-house while utilizing operational/commercial partners outside India.
2. **Siddharth Mehta asked about revenue projections, capex, and commercial timelines.** Management stated revenue will see a slow increase initially (hockey stick curve after 12-18 months), capex will be INR 200-300 crore annually for biologicals, and Zaynich operations will breakeven in 12 to 18 months.
3. **An unnamed analyst asked about the US manufacturing vs. Europe supply strategy for Zaynich.** Management clarified that Europe/US supplies are sourced from the European US FDA-approved site (a de-risked strategy), while India and emerging markets will be supplied from India.
4. **An unnamed analyst asked about the US commercial go-to-market strategy and whether they are using a partner.** Management clarified they are executing the strategy themselves with an in-house leadership team, but utilizing a commercial partner/3PL provider for operational logistics, distribution, and pharmacovigilance.
5. **An unnamed analyst asked about the peak sales potential and timelines for Zaynich.** Management reiterated global peak sales guidance of $1.5 billion to $2 billion per year.

### One-line takeaway
Wockhardt has achieved a historic US FDA approval for its novel antibiotic Zaynich, shifting its strategic focus from liquidity survival and generic restructuring to scaling high-margin specialty biosimilars and global innovation-led growth.
