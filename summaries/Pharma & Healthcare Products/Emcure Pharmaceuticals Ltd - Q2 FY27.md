# Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong broad-based growth across all geographies, expanding margins, and an upgraded revenue guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,820 crore (up 24% YoY, up 9% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 575 crore (up 31% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.4% (expanded by ~100 bps YoY, up ~70 bps QoQ)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 325 crore (up 29% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 11.5% (expanded by 45 bps YoY)
- **Domestic (India) Revenue:** Rs 1,155 crore (up 12% YoY)
- **International Revenue:** Rs 1,665 crore (up 34.5% YoY, accounting for 59% of total revenue)
- **Europe & UK Revenue:** Rs 604 crore (up 36% YoY)
- **Canada Revenue:** Rs 431 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **Rest of World (RoW) Revenue:** Rs 630 crore (up 41% YoY)
- **R&D Spend:** Rs 111 crore (~4% of revenue from operations)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 1,317 crore as of September (increase of Rs 263 crore over March '26)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 5,401 crore (up 23.6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 1,083 crore (up 28.6% YoY, margin 20%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 618 crore (up 33% YoY, margin 11.4%)

### Highlights
- Broad-based growth achieved across all geographies, supported by base business expansion and new launches.
- Women's health, cardiometabolic, and CNS portfolios drove domestic growth; organic domestic growth reached 10%.
- Board approved the amalgamation of wholly owned subsidiaries Zuventus Healthcare and Emcutix Biopharmaceuticals into Emcure on October 12th to simplify group holding structure.
- Received over 20 product approvals globally, including liposomal amphotericin B in Brazil and Mexico, and bevacizumab for wet AMD in India.
- Received a positive recommendation from India's Subject Expert Committee for lenacapavir injection for PrEP; dossiers filed with WHO and US FDA.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management raised full-year FY27 revenue growth outlook: now expects revenue growth to **exceed 20%** (compared to mid-teens previously guided).
- EBITDA margin improvement for the year is now expected to be at the **upper end** of the guidance range of **75 to 100 bps**.
- Reaffirmed commitment to the objective of becoming **net cash by the end of FY28**.
- R&D investments expected to step up in H2, staying within the total year bracket of **4% to 5%** of revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic industry growth is benefiting from improved healthcare access, awareness, demand for branded generics, and diagnostic uplift from therapies like GLP-1/obesity.
- Zuventus operations and team are stabilizing; management expects H2 growth for Zuventus to outpace the prior year and domestic growth to align with industry levels by exit FY27.
- ARV business in RoW is backed by a strong order book, with non-ARV growth accelerating via new launches.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin for Q2 was 59.5% (improved ~110 bps QoQ driven by product/business mix).
- Employee costs rose 13% YoY to Rs 435 crore; other expenses rose 20% to Rs 669 crore due to higher sales and R&D.
- Management noted cost pressures from imported raw materials, freight, insurance, and transit times, which offset favorable currency gains. SG&A growth is expected to remain slower than revenue growth, providing operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Acquisition-related payouts during H1 totaled Rs 551 crore (including final payout for Canada acquisition, minority stake in Genova, and UK product portfolio payment).
- Liposomal amphotericin B has two diversified manufacturing sites approved for key markets like the UK and Europe.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 1,317 crore as of September 2026, up from Rs 1,054 crore in March 2026 due to borrowings funding acquisition-related payouts.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted potential risks from macroeconomic and geopolitical situations (e.g., potential oil price spikes from conflicts) impacting future growth trajectories.
- Potential future competition in Europe for liposomal amphotericin B.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tushar Manudhane asked about other growth products in Europe alongside amphotericin B.** Management (Samit Mehta) highlighted *treprostinil* as an attractive, high-margin product expected to contribute significantly.
2. **Alankar Garude asked about the timeline for Zuventus and domestic organic growth to match industry levels.** Management (Vik Shastri / Piyush Nahar) stated that H2 will be strong versus the prior year, and organic growth is expected to match or exceed industry levels by the exit of FY27.
3. **Bansi Desai asked about the profitability hierarchy across segments.** Management (Vik Shastri) noted India is the top vertical in profitability (though in-licensed multinational products have lower standalone margins), followed by strong profitability in Europe, Canada, and non-ARV ROW, with ARV margins lower than the corporate average.
4. **Foram Parekh asked about the geographic penetration of liposomal amphotericin B in Europe.** Management (Tajuddin Shaikh) stated they have penetrated about 50% of the market (active in about 20 countries, with ~20 more countries pending approval/launch).
5. **Alankar Garude asked about the constant currency impact on international sales.** Management (Piyush Nahar) clarified that currency impact on international top-line was about 10%, with international constant currency growth at 24% (overall 18.5%).

### One-line takeaway
Emcure delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 24% revenue growth and margin expansion, prompting management to raise its full-year revenue growth guidance to over 20%.
