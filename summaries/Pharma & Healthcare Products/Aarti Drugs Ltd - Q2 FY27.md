# Aarti Drugs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved record consolidated revenue and highest-ever quarterly EBITDA, supported by strong API realizations and export-led growth in the formulations segment.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 829 crore (+27% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 138 crore (+63% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 16.6% (+370 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 83 crore (+83% YoY).
*   **API Segment Revenue:** Rs 605 crore (+17% YoY).
*   **Specialty Chemicals Revenue:** Rs 72 crore (+92% YoY).
*   **Formulations Revenue:** Rs 130 crore (+58% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **API Performance:** Volume growth of 5% and realization growth of 16-18% (standalone).
*   **Formulations:** Driven by a 73% YoY increase in exports; oncology segment initiated in the US market.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Methylamines facility (Saykha) operated at 72% utilization; captive consumption of antidiabetic portfolio reached 85%.
*   **Tarapur Facility:** Produced 85 tonnes of technical grade methyl salicylate in the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management expects 15% EBITDA margin to be sustainable.
*   **Volume Growth:** Management targets 5-10% volume growth for the year.
*   **Formulations:** Management expects to achieve the Rs 1,000 crore revenue milestone earlier than previously planned.
*   **Oncology:** Management estimates oncology revenue contribution to reach late 30s to early 40s % by FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   API prices remain firm due to geopolitical developments in West Asia.
*   Customers are prioritizing reliable and uninterrupted supply of APIs.
*   Pricing growth is driven by gas-based products and products linked to crude prices.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Formulation EBITDA margin was 28% in Q2, which management termed "exceptional."
*   Pricing pass-through is quick for new orders, while pending orders are supplied at lower rates.
*   Raw material inventory is maintained at 40-50 days; management intends to match raw material booking with sales booking to avoid inventory risk.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Tarapur:** Expanding USFDA-approved block with three new multipurpose plants; commercial production targeted for end of FY29.
*   **Sarigam:** Increasing metformin capacity from 1,400 to 1,700 tonnes/month (early FY28) and adding a 500 tonnes/month USFDA-compliant block (end of FY28).
*   **Baddi:** Brownfield expansion underway to double oral solid dosage manufacturing capacity.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Pricing Volatility:** Potential for volatility in crude and gas prices impacting input costs.
*   **Regulatory/Legal:** Anti-dumping duty process for salicylic acid is ongoing; management expects initiation before year-end.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How much of the growth is price vs. volume?**
    *   *Management:* For the standalone business, price growth was 17-18%, and volume growth was 5% for API and specialty chemicals combined.
*   **Q: Is the 28% margin in formulations sustainable?**
    *   *Management:* It was an exceptional quarter. While they have a 4-month order book, they expect margins to settle in the "early 20s" going forward.
*   **Q: What is the status of the oncology business?**
    *   *Management:* It has initiated with one product in the US, but contribution is currently small. They plan to file 4-5 oncology products in the next 12 months.
*   **Q: What are the future capex plans?**
    *   *Management:* Future focus includes a CDMO block for intermediates/specialty chemicals, heavy R&D investment in formulations, and expanding regulatory market presence.

### One-line takeaway
Aarti Drugs delivered a record-breaking quarter driven by strong API pricing and export-led formulation growth, with management signaling confidence in sustaining 15% margins despite potential pricing volatility.
