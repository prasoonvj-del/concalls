# Jagsonpal Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a bolder strategy, scaling up ambitions with organic momentum, inorganic scale (two acquisitions in the quarter), and strong financial strength with quarterly revenues expected to cross Rs 100 crore in Q3.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 81 crore, up 9.3% YoY
- **Consolidated revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 96 crore, up 29% YoY
- **Standalone revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 164 crore
- **Consolidated revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 179 crore
- **Standalone gross margin (Q2 FY27):** 64.1%
- **Standalone gross margin (H1 FY27):** 64.7% (up 70 bps YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 22.7%
- **Consolidated EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27):** 19.3%
- **H1 EBITDA margin (Standalone/Blended context):** 22.9% (up 40 bps YoY)
- **Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 13 crore (margin 15.5%)
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 12 crore (margin 12.8%)
- **Cash and bank balance:** Over Rs 135 crore
- **Consolidated net operating cash (H1 FY27):** Rs 25 crore
- **Organic growth (H1 FY27):** 9.3%
- **Power brands revenue contribution:** ~58% (top 10 brands)
- **MR productivity growth:** Up ~10%
- **Doctor reach:** ~1,37,000 doctors
- **IPM (Indian Pharma Market) growth:** ~12% (July-Sept '26); ~11% (H1 FY27)
- **Jagsonpal market performance:** ~15% growth in July-Sept '26 and H1 FY27

### Highlights
- Completed two acquisitions: Aequitas (acquired July 2026, providing entry into hospital/institution channel) and Group Pharma wellness portfolio (acquired October 2026, adding two established brands in hematinics and respiratory across 6 states).
- Yash Pharma business acquired in May 2024 is now fully integrated.
- Top 10 power brands drive 58% of revenue; 9 of these rank in the top 5 of their markets. Specific brand outperformance: Maintane (+29%), Metadec (+14%), Lycored (+15%), Endoreg (+20%).
- Gynaecology is the primary driver (engaging ~35,000 gynaecologists), followed by orthopaedics and dermatology.
- First-ever consolidated financial statements announced along with standalone results.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects quarterly revenues to cross Rs 100 crore in Q3 FY27.
- Reaffirmed vision to be a Rs 500 crore organization by FY 2028 purely organically (without factoring in future acquisitions beyond those already completed).
- Consolidated EBITDA margins expected to be close to or in excess of 22% going forward (with Q3 expected to deliver over 22%).
- Targeting growth in excess of 1.5x of the expected pharma industry growth in Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Corporate hospital chains are now contributing in excess of 11.5% to the Indian pharmaceutical industry.
- Strategic shift towards sub-chronic and chronic segments to reduce dependence on acute therapy.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Standalone EBITDA margins remained stable at 22.7% despite advance investments in people, marketing, and medical inputs.
- MR productivity up by ~10% following investments in annual increments and Labour Code-aligned salary structures which reduced attrition.
- Hospital business carries lower gross and EBITDA margins compared to the core standalone business.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Asset-light model maintained; no manufacturing backend owned currently, though long-term export/international considerations are under discussion with promoters and the Board.
- No manpower additions planned beyond current budgeted numbers for Q3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stands at over Rs 135 crore.
- Consolidated net operating cash generated in H1 FY27 was Rs 25 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Equirex growth is constrained as it falls under restricted NRx portfolio (prescription-only availability limits sales push).
- Hospital business operates at lower margins, which impacts consolidated margin metrics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankur Kumar (Alpha Capital)** asked how the Rs 500 crore revenue target by FY2028 is achievable given historical 10% CAGR. *Management stated the target is pure organic, driven by strategic execution and accelerated momentum without further acquisitions.*
2. **Sajal Kapoor (Antifragile Thinking)** requested a separate line item or transparency on organic vs. inorganic growth post-Group Pharma consolidation. *Management agreed to provide regional/state-level splits and commentary for transparency.*
3. **Mihir Damania (Fident Asset Management)** questioned the rationale of entering hospital channels and wellness portfolios rather than deepening core gynae/derma. *Management explained that hospitals surged to 11.5% of IPM, requiring immediate institutional entry, and Group Pharma provided cultural fit and geographical expansion.*
4. **Urmish Shah (Moneywisers)** asked about de-growth/slower growth in Indocap and Equirex. *Management clarified Indocap grew 4.5% (slightly below market) with packaging innovations underway, while Equirex is limited by NRx restrictions.*
5. **Vansh Gupta (Prescient Capital)** asked if brand premiumization would lift gross margins above the historical 64%-65% band. *Management noted standalone margins will edge up 50-75 bps annually, but consolidated margins will reflect the lower-margin hospital business mix.*

### One-line takeaway
Jagsonpal reported Q2 FY27 standalone revenue of Rs 81 crore (up 9.3% YoY) and consolidated revenue of Rs 96 crore, backed by successful integration of recent acquisitions and a clear path toward its Rs 500 crore revenue vision by FY2028.
