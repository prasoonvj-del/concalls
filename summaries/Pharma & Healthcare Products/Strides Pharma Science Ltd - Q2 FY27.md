# Strides Pharma Science Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved record quarterly revenue and EBITDA, with the Ex-US business successfully surpassing the US business in revenue contribution ahead of schedule.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** ₹14,297 million (17% YoY, 13% QoQ).
*   **Gross Margin:** 56.0% (vs 57.8% YoY, 60.9% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** ₹2,509 million (8% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 17.6% (vs 19.0% YoY, 18.2% QoQ).
*   **Operational PAT:** ₹1,358 million (12% YoY).
*   **Operational EPS:** ₹14.7 (vs ₹13.2 YoY).
*   **US Revenue:** ₹6,855 million ($73 million, 9% YoY).
*   **Ex-US Revenue:** ₹6,963 million (31% YoY).
*   **Net Debt-to-EBITDA:** 1.72x.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Milestone:** Ex-US business now contributes 49% of total revenue (up from 43% in Q2 last year), surpassing the US business for the first time.
*   **Profitability:** EBITDA crossed the ₹2,500 million mark for the first time in a single quarter.
*   **Governance:** Appointed Mr. Biren Gabhawala as an Independent Director.
*   **US Portfolio:** Launched 3 products in Q2; commercialized portfolio stands at 75 products. Holds top-three market positions in 38 products.
*   **Controlled Substances (CS):** 5 commercialized CS products contribute ~6% of US revenue; pipeline includes >10 CS products (nasal sprays, patches, films).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY28 Aspiration:** Management stated the overall FY28 growth aspiration remains intact, though the US revenue target of $375 million is now expected to be reached slightly beyond FY28.
*   **H2 Outlook:** Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1, driven by new launches and business wins.
*   **Margins:** Management is confident in sustaining H1 gross margins (58.3%) going forward.
*   **Debt:** Management expects the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio to stabilize and improve by the end of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **US Market:** Drug-shortage opportunities in oral solids have largely normalized; growth is now focused on differentiated platforms (nasal sprays, patches, films).
*   **Ex-US Market:** Demand is described as "sticky" and broad-based across UK, Europe, Nordics, and Africa.
*   **Controlled Substances:** Growth is currently constrained by quota allocations, but management expects progressive improvement in allocations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Compression:** Gross and EBITDA margins were impacted by higher material, freight, and logistics costs linked to the geopolitical situation.
*   **Cost Mitigation:** Management is utilizing alternate sourcing and procurement initiatives to address cost pressures.
*   **Finance Costs:** Management expects finance costs to remain in the ₹40 crore to ₹45 crore range.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Guidance:** Management expects FY27 capex to be around ₹300 crores.
*   **Investments:** Capital is being deployed into nasal spray and transdermal patch programs to broaden the addressable market.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Cash-to-cash cycle is 137 days (up 13 days from March 2026).
*   **Inventory:** Increased inventory levels were a deliberate strategy to counter elongated supply chain lead times and ensure service levels.
*   **Debt:** Net debt increased by ₹211 crores in H1, primarily driven by working capital requirements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical Impact:** Ongoing geopolitical disruptions are affecting supply chain lead times and logistics costs.
*   **US Pricing:** Competitive forces and price erosion in the US market require constant adjustment of product mix.
*   **Quota Constraints:** Growth in Controlled Substances remains dependent on regulatory quota allocations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is driving the improved momentum in non-US markets?** Management (Badree) attributed it to a broad-based, sticky portfolio across B2B and B2C channels and expansion into new territories.
*   **Q: Why is the US revenue flat in dollar terms?** Management (Vikesh) explained it is a combination of price erosion and volume adjustments, offset by new launches and cost improvement plans.
*   **Q: Does the FY28 aspiration hinge on specific products?** Management (Vikesh) clarified it is broad-based and not dependent on a single product.
*   **Q: Will working capital remain elevated?** Management (Badree) stated the cash conversion cycle will improve in coming quarters as inventory normalizes.
*   **Q: Is the debt-free aspiration still valid?** Management (Vikesh) confirmed the aspiration remains, noting the current debt increase is largely working capital-driven rather than structural.

### One-line takeaway
Strides delivered record revenue and EBITDA in Q2, successfully pivoting to an Ex-US-led growth model while navigating US-specific regulatory and supply chain headwinds.
