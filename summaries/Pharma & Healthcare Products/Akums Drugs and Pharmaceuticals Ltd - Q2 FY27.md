# Akums Drugs and Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit volume growth in CDMO, improved profitability, and successful integration of new acquisitions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,342 crore (+31.9% YoY, +15.1% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 195 crore (+106% YoY, +11.5% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 14.5% (vs 9.3% in Q2 FY26; 15% in Q1 FY27).
*   **PAT:** Rs 115 crore (+168.3% YoY, +13.5% QoQ).
*   **CDMO Revenue:** Rs 1,115 crore (+38.6% YoY, +15.6% QoQ).
*   **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 1,680 crore.

### Highlights
*   **CDMO Performance:** Driven by >20% volume growth on a September MAT basis. Operating at >50% utilization.
*   **Acquisitions:** Successfully integrated two manufacturing plants acquired from Oriflame to strengthen beauty and personal care play.
*   **Digital Transformation:** Successfully transitioned to S/4HANA for real-time analytics.
*   **Sustainability:** Awarded EcoVadis Bronze medal and CII Water Management Champion Award.
*   **Trade Generics:** Achieved profitability for the third consecutive quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Domestic Branded Formulations:** Management expects growth on par with IPM by FY28.
*   **Zambia JV:** Commercial supplies from the Indian plant scheduled for Q4 FY27; revenue booking expected in Q1 FY28.
*   **Europe CDMO:** Supplies expected to commence in FY28; dossier filed, approval expected in 1-2 quarters.
*   **API Segment:** Management aims for breakeven in the next 3-4 quarters.
*   **Baddi Facility:** Phase 1 (oral solids, 6 billion units capacity) to be fully operational by Q1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   CDMO demand remains strong despite global headwinds and input material volatility.
*   Management noted that API prices have risen 5-7% YoY and 2-3% QoQ, with a slight upward trajectory expected in the near term.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   R&D expenditure is ~3% of CDMO revenue (approx. Rs 130 crore).
*   Domestic branded business EBITDA margin was impacted by investments in manpower (increased field force to 1,600).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **H1 FY27 Capex:** Rs 210 crore (includes Oriflame acquisition and Baddi plant investment).
*   **Baddi Facility:** Phase 1 capacity of 6 billion tablets; total capacity planned at 12 billion tablets.
*   **Zambia Plant:** Technical discussions complete; operational target is 2029.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Free cash flow for H1 FY27 was Rs 45.5 crore.
*   Operating cash flow to EBITDA ratio stands at 69.2%.
*   Management indicated flexibility to use internal funds or debt for future capex, depending on cost-benefit analysis.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory/Legal:** Addressed a show-cause notice (FY19-25) regarding NPPA, stating the company has no financial liability as it acts as a job worker for a marketing company.
*   **API Losses:** Segment currently bleeding ~Rs 5 crore per quarter.
*   **Input Costs:** Volatility in raw materials, KSMs, and solvents.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Zambia Revenue Recognition:** Management clarified that while goods will be dispatched in Q4 FY27, revenue will be booked in Q1 FY28 due to shipping timelines to the Lusaka warehouse.
*   **Oriflame Contribution:** Management stated that PAT contribution from the Oriflame acquisition is currently "negligible" as they are in the process of building customers and revenue.
*   **CDMO Volume Drivers:** Management attributed the 20% volume growth to a mix of existing and new customers across prescription, private label, and regional segments.
*   **Domestic Branded PCPM:** PCPM is currently 2.5; management noted a temporary dip due to the addition of 200 new field force members, but mature divisions operate above 3.
*   **API Strategy:** Management confirmed they are pruning low-margin products and focusing on general products to achieve breakeven.

### One-line takeaway
Akums delivered strong Q2 results led by robust CDMO volume growth and is strategically positioning for future expansion via the Baddi facility, European market entry, and the Zambia JV.
