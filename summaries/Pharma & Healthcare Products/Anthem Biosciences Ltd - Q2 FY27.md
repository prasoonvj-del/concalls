# Anthem Biosciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust performance with strong consolidated revenue and margin expansion driven by cost efficiencies, yield optimization, and workforce productivity.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 594 crore (CRDMO: INR 509 crore, Specialty Ingredients: INR 85 crore)
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** INR 1,012 crore (CRDMO: INR 850 crore, Specialty Ingredients: INR 162 crore)
- **EBITDA:** INR 330 crore (includes other income of INR 59 crore; Q2 FY26 other income was INR 48 crore)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 50.6%
- **H1 EBITDA:** INR 506 crore (includes other income of INR 84 crore; H1 FY26 other income was INR 70 crore)
- **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 46.2%
- **PBT:** INR 296 crore
- **PAT:** INR 211 crore
- **PAT Margin:** 32.04%
- **H1 PAT:** INR 331 crore
- **H1 PAT Margin:** 30.2%
- **Net Cash Position:** INR 1,534 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- **Commercial Molecules:** 15 (up from 14)
- **Late-Stage Molecules:** 9 (down from 10)
- **Geographic Split (International vs Domestic):** ~84% international (Europe ~60%, U.S. 22-24%, ROW 1-2%), ~16% India domestic
- **Segment Split (CRDMO vs Specialty Ingredients):** ~85% CRDMO (65% commercial manufacturing, 20% R&D and development supplies), ~15% Specialty Ingredients
- **Customer Concentration:** Top 5 ~50%, Top 10 ~80%
- **Procurement Sourcing:** Domestic ~75-78%, China ~20-21%
- **Capex Guidance:** INR 1,200 crore for Unit 4 Phase 1 (intact)

### Highlights
- Delivered robust Q2 revenue and expanded EBITDA and PAT margins YoY through strict discipline around cost efficiencies, yield optimization, and workforce productivity.
- One late-stage program approved during the quarter, moving total commercial molecules to 15.
- Successfully reduced reliance on Chinese procurement over the last 18 months, with domestic procurement now at 75-78%.
- First patent expiry for key molecules is not until 2032.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of sustaining strong performance and high margins for the full year, aiming to deliver historical double-digit growth rates despite a low Q1.
- Unit 4 Phase 1 capex guidance of INR 1,200 crore remains intact; facility setup is equivalent to Units 1, 2, and 3 put together in Phase 1.
- Specialty Ingredients segment is targeted to remain around 20% of sales annually.
- Aspirationally targeting an asset turn of 1.5x.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying demand remains strong; seeing a better inflow of queries, RFPs, and RFQs, though biotech funding revival is a mixed bag amid geopolitical uncertainty.
- Customers increasingly view Anthem as a solution provider for complex molecules, intellectual contribution, and IP generation rather than just a contract manufacturer.
- New modalities like oligonucleotides, peptides, ADCs, and RNAi have been a focus for over a decade, with projects moving from lab scale to commercial scale.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin improvement driven by a mix of factors, including reduced reliance on Chinese sourcing, complete in-house manufacturing, and product mix.
- Q2 margin uptick supported by operating leverage following a lower Q1.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Unit 4 (30-acre large-scale facility) Phase 1 capex guidance remains INR 1,200 crore, with commissioning expected around March 2028.
- Phase 2 of Unit 4 will be equivalent in size to Phase 1.
- Planned investments include biologics manufacturing and drug product (fill-finish) capabilities, oligonucleotides, and cybersecurity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position stands at INR 1,534 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainty causes unease and impacts mindsets.
- Clinical trial failures in late-stage programs remain a risk as outcomes are dependent on trial results.
- The impact and unknown trajectory of AI in drug discovery was noted as a potential wild card, though management believes it could increase overall molecule pipelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gourav Bhama asked about the movement of a late-stage molecule to commercial and prospects.** Management (Gawir Baig & Ajay Bhardwaj) stated the approval occurred in Q2 FY27 from an emerging biotech portfolio in a chronic care category, but declined to provide specific indications or peak sales due to client confidentiality.
2. **Sidharth Negandhi asked for revenue salience (geography, CRO/CDMO split, customer concentration) and China supply updates.** Management (Gawir Baig) shared that international revenue is 84% (Europe ~60%, US 22-24%), CRDMO is 85% (65% commercial), top 5 customers represent ~50%, and Chinese procurement dropped to 20-21% while domestic procurement rose to 75-78%.
3. **Bansi Desai asked about the commercial potential of recent launches versus patent expiries and preferred supplier status.** Management (Ajay Bhardwaj) noted patent expiries begin in 2032, new launches address a market potential exceeding $10 billion, and Anthem currently enjoys sole supplier status on these products before big pharma brings in second sources.
4. **Roni Naik asked about peptide exposure beyond GLP-1 and capex allocation for NCE vs NBE.** Management (Ajay Bhardwaj) stated they are doing extensive peptide work outside metabolic therapies (oncology, immunology) and expect 30-35% of capex to go into biology.
5. **Saion Mukherjee asked about headroom in older molecules and traction for lateral/second-source contracts.** Management (Ajay Bhardwaj) noted older molecules are still growth products with headroom up to 2032-2040, and while mass switching to second sources takes time, being qualified as an approved second supplier is a major win for future ramp-ups.

### One-line takeaway
Anthem Biosciences delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with a consolidated revenue of INR 594 crore and an EBITDA margin of 50.6%, supported by strong execution in complex CRDMO programs and margin optimization.
