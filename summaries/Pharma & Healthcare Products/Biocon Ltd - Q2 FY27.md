# Biocon Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management described the company as being at a "defining inflection point," citing strong H2 visibility, new contract wins, and operational leverage.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue Growth (YoY):** 2% (Q2); 6% (H1).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 865 crore.
*   **Net Profit (before exceptionals):** Rs 44 crore.
*   **Interest Costs:** Rs 240 crore (down 12% YoY).
*   **Biosimilars Revenue:** Rs 2,809 crore (H1 growth 9% YoY).
*   **Generics Revenue:** Rs 774 crore.
*   **Generics EBITDA:** Rs 64 crore (vs Rs 7 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Services Revenue:** Rs 833 crore (13% QoQ growth).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Wins:** Secured a global settlement for Pebrilzo (Pertuzumab) with Genentech; launches expected in EU/UK from early 2027 and US from Q3 2028.
*   **Brazil Contract:** Awarded a 10-year, $400 million contract for Pertuzumab under Brazil's PDP program; supplies begin Q4 FY27.
*   **North America:** Added over $100 million in new contracts and payor wins. Yesintek (Ustekinumab) achieved 34% market share.
*   **Revenue Deferral:** Approximately 5% of quarterly revenue was deferred due to logistics/geopolitical issues; these were booked in October.
*   **Leadership:** Announced upcoming transitions for COO (George Kaliviotis) and CTO (Mukesh Rathi) effective 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1.
*   Biosimilars EBITDA margins are expected to remain in the mid-20s range.
*   Management targets double-digit growth (in dollar terms).
*   Syngene (Services) expects business trajectory to improve as the year progresses.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Insulin:** Demand remains strong; capacity expansion in Malaysia (second fill-finish line) is supporting growth.
*   **Generics:** GLP-1 portfolio (liraglutide) remains a key growth driver.
*   **Biosimilars Strategy:** Management emphasized balancing profitability and volume over chasing market share, particularly in the Part B (medical benefit) space.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Generics:** EBITDA margin improved to 8% (from 1% YoY).
*   **Services:** EBITDA margin improved to 20% (from 15% in Q1).
*   **H1 Headwinds:** $5 million in freight costs and $5 million in solvent costs were subsumed in margins; management expects these to ease in coming quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Commissioned second fill-finish line in Malaysia for insulin.
*   Focus remains on utilizing existing capacity and improving operating leverage.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Interest costs declined 12% YoY to Rs 240 crore due to balance sheet strengthening.
*   Management remains focused on debt reduction and improving ROCE.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical situation causing logistics delays and revenue deferrals.
*   Competitive pressure in biosimilars (Part B vs Part D dynamics).
*   Syngene navigating reduced demand from a large biologics client.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Surya Patra (Phillip Capital):** Asked about the $100m contract wins. Management clarified these are driven by new product launches (e.g., Yesintek) and confirmed Pertuzumab revenue starts in Q4 FY27.
*   **Charul Agrawal (BofA):** Asked if revenue deferrals were due to new products. Management clarified it was a one-off logistics issue, not product-specific.
*   **Kenil Mehta (Boring AMC):** Asked about Semaglutide fermentation vs. chemical synthesis. Management stated the fermentation route offers superior cost advantages and scalability.
*   **Tushar Manudhane (Motilal Oswal):** Asked about growth scale-up. Management reiterated confidence in double-digit growth in dollar terms.

### One-line takeaway
Biocon is transitioning to commercial acceleration, supported by new product launches (Pertuzumab, Yesafili) and operational efficiencies, with management guiding for a stronger H2.
