# Sudeep Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA, supported by strong core growth and margin expansion despite elevated sea freight costs and a temporary pause in the Fortified Rice program.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 196.7 crore (up 21% YoY, 24% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 355 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 73.2 crore (up 32% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 128.1 crore (up 29% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 64.7% (vs 62.5% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 37.2% (up over 300 bps YoY from 34.1%; 34.7% in Q1 FY27)
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 51.8 crore (up 11% YoY, 28% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 92.4 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 26.3%
- **Specialty Ingredients Revenue:** INR 54 crore (up 9% QoQ, down 25% YoY)
- **PFN Revenue:** INR 142 crore (up 57% YoY; contributed ~72% of consolidated revenue)
- **PFN EBITDA Margin:** ~41% (vs 33% YoY)
- **Selling and Distribution Expenses:** ~INR 8 crore above normal average due to elevated sea freight
- **Net Working Capital Days:** 214 days as of 30 September 2026 (same as March 2026)
- **Capex in FY27 (H1/YTD):** INR 77 crore (towards Battery Materials at Dahej and greenfield facility)
- **Net Debt:** INR 72.5 crore as of 30 September 2026 (vs INR 33.6 crore as of 31 March 2026)
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.07x (vs 0.04x as of 31 March 2026)
- **Annualized ROCE (H1 FY27):** 22.4%
- **Annualized ROE (H1 FY27):** 19%
- **Exports vs Domestic (Q2):** Exports 65% (up 24% YoY), Domestic 35%

### Highlights
- Delivered highest ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA.
- Nandesari greenfield facility received US FDA approval; annual capacity of 51,000 tonnes covering PFN and newer categories (glycinates, gluconates, citrates, phosphates).
- Magnesium bisglycinate (AbsorBis range / MaxZobis brand) average realizations are >3x the PFN portfolio average; commercial supplies started for select customers, with larger volumes expected from Q4.
- Government of India's Fortified Rice program temporarily paused in Q2 (contributed INR 40-50 crore last year; roughly 20-25% of overall Specialty Ingredients revenue).
- NSS secured 5 customer approvals and has ~12 customer projects in evaluation/development; shifting sourcing to India to improve competitiveness.
- Sudeep Advanced Materials (SAM) Dahej project remains on track for commercial operations in April 2027 (Phase 1: 25,000 tonnes of battery-grade iron phosphate).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Specialty Ingredients to return to positive YoY growth in Q3 and Q4, driven by customer contracts and new approvals (without assuming a restart of the Fortified Rice program).
- Management targets bringing the working capital cycle down to 170-180 days (excluding Battery Materials inventory) and eventually to 150 days.
- Management aims for magnesium bisglycinate to become one of the top 3 revenue contributors within PFN over the next 2-3 years (potential >INR 100 crore annual revenue as a single ingredient).
- Management expects to provide a more concrete update on firm binding offtake contracts for Battery Materials in Q4.
- Management targets ~15-20% utilization for the Nandesari greenfield site in FY28, ramping up to 75% optimum utilization over 3-4 years (delivering ~3x asset turnover).
- NSS targets operating closer to a 30% EBITDA margin in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PFN demand is strong across India, Africa, APAC, and Europe, driven by phosphates and higher-value ingredients.
- Phosphates portfolio grew close to 70-80% YoY.
- In Battery Materials, strong demand supports the first 100,000-tonne annual capacity, and customer discussions for the next phase (towards 200,000 tonnes) are progressing faster than expected.
- Sudeep claims to be the first and only company outside China to produce battery-grade iron phosphate at scale, utilizing an indigenous green technology with lower effluent and solid waste.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Elevated sea freight costs increased selling and distribution expenses by ~INR 8 crore, which management absorbed without passing on to customers.
- Effective tax rate rose to 27% (vs ~21% last year) due to increased depreciation from new capitalized assets and a higher revenue mix from PFN (tax rate ~28%).
- Purified phosphoric acid (key raw material) saw a significant price upswing; management strategically stocked inventory in Q1 to cover Q2 and part of Q3 needs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Nandesari greenfield facility (51,000 tonnes) capitalized and put to use in Q2; FDA approval received.
- Phase 1 of Dahej Battery Materials project has an annual capacity of 25,000 tonnes, scaling toward 100,000 tonnes and planned expansion up to 200,000 tonnes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flow from operations for H1 FY27 reached INR 43.5 crore (up INR 26 crore YoY).
- Strategic stocking of purified phosphoric acid and battery project materials kept net working capital at 214 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary pause in the Government of India's Fortified Rice program with no visibility on a restart during the financial year.
- Elevated sea freight costs impacting logistics expenses.
- NSS had a softer quarter with lower revenue and earnings.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sanjesh Jain (ICICI Securities) asked about the underlying growth of Specialty Ingredients excluding Fortified Rice and segment margin divergence.** Management replied that fortified rice accounts for 20-25% of specialty revenue, but non-fortified premix demand in Q3/Q4 will offset the loss. Segment margins were depressed due to low premix revenue and NSS drag, but will recover.
2. **Mannat (Dhamma Capital) asked about the go-to-market strategy and capacity allocation for the bisglycinate business.** Management stated that ~50% of volume will go to very large tolling/contract manufacturing companies in the US and globally (with whom Sudeep has approvals), and 50% to diversified smaller accounts, targeting 70-80% of the fungible greenfield capacity for bisglycinate.
3. **Nirali Shah (Ashika Investment Managers) asked what differentiates Sudeep's battery-grade iron phosphate from competitors.** Management highlighted that Sudeep is the first and only non-Chinese player at scale, using a proprietary green technology that reduces effluent by 90% and solid waste by 95% compared to conventional Chinese processes.
4. **Tanmay (Finterest Capital) asked about the greenfield facility's ramp-up and expected asset turnover.** Management expects 15-20% utilization in FY28 and 75% optimum utilization in 3-4 years, yielding roughly 3x asset turnover.
5. **Gagan (Emkay Global) asked if Phase 2 of Battery Materials starts only after binding contracts are signed and how non-China sourcing of phosphoric acid is secured.** Management stated binding commitments are targeted for Q4 (before April 2027 commissioning), and ex-China phosphoric acid is secured from India, Morocco, Israel, Vietnam, and Mexico.

### One-line takeaway
Sudeep Pharma reported a record Q2 FY27 with 21% revenue and 32% EBITDA growth, bolstered by robust PFN demand and margin expansion, while securing US FDA approval for its Nandesari greenfield facility and advancing toward its April 2027 battery materials commercialization.
