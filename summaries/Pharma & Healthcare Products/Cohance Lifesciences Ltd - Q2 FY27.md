# Cohance Lifesciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Sequential operational recovery in revenue and adjusted EBITDA, but year-on-year performance remains lower due to headwinds in Pharma CDMO and AgChem, alongside recent quality audits and a facility closure order.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR4,767 million (up 13% QoQ)
- Adjusted EBITDA: INR458 million (adjusted EBITDA margin at 10%)
- H1 Capex: a little over INR1 billion (excluding acquisitions/ownership investments)
- Cash and liquid investments: INR2.9 billion against borrowings of INR2.3 billion

### Highlights
- Pharma CDMO led sequential recovery with revenues growing 34.5% QoQ, driven by small molecules.
- Secured three significant lateral programs in small molecules (two commercial, one phase III) from large global pharma customers.
- Sapala (nucleic acids) grew 33.6% YoY in Q2 and 83% in H1.
- NJ Bio successfully delivered two ADC GMP campaigns and is progressing toward breakeven; bioconjugation capacity expansion remains on track for early FY28 commercial availability.
- USFDA inspections completed at Pashamylaram, Shamshabad, and Jaggayyapeta with CAPA responses submitted.
- *Record comparison:* Unlike prior quarters of steady declines, management highlighted sequential improvement, though a recent closure order at the Suryapet facility introduces a new operational risk not present in previous calls.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to deliver year-on-year growth on both revenue and EBITDA.
- Management expects Specialty Chemical revenue to decline YoY in FY27 due to legacy AgChem pressure and generic headwinds.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Small molecule order and reload position for Q3 and Q4 has improved.
- ADCs experienced customer destocking in H1, but the development funnel and order visibility are improving for H2.
- Generic API pricing environment remains relatively stable (neither massively rising nor falling).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 10% in Q2, up from INR92 million in Q1.
- Higher raw material prices affected select API products; selective price pass-throughs and process cost improvements are underway.
- Inventory increased to INR7.3 billion (from INR5.6 billion) to support expected H2 orders.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex deployed: a little over INR1 billion.
- Investments focus on ADC payload-linkers, bioconjugation adjacencies, and enhanced R&D infrastructure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position: cash and liquid investments of INR2.9 billion vs borrowings of INR2.3 billion.
- Receivables improved materially during H1; inventory increased to support scheduled H2 orders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Suryapet facility received a closure order/notice; management is assessing the impact and engaging with authorities.
- USFDA inspections concluded across multiple facilities, awaiting final regulatory classifications.
- Persistent generic pricing pressure and lower AgChem CDMO volumes impacting Specialty Chemicals.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie Capital) on new commercial small molecule projects:** Management stated both are patent-protected RSMs from large innovators, expected to contribute in Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28.
- **Sajal Kapoor (Investor) on Suryapet revenue share and H2 guidance:** Management stated it is too early to assess, noting the plant is a non-GMP facility and relatively small, while focus remains on regulatory engagement.
- **Sajal Kapoor (Investor) on NJ Bio subsidiary cost drop:** Management clarified that Q2 had limited restructuring costs and no further costs are anticipated in H2.
- **Shivlal Khandelwal (Investor) on USFDA inspections impact on H2:** Management stated they see no impact currently and are awaiting FDA classification outcomes over the next 1 to 3 months.
- **Shyam Srinivasan (Investor) on API+ pricing environment:** Management noted the generic API pricing environment is relatively stable, with no massive increases or decreases.

### One-line takeaway
Cohance delivered a sequential recovery in Q2 FY27 led by Pharma CDMO and nucleic acids, with management guiding for YoY growth in H2 despite regulatory overhangs and plant closure risks.
