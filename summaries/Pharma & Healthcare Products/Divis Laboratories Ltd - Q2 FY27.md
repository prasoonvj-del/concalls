# Divis Laboratories Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong top-line and bottom-line expansion driven by Custom Synthesis growth, raw material cost efficiencies, and robust customer traction in peptides.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 3,770 crore (+32% YoY vs Rs 2,860 crore).
- **Profit Before Tax (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,505 crore (+65% YoY vs Rs 912 crore).
- **Profit After Tax (Q2 FY27):** Rs 1,127 crore (+64% YoY vs Rs 689 crore).
- **Total Income (H1 FY27):** Rs 6,907 crore (+28% YoY vs Rs 5,389 crore).
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 2,029 crore (+64% YoY vs Rs 1,234 crore).
- **Standalone Revenue Growth (H1 FY27):** 17% YoY on a constant currency basis.
- **H1 Business Mix:** Generics 38%, Custom Synthesis (CS) 62%.
- **Geographic Mix:** Exports ~90% of standalone sales; Europe and North America ~75%.
- **Nutraceutical Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 571 crore (vs Rs 492 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Net Material Consumption:** ~32% of sales in H1 FY27 (vs 40% in H1 FY26).
- **Capitalization:** Rs 772 crore capitalized in Q2 FY27; Rs 1,223 crore in H1 FY27. CWIP stands at Rs 1,638 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Highlights
- Custom Synthesis contributed 62% to H1 FY27 sales, propelled by ongoing validation batches and commercialization of previously validated programs.
- Generic segment volumes remained stable, offsetting persistent pricing pressures in key categories.
- Material consumption costs improved to 32% of revenue from 40% YoY, benefiting from higher CS mix and backward integration operations at Unit 3 (Kakinada).
- *Record Context:* Performance builds on the strong Q2 FY27 reported results (PAT of Rs 1,127 crore, up 64% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guides for double-digit revenue growth to continue in H2 FY27.
- Annual capex is expected to be around Rs 2,000 crore (±20%) over the next 2–3 years based on strong RFP traction.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and Red Sea/Suez Canal transit disruptions continue to push up ocean/air freight rates and extend lead times.
- Solvents and petrochemical-based key input materials face price volatility and remain elevated.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Company maintains a 3-month rolling inventory cover for critical inputs to prevent supply disruptions.
- Gross margin improvement was driven by elevated CS execution and strategic backward integration in Kakinada freeing up efficiencies.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Installing multiple multi-ton 3,000-litre Solid Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS) lines to meet demand across innovation-led customer programs.
- Three major dedicated capex programs are progressing through validation; one project is near commercialization, while two others are expected to commercialize over the next 1–2 quarters.
- Unit 1 holds 22 production blocks, Unit 2 holds 25, and Unit 3 holds 6; only 3 blocks are dedicated, leaving the rest as flexible multipurpose capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balances stood at Rs 2,181 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Trade receivables were Rs 3,705 crore; inventories stood at Rs 5,325 crore.
- Operating cash flows were impacted by strategic inventory stocking and heavy ongoing capital investment disbursements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated shipping costs, extended transit times, and petrochemical input cost inflation caused by geopolitical conflict.
- Regulatory inspections or qualification delays could affect the exact commercialization timeline of dedicated projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amey Chalke (JM Financial):** Asked about the revenue and margin sustainability from validation batches in the Custom Synthesis segment.  
  *Management:* Validation batches are part of an ongoing process. Once validated, products move to regulatory filing and qualification. It is difficult to split validation revenues, but CS overall forms 62% of H1 sales.
- **Surya Narayan Patra:** Inquired about the drivers of raw material cost reductions and GLP-1 peptide progress.  
  *Management:* Cost reductions stem from higher CS share and backward integration at Unit 3 Kakinada. SPPS 3,000-litre reactors are being installed based on customer requirements; Divi's focuses strictly on innovator/innovative peptides, not generic peptides.
- **Vivek Agrawal:** Asked regarding total SPPS capacity installed and liquid phase peptide scaling.  
  *Management:* NDA constraints prevent sharing exact reactor capacity, but Divi's aims to be among the top three global players in futuristic peptide capacity. Liquid phase peptide capabilities are fully established and scalable via existing pilot infrastructure.
- **Shyam Srinivasan:** Inquired about LOE impact on key customer products and lower cash conversion relative to PBT.  
  *Management:* Declined to comment on specific customer molecules under NDA. Lower cash flow conversion is due to large ongoing capex and protective raw material stocking.
- **Neha Manpuria:** Asked if capacity bottlenecks exist for FY29/FY30 given high utilization levels.  
  *Management:* No capacity shortages are foreseen; capex is proactively distributed across Units 1, 2, and 3 based on long-term customer contracts and visibility.

### One-line takeaway
Divi’s Laboratories delivered strong Q2 FY27 profit growth of 64% YoY, anchored by high-margin Custom Synthesis execution and expanding peptide manufacturing infrastructure.
