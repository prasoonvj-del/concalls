# Gufic Biosciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady progress on Indore regulatory milestones, higher output across lines, and growth in international and domestic segments despite raw material and transport pressures.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained 15%-20% YoY revenue growth.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to acknowledge near-term inventory build-up and raw material price pressures, alongside regulatory readiness at Indore.
- New topics or risks: U.S. FDA pre-approval inspection at Indore with 3 non-critical observations; rupee devaluation and transport cost impacts on gross margins; strategic inventory buffering for geopolitical supply chain risks.
- Last quarter's promises: Indore facility underwent US FDA pre-approval inspection and submitted EU compliance reports as anticipated; aesthetic fillers launch remains on track for December/January.

### Highlights
- **Indore Facility:** Registered with the U.S. FDA; underwent pre-approval inspection with 3 non-critical observations (compliance submitted). EU GMP compliance report submitted; awaiting certification. Hosted audits from U.S. and U.K. prospective partners. Depot suite ready for trials and media fills; spray-dried lipid antifungal and injectable emulsion formats progressing.
- **International Business:** Received 3 registrations and first orders in Saudi Arabia; registration and commercial order in Chile; first orders for antifungals in U.K. and Germany; entered Italy and expanded Gufic Ireland distribution to 5 European countries.
- **Domestic & Hospital Injectables:** AWABAC launched in June expanded into corporate/tertiary hospitals. Sparsh division mostly consists of own-manufactured brands. Dual-chamber bags (DCB) continuing acceptance; introduced prefilled syringe Cetrocare and pain management allopathic brand Napro Pure. Stunnoxtoxin completed 5 years; initiated 3 clinical studies.
- **R&D & Pipeline:** 17 active and pipeline studies.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets 15%-20% YoY revenue growth.
- Management targets a 1% YoY improvement in EBITDA margins, aiming for 22%-25% in the near future.
- Indore plant is EBITDA breakeven currently; management expects it to cover depreciation and interest by Q4.
- U.S. commercial entry from Indore anticipated by Q3 FY28; European entry targeted by September 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic business growing at ~12% YoY, international at ~20%-22%, and CMO at ~4%-5%.
- Regulated markets (Europe, South Africa, Brazil, Colombia, U.K., etc.) comprise 65%-70% of revenue.
- India market remains price-sensitive, making new delivery system adoption gradual.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin dipped from 18.1% (implied earlier/Q1) to 17.23% due to rupee devaluation increasing imported API costs and higher domestic/international transport costs.
- Management expects API and transport cost pressures to normalize by Q4.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital expenditure of Rs 20 crore-Rs 30 crore per year funded entirely from internal accruals.
- EUR 1.5 million spent this year on international product registrations/dossiers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased due to strategic buffering (holding Rs 40 crore-Rs 50 crore extra inventory due to Middle East/Iran war supply chain risks), expected to normalize from ~Rs 370 crore down to Rs 325 crore-Rs 330 crore by March 2027.
- Debtor days improved from 108 days to 100 days.
- No long-term loans taken in the last year; semaglutide and Indore investments funded via internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain vulnerabilities and high prices for certain APIs (meropenems, hormones) and utilities.
- Price erosion in hospital and critical care segments impacting value growth despite volume growth.
- 3 Form 483 observations received during the U.S. FDA pre-approval inspection at Indore.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Pal asked about gross margin dip and Indore breakeven.** Management attributed it to rupee devaluation raising API import costs and transport expenses, expected to normalize by Q4. Indore is EBITDA positive and expected to cover depreciation/interest by Q4.
2. **Vishal Mehta asked about rising debt/working capital and peak revenue potential.** Management explained inventory was built up deliberately by Rs 40 crore-Rs 50 crore as a buffer against supply chain shocks; debtors improved to 100 days. Revenue growth guidance was reiterated at 15%-20% YoY.
3. **Suyash Bhave asked about dossier investment timelines and botulinum toxin revenue.** Management stated EUR 1.5 million-EUR 1.6 million dossier investments will mostly wrap up by March 2027 targeting 156-182 MAs. Botulinum toxin annualized MAT revenue is $3.5 million-$3.6 million, targeted to grow 25%-30% YoY.
4. **Vishal Manchanda asked for segment revenue breakup and EU audit delays.** Management stated mix is ~48%-49% domestic, ~25%-26% export, ~19%-20% CMO, and remainder APIs/tenders. EU delay was caused by repeating a smoke study for Annex 1 compliance.
5. **Dheeraj Kumar Reddy asked about botulinum toxin export plans and peptide work.** Management stated international botulinum expansion requires a dedicated Rs 15 million-Rs 20 million investment which is deferred to focus on existing pipelines. For peptides, both generic (cetrorelix, leuprolide, octreotide, triptorelin) and innovative pipelines are being pursued.

### One-line takeaway
Gufic delivered steady H1/Q2 revenue and profit growth while advancing Indore's regulatory clearances and absorbing temporary API import and transport cost headwinds.
