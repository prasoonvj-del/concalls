# Hexagon Nutrition Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 43.2% YoY driven by demand across domestic and international markets, despite margin pressures from geopolitical issues.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: Rs 104.3 crore (up 43.2% YoY from Rs 72.9 crore).
- Gross profit: Rs 45.8 crore (up 34.1% YoY from Rs 34.1 crore).
- EBITDA: Rs 11.8 crore (up 17.3% YoY from Rs 10.1 crore).
- EBITDA margin: 11.3% (down from 13.8% YoY).
- Profit before tax (PBT): Rs 10.6 crore (up 25.1% YoY).
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 8.1 crore (up 25.1% YoY from Rs 6.5 crore).
- PAT margin: Approximately 7.7%.
- Segment revenue mix: Premix 62%, Branded 28%, ESG/therapeutic 10%.
- Blended capacity utilization: 47% (facility-wise: Nashik 55%-60%, Tuticorin 35%-40%, Chennai 25%-30%).
- Current order book: Rs 104 crore (70%-75% premix and ESG, rest branded; to be executed in Q2 and partly Q3).
- ESG order pipeline for the year: Rs 75 crore to Rs 100 crore.

### Highlights
- First quarterly earnings call following successful listing on BSE and NSE in June 2026.
- Branded business grew 22% YoY (branded portfolio includes PentaSure, PediaGold, Obesigo, and online-focused Nutrone Women).
- Premix segment grew 55% YoY; ESG/therapeutic segment grew 42% YoY.
- Field force expanded by over 50% to increase reach into Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
- Geopolitical situation in West Asia has not materially impacted business; exports diversified with West Asia contributing less than 20% of total exports.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for FY27 revenue growth of 20% to 25% (conservatively positioned).
- Branded business targeted to reach 35% of total revenue within the next couple of years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural growth driven by rising awareness of preventive healthcare, balanced nutrition, immunity, and active lifestyles.
- International growth supported by Central Asia and Africa, with future expansion planned into Europe and North America.
- Uzbekistan facility strategy is to cater to domestic Central Asian demand, neighboring 'stans', and Russia.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin contracted by over 3% YoY due to a rise in critical raw material costs (e.g., whey protein) caused by the West Asia crisis.
- Other expenses increased due to higher selling and distribution costs, specifically ocean freight driven by geopolitical impacts, and investments in field force expansion.
- Management implemented a 10% to 15% MRP hike across select branded products to mitigate raw material cost pressures, with impacts expected from Q2 onwards.
- International raw material procurement stands at 25% to 30%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore over the next 1 to 1.5 years for the redevelopment and demolition of the Nashik factory premix facility. 
- 70% of this capex will be funded via long-term bank borrowings.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory days stood at 142 days in Q1 (compared to 130+ days YoY), driven by strategic stocking of critical raw materials for the ESG and premix order book.
- Operating cash flows and collections improved compared to Q1 FY26.
- No major changes in customer credit terms.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia leading to higher ocean freight costs and rising prices of key raw materials like whey protein.
- Short-term bottom-line impact from investments in field force expansion and higher freight expenses.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sparsh Bedmutha (Perpetual Capital Advisors) asked about export market concentration and capacity utilization.** Management stated top 5 export countries account for 25% to 40%, and blended capacity utilization stands at 47%.
2. **Mayuresh Raut asked about revenue mix evolution and new therapy traction.** Management replied that branded business is targeted to reach 35% of total revenue in the next couple of years; fertility nutrition is being introduced in export markets and Neutron Women is in its infancy online.
3. **Akhilesh asked about the disconnect between Q1 growth (43%) and guidance (20%-25%), and gross margin contraction.** Management stated guidance is conservative, Q2 order book stands at Rs 100 crore, seasonality plays a role (H2 is stronger), and gross margin pressure from raw material inflation is being addressed via 10%-15% MRP hikes.
4. **Abhishek Maheshwari asked about the increase in other expenses.** Management confirmed it was driven by ocean freight increases from the West Asia crisis and strategic investments in field force and marketing.
5. **Varun asked about cash flows, working capital, and FY27 capex.** Management stated operating cash flows improved, inventory days are at 142 due to strategic stock, and FY27-28 capex for the Nashik facility will be Rs 25-30 crore (70% debt-funded).
6. **Yash Parkar asked about growth constraints and D2C channel mix.** Management stated there are no constraints in manufacturing capacity or working capital, distribution is expanding into Tier 2/3 cities, and e-commerce fulfillment is strong (85% delivered within 24 hours).

### One-line takeaway
Hexagon Nutrition delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 43.2% revenue growth, though EBITDA margins faced temporary headwinds from raw material inflation and freight costs that management is mitigating through recent MRP hikes and operational scale.
