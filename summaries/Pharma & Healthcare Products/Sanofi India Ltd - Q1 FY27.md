# Sanofi India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 2026 (Quarter and half year ended 30th June 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit growth in the diabetes business and profit improvement offsetting one-off impacts in partnership and export segments.

### Key numbers
- Total Income: +7% YoY for Q2; YTD H1 at -4% YoY
- Sales (Domestic & Export): +6% YoY for Q2; YTD H1 at -2% YoY
- Domestic Sales: +8% YoY for Q2
- Export Sales: -2% YoY for Q2; same trend in YTD H1
- Diabetes Portfolio Growth: +17% YoY for H1; Q2 diabetes business up by close to 14% YoY
- Soliqua Growth: +16% quarter-by-quarter
- Profit Before Tax and Exceptional Items (PBT): +19% YoY for Q2 (from Rs 94 crore to Rs 112 crore)
- PBT as % of Net Sales: 27% for Q2 (up from 24% YoY)
- Operating Expenses: -5% YoY for Q2; -15% YoY for YTD H1
- Cash Generation: Up by 34% YoY

### Highlights
- Diabetes portfolio powered by innovative portfolio (Toujeo and Soliqua); Lantus volume acceleration at +6%, Toujeo value market share up by 11%.
- Sanofi holds 47% market share by value in the overall diabetes market and 58% in value (61% in volume) in the basal analog segment.
- Public sector expansion (CGHS, ESI, Railway, State, Army) drove ~70% growth in public sector diabetes care.
- CSR projects on track to reach 600,000+ direct beneficiaries by end of 2026, including an MoU signed with NHM in Madhya Pradesh.
- Goa manufacturing site specializing in certain products to offset mature product challenges in Australia by expanding into France, Italy, Turkey, and Russia.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H1 diabetes momentum to repeat in the second half of 2026.
- Management expects dividend payout ratio not to be lower than past levels, though no forward-looking commitment is made.
- Partnership growth with Emcure and Cipla is not expected to reach industry growth levels in 2026; 2027 is currently being evaluated.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Diabetes remains a progressive disease with underdiagnosis opportunities (100 million patients in India).
- GLP-1 market increased share of voice, acting as a complementary therapy alongside basal insulins (Toujeo and Lantus) and driving Soliqua growth (+16%).
- Novo Nordisk’s once-weekly basal insulin (Awiqli) launch is viewed as a welcoming sign that expands awareness and opens up insulin initiation for hesitant patients, while once-daily insulins serve different patient profiles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Financial discipline and operational excellence led to a 5% reduction in Q2 opex and 15% reduction in H1 opex, focusing on employee costs and other opex.
- PBT margin improved by 300 bps YoY to 27% in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Goa site being specialized to manufacture worldwide for certain products and working to bring back outsourced products from CMOs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash reserves grew by 34% YoY.
- Healthy financial situation with no major risks.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Partnership business (CV, CNS) showing subdued growth (Q2 up 2% YoY) due to high competition and the absence of 2025 transition one-offs (safety stock build-up and sales returns).
- Heavy competition and mature product pressures in the Australian export market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rajkumar Vaidyanathan (RK Investments) asked why the partnership business (CV, CNS) showed anemic growth (2% in Q2) and whether partners are sidelining Sanofi products.** Management attributed the slowdown to 2025 transition one-offs (safety stock and sales returns) and high market competition, but clarified that partners (Emcure and Cipla) have dedicated standalone teams and are not sidelining Sanofi products.
- **Rusmik Oza (9 Rays) asked about dividend payout sustainability.** Management stated that while no forward-looking commitment is made, cash generation is up 34% and payouts are expected to remain in line with past trends.
- **Divyaxa Agnihotri asked about the threat of new entrants like Novo Nordisk's Awiqli and long-term insulin growth sustainability.** Management stated that once-weekly and once-daily insulins cater to different patient profiles, and the new entry will increase overall market awareness without negatively impacting the existing sticky basal insulin base.
- **Kartik Bane asked about the overall insulin market growth trend post-GLP-1.** Management noted that while GLP-1 impacted initial phases, insulin remains the gold standard, and analog insulin continues to see double-digit growth.
- **Yasser Lakdawala asked about utilizing cash for an open-market buyback.** Management answered that buybacks are not currently part of the strategy and are matters for Board discussion.

### One-line takeaway
Sanofi India delivered a strong Q2 FY26 with a 19% increase in PBT and double-digit growth in its diabetes franchise, successfully absorbing transition-related headwinds in its partnership segment.
