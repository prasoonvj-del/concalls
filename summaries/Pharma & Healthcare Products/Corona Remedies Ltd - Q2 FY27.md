# Corona Remedies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong and consistent performance, outperforming the Indian pharmaceutical market driven by volume growth and core therapy strength.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 441 crore, up 22.2% YoY (vs Rs 361 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Growth of 22% YoY. Excluding acquired brand Wokadine, growth was 21.5% for both Q2 and H1 FY27.
- **India Business Revenue Growth:** 22.9% YoY for Q2 FY27 and 22.8% for H1 FY27 (contributed 97% of total revenue).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 96 crore, up 22.3% YoY (vs Rs 78.5 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** Stable at 21.7%. H1 FY27 EBITDA margin stood at 21.9% (EBITDA grew 27.5% YoY in H1).
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 60 crore, up 15.3% YoY (vs Rs 52 crore in Q2 FY26). PAT margin was 13.7%.
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 120 crore, up 22.2% YoY; PAT margin at 13.9%.
- **ROCE (H1 FY27):** 49.8%.
- **ROE (H1 FY27):** 32.9%.
- **International Exports:** Rs 12.6 crore for Q2 and Rs 25.3 crore for H1 FY27; expected to close the year around Rs 60 crore to Rs 65 crore.
- **Medical Representatives (MR):** 3,111 total MRs (including 400 added in Q4 FY26; 46 dedicated to the IVF task force).
- **IVF Task Force PCPM:** Reached ~Rs 7 lakh PCPM in the first 6 months, expected to reach Rs 8 lakh to Rs 9 lakh by year-end.
- **NLEM Contribution:** 7.5% within Corona.

### Highlights
- Ranked 14th in the covered market and 26th overall in the Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM).
- Chronic business grew by ~29%, delivering nearly 2x the growth of the corresponding market segment (14.9%).
- Wokadine integration and business model changes are underway; management remains confident in achieving a 25% CAGR for Wokadine over 3 to 4 years.
- Secured EU GMP and ANVISA clearances for international operations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained revenue guidance of 1.5x the IPM growth (implying ~15% revenue growth based on a 10-12% IPM growth assumption).
- PAT growth guidance stands at 20%.
- International business expected to grow at ~15% next year.
- International revenue projected to remain less than 10% of total revenue even after 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- IPM delivered low to mid-double-digit growth since December 2025, registering a monthly growth of 13.5% in September 2026 as per PharmaTrac.
- Growth is increasingly driven by volume, new introductions, and price increases.
- Chronic segment of IPM is growing faster at 14.9% (25% higher than the overall market).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Moderate PAT growth relative to revenue is attributable to higher depreciation and amortization charges from newly commissioned facilities and brand acquisitions.
- Infusion of 400+ medical reps in the previous quarter impacted operating margins temporarily.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Entered new therapies including infertility (IVF), nephrology, gastroenterology, spine, rheumatology, and CNS.
- IVF task force covers ~2,500 professionals (75-80% of the target market); no immediate plans to expand the MR team further until late CY27 or early FY28.
- Investing in hormone dossiers (10-12 in preparation) targeting Europe, Brazil, Mexico, CIS, Russia, LATAM, and Asia over a 2-3 year horizon, backed by backward integration via a stake in La Chandra (hormonal API player) and a forward plant in Ahmedabad.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific balance sheet or debt figures reported, other than strong return ratios (ROCE 49.8%, ROE 32.9%).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Integration and business model changes for Wokadine were noted as slightly slower than expected.
- Regulatory environment regarding NLEM price controls and trade margin caps; management stated NLEM contribution is low at 7.5% and they are currently unaffected by trade margin caps.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pratik Dharmshi (Union Mutual Fund) on new therapies strategy:** Management explained that infertility is being streamlined currently, and other therapies will be pursued organically or inorganically. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Kushal Shah on Wokadine and EBITDA margins:** Management stated Wokadine base sales were maintained with a 25% CAGR target over 3-4 years, and clarified they do not give explicit EBITDA margin guidance. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Rahul Jeewani on IVF PCPM and MR additions:** Management noted PCPM reached Rs 7 lakh with 46 MRs covering ~2,500 doctors, and no further team expansion is planned for CY27. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Alankar Garude on GLP-1 (Semaglutide) market expectations:** Management stated the semaglutide market is estimated at Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,400 crore in its first year, and Corona remains in the top 10 players. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Sidharth Negandhi on hormone therapy selection rationale:** Management highlighted their strong position in women's healthcare in India, backward integration via La Chandra, and long-term potential in global hormonal markets. *(Directly answered)*
6. **Rahul Jeewani on NLEM price control risks:** Management stated NLEM contribution is around 7.5% and top brand SKUs have minimal overlap, minimizing direct risk. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Corona Remedies reported a robust Q2 FY27 with 22% revenue growth and 22.9% growth in its India business, comfortably outperforming the broader pharmaceutical market through strong chronic segment traction and core brand execution.
