# Indoco Remedies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong revenue growth and international performance were offset by domestic seasonal headwinds and elevated freight costs impacting margins.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 506.9 crore (up 7.4% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 438.1 crore (up 2% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 46.4 crore (9.2% margin).
*   **Domestic Formulation Revenue:** Rs 212.8 crore.
*   **International Formulation Revenue:** Rs 172.0 crore (up 12.1% YoY).
*   **Regulated Markets Revenue:** Rs 110.9 crore (up 21.1% YoY).
*   **US Revenue:** Rs 39.1 crore (up 16.4% YoY).
*   **Europe Revenue:** Rs 67.5 crore (up 23.5% YoY).
*   **API Revenue:** Rs 45.1 crore (up 4.6% YoY).

### Highlights
*   Consolidated revenues crossed the Rs 500 crore mark for the first time.
*   Plant-1 at Goa received a successful MHRA audit; AnaCipher cleared ANVISA inspection.
*   Flagship brand *Cyclopam* crossed the Rs 200 crore threshold (sales at Rs 206 crore).
*   Gastrointestinal portfolio grew 30.3%; Stomatological business grew 34.8%.
*   Management incorporated "Warren Life Sciences" to potentially separate the API and OTC businesses.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Domestic:** Management maintains guidance for "high single-digit" growth for FY27.
*   **International:** Management expects "decent double-digit" growth for the full year.
*   **Europe/UK:** Management targets 20%+ growth for the next 1-2 years.
*   **USFDA Audit:** Management expects clarity on the recent 7 observations by the first week of December.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Domestic business faced headwinds from an "absent monsoon," impacting seasonal acute therapies (Febrex Plus, Oxipod, ATM).
*   International business growth was impacted by geopolitical conflict, leading to elevated freight costs (Rs 13 crore impact).
*   Emerging markets were impacted by the divestment of the Ophthal portfolio (approx. Rs 10 crore impact).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Consolidated EBITDA margin was 9.2% (vs 9.1% YoY).
*   Gross margins were impacted by a ~30% increase in COGS, RMPM, freight, and solvents.
*   Employee costs as a percentage of sales decreased by a couple of percentage points.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Management stated actual Capex (land, etc.) for the year is less than Rs 25 crore.
*   Cash flow capitalized amount (Rs 60 crore in H1) relates to intangibles (ANDA/dossiers).
*   No major Capex expected for the remainder of FY27 or next year.
*   Management stated current infrastructure can support 4x the current international topline.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Repaid Rs 65 crore of debt at the consolidated level in H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical conflict causing elevated freight costs.
*   USFDA audit observations (7 observations).
*   High dependence on seasonal acute therapies in the domestic market.
*   Price erosion and competition in the US generic market.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why are expenses up despite headcount reduction?** A: Management attributed this to a Rs 13 crore freight cost impact due to geopolitical conflict.
*   **Q: Why is domestic growth lagging?** A: Management cited poor monsoon impacting seasonal acute therapies (Febrex Plus, Oxipod), while core brands like Cyclopam and Cital performed well.
*   **Q: Status of USFDA audit?** A: Management received 7 observations, noted they are not related to data integrity or cleanliness, and expects a positive outcome by early December.
*   **Q: Why separate Warren Remedies?** A: Management stated the API and OTC businesses are "chalk and cheese" and separation allows for better financial tracking.
*   **Q: Can Europe/UK grow 20%+?** A: Management confirmed this is the target for the next 1-2 years.

### One-line takeaway
Indoco achieved record consolidated revenue of Rs 506.9 crore, but profitability was constrained by elevated freight costs and domestic seasonal headwinds, with management focusing on operational efficiency and international growth.
