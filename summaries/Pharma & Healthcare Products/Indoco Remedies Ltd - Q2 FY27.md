# Indoco Remedies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - strong international growth and regulatory progress offset by domestic acute portfolio weakness and margin pressure from freight/material costs

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 5,069 million (Rs 506.9 crore), up 7.4% YoY
- Standalone revenue: Rs 4,381 million, up 2% YoY
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 464 million, margin 9.2% (vs 9.1% YoY)
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs 403 million, margin 9.2% (vs 12.4% YoY)
- Domestic formulations: Rs 2,128 million (down from Rs 2,261 million YoY)
- International formulations: Rs 1,720 million, up 12.1% YoY
  - Regulated markets: Rs 1,109 million, up 21.1% YoY
  - US: Rs 391 million, up 16.4% YoY
  - Europe: Rs 675 million, up 23.5% YoY
  - Emerging markets: Rs 612 million (vs Rs 618 million YoY)
- API: Rs 451 million, up 4.6% YoY
- Allied services (AnaCipher + Indoco Analytical): Rs 82 million, up 20.8% YoY
- Freight cost impact: Rs 13 crore additional
- Debt repayment H1: ~Rs 65 crore consolidated
- MR productivity (PHY): 3.25-3.3
- R&D spend YTD: 4% of topline (guidance ≤5%)
- Warren toothpaste revenue: Rs 36 crore quarter
- FPP (US front-end) H1 revenue: >Rs 100 crore, profitable

### Revenue mix
- Domestic formulations: Rs 2,128 million (42% of consolidated) vs Rs 2,261 million YoY
- International formulations: Rs 1,720 million (34%) vs Rs 1,533 million YoY
  - Regulated markets: Rs 1,109 million (22%) vs Rs 915 million YoY
  - Emerging markets: Rs 612 million (12%) vs Rs 618 million YoY
- API: Rs 451 million (9%) vs Rs 431 million YoY
- Allied services: Rs 82 million (1.6%) vs Rs 68 million YoY

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 500 crore quarterly consolidated revenue for first time (Rs 506.9 crore)
- Cyclopam crossed Rs 200 crore annual sales (Rs 206 crore), #2 in antispasmodics
- GI portfolio grew 30.3%, stomatological 34.8% YoY
- New products (last 2 years) contributing ~Rs 100 crore on MAT basis
- Plant-1 Goa: successful MHRA audit; ANVISA inspection cleared
- Regulated markets revenue up 21% YoY driven by Europe (+23.5%) and US (+16.4%)
- API revenue up 4.6%; allied services up 20.8%
- FPP (US front-end) turned profitable, H1 revenue >Rs 100 crore
- Warren toothpaste division near breakeven at Rs 36 crore quarterly revenue
- Debt repayment ~Rs 65 crore consolidated in H1
- Incorporated Warren Life Sciences to separate API and OTC businesses

### Guidance & outlook
- Domestic formulations: high single-digit growth for FY27 maintained
- International regulated markets: target 20%+ growth for next 1-2 years
- Emerging markets: expect double-digit growth in H2 after ophthal divestment impact (~Rs 10-12 crore)
- Capex: minimal, physical capex <Rs 25 crore for year; no major spend H2 or next year
- R&D: ≤5% of topline (YTD 4%)
- US FDA resolution for Goa Plant-1 expected by first week December
- Warren API plant US audit hoped by Q2 FY28

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic: acute brands (Febrex Plus, Oxipod, ATL) degrew 16-20% due to absent monsoon, offsetting chronic brand growth (Cyclopam, Cital, dental)
- International: regulated markets strong with steady order positions; emerging markets impacted by ophthal portfolio divestment to Sunways (~Rs 10 crore sales loss)
- LATAM grew 57% YoY
- Geopolitical conflict driving elevated freight costs
- US generics: persistent price erosion and competition noted as characteristic of market

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated EBITDA margin 9.2% (vs 9.1% YoY); standalone 9.2% (vs 12.4% YoY)
- Freight impact: Rs 13 crore additional this quarter
- Material costs (COGS, RMPM, solvents) up ~30% YoY pressuring gross margins
- Gross margin target 68-70% delayed by cost inflation
- Employee costs as % of sales dipped a couple of percentage points
- MMP (manufacturing process improvements) yielding more output per batch but offset by external cost increases

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex Rs 60 crore includes capitalized ANDAs/dossiers; physical capex <Rs 25 crore for year
- No major capex expected H2 FY27 or FY28
- Warren API plant: validation batches ongoing, KSM production for Patalganga
- Plants running single shift; can add shifts as needed
- Management estimates current infrastructure can support 4x current international revenue

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt repayment ~Rs 65 crore consolidated in H1
- H1 operating cash flow Rs 50 crore (ex-exceptional) vs EBITDA Rs 80 crore (60-65% conversion)
- Working capital stuck in H1 expected to release in H2 improving cash conversion

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Freight cost volatility from geopolitical conflict
- US FDA audit outcome for Goa Plant-1 uncertain (7 observations, none data integrity/cleanliness)
- Domestic acute portfolio vulnerability to seasonality/monsoon
- US generics price erosion and competition
- Warren toothpaste scaling requires significant ad spend (Sensodyne spends Rs 350 crore)
- Emerging markets revenue impact from ophthal divestment (~Rs 10-12 crore)

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Freight & domestic portfolio (Arvind, Unique PMS):** Freight impact quantified at Rs 13 crore. Acute brands degrowth offsetting chronic growth; distribution improvements gradual but prescription growth positive for degrowing brands. US FDA observations procedural, response submitted, decision by early Dec.
2. **Warren & FPP (Rudraksh Raheja):** Warren API plant US audit expected Q2 FY28. FPP H1 revenue >Rs 100 crore, profitable; sustainable margins to be watched. Warren toothpaste Rs 36 crore, near breakeven; API minimal. Capacity: single shift, can expand; infrastructure supports 4x international revenue. Debt repayment on track (~Rs 65 crore H1).
3. **Goa plant strategy & Sensodent (Kirtan Donga):** US preferred over fragmented Europe; Europe remains option. Sensodent gross margins excellent but not disclosed.
4. **Margins & FPP profitability (Kenil Mehta):** Gross margin recovery to 68-70% delayed by material/freight costs. MMP savings hard to quantify due to external factors. FPP profitability from consistent supply, de-risking, better coordination, scale. Cash flow conversion to improve in H2.
5. **Margin profile by geography (Janish Shah):** Domestic 55% of revenue (branded), API 10%, services 2-3%, regulated markets 30-35%. Regulated markets currently contract manufacturing heavy; future first-to-file products to improve margins.
6. **Guidance & MR productivity (Zain):** Domestic high single-digit guidance maintained; growth impacted by ophthal divestment (Rs 65 crore base). Export: regulated 20%+, emerging double-digit H2. MR productivity 3.25-3.3. Capex minimal, R&D ≤5%.
7. **Warren losses & capital allocation (Hardik Jatheliya):** API business driving losses; toothpaste near breakeven. Ad spend controlled but cycles; Sensodyne ad budget Rs 350 crore. Losses to reduce with API utilization ramp-up.

### One-line takeaway
Indoco delivers record quarterly revenue crossing Rs 500 crore driven by strong regulated markets growth (21%) and API/allied services, but domestic formulation declines on acute portfolio weakness and margins pressured by freight/material cost inflation; US FDA resolution and domestic recovery key near-term catalysts.
