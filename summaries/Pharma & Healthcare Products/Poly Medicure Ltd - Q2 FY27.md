# Poly Medicure Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue recovery, robust domestic growth (22%), and resilient EBITDA margins despite raw material headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone H1 Revenue:** INR 919 crore, up 15% YoY (Domestic: INR 318 crore, up 20%; International: INR 595 crore, up 12.5%).
- **Standalone H1 Gross Profit:** INR 645 crore, up 17% YoY; margin expanded 111 bps to 70.1%.
- **Standalone H1 Operating EBITDA:** INR 255 crore, up 20% YoY.
- **Standalone H1 PAT:** INR 180 crore (margin 18.6%).
- **Standalone Q2 Revenue:** INR 488 crore, up 17% YoY (Domestic: INR 172 crore, up 22%; International: INR 313 crore, up 15%).
- **Standalone Q2 Gross Profit:** INR 336 crore, up 16% YoY; margin at 68.9%.
- **Standalone Q2 Operating EBITDA:** INR 134 crore, up 20% YoY; margin at 27.4%.
- **Standalone Q2 PAT:** INR 92 crore (margin 18.1%).
- **Consolidated H1 Revenue:** INR 1,106 crore, up 31% YoY (organic growth 14%).
- **Consolidated H1 Gross Profit:** INR 793 crore, up 36% YoY; margin expanded 270 bps to 71.7%.
- **Consolidated H1 Operating EBITDA:** INR 266 crore, up 19% YoY.
- **Consolidated H1 PAT:** INR 180 crore.
- **Consolidated Q2 Revenue:** INR 581 crore, up 31% YoY.
- **Consolidated Q2 Gross Profit:** INR 407 crore, up 32% YoY.
- **Consolidated Q2 Operating EBITDA:** INR 139 crore, up 20% YoY.
- **Consolidated Q2 PAT:** INR 95 crore.
- **Liquidity & Net Cash:** Liquidity at INR 839 crore vs. consolidated debt of INR 377 crore (Net cash of INR 460 crore) as of September 30, 2026.
- **Capex (H1):** INR 124 crore (INR 124 crore in India, INR 15 crore overseas).
- **Segment H1 Revenue:** Infusion therapy INR 542 crore (up 11%); Orthopaedics INR 92 crore; Renal INR 94 crore (up 7.5%); Cardiology INR 56 crore; Other segments INR 317 crore (up 23%).
- **Segment Q2 Revenue:** Infusion therapy INR 284 crore (up 12%); Orthopaedics INR 43 crore; Renal INR 51 crore (up 20%); Cardiology INR 27 crore; Other segments INR 173 crore (up 26%).
- **Dialysis Machines Sold (H1):** Over 300 (cumulative ~1,250).
- **DES Stents Implanted (as of Oct):** Close to 18,000.
- **US Guidance:** USD 7 million-USD 8 million group-level sales for FY27; building to USD 15 million-USD 25 million in the next 3-4 years.

### Highlights
- Established a direct presence in the U.K. (working on 3 projects, targeting go-lives in Q1 FY27, Q4 FY27, and Q3 of next financial year) and optimized Medyneo (Brazil subsidiary, first shipments dispatched in October).
- Added 30 new customers in international markets over the last six months.
- DGHS issued a circular in late October mandating single-use medical devices (dialyzers), which management notes will increase product demand in the long run.
- Booked an exceptional item of INR 96 million due to the reversal of a contingent consideration/earn-out milestone after a PendraCare development project failed clinical/regulatory trials.
- *Comparison with Record:* The call continues the stated strategy of shifting from a consumables manufacturer to a diversified med-tech player via organic and inorganic routes, with a clear path toward the 2030 double-revenue vision.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance (FY27):** Reaffirmed. Consolidated: INR 2,300 crore-INR 2,400 crore; Standalone: INR 1,900 crore-INR 1,950 crore (Domestic up 20%, International up 15%).
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance (FY27):** Standalone guidance raised from 25%-27% to 26%-27.5%. Consolidated guidance maintained at 23%-25%.
- **Gross Margin Outlook:** Standalone full-year expected in the range of 68%-69%; consolidated expected to remain in the 70%+ category.
- **Capex Guidance (FY27):** Maintained at INR 200 crore-INR 225 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic business showing strong growth (22% in Q2, >19% for H1).
- European organic business grew close to 40% in Q2 (29% for H1), rebounding strongly from inventory normalization and deeper customer engagement.
- ROW market down 7% in Q2 (-1.8% in H1) due to West Asia geopolitical challenges, container availability, and high freight costs.
- Dialysis business facing continued Chinese dumping; anti-dumping case filed in late June is being pursued with the government.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Proactively mitigated potential 200-300 bps gross margin headwinds from West Asia raw material inflation via inventory management, calibrated price hikes (4%-5% in Q1), and high-value product sales.
- Short-term debt increased due to higher working capital requirements.
- Lease liabilities increased due to the adoption of IFRS accounting by underlying subsidiaries starting this financial year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total H1 capex spent at INR 124 crore out of the annual INR 200 crore-INR 225 crore guidance.
- Expanding orthopaedic and cardiology businesses alongside core plant balancing.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong net cash position of INR 460 crore (cash of INR 839 crore against debt of INR 377 crore).
- Long-term debt in Italian subsidiary has an ongoing repayment schedule of EUR 1.5 million-EUR 2 million per year over the next 5 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical challenges in West Asia (impacting Middle East and Iranian markets, affecting ROW growth and PendraCare exports).
- Delays in public health tendering in Italy impacting Citieffe growth.
- Ongoing pressure from Chinese dumping in the renal/dialysis segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Simran Thakkar (Beas Capital) on the exceptional item:** Management clarified that an earn-out milestone for PendraCare was reversed (INR 96 million) because a contract development project failed clinical/regulatory trials and was shelved.
2. **Sidharth Negandhi (CWC) on subsidiary performance & ROW:** Management explained that Citieffe's slowdown was due to Italian tender delays, while PendraCare suffered from Middle East/Iran exposure (20% of revenue) amid regional conflict. ROW markets faced logistical/freight hurdles.
3. **Bhavesh Jain (DV Investment Advisors) on subsidiary margins:** Management stated that PendraCare is currently making EBITDA losses due to revenue drops, while Citieffe and Plan 1 Health operate at mid-teen EBITDA margins; consolidated numbers also absorb setup costs for Brazil and the U.K.
4. **Jasdeep Walia (Clockvine Capital) on renal business and competition:** Management noted that while Chinese dumping continues, a recent DGHS circular mandating single-use dialyzers is expected to structurally boost domestic demand.
5. **Ashish Sethi (UTI Asset Management) on acquired business margin recovery:** Management expects acquired assets to return to historical margins and trend toward 20% over 3-5 years through synergy projects and operating leverage.

### One-line takeaway
Poly Medicure delivered robust H1/Q2 FY27 performance with 31% consolidated revenue growth and raised standalone EBITDA margin guidance to 26%-27.5%, underpinned by domestic momentum and a strong European recovery.
