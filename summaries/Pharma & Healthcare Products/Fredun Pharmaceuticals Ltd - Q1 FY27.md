# Fredun Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, alongside expanding operating margins and profitability.

### Key numbers
- Stand-alone total income: INR 228.25 crore, up 90.44% YoY
- EBITDA: INR 32.78 crore, up 92.90% YoY
- EBITDA margin: 14.36%, expanded by 18 bps YoY
- Net profit (PAT): INR 13.17 crore, up 94.63% YoY
- Net profit margin: 5.77%, expanded by 12 bps YoY

### Highlights
- Company added 43 manufacturing locations across 5 plants in Palghar.
- Fredun GX is now present across 19 states.
- Wagr.in (pet care platform) had a soft launch on 15th June; onboarding almost all big brands and 1,500 to 2,000 medical products.
- Planning to launch cat food and a functional line for cats in the coming 6 to 7 months (cat food launch targeted by Q3/Q4 of this financial year).
- Opening pet diagnostics centers in Worli (Mumbai), Malad, and Vashi.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a blended revenue growth of around 30% to 35% for the next 3 years.
- Fredun GX line expected to grow at 30% to 35% YoY for the next 4 years.
- Pet care business targeted to grow at 40% to 50% YoY for the next 3 to 4 years, aiming for INR 100 crore within 3 to 3.5 years of launching plants.
- CapEx guidance: INR 30 crore to INR 40 crore (or INR 35 crore to INR 45 crore) per year for the next 2 years (FY27 and next year).
- Revenue target for the current year: Management stated they are on track to achieve/overachieve their target of around INR 800 crore (dismissed a specific INR 1,000 crore revenue query).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pharma penetration in India remains poor even in 2026.
- Pet care products see high repeatability and strong response across metros and Tier 2/3/4 cities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Credit rating improved from BBB to BBB+, helping reduce interest rates.
- Inventory days expected to rationalize to around 120 days within the next 4 quarters (hovering between 110 to 125 days).
- Gross margins by segment: Pet care at 45% to 55%, Mobility at 40% to 50%, Nutrition at 35% to 45% (expanding to 45% to 50%), Dermaceutics at 70% to 75%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Goal by end of December 2028 / early FY29 is to be one of the largest manufacturing units at a single location in the country.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital stands at around INR 170 crore to INR 175 crore on a run rate of around INR 850 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management other than standard business and reporting cycles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinod Shah (VS Ventures):** Asked about 3-5 year visibility. Management stated a blended growth of 30% to 35% is on the charts driven by new-age brands and capacity expansions.
2. **Mayur Parikh (VY Capital):** Asked about domestic growth via Fredun GX across states. Management stated GX is in 19 states with 30%-35% growth expected for the next 4 years.
3. **Keshav Toshniwal (Karnakala Capital):** Inquired about Wagr.in. Management detailed the soft launch, onboarding of brands/pharmacies, and creation of a comprehensive pet parenting ecosystem.
4. **Shreya Bajaj (Sirin Alpha) / Nabendu Mondal:** Asked about CapEx and Q1 performance drivers. Management guided INR 35-45 crore CapEx for the next 2 years and explained Q1 was boosted by advance order booking at lower price increases in the previous quarter.
5. **Khushi Jain (Share India) & Ashish Malani:** Asked about working capital and interest costs. Management stated working capital is comfortable (~INR 170-175 cr) and interest costs are reducing due to better cash flows and an upgrade to BBB+ credit rating.
6. **Nirali Shah (Ashika Investment Managers) & Ketan Pathak:** Inquired about margin levers and INR 1,000 cr revenue target. Management noted segment margin mixes will aid profitability over time, declined to commit to a specific INR 1,000 cr target for the year while confirming they are on track to exceed INR 800 cr.

### One-line takeaway
Fredun Pharmaceuticals delivered a robust Q1 FY27 with ~90% YoY growth in revenue and PAT, backed by expansion in pet care, GX, and manufacturing capacities.
