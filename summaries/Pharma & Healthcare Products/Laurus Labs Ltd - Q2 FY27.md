# Laurus Labs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong CDMO growth, steady affordable medicine momentum, and record revenues and profits.

### Key numbers
- **Total Revenues (Q2 FY27):** INR2,206 crore
- **Total Income from Operations (H1 FY27):** INR4,233 crore (up 31% YoY)
- **CDMO Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR875 crore (up 86% YoY)
- **CDMO Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR1,710 crore (up 77% YoY)
- **Affordable Medicines Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR1,302 crore (up 15% YoY)
- **Affordable Medicines Revenue (H1 FY27):** Healthy growth of 13% YoY
- **Laurus Bio Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR29 crore
- **Laurus Bio Revenue (H1 FY27):** INR64 crore
- **Gross Margins (Q2 FY27):** 63%
- **Gross Margins (H1 FY27):** 62.9%
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR762 crore
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 34.5% (expanded 2.7 percentage points YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR1,406 crore (margin at 33.2%)
- **Profit After Tax - PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR441 crore
- **Profit After Tax - PAT (H1 FY27):** INR809 crore
- **ROCE (H1 FY27):** 22.5% (vs. 17.7% in FY26)
- **R&D Spend to Sales (H1 FY27):** 4.3% (increased over 34% YoY)

- **Capex (H1 FY27):** INR864 crore
- **Net Debt:** INR2,466 crore
- **Debt-to-EBITDA:** 1.02 (vs. 1.28 in previous quarter)
- **Small Molecule API Reactor Volume:** Over 9,000 kiloliters
- **Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS) Reactor Volume:** Over 16,000 liters
- **ARV API Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR625 crore
- **ARV FDF Revenue (Q2 FY27):** INR677 crore
- **Regulatory Filings:** Cumulative 92 DMFs filed to date; Developed market formulation: 2 dossiers filed and 2 approvals in H1, cumulative 96 products filed to date.
- **Quality Audits:** 59 quality audits cleared in H1 with no critical findings.

### Highlights
- Delivered record revenue and profits in Q2 FY27, backed by CDMO execution and affordable medicine stability.
- Commissioned commercial-scale peptide manufacturing block.
- Announced agreement to acquire a majority 60% stake in Canada-based Galenvs Sciences (specializing in oligonucleotides and precision magnetic nanoparticles-based purification).
- Announced acquisition of a majority stake in SI & C Resins (peptide resin technology company in Hyderabad) to in-source critical solid support resins and chromatography stationary phases.
- Underwent 59 quality audits by global agencies and partners with zero critical findings.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains guidance of over 60% gross margins and over 30% EBITDA margins.
- Guided INR3,000 crore capex for FY27 and FY28 combined; expects capital expenditure to continue around INR1,500 crore per year thereafter based on current understanding.
- KRKA joint venture formulation facility Phase 1 production blocks scheduled to commence operations in mid-2027; expects meaningful revenues by FY28/FY29.
- Commercial fermentation facility Laurus Bio 4 expected to be operational by the end of the year, with customer capacity reservations advancing.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust demand for specialized capabilities, integrated offerings, and customer relationships across human health and animal health businesses.
- Peptide manufacturing capabilities are fully integrated, offering complete API synthesis rather than just fragments.
- Crop Science discussions ongoing with partners, though contribution remains miniscule compared to human and animal health.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins maintained around 63% due to improved product mix, division mix, and operational efficiencies.
- EBITDA margins expanded via operational leverage.
- Raw material cost contribution of peptide resins in peptide manufacturing varies between 10% to 20% depending on fragment length.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Small molecule API reactor volume in operation surpassed 9,000 kiloliters with further expansions planned at the Vizag site.
- SPPS capacity exceeds 16,000 liters with further expansions underway.
- Laurus Bio's commercial fermentation facility (Laurus Bio 4) targeted to be operational by end-2027 (trials in Q4, commercial supplies in Q1 FY28); animal health GMP fermentation facility operational in H2 next year.
- KRKA formulation Phase 1 production blocks start operations mid-2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at INR2,466 crore; debt-to-EBITDA improved to 1.02.
- H1 operating cash flow impacted by inventory changes; management expects inventory utilization in upcoming quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted that new modalities (like oligos and ADCs) will not immediately scale to the mammoth levels of the ARV business within 4–5 years.
- Confidentiality limits granular disclosures on specific product pipelines, customer concentrations, and exact capex breakdowns for individual modalities.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Tushar (Motilal Oswal) on Peptide commercial timeline:** Management stated that commercial revenues from peptide capacities are expected around the '29 timeframe, with validation campaigns utilizing high asset capacity beforehand.
2. **Sajal Kapoor (Antifragile Thinking) on competitive edge and modality strategy:** Dr. Satya explained that unlike 2023, Laurus now has broad technical capabilities (flow chemistry, biocatalysis, fermentation, OEB 5 payload linkers) to handle complex customer tech packs (excluding mAbs and cephalosporins/penicillins).
3. **Chirag Shah (White Pine) on sequential CDMO jump:** Management clarified that the sequential revenue increase was driven by an overall business performance, led by affordable medicines and stable CDMO execution without specific one-offs.
4. **Foram Parekh (BOB Capital Markets) on EBITDA margin sustainability:** Management reiterated comfort in maintaining over 60% gross margins and over 30% EBITDA margins.
5. **Alankar Garude (Kotak Institutional Equities) on talent and capability gaps:** Dr. Satya noted that gaps in technology are bridged via strategic acquisitions (e.g., Galenvs for oligo R&D talent, SI & C Resins for peptide resins, Laurus Bio for fermentation) rather than building internal talent from scratch.

### One-line takeaway
Laurus Labs delivered record Q2 FY27 performance driven by strong 86% YoY CDMO growth, healthy 34.5% EBITDA margins, and aggressive capability investments in peptides and oligonucleotides.
