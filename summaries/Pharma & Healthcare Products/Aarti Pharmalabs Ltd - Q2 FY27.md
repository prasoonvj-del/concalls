# Aarti Pharmalabs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, strong YoY growth across segments, and healthy progress on ongoing capacity expansions.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** INR613 crores vs INR417 crores (up 47% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR136 crores vs INR74 crores (up 84% YoY)
- **PAT:** INR66 crores vs INR24 crores (2.75x increase YoY on low base)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 22.2% vs 17.7% YoY
- **Segment Split (Q2 FY27):** Xanthine derivatives and allied products (50%); API and intermediate (30%); CDMO/CMO (13%); Other sales including trading, export incentives, and scrap (6%)
- **Xanthine Volume Split:** Beverages (62%), Others (38%)
- **Xanthine Geographical Split:** Exports (59%), Domestic (41%)
- **API/Intermediate Sub-segment Split:** Regulated market (57%), ROW market (33%), Non-regulated market (10%)
- **CDMO Metrics:** 23 customers, 59 active projects (39 in commercial stages, 20 under development)
- **Working Capital Cycle:** ~165 to 167 days
- **Capex Guidance:** INR350 crores to INR400 crores total for FY27
- **Xanthine Realization:** Up 15% YoY average; volume expected to jump 15% YoY in FY27
- **Shipment Spillover Impact:** ~5% of quarter's Xanthine sales stuck due to dispatch constraints

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue of INR613 crores.
- Xanthine capacity expansion ramp-up is progressing, targeting full capacity by the end of FY27 and sequential utilization next year.
- Steroid API block capacity expansion completed, contributing to production and revenue after Q1 constraints.
- Groundbreaking of Atali dedicated block (Block No. 2) completed; targeted for commercialization in H2 FY28. Atali Phase 1B trials underway with capitalization expected in Q3.
- Setting up a peptide lab to be operational by the end of FY27, eyeing customer business in FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track to achieve 40% to 50% revenue growth over last year in the CDMO/CMO segment (target of USD 100 million or INR1,000 crores in CDMO, whichever comes first).
- Reiterated long-term strategy of growing CDMO/CMO as the high-growth pillar, followed by API and intermediates, with Xanthine as the cash cow.
- API and intermediate base expected to run at INR180 crores to INR200 crores per quarter for H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Xanthine demand remains stable; management holds a dominant global position (second largest capacity in the world).
- CDMO business experiencing strong momentum, driven by China Plus One strategy and transition of projects.
- API business recovering from Q1 block shutdowns; focus shifting toward regulated markets and higher-margin Onco products.
- Expanding modalities into peptides, oligonucleotides, and ADC linkers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs stayed elevated due to geopolitical turmoil; management is passing on increases to customers.
- EBITDA margins saw a slight downward pressure of a couple of percentage points due to raw material increases and product mix, but absolute gross margins are expected to increase.
- Depreciation cost is expected to rise in H2 due to asset capitalization (Atali Phase 1B, Xanthine intermediate block). Finance costs expected to remain similar.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total FY27 capex targeted at INR350 crores to INR400 crores.
- Atali Phase 1 (1A & 1B) undergoing capitalization in Q3.
- Xanthine nameplate capacity to reach 9,600 tonnes, operating at 7,500 to 8,500 tonnes over next two years.
- Peptide and oligonucleotide labs to be operational by end of FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term borrowings/debt increased to support ongoing capex. Debt expected to rise further to fund remaining FY27 capex.
- Working capital cycle remains around 150 to 167 days, driven by inventory build-up for H2 CDMO scaling.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical turmoil causing elevated raw material costs and potential dispatch/shipment constraints.
- Spot market xanthine prices may face pressure if raw material/crude prices decline or due to competition.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ahmed Madha (Unifi Capital):** Asked about Atali Phase 1 capitalization and working capital impact. Management stated Atali Phase 1B trials are ongoing with Q3 capitalization, and inventory/working capital is elevated to support strong H2 CDMO scaling.
2. **Sudarshan Padmanabhan (Ashika Investment Managers):** Inquired about margin dilution and working capital. Management noted raw material pass-through caused slight percentage margin pressure, but absolute gross margins and operating leverage will support H2.
3. **Rahul Jain (Institutional Investor):** Asked about upgrading guidance given strong H1 performance. Management declined to give pointed annual guidance, re-emphasizing confidence in multi-year strategy and execution.
4. **Rupesh Tatiya (Portfolio Advisors):** Questioned CDMO project pipeline movement vs. INR1,000 crore revenue visibility. Management clarified that 10 to 12 projects out of 59 (the 80/20 rule) will drive INR700–INR800 crores of the potential revenue.
5. **Ankit Gupta (Bamboo Capital):** Asked about sustainability of Xanthine pricing and capacity utilization. Management stated absolute margins can sustain for next financial year, with nameplate capacity reaching 9,600 tonnes.

### One-line takeaway
Aarti Pharmalabs delivered a record Q2 FY27 driven by 47% revenue growth and an 84% surge in EBITDA, supported by scaling CDMO projects, recovery in steroid APIs, and ongoing capacity capitalization.
