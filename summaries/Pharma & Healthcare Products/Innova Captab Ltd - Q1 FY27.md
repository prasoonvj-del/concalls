# Innova Captab Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY revenue and EBITDA growth, driven by healthy demand across CDMO and branded generic segments.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: INR 470.9 crores (up 34% YoY)
- CDMO revenue: INR 328 crores / INR 328.7 crores (up 32% YoY)
- Branded generic revenue: INR 142 crores / INR 142.2 crores (up 39% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 75.1 crores (up 33% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 16%
- PAT: INR 44.1 crores (up 42% YoY)
- Export contribution: 32% of total revenue
- Jammu facility revenue: INR 107 crores (up from INR 90 crores previous quarter)
- Jammu facility EBITDA: INR 1 to 1.5 crores (positive EBITDA)
- Jammu facility annualized capacity utilization: 25% to 30%
- Cash conversion cycle: 90 days (+/- 10 days)
- R&D spend: 0.7% to 1% of total revenue
- Volume growth (overall manufacturing capability level): 20% to 22% YoY (ex-Jammu volume growth at 12% to 14%)
- Maintenance capex guidance: INR 20 crores to INR 25 crores annually
- Growth capex guidance (existing capability augmentation/debottlenecking): INR 20 crores to INR 30 crores annually

### Highlights
- Started FY27 on a strong note, with performance broadly in line with the outlined trajectory.
- Growth supported by steady order flows, expanding product portfolio, and deeper engagement with existing and new customers.
- Jammu plant successfully ramped up, achieving positive EBITDA and scaling up as expected.
- Branded generic business maintains healthy momentum through presence in existing markets and geographical expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 guidance of 20%+ volume growth, with profitability outpacing revenue.
- Long-term asset turn from the Jammu plant is expected to be north of 3x at optimum utilization.
- Jammu plant optimum revenue potential estimated at INR 1,400 crores at 65% to 70% utilization.
- Normal BAU blended EBITDA margin profile expected to remain in the 15% to 16% range (+/- 2%), with potential to expand towards 17% to 18% as Jammu matures.
- Full-year gross margin expected to maintain between 35.5% (+/- 2%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Healthy demand witnessed across customer bases in both domestic and international markets.
- CDMO business focuses on increasing wallet share and adding new customers across therapeutic categories and dosage forms.
- Branded generic business operates on an arm's length basis, treated as a normal CDMO customer by manufacturing plants.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin dip YoY (1% to 1.5%) attributed to business mix changes.
- Logistics costs saw a slight increase due to global supply chain conditions, but were duly factored into overall operations.
- API pricing model is generally a pass-through; management does not speculate on API prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Maintenance capex guided at INR 20 crore to INR 25 crore; growth/debottlenecking capex at INR 20 crore to INR 30 crore annually.
- Baddi new plot (bought last year) plans remain on the same line; concrete plan to be announced post Board approval.
- Future expansions likely in tranches (e.g., INR 200 crore to INR 250 crore) rather than massive single-shot investments like Jammu.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Initial working capital for Jammu was built and factored in previously.
- Cash conversion cycle maintained at 90 days (+/- 10 days).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Challenges in achieving 20%+ growth will always exist, requiring the team to overcome operational hurdles.
- Geopolitical and macro-political shifts may impact business mix and operating environment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Achal Maheshwari (Naredi Investment):** Asked about Jammu capacity utilization and asset turns. *Management stated 25%-30% annualized utilization and expected asset turns north of 3x at optimum levels.*
2. **Ankit Shah:** Asked for volume vs. realization growth breakup and Jammu profitability. *Management stated 20%-22% overall volume growth (12%-14% ex-Jammu) and reported positive Jammu EBITDA of INR 1-1.5 crores.*
3. **Pritesh Chheda:** Inquired about future margin trajectory and capex breakdown. *Management guided normalized margins at 15%-16% (+/- 2%), potentially heading to 17%-18% as Jammu matures, and detailed maintenance/growth capex ranges.*
4. **Vansh Gupta:** Questioned potential conflict of interest between CDMO and branded generic businesses. *Management clarified that branded generics are treated like normal CDMO customers on an arm's length basis, which is accepted industry practice.*
5. **Siddhant Mantri:** Asked about Jammu plant revenue potential and ramp-up visibility. *Management stated a peak revenue potential of INR 1,400 crores at 65%-70% utilization, driven by ongoing customer/product audits and approvals.*

### One-line takeaway
Innova Captab delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 34% revenue growth and positive EBITDA contribution from the newly ramped-up Jammu facility, keeping its 20%+ volume growth guidance firmly on track.
