# Gujarat Themis Biosyn Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a transformational journey, strong volume-led revenue growth, and key strategic acquisitions (MicroBiopharm Japan and Sanofi brands) to build a fermentation-based CDMO platform.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 43.8 crore (up 22.1% YoY from Rs 35.9 crore)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 20.8 crore (up 49.4% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 47.5% (up 867 bps YoY)
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 11.1 crore (up 22.1% YoY from Rs 9.1 crore)
- **Gross block (including CWIP):** Rs 435 crore in FY26 (compared to Rs 62 crore in FY23)
- **Incremental capex added over last 3 years:** Rs 370 crore

### Highlights
- Financial performance was driven by a robust increase in sales volumes and healthy product demand.
- Company is transitioning from a fermentation intermediate player to an integrated fermentation-led pharmaceutical CDMO platform.
- Announced acquisition of MicroBiopharm Japan (specializing in precision fermentation, immunosuppressants, oncology, anti-infectives, peptides, plasmids, ADCs, and enzyme engineering).
- Announced acquisition of 13 established anti-TB and anti-infective global brands from Sanofi France across 55 countries in Europe, Middle East, and Africa.
- Merger with Themis Medicare was called off after 6 months to focus purely on the fermentation-based CDMO strategy.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a base-case top-line growth on a high-teen basis over the next 3 to 5 years.
- Expanded fermentation site capacity starts full steam by the end of August 2026; output to be seen from the current quarter and H2 FY27.
- Promoter share pledge is guided to go down significantly within 1 year to 15 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global pharmaceutical industry shows increasing demand for complex APIs, specialty pharmaceutical ingredients, and fermentation-based products.
- Market is shifting from Rifampicin towards Rifapentine (which requires more Rifa-S intermediate per kilo), and GTBL is ready with both APIs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Hybrid power project Phase 1 is expected to go live in September, followed by Phase 2 two months later, which management states will allow power procurement at a significantly lower price and boost EBITDA margins.
- Interest costs for international acquisitions are noted to be significantly lower in target geographies than the 10% mentioned by analysts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fermentation capacity has practically doubled with the addition of 540 KL capacity (along with R&D and cGMP pilot facilities).
- Remaining standalone capex for base business in FY27 is estimated around Rs 10 crore to Rs 15 crore (plus maintenance capex), bringing total base capex to around Rs 20 odd crore.
- Asset turns on the newly added Rs 200 crore fermentation and API facilities are projected at 1.4x to 1.5x.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains healthy with strong historical cash flows.
- Funding for the MicroBiopharm Japan deal is secured via multiple avenues; management is evaluating an optimal mix of debt (up to an available INR 450 crore flexibility option / group guarantees) and equity (raising up to INR 1,000 crores via QIP).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management admitted being delayed by almost a year in implementing recent projects due to capacity constraints in fermentation blocks.
- Potential integration risks and management bandwidth concerns regarding two back-to-back overseas acquisitions were raised by investors.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harsh Upadhyay (Individual Investor) on acquisition financing and ROI:** Asked how the INR 3,000 crore acquisition (INR 1,000 crore QIP + INR 2,000 crore debt) makes financial sense with a 10% cost of capital. Management replied that interest costs in target geographies are significantly lower than 10%, and the acquired businesses are cash-flow positive.
2. **Nirali Shah (Ashika Investment Managers) on MBJ capabilities:** Asked which MBJ platforms are commercialized vs. 5-year options. Management stated ADCs are new and patented without active CDMO projects yet, while peptides, plasmids, oncology, precision fermentation, immunosuppressants, and anti-infectives are already commercialized.
3. **Mithun Maity (MMM Capital) on Sanofi asset-light model:** Inquired who manufactures Sanofi products post-deal. Management explained a 3-year transition service agreement is in place, utilizing existing/new CMOs while tech transfers happen within the first year.
4. **Hitesh (Kosha Capital) on management bandwidth and past performance of MBJ:** Expressed worry about thin management bandwidth for two overseas acquisitions and MBJ's past growth. Management clarified they are not turning around distressed assets as both are steady businesses, and local management in Japan will remain.
5. **Harsh Upadhyay on Optimus Drugs dispute:** Asked about the recent dispute. Management stated the dispute is behind them and business is restarting, noting alternative buyers were readily available anyway.

### One-line takeaway
Gujarat Themis Biosyn is transforming into an integrated fermentation-based CDMO platform backed by major capacity expansion and two strategic overseas acquisitions (MicroBiopharm Japan and Sanofi brands).
