# Aurobindo Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 10,263 crore with 21% EBITDA margins and reaffirmed full-year FY27 guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 10,263 crore (up 24% YoY).
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 2,156 crore (up 28% YoY); EBITDA Margin at 21%.
- **Gross Margin:** 61.2% (vs 59.7% in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT:** Rs 1,124 crore.
- **Formulations Revenue:** Rs 9,102 crore (up 24% YoY; ~89% of consolidated revenue).
- **API Revenue:** Rs 1,162 crore (up 21% YoY; ~11% of consolidated revenue).
- **US Revenue:** Rs 4,555 crore / USD 477 million (up 25% YoY); launched 10 products, filed 7 ANDAs, and received 12 final approvals in Q2.
- **Europe Revenue:** €288 million (up 18% YoY in constant currency).
- **Growth Markets Revenue:** Rs 1,026 crore / USD 107 million (up 16% YoY).
- **ARV Revenue:** USD 38 million.
- **R&D Expenditure:** Rs 376 crore (vs Rs 344 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **CapEx:** USD 89 million in Q2 (USD 47 million for development capabilities and USD 42 million for strategic priorities).
- **Cash Position:** Net cash position of USD 189 million; free cash flow generated in Q2 was USD 146 million.
- **Average Finance Cost:** 4.9%.

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 10,263 crore, driven by growth across US, Europe, and API segments.
- Completed acquisitions of Lannett (expanding capabilities in complex/controlled substances) and A1 Biochem (establishing an integrated small-molecule CRDMO handling >800 projects).
- Launched ADQUEY, the company's first NDA/NCE product for atopic dermatitis, establishing a dedicated dermatology business unit.
- Launched generic Advair and received approval for generic QVAR in the complex inhalation segment.
- Received Voluntary Action Indicated (VAI) classification for the Raleigh facility.
- China oral solid dosage facility is nearing breakeven in Q2 FY27 with upwards of 15 products commercialized.
- Pen-G plant is operating EBITDA positive (prior to PLI); producing ~10,000 tonnes out of 15,000 tonnes total capacity to fulfill Indian demand (~9,000 tonnes), internal consumption, and exports.
- TheraNym biologics contract manufacturing business is operating on a pure-play CMO model anchored around one key customer (MSD) for Schedules 1, 2, and 3.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Guidance Reaffirmed:** Double-digit revenue growth, EBITDA margin >21%, and absolute EBITDA >Rs 8,000 crore, with potential upside from strategic businesses.
- **FY28 Outlook:** Management expects FY28 to be significantly better than FY27, driven by full-year Lannett benefits, China plant ramp-up, and expanding biosimilar contributions.
- **EBITDA Margin Target:** Management expects to expand EBITDA margins to upwards of 22% in coming years (FY28).
- **CapEx Guidance:** Routine maintenance and development CapEx expected to run at ~$50 million per quarter (~$200 million annually).

### Demand & industry commentary
- **US:** Non-steroidal topical dermatology market is estimated at ~$1 billion (growing ~20% annually). Inhalation/respiratory space offers multi-billion dollar addressable market opportunities across MDI and DPI formats.
- **Europe:** Sustaining double-digit growth; UK market accepting biosimilars (Trastuzumab, Bevacizumab) with repeat NHS orders.
- **Biosimilars:** Industry tier-1 generic competitors are making long-term investments; Aurobindo/CuraTeQ is pursuing a targeted, deliberate strategy focusing on complex formats (subcutaneous and device combinations) and regional partnerships (e.g., Orion, STADA).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material procurement costs rose due to regional issues in the Gulf; full cost impact on consumption to manifest in upcoming quarters.
- 6-APA prices increased 10–15 days prior to the call; domestic pricing impact expected within 15–20 days.
- Freight rates increased during the quarter.
- Margin expansion was driven by operating leverage across European and US units and product mix improvements.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Maintenance and development CapEx run-rate maintained at ~$50 million/quarter.
- Lannett manufacturing capacity currently underutilized; management is evaluating selective, high-margin CDMO opportunities (~10–15% capacity currently utilized for CDMO).
- Vizag Unit 15 serves as a core manufacturing driver for European sales; Vizag injectables scaling up.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a net cash balance of USD 189 million.
- Strong operating discipline generated USD 146 million in net operating cash flow during the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher raw material procurement and freight costs from supply chain and Gulf disruptions flowing into next quarter's consumption.
- Long adoption and market access timelines associated with commercializing branded NDA products (ADQUEY).
- LOE (Loss of Exclusivity) timing dependencies for major inhalation product launches ( inflection timeline targeted around 18+ months out / FY29).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Clarification on the timeframe and drivers for long-term EBITDA margin targets?**  
  *A:* CFO clarified the long-term margin target is 22% (not 28%). Drivers include Lannett capacity/yield optimization, China facility reaching breakeven, and scaling biosimilars/biologics.
- **Q: Does Lannett drag down consolidated EBITDA margins, and what is its CDMO strategy?**  
  *A:* Management clarified Lannett's EBITDA is close to Aurobindo's corporate average. Cost rationalization benefits are flowing through. Lannett currently uses 10–15% of capacity for CDMO; management will add CDMO contracts selectively based on margin profiles.
- **Q: What is the status of pricing, capacity, and anti-dumping duty for the Pen-G plant?**  
  *A:* Anti-dumping duty petitions are filed and government notifications issued. 6-APA prices increased recently. The plant is EBITDA positive without PLI benefits, producing ~10,000 tonnes (out of 15,000 tonnes capacity) to serve domestic demand (~9,000 tonnes) and captive/export needs.
- **Q: Are there plans to onboard additional CDMO partners for the TheraNym biologics facility beyond MSD?**  
  *A:* TheraNym is structured strictly as a CMO around one anchor customer (MSD) for Schedules 1, 2, and 3 to establish track record; any business model expansion to other clients would be considered post-2030.
- **Q: What is the timeline for the inflection in the complex respiratory/inhalation business?**  
  *A:* Near-term growth relies on generic Advair and an impending in-house MDI launch. Larger pipeline filings depend on LOE events over the next 12–18+ months, making mid-2029 (FY29) the target window for full scale-up.
- **Q: What is Eugia’s revenue growth outlook and geographic split?**  
  *A:* Eugia remains on track for double-digit growth in FY27. Geographic mix shifted from 70:30 (US:RoW) historically to 55:45 currently, moving toward a 50:50 balance by FY28–29.

### One-line takeaway
Aurobindo Pharma delivered record Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs 10,263 crore with 21% EBITDA margins, leveraging US and Europe market strength while integrating Lannett and advancing its respiratory and biosimilar pipeline.
