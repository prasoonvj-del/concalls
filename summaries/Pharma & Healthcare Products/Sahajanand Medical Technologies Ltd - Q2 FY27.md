# Sahajanand Medical Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-08 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever revenue, EBITDA, and PAT driven by broad-based growth across product categories and regions with expanding margins.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 373 crores, up 24.7% YoY (H1 revenue up 24.9% to INR 725 crores; constant currency growth ~16% in Q2, ~15% in H1).
- **EBITDA:** INR 65 crores, up 36.6% YoY (H1 EBITDA up 43.4% to INR 114 crores).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17.6%, up 154 bps YoY (H1 EBITDA margin at 15.7%).
- **PAT:** INR 26 crores, up 26.6% YoY (H1 reported PAT up 7.5% to INR 41 crores; underlying H1 PAT growth ~39% excluding a Q1 FY26 one-off tax credit of INR 8.7 crores).
- **Gross Margin:** H1 gross margin at 74.3% vs 76% YoY (down ~170 bps, primarily due to mix shift towards traded products and regional mix).
- **ROCE:** 16.7% on an annualized basis (up from 12.2% in FY 2026).
- **Return on Equity:** 11.2% (up from 9.8% in FY 2026).
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** Improved to 128 days in H1 FY27 (down from 138 days in FY26 and 144 days in FY25).
- **Net Debt:** INR 223 crores as of September (Net debt to EBITDA multiple close to 1x; cash and liquid investments at INR 114 crores, total borrowings at INR 300 crores).
- **Capacity Utilisation (H1):** DES Surat at 79%, Telangana at 20%; Balloon Surat at 72%, Telangana at 57%; Surat drug-coated balloon line at 40%.

### Highlights
- Growth came from all three product categories: Vascular intervention (58% of H1 revenue, up 16% YoY), Structural heart (accelerated in Q2, up ~40% for both TAVI and occluders), and Other products (fastest-growing in H1, up 52% largely due to EU IVUS sales).
- Geographic performance: ROW grew fastest in H1 (up 37%, now 40% of revenue); Europe grew steady at 23% (36% of revenue); India grew 15% in Q2 (up from 6% in Q1).
- Operating leverage resulted in employee costs falling from 28.8% to 27.1% and other expenses falling from 31.5% to 29.7% of revenue.
- Regulatory updates: Hydra Elevate received CDSCO approval; AVDC 1.5 received CE approval; Sironity (sirolimus drug-coated balloon) commercialized in India; Supraflex Cruz achieved EU MDR certification (with Pipit NC/SC audits complete, and Hydra/Cocoon technical files submitted ahead of end-2027 deadlines).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for a 150-200 bps EBITDA margin improvement every year (targeting ~20% EBITDA margin by FY 2029).
- Top-line constant currency growth guidance maintained at mid-to-high teens (conservatively mid-teens baseline for current and next year).
- Capex guidance for FY 2027 and FY 2028 is broadly INR 70 crores per year.
- Working capital days expected to decline further from 128 days to around 100-110 days over the next three years.
- Effective tax rate (ETR) expected to remain in the 32%-35% range for the next 2-3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural heart expected to grow faster than vascular intervention due to a lower base, larger addressable market, and faster growth.
- DGHS (Government of India) notification on single-use medical devices acts as a favorable tailwind for single-use products like PTCA catheters (Pipit SC and NC), though implants like stents and TAVI were already single-use.
- H2 revenue typically accounts for ~52% of the annual total (vs ~48% in H1).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined by ~170 bps YoY to 74.3% in H1 due to faster growth in traded products (lower gross margins) and shifts in regional mix, alongside investments in quality and higher input/labor costs.
- Traded products expansion drives operating leverage via existing sales teams, helping EBITDA grow faster than revenue.
- R&D expenditure maintained at 9%-10% of revenue, fully expensed through P&L with no capitalization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- INR 44 crores of capex incurred in H1 FY27; full-year FY27 and FY28 capex guided at INR 70 crores annually.
- Capex focused on vertical integration, automation, maintenance at the Surat plant, and capacity enhancement.
- Telangana plant provides substantial headroom to scale without proportional capital spend; capacity additions will not require massive capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total equity at ~INR 745 crores and borrowings at INR 300 crores.
- Capital employed increased by INR 72 crores in H1, primarily due to working capital build.
- Inventory days remain a key focus area for reduction to optimize working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher tax rate (ETR at ~35%) driven by high tax jurisdictions (Spain, Brazil, Germany) and subsidiaries in losses where deferred tax assets are not created.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Capex and Peak Revenue Potential:** Analyst asked about peak revenue potential from existing assets and the INR 70 crores annual capex. Management stated capex is focused on vertical integration and capabilities rather than raw capacity addition, and declined to give a peak revenue calculation due to product-mix variances (especially Hyderabad plant scaling).
2. **EBITDA Margin Trajectory:** Analyst inquired about steady-state margins. Management guided for 150-200 bps margin expansion annually to reach ~20% by FY29, driven by operating leverage in international markets (Europe) where upfront sales teams are already built.
3. **EU MDR Status for Portfolio:** Asked about timelines and status of other products. Management confirmed Supraflex Cruz is MDR certified, Pipit NC and SC completed stage 1 & 2 audits, and Hydra and Cocoon files are submitted, all expected well within the end-2027 deadline.
4. **European Structural Heart Strategy & Market Share:** Asked about market share and cross-selling in Europe. Management stated TAVI market share in Europe is low single digits (~1.5%-2%), but cross-selling via the existing coronary stent sales force is highly effective since the implanting physicians are identical.
5. **Trading Segment Growth (IVUS):** Queried about the rising share of traded products. Management explained that IVUS demand surged following favorable reimbursement guideline updates in developed markets, acting as an EBITDA-accretive tailwind despite gross margin dilution.

### One-line takeaway
SMT delivered a record Q2 FY27 with 24.7% revenue growth and 36.6% EBITDA growth, underpinned by operating leverage, expanding structural heart sales, and a robust international pipeline.
