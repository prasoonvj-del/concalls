# Alembic Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth across all business segments, significant EBITDA margin expansion, and raised full-year revenue guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,603 crore (up 36% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 4,753 crore (up 31% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 22.2% (expanded ~520 bps YoY from 17%).
*   **Gross Margin:** 73.7% (expanded 70 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 335 crore (up 81% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 1,236 crore (<1x full-year EBITDA).
*   **R&D Expenditure:** 8% of revenue.
*   **Capex (H1):** Rs 250 crore.

### Highlights
*   **U.S. Generics:** Exceptional performance with 72% YoY revenue growth, driven by base portfolio volume growth (23%) and new launches (e.g., bosutinib).
*   **India Business:** Delivered 14% YoY growth; Human Health grew 11% YoY, with chronic and specialty therapies outperforming. Animal Health grew 23% YoY.
*   **API Business:** Sustained strong trajectory with 39% YoY volume-led growth.
*   **U.S. Branded Business:** Currently contributes ~3% of total U.S. revenue; management noted it is tracking in line with expectations.
*   **Strategic Collaboration:** Expanded partnership with DifGen Pharmaceuticals for 20+ products (transdermal patches, complex injectables, ophthalmics); contributions expected from FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue Growth:** Raised to 20%–25% (from mid-teens).
*   **U.S. Business:** Expected to grow up to 40% YoY for FY27.
*   **API Business:** Expected to deliver at least high-teens growth.
*   **India Business:** Target is to reach market-level growth (IPM) in the next 2–3 quarters.
*   **Ex-U.S. Markets:** Expected to grow at mid-teens.
*   **U.S. Branded Business:** Management expects to break even by the end of FY27 or H1 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **India Market:** Chronic and specialty segments are performing well; acute portfolio recovery is expected over the next few quarters.
*   **U.S. Market:** Pipeline supports a cadence of 30+ launches over the next 18 months, with ~25% being First-to-File (FTF) or shared Day-1 opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin Guidance:** Maintained at 70%–75%.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Management expects the current ~22% margin profile to hold for the second half of FY27.
*   **Cost Pressures:** Higher logistics/freight costs and solvent costs were noted but offset by favorable product mix and currency depreciation.
*   **U.S. Branded Drag:** A 150–200 bps dilution on margins is expected for the full year due to U.S. branded business investments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex Strategy:** Major cycle is behind; focus is on debottlenecking and incremental line additions rather than greenfield investment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Currently elevated due to longer collection cycles on new U.S. launches, expected to unwind over coming quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Exclusivity:** Bosutinib exclusivity ends in November 2026; management is uncertain about the competitive landscape post-exclusivity.
*   **Acute Portfolio:** India acute business continues to face execution challenges, though management expects improvement.
*   **Logistics:** Volatility in shipping lines and crude oil prices remains a factor for freight costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you quantify the impact of the U.S. branded business (Pivya) drag?** A: Management confirmed a 150–200 bps dilution for the full year, expected to turn to breakeven by H1 FY28.
*   **Q: Is the India business growth sustainable?** A: Management believes commercial interventions (focus on chronic/specialty, field force productivity) are working and expects to bridge the gap to IPM growth in 2–3 quarters.
*   **Q: What is the nature of the DifGen deal?** A: It is a licensing deal involving profit sharing; DifGen handles most manufacturing, while Alembic manages the front-end.
*   **Q: How will margins evolve post-exclusivity products?** A: Management expects to maintain 70%–75% gross margins and aims for ~20% EBITDA margins in the near term as operating leverage kicks in and the branded business breaks even.

### One-line takeaway
Strong U.S. and API performance drove a significant earnings beat, prompting a guidance upgrade, while the company shifts focus to operational efficiency and scaling its U.S. branded franchise.
