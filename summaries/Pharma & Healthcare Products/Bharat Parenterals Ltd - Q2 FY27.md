# Bharat Parenterals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a "commercial inflection" with the group moving from loss to profit, driven by strong performance in Innoxel and recovery in the standalone business.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 136.3 crore (up 110% YoY, up 45% QoQ).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 26.6 crore (17.3% margin).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 11.8 crore (vs. loss of Rs 8.6 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 78.6 crore (up 88.4% YoY, up 41.5% QoQ).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 7 crore (9% margin, down from 12.3% in Q1 FY27).
*   **Innoxel Revenue:** Rs 44.5 crore (up 253% YoY, up >100% QoQ).
*   **Innoxel EBITDA:** Rs 14.6 crore (33% margin).
*   **Varenyam Healthcare Revenue:** Rs 19.7 crore (up 33.2% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Commercial Inflection:** Management stated Q2 is the first quarter where the group's profit inflection is visible.
*   **Innoxel Performance:** Innoxel is now the largest EBITDA contributor (producing >60% of group EBITDA). Revenue is primarily driven by licensing and milestone income.
*   **US Commercial Supply:** First product under a CMO agreement was exported to the US on October 20, 2026.
*   **Standalone Recovery:** Domestic tender book reduced from Rs 119 crore to Rs 65 crore as supplies were executed.
*   **Fundraise:** Board approved a preferential issue of 9,12,759 shares at Rs 1522.85/share, raising ~Rs 139 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Standalone:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 10-15% and EBITDA margin of 10-15%.
*   **Innoxel:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 35-45% and EBITDA margin of 20-25%.
*   **Varenyam Healthcare:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20-25% and EBITDA margin of 8-13%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Capacity:** General block ran at 70% utilization in H1; Beta-lactam at 33% and Cephalosporins at 31.5%.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Management noted a shortage of qualified mammalian scale-up capacity for biologics, driving the pivot toward CDMO services in Varenyam Biolifesciences.
*   **US Market:** Management expects 10-15% market conversion for 505(b)(2) NDAs in the first couple of years post-launch.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Input Costs:** Standalone gross margin declined to 35% (from 44.1% in Q2 FY26) due to sharp increases in input material prices caused by geopolitical conflicts.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Innoxel's cost base is largely fixed; revenue growth is flowing directly to profit.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Varenyam Biolifesciences:** Targeting construction completion by Q2 of calendar year 2028.
*   **Capex:** Standalone business spent Rs 15 crore on capex in H1.
*   **Innoxel:** Installed a new liposomal skid; exhibit batches scheduled for November.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Flow:** Consolidated cash from operations was Rs 83.3 crore in H1.
*   **Debt:** Consolidated borrowings stood at Rs 160 crore. Debt-to-equity is 0.08x.
*   **Receivables:** Standalone trade receivables reduced to Rs 105 crore (from Rs 120 crore in March).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Margin Pressure:** Standalone margins remain under pressure on orders priced before the recent input cost spike.
*   **Lumpy Revenue:** Innoxel's licensing and milestone income is lumpy and does not arrive evenly every quarter.
*   **Regulatory:** EU GMP inspection dates are not yet confirmed.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Will Innoxel cross Rs 300 crore revenue by FY29?** Management declined to give a specific number, stating it is too early, but acknowledged the likelihood based on the pipeline.
*   **Q: Can Innoxel margins exceed 50-60% at scale?** Management noted that margins will improve with profit-share models but declined to provide a specific target, preferring to wait for commercialization.
*   **Q: Is the fundraise for Varenyam Bio or Innoxel?** Management stated funds are earmarked for all subsidiaries based on requirements and earnings potential.
*   **Q: How does the 505(b)(2) pricing work vs. innovators?** Management stated 505(b)(2) products typically launch at a 20-40% discount to the innovator but remain above generic levels.
*   **Q: Are you competing with Chinese CMOs?** Management stated they are not competing with Chinese CMOs as they focus on differentiated, complex products rather than volume-based generics.

### One-line takeaway
Bharat Parenterals has achieved a significant operational inflection point, with Innoxel's high-margin CDMO model now driving group profitability while the standalone business recovers.
