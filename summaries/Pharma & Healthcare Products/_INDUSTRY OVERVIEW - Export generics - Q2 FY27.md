# Pharma & Healthcare Products - Export generics: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the export generics sector reported a strong Q2 FY27, characterized by robust double-digit revenue growth and healthy operating profitability. Growth was largely propelled by expanding footprints in regulated markets (US and Europe) and resilient domestic branded formulations. However, operational performance was tempered by rising logistics, freight, and raw material costs driven by West Asian and Red Sea geopolitical disruptions, alongside base-portfolio price erosion in select US generics.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board noted healthy, broad-based demand across the US, Europe, and India chronic/specialty segments. Domestic chronic therapies generally outperformed acute portfolios.
*   **Divergence:** While companies with strong complex generic and specialty pipelines (e.g., Zydus, Aurobindo, Emcure) reported market share gains and raised guidance, others faced headwinds. Lupin and Natco called out steep price erosion in specific US products (e.g., Tolvaptan and Pomalidomide) due to new generic entrants. Meanwhile, domestic acute therapy growth remained muted or uneven for players like Alembic and Ipca.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Freight and Logistics:** Geopolitical disruptions in West Asia, the Gulf, and the Red Sea severely impacted logistics. Marksans, Strides, Granules, and Ipca (specifically subsidiary Unichem) highlighted doubled freight costs and reliance on air shipments due to transit delays.
*   **Input Costs:** Raw material and solvent costs trended higher, though many companies (such as Alembic, Lupin, and Zydus) successfully offset these pressures through favorable product mix, operational efficiencies, and currency tailwinds.

## Capex & capacity
*   Major greenfield capex cycles are largely tapering off for several players, shifting toward debottlenecking and specialized capacities. 
*   **Expansion Highlights:** Gland Pharma outlined a ~Rs 3,000 crore capex plan over 4 years; Granules initiated a INR 2,000 crore organic capex program focused on peptide CDMO and U.S. platforms; Zydus is doubling its fully-utilized transdermal patch capacity; Kwality acquired land for Unit 7; and Alembic noted its major capex cycle is behind it.

## What changed since last quarter
Several companies upgraded their full-year outlooks following strong H1 execution:
*   **Alembic Pharmaceuticals:** Raised full-year revenue growth guidance to 20%–25% (from mid-teens).
*   **Emcure Pharmaceuticals:** Raised full-year revenue growth guidance to exceed 20% (from mid-teens).
*   **Kwality Pharmaceuticals:** Revised FY27 revenue guidance upwards to Rs 720 crore+ (from Rs 700 crore+).
*   **Natco Pharma:** Raised full-year PAT guidance to INR 775–800 crores (from ~INR 750 crores).

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Regulatory & Compliance:** Progress on remediation and responses to US FDA observations at facilities like Granules' Gagillapur plant and Sun Pharma’s Assam plant.
*   **Logistics & Freight Inflation:** Whether shipping cost pressures and Red Sea disruptions ease or continue to impact margins (notably for Marksans, Strides, and Ipca).
*   **New Product Milestones:** Timelines for high-value launches, such as Zydus’s Saroglitazar US launch preparation, Aurobindo’s respiratory portfolio scale-up, and Emcure’s Zuventus integration.

## Company one-liners
*   **Alembic Pharmaceuticals:** Strong US and API performance drove a significant earnings beat and guidance upgrade.
*   **Aurobindo Pharma:** Delivered record Q2 revenue and 21% EBITDA margins while advancing complex respiratory and biosimilar pipelines.
*   **Emcure Pharmaceuticals:** Robust Q2 revenue growth and margin expansion prompted management to raise full-year revenue guidance to over 20%.
*   **Gland Pharma:** Leveraging a net-cash balance sheet to aggressively expand CDMO capacity while managing temporary Cenexi headwinds.
*   **Granules India:** Achieved record quarterly revenue and 39% PAT growth despite freight cost inflation and 11 new US FDA 483 observations.
*   **Ipca Laboratories:** Delivered strong double-digit growth and margin expansion despite significant logistics cost headwinds.
*   **Kwality Pharmaceuticals:** Scaled global footprint and specialty portfolio, leading to raised FY27 guidance.
*   **Lupin Ltd:** Solid top-line growth in India and Europe buffered near-term US generic price erosion.
*   **Marksans Pharma:** Delivered record Q2 revenue with 30.5% growth backed by strong geographical diversification and net-cash position.
*   **Natco Pharma:** Raised full-year PAT guidance and eliminated all bank debt using rights issue proceeds.
