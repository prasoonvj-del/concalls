# Pharma & Healthcare Products - Domestic formulations: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The domestic formulations and broader pharma sector demonstrated resilient growth in Q2 FY27, driven by strong chronic portfolio traction and volume outperformance against the Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM). Several companies (Cipla, Mankind Pharma, Corona Remedies, Jagsonpal, and Bharat Parenterals) reported robust revenue expansion and improving profitability. However, headwinds persisted in select pockets, including U.S. price erosion (Alkem), freight cost spikes (Indoco), and domestic seasonal weather disruptions.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board are bullish on the chronic and specialty segments, which continue to outpace acute therapies and grow faster than the broader IPM (noted by Alkem, Cipla, Corona, and Mankind). 
*   **Divergence:** Views on acute and trade generics varied. Indoco Remedies reported domestic headwinds from an "absent monsoon" impacting seasonal acute therapies like Febrex Plus and Oxipod. Alkem turned more cautious on trade generics due to intense competition from major players and credit tightening. Meanwhile, Mankind Pharma noted temporary weakness in its consumer healthcare division across East and South India, even as its core prescription business surged.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Inflation & Freight:** Indoco Remedies highlighted a ~30% increase in COGS, RMPM, and a Rs 13 crore freight cost impact stemming from geopolitical conflicts. Bharat Parenterals noted margin pressure on standalone orders due to input material price spikes. Alkem flagged the incoming full-period impact of Minimum Import Prices (MIP) on Pen G and amoxicillin/clavulanic acid in H2.
*   **Margin Expansion & Resilience:** Cipla expanded EBITDA margins by 200 bps QoQ to 18.7% on an improved product mix. Mankind Pharma saw gross margins rise to 72.2% (+90 bps YoY) via price increases and favorable mix. Jagsonpal maintained stable standalone EBITDA margins at 22.7% despite growth investments.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Alkem Laboratories:** Planned Enzene capex of INR 300–350 crore over 3 years; MedTech capex of INR 400 crore over 3–4 years.
*   **Aptus Pharma:** Planning an initial manufacturing capex of ~Rs 35 crore (Rs 25 crore funded via a preferential issue) to manufacture ~20% of its formulations in-house by March 2028.
*   **Bharat Parenterals:** Standalone business spent Rs 15 crore on capex in H1; Varenyam Biolifesciences construction is targeted for completion by Q2 CY28.
*   **Mankind Pharma:** Spent Rs 214 crore in H1 FY27, guiding for 6%–7% of revenue for the full year.
*   **Veerhealth Care:** Expanded its Vapi facility to 90,000 sq. ft. and is adding 3 new manufacturing lines by December to reach 90%–100% capacity utilization.
*   **Jagsonpal & Indoco:** Maintaining asset-light models; Indoco reported actual land/other capex under Rs 25 crore for the year with no major capex planned.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Alkem Laboratories:** Adjusted its full-year India growth target down to ~11% (from ~12%) and U.S. growth to mid-single digits.
*   **Cipla:** Maintained its full-year FY27 EBITDA margin guidance (18.5%–20%) and $1 billion North America exit run rate. 
*   **Mankind Pharma:** Maintained EBITDA margin guidance of 25.5%–26.5% for FY27.
*   **Fredun Pharmaceuticals:** Stated that they have already overachieved their INR 800 crore revenue target for FY27.

## Company one-liners
*   **Alkem Laboratories:** Alkem matched IPM growth at 13.8% YoY driven by strong chronic portfolio execution and international momentum, while navigating Daman regulatory hurdles and gestation investments.
*   **Aptus Pharma:** Aptus Pharma reported significant revenue and profit growth in H1 FY27, backed by a diversified 300+ formulation portfolio and prescription-led model.
*   **Bharat Parenterals:** Bharat Parenterals has achieved a significant operational inflection point, with Innoxel's high-margin CDMO model now driving group profitability while the standalone business recovers.
*   **Cipla:** Cipla expanded EBITDA margins by 200 bps QoQ to 18.7% and hit record India sales while maintaining FY27 targets of 18.5%–20% EBITDA margin and a $1 billion US exit run rate.
*   **Corona Remedies:** Corona Remedies reported a robust Q2 FY27 with 22% revenue growth and 22.9% growth in its India business, comfortably outperforming the broader pharmaceutical market.
*   **Fredun Pharmaceuticals:** Fredun Pharmaceuticals posted robust Q2 FY27 results with ~84% YoY revenue growth and ~93% PAT growth, driven by rapid scaling across new-age consumer verticals like pet care and mobility.
*   **Indoco Remedies:** Indoco achieved record consolidated revenue of Rs 506.9 crore, but profitability was constrained by elevated freight costs and domestic seasonal headwinds.
*   **Jagsonpal Pharmaceuticals:** Jagsonpal reported Q2 FY27 standalone revenue of Rs 81 crore and consolidated revenue of Rs 96 crore, backed by successful integration of recent acquisitions.
*   **Mankind Pharma:** Mankind Pharma delivered strong Q2 results with 17% revenue growth and margin expansion, supported by chronic segment strength and volume outperformance.
*   **Veerhealth Care:** Veerhealth Care achieved a major inflection point in Q1 FY27 by matching its entire FY26 turnover in a single quarter, driven by US FDA accreditation and major domestic contract manufacturing wins.
