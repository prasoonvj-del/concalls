# Gland Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by CDMO and US market performance, despite temporary Cenexi shutdown impact.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 1,833.6 crore (+23% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 480.4 crore (+53% YoY).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 503.0 crore (27% margin).
*   **PAT:** Rs 290.8 crore (+58% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 3,633.9 crore (+21% YoY).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 607.7 crore (+52% YoY).
*   **CDMO Revenue:** Rs 822.7 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **Critical Injectables Revenue:** Rs 1,010.9 crore (+18% YoY).
*   **US Revenue:** Rs 1,179.5 crore (+42% YoY).

### Highlights
*   CDMO segment contributed ~45% of total revenue.
*   Vizag oncology facility cleared US FDA inspection with zero observations; Dundigal facility had four procedural observations (no data integrity or repeat issues).
*   New CEO Deepak Sapra joining Nov 16; Ajay Sahni joined as President of Cenexi.
*   *Record Note:* The company continues its aggressive capacity expansion (brownfield + greenfield), maintaining the pattern of heavy investment for future growth.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintained full-year growth guidance of 15% on a constant currency basis.
*   Cenexi profitability expected to return from Q3 onwards.
*   US growth expected to sustain; ROW markets expected to improve.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong global demand for sterile injectables and outsourcing.
*   US market growth driven by new launches and CDMO programs.
*   ROW performance impacted by delays in Saudi Arabia NUPCO tender results.
*   Initiating in-licensing of innovative products from China to enter the Indian market.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Gross margin: 63% for the quarter.
*   R&D spend: Rs 107.7 crore (6% of revenue).
*   Effective tax rate: ~33% (impacted by Cenexi losses).
*   Cenexi reported EUR 41 million revenue with EUR 6 million EBITDA loss due to planned annual summer shutdowns.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Total capex plan: ~Rs 3,000 crore (excluding greenfield) over next 4 years.
*   Brownfield expansion at Pashamylaram: Rs 1,000 crore for three isolator-based vial lines (commercialization FY30).
*   Greenfield project at Vizag SEZ: Planning stage, details to be announced next quarter.
*   Current capacity utilization for liquid vials is 92%, expected to hit 102% next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Cash and cash equivalents: Rs 3,441.2 crore.
*   Net cash position: Rs 3,221.6 crore.
*   Working capital days: 158 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Cenexi remains loss-making due to planned shutdowns.
*   Capacity constraints in liquid vials (utilization at 92%).
*   ROW market volatility due to tender delays (Saudi Arabia).
*   Currency fluctuations impacting reported numbers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Sajal Kapoor (on capex strategy):* Management clarified they invest based on 20% idle capacity thresholds and signed contracts/forecasts, not just soft demand.
*   *Sajal Kapoor (on Cenexi):* Management confirmed they are sticking to previous guidance of high single-digit margins for Cenexi for FY27 despite H1 losses.
*   *Dhawal Khut (on China strategy):* Management explained the new CIO role in China is for global acquisitions and in-licensing complex products/technologies.
*   *Karan Vora (on CDMO vs. Injectables):* Management clarified CDMO margins are 5-10% better than the core business.

### One-line takeaway
Gland Pharma is leveraging a strong balance sheet to aggressively expand capacity for its high-growth CDMO business while navigating temporary profitability headwinds at Cenexi.
