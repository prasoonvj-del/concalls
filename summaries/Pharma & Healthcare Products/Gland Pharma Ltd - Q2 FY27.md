# Gland Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong 23% YoY revenue growth, 53% EBITDA growth, 58% PAT growth, CDMO momentum (45% of sales), US market 42% growth, robust net cash position, and accelerated capacity expansion.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 18,336 million (Rs 1,833.6 crore), +23% YoY  
- EBITDA: INR 4,804 million, +53% YoY; margin 26% (adjusted 27%)  
- PAT: INR 2,908 million (Rs 290.8 crore), +58% YoY  
- H1 FY27 Revenue: INR 36,339 million, +21.4% YoY; Adjusted EBITDA margin 28%; PAT +52% YoY  
- CDMO Revenue: INR 8,227 million, +31% YoY (45% of total); H1: INR 17,142 million, +25% YoY (47%)  
- Critical Injectables Revenue: INR 10,109 million, +18% YoY  
- US Revenue: INR 11,795 million, +42% YoY; H1: INR 21,605 million, +37% YoY  
- Europe & Regulated Markets: INR 4,110 million, +23% YoY  
- Rest of World: INR 2,044 million (impacted by NUPCO tender delay)  
- India: INR 387 million  
- Gross Margin: Q2 63%, H1 64% (flat YoY)  
- R&D Spend: INR 1,077 million (6% of revenue)  
- Cash & Equivalents: INR 34,412 million; Net Cash: INR 32,216 million  
- Operating Cash Flow H1: INR 6,027 million; Working Capital Days: 158  
- Capacity Utilisation (liquid vials): 92%, expected 102% next year without expansion  
- Cenexi: Revenue EUR 41 million, EBITDA loss EUR 6 million (planned shutdowns)  
- Milestone Income: INR 95 crore; Profit Share: INR 202 crore (11% and 5%)  

### Highlights
- CDMO now 45% of revenue (47% H1), growing 31% YoY; critical injectables +18% YoY  
- US growth driven by new launches (4 in quarter) and CDMO programs; no lumpiness  
- Quality: Vizag oncology US FDA zero observations; Dundigal four procedural (non-data integrity, non-repeat)  
- Capacity expansion: INR 2,000 crore program on track (ophthalmic, BFS, oncology isolator); bag capacity awaiting FDA inspection  
- New brownfield at Pashamylaram: INR 1,000 crore, three isolator-based vial lines, commercial FY2030  
- Greenfield at Vizag SEZ: land acquired, board approval next quarter, 4-year phased program  
- Total capex outlook ~INR 5,000 crore over 4 years (INR 3,000 crore brownfield + ~INR 2,000 crore greenfield) adding 300-400M units  
- R&D: 11 ANDAs filed, 10 approvals, 4 US launches; focus on complex injectables (suspension, peptide, hormones, ophthalmic)  
- Two in-licensing deals for long-acting injectables from China in last two quarters: first $800M (manufacture India), second $2B (manufacture China, clinicals by Gland, 3-year timeline)  
- Leadership: CEO Deepak Sapra joining Nov 16; Cenexi President Ajay Sahni onboarded; key hires in tech transfer, project management, strategy, BD China, CIO China  
- Cenexi transformation: expect profitability from Q3; FY27 guidance EUR 200M revenue at high single-digit margin maintained  
- Full-year guidance: 15% constant currency growth maintained  

### Guidance & outlook
- Full-year revenue growth: 15% on constant currency basis (maintained)  
- US growth to continue (new launches + CDMO); ROW to improve post NUPCO tender results  
- Cenexi profitability from Q3; effective tax rate to normalize  
- Capex: INR 3,000 crore committed brownfield (2,000 + 1,000); greenfield amount TBD next quarter  
- GLP-1: tech transfer revenue in FY27; launches possible Q1/Q2 FY28  
- In-licensed products: first ($800M) tech transfer to India; second ($2B) manufactured in China, clinicals by Gland  
- India strategy: focus on innovative products via in-licensing, not generics  

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global sterile injectable demand rising; outsourcing trend expanding  
- Strong innovator/specialty pharma interest in CDMO partnerships; pipeline robust in quality and scale  
- Microspheres & respiratory: three contracts signed, revenue post 2029-2030  
- US: volume growth, new launches, CDMO momentum  
- Europe: customer engagement, higher volumes, recent launches  
- ROW: healthy fundamentals; NUPCO delay temporary (13 products participated)  
- Complex injectables (GLP-1, peptides, suspensions) key focus  

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin flat YoY (63% Q2, 64% H1); sequential dip due to Cenexi lower revenue (higher gross margin business)  
- EBITDA margin expansion (26% reported, 27% adjusted vs ~21% YoY) despite R&D increase to 6% of revenue  
- H1 adjusted EBITDA margin 28%  
- CDMO margins 5-10% better than critical injectables (regulated market focus)  
- Cenexi: high gross margin but high costs; cost reduction via headcount optimization and faster lines  
- Effective tax rate ~33% Q2, ~30% H1 (elevated due to Cenexi losses with no tax benefit)  

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ongoing INR 2,000 crore: ophthalmic, BFS, oncology/sterile isolator capacity  
- Bag capacity installed, awaiting US FDA inspection  
- New brownfield Pashamylaram: INR 1,000 crore, three isolator vial lines, commercial FY2030  
- Greenfield Vizag SEZ: land acquired, planning phase, board approval next quarter, 4-year phased  
- Capex backed by signed CDMO contracts with volume forecasts (some take-or-pay, some not)  
- Liquid vial utilisation 92% → 102% next year without expansion  

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash INR 32,216 million (cash INR 34,412 million)  
- H1 operating cash flow INR 6,027 million  
- Working capital days improved to 158 (inventory optimization)  
- No debt; strong flexibility for organic/inorganic growth  

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cenexi loss-making 3 years post-acquisition; FY27 guidance requires H2 EUR 111M at 18-20% margin  
- NUPCO tender delay impacting ROW revenue  
- Capacity constraints in liquid vials, ophthalmic, bags – risk of lost revenue if expansions delayed  
- Dundigal US FDA: four procedural observations  
- Higher tax rate until Cenexi profitable  
- Greenfield capex unfinalized; execution risk  
- GLP-1/in-licensing timelines uncertain  
- Some CDMO capex without take-or-pay commitments  
- Cenexi transformation risk: cost cuts, line upgrades with shutdown periods
