# Windlas Biotech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - 15th consecutive quarter of record revenue and crossing Rs. 500 crores in H1 revenue for the first time.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 264 crore (up 19% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 512 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (ex-ESOP):** Rs. 36 crore, margin at 13.7%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT (ex-ESOP):** Rs. 25 crore, margin at 9.5%
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA (ex-ESOP):** Rs. 70 crore, margin at 13.7%
- **H1 FY27 PAT (ex-ESOP):** Rs. 50 crore, margin at 9.7%
- **Reported EBITDA / PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 29 crore / Rs. 18 crore
- **Reported EBITDA / PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs. 56 crore / Rs. 36 crore
- **H1 FY27 EPS:** Highest-ever at Rs. 17.21
- **CDMO Revenue:** Q2 FY27 at Rs. 215 crore (up 34% YoY); H1 FY27 at Rs. 422 crore (up 32% YoY)
- **Trade Generics and Institutional Revenue:** Q2 FY27 at Rs. 36 crore; H1 FY27 at Rs. 66 crore
- **Export Revenue:** Q2 FY27 at Rs. 12 crore (up 26% YoY); H1 FY27 at Rs. 23 crore (up 46% YoY)
- **Net Liquidity / Cash:** Rs. 223 crore as of H1 FY27
- **Dividend Paid (FY26):** Rs. 13 crore (Rs. 6.30 per share)
- **Buyback Size:** Rs. 47 crore completed without promoter participation
- **ROCE / ROE:** 28% and 24% respectively
- **IPM Volume Growth:** 2.3% during the quarter

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs. 500 crores in half-yearly revenue for the first time in H1 FY27.
- Plant 6 commercialized in September 2026 for oral solid dosage expansion.
- Plants 4 and 5 (injectable facility) received EU GMP certification during Q2 FY27.
- Reshaped trade generics portfolio by replacing codeine-based products with new non-codeine launches.
- (Continues record: Plant 6 commercialization and EU-GMP certifications for Plants 4 and 5 are fully confirmed in the call).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its stance of not providing formal guidance for segments, quarterly margins, or specific growth rates.
- Management remains optimistic about long-term growth across all three verticals (CDMO, trade generics, and exports).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian pharmaceutical market (IPM) reported a volume growth of 2.3% during the quarter.
- Regulatory enforcement has tightened significantly across India, with regulators picking up more than 1 lakh samples for random testing and shuttering non-compliant plants; management views stricter quality standards as beneficial for organized players.
- Codeine-based formulations faced industry-wide pullbacks by brand owners.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 13.7% due to operational efficiencies and scaling.
- Trade generics carry higher material margins compared to the CDMO business.
- API price volatility exists across 700–800 APIs, but management attributes overall growth to team execution rather than raw material price swings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Plant 6 commercialized on September 28, 2026, adding oral solid capacity (serving up to INR 1,100 crores at company level, with 10% potential efficiency improvement).
- Future oral solid and injectable capacity expansions will be triggered once existing facilities approach peak utilization.
- Working on an internal opex-reduction/operational-efficiency project involving an estimated Rs. 25 crore to Rs. 30 crore capex.
- Actively evaluating inorganic acquisition opportunities in other dosage forms, though nothing is in final stages.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Closed H1 FY27 with net liquidity (cash) of Rs. 223 crore.
- Funded capex and buyback (Rs. 47 crore) from internal resources without promoter participation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Export markets and product registrations have longer lead times and binary outcomes.
- Trade generics revenue was impacted over recent quarters due to the phase-out of codeine-based products.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Krisha Shah (Mangal Keshav Finance) asked about Plant 6's small initial capacity and future export focus.** Management replied that capacity is scaled phase-wise as peak levels are reached, and exports are being built systematically through plant approvals (EU GMP, Philippines) and dossier filings.
2. **Ankit Gupta (Analyst) asked about the sustainability of CDMO growth and the impact of API/crude prices.** Management declined to give short-term guidance, stating growth is a result of business development efforts rather than API price movements.
3. **Ishit Desai (FODs Family Office) asked about trade generic margins and whether the company would move into innovator/early-stage development.** Management clarified trade generics have higher material margins, and regarding innovator work, they are fully focused on the massive opportunity within their existing three generic/CDMO verticals.
4. **Dhwanil Desai (Analyst) asked about European CDMO traction and injectable approvals.** Management noted they have submissions/filings in Europe for injectables but no approvals yet, and commercial revenues will reflect in the P&L as approvals land.
5. **Gautam Gosar (Analyst) asked about capital allocation given the Rs. 220+ crore cash balance and low capex.** Management stated cash provides optionality for future capex, potential acquisitions, buybacks, and dividends in a disciplined manner.

### One-line takeaway
Windlas Biotech delivered its 15th consecutive quarter of record revenue (up 19% YoY to Rs. 264 crore in Q2 FY27), crossing the Rs. 500 crore half-yearly milestone for the first time, backed by strong CDMO traction, newly commercialized Plant 6, and EU-GMP-certified facilities.
