# Granules India Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted record revenue crossing INR15,000 million, 8 consecutive quarters of sequential growth, and robust growth in EBITDA and PAT despite challenging input costs.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR15,600 million, up 20% YoY and 6% QoQ.
- **Gross Margin:** 64.4%, down 134 bps YoY and 126 bps QoQ.
- **Gross Profit:** INR10,039 million, up 18% YoY and 4% QoQ.
- **EBITDA:** INR3,376 million, up 21% YoY and stable QoQ.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 21.6%, up 19 bps YoY and down 131 bps QoQ.
- **PBT:** INR2,434 million, up 38% YoY and 1% QoQ (Margin at 15.6%, up 200 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** INR1,818 million, up 39% YoY.
- **R&D Expenses:** INR885 million (5.7% of sales), up 26% YoY and 1% QoQ.
- **Net Cash/Debt:** Net cash position of INR8,280 million (compared to net debt of INR1,012 million in Q1 FY27).
- **Net Working Capital to Sales:** 30% (compared to 29% in Q1 FY27 and Q2 FY26).
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 231 days (vs 229 days in Q1 FY27).
- **Cash Flow from Operations:** INR650 million (vs INR3,874 million in Q1 FY27 and INR1,937 million in Q2 FY26).
- **Capex:** INR1,304 million (vs INR890 million in Q1 FY27 and INR2,112 million in Q2 FY26).
- **ROCE:** 17.6%, up 138 bps YoY.

### Highlights
- Crossed INR15,000 million in quarterly revenue for the first time in company history.
- Gagillapur facility underwent a U.S. FDA cGMP inspection (Oct 19–30), receiving 11 Form 483 observations with no data integrity, cross-contamination, or facility upkeep issues; no impact on manufacturing or distribution.
- Senn Tides' Zurich site completed Swissmedic GMP inspection with no critical findings and acceptable GMP compliance.
- Shift towards high-barrier areas: CII, ADHD, oncology, MUPS, complex formulations, and first-to-file strategies (e.g., DYANAVEL XR, Sodium oxybate ER, Ruxolitinib ER).
- 26 ANDAs awaiting U.S. FDA approval.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects current growth rate and progress to continue without change.
- Profitability and absolute profit expected to grow despite input/freight cost pressures.
- Senn Tides aims for EBITDA/PAT neutrality by FY27 end, depending on Q4 product/project mix.

### Demand & industry commentary
- North America grew, led by controlled substances; Europe sustained momentum; Rest of World (LATAM) expanding.
- Geopolitical developments in West Asia continue to pressure input and logistics costs; customer price adjustments follow with a lag.
- Peptide CDMO business (Senn Tides) seeing strong RFP/RFQ inflow, revival of a theranostics-related project, and renewed engagement with major peptide CDMO players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin contraction driven by higher input costs from West Asia conflicts, partly offset by lower-cost safety inventory carried into the U.S.
- Other expenses increased QoQ primarily due to higher freight costs from West Asia conflicts.
- Remediation expenses expected to moderate significantly from Q4 onwards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Genome Valley (GLS) capacity utilization currently under 25%, expected to cross 50% by Q4; Gagillapur utilization around 75%.
- Planned INR2,000 crores organic capex over next 3–4 years directed toward peptide CDMO infrastructure (Zurich and India/Vizag), U.S. platform/distribution, oncology launches, API expansions, and IT digitalization.
- Vizag peptide facility planning and design underway; investment is not contingent on specific customer tie-ups.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Moved to a net cash position of INR8,280 million following the receipt of balance proceeds of warrants.
- Inventory deliberately built up in the U.S. for safety stock, impacting short-term cash conversion and operating cash flows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- West Asia conflicts pressuring input and logistics/freight costs.
- Ongoing U.S. FDA 483 observations at Gagillapur requiring CAPA submission and remediation.
- Timing of early-entry complex/FTF opportunities depends on regulatory and litigation outcomes, causing uneven contributions across quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sajal Kapoor (Antifragile Thinking) asked about Senn Tides' H2 targets and Vizag capex contingencies.** Management stated they are hopeful of closing near EBITDA/PAT neutrality based on heavy H2 executions, and confirmed the Vizag capex proceeds independently of specific customer lock-ins.
2. **Sajal Kapoor asked about Gagillapur 483 observations versus previous warning letter remediation.** Priyanka Chigurupati clarified that the 11 observations were fresh and did not concern areas cited in the prior warning letter.
3. **Tushar Manudhane (Motilal Oswal) asked about Gagillapur capacity utilization and timelines.** Management noted current utilization is ~75%, and responses to the 483 will be submitted within 15 business days, with resolution typically taking ~90–100 days.
4. **Shashank Krishnakumar (Emkay Global) asked about other expenses and remediation costs.** Mukesh Surana attributed the increase to West Asia-driven freight costs and stated remediation expenses will moderate from Q4.
5. **Yash Mehta (Grit Securities) asked about the return profile of the INR2,000 crore capex given lower GLS utilization.** Management stated hurdle rates protect ROCE, and optimal utilization across new and existing assets will generate strong double-digit ROCE.
6. **Sameer Baisiwala (Sakman Capital) asked about growth drivers during the gap years prior to FTF launches in 2.5–3 years.** Management pointed to 5 pending U.S. launches in H2, non-U.S. global filings across geographies, and product licensing.

### One-line takeaway
Granules India delivered record Q2 revenue of INR15,600 million and 39% PAT growth, backed by strong complex generic demand and a transition to a net cash balance sheet, even as it addresses 11 new U.S. FDA observations at Gagillapur and West Asia-driven freight inflation.
