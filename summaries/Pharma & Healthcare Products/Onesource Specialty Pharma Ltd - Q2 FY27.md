# Onesource Specialty Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Third consecutive quarter of sequential growth with strong revenue and EBITDA expansion driven by new DDC capacity.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Increased 37% YoY, 15% QoQ, exceeding INR5 billion (Rs 500 crore).
- **EBITDA:** Grew 53% YoY, 32% QoQ.
- **EBITDA Margin:** Expanded over 400 bps QoQ to 32%.
- **H1 EBITDA Growth:** 47% YoY.
- **FY28 Outlook:** Reaffirmed $400 million revenue and $160 million EBITDA.

### Highlights
- Second DDC cartridge line running commercial batches as of September, doubling sterile production days despite a 6-month geopolitics-induced delay.
- Third DDC line arrived at site from Germany, ready for qualification; commercialization expected by start of FY28.
- Partner Hikma launched the first generic semaglutide in Saudi Arabia.
- Dr. Reddy's API supply commenced for restarting in Canada and launching in multiple other approved markets.
- Secured multiple new biologics wins, including two end-to-end development-to-commercialization projects from a U.S. biotech and a program for a top global animal health company.
- Planned injectables site shutdown initiated at the end of Q2 to add prefilled syringes and new lyophilization capacity.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be stronger than H1.
- Reaffirmed FY28 guidance of $400 million revenue and $160 million EBITDA.
- Anticipates ROCE returns to reach the high 20s by the end of FY28.
- Debt levels anticipated to remain under 1.5x EBITDA.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global lyophilization demand remains strong, with shortages largely driven by injectables.
- Semaglutide demand remains robust; India units grew from 20,000–25,000/month pre-patent expiry to >200,000/month in September; Canada market expanded from 8 million to 12-17 million units.
- Biologics RFP funnel is at its highest ever.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage resulted in EBITDA growth outpacing revenue growth.
- Geopolitical shipping delays impacting supply chains and working capital cycle times by 45 to 60 days, causing minor P&L freight/fuel friction, but raw material pass-through insulates P&L.
- Currency fluctuation impact reported at approximately $350k.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Second cartridge line doubles sterile production days; operates at 500-liter batch sizes.
- Third line arrived for qualification; targeted to be fully operational for FY28.
- Injectables facility shutdown ongoing in Q2/Q3, with new capacity available from January.
- Planning a greenfield softgel site to handle high-potent softgels and expand capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade receivables increased in H1 due to higher shipment volumes and temporary 45-60 day transit delays from geopolitical routing.
- Brought-forward losses expected to maintain the current ETR until at least Q3 FY28.
- Net debt-to-EBITDA expected to stay under 1.5x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues in the Gulf caused extended transit times and delayed the arrival/start of new manufacturing lines by about 6 months.
- Working capital cycles impacted by delayed shipping routes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rupesh Tatiya (Long Equity Partners) on softgel utilization and DDC ramp-up:** Management stated softgel visibility is 80%+ with a greenfield site planned; cartridge line ramp-up is fast due to strong demand.
- **Abdulkader Puranwala (ICICI Securities) on receivables:** Management clarified that higher receivables are due to volume growth and temporary 45-60 day transit delays, not credit quality issues.
- **Girish Bakhru (OrbiMed) on batch sizes and realizations:** Management noted batch sizes on new lines are 500 liters (doubling output/capacity), and realizations are volume-based rather than market-based, with efficiency savings shared with customers.
- **Chirag Shah (White Pine) on employee costs and consumables:** Management stated employee costs are conscious investments for specialized Unit 2 capabilities, while consumables vary with manufacturing/engineering batch activity.
- **Harshal Patil (ASK Wealth Advisors) on tax rate:** Management stated brought-forward losses will keep ETR at current levels until at least Q3 FY28.

### One-line takeaway
Onesource delivered a strong Q2 with 37% revenue growth and 32% EBITDA margins, backed by new DDC line commercialization and a robust biologics order funnel supporting its FY28 guidance.
