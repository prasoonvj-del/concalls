# Evonile Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong strategic progress in building an independent platform and portfolio, offset by transition-related revenue contraction and one-off costs.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 56 crore (vs Rs 90 crore in Q2 FY26; includes Rs 41 crore from a major customer in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 160 crore (vs Rs 178 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Employee Costs:** Rs 14 crore (vs Rs 5 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Other Expenses:** Rs 11 crore (flat YoY excluding one-off expenses).
- **Adjusted Operating EBITDA:** Negative Rs 16 crore for the quarter; Rs 19 crore for H1.
- **One-time Items / Exceptional Costs:** Rs 83 crore total, comprising an exceptional item of Rs 68 crore (including Rs 61 crore from the termination of exclusive promotion rights and inventory arrangements with a large customer) and transition/acquisition-related one-off expenses of about Rs 15 crore in other expenses.
- **Expected Q3 One-time Costs:** Rs 15 crore to Rs 20 crore.
- **Expected Finance Cost:** Rs 15 crore for Q3; Rs 10 crore to Rs 15 crore for Q4.
- **Incremental Quarterly Expense Run-rate:** Rs 45 crore to Rs 50 crore higher compared to the Q1 base once fully stable.

### Highlights
- Company transitioned from a single-customer model to an independent operation with its own distribution network (over 20 CFAs onboarded), supply chain control, and standalone systems (accounting, HR, field reporting).
- Acquired cardiology brand Minipress XL from Pfizer (market share >50%).
- Signed an exclusive distribution and promotion agreement for an ophthalmology retina portfolio (Accentrix and Pagenax).
- Flagship brands now span across seven therapy areas: cardiology, pain management, wellness, transplant, gynecology, neurosciences, and ophthalmology.
- Appointed an independent chair and experienced leadership team.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to complete the field force build-out (targeting ~900 medical representatives, including ~300 for Minipress) by Q4 FY27.
- Business expected to stabilize by Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28, with Q1 FY28 serving as the right measure of the stable annualized base.
- Organic revenue base expected around Rs 600 crore, with an ambition to double it organically over the next 5 to 6 years, supplemented by acquisitions.
- Debt reduction planned significantly by March 31, 2027, via a rights issue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM) long-range growth expected within 8% to 10%, driven by volume growth (3% to 4%), price growth (5% to 6%), and new introductions (1% to 2%).
- Underlying demand for inherited brands remains strong due to high doctor trust and prescriber recall (flagship brands like Voveran, Methergene, Calcium Sandoz, and myFORTic hold #1 positions in covered markets).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin impact from product mix and new sourcing efficiencies expected to be visible starting Q1 FY28 due to existing bridging stock in the market.
- Employee and selling costs will step up in phases through H2 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Building a dedicated therapy-aligned field force across metros and smaller towns.
- Continuously scouting for opportunistic, financially prudent, and strategically synergistic M&A and in-licensing deals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Intent to keep the balance sheet leveraged reasonably without maintaining a very high debt load.
- Debt reduction targeted by March 31, 2027, through a rights issue.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Transition effects and one-time correction in revenue caused near-term operating losses (adjusted operating EBITDA of negative Rs 16 crore).
- Upfront investments in people and systems preceded revenue realization.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aman Goyal (IIFL Capital) asked about MR strength and amortization of Minipress goodwill:** Management stated they are targeting ~900 MRs by end of Q3/early Q4 (with ~300 dedicated to Minipress), hiring experienced industry personnel. Goodwill amortization for Minipress is planned over 20 to 25 years.
- **Akash Dobhada asked about market share retention for Minipress:** Management expressed high confidence in growing Minipress ahead of the market (which grew at ~9% vs brand at ~6%) by deploying a dedicated field force.
- **An analyst asked about sourcing efficiencies and gross margins:** Management noted that bridging stock is currently being cleared, and gross margin improvements will be visible from Q1 FY28 onwards.
- **An analyst asked about finance costs and net debt reduction:** Management guided for Q3 finance costs of ~Rs 15 crore and Q4 of Rs 10 to 15 crore, with significant debt reduction expected by March 31 via a rights issue.
- **Sanjay (Gold Stone Capital) asked about FY28 revenue guidance:** Management clarified that while the stabilized annualized base is around Rs 600 crore (achievable by Q4 FY27/Q1 FY28), FY28 will see growth built on top of that base.

### One-line takeaway
Evonile Pharma is executing a foundational transition from a single-customer model to an independent, multi-therapy direct-to-market player, absorbing near-term one-off transition costs while targeting above-market organic growth and debt reduction by FY28.
