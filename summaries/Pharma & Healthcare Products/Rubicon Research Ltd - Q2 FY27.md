# Rubicon Research Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance across revenue, EBITDA, and PAT with upgraded full-year EBITDA margin guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 577 crore, up 40% YoY (vs Rs 412 crore in Q2 FY26; Q1 FY27 was Rs 534 crore). 
- **Operating pre-R&D EBITDA:** Rs 206 crore (35.8% margin) vs Rs 141 crore (34.1% margin) in Q2 FY26.
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 144 crore (25.0% margin), up 53% YoY vs Rs 94 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **Total EBITDA (with other income):** Rs 146 crore, up 54% YoY vs Rs 95 crore in Q2 FY26.
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 90 crore, up 67% YoY vs Rs 54 crore in Q2 FY26 (Q1 FY27 PAT was Rs 85 crore, which included a one-time insurance claim of Rs 3.6 crore).
- **EPS:** Rs 5.41 vs Rs 3.47 in Q2 FY26.
- **R&D Expenses:** Rs 62 crore (10.8% of revenue).
- **H1 FY27 Performance:** Revenue at Rs 1,111 crore (+45% YoY), Operating EBITDA at Rs 273 crore (+57.5% YoY), PAT at Rs 175 crore (+80% YoY).
- **Arinna Lifesciences contribution:** Added Rs 19.4 crore to Q2 FY27 revenue, with immaterial EBITDA contribution.

### Highlights
- Sequential USD revenue grew 5% QoQ, resuming growth following previous tactical product rationalizations.
- Received 3 product approvals in Q2 FY27; overall commercialization rate stands at 84%.
- Specialty products contributed 36% to gross profit.
- Over Rs 400 crore (>25% of total capital employed) is currently tied up in pre-revenue/early-stage assets (Pithampur, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, New Jersey facilities, and Arinna).

### Guidance & outlook
- Operating EBITDA margin guidance upgraded for FY27 to **24%+** (up from prior guidance of 23%+).
- Cumulative R&D spend guided at Rs 500 crore over the three-year reference cycle (FY26, FY27, and Q1 FY28); Rs 313 crore completed over the past 6 quarters.
- Net working capital expected to normalize in the range of 125–130 days.
- Management strategy for Arinna Lifesciences is to first beat Indian Pharma Market (IPM) revenue growth before turning focus to profitability.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pricing environment remains stable, supported by focus on specialty and differentiated formulations.
- Growth remains broad-based with stable revenue contribution from top 5 and top 10 products.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross and operating EBITDA margin expanded YoY despite freight and geopolitical cost pressure.
- Margin expansion driven by higher specialty share, legacy product market share gains, operational leverage, and rationalization of lower-margin products.
- All R&D spends continue to be fully expensed through the P&L.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New Jersey facility added Rs 85 crore to fixed assets (Rs 29 crore cash payout, Rs 55 crore non-cash right-of-use asset).
- Pithampur (Indore) manufacturing site expected to generate revenues from FY28 onwards.
- Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) and New Jersey sites currently remain pre-revenue pending filings and regulatory steps.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow for H1 FY27 stood at Rs 99 crore.
- H1 FY27 investing cash outflow was Rs 172 crore (Arinna, New Jersey acquisition, capex).
- Net working capital stood at 117 days as of September 30, 2026 (improved from 126 days in March 2026 due to quarterly fluctuations).
- Annualized ROACE stood at 36% as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **US FDA Inspections:** Recent unannounced inspection in October 2026 at Ambernath resulted in 5 procedural observations (3 systems improvement, 2 product application-related); Satara site in October 2026 had 1 procedural observation. Management confirmed zero data integrity issues.
- **Regulatory change:** Revised Indian licensing rules now mandate export license applications only after US FDA product approval, shifting certain product launch timelines into subsequent quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nikhil Mathur (HDFC MF):** Query on unproductive R&D spend.  
  *Management:* Follows a stage-gated approach to stress-test and de-risk projects before committing major capital, keeping unproductive R&D spend minimal.
- **Nikhil Mathur (HDFC MF):** Clarification on impact of pre-revenue sites on returns.  
  *Management:* Confirmed that capital for Pithampur, CSN, New Jersey, and Arinna sits in the balance sheet denominator without material profit contribution yet in the numerator.
- **Gagandeep Singh:** Timeline for revenue contribution from non-operating plants.  
  *Management:* Pithampur revenue starts in FY28; CSN has no fixed date; New Jersey requires product filings and FDA inspection before revenues start.
- **Harsh Kundnani:** Drivers of margin expansion and Arinna's long-term margin potential.  
  *Management:* Expanded margins stem from portfolio mix and market share gains. For Arinna, immediate priority is executing revenue growth levers to beat market growth prior to focusing on margins.

### One-line takeaway
Rubicon delivered 40% YoY top-line growth and upgraded full-year EBITDA margin guidance to 24%+ driven by specialty portfolio scaling and strong R&D productivity.
