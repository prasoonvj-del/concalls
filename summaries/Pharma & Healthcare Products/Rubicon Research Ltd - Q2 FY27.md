# Rubicon Research Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sequential and year-over-year growth across revenue and profitability, along with an upgrade in EBITDA margin guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 577 crore in Q2 FY27 vs Rs 412 crore in Q2 FY26 (up 40%); H1 FY27 revenue at Rs 1,111 crore (up 45% YoY).
- **Pre-R&D EBITDA:** Rs 206 crore (margin at 35.8% vs 34.1% in Q2 FY26); H1 pre-R&D EBITDA at Rs 394 crore (35.4% margin).
- **R&D Expense:** Rs 62 crore (10.8% of operating revenue).
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 144 crore vs Rs 94 crore in Q2 FY26 (up 53%); H1 operating EBITDA at Rs 273 crore (up 57.5% YoY).
- **EBITDA (including other income):** Rs 146.4 crore vs Rs 95 crore in Q2 FY26 (up 54%).
- **PAT:** Rs 90 crore vs Rs 54 crore in Q2 FY26 (up 68% YoY; Q1 FY27 PAT was Rs 85 crore); H1 PAT at Rs 175 crore (up 80% YoY).
- **EPS:** Rs 5.41 vs Rs 3.47 in Q2 FY26.
- **Net Working Capital:** 117 days as of 30th September 2026 (down from 126 days in March 2026 due to quarterly fluctuations; expected to normalize at 125-130 days).
- **ROACE:** 36% as of 30th September 2026 (in line with March 2026).
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 99 crore for H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Arinna Lifesciences acquisition consolidated; contributed Rs 19.4 crore to revenue in Q2, with immaterial EBITDA contribution in both quarters.
- Sequential USD revenue growth rebounded to 5% after tactical measures caused a pause in the previous quarter.
- Three product approvals received in Q2; commercialization rate strong at 84%.
- Regulatory update: All four manufacturing locations (Ambernath, Satara, Pithampur, and New Jersey) inspected by the US FDA within the last six months. Ambernath received 3 procedural systems and 2 product application observations in October 2026; Satara received 1 procedural observation in October 2026; Pithampur received 2 observations in July 2026. None relate to data integrity.
- *Note vs Record:* The call notes regulatory inspections across all plants within six months, continuing the strong compliance focus noted in prior investor communications.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Operating EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Management upgraded full-year operating EBITDA margin guidance to **24% plus** (up from the 23% plus guided previously).
- **R&D Spend Guidance:** On track to achieve the guided INR 500 crore R&D spend over the 2026-2027 and Q1 2028 cycle (INR 313 crore already completed over six quarters).
- **Facility Operationalization:** Indore (Pithampur) facility expected to contribute to revenue from FY 2028 onwards. Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (CSN) and New Jersey facilities have no exact timeline provided yet for revenue commencement.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Revenue growth is well-balanced across products; top 5 and top 10 revenue concentrations remain range-bound.
- Pricing remains stable, driven by a focus on specialty and differentiated products.
- Specialty contribution to gross profit stands at 36%.
- Indian regulatory changes now require FDA approval before applying for export licenses, which will impact the launch timing of some approvals into upcoming quarters.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Pre-R&D EBITDA margin improved to 35.8% from 34.1% YoY, supported by a healthy share of specialty products and operating leverage, despite difficult geopolitical situations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fixed asset base for the New Jersey facility grew by Rs 85 crore (cash payout Rs 29 crore, non-cash ROU Rs 55 crore).
- Total capital employed includes ~Rs 400 crore (>25%) allocated to pre-revenue or pre-material contribution assets: Pithampur, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, New Jersey facilities, and Arinna Lifesciences.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt/cash movement driven by the acquisition of Arinna Lifesciences, the New Jersey manufacturing facility, and capex.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations continue to present cost and operational challenges.
- Indian regulatory changes for export licensing require post-approval applications, potentially delaying immediate commercial shipping.
- Pre-revenue facilities (Pithampur, CSN, New Jersey) and Arinna are currently adding to capital employed without material revenue contribution, impacting ROACE denominator effects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nikhil Mathur (HDFC Mutual Fund) on R&D waste/efficiency and productivity metrics:** Management stated they use a rigorous stage-gated approach to keep non-productive R&D low, and R&D productivity has risen over rolling 3-year cycles (reaching 5.8x for the 2024-2027 cycle).
2. **Nikhil Mathur on pre-revenue facilities:** Management confirmed that Pithampur, CSN, and New Jersey are currently contributing zero revenue while adding to the denominator of ROACE.
3. **Gagandeep Singh on timelines for new manufacturing plants:** Management clarified Indore (Pithampur) revenue contribution is expected from FY 2028 onwards, while CSN and New Jersey timelines were kept open.
4. **Harsh Kundnani on drivers of margin expansion:** Management attributed the margin expansion to a combination of specialty product mix, operating leverage, stable pricing, and legacy product market share gains, after absorbing geopolitical headwinds.
5. **Harsh Kundnani on Arinna integration and margins:** Management stated they are currently focused on driving Arinna's revenue growth to beat IPM growth before focusing on profitability metrics.

### One-line takeaway
Rubicon delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with 40% revenue growth and 53% operating EBITDA growth, prompting management to upgrade full-year operating EBITDA margin guidance to 24% plus.
