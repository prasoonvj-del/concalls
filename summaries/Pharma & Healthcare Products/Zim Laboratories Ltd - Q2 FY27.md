# Zim Laboratories Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Transition year with ongoing regulatory remediation, though seeing encouraging sequential recovery and demand visibility pending EU-GMP recertification.

### Key numbers
- **Total Operating Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 1,054 million (up 19% YoY, up 12% QoQ).
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 77 million (broadly flat YoY vs Rs. 78 million in Q2 FY26; up from Rs. 34 million in Q1 FY27).
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 7.3% (up 365 bps QoQ vs 3.7% in Q1 FY27).
- **Net Loss (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 21 million (improved from net loss of Rs. 40 million in Q1 FY27).
- **Total Operating Income (H1 FY27):** Rs. 1,996 million (up 24% YoY).
- **EBITDA & Margin (H1 FY27):** Rs. 111 million; 5.6% margin (vs 8.4% in H1 FY26).
- **PBT (H1 FY27):** Net loss of Rs. 61 million (vs loss of Rs. 23 million in H1 FY26).
- **NIP and OTF Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 149 million (up 84% YoY), contributing 14% of total operating income (16% including dossier license fees).
- **NIP and OTF Contribution (H1 FY27):** Rs. 337 million including license income (up 68% YoY vs Rs. 200 million in H1 FY26).
- **Pharmaceutical Revenue Contribution (Q2 FY27):** ~Rs. 830 million or 79% of total operating income.
- **H1 YoY Growth:** Pharma grew 16% YoY; Nutraceutical grew 58% YoY.
- **Total Debt (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs. 1,462 million (comprising term loan of Rs. 841 million and working capital loan of Rs. 621 million).
- **Gross Block:** Increased to Rs. 4,188 million (from Rs. 3,862 million at end of FY26).
- **R&D Allocation (Q2 FY27):** Rs. 99 million or 9.4% of total operating income (including Rs. 45 million towards bio-equivalent studies and regulatory filings). H1 total R&D allocation: ~Rs. 182 million.
- **One-time / Remediation OPEX (H1 FY27):** ~Rs. 5.5 crores incurred in EU-GMP consultant fees and separation costs.

### Highlights
- Nagpur manufacturing facility underwent joint re-inspection by German and Portuguese regulatory authorities in May 2026; management received the draft final report, submitted proactive CAPA responses, and is awaiting the signed final inspection report and complete CAPA acceptance.
- Received a letter from TGA Australia, opening up the Australian market with initial small test market orders and enquiries received.
- Modernized and operationalized new Nutraceutical and Cephalosporin (CEPHA) facilities; old CEPHA units, Unit-3, and ancillary Nutra units taken out of use to right-size cost base and drive automation.
- Received 4 marketing authorizations during Q2 (1 NIP, 3 OTF); total filings stand at 48 across Pharmerging and ROW markets.
- Export contributed ~86% of total operating income in Q2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue of Rs. 415-Rs. 425 crores organically, which could reach Rs. 450-Rs. 460 crores if EU recertification and supplies materialize.
- H2 FY27 growth projected at ~10%-20% over H1 numbers.
- Long-term vision for FY30 targets 20%-25% compounded revenue growth and high-teens EBITDA margins (18%-20%) once revenues scale to ~Rs. 700 crores and operating leverage kicks in.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand visibility in regulated markets (EU/UK) remains strong with firm commitments and partner interest, led by products like Pancreatin and Tamsulosin.
- ROW growth remains stable across LATAM, Southeast Asia, Middle East, and Africa.
- Legacy business expected to grow at a steady ~10%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins maintained at ~57%-58% range.
- Employee and operating expenses increased in H1 due to top-level hiring, validation staffing for new Nutra/CEPHA plants, and EU-GMP consulting/remediation fees (~Rs. 5.5 crores one-time in H1).
- Management states one-time EU consultant and separation costs will not fully recur in H2, reducing monthly OPEX run-rate towards ~Rs. 15 crores.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gross block increased to Rs. 4,188 million due to recent investments (~Rs. 200 crores in CAPEX for Pancreatin suite and Nutra facility).
- Management states no substantial future CAPEX is required to meet targets up to FY30; existing capacity can scale up to Rs. 700 crores (operating at 80%-90% utilization by then).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt stood at Rs. 1,462 million (Term loan: Rs. 841 million, Working capital loan: Rs. 621 million) as of September 30, 2026, up from Rs. 607 million term loan at FY26-end due to capital investments.
- Focus remains on improving working capital efficiency and operating cash flows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory timelines for final EU-GMP certification depend entirely on European authorities and EMA meeting schedules.
- Geopolitical uncertainties and conflicts in the Middle East present external global volatility risks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Anil Desai (Turtle) asked about EU-GMP timelines and whether FY27 revenue could reach Rs. 450 crores with 30%-40% growth next year.** Management (Dr. Anwar Daud / Zain Daud) replied that they are in the final stages of responding to minor clarifications on the draft report, expect UK supplies to potentially initiate upon in-principle CAPA acceptance, and target Rs. 415-Rs. 425 crores base, scaling to Rs. 450-Rs. 460 crores if EU supplies start.
2. **Dhyey Naik asked about the geographical contributors to export growth and TGA Australia commercialization.** Management stated LATAM and Southeast Asia drove growth, while Australian test orders are ongoing with commercial supplies expected in Q3 and meaningful revenue in Q4/FY28.
3. **Poojit Agarwal (Individual Investor) asked if 20%-25% CAGR to Rs. 700 crores by 2030 requires more CAPEX.** Management stated current capacity and recent Rs. 200 crore investments are sufficient up to Rs. 700 crores without major new CAPEX, with EBITDA margins expanding to 18%-20% via operating leverage.
4. **Akshay Darji asked about expected EU approval timelines.** Dr. Anwar Daud answered that once final clarifications are submitted in a couple of days, EMA/UK processes could take 1 to 1.5 months depending on authority review schedules.
5. **Rohit (ithought PMS) asked about the normalized monthly OPEX run-rate post one-time remediation costs.** Zulfiquar Kamal clarified that H1 monthly run-rate averaged ~Rs. 15.5 crores due to new hirings and ~Rs. 5.5 crores in H1 one-time remediation costs, which will normalize closer to Rs. 15 crores going forward.
6. **Ankit Gupta asked for a breakdown of the projected Rs. 130-Rs. 140 crores EU revenue next year.** Zain Daud confirmed that Pancreatin is expected to contribute Rs. 70-Rs. 80 crores, Tamsulosin Rs. 20-Rs. 30 crores, and other smaller products Rs. 5-Rs. 10 crores each, backed by existing partner orders awaiting EU-GMP clearance.

### One-line takeaway
Zim Laboratories is navigating a transition year marked by heavy remediation and staffing investments, awaiting final EU-GMP clearance to unlock high-margin regulated market growth and operating leverage.
