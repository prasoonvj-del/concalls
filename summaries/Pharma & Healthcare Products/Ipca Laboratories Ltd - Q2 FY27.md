# Ipca Laboratories Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth across domestic and export segments, with margin expansion despite significant logistics and input cost headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,910 crore (up 14% YoY from Rs 2,557 crore).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 22.97% (vs 21.68% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 26.6% (vs 25.46% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Domestic Formulation:** Rs 1,157 crore (up 14% YoY).
*   **Export Formulation (Standalone):** Rs 653 crore (up 32% YoY).
*   **Promotional Branded Business (ROW):** Rs 172 crore (up 19% YoY).
*   **Generic Market (ex-tender):** Rs 364 crore (up 36% YoY).
*   **Institutional Generic Business:** 45% growth for the quarter.
*   **API Business:** 6% growth for the quarter.

### Highlights
*   **Brand Performance:** Zerodol umbrella brand crossed Rs 1,500 crore in sales (IQVIA, Sept 2026); now the 4th largest brand in IPM.
*   **Segment Growth:** Chronic segment grew 20% (vs IPM 17%); Acute segment grew 11% (at par with market).
*   **Unichem Performance:** EBITDA margin was 11.3%; impacted by Rs 30 crore in additional freight costs.
*   **Geographic Performance:** Strong generic growth in Canada (45%), South Africa (46%), U.S. (31%), Australia/New Zealand (24%), and Europe (23%).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management maintains guidance of 14% to 16% for FY27.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management maintains consolidated EBITDA margin guidance of 22% to 23%.
*   **Long-term Margin:** Management expects annual EBITDA margin improvement of 1% to 1.25% (consolidated) and 1.5% (standalone).
*   **Institutional Business:** Likely to exceed the initial Rs 300 crore guidance, potentially reaching Rs 325–340 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Pain Segment:** Growing at 14%–15%; management expects this momentum to continue for the next 3–4 years.
*   **Acute Segment:** Growth is muted in antimalarials, antibacterials, and cough/cold portfolios.
*   **U.S. Market:** Portfolio grew ~9% in dollar terms.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost Pressures:** Significant increases in input, logistics, shipping, fuel, and energy costs.
*   **Freight Impact:** Unichem freight expenses increased by Rs 31.2 crore; air shipment usage rose to 25% (from 5%–6%) due to shipping delays.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Not discussed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Status:** Company is practically debt-free with ~Rs 700 crore cash in books.
*   **Borrowings:** Small rupee term loan of Rs 155 crore; expected to reduce to Rs 70–75 crore by year-end.
*   **Interest Costs:** Expected to decline in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Logistics:** High freight costs and reliance on air shipments (for Unichem) remain a margin drag.
*   **Input Costs:** Fluctuating raw material prices.
*   **Subsidiaries:** Pisgah subsidiary is currently loss-making; profitability expected by next financial year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Zerodol Growth Drivers:** Management attributed 14%–16% growth to ~8%–9% volume growth and ~4%–4.5% price growth.
*   **Unichem Margin Sustainability:** Management noted margins may remain between 11%–13% if freight/energy costs stay high, but expects improvement if the situation normalizes.
*   **Institutional Business Volatility:** Management clarified that the 45% growth was partly due to shipments shifted from the previous year; they remain conservative on future growth.
*   **U.K. Market:** Management noted the U.K. business has stabilized and turned profitable after facing pricing pressure on old generic products last year.

### One-line takeaway
Ipca Laboratories delivered strong double-digit growth and margin expansion despite significant logistics-related cost headwinds, while maintaining a debt-free balance sheet.
