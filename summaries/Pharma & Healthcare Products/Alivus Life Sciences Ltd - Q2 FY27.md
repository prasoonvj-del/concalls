# Alivus Life Sciences Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy execution, significant profitability improvement, and strong momentum across non-GPL and CDMO businesses.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 682 crore, up 16% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 1,322 crore, up 11.1% YoY)
- **Non-GPL business growth:** 20.8% YoY (contributes 79% of overall revenues)
- **GPL revenues:** INR 700 crores to INR 750 crores expected for full year FY27 (Q2 GPL growth at 1% YoY)
- **Generic API business growth:** 11% YoY in Q2
- **CDMO revenue:** INR 50 crore, up 32.3% QoQ and nearly 80% YoY
- **Gross Profit:** INR 439 crore, up 29.5% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 825 crore, up 22.9% YoY)
- **Gross Margins:** 64.4%, up 420 bps QoQ and 670 bps YoY (H1 FY27: 62.3%, up 590 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 285 crore, up 47% YoY (H1 FY27: INR 519 crore, up 38.4% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margins:** 41.8%, up 520 bps QoQ and 880 bps YoY (Adjusted EBITDA margin excluding one-time income: ~39.5%) (H1 FY27: 39.3%, up 780 bps YoY)
- **PAT:** INR 197 crore (H1 FY27: INR 357 crore)
- **PAT Margins:** 28.9%, up 390 bps QoQ and 680 bps YoY (H1 FY27: 27%, up 590 bps YoY)
- **R&D Expenditure:** INR 27 crore in Q2 FY27 (4% of sales); INR 51 crore in H1 FY27 (3.9% of sales)
- **Capex:** INR 105 crore in Q2 FY27; INR 190 crore in H1 FY27
- **Free Cash Flow:** INR 270 crore generated in H1 FY27
- **Cash and Cash Equivalents (incl. short-term investments):** INR 1,012 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **DMF and CEP filings:** Over 630 globally as of September 30, 2026

### Highlights
- Improved product mix, new product launches, and operating efficiencies drove margin expansion.
- One-time items included a validation supply of a complex product with high margins and an income accrual from a customer.
- Chronic therapies contributed 67% to the top line; CVS and CNS led growth, contributing 50%.
- Net debt-free balance sheet with strong cash generation.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated FY27 revenue growth guidance of 10% to 12%.
- Management is confident of delivering EBITDA margins in the 35% to 36% range for FY27 (with a floor of 30% to 32% for next year if nothing works out).
- CDMO business is targeted to double in 3 to 4 years, contributing 12% to 15% of overall revenues.
- Full year GPL revenues expected around INR 700 crore to INR 750 crore with stronger H2 contribution following historical trends.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Healthy demand across regulated and emerging markets (Europe, Japan, North America, LATAM, ROW).
- Pricing environment for generics is stable with regular, manageable erosion (erosion was slightly less in H1).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price inflation managed by passing costs to customers and judicious inventory interventions.
- Solvents constitute ~7% to 8% of COGS (10% to 12% of raw material cost); crude price spikes pose a potential headwind.
- Operational costs controlled by shifting from gas to bio-briquettes for boilers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Solapur facility Phase 1 remains on schedule to become operational in Q3 FY27; initially acts as a backward integration site for intermediates before expanding to API EU-GMP supplies.
- Taloja R&D center construction progressing well, scheduled for early next year.
- Evaluating a dedicated oral solids formulation manufacturing facility (18-24 month timeline).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains net debt-free.
- Generated strong free cash flow of INR 270 crore in H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential crude price volatility impacting solvent costs.
- Middle East conflict impacts on freight costs (though small component).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ahmed Madha (Unifi Capital) on one-off nature and sustainability:** Management explained the one-offs comprised a high-margin validation supply of a complex product and a customer income accrual, and guided normalized gross margins to sustain around 60%.
2. **Yog Rajani (Omega Portfolio Advisors) on CDMO scaling:** Management noted growth was driven by existing projects and newer additions, with discussions underway for further advanced-stage projects.
3. **Ankit Minocha (Adezi Family Office) on sustainable margins:** Management guided a margin floor of 30% to 32% for FY28 even if external factors falter, driven by launch momentum and inventory management.
4. **Nitin Agarwal (DAM Capital) on Solapur strategic impact:** Management highlighted Solapur will act as a major 2-3 year growth driver, initially for backward integration (intermediates) and later for APIs.
5. **Alankar Garude (KOTAK) on flow chemistry benefits:** Management stated commercialized flow chemistry knocked down COGS to less than half compared to batch processing, with 5 more flow projects to execute commercially in 18-24 months.

### One-line takeaway
Alivus Life Sciences delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 16% revenue growth and strong margin expansion, backed by traction in non-GPL and CDMO portfolios, prompting management to reiterate its 10-12% revenue growth and 35-36% EBITDA margin guidance for the year.
