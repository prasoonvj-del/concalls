# Kwality Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited strong revenue growth targets, robust registration pipelines, and aggressive expansion plans.

### Key numbers
- **FY27 Revenue Expectation:** >Rs 700 crore
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** 26% to 27%
- **FY30 Revenue Target:** ~Rs 1,500 crore
- **Oncology Q1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 30 crore to Rs 35 crore
- **Debtor Days:** Expected to come down to 165–170 days (from 208 days)
- **Total Planned Capex (Multi-year):** ~Rs 185 crore to Rs 190 crore (breakup: Hormone ~Rs 70 crore, Oncology ~Rs 50 crore, BE programs ~Rs 25-30 crore, Working capital ~Rs 20 crore, Biosimilar R&D ~Rs 10-15 crore; plus Rs 150 crore for biosimilar clinical trials between Q3 FY28 and Q4 FY29)

### Highlights
- Company has 5 operational units (biologicals, general facility, oncology, cephalosporin, beta-lactam); the 6th unit (hormone plant) will commence by November 2026.
- Unit 5 (biological section) successfully completed pre-clinical studies for Erythropoietin; permission for clinical trials obtained, to begin by November/December 2026.
- Secured multiple general facility registrations in MENA and LATAM regions (Mexico, Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia, Saudi Arabia).
- Planned appointment of KPMG as auditor in Q3 or Q4 FY27, pending software/system upgrades.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue to cross Rs 700 crore to Rs 720 crore with EBITDA margins of 26% to 27%.
- Expects 25% to 30% YoY growth for FY28 (taking 25% as minimum).
- Reaches ~Rs 1,000 crore revenue by FY29 with 29% to 30% EBITDA margins.
- Reaches ~Rs 1,400 crore to Rs 1,500 crore revenue by FY30.
- Hormone facility (Unit 6) expected to generate Rs 70 crore to Rs 80 crore in immediate sales in FY28, scaling to Rs 150 crore to Rs 200 crore by FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Targeting first and second generic markets with a portfolio of 200-250 products across 70-80 countries.
- Each new registration contributes ~1.5 million to 2 million in yearly sales.
- Low competition in niche LATAM markets for approved injectable lines with ready dossiers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q1 FY27 gross margins stood at 53% (improved from 56%–57% in the previous quarter); long-term ideal gross margin guided at 47% as product mix shifts.
- 80% to 85% of total procurement is from domestic APIs; only 15% from China, with no major supply disruptions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Hormone plant manufacturing license expected in November 2026; commercialization in ROW markets thereafter.
- Oncology expansion underway; will enhance capacity by 45% to 50% upon completion by March 2027.
- General facility operating at 75%–80% utilization; Oncology at 75%–80%; Cepha/Beta at 20%–25%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debtor days improving from 208 days to 165–170 days due to collections from MENA, CIS, and GCC regions.
- Capex being funded largely through running cash flows; an extended loan facility (Rs 15–20 crore) kept as backup if needed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted external regulatory delays or timing of approvals by Ministry of Health in various countries as potential variables impacting sales targets.
- Caution against getting carried away by small successes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aryan Mehta (Shravas Capital) on Oncology revenue mix and biologicals:** Management clarified oncology registrations are delayed to Q4 FY27 (guiding 25-30% mix), while Erythropoietin clinical trials begin Nov/Dec 2026 with Indian sales immediate and ROW submissions starting in 4 months.
2. **Utkarsh Somaiya (Elko Quantum Solutions) on Auditor change:** Management confirmed plans to onboard KPMG in Q3/Q4 FY27 once internal software/system upgrades are completed.
3. **Abhijeet on Capex funding and Biosimilars JV:** Management stated capex is funded via internal accruals (holding a backup Rs 15-20 crore loan line) and detailed a joint venture structure in Algeria for local fill-finish of biosimilars.
4. **Kunal Dube on Oncology guidance vs actuals:** Management clarified that Q1 oncology sales were Rs 30-35 crore, maintaining the full-year oncology guidance at around Rs 100-110 crore while expecting stronger general facility registration growth.
5. **Subhanu Bangal (3 Head Capital) on FY30 revenue mix:** Management detailed that by FY30, general generics will comprise 35-40%, hormone 15-20%, oncology 25%, and biosimilars/Erythropoietin 15-30%.

### One-line takeaway
Kwality Pharmaceuticals remains confident in scaling past Rs 700 crore in FY27 and reaching Rs 1,500 crore by FY30, driven by aggressive global registrations, upcoming hormone and biological units, and niche injectable focus.
