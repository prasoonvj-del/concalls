# Tarsons Products Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered second consecutive quarter of over 20% revenue growth, driven by existing product portfolio and strong international export recovery.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained expectation of delivering similar performance for FY27; no specific quantitative growth guidance given.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more positive regarding demand momentum and enquiry levels, though input cost pressures from West Asia tensions persist.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Container availability and transportation logistics flagged as a major bottleneck for export shipments; old risks of US tariffs and slower adoption timelines remained relevant.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Select existing product lines commenced operations at Panchla, radiation line warehousing space and Amta expanded capacities fully operational, while cell culture lines commissioning pushed to H2.

### Key numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs. 100+ crore (grew 25% YoY)
- Consolidated revenue: Rs. 233.9 crore for H1 FY27 (grew 21% YoY)
- Standalone domestic revenue growth: 19% YoY in Q2 FY27
- Standalone export revenue growth: 37% YoY in Q2 FY27 (33% in H1)
- Nerbe revenue growth: 7% in Q2 FY27 and 13% in H1 FY27 YoY
- Standalone gross profit: Rs. 67.4 crore (grew 15% YoY in Q2 FY27)
- Standalone EBITDA: Rs. 30.6 crore (grew 16.9% YoY in Q2 FY27)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs. 31.1 crore (grew 13.3% YoY in Q2 FY27)
- Standalone operating cash flow (H1 FY27): Rs. 49 crore (vs Rs. 40 crore in H1 FY26)
- Consolidated operating cash flow (H1 FY27): Rs. 53 crore (vs Rs. 44 crore in H1 FY26)
- Gross debt: Rs. 370 crore to Rs. 380 crore
- Planned capex for the year: Rs. 30 crore to Rs. 40 crore

### Highlights
- Q2 standalone revenue crossed Rs. 100 crores for the first time in company history.
- Growth was largely driven by the existing product portfolio with minimal contribution from new product categories.
- Panchla and Amta facilities are in the final phase of expansion; select product lines at Panchla commenced operations, while radiation line warehousing and Amta expanded capacities are fully operational. Cell culture lines commissioning expected in H2.
- International export business witnessed a strong recovery, growing 37% in Q2 and 33% in H1.
- Operating expenses of newly established facilities are fully reflected in the P&L, while meaningful revenue contributions are yet to ramp up.
- (Note on record: Cell culture commissioning timeline has slipped from Q2 into H2, continuing past delays).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is optimistic about delivering similar performance for FY27.
- Expects investments to translate into meaningful revenue growth over the next 2 to 3 years, with benefits visible from FY28 onwards.
- Total revenue potential from older facilities (Rs. 400 crore) and newer facilities (Rs. 400 crore) estimated at close to Rs. 800 crores standalone.
- Planned capex restricted to Rs. 30 crore to Rs. 40 crore (commercial and maintenance capex).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Private sector demand (CRO, CDMO, pharma/biopharma, vaccine production) is quite strong.
- Government and semi-government research institute demand is yet to recover and pick up.
- Global crude-linked raw material prices surged between 30% and 50% since the onset of the West Asia conflict in March 2026.
- Container availability and transportation to Europe and US pose a large concern, leading to inventory build-up awaiting shipment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins pressurized by raw material prices staying 25% to 35% higher than original pre-West Asia crisis levels.
- Calibrated price increases implemented selectively to offset input costs without hurting competitiveness.
- EBITDA impact mitigated by volume growth and operating leverage.
- Nerbe EBITDA margins subdued due to new employee hiring, cost inflation, and international sales team expansion costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Final phase of large capex program; select lines at Panchla, radiation line warehousing, and Amta expanded capacities are operational.
- Cell culture lines at Panchla expected to be commissioned in H2 (over 50% of SKUs successfully launched/tested internally; full solution rollout ongoing).
- Sterilization facility at Amta operational for a year, delivering cost savings of 20% to 25% on sterilization costs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total gross borrowing maintained between Rs. 370 crore and Rs. 390 crore.
- Raw material inventory maintained around Rs. 47 crore to Rs. 48 crore; minimum inventory at Rs. 25 crore.
- Inventory days at 100 to 105 days; debtor days at 82 days; overall working capital cycle at 171 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sharp volatility in crude oil and crude-linked commodities due to West Asia conflict.
- Inability to fully pass on total input cost increases to customers due to brutal domestic and international competition.
- Severe container availability and transportation issues delaying export shipments.
- Slower government and semi-government research demand recovery.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya (Securities Investment Management) asked about export growth drivers and sustainability.** Management stated growth was driven by wallet share gains and customer restocking, alongside container backlog issues releasing shipments, and noted long-term growth will normalize below 35-37%.
2. **Aditya asked about the delay in commissioning the cell culture facility.** Management explained the delay is due to the stabilizing of multiple SKUs (>50% launched internally, remaining rolling out over next few months).
3. **Jasdeep Walia (Clockvine Capital) asked about Nerbe's subdued margins and growth.** Management explained that lower EBITDA margins are due to new employee hiring and expansion costs beyond Germany, while gross margins remain stable.
4. **Rushil (Analyst) asked if increased competition has hampered EBITDA margins over a 3-4 year view.** Management clarified that material margins cycle between 68% and 79% based on raw material crises, while EBITDA compression is due to structural overhead costs of new facilities awaiting revenue scale-up.
5. **Rajiv Shah (Investor) asked about gross borrowings and capex plans.** Management stated gross debt will remain around Rs. 370 crore to Rs. 390 crore with annual capex capped at Rs. 30 crore to Rs. 40 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Tarsons posted record Q2 standalone revenue crossing Rs. 100 crore with 25% YoY growth, driven by core demand and a 37% export surge, while profitability absorbed headwinds from 30-50% higher raw material costs and new facility overheads.
