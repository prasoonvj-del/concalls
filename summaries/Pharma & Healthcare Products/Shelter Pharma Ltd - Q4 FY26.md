# Shelter Pharma Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-27 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** H2 & FY2026
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue and profit growth, expansion into new domestic and international markets, and clear long-term growth targets.

### Key numbers
- **H2 FY26 Revenue from operations:** Rs 39.84 crore (up 50% YoY)
- **H2 FY26 EBITDA:** Rs 6.40 crore (up 25% YoY)
- **H2 FY26 PAT:** Rs 4.51 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **FY26 Revenue from operations:** Rs 73.13 crore (up 44% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA:** Rs 12.72 crore (up 26% YoY)
- **FY26 EBITDA margin:** 17.39%
- **FY26 PAT:** Rs 9.03 crore (up 25% YoY)
- **FY26 PAT margin:** 12.3%
- **FY26 ROCE:** 26.04%
- **FY26 ROE:** 19.39%
- **FY26 Export revenue:** Rs 6 crore (up 164% YoY)
- **Segment revenue breakdown:** 55% veterinary, 45% human healthcare
- **Export vs domestic pricing:** Exports are roughly 20% to 25% higher than the domestic market
- **Land acquisition cost:** Around Rs 2.5 crore
- **Upcoming plant capex guidance:** Rs 12 to 15 crore
- **Lemon & Barley Water product revenue:** Rs 3.0 to 3.5 crore
- **Sales team size:** Around 80 people across India (plus 12 office staff, 50 factory staff)
- **Distribution network:** 12,000+ doctors, 5,000+ retail pharmacy network

### Highlights
- Company is celebrating over six decades of experience in natural healthcare and wellness since 1965.
- Portfolio consists of nearly 300 formulations (approx. 200 in human healthcare, nearly 100 in animal healthcare) covering tablets, capsules, powders, syrups, oils, and ointments.
- Export footprint includes UAE, Kuwait, Iraq, Qatar, Nepal, Bangladesh, Mauritius, Yemen, and Sudan, with advanced discussions in Africa.
- Acquired land near Ahmedabad, Gujarat, for a second manufacturing facility to scale operations toward a 2030 vision.
- Promoter stake reduction was explained as internal structuring/family exit rather than an open market stake sale.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets annual revenues of around Rs 200 crore by FY2030 through capacity expansion, innovation-driven product launches, and global market penetration.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to return to 20% to 22%+ over the next 2-3 years, noting current margins are impacted by aggressive sales team and channel expansion.
- Management expects FY27 growth to remain strong, driven by deeper pan-India penetration and doubling of exports.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India Ayurvedic products market was valued at INR 1,070 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach INR 3,728 billion by 2034 (15.52% CAGR).
- Shift towards natural, chemical-free wellness, supported by the Ministry of AYUSH and e-commerce acceleration.
- Middle East war crisis has not impacted exports; essential pharmaceutical shipments to UAE and Africa are proceeding without hurdles or margin dips.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material advances on the balance sheet are for long-term procurement, future export requirements, and building stock to combat price volatility through bulk purchases.
- Human and veterinary medicine margins are similar due to comparable raw material usage.
- Introduction of nutraceuticals is expected to support margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Existing plant in Himatnagar has capacity to scale up to Rs 100 crore in revenue (closed FY26 at Rs 75 crore).
- New plant installation near Ahmedabad is planned to start around the end of calendar year 2027, requiring an estimated capex of Rs 12 to 15 crore funded primarily via internal accruals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is low-debt / debt-free with strong return ratios (ROCE 26.04%, ROE 19.39%).
- Working capital days remain elevated (around 234 days) as the company offers credit periods (targeting reduction from 120/90 days down to 60 days and eventually one month) to new state distributors and suppliers.
- Preference shares were issued primarily for working capital to support the rapidly expanding sales team (scaling from 5 to 80+ personnel).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Higher working capital cycle and negative operating cash flow (-Rs 12.48 crore) driven by market expansion and offering credit terms to new distributors.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinodchandra asked about the nature of loans and advances and promoter stake sale.** Management replied advances are for long-term raw material procurement and exports, while the promoter stake reduction was internal family structuring, not an open-market sale.
2. **Cyril questioned why the company's market cap is still small despite 60 years of legacy.** Management explained that older generations were content with local operations in Himatnagar, and aggressive regional and international expansion only began in the last 10 years.
3. **Urvish asked about capex timelines, expected revenue, and reliance on small-ticket orders.** Management stated the Rs 12-15 crore capex plant installation will begin by end of CY2027, aimed at supporting the FY2030 Rs 200 crore target, and noted plans to tap larger volume channels like state governments and national procurement schemes (KPKB).
4. **Darshan Chandra questioned the drop in EBITDA margins from ~20% to ~17% despite higher revenue.** Management clarified the dip is due to aggressive front-end investments in sales teams and channel expansion, but margins will recover to 20%–22% going forward.
5. **Darshan Chandra asked about the large preference share issue diluting equity.** Management stated the funds were raised for working capital to support the sales force expansion from 5 to 80+ representatives.

### One-line takeaway
Shelter Pharma delivered robust FY26 revenue and profit growth (44% and 25% YoY) and is investing internal accruals into an upcoming Ahmedabad facility and pan-India/export expansion to target Rs 200 crore revenue by FY2030.
