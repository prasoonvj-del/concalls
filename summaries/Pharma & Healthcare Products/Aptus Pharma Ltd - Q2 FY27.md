# Aptus Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong revenue growth, higher profitability, and expansion across verticals.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 48.90 crore (compared to Rs 14.38 crore in H1 FY26)
- Total income (H1 FY27): Rs 48.96 crore
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 7.68 crore
- Profit before tax (H1 FY27): Rs 6.83 crore
- Profit after tax (H1 FY27): Rs 5.08 crore
- Total assets (as of September 30, 2026): Rs 54.55 crore
- Inventory (as of September 30, 2026): Rs 16.25 crore
- Trade receivables (as of September 30, 2026): Rs 28.64 crore
- Employee strength: 142 direct front-line medical representatives on roll; over 150+ total professionals and 200+ sales representatives across distribution networks.
- Attrition ratio: Below 5%-6%
- Initial manufacturing capex planned: Approximately Rs 35 crore (with Rs 25 crore funded via a preferential issue)

### Highlights
- Portfolio comprises over 300 formulations across acute, chronic, wellness, and OTC products.
- Distribution network includes over 250 distributors and sub-distributors, retail availability exceeding 45,000, and trusted by over 10,000 doctors.
- Revenue mix across segments: Acute therapy contributes ~44%, chronic and semi-chronic therapies contribute ~20%, wellness and OTC contribute ~20%, and sales of services represent ~16%.
- Aiming to achieve 46% geographic coverage across India by March 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide specific forward-looking guidance for topline, EBITDA, or PAT margins, or peak revenue potential for the capex.
- Management target: Establish a pan-India presence by 2029 with own manufacturing facilities and expanded product portfolio.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India ranks third globally in pharmaceutical production by volume, supplies ~20% of global generic medicines, and exports to over 200 countries.
- Lead business model is prescription generation, which management stated will continue alongside growing OTC and wellness divisions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management noted that pharmaceutical industry gross margins are generally good.
- Focus is maintained on cost discipline, improving operating efficiency, and managing sales and distribution expenses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Proposed manufacturing expansion by March 2028 with a target to manufacture ~20% of the product portfolio via own capabilities for self-consumption, while remaining capacity will be used for exports to ROW (Rest of the World) countries (no CDMO plans).
- Initial manufacturing capex planned at approximately Rs 35 crore, of which Rs 25 crore will be raised through a preferential issue (pending exchange approval), with the rest arranged as needed.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total assets stood at Rs 54.55 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Inventory was Rs 16.25 crore and trade receivables stood at Rs 28.64 crore.
- Management emphasized close monitoring of working capital, inventory levels, collection efficiency, and credit discipline.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Increasing absolute working capital requirements as revenue scales, requiring careful management of receivables, inventory, and liquidity.

### One-line takeaway
Aptus Pharma reported significant revenue and profit growth in H1 FY27, backed by a diversified 300+ formulation portfolio and prescription-led model, while laying out long-term plans for pan-India expansion and in-house manufacturing by 2028–2029.
