# Symbiotec Pharmalab Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong 43% YoY revenue growth and 88% YoY EBITDA growth driven by API volume expansion, milestone recognition, and key CDMO/injectable wins.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (~20% revenue growth and ~25% EBITDA growth for FY27, +/- 5% points).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more positive following the official signing of the US DCV agreement and the definitive European alternative protein manufacturing agreement.
- New topics or risks: Recognized INR 53 crore in milestone revenue; successfully completed 4 major regulatory inspections (EIR for Rau and Pithampur SEZ, EU-GMP for both); slight couple-month delay on the insulin plant due to SME-driven adjustments.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on signing the definitive US DCV agreement (done Sep 30), converting the European alternate protein term sheet into a binding agreement, and filing the first ANDA in September.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 280 crore (up 43% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue up 25% YoY.
- API Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 227 crore (up 16% YoY); H1 FY27 API revenue up 11% YoY.
- Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27, ex-IPO expenses of Rs 3 crore): Rs 94 crore (up 88% YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA at Rs 140 crore (up 29% YoY).
- PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 52 crore (up 169% YoY).
- Milestone Revenue recognized in Q2: Rs 53 crore.
- H1 FY27 Capex: Rs 113 crore.
- Net Debt (as of Sep 30, 2026): Rs 314 crore.

### Highlights
- Signed a long-term agreement on September 30 for the US market with a leading global company for 2 complex injectable products using the Dual-Chamber Vial (DCV) platform (addressable market ~USD 300 million).
- Signed a definitive manufacturing agreement with a Europe-based alternate protein company for a multi-million liter fermentation capacity (Phase 0 using 100 KL, Phase 1 using 750 KL).
- Completed 4 major regulatory inspections over the last 6 months, receiving EIR from the FDA for Rau and Pithampur SEZ alongside EU-GMP certifications for both.
- First ANDA for a DCV product filed with the FDA in September (accepted without RTR); second filing planned for Q4 FY27.
- Insulin drug substance 5-year take-or-pay contract facility expected to be commissioned in Q4 FY27, with sales commencing mid-FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains guidance of ~20% revenue growth and ~25% EBITDA growth for FY27 (+/- 5% points).
- Corporate-level capex commitment remains disciplined at INR 200 crores to INR 300 crores annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- API growth driven primarily by volume and favorable product/geography mix; ~70% of API business is focused on regulated markets.
- Strong global traction and scarcity of large-scale fermenters position the company well for biomanufacturing CDMO services.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- API gross margins held steady at >60% for Q2 and H1; API EBITDA margins stable at ~30%.
- One-time IPO expenses of Rs 3 crore debited to the P&L in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ujjain fermentation site expanded by acquiring 40 more acres, bringing total land to 70–72 acres with 400 KL existing capacity (Phase 0).
- Phase 1 expansion for the European alternate protein customer will add 750 KL capacity, taking 12–15 months to build.
- Vegan D3 plant construction and qualification on track to finish by the end of this fiscal year, with sales starting early next fiscal year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at Rs 314 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Trade receivables increased due to milestone revenue invoiced on the last day of the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ongoing price pressure on APIs across verticals.
- Regulatory and formulation development timelines depend on partners; insulin plant commissioning delayed by a couple of months due to evolving regulatory standards and SME review.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kunal Dhamesha (Macquarie Capital):** Asked about the European alt-protein agreement size and profile. Management stated it is a 10-year take-or-pay agreement; Phase 0 uses 100 KL this fiscal year, Phase 1 uses 750 KL (12–15 months to build), generating toll fees in the tens of millions of dollars by FY28-29.
2. **Smit Doshi:** Asked about the spike in trade receivables (from Rs 197 cr to Rs 268 cr). Management explained it was due to milestone invoicing on the last day of the quarter, which normalizes subsequently.
3. **Amey Chalke:** Asked if API growth is volume or price-driven. Management clarified it is largely volume-driven along with a favorable product and geography mix, overcoming general API price pressures.
4. **Rahil Dasani:** Asked about alternative protein partner viability and capex risks. Management responded that extensive due diligence is conducted, ensuring partners are premium venture-backed entities with capital and existing take-or-pay customer contracts.

### One-line takeaway
Symbiotec delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 43% revenue growth and 88% EBITDA growth, boosted by API volume growth, Rs 53 crore in DCV milestone revenue, and a major new European CDMO agreement.
