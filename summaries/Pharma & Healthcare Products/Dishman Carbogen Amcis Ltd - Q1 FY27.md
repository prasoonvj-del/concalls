# Dishman Carbogen Amcis Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management highlighted a "soft quarter" due to revenue deferment and forex impacts, but expressed optimism for the full year based on new project acquisitions and operational improvements.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 6,776 crore (down 4% YoY from Rs 7,080 crore).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 600 crore (vs Rs 1,406 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **CDMO Segment Revenue:** Rs 5,343 crore (vs Rs 6,112 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Marketable Molecules Revenue:** Rs 1,432 crore (vs Rs 968 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **CDMO EBITDA Margin:** 6.3% (vs 17.9% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Marketable Molecules EBITDA Margin:** 18.6% (vs 32% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Finance Cost:** Rs 370 crore.
*   **Loss Before Tax:** Rs 512 crore.
*   **Net Debt (excluding lease liabilities):** CHF 153.6 million.
*   **CapEx (Q1):** CHF 4.9 million.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Updates:** Successfully completed MFDS (South Korea) inspection at the Naroda site.
*   **New Leadership:** Appointed Angela Ameriks as Global Chief Commercial Officer to strengthen sales and prospecting.
*   **Project Wins:** One new commercial product from a big pharma client approved by U.S. FDA; 13+ late-phase projects in the portfolio.
*   **Tech Transfer:** Signed a commercial contract to transfer a legacy project from Switzerland to the Bavla site; three other tech transfers are in advanced discussion stages.
*   **Marketable Molecules:** Growth driven by cholesterol and Vitamin D/analogues business; secured new, more attractive wool grease sources.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management expects single-digit YoY growth for the full year.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Management expects margins to be similar to or slightly higher than the previous year.
*   **Long-term (FY28-FY29):** Management expects at least double-digit growth in French and Indian operations, aiming for >10% overall revenue growth and EBITDA margins returning to the 25-26% range.
*   **Indian Entity:** Revenue expected to increase by 30-35% YoY with operating margins near 10%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Order Flow:** Order income is picking up, though market pressure persists.
*   **Biotech Funding:** Management noted a slowdown in funding for biotech companies, impacting development work.
*   **Destocking:** Post-pandemic destocking continues to impact demand.
*   **Lumpiness:** Management emphasized that the business is inherently lumpy due to the nature of new chemical entities and B2B shipment cycles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Forex Impact:** Reported a foreign exchange loss of Rs 117.3 million due to USD/CHF fluctuations.
*   **Cost Structure:** Employee expenses (approx. 50% of P&L) and other expenses are largely fixed.
*   **Revenue Deferment:** A specific order worth Rs 10 million was postponed to the latter half of the year, negatively impacting Q1 results.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity:** Co-investment with Japanese clients is on track, with operations expected to start next year.
*   **Expansion:** Actively recruiting sales roles in Europe (UK, Central Europe) and the U.S. West Coast.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Refinancing:** Shareholders approved raising up to CHF 200 million via external commercial borrowing (ECB) at the promoter entity level to prepay high-cost debt in India.
*   **ECB Terms:** Interest cost of 4% all-inclusive, 10-year tenor; expected to conclude in 60-90 days.
*   **Goodwill:** Standalone balance sheet goodwill is approx. Rs 550 crore; consolidated goodwill is in excess of Rs 4,000 crore (restated at closing exchange rates).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Profitability Volatility:** Management acknowledged the "lumpy" nature of the business, leading to quarterly fluctuations between profit and loss.
*   **French Entity:** Taking longer than expected to reach breakeven.
*   **Regulatory/Compliance:** Ongoing impairment testing for goodwill and the need for continuous regulatory compliance (e.g., EDQM/FDA).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Questioned the lack of stability in quarterly profits. **Management:** Explained that fixed costs (50% employee expenses) combined with B2B shipment lumpiness cause volatility; advised looking at the business on a 2-3 year horizon.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the promoter fundraise mechanism. **Management:** Confirmed it will be a direct ECB infusion into the Indian entity from the promoter entity, backed by personal assets and guarantees.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the "bottleneck" in top-line growth. **Management:** Cited the reliance on development work for 700-800 programs and the time required for these to transition to commercial stages.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the share of ADC revenue. **Management:** Stated it was roughly Rs 150 crore for the quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Dishman Carbogen Amcis reported a weak Q1 due to revenue deferment and forex losses, but management remains confident in a full-year recovery driven by new tech transfers, commercialized molecules, and a planned debt-refinancing exercise.
