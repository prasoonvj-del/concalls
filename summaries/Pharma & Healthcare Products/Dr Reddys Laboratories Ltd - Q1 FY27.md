# Dr Reddys Laboratories Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-28 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Performance pressured by a Rs 240 crore semaglutide API provision and Middle East cost headwinds, but management expressed strong confidence in an H2 recovery and resilient double-digit base business growth.

---

### Key numbers
* **Revenues:** Rs 8,071 crore (US$853 million), down 5.6% YoY, up 7.4% QoQ.
* **Gross Margin:** 46.5% (down 1,039 bps YoY, up 169 bps QoQ); normalized gross margin excluding semaglutide API provision was 49.4%.
* **EBITDA:** Rs 1,009 crore (US$107 million); EBITDA margin at 12.5% (down 1,416 bps YoY, down 55 bps QoQ); normalized EBITDA margin at 15.4%.
* **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 553 crore (6.8% margin); normalized PBT margin at 9.8%.
* **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 443 crore (5.5% margin), down from earlier periods; diluted EPS at Rs 5.32.
* **R&D Spend:** Rs 577 crore (7.1% of revenues), down 8% YoY, up 6% QoQ.
* **SG&A Spend:** Rs 2,882 crore (36% of revenues), up 12% YoY, up 4% QoQ.

---

### Highlights
* **Semaglutide API Challenges:** Operations took a Rs 240 crore provision for inventory and rejected batch costs. The company had sold 180,000 pens prior to pausing supplies, missing an estimated 3–4 million pens. Management identified the root cause and targets resuming API supply by late September and commercial pen supplies by November 2026.
* **Segment Performance:**
  * **North America Generics:** US$236 million (down 41% YoY, up 19% QoQ), impacted by lower lenalidomide revenues.
  * **India:** Rs 1,718 crore (up 17% YoY, 10% QoQ); organic growth was 15.5%. Outperformed IPM (MQT 14.6% vs 13.5% IPM).
  * **Emerging Markets:** Rs 1,833 crore (up 31% YoY, 2% QoQ) across 43 new product launches.
  * **Europe:** €131 million (flat YoY, down 3% QoQ).
  * **PSAI:** US$91 million (down 5% YoY, down 10% QoQ).
* **Key Launches:** Launched bosutinib (with 180-day exclusivity for 400 mg strength) and nintedanib in the US; launched semaglutide in Canada and oral semaglutide in India.
* **Regulatory Inspection:** USFDA inspected Bachupally biologics site (PLI for abatacept/rituximab) in June 2026, issuing 7 observations. Response was submitted within stipulated timelines.

---

### Guidance & outlook
* **Semaglutide Resumption:** Expects to supply 6 to 7 million pens between November 2026 and March 2027.
* **EBITDA Margins:** Management maintains an underlying margin target in the neighbourhood of 20%, expecting margins above this level once semaglutide supplies resume in H2.
* **US Growth:** Guided for double-digit growth in the US base business (excluding lenalidomide). Targeting 27 launches in the US in FY27, including a launch in Q2 with revenue potential in the tens of millions of dollars.
* **Tax Rate & R&D:** Full-year effective tax rate expected at 24%–25%; R&D spend targeted at 7%–8% of sales.
* **Abatacept Approval:** Mid-December 2026 remains the goal date for US FDA IV approval; launch planned immediately upon approval.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* In Canada, semaglutide pricing is CAD 78 with standard distribution discounts; generic conversion is expected to reach 60% and trend higher.
* Core US generic market remains a low single-digit growth environment with continuous price erosion, which Dr. Reddy’s offsets by scaling those products across Europe and Emerging Markets.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Solvents and freight costs inflated by the Middle East conflict reduced EBITDA margin by ~1%; management expects elevated costs to persist through December 2026.
* 75% to 80% of the YoY and QoQ SG&A increases were driven by adverse forex movements and conflict-related freight spikes.
* NRT integration changed accounting (rebates/discounts recognized net of revenue vs prior fee model); this is profit-neutral.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Capex:** Q1 cash capex outflow was Rs 307 crore. FY27 full-year capex guided at ~Rs 1,800 crore (down from Rs 2,500–2,700 crore earlier), expected to remain around this run-rate next year.
* Semaglutide API theoretical capacity can reach north of 300 to 550, sufficient for captive requirements and external B2B commitments.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **Net Cash:** Rs 3,057 crore (US$323 million) as of June 30, 2026.
* **Operating Working Capital:** Rs 14,353 crore, down Rs 81 crore from March 31, 2026.
* **Cash Flow:** Operating/free cash flow before acquisitions was negative Rs 216 crore for the quarter.
* **Hedging:** US$354 million hedged between Rs 92.34 and 94.63/USD through March 2027; RUB 2.8 billion hedged at Rs 1.26/RUB maturing in 3 months.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Resolution risk around semaglutide API batch scale-up (management assigns an 80%–90% probability of success).
* Ongoing Middle East conflict driving elevated solvent costs and freight expenses.
* US FDA regulatory clearance for the Bachupally biologics site (7 observations) which could delay the targeted mid-December abatacept goal date.
* US generic baseline price erosion.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **On Semaglutide Resolution Confidence:** Management stated the root-cause analysis is complete and remediation is active with target testing around September 22–23; success confidence is pegged at 80%–90%.
* **On US Base Business Growth History:** Management acknowledged past productivity issues and R&D delays in complex generics (e.g., iron sucrose, conjugated estrogens), but noted product leverage across Emerging Markets and Europe generated healthy overall ROI.
* **On Abatacept Manufacturing Site:** Management clarified abatacept was filed solely from the in-house Bachupally site (CCM5 and FFM2), dismissing intentions of using a CMO prior to initial approval.
* **On Potential US Tariffs on Generics:** Management dismissed near-term concern, calling recent announcements early-stage dialogue with a purported two-year transition window, and noted 25%–30% of US revenues are already manufactured via US-based CMOs.
* **On Quantifying Biosimilar/Peptide Drag:** Management agreed current P&L bears unabsorbed costs for ready biologics (CCM5) and peptide facilities, but deferred providing a specific rupee cost mismatch figure.

---

### One-line takeaway
Q1 FY27 results absorbed a heavy hit from semaglutide write-offs and geopolitical cost inflation, leaving performance contingent on an H2 manufacturing recovery and the mid-December abatacept BLA outcome.
