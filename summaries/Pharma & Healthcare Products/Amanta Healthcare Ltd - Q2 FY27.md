# Amanta Healthcare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 17% YoY, EBITDA grew 22% YoY, and PAT grew 26% YoY.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 83.1 crore, up 17% YoY (from Rs 71 crore)
- **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 151.9 crore, up 12% YoY
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 18 crore, up 22% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 21.6%
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 33 crore, up 8% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin (H1 FY27):** ~22%
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 4.86 crore, up 26% YoY
- **PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** ~6%
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 8.17 crore, up 12% YoY
- **Finance Cost (Q2 FY27):** Rs 6 crore
- **Depreciation (Q2 FY27):** Rs 5 crore, up 13% YoY
- **PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 7 crore, up 39% YoY
- **Total Borrowings (as of September end):** Rs 190 crore (including Rs 45 crore working capital)
- **Annual Debt Repayment:** ~Rs 30-35 crore

### Highlights
- SteriPort Line 3 commissioned in the first week of September 2026 at the Kheda facility, increasing SteriPort capacity by ~75% (from Rs 6.6 crore/year to Rs 11.6 crore/year; management also referred to capacity in bottle units reaching 12 crore). Line 3 reached ~55% utilization by end of Q2 and is expected to reach 100% capacity by November 20, 2026.
- 10.8 MW captive solar plant operational since the end of Q1 FY27.
- Debt-to-equity ratio improved from 3.5x in FY23 to ~1.1x in FY26.
- International markets contributed ~32% of revenue in Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects SteriPort Line 3 to contribute ~Rs 60 crore to the top line in FY27, meeting the earlier guidance of ~Rs 110 crore annual revenue at optimal utilization.
- Captive solar plant expected to deliver ~Rs 9 crore in annual power cost savings.
- Small Volume Parenterals (SVP) capacity expansion planned to start in Q1 FY28, increasing capacity by ~52% (from Rs 20.9 crore to Rs 31 crore).
- Two-year aim: Revenue growth of 20%+ CAGR and EBITDA margins of 25%+.
- Full year FY27 top line projected around Rs 420 to Rs 430 crore on an annualized basis.
- FY28 SVP contribution initially guided conservatively at ~Rs 16 crore top line due to low initial utilization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- SteriPort demand remains strong across high-value therapies (onco care, critical care, anesthesia, pediatrics). Over 35 crore bottles of SteriPort marketed since 2016 with zero fungal contamination complaints.
- Company serves ~200 to 250 channel partners pan-India.
- SVP expansion is primarily aimed at inhalation solutions and ophthalmics (multi-dose preservative-free).
- Company plans to target advanced markets (Canada, Australia, South Africa, Europe) for exports once SVP inhalation lines and MedSafe approvals are in place.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Polymer prices (tied to oil) experienced over 70% volatility, which the company absorbed during the quarter.
- Average price hikes taken across products range from Rs 0.70 to Rs 1.80 per bottle (~4% to 4.5% at a blended level).
- Structural power cost savings expected from the 10.8 MW captive solar plant.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- SteriPort Line 3 commissioned in September 2026; capacity at ~12 crore units.
- SVP facility expansion under construction, expected to be operational in Q1 FY28, increasing SVP capacity by ~52% (from Rs 20.9 crore to Rs 31 crore).
- Future capex will focus on product pipeline/development, factory automation/mechanization to reduce manpower costs, and regulatory/dossier filings.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt stands at ~Rs 190 crore (including Rs 45 crore working capital) as of September end.
- Expected debt by end of FY27: ~Rs 130 crore term loan + working capital (~Rs 175-180 crore total).
- Target debt profile in 2 years: ~Rs 100 crore term loan + ~Rs 50 crore working capital.
- Annual debt repayment scheduled at ~Rs 30-35 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in polymer prices linked to crude oil fluctuations.
- Regulatory hurdles and tech-transfer complexities (e.g., suspension products like budesonide) could impact the ramp-up speed of the new SVP line.
- NPPA/NLEM pricing regulations could impact retail/distribution margins (though management noted it affects retail/hospital margins rather than manufacturer profitability).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aniket Nikumb (ABN Capital):** Asked about new product areas for the SVP expansion. Management explained focus is on 12 SVP products split between inhalation (6 products, 2 commercialized) and ophthalmics (preservative-free), with out-licensing and direct exports planned.
2. **Chirayu (Aarth Growth Fund):** Inquired about gross margin pressures despite consistent EBITDA. Management attributed it to absorbed polymer price volatility and noted price realizations (up ~4.5%) will help expand margins as polymer prices normalize.
3. **Venkat Reddy (Investor):** Asked about export resumption to advanced markets like Australia and Canada. Management clarified that SteriPort is India-centric, but new SVP inhalation lines will free up other capacities for advanced market exports (Australia, South Africa, Canada, Europe).
4. **Saket Saurabh (Sagari Capital):** Asked about the extent of price hikes taken. Management stated price hikes average ~Rs 1 per bottle (~4.5%).
5. **Vedant (Mars Investments):** Asked for SVP revenue contribution and margin estimates for FY28. Management guided conservatively for Rs 16 crore top line from SVP in FY28 due to low initial utilization, noting that detailed simulation for inhalation portfolio will be shared later.

### One-line takeaway
Amanta Healthcare reported a strong Q2 FY27 with 17% revenue and 26% PAT growth, driven by the commercialization of SteriPort Line 3 and structural cost savings from a new captive solar plant, while preparing for an upcoming SVP capacity expansion in Q1 FY28.
