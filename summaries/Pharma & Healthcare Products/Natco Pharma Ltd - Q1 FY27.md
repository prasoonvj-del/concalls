# Natco Pharma Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Lower revenues due to Lenalidomide decline, offset by base business growth and strong profit contribution from associate Adcock Ingram.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Revenues:** INR 794.4 crores compared to INR 1,390.6 crores in Q1 FY26.
- **EBITDA (including other income):** INR 245.7 crores.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 30.9%.
- **Consolidated PAT:** INR 206.5 crores compared to INR 269 crores in Q4 FY26 (which included a onetime tax benefit of INR 115 crores).
- **Normalized PAT Growth:** 34% QoQ.
- **Adcock Ingram Holdings Limited (Associate):** Revenue at INR 1,582.8 crores; PAT at INR 242.2 crores. NATCO's share of profit stood at INR 84.3 crores.
- **Brazil Revenue:** INR 178 crores (180% YoY growth).
- **Canada Sales:** INR 56 crores.
- **Domestic Formulation Revenue:** INR 136 crores (base business normalized around INR 107-130 crores).
- **Crop Health Science Revenue:** INR 40 crores in Q1.
- **Net Cash:** INR 1,400 crores as of today.

### Highlights
- Decline in total revenues was largely driven by lower Lenalidomide revenue compared to the corresponding quarter last year.
- Acquired an additional 13.25% stake in Adcock Ingram in July 2026, taking total holding to 49% for a total investment of ~INR 3,000 crores across two tranches (with the recent tranche at INR 1,060 crores).
- Strong performance in Brazil (driven by oncology pipeline) and domestic formulation (boosted by semaglutide and oncology).
- Board is exploring a fundraising plan of INR 2,000 crores via QIP, rights issue, or other options for potential M&A and acquisitions.
- Crop Health Science division experienced losses in Q1 due to El Nino and delayed rains, but expects to break even for the full year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained full-year PAT guidance at ~INR 750 crores.
- Gross sales guidance maintained between INR 3,300 crores to INR 3,400 crores.
- Crop Health Science full-year revenue guidance maintained at ~INR 750 crores.
- Domestic formulation volume expected to increase by about 25% this year.
- Organic capex guidance remains at INR 250 crores to INR 300 crores annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- South African associate Adcock Ingram benefited from a strong flu season, creating a seasonal earnings bump.
- Semaglutide domestic brand is doing ~INR 2 crores per month with intense competition and pricing pressure, currently breaking even.
- Long-term R&D investments focus on 2028-2035 pipelines (70%-80% of R&D spend).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses declined YoY due to high R&D spend and legal costs in the previous year's quarter; current quarter run rate is not indicative of future quarters as R&D is milestone-dependent.
- India effective tax rate under the new regime is expected to be ~25.16% (with CSR/donation disallowances bringing it to ~27%); consol tax rate was higher this quarter due to higher contribution from Brazil (>35% tax rate).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Organic capex is expected at INR 250 crores to INR 300 crores per year.
- Carfilzomib plant upgrade on track for completion by end of the year for a calendar '27 launch.
- Filed 3 dossiers for NATCO products in Adcock (South Africa); expect launches around 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash stands at INR 1,400 crores after deploying ~INR 3,000 crores for Adcock stakes over the past year.
- Planning a INR 2,000 crores fundraise (evaluating QIP, rights, etc.) for M&A and acquisitions (one smaller in India, one very large outside India).
- Land bank exists (~15%-20% tied up in frivolous litigations), open to monetization at the right price.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High quarterly volatility in R&D and other expenses.
- Intense competition and cutthroat pricing in the domestic semaglutide market.
- El Nino fears and delayed rains impacting the Crop Health segment.
- Patent litigation uncertainties (e.g., Olaparib trial timelines and exclusivity determinations).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vamsi (ASK IM) on cash levels and fundraising:** Management clarified that despite INR 1,400 crores net cash, funds are needed for pending short-term loans, capex, and larger M&A opportunities under evaluation.
- **Kunal Randeria (Axis Capital) on other expenses:** Management explained that lower other expenses this quarter are due to lumpy R&D milestone/clinical trial spends last year, and current levels are not a fixed run rate.
- **Abhigyan Srivastav (Marcellus) on Olaparib/Carfilzomib:** Management stated Carfilzomib plant upgrade is on track for a calendar '27 launch, while Olaparib trial is scheduled in the next few months with exclusivity determinations still open.
- **Tanya Chaudhary (Investec) on Semaglutide:** Management noted the brand does ~INR 2 crores/month, breaking even amid intense cutthroat competition and settling prices.
- **Vamsi (ASK IM) on PAT Guidance:** Management warned analysts *not* to annualize Q1 Adcock profit numbers, reiterating the full-year PAT guidance of INR 750 crores due to seasonal flu-driven bumps in South Africa.

### One-line takeaway
Natco Pharma reported lower Q1 revenues due to Lenalidomide normalization, but offset it with strong domestic/Brazil growth and a profit spike from its increased 49% stake in Adcock Ingram, while maintaining full-year guidance and evaluating a INR 2,000 crores fundraise for future M&A.
