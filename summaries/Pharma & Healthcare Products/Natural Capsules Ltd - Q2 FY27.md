# Natural Capsules Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Capsules business performed well with volume growth and margin expansion, but API revenue and partner programs are slower than expected, leading to a recalibrated outlook.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 51.15 crore (up 12% YoY, 5% QoQ)
- **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 3.01 crore (vs loss of Rs 0.88 crore in Q2 FY26 and profit of Rs 1.12 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Q2 EBITDA Margin:** 5.88% (up 781 bps YoY, 358 bps QoQ)
- **Q2 Loss Before Tax:** Rs 4.58 crore (vs Rs 7.70 crore YoY, Rs 5.83 crore QoQ)
- **Q2 Net Loss:** Rs 4.22 crore (vs Rs 6.99 crore YoY, Rs 5.74 crore QoQ)
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 99.86 crore (up 10% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 4.13 crore; Margin: 4.14% (vs loss of Rs 1.13 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 Net Loss:** Rs 9.97 crore (vs Rs 13.13 crore YoY)
- **H1 Cash from Operations:** Rs 7.18 crore
- **H1 Capex:** Rs 3.76 crore
- **H1 Net Financing Outflows:** Rs 3.20 crore
- **Q2 Capsule Revenue:** Rs 47.48 crore; EBITDA: Rs 7.63 crore (H1 Revenue: Rs 90.50 crore; EBITDA: Rs 14.53 crore)
- **Q2 API Revenue:** Rs 3.67 crore; EBITDA: -Rs 4.43 crore (H1 Revenue: Rs 9.36 crore; EBITDA: -Rs 10.17 crore)
- **Consolidated Gross Debt (as of Sept 30, 2026):** Rs 115.91 crore

### Highlights
- Converted idle U.S.-demand-lacking HPMC capsule lines to gelatin in Q1; second HPMC line is currently being converted for H2 benefit.
- Appointed a new COO for API operations (9 years at Shilpa Medicare) who closed loss-making product lines and customer accounts (e.g., hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, and dexasodium phosphate).
- Warrant issue to promoters executed to shore up working capital and fund potential inorganic acquisition opportunities.
- *Record Check:* The strategic conversion of idle HPMC lines to gelatin, noted in the investor deck, is highlighted in the call as the primary driver of Q2 capsule volume/revenue growth.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Recalibrated to Rs 205-210 crore.
- **FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Recalibrated to Rs 10-12 crore.
- **FY27 API Revenue:** Revised down from Rs 70-80 crore to Rs 12-15 crore due to domestic dumping and strategy shift.
- **FY28 Consolidated Revenue:** Expected at around Rs 280 crore (Capsule: ~Rs 230 crore).
- **FY28 Consolidated EBITDA / Cash Income:** Expected at around Rs 32-35 crore.
- **FY29 API Revenue Aspiration:** Rs 150-170 crore; FY30 target of Rs 250 crore with 20% EBITDA margin.
- **Breakeven Timelines:** API cash breakeven expected in FY28; EBITDA neutral/breakeven for API subsidiary expected in Q4 FY28 at Rs 120-150 crore revenue; Consolidated PBT/profitability expected in Q1 FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Gelatin capsule demand remained firm with new customer additions (Mankind Pharma added; Zydus Lifesciences audit completed, business expected soon).
- U.S. demand for HPMC capsules remains absent.
- Container availability and freight rates remained a concern in Q2, managed without losing profitability.
- Domestic API generic market suffers from unhealthy dumping by Chinese suppliers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Capsule realization improved by ~3% in Q1, holding steady through Q2.
- Gelatin prices expected to rise due to higher international coal and oil prices; management expects to pass on raw material cost increases.
- Capsule EBITDA margins range between 14-16%, potentially going up to 17%.
- Power cost reduction of ~Rs 1.5 crore per annum expected from next year for the API division via a new local solar project tie-up starting Nov 1. Karnataka transmission charges had previously reduced solar savings at the Bangalore plant.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Second HPMC line conversion to gelatin underway to add volumes in H2.
- Planned pipeline of 16 molecules for US DMF and European CEP filing: 8 in FY28, 8 in FY29; US FDA audit projected by Q1 FY29.
- Future API expansion plans include a sterile cleanroom block for injectable grade APIs, pending funding/equity infusion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated gross debt stood at Rs 115.91 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- GST paid on API division capex (approx Rs 20-23 crore) is stuck in current assets/GST department and is claimable only against domestic revenue/value addition over the next 4-5 years.
- Exploring equity infusion, BIRAC/DBT government funding, and foreign concessional debt for API operations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued absence of U.S. HPMC demand; HPMC revenue guidance of Rs 20 crore abandoned.
- API business running at significant losses; domestic generic sales impacted by Chinese dumping.
- Fermbox Bio contract faced scale-up challenges beyond 10 KL (at 60 KL commercial production), yielding lower-than-anticipated results; client returned to R&D lab to improve processes.
- High levels of GST locked up with the government that cannot be recovered via exports.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Yug Baid asked about segment splits and debt:** Management provided segment revenues, EBITDA for capsules and API, and confirmed consolidated gross debt at Rs 115.91 crore.
2. **Love Gupta asked about API breakeven and FY27/28 guidance:** Management guided FY27 consolidated revenue to Rs 205-210 crore and provided FY28 top-line/EBITDA estimates.
3. **Sanchita Sood asked about the drop in API guidance:** Management explained the strategic pivot away from loss-making domestic generic lines due to Chinese dumping, reducing API revenue forecast to Rs 12-15 crore.
4. **Shikha asked about PE investor clawback targets and board representation:** Management noted clawback targets are informally being extended due to delays, confirmed intent to add pharma-experienced independent directors, and agreed to add a milestone tracker in future presentations.
5. **Keshav Garg asked about stuck GST and merger/demerger tax efficiency:** Management clarified GST is locked with the GST department and recoverable only via domestic sales over 4-5 years; agreed to evaluate a merger at the Board level for tax efficiency and GST unlocking.

### One-line takeaway
Natural Capsules reported strong Q2 volume growth and margin recovery in its core capsule business by converting idle HPMC lines to gelatin, but significantly lowered its API guidance as it restructures the division away from loss-making domestic sales toward regulated exports and contract manufacturing.
