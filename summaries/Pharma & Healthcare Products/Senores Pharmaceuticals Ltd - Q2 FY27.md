# Senores Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA, marking the ninth consecutive quarter surpassing guidance.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 192 crore (up 25% YoY) for Q2 FY27; Rs 372.5 crore (up 30% YoY) for H1 FY27
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 58 crore (up 38% YoY) for Q2 FY27; Rs 111 crore (up 58% YoY) for H1 FY27
- EBITDA margin: ~30% (improved by 290 bps YoY for Q2; 530 bps YoY for H1)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 36.6 crore (up 22% YoY) for Q2 FY27; Rs 67 crore (up 31% YoY) for H1 FY27
- Operating cash flow (H1 FY27): Rs 38 crore
- Capex (H1 FY27): ~Rs 46 crore
- Regulated market revenue growth: 28% YoY in Q2; 34% YoY in H1 FY27
- Emerging market revenue growth: ~21% in Q2; 25% in H1 FY27
- Apnar facility Q2 revenue: ~Rs 21 crore
- Total approved ANDAs: 64 (as of September 2026), of which 23 are commercialized

### Highlights
- Regulated market growth driven by portfolio expansion and differentiated sales/distribution channels.
- Received 1 new ANDA approval and 3 sANDAs (comprising 4 strengths) in Q2; 3 sANDAs commercialized.
- CMO/CDMO front commercialized 3 ANDAs covering 12 strengths.
- Pipeline consists of 41 newly developed molecules covering ~130 strengths.
- Installed a pre-filled syringe line for emerging markets to support GLP-1 and other products; implemented SAP HANA and AI-backed applications.
- Acquired US FDA-approved plant (Apnar) successfully cleared inspection with one minor procedural 483; currently operating at 90%-100% capacity largely for product validation.
- Subsidiaries Zoraya and Amerisyn are expected to start contributing from H2 FY27.
- Appointed M/s Talati & Talati as joint statutory auditors.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 guidance of 30% to 40% revenue growth and 50% to 60% PAT growth.
- Management expects annual growth around 25% over the next 2 to 3 years beyond FY27.
- H2 is anticipated to be stronger than H1 due to upcoming product launches, improved commercialization, and contributions from Zoraya and Amerisyn.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Controlled substance pipeline has strong visibility with 4 to 5 launches expected before March 31, 2027.
- Emerging markets have ~558 approved registrations and ~944 filed/waiting for approval.
- India branded generics business shifted from volume-focus to margin-focus, improving gross margins from 18%-20% to over 60% with a target revenue of Rs 50-60 crore for the year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin stood at 30% for Q2; reported EBITDA is higher due to other income.
- Q2 P&L includes ~Rs 4 crore of initial setup and operating costs for Zoraya and Amerisyn without revenue contribution.
- Emerging market margins dipped slightly in Q2 due to large orders of competitive beta-lactam products; expected to return to mid-to-lower teens for the full year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Apnar facility is targeting 4 total commercial lines by March 31, 2027, reaching a total production capacity of ~1.2 billion units.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Improved working capital efficiency and reduced receivable/working capital days.
- Unbilled revenue (other financial assets) increased due to shipments billed at the end of September.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as systemic risks, other than inherent timing variances in quarterly business execution and regulatory approval processes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gaurav Shukla (Finvestors) asked about Apnar facility utilization.** Management replied it operates at 90%-100% capacity primarily for validation work, with 4 lines expected by March 2027.
2. **Kalash Jain asked about the margin dip in emerging markets.** Management explained it was due to competitive beta-lactam orders and expects normalization to mid-teens.
3. **Hrushikesh Shah asked how the 50%-60% PAT guidance is achievable given subsidiary costs.** Management cited operating leverage, stronger H2 seasonality, and new product commercializations.
4. **Siddharth questioned the slowdown in new ANDA launches in Q2.** Management clarified they received controlled substance approvals (which require quota allocation delays) and 3 sANDAs, with multiple launches scheduled for November/December.
5. **Richa Chowdhary asked about the long-term growth guidance revision from 30%-40% to 20%-25%.** Management indicated they remain confident in sustainable growth and will evaluate guidance closer to fiscal year-end.

### One-line takeaway
Senores Pharmaceuticals delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA in Q2 FY27, backed by robust ANDA approvals and upcoming commercialization from subsidiaries Amerisyn and Zoraya, keeping full-year guidance on track.
