# Shilpa Medicare Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA, driven by growth across all four business segments.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 538 crore (up 35% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 1,007 crore (up 44% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 151 crore (up 37% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 28% (H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 29%)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 290 crore (up 40% YoY)
- Operating PBT (before share of profit from JVs/associates and exceptional items): Rs 104 crore for the quarter; Rs 202 crore for H1
- PAT: Rs 79 crore for the quarter; Rs 180 crore for H1 (nearly 100% growth in H1 YoY)
- H1 FY27 Capex: Rs 252 crore
- Net Debt: Rs 612 crore
- ROCE: Improved to 13.3% (up from 9% in FY25); 18.3% on an adjusted basis
- Non-captive API Revenue: Rs 255 crore for the quarter (up 24% YoY); Rs 470 crore for H1 (up 20% YoY)
- Formulation Revenues: Rs 210 crore for the quarter (up >50% YoY); Rs 407 crore for H1 (up 70% YoY). Base business grew 87% YoY excluding licensing.
- Biologics Revenues: Rs 65 crore for the quarter (up 150% YoY); Rs 117 crore for H1 (up 92% YoY)

### Highlights
- Management stated that the years of investing ahead of revenue are behind them and harvesting is beginning.
- API delivered its highest ever quarterly revenue.
- Formulation India business grew 2.5x in H1 YoY, led by Nor-UDCA (first-in-class NAFLD treatment; 1 year since launch with zero recalls).
- Biologics contributes 12% of revenue with a portfolio of 7 biosimilars (average global sales >USD 10 billion).
- Recombinant human Albumin global Phase III protocol approved by CDSCO and EMA; IMPD submission targeted in Europe in H2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of continuing revenue momentum in upcoming quarters.
- Management expects ROCE to improve further with business mix improvement and higher operating leverage.
- No specific revenue guidance or revenue indications provided.

### Demand & industry commentary
- API raw material costs have gone up globally.
- Formulation growth driven by complex finished dosage formulation portfolio in the EU, supported by U.S. and RoW.
- Biologics growth driven by continued deal momentum in licensing, partnerships, and CDMO.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins declined sequentially and YoY by around 700 bps, attributed to a higher proportion of API sales versus formulation (formulation has higher gross margins) and globally higher raw material costs in API.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex of Rs 252 crore in H1 FY27 funded primarily by internal accruals.
- Commissioning of one of the largest integrated peptide manufacturing capacities on track for the end of FY27 (adding 2 manufacturing lines to the existing 4, taking peptide manufacturing to tonnage scale).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands stable at Rs 612 crore despite the significant capex program.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Jadcherla plant U.S. FDA remediation measures are ongoing, though management noted 0 risk to current revenues as critical U.S. filings have been moved to alternate CMO partners.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Parth Mehta asked about the 700 bps gross margin decline.** Management (Keshav Bhutada) explained it was due to higher API sales share (which has lower gross margins than formulations) and rising global API raw material costs.
2. **Manish Bhandari asked about peptide capex and Albumin revenue potential.** Management stated peptide capex is phased and dependent on customer uptake; they do not provide revenue guidance for Albumin.
3. **Shrikant Akolkar asked about peptide capacity, order visibility, and late-stage CDMO programs.** Management clarified capacity is for a mix of CDMO, GLP-1, and HPAPI based on customer indications, and out of 4 late-stage CDMO programs, 1 is expected to commercialize next year and 3 in subsequent years.
4. **Krisha Kansara asked about the rationale for carving out the CDMO platform.** Management stated it creates a dedicated platform to leverage their one-stop integrated solution model (from KSM/peptides to final API and biologics fill-finish).
5. **Amish Kanani inquired about Jadcherla U.S. FDA status and plant preparedness.** Management reiterated zero revenue impact from Jadcherla U.S. issues as U.S. products are manufactured at contract manufacturing sites, while EU and other regulatory audits are cleared.
6. **Loveleen Bagga asked about service/licensing income break-up and COGS.** Management stated service/licensing income does not have direct product COGS, but has associated R&D and operating expenses, and did not provide a specific financial break-up.

### One-line takeaway
Shilpa Medicare delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue and EBITDA in Q2 FY27, backed by multi-engine growth across API, formulations, and biologics, while advancing key complex pipelines and peptide manufacturing capacity expansions.
