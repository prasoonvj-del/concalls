# Symbiotec Pharmalab Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-28 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management is highly optimistic about future growth driven by new verticals (biotech CDMO and complex injectables) and a solid, cash-generative API business, despite near-term earnings drag from new investments.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue (Q1 FY27): Rs 218 crore (up 7% YoY).
- API Revenue (Q1 FY27): Rs 215 crore (up 6% YoY, driven by volume growth).
- API Gross Margins: 60%+ for Q1 FY27.
- API EBITDA (Q1 FY27): Rs 66 crore (up 9% YoY).
- Consolidated EBITDA (Q1 FY27): Rs 45 crore.
- Consolidated Net Profit (Q1 FY27): Rs 14 crore.
- New Business Expense Drag (Q1 FY27): Rs 23 crore of operating expenses and Rs 11 crore of depreciation.
- Net Debt: Rs 398 crore as of June 30, 2026; stands at approximately Rs 326 crore currently (post-IPO proceeds and Q2 capex).
- Capex for Q1 FY27: Approximately Rs 68 crore.
- Cumulative API Gross Block: Close to INR 640 crores as of June 30, 2026.
- API Capacity Utilization: 70% to 80%.
- FY27 Guidance: Targeting 20% growth in revenue and 25% growth in EBITDA (+/- 5%).

### Highlights
- Recently completed a U.S. FDA audit at the Pithampur site in August 2026 and submitted responses to procedural observations; awaiting EIR.
- Filed the first ANDA molecule for Dual-Chamber Vials (DCV) in September 2026; second molecule filing scheduled for Q4.
- Signed a 5-year take-or-pay agreement for insulin as a drug substance in the biopharma space.
- Secured a 10-year take-or-pay contract with a US-based alternative protein player and a 10-year take-or-pay term sheet with a European alternate protein player.
- Signed a term sheet with a US-based pharmaceutical multinational in August 2026 for the DCV platform; definitive agreement expected in early Q3 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guides for 20% revenue growth and 25% EBITDA growth for FY27 (+/- 5% due to global uncertainties).
- FY27 H2 is expected to be very strong, backed by milestone cash flows and small biotech CDMO revenues in Q4.
- Minimum planned capex of Rs 200 crore to Rs 250 crore annually for the next 2 to 3 years.
- Revenues from the INR 1,000 crores of new capex are expected to commence in the next 6 to 12 months.
- Biologics/insulin division revenues are expected to kick in early next fiscal year (FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Over 70% of FY26 revenues come from customers served for more than 7 years; average relationship tenure with top 5 customers exceeds 10 years.
- Global shift in biomanufacturing creates a multi-billion dollar TAM (Total Addressable Market) with McKinsey and BCG estimating over a billion liters of fermentation capacity needed in the next decade.
- Symbiotec claims to have one of the lowest capital costs per kiloliter of fermentation capacity globally.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- API gross margins expected to continue around 60%.
- API EBITDA margins expected to stay close to 30%.
- New business opex drag of ~Rs 23 crore and depreciation of ~Rs 11 crore per quarter expected to continue for the next 2-3 quarters.
- Mature complex CMO and complex injectable spaces typically command gross margins >70% and EBITDA margins >35%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Cumulative investment of over INR 650 crores in existing API facilities (Rau and Pithampur SEZ) generating ~INR 900 crores of revenue (1.5x asset turnover).
- Invested close to INR 1,000 crores in two new facilities: biotech CDMO large-scale fermentation plant and Mhow complex injectable facility.
- Phase 0 utilizes existing 400 kL fermentation facility; Phase 1 expansion of 600 kL (brownfield) planned to start construction in Q3 FY27, taking 15-18 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced to ~Rs 326 crore post-IPO proceeds (net Rs 142 crore) and Q2 capex.
- Phase 1 expansion capex to be funded by internal accruals and customer capital contributions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global operating environmental uncertainties (shipping costs, logistics, solvent price volatility).
- Lumpy near-term earnings due to expenses from new businesses ahead of commercial revenues.
- Regulatory and formulation development timelines outside company control for partner-dependent projects (e.g., generic Premarin).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Saion Mukherjee (Nomura):** Asked about 5-year visibility, capacity ramp-up, and revenue potential for the fermentation business.
   * *Management:* Explained Phase 0 (existing 400 kL) and Phase 1 (600 kL expansion) visibility; contracts are multi-million dollar take-or-pay opportunities, though specific numbers remain confidential.
2. **Harith Ahamed (Avendus Spark):** Inquired about the persistence of the INR 23 crore quarterly opex drag and milestone revenue triggers.
   * *Management:* Confirmed the run-rate will continue for 2-3 quarters and stated that H2 milestone revenues will offset the operating expense drag.
3. **Anubhav Sahu (Systematix/Brokerage):** Asked if customer participation in capex involves profit sharing.
   * *Management:* Clarified it is a take-or-pay service model without profit sharing; customers pay for time and service.
4. **Raaj (Individual Investor):** Asked about the timeline for the generic Premarin launch.
   * *Management:* Stated that API development is complete, but timing depends on the partner's formulation development and FDA approval process.
5. **Rohan Kampani:** Asked about steady-state gross and EBITDA margins for mature new businesses.
   * *Management:* Indicated industry trends point to >70% gross margins and >35% EBITDA margins for complex CMO/injectables.

### One-line takeaway
Symbiotec delivered stable Q1 FY27 API performance (revenue up 7% YoY to Rs 218 crore) while absorbing pre-commercialization drags from its INR 1,000 crore investments in biotech CDMO and complex injectables, maintaining a 20-25% growth guidance for FY27.
