# Dishman Carbogen Amcis Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reported a soft quarter with sharp revenue and operating profit declines due to order deferrals and lower development revenue, but expressed optimism for medium-term recovery driven by tech transfers and structural cost-saving initiatives.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Lowered from expecting single-digit YoY revenue growth for FY27 to management stating "we might not see a growth in the revenues" for the full year.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from optimistic full-year growth expectations to acknowledging a "soft quarter" driven by biotech funding delays and deferred shipments, though early-phase and big pharma dialogues improved.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New mention of potential VC funding shifts from AI back to pharma; completion of the large BWT water plant at Bavla; semiconductor project quotation request. Old topics like specific forex loss impacts were less prominent, while biotech funding headwinds persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: The CHF 200 million ECB drawdown promised within 60-90 days last quarter was delayed and is now targeted for Q3/Q4 due to multi-jurisdictional and legal/family agreement processes. Legacy tech transfer to Bavla was initiated as promised.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 507 crores (down 22% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 46 crores (H1 EBITDA: INR 106 crores)
- **CDMO Segment Revenue:** INR 388 crores (H1: INR 929 crores)
- **CDMO Segment EBITDA Margin:** 12.3% (H1: 8.7%)
- **Marketable Molecules Revenue:** INR 118 crores (H1: INR 256 crores)
- **Employee Expenses:** INR 321 crores
- **Other Expenses:** INR 105 crores (down 23% YoY)
- **Cost of Goods Sold:** INR 34 crores (~7%)
- **Depreciation:** INR 90 crores (H1: INR 181 crores)
- **Finance Cost:** INR 40 crores
- **CapEx:** $5.4 million in Q2 ($10.3 million in H1); full-year guidance around INR 200 crores.
- **Consolidated Net Debt:** CHF 165 million.
- **French Facility:** Revenue CHF 1 million; negative EBITDA of CHF 1.73 million.

### Highlights
- Completed the last large Bavla CapEx: installed the largest BWT Switzerland purifying water plant in India.
- Secured the first commercial contract for the Naroda site following product market approval of a macrocyclic peptide in July 2026.
- Semiconductor project collaboration resulted in a customer requesting an initial volume quotation of several 100 metric tons.
- Initiated the first commercial technology transfer of a Carbogen Amcis legacy product to Bavla (validation expected March/April), with a second tech transfer underway.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-Year FY27:** Management expects top-line growth over last year may not materialize due to deferred shipments and biotech funding delays.
- **Medium-Term (FY28-FY29):** Management guides for 10-12% annual top-line growth and EBITDA margins in excess of 20-21%.
- **Bavla Facility:** Management expects full-year revenue to exceed INR 400-450 crores next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Early-phase projects show a slight upward trend, though biotech customers experienced funding delays partly due to capital diversion toward AI.
- Order income from existing commercial products is increasing following post-pandemic destocking.
- Sales strategy adjusted to target big pharma with large quantities combined with Indian production.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed-cost structure (employee expenses at INR 321 crores) magnified the negative impact of lower revenues on EBITDA.
- Vitamin D analogs (Calcifediol, Calcitriol) declined during the quarter, reducing marketable molecules segment margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q2 CapEx stood at $5.4 million; full-year CapEx guided near INR 200 crores (inclusive of maintenance and SAP digital transformation).
- Co-investment expansion project with the Japanese customer remains on track.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash balance stands close to INR 1,000 crores, offset by borrowings resulting in a consolidated net debt of CHF 165 million.
- CHF 200 million ECB drawdown is targeted before Christmas or in Q4, expected to reduce quarterly interest costs from ~INR 40 crores to INR 20-25 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inherent lumpy nature of the CDMO business and order deferrals by biotech clients.
- Continued negative EBITDA generation at the French facility (CHF 1.73 million).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Smit Shah (JHP Securities)** asked why Bavla has not secured large multi-crore single molecule contracts despite 20+ years of experience. Management replied that historical focus was small biotech, but they are now targeting big pharma and executing tech transfers to utilize Bavla assets.
- **Tarang Agarwal (Old Bridge Asset Management)** asked what caused H1 underperformance and recovery timelines. Management cited early-phase biotech funding delays and reiterated medium-term confidence.
- **Ojas Mehta (Sunk Cost Fallacy Capital)** asked how high Swiss lab costs will be managed. Management stated they continuously align resources based on 12-18 month pipelines.
- **Vivian Joshi** questioned recurring project and fundraising delays. Management explained that multi-jurisdictional legal steps, family agreements, and European summer holidays caused the timeline extensions.
- **Vivek Kumar** asked if FY27 revenue of INR 2900 crores will be achieved. Management stated they do not expect growth over the previous year.

### One-line takeaway
Dishman Carbogen Amcis reported a weak Q2 FY27 with a 22% revenue drop due to order deferrals and lower development revenues, but management expects a medium-term recovery backed by ongoing tech transfers and lower interest costs from upcoming ECB debt refinancing.
