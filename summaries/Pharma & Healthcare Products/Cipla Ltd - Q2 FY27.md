# Cipla Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - EBITDA margins expanded 200 bps QoQ to 18.7% alongside record One India revenue, while full-year margin and US exit run rate guidance were maintained.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained full-year FY27 EBITDA margin guidance of 18.5% to 20% and North America FY27 exit run rate of $1 billion.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted more positive due to sequential margin improvement, product approvals (generic Advair), and strong double-digit growth in One India branded prescriptions.
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter:** Announced licensing of a HER2 bispecific ADC from CTTQ and a Keytruda biosimilar partnership with Qilu for North America. Pithampur facility received 7 Form 483 observations during a US FDA inspection; Invagen Long Island site received zero Form 483s.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Generic Advair approved in Q2 (launch set for Q3 FY27); generic Ventolin supply ramp-up ongoing from the US facility.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 7,404 crore (up 4% QoQ).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 18.7% (excl. other income; up 200 bps QoQ).
- **Gross Margin:** 64.4% (up 7% QoQ).
- **PAT:** Rs 878 crore (11.9% of sales; PAT margin up 77 bps QoQ).
- **R&D Expense:** Rs 423 crore (5.7% of revenue).
- **Effective Tax Rate (ETR):** 27.2%.
- **North America Revenue:** $172 million (up $10 million QoQ).
- **EMEU Revenue:** $101 million.
- **One India Growth:** 12% YoY total; Branded prescription up 15% YoY.

### Highlights
- One India achieved its highest-ever quarterly revenue, driven by chronic therapies (60.3% MAT mix) and volume leadership in IPM.
- Received FDA approval for generic Advair (first DPI from US manufacturing base); launch planned in Q3 FY27.
- Maintained #1 market share in US Albuterol MDI at 22% (last week of Sept '26).
- Partnered with Qilu Pharmaceutical for US commercialization of biosimilar Pembrolizumab (Keytruda).
- In-licensed a novel HER2 bispecific antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) from CTTQ (SinoBiopharm Group) for oncology.

### Guidance & outlook
- **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained full-year FY27 guidance of 18.5% to 20%, expecting sequential quarterly expansion in H2.
- **North America:** Maintained target to exit FY27 at a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate, relying on 3 upcoming H2 launches (2 respiratory, 1 peptide) and scale-ups of Ventolin and Advair.
- **R&D Expense:** Guided at 6% to 7% of revenue for FY27.
- **Pipeline:** Planning 45 to 50 US filings over the next 3 years; developing 4 global biosimilar assets.
- **ETR:** Guided between 27% and 27.5% for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **India Chronic Segment:** Outperformed market with Respiratory up 11% YoY, Anti-diabetes up 50% YoY, Urology up 11% YoY, and Dermatology up 16% YoY.
- **GLP-1 Portfolio:** Tirzepatide (Yurpeak) is the #2 category brand in India, growing 5% to 10% MoM.
- **South Africa:** Private market secondary growth stood at 6.5%, beating market growth of 5.0%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Total expenses increased 4% QoQ to Rs 3,382 crore due to planned launch investments and manufacturing readiness.
- Gross margin reached 64.4%, expanding 7% QoQ primarily on an improved product mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Biosimilar pipeline advancing with 4 assets under internal development via joint venture Aspergen (majority owned with Kemwell).
- North America manufacturing plant continues capacity ramp-up for MDI and DPI rescue therapies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Net Cash Position:** Rs 9,823 crore in net cash and cash equivalents.
- **Total Debt:** Rs 699 crore (including lease liabilities).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- US FDA follow-up inspection at the Pithampur facility yielded 7 Form 483 observations (currently under OAI status for ~3.5 years; no repeat or data integrity issues).
- Lanreotide is not expected to contribute to FY27 revenues due to ongoing site remediation and tech-transfer timelines.
- Ongoing geopolitical and market-specific headwinds impacted EMEU performance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: How will US revenue bridge the gap to the $1 billion exit run rate without Lanreotide?**  
  *A: Management stated Lanreotide is excluded from the $1 billion exit run rate calculation. The gap will be filled by ramping up Ventolin and Advair alongside 3 new H2 FY27 approvals (2 respiratory, 1 peptide).*
- **Q: Why was generic Advair's commercial launch delayed following July approval?**  
  *A: Management explained it is the first DPI produced out of their new US facility, requiring additional preparation time; commercial launch will occur in Q3 FY27.*
- **Q: What is the status of the 7 observations at the Pithampur plant?**  
  *A: Management noted there were zero repeat and zero data integrity observations, and they are actively engaging with the FDA to convert the plant status to VAI.*
- **Q: Why did South Africa primary performance lag secondary market growth?**  
  *A: Management attributed it to accounting classification changes and a lost tender; a favorable court ruling was recently secured to restore tender access.*
- **Q: What is the launch timeline for the Keytruda biosimilar?**  
  *A: Management plans to file the US application next year to participate in the first wave of generic launches upon patent expiration around 2029.*

### One-line takeaway
Cipla expanded EBITDA margins by 200 bps QoQ to 18.7% and hit record India sales while maintaining FY27 targets of 18.5%–20% EBITDA margin and a $1 billion US exit run rate.
