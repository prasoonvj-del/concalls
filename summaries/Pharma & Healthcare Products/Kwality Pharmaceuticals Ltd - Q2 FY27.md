# Kwality Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth, raised revenue guidance, and highlighted successful regulatory approvals and working capital improvements.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised FY27 revenue guidance from Rs 700 crore+ to Rs 720 crore+ (management believes Rs 750 crore is achievable).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive; management is increasingly confident in the Rs 1,000 crore revenue target for FY28.
- **New topics or risks:** Confirmed appointment of KPMG as auditors; acquired 4 acres for Unit 7; detailed biowaiver application strategy for biologics (erythropoietin, pembrolizumab).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Hormone plant commercialization (Q3 FY27) remains on track; KPMG appointment delivered.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 181 crore (up 63% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 343 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 27.5%.
- **PAT:** Rs 29 crore (doubled YoY).
- **Working Capital Cycle:** Reduced to 130 days (from 170 days in FY26).
- **Debtor Days:** Reduced to 178 days (from 208 days).

### Highlights
- Received European-GMP certification from Hungary for cephalosporin and oncology facility.
- Completed bioequivalence (BE) studies for 19 out of 40 molecules.
- Acquired 4 acres of land for proposed Unit 7 manufacturing facility.
- KPMG appointed as auditors.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue:** Revised upwards to Rs 720 crore+ (management believes Rs 750 crore is achievable).
- **FY28 Revenue:** Target Rs 950 crore–Rs 1,000 crore+.
- **FY29 Revenue:** Target Rs 1,000 crore+ (management suggests it could cross Rs 1,000 crore in FY28).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 27%–28% for FY27; aiming for 30% by FY29.
- **Erythropoietin:** Expect Rs 30 crore–Rs 40 crore revenue in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by MENA (Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia) and LATAM (Mexico, Colombia) regions.
- Focus shifting to regulated markets (Europe, Brazil, Mexico).
- Targeting 200 BE studies by 2029/2030.
- Biologics (erythropoietin) commercialization via biowaiver applications in ROW markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Procurement:** 85% from India, 10%–15% from China.
- **Raw material prices:** 4%–5% increase in Indian API prices, but offset by high-margin product mix.
- **EBITDA margin levers:** Shift from unregulated to regulated markets and new product registrations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Unit 7:** 4 acres acquired; to be completed in three phases (FY28, FY29, FY30).
- **Capex:** Rs 140 crore–Rs 150 crore planned for FY27 (mostly complete, Rs 20 crore–Rs 25 crore pending).
- **Future Capex:** Estimated Rs 500 crore–Rs 600 crore over three years (Unit 7 + BE studies).
- **Capacity:** Third shift running in 6-7 lines (oral solid/injectables).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital cycle improved to 130 days (from 170 days).
- Debtor days improved to 178 days (from 208 days).
- **Funding:** Expansion to be funded via internal accruals and potential bank loans; no equity dilution planned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory approval timelines (e.g., Ministry of Health in various countries).
- Uncertainty regarding U.S. FDA audit outcomes for ANDA filings.
- Seasonal impact (holidays in Dec/Jan) on export volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Deepak Chokhani:** Asked if FY29 targets are conservative. Management: Yes, FY28 could hit Rs 1,000 crore if registrations (Europe/Brazil) come through.
2. **Nirali Shah:** Asked about cephalosporin facility/EU-GMP. Management: Targeting Mexico (Q4 FY27), Brazil (Q2 FY28), and Europe (Q2 FY28).
3. **Abhijeet:** Asked about direct vs. partner exports. Management: 20% of sales via partner MAs (unregulated), 80% via Kwality MAs (regulated).
4. **Manan Vandur:** Asked about biologics/erythropoietin clinical trials. Management: Using biowaiver applications for immediate supply; clinical trials required for full registration.
5. **Vivek Gupta:** Asked about Unit 7 potential. Management: Unit 7 is for 200 BE studies; revenue potential could be Rs 2,000 crore–Rs 4,000 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Kwality Pharmaceuticals is aggressively scaling its global footprint and high-margin specialty portfolio, evidenced by raised FY27 guidance and a clear roadmap for biologics and BE-led growth.
