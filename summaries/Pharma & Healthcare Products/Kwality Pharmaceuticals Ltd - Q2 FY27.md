# Kwality Pharmaceuticals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted 63% YoY revenue growth, doubling of PAT, upward revision in FY27 guidance, and appointment of KPMG.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised; FY27 revenue guidance revised to Rs 720 crore+ (achievable target Rs 720 crore–Rs 750 crore) from >Rs 700 crore earlier.
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained strong; management increased confidence in LATAM, MENA, and EU regulated market expansion.
- **New topics or risks:** Onboarded KPMG as auditor; acquired 4 acres of land for proposed Unit 7 facility; introduced biowaiver route strategy for biosimilars (Erythropoietin, Pembrolizumab).
- **Last quarter's promises:** KPMG onboarding completed; 19 BE studies completed (out of 40 planned); received EU-GMP certification from Hungary for cephalosporin and oncology facilities.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 181 crore (+63% YoY from Rs 111 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 343 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 27.5%.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 29 crore (doubled YoY).
- **Working Capital Cycle:** Reduced to 130 days from 170 days in FY26.
- **Debtor Days:** Reduced to 178 days from 208 days in FY26.
- **Dossiers & BE Progress:** 19 out of 40 BE studies completed; 19 dossiers ready for filing.

### Highlights
- Acquired 4 acres of land for proposed Unit 7 manufacturing plant.
- Received EU-GMP approval (Hungary) for oncology and cephalosporin lines.
- Generated 1% of Q2 FY27 revenue from Erythropoietin via biowaiver route (e.g., Egypt).
- Currently 80% of partner registrations are Kwality-owned Market Authorizations (MAs), targeting 80% Kwality-owned MA revenue by FY29.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue:** Revised upwards to Rs 720 crore+ (achievable Rs 720 crore–Rs 750 crore); PAT guided at Rs 122 crore+.
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Guided at 27.5%–28%.
- **FY28 Revenue:** Guided at Rs 950 crore–Rs 1,000 crore+ subject to EU and Brazil approvals.
- **FY29 Revenue:** Guided to comfortably cross Rs 1,000 crore (25%–30% YoY growth); 19 BE products expected to contribute ~Rs 300 crore.
- **EBITDA Margin Target:** 28%–32% long-term for Kwality-owned registrations; overall EBITDA margin can reach 30% if revenue crosses Rs 1,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven by new market registrations in MENA (Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia) and LATAM (Mexico, Colombia).
- Shift toward high-margin regulated/semi-regulated markets; Middle East/GCC is not the prime focus due to slower movement.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Procurement: 85% domestic India, 10%–15% China; 4%–5% API price increase absorbed without raising finished goods prices.
- Gross cost expected to drop to 44%–46% (gross margins 54%–55%) as registered specialty products replace low-margin supply.
- EBITDA margins: 24%–26% on partner-owned registrations vs 28%–32% on Kwality-owned registrations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Ongoing Capex:** Rs 140 crore–Rs 150 crore near completion; remaining Rs 20 crore–Rs 25 crore to finish by April 2027.
- **Future Capex (FY28–FY30):** ~Rs 500 crore–Rs 600 crore total (Rs 200 crore–Rs 250 crore for Unit 7; Rs 250 crore–Rs 300 crore for 200 BE studies across 3 years).
- **Unit 7:** Planned in 3 phases (FY28–FY30) to commercialize up to 200 BE products.
- **Cephalosporin Registration Timeline:** Mexico approval expected Q4 FY27; Brazil and EU expected Q2 FY28. US ANDA targeted for filing by FY28.
- **Capacity:** Third shift already operating in oral solids and 6–7 injectable lines.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Receivables: Stuck Middle East debtors (Rs 60 crore–Rs 70 crore) realized in Q1/Q2 FY27.
- Funding: Expansion to be funded via internal accruals and cash flows, supplemented by minor bank loans. No equity dilution planned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory approval and MOH query resolution timelines (12–18 months in LATAM/EU).
- Year-end international market closures (December through Jan 15) impacting Q3 shipping schedules.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Deepak Chokhani (Raedan Capital) on FY28/FY29 conservatism:** Management confirmed FY28 revenue can reach Rs 950 crore–Rs 1,000 crore+ if EU and Brazil approvals arrive on schedule.
- **Abhijeet (129 Wealth) on direct vs partner export margins:** Management clarified partner-owned MAs yield 24%–26% EBITDA, while Kwality-owned MAs yield 28%–32% EBITDA; 80% of future sales will be on Kwality-owned MAs.
- **Manan Vandur (Wallfort Fund) on biowaiver route for biologics:** Management explained biowaiver allows temporary/immediate commercial supplies based on preclinical data matching innovator standards, prior to full clinical trials.
- **Vivek Gupta (Advent Consulting) on Unit 7 capacity potential:** Management stated Unit 7 infrastructure is built to support up to 200 BE studies, offering potential multi-fold scaling beyond Rs 2,000 crore at full maturity.
- **Utkarsh Somaiya (Eiko Quantum) on dilution risk for large capex:** Management stated there is no plan for equity dilution; internal cash flows (~Rs 150 crore–Rs 180 crore PAT potential) and existing bank lines will cover the Rs 500 crore–Rs 600 crore multi-year capex.

### One-line takeaway
Kwality Pharmaceuticals is accelerating top-line growth toward Rs 1,000 crore by FY28 via self-funded BE filings, direct LATAM/EU registrations, and Unit 7 expansion.
