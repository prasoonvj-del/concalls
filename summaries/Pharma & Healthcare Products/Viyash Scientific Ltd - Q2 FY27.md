# Viyash Scientific Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth momentum continues with fifth consecutive quarter of double-digit growth, successful acquisition of BioForLife, and a near net-debt-free balance sheet.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 growth guidance (mid-teens, 13%-15% overall; Animal Health 20%+).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "wait-and-watch" in API (Q1) to "strong visibility" in H2.
- **New topics:** Detailed integration strategy for BioForLife (geo-extension of 10 products); specific focus on "day one" launch strategy for 2029 companion animal products; commissioning of oncology R&D center.
- **Last quarter's promises:** BioForLife acquisition closed (effective Oct 1, 2026); net debt reduced further (from Rs 86 crore to Rs 26 crore); goodwill amortization dropped as guided (from Rs 25 crore to Rs 8.9 crore).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 988 crore (up 16.2% YoY, 4.4% QoQ).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,934 crore (up 17.8% YoY).
- **Pre-ESOP EBITDA:** Rs 208 crore (up 10.3% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 21.1% (vs 22.1% in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 Pre-ESOP EBITDA:** Rs 412 crore (up 30% YoY, margin 21.3%).
- **PAT:** Rs 85 crore (up 16% YoY).
- **Adjusted PAT (normalized ESOP):** Rs 110 crore.
- **Net Debt:** Rs 26 crore (down from Rs 166 crore in March 2026).
- **Gross Margin:** 53.7% (vs 55.5% in Q2 FY26).
- **ESOP Expense:** Rs 67 crore in H1 FY27 (vs Rs 26 crore in H1 FY26).

### Highlights
- Completed acquisition of BioForLife (Italy) effective Oct 1, 2026; consolidation starts Q3 FY27.
- Net debt-to-LTM EBITDA is now negligible (zero).
- Commissioned new oncology finished doses R&D center in Hyderabad (OEB 5 capability).
- Received clean EIR for US FDA audit and successfully completed WHO-Geneva, EU GMP, and Mahad plant inspections.
- Animal health R&D spend tripled to ~Rs 10 crore/quarter run rate.
- Two CSR/ESG awards received this quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Growth:** Confident of 20%+ growth for the full year (management noted "20%+ is quite possible").
- **ESOP Cost:** Expected to be ~Rs 148-149 crore for FY27; dropping to ~Rs 32 crore in FY28 and normalizing to ~Rs 25 crore/year thereafter.
- **Capex:** Guidance of ~Rs 150 crore for FY27; similar levels expected for next 2-3 years.
- **Amortization:** Goodwill amortization to be Rs 4 crore in Q3, closing thereafter.
- **Long-term:** Aspiration to reach USD 1 billion by 2032 (~17%-18% CAGR).

### Demand & industry commentary
- **US Business:** Q2 YoY decline attributed to a one-time exclusive product launch in Q2 FY26; business has stabilized at ~Rs 110-115 crore/quarter run rate.
- **API:** Strong visibility for H2; management expects 12%-13% growth.
- **Companion Animal:** Fastest growing segment; targeting $30 million revenue this year (60% growth).
- **Emerging Markets:** Strong growth in Brazil (volume) and Turkey (new products).
- **Genericization:** Europe generic volume penetration is ~50%-60%; expected to reach 70%-80% in 3-4 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin volatility (53.7% vs 55.5% YoY) driven by the absence of the high-margin exclusive US launch from last year and raw material/solvent price hikes.
- Management expects 54%-55% gross margin to sustain.
- Solar energy capacity increasing; adding 4-5 MW capacity this year to improve opex.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Dedicated companion animal facility in Ambernath on track for Q1 FY28 commissioning.
- Vietnam entity registered; commercial sales expected early next year.
- Planning one additional manufacturing line for animal health in FY28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced to Rs 26 crore; no immediate plans for aggressive debt repayment as priority is growth capex and potential acquisitions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Gestation:** Complex/high-potent products have 3-5 year gestation; major revenues from oncology/companion animal pipeline expected post-2029.
- **Execution:** "Day one" launch strategy for 2029 companion animal products requires precise timing and regulatory approvals.
- **External:** Geopolitical issues impacted API procurement in H1.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **US Growth Sustainability:** Management clarified that while Q2 YoY looks negative due to a high base (exclusive launch last year), the business is stable at ~Rs 110-115 crore/quarter and expected to grow double-digits for the year.
- **Acquisition Strategy:** Management confirmed they are exploring "sizable" acquisitions for next year, prioritizing market access and companion animal portfolio over manufacturing capacity.
- **Carlyle Stake:** Management stated they have no visibility on Carlyle's exit plans for the next 12 months.
- **Oncology Pipeline:** Material contribution expected only from FY30.
- **Capex vs. Debt:** Management prioritized growth capex and M&A over immediate debt repayment.

### One-line takeaway
Viyash Scientific delivered a robust H1 with 17.8% revenue growth and a near net-debt-free balance sheet, while aggressively scaling R&D and manufacturing infrastructure to capture the 2029 companion animal patent cliff.
