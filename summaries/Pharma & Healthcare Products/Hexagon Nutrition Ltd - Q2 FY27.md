# Hexagon Nutrition Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-21 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 27% YoY for H1, gross margins improved sequentially by 310 bps in Q2, and order book remains robust.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained at 20% to 25% revenue growth YoY.
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, focusing on sequential margin recovery and inventory normalization for H2 execution.
- New topics or risks: Collaboration with Particles for Humanity for stable Vitamin A+, an exceptional loss of Rs 1.1 crores on plant equipment disposal, and review of the non-performing South Africa trading entity.
- Last quarter's promises: Nashik facility reconstruction work initiated (as promised), and field force expanded to 270 personnel.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 214 crore (up 27.3% YoY from Rs 168 crore).
- Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 109.7 crore (up 15.2% YoY, up 5.1% QoQ).
- Q2 FY27 Gross Margin: 47% (up 310 bps QoQ from 43.9%; down from 53.1% YoY).
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 22.7 crore (or Rs 23.7 crore before exceptional item); H1 EBITDA margin 10.6% (11.1% pre-exceptional).
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 10.9 crore (or Rs 12 crore before exceptional item); Q2 pre-exceptional EBITDA margin 10.9%.
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 16.2 crore (vs Rs 15.6 crore YoY).
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 8.2 crore.
- Inventory: Rs 136 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up from Rs 84 crore in March 2026).
- Cash, bank deposits, and liquid funds: Rs 46.5 crore against borrowings of Rs 37.4 crore (net cash positive by ~Rs 9 crore; debt-to-equity ratio ~0.15).
- Blended capacity utilization: ~30% in H1 (Nashik ~55%, Chennai ~25%, Tuticorin ~26%).
- ESG order book: ~Rs 100 crore total, with ~Rs 75 crore expected to be shipped in H2.
- Field force: ~270 personnel on ground.

### Highlights
- Collaborated with Particles for Humanity for new product development of a stable form of Vitamin A+ powder.
- Nashik facility reconstruction work initiated, tracking progress regularly.
- Equity instruments of ~Rs 12 crore converted into equity shares during H1, increasing share capital to Rs 12.3 crore.
- Evaluated strategic options for the South African trading subsidiary (HNPTY) due to zero business during the period.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20% to 25% YoY.
- Expected revenue momentum to continue in H2 driven by the field force in the branded business and a strong order book in the premix and ESG businesses.
- Projected gross margins to improve progressively as pricing and procurement actions take effect.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Clinical nutrition market in India is sized at ~Rs 6,300 crores, with Hexagon positioned at 3rd place among major competitors (Abbott, Nestlé, Fresenius Kabi).
- Premix market size is estimated at ~Rs 8,500 crores for vitamins and ~Rs 11,500 crores for minerals.
- Exports focus primarily on Asian and African regions, with future expansion planned into Europe and North America. Uzbekistan facility caters to Central Asian demand and neighboring countries.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 gross margin recovered by 310 bps QoQ to 47% driven by pricing actions, longer procurement contracts, and product mix optimization.
- Raw material prices (specifically whey protein) rose in H1, impacting YoY margins, though they stabilized in Q2.
- Employee costs increased to 14.7% of revenue due to field force investments. Advertising and promotion expenses stood at 5% to 6% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex was Rs 4 crore.
- Nashik facility reconstruction planned at a cost of Rs 20 crore to Rs 25 crore, with major funding via a ~Rs 20 crore bank loan.
- Existing capacity can support an additional Rs 200 crore to Rs 300 crore in revenue without requiring fresh plant capex.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased to Rs 136 crore from Rs 84 crore due to strategic stocking for confirmed H2 orders and raw material security; expected to normalize by March 2027.
- Operating cash flow for H1 was negative Rs 8.6 crore due to inventory buildup.
- Trade receivables improved to Rs 77 crore from Rs 82 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material price volatility (specifically whey protein).
- Temporary cash flow impact from inventory buildup for H2 orders.
- South African trading subsidiary has accumulated losses and no active business, with wind-up under consideration.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Divya Reddy (Sunshine Capital) asked about PAT decline despite 27% revenue growth.** Management attributed it to gross margin compression from higher raw material prices, investments in field force and admin expenses, and a one-time exceptional loss on plant disposal.
2. **Abhishek Maheshwari (Managers LLP) asked about raw material cost trends.** Management stated raw material prices (including whey) were fairly stable in Q2.
3. **Shravan Modi asked about market share and competitive position.** Management detailed market sizes and competitors in clinical nutrition and vitamin/mineral premixes, emphasizing product differentiation.
4. **Bhavya Chheda (Seven Islands PMS) asked about RUF order execution and field force strength.** Vikram Kelkar stated the total order book is close to Rs 100 crore (~Rs 75 crore to be shipped in H2), and field force count stands at 270.
5. **Mahima Rathod asked about plant capacity utilization and capex plans.** Management reported blended H1 capacity utilization at 30% (Nashik 55%, Chennai 25%, Tuticorin 26%) and noted no major capacity capex is required up to the next Rs 200-300 crore of revenue.

### One-line takeaway
Hexagon Nutrition delivered 27.3% H1 revenue growth and sequential gross margin recovery in Q2, while temporarily ramping up inventory to execute a robust H2 order book.
