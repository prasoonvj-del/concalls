# Orchid Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - EBITDA expanded 148% YoY driven by strong gross margin expansion and operational integration, despite macro energy cost headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Management continues policy of withholding short-term quantitative guidance; project timelines for 7-ACA in March 2027 and Cefiderocol in December 2026 remain unchanged).
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone remained positive, supported by margin normalization; demand for injectable cephalosporins showed recovery while oral cephalosporins remain under pricing pressure due to market overcapacity.
- **New topics or risks:** Fuel and energy costs surged ~30% YoY due to Middle East hostilities; recognized two one-off costs totaling ~Rs 10 crore (Rs 5 crore ECL provision and Rs 5 crore merger impairment loss); Board approved potential fundraising for 7-ACA cost overruns, Cefiderocol debt repayment, and downstream expansion.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Dhanuka merger integration started with initial savings expected by end-FY27; Exblifep expansion progressed with a new Latin America deal signed ($80 million 10-year value).

---

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 341 crore, up 14% YoY (vs Rs 299 crore) and up 12% QoQ.
- **Gross margin:** ~39%, expanding ~800 bps YoY from 31% in Q2 FY26.
- **EBITDA (incl. other income):** Rs 52 crore, up 148% YoY (vs Rs 21 crore).
- **EBITDA margin:** 15% (vs 7% in Q2 FY26).
- **PAT:** Rs 28.6 crore (vs net loss of Rs 0.9 crore in Q2 FY26). EPS at Rs 4.77.
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 645 crore (+15% YoY); H1 EBITDA: Rs 77 crore (+148% YoY); H1 PAT: ~Rs 32 crore.
- **H1 Operating Cash Flow:** ~Rs 118 crore.

---

### Highlights
- Merger integration of Dhanuka Laboratories is underway across procurement, manufacturing, quality, and support functions, with visible cost savings expected by late FY27.
- EXBLIFEP royalty and licensing fee annual run rate reached $3 million to $4 million. Signed Latin America licensing agreement with estimated 10-year sales of ~$80 million; Russia deal 10-year estimated sales stand at ~$178 million.
- Exblifep launched in 4 out of 5 major European markets (UK launch pending guaranteed minimum quantity/price model finalization).
- Antimicrobial Stewardship (AMS) domestic division EBITDA drag reduced to ~Rs 1.5 crore per quarter; currently marketing 15 products (18 SKUs) and regularly supplying >120 hospitals.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to perform better than H1, in line with historical seasonal trends.
- Management targets 3-4 additional EXBLIFEP licensing agreements over the next two quarters across Australia, Southeast Asia, Japan, Latin America, and North Africa.
- Over a 2-year horizon, management anticipates significantly higher global commercial product availability.
- Exclusivity/patent coverage for Exblifep expected up to 2034–2036 across key geographies (e.g., Germany PTE granted to Nov 2036).

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Injectable cephalosporin APIs demonstrated demand and price recovery in Q2 FY27, whereas oral cephalosporin APIs remain constrained by overcapacity and intense pricing competition.
- 7-ACA raw material prices have remained stable around $50 (±10%) over the past 5-6 years, benefiting from recent Indian Rupee devaluation.
- Ceftriaxone downstream API prices move in direct tandem with 7-ACA costs, acting as a nominal value-addition business.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel and energy expenses increased by nearly 30% YoY due to Middle East war disruptions.
- Operating performance absorbed one-off accounting impacts: ~Rs 5 crore Ind AS ECL provision and ~Rs 5 crore merger impairment loss.
- Employee costs for H1 FY27 remained flat at Rs 59 crore (+2% YoY) against a 15% growth in top-line revenue.
- Gross margin benchmark target is maintained above 35%, driven by high-margin complex injectables.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **7-ACA Jammu Project:** 1,000 MTPA fermentation plant scheduled for commercial operations in March 2027; pilot technology validated, and yield improvement work continues with technical consultants.
- **Cefiderocol Project:** Rs 173 crore invested as of Sept 30, 2026. Commissioning targeted for Dec 2026; official inauguration set for Jan 18, 2027; India launch targeted for Q3 FY27 / H2 2027 post-DCGI clinical trial waiver and licensing approvals.
- Plant utilization: Legacy Dhanuka facilities operate at ~80%-90% utilization; Orchid legacy facilities operate at ~60%. Cross-site customer approvals are underway for site rationalization.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital utilization decreased to Rs 412 crore at Sept 2026 from Rs 464 crore at March 2026.
- Working capital cycle improved to 118 days from 137 days at March 2026; receivable days reduced to 100 days from 113 days.
- Board approved fund-raising initiatives to cover 7-ACA cost overruns, repay Cefiderocol debt, and fund downstream Chennai product investments.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Middle East regional conflict caused delays in EXBLIFEP commercial launches in Middle East and South Africa (now rescheduled for Q1 2027).
- Delayed regulatory timelines in key markets like Japan and Brazil could extend time-to-market.
- Cost overruns on the 7-ACA project due to commodity price increases in recent months.

---

Orchid Pharma delivered a strong margin-led profitability recovery in Q2 FY27 while advancing its key growth drivers—the 7-ACA plant commissioning, Cefiderocol platform, and EXBLIFEP global monetization.
