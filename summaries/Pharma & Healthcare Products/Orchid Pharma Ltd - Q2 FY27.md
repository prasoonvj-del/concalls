# Orchid Pharma Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Pharma & Healthcare Products*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 14% YoY, EBITDA jumped 148% YoY, and gross margins expanded by ~8 percentage points.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained; no short-term profit margin or specific annual growth guidance given.
- Tone and demand view: Maintained; injectable cephalosporins show recovery while orals remain constrained; second half expected to be better.
- New topics or risks: Recognized a one-time impairment loss of ~Rs 5 crore due to the Dhanuka Laboratories merger and an expected credit loss provision of ~Rs 5 crore; noted cost overruns in the 7-ACA project due to commodity price increases.
- Last quarter's promises: 7-ACA Jammu project remains on schedule for March 2027 commercial operations; Cefiderocol project on track for December 2026 commissioning.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 341 crore (up ~14% YoY from Rs 299 crore in Q2 FY26; up ~12% QoQ).
- Revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 645 crore (up ~15% YoY from Rs 562 crore).
- Gross margin: ~39% (up ~8 percentage points YoY from 31%); H1 gross margin at ~36% (up ~6 percentage points YoY).
- EBITDA (including other income): Rs 52 crore (up ~148% YoY from Rs 21 crore); EBITDA margin at 15% (vs 7% YoY).
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 77 crore (up ~148% YoY from Rs 31 crore); EBITDA margin at 12% (vs 6% YoY).
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 28.6 crore (vs loss of Rs 0.9 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 PAT at ~Rs 32 crore.
- Earnings per share (EPS): Rs 4.77.
- Working capital utilization: Rs 412 crore at end of September 2026 (down from Rs 464 crore in March 2026).
- Working capital days: 118 days (down from 137 days).
- Receivable days: 100 days (down from 113 days).
- Operating cash flow (H1 FY27): ~Rs 118 crore.
- AMS quarterly EBITDA drag: ~Rs 1.5 crore.
- Exblifep/royalty annual run rate: $3 million - $4 million per year.
- Russia 10-year estimated Exblifep sales: ~$178 million.
- Latin America (first agreement) 10-year estimated Exblifep sales: ~$80 million.
- Cefiderocol project investment: Rs 173 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Employee expenses (H1 FY27): Rs 59 crore (up ~2% YoY from Rs 58 crore).
- Other expenses (H1 FY27): Rs 135 crore (vs Rs 114 crore in H1 FY26).

### Highlights
- Merger of Dhanuka Laboratories Limited with Orchid Pharma became effective on July 10, 2026 (appointed date: April 1, 2024); comparative figures restated.
- Focused integration initiatives started across procurement, manufacturing, quality systems, product planning, and support functions; first savings expected towards the end of FY27.
- Signed first license agreement for Latin America for Exblifep.
- Cefiderocol inauguration scheduled for January 18, 2027; targeted India launch in Q3 of FY27 (calendar year 2027 context/subject to approvals).
- Jammu 7-ACA project pilot technology validated, with commercial operations scheduled for March 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to potentially be better, following past trends, but noted it is premature to give a straight-line recovery or full-year guidance.
- Three to four additional Exblifep license agreements targeted over the next two quarters.
- Cefiderocol commissioning targeted by December 2026, validation/initial batches Jan-Mar 2027, and India launch targeted for Q3 of FY27 (subject to regulatory approvals and clinical trial waiver).
- 7-ACA commercial operations scheduled for March 2027; plant designed for 1,000 metric tons per annum.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Core market pricing and demand remain competitive; injectable cephalosporin business has seen recovery, while oral businesses still face constraints due to demand or capacity.
- 7-ACA price has been stable around $50 plus/minus 10% over the last 5-6 years, and current currency devaluation favors the company.
- Europe demand is not robust, but complex injectables with limited competition are supporting margins.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fuel and energy costs were nearly 30% higher YoY due to the Middle East war.
- Recognized a one-time expected credit loss provision of ~Rs 5 crore and a one-time impairment loss of ~Rs 5 crore due to the Dhanuka merger.
- Gross margin improvement driven by normalization of inventory position, better volumes, and favorable product mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Jammu 7-ACA project has completed a significant portion of its investment (designed for 1,000 MTPA).
- Cefiderocol project investment stands at Rs 173 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Dhanuka facility historically operated at 80%-90% capacity utilization, and Orchid at 60%; management is working to rationalize and cross-approve capacities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital utilization reduced to Rs 412 crore from Rs 464 crore in March 2026.
- Operating cash flow for H1 FY27 stood at ~Rs 118 crore.
- Board approved fundraising options to fund 7-ACA cost overruns, repay cefiderocol-related debt, and fund downstream product investments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Core market pricing and demand remain competitive; recovery will not be in a straight line.
- Middle East disruptions delayed Exblifep launches in the Middle East and South Africa (now expected by Q1 2027).
- Cefiderocol timeline subject to successful validation, manufacturing license, clinical trial waiver, and commercial arrangements.
- 7-ACA project facing cost overruns due to increased commodity prices over the last 6-8 months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Burramsetty Suresh asked about the US Exblifep timeline and fundraising plans.** Management replied that the US market is currently in preliminary discussions without a confirmed timeline, and fundraising plans depend on market conditions and board approvals without a fixed timeline.
2. **Murli asked about 7-ACA external sales timing.** Management stated that external sales will start significantly later, as the initial phase will prioritize internal consumption upon plant commissioning.
3. **Nikhil Upadhyay asked about 7-ACA pricing stability and yield improvements.** Management noted 7-ACA prices have been stable around $50 (+/- 10%) over 5-6 years, and external consultants have an incentive-based agreement to help further improve yields and lower raw material costs.
4. **Neeraj asked about the reasons for incremental fundraising.** Management explained funds will cover 7-ACA cost overruns from commodity price increases, cefiderocol-related debt repayment, and downstream product investments in Chennai.
5. **Vishal Manchanda asked about Cefiderocol India commercialization rights.** Management confirmed it is a done deal "at an almost signature level" with assurance from Daiichi/Shionogi/GARDP, and work towards it has begun.

### One-line takeaway
Orchid Pharma delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with a 14% YoY revenue increase and a 148% jump in EBITDA, supported by gross margin expansion, despite headwinds from higher fuel costs and one-time provisions.
