# Scoda Tubes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew modestly YoY, but EBITDA margins contracted sharply due to high input costs and intense competition.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 148.9 crore (up 2.5% YoY from Rs 145.3 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 273.3 crore (up 12.6% YoY from Rs 242.7 crore).
- **Q2 FY27 Geographic Mix:** Domestic 63% (Rs 93.8 crore), Exports 37% (Rs 55.1 crore).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 15.8 crore (vs Rs 22.3 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 10.6% (contracted by 479 bps YoY from 15.38%).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA & Margin:** Rs 31.8 crore, 11.6% margin.
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 10 crore (3.7% margin).
- **H1 FY27 Capex Incurred:** Approx Rs 40 crore (machinery and solar plant).
- **Balance Sheet (As of Sept 30, 2026):** Net fixed assets at Rs 217.2 crore, Capital work-in-progress (CWIP) at Rs 45.4 crore, Gross borrowings at Rs 229.2 crore.
- **Order Book:** Around Rs 185 crore (entirely seamless).
- **Seamless Capacity Utilisation Guidance (FY27):** ~70%.

### Highlights
- Management commissioned the first phase of captive solar capacity (4.99 MW DC) dedicated to seamless operations in August 2026.
- The company completed the RINA Marine audit in July 2026 (final approvals awaited) to access European marine engineering and shipbuilding markets.
- *Record Comparison:* The call confirms the 12.6% H1 revenue growth cited in the November 6 investor deck, but highlights execution delays on the welded line and ongoing margin compression.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20% to 25%, supported by the upcoming welded facility commercialisation and healthy order pipeline.
- Management refrained from providing specific margin guidance due to geopolitical uncertainties.
- Management expects peak debt to reach Rs 250 crore for the current financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic oil and gas sector demand remains low; primary domestic demand is stemming from the power sector (BHEL/NTPC) and chemical sector.
- Emerging demand and inquiries are noted from data centers, cooling infrastructure, HVAC, industrial water treatment, and desalination projects.
- Private players added 60,000 to 70,000 MTPA of new seamless capacities, increasing competitive intensity.
- Chinese imports for ordinary grades are reducing to near-negligible levels following antidumping duties.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Power and fuel expenses increased from 3% to 4.9% of revenue.
- Freight expenses rose from 1.4% to 2.9% of revenue.
- Gross margins are ~32%-33% for seamless products and lower at ~25% for welded products.
- Intensified competition restricted the company's ability to pass on cost hikes via price increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Welded expansion (up to 16 inches) trial production delayed to Q4 FY27 (commercial operations starting January 2027) due to construction and labor disruptions caused by gas issues in March-April.
- Additional brownfield welded expansion of 8,000 MTPA (outlay of ~Rs 40 crore, funded via internal accruals and long-term borrowings) is on schedule for commissioning in H1 FY28 (April-June 2027), taking total welding capacity to 21,150 MTPA. Total budgeted capex for this is Rs 45 crore.
- Dedicated solar power capacity for welded operations is on track for Q4 FY27 commissioning.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross borrowings increased to Rs 229.2 crore as of September 30, 2026, due to the 8,000-tonne welded capacity addition and ground-mounted solar facilities.
- Working capital cycles: welded takes 130-140 days; seamless takes 180-185 days.
- Inventory levels rose due to higher production and slower-than-anticipated sales conversion amid competition.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated power, fuel, and freight costs exerting severe margin pressure.
- Intense competition from private players limiting pricing power.
- Slower recovery in oil and gas demand and delayed customer capex.
- Construction and labor delays affecting project timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aryan Bhatia (InVed) on welded line delay:** Management explained that gas issues in March and April disrupted civil construction and labor availability, causing a 2-3 month delay.
- **Aryan Bhatia (InVed) on peak debt:** Management stated peak debt will reach Rs 250 crore with no immediate reduction plans.
- **Shreyans Jain (3A Capital Services) on top-line growth lagging peers:** Management stated revenue growth was constrained in value terms because competitive pressures prevented price hikes, though volume execution continues.
- **Rohit Suresh (Samatva Investments) on European import quotas:** Management clarified that EU antidumping duties operate on a shared volume slab system (max paid 25% so far) rather than a flat ban, allowing European customers to continue importing.
- **Hemkesh Khattar (Green Portfolio) on domestic weakness:** Management noted domestic oil and gas is slow, but power sector opportunities (BHEL/NTPC) provide forward visibility.

### One-line takeaway
Scoda Tubes delivered subdued Q2 FY27 profitability as higher operating costs and intense private competition compressed EBITDA margins by 479 bps YoY, though management maintains its 20%-25% annual revenue growth guidance on upcoming welded capacity additions.
