# Rajputana Stainless Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and PAT growth driven by specialty products and a healthy order book.

### Key numbers
- Q2 Revenue: Rs 360 crore (up 33% YoY)
- Q2 Operating EBITDA: Rs 33.4 crore (up 38% YoY)
- Q2 EBITDA Margin: 9.3%
- Q2 Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 23.7 crore (up 79% YoY)
- H1 Revenue: Rs 666 crore
- H1 PAT: Rs 43.9 crore (up 80% YoY)
- Order Book at start of H2: ~Rs 500 crore (including >Rs 80 crore in aerospace and defense)
- Capacity Utilisation: Billets at 99%, Rolled products at 99.45%, Bright bar at 60%
- Trade Receivables: Increased from Rs 137 crore to Rs 232 crore (management noted Rs 242 crore elsewhere in the call)
- H1 Operating Cash Flows: ~Rs 4.5 crore

### Highlights
- Company became debt-free in April using IPO proceeds.
- Shifted focus from traders to direct end-users (e.g., BHEL, L&T), increasing realization and specialty product share.
- Using AI operating system 'Fulcrum OS' to integrate dispatches, production, and quality control.
- Captive solar facility expected to commence by end of December 2026 (power cost reduction from Jan 1, 2027); windmills targeting production start by end of March 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects capacity expansion from 48,000 tonnes to 60,000 tonnes to go live in Q4 FY27.
- Long-term target to reach 2 lakh tonnes capacity by 2031.
- Free cash flow expected to be positive for the full year.
- Guidance: none (quantitative targets provided for capacity and long-term vision).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand driven by AI-related machinery manufacturing (fasteners), oil & gas, and defense & aerospace.
- Seamless pipe industry estimated at ~2.1 lakh tonnes/year, growing at ~15% annually; Indian plants running at ~85% utilization.
- Import barriers have helped domestic players increase capacity utilization in seamless pipes.
- India is a favored destination for global scrap exporters, with transit costs absorbed by scrap dealers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material procurement is make-to-order and hedged against the order book; consumables (unhedged) comprise ~10% to 12% of raw material cost.
- Power conversion cost accounts for a major share; captive solar and wind projects expected to cover ~80% of power costs and improve profitability.
- Operating costs grew faster than sales, impacting EBITDA margin expansion relative to operating margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current manufacturing capacity: 48,000 tonnes melting, 36,000 tonnes rolling, 6,000 tonnes bright bar.
- Expanding melting capacity to 60,000 tonnes (targeted for Q4 FY27) funded via internal accruals.
- Entering seamless pipe manufacturing (B2B/consumer vertical); unutilized IPO proceeds of Rs 18.57 crore earmarked for this, with machinery selection currently underway for long-term durability.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is debt-free.
- Working capital increased due to a shift towards direct end-users (payment terms of 60-90 days) and 33% YoY revenue growth.
- Debtor days are ~52 days; payables are lower than 10 days.
- Loans and advances of Rs 34.5 crore pertain to an interest-bearing inter-corporate deposit (not a related party).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Direct end-user billing extends receivable cycles (60-90 days), tying up cash in working capital.
- Potential delays in material imports, though no issues faced currently due to long-term commitments.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shreyas (Genuity Capital)** asked about capacity utilization and production volumes. Management stated 99%+ utilization across melting and rolling, 60% in bright bars, and outlined the 60,000-tonne expansion timeline for Q4 FY27.
2. **Vinit Thakur** questioned the low operating cash flow (Rs 4.5 crore) against a PAT of Rs 43.9 crore and rising receivables. Management explained that cash was locked in working capital due to faster revenue growth and a shift from traders to direct end-users with 60-90 day payment cycles.
3. **Mudit Daga** asked about loans and advances and gross margin compression despite raw material hedging. Management clarified the advances are interest-bearing inter-corporate deposits, and explained that unhedged consumables (~10-12%) and rising other costs impacted margins.
4. **Maitri Shah** inquired about aerospace/defense contributions and seamless pipe export strategies. Management noted rising domestic and international inquiries, robust Middle East/European demand, and OEM-backed aerospace growth.
5. **Arnav** asked about entry barriers and market size in seamless tubes. Management stated the market is ~2.1 lakh tonnes growing at 15%, with technical know-how as the main entry barrier, and highlighted their scrap-to-finished-product integration advantage.

### One-line takeaway
Rajputana Stainless posted strong Q2 revenue and PAT growth of 33% and 79% respectively, becoming debt-free while advancing plans to expand melting capacity to 60,000 tonnes and enter the seamless pipe segment.
