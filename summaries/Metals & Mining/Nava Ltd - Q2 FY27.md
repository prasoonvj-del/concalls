# Nava Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest ever quarterly revenue and doubled PAT year-on-year.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (no specific numerical revenue or profit guidance given, though project timelines reaffirmed).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more positive on power sector rebound in India and milestone achievements in Zambia.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Lithium exploration drilling hurdles resolved; land monetization advised to be held on by board based on real estate study.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: 100MW solar project in Zambia was commissioned in September as promised (88 MW commissioned as of date, full 100 MW by month-end).

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: INR1,252 crores (growth of nearly 30% YoY)
- Consolidated profit after tax (PAT): INR403 crores (more than doubling YoY)
- ZESCO receivables recovered: USD10.5 million (balance down to USD2.6 million)
- 100 MW solar power project emergency tariff: USD0.09 per year
- Zambia Kwacha depreciation: 8% QoQ
- Other income (fair market value gain at Singapore company): INR45 crores
- Peak debt: USD450 million (mean level around USD300 million to USD350 million over next four years)
- Integrated sugar project cost: USD200 million (50/50 debt-equity)

### Highlights
- Achieved highest ever quarterly revenue from operations of INR1,252 crores and consolidated PAT of INR403 crores.
- Commissioned 100 MW solar power project in Zambia in September (88 MW active, 100 MW by end of month), receiving an emergency tariff of USD0.09.
- Recovered USD10.5 million from ZESCO; outstanding balance reduced to USD2.6 million.
- Resumed silicomanganese production at Odisha operations.
- Avocado operations in Zambia preparing for first commercial harvest and sales; Kawambwa sugar complex targeting March 2028 commissioning.

### Guidance & outlook
- MEL Phase II (300 MW) power expansion commissioning targeted for July 2027; EBITDA expected to be similar to Phase I.
- Dividend policy for Nava Limited remains around 25% of standalone PAT.
- Management plans to hold land assets based on real estate advisor recommendations due to fast development pace.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Power sector in India showed a strong rebound, with IEX rates growing 40% YoY.
- Surplus power for 150 MW merchant capacity in India secured through bilateral contracts up to May.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margin dipped from 43% to 38% due to forex losses on capital advances from Kwacha depreciation and increased fuel costs in mining.
- Zambia Kwacha depreciated by 8% QoQ, reducing current deferred tax liability.
- Mining PAT flat due to forex losses and higher fuel costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- MEL Phase II 300 MW expansion proceeding toward July 2027 commissioning; major overhaul for Phase I planned starting around May (two months per unit).
- Lithium exploration in Zambia to undertake drilling over the next year following dispute resolution.
- Cote d'Ivoire manganese concession exploring conversion from exploration to exploitation after encouraging 2 square kilometer detailed exploration.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and cash equivalents over INR2,000 crores, held largely in dollar accounts for Phase II project and solar drawdown yielding around 4.5% interest.
- Long-term borrowings around INR3,000 crores.
- Peak debt at USD450 million with a 3+7 year repayment schedule (3-year moratorium, 7-year repayment); mean debt expected around USD300 million to USD350 million.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Currency fluctuations (Zambia Kwacha depreciation impacting capital advances and mining costs).
- Potential temporary margin impacts from major overhauls of Phase I units starting around May 2027.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vansh (Giriraj) asked about full-year guidance and incremental EBITDA for the 300 MW plant:** Management declined to give specific revenue guidance, noted the 100 MW solar plant gets a USD0.09 tariff, and stated Phase II EBITDA will match Phase I.
- **Gopal Kankani (Associates and Company Secretaries) questioned why huge cash balances earning 4%–5% are held while borrowings exist:** Management explained the dollar funds are drawn for the Phase II project in Zambia to mitigate dollar deficiency and secure payments due soon.
- **Kumar Saurabh (Scientific Investing) asked about PLF ramp-up time for Phase II:** Management stated PLF will reach 80%+ within 2-3 months, but noted Phase I will undergo staggered maintenance overhauls.
- **Anand Darshan (Market) asked about other income sustainability and mining flat profitability:** Management stated INR45 crores of other income came from fair market value gains in Singapore and will fluctuate, while mining flat profits were due to forex losses and higher fuel costs.
- **Bhagwandas Girhe asked about land monetization of Nacharam land:** Management stated that based on real estate advisor recommendations, the company decided to hold onto the land due to fast development pace despite conversion fees.

### One-line takeaway
Nava delivered record quarterly revenue of INR1,252 crores and doubled PAT to INR403 crores, driven by strong core operations, robust Indian power demand, and successful commissioning of its 100 MW Zambian solar project.
