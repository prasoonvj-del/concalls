# Venus Pipes & Tubes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the company's highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 361 crore, record exports, and early execution on strategic spooling capacities, despite short-term margin pressures from freight and selling prices.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained ~15% volume growth guidance for pipes on an annual basis; no specific percentage number reiterated for overall revenue or target EBITDA margin % (though management expects absolute profitability and margins to improve in H2).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more upbeat regarding the conversion of data center LOIs into firm orders and rapid scaling of new export geographies (US); freight cost pressures were highlighted as a temporary Q2 phenomenon showing signs of easing.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Added details on specific sector-wise spooling margins (data center/power at >30%, oil & gas at 20%-25%, nuclear at >35%-40%) and preferential allotment details (raising Rs 372 crore to retire Rs 344 crore of debt). EU quota concerns were downplayed as exports remained steady.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The spooling facility was commenced ahead of the original timeline (slated for end of Q3 in Q1) and translated an initial portion of the Rs 185 crore LOI into a ~Rs 29 crore firm order.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 361.1 crore (up 23.9% YoY from Rs 291.5 crore in Q2 FY26)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 681.6 crore (up 20% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 51.7 crore (up 9.1% YoY from Rs 47.4 crore in Q2 FY26)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 103.2 crore (up 11.8% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 14.3% (vs 16.3% in Q2 FY26)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 15.1%
- PAT: Rs 26 crore (compared to Rs 26.1 crore in Q2 FY26)
- PAT margin: 7.2%
- Export revenue: All-time high of Rs 147.2 crore (up over 50% sequentially; 41% of revenue)
- Revenue mix for Q2 FY27: Seamless pipes 54.7%, Welded pipes 37.6%, Fittings 2.5%, Other products 5.2%
- Segment growth: Seamless and welded businesses grew ~20% each YoY
- Volume growth: H1 volume growth around 8% YoY; full-year volume growth targeted at ~15%
- Order book: Over Rs 670 crore (including ~Rs 30 crore data center order, excluding the remaining Rs 156 crore LOI balance)
- Capacity utilization: Welded >60%, Seamless >80%
- Debt/Repayment: Raised Rs 372 crore through preferential allotment to repay ~Rs 344 crore of borrowings

### Highlights
- Raised Rs 372 crore via preferential allotment to retire ~Rs 344 crore of debt, with annual interest savings of Rs 28-30 crore expected to reflect from H2 FY27.
- Commenced pipe spooling operations ahead of original timeline and secured a ~Rs 29 crore firm order out of the Rs 185 crore data center LOI.
- Export business reached a milestone of Rs 147 crores (41% of revenue), driven largely by strong traction in the US.
- Continuing progress as a one-stop solution provider with expanded SKUs, fittings, and spooling capabilities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets annual volume growth of ~15% for the pipe segment in FY27.
- Expects H2 FY27 performance to be stronger, supported by contribution from fittings (targeting Rs 40-50 crore in H2) and spooling (targeting ~Rs 70 crore from data centers in H2).
- Expects EBITDA margins to improve in H2 with moderating freight rates, lower finance costs, and higher share of value-added products.
- Capex guidance for FY27 estimated between Rs 130 crore and Rs 140 crore (including solar plant, spooling/fittings expansion, and maintenance).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remains strong across traditional sectors (power, engineering, oil & gas) alongside emerging areas like CNG station infrastructure, semiconductors, and solar power.
- Global demand remains supported by supply chain diversification toward India.
- US market is the primary driver for exports, complemented by Europe and the Middle East.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Selling prices increased by >10% YoY and >5% QoQ, leading to optical moderation in EBITDA margin %.
- Excluding price increases, Q2 EBITDA margin would be ~15.5%; excluding higher export freight and fuel costs as well, it would be above 16%.
- EBITDA per kg (excluding fittings) stood at ~Rs 62/kg in Q2 FY27 compared to ~Rs 64/kg in Q2 FY26, primarily impacted by freight costs.
- Power and fuel costs account for >3.5% of revenue, having increased by ~1% of sales since April 2026.
- Net working capital days on sales at roughly 120 days; debtor days at ~75-80 days.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Full-year FY27 capex projected at Rs 130-140 crore (comprising new capacity, fittings/spooling, solar plant, and maintenance).
- Second seamless pipe facility, operational since May 2026, contributing to volume growth.
- Spooling capacity being executed for data centers first; potential future expansion planned for power, oil & gas, and nuclear sectors.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Preferential allotment of Rs 372 crore is being utilized to retire ~Rs 344 crore of debt.
- Finance cost savings of Rs 12-13 crore expected in H2 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global challenges: Middle East conflict, higher energy/commodity prices, disrupted shipping routes, supply chain disruptions, and rising freight costs.
- Temporary margin compression caused by elevated export freight and fuel costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sneha Talreja (Nuvama):** Asked about realization vs volume split and spooling sector expansion roadmap. Management clarified that volume growth is targeted at 15% for the year, and spooling will expand sequentially from data centers to power, oil & gas, and nuclear, with target EBITDA margins ranging from 20% to 40% across sectors.
- **Pritesh Chheda:** Questioned the movement in EBITDA per kg and margin changes. Management explained that the drop from Rs 64 to Rs 62 per kg was driven by higher freight costs, which are expected to normalize as freight rates trend downward.
- **Mahek Talati:** Asked about employee expenses and interest cost savings. Management noted employee expenses grew due to plant additions and capacity ramp-ups, and confirmed interest savings of Rs 12-13 crore in H2 from the debt repayment.
- **Shashank Kanodia:** Inquired about capex guidance revisions. Management updated the full-year capex outlay to Rs 130-140 crore (incorporating solar power plant and earlier announcements).
- **Dhiral Shah:** Asked about freight cost pass-through and Europe quotas. Management stated freight costs are being passed on in new orders, and European quotas have not significantly hurt their steady export run-rate there.

### One-line takeaway
Venus Pipes delivered record Q2 revenue of Rs 361 crore backed by strong export and domestic demand, while deploying preferential capital to eliminate debt and scaling high-margin spooling and fittings to drive H2 profitability.
