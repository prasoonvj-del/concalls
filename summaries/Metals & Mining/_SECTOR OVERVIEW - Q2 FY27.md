# Metals & Mining: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The overall sector tone for Q2 FY27 is predominantly **positive**, characterized by record-high revenues, robust volume growth, and strategic capacity expansions. Managements across specialty wires, steel tubes, recycling, and primary steel reported healthy demand resilience despite headwinds from monsoon-related disruptions, volatile coking coal and gas costs, and international freight pressures. Several players are aggressively scaling up through greenfield and brownfield capex, strategic backward integration, and diversification into high-value sunrise sectors like defence, aerospace, and renewable energy infrastructure.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Domestic demand across infrastructure, power transmission, automotive (particularly SUVs and two-wheelers), and railways remains robust. Sunrise sectors such as defence, aerospace, green hydrogen, and data centers are emerging as major demand drivers.
* **Divergence:** While most companies reported structural growth, **Vardhman Special Steels** noted a slowdown in the tractor segment alongside solid passenger vehicle and two-wheeler demand. Furthermore, while primary and specialty players are benefiting from infrastructure push, downstream stainless steel players like **Sambhv Steel Tubes** highlighted severe margin pressures and pricing disruption due to heavy Chinese dumping of 200 and 300 series coils following QCO norm relaxations.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Agreements:** Logistics, international freight, and fuel/gas costs acted as margin headwinds across multiple players like **Goodluck India** and **Vardhman Special Steels**. Monsoon disruptions created temporary yield losses and operational hurdles (noted by **Sambhv Steel Tubes** and **SAIL**). 
* **Disagreements / Divergences in Sourcing & Mitigation:** 
  * **Bansal Wire Industries** faced acute short-term wire rod shortages in October due to supplier diversion to TMT.
  * **Gravita India** successfully navigated scrap sourcing challenges by establishing international yards (e.g., upcoming US subsidiary) and benefiting from the 57th GST Council's reverse charge mechanism (RCM) on e-waste scrap.
  * **Rajputana Stainless** and **Vardhman Special Steels** noted that shifting toward direct end-users lengthened receivable cycles (60-90 days), tying up working capital.
  * **SAIL** reported higher imported coking coal consumption prices at INR 22,930 per tonne, expected to edge up to INR 23,800–23,900 in Q3.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is in a heavy multi-year investment cycle:
* **SAIL:** Guiding for INR 15,000 crore capex in FY27, exceeding INR 20,000 crore next year, and totaling INR 1,00,000 crore to INR 1,20,000 crore through FY31 to expand capacity from 20 to 35 million tonnes.
* **Gravita India:** Rs 1,680 crore total capex plan through FY29 to scale total capacity from ~480,000 to 800,000 tonnes.
* **Sambhv Steel Tubes:** Expanding via the Kesda stainless steel facility, Sarora power plant, DFT route pipe expansion, and a new Rs 148 crore stainless steel seamless pipe plant.
* **Goodluck India:** Scaling defence shell capacity to 4 lakh shells and non-defence capacity to 6 lakh tonnes via debottlenecking.
* **Rajputana Stainless:** Expanding melting capacity to 60,000 tonnes by Q4 FY27 and entering seamless pipe manufacturing.
* **Bansal Wire:** Nearing completion of the Dadri facility (60,000 tonnes) and advancing the Sanand greenfield project.
* **Kalyani Cast-Tech:** Commissioned the Shivlakha Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal and advancing container/wagon manufacturing capabilities.
* **Vardhman Special Steels:** Pursuing brownfield expansion to 360,000 tonnes, setting up a new forging plant, and laying groundwork for a larger greenfield phase.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Bansal Wire Industries:** Shifted tone from "recovering from disruption" (due to Q1 operational issues) to celebrating a "landmark quarter" with record profitability; maintained its 20% YoY volume growth target for H2.
* **Sambhv Steel Tubes:** Upwardly revised its FY27 EBITDA margin per tonne guidance to Rs 8,000–8,500 (total EBITDA expected upward of Rs 550 crore).
* **Vardhman Special Steels:** Revised FY27 sales volume guidance upward to ~260,000 tonnes (at the higher end of the initial range) while highlighting rising project cost estimates for its greenfield expansion.
* **SAIL:** Revised full-year sales volume guidance slightly to around 21 million tonnes (from 22 million tonnes).

## Company one-liners
* **Bansal Wire Industries:** Delivered a record-breaking quarter with strong cash generation and margin expansion, and despite short-term raw material supply constraints, management remains confident in achieving its 20% volume growth target for the second half of the year.
* **Goodluck India:** Strong operational performance and strategic restructuring position the company for long-term growth in defence and high-value engineering, despite near-term margin headwinds from logistics costs.
* **Gravita India:** Gravita delivered resilient Q2 FY27 results (revenue +38% YoY, PAT +6% YoY) despite raw material headwinds, and is mitigating sourcing risks through international yard expansion in the US, domestic RCM tailwinds, and copper integration.
* **Kalyani Cast-Tech:** Kalyani Cast-Tech is leveraging proprietary design innovations and its integrated Shivlakha logistics hub (rail siding, ICD, container, and wagon manufacturing) to drive domestic containerization and long-term growth.
* **Rajputana Stainless:** Rajputana Stainless posted strong Q2 revenue and PAT growth of 33% and 79% respectively, becoming debt-free while advancing plans to expand melting capacity to 60,000 tonnes and enter the seamless pipe segment.
* **Sambhv Steel Tubes:** Sambhv Steel Tubes delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with stable margins, strong cash generation, and an ongoing expansion path toward its Vision 2030 targets, supported by backward integration and entry into stainless steel seamless pipes.
* **Steel Authority of India Ltd:** SAIL reported strong H1 performance with a 55% jump in EBITDA and lower debt, though margins faced pressure from higher coking coal costs and monsoon-impacted operations, while aggressive multi-year capacity and raw material expansions lie ahead.
* **Vardhman Special Steels:** Vardhman Special Steels reported robust Q2 FY27 performance with a 39% surge in operating revenue and record sales volumes, though management flagged higher upcoming project costs and potential equity/debt adjustments for its greenfield expansion.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Raw Material & Import Pressures:** Resolution of wire rod supply blips (**Bansal Wire**) and government policy responses (anti-dumping / MIP) to Chinese stainless steel coil dumping (**Sambhv Steel Tubes**).
* **Capex & Project Execution:** Progress on major capacity expansions, such as SAIL's modernization tendering, Sambhv's Kesda and seamless pipe projects, Gravita's US scrap yard and copper expansions, and Vardhman's greenfield/brownfield timelines.
* **Order Execution & Working Capital:** Realization of defence order execution milestones (**Goodluck India**), working capital normalization and receivable cycles given the shift to direct end-users (**Rajputana Stainless**, **Vardhman Special Steels**).
* **Coking Coal Cost Trends:** Actual trajectory of imported coking coal prices against SAIL's projected INR 23,800–23,900/tonne range for Q3.
