# Metals & Mining: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector delivered a record-breaking quarter, with a significant majority of companies reporting all-time high revenues, EBITDA, and net profits. The overarching theme is a strategic pivot toward **value-added products (VAP)** and **backward integration** (captive power, coal, and slurry pipelines) to insulate margins from commodity volatility. While domestic demand remains robust, the sector is navigating a complex global environment characterized by geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and logistics bottlenecks.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is broad agreement that domestic demand remains strong, driven by infrastructure (PM Gati Shakti, railways, water projects), automotive, and the power sector. Companies like **BMW Ventures**, **Goodluck India**, and **Jindal Steel** highlighted the "on the boil" nature of power transmission and infrastructure demand.
*   **Divergence:** While domestic demand is healthy, the export outlook is polarized. Companies like **JTL Industries** and **Goodluck India** are successfully navigating export markets, whereas **Jindal Saw** and **Gallantt Ispat** flagged significant headwinds due to geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East and logistics/freight disruptions. **Jindal Stainless** noted a specific concern regarding a surge in substandard imports from China following the suspension of Quality Control Orders (QCO).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Headwinds:** Freight and energy costs are the primary margin pressures. **Aeroflex Industries**, **Goodluck India**, and **Jindal Saw** specifically cited rising international shipping costs and fuel prices as margin dampeners.
*   **Efficiency Gains:** Companies are aggressively countering cost pressures through operational efficiency. **Jindal Steel** commissioned a slurry pipeline to save Rs 700/tonne in freight. **Lloyds Metals** and **Sambhv Steel** are also utilizing slurry pipelines to lower logistics costs. **Sarda Energy** and **Shyam Metalics** are focusing on captive power and coal to hedge against raw material volatility.
*   **Margin Divergence:** While most companies expanded margins, **Gallantt Ispat** and **Jindal Saw** reported margin compression due to plant shutdowns and input cost inflation. Conversely, **Hindustan Zinc** and **NALCO** benefited from strong commodity prices and lower production costs.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is in a massive expansion phase. Almost every company is executing multi-year capex plans:
*   **Aggressive Expansion:** **Jindal Steel** is executing a Rs 47,000 crore plan. **Lloyds Metals** has a Rs 13,000 crore capex plan for FY27. **SAIL** is embarking on a massive Rs 1.2 lakh crore expansion over four years.
*   **Strategic Focus:** Capex is increasingly directed toward downstream value-added facilities (e.g., **JTL Industries**' color-coated lines, **Sambhv Steel**'s stainless steel seamless pipe plant) rather than just raw capacity.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Tone Shift:** Many companies (e.g., **Bansal Wire**, **Hariom Pipe**, **JTL Industries**) shifted from a "recovery from disruption" tone in Q1 to a "landmark quarter" tone in Q2.
*   **Guidance Adjustments:** **Jindal Stainless** revised its volume growth guidance downward (4-6% from 7-9%) due to import pressures, while **Sambhv Steel** and **Hariom Pipe** upwardly revised their EBITDA guidance.
*   **Operational Normalization:** Companies that faced Q1 shutdowns (e.g., **Hariom Pipe**, **Gallantt Ispat**) successfully resumed operations, leading to a strong Q2 rebound.

## What to watch next quarter
1.  **Freight & Logistics:** Will the Middle East situation continue to inflate freight costs and delay export shipments?
2.  **Import Policy:** Will the government reinstate QCOs or impose anti-dumping duties to curb the influx of substandard Chinese stainless steel?
3.  **Raw Material Availability:** Will the short-term wire rod and coal shortages (flagged by Bansal Wire and SAIL) normalize?
4.  **Capex Execution:** Monitoring the commissioning of new downstream facilities (e.g., JTL's GP lines, NALCO's 5th stream refinery).
