# Deccan Gold Mines Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - The company has successfully transitioned from an explorer to a producer with two active gold mines and has clearly defined its growth strategy across gold and critical mineral verticals.

### Key numbers
*   **Jonnagiri (Geomysore Services) Revenue:** Rs 87 crore (from 59 kg gold sold).
*   **Deccan Gold PAT Share:** Rs 6.35 crore.
*   **Jonnagiri Production:** 112 kg dore bar produced; 90 kg bullion produced.
*   **Inventory:** ~80 kg gold equivalent in stock (40 kg gold + 60 kg dore bar).
*   **Fundraise:** Rs 137 crore approved by the Board (via CCDs, equity shares, and warrants).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Milestone:** Transitioned from explorer to producer; Jonnagiri (India) and Altyn Tor (Kyrgyzstan) are now operational.
*   **Strategic Verticals:** Two distinct segments: Gold (Jonnagiri, Altyn Tor, Finland, Ganajur) and Critical Minerals (Nickel/Copper/Platinum in Chhattisgarh, Tungsten in Spain, Lithium/Tantalum in Mozambique).
*   **Jonnagiri Expansion:** Inaugurated June 2026; targeting capacity increase from 1,010 TPD to 2,500 TPD, aiming for 2 tons of gold per annum by 2029-30.
*   **Altyn Tor:** Commenced dore bar production; new tailings dam under construction to support 10-year mine life.
*   **Board Changes:** Mr. Elango appointed Chairman; Ms. Jade Devenish appointed Non-Executive, Non-Independent Director.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Production Targets (Jonnagiri):** FY27: 500-600 kg; FY28: 750-800 kg.
*   **Production Targets (Altyn Tor):** FY27: 150-160 kg; FY28: 300-350 kg.
*   **Finland (Kalevala):** Drilling to commence Sept 15, 2026; feasibility study planned for 2027.
*   **Critical Minerals:** Mozambique project production targeted for 2028.
*   Management expects the next quarter to be "much bigger" in terms of revenue and profit contribution.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong demand for battery metals (Lithium, Nickel, Graphite) driven by India's energy storage sector.
*   Tungsten demand rising due to defense and space technology applications.
*   Critical mineral price trends (last 1 year): Tungsten (+622%), Tantalum (+196%), Lithium (+108%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management expects to stabilize at 65-70% within 1-2 quarters.
*   **Refining:** Jonnagiri has an on-site refinery; Altyn Tor will utilize government refineries (settled at LBMA prices).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Bhalukona (Nickel/Copper/Platinum):** Planning 3,000 TPD processing plant; estimated capex Rs 650-700 crore.
*   **General Capex:** Estimated Rs 400-500 crore per 1,000 TPD plant.
*   **Funding Strategy:** Management plans to use a mix of debt, equity, and potential off-take arrangements (especially for critical minerals) to fund the estimated ~Rs 2,000 crore total capital requirement.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Inventory: 80 kg gold equivalent in stock at end of Q1.
*   Fundraise: Rs 137 crore approved to accelerate drilling and exploration.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Ganajur Project:** Currently in court; awaiting verdict.
*   **Tanzania Project:** Under review; results not fully satisfactory.
*   **Hutti Gold Mine:** Currently a prospecting license; not a priority compared to Ganajur.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: How will cash flows from Jonnagiri be realized?* A: Via dividends, but likely not this year due to expansion capex requirements.
*   *Q: Why are PAT/EBITDA lower than expected?* A: Only 59 kg sold; inventory held back; margins expected to stabilize in 1-2 quarters.
*   *Q: How will projects be funded?* A: Mix of debt, equity, and off-take arrangements (especially for critical minerals).
*   *Q: Status of Ganajur?* A: Awaiting court decision; management optimistic based on similar case precedents.
*   *Q: Will there be a physical AGM?* A: Yes, in Mumbai.

### One-line takeaway
Deccan Gold has successfully transitioned to a producer with two active gold mines and is aggressively scaling its critical mineral portfolio, with significant capital expenditure planned for future growth.
