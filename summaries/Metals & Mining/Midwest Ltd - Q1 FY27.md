# Midwest Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong revenue (35% YoY), EBITDA (25% YoY), and PAT (27% YoY) growth despite diesel price volatility, alongside key strategic international developments.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth YoY: 35%
- EBITDA growth YoY: 25%
- PAT growth YoY: 27%
- Granite revenue guidance (FY27): INR720 crore
- Quartz revenue guidance (FY27): INR100 crore to INR120 crore
- Total revenue guidance (FY27): INR840 crore (against past year's INR645 crore)
- Quartz production volume (Q1): ~5,000 tonnes sold
- Quartz realization: ~INR10,000 per tonne
- Quartz revenue guidance (Phase 1, FY28): INR180 crore to INR200 crore
- Gearing ratio: 0.2 times

### Highlights
- Signed an MoU with Indonesia's state-owned company PERMINAS to explore, mine, and value-add critical minerals/rare earths (accessing both light and heavy rare earths like dysprosium and terbium via ionic clays).
- Sri Lanka project policy finalized and ratified; received formal communication to confirm capacities, timelines, and investments, with ground-breaking expected around October.
- Quartz Phase 1 plant commercialized with teething issues behind; sold ~5,000 tonnes in Q1.
- Granite volume grew by ~10% YoY in key segments (Black Galaxy and Absolute Black).

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 total revenue estimated at ~INR840 crore (Granite: INR720 crore; Quartz: INR100 crore-INR120 crore).
- Granite EBITDA margins expected to remain in line with previous years, impacted by 50 bps to 100 bps depending on diesel prices.
- Blended EBITDA margin for FY28 expected around 29%-30% as quartz scales up.
- Quartz Phase 1 target: Reach 10,000 tonnes/month run rate by end of Q2/next quarter, and exceed 15,000 tonnes/month by Q4 FY27, turning profitable by year-end.
- Sri Lanka and Indonesia rare earth projects expected to start contributing numbers by FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Granite demand remains good, with 35% topline growth and better realizations; bulk of exports go to China.
- Quartz mix is roughly 65% engineered stone and 35% solar.
- Heavy rare earths (DyTb) are critical for powerful magnets in defense/aerospace and are 4 to 5 times more expensive than light rare earths.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Diesel is a major cost head (almost 13% of cost), which spiked from INR85 to INR125-INR130/litre, pulling EBITDA margins down by 190 bps YoY in Q1.
- Proactive fleet electrification and solar back-integration helped minimize diesel impact.
- Granite prices were hiked by 3% to 5% to offset cost pressures.
- Fuel prices have rationalized somewhat in Q1 compared to previous peaks.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Quartz Phase 2 capex: INR125 crore, split over current and next year, with commissioning expected in the next 10 to 12 months. Phase 2 revenue contribution expected in FY28.
- Sri Lanka plant building capex: ~INR120 crore, funded from internal accruals.
- Indonesia JV capex for the first project will be higher than Kerala due to overseas mining of clay; project report to be ready in 45-60 days.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gearing ratio stands at 0.2 times.
- Fresh capital raised is sitting in deployment and will scale up ROCEs/ROEs as revenues kick in.
- Promoter holding is at 77%; no near-term plans to bring it down to 75%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Diesel price volatility and geopolitical conflicts affecting fuel and operational costs.
- Bureaucratic delays with government agencies (e.g., Kerala JV with KMML experiencing delays due to new government/leadership settling down).
- Seasonal impacts from rains and Chinese New Year concentration in Q4.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Alok Deora asked about the conservative FY27 revenue guidance of INR840 crore given the Q1 run rate.** Management replied that they prefer to be conservative and over-deliver, accounting for seasonal rains and stabilization.
- **Prateek Singh inquired about the Indonesia JV structure between Midwest Limited and Midwest Energy.** Management clarified that two JVs will be formed: Midwest Limited will partner with PERMINAS for the mine-to-oxide stage, while Midwest Energy will handle the oxide-to-magnet stage later.
- **Balamurali asked about the peak potential timeline for the Sri Lanka HMS business.** Management stated that cash flows and meaningful contributions will start in FY29.
- **Archit Agarwal questioned the drop from the earlier guidance of 150,000 tonnes of quartz production in FY27.** Management explained that technical glitches last quarter trimmed the target to 120,000 tonnes.
- **Manish Gupta asked about funding plans for the heavy capex pipeline.** Management stated that debt headroom is available (gearing at 0.2x), fresh equity was raised for Phase 2 quartz, and internal accruals will support Sri Lanka.

### One-line takeaway
Midwest Ltd posted a strong Q1 with 35% revenue growth despite diesel cost pressures, and is expanding aggressively into quartz and rare earths via strategic projects in Sri Lanka and Indonesia.
