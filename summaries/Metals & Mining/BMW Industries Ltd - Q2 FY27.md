# BMW Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - driven by strong profit growth, improving capacity utilization across downstream businesses, and the commissioning of the first phase of the Bokaro greenfield expansion.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained consolidated revenue CAGR of ~70% to 75% over FY25 to FY28, operating EBITDA and PAT CAGR of ~40% to 45% and ~35% to 40% respectively, with margins stabilizing at 12% to 13% (EBITDA) and 5% to 6% (PAT) by FY28. (Old and new numbers are the same).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from transitionary preparation to active execution and sales commencement following the October 5, 2026 commissioning of the colour-coated line at Bokaro.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Discussion on hedging tools for base metals (zinc, aluminum) for Galvalume/ZAM emerged; fuel price volatility concerns from the previous quarter disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The promise to commission the colour-coated segment at Bokaro in Q2 FY27 was delivered on October 5, 2026.

### Key numbers
- Operating income: Rs. 182 crore (up 25.3% YoY)
- Operating EBITDA: Rs. 35 crore (margin at 19.2%)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs. 22 crore (up 42.7% YoY)
- PAT margin: 11.1% (improved by 76 bps YoY)
- ROCE (annualized as of Sept 30, 2026): 10% (13%+ excluding Bokaro capital)
- ROE (annualized as of Sept 30, 2026): 9.7%
- Net debt: Rs. 835 crore (net debt-to-equity of 0.70x), including Rs. 307 crore long-term borrowings for Bokaro
- Total capital deployment towards Bokaro (as of Sept 30, 2026): Rs. 516 crore (Rs. 209 crore internal accruals, Rs. 307 crore debt); ~Rs. 60 crore capitalized on Oct 5, 2026. Total outlay estimated at Rs. 803 crore.

### Highlights
- Commissioned the first phase of the Greenfield Downstream Steel Complex at Bokaro; colour coating line received Consent to Operate on October 5, 2026.
- Galvalume commissioning slated for Q4 FY27; ZAM and GI lines expected in H1 FY28.
- Peak debt estimated at Rs. 800 crore to Rs. 900 crore (including working capital).
- Appointed Mr. Rajat Kulshrestha as Chief Business Officer for the Coated Business.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reaffirmed FY25-FY28 consolidated revenue CAGR of ~70% to 75%.
- Operating EBITDA and PAT CAGR expected at ~40% to 45% and ~35% to 40% respectively by FY28.
- EBITDA and PAT margins expected to stabilize at ~12% to 13% and ~5% to 6% by FY28.
- Peak revenue from Bokaro estimated at Rs. 3,000 crore to Rs. 3,500 crore around FY29/FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian steel consumption growing at >10%, supported by real GDP growth of 10% to 11% and government policies (safeguard duties, QCOs, steel policy target of 300 million to 600 million tons).
- Shift occurring from traditional coated roofing (GI) to aspirational colour-coated consumption, and from hot-dip galvanized to pre-galvanized, Galvalume, and ZAM (zinc-aluminium-magnesium).
- Coated market size expected to reach $35 billion to $40 billion (Rs. 3.5 lakh crore to Rs. 4 lakh crore) by FY30.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price increases impact working capital needs and percentage margin calculations, but rupee-per-ton margins remain stable.
- Not currently hedged against metal price volatility (zinc, aluminium) for alloy coatings, but management plans to use hedging tools once commercial production starts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bokaro facility total saleable capacity: 5.5 lakh tonnes (includes 2 lakh tonnes colour-coated, plus Galvalume, galvanized, and ZAM).
- Existing traditional conversion business (CGL complex, rolling mill) has no major expansion plans, with minor specialized investments continuing in pipes and tubes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Incremental working capital requirement for Bokaro estimated at 60 to 65 days.
- Repayment of Bokaro term loans scheduled to start in September 2028 (COD September 2027), with intent to prepay using cash flows, PLI incentives, state subsidies, and capital reimbursements without penalties.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Exposure to raw material price fluctuations and market vagaries in the new proprietary manufacturing business model (unlike the legacy conversion model).
- Transitionary phase of capital deployment temporarily depresses consolidated ROCE until Bokaro ramps up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Yash Anand asked about the landscape and sales strategy for the new Bokaro business:** Management explained the transition to a B2B model and noted that their 2 lakh ton colour-coated output (and 5.5 lakh ton total alloy output) is a tiny fraction of growing national demand. *(Directly answered)*
2. **Bhavesh Bhatia asked about the spike in other income:** Management clarified it was due to the monetization and sale of assets no longer in use, aimed at improving balance sheet ratios. *(Directly answered)*
3. **Aryan Bhatia asked about peak debt and export focus:** Management stated peak debt is estimated at Rs. 800 crore to Rs. 900 crore, and while not averse to exports, the primary focus remains opportunistic and domestic. *(Directly answered)*
4. **Harshh Saraswat asked about incremental working capital assumptions for Bokaro:** Management confirmed an assumption of 60 to 65 days of working capital requirement. *(Directly answered)*
5. **Mahesh Attal asked about the strategic vision behind moving downstream:** Management explained that after 30+ years of conversion work for others, the strong balance sheet and eager team make it the right time to control their own growth journey in value-added steel. *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
BMW Industries commissioned the first phase of its Bokaro greenfield expansion, marking its entry into proprietary colour-coated steel manufacturing while maintaining its multi-year growth guidance.
