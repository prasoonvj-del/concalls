# Hindalco Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Hindalco delivered record performance in India upstream and copper, backed by strong operational metrics, Novelis recovery, and steady margin resilience.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (Novelis long-term EBITDA per tonne guidance kept at $600/tonne; consolidated net debt to EBITDA target capped at ~2.0x).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains strongly positive. Domestic aluminium demand growth accelerated to 6% YoY in Q2 (vs 3% in Q1), led by automotive, packaging, and data centers.
- **New topics or risks:** Capitalized pot relining for the first time (+Rs 130 crore to EBITDA and +Rs 130 crore to depreciation); copper EBITDA boosted by elevated sulfuric acid prices (Rs 16,000/tonne) and one-offs; Yeongju strike impact at Novelis ($20 million in Q2, similar expected in Q3); AluChem partnership terminated, pursuing internal/alternate routes for tabular alumina.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered first cold rolled coil at Bay Minette greenfield plant in September 2026.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Business EBITDA:** Rs 13,869 crore, up 52% YoY.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 7,533 crore, up 59% YoY.
- **India Business Segment EBITDA:** Rs 9,011 crore, up 66% YoY.
- **India Business PAT:** Rs 5,852 crore, up 91% YoY.
- **Upstream Aluminium:** Shipments up 1% YoY; Revenue up 33% YoY; EBITDA Rs 7,009 crore (up 55% YoY); EBITDA margin at 52%; EBITDA per tonne at $2,125/tonne.
- **Downstream Aluminium:** Shipments 119 KT (up 6% YoY); EBITDA Rs 329 crore (up 26% YoY); EBITDA per tonne at $289/tonne.
- **Copper:** Metal shipments 116 KT (up 2% YoY); CCR volumes 96 KT (stable); EBITDA at a record Rs 1,673 crore (up 164% YoY).
- **Operating Cash Flow & Capex:** Consolidated cash flow generated was Rs 23,319 crore (+57% YoY); capex invested was Rs 20,719 crore (+83% YoY).
- **Hedging:** H2 FY27 aluminium hedged at 29% at $3,000/tonne and INR at 21% at Rs 93/$; FY28 aluminium hedged at 26% at $3,168/tonne.

### Highlights
- Zero fatalities across all Indian operations during the quarter with LTIFR improving to 0.16.
- Recycled 62% of total waste generated (93% bauxite residue excluding Utkal, 77% ash, 110% copper slag).
- Renewable energy capacity reached 643 MW (plus 35 MW pump storage), targeting 884 MW RE plus 125 MW RE-RTC by FY27 exit.
- Non-LME-linked business EBITDA (Novelis, aluminium downstream, copper) rose to Rs 6,860 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Novelis:** Maintained long-term EBITDA guidance of $600/tonne; expects return to positive free cash flow and deleveraging by end of FY27.
- **Copper Normalized Run-Rate:** Guided at an underlying sustainable quarterly EBITDA of Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,300 crore.
- **Alumina Prices:** Realization currently around $350/tonne, expected to firm closer to $400/tonne next year.
- **LME Outlook:** Consensus ranges between $2,600 and $2,800/tonne for next year.
- **Downstream EBITDA:** Guided to cross $300/tonne as product qualifications pick up at Aditya FRP.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global aluminium market faced a deficit of ~0.5 million tonnes in Q3 CY26 and is projected to end CY26 in a deficit of ~0.8 million tonnes.
- Global aluminium consumption reached ~19 million tonnes in Q3 CY26 (+1% YoY), driven by China (+4% YoY), while rest-of-world fell 2% YoY.
- Indian aluminium demand grew ~6% YoY to 1.7 million tonnes in Q2 FY27, backed by auto, packaging, industrial, and expanding data center demand.
- Indian refined copper demand decreased 3% YoY to 388 KT due to weak consumer durables and electrical equipment demand. Spot TC/RCs remained negative (-48 to -58 cents/lb).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Indian aluminium cost of production rose ~5% QoQ in Q2 FY27 due to heavy monsoons in northern operations impacting coal linkage and higher CP coke prices.
- Q3 FY27 aluminium cash costs expected to increase by another ~5% QoQ due to sustained coal pressures before easing in Q4.
- Sulfuric acid realizations surged to ~Rs 16,000/tonne in Q2 (vs Rs 7,000/tonne YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total India capex guided at Rs 12,000 crore to Rs 13,000 crore in FY27 (Rs 6,000 crore spent in H1).
- Over Rs 50,000 crore projects currently executing (Rs 20,000 crore spent by FY27-end); another Rs 50,000 crore under evaluation across a 5-year timeline.
- Commissioning timelines: Pakhajan copper recycling plant in Dec 2026; Aditya Alumina Refinery in Dec 2027; Aditya Smelter line 1 (180 KT) in Dec 2027; Aditya Smelter line 2 in Dec 2028; Copper smelter expansion in Dec 2028.
- Upstream optionality includes expanding Mahan smelter by an additional 720 KT brownfield capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt-to-EBITDA stood at 1.80x as of September 2026 (target maintained around 2.0x).
- India operations hold net cash of Rs 9,000 crore (treasury of Rs 16,000 crore against long-term debt of Rs 7,000 crore); net debt to EBITDA is -0.35x.
- Novelis leverage targeted to peak at ~4.0x in Q4 FY27, before deleveraging to 2.5x–2.8x over the next two years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued tight copper concentrate market with negative spot TC/RCs.
- Monsoons and coal supply constraints in Northern India elevating power costs in Q2 and Q3.
- Ongoing trade fragmentation, tariff headwinds, and Middle East geopolitical supply threats.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pinakin (HSBC):** *When will detailed timelines for the second Rs 50,000 crore expansion phase be available?*  
  *Management:* Current focus is executing the first Rs 50,000 crore phase (Pakhajan Dec 2026, Aditya refinery/smelter Dec 2027/2028). Subsequent projects like Mahan will be staggered every 1.5 years to stay under 2x net debt/EBITDA.
- **Sumangal Nevatia (Kotak Securities):** *What is driving the higher copper EBITDA and its run-rate?*  
  *Management:* Normalized run-rate is Rs 1,200–1,300 crore per quarter, boosted by sulfuric acid prices (Rs 14,000–16,000/tonne vs Rs 7,000 YoY) and precious metals.
- **Jashandeep Singh Chadha:** *What was the impact of capitalizing pot relining costs?*  
  *Management:* Pot relining capitalization added ~Rs 130 crore to quarterly EBITDA and added an equivalent ~Rs 130 crore to depreciation.
- **Pallav Agarwal (HSBC):** *Can you quantify the one-offs in the copper segment EBITDA?*  
  *Management:* Non-repeatable gains totaled Rs 350–400 crore, stemming from copper slag sales accruals, derivative adjustments, and extra precious metal sales.
- **Satyadeep Jain (Ambit Capital):** *What return metrics are targeted for new upstream expansions at $2,600 LME?*  
  *Management:* Projects are structured to deliver mid-teens IRRs at $2,600 LME, supported by full backward integration into captive coal and bauxite.
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec):** *What constraints govern the pacing of brownfield expansions?*  
  *Management:* Execution capability (EPC contractor availability) and balance sheet leverage limits dictate project pacing, not market demand constraints.

### One-line takeaway
Hindalco delivered strong Q2 financial results led by record copper profits, robust aluminium margins, and structural upstream cost integration, while disciplined capital allocation keeps consolidated net debt below 2.0x.
