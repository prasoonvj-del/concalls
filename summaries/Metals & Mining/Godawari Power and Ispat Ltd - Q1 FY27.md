# Godawari Power and Ispat Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Resilient revenue and realizations, but profitability softened sequentially due to higher input costs from market iron ore procurement and elevated coal/gas prices.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Recorded YoY and sequential growth (exact aggregate figure not stated).
- EBITDA Margin: 19.1%
- PAT Margin: 12.7%
- Pellet Capacity Utilisation: 77% (4.7 million tonnes expanded capacity) in Q1; expected to ramp up to 80%-85% in FY27.
- Ari Dongri Beneficiation Plant Capex Incurred: INR218 crores till June 26.
- BESS Project Capex Incurred: INR501 crores till date.
- CRM Project Planned Capex: INR1,100 crores (funded through INR550 crores debt and balance internal accruals; INR80 crores already spent).
- Land Cost for CRM Project (Maharashtra): INR50 crores for a 35-acre parcel.
- EV Fleet (Mines): 15 EV dumpers, 24 EV loaders, and 15 EV excavators added/total fleet.
- CBAM Carbon Emission Intensity: 3.180 CO2 tonnes in Q1 FY27 (improved 1.9% QoQ and 4.2% YoY).
- WSA Carbon Emission Intensity: 2.485 tonnes CO2 per tonne of steel production in Q1 FY27.
- Imported Coal Cost: Increased to INR13,000 from INR10,500 in Q4 and early Q1.

### Highlights
- Profitability was impacted sequentially by higher input costs due to increased market sourcing of iron ore and elevated coal/gas prices following the West Asia crisis.
- Iron ore mining volume declined due to space constraints for overburden dumping caused by delays in tree-cutting permissions on additional allotted government land.
- Value-added products recorded healthy YoY growth led by sponge iron, billet, and rolled production.
- Ari Dongri mine expansion is progressing; ramp-up expected from Q3 following the beneficiation plant commissioning, with full-scale operations targeted for FY28.
- Proposed 1 million tonne integrated steel project kept in abeyance due to water allocation delays and approval issues.
- CRM complex relocated from Chhattisgarh to Maharashtra (near Sambhaji Nagar) to leverage state incentives, subsidies, and local automobile demand; commissioning targeted for December 27 (Q3 FY28).
- 25 MW solar plant commissioned in May 2026; 100 MW solar project under construction (targeted for September 2026 commissioning); 45 MW base project for solar storage targeted for Q3 FY26 (as stated).
- CRISIL reaffirmed credit rating at AA- Stable for long-term facilities.
- Recognized in 2025 Burgundy Private Hurun India 500 list.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track to deliver FY27 guidance, with Q1 volume achieving 16% to 29% of full-year guidance (though pellet production guidance of 4.0 million tonnes is expected to be slightly lower due to a 45-day shutdown).
- Mining production guidance for FY27 remains unchanged at 3.4 million tonnes.
- Pellet capacity utilization expected to reach 80%-85% in FY27.
- Margin improvement expected from Q4 FY27 following the commissioning of the beneficiation plant and greater captive iron ore pellet utilization.
- Vision 2030 targets (4x revenue, 3x EBITDA and PAT) maintained (steel plant removed from calculations; driven by CRM complex, BESS, and core operations).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's iron ore production expected to rise 8% to 340-345 million tonnes in FY27.
- Pellet production provisionally stood at 120 million tonnes in FY26 (up from 109 million tonnes in FY25).
- Global iron ore prices resilient at USD95-USD105/tonne; expected to hover between USD90-USD100/tonne.
- Domestic pellet prices range between INR9,000 to INR11,000/tonne, currently around INR10,000/tonne.
- India's steel demand outlook remains strong, supported by infrastructure, housing, railways, and manufacturing.

### Costs, raw materials & margins
- Iron ore mining costs increased due to lower production volumes and higher diesel consumption.
- Imported coal cost rose from INR10,500 to INR13,000.
- PNGRB guidelines increased gas purchase values by 40%-45%, making merchant pellet operations unviable during periods of soft pellet prices.
- Beneficiation at mines expected to reduce mining cost of usable concentrate below INR2,700 from FY28 (current cost INR3,000-INR3,500).
- Maharashtra industrial policy incentives expected to improve CRM complex margins by an additional 2%-3% (targeting 10%-11%).
- EV adoption in transportation reduced operating costs by ~75% and CO2 emissions by ~88%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ari Dongri mine expansion: Beneficiation plant ramp-up in Q3, full scale in FY28.
- CRM complex (Maharashtra): INR1,100 crores capex, land allotment expected end of August 2026, construction starts October 2026, commissioning December 2027.
- BESS project: Scheduled for commissioning in Q1 FY28 (container roll-out); INR501 crores incurred to date.
- Solar expansion: 25 MW commissioned in May 2026, 100 MW targeted for September 2026, 45 MW storage targeted for Q3 FY26.
- 1 million tonne integrated steel project and 150 MW solar project kept in abeyance.
- Boria Tibbu expansion: TOFR to be filed later; targeting 4 million tonne capacity by FY31.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- With the integrated steel plant put on hold, management stated no requirement to borrow money for current projects; sufficient free cash flows will fund them.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in government approvals (tree-cutting permissions, water allocation) impacting project timelines and mining operations.
- Volatility in imported coal, gas prices (PNGRB guidelines), and merchant iron ore prices impacting interim margins and forcing plant shutdowns.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Manav Gogia (YES Securities) asked about the status of the integrated steel plant and Vision 2030 numbers.** Abhishek Agrawal replied that the steel plant is kept on hold due to water allocation delays and should be viewed as an optional growth project. Vision 2030 numbers have been revised to exclude the steel plant and now reflect the CRM complex and BESS project.
2. **Aman Kothari (Aequitas Investment) asked about the sensitivity of iron ore pricing on profitability.** Abhishek Agrawal stated a INR100 change in pellet price impacts profit by INR40 crores annually on a 4 million production base, but pellet prices are primarily driven by steel sentiment.
3. **Yogansh (Mittal Analytics) asked about cost savings post-beneficiation and mining ramp-up.** Abhishek Agrawal explained that mining costs will drop below INR2,700/tonne from FY28 (from INR3,000-INR3,500 currently), and 100% captive pellet feed will be achieved.
4. **Sunil Jain (Nirmal Bang) asked about the nature of pending government approvals for mining dumping land.** Dinesh Gandhi and Abhishek Agrawal clarified that full mining land is available, but additional land required specifically for overburden dumping is awaiting final state government tree-cutting/revenue land approvals, expected by end of September.
5. **Rohan Mehta (Institutional Investor) asked about the breakdown of raw material cost increases.** Abhishek Agrawal stated that iron ore market procurement accounts for 75% of the cost increase and imported coal accounts for 25% (coal cost rose from INR10,500 to INR13,000).

### One-line takeaway
Godawari Power & Ispat delivered a resilient Q1 FY27 supported by steady realizations, though near-term profitability was compressed by high input costs and mining volume constraints, prompting the shelving of the integrated steel plant and the relocation of the CRM project to Maharashtra.
