# Pondy Oxides & Chemicals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth driven by the copper vertical and a strategic focus on value-added lead products, despite lead supply chain disruptions.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 931 crore (up 56% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Up 55% YoY
- **EBITDA:** Rs 56 crore (up 30% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Up 33% YoY
- **PAT:** Rs 36 crore (up 32% YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Up 43% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 6%
- **PAT Margin:** 3.9%
- **Lead EBITDA per ton:** Highest ever at Rs 21,595
- **Copper EBITDA per ton:** Rs 48,488 (up 66% YoY)
- **Working Capital Days:** 46 days (vs 53 days earlier)
- **Copper Expansion Capex Incurred:** Around Rs 25 crore

### Highlights
- Copper vertical achieved highest ever quarterly production and sales, with both volumes growing >3x YoY.
- Copper EBITDA per ton rose 66% YoY to Rs 48,488. Copper is expected to contribute ~45% of overall revenue in FY27.
- Lead production and sales moderated due to supply chain constraints, but management consciously prioritized high-margin, value-added products (85% of lead revenue).
- CRISIL upgraded POCL's outlook to A positive from A stable while reaffirming credit ratings.
- Plastic division turned profitable with ~Rs 15 lakh net profit on ~800 tonnes production. Aluminium production stood at ~200 tonnes.
- Unmonetized EPR credits remain in the books.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains a target 2030 roadmap: >15% volume growth, 20%+ CAGR in revenue and profitability, EBITDA margins >8%, ROCE >20%, and >60% revenue from value-added products.
- Sustainable lead EBITDA level guided at Rs 18,000 to Rs 20,000 per metric ton.
- Annual value-added mix for lead targeted at 65% to 70% for FY27 (vs 63% last year).
- Copper EBITDA per ton guided in excess of Rs 40,000 prior to full cathode integration; blended margin profile on copper cathode guided at Rs 60,000 to Rs 65,000 per ton.
- Total FY27 capex guided at ~Rs 175 crore (~Rs 20-25 crore maintenance, ~Rs 140-150 crore for the new copper plant).
- Lead volume commitment for FY27 is expected to be caught up or close to the ~1.25 lakh to 1.3 lakh tonnes committed last quarter, depending on supply chains.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management stated there is no demand softening globally; the lead volume moderation is solely due to supply chain and shipping delays (specifically via the Hormuz route).
- Export markets: Domestic vs. export mix stands at 55% and 45% overall; export mix for lead and copper is 55% and 25% respectively. Lead exports go predominantly to Southeast Asia.
- Domestic scrap pricing remains higher than imports even after factoring in EPR benefits, though domestic procurement is being re-evaluated.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses increased due to higher fuel prices and additive prices, alongside a small impact from logistics/freight.
- Middle Eastern scrap procurement is well below 5%; alternative sourcing is being expanded into Southeast Asia and South America.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Copper Cathode Facility (Thervoy Kandigai, Tamil Nadu):** 36,000 metric tonnes per annum facility with a ~Rs 200 crore total investment, fully funded via internal accruals (~Rs 25 crore spent so far). Phase 1 (18,000 MTPA) on track for commissioning by December 2026 (trial runs in Q4 FY27); Phase 2 targeted for Q3 FY28.
- **Copper Recycling Capacity:** The incremental 6,000 MTPA commissioned in Q4 FY26 ramped up well (~75% capacity utilization through FY27, including old capacities). Total existing copper recycling capacity is 12,000 MTPA.
- **Lead Capacity:** TKD new lead capacity utilization is <50% due to the focus on higher-margin, value-added production.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Fully funded via internal accruals; working capital cycle improved to 46 days. Payment cycle is tied to a week before material arrival at port, keeping working capital unstretched.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain disruptions and shipping delays via the Hormuz shipping route impacting raw material scrap imports.
- Higher domestic scrap pricing limiting domestic procurement options.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dheeraj Ram (360 ONE Capital) asked about lead volume catch-up vs. guidance:** Management stated they are confident of closing up to the committed numbers if supply chains stabilize in the next 1-2 months.
- **Sagar Shah (Spark Capital) asked if Western demand for lead was softening:** Management clarified there is no demand slowdown, only supply chain delays.
- **Khush (Geojit CMS) asked about alternate sourcing mitigation:** Management noted they are pushing for alternative supplies from Southeast Asia and South America, though inward freights will dictate pricing.
- **Utkarsh Somaiya (Elko Quantum Solutions) asked about FY28 copper recycling vs. cathode volumes:** Management clarified that 36,000 MTPA cathode capacity will utilize ~8,000 to 9,000 tonnes of internal recycling feedstock, with balance sourced via imports, resulting in a notional sales volume realization equivalent to ~40,000 tonnes.
- **Aniket Gada (Investor) asked about EPR credit impact and monetization:** Management confirmed credits remain unmonetized and domestic procurement will scale if domestic price deltas improve.

### One-line takeaway
POCL delivered robust Q1 FY27 revenue (up 56% YoY) and PAT (up 32% YoY) driven by a 3x surge in copper volumes and a high-margin value-added lead product mix (85%), while keeping its Rs 200 crore copper cathode expansion on track for December 2026.
