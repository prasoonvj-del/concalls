# Gallantt Ispat Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 13% YoY, but margins were compressed due to planned and unplanned plant shutdowns and higher input costs.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 1,146 crore (up 13% YoY from INR 1,013 crore)
- **EBITDA:** INR 111 crore (down from INR 185 crore in Q1 FY27 and INR 135 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 9.64% (vs 16.19% in Q1 FY27 and 12.91% in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA per ton:** INR 6,150 (vs INR 8,010 in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT:** INR 61 crore (PAT margin of 5.3%)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 2,292 crore (up 7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 296 crore (margin of 12.91%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 184 crore
- **TMT Sales (Q2):** 1,65,000 tonnes (vs 1,92,000 tonnes in Q1 FY27)
- **Kutch Rolling Mill Utilization:** 68% in Q2 FY27 (vs 66% in Q1 FY27)
- **Net Worth:** INR 3,487 crore as of September 30, 2026
- **Borrowings:** INR 592 crore (mostly working capital; zero term debt for projects)
- **H1 Capex:** INR 278 crore (INR 141 crore spent in Q2); cumulative spend of INR 650 crore out of the INR 3,000 crore program.

### Highlights
- Undertook a planned shutdown at the Gorakhpur plant to integrate expanded capacity to 1.3 million tonnes annually and an unplanned extension of a power plant shutdown due to a turbine issue requiring specialist support.
- Awarded a coal block in Singrauli in August 2026.
- 18 MW solar plant at Siddhpur (Gujarat) completed and set to resume operations by November 2026; 60 MW plant at Prayagraj (UP) on track for Q4 FY27.
- Shareholders approved a final dividend of INR 2 per share for FY26 at the AGM on September 30, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects performance to recover in Q3 as new capacity comes on stream, power plant resumes operations, and firmer steel prices take effect.
- Medium-term growth plan, including evaluating a larger greenfield opportunity built around raw material bases, will be presented in Q4 FY27.
- Captive iron ore mines targeted to become operational in FY28, expected to add approximately INR 2,000 per tonne to EBITDA once fully utilized.
- Solar projects expected to save INR 30 to INR 40 crore annually in power costs once operational.
- Balance capex of INR 500 crore planned for the rest of the year (INR 400 crore for Gorakhpur, INR 240 crore for mines, INR 170 crore for solar, INR 100 crore for Kutch).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Finished steel consumption in India grew 7.5% to about 85 million tonnes in H1.
- TMT prices in September were about 21% higher than a year ago.
- Demand is driven by infrastructure, housing, and urbanization; activity expected to pick up further in the construction season.
- Gallantt commands a 5% to 6% price premium over unbranded TMT in UP and 3% to 4% in Gujarat.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Coal and freight stayed elevated; metallics (sponge iron, pig iron) rose sharply in the second half of Q2.
- Coal price impact affected raw material cost by 4.5% to 5% on total sales revenue (approx INR 2,500 per tonne on TMT prices); iron ore rose 1%, resulting in a total raw material cost increase of approx INR 3,000 per tonne in Q2.
- Lower captive power availability during the power plant shutdown raised power costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Gorakhpur capacity expanding from 1 million to 1.23 million tonnes annually (operational in November 2026).
- Upstream debottlenecking at Gujarat unit on track for completion this financial year (power efficiency in Q3, sponge iron plant integration in Q4).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash positive with INR 426 crore in bank balances/liquid investments and INR 266 crore in short-term treasury deployment.
- Inventory rose to INR 669 crore from INR 551 crore in March due to shutdown stocking for new capacities; expected to normalize in Q3.
- Capital work-in-progress increased to INR 598 crore from INR 349 crore in March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Unplanned extension of the Gorakhpur power plant shutdown due to turbine issues.
- Global steel uncertainty, geopolitical tensions, and elevated energy, freight, and coal costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vini Jain (Jain Investments) on Coal Block and Funding:** Management stated the Singrauli coal block and iron ore blocks will provide security of supply and lower costs. On medium-term funding for the greenfield plan in Q4 FY27, they stated they largely rely on internal accruals, though the mix will depend on project size.
2. **Preeti Sharma on Margin Compression Drivers:** Management explained that higher coal costs impacted raw material costs by ~5%, while realizations increased only late in September. The price rise impact will flow into Q3.
3. **Anirudh Sharma on Inventory Increase:** Management stated inventory rose due to shutdown staging for new capacities and will normalize in Q3 once plants operate fully.
4. **Nimesh Pandya on Gorakhpur Ramp-up:** Management expects Gorakhpur to reach 85% to 90% capacity utilization within a month to a month and a half of starting operations in November, realizing full potential in Q4.
5. **Jimit Mehta on Coal Block Timeline:** Management stated they have 1 month to conclude due diligence, after which it will take 2 to 3 years to operationalize, eventually eliminating imported South African coal at Gorakhpur.

### One-line takeaway
Gallantt Ispat's Q2 FY27 margins faced a temporary compression due to plant shutdowns and input cost inflation, but management expects a swift recovery in H2 supported by capacity expansions, raw material integration, and solar cost savings.
