# Metals & Mining - Steel pipes & tubes: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the steel pipes and tubes sector reported a generally robust Q2 FY27, marked by record volumes and revenues for several players (such as APL Apollo, Aeroflex, Bansal Wire, Goodluck, Hariom, Hi-Tech, JTL, SG Mart, Sambhv, and Venus). Growth was primarily anchored by strong domestic housing, power infrastructure, and emerging sunrise sectors like data centers and defense. However, profitability for select players (notably Jindal Saw, Scoda Tubes, and Ratnamani) faced severe headwinds due to Middle East geopolitical conflicts, international container shortages, surging ocean freight rates, and localized input cost pressures.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Strong structural demand across domestic housing, automotive, power transmission/evacuation, railways, and emerging applications like solar energy structures and data center liquid cooling (highlighted by Aeroflex, Hi-Tech, SG Mart, Sambhv). 
*   **Divergence (Water Infrastructure vs. Industrial/Export):** While industrial, defense, and power sectors remain robust, companies exposed to water infrastructure (such as Jindal Saw and Ratnamani) reported sluggish demand due to state government budget constraints and slow fund releases under schemes like the Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM). Conversely, export-heavy players like Venus and Usha Martin noted strong traction in the US and Europe despite logistics hurdles.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Freight and Logistics:** Nearly all exporters and international players (APL Apollo, Aeroflex, Goodluck, JTL, Ratnamani, Usha Martin, Venus) cited margin compression caused by container non-availability and sharp spikes in international ocean freight rates stemming from Middle East geopolitical tensions.
*   **Power and Input Costs:** Rising power tariffs and fuel expenses impacted margins for Hariom, Scoda, and Venus. Several companies are mitigating this through captive renewable energy investments (Rajputana, Hariom, Scoda, Venus) or backward integration (Sambhv, SG Mart, Hariom).
*   **Pricing & Spread:** Primary vs. secondary steel price gaps widened (APL Apollo, JTL), creating margin divergence depending on a company's ability to pass on raw material price increases or pivot toward value-added products (VAP).

## Capex & capacity
The sector remains in an aggressive expansion phase, focusing on value-added products and backward integration:
*   **APL Apollo:** Targeting 8 million tonnes total capacity by FY28; expanding UAE capacity to 500,000 tonnes.
*   **Aeroflex:** Announced Rs 202 crore capex to scale stainless steel flexible flow solutions to 30 million meters and skids to 30,000 units by March 2028.
*   **BMW Industries:** Commissioned Phase 1 of the Bokaro greenfield downstream complex (colour coating line); peak debt estimated at Rs 800–900 crore.
*   **Bansal Wire:** Dadri brownfield and Sanand greenfield expansions on track to reach ~8.3 lakh tonnes capacity.
*   **Hi-Tech Pipes:** Advancing toward 2 million tonnes capacity by FY29; H2 capex pegged at Rs 150–200 crore.
*   **JTL Industries:** Mangaon facility expanding via GP and color-coated lines; H2 capex guided at Rs 125–150 crore.
*   **Rajputana Stainless:** Expanding melting capacity to 60,000 tonnes by Q4 FY27 and entering seamless pipes.
*   **Sambhv Steel:** Expanding via Kesda unit (3.6 lakh tonnes + 25 MW power) and a Rs 148 crore stainless steel seamless pipe facility.
*   **Usha Martin:** Expanding elevator rope capacity at Ranchi by 6,000 metric tonnes/annum (fully operational by Q1 FY28).
*   **Venus Pipes:** Full-year FY27 capex guided at Rs 130–140 crore (solar, spooling, fittings, and maintenance).

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Adjustments:** Hi-Tech Pipes revised its FY27 volume guidance to at least 6.5 lakh tons (from 6.5–7 lakh tons) and FY28 guidance to 8.5–9 lakh tons. 
*   **Execution & Milestones:** BMW Industries shifted from preparatory phase to active execution with the October 5 commissioning of Bokaro's colour coating line. Venus Pipes commenced its pipe spooling facility ahead of schedule, securing its first firm data center order. Hariom Pipe resumed operations at its Perundurai plant after a Q1 shutdown.
*   **Balance Sheet Actions:** Venus Pipes raised Rs 372 crore via preferential allotment to retire ~Rs 344 crore of debt. Usha Martin agreed to divest its non-core UM Cables division for Rs 77 crore.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Ramp-up and commercial utilization of newly commissioned capacities (Bokaro phase 1, Maharashtra GP/color-coated lines, Venus spooling).
*   Normalization of international container freight rates and mitigation of Middle East supply chain disruptions (impacting Jindal Saw, Ratnamani, Usha Martin, Venus).
*   Execution of large order books and conversion of LOIs (Jindal Saw, Ratnamani, Venus).
*   Working capital and inventory normalization across companies facing elongated collection cycles (Rajputana, Scoda, Ratnamani).
