# Usha Martin Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue growth, sustained EBITDA margins around 20%, and strong cash generation despite challenging international logistics and Middle East headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 financial targets of 15% revenue growth, operating EBITDA margins around 20%, and ~100% cash conversion (previous volume growth guidance of 10-12% was modulated to second-half volume recovery focus, while retaining overall topline goals).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains confident, though tempered regarding near-term international volume growth due to lingering geopolitical issues and sharp spikes in freight rates seen between February and September.
- New topics or risks: Entered into an agreement to divest the non-core UM Cables division (received Rs 77 crore consideration); launched a comprehensive turnaround program for the Thailand facility; utilized the Punjab Government’s One-Time Settlement Scheme (paid Rs 4 crore to settle entry tax disputes, reducing contingent liability by ~Rs 37 crore). Old risks around Middle East logistics and freight cost spikes persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Elevator rope capacity expansion at Ranchi is progressing and being commissioned as planned, with full incremental capacity (500 metric tons/month or 6,000 metric tons/annum) operational by Q1 FY28.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 1,000 crore (up 12.4% YoY)
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 202 crore (up 16.5% YoY)
- EBITDA Margin: 20.2% (up 70 bps YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT) from continuing operations: Rs 136 crore (up 10% YoY excluding previous year's one-time exceptional income of Rs 16 crore)
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue: Rs 2,021 crore (up 14.8% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA: Rs 410 crore (up 28.5% YoY; margin at 20.3%)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 278 crore (vs Rs 230 crore in H1 FY26)
- Operating EBITDA per ton: Rs 40,680 (vs Rs 34,915 in Q2 FY26; Rs 40,580 in Q1 FY27)
- Wire Rope Segment Revenue Growth: ~13% YoY (volumes marginally lower)
- Domestic Wire Rope Volumes: 12,700 metric tonnes in Q2 (up 14% YoY; realizations up 12%)
- Middle East Wire Rope Volumes: Down ~30% YoY (loss of ~2,500 tonnes in H1)
- US Entity H1 Performance: Volume up ~12%, revenue up ~22% YoY
- Europe Sales H1 Growth: Up ~11% YoY (primarily via service and product mix)
- Wires Business: Volumes up 21.7% YoY; revenue up 35% YoY
- LRPC Volumes: Down by about 4,000 tonnes in H1 (from 23,000 to 19,000)
- Plasticated LRPC Capacity: 6,000 tonnes (targeting 3,500 tonnes in FY27)
- Operating Cash Flow Before Tax (H1): Rs 413 crore (101% cash conversion)
- Free Cash Flow (H1): Rs 186 crore
- Net Cash Position: Rs 415 crore as of September 2026
- Capital Expenditure (H1): Rs 143 crore
- Net Working Capital Days: Reduced to 185 days (from 194 days in March 2026; inventory days down to 166 days)
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): ~21.6% (vs 20.6% at end of FY26)
- Capacity Utilisation: Across the board at 70% - 75%

### Highlights
- Signed an agreement for the divestment of the UM Cables division, receiving Rs 77 crore in consideration with final payment expected within 30 days.
- Launched a comprehensive turnaround program for the Thailand facility (profitability currently at 6%-7% EBITDA margins, targeted to improve to 14%-15% over 18 months) to lower costs and upgrade product mix.
- Settled old entry tax disputes via Punjab Government's One-Time Settlement Scheme for Rs 4 crore, reducing contingent liability by ~Rs 37 crore.
- Commissioned new mining rope capacity of ~100 tonnes per month for higher value-added products.
- Secured a three-year port contract with a large port operator in Southeast Asia.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of achieving FY27 financial targets: 15% revenue growth, operating EBITDA margins around 20%, and ~100% cash conversion.
- Expects volume growth to improve during the second half despite an uncertain external environment.
- Organic capex guidance maintained at Rs 200 crore to Rs 250 crore annually.
- Elevator rope capacity is expected to add ~Rs 130 crore to Rs 150 crore in topline for FY28.
- Plasticated LRPC volume target is 7,000 to 8,000 tonnes for next year and ~10,000 tonnes the year after, with capacity expansion planned up to 11,000-12,000 tonnes per annum by June next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic wire rope demand remained healthy despite monsoons (12,700 tonnes, up 14% YoY).
- International demand in the Middle East was soft due to geopolitical uncertainty, higher freight costs, and cautious distributor stocking, impacting general-purpose ropes.
- U.S. and European markets showed value growth through specialized and higher-value rope applications, though logistics disruptions and 4x-6x peak freight rate increases (Feb-Sep) impacted general-purpose volumes.
- Indian elevator industry is growing at 20%-25% annually.
- Domestic wire demand was robust, led by the auto sector, springs, and power/transmission sectors.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Freight rates to key markets (U.S., Europe, Middle East) increased by 4x-6x at peak between February and September, but have begun easing since September.
- Successfully held operating EBITDA margins at 20.2% through a better product mix (higher value-added mix) and passing on cost increases where possible in specialized segments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex stood at Rs 143 crore.
- Elevator rope expansion at Ranchi (6,000 metric tonnes per annum) is being commissioned, with full incremental capacity (500 tonnes/month) operational by Q1 FY28.
- Commissioned 100 tonnes/month of higher value-added mining rope capacity.
- Preparing to expand plasticated LRPC capacity from 6,000 tonnes to 11,000-12,000 tonnes per annum by June next year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Closed September 2026 with a net cash position of Rs 415 crore.
- Operating cash flow before tax in H1 was Rs 413 crore (101% conversion).
- Net working capital days improved to 185 days (inventory days down to 166).
- ROCE stood at 21.6%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainty, conflict in the Middle East, and volatile logistics/freight costs affecting general-purpose rope volumes and distributor stocking.
- Conventional black LRPC volumes under margin pressure due to commoditization and low domestic pricing.
- Soft profitability at the Thailand facility (addressed via ongoing turnaround program).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aman Sonthalia (AK Securities) on flat rope volumes:** Management explained that a 30% volume drop in the Middle East and logistics hurdles offset growth in India and the US, but profitability was protected via product mix and freight pass-throughs.
2. **Varun Jain (Dolat Capital) on elevator rope financial impact:** Management confirmed the 6,000-tonne capacity addition should sell out domestically, adding ~Rs 130 crore to Rs 150 crore in topline for FY28 at historical segment margins.
3. **Sahil Doshi (Thinqwise) on volume guidance recoverability:** Management noted that general-purpose rope volumes were delayed rather than lost due to high freight rates (4x-6x spikes), and recovery is expected as freight rates soften.
4. **Prolin Nandu (Edelweiss Public Alternatives) on limits of value-add mix:** Management stated that value-add is currently ~71% in value terms (under 60% in volume terms) and has further room to scale upwards beyond 75%.
5. **Shraddha Kapadia (SMIFS) on wire segment growth drivers:** Management stated growth is driven by the domestic auto sector, door springs, and power/transmission sectors, where Usha Martin holds a low initial market share.

### One-line takeaway
Usha Martin delivered resilient Q2 FY27 performance with 12.4% revenue growth, 20.2% operating EBITDA margins, and a robust net cash balance of Rs 415 crore, driven by a rich product mix despite Middle East geopolitical and freight headwinds.
