# Kalyani Cast-Tech Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights strong order book, successful commissioning of the Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal ahead of time, and continuous innovations driving business resilience despite geopolitical uncertainties.

### Key numbers
- **Order book:** INR 133 crore (expected to reach ~INR 170 crore; Government share: INR 45 crore)
- **Revenue growth guidance (FY27):** Top line expected to reach up to INR 170 crore
- **PAT and EPS growth (FY25 vs FY26 stated in presentation):** PAT and EPS grew by almost 20%; Revenue increased by 7.7%
- **Capacity & Utilization:** Rewari facility capacity ~7,000 TEUs (utilization 75% to 80%, ~5,400 TEUs manufactured last year); Shivlakha facility capacity ~10,000 TEUs (production started in June, ~500 containers manufactured) + wagon manufacturing capacity ~2,400 wagons per annum
- **Debt/Funding:** INR 100 crore debt sanctioned from HDFC Bank (unused till now; funding managed through equity/warrants and internal accruals)

### Highlights
- Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal at Shivlakha (Kutch, Gujarat) commissioned before time and inaugurated by the Minister for Railways on September 15, 2026.
- Received permission to construct and operate an Inland Container Depot (ICD) on September 10, 2026; construction of warehousing started with operations expected by the start of the next financial year.
- Wagon manufacturing facility complete; application submitted to RDSO for G-105 audit/certification. Stage 1 design approval cleared by RDSO, with final approval expected by March 31, 2027.
- Added new customers including JSW Ports (~1,000 to 1,500 containers in FY), Indian Navy, and Central Warehousing Corporation (~INR 30 crore order).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H1 numbers to be impacted by geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disturbances, but anticipates catching up strongly in H2.
- Guided for FY27 top line to reach around INR 170 crore.
- Management maintains a long-term vision of touching INR 4,500 crore revenue over the next 7 to 8 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Innovations (such as specialized dwarf containers, stainless steel salt containers, wider wheat containers with hatches, 40-foot super high-cube cotton bale containers, and triple-deck two-wheeler containers) comprise almost 50% of the current order book.
- Indian Railways is adopting more container-friendly policies, enabling higher volumes and modal shift from road to rail.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Container manufacturing PAT margins expected around 10% to 11%.
- ICD and trade terminal operations EBITDA margins expected in the range of 25% to 30%.
- Wagon manufacturing has 60% to 70% bought-out items, so value addition is lower compared to containers.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Shivlakha facility spans 144 to 150 acres, integrating container manufacturing, wagon manufacturing, a rail terminal, and an ICD.
- Potential future greenfield expansion for 100,000 container capacity estimated to cost around INR 1,400 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Zero-EMI company currently; capex funded via internal accruals, equity/warrants, with an untapped INR 100 crore debt sanction from HDFC Bank.
- Trade receivables from Briskworld Ventures (INR 4.67 crore / INR 6.4 crore noted in annual report) are being recovered.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties and global business volatility causing supply chain disturbances and impacting H1 performance.
- Container industry demand can experience yo-yo growth (high volatility).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kalyan (Stocks Stowing Limited) on Government Container Scheme:** Asked if Kalyani Cast-Tech benefits from the INR 10,000 crore container manufacturing scheme. Management stated they would qualify from day one if they scale capacity to 25,000 TEUs, but await detailed guidelines.
2. **Jayakumar (Individual Investor) on Wagon Moat:** Asked about the competitive moat in wagon manufacturing. Management replied they are not in a rat race for standard wagons; they designed their own patented wagon under new railway policies to avoid general-purpose competition.
3. **Venkat Velupally (Advisors LLP) on Capital and Margins:** Asked about capital adequacy and margins. Management noted they can raise funds via debt or preferential shares if needed, and guided to 10%-11% PAT for containers and 25%-30% EBITDA for ICD operations.
4. **Yash Anand (Individual Investor) on Order Book Mix:** Asked about government vs. private order mix. Management stated INR 45 crore is from government, while private/business associates make up the rest and drive innovation demand.
5. **Anil Sarin (Individual Investor) on Patent Protection:** Asked about copycat competition for special containers. Management responded that while copycats exist, their strategic hub locations (North and West) and cost efficiency are key differentiators.

### One-line takeaway
Kalyani Cast-Tech is leveraging proprietary design innovations and its integrated Shivlakha logistics hub (rail siding, ICD, container, and wagon manufacturing) to drive domestic containerization and long-term growth.
