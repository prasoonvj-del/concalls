# Hindustan Zinc Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly net profit, record EBITDA, lowest zinc cost of production since underground transition, and revised full-year cost guidance lower.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 15,093 crore, up 77% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 9,285 crore (record high), up 108% YoY; EBITDA margin at 62%.
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 6,412 crore (record high), up 142% YoY.
- **Zinc Cost of Production (COP):** $703 per ton (excluding royalty), down 29% YoY.
- **Production Output:** Mined metal output up 5% YoY (highest-ever Q2 output); refined metal output up 7% YoY; saleable silver output up 20% YoY to 173 tonnes.
- **H1 FY27 Financials:** Revenue at Rs 28,840 crore (up 77% YoY), EBITDA at Rs 17,359 crore (up 108% YoY), and PAT at Rs 11,881 crore (up 143% YoY).
- **Free Cash Flow:** Rs 6,389 crore generated in Q2 before growth capex.

### Highlights
- Zinc cost of production for Q2 dropped 29% YoY to $703 per ton, driven by higher operational efficiency and sulfuric acid byproduct realization benefits.
- Silver contributed ~45% of total profitability in H1 FY27.
- Monetized 10 KT of lead concentrate in Q2 (containing 10 tonnes silver and 6,000 tonnes lead), generating Rs 300 crore revenue and Rs 155 crore profit.
- Renewable energy accounted for 27% of power consumption in Q2, with subsidiary HZAPL running on 100% renewable power.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Zinc COP Guidance:** Revised downward for full-year FY27 to $825–$850 per ton from the earlier $975–$1,000 per ton.
- **Silver Production Guidance:** Management maintained full-year guidance at 680 tonnes for FY27 and expects to potentially reach 730–750 tonnes in FY28 post debottlenecking.
- **Capex Guidance:** Reiterated full-year capex guidance of Rs 5,000 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global refined zinc demand for 2026 is forecasted at 13.87 million tonnes against supply of 13.69 million tonnes, leaving an ~180 KT market deficit.
- Global silver market is expected to remain in deficit for the 6th consecutive year in 2026, with an estimated shortfall of ~1.44 thousand tonnes.
- Management expects persistent mine closures and grade declines over the next decade to keep zinc markets tight while infrastructure and urbanization drive demand growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Domestic coal usage stood at 32% during Q2 FY27.
- High sulfuric acid byproduct realizations significantly supported zinc COP reduction, offset partially by ~$50 per ton escalation in geopolitical/input costs.
- Ore grade in Q2 was 7.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total Q2 capex was ~Rs 1,900 crore (Rs 1,200 crore sustaining capex and Rs 700 crore growth capex). Higher capex spend is slated for Q3 and Q4.
- Engineering and procurement for the 250 KTPA integrated zinc expansion are nearly complete; mine development is in progress.
- The 10 MTPA tailings reprocessing plant expansion is progressing as planned.
- Exploration programs for three critical mineral blocks are targetted to complete by Q3 FY29 to define resources. Mining lease has been executed for the Gundlupet REE & Yttrium block.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash balance stood at Rs 11,268 crore at the end of Q2 FY27.
- Total H1 contribution to the national exchequer surpassed Rs 13,000 crore.
- H1 FY27 delivered 15% total shareholder return (Rs 64 per share capital appreciation and Rs 11 per share dividend).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical issues, trade/energy disruptions, changing tariff policies, and regional growth divergences pose near-term cost/price volatility.
- Geopolitical challenges created an ~$50 per ton cost impact on operations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pallav Agarwal (Antique):** *Details on zinc and silver hedges for Q3 and Q4?*
  - **Management:** Q3 has 40 KT zinc hedged at $3,476/ton and 8 tonnes silver hedged at $68/oz. Q4 has 36 KT zinc hedged at $3,698/ton.
- **Kartikeya Pandey:** *What was domestic coal mix and Q2 capex breakdown?*
  - **Management:** Domestic coal mix was 32%. Q2 capex comprised Rs 1,200 crore sustaining capex and Rs 700 crore growth capex. Full-year capex remains guided at Rs 5,000 crore.
- **Anuj Khandelwal:** *Updates on newly acquired critical mineral blocks?*
  - **Management:** Exploration programs for 3 blocks will wrap up in Q3 FY29 to determine resources. Gundlupet REE/Yttrium mining lease is executed; production clarity expected in 2–3 years.
- **Shweta Dikshit:** *Breakdown of lead concentrate sales in Q2 and future plans?*
  - **Management:** Q2 lead concentrate sales generated Rs 300 crore revenue and Rs 155 crore profit from 10 KT concentrate. Sales are opportunistic based on smelter capacity vs. concentrate availability.
- **Sumangal Nevatia:** *Specific revenue contribution and guidance for sulfuric acid?*
  - **Management:** Refused to provide specific forward guidance or breakdown on sulfuric acid revenue/COP credits, citing it as an auction-priced byproduct driven by market dynamics.

### One-line takeaway
Hindustan Zinc delivered a record financial performance in Q2 FY27 on robust operational volumes, strong silver contributions, and reduced cost of production, prompting a downward revision in full-year zinc COP guidance.
