# Hindustan Zinc Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and net profit driven by strong metal production, supportive commodity prices, and lower cost of production.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR15,093 crore (up 77% YoY)
- **H1 Revenue from operations:** INR28,840 crore (up 77% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR9,285 crore (up 108% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** INR17,359 crore (up 108% YoY)
- **EBITDA margin:** 62%
- **Net profit:** INR6,412 crore (up 142% YoY)
- **H1 Net profit:** INR11,881 crore (up 143% YoY)
- **Mined metal production:** Up 5% YoY (highest-ever Q2 output)
- **Refined metal production:** Up 7% YoY
- **Saleable silver production:** 173 tonnes (up 20% YoY); H1 total at 321 tonnes (plus 20 tonnes in concentrate)
- **Zinc cost of production (COP):** $703 per tonne (excluding royalty) in Q2; H1 average at $776 per tonne
- **Free cash flow before growth capex:** INR6,389 crore in Q2
- **Net cash balance:** INR11,268 crore as of quarter-end
- **Q2 Capex:** INR1,200 crore (sustaining) + INR700 crore (growth) = INR1,900 crore total
- **Renewable energy share:** 27% of power consumption

### Highlights
- Recorded zero fatalities across all operations in Q2.
- Subsidiary Hindustan Zinc Alloys Private Limited (HZAPL) is now using 100% renewable power.
- Monetized 10 KT of lead concentrate in Q2 containing ~10 tonnes of silver and ~6,000 tonnes of lead, generating INR300 crore revenue and INR155 crore profit.
- Added nearly 3,000 Nand Ghars in Rajasthan, bringing the total to 12,263.
- Contributed over INR13,000 crore to the national exchequer in H1 through taxes, royalties, duties, and statutory levies.
- Delivered 15% total shareholder return in H1 (INR64 per share capital appreciation + INR11 dividend).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised full-year FY27 zinc cost of production guidance from $975–$1,000 per tonne down to $825–$850 per tonne.
- Full-year capex guidance maintained at approximately INR5,000 crore.
- Renewable energy share targeted to increase from 27% to 35% in upcoming quarters.
- Silver production guidance for FY27 maintained at 680 tonnes.
- Exploration of three critical mineral blocks to conclude in Q3 FY29; Gundlupet REE/Yttrium mining lease execution underway with business plan indications in 2–3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India GDP growth projected at 6.4% in 2026 and 6.7% in 2027 (above global averages of 3% and 3.4%).
- 2026 refined zinc demand forecasted at 13.87 million tonnes against supply of 13.69 million tonnes (~180 KT deficit); market expected to remain tight due to limited new projects, mine closures, and declining grades.
- Silver expected to remain in deficit for the sixth consecutive year in 2026 (~1.44 thousand tonnes shortfall), supported by increasing industrial and new-tech demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 zinc COP stood at $703 per tonne (excluding royalty), supported by positive byproduct realization (sulfuric acid) offset by ~$50 cost escalations from geopolitical issues.
- Domestic coal usage percentage was 32% in Q2.
- Sulfuric acid sold on an auction basis; historical revenue contribution noted around INR1,000–INR1,200 crore annually.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Engineering and procurement for the 250 KTPA integrated zinc expansion are almost complete; mine development is underway.
- 10 million tonnes per annum tailing reprocessing plant progressing as per plan.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Closed the quarter with a net cash balance of INR11,268 crore.
- Generated free cash flow before growth capex of INR6,389 crore in Q2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global business environment remains challenging due to escalating geopolitical tensions, energy and trade disruptions, changing tariff policies, and divergent regional growth trends causing near-term volatility in inflation, currencies, and financial markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pallav Agarwal (Antique Stock Broking):** Asked about hedging details. *Management answered that Q3 has 40 KT of zinc hedged at $3,476 and Q4 has 36 KT at $3,698; Q3 also has 8 tonnes of silver hedged at $68 per ounce.*
- **Pallav Agarwal:** Inquired if sulfuric acid strength could drive further positive COP surprises. *Management stated they expect to remain broadly in the same range, guiding full-year COP to $825–$850 per tonne.*
- **Kartikeya Pandey:** Asked about domestic coal usage and capex bifurcation. *Management replied domestic coal usage was 32%, and Q2 capex included INR1,200 crore sustaining and INR700 crore growth, with full-year capex guided around INR5,000 crore.*
- **Anuj Khandelwal:** Asked about critical mineral blocks and ore grade. *Management stated three blocks are under exploration until Q3 FY29, the Gundlupet block is a newly executed mining lease, and Q2 ore grade was 7.6%.*
- **Shweta Dikshit:** Inquired about lead concentrate sales revenue/margins and silver guidance. *Management stated 10 KT of concentrate yielded INR300 crore revenue and INR155 crore profit; FY27 silver production guidance of 680 tonnes is maintained.*
- **Sumangal Nevatia:** Asked for COP without sulfuric acid credits and historical sulfuric revenue. *Management declined to provide isolated byproduct cost/revenue guidance as it fluctuates based on market auction prices, noting historical annual ranges around INR1,000–1,200 crore.*

### One-line takeaway
Hindustan Zinc delivered record-breaking Q2 FY27 performance with EBITDA surging 108% YoY to INR9,285 crore and net profit up 142% to INR6,412 crore, bolstered by strong metal output, high commodity prices, and reduced production costs.
