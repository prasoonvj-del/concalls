# Hi-Tech Pipes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered robust volume and revenue growth, improving working capital, and maintaining healthy EBITDA per ton despite macro headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Volume guidance for FY27 was revised to at least 6.5 lakh tons (from 6.5 lakh to 7 lakh tons previously); FY28 volume guidance shifted to 8.5 lakh to 9 lakh tons (from 1 million tons previously).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, highlighting peak solar demand in H2 and easing logistics headwinds as the monsoon and war impacts subside.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Addressed market rumors regarding APL Apollo buying shares; old supply chain/import consignment issues from high-seas trading were not mentioned.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Timelines for Sanand DFT, API facility, and Hindupur coated facility were updated to Q4 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Sales volume:** 1.65 lakh tons in Q2 FY27 vs 1.25 lakh tons in Q2 FY26, up 32% YoY; 3.21 lakh tons in H1 FY27 vs 2.49 lakh tons in H1 FY26, up 29% YoY.
- **Revenue from operations:** INR1,466 crores in Q2 FY27 vs INR850 crores in Q2 FY26, up 70.7% YoY; INR2,879 crores in H1 FY27 vs INR1,650 crores in H1 FY26, up 74.47% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** INR56.58 crores in Q2 FY27 vs INR44.33 crores in Q2 FY26, up 27.63% YoY; INR106 crores in H1 FY27 vs INR85 crores in H1 FY26, up 24.12% YoY.
- **EBITDA per ton:** INR3,429 in Q2 FY27 vs INR3,540 in Q2 FY26 (and INR3,500 overall for H1).
- **Net working capital days:** Decreased from 56 days to 33 days in H1 FY27.
- **Debt equity ratio:** Stable at 0.23x vs 0.18x.
- **Net debt:** INR300 crores as on H1 (term loans and working capital).
- **Capex:** INR150 crores to INR200 crores in H2 to complete 3.5 lakh tons of capacity; annual capex indicated around INR150 crores for FY27 and FY28.

### Highlights
- Sales volume grew 32% YoY to 1.65 lakh tons in Q2 FY27.
- Working capital days improved significantly from 56 days to 33 days in H1 FY27.
- Brand visibility initiatives included participating in industry exhibitions and trade shows.
- Management explicitly denied market rumors of APL Apollo buying shares in the company.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Volume guidance:** At least 6.5 lakh tons for FY27; 8.5 lakh tons to 9 lakh tons for FY28; 1.1 million tons to 1.2 million tons for FY29.
- **Capacity milestones:** 1.35 million tons by end of FY27; 1.7 million tons in FY28; 2 million tons by FY29.
- **Revenue potential:** Approximately INR9,000 crores of revenue from the 2 million ton capacity at a 65% utilization level.
- **EBITDA per ton:** Expected to range from INR4,000 to INR4,500 per ton as value-added products (VAP) share increases from 40% to 55%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth supported by broad-based demand across major product categories and key end-user industries.
- Solar and export segments making increasingly valuable contributions; solar sector entering its peak season in H2.
- Emerging sectors like data centers showing promising demand alongside traditional sectors.
- Solar segment identified as a 1 million ton market opportunity (calculating 20-22 tons per 1 megawatt on 40-50 GW annual installations).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 faced challenges from energy prices, logistics, and ocean freight costs.
- Maintained EBITDA per ton at approximately INR3,500 despite headwinds.
- Logistics cost pressures are easing as the effects of the war subside.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Committed to long-term vision of becoming a 2 million ton capacity company by FY29, adding 1 million tons total by FY29.
- Sanand Unit II Phase 3 DFT facility progress on schedule for Q4 FY27 commissioning.
- API facility advancing as planned for Q4 FY27 readiness, targeting oil and gas transportation.
- Hindupur integrated coated facility construction on track for Q4 FY27 commissioning.
- Balance sheet shows CWIP of INR200 crores and advances in non-current assets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stood at INR300 crores as of H1 FY27.
- Net working capital days reduced to 33 days, with management aiming to bring it under 30 days.
- Capex funded through a mix of internal accruals and term loans, with a strong focus on internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Challenges from geopolitical conflicts/war and monsoon season impacting H1 operations.
- Energy and logistic/ocean freight cost pressures during H1.
