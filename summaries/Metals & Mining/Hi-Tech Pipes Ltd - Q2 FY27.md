# Hi-Tech Pipes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong YoY volume and revenue growth driven by solar, export, and core segments despite seasonal and freight challenges.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall trajectory; FY27 volume guided at "at least 6.5 lakh tons" (vs 6.5–7.0 lakh tons in Q1); FY28 volume guided at 8.5–9.0 lakh tons (vs 1.0 million tons in Q1).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive; management expects higher demand in H2 as monsoons end and solar installation peaks.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared:
  - Explicitly denied market rumors regarding APL Apollo buying shares in the company.
  - DFT facility commissioning timeline at Sanand shifted slightly from Q3 FY27 to Q4 FY27.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered:
  - Working capital management delivered strong results, with net working capital days falling to 33 days in H1 FY27 (from 56 days).
  - API and Hindupur facilities remain on track for Q4 FY27 commissioning.

### Key numbers
- **Sales Volume:** 1.65 lakh tonnes in Q2 FY27, up 32% YoY (vs 1.25 lakh tonnes in Q2 FY26).
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 1,466 crore in Q2 FY27, up 70.7% YoY (vs Rs 850 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 56.58 crore in Q2 FY27, up 27.63% YoY (vs Rs 44.33 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **EBITDA per ton:** Rs 3,429 in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 3,540 in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 2,879 crore, up 74.47% YoY; H1 sales volume grew 29% YoY to 3.21 lakh tonnes.
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 106 crore, up 24.12% YoY.

### Highlights
- Volume and revenue growth were supported by solar tubes, exports, and emerging demand from data centers.
- Management squashed market rumors of APL Apollo purchasing equity shares in Hi-Tech Pipes.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Sales Volume Guidance:** At least 6.5 lakh tonnes in FY27; 8.5 to 9.0 lakh tonnes in FY28; 1.1 to 1.2 million tonnes in FY29.
- **Target Revenue:** Expected at ~Rs 9,000 crore at 65% utilization once full 2 million tonnes capacity is reached by FY29.
- **EBITDA per ton Guidance:** Expecting to maintain ~Rs 3,500/ton in H2 FY27; long-term target of Rs 4,000 to Rs 4,500/ton as Value-Added Product (VAP) share increases from 40% to 55%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India adds 40–50 GW of solar capacity annually, where every 1 MW requires 20–22 tonnes of steel tubes/pipes, representing a 1 million ton annual addressable market.
- Demand in H2 FY27 is projected to pick up strongly following the monsoon slowdown and peak solar installation season.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margins were pressured by higher ocean freight, logistics costs, and energy prices linked to geopolitical tensions, which management expects to ease in H2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Adding 3 lakh tonnes capacity in Q4 FY27 to reach 1.35 million tonnes total capacity by FY27-end (moving to 1.7 million tonnes in FY28 and 2.0 million tonnes in FY29).
- Three major projects commissioning in Q4 FY27: DFT facility at Sanand Unit II Phase 3, API pipes facility, and Hindupur integrated coated facility.
- H2 FY27 Capex: Guided at Rs 150 crore to Rs 200 crore (CWIP stands at Rs 200 crore plus non-current advances). Annual capex for FY27 and FY28 expected around Rs 150 crore each.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net Debt: Stand at ~Rs 300 crore as of H1 FY27 across term loans and working capital.
- Debt-to-Equity ratio: Stable at 0.23x (vs 0.18x).
- Working Capital: Net working capital days reduced significantly from 56 days to 33 days in H1 FY27, with management targeting below 30 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated ocean freight and logistics costs caused by geopolitical situations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rithik Dhariwal (GK Capital):** Inquired about rumors of APL Apollo buying shares in Hi-Tech Pipes. *Management Answer:* Denied the rumors completely, stating no such talks or developments exist.
- **Pallav Agarwal (Antique Stock Broking):** Asked for volume guidance across FY27–FY29 and capex plans. *Management Answer:* Guided volumes of 6.5 lakh tonnes (FY27), 8.5–9 lakh tonnes (FY28), and 1.1–1.2 million tonnes (FY29); H2 capex of Rs 150–200 crore.
- **Mithun Soni (GeeCee Holdings):** Asked about blended EBITDA per ton trajectory. *Management Answer:* Expecting EBITDA per ton to reach Rs 4,000–4,500 as VAP mix moves from 40% to 55%.

Hi-Tech Pipes sustained 30%+ volume growth, expanded working capital efficiency, and kept its 2 million ton FY29 capacity expansion plan firmly on track.
