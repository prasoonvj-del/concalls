# Mishra Dhatu Nigam Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Significant YoY growth across turnover, VoP, PBT, and PAT, with management expecting H2 EBITDA margins to surpass last year's levels.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained top-line growth target of ~20% (~Rs 1,600 crore turnover target from Ministry); upgraded margin outlook to surpass last year's 22.8% EBITDA margin in H2 (compared to Q1 expectation of 20%-21% normalization).
- Tone and demand view: Turned more optimistic on margin recovery and aerospace order conversion.
- New topics or risks: Detailed discussion on the MoU with Heavy Water Board (DAE) for nickel and cobalt scrap recovery; metal bank setup progressing toward FY-end operationalization.
- Last quarter's promises: S-400 certification secured in H1 as promised; spring plant became operational.

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Turnover: Rs 295.91 crore (up 41.09% YoY from Rs 209.73 crore)
- Q2 FY27 Value of Production (VoP): Rs 310.8 crore (up 21.2% YoY from Rs 256.38 crore)
- Q2 FY27 PBT: Rs 38.75 crore (up 102.6% YoY from Rs 19.13 crore)
- Q2 FY27 PAT: Rs 27.69 crore (up 116.84% YoY from Rs 12.77 crore)
- H1 FY27 Turnover: Rs 535.4 crore (up 40.81% YoY)
- H1 FY27 VoP: Rs 571.15 crore (up 14.76% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PBT: Rs 62.67 crore (up 64.4% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 44.01 crore (up 72.05% YoY)
- Order Book (as of October 1, 2026): Rs 2,164 crore (Aero 35%, Space 22%, Naval 14%, Missile 10%, Energy 8%, Others balance)
- Scrap inventory: Rs 353 crore (down from opening Rs 392 crore)

### Highlights
- Secured S-400 certification in H1 FY27.
- Signed an MoU with the Heavy Water Board (Department of Atomic Energy) for long-term recovery of nickel and cobalt from plant scrap.
- Spring manufacturing plant is now operational.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a 20% top-line growth for the year and aims to meet the Ministry's target of Rs 1,600 crore turnover.
- Management expects H2 FY27 EBITDA margins to surpass last year's 22.8% figure as sales volumes increase.
- Management expects Rs 450 crore to Rs 500 crore of order inflows in Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Company is in touch with private aerospace OEMs (GE, Safran, CFM, Pratt & Whitney) for supply audits and certifications.
- Executing trial orders for specialized titanium alloy grades for the AMCA program.
- Average raw material mix utilizes roughly 70% virgin metal and 30% scrap, depending on customer approvals (e.g., Gaganyaan requires 100% virgin).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Majority of contracts are fixed-price; for delivery under 18 months, price variation clauses are disallowed, so pricing factors expected escalation based on experience.
- Raw material prices are showing signs of cooling off.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rs 1,000 crore capex plan received in-principle Board approval; technical specifications are under finalization, financial approval is pending, and execution will take 3 years.
- Ordered a ~Rs 40 crore 3D powder manufacturing unit with a preliminary BAFA license; facility expected within 1 year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Scrap inventory reduced to Rs 353 crore in H1 due to improved usage.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical volatility affecting raw material prices.
- Lengthy audit and certification cycles with global private aerospace OEMs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shashi Ranjan (Anandan Capital) on turnaround time:** Management explained that vacuum-melted batch processes have chamber size limitations, resulting in a 2-3 month cycle time from raw material to finished superalloy.
- **Ravi Naredi (Naredi Investments) on capex timeline:** Management stated the Rs 1,000 crore capex will take 3 years once financial sanction and specifications are finalized.
- **Abhishek Jain (KRIIS PMS) on scrap vs virgin usage:** Management noted an average of 70% virgin and 30% scrap usage, and highlighted the Heavy Water Board MoU for internal nickel/cobalt recovery.
- **Arya Shah (Whitestone PMS) on 3D powder printer and spring plant:** Management confirmed the Rs 40 crore powder facility is ordered with a BAFA license, and the spring plant is now operational.
- **Henil Bagadia (EquiCorp) on Wide Plate Mill and DAE MoU:** Management clarified that the wide plate mill is a strategic asset utilized based on project orders, and the DAE MoU aims to secure critical nickel/cobalt supplies domestically.

### One-line takeaway
MIDHANI posted a strong Q2 FY27 with a 41% YoY jump in turnover and a 116% surge in PAT, supported by management's confidence in beating last year's EBITDA margins in H2.
