# Bhagyanagar India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Achieved record revenue of Rs 1,000 crore and stable margins, but management lowered volume growth guidance and flagged headwinds from steep copper price corrections.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Volume growth guidance lowered from 12%-15% to 10% YoY. EBITDA margin guidance updated to 4.5%-5.0% (previously 5%-5.5%).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from strong unmitigated optimism to addressing a steep copper price correction, US inventory overhang, and a temporary market freeze affecting Q3.
- New topics or risks: Steep copper price drop (15%-16% from peak), US inventory release of ~1 million tonnes, anti-AI/anti-trade fund liquidation. Old topics: GST tax case and funding rounds were not discussed.
- Last quarter's promises: Demerger executed (shares allotted 1:1 on Oct 8, 2026; BSE/NSE in-principle approval received; listing targeted for Dec 2026). Capacity expansion to 45,000 tonnes reaffirmed for late next year (Q4 FY28).

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,000 crore (highest ever)
- EBITDA: Rs 52 crore
- EBITDA margin: 5.08%
- PAT: Rs 30.5 crore
- Total tonnage: 7,300 tonnes
- EBITDA per kg: Rs 72
- Inventory: Rs 303 crore as of September 30, 2026
- Capex spent: Rs 9.45 crore in H1 out of Rs 40 crore total spread over two years

### Highlights
- Crossed Rs 1,000 crore in quarterly turnover for the first time.
- ICRA upgraded the company's credit rating to A-.
- Completed copper business restructuring into Tieramet Limited; shares allotted on a 1:1 basis on October 8, 2026, with in-principle approvals from BSE and NSE.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management lowered full-year volume growth guidance to 10% YoY (from 12%-15%).
- Management expects Q3 revenue to decline due to falling copper prices, but anticipates recovery in Q4.
- Management guided EBITDA margins to remain between 4.5% and 5.0%.
- Management maintained the long-term target of Rs 5,000 crore revenue by FY30.
- Capacity expansion from 35,000 to 45,000 metric tonnes scheduled for the end of next year (Q4 FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Copper prices corrected steeply by 15%-16% from peak due to fund liquidation and anticipated US inventory release.
- About 1 million tonnes of copper inventory is locked in the US, while the rest of the world (including China and India) faces shortages and backwardation.
- AI data center bus bar volumes are growing at 20%-25% annually.
- EPR was officially launched on April 1, 2026, but management sees slow enforcement and expects no major short-term financial impact.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- ~85% of copper exposure is hedged on MCX.
- Purchases and sales are linked to LME percentages.
- Commodity segment margins averaged ~3% (up from normal 2%) due to export opportunities to China; value-added product margins range from 4% to 10% (averaging 7%-7.5%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex target of Rs 40 crore over FY27 and FY28 is focused on scrap processing infrastructure and byproduct (plastic) value addition.
- Current capacity stands at 35,000 metric tonnes (with ~20,000 to 25,000 tonnes dedicated to value-added products). CTC wire capacity is 100-150 tons, operating at ~50 tons/month.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory levels elevated at Rs 303 crore as of September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Steep and sudden correction in global copper prices.
- Market freeze and temporary demand slowdown when copper prices drop.
- Uncertainty regarding the absorption of 1 million tonnes of US-locked inventory.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pratik Srivastava (on copper price correction and inventory):** Asked about the impact of falling copper prices and inventory (Rs 303 crore). Management stated 85% is hedged on MCX, Q3 top line will be impacted, but Q4 will see normalization.
2. **Dheeraj Thakur (on volume guidance cut and FY30 target):** Asked why volume growth was moderated to 7% and guidance cut. Management cited market freeze during price drops, lowered volume growth guidance to 10%, but reaffirmed the FY30 Rs 5,000 crore target.
3. **Darshil Jhaveri (on capex and EPR):** Asked about capex focus and EPR. Management clarified the Rs 40 crore capex targets scrap processing/byproducts, and EPR enforcement remains slow.
4. **Surya Narayan (on EBITDA per kg increase):** Asked why EBITDA per kg doubled. Management explained LME-linked pricing mechanics and higher commodity exports to China yielding 3% margins.
5. **Punit Mittal (on segment margins):** Asked for margin differentials between commodities and value-add. Management stated commodities yield 2%-3% while value-added products range from 4% to 10% (averaging 7%-7.5%).

### One-line takeaway
Tieramet delivered a record Q2 revenue of Rs 1,000 crore and completed its demerger, but management lowered volume growth guidance to 10% amid steep copper price corrections and expected Q3 headwinds.
