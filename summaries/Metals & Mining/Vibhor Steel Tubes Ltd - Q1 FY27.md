# Vibhor Steel Tubes Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong order books, successful diversification into new infrastructure products, and a 20% YoY revenue increase.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue growth:** 20% YoY.
*   **Sundargarh plant capacity utilization:** 18%.
*   **Jharsuguda pipe order book:** 1,800 tonnes.
*   **Transmission line tower (TLT) order book:** ~2,000 tonnes.
*   **Pole/High-mast order book:** 250 tonnes.
*   **Revenue mix:** 83% pipes, 12% crash barriers, 5% others.
*   **Jindal Steel revenue dependency:** 80-82%.

### Highlights
*   Jharsuguda plant is the primary growth driver, contributing significantly to the 20% revenue increase.
*   Diversification into new segments: Highway guardrails (crash barriers), transmission line towers (TLT), octagonal poles, and high-mast poles.
*   New galvanizing tank at Jharsuguda expected to be operational by September 1, 2026, to alleviate current capacity bottlenecks.
*   Management aims to reduce dependency on Jindal Steel to 70% over time, though they noted the current arrangement provides marketing efficiency and brand premium.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects 20-25% revenue growth for the full year.
*   Expects Q2 and Q3 to show increased contribution from new products (TLT, poles).
*   Targeting 300 tonnes/month for poles by October 2026.
*   Management stated the company is "on track" to achieve its targets.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Infrastructure push from the government is driving strong domestic demand despite the monsoon season.
*   High freight charges are causing steel manufacturers to focus on domestic sales.
*   Transmission line demand is high due to the national focus on electricity production.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Overall company margins are ~2%.
*   Margin hierarchy: Monopole (highest) > TLT > Pole > Crash barrier > Pipe (lowest).
*   TLT and pole segments have seen margins up to 10%.
*   Crash barrier margins are slightly above pipe margins (~3%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   New galvanizing tank at Jharsuguda expected operational by September 1, 2026.
*   Planning a new unit in North India to cater to regional demand; land purchase talks are underway.
*   A new subsidiary has been incorporated for the North India plant to improve operational visibility.
*   Management noted that capacity utilization is managed product-wise, with galvanizing tank capacity being the primary constraint.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   High revenue dependency on Jindal Steel (80-82%).
*   Galvanizing tank capacity constraints are limiting order fulfillment.
*   Pending certifications for certain products (e.g., Monopoles).
*   Monsoon-related delays in commissioning new equipment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why not take export orders?** Management stated they are prioritizing domestic demand and existing dealer MOUs, and are constrained by full galvanizing capacity.
*   **Q: What is the status of future products?** Management is working on RSJ poles (received a 500-tonne order) and is researching solar structures and railway bridges.
*   **Q: Why incorporate a new subsidiary?** Management explained it is to provide clear visibility on the turnover, depreciation, and performance of the planned North India plant.
*   **Q: Can you quantify the revenue impact of the new galvanizing tank?** Management estimated the additional 1,500 tonnes of galvanizing capacity could translate to Rs 100-150 crore in revenue.
*   **Q: Is the Jindal dependency a risk?** Management views it as a "win-win" due to the brand premium and lack of need for independent marketing, but intends to reduce it to 70% as other products scale.

### One-line takeaway
Vibhor Steel is leveraging strong domestic infrastructure demand to diversify its product mix beyond pipes, aiming for 20-25% annual revenue growth while managing capacity constraints.
