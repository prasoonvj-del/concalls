# Manaksia Coated Metals & Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-18 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights record EBITDA per ton, successful stabilization of the new Alu-Zinc line, and clear visibility on growth drivers from upcoming capacity additions.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 263 crore (+15% QoQ, +3.6% YoY).
*   **Price Realization:** Rs 88,597/ton (vs Rs 79,180/ton in Q4 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 29.08 crore (+86% QoQ).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 11.06% (+422 bps QoQ).
*   **EBITDA per ton:** Rs 10,400 (highest ever recorded).
*   **PBT:** Rs 18.93 crore (+197% QoQ).
*   **PAT:** Rs 14.10 crore (+163% QoQ).
*   **PAT Margin:** 5.36% (+301 bps QoQ).
*   **EPS:** Rs 1.31 (+102% QoQ).
*   **Order Book:** ~Rs 450 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Status:** Alu-Zinc line stabilized at 62% capacity utilization; Pre-Painted steel ran at 95.4% utilization.
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Management stated all new Q1 orders are priced to fully cover raw material/freight costs with a margin buffer.
*   **Market Expansion:** Entered 4 new international markets: Latvia, Brazil, Jamaica, and Somalia.
*   **Exports:** Contributed 65% of total volume (18,221 tons); export revenue grew 20% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Revenue:** Management targets ~Rs 1,300–1,350 crore (based on ~150,000 tons).
*   **FY28 Revenue:** Management targets ~Rs 1,700–1,750 crore (based on 180,000–200,000 tons).
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Management expects margins to be sustainable and potentially grow due to new capacity and solar power savings.
*   **Phase 2 Capex:** Planned outlay of Rs 350 crore for backward integration (cold rolling) and 2nd Alu-Zinc line.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains strong across infrastructure, building materials, home appliances, and automotive sectors.
*   Order book visibility is 4.5–5 months.
*   Management noted they are a small player with significant headroom to gain share from long-term customers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Fuel/freight costs normalized after Q4 spikes; management is moving to natural gas (GSPC pipeline) to derisk fuel supply.
*   Working capital cycle is currently ~75 days; management expects significant compression to single digits after the cold rolling mill is commissioned.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **2nd Color Coating Line:** Commissioning in Q2 FY27; increases Pre-Painted capacity from 86,000 to 236,000 tons/annum.
*   **7MW Solar Plant:** Commissioning in Q2 FY27; expected to offset 50–55% of grid power consumption at Kutch.
*   **Capex Spent:** Rs 140 crore deployed to date on Alu-Zinc upgrade, 2nd color coating line, and solar plant.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 115 crore.
*   **Debt-to-Equity:** ~1x; management expects peak leverage not to exceed 1.25x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Past disruption in fuel/freight due to Middle East conflict; currently mitigated by diversified sourcing.
*   **Operational:** Teething troubles in the new Alu-Zinc line (expected to resolve in ~3 months).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Is the record EBITDA/ton sustainable?** A: Management believes it is sustainable and has potential for further growth as capacity utilization increases and solar savings kick in.
*   **Q: Why did volumes decline YoY?** A: Management attributed this to the ramp-up phase of the new Alu-Zinc line, not a lack of demand.
*   **Q: How will the cold rolling mill impact working capital?** A: It will allow for fewer SKUs and in-house processing, drastically reducing inventory and compressing the cycle to single digits.
*   **Q: How will the new capex be funded?** A: A mix of internal accruals, debt, and equity; estimated additional debt requirement is ~Rs 100 crore.
*   **Q: Any dip in margins expected during the 2nd color coating line ramp-up?** A: Management does not foresee a dip as the order book is clear and the new line will consume captive Alu-Zinc production.

### One-line takeaway
Manaksia Coated Metals delivered a strong Q1 with record unit economics and expects a step-change in profitability in H2 FY27 driven by the commissioning of a new color coating line and a captive solar plant.
