# VMS TMT Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-24 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 16.16% YoY, but profitability was impacted by high raw material costs and forex volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 247.88 crore (vs Rs 213.39 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Revenue Growth:** 16.16% YoY.
*   **PAT:** Rs 2.45 crore.
*   **TMT Production:** 34,400 metric tons.

### Highlights
*   Commissioned in-house billet manufacturing facility (2.16 lakh tonnes capacity) to strengthen backward integration.
*   15 MW captive solar power plant under development; 12 MW operational as of August 7, 2026.
*   Proposed amalgamation with Aditya Ultra Steel Ltd approved; expected to expand market presence in Saurashtra and Kutch.
*   Distribution network: 3 distributors and 227 dealers in Gujarat.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets a top line of >Rs 2,000 crore in the future.
*   Expects 10-15% annual growth in dealer network and sales.
*   Solar plant expected to generate ~Rs 10 crore in annual savings after debt repayment.
*   Management expects margins to improve in Q3 and Q4.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strategy focuses on Tier 2, Tier 3, and rural markets; management avoids institutional/infrastructure segments due to high competition.
*   Demand remains supported by infrastructure and real estate activity in Gujarat.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins compressed due to rising imported scrap prices (attributed to Iran conflict) and forex volatility.
*   Scrap accounts for 50-60% of raw material requirements.
*   Interest costs reduced following debt repayment via IPO proceeds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   TMT capacity: 2 lakh metric tons; Billet capacity: 2.16 lakh metric tons.
*   Current TMT capacity utilization is ~80%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Company has largely paid off fixed debt.
*   Inventory increased to Rs 228.35 crore (as of FY26) due to product mix (higher-value 550D) and monsoon seasonality; management expects normalization post-Diwali.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions (Iran conflict) impacting raw material (scrap) prices.
*   Forex volatility affecting import costs.
*   Minor monsoon impact on sales in Q1/Q2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Inventory levels:** Management attributed high inventory to the addition of higher-value products (550D) and monsoon seasonality, expecting levels to drop below Rs 200 crore post-Diwali.
*   **Solar benefits:** Management confirmed ~Rs 4/unit savings, totaling ~Rs 10 crore annual EBITDA/profit impact.
*   **Amalgamation benefits:** Management expects the merger with Aditya Ultra Steel to expand market reach (Saurashtra/Kutch) and improve negotiation power with vendors.
*   **Scrap sourcing:** Management stated imported scrap is preferred over local due to quality consistency and bulk availability.
*   **Margin decline:** Management explained that while sales volume and price per ton increased, the cost of imported scrap rose faster, compressing margins.

### One-line takeaway
Management is focused on operational integration (billets, solar) and network expansion to drive long-term growth, despite near-term margin pressure from raw material volatility.
