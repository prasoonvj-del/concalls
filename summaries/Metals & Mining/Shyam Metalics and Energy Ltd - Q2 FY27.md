# Shyam Metalics and Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue, EBITDA, and PAT grew both YoY and sequentially, supported by strong volume growth and downstream scale-up.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 5,960 crore (up 33.4% YoY, 9.3% QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 857 crore (up 40.9% YoY, 5.5% QoQ); Margin: 14.4%
- **Operating EBITDA (excluding other income):** Rs 823 crore (up 52.6% YoY, 7.5% QoQ); Margin: 13.8% (up 170 bps YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 511 crore (up 96.2% YoY, 45.7% QoQ); Margin: 7.5%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 11,415 crore (up 28.4% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 1,670 crore (up 34.4% YoY); Margin: 14.6%
- **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA:** Rs 1,588 crore (up 41.9% YoY); Margin: 13.9%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 862 crore (up 56.4% YoY)
- **Sales Volume (Q2 FY27):** 15.49 lakh tonnes (up 26% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Volume:** 29.32 lakh tonnes (up 20% YoY)
- **Carbon Steel Flat Product Sales:** ~81,500 tonnes (more than doubled YoY)
- **Stainless Steel Volume:** ~29,850 tonnes (up 20.7% YoY); Realizations up 35.2% YoY
- **Specialty Alloy Volume Growth:** 4.7% YoY; Realizations up 18.4% YoY
- **Aluminum Volume Growth:** 3% YoY; Realizations up 27.7% YoY
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 1,544 crore
- **Capex Incurred (Q2):** Rs 716 crore; (H1): Rs 1,291 crore
- **Total Capex Since Listing:** Rs 9,921 crore (~53% of total planned Rs 18,785 crore); Rs 6,671 crore capitalized
- **Balance Planned Capex:** ~Rs 8,864 crore (to be deployed over next 2 to 3 years)
- **Working Capital:** Negative 16 days as of September 2026
- **Net Debt:** Modest at Rs 192 crore as of September 2026
- **RoE:** 14%; **RoCE:** 20% (as of September 2026, excluding CWIP of ~Rs 3,000 crore)

### Highlights
- Changed depreciation methodology from WDV to straight-line method in Q2 to align with industry practice.
- Commissioned 18,000 tonnes per annum aluminum foil facility at Sambalpur in July, taking total foil capacity to ~42,000 tonnes per annum.
- Commissioned 1.5 million tonnes beneficiation plant at Sambalpur for Rs 150 crore.
- NCLT approved amalgamation of Shree Venkateshwara Electro steel cast into Shyam Sel Limited, providing tax benefits of over Rs 15 crore through carryforward losses.
- Proposed acquiring 51% controlling stake in Alom Solar Private Limited (bidder for Phutamura coal block in Chhattisgarh with estimated reserves of 170 million tonnes; estimated capex Rs 360 crore, company share ~Rs 185 crore).
- Commissioned ~9 megawatt solar plant in Jamuria.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets RoE of 16% and RoCE of 24% by FY31.
- Stainless steel business expected to contribute ~Rs 1,100 crore EBITDA and SBQ business ~Rs 1,000 crore EBITDA by FY31.
- Alom Solar / Phutamura coal block expected to generate incremental EBITDA of close to Rs 400 crore by FY31, with production expected by July 2027.
- Overall business EBITDA margin targeted to increase from ~11.5%-12% to 14.5%-16%.
- Maharashtra 9 million tonnes crude steel project (MOU signed) planned in phases (first phase 4.5 to 5 million tonnes) over 8 to 10 years, post-2029/30.

### Demand & industry commentary
- World Steel Association October outlook projects flat global steel demand in 2026 and 1.7% growth in 2027; India demand expected to grow 7.9% in 2026 and 8% in 2027.
- Aluminum foil demand supported by Middle East disruptions, plant closures, and growing domestic/export demand.
- Huge shortage of HR currently in the country.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Stainless steel EBITDA expected around Rs 13,000 to 15,000 per tonne.
- Carbon steel EBITDA averages around Rs 6,000 to 7,000 per tonne.
- Aluminum EBITDA around Rs 40,000 per tonne.
- Mittal Corp EBITDA per tonne is around Rs 10,000.
- Raw material security enhanced via backward integration, beneficiation, and captive coal block acquisition.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continuing Vision 2031 expansion: 1.6 million tonnes HR mill, Rs 900 crore 0.8 million tonnes specialty bar combo mill auto steel project at Kharagpur.
- Stainless steel expansion of Rs 2,700 crore in Odisha (including SBQ plant) targeted for completion by March 2029.
- Aluminum downstream program includes planned 60,000 tonnes per annum FRP facility.
- Maharashtra mega project MOU signed for 9 million tonnes capacity (initial phase 4.5-5 million tonnes; capex outflow not immediate).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Expansion funded primarily through internal accruals.
- Working capital at negative 16 days as of September 2026.
- Net debt at Rs 192 crore.
- CWIP stands at approximately Rs 3,000 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as material risks by management, other than normal raw material price volatility (mitigated via captive integration).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs):** Asked about volume drivers and the Maharashtra MOU contours. *Management answered that value-added share will rise to 70-80% over 2 years, and the Maharashtra project is a long-term (post-2029/30) 8-10 year plan in phases starting with 4.5-5 MTPA, requiring no immediate major financial outflow.*
- **Satyadeep Jain (Ambit Capital):** Questioned the shift back to a cyclical sector (Maharashtra project) given the strategy to move into value-added/less cyclical sectors. *Management clarified it will be 100% under Shyam Metalics, integrated with flat products and local mining, alongside ongoing value-added expansions.*
- **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities):** Inquired about capital allocation and whether West Bengal capacity would stall due to Maharashtra. *Management stated West Bengal expansion will proceed after stabilization, and Maharashtra is at a very initial stage with no immediate debt intent, maintaining a strong balance sheet cushion.*
- **Rajesh Majumdar (360 ONE Capital):** Asked about Mittal Corp performance and rising competition in coated steel. *Management replied Mittal Corp capacity utilization is ~85% with RoCE >20-25% and EBITDA of ~Rs 10,000/tonne; for coated steel, they noted market growth and backward integration insulate them from competition.*
- **Tanuj Nangalia (SKP):** Asked why raw material cost fell as a % of revenue. *Management stated raw material prices rose along with market prices through a pass-through mechanism.*

### One-line takeaway
Shyam Metalics delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with revenue up 33.4% YoY and PAT up 96.2% YoY, driven by volume growth and downstream scaling, while outlining long-term multi-metal expansion plans under Vision 2031.
