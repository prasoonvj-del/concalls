# Hariom Pipe Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a recovery in operations, volume growth, and a strong second-quarter performance following the resumption of the Perundurai plant.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 554 crore (up 65% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 68 crore (up 58% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 29 crore (up from Rs 10 crore YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 12.21% (up from 11.63% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue (Standalone):** Rs 983 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA (Standalone):** Rs 118 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT (Standalone):** Rs 46 crore (up 35% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 984 crore (up 24% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 118 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT:** Rs 42 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Sales Volume:** 83,117 tonnes (up 32% QoQ)
- **H1 FY27 Sales Volume:** ~1.46 lakh tonnes (up 6% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Average Selling Price:** Rs 67,265 per tonne (up 17% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 8,042 (up 11% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 8,140 (up 15% YoY)
- **Standalone Net Worth (as of Sept 30):** Rs 705 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.62x (improved from 0.65x YoY)
- **Solar Operational Capacity:** 15 MW AC across 3 locations (subsequent to H1; total portfolio 60 MW across 13 locations in Maharashtra)

### Highlights
- Perundurai plant resumed operations on July 13 after a temporary first-quarter closure (110 days); issues resolved.
- First 5 MW AC solar project commissioned in July; two additional 5 MW AC projects commissioned in October. Target is 52 MW across 11 locations by January 2027.
- Metal Mart (trading/distribution subsidiary) completed documentation and obtained GST registration; preparing for fuller operations.
- Promoters issued warrants at Rs 333, and initial 25% margin money has been received.
- Plant-wise Q2 sales volumes: Mahabubnagar ISP unit ~29,000 tonnes, Mahabubnagar galvanized unit ~29,600 tonnes, Perundurai plant ~23,400 tonnes, Anantapur sponge unit ~1,000 tonnes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a minimum 10% to 15% growth for the second half of FY27 and similar growth for FY28.
- Management targets a long-term vision of becoming a Rs 10,000 crore top-line company in 5 to 6 years.
- EBITDA margin expected to be consistently maintained above 12%. With future backward integration (HR coil), EBITDA could potentially increase to 13%-15%.
- Volume growth target is 25% to 30% annually, subject to market conditions and potential acquisitions.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Focus remains on value-added products, OEMs (contributing ~20% of overall volumes), and expanding geographical presence and dealer networks.
- Dealer network accounts for ~80% of sales, providing secure collections and spread risk.
- Steel is a commodity, so management prioritizes volume growth and sustainable margins.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 power and fuel expenses rose to ~Rs 25 crore (from Rs 18 crore in Q1) due to the restart of Perundurai operations, optimum capacity utilization at Mahbubnagar, and higher power tariffs/charges. Power consumption per tonne dropped from 135 units in Q1 to 98 units in Q2.
- A 25-year PPA MOU was signed for Tamil Nadu operations to source ~4 MW DC solar power to reduce high power costs in the state.
- Finished goods prices generally move in tandem with raw material price increases, protecting margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Peak revenue potential from existing operational finished goods capacity (521,000 metric tonnes) is estimated at Rs 2,500 crore to Rs 2,800 crore.
- A non-binding MOU signed with the Andhra Pradesh government for an integrated steel plant is intended to secure faster approvals and licenses (pollution, power, water, etc.) within existing land facilities. Implementation will take the next 3 to 4 years and include future HR coil manufacturing for backward integration.
- Solar subsidiary targeting 52 MW installation by January 2027, expected to generate Rs 28-30 crore top-line and Rs 3-4 crore PAT after debt and depreciation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Internal accruals are being used to reduce debt year-on-year.
- Minimum inventory of 800+ SKUs is maintained to service dealer orders in single lots.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Land scarcity at 2 locations for the solar project in Maharashtra (industrial-built areas instead of agricultural); if unresolved, implementation will be restricted to 52 MW.
- Delays or friction with state government approvals (e.g., Maharashtra MOU pending for over 1.5 years).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Adi Jha asked about specific H2 and FY28 guidance numbers.** Management (Rupesh Kumar Gupta) stated a minimum 10% to 15% growth for H2 and next year, while CFO Amitabha Bhattacharya noted precise percentage predictions are difficult due to natural/organic market conditions.
2. **Dharmen Parikh asked about CapEx funding and margin impact for the steel plant.** Management replied that backward integration into HR coils will take 3-4 years and could potentially elevate EBITDA margins from 12% to 13%-15%.
3. **Dakshita Mishra asked about drivers for higher EBITDA per tonne (Rs 8,140 in Q2).** Management cited reduced fixed costs at Perundurai post-restart, higher margins from integrated steel products compared to HR coil-based products, and improved operational efficiency.
4. **Dhruvin Doshi questioned the rationale for venturing into the solar power business.** Management (Rupesh Kumar Gupta) explained it was initially a test case under the PM-KUSUM scheme to understand Maharashtra government procedures and public behavior before larger expansions, aligning with future green steel/motivation goals.
5. **Adi Jha questioned the solar diversification as a shareholder.** Management (Rupesh Kumar Gupta) acknowledged it wasn't their core cup of tea initially but defended it as a small test investment with central government subsidies and no major core capex risk.

### One-line takeaway
Hariom Pipe Industries delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with a 65% YoY revenue jump and margin expansion following the successful restart of its Perundurai unit, while maintaining a long-term vision of reaching a Rs 10,000 crore top-line in 5 to 6 years through organic growth and strategic expansions.
