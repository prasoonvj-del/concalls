# Vibhor Steel Tubes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by momentum in the Odisha plant and higher-margin infrastructure products.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained strong growth outlook, expecting production from the new North region unit in Q1 of next year (6 to 8 months).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from monsoon-related constraints to a more aggressive second half, noting that Q1 was unusually progressive and robust.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Added applications for railway (RDSO) and telecom towers; Power Grid approval for transmission line towers is near-final and expected very soon.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: The new galvanizing tank in Odisha is operational, and subsidiary Vibhor Infra in Hisar is progressing towards production in Q1 FY28.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth YoY: 27%
- EBITDA growth YoY: 54% (Management also stated 34.72% year-on-year)
- Revenue bifurcation: Pipes 81%, highway guardrails 12%, towers, poles and others 6%-7%
- Sundargarh pipe capacity utilization: 1,500 to 2,000 tonnes
- Highway guardrail installed capacity: 1,500 tonnes (hitting around 1,000 tonnes)

### Highlights
- Maharashtra and Hyderabad manufacturing units and galvanizing tanks are running at maximum capacity.
- Odisha (Sundargarh) plant is gaining operational momentum, with both galvanizing tanks now operational.
- Incorporated subsidiary unit Vibhor Infra in Hisar, Haryana, with production expected to start in Q1 of next year.
- Received CPRI Bangalore certification for monopoles (132 rating), with plans to apply for 220 and 400 ratings.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the second half of the year to remain very promising due to contractor and EPC deadlines.
- Production from the new North region (Haryana) unit is expected to start around Q1 of next year.
- Target to reach 2,500 tonnes in crash barriers this year, scaling to 3,500 to 4,000 tonnes once Haryana is fully utilized.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Steel prices and zinc prices have increased due to geopolitical reasons and steel manufacturer breakdowns, but higher prices have been easily absorbed by strong domestic demand.
- Transmission line tower demand is robust nationwide, particularly in Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, and Rajasthan, supported by solar and power transmission rollouts.
- Road projects are expected to accelerate ahead of 2029 elections, aiding highway guardrail demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher inventory levels and contribution from higher-margin products supported profitability.
- Margin hierarchy: Highway guardrails are at least 1% higher than pipes; transmission line towers are 2% to 3% higher.
- Balanced inventory management is being maintained to protect EBITDA against potential downward steel price fluctuations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Haryana subsidiary (Vibhor Infra) is being established to capture North Indian demand for highway guardrails, poles, and transmission line towers.
- Current focus is on maximizing the capacity utilization of existing installed capacity in Odisha rather than adding new total capacity immediately.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory levels are kept in check with sales to avoid over-leveraging.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical conflicts and shipping cost increases have driven up raw material (steel and zinc) prices.
- Certain key regulatory approvals (such as Power Grid and telecom) are still pending, though expected soon.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Are there any further products in the pipeline to be launched soon?** Management stated they have applied for railway (RDSO) products and telecom towers, expecting telecom approvals first.
- **Q: What are the reasons for the 50% EBITDA growth and 90%-92% PAT growth?** Management cited inventory growth and the initial contribution from higher-margin products, alongside disciplined inventory balancing.
- **Q: What is the capacity utilization of the Sundargarh plant as of H1 FY27?** Management explained utilization is measured via galvanizing tanks; one tank is at full capacity (~2,000 tonnes) and the second is awaiting telecom and transmission line approvals.
- **Q: How much of total revenue comes from Jindal Steel, and what is the dependence?** Management noted comfort in the long-standing relationship (since 2003) with an agreement lasting another 4-5 years, while their primary growth focus remains on new infrastructure products.
- **Q: Will business be impacted due to upcoming state elections in 2027?** Management stated elections do not impact their product lines because applications span diverse sectors (agriculture, railways, power transmission) with fixed statutory deadlines.

### One-line takeaway
Vibhor Steel is driving robust revenue and EBITDA growth through strong domestic demand, rising steel prices, and a strategic scaling of higher-margin value-added infrastructure products in Odisha and upcoming northern facilities.
