# Midwest Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 growth (24% revenue, 18% PAT), quartz segment turned EBIT positive, and strategic progress in granite mining despite working capital headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Quartz revenue guidance for FY27 adjusted to ~Rs 90 crore (from previous Rs 100-120 crore).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "teething issues" to "stabilization" in quartz; demand remains robust.
- **New topics/risks:** New: Secured 3 granite mines from APMDC. Old: KMML rare earth project remains stalled (waiting for administrative team).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Quartz Phase 1 stabilization achieved (16,000 tonnes in Q2); APMDC granite expansion initiated.

### Key numbers
- **H1 Revenue growth:** 24% YoY.
- **H1 PAT growth:** 18% YoY.
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 182 crore (approx).
- **Q2 Quartz volume:** 16,000 tonnes.
- **Q2 Quartz revenue:** Rs 13.5 crore.
- **Leverage (Gearing ratio):** 0.2 times.
- **FY27 Quartz revenue target:** ~Rs 90 crore.

### Highlights
- Quartz business achieved EBIT profitability in Q2.
- Secured LOIs from APMDC for 3 new granite mines (1 Black Granite, 2 in Chittur, 2 in Srikakulam).
- Deployed 10 electric trucks (annual savings of Rs 2-2.5 crore); group captive renewable energy arrangement saving Rs 2/unit.
- Rare earth progress: Indonesia (definitive agreement expected this quarter), Sri Lanka (permissions expected this quarter), KMML (stalled due to lack of administrative team).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Quartz volume targets:** Q3 (25,000-30,000 tonnes), Q4 (35,000-40,000 tonnes).
- **Granite volume growth:** 10-12% expected for remainder of FY27.
- **Capex:** Rs 300 crore planned over 3-4 years (includes Rs 120 crore for quartz Phase 2).
- **Phase 2 Quartz:** Commissioning by Q2 next year (regular plant), HPQ by Q3/Q4 next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Quartz:** Demand remains strong in both domestic and export markets; US tariff risk currently not impacting shipments.
- **Granite:** Demand stable; 30-35% YoY increase in fuel costs fully passed on to customers via price hikes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Fuel costs:** 30-35% YoY increase impacted margins by ~6% (fully offset by price increases).
- **Quartz margins:** Expected to exceed granite margins once at full capacity.
- **Energy savings:** Rs 4-5 crore/annum expected from current EV and solar initiatives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Quartz Phase 2:** Building construction started, machinery orders placed.
- **Rare Earths:** Sri Lanka is the primary near-term revenue source; Indonesia project to follow in 2-3 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Working capital:** Increased due to extended credit periods for domestic customers and higher inventory (quartz/granite).
- **Leverage:** Maintained at 0.2 times.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **KMML project:** Stalled due to lack of administrative team in Kerala government.
- **Working capital:** Higher receivables due to domestic market push.
- **Quartz:** Underutilization in early H1 (now resolving).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Alok Deora (Quartz volume/margins):** Management confirmed 16k tonnes in Q2, targeting 25k-30k in Q3.
- **Sanj Arya (CFO negative):** Explained by extended credit to domestic customers and inventory build-up.
- **Sanj Arya (Rare Earths):** Management clarified KMML is stalled, others on track.
- **Chirag Gandhi (Quartz guidance):** Management lowered FY27 expectation to ~Rs 90 crore.
- **Shreya (Working Capital):** Management attributed spike to new JV and domestic credit push; promised improvement in coming quarters.

### One-line takeaway
Midwest Ltd achieved quartz EBIT profitability and secured new granite mines, while navigating higher working capital and stalled rare earth projects in Kerala.
