# Deccan Gold Mines Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operational milestones achieved with Swarnagiri and Kyrgyzstan projects, alongside validation of profitability and execution capabilities.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained production targets broadly, but management noted winter operations in Kyrgyzstan need 15-20 days to fully assess before giving precise output numbers.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted to greater confidence in execution following successful commissioning in Kyrgyzstan and strong profit contributions from Swarnagiri.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Detailed discussion on Kyrgyzstan refining process (sending test doré to the national refinery) and the Supreme Court batch hearings involving Ganajur/Indocil. Tanzania project was not emphasized.
- Last quarter's promises: Rs 137 crore fundraise was approved by BSE recently; drilling in Finland/Mozambique was delayed due to the wait for these BSE approvals and funds.

### Key numbers
- **Swarnagiri Gold Production:** 135 kg (up from 89 kg in Q1 FY27).
- **Swarnagiri Bullion Sold (H1 FY27):** 109 kg (including 50.4 kg refined bullion sold in Q2).
- **Deccan Gold Share of Profit (H1 FY27):** Rs 20.57 crore (Standalone PAT for H1 at ~Rs 20 crore; Q2 standalone PAT at Rs 13.91 crore).
- **Consolidated Net Loss (H1 FY27):** Rs 9.28 crore.
- **Fundraise:** Rs 137 crore approved by BSE (in the process of collecting funds).
- **Swarnagiri Inventory:** 133 kg of doré plus bullion; valued at Rs 134 crore (at production cost).
- **Kyrgyzstan Test Consignment:** 1.2 kg of doré dispatched.
- **Bhalukona Resource Estimate:** ~4 million tonnes of economic ore at 0.24% nickel equivalent (15 drill holes completed).
- **Spain Drilling:** 5,400 meters completed at Logrosán.

### Highlights
- Swarnagiri (formerly Jonnagiri) name changed and project demonstrated ~50% PAT or ~70% EBITDA margins.
- Commissioned the Altyn-Tor gold project in Kyrgyzstan on September 12, 2026.
- Supreme Court heard connected matters on November 17, 2026, regarding Ganajur/Indocil; next hearing posted for December 1, 2026.
- Delayed drilling in Finland and Mozambique due to waiting for BSE approval on the Rs 137 crore fundraise.

### Guidance & outlook
- Swarnagiri targeting ~450 to 500+ kg production for FY27, scaling toward 1,000 kg per annum with new approvals, and up to 2 tonnes long-term.
- Altyn-Tor targeting 180 kg in current financial year, ramping up to 300 kg next year, and 600-800+ kg post underground integration.
- Management expects consolidated losses to narrow as projects move toward commercial production.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Critical minerals demand driven by EVs, energy storage, and defense (tungsten, nickel, lithium, graphite).
- India shifting from a net exporter to a net importer of graphite, increasing local strategic importance.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Swarnagiri operating at ~70% EBITDA margins and ~50% PAT margins; production cost estimated at ~Rs 4,000 per gram of gold.
- Altyn-Tor PAT margins expected to stabilize around 35% to 40%.
- Kyrgyzstan royalty is 17% revenue share to Kyrgyzaltyn, with 83% to Avelum Partners. Finland royalty is 2.5% (corporate tax ~20%), and Spain has 0% royalty on tungsten (corporate tax ~25%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Swarnagiri running at ~40 kg/month (~135 kg/quarter); aiming for a capacity of 1,000 kg/annum and eventually 2 tonnes/annum.
- Bhalukona evaluated for a 3,000 TPD processing plant; Spain (Logrosán) and Finland (Kalevala/Pahkalampi) exploring 800 TPD processing plants.
- Mozambique targeting small-scale hard-soil operations and commercial production by 2028.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Rs 137 crore fundraise via CCDs, equity shares, and warrants near closure.
- Swarnagiri inventory valued at Rs 134 crore based on production cost across ROM, in-circuit, doré, and bullion.
- Deccan Gold has lent ~Rs 276 crore to Kyrgyzstan at 15% interest.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Winter conditions in Kyrgyzstan pose operational challenges for the newly commissioned plant during the initial 15-20 days trial.
- Ganajur project remains contingent on the outcome of the Supreme Court batch hearings.
- Consolidated results continue to show losses (Rs 9.28 crore in H1 FY27) due to pre-commercial capital expenditure in subsidiaries.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ashit Kothi asked how cash flows/funding will work given Jonnagiri dividends are pending.** Management replied exploration is funded via the Rs 137 crore raise, and future critical mineral capex may use debt, equity, or downstream offtake arrangements.
- **Jaiprakash Dargad questioned the consolidated losses despite standalone profits.** Management explained consolidated financials absorb pre-commercial capex and losses from tier-2 subsidiaries and associate operations, though losses are narrowing.
- **Viraj Mahadevia asked about lifetime revenues and timelines for Bhalukona, Finland, Spain, and Mozambique.** Management stated full production is likely around 2029-2030, though smaller-scale pilot operations could yield earlier revenues by 2028 depending on beneficiation test results.
- **Srigopal Bajaj asked why Swarnagiri inventory is held back rather than sold.** Management explained inventory was held to capture higher pricing during the Indian festive season, with significant sales executed in October-November.
- **Hitesh Gupta asked about Ganajur being tied to the Indocil case.** Management clarified they are interveners, but engaged a senior Supreme Court counsel to argue their case stands stronger on merits (having progressed to ML application) separate from Indocil.
- **Sagar Parekh asked about guidance revisions given falling gold prices.** Management noted that while gold prices may eat into profits slightly, Swarnagiri's low production cost (~Rs 4,000/gram) keeps EBITDA margins resilient at 65-67%.

### One-line takeaway
Deccan Gold validated its gold production profitability through Swarnagiri and commissioned its Kyrgyzstan project, while actively pursuing critical mineral exploration and awaiting a Supreme Court hearing for Ganajur.
