### Highlights
- Order book surged 30% QoQ to INR 2,200 cr, reflecting improved oil & gas capex after 2-3 years stagnation.  
- Seamless volume guidance of 430,000 tons FY27 maintained; H1 achieved ~200,000 tons.  
- Inventory valuation impact (Ind AS) depressed Q2 EBITDA/ton; management says notional, will reverse in Q3 as inventory converts to sales.  
- Demerger scheme submitted to exchanges; three entities split by size range/product: Nagothane (0.5-20 inch, oil & gas), Mangaon (0.5-6 inch, cold-drawn/drill pipe), Telangana (up to 14 inch, cylinder pipes); approval expected Jan/Feb 2027.  
- Cold-drawn pipe project completed; Telangana finishing line under implementation, to be activated in FY27; hot mill upgrade (INR 350 cr capex, INR 1,000 cr turnover) to follow.  
- Management attributes weak reported PAT (record: -42% YoY) to notional inventory valuation under Ind AS, expects reversal in Q3.  

### Guidance & outlook
- Seamless volume target 430,000 tons FY27 maintained.  
- Order pipeline strong with uptick in upstream and export segments.  
- Focus on executing current order book to reduce inventory and debtors.  
- Demerger approval expected Jan/Feb 2027.  
- Telangana finishing line commissioning in FY27.  
- Hot mill upgrade (INR 350 cr) after Telangana line completion.  
- Anti-dumping duty reimplementation decision expected Jan 2027.  
- Samudra Manthan scheme impact uncertain and long-dated; ground activity needed before orders.  

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian oil & gas sector expenditure improving after 2-3 years stagnation; tenders and awards picking up.  
- Export demand driven by North American rig count recovery.  
- Competition from imports persists; awaiting anti-dumping duty reimplementation.  
- No seamless pipe opportunity seen in US data center captive power plants (pipes used for drilling, not transportation).  
- Cross-country pipeline orders will come as tenders are released.  

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 EBITDA/ton: seamless INR 9,220, ERW INR 1,830 (down from Q1 seamless INR 15,660).  
- Decline attributed to notional inventory valuation loss under Ind AS due to higher inventory levels amid rising steel prices (billets up rapidly in Q1, less in Q2).  
- Other expenses flat QoQ (INR 238 cr vs INR 236 cr) despite ~7,000 tons higher seamless and ~9,000 tons higher ERW dispatches.  
- Margins expected to improve in Q3 as inventory converts to sales.  

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Cold-drawn pipe project completed.  
- Telangana finishing line under implementation, to be activated in FY27; no orders yet for expanded capacity.  
- Hot mill upgrade planned (INR 350 cr capex, INR 1,000 cr estimated annual turnover) after Telangana line completion.  
- Total seamless capacity 430,000 tons/year guidance.  

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and treasury portfolio increasing YoY; surplus invested in bonds, mutual funds, ETFs (gold, silver, Nifty Smallcap, Nifty 500).  
- Inventory and debtors elevated as of Sep 30; focus on order execution to reduce.  
- No debt mentioned; net cash position implied but not quantified.  

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Notional inventory valuation volatility in rapidly changing steel price environments.  
- Anti-dumping duty decision pending (Jan 2027).  
- Demerger approval pending (stock exchanges, then NCLT).  
- Treasury portfolio exposed to equity market volatility (notional MTM losses in other income).  
- Samudra Manthan scheme impact uncertain and long-dated.  
- Management defensive on shareholder returns and capital allocation; no buyback commitment.

### One-line takeaway
Strong order book recovery and volume trajectory offset by temporary notional inventory hit; demerger and capacity expansion on track but capital allocation clarity lacking.
