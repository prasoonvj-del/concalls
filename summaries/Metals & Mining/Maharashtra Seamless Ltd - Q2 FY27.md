# Maharashtra Seamless Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust order book growth and noted that margin pressure is entirely due to notional inventory valuation adjustments, expecting reversals in subsequent quarters.

### Key numbers
- Order book: Rs 2,200 crores (up over 30% QoQ from Rs 1,700 crores; up to Rs 2,203 crores per investor deck).
- Seamless pipes dispatches (H1 FY27): ~200,000 tonnes.
- Seamless EBITDA per tonne: Rs 9,220.
- ERW EBITDA per tonne: Rs 1,830.
- Net cash position: Rs 3,701 crores (from investor deck).
- Net profit: Rs 73 crores (-42% YoY, per record).

### Highlights
- Order book increased by over 30% QoQ to Rs 2,200 crores, driven by upstream oil & gas and export segments.
- Decline in EBITDA per tonne was attributed by management to notional inventory write-downs under Ind AS due to procurement prices exceeding quarter-end inventory prices; management expects these to reverse once inventory is converted and dispatched.
- *Comparison with Record:* The call confirms the Rs 2,203 crore order book and Rs 3,701 crore net cash position reported in the investor deck on 2026-11-13, and clarifies the weak net profit (-42% YoY) as a notional inventory accounting impact rather than operational underperformance.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains seamless pipe dispatch guidance of around 430,000 tonnes for FY27 (having achieved ~200,000 tonnes in H1).
- Expects better margins in Q3 FY27 as current inventory is converted and dispatched and inventory valuation adjustments reverse.
- Management expects tendering activity and rig counts in India to improve, supported by recent developments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Uptick observed in the upstream oil & gas segment and export markets (particularly North American and Canadian rig counts).
- Execution timeline for the order book typically ranges between 2 to 4 months.
- Anti-dumping duty reimplementation decision for imports is expected around January 2027.
- Management stated that data centers do not generate demand for seamless pipes (which are used for drilling rather than high-temperature/high-pressure transport in data centers).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel billet prices increased rapidly in Q1, while increases slowed in Q2.
- Other expenses remained stable QoQ (Q1 FY27 at Rs 238 crores vs Q2 FY27 at Rs 236 crores) despite higher dispatch volumes.
- Treasury other income declined due to notional losses from equity and gold/silver ETF exposures following market declines since June 30th.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Telangana finishing line is under implementation and will be activated within FY27; new orders for this capacity will only be taken after full commissioning.
- Hot mill upgrade (Rs 350 crore capex allocation) timeline will be decided after the Telangana finishing line is completed.
- Proposed demerger will split seamless pipe operations into three companies based on size ranges and specializations: Nagothane (0.5 to 20 inch; oil & gas, premium connections), Mangaon (0.5 to 6 inch; cold-drawn and drill pipes), and Telangana (up to 14 inch; cylinder pipes). Approval from stock exchanges is expected by January or February 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company continues to hold substantial surplus funds and cash in its treasury (invested in bonds, mutual funds, gold/silver ETFs, liquid funds, and equity ETFs).
- Working capital elevated due to higher inventory and debtors as of September 30, 2026; management's focus is to execute orders rapidly to reduce these levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inventory valuation knock-down effect due to procurement prices being higher than quarter-end inventory prices.
- Exposure of treasury portfolio to financial market volatility (equity and commodity ETFs).
- Import competition remains a risk pending the anti-dumping duty review in January 2027.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohammed Farooq Umar (Pearl Capital) on shareholder returns and buybacks:** Management stated that the company generates cash and remains on the lookout for opportunities, but performance is tied to the oil and gas sector cycle, which has only recently started recovering.
2. **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Co) on EBITDA per tonne and inventory impact:** Management explained that lower EBITDA per tonne was entirely due to notional Ind AS inventory valuation adjustments, which will reverse in Q3 upon dispatch.
3. **Saket Kapoor on the demerger and registered office shift:** Management stated the registered office moved to Gurgaon as decision-making happens there, and the three-way demerger is structured around distinct plant size ranges, geographies, and product specializations.
4. **Kirtan Mehta (Baroda BNP Paribas) on order book execution and pipeline:** Management stated the execution timeline is 2 to 4 months for both seamless and ERW, and current focus is on executing existing orders to reduce inventory and debtors.
5. **Vijay Kumar (Trustline India) on U.S. data center opportunities:** Management clarified that seamless pipes are used for drilling, not transportation, so data center captive power plants do not create meaningful demand for their seamless pipes.
6. **Ankur Sanwaria (Individual Investor) on DGTR anti-dumping recommendation ($268/tonne):** Management declined to comment as the matter is sub judice with authorities.

### One-line takeaway
Maharashtra Seamless reported a muted Q2 FY27 net profit due to notional inventory valuation adjustments, but management expects a sharp margin reversal in Q3 backed by a robust Rs 2,200+ crore order book and strong net cash of Rs 3,701 crores.
