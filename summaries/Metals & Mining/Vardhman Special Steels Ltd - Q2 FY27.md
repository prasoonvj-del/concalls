# Vardhman Special Steels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong demand, operational efficiencies from recent capex, and record sales volumes resulting in higher profitability.

### Key numbers
- **Sales Volume:** Over 70,000 tonnes (compared to 59,000 tonnes in Q1 FY27).
- **Revenue Growth YoY:** 39%
- **Total Sales Growth YoY:** 27%
- **EBITDA and PAT Growth YoY:** Up by about 50% for the quarter.
- **FY27 Sales Volume Forecast:** Expected to be about 260,000 tonnes (at the higher end of initial forecast).
- **Borrowings:** Rs 162.93 crore as of 30th September 2026 (increased due to Ind AS reclassification of creditors against LC discounting).
- **Trade Receivables:** Rs 410 crore as of 30th September 2026 (up from Rs 277 crore in Q4).
- **Solar Plant Expansion:** Additional 22.5 megawatts (20 MW for the current plant, 2.5 MW for the NDT line).
- **Direct and Indirect Exports:** 6% to 7% direct and 6% to 7% indirect through ASC.

### Highlights
- Foundation stone for a new forging plant was laid by the Chief Minister of Punjab, Mr. Bhagwant Mann, in late July 2026.
- Received approval from a European OE; mass manufacturing has started, with sales starting in January.
- Successful approval and start of mass manufacturing for a major import substitution for a domestic major.
- New NDT line to be commissioned by December; new peeling line to be commissioned by early November.
- Shifted to SAP S/4 HANA on 1st July 2025, using batch-wise costing without month-end/quarter-end inventory revaluation.
- Government GST benefit under 7-year sanction ended in August 2026; remaining electricity duty benefit continues for 10 years up to August 2029.

### Guidance & outlook
- **EBITDA per tonne Guidance:** Maintained between Rs 9,000 to Rs 12,000 per tonne (management noted current quarter is at the higher end and not to be treated as standard).
- **FY27 Sales Volume Guidance:** Revised to about 260,000 tonnes.
- **New Plant Timelines:** Environmental approval filing expected by December 2026; project completion targeted by September 2029 and stabilization by March 2030.
- **Forging Plant Timeline:** Expected to come up by March 2028.
- **Financing/Capital Structure Outlook:** Management is evaluating a 1:1 debt-equity ratio (diluting a bit more equity via rights issue in the second half of next calendar year and increasing debt component), and examining suspending dividends for 3–4 years.
- **Project Costs:** Management stated project costs for the new steel plant will be higher than the INR 2,000 crore envisaged due to land, equipment, rupee depreciation after the Iran war, higher sea freight, and commodities; scope being increased to 700,000 tonnes in the second phase.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand was strong, partly due to GST cuts last year on 2-wheelers, smaller vehicles, and good SUV sales.
- Non-automotive space demand has increased, impacting competitors and tightening material supply.
- Unscheduled shutdowns from a large company led to slight market shortages and pricing pull.
- Tractors are showing signs of slowing down, while passenger vehicles and two-wheelers remain solid.
- Tailwinds of green steel are visible with customers inquiring, though no price benefit exists yet, it aids share of business.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operational efficiencies and cost savings derived from past capex (Kocks Block and reheating furnace) and the power plant aided margins.
- Realizations improved due to demand-supply imbalances and contractual price resets (other companies sent price increase letters ranging from Rs 3,500 to Rs 4,000 a tonne).
- Yields improved due to transitioning from a 4.2-meter billet to a 5.2-meter billet with the new reheating furnace and Kocks Block.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Brownfield Capacity Expansion:** Applied to increase capacity from 300,000 tonnes to 360,000 tonnes; awaiting final environmental clearance (EC recommendation received). Estimated capex for this expansion is Rs 100 crore to Rs 115 crore.
- **Existing Plant Capex:** Budgeted at about Rs 150 crore for the next 3 years.
- **Forging Plant:** Phase 1 will absorb about 15,000 tonnes of steel annually at full capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings increased to Rs 162.93 crore as of 30th September 2026 (from Rs 92 crore at Q4 end) due to Ind AS reclassification of LC discounting creditors into borrowings.
- Trade receivables increased due to incremental sales in August and September 2026 (sales volume increased from 59,000 tonnes in Q1 to over 70,000 tonnes in Q2); credit days increased by 4 to 5 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Project implementation risk is the primary risk flagged by management.
- Commodity price inflation, equipment cost inflation, currency depreciation, and higher sea freight are impacting project costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Disha (Sapphire Capital) on EBITDA per tonne and inventory gains:** Management clarified that inventory gains exist, but reiterated that guidance remains at Rs 9,000 to Rs 12,000 per tonne and current high levels are not sustainable.
2. **Ritwik Sheth (One Up Financial) on brownfield EC approval timeline:** Management noted that even if EC arrives soon, the shutdown must align with the lean period in late December, taking about a year post-approval.
3. **Anandh Dharshan (360 ONE Capital) on revised capex and payback:** Management declined to give final revised figures, stating all reconfigurations and costs will be finalized by March/April.
4. **Chaitanya Ramesh Rao (Individual Investor) on ring gear/forging line capacity and EBITDA:** Management stated full capacity will be 15,000 tonnes and declined to commit on EBITDA since it is a new business.
5. **Divyansh Gupta (Latent PMS) on receivables jump:** Management explained it is directly proportional to the surge in sales volume during August and September 2026.
6. **Vansh Rathore (JJ Advisory) on downside risks:** Management highlighted project implementation risk as the primary concern.

### One-line takeaway
Vardhman Special Steels reported robust Q2 FY27 performance with a 39% surge in operating revenue and record sales volumes, though management flagged higher upcoming project costs and potential equity/debt adjustments for its greenfield expansion.
