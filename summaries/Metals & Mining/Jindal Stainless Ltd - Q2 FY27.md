# Jindal Stainless Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious / Mixed - Strong operational recovery from Q1 disruptions with improved EBITDA/tonne guidance, tempered by concerns over a surge in substandard imports due to suspended quality control orders.

### Key numbers
- QoQ Growth: Revenue ~14%, EBITDA ~8%, PAT ~17%.
- Finished goods sales volume: Up 14% QoQ and 3% YoY.
- H1 FY27 deliveries: 1.25 million tonnes (down 2% YoY).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated EBITDA: INR 2,762 crores (up ~2% YoY).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated PAT: INR 1,666 crores (up ~9% YoY).
- Consolidated Net Debt (as of Sept 30, 2026): INR 3,121 crores.
- Net debt-to-EBITDA ratio: 0.55x; Net debt-to-equity ratio: 0.15x.
- Capex: INR 895 crores in Q2; INR 1,555 crores total in H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Board approved the amalgamation of four wholly owned subsidiaries into Jindal Stainless Limited: Jindal United Steel Limited, Chromeni Steels Limited, JSL Super Steels Limited, and Rabirun Vinimay Private Limited.
- CARE Ratings upgraded long-term debt credit rating to AA+ Stable.
- Entered into a technical assistance agreement with Japan's JFE Steel for selected ferritic grades in the 400 series.
- Reduced Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions intensity by 12% compared to FY26 levels.

### Guidance & outlook
- Full-year volume growth guidance revised to 4% to 6% (from earlier 7% to 9%).
- EBITDA per tonne guidance raised to INR 20,000 to INR 22,000 per tonne (from earlier INR 18,000 to INR 20,000 per tonne).
- Capex guidance maintained at INR 2,400 crores to INR 2,600 crores.
- Net debt guidance maintained at INR 2,500 crores to INR 2,800 crores.
- Long-term volume target of 3.5 million tonnes per annum by FY29 remains on track.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic demand remained robust across automotive, railways, metros, lifts, elevators, and white goods.
- Exports grew 20% QoQ and 36% YoY, accounting to 11% of total volumes in Q2 FY27.
- Domestic market facing severe import pressure following the suspension of the Quality Control Order (QCO) in April 2026, leading to a surge in substandard imports from China.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs reflected a time lag from high nickel prices witnessed in Q1.
- Series-wise volume mix in Q2 FY27: 200 series (37%), 300 series (47%), and 400 series (16%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Cold rolling mills are under commissioning; HAPL line commissioning expected by the end of this year or early next year.
- Maharashtra plant land acquisition is progressing slower than anticipated; clear plans expected in another quarter or two.
- Chromeni plant running at ~85% capacity utilization and Rathi at ~79%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt stood at INR 3,121 crores as of September 2026.
- Brought forward losses in subsidiaries to be merged provide tax benefits valued at approximately INR 100 crores to INR 125 crores upon NCLT approval.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Suspension of QCO encouraging dumping of cheap, substandard Chinese material.
- Reduced EU export quotas limiting volume off-take in Europe.
- Potential geopolitical uncertainties impacting industrial gas availability and prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs)** asked about import influx and anti-dumping duties; management stated Chinese imports are primarily 200 series and noted DGTR extended the ADD timeline to March 28, 2027, with a decision expected by December 31.
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec)** questioned the second-half implied volume growth confidence and JFE commercial terms; management cited downstream facility ramp-ups and noted payments to JFE are service-based (fees not disclosed).
- **Ashish Kejriwal (Equirus)** asked why domestic volumes were flat YoY despite robust demand; management clarified it was a strategic choice balancing product/grade mix rather than a demand constraint.
- **Tushar Chaudhari (Prabhudas Lilladher)** asked for series-wise volume mix; IR provided breakdown as 37% (200 series), 47% (300 series), and 16% (400 series).

### One-line takeaway
Jindal Stainless delivered a resilient Q2 recovery with margin expansion, prompting management to raise its full-year EBITDA per tonne guidance despite ongoing headwinds from cheap imports.
