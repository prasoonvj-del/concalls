# Mishra Dhatu Nigam Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-22 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line and profit growth, but margins impacted by raw material price volatility and a spike in LPG fuel costs.

### Key numbers
- Turnover: Rs. 239.49 crore (up 40.46% YoY)
- Value of Production: Rs. 260.36 crore (up 7.9% YoY)
- PBT: Rs. 23.92 crore (up 25.89% YoY)
- PAT: Rs. 16.31 crore (up 27.42% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs. 46.6 crore (up 12.89% YoY)
- Order Book (as of July 1, 2026): Rs. 2,329 crore (Defense 66%, Space/ISRO 21%, Energy 9%, Others 4%)
- Scrap inventory reduction: Rs. 17 crore in Q1
- Adverse raw material price variance impact: Rs. 13 crore in Q1

### Highlights
- Received S400 certification from General Electric USA on August 13 for testing mechanical, chemical, and metallography test specimens.
- Qualified non-destructive testing personnel for NAS410 (aiming for Nadcap certificate by end of FY27).
- Planning to obtain ISO 27001 (Information Security Management) and ISO 50001 (Energy Management System) certificates in FY27.
- Successfully isothermally forged a nickel-based super alloy for fighter aircraft engines using MIDHANI's 6,000-ton press; received orders for 4 super alloy grades and 3 titanium alloy grades.
- Successfully rolled 7000 series aluminum alloy on the wide plate mill for a PSU (first commercial aluminum rolling order).
- Fabricated bulletproof jackets using DRDO/IIT Delhi technology ("ABHED"); currently under testing.
- Received sample order for the spring plant from BEML.
- Obtained preliminary BAFA (German Export License Clearing Agency) license for metal powder equipment.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 growth to be higher than FY26, despite Q1 headwinds.
- Management targets exports to constitute around 10% of total turnover, aiming for 10% to 15% annual growth in exports over the next 3 to 4 years.
- Normalised EBITDA margins (20%-21% range) expected to return from Q3 onwards once LPG and raw material issues stabilize.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Titanium and super alloy contributions are growing compared to low-alloy or stainless steel.
- Strong domestic push for indigenization in defense and aerospace (e.g., AMCA program, aero engines).
- MIDHANI stands first in material supplies for nuclear power plants and expects more orders following the prototype fast breeder reactor attaining criticality.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin compressed due to abnormal increases in raw material prices (moly, tungsten, nickel, cobalt) and a doubling of LPG prices due to war crises.
- Price variance adverse impact was ~Rs. 13 crore; LPG cost hit was ~Rs. 5 crore in Q1.
- Fixed-price defense contracts limit the ability to pass on freight and raw material cost spikes immediately.
- Metal bank (customer-owned, MIDHANI-operated) is in advanced procurement stages and expected to be operational by the end of Q2 to mitigate raw material supply delays and cost volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- FY27 Capex is guided around Rs. 50 crore to Rs. 60 crore, mostly for maintenance.
- A larger Rs. 1,000 crore CapEx proposal over 2-3 years to replace aging equipment and expand capacity is currently under evaluation by the Ministry and Board.
- The joint venture plant proposal with NALCO at Nellore (Utkarsha Aluminium Dhatu Nigam Limited) is being formally closed down.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Scrap inventory was reduced by Rs. 17 crore in Q1 (following a Rs. 75 crore reduction last year).
- Metal bank will be customer-owned and operated within MIDHANI's campus, thus not impacting MIDHANI's balance sheet or working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in key raw material prices (nickel, molybdenum, tungsten, cobalt).
- Geopolitical crises driving up LPG fuel costs, impacting furnace operations and margins.
- Inability to pass on freight and raw material cost increases due to fixed-price contract structures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ajinkya Jadhav (KRIIS Portfolio) on GE S400 certification:** Management clarified that MIDHANI can now independently test and certify customers' specimens domestically, generating new revenue without sharing fees with GE.
- **Abhishek Jain (KRIIS PMS) on margin recovery:** Management stated that margins should normalize to 20%-21% by Q3 once LPG and raw material headwinds subside.
- **Renjith Sivaram (Mahindra Manulife) on scrap inventory:** Management explained that scrap is a "gold mine" required for melting, and they strategically sell only when optimal levels are exceeded.
- **Amitabh Vatsya (Southern Ventures) on raw material sourcing for titanium:** Management stated they freely import titanium sponges from East Europe/former Russian Federation without current U.S. sanctions/supply chain constraints.
- **Ajinkya Jadhav on order book pricing mode:** Management confirmed that business is now won via competitive bidding rather than nomination, which structurally impacts legacy margin levels.

### One-line takeaway
MIDHANI delivered strong 40% YoY top-line growth in Q1 FY27, but profitability was dented by a doubling of LPG fuel costs and raw material price spikes, with management expecting normalization by Q3.
