# Ratnamani Metals & Tubes Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong order book growth and capacity expansion are offset by margin pressure from geopolitical-led logistics costs and working capital intensity.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,348 crore (+13% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 220 crore (16.3% margin).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 133 crore.
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 1,104 crore (+18% YoY).
*   **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 139 crore (12.6% margin).
*   **Order Book:** >Rs 2,600 crore (+30% QoQ).
*   **RFSS Revenue:** Rs 157 crore (vs Rs 110 crore YoY).
*   **RFSS EBITDA:** Rs 86 crore (54.7% margin).
*   **RFSS Order Book:** >Rs 3,000 crore.

### Highlights
*   Commissioned Spiral Mill-3 (100,000 tonnes capacity) and external 3LPE coating plant (25 lakh sq meters) in Odisha.
*   Standalone credit rating upgraded to CRISIL AA+/Stable; RFSS rating upgraded to CRISIL AA.
*   Secured global supply frame agreement for aerospace tubes (Safran).
*   Received approval for stainless steel seamless U-tubes for carbamate condensers (fertilizer sector).
*   *Record Check:* The transcript confirms the commissioning of the 3LPE plant and Spiral Mill-3, and the aerospace agreement, consistent with our records.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains FY27 guidance: Standalone revenue Rs 4,000-4,200 crore; Consolidated revenue Rs 5,000-5,200 crore.
*   Expects margin improvement in H2 FY27 and next year as utilization increases.
*   Long-term EBITDA margin target: 15-18% (±1%).
*   RFSS long-term EBITDA margin target: 25-30%.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Stainless steel demand is picking up (thermal power, oil & gas, data centers).
*   Carbon steel demand is improving but remains challenging.
*   Water sector demand remains weak due to state government budget constraints.
*   Middle East bidding pipeline for carbon steel is >Rs 5,000 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Profitability pressured by increased freight and forwarding expenses due to geopolitical uncertainties.
*   US export orders are on DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) basis, inflating both revenue and "other expenses" (duty + freight).
*   No pass-through clause for freight/fuel volatility in existing orders; company absorbs costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Spiral Mill-3 (18m pipe capability) commissioned.
*   External 3LPE coating plant commissioned in Odisha.
*   RFSS expansion project partially commissioned; induction bend production to start next quarter.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Standalone business remains debt-free.
*   Working capital cycle impacted by inventory accumulation (deferred export shipments due to logistics) and receivables.
*   Management expects 5-6 months of inventory + receivables as a norm.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical tensions causing logistics/freight cost spikes.
*   50% import tariff on US exports impacting cost structure.
*   Weak domestic water sector demand.
*   Negative cash flow from operations in H1 due to working capital buildup.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Why did other expenses spike?* A: Management explained it is due to DDP-based US exports where duty and freight are booked as costs, inflating both revenue and expenses.
*   *Q: Is the 50% EBITDA margin at RFSS sustainable?* A: Management clarified long-term sustainable margins are 25-30%; current high margins are due to specific product mix.
*   *Q: Are you passing on freight costs?* A: No, existing orders have fixed prices; the company absorbs the volatility.
*   *Q: Why is cash flow negative?* A: Working capital intensity; inventory buildup due to deferred shipments and the receivables cycle.

### One-line takeaway
Strong order book and capacity expansion provide growth visibility, but near-term profitability remains constrained by geopolitical-led logistics costs and working capital intensity.
