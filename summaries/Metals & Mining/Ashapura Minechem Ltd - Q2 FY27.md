# Ashapura Minechem Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew YoY and operations remained resilient despite seasonal monsoon impacts in Guinea and elevated freight costs pressurizing margins.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: FY27 bauxite export target revised down from 12 million tonnes to ~8.5 million tonnes based on H1 performance and H2 targets (>5 million tonnes), while FY28 target of 15 million tonnes was maintained.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more cautious due to seasonal monsoon and peak freight headwinds, though management noted freight has started correcting from recent highs.
- New topics or risks: Iron ore operations commercialization timeline explicitly pushed to next financial year (FY28).
- Last quarter's promises: Boffa port capacity enhancement to 8 million tonnes completed; GSM port jetty completion targeted by March '27 (consistent with Q1).

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Income from Operations: Rs 1,063 crore (up 12% YoY from Rs 953 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 138 crore (vs Rs 132 crore in Q2 FY26).
- Consolidated PBT: Rs 78 crore (vs Rs 81 crore in Q2 FY26).
- H1 FY27 Consolidated Income from Operations: Rs 2,680 crore (up 16% YoY from Rs 2,308 crore).
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 327 crore (vs Rs 320 crore in H1 FY26).
- H1 FY27 PBT: Rs 208 crore (vs Rs 213 crore in H1 FY26).
- Guinea Bauxite Exports (Q2 FY27): 1.21 million tonnes (vs 2.34 million tonnes in Q1 FY27).
- Guinea EBITDA Contribution: USD 5.3 per metric ton (vs USD 6.3 in Q1 FY27).
- Vertical-wise Q2 FY27 Turnover & EBITDA: Guinea (Turnover: Rs 783 crore, EBITDA: Rs 109 crore); Bentonite, Allied Minerals & White Performance Materials (Turnover: Rs 280 crore, EBITDA: Rs 29 crore); Specialty Absorbent Solutions (Turnover: Rs 115 crore, EBITDA: Rs 13 crore); Advanced Ceramic Materials (Turnover: Rs 127 crore, EBITDA: Rs 13 crore).

### Highlights
- Guinea operations faced seasonal monsoon headwinds during Q2, reducing export volumes sequentially.
- Boffa port handling capacity enhanced from 5 million tonnes to 8 million tonnes per annum.
- Company was awarded six bentonite mines in Gujarat.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets exporting more than 5 million tonnes of bauxite over the next two quarters (H2 FY27).
- Management remains confident in achieving its guidance of 15 million tonnes bauxite exports in FY28.
- Management expects to commence iron ore operations and achieve good results in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 is traditionally a weaker quarter for Guinea operations due to equatorial monsoon conditions.
- Ocean freight spot rates for bulk shipments (C3 index) have corrected by 15% to 18% from recent highs, with forward contract prices lower than spot rates.
- No formal bauxite export quota system has been implemented yet by the Guinea government; management expects updates by the end of the quarter.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability was impacted by higher ocean freight, fuel costs, and elevated input costs (particularly sulfuric acid for bleaching clay).
- Guinea bauxite operations maintained an EBITDA contribution of USD 5.3 per metric ton.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Boffa port handling capacity is now fully operational at 8 million tonnes per annum.
- GSM port new jetty construction is progressing, targeted for completion by March 2027, which will increase capacity from 6 million tonnes to 10 million tonnes per annum.
- Planned capex of Rs 150 crore to Rs 170 crore across India business verticals for manufacturing capabilities and product expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical uncertainties impacting fuel prices, marine logistics, and overall input costs.
- Elevated ocean freight rates compressing margins and causing buyer caution.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kriti Tripathi (NVS Corporate)** asked about FY27 volume revisions and freight trends. Management stated FY27 will be lower than initial plans due to H1 headwinds, but reiterated confidence in the FY28 target of 15 million tonnes and noted freight markets are showing normalization signs.
- **Krish** asked about the status of the Guinea export quota system. Management stated no formal quota system has been released yet, but if implemented at around 150 million tonnes, it could improve EBITDA by a couple of dollars per tonne.
- **Nalin Shah (NVS Brokerage)** asked about bauxite realizations and tax liabilities. Management stated bauxite prices are stable to slightly higher, and tax liability will gradually increase as past accrued losses are exhausted.
- **Vardhman (AVS Group)** asked about freight rates and India capex details. Management stated spot freight rates are around USD 36 (down from a peak of USD 43) and explained that the Rs 150-170 crore capex is targeted at value-added import substitutes for oil & gas, foundry, and coatings industries.
- **Harshad Mehta (Safetech)** asked about iron ore operations. Management stated iron ore operations are expected to start next financial year (FY28) with lower volatility and favorable bottom-line contribution.

### One-line takeaway
Ashapura Minechem delivered steady YoY revenue growth in Q2 despite seasonal monsoon headwinds in Guinea and elevated freight costs, while keeping its long-term FY28 bauxite export targets intact.
