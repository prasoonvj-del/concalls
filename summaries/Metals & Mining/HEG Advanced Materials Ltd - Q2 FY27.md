# HEG Advanced Materials Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Structural demerger completed, strong order visibility, and robust demand across advanced materials and green power.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Continuing Operations):** Rs 430 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 176 crore (Q2 FY26); Rs 726 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 329 crore (H1 FY26).
- **Total Income:** Rs 547 crore (Q2 FY27); Rs 871 crore (H1 FY27).
- **EBITDA (Continuing Operations):** Rs 313 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 129 crore (Q2 FY26); Rs 438 crore (H1 FY27) vs Rs 270 crore (H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 57% (Q2 FY27); 50% (H1 FY27).
- **Profit After Tax (Continuing Operations):** Rs 209 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 69 crore (Q2 FY26); Rs 269 crore (H1 FY26: Rs 168 crore).
- **Consolidated Equity:** Rs 2,510 crore (as of September 30, 2026).
- **Total Assets:** Rs 4,436 crore.
- **Cash and Bank Balances:** Rs 1,300 crore.
- **Long-term Borrowings:** Rs 1,036 crore (net cash positive).
- **Total Planned Capex:** ~Rs 5,500 crore.

### Highlights
- Completed demerger from HEG Limited, operating as HEG Advanced Materials Limited (HEGAM).
- Anode Phase 1 (20,000 tonnes at Dewas) is 61% complete, targeting commercial commissioning by June 2027.
- Graphene 10 metric tonne pilot plant operational; 500 metric tonne commercial plant targeted by September 2028 with 90 metric tonnes of confirmed orders and LOIs.
- RePlus scaling from 1 GWh to 6 GWh total capacity (COD March 2027); currently executing ~1.5 to 2 GWh of solutions.
- Note on Record: Our record noted a Rs 609.50 crore approval for the 20,000-tonne anode plant on Nov 11, 2026; management stated total capex for the current 20,000-tonne capacity is approximately Rs 2,350 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects steady-state revenue of Rs 7,500 crore to Rs 8,200 crore by FY29–FY30.
- Group-level annual EBITDA margin projected at 25% to 28%.
- Anode EBITDA margins expected in the range of 28% to 33%, reaching north of 35% with crucible backward integration.
- Graphene EBITDA margins expected higher than 45%.
- RePlus EBITDA margins expected at double digits (10% plus minus 1% to 2%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global lithium-ion battery production projected to climb from ~3 TWh in 2026 to 8 TWh by 2035; graphite anode demand expected to jump from 2.9 million metric tonnes to 8 million metric tonnes.
- Non-China anode demand set to exceed 1 million tonnes by 2030; Indian domestic cell capacity projected to reach 250 GW by 2035.
- Stationary BESS accounting for nearly 28% of total lithium-ion market by 2030; renewable energy storage demand estimated at 480 GWh in the next 10 years.
- RePlus seeing strong traction in EMEA (China plus one).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw coke and power comprise over 60% of anode cost; key raw material suppliers sourced from non-China geographies (U.S., U.K., Japan).
- Crucible backward integration will save ~40% compared to imports and eliminate China dependency.
- Power segment EBIT included one-time items (transmission line arrears and insurance claim) comprising ~40% of Q2 power EBIT; regular EBIT is ~60%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Total Capex (~Rs 5,500 crore breakdown):** 
  - Current 20,000-tonne anode capacity: ~Rs 2,350 crore.
  - Additional 24,000-tonne anode capacity (Phase 2, scaling to 44,000 tonnes by March 2029): ~Rs 1,600 crore.
  - Graphene (500 tonnes): Rs 500 crore.
  - Crucible facility (80,000 units): Rs 475 crore.
  - RePlus expansion (1 GWh to 6 GWh): Rs 250 crore.
  - BESS IPP project in Gujarat: Rs 230 crore.
  - Potential standalone solar C&I project (300 MW): ~Rs 1,200 crore (under discussion, not yet included in capex).
- **Year-on-Year Capex Outflow:** ~Rs 2,300 crore in FY27, followed by Rs 1,600 crore to Rs 1,700 crore in FY28, and balance in FY29.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balances stand at Rs 1,300 crore against long-term borrowings of Rs 1,036 crore.
- Working capital in RePlus is currently high due to 70%-80% contribution from EPC business, which will shift toward product manufacturing and supply (target 50:50 next year, 20% EPC in 2 years).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Power business and hydro generation are seasonal, highly dependent on weather/water availability (75% in H1, 25% in H2).
- First-year plant construction for anodes carries execution challenges; initial capacity utilization assumed at a humble 35%.

### One-line takeaway
HEG Advanced Materials is aggressively investing ~Rs 5,500 crore to build an integrated non-China advanced materials and energy storage platform, backed by stable cash flows from its green power business.
