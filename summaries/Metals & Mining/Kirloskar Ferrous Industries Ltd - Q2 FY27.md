# Kirloskar Ferrous Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited continued margin pressures in tubes, a slow recovery in high-value segments, and potential softening in the tractor industry, despite sequential volume and revenue growth.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,057 crore (up 16% YoY from Rs 1,755 crore)
- **PBT (Bottom-line):** Rs 138 crore (up from Rs 120 crore YoY)
- **Pig Iron Sales (External):** 1,34,000 tonnes (up 3.5% YoY); Revenue: Rs 581 crore (up 20% YoY)
- **Castings Sales (Net):** 47,888 tonnes (up 20% YoY); Gross Sales crossed 50,000 tonnes; Revenue: Rs 633 crore (up 30% YoY)
- **Tubes Sales:** 48,547 tonnes (down 2% YoY)
- **Steel External Sales:** Rs 206 crore (up 48% YoY from Rs 139 crore)
- **Realization Changes (YoY):** 
  - Pig Iron: Rs 43,054 per tonne (up 12.4%)
  - Castings: Rs 1,30,000 per tonne (up 6.8%)
  - Tubes: Rs 1,16,953 per tonne (up 1.6%)
  - Steel: Rs 77,500 per tonne (up 7.6%)
- **H1 Production Highlights:** Pig iron hot metal production at 3,31,896 tonnes (up 3% YoY); Castings production at 93,000 tonnes (up 20% YoY); Steel production at 1,33,000 tonnes (up 14% YoY); Tubes production down 7.5%.

### Highlights
- Solapur Phase 1 and Phase 2 second foundry line running at ~5,200 tonnes monthly production/sales; commissioning of the two-part (No-Bake) foundry expected by January to target large castings up to 2 tonnes.
- Rajpura Phase 2 expansion planned by fiscal year-end (31st March) to increase capacity close to 4,000 tonnes/month.
- Exported one cargo of 31,000 metric tonnes of pig iron due to better international realizations.
- Anti-dumping duty on tubes extended for another 5 years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 tube sales volume to exceed 100,000 metric tonnes to achieve growth over the previous year.
- Management targets casting sales of ~1 lakh metric tonnes per quarter in H2 (gross sales basis).
- Long-term vision aims for 15% volumetric growth YoY in the casting business, targeting 2.5 lakh tonnes capacity and ~0.5 million tonnes of pig iron sales long-term (with balance converted to steel).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Casting demand from tractor, automotive, earth-moving, and stationary engine markets was good in Q1 and Q2, though management noted small corrections/softness ahead in the tractor industry.
- Auto and earth-moving equipment continue strong demand for castings.
- Tube demand faced challenges in Q1 due to delayed oil and gas projects, with recovery seen in Q2; however, the tube market overall is currently described as "not very strong."
- Steel market lacks strong pickup despite price improvements.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Coking coal prices and adverse rupee-dollar exchange rates created cost headwinds over the past 3-4 quarters.
- Castings operate on a quarter-on-quarter price-pass mechanism, meaning recent cost/price increases reflect with a lag.
- Solapur rejection rates are expected to decline from 10%-11% down to 7%-7.5% in the next quarter; Rajpura rejections targeted below 5%.
- Green energy integration (e.g., 35 MW Solapur windmills) and ongoing productivity/quality drives are expected to aid margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Koppal alloy steel plant project planning is nearly finalized with technology design partners; commercial negotiations underway with ordering expected in 2-3 months and 20 months for execution post-ordering.
- Rolling mill capacity expansion in Jejuri, expander mill project in Baramati, and small piercing mills in Ahmednagar have entered execution stage.
- Machining revenue is at ~Rs 50-60 crore annually (vs ~Rs 200 crore investment); management targets crossing Rs 100 crore in machining within 1-2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *No specific aggregate net debt or working capital figures were disclosed in the transcript.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slowness and potential demand corrections in the tractor industry.
- Adverse foreign exchange (rupee-dollar) and volatile coking coal prices impacting input costs.
- Delayed oil and gas project executions impacting high-margin tube orders.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pratik Kothari (Unique PMS) on Solapur / Jejuri timelines:** Management clarified Solapur phase 2 is ramping up, Rajpura phase 2 will scale by March 31, and Koppal alloy steel project ordering will happen in 2-3 months with a 20-month execution window.
2. **Dhruvesh Kanakia (Consensus/Other) on casting margin contraction despite volume/realization growth:** Management explained the Q-o-Q pricing lag for castings, ongoing green energy integration, and higher initial rejections on thin-walled components (expected to drop to 7-7.5% next quarter).
3. **Deepak D on pig iron domestic drop vs exports:** Management clarified the 31k-tonne export was a opportunistic sweet spot due to better pricing, but domestic remains the primary focus.
4. **Manish Goyal on machining revenue vs investment:** Management admitted machining revenue (~Rs 50-60 cr) is low relative to investments (~Rs 200 cr) due to model changes and lower customer pickup, but targets Rs 100 cr within 1-2 years.
5. **Bharat Sheth on tube margin compression:** Management attributed it to Q1 volume drops, delayed oil/gas/export dispatches (pushed to October), and lower-margin line pipe sales due to delayed project orders.

### One-line takeaway
Kirloskar Ferrous reported steady 16% YoY revenue growth and margin recovery in basic metals, but faces execution and demand headwinds in value-added tubes and softening tractor foundry segments.
