# Pondy Oxides & Chemicals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Supply chain disruptions and elevated raw material/logistics costs pressured lead volumes and margins, though copper scaling and cost renegotiations offer a path to recovery.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Lead volume guidance for FY27 was lowered (the earlier ~1.25 lakh to 1.3 lakh tonnes target does not hold for this financial year; next year targets a minimum of 25,000 tonnes per quarter / >1 lakh tonnes). Copper volume and EBITDA guidance remained intact.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from "no demand softening, only supply chain delays" to acknowledging prolonged geopolitical supply chain disruptions in West Asia and higher input/logistics costs that impacted margins, with a gradual recovery expected by Q4.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Increased focus on customer contract renegotiations with variable pricing clauses starting Jan/Feb; detailed breakdown of copper cathode feedstock (60% internal recycling, 40% sold/external). Old risk of Hormuz route disruption persisted and was expanded into broader West Asia supply chain impacts.
- Last quarter's promises: Copper expansion remains on track (INR60 crores incurred out of ~INR140 crores for Phase I, commissioning by December 2026). Lead value-added mix dropped from 85% in Q1 to 60%-65% in Q2 due to customer demand mix.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,639 crore (up 33% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 55.6 crore
- **H1 Lead EBITDA per ton:** Rs 18,617
- **H1 Copper EBITDA per ton:** Rs 44,536 (up 30% YoY)
- **H1 Copper Production / Sales Volumes:** 5,649 tonnes / 6,040 tonnes (up >3x YoY)
- **Lead Segment Value-Added Proportion (Q2):** 60%-65% (vs 85% in Q1)
- **Expanded Lead Capacity Utilization (H1):** 40% (72,000 MTPA plant)
- **Copper Recycling Capacity Utilization (H1):** 100% of 12,000 MTPA capacity (6,000 tonnes done in H1)
- **Copper Cathode Project Capex Incurred:** ~Rs 60 crore out of ~Rs 140 crore (Phase I)
- **Revenue Mix:** Domestic 54%, Exports 46% (Exports were 55% of lead and 39% of copper)

### Highlights
- Copper business scaled up significantly, growing ~4.5x and contributing 47% of H1 FY27 top line.
- Cash flow from operations turned positive in H1 FY27 while maintaining a net cash and debt-free balance sheet.
- Incremental 6,000 MTPA copper recycling capacity (commissioned Q4 FY26) ramped up, expected to hit ~90% utilization in FY27.
- Expanded 72,000 MTPA lead capacity at Thervoy Kandigai operated at 40% utilization in H1 due to supply constraints, targeting 70% in coming quarters.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its 2030 roadmap: >15% volume growth, 20%+ CAGR in revenue and profitability, EBITDA margins >8%, ROCE >20%, and >60% revenue from value-added products.
- FY27 Lead Volume Target: Expects closer to 75,000 tonnes for the full year, with next year running at a minimum of 25,000 tonnes per quarter (>1 lakh tonnes).
- Copper Volume Target: FY27 copper volume expected closer to 10,000 tonnes; next year (FY28) target in excess of 20,000 tonnes.
- Copper Cathode EBITDA Guidance: Rs 65,000 per ton on cathode; copper recycling at Rs 35,000-40,000 per ton.
- Lead EBITDA per ton guided to remain around Rs 18,000-20,000 range.
- Copper Cathode Timeline: Phase I (18,000 MTPA) targeted for commissioning by December 2026, trial runs in Q4 FY27; Phase II (balance 18,000 MTPA) targeted for Q3 FY28. Total Phase I capex outlay is ~Rs 140 crores.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Geopolitical developments in West Asia caused global supply chain and shipping route disruptions, affecting material availability and shipment schedules, though gradual easing is visible.
- Copper demand-supply gap in India remains high, driving robust long-term demand despite aggressive industry expansions.
- Lithium-ion recycling: Management stated they are not currently looking at lithium-ion battery recycling due to unconvincing volumes, unproven long-term feasibility, and alternative chemistries (like sodium-ion) emerging.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Profitability in H1 under pressure due to elevated input costs, power/fuel, fluxes, and logistics, with COGS growing faster than revenue.
- Domestic scrap procurement pricing is slightly more expensive than international sourcing.
- Sourcing mix shifted toward South America, Southeast Asia, and domestic yards to bypass West Asian disruptions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 36,000 MTPA copper cathode facility at Thervoy Kandigai, Tamil Nadu: Total investment ~Rs 200 crores funded via internal accruals (~Rs 60 crores incurred).
- Cathode raw material sourcing: ~60% to be supplied from internal recycled copper, and ~40% (approx 13,000-13,500 tonnes for Phase I) procured externally (mostly imports).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company maintains a net cash and debt-free position.
- Trade receivables decreased from previous levels (noted as reduced to Rs 144 crore in the transcript details).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions in West Asia impacting shipping routes, scrap availability, and lead shipment schedules.
- Elevated power, fuel, fluxes, and logistics costs compressing EBITDA per ton in the lead segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Dheeraj Ram (360 ONE Capital) asked about lead volume recovery:** Management noted a turnaround is expected in Q4, guiding for a minimum of 25,000 tonnes/quarter next year.
- **Sagar Shah (Spark) asked about lead segment margin drop:** Management explained that value-added mix dropped to 60%-65% this quarter, combined with higher power, fuel, and flux costs, but guided for recovery to Rs 18,000-20,000 per ton by Q4 via contract renegotiations.
- **Shashank Kanodia (Securities) asked about lead volume feasibility for FY27:** Management stated they may fall short of earlier aggressive targets, aiming closer to 75,000 tonnes for the full year.
- **Digant Haria (Wealth) asked if the business model is under threat due to shipping costs:** Management explained shipping costs are passed through via variable contract renegotiations starting Jan/Feb, and availability remains intact.
- **Pawan Kumar (Research) asked about lithium-ion recycling plans:** Management stated they are not entering lithium-ion recycling due to low volumes and uncertain technology stickiness compared to sodium-ion.
- **Jigar Jani (Investor) asked about EU export bans and import regions:** Management stated EU import exposure is small (~5%-7%) and current procurement relies on US, UK, South America, Australia, and Southeast Asia.

### One-line takeaway
POCL reported a resilient H1 revenue growth of 33% YoY driven by a 4.5x scale-up in copper, but faced volume and margin pressures in lead due to West Asian supply chain disruptions and input cost inflation, even as its Rs 200 crore copper cathode expansion remains on track for December 2026.
