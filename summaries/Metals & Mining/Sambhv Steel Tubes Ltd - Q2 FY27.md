# Sambhv Steel Tubes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a robust set of numbers, healthy cash generation, and strong operational performance despite monsoon headwinds and normalization from a Q1 outlier.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 840 crore
- **EBITDA:** Rs 105 crore (Margin: >12%)
- **PAT:** Rs 61 crore (Margin: >7%)
- **Value-Added Products Volume Growth:** 27% YoY
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 213 crore generated in Q2
- **Net Debt-to-EBITDA:** 1.04x for H1
- **Stainless Steel Seamless Pipes Capex:** Rs 148 crore (Capacity: 14,400 tonnes per annum)
- **Stainless Steel Division EBITDA Margin:** Rs 12,000 to Rs 13,000 per tonne

### Highlights
- Value-added product sales volume increased by 27% YoY, improving the product mix.
- Kesda and Sarora capacity expansions remain on track for on-time commissioning.
- Entered the stainless-steel seamless pipes and tubes business with an investment of Rs 148 crore.
- Received PLI approval and signed an MoU under PLI 1.2 for seamless pipe sales to priority sectors, eligible for approximately 13% revenue incentive.
- Secured a warrant proceeds of Rs 25 crore earmarked for plant, machinery, and land acquisition for the seamless project.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Volume Guidance:** Aiming for total value-added product sales of 4,10,000 to 4,25,000 tonnes (comprising ~2,40,000+ tonnes of MS pipes/tubes, 1,10,000 to 1,20,000 tonnes of galvanized coated products, and 50,000 to 60,000 tonnes of stainless steel coil). Total sales including Kesda plant expected at ~5.5 lakh tonnes.
- **FY27 EBITDA Guidance:** Upwardly revised EBITDA margin per tonne bracket to Rs 8,000 to Rs 8,500 for the full year; total EBITDA expected upward of Rs 550 crore.
- **FY28 Outlook:** Volume expected to be upward of 7 lakh tonnes; EBITDA expected upward of Rs 750 crore once the Kesda unit is fully utilized.
- **Net Debt-to-EBITDA Guidance:** Expected to increase slightly to 1.4x to 1.5x by the end of the year due to ongoing capex cycles.
- **Seamless Project Timeline:** Commercial production targeted in 1.5 years (live by March 2028); aiming for 10,000 tonnes production by FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India is experiencing robust double-digit growth in stainless steel demand, driven by sunrise sectors like semiconductors, data centers, green hydrogen, coal gasification, aerospace, defense, and nuclear power.
- Current waiting periods for seamless pipes are 6 to 8 months, indicating strong demand.
- Government policy (Vision 2047 and National Steel Policy) aims for self-sufficiency in alloy and stainless steel.
- Chinese dumping of 200 and 300 series coils is undercutting domestic pricing, though the government is evaluating minimum import prices and anti-dumping measures.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material yields were impacted during the monsoon due to moisture content.
- Q1 FY27 was noted as a black swan outlier due to abnormal price increases; Q2 margins represent a practical, sustainable baseline.
- Backward integration covers >80% of coil requirements in-house, shielding the company from external HR pipe scarcity.
- Captive power plant additions (30 MW at Sarora by Sep 2027; 25 MW at Kesda by Mar 2027) expected to yield cost savings of Rs 60 crore to Rs 70 crore and 1% to 1.5% EBITDA margin accretion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Kesda Stainless Steel Facility (3.6 lakh tonnes + 25 MW power):** Live by March 2027.
- **Sarora Power Plant (30 MW):** Live by September 2027 (Capex: Rs 150 crore).
- **DFT Route Pipe Manufacturing Expansion:** Adding 1.5 lakh tonnes (taking capacity from 3.5 lakh to 5 lakh tonnes), live by September 2027.
- **Stainless Steel Seamless Pipe Facility:** Rs 148 crore capex, 14,400 tonnes per annum capacity, live by March 2028.
- **Future Vision 2030 (Phase 2 & 3):** Estimated at Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 2,000 crore, subject to board approval post-Kesda commissioning.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital days improved to 4 to 5 days in Q2 due to destocking, improved customer collections, and better supplier credit terms; management views an optimum level at 20 to 25 days.
- Working capital for the seamless project is estimated at Rs 50-60 crore in FY29, scaling to a maximum of Rs 80-90 crore by FY30.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Relaxation of QCO norms has led to heavy Chinese dumping of 200 and 300 series coils, shrinking stainless steel margins.
- Seasonal monsoon yield losses due to moisture content in raw materials.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Soham Jain (Dalal & Broacha):** Asked about the drivers behind the decline in EBITDA per ton from Q1 to Q2. Management explained it was due to monsoon yield loss, Chinese dumping pressure on stainless steel, and normalization from the Q1 price outlier.
2. **Devesh Lakhotia (Individual):** Inquired about the technology and sourcing for the seamless pipe project. Management responded they are utilizing the piercing method for capital efficiency, sourcing round bars domestically in Phase 1, and targeting sunrise sectors.
3. **Shweta Dikshit (Individual):** Asked about raw material scrap constraints and go-to-market strategy for seamless pipes. Management stated they import nickel substitute alloys easily and will initially target the commercial/replacement market before securing OEM approvals.
4. **Yash Desai (Individual):** Asked about H2 guidance revisions. Management confirmed an upward revision of the EBITDA per ton bracket to Rs 8,000-8,500 for FY27 and maintained the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio target.
5. **Pranav Jain (Individual):** Questioned confidence in stainless steel expansion given peer struggles with Chinese dumping. Management highlighted that they manufacture sub-1mm coils (0.1 mm to 0.4 mm) which are majorly imported, and benefit from the PLI scheme.

### One-line takeaway
Sambhv Steel Tubes delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with stable margins, strong cash generation, and an ongoing expansion path toward its Vision 2030 targets, supported by backward integration and entry into stainless steel seamless pipes.
