# Steel Authority of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong volume growth, higher sales turnover, and robust H1 performance despite input cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Crude Steel Production (Q2 FY27):** 4.85 million tonnes (up 2% QoQ, 4% YoY)
- **Sales Volume (Q2 FY27):** 5.2 million tonnes (up 5% YoY, 24% QoQ)
- **Sales Turnover (Q2 FY27):** INR 31,766 crore (up 20% YoY, 22% QoQ)
- **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** INR 4,561 crore
- **PBT (Q2 FY27):** INR 2,356 crore (vs. INR 553 crore in CPLY, INR 2,159 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** INR 1,774 crore (vs. INR 427 crore in CPLY, INR 1,636 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Borrowings / Debt (as of 30 Sept 2026):** INR 20,583 crore (reduction of INR 1,080 crore in H1)
- **Debt-Equity Ratio (Q2 FY27):** 0.34x
- **Crude Steel Production (H1 FY27):** 9.6 million tonnes (up 2% YoY)
- **Sales Volume (H1 FY27):** 9.33 million tonnes
- **Sales Turnover (H1 FY27):** INR 57,776 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **EBITDA (H1 FY27):** INR 8,917 crore (up 55% YoY)
- **PBT (H1 FY27):** INR 4,515 crore (up 213% YoY)
- **PAT (H1 FY27):** INR 3,410 crore (up 207% YoY)
- **Cost of Borrowings:** 6.34%
- **Total Steel Inventory (as of 30 Sept 2026):** 1.59 million tonnes (0.86 million tonnes saleable, 0.73 million tonnes in-process)
- **Iron Ore Fines Sales (H1 FY27):** 2.46 million tonnes (generating INR 388 crore profit)
- **H1 Capex:** INR 5,500 crore

### Highlights
- Inventory reduced from 2.15 million tonnes (Q1 FY27) and 1.8 million tonnes (31 March 2026) to 1.59 million tonnes.
- Iron ore fines sales increased significantly; target 7 million to 8 million tonnes for the year.
- Headcount reduced to 48,000 by end of Q2, expected to reach 46,000 by year-end.
- Labour code provision of INR 198 crore taken during the quarter (part of other expenses).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 and Q4 to be stronger with improving realizations and cost-reduction actions.
- Full-year sales volume guidance revised to around 21 million tonnes (from 22 million tonnes).
- Capex guidance: INR 15,000 crore for FY27, in excess of INR 20,000 crore for next year, and total capex of INR 1,00,000 crore to INR 1,20,000 crore over FY27 to FY31.
- Iron ore production target: 48 million to 50 million tonnes for FY27; ambitious long-term target of 100 million tonnes by FY30-31 / FY31-32.
- Employee count target: 26,000 by end of 2031.

### Demand & industry commentary
- WSA projects stable global steel demand for calendar year 2026 and 1.7% growth in calendar year 2027.
- China's steel demand expected to shrink by 2% and 1% in CY26 and CY27; production dipped 3% up to August.
- India's steel consumption growth expected at 8% for CY26 and CY27. India remains a net importer (0.6 million tonnes in H1 FY27).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Imported coking coal consumption price rose to INR 22,930 per tonne in Q2 (vs. INR 21,300 in Q1); projected at around INR 23,800–23,900 in Q3.
- Blast furnace productivity and techno-economic parameters were impacted in Q2 due to unprecedented monsoon rains in Jharkhand and Odisha, but have recovered in October.
- Raw material basket relies 85% on imported coking coal and 15% on indigenous coal; Tasra coking coal mine expected to start production in January 2027 to reduce import dependence.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capacity expansion planned from 20 million tonnes to 35 million tonnes across 4 units (IISCO, Bokaro, Bhilai, Rourkela) over the next 4 years.
- IISCO expansion execution underway; Bokaro in advanced tendering; Bhilai tendering starting shortly; Rourkela DPR prepared for approval.
- Funding mix for expansion: Internal accruals (estimated INR 10,000 crore to INR 12,000 crore annually, totaling INR 40,000 crore to INR 50,000 crore), with the balance through FPO and debt.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings reduced by INR 1,080 crore in H1 to INR 20,583 crore.
- Inventory days stand at 15 days (down from 17.5 days as of 31 March 2026).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential increase in borrowing costs due to recent 25 bps repo rate hikes.
- Geopolitical tensions and international price volatility affecting coking coal costs.
- Statutory approvals and permit challenges in Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh group of mines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Lahoti (Aditya Birla Institutional Equities) on FPO timing vs. capex load:** Management noted the suggestion to time the FPO carefully to protect return ratios and investor confidence rather than loading equity upfront.
- **Parthiv Jhonsa (Anand Rathi) on coal cost trajectory:** Management clarified that Q2 imported coal averaged INR 22,930/tonne, expected to hover around INR 23,800–23,900 in Q3 due to international spikes before stabilizing.
- **Pinakin Parekh (HSBC) on capex cash outflow timing:** Management stated capex will scale up to INR 15,000 crore in FY27, exceed INR 20,000 crore in FY28, and total INR 1.2 lakh crore over 4 years.
- **Ritesh Shah (Investec) on iron ore fines profitability and issues:** Management reported H1 iron ore fines profit of INR 388 crore and highlighted permit/approval bottlenecks in Jharkhand mines.
- **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities) on land availability for expansion:** Management confirmed that all 4 expansion sites have encroachment-free land available.

### One-line takeaway
SAIL reported strong H1 performance with a 55% jump in EBITDA and lower debt, though margins faced pressure from higher coking coal costs and monsoon-impacted operations, while aggressive multi-year capacity and raw material expansions lie ahead.
