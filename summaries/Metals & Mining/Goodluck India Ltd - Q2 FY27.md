# Goodluck India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and volume growth, coupled with strategic corporate restructuring (amalgamation) and clear long-term visibility in defence and aerospace.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 1,370 crore (Rs 13.7 billion), up ~38% YoY.
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 127 crore (Rs 1.27 billion), up 31% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 9.3% (vs 9.18% in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 50.6 crore (Rs 506 million), up ~19% YoY.
*   **Sales Volume:** 1,33,374 tonnes, up 18.1% YoY.
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 2,660 crore (Rs 26.6 billion), up 34% YoY.
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 120 crore (Rs 1.2 billion), up 42.4% YoY.

### Highlights
*   **Corporate Restructuring:** Proposed amalgamation of Goodluck Defence and Aerospace India Ltd and Goodluck Green Energy with Goodluck India Ltd to improve strategic alignment and organizational efficiency.
*   **Defence Milestone:** GDAL emerged as the sole technically qualified bidder for 155mm HE M1A4 artillery shells for Munitions India Ltd.
*   **Aerospace Expansion:** New initiative with Rs 80 crore investment, targeting Rs 180 crore in business benefits.
*   **Export Growth:** Export revenue grew ~38% in H1 FY27, contributing ~28% of total volumes.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Growth:** Management expects 15-20% YoY revenue growth.
*   **Defence Targets:** Management remains confident in achieving the Rs 300 crore revenue target for the defence business in FY27.
*   **Long-term Potential:** Peak revenue potential for the 4 lakh shell capacity is estimated at Rs 1,000 crore+ (by FY29).
*   **Aerospace:** Target of Rs 180 crore revenue in 4-5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Core Engineering:** Strong demand across infrastructure, power transmission, railways, and automotive sectors.
*   **Power Transmission:** Sector is "on the boil" due to high demand for power evacuation infrastructure.
*   **Defence:** Market is mature with high export demand; management claims to be ahead of competition in technology (e.g., ERFB shells).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Headwinds:** EBITDA margins impacted by ~150 bps due to higher international freight and fuel (gas) costs.
*   **Product Mix:** Value-added products contribute 78% to EBITDA (68% of volume); non-value-added products contribute 22% to EBITDA (32% of volume).
*   **Precision Tubes:** EBITDA margin in the range of 11-13%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Defence:** Expansion from 1.5 lakh to 4 lakh shells is on track.
*   **Non-Defence:** Expansion from 5 lakh to 6 lakh tonnes is ongoing via debottlenecking (12-18 month timeline).
*   **Hydraulic Tubes:** Capacity utilization at 65-70%, expected to reach 75-80% in H2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** Cycle improved by 7 days from March 2026 exit level.
*   **Debt:** Management intends to keep debt within 2.5x of EBITDA.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Execution:** Defence order execution is subject to government clearances, inspections, and geopolitical conditions.
*   **Macro:** Higher fuel and freight costs and uncertain global trade conditions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Timeline for the proposed merger?** A: Management stated 12-15 months; the process is currently in progress.
*   **Q: Defence order execution lumpiness?** A: Management noted the business is not inherently lumpy, but execution timelines are dependent on government approvals and geopolitical factors.
*   **Q: Debt reduction plans?** A: Management is focused on maintaining debt within 2.5x EBITDA rather than becoming net-debt-free in the near term.
*   **Q: Aerospace opportunity?** A: Management confirmed they are working with major global players like Safran, Airbus, and Boeing.
*   **Q: Capacity utilization?** A: Non-defence capacity is at 98-99%; debottlenecking is the primary strategy for further capacity expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational performance and strategic restructuring position the company for long-term growth in defence and high-value engineering, despite near-term margin headwinds from logistics costs.
