# Sarda Energy & Minerals Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever half-yearly EBITDA and PAT, driven by strong energy contributions and integrated operations despite planned shutdowns.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR1,326 crores (up ~4% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR799 crores (up ~5% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** INR3,221 crores
- **H1 FY27 Cash Profit:** INR1,225 crores
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR564 crores
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR320 crores
- **Q2 FY27 Total Income:** INR1,505 crores
- **H1 FY27 Pellet Output at Raipur:** Highest ever at 4,29,059 tonnes
- **Liquidity:** Exceeding INR2,600 crores as of 30th September 2026
- **Thermal Power Realization (Q2):** ~INR5.30 per unit (SKS Power ranged from INR5.20 to INR5.50)
- **Hydropower Realization (H1):** Above INR6.00 per unit
- **Pellet Realization:** Q1 above INR10,000/tonne, Q2 slightly below at ~INR9,800/tonne
- **Thermal Generation (SKS):** H1 achieved 202 crore units; expects to cross 420 crore units for the full year.

### Highlights
- Secured preferred bidder status for the Avagudem manganese block in Andhra Pradesh.
- Replaced 3-megawatt turbogenerator at Raipur commenced operations in August 2026.
- Completed a 47-day planned maintenance shutdown for one 300-megawatt unit at the 600-megawatt thermal power plant.
- Credit ratings upgraded by CRISIL: SEML upgraded by one notch from AA- to AA; Sarda Metals & Alloys Limited upgraded by two notches from A to AA-.
- Energy segment contributed more than 75% of H1 EBITDA.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management does not provide forward-looking numerical guidance on revenues or earnings.
- Environmental clearance for the 600MW to 1,200MW thermal expansion expected within the current financial year.
- Shahpur West high-grade coal mine targeted for commissioning before the end of the current financial year.
- Bartunga Hill coal mine expected to open by the end of the next financial year.
- 50-megawatt captive solar project expected to be commissioned next quarter (subject to transmission right-of-way resolution).
- Avagudem manganese mine operations expected to commence in approximately 12 to 14 months.
- No major thermal power shutdowns foreseen for the remainder of the current financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India’s power generation grew 11% YoY to ~536 billion units in Q2; peak power demand rose 18% YoY to 270 GW.
- Solar generation increased 39% YoY in Q2.
- Domestic steel consumption continues to grow, with flat steel more buoyant than long sections. Flat steel prices range around INR75,000–INR77,000; long product prices in Mumbai near INR54,000.
- Domestic mining production impacted by heavy monsoons, easing expected in 2 to 3 months.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Bulk freight rates for imported coal increased to USD36–USD38 (from usual USD18–USD22).
- Australian coking coal prices are high due to seasonal flooding.
- Avagudem manganese mine expected to save INR200 to INR400 per tonne and provide low-grade ore security for 4–5 years.
- Steel segment revenue impacted because the billet and wire rod plants were not operated to prioritize selling power at remunerative market prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- All ongoing expansion projects funded through internal accruals.
- MoEF has approved terms of reference for expanding the 600MW thermal plant to 1,200MW.
- Approval process for 3 small hydropower projects in Chhattisgarh (74MW total) progressing as scheduled.
- Arunachal Pradesh 66MW hydropower project has key statutory approvals and land acquisition completed; approach road work commenced.
- Regulatory approvals for Gare Palma IV/5 and Senduri coal blocks progressing as planned.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains net debt-free on both standalone and consolidated bases.
- Total liquidity exceeded INR2,600 crores as of 30th September 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High ocean freight rates (due to West Asia tensions) and elevated coking coal prices creating raw material cost pressures.
- Heavy monsoons caused flooding in domestic mines, temporarily reducing production and pushing up domestic raw material prices.
- Seasonal lower hydropower profitability expected in H2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Malika (Agarwal Investment) on Avagudem manganese mine benefits:** Management stated it will save INR200–INR400 per tonne, meet low-grade requirements for 4–5 years, and operations will start in 12–14 months after paperwork.
2. **Deepak Kumar (Individual Investor) on thermal shutdown timing:** Management clarified that the 47-day shutdown was a routine periodical maintenance of one 300MW unit during the rainy season (not peak demand time) and generation for the full year will exceed last year.
3. **Atharv (Individual Investor) on logistics cost quantification:** Management noted imported coal freights doubled from USD18–22 to USD36–38 per ton, adding pressure alongside high coking coal prices.
4. **Kamlesh Bagmar (Lotus Asset Managers) on power PPA tying:** Management stated 60% of saleable thermal power capacity is tied up, while Sikkim hydro power remains untied as a strategy.
5. **Priyansh Lakhotia (Individual Investor) on revenue loss from shutdowns:** Management quantified the 300MW thermal unit revenue loss at approximately INR130–INR140 crores, and noted steel billet/wire rod plants were idled to sell power externally instead.

### One-line takeaway
Sarda Energy & Minerals delivered record H1 EBITDA and PAT, anchored by strong energy realizations and a net-debt-free balance sheet, while progressing key brownfield power and coal mine expansions.
