# Godawari Power and Ispat Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious — Revenue grew 25% YoY, but profitability compressed significantly due to market iron ore sourcing, elevated coal/energy prices, and lower pellet realizations, forcing cuts to full-year production guidance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Cut. Iron ore mining production guidance reduced from 3.4 million tonnes to 2.5 million tonnes; iron ore pellet production guidance reduced from 4.4 million tonnes to 3.5 million tonnes.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more cautious on near-term margins for Q3 due to ongoing approval delays and high energy costs, though structural steel demand outlook remains positive.
- New topics or risks: Board approved Rs 100 crore capex for a container line, Rs 135 crore for a 3 GWh C&I BESS project, Rs 26 crore for 26% stake in SolarCraft Power India 32 Ltd, and Rs 100 crore for 50 EV dumpers. Formed Godawari Energy Solutions Ltd to bid for BESS EPC tenders. Decided to stop new inter-corporate loans and recall existing Rs 120 crore loans within 9–12 months.
- Last quarter's promises: 100 MW solar project delayed from September 2026 to December 2026. CRM complex land allotment executed (31.45 acres at Bidkin), but commissioning extended from December 2027 to March 2028. Steel melting shop expansion (5.25 to 5.75 lakh MT) completed and operational.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 1,632 crore (up 25% YoY).
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 3,382 crore (up 29% YoY).
- Q2 EBITDA Margin: 12.0%.
- Q2 PAT Margin: 5.2%.
- H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin: 15.7%; PAT Margin: 9.1%.
- Free Cash: Around Rs 900 crore as on date.

### Highlights
- High-grade pellet merchant sales were zero during the quarter due to captive consumption of mined ore, creating a ~Rs 1,000/tonne realization impact.
- Steel melting shop capacity expanded from 5.25 lakh MT to 5.75 lakh MT and commenced commercial production in Q2, enabling near 100% captive billet utilization of sponge iron.
- Re-branded group corporate identity under "ONE Godawari" and tagline "Powering New Horizons".
- The record of integrated steel plant being on hold continues as management confirmed no current plans to proceed due to pending water approvals.

### Guidance & outlook
- Revised FY27 production targets: Iron ore mining cut to 2.5 million tonnes (from 3.4 MnT); pellet production cut to 3.5 million tonnes (from 4.4 MnT). Ferroalloys and sponge iron production remain on track to meet annual targets (>50% achieved in H1).
- Q3 FY27 performance expected to be similar to Q2 FY27; marginal recovery expected in Q4 FY27, with normalized margins returning from Q1 FY28.
- Target 5 million–5.2 million tonnes mining in FY28 (~0.5 million tonnes market procurement), reaching 100% captive sourcing (6 million tonnes capacity) from October 2027 (FY29).
- BESS 20 GWh project: First container rollout targeted by Q1/early Q2 FY28 (June–July 2027); expected to achieve 20%–25% utilization (5 GWh) in Year 1 with ~5%–7% EBITDA margins.
- Maintained Vision 2030 target of 4x revenue, 3x EBITDA, and 3x PAT growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic crude steel production grew 2.9% YoY and finished steel consumption grew 7.6% YoY in H1 FY27.
- International iron ore prices trading at $90–$92/tonne due to weak Chinese demand; near-term average expected around $90/tonne with downside risk from Simandou ramp-up.
- Domestic pellet prices expected to hover in the Rs 9,500–Rs 10,500/tonne range ex-Raipur.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- DRI coal costs increased by Rs 1,000–Rs 1,500/tonne; energy/power costs rose by Rs 0.50–Rs 0.75/unit due to the West Asia conflict.
- Market procurement of iron ore fines added Rs 3,000–Rs 3,500/tonne in input costs on market-purchased volumes.
- Merchant pellet EBITDA margin compressed to Rs 500–Rs 700/tonne due to high input ore and fuel costs.
- Non-pellet steel supply chain (sponge to billets/wire rod) maintained EBITDA margins of Rs 6–Rs 7/kg.
- At full captive mining capacity and Rs 10,000/tonne pellet realization, management expects pellet EBITDA margin of over Rs 4,000/tonne (production cost ~Rs 5,500–Rs 6,000/tonne).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ari Dongri Beneficiation Plant: 5.4 MnT plant targeted for commissioning in Q3 FY27.
- CRM Complex (Maharashtra): Land executed (31.45 acres at AURIC Bidkin Industrial Area); capex backed by Rs 500 crore tied-up debt/incentives; commissioning expected by March 2028. Eligible for 9% gross SGST refund under Maharashtra Mega status policy.
- Container Manufacturing Line: Rs 100 crore capex approved alongside CRM complex; commissioning targeted for March 2027.
- BESS Projects: 20 GWh utility-scale project progressing for Q1 FY28 commissioning; new 3 GWh C&I storage project (261 kWh and 418 kWh units) approved at Rs 135 crore capex (commissioning March 2028).
- Renewable Power: 100 MW solar plant delayed to December 2026; 45 MW BESS solar storage expected by December 2026. Investing Rs 26 crore for 26% equity in SolarCraft Power India 32 Ltd for a 50 MW group captive solar plant.
- Fleet Decarbonization: Rs 100 crore capex approved for 50 EV dumpers across mines/plants.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Financial closure achieved for BESS project: Rs 2,795 crore credit facilities sanctioned (Rs 700 crore term loan, Rs 100 crore fund-based working capital, Rs 1,995 crore non-fund-based limits).
- CRM project funded via Rs 550 crore base facility.
- Inter-corporate loans stand at Rs 120 crore (earning 12%–15% interest); to be fully recalled over the next 9–12 months with zero future disbursements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in government tree-cutting permissions for overburden dumping grounds impacting captive mining output.
- Sustained high imported coal and energy prices driven by Middle East geopolitical conflict.
- Volatility in merchant pellet realizations and raw material fines pricing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sucrit D. Patil (Eyesight Fintrade):** *How are you managing risk of mining clearance delays and financial risk of large debt?*  
  **Management:** Tree cutting approvals are expected imminently (within days in Q3), allowing land clearing in 3–4 weeks and mine ramp-up in Q4. Of the Rs 2,795 crore BESS facility, funded debt is only Rs 800 crore (Rs 700 crore term loan). Supported by Rs 900 crore free cash and Rs 500 crore tied up for CRM, no cash strain is foreseen.
- **Tanuj Nangalia:** *How much margin drop was from loss of pellet premium vs pricing drop?*  
  **Management:** Zero high-grade pellet merchant sales occurred (captively used), creating an immediate ~Rs 1,000/tonne loss on those volumes. Current merchant pellet EBITDA is only Rs 500–Rs 700/tonne due to market fines and fuel costs.
- **Sahil Sanghvi (Monarch Networth):** *What is the strategy for the 3 GWh C&I BESS project and 20 GWh container off-take?*  
  **Management:** The 3 GWh line (Rs 135 crore capex) targets B2C/C&I DG-set replacement (261–418 kWh units) starting Dec 2027. For the 20 GWh grid line, formed an in-house EPC firm (Godawari Energy Solutions) to bid for SECI/NTPC tenders to consume 40%–50% of output internally.
- **Varun Mehta:** *What are expected BESS EBITDA margins?*  
  **Management:** BESS EBITDA margins will be 5%–7% (generating ~Rs 600 crore EBITDA on 15 GWh / Rs 13,000 crore turnover at 80% capacity), yielding a 3 to 3.5-year payback.
- **Nitin Shah:** *What is the current status and strategy on inter-corporate loans?*  
  **Management:** Currently Rs 120 crore is lent out at 12%–15%. All loans will be recalled within 9–12 months to fund ongoing capex, and no further corporate loans will be disbursed.

### One-line takeaway
Godawari Power's near-term earnings remain compressed by captive mining approval delays and high energy costs, but execution on its BESS, CRM, and mine expansion projects positions it for a margin recovery starting Q4 FY27.
