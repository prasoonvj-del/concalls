# Baroda Extrusion Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlights a debt-free balance sheet, strong order book, and clear expansion plans to capture demand in electrical and EV sectors.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 47.90 crore (vs Rs 38.05 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 3.68 crore (vs Rs 1.08 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 2.64 crore (vs Rs 0.88 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EPS:** 0.13 (vs 0.06 in Q1 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 20 crore (as of 31st July 2026).
*   **Capacity:** Currently ~145 tonnes/month; targeting >500 tonnes/month (6,000 tonnes/annum) post-expansion.

### Highlights
*   Company celebrated its 35th anniversary.
*   Reported as virtually debt-free with positive net worth.
*   Maintains 5 BIS licenses and a customer base of 700+.
*   Product portfolio includes solid, hollow, sections, nuggets, and customized copper/alloy products.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects 35-40% revenue growth YoY.
*   Management aims to reach Rs 1,000 crore revenue in 3-5 years.
*   Margins are expected to remain consistent with a 1-2% variance.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong demand tailwinds from electrical infrastructure, transformers, switchgears, data centers, and EV sectors.
*   Supplying to government entities like BARC and BHEL via the GeM portal.
*   Export opportunities are increasing, particularly for copper strips in the US market.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin improved to 7.7% (from 2.86% in Q1 FY26) driven by cost control and higher volumes.
*   Company does not hedge; it mitigates copper price volatility by buying raw materials against specific customer orders.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Planned Capex of Rs 20-25 crore for a heavy capacity hydraulic extrusion press and other machinery.
*   Expansion will occur on existing land adjacent to the current factory.
*   New capacity will allow for a wider range of copper and copper alloy products.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management stated the company is virtually debt-free.
*   Bad debt ratio is described as "very minor."

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Historical challenges mentioned include fire incidents, liquidity pressures, and global copper market crises (e.g., 1997).
*   Reliance on specific government orders (GeM) and potential volatility in copper prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is the revenue/EBITDA guidance?** A: Management expects 35-40% growth YoY and consistent margins.
*   **Q: Are you open to inorganic growth?** A: Management is open to mergers or takeovers if they fit the business and allow Baroda Extrusion to play a lead role.
*   **Q: What is the "right to win" to reach Rs 1,000 crore?** A: Management cited strong relationships, BIS licenses, an integrated team, and a debt-free status as key competitive advantages.
*   **Q: Are you supplying niche products for defense?** A: Currently supplying via GeM; management is under development for heavy hollow pipes specifically for defense requirements.
*   **Q: Why is current capacity utilization low?** A: Management clarified that current production is ~145 tonnes/month (including job work) and that they are now focused on upgrading machinery to improve efficiency.

### One-line takeaway
Baroda Extrusion is leveraging its debt-free status and 35-year legacy to pivot from a foundation-building phase to an aggressive expansion phase, targeting a 3-5 year revenue goal of Rs 1,000 crore.
