# Bombay Coated And Special Steels Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-27 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth, successful IPO listing, and aggressive debt reduction despite slight margin normalization.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 677.32 crore (vs Rs 491.51 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 280.24 crore (vs Rs 186.64 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 50.46 crore (Margin: 7.45%)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 17.82 crore (Margin: 6.36%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 19.91 crore
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 4.84 crore
- **H1 FY27 Sales Volume:** 68,952 metric tonnes (vs 59,545 metric tonnes in H1 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Sales Volume:** 27,835 metric tonnes (vs 22,854 metric tonnes in Q2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 6,403 (vs Rs 7,247 in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 7,319 (vs Rs 6,703 in H1 H2 FY26)
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Margin:** 11.61% (vs 14.96% in Q2 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Margin:** 12.41%
- **IPO Proceeds Raised:** Rs 183.05 crore
- **Working Capital Days (As of Sept 30, 2026):** Inventory days: 51, Receivable days: 41, Payable days: 10, Cash conversion cycle: 82 days
- **Return Metrics (H1 FY27):** RoE: 30.28%, ROCE (annualised): 17.74%
- **Long-term Vision (FY2031):** 0.5 to 0.6 million tonnes volume, ~Rs 5,000 crore top-line

### Highlights
- Successfully listed on BSE and NSE on November 5, 2026, raising Rs 183.05 crore.
- Procured land at Supa, Parner district in MIDC on November 19, 2026, to add an automotive steel vertical.
- Fully utilized IPO proceeds (Rs 125 crore for long/short-term borrowings and Rs 14-15 crore for Siemens) to repay debt, bringing debt-to-equity down to target levels.
- Top 5 customers contributed 52.27% of revenue in H1 FY27 (key clients include Amber Enterprises, Western Refrigeration, Haier Enterprises, Voltas, Frigoglass, PG Electroplast).
- *Continues record:* Successfully completed the IPO, took possession of the Supa MIDC land, and executed debt repayments as previously announced.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains historical top-line/volume growth in the range of 30% CAGR (and 15%-20% volume growth specifically).
- Management expects H2 FY27 sales volume to touch approximately 100,000 tonnes (~1 lakh tonnes).
- Management targets a debt-to-equity ratio below 1x by March 2027 closing (down from 2.48x as of September 30, 2026).
- Anticipates interest cost savings of Rs 3.5 crore to Rs 4 crore over the next 4 months.
- FY2031 vision targets 0.5 to 0.6 million tonnes in sales volume and ~Rs 5,000 crore top-line, split roughly 50-50 or 60-40 between appliances and automotive.

### Demand & industry commentary
- 87% of top line is derived from the white goods and home appliance industry (primarily ACs, refrigerators, washing machines, and water heaters).
- AC and refrigeration segments contribute 95%-96% of appliance revenue equally.
- Appliances industry growing at an 18%-22% CAGR; AC penetration in India is low at 8%.
- Business is seasonal; H2 typically accounts for 60% of volumes (a 40-60 split between H1 and H2).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross and EBITDA margins declined QoQ/YoY due to rising steel prices compressing percentage metrics, though absolute EBITDA per tonne remains robust.
- Authorized service partner of JSW Steel Limited and JSW Steel Coated Products Limited since 2018 for appliance-grade steel.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Incurred Rs 22 crore in growth capex from March up to Q2 FY27.
- Average annualized capacity utilization is ~62%, touching 95%-100% during peak season.
- New Supa facility expected to generate ~Rs 500 crore in annual top line once operational, pending steel mill approvals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity ratio stood at 2.48x as of September 30, 2026, targeted to drop below 1x by March 2027.
- Repaid ~Rs 140 crore total borrowings using IPO proceeds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted that within the white goods sector, service centers typically cater to only one primary steel mill to maintain transparency, restricting secondary mill tie-ups for the same vertical.
- Seasonality leads to lower volumes and margins in H1 compared to H2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Pakshal (IMAP India) on EBITDA per ton & debt:** Asked about the 10%-11% decline in EBITDA per ton and IPO fund utilization. Management clarified that absolute EBITDA per ton (Rs 6,403 in Q2) is robust and better than historical annual averages, and confirmed full repayment of Rs 140 crore of debt to bring D/E below 1x by March 2027. *(Did not dodge)*
2. **Vishal Verma on steel mill exclusivity:** Asked if the company can process for other mills alongside JSW. Management explained industry norms restrict servicing multiple mills within the same appliance vertical, though new verticals like automotive can use different mills. *(Did not dodge)*
3. **Yash Rai on capacity utilization and capex:** Asked about capacity utilization and future capex plans. Management stated annualized utilization is ~62% (touching 95%-100% in peak season) and asked the analyst to email for detailed yearly capex figures pending mill approvals. *(Did not dodge)*
4. **Deepak Poddar on volume growth drivers:** Asked about volume CAGR and mix evolution. Management guided for 15%-20% sustainable volume growth and a shift toward a 50-50 or 60-40 appliance-to-automotive revenue mix by FY2031. *(Did not dodge)*
5. **Pranay Zaveri on H2 guidance vs. customer outlook:** Pointed out that a major customer (Amber Enterprises) lowered guidance and asked about H2 profitability. Management clarified they expect ~1 lakh tonnes in H2 volumes and that PBT will benefit from interest savings despite percentage margin normalization. *(Did not dodge)*

### One-line takeaway
Bombay Coated & Special Steels reported solid H1 FY27 volume growth and successfully utilized its IPO proceeds to slash debt below 1x D/E, while laying the groundwork for an automotive steel expansion and targeting Rs 5,000 crore top-line by FY2031.
