# APL Apollo Tubes Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly sales volume, EBITDA, and net profit driven by premium branding and cost efficiencies despite regional disruptions.

### Key numbers
* **Sales Volume:** 960,000 tonnes in Q2 FY27.
* **EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 6,000 in Q2 FY27.
* **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Cash on books increased by 15% QoQ to Rs 1,600 crore as of September 2026 (Rs 1,400 crore in June 2026); total cash balance including other financial assets stood at ~Rs 2,500 crore.
* **Working Capital Days:** 0 days.
* **H1 FY27 Volume Growth:** APL Apollo brand and roofing products sales volumes grew 20% YoY in H1 FY27.
* **H1 FY27 CAPEX Spent:** Rs 400 crore (vs Rs 600 crore spent in full-year FY26).
* **H1 FY27 Free Cash Flow:** ~Rs 200 crore generated post-CAPEX (operating cash flow before CAPEX was ~Rs 700 crore).
* **Power Cost:** Rs 6.2–6.3 per unit in Q2 FY27 (targeted at Rs 5 per unit next year).
* **Freight Cost:** Reduced by Rs 150 per tonne QoQ in Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
* Recorded best-ever quarterly performance in sales volume, EBITDA, and net profit.
* Commissioned a specialized tube mill producing sizes from 250x250 mm diameter with 16 mm thickness, targeting Defence and Aerospace sectors (samples submitted to DRDO and ISRO).
* Roofing sheet quarterly capacity stands at 175,000 tonnes (700,000 tonnes annual capacity); achieved 135,000 tonnes volume in Q1 FY27 and targeting 175,000 tonnes in Q3 FY27.
* Apollo brand and roofing products contribute 92%–93% of overall sales volume.
* Light structures accounted for 25% of the product mix in Q2 FY27, impacting gross profit per tonne by ~Rs 500 QoQ due to lower realization.

### Guidance & outlook
* **FY27 EBITDA Guidance:** On track for 20% YoY growth on an absolute basis to reach at least Rs 2,200 crore; could be higher if UAE operations recover in H2.
* **EBITDA per tonne:** Rs 6,000 per tonne is deemed sustainable for H2 FY27, with Q3 FY27 expected to be above Rs 6,000 per tonne.
* **Volume Growth Guidance:** Management expects 15%–20% YoY volume growth for APL Apollo brand and roofing products in H2 FY27; overall volume guidance for low-margin segments (SG Premium) remains uncommitted due to raw material availability.
* **FY28 EBITDA Outlook:** Management estimates FY28 absolute EBITDA could reach Rs 2,500 crore to Rs 3,000 crore once UAE operations normalize and domestic steel supply stabilizes.
* **Free Cash Flow:** Expected to surpass last year’s Rs 1,300 crore level in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Domestic housing demand (accounting for 50% of end-use) remains strong despite rainfall deficits.
* Dealer and OEM order book sits at 75,000 to 100,000 tonnes out of total inventory of 150,000 to 200,000 tonnes.
* Secondary vs. primary steel price gap has widened, creating cash losses in secondary-aligned market segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Domestic raw material (HR coil) prices were ~Rs 60,000 per tonne, while APL Apollo Black pipes commanded ~Rs 70,000 per tonne, generating a conversion margin of ~Rs 10 per kg.
* Margin expansion driven by cessation of non-profitable volumes, scaling of roofing sheet capacity, freight cost reduction, and power cost rationalization.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Target capacity expansion: 7 million tonnes (greenfield plus brownfield) + 1 million tonnes de-bottlenecking capacity within the next two years (8 million tonnes total capacity targeted by FY28).
* UAE brownfield expansion: Expanding capacity from 300,000 tonnes to 500,000 tonnes (0.5 million tonnes) by March 2027 by adding two new mills producing high-margin products (target EBITDA of Rs 8,000–10,000 per tonne).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Net cash position stands at ~Rs 1,600 crore.
* Cash generation is expected to increase net cash balance to Rs 2,300 crore in H2 FY27, with Rs 200–300 crore earmarked for balance CAPEX in H2.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East impacting UAE plant operations (currently operating at an annualized run-rate of 100,000 tonnes vs 500,000 tonne capacity).
* Shortage of domestic steel supply due to steel plant shutdowns during November.
* Disruption in container trade and high ocean container freight rates impacting Indian export sales.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Bharat Shah (ASK):** *What caused the lower free cash flow generation in H1 FY27 compared to FY26?*  
  *Management Answer:* H1 included Rs 400 crore of CAPEX spending out of annual plans and full dividend payouts; H2 CAPEX will be low (~Rs 200 crore) with zero dividends, leading to FY27 FCF exceeding FY26's Rs 1,300 crore.
* **Sneha (Unifi Capital):** *Is the EBITDA per tonne of Rs 6,000 sustainable going forward?*  
  *Management Answer:* Yes, it is fully sustainable and expected to cross Rs 6,000 in Q3 FY27 due to brand pricing power, cost optimization, and favorable conversion spreads (Rs 10/kg over HR coil).
* **Omkar Ghugardare (B&K Securities):** *What is the current status of the UAE plant operations?*  
  *Management Answer:* UAE supply chains remain constrained by geopolitical issues affecting imports/exports; capacity of 0.5 million tonnes is ready without incurring cash losses, but operating visibility depends on external regional stabilization.
* **Shweta Dikshit (Elara Capital):** *Will margins contract if HRC steel prices fall from Rs 65,000 to Rs 55,000?*  
  *Management Answer:* Margins will remain resilient; structural margin gains came from product mix changes, cost reductions, and structural tubes for DRDO/ISRO. If steel prices drop, volume from low-margin SG Premium will pick up, maintaining absolute EBITDA.
* **Kumar Saumya:** *Will adding 2 million tonnes of capacity over 1.5 years drag down EBITDA per tonne due to fixed costs?*  
  *Management Answer:* No, new capacity additions are either brownfield or small greenfield setups with minimal incremental fixed costs, avoiding operating leverage hits seen in prior expansion cycles.

### One-line takeaway
APL Apollo delivered record profitability and expanded EBITDA per tonne to Rs 6,000 by shifting away from low-margin volumes toward branded and specialized structural products.
