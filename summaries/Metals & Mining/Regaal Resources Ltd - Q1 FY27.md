# Regaal Resources Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Completed major expansion, leading to higher value-added contribution and strong margin expansion despite transition-related integration impacts.

### Key numbers
- **Operating Income:** Rs. 202.15 crores (down 18% YoY due to deliberate reduction in low-margin trading activity)
- **Value-Added Revenue:** Rs. 80.53 crores (up 30.3% YoY)
- **Value-Added Margin:** 39.8% (expanded from 25.1% in Q1 FY26)
- **Export Contribution:** 10.4% of total (up from 4.9% in Q1 FY26)
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 30.98 crores (up 26.6% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 15.3% (expanded 540 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 13.33 crores (up 47% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 6.6% (improved 291 bps YoY)
- **Maize Crushing Volume:** 69,689 metric tonnes (vs. 64,770 metric tonnes in Q1 FY26)
- **Capacity Utilisation:** 71.4% (temporarily impacted by integration and planned shutdown days)
- **Net Debt:** Rs. 735.32 crores as of June 30, 2026
- **Project Outlay / Incurred:** Outlay of approx. Rs. 664 crores, with Rs. 552 crores incurred as of June 30, 2026
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 130 days

### Highlights
- Commissioned expanded crushing capacity on May 26, 2026, doubling capacity from 825 MT/day to 1,650 MT/day.
- New facilities added: 180 MT/day liquid glucose facility, 50 MT/day maltodextrin powder facility, and expansion of captive co-generation power plant from 7.1 MW to 15.8 MW.
- Established Regaal as the largest maize wet milling facility in eastern India.
- Deliberately scaled down low-margin trading activity from 19.5% to 3.3% of turnover in Q1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects annual maize crushing to exceed 4,00,000 tonnes for FY27.
- Value-added products expected to contribute 20% to 22% of total turnover in FY27.
- Management expects capacity utilisation to improve progressively from Q2 FY27 onwards.
- Net interest cost for FY27 guided to be around Rs. 39 to Rs. 40 crores (flat compared to FY26).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Bihar and Karnataka maize crops noted as having high recovery rates due to large grain size.
- Indian maize and starch exports remain highly competitive globally compared to Ukraine and USA prices.
- Demand for liquid glucose seeing strong uptake with major MNCs; maltodextrin powder sampling ongoing with MNCs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 80% to 95% of maize inventory is procured directly from farmers during the Rabi season (April-July) at doorstep FOR prices with no logistics cost.
- Effective borrowing cost contained due to interest subventions under Bihar's Industrial Investment Promotion Policy.
- EBITDA per ton and percentage margin guidance avoided by management due to volatility in raw material and finished goods prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Major CAPEX cycle is substantially behind; upcoming focus is on stabilization, ramp-up, and deleveraging.
- Upcoming product entries in FY27: Dextrose Anhydrous, Dextrose Monohydrate, Hydrol, and specialized modified starches (cationic starch, carboxy methyl starch, pre-gel starch, spray starch).
- Supporting infrastructure additions: 50,000 tonnes storage across two new silos, 50,000 sq. ft. maize godown, and 1.6 MW methane gas-based power plant.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands at Rs. 735.32 crores, peaking in H1 due to seasonal advance maize procurement and expansion funding, with expectations to reduce in H2.
- Inventory days elevated due to timing of capacity commissioning near the end of Q1 and seasonal advance procurement.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Integration and stabilization of doubled capacity required planned shutdown days (approx. 9 days lost in Q1).
- Volatility in external raw material (maize) and finished goods market prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shivam Gupta (Trinetra Asset Managers) asked about utilization run rates and value-added contribution:** Management stated annual crushing is expected above 4,00,000 tonnes, with value-added share moving from 3% to 20%-22% of turnover.
2. **Diwakar (Individual Investor) questioned the interest cost and subvention limits:** Management clarified that net interest cost for FY27 will be around Rs. 39-40 crores, backed by Bihar government subsidies on separate term loans.
3. **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) asked about low-cost advantages and inventory sourcing:** Management explained that procurement directly from Bihar farmers reduces freight costs, and Bihar maize offers superior recovery rates.
4. **Harsh Saraswat (Srujan Alpha Capital) asked about maize price trends and peak debt:** Management noted local doorstep maize prices remain stable around Rs. 22.5-23/kg, and H1 represents peak debt due to seasonal inventory build-up.
5. **Surya Narayan (Analyst) asked about production loss during Q1:** Management stated approximately 7,200 tonnes were lost across 9 shutdown days for plant integration.

### One-line takeaway
Regaal Resources successfully doubled its maize processing capacity to 1,650 MT/day in Q1 FY27, shifting focus toward margin-accretive value-added products and volume ramp-up while leveraging regional raw material cost advantages.
