# Jindal Steel Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record sales volume and revenue, coupled with successful commissioning of key infrastructure (slurry pipeline) and improved debt metrics.

### Key numbers
*   **Gross Revenue:** Rs 19,448 crore (up 44% YoY).
*   **Net Revenue:** Rs 16,704 crore (up 43% YoY).
*   **Steel Sales Volume:** 2.38 million tonnes.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 2,504 crore (up 34% YoY).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA/tonne:** Rs 10,529/tonne (up 5% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 771 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 14,786 crore (reduced by Rs 1,141 crore QoQ).
*   **Capex (Q2):** Rs 1,228 crore.
*   **Capex (H1):** Rs 3,187 crore.

### Highlights
*   Achieved highest-ever quarterly gross revenue.
*   Commissioned 192-km slurry pipeline; expected to save Rs 700/tonne in freight costs by eliminating ~500 trucks/day.
*   Successfully switched reheating furnaces and coating lines to coal gasification (syngas), eliminating reliance on propane/natural gas.
*   Net debt-to-EBITDA ratio improved to 1.51x (from 1.71x in June).
*   Cash and cash equivalents stood at Rs 7,237 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue Goal:** Aiming for Rs 1 lakh crore annual sales.
*   **Production:** Targeting 11 million tonnes for FY27; H2 target is 6 million tonnes (approx. 1 million tonnes/month).
*   **Capex:** Annual growth capex guidance of Rs 7,500 crore to Rs 10,000 crore.
*   **Outlook:** Management expects Q3 and Q4 to surpass Q2 performance.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Industry growth is ~10% YoY.
*   Domestic demand remains strong; export market is picking up.
*   Management sees significant opportunity in the European market (28 million tonnes deficit) if FTA/quota systems are relaxed.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Coking Coal:** Expecting $20-$25/tonne cost increase in Q3.
*   **Fuel/Energy:** West Asia conflict impact (diesel/freight) estimated at $4-$5/tonne.
*   **Captive Coal:** Currently meeting 50-55% of requirements; aiming for 65-70%.
*   **Iron Ore:** Kasia mine ramping up to 30,000 tonnes/day.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Product Mix:** Shifting toward higher value-added products; aiming for 65% flat products (including plates) and 35% long products.
*   **New Projects:** Pellet plant and DRI plant commissioning expected in June 2027.
*   **Remaining Capex:** Approximately Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 9,000 crore remaining on the total Rs 47,000 crore plan.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net debt-to-EBITDA maintained below 1.5x target.
*   Focus remains on disciplined capital allocation funded through internal accruals.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical instability (West Asia, Russia-Ukraine) impacting fuel and coking coal prices.
*   Input cost volatility (coking coal, freight).
*   Monsoon-related disruptions to mining operations (now resolved).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Slurry Pipeline:** Management confirmed commercial operations start immediately post-call, providing a direct Rs 700/tonne freight saving.
*   **Propane vs. Syngas:** Management stated they will *not* switch back to propane, as they have permanently modified burner systems for syngas and prefer the cost advantage.
*   **Captive Coal Economics:** Management confirmed they remain better off with captive coal despite auction premiums, citing raw material security during monsoon disruptions.
*   **Power Sales:** Management noted power sales are a "miniscule" part of the Rs 1 lakh crore revenue goal and occur only when there is surplus capacity.

### One-line takeaway
Strong operational performance driven by record sales and strategic cost-saving initiatives (slurry pipeline, coal gasification), with a clear path to deleveraging and capacity ramp-up.
