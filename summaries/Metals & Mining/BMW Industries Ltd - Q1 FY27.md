# BMW Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - underpinned by healthy profit growth, improving downstream utilizations, and continued progress on strategic expansion.

### Key numbers
- Operating income: INR166.0 crores (up 11.6% YoY)
- Gross profit: INR112.7 crores
- Gross profit margin: 67.9% (expanding by 536 bps YoY)
- Operating EBITDA: INR33.7 crores (up 7.1% YoY)
- Operating EBITDA margin: 20.3% (compared to 21.2% in Q1 FY26)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR19.1 crores (up 25.8% YoY)
- PAT margin: 10.8% (improving 92 bps YoY)
- Rolling mill annualized capacity utilization: ~83.5%
- Pipes and tubes annualized capacity utilization: ~40.1%
- Annualized ROCE as of June 30, 2026: 9.5%
- Annualized ROE as of June 30, 2026: 9.4%
- Net debt: INR468.9 crores (net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.57x)
- Long-term borrowings drawn for Bokaro project: INR202.4 crores
- Internal accruals deployed towards expansion: INR139.2 crores (total capital deployed INR341.6 crores)

### Highlights
- Achieved strong start to FY27 with healthy profit growth and improving utilizations across the downstream business.
- Rolling mill achieved ~83.5% annualized capacity utilization, while pipes and tubes operated at ~40.1% with sequential production growth.
- Capital drawdown for Bokaro is reflected in capital employed, though the plant has not yet begun contributing to returns as it is in a transitory capital deployment phase.
- Management stated trade receivables payment realization follows a standard model, with full pending amounts successfully received in the first week to two weeks of the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated earlier guidance of approximately 70% to 75% consolidated revenue CAGR over FY25 to FY28, supported by phased commissioning and ramp-up of Bokaro and strategic organic growth.
- Operating EBITDA and PAT are expected to grow at a CAGR of approximately 40% to 45% and 35% to 40% respectively over FY25 to FY28.
- EBITDA and PAT margins are expected to gradually stabilize at approximately 12% to 13% and 5% to 6% by FY28 as scale, integration, and operating leverage materialize.
- Commissioning of the color-coated segment at Bokaro begins in Q2 FY27, with cold rolling and Galvalume lines to be commissioned subsequently over the next 6 quarters.
- Pipes and tubes stable-state utilization of 65% to 70% is expected to be achieved by FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Steel consumption in India is growing at 10%+ (higher than GDP + inflation growth), driven by government infrastructure spending and private sector investments.
- Demand in the Eastern region is expected to grow via rural housing, warehousing, and industrial buildings.
- Government anti-dumping duties, Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and PLI schemes have created opportunities for domestic players and import substitution.
- MNRE recently passed an order encouraging the use of ZAM (Zinc-Aluminum-Magnesium) products for rooftop solar, supporting the coated products universe (Galvalume, Galvanized, and ZAM).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating EBITDA margin contracted YoY due to a sharp increase in fuel prices caused by the Middle East conflict.
- Fuel prices have since moderated considerably.
- Management has initiated discussions with customers to incorporate gas prices into price variation mechanisms to mitigate future fuel cost volatility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bokaro greenfield project commissioning commences from Q2 FY27, with color-coated line hot trials underway and capitalization expected in Q2.
- Total throughput capacity of the Bokaro facility is ~0.5 million tonnes.
- The company operates a balanced business model integrating traditional conversion with a proprietary supply model (sourcing input materials directly and supplying finished products).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total net debt stood at INR468.9 crores (net debt-to-equity at 0.57x), with INR202.4 crores as long-term borrowings for the Bokaro project.
- INR139.2 crores of internal accruals deployed towards expansion, taking total capital deployed to Bokaro to INR341.6 crores.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in fuel and power prices (arising from geopolitical conflicts) impacted operating EBITDA margins during the quarter.
- Maintaining consistent product quality domestically is a key challenge noted for color-coated suppliers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bhavesh (Individual Investor) asked about the FY27 revenue run rate and targets:** Management (Harsh Bansal) refrained from giving specific guidance for FY27, noting that guidance remains on track for FY28 as capacities ramp up over the next 6 quarters. *(Management did not dodge, but clarified policy on short-term guidance).*
2. **Bhavesh asked about the status of delayed trade receivables from the previous quarter:** Management confirmed that full pending receivables were successfully realized within the first 10-15 days of the quarter, as per their standard customer payment cycle. *(Directly answered).*
3. **Bhavesh asked if the company has a competitive advantage to pass on Iran-war-led fuel cost increases:** Management stated that increased costs affected everyone on a level playing field, and discussions are ongoing with customers for fair price variation mechanisms to avoid future volatility. *(Directly answered).*
4. **Sashwat Jalan asked about the demand-supply landscape for color-coated and galvanized products in the East given new capacities:** Management noted that steel consumption is growing above 10%, supported by infrastructure and private spend, and the East has catching up to do in economic growth, creating demand for roofing, cladding, and warehousing. *(Directly answered).*
5. **Bhavesh asked about vision for the next 24-48 months and potential shareholder rewards (buybacks/dividends):** Management (Sanjeev Sancheti) responded that capital allocation is currently focused on completing the large expansion at Bokaro, but they will consider stakeholder interests regarding buybacks at the right opportunity. *(Directly answered).*

### One-line takeaway
BMW Industries reported a stable Q1 FY27 with 11.6% revenue and 25.8% PAT growth, while keeping its multi-year CAGR growth guidance intact as it prepares to commission its Bokaro greenfield plant in Q2 FY27.
