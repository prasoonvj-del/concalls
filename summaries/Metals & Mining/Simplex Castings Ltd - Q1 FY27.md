# Simplex Castings Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-24 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (35% YoY), improved PAT margins, and an expanded order book supporting the "Simplex 2.0" growth strategy.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 60.95 crore (up ~35% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 11.52 crore (up ~25% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 18.89%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 6.86 crore (up ~45% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 11.25% (up from 10.48% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 150 crore+.

### Highlights
*   Management initiated "Simplex 2.0," focusing on scaling legacy capabilities in heavy castings, fabrication, and machining.
*   Strategic shift in product mix at Unit 1 towards faster-moving products to optimize working capital.
*   Order book expanded to Rs 150 crore+ (historical range: Rs 80-100 crore).
*   Management confirmed the company is on track to achieve its Rs 300 crore revenue target for FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management targets Rs 500 crore revenue by FY28, potentially via organic or inorganic growth.
*   Working capital cycle target: 60-70 days by FY28.
*   Defense and shipbuilding segments are expected to contribute 10-15% of business.
*   Management intends to maintain an EPC business revenue of ~Rs 100 crore annually, focusing on projects with 15-20%+ margins.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Strong demand witnessed across steel, power, railway, mining, defense, oil & gas, and shipbuilding.
*   Railways identified as a key new growth vertical; management expects to manufacture ~200 bogies per month once wagon orders are placed.
*   Management noted significant opportunities in the metallurgical sector (ferrous and non-ferrous).
*   Green hydrogen project tender was closed due to unfavorable GST/grant structure issues; the company did not participate in the reissued tender.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Management noted that while fabrication/railway business may have similar margins to current levels, they are selecting more complex/niche products to drive PAT margin improvement.
*   Interest cost for bill discounting platforms (RXIL/Invoice Mart) is ~5-5.5% annualized.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Current capacity utilization: 50-60%; management targets 80% by the end of the next financial year.
*   Capital work in progress: ~Rs 30 crore (deployed in working capital and expansion at Tedesara unit).
*   Fabrication capacity expansion planned to reach 18,000 tonnes (1,500 tonnes/month).
*   Foundry expansion is currently limited to debottlenecking (Rs 4-5 crore investment).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management stated the current fundraise is sufficient to reach the Rs 500 crore revenue target.
*   Working capital cycle improvement is a priority; management is utilizing bill discounting platforms to reduce realization cycles to 30-35 days post-dispatch.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Order book execution carries liquidated damages (5-10% if delayed).
*   Working capital cycle improvement is a gradual process.
*   EPC business is limited by banker guarantee limits.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Capacity utilization?* A: 50-60% currently; targeting 80% by end of next FY.
*   *Q: Working capital reduction strategy?* A: Shifting to faster-moving products (e.g., railway bogies) and using bill discounting platforms (RXIL/Invoice Mart).
*   *Q: Defense/Shipbuilding contribution?* A: Targeting 10-15% share; focusing on ordnance factories and sub-assemblies.
*   *Q: EPC business plans?* A: Targeting Rs 100 crore revenue; only interested in projects with 15-20%+ margins; limited by bank guarantee capacity.
*   *Q: Green Hydrogen project status?* A: Closed due to unfavorable GST/grant conditions in the tender.

### One-line takeaway
Simplex Castings is pivoting towards a "Simplex 2.0" strategy, focusing on faster-moving products, railway/defense diversification, and working capital optimization to support a Rs 500 crore revenue target by FY28.
