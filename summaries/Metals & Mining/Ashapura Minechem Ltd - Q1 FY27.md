# Ashapura Minechem Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-24 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Resilience shown in revenue growth and operational scale, but profitability impacted by geopolitical headwinds, high fuel costs, and elevated ocean freight.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Income from Operations:** Rs 1,616 crore (up 19.2% YoY from Rs 1,356 crore).
*   **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 188.9 crore (vs Rs 187.7 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 11.7% (vs 13.8% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PBT:** Rs 130.03 crore (down 1.4% YoY from Rs 131.84 crore).
*   **EPS:** Rs 12.07 (vs Rs 11.5 in Q1 FY26).
*   **Guinea Bauxite Volumes:** 2.34 million tonnes (vs 3.16 million tonnes in Q4 FY26 and 2.05 million tonnes in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA per tonne (Bauxite):** USD 6.3 (improved from USD 5.9 in Q4).

### Highlights
*   **Guinea Operations:** Contributed 84% of consolidated turnover (Rs 1,360 crore) and Rs 163 crore EBITDA.
*   **Port Infrastructure:** Boffa port capacity enhanced from 5 million tonnes to 8 million tonnes per annum. New jetty at GSM port under construction, expected to be operational by Q4 FY27, increasing capacity from 6 million tonnes to 10 million tonnes. Total combined port capacity to reach ~23 million tonnes.
*   **India Business:** Performed well across three verticals (Bentonite/White Minerals, Bleaching Clay, Advanced Ceramics). Advanced ceramic materials showed growth in profitability due to a favorable sales mix.
*   **Bauxite Washing Plant:** New plant (20,000 tonnes/day capacity) operational; aimed at sustainability and quality improvement rather than immediate EBITDA expansion.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Targets:** Management remains confident in achieving full-year targets with a potential variation of +/- 10%.
*   **FY28 Targets:** Confident in achieving or exceeding the 15 million tonnes bauxite volume target.
*   **Long-term:** Management expects bauxite sales to reach ~Rs 10,000 crore in the medium term.
*   **EBITDA per tonne:** Management expects levels to remain near USD 5.5–USD 6.0 in the short term, with hopes to return to historical levels near USD 10 over the medium to long term.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Bauxite:** Demand outlook remains positive; four new refineries in China are expected to create additional demand of 20–30 million tonnes.
*   **Freight:** Abnormal ocean freight rates and geopolitical uncertainty are pushing landed costs upward and impacting margins.
*   **Guinea Quota System:** Government expected to implement a bauxite export quota system before the end of 2026, which management views as positive for global prices and freight cost reduction.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margins:** Impacted by higher fuel, marine logistics, and raw material costs.
*   **Bleaching Clay:** Faced significant pressure due to a five-fold increase in sulphuric acid prices (currently >INR 30/kg).
*   **Logistics:** High freight costs remain an overhang on margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Planned capex of ~INR 200 crore across various projects and business verticals.
*   **Iron Ore:** Working on commercialization; currently in trial production stage.
*   **Bauxite Washing Plant:** Investment of ~USD 15 million (operate and transfer model).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   *Not explicitly discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical instability impacting Middle East and European markets.
*   Elevated ocean freight rates and vessel unavailability.
*   Volatility in customer demand.
*   Potential for lower-grade bauxite resource depletion if washing plant usage increases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you quantify the EBITDA impact of the washing plant?** A: Management stated it is EBITDA neutral/sustainability-focused, not a direct margin driver.
*   **Q: Any update on the Fako bauxite deposit/China Railway MOU?** A: Still in the study/development phase; no material progress to report until next year.
*   **Q: Why is the Q1 volume down sequentially?** A: Management cited challenges with vessel availability and high freight costs, noting Q2 is typically a weak quarter with growth expected in Q3/Q4.
*   **Q: Will the 15 million tonne FY28 target be met?** A: Management confirmed they are on track.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Guinea quota system?** A: Expected before the end of this year; management believes it will rationalize volumes and support prices.

### One-line takeaway
Ashapura Minechem is navigating significant logistics and geopolitical headwinds by focusing on operational efficiency and capacity expansion, while banking on a future bauxite quota system and value-added product growth to restore margins.
