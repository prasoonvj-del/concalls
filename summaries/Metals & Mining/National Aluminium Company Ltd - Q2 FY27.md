# National Aluminium Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Metals & Mining*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the best-ever half-yearly results for revenue, PBT, PAT, and EBITDA since inception.

### Key numbers
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 11,143 crore.
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 5,539 crore.
*   **H1 PBT:** Rs 5,160 crore.
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 3,837 crore (up 54% YoY).
*   **Q2 PAT:** Rs 1,835 crore (up 28% YoY).
*   **Cash Balance:** Rs 12,000 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Operational Performance:** Achieved best-ever H1 financial results.
*   **5th Stream Refinery:** Mechanical completion in advanced stages (90-92% complete); integrated trials and production start planned for late December 2026 or early January 2027.
*   **Pottangi Mines:** Facing local resistance and tree-clearing delays; management expects resolution in 2-3 months.
*   **Utkal Coal:** Production target of 4.8 million tonnes for FY27; H1 production was 2.1 million tonnes.
*   **Smelter Expansion:** Technology license signed with EGA (Dubai); DPR expected Jan/Feb 2027; board approval targeted for February; commissioning planned for Q1 FY31.
*   **Power JV:** JV with Neyveli Lignite Corporation (NLC) approved by DIPAM; DPR in progress to secure coal at production cost.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Alumina Production:** Targeting 2.45 million tonnes for FY27.
*   **Alumina Realization:** Expecting average realization of Rs 35,000/tonne in H2 (vs Rs 33,200/tonne in H1).
*   **Alumina Pricing:** Expecting $370-$380/tonne in export markets.
*   **Margins:** Management expects stable margins in H2 due to efficiency gains and cost control.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Global alumina market impacted by geopolitical tensions (Strait of Hormuz), increasing shipping/insurance costs.
*   Expect demand for alumina to increase as Middle East smelters ramp up production in H2.
*   Domestic alumina pricing is linked to export spot tender averages.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **H1 Cost Pressures:** Significant increase in caustic soda, CP coke, and fuel oil prices.
*   **Power & Fuel:** H1 power/fuel costs increased by Rs 317 crore YoY, driven by fuel oil usage and external power purchases due to coal supply/quality issues.
*   **H2 Outlook:** Management expects cost reduction of Rs 500-1,000/tonne in alumina production via improved techno-economics (e.g., reducing caustic soda consumption by 6-7 kg) and better coal availability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **FY27 Capex:** Actual H1 capex was Rs 800 crore; full-year guidance is >Rs 1,800 crore.
*   **Long-term Capex:** Smelter expansion and power plant investment estimated at Rs 25,000 crore over the next 4-5 years, with major spending in FY28-FY30.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Company remains cash-rich with Rs 12,000 crore in cash/deposits.
*   Interest income increased due to higher fund base and better deposit rates.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Shipping restrictions in the Strait of Hormuz impacting export logistics.
*   **Operational:** Local resistance at Pottangi mines and public hearing delays.
*   **Input Costs:** Volatility in caustic soda and CP coke prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Status of 5th Stream refinery and Pottangi mines?** Management stated 5th stream production starts Jan 2027; Pottangi issues are being addressed at the ministry/state level with a 2-3 month resolution timeline.
*   **Q: Why did power/fuel costs spike in H1?** Management cited railway rake shortages for coal, forcing reliance on fuel oil and external power purchases; expects normalization in H2.
*   **Q: Strategy for alumina pricing?** Management noted pricing is market-dependent; they are exploring long-term tie-ups with Middle East smelters to secure better realizations.
*   **Q: Impact of pay commission?** Management expects a 15% increase in employee costs starting Q4, but notes this will be offset by the retirement of high-salary employees and induction of fresh talent.
*   **Q: Will alumina prices stay high?** Management expects prices to hover around $370-$380/tonne, noting that while new capacity is coming online, demand remains tight.

### One-line takeaway
NALCO delivered record H1 performance and maintains a strong cash position, with management focused on commissioning the 5th Stream refinery and resolving mining bottlenecks to drive H2 efficiency.
