# Orkla India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue growth accelerated to 18.4% and volumes improved sequentially despite elevated input cost inflation.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from sale of products growth (Q2 FY27): 18.4% YoY
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 776 crore, up 19.4% YoY
- Volume growth (Q2 FY27): 2.9% YoY
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 124 crore, up 12.6% YoY
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 15.9% (moderated by ~100 bps YoY)
- Underlying EBITDA growth excluding PLI and Project Bolt (Q2 FY27): 11.6% YoY
- PAT growth (Q2 FY27): 19.2% YoY
- H1 FY27 Revenue from operations: Rs 1,435 crore, up 15.1% YoY
- H1 FY27 Volume growth: 2.3% YoY
- H1 FY27 Reported EBITDA margin: 16.6% (Adjusted/underlying: 17.2%)
- Spices category growth (Q2 FY27): 25.1% YoY (price increase ~20%)
- Convenience foods category growth (Q2 FY27): 7.7% YoY
- Domestic business growth (Q2 FY27): 20% YoY
- International business growth (Q2 FY27): 11.7% YoY (GCC up 22.7% YoY)
- Digital commerce contribution to domestic business: 10% in Q2 FY27 (up from 8.9% in Q1 FY27)
- Digital commerce rest of India revenue growth: 33.6% YoY in Q2 FY27
- Innovations: 38 new product launches in H1 FY27 (23 in Q1, 15 in Q2)

### Highlights
- Revenue growth accelerated for the second consecutive quarter, broad-based across categories and geographies.
- Project Bolt enters its final implementation quarter, driving digital commerce reach and expanding addressable markets in top 28 cities.
- Kerala distribution restructuring progressing as planned: convenience food sales productivity reached 1.27x Q4 FY26 levels; effective coverage up 1.1x; standalone modern trade spice volumes grew 16% QoQ in Q2; spice restructuring started in Kasaragod and Kannur districts.
- Wet batter business expanded from Bengaluru to Hyderabad 3 months ago, achieving 5% to 7% market share ahead of business plan.
- *Note on Record:* Management commentary and results align with the company's recent investor deck and results release, continuing strong revenue momentum despite margin compression from commodity inflation.

### Guidance & outlook
- No specific numerical revenue or EBITDA guidance was initiated or updated by management for the full year. 
- Management stated an ambition to grow beyond 10%-12%, indicating that with normalized inflation of 5%-7%, volume growth could reach 8% to 10%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Resilient Indian economy and healthy consumer demand supported domestic growth.
- Spices inflation driven significantly by chilli and coriander price increases over the previous year.
- Unorganized market constitutes ~60% of the South India spice market, while organized share has grown to ~40% over the last 5 years.
- Quick commerce (Zepto, Swiggy, Amazon) is scaling rapidly and proves margin-accretive at the gross margin level due to a higher mix of convenience foods.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Severe input cost inflation in key spices (chilli and coriander) led to price increases of over 20%.
- EBITDA margins moderated by ~100 bps YoY to 15.9% due to elevated input costs and investments in Project Bolt and brand building.
- Pure spices pricing directly tracks wholesale commodity movements with a ~10% markup, while blended spices follow a calibrated pricing approach.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing footprint consolidation and digitalization programs are ongoing, particularly for the Eastern brand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Surplus cash balances generate higher other income supporting PAT. (No specific debt or working capital numbers provided in the transcript).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Sudden speculative movements in spice commodity prices observed at the end of September.
- High inflation in pure spices has created some drag on volume growth.
- Substantial competition exists in the emerging sweets and meals segments from regional and national brands.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kunal Thanvi (Banyan Tree Advisors) on EBITDA calculation:** Management clarified that EBITDA excludes finance costs, foreign exchange fluctuations/losses, fair value losses on financial instruments (reported under other expenses per Ind-AS), exceptional items, and depreciation.
2. **Umang Shah (Investor) on spice pricing and commodity drivers:** Management explained that pure spices track wholesale market prices closely, while blended spices use calibrated pricing. Chilli and coriander were cited as the two key commodities driving inflation.
3. **Vishal Shah (Pravin Ratilal Wealth) on competitive pricing behavior:** Management stated that market leaders typically initiate price hikes, and peers follow within 21 to 30 days to protect processing and transportation margins.
4. **Yatin Mohane (Investor) on wet batter scale-up:** Management confirmed the wet batter business was successfully launched in Hyderabad 3 months ago, capturing a 5%-7% market share ahead of plan, and will be stabilized before expanding to other large cities.
5. **Saurabh Beria (Investor) on quick commerce pricing vs. competitors:** Management defended Orkla's pricing (e.g., MTR sambar masala), citing authentic recipes, fewer fillers/lower spice counts, and strong brand credibility.
6. **Manjit Bheriya (Investor) on quick commerce margins:** Management confirmed quick commerce is gross-margin accretive due to a higher mix of convenience foods, though investments in brand building and ads are ongoing.

### One-line takeaway
Orkla India delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 18.4% revenue growth and improving sequential volumes, successfully absorbing spice inflation through calibrated pricing while advancing strategic distribution and digital projects.
