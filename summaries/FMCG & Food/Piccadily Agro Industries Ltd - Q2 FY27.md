# Piccadily Agro Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue and branded volumes grew strongly YoY, but margins faced headwinds from heightened raw material costs, logistics pressures, and international headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Management maintained its earlier full-year expectations, stating they expect to achieve the guidance given earlier (previous quarter guided 60%-70% branded alco-bev growth, 60% company revenue growth, and 23%-24% EBITDA margins).
- **Tone and demand view:** Tone turned more cautious regarding cost pressures and international headwinds (UAE and US tariffs), contrasting with last quarter's unbridled optimism, though demand in North India for Q3 is viewed positively.
- **New topics or risks:** High-sea logistics costs, FTA pressures in international markets, UAE sales dropping to 10% of natural sales due to geopolitical situations, U.S. tariffs impacting sales, and discontinuation of standalone bulk vs IMFL breakdown in presentation disclosures.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Chhattisgarh plant scaling up (expected to reach ~70% utilization in H2) and barrel inventory crossing 96,000 en route to the 100,000+ target by FY27 end, remaining on track.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 277.9 crore (up 19.4% YoY)
- **Distillery revenue:** INR 271.3 crore (up 30.5% YoY; >97% of quarterly revenue)
- **H1 revenue from operations:** INR 548.4 crore (up 18.8% YoY)
- **H1 distillery revenue:** INR 477 crore (up 28.7% YoY)
- **H1 branded alcobev revenue (Indri, Camikara, Cashmir, Whistler):** INR 175 crore (up 29% YoY)
- **Q2 branded alcobev volumes:** Up 38% YoY (H1 volumes up 45% YoY)
- **Q2 EBITDA:** INR 53 crore (up 9.5% YoY)
- **Q2 EBITDA margin:** 21.7% (standalone)
- **Net sales revenue (NSR) for H1:** INR 9,274 per case
- **Maturation inventory:** Crossed 96,000 barrels (en route to 100,000+ by end of FY27)

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever second-quarter revenue from operations.
- Expanded footprint to 33 countries (adding Qatar and Oman) and 43 duty-free locations (23 in India, 20 international).
- Brands now reach 29 Indian states/UTs (including CSD) and over 25,700 retail and on-trade outlets domestically.
- Sugar business demerger regulatory process is underway.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 to be significantly stronger, driven by festive and wedding season demand and scale-up at Indri and Chhattisgarh.
- Management expects to achieve the full-year guidance provided earlier.
- Capacity utilization for H2 is expected to reach ~70% in Chhattisgarh and 70% to 75% in Indri.
- Management guides for Indri volume growth of 20% to 22% YoY over the next 3 to 4 years to reach ~5 lakh cases.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand pickup entering Q3, particularly for brown spirits in North India during the winter season.
- International markets faced headwinds: UAE sales dropped to 10% of natural sales due to geopolitical situations, and U.S. tariffs impacted sales (though market share was maintained).
- Indri holds an estimated 40% market share in Indian single malt based on internal numbers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Experienced exceptional pressure from heightened raw material costs (corn crop failures, droughts in South), transportation, and high-sea logistics driven by global geopolitical factors.
- Raw material cost pressures are expected to ease over the next 6 to 8 months with new crops arriving in December.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Malt capacity stands at 30 KL per day; combined ENA and ethanol capacity stands at 420 KLPD across both facilities.
- Chhattisgarh plant is scaling up with ethanol orders already allocated for FY27-28.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or working capital numbers provided, but management emphasized ongoing focus on improving capacity utilization, strengthening working capital, and converting investments into cash flow and profitability.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Exceptional cost pressures from raw materials, transportation, and high-sea logistics.
- International market volatility, FTA pressures, U.S. tariffs, and UAE geopolitical headwinds impacting exports.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Himanshu Shah (Dolat Capital) on discontinuation of bulk vs. IMFL revenue disclosure:** Management provided the numbers (INR 110 crore gross including excise / INR 92 crore net for Q2) and stated they would review disclosure formats.
2. **Himanshu Shah on moderation in growth:** Management cited international volatility and FTA pressures in Q1/Q2, which have now settled, providing a clearer growth path.
3. **Ruchika Bhatia (Alchemy Capital) on raw material pressures and margins:** Management explained weather/crop issues (corn/rice) and stated pressures should ease with December crops, maintaining earlier guidance.
4. **Kumar Saurabh (Scientific Investing) on barrel economics:** Management detailed barrel acquisition costs (USD 250 to USD 1,000; ~INR 30,000 to INR 35,000 including duties) and usage cycles (bourbon 3 times, wine barrels twice, lasting 4 to 7 years per cycle).
5. **Debashish Neogi (Abaan Dubai) on international headwinds:** Management acknowledged UAE sales are down to 10% of natural sales due to geopolitics and U.S. tariffs impacted sales, while noting global single malt market share is resilient at ~40%.

### One-line takeaway
Piccadily Agro delivered its highest-ever Q2 revenue, driven by a 30.5% growth in distillery revenue and a 38% surge in branded volumes, though margins faced headwinds from raw material inflation and international logistics pressures.
