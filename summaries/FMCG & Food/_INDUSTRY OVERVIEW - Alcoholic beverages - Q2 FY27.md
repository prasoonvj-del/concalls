# FMCG & Food - Alcoholic beverages: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The alcoholic beverages sector in Q2 FY27 witnessed robust volume growth and strong momentum in premiumisation across major players, though performance diverged significantly based on raw material exposure and regional regulatory challenges. While companies focusing heavily on the Prestige & Above (P&A) segment (such as Radico Khaitan, Allied Blenders, and Tilaknagar Industries) delivered record revenues and earnings expansion, others faced intense margin compression due to a "perfect storm" of record-high grain, glass, and packaging inflation (notably Associated Alcohols and Piccadily Agro). Backward integration, new product launches in craft/white spirits (tequila, RTD), and strong festive pipeline execution were common strategic themes.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the board report strong underlying consumer demand for premium and luxury offerings, supported by urbanization, younger demographics, and robust structural shifts toward white spirits (vodka) and brown spirits (single malts/brandy). Festive and wedding season demand heading into H3 is universally viewed as a major volume catalyst.
* **Divergence on Regional Markets & Segments:** 
  * *South India:* Tilaknagar Industries and Allied Blenders highlighted strong recoveries and massive volume potential in states like Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh due to upcoming liberalisation and market share gains. Conversely, Karnataka remained soft for Sula Vineyards due to excise shifts favoring beer.
  * *Mass vs. Premium:* While Sula Vineyards and Globus Spirits noted continued pressure on Economy and Popular segments due to aggressive competitor discounting, companies like Allied Blenders and Radico Khaitan saw healthy multi-category growth supported by brand refreshes and portfolio premiumization.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Inflationary Pressures:** Grain prices (maize, rice) reached all-time highs (e.g., maize at ~Rs 30,000/ton for Associated Alcohols; rice at Rs 27-28/kg and maize at Rs 23-24/kg for Globus Spirits), alongside spikes in glass bottles, PET, and logistics costs driven by global geopolitical tensions (West Asia crisis). Piccadily Agro and Associated Alcohols bore the brunt of these pressures, resulting in severe margin contractions.
* **Mitigation & Divergence:** Companies with strong pricing power, premium product mixes, and backward integration—such as Radico Khaitan (EBITDA margin at 20.4%, up 462 bps YoY), Allied Blenders, and Tilaknagar Industries—successfully protected or expanded gross margins. Sula Vineyards absorbed a temporary 280 bps gross margin decline due to 100% wine grape procurement from Harvest 2026, but anticipates cost relief starting Q4 FY27 with the upcoming bumper harvest.

## Capex & capacity
* **Capacity & Backward Integration:** Managements are aggressively investing in internal capabilities. Allied Blenders commissioned a captive malt distillery in Rangapur (Telangana) and operationalized a Moradabad bottling plant to save on franchise fees. Globus Spirits focused capital entirely on consumer business expansion, utilizing QIP proceeds for debt reduction and whiskey expansion. Tilaknagar Industries and Piccadily Agro continued scaling up distillation and malt maturation barrel inventories (Piccadily crossing 96,000 barrels).
* **Hospitality & Tourism:** Sula Vineyards operationalized its Nashik banquet facility (Vintage Room) and finalized plans for a 35-key hotel at York Estate (Rs 25 crore capex). Fratelli Vineyards, however, chose to defer its vineyard tourism hospitality project to focus bandwidth on its core winemaking and RTD scaling.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Radico Khaitan:** Upgraded its Prestige & Above volume growth guidance for FY27 from earlier levels to 30% following record Q2 performance.
* **Associated Alcohols & Breweries:** Shifted tone from positive to cautious, citing "unnatural" 30-year high raw material price spikes and heavy marketing investments.
* **Piccadily Agro:** Turned more cautious regarding export cost pressures, high-sea logistics, and international headwinds (UAE sales dropping to 10% of natural sales due to geopolitical factors), despite maintaining full-year guidance.
* **Fratelli Vineyards:** Deferred broader revenue growth guidance for FY27/FY28 until Q3 review and slowed down its vineyard tourism project.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Festive & Wedding Offtake:** Actual translation of anticipated North Indian winter and festive demand into primary and secondary volume growth, especially for brown spirits and RTDs.
* **Raw Material Stabilization:** Easing of grain and packaging inflation as new crop arrivals enter the market from December/January onwards (noted by Globus Spirits, Piccadily Agro, and Sula).
* **Regulatory & Policy Updates:** Progress on state-level price revisions (Telangana and Andhra Pradesh for Tilaknagar/Fratelli) and developments in Tamil Nadu market liberalisation.
* **Malt Maturation & New Launches:** Scaling progress of newly launched luxury portfolios (e.g., ABD Maestro, Piccadily's Indri, AABL's Arcana Tequila, Sula's wine-based RTD).

## Company one-liners
* **Allied Blenders and Distillers:** Delivered record Q2 volumes crossing 10 million cases with 15.8% revenue growth, driven by aggressive premiumisation and backward integration.
* **Associated Alcohols & Breweries:** Aggressively diversifying into premium segments (Tequila/RTD), but near-term profitability is severely constrained by record-high input costs and heavy brand-building investments.
* **Fratelli Vineyards:** Reported a 10% revenue rise and turned EBITDA positive in H1 FY27, driven by strong RTD scaling, even as regulatory headwinds in UP and Karnataka weighed on the premium wine segment.
* **Globus Spirits:** Achieved 15% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, bolstered by consumer division strength and a de-risked balance sheet following a Rs 200 crore QIP.
* **Piccadily Agro Industries:** Delivered its highest-ever Q2 revenue on 30.5% distillery growth, though margins faced headwinds from raw material inflation and international logistics pressures.
* **Radico Khaitan:** Delivered its strongest quarter in history with record revenues and a 36% jump in P&A volumes, prompting an upgrade to its FY27 volume growth guidance to 30%.
* **Sula Vineyards:** Delivered 34% profit growth as premiumization and cost cuts offset temporary grape cost pressures, setting the foundation for margin expansion from Q4 FY27.
* **Tilaknagar Industries:** Reported robust Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 173% YoY driven by solid secondary volumes and Imperial Blue integration while navigating packaging and ENA inflation.
