# Sundrop Brands Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, margin expansion, and successful execution of premiumization and innovation strategies.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 463 crore (up 21% YoY); H1 revenue Rs 891 crore (up 18% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Expanded by 240 bps (normalized) or 370 bps (adjusted) YoY.
*   **E-commerce Growth:** 43% YoY (contributes 19% of total sales).
*   **Segment Growth (Q2):** Sundrop (19%), Del Monte (26%), B2C business (18%).
*   **ACT II (Popcorn):** RTE segment grew 38% in value and 23% in volume.
*   **Italian Portfolio:** Volume growth of 30% (Q2).
*   **NPD Contribution:** ~6% of overall sales from products launched in the last 2 years.

### Highlights
*   Management reported broad-based growth across core categories and emerging channels.
*   Completed Sales Force Automation covering 400,000 outlets; 80% of outlets covered regularly in Q2.
*   Launched 24 new products in Q2.
*   *Record Note:* The company continues its strategy of focusing on core categories and premiumization, consistent with previous records.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management is confident in sustaining growth momentum, citing 2 years of consistent performance.
*   Targeting 4-5% volume growth in the Edible Oil segment.
*   Aiming to maintain A&S (Advertising to Sales) ratio at 9-10% for core categories.
*   Management expects potential price increases in Q4 due to annual contract renewals in B2B and export segments.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Consumption environment remains strong with stable FMCG demand despite inflationary headwinds.
*   Consumers are becoming more discerning, showing willingness to spend on trusted brands and convenience.
*   Quick commerce is identified as a structural tailwind for snacking and impulse consumption.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Gross margin increased by 19% to Rs 107 crore.
*   Foods business margin improved by 70 bps.
*   Management implemented calibrated price increases to offset inflation; noted that raw material environment has recently remained range-bound.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   The company is utilizing a capital-efficient model, combining internal manufacturing with third-party (3P) capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Operating cash flow was negative (~Rs 49-50 crore) due to strategic inventory build-up (corn, oats) to mitigate supply uncertainty.
*   DSOs (Days Sales Outstanding) remained constant.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Peanut butter volume growth remains under pressure, though management noted it is stabilizing (-1% in Q2 vs -8% in Q4 FY26).
*   Potential volatility in commodity prices, though management currently views the environment as manageable.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is driving the 43% e-commerce growth?** Management: Driven by structural tailwinds in quick commerce and internal investments in tech/performance marketing, which have allowed the company to gain share.
*   **Q: Why was operating cash flow negative?** Management: Due to strategic inventory build-up in corn and oats to mitigate potential supply uncertainty; expects normalization in Q3.
*   **Q: What is the status of the peanut butter category?** Management: Saliency of new value-added products (chocolate/high-protein) reached 12.4%; decline rates have stabilized to -1% in Q2.
*   **Q: Is the 30% volume growth in the Italian portfolio sustainable?** Management: Driven by pasta (33% growth); normalized growth is expected to be closer to 25-26%.
*   **Q: Outlook for Edible Oil margins?** Management: Strategy is to protect margins while targeting 4-5% volume growth; does not anticipate immediate margin hits in the next 3-4 months.

### One-line takeaway
Sundrop Brands is successfully leveraging its diversified portfolio and e-commerce acceleration to drive profitable growth, while managing inflationary pressures through calibrated pricing and operational efficiencies.
