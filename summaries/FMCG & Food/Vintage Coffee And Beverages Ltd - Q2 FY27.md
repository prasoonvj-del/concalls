# Vintage Coffee And Beverages Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Continued healthy revenue growth, expanding operating margins, and strong business momentum with capacity utilization at peak levels.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 222.97 crore (up 64.4% YoY, 38.5% QoQ; compared to INR 135.61 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **Gross margin:** 27.6% (up 293 bps YoY, down 133 bps QoQ)
- **EBITDA:** INR 42.16 crore (up 88.8% YoY, 33.5% QoQ; compared to INR 22.33 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA margin:** 18.9% (up 244 bps YoY)
- **PAT:** INR 28.06 crore (up 57.3% YoY, 34.9% QoQ; compared to INR 17.83 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **PAT margin:** 12.6%
- **Effective tax rate:** 26.1%
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 383.97 crore (up 61.9% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Gross margin:** 28.1% (up 266 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 73.74 crore (up 82.7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA margin:** 19.2% (up 219 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 48.85 crore (up 52.3% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Operating cash flow:** INR 51.16 crore
- **Q2 Production & Sales:** Production at 2,737 metric tonnes; Sales at 2,581 metric tonnes

### Highlights
- Enhanced installed production capacity stands at 11,000 metric tonnes per annum (following commissioning of 4,500 metric tonnes in end-March 2026).
- 1.3 crore promoter shares (50% of total pledged shares) pledged with PNB were successfully released during Q2.
- Amalgamation of wholly owned subsidiaries (Vintage Coffee Private Limited and Delecto Foods Private Limited) accounted for from October 1, 2025; prior-year figures not restated.
- Revenue mix is approximately 55% small packs and 45% bulk; geographic mix is 85% exports and 15% domestic.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 margins to remain broadly in the same range with marginal improvements.
- Management expects to achieve an ~80%–85% growth rate overall in FY28–FY29 as freeze-dried coffee (FDC) operations scale up.
- FDC Phase 1 trial production planned for August, with commercial production expected from September 2027 (H2 FY28); ~70% capacity utilization planned for FDC in H2 FY28.
- FDC Phase 2 commercial operations expected to start in April 2029, with full capacity in FY30.
- Volume guidance for spray-dried and agglomerated coffee is 10,500 metric tonnes for FY27, backed by firm customer commitments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand for freeze-dried coffee is strong; letters of intent received for 100% of planned FDC capacity.
- Indian instant soluble coffee market is growing at ~12% to 14% annually.
- Coffee bean prices expected to remain stable or decline slightly; India expected to source 65%–70% locally and 30%–35% from Uganda and Indonesia.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher other expenses driven by temporary shipping/ocean freight cost increases due to geopolitical situations, which are passed on to customers.
- FDC margins expected to be 2%–3% higher than existing levels; further 1%–1.5% increase expected with Phase 2.
- Chicory crop shortage limited volume growth to ~5%, while prices moved up by 4%–5%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total estimated capex for the first 5,500 metric tonne FDC project is INR 550 crore, out of which INR 220 crore has been incurred till date; another INR 150 crore to be spent in H2 FY27.
- Next phase of 5,500 metric tonne FDC (brownfield project) estimated at ~INR 350 crore capex.
- Asset turnover for the FDC business estimated at 1.4x (turnover of INR 670 crore to INR 710 crore) with ROCE similar to the existing business.
- 22 acres acquired from the Government of Telangana at TGIIC Food Processing Park for FDC expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows continue to remain positive (INR 51.16 crore generated in H1 FY27).
- Board approved fundraising/equity dilution up to INR 325 crore as a fallback option due to negative pledge clauses on foreign currency loans from European banks restricting Indian bank lines; alternative funding options are actively being explored.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations impacting international logistics and container availability could cause shipment delays.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gaurav Shukla (Finvesters) asked about volume vs. realization growth:** Management clarified that Q2 revenue growth was primarily driven by volume growth rather than higher realizations.
- **Jatin Damania (SVAN Investments) inquired about FY28 growth trajectory:** Management stated FDC commercialization in H2 FY28 will contribute 50% utilization in the second half, leading to full utilization and high growth in FY29.
- **Ishan Modi (Individual Investor) questioned the shift toward equity dilution:** Management explained that foreign bank loans carry negative pledge clauses preventing Indian bank borrowings, making equity dilution a backup option if alternative financing isn't secured.
- **Umesh Gupta (AlphaOptimus PMS) asked about the B2C strategy timeline:** Management stated that large-scale B2C expansion (domestic and international) will be explored from FY30 onwards after establishing the second FDC line, though they currently sell on e-commerce and supply private-label brand owners.
- **Janav Shah (Individual Investor) asked about FDC project delays:** Management clarified that the 1-2 month shift (from June-July to August-September) was solely due to extra time required for ESG compliance formalities demanded by European lenders.

### One-line takeaway
Vintage Coffee delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with revenue up 64.4% YoY and EBITDA up 88.8% YoY, while progressing firmly on its upcoming 5,500 MT FDC capacity expansion targeted for commercial production in H2 FY28.
