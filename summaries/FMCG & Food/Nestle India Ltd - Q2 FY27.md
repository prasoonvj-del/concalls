# Nestle India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-07 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume-led growth, expanding rural reach, accelerated premiumization, and effective cost optimization.

### Key numbers
- Penetration levels for core brands (MAGGI, NESCAFÉ, KITKAT) are in the mid-50s annually.
- Distribution points increased by close to 4 times since 2021; added ~500,000 retail outlets over the last 5 years.
- Premium portfolio contribution increased from 11% to 14%, growing ~500 bps ahead of overall growth.
- Advertising spends increased by close to 40% YoY over recent quarters.
- Cost-saving efficiencies reached 2.6% in 2025 (compared to 1.8%-1.9% normally).
- Capex exceeded Rs. 6,400 crore (64 billion) over the last 5 years; recent investments include >Rs. 170 crore for a new MAGGI line and ~Rs. 225 crore for a new Munch line.
- Total Shareholder Return (TSR) exceeded 15% in the last one year.

### Highlights
- Delivered consistent double-digit growth over the last 5 years, supported by volume recovery and broad-based execution.
- India is the largest market globally for MAGGI and KITKAT.
- NESCAFÉ recorded its 20th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth.
- E-commerce and q-commerce channels are outperforming market growth, acting as launchpads for innovations and premiumization.
- Out-of-home business serves 535 million customers and is the second largest for Nestlé in Asia, Africa, and Oceania.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they do not provide forward-looking numerical projections.
- Management remains confident in the medium-to-long-term secular growth story, driven by low category penetration and an expanding affluent consumer base.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted a slight slowdown in overall market growth as reported by Nielsen, alongside food inflation pressures.
- Rural markets have shown relative resilience compared to urban areas.
- Out-of-home food and beverage consumption has nearly doubled over the past decade.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Accelerated cost-saving programs (2.6% efficiency in 2025) are funding increased brand investments.
- Headwinds noted in energy, packaging, oil, shipping disruptions, and currency volatility.
- Management stated an intent to maintain margins in line with past track records through structural efficiencies.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continued focus on "Make in India" manufacturing and direct distribution expansion.
- Invested over Rs. 6,400 crore in capex over the last 5 years; commissioned new lines for MAGGI (>Rs. 170 crore) and Munch (~Rs. 225 crore).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash generation supporting reinvestment in brands and capacity.
- Shareholder base exceeds 500,000.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slowdown in overall market growth as reported by Nielsen.
- Food inflation and cost volatility across energy, packaging, oil, shipping, and currencies.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) asked about the performance of new businesses (cereals, pet food, Nespresso) and the drivers of recent scale-up.** Management answered that strong brands, people, disciplined execution, and cost optimization are driving the growth flywheel.
- **Latika Chopra asked about the sustainability of double-digit volume growth and margin outlook.** Management declined forward-looking estimates, expressed confidence in secular volume opportunities, and stated an intent to maintain historical margins.
- **Arnab Mitra asked about milk and nutrition growth and functional food participation.** Management highlighted science-backed product launches and a measured, thoughtful approach to new health trends.
- **Avi Mehta asked about rural reach expansion and infant nutrition consumer acceptance following sugar concerns.** Management stated rural reach is an ongoing journey with significant headroom, and that both zero-added-sucrose (ZAS) and regular CERELAC variants have consumer traction.
- **Nihal Jham asked about nutrition volume growth and confectionery growth drivers.** Management attributed confectionery success to brand strength, Visi cooler availability, and right price-point packaging.

### One-line takeaway
Nestlé India is sustaining accelerated, volume-led growth and premiumization through robust brand equity, direct distribution expansion, and disciplined cost-efficiency programs.
