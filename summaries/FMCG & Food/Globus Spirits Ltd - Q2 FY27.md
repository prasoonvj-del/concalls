# Globus Spirits Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Continued progress in the consumer business, strengthening of the financial position, and favourable industry tailwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Manufacturing Revenue (Q2):** INR 455 crore
- **Manufacturing Volumes (Q2):** 55.26 million litres
- **Manufacturing EBITDA (Q2):** INR 25.4 crore
- **Manufacturing EBITDA per litre:** INR 4.6 (Q2), INR 5.59 (H1), trailing 12-month at approx INR 6.6
- **Prestige & Above (P&A) Revenue:** INR 59.9 crore (up 54% YoY), INR 114.9 crore (H1)
- **P&A Volumes:** 0.46 million cases (up 64% YoY), 0.88 million cases (H1)
- **P&A EBITDA loss:** Narrowed to INR 0.2 crore
- **Regular & Others (R&O) Revenue:** INR 242.8 crore (up 14% YoY), INR 499.2 crore (H1)
- **R&O Volumes:** 4.24 million cases (up 16% YoY)
- **R&O EBITDA:** INR 38.6 crore (up 4% YoY), INR 82.6 crore (H1)
- **Total Revenue (Q2):** INR 758 crore (up 15% YoY)
- **Total Revenue (H1):** INR 1,541 crore (up 11% YoY)
- **Total EBITDA (Q2):** INR 63.8 crore (up 2% YoY)
- **Total EBITDA (H1):** INR 143.3 crore (up 17% YoY)
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** INR 139 crore (compared to INR 270 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Debt Reduction:** INR 200 crore reduction between March and September (utilizing INR 155 crore from the INR 200 crore QIP by September end)
- **Debt-to-EBITDA:** Improved from 2.63x (March end) to approx 2x (September end)
- **Power and Fuel Cost:** INR 6.3 per kg (Q2), total INR 60.43 crore for the quarter.

### Highlights
- Completed a INR 200 crore QIP during the quarter, strengthening the balance sheet and reducing debt.
- Prestige & Above (P&A) segment moved close to EBITDA breakeven, with 5 profitable states across a 12-market footprint.
- Introduced 'DWAAR' (Indian blended luxury whiskey) initially in Haryana, Chandigarh, and Goa.
- Uttar Pradesh continues to expand the R&O growth base alongside the strong Rajasthan market.
- Estimated interest savings of approx INR 6 crore in H2 FY27 (INR 11 crore to INR 12 crore on an annualized basis) from debt repayment.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its annual EBITDA guidance of INR 5 to INR 7 per litre for the manufacturing segment.
- R&O EBITDA margins guided to remain in the 16% to 18% range.
- Planned entry into 2 new states and 5 additional duty-free channels, alongside targeted Canteen Stores Department (CSD) entry in Q3 FY27 for P&A.
- Re-entering West Bengal in the next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Reduced availability of sugar-based ethanol in ethanol supply year '26-'27 is expected to create greater procurement opportunities for grain-based producers.
- Enhanced broken rice availability expected due to FCI reducing permissible broken grain content in raw rice from 25% to 10% progressively from Kharif '26-'27.
- Greater FCI availability is expected to moderate pressures on rice and maize prices.
- UP shifting country liquor from molasses-based to grain-based is driving volume growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 experienced higher raw material and fuel costs due to seasonality ahead of the new crop season and scheduled plant maintenance.
- End-Q2 prices: Rice at approx INR 27-28 per kg, maize at approx INR 23-24 per kg.
- Packing material inflation impacted R&O margins to some extent due to global petroleum price trends affecting packaging.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital is 100% focused on growing the consumer business; no capacity expansion planned in Rajasthan.
- Manufacturing capacity utilization expected to be meaningfully higher in the coming year (previously consistently between 80% and 85%).
- Remaining liquid QIP proceeds to be used for additional bottling and whiskey-related expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows moderated due to an increase in trade receivables driven by higher business volumes.
- Debt-to-EBITDA improved to ~2x from 2.63x in March.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in quarterly commodity and fuel prices during Q2.
- Packing material inflation driven by petroleum price trends.
- Delhi excise market experiencing compressed industry volumes due to porous borders, leading to re-evaluation of plans.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit Asset Management)** asked about key states for P&A and how the INR 3/litre maize ethanol price cut impacts the INR 5-7/litre EBITDA guidance. Management stated growth will be broad-based across 7-8 profitable states, and raw material flexibility (FCI and broken rice) protects the INR 5-7 guidance.
- **Himanshu Shah (Dolat Capital)** enquired about the sharp drop in retail/manufacturing EBITDA and maize share. Management reiterated that Q2 seasonality and commodity spikes cause temporary inflation, and maize share in ethanol may drop from ~60% in H1 depending on profitability.
- **Hitaindra Pradhan (Maximum Capital)** asked about R&O margins and ENA price risks from peer switching. Management guided 16-18% margins for R&O and noted that regional constraints, local demand, and feedstock switching mitigate ENA glut risks.
- **Nitin Awasthi (InCred)** asked about UP profitability timing and ethanol industry opportunities. Management stated UP is on an improving journey toward optimal profitability and confirmed strong volume offtake expected in the upcoming ethanol year.
- **Imran Khan (Longbow India)** asked about Rajasthan market dynamics, capacity utilization, and Delhi market performance. Management confirmed stable single-digit growth in Rajasthan with a ceiling on capacity, and noted Delhi is currently uninteresting due to compressed industry volumes.

### One-line takeaway
Globus Spirits reported 15% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, bolstered by strong consumer division performance and a de-risked balance sheet following a INR 200 crore QIP, while maintaining its annual manufacturing EBITDA guidance of INR 5-7 per litre despite seasonal input cost pressures.
