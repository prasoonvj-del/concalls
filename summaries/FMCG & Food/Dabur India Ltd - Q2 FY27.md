# Dabur India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Management highlighted robust top-line growth and GT consolidation success, but acknowledged margin pressure from supply chain inflation and an internal "red" rating on strategic M&A targets.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue Growth:** 11.6% YoY
- **India Standalone Revenue Growth:** 8.9% YoY
- **India FMCG Revenue Growth:** 10.7% YoY (Volume growth: 5.5% YoY, Pricing growth: 5.2% YoY)
- **International Business Growth:** 20% YoY
- **India Standalone Operating Margin Dilution:** 10 bps YoY
- **Segment Revenue Growth (YoY):** Healthcare up 6.9%; F&B and HPC grew in double digits.
- **Premium Portfolio Salience:** 20% of total revenue (up from 17% over 18 months), growing at 4X overall company growth rate.
- **Digital Spends:** 43% of total media spend.
- **General Trade (GT) Consolidation:** 43% reduction in GT distributors under Project Saksham; reduced distributor attrition by 80%, increased throughput by 2X, and boosted ROI by 30%.
- **Project Samriddhi Savings:** Delivered >Rs 115 crore over the past 18 months.
- **Cash War Chest:** ~Rs 6,000 crore available in India for capital allocation/M&A.

### Highlights
- Completed an 18-month assessment of its 3-year vision: achieved green ratings on portfolio simplification (6 brands, 350 SKUs eliminated) and Project Saksham, but rated strategic M&A as "red".
- Initiating a mid-term strategic vision exercise (Vision 2031) 1.5 years ahead of schedule; results to be shared in 6–8 months.
- Launched the internal "Ignite" program across 4 focus pillars: energizing core/scaling stars, execution excellence, future-ready portfolio, and operating efficiencies.
- Sesa acquisition integration scheduled for December 1, 2026, with retrospective effect from April 1, 2026.
- Dabur Ventures made its first minority investment in D2C brand RAS (October 2025/early 2026); incubating internal D2C nutraceutical brand "Siens by Dabur".
- *Pattern check*: Continues the pattern of resilient top-line growth and double-digit PAT performance noted in recent quarter results.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Guidance:** Reassourced full-year consolidated revenue growth guidance at double-digit levels.
- **A&P Spends:** Planning an incremental 150 to 200 bps allocation towards consumer-facing brand investments.
- **Premiumization:** Target to raise premium portfolio contribution to 23% over the next 18 months.
- **Efficiency Target:** Aiming for 1.5x savings of Project Samriddhi (targeting Rs 170 crore to Rs 200 crore) via project IRIS (continuous replenishment and forecasting).
- **Rural Reach:** Targeting direct coverage of over 100,000 geotagged villages.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Macro environment remains challenging due to Middle East conflicts driving crude prices and broader hyperinflation, restricting overall FMCG consumption growth.
- Urban quick commerce channel is performing strongly and acts as a complementary platform for launching premium SKUs and innovations.
- Alternate channels (Modern Trade and E-commerce) sustained strong double-digit growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating profits faced headwinds from input cost inflation, higher supply chain expenses, and elevated international freight/logistics costs.
- India standalone margin contraction was limited to 10 bps YoY due to aggressive cost mitigation in the HPC segment.
- Management expects potential gross margin expansion next fiscal year if geopolitical and inflation conditions soften.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ramping up the "Retail Next" program to cover 40,000 high-weighted GT stores, targeting sales growth 1.5x higher than general GT.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Retains a strong balance sheet with ~Rs 6,000 crore cash war chest in India for synergistic M&A.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slower growth in health supplements (Chyawanprash), requiring format changes (gummies) and usage occasion repositioning.
- Delayed inorganic growth/M&A progress due to high market valuations and strict financial/synergistic parameters.
- Supply chain hyperinflation and elevated freight rates weighing on international business margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (UBS):** Asked about the "red" status on M&A targets and D2C product strategy.  
  *Answer:* Management will not rush M&A to meet arbitrary deadlines; deals must be category-adjacent and sensibly valued. Building internal D2C capabilities like Dabur Shop and "Siens by Dabur" while integrating Sesa.
- **Mihir Shah (Nomura):** Asked about flat post-COVID Chyawanprash growth, beverage strategy, GT distributor cuts, and leadership structure (Group CEO).  
  *Answer:* Chyawanprash requires format additions (gummies) and daily-relevance messaging. GT distribution cut of 43% was a network consolidation without losing geographic coverage. No global CEO will be appointed; Herjit Bhalla leads India/SAARC and sits on the board, while Mohit Narang provides global oversight.
- **Avi Mehta (360 One):** Asked where funding for higher A&P spends will come from and if EBITDA flow-through will be affected.  
  *Answer:* Funding will come from internal cost savings (Project IRIS/Samriddhi) and expected gross margin recovery next fiscal year, protecting P&L structure.
- **Amit Sachdeva (UBS):** Asked why Dabur targets double-digit (~10%) growth rather than setting aggressive 15–20% targets.  
  *Answer:* Rejecting status quo; pulling forward the strategic vision to 2031 to unlock 20–50% growth rates in fast-growing pockets like OTC, digestives, and premium F&B.
- **Latika Chopra:** Asked if management structural changes or sales/marketing capability upgrades are required to accelerate growth.  
  *Answer:* Dabur's execution and commercial capabilities are best-in-class; no major organizational restructuring is needed. Incremental A&P will be funded via disciplined cost savings.

### One-line takeaway
Dabur is advancing its medium-term strategic roadmap (Vision 2031) while funding an extra 150–200 bps of A&P via Rs 170–200 crore in cost savings to maintain double-digit revenue growth.
