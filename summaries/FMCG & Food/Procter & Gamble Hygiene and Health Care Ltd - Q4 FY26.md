# Procter & Gamble Hygiene and Health Care Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-16 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** FY 2026 (Fiscal Year ended March 31, 2026)
**Tone:** Cautious - Sales were flat amidst a dynamic operating environment, media fragmentation, and consumer value-consciousness, though structural savings drove profit growth.

### Key numbers
- **Sales/Revenue:** ~Rs 4,300 crore, flat YoY
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** ~Rs 850 crore, up 19% YoY
- **Productivity Savings:** Rs 86 crores delivered in the last year
- **Whisper Period Panties Milestone:** Sold over 10 crore units in the last year (tripled the business)
- **Long-term 10-year CAGRs:** Mid-single digit sales growth, 8% compounded annual profit growth

### Highlights
- Delivered 19% structural profit growth despite muted sales through targeted and cohesive savings programs.
- Whisper Period Panties sold over 10 crore units, tripling the business and driving category growth; recognized as the #1 choice on quick commerce.
- Upgraded Whisper Nights portfolio with a new soft top-sheet and amplified communication around period pants and night-time leakage protection.
- Upgraded Vicks product lineup: launched new Vicks Cough Syrup (non-drowsy formula), upgraded Vicks ZzzQuil Natura Sleep Gummies (combining 5 mg melatonin and 50 mg natural ashwagandha with moisture-proof packaging), and executed culturally relevant marketing (first-ever concert and stand-up comic integrations for Vicks Drops).
- Celebrated P&G Shiksha milestone of impacting over 1 crore children from underserved communities.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the operating environment to remain dynamic and evolving, with near-term challenges continuing due to inflation and shifting consumer consumption trends.
- Management remains committed to long-term value creation and balanced growth through its Integrated Growth Strategy.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Feminine Hygiene category is growing at 7–10%, while healthcare sub-categories are growing at 2–3% (with volatility based on monsoons).
- Consumer consumption trends are shifting due to inflation across food, energy, and healthcare; consumers are value-conscious and discerning.
- Rural consumption continues to be higher than urban consumption, but a softening trend is visible due to an uptick in inflation rates.
- Intense competition noted with over 50 new regional and D2C players entering the feminine hygiene category over the last 10 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Delivered structural margin expansion of 1 point (100 bps) this year.
- Implemented multi-year savings master plans; delivered Rs 86 crores of savings in the last year via targeted productivity programs.
- Noted global supply chain and commodity surcharge challenges related to the West Asia conflict, with crude prices and resin prices increasing by 30-50%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained a record of 30 years of consistent dividend payout.
- Return on equity has tripled over the past 10 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Media fragmentation and cluttered landscape making consumer attention difficult to capture.
- Inflation across food, energy, and healthcare taking a toll on consumer wallets.
- Macro-economic risks, global trade policy uncertainties, and commodity price inflation driven by the West Asia conflict (crude and resin prices up 30-50%).
- Increased competitive intensity from 50+ regional and D2C players in feminine hygiene.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bay Capital India Fund:** Asked why revenue growth has slowed significantly over 5 years despite ad spends, distribution expansion, and new launches, and questioned frequent CEO/CFO changes. *Management answered that consumer habits and high competitive intensity shifted, but long-term strategy remains constant, explaining that P&G's grow-from-within talent model rotates leaders globally to build holistic skills.*
2. **SARETY.com:** Asked why the company is not innovating high-demand products in feminine hygiene despite changing preferences. *Management answered that innovation includes continuous line-up upgrades and pioneering the Whisper Period Panties segment.*
3. **Club Millionaire Financial Services / SARETY.com / Vinay Shetty (Bajaj Life Insurance):** Asked about industry growth, moats, and dealing with increasing competition. *Management highlighted their consumer-first approach, superior product performance, brand communication, and the "save-to-reinvest" productivity model as key moats.*
4. **Mudit Minocha (M3 Investment) & Krishnan Sambamoorthy (Ashika Institutional Equities):** Asked about reasons for the revenue slowdown and sluggish feminine hygiene revenue growth. *Management pointed to evolving consumer cohorts, rapid rise of 50+ regional/D2C competitors, and clutter, while emphasizing their focus on Nights and Panties portfolios to capture growth.*
5. **Meenakshi (Hindu Business Line) & Vishal Punmiya (Yes Securities):** Asked if West Asia conflicts increased cost pressures. *Management acknowledged that crude and resin prices spiked 30-50% but emphasized that supply chain continuity was maintained without stock-outs due to deep supplier partnerships and business continuity planning.*

### One-line takeaway
P&G Hygiene and Health Care Ltd reported flat annual sales of ~Rs 4,300 crore but grew PAT by 19% to ~Rs 850 crore via Rs 86 crores in productivity savings, even as it navigates a competitive, inflationary environment and evolving consumer paths to purchase.
