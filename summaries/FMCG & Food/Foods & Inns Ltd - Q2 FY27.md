# Foods & Inns Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - While the company reported a loss due to forex and faced export logistics headwinds, management highlights strong volume growth, record spray drying performance, and a resolution to shipping delays.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained volume growth guidance of 17-18% YoY.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from bullish to cautious optimism; management believes the "worst is over" following the Q2 loss, with export logistics improving.
- **New topics:** Discontinuation of forward hedging policy; inauguration of cluster development program (Nashik); record Spray Drying sales in October.
- **Old vs. New:** Export shipping delays (previously a major risk) are now described as softening/improving; CFO transition not discussed.

### Key numbers
- **Forex loss:** Rs 10.6 crore (booked in other expenses).
- **Spray Drying (H1):** 474 MT volume (+15% YoY); revenue Rs 11 crore (+7% YoY).
- **Unshipped orders:** 3,300 MT (60% shipped in October).
- **Subsidy:** Applied for (last 4 years total: Rs 84 crore; FY26: ~Rs 28-29 crore).
- **Volume growth guidance:** 17-18% YoY.

### Highlights
- Reported a loss due to notional forex impact from forward covers; management has decided to stop booking forward covers.
- Export dispatches delayed in Q2 due to container non-availability; 60% of the 3,300 MT backlog shipped in October.
- Spray Drying division achieved record sales of 100 MT in October.
- Selected for National Horticulture Board cluster development program (Nashik region) to onboard 15,000+ farmers.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 and Q4 to recover; full spillover of unshipped orders expected in Q3.
- Frozen segment growth target: 25-30% over the next 3 years.
- Spray Drying volume growth: Expected to cross 550 MT in H2 (annualized 18-19% growth).

### Demand & industry commentary
- No order cancellations; only delayed call-offs.
- Significant surge in demand for frozen foods in GCC countries; tied up with a Saudi customer.
- Frozen segment margins are better in exports than domestic.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Forex loss of Rs 10.6 crore impacted P&L.
- Working on a cost-plus model; struggling to pass on inflation in the pulping segment.
- Spray Drying EBITDA margin: ~18%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Spray Drying capacity expansion (+120 MT/annum) to start commercial production by mid/end-January 2027.
- Warehouse in Gujarat to be completed by mid-December.
- Government capital subsidy (35-38%) approved for frozen segment expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Long-term liability increased to Rs 91 crore (from Rs 53 crore) due to new long-term facility from Central Bank to manage shipment delays.
- Management does not anticipate needing further borrowing.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Forex volatility (notional loss).
- Export logistics (container availability/freight rates).
- Difficulty in passing on inflation in the pulping segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash Somani (YS Capital):** Asked about unshipped orders (3,300 MT) and forex loss. Management confirmed 60% shipped in Oct and explained the forex loss was due to forward contracts.
- **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company):** Asked about forex loss and capex. Management explained the loss was "notional" and confirmed capex for Spray Drying and warehouse.
- **Amish Kanani (Knowise Investment Managers):** Asked about pricing strategy and margins. Management admitted difficulty in passing on inflation in pulping but noted better margins in frozen/spray drying.
- **Amit Gori (Individual Investor):** Asked about promoter stake/valuation. Management stated the "worst is over" and expressed intent to consolidate holdings.

### One-line takeaway
Despite a Q2 loss driven by forex and logistics, management expects a recovery in H2, supported by record spray drying performance, capacity expansion, and improved export shipping conditions.
