# Som Distilleries & Breweries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Performance was severely impacted by the suspension of the Bhopal plant, though operations have now been restored and a volume recovery is underway.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Revenue guidance narrowed to ~INR 1,000 crores (from INR 1,000–1,100 crores) with ~10% EBITDA margin.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from severe uncertainty due to plant closure to recovery mode following the restoration of Bhopal operations.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Gross debt increased by INR 100 crores in Q2, pushing the gross debt-to-equity ratio up to 0.44.
- Last quarter's promises: Bhopal plant reopening was achieved; UP ramp-up is ongoing; single malt initiative remains in the pipeline.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** INR 454.7 crores (down ~43% YoY from INR 800.1 crores).
- **H1 FY27 Gross Profit:** INR 149.7 crores (vs INR 300 crores YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 32.91 crores (vs INR 112.5 crores YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 1.7 crores (vs INR 61.56 crores YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 7.24% (vs 14.07% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** ~0.4% (vs 7.7% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Volumes:** 77.47 lakh cases (lower by ~39% YoY).
- **Q2 FY27 Volumes:** 31.68 lakh cases.
- **Q2 FY27 Total Income:** INR 185.9 crores.
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 17.7 crores.
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 0.14 crores.
- **Installed Capacity:** 48.2 million cases in beer, 5.1 million cases in IMFL.
- **Gross Debt-to-Equity:** 0.44 as of September 2026 (vs 0.30 at March 2026).

### Highlights
- Operations at the Bhopal facility have been officially restored, with production and dispatches recommenced.
- Beer remains the principal contributor: 98.97% of total volume and 93.18% of revenue in H1 FY27.
- Odisha, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu supported volumes during the first half; Tamil Nadu reached ~4% market share in Q2.
- Stocks were dispatched to Andhra Pradesh by the end of October.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guides for FY27 turnover of approximately INR 1,000 crores with an EBITDA margin of nearly 10%.
- H2 net revenue expected to be approximately INR 550 crores.
- Aims to recover ~45% market share in Madhya Pradesh within 3 to 6 months (by February).
- UP Phase 2 project (~INR 210 crores) and funding mix (debt vs equity) will be evaluated in March 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Stepping into the off-season for beer in Central and North areas as winter sets in.
- UP plant placement is in initial stages (about 1/3 of the market, ~2,000 outlets); capacity utilization is not yet at 20-25%.
- IMFL brand Mahavat rollout is ongoing in Madhya Pradesh, Delhi, and UP; management targets 5-6% IMFL market share in 6 months to 1 year, and 15-20% revenue from IMFL in the medium term.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Can prices increased slightly with some stability returning; glass bottle prices stabilized after Q1 increases.
- Realization dropped to INR 550 (from INR 607 YoY) due to a lower share of cans from MP in the base period.
- All Bhopal losses and finished stock inventory accounted for in Q2; management confirmed no inventory write-offs are required as stocks are good.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total installed capacity stands at 48.2 million cases (beer) and 5.1 million cases (IMFL) across Bhopal, Hassan, and Odisha.
- Sanction letters for UP Phase 2 (~INR 210 crores) secured from banks.
- Hassan plant conversion to a fully owned subsidiary is in progress.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt increased by INR 100 crores during Q2.
- Gross debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.44 as of September 2026 (compared to 0.30 in March 2026).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Onset of the seasonal lean period for beer consumption in Central and North India.
- Low capacity utilization at the newly commissioned UP plant currently.
- Higher leverage following the operational disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shivakshi Bhardwaj asked about MP market share recovery and IMFL targets.** Management stated they expect to recover their 45% MP market share in 3–6 months and target 5–6% IMFL market share within a year.
2. **Nagaraj asked about UP Phase 2 capex and Bhopal loss provisions.** Management replied that Phase 2 (~INR 210 cr) will be evaluated in March 2027, and all Bhopal losses/inventory issues are fully accounted for with no write-offs needed.
3. **Sunil Jatakia asked about inventory expiry and MP license renewal.** Management confirmed no write-offs on the INR 25 crores inventory and expects smooth annual license renewal in March 2027.
4. **Jitaksh Gupta asked about non-Bhopal Q2 performance and H2 revenue outlook.** Management credited Odisha, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu, guiding for ~INR 550 crores in net revenue for H2.
5. **Rahul asked about third-party bottling margins for Carlsberg versus own brands.** Management declined to disclose exact margins, citing long-term capacity utilization benefits.

### One-line takeaway
Som Distilleries absorbed a severe H1 earnings contraction due to its Bhopal plant shutdown, but with operations resumed and geographic expansion scaling, management guides for a revenue recovery to ~INR 1,000 crores in FY27.
