# Colgate Palmolive (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27 (Investor Day 2026 held on August 21, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Strong sales and profit growth, supported by premiumisation, expanding distribution, and effective A&P investments.

### Key numbers
- Sales: Rs 1,486 crore (12% YoY growth for the quarter; 10.5% YoY for the six-month period).
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): up 22.3% YoY.
- EBITDA margin: 34% (stated as 31.6% or higher; up 158 bps YoY, noted as highest in the industry/nearest competitor).
- A&P (Advertising & Promotion) investment: 15.8% of sales (noted as stepped up).
- Direct retail footprint: 5.5 lakh outlets (total reach spans 7.1 million outlets).
- Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 121%.
- Net Working Capital: -15% of sales (negative net working capital).
- Dividend payout: ~70% (69.7% reported in Q1; historical block FY24-FY26 at 70%).

### Highlights
- Celebrated major milestones: Global Colgate existence 220th birthday; Colgate-Palmolive India 90th year.
- Launched new innovations including Colgate Visible White Purple (using purple-yellow color wheel neutralization) and Harry Potter themed pump formats for kids/adults.
- PerioGard received certification from the Indian Society of Periodontology for gum disease management.
- Expanded direct distribution footprint to 5.5 lakh outlets and enhanced e-commerce capabilities.
- Maintained negative working capital (-15%) and high ROCE (121%).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to continue stepping up investments behind brands to drive consumption and premiumisation.
- Management guided that operating margins/EBITDA percentage may have an upward bias, but short-term levels will depend on investment intensity.
- Guidance: maintained (implied focus on steady mid-single-digit volume growth alongside premium mix improvement).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Urban and rural markets showing steady state, though near-term demand can see quarterly volatility.
- Toothpaste household penetration in India is high at 76%, but daily brushing frequency (45% brush twice a day) and toothbrush replacement frequency (every 9 months vs 3 months recommended) remain low, providing massive headroom for consumption growth.
- Premiumisation trend is strong; consumers are willing to trade up for differentiated benefits (whiteness, freshness, gum care).
- E-commerce and quick-commerce channels are growing in double digits and expanding rapidly beyond metros into tier-2/tier-3 towns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins remained healthy, driven by manufacturing efficiencies, localization of sourcing, replacing imported materials with local alternatives, supply chain automation, and favorable product mix.
- Savings generated from efficiencies are fully plowed back into A&P and product superiority (e.g., Arginine technology, packaging upgrades).
- Commodity volatility and inflation are closely monitored; management noted they are using pricing and pack-size adjustments judiciously.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus remains on expanding direct distribution reach (reaching 5.5 lakh direct stores out of 7.1 million total reach).
- Continued scaling of digital and e-commerce fulfillment infrastructure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash generation with negative net working capital (-15% of sales).
- Balance sheet remains debt-free with robust cash reserves.
- ROCE stands at an industry-leading 121%.
- Dividend payout ratio sustained at approximately 70%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Personal care segment (e.g., handwash, body wash) noted as an area of past disappointment, though management sees green shoots through recent partnerships.
- Macro headwinds including deficit monsoons, commodity volatility, and inflation.
- Low toothbrush replacement rates (9 months average) and low twice-daily brushing habits (45%) in India.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abneesh Roy (Nomura/Other):** Asked about the sustainability of outperformance amid high competitive intensity and if new entrants in sensitivity could derail margins. 
   - *Management reply:* Growth is driven by structural interventions, product superiority, and brand equity rather than competitor actions. They welcomed competition as it expands the nascent sensitivity category.
2. **Nihal Shah (Nomura):** Inquired about e-commerce growth rates and dual-brushing adoption.
   - *Management reply:* E-commerce is growing in double digits, driven by discovery and travel formats. Dual brushing metrics are tracked via behavioral campaigns like "Brush Twice a Day".
3. **Latika Chopra (JP Morgan):** Asked if operating margins would moderate given rising A&P investments (15.8%).
   - *Management reply:* Margins have an upward bias, but short-term levels will fluctuate based on the elastic reinvestment into growth and A&P.
4. **Shilpa Soman (CLSA):** Questioned A&P spending sustainability and whether lower-pyramid consumers are missed due to digital shifts.
   - *Management reply:* 50-60% of turnover/savings is ring-fenced for A&P. Even at the bottom of the pyramid, mobile phone penetration ensures digital reach is effective.
5. **Harit Kapoor (Investec):** Asked about distribution expansion triggers and natural/ayurvedic portfolio performance.
   - *Management reply:* Expansion targets high-growth urban corridors and cluster agglomerations. On naturals, consumers ultimately buy on core efficacy (fresh breath, whiteness, cavity prevention) delivered by their masterbrands.

### One-line takeaway
Colgate-Palmolive India delivered robust Q1 FY27 results (12% sales growth, 34% EBITDA margin, PBT up 22.3%), aggressively reinvesting efficiency savings into A&P and premiumisation innovations while maintaining an industry-leading 121% ROCE.
