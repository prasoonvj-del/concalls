# Tata Consumer Products Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 14% YoY with 18% EBITDA growth and 40 bps margin expansion, led by strong momentum in growth businesses and salt.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue growth:** Consolidated top-line up 14% YoY (11% in constant currency; India business volume growth at 11%). Added Rs 700 crore in turnover YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Consolidated EBITDA up 18% YoY.
- **EBITDA margin:** 14%, expanding 40 bps YoY (H1 margin expanded 60 bps YoY). Overall segment margins up 50 bps YoY.
- **Net profit:** Rs 470 crore, up 16% YoY (H1 net profit up 22% YoY). Profit before tax up 21% YoY.
- **EPS:** Rs 4.75; Adjusted EPS at Rs 5.18, up 17% YoY (H1 adjusted EPS at Rs 9.85, up 21% YoY).
- **A&P spend:** Reported A&P-to-sales at 5.5% in Q2 (5.8% in H1); total A&P including reclassified visibility spends ranges between 6.3% and 6.5%.
- **Segment financial performance:**
  - **India Salt:** Revenue close to Rs 1,100 crore, up 15% YoY; volume up 9% YoY.
  - **India Tea & Coffee:** Revenue down 1% YoY; volume flat YoY.
  - **Growth Businesses:** Revenue up 40% YoY to close to Rs 1,400 crore; contributes 38% of India business.
    - **Sampann:** Revenue up 57% YoY; EBITDA margins expanded 270–280 bps YoY. Combined ARR of dry fruits and cold-pressed oil crossed Rs 1,000 crore.
    - **Ready-to-Drink (RTD):** Revenue up 47% YoY; volume up 34% YoY.
    - **Capital Foods & Organic India:** Combined revenue up 15% YoY (domestic business up 24% YoY; Capital Foods up 18% YoY, Organic India up 9% YoY).
    - **Soulfull:** Revenue grew ~23% YoY.
  - **International Business:** Revenue up 7% YoY (down 2% YoY in constant currency). UK revenue down 8%, US up 4% (coffee constant currency up 6%), Canada up 8% in constant currency.
  - **Non-Branded Business:** Revenue up 12% YoY (9% in constant currency). Soluble coffee volume grew 21% YoY.
  - **Starbucks:** Revenue up 15% YoY with ~10% traffic growth; opened 7 new stores to reach 505 total stores.
  - **Alternate Channels Run-rate:** Vending run-rate at ~Rs 150 crore (10,000 outlets); HoReCa run-rate at Rs 200–225 crore (>10,000 outlets).

### Highlights
- Deployed AI sales companions for ~1,600–1,700 salespeople to optimize outlet-level ordering, targeting 100% sales force deployment by December.
- Operationalized AI applications for green leaf quality inspection (120 types), Himalayan factory online bottle inspection, trade spend optimization, and procurement price forecasting.
- Rolled out 22 new product launches in Q2 (36 in H1 FY27), including Tata Salt Plus, Gold Reserve tea, Chakra Select, Kanan Devan Gold, and Pahadi Ghee under Sampann.
- Starbucks recorded its 5th to 6th consecutive quarter of positive same-store sales growth.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Full-year margin guidance:** Maintained 50 to 70 bps YoY EBITDA margin expansion target for FY27.
- **Medium-term margin target:** Management targets overall company EBITDA margin to reach 17% to 20% in the next ~3 years.
- **Category growth outlook:** Tea and salt expected to grow mid-to-high single digits; growth categories expected to maintain 30%+ revenue growth over the next 2-3 years.
- **Capital Foods & Organic India guidance:** Management re-affirmed 25% to 30% long-term growth outlook.
- **Tea volume:** Target low-to-mid single-digit volume growth, with value growth outpacing volume starting Q3 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall domestic tea industry demand remained soft, declining ~6% YoY per third-party data; TCPL maintained stable market share (~30% of industry remains unbranded).
- Prolonged heatwave in the UK temporarily curtailed tea consumption, though colder weather entering Q3 is expected to drive category recovery.
- Quick commerce continues rapid expansion into Tier-2 towns; TCPL products are tracking in line with dark store expansion rates.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cumulative tea inflation reached 8% to 10% between Q1 and Q2 before moderating to 2%–3% weekly; price hikes of 8%–10% fully offset input inflation.
- Salt prices were raised from Rs 30 to Rs 32 per orange bag in May, and further raised by Rs 2 to Rs 34 in early October.
- Raw coffee commodity prices began softening in the US (trending at $3.10–$3.15 vs. $3.30–$3.50 last year).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 7 stores in Q2 to reach 505 Starbucks stores; store network expansion expected to accelerate.
- Direct distribution reach stands at ~1.9 million+ stores, with numeric reach at 4.5–4.6 million stores.
- Sampann operates on an asset-light, zero-capex model.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not specifically discussed in the call.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- International operations experienced logistics friction from a 10–12 day port disruption at Mundra in early September and shipping route detours around the Middle East.
- Raw material inflation in tea, salt, and Capital Foods temporarily compressed standalone India segment margins in Q2 prior to price hikes taking full effect.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Mihir Shah (Nomura):** *What is the quantum of price hikes in tea and full-year margin expectation?*
  - **Management:** Price hikes of 8%–10% taken between Q1 and Q2 cover input inflation. Full-year EBITDA margin guidance of 50-70 bps expansion is maintained, with YoY margin expansion expected in H2.
- **Abhineesh Roy (Nuvama):** *How did the Mundra port issue impact Capital Foods and Organic India international growth?*
  - **Management:** A 10-day strike at Mundra and shipping diversions caused Q2 international shipment delays; domestic growth remained strong at 24%, and overall growth guidance of 25%-30% stands intact.
- **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital):** *Is the 17%-18% India margin target realistic given the expanding share of dilutive growth businesses?*
  - **Management:** Yes, within 3 years EBITDA margin should hit 17%+. Growth portfolio margins are improving (Sampann expanded to 14%-15% from -5% historically), and fixed-cost scale leverage will drive expansion.
- **Vivek M. (Jefferies):** *How do you evaluate and cull underperforming new product innovations?*
  - **Management:** Performance is reviewed monthly. Innovations missing top-line or margin targets after 6 months receive maximum 3 months of extended support; if targets are still missed, they are pulled. About one-third of launches are culled.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Consumer Products delivered strong 14% revenue and 18% EBITDA growth led by 40% momentum in growth businesses and calibrated price hikes to protect margins against raw material inflation.
