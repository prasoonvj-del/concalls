# Tata Consumer Products Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated top line grew 14% YoY with UVG of 11% in the India business, EBITDA grew 18%, and management expressed confidence in margin expansion despite input cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Up 14% YoY (11% in constant currency); added INR 700 crores to turnover.
- Consolidated EBITDA: Up 18% YoY; margin expanded 40 bps YoY to 14%.
- Net Profit (PAT): INR 470 crores, up 16% YoY; Adjusted EPS at INR 5.18 (H1 FY27 Adjusted EPS at INR 9.85).
- India Business: Salt volume up 9%, revenue up 15% (Salt crossed close to INR 1,100 crores); India Tea/Coffee volume flat, revenue down 1%.
- Growth Businesses: Up 40% YoY, contributing 38% to the India business; Sampann up 57%; Ready-to-Drink (RTD) volume up 34%, revenue up 47%; Capital Foods and Organic India domestic business up 24%.
- International Business: Up 7% YoY, constant currency negative 2%; U.K. revenue down 8%, U.S. business up 4%.
- Non-Branded Business: Up 12% YoY (9% in constant currency); soluble volume up 21%.
- Starbucks: Revenue up 15% YoY; 7 stores opened, reaching 505 stores.
- A&P: A&P-to-Sales was 5.5% for Q2 and 5.8% for H1 (total A&P including visibility is around 6.3% to 6.5%).

### Highlights
- India Salt touched close to INR 1,100 crores (up 15% revenue, 9% volume); Orange bag price moved from INR 32 to INR 34 in October first week.
- Growth businesses crossed close to INR 1,400 crores, powering ahead of salt and tea-coffee.
- Deployed AI across 1,600 to 1,700 salesmen (aiming for 100% by December) for personalized sales companions and voice-agent ordering; AI also deployed for tea leaf inspection, Himalayan factory bottle inspection, procurement price guiding, invoice automation, and trade spend optimization.
- Launched 22 new products in Q2 (total 36 for H1) focused on health, wellness, convenience, and premiumization (e.g., Tata Salt Plus, Gold Reserve tea, Chakra Select, Kanan Devan Gold, Himalayan Pahadi ghee, Tata Lyfe alkaline water, Tata Spring Alive).
- HoReCa channel is at a INR 200–225 crores ARR with over 10,000 outlets; Vending is at a INR 150 crores run rate across 10,000 outlets. Soulfull grew 23%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for 50 to 70 bps margin expansion for the full year and remains confident of continuing to deliver margin expansion in H2 YoY.
- Capital Foods and Organic India guidance maintained at 25% to 30% growth going forward.
- Growth categories expected to grow at 30% plus over the next 2 to 3 years.
- India EBITDA margin target is in the 17% to 20% ballpark over the next 3 to 5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India tea industry was soft overall (third-party numbers show about negative 6%), while TCPL's market share is stable.
- U.K. tea market was impacted by a heat wave during the quarter, but weather has turned, and shares held up.
- Quick commerce continues to power ahead; TCPL's growth in quick commerce is in line with leading players.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inflationary pressures seen on tea and salt, leading to margin contraction in the India segment; however, calibrated price increases have been taken (8% to 10% in tea; salt price increased to INR 34).
- Robusta and Arabica coffee prices continue to soften, impacting realizations, but margins for plantations/non-branded remain healthy. Soluble business margins are steady.
- Sampann margins expanded by 270–280 bps YoY.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Sampann is an asset-light model with no capex.
- Opened 7 new Starbucks stores in Q2, bringing the total to 505; expects acceleration of store openings going forward.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not explicitly detailed in transcript figures other than strong overall financial performance and cash generation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- International business impacted in Q2 by shipping disruptions and 10 to 12 days of disruption at the Mundra port in early September.
- U.K. business impacted by a prolonged heat wave during the quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mihir Shah (Nomura) on tea procurement and pricing:** Management stated peak procurement is ongoing post-Puja in the north; 8% to 10% pricing has been taken to cover inflation, and value growth is expected to outpace volume starting Q3.
2. **Abhineesh Roy (Nuvama) on AI cost vs. top-line impact:** Management noted AI will bring cost takeouts, but the real differentiator is top-line improvement via targeted per-call lines and memory functions.
3. **Vivek M. (Jefferies) on RTD value-volume gap:** Management explained the gap is due to pricing/mix and the strong recovery of higher-value Tata Gluco Plus at the INR 10 price point, alongside new high-margin innovations.
4. **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital) on India EBIT margin targets to 17-18%:** Management confirmed the medium-term endeavor for a good food and beverage business in India is 17% to 20% EBITDA, targeting this within the next 3 years.
5. **Arnab Mitra (Goldman Sachs) on RTD sustainability:** Management stated their INR 10 business model remains profitable despite high GST and channel margins, allowing them to sustain growth where competitors face stress.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Consumer Products delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 14% revenue and 18% EBITDA growth, driven by stellar performances in growth businesses (Sampann, RTD) and salt, while management actively deploys AI across sales and operations to drive efficiencies and top-line growth.
