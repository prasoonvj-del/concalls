# Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 16% YoY with margin expansion driven by price actions and cost optimization, despite inflation and West Asia disruptions.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 548.7 crore, up 16% YoY and 12.9% QoQ
- Gross profit: Rs 258.9 crore, up 20% YoY; gross margin at 47.2% vs 45.6% in Q1 FY26
- EBITDA: Rs 72.1 crore, up 23.8% YoY; EBITDA margin at 13.1%, an improvement of 80 bps YoY
- PAT: Rs 38.8 crore, up 25.5% YoY; PAT margin at 7.1%
- Biscuit segment revenue: Rs 325 crore, up 15.7% YoY
- Bakery segment revenue: Rs 215 crore, up 17.5% YoY
- Quick commerce growth: 58% YoY
- Naturbaked monthly revenue run rate: Crossed Rs 1 crore
- Export contribution to top line: Close to 35%

### Highlights
- Domestic demand remained healthy across retail and institutional channels.
- Kolkata bakery unit commissioned in Q4 FY26 is servicing East markets; Khopoli plant (Maharashtra) commissioned in March 2026 is stabilizing well.
- U.S. market returned to a growth trajectory, supported by new product launches like peanut butter crackers with Walmart.
- Project IMPACT and calibrated price increases successfully neutralized most of the quarter's inflation.
- Management guided to reach a 14% EBITDA margin target by Q4 FY27 and a 16% EBITDA margin by FY30.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year revenue growth in mid-teens (17-19%).
- Management targets reaching a 14% EBITDA margin in Q4 FY27 and 15%-16% by FY30.
- Management plans to scale towards a revenue milestone of Rs 4,000 crores by FY30.
- Guidance: Maintained (mid-teens growth).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic biscuit sales growth was high-single digits.
- QSR/institutional bakery business is back on an upswing with positive trends after multiple quarters of slow growth.
- Quick commerce is of strategic significance, growing 58% YoY.
- International export order book strengthened materially despite vessel availability issues and rising freight costs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inflationary pressures stemmed from raw material and packaging materials, escalation in fuel costs, and minimum wage hikes.
- Overall input cost inflation impacted the quarter by approximately 1.5% YoY, with larger impacts expected in Q2.
- Company took an overall price increase of 2% to 3% in consumer businesses (English Oven and Cremica) during the quarter.
- Project IMPACT is expected to deliver 0.4% to 0.5% in cost savings this year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current capacity and planned investments can cover revenues up to approximately Rs 3,400 crore to Rs 3,500 crore.
- FY27 capex (excluding spillover) is planned at close to Rs 200 crore, funded 60% via internal accruals and 40% via borrowings.
- Bangalore plant location is shortlisted, with new lines under finalization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-equity ratio described by management as comfortable and healthy.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Headline consumer inflation climbed to around 4.4% in June 2026.
- West Asia conflict contributed to raw material, packaging, and fuel inflation, alongside minimum wage hikes.
- Shipping environment remains difficult with unnormalized vessel availability and rising freight and logistic costs.
- Intense competition in the biscuit side, especially in North India.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital) asked about export sustainability and restocking impact:** Management clarified that growth is driven by organic demand and new SKUs (like peanut butter crackers), not pipeline restocking.
- **Harit Kapoor (Investec) asked about gross margin expansion despite inflation:** Management stated the expansion was aided by a better product mix, particularly higher growth in the Bakery segment.
- **Binay Shukla (PhillipCapital) asked about segment EBITDA margins:** Management stated Bakery EBITDA is above the company average, while Biscuit EBITDA is slightly below.
- **Amit Purohit (Elara Capital) asked about price hikes and inflation pass-through:** Management noted a 1.5% inflation impact in Q1, with 2-3% price hikes partially realized in Q1 and fully effective by Q3.
- **Shirish Pardeshi (Motilal Oswal) asked about U.S. tariff impact:** Management stated that U.S. customers remain optimistic, none stopped working with future capabilities, and new SKUs are expanding.

### One-line takeaway
Mrs. Bectors delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 16% revenue growth and 80 bps EBITDA margin expansion, neutralizing cost inflation through agile pricing and Project IMPACT.
