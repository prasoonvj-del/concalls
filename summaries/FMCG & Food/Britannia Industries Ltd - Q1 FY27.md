# Britannia Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong volume growth, recovery in General Trade, and successful execution of strategic priorities despite inflationary headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations (Consolidated):** Rs 4,964 crore (9.5% YoY growth).
*   **Standalone Revenue Growth:** 10% YoY.
*   **PAT:** 11.9% of revenue (13.6% YoY growth).
*   **Volume Growth:** 9% YoY.
*   **Operating Profit:** 15.3% (5-year trend).
*   **Croissant Business Growth:** >30% YoY.
*   **Quick Commerce:** 80-85% of total e-commerce sales.

### Highlights
*   **General Trade (GT) Recovery:** GT is growing at 1.5x the rate of last year. The "dual pricing" issue that impacted rural/wholesale channels in previous quarters has been resolved as of June.
*   **Channel Performance:** E-commerce and other nimble channels are growing at ~2.5x the rate of GT.
*   **Strategic Pillars:** Focus remains on sales/supply chain efficiency, brand experience, innovation, "Many Indias" (regional focus), and sustainability.
*   **Regional Strategy:** Identified 6 "key states" (largest/most profitable) showing upswing; other states are growing even faster (1.3x of key states).
*   **Product Innovation:** Strong performance in adjacencies (cakes, rusk, wafers) and dairy (double-digit growth). New launches like Bourbon Roll, Butter Toastea, and Sattvam Ghee are performing well.
*   **Management:** The management team structure is now complete following recent appointments.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Volume/Value/Margin:** Management aims for a "virtuous triangle" of volume, value, and profitability.
*   **Pricing:** Expect an additional 1.5% to 2% pricing action (via shrinkflation) in coming quarters to mitigate input cost inflation.
*   **International Business:** Expecting a return to growth this quarter following 4 months of turbulence in the Middle East and North America.
*   **Future Platforms:** Committed to expanding beyond bakery into health/wellness platforms; internal teams are working on this.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Demand Environment:** Strong; the company exited the quarter with mid-teens growth.
*   **GST Impact:** Management noted that GST rate cuts on biscuits (largely sold at Rs 5/Rs 10 price points) do not drive immediate volume elasticity compared to larger pack sizes, as consumers receive more product rather than a lower price.
*   **Competition:** Focused on winning in "Many Indias" through localized marketing (e.g., Milk Bikis in Tamil Nadu) and better collaboration between sales and marketing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflationary Pressures:**
    *   **Industrial Fuel (LPG/PNG):** Prices spiked 2.5x in April/May; currently at 1.5x of February levels.
    *   **Palm Oil:** Prices up >20% (currently ~Rs 140/kg).
    *   **Sugar:** Prices increased by >Rs 7/kg in the last 2-3 weeks.
*   **Mitigation:** Only ~50% of inflation was mitigated via price increases (shrinkflation) in Q1.
*   **Efficiency:** Focus on packaging optimization, waste reduction, and shifting to alternate fuels (biomass) to reduce LPG/PNG dependence.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **PLI Scheme:** No PLI incentives were booked in FY26 or Q1 FY27 as the company did not meet the prescribed threshold growth.
*   **Inorganic Growth:** Open to M&A if it provides strong brands, speed, or specific technology/capabilities, but remains selective and cautious.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Phantom Stock:** Impact was ~Rs 1 crore for the quarter.
*   **Inventory:** No benefit from old inventory; management denied any "loading" or channel stuffing to boost Q1 numbers.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Input Costs:** Volatility in palm oil, sugar, and fuel prices remains a significant watch-out.
*   **Geopolitics:** Uncertainty in international markets (Middle East/North America) persists.
*   **El Niño/Rainfall:** Potential impact on wheat/flour prices in the coming season.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On "Mid-teens" exit growth:** Management clarified this was driven by organic demand and the resolution of the dual-pricing issue, not channel stuffing.
*   **On Ad Spends:** Management confirmed ad spends are ahead of sales growth to improve brand health parameters; no specific % guidance provided.
*   **On Margin Sustainability:** Management acknowledged input cost inflation is a reality but expressed confidence in maintaining levers to manage the business, though they did not explicitly guarantee FY26 margin levels.
*   **On Croissant:** Management stated the business is growing at 30%+ and is margin-accretive to the company.
*   **On GT Structural Changes:** Management is empowering regional teams, increasing local influencer spend, and converting sub-distributors to direct distributors.

### One-line takeaway
Britannia delivered a strong Q1 with 9% volume growth and a recovery in General Trade, though management remains cautious regarding persistent inflationary pressures in key commodities like sugar, palm oil, and fuel.
