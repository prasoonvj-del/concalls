# Gillette India Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-16 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** FY26 (Full Year)
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted consistent topline and bottom-line growth, successful execution of the integrated growth strategy, and market leadership despite macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 3100 crore (up 8% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 650 crore (up 23% YoY).
*   **Margin Expansion:** >300 bps structural margin improvement.
*   **Productivity Savings:** Rs 38 crore delivered in the fiscal year.
*   **5-Year CAGR:** Profit growth of 17%; Net profit margin increased by ~600 bps.

### Highlights
*   **Integrated Growth Strategy:** Focus on daily-use products, product superiority, productivity, constructive disruption, and organizational enablement.
*   **New Launches:** Introduced Gillette Guard 3in1 (men’s grooming), Oral-B Sensitive Care (oral care), and a new range of Gillette Trimmers.
*   **Digital/Tech:** Utilizes "Supply 3.0" and AI-assisted tools to process over 2 million data checks daily for on-shelf availability.
*   **CSR:** P&G Shiksha program reached a milestone of impacting over 1 crore children.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management intends to "double down" on the current integrated growth strategy.
*   Management anticipates near-term challenges, including inflation, global trade policy shifts, and commodity price volatility due to the West Asia crisis.
*   Management remains optimistic about opportunities despite potential mid-term growth fluctuations.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Category Growth:** Male grooming is growing at ~12%; Female grooming is growing >20%.
*   **Oral Care:** Manual oral care is growing at high single-digits; Power oral care is growing at double-digits.
*   **Consumer Behavior:** Rural consumption remains higher than urban, though softening due to inflation. Consumers are becoming more disciplined and thoughtful regarding spending.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Crude oil and resins have seen price increases of ~50%.
*   **Mitigation:** Productivity programs and supply chain optimization are being used to mitigate cost headwinds and fuel investments in superiority.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   85% of products are manufactured domestically.
*   The company exports finished goods to more than 10 countries.
*   Bhiwadi manufacturing facility issues were resolved in a few days with zero supply-related impact.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Media Fragmentation:** Increasing difficulty in capturing consumer attention.
*   **Retail Shifts:** Rapid changes in the retail landscape (Agentic AI, Quick Commerce).
*   **Export Revenue:** One-time impact on export revenue due to the closure of the distribution agreement with an affiliate in Bangladesh.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why does revenue growth appear muted/volatile?** Management clarified that the base includes a one-time impact from the closure of the Bangladesh distribution agreement; excluding this, growth rates would double.
*   **Q: How is the company countering competition in men's shaving?** Management stated they are upgrading the portfolio with versatile solutions like the new Gillette Trimmers and Gillette 3in1 to meet evolving consumer needs.
*   **Q: Are the 300bps margin improvements sustainable?** Management stated that productivity is embedded in their operating model and they are working with a multi-year view on savings to ensure long-term margin health.
*   **Q: Is volume or value growth more important?** Management emphasized that "balanced growth" (both volume and value) is the only way to drive long-term value creation and fuel investments.
*   **Q: What is the outlook for the new Trimmers category?** Management expects the category to grow at early double-digit rates and aims to grow faster than the category.

### One-line takeaway
Gillette India delivered strong FY26 results with 8% revenue and 23% profit growth, leveraging premiumization and productivity to navigate inflationary headwinds and structural changes in the retail landscape.
