# Tilaknagar Industries Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust secondary volume growth, successful brand integrations, and ongoing market share gains.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs. 1,035 crore (up 173% YoY); Adjusted for subsidy: Rs. 1,033 crore (up 172% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 148 crore (up 146% YoY); Margin: 14.3%. Adjusted for subsidy: Rs. 146 crore; Margin: 14.1%.
- **PAT (adjusted):** Rs. 68 crore (up 30% YoY); Margin: 6.6%. Adjusted for subsidy: Rs. 66 crore; Margin: 6.4%.
- **Volumes:** Overall volumes increased 160% YoY and 2% QoQ. Imperial Blue secondary volume growth: 8.5% QoQ. Mansion House Brandy secondary volume growth: 7.1% QoQ.
- **NSR:** Moderated by 0.75% QoQ to Rs. 1,174 per case (from Rs. 1,183 in Q1 FY27); increased 3.2% YoY from Rs. 1,138 in Q2 FY26. H1 FY27 NSR grew 4.2% to Rs. 1,179.
- **Gross Profit (adjusted for subsidy):** Rs. 432 crore; Margin: 41.8%.
- **Debt (as of Sep 30, 2026):** Gross debt at Rs. 2,202 crore; Net debt at Rs. 2,074 crore.
- **Subsidy Income:** Rs. 20 crore in Q1 FY27; Rs. 2 crore in Q2 FY27.
- **Exceptional Expenses:** Rs. 11 crore in Q2 FY27 (predominantly TSMA fees and integration expenses).

### Highlights
- Imperial Blue market share increased 100 bps QoQ, rising in 17 states (significant gains in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Assam, Rajasthan). Launched in Kerala in Oct 2026; Tamil Nadu launch upcoming.
- Mansion House Brandy market share in Prestige & Above segment increased 70 bps QoQ. Became highest selling brandy in Karnataka in Sep 2026 (IB market share crossed 40%, up 900 bps QoQ).
- Mansion House Reserve Brandy in Tamil Nadu saw 25%+ QoQ and 50% YoY volume growth in Sep 2026.
- Made a strategic investment of Rs. 22 crore for a 30% stake in Black Tiger Distilleries (makers of Bodega Suprema No. 5 Tequila) across 2 tranches (Rs. 6 crore invested, Rs. 16 crore in next 12 months).
- Expanded luxury brands (Monarch Legacy Edition Brandy, Seven Islands Pure Malt Whisky, Samsara Gin) into Assam. Samsara grew 50% in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Volume Growth:** Management maintains high single-digit to low double-digit volume growth for the combined business.
- **Post-FY27 Growth:** Mid-teens volume growth and mid-to-high teens revenue growth over the next couple of years.
- **EBITDA Margins:** Expected to reach 16%-18% for the combined business by FY29 with an upward bias (includes ~100 bps benefit from UK FTA at the upper end).
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** Guided to go below 1.0x by March 2029. Target to bring absolute net debt in March 2027 below March 2026 levels.
- **Exceptional Integration Costs:** Expected around Rs. 10 crore - Rs. 11 crore per quarter for Q3 and Q4 FY27; no TSMA fees in FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- White Spirits category is growing fast largely in UP, Maharashtra, and Rajasthan, but is not very large in South India.
- Early signals of regulatory reforms observed in Tamil Nadu (largest brandy market in India).
- Strong seasonal execution in the north ahead of the festive season.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Packaging inputs (especially glass) faced inflationary pressures due to geopolitical tensions/West Asia crisis. ENA prices showed inflationary trends exiting Q2 due to rising fuel and grain costs.
- Net raw material/West Asia inflationary impact estimated at ~2.5% (approx. Rs. 25 crore on a quarterly basis).
- Gross margins protected via supply chain and cost optimization initiatives (market bottle utilization rates). Excluding inflation, gross margins would be closer to 44% and EBITDA margins around 16.5%.
- A&P reinvestment increased 61 bps QoQ in Q2 FY27 due to ATL/BTL execution ahead of festivals.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Imperial Blue distribution leveraged to re-launch Mansion House Brandy in Daman and Diu, launch Madira Rum in Uttarakhand, and Imperial Black in UP and Meghalaya.
- Tequila brand Bodega launched in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Goa, and Delhi.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Annualized net working capital days on gross basis at ~65 days (including Telangana overdues of ~Rs. 550 crore). Telangana operates on a 6-month overdue cycle.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued inflationary pressures on packaging inputs (glass) and ENA prices.
- Uncertainty regarding the timing of state-level price increases (e.g., Telangana and Andhra Pradesh).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on Tamil Nadu volume potential:** Management stated that at their peak, they sold ~3 million cases of P&A brandy in Tamil Nadu, whereas current ARR is less than 0.5 million cases, positioning TI as a major beneficiary of reforms.
2. **Sanjay Manyal (Institutional Equities) on Imperial Blue run-rate:** Management clarified that despite doing 2 million cases in May-June, Q2 seasonality in North India impacts drinking; they maintain double-digit growth guidance for IB for FY27.
3. **Mehul Desai (JM Financial) on Primary vs. Secondary gap & Margins:** Management explained that the gap converges over the year and is influenced by end-of-quarter dispatches. Margin expansion guidance is relative to H1 FY26.
4. **Karan Kamdar (Equirus) on Pricing Actions:** Management noted that price increases were taken in Northeast states (Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh), while discussions are ongoing with governments in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
5. **Nitin Gupta (Kotak Securities) on UK FTA benefit:** Management stated that ~100 bps has been baked into the upper end of the FY29 margin guidance (no Q2 benefit due to existing inventory).
6. **Mudit Daga on Working Capital:** Management noted annualized net working capital days are at ~65 days on a gross basis, factoring in Telangana's 6-month overdue cycle (approx. Rs. 550 crore).

### One-line takeaway
Tilaknagar Industries reported robust Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 173% YoY driven by solid secondary volumes and integration of Imperial Blue, while navigating packaging and ENA inflation through cost optimization and reiterating its FY29 margin and debt-reduction targets.
