# Fratelli Vineyards Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue is growing, EBITDA has turned positive in H1, and core brands/RTD are seeing strong traction despite regional policy headwinds.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Net Revenue: Rs 91.4 crore (up 10% YoY from Rs 83.4 crore).
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Profit of Rs 1.4 crore (compared to a loss of approximately Rs 1 crore in H1 FY26).
- H1 FY27 Gross Margin: 78% (Q2 FY27 gross margin stood at 76.7%).
- J'NOON H1 growth: 23% YoY.
- Shotgun RTD H1 revenue: Doubled, touching nearly 100,000 cases (H1 RTD sales at approximately Rs 18.5 crore vs Rs 8.5 crore last year).
- Distribution: 32,000 touchpoints across 28 states (Shotgun in over 11,000 touchpoints).
- Rights issue: Raising up to Rs 29.87 crore.
- Stuck Government Receivables: Approximately Rs 18 crore (VAT refund from Maharashtra and dues from Telangana).

### Highlights
- J'NOON (luxury) grew 23% YoY in H1; total luxury segment growth was muted at 4-5% due to a temporary high base and CSD degrowth for SETTE.
- Shotgun RTD reached ~100,000 cases in H1; management targets 200,000 cases by the end of FY27. Introduced a new Jamun flavor and expanded to Karnataka.
- Expanded footprint internationally to 18 countries (entering 4 new ones in H1) and plans domestic entry into Jammu & Kashmir and TASMAC (Tamil Nadu) in FY27.
- Rights issue of up to Rs 29.87 crore opened (closing November 17); promoters subscribing to their entire entitlement and any unsubscribed portion.
- Vineyard tourism project has been deferred/slowed down; management wants to focus bandwidth on the core winemaking business and will relook at viability next financial year. (Note: Continues previous plans of deferral noted in past calls).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management deferred broader revenue growth guidance for FY27 and FY28 until the Q3 results/season review.
- Gross margin guidance for FY27 maintained at 77% to 80%.
- Expected net profitability (PAT breakeven) to potentially occur in Q1 or Q2 of next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Delhi and Uttar Pradesh faced regulatory excise challenges in Q1; Delhi recovered well in Q2, while UP is seeing recovery in Q3. UP is down ~30% for the company on an H1 basis.
- Telangana emerged as one of the strongest markets in H1. Kerala, Goa, Orissa, and Pondicherry also supported growth.
- HoReCa segment split is approximately 65:35 relative to retail for the bottles business; RTD is 97-98% in retail.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin softened slightly to 76.7% in Q2 (vs 79-80% earlier) due to product mix change (higher RTD contribution of Rs 18.5 crore) and a moderate, industry-wide increase in sugar costs.
- Other expenses rose 44%, driven by RTD marketing expenses (AMP spend at 9-10% of RTD sales; overall bottle spend at 5-6% of net sales). This elevated AMP range (8-10% for RTD) is expected to persist for 2-3 quarters.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Existing manufacturing capacity is sufficient for RTD to grow double from current levels without additional capex.
- Rights issue proceeds will be utilized to strengthen the balance sheet, settle trade payables, fund winery equipment and oak barrel requirements at subsidiaries, and for general corporate purposes.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt levels are considered comfortable with healthy debt-equity ratios; rights issue proceeds will temporarily reduce working capital utilization rather than drive a major debt reduction.
- Around Rs 18 crore is stuck in government receivables (Maharashtra VAT and Telangana dues).
- Holding higher bulk wine inventory than desired; corrective measures taken to reduce it over the next few quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Regulatory and excise policy volatility in key states like Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka impacting premium segment growth.
- Delayed government receivables (VAT refunds/dues) straining cash flows.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Deepesh Sancheti (Maanya Finance) asked about luxury segment growth vs J'NOON:** Management explained SETTE was flat due to a high base in CSD last year, though civil markets did well.
- **Deepesh Sancheti asked about vineyard tourism:** Management confirmed the 10-acre hospitality project is deferred, and land is on a flat dry lease from the promoter family for farming.
- **Ankur asked about gross margin decline:** Management attributed it to the rising share of RTD sales and higher sugar costs, reiterating the 77-80% full-year gross margin band.
- **Uttam Purohit asked about weak cash flows and interest coverage:** Management noted that easing stuck government receivables (Maharashtra/Telangana) and reducing bulk wine inventory will improve cash flows.
- **M.N. Sarda asked about net profit breakeven and stuck VAT:** Management expects net profitability by Q1/Q2 next year and stated ~Rs 18 crore is stuck in government dues with no interest paid.

### One-line takeaway
Fratelli Vineyards reported a 10% revenue rise and turned EBITDA positive in H1 FY27, driven by strong RTD scaling, even as regulatory headwinds in UP and Karnataka weighed on the premium wine segment.
