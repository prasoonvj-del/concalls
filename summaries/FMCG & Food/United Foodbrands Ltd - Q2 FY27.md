# United Foodbrands Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the fourth consecutive quarter of strong, broad-based performance, robust volume-led growth, and strengthening unit economics.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 429 crores, up 40.9% YoY
- **Consolidated SSSG:** 24.5% YoY
- **Dine-in Transaction Volumes:** Grown ~56% YoY (Consolidated); 60.8% YoY for Barbeque Nation India
- **Delivery Business Growth:** 47% YoY (Consolidated)
- **Barbeque Nation India SSSG:** 27.8%; Revenue growth: 41.4%
- **Barbeque Nation International SSSG:** 26.5%; Revenue growth: 59% YoY; Dine-in transaction volume growth: 64% YoY
- **Premium CDR Revenue Growth:** 28.5%; SSSG: 7.8%; Dine-in volume growth: ~25%
- **Network Additions:** 9 new restaurants opened during Q2; total network at 274 restaurants; 19 restaurants under construction
- **Gross Margin:** 65.5% (down ~30 bps QoQ)
- **Pre-Ind AS Restaurant Operating Margin (ROM):** 13.4% (expansion of ~520 bps YoY; absolute growth of 130% YoY)
- **Mature Portfolio Pre-Ind AS ROM:** 14.7% for Q2; 15.5% for H1
- **New Restaurant Portfolio Pre-Ind AS ROM:** 7.4% in Q2 (vs 6.1% in Q1 FY27)
- **Pre-Ind AS Adjusted Operating Margin:** 6.1% (expansion of ~500 bps YoY)

### Highlights
- Multi-engine portfolio (Barbeque Nation India, International, and Premium CDR) delivering consistent growth.
- ~91% of dine-in volumes driven through captive channels (app, website, reservation call center, and walk-ins).
- Pre-reserving customers within dine-in grew to >80% (compared to ~70% a year ago).
- Loyalty program adoption grew ~3x YoY, driving a higher share of repeat business.
- International business launched value-added marketing initiatives, digital rollouts in the Middle East, and session/day-wise analytics.
- FY27 network expansion target: on track to cross 300 restaurants by the end of FY27; medium-term plan to add 40 to 50 restaurants annually, with an internal target of ~500 restaurants by FY30.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track to cross 300 restaurants by the end of FY27 and add 40 to 50 restaurants annually (upgraded FY30 target from ~400 to 425–450 restaurants, with an internal target of 500).
- Long-term SSSG expectation: 5% to 7%.
- Management expects new store cohort drag to continue narrowing and margins to follow as directional levers play out.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth is entirely volume-led.
- Demand environment remains strong, though Q2 saw seasonal vegetarian periods like Shraad and Shravan.
- Barbeque Nation positioned as a value-for-money, trusted destination for celebration and group dining.
- Big Buffet concept expanding successfully in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets (operating in ~25% of restaurants), widening the total addressable market (TAM) toward 600 restaurants across 700 districts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin impacted by ~30 bps QoQ due to input category inflation.
- Employee costs reflect ongoing expansion hiring, with backend/store wage inflation tracked at 6%–7% and 2%–3% respectively.
- Corporate overheads increased due to backend capability hires and incremental provisions for future loyalty program redemptions (~0.3% of overall sales).
- Pre-Ind AS mature portfolio ROM holds at 14.7% for Q2, while new store cohort ROM improved to 7.4%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 9 restaurants opened in Q2; 19 under construction for Q3 and Q4.
- Expansions funded primarily from internal accruals.
- New store design rightsized to ~3,000 sq. ft. to improve throughput per sq. ft. and reduce rental/utility overheads.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt position marginally higher during the quarter due to capex for expansion, largely offset by operating cash flows.
- Operates on prudent leverage relative to underlying business.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Inflationary environment in input categories is slightly higher than expected, impacting sequential gross margins.
- Seasonal impact of vegetarian periods (e.g., Shravan/Shraad, and Navratri in North India) creates temporary dips in volumes and regional SSSG.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital) on increasing average revenue per outlet (INR 6.4 crores H1 FY27) towards potential INR 9–10 crores:** Management (Rahul Agrawal) stated the pathway relies on throughput optimization (especially weekday/weekend lunches), deeper captive channel engagement/loyalty, delivery scaling (Barbeque in a Box, UBQ, Dum Safar), and calibrated selective price hikes once volumes stabilize. (Did not dodge)
2. **Keshav Bagri (ArthosAlpha) on corporate cost increases and H2 margin expectations:** Management explained the sequential rise was due to backend/digital/marketing hires and a ~0.3% sales impact from provisions for increased loyalty program redemptions. They believe corporate ratios will scale down with revenue growth. (Did not dodge)
3. **Viraj Mehta (Enigma Investment Partners) on Premium CDR SSSG and bandwidth focus:** Management clarified that Premium CDR is a separate legal entity managed by a founder CEO, operating well with mature margins upwards of 18% in South India, while newer markets (Mumbai/Delhi) are in a brand-building phase. (Did not dodge)
4. **Vivek Kumar (Geometric Securities) on sustainable SSSG and Q3 momentum:** Management stated that while recent quarters averaged ~20% (recovering past ground), a sustainable long-term SSSG is 5% to 7%, driven by volume growth and throughput optimization. (Did not dodge)
5. **Manjeet Buaria (Saamya Advisors) on new store drag at an accelerated pace of 45-50 additions:** Management stated that the gap between mature and consolidated margins is shrinking (down to 1.3%), and new cohorts are ramping up well, so acceleration should not pose a margin drag. (Did not dodge)

### One-line takeaway
United Foodbrands delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 40.9% revenue growth and 24.5% SSSG, driven entirely by volume expansion, structural improvements in unit economics, and robust captive channel engagement.
