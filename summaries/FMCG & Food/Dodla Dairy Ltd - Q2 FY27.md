# Dodla Dairy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Healthy revenue growth driven by volume and realizations, but profitability remains under pressure due to elevated raw material and input costs.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: INR 1,196 crore (up 17.4% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 64.1 crore
- EBITDA Margin: 5.4%
- PAT: INR 36.6 crore (up to 3.1% margin)
- H1 FY27 Revenue: INR 2,394 crore (up 18.2% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: INR 129 crore
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR 77.2 crore (3.2% margin)
- Milk Procurement Volume: 21.3 lakh liters per day (up 9.3% YoY)
- Milk Sales Volume: 14.2 lakh liters per day (up 8.4% YoY)
- Average Milk Procurement Cost: INR 43.2 per liter (compared to INR 41 per liter in Q1 and INR 37.3 per liter in Q2 FY26)
- Average Milk Sales Realization: INR 61.9 per liter (compared to INR 59.4 per liter in Q1 and INR 57.3 per liter in Q2 FY26)
- Value-Added Products (VAP) Revenue: INR 327 crore (up 11.7% YoY)
- Curd Volume Growth: 444.7 metric tons per day (up 23% YoY)
- Africa Business Revenue: INR 179 crore (up 56% YoY)
- Orgafeed (Feed) Revenue Growth: 40% YoY
- OSAM Revenue: INR 82.37 crore (Volume: 1.33 lakh LPD, EBITDA margin: 1%)
- H1 FY27 Capex incurred: INR 66 crore

### Highlights
- Implemented a milk price increase of ~INR 2.5 per liter during the fag end of Q2; full impact expected in Q3.
- Spread between milk sales realization and procurement cost narrowed to INR 18.7 per liter (vs INR 20 per liter in Q2 FY26).
- Packing material costs rose 41.6% YoY.
- Board approved a joint venture in India with Meadow India Holdings Limited (40% Dodla, 60% Meadow) to market and sell B2B dairy ingredients.
- Inventory built up by INR 76 crore in H1 FY27, total inventory at INR 232 crore as of September 2026 (including SMP and butter). Cash and bank balance at INR 659.4 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins for India operations to return to 7.5% to 8% in Q3 (October-December quarter) and sustain going forward.
- If cooperatives implement larger price hikes as announced, management will commensurately increase sales prices to improve profitability.
- Maharashtra liquid milk operations to start in March-April, and powder operations by September.
- Africa greenfield operations targeted to run by the beginning of FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Summer-oriented VAP demand moderated due to seasonality (monsoon in Q2), but festive demand for sweets, ghee, and festive products expected to benefit Q3.
- Paneer volume grew 64% (+2.5 tons per day); lassi doubled (up 163%, ~15,000 liters); ice cream and other categories grew.
- Dairy farming is slowly shifting from unorganized backyard farming to organized per-acre mainstream farming with higher productivity. Cow milk production is rising while buffalo milk is diminishing due to meat demand and flush-to-summer variations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Procurement prices reached peaks early (front-loaded in Q1/Q2); management expects status quo with no major immediate price hikes in procurement outside minor local variations of INR 0.20-0.30 per liter.
- Elevated operating costs due to input inflation, packaging, transport, fuel, minimum wages, and shift in product mix.
- Curd realization at INR 59.15 per liter (gross margin 19%, EBITDA margin 12%). Buttermilk and lassi margins at 20% to 23%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex of INR 66 crore primarily towards Maharashtra expansion. Total project costs (Maharashtra, Africa, OSAM) may see 5-10% inflation over project period.
- Planning a potential cattle feed plant in Maharashtra once the Itkal plant reaches higher utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance at INR 659.4 crore as of September 2026.
- Total inventory at INR 232 crore as of September 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated raw milk procurement prices and general inflation (logistics, packaging, minimum wages) keeping profitability under pressure.
- Potential pressure on feed raw materials (maize) due to competing government policies like ethanol and compressed biogas blending.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhishek Mathur (Systematix) asked about procurement cost trends and pricing strategy.** Management stated procurement has peaked and will remain elevated; future price hikes depend on cooperative actions, and margins are targeted to return to 7.5%-8% in Q3.
2. **Nikhil Upadhyay (SIMPL) asked why procurement prices rose in Q1/Q2 despite building inventory.** Management explained prices were front-loaded due to farmer-level input cost pressures (feed/fodder) and industry-wide trends, but supply variability is reducing.
3. **Yash (Awriga Capital) asked about the Meadow India JV details.** Management clarified it is an exploratory B2B joint venture with a small initial investment (INR 1 crore for 40%) to research and develop tailor-made dairy ingredients rather than consumer-facing products.
4. **Ritesh Shah (Investec) asked about state-wise procurement breakdown and El Nino impact.** Management provided state volumes (Andhra Pradesh 4.76, Karnataka 5.1, Maharashtra 2.99, Tamil Nadu 4.25, Telangana 0.45 lakh liters) and noted El Nino impacts were slight in Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu.
5. **Shirish Pardeshi (Motilal Oswal) asked about OSAM integration progress and competition.** Management noted they are fixing plant automation (CIP) and transitioning from an agent model to direct farmer payments (targeting 70-80% by March), competing primarily against state cooperatives (Sudha, Medha) and newer entrants.

### One-line takeaway
Dodla Dairy reported strong 17.4% YoY revenue growth to INR 1,196 crore in Q2 FY27, but margins were squeezed by early procurement cost inflation, prompting a late-Q2 price hike and expectations of a margin recovery to 7.5%-8% in H2.
