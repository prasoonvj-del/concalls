# Godrej Consumer Products Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong broad-based underlying volume growth across India, Indonesia, and Africa, with double-digit profit growth.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue growth: 19% YoY (15% in constant currency terms)
- Consolidated underlying volume growth (UVG): 8%
- Consolidated EBITDA growth: 12% YoY
- Consolidated net profit (PAT): Rs 523 crore, up 14% YoY
- Stand-alone business sales growth: 13%, driven by 7% UVG
- Stand-alone business trade inventory correction impact: Estimated 150 bps
- Home Care value growth: 19%
- Personal Care value growth: 8%
- Indonesia sales growth: 20% (8% UVG); EBITDA growth: 12%
- GAUM (Godrej Africa, USA and Middle East) sales growth: 40% in INR terms (23% in constant currency terms), with 16% UVG; EBITDA growth: 43%

### Highlights
- Second consecutive quarter of high-teens revenue growth, high single-digit UVG, and double-digit profit growth.
- GAUM delivered its 7th consecutive quarter of double-digit top-line growth and 11th consecutive quarter of double-digit bottom-line growth.
- Air fresheners and home care liquids performed strongly; market share gained in Household Insecticides (HI).
- Godrej Fab maintained a strong double-digit growth trajectory; Godrej Spic scaled nationally during Q1 and witnessed healthy consumer repeat and sequential growth.
- Launched Godrej Rizz in selected markets across West and South India; launched Godrej Zap (stain removers online).
- Cinthol body wash scaled sequentially; Muuchstac face wash progressed beyond the business plan.
- Hair colors maintained momentum and gained market share; perfumes and deodorants delivered strong double-digit growth.
- HI incense stick launched in Africa with good early traction.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management held FY27 guidance: high single-digit UVG in stand-alone and double-digit revenue and EBITDA growth at a consolidated level.
- Management stated they remain on track to deliver and have the confidence to exceed guidance in certain areas as revenues are running ahead of plan in H1.
- Management plans to focus on EBITDA YoY rather than EBITDA percentages due to commodity inflation, aiming to maintain growth in EBITDA at a consolidated level.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Subdued monsoon conditions noted in India; El Nino created tailwinds in Indonesia (increased mosquito infestation) while having a mild negative impact in India.
- Detergents are benefiting from industry formalization and a shift from powders to liquids.
- Soaps and detergents experienced aggressive price increases, and margins are still below normative levels; further pricing is expected.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost environment remained significantly challenging with high inflation across crude-linked derivatives, palm oil, and commodity inputs, leading to one of the highest inflationary impacts in recent years.
- Impact of inflation on India and Indonesia portfolios was approximately high single digit.
- Mitigation actions include stringent pricing actions, large cost savings programs, media and trade-level investment optimization, and increased salience of digital investments for higher ROI.
- Commodity inflation in Q3 is expected to be mildly higher compared to Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- GAUM is operating with very high capacity utilizations that are constraining growth, with capacity expansions scheduled in the next few months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade inventory correction of approximately 150 bps was executed in the stand-alone business to maintain healthy channel inventory levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High commodity inflation across crude derivatives, palm oil, and other inputs.
- Subdued monsoon conditions in India during the quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on digital advertising and management transition:** Management stated they are building capabilities to move to top percentile in digital advertising over the next few quarters and are running through a robust process to hire an India CEO with Godrej values and an entrepreneurial mindset.
- **Percy (IIFL Capital) on Personal Care and soaps slowdown:** Management explained that soaps saw a trade inventory correction, large SKU buildup, and price-volume recalibration amidst high commodity inflation.
- **Harit Kapoor (Investec) on advertising spends:** Management noted that A&P spend declines are a combination of category slowdown/inflation in soaps and detergents where share of voice is optimized, and better media practices/agency efficiencies delivering higher reach at lower costs in other portfolios.
- **Jay Doshi (Kotak Securities) on international sustainability (Indonesia and GAUM):** Management stated that Indonesia's growth had a few bps benefit from El Nino weather conditions, while GAUM's growth is a structural shift driven by core growth and incubation of new categories across high capacity utilizations.

### One-line takeaway
Godrej Consumer Products delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 19% revenue growth and 8% UVG, driven by robust performance across India, Indonesia, and Africa, despite facing significant commodity cost inflation.
