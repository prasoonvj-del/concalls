# Honasa Consumer Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth (32%) and volume growth (30.5%) driven by core brand Mamaearth and successful scaling of younger brands.

### Key numbers
*   Revenue growth: 32% YoY.
*   Volume growth: 30.5%.
*   EBITDA: INR 110 crore.
*   PAT: INR 90 crore.
*   Cash generated: INR 83 crore.
*   Focus categories growth: 35%+.
*   E-commerce growth: 20%+.
*   General Trade (GT) secondary sales growth: 40%+.
*   Modern Trade (MT) offtake growth: 40%+.

### Highlights
*   **Mamaearth:** Accelerated to high-teens growth; Rice face wash is now the #1 face wash; Rosemary shampoo crossed INR 100 crore ARR.
*   **The Derma Co:** Second brand to cross INR 1,000 crore ARR; EBITDA margin entered the teens.
*   **Young Brands:** Growing at 40%+ (excluding The Derma Co).
*   **BTM Ventures:** Reached INR 150 crore ARR (100% growth since acquisition).
*   **Market Share:** Gained 350 bps in face washes and 160 bps in shampoos.
*   **New Launch:** Entered the fragrance category with "FIKN," an elixir-based brand with a patented design and 12-hour stay claim.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management maintains a 5-year CAGR goal of high-teens for the company and double-digit for Mamaearth.
*   Management expects the current year to perform better than the planned CAGR growth profile.
*   Management targets 100-150 bps EBITDA margin expansion annually to reach 15% in 5 years.
*   Management prioritizes growth over margin expansion if reinvestment opportunities arise.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Fragrances:** India penetration is 3% vs 11% globally; the category is shifting toward e-commerce.
*   **Quick Commerce:** Management views this as a "branded purchase play" with high search-led demand; believes the format favors brands with high velocity due to constrained physical space.
*   **Gen Z:** Represents 45% of the buying cohort in B2C categories, particularly in e-commerce.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin improvement: 300-350 bps from mix impact, 100 bps from operating leverage, and 50 bps from seasonality.
*   Inflation: Packaging material costs are rising due to crude oil trends; management expects Q2 impact but plans to offset this via calibrated price increases.
*   Inventory: Distribution system is operating with less than 30 days of inventory.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Distribution: Focused on 100 cities; GT reach is 50,000 outlets for The Derma Co.
*   Nutraceuticals: Created "Honasa Health" subsidiary; currently in the diligence/precedent process for the Fluence Pharma acquisition.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Company maintains a negative working capital position.
*   Collections are at the highest-ever levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Inflationary pressure on packaging materials (crude oil derivatives).
*   Seasonality: Q1 is a stronger quarter for face wash/sunscreen; management noted Q2 may see a sequential dip.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Vivek M (Jefferies) on growth sustainability:* Management stated they expect to beat the planned CAGR growth profile this year.
*   *Vivek M (Jefferies) on Quick Commerce share:* Management explained they track category-level data with partners; brand strength drives search-led demand, which is critical for the format.
*   *Videesha Sheth on scaling younger brands:* Management noted the playbook involves finding differentiated propositions; they are currently re-hashing packaging/propositions for Aqualogica.
*   *Jay Doshi on fragrance failure in Mamaearth:* Management explained early experiments lacked product-market fit (PMF); they decided to launch dedicated fragrance-first brands (FIKN) instead.
*   *Nitin Shakdher on raw material inflation:* Management stated inventory management helped Q1, and they plan to use calibrated price increases to offset Q2 inflationary impacts.

### One-line takeaway
Honasa delivered strong Q1 FY27 growth driven by core brand Mamaearth and successful scaling of new brands, while maintaining a focus on long-term margin expansion and category diversification into fragrances and nutraceuticals.
