# Parag Milk Foods Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue driven by strong volume growth and successful navigation of milk inflation through a premium product mix.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained; management reiterated confidence in growth trajectory and the INR 10,000 crore long-term aspiration.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "transient volume slowdown" in B2B (Q1) to "healthy demand" and "strong volume growth" in Q2.
- **New topics:** Board approval for the amalgamation of Bhagyalaxmi Dairy Farms; specific capex breakdown of INR 205 crore for paneer and cheese capacity expansion.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on revenue growth (23% YoY) and margin stability despite inflationary pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 1,241 crore (up 23% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** INR 2,185 crore (up 18% YoY).
- **Volume Growth:** 12% (Q2).
- **Flagship Categories (Ghee, Cheese, Paneer, Curd):** INR 778 crore (63% of revenue, 20% value growth, 7% volume growth).
- **New Age Business (Pride of Cows, Avvatar):** INR 154 crore (12% of revenue, 71% growth).
- **Milk Procurement:** 21 lakh litres/day (up 5% YoY).
- **Milk Price:** INR 45/litre (up 18% YoY, 7% QoQ).
- **Gross Profit:** INR 323 crore (up 24% YoY).
- **Gross Margin:** 26% (vs 25.8% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** INR 101 crore (up 37% YoY, excluding one-off).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.1% (vs 7.3% YoY).
- **PAT:** INR 42 crore (up 42% YoY).
- **Net Debt:** INR 678 crore (vs INR 484 crore in March 2026).

### Highlights
- Highest-ever quarterly revenue achieved.
- Board approved the amalgamation of Bhagyalaxmi Dairy Farms to unlock operational synergies.
- *Note: This call continues the pattern of aggressive capacity expansion and structural consolidation noted in our records.*

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects high single-digit volume growth in core categories (Paneer/Ghee) for coming quarters.
- No further price increases planned for core categories.
- New Age business expected to reach 25% revenue contribution as part of the long-term INR 10,000 crore goal.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong shift from unorganized to organized/branded dairy products due to consumer focus on purity and traceability.
- HoReCa business grew in double digits.
- Raw milk output growth expected at only 4% for the year due to weather conditions and higher costs.
- Paneer market: 70-80% is currently unorganized/analogue, presenting significant conversion potential.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Milk cost increased by INR 7/litre YoY; gross margin per litre improved from INR 14 to INR 17.
- A&P spend: INR 20 crore "exceptional" spend this quarter for brand building; annual budget remains range-bound at INR 130-150 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total outlay of INR 205 crore approved.
- **Paneer:** Capacity to increase 4x (20 MT/day to 80 MT/day) by June 2027 (INR 100 crore investment).
- **Cheese:** Capacity to double (60 MT/day to 120 MT/day) by Q2 FY28 (INR 105 crore investment).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased due to strategic inventory buildup for the festive season and inflation.
- Debt funded via ECLGS 5.0 and new working capital lenders.
- Management does not foresee debt levels decreasing materially due to ongoing capex requirements.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Milk price softening unlikely in Q3 due to drought and fodder availability issues.
- Inflationary pressure on raw materials persists.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Inventory levels:** Management stated high inventory is a strategic choice for the festive season and inflation, expected to stabilize.
- **Margins:** Management emphasized a blended portfolio approach; New Age products are contributing to improved gross margins.
- **Competition:** Management does not expect a price war; competitive advantage lies in brand strength and distribution.
- **Debt:** Management clarified that debt will not decrease materially as it is being deployed for ongoing capex.
- **Paneer potential:** Management highlighted that 70-80% of the paneer market is unorganized, providing a long runway for growth.

### One-line takeaway
Parag Milk Foods achieved record revenue of INR 1,241 crore, leveraging strong volume growth and a premium product mix to navigate milk inflation, while initiating significant capacity expansion in cheese and paneer.
