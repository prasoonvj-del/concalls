# Sheetal Cool Products Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 16.8% YoY, margins improved, and management announced corporate actions to enhance stock liquidity.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 65.3 crore (Q2 FY27) vs Rs 55.9 crore (Q2 FY26); +16.8% YoY.
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 198 crore vs Rs 168.8 crore (H1 FY26); +17.3% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 8.4 crore (12.8% margin) vs Rs 6.3 crore (11.3% margin) in Q2 FY26.
*   **PAT:** Rs 3.2 crore vs Rs 1.6 crore (Q2 FY26).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 10.2 crore.
*   **Debt-Equity Ratio:** 0.6x (vs 0.3x in FY26).
*   **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 228 days (vs 212 days in FY26).

### Highlights
*   **Corporate Actions:** Board approved a 1:2 stock split (face value Rs 10 to Rs 5) and a 5:1 bonus issue. Authorized share capital increased to Rs 68.5 crore to support these actions.
*   **Product Launches:** Launched 4 products, including premium variants (Jumbo Mango Dolly, Jumbo Chocobar) and new flavors (Red Velvet, Pista Kunafa candy).
*   **Strategy:** Management is focusing on premiumization (increasing milk fat content to 8-10%) and deepening distribution in metro markets to counter seasonality.
*   **Record Note:** The company continues its pivot to a diversified FMCG model, with the call confirming the expansion into RTE/RTC and international markets noted in our previous records.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Long-term Target:** Management aims for Rs 1,000 crore revenue by FY30.
*   **Capex:** Planning ~Rs 10 crore capex for next summer to add machinery and capacity.
*   **RTE/RTC:** Expect 100% capacity utilization within 2 years (currently at 40%).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Seasonality:** Ice cream consumption dips in winter/monsoon; company mitigates this by pushing non-seasonal dairy (paneer, ghee) and focusing on metro markets.
*   **Geographic Mix:** 65% of revenue from Gujarat, 35% from other states.
*   **Milk Procurement:** 1.5 lakh liters/day in summer; 70,000-80,000 liters/day in other seasons.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** Improved to 39.8% (Q2 FY27) from 35.1% (Q2 FY26).
*   **Inflation Management:** For products <Rs 30, management reduces quantity (ml); for products >Rs 30, they pass on price hikes to consumers.
*   **Logistics:** High costs in non-Gujarat states; management plans to set up "child plants" in high-revenue states to localize production and protect margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Freezers:** Targeting 10,000 freezer deployments; >50% completed.
*   **Renewable Energy:** 90% of electricity requirements met via solar and wind.
*   **Funding:** Future expansions will be funded through a mix of debt and equity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Current Ratio:** 2.3x.
*   **Procurement Model:** Uses exclusive aggregators and bulk milk chilling points rather than direct farmer procurement to keep operational complexity and capex lower.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Working Capital:** Cash conversion cycle increased to 228 days.
*   **Logistics:** High transportation costs in non-core markets squeeze margins.
*   **Seasonality:** Heavy reliance on ice cream remains a structural risk.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why not procure milk directly from farmers like peers?** A: Management stated it increases operational complexity and capex; they prefer paying a premium to aggregators to ensure quality.
*   **Q: Why has growth been stagnant in recent years?** A: Management attributed it to insufficient freezer deployment; they have now changed strategy to aggressively deploy 10,000 freezers.
*   **Q: How do export margins compare to domestic?** A: Management noted exports provide better margins than the domestic business.
*   **Q: Status of Quick Commerce?** A: It is in the early stages and currently a "burning" (loss-making) channel, but the company is in talks with partners to list their full range.
*   **Q: Why the 1:2 split and 5:1 bonus?** A: To enhance stock liquidity and broaden the retail investor base.

### One-line takeaway
Sheetal Cool Products is aggressively scaling its distribution (10,000 freezers) and RTE/RTC portfolio to reach a Rs 1,000 crore revenue target by FY30, while utilizing corporate actions to improve stock liquidity.
