# HMA Agro Industries Ltd - Q2 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2026-27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy revenue growth, nearly doubled standalone EBITDA in H1, and significant profitability improvement.

### Key numbers
* **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 2,733.11 crore (YoY growth ~17.7%, QoQ growth ~31.90%)
* **Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 130.35 crore (YoY growth ~70.56%, QoQ growth ~123.13%)
* **Standalone EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 4.51% (compared to 3.37% in Q2 FY26 and 2.67% in Q1 FY27)
* **Standalone PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 108.41 crore (YoY growth ~68.18%, QoQ growth ~153.67%)
* **Standalone H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 4,805.25 crore (YoY growth ~48.42%)
* **Standalone H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 178.63 crore (YoY growth ~97.98%)
* **Standalone H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 151.15 crore (YoY growth ~104%)
* **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 2,779.48 crore (YoY growth ~28.96%, QoQ growth ~31.71%)
* **Consolidated EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 153.54 crore (YoY growth ~16.70%, QoQ growth ~89.44%)
* **Consolidated EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 5.25% (compared to 6.10% in Q2 FY25-26 and 3.84% in Q1 FY27)
* **Consolidated PBT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 132.48 crore (YoY growth ~13.7%)
* **Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 104.57 crore (YoY growth ~16.46%, QoQ growth ~107%)
* **Consolidated PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 3.76% (compared to 4.17% in Q2 last year and 2.39% in Q1 FY27)
* **Consolidated H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 4,889.80 crore (YoY growth ~49.17%)
* **Consolidated H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 234.58 crore (YoY growth ~58.30%)
* **Consolidated H1 FY27 PBT:** Rs 194.99 crore (YoY growth ~64.63%)
* **Consolidated H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 155.08 crore (YoY growth ~90.39 crore last year)
* **Capacity Utilization:** 65-70%

### Highlights
* Delivered robust top-line and bottom-line growth across standalone and consolidated segments for Q2 and H1 FY27.
* Standalone EBITDA nearly doubled in H1 FY27 to Rs 178.63 crore compared to Rs 90.23 crore in H1 FY25-26.
* Client base spans 49 countries across four major continents.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management stated that growth momentum will continue due to strong global demand and food security storage needs.
* Management expects shipping/logistics costs to eventually normalize, which could improve margins by roughly 1.5% to 2%.
* No major large-scale capex is required currently as existing capacity utilization is at 65-70%.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Global buying patterns are increasing due to uncertainties and food storage needs.
* Indian products are more economical and competitive compared to global peers in Australia, New Zealand, and Brazil, which face higher cattle-rearing and logistics costs.
* Key growth regions include Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia), South Africa, GCC/MENA region, and Egypt.
* Buffalo meat is 100% organic by default (no growth hormones used) and meets rising global demand for protein-rich, natural diets.
* Diversifying into other food product baskets such as rice, seafood, and fruits to complement existing offerings.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Shipping freight rates have increased significantly (6 to 10 times at peak), impacting overall logistics costs, but have stabilized over the last two months with no major increases.
* Premium chilled meat segments and vacuum-chilled offerings provide higher realizations and margin expansion opportunities.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Current capacity utilization is around 65-70%, leaving headroom to scale volumes without heavy capex.
* Capital expenditure is restricted to routine requirements and small machinery; capital needs are primarily for working capital to support higher orders.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Working capital requirements may increase if order volumes grow further.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Elevated shipping and trade costs/freight volatility globally.
* Geopolitical uncertainties and Middle East tensions impacting logistics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Priya Mehta (Individual Investor):** Asked about the impact of elevated logistics costs and margin protection. Management replied that sales prices already factor in current costs, and normalization of freight could boost margins by 1.5-2%.
* **Praveen Gupta (Export Group):** Inquired about the price difference between Indian and Australian red meat. Management explained that Australian live cattle prices are much higher due to their farming/rearing culture, making Indian meat roughly half the price.
* **Deepak Karwa (Individual Investor):** Asked about the competitive advantage and demand drivers for buffalo meat. Management emphasized HMA's 4-generation legacy, strong brand reputation, supply chain reliability, and the inherent organic nature of Indian buffalo meat.
* **Atharva Kulkarni (Individual Investor):** Questioned whether revenue growth was volume-led or value-led, and about capex plans. Management stated growth is driven by *both* volume and value (due to higher logistics costs passed through), and reiterated that no major capex is planned since capacity utilization is at 70%.

### One-line takeaway
HMA Agro delivered strong double-digit revenue and profit growth in Q2/H1 FY27, backed by robust international demand for Indian buffalo meat and ample existing capacity headroom.
