# Krishival Foods Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong revenue and EBITDA growth across segments despite a challenging inflationary environment, while executing on capacity expansion and distribution reach.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 85.66 crore (up 28% YoY from Rs 66.67 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 13.87 crore (up 53% YoY), EBITDA margin 16.6%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 6.82 crore (up 18% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 174.62 crore (up 50% YoY from Rs 116.19 crore)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 24.56 crore (up 63% YoY), EBITDA margin 13%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 12.42 crore (up 22% YoY)
- **Nuts & Dry Fruits Segment Revenue (Q2):** Rs 63.16 crore (up 19% YoY), EBITDA of Rs 10.44 crore (up 33% YoY), EBITDA margin 17%
- **Ice Cream Segment Revenue (Q2):** Rs 22.50 crore (up ~65% YoY), EBITDA of Rs 3.53 crore (up 566% YoY), EBITDA margin 16%
- **Ice Cream Deep Freezers:** 18,752 units as of end of Q2 FY27
- **Ice Cream Retail Touchpoints:** 35,000+ across Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Tamil Nadu
- **Ice Cream Distributors:** 205 (added 85 new distributors in Q2; ~95% retention rate)
- **Export Shipment (Singapore, Q2):** USD 384,000

### Highlights
- Added 10 metric tonnes finished kernel production capacity per day in May 2026, taking total installed capacity to 20 metric tonnes per day (Nuts division).
- Appointed Mr. Vivek Kumar Jain as Chief Business Officer (CBO) effective October 23, 2026.
- Expanded ice cream distribution geographically into Tamil Nadu (Vellore, Hosur, Erode, Tiruvannamalai, Salem).
- Secured the first premium retail experiential outlet location for "Mellow & Co." at Apte Road, Deccan, Shivaji Nagar, Pune (1,400 sq. ft. carpet space), slated for launch shortly, with plans for 25 FOCO outlets in and around Pune.
- Maintained strong B2B partnerships in the nuts division with mithai partners like Prashant Corner, Meetha by Radisson, Haldiram Delhi, and Home Sweet Gurgaon.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets approximately 50% growth in top line and 50% growth in profitability for FY27.
- Targeting equal contribution from healthy snacks and ice cream divisions by FY29.
- Set a target of consolidated revenue of Rs 1,500 crore by FY29.
- Targeting ice cream capacity utilization of ~37% in the current financial year, with full capacity utilization expected by Q1 FY29 (representing Rs 600 crore top line potential).
- Targeting ice cream deep freezer network of 27,000 units by the end of FY27.
- Nuts segment capacity targeted to reach 40 metric tonnes finished kernel production per day by March 2028.
- Management expects FY29 to act as the financial inflection point for net profit expansion and operating leverage.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Broader FMCG operating environment in Q2 FY27 was challenging due to inflationary pressure and evolving consumer demand (retail inflation at 5.4%, food inflation at 7.74% in September 2026).
- Consumers are balancing affordability with premiumization, prioritizing product quality, taste, nutrition, convenience, and differentiation.
- Ice cream segment experienced strong traction despite July and August being seasonally slack due to monsoons.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost pressures (El Niño impact, crude oil, sugar, and crop patterns) were successfully managed via backward integration and sound supply chain/sourcing execution.
- Nuts business EBITDA margin is targeted at 15% to 16%; management notes EBITDA beyond 18% in nuts is very challenging.
- Ice cream EBITDA margins came in higher than expected (16% in Q2 vs. 8% internal target) due to sound input cost management, packing material control, and the depot distribution system.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Finished nut kernel capacity stands at 20 metric tonnes/day, moving towards 40 metric tonnes/day by FY28.
- Ice cream manufacturing plant in Sambhajinagar has an installed capacity of 1 lakh litres/day (along with 20 metric tonnes/day milk products and 10 metric tonnes/day bakery products).
- "Mellow & Co." retail expansion will utilize a Hub-and-Spoke FOCO (Franchisee-Owned, Company-Operated) model to manage real estate/overhead costs and prevent brand dilution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory levels increased due to a strategic build-up to counter El Niño, crop patterns, and inflationary pressures.
- Receivables increased due to a sales spike in September (covering festive/Diwali season build-up) and a ~300% growth in ice cream sales during H1, alongside a shift in the nuts B2C mix toward e-commerce and modern trade (rising from 5-7% to 20% in H1).
- Borrowings in the nuts division are not expected to increase over the next 1–1.5 years; marginal debt increases may occur in the ice cream division next year to support expansion, which management expects to be balanced by top-line and EBITDA growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External macroeconomic challenges highlighted include inflationary pressures, food inflation, El Niño weather disruptions, and volatile crude oil and sugar prices.
- High depreciation and finance costs currently constrain net profit expansion relative to EBITDA growth due to upfront investments and deep freezer additions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ishan Modi (Individual Investor) on Nuts Division Growth vs. Ice Cream Growth:** Management explained that nuts are growing at 15%-20% in a staggered capacity ramp-up mode (utilizing 25% of new capacity initially), whereas ice cream is growing on a lower base with high industry momentum.
2. **Ishan Modi on Spiked Receivables and Inventories:** Management clarified that September sales spiked heavily ahead of Diwali/festive demand, ice cream sales tripled in H1, and inventory was intentionally built up as a buffer against El Niño and raw material inflation.
3. **Mahesh Kumar (MU Investments) on Rationale for FOCO Model in Mellow & Co.:** Management stated the FOCO model avoids brand dilution and poor customer experience, while keeping company overheads low (franchisee pays rent and electricity) allowing quicker breakeven.
4. **Nimesh Pandeya (Individual Investor) on Export Strategy for Nuts:** Management highlighted that Singapore exports reached USD 384,000 in Q2 (300 touchpoints), and the company is bullish on entering the USA market next year with dedicated distribution, followed by Japan, to help balance domestic seasonality and secure high margins.
5. **Ishan Modi on High Depreciation and Net Profit Inflection Point:** Management noted that current depreciation reflects upfront investments in deep freezers and backend capacity, guiding that **FY29** (targeting Rs 1,500 crore top-line) will be the inflection point where operating leverage drives net profit expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Krishival Foods delivered robust H1 FY27 revenue (up 50% YoY) and EBITDA growth (up 63% YoY) driven by margin outperformance in ice cream and steady execution in nuts, keeping the company on track for its FY27 and FY29 targets.
