# FMCG & Food - Personal & home care: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Q2 FY27 witnessed strong top-line and volume growth across major players (led by HUL at 15% USG, GCPL at 19%, and Emami and Honasa both at 36%), driven by resilient demand and distribution expansions. However, the sector faced a severe test from commodity and crude-linked raw material inflation, which compressed gross and operating margins for several companies—most notably Jyothy Labs—prompting calibrated price hikes and aggressive cost efficiencies.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board note that rural demand is stable-to-improving, often growing slightly ahead of urban markets. Quick-commerce has emerged as a powerhouse channel, particularly for scaling premium portfolios and new launches.
*   **Divergence:** While mass essential categories face elasticity pressures and inflation headwinds, premium portfolios are vastly outperforming (e.g., Dabur's premium portfolio growing at 4x the company average; Honasa's focus categories up 40%+). Meanwhile, Jyothy Labs reported that general trade remains subdued compared to modern trade and e-commerce.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Agreements:** Almost all companies cited significant input cost inflation driven by crude derivatives, palm oil, and packaging materials. Companies are deploying calibrated price increases (e.g., Bajaj planning ~5% in Q3, Emami implementing ~5.5%) and cost-saving programs (e.g., Dabur’s Project Samriddhi/IRIS, HUL’s A&P productivity tools) to protect absolute profit pools.
*   **Disagreements/Divergences:** The impact was unevenly distributed. Jyothy Labs suffered a massive margin squeeze (gross margin crashing to 34.8% and EBITDA margin down to 2.5%), breaking historical resilience patterns. Conversely, Zydus Wellness and Bajaj Consumer Care successfully expanded or maintained robust margins (Bajaj at 25% EBITDA) through operational efficiencies and pricing power.

## Capex & capacity
*   Physical capacity expansion took a back seat to distribution and reach scaling. Companies focused on expanding direct reach (e.g., Bajaj's Aarohan 2.0 aiming for 10% annual direct reach growth; Dabur's Retail Next and Project Saksham; HUL adding 30,000 GT outlets). An exception is Godrej Consumer Products (GCPL), which noted that GAUM (Africa, USA, Middle East) is operating near capacity constraints, triggering scheduled expansions.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Honasa Consumer:** Shifted tone from Q1 caution regarding a potential sequential dip to reporting its "highest growth quarter since public listing" (36% revenue growth).
*   **Dabur India:** Pulled forward its mid-term strategic vision exercise (Vision 2031) by 1.5 years following an 18-month strategic assessment.

## What to watch next quarter
*   The effectiveness and consumer acceptance of upcoming price hikes (such as Bajaj's ~5% hike in Q3).
*   Whether crude-linked raw material inflation peaks or continues to pressure gross margins for companies like Jyothy Labs and HUL.
*   Post-Diwali rural demand recovery and execution of acquisitions (e.g., Dabur's Sesa integration starting Dec 1, 2026).

## Company one-liners
*   **Bajaj Consumer Care:** Successfully driving volume growth via distribution expansion (Aarohan) and operational efficiencies while using calibrated price hikes to protect profits.
*   **Dabur India:** Advancing its Vision 2031 roadmap while funding an extra 150–200 bps of A&P via cost savings to maintain double-digit revenue growth.
*   **Emami Ltd:** Strong domestic volume growth and strategic investments are offsetting international geopolitical headwinds and margin pressures.
*   **Godrej Consumer Products:** Delivered 19% revenue growth and 8% volume growth, overcoming high commodity inflation across key global geographies.
*   **Hindustan Unilever:** Achieved 15% USG and 7% volume growth by taking calibrated price hikes to protect market share while driving premiumisation.
*   **Honasa Consumer:** Delivered its strongest post-listing quarter with 36% revenue growth, driven by focus categories and favorable channel mix.
*   **Jyothy Labs:** Navigating a severe margin squeeze from crude-linked inflation by prioritizing market share and affordability over immediate price hikes.
*   **Marico Ltd:** Reported strong all-round performance with highest profit growth in 50 quarters and raised full-year profitability guidance.
*   **Zydus Wellness:** Delivered 74.6% net sales growth and a 271.7% jump in EBITDA, powered by domestic demand recovery and gross margin expansion.
