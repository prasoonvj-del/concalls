# Varun Beverages Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q3 & 9M CY2026
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth across India and international markets, offsetting raw material pressures through operational efficiencies.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 66,075.6 million (up 33.2% YoY)
- 9M CY2026 Revenue: Rs. 218,146 million (up 23.3% YoY)
- Consolidated sales volume: 354.4 million cases (up 28.7% YoY; India up 22.5%, International up 39.9%)
- EBITDA: Rs. 14,267.1 million (up 25.1% YoY)
- 9M CY2026 EBITDA: Rs. 52,746 million (up 19.9% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 21.6% (down 140 bps YoY)
- PAT: Rs. 8,306 million (up 13.5% YoY)
- 9M CY2026 PAT: Rs. 32,023 million (up 15.5% YoY)
- Gross margin: 53.1% (down 334 bps YoY)
- Volume mix: CSD 73.3%, juice-based drinks 4.8%, packaged drinking water 21.9%
- Interim dividend: Rs. 0.50 per share (25% of face value), total cash outflow Rs. 1,691 million

### Highlights
- Sales volume growth supported by extensive distribution network, expanded manufacturing capacities, and chilling infrastructure investments.
- Realizations supported by growth in higher-value categories (dairy) and premium offerings (‘Ad Rush’, ‘Evervess’ mixers range).
- Merger of Twizza with BevCo completed effective August 1, 2026; beverage operations started in Kenya with new brands ‘BOLT UP’ and ‘XTREME FIZZ’.
- Exclusive distribution agreement signed with Mondelez in Zimbabwe.
- Incorporated KIVA Spirits and Company Limited to explore ready-to-drink beverages, alcoholic beverages, and allied products.
- Low-sugar or no-sugar products share in India increased from 44% in Q3 CY2025 to 88% in Q3 CY2026, offsetting a ~16% increase in sugar prices.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to sustain healthy growth long-term using expanded capacities and market infrastructure.
- Capex for international markets in CY2027 is projected at $90 million to $100 million.
- India capex for CY2027 is projected at around Rs. 1,000 crore (including a greenfield plant at Suratgarh, Rajasthan for Visi-coolers and one PET line).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India volume growth extended the positive trend seen since May; international markets saw robust growth (39.9%), with Africa out of capacity in most places.
- Energy drink segment (Sting) is recovering following FSSAI labeling clarifications regarding the word 'energy'.
- Rs. 10 price point contributes only 0.2%-0.3% to overall volume; management is not focusing on expanding it as unit economics are unsustainable.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin moderated by 334 bps YoY due to higher packaging costs (preforms, shrink films, closures, cans) from geopolitical disruptions (~300 bps pressure).
- Sales of non-beverage products (preforms and caps) grew ~104%, impacting gross margin in India by ~100 bps due to higher COGS.
- rPET mix stands at close to 25%, helping negate overall virgin resin costs.
- Management stated gross margins have likely troughed based on present price trends.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investing in capacity additions in Morocco, Zimbabwe (along with Zambia support), DRC, and South Africa (Twizza integration).
- Reached over 4.2 million outlets, aiming to add a couple of hundred thousand outlets annually.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Depreciation and finance costs increased by 34.5% and 53.5% respectively, due to the Twizza acquisition in South Africa and associated debt funding.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions impacting packaging material costs and fuel prices.
- Uncertainty regarding the duration of high raw material prices and potential industry-wide price-pack architecture changes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on gross margins and packaging costs:** Management clarified that packaging purchases were unhedged beyond Q2, leading to higher cost absorption this quarter. More than half of the 3%-4% impact has been mitigated through internal cost reductions.
- **Vivek Maheshwari (Jefferies) on competition and realization trends:** Management noted that CSD realizations reversed downward pressure due to operational efficiency, leverage, and mix, independent of raw material inflation.
- **Anand Shah (Mirae Asset) on Alcobev strategy:** Management stated the focus will be on Prestige-and-above portfolios and RTDs, leveraging existing off-premise distribution relationships in India and Africa.
- **Arnab Mitra (Goldman Sachs) on international margins:** Management explained that international margins held up better due to Twizza's backward integration and scale benefits.
- **Jay Doshi (Kotak) on AI adoption:** Management highlighted extensive use of AI for outlet geo-mapping, route optimization, order management towers, B2B app 'Bandhan' (0.5 million monthly active retailers), and cooler purity tracking.

### One-line takeaway
Varun Beverages delivered strong consolidated volume growth of 28.7% and revenue growth of 33.2% in Q3 CY2026, successfully absorbing packaging cost headwinds through robust operating leverage, product innovation, and low-sugar portfolio transitions.
