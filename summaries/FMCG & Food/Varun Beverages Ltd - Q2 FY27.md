# Varun Beverages Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q3 CY2026  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong consolidated revenue and volume growth in both domestic and international markets, despite raw material price pressures.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue from operations:** Rs. 66,075.6 million, up 33.2% YoY.
* **Consolidated sales volume:** 354.4 million cases, up 28.7% YoY (India volume up 22.5% YoY, International volume up 39.9% YoY).
* **EBITDA:** Rs. 14,267.1 million, up 25.1% YoY.
* **EBITDA margin:** 21.6%, down 140 bps YoY.
* **Gross margin:** 53.1%, down 334 bps YoY.
* **PAT:** Rs. 8,306 million, up 13.5% YoY (compared to Rs. 7,315 million in Q3 CY2025).
* **Net realization per case:** Improved by 2.2% YoY globally (up 0.5% in India).
* **Interim Dividend:** 25% of face value (Rs. 0.50 per share), with total cash outflow of Rs. 1,691 million.

### Highlights
* **Twizza Merger:** Effective August 1, 2026, Twizza's merger with BevCo was completed to unlock operational synergies.
* **African Operations:** Commenced beverage operations in Kenya and launched two new brands, ‘BOLT UP’ and ‘XTREME FIZZ’.
* **Distribution Agreement:** Signed an exclusive distribution agreement with Mondelez in Zimbabwe (where Mondelez had an existing ~$11 million business).
* **Alcobev Expansion:** Incorporated KIVA Spirits and Company Limited to explore opportunities in Ready-To-Drink (RTD) beverages and alcoholic beverage segments.
* **Low/No Sugar Mix:** Low/no sugar products share in India doubled to 88% in Q3 CY2026 from 44% in Q3 CY2025.
* **Category Mix:** Carbonated Soft Drinks (CSD) accounted for 73.3%, Packaged Drinking Water 21.9%, and Juice-based drinks 4.8% of volume mix.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management expects long-term healthy volume growth supported by expanded manufacturing capacities and chilling infrastructure.
* Gross margins are expected to have troughed in Q3 CY2026 based on current commodity price trends.
* Planning aggressive numeric and weighted distribution expansion for CY2027, adding a couple of hundred thousand outlets per year to the existing ~4.2 million outlet reach.

### Demand & industry commentary
* India demand recovered following weather disruptions earlier in the year, supported by low per-capita consumption headroom.
* Sting energy drink demand and volumes are recovering post-FSSAI labeling adjustments; Sting pack size was downsized from 300 ml to 250 ml in May/June.
* African markets are seeing structural growth due to low penetration and growing consumer demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Packaging Costs:** Geopolitical issues increased preforms, shrink films, closures, and cans costs, creating ~300 bps margin pressure in India and 272 bps internationally.
* **Sugar Costs:** A ~16% increase in sugar prices was largely offset by transitioning 88% of India portfolio to low/no sugar options.
* **Non-Beverage Sales:** Sales of preforms and caps grew ~104% YoY in India, diluting gross margin by ~100 bps due to higher COGS while contributing positively to overall operational earnings recovery.
* **rPET Usage:** Recycled PET (rPET) constitutes ~25% of total packaging, helping control resin input costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **CY2027 India Capex:** ~Rs. 1,000 crore, including a greenfield plant at Suratgarh (Rajasthan) for Visi-coolers and a PET line.
* **CY2027 International Capex:** $90 million to $100 million for adding capacity lines across South Africa, Zimbabwe, Morocco, and DRC.
* Capacity was constrained in Morocco and Zimbabwe during peak periods; supply from Zambia is supporting Zimbabwe.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Depreciation grew 34.5% YoY and finance costs increased 53.5% YoY, primarily due to the acquisition of Twizza and associated debt funding.
* Other income fell 18.8% YoY due to stable currency movements relative to the prior year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Prolonged geopolitical tensions affecting crude oil prices and PET raw material packaging costs.
* Turmoil in distributor/retailer channels regarding FSSAI energy drink labeling regulations (though impact is diminishing).

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama):** Asked about gross margin outlook and packaging cost drivers.  
  *Management:* Stated that packaging input costs rose due to geopolitical disruption post-February. Over half of the 3-4% gross margin pressure has been mitigated via operational efficiencies and downsizing Sting from 300 ml to 250 ml.
* **Vivek Maheshwari:** Asked about energy drinks (Sting) status following regulatory issues.  
  *Management:* Explained FSSAI restricted the word "energy" for carbonated beverage registrations. After initial distributor stocking hesitancy (up to ~25% negative impact), month-on-month volumes have turned positive again.
* **Latika Chopra:** Asked if gross margins have troughed and regarding Rs. 10 price point strategy.  
  *Management:* Confirmed gross margins have likely troughed under current commodity prices. Stated Rs. 10 price point accounts for only 0.2–0.3% of total volume and VBL will not pursue non-sustainable unit economics.
* **Arnab Mitra:** Asked about international margin resilience and input cost pass-through.  
  *Management:* Attributed international margin strength to backward integration via Twizza, operating scale, and stable African currencies.

### One-line takeaway
Varun Beverages delivered strong 28.7% volume growth driven by international expansion and domestic recovery, successfully mitigating significant packaging cost inflation through operational scale and portfolio optimization.
