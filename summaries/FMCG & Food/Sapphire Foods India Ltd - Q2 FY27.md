# Sapphire Foods India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Best quarter of revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth in 16 quarters, driven by strong transaction growth and value offerings despite an inflationary environment.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** INR 885 crores, up 20% YoY
- **Consolidated Restaurant EBITDA:** INR 128.6 crores (Margin: 14.5%), up 21% YoY
- **Consolidated Restaurant EBITDA Margin (percentage of restaurant sales):** 11.5%, up 20 bps YoY
- **Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA:** INR 62.4 crores (Margin: 7.1%), up 39% YoY (margin up 100 bps YoY)
- **Consolidated Adjusted PBT:** INR 12 crores (1.4%)
- **Consolidated PBT (post Ind AS):** Minus INR 60 lakhs (-0.1%)
- **KFC SSSG:** 14% (close to double-digit after adjusting for the Navratri shift)
- **KFC Revenue Growth:** 26% YoY
- **KFC Gross Margin:** 67.7%, up 50 bps YoY
- **KFC Restaurant EBITDA Margin:** 14.5%, up 70 bps YoY
- **Pizza Hut SSSG:** 5% (highest in the last 7 quarters)
- **Pizza Hut Revenue Growth:** 7% YoY
- **Pizza Hut Gross Margin:** 76.3%, up 190 bps YoY
- **Pizza Hut Restaurant EBITDA:** Loss of 2.8%
- **Sri Lanka Revenue Growth:** 9% in LKR terms
- **Sri Lanka SSSG:** 5%
- **Sri Lanka Restaurant EBITDA Margin:** 12.5%
- **Total Restaurant Count:** 1,086 (added 15 KFC restaurants, 1 Pizza Hut in Sri Lanka during the quarter)
- **Cash Balance:** Close to INR 250 crores on books
- **Borrowings:** INR 35.9 crores as of September end

### Highlights
- Delivered the best quarter of revenue growth (20%) and adjusted EBITDA growth (39%) since COVID (last 16 quarters).
- Focus on driving transaction growth and Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG) through compelling everyday value offerings (INR 99, INR 149 in Tamil Nadu) and increased marketing spend against the industry tide.
- Dine-in and takeaway (DITA) grew faster than delivery, with KFC DITA mix improving to 57%.
- Digital kiosks implemented in 80% of KFC stores.
- Pizza Hut recorded its second consecutive quarter of positive SSSG (5%) with double-digit Same-Store Transaction Growth (SSTG). Pizza Hut online discounting reduced.
- Sri Lanka business maintained positive SSSG despite short-term inflation challenges from the Middle East conflict.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects cost inflation to peak in Q3 FY27.
- Combined SSSG for Q2 FY27 and Q3 FY27 expected to be in high single digits after factoring in the Navratri festive shift.
- KFC store expansion guidance maintained at 60 to 80 stores for the year.
- Pizza Hut expansion in calendar year 2026 to remain largely neutral; management is awaiting CCI approval for greater coordination between Devyani and Sapphire before aligning on brand strategy.
- Marketing spend increase guided at 50 to 100 bps for KFC and 100 to 150 bps for Pizza Hut for FY27.
- No additional borrowings foreseen in the near future given a healthy cash balance of ~INR 250 crores.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating in a difficult inflationary environment impacting all cost lines (food, minimum wages, utilities) and consumer discretionary spending.
- Minimum wages increased substantially across many states, led by Karnataka (up 26%).
- Consumer recruitment strategy (INR 99 and INR 149 meals) successfully attracting new cohorts, with 60% to 70% repeat transactions observed.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Inflation observed across food (poultry inflation impacting KFC, cheese prices covered until Q2 FY27), minimum wages, and energy (gas and electricity; energy inflation heavily impacting Pizza Hut).
- Price hikes of 2% to 3% taken in KFC and Pizza Hut at the beginning of the financial year / end of Q1 FY27.
- Management stated they are less concerned about short-term gross margin percentage and are prioritizing transaction growth and SSSG over gross margin points.
- Dine-in and takeaway channels yield significantly higher final margins than delivery due to the absence of delivery commissions and costs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 15 KFC restaurants and 1 Pizza Hut in Sri Lanka during the quarter.
- 60% to two-thirds of KFC store openings are in metro towns (>1 million population); 70% to 75% of openings are in existing cities where Sapphire is already present.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total borrowings stood at INR 35.9 crores at September end, structured as multipurpose long-term loans.
- Cash balance stands at close to INR 250 crores.
- Finance cost increases are primarily driven by Ind AS 116 lease accounting related to new store additions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High inflationary environment across raw materials (poultry, cheese), energy, and minimum wages putting pressure on costs and consumer discretionary income.
- Short-term margin challenges in Sri Lanka due to inflation fallout from the Middle East war.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay Global) asked about the festive shift impact and resulting normalized SSSG:** Management clarified that combined Q2 and Q3 SSSG will be high single digits, implying normalized SSTG will be slightly lower than high single digits. Management did not dodge.
- **Harish Advani (Axis Capital) asked about repeat rates from INR 99 / INR 149 meal customers:** Management answered that 60% to 70% repeat transactions are observed, and returning customers exhibit higher ticket sizes. Management did not dodge.
- **Saksham Tyagi (Desvelado Advisory) asked about Pizza Hut store expansion and brand strategy:** Management explained that expansion is neutral for calendar year 2026, and they are awaiting CCI approval for alignment between Devyani and Sapphire before driving brand turnaround and growth. Management did not dodge.
- **Anuj D. (Antique Stock Broking) asked about the sustainability of Pizza Hut gross margins and value mix:** Management reiterated they will prioritize SSSG and transaction growth over gross margin points, and value mix remained stable sequentially. Management did not dodge.
- **Rudraksh Raheja (ithought Financial Consulting) asked about timelines for Pizza Hut store-level profitability:** Management stated once CCI approval comes in and both companies align, it will take a couple of quarters for the brand to fire on all angles. Management did not dodge.

### One-line takeaway
Sapphire Foods delivered its best revenue (20%) and adjusted EBITDA (39%) growth in 16 quarters, successfully leveraging everyday value promotions to drive strong transaction-led SSSG despite persistent cost inflation.
