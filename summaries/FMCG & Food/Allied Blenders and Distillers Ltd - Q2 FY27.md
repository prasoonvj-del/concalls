# Allied Blenders and Distillers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth, cross-millionaire brand performance, and strategic progress in backward integration and premiumisation, despite supply chain headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Income from Operations (Consolidated):** Rs 1,152 crore (up 15.8% YoY, 17.1% QoQ)
- **Total Volumes:** 10.0 million cases (up 11.1% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 45.3% (expansion of 90 bps YoY); like-to-like gross margin excluding supply chain disruptions: 47.9% (expansion of 342 bps YoY)
- **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 143 crore (up 10.0% YoY, 19.2% QoQ); EBITDA margin: 12.4% (lower by 66 bps YoY)
- **Like-to-Like EBITDA (excl. supply chain impact):** Rs 172 crore (up 32.3% YoY); like-to-like EBITDA margin: 14.9% (expansion of 186 bps YoY)
- **Reported PAT:** Rs 69 crore (up 9.7% YoY, 52.0% QoQ)
- **Like-to-Like PAT (excl. supply chain impact):** Rs 91 crore (up 44.3% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Income from Operations:** Rs 2,135 crore (up 10.9% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 263 crore (up 5.8% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Reported PAT:** Rs 114 crore (vs. Rs 119 crore in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 269 crore (vs. Rs 147 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 1,273 crore as of September 2026 (vs. Rs 981 crore as of March 2026)
- **Net Debt to EBITDA:** 2.2x; Net Debt to Equity: 0.8x
- **Estimated impact of global supply chain disruptions:** Rs 29 crore in Q2 FY27; Rs 53 crore in H1 FY27
- **ICONiQ White Volumes:** 3.2 million cases in Q2 FY27 (up 20.6% YoY); 6.2 million cases in H1 FY27 (up 26.8% YoY)
- **Mass Premium & Others Net Sales:** Rs 468 crore (up 15.9% YoY, 21.1% QoQ); Volumes: 5.5 million cases (up 15.5% YoY); Realisation per case: Rs 848
- **Prestige & Above Net Sales:** Rs 605 crore (up 13.4% YoY, 7.5% QoQ); Volumes: 4.5 million cases (up 6.1% YoY); Realisation per case: Rs 1,339
- **Malt Distillery Capex (Rangapur, Telangana):** ~Rs 75 crore investment, annual capacity ~4.0 million bulk litres
- **UP Bottling Plant Initial Capacity:** 6 million cases per annum (upgraded with ~Rs 40 crore investment)

### Highlights
- Quarterly volumes crossed 10 million cases for the first time.
- Achieved highest-ever monthly volume of over 3.5 million cases in September 2026.
- ICONiQ White exceeded 1 million cases every month of the current fiscal and recognized as the world's fastest-growing millionaire whisky brand for three consecutive calendar years (CY2023, CY2024, CY2025).
- Refreshed identity and premium packaging for Officer’s Choice Blue launched from September 2026 in UP, Maharashtra, Haryana, and West Bengal.
- Launched premium whisky *The Indian Edit* in September 2026 across 7 states.
- Commissioned captive malt distillery at Rangapur, Telangana in October 2026.
- Commenced commercial operations at the Moradabad bottling facility in Uttar Pradesh in October 2026, saving ~Rs 27 per case in franchise fees.
- India Ratings & Research upgraded ABD's rating by two notches to 'IND AA-' with a Stable outlook.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects top-line growth in line with mid-teens guidance.
- FY27 EBITDA margins are targeted to be broadly in line with FY26.
- ICONiQ White target: ~14 million cases for FY27 (over 25% growth) and aiming for 20 million cases in the next 2 years.
- ABD Maestro luxury portfolio turnover expected to reach ~Rs 80 crore in FY27 (vs. ~Rs 40 crore in FY26).
- Backward integration initiatives expected to provide an EBITDA margin benefit of ~300 bps by FY28 and an additional 150 bps in FY29.
- Total capex plan of ~Rs 1,600 crore over 3 years (with ~25% spent up to FY26; annual maintenance capex estimated at Rs 75 crore to Rs 100 crore).
- Leverage metrics expected to return within stated framework (Net Debt/EBITDA < 2.0x, Net Debt/Equity < 0.75x) on a full financial year basis.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth was broad-based across Mass Premium & Others and Prestige & Above, with all four millionaire brands growing sequentially.
- Andhra Pradesh brandy category witnessed strong consumer response, achieving near double-digit market share in the state in the mass premium category.
- Officer’s Choice Whisky witnessed a strong bounce back in the Southern region, supported by Eastern regions and exports.
- International footprint expanded to 42 countries in Q2 FY27 (from 36 in Q4 FY26).
- Management views Tamil Nadu as a major upcoming market opportunity akin to UP due to market liberalisation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded by 90 bps YoY to 45.3%, aided by brand mix, higher realisations, and PET backward integration, despite supply chain headwinds.
- Inflationary pressures remain active; global supply chain disruptions impacted H1 FY27 EBITDA by Rs 53 crore.
- India-UK Free Trade Agreement expected to provide a 70 bps margin benefit this year once bulk Scotch import duty clarity is achieved.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Rangapur PET bottle manufacturing facility continues to be EBITDA accretive.
- Minakshi Agro Industries (Aurangabad bottling unit subsidiary) expected to be operational in the next couple of months; ENA distillery expansion on track for H1 FY28.
- Announced setting up a 66 million bulk litre per year dual-mode distillery and expanding UP bottling capacity to 13 million cases per year by Q3 FY28.
- First overseas local production arrangement announced in Malaysia via an asset-light co-bottling model.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased by Rs 292 crore to Rs 1,273 crore as of September 2026, driven by milestone-based capex payments of Rs 351 crore in H1 FY27.
- Net Debt to EBITDA stood at 2.2x and Net Debt to Equity at 0.8x as of September 2026.
- Operating cash flow improved to Rs 269 crore in H1 FY27 (compared to Rs 147 crore in H1 FY26).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global supply chain disruptions and West Asian crisis creating inflationary pressures and component volatility (estimated Rs 53 crore impact in H1 FY27).
- Uncertainties raised by government authorities regarding the import duty of bulk Scotch under applicable HSN codes.
- State-level pricing constraints and regulatory dependencies.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhiraj Mistry (Jefferies) on ICONiQ White QoQ volume flatness:** Management clarified that Q1 to Q2 sequential flatness was an aberration, targeting 14 million cases for the year driven by upcoming festive season activations.
2. **Avnish Roy (Nuvama) on Malaysia co-bottling revenue recognition:** Management explained that raw/packaging materials are sent from India, and business surplus is recognized under other income via a contract bottling model.
3. **Mihir Shah (Nomura) on P&A growth slowdown vs. Mass Premium acceleration:** Management stated that P&A value growth was 13.4% (volume up 6.1%), but Mass Premium grew faster due to regional brand revival and Andhra Pradesh brandy gains; saliency will shift back towards P&A with new launches and season momentum.
4. **Harsh Sheth (ICICI Securities) on Maestro touchpoints and margins:** Management noted expansion to 8,000+ touchpoints across 80% of addressable markets, with retail sales integration expected to drive scale and turnover to ~Rs 80 crore in FY27.
5. **Nitin (HDFC Securities) on portfolio cannibalization risk:** Management denied cannibalization by ICONiQ White, stating ICONiQ is capturing new value-for-money demand, while core legacy brands are being refreshed with new packaging to revive volumes.

### One-line takeaway
Allied Blenders and Distillers delivered record Q2 volumes crossing 10 million cases with 15.8% revenue growth, driven by aggressive premiumisation and backward integration, while absorbing supply chain headwinds.
