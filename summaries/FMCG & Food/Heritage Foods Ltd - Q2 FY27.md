# Heritage Foods Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-19 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and sequential margin improvement achieved despite persistent inflationary headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 13,196 million (up 19% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 26,577 million (up 18% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 740 million (Margin: 5.6%).
*   **EBITDA Margin Change:** Improved 98 bps sequentially over Q1 FY27.
*   **PAT:** Rs 309 million.
*   **Milk Procurement:** 17.28 lakh liters/day (up 7% YoY).
*   **Milk Sales:** 12.35 lakh liters/day (up 2% YoY).
*   **VAP Revenue (excl. ghee/butter):** Rs 4,510 million (up 32% YoY).
*   **Broader VAP Revenue (incl. ghee/butter):** Rs 5,408 million (up 31% YoY).
*   **Heritage Nutrivet Revenue:** Rs 853 million (up 47% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **VAP Growth:** Broad-based growth led by lassi (89%), buttermilk (56%), ice cream (44%), paneer (21%), and curd (15%).
*   **Product Milestones:** Daily curd sales crossed 500 tonnes; quarterly yogurt volumes exceeded 100 tonnes; Nourish+ high-protein paneer accounts for >15% of the paneer portfolio.
*   **VAP Contribution:** Increased to 42.5% of total revenue in Q2 (from 37.9% YoY).
*   **Innovation:** Launched Nourish+ High Protein Curd, Greek Yogurt, and expanded Alpenvie ice cream range.
*   **Sustainability:** Wind farm (6.3 MW) received the Indian Wind Power Association's Best Performing Wind Farm Award for 2025-26.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Operating Leverage:** Management expects operating leverage to improve in Q3 as volumes and throughput increase.
*   **Procurement:** Management hopes for a buffalo flush post-Diwali to potentially ease procurement costs.
*   **Ice Cream:** Management aims to reach Rs 500 crore revenue in 6-7 years (excluding Get-A-Way).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Opportunity:** The organized dairy industry (estimated Rs 5-5.5 lakh crore) has significant headroom for growth through conversion from the unorganized sector.
*   **Regional Dynamics:** El Nino impact is mixed; some regions face drought while others have seen normal/above-normal rainfall.
*   **Strategy:** Focus remains on deepening presence in existing markets rather than geographic expansion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Procurement Inflation:** Average landed milk procurement cost increased 11% YoY to Rs 47.73/liter.
*   **Pricing:** Weighted average price hike taken was 8% (Milk ~6%, VAP 9-10%).
*   **Pass-through:** Price hikes are staggered; management noted that September price hikes will benefit Q3 financials.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Ice Cream:** Newly commissioned facility provides additional capacity for Alpenvie and Get-A-Way brands.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   The company facilitates third-party lending for farmers to meet working capital needs and herd expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Inflation:** Elevated milk procurement costs (11% YoY) continue to weigh on near-term profitability.
*   **Supply Constraints:** El Nino-related supply-side constraints and uncertainty regarding milk arrivals.
*   **Competition:** Acknowledged entry of large conglomerates into dairy, though management views this as category-expanding.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Can you pass on the 11% procurement inflation?** A: Management stated they cannot increase consumer milk prices by 11% directly, but VAP and premium products allow for better price realization.
*   **Q: Will margins improve in Q3?** A: Management expects operating leverage to continue improving; if raw milk prices stabilize or drop, margins will expand further.
*   **Q: Why is cow milk inflation high?** A: Management noted a widening supply-demand gap and a shift by producers/brands toward cow milk as a cheaper alternative to buffalo milk, driving up cow milk prices.
*   **Q: Impact of new competition?** A: Management believes the industry is large enough (Rs 14 lakh crore total) for all players to grow and that their strategy of "going deeper" differentiates them.
*   **Q: Is the worst of the procurement cycle over?** A: Management declined to speculate, noting they are in a "wait-and-watch" mode regarding the buffalo flush.

### One-line takeaway
Heritage Foods is successfully driving revenue growth and VAP expansion to offset high procurement costs, with management banking on operating leverage and a premium product mix to drive margin recovery.
