# Bajaj Consumer Care Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-21 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong volume growth and operational efficiencies delivered robust profitability despite raw material volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 233 crore (+2.7% YoY).
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 674 crore (+28% YoY).
*   **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 53 crore (25% margin).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 167 crore (+85% YoY).
*   **Q2 PAT:** Rs 70 crore (21% margin).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 141 crore (+75% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** 59.9% (Q2); 60.8% (H1).
*   **A&P Spend:** +7% YoY (Q2); +18% YoY (H1).

### Highlights
*   **Core Brand:** Almond Drops Hair Oil (ADHO) delivered double-digit unit volume growth.
*   **Project Aarohan:** Phase 1 & 2 (13 states) largely complete, adding 1 lakh+ outlets, 1,000+ distribution points, and closing 600+ van routes.
*   **Aarohan 2.0:** Management announced a new pan-India program aiming for 10% annual expansion in direct reach.
*   **Growth Portfolio:** Relaunch and new launches in amla oils, body lotion, and Banjara's; management reported improved profitability for this segment.
*   **International Business:** Continued rebound in Q2; growth ahead of company average (excluding Bangladesh, which faces a tough operating environment).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management maintains aspiration for double-digit long-term growth; expects high-teens growth for FY27.
*   **Pricing:** Management plans a ~5% price increase in Q3 (split between 2-3% frontal price hike and ml-age reduction) to manage cost inflation.
*   **Strategy:** Management remains optimistic about demand resilience despite the inflationary environment.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Channels:** General Trade (GT) and Organized Trade both grew in the mid-20s.
*   **Rural vs. Urban:** Rural business continues to grow marginally ahead of urban.
*   **Quick-commerce:** Remains the fastest-growing sub-channel.
*   **Strategy:** For large brands (ADHO), quick-commerce is a demand fulfillment channel; for premium/niche products, it serves as both demand creation and fulfillment.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Continued volatility in raw materials due to the West Asia war and edible oil inflation.
*   **Margin Strategy:** Management focuses on protecting the absolute EBITDA pool rather than percentage margins.
*   **Efficiencies:** Strong savings in fixed and employee costs helped maintain a 25% EBITDA margin.
*   **Manufacturing:** Guwahati factory production outsourced to a third party to drive operating efficiencies.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Focus remains on distribution expansion (Aarohan 2.0) rather than physical capacity expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management emphasized commitment to prudent financial management.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Volatility in key raw material prices.
*   Operating environment in Bangladesh.
*   Potential impact of sustained inflation on discretionary spending.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **ADHO Performance Drivers:** Management stated growth is a combination of category improvement and internal actions (Aarohan distribution expansion), estimating 2-3% of volume growth is directly attributable to distribution expansion.
*   **Quick-commerce Strategy:** Management clarified that the channel strategy is brand-specific, serving as fulfillment for mass brands and demand creation for premium/niche variants.
*   **Banjara's Strategy:** Management is leveraging the brand's strong equity in South India and merging distribution with Bajaj to enter larger, mass-market categories.
*   **Direct Distribution Efficacy:** Management believes 75-80% index to peers is the "sweet spot" for direct distribution, which will help execute better range presence and support the growth portfolio.
*   **Purchase of Stock-in-Trade:** Management explained the increase in purchase of stock-in-trade is due to outsourcing the Guwahati factory production to a third party.

### One-line takeaway
Management is successfully driving volume growth through distribution expansion (Aarohan) and operational efficiencies, while using calibrated price hikes to protect absolute profits amidst raw material volatility.
