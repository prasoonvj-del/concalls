# Emami Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong top-line double-digit growth across core and strategic portfolios despite input cost pressures and minor international headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,088 crore (+36% YoY; like-to-like +24% YoY).
- **Domestic Revenue Growth:** +49% YoY (+32% like-to-like).
- **Domestic Volume Growth:** +43% YoY (+26% like-to-like).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 222 crore (+25% YoY).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 194 crore (+29% YoY).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 144 crore (-3% YoY, impacted by tax rate normalization).
- **Gross Margin:** Moderated by 520 bps YoY.
- **Strategic Investments Contribution:** Contributed ~26% of domestic business (+73% YoY like-to-like).
- **Capital Allocation:** Completed share buyback of Rs 281 crore (73 lakh shares); declared 1st interim dividend of Rs 5/share (Rs 214 crore payout).

### Highlights
- **Skincare & Haircare:** Skincare grew 31% YoY (talc doubled, BoroPlus near 20s%); Hair & scalp care grew 30% YoY (Navratna 30s%, 7 Oils in One nearly doubled).
- **Healthcare:** Healthcare grew 11% YoY; Pain management and OTC grew in high-teens %.
- **Strategic Investments Performance:** Axiom and Brillare grew >2X; IncNut grew >50%; The Man Company (TMC) grew >30% YoY.
- **Channel Shift:** Organized channels reached 37% of domestic business; QuickCommerce represents ~15% of digital sales.
- **New Launches:** Launched BoroPlus baby care range (Maa range) priced 25%–50% lower than peers, and Kesh King Scalp First range targeting premium price points (Rs 500–1,000).

### Guidance & outlook
- **H2 Growth:** Management targets total business revenue growth of 18%–20% YoY in H2 FY27.
- **Strategic Brands Target:** Management raised full-year target for strategic investment portfolio to Rs 800+ crore (run-rate basis points to Rs 1,000–1,100 crore).
- **A&P Spends:** Expected to remain in the 18%–19% of sales range for FY27.
- **Price Hikes:** Full-year price hikes stand at ~5.5% to pass on cost increases.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Domestic & Rural:** Mixed performance across rural areas. Southern/Western regions and parts of UP saw stress due to lower rainfall/drought-like conditions; districts with good rainfall performed well. Demand expected to improve post-Diwali entering peak winter season.
- **International:** Q2 declined 2% YoY due to floods in Nepal and supply issues/geopolitical disruptions in Gulf/Iraq/Russia. Excluding Nepal and Iraq, international revenue grew 6% YoY. International business returned to double-digit growth in October.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin declined 520 bps YoY: 400 bps due to raw material cost inflation and 120 bps due to product/business mix changes post-integrations.
- Raw material inflation impact expected to persist through H2 FY27 due to inventory pricing, with input cost benefits accruing in the next fiscal.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Scaling strategic D2C brands beyond D2C into QuickCommerce, modern trade, and international markets.
- End-to-end AI and data analytics platform implementation across forecasting, supply chain, invoicing, and frontline booking expected to finish next quarter, with gains starting from April.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash distribution via share buyback of Rs 281 crore and interim dividend of Rs 214 crore.
- Effective tax rate normalized following the phase-out of fiscal benefits, leading to a 3% decline in PAT despite a 29% increase in PBT.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated input cost inflation weighing on gross margins.
- Supply chain disruptions due to regional conflict in the Gulf/Black Sea and delayed shipping of spray/gaseous OTC pain management goods (hazardous shipping restrictions, ~2% of global business affected).
- Volatility in rural demand across rainfall-deficient regions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama):** *How are pricing and R&D differentiated for baby care and Kesh King Scalp First launches?*  
  **Management:** Baby care is priced 25%–50% cheaper than market alternatives targeting mass segments via e-com/QuickCom. Kesh King Scalp First targets a premium positioning (Rs 500–1,000) using specialized R&D formulations (scalp detoxifying serums) while staying below higher-end imported/D2C peers.
- **Harit Kapoor:** *Where will A&P spends settle over the next two years?*  
  **Management:** Management is in spending mode to drive top-line momentum, but expects full-year A&P spends to stay within 18%–19% of sales.
- **Arnab Mitra:** *Breakdown of the 520 bps gross margin contraction between inflation and mix?*  
  **Management:** 400 bps was driven purely by raw material cost increases and 120 bps by product mix shift.
- **Rahul Jha:** *Why is international performance lagging peers like Dabur in Middle East/Bangladesh?*  
  **Management:** Peers have extensive international manufacturing facilities (11 plants for Dabur), whereas Emami relies heavily on exports from India. Geopolitical disruptions in the Black Sea and Hormuz/Iraq, along with export restrictions on gaseous OTC sprays (~2% of business), uniquely hampered Emami.

### One-line takeaway
Emami delivered strong top-line momentum (+36% consolidated revenue) driven by core volume growth and rapid strategic brand scaling, though raw material inflation compressed gross margins by 520 bps.
