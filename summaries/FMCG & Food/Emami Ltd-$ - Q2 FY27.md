# Emami Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong double-digit growth in core and strategic portfolios, with management noting international business has turned to growth in October.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,088 crore (+36% YoY).
*   **Domestic Sales:** +49% YoY (Volume growth +43%).
*   **Like-to-Like (LTL) Consolidated Revenue:** +24% YoY.
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 222 crore (+25% YoY).
*   **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 194 crore (+29% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 144 crore (-3% YoY), impacted by normalization of effective tax rate.
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 2,127 crore (+25% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 449 crore (+15% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin:** Declined 520 bps YoY (400 bps due to RM costs, 120 bps due to business mix).

### Highlights
*   **Core Portfolio:** Skincare grew 31% (Talc >2X, BoroPlus ~20s, Fair & Handsome double-digit); Hair/Scalp grew 30% (Navratna 30s, Kesh King high teens); Healthcare grew 11%.
*   **Strategic Investments:** LTL growth of 73%; contributed ~26% of domestic business. Axiom and Brillare sales >2X; IncNut >50%; The Man Company >30%.
*   **Channel:** Organized channels contribute 37% of domestic business; QuickCom accounts for 15% of digital sales.
*   **International:** Revenue declined 2% due to Nepal floods and Gulf supply chain disruptions. Excluding Nepal and Iraq, growth was 6%. Management stated October performance was positive.
*   **Corporate Action:** Completed Rs 281 crore share buyback (73 lakh shares); declared interim dividend of Rs 5 per share (payout Rs 214 crore).
*   *Note:* The PAT decline of 3% contradicts the "mixed" record of 2% decline; the transcript figure is used here.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Core Business:** Management expects 18%-20% growth for the second half of FY27.
*   **Strategic Investments:** Management raised guidance to Rs 800-850 crore for the year.
*   **A&P Spend:** Management guided for 18%-19% of sales for the full year.
*   **AI/Data:** Management expects to complete the AI platform rollout next quarter, with real gains expected from April 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Rural:** Described as a "mixed bag." South and West markets impacted by low rainfall/drought; U.P. performance mixed. Management expects post-Diwali pick-up.
*   **Pricing:** Implemented ~5.5% price increase for the full year to offset inflation.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Management expects second-half margins to improve as the company benefits from input cost corrections on current procurement.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Expanding Axiom in modern trade and QuickCom; IncNut expanding beyond D2C into marketplaces.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Geopolitical:** Ongoing regional conflicts in the Gulf and supply chain disruptions in Iraq/Russia/Ukraine impacting international business.
*   **Inflation:** Elevated input costs weighed on margins in Q2.
*   **Rural Volatility:** Rainfall deficits in specific regions impacting consumption.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on new launches:** Asked about baby care and Kesh King Scalp First. Management stated baby care is priced 25%-50% lower than competitors to target mass market; Kesh King Scalp First is R&D-led and positioned as a premium product.
*   **Abneesh Roy on strategic investments:** Asked about profitability. Management clarified they are in a growth/investment phase rather than just gestation, with plans to take products into General Trade (GT).
*   **Harit Kapoor on A&P:** Asked if 20% A&P is structural. Management guided to 18%-19% for the full year, prioritizing top-line growth.
*   **Arnab Mitra on International:** Asked about the decline. Management clarified that excluding disrupted geographies (Iraq/Nepal), the business is growing; October was positive.
*   **Rahul Jha on Strategic Investment guidance:** Asked if guidance could be upgraded. Management raised the target to Rs 800-850 crore.
*   **Saksham Tyagi on AI:** Asked about progress. Management stated they are ahead of schedule, with pilot phases showing impressive results.

### One-line takeaway
Strong domestic volume growth and strategic investment scaling are offsetting international geopolitical headwinds and margin pressure, with management signaling confidence in H2 performance.
