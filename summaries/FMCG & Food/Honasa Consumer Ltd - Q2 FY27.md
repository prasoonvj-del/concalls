# Honasa Consumer Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest growth quarter since IPO (36% revenue growth), doubling EBITDA, and strong volume-led performance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (15% EBITDA margin goal over 4 years).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from Q1's caution regarding a potential sequential dip in Q2 to reporting the "highest growth quarter since public listing."
- **New topics:** Hair color category transition (dye to crème) highlighted as a key opportunity; collaboration with Chaayos; new body wash category for The Derma Co.
- **Promises:** Delivered on focus category strategy (now 80%+ of revenue).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue growth:** 36% YoY.
- **Volume growth (UVG):** 31.3%.
- **EBITDA margin:** 12.7%.
- **EBITDA margin improvement:** 250-270 bps (A&P/mix) + 110-140 bps (operating leverage).
- **Mamaearth growth:** High-teens.
- **Younger brands growth:** 45%+ YoY.
- **Offline reach:** 3.2 lakh outlets (Mamaearth).
- **The Derma Co. ARR:** INR 1,000 crore+.

### Highlights
- Highest growth quarter since IPO.
- Focus categories (80% of revenue) grew 40%+.
- Market share gains: 140 bps in face wash, 50 bps in shampoo (Nielsen).
- Offline momentum: General Trade (GT) and Modern Trade (MT) grew 40% each.
- BBlunt hair color portfolio growing 100%+ YoY.
- *Record Note:* This quarter continues the pattern of strong volume-led growth and successful scaling of younger brands noted in Q1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the 15% EBITDA margin goal over the next 4 years.
- Management expects to hold the current EBITDA margin range (~12.5%) for the near term.
- Management plans to enter the hair color category with a new brand (details to follow).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's per capita beauty spend is low (lower than Bangladesh); significant headroom for growth.
- Key drivers: Women joining the workforce, rising men's awareness.
- Quick commerce: Styling categories unlocking due to 10-minute delivery hacks.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin managed at 70% despite crude oil cost pressures via price increases and cost management.
- EBITDA margin expansion driven by favorable channel mix (offline, QC) and A&P efficiency.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Mamaearth outlet reach: 3.2 lakh outlets (26% YoY growth).
- The Derma Co. outlet reach: 65,000.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Crude oil price volatility impacting packaging costs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Videesha Sheth (Ambit):* Asked about younger brand growth ex-Derma Co. Management confirmed 40%+ growth.
- *Nihal Mahesh Jham (Nuvama):* Asked if margin improvement is due to channel mix. Management confirmed channel mix (offline/QC) is a key driver of "other expenses" improvement.
- *Sanjay Manyal (ICICI):* Asked about BBlunt and hair color. Management confirmed 100%+ growth in BBlunt hair color and plans for a new hair color brand.
- *Nitin Gupta (Investec):* Asked about volume vs. value divergence. Management attributed it to MRP increases (crude impact) and channel/brand mix.

### One-line takeaway
Honasa delivered its strongest post-listing growth quarter, driven by volume, successful focus category execution, and favorable channel mix, while maintaining a long-term 15% EBITDA margin target.
