# Honasa Consumer Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest post-listing net sales growth (36% YoY) with doubling EBITDA and strong 31.3% volume growth.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (Target of reaching 15% EBITDA margin over 4 years; near-term margin range expected around 12.5%).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from strong Q1 performance to even stronger execution, delivering highest quarterly post-listing growth driven by focus categories and broad-based channel growth.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New focus on hair color market transition from dye to crème format and BBlunt hair color momentum; crude oil cost pressures managed via price hikes. Old topics like the Fluence Pharma acquisition and FIKN fragrance brand launch were not discussed.
- Last quarter's promises: Price increases taken to offset crude/packaging inflation were successfully executed, maintaining gross margins at 70%.

### Key numbers
- Net Sales Growth: 36% YoY
- Underlying Volume Growth (UVG): 31.3% YoY
- Gross Margin: 70%
- EBITDA Margin: 12.7% (EBITDA doubled YoY)
- EBITDA Improvement Drivers: 250–270 bps from A&P and channel mix efficiencies; 110–140 bps from operating leverage
- Channel Growth YoY: E-commerce 20%+, General Trade 40%, Modern Trade 40%
- Focus Categories Contribution: 80%+ of total revenue (grew >40% YoY)
- Younger Brands Growth: 45%+ YoY overall (40%+ YoY excluding The Derma Co.)
- Mamaearth Reach & Offtake: Available at 3.2 lakh outlets (ND up 26% YoY); Modern Trade offtake up 40%+ YoY; Same-store growth up 30%+ YoY

### Highlights
- Focus categories contribute 80%+ of total business and represent a Rs 50,000 crore market where Honasa currently holds ~5% market share.
- Mamaearth sustained high-teens growth across both online and offline channels; gained 140 bps market share in face wash and 50 bps in shampoo (Nielsen).
- The Derma Co maintained its ARR of Rs 1,000+ crore with a 1.2% face wash market share and offline reach of 65,000 outlets.
- BBlunt hair color portfolio grew over 100% YoY post-relaunch, benefiting from quick-commerce adoption for quick styling hacks.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its target of expanding EBITDA margins to 15% over a 4-year horizon.
- For H2 FY27, management expects to sustain EBITDA margins around the current ~12.5% trajectory.
- Management plans to evaluate launching in the hair color category under another existing brand in the near future.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Per capita BPC spend in India is among the lowest globally (lower than Bangladesh; Indonesia has 4x India's per capita consumption), providing structural long-term growth tailwinds over the next decade.
- Category expansion is propelled by rising female workforce participation and increased grooming adoption among men.
- The Indian hair color category is transitioning from dye to crème format, with non-black styling colors and toxin-free formulations driving penetration.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Managed crude-led raw material cost inflation by taking calibrated price increases while holding gross margins at 70%.
- A&P efficiency improved due to favorable channel mix towards offline and quick commerce, reducing third-party platform sales commission expenses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanded Mamaearth's direct numeric distribution to 3.2 lakh outlets (+26% YoY).
- Deepened distribution footprint in Modern Trade (40%+ YoY offtake growth) and General Trade (30%+ same-store growth).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash generation accompanied top-line expansion during the quarter.
- Inventory turns improved according to Nielsen data, leading to lower stock pressure across trade channels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material cost inflation linked to crude oil price fluctuations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Videesha Sheth (Ambit Capital) on younger brands growth excluding The Derma Co.:* Management confirmed that excluding The Derma Co., younger brands still grew 40%+ YoY.
- *Videesha Sheth (Ambit Capital) on drivers of Mamaearth's growth:* Management stated that high-teens growth is happening equally across online and offline channels, with 80% of growth coming from same-store distribution efficiency rather than new outlet additions.
- *Nihal Mahesh Jham (Nuvama) on margin sustainability:* Management explained that margin improvements were driven by channel mix shifts and operating leverage, and chose to conservatively guide to maintaining ~12.5% EBITDA margins for H2.
- *Sanjay Manyal (ICICI Direct) on hair color strategy:* Management shared that BBlunt hair color grew 100%+ YoY and revealed plans to introduce a hair color line under another portfolio brand.
- *Nitin Gupta (Emkay Global) on volume vs value growth divergence:* Management explained that MRP increases to offset crude inflation along with channel/brand mix changes accounted for the gap between 31.3% volume growth and 36% net sales growth.

### One-line takeaway
Honasa delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance with 36% net sales growth and 12.7% EBITDA margin, supported by high-teens growth in Mamaearth, 45%+ growth in younger brands, and offline distribution scaling.
