# Westlife Foodworld Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered the strongest performance in 13 quarters with double-digit growth in guest counts and same-store sales, alongside healthy margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR7.47 billion, up 16% YoY
- **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG):** 10.1% YoY
- **Operating EBITDA:** Up 29% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 13.1%, up 130 bps YoY
- **Restaurant Operating Margin:** 19.3%
- **Cash PAT:** INR526 million (7% of sales)
- **Inflationary Pressure:** ~190 bps across commodities, utilities, and employee costs absorbed during the quarter
- **Network Expansion:** 11 new restaurants opened; total footprint at 492 across 80 cities
- **App Downloads & MAU:** >58 million cumulative downloads, ~4.2 million monthly active users
- **Digital Contribution:** >75% of sales, up ~60 bps YoY
- **Channel Growth:** On-premise up 17% YoY; off-premise up 15% YoY

### Highlights
- Marked 30 years of McDonald's West and South in India (October); approaching the 500th restaurant milestone.
- Growth was broad-based across markets, regions (convergence between West and South), and channels, driven by higher guest counts and frequency.
- Everyday Value platform (including the INR99 entry meal and GMALite) and "Let's Family at McDonald's" platform drove consumer traffic.
- Optimized discounting rather than increasing promotional intensity, helping offset inflationary pressures without major pricing hikes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains on track for targets of **580+ restaurants by March 2028**.
- Annual store addition guidance maintained at **60 to 65 restaurants per year**.
- Per-store capital cost ranges between **INR3 crores to INR3.5 crores**.
- Full-year capex guidance expected to be within **INR250 crores to INR300 crores**.
- Profitability guidance maintained at **100 to 150 bps improvement in profitability year-on-year**.
- Momentum continued into October, providing confidence heading into the festive season.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Guest count grew in double digits in each month of Q2 FY27.
- Growth reflects a classical QSR cycle where volume/penetration growth precedes ticket size/value growth.
- Inflation is viewed positively by management as consumers trade down to trusted value formats like McDonald's.
- Competitive intensity from smaller/unbranded players was not seen as a major barrier; execution, value, and brand strength are key differentiators.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Absorbed ~190 bps of inflationary pressure across commodities, utilities, and employee costs (including extraordinary minimum wage inflation in Karnataka).
- Pricing strategy remains conservative: typically takes 2% to 4% price increases annually (absorbing ~50% of normal 6% to 8% inflation) while leveraging discount optimization and cost-saving initiatives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 11 new restaurants in Q2 FY27; total footprint stands at 492 restaurants across 80 cities.
- Per-store capex stands at INR3 crores to INR3.5 crores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in commodities (e.g., sharp spikes in oil) and extraordinary employee cost/minimum wage inflation (particularly in South India/Karnataka).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital) on promotional intensity and pricing:** Management clarified that discounts were actually *reduced* rather than increased, with value maintained via structural platforms like the INR99 meal rather than extra discounting. Pricing adjustments are kept to 2%–4% annually.
2. **Harit Kapoor (Investec) on value share and unit economics:** Management noted that value is a cross-menu strategy (not just low gross margin items) and delivers operating leverage, proven by 29% operating EBITDA growth outpacing 16% revenue growth.
3. **Latika Chopra (JPMorgan) on wage/commodity inflation and margins:** Management acknowledged minimum wage spikes in Karnataka and oil price inflation, but reiterated the target of 100–150 bps margin improvement for the year through internal mitigations and volume leverage.
4. **Rahul Agarwal (IKIGAI Asset) on SSSG composition and capex:** Management confirmed SSSG of 10.1% was overwhelmingly driven by double-digit guest count growth; capex per store was reaffirmed at INR3–3.5 crore and total annual capex at INR250–300 crore.
5. **Tejas Shah (Avendus Spark) on regional convergence and sustainability:** Management stated that South and West growth rates have converged due to strengthened operating structures in the South, and that guest-count-led growth indicates structural sustainability rather than a short-term spike.

### One-line takeaway
Westlife Foodworld delivered its strongest performance in 13 quarters in Q2 FY27, posting 16% revenue growth and 10.1% SSSG driven by double-digit guest count increases, while expanding EBITDA margins by 130 bps despite 190 bps of inflationary headwinds.
