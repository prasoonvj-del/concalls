# Adf Foods Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and volume expansion, tempered by logistics-led guidance revision.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 200.9 crore (+23.6% YoY).
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 163.3 crore (+16.6% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 39 crore (+9% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 19.4%.
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 25.3 crore.
*   **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 368.2 crore (+24.6% YoY).
*   **H1 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 68.6 crore (+15.8% YoY).
*   **H1 Consolidated PAT:** Rs 42.6 crore (+2.4% YoY).
*   **Net Surplus:** Rs 97.4 crore.

### Highlights
*   Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue, crossing the Rs 200 crore milestone.
*   Volume growth accounted for 60-65% of total growth.
*   Surat greenfield facility utilization was 15% in Q2; management targets 35-40% by FY27 end.
*   Truly Indian brand present in >3,500 US stores; new listings secured in Kroger, Meijer, and Sprouts.
*   Launched A2 Gir Cow Danedar Ghee under the Soul brand.
*   CRISIL credit rating upgraded to A+/Stable.
*   Board declared an interim dividend of 30% of face value.
*   *Record check:* Management confirmed Surat Phase 1 is operational, but lowered FY27 revenue guidance from >Rs 900 crore to Rs 850 crore due to shipping constraints.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management revised FY27 revenue guidance to Rs 850 crore (down from Rs 900 crore) due to persistent shipping and container availability constraints.
*   Management expects to maintain "high teens" EBITDA margins for the next two quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains robust across international markets, particularly for frozen foods and ready-to-eat/cook categories.
*   Logistics disruptions (container availability, vessel routes) remain a significant challenge, causing carry-forward shipments.
*   Middle East transit times have increased from 7-10 days to 35-40 days, with freight rates 7-8x higher.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Margins were impacted by elevated ocean freight and logistics costs.
*   COGS included a ~Rs 3 crore tariff refund benefit.
*   PLI benefit is in its final year; management noted that PLI receipts require 2x spending on marketing/advertising in foreign markets.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Total capex invested over the last 2.5 years: >Rs 145 crore.
*   Remaining capex for FY27: ~Rs 40-45 crore (Total capex plan: ~Rs 190 crore).
*   ~Rs 60-65 crore of remaining capex is allocated to new products.
*   Management targets an asset turn of 3x on investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net surplus of Rs 97.4 crore at the end of H1 FY27.
*   Inventory days increased by ~2 weeks due to shipping delays.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Shipping and logistics disruptions (container availability, vessel routes).
*   Elevated ocean freight costs.
*   Geopolitical developments impacting shipping routes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is the remaining capex and asset turn expectation?** A: Management stated ~Rs 40-45 crore remains for FY27, with ~Rs 60-65 crore of the total Rs 190 crore dedicated to new products; target asset turn is 3x.
*   **Q: Why was the revenue guidance lowered?** A: Management explained that shipping/container constraints made achieving the Rs 900 crore target difficult, hence the revision to Rs 850 crore.
*   **Q: What is the utilization of the Surat plant?** A: Utilization was 15% in Q2; management expects to reach 35-40% by the end of the fiscal year.
*   **Q: Are there one-offs in the margins?** A: Management confirmed a ~Rs 3 crore tariff refund in COGS; insurance claims are gross margin neutral.

### One-line takeaway
Strong underlying demand and volume growth are being hampered by persistent shipping and logistics bottlenecks, leading to a downward revision in FY27 revenue guidance.
