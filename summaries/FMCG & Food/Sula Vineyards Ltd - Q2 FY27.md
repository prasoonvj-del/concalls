# Sula Vineyards Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong 34% YoY PAT growth, significant net debt reduction, and three consecutive quarters of positive revenue growth.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained positive outlook; management expects to close H2 FY27 on a strong note.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "Mixed" to "Positive" due to strong earnings momentum and successful premiumization.
- **New topics:** Drought conditions in Maharashtra (management expects bumper harvest for table grapes, aiding margins); in-house logistics initiative (expected savings of Rs 1 crore annually from Q4 FY27).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Vintage Room (event pavilion) is now operational; York winery relocation to Domaine Rasa is on track for completion by end of Q3 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 136 crore (+4% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 27.5 crore (+8% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 8 crore (+34% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.3% (+85 bps YoY).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 306 crore (down Rs 49 crore YoY).
- **Wine Tourism Revenue:** Rs 16.3 crore (+24% YoY).
- **Elite & Premium Portfolio Growth:** 9% YoY.

### Highlights
- Elite & Premium portfolio contribution to own brands reached a record 82% (+400 bps YoY).
- 'The Source' brand grew >30% YoY; Sula Merlot grew >100% YoY.
- Telangana is now the company's 2nd largest market, overtaking Karnataka.
- Solar energy now accounts for 97% of winery energy requirements (H1 FY27).
- Vintage Room (5,000 sq. ft. event pavilion) is operational.
- Operating expenses reduced by 4% YoY.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects gross margins to moderate and improve from Q4 FY27 as the procurement mix normalizes.
- Targeting 22-23% EBITDA margins over the next couple of years.
- Expects to build on Q2 earnings momentum for the remainder of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Maharashtra demand is improving; Karnataka remains subdued due to excise policy favoring beer.
- Economy & Popular portfolio remains under pressure due to unsustainable discounting by competitors.
- Management expects to launch wine-based RTD (Ready-to-Drink) products early next month.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin impacted by 150 bps due to higher blended grape costs (procurement mix skewed to wine grapes).
- Packing material costs rose due to geopolitical issues but are stabilizing.
- Average borrowing cost declined to 7.3% (-30 bps YoY).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Finalized plans for a 35-key hotel at York Estate (target opening Q4 FY28, Rs 25 crore investment, 5-6 year payback).
- Relocating York winery operations to Domaine Rasa by end of Q3 FY27 to repurpose York facility into a boutique hotel.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt reduced to Rs 306 crore.
- WIPS outstanding receivables: Rs 85 crore (post-November payout).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Drought conditions: Potential irrigation challenges for farmers in the following harvest (starting April 2027).
- Haryana policy: Discriminatory towards Indian wines vs. imported wines.
- Delhi market: Continued regulatory challenges.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kaustav Bubna (Kamana Holdings) on drought impact:** Management stated last year was harder; this year’s dry/sunny conditions suggest a bumper table grape harvest, which will reduce raw material costs for popular/economy wines.
- **Nikhil Upadhaya (SiMPL) on Telangana growth:** Management clarified growth is sustained and not just a base effect; Telangana is now >70% red wine-oriented.
- **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS) on demerger:** Management noted that while tourism is growing, there is no "burning reason" to demerge currently, though it remains under discussion.
- **Siddharth Bhattacharya (Investment & Infrastructure) on gross margin hit:** Management explained the hit was a one-time inventory correction to reduce carryover liquid; expects normalization from Q4 FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Sula delivered robust profit growth and debt reduction, with management signaling a structural improvement in gross margins starting Q4 FY27 as inventory levels normalize.
