# Zydus Wellness Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Robust revenue and EBITDA growth driven by recovery in demand, strong performance in domestic and international portfolios, and gross margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated net sales: Rs 11,227 million (up 74.6% YoY)
- H1 FY27 net sales: Rs 25,526 million (up 70.1% YoY)
- Domestic portfolio growth: 29.2% YoY for the quarter; 13.3% YoY for H1
- Seasonal brands growth: 123.5% YoY for the quarter on a low base; H1 degrowth of 8.7%
- Skin and Hair care growth: 37.5% YoY for the quarter; 35.8% YoY for H1
- Food & Nutrition growth: 25.1% YoY for the quarter; 20.7% YoY for H1
- International business like-to-like growth: 12.4% YoY for the quarter; 18.6% for H1
- EBITDA: Rs 855 million (up 271.7% YoY) for the quarter; Rs 3,272 million (up 83.2% YoY) for H1
- Comfort Click growth: 12% for the quarter (vs 25% last quarter); 18% for H1

### Highlights
- Sugar Free entered the Monk Fruit Sweetener category with a No Erythritol formulation and replicated its double-digit growth performance.
- RiteBite Max Protein launched Crunchy cookies (10 grams protein, no palm oil, maida, or added sugar) and sustained strong growth leadership in protein snacking.
- Comfort Click (WeightWorld brand) expanded portfolio with Vitamin B Complex, Omega-3 Triglycerides, and D3 and K2.
- Everyuth delivered double-digit growth, led by tan removal and peel-off franchises, improving ranking to number 4 in facial cleansing (INR 4,500 crore category).
- Complan registered double-digit growth for 3 consecutive quarters, supported by celebrity Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, 50% lower sugar relaunch, and Complan NutriGro toddler relaunch.
- Cuticolor expanded portfolio with Bright Brown in the safer hair color segment.
- Nutralite delivered strong double-digit growth despite commodity headwinds.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in delivering sustainable growth and further margin expansion.
- Management maintains the double-digit growth expectation for the Comfort Click international business.
- Steady-state A&P spend expected to remain consistent at 12.5% to 13% for the domestic business and 23% to 24% for Comfort Click.
- Comfort Click steady-state EBITDA margins envisaged between 14% and 15%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumption trends reflect sustained positive momentum amid high commodity-driven inflation, supported by steady demand recovery and improving rural offline markets.
- E-commerce and quick-commerce maintained strong momentum, increasing sales contribution.
- Rural demand holds rather well, with growth rates slightly higher than urban across company portfolios.
- The VMS (Vitamin, Mineral, Supplements) addressable market in Europe is estimated at over $11 billion.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion continued in the core business, aided by higher margins from Comfort Click.
- Management noted pricing power across the brand portfolio to pass on inflationary input costs, though occasional lags may occur.
- Operating leverage helped improve EBITDA margins as fixed costs did not increase in sync with top-line growth.
- Domestic A&P spend operates at 12.5% to 13%; Comfort Click A&P operates at 23% to 24%.
- Calibrated price increases will be evaluated over H2 based on individual commodity movements (e.g., milk, PET/packaging plastics, palm oil).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued inflation could impact consumer confidence and reduce consumption.
- Higher-than-imagined competitive intensity noted in core markets for Comfort Click.
- Temporary challenges and slowdowns on Amazon in 1 or 2 international markets and website transition issues in one country impacted Comfort Click growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Siddhesh Deshmukh (IIFL Capital) asked about the slowdown in Comfort Click growth from 25% to 12%.** Management attributed it to a Prime Day shift between June and July, website migration issues in one market, and minor marketplace slowdowns, while reiterating confidence in double-digit growth.
2. **Ronak Shah (Equirus Securities) asked about operating margin sustainability amid inflation and Comfort Click acquisition base.** Management stated gross margins are improving product-by-product through pricing power, and EBITDA margins will benefit from operating leverage and disciplined A&P.
3. **Kinjal Mota (Banyan Tree Advisors) inquired about steady-state A&P as a percentage of sales.** Management stated domestic A&P will remain consistent at 12.5% to 13% and Comfort Click at 23% to 24%.
4. **Binay Shukla (Phillip Capital) asked about rural demand trends.** Management confirmed rural demand is robust, with growth rates slightly higher than urban.
5. **Nandan Vartak (Wealth Managers) questioned H2 gross margin sustainability against a high base.** Management stated the intent and actions are to match or improve gross margins, though volatility makes it hard to predict.

### One-line takeaway
Zydus Wellness delivered robust Q2 FY27 performance with 74.6% YoY net sales growth and a 271.7% surge in EBITDA, driven by broad-based domestic momentum, double-digit growth across key brands, and ongoing gross margin expansion.
