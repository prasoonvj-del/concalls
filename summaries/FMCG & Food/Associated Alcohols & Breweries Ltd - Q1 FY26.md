# Associated Alcohols & Breweries Ltd - Q1 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-30 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY 2026-27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong momentum in the IMFL proprietary segment with robust volume and value growth, offset by near-term margin pressure in ethanol.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Income from Operations:** Rs 2,809 million (5% YoY growth)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 299 million (Overall EBITDA margin: 11%)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 178 million (PAT margin: 6%)
- **Potable Alcohol Division EBITDA Margin:** 18%
- **IMFL Proprietary Revenue:** Rs 729 million (highest ever, up 58% YoY)
- **IMFL Proprietary Volume:** 0.79 million cases (up 40% YoY)
- **IMFL Proprietary Realization:** Rs 823 per case (up 13% YoY)
- **IMFL Proprietary EBITDA Margin:** 20%
- **Central Province (CP) Series Volume Growth:** 260% YoY (from 20,300 cases to 73,000 cases in Q1 FY27)
- **ENA Volume:** 7.3 million liters (up 25% YoY; Revenue up 25% YoY to Rs 456 million)
- **Captive Consumption of ENA:** 59% (compared to 50% in FY26)
- **Ethanol Volume Growth:** 28% YoY
- **IMFL Licensed Business Volume:** 0.1 million cases (Revenue: Rs 132 million)
- **Total IMFL Business Volume:** 1.7 million cases (Revenue: Rs 734 million; EBITDA margin: 18%)
- **Average Grain Prices:** Rs 21,000 per metric tonne

### Highlights
- IMFL proprietary revenue reached a record Rs 729 million, driven by the CP series (Rum, Vodka, Orange Vodka, Whiskey), Nicobar Gin, and Hillfort Whiskey.
- IMFL proprietary contribution to overall revenue expanded to 23% (up from 17% in FY26).
- Successfully entered Odisha in Q1 FY27.
- Registration process underway for Ready-to-Drink (RTD) presence across 8 additional states (Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Rajasthan, Delhi, Karnataka, West Bengal, Goa) following its Madhya Pradesh launch.
- Scheduled launch of premium tequila (initially in MP) and brandy (in Kerala) in Q2.
- Acquisition of SDF Industries in Kerala is progressing; upgrade and automation underway with plant operations expected by December '26 and full-fledged operation from April 1st.
- Malt maturation is ongoing to support captive consumption and the future launch of single malt whiskey.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in delivering around 30% volume growth in the IMFL proprietary portfolio for FY 2026-27.
- Revenue of the IMFL proprietary business targeted to grow at 30% YoY over the next 2-3 years.
- RTD segment targets 3% to 4% market share in entered states; Malt targets ~4% market share over time.
- Tequila (priced at Rs 5,000 to Rs 7,000 MRP) targets an initial 10% to 15% market share in its category.
- SDF plant capacity is planned to build up to 3.5 lakh to 4 lakh cases per month.
- Ethanol revenue for the year expected to remain flat YoY as per maximum capacity.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Central province continues to be the key growth driver; CP series reflects strong brand equity.
- Consumer preference shifting toward ease-of-consumer formats and flavored beverages, presenting a long-term opportunity for RTD.
- IMIL (country liquor) business remains quota-specific and stable at top thresholds set by annual tenders.
- Licensed IMFL business impacted by a slight industry degrowth in seasonal categories like Celebration Rum.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Overall EBITDA margin declined from 14% to 11% primarily due to negative margins in the ethanol business caused by oversupply, lower quota allocation, and lower realizations from private OMCs (Rs 52-54 vs government quota average of Rs 64).
- Ethanol breakeven realization estimated around Rs 57 to Rs 60.
- IMFL proprietary EBITDA margin held at a healthy 20% despite brand-building investments, though compressed by 2% due to a >10% increase in raw material grain prices.
- Energy efficiency and fuel price reductions aided overall plant cost savings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- SDF Industries (Kerala) upgrade and automation ongoing; automated machinery installation underway for operations by December '26.
- Malt maturation initiated 8-9 months ago; revenue expected from Q3 next year onwards.
- Expanding geographic footprint: entered Odisha; planning entries into Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Dedicated ethanol plant maintained to avail interest subvention and subsidy schemes, plus energy cost benefits.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ethanol segment realizations under pressure due to market oversupply and lower government quota allocations.
- Upward pressure on grain prices (averaged Rs 21,000 per MT in Q1), impacting margins across segments.
- High entry costs in competitive markets like Maharashtra necessitate a slow, steady rollout.
- Tough competition in the whiskey segment against global leaders making scaling of brands like Hillfort a gradual process.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vinay Rawal asked if the company plans to shift ethanol capacity to ENA given ethanol operating losses.** Management clarified they do not plan to shift as they run a dedicated plant for subsidies and energy integration, choosing to run ethanol for marginal contributions despite lower private OMC prices.
2. **Pawandeep Bhatia questioned the volume/revenue drop in the licensed IMFL segment.** Management explained that the Inbrew business ended mid-Q2 last year and Celebration Rum is seasonal, leading to flat-to-lower numbers, while focus has shifted to proprietary brands.
3. **Shreya Chatterjee inquired about ethanol breakeven pricing and guidance.** Management stated breakeven is around Rs 57-60, and revenue will match last year's capacity limits while awaiting new October tenders.
4. **Manoj asked why the plant isn't switching to ENA interchangeability given ethanol losses.** Management reiterated that maintaining a dedicated ethanol plant is required for interest subvention and provides boiler/energy cost efficiencies.
5. **Pawandeep Bhatia asked about the addressable market size for Tequila and RTD.** Management stated the Indian tequila market is ~1.5 lakh cases (with their target price bracket representing 40,000 cases), and RTD is growing at 15%-17% annually, strategically positioned at 8% ABV to tap into the strong beer market.

### One-line takeaway
Associated Alcohols delivered a record quarter for its high-margin IMFL proprietary segment (up 58% YoY), though consolidated profitability was weighed down by oversupply and pricing pressures in the ethanol business.
