# FMCG & Food: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the FMCG & Food sector reflected robust top-line momentum and volume growth across most players, driven by premiumisation, value-added portfolio scaling, and expanding distribution networks. Major players like **Hindustan Unilever (HUL)** and **Godrej Consumer Products** reported high-teens revenue growth (15% and 19% respectively), while specialized food and dairy companies like **Milky Mist** (+41.6%), **Gopal Snacks** (+22.9%), and **LT Foods** (+17%) also posted strong double-digit revenue expansions. However, this growth coexisted with notable input cost inflation across commodities, milk, and freight.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus / Agreement:** Managements broadly agreed that underlying demand remains resilient with sequential improvements. **HUL** noted that rural demand has seen a sequential increase and growth is broad-based. **Godrej Consumer** reported robust broad-based underlying volume growth (UVG) across India, Indonesia, and Africa (8% consolidated UVG). **LT Foods** and **Gopal Snacks** highlighted strong core demand in basmati and snack categories respectively.
*   **Divergence:** While most companies struck an optimistic note, **Milky Mist** pointed out a challenging demand environment with slower-than-expected festive demand recovery and high retail/food inflation. In the alcoholic beverages sector, **Globus Spirits** noted that the Delhi excise market is experiencing compressed industry volumes due to porous borders, whereas **Allied Blenders and Distillers** reported strong broad-based volume growth and record-breaking sales.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Consensus / Agreement:** Input cost inflation was a universal theme, with companies grappling with rising prices in crude derivatives, palm oil, packaging laminates, milk procurement, and paddy. To protect profitability, companies relied on calibrated price hikes, grammage adjustments, premiumisation, and cost-efficiency programs.
*   **Company Specifics:** 
    *   **HUL** noted that commodity prices remain elevated, leading to a ~400 bps YoY contraction in gross margin as absolute costs were managed to protect consumer franchises.
    *   **Godrej Consumer** cited one of the highest inflationary impacts in recent years across crude-linked derivatives and palm oil.
    *   **Milky Mist** faced 11-12% milk procurement cost inflation, offset by ~10% price hikes and a richer product mix.
    *   **LT Foods** and **Gopal Snacks** highlighted higher raw material opening costs (paddy crops up 22-25% for LT Foods; palm oil and laminate pressures for Gopal). 
    *   **CCL Products** maintained a stable EBITDA per kg in the Rs 135-140 range.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Allied Blenders:** Commissioned a captive malt distillery in Rangapur, Telangana (~Rs 75 crore) and commenced commercial operations at the Moradabad bottling facility.
*   **CCL Products:** Evaluating brownfield expansion (approx. 6,000 tonnes) or strategic partnerships/JVs for freeze-dried capacity.
*   **Globus Spirits:** Capital is 100% focused on the consumer business; no capacity expansion planned in Rajasthan.
*   **Godrej Consumer:** GAUM (Africa/US/Middle East) operating at very high capacity utilization; expansions scheduled in the next few months.
*   **Gopal Snacks:** Nagpur besan plant commenced operations in September; JVs running out of capacity.
*   **HUL:** Expanded direct distribution (adding 30,000 general trade outlets) and doubled modern trade asset deployment over the last 2 quarters.
*   **LT Foods:** First phase of the new Madhya Pradesh facility for Nature Bio Foods starting this FY; ongoing investments in U.S. ready-to-heat (RTH) facilities.
*   **Milky Mist:** Commissioned a new Skyr/Greek yogurt facility in Perundurai (Rs 40 crore investment) and acquired 50 acres in Baramati, Maharashtra.

## What changed since last quarter
*(Note: Explicit Q1-to-Q2 comparisons were primarily highlighted by CCL Products in the provided summaries.)*
*   **CCL Products:** Management's tone shifted from a "wait and watch" stance to seeing the return of medium and long-term contracts as coffee prices stabilized in a range-bound manner. Net debt also successfully reduced from Rs 963 crore in Q1 to Rs 742 crore in Q2.

## Company one-liners
*   **Allied Blenders and Distillers:** Allied Blenders and Distillers delivered record Q2 volumes crossing 10 million cases with 15.8% revenue growth, driven by aggressive premiumisation and backward integration, while absorbing supply chain headwinds.
*   **CCL Products (India):** CCL Products delivered a resilient performance with strong volume growth and continued deleveraging, while management remains confident in maintaining 15% growth guidance and is actively evaluating strategic options to fuel long-term capacity needs.
*   **Globus Spirits:** Globus Spirits reported 15% YoY revenue growth in Q2 FY27, bolstered by strong consumer division performance and a de-risked balance sheet following a INR 200 crore QIP, while maintaining its annual manufacturing EBITDA guidance of INR 5-7 per litre despite seasonal input cost pressures.
*   **Godrej Consumer Products:** Godrej Consumer Products delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 19% revenue growth and 8% UVG, driven by robust performance across India, Indonesia, and Africa, despite facing significant commodity cost inflation.
*   **Gopal Snacks:** Gopal Snacks delivered robust H1 revenue growth of 26.7% and margin expansion via selective price/grammage adjustments and backward integration, while maintaining full-year guidance of Rs 1,800-1,900 crore top-line.
*   **Hindustan Unilever:** HUL delivered its highest USG in 14 quarters (15%) with 7% UVG, balancing price hikes with volume growth amidst elevated commodity inflation.
*   **LT Foods:** LT Foods reported healthy H1 FY27 revenue growth of 22% driven by strong core basmati demand and working capital improvements, though profitability faced near-term pressure from freight inflation and higher brand investments.
*   **Milky Mist Dairy Food:** Milky Mist delivered a strong Q2 with 41.6% revenue growth and 15.2% EBITDA margins, driven by high-growth value-added categories like paneer and yogurt, with management confident in sustaining this momentum despite input cost volatility.

## What to watch next quarter

*   **CCL Products:** Finalization of freeze-dried capacity expansion plans and performance of US market brand launches.
