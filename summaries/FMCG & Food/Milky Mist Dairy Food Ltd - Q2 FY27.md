# Milky Mist Dairy Food Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record revenue and EBITDA margins, driven by strong demand in value-added dairy categories and successful execution of the high-protein product strategy.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (Q2):** Rs 1,089 crore (+41.6% YoY).
*   **Revenue (H1):** Rs 2,062.9 crore (+42.5% YoY).
*   **Gross Profit (Q2):** Rs 368.8 crore (+46.4% YoY).
*   **Gross Margin (Q2):** 33.9% (+112 bps YoY).
*   **EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 165.2 crore (+53.9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q2):** 15.2% (vs 14% in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT (Q2):** Rs 77.9 crore (vs Rs 28.4 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT Margin (Q2):** 7.2% (vs 3.7% in Q2 FY26).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 32.62 crore.
*   **ROCE:** 16.1% (annualized); **ROE:** 21.1%.

### Highlights
*   **Category Performance:** Paneer (star product) grew 44% YoY (value) and 28% (volume). Yogurt grew 168% YoY, cheese 31% YoY, and ice cream 43% YoY.
*   **High-Protein Portfolio:** Includes Pro Milk, Skyr/Greek yogurt, paneer, and cheddar cheese; contributes 12-13% of top-line. Management expects this to reach 18-20% in 2-3 years.
*   **Distribution:** 4 lakh retail touchpoints, 4,200 distributors, and 45,000+ visi coolers/freezers.
*   **Channel Mix:** Online channels (e-com/quick commerce) contributed 21% of H1 FY27 revenue (vs 11% in H1 FY26).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to sustain current growth momentum and 15% EBITDA margins in H2.
*   Paneer capacity utilization is expected to reach maximum levels in 24-30 months.
*   Targeting 15,000 per freezer per month throughput for ice cream.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   FMCG sector facing challenging demand and cost environment; retail inflation at 5.54% and food inflation at 7.74% (Sept '26).
*   Industry seeing slower-than-expected festive demand recovery.
*   Management noted increased regulatory scrutiny on analogue paneer; Milky Mist is benefiting from the shift toward branded, "clean label" products.
*   Organized paneer sector penetration expected to rise to 25-30% (currently ~10%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   H1 milk procurement cost inflation was 11-12%.
*   Company implemented ~10% price hikes in Q1/Q2 to offset input cost inflation.
*   Tamil Nadu (85% of procurement) seeing surplus rains, stabilizing milk prices.
*   High-margin categories (Yogurt, Ice Cream, Paneer) are supporting overall margin sustainability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Commissioned new Skyr/Greek yogurt facility (Perundurai) with Rs 40 crore investment (150 tonnes/day capacity).
*   Current paneer capacity: 100 tonnes/day (65% utilization); planning additional 200 tonnes/day at Perundurai.
*   Maharashtra (Baramati): Acquired 50 acres; facility expected to be operational in 3 years.
*   Procurement: 246.5 million litres in H1 (+31% YoY) via 92,000+ farmers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   IPO proceeds used to repay Rs 750 crore of debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Input cost inflation (cattle feed +15-20% YoY; milk prices +30% in some markets).
*   Regulatory scrutiny on dark stores/storage conditions.
*   Geopolitical uncertainties and El Nino impact on milk supply.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Protein Salience:** Management clarified that high-protein products currently contribute 12% of revenue and are expected to reach 20% in 2-3 years with 50%+ gross margins.
*   **Milk Inflation:** Management stated they can absorb a 3.5-5% milk price increase through product mix and calibrated pricing without impacting margins.
*   **Channel Strategy:** Quick commerce is a key growth driver; management is actively working with platforms on SOPs for temperature/storage compliance.
*   **Capex:** The Maharashtra plant is currently in the design/approval stage; management noted minimal cash capex is being deployed there currently.
*   **Procurement Risk:** To de-risk the 85% reliance on Tamil Nadu, the company is expanding procurement to Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Andhra Pradesh.

### One-line takeaway
Milky Mist delivered a strong Q2 with 41.6% revenue growth and 15.2% EBITDA margins, driven by high-growth value-added categories like paneer and yogurt, with management confident in sustaining this momentum despite input cost volatility.
