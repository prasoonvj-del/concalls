# Marico Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · FMCG & Food*

Here is a factual, structured summary based strictly on the earnings call transcript.

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong all-round performance with highest profit growth in 50 quarters and raised full-year profitability guidance.

---

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Revenue:** Up 13% YoY (grew 22% on a 2-year CAGR basis).
* **India Volume Growth:** Up 10% YoY (8% on a 2-year CAGR basis).
* **International Business Growth:** Up 13% YoY in constant currency (CC) terms; up 23% YoY in INR terms (16% CC 2-year CAGR).
* **Gross Margin:** Expanded by ~600 bps YoY.
* **EBITDA:** Up 27% YoY (17% on a 2-year CAGR basis).
* **EBITDA Margin:** Expanded by 200 bps YoY to 18.2%.
* **PAT:** Up 31% YoY (28% growth in H1 FY27).
* **Advertising & Promotion (A&P) Spend:** Up 44% YoY.
* **H1 FY27 Aggregates:** Revenue grew 18% YoY, EBITDA grew 26% YoY, and PAT grew 28% YoY.

---

### Highlights
* **Parachute:** Volume grew 13% YoY (highest in 22 quarters); gained ~570 bps volume market share to reach a high of 60%. Delivered positive value growth despite price drops.
* **Value-Added Hair Oils (VAHO):** Grew 21% in value (6th consecutive quarter of stellar growth); gained 80 bps market share on a MAT basis. Almond hair oil run rate tracked to hit >Rs 100 crore ARR by FY28.
* **Foods Portfolio:** Grew 45% YoY, crossing an annualized revenue run rate (ARR) of Rs 1,500 crore. Saffola Foods (Oats, Muesli, Soya Chunks) delivered early-teens growth. Soya Chunks reached an ARR of ~Rs 100 crore.
* **Premium Personal Care:** Crossed an ARR of Rs 500 crore, led by Livon Serums and Parachute Advansed Protein Shampoo. Shampoo category ARR set to cross Rs 100 crore this year.
* **Digital-First Personal Care:** Portfolio reached ~Rs 1,150 crore ARR. Beardo expanded >6x since acquisition (2020) and is expected to cross Rs 400 crore ARR this year with a 1,900 bps margin improvement.
* **International Segments:** Vietnam grew 22% CC YoY; MENA grew 15% CC YoY; South Africa grew 15% CC YoY; New Country Development & Exports grew 17% CC YoY; Bangladesh grew 5% CC YoY.

---

### Guidance & outlook
* **FY27 Revenue:** Raised outlook to teens growth, expecting to comfortably cross Rs 15,000 crore (with an opportunity to push towards mid-teens).
* **FY27 Volume:** India business volume growth is expected to touch double digits for the full year.
* **FY27 EBITDA:** Raised growth guidance to mid-twenties YoY (up from ~20% guided earlier).
* **Medium-Term Vision:** Management preponed its Rs 20,000 crore revenue target to FY29 (from FY30 earlier), aiming to double revenue from Rs 10,000 crore in 4 years purely organically.
* **Medium-Term EBITDA:** Operating profit growth target reaffirmed at mid-teens CAGR for FY28/FY29.
* **A&P Spends:** Expected to normalize to 10%–10.5% of sales on a steady-state basis.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* Economic activity in India remained resilient, supported by domestic consumption and investment, though food, edible oil, and fuel pushed consumer inflation higher.
* Urban consumer sentiment remains resilient. Regional and smaller unorganized FMCG players face squeeze under raw material price swings, accelerating consolidation toward larger players.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Copra prices remained rangebound, about 35% below last year's peak.
* Crude-linked derivatives and vegetable oils have moved higher due to geopolitical and weather-related concerns.
* Gross margins expanded ~600 bps YoY, driven by favorable portfolio mix, improved profitability of new growth engines, and softer copra input costs.
* Management relies on procurement efficiency, brand pricing power, and internal cost management to cushion crude-linked inflation.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Project SETU expanded direct reach by >1.5x and nearly doubled rural stockists, contributing an estimated 1%–2% incremental volume growth.
* Rolling out SETU 2.0 targeting urban routes, AI-led selling, tech assortment, and GTM expansion for premium personal care, food, and digital-first personal care brands. Playbook expanded to Vietnam and Bangladesh.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Project SETU achieved a balanced rhythm of daily sales (~3.5% daily), leading to below-the-line (BTL) savings, significantly reduced trade/distributor stock pipeline, and eliminated month-end sales skew.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Geopolitical developments in West Asia causing higher energy prices, supply chain risks, and freight volatility.
* Inflationary risks in crude-linked derivatives and vegetable oils.
* Deficient rainfall in select Indian states potentially impacting localized rural demand.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A

* **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama): What drove the EBITDA growth upgrade to mid-20s despite inflation in non-copra inputs?**  
  *Management Response:* Driven by a combination of favorable product mix, procurement position efficiencies, sustained volume outperformance in Parachute despite price cuts, margin expansion across new growth engines (Foods/Digital), and disciplined cost management.

* **Arnab Mitra (Goldman Sachs): Why did Parachute volume surge 13% in a deflationary environment?**  
  *Management Response:* Attributed to targeted price cuts on mid/larger loyalty packs (reversing down-trading), thin pipeline inventory due to Project SETU enabling instant price-drop passthrough in rural markets, expanded rural stockist coverage (1.8x), and cost pressure on unorganized competitors.

* **Tejash Shah (Avendus Spark): What is the scope and objective of Project SETU 2.0?**  
  *Management Response:* Focuses on both execution quality and expansion into urban chemist/cosmetic outlets, driving gamification, automated last-mile selling, and expanding digital/food brands into General Trade.

* **Harit Kapoor (Investec): What is driving performance in recent acquisitions like Cosmix and 4700 BC?**  
  *Management Response:* Both are tracking ahead of initial acquisition run rates. Cosmix delivers high-teens operating margins. 4700 BC benefited from quick-commerce and strong festive gifting demand; its breakeven timeline will be significantly ahead of target.

* **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital): Does upgrading FY27 EBITDA growth to mid-20s raise the base or expectation for FY28?**  
  *Management Response:* The upgrade applies specifically to FY27 visibility due to locked-in procurement costs and revenue momentum. FY28 absolute EBITDA growth guidance remains maintained at mid-teens.

---

### One-line takeaway
Marico delivered robust all-round Q2 growth, prompting management to raise FY27 EBITDA growth guidance to mid-twenties% and pull forward its Rs 20,000 crore revenue target to FY29.
