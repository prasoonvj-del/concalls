# Marico Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong all-round performance with consolidated revenue growth of 13%, EBITDA growth of 27%, and PAT growth of 31%, alongside an upgraded annual outlook.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue grew 13% YoY.
- India business volume growth at 10% YoY.
- International business constant currency growth at 13% YoY (23% in INR terms).
- Gross margin expanded by approx. 600 bps YoY.
- A&P expenditure increased by 44% YoY.
- EBITDA margin expanded by 200 bps YoY to 18.2%.
- EBITDA grew 27% YoY; PAT grew 31% YoY.
- 2-year CAGR: Revenue 22%, EBITDA 17%.
- Parachute volume growth: 13% YoY (highest in 22 quarters); market share up ~570 bps to 60%.
- Value-Added Hair Oil (VAHO) value growth: 21% YoY; value market share up 80 bps (MAT basis).
- Saffola Edible Oil revenue grew 6% YoY; volume declined high single-digit due to deliberate culling of low-margin variants.
- Foods grew 45% YoY, crossing an ARR of Rs 1,500 crore.
- Premium Personal Care ARR crossed Rs 500 crore.
- Digital-first personal care portfolio at ~Rs 1,150 crore ARR.
- Beardo expected to cross Rs 400 crore ARR this year (up >6x since 2020) with profitability up 1,900 bps.
- International performance (constant currency): Bangladesh +5%, Vietnam +22%, MENA +15%, South Africa +15%, New Country Development and Exports +17%.

### Highlights
- Highest profit growth in the last 50 quarters.
- Project SETU expanded direct reach by >1.5x and nearly doubled stockists, driving 1% to 2% incremental volume growth. Embarking on SETU 2.0 focused on AI/tech-led growth.
- Almond hair oil on track to scale to >Rs 100 crore ARR by FY28 (medium-term aspiration of Rs 200-250 crore ARR by FY30).
- Shampoo portfolio crossing Rs 100 crore ARR this year (medium-term aspiration of Rs 400-500 crore ARR).
- Aiming to reach the Rs 20,000 crore vision target by FY29 instead of FY30 (doubling from Rs 10,000 crore in 4 years organically).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects teens revenue growth for the full year, comfortably crossing the Rs 15,000 crore revenue mark, with an opportunity to push towards mid-teens.
- Management expects mid-twenties EBITDA growth for the full year (up from ~20% estimated earlier).
- India business full-year volume growth expected to touch double digits.
- Long-term vision of Rs 20,000 crore by FY30 pulled forward as an aspiration to FY29 with mid-teens EBITDA CAGR.
- Steady-state A&P spend expected around 10% to 10.5% of sales going forward (with double-digit annual A&P growth in India).
- Medium-term growth expectations: Parachute and Saffola mid-single-digit, VAHO double-digit value growth, and growth portfolio (Foods, Premium Personal Care, Digital) at 20% to 25%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global environment volatile with geopolitical energy/supply chain concerns; Indian economic activity resilient supported by domestic consumption and investment.
- Consumer inflation increased, led by food, edible oil, and fuel.
- Urban and rural consumption resilient; smaller/regional players squeezed during tough times while larger players consolidate.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Favorable portfolio mix, improving profitability of new growth engines, and softer copra prices drove gross margin expansion (~600 bps YoY).
- Copra prices stayed rangebound, ~35% below last year's peak.
- Crude-linked derivatives and vegetable oils rose further due to geopolitics and weather concerns.
- Pricing power, softer copra, and an aggressive cost management program cushion crude-linked inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Consolidated manufacturing and media buying synergies being implemented across digital brands (like True Elements, 4700BC, Cosmix, and Just Herbs).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Trade pipeline is among the thinnest in the industry; uniform sales rhythm with no end-of-month/quarter loading.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical developments in West Asia leading to higher energy prices and supply chain inflation.
- Deficient rainfall in certain states and potential higher inflation in international markets if crude remains high.
- Management mindful of avoiding complacency, overconfidence, and arrogance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on EBITDA upgrade drivers:** Management explained it is a combination of favorable mix/culling unprofitable segments, procurement efficiencies, Parachute volume outperformance in a deflationary cycle, improved diversification profitability, and cost management.
2. **Arnab Mitra (Goldman Sachs) on Parachute's strong volume growth drivers:** Management cited selective price drops on loyalty packs, thin trade pipelines via SETU, expanded stockist reach (1.8x), temporary supply chain advantages for large players, and subdued irrational competition.
3. **Harit Kapoor (Investec) on 4700BC and Cosmix integration:** Management stated both brands are tracking ahead of acquisition assumptions, with Cosmix highly profitable (high-teens operating margins) and 4700BC breakeven accelerating due to early synergy realization and quick commerce scaling.
4. **Percy Panthaki (IIFL Capital) on FY28 EBITDA trajectory vs. FY27 upgrade:** Management clarified that the upgrade applies to FY27 due to better visibility and earlier-than-expected margin normalization, while reiterating a mid-teens EBITDA growth guidance for FY28/FY29.
5. **Mihir Shah (Nomura) on growth portfolio sustainability:** Management stated the growth portfolio (Foods and Personal Care) is expected to deliver 20% to 25% growth while improving profitability, even as A&P normalizes through shared synergies.

### One-line takeaway
Marico delivered a stellar quarter with 13% revenue growth, a 200 bps EBITDA margin expansion, and an upgraded mid-twenties EBITDA growth guidance for the full year, driven by strong core execution and scaling new growth engines.
