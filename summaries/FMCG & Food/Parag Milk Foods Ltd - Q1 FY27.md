# Parag Milk Foods Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever Q1 revenue of INR 945 crore with stable gross margins and strong growth in new-age brands, despite transient volume slowdowns in B2B flagship categories.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 945 crore (up 11% YoY value growth, 3% volume growth).
- **EBITDA:** INR 70 crore (up 6% YoY absolute growth).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.4% (vs 7.7% in Q1 FY26).
- **PAT:** Declined 20% YoY, primarily due to the current tax impact.
- **Milk Prices:** INR 42 per litre (up 13% YoY, flat QoQ).
- **Gross Profit:** INR 258 crore (up 11% YoY).
- **Flagship Categories Contribution:** 61% of Q1 revenue (volume down 2% YoY due to transient B2B slowdown, B2C robust).
- **New Age Business Growth:** Up 59% YoY; contributes ~13% of overall revenue (vs 9% in Q1 FY26).
- **Go Cheese Market Share:** 35% (#2 in India).
- **Website/Google Searches for Avvatar:** Up almost 200% over the last 2 months.
- **Avvatar Channel Mix:** 75% from quick-commerce, e-commerce, and website; 25% from traditional retail and specialized protein outlets.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever Q1 revenue and maintained gross profit growth inline with revenue growth (11%), demonstrating successful pass-through of cost push via calibrated pricing, product mix, and promotional optimization.
- Flagship categories (ghee, cheese, paneer, and dahi) form the backbone of the business.
- New-age business (Pride of Cows and Avvatar) expanded well beyond whey protein powders into protein bars, ready-to-drink (RTD) products, creatine, and functional nutrition.
- Launched ready-to-drink Avvatar coffee (15g protein per serving) with encouraging response.
- Engaged in a viral marketing tie-up with *India's Got Latent Season 2* (Samay Raina), generating over 80 million unique reaches.
- Rakesh Kothari elevated as Chief Financial Officer (CFO) effective August 7, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains constructive for upcoming quarters, expecting monsoon benefits on milk availability and supportive festive demand for value-added categories.
- Management expects to break the 10% annual sales growth benchmark in FY27.
- Long-term distribution goal: reach over 1.5 million outlets in the next 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global whey protein demand increased exponentially due to fat-reduction medications and lifestyle shifts, creating a structural demand-supply gap.
- Maharashtra FDA ban on analogue paneer (made from vegetable fats instead of milk fat) is positive for organized, pure-milk players like Parag.
- B2B demand faced transient slowdowns as management consciously held back on aggressive pricing/discounts to protect overall margins.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw milk prices stood at INR 42 per litre in Q1 (up 13% YoY, flat sequentially).
- Gross margins remained stable at 27.3% (down ~70 bps sequentially from 28%).
- New-age business carries superlative margins (almost double the company average).
- State/PSI incentives reduced following GST rate changes in September 2025 (ghee/cheese reduced from 12% to 5%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Doubling cheese production capacity from 60 metric tonnes per day to 120 metric tonnes per day over the next 1.5 years (target by March 2028).
- Capacity expansion will enable parallel expansion in whey protein generation.
- Exploring solar power projects across various sites and biogas energy generation from farm cattle.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Focused on disciplined capital allocation, stronger cash flows, sound governance, and long-term value creation.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Milk prices are subject to dynamic commodity fluctuations; slight increases may occur due to monsoon humidity or festive demand seasonality.
- B2B segment faces intense pricing pressure from competitors, leading management to consciously sacrifice certain low-margin volume contracts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kiran (TableTree Capital) on breaking the 10% sales growth band:** Management stated that FY27 will break the 10% growth benchmark due to stronger Q2/Q3 festive demand and a higher share of high-value new-age products. *(Management did not dodge)*
2. **Kanishk Gupta on new-age distribution availability:** Management explained that products like protein bars and RTD coffee are channel-specific (vending machines, pharmacies, quick-commerce) rather than mass distribution. *(Management did not dodge)*
3. **Neogi Debashish on stagnant operating margins despite premiumization and high media spends:** Management responded that cost pushes (milk inflation) are passed on via a blended portfolio approach, and Q1 typically has lower fixed overhead absorption compared to festive quarters. *(Management did not dodge)*
4. **Viraj (Enigma Investment Partners) on core category volume decline despite distribution expansion:** Management clarified that B2C core volumes are not degrowing; the overall -2% core decline is strictly due to voluntarily walking away from unprofitable B2B contracts. *(Management did not dodge)*
5. **Ashish Kumar Singh on Avvatar price hikes and cost of production:** Management declined to disclose exact cost of production per kg for products, citing company policy, but maintained that portfolio blending protects overall profitability. *(Management dodged specific product-level cost disclosures)*

### One-line takeaway
Parag Milk Foods delivered its highest-ever Q1 revenue of INR 945 crore (up 11% YoY) with stable margins, driven by 59% growth in its high-margin new-age brands, even as it consciously protected profitability over volume in select B2B channels.
