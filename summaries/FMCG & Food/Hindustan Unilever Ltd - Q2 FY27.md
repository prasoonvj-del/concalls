# Hindustan Unilever Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27 (Quarter ended September 30, 2026)  
**Tone:** Positive - HUL recorded its highest Underlying Sales Growth in 14 quarters at 15%, supported by 7% volume growth and broad-based broad segment delivery.

### Key numbers
* **Turnover:** Rs. 18,071 crore, up 15% YoY (Underlying Sales Growth / USG).
* **Underlying Volume Growth (UVG):** 7% YoY.
* **EBITDA:** Rs. 4,089 crore, up 8% YoY.
* **EBITDA Margin:** 22.6% (contracted 140 bps YoY).
* **PAT (before exceptional items):** Rs. 2,795 crore, up 9% YoY.
* **Reported PAT:** Flat YoY (impacted by a one-off tax-related gain of Rs. 273 crore in the base period).
* **H1 FY27 Turnover:** Rs. 35,255 crore with 12% USG and 6% UVG; PAT bei grew 9% YoY.
* **Interim Dividend:** Rs. 19 per share approved for FY27.

### Highlights
* Broad-based segment growth: Home Care and Beauty & Wellbeing delivered strong double-digit USG driven by double-digit UVG; Personal Care and Foods delivered 9% USG each.
* General trade distribution expanded by adding 30,000 outlets during the quarter with double-digit assortment growth.
* Modern trade category development stepped up, nearly doubling asset deployment over the last two quarters.
* Brand/Format Extensions: Launched Horlicks Protein Powder; extended Simple barrier repair range for sensitive skin; relaunched Kissan condiments with contemporary packaging.
* Recognised as 'Top Supply Chain Icon' by the Institute of Supply Chain Management (2026 ranking).

### Guidance & outlook
* **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained medium-term margin guidance range of 22% to 24%.
* **Strategy:** Confident of growing ahead of the market via the 'Winning In New India' (WINI) framework, driven by competitive volume-led revenue and profit growth.
* Structurally, management expects the portfolio to move towards higher Gross Margins over a 3- to 5-year horizon.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Demand environment remains positive and resilient, supported by strong economic momentum (India Q1 GDP growth at 7.8%).
* Rural demand showed sequential recovery over the last 4 quarters; both urban and rural demand are growing across categories and channels.
* Non-agricultural sources now contribute 30%–35% of rural incomes, insulating rural demand alongside MSP hikes and MGNREGA benefits.
* Category demand in core essentials (dishwash, laundry, tea, soaps) exhibits lower price elasticity compared to high-value discretionary categories.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Gross Margin:** Dropped ~400 bps YoY due to elevated commodity inflation (especially petrochemical inputs in Home Care and tea inflation).
* **Pricing Strategy:** Management implemented calibrated price increases (price growth of 8%) strictly to recover absolute input cost increases rather than margin percentages.
* **A&P Spends:** Dynamic calibration applied; Home Care absolute A&P was rationalised by ~Rs. 250 crore, while A&P absolute spends were increased across all other categories. SOV:SOM ratios improved from 1 to 1.1.
* A&P productivity savings of ~10% targeted via non-working media efficiencies, buying scale, and AI tools (Sangam).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* *Not discussed in detail on the call.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Approved an interim dividend of Rs. 19 per share for FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Persistent and elevated commodity cost inflation, particularly in petrochemical inputs for Home Care and mass tea prices.
* Potential localized demand/crop impacts from monsoon variations and elevated food inflation.
* Health & Wellbeing sub-segment exhibited subdued short-term performance (though 2-year CAGR for OZiva remains strongly double-digit).

### Segmental Performance Breakdown
* **Home Care:** USG 22%, UVG double-digit; Fabric Wash and Household Care both registered double-digit volume growth. Segment EBIT margin stood at 16% on ~Rs. 7,000 crore turnover base.
* **Beauty & Wellbeing:** USG 16%, UVG double-digit. Hair Care, Skin Care, and Colour Cosmetics delivered double-digit volume-led growth (accelerated by TRESemme, Minimalist, Simple, Vaseline, and Dove).
* **Personal Care:** USG 9%. Skin Cleansing grew double-digits (led by Pears and Dove bars); Oral Care recorded low-single-digit overall growth with double-digit growth in premium innovations.
* **Foods:** USG 9%, low-single-digit UVG. Coffee grew high-single digits; mass Tea was impacted by inflation; Lifestyle Nutrition (Horlicks) and Packaged Foods registered broad-based double-digit growth.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Latika Chopra (JP Morgan):** *Asked whether input cost inflation will moderate volume growth momentum, especially in rural markets.*  
  **Management:** Rural growth is sequentially stepping up, with broad-based growth across channels and geographies. Given that HUL operates in everyday essential categories, demand elasticity is lower.
* **Mihir Shah (Nomura):** *Inquired if price hikes fully cover absolute cost inflation and what the medium-term Gross Margin trajectory looks like.*  
  **Management:** Price hikes cover absolute inflation impact. Structurally, the portfolio is positioned to expand Gross Margins over a 3- to 5-year period, despite short-term cyclical variations.
* **Amit Sachdeva (UBS):** *Questioned why operating leverage didn't offset Gross Margin contraction, leading to moderate PAT growth relative to top line.*  
  **Management:** Operating leverage absorbed 260 bps of the 400 bps Gross Margin drop, limiting EBITDA margin contraction to 140 bps. Absolute PAT bei grew 9%, in line with the strategy to prioritize volume growth and market position protection over quick margin recovery.
* **Arnab Mitra (Goldman Sachs):** *Asked about divergence in performance between Home Care/B&W vs. Personal Care/Tea.*  
  **Management:** Personal Care is showing sequential acceleration (double-digit growth in cleansing). Mass tea demand faced temporary softness due to sharp cost inflation, which will normalize over time.
* **Nihal Jham (HSBC):** *Asked about the margin moderation in Home Care despite strong volume growth.*  
  **Management:** Home Care inputs face steep petrochemical inflation, creating a temporary lag between cost and price hikes. Home Care absolute EBIT remains robust at 16% on a ~Rs. 7,000 crore quarterly revenue base.
* **Kunal Vora (BNP Paribas):** *Sought impact of quick commerce expansion into Tier-2/3 towns.*  
  **Management:** Quick commerce is highly beneficial as a channel to reach affluent consumers with premium assortments, where HUL holds a disproportionately high market share.

### One-line takeaway
HUL delivered its strongest top-line growth in 14 quarters (15% USG, 7% UVG) by taking calibrated price hikes to protect volume market share while structurally driving portfolio premiumisation.
