# Procter & Gamble Health Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-18 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** FY 2025-26 (12-month period ended March 31, 2026)
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong double-digit growth in both top-line and bottom-line, driven by successful product innovations and an integrated growth strategy.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** ₹1400 crore (up 16% YoY).
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** ₹327 crore (up 30% YoY).
*   **Productivity Savings:** ₹35 crore delivered in FY26.
*   **Distribution Expansion:** Added nearly 30,000 pharmacies and HCPs to coverage.
*   **Volume Growth:** Mid-single digits in FY26.
*   **Innovation Contribution:** New launches (Livogen Iron Gummies, Neurobion Nerve Pain Relief Cream, Evion L5000) collectively contributed early single-digit points to overall growth.

### Highlights
*   **Integrated Growth Strategy:** Focuses on five pillars: Portfolio, Superiority (product, package, communication, retail execution), Productivity, Constructive Disruption, and Organization.
*   **New Product Launches:**
    *   *Livogen Iron Gummies:* Targeted at iron deficiency; ranked #1 in category on Amazon.
    *   *Neurobion Nerve Pain Relief Cream:* Targeted at nerve pain; 1 lakh HCPs educated on benefits.
    *   *Evion L5000:* Targeted at Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease; contributed incremental prescription share of early single digits.
*   **Export Performance:** Recorded 3X growth in Nepal and high single-digit growth in Sri Lanka.
*   **CSR:** P&G SEHAT initiative has impacted over 15 lakh lives since inception.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management refrained from providing specific forward-looking revenue or margin guidance.
*   Management expressed confidence in continuing growth momentum, citing strong brand fundamentals, innovation pipeline, and potential for deeper market penetration.
*   Aims for sustainable, balanced growth (top-line and bottom-line) over the long term.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **VMS Market:** Experiencing steady growth driven by rising health awareness, lifestyle-linked health challenges, and a shift toward preventive healthcare.
*   **E-commerce:** Rapidly growing and material channel; seeing encouraging growth across brands.
*   **GLP-1 Impact:** Management views GLP-1 adoption as a complementary opportunity, as patients often require nutritional foundations (VMS) alongside medical treatments.
*   **Consumer Trends:** Shift toward new formats like gummies vs. traditional tablets/capsules.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** Substantial improvement over the last 4 years, driven by productivity, favorable product mix, and pricing within NPPA limits.
*   **Productivity:** A "strategic choice" and "ongoing muscle" used to mitigate costs and fund investments in superiority.
*   **SGA Costs:** Management continues to invest in demand generation, advertising, and retail execution.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Focus on expanding coverage in "extra urban white space geographies."
*   Added nearly 30,000 pharmacies and HCPs to coverage in the last 2-3 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Not discussed in detail.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   None explicitly flagged as "risks" by management, though they noted the volatility of the external landscape and the need for agility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Sustainability of 36% EBITDA margins and impact of brand building?**
    *   *A:* Management stated margin improvement is driven by productivity, product mix, and pricing. They will maintain a balanced approach to growth, continuing to invest in superiority.
*   **Q: Can you quantify the distribution reach and bridge the gap to 8-9 lakh pharmacies?**
    *   *A:* Management highlighted the addition of 30,000 pharmacies/HCPs in the last 2-3 years via a medically trained sales force and GTM model changes.
*   **Q: How much of the growth is volume vs. price?**
    *   *A:* Management noted mid-single-digit volume growth in FY26, with the remainder of the 16% revenue growth driven by pricing (within NPPA limits) and product mix.
*   **Q: Are you planning to enter probiotics or protein supplements?**
    *   *A:* Management did not confirm specific new categories, focusing instead on their current innovation pipeline and consumer-first research.
*   **Q: Is the 15% sales growth aspiration fair for investors?**
    *   *A:* Management expressed optimism about continuing growth momentum based on brand strength and market potential but declined to confirm specific targets.

### One-line takeaway
P&G Health delivered a strong fiscal year with 16% revenue and 30% PAT growth, underpinned by successful new product launches and a disciplined focus on productivity and distribution expansion.
