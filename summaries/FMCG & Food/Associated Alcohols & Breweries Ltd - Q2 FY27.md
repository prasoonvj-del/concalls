# Associated Alcohols & Breweries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Negative - Significant margin compression due to raw material inflation and increased marketing spend, despite robust volume growth in the proprietary segment.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained 30-35% volume growth target for IMFL Proprietary.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from positive to cautious; management highlighted "unnatural" raw material price increases not seen in 30 years.
- **New topics:** Launched 'Arcana' (Blanco tequila) and 'Kultur' (hard seltzer); increased focus on digital/HoReCa marketing.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Malt maturation process is ongoing as planned; no specific update provided on SDF Industries acquisition status.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,716 million (7% YoY growth).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 177 million (7% margin).
- **PAT:** Rs 80 million (3% margin).
- **IMFL Proprietary Revenue:** Rs 577 million (39% YoY growth).
- **IMFL Proprietary Volume:** 28% YoY growth (Q2); 34% YoY growth (H1).
- **ENA Volume:** 7.4 million liters (36% YoY growth).
- **Ethanol Revenue:** Rs 763 million (1% EBITDA margin).
- **IMIL Volume:** 0.90 million cases (flat YoY).

### Highlights
- Launched 'Arcana' premium Blanco tequila in Madhya Pradesh (Rs 7,500/bottle); planning expansion to Karnataka, Maharashtra, Haryana, Goa, and Delhi.
- Launched 'Kultur' hard seltzer in Madhya Pradesh; registration underway in 4 states.
- Central Province series volume grew 56% YoY.
- Malt plant (6,000 liters/day) commissioned Sept 2025; maturation started Nov 2025; utilization expected from Q3 FY28.
- Ethanol plant operated at 95% capacity utilization in H1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects IMFL Proprietary volume growth of 30-35%.
- Expects margin recovery from April onwards, driven by price hikes and malt plant benefits.
- Targets ~Rs 10 crore revenue from new Tequila and RTD products in the next year.
- Management expects ethanol raw material prices to ease in ~1 month as new crop arrives and FCI rice allocation increases.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Grain prices at all-time high (~Rs 30,000/ton for maize).
- Ethanol segment faces oversupply; OMC tender price for maize-based ethanol reduced from Rs 71.8 to Rs 68.
- Tequila market is niche (25,000-30,000 cases/annum) but growing at 35-40% annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin declined to 7% (vs 11% in Q1 FY27) due to higher grain, glass, and PET bottle costs.
- Other expenses increased due to higher contract manufacturing (doubled) and marketing/branding spend (Rs 2.5-3 crore this quarter vs Rs 50-60 lakh in same quarter last year).
- Raw material costs: Maize up Rs 2,000-2,500/ton; PET bottles up from Rs 101/kg to Rs 145/kg; glass bottles up 30%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Malt maturation adds ~Rs 3 crore/month to inventory.
- Preparing for strategic entry into Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh by end of FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management is managing working capital to accommodate the planned malt maturation investment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued pressure on margins from elevated raw material and packaging costs.
- Legal: Ongoing Supreme Court case regarding CCI inquiry (government is defending); FSSAI order remains under stay in MP High Court.
- High entry costs for new markets (e.g., Maharashtra) due to trade schemes and discounts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Why did margins drop significantly?** Management attributed it to a "perfect storm" of raw material inflation (grain/packaging) and increased marketing spend for new brand launches.
- **When will margins recover?** Management expects recovery from April onwards via price hikes and the start of malt plant benefits.
- **Why is the ethanol business struggling?** Oversupply and reduced OMC tender prices (Rs 68 vs Rs 71.8) are pressuring realizations.
- **What is the Tequila strategy?** Focus on premium positioning via events, PR, and influencer marketing to build brand perception.
- **Why are other expenses up?** Primarily due to increased contract manufacturing and marketing/branding costs for new launches.

### One-line takeaway
AABL is aggressively diversifying into premium segments (Tequila/RTD) to drive long-term growth, but near-term profitability is severely constrained by record-high input costs and heavy brand-building investments.
