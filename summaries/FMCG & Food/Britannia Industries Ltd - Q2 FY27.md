# Britannia Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 16.6% YoY with 13% volume growth, strong General Trade recovery, e-commerce momentum, and recovery in international operations despite multi-commodity inflation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained/None explicitly provided; management indicated further price increases (via shrinkflation) are in execution to offset input cost inflation.
- **Tone and demand view:** Volume growth accelerated from 9% YoY in Q1 to 13% YoY in Q2; General Trade (GT) growth accelerated significantly (2.6x of FY26 rate); International business recovered after Middle East geopolitical disruptions.
- **New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared:** Milk price spike (+16% YoY) and packaging laminate inflation (+18% YoY) emerged as primary cost headwinds; Reliance's entry into biscuits (Tamil Nadu) was evaluated; odd pricing (Rs 4.5) was completely eliminated from the market.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the promised pricing actions (implemented 3.5% price increase in Q2 vs 1.5–2% guidance) and achieved double-digit international business recovery.

---

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 5,542 crore (+16.6% YoY, +11.6% QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 10,506 crore (+13.1% YoY) — crossed Rs 10,000 crore in H1 for the first time.
*   **PAT:** Rs 726 crore (13.0% of revenue, +10.2% YoY, +21.9% QoQ).
*   **H1 FY27 PAT:** 12.5% of revenue (+11.7% YoY).
*   **Volume Growth:** 13% YoY.
*   **Operating Profit Growth:** 8.5% YoY (16.2% of revenue in H1).
*   **PBT Growth:** 10.6% YoY.
*   **Price Hike Taken:** 3.5% in Q2 FY27.
*   **Channel Salience:** E-commerce reached ~7% of total revenues (double-digit share in urban markets).
*   **Adjacent Bakery Business Growth:** >20% YoY.

---

### Highlights
*   **GT Recovery:** GT growth in Q2 FY27 reached 2.6x the full-year FY26 rate, driven by semi-urban and rural demand resilience and premiumization.
*   **Adjacencies & Innovations:** Adjacent bakery portfolio grew >20% YoY, led by croissants and cakes. Launched Bourbon Modak (Ganesh Chaturthi, sold out), Cheeze Dipped, NutriChoice range expansions, and flavored cheese slices (Korean Chilli).
*   **International Recovery:** International business recovered to good double-digit growth following Middle East war disruptions in prior quarters, despite elevated regional freight costs.
*   **Pricing Rationalization:** Fractional/odd pricing (Rs 4.5 price point) has been completely buried across the industry; market has fully transitioned back to standard Rs 5 and Rs 10 price points.
*   *Pattern check:* Continues record pattern of strong double-digit top-line performance backed by GT, e-commerce, and >20% growth in adjacent bakery categories.

---

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Pricing Levers:** Further price increases (via shrinkflation) are in the pipeline as current RM inflation exceeds the 3.5% price hike taken in Q2.
*   **Platform Expansion:** Management plans to expand non-cheese dairy formats and health platforms (protein, zero-added-sugar variants) while scaling e-commerce as a dedicated strategic business unit.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Consumer Resilience:** Rural and semi-urban markets displayed strong transaction and volume trends, showing continuous demand stability across lower LPPs (Rs 5 / Rs 10).
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Assessing Reliance's entry in Tamil Nadu; management noted that disrupting Rs 5/10 LPPs in biscuits is much harder than in beverage/cola categories due to pack weight dynamics.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Wheat Flour:** +9% QoQ, marginally down YoY.
*   **Refined Palm Oil:** +11% QoQ, +17% YoY (driven by crude and El Niño/rainfall deficit).
*   **Sugar:** +4% QoQ, +6% YoY.
*   **Milk:** +16% YoY, +2% QoQ (unseasonal price increase during winter months).
*   **Laminates (Packaging):** +7% QoQ, +18% YoY.
*   **Fuel (Gasoline/LNG):** Sequential relief QoQ, but remains +45% YoY.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Persistent RM Inflation:** Accelerated cost spikes in milk, packaging laminates, and palm oil.
*   **Freight & Logistics:** Unusually high operational and shipping costs in Middle East/Africa regions.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Mihir Shah (Nomura): How should investors view gross margin trajectory given RM inflation?**  
    *Management:* Inflation across agri-commodities and packaging is elevated. The 3.5% price increase covered only a part of cost inflation; additional shrinkflation measures are currently being rolled out to close the gap.
*   **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama): What is the threat from competition (Malkist, Reliance) and odd-pricing recovery?**  
    *Management:* Britannia countered Malkist with Cheeze Dipped. Reliance is testing Tamil Nadu, but LPP disruption in biscuits is difficult. Rs 4.5 pricing is completely gone, and Britannia has fully restored its shelf space and market share.
*   **Aditya Soman: Is the GT recovery base-effect driven or structural?**  
    *Management:* Last year's GST base effect accounts for only 1.5–2% of growth. The GT recovery is structural, driven by rural/semi-urban demand, premiumization, and internal distribution execution under "Winning in Many Indias".
*   **Nihal Mahesh Jham: What is the potential of e-commerce and performance of adjacencies?**  
    *Management:* E-commerce (~7% share overall) is double-digit in urban areas excluding Rs 5/10 LPPs and serves as a vehicle for premiumization. Adjacencies grew >20%, led by croissants and cakes.
*   **Latika Chopra: What is the medium-term strategy and portfolio mix?**  
    *Management:* Biscuits account for 78–80% of sales and will continue growing via core innovations. Growth will be rounded across adjacencies (>20% growth), e-commerce-specific products, health platforms, and premiumization.

---

### One-line takeaway
Britannia delivered strong 16.6% revenue and 13% volume growth behind a structural GT recovery and >20% growth in adjacencies, while deploying further shrinkflation hikes to tackle multi-commodity cost inflation.
