# IGL Spirits Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Margin expansion through product mix shift towards IMFL offset challenges in biofuels, meeting guidance targets.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Revenue:** Rs 2,295 crore (+10.3% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Revenue:** Rs 4,837 crore (+14.3% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Net Revenue:** Rs 706 crore (-7% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Net Revenue:** Rs 1,389 crore (-4% YoY from Rs 1,450 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 125 crore (flattish YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 245 crore (vs Rs 238 crore YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 17.7% (+129 bps YoY from 16.4%)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 17.6% (vs 16.5% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 8.7% (improvement of 100+ bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 120 crore (vs Rs 112 crore YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Margin:** 39.5% (vs 35.4% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Biofuel EBITDA:** Rs 71 crore (margin 10.2% vs 8.4% YoY)
- **Gross Borrowings:** Rs 714-715 crore (down by Rs 88 crore from March)
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 186 crore (positive)
- **Gross Debt-to-Equity:** Improved from 0.66x to 0.54x

### Highlights
- First earnings call and results of IGL Spirits Limited as an independently listed company following demerger from India Glycols and listing on BSE and NSE on 4th November 2026.
- Prioritized margins over volume; revenue mix shifted towards high-margin IMFL (IMFL revenue share reached 18% in Q2, expected 24% in Q3, targeting ~30% by Q4).
- Potable spirits realization per case moved northwards from Rs 410 to Rs 432. Standalone IMFL realization at Rs 832.
- Non-IMFL volumes in UP dropped in Q2 (6.2 million cases vs 6.4 million last year) due to government mandates on higher-strength alcohol and 75% supply limits in core districts requiring 25% traded brands.
- Operating cash flow surged to Rs 186 crore, enabling an Rs 88 crore debt repayment. Management reiterated becoming debt-free by FY 2028-29.
- *Note on Record:* The record notes a strategic shift toward high-margin IMFL and state rollouts, which the call heavily confirms and details.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated FY27 EBITDA guidance of Rs 500 crore to Rs 550 crore, stating it is within reach (delivered Rs 245 crore in H1).
- Endeavour is to take IMFL contribution of potable spirits EBITDA close to 50% by the end of this fiscal, and total EBITDA (including ethanol) to 50% from IMFL over the next two years.
- Long-term aspiration: Rs 1,000 crore EBITDA in the next 4 to 5 years.
- Biofuel/ethanol supply expected to touch 20-21 crore litres in the current ESY/financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- IMFL is the key growth engine; IMFL volumes grew by 84% and revenue by 42% in Q2. Delivered 2.8 million cases in H1, on track to exceed 2x last year's IMFL volume.
- Non-IMFL business acts as a cash cow with low working capital and stable margins (23%-25%), though industry growth is flat. Management is market leader in UP (25-degree category in 27-28 districts with 75% mandated quota) and Uttarakhand.
- Expanding footprint: successful in West Bengal Expression of Interest (launch Dec-Jan), aggressively pursuing Andhra Pradesh, Daman, Silvassa, Rajasthan, Punjab, Himachal, and the Northeast.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Grain prices for biofuels rose from Rs 21/kg in Q1 to Rs 23/kg in Q2, expected to reach Rs 26.50–27/kg in Q3 before stabilizing from December 2026 with new crop availability.
- Biofuel/ENA manufacturing cost per liter in Q2 was ~Rs 56, expected to rise to Rs 64-65 due to grain inflation. ENA margins remain double that of biofuels.
- Coal prices increased from ~Rs 5,500 to Rs 6,800 in Q2, impacting power and fuel costs (Q2 power & fuel cost at Rs 56 crore vs Rs 45 crore QoQ), expected to stay same in H2. Power costs are mitigated by using molasses pent wash and seasonal rice husk.
- FCI rice (30% allocation) helps cushion grain cost inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- H1 capex was Rs 54 crore, with closing CWIP at Rs 65 crore, attributable to tank/machine maintenance and ENA capacity enhancement.
- Kashipur ENA manufacturing capacity is expanding from ~30 lakh liters per month to ~55 lakh liters per month, expected to commence from end-December or January first week.
- Combined distillery capacity at Kashipur and Gorakhpur stands at 1,100 KLPD (grain or molasses).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total assets at Rs 3,323 crore (vs Rs 3,259 crore in March). Shareholder funds at Rs 1,329 crore.
- Borrowings reduced by ~Rs 80-88 crore to Rs 714-715 crore via term loan repayments from positive operating cash flows (Rs 186 crore).
- Gross debt-to-equity improved to 0.54x from 0.66x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rising input costs: grain prices and coal prices increasing sequentially, squeezing thin biofuel margins.
- Regulatory interventions in non-IMFL (mandated higher alcohol strength and 75% district capping) adversely impacted Q2 non-IMFL revenue.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Neil Bahal (Negen Capital) asked about the multi-year target for IMFL share in overall EBITDA.** Management answered the endeavor is to reach 50% (including ethanol) over the next two years.
2. **Balasubramanian (Investor) asked about realization trends and premiumisation strategy.** Management clarified that potable realization is Rs 432 (blended with non-IMFL) and standalone IMFL is Rs 832, aiming for Rs 1,000 as IMFL mix scales to 30%.
3. **Saket Kapoor interrograted grain price trajectories and ENA capacity expansion.** Management confirmed Q3 grain prices will hit Rs 26.50/kg and detailed the Kashipur ENA capacity expansion from 30 lakh to 55 lakh liters/month starting late December.
4. **Kushal Binani (Daksham Capital) questioned the path to a Rs 1,000 crore EBITDA target given flat non-IMFL growth.** Management explained growth will stem from scaling the IMFL portfolio across 12 markets with low initial overheads.
5. **Manoj Reddy Samma asked about the Amrut partnership model and Tamil Nadu entry.** Management clarified they source the blend from Amrut and manufacture using their own ENA; Tamil Nadu is low priority due to local manufacturing mandates and lack of a strong brandy portfolio.

### One-In-All Takeaway
IGL Spirits delivered stable H1 FY27 performance with a 17.6% EBITDA margin, successfully offsetting regulatory headwinds in non-IMFL and margin pressure in biofuels by aggressively shifting its revenue mix toward high-margin IMFL.
