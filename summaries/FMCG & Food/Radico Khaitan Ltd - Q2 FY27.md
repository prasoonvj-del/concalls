# Radico Khaitan Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly revenue, volumes, and profitability, driven by 36% growth in Prestige & Above (P&A) volumes and upgraded full-year P&A volume growth guidance.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue:** Rs. 1,746 crore.
* **EBITDA:** Rs. 356 crore; EBITDA Margin: 20.4% (+462 bps YoY).
* **Gross Margin:** 48.0% (+440 bps YoY, -109 bps QoQ).
* **Total IMFL Volume:** >10 million cases (+7% YoY).
* **Prestige & Above (P&A) Volume:** 5.28 million cases (+36% YoY).
* **Segment EBITDA Margins:** IMFL business at 24%+; Non-IMFL business at 9.5% to 10%.

### Highlights
* Delivered record volume, revenue, and EBITDA margin during Q2 FY27.
* Product mix and premiumization contributed +510 bps YoY to Gross Margin, while price increases added +85 bps YoY.
* Launched *Magic Moments Aam Panna* vodka in August; flavored vodkas now account for ~75% of the company's total vodka portfolio.
* Expanded international travel retail presence to 4 major U.K. airports (Heathrow, Gatwick, Birmingham, Manchester), targeting 100 duty-free locations globally (currently at ~70).
* Leadership elevations: Dilip Banthiya appointed Executive Director & CFO; Saket Somani elevated to Deputy CFO.

### Guidance & outlook
* **P&A Volume Growth:** Upgraded guidance to 30% growth for FY27.
* **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained guidance at ~20% for full year FY27.
* **Luxury Portfolio:** On track to meet guidance of 25% YoY value growth in FY27 (off a FY26 base of Rs. 475 crore).
* Management expects long-term gross margins to continue expanding past FY27 as vodka and luxury share increases.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Vodka's share in overall IMFL reached 6.3% in Q2 FY27 (up from 3.7% in FY25 and 4.5% in FY26), driven by shifting consumer preferences among Gen Z and female demographics.
* Industry P&A volume growth stood at 5.8% to 6.0% for the quarter, compared to Radico’s non-vodka P&A growth of ~20% and vodka growth of 49%.
* Policy changes in Karnataka (duty reduction and free pricing) led to >100% YoY growth in Radico’s premium volumes in the state. Karnataka accounts for 6% of company saliency.
* Tamil Nadu is seeing positive trends with orders shifting to a tertiary basis, driving a 20% volume growth for Radico in the state.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Q2 gross margin faced a ~Rs. 27 crore negative financial impact (~155-160 bps) due to packaging material inflation (glass bottles, PET resin, and laminates) triggered by West Asia conflicts.
* Sequential gross margin compression (-109 bps) was driven by cyclical brand mix shifts and grain price inflation.
* Grain prices are expected to stabilize from November onward due to the new crop season and elevated FCI grain allocation (72 lakh metric tonnes) under the ethanol policy.
* Strategic inventory buildup in raw materials helped buffer input cost pressures during H1 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Capex remains disciplined and focused on maintenance, operational efficiencies, and essential capacity optimization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Working capital experienced a temporary build in Q2 FY27 to support strong premium portfolio growth, new brand launches, and strategic raw material stocking; expected to normalize in H2 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Geopolitical conflicts in West Asia leading to volatile freight rates and supply chain cost impacts on packaging materials (glass, resin, laminates).
* Industry-wide Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) investigation regarding HSN code classification (2208) for Scotch imports (management confirmed compliance with existing laws).

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **DRI Investigation:** Management clarified that imported Scotch has consistently used HSN code 2208 for 10–20 years. Management believes the company is in full compliance with current laws.
* **U.K. Free Trade Agreement (FTA) & Competition:** Management noted an anticipated 8–10% tariff reduction on Bottled-in-Origin (BIO) products. Their luxury single malts (*Rampur*, *Virasat*) are priced higher than standard BIO products, minimizing competitive impact.
* **Vodka Category Outlook:** Management expects vodka to outpace other spirit categories over the next 3–5 years, citing global benchmark vodka share (~28%) versus India (~6.3%).
* **Regular Segment Growth:** The segment declined YoY due to base effects (Maharashtra policy shift, Andhra Pradesh route-to-market change). Management expects benign 1–2% volume growth going forward.
* **Price Revisions:** Industry associations typically initiate state excise policy price increase representations starting November ahead of February state policy announcements.

### One-line takeaway
Radico Khaitan delivered record performance behind 36% growth in its premium portfolio, upgrading its FY27 P&A volume guidance to 30% while retaining a 20% EBITDA margin target.
