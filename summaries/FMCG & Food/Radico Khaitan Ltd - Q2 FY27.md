# Radico Khaitan Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered the strongest quarter in history with record volume, revenue, and profitability, driven by premiumization.

### Key numbers
- IMFL Volume: >10 million cases (7% YoY growth)
- Prestige & Above (P&A) Volume: 5.28 million cases (36% YoY growth)
- Revenue: Rs. 1,746 crore
- EBITDA: Rs. 356 crore
- EBITDA Margin: 20.4% (up 462 bps YoY)
- Gross Margin: 48.0% (up 440 bps YoY, down 109 bps QoQ)
- Packaging material impact on Gross Margin: ~Rs. 27 crore (~155-160 bps)
- Luxury Portfolio Value Growth Guidance: 25% for FY27 (on Rs. 475 crore base last year)

### Highlights
- Achieved record volume, revenue, and profitability in Q2 FY27.
- Vodka category structural shift accelerating; category share of IMFL reached 6.3% in Q2 (up from 3.7% in FY25 and 4.5% in FY26).
- Launched Magic Moments Aam Panna in August with a digital-first campaign.
- Extended After Dark’s new look to 10 states; 8PM Premium Black and Royal Ranthambore continuing strong momentum.
- Became the first Indian spirits company present across 4 major U.K. airports (Heathrow, Gatwick, Birmingham, Manchester); total GTR presence reached ~70 duty-free locations, targeting 100.
- Leadership changes: Dilip Banthiya joined the Board as Executive Director and CFO; Saket Somani elevated to Deputy CFO.

### Guidance & outlook
- Upgraded Prestige & Above volume growth guidance to 30% for FY27.
- Confident of sustaining an EBITDA margin of around 20% for FY27.
- Reaffirmed luxury portfolio value growth target of 25% for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry growth in P&A stands at 5.8% to 6%; Radico's P&A grew 36%.
- Younger generation (Gen Z) and women are driving a structural shift toward lighter, mixable white spirits (vodka). Flavored vodka accounts for ~75% of Radico's vodka portfolio.
- Karnataka led strong regional growth (premium volumes up >100%), though its saliency remains at 6%.
- Tamil Nadu showing positive tertiary offtake in September (volumes up 20%); new digital payment rule for orders >1,000 introduced.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion of 440 bps YoY driven by premiumization/product mix (+510 bps) and price increases (+85 bps), offset by packaging volatility.
- Packaging material prices impacted gross margin by ~Rs. 27 crore due to West Asia conflicts affecting glass bottles, PET resin, and laminates.
- Grain prices supported by built-up stock; new crop season from November and FCI ethanol allocation of 72 lakh metric tonnes expected to stabilize grain prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex is disciplined, directed towards maintenance, operational efficiencies, and essential capacity optimization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Temporary investment made in working capital to support premium portfolio growth, new launches, and secure raw materials amid price volatility; expected to normalize in H2.
- Maintained a strong balance sheet with improved ROE and ROCE.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in raw material (grain) and packaging material (glass, PET, laminates) prices due to geopolitical issues (West Asia war).
- Industry-wide DRI scrutiny/investigation regarding the HSN code and import of bulk Scotches (management stated Radico complies with current laws and uses code 2208 as it has for 10-20 years).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Aditya Soman (CLSA) on DRI investigation & raw material costs:** Management clarified that Radico has always used HSN code 2208 for Scotch imports and views it as an industry-wide study by the DRI; reiterated 20% EBITDA margin guidance despite raw material volatility.
2. **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama) on U.K. FTA & volume drivers:** Management stated U.K. FTA reduced BIO costs by 8%-10%, but Radico's single malts (Rampur, Virasat) are priced higher than BIO, posing no challenge. Growth is driven by the vodka shift and strong traction in Karnataka.
3. **Harit Kapoor (Investec) on export headwinds:** Management noted West Asian geopolitical issues impacted shipments initially, but volumes have bounced back; current challenge is elevated freight rates, partly absorbed by customers.
4. **Rahul Agarwal (Ikigai Asset) on U.P. election impact:** Management stated Uttar Pradesh liquor business is large (~Rs. 60,000-65,000 crore) with a well-set system, and business does not slow down during elections.
5. **Harsh Sheth (ICICI Securities) on realization and Regular segment:** Management stated Q2 realization grew 2.2% QoQ (12% YoY due to premium mix shift); Regular segment decline is base-led from Maharashtra/Andhra Pradesh and expected to normalize to 1%-2% growth.

### One-line takeaway
Radico Khaitan delivered its strongest quarter in history with record revenues and a 36% jump in Prestige & Above volumes, leading management to upgrade its FY27 P&A volume growth guidance to 30% while holding a 20% EBITDA margin target despite input cost volatility.
