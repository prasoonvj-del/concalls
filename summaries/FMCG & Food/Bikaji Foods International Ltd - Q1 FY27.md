# Bikaji Foods International Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management noted a robust demand recovery in the second half of the quarter after early operational disruptions, with mid-teens revenue growth expected ahead.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth (primary): 12.5% YoY
- Volume growth: 7.7% YoY
- EBITDA margin: 13.5% (vs. 12.2% in Q4)
- Ethnic Snacks growth: 11.4% YoY
- Western Snacks growth: 21.3% YoY
- Sweets growth: 4.4% YoY
- Papad growth: -6.5% YoY
- Core states growth: 11% YoY
- Focus states growth: 19% YoY (UP grew ~37%)
- Other states growth: 5.6% YoY
- Export growth: -2.2% YoY
- Family pack growth: 11% YoY; Impulse pack growth: 10.5% YoY
- Retail business (THF) growth: 71.8% YoY; store count increased from 15 to 28 over the year.
- Direct reach: 3,70,000+ outlets (added 17,000 outlets in Q1)
- PLI contribution to EBITDA: ~150 bps (INR 12.5 crore in Q1 out of INR 14 crore other operating income)

### Highlights
- First 45 days impacted by supply/production issues due to the sad demise of the Chairman (3-4 days pan-India plant shutdown) and Bengal elections causing migrant Bengali labor to leave Bikaner.
- Post 45 days, strong demand seen across Ethnic and Western snacks; June recorded the highest-ever deliveries.
- Invested heavily in marketing during Q1 (Pankaj Tripathi campaign in UP, Western snacks launches, international exhibitions) to build festive momentum.
- Completed construction of ~1 lakh square feet ASRS facility in Bikaner for supply chain efficiency (holding 1.2 to 1.3 lakh extra cartons).
- Bikaneri Bhujia production to be decentralized to a second plant outside Bikaner to mitigate single-location risks.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management target for the full year is 15% plus overall revenue growth (or mid-teens around 16% +/-).
- Full-year EBITDA margin target: 13% to 13.5% (including PLI).
- Long-term margin target: 15% over the next 3 years.
- Full-year A&P spend target: ~2% of sales, though heavier in Q2 and Q3 due to the festive season.
- Bikanerwala (THF) expansion plan: 10 new stores this year (taking total from 22 to 35+), targeting 50 stores in 2.5–3 years, aiming for 50-60% YoY growth. 2 Bikaji stores planned for this year.
- Exports contribution expected to rise from 3-4% to 5.5-6% over 3-4 years; US business expected to grow 3x in 2 years.
- Sweets growth targeted at 12-13% for Q2-Q3; gifting upwards of 17-18%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Quick commerce (Q-com) channel growing exponentially, with Bikaji's Q-com growth upwards of 100% in Q1.
- General Trade (GT) and Modern Trade (MT) demand and festive commitments for the next 3 months are very positive.
- Category growth over the past 2 years has been single-digit (recovering from previous double-digit trends), but seeing a clear uptick post-GST rationalization (from 12% to 5%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost pressures noted in edible oils (due to geopolitical issues and lower rainfall) and pulses (moth dal and chana dal). Coal prices also increased manufacturing costs by 30-40 bps.
- Implemented 2 price increases in the last 4 months (one MRP increase in April). No further MRP hikes planned before Diwali.
- PLI benefit contributes ~150 bps to EBITDA this year; management expects it to take 1.5 to 2 years to bridge the margin gap once PLI phases out next year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- ASRS warehouse facility operational this month in Bikaner (~1 lakh sq. ft.).
- Nepal JV plant construction underway with partner (INR 15 crore investment each); local production expected in 8 to 9 months.
- Bakery segment trials ongoing; targeting exports, e-com/q-com, and HoReCa channels over the next 2 quarters.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not discussed in detail, other than JV investments (e.g., INR 15 crore in Nepal).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Single-location manufacturing concentration in Bikaner for core bhujia exposed the company to labor migration (Bengal elections) and local disruptions.
- U.S. tariff uncertainties and crude oil price surges causing 2x-3x freight cost spikes, delaying export shipments.
- Weather-dependent manual production for papads impacted by early monsoons.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama):** Asked about the unique Bikaner labor/election issue and long-term mitigation. *Management explained core bhujia is concentrated in Bikaner, but they are decentralizing production to a second plant outside Bikaner.*
- **Percy Panthaki (IIFL):** Questioned whether a 5-day production stoppage truly impacts a whole quarter with distributor pipelines. *Management clarified that dispatches were halted and labor shortages constrained operations through mid-May, but June recovery was the highest ever.*
- **Shirish Pardeshi (IIFL Securities):** Asked about D2C/Q-com private label competition and margins. *Management stated private labels do not threaten established brands significantly, and consumer loyalty persists.*
- **Abhishek Mathur (Analyst):** Inquired about the 13.5% margin guidance versus a 15% operating margin media statement. *Management clarified that 15% is a 3-year long-term goal, while 13.5% is the target for FY27 due to heavier festive ad spends in H2.*
- **Anand Shah (Analyst):** Asked if the 13-13.5% margin includes PLI. *Management confirmed it includes PLI.*

### One-line takeaway
Bikaji delivered a 12.5% revenue growth and 13.5% EBITDA margin in Q1 FY27 despite early-quarter labor and supply disruptions, and remains confident of mid-teen revenue growth and 13-13.5% EBITDA margins for the full year, driven by strong festive demand.
