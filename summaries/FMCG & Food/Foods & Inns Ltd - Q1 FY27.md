# Foods & Inns Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management is bullish on domestic demand and frozen foods expansion, despite short-term export logistics challenges.

### Key numbers
- Frozen segment grew at 30% CAGR over the last 2 years; Q1 frozen growth was 20%.
- Frozen segment revenue last year was USD 12 million; Q1 FY27 was USD 3 million, targeted to reach USD 30+ million in 3 years.
- Kusum spices grew by 14.7% YoY.
- Export segment represents 35% to 40% of the business; domestic represents 55%.
- Average realization declined by 18.5% YoY (noted in Q&A).

### Highlights
- Mango production in Q1 showed good fruit quality and reasonable prices.
- Coca-Cola celebrated 50 years of Maaza and acknowledged the company's association, support, and sustainable sourcing.
- Sustainability efforts initiated in 2014 were recognized by a large French customer (associated for 2 years) and extended to a UK-based brand.
- Commercial production and auditing of the pectin segment have started; sampling and consumer testing are underway with overseas brands, with opportunities expected from October/November onwards.
- CFO Anand Krishnan resigned, with his last day on August 11th; the process to appoint a new CFO is underway with shortlisted candidates.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to catch up on pending export dispatches over the next 3 quarters.
- Management anticipates domestic volume growth driven by favorable juice/drink consumption conditions due to the El Nino effect.
- Management maintains a target of 20%+ growth for the frozen segment, aiming for USD 30+ million in 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Export dispatches slowed down due to vessel non-availability and significant ocean freight increases; however, there are no order cancellations, only delayed call-offs.
- Indian beverage industry expects favorable conditions for juice and drink consumption due to the El Nino effect.
- U.S. and UK markets show huge demand opportunities in the frozen segment, driven partly by food security concerns amid global uncertainties.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- The company works on a cost-plus model where raw material prices are variable, but other costs and margins remain within a plus/minus 5% range.
- Raw material prices for mango have dropped significantly compared to previous years (e.g., from an average of Rs 25 in FY24 to Rs 9 currently), reducing final realizable values proportionally.
- The frozen segment is currently able to absorb higher freight costs, unlike other segments which take a more conservative approach.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- The company is in the process of expanding its frozen food capacity to cater to domestic and export demand.
- Trial runs for the frozen pulp division in the West Asia market have been completed.
- Exploring joint ventures in certain geographies for brand expansion where manufacturing remains with Foods and Inns.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Delayed export shipments have caused a blockage in working capital, resulting in higher interest costs to bear.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Non-availability of vessels and extraordinary ocean freight rates impacting export dispatches and blocking working capital.
- Longer approval time for pectin sampling and consumer testing by brands.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nalin Shah (NVS Brokerage) on export logistics and perishable risks:** Management clarified that processed finished products in drums/cans have a 2-year shelf life, so there is no risk of spoilage from shipment delays.
2. **Khushi Solanki (Individual Investor) on export backlog and working capital:** Management stated that the backlog clearing time is not in their control (estimated 45-50 days), and air freight is ruled out due to being 30x the cost of ocean freight.
3. **Ankur Agarwal (RC Business House) on stagnant top-line and promoter holding:** Management explained that top-line value dropped due to plunging raw material prices (e.g., mango prices dropping from Rs 25 to Rs 9) while volumes grew; management welcomed suggestions on increasing promoter stake.
4. **Madhuri (Retail Investor) on B2C expansion and off-season utilization:** Management noted that tomato is the second-largest capacity utilization product during the off-season, alongside 365-day operations in frozen, spray drying, and spice divisions.
5. **Amit Gori (Individual Investor) on online availability and CFO replacement:** Management confirmed availability on Zepto, Blinkit, and select BigBasket geographies, and noted that CFO candidates have been shortlisted following Anand Krishnan's resignation.

### One-line takeaway
Despite short-term export shipping delays and working capital blockages, Foods & Inns remains bullish on domestic demand, strong frozen food growth, and expanding capacity.
