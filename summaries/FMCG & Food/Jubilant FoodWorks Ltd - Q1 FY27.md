# Jubilant FoodWorks Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong Popeyes momentum, steady delivery growth, and confidence in reaching full-year growth targets despite cost headwinds.

### Key numbers
* **Domino's LFL Growth:** 2.5% YoY in Q1 FY27 (compared to 11.6% in Q1 FY26).
* **Popeyes LFL Growth:** 45% YoY in Q1 FY27 (up from 9.2% in Q1 FY26).
* **Gross Margin:** 75.5%.
* **Personnel Costs:** Up 12% YoY on a standalone basis and 15.6% YoY on a consolidated basis.
* **Cost Headwinds:** ~200 bps total cost headwind, including a 120 bps impact from LPG prices.
* **Price Hikes:** Calibrated price increases of ~140 bps taken to offset input costs.

### Highlights
* **Popeyes Scaling:** Emerged as a second growth engine with 45% LFL growth, driven by product quality, marination processes, supply chain support, and localized product innovations (e.g., 6 chicken wing flavors). 7 cities surpassed Rs 100K Average Daily Sales (ADS).
* **Domino's Dine-In Strategy:** Appointed dedicated channel leadership and regional teams to drive store dine-in traffic across 400 targeted stores; introduced store-exclusive deals ("Best Deals Ever Wednesday") and solo menu propositions (<Rs 250 ticket size).
* **Capital Reallocation:** Stopped capital deployment into Hong's Kitchen and exited Dunkin' to reallocate capital exclusively toward higher-ROI formats (Domino's and Popeyes).
* **Delivery Performance:** Delivery channel order volumes continued to grow; digital adoption remains high on the proprietary Domino's app.

### Guidance & outlook
* **LFL Growth Guidance:** Management expects 5%–7% LFL growth for the remainder of FY27, noting Q2 FY27 started on a better note than Q1 FY27.
* **Margin Target:** Reiterated target of 200 bps EBITDA margin expansion (100 bps from Domino's and 100 bps from emerging brands), with emerging brands tracking ahead of schedule.
* **Popeyes Ambition:** Targeting to scale Popeyes into a profitable Rs 1,000 crore brand.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Management noted consumer demand remains strong across eating-out and protein-rich categories, with higher growth seen in delivery.
* Delivery minimum order value (MOV) across the QSR industry has shifted lower to Rs 99 and Rs 49.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Faced cost pressure from LPG (+120 bps), wage increases across 14–15 states, new labor code notifications, diesel/logistics costs, and commodity inflation in cheese (dairy), imported edible oil, and chicken.
* Net margin headwind restricted to 20 bps QoQ via calibrated price hikes, store-level wastage reductions, productivity improvements (orders per hour), and commissary supply chain efficiencies.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Capex Guidance:** Maintained full-year capex guidance at Rs 750 crore to Rs 900 crore.
* Capex allocation is shifting primarily to revenue-generating store openings (Domino's and Popeyes), existing store dine-in redesigns, and technology.
* Supply chain investments have peaked post-commissioning of the Mumbai food factory, with existing infrastructure capable of supporting up to ~5,000 stores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Turned free cash flow positive in FY26 after dipping in FY24–FY25; expects operating cash flow growth to outpace revenue.
* Internal capital allocation focus is centered on ROCE maximization and EPS improvement.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Prolonged commodity inflation in dairy, cheese, edible oil, and chicken.
* Lower delivery MOV (Rs 99 / Rs 49) reduces average order value (AOV) and increases delivery cost per order.
* Unfavorable geopolitical conditions sustaining elevated fuel and LPG prices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Kunal Vora (BNP Paribas):** *What drove Popeyes LFL acceleration to 45% and what is the capex guidance?*  
  **Management:** Product differentiation, localized flavors (6 wing flavors), fresh chicken marination, and strong store execution drove growth. Capex guidance is maintained at Rs 750 crore to Rs 900 crore, indexed to store rollout and technology.
* **Vivek M (Institutional Equities):** *What specific steps are being taken for dine-in and can you reach 5%-7% LFL growth?*  
  **Management:** Created dedicated channel leadership, rolled out in-store exclusive Wednesday promotions, and targeted solo occasions (<Rs 250). Confirmed confidence in achieving 5%–7% LFL growth as Q2 FY27 started stronger than Q1 FY27.
* **Tejash Shah:** *Has Popeyes reached sustainable unit economics and what is the plan for free cash flow?*  
  **Management:** 7 cities hit >Rs 100K ADS; overhead amortization as scale increases will drive EBITDA profitability. Exited low-ROI formats (Dunkin', Hong's Kitchen) and reduced supply chain capex to maximize ROCE.
* **Latika Chopra (CLSA):** *Are the 200 bps EBITDA margin expansion targets intact despite cost inflation?*  
  **Management:** Emerging brands are delivering ahead of track on their 100 bps margin target. Domino's is offsetting inflation via calibrated pricing and structural cost optimizations, maintaining full guidance.
* **Nihal Mahesh Jham:** *How does dropping MOV to Rs 99 impact business profitability?*  
  **Management:** The drop was a competitive necessity to match aggregators. While it dilutes AOV and adds variable cost per order, it protects app channel retention; volume growth is required to translate this into EBITDA expansion.

### One-line takeaway
Jubilant FoodWorks is leveraging robust Popeyes momentum and supply chain efficiencies to offset LPG and wage inflation while targeting 5%–7% LFL growth and margin expansion.
