# Jyothy Labs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Underlying business growth remains healthy (12.4% value growth ex-Pril/Fa), but profitability is severely impacted by unprecedented raw material inflation and margin compression.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 760 crore (+3.3% YoY).
*   **Revenue (ex-Pril/Fa):** +12.4% value growth, +8.1% volume growth.
*   **Gross Margin:** 34.8% (vs 48.1% YoY, 38.5% QoQ).
*   **Operating EBITDA Margin:** 2.5% (vs 16.1% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 19 crore.
*   **PAT:** Rs 10.6 crore.
*   **H1 Revenue:** Rs 1,533 crore (+3.1% reported).
*   **H1 Gross Margin:** 36.7% (vs 48% YoY).
*   **H1 EBITDA Margin:** 5.5% (vs 16.3% YoY).

### Highlights
*   Pril and Fa brand license expired in May 2026; exit impacted reported volumes.
*   Fabric Care grew 10.4% in value; liquid detergents grew >50% YoY.
*   Dishwash (Exo) grew 23% YoY.
*   Personal Care grew >11% in value.
*   Board approved a share buyback of Rs 147 crore at Rs 215 per share (promoters not participating).
*   *Note:* Management clarified that M&A is not on hold; they are actively evaluating opportunities that meet their criteria.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects underlying business to sustain healthy growth in Q3, led by Fabric Care and new products.
*   Margin recovery is expected to be progressive; full recovery in H2 cannot be assumed unless commodity prices stabilize.
*   Internal aspiration is to maintain double-digit growth despite volatility.
*   Effective tax rate expected to be 25-26% for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Rural markets outperforming urban; urban showing gradual recovery.
*   Modern trade, e-commerce, and quick commerce maintained strong growth; general trade remained subdued.
*   FMCG sector facing challenges from geopolitical uncertainty, elevated commodity prices, and deficient monsoon.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Gross margin compressed to 34.8% due to "unprecedented" inflation in crude derivatives, LABSA, SLES, soap noodles, and packaging.
*   Crude-linked inputs constitute 60-65% of the consumption basket.
*   Packaging costs account for 18-20% of the cost base; HDPE/polypropylene prices up 55-60%.
*   Took ~4% price increase in Q2; evaluating further selective pricing and grammage adjustments.
*   Employee costs ~12% of revenue (+5.7% YoY).
*   A&P expenses 7.4% of revenue (+100 bps QoQ).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Focus on scaling newer formats (e.g., Maxo Agarbatti).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net working capital increased to 23 days (vs 15 days in March) due to higher inventory value.
*   Expect working capital to return to 18-20 days.
*   Cash position: ~Rs 846 crore; Debt: 0.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Severe margin pressure due to high raw material costs (crude derivatives).
*   Deficient monsoon raising concerns about rural purchasing power.
*   Renewed inflationary pressure observed in October.
*   *Note:* The company is facing significantly higher margin pressure than in previous inflationary cycles (FY22/23), breaking the pattern of historical margin resilience.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Rushabh Shah (BugleRock):* Asked about margin recovery steps. Management cited NPDs, cost efficiencies, and product mix management.
*   *Rushabh Shah (BugleRock):* Questioned if Exo can replace Pril. Management expressed confidence in Exo's brand equity.
*   *Amit Purohit (Elara):* Asked why price hikes were only 4% vs. 13-14% inflation. Management stated they avoided indiscriminate hikes to protect consumer affordability and market share.
*   *Vishal Gutka (ASK):* Asked about the Rs 5,000 crore revenue target given M&A "on hold" rumors. Management clarified M&A is not on hold; they are waiting for the right opportunity.
*   *Navin (ithoughtpms):* Asked about crude dependency. Management noted they are developing products with lower crude dependency for the future.

### One-line takeaway
Jyothy Labs is navigating a severe margin squeeze caused by unprecedented crude-linked inflation, prioritizing long-term market share and consumer affordability over immediate price hikes, while maintaining a strong, debt-free balance sheet.
