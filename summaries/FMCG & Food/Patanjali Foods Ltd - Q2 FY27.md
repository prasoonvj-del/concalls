# Patanjali Foods Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited margin compression in edible oils due to custom duty cuts, rising CPI inflation, and muted rural demand.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained broad execution trajectory (HPC growth target ~15%), though no specific revenue/EBITDA numerical guidance updates were issued.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more cautious due to rising headline CPI inflation (6.09% in October), a 13% monsoon deficit, and rural demand remaining muted.
- New topics or risks: Government reduction of effective import duty on CPO and soya oil (to 11%) and sunflower oil (to 5.5%) on Sep 24; standardization of edible oil pack sizes; entry into the namkeen category. Old risks like El Niño and commodity inflation persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Edible oil margins compressed to 3% (from 5.22% in Q1) following duty cuts; staples volume was consciously scaled back to protect margins as promised.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 11,323 crore (+16% YoY)
- Operating EBITDA: Rs 426 crore (Margin: 3.76%)
- PBT: Rs 409 crore (Margin: 3.59%)
- H1 Revenue from operations: Rs 22,660 crore (+22% YoY)
- H1 Operating EBITDA: Rs 969 crore (Margin: 4.28%)
- Edible Oil Revenue: Rs 8,532 crore (+22% YoY, Margin: 3%)
- FMCG Revenue: Rs 2,923 crore (EBITDA: Rs 221 crore, Margin: 7.57%)
- Biscuit Revenue: Rs 638 crore (+28% YoY, Margin: 11.91%)
- Staples Revenue: Rs 839 crore (EBITDA: Rs 15.61 crore)
- HPC Revenue: Rs 714 crore (+11% YoY; Dental care Rs 378 crore, Skincare Rs 188 crore, Home care Rs 91 crore, Hair care Rs 55 crore)

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever H1 revenue of Rs 22,660 crore.
- Entered the namkeen category with 10 products across Rs 10, 200g, and 400g packs.
- Oil palm cultivated area reached 127,265 hectares across 6.63 lakh allocated hectares.
- Government reduced effective import duty on crude palm oil and crude soybean oil to 11% (from 16.5%) and sunflower oil to 5.5% (from 16.5%) on September 24.
- Transferred lease rights for the Indore (Mangliya) land/plant from Patanjali Ayurved for Rs 185 crore to relocate the factory away from residential areas.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects commodity inflation to continue in the near term.
- Management expects HPC to close the year at around 15% growth.
- Management expects winter and wedding seasons to support demand for ghee and other categories.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Urban markets remained relatively resilient while rural demand was relatively muted.
- CPI inflation rose steadily through the quarter to 6.09% in October, with food inflation at 8.94%.
- Monsoon ended with a 13% rainfall deficit, the weakest since 2015.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Edible oil EBITDA margin dropped to 3% from 5.22% in Q1 due to duty cuts and immediate pass-through to consumers.
- Input costs rose across wheat, sugar, milk procurement, soybean, crude-linked inputs, freight, and logistics.
- Staples volume was consciously curtailed to protect margins against commodity inflation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Oil palm cultivated area at 127,265 hectares (34% in prime yielding phase 7-25 years, 8% young phase, 57% immature up to 3 years).
- Submitted expression of interest for oil palm development in Bihar under the National Mission on Edible Oils.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Paid Rs 185 crore for lease rights transfer of Indore land from Patanjali Ayurved.
- Received final past-period tax refund installment from the tax department; no further major past refunds expected.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rising CPI inflation and food inflation (8.94% in October).
- 13% monsoon deficit and El Niño impact on Kharif harvest and Rabi sowing.
- Input cost pressures across key raw materials and freight.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ashutosh (ICICI Securities) on toothpastes/biscuits competition:** Management stated oral care grew 16% QoQ to Rs 378 crore, maintaining confidence despite heightened competitive intensity.
- **Ashutosh (ICICI Securities) on inorganic expansion:** Management stated they selectively evaluate D2C/smaller company opportunities, but valuation mismatches have kept deals on hold.
- **Abhishek Mathur (Systematix) on volume vs realization:** Management stated edible oil volume grew ~3.9% with balance from price increases; biscuits volume grew ~12.53%.
- **Abhishek Mathur (Systematix) on staples de-growth:** Management stated it was a conscious call to avoid pushing loss-making volume amid commodity inflation.
- **Dhiraj Mistry on duty cut inventory losses:** Management stated impact was minimal due to daily tracking and float/bonded warehouse positioning, though EBITDA margin compressed.

### One-line takeaway
Patanjali Foods reported 16% revenue growth in Q2 FY27, but edible oil margins compressed due to duty cuts, while management consciously reined in staples volume amid rising inflation.
