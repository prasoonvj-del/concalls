# Modi Naturals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted solid overall performance, expansion of ethanol capacity to 600 KLPD, securing sizeable OMC orders, and expected easing of input cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 204.9 crore (+38.9% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 18.6 crore (+23.2% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 9.1%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 11 crore (+9.4% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 361.9 crore (+19.3% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 40.8 crore (+25.1% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 11.3%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 23.5 crore (+14.4% YoY)
- **H1 Segment Revenue & EBITDA:**
  - Consumer Division: Revenue Rs 87.4 crore, EBITDA Rs 4.9 crore
  - Bulk Division: Revenue Rs 80.8 crore, EBITDA Rs 0.7 crore
  - Ethanol Division: Revenue Rs 193.7 crore, EBITDA Rs 34.9 crore
- **H1 FY27 Return Ratios:** ROCE 19.7%, ROE 24.7%
- **Balance Sheet Metrics:** Debt to equity 0.85x; Net working capital cycle days as on 30th September: 60 days
- **Ethanol Orders & Capacity:** Capacity expanded from 282 KLPD to 600 KLPD; secured orders worth approx Rs 494 crores (7.8 crore liters) from various OMCs for the ESY starting November 1, 2026.
- **FY27 Guidance Range:** Revenue guidance of Rs 925 crores to Rs 965 crores.

### Highlights
- Extended association with Karisma Kapoor as brand ambassador for Oleev for another year.
- Ethanol capacity successfully ramped up to 600 KLPD, with full operational status achieved around August.
- Byproducts contribute 18% to 20% revenue to the ethanol division.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of achieving FY27 revenue guidance of Rs 925 crores to Rs 965 crores, driven by consumer division growth, progressive ramp-up of expanded ethanol capacity, and a favorable input cost environment in H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- FMCG sector shows a gradual recovery in demand with a preference for quality, convenience, and value.
- Ethanol industry moving beyond E20 with regulatory permits for E85 and E100; feedstock landscape emphasizing grain-based sources like maize and rice.
- Bulk division realizations expected to moderate following recent commodity price corrections.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Sharp increase in input costs (edible oil, grain prices) outpaced selling price adjustments in Q2, causing margin pressure.
- Signs of normalization in input prices observed exiting Q2 into Q3.
- Long-term sustainable EBITDA margin profiles: Ethanol at 12% to 15%, Consumer at 8% to 9%, Bulk at 5% to 8%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ethanol manufacturing capacity expanded from 282 KLPD to 600 KLPD. Total capex for the ethanol expansion stated to be roughly Rs 300 crores.
- New product launches planned over the next 2 to 3 months across snacking, grocery, and high-protein categories under Oleev sub-brands and ready-to-eat popcorn under PIPO.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt to equity stands at 0.85x. Net working capital cycle at 60 days as of 30th September. Short-term borrowings noted by an analyst to have come down sharply.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated input costs across commodities and packaging put pressure on margins during Q2.
- Edible oil and grain price volatility impacting operating margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankit Kanodia (Zen Nivesh Advisors) on Consumer Segment Stagnation:** Asked why consumer sales have stagnated between Rs 150-180 crores over the past five years. *Management answered* that olive oil price declines impacted the top line, but food categories are growing well and will reflect in future quarters.
2. **Ankit Kanodia on Ethanol Order Execution:** Inquired if there was any slippage in ethanol orders. *Management answered* that there are no order slippages; expanded capacities became fully operational around August, so full-quarter revenue impacts will show in Q3.
3. **Praveen Sharma (Individual Investor) on Guidance vs. Margin Pressures:** Questioned if margin pressures from lower OMCs maize ethanol prices and higher mandi raw material costs would threaten guidance. *Management answered* that a higher proportion of FCI rice ethanol sets fixed price/margin structures, ensuring confidence in guidance.
4. **Hetvi Sanghvi on Sustainable Margins:** Asked for sustainable margin targets across verticals. *Management answered* 12%-15% for Ethanol, 8%-9% for Consumer, and 5%-8% for Bulk.
5. **Rohit Mehra (SK Securities) on Ethanol Capex and Byproduct Contribution:** Asked about total capex and byproduct revenue share. *Management answered* total capex is roughly Rs 300 crores, and byproducts contribute 18%-20% to ethanol revenue.

### One-line takeaway
Modi Naturals delivered double-digit YoY revenue and PAT growth in H1 FY27, backed by its ethanol capacity expansion to 600 KLPD and strong order inflows, offsetting short-term raw material inflation pressures in its consumer and bulk segments.
