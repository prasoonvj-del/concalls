# Patanjali Foods Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-24 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly revenues and strong growth across edible oils and FMCG, while reaffirming full-year guidance despite caution regarding rural demand and staples.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 11,337 crore (+29% YoY).
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 543 crore (Margin: 4.80%).
*   **PBT:** Rs 453 crore (Margin: 4%).
*   **Edible Oil Revenue:** Rs 8,505 crore (Margin: 5.22%).
*   **Oil Palm Plantation Revenue:** Rs 740 crore (+25% YoY).
*   **FMCG Revenue:** Rs 2,938 crore (EBITDA: Rs 190 crore, Margin: 6.45%).
*   **Biscuits Revenue:** Rs 560 crore (+27% YoY, Margin: 15.35%).
*   **Textured Soya Revenue:** Rs 160 crore (+14% YoY, +50% QoQ, Margin: >18%).
*   **HPC Revenue:** Rs 629 crore (EBITDA: Rs 122 crore).
*   **Staples EBITDA:** Negative Rs 59 crore.
*   **Ethnic Foods EBITDA:** Rs 9 crore.

### Highlights
*   Fourth consecutive quarter of highest-ever quarterly revenues.
*   Edible oil growth led primarily by mustard oil.
*   HPC segment performance: Dental care (Rs 325 crore), Skin care (Rs 165 crore), Home care (Rs 83 crore).
*   New product launches: Rose Kanti soap, Dant Kanti Sensitive toothpaste, Super Dishwash liquid, Sweet Lime pickle, Almond and Chyawanprash cookies.
*   Distribution: E-commerce and quick commerce channels growing at 25% YoY (currently 15% of total revenue).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management reaffirmed full-year guidance: Edible oil margin 3-5% with 3-5% volume growth; Food & FMCG 8-10% growth; Beauty & Personal Care ~15% growth; FMCG vertical EBITDA growth 12-15%.
*   Management expects to cross Rs 12,500 crore in FMCG revenue for the year.
*   Management aims to increase revenue contribution from modern trade/e-commerce/quick commerce to 20% within 18 months.
*   Management targets ~Rs 2,500 crore annualized EBITDA in the next 18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted rural stress-related pressure in the staples category for the current quarter.
*   Management expects food inflation due to El Nino and geopolitical factors, but believes government grain inventories and policy interventions will mitigate impact.
*   Management remains bullish on commodity pricing for top-line growth but cautious on margin/demand side.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Commodity price inflation (veg oil) was a net positive due to long positions in edible oils.
*   Input cost pressure noted in FMCG, packaging, freight, and logistics.
*   Management used calibrated price increases in edible oils and targeted discounts in FMCG.
*   Physical-oriented hedging strategy used alongside import purchases to improve margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Oil palm cultivated area: 1,15,861 hectares (37% in prime yielding phase of 7 to 25 years).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   El Nino impact on production and availability.
*   Potential demand contraction in rural markets.
*   Geopolitical tensions affecting export-oriented markets (Middle East).
*   Input cost pressures in Home and Personal Care.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama):** Asked about dishwash competition and biscuit pricing. Management stated they have no intention of exiting dishwash and are committed to Rs 5/Rs 10 price points in biscuits.
*   **Abhishek Mathur (Systematix):** Asked about food segment recovery. Management attributed it to price inflation (12%) and volume growth (5%).
*   **Keshav Harlalka (BHH Securities):** Questioned the valuation of the HPC acquisition. Management clarified it was a slump sale with no earnings multiple, effectively "gifted" by the parent, and already profitable.

### One-line takeaway
Patanjali Foods delivered record Q1 revenue growth led by edible oils and FMCG, while management maintains a cautious outlook on rural demand and staples despite reaffirming full-year growth guidance.
