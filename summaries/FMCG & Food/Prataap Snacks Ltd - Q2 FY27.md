# Prataap Snacks Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong top-line growth crossing Rs. 500 crore, but gross and operating margins compressed due to severe input cost inflation.

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs. 541 crore (up 26% YoY, 10% QoQ)
- H1 FY27 revenue from operations: Rs. 1,031 crore (up 23% YoY)
- Gross margin: 25.5% (vs. 29.8% in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA: Rs. 12.9 crore (vs. Rs. 22.9 crore in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 2.4%
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs. 32 crore
- Transaction/volume growth (number of packets): 17% YoY in Q2
- Pricing growth: 8% to 9% YoY in Q2
- Quick commerce contribution: 1.2% to 1.5% of overall sales; target of ~5% over the next 3 years
- Distribution reach: Available at >2.5 million outlets; ~2,200 direct distributors and ~3,000 sub-distributors (total reach ~5,500)
- Category mix: Namkeen ~25% (up from 20% last year), Pellets ~19%-20%, Rings ~10%, Extruded category total ~39%
- MRP 5/- mix: Reduced from 85% last year to 82%; MRP 10-20 packs increased to 14.5%
- Greenfield project cost: Rs. 421-422 crore (funded by internal accruals and ~Rs. 250 crore term loan at <8% interest)
- Existing land monetization value estimate: Rs. 250 crore to Rs. 300 crore

### Highlights
- Quarterly revenue crossed Rs. 500 crore for the first time, reaching Rs. 541 crore.
- Growth is broad-based, led by Namkeen and Potato Chips.
- Acquired RLOP Foods during the quarter, securing ~50 acres of leasehold land near Indore for a new greenfield integrated manufacturing facility.
- Planned a greenfield facility near Indore to consolidate 7 smaller stand-alone units within the current Indore campus, expected to start commercial production in ~15 months (target Jan 1, 2028).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to deliver strong double-digit revenue growth for the full year FY27.
- Targeting medium-term margins of 9%-10% EBITDA through structural levers over 2-3 years.
- Second-half margins are targeted to be similar to H1 levels (~3% EBITDA).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Inflationary cost environment has led local/unorganized players to struggle with palm oil and laminate supplies, resulting in a reverse shift toward packaged players and market share gains for Prataap Snacks.
- Prataap Snacks' pricing remains slightly higher than local players (e.g., Rs. 4.15 vs. Rs. 3.75 for a Rs. 5 product).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input costs saw significant inflation, specifically palm oil, potatoes, packaging laminates, corrugated boxes, fuel, logistics, and gas prices.
- Palm oil and packaging laminate prices peaked post the US-Iran war, leading to an immediate ~8 percentage point negative impact on gross margins compared to Q4, plus ~1.5% impact from energy and logistics.
- Mitigated ~7.5 percentage points of the impact through price realizations, grammage rationalization, and optimizing channel margins/trade schemes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Proposed greenfield plant near Indore: 50-acre leasehold land from state government, capacity of ~65,000 to 70,000 metric tons.
- Project cost of Rs. 421-422 crore; debt funding of ~Rs. 250 crore, remainder from internal accruals.
- Existing Indore land (14 acres) to be monetized (estimated value Rs. 250-300 crore) to prepay the term loan.
- New facility will allow doubling capacity without basic infra investments and aims to improve operating profit by 1.5% to 2% over 1.5 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term borrowings include Rs. 25 crore OD and Rs. 4 crore installment for the Jammu plant loan.
- Term loan drawdown for the new project will mainly occur next year; current interest rate is <8%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated commodity prices (palm oil, packaging laminates, corrugated boxes) and energy/logistics costs putting pressure on gross and operating margins.
- Inefficiencies from multiple stand-alone units operating within the current Indore campus.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Bhavik Shah (Invexa Capital) asked about gross margin sustainability and pricing power.** Management explained that short-term gross margins were hit by a sudden 8% RM spike, largely mitigated via price hikes and grammage cuts, while structural margin gains (1.5%-2%) will come from consolidating the Indore manufacturing units.
2. **Shirish Pardeshi (Oswal) asked about volume vs. value growth and the drivers.** Management stated volume (transaction/packet) growth was 17% in Q2, driven by broad-based growth in Namkeen and Potato Chips and technology-backed distribution monitoring.
3. **Abhishek Mathur (Systematix) asked about industry structure shifts during inflation.** Management noted a reverse shift from loose/local players to packaged players because local players cannot manage palm oil/laminate supplies, aiding market share gains.
4. **Gaurang Kakkad (Invexa Capital) asked about the sustainability of Q2 margins in H2.** Management confirmed they target H2 margins similar to H1 levels (~3% EBITDA) via continued grammage and channel margin adjustments.

### One-line takeaway
Prataap Snacks delivered record Q2 revenue above Rs. 500 crore (up 26% YoY) driven by volume growth and distribution expansion, but profitability remained severely compressed due to peak raw material inflation, prompting plans for a consolidated greenfield facility and structural cost levers.
