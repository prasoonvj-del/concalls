# Bikaji Foods International Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · FMCG & Food*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management noted an encouraging quarter with top-line growth close to 13% and robust snacking demand, though margins were impacted by raw material inflation and a shift in the festive season.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained overall full-year revenue growth target at high teens (specifically 17% plus at YTD level); operating margin guidance adjusted with Q3 under pressure and Q4 expected to normalize at 13%+ EBITDA.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from recovery post-operational disruptions (Q1) to strong momentum and robust demand across general and modern trade, though Q2 sweets/gifting was impacted by Diwali shifting to Q3.
- New topics or risks: Input cost pressures in pulses and bad crop leading to a 1.7% gross margin hit on a standalone level; announcement of a 3% price increase post-Diwali (from November 15th); expansion of store models for THF (including smaller 700-800 sq ft formats and experiential 'Foundré' store in Gurgaon).
- Last quarter's promises: Added 20,000 outlets in Q2 (continuing distribution expansion); maintained A&P spend target at ~2% of sales.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth: close to 13% YoY (Q2)
- Snacking business value growth: 20%+ (specifically 22%) YoY
- Snacking volume growth: 15%+ YoY (overall volume growth: 5%)
- Ethnic Snacks growth: 19.7% YoY
- Western Snacks growth: 44% YoY
- Core market snacking growth: 17.5% (overall core market growth); Focus market snacking growth: 35%+ (focus market performance grew at 7.6% overall)
- Standalone gross margin: 34.4%
- Consolidated EBITDA margin: 12.6%
- Retail business (THF) growth: 58% in Q2, 64% overall (yearly level); store count at 34, targeting 40 by end of year
- Outlets added: ~20,000 in Q2
- Full year revenue growth guidance (YTD level): 17%+ (high teens)

### Highlights
- Festival shift: Diwali shifted from Q2 (last year) to Q3 (this year), impacting packaged sweets and gifting dispatches in Q2; management advised looking at Q2 and Q3 combined.
- Marketing campaigns: Partnered with Paytm for a cashback campaign across 33 SKUs (INR 5 and INR 10 OTG packs) running till March; launched 11 new films featuring Amitabh Bachchan and extended campaigns with Pankaj Tripathi.
- Bikanerwala (THF): Opening 5-6 more stores in the next six months to reach 40 by year-end; full year revenue expected at INR 160 crore - INR 170 crore (60-70% growth); testing smaller 600-700 sq ft formats and opening a premium experiential store 'Foundré' in Gurgaon.
- Bikaneri Bhujia risk mitigation: Mother depot operational to manage inventory against potential disruptions; management confirmed manufacturing outside Bikaner cannot carry the 'Bikaneri Bhujia' GI tag.

### Guidance & outlook
- Full year revenue growth guidance: 17%+ at YTD level (high teens).
- Full year EBITDA margin: Expected to exit Q4 at 13%+ run rate; Q3 expected to be challenging due to delayed price hikes, with Q4 normalizing.
- Full year A&P spend target: Maintained at ~2% of sales.
- THF growth outlook: Expected to grow at 50%-60% YoY for the next 3-4 years, targeting 31-33 stores by year-end.
- Raw material outlook: No further price increases required till March end as edible oil, crude/diesel, and packing material prices soften and fresh crop arrives.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Snacking momentum remains strong with volume growth led by demand.
- Impulse packs (INR 5 and INR 10) grew by 20%, driven by Paytm offers and on-the-go consumption.
- Demand across general trade, modern trade, and all channels reported as good.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin hit: Standalone gross margin impacted by 1.7% due to multiple price increases in key raw materials (bad crop leading to price increases in all pulses) from March to April.
- Price action: MRP increases and price corrections of ~3% taking effect from November 15th post-Diwali.
- Cost trends: Edible oil prices, crude/diesel, and packing materials softening by 3%-4% in the last two weeks.
- Other expenses: Increased due to higher manufacturing, fuel, logistics costs, and a 0.7% increase in ad cost YoY.
- Small pack grammage: Grammage cut of ~3%-4% on a weighted average for INR 5 and INR 10 packs.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- THF retail store count to reach 40 by the end of the year (currently at 34).
- Experimenting with smaller 700-800 sq ft store formats for malls alongside the standard 2,000-2,500 sq ft outlets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *Not discussed in detail.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cost pressures from raw material inflation (pulses, sugar, edible oils) impacting gross margins.
- Single-location constraints for Bikaneri Bhujia (production outside Bikaner cannot use the GI tag).
- Temporary margin pressure in Q3 due to delayed price pass-throughs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Binay Shukla (PhillipCapital India):** Asked about focus market growth volatility and sweets contribution. *Management explained focus market growth was impacted solely by the sweets/gifting shift to Q3, with sweets contributing ~25% in focus states, and guided for high teen overall growth.*
2. **Abneesh Roy (Nuvama):** Inquired about out-of-home consumption impact and competitive price hikes. *Management stated single-consumption packs (INR 5/10) grew faster with no heat felt, and explained that festive packing timelines prevented sharp pre-festival price hikes, which are scheduled post-Diwali.*
3. **Shirish Pardeshi (IIFL Securities):** Asked about inflation, pulse price impact, and THF store economics. *Management stated the 3% price hike plus softening edible oils cover costs till March, and reported 6-8% same-store sales growth for THF while outlining smaller store formats and the new 'Foundré' experiential store.*
4. **Abhishek Mathur:** Asked about Bikaneri Bhujia alternative manufacturing locations and GI tag implications, as well as other expenses. *Management confirmed alternative manufacturing cannot use the 'Bikaneri Bhujia' label, and attributed other expense growth to higher manufacturing, fuel, logistics, and a 0.7% increase in ad spends.*
5. **Nitin:** Inquired about Q3 margin resilience given higher sweets contribution and details on the Paytm cashback arrangement. *Management noted that Q3 margins face early cost pressures before price hikes take effect, and clarified that Paytm absorbs redemption costs with no direct cash investment from Bikaji.*

### One-line takeaway
Bikaji delivered robust Q2 snacking growth of 22% and expects high teen revenue growth for the full year, absorbing a temporary gross margin hit from pulse inflation with a 3% price hike post-Diwali.
