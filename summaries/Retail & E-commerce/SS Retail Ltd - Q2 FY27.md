# SS Retail Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT with strong YoY growth.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 940 crore (up 87% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 1,730 crore (up 73% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Gross Margin:** 12.7% (up 71 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Gross Margin:** 12.7% (up 61 bps YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 52.4 crore (up 99% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 5.6% (up 35 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 97.3 crore (up 73% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 28 crore (up 160% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 51.7 crore (up 102% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Same-Store Sales Growth (SSG):** 46% (H1 FY27 SSG at 38%)
- **Store Network:** 574 stores across 228 cities and 5 states (added 38 stores in Q2 FY27), spanning ~2.8 lakh sq ft.
- **Average Selling Price (ASP):** INR 34,050 in H1 FY27 (up ~33% YoY from INR 25,513 in H1 FY26).
- **Pre-owned Revenue Contribution:** 7.3% in Q2 FY27 (vs 6.5% in Q2 FY26); Mobile Exchange Wala present across 108 stores.

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA, and PAT in Q2 FY27.
- Added 38 stores during Q2 FY27, taking total network to 574 stores.
- Pre-owned business (Mobile Exchange Wala) expanded to 108 stores (targeting ~30% network penetration by FY29).
- Geographic diversification: Non-Maharashtra revenue contribution increased to 8% in Q2 FY27 from 6% in Q2 FY26.
- Operating format mix in Q2 revenue: COFO at 73%, FOFO at 16%, and FOCO at 11%.
- Product mix in Q2 revenue: Mobile phones at 86%, pre-owned devices at 7.3%, accessories & protection at 5%, and TVs & laptops at 1.7%.
- Note: Continues the rapid store expansion and capital-efficient franchise model outlined in previous records.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated the target of reaching 750 stores by FY28 and doubling revenue over the next two years from the FY26 base.
- Management guided for an SSG of around 35% for the full year FY27, moving to double-digit SSG in subsequent years as ASP stabilizes.
- Management expects gross margins to expand towards 13%+ over the next 18-24 months supported by direct OEM sourcing.
- Management expects progressive EBITDA and PAT margin expansion driven by store maturity and operational leverage.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry experiencing structural transformation with value expansion driven by premiumization and ASP increases (~20% rise), leading to a ~15% drop in volumes in the below-INR 15k category.
- Festive season demand was encouraging, supported by premium smartphone upgrades, consumer financing (supporting nearly 70% of sales), and improving consumer aspirations.
- Repeat buyers accounted for over 60% of customers.
- Management noted that e-commerce electronic sales are in continuous decline due to consumers preferring look-and-feel, physical store experience, and financing options.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded by 71 bps YoY to 12.7% in Q2 FY27, driven by a 50/50 split between better OEM margins and lower discounting.
- Other expenses increased due to higher DBD (finance) charges and partner commission payouts/incentives linked to strong growth.
- Debt-to-equity reduced from 0.9x to 0.4x.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 38 stores in Q2 FY27 (total 574 stores).
- Unit capex requirement is approximately INR 15 lakh per store. Total capex for FY27 is expected around INR 20 crore (± INR 2 crore) and similar for FY28.
- Approximately 50% of new store additions in FY27-FY28 will be in Maharashtra and 50% outside Maharashtra.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased to INR 583 crore in H1 FY27 (54 days) due to festive stocking; management expects inventory days to normalize below 50 days by the end of Q3.
- Interest costs expected to decrease further as IPO proceeds hit working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Apple demand showed some muted volume growth post Q3 due to price hikes and launch delays, though the Pro series saw strong demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Aman Saifee (Stallion Asset) on SSG assumptions and pre-owned inventory:** Management clarified that 35% SSG guidance for the year is largely ASP-driven (35% value, 3% volume), and pre-owned inventory runs at ~40 days (8-10 days faster than new mobiles).
- **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay Global) on non-Maharashtra traction and gross margins:** Management stated that new states (MP, Karnataka, Gujarat, Goa, and newly entered Chhattisgarh) show strong traction, and direct OEM sourcing over the next 12-18 months will further expand gross margins.
- **Utkarsh (Systematix Group) on inventory and operating leverage:** Management explained inventory rose due to festive stocking (expecting reduction below 50 days by Q3 end) and noted that H1 expenses rose due to partner commissions and DBD charges, with operating leverage expected to play out in H2.
- **Deepak on gross margin drivers and 6% EBITDA sustainability:** Management detailed that the 70 bps gross margin expansion was split 50/50 between OEM margin improvements and lower discounting, and that store maturity will drive EBITDA expansion to offset any future normalization in discounting.

### One-line takeaway
SS Retail reported record Q2 performance with an 87% YoY revenue jump and robust store additions, reaffirming its guidance to double revenue by FY28.
