# Purple Style Labs Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects continued revenue growth, improving gross profit margins, and narrowing cash outflows, backed by long-term industry tailwinds.

### Key numbers
- **GMV:** INR 397 crore for H1 FY27 (compared to INR 310 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 262 crore for H1 FY27 (compared to INR 214 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Gross Profit:** INR 103 crore for H1 FY27 (compared to INR 83 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 39% for H1 FY27 (compared to 38% in H1 FY26).
- **EBITDA:** Loss of approximately INR 7 crore for H1 FY27 (compared to a loss of INR 5 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Improved to INR 84,000 in H1 FY27 from INR 69,000 in H1 FY26; US store AOV north of $1,500.
- **Inventory:** INR 196 crore as of September 2026 (compared to INR 208 crore in September 2025).
- **Net Working Capital:** Reduced to INR 59 crore as of September 2026 (compared to INR 84 crore in September 2025).
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Outflow reduced to INR 13 crore in H1 FY27 (compared to an outflow of INR 50 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Free Cash Flow:** Improved to INR 19 crore in H1 FY27 (compared to INR 72 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Geographical GMV Contributions (H1 FY27):** Mumbai contributed INR 105 crore, Delhi contributed INR 85 crore, and USA contributed INR 64 crore (vs INR 42 crore in H1 FY26).

### Highlights
- Active designer base of 1,100+ and 2 lakh+ products listed online; operates 14 stores across 11 cities (including international stores in London and New York).
- Post-FY25, management consciously trimmed the long tail of designers and customers, sharpening focus purely on luxury and opening large-format stores in Mumbai, Delhi, and New York.
- Real jewelry has been introduced in flagship stores as an upselling category, contributing to higher AOV with minimal inventory footprint.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains its long-term GMV target of INR 2,100 crore by FY30 (with >8% market share) and INR 20,000 crore GMV by 2040 (with 18% market share).
- Expects gross margins for FY27 to be higher than FY26.
- Management anticipates better cash flows in H2 due to seasonally higher revenues and lower interest costs.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Luxury consumption in India is estimated to touch INR 7.6 lakh crore by 2040 (~USD 80 billion) at ~13% CAGR.
- Luxury wedding and occasion wear industry is expected to reach INR 1.35 lakh crore by 2040.
- Top 1,000 Indian designer wear market is currently over INR 10,000 crore and expected to cross INR 1 lakh crore by 2040, with the consumer base expanding to 1.5 million.
- Wedding shopping faced a slight delay (Diwali minus 3 months), but management expects delayed performance to reflect positively in Q3 and Q4.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Fixed cost structures remain largely flat over Q1 and Q2. Annual cash-based expenses are in the range of INR 360 crore to INR 370 crore.
- Depreciation of ROU assets for H1 stood at higher levels, with total cash rent spend around INR 120-150 crore annually (including US upfront lease adjustments).
- Operating leverage is expected to trigger once gross profit crosses the fixed cash expense threshold.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total projected capex and deposit investment for upcoming FY30 expansion projects (Delhi 100K sq. ft. store, store upgrades in Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Bangalore, and London) is estimated at INR 75 crore to INR 85 crore over the next 18 months.
- Store footprint additions for the FY30 plan include a new 100K sq. ft. Gen 4 store in Delhi, upgrading existing Gen 2 stores in Ahmedabad, Kolkata, and Bangalore to Gen 3/large-format stores, and upgrading the London store from Gen 1 to Gen 3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Capital-light inventory model driven significantly by backorders where customers pay 100% upfront and designers are paid post-delivery.
- Interest cost combined for H1 was INR 40 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Minor potential headwinds mentioned from geopolitical issues or travel trends affecting destination weddings, though mitigated by international store presence (e.g., New York).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sidhaant Lodaya (Sanchi Fund):** Asked about mature store EBITDA and revenue conversion from GMV. *Management answered that profitability is a function of absolute gross profit crossing fixed cash expenses rather than fixed store-level percentages, and suggested an 80% (+/- 3%) annual conversion rate from GMV to revenue.*
2. **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay Global):** Inquired about total capex for the FY30 roadmap and H2 cash flow expectations. *Management guided to INR 75-80 crore of incremental capex/deposits over 18 months and stated that H2 cash flows are expected to be stronger based on historical seasonality and reduced operational losses.*
3. **Saurabh Ginodia (SMIFS):** Asked about working capital intensity and gross profit margin trends. *Management reiterated that backorders keep net working capital low (INR 60 crore vs INR 196 crore inventory) and expects gross margins to improve in FY27 over FY26.*
4. **Nirav Rajiv (Aditya Birla Sun Life):** Queried about festive/wedding season trends in Mumbai stores and the US store ramp-up. *Management noted that Mumbai is tracking towards a INR 250 crore+ full-year GMV run rate and that New York is seeing strong growth following its initial learning curve.*
5. **Rohit Mehra (SK Securities):** Asked about the bifurcation of US store vs. online sales and store-level AOV. *Management stated they view the US market holistically as an omnichannel business, noted a store AOV north of $1,500 (approx. 3x online AOV), and mentioned a decision on the short-term New York lease will be taken by the Q3 call.*
6. **Shantanu Basu (SMIFS):** Asked about real jewelry traction and London store plans. *Management stated real jewelry base is currently too small for separate line-item reporting but helps drive AOV, and confirmed the London store upgrade aligns with the INR 80-85 crore multi-project capex plan.*

### One-line takeaway
Purple Service Labs reported steady H1 FY27 GMV growth of INR 397 crore and improved gross margins to 39%, while keeping net working capital lean as it progresses toward its long-term FY30 GMV target of INR 2,100 crore.
