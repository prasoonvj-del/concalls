# Retail & E-commerce - Restaurants & QSR: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Management commentary across the sector highlights a generally positive tone characterized by robust revenue growth, transaction-led Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG), and volume expansion, alongside persistent macro headwinds. Companies reported strong execution on value platforms and digital engagement (apps, kiosks), though margins faced pressure from food and utility inflation as well as state-level minimum wage hikes.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements broadly observed a price-sensitive consumer base navigating a tight discretionary spending environment. Value platforms (such as INR 99 and INR 149 combos) were widely deployed to capture traffic. 
*   **Divergence:** While companies like Westlife Foodworld, United Foodbrands, Restaurant Brands Asia, and Sapphire Foods reported strong traffic-led SSSG (ranging from 10% to 24.5%), Jubilant FoodWorks maintained an LFL target of 5%–7%, and Devyani International noted a bifurcated "K-shaped" economy with localized pressure on discretionary spending. Travel Food Services faced a unique passenger traffic decline (-4.8% YoY) due to the West Asia crisis.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Inflation Pressures:** Steep inflation in raw materials (poultry, dairy, edible oils, and cheese), energy/LPG prices (nearly double YoY for some), and state-level minimum wage hikes (notably in Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra, and Telangana, with Karnataka up 26%) were reported across the board. Westlife Foodworld absorbed ~190 bps of inflation, while Jubilant FoodWorks took a 200 bps hit.
*   **Mitigation Strategies:** Companies relied on staggered, calibrated price hikes (typically 1% to 4%), discount optimizations, supply chain efficiencies, cluster DC expansions, and productivity initiatives. Several players (e.g., Sapphire Foods, Speciality Restaurants) prioritized transaction volume growth over short-term gross margin percentages.

## Capex & capacity & expansion
*   **Expansion Plans:** Aggressive store additions continued. Devyani International reaffirmed guidance of 200–220 net new stores; Sapphire Foods guided for 60–80 KFC additions; Westlife Foodworld maintained 60–65 stores annually toward 580+ by March 2028; United Foodbrands targeted crossing 300 restaurants by FY27 and 500 by FY30; Speciality Restaurants targeted 200 outlets by FY2030; and Jubilant FoodWorks maintained 40–45 Popeyes additions.
*   **Format Innovations:** Companies experimented with co-located food-court setups, digital kiosk rollouts (e.g., 80% of KFC stores by Sapphire), kitchen-within-kitchen models (Speciality Restaurants), and low-capex cloud kitchen formats (Vegorama Punjabi Angithi).

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Devyani International:** Awaiting SEBI no-objection for the Sapphire Foods merger to proceed to NCLT, targeting closure by the end of the current financial year.
*   **Travel Food Services:** Shifted from "flat passenger traffic" in Q1 to a challenged Q2 with managed airport passenger traffic declining 4.8% YoY due to the West Asia crisis; receivables normalization completed.
*   **Restaurant Brands Asia:** Approved a primary capital infusion of Rs 130 crore for Indonesia, tied to milestones for asset upgrades following green shoots from the "Super Hemat" campaign.
*   **Jubilant FoodWorks:** Maintained full-year LFL guidance of 5%–7% while providing detailed breakdowns on DP Eurasia hyperinflation accounting dynamics and West Asia supply chain cost impacts.

## What to watch next quarter
*   Progress on the Devyani International and Sapphire Foods merger timeline and post-merger integration.
*   Sustained performance of value-led menu offerings (INR 99/INR 149 combos) and their impact on repeat transaction rates.
*   The trajectory of raw material inflation (poultry, cheese, edible oil) and minimum wage implementation impacts.
*   Turnaround execution and asset upgrades in international operations (such as Burger King / Popeyes Indonesia).

## Company one-liners
*   **Devyani International:** Strong operational execution and margin expansion despite macro headwinds, with a clear focus on long-term store expansion and the upcoming Sapphire Foods merger.
*   **Jubilant FoodWorks:** Defending margins through calibrated pricing and supply chain scale while expanding Popeyes and refurbishing Domino's stores to maintain its 5%–7% LFL growth trajectory.
*   **Restaurant Brands Asia:** Reported a strong Q2 FY27 with a 14% SSSG in India and a 281% jump in company-level EBITDA, while showing early turnaround green shoots in Indonesia following value-led promotions.
*   **Sapphire Foods India:** Delivered its best revenue (20%) and adjusted EBITDA (39%) growth in 16 quarters, successfully leveraging everyday value promotions to drive strong transaction-led SSSG despite persistent cost inflation.
*   **Speciality Restaurants:** Reported strong Q2 FY27 performance with revenue up 18.24% YoY and PAT up 45.75% YoY, driven by healthy SSSG (8.1%), a growing hybrid delivery mix (32%), and disciplined execution of its profitable expansion roadmap toward 200 outlets by FY2030.
*   **Travel Food Services:** Delivered robust double-digit sales and profit growth in Q2 FY27 despite a 4.8% decline in passenger traffic, driven by aggressive network expansion, disciplined execution, and a strong debt-free balance sheet.
*   **United Foodbrands:** Delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 40.9% revenue growth and 24.5% SSSG, driven entirely by volume expansion, structural improvements in unit economics, and robust captive channel engagement.
*   **Vegorama Punjabi Angithi:** Posted strong H1 FY27 results with revenue up 46.53% YoY and PAT up 62.58% YoY, supported by steady outlet expansion and upcoming automation and new brand launches.
*   **Westlife Foodworld:** Delivered its strongest performance in 13 quarters in Q2 FY27, posting 16% revenue growth and 10.1% SSSG driven by double-digit guest count increases, while expanding EBITDA margins by 130 bps despite 190 bps of inflationary headwinds.

*Based on: Devyani International Ltd, Jubilant FoodWorks Ltd, Restaurant Brands Asia Ltd, Sapphire Foods India Ltd, Speciality Restaurants Ltd, Travel Food Services Ltd, United Foodbrands Ltd, Vegorama Punjabi Angithi Ltd, Westlife Foodworld Ltd*
