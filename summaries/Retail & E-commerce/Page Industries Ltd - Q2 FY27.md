# Page Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Management reiterated double-digit volume growth guidance despite H1 volume at 5.6%, facing raw material inflation and supply fulfillment bottlenecks.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained double-digit volume growth guidance for full-year FY27 (requiring ~14% volume growth in H2 FY27) and maintained EBITDA margin band of 19%–21%.
- Tone and demand view: Consumption trends remained positive; Q2 primary billing was moderated due to the festive shift (Pujo/Diwali) moving from September to October/November, leading to deferral into Q3.
- New topics or risks: Addressed non-compliance of FEMA/FDI provisions regarding online sale of imported goods (Speedo shifted to third-party franchising); SKU-specific fulfillment dropped to 96%–97% due to demand spikes and geopolitical import delays.
- Last quarter's promises: The 3-day billing disruption from late June was fully recovered in July; the 2.5% price hike was fully effective in Q2 FY27; Disney/Marvel merchandise launched with encouraging initial response.

### Key numbers
- **Sales Value:** Rs 1,439.61 crore, up 11.5% YoY (H1: Rs 2,860.06 crore, up 9.7% YoY)
- **Sales Volume:** 59.8 million pieces, up 5.5% YoY (H1: 121.7 million pieces, up 5.6% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 300.14 crore, up 7.4% YoY; EBITDA margin at 20.8% (down 90 bps YoY from 21.7%)
- **PAT:** Rs 202.54 crore, up 4.0% YoY (H1: Rs 395.35 crore, flat YoY)
- **Inventory Days:** 74.0 days (vs 74.8 days at the start of FY27)
- **Net Working Capital Days:** 51.3 days (vs 55.9 days at the start of FY27)

### Highlights
- Price realization and product mix expanded the value-volume growth spread to ~600 bps in Q2 FY27 (11.5% value vs 5.5% volume).
- Order fulfillment rates dropped from historical 98%–99% to 96%–97%, causing a 3%–4% primary order loss in men's innerwear and women's outerwear due to localized demand spikes and imported raw material delays.
- S/4HANA digital implementation is on track for Q4 FY27 go-live; launched a new distribution center in West Bengal for D2C/e-commerce fulfillment.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its full-year FY27 guidance of double-digit volume growth, which implies an asking rate of ~14% volume growth in H2 FY27.
- Management maintained its targeted annual EBITDA margin range of 19%–21%.
- Outlined potential for another price increase if raw material inflation persists, to be evaluated month-by-month.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Competitive intensity in Q1 and Q2 FY27 remained subdued compared to the past three years, with discounting predominantly concentrated around the festive season.
- Athleisure/outerwear and women's innerwear categories continue to outpace men's innerwear growth.
- Primary shipments dipped sharply in September 2026 as channel stocking deferred to October due to later Pujo and Diwali timing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material inflation in Q2 FY27 ran at 6.5%–7.0% YoY across cotton and crude-oil derivatives, partially mitigated by the 2.5% price hike taken in May 2026.
- Softening observed in September reversed in October, returning input costs to elevated Q1 levels.
- Gross margins contracted 280 bps YoY as lower-cost raw material inventory pre-positioned in Q4 FY26 was exhausted in Q1 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Retail footprint closed at 115,426 MBOs, 1,662 EBOs, and 921 Large Format Stores; network additions targeted at 8,000–10,000 outlets for FY27.
- Odisha and K.R. Pet-2 facilities are currently running at ~40% of peak capability, with full ramp-up and peak capacity expected by the end of FY28 (next 1.5 years).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory days stood at 74.0 days; net working capital days improved by 4.6 days to 51.3 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- RBI compounding application for past FDI non-compliance (inadvertent online sale of imported goods between 2014–15 and 2026 involving Rs 48 crore) requires post-facto approval from DPIIT; management estimates penalty risk at ~0.75% of the amount (~Rs 36-50 lakh), with an extreme statutory limit of 3x.
- Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East disrupted shipping timelines and container clearance for imported raw material inputs.
- Ongoing legal dispute regarding Karnataka support-staff minimum wage revisions remains sub judice (garmenting tailors excluded).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Videesha Sheth):** How will you achieve the steep ~14% volume growth ask rate in H2 FY27 to hit double digits?  
  *A (Karthik Yathindra):* Confidence stems from three levers: shifting primary festive shipments and e-commerce sale builds from September to October/November, key festive product launches in men’s innerwear and bras backed by marketing campaigns, and steady underlying tertiary demand.
- **Q (Ashutosh Joytiraditya / Videesha Sheth):** What are the next steps and potential financial liability regarding the FEMA issue?  
  *A (Deepanjan B.):* RBI directed Page to seek post-facto approval from DPIIT for past contraventions spanning 2014–2026 on Rs 48 crore of imported goods. Page is fully compliant currently (Speedo restructured to third-party franchising); based on precedence, penalty is estimated at 0.75% (~Rs 36-50 lakh), though the outlying statutory max is 3x. Operations face zero impact.
- **Q (Jignesh Kamani / Rishi Mody):** Why did fulfillment drop to 96%–97% causing a 3%–4% order loss, and is this structural?  
  *A (Karthik Yathindra & V.S. Ganesh):* Not structural. It stemmed from sharp demand spikes in specific SKUs (men's innerwear and women's outerwear) alongside Middle East shipping delays for imported inputs, while two new manufacturing plants are still ramping up. Fulfillment has recovered to 97%–98%.
- **Q (Sameer Gupta):** Why did employee costs grow only 3.9% despite wage increases, and will it accelerate?  
  *A (Karthik Yathindra):* Headcount was optimal in H1 during initial plant ramp-up. Manpower costs and headcount will accelerate in H2 as Odisha and K.R. Pet-2 ramp up. The Karnataka garmenting wage revision from April is fully accounted for, while the non-garmenting revision remains sub judice.

### One-line takeaway
Page Industries absorbed input cost pressures to deliver 20.8% EBITDA margins, banking on delayed festive primary billing and plant ramp-ups to bridge a steep 14% H2 volume ask.
