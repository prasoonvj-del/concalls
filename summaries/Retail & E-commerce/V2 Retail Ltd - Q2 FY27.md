# V2 Retail Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27

**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume-led top-line and gross margin expansion were offset by subdued reported SSSG due to a festive timing shift, higher store opening costs, and increased debt.

---

### Key numbers
* **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 905.6 crore, up 28% YoY (vs Rs 708.6 crore in Q2 FY26).
* **Gross Profit:** Rs 281.3 crore, up 42% YoY; Gross margin at 31.1% (up 310 bps YoY from 28.0%).
* **EBITDA (Post-Ind AS):** Rs 120.3 crore, up 41% YoY; Margin at 13.3% (vs 12.1% in Q2 FY26).
* **EBITDA (Pre-Ind AS / Cash EBITDA):** Rs 53.4 crore, up 20% YoY; Margin at 5.9% (vs 6.3% in Q2 FY26).
* **PAT:** Rs 18.2 crore, up 6% YoY; Margin at 2.0%. PAT growth was muted due to Rs 68 crore depreciation on right-of-use assets (vs Rs 37 crore in Q2 FY26).
* **Volume Growth:** 31% YoY in Q2 FY27 (43% YoY in H1 FY27).
* **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG):** Reported calendar SSSG was -14.9% in Q2 (-3.7% in H1 FY27); normalized SSSG was +0.5% adjusted for early Durga Puja timing in Q2 FY26.
* **Sales Per Square Foot (PSF):** Rs 700 per month in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 933 in Q2 FY26); mature stores (>2 years old) continue at Rs 1,000+ PSF.
* **Average Bill Value (ABV):** Rs 917 in H1 FY27 (vs Rs 900 in H1 FY26).
* **Average Selling Price (ASP):** Rs 307 in H1 FY27 (vs Rs 309 in H1 FY26).
* **Full Price Sales:** 91% in Q2 & H1 FY27 (vs 92% YoY).

---

### Highlights
* Opened 49 new stores and closed 3 in Q2 FY27, reaching a total store count of 427 across 340+ cities (4.6 million sq. ft. total space). Total H1 FY27 store openings stood at a record 106.
* Over 60% of H1 new store additions were in core states: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, and Assam.
* Product mix for H1 FY27: Menswear 40%, Ladieswear 29%, Kidswear 22%, Lifestyle 9%.
* Logistical enhancements: Implemented daily to alternate-day store replenishment via hub-and-spoke logistics across 3 warehouses and 22 hubs.

---

### Guidance & outlook
* **FY27 Revenue & Profit Growth:** Management maintained full-year revenue and PAT growth targets of >50% YoY. H2 FY27 sales expected to grow ~60% YoY (7%–9% SSSG from mature stores).
* **FY27 Margins:** Full-year pre-Ind AS EBITDA margin targeted at 7.5%–8.0% (post-Ind AS EBITDA margin at 13.5%–14.5%); gross margin targeted at 29%–30%.
* **Store Openings:** Plan to add 60 to 70 stores in H2 FY27 (full year total ~170 to 190 stores).
* **FY28 Contingency Plan:** Management guides to 120–150 store additions in FY28; however, if Q3 SSSG trends remain soft, store additions will be moderated to 100 stores, lowering expected revenue growth to 35%–40%.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
* Management noted overall muted consumer sentiment and demand slowdown in Q2, with footfalls down ~3% and ABV down ~1%.
* Durga Puja sales in October were flat YoY on a period-to-period basis, though calendar-to-calendar October SSSG registered double-digit growth.
* Early winter demand trends in November show positive traction with store winter stock fill rates 20% higher YoY.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Raw material cost inflation ranged between 3% and 7% YoY; management passed this on via MRP hikes of 5% to 10% on core products while maintaining overall ASP flat via lower-priced product mix adjustments.
* Gross margin expansion of 310 bps YoY in Q2 was driven by lower depth of discounting (30% average discount vs 50% YoY) and shifting campaign timing (bill buster offer moved to Q3/October).

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Expanded retail footprint to ~4.6 million sq. ft. as of September 30, 2026.
* Current infrastructure (backend design, sourcing, warehouses, hubs) is built to support additions of 150+ stores annually.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **Borrowings:** Increased to Rs 528 crore as of September 30, 2026 (Rs 45.6 crore non-current, Rs 483 crore current) vs Rs 253 crore in March 2026, primarily funding pre-festive stocking and 106 H1 store openings.
* **Inventory:** Stood at Rs 1,447.8 crore (vs Rs 1,141 crore in March 2026) to support H2 store pipeline and Q3 festive/winter stocking.
* **Working Capital:** Net working capital expanded to 88 days (vs 81 days in FY26); expected to normalize to 75–80 days by March 2027 following festive inventory liquidations. Average vendor payment cycle remains 45–65 days.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Prolonged muted SSSG could delay the maturity curve of newly opened store cohorts beyond the typical 2-to-3-year timeline.
* Short-term net profit pressure from high initial depreciation (Rs 68 crore in Q2) following aggressive store additions.
* Higher short-term debt and working capital days due to inventory buildup ahead of Q3.

### Notable analyst Q&A

* **Q (Vaibhav, Haitong):** What drove negative Q2 SSSG and what is the Q3 outlook?  
  **A:** Calendar SSSG of -14.9% was caused by the festive calendar shift (Durga Puja moved to Q3). Adjusted SSSG was +0.5%. October SSSG was double-digit on a calendar basis but flat period-to-period. Management expects Q3 to be its largest revenue and profit quarter ever, targeting 5%–7% period-to-period Q3 SSSG.

* **Q (Ankush Agrawal, Surge Capital):** Is lower SSSG driven by poor new store cohorts, and will growth targets be lowered?  
  **A:** Data across ABV, ASP, and store cohorts shows slowdown is broad-based across competitors. If muted SSSG persists through Q3, FY28 store expansion will be cut from 150–200 stores to 100 stores.

* **Q (Avinash, MOSL):** How did gross margins expand by 310 bps during raw material inflation?  
  **A:** Expansion was driven by shallower discounts (average discount 30% vs 50% YoY) and shift of the Dussehra bill-buster campaign to Q3. Cost increases of 3%–7% were offset by MRP hikes and product mix shifts to blended fabrics.

* **Q (Meet Mehta, Entigrity):** Why did Q2 sales per sq. ft. drop to Rs 700?  
  **A:** Q2 is seasonally the weakest quarter, typically 40%–45% below annual averages when unadjusted for festivals. On a trailing 12-month basis, new stores average >Rs 730 PSF and mature stores average >Rs 1,000 PSF.

* **Q (Onkar, Shree Investments):** What is the net profit and revenue outlook if store additions slow down?  
  **A:** If store additions drop to 100 in FY28, revenue growth will slow to 35%–40%. For FY27, full-year revenue and PAT growth are both expected to exceed 50%.

---

### One-line takeaway
V2 Retail achieved strong 28% revenue growth and gross margin expansion driven by aggressive H1 store expansion, but short-term SSSG and net profit faced pressure from festival timing shifts and higher store-opening depreciation.
