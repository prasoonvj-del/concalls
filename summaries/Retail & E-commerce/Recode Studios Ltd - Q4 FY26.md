# Recode Studios Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-08 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** H2 FY26 and FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue and PAT growth, successful market penetration, and a clear strategy for continued expansion.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 80 crore (FY26) vs Rs 48 crore (FY25); Rs 43 crore (H2 FY26) vs Rs 29 crore (H2 FY25).
*   **Revenue Growth (YoY):** 67% (FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 16 crore (FY26) vs Rs 6 crore (FY25).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 20% (FY26) vs 13% (FY25).
*   **PAT:** Rs 11.22 crore (FY26) vs Rs 3.11 crore (FY25).
*   **PAT Growth (YoY):** 260% (FY26).
*   **PAT Margin:** 14% (FY26) vs 6.5% (FY25).
*   **ROE:** 78% (FY26).
*   **ROCE:** 59% (FY26).
*   **Average Order Value (AOV):** Increased by Rs 100 (current AOV is Rs 800).
*   **Repeat Purchase Rate:** 40% month-on-month (online).

### Highlights
*   **Business Model:** Operates an asset-light model in the beauty and personal care segment with ~350 SKUs.
*   **Omni-channel Presence:** 22 retail stores (3 COCO, 19 FOFO) and 6 warehouses/dark stores.
*   **Channel Mix:** Currently ~60% online and ~40% offline; management expects a 50-50 split in the current fiscal year.
*   **Marketing:** Allocates 20% of revenue to advertising/marketing.
*   **Product Strategy:** Focus on "affordable luxury." Plans to launch 20-25 new products in the next 6 months, including higher-value items (MRPs of Rs 999, Rs 1,500, and Rs 2,500).
*   **Operational Updates:** Stopped the FOFO franchise model; shifting focus to dark stores (partnering with third parties) to optimize costs.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth Target:** Management targets at least 50% growth in FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management expects EBITDA margins to remain consistent with FY26 levels, as additional profits will be reinvested into marketing and branding.
*   **Quick Commerce:** Plans to foray into quick commerce in the next 1-2 months; currently developing an in-house quick commerce service.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Trends:** Consumers are increasingly experimental and open to affordable Indian brands.
*   **Seasonality:** H1 is typically weaker for color cosmetics due to humid weather; H2 (specifically OND quarter) is stronger due to festivals and winter.
*   **Competitive Positioning:** Management positions Recode as a replacement for international brands, focusing on "affordable luxury."

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Drivers:** EBITDA improvement in FY26 was driven by revenue growth and better bargaining power with suppliers.
*   **Supply Chain:** Sources products from Germany, Thailand, China, and India. Management stated that some high-quality color cosmetic products cannot currently be manufactured in India to their required standards.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Warehouse:** A new warehouse in Ludhiana is expected to commence operations in April 2027.
*   **Modern Trade:** Recently opened a kiosk in Omaxe Mall (New Delhi); in discussions with Shoppers Stop, Lifestyle, and Dabur New for further expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Receivables:** Increased to Rs 15 crore (from Rs 8 crore last year).
*   **Working Capital:** Management noted that IPO proceeds are being deployed toward working capital to improve product availability and inventory levels.
*   **Payment Terms:** Nykaa (classified as B2B) pays upfront; other channels have varying credit terms.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Quick Commerce:** Management noted that current quick commerce platforms are "loss-making" and that the company is cautious about spending heavily to acquire low-value sales.
*   **Inventory/Returns:** While dead inventory is low (~1%), management manages slow-moving stock by distributing it as "goodies" in self-grooming masterclasses before expiry.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Asset-Light vs. Heavy:** Management confirmed they will remain asset-light and follow the current trajectory.
*   **On FOFO Model:** Management clarified they have stopped the FOFO franchise model and are pivoting to a dark store model to manage high rentals and operational costs.
*   **On Nykaa Classification:** Management classifies Nykaa as B2B because they purchase inventory upfront, unlike D2C marketplaces.
*   **On EBITDA Margin Stability:** Management explained that any margin expansion from scale will be offset by increased marketing spend to acquire new customers.
*   **On Quality Control:** Management emphasized that they conduct site visits during batch filling at third-party manufacturers to ensure quality standards.

### One-line takeaway
Recode Studios delivered strong FY26 growth and profitability, pivoting toward a dark-store-led offline expansion while maintaining a disciplined 20% revenue-to-marketing spend ratio.
