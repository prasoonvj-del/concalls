# Shiprocket Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 35% YoY, adjusted EBITDA rose 3.3x to Rs 14.2 crore, and the company turned PAT positive.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (management stated they are largely on track to deliver on the medium-term vision and roadmap).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more bullish on quick commerce and AI tools integration.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Introduced Aitlas (AI visibility tracking tool) and Hydra (AI writer). Cross-border volatility was not mentioned as a major headwind this quarter.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): Launched features like agentic AI experience, Aitlas, and Hydra as promised.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Up 35% YoY to over Rs 670 crore (Core revenue Rs 460 crore, up 25%; Emerging segment revenue Rs 210 crore, up 61%).
- **GMV:** Up 33% YoY (TTM GMV over Rs 37,400 crore).
- **Transactions:** Up 34% YoY (TTM transactions at 233 million; core transactions up 31%).
- **Merchant Count:** Active merchants over 236,000 (up 8% merchant count additions; ARPU up 25%).
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 14.2 crore (margin at 2.1%), up 3.3x YoY from Rs 4 crore (Core Adjusted EBITDA Rs 59 crore at 12.8% margin; Emerging Adjusted EBITDA margin improved from -31.5% to -21.2%).
- **PAT:** Rs 0.6 crore (Rs 6 million) compared to a loss of Rs 20 crore a year ago.
- **Cash from Operations:** Rs 55 crore in H1 (2.3x of H1 last year).
- **Contribution Margin:** Rs 130 crore (19.3% margin, up 108 bps sequentially). Core contribution margin at 21.1% (Rs 97 crore). Emerging contribution margin at 15.4% (Rs 33 crore).
- **Sub-segment growth YoY:** MarTech (Fastrr) up 102% (annualized GMV US$ 1.3 billion / ~Rs 11,200 crore); Omnichannel up 79%; Cross-border up 9%.

### Highlights
- Turned PAT positive, driven by operating leverage from platform scale, lower ESOP costs (under 4% of revenue), and higher other income from IPO funds.
- Emerging segment revenue added Rs 32 crore sequentially (largest in six quarters), with its adjusted EBITDA margin improving by 10 percentage points YoY.
- Shifting MarTech (rebranded as Fastrr) and Omnichannel products to a broader merchant base (9.3% of core merchants use Emerging products).
- Launched agentic AI experience (Analytics agent in beta for ~1,000 users), Aitlas (AI visibility tracker), and Hydra (AI content writer).
- IPO occurred in the third week of August; finance cost reduction and higher other income reflect half a quarter's impact.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they are largely on track to deliver on the medium-term vision and roadmap.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Online retail is about 8% of retail, heading to 15% by 2030. India has ~60 million retail MSMEs, with ~45% of online retail GMV coming from tier 2 and beyond.
- Q3 is traditionally a seasonally lower volume quarter for D2C brands as they pull out of CPCs to push inventory into marketplaces.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Contribution margin is growing faster than revenue (every incremental rupee of revenue came in with ~22% contribution margin).
- ESOP costs are under 4% of revenue, down from 6% a year ago.
- Fixed overhead costs are expected to grow much slower than revenue in the core business.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Asset-light model: carrier-neutral, no owned fleet, utilizing AI to select courier partners and avoid stress lanes via RADAR (AI control tower).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Generated ~Rs 55 crore of operating cash flow in H1.
- Loan paid down and balance sheet strengthened via IPO funds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential stress lanes in courier networks (managed via RADAR).
- Seasonality in Q3 leading to lower D2C volumes.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA Securities) on core margins and food delivery:** Asked if current core margins are steady-state and about the food delivery strategy. Management stated margins will improve in the longer run due to P&L structure, and restaurants are being tested as a new category using Fastrr, checkout, and hyper-local delivery. Management did not provide a specific future timeline for margin jumps.
2. **Della Desai (Axis Capital) on realization/yield trends:** Asked about realization declines despite carrier cost inflation passes. Management explained it is a mix effect based on customer size cohorts and courier mix rather than direct yield drops.
3. **Avnish Sharma (JM Financial) on 3PL competition:** Asked about top supply partners increasing focus on D2C. Management stated Shiprocket is concentrated on providing a unified end-to-end stack (marketing, checkout, shipping) which differentiates them.
4. **Garima Mishra (Kotak) on Emerging segment sustainability and fixed costs:** Asked if Emerging revenue additions and core fixed cost growth are sustainable. Management confirmed that overhead cost growth will lag revenue growth and Emerging momentum is backed by unified decision-makers.
5. **Kunal Thanvi (Banyan Tree Advisors) on power merchants and omnichannel unit economics:** Asked about stagnant power merchant counts and omnichannel monetization. Management noted that new merchants funnel upward into power merchants over time, and omnichannel charges merchants for slotted B2B dark store replenishment rather than quick commerce platforms.

### One-line takeaway
Shiprocket delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 35% revenue growth, a 3.3x jump in adjusted EBITDA to Rs 14.2 crore, and turned PAT positive, propelled by core profitability and 61% growth in emerging businesses.
