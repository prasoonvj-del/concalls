# Lenskart Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, significant PAT expansion, and successful execution of store densification and international scaling strategies.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** +34% YoY.
*   **PAT:** ₹228 crore (+182% YoY).
*   **India Revenue:** ₹1,531 crore (+30.7% YoY).
*   **India EBITDA (pre-Ind AS 116):** ₹236 crore (+51.5% YoY); Margin: 15.4% (up from 13.3%).
*   **International Revenue:** ₹1,203 crore (+38% YoY).
*   **International EBITDA (pre-Ind AS 116):** ₹127 crore; Margin: 10.6% (up from 4.5%).
*   **India SSSG:** 18.3%.
*   **India SPSG (Same Pin Code Sales Growth):** 24%.
*   **India Eye Tests:** 63 lakh (+42.7% YoY).
*   **India Eyewear Units:** 82 lakh (+22.8% YoY).
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** ₹297 crore.
*   **ROCE:** 23% (up from 14.6% last year).

### Highlights
*   **Consolidated Product Margin:** Crossed 70% for the first time.
*   **Expansion:** Added 116 net new stores in India and 16 net new stores internationally.
*   **Technology:** Remote optometry now operational in 786 stores (up from 168 at FY25 end).
*   **Product Strategy:** Successfully launched ₹500 price point (Hustlr Club) and saw strong traction in premium segments (₹30,000+ progressive lenses doing ~₹250 crore annually).
*   **International:** Growth is volume-led and same-store-led; Owndays and Meller brands showing strong performance.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **India Potential:** Management believes India can support 10,000+ stores (currently ~1,500+).
*   **International:** Management plans to accelerate store additions in select markets (e.g., Thailand) in the future, though not necessarily this year.
*   **Strategy:** Focus remains on "engineering" vision infrastructure, including AI-driven eye tests and supply chain optimization.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Creation:** Management views the market as "being built" rather than captured, with 78 crore Indians needing vision correction (projected to reach 94 crore by FY30).
*   **Densification:** The strategy of increasing store density in existing pin codes is successfully adding demand rather than dividing it.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Expansion:** India product margin reached 64.2% and International reached 77.1%, absorbing currency headwinds.
*   **Marketing:** India marketing spend improved from 5.7% to 4.8% of revenue due to increased brand pull.
*   **Currency:** Management stated the business has a natural hedge due to non-INR revenue streams and is offsetting headwinds via in-house manufacturing and volume-based vendor discounts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Spent ₹75 crore on store capex and ₹132 crore on plant capex (primarily Hyderabad facility).
*   **Logistics:** Developed an "air plus train plus bus" ecosystem to facilitate delivery in Tier 2/3 towns.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow (₹297 crore) fully funded capex, resulting in a positive net cash inflow of ₹116 crore (before M&A and equity).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Talent:** Management identified maintaining agility and culture as the organization scales as a key challenge.
*   **Currency:** Short-term headwinds possible if currency conditions worsen.
*   **NPS:** Management acknowledged recent social media confusion regarding policies, noting that NPS is "bouncing back."
*   **Cannibalization:** Risk of down-trading with the new ₹500 price point; management is mitigating this via a "house of brands" strategy and omnichannel customer journeys.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Avi Mehta (Store growth vs. SSSG):** Management confirmed densification is working, with 18.3% SSSG proving demand is not being divided.
*   **Vivek M (Supply-side constraints):** Management highlighted remote optometry and AI eye tests as critical "unlocks" to enable faster store expansion.
*   **Tejas Shah (NPS/Competition):** Management emphasized that NPS is recovering and the focus remains on market creation rather than competitive share-shifting.
*   **Garima Mishra (International store sweating):** Management noted the playbook is similar to India and they are preparing for future acceleration in international markets.
*   **Devanshu Bansal (₹500 price point cannibalization):** Management explained they use "house of brands" and tech-enabled journeys to ensure the entry-level price point attracts new customers rather than cannibalizing existing ones.

### One-line takeaway
Lenskart is aggressively scaling its "vision infrastructure" via densification and tech-led market creation, delivering strong profitability growth alongside rapid expansion.
