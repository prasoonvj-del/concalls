# Neetu Yoshi Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** H1 & FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in achieving its full-year guidance and expanding capacity utilization despite a temporary margin dip caused by rising raw material costs and billing lags.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 59.15 crore (+34% YoY for H1 FY27)
- Order book: Approx Rs 160 crore in hand
- Capacity utilization: Old plant running at full efficiency; new Haridwar plant at 20%
- Combined peak revenue capacity: Approx Rs 360 crore across all facilities
- Balance sheet cash/bank balance expected at year-end: Approx Rs 15 crore (debt-free, no working capital or term loans)
- H1 CFO: Rs 20 crore

### Highlights
- Commercial production at the new Haridwar facility commenced on September 13, 2026, adding 13,200 MTPA to installed capacity (taking total combined capacity to 21,000 MTPA).
- Product revenue contribution mix stands at approximately 40% wagon section, 40% track section, and 20% coach section.
- Received RDSO approval for constant contact side bearer (CCSB) and friction wedge; brake support for FIAT bogies is in prototype development.
- *Continues pattern from earlier record:* Peak revenue potential target is upgraded to Rs 360 crore (vs Rs 350 crore previously projected by FY28).

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 revenue guidance maintained at Rs 200 crore.
- FY28 revenue targeted at Rs 350 crore.
- FY29 revenue growth expected around 25% over FY28.
- PAT margin target maintained at 25% for the full year FY27.
- New Haridwar facility utilization targeted at 50% for FY27 and 100% for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Wagon market is picking up following fresh order releases to wagon makers, driving sub-assembly demand.
- Track section demand is robust across UP, Haryana, and Punjab.
- Filling 15 to 20 tenders daily to gauge market trends and secure orders.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA grew 6.5% YoY in H1 FY27, lagging revenue growth due to higher purchase costs (steel and chemicals).
- Price variation clauses (PVC) exist in contracts; government/railway supplies experience a lag because supplementary bills are raised only after R-notes are generated, while private negotiations take a couple of months to reflect cost increases. Management stated supplementary bills recover extra costs directly without absorbing losses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of approximately Rs 10 crore in FY27 directed toward the track section (crossings and turnout systems).
- Further capex acceleration expected in H2 FY28 to expand track section capacity from Rs 50-60 crore to Rs 100 crore potential.
- Bogie manufacturing capacity is 500 bogies per month once approvals finalize.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free with no working capital loans or term loans.
- Trade receivables reduced by Rs 6 crore in H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rejections occasionally happen with railways or during private product development; rejected material is returned, manufactured anew, and debited from pending bills.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sankalp Pandey asked why margins declined despite PVC clauses.** Management explained the timing lag in generating R-notes for railways and the time taken to revise rates with private clients.
- **Achuth Prabhat questioned how the Rs 200 crore guidance is achievable after a weak H1 requiring Rs 140 crore in H2.** Management broke down the revenue potential: old plant (Rs 50 crore in H2), expanded old facility (Rs 30-35 crore), and the new Haridwar plant operational since mid-September.
- **Anil Shenoy asked about mainboard migration plans.** Management noted they completed one year since listing in June/July, current guidelines require three years, but they are watching upcoming January guideline changes.
- **Mehak Jain inquired about the export outlook.** Management stated exports are not targeted for FY27 and will be considered next year.
- **Abhishek Malhotra asked about new product approvals like CCSB and friction wedge.** Management confirmed CCSB has Rs 11-12 crore in orders currently running, friction wedge revenue has started, and brake support prototype is expected in two months.

### One-line takeaway
Neetu Yoshi Ltd. remains confident in its Rs 200 crore FY27 revenue and 25% PAT margin guidance, backed by a Rs 160 crore order book and the newly operational Haridwar facility ramping up in H2.
