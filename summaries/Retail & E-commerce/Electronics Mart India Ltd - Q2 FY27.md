# Electronics Mart India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong revenue and EBITDA margin expansion despite Q2 being a seasonally softer quarter between summer and the festive period.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained at 18% to 20% revenue growth for the full year.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from an AC-heavy summer peak (Q1) to a mobile-led revenue mix (53% in Q2), with management noting festive demand off to a strong start.
- New topics or risks: Entry into West Bengal (Kolkata operations starting mid-November) and minimum wage hikes impacting employee expenses. Old risks around summer dependency balanced by an extended AC season carrying into Q2.
- Last quarter's promises: Kolkata store openings slightly delayed to mid-November (previously targeted around Diwali), but management remains confident on total store additions of 25-30 for the year.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,900 crore (up 19% YoY)
- **Retail Sales:** Rs 1,807 crore (up 19% YoY)
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 4,319 crore (up 30% YoY)
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 293 crore (up 31% YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 15.4% (up 130 bps YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 131 crore (up 61% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 6.9% (up 180 bps YoY)
- **Pre-Ind AS EBITDA:** Rs 90 crore at 4.8% (vs Rs 46 crore at 2.9% YoY)
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 369 crore (up 93% YoY) with an 8.6% margin
- **PAT:** Rs 40 crore (up 149% YoY)
- **PAT Margin:** 2.1% (vs 1% YoY)
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 161 crore (up 326% YoY)
- **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG):** 16.5% for Q2 FY27; 27.5% for H1 FY27
- **Working Capital Days:** 61 days as of 30th September 2026 (vs 77 days as of 30th September 2025; 73 days as of 31st March 2026)
- **H1 Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 384 crore (post-Ind AS); Rs 304 crore (pre-Ind AS)
- **Total Equity:** Rs 1,787 crore
- **TTM ROCE / ROE:** 18.5% / 12.9%
- **Average Ticket Size:** Over Rs 25,700 (up nearly 15% YoY)

### Highlights
- **Category Mix:** Mobile phones contributed 53% of Q2 product revenue (up 31% YoY). Air conditioners grew 52% YoY due to an extended summer. Built-in appliances grew 35%, dishwashers 41%, tablets 52%, and wearables 26%.
- **Cluster Performance:** South cluster revenue up 28% in H1 with EBITDA margins up 320 bps to 9.3%. North cluster revenue grew 45% in Q2 and 36% in H1, with EBITDA margins rising to 3.4% from 1% YoY.
- **Store Network:** Added 6 net stores in H1, reaching a total of 229 stores across 102 cities (222 MBOs, 7 EBOs) and 2 million sq ft of retail area, supported by 14 central warehouses.
- **Store Maturity:** 102 stores >4 years old deliver a 9.7% EBITDA margin; 127 stores <4 years old stand at 6.4%.
- **Customer Metrics:** Served 16.8 lakh customer bills in H1 (up 20% YoY); bill cuts dropped 3% in Q2 due to festive timing shifts vs last year.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Maintained at 18% to 20% for FY27.
- **Gross Margin:** Striving to achieve ~15% blended gross margin in H2.
- **EBITDA Margin:** Pre-Ind AS EBITDA margin guided at 6% to 6.5% (post-Ind AS at 7.5% to 8%).
- **Store Additions:** Guided to add 25-30 stores comfortably in FY27, with a similar or slightly higher pace in FY28.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Estimated around Rs 600 crore (pre-Ind AS) for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Festive season demand off to a strong start, well-stocked across categories.
- Television category expected to be flattish due to high base/pent-up demand from GST changes last year.
- Entry-level mobile and laptop volumes saw a slight slowdown, while premium models (above Rs 50,000 and iPhones) showed positive growth.
- West Bengal operations are scheduled to launch by mid-November with 4-5 initial stores out of 22 signed properties.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion driven by extended AC sales in Q2 and periodic price escalations in mobile/IT categories allowing liquidation of lower-cost inventory.
- Employee expenses rose 18% YoY due to minimum wage hikes (raising entry-level wages from Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000), salary revisions, and upfront hiring/training for upcoming Kolkata and Delhi stores.
- Incentive income from brands and NBFC partners was Rs 11 crore in Q2 (vs Rs 7 crore in Q2 FY26).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Kolkata market entry progressing with 4-5 stores opening by mid-November; 15 stores expected to open over the next 2-3 quarters out of 22 signed properties.
- Total retail area stands at 2 million sq ft across 14 central warehouses.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital days increased from June levels to 61 days due to inventory buildup ahead of the festive season.
- Total equity at Rs 1,787 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Periodic price escalations and supply constraints on select high-end smartphone variants (e.g., iPhone Pro Max/Duo).
- Volume pressures in entry-level mobile phones and laptops due to rising price thresholds.
- Television category demand normalisation post last year's high base.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Zaki Nasser (Nasser Investments) asked about Q3/Q4 expectations and West Bengal timeline:** Management stated festive demand is on track, Q4 summer onset could provide upside, and West Bengal operations launch by mid-November with 4-5 stores.
2. **Vineet Prasad (Investec) asked about gross margin sustainability:** Management explained H1 benefited from frequent price escalations and extended AC sales; guided to a moderated ~15% blended gross margin for H2.
3. **Utkarsh Nopany (Axis Capital) asked about store addition slowdown and staff cost rise:** Management noted properties (especially Kolkata) take time to ready, reiterated 25-30 store guidance for the year, and cited minimum wage hikes and advance hiring for new clusters as reasons for higher employee costs.
4. **Akhil Parekh (Public Matrix) asked about operating cash flow guidance:** Management confirmed pre-Ind AS operating cash flow is expected around Rs 600 crore for the full year FY27.
5. **Hitaindra Pradhan asked about mobile price increases impacting volume:** Management noted entry-level volumes dipped as entry price points shifted higher, but premium sales (above Rs 50,000) remain resilient with higher ASPs.

### One-line takeaway
EMIL delivered a resilient Q2 with 19% revenue growth and an 180 bps YoY expansion in EBITDA margins to 6.9%, driven by premiumisation and strong multi-category momentum ahead of the festive season.
