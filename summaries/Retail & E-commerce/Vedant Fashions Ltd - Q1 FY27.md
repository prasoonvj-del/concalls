# Vedant Fashions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-02 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in the core business strengths, upcoming second-half wedding season, and strategic initiatives.

### Key numbers
- Retail sales (sale to customers): INR 4,195 million (up 3.4% YoY)
- Revenue from operations: INR 3,014 million or INR 301 crores (up 7.2% YoY)
- Domestic Same-Store Sales Growth (SSG) without SIS: 3.8% YoY
- Gross margin: 65.7%
- EBITDA margin: 44.6%
- EBITDA growth: 10.8% YoY
- PAT: INR 81 crores (up 14.7% YoY)
- PAT margin: 26.7%
- Cash conversion ratio (Operating cash flow to PAT, excluding finance income, trailing 12 months ended June 2026): approx. 101%
- Inventory days (TTM June 2026): 34 days
- Franchisee revenue per square foot requirements (Tier-wise): INR 7,000 (Tier 3), INR 8,500 (Tier 2), INR 10,000 - INR 10,500 (Tier 1)
- Franchisee capex per square foot: INR 2,100 - INR 2,150 (Tier 2/3), INR 2,500 (Tier 1)
- Combined salience of MBO, SIS, and e-commerce channels: approx. 5%

### Highlights
- Launched VFL Brahma, connecting company data directly to an AI brain for speed and efficiency.
- Continued mega campaign "Made for Each Other" featuring Rashmika Mandanna and Vijay Deverakonda crossed 1 billion views.
- Twamev brand advanced its "Truly You" proposition with celebrity narratives (Nupur Sanon, Salim Merchant).
- Diwas executed targeted digital and monsoon initiatives and secured partnerships across marketplaces (Myntra, Amazon, Blinkit, Zepto).
- Split the MBO plus SIS vertical into a dedicated leadership team with distinct benchmarks.
- Aggressively executed larger store closures in Q1 to avoid off-season Q2 rentals; gross openings planned for late Q2 and H2.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains focused and confident in core business strengths, brand equity, operational efficiency, and back-end infrastructure for sustained long-term growth.
- Expects overall financial year to be net positive, with high single-digit SSG doable for the balance 9 months of FY27, followed by store openings.
- Anticipates November to March to be a fantastic period for weddings, while October might be slightly weaker due to Adhik mass shifting Navratri.

### Demand & industry commentary
- SSG growth in Q1 was a mix of volume and price (broadly half and half).
- Premium brand Twamev outperformed the company growth average.
- Mohey grew faster than the company average, driven by non-bridal categories (stitched suits, sarees, crop-top lehengas).
- Industry-wide, smaller regional and local players who entered 3-4 years ago are closing stores due to dead stock pain in the celebration wear vertical; net store counts are starting to come down across top states.
- International markets (UAE and North America) faced pressure from war and tariffs respectively, but management expects to overcome these.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Maintained industry-leading gross margin of 65.7% and EBITDA margin of 44.6%.
- Gross margins improved compared to Q4 FY26 (65%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Tail store closures typically run at 3% to 4% in a financial year.
- Gross store openings slated towards the end of Q2 and early Q3.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong cash conversion ratio of approx. 101% for TTM June 2026.
- Inventory levels maintained at 34 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- UAE market impacted by war; North America market impacted by tariffs.
- Celebration wear vertical involves painful dead stock issues, making it difficult for new/regional players to sustain.
- September-October period may be slightly weaker due to the shift in Navratri caused by Adhik mass.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sameer Gupta (IIFL Capital) on store closures vs. competition:** Management clarified that closures are due to market shifts, opening larger neighborhood stores, or initial new store opening mistakes—not due to competition. Aggressive closures were frontloaded into Q1 to dodge Q2 off-season rentals.
- **Rahul Agarwal (Ikigai Assets) on SSG and store rollout sequence:** Management confirmed that high single-digit SSG for the balance 9 months is doable, which will precede new store rollouts.
- **Parth Sodha on premiumization and ASP/volume mix:** Management stated Q1 SSG was an equal mix of volume and price, with Twamev outperforming and higher ASP growth targeted starting mid-Q2.
- **Ashutosh Joytiraditya on primary vs secondary sales mismatch:** Management explained auto-replenishment causes quarterly primary/secondary variances and advised reviewing performance at a full financial year level.
- **Santanu (SMIFS Limited) on Mohey growth strategy:** Management highlighted doubling down on non-bridal categories (stitched suits, sarees, crop-top lehengas) and targeted digital marketing.
- **Gaurav Jogani (JM Financial) on industry store rationalization:** Management noted internal research across 8 states shows overall store counts and large player footprints coming down as non-viable competitors exit celebration wear due to dead stock pain.

### One-line takeaway
Vedant Fashions reported steady Q1 FY27 performance with 7.2% revenue growth and strong margins, leveraging technology, aggressive H2 marketing, and industry consolidation.
