# Rashi Peripherals Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered record-high Q2 and H1 revenue, EBITDA, and PAT with annualized ROCE/ROE close to 23%, though management remains cautious on consumer demand.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained general growth outlook, providing a long-term sustainable EBITDA margin target of ~2.75% and discussing a potential QIP of Rs 900 crore as advanced preparation.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted slightly to acknowledge potential consumer market contraction due to continuous price rises, while commercial demand remains robust.
- **New topics or risks:** QIP dilution concerns raised by analysts; net debt-to-equity rose to 0.76x and operating cash flows turned negative for the half due to deliberate inventory buildup and acquisition funding. Old topics: PC refresh cycles and component price rises continued.
- **Last quarter's promises:** VDA Infosolutions acquisition successfully consolidated (contributed 1 month in Q2); Restar JV operationalized with the addition of 2 Japanese brand lines.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 6,185 crore (up 48.8% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 220 crore (up 112% YoY); margin: 3.56% (expansion of 106 bps YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 131 crore (up 121% YoY); margin: 2.1% (expansion of 69 bps YoY)
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 5,708 crore (up 42% YoY)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 196 crore (up 96% YoY); margin: 3.44%
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 153 crore (up 172% YoY); margin: 2.68%
- **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 11,287 crore (up 55% YoY)
- **H1 Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 375 crore (up 81% YoY); margin: 3.33%
- **H1 Consolidated PAT:** Rs 235 crore (up 95% YoY); margin: 2.08%
- **Annualized ROCE / ROE:** Close to 23% for the quarter (highest since listing).
- **Working capital days:** 55 days for H1 (improved from 61 days a year ago; debtors: 43 days, creditors: 48 days).

### Highlights
- Highest-ever profits for Q2 and H1 on both standalone and consolidated bases; H1 profits exceeded 80% of the entire previous year's profits.
- Growth breakdown (50% YoY): ~30% from price increases, 10-11% from volume/units, and ~10% from new businesses.
- VDA Infosolutions consolidated for 1 month in Q2 (contributed <2% of revenue).
- Semiconductor revenue in H1 reached 2x of the last full financial year through the Restar JV.
- *Note vs record:* Net debt-to-equity increased to 0.76x and operating cash flows turned negative for the half due to deliberate inventory buildup and acquisition funding, breaking the previous pattern of lower leverage.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a 10% to 15% price increase contribution in H2 (slowing compared to H1).
- Management guided to a sustainable long-term EBITDA margin of around 2.75%.
- Enterprise business targeted to contribute around 20% next year.
- Semiconductor business aiming for 3x to 4x of last year's figures this financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Commercial market (~70% of India's ICT market) continues to grow strongly; consumer market faces potential contraction due to continuous price rises.
- Deepavali timing shift (Oct 20 in 2025 vs Nov 8 this year) led to inventory buildup at the end of September and October.
- Management stated India's AI adoption is at a nascent stage with minimal risk of a crash compared to global markets, expecting good opportunities for the next 2 to 3 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins improved by close to 100 bps in the quarter due to mix and pricing discipline.
- ESOP amortization was Rs 3.8 crore in Q2; total depreciation run-rate expected around Rs 12 crore per quarter post-consolidation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Management discussed a potential QIP of Rs 900 crore as advanced preparation for growth capital (providing an incremental deployable capital capacity of Rs 1,800 to Rs 2,000 crore).
- Expanding local design capabilities in Bangalore under Restar Corporation guidance.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity rose to 0.76x, reflecting acquisition debt and deliberate inventory buildup.
- Operating cash flows for the half-year were negative.
- Working capital efficiency improved to 55 days for H1 from 61 days a year ago.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Softening consumer demand and affordability pressures due to continuous price increases.
- Negative operating cash flow and higher debt levels for the half-year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Hitesh Goel (Origin Capital) on inventory and QIP:** Management explained that inventory buildup was driven by the Deepavali timing shift and strong commercial demand; stated the QIP is strictly advanced preparation for future growth and may not be executed if unneeded.
- **Tanay Desai (11H Advisors LLP) on sustainable margins and AI:** Management guided to a ~2.75% sustainable EBITDA margin and noted that India's AI adoption is nascent with low crash risk.
- **Karan Kamdar (Choice Institutional Research) on growth split:** Management dissected the 50% growth into ~30% price, 10-11% volume, and ~10% new businesses.
- **Aejas Lakhani (Unifi AMC) on Restar JV:** Management highlighted that the JV brings Japanese brand distribution lines, design expertise from 200 engineers in Tokyo, and access to Japanese manufacturers in India (e.g., Maruti, Daikin).
- **Madhur Rathi (Counter Cyclical Investments) on data centers:** Management stated they chose not to actively pursue large AI data center deals (worth Rs 20,000 crore in Q2) to protect ROCE and working capital while maintaining a 50% growth rate.

### One-line takeaway
Rashi Peripherals delivered a record Q2 with ~49% revenue growth and ~23% annualized ROCE/ROE, driven by strong commercial demand and pricing discipline, while increasing leverage and inventory for strategic expansion.
