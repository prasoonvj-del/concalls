# Meesho Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Contribution margins improved significantly driven by logistics efficiencies, and festive demand remained robust despite seller-side supply chain disruptions.

### Key numbers
- **Contribution Margin:** 6% this quarter (management cautioned not to extrapolate for the rest of the year).
- **ATU Increase:** LTM ATU add increased by 10 million over the last quarter.
- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** Minus 1% currently.
- **New Initiatives Annual Cap:** INR200 crores (including Kirana Club).

### Highlights
- Logistics speed improved by 15% faster deliveries, a continuing trend expected to continue through Q2 and Q3 put together.
- Valmo continues to make progress in reducing the cost of doing business and capacity availability during the festive season.
- Financial services (seller and consumer lending products) are showing encouraging signs, expanding supply base from NBFCs and looking at bank partners.
- Completed the acquisition of Kirana Club recently (as of the call date), which will run fairly independently under its founders.
- Long-term 5-year guidance for F '31 maintained at 25% CAGR on NMV.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the longer-term contribution margin targets for the course of the year; CM will be substantially better than FY26.
- Contribution margin expected to restore some lost logistics-related spread back to FY25 levels during the course of the year.
- Server costs expected to normalize as a percentage of NMV back to the baseline by Q4 FY27.
- Long-term fixed cost base aspiration is around 3% of NMV by FY31.
- Long-term NMV growth guidance maintained at 25% CAGR towards F '31.

### Demand & industry commentary
- User behavior shows no concern on the demand side; healthy traffic conversion driven by utility products, personal care, beauty, home, kitchen, and baby care.
- Seller-side supply chain disruptions noted due to volatile petroleum/crude prices impacting sourcing across categories.
- Festive season sales have been better than expectations on the demand side, hitting all-time high numbers and strong growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Primary cause for Q2 CM improvement was logistics efficiencies (improved RTO %, increased order density, network redesign, and Valmo efficiencies).
- Additional server costs (especially GPUs) incurred in Q2 due to global capacity constraints and pre-procuring for the festive season.
- Marketing spend rebalanced based on CAC and LTV; internal guardrails and hurdle rates remain unchanged.
- Management follows a strict customer-first policy: no hidden fees like platform fees or convenience fees are charged at the cart level.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Majority of capex investments for Valmo automation nodes are made by Valmo entrepreneur partners rather than the company directly.
- Middle-mile network design changes (using bigger trucks, gateway/sorter node utilization) helped drive efficiency.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Quarterly Free Cash Flow (FCF) improvement highlighted, with negative working capital release contributing during the quarter due to festive season timing.
- Normalized ongoing working capital cycle is around 20 days negative.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in crude and petroleum prices creating supply chain disruptions and pain points for sellers.
- Elevated server/GPU costs due to sudden global price overshoots.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Vivek M (Jefferies) on Contribution Margins & Logistics:** Management explained that the 6% CM should not be extrapolated due to lead-lags in passing efficiencies back via lower pricing, and attributed speed/cost gains to network density, network design, and GeoIndia LLM.
2. **Ankur Rudra on Marketing & CAC:** Management clarified that marketing spend rebalancing and base effects from festival timing shifts dictate quarterly metrics, and LTV/CAC guardrails remain strict.
3. **Swapnil on Platform Fees:** Management reiterated their firm stance against adding cart-level platform or convenience fees to maintain mass-market consumer trust.
4. **Swapnil on Fixed Costs & Kirana Club:** Management explained elevated server/marketing costs in Q2, guided for a long-term fixed cost target of 3% of NMV by FY31, and confirmed Kirana Club is included within the INR200 crore annual new initiatives cap.
5. **Vijit Jain on RTOs and Guidance:** Management noted RTOs are declining secularly due to higher prepaid shares and COD-reduction software innovations, and maintained the F '31 growth guidance.

### One-line takeaway
Meesho delivered a strong Q2 FY27 driven by structural logistics efficiencies and robust festive demand, while maintaining its long-term margin and growth trajectories without compromising customer-first pricing policies.
