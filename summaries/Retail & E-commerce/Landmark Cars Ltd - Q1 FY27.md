# Landmark Cars Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Pro forma revenue grew over 22% YoY with PAT nearly doubling, driven by improving operating leverage and cost discipline.

### Key numbers
- Pro forma revenue from operations: grew over 22% YoY
- Profit after tax (PAT): nearly doubled YoY
- Mercedes-Benz ASP: increased to approx INR79 lakh in Q1 FY27 from INR73 lakh in Q4 FY26
- Mercedes-Benz H1 CY2026 sales: 9,786 units (up 9% YoY)
- EV share of vehicles sold by value at Landmark: 30%
- ChargeZone charging points operated: close to 16,000 across India
- Lease amortization: around INR19 crore for the quarter
- Lease and interest cost put together: INR27 crore (approx INR25 crore rental cost)
- Capex guidance for FY27: INR50 crore (holding in line as of Q1)
- New vehicle sales margin: 2.3% in Q1 FY27 (up from 2% in FY26 and 1.7% in FY25)

### Highlights
- Delivered best-ever Q1 performance in a seasonally soft quarter.
- Signed an MoU with ChargeZone to create an EV charging ecosystem; customers receive wallet credit redeemable at ChargeZone while Landmark earns a share of charging revenue.
- Inaugurating a large 50,000 sq. ft. workshop in Mumbai (Jogeshwari) to cater to Mercedes-Benz, BYD, and Jeep.
- Received an LOI for an additional MG showroom in Ahmedabad, taking total MG network to 17 outlets.
- BYD outlets in Pune became operational in July; new Mahindra workshop starting in Hyderabad soon.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a more normalized and consistent demand trajectory through the later part of the year.
- Capex guidance maintained at around INR50 crore for the year.
- Management expects new vehicle sales margin trajectory to continue upwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Private vehicle car market saw strong momentum due to macro factors and post-GST cuts.
- Premium and mass segments grew faster than the luxury segment over the last few months.
- Luxury market currently hovers around 50,000 units; passenger car market expected to reach 5 million units this year.
- Initial studies show no negative impact on aftersales revenue from EVs compared to ICE vehicles due to higher running frequency and higher accident repair costs for EVs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price hikes globally led OEMs to take price hikes; Landmark gets a percentage of hiked amounts, increasing absolute margins.
- Focus on cost continues; management believes employee and other expenses as a percentage of revenue still have room to go down.
- Gross profit is considered less relevant due to varying brand mix and aftersales ratios; focus is on EBITDA, PAT, and cash generation.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continuing to expand service facilities and workshop capacity (measured in bays rather than number of workshops).
- Asset allocation is kept fungible across brands within geographies.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flow was robust (around INR60 crore generated in Q1).
- Excess cash flow will be used to repay working capital loans until money is needed.
- Borrowing costs are stable.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Operating in a VUCA (volatile, uncertain, complex, ambiguous) world with potential for macro shocks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Arnav Sakhuja (Ambit Capital):** Asked about Mercedes margin impact from price hikes. Management clarified absolute margins increase as they earn a percentage of hiked amounts.
- **Akhil (360 ONE Capital):** Inquired about deployment of strong cash flows and new vehicle sales margin levers. Management noted they are evaluating OEM/takeover opportunities on their terms, and target achievements/pricing strength are helping vehicle margins.
- **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit Asset Management):** Asked about showroom vs. workshop mix and EV aftersales contribution. Management explained workshops are consolidated/scaled by bays, and EV aftersales upside is significant.
- **Lokesh Manik (Vallum Capital):** Asked if ChargeZone partnership conflicts with OEMs. Management stated ChargeZone works with major OEMs, so it causes no conflict and acts as an onboarding/engagement tool.
- **Abhisar Jain (Monarch AIF):** Asked about employee/other expense trends and gross profit breakup. Management affirmed cost focus continues and took the request for GP breakup back.

### One-line takeaway
Landmark Cars delivered its best-ever Q1 with pro forma revenue up 22%+ YoY and PAT nearly doubling, supported by strong EV traction, new OEM partnerships, and an industry-first EV charging revenue-share model.
