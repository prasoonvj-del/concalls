# Popular Vehicles and Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong organic growth, successful integration of recent acquisitions, and an encouraging demand outlook for the upcoming festive season.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue from operations:** Rs 1,890 crore (~44% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 71.5 crore (~87% YoY growth).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 3.8% (vs 2.9% in Q1 FY26).
*   **Reported PBT:** Rs 1.9 crore (vs loss of Rs 11 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 62 crore (~82% YoY growth).
*   **Adjusted PBT:** Rs 11.2 crore.
*   **New Vehicle Volumes:** +81% YoY (consolidated).

### Highlights
*   Acquired businesses (Maruti/RKS, BharatBenz/Globe, Audi/Olympus) are now collectively EBITDA positive (Rs 9.4 crore).
*   Revenue contribution from Kerala declined below 50% for the first time, reflecting successful geographic diversification.
*   Organic revenue grew 33% YoY; organic new vehicle volumes grew 58% YoY.
*   Network expansion: Added Maruti Service Center (Koyilandy), 2 Tata CV outlets (Perumbavoor, Kazhakuttam), and a JLR facility (Nagpur).
*   Management stated the company is likely #1 in revenue terms among dealership entities in India.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects acquired businesses to achieve sustainable PAT-level profitability from Q2 onwards.
*   FY27 revenue growth target: ~20-25% (targeting Rs 8,200-8,300 crore).
*   Blended EBITDA margin target: ~4% for the year (lowered from previous 5% guidance due to higher Commercial Vehicle mix).
*   Expects service volume growth of 6-7% from Q2 onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains strong across PV, CV, and EV segments.
*   Inquiries growing ~17-18% MoM; bookings growing 22% YoY.
*   Construction sector (CV/Tipper segment) seeing low to negative growth due to environmental concerns and slowdown.
*   Entry-level PV segment recovering due to GST reforms.
*   Festive season (Onam, Diwali) outlook is positive.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margins impacted by a higher mix of Commercial Vehicles (lower margin profile).
*   Acquisition-related depreciation and finance costs (IndAS) continue to impact reported profitability below EBITDA.
*   One-off benefit of Rs 5 crore in other income from lease modification (Telangana).
*   Management is in discussions to renegotiate long-term contracts with lubricant and paint companies.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   No major new acquisition-led expansion planned; focus is on replacement capex and current projects.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Inventory days: ~32 days (vs 50 days a year ago).
*   Absolute inventory increased 14% YoY despite significant revenue growth.
*   Debt levels higher YoY due to FY26 acquisitions/expansion; priority is to reduce debt using cash from operations.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Supply constraints in EV (Ather) and JLR.
*   Shortage of spare parts causing vehicle delays in workshops.
*   Construction sector slowdown impacting CV (Tipper) segment.
*   High depreciation and finance costs from acquisitions impacting reported PAT.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Margin guidance of 5%?* A: Management clarified they are now targeting ~4.3-4.4% for the year because the CV mix is higher than initially modeled.
*   *Q: Why service volumes down 5%?* A: Impacted by rationalization of low-value job cards and divestment of Honda/Piaggio; management expects growth from Q2.
*   *Q: Are new vehicle sales profitable?* A: Yes, even after interest costs, all segments (Maruti, JLR, CV, Ather) are EBITDA positive.
*   *Q: Impact of price hikes?* A: Minimal impact on demand; mostly absorbed by dealer rebate funds.

### One-line takeaway
Popular Vehicles is successfully scaling its diversified multi-OEM platform, with acquisitions turning EBITDA positive and a strong festive demand outlook, though margin expansion is tempered by a higher mix of lower-margin commercial vehicles.
