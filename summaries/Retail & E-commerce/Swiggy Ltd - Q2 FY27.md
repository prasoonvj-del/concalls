# Swiggy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited intense competition and the need to build a resilient, habit-driven core business before aggressive expansion, while actively managing investments in new experiments like Toing.

### Key numbers
- **Instamart QoQ NOV Growth:** 13.4%
- **Instamart MTU Addition:** 1.3 million
- **Instamart Frequency Growth:** 4% QoQ
- **Food Marketplace Guidance (Standalone):** 18-20% YoY growth
- **Toing Food Growth Contribution:** Can push overall food marketplace growth to 25-30%
- **Quick Commerce Contribution Margin Guidance:** 0 to -100 bps with sequential improvement
- **Quick Commerce Adjusted EBITDA Improvement Guidance:** 100 bps for the current quarter
- **Store Capacity Threshold for New Openings:** ~2,000 orders/day; mature stores reach 2,300-2,500 orders/day
- **Network Store Addition at Max Capacity:** ~5-7% of the network every quarter
- **Instamart Orders per Day per Store:** ~1,200 orders (reflecting ~9% sequential increase)
- **Food Marketplace Take Rate:** Exceeded 26% (highest ever)

### Highlights
- Food delivery and quick commerce (Instamart) are focusing on cohortized user bases, habit building, and differentiated assortment (e.g., Noice, Nectr).
- Toing (value food delivery pilot) is 4 quarters old; management views it as TAM expansion and insurance, with 2 out of 3 users being new to the platform and dual-app users showing 25% higher order frequency.
- Instamart getting back on a growth trajectory with improved user retention and higher pod productivity.
- Transitioning Instamart to an inventory model starting mid-current quarter, expected to fully transition over the next 2-3 quarters.
- Geographically, quick commerce is not planning near-term city additions, focusing entirely on existing top-tier cities (130+ cities).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Food Marketplace:** Management maintains 18-20% standalone YoY growth guidance and targets a steady-state GOV margin of 5%.
- **Quick Commerce:** Contribution margin guided at 0 to -100 bps with sequential improvement; Adjusted EBITDA expected to improve by 100 bps in the current quarter.
- **Toing Investments:** Investments to remain range-bound in the short term while evaluating unit economics and retention.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Competitive intensity remains high across both food and quick commerce, with no immediate easing expected.
- Festive season is witnessing intense competition.
- Toing attracts a younger demographic (70-80% sub-30 years of age).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Toing operates with low fixed overheads since it runs on the original Swiggy chassis; spending is primarily on discretionary user acquisition and activation.
- Food delivery monetization reached an all-time high with take rates exceeding 26%.
- Operating leverage is expected to accrue as scale and density improve.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No near-term plans to increase geographical coverage for quick commerce; store expansion is strictly a function of capacity maxing out in existing zip codes (~5-7% of the network per quarter reaching threshold).
- Average store setup/stabilization takes 2-3 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt figures discussed; management noted substantial cash on the balance sheet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High competitive intensity from established players and new entrants (e.g., Rapido, Flipkart Minutes).
- Risk of margin compression if quick commerce growth and unit economics do not compound as planned.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gaurav Malhotra (Axis Capital) on Toing cannibalization & quick commerce losses:** Management clarified that Toing overlap with the core app is reducing, and 2 out of 3 users are new to the platform. Quick commerce delta losses are tied to growth resets and cohort investments.
2. **Vijit Jain (Citi) on Toing viability & retention:** Management stated Toing retention is better than the core app, and dual-app frequency is 25% higher; requested 1-2 more quarters before calling it a permanent scale-up.
3. **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA) on dark store expansion strategy vs. peers:** Management explained they do not need rapid city/store additions since existing coverage is dense and speed is best-in-class; store expansion is triggered solely when individual pods cross ~2,000 orders/day.
4. **Jignanshu Gor (Bernstein) on quick commerce monetization gaps:** Management highlighted that co-creation with brands via differentiated assortment (Noice/Nectr) and evolving consumer fee structures will drive monetization alongside growth.
5. **Aditya Soman (CLSA) on execution gap with peers and low SKU penetration:** Management reiterated their priority is to build a resilient, habit-driven core business and achieve Adjusted EBITDA zero before deploying excess cash on secondary expansion flanks.

### One-line takeaway
Swiggy is prioritizing a resilient, habit-focused core business and margin improvement in quick commerce while treating value food delivery pilot Toing as a range-bound, high-potential TAM expansion.
