# Rashi Peripherals Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered the strongest quarter in the company's history with record revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and annualized ROCE/ROE, driven by strong PC refresh cycles, enterprise demand, and firm component pricing.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 5,100 crores (up 61.9% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 155 crores (up 50% YoY); reported as Rs 173 crores (up 55% YoY) with a 3.38% margin.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 105 crores (up 69.5% YoY) with a 2.05% margin; Diluted EPS: Rs 15.25.
- **Consolidated PBT:** Rs 139 crores (up 73% YoY) with a 2.72% margin.
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 4,832 crores (up 58% YoY).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 164 crores (up 50% YoY) with a 3.38% margin.
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 97 crores (up 65% YoY) with a 2.01% margin.
- **Standalone PBT:** Rs 130 crores (up 65% YoY) with a 2.69% margin.
- **Annualized ROCE / ROE:** ~19.5% and ~19.8%
- **Working capital days:** 56 days (Inventory days: 55, Debtors' days: 41, Creditors' days: 40).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 1,285 crores as of end of June.
- **Semiconductor Revenue Growth:** Semiconductor revenue grew by 70% in Q1 compared to FY26.

### Highlights
- Achieved highest annualized ROCE and ROE since listing.
- Executed on three strategic pillars: scaled core computing/enterprise engine; announced strategic acquisition of a majority stake (67% in the first year) in VDA Infosolutions (a pan-India system integrator with FY24 revenue of Rs 850 crores); and formed a JV with Japan's Restar Corporation (named Rashi Restar Semiconductor Solutions Private Limited, 74% Rashi and 26% Restar) focused on advanced image sensing solutions.
- Extended reach by opening two new branches in Udaipur and Dhule.
- Dell commercial business contributed roughly 5% of business in the previous quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the Q1 price uptrend and strong demand to continue into Q2 (July-August-September), though the speed of price increases may halve.
- Targeting more than US$100 million in revenue under the semiconductor JV in the next three years.
- Management remains confident of maintaining its historical 20% CAGR run-rate and delivering above-industry growth.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Three converging industry forces: multi-year PC refresh cycle (Windows 10 migration), AI PC inflection (higher ASPs), and fundamental component economics shift (AI data center demand shifting memory capacity to high-bandwidth memories, causing conventional DRAM/NAND shortages and firm prices persisting through 2028).
- Indian ITC distribution opportunity exceeds Rs 1.5 lakh crore with mid-teen PC penetration.
- B2B split: ~30-35% consumer-centric, ~60-65% commercial (split between SMB and enterprise).
- Consumer segment faces affordability pressures due to laptop prices doubling YoY; commercial segment is preponing purchases to mitigate expected price gains.
- Entry-level laptops face ~50% shortage; medium/premium notebooks are available if higher prices are accepted.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Growth in absolute profit was faster than revenue because as prices rise, absolute margin grows while the cost base stays broadly stable.
- Gross margin contraction attributed to product mix shift toward PES (lower margin compared to LIT) and accounting treatment of a hived-off subsidiary.
- Price increases in Q1 contributed 30-35% to the 60% top-line growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added branches in Udaipur and Dhule to target C and D class cities.
- Semiconductor JV includes direct skill transfer from Japanese design experts to train local engineers, backing 50+ local engineering hires over the next two years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strict working capital discipline maintained at 56 days.
- Debt-to-equity ratio is around 0.5.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential risk of supply constraints/shortages continuing in H2 due to global AI demand and component shortages.
- Elevated pricing and potential consumer affordability pressures in H2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Khetan (Laburnum Capital) on India vs global demand:** Management cited India's strong economic situation, high focus on digitization, and emphasis on education as drivers making India immune to global shocks.
- **Bhavin Chheda (Enam Holdings) on growth breakdown:** Management clarified that 30-35% of growth came from price increases, 5-10% from new products/brands, 20-25% from increased quantity, and ~10% from market share gains.
- **Aejas Lakhani (Unifi AMC) on large AI data center deals:** Management explained they are prioritizing run-rate business (which yields higher ROCE and margins) over large mega-deals (like Yotta) in Q1 to protect working capital and maintain a 0.5 debt ratio, though they remain active in smaller component supplies and can pick up large deals if needed.
- **Aasim (DAM Capital) on channel inventory:** Management stated T2 partners are holding inventory to earn double-digit margins amidst rising prices, but demand remains robust.
- **Siddhartha Grover (Equirus PMS) on semiconductor JV target:** Management stated the JV targets over US$100 million in revenue within the next three years with higher margins than co-distribution.

### One-line takeaway
Rashi Peripherals delivered a record-breaking quarter with ~62% revenue growth and peak returns, successfully executing on high-margin strategic pivots in enterprise integration and semiconductors while maintaining strict working capital discipline.
