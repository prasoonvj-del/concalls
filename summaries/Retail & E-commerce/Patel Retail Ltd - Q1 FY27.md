# Patel Retail Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-27 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a strong start to the year with healthy growth across key financial parameters, expanding retail network, and good traction in private labels.

### Key numbers
- Total income: INR310.24 crores (69.35% YoY growth vs INR183.19 crores in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA: INR19.68 crores (23.92% YoY growth vs INR15.88 crores in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 6.3% (decline of 133 bps YoY)
- Profit after tax (PAT): INR9.52 crores (37.43% YoY growth)
- Earnings per share (EPS): INR2.85
- Retail sales (Q1): INR101 crores
- Average monthly sales per store: INR80 lakhs to INR1 crores (approx 5,000 to 6,000 per sq ft revenue for Q1)
- Mature store sales per sq ft: INR20,000 on average
- Private label contribution: 17.55% to retail sales, 37% in export sales, 45% in domestic sales (overall combined contribution 17.5%)
- Apparel and general merchandise category contribution: 8% to 9%
- Madhya Pradesh expansion sales (Indian Chaska): INR10 lakh to INR12 lakh per month
- Product wastage/expiry in retail: Less than 0.1%

### Highlights
- Added new retail stores at Rasayani and Babgaon during Q1; opened 53rd store at Uran in Raigad after the quarter close.
- Expanded flagship brand Indian Chaska into Madhya Pradesh in August, taking its presence to eight states and one union territory.
- Private label brand Indian Chaska averaging INR10 lakh to INR12 lakh per month in Madhya Pradesh.
- New stores are operationally breakeven since day one.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects EBITDA margins to recover towards 8% to 9% going forward.
- Management expects much better positive cash flow from operations by H1 FY27 as current assets convert into cash.
- Target to open another 8 to 10 stores in FY27, with expected revenue of INR1 crores per month per store.
- Target to increase processing facility capacity utilization to 80% to 82% in the medium term (by end of FY27/FY28).
- Five-year vision includes entering Western suburbs and Pune/PCMC region via cluster-based expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Staple category (USP of the company) contributes over 50% of revenue and remains a touch-and-feel category, though society-focused activations are being conducted to boost e-commerce.
- Rural expenditure and urban expenditure gap has narrowed from 81% in 2011 to 63%, supporting focus on tier-2 and tier-3 pockets.
- Protein scarcity identified as a major issue; company plans to launch new categories addressing this.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- 133 bps decline in EBITDA margin was driven by a mix of manufacturing, export, and commodity-linked businesses having higher raw material intensity and volatility during Q1.
- Apparel holding period is 15-20 days.
- Automation at processing facilities is expected to reduce labor costs and improve quality control.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Payback period for a new store is 24 months to recover capex and rotate inventory.
- Typical new store size/investment is in the range of 5,000 to 6,000 per square foot.
- Current processing facility capacity utilization is between 50% to 55%.
- Focusing on cluster-based expansion in tier-2 and tier-3 cities / outskirts of Navi Mumbai and Pune/PCMC.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Significant amount deployed in current assets in fiscal 2026; expected to convert current assets into cash in FY27.
- Operating cash flow expected to be positive by H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Raw material market volatility impacting margins.
- Consumer mentality in tier-3/tier-4 operating locations remains touch-and-feel, impacting online adoption.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Keval Mehta (Mehra Securities) asked about the drop in EBITDA margin to 6.3% and path to 8%-9%.** Management explained it was due to timing and raw material volatility in manufacturing, export, and commodity-linked segments, which will normalize.
2. **Vidhi Purohit asked about online sales contribution and app downloads.** Management noted that staples are touch-and-feel items in Tier-3/Tier-4 cities, but quick commerce trials and society activations are ongoing.
3. **Sakshi Shinde asked about quick commerce strategy against in-house brands of platforms.** Management stated they are in talks with Blinkit and Zepto and plan to use custom-bundled SKUs/combos to maintain margins and advertising cost sustainability.
4. **Yash Rathod asked about processing facility capacity utilization.** Management stated current utilization is 50%-55% and targets 80%-82% by FY27/FY28.
5. **Akansha Singh asked about apparel inventory holding.** Management stated the holding period is 15-20 days.
6. **Ishita Nagar asked about Madhya Pradesh traction for Indian Chaska.** Management replied it averages INR10-12 lakh per month due to high consumer loyalty in spices.

### One-line takeaway
Patel Retail reported strong revenue growth of 69% YoY in Q1 FY27 driven by store expansions, though margins faced pressure due to raw material volatility.
