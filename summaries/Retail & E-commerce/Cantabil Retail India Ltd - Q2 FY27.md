# Cantabil Retail India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust H1 performance and expected H2 demand recovery driven by the festive and wedding season, despite a festive calendar shift impacting Q2.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained annual revenue target of Rs 1,000 crores for FY27.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from moderate/stable in Q1 to anticipating strong recovery in H2 due to delayed festivities and upcoming wedding season.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Changed accounting estimate for leases to a 6-year standard norm, increasing PAT by approximately Rs 3 crores and reducing lease liabilities.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Planned 28-30 stores (55,000-60,000 sq. ft.) in Q2; delivered 23 stores adding ~80,000 sq. ft. across Q1 and Q2.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 370.5 crore (up 11% YoY from Rs 334.7 crore)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 103.8 crore (up 14% YoY from Rs 91.9 crore)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 28% (vs 27.2% in H1 FY26)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 23.4 crore (up 9% YoY from Rs 21.4 crore)
- **H1 FY27 PAT Margin:** 6.3%
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 191.7 crore (up 9% YoY from Rs 176.0 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 44.4 crore (up 5% YoY from Rs 42.1 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 23.2%
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 7.0 crore (up 4% YoY from Rs 6.8 crore)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 3.7%
- **Store Count:** 690 stores covering 9.89 lakh sq. ft.
- **Online Sales Contribution:** ~5.5% of total sales in Q2 FY27

### Highlights
- Delivered 5-year CAGRs of 22% in revenue and 26% in PAT.
- Changed accounting estimate for leases to a 6-year standard norm to keep the balance sheet light, boosting Q2 PAT by approximately Rs 3 crores and lowering lease liabilities.
- Opened 23 stores in Q2 (70% family stores, rest gender-dedicated).
- Kids category contributes 3% to 4% of total sales.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its annual revenue target of Rs 1,000 crores for FY27 (plus/minus 4% to 5%).
- Annual SSG targeted at 4% to 5%.
- Internal long-term target is to double revenue in the next 4 to 5 years (implying ~20% annual growth).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 SSG declined marginally by 2% due to a shift in the festive calendar (additional Hindu month in July delaying weddings, and Durga Puja shifting from September to October).
- Festive sales showed positive like-to-like growth once the season arrived in Q3.
- Wedding season demand is expected to drive H2 growth.
- Ground-level consumption slowdown is primarily sentimental due to Q2 numbers rather than actual structural negativity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Slight increases in cotton prices will be passed on to consumers; management does not see contraction in gross margins.
- Pre-Ind AS EBITDA and PAT pressures in Q2 were attributed to the marginal 2% decline in same-store sales growth.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added ~80,000 sq. ft. across Q1 and Q2, and aspires to add at least 55,000 sq. ft. in Q3.
- Company-owned store expansion continues as good locations are secured directly and offer stable, long-term ROI compared to available franchisee interest for large formats.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Short-term borrowings increased to Rs 27 crores purely as working capital utilization for winter inventory procurement as of September 30.
- The Rs 15 crore loan given out will be cleared off in time by February.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Franchisee expansion remains constrained because smaller players lack the capital allocation required for newer, larger-format stores.
- Online channel margins have not seen much improvement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nipun Khemka asked why capital allocation to self-owned stores continues despite slowing sales growth, and whether to revert to the franchisee model.** Management replied that store expansion is a long-term plan not altered by short-term quarterly shifts, and while the franchisee policy is open, franchisees lack the capital for large stores while company-owned stores offer better stability and ROI.
- **Raj Shah asked about FY27 growth targets given the festive shift and inventory levels.** Management stated the Rs 1,000 crore target remains intact as wedding season sales in Q3 and Q4 will compensate, and inventory is seasonally high for winter and weddings.
- **Raj Shah asked about the substantial reduction in lease liabilities and ROU assets.** Management explained they changed the accounting estimate for leases to a 6-year standard norm, which increased Q2 PAT by approximately Rs 3 crores.
- **Arjun Gaikwad asked about the Rs 27 crore short-term borrowing.** Management clarified it is purely a working capital limit utilization for winter procurement and the company has no long-term capex loans.

### One-line takeaway
Cantabil reported an 11% YoY revenue growth in H1 FY27 despite a Q2 festive shift, keeping its Rs 1,000 crore annual revenue target intact on expectations of a strong H2 wedding season.
