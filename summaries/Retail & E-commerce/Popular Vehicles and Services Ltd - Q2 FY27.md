# Popular Vehicles and Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong festive demand, broad-based growth across all segments, and successful integration of recent acquisitions.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20% to 25% over FY26 and expects to end the year financially profitable. EBITDA margin guidance updated to ~4.2% for the year (vs ~4% targeted last quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from an "encouraging outlook" to reporting actual bumper festive growth during Onam and October across all segments.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New topics include the IRDAI draft paper on insurance commissions, the promoter family settlement (Mr. John Paul stepping away), and the full-scale commercial operation of Yanik (B2B e-commerce) and BKT tyre distribution. Supply constraint mentions largely disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Acquired businesses achieved further scale; service volumes rebounded with reported overall volume up 88% (Errata: 8%) and service income up 22% for passenger vehicles.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** Rs 2,089 crore (~36% YoY growth in Q2 FY27); Rs 3,984 crore (~40% YoY growth in H1 FY27)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 77.7 crore (~57% YoY growth in Q2 FY27); Rs 149.8 crore (~70% YoY growth in H1 FY27)
- **EBITDA margin:** 3.7% in Q2 FY27 (vs 3.2% in Q2 FY26); 3.7% in H1 FY27 (vs 3.1% in H1 FY26)
- **Reported PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 4.3 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs loss of Rs 3.6 crore in Q2 FY26); Rs 6.1 crore in H1 FY27
- **Adjusted PBT:** Rs 18.6 crore in Q2 FY27; Rs 29.8 crore in H1 FY27
- **PAT:** Rs 2.2 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 0.6 crore in Q2 FY26); Rs 3.6 crore in H1 FY27 (vs loss of Rs 8.2 crore in H1 FY26)
- **Cash PAT:** Rs 43.6 crore in Q2 FY27 (~56% YoY growth); Rs 84.5 crore in H1 FY27 (~84% YoY growth)
- **New Vehicle Volumes (PV including luxury):** 11,818 units in Q2 FY27 (+39% YoY); 22,293 units in H1 FY27 (+57% YoY)
- **PV Service Volumes:** 196,652 units in Q2 FY27 (+5% YoY); Service income grew ~22% to Rs 181 crore
- **CV New Vehicle Volumes:** 3,549 units in Q2 FY27 (+27% YoY); 7,044 units in H1 FY27 (+34% YoY)
- **CV Service Volumes:** 54,906 units in Q2 FY27 (+9% YoY); Service income grew ~15% to Rs 100 crore
- **EV New Vehicle Volumes:** 3,685 units in Q2 FY27 (+115% YoY); 7,015 units in H1 FY27 (+131% YoY)
- **EV Service Volumes:** 15,792 units in Q2 FY27 (+59% YoY); Service income grew ~64% to ~Rs 4 crore
- **New vehicle inventory days:** ~41 days (vs 44 days in Q2 FY26)
- **State-wise revenue contribution (Q2 FY27):** Keralam 50%, Tamil Nadu 22%, Karnataka 14%, Telangana 7%, Maharashtra 5%, Punjab 2%, Andhra Pradesh 0.4%

### Highlights
- Broad-based growth across passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, luxury vehicles (JLR and newly added Audi), and electric vehicles, alongside improving after-sales business.
- Completed approximately one year since acquiring BharatBenz operations in Punjab and Maruti Suzuki operations in Telangana; integration largely completed and operations scaling up.
- Commenced operations at the new Maruti Service Center at Karukachal and Tata Motors CV Center at Kayamkulam (both in Keralam).
- Commenced full-scale commercial operations of Yanik (B2B e-commerce marketplace for spare parts via Zparex Digisolutions), onboarding ~3-4 sellers and 600-odd workshops.
- Commenced BKT tyre distribution operations.
- Promoter family concluded an amicable family settlement under which Mr. John Paul stepped away effective October 1, 2026; shareholding transfers in phases by December 29. Management stated no change to day-to-day operations, OEM partnerships, or growth plans.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 revenue growth to be 20% to 25% over FY26 and expects to end the year financially profitable.
- Management expects EBITDA margin to end around 4.2% for FY27, and targets closer to 4.5% to 4.6% for next year (FY28).
- Expects earnings momentum to strengthen progressively over coming quarters supported by higher service volumes, better operating efficiencies, improved utilization, and recurring revenues.
- Yanik: No profit contribution expected for FY27; expects meaningful contribution to EBITDA margins later.
- BKT Tyres: No contribution expected in FY27; expects much higher contribution next year as BKT scales production.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Supported by healthy demand during Onam and subsequent festive periods (Diwali), with strong customer interest, showroom footfalls, and bookings.
- Festive offers and improved vehicle affordability following GST reforms supported demand, particularly in the entry-level passenger vehicle segment.
- Discounting levels significantly lower compared to the corresponding period last year, reflecting a favorable demand environment and improved pricing discipline.
- Commercial vehicle demand remained healthy despite macroeconomic challenges.
- Electric two-wheeler demand remained encouraging across existing networks and newer markets, driven by Ather's expanding portfolio.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Increasing contributions from commercial vehicles moderate consolidated EBITDA margin profile due to lower relative margins in CVs.
- Depreciation and finance costs associated with acquired operations continue to impact profitability below EBITDA.
- Q1 FY27 other income included a one-off benefit of ~Rs 5.4 crore from a lease modification relating to Telangana operations (adjusted H1 FY27 EBITDA excluding this was ~Rs 144.4 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Strategic buildup of vehicle inventory resulted in higher inventory levels and a sequential increase in borrowings.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- New vehicle inventory days stood at ~41 days (vs 44 days in Q2 FY26).
- Sequential increase in borrowings due to strategic vehicle inventory buildup for the festive season.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commercial vehicles operate at relatively lower EBITDA margins, which moderates consolidated EBITDA margins as their contribution grows.
- Depreciation and finance costs from acquired operations weigh on reported profitability below EBITDA.
- Draft paper on insurance commission regulations by IRDAI poses a monitoring point for insurance commission revenue (~Rs 80 crore in FY26), though management expects industry-wide compensation discussions with OEMs and IRDAI to mitigate adverse impacts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Raj Mehta (Wisdom Advisors) asked about the performance and profit contribution of Yanik and BKT Tyres.** Management replied that FY27 will see no profit contribution from Yanik as it just started (3-4 sellers, 600 workshops onboarded), with meaningful EBITDA margin contribution expected later. BKT Tyres will also not contribute in FY27, with higher contributions expected next year as production scales up.
- **Raj Mehta asked about retail sales performance during the festive period (Onam through Diwali).** Management stated Maruti standalone grew ~44%, EV volumes grew 115%+, CVs (Tata and BharatBenz) saw double-digit growth, and the luxury segment saw handsome double-digit growth, with October continuing double-digit growth across all segments.
- **Raj Mehta asked about the EBITDA margin outlook for H2 FY27 and FY28 given increasing CV contribution.** Management stated EBITDA margin stands at 3.7%, expects it to increase to around 4.2% this year, and closer to 4.5% to 4.6% next year as segment margins improve by ~1%.
- **Harsh asked about the potential impact of the draft IRDAI paper on insurance commissions (given Rs 80 crore earned in FY26) and OEM compensation.** Management stated OEMs and FADA are in continuous discussions with IRDAI, emphasizing that auto dealers actively participate in vehicle repairs. Too early to comment on exact mechanics until final rules, but discussions are ongoing regarding compensation through vehicle margins or other revenue streams.
- **Harsh asked if there is a threat to the FY28 margin guidance of ~4.5% if there is an adverse verdict.** Management answered "No", explaining that 4,000 to 5,000 PV dealers have raised the issue with IRDAI and OEMs, and since it is a meaningful number to profitability, compensation will likely occur either way.

### One-line takeaway
Popular Vehicles delivered strong Q2 revenue and EBITDA growth (~36% and ~57% YoY respectively) driven by robust festive demand, successful integration of acquisitions, and scaling after-sales operations.
