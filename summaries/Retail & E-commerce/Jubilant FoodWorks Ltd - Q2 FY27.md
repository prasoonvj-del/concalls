# Jubilant FoodWorks Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Management expressed confidence in meeting full-year LFL growth targets and protecting gross margins, despite 200 bps of unplanned cost headwinds and hyperinflation accounting drag in DP Eurasia.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained full-year LFL growth guidance of 5%–7% (Q3 on track to meet plan). Popeyes network expansion guidance maintained at 40–45 store additions for the year.
- **Tone and demand view:** Dine-in and takeaway (DITA) revenue growth turned positive overall with daily sales declines arrested; delivery growth remains very strong (especially late-night). Demand remains steady with no visible consumption slowdown in urban markets.
- **New topics or risks:** Detailed discussion on DP Eurasia (Turkey) accounting dynamics where hyperinflation adjustments make reported LFL negative despite positive transaction growth; additional cost pressures from West Asia geopolitical crisis affecting oil/LPG and commodity supply chains.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Dine-in store refurbishments delivered ~100 stores completed out of ~400 targeted; calibrated price hikes of 150–200 bps executed as planned.

### Key numbers
* **Revenue:** Rs 2,627 crore (+12% YoY) *(from external earnings release record)*.
* **Net Profit:** Rs 108 crore (-42% YoY) *(from external earnings release record)*.
* **Price Increases:** Calibrated 150 to 200 bps price increases taken using machine learning models across day parts and channels.
* **App Growth:** Monthly Active Users (MAUs) grew 25% YoY, while Monthly Transacting Users (MTUs) grew 9% YoY.
* **Store Refurbishment:** Completed ~100+ stores to date, targeting ~400 stores over the next 3 quarters.
* **Domino's Unplanned Cost Headwinds:** 200 bps hit (LPG pricing, wage inflation); company absorbed 140 bps via structural initiatives, leaving a 60 bps residual impact.

### Highlights
* **Dine-In & Takeaway (DITA) Turnaround:** Overall DITA revenue grew during the quarter and average daily sales decline was arrested through front-of-house store refurbishments, local store marketing, and exclusive value propositions.
* **Popeyes Scaling Track:** Network stands at ~100 stores across 25 cities. Identified expansion roadmap to add 150 stores toward a Rs 1,000+ crore revenue run-rate; targeting Average Daily Sales (ADS) closer to Rs 1 lakh.
* **Product Innovations:** New platform additions like 2-in-1 Cheese Burst, Chicken Maxxx high-protein topping range, Cold Coffy, and entry-level Mousse Cakes contributed double-digit share to overall top-line growth.
* *Pattern continuation:* Continuation of earnings decline trend noted in Q2 FY27 results (PAT -42% YoY), despite 12% revenue growth.

### Guidance & outlook
* **LFL Growth:** Maintained full-year LFL growth target of 5%–7%. Q3 FY27 performance is tracking on plan.
* **Popeyes Expansion:** Maintained guidance of adding 40–45 Popeyes stores in FY27.
* **Margin Expansion Levers:** Identified an additional 100–200 bps gross margin expansion potential in Popeyes via procurement scale, unit-level cost optimizations, and equipment/labor productivity.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Delivery market in India remains highly under-penetrated (~5–6 million daily deliveries in India vs ~90 million in China).
* Management sees zero rural/demand-side slowdown drag since JFL operates 100% in urban locations across 2,500–2,600 store catchments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Inflation Pressures:** Milk/dairy, cheese, chicken, monsoon-affected vegetables, LPG pricing, and state-level wage hikes created input cost pressure. Geopolitical conflicts in West Asia further impacted commodity and fuel costs.
* **Cheese Cover:** Management confirmed cheese supply contracts remain covered for the remainder of FY27.
* **Margin Defense:** Calibrated price increases, discount optimization, product mix accretion, and shared supply chain/logistics scale with Domino's helped mitigate margin headwinds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* Refurbishment program for ~400 targeted Domino's stores focuses on front-of-house upgrades (furniture, paint, color schemes) to be completed over 3 quarters.
* Co-located food-court setup stores (Domino's and Popeyes operating side-by-side with shared seating and common bike delivery infrastructure) are being trialed opportunistically in high-street locations to lower lease and operating cost overheads.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Capital allocation continues to prioritize high-ROCE/ROI store additions and supply chain leverage without starving high-performing emerging formats like Popeyes.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Unplanned spikes in LPG and crude oil prices due to ongoing West Asia conflict.
* Regulatory wage hikes and mandatory labor code shifts across multiple Indian states.
* Divergence between MAU growth (25%) and MTU growth (9%) reflecting lower initial conversion rates among new online users.
* Hyperinflation accounting treatments distorting Turkey (DP Eurasia) reported LFL metrics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Nihal Mahesh Jham (HSBC):** *Are further price hikes needed given dairy inflation, and what explains the divergence between 25% MAU growth and 9% MTU growth?*  
  **Management:** Calibrated price increases of 150–200 bps were taken; further hikes will be taken if required. India's online delivery remains highly under-penetrated (~5–6 million deliveries/day vs China's 90 million/day), so digital acquisition of new users takes priority even if initial conversion is lower.
* **Devanshu Bansal:** *Why are Popeyes store-level margins still lagging competitors despite strong gross margins and ADS?*  
  **Management:** High-street and mall rentals are structurally higher for a newer entrant compared to 10–15-year-old leases. Margins will expand toward target levels as ADS approaches Rs 1 lakh, store labor/equipment costs are optimized, and marketing costs amortize across 250+ stores.
* **Avi Mehta (360 ONE):** *How does hyperinflation affect Turkey's operational performance versus reported EBITDA/LFL?*  
  **Management:** Transaction growth per store in Domino's Turkey is positive and local price hikes exceed store inflation. Negative LFL is a function of mandatory hyperinflation accounting adjustments against headline government inflation numbers; operationally, the business continues to grow in local currency and USD terms.
* **Gautam Rathi:** *What impact are refurbished stores having on dine-in momentum?*  
  **Management:** Refurbishment is part of a 3-pillar program (inviting store environment, carryout/dine-in value focus, and CRM-led local store marketing). The goal is to ensure dine-in/takeaway ceases to be a drag on LFL performance.

### One-line takeaway
Jubilant FoodWorks is defending margins through calibrated pricing and supply chain scale while expanding Popeyes and refurbishing Domino's stores to maintain its 5%–7% LFL growth trajectory.
