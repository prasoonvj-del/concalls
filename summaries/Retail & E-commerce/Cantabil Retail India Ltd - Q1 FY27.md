# Cantabil Retail India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-10 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by healthy revenue growth, improving store productivity, and industry-leading margins despite a moderate July.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 178.8 crore (up 13% YoY from Rs 158.7 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 59.4 crore (up 21% YoY from Rs 49 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 33.2% (vs 30.8% in Q1 FY26)
- **PAT:** Rs 16.3 crore (up 11% YoY from Rs 14.7 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **PAT Margin:** 9.1% (vs 9.2% in Q1 FY26)
- **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSG):** 4.04%
- **Store Count:** 667 stores covering 9.42 lakh sq. ft.
- **Finished Goods Inventory:** 114 days (down from 121 days in FY25)
- **Working Capital Cycle:** ~100 to 105 days

### Highlights
- Delivered 5-year historical CAGRs of 22% in revenue and 26% in PAT.
- Average store size for Q1 FY27 openings increased to 1,810 sq. ft.; management targets an overall average store size of ~1,500 sq. ft. by the end of the financial year.
- Online sales contribution stood at 5% for the quarter (impacted by an end-to-end software integration), with an annual target of 8%.
- Out of a Rs 25 crore loan given to a real estate developer in March, Rs 10 crore was recovered in Q1, with the balance Rs 15 crore expected to be returned by February.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its annual revenue target of Rs 1,000 crores for FY27.
- Annual Same-Store Sales Growth (SSG) targeted at ~5%.
- Annual gross margin target maintained at ~60%.
- Annual EBITDA margin guided at 28% to 30% (delivered 33.2% in Q1 due to Ind AS impact and larger store formats).
- Expected steady PAT margins of 11% to 12% going forward.

### Demand & industry commentary
- July demand was moderate, partly due to the shifting of festive dates (Raksha Bandhan and Diwali falling later compared to the previous year).
- Management expects volume growth to pick up in H2 and the winter season, aided by festive demand and new store additions.
- Raw material (and cotton) prices saw a 10% to 15% increase, which management successfully passed on to customers without impacting sales; management expects further cost corrections.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expansion driven by a mix of product mix changes and inflation correction.
- Employee costs absorbed the impact of minimum wage hikes in Haryana and UP (30%-35% hike in Haryana, where the factory is located); this run-rate will continue.
- Marketing spend currently ranges around 2%, with plans to increase digital marketing spend.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Opened 15 stores in Q1 (adding 27,000 sq. ft.); plans to open 28 to 30 stores (55,000 to 60,000 sq. ft.) in Q2.
- Total retail presence stands at 667 stores across 9.42 lakh sq. ft.
- Around 20-25 non-performing or outdated stores are closed annually as part of normal operations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Finished goods inventory stands at 75% for <1 year, 20% for 1-3 years, and 5% for >3 years (surplus factory outlets).
- Company remains virtually debt-free.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Franchisees in Tier 3 towns are hesitant to invest in larger-format stores (1,500+ sq. ft.), impacting the expansion of the FOCO model.
- Software integration issues caused a temporary dip in online sales contribution during the quarter.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bhargav Buddhadev (Ambit Asset Management) questioned the Rs 25 crore real estate developer loan.** Management replied that Rs 10 crore was recovered in Q1, the remaining Rs 15 crore will be cleared by February, and such transactions will not be repeated.
- **Disha (Sapphire Capital) asked about the sustainability of Q1's 33.2% EBITDA margin vs. the 28-30% guidance.** Management clarified that 33.2% included Ind AS impacts from larger stores, but the annual margin will be maintained around 30%+.
- **Niril asked about store-level maturity sales.** Management stated that fully matured stores generate ~Rs 1.5 crore average sales, while new stores generate ~Rs 1 crore, showing a 10-15% difference.
- **Yash Tawani asked why the FOCO model store count dropped/stagnated.** Management explained that large store formats require higher investments, which Tier 3 franchisees are uncomfortable funding.
- **Aditya questioned the feasibility of the Rs 1,000 crore FY27 revenue target given Q1 run-rates.** Management defended the target by highlighting planned aggressive store additions (55,000 to 60,000 sq. ft. in Q2 alone) and expected H2 volume recovery.

### One-line takeaway
Cantabil delivered a resilient Q1 FY27 with 13% revenue growth and 33.2% EBITDA margins, keeping its Rs 1,000 crore annual revenue target intact through aggressive large-format store additions and expected H2 festive demand.
