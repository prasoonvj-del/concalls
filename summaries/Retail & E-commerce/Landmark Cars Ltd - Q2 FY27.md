# Landmark Cars Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever EBITDA in the history of the company and highest ever new car sales EBITDA, driven by secular industry growth and strong festive momentum.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Capex guidance raised from around INR50 crores to approximately INR80 crores (an additional INR30 crores for JSW, Denza, and the Jogeshwari workshop).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from a seasonally soft Q1 view to a highly confident H2 outlook, supported by strong Diwali momentum and traditional H2 strength.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: New partnership with OLX India for pre-owned cars; IRDAI motor insurance consultation paper added as a new regulatory watch-point.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Jogeshwari multi-brand workshop commissioned; Denza and JSW partnerships progressing as planned.

### Key numbers
- Pro-forma revenue growth: 11% YoY in aftersales
- EBITDA share from new car sales: 39% (or 40% as stated initially)
- Per service revenue: INR30,300+ in the quarter
- New car inventory: ~35 days
- Operating cash flow (OCF): Negative INR16.9 crores for the quarter
- Gross borrowings: INR144.5 crores
- Capex guidance for the year: Increased by another ~INR30 crores (totaling higher than the initial INR50 crores)

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever EBITDA in the history of the company and highest-ever new car sales EBITDA.
- Signed an agreement with OLX India (CarTrade Tech) for a phygital pre-owned car business on a co-branded, revenue-sharing, and jointly funded basis.
- Commissioned a large state-of-the-art multi-brand workshop in Jogeshwari (replacing three smaller workshops in the Mumbai region).
- Received LOI from JSW Motors for Ahmedabad and Bhopal; JSW Combat (Jetour T2 based) goes on sale early Q4 FY27.
- BYD launched luxury brand Denza (D9 luxury MPV and Z9 GT); sold out for the next quarter pre-launch.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects industry growth momentum to continue for the next few quarters.
- Second half of the year (H2) is anticipated to be much stronger than the first half, following historical trends.
- Capex expectation increased by roughly INR30 crores above the original INR50 crores estimate due to JSW, Denza, and Jogeshwari workshop investments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- PV industry expected to comfortably cross 5 million vehicles this financial year.
- Nearly 29% of vehicles sold by Landmark are new energy vehicles (NEVs), nearly 3x the industry average.
- EV aftersales revenue shows no negative impact compared to ICE; higher running frequency and accident propensity drive higher EV workshop visits and repair revenue.
- FADA reports industry inventory at ~45 days, while Landmark is lower at ~35 days.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee expenses at 3.9% and other expenses at 3.4% of pro forma revenue.
- Continuous internal focus on maintaining a frugal organization and driving down cost percentages further.
- Aftersales gross margin ~40%, EBITDA margin ~18%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned Jogeshwari multi-brand workshop for operating leverage.
- Expanding footprint with Denza in Mumbai and JSW dealerships in Ahmedabad and Bhopal.
- Infrastructure and people remain fungible across brands.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory levels rose temporarily due to strong demand and strategic procurement ahead of price hikes.
- OCF turned negative at INR16.9 crores for the quarter due to seasonal and inventory impacts.
- Gross borrowings rose to INR144.5 crores; management plans to repay debt with free cash flow over time.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Business disruption in Gujarat workshops during August due to heavy rains and flooding.
- Ongoing IRDAI consultation paper on motor insurance being reviewed by FADA; potential regulatory changes to watch.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vijay Pandey (Axis Capital):** Asked about margin sustainability and capex. Management stated margins are sustainable due to mature outlets, and capex guidance was raised by ~INR30 crores for JSW/Denza/Jogeshwari.
- **Simran Thakkar:** Inquired about OLX partnership model and negative OCF/higher debt. Management explained cars are jointly acquired/funded on Landmark's balance sheet with profits shared after costs, and inventory will normalize.
- **Ronak Mehta:** Asked about KPIs for OLX partnership and positioning in newer brands. Management highlighted shared digital reach, joint branding, and top partner status for BYD and JSW.
- **Lokesh Manik:** Asked about IRDAI motor insurance guidelines. Management noted it is at the consultation paper stage and FADA has made strong representations.
- **Prolin Nandu:** Questioned why EV service revenue is higher than ICE. Management explained EVs are driven more, leading to higher mileage, compressed service intervals, and higher accident repair frequencies.

### One-line takeaway
Landmark Cars delivered its highest-ever quarterly EBITDA and new car sales EBITDA, supported by robust NEV adoption, new brand partnerships like Denza and JSW, and strong after-sales realizations.
