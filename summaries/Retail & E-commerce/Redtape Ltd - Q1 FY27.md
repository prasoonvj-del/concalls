# Redtape Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever Q1 absolute profit with strong gross and EBITDA margins despite soft early-quarter demand and cost pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** INR 480 crores (up 3.7% YoY from INR 460 crores).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 47 crores (up 19.4% YoY from INR 39 crores).
- **Gross Margin:** 47.5%.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 20.4%.
- **Other Income:** INR 8 crores to 9 crores (down from INR 28 crores in the corresponding previous quarter due to lower e-commerce rebate income).
- **Inventory Days:** 173 days (targeted to reduce to 150 days).
- **Store Openings:** 33 new stores opened in Q1 FY27; 150+ stores targeted for the full year.
- **Export Revenue:** Around INR 2.5 crores in Q1 FY27.
- **Category Mix (Q1 turnover):** Footwear 56%, Apparel 39%, Accessories 5%.
- **Channel Mix (Q1):** E-commerce contribution declined to 22% (from 30%).

### Highlights
- Acquired the rights to the global sportswear brand *Sprandi* for India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, and other countries in April 2026; launch planned by end of September via online and retail channels.
- Appointed a master distributor in the UK (*Style Label Manchester*) with transactions already underway.
- Sunglasses are present in ~80% of retail stores, while hard luggage is primarily sold online (present in only 10%–15% of stores).
- Non-BIS inventory is still held by the company, awaiting the industry's extended timeline.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Store Additions:** Management aspires to open 150+ stores by the end of FY27.
- **Margins:** Management hopes to keep EBITDA margins stable around 20%.
- **Growth:** Management aspires to continue the same historical growth stride in FY27.
- **Inventory:** Target to reduce inventory days to 150 days.
- **E-commerce:** Aspiration is for e-commerce contribution to return to the ~30% level over time.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q1 FY27 was a mixed quarter for discretionary consumption; early quarter saw softness and cautious consumer spending before improving.
- Core India retail business remains healthy, seeing improving demand trends as the quarter progressed.
- Overseas stores in the UAE saw an initial revenue impact due to geopolitical conflict/war, but customers have returned and recent numbers are better than last year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Protected consumer value and managed costs through sourcing, supply chain, and retail operations without increasing MRPs.
- Average Selling Prices (ASPs) improved sequentially across footwear and apparel via product and category mix.
- Faced wage-related pressures in key manufacturing states and general inflation.
- Lower e-commerce rebate/discount income from portals impacted other income.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Store sizes for new expansion in South, West, and East India range from 800 to 1,500 square feet. Store ramp-up takes 45–50 days for 1,000 sq ft stores and ~75 days for 3,000–4,000 sq ft stores.
- Opened a store in Forum Mall, Calcutta, adjusting to regional mall vs. high-street dynamics.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory stands at 173 days.
- Income tax search proceedings (from September 2025) are an ongoing process expected to take 2–3 years to close; management stated there is no risk or claim of any material liability.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Near-term unevenness across channels and pressure on key cost lines (inputs, labor/minimum wage hikes, sourcing).
- Online marketplaces pushing for higher discounting; refusal to participate led to lower e-commerce turnover.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sameer Gupta (Sapphire Capital) on e-commerce discounting:** Asked why e-commerce declined and if there is an algorithm for discounting. *Management stated it was a conscious choice to avoid deep discounting and protect brand margins, evaluating discounts on a case-by-case event basis rather than a rigid rule.*
2. **Amit Dharnidharka on online revenue drop and guidance:** Asked if the drop from 30% to 22% is a one-off. *Management affirmed it is a one-off brand protection measure and expects e-commerce contribution to eventually return to 30%.*
3. **Sanjay Munjal on hard luggage and mall presence:** Asked why luggage is sparse in offline stores and about mall strategies. *Management explained that hard luggage has predominantly shifted online across the industry, whereas sunglasses remain offline-heavy (80% store presence). Malls are entered selectively based on regional preference (e.g., South/West vs. North high streets).*
4. **Pawan Kumar on gross margin improvement:** Asked what drove the gross margin expansion. *Management attributed it to retail impact, operational efficiencies, and optimized supply chain.*
5. **Kushal on market rumors:** Asked about potential promoter stake sale to PE. *Management declined to comment on market speculation.*

### One-line takeaway
Redtape delivered a resilient Q1 FY27 with a 19.4% YoY increase in PAT and stable 20.4% EBITDA margins by prioritizing channel discipline and brand profitability over deep e-commerce discounting amidst soft early-quarter demand.
