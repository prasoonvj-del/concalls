# Khazanchi Jewellers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-28 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth driven by momentum in B2B and B2C segments, operating leverage, and scale benefits.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 586.36 crores, up 45% YoY (vs INR 403.84 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** INR 39.98 crores, up 89% YoY (vs INR 21.15 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 6.82%, up 158 bps YoY
- **PAT:** INR 27.83 crores, up 84% YoY (vs INR 15.15 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **PAT Margin:** 4.75%, up 99 bps YoY
- **EPS:** INR 11.16, up 82.35% YoY
- **B2B Working Capital Cycle:** 40 to 45 days
- **Retail Working Capital Cycle:** 100 to 120 days
- **Inventory Turnover Days:** 73 days (vs 63 days in Q4 FY26)
- **Total Inventory:** Approximately INR 460 crores as of 30th June
- **Total Bank Limits (Debt):** INR 100 crores (working capital)

### Highlights
- Completed 3 years since listing on BSE SME platform in August 2023; shareholders approved migration to the main board of both BSE and NSE (expected completion in ~2 months).
- Q1 FY27 marked the first full quarter of the new flagship showroom in Chennai, running at a monthly revenue run rate of INR 30 crores to INR 35 crores.
- Successful implementation of dedicated ERP systems in the new showroom, aiding product movement analysis, customer demand tracking, and reordering.
- Top 10 B2B clients account for no more than 5% to 6% of total revenue each, reflecting a diversified client portfolio.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets a revenue mark of INR 5,000 crores by 2030, with an annual revenue growth of approximately 25% to 30%.
- Targeting to increase B2B and B2C segments at a consistent 25% to 30% growth rate.
- Aiming to increase B2C retail contribution to approximately 40% of overall revenue by FY 2030 (up from current levels).
- Expecting full-year top-line growth of around 30% if current momentum continues.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying quantum/volume growth in jewellery for Q1 was around 15%.
- Steep rises in gold prices typically cause a temporary slowdown for 10-15 days, after which prices are digested and demand normalizes.
- Upcoming wedding and festival seasons expected to drive strong demand.
- Old gold exchange accounts for 15% to 20% of total retail sales and has increased.
- Bridal and wedding jewellery accounts for roughly 60% of retail sales.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA and PAT grew faster than revenue due to improving business mix, operating leverage, and benefits of scale.
- B2B margins are typically 5% to 6%, while retail stores are north of 10%. Overall margins are expected to improve as retail contribution reaches 40% by 2030.
- Marketing and advertising budget is maintained at 5% to 7% of total earnings.
- Making charges are protected through design differentiation and lower sourcing costs via combined B2B/B2C scale.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned rollout of 8 to 10 stores over the next 3 to 4 years (by 2030), combining flagship showrooms and boutique minimal jewellery stores.
- Initial store openings will focus on Tamil Nadu before exploring other states (such as Andhra Pradesh).
- Stores will initially be company-owned.
- Expansion and inventory requirements for upcoming stores will be funded via internal earnings, with debt raised as deemed necessary by management.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flows were negative due to additional inventory stocking for the new retail store. Management expects positive cash flow in the current fiscal year.
- Total bank working capital limits stand at INR 100 crores; no long-term debt.
- Customer gold savings plan launched in Q1 to help customers save and repeat purchases.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Cash flow was negative in the previous period due to inventory build-up for the new retail store.
- Steep and sudden increases in gold prices temporarily impact volume demand for a fortnight.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Keval Mehta):** Asked about underlying volume growth in grams. **A:** Quantum growth was around 15%; exact gram numbers offered via email follow-up.
- **Q (Rohit):** Inquired whether B2B growth will come from wallet share gains or new client additions. **A:** Management stated they continuously add new clients and hire staff to drive B2B growth alongside managing existing relationships.
- **Q (Miten Shah):** Asked about negative cash flows and future funding for retail expansion. **A:** Management explained inventory investment caused negative cash flow previously, but internal accruals and correct fund plans will ensure positive cash flow and fund expansion.
- **Q (Yash Rathore):** Asked about making charge pressure and the app/omnichannel strategy. **A:** Management noted design differentiation protects margins, and a B2B design app exists while a retail e-commerce platform and gold savings app are launching soon.
- **Q (Aditi Jain):** Asked about inventory levels and customer advance schemes. **A:** Total inventory is ~INR 460 crores, and a customer gold savings plan has been operationalized this quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Khazanchi Jewellers delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with a 45% jump in revenue and faster-growing profitability, backed by its flagship retail store ramp-up, as it eyes main board migration and a INR 5,000 crore revenue target by 2030.
