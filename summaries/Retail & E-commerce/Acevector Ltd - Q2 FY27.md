# Acevector Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1, Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong growth in fashion NMV, AI-driven operational efficiencies, and a narrowing of adjusted EBITDA losses sequentially.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 285.5 crore (+17% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 151.2 crore (+17% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA Loss (Q2 FY27):** Rs 8.6 crore (vs Rs 2.2 crore in Q2 FY26; narrowed from Rs 18.3 crore in Q1 FY27).
*   **Snapdeal Fashion NMV Growth (H1 YoY):** 31%.
*   **Snapdeal Overall NMV Growth (H1 YoY):** 10%.
*   **Snapdeal Delivered Units Growth (H1 YoY):** 16% (App-delivered units +23% YoY).
*   **Unicommerce Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 110.4 crore (+15% YoY).
*   **Snapdeal Unit Economics:** 10% contribution margin of NMV; ~Rs 40 unit contribution margin per order (ASP ~Rs 400).
*   **Snapdeal Customer Base:** >1.3 crore annual transacting customers (+12% YoY).
*   **Snapdeal Purchase Frequency:** 4.1 orders per customer (+11% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Business Structure:** AceVector operates two businesses: Snapdeal (value-focused e-commerce marketplace) and Unicommerce (AI-first e-commerce enablement platform).
*   **Snapdeal Strategy:** Focused on the "value-conscious" segment (Rs 300–800 price bracket) using three AI-driven pillars: FashionX (curation), CustomerX (discovery), and CreatorX (influencer content).
*   **Unicommerce:** Serves 8,500+ clients; operates a pure SaaS model with usage-linked pricing.
*   **AI Integration:** Snapdeal reported 90% reduction in catalog onboarding time (SnapList AI) and 89% of support queries resolved by AI (SIA AI).
*   **Group Synergies:** Shared corporate functions (Finance, Legal, HR) and group-level contract negotiations to drive cost efficiencies.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Unicommerce:** Management expects 15%+ growth in Uniware and 20%+ growth in Shipway from Q4 FY27 onwards.
*   **Unicommerce:** Management expects the pace of incremental investments to moderate in H2 FY27, leading to higher profitability and stronger operating leverage.
*   **Snapdeal:** Management noted a "very encouraging" start to October, with 60% YoY unit growth in the first 15 days.
*   **Category Mix:** Management expects the portfolio share of Fashion, Home, and Beauty/Personal Care to remain in the current ballpark.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Snapdeal:** 80% of sales originate from outside metros.
*   **Demographics:** Gen Z constitutes nearly two-thirds of new customers in H1.
*   **Retention:** 80% of volumes are driven by repeat purchases.
*   **Market View:** Management believes vertical e-commerce platforms in the value segment will gain salience, similar to the trajectory of branded fashion verticals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Snapdeal:** Operates an asset-light, zero-inventory, 100% 3PL-led model with 0% commission to sellers.
*   **Unicommerce:** Adjusted EBITDA moderation in H1 was attributed to increased investments in products, people, and market reach.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Seasonality:** Q2 growth was impacted by the shift of festive timings from Q2 last year to Q3 this year.
*   **Disclaimer:** Management noted that forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties and do not guarantee future performance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: How is CAC and payback evolving?** A: Management stated the payback period for new customers is typically 12–18 months.
*   **Q: Can you quantify AI outcomes?** A: 81% of orders are influenced by AI-powered discovery; 4 crore+ monthly product discovery views via image search; 1 crore+ monthly conversational intent-driven searches.
*   **Q: Is there a strategy shift away from non-fashion categories?** A: Management clarified that while fashion is the largest category (68%+ of NMV), the business remains focused on Fashion, Home, and Beauty/Personal Care.
*   **Q: What was the impact of the festive shift?** A: Management advised looking at Q2 and Q3 together to assess performance, noting the 60% YoY unit growth in the first 15 days of October as a positive indicator.

### One-line takeaway
AceVector is leveraging AI-driven efficiencies across its value-focused e-commerce marketplace (Snapdeal) and SaaS enablement platform (Unicommerce) to drive growth and improve profitability.
