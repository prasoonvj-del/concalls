# Retail & E-commerce - E-commerce & quick commerce: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the E-commerce and Quick Commerce sector reflected a dual narrative: strong top-line scaling, improved operational leverage, and narrowing losses across horizontal platforms, enablement SaaS, and logistics, contrasted with persistent caution around intense competitive intensity and margin pressures in quick commerce. While players like **Shiprocket**, **FSN E-Commerce (Nykaa)**, and **Brainbees (FirstCry)** reported significant bottom-line milestones (such as turning PAT positive or cutting net losses substantially) and robust revenue growth (14% to 35% YoY), quick commerce and food delivery heavyweights like **Swiggy** voiced caution regarding high competitive intensity and focused heavily on habit-building and unit economic resilience rather than aggressive footprint expansion. Meanwhile, value-focused platforms like **AceVector (Snapdeal)**, **Meesho**, and infrastructure/B2B enablers like **ArisInfra** highlighted steady formalization, logistics efficiency, and surging demand outside tier-1 metros.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across consumer-facing platforms (**Brainbees**, **FSN E-Commerce**, **Meesho**, **Snapdeal**) noted robust underlying demand and healthy traffic conversion, particularly driven by tier-2/3 cities and beyond (e.g., Snapdeal noted 80% of sales from outside metros; Brainbees expanded RocketBees to 102 cities). 
*   **Festive Shift Impact:** Several companies (**AceVector**, **Brainbees**, **FSN E-Commerce**, **Meesho**) pointed out that Q2 growth was temporarily impacted by the shift of festive timings (Dussehra/Diwali moving from Q2 last year to Q3 this year), advising analysts to view Q2 and Q3 together.
*   **Divergence:** While value and fashion platforms saw resilient demand, **Swiggy** adopted a more cautious tone, citing intense ongoing competition in quick commerce and food delivery without immediate easing in sight. Conversely, **Brainbees** noted that competitive intensity in diapering actually started to soften during the quarter.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Logistics & Operational Efficiencies:** A major driver of margin expansion across the board was logistics optimization. **Meesho** highlighted structural contribution margin improvements driven by Valmo and delivery speed gains; **Brainbees** cited a 100 bps drop in direct costs due to RocketBees reverse-pickup efficiencies; and **Shiprocket** reported a contribution margin increase to 19.3% as incremental revenue carried a ~22% contribution margin.
*   **Gross Margins & Raw Materials:** **FSN E-Commerce** expanded gross margins to 46% (up 106 bps YoY). However, **Brainbees** noted ongoing crude-linked raw material pressure on gross margins, though it is beginning to stabilize. **ArisInfra** successfully passed on raw material price increases almost in real-time due to short-duration orders.
*   **Profitability Milestones:** **Shiprocket** turned PAT positive (Rs 0.6 crore) and **FSN E-Commerce** posted a 207% jump in PAT (Rs 101 crore). **AceVector** narrowed its consolidated adjusted EBITDA losses sequentially to Rs 8.6 crore.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Asset-Light and Controlled Growth:** Most enablers and platforms maintain asset-light models. **Shiprocket** relies on an AI-driven, carrier-neutral network without an owned fleet; **ArisInfra** expanded partner manufacturing capacity to 11.7 million tonnes with zero capital investment in FY26/FY27.
*   **Store and Dark Store Expansion:** **Brainbees** officially restarted offline store expansion, targeting 90–100 stores annually (Rs 2,000–2,200 per sq ft capex). In contrast, **Swiggy** and **Eternal** (noted via operational commentary) maintain strict capacity thresholds—Swiggy is not aggressively adding new cities for quick commerce, opting to expand dark stores only when existing pods cross ~2,000 orders/day (representing 5–7% of the network per quarter).

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance:** Maintained across most companies (**Brainbees**, **FSN E-Commerce**, **Shiprocket**, **ArisInfra**, **Meesho**), with managements expressing confidence in meeting medium-term roadmaps.
*   **Tone Shifts:** **Swiggy** remained cautious regarding competitive intensity and quick commerce unit economics, whereas **Shiprocket** shifted to a more bullish tone on quick commerce and AI tools integration (launching Aitlas and Hydra). **Brainbees** shifted its tone on diapering competition from "heightened" in Q1 to "softening" in Q2, while ramping up focus on the upcoming Swara manufacturing IPO.
*   **Delivery on Promises:** **Brainbees** successfully resumed its offline store expansion engine; **Shiprocket** delivered on its AI-agent and tracking tool rollouts.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Festive Season Execution:** Performance of Q3 post-festive shift, particularly how inventory normalization and demand play out for **Brainbees**, **FSN E-Commerce**, **AceVector**, and **Meesho**.
*   **Quick Commerce Unit Economics:** Margin trajectories and dark store productivity for **Swiggy** and quick commerce enablers as competition persists.
*   **Corporate Actions:** Progress on the **Brainbees (FirstCry)** manufacturing arm ("Swara") IPO process.
