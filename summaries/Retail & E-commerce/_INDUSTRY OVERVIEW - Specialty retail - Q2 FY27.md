# Retail & E-commerce - Specialty retail: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
Managements across the specialty retail and e-commerce sector delivered resilient performance in Q2 FY27, characterized by record-breaking revenues and profits for major distributors and multi-brand operators driven by premiumization, enterprise AI infrastructure demand, and robust festive momentum (Diwali/Onam). However, consumer-facing discretionary segments faced volume pressures and margin compression due to elevated raw material costs, geopolitical supply chain disruptions in the Middle East, weather-related disruptions (floods in Gujarat), and continuous price hikes. 

## Demand-outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Festive demand across India (Onam through Diwali) was robust, with strong showroom footfalls and bookings reported by automotive dealers like Landmark Cars and Popular Vehicles, alongside solid electronics offtake. Enterprise IT demand remains exceptionally strong, powered by AI server/GPU adoption and data center buildouts noted by Rashi Peripherals and Redington.
* **Divergence:** While enterprise and high-end consumer demand (premium smartphones, high-value cars, commercial PCs) surged on the back of wealth-backed credit buyers, entry-level consumer demand showed clear volume softness and elasticity pressures due to continuous price increases. Electronics Mart and Redington highlighted volume dips in entry-level mobiles and PCs, contrasting with strong luxury and premium growth.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Cost Pressures:** Minimum wage hikes (Electronics Mart raising entry-level wages from Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000) and post-IPO hiring (Recode Studios adding 65–75 employees) drove up employee expenses. Raw material volatility, geopolitical freight charges in the Middle East, and currency headwinds (INR vs. RMB/USD) squeezed gross margins for manufacturers like Flair Writing Industries.
* **Mitigation:** Companies are mitigating margin pressure through price escalations, reducing retail discount schemes (Flair), and leveraging internal scale and in-house manufacturing (Lenskart).

## Capex & capacity
* **Expansion & Infrastructure:** Landmark Cars increased its capex guidance by ~INR 30 crore (totaling over INR 80 crore) for new partnerships (JSW, Denza) and the Jogeshwari workshop. Creative Newtech secured a massive BSNL BharatNet infrastructure order requiring INR 1,700 crore in capex over 3.5 years. Rashi Peripherals discussed a potential INR 900 crore QIP as advanced growth preparation. Lenskart continues its aggressive store expansion and scaling of its 50 million pairs/year Hyderabad facility.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Electronics Mart India Ltd:** Maintained full-year revenue growth guidance at 18%–20%. Shifted tone from AC-heavy summer peak to mobile-led Q2 mix (53%). Kolkata store openings experienced slight delays to mid-November.
* **Landmark Cars Ltd:** Raised capex guidance from ~INR 50 crore to ~INR 80 crore. Shifted from a seasonally soft Q1 view to a highly confident H2 outlook. Added OLX India partnership for pre-owned cars.
* **Lenskart Solutions Ltd:** Reiterate store addition pace; successfully recovered NPS to 79.6.
* **Popular Vehicles and Services Ltd:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20%–25% and updated EBITDA margin guidance to ~4.2% (vs. ~4% previously).
* **Rashi Peripherals Ltd:** Maintained growth outlook while discussing a potential INR 900 crore QIP; noted consumer market contraction risks from continuous price rises.
* **Redington Ltd:** Maintained robust growth expectations; highlighted rising opex due to AR provisioning and Middle East war-related costs.

## What to watch next quarter
* Festive season sell-through sustainability and inventory normalization in consumer durables and electronics (Electronics Mart, Rashi Peripherals, Redington).
* Execution and financial contribution of large infrastructure projects and acquisitions (Creative Newtech's BSNL project and Infinova acquisition; Popular Vehicles' new acquisitions and Yanik B2B platform).
* Working capital days, inventory levels, and debt reduction progress for companies that saw temporary working capital stretch due to festive stocking (Landmark Cars, Rashi Peripherals).
* Regulatory watch-points such as the IRDAI consultation paper on motor insurance commissions affecting auto dealers (Landmark Cars, Popular Vehicles).

* **Creative Newtech Ltd:** Delivered robust H1 growth and secured a ~INR 3,195 crore BSNL infrastructure order while scaling its proprietary consumer technology brands.
* **Electronics Mart India Ltd:** Posted resilient Q2 performance with a 19% revenue jump and margin expansion, balancing AC carryover with surging mobile demand.
* **Flair Writing Industries Ltd:** Reported a muted quarter marred by Gujarat factory floods and raw material inflation, forcing a downward revision in annual growth guidance to 12%.
* **Landmark Cars Ltd:** Hit record-breaking quarterly EBITDA driven by dominant NEV penetration, after-sales growth, and new luxury brand partnerships.
* **Lenskart Solutions Ltd:** Continued its rapid global scaling and vertical integration, achieving over Rs 10,000 crore in trailing revenue while fully recovering customer satisfaction scores.
* **Popular Vehicles and Services Ltd:** Capitalized on strong festive momentum across southern markets, driving 36% revenue growth and successfully integrating recent regional dealership acquisitions.
* **Rashi Peripherals Ltd:** Achieved record-high Q2 profitability and 23% ROCE fueled by commercial ICT demand, while strategically building inventory and leverage.

* **Redington Ltd:** Delivered historical highs in revenue and profit, successfully leveraging the enterprise AI server transition and premium mobility trends.
