# Om Galaxy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-25 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** H1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 revenue growth of 72% YoY, robust order book, and expanding manufacturing capacity.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs. 78 crore (up 72% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs. 19 crore
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 24.8%
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs. 8.8 crore (up 56% YoY)
- **Revenue Breakup (H1):** Pipe fitting moulds Rs. 48 crore (up 45% YoY from Rs. 33 crore); Automotive moulds Rs. 17 crore (from Rs. 6.5 crore); Industrial moulds Rs. 2.7 crore (from Rs. 3 crore); Hot runner systems Rs. 1.4 crore (from Rs. 1 crore); WONDRA B2C cleaning products Rs. 9.1 crore (from Rs. 1.7 crore).
- **Order Book:** Rs. 165 crore as of September 30, 2026 (includes a non-recurring one-time project-based order of Rs. 70 crore from a customer). Total one-time order in hand was Rs. 84 crore, out of which Rs. 12 crore was executed in H1.
- **WONDRA Run Rate:** Current daily run rate at Rs. 2 crore.

### Highlights
- Management noted this is the company's maiden earnings conference call.
- The company is operating at over 100% capacity and outsourcing some work.
- *Pattern Continuity:* Continues its high-growth trajectory in line with previous records, while maintaining 25-26% margins despite minor negotiation impacts.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth Guidance:** Management expects a 25% to 30% revenue growth trajectory. FY27 revenue is targeted around Rs. 200–240 crore based on execution of the Rs. 165 crore order book (with a potential 10-15% variance).
- **EBITDA Margin Guidance:** Sustainable at 25% to 26% (plus or minus). WONDRA EBITDA margin targeted around 15% as volume scales.
- **Capacity Utilization Guidance (Post-Expansion):** FY28: 25-30%; FY29: 60-65%; FY30: 80-85%. Peak revenue potential from the 3100 MTPA capacity is estimated at Rs. 400-450 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India is facing a major bottleneck in automotive tooling due to FAME subsidies and government policies making local manufacturing mandatory for automotive OEMs.
- Demand visibility remains strong until 2030, with major OEs (Mahindra, Tata Motors, TVSM, Royal Enfield, Maruti) sharing launch periods and tooling requirements in advance.
- Pipe-fitting demand is supported by continuous launches across 2000-2500 SKUs and the evolution of pipe types (PVC, CPVC, HDPE, OPVC).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material volatility is mitigated by purchasing large steel mother blocks (e.g., 25-40 tons) at fixed prices and committing to absorb the entire block over a period. Cycle times of 4-6 months match fixed-price periods.
- Minor EBITDA margin dip in H1 was attributed to price negotiations on a large Rs. 84 crore one-time order and initial overhead/absorption costs in the WONDRA segment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Current Capacity:** ~1200 metric tonnes (~80,000 sq. ft. area).
- **Expansion Plans:** Moving to a new 1,90,000 sq. ft. building, expanding capacity by 2-3x (reaching ~3100 MTPA).
- **Timeline:** Construction and shifting targeted for completion between March and July 2027; full shifting to take 9 months to 1 year, with commercial production reflecting in FY28-FY29.
- **IPO Proceeds Deployment:** Marginally deployed for building construction and loan repayment as of September 30, 2026; balance to be deployed by June-July 2027.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Fresh debt will be required for working capital and machine loans post-capex.
- Working capital inventory/WIP levels correspond directly to the size of the active order book.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Customer Concentration:** Top 10 customers contribute ~70% of revenues, with the largest customer contributing ~20%.
- **Auditor Qualification:** Auditors qualified the accounts due to non-provision of interest payable on delayed payments to MSME suppliers (data on delays not readily available). Management clarified that vendor terms vary widely and no interest liability notices have been received.
- **Segment Conflict Risk:** To avoid competing with core B2B mould customers (pipe-fitting and automotive OEMs), the company restricted forward integration strictly to the B2C cleaning (WONDRA) segment.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Neil Bahal (Negen Capital) on One-Time Orders:** Management explained that one-time orders represent major capital investments by pipe-fitting majors expanding their product ranges (e.g., scaling from 50 to 500 ranges), which are non-recurring projects distinct from regular mould replacements.
2. **Naman Agrawal (Equity Fund) on Raw Material Hedging:** Asked about commodity volatility; management detailed their mother-block procurement strategy where steel blocks are pre-booked and locked in at fixed prices.
3. **Keshav Garg (Counter Cyclical PMS) on WONDRA Rationale:** Questioned the logic of entering the B2C cleaning segment. Management defended it as a high-growth, unorganized forward-integration play that avoids channel conflicts with core B2B pipe/auto clients, backed by a 18,000-store sales network.
4. **Anil Gupta (Individual Investor) on MSME Auditor Qualification:** Raised governance concerns regarding unprovided MSME delayed-payment interest. Management responded that manufacturing trial periods and terms vary, and no vendor has claimed interest.
5. **Piyush Bangar (Vijit Global) on FY27 Revenue Potential:** Asked if FY27 revenue would hit Rs. 200–240 crore. Management confirmed the estimate is reasonable based on the Rs. 165 crore order book, applying a conservative 10-15% variance buffer.

### One-line takeaway
Om Galaxy delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 72% revenue growth and a robust Rs. 165 crore order book, driven by deep competitive moats in precision tooling and vertical integration, even as it scales up capacity 3x by mid-2027.
