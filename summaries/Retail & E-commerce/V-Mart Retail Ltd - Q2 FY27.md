# V-Mart Retail Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 18% YoY, but management highlighted the impact of a 19-day festive shift, inflationary pressures, and high competitive intensity.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue Growth:** 18% YoY.
*   **SSG:** 3% (11% adjusted for festive shift).
*   **Footfall Growth:** 36% YoY.
*   **Memo Count Growth:** 15% YoY.
*   **EBITDA Margin (Post-IndAS):** 9% (vs 8.9% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (Unlimited):** 11.6% (vs 10.5% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (V-Mart):** 8.8%.
*   **Inventory:** Increased 12% YoY; Days of inventory reduced by 7 days to 90.
*   **Short-term Borrowings:** Rs 190 crore (vs Rs 100 crore at March end).
*   **H1 Capex:** Rs 85 crore (vs Rs 60 crore YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Store Network:** Reached 613 stores (added 23, closed 1 in Q2).
*   **Unlimited (South India):** Sales per sq ft (SPSF) at Rs 681 (YTD), up 15%. Management stated the format is now matching V-Mart's profitability.
*   **LimeRoad:** 55% top-line growth; losses reduced by 32% (now 8% of revenue vs 18% previously).
*   **Omnichannel:** 37% growth in digital users; 58% of online orders fulfilled from physical stores.
*   **Leadership:** Anand Agarwal (CFO/COO) is managing retail operations; Varin Agarwal is leading merchandising.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth:** Management targets 17-20% sales growth.
*   **Expansion:** Management aims to open 90-100 new stores in the current financial year.
*   **Pricing:** Management expects a 5-6% price rise to pass on inflation; may require further hikes in Q4 if crude oil prices remain at $110-115.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Macro:** Geopolitical uncertainty and volatile raw material prices are impacting supply chains.
*   **Consumer:** High food inflation is pressuring household budgets. Gen Z and Gen Alpha customers now comprise >45% of the consumer base.
*   **Competition:** High intensity in the hinterland (East/Central/North belt: Odisha, Bengal, Bihar, Jharkhand, UP) with 150+ competitor stores opened in target markets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** 33.5% (vs 33.6% YoY).
*   **Expenses:** Employee cost grew 15%; other expenses grew 18%.
*   **Raw Materials:** Crude oil shift ($55 to $75) is already built into the system.
*   **Efficiencies:** Vendor payments are being made 9 days earlier to improve partnerships.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Store Size:** Average store size is 7,500-8,000 sq ft.
*   **Efficiency:** Management noted that 100 stores were opened in 14 months previously, with a goal to achieve this in 12 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt:** No long-term debt; short-term borrowings are for seasonal working capital.
*   **Inventory:** Per-store inventory is lower YoY despite the festive stock build.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Inflation:** Rising minimum wages in key states (UP, Haryana) are increasing employee costs.
*   **Competition:** Significant competitive store openings in core markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Avi Mehta (360 ONE):** Asked if margins have bottomed out. Management stated they are in control but face cost pressures and are managing via efficiency.
*   **Dhruv Sitlani (Leo Capital):** Asked for October SSG. Management declined to report mid-month data.
*   **Kaivalya Baing (IIFL):** Asked about competitive intensity. Management identified the East/Central/North belt as the primary battleground.
*   **Avinash Karumanchi (MOSL):** Asked about the Unlimited turnaround. Management credited product lines, youth focus, and a 5% pricing premium over V-Mart.
*   **Yash Sonthaliya (Edelweiss):** Asked about operating efficiency. Management cited store productivity, analytics, and power consumption management.

### One-line takeaway
V-Mart delivered 18% revenue growth despite a festive shift, with strong performance from the Unlimited format and improved operational efficiencies, though competitive intensity and inflation remain key headwinds.
