# Spencers Retail Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported a strong quarter with 13% YoY revenue growth at the consolidated level and a 2x improvement in EBITDA, marking the second consecutive quarter of growth.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenues:** Rs. 469 crore (vs Rs. 416 crore in Q1 last year, 13% YoY growth; 8% QoQ growth vs Q4).
- **Consolidated Gross Margins (Absolute):** Rs. 93 crore (vs Rs. 86 crore in Q1 last year).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 9.4 crore, roughly 2% of sales (vs Rs. 4.7 crore or 1% of sales in Q1 last year).
- **Spencer's Format Growth:** 18% YoY growth, 8% vs Q4; online business grew at 49%. Offline business grew at 14%.
- **Spencer's Sales per Square Foot (SPSF):** Rs. 1,850 per sq. ft.
- **Spencer's EBITDA:** Rs. 18 crore, 4.4% of sales (vs Rs. 15 crore in Q1 previous year which included Rs. 7 crore non-operating income).
- **Nature's Basket Sales:** Rs. 59 crore (vs Rs. 55 crore in Q4; Rs. 69 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Nature's Basket EBITDA:** Negative Rs. 2.5 crore (vs negative Rs. 4.5 crore in Q4; slightly positive in Q1 FY26).
- **Consolidated PBT:** Negative Rs. 60 crore (vs negative Rs. 66 crore in Q4 last year).
- **Membership:** Over 125,000 members contributing 1/3 of monthly sales.
- **Online Unit Order Economics (Spencer's/Jiffy):** Contribution per order positive at Rs. 18 (vs negative Rs. 18 in Q1 last year; Rs. 12 in Q4). Average bill value (ABV) at 780 plus.
- **Total Debt (Consolidated):** Rs. 1,266 crore (Spencer's Retail [SRL] Rs. 1,019 crore, Nature's Basket [NBL] Rs. 237 crore).

### Highlights
- Second consecutive quarter of delivering good growth over the previous year; eighth consecutive month of growth for Spencer's over the previous year.
- Spencer's operating expenses were 5% lower versus Q1 last year.
- Membership program launched in July of last year now covers ~25% of active monthly customer base. Members have 2x retention rate, 3x monthly spend, and ~2x frequency compared to non-members.
- Nature's Basket underwent a management change ~45 days ago and a reset plan is in place focusing on core strengths (fresh fruits/vegetables, meat, cheese, imported packaged goods).
- Online fulfillment for Jiffy maintains under-30-minute delivery and in-full rate above 90%, with 67% repeat rates and 85+ NPS scores.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year cumulative growth rates on the online business (Jiffy) to steady around 25%.
- Spencer's offline business expected to grow at mid to high single digits.
- Nature's Basket expected to start seeing early double-digit growth from Q3/Q4 once reset and optimization finish.
- Internal target for Spencer's SPSF is reaching Rs. 2,000 in the festive quarter.
- No new store openings planned for Nature's Basket or Spencer's in the current fiscal, except calibrated relocation/cluster stores. Refurbishment capex planned for Nature's Basket.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth driven ~70% by higher number of bills (NOBs) and ~30% by higher average bill value (ABV).
- Category mix stable; staples and liquor up by 100 bps each, non-food portfolio (apparel, E&E, GM) down by 100 bps, and fresh at the same level.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Spencer's gross margins steady at like-for-like 19% (reported 18.2% vs 19.1% due to membership accounting treatment).
- Support costs at lower levels due to optimization.
- Nature's Basket gross margins flat; limited scope for store-level cost optimization due to experiential format, focus is on top-line throughput and rupee gross margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Limited capex allocated for renovating experiential stores in Nature's Basket.
- No new store additions in FY27; calibrated expansion plan in FY28 once targets are reached.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total consolidated debt stands at Rs. 1,266 crore (SRL Rs. 1,019 crore, NBL Rs. 237 crore).
- Refinancing process for a large part of debt has started and will execute during the current month.
- Focus maintained on working capital allocation and inventory optimization.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Nature's Basket experienced a muted performance with a 13% YoY degrowth in Q1, attributed to internal issues around inventory and online sales push, which management is resetting.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Anita Bajaj (investor) asked about portfolio growth trajectories over 2-3 years across formats:** Management stated Jiffy will steady at ~25%, Spencer's offline at mid-to-high single digits, and Nature's Basket at early double-digits post-reset.
- **Anita Bajaj asked about KPIs to track Nature's Basket turnaround:** Management highlighted 3 operating KPIs: sales per square foot (SPSF), rupee gross margins, and cost control (overhead/support costs).
- **Anita Bajaj asked about membership growth potential:** Management stated they look at absolute numbers (target ~200,000 members shopping at least 5 times a month) rather than percentage contribution caps.
- **Parikshit Gupta asked about the driver for growth (volumes vs ABV):** Management clarified it was ~70% higher number of bills (NOBs/volumes) and ~30% higher ABV.
- **Parikshit Gupta asked about integrating Nature's Basket with quick commerce / online aggregators (Blinkit, Instamart):** Management stated they explored it, but substantial margin sharing and aggregators developing their own gourmet sections made it commercially unviable compared to building their own platform sustainably.
- **Parikshit Gupta asked about total debt and refinancing status:** Management confirmed total consolidated debt of Rs. 1,266 crore and stated refinancing is underway for completion in the current month.

### One-line takeaway
Spencer's Retail delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 13% revenue growth and a 2x increase in EBITDA, driven by higher transaction volumes, membership traction, and tight cost control, even as Nature's Basket undergoes an operational reset.
