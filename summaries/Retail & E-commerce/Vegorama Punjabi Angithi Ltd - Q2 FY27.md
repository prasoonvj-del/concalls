# Vegorama Punjabi Angithi Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with revenue growing 46.53% YoY and EBITDA margin expanding by 152 bps, supported by network expansion and solid operational execution.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 10,005.96 lakhs (up 46.53% YoY from Rs 6,828.57 lakhs)
- **Total Income:** Rs 10,083.56 lakhs (up 47.27% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 1,430.63 lakhs (up 53.59% YoY from Rs 894.46 lakhs)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 14.62% (up 152 bps YoY from 13.10%)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 1,396.98 lakhs (up 64.05% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 1,039.63 lakhs (up 62.58% YoY from Rs 638.23 lakhs)
- **PAT Margin:** 10.39% (up 104 bps YoY from 9.35%)
- **Diluted EPS:** Rs 6.26
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 1,208.32 lakhs for H1 FY27
- **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs 953.48 lakhs (along with other bank balances of Rs 1,949.58 lakhs)
- **Borrowings:** Long-term borrowings of Rs 335.53 lakhs; short-term borrowings of Rs 15.78 lakhs

### Highlights
- Network stands at 27 Cloud Kitchens and 2 fine dining restaurants spread across Delhi-NCR and other markets.
- Added 4 new Cloud Kitchens and recommenced 1 existing location during H1 FY27.
- Revenue mix: E-commerce contributed 91.58%, takeaway and catering 7.05%, dine-in 0.98%, and Corporate Thali 0.39%.
- *Comparison with Record:* H1 FY27 revenue reached ~Rs 100.06 crore, successfully hitting the milestone stated in earlier corporate records of scaling the pure-vegetarian cloud kitchen network to ₹100 cr revenue in H1 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets 60% to 70% growth for the full year FY27.
- Management targets Rs 365 crores in revenue for FY27-28 and Rs 1,000 crores in revenue within the next 3 to 4 years.
- Management plans to launch 15 to 18 Cloud Kitchens for a new homestyle food brand ("Maa Ka Khana") in Delhi-NCR in the coming months.
- Management plans to roll out the premium brand "Swaad of Punjab" across existing Cloud Kitchen locations starting January 15.
- Fully automated central kitchen in Bahadurgarh expected to be operational in February 2027 (commissioned in Q4).
- Gurugram Sector 89 fine dining and celebration hall project commercial operations expected to commence in FY27-28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- High demand observed in Delhi-NCR driven by nuclear families and daily ordering patterns.
- Aggregators (Zomato, Swiggy) encouraged the company to enter the homestyle food segment because existing market options rely on frozen food, whereas Vegorama offers live cooking.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management targets a 1.5% to 2% improvement in operating margins through structural efficiencies from the new central kitchen (production efficiency, reduced manpower, and minimized food wastage).
- Zero-commission orders via ONDC and company apps contribute to margin protection.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- "Maa Ka Khana" outlets involve low capex per outlet (estimated at Rs 10 lakhs to Rs 15 lakhs) with a 300 to 450 sq ft format adjacent to existing kitchens.
- Bahadurgarh central kitchen covers 5,400 sq ft across ground and first floors; imported machinery from Germany and Taiwan is in transit, targeted to go live in February 2027.
- Gurugram Sector 89 project is an owned 16,400 sq ft, 4-story property requiring no rental costs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total assets stood at Rs 7,182.18 lakhs as of September 2026.
- Shareholder funds increased to Rs 6,269.58 lakhs compared to Rs 2,637.27 lakhs as of March 2026.
- Net cash position remains strong with cash and bank balances totaling Rs 2,903.06 lakhs against total debt of Rs 351.31 lakhs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Local market fluctuations and rider availability issues noted in select micro-markets like Faridabad.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rohan (Individual Investor)** asked about outlet revenue potential and expansion beyond North India; management responded that the immediate priority is achieving Rs 365 crores in Delhi-NCR before scaling to Punjab, Agra, and Jaipur.
- **Abhi Jain (AJ Capital)** asked why expand into banquets and fine dining instead of sticking strictly to the cloud kitchen format; management explained that physical dining drives brand awareness, lifting nearby cloud kitchen sales by 30% to 35%.
- **Manav Kutari (Investor)** asked about turnover breakdown by store maturity; management stated that fully mature stores generate Rs 90 lakhs to Rs 1.05 crores monthly, while weaker/newer ones generate Rs 50 to Rs 55 lakhs monthly.

### One-line takeaway
Vegorama Punjabi Angithi posted strong H1 FY27 results with revenue up 46.53% YoY and PAT up 62.58% YoY, supported by steady outlet expansion and upcoming automation and new brand launches.
