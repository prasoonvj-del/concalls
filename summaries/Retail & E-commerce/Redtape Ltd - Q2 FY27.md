# Redtape Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating performance and profitability improved despite a revenue dip caused by the shifting of the Big Billion Days (BBD) sale from September last year to October this year.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** None / Maintained (management stated they are on track to deliver historical growth trends).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from early-quarter softness in Q1 to positive momentum in October, with management anticipating a bumper Q3.
- **New topics or risks:** Detailed discussion on the negative impact of a 19-day delay in Diwali/festive timing on peak vs. lean seasonal periods. Old topics like e-commerce discounting normalized as e-commerce mix rebounded to 31%.
- **Last quarter's promises:** E-commerce contribution successfully returned to ~31% (aligned with the stated aspiration to recover from Q1's 22% dip); brand website sales grew significantly to INR 31.9 crores.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** INR 474 crores (down 3.5% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** INR 955 crores (broadly stable YoY).
- **Gross Profit Growth:** 15.7% YoY.
- **Gross Margin:** 45.4%.
- **EBITDA:** INR 89 crores (up 8.2% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 18.9% (expanded by 204 bps YoY).
- **H1 EBITDA Growth:** 12.4% YoY.
- **PAT:** INR 36 crores (up 27.3% YoY).
- **H1 PAT Growth:** 22.7% YoY.
- **Brand Website (RedTape.com) Sales:** INR 31.9 crores (compared to INR 3.9 crores YoY).
- **Store Additions:** 49 EBOs and 5 shop-in-shops added in Q2; total network at 756 stores across 369 cities.
- **Category Revenue Mix:** Footwear 63%.
- **Channel Mix:** Online ~31%, Retail ~70%.

### Highlights
- Revenue comparison impacted by the Big Billion Days (BBD) moving from September (Q2) to October (Q3).
- E-commerce mix recovered to 31% (compared to 22% in Q1).
- Maintained strict discipline on pricing, discounting, and channel mix to protect profitability.
- Retail network expansion continued via franchise and COCO models, keeping expansion capital-light.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated they are on track to deliver their expected growth trajectory and look forward to a bumper Q3 driven by the festive/winter season and the shifted BBD sales.
- Focus remains on brand strengthening, reach expansion, retail productivity, and margin/pricing discipline.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating environment remained uneven through H1.
- A 19-day shift in Diwali lengthened the lean period and shortened the peak shopping season.
- October saw consumers returning to shopping with positive momentum.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Disciplined approach to pricing and discounting protected margins.
- High single-digit Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG) in retail provided fixed-cost leverage, boosting profitability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 49 EBOs and 5 shop-in-shops in Q2, reaching 756 stores across 369 cities.
- Franchise model continues to drive capital-light store expansion.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Franchise stores operate via security deposits with stock risk borne by the company; billing and GST are centralized.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Uneven operating environment and consumer behavior influenced by market forces.
- Calendar shifts (e.g., 19-day delay in Diwali) extending lean periods and reducing peak shopping seasons.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dheeraj Thakur (Elara Capital) on distribution and store metrics:** Asked how store formats and dealer networks operate. *Management clarified they do not use wholesale/distributors on books; stores are operated via COCO, franchise (FOFO), or third-party staff management, with a fixed 25% margin given to franchisees while stock risk and billing remain with the company.*
2. **Devanshu Bansal (Motilal Oswal) on festive shift impact:** Asked for comparable 7-month growth metrics given the BBD shift. *Management explained that a 19-day later Diwali increases the lean period and shortens the peak season every third year, but expressed confidence that fundamentals remain strong and October numbers look good for Q3.*
3. **Devanshu Bansal on margin drivers despite soft top-line:** Asked what drove margin expansion amidst fixed costs. *Management attributed it to high single-digit retail SSSG providing fixed-cost leverage and maintaining discipline on e-commerce discounting.*

### One-line takeaway
Redtape delivered a profit-focused Q2 FY27 with PAT up 27.3% YoY and EBITDA margins expanding by 204 bps to 18.9%, successfully absorbing a top-line dip caused by the festive sale shift through retail SSSG and channel recovery.
