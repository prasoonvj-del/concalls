# Credo Brands Marketing Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth of 16% YoY and margin expansion supported by positive same-store sales growth and success of the Mufti 2.0 strategy.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management guided to try to maintain 8% to 10% revenue growth for the full year (vs no numerical growth targets given last quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from cautious/soft to positive, noting good momentum in October and third consecutive quarter of positive SSSG.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Evolving geopolitical situation and potential inflationary pressures were newly flagged as risks.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Continued rolling out Mufti 2.0 stores (57 stores total now, with 14 opened/renovated in Q2).

### Key numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 189 crore (up 16% YoY) for Q2 FY27; Rs 314.6 crore (up 11% YoY) for H1 FY27
- Gross Profit: Rs 111 crore (up 19% YoY) for Q2 FY27; Gross margin: 58.6% (up 150 bps YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 60 crore (up 24% YoY) for Q2 FY27; EBITDA margin: 31.4% (up 200 bps YoY)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 27 crore (up 45% YoY) for Q2 FY27; PAT margin: 14.4% (up 290 bps YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 86.1 crore (up 9% YoY); EBITDA margin: 27.4%
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 29.6 crore (up 18% YoY); PAT margin: 9.4%
- Store Count: 419 EBOs across 227 cities as of September 30, 2026
- Mufti 2.0 Identity Stores: 57 stores total (including 12 renovated stores and 14 total opened/renovated in Q2)
- Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG): 3.0% for Q2 FY27; 2.81% for H1 FY27
- Advertising and Brand Building Spend: 6.7% of revenue in H1 FY27 (8% to 10% planned for full year)
- Revenue per square foot: Rs 5,836 for H1 FY27 vs Rs 5,407 in H1 FY26
- Trade Receivables: Rs 285 crore as of September 30, 2026
- Inventory: Rs 130 crore in the company's books as of September 30, 2026
- Trailing 12-month ROCE: 14.6%; ROE: 11.9% as of September 30, 2026

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 189 crore, supported by positive SSSG and better price realisations.
- Marked third consecutive quarter of positive same-store sales growth.
- Stepped up quality of merchandise across shirts, t-shirts, and bottom wear, leading to higher average selling prices (ASPs).
- Channel mix for Q2 FY27: EBOs 47%, MBOs 36%, Online 12%, LFS 4%, Others 1%. Channel mix for H1 FY27: EBOs 53%, MBOs 29%, Online 12%, LFS 4%, Others 2%.
- Maintained a debt-free status with zero borrowings as of September 30, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to maintain 8% to 10% revenue growth for the full year FY27.
- Full-year advertising and brand building spend guidance maintained at 8% to 10% of revenue.
- Management expects to renovate about 20 to 30 stores total this year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Operating backdrop for discretionary categories remained mixed with uneven footfalls, but October momentum has been good compared to last year.
- Long-term structural shifts remain positive: move from unbranded to branded, rising aspirations in Tier 2/3 cities, and preference for casual/lifestyle clothing.
- Consumers remain careful and selective with spending.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins expanded by 150 bps YoY to 58.6% due to a richer product mix.
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin saw a marginal decline to 27.4% due to higher investments in advertisement and brand building (6.7% of H1 revenue).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Reshaping the store network with discipline, prioritizing productivity and throughput per store over pure store count.
- Renovated stores are showing an 8% to 10% growth post-renovation.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free with zero borrowings.
- Trade receivables peak at reporting dates due to seasonal billing and pumping of new stock for the autumn/winter season, which management stated normalizes in coming months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Evolving geopolitical situation and potential inflationary pressures could weigh on consumer sentiment and discretionary demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nilesh Doshi (Prospero Tree AMC)** asked about special factors driving Q2 performance and the high trade receivables (Rs 285 crore vs H1 revenue of Rs 314 crore). Management replied that the performance reflects structural changes and product/store upgrades rather than a one-off, and explained that receivables peak due to seasonal dispatches at quarter-end and normalize subsequently.
2. **Sakshi Pratap (Pratap Securities)** asked for SSSG numbers and performance comparison of new vs old stores. Management stated Q2 SSSG was 3.0% and H1 SSSG was 2.81%, noting that renovated Mufti 2.0 stores show 8% to 10% higher growth.
3. **Azhar (Individual Investor)** questioned the financial justification of spending ~Rs 20-22 crore on marketing in H1 given current bottom-line impact. Management responded that brand building is a multiyear investment to communicate the reinvented Mufti 2.0 identity and will take a couple of years to reflect in profitability.
4. **Jay Jain (Jay Jain Capital)** asked about the sustainability of MBO growth and long-term 4-5 year aspirations. Management stated they expect MBOs to continue growing around 10% and declined to give specific 4-5 year numerical guidance while remaining confident in profitable growth.

### One-line takeaway
Credo Brands delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 189 crore (up 16% YoY) and a 45% jump in PAT to Rs 27 crore in Q2 FY27, driven by positive same-store sales growth, higher realisations, and the ongoing rollout of the Mufti 2.0 retail identity.
