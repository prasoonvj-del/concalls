# Neetu Yoshi Ltd - Q4 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-06-10 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** H2 FY26 / FY26
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with 44% YoY growth in total income, 53% growth in PAT, positive cash flows, and robust revenue guidance for FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (FY26):** INR 101 crore (up 44% YoY)
- **PAT (FY26):** INR 25 crore (up 53% YoY)
- **Revenue Guidance (FY27):** INR 210 crore to INR 220 crore
- **PAT Margin Guidance (FY27):** 25% (plus-minus)
- **Peak Revenue Potential (Existing + New Plant):** INR 340 crore to INR 350 crore by next financial year (FY28)
- **Current Order Book:** INR 140 crore to INR 150 crore (executable within FY27)
- **Receivables (as of March 31):** INR 32 crore (reduced to INR 20 crore pending by mid-April)
- **Tax Rate:** 17.5% (15% tax + 2.5% cess) due to central government lifetime scheme for eligible companies incorporated between 2020 and 2024.
- **Track Section Revenue (Current):** ~INR 20 crore (targeted INR 60 crore to INR 70 crore in two financial years)
- **Warrant Issue Amount:** ~INR 29 crore

### Highlights
- New bogie/component manufacturing plant is ready, with commercial production and first invoices starting in June.
- Company is diversifying into track parts (turnouts), locomotive, coach, mining, and power/thermal sectors.
- Secured RDSO approvals for 25 products, with another 15-20 products in the approval pipeline.
- Applied for approvals with ICF, MCF, and RCF in addition to RDSO.
- Promoters and management attribute strong margins to high entry barriers (critical safety Class A components, rigorous multi-stage approval processes) and being a debt-free company with tax benefits and low power/interest costs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for FY27 revenue of INR 210 crore to INR 220 crore and a PAT margin of around 25%.
- Peak revenue potential of INR 340 crore - INR 350 crore projected for FY28 after full ramp-up of the new plant and track expansion.
- Aiming to become a complete wagon manufacturing company in 3 years.
- Target to start exports to markets like the US or UK by next financial year (FY28).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted a brief slowdown in the wagon industry, but stated demand is recovering with the new 1-lakh wagon order rollout.
- Strong potential seen in coach and track segments, as well as thermal power plants and mining sectors.
- Indian Railways' focus remains high on development and maintenance leasing models.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material price increases are managed via Price Variation Clauses (PVC) in government/tender orders and price adjustments with private clients.
- There is a timing lag in price escalation pass-through, which management mitigates by continuously developing higher-margin new products.
- Operating with a lower tax rate of 17.5% (vs. standard 25%) under a lifetime central government scheme.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- INR 50 crore IPO proceeds utilized for the new bogie manufacturing facility (capable of handling products up to 1 ton; old plant handles <100 kg products).
- ~INR 10 crore remaining IPO funds for the bogie plant fully utilized for commissioning.
- Future capex for rubber/spring assembly lines and other railway segments will be funded through internal accruals.
- Raised ~INR 29 crore via preferential warrants primarily to fund working capital requirements for the track section (rail procurement and 2-month inventory holding).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains debt-free.
- Working capital needs temporarily increase in February/March due to exhaustion of Indian Railways' fund allocations, with collections sharply recovering starting April 1st.
- Trade receivables stood at INR 32 crore as of March 31, of which half was collected by April 15, leaving ~INR 20 crore pending.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Long and tedious multi-stage approval process (document scrutiny, prototype, field trials) acts as an entry barrier and can cause timelines (3 to 7 months) for new product clearances to vary.
- Potential margin pressure from new entrants is offset by continuous development of new higher-margin product lines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Paras Chheda (Purpleone Vertex Ventures) on Peak Revenue & Margins:** Management clarified peak revenue potential is INR 340-350 crore by next financial year and maintained the 25% PAT margin guidance for FY27.
2. **Vansh (3A Capital) on Warrant Issuance:** Management explained the ~INR 29 crore warrant raise is specifically to support the increased working capital cycle and raw material inventory (rails at INR 65-70/kg) for the track section.
3. **Shubham Padhiar (Analyst) on Working Capital/Receivables:** Management reiterated that high March receivables are due to Railways' yearly fund cycles and normalize significantly within April.
4. **Ketan R Chheda (Analyst) on Dilution vs Debt:** Challenged why equity dilution via warrants was used for working capital instead of debt. Management defended the move as a way to keep debt/interest costs at zero and mentioned promoters also participated.
5. **Vishal (Analyst) on Plant Capacity Bins:** Management clarified the old plant handles products under 100 kg (INR 110 crore revenue) while the new plant handles products up to 1 ton (INR 100 crore revenue).
6. **Rajesh Singla (Analyst) on FY28 Outlook:** Management reiterated expectations of reaching ~INR 350 crore revenue in FY28 with ~25% PAT margins.

### One-line takeaway
Neetu Yoshi Ltd delivered robust FY26 results (44% revenue growth, 53% PAT growth), boasts a debt-free balance sheet with a 17.5% tax rate, and guides for FY27 revenue of INR 210-220 crore at a 25% PAT margin as its new bogie and component manufacturing facility commences operations.
