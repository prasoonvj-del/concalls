# Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue growth was moderated due to a festive calendar shift (moving Pujo into Q3) and a subdued wedding season, impacting operating leverage and profitability.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained annual cash funding and capex expectations, with standalone cash projected to end the year around Rs 350-400 crore.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from stable/cautious to noticing strong green shoots in early festive demand (Onam and Pujo delivering double-digit LTL growth), though overall Q2 was weighed down by the calendar shift.
- New topics or risks: Explicit mention of a 4%–5% cost inflation impacting Pantaloons, leading to a potential 100 bps gross margin impact. TCNS turnaround remains challenging with low-single-digit LTL and continuing underperformance.
- Last quarter's promises: Store additions continued (35 stores added in Q2, over 80 in H1), and TMRW fundraise ($100 million to $150 million) is evaluated for the next 12 months.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,030 crore (up 2% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 67 crore.
- **Pantaloons Segment Growth:** 10% YoY on a festive-adjusted basis (6% reported).
- **Ethnic Portfolio Revenue:** Rs 517 crore (up 2% YoY; up 12% excluding TCNS).
- **Luxury Portfolio Growth:** 20% in the first half of the year (11% in Q2).
- **TMRW Growth:** 10% YoY (19% including WROGN).
- **OWND Growth:** 58% YoY.
- **Standalone Gross Cash:** Rs 628 crore (consolidated gross cash at Rs 1,143 crore).
- **Retail Network:** 1,268 stores spanning approximately 7.8 million square feet (added 35 stores in Q2, over 80 in H1).

### Highlights
- Festive shift moved Pujo and key celebrations into Q3, weighing on Q2 revenue and operating leverage.
- Emerging growth platforms continued to scale: ethnic (excluding TCNS) grew 12%, luxury grew 11%, and TMRW grew 10%.
- Pantaloons recorded strong Onam and Pujo festive performances with double-digit LTL growth.
- TCNS underwent network rationalization with ~60 underperforming stores closed over the past 12 months, impacting near-term reported revenue.
- Jaypore reported a further reduction in losses.
- TMRW improved profitability YoY for the fourth consecutive quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a stronger H2 driven by festive and wedding-led demand, historical seasonality, and working capital release.
- TMRW plans to raise capital ($100 million to $150 million) over the next 12 months.
- Pantaloons aims to push EBITDA margins past 20% over the next couple of years (currently close to 18% post-Ind AS).
- Standalone net cash balance is projected to end the year around Rs 350-400 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer demand trends in Q2 were affected by the festive shift and a subdued wedding season.
- Early festive trends (Onam and Pujo) showed strong double-digit same-store growth, while broader festive-to-date trends registered mid- to high-single-digit LTL growth.
- Women's western wear and fusion wear are currently outperforming as preferred consumer categories.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost inflation of 4%–5% is being experienced across businesses, with an estimated full-impact gross margin hit of ~100 bps for Pantaloons as unabsorbed costs flow through.
- Profitability declined due to the festive shift causing a loss of operating leverage and continued investments in newer businesses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 35 stores during Q2, taking H1 total additions to over 80 stores. Total network stands at 1,268 stores (7.8 million sq. ft.).
- Pantaloons network additions are expected to pick up trajectory from FY28 onwards (adding 30-40 stores annually).
- Tasva network stands at 92 stores; OWND stands at 90 stores; TMRW network (including WROGN) spans over 150 EBOs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated gross cash stood at Rs 1,143 crore; standalone gross cash at Rs 628 crore.
- Working capital is expected to release in H2 following the festive inventory buildup.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Festive calendar shift and subdued wedding season created temporary headwinds for Pantaloons and ethnic wear.
- TCNS continues to face near-term operational challenges and lagging growth, requiring ongoing store rationalization (~60 closures).
- Cost inflation pressures could impact gross margins if price hikes cannot be fully passed on.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ashish Kanodia (Citi) on cash flow and debt:** Management clarified that standalone cash is expected around Rs 350-400 crore by year-end, with TMRW evaluating a $100M-$150M fundraise in the next 12 months.
- **Garima Mishra (Kotak Securities) on Pantaloons strategy and growth:** Management stated that organic store additions will accelerate from FY28 onwards, while current focus remains on improving store productivity and digital integration (RFID).
- **Abhijeet Kundu (Antique Broking) on Pantaloons store additions:** Management noted that net store additions will be marginal (~20 stores) for the full year due to ongoing closures of older formats, but larger, higher-quality stores are being prioritized.
- **Archana Menon (Morgan Stanley) on TCNS performance:** Management explained that TCNS performance is currently lagging Pantaloons by 500-600 bps due to operational underperformance rather than seasonality, though old inventory liquidation is largely complete.
- **Kunal Bhatia (Kiesh) on Pantaloons organic growth vs industry:** Management stated that while overall growth appears lower due to calibrated expansion, same-store festive growth is at par with the industry best.

### One-line takeaway
ABFRL's Q2 performance was temporarily dampened by a festive calendar shift and subdued wedding demand, but management anticipates a strong rebound in H2 backed by green shoots in festive consumption and scaling emerging platforms.
