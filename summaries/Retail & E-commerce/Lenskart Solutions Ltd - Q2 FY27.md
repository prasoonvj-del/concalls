# Lenskart Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth, successful NPS recovery, and continued operational scaling.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (management reiterated intent to deliver last year's store addition pace).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "bouncing back" on NPS to "back at 79.6" (recovery achieved).
- **New topics:** Introduction of "Lenskart evolv" (new concept store), "Shunya" collection, "Drysh" (AI-assisted eye test pilot), and further details on "Hustlr Club."
- **Last quarter's promises:** NPS recovery (delivered), Hyderabad facility progress (on track).

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** ₹2,845 crore (+32.5% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Pre-Ind AS 116):** ₹624 crore (+46.6% YoY); Margin 13.8% (up from 11.1% YoY).
- **PAT:** ₹232 crore (more than doubled YoY).
- **India Revenue:** ₹1,668 crore (+35.3% YoY).
- **India EBITDA (Pre-Ind AS 116):** ₹267 crore (Margin 16%, up 260 bps YoY).
- **International Revenue:** ₹1,201 crore (+29.5% YoY).
- **International EBITDA (Pre-Ind AS 116):** ₹127 crore (Margin 10.6%, up from 7.3% YoY).
- **India SSSG:** 20.8%.
- **India Eye Tests:** 70 lakh (+44% YoY).
- **Stores:** 165 net new stores globally (Total: 3,624).

### Highlights
- **Trailing 12-month metrics:** Crossed ₹10,000 crore revenue, ₹2,200 crore EBITDA, ₹1,300 crore Pre-Ind AS EBITDA, and ~₹800 crore PAT.
- **NPS:** Recovered to 79.6 (from 77.6 in June).
- **Hustlr Club:** Crossed lakhs of members; ₹500 price point strategy gaining traction.
- **Lenskart Gold:** Crossed 1 crore active members (+39% YoY); ₹73 crore fees collected (+55% YoY).
- **Hyderabad facility:** Built for 50 million pairs/year capacity; ~12 months from commissioning.
- **Product launches:** John Jacobs (Masaba, Shantanu & Nikhil), Shunya collection, and Meller Diwali collection.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Margins:** Management aims for 25% EBITDA (Pre-Ind AS 116) margin long-term; no specific timeframe provided.
- **Strategy:** Growth remains the top priority; profitability is running ahead of expectations.
- **Store expansion:** Management intends to maintain the store addition pace of the previous year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Market Creation:** 94 crore Indians will require glasses by 2030; goal is 10,000+ stores.
- **Densification:** Same pin code growth (27.1%) is outperforming SSG (20.8%), indicating densification adds demand rather than dividing it.
- **International:** Myopia rates growing rapidly in SE Asia; strategy remains volume-led growth.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **India Product Margin:** 64.0% (vs 63.4% YoY).
- **International Product Margin:** 77.1% (vs 75.5% YoY).
- **Currency:** INR/RMB depreciation (₹14.2 vs ₹11-12 previously) is a headwind; offset by in-house manufacturing and scale.
- **India Rent:** Fell to 5.7% of revenue (from 6.1%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Store Capex (H1):** ₹187 crore for 297 net new stores.
- **Plant/Other Capex (H1):** ₹297 crore.
- **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** ₹388 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **ROCE:** 22.5% for H1 (vs 19% last year).
- **Inventory:** Rebuilt ahead of the festive season.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Currency:** Continued volatility in INR vs RMB.
- **Execution:** Maintaining NPS and culture while scaling rapidly.
- **Smart glasses:** Early stage, learning phase; not rushing to undercut on price.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay):** Asked about margin trajectory. Management: Year-on-year expansion expected, but advised looking at trailing 12-month, not quarterly.
- **Nihal Jham (HSBC):** Asked about ASP/Premiumization. Management: ASP increase driven by premium lens adoption (OWNDAYS), not active ASP management.
- **Mihir Shah (Nomura):** Asked about international growth sustainability. Management: Volume-led growth; too early to call 10.6% margin the "new normal."
- **Amit Purohit (Elara):** Asked about Lenskart Evolv. Management: Pilot project to raise brand aspiration/experience, not a strategy for higher ASP.
- **Arnab Mitra (Goldman Sachs):** Asked about AI eye tests. Management: "Drysh" pilot is a breakthrough to increase optometrist efficiency (20x potential).

### One-line takeaway
Lenskart delivered strong growth and margin expansion while successfully recovering NPS, driven by deep vertical integration and tech-led market creation.
