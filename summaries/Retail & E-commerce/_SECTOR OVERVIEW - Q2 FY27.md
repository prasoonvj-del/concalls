# Retail & E-commerce: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 reporting season highlighted a resilient sector navigating seasonal calendar shifts and input cost pressures. The primary swing factor in quarterly growth was the shift of major Indian festivals (Navratri, Durga Puja, Dussehra) and key wedding dates out of September (Q2) into October/November (Q3). This timing lag temporarily moderated Q2 reported revenues, primary distributor billings, and same-store sales growth (SSSG) across apparel and footwear brands. However, strong early Q3 festive momentum and high-single to double-digit festive Like-for-Like (LTL) metrics across companies indicate underlying consumer sentiment remains intact. 

Simultaneously, cost pressures emerged across raw material segments—specifically cotton, crude-oil derivatives, poultry, and dairy—prompting calibrated price increases (1.5% to 6%) across several retail and apparel portfolios. Expansion strategies remain bifurcated: value-focused multi-brand retailers and lifestyle apparel chains are aggressively shifting toward larger-format stores to improve throughput and customer experience, while digital e-commerce players are expanding quick-commerce fulfillment and leveraging artificial intelligence (AI) tools to reduce customer acquisition and operational costs.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far**  
Indian retail and e-commerce historically relied on multi-brand wholesale distributors, high street storefronts, and discount-driven online marketplaces. Over the past five years, the industry has shifted rapidly toward Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) channels, franchised asset-light networks, unified omnichannel inventory, and ultra-fast quick-commerce delivery nodes [Background].

**Shifts underway**  
*   **Rapid scaling of Quick Commerce fulfillment** · *Scaling* · Marketplaces and specialized retailers are expanding quick-commerce services to meet demand for sub-hour delivery. Brainbees Solutions scaled FirstCry Qwik to 16 cities; FSN E-Commerce Ventures expanded Nykaa Now (30-60 min delivery); Dollar Industries saw quick commerce sales jump 58.5% YoY; and Recode Studios partnered with Nykaa Now across 7 cities [Brainbees Solutions concall Q2 FY27; FSN E-Commerce concall Q2 FY27; Dollar Industries concall Q2 FY27; Recode Studios concall Q2 FY27]. This enables retailers to capture higher-frequency, impulse purchases.
*   **Upgrading to larger-format retail stores** · *Scaling* · Apparel and footwear retailers are phasing out smaller formats (<300-500 sq. ft.) in favor of larger flagship outlets (>750-1,200 sq. ft.) to drive higher sales per square foot and accommodate broader inventory collections. Kewal Kiran Clothing is phasing out K-Lounge for 1,000+ sq. ft. single-brand stores; Go Fashion closed 23 smaller stores while expanding >750 sq. ft. formats; and Credo Brands is rolling out its larger Mufti 2.0 store identity [Kewal Kiran Clothing concall Q2 FY27; Go Fashion concall Q2 FY27; Credo Brands concall Q2 FY27]. Larger footprints improve visual merchandising and customer conversion.
*   **AI integration for operational efficiency** · *Early* · E-commerce enablement platforms and direct platforms are deploying AI tools to lower customer acquisition costs (CAC), accelerate cataloging, and automate customer service. AceVector (Snapdeal/Unicommerce) achieved a 90% drop in catalog onboarding time via SnapList AI and handled 89% of queries via SIA AI; Shiprocket launched Aitlas and Hydra AI tools; and Vedant Fashions deployed AI store cameras to track real-time customer conversion rates [AceVector concall Q2 FY27; Shiprocket concall Q2 FY27; Vedant Fashions concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**  
*   **24-Hour Retail Approval** · The Goa government allowed shops and commercial malls to operate 24 hours a day [Govt: Goa state notification, Nov 2026]. This benefits regional retail operators and food service providers through extended trading hours and asset utilization.
*   **Labor Code and Wage Revisions** · State-level minimum wage increases in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Kerala led to employee cost inflation for multi-store operators [Devyani International concall Q2 FY27; Westlife Foodworld concall Q2 FY27].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Where they agree:** Managements broadly agree that underlying consumer demand remains healthy, particularly in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. Despite a softer Q2 due to the festive calendar shift, demand during October and the onset of the wedding season showed a strong rebound. Premiumization continues to outpace volume growth across categories including electronics (Electronics Mart India), beauty (FSN E-Commerce Ventures), and luxury luxury apparel (Purple Style Labs).

**Where they diverge:** Views split on entry-level volume trends. Mass-market and value retailers (V-Mart Retail, Page Industries, Dollar Industries) reported volume softness or trading-down pressures among lower-income consumer cohorts due to food and general inflation. Conversely, quick-service restaurant (QSR) chains diverged on channel traction: Westlife Foodworld and Sapphire Foods (KFC) reported strong double-digit dine-in and transaction volume growth driven by value meal platforms, whereas Page Industries and Go Fashion noted extended channel inventory digestion periods ahead of the festive season.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Textiles & Apparel:** Raw material prices—particularly cotton yarn and crude-derived synthetic fabrics—inflated by 5% to 10% YoY. Companies like Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands, Arvind Fashions, and Dollar Industries passed on these costs via selective 1.5% to 6% price increases. Gross margins contracted temporarily for Page Industries (down 280 bps YoY) and Go Fashion (down 200 bps) due to high-cost inventory liquidation.
*   **QSR & Food Services:** Poultry, dairy, cheese, and LPG costs experienced inflation. Devyani International and Jubilant FoodWorks absorbed 140–200 bps of input headwinds, counterbalancing them through calibrated 1.5%–2% price hikes, discount optimizations, and long-term cheese procurement contracts.
*   **Electronics & General Retail:** Electronics Mart India leveraged extended summer air conditioner sales and price escalations in premium mobile devices to expand gross margins by 130 bps YoY to 15.4%. Redington experienced lower gross margins on large GPU server deals but expanded operating leverage.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Store Network Expansion:** Retailers maintained aggressive brick-and-mortar rollouts. Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands added over 100 stores in Q2; Lenskart added 165 net new stores globally; Electronics Mart India is entering West Bengal with 15 planned stores; and SS Retail is targeting 750 stores by FY28.
*   **Logistics & Manufacturing:** Arvind Fashions is establishing an in-house shirt manufacturing facility in Madhya Pradesh (3 lakh units/month capacity) to mitigate Bangladesh sourcing risks and currency volatility. Om Galaxy is expanding its tooling capacity by 2-3x to 3,100 MTPA by mid-2027. Recode Studios and AceVector (Snapdeal) maintain strict asset-light models, utilizing third-party logistics (3PL) and contract manufacturing.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance updates:** Landmark Cars raised its annual capex guidance from INR 50 crore to INR 80 crore to support new OEM facilities (JSW Motors, Denza). Flair Writing revised its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance downward from 15% to 12%+ following temporary flood-related factory disruptions in Gujarat.
*   **Demand tone shifts:** Popular Vehicles & Services, Credo Brands, and Redtape shifted from a cautious Q1 stance to positive momentum, citing strong October dispatches and festive pull-through. Conversely, Page Industries and Go Fashion noted temporary order fulfillment disruptions and raw material cost lags.
