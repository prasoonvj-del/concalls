# Retail & E-commerce: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season across the Retail & E-commerce sector reflected a resilient operating environment tempered by two major headwinds: a calendar shift of the festive season (Dussehra/Navratri) into Q3, and unseasonal weather/monsoons leading to localized flooding (particularly in Vapi and Daman, impacting manufacturing for Flair Writing and Kewal Kiran). Despite these challenges, managements across apparel, QSRs, electronics, and quick commerce reported solid volume-led growth, margin stabilization through disciplined discounting, and aggressive store expansions. Premiumization and direct-to-consumer (D2C) channels continued to outperform, while quick commerce players like Eternal (Blinkit), Swiggy (Instamart), and Meesho scaled operational efficiencies and expanded into tier-2 cities.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Almost all consumer-facing and retail managements (including Aditya Birla, Arvind Fashions, EMIL, Devyani, Sapphire Foods, V-Mart, Vishal Mega Mart, and Westlife) agreed that Q2 revenue growth was artificially suppressed by 3% to 8% due to the festive season shifting from September to October. However, October trends confirmed a robust rebound in festive and wedding demand.
*   **Divergence:** Discretionary demand showed a K-shaped bifurcation. Mass-market and value apparel players like Go Fashion and Monte Carlo reported flat-to-negative SSSG due to inflationary pressures squeezing household budgets and localized competition. Conversely, premium and luxury players (FSN E-Commerce/Nykaa, Purple Style Labs, Lenskart) and value-driven QSRs (Westlife, Sapphire, United Foodbrands, Restaurant Brands Asia) reported strong transaction-led growth, higher average order values (AOVs), and double-digit guest count expansions.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Input Cost Inflation:** Managements across sectors highlighted pressures from rising raw material costs (cotton, pure wool, fabrics, poultry, cheese, and crude-linked packaging) and sharp minimum wage hikes (notably in Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana). Flair Writing noted margin hits from raw material volatility, while Devyani and Sapphire flagged steep poultry and LPG inflation.
*   **Mitigation Strategies:** Companies adopted distinct pricing and cost levers. 
    *   *Price Hikes:* Aditya Birla took a 6% price hike; Arvind Fashions, Sapphire, Devyani, and Westlife took calibrated 1% to 4% increases.
    *   *Discount Rationalization:* Arvind Fashions, Kewal Kiran, and Westlife protected gross margins by cutting back unnecessary market discounting rather than raising MRPs.
    *   *Operational Efficiencies:* Meesho and ArisInfra leveraged logistics redesign and technology (Valmo, AI tools) to lower cost-to-serve, while Eternal and Swiggy focused on density and operating leverage in quick commerce.

## Capex & capacity & expansion
*   Physical retail expansion accelerated notably in Q2. Aditya Birla added over 100 stores, Lenskart added 165 net global stores, Vishal Mega Mart added 26 new stores, and Westlife opened 11 new restaurants. 
*   In quick commerce, Eternal (Blinkit) and Swiggy (Instamart) announced accelerated store/dark store rollouts targeting tier-2 cities with rapid payback models.
*   Manufacturing backward integration was a key theme: Arvind Fashions invested in an in-house shirt manufacturing facility in Madhya Pradesh to de-risk imports, while Raymond advanced its Andhra Pradesh greenfield aerospace and auto facility.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Cuts & Revisions:** Flair Writing revised its annual revenue growth guidance down to 12% (from 15%) following flood-related manufacturing losses. Most other players (EMIL, Lenskart, Arvind Fashions, Sapphire) maintained their growth guidance.
*   **Tone Shifts:** Go Fashion and Monte Carlo shifted to a more cautious tone regarding mass-market volume recovery, while QSRs (Westlife, Sapphire, RBA) and quick commerce players turned notably more bullish as logistics bottlenecks eased and festive demand materialized.
*   **Operational Milestones:** Lenskart successfully recovered its Net Promoter Score (NPS) to 79.6; FSN E-Commerce expanded its House of Nykaa portfolio via the 'Simply Nam' acquisition.

## What to watch next quarter
1. **Festive and Wedding Season Execution:** Evaluating whether October-November sales (Q3) fully recover the revenue deferred from Q2 due to the calendar shift.
2. **Raw Material & Wage Inflation Passthrough:** Monitoring whether companies can successfully defend gross margins through price hikes and discount cuts without hurting volume growth.
3. **Quick Commerce Unit Economics:** Tracking the margin trajectory and contribution profit improvements of Blinkit, Instamart, and quick-delivery pilots as they scale store density in tier-2 cities.
4. **QSR Recovery in International Markets:** Progress on the asset upgrade program in Burger King Indonesia (RBA) and the resolution of Popeyes performance drag.
