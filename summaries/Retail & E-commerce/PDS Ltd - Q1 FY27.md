# PDS Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Good momentum with 15% revenue growth, 90% EBITDA growth, and significant improvements in working capital and net debt.

### Key numbers
- **GMV:** ₹5,146 crores, up 11% YoY
- **Revenue:** ₹3,444 crores, up 15% YoY (North America sales grew 48%)
- **Gross Profit:** ₹690 crores, up 18.5% YoY
- **Gross Margin:** 20%, up 63 bps YoY
- **EBITDA:** ₹96 crores, up 90% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 2.8%, up 111 bps YoY
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** ₹29 crores, up 43% YoY
- **Order Book:** ₹6,095 crores, up 23% YoY
- **Net Working Capital:** Reduced to 1 day (from 4 days at March 2026)
- **Net Debt:** ₹29 crores, reduced by 73% during the quarter
- **Reported ROCE:** 19% (ROCE excluding new verticals: 26%)

### Highlights
- Signed new mandates from marquee customers representing an annual business potential of approximately US $330 million (3 to 4-year horizon).
- Partnered with Busana Apparel Group to enhance manufacturing capabilities and upgrade customer/margin profiles.
- Initiated AI and digital transformation (including upgrading SAP platform to S/4HANA) to drive productivity and scale without proportional cost increases.
- ESG score improved to 62 (categorized as 'strong').
- Sourcing segment revenue at ₹3,272 crores (up 16% YoY) with EBIT of ₹58 crores (margin 1.8%). Manufacturing segment revenue at ₹227 crores (up 4% YoY) with EBIT of ₹15 crores (margin 6.5%).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is committed to having Q2 reflect similar positive trends before revisiting any broader guidance mid-year.
- For the full year, total P&L impact of new initiatives/investments is expected to remain broadly in line with previous expectations.
- Ted Baker losses for the full year are expected not to exceed $2–$3 million.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural shifts in the global apparel and retail industry favor PDS due to demand for simpler, more diversified, and cost-efficient supply chains.
- U.S. market scaling up rapidly as key accounts opened in prior years move past trial orders to higher volumes.
- European growth driven by accounts in Spain (Mango), Eastern/Southern Europe (CCC), and replacement accounts in Germany.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee expenses in USD decreased 2.4% YoY and 6.2% QoQ due to closures of loss-making verticals, offset by bonus payouts and merit increments.
- Other expenses in dollar terms increased by only 1.5%.
- Finance costs were ₹37 crores (up 11% YoY in INR terms, flat in USD terms), influenced by factoring lines and early payment discounts (EPD).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total investments in new verticals reduced by 8% YoY to ₹37 crores during the quarter (brand management down 38%, design-led sourcing down 5%).
- PDS Ventures investments to be restricted to under ₹10 crores a year ($1 million to $1.5 million).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash generated from operations stood at ₹151 crores.
- Net debt to equity at 0.02x; net debt to EBITDA at 0.07x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Ted Baker UK/US retail franchise operations went into administration, impacting the agency business and resulting in a Q1 PBT loss of approximately $2 million.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital) on order book vs. guidance:** Management stated they started the year cautiously, and while Q1 was strong, they prefer to focus on delivering another good quarter before revisiting guidance mid-year.
2. **Samvit Patel (DT! Partners) on AI impact:** Management explained AI is used for productivity enhancement (e.g., design and order management) allowing higher volumes with flat headcount rather than adding manpower costs.
3. **Ritika Sheth (Anantaya Wealth Advisors) on the 555 strategy:** Management reaffirmed their belief in the long-term goal, noting past headwinds (Gerry Weber, Matalan) were successfully resolved without delinquencies, and focus remains on sequential margin and PAT improvements.
4. **Neel Chhabra on competitive risk from retailers bypassing PDS:** Management noted factories are a commodity and retailers value PDS's professional management, compliance, and multi-vendor consolidation platform to reduce lead times.
5. **Komal on Ted Baker losses and brand management:** Management clarified Q1 PBT loss for Ted Baker was ~$2 million, with full-year losses expected capped at $2–$3 million, while other brand management businesses generate profitable revenue of ~₹350 crores annually.

### One-line takeaway
PDS Ltd delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with 15% revenue growth, a 90% jump in EBITDA, and sharply lower net debt, driven by traction in North America and improved operational efficiencies.
