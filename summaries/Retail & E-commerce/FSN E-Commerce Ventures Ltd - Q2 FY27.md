# FSN E-Commerce Ventures Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and profit growth with margin expansion across verticals despite the festive season shifting to Q3.

### Key numbers
- **GMV:** INR 6,151 crore (30% YoY)
- **Net Revenue:** INR 3,006 crore (28% YoY)
- **Gross Profit:** INR 1,382 crore (31% YoY); Margin at 46%
- **EBITDA:** INR 263 crore (65% YoY); Margin at 8.7% (up 196 bps YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 101 crore (207% YoY); Margin at 3.4%
- **Beauty NSV:** INR 4,400+ crore (GMV up 25%, NSV up 28% YoY)
- **Fashion NSV:** INR 509 crore (47% YoY)
- **House of Nykaa Annualized GMV:** INR 4,000 crore; NSV INR 2,300 crore (up 36% YoY)
- **Superstore NSV Growth:** 28% YoY
- **ROCE:** 27.3% (H1 FY27)

### Highlights
- **Acquisitions & Partnerships:** House of Nykaa acquired an 82% stake in 'Simply Nam' (founded by Namrata Soni) for INR 33 crore. Partnered with L'Oreal to launch "Nykaa BOLD" to invest in high-growth Indian BPC brands.
- **Global Brand Launches:** Launched 330+ beauty brands and 1,700+ fashion brands in the last year, including exclusive launches like Yves Rocher, Oribe, Mamonde, COS, and Superdry.
- **Nykaa Fashion Luxe:** Launched a separate dedicated luxury shop-in-shop experience featuring global brands like Prada, Fendi, and Valentino.
- **Nykaa Now:** Rapidly scaling quick-delivery service offering 30-60 minute delivery with over 1,000 marquee brands.
- **Events:** Hosted the 4th edition of Nykaaland in Delhi with 30,000 attendees and 150 million social media reach.
- **Retail Expansion:** Added 73 stores in the last year, taking total beauty stores to 338 across 111 cities. Same-store sales growth (LFL) for retail was over 20%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains optimistic about Q3 (festive season).
- Long-term guidance maintained: Fashion aims for high single-digit EBITDA margins by FY30 and 10%+ in steady state. Beauty retail store network targeted to reach ~600 stores over the next few years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Beauty category sees ongoing brand proliferation globally and in India, benefiting Nykaa as a retailer of choice.
- Premiumization trend continues to drive Average Order Value (AOV) higher across both online and retail channels, rather than inflation.
- Fashion customer acquisition cost (CAC) decreased 30% YoY while new customer growth grew 31%, aided by a broader assortment and affluent customer targeting via device types (iOS/premium Android).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated gross margin expanded 106 bps YoY to 46%, supported by House of Nykaa contribution and marketing income.
- Fulfillment expenses increased by 47 bps due to infrastructure expansion for O2D efficiencies.
- Marketing and S&D expenses improved by 50 bps YoY to 15.2% of revenue.
- Employee and other expenses improved by 87 bps YoY to 11.9% of revenue due to scale efficiencies.
- Fashion vertical reached EBITDA breakeven at INR 1 crore (0.2% margin) for the third successive quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex remains steady at about 1% of revenue, deployed toward retail store expansion, fulfillment capabilities, and tech.
- Fixed asset turnover increased to 10.8x in H1 FY27 (from 9.9x in FY26).
- Superstore expanded to 1,200 cities with 0.5 million onboarded retailers, while BDE headcount remained stable at 2,300.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital days remained stable at 28 days in H1 FY27.
- Operating cash flows were negative in H1 FY27 due to inventory buildup for the festive season (falling in Q3 this year vs. Q2 last year), expected to normalize by end of Q3.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management, aside from industry-wide brand proliferation dynamics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kapil Singh (Nomura/Instinet):** Asked about AI shopping agents bypassing Nykaa and brand proliferation risks. *Management answered that Nykaa's moats (large/unique assortment, fast delivery, high-quality advice) protect discovery, and brand proliferation actually benefits retailers.*
- **Aditya Soman (Morgan Stanley):** Inquired about LFL retail growth drivers and the impact of H&M/Nike on fashion customer acquisition. *Management clarified that LFL is driven by premiumization and assortment momentum, while marquee brands like H&M and Nike boost overall platform pull and lower CAC.*
- **Sachin Salgaonkar (BofA Securities):** Questioned ad revenues as a % of GMV and if Fashion margin guidance (FY30) could be achieved earlier given current hockey-stick growth. *Management stated that ad platform capabilities are improving, but long-term guidance remains disciplined as repeat customer cohorts take time to mature in fashion.*
- **Swapnil Potdukhe (JM Financial):** Asked about Q3 festive slowdown trends and negative operating cash flows. *Management noted it is too early to judge the full festive quarter as big sales occur in late November, and cash flow reflects temporary festive inventory buildup.*

### One-line takeaway
FSN E-Commerce Ventures delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with 28% revenue growth, a 65% jump in EBITDA, and continued margin expansion across both beauty and fashion verticals.
