# Electronics Mart India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strongest quarter to date with record revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth, driven by a robust summer season and market share gains.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 2,419 crore (up 39% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 239 crore (up 118% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 9.9% (vs 6.3% in Q1 FY26).
*   **PAT:** Rs 121 crore (up 458% YoY).
*   **SSSG:** 34.2%.
*   **Working Capital Days:** 42 days (down from 73 days in March 2026).
*   **Working Capital Borrowings:** Rs 97 crore (down from Rs 658 crore at the start of the quarter).

### Highlights
*   **Seasonal Performance:** Air conditioners (ACs) had their best quarter in both volume and value.
*   **Revenue Mix:** Large appliances contributed 48% of revenue; Mobile phones contributed 39%.
*   **Cluster Performance:** South cluster (40% revenue growth) remains the core engine with a 10.9% EBITDA margin. North cluster recorded 29% revenue growth and a 4.9% EBITDA margin.
*   **Store Maturity:** Company operates 96 mature stores (>4 years) and 131 non-mature stores (<4 years). Non-mature stores delivered an 8.1% EBITDA margin.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Revenue:** Management targets 18-20% revenue growth for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management targets a gross margin of 15-15.5% and a post-Ind AS EBITDA margin of 7.5-8% for FY27.
*   **Interest Cost:** Expected to be Rs 10 crore lower than the previous year.
*   **Expansion:** Entering West Bengal with 5 stores by Diwali and 10-12 stores by March 31, 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted a trend of unorganized players exiting, with organized retail gaining market share.
*   South cluster outperformed; Tamil Nadu was the top market for cooling products.
*   Management expects the festive period (Q3 FY27) to be strong.
*   No major volume challenges in mobile/IT hardware; the company pre-plans inventory to avoid stock-outs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin:** Expanded to 17.2% (vs 14.6% YoY), driven by the cooling product mix and price escalation advantages in IT products.
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Management clarified that IT product margin gains are temporary/periodic and not a permanent increase.
*   **Operational Costs:** Store-level costs (rent, manpower, marketing) are benchmarked under 8-10% of revenue.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capex:** Plan to open 25-30 stores in FY27. Total capex guidance is ~Rs 100 crore for new stores plus ~Rs 50 crore for property acquisition in Kolkata.
*   **Funding:** Expansion is funded via internal accruals.
*   **Franchising:** Management stated they are flexible but currently see no significant opportunity or success in the large-format appliance space for franchising; focus remains on company-owned, company-operated stores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Cash Flow:** Pre-Ind AS operating cash flows were Rs 631 crore; post-Ind AS cash flows were Rs 671 crore.
*   **Returns:** ROCE stood at 20.1% (TTM); ROE stood at 11.9% (TTM).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Market Volatility:** North cluster market performance for cooling products was negative this year, though EMIL grew due to a low base.
*   **Margin Volatility:** Management cautioned that IT product margin benefits are volatile and dependent on price fluctuations.
*   **Seasonality:** Performance is heavily dependent on the summer season (Q1) and the festive season (Q3/Q4).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Is 18-20% revenue growth guidance too conservative given Q1 performance?* A: Management maintains it is achievable, noting Q1 was strong due to seasonality and they prefer to be conservative.
*   *Q: Why did mobile mix decline?* A: Management explained it is a seasonal shift; cooling products dominate Q1, while other quarters will see a more balanced mix.
*   *Q: Why is North SSG low?* A: Management attributed it to the overall North market de-growing for cooling products this year.
*   *Q: What is the payback period for new stores?* A: ~10-11 months in South; ~16-18 months in North.
*   *Q: Are you open to franchising?* A: Management is flexible but currently sees no significant opportunity/success in the large-format appliance space for franchising.

### One-line takeaway
EMIL delivered a record-breaking quarter driven by strong summer seasonality and market share gains, with management maintaining a disciplined, internally-funded expansion strategy.
