# RBZ Jewellers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong broad-based revenue growth, robust retail traction, and successful execution of store expansion despite short-term margin pressures from promotional and inventory-carrying costs.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 225 crore, up 55% YoY
- EBITDA: Rs 29 crore, up 3.5% YoY; EBITDA margin: 13%
- PAT: Rs 16 crore; PAT margin: 7.3%
- Segment growth: Retail up 72% YoY to Rs 148 crore; Wholesale up 31% YoY; Job Work up ~19% YoY to ~Rs 3.3 crore
- Volume split (Q2): Retail 96 kg, Wholesale 47 kg, Job Work 219 kg; Total volume: 363.58 kg
- Surat store average sales: ~Rs 50 lakh/day in October
- Surat average ticket size: Rs 3.5 lakh to Rs 4 lakh
- Gold Metal Loan (GML) exposure: ~Rs 14-15 crore (~14-15 kg)
- Marketing expense budget (FY27): Rs 20 crore to Rs 25 crore

### Highlights
- Opened a new large-format retail store in Surat in late September 2026, inaugurated by actor Vicky Kaushal.
- Participated in 5 major jewellery exhibitions and trade shows across Saurashtra, North Gujarat, and Ahmedabad.
- Shifted inventory from B2B to B2C to support retail store openings, involving bullion sales used for vendor payments.
- Maintained a strict zero-cash transaction policy at flagship retail brand Harit Zaveri Jewellers.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to scale GML to Rs 100 crore - Rs 150 crore (100+ kg) within the next 6 months (by Q1 FY28).
- Capex breakeven for new large-format stores targeted within a maximum of 1 year; mature store inventory turnover expected to reach 2.5x.
- Planned store openings: Rajkot between November 21 and 25, 2026; Maninagar in December 2026; Gandhinagar in January 2027 (total 4 store additions in FY27).
- Long-term vision (by 2030) to be among the leading retail brands in Gujarat and the West zone, adding 3-4 stores annually.
- Consolidated EBITDA margin sustainability guided at 11% to 12%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Festival and wedding demand (Navratri/Shraadh period) showed a warm and optimistic response, particularly in Surat.
- Gold price volatility causes temporary consumer hesitation for about a week before behavior normalizes.
- Shift in festive calendar (Navratri falling later in the year compared to last year) impacted delivery timelines and volumes slightly in Q2, with October showing complete normalcy.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins impacted by a higher proportion of bullion sales used for vendor payments following B2B-to-B2C stock transfers, alongside negligible inventory gains due to flat/depreciating gold prices.
- Finance costs rose from Rs 2.55 crore to Rs 5.54 crore due to inventory carrying costs for the Surat store buildup.
- Depreciation increased ~2.5x–3x from Rs 86 lakh to Rs 1.7 crore.
- One-time promotion and marketing expenses stood at Rs 5 crore to Rs 6 crore for the Surat launch.
- Retail gross margins range around 14% to 16% (depending on studded ratios).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Surat store inventory size is typically ~Rs 150 crore; store capex is Rs 10 crore to Rs 15 crore.
- Rajkot store will be a 3-storey, larger format store (a few thousand square feet larger than Surat).
- Manufacturing facility features advanced casting, laser, and 3D-printed technologies with 200+ professionals and ~250 skilled artisans.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital being optimized by increasing GML share (carrying lower interest of ~3.5%-4% vs. CC limits at ~9%) to hedge unhedged inventory down to near-zero over the next 2 to 2.5 years.
- GML targeted to allow higher gearing (up to 2:1 debt ratio) to fund further store expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Short-term margin dilution from new store pre-opening/launch marketing expenses, higher interest carrying costs, and bullion sales.
- Gold price volatility leading to temporary pauses in customer decision-making.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Shikhar (Vivog Commercial):** Asked about reasons for gross and EBITDA margin drops. Management explained it was due to bullion sales for vendor payments (zero-profit transfer from B2B to B2C), higher finance costs (inventory carrying for Surat), higher depreciation, and Rs 5-6 crore in one-time promotional expenses.
- **Yash (Viansh Ventures):** Inquired about store breakeven metrics and inventory turns. Management stated capex breakeven within 1 year, year-1 inventory turns of 1.2x, and mature store turns of 2-2.5x.
- **Deepesh (Maanya Finance):** Questioned the GML ramp-up and debt utilization. Management confirmed plans to reach Rs 100-150 crore in GML within 6 months, using freed-up capital to cycle through GML and fund additional store expansion at lower interest rates.
- **Ruchit Shah (Individual Investor):** Asked about volume dips in wholesale/job work. Management clarified it was purely due to the shift in the Navratri calendar (delay by ~20 days), with October operations returning to complete normalcy.

### One-line takeaway
RBZ Jewellers delivered a 55% YoY revenue jump driven by robust retail demand, even as profitability faced near-term headwinds from one-time store launch costs and inventory-carrying expenses.
