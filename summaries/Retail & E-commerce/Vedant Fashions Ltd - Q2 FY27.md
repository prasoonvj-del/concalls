# Vedant Fashions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue growth was subdued due to a festive calendar shift and the Shradh period falling in late Q2, though management expressed confidence in a strong H2.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained a focus on delivering strong long-term growth and high single-digit SSG, with no explicit numerical annual guidance provided (same as last quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from optimistic about Q2 to cautious regarding the muted Q2 performance (sales up 0.6% YoY to customers, revenue up 2.4% to INR 269 crores), while remaining confident about H2 (November-January heavy) based on pre-Diwali trends (D minus 30 days).
- New topics or risks: Detailed discussion on AI camera tracking for store conversion rates, the viral Twamev "Orry" campaign, and observation of competitor consolidation/store closures due to dead stock pain.
- Last quarter's promises: Frontloaded store closures were executed (net addition of 5,000 sq ft in Q2), and six new collections were readied for Q3 launch as promised.

### Key numbers
- Customer sales (sales of our customers): INR 3,515 million (up 0.6% YoY)
- H1 customer sales: INR 7,710 million (up 2.1% YoY)
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): INR 269 crores (up 2.4% YoY)
- Profit after tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27): INR 56 crores (up 0.2% YoY)
- H1 FY27 revenue from operations: INR 571 crores (up 4.8% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: INR 137 crores (up 8.3% YoY)
- Gross margin (H1 FY27): 65.3%
- EBITDA margin (Q2 FY27): 39% (post-Ind AS)
- Cash conversion ratio (Trailing 12 months ended September 2026): approx. 93% (operating cash flow over PAT excluding finance income)
- EBO count (as on September 30, 2026): 497 EBOs spanning 1.67 million sq ft (vs 508 EBOs and 1.67 million sq ft in Q2 FY26)
- Mohey EBO count: 6 to 7 stores; Mohey revenue salience: just above double digits
- Net retail area addition (Q2 FY27): net addition of about 5,000 square feet

### Highlights
- Q2 performance impacted by Navratri moving from September in FY26 to Q3 in FY27, replaced by the last 10 days of Q2 being the Shradh period.
- Launched the Manyavar Celebration Club loyalty program offering 1% points to drive repeat purchases.
- Deployed AI cameras across stores to track conversion rates and drive targeted store-level interventions (training, manager review, category management).
- Marketing campaigns scaled: Twamev's "Truly You" campaign featuring Orry surpassed 700 million views; Manyavar launched a quiet luxury campaign, "After Hours" collection, and short-format AI-optimized Meta/Google ads (150 short videos); Mohey ran "Made for Momentum"; Diwas partnered with Flipkart Fashion ("Bua Bouncer").
- MBO business achieved 100% of its budget for Q2 with high double-digit growth expectations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident in delivering strong H2 numbers driven by November, December, and January wedding seasons.
- Targets a long-term high single-digit SSG.
- Expects mid-single-digit fleet area additions for the current financial year and next year, eyeing early double-digit store openings by FY 2029.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pre-Diwali trends (D minus 30 days comparable period) show green shoots.
- Local and regional competitors are facing severe dead stock pain and P&L losses, leading to net store consolidation and closures over the past two quarters.
- Twamev is targeted at 30 best stores in top 30 markets; Diwas is on track to cross INR 100 crores this year.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industry-leading gross margin of 65.3% maintained for H1 FY27.
- Q2 EBITDA margin reported at 39% (post-Ind AS), impacted by operating deleverage due to lower seasonal revenue. Management expects to make up margin streams in H2.
- Internal inventory replenishment algorithms updated to double design breadth over two and a half years without increasing dead stock levels.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Net addition of 5,000 sq ft of retail area in Q2 after phased expansions and strategic closures.
- Fleet area growth guided at mid-single digits for FY27 and next year, moving to early double digits in FY 2029.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Healthy cash conversion ratio of 93% for the trailing 12-month period ended September 2026.
- Other current assets increased from INR 56 crores to INR 256 crores due to redemption from liquid mutual funds on September 30, with funds credited to the bank account on October 1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Muted H1 growth due to festive calendar shifts (Navratri moving to Q3, Shradh period in late Q2).
- Dead stock challenges inherent to the men's celebration wear industry affecting unorganized/new regional players.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Prerna (DAM Capital) on full-year SSG and October trends:** Management stated D minus 30 days pre-Diwali metrics show green shoots and targets high single-digit SSG over the long term.
- **Rishi Mody (Mody Advisory) on AI camera usage and marketing mix:** Management explained AI cameras track store conversions against city/state averages to trigger targeted store interventions; differentiated Manyavar's mass awareness vs. Twamev's nascent brand-building campaigns.
- **Aashish (InvesQ) on competition consolidation:** Management noted regional competitors are facing losses and dead stock pain, leading to net store closures over the last two quarters.
- **Sameer Gupta (IIFL Capital) on increased design breadth and dead stock risk:** Management clarified that core inventory replenishment algorithms and SOPs were redesigned over 6-7 months to double design breadth without increasing dead stock.
- **Rahul Agarwal (Ikigai Asset Manager) on 3-4 year vision:** Management reiterated the 10-year vision to own Indian wear, focusing on menswear price tiers, scaling Mohey women's celebration wear, and potential M&A in Indian wear.
- **Yatin (ICICI Securities) on other current assets surge:** Management explained the rise from INR 56 crores to INR 256 crores represents liquid mutual fund redemptions executed on September 30, received in the bank on October 1.

### One-line takeaway
Vedant Fashions reported subdued Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 2.4% due to festive calendar shifts, but management remains confident of a strong recovery in H2 backed by aggressive marketing, new collections, and industry-wide competitor consolidation.
