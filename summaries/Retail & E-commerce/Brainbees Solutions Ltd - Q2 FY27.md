# Brainbees Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 14.1% YoY (strongest in 6 quarters) and net losses narrowed by ~75% YoY, signaling successful operational consolidation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (management expects growth momentum to continue in subsequent quarters).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "heightened competitive intensity" in diapering (Q1) to "starting to soften" in Q2.
- **New topics or risks:** The IPO process for the manufacturing arm, "Swara," is now a primary strategic focus.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Store expansion (paused last year) has officially restarted; 50-60 stores planned for H2 FY27.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,395 crore (+14.1% YoY).
- **Consolidated Net Loss:** Rs 12.6 crore (narrowed from Rs 50.5 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **India Multichannel Revenue Growth:** 18.6% YoY (strongest in 8 quarters).
- **India Multichannel Adjusted EBITDA:** 6.9% (up 120 bps QoQ).
- **Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA:** Rs 117 crore (margin 4.9%).
- **AUTC:** 12.1 million (+10% YoY).
- **GMV:** Rs 3,077 crore (+9.2% YoY).

### Highlights
- **India Multichannel:** Offline GMV grew 13% despite 10 fewer stores, driven by product assortment strategy.
- **RocketBees:** Expanded to 102 cities (from 13 in Q2 FY26), covering 60% of online shipments with 20% faster delivery.
- **FC Qwik:** Expanded to 16 cities; management aims for 10% of total online volume to be serviced via Qwik.
- **Swara (Manufacturing):** Management confirmed the IPO process is underway; the entity will remain a consolidated subsidiary.
- **Preschools:** Network reached 512 schools (added ~80 in the last 12 months).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects growth momentum to continue in both online and offline businesses.
- Expects gross margin recovery in Q3 and Q4 as crude-linked raw material pressures stabilize.
- GlobalBees expects growth recovery in Q3 following the completion of warehouse/brand rationalization.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Diapering competitive intensity has started to soften.
- Festive shift (Dussehra moved 3 weeks later to October) impacted Q2 GMV; management expects Q3 to be stronger.
- Non-diapering portfolio (85% of GMV) remains robust.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Direct costs dropped 100 bps QoQ (India Multichannel) due to RocketBees reverse pickup efficiencies (cost is one-third of third-party logistics).
- Gross margin compression (crude-linked) persists but is stabilizing; management expects to pass on costs.
- Marketing spend: Absolute amount increasing, but % of revenue declining due to efficiency.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex: Rs 2,000-2,200 per sq ft for new stores.
- Target: 90-100 stores annually for the next 2-3 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital: 61 days (down from 67).
- Inventory: 105 days (up from 99 due to festive shift).
- Other current assets: Increased due to GST receivables and capex advances for Swara expansion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East.
- Crude-linked raw material price volatility.
- Competitive intensity in quick commerce (though softening).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Adithya Srinivasan (IThought PMS) on Swara IPO:** Management noted that while disclosure is restricted due to the RHP process, they expect the manufacturing business to be a listed company soon.
2. **Ranodeep S on Swara IPO rationale:** Management stated the goal is to unlock value, provide independent access to capital, and create a focused strategy for the hygiene category.
3. **Avnish Sharma on Globalbees:** Management confirmed rationalization is complete; Q3 should show stronger growth and improved margins.
4. **Shashank Pore on Kuber Mart litigation:** Management clarified that the NCLT rejected the bankruptcy application; no additional liability is expected.
5. **Garima Mishra on store expansion:** Management confirmed the expansion engine has restarted, with 50-60 stores planned for the remainder of FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Brainbees achieved its strongest revenue growth in 6 quarters (14.1% YoY) and a 75% reduction in net losses, signaling a successful pivot toward operational efficiency and renewed store expansion.
