# Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Broadly stable demand and steady growth in established businesses were offset by a muted quarter in ethnic wear (impacted by Adhik Maas and fewer wedding dates) and continued investment-led losses in newer platforms.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 2,026 crore (up 11% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 167 crore (margin of 8.2%), lower YoY due to lower treasury income and investments in newer businesses.
- **Reported Net Loss:** Rs 249 crore (compared to a loss of Rs 234 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Pantaloons Segment Revenue:** Rs 1,204 crore (up 10% YoY), with Pantaloons brand growing 7% (LTL growth of 4%) and OWND growing 55% YoY.
- **Pantaloons EBITDA Margin:** 15.9%.
- **Ethnic Portfolio Revenue:** Rs 454 crore (up 4% YoY), with 5% LTL growth. Tasva grew 35% YoY.
- **Standalone Gross Cash:** Approximately Rs 1,000 crore at the end of the quarter.
- **Total Retail Network:** 1,286 stores spanning over 7.9 million square feet (added over 45 stores during the quarter).

### Highlights
- Established businesses delivered steady high-single-digit growth with stable margins.
- Newer businesses (TMRW, Galeries Lafayette, OWND, Tasva) grew ~30% YoY, driven by retail footprint expansion.
- TMRW primary sales grew 11% YoY, while secondary sales grew 16%; cash losses narrowed sequentially.
- Tasva marked its eighth consecutive quarter of positive LTL performance, reaching 90 stores.
- Standalone cash deployment is on track, expected to fund businesses for the next 2 years (annual cash funding requirement of Rs 500 crore to Rs 550 crore), with the business expected to be FCF positive by FY29/FY30.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects TMRW to maintain a 20%+ revenue growth rate for the year and achieve brand-level cash profitability over the next 12 to 18 months.
- The ethnic portfolio is guided to grow north of 20% for the full year, with H2 significantly higher than H1.
- Management expects the portfolio to become fully profitable by FY29.
- Annual capex (including working capital needs) is guided at approximately Rs 450 crore for the year, with ~Rs 300+ crore specifically for capex (new stores and renovations).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer demand environment remained broadly stable and in line with recent quarters. May saw better traction, while June reflected dynamics ahead of the end-of-season sale.
- Occasion-led consumption saw moderation due to Adhik Maas impacting the peak wedding season and lower wedding-related purchases.
- Management noted a transition into a more slightly inflationary phase in H2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material inflation of ~4% is anticipated in Pantaloons and OWND, primarily impacting H2. Management chose to absorb a portion of this cost rather than passing full price hikes to consumers to protect volumes.
- EBITDA margin contraction YoY was attributed to lower treasury income and scaling investments in newer businesses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added over 45 stores across the portfolio during the quarter.
- Pantaloons plans to add ~20 new stores in H1.
- Tasva plans to add 25-30 stores this year.
- TMRW plans to add 75+ stores across its brand portfolio this year.
- TCNS plans 35-40 store additions for the year (~10% space addition).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone gross cash stood at ~Rs 1,000 crore at quarter-end (down from ~Rs 1,150-1,200 crore at the start of the year), projected to be ~Rs 500 crore by year-end.
- TMRW previously raised Rs 500 crore for its growth plan, requiring no further ABFRL capital contribution.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Adhik Maas and fewer wedding dates adversely impacted wedding-wear and designer businesses.
- Non-retail channels for TCNS were intentionally de-grown as part of cleanup, resulting in a low LTL performance (2%) and a disappointing organic quarter.
- OWND and Galeries Lafayette remain in investment phases with operational losses.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Garima Mishra (Kotak Securities) on Pantaloons growth breakdown:** Management clarified that Pantaloons brand grew 7-7.5% while OWND contributed 2-2.5% to segment growth; OWND is still in an investment phase and pulled down segment profitability.
- **Tejash Shah (Avendus Spark) on LTL moderation in Pantaloons:** Management explained that LTL fluctuated (weaker April, strong May, lower June) averaging 4% for the quarter, but expects high single-digit annual LTL.
- **Archana Menon (Morgan Stanley) on TCNS performance and cleanup:** Management admitted Q1 was disappointing with 2% LTL due to old inventory liquidation, but stated the cleanup phase is largely behind them and recovery is expected from the festive season.
- **Devanshu Bansal (Emkay Global) on Galeries Lafayette:** Management stated the business is operationally loss-making due to early-stage overheads, but expects The Collective and Galeries Lafayette combined to break even at least in H2.
- **Sameer Gupta (IIFL Capital) on TCNS turnaround timeline:** Management explained that fashion supply chains have longer lead times, but the heavy inventory cleanup is complete and festive season onwards will see a turnaround.
- **Prerna Jhunjhunwala (Elara Capital) on TMRW profitability timeline:** Management reiterated that FY29 is the expected year for TMRW to approach profitability, with 2 out of 6 brands already profitable at the brand level.

### One-line takeaway
ABFRL delivered 11% revenue growth driven by steady established brands and scaling new platforms, even as wedding-wear headwinds and investments in newer formats impacted near-term profitability.
