# Retail & E-commerce - Apparel & lifestyle retail: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the apparel and lifestyle retail sector reflected a recurring theme: solid top-line growth for many players, but compressed profitability and subdued reported Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG) due to an external calendar shift. A late festive and wedding season (pushing Dussehra and Diwali out of September/Q2 into October/Q3) combined with raw material inflation (cotton, wool, and fabric price hikes) created headwinds for Q2. However, managements across the board pointed to robust underlying festive demand tracking into H2, aggressive store expansions into larger formats, and strategic supply chain adjustments.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Every company impacted by the calendar shift (including Aditya Birla, Arvind Fashions, Go Fashion, Kewal Kiran, Monte Carlo, Sai Silks, V-Mart, and V2 Retail) agreed that Q2 demand was temporarily depressed or flat due to Dussehra/Diwali moving out of the quarter. All expect a strong rebound and robust growth in H2 driven by pent-up festive and wedding demand.
*   **Divergence:** While mass and value-market players like V-Mart noted high competitive intensity in hinterland regions (with 150+ competitor stores opening in target markets), luxury players like Purple Style Labs reported surging Average Order Values (AOVs) touching INR 84,000 and booming demand for designer occasion wear. Similarly, while standard apparel brands saw formal/wedding wear split evenly with casuals (Aditya Birla reporting a 50-50 mix), regional saree players like Sai Silks noted strong cluster-led demand despite temporary state-level weather and competition hurdles.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Raw Material Pressures:** Companies broadly reported input cost inflation across cotton, wool, and general fabrics (e.g., pure wool prices up nearly 20% for Monte Carlo; 8-10% blended RM inflation for Go Fashion; 5-6% for Sai Silks and Arvind Fashions). 
*   **Pricing Actions:** Responses to inflation varied. Aditya Birla took a 6% price hike for the season; Arvind Fashions and Kewal Kiran plan 1.5% to 2% and MRP adjustments respectively; Monte Carlo instituted an 8% to 9% winter price hike, while Go Fashion implemented calibrated price hikes to recover gross margins over the next two quarters.
*   **Discounting Divergence:** Arvind Fashions and Aditya Birla expanded gross margins by successfully *reducing* discounting. Conversely, Monte Carlo saw rebates and discounts surge to 12% of revenue (vs. 9% last year) due to early inventory liquidation of older stock.

## Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Retail Footprint Growth:** Aggressive store expansions continued. Aditya Birla added 100+ stores in Q2 (network at 3,434 stores); Arvind Fashions targets ~150 gross store openings this fiscal year; Purple Style Labs is investing INR 75-85 crore over 18 months for large-format upgrades (including a 100K sq. ft. Gen 4 store in Delhi); V-Mart aims for 90-100 store openings. 
*   **Strategic & Non-Core CapEx:** Arvind Fashions is setting up an in-house shirt manufacturing facility in Madhya Pradesh (3 lakh units/month capacity) to de-risk imports from Bangladesh. Monte Carlo is undertaking an unusual 35-megawatt solar project in Madhya Pradesh to optimize corporate returns. Raymond Lifestyle is expanding its Hindupur garmenting plant from 3 to 10 lines.

## Company one-liners
*   **Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Ltd:** Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands delivered its 4th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth (11% YoY to Rs 2,266 crore) and 5th consecutive quarter of margin expansion, backed by robust retail expansion, new product traction, and strong festive demand expectations for H2.
*   **Arvind Fashions Ltd:** Arvind Fashions delivered record Q2 revenue and EBITDA driven by strong direct-to-consumer momentum, lower discounting, and robust festive demand, while initiating strategic in-house manufacturing to de-risk its supply chain.
*   **Go Fashion (India) Ltd:** Go Fashion reported a muted Q2 with a 3% revenue de-growth due to festive timing shifts and raw material pressures, but management remains confident that store consolidation, larger formats, and pricing actions will drive recovery in H2.
*   **Kewal Kiran Clothing Ltd:** KKCL delivered resilient Q2 financial performance with 5% revenue and 15% PAT growth despite Vapi flood disruptions and festive shifts, while strategically realigning its Vision 2028 retail footprint toward larger single-brand EBOs and reduced discounting.
*   **Monte Carlo Fashions Ltd:** Monte Carlo Fashions reported modest H1 revenue growth of 6.25% with an H1 net loss, heavily weighed down by raw material inflation and inventory liquidation discounts, but management maintains its double-digit revenue growth guidance for FY27 driven by the upcoming winter and festive season in Q3.
*   **Purple Style Labs Ltd:** Purple Service Labs reported steady H1 FY27 GMV growth of INR 397 crore and improved gross margins to 39%, while keeping net working capital lean as it progresses toward its long-term FY30 GMV target of INR 2,100 crore.
*   **Raymond Lifestyle Ltd:** Raymond Lifestyle delivered a resilient Q2 with record revenue and improved cash flows, maintaining its FY27 guidance despite input cost pressures and a delayed festive season.
*   **Raymond Ltd:** Raymond delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 21% YoY revenue growth and 15.1% EBITDA margin, underpinned by strong aerospace momentum, expanding precision engineering capabilities, and disciplined execution of its upcoming Andhra Pradesh greenfield facility.
*   **Sai Silks (Kalamandir) Ltd:** Despite a challenging H1 marked by festive calendar shifts and negative SSSG, Sai Silks expects a strong recovery in H2 driven by robust wedding and festive momentum, maintaining its full-year revenue guidance of Rs 1,850-1,900 crore.
*   **V-Mart Retail Ltd:** V-Mart delivered 18% revenue growth despite a festive shift, with strong performance from the Unlimited format and improved operational efficiencies, though competitive intensity and inflation remain key headwinds.
*   **V2 Retail Ltd:** V2 Retail reported strong volume-led top-line and gross margin expansion, which were offset by subdued reported SSSG due to a festive timing shift, higher store opening costs, and increased debt.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **H2 Festive and Wedding Sell-Through:** Execution and actual sales realization of the delayed Dussehra and Diwali festivities, alongside the crucial winter/wedding season across companies like Aditya Birla, Arvind Fashions, Raymond Lifestyle, Sai Silks, and Kewal Kiran.
*   **Price Hike Efficacy:** Consumer acceptance of price increases implemented by Aditya Birla, Arvind, Go Fashion, Monte Carlo, and V-Mart to offset cotton and wool cost inflation.
*   **Store Format Migrations:** Progress on Go Fashion's closure of sub-300 sq. ft. stores in favor of larger formats, V-Mart's store acceleration pace, and Purple Style Labs' high-end store upgrades.
*   **Working Capital & Debt Levels:** Monitoring inventory stabilization post-festive stockpiling and the realization of guided free cash flow generation (e.g., Arvind's expected Rs 150-175 crore FCF release).
