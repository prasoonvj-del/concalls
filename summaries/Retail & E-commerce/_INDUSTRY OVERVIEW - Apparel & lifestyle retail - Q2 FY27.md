# Retail & E-commerce - Apparel & lifestyle retail: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The overarching theme across the apparel and lifestyle retail sector in Q2 FY27 was the impact of a **festive calendar shift**. Key celebrations like Durga Puja, Navratri, and Diwali, along with associated online shopping events (e.g., Big Billion Days), shifted from Q2 (September) into Q3 (October/November). This shift, combined with an additional intercalary Hindu month (Adhik Maas) and the Shradh period landing late in Q2, temporarily depressed Q2 primary shipments, like-to-like (LTL) sales growth, and operating leverage for several players.

Despite the temporary Q2 top-line drag, managements across the board reported immediate green shoots in October and remain optimistic about H2 FY27. Drivers include concentrated festive consumption, a dense H2 wedding calendar, and the release of seasonal working capital. Meanwhile, companies faced input cost headwinds from raw cotton, fabric, and freight inflation, prompting calibrated price increases of 1.5% to 6% entering the autumn-winter season.

## Where the industry is heading

**Business model so far**  
Indian apparel retail historically operated on multi-brand wholesale distribution, unorganized local retailing, and small-format EBO networks. Over the last five years, the industry has rapidly shifted toward direct-to-consumer (D2C) online channels, larger flagship store formats, and capital-light franchise execution models (COFO/FOCO) to improve inventory turns and customer experience. [Background]

**Shifts underway**  
* **Format rationalization towards larger EBOs** | *scaling* | Retailers are actively closing smaller sub-scale stores (<300–500 sq. ft.) and migrating to larger flagship outlets (>750–1,200 sq. ft.) to support broader category depth and superior economics. Go Fashion closed 23 smaller stores while scaling larger formats (>750 sq. ft.) which delivered 2%–3%+ positive SSSG [Go Fashion (India) Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Kewal Kiran Clothing is phasing out its multi-brand K-Lounge format in favor of 1,000–1,200 sq. ft. single-brand EBOs [Kewal Kiran Clothing Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. Purple Style Labs is upgrading to large-format flagship stores, including a 100,000 sq. ft. store in Delhi [Purple Style Labs Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
* **Supply chain localized de-risking** | *early* | Companies are building in-house manufacturing capabilities to hedge against import dependencies and currency fluctuations. Arvind Fashions is setting up a 3 lakh unit/month shirt facility in Madhya Pradesh to de-risk imports from Bangladesh and mitigate rupee depreciation [Arvind Fashions Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
* **Quick commerce channel adoption for innerwear/essentials** | *scaling* | High-density hyper-local delivery is emerging as a rapid growth engine for innerwear and basic apparel. Dollar Industries recorded 58.5% value growth in quick commerce, expanding its revenue share to 5.4% [Dollar Industries Ltd concall Q2 FY27].
* **Global brand licensing and designated supply** | *scaling* | Indian apparel operators are leveraging global brand licenses to scale both domestic D2C and international B2B export streams. Thomas Scott executed global designated supplier and licensing agreements for Forever 21 and Dockers [Thomas Scott (India) Ltd concall Q2 FY27]. PDS Ltd expanded its Sourcing-as-a-Service platform, securing a $550 million 5-year contract with a French retailer [PDS Ltd concall Q2 FY27].

**Government push**  
* **Goa 24-hour retail operations approval**: Allows shops and malls in Goa to operate 24 hours to boost retail consumption [Govt: Goa govt: Shops and malls can remain open 24 hours, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence

### Consensus
* **H2 Rebound**: Managements universally agree that demand deferred from Q2 will materialize strongly in Q3 and Q4, propelled by the delayed festive dates and a heavy wedding season through mid-December into Q4 (Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands, Vedant Fashions, Cantabil, Sai Silks).
* **Tier 2/3 Aspirations**: Consumption in smaller towns and hinterlands remains resilient, with Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands noting a 6th consecutive quarter of double-digit LTL growth in towns under 1.5 lakh population.

### Divergence
* **Value & Mass vs. Premium/Luxury**: Value retail and footwear (SS Retail, Redtape, V-Mart) demonstrated strong volume growth and same-store sales outperformance during Q2. Conversely, celebration wear (Vedant Fashions) and ethnic retail (ABFRL TCNS, Sai Silks) reported muted or negative Q2 SSSG due to the timing of wedding dates and Shradh, though expecting a steep turnaround in H2.
* **Store Footprint Aggression**: Players like SS Retail (added 38 stores in Q2), Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands (added 100+ stores), and V2 Retail are aggressively expanding store networks. In contrast, ABFRL (Pantaloons), Go Fashion, and Credo Brands (Mufti) are slowing net store expansion or rationalizing network footprint to focus on productivity per square foot.

## Costs, raw materials & margins

* **Raw Material Inflation**: Cotton, yarn, pure wool, and synthetic fabric prices rose between 5% and 10% YoY. Pure wool costs surged nearly 20% (Monte Carlo), while raw cotton candy prices reached Rs 68,000–72,000 (Dollar Industries).
* **Price Pass-Through Actions**:
  * **Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands**: Implemented a 6% price hike for the season.
  * **Dollar Industries**: Planned a 2% to 3% price hike in December.
  * **Arvind Fashions**: Offset a 5–6% RM cost increase with a 1.5% to 2% MRP hike.
  * **Page Industries**: Absorbed part of a 6.5–7% RM inflation using a 2.5% price hike taken in May 2026, while evaluating further increases.
  * **Monte Carlo**: Raised prices by 8% to 9% for the winter line.
* **Margin Compression**: Profitability across several companies (Go Fashion, Page Industries, Raymond Lifestyle, Rupa) was temporarily pressured by unabsorbed freight/RM inflation, higher early discounting on older stock, and loss of operating leverage during the lean Q2 period.

## Capex & capacity

* **Store Expansion Capex**: Capex remains heavily weighted toward retail store rollouts and logistics infrastructure. Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands maintained full-year capex at ~Rs 300 crore; SS Retail guided to ~Rs 20 crore annually for store expansion; V-Mart incurred Rs 85 crore in H1.
* **Manufacturing Facilities**:
  * **Arvind Fashions**: Spent ~Rs 100 crore in H1 on its new MP shirt manufacturing plant (3 lakh units/month capacity).
  * **Rupa & Company**: Approved Rs 83 crore capex over 2–2.5 years for a West Bengal factory-cum-warehouse and modern trade storage facility.
  * **Raymond Ltd (Engineering/Auto)**: Progressing on its Andhra Pradesh greenfield facility (INR 400 crore outlay by Q3 FY28).
  * **Page Industries**: Ramping up Odisha and K.R. Pet-2 facilities toward peak capability by FY28 end.
  * **Neetu Yoshi**: Operationalized its 13,200 MTPA Haridwar facility, taking combined capacity to 21,000 MTPA.

## What changed since last quarter

* **Guidance & Targets**:
  * **Credo Brands**: Provided explicit 8% to 10% annual revenue growth guidance (vs. no numerical targets in Q1).
  * **Monte Carlo & Cantabil**: Reaffirmed full-year targets (Cantabil maintaining Rs 1,000 crore revenue target; Monte Carlo expecting low double-digit top-line growth).
  * **Page Industries**: Maintained full-year double-digit volume growth guidance, which implies a steep ~14% volume growth requirement in H2 FY27.
* **Tone Shifts**:
  * **Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands & Credo Brands**: Shifted from cautious to strongly positive behind retail store performance and Mufti 2.0 rollouts.
  * **Vedant Fashions & ABFRL**: Shifted from stable to cautious regarding Q2 reported performance, citing severe calendar shifts, while keeping strong confidence in H2.
* **Balance Sheet Health**:
  * **PDS Ltd & Raymond Lifestyle**: Transitioned to net cash positions (PDS at Rs 154 crore net cash; Raymond Lifestyle at Rs 69 crore net cash).
  * **Rupa & Company**: Shifted from net cash to debt-positive due to a Rs 75 crore increase in working capital needs.

## What to watch next quarter

1. **H2 Festive & Wedding Realization**: Extent to which delayed festive demand and high-density wedding dates in Q3/Q4 translate into double-digit SSSG for Vedant Fashions, Sai Silks, ABFRL, and Cantabil.
2. **Consumer Acceptance of Price Hikes**: Absorption of the 2% to 9% price hikes taken across brands (Dollar, ABLBL, Monte Carlo, Go Fashion) without dampening volume growth.
3. **Working Capital & Inventory Unwinding**: Inventory normalization and cash flow generation in H2 following pre-festive stock accumulation (Arvind Fashions expecting Rs 150–175 crore H2 FCF release; Dollar reducing debt by Rs 50–60 crore).
4. **Regulatory and Operational Restructuring**: RBI compounding process for Page Industries regarding historical FDI/FEMA compliance (~Rs 48 crore online imported goods sales); turnaround progress on loss-making Ted Baker at PDS Ltd.
