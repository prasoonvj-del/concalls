# IndiaMART InterMESH Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited elevated churn at the Silver subscription level, ongoing data protection measures against LLMs/AI web scrapers, and macroeconomic/technological volatility.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: Rs. 429 crore (10% YoY growth)
- Consolidated collections from customers: Rs. 443 crore (9% YoY growth)
- Consolidated deferred revenue: Rs. 2,029 crore (16% YoY growth)
- Total paying supplier base: 2,17,000 at the end of Q2 (net decline of 615 suppliers QoQ)
- Unique business enquiries: 27 million
- BUSY billing: Rs. 46 crore (20% YoY growth)
- BUSY revenue from operations: Rs. 37 crore (27% YoY growth)
- BUSY deferred revenues: ~Rs. 155 crore (40% YoY growth)
- BUSY cash from operations: ~Rs. 12 crore
- BUSY new licenses sold: ~11,000 (total 4,66,000)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs. 159 crore (37% margin)
- Consolidated other income: Negative Rs. 8 crore (due to MTM losses on bond yields)
- Consolidated net profit: Rs. 91 crore
- Cash generated from operations: Rs. 135 crore
- Consolidated cash and treasury balance: Rs. 3,234 crore
- Advertising expenses: ~Rs. 8 crore to Rs. 10 crore per quarter

### Highlights
- Platinum and Gold customers contribute ~50% of the customer base and ~75% of revenue, maintaining good upsell and retention rates.
- Net decline of 615 suppliers in Q2 was primarily driven by elevated churn at the Silver subscription level.
- BUSY launched 'BUSY Magic' featuring a new UI interface, and is in the final leg of revamping its entire technology base.
- AI is being deployed extensively across pre-sales, sales data mapping, service ticket resolution, cataloguing, user verification, and voice-based applications (~80,000 to 100,000 buyers served daily via Voice AI).
- Management stated all expenses (including manpower and AI research) are expensed out immediately with no capitalization of personnel or software expenses.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided a long-term EBITDA margin of 33% for IndiaMART.
- Management expects BUSY to maintain 20% to 30% overall growth rates from Q3 onwards.
- Management stated they will not aggressively push gross customer additions until Silver churn improves.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Unique business enquiries and active buyers declined ~10% to 15% from historical highs, partly due to defensive measures against AI/LLM scraping and Google AI Overviews expanding to ~50%+ of searches.
- Management noted a massive technological shift and "fight for data," leading them to place strategic data behind logins to protect proprietary information.
- Management stated value growth and volume growth are expected to go hand-in-hand in the longer run (10% value, 10% volume), though recent growth has been driven by ARPU.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Quarterly EBITDA margin was 37% (consolidated), with standalone margins higher due to upfront cost recognition and revenue lagging by ~18 months.
- ESOP expenses declined due to the second-year design curve of the ESOP scheme and employee attrition, and are expected to remain near current baseline levels until a new pool is created.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No significant capital commitments are currently made for credit/lending pilot initiatives; any capital exposure will be strictly controlled and communicated.
- Employee headcount increased by over 30 employees in the business side QoQ.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated cash and treasury balance stood at Rs. 3,234 crore.
- Other income was negatively impacted by notional MTM losses on bond yields due to rising yields, which management expects to reverse in upcoming quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated churn at the Silver subscription tier.
- Volatility from AI/LLM web scraping, data protection requirements, and Google AI Overviews impacting organic traffic and business enquiries.
- Unpredictability in the pace of technological shifts over the next few quarters to years.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kunal Thanvi (Banyan Tree Advisors) asked about the declining trend in active buyers/enquiries and potential impact from GenAI searches.** Dinesh Agarwal (CEO) replied that the 10-15% decline is partly strategic (putting proprietary data behind logins to counter LLM scraping) and partly due to Google AI Overviews expanding. Management is okay letting go of 10-20% of low-intent queries and focusing ad spends on top-quartile categories. (Did not dodge).
2. **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities) asked about the maturity of AI deployment and whether efficiency gains offset costs.** Dinesh Agarwal responded that AI is deployed across 20+ operational areas and is expensed 100% immediately; short-run costs of experimentation offset savings, but downside protection and long-term operating leverage are anticipated. (Did not dodge).
3. **Ritvik Agrawal (Investment Managers) asked about the long-term sustainability of ARPU-led growth versus volume growth.** Dinesh Agarwal stated that long-term growth should ideally be a balanced 10% value and 10% volume, but volume growth will lag until Silver churn is fixed and targeted acquisition improves. (Did not dodge).
4. **Abhisek Banerjee (ICICI Securities) asked about the B2B lending facilitation pilot and risks compared to transactional platforms.** Dinesh Agarwal and Prateek Chandra explained that IndiaMART's strength is capturing purchase intent and supply chain information, and credit enablement is merely to support the marketplace rather than build a high-risk lending book. (Did not dodge).
5. **Shreya Chatterjee (Ageless Capital) asked what specific measures are being taken to reduce Silver churn and when it might improve.** Dinesh Agarwal detailed service level improvements, TrustSEAL verification, and buyer protection programs, but declined to provide a timeline guidance for Silver churn recovery due to ongoing uncertainties. (Did not dodge).

### One-line takeaway
IndiaMART delivered a stable quarter with 10% revenue growth and 37% EBITDA margin, but continues to face headwinds from elevated Silver supplier churn and defensive data-protection measures against AI scraping.
