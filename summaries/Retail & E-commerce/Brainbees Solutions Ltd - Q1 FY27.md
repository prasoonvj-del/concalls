# Brainbees Solutions Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue grew 13% YoY (strongest in last 5 years) and India multi-channel revenue grew 17.7% YoY (strongest in last 7 quarters), alongside structural operational improvements.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated GMV:** Rs 2,807 crore (up 12% YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 2,106 crore (up 13% YoY)
- **Consolidated AUTC:** 11.8 million (up 10% YoY)
- **Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** 4.24% (vs 4.98% YoY)
- **Consolidated Gross Margin:** 36.5% (vs 38.5% YoY)
- **Consolidated Cash after Profit (PAT):** Rs 50.8 crore (up 2.4% YoY) / Loss after tax reduction of 34% YoY.
- **India Multi-Channel Revenue Growth:** 18% YoY (GMV up 12% YoY, AUTC up 10% YoY, Orders up 12% YoY)
- **International Business Revenue Growth:** 12% YoY (AUTC up 7%, GMV up 9%)
- **International Adjusted EBITDA Loss Reduction:** 22.3% YoY (losses down from 10% to 7% of revenue)
- **GlobalBees Revenue Growth:** Flat-ish; adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 4.3% (or 3.9% as mentioned later) from 1% YoY.
- **Pre-school Business Net Revenue:** Rs 19 crore (up 47% YoY); Adjusted EBITDA at Rs 5 crore (up 65% YoY, margin at 26% vs 23% YoY).

### Highlights
- **India Multi-Channel:** RocketBees delivery framework expanded from 62 to 72 cities, covering over 50% of total online shipments with ~20% improvement in turnaround time (TAT). FC Qwik expanded from 5 to 12 cities, delivering 125,000 shipments (over 100% growth from March) with TAT reduced to 2-3 hours.
- **Offline Business:** Realignment of product portfolio from width to depth drove 15% GMV growth in offline stores. Planned net addition of 90-100 stores for the fiscal year.
- **International (Middle East):** Focused on sustainable growth and margin expansion despite geopolitical disruptions. Gross margins expanded by 280 bps YoY.
- **GlobalBees:** Growth was flat due to a planned transition/shifting of a core brand's warehouse and inventory, expected to normalize by Q3. Organic growth only (last acquisition Sept 2022).
- **Pre-school (FirstCry Intellitots):** Reached 500+ pre-schools; management targets over 1,000 pre-schools over the next couple of years.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects structural growth rates for India multi-channel to remain elevated in subsequent quarters.
- Full recovery of gross margin pressures from crude-linked raw material prices and rupee depreciation expected by the end of Q2 FY27.
- Diapering category competitive intensity has started to soften in the ongoing quarter.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Diapering category (15% of business) witnessed heightened competitive intensity from quick commerce and private labels, but market signals show it has started to soften.
- Non-diapering portfolio (85% of GMV) remains robust.
- Middle East market sustains healthy growth despite geopolitical disruptions.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Consolidated gross margin declined to 36.5% from 38.5% YoY due to competitive intensity in diapering and manufacturing margin impacts from rupee depreciation and higher crude-linked raw material prices (Q4 FY26 saw a 280 bps drop; 20 bps recovered in Q1 FY27).
- International adjusted EBITDA losses reduced by 22.3% YoY; losses improved by 320 bps from 10% to 7% of revenue.
- GlobalBees adjusted EBITDA margin improved by 290 bps YoY to 3.9%-4.3%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Net addition of 90 to 100 stores planned for the full fiscal year.
- FC Qwik dark store infrastructure and COCO stores being utilized to scale quick delivery.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not specifically discussed in detail regarding debt/working capital totals, but company highlighted capital efficiency and ongoing loss reduction across international and GlobalBees segments.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East.
- Intense competition and aggressive pricing in the diapering category from quick commerce and other players.
- Rupee depreciation and crude-linked raw material inflation impacting manufacturing margins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jay Laddha (on margin recovery timeline):** Management clarified that while diapering competition will take 4-6 quarters to fully normalize, crude/rupee-related margin hits in manufacturing will be fully recovered by the end of Q2 FY27.
2. **Aditya Kumar (on multi-channel growth sustainability):** Management stated growth will remain elevated due to operational inputs like RocketBees, FC Qwik, and offline depth strategy.
3. **Ranodeep Sen (on diapering strategy & wins):** Management noted that diapering requires high consumer retention on established brands, and competitive discounting is unsustainable; loyalty will be maintained via the full baby ecosystem and FC Qwik.
4. **Percy Panthaki (on India revenue vs GMV growth divergence):** Management explained the difference is a combination of online/offline/manufacturing mix and minor impacts from GST changes on MRP.
5. **Harsh Gokal Gandhi (on diapering profitability delta):** Management stated they do not make losses in diapering, and the non-diapering segment (50% fashion, 50% home brands) yields healthy margins.
6. **Archana Menon (on offline KPIs and store expansion):** Management highlighted TAT improvements and announced plans for 90-100 net store additions in FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Brainbees Solutions delivered its strongest consolidated revenue growth in five years (13% YoY) for Q1 FY27, driven by strong India multi-channel momentum (18% growth) and ongoing loss reduction in international and GlobalBees segments, despite near-term gross margin pressures in diapering.
