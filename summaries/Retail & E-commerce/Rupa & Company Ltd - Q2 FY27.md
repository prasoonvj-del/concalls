# Rupa & Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue remained flat YoY, but profitability improved due to cost optimization; management remains cautious regarding intense industry competition.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (targeting 10% revenue growth and 9% EBITDA margin for H2).
- **Tone and demand view:** The cautious stance on competitive intensity persists; management confirmed that price wars and discount-led competition remain unchanged.
- **New topics:** The company shifted from a net cash position to debt-positive due to a Rs 75 crore increase in working capital. A specific capex plan of Rs 83 crore was announced.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Management successfully rationalized advertisement spend to 4.7% (vs 5.1% YoY), contributing to margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 320.4 crore (flat YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 26 crore (+15.9% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.1% (+110 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 17 crore (+17.4% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 5.3% (+80 bps YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 522.8 crore (+3.7% YoY).
- **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 41.7 crore (+20.4% YoY).

### Highlights
- Athleisure and Thermal wear categories demonstrated encouraging traction.
- Modern trade contributed 5% to overall business.
- Board approved the appointment of Mr. Sunirmal Talukdar as an Independent Director.
- *Note:* The company is now debt-positive, breaking the previous pattern of maintaining a net cash surplus.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 revenue growth of ~10% and an EBITDA margin of ~9%.
- Management aims to strengthen secondary sales and build a stronger sales leadership team.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Thermal segment registered 30%+ growth during the quarter.
- Management described the hosiery market as facing "intense competition," characterized by price wars, aggressive schemes, and extended credit days.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin stood at 27.6% (vs 27.4% in Q2 FY26).
- EBITDA improvement was driven by a 30 bps gross margin expansion, lower advertisement costs (4.7% vs 5.1% YoY), and other expense savings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Board approved a total capex of Rs 83 crore to be spent over 2-2.5 years:
    - Rs 58 crore for a factory-cum-warehouse at West Bengal Hosiery Park.
    - Rs 25 crore for a warehouse/storage facility for accessories dedicated to modern trade.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital increased by Rs 75 crore between March '26 and September '26, necessitating an increase in borrowings.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Competitive Intensity:** Management highlighted that the industry is facing a "tough competitive time" with price wars on schemes and discounts.
- **Raw Material Prices:** Management noted that raw material prices remain high.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why did EBITDA grow while revenue remained flat?** A: Management attributed this to gross margin improvement, savings in advertisement costs (4.7% vs 5.1%), and other expense efficiencies.
- **Q: Why is the company now debt-positive?** A: Management explained that working capital requirements increased by Rs 75 crore from March '26 to September '26.
- **Q: What is the status of competition in the hosiery market?** A: Management stated the intensity has not subsided; there is a persistent price war regarding schemes, discounts, and credit days.
- **Q: Can you provide color on the 2-3 year view given the current margin profile?** A: Management noted the industry is going through a tough time, but expects things to improve in coming quarters.

### One-line takeaway
Rupa & Company is prioritizing operational efficiency and cost control to drive profitability in a stagnant revenue environment, while funding a significant Rs 83 crore capex plan despite rising working capital needs.
