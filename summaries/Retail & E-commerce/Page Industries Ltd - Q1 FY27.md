# Page Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew 7.9% YoY, but PAT declined 4% YoY due to raw material inflation and quarter-end logistics disruptions.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 1,420.4 crore (INR 14,204 million), up 7.9% YoY.
- **Sales Volume:** 61.9 million pieces, up 5.7% YoY.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 289.0 crore (INR 2,890 million), down 1.9% YoY; EBITDA margin stood at 20.3% (down 210 bps YoY from 22.4%).
- **PAT:** Rs 192.8 crore (INR 1,928 million), down ~4% YoY.
- **Inventory Days:** 66 days at quarter-end (vs. 73 days at Q1 start).
- **Net Working Capital Days:** ~54 days at quarter-end (vs. 56 days at Q1 start).

### Highlights
- Quarter-end primary billing was impacted by ~3 additional days of undelivered shipments (~7 days total vs. normal 3-4 days) due to third-party logistics and labor issues, pushing revenue recognition into Q2 FY27.
- Secondary and tertiary sales growth outpaced reported primary revenue growth.
- Launched the street fashion collection 'JKY Groove 3' across ~500 Exclusive Brand Stores (EBOs).
- Partnered with Disney and Marvel for character merchandise, with majority of revenue realization expected in Q2 FY27.
- Consumer Data Platform (CDP) scheduled to go live by end-August 2026 to optimize direct-to-consumer operations and analytics.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its full-year FY27 guidance of double-digit volume growth, expecting a strong volume trajectory over the next three quarters.
- Management maintained its full-year EBITDA margin target range of 19% to 21%.
- Management expects a larger spread between volume and value growth from Q2 FY27 onwards due to product mix and full realization of price hikes.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Underlying consumer demand remained healthy across EBOs, e-commerce, and multi-brand retail outlets.
- Tertiary sales in EBOs showed sequential improvement in Q1 FY27 compared to Q4 FY26.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Input cost pressures were witnessed across cotton, nylon, synthetic packaging, and petroleum-based materials during March–May before moderating in late June.
- Implemented a 2.2% weighted average price hike in mid-May 2026 (following a 2.5% price hike in January 2026 for product enhancements).
- Full benefits of the May price hike were not realized in Q1 due to FIFO inventory billing principles and will accrue starting Q2 FY27.
- Management chose to partially absorb input cost inflation to remain price-competitive.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Scaling up manufacturing facilities at Odisha and K.R. Pet to enhance internal production capacity.
- Retail footprint comprises 115,871 Multi-Brand Outlets (MBOs), 1,640 EBOs, and 930 Large Format Stores (LFS).
- Speedo offline channel consolidated to ~700 MBOs in Q1 FY27 as Speedo's online sales contribution crossed 35%.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital days reduced to ~54 days.
- Management stated inventory levels at both company and distribution levels have normalized to optimal levels, with plans to expand in-house and outsourced manufacturing to meet demand.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in global petroleum prices and raw material inputs (cotton/nylon).
- External geopolitical uncertainties (e.g., Middle East situation) posing potential cost escalation risks.
- Temporary supply-chain and logistics disruptions impacting last-mile delivery to distributors.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Ashutosh Joytiraditya): What caused the Q1 volume growth to miss the double-digit trajectory target?**  
  *A (Karthik Yathindra):* The miss was driven by trade scheme restructuring under the Auto-Replenishment System (ARS) to avoid front-loading quarters, alongside temporary quarter-end logistics disruptions. Management remains confident in achieving double-digit volume growth for full-year FY27.
- **Q (Saurabh Kundan): What was the exact quantum and nature of the logistics disruption?**  
  *A (Karthik Yathindra):* Fuel price escalation rumors and third-party labor drive concerns disrupted distributor deliveries in late June. Undelivered invoiced billing increased by ~3 days over the normal average, pushing that revenue into Q2 FY27.
- **Q (Sameer Gupta): What is the potential P&L impact from the Karnataka minimum wage revisions?**  
  *A (Karthik Yathindra & V.S. Ganesh):* Garmenting-specific minimum wage revisions were already actioned in April and absorbed in P&L. The mid-May general revision excluded garment tailors and affected only minor non-garmenting support staff; that revision has also been temporarily put on hold by the state government.
- **Q (Sameer Gupta): Why did ad spend fall 5% in FY26 despite 60% e-commerce growth, and is the channel shifting to outright sales?**  
  *A (Karthik Yathindra):* Quick commerce growth drove a shift toward outright sales models over marketplace models. Ad spend was lower YoY because FY25 included one-off Cricket World Cup campaign expenses; baseline brand marketing spends will remain at 4-5% of revenue.

### One-line takeaway
Page Industries faces temporary Q1 margin and billing headwinds from input inflation and logistics delays, but retains its double-digit volume growth target and 19-21% EBITDA margin guidance for FY27.
