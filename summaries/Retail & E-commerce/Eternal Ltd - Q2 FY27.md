# Eternal Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in quick commerce expansion, robust unit economics in smaller cities, and steady growth across core food and quick commerce segments.

### Key numbers
- **Blinkit NOV per day per store:** INR 9 lakh (steady-state potential guided up to INR 11 lakh).
- **Quick commerce SKU mix:** 90-95% 1P (First Party).
- **Blinkit MTU in mature cities:** Multiple times that of Zomato.

### Highlights
- Food delivery competitive intensity increased in Q2, partly driven by Zomato's aggressive investments in new customer acquisition and user frequency.
- Quick commerce expansion will accelerate, focusing primarily on smaller cities due to robust unit economics and rapid paybacks.
- Developed a privacy-focused LLM application layer named `dark.ai` for power users (using open-source models), currently in internal pilot and housed under Eternal.
- Enterprise AI initiatives like `Nugget` are being worked on; management expects the next 12 months to determine their viability.
- Gourmet store footprint expanded to Bangalore and Mumbai.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Food Delivery Margins:** Management expects margins to remain flattish over the next 1-2 years as surplus is reinvested into growth.
- **Blinkit NOV Growth:** NOV growth is expected to pick up starting the December (OND) quarter on a YoY basis.
- **FY29 Guidance:** Management maintained the $1 billion Adjusted EBITDA guidance for FY29 without changes.
- **Store Capex:** Blended capex guidance for new dark stores remains at INR 2.5 crore, factoring in tier 2 city expansion.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Smaller cities show robust unit economics and less competition, providing first-mover advantages.
- Assortment in expansion cities is a subset of metros, but lesser-available products sell faster in smaller towns.
- Mature cities continue to see rapid growth in NOV and MTU without plateauing.
- Management expressed surprise at new players entering the already well-penetrated food delivery category.
- Agentic commerce adoption in India is close to zero, but the company is monitoring and preparing for it.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage is the primary driver of Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion in quick commerce.
- Costs below the contribution margin include variable and fixed components (e.g., technology server costs priced in dollars).
- Incrementally higher dark store counts and new store investments temporarily offset contribution margin gains from higher NOV per store.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Accelerated store additions for Blinkit will begin in the OND quarter.
- Capex for `dark.ai` is minimal currently (hosted on existing compute/GPUs), with potential for infrastructure scaling only if demand surges.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No incremental working capital changes expected from the 90-95% 1P quick commerce model.
- The company holds a strong balance sheet capable of funding new tech/AI bets.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Foreign exchange rate movements impact dollar-denominated server and technology costs below the contribution margin.
- Shorter-term lumpy store additions can cause non-linear metric movements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Garima Mishra (Kotak):** Asked about food delivery margin peaks and quick commerce tier 2 expansion. Management stated margins will stay flattish as surplus is reinvested into growth, and smaller cities show strong paybacks.
- **Aditya Soman:** Inquired about `dark.ai` capex and monetization. Management clarified it is an application layer using open-source models, currently expensed under 'Others' with minimal capex unless scaled.
- **Manish Adukia:** Asked about Blinkit NOV growth reacceleration. Management confirmed NOV growth will pick up starting the December quarter on a YoY basis.
- **Swapnil Potdukhe:** Asked about contribution margin flatness despite higher NOV per store. Management explained that new store investments, changing product mix, and higher store sizes/costs mute the linear flow-through.
- **Rishi Jhunjhunwala:** Asked if future value creation leans towards tech assets. Management affirmed they will back opportunities where they possess core insights and a right to win.

### One-line takeaway
Eternal is aggressively reaccelerating its quick commerce store rollout in smaller cities and investing in growth across food delivery while incubating nascent AI initiatives.
