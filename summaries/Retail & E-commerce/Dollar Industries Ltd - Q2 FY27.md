# Dollar Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-18 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Operating income rose 14.3% YoY to Rs 539 crore, supported by strong volume growth, quick commerce acceleration, and robust export performance.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance at 11% to 13% and EBITDA margin guidance at 11.5% to 12.5%.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from cautious/mixed in Q1 to positive, noting good festive demand and robust volume recovery (8% YoY growth), though gross margins faced pressure from rising cotton prices.
- **New topics or risks:** Addressed rising cotton and yarn prices (cotton candy prices rising to Rs 68,000-72,000) and plans for a 2% to 3% price hike in December. Mentioned slowing down Project Lakshya expansion into new states due to intense market competition, focusing instead on consolidation.
- **Last promises delivered:** Working capital showed continued improvement (cash conversion cycle down to 157 days from 160 days in Q1).

### Key numbers
- **Operating Income / Revenue:** Rs 539 crore, up 14.3% YoY (H1 FY27: Rs 944 crore, up 8.4% YoY).
- **Volume Growth:** 8% YoY for Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27: 4% YoY).
- **Gross Profit:** Rs 171 crore, up 4.5% YoY; Gross profit margin at 31.8% (down ~298 bps YoY).
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs 61 crore; EBITDA margin at 11.2% (H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 108 crore, margin at 11.5%).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 36 crore; PAT margin at 6.6% (H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 62 crore, margin at 6.5%).
- **Net Debt:** Rs 288 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.29x.
- **Net Debt to Annualized Operating EBITDA:** 1.33x.
- **Annualized RoE / RoCE:** 12.7% / 14.4%.
- **Cash Conversion Cycle:** 157 days (Receivable days: 120, Inventory days: 109).
- **Segment Contributions (Q2 FY27):** Dollar Always: ~40%, Dollar Man (Bigboss): ~34%, Dollar Thermal: 12.6%, Dollar Woman (Missy): 7.1%, Force NXT: 4%, Dollar Protect: ~2%.

### Highlights
- Quick commerce grew 58.5% in value and 52.4% in volume, increasing its revenue share to 5.4% (vs 4% in Q2 FY26).
- Export business delivered 57% value growth and 45.5% volume growth.
- Dollar Thermal (winterwear) grew 17.7% in value and 10.2% in volume, contributing 12.6% to revenue. Dollar Protect saw strong traction from a favorable monsoon.
- Eastern region grew 25.6% in value and 16.3% in volume. Northern region grew 14.6% in value and 6.2% in volume.
- Spinning plant capacity utilization stood at around 85% in H1 FY27.
- Advertisement expenses stood at 6.5% of revenue for H1, with annual expenditure capped at Rs 100 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Revenue Growth:** Guided at 11% to 13%.
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin:** Guided at 11.5% to 12.5%.
- **FY27 Volume Growth:** Expected at 8% to 9% on a full-year basis.
- **Medium-Term Target (2-3 years):** EBITDA margin target of 13.5% to 14.5%, driven by a product mix shift toward high-margin products (targeting an increase from 28% to 35%), working capital control, and operating leverage. Revenue CAGR target of 12%.
- **Debt-Free Target:** Management reiterated its target to become debt-free by FY28 or FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Consumer sentiment remains positive, supported by good festive demand. 
- Consumers are postponing purchases rather than trading down to lower-priced brands during tight liquidity periods.
- Competitive intensity remains high and intense in the mid-market segment, though it is expected to normalize over the next 1-2 quarters.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross profit margin moderated to 31.8% due to rising cotton and yarn prices (cotton candy prices increased from Rs 60,000 in Q1 to Rs 68,000-72,000).
- Management intends to implement a 2% to 3% price hike in December to pass on rising raw material costs.
- Disciplined cost management, overhead controls, and operating leverage cushioned the EBITDA margin impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Project Lakshya expansion into new states has been temporarily slowed down due to competitive intensity and market disruptions; current focus is on optimizing existing operational areas (covering parts of West, South, North, and Central regions).
- Spindle capacity additions (22,000 spindles plus 20,000 operational since Dec '24) remain active.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased seasonally to Rs 288 crore as of September 30, 2026, due to working capital requirements for winter inventory buildup; management expects to reduce working capital debt by Rs 50 crore to Rs 60 crore in H2.
- Debtor days for Lakshya distributors stand at 85-90 days, compared to 120-125 days for non-Lakshya distributors, with a long-term company-level target of 75-80 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Elevated cotton and yarn prices putting pressure on gross margins.
- Continued intense discounting and competitive pressure from existing players in the mid-market segment.
- Delayed rollout of Project Lakshya into new states to prevent market disruption and loss of market share.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Gunit Singh (Counter Cyclical PMS):** Asked about the reason for gross margin decline compared to peers and upcoming price hikes.  
   *Management Answer:* Gross margins declined due to rising cotton/yarn prices (cotton candy at Rs 68,000+). Management plans a 2%-3% price hike in December.
2. **Rudraksh Raheja (ithought Financial Consulting):** Inquired about the slowdown in Project Lakshya and employee cost increases.  
   *Management Answer:* Lakshya expansion was slowed down to protect market share against intense competition. Employee costs rose due to sales force additions for Project Lakshya and increased spinning plant capacity.
3. **Shreya Baheti (Anand Rathi):** Asked about the volume growth breakup, ASP increases, and demand trends in Tier 2/3 cities.  
   *Management Answer:* Q2 growth included 8% volume, 4% price hikes, and ~2% product mix. Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities remain strong consumption hubs where vests are treated as outerwear.
4. **Logeshkumar Jaganathan (JLK Ventures):** Asked if a share buyback is being considered given the lower stock price.  
   *Management Answer:* No buyback is planned; focus remains on becoming debt-free in a couple of years and paying dividends.
5. **Rohit (ithought PMS):** Questioned the geographic reach and sluggish contribution growth of Project Lakshya.  
   *Management Answer:* Confirmed that major states are partially covered and new state rollouts are paused temporarily due to unfavorable market conditions, but existing covered states have seen strong revenue surges (e.g., Gujarat from Rs 25 cr to Rs 60-65 cr).
6. **Ashwin Reddy (Individual Investor):** Asked what indicators suggest competitive intensity will ease in two quarters.  
   *Management Answer:* Market normalization processes are underway among existing players, and management expects better visibility in 1-2 quarters to resume broader Lakshya rollouts.

### One-line takeaway
Dollar Industries delivered a strong 14.3% revenue growth rebound in Q2 FY27 driven by volumes and quick commerce, absorbing raw material cost pressures through operational efficiencies while preparing price hikes for H2.
