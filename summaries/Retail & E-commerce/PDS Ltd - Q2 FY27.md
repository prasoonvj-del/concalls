# PDS Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved highest-ever quarterly revenue and transitioned to a net cash position, though management maintains a cautious outlook due to global macro uncertainty.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained a cautious stance; management continues to target 50-70 bps EBITDA margin improvement and 50-75 bps PAT margin improvement annually, despite strong H1 performance.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "building the platform" to "scaling the platform." Demand remains strong, particularly in the Americas, but management emphasizes "cautious execution" due to geopolitical and inflationary pressures.
- **New topics:** Divestment of DBS Lifestyle; resignation of Independent Director BG Srinivas.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on the goal of strengthening the balance sheet (achieved net cash position of ₹154 crore).

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** ₹4,432 crore (up 29% QoQ, 30% YoY).
- **H1 Revenue:** ₹7,876 crore (up 23% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** ₹153 crore (up 49% YoY, margin 3.5%).
- **H1 EBITDA:** ₹249 crore (up 62% YoY, margin 3.2%).
- **PAT:** ₹70 crore (up 144% QoQ, 44% YoY, margin 1.6%).
- **H1 PAT:** ₹98 crore (up 44% YoY, margin 1.3%).
- **Order Book:** ₹5,670 crore (up 13% YoY).
- **Net Cash:** ₹154 crore (vs. ₹105 crore net debt in March 2026).
- **Interim Dividend:** ₹2.2 per share.

### Highlights
- Americas region grew 54% in Q2 and 44% in H1.
- Secured a 5-year Sourcing as a Service contract with a large French retailer (annual volume potential of ~$550 million).
- Portfolio rationalization: Divested DBS Lifestyle and merged Design Arc into Poeticgem.
- Investment in new verticals: ₹34 crore in H1 (vs. ₹72 crore in H1 last year).
- Net working capital improved to negative 5 days (from positive 4 days in March 2026).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to reach a ~3% PAT margin in 2-3 years.
- Expects to maintain mid-teens revenue growth.
- Management expects employee cost growth to settle in Q3/Q4, targeting a 5-7% increase for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry consolidation is benefiting PDS as retailers seek professional, compliant, and multi-vendor consolidation platforms.
- U.S. market is the fastest-growing; 7 of the top 10 U.S. retailers are now active accounts.
- Management views geopolitical disruption as an opportunity for PDS's agile, asset-light model to help retailers diversify supply chains.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin held firm at 20%.
- Employee expenses: ₹396 crore (up 17% QoQ); increase attributed to preponement of annual increments and incentives to Q2.
- Other expenses: ₹340 crore (up 33% QoQ); driven by freight, license fees, and SaaS onboarding costs.
- Finance costs: Down 13% in dollar terms YoY due to reduced invoice financing and higher use of operational cash.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Manufacturing segment: Revenue ₹452 crore, EBIT ₹26 crore (5.8% margin).
- Partnered with Busana Group (Indonesia) to upgrade manufacturing capabilities and target high-earning fashion customers (e.g., Hugo Boss, Lululemon).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Prepaid ₹35 crore long-term facility in India.
- Net working capital is negative 5 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Ted Baker:** H1 revenue ₹268 crore (up 30%), but incurred a ₹11 crore loss. Management is exploring strategic tie-ups or cost rationalization (relocation) to reach breakeven.
- **Manufacturing:** Revenue growth was flat in H1 due to customer caution in Bangladesh; management expects Knit Gallery growth to offset this.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Rahul Jain (Credence Wealth) on guidance:** Management declined to provide specific revenue guidance, citing global geopolitical/inflationary toughness, preferring to focus on execution.
- **Aditya (AK Investments) on Ted Baker:** Management admitted the operation is challenging and is a high priority for fixing or restructuring within this financial year.
- **Krunal Shah (ENAM Investments) on GMV:** Executed GMV in H1 was ₹11,366 crore (up 12.5% YoY), with no significant contribution yet from the two new major contracts.
- **Dhwanil Desai (Turtle Capital) on margin trajectory:** Management reaffirmed the "555" potential (5% PBT/PAT) but noted that near-term focus remains on 50-70 bps EBITDA margin improvement due to the cautious macro environment.
- **Rudraksh Raheja (ithought Financial) on minority interest:** Management explained the rising trend is due to Ted Baker losses and manufacturing performance; expects normalization to ~25% as these issues are resolved.

### One-line takeaway
PDS delivered record revenue and a net cash balance sheet, successfully scaling its U.S. business and SaaS model, though management remains cautious on macro headwinds and is actively working to turn around the loss-making Ted Baker operations.
