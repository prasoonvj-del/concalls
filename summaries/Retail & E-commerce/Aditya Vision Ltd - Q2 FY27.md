# Aditya Vision Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered best-ever Q2 performance with strong revenue and profit growth despite macro headwinds.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 586 crore (+28% YoY); H1 FY27 revenue Rs 1,779 crore (+27% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 45.65 crore (+31.6% YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA Rs 170.13 crore (+36.8% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 7.8% (+21 bps YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA margin 9.6% (+66 bps YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 18.47 crore (+45.2% YoY); H1 FY27 PAT Rs 95.69 crore (+41% YoY).
*   **PAT Margin:** 3.2% (+37 bps YoY); H1 FY27 PAT margin 5.4% (+52 bps YoY).
*   **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG):** 18% for H1 FY27.
*   **Store Count:** 214 stores (vs 188 a year ago).
*   **Retail Footprint:** 9.51 lakh square feet (+18.3% YoY).

### Highlights
*   Growth driven by ACs, mobiles, and refrigerators, aided by an extended summer season.
*   Bihar remains the primary revenue contributor (73%), followed by Uttar Pradesh (15%), Jharkhand (11%), and Chhattisgarh (1%).
*   Successfully entered three new states in 2026: Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, and West Bengal.
*   Management stated H1 FY27 PAT has reached over 90% of the entire PAT reported in FY 2025.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Store Expansion:** Management expects to close FY27 with ~230-235 stores and add ~35 stores in the following year.
*   **Margins:** Management maintains normalized gross margin guidance of 15%-15.5% and EBITDA margin guidance of 8%-10% annually.
*   **Demand:** Management expects demand to pick up as the festive season (Dhanteras/Diwali) approaches, despite slow sales in October.
*   **Tailwinds:** Management identified the potential 8th Pay Commission as a significant future revenue driver.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Headwinds noted: West Asia crisis, energy supply disruptions, rising crude oil prices, and flood/cyclone disruptions in Bihar.
*   Consumer sentiment impacted by inflation in essential staples and cooking gas.
*   Management noted that while consumer demand has moderated, underlying demand remains resilient.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Gross margin expansion of 125 bps in Q2 was driven ~75-80 bps by price hikes, with the remainder from normal trading.
*   Indirect expenses increased due to higher sales volume, financing costs, credit card/CMS charges, and store expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Average capex per store is ~Rs 90 lakhs.
*   Capex in new markets (West Bengal/MP) is 10%-15% higher than in Bihar due to logistics and freight costs.
*   Management expects new stores to break even in approximately one year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Inventory: Rs 865 crore (vs Rs 840 crore as of March 2026).
*   Borrowings: Rs 293 crore (vs Rs 331 crore as of March 2026).

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Macroeconomic volatility and geopolitical disruptions.
*   Difficulty in quantifying the exact revenue impact of natural disasters (cyclones/floods).
*   Slow demand observed in October.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the sustainability of gross margin expansion. **Management:** Clarified that 75-80 bps of the 125 bps expansion was due to price hikes and is not permanent; normalized guidance remains 15%-15.5%.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the impact of new store openings on margins. **Management:** Stated that margin dilution from new stores is compensated by the maturation of older, established stores.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about competitive landscape in West Bengal. **Management:** Noted presence of national players (Reliance, Croma) but plans to target border districts first to leverage existing brand recall.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the 64% jump in indirect expenses. **Management:** Attributed this to sales growth, increased financing/CMS costs, and the addition of 30 stores over the last year.

### One-line takeaway
Aditya Vision delivered strong H1 growth despite macro headwinds and is aggressively expanding its footprint into new states while maintaining disciplined margin guidance.
