# Vishal Mega Mart Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-26 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue and store expansion momentum despite festive shift headwinds and inflationary pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2 FY27):** Rs 3,399 crore, up 14% YoY
- **Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG) (Q2 FY27):** 5.4% (impacted by 3%–4% due to festive shift; adjusted SSSG ~9%)
- **Operating EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 262 crore, up 3.7% YoY
- **Operating EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 7.7% vs 8.5% YoY
- **Adjusted PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 172 crore, up 6.4% YoY
- **Adjusted PAT Margin (Q2 FY27):** 5.1% vs 5.4% YoY
- **Revenue from Operations (H1 FY27):** Rs 7,126 crore, up 16.4% YoY
- **SSSG (H1 FY27):** 7.7% (adjusted SSSG ~9.5%)
- **Operating EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 649 crore, up 12.5% YoY; margin at 9.1% vs 9.4% YoY
- **Adjusted PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 437 crore, up 15.8% YoY; margin at 6.1% vs 6.2% YoY
- **Store Additions:** 26 gross new stores in Q2 (53 in H1), total store count at 844 across 582 cities
- **Trading Area:** 1.41 crore square feet
- **Private Brands Contribution:** 74% in H1 FY27
- **Quick Commerce Reach:** 781 stores across 532 cities; registered users crossed 1.5 crore

### Highlights
- Festive shift (Durga Puja, Dussehra, Navratras falling in October Q3 instead of September Q2) created temporary operating deleverage in Q2.
- Launched the first pilot store of 'Belong & Co.', a new apparel format catering to young urban consumers, with initial conversion rates north of 50%.
- Small format stores reached a count of 18 (focused on UP and Haryana).
- Quick commerce expanded; management plans to invest aggressively in advertising and digital media starting from Delhi.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to achieve double-digit SSSG for the full year.
- Guidance maintained to open more than 100 stores in FY27, with store counts accelerating annually.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to improve by roughly 30 bps annually over the medium term driven by double-digit growth outpacing store-level cost inflation (6%-7%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand conditions remain steady despite renewed raw material inflation and an uneven monsoon.
- Q2 SSSG was largely driven by market share gains rather than higher spending by existing customers.
- No moderation in customer demand observed as the festive season kicked off in Q3.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margins remained stable; management absorbed cost inflation through cost reengineering and selective price hikes at higher price points.
- No price increases were taken on opening price points or mid-price points to protect vulnerable customers.
- Employee costs were elevated due to minimum wage increases (effected in Q1) and new store additions.
- Rental cost growth trailed retail area additions due to expansion into Tier 2 and Tier 3 towns.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Commissioned a fully automated large warehouse in North India; plans are underway for similar warehouses in East, South, and West India.
- Large format store potential estimated at 2,000 stores (currently at 850-odd).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The Board is evaluating the deployment of the large cash reserves on the balance sheet.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Operating deleverage during quarters impacted by festive shifts.
- Persistent raw material inflation and uneven monsoon patterns creating temporary disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Percy (IIFL) asked about Q3 SSSG expectations post the festive shift:** Management confirmed Q3 SSSG will need to be above 10% to achieve full-year double-digit targets.
- **Harit Kapoor asked about pricing strategy and regional differences:** Management reiterated that opening price points are untouched, and regional demand remains consistent despite uneven rainfall.
- **Prerna Jhunjhunwala asked about gross margin trends with apparel mix shifts:** Management clarified that Q3 will see a higher apparel bias due to festive timing, but gross margins will remain stable.
- **Rahul Agarwal asked about medium-term store addition cadence and margins:** Management stated the intent to accelerate absolute store openings beyond 100+ annually without diluting profitability.
- **Devanshu Bansal asked about the large cash balance deployment:** Management highlighted investments in new store formats, automated warehouses, quick commerce marketing, and active board-level evaluation of capital allocation.

### One-line takeaway
Vishal Mega Mart delivered steady 14% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 despite festive shifts, backed by aggressive store expansion, strong private brand penetration, and disciplined pricing that protected vulnerable consumers from inflation.
