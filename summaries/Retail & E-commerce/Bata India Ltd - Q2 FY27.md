# Bata India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the fourth consecutive quarter of positive growth, double-digit growth in franchisee and I&D channels, and a 31%+ increase in adjusted PBT, supported by higher availability and premiumization.

### Key numbers
- Revenue growth: 7.5% YoY (turnover of 8.6 million units / volume context stated as turnover of 8.6 million for the quarter)
- Adjusted profit before tax (PBT) growth: Over 31% YoY
- Adjusted operating cash profit growth: Over 9% YoY
- Gross margin change: +8 bps reported YoY (underlying expansion upward of 70 to 80 bps before channel mix dilution)
- ASP increase range: 5% to 6%
- Availability increase: 1.33x compared to last year
- Store clutter: 0.71x compared to 2 years back
- Freshness in stores: Over 90% (also cited as 84%-85% fresh sales)
- Aged inventory: Close to 5%
- Stock turn: Higher than 2.6
- Ad spend growth: Over 4% on a half-yearly basis YoY

### Highlights
- Sanjay Rao hosted his first earnings call as Managing Director and CEO.
- Recorded positive growth for the fourth consecutive quarter.
- Franchisee and I&D channels recorded double-digit growth; I&D grew in mid-teens with handsome secondary sales growth.
- E-commerce grew at a healthy pace despite the deferment of orders from selected B2B partners.
- Fresh inventory sales reached an all-time high of about 84%-85%.
- Operational efforts including ZBM and the "Customer First" project continued to contribute to margins.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains the "Reignite India 2030" ambition to double the India turnover by 2030 (implying mid-single-digit to double-digit CAGR over the next 4 years).
- Aiming for sustainable and profitable growth with a bottom-line trajectory expected to match or exceed top-line doubling to fund investments.
- Global product lines (including sneaker lines) are in the pipeline to hit stores in H2 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall volume growth was reasonably democratic, but higher price points performed better, driving ASP growth.
- Positive footfalls recorded in stores despite general market headwinds.
- Festive calendar shifted Durga Puja and Diwali buying from Q2 to Q3. Positive trends observed in footfalls and secondary sales in October and early November.
- The market has become crowded with large and small competitors entering the field over the past decade.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Committed to protecting gross margins through measures like reducing markdowns and driving fresh inventory sales.
- Commodity and currency volatility remain extremely high; price increases are being calibrated and rolling out starting from Q1 to mitigate raw material inflation.
- Channel mix shift towards franchising and e-commerce impacts reported gross margins (diluting reported expansion to 8-10 bps), but improves overall profitability.
- Ad spend absolute numbers grew, with a focus on community building and efficient digital deployment.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Franchisee channel and I&D recorded double-digit growth.
- Targeting double-digit new store openings in the coming year, focusing heavily on franchisees while selectively looking at COCO (Company Owned Company Operated) store opportunities.
- Also focusing on store relocations to commercially better locations and store renovations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Stock turn is higher than 2.6.
- Freshness in stores is over 90%; aged inventory is close to 5%.
- Average inventory holding sits at 5 to 6 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodity and currency volatility.
- Missed trends in sneakerization in the past, leading to challenges with the younger demographic (Gen Z).
- Market headwinds and general footfall pressures in the retail industry.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sameer Gupta (IIFL Capital) asked about immediate near-term priorities, how to make the brand palatable to the younger generation without hurting margins, and ad spend efficiency.** 
   *Management answered that priorities include pushing growth opportunities like franchising, premiumization, and casualization, alongside introducing global sneaker lines. Margins will be protected via operational efficiencies, and ad spend is being optimized toward community building.*
2. **Aryan Garodia (Ambit Capital) asked for the underlying breakdown between SSG, volume, and ASP, and the impact of the 5-6% price hike.** 
   *Management stated SSG had good single-digit growth, ASP was in the 5% to 6% range, and volume grew marginally. The recent price hikes (starting in Q1) will reflect more in subsequent quarters, with current growth largely driven by premiumization and fresh sales.*
3. **Prerna Jhunjhunwala (DAM Capital) asked about gross margin sustainability and when healthy volume growth upwards of mid-single/high single-digits will appear.** 
   *Management stated gross margins will remain range-bound due to channel mix and commodity volatility, while volume gains will become more visible in Q3 due to festive and e-commerce calendar shifts.*
4. **Gaurang Kakkad (Dolat Capital) asked about market share loss recovery, long-term CAGR targets, and store addition plans.** 
   *Management reaffirmed the 2030 doubling target (implying mid-single to double-digit CAGR) and guided for double-digit new store openings next year, emphasizing franchisees while remaining opportunistic on COCO stores.*
5. **Sudhir Kedia (Valuewise Investment Managers) questioned whether Bata's decade-long subdued performance was internal or external, and asked if bottom-line growth would match top-line doubling.** 
   *Management acknowledged it was a mix of both internal capabilities (lack of agility in sneaker trends) and external factors (crowded field, volatility). They target bottom-line growth equal to or exceeding top-line doubling, subject to external macro factors like exchange rates.*

### One-line takeaway
Bata India reported a 7.5% revenue growth and 31%+ increase in adjusted PBT in Q2 FY27, driven by premiumization, improved product availability, and double-digit franchisee expansion, while management prepares global sneaker lines to capture the youth demographic and achieve its 2030 doubling ambition.
