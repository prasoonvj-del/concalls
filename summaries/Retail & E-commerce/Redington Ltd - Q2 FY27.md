# Redington Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered the strongest quarterly revenue and profit after tax in the company's history amidst geopolitical disruption and supply chain complexity.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 43,002 crore, up 48% YoY
- Profit after tax (PAT) excluding exceptional items: Rs 581 crore, up 50% YoY (Excluding Arena: revenue up 54% to Rs 43,002 crore [Note: transcript mentions consolidated revenue matches total] and PAT up 55% to Rs 657 crore)
- H1 FY27 consolidated revenue: Rs 77,967 crore, up 41% YoY
- H1 FY27 PAT excluding exceptional items: Rs 1,067 crore, up 61% YoY
- India distribution business growth: 67% YoY
- Middle East and Africa growth: Middle East up 41%, UAE up 39%, Saudi and Levant (GCCL) up 121%, Africa up 35%
- Segment growth YoY: Mobility up 40% (34% of consolidated mix; SISA up 70%); Endpoint Solutions (ESG) up 38% (30% of mix); Technology Solutions Group (TSG) up 91% (20% of mix); Software Solutions Group (SSG) up 39% (15% of mix)
- Large deals (TSG): Exceeded Rs 3,800 crore (~9% of group revenue)
- Working capital days: 35 days (at the lower end of the 35-40 day guidance range)
- Return on Equity (ROE): 21.6% (24.4% excluding Arena); Net ROCE: 22.7% (28% excluding Arena)
- Arena subsidiary share of loss: Rs 115 crore (includes business loss, lira business transition loss, and Rs 40 crore exceptional impairment of intangibles; revenue down 37% YoY)
- Sustainable opex guidance: Rs 950 crore to Rs 1,000 crore a quarter

### Highlights
- Recorded the highest quarterly revenue and PAT in company history, continuing a pattern of record-breaking results noted in our earlier record.
- Mobility segment driven by premiumization, new product launches (NPIs), and selective price increases.
- TSG growth heavily driven by AI servers and GPUs with richer configurations and ASPs up 4x; large deals largely stemming from data centers / neo-cloud.
- Arena subsidiary exited the VDF contract and the Lira local currency business, operating with a completely new management team.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects consolidated growth in H2 to be north of 25%, assuming no severe demand contraction or escalation of geopolitical disruptions.
- TSG and SSG expected to remain strong growth drivers in H2.
- Cautious outlook on ESG and Mobility due to price-volume elasticity and potential demand dampening from price hikes (30% to 50% price increases).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprise AI adoption is transitioning from CPUs to GPUs; commercial AI PC adoption is >40% compared to ~10% for consumers.
- Consumer unit volumes for PCs and smartphones are declining, with revenue growth primarily driven by higher Average Selling Prices (ASPs).
- Neo-cloud data center buildout in India presents a massive multi-year infrastructure opportunity.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating expenses grew at 40%, lower than revenue growth of 48%.
- Gross margins for TSG large deals are lower than run-rate business, while SSG gross margins are higher than 6% with superior profitability.
- Opex increases impacted by AR provisioning (Rs 213 crore total, including Rs 67 crore in SISA and Rs 135 crore in Arena), strategic investments in SSG, and war-related insurance/logistic cost increases in the Middle East.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital investment on funded portions for large data center deals is nearly nil (only non-funded limits utilized).
- Rolled out CloudQuarks 2.0 across Middle East and Africa; expanded AI learning centers and setup AI COE in Singapore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity stands around 0.5x to 0.6x (comfortable compared to global distribution benchmarks of 0.6x to 0.8x).
- Working capital days maintained at 35 days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical disruptions and logistical stress in the Middle East (Strait of Hormuz closed, longer turnaround times and higher insurance TAT).
- High channel inventory in ESG due to speculative stocking ahead of price hikes.
- Ongoing volatility, liquidity challenges, and curveballs in the Turkey (Arena) market.
- Potential demand contraction / volume elasticity risk from steep price hikes across consumer devices.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Nitin (Investec) asked about gross margin trends and TSG large deal sustainability:** Management explained gross margins reflect the structural CPU-to-GPU migration rather than short-term pricing anomalies, and large deal pipelines are driven by ongoing enterprise AI adoption.
- **Pratik Kothari (Unique PMS) asked how to reconcile strong company growth with reports of vendor production cuts:** Management responded that growth is supported by premiumization trends (wealth/credit-backed buyers) offsetting weaker entry-level demand.
- **Hitesh Goel (Aurigin Capital) asked about the price vs. volume split in PCs and smartphones:** Management stated price increases range between 30% to 50%, with consumer unit volumes declining while commercial PC demand remains steady.
- **Aejas Lakhani (Unifi AMC) questioned the sharp rise in opex and provisioning:** Management detailed the opex breakdown (AR provisioning, SSG investments, Middle East war-related costs) and guided sustainable quarterly opex at Rs 950 crore to Rs 1,000 crore.
- **Ashish Konaje (Leo Capital) asked about Neo Cloud data center opportunities and return profiles:** Management stated hardware sourcing and orchestration deals involve zero funded capital investment, yielding high returns on capital with minimal incremental opex.

### One-line takeaway
Redington delivered record-breaking Q2 FY27 financial performance, powered by explosive growth in high-value enterprise technology (TSG/SSG) and premium mobility, successfully offsetting volume softness and localized geopolitical headwinds.
