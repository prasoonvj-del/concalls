# Recode Studios Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Retail & E-commerce*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust revenue, robust festive season demand, and confidence in achieving 50% annual growth despite front-loaded H1 investments.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 45.82 crore (up 24.04% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 6.45 crore (up 8.75% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 4.92 crore (up 26.77% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin: ~14% (operating margin)
- FY27 Full-Year Revenue Guidance: ~50% growth
- FY27 Full-Year EBITDA Margin Guidance: Closer to 25% on a blended basis
- FY27 Aflairza Revenue Guidance: Rs 20 crore
- FY27 Aflairza PAT Guidance: Rs 3 - 3.5 crore (Recode to acquire 51% stake in January 2027)
- Product Portfolio: Expanded from 350 to around 400 SKUs; target of ~500 SKUs in H2
- Nykaa Presence: 34 touchpoints with 60-minute delivery via Nykaa Now across 7 major cities
- IPO Fund Utilization: Rs 3.64 crore in hand currently

### Highlights
- Front-loaded investments and expenses in H1 towards team strengthening, new verticals, quick commerce, and organized retail weighed on operating margins; management stated these investments are paying off starting October.
- Acquired a stake in Aflairza as part of a long-term strategy to build a multi-brand portfolio of 5-7 brands over 5-7 years, aiming for independent operation before potential integration or IPO.
- Continued deep PAN India penetration spanning Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, and Tier 4 cities, including dark store openings in Odisha and Jammu.
- *Note on Record:* Despite an earlier stated shift away from FOFO stores in Q4 FY26, management mentioned opening FOFO stores in this call once dark store traction is established.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of at least 50% YoY.
- Blended EBITDA margin for full-year FY27 expected to recover closer to 25%.
- Aflairza expected to close FY27 at Rs 20 crore revenue and Rs 3 - 3.5 crore PAT, with Recode increasing its stake to 51% in January 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pre-Diwali demand was described as excellent and record-breaking; post-Diwali saw a brief muted period due to short holidays before bouncing back over the last 3 days.
- Color cosmetics demand is heavily weighted toward H2 due to the Indian Hindu wedding season running from November 20 through July.
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC) averages around 20% of revenue across channels; website AOV stands at Rs 900 with 40% repeat customers month-on-month.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- H1 margins were lower due to employee cost increases from post-IPO hiring (65-75 new employees added) and quick-commerce kiosk investments.
- Target employee expense ratio is 5% - 7% of revenue.
- Marketing spend runs at approximately 20% of revenue.
- Supplier advances include amounts given to vendors in China and Thailand, earning 12% interest income.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Asset-light model maintained with zero in-house manufacturing; all products sourced from third-party manufacturers in India and abroad.
- Adding ~100 SKUs in H2 to reach ~500 SKUs total.
- No brand ambassador planned for the next 2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital and trade receivables are intentionally extended to provide leverage to retailers for deep market penetration.
- IPO working capital allocation fully utilized as reported; Rs 3.64 crore remaining in hand.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- H1 operating margins and revenue underperformed internal expectations due to front-loaded expenses during a seasonally weak period.
- Short holiday period post-Diwali caused temporary demand softening.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Omkar Pipikar (Individual Investor):** Asked about the 75 crore top-line implied by the 50% growth guidance and Diwali demand. Management explained H1 was an investment phase and H2 sales will drive the full-year target.
- **Sunil Kateshiya (Tanush Investments):** Asked about competitiveness against VC-funded peers like Renee and Sugar. Management stated Recode operates profitably in public markets with a 20% marketing spend versus 50% for peers, comparing itself to international rather than Indian brands.
- **Kailash Kabra (KK Investments):** Asked about Tier 2/3 positioning and fund utilization. Management clarified demand stems from Tier 1 while roots are built across Tier 2-4, and confirmed IPO working capital is utilized with Rs 3.64 crore remaining.
- **Sahil Chopra (KIFS Capital):** Questioned the sharp increase in trade receivables and short-term advances. Management explained advances are given to suppliers (including China/Thailand) earning 12% interest, and trade receivables are due to intentional inventory placement for deep geographic expansion.
- **Mr. Ram (SNA):** Inquired about employee counts, CAC, and manufacturing strategy. Management stated 65-75 employees were added, CAC averages 20% of revenue across channels collectively, and an asset-light outsourcing model will be strictly maintained.

### One-line takeaway
Recode Studios reported a muted H1 FY27 due to front-loaded hiring and investments but management remains confident in delivering 50% revenue growth and ~25% EBITDA margins for FY27, backed by strong H2 wedding season demand and expanding regional distribution.
