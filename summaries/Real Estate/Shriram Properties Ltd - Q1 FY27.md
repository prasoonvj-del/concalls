# Shriram Properties Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasizes strong operational momentum, record Q1 sales, and high visibility for revenue/earnings growth in H2.

### Key numbers
*   **Sales Value:** Rs 484 crore (up 10% YoY).
*   **Sales Volume:** 0.85 million sq ft (up 4% YoY).
*   **Collections:** Rs 365 crore (up 8% YoY).
*   **Revenue:** Rs 271 crore (up 4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 42 crore.
*   **PAT:** Rs 11 crore.
*   **Units Handed Over:** 690.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 432 crore.
*   **Net Debt/Equity:** 0.29x.

### Highlights
*   **Launches:** Launched 0.9 million sq ft across three projects (Shriram Stellar in Chennai, Shriram Southbrook in Kolkata, and Green Meadows in Chennai).
*   **Sales Performance:** Plotted development in Kolkata saw 55% of inventory sold within 30 days; Shriram Stellar (Chennai) saw 20% sold in the launch weekend.
*   **Business Development:** Added 0.7 million sq ft with an estimated GDV of Rs 650 crore.
*   **Pipeline:** Total pipeline stands at 33.7 million sq ft (16 million ongoing, 17.7 million upcoming).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Guidance:** Management maintains full-year guidance, expecting stronger revenue and earnings in Q2 and H2 due to scheduled project completions.
*   **FY28 Mission:** Management reiterated targets of Rs 5,000 crore sales value, Rs 2,500 crore revenue, and Rs 250 crore PBT.
*   **Growth Targets:** Management projects 20-25% growth in both revenue and PAT for FY27.
*   **Margins:** Management expects PBT margins to reach 8-9% in FY27 and stabilize at ~10% by FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management reports steady demand in core markets (Bangalore, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata) with no material slowdown in conversion rates or application momentum.
*   Mid-market, end-use segment remains strong; investment-oriented demand may taper due to market volatility.
*   Sustenance sales prices are increasing by 4-5% annually.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Q1 margins were muted due to product mix, with 40% of revenue coming from legacy, low-margin Kolkata projects.
*   Management expects EBITDA margins to reach 22-24% by FY28 as higher-margin projects reach completion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Invested Rs 88 crore in new projects during Q1.
*   Management aims to double the upcoming project pipeline over the next 18-24 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Gross external debt is Rs 651 crore; cash and equivalents are Rs 219 crore.
*   Cost of debt is ~11%.
*   Management noted gearing may rise temporarily due to aggressive growth and pipeline acquisition, but the long-term comfort zone is ~0.5x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Quarter-to-quarter margin volatility due to project mix and handover timing.
*   Potential for temporary increase in gearing levels to fund growth.
*   Volatility in investment-oriented demand.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Questioned the disconnect between low historical EBITDA margins (7-8%) and the 10% PBT guidance. **Management:** Clarified that "other operating income" (JV/development rights) is often misclassified by analysts; expects margin improvement via product mix.
*   **Analyst:** Questioned the disconnect between high sales guidance (40-50% growth) and lower collection growth (26-30%). **Management:** Explained that collections follow project progress and current launches are back-ended in Q3/Q4.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about Kolkata land monetization. **Management:** Stated they are moving away from bulk land sales in favor of villas/plots/apartments to achieve higher per-acre contributions (Rs 6.5-7.5 crore/acre).
*   **Analyst:** Suggested providing quarterly updates to improve transparency. **Management:** Agreed to resume the practice of providing quarterly operational updates.

### One-line takeaway
Management maintains FY27 guidance, banking on a back-ended launch and handover schedule to drive revenue and margin expansion despite a modest Q1.
