# DLF Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong performance driven by launch sellouts, rising super-luxury realizations, robust cash generation, and sustained rental growth.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,298 crore (includes a one-time gain of Rs 738 crore in other income from JV valuation).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 1,060 crore.
- **Consolidated Net Profit (PAT):** >Rs 1,300 crore.
- **New Sales Bookings:** ~Rs 3,800 crore.
- **Collections:** Rs 2,780 crore; **Operating Cash Flow:** ~Rs 1,858 crore.
- **Net Cash Position:** ~Rs 15,100 crore (after dividend payout of ~Rs 1,980 crore).
- **DCCDL Financials:** Revenue Rs 2,014 crore, EBITDA Rs 1,537 crore, Net Profit >Rs 750 crore (up 17% YoY).

### Highlights
- Launched luxury retirement project *Aureva* (~Rs 1,985 crore total potential), achieving complete sellout with Rs 1,900 crore reported in Q2.
- Super-luxury project *Dahlias* continues strong traction with 276+ units sold to date; average realizations exceeded Rs 1,00,000 per sq ft in Q2 FY27.
- Commercial portfolio reached highest-ever occupancy levels of 96% by area and 97% by value across 50 million sq ft.
- Commissioned Summit Plaza and Midtown Plaza in Q2 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Inflection Point:** Accounting revenues will accelerate from FY27-FY28 onwards upon completion of *The Arbour*, followed by three *Privana* projects and *Dahlias*.
- **Upcoming Launches:** Planned launch in Mumbai during Q3 FY27, and a luxury-plus product in DLF Phase 4 Gurgaon (Hamilton vicinity) in Q4 FY27.
- **Rental Portfolio Exit Run Rate:** DLF standalone rental exit run rate projected at Rs 175-180 crore quarterly (~Rs 750 crore annualized, ex-Atrium Place). DCCDL exit rentals targeted at ~Rs 6,800 crore.
- Management reiterated maintaining an annual dividend policy.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Super-luxury residential demand remains extremely buoyant, establishing higher price benchmarks across North and South tower developments.
- Occupancy in annuity office and retail portfolios remains at record highs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Development business gross margin potential stands at ~Rs 40,000 crore with expected cash generation of >Rs 42,500 crore.
- Other expenses increased marginally due to marketing and brokerage costs; management expects future quarterly run-rate to track the average of the last two quarters.
- Sustainable quarterly other income run-rate is expected at Rs 150-200 crore (interest income, subsidiary interest).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Land payments of ~Rs 350 crore incurred in Q2 to clear encumbrances on 7.06 acres and enter a fresh collaboration for 7.48 acres, creating a contiguous 36-acre land parcel in Sector 61, Gurugram.
- DCCDL additions: Downtown Chennai Blocks 4 & 5 (3.6 million sq ft) under construction, operational by CY2027. Downtown Gurgaon (Blocks 5 to 8) and Mall of India Gurgaon scheduled for completion by FY30.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains in a strong net cash position of ~Rs 15,100 crore.
- Unsold inventory value stands at Rs 15,000 crore, with unrecognised margin potential of Rs 10,825 crore (~Rs 11,000 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential construction progress slowdown in Q3 due to seasonal festival labor migration (Chhath Puja) and implementation of GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan) environmental restrictions in NCR during winter months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Puneet (HSBC) on Dahlias pricing and Experience Centre:** Management answered that pricing algorithms automatically adjust upwards with sales (prices rose ~10% this quarter; North units achieved Rs 120-125 crore, penthouse Rs 271 crore). Experience Centre launch is targeted by December.
- **Abhinav Sinha (ICICI Securities) on construction cash flows and GRAP:** Management clarified that 30-40 days of GRAP in Gurgaon could cause temporary blips in Q3 outflows, but labor/contractors are in place and project completion timelines remain unaffected.
- **Shrenik Jain (J.P. Morgan) on lag in revenue recognition:** Management stated DLF follows the Completed Contract Method (recognition occurs post-OC). Inflection begins in FY27-FY28. Additionally, Cyber City (~Rs 2,000 crore/qtr) and Atrium Place (~Rs 150-175 crore/qtr) revenues are not consolidated line-by-line due to minority partner governance rights despite DLF's 67% stake.
- **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital) on recent land cash outflows:** Management explained Rs 350 crore went toward acquiring 7.06 acres via IREO debt/bond settlement and acquiring an adjoining 7.48 acres to create a contiguous 36-acre parcel in Sector 61.

### One-line takeaway
DLF continues to build strong cash reserves via sellout luxury launches, with accounting revenues poised for a strong multi-year ramp-up starting FY28 as major projects hit completion.
