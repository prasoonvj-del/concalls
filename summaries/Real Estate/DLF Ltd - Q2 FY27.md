# DLF Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-22 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects strong new sales bookings, robust operating cash flows, highest-ever rental portfolio occupancy, and a solid net cash position.

### Key numbers
- New sales bookings: ~Rs 3,800 crore
- Collections: Rs 2,780 crore
- Operating cash flow: ~Rs 1,858 crore
- Net cash position: ~Rs 15,100 crore
- Dividend payout in current quarter: ~Rs 1,980 crore
- Rental portfolio: 50 million square feet (occupancy at 96% by area, 97% by value)
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 2,298 crore (includes a one-time gain of Rs 738 crore as other income due to valuation of a JV investment)
- EBITDA: Rs 1,060 crore
- Net profit: >Rs 1,300 crore
- Development business gross margin potential: ~Rs 40,000 crore; cash generation potential: >Rs 42,500 crore
- DCCDL consolidated revenue: Rs 2,014 crore
- DCCDL EBITDA: Rs 1,537 crore
- DCCDL net profit: >Rs 750 crore (17% YoY growth)
- Dahlias gross sales (including Camellias): 19 units for the quarter; total units sold so far: 274–276 units (approx 280)
- Dahlias average realization: >Rs 100,000 per sq ft (prices up by almost 10% this quarter)
- DLF rental exit run rate (quarterly, ex-Atrium): ~Rs 175-180 crore (~Rs 750 crore annual)
- DCCDL exit rental guidance: ~Rs 6,800 crore

### Highlights
- Launched Aureva (luxury retirement housing address), achieving a complete sellout.
- Continued strong demand for the super-luxury project Dahlias; North-facing units sold at ~Rs 120-125 crore each (10,000 sq ft), and a penthouse reported at Rs 271 crore; recent South-facing units clocked Rs 100 crore per apartment.
- Balance surplus from unsold inventory valued at Rs 15,000 crore, with a balance margin of Rs 10,825 crore (~Rs 11,000 crore).
- Land transactions: Completed encumbrance removal for a 7.06-acre parcel in Sector 61 (part of 28.5 acres from IREO) and entered a collaboration for a fresh 7.48 contiguous acres, creating a 36-acre contiguous land parcel.
- Commissionings: Summit Plaza and Midtown Plaza commissioned in the current quarter; Promenade Goa targeted around Christmas/New Year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the inflection point for accounting revenue recognition to be FY27-FY28, driven by project completions starting from The Arbour and followed by the three Privana projects and Dahlias over the next three years.
- DCCDL commercial completions: Block 7 of Downtown Gurgaon and Chennai Block 3 (Blocks 4 and 5, totaling 3.6 million sq ft) expected next fiscal year (FY28). Mall of India Gurgaon and remaining Downtown Gurgaon blocks targeted for completion by FY30.
- Annual dividend payout policy maintained by management for DLF.
- Other expenses guidance: Average of the last two quarters to be used as a forward run rate.
- Other income normal run rate: ~Rs 150 to 200 crore per quarter (excluding one-offs).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Absolute strong demand observed for super-luxury housing, particularly Dahlias and Aureva.
- Price algorithm for Dahlias ensures progressive price increases with every sale, independent of velocity or number of sales.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses include brokerage and marketing overheads incurred for future projects; management noted a marginal increase in line with general overhead trends.
- Construction outflows in Q3 may face a slight blip due to seasonal labor migration (Chhath Puja) and potential GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan) restrictions in the Delhi-NCR region during the AQI season.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Land payments of ~Rs 350 crore made during the quarter towards clearing IREO land encumbrances and a new collaboration in Sector 61, Gurugram.
- Pipeline launches: Mumbai project targeted for Q3, a Gurgaon luxury-plus project in Phase 4 targeted for Q4, and Magnolias 2 in Q4.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong net cash position of ~Rs 15,100 crore after a dividend payout of close to Rs 1,980 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential construction slowdown in Q3 due to chronic labor migration for Chhath Puja and implementation of GRAP guidelines in Gurugram/NCR impacting construction for 30 to 40 days.
- Accounting revenues remain subdued in the short term due to the completed contract method of accounting and non-consolidation of Cyber City (~Rs 2,000 crore/quarter) and Atrium Place (~Rs 150-175 crore/quarter) due to minority partner AVM rights.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Puneet (HSBC) on Aureva sales timing:** Management clarified that initial booking amounts (Rs 50 lakh) and KYC/paperwork were completed by September 30th, with minor spillovers into October due to NRI remittance and banking holidays.
2. **Abhinav Sinha (Equities) on construction cash flows and GRAP:** Management acknowledged potential short-term headwinds in Q3 construction outflows due to labor migration and anticipated GRAP restrictions, but called it a passing challenge rather than a crisis.
3. **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital) on inventory and land parcels:** Management clarified the difference between inventory value (Rs 15,000 crore) and balance margin (Rs 10,825 crore), and detailed the 36-acre contiguous land consolidation in Sector 61.
4. **Shrenik Jain (J.P. Morgan) on subdued accounting revenue vs. market expectations:** Management explained the completed contract method lag (inflection expected in FY27-FY28) and auditor-mandated non-consolidation of Cyber City and Atrium Place due to AVM rights. Management did not dodge the question.

### One-line takeaway
DLF reported robust Q2 FY27 operating performance led by strong luxury pre-sales (Dahlias, Aureva), record rental occupancies, and a net cash surplus of Rs 15,100 crore, even as accounting revenue recognition awaits project completions starting FY28.
