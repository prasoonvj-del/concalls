# Ashiana Housing Ltd-$ - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Softer residential demand and lower revenue recognition due to delivery timing, though management remains confident in long-term strategy and FY27 guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Booking value (Q1 FY27):** INR 358 crores (3.6 lakh square feet sold across 234 units)
- **Collections (Q1 FY27):** INR 409 crores (6% YoY growth)
- **Average realization:** INR 9,923 per square foot (37% YoY increase)
- **Revenue from operations (Q1 FY27):** INR 107 crores (compared to INR 293 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **Operating cash generation (Q1 FY27):** INR 121 crores (compared to INR 108 crores in Q1 FY26)
- **NCD Redemption:** INR 31.25 crores redeemed to ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund (25% of original issue)
- **Presales (as of July 31, 2026):** Reached about INR 859 crores
- **FY27 Presales Guidance:** INR 2,200 crores
- **Vadgaon, Pune Land Acquisition:** 28.55 acres (~20 lakh square feet estimated saleable area, ~INR 1,800 crores potential sales value)
- **Long-term ROE Floor:** At least 15%

### Highlights
- Residential demand softened in Q1 FY27 amid global economic uncertainties and a wait-and-watch approach among homebuyers.
- Premiumization continued as a defining trend toward larger, well-designed homes from established players.
- Senior Living remained insulated due to structural demographic-led growth drivers.
- Commenced handover for Phase 1 of Ashiana Nitara in Jaipur during the quarter.
- Acquired 28.55 acres at Vadgaon, Maval in Pune for a Senior Living project (largest ever land acquisition for Senior Living by the company).
- Total budget to deploy about INR 800 crores in FY27 for business development.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H1 FY27 exit presales between INR 1,050 crores and INR 1,100 crores.
- Management maintains FY27 presales guidance of INR 2,200 crores, driven by launches in H2 (including Ashiana Aaroham's Phase 3 in Gurugram).
- Management targets ~INR 2,000 crores of revenue for FY27 based on delivery and revenue schedules.
- Long-term strategic goal is to maintain a floor ROE of at least 15% year-on-year, with reported ROE potentially higher (e.g., ~20% in FY27).
- Senior Living aims for a 25% CAGR long-term, targeting INR 1,500 crores of presales from Senior Living by FY 29-30, and a long-term medium-term presales target of INR 3,000 to INR 4,000 crores.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Residential demand softened moderately in Q1 FY27 after strong FY26 momentum.
- Prices remain resilient and new project launches hold firm.
- Senior Living demand is strong, supported by urbanization and changing family structures, with limited organized supply.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Target gross profit margin at the project level is ~30%.
- Typical model: ~12% SG&A, 18% PBT margin, and 12-13% PAT margin.
- Sales and marketing costs in established locations (e.g., Chennai - Shubham) have fallen to 4% to 5% of sales.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Budgeted capital deployment of INR 800 crores for FY27 (including INR 180 crores deployed in Q1).
- Vadgaon (Pune) project has a 10-year development timeframe (or ~7 years if absorption accelerates to 3 lakh sq ft/year) with an expected launch in ~18 months.
- Active business development discussions ongoing in Bengaluru (Kanakapura Road, South Bengaluru), Jamshedpur, Chennai, Mumbai, Pune, and NCR.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash generation remained robust at INR 121 crores in Q1 FY27.
- Redeemed 25% (INR 31.25 crores) of NCDs issued to ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund.
- Vadgaon land acquisition financed via NCDs issued to landlords to mimic a 25% equity stake, alongside a 6% revenue share.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Quarterly reported revenues and profits will fluctuate based on the timing of Occupancy Certificates (OC) and project handovers.
- Temporary dip in presales possible in the near term due to lack of inventory in regular housing markets (Gurugram, Jaipur, Bhiwadi) as capital shifts toward Senior Living.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rohan Joshi (Individual Investor) on FY27 guidance confidence given soft Q1:** Management stated that July sales reached ~INR 859 crores following the launch of Ashiana Oma, and H2 will be driven by launches like Ashiana Aaroham Phase 3 in Gurugram, maintaining the INR 2,200 crores presales target.
2. **Rohit (iThought PMS) on Bengaluru CPs and ROE trajectory:** Management noted progress on CPs in South Bengaluru (Kanakapura Road) with definitive documentation expected soon, and confirmed that while 15% is the long-term floor ROE, reported ROE could cross 20% in FY27.
3. **Chetan Thacker (M3 Investment) on project margin structure:** Management stated that achieving ~30% gross margins, 12% SG&A, and operating leverage in Senior Living will support the 15% floor ROE.
4. **Aditya Banerjee (Individual Investor) on volume slowdown vs. revenue lunkiness:** Management explained that quarterly revenue volatility is tied strictly to OC timings (e.g., Ashiana Anmol Phase 3 and Amarah Phase 1 OCs received in mid-July fell into Q2).
5. **Khushi Solanki (Individual Investor) regarding Vadgaon acquisition structure:** Management clarified that NCDs were issued to landlords to accommodate their desire for a 25% minority stake/revenue participation alongside a 6% revenue share, rather than deploying internal cash fully.

### One-line takeaway
Ashiana Housing reported a soft Q1 FY27 in revenues and bookings due to project delivery timings, but management remains confident in achieving its INR 2,200 crores presales guidance for FY27 while pivoting capital toward high-margin Senior Living projects.
