# Marathon Nextgen Realty Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operating performance, increased booking values, and a major strategic acquisition of Adani's 60% stake in Monte South to move to 100% ownership.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management did not provide specific numerical guidance, maintaining a positive growth stance.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more bullish regarding luxury and mid-income absorption, supported by a near-doubling of booking values and area sold QoQ.
- New topics or risks: The acquisition of Adani's 60% stake in Monte South for Rs 335 crore (Rs 200 crore already paid) was finalized; three new projects added in Versova, Sewri, and Kanjur (combined GDV Rs 1,740 crore). Old risks regarding NCLT progressed with positive stakeholder reports filed.
- Last quarter's promises: NCLT stakeholder meetings were successfully convened with positive reports filed; merger proceedings advanced to NCLT hearings this month.

### Key numbers
- Total Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 145 crore
- Total Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 362 crore
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 61 crore
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 127 crore
- Profit After Tax (PAT) (Q2 FY27): Rs 45 crore
- Profit After Tax (PAT) (H1 FY27): Rs 98 crore
- Pre-sales / Booking Value (Q2 FY27): Rs 154 crore (Residential: Rs 138 crore; Commercial Futurex: Rs 107 crore on combined basis)
- Pre-sales / Booking Value (H1 FY27): Rs 240 crore
- Collections (Q2 FY27): Rs 127 crore
- Collections (H1 FY27): Rs 244 crore
- Area Sold (Q2 FY27): 71,000 sq ft (Existing portfolio; nearly double of Q1's 38,000 sq ft)
- Area Sold (H1 FY27): 109,000 sq ft
- Monte South Acquisition Cost: Rs 335 crore for 60% stake (Rs 200 crore paid)
- Monte South Remaining GDV (100% basis): ~Rs 6,500 crores to Rs 7,000 crores (~15 lakh sq ft remaining)
- New Project Additions: Versova, Sewri, Kanjur (Combined estimated GDV: Rs 1,740 crores)

### Highlights
- Board approved acquisition of Adani Group's 60% stake in Monte South for Rs 335 crores, increasing ownership to 100% and enabling full revenue consolidation from next quarter.
- Monte South features 25 lakh sq ft total area across four 64-storey residential towers and a commercial tower; Tower A received full OC, Tower B received OC up to 45th floor (RCC fully complete), and Tower C is complete up to 35th floor.
- Added three new projects in Versova, Sewri, and Kanjur with combined estimated GDV of Rs 1,740 crores.
- Merger front: NSE/BSE "No Adverse Observation" letter submitted to NCLT; stakeholder meetings completed with positive reports filed; NCLT hearings scheduled for the month.
- Ended September with a net debt-free balance sheet and a positive net cash position.
- Marathon Futurex saw a leading financial services group expand by ~45,000 sq ft.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Monte South commercial to be launched next financial year, with Tower D targeted for FY29.
- Bhandup and Panvel projects will drive upcoming residential sales momentum.
- Management anticipates healthy festive season demand to support future sales run rates.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Residential booking values rose across affordable, mid-income, and luxury segments, supported by established Mumbai micro-markets.
- Panvel benefits from the operational Navi Mumbai International Airport and improving infrastructure, driving conversions from eastern Mumbai.
- Bhandup demand is supported by upcoming infrastructure like the Goregaon-Mulund Link Road (GMLR).
- Redevelopment acquisition requires rigorous initial vetting, taking 8 to 12 months for environmental clearances and government approvals.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Monte South project operates at an expected EBITDA margin of 30% to 35%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Balance construction spend for Monte South Tower B is around Rs 200 crores.
- Deployed capital for acquiring Adani's 60% stake in Monte South stands at Rs 335 crores (Rs 200 crores paid already).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Ended September 2026 with a net debt-free balance sheet and a positive net cash position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as major operational bottlenecks during the call, aside from standard government approval timelines for new launches and redevelopment clearances.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mihir (FB Capital) asked about Monte South's development potential and surplus.** Management stated that out of 25 lakh sq ft, 10 lakh sq ft is sold, leaving 15 lakh sq ft (~Rs 7,000 crores future sales) which yields an estimated surplus of ~Rs 2,100 crores at 30%+ EBITDA margins.
2. **Jojo Shaju (Tirthan Capital) asked about Monte South quarterly pre-sales run rate and demand slowdown concerns.** Management responded that sales are growing QoQ (reaching Rs 146 crores in Q2) and luxury demand remains strong due to established scale and amenities.
3. **Manav (Jain Investment) asked about launch plans for H2 FY27 and FY28, and reasons for zero launches in Q2.** Management explained that project launches involve lengthy back-end approvals and land conversions, and they are preparing for larger launches in upcoming quarters.
4. **Rohit Mehra (SK Securities) asked about the timeline for monetizing Monte South Tower D and commercial tower.** Management stated the commercial tower will launch next financial year (taking 4-5 years to complete), while Tower D is planned for FY29 launch.
5. **Ashish Singh (NH Securities) asked about pricing realizations at Monte South.** Management confirmed prices have firmed up with a ~10% value increase over the last year across ready/near-ready towers.

### One-line takeaway
Marathon Nextgen Realty delivered robust Q2 FY27 operating growth, secured 100% ownership of the landmark Monte South project for Rs 335 crores, and maintained a net debt-free balance sheet.
