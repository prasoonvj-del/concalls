# Arvind SmartSpaces Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by highest-ever quarterly presales, collections, and upwardly revised growth guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Bookings (Presales):** INR 906 crore in Q2 FY27 (more than double of INR 432 crore in Q2 FY26); INR 1,338 crore in H1 FY27 (120% YoY growth).
- **Collections:** INR 418 crore in Q2 FY27; INR 754 crore in H1 FY27 (77% YoY growth).
- **Operating Cash Flows:** Net operating cash flows of INR 152 crore in Q2 FY27; INR 233 crore in H1 FY27.
- **Financial Performance (H1 FY27):** Revenue at INR 475 crore (up 96% YoY from INR 242 crore); EBITDA at INR 197 crore (3.5x YoY); PAT at INR 120 crore (4x YoY).
- **Financial Performance (Q2 FY27):** Revenue at INR 157 crore; EBITDA at INR 45 crore (up 46% YoY); PAT at INR 23 crore (up 25% YoY).
- **Net Debt:** INR 200 crore as of September 30, 2026 (compared to INR 215 crore as of June 30, 2026). Debt-to-equity stands at 0.26.
- **Unrealized Operating Cash Flow:** INR 5,197 crore from the existing project pipeline.
- **Business Development:** Cumulative GDV added in FY27 reached approximately INR 3,100 crore as of September 2026.
- **Project Specific Metrics:** Arvind Sylva (Bengaluru) recorded bookings exceeding INR 500 crore (60% of inventory) within 30 days of launch; Sarjapur Road project sold at INR 12,600/sq. ft. with a top-line potential of INR 470 crore.

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly presales and collections in Q2 FY27.
- New launches accounted for 65% of Q2 bookings; sustenance sales contributed 35% (sustenance crossed nearly INR 300 crore a quarter in H1).
- Decentralized operating structure implemented across Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, and MMR is operational with dedicated regional leadership.
- **Record check:** Unlike past periods where growth was heavily launch-dependent, H1 FY27 demonstrated sustained traction with strong sustenance sales across Gujarat and Bengaluru.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Bookings Guidance:** Revised upwards from INR 2,100–2,200 crore (35%-40% growth) to INR 2,600–2,700 crore (70%-75% YoY growth).
- **Business Development Guidance:** Confident of achieving the upper end of the INR 4,000–5,000 crore GDV addition guidance for FY27.
- **Margin Guidance:** Reiterate EBITDA margin guidance in the range of 22% to 25%.
- **Long-term Outlook:** Management targets a long-term growth rate of ~30% YoY and aims to reach ~INR 5,000 crore scale in the medium term.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian real estate sector shows resilience supported by urbanization, formalization, and GCC expansion.
- Demand in the mid- to premium residential segment is driven by life-stage transitions (first home ownership, family formation, lifestyle upgrades) rather than speculation.
- Homebuyers increasingly prioritize quality, design, location, amenities, wellness, and living experiences.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management noted that historical price growth and cost control have driven P&L margins above underwriting baselines.
- Underwriting is conservative, assuming current prices without factoring in speculative future price hikes.
- Inflation and contingency buffers are factored into recent underwriting, mitigating the impact of recent commodity and geopolitical cost hikes.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Medium-term portfolio focus aims for a 70:30 mix favoring Joint Developments (JD) / Joint Ventures (JV) over outright land purchases in terms of GDV addition.
- Planning 4 more launches in H2 FY27 (2 in MMR/Mumbai, 2 in Bengaluru) with booking value potential of INR 2,200–2,500 crore.
- Aiming to reverse the current horizontal-to-vertical portfolio mix from 65:35 to 65% vertical over the next 4 years.
- Fourth geographic market expansion will likely be evaluated after 2-3 years once Bengaluru and Mumbai units mature.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet remains healthy with net debt at INR 200 crore and a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.26.
- Access to a platform availability with HDFC provides additional financial firepower.
- Capable of supporting land outflows of close to INR 1,000 crore annually via operating cash flows and debt headroom.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Broader economy faces challenges and increased cost pressures from geopolitical uncertainties (West Asia developments, crude oil volatility, currency fluctuations, and inflation).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Jainam Shah (Equirus Securities):** Asked about the divergence between high reported P&L/gross margins and conservative 20%-25% EBITDA margin guidance. 
   - *Management reply:* The 22%-25% range is the baseline used for new underwriting, whereas actual financials have benefited from past price appreciation and cost efficiencies.
2. **Jainam Shah (Equirus Securities):** Inquired how balance sheet safety and capital discipline will be maintained with aggressive 30% growth targets.
   - *Management reply:* Emphasized a 70:30 JD-to-outright GDV mix, regional leadership sourcing quality deals, and disciplined capital allocation.
3. **Amit Srivastava (360 ONE Capital):** Asked about the brand positioning and pricing strategy for upcoming Mumbai (Goregaon) launches.
   - *Management reply:* Targeting the premium category with ticket sizes between INR 2.5 crore to INR 5 crore, backed by strong locations and thoughtful design.
4. **Harsh Pathak (Motilal Oswal):** Questioned what milestones would trigger entry into a fourth geographic market.
   - *Management reply:* No specific number, but expects to evaluate a fourth market after 2-3 years once Bengaluru and Mumbai are deeply established as self-operating units.
5. **Surender Singh (My Equity Sherpa):** Asked about internal guardrails ensuring projects are delivered on time without delays.
   - *Management reply:* Track construction cost outflows and project plans on a day-to-day basis across regions with senior governance and a sharply rising construction spend run-rate.

### One-line takeaway
Arvind SmartSpaces delivered a stellar H1 FY27 led by record presales, strong collections, and an upgraded FY27 bookings guidance of INR 2,600–2,700 crore, backed by disciplined capital allocation and expansion in vertical housing.
