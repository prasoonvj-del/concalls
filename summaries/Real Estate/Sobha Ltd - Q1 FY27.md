# Sobha Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-27 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest ever quarterly real estate sales of INR 3,656 crore, up 76% YoY, driven by strong launches in Bangalore and NCR.

### Key numbers
- Real estate sales value: INR 3,656 crore (up 76% YoY)
- Volume sold: 2.34 million sq ft across 1,432 homes
- Average realization: INR 15,655 per sq ft
- Bangalore sales: INR 2,067 crore (57% of total)
- NCR sales: INR 1,384 crore
- Total income: INR 1,330 crore (up ~48% YoY)
- Real estate revenue: INR 1,107 crore (up ~60% YoY vs INR 690 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Contractual, manufacturing and retail revenue: INR 171 crore (vs INR 162 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Other businesses revenue: INR 170 crore; Commercial rental income: INR 23 crore
- Profit After Tax (PAT): INR 50.7 crore (vs INR 13.5 crore in Q1 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: 9.7%
- Operational cash inflows: INR 1,924 crore (up 8.2% YoY)
- Real estate collections: INR 1,756 crore (vs INR 1,599 crore in Q1 FY26)
- Contractual and manufacturing collections: INR 168 crore
- Total net operational cash flow generated: INR 312 crore; Cash flow generated after expenses: INR 290 crore
- Land investments: INR 370 crore; Capex: INR 70 crore
- Overall net cash outflow: INR 149 crore
- Net debt ratio: negative 0.14; Net cash position: INR 659 crore
- Gross debt: INR 1,110 crore; Cash and cash equivalents: INR 1,769 crore
- Average borrowing cost: 7.62%
- Yet-to-be-recognized revenue from past sales: INR 20,553 crore
- Unsold inventory: 14.94 million sq ft as of Q1 FY27
- Projected receivable from sale and unsold value: ~INR 31,000 crore against remaining cost of INR 19,000 crore (projected margin cash flow of INR 12,000 crore)
- Homes completed: 677 homes covering 1.08 million sq ft

### Highlights
- Launched 3 new projects across Bangalore and Gurgaon in Q1 FY27 with combined saleable area of 6.89 million sq ft and potential sale value of ~INR 10,000 crore.
- Key launches included *SOBHA One World* (Bangalore; 3.4 million sq ft released, ~40% sold at launch) and *SOBHA Crescent* (Gurgaon; ~60% sold in Q1 FY27).
- Acquired a 1.3-acre land parcel in Mumbai for ~INR 180 crore and invested in a Greater Noida joint development project (combined GDV for both new additions estimated at INR 2,700 crore to INR 3,000 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims for at least 30% growth in presales for FY27.
- Planned FY27 project completions: 6 to 6.5 million sq ft (~20% higher than last year's 5.4 million sq ft).
- Forthcoming launch pipeline: 20.77 million sq ft across 17 projects, of which 9 projects (~8.2 million sq ft, translating to ~INR 12,000 crore GDV) are planned for the remaining 9 months of FY27 (4 in Bangalore, 2 in NCR, 1 in Hyderabad, 2 in Kerala).
- Net debt expected to remain around zero ("0 level") for the year.
- Full-year land investments/payments expected to be between INR 1,500 crore to INR 1,600 crore.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to expand sequentially in H2 FY27, targeting closer to 17% to 20% by Q4 FY47.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Overall demand scenario is stable and steady across launches and post-launch periods.
- Milestone billing collections were lower in Q1 due to labor shortages in April and May, impacting billing cycles.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Project-related expenditure increased due to higher construction activity.
- Sales and marketing expenditure increased due to new project launches.
- Minimum wages in Karnataka hiked by 60% in May; management is evaluating the impact on overall labor/technician costs but expects to absorb it within project budgets.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q1 capex stood at INR 70 crore.
- Q1 land investments stood at INR 370 crore (including Mumbai outright buy and Greater Noida JDA).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Ended Q1 FY27 with net cash of INR 659 crore.
- Gross debt at INR 1,110 crore; cash and cash equivalents at INR 1,769 crore.
- Low average borrowing cost maintained at 7.62%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Labor shortages in April and May constrained milestone achievement and billing execution.
- Regulatory approval uncertainties for the newly acquired Mumbai project could delay its launch timeline.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Girish Choudhary (Avendus Spark):** Asked about FY27 presales targets and reasons for modest collections. Management stated they target at least 30% presales growth and explained collections were impacted by late-quarter sales timing and April-May labor shortages affecting milestone billing.
- **Puneet Gulati (HSBC):** Questioned the reduction in forthcoming project margins in presentation slide 15. Management clarified that the drop is due to a higher mix of joint development projects and exclusion of own-land projects like Hoskote.
- **Parikshit Kandpal (HDFC Securities):** Inquired about demand recovery triggers and geographical breakup of upcoming land capex. Management noted preparation advantage drove sales, and guided that incremental land buys post-commitments (~INR 600–700 crore) will be split mainly between Bangalore, NCR, and Mumbai.
- **Biplab Debbarma (Emkay Global):** Asked about the Mumbai and Greater Noida deal details and margin recovery trajectory. Management detailed the Mumbai land acquisition (1.3 acres, INR 180 crore) and Greater Noida JDA, and reiterated expectations of margin expansion up to 17%-20% by Q4 FY27.
- **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital):** Asked about the Gurgaon commercial project's status. Management stated they are evaluating retaining it for rental income rather than selling, and thus removed it from the sale pipeline temporarily.

### One-line takeaway
Sobha delivered a record-breaking sales quarter driven by major launches in Bangalore and NCR, backed by a net cash balance sheet, while management guides for margin expansion and 30% presales growth in FY27.
