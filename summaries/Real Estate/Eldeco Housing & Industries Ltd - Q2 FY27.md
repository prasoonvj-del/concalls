# Eldeco Housing & Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong growth in revenue and PAT, robust collections, and clear visibility on future project launches, despite a temporary dip in margins due to product mix.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (targeting launch of projects 5, 6, and 7 by March '27), though management added caveats regarding potential election-related delays.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "tremendous wave" to "robust" and "undersupplied" market; management noted potential election-related slowdown in approvals.
- **New topics or risks:** Specific mention of upcoming state elections impacting approval timelines; clarification on the limited depth of the Lucknow commercial market.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Latitude 27 revenue recognition timeline clarified (likely spilling into next FY, vs. previous "March-May 2027" target).

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs 52 crore (+49% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 103 crore (+55% YoY).
- **PAT:** Rs 7.4 crore (+180% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 22.5 crore (+290% YoY).
- **Q2 Booking Value:** Rs 94.9 crore (+7.8% YoY).
- **H1 Booking Value:** Rs 200 crore.
- **Q2 Collections:** Rs 125 crore (+37.5% YoY).
- **H1 Collections:** Rs 256 crore (+51% YoY).
- **Construction Spend:** Rs 60 crore (Q2); Rs 117.7 crore (H1).

### Highlights
- Delivered 125 units (1.57 lakh sq ft) in Q2; 177 units (2.07 lakh sq ft) in H1.
- Acquired 9.72 acres of land during the quarter.
- Eldeco City Courtyard (commercial): 45% of area sold.
- *Record Note:* The company continues to execute on its land pipeline, consistent with the "aggressive expansion" noted in Q1.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets launching projects 5, 6, and 7 (Shaheed Path, Palm Springs, Namah) by March '27, subject to approvals.
- Management aims to triple the company size over the next 5-6 years.
- Latitude 27 revenue recognition likely to shift to next financial year (previously targeted March-May 2027).
- Management expects inflation + 3-4% annual price increases.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Lucknow market described as "robust" and "undersupplied."
- Residential demand is strong; horizontal (plots/villas) market is resilient to price hikes.
- Group housing (vertical) shows some resistance to price hikes; management is "careful and calibrated."
- Sweet spot for demand: Rs 1 crore – Rs 3 crore.
- Commercial market: Not a deep GCC-driven market; limited to local shopping centers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin fell to 56% (vs 62% in previous quarter) due to product mix (Imperia plots/villas).
- Latitude 27 EBITDA margin expected at ~30%.
- Management is passing on land cost increases; construction cost increases are being partially absorbed/passed on.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focus on building development pipeline while maintaining capital discipline.
- Payment moratoriums from LDA (Lucknow Development Authority) help control capital deployment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Management emphasized capital discipline and controlled deployment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- State elections expected to cause a slowdown in the approval process.
- "Touch-and-go" situation for revenue recognition of Latitude 27 (completion certificate timing).
- Vertical housing segment shows some price resistance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- *Q: Why did margins fall QoQ?* A: Product mix (Imperia plots/villas vs. previous mix).
- *Q: Latitude 27 revenue recognition?* A: Likely next FY; construction is ahead of schedule.
- *Q: Total GDV/Future pipeline?* A: Management estimated ~Rs 5,500 crore to be executed over 5-6 years.
- *Q: Impact of price hikes?* A: Horizontal market is resilient; vertical market shows resistance.
- *Q: Gorakhpur update?* A: Term sheet signed, but no material capital exposure yet.

### One-line takeaway
Eldeco reported strong H1 growth and continues to build a robust development pipeline, though revenue recognition for key projects like Latitude 27 may shift to the next fiscal year.
