# Signatureglobal (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed strong confidence in meeting full-year targets, supported by healthy pre-sales run-rates, expanding land bank, and steady demand in NCR.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained annual pre-sales target of Rs 10,000 crore (Rs 100 billion) and revenue completions target of Rs 5,000 crore (Rs 50 billion).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remains positive; demand in Gurugram/NCR remains steady with single-digit price escalations acting as an inflation hedge.
- **New topics or risks:** Entry into Farrukhnagar micro-market for organized farmhouse/villa community; explicit accounting for annual NGT/GRAP construction bans by building a 10-month operational plan.
- **Last quarter's promises:** Achieved ~40% of annual sales target in H1 despite only one major launch; collections recovered from Q1 aberration, reaching Rs 1,700 crore in H1.

---

### Key numbers
*   **H1 FY27 Pre-sales:** Rs 3,800 crore (~1.6 million sq ft, 675 units sold).
*   **Realization:** Crossed Rs 17,000/sq ft across the portfolio.
*   **H1 FY27 Collections:** Rs 1,700 crore.
*   **H1 FY27 Operating Cash Surplus:** Rs 480 crore (28% of collections).
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue Recognized (Completions):** ~Rs 1,400 crore.
*   **Q2 Margins:** Gross margin at 36%; EBITDA margin at 18%.
*   **H1 PAT:** ~Rs 50 crore.

---

### Highlights
*   Achieved 38-40% of full-year pre-sales target in H1 with just one major launch (Tonino Lamborghini Residences, Sector 71, Gurugram).
*   Added ~10 million sq ft of developable area in H1 (net addition 8.4 million sq ft post sales), expanding future pipeline.
*   Acquired/collaborated on a 194-acre land parcel in Farrukhnagar, Gurugram (25 acres acquired, 169 acres collaboration) to develop an organized farmhouse/villa community and resort.
*   Collaborated on 31 acres in Sohna (near Project Dakshin), with additional additions in Sector 71 and Sector 37D.
*   Progressing on the 5.6 million sq ft mixed-use yield project with RMZ Group on Southern Peripheral Road (KPF appointed as architect).

---

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Pre-sales:** Maintained FY27 target at Rs 10,000 crore (targeting 60% of pre-sales in H2 FY27).
*   **Revenue Completions:** Maintained FY27 target at Rs 5,000 crore.
*   **Operating Cash Surplus:** Projected to reach Rs 1,200 crore - Rs 1,500 crore by the end of FY27.
*   **Launch Pipeline:** 27 million sq ft in preparation for launch over the next two years (GDV Rs 30,000 crore - Rs 35,000 crore). Upcoming H2 launches include 2+ million sq ft in Sector 71, iconic single tower on Dwarka Expressway (Sector 37D), and 400 units in Sohna (Dakshin).

---

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Real estate demand in Gurugram remains stable, driven by connectivity improvements along Dwarka Expressway, SPR, and Sohna.
*   Primary market pricing benefits from limited secondary stock availability, with post-2022 mid-income supply still under construction.
*   Prices rising at single-digit rates year-on-year, providing an inflation hedge without dampening demand.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Construction cost inflation expected at 7% - 9% (late single digits) for the year across material and labor.
*   Full-year construction spend expected to remain under Rs 2,000 crore.
*   Brokerage expense on premium projects (e.g., Tonino Lamborghini) ranges between 3% and 5% (blended ~4%).

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Full-year business development spend expected in line with operating cash flows (Rs 1,200 crore - Rs 1,500 crore).
*   Delivered 19 million sq ft to date; 9 million sq ft approaching delivery; 50-51 million sq ft ongoing/upcoming pipeline.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Debt:** Stand at Rs 450 crore (Gross debt at Rs 2,870 crore; Cash & cash equivalents at Rs 2,400+ crore).
*   **Credit Rating:** CARE A+ with a Stable outlook.
*   Business development expanded without driving up net debt, as acquisitions were funded via operating cash flows and land collaborations.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **GRAP/NGT Construction Bans:** Pollution-related seasonal bans in NCR impact active construction windows; management factors a 10-month working year into project timelines.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On upcoming H2 launches and GDV:** Management confirmed 2+ million sq ft in Sector 71, <0.5 million sq ft in Sector 37D, and ~400 units in Sohna are planned for H2.
*   **On full-year business development spend:** Management stated BD spending will track operating cash flows (Rs 1,200 - 1,500 crore) without raising net debt significantly.
*   **On Farrukhnagar market potential:** Management explained this caters to legit, gated, 1+ acre farmhouses near Delhi Airport, taking advantage of Delhi master plan restrictions requiring >2.5 acres for legal farmhouses.
*   **On lower H1 construction cost spend (Rs 880 cr vs Rs 970 cr YoY):** Management clarified that heavy execution on plotted developments (City of Colours Manesar & Dakshin X Factor) required lower capital outlay compared to high-rises.

---

### One-line takeaway
Signature Global maintains a strong balance sheet with net debt at Rs 450 crore while expanding its developable pipeline to 50+ million sq ft and staying on track to achieve Rs 10,000 crore pre-sales in FY27.
