# Signatureglobal (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust H1 sales of INR38 billion, strong cash surplus generation, and maintained full-year guidance supported by resilient mid-income and premium housing demand in Gurugram and NCR.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (Annual pre-sales guidance at INR10,000 crore).
- Tone and demand view: Remained positive, emphasizing strong performance in the upper middle/entry-level premium segment (Tonino Lamborghini Residences) and resilient demand.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Farrukhnagar farmhouse/villa community development and Delhi Master Plan tailwinds were highlighted as new topics; Middle East conflict references disappeared.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: H1 sales achieved ~40% of the annual target (INR38 billion), aligning with management's plan.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Pre-sales: INR38 billion (1.6 million sq ft, ~675 units)
- H1 FY27 Collections: INR17 billion
- H1 FY27 Operating Cash Surplus: INR4.8 billion (~28% of collections)
- Portfolio Realizations: >INR17,000 per sq ft
- Net Debt: INR4.5 billion (Gross debt: INR28.7 billion, Cash: INR24+ billion)
- Credit Rating: CARE A+ with stable outlook
- H1 FY27 Revenue recognized from completions: INR14 billion
- H1 FY27 Gross Profit margin: 31% (Q2 FY27: 36%)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 13% (Q2 FY27: 18%)
- Q2 FY27 PAT: INR50 crores (Note: Results filing mentioned Net Profit of INR67 crores)
- H1 FY27 Business Development / New area added: ~10 million sq ft developable area

### Highlights
- H1 pre-sales reached INR38 billion (~40% of the INR100 billion annual guidance).
- Added ~10 million sq ft of developable area in H1, including a 194-acre land parcel (25 acres acquired, 169 acres via collaboration) in Farrukhnagar, Gurugram, for a farmhouse/villa community, and 31 acres in Sohna.
- Strong response for Tonino Lamborghini Residences (Sector 71, Gurugram; >800 units).
- RMZ Group mixed-use development (5.6 million sq ft on SPR) progressing well on design and approvals with architect KPF.
- Portfolio status: 19 million sq ft delivered to date; 9 million sq ft reaching delivery stage soon; 23 million sq ft launched since Feb 2024.

### Guidance & outlook
- Pre-sales: Management maintained annual guidance of INR100 billion.
- Completions/Revenue: Management maintained annual completion guidance of INR50 billion.
- Collections & OCF: Full-year collections expected to rise significantly in H2, with cash surplus targeted between INR12 billion to INR15 billion.
- Business Development spend: Expected to align with operating cash flows (INR12 billion to INR15 billion).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Home sales across top seven cities crossed 1 lakh units in Q3 2026 (up 10% QoQ, ANAROCK report); new launches up 18%, prices up 7% YoY.
- Delhi NCR saw fastest price rise at 12%; Gurugram accounts for 43% of NCR sales.
- Real estate acting as an inflation hedge; limited secondary supply is driving primary sales.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Construction cost escalation tracking at late single digits (7%–9% annually).
- Brokerage slabs for projects like Lamborghini range between 3%–5% (closer to 4%).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Business development in H1 added ~10 million sq ft (net addition 8.4 million sq ft), funded via operating cash flows and collaboration agreements without significant debt increase.
- Farrukhnagar project leverages Delhi Master Plan rules (minimum 2.5 acres required in Delhi for farmhouses vs >1 acre in Gurugram), providing a competitive advantage.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands at INR4.5 billion; gross debt at INR28.7 billion with INR24+ billion cash.
- Debt remains within comfortable limits due to operating cash flow funding and collaboration models.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Recurrent NGT bans/GRAP restrictions on construction due to pollution are factored into annual planning (assuming ~10 working months per year).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Parvez Qazi (Nuvama Group) on H2 launches and BD spend:** Management detailed upcoming launches in Sector 71 (2 million+ sq ft), Dwarka Expressway (single iconic tower), and Sohna (400 units). BD spend for the year is expected to match operating cash flows (INR12 billion–INR15 billion).
- **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital) on sustenance sales and Farrukhnagar:** Management explained sales are aligned with the 40% H1 target and lack of heavy new launches in early H1. Market size for Farrukhnagar is unorganized/high-ticket in Delhi, but Signatureglobal offers an organized, approved product at a lower ticket size.
- **Adhidev Chattopadhyay (ICICI Securities) on GRAP impact:** Management stated construction bans are perpetual and factored into guidance by planning for 10 active months per year.
- **Harsh Pathak (Motilal Oswal) on construction costs and cash flow line items:** Management attributed lower H1 construction cash flow spend to heavy-lifting on plotted developments (City of Colours in Manesar and Dakshin industrial plotting) nearing completion, which involve lower direct construction costs.

### One-line takeaway
Signatureglobal delivered a solid H1 with INR38 billion in pre-sales and robust business development, maintaining its full-year pre-sales guidance of INR100 billion.
