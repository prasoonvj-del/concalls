# EFC (I) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported healthy revenue growth and strong profitability supported by its integrated business model across leasing, design & build, and furniture manufacturing.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations: Rs 332 crore (up 30% YoY from approx Rs 255 crore in Q2 FY26)
- Consolidated EBITDA: Rs 134 crore (up 21% YoY)
- Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 77 crore (up 36% YoY from Rs 57 crore in Q2 FY26)
- EBITDA margin: approx 40%
- PAT margin: approx 23%
- H1 FY27 consolidated revenue: approx Rs 650 crore (up 30% YoY from Rs 474 crore)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: approx Rs 257 crore (up from Rs 213 crore)
- H1 FY27 PAT: approx Rs 148 crore (up 43% YoY from Rs 103 crore)
- H1 FY27 EPS: Rs 10.11 (vs Rs 7.09 in H1 FY26)
- Leasing segment Q2 revenue: approx Rs 168 crore (up 30% YoY from Rs 129 crore); H1 revenue: approx Rs 322 crore (vs Rs 252 crore)
- Design & Build (D&B) segment Q2 revenue: approx Rs 129 crore (up 16% YoY from Rs 112 crore); H1 revenue: approx Rs 230 crore (vs Rs 196 crore)
- Furniture segment Q2 revenue: approx Rs 35 crore (up 149% YoY from Rs 14 crore); H1 revenue: approx Rs 63 crore (vs Rs 27 crore)
- D&B order book: Rs 300 crore plus
- Ultrafresh acquisition valuation: Rs 54 crore (approx 1.49x FY26 revenue)
- EFC share price for swap transaction: Rs 270 per share

### Highlights
- Operating across three business verticals: Leasing, Design & Build, and Furniture Manufacturing.
- Proposed corporate restructuring filed with NCLT to demerge the leased-premises managed office operations into the listed parent EFC (I) Limited, while keeping owned properties in the wholly-owned subsidiary EFC Limited.
- Acquired Ultrafresh Modular Solutions via a share swap (valued at Rs 54 crore, issuing shares at Rs 270 each), gaining modular home furniture capabilities, a manufacturing facility in Nalagarh (Himachal Pradesh), and ~160 touchpoints across Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
- Monetized a property via Sanvritti Alpha Private Limited through an asset sale and leaseback model, generating a return on capital of around 39% to 40%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident in achieving annual targets across all three verticals for revenue and profit.
- Annual D&B growth target set at 50% to 60%.
- Expected normalized margins: Consolidated EBITDA margin upwards of 20% plus; Leasing corporate level at 25% (center level 30%); D&B at 18% to 22%; Furniture Manufacturing at optimal capacity around 24% to 25%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's office market recorded ~66 million square feet of absorption during the first 9 months of '26, driven by GCC expansion and data investments.
- India furniture market estimated at $25 to $29 billion, growing steadily.
- Enterprises are prioritizing flexible, efficient workspaces and speed of execution without large upfront capital commitments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 margins saw slight pressure due to a substantial increase in prices of materials and services.
- Quarterly margins fluctuate due to project execution timing differences and milestone billing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Ultrafresh acquisition expands manufacturing capacity into North India and adds a modular kitchen/wardrobe product segment.
- Asset-light leasing model depends on managed workspaces rather than real estate ownership, though 8% to 10% of the portfolio is owned.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- D&B working capital cycle expected to be maintained around 60 to 90 days (or 90 to 120 days inclusive of retention).
- Retention payments generally run around 5% of total project value.
- Asset-backed debt on owned properties is reduced via asset sale and leaseback monetization transactions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- D&B and project-driven businesses face quarterly timing variations based on execution schedules and milestone billing.
- Working capital deployment is required for institutional D&B projects.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pramod Mohan (GG Family Office) asked about portfolio occupancy and the Sanvritti Alpha transaction.** Management stated occupancy remains above 90% and explained that Sanvritti Alpha involved the sale and leaseback of a redeveloped Pune property, generating a 39%–40% ROCE and aiding debt reduction.
- **Sahil Singh asked about D&B growth slowing to 16% in H1 against a 50%-60% annual target, and margin divergence.** Management answered that H1/quarterly numbers are not indicative due to project timelines, and reiterated confidence in the annual target supported by a >Rs 300 crore order book.
- **Vishal Kunden asked about the rationale and valuation of the Ultrafresh acquisition.** Management stated it was valued at 1.49x FY26 revenue (Rs 54 crore total) based on big four valuations, while EFC shares were valued at Rs 270 each, bringing strategic manufacturing and distribution access in North India.
- **Ashutosh Chaudhary asked about D&B working capital and debtor aging.** Management stated D&B working capital cycles are maintained between 60 to 90 days (up to 120 days with retention) and do not grow disproportionately.
- **Shailendra (Damani & Damani) asked about demerger rationale, corporate governance, and arm's length pricing.** Management explained the demerger separates asset-light operations from asset ownership, and assured that all inter-company and subsidiary transactions adhere strictly to arm's length pricing determined via big four accounting firms.

### One-line takeaway
EFC delivered 30% YoY revenue growth and 36% PAT growth in Q2 FY27, backed by its integrated leasing, D&B, and furniture model, while advancing structural demerger and acquisition plans.
