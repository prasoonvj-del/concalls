# Dev Accelerator Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, tripled PAT, and strong operating leverage.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 80.2 crore (up 54.8% YoY from Rs 51.8 crore; up 49.2% QoQ from Rs 53.8 crore).
- **Consolidated Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 134 crore (up 24.7% YoY).
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 63 crore (up 51.7% YoY from Rs 41.5 crore).
- **Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 105 crore (up 30.5% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA (Ind-AS, Q2 FY27):** Rs 40.8 crore (up 54.3% YoY); Margin: 50.8%.
- **Consolidated EBITDA (H1 FY27):** Rs 71.1 crore (up 34.4% YoY); Margin: 53.0% (vs 49.2% in H1 FY26).
- **Consolidated Profit Before Tax (Q2 FY27):** Rs 6.3 crore (vs Rs 1.7 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 5.5 crore (vs Rs 1.8 crore YoY).
- **Consolidated PAT (H1 FY27):** Rs 7 crore (vs Rs 1.9 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Consolidated Cash EBIT (EBITDA less lease rental paid, Q2 FY27):** Rs 19.1 crore (vs Rs 9.1 crore YoY); Margin: 23.8% (up 625 bps YoY).
- **Consolidated Cash EBIT (H1 FY27):** Rs 31.5 crore; Margin: 23.5% (vs 17.9% in H1 FY26).
- **Standalone EBITDA (Ind-AS, Q2 FY27):** Rs 43.9 crore (up 58.8% YoY); Margin: 69.7% (vs 66.6% YoY).
- **Standalone EBITDA (H1 FY27) Margin:** 68.2%.
- **Operational Portfolio:** ~1.16 million square feet (vs 0.89 million square feet YoY) across 17,400 operational seats, 28 centers, 12 cities, and 6 states.
- **Occupied Seats / Occupancy:** 15,800 occupied seats (vs 12,019 seats in Q2 FY26); Overall occupancy at 91%.
- **Enterprise Client Revenue Contribution:** ~75% (vs 65% in Q2 FY26 and 70% in Q1 FY27).
- **Revenue-to-Rent Ratio:** Improved to 2.4x.
- **Brokerage Expense:** Reduced to 1.13% of revenue (vs 1.6% previously).
- **Pipeline Capacity:** 17 new centers covering ~2.8 million square feet (targeting a total of ~4.16 million square feet across 49 centers).
- **Design & Build Subsidiary (Neddle and Thread) H1 Revenue:** ~Rs 30 crore (external clients).
- **Technology Subsidiary (SaaSJoy Solutions) H1 Revenue:** ~Rs 2.8 crore.
- **Capital Raising (August 2026):** Raised Rs 100 crore through secured, rated, listed NCDs.
- **Gross Debt / Net Debt (as of September 2026):** Gross debt at ~Rs 222 crore; Cash and cash equivalents at ~Rs 37 crore; Net debt at ~Rs 185 crore (vs Rs 89 crore at the end of last year).

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly consolidated revenue of Rs 80.2 crore.
- Demonstrated operating leverage: Employee costs remained largely unchanged during H1, and other expenses decreased from 11% to 1.7% of total revenue.
- Ahmedabad asset "Capital One" (3.15 lakh square feet) became operational with ~95% pre-leasing.
- Signed a development management project of ~8.6 lakh square feet in Ahmedabad (India's largest managed office space asset), planning an investment of ~Rs 100 crore over time for ~8,500 seats targeting ~Rs 120 crore revenue at scale.
- Launched a partnership with Inc42 to source PropTech solutions.
- Engaged in conversations with larger REIT players to acquire existing assets under the PropCo-OpCo model.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to bring pipeline centers into operation in phases, deepen enterprise relationships, grow GCC services, and improve cash conversion in H2 FY27.
- Management intends to scale the platform beyond 4 million square feet.

### Demand & industry commentary
- CBRE report cited office leasing across India's top 9 cities reached 66.4 million square feet (up 8% YoY), with Global Capability Centers (GCCs) accounting for 28 million square feet (42%).
- Colliers report noted flexible operators leased 12.6 million square feet across top 7 markets (up 37% YoY, 23% of overall leasing).
- Approximately 78% of DevX's operational area is in Tier 2 states.
- Target audience for GCC platform includes mid-to-large-size organizations with $250 million to $750 million in revenue.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage resulted in flat employee costs and a decline in other expenses to 1.7% of revenue in H1.
- Standalone EBITDA margin stood at 69.7% in Q2 FY27.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned investment of ~Rs 100 crore for fit-outs, furniture, and security deposits in the 8.6 lakh square feet Ahmedabad development management project.
- Expansion plans aim to scale total operational footprint to 4.16 million square feet across 49 centers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Raised Rs 100 crore via NCDs in August 2026 to refinance existing loans and fund expansion.
- Consolidated gross debt at Rs 222 crore and net debt at Rs 185 crore as of September 2026.
- Management clarified that Ind-AS lease liabilities and Right-of-Use assets represent operating lease commitments rather than traditional bank borrowings.
- Priority for H2 is cash conversion through faster billing and collections.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management in the transcript.
