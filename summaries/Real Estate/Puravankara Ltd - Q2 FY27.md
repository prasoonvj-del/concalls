# Puravankara Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong growth in sales, collections, and handovers alongside meaningful debt reduction.

### Key numbers
- Presales: INR2,762 crores (up 109% YoY) on sales volume of 2.38 million square feet.
- Average price realization: INR11,607 per square foot (up 32% YoY).
- Customer collections: INR1,301 crores (up 24% YoY) in Q2; INR2,500 crores (up 31% YoY) for H1.
- H1 Total Revenue: INR1,928 crores (up 60% YoY).
- Q2 Revenue from operations: INR1,033 crores (vs INR644 crores YoY).
- Q2 Total income: INR1,051 crores (up 59% YoY).
- Q2 EBITDA: INR289 crores; EBITDA margin: 27%.
- Q2 Profit After Tax (PAT): INR82 crores (vs loss of INR42 crores in Q2 FY26); H1 PAT: INR107 crores (vs loss of INR111 crores YoY).
- H1 total sales including asset monetization: INR4,200 crores (37.51% of annual guidance).
- Gross debt: INR3,763 crores (declined by approx INR180 crores); Net debt: INR2,366 crores (reduced by INR470 crores).
- Net debt-to-equity ratio: 1.26x; Weighted average cost of debt: 10.94%.
- Purva Zentech commercial asset monetization: INR626 crores asset value (INR50-60 crores deferred consideration expected).
- Ongoing portfolio: 33.61 million square feet at the end of September.
- Planned launch program: 14.5 million square feet with estimated GDV of approx INR22,600 crores.
- H1 Business development / land additions: INR14,100 crores estimated GDV.

### Highlights
- Monetized 5.46 lakh square feet of commercial office space at Purva Zentech for INR626 crores enterprise value.
- Delivered 977 homes (0.97 million square feet) in Q2; 1,722 homes for H1.
- Entered the NCR market via a land acquisition from Greater Noida Authority in the DMIC area; added a Goregaon (Bangur Nagar) redevelopment project in Mumbai.
- Q2 includes an exceptional gain of INR149 crores.

### Guidance & outlook
- FY27 annual sales guidance maintained at INR11,200 crores (including commercial sales).
- Full-year collections guidance maintained at INR6,000 crores.
- Operating surplus guidance for the year expected to cross the INR1,000 crores mark.
- Planned launches of INR15,000 crores to INR16,000 crores targeted for the current financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management sees no slowdown or phasing out of residential demand, supported by job generation, urban migration, and high consumer confidence.
- Industry consolidation continues, benefiting top listed developers with strong track records and brand value even amidst minor segment fluctuations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Weighted average EBITDA margin at the project level is around 30%.
- Other expenses increased due to approval expenses, statutory taxes, and initial marketing costs for planned launches (12 in South, 4 in West).
- Redevelopment initial capital outlay is about 60% lower than outright land purchases, with project EBITDA margins ranging from 22% to 30%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Remaining capex for Aerocity Phase 2 is around INR450 crores; Hebbal construction capex estimated at close to INR700 crores for 1.3 million square feet.
- Targeting land and GDV additions of around INR15,000 crores annually.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt reduced by ~INR180 crores QoQ; net debt reduced by INR470 crores QoQ to INR2,366 crores.
- Debt repayment of INR250 crores against Purva Zentech made from transaction proceeds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Bangalore approvals faced some challenges due to the upgradation of computer systems.
- Cuffe Parade (Mumbai) launch might slip from Q4 into Q1 of the next financial year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Deepak Purswani (Svan Investments) asked about launch confidence for big-ticket projects in H2 and meeting the INR11,200 crore guidance.** Management (Mallanna Sasalu and Rajat Rastogi) responded that while some approvals in Bangalore and Mumbai (like Cuffe Parade) might push into next year, the strong pipeline keeps them confident of hitting guidance.
- **Deepak Purswani asked about the rise in other expenses impacting EBITDA margins.** Neeraj Gautam (CFO) explained that other expenses include upfront approval costs, rates, taxes, and marketing expenses for scheduled project launches.
- **Rahul Singh (Eternal Capital) inquired about assumptions behind commercial surplus and rental income.** Rajat Rastogi stated they estimate surplus using INR65 per sq ft rentals at a 7.75% cap rate for Aerocity, and expect INR330 crores in annual rental income once Aerocity and Hebbal are fully ready and leased.
- **Sarthak Jain (Individual Investor) asked about return thresholds (IRR/ROCE) for new land acquisitions.** Mallanna Sasalu stated gross profits below 25% are not pursued, though JDAs with lower capital risk may be evaluated around 20%.
- **Vishal Singh (Individual Investor) asked what drove Q2 presales growth.** Mallanna Sasalu replied it was a combination of new launches, land acquisitions, and strong sustenance sales led by Bangalore and Mumbai.

### One-line takeaway
Puravankara reported robust Q2 FY27 presales and revenue growth, significantly reduced net debt through asset monetization, and maintained its full-year sales guidance of INR11,200 crores.
