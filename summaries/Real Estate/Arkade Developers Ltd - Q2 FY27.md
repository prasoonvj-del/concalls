# Arkade Developers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong collections and robust project pipeline, offset by moderate pre-sales and a YoY decline in revenue recognition.

### Key numbers
- **Collections:** Rs. 183 crore (up 22% YoY) in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 collections at Rs. 347 crore (up 8% YoY).
- **Pre-sales:** Rs. 151 crore in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 pre-sales at Rs. 306 crore.
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 145 crore in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 revenue at Rs. 292 crore.
- **Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 35 crore in Q2 FY27 (EBITDA margin at 24%); H1 FY27 operating EBITDA at Rs. 62 crore (EBITDA margin at 21.4%).
- **Gross Margin:** Improved to 35% in Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 gross margin at 32%).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 25 crore in Q2 FY27 with a PAT margin of 17.14%; H1 FY27 PAT at Rs. 44 crore (PAT margin at 15.1%).
- **Liquidity / Net Debt:** Liquid cash balance of Rs. 119 crore against gross debt of Rs. 56 crore, resulting in a net liquid cash position of Rs. 63 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Development Pipeline:** Total estimated GDV of approximately Rs. 19,000 crore across 5.7 million square feet of saleable carpet area.
- **Recent Business Development:** Added 4 redevelopment projects across Malad, Kandivali, and Borivali spanning ~11 acres with ~15 lakh sq. ft. saleable carpet area and ~Rs. 5,000 crore GDV potential.
- **Approval Costs Incurred:** ~Rs. 70 crore incurred towards approval costs for upcoming projects (lying in inventory).

### Highlights
- Handed over flats at Arkade Eden (Malad West) and received the OC for Arkade Pearl Phase 1 ahead of the committed RERA timeline.
- Management attributed the YoY revenue decline to a higher base in the corresponding quarter last year when four projects crossed the 25% construction threshold for revenue recognition, a milestone absent in Q2 FY27.
- Entered into an MOU for a greenfield/land parcel in Bhandup (Woollen Mills); transaction expected to complete in coming months.
- Thane Greenfield project (Rs. 2,000 crore GDV) received its initial LOI approval; awaiting CC and RERA application.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targeted the launch of two projects with a combined GDV of ~Rs. 2,850 crore in Q3 FY27.
- Thane Greenfield project planned for launch in Q4 FY27 (tower-wise phase launches over two years).
- Management remains confident in achieving full-year pre-sales targets, supported by upcoming Q3 launches.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Pre-sales remained moderate in H1 due to new launches being shifted by a couple of quarters, though existing projects enjoy healthy demand.
- Competition in the Mumbai redevelopment market has increased drastically over the past 12–18 months with fair share of participation from various developers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin improvement to 35% driven by higher realizations on existing ongoing projects and natural appreciation post-OC receipt (Arkade Eden and Arkade Pearl).
- Construction expenses are inventorized and recognized in tandem with revenue percentages over a 2-3 year period.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Focused strictly on the MMR region (Western suburbs, Thane, evaluating South Mumbai, North Mumbai, and Navi Mumbai proposals).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash positive with Rs. 63 crore net liquid cash (Rs. 119 crore cash vs. Rs. 56 crore gross debt).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as major challenges by management, though pre-sales moderation was noted due to launch timing shifts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sahil Patani (Stokes Capital) on demand moderation and large-scale execution:** Management clarified that pre-sales moderation was specific to Arkade due to delayed launch timelines, while existing demand remains steady. For large projects like Filmistan (~Rs. 4,000 crore GDV), they have onboarded experienced consultants (architects, MEP, landscape, execution contractors) and expanded manpower.
2. **Rohan Joshi (Individual Investor) on Arkade Rare delay allegations:** Management refuted data on project delays, stating they actually pre-poned the RERA handover date from December 2028 to December 2027.
3. **Trishla on revenue decline and margin drivers:** Management explained the YoY revenue drop was due to a high base effect from crossing the 25% construction threshold last year. Gross margins improved due to better price realizations and OC receipts.
4. **Sahil Vaidya on Thane project launch strategy:** Management confirmed a phase-wise launch plan across 5 towers on 6 acres over the next two years.
5. **Sarthak on Mumbai redevelopment competition:** Management acknowledged a drastic increase in competition over the last 12–18 months but highlighted their 4 decades of experience, 15+ completed redevelopment projects, and strict location/GDV-based bidding criteria as differentiators.
6. **Jay Jain on cash flows and approval costs:** Management stated that ~30% of the cost for ongoing projects is yet to be incurred, and ~Rs. 70 crore has been incurred toward approval costs for upcoming projects (lying in inventory).

### One-line takeaway
Arkade Developers reported a muted Q2 in terms of revenue and pre-sales due to launch timing shifts, but maintained a strong net-cash balance sheet, healthy collections growth (up 22% YoY), and a massive ~Rs. 19,000 crore GDV pipeline backed by aggressive MMR redevelopment additions.
