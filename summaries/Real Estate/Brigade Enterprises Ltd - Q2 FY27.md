# Brigade Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong real estate EBITDA growth, robust collections, and a return to an active project launch cycle.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,250 crore
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 478 crore (margin 38%, up 1,200 bps YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 219 crore (up 29% YoY); PAT after minority interest: Rs 194 crore
- **Real Estate Segment:** Revenue Rs 760 crore; EBITDA Rs 178 crore (up 62% YoY, margin 24% vs 12% YoY)
- **Leasing Segment Revenue:** Rs 329 crore (down 3% YoY due to a base effect of Rs 26 crore one-time finance lease income in Q2 FY26; up 4% normalized)
- **Leasing Segment EBITDA:** Rs 242 crore (margin 73%)
- **Hospitality Segment Revenue:** Rs 161 crore; EBITDA Rs 59 crore (up 41% YoY, margin 37%, up 700 bps YoY)
- **Residential Net Sales (Presales):** Rs 1,827 crore on 1.32 million sq ft (up 72% QoQ in value, 78% QoQ in volume; 10% lower YoY due to launch timing)
- **Average Realization:** Rs 13,841 per sq ft (up 29% YoY)
- **Collections:** Total Rs 1,633 crore (Real Estate Rs 1,115 crore, Leasing Rs 343 crore, Hospitality Rs 175 crore)
- **Net Debt:** Rs 2,765 crore (Gross debt Rs 5,439 crore; Cash & cash equivalents Rs 2,674 crore)
- **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.31x
- **Average Cost of Debt:** 7.61%
- **Operational Leasing Portfolio:** 9.6 million sq ft at 90% occupancy (added 0.27 million sq ft in Q2)
- **Hospitality Metrics:** ARR Rs 7,876 (up 11% YoY); RevPAR Rs 5,922 (up 10% YoY); Occupancy 75% (Bengaluru occupancy at 81%)

### Highlights
- Launched 2.4 million sq ft of residential projects in Q2 at an estimated GDV of ~Rs 3,000 crore (including Brigade Barcelona in Neopolis, Hyderabad at Rs 475 crore sales, Brigade Misty Greens in Mysuru, and Azalea at Brigade Meadows in Bengaluru).
- Added 0.27 million sq ft via completions of Brigade Square in Trivandrum (100% pre-leased) and Arcadia at Brigade Cornerstone Utopia in Bengaluru.
- BHVL's PAT more than doubled YoY to Rs 22 crore, aided by lower finance costs post-IPO debt repayment.
- Business development: Added ~12.5 to 13 million sq ft totally in H1 FY27 (~10 million sq ft in Q2), translating to ~Rs 13,000 crore of GDV (split roughly half-and-half between Bengaluru and Hyderabad/Chennai).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated the full-year presales guidance of **Rs 9,000 crore**, supported by ~7 million sq ft of launches (GDV Rs 7,600 crore) planned for H2 FY27.
- Management expects operating cash flow for the entire financial year to be higher than FY26.
- Net debt-to-equity ratio outlook for the year end is projected to remain comfortably under 0.5x (between 0.4x and 0.5x).
- Real estate revenue margin profile for FY27 is expected to be in the early 20s (22% to 24%); full-year residential revenue recognition is targeted at around Rs 5,000 crore.
- Office leasing portfolio is projected to grow from 9.5-9.6 million sq ft to 12.5 million sq ft by FY29, with overall rental income expected to reach ~Rs 1,700 crore in FY29.

### Demand & industry commentary
- RBI repo rate hike of 25 bps has not impacted customer sentiment on ground; home loan customer share holds steady.
- Office leasing demand continues to reflect a flight to quality: GCCs accounted for 41% of Q2 leasing, and green-certified tech parks captured 61% of market absorption.
- Tech sector job loss/AI concerns have not impacted residential demand; demand has become more premium, with talent shifting from IT firms to GCCs and other tech operations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average real estate realization increased 29% YoY to Rs 13,841/sq ft due to disciplined price hikes and a shift toward higher-end homes.
- Depreciation increased due to the completion of 2 leasing facilities (2.7 lakh sq ft) in Q2.
- Accounting adjustment under Ind AS 109 reinstated ~Rs 90 crore of BRS reconciliation items into creditors/payables.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Balance capex to complete the 6.8 million sq ft commercial development under construction is approximately Rs 3,350 crore (Breakdown for office: ~Rs 400 crore in H2 FY27, ~Rs 1,500 crore in FY28, ~Rs 1,500 crore in FY29).
- Balance payable for land payments is approximately Rs 2,000 crore, expected to be paid over the next 1 year (subject to milestone completions for JDs).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated gross debt stood at Rs 5,439 crore; net debt at Rs 2,765 crore. 85% of debt is backed by rental cash flow and GOP from Leasing and Hospitality.
- Trade receivables increased by ~Rs 200 crore because a major milestone was billed in September and collections were in progress (~Rs 250-260 crore remained to be collected).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Slower pace of absorption at Brigade Barcelona in Hyderabad compared to Brigade Gateway Hyderabad, though management remains confident in pricing and demand.
- Chennai project approvals could see a minor push-out into the next fiscal year due to government transition dynamics.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sucrit D. Patil (Eyesight Fintrade):** Asked about residential volume guidance and execution risks. Management reiterated the Rs 9,000 crore presales guidance and stated execution risks are routine (securing approvals).
2. **Biplab Debbarma (Emkay Global):** Asked about IT sector job losses impacting Bangalore resi demand. Management answered that demand remains resilient with premiumization and talent absorption by GCCs.
3. **Jimeet Shah (Monarch AIF):** Inquired about leasing status/vacancies at WTC Bengaluru and Twin Towers. Management noted active pipelines, 5% annual escalations, and re-leasing spreads at ~15% higher than previous rates.
4. **Harsh Pathak (Motilal Oswal):** Asked about trade receivables and lower reported construction cash outflows. Management clarified that a billing milestone in late September resulted in pending collections, and an Ind AS 109 accounting adjustment reinstated ~Rs 90 crore into payables.
5. **Parvez Qazi (Nuvama):** Asked about ticket-size distribution for upcoming Bangalore launches. Management stated that under-3-crore ticket sizes have strong absorption, with North Bangalore inventory mostly priced between Rs 1 crore and Rs 1.5 crore, and ~20% around Rs 3 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Brigade Enterprises reported a robust Q2 FY27 driven by strong real estate margins, steady annuity cash flows, and aggressive land/launch pipelines, while reiterating its full-year Rs 9,000 crore presales guidance.
