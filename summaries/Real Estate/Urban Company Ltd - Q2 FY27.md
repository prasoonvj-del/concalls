# Urban Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-17 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Solid execution with strong transaction and revenue growth, expanding adjusted EBITDA, and aggressive investments in new categories and international markets.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated transaction value (NTV) grew 39.1% YoY to ₹1,432.3 crores.
- Revenue from operations grew 41.4% to ₹537.3 crores.
- Annual transacting customers grew 34.5% YoY to 15.1 million.
- Adjusted loss recorded at ₹144 crores, which included ₹174 crores of investments in new businesses (Native and InstaHelp). Excluding these investments, adjusted EBITDA was positive at ₹30 crores, up 213% YoY.
- International markets (UAE and Saudi Arabia) revenue grew 66.2% YoY.
- Native's loss narrowed by 3.5% YoY; loss per NTV fell 19% QoQ from ₹346 to ₹279.
- Proposed fundraise / treasury flexibility: closed with ₹1,901 crores in treasury, and proposed a fundraise of up to ₹2,500 crores.

### Highlights
- Rapid user acquisition (46.3% YoY growth in transacting users) and compounding repeat behavior driven by micro-market densification.
- UC Instant is delivering services within an hour (often minutes) in 7–8 cities.
- Native water purifiers scaling up with new product launches (M3 water purifier, Lock Ultra); expanding into offline and modern retail channels cautiously.
- InstaHelp orders grew 63% QoQ to 6.23 million.
- International operations continuing strong growth with operations fully moved out of Q3 seasonality impacts.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated the goal to achieve consolidated profitability and breakeven by FY28, and a target of ₹1,000 crores PAT by FY31.
- Expects combined international markets growth in the range of 50% to 55%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Penetration in large categories and cities remains low (below 10%), with smaller cities growing faster.
- Service professional additions grew 16% YoY; liquidity coefficients improving ahead of the festive season.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Contribution margin expanded, aided by AI-led cost efficiencies, partner support cost optimization, and densification.
- Native R&D expenses and upfront investments form the bulk of current new business losses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Proposed a fundraise of up to ₹2,500 crores to provide strategic flexibility for organic expansion and potential M&A / bolt-on acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Treasury balance stood at ₹1,901 crores at the close of the period. Generated ₹119 crores in operating cash flows over the past four quarters.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High competitive intensity from competitors, to which management responds aggressively to maintain market share.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Morgan Stanley on Core growth drivers:** Management stated growth is driven by repeat cohorts, densification enabling UC Instant (1-hour delivery), AI-enabled training and quality control, and micro-market expansion.
- **Goldman Sachs on M&A strategy and fundraise:** Management clarified that the ₹2,500 crore fundraise is to provide strategic flexibility for organic build-out, international expansion, and potential value-accretive bolt-on M&A.
- **Bank of America on dilution vs. targets:** Management noted that the fundraise provides a cushion for aggressive execution and potential competitive intensity, while keeping medium- and long-term FY31 targets unchanged.
- **Motilal Oswal on partner availability/churn:** Management stated partner acquisition focuses on quality, customer-centricity, and earnings retention; steady-state payout efficiency remains targeted at ~₹150/hour for gig workers.
- **JM Financial on professional growth and margins:** Management highlighted that service professionals are a leading indicator, and contribution margins are improving due to technology-led efficiencies and scale.

### One-line takeaway
Urban Company delivered robust top-line growth and expanding core profitability while aggressively investing in new verticals like Native and InstaHelp and seeking a ₹2,500 crore fundraise for strategic flexibility.
