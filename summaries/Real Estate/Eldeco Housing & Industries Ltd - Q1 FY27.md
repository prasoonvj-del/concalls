# Eldeco Housing & Industries Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive – Strong growth in revenue and profitability driven by execution momentum and high-margin horizontal project contributions.

### Key numbers
*   **Total Income:** Rs 50.3 crore (+63% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 18.7 crore (+243% YoY); **Margin:** 37.1%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 15.1 crore (+382% YoY); **Margin:** 30.0%.
*   **Booking Value:** Rs 105.7 crore.
*   **Area Booked:** 1.26 lakh sq ft.
*   **Collections:** Rs 131.2 crore (+68% YoY).
*   **Construction Spend:** Rs 57.8 crore (+47.2% YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Project Launches:** Successfully launched 'Eldeco Imperia Avenue' (44 units, Rs 14.6 crore booking value) and the 'Faith' tower at Eldeco Trinity.
*   **Land Bank:** Executed a contract for 50+ acres of contiguous land in Lucknow and completed aggregation of ~15 acres.
*   **Revenue Mix:** Imperia Phase 2 contributed ~85% of Q1 revenue, delivering gross margins of ~60%.
*   **Deliveries:** Delivered 52 homes (49,400 sq ft).
*   **Legacy Inventory:** ~Rs 75 crore of legacy inventory remains; management aims to liquidate 40-60% in FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Launches:** Management expects 100% of forthcoming projects (serial numbers 4-7 in presentation) to be launched within FY27.
*   **Long-term:** Management anticipates a "pivotal change" in the trajectory of presales and sales from FY28 onwards.
*   **Latitude 27:** Targeting revenue recognition of 15-20% of project GDV (Rs 275-300 crore) between March and May 2027.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Lucknow market is experiencing a "tremendous wave of development" with steady residential demand and upward-trending commercial rentals.
*   The city is seeing significant interest due to infrastructure developments like the Kanpur-Lucknow Expressway.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin expansion (37.1%) was driven by the high-margin horizontal development mix (Imperia).
*   Management intends to maintain a balance between vertical and horizontal developments on an opportunistic basis.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Focus remains on converting the expanded land pipeline into development opportunities to maintain capital efficiency.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Management noted the company has a strong balance sheet.
*   Regarding capital allocation (e.g., buybacks), management stated these are under "active consideration" but provided no specific commitment.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Approvals:** Management noted that project approvals can be volatile ("up and down").
*   **Execution:** Past projects received 4-month RERA extensions due to commodity price pressure and labor displacement.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: What drove the 37% EBITDA margin?* Management attributed it to the shift toward high-margin horizontal developments (Imperia) compared to previous vertical-heavy quarters.
*   *Q: What is the plan for legacy inventory?* Management aims to liquidate 40-60% of the remaining Rs 75 crore inventory in FY27.
*   *Q: When will Latitude 27 revenue be recognized?* Management is targeting March-May 2027 for 15-20% of the GDV.
*   *Q: Why not use buybacks given the stock price?* Management acknowledged the sentiment and stated that capital allocation methods are under active consideration, though they prioritize reinvestment for growth.
*   *Q: What is the outlook for FY28?* Management expects a significant change in the trajectory of presales and sales starting FY28.

### One-line takeaway
Eldeco delivered a strong Q1 with significant margin expansion driven by horizontal project sales and is aggressively expanding its land bank in Lucknow to support growth from FY28.
