# Embassy Developments Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved the strongest quarter in company history with record pre-sales and collections, driven by robust brand pull and successful new launches.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (FY27 pre-sales at Rs 8,000 crores, collections at Rs 3,000 crores, and launch GDV at Rs 19,400 crores).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted even more bullish following record Q2 pre-sales of Rs 3,225 crores and a landmark single-home transaction of Rs 711 crores at Juhu.
- New topics or risks: CEO Sachin Shah announced he is stepping down effective December 31, 2026 (succession plan in place, with Aditya Virwani taking over as MD & CEO starting January 1, 2027); promoter warrants fully exercised and Rs 363 crores of shareholder debt repaid. Old risks of completion-based accounting losses and legal overhangs persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Delivered on Q2 launch cadence, launching Riverine, South Reserve, and Terazza with an aggregate GDV of Rs 8,397 crores (surpassing the pre-launch estimate of Rs 7,500 crores).

### Key numbers
- Pre-sales (Q2 FY27): Approx. Rs 3,225 crores.
- Pre-sales (H1 FY27): Rs 4,093 crores (up 574% YoY; ~51% of full-year guidance of Rs 8,000 crores).
- Collections (Q2 FY27): Approx. Rs 546 crores (up 10% QoQ).
- Collections (H1 FY27): Approx. Rs 1,041 crores (up 53% YoY; ~35% of full-year guidance of Rs 3,000 crores).
- Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27): Rs 292 crores (vs. Rs 494 crores in Q2 FY26).
- Total income (Q2 FY27): Rs 311 crores (vs. Rs 537 crores in Q2 FY26).
- EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Negative Rs 131 crores.
- Loss After Tax (Q2 FY27): Rs 271 crores.
- Net institutional debt: Approx. Rs 3,982 crores as of September 30, 2026 (after cash and cash equivalents of Rs 1,258 crores).
- Net debt to equity: 0.42x as of September 30, 2026.
- Outstanding shareholder debt: Rs 1,103 crores as of September 30, 2026 (subsequently reduced via Rs 363 crores warrant repayment).
- Launch GDV (Q2 FY27): Rs 8,397 crores across 3 projects.
- Cumulative unsold inventory and sold receivables: Rs 25,536 crores (Rs 8,290 crores sold receivables, Rs 17,246 crores unsold inventory).

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever quarterly pre-sales of Rs 3,225 crores in Q2 FY27, surpassing the previous record of Rs 2,632 crores in Q4 FY26.
- Launched three projects in Q2 FY27: Riverine at Embassy Origins Bengaluru (GDV Rs 3,155 crores), South Reserve at Embassy Origins Bengaluru (GDV Rs 1,957 crores), and Embassy Terazza Juhu, Mumbai (GDV Rs 3,285 crores).
- Embassy Terazza Juhu recorded India's largest single residential transaction to date at approximately Rs 711 crores for a single residence.
- Completed allotment of Rs 3.25 crore convertible warrants to the promoter group at Rs 111.51 per warrant, utilizing proceeds to repay Rs 363 crores of shareholder debt. Warrants were fully exercised ahead of tenure.
- Bank loan facility credit rating upgraded by one notch from IVRA- stable to IVRA stable.
- Leadership transition announced: Sachin Shah to step down as CEO and Executive Director effective December 31, 2026, remaining as Senior Advisor; Aditya Virwani re-designated as Managing Director and CEO starting January 1, 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reaffirmed FY27 guidance across all operating parameters: launch GDV of approx. Rs 19,400 crores (9 owned projects of Rs 13,300 crores, 2 DM projects of Rs 6,100 crores), pre-sales of Rs 8,000 crores, and collections of Rs 3,000 crores.
- Long-term development pipeline stands at 20.3 million square feet with an estimated GDV of approx. Rs 23,470 crores.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is overwhelmingly driven by genuine end-users seeking homes.
- Embassy Citadel in Worli clocked over Rs 1,350 crores in sales in under 9 months without a sample flat or on-site sales office.
- Channel partners across Bangalore, Mumbai, and NCR view the Embassy brand as a gold standard.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Park Panvel project has around 2,000 laborers on-site, consuming Rs 40 crores monthly; OC for initial towers expected around mid-next year.
- Nashik land bank (1,400 acres within a 3,251-acre fully paid land bank) is being evaluated for data center development alongside amicable resolution of past issues with MIDC.
- Strengthened senior leadership with Ketan Nanda as Business Head for NCR and Alok Sherry as Head of Business Development in Mumbai.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net institutional debt stands at Rs 3,982 crores as of September 30, 2026, with net debt to equity at 0.42x.
- Management expects surplus cash generation to begin around the end of Q2/Q3 next year, leading to a reduction in debt levels.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Reported accounting losses due to the completion-based accounting method (timing gap between pre-sales/collections and revenue/profit recognition).
- Legal/Regulatory overhangs: Canara Bank NCLT case pending Supreme Court hearing (scheduled for November 19) and KIDB land resumption notice (stay obtained from High Court).
- Sky Terraces project delayed due to pending GBA planning authority meeting.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kirit Gogri (Family Office)** asked about collection visibility for H2 to reach Rs 3,000 crores and debt levels. *Management answered that foundation-stage project collections will scale up as execution advances, and debt reduction is expected to start around Q2/Q3 next year as cash surplus exceeds capex.*
- **Rusmik Oza (EquiResearch)** asked about launch scheduling, Panvel project cash flows and units, and H2 operating cash flow. *Management stated Panvel requires ~Rs 40 crores monthly, holds Rs 1,841 crores in sold receivables/inventory, targets Rs 300-400 crores in surplus, and confirmed positive operating cash flow by March.*
- **Roshan (Investor)** questioned rising losses, reverse merger benefits, and why the company didn't pursue an IPO instead. *Management reiterated that P&L losses reflect legacy completion-based accounting, goodwill is tested semi-annually, and overall cash surplus is close to Rs 30,000 crores across projects.*
- **Ranjay Popli (Investor)** asked about Blackstone debt conversion plans and GBA meeting delays for Sky Terraces. *Management stated converting Blackstone debt at current depressed stock prices is too dilutive and will be revisited later; Sky Terraces is delayed by GBA but targeted for Q4 (offset by Juhu outperformance).*
- **Vinayak (Investor)** asked about status of legal cases (Canara Bank, KIDB) and when pre-merger NAM/Indiabulls projects will show profits. *Management stated they have a very strong legal case in both matters (KIDB stay granted, Canara Bank hearing Nov 19) and explained that legacy ongoing projects (Edge, Verde, East Avenue) complete in FY28/FY29.*

### One-line takeaway
Embassy Developments delivered record pre-sales and collections in Q2 FY27 backed by strong brand momentum, while management maintained full-year guidance despite near-term reported accounting losses.
