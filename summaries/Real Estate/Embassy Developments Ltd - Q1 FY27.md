# Embassy Developments Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-16 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong operational momentum, healthy demand, and a clear path to scaling the residential platform despite reported accounting losses.

### Key numbers
*   **Presales:** Rs 868 crore (up 338% YoY).
*   **Collections:** Rs 496 crore (up 54% YoY).
*   **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 217 crore (vs Rs 681 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **EBITDA:** Negative Rs 106 crore (vs positive Rs 2 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Net Loss:** Rs 234 crore (vs net loss of Rs 166 crore in Q1 FY26).
*   **Construction Spend:** Rs 276 crore (approx. 56% of collections).
*   **Gross Institutional Debt:** Approx. Rs 4,500 crore.
*   **Net Institutional Debt:** Approx. Rs 3,300 crore.
*   **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Approx. Rs 1,200 crore.
*   **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.35x.
*   **Shareholder Debt:** Rs 1,063 crore (Rs 700 crore from Blackstone, Rs 363 crore from Embassy Group).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Focus:** Management emphasized that reported financial results are impacted by completion-based accounting, while underlying operating metrics (presales/collections) remain strong.
*   **Project Milestones:** Received Occupancy Certificates (OC) for Embassy One 09 (Gurgaon) and 5 towers at Golf City (Savroli).
*   **Approvals:** Embassy Citadel (Mumbai) secured upfront approval for all 81 floors.
*   **Capital Structure:** Board approved preferential allotment of convertible warrants to the promoter (Embassy Group) at Rs 111.51 per share to repay outstanding shareholder debt. Promoters committed to conversion within 6 months.
*   **Inventory:** Cumulative unsold inventory across residential and commercial launches stands at Rs 13,630 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **FY27 Presales Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 6,000 crore (owned developments) and Rs 2,000 crore (development management projects).
*   **FY27 Collections Guidance:** Maintained at approx. Rs 3,000 crore.
*   **Launch Pipeline:** 11 projects planned for FY27 with a combined GDV of Rs 19,400 crore. 4 projects are targeted for launch in Q2 FY27.
*   **Long-term Pipeline:** Approx. 20.3 million sq. ft. with estimated GDV of Rs 23,470 crore beyond FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains healthy across premium and luxury residential segments.
*   Industry is consolidating towards developers with proven track records and trust.
*   Bangalore market performance: Approx. 72% of launch inventory sold within 6 months.
*   Pan-India sales: Approx. 60% of inventory launched in FY26 has been sold.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Average cost of debt is around 14%.
*   Blackstone debt carries an 18% interest rate, which is accrued and added to gross debt.
*   Management aims to refinance high-cost debt as project collections accelerate.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Construction activity is progressing; management expects an inflection point by mid-next calendar year as projects move out of the ground and slab cycles accelerate.
*   Actively looking for new projects in NCR and MMR regions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net debt to equity target remains in the 0.3x to 0.35x range.
*   Management expects cost of debt to decrease first, followed by a reduction in overall debt levels starting March/April next year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Accounting Timing:** Significant timing difference between operating performance (presales/collections) and reported financial results due to completion-based revenue recognition.
*   **Regulatory/Approvals:** Potential for quarterly variability in launches due to regulatory processes (e.g., Bangalore planning authority delays).
*   **Land Bank:** Nashik land (1,400+ acres) is subject to ongoing legal/debonding processes with MIDC, expected to take 6-9 months.
*   **Legacy Issues:** Ongoing efforts to rectify legacy issues from the acquired Indiabulls portfolio.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the negative operating cash flow in Q1?** A: Management explained it was due to the timing of launches (mostly Q3/Q4 of last year) and that collections typically lag 6 months behind launches; positive cash flow is expected from Q2 onwards.
*   **Q: Why the preferential allotment of warrants?** A: Management stated it is to signal promoter confidence and repay shareholder debt, reducing the cost of capital.
*   **Q: Are you facing delays in Bangalore launches?** A: Management clarified that Q1 non-launches were deliberate (strategy/pricing) rather than purely regulatory, though they acknowledged the planning authority environment is challenging.
*   **Q: Can you adopt percentage completion accounting?** A: Management confirmed they are actively exploring this and will review it over the next couple of quarters.
*   **Q: What is the status of the Nashik land?** A: Management is working on an amicable solution with MIDC and a debonding exercise, which is expected to take 6-9 months.

### One-line takeaway
Embassy Developments is prioritizing execution and collections to drive cash flow, with management signaling confidence in hitting FY27 targets despite near-term reported accounting losses.
