# Real Estate - Residential Developers: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector narrative for Q2 FY27 is defined by a "tale of two markets." While the premium and luxury residential segments continue to show remarkable resilience and pricing power, the affordable housing segment is exhibiting clear signs of fatigue. Managements are largely focused on aggressive business development (GDV additions) and maintaining strong balance sheets, though a recurring theme across the sector is the slippage of launch timelines. Companies are increasingly shifting toward asset-light models (JDA/JV) to manage capital allocation.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus (Premium/Luxury Strength):** There is broad agreement that the premium and luxury segments (>Rs 1 crore) remain the primary growth engine. **Lodha**, **DLF**, **Godrej Properties**, **Sunteck**, and **Sri Lotus** all reported strong demand in these categories. **Brigade** noted that even with interest rate hikes, customer sentiment remains steady. **Ganesh Housing** and **Suraj Estate** highlighted strong traction in commercial and luxury residential segments, respectively.
*   **Divergence (Affordable Weakness):** A clear divergence exists regarding the affordable segment. **Aditya Birla** and **Arihant** explicitly flagged weakness in segments below Rs 1 crore, citing affordability and "own-contribution" challenges for end-users. **Mahindra Lifespaces** noted a slight slowdown in velocity due to macro headwinds, contrasting with the more bullish outlooks of luxury-focused peers.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Margins:** Most developers are maintaining project-level EBITDA margins in the 25%–35% range. **Sri Lotus** (39.9%) and **Ganesh Housing** (80.7% in Q2, though volatile) reported high margins, while **Kalpataru** (16.6%) and **Keystone** (14.5%) operated at lower levels.
*   **Inflation:** Input cost pressure remains a persistent theme. **Arihant** and **Mahindra Lifespaces** noted that construction costs have risen post-geopolitical conflicts. **Raymond Realty** is mitigating this via fixed-price EPC contracts, while **Arvind SmartSpaces** and **Eldeco** are passing on costs through price hikes, though **Eldeco** noted price resistance in vertical housing.

## Capex & capacity
*   **Aggressive Expansion:** Developers are aggressively adding to their GDV pipelines. **Arkade** added ~Rs 5,000 crore, **Brigade** added ~Rs 13,000 crore, **Sunteck** added a new 4.5-acre parcel in Borivali, and **Sri Lotus** added ~Rs 2,700–2,800 crore.
*   **JDA/JV Shift:** There is a strategic pivot toward asset-light models. **Arvind SmartSpaces** is targeting a 70:30 JDA/JV mix. **Sunteck** and **Raymond** are also heavily utilizing JDA models to scale without over-leveraging.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Launch Slippage:** A significant number of companies reported delays in project launches, pushing them into H2 FY27 or FY28. This includes **Aditya Birla** (Worli Tower C), **Arkade** (launch shifts), **Arihant** (Town Villas), **Brigade** (Chennai projects), **Eldeco** (projects 5, 6, 7), **Keystone** (Dindoshi/Om Nagar), and **Puravankara** (Cuffe Parade).
*   **Accounting Shifts:** Several companies are managing investor expectations regarding revenue recognition. **DLF**, **Kalpataru**, and **Lodha** emphasized that reported accounting revenues remain subdued due to the "completed contract method," with inflection points expected in FY27-FY28.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Construction Headwinds:** Watch for the impact of GRAP (pollution) restrictions in NCR on **DLF**, **Godrej**, and **Lodha**, and potential election-related approval delays for **Eldeco**.
*   **Launch Execution:** With so many companies pushing launches to H2, the ability to execute these in Q3/Q4 is critical for meeting annual guidance.
*   **Commercial Leasing:** Monitor the leasing momentum for **Ganesh Housing** (Million Minds) and **Suraj Estate** (One Business Bay) as indicators of commercial demand.
