# Arihant Superstructures Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Steady demand, ongoing project executions, and strategic hospitality tie-ups, though near-term presales saw a temporary dip ahead of planned festive launches.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Operating Revenue:** Rs 265 crore (up 9% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 56 crore (Margin: 20.97%)
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 20.3 crore (Margin: 7.68%)
- **Q2 FY27 Operating Revenue:** Rs 133 crore (up ~9% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** Rs 28 crore (down 7% YoY, up 1.5% QoQ; Margin: 21%)
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** Rs 10.6 crore (Margin: 7.92%)
- **Net Worth:** Rs 475 crore
- **Net Debt:** Rs 827 crore
- **Unsecured Loans:** Rs 435 crore
- **Q2 FY27 Sales Bookings:** 236 units, 2.2 lakh square feet, valued at Rs 173 crore
- **Q2 FY27 Average Price:** Rs 7,822 per sq ft; Rs 73.15 lakhs per unit
- **H1 FY27 Sales Bookings:** 457 units, 4.52 lakh square feet, valued at Rs 345.8 crore
- **H1 FY27 Average Price:** Rs 7,650 per sq ft; Rs 75.6 lakhs per unit
- **Collections:** Rs 154.5 crore (Q2); Rs 316 crore (H1)
- **Unsold Ready Inventory:** ~275 units valued at Rs 53 crore
- **Blended Cost of Debt:** ~13%, expected to drop to ~12.5% by end of FY27

### Highlights
- Signed an agreement with ITC Hotels Limited on September 24 to develop a 227-room luxury hotel (including 44 suites under the 'Mementos by ITC' brand) on a 10-acre land parcel at World Villas, Chowk.
- Club 10 Gymkhana construction progressed to the first slab with over 800 memberships gathered; announced a 108-key budget hotel with operator finalization underway.
- Unsold ready inventory stands at ~275 units valued at Rs 53 crore.
- Shifted a high-cost STCI debt to ICICI Bank, lowering interest rate from 11.75% to 8.6%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a 20% growth in revenue figures for FY27.
- Management targets annual EBITDA margins upwards of 25% for the full financial year.
- Town Villas launch pushed to the next financial year (FY28).
- Annuity income from the hotel and Club 10 Gymkhana expected to contribute to P&L from FY30 onwards.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted end-user demand remains healthy with prices continuing to rise in major markets, buyers moving up the price ladder, and supply running ahead of absorption.
- Affordable housing face affordability and own-contribution challenges as disposable incomes are prioritized toward leisure and other purchases.
- Mid-income segment (ticket size Rs 50 lakhs to Rs 1.5 crore) seeing good healthy absorption across Kharghar, Panvel, and Shilphata.
- Luxury segment is currently outperforming both affordable and mid-income segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Labor availability and input raw material prices stayed tight; management aims to recover increases through pricing on unsold inventory rather than chasing volume.
- Overall construction cost saw an increment of 4% to 5% over the last 8-9 months post the Asia war.
- P&L cost fluctuations are driven by project mix.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- World Villas Phase 1 (178 villas out of 391 total villas; GDV ~Rs 1,200 crore) has sold ~60 villas; balance sales expected over the next 2 years.
- Total land under working is 320 acres; unstarted land bank includes 99.5 acres at Chowk (Town Villas) and 7-8 acres at Panvel.
- Shilphata JV signed on a 14% area-sharing basis with the landowner (86% to Arihant).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt is around Rs 900 crore (INR 9 billion).
- Interest cost for the quarter was INR 148 million (down 13% YoY).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Affordability and own-contribution remain a challenge for the affordable housing end-user class.
- Society redevelopment agreements (in Thane/elsewhere) are taking longer to finalize due to complex drafting and multiple member involvements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Yash Somani (YES Securities) asked about the 27% YoY decline in Q2 presales.** Parth Chhajer explained that Q3 and Q4 have more launches lined up in Panvel and coincide with the festive season, whereas Q2 focused on clearing existing multi-phase inventory.
2. **Yash Somani asked about the blended cost of debt and future trajectory.** Parth Chhajer stated current average cost is ~13%, expected to drop to ~12.5% by year-end, aided by refinancing high-cost debt (e.g., shifting STCI debt at 11.75% to ICICI Bank at 8.6%).
3. **Khushi Solanki (Agarwal & Co.) inquired about FY27 revenue and EBITDA margin targets.** Parth Chhajer guided for a 20% revenue growth and full-year EBITDA margins upwards of 25%, noting quarterly fluctuations are due to project/revenue mix.
4. **Ritik Nagpal (Aryan Securities) asked about the status of asset-light society redevelopment projects.** Parth Chhajer answered they are on the verge of signing development agreements by Q3, though delayed due to the lengthier multi-member drafting process.
5. **Ritik Nagpal asked about the Town Villas saleable area and launch timeline.** Parth Chhajer stated total saleable area increased to ~58 lakh sq ft (GDV ~Rs 4,800 crore) due to increased land parcel (99.5 acres) and design changes; the launch is pushed to the next financial year.

### One-line takeaway
Arihant Superstructures reported a 9% YoY revenue increase for Q2 FY27 alongside a strategic luxury hotel tie-up with ITC, while guiding for 20% revenue growth and >25% EBITDA margins for FY27.
