# Aditya Birla Real Estate Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2/H1-FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial growth, robust collections, and strategic redesign of projects to enhance long-term value, despite temporary presales adjustments.

### Key numbers
- **1HFY27 Total Income:** Rs 355 crore (up 309% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 268 crore (up 755% YoY)
- **1HFY27 Collections:** Rs 1,465 crore (up 39% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Collections:** Rs 752 crore (up 47% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 Booking Value (Presales):** Rs 298 crore
- **Birla Tisya EBITDA:** Rs 181 crore at 28% EBITDA margin
- **Birla Navya EBITDA:** Rs 39 crore at 35% EBITDA margin
- **Cumulative Collections:** Crossed Rs 10,000 crore milestone
- **Hinjewadi Phase 1 Pune JDA GDV Potential:** Approx Rs 1,650 crore (75% revenue share) with Rs 50 crore upfront payment
- **Worli Tower C Net GDV (Revised):** Approx Rs 1,900 crore (Tower C GDV increased by Rs 3,000 crore)
- **Business Development (GDV added recently):** Approx Rs 8,000 crore total; Rs 6,200 crore added in the current fiscal year so far

### Highlights
- Crossed cumulative collections of Rs 10,000 crore across projects.
- Delivered Birla Tisya project 4 to 5 months ahead of RERA schedule with a 95% NPS score.
- Redesigned Tower C at Worli by amalgamating adjacent plots and reconfiguring FSI, deferring the launch to Q2 of the next financial year.
- Secured a JDA for 5.6 acres in Hinjewadi Phase 1, Pune, in October 2026.
- CARE improved BEPL's credit outlook from AA stable to AA positive.
- Ranked high in the 2026 GRESB assessment (scored 95.9/100, 5-star rating for standing investments, 100/100 for development, and 1st in Asia for management score).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains firm on reaching presales of Rs 15,000 crore by FY29.
- Management expects to cross the business development guidance of Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 15,000 crore for the year (already achieved ~Rs 6,000 crore plus).
- Estimated construction cost for the full financial year is expected to be around Rs 1,300 crore.
- Commercial capex for 1.3 million square feet is estimated at Rs 1,400 crore to Rs 1,500 crore over the next 3 to 4 years, aiming to grow annual rental income from Rs 150 crore to beyond Rs 1,500 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's 1QFY27 GDP growth stood at 7.8%; RBI revised FY27 GDP forecast upward to 7.1%.
- MMR residential sales reached approx Rs 59,500 crore, up 11% YoY. Pune and Bengaluru absorption grew 3% YoY; prices increased 3% in Pune and 8% in Bengaluru.
- Commercial office leasing reached ~21 million square feet in Q2 FY27 (up 6% YoY), driven by GCCs (41% share).
- Premium and luxury residential markets (above Rs 1 crore) continue to see strong resilience and demand, while segments below Rs 1 crore show signs of weakness.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Project-level EBITDA margins generally range from 25% to 30%, with specific flagship projects like Birla Navya achieving 35% and Niyaara expected at 40% and above.
- Sales, marketing, and overhead expenses are charged directly to the P&L.
- Acquisition costs for business development typically range between 15% to 17% of the added GDV.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total expected construction spend for FY27 is ~Rs 1,300 crore.
- Commercial build-out to commence with a 1.3 million square feet project at Birla Niyaara (approval process takes 9-10 months, construction 4 years).
- Active discussions ongoing for additional redevelopment opportunities in MMR micro-markets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is net debt zero / well-capitalized with strong cash balances and mutual funds.
- Open to gearing the balance sheet depending on acquisition opportunities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Deferment of the Worli Tower C launch to next financial year may impact near-term presales.
- Some project cancellations observed (e.g., Arika, Pravaah), though units are being resold at higher prices.
- Global market sentiment and macroeconomic headwinds pose minor drags on plotted development (e.g., Birla Mrida).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Jainam Shah (Equirus Securities) asked about cancellations and margin definitions:** Management clarified they report net sales only and explained the accounting treatment where overheads are expensed to P&L while direct/operation-related expenses are inventorized.
- **Harsh Pathak (Motilal Oswal) questioned the Worli Niyaara GDV discrepancy:** Management explained that amalgamating the original plot with the Wadia plot and reconfiguring FSI increased Tower C GDV by Rs 3,000 crore and net GDV by Rs 1,900 crore.
- **Biplab Debbarma (Emkay Global) asked about FY27 presales trajectory given the Niyaara launch delay:** Management declined short-term quarterly guidance, emphasizing long-term value creation over meeting short-term linear sales targets.
- **Akash Gupta (Nomura) queried the sustenance sales pace:** Management stated that 78% of overall inventory is already sold, leaving limited inventory, and slower sustenance sales in certain projects are offset by strong past launch absorption and high value retention.
- **Abhinav Sinha (Jefferies) asked about debt trajectory and commercial plans:** Management reiterated a well-capitalized, net-zero debt balance sheet, remaining open to judicious gearing while scaling annual rental income targets to above Rs 1,500 crore.

### One-line takeaway
Aditya Birla Real Estate delivered strong 1HFY27 income and collection growth, prioritizing long-term value creation and portfolio optimization over short-term presales targets, while maintaining a robust balance sheet and expanding its business development pipeline.
