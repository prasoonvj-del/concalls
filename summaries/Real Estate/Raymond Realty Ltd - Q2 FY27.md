# Raymond Realty Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by nearly doubled quarterly pre-sales without new launches, steady revenue growth, and expansion in EBITDA margins.

### Key numbers
- Total income: INR 757 crore (up 7% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR 115 crore (up from 14% to 15% margin YoY)
- Customer collections: INR 682 crore (up 67% YoY); H1 cumulative collections at INR 1,233 crore
- H1 booking values: INR 1,602 crore (up 111% YoY)
- Net debt: INR 912 crore; Gross debt: INR ~1,200 crore
- Debt-to-equity ratio: 0.8x (Net debt-to-equity around 0.5x)
- Total interest cost for Q2 FY27: INR 44 crore (includes INR 27 crore bank borrowings and INR 17 crore government authority approvals interest)
- Liquidity buffer: INR 308 crore; Cost of debt: 9.6%
- Total portfolio GDV: Approximately INR 52,000 crore (includes INR 8,500 crore added in H1 in Parel)
- Thane land parcel GDV: INR 25,000 crore (INR 16,500 crore under development; INR 9,770 crore sold and INR 7,977 crore collected to date)
- JDA GDV: INR 27,000 crore (approx. 52% of total portfolio GDV); JDA sales value to date at INR 3,432 crore and collections at INR 857 crore
- Subsidiary fundraise: INR 210 crore from ICICI Prudential AMC promoted AIF via OCPRS

### Highlights
- Achieved a landmark milestone by nearly doubling quarterly pre-sales without any new launches through sales velocity and steady price realizations.
- Received occupation certificate for Address by GS 1 Tower B in Thane (270 units, ~3.5 lakh sq. ft.) ahead of the RERA completion timeline.
- Non-Thane JDAs contributed 57% of pre-sale value, while Thane contributed 43%.
- Subsidiary company raised INR 210 crore from ICICI Prudential AMC AIF for the Mahim redevelopment project (representing ~60% of peak investment for the project). Promoter committed INR 410 crore equity infusion via preferential issue.
- Two project launches scheduled in Mahim in H2 with a combined GDV of approx. INR 4,100 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains full-year FY27 guidance: minimum 20% pre-sales growth, ROCE of 20%, EBITDA margins of 17% to 19%, and PAT margins of 9% to 10%.
- Management expects a 20% pre-sales CAGR over the next 5 years.
- Business development target: adding INR 8,000 crore to INR 10,000 crore worth of GDV every year.
- Net operating cash flow pre-tax expected to be INR 10,000 crore plus from the current portfolio as projects execute.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management stated that interest rate hikes of 25-50 bps will not significantly impact their target segment (mid-market and above, starting from INR 1.5 crore up to INR 20 crore), as buyers in this segment are less sensitive to such changes.
- Festive season beginning in H2 is expected to drive better sales compared to H1.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins fluctuate by project phase due to upfront marketing and construction setup costs but are expected to normalize as projects move into mid-stage.
- Ongoing projects have fixed-price contracts with EPC contractors with no provision for cost increases; management includes contingency buffers in project budgets to absorb temporary fluctuations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total GDV portfolio stands at ~INR 52,000 crore across MMR, with asset-light JDA projects accounting for 52%.
- Future equity requirements over the next 5 years estimated at INR 500 crore to INR 600 crore apart from raised funds.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt closed at INR 912 crore; gross debt at ~INR 1,200 crore. Debt levels expected to remain in the INR 1,200 crore to INR 1,500 crore range over the next 1-2 years while staying well within the 1:1 debt-to-equity ceiling.
- Total interest cost for full year expected to follow H1 run rate.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential construction cost and labor inflation post-elections, though ongoing projects are protected by fixed-price contracts.
- Macroeconomic uncertainty and potential interest rate hardening.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Pruthul Shah (Anubhuti Advisors LLP):** Asked about the ICICI AMC deal structure and why a preferential issue to promoters was announced despite comfortable debt levels. *Management answered that ICICI infused INR 210 crore via OCPRS (40% capital contribution by Raymond Realty plus a 5% top-line execution fee), while promoter equity infusion supports faster, disciplined growth without over-leveraging.*
- **Avantika Jawahar:** Inquired about revenue guidance. *Management clarified they did not provide a revenue guidance due to moving parts in percentage-completion turnover calculations, but reiterated confidence in delivering 20% EBITDA and pre-sale growth.*
- **Rishabh Kothari:** Asked about the impact of potential interest rate hikes on MMR demand and the disconnect between operating performance and the stock price. *Management replied that their lowest ticket size is INR 1.5 crore, shielding them from rate sensitivity, and addressed stock price disconnect through a strategy of expanding institutional coverage (targeting 4 covering brokers by year-end) and IR outreach.*
- **Bhavin Modi:** Asked if gross debt of INR 1,200 crore is peak debt and whether OCPRS will reflect as debt. *Management stated debt will remain around INR 1,200 crore to INR 1,500 crore for 1-2 years, and OCPRS from ICICI will not be classified as debt on the balance sheet.*
- **Yeshas Paramesh:** Inquired about EPC contract price escalation and ICICI OCPRS coupon. *Management confirmed ongoing contracts are fixed-price with contingency buffers, and the OCPRS coupon is virtually zero as it functions as an equity investment.*

### One-line takeaway
Raymond Realty reported a robust Q2 FY27 with a 7% YoY revenue rise, a 15% EBITDA margin, and accelerated pre-sales, while advancing its asset-light JDA model and institutional equity tie-ups.
