# Veegaland Developers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong revenue, EBITDA, and PAT growth, alongside a successful IPO listing.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** INR106.34 crores (+28.66% YoY from INR82.66 crores).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA (incl. other income):** INR22.78 crores (+77% YoY from INR12.82 crores).
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 20.90% (+550 bps YoY from 15.40%).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR15.99 crores (+92% YoY from INR8.31 crores).
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 14.70% (+470 bps YoY from 10%).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR180.66 crores (+45% YoY from INR124.16 crores).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR37.09 crores (+96% YoY from INR18.92 crores).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR25.71 crores (+123% YoY from INR11.53 crores).
- **Q2 Operational Metrics:** Pre-sales value +37.6% YoY; area booked +19.6% YoY; gross collections +88.2% YoY.
- **Average Realization:** Increased to INR9,286 per sq ft in September 2026 (from INR8,539 in June 2026).
- **Balance Sheet:** Unencumbered cash of INR180 crores post-IPO; debt-to-equity ratio at 0.15%. Land bank at ~6.4 acres (Kochi and Kozhikode).

### Highlights
- Successfully completed an IPO, raising INR210 crores, with shares listing on September 18, 2026.
- The call validates and builds upon the robust Q2 growth reported in the November 7 investor deck.
- *Amora* project achieved 68% booking within 4 months of launch.
- *Fortune* project received RERA approval and has been launched.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to maintain stable and consistent margins going forward (supported by the luxe product mix).
- Upcoming projects (*Sarovar* and *East Port*) scheduled to launch in Q3 FY27. Unnamed land bank projects expected to launch in 6 to 9 months.
- Management declined to provide specific numerical forward-looking projections for pre-sales or revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Strong demand across premium and luxe housing segments, driven by IT/Technopark expansions in Kochi and Trivandrum, and the Vizhinjam port development.
- NRI buyers account for 48%–50% of sales; demand from Middle East NRIs remains strong due to geopolitical uncertainties prompting preferences for home ownership in Kerala.
- RERA compliance in Kerala is effective, increasing market share for organized developers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Steel prices hovered around INR68,000–INR71,310 per tonne; other inputs (cables, PVC, CP/sanitary items) saw slight increases.
- Margin expansion driven by a shift in product mix toward higher-margin "luxe" series projects launched in H2 FY26 and cost efficiencies realized upon project completions.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Evaluating expansion into neighboring geographies, though no specific locations are finalized yet.
- Focus remains on boutique apartment models (75–150 units, ~1 to 1.25 acres) for quick project turnarounds and optimized capital deployment.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity ratio is low at 0.15%; management plans to maintain project-level term loans with scheduled commercial banks for financial discipline without over-leveraging.
- RERA-compliant project escrow accounts are maintained; collections are milestone-based with no seasonality in collections.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Material price fluctuations are closely monitored, though no margin squeeze has been experienced to date.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sabin Joy (Individual Investor) on expansion outside Kerala:** Management stated they are evaluating neighboring geographies but have no finalized specific locations to announce yet.
2. **Shravan Modi on launch timelines and pre-sales projections:** *Fortune* is launched; *Sarovar* and *East Port* are slated for Q3 FY27. Management declined to give forward-looking pre-sales projections.
3. **Aditya Deorah on use of IPO cash and future cash flows:** IPO proceeds are earmarked for ongoing projects and general corporate purposes; future operational liquidity will fund new developments and geographies.
4. **Jimit Mehta on margin sustainability:** Management attributed margin gains to the luxe product mix and cost efficiencies, expressing confidence in maintaining stable margins going forward.
5. **Lakshminarayanan on revenue visibility and land bank:** Management stated unrecognised revenue visibility stands at ~INR508 crores stretching up to FY31, supported by a 6.4-acre land bank in Kochi and Kozhikode.

### One-line takeaway
Veegaland Developers delivered a robust Q2/H1 FY27 performance marked by significant margin expansion, strong NRI-backed pre-sales demand, and a well-capitalized balance sheet following its September 2026 IPO.
