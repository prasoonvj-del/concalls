# Nirlon Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stable financial performance, 99.8% average portfolio occupancy, and consistent dividend track record.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income:** Rs. 174 crore (Q2 FY27, +3% YoY); Rs. 347 crore (H1 FY27, +3% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs. 137 crore (Q2 FY27, +3% YoY); Rs. 270 crore (H1 FY27)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 78.4% (Q2 FY27); 77.88% (H1 FY27)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Approx. Rs. 72 crore (Q2 FY27); Rs. 141 crore (H1 FY27)
- **PAT Margin:** 41.3% (Q2 FY27)
- **Average Portfolio Occupancy (NKP and Nirlon House):** 99.8% (Q2 FY27)
- **Lease Renewal:** Approx. 37,000 sq. ft. renewed by NIUM India Private Limited at Nirlon Knowledge Park (NKP) for FY28.
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** Rs. 345 crore at the end of H1 FY27.
- **Dividend:** FY26 dividend was Rs. 30 per share.

### Highlights
- Average occupancy across NKP and Nirlon House stood at 99.8% during Q2 FY27.
- NIUM India Private Limited renewed ~37,000 sq. ft. at NKP ahead of its FY28 due date.
- YoY comparison for PAT is not comparable because Q2 FY26 included a one-time impact from re-measurement of deferred tax liability upon moving to the new tax regime.
- Management started a process to evaluate potential commercial value of Nirlon House (NH) for an as-is-where-is basis sale due to multiple owners and strata-ownership complications.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims to maintain consistency in dividend payments.
- Target annual rental escalations are around 5% annually, shifting away from the historical 15% every 3 years model.
- Principal repayment of the HSBC facility will be as per schedule; HSBC loan repayment starts next year from May 2027 onwards (Rs. 57 crore moved from long-term to short-term borrowings).

### Demand & industry commentary
- GCC (Global Capability Center) demand in the micro-market is perceived as strong and stable enough to absorb upcoming vacating spaces if necessary.
- Direct live inquiries are limited due to near 100% occupancy in NKP.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other income growth is primarily driven by interest income earned on surplus cash flows.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Nirlon House redevelopment is currently stuck due to multiple (14) individual owners/tenants, prompting the evaluation of an as-is-where-is sale.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stood at Rs. 345 crore at the end of H1 FY27.
- Principal repayments to HSBC will follow the facility agreement schedule with no prepayments currently planned.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Strata-ownership and multiple owners (14 individual owners) at Nirlon House restrict redevelopment options for a listed, institutionally owned entity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Laksh Jain (Individual Investor) on Cash Utilization and Dividend Policy:** Asked if surplus cash (~Rs. 550+ crore expected for the full year) would lead to higher dividends or prepayment. Management stated no specific plan is finalized yet, but they intend to remain consistent with past dividend payments and follow the loan repayment schedule as per the HSBC facility.
2. **Dilip Ashok Jain (Individual Investor) on Nirlon House Monetization:** Asked if GIC considers an outright sale of Nirlon House land given redevelopment hurdles. Management confirmed that because Nirlon House is strata-owned with multiple owners and complications, they have started a process to evaluate its potential commercial value on an as-is-where-is basis.
3. **Khushi Dutta on Rental Realization and Escalations:** Asked about current rental realizations and future escalations. Management stated they target annual escalations of around 5% and keep rates consistent with the surrounding micro-market profile.
4. **Rohan Joshi on Renewal Pricing and GCC Demand:** Asked about rental escalation for the NIUM India renewal (~37,000 sq. ft.) and GCC demand trends. Management declined to share specific individual pricing (noting it is a registered agreement in the public domain) and stated GCC demand remains stable and consistent.

### One-line takeaway
Nirlon reported stable Q2 FY27 performance with 99.8% occupancy and 3% YoY revenue growth, while evaluating an as-is-where-is sale for the strata-owned Nirlon House asset.
