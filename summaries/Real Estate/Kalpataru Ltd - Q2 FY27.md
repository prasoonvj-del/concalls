# Kalpataru Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited a challenging macro environment for a second consecutive quarter, marked by global economic uncertainties, volatile geopolitical developments, and evolving interest rate trajectories, alongside reporting a net loss for the quarter.

### Key numbers
- Pre-sales: Rs 1,258 crore (down 5% YoY) for Q2; Rs 2,587 crore (flat YoY) for H1
- Sales collection: Rs 1,189 crore for Q2; Rs 2,554 crore (up 9% YoY) for H1
- Revenue from operations: Rs 437 crore for Q2; Rs 909 crore for H1
- Adjusted EBITDA: Rs 73 crore (margin of 16.6%) for Q2; Rs 167 crore (margin of 18.4%) for H1
- Net profit/loss: Loss of Rs 71 crore for Q2; Loss of Rs 100 crore for H1
- Gross debt: Rs 9,179 crore as of September 30, 2026 (flat QoQ)
- Cash and cash equivalents: Rs 856 crore as of September 30, 2026
- Net debt: Rs 8,323 crore as of September 30, 2026
- Net debt-to-equity ratio: 2.1x as of September 30, 2026
- Project completions: 1.58 million square feet across ~1,500 units in Q2; 2.37 million square feet across over 2,200 units in H1

### Highlights
- Kalpataru Park City, Thane, saw walk-ins up by 25% YoY and conversion rates double, driving pre-sales growth of 130% to ~Rs 420 crore in H1.
- Launched the premium project 'Kalpataru Elaara' in Bangur Nagar, Goregaon West, in mid-September (30% sold).
- Q1 launch 'Kalpataru Vian, Hrushikesh, Lokhandwala' continued its sales momentum in Q2.
- Delivered over 2,200 units (2.37 million square feet) in H1, remaining on track to deliver 5.5 million square feet out of 14 million square feet of ongoing projects planned across FY27, FY28, and FY29.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 pre-sales guidance at Rs 6,500 crore.
- Management expects H2 to be better than H1, driven by sustenance sales across ongoing projects and upcoming festival period demand.
- Year-end net debt is guided to remain flat compared to FY26.
- Net operating cash flows and gross cash flows are expected to remain more or less the same as compared to FY26.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand remains healthy across all price brackets (under Rs 3 crore, Rs 3 crore to Rs 10 crore, and luxury in SoBo/Worli).
- Plotted developments in Nagpur and Karjat are seeing good demand.
- Sector consolidation continues as consumer preferences shift towards established, execution-focused developers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Price hikes of 3% to 5% have been implemented across projects to absorb inflationary impacts and future cost increases.
- The company follows the project completion method for revenue recognition for most of its projects.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Upcoming launches planned for H2 include Suman Nagar at Chembur (Q4), Kalpataru Blossoms at Pune (before December), and Kalpataru Ardene at Nagpur (Q4).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased slightly QoQ due to the consumption of working capital cash balances.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- External environment marked by global economic uncertainties, volatile geopolitical developments, and evolving interest rate trajectories.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sourabh Gilda (JM Financial)** asked for a bridge between H1 pre-sales and the full-year Rs 6,500 crore guidance. *Management answered that 40% is typically achieved in H1 and 60% in H2, supported by sustenance sales and upcoming festival demand.*
- **Sourabh Gilda (JM Financial)** asked about the launch readiness and approval status for upcoming projects. *Management stated Suman Nagar (Chembur) has IOD and is on track for Q4; Nagpur has pre-approval and expects RERA for Q4; Pune Blossoms has applied for RERA and expects a launch before December.*
- **Sourabh Gilda (JM Financial)** asked about demand trends across the price spectrum. *Management replied that demand is healthy everywhere, from sub-3 crore brackets up to South Mumbai luxury.*
- **Vishal Singh (Retail Investor)** asked about year-end net debt guidance. *Management stated net debt will remain flat compared to FY26 as funds are consumed for project execution and business development.*
- **Ashi (MyCel Advisors)** asked about the quantum of price hikes taken to combat inflation. *Management stated price hikes of 3% to 5% have been implemented.*

### One-line takeaway
Kalpataru reported net losses in Q2 FY27 due to the project completion accounting method, but management maintained its full-year pre-sales guidance of Rs 6,500 crore, backed by strong execution, upcoming launches, and healthy festival demand.
