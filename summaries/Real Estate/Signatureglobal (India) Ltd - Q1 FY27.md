# Signatureglobal (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management expressed confidence in achieving annual guidance despite macro headwinds, citing strong demand and successful project launches.

### Key numbers
*   **Pre-sales:** Rs 2,000 crore.
*   **Collections:** Rs 670 crore.
*   **Launch GDV (Q1):** Rs 4,400 crore.
*   **Realization:** >Rs 17,000/sq ft.
*   **Cash & Bank Balances:** Rs 2,500 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** <Rs 390 crore.

### Highlights
*   Launched a branded residence project (Tonino Lamborghini) in Sector 71, Gurugram (12+ acres, 2 million sq ft); sold 300+ units out of 400+ opened in Phase 1.
*   Portfolio status: 19 million sq ft delivered to date; 9 million sq ft under development with an average completion timeline of 2-3 quarters.
*   Since February 2024, the company has launched 23 million sq ft with a total GDV of Rs 33,400 crore.
*   Management stated that the company is actively evaluating business development opportunities outside the Delhi NCR market.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Pre-sales:** Management maintains guidance of Rs 10,000 crore for FY27.
*   **New Launches:** Management maintains guidance of Rs 15,000 crore for FY27.
*   **Revenue Recognition:** Management expects to recognize revenue exceeding Rs 5,000 crore for FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted the Gurugram market remains supply-constrained, with demand outpacing supply.
*   Industry-level price increments are tracking at "inflation-plus" levels.
*   Despite macro headwinds (Middle East conflict), demand for premium and experience-led living remains resilient.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Management observed that raw material and labor costs are trending up in "late single digits" (7-8% annually).
*   No inordinate spikes in key building material costs were reported during the quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Land Capex:** Management guided for Rs 1,500-1,800 crore for FY27.
*   **Strategy:** Focusing on large-format, low-rise, mid-income developments for expansion into new markets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net debt is currently at low levels (<Rs 390 crore).
*   Management expects net debt to rise slightly during the year due to planned business development activities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Q1 collections were lower than historical trends due to lumpy milestones slipping into subsequent quarters.
*   Management acknowledged macro headwinds, specifically referencing the Middle East conflict, though noted minimal impact on operations.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On expansion outside NCR:** Management confirmed they are evaluating opportunities and prefer large-format, low-rise, mid-income developments to establish brand recognition and showcase delivery capability.
*   **On land Capex:** Management provided a guidance range of Rs 1,500-1,800 crore for the year.
*   **On monetization cycle for branded residences:** Management explained they target 50% sales within 3-6 months of launch, followed by a staggered release of the remaining inventory to balance cash flow and price appreciation.
*   **On weak collections:** Management clarified that the lower collection figure was an "aberration" due to the timing of project milestones and expects to return to higher run rates in coming quarters.
*   **On cost escalation:** Management reiterated that they budget for a 7-8% annual increase in construction costs.

### One-line takeaway
Signature Global maintains a strong balance sheet and positive outlook, targeting Rs 10,000 crore in pre-sales for FY27, supported by a robust launch pipeline and resilient demand in the Gurugram market.
