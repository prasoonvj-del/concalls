# Marathon Nextgen Realty Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Multi-quarter high total income, healthy profitability, steady execution progress, and robust demand across segments.

### Key numbers
- Total Income: Rs 217 crore
- EBITDA: Rs 66 crore
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 52 crore
- Area sold (existing portfolio, MNRL 40% stake in Monte South): ~38,000 sq ft; Booking value: Rs 86 crore; Collections: Rs 118 crore
- Area sold (including merged portfolio): 46,000 sq ft; Booking value: Rs 108 crore; Collections: Rs 146 crore
- Monte South sales: ~35,000 sq ft; Booking value: Rs 125 crore; Collections: Rs 140 crore
- Marathon Nexzone (Panvel) sales: 14,000 sq ft; Booking value: Rs 17 crore; Collections: Rs 43 crore
- Bhandup projects (NeoPark and NeoSquare) sales: 8,000 sq ft; Booking value: Rs 14 crore; Collections: Rs 9 crore
- Marathon Futurex commercial sales (combined basis): ~4,000 sq ft; Booking value: Rs 19 crore; Collections: Rs 23 crore
- Millennium commercial sales: >1,000 sq ft; Booking value: Rs 3 crore; Collections: Rs 1 crore
- Unsold GDV (existing portfolio): ~Rs 8,000 crore
- Upcoming project GDVs: Monte South Commercial (Rs 3,400 crore), Monte South Residential Tower D (Rs 1,600 crore), Marathon Neo Series in Bhandup (~Rs 2,800 crore), Versova redevelopment (>Rs 450 crore), Sewri redevelopment (~Rs 450 crore). Total new redevelopment GDV added: Rs 900 crore.
- Available acquisition capital: ~Rs 200 crore

### Highlights
- Started FY27 with multi-quarter high total income of Rs 217 crore.
- Monte South Tower A received occupation certificate (OC); Tower B achieved RCC completion up to 65th floor tariff and part OC up to 45th floor (remaining OC up to 65th floor expected this financial year); Tower C RCC completed up to 28th floor.
- Marathon Nexzone (Panvel) achieved full OC for Cedar and Daffodil towers, enabling handovers and collections.
- NeoSquare (Bhandup) received its occupation certificate.
- Entered two redevelopment opportunities: Versova (Sunset Spaces, ~1.5 acres, low-density residential, GDV >Rs 450 crore) and Sewri (JDA for cluster redevelopment, ~7,500 sq m, GDV ~Rs 450 crore).
- Proposed group merger NCLT meetings for stakeholders/shareholders planned for the first week of September 2026. Merger brings over 400 acres of land, ongoing projects, and ready inventory at Futurex.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 outlook to be positive and better than last year, with collections likely on the heavier side.
- Management expects to fully deploy the surplus acquisition capital of ~Rs 200 crore into new projects during FY27.
- Amalgamation completion timeline depends on NCLT available dates.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Robust demand observed for well-located premium residential developments and commercial grade-A space.
- Footfalls at Monte South have dramatically increased.
- Panvel micro-market benefiting from Navi Mumbai International Airport operation, Atal Setu connectivity, and Panvel-Karjat corridor progress (deadline December 2026), connecting peripheral areas by Mumbai local trains.
- Bhandup micro-market benefiting from improving connectivity, including the Goregaon-Mulund Link Road (GMLR) twin tunnels project.
- Mumbai redevelopment opportunities are vast due to aging 40-50 year old buildings and revised FSI calculations, though management remains selective on prime locations and strict financial metrics.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Target EBITDA margins for acquiring projects: 30% to 35%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- ~Rs 200 crore of surplus acquisition capital available to be fully deployed in new projects in FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company remains in a net cash position with a debt-free balance sheet following the Rs 900 crore QIP.
- Ready-to-move-in inventory sales yield collections within a TAT of ~3 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- NCLT benches are heavily loaded with work, making hearing dates challenging to secure.
- Developers offering very high percentages in redevelopment projects often get stuck; management warned that overly pricy deals risk failure.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mihir Shah (MP Securities) asked about the launch timeline for Versova and Sewri and additional redevelopment GDV additions over 12-24 months.** Management reiterated the INR 900 crore addition (INR 450 crore each) and total existing GDV of INR 8,000 crores, without giving specific launch dates.
2. **Karan Mehra (Mehta Investments) asked about business outlook/drivers for FY27 and collection trajectory.** Management stated the outlook is positive, driven by demand in commercial, Monte South, newly launched Bhandup phases, and ready inventory collections within a 3-month TAT.
3. **Dev Ajmera (Individual Investor) asked about the reduction in other income and NCLT amalgamation timelines.** Management explained other income dropped because a specific Futurex investment property floor was not sold this quarter, and NCLT shareholder meetings are set for September before the second hearing.
4. **Manav Jain (MJ Investment) asked about the scale of the redevelopment pipeline and views on pricy deals.** Management noted huge overall opportunities in Mumbai but emphasized strict financial metrics and avoiding overly high occupant offers that cause projects to stall.
5. **Pratisha Shah (Sai Advisory) asked about capital deployment plans for FY27 and target return thresholds.** Management stated the INR 200 crore surplus capital will be fully deployed in FY27, targeting 30-35% EBITDA margins.

### One-line takeaway
Marathon Nextgen Realty started FY27 with a multi-quarter high total income of Rs 217 crore, a debt-free net cash balance sheet, and strong execution momentum across residential, commercial, and new redevelopment projects.
