# Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-19 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong pre-sales growth, doubled EBITDA, and robust expansion in Mumbai's luxury real estate market.

### Key numbers
- Q2 FY27 Pre-sales: Rs 470 crore (up 83% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Pre-sales: Rs 879 crore (up 176% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 Total revenue: Rs 269 crore (up 53% YoY)
- H1 FY27 Total revenue: Rs 401 crore (up 69% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 107 crore (up 113% YoY)
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin: 39.9%
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 155 crore (up 95% YoY)
- H1 FY27 EBITDA margin: 38.8%
- Q2 FY27 Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 89 crore (up 91% YoY from Rs 46 crore)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 134 crore (up 86% YoY)
- H1 FY27 PAT margin: 33.5%
- Collections: Rs 60 crore for Q2; Rs 209 crore for H1
- Net cash position: Rs 494 crore (as of September 30, 2026)
- Estimated project-level surplus: Approximately Rs 9,500 crore over the development cycle
- IPO net proceeds: Rs 732 crore (Rs 305 crore deployed as of September 30, 2026)

### Highlights
- Lotus Trident (Andheri West) contributed pre-sales of Rs 178 crore in its first full quarter since launch.
- Added three new projects during the quarter (Lotus Impressa at Shivaji Park, Lotus Stellar in Versova, and Lotus Supremus in Juhu), adding Rs 2,700 to Rs 2,800 crore of GDV.
- Total ongoing and upcoming pipeline comprises 24 projects with an aggregate GDV of approximately Rs 20,000 crore (19 are redevelopment-led).
- Upcoming launches in H2 FY27 include four projects: Lotus Aurelia, Lotus Sky Plaza, Lotus Portofino, and Lotus Odyssey (estimated combined GDV of Rs 3,500 to Rs 4,000 crore).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 pre-sales guidance of Rs 1,800 crore to Rs 2,000 crore.
- Management expects revenue and PAT growth of 55% to 60% over FY26 levels.
- Management expects EBITDA and PAT margins to sustain at 35% to 40% and 25% to 30%, respectively.
- Four project launches planned for H2 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Mumbai housing sales in the first nine months of calendar 2026 reached their highest since 2018, led by the luxury segment.
- Sales of homes priced above Rs 10 crore reached over Rs 18,500 crore in H1 calendar 2026; prime residential prices rose 6.2% YoY.
- Management notes that the 25 bps RBI repo rate hike to 5.5% will likely weigh on affordable and mid-income housing, but luxury buyers rely less on home loans, making demand in their micromarkets resilient.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- High margins attributed to a zero net debt position (no interest burden), minimal marketing/awareness expenses, fast project cycles (2.5 to 3.5 years), efficient fund deployment, and premium pricing policy.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total pipeline stands at 24 projects with ~Rs 20,000 crore GDV.
- GIFT City project: 5.5-acre land parcel planned for mixed-use (commercial, retail, residential) yielding ~1.3 million to 1.5 million square feet upon town planning scheme approval.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net cash position stands at Rs 494 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Out of Rs 550 crore earmarked from IPO proceeds for subsidiaries (Amalfi, Arcadian, Varun), Rs 305 crore was deployed by September 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Collections are linked to construction milestones and can vary quarter-to-quarter (collections pick up as projects reach plinth/billing milestones).
- Regulatory approval timelines for certain projects (e.g., Lotus Monarch) involve multiple parties, leading to conservative schedule estimates.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Ankit S. Mehta (Roha Wellworth Securities) asked about the sustainability of margins above 35% during expansion.** Management (Anand Pandit) replied that zero net debt, low marketing spends, fast 2.5-3.5 year project cycles, and premium pricing support sustainable high margins.
- **Siddharth S. (NAFA Asset Managers) questioned the collection trajectory relative to pre-sales.** Management (Sanjay Kumar Jain) explained that payments are construction-linked (5-10% at launch, ~25% at plinth level) and collections will improve as ongoing projects advance out of the basement.
- **Rahil Bohra (Polycab's Family Office) asked about timeline shifts for upcoming projects.** Management (Sanjay Kumar Jain) noted timelines for projects like Lotus Monarch were extended conservatively due to multiple stakeholders (society, trust, Charity Commissioner, government).
- **Prabal Gandhi (Equirus) asked about the low contribution from the Prabhadevi launch.** Management (Anand Pandit/Sanjay Kumar Jain) answered that Prabhadevi is a new micromarket with a unique 270-degree sea-view Art Deco project at a premium price point, and traction will increase post-plinth.
- **Harsh Pathak asked about approval status for H2 projects.** Management (Anand Pandit) stated that more or less 90% of approvals are in place, ensuring confidence in launching the four planned H2 projects.

### One-line takeaway
Sri Lotus Developers and Realty reported strong H1 FY27 pre-sales growth of 176% and doubled EBITDA, backed by a net cash balance sheet and a robust Mumbai luxury redevelopment pipeline.
