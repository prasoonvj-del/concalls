# Shriram Properties Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong sequential sales growth, significantly higher profitability, robust collections, and a high-visibility H2 handover pipeline.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 guidance, clarifying full-year revenue range at Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 1,600 crore and EBITDA around Rs 300 crore; reaffirmed FY28 targets (Rs 5,000 crore sales value, Rs 2,500 crore revenue, Rs 250+ crore PBT).
- **Tone and demand view:** Remained positive, highlighting strong ground momentum across core markets and swift absorption of plotted/villa projects.
- **New topics or risks:** Completed strategic JV/landowner restructuring and debt consolidation (gross debt spiked to Rs 1,118 crore, net debt to Rs 761 crore); permanently shifted Kolkata land monetization strategy from bulk sales to development-led (plots, villas, apartments).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Delivered on Q2 sequential sales growth (Rs 583 crore, up 20% QoQ) and executed planned JV conversions.

### Key numbers
*   **Sales Value (Q2 FY27):** Rs 583 crore (0.91 million sq ft, up 20% QoQ).
*   **Sales Value (H1 FY27):** Rs 1,067 crore (1.76 million sq ft).
*   **Collections (Q2 FY27):** Rs 431 crore (up 18% QoQ); H1 collections at Rs 795 crore (up 10% YoY).
*   **Units Handed Over:** 760 units in Q2; 1,450 units in H1.
*   **Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Rs 272 crore (up 19% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 62.4 crore (compared to Rs 18.6 crore YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** ~23%.
*   **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 21.6 crore (up 151% YoY).
*   **Gross External Debt:** Rs 1,118 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up from Rs 651 crore in June).
*   **Cash and Cash Equivalents:** Rs 357 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 761 crore (Net debt-to-equity at 0.51x).

### Highlights
*   Converted 5 joint ventures and key projects (including 107 Southeast, WYT Field, 122 West) into wholly owned subsidiaries, adding ~Rs 1,800 crore in consolidated revenue recognition potential.
*   Refined Kolkata strategy to development-led monetization (villas and plots), achieving >75% inventory absorption within 90 days for recent launches.
*   H2 launch pipeline includes 9 projects aggregating ~5.25 million sq ft of supply potential.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management guided for full-year FY27 revenue of Rs 1,500 crore to Rs 1,600 crore and EBITDA of ~Rs 300 crore.
*   Management reaffirmed FY28 targets: Sales value of Rs 5,000 crore, revenue of Rs 2,500 crore, and PBT of Rs 250+ crore.
*   Management stated net debt-to-equity will be managed within a comfort zone below 0.75x, aiming to return closer to 0.5x.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management stated market demand recovered smartly in the September quarter across Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, and Pune.
*   Pune recorded the strongest PAN-India growth with 20% QoQ.
*   Management noted no visible ground impact on customer decision-making from interest rate or AI concerns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Q2 EBITDA margin improved to ~23% due to favorable product mix and flat total expenses despite 20% revenue growth.
*   Average borrowing cost remained stable at ~11%.
*   Management stated cost escalation buffers are built into long-term project budgets.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Invested Rs 105 crore in new projects during H1 (only Rs 17 crore in Q2).
*   Evaluating ~8.8 million sq ft (Rs 7,500 crore GDV) in new business development across Bengaluru and Chennai, while exploring MMR entry.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Gross debt increased to Rs 1,118 crore and net debt to Rs 761 crore due to JV consolidation and ASK exit payouts (~Rs 300 crore total incremental debt impact).
*   Undrawn borrowing limits stood at Rs 245 crore.
*   Credit rating maintained at CRISIL A- with a positive outlook.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Temporary increase in reported leverage (net debt-to-equity rose to 0.51x) following JV debt consolidation.
*   Execution dependency on timely regulatory approvals, occupancy certificates, and handovers in H2.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Samarth Khandelwal (RoboCapital) asked why JV partners exited.** Management answered that landowners completed their construction roles and wanted to move on, while the buyout gave Shriram operational control and ~Rs 150 crore in net positive value addition. (Did not dodge)
*   **Drashti Shah (Individual) asked about the YoY revenue decline and H2 confidence.** Management explained that Q2 had minimal planned launches by design, whereas H2 is heavily weighted with 5.25 million sq ft of launches and 2,200+ pending handovers. (Did not dodge)
*   **Raj Mehta (Individual) asked about Kolkata land monetization and inflation risks.** Management stated villas/plots yield Rs 7-8 crore contribution per acre, targeting ~Rs 2,000 crore total cash flow from Kolkata land over 3-5 years; mid-23% EBITDA margins are protected by budgeted cost buffers. (Did not dodge)
*   **Nitin Jain (Individual) asked for clarification on FY27 revenue guidance.** Management clarified the expected range is Rs 1,500–1,600 crore with ~Rs 300 crore EBITDA, reaffirming FY28 targets. (Did not dodge)

### One-line takeaway
Shriram Properties delivered strong Q2 financial and operational recovery, backed by JV consolidations, robust collections, and a heavy H2 handover pipeline, while pivoting its Kolkata land strategy to high-margin development.
