# Refex Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Solid execution in established businesses, encouraging early progress in new segments, and healthy YoY growth despite seasonal monsoon challenges.

### Key numbers
- Standalone Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 560 crore (up 36% YoY from Rs 411 crore)
- Standalone EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 91 crore (up 22% YoY from Rs 74 crore)
- Standalone EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27): 16%
- Standalone PAT (Q2 FY27): Rs 60 crore (up 13% YoY from Rs 53 crore)
- Standalone Revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 1,179 crore (up 55% YoY from Rs 760 crore)
- Standalone EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 196 crore (up 17% YoY from Rs 114 crore)
- Standalone PAT (H1 FY27): Rs 133 crore (up 55% YoY from Rs 86 crore)
- Wind Energy Business Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 201 crore 
- Wind Energy Business EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 11.32 crore (vs minus Rs 3 crore in previous quarter)
- Mobility Business Revenue (Q2 FY27): Rs 36 crore (up 10% QoQ from Rs 33 crore)
- Mobility Business EBITDA (Q2 FY27): Rs 1.3 crore (vs Rs 0.8 crore in previous quarter)
- Trade Receivables: Rs 1,251 crore
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: Rs 322 crore
- Bank Balance: Rs 153 crore

### Highlights
- Ash & Coal Handling segment faced seasonal monsoon impact in Northeast and Central India, reducing execution volumes temporarily; managed growth by executing in southern geographies with less rain.
- Entered Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) business as forward integration; first plant commenced operations in Charholi, Pune on 25 September 2026, followed by Hoskote and Kalyan.
- Wind turbine manufacturing business completed initial supplies to all 3 customers (Torrent, KP Energy, and Jindal); order book stands around Rs 1,100 crore - Rs 1,200 crore.
- Green Mobility business achieved positive EBITDA of Rs 1.3 crore; pursuing profitable growth through driver-cum-owner models, contract discipline, operational density, and automation.
- Demerger process for Green Mobility completed key procedural milestones; final NCLT approval awaited (listed for 11 November 2026), expected completion in 2 to 3 months.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains standalone revenue growth guidance of 20% to 25% for the coming years.
- Wind Energy business targeting EBITDA margin of 4% to 5% and PAT margin of 2.5% to 3% for the current financial year and H1 of next year.
- Wind Energy business execution targeted at Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,400 crore for the full financial year.
- Ash handling volume target of 90,000 metric tonnes per month by Q4.
- Management aims to bring Green Mobility business to PAT-level profitability in a year's time.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's thermal power capacity planned to increase to 150 GW by 2035 (vs earlier envisaged 97 GW), providing strong structural growth for ash handling.
- Stricter environmental compliance accelerating outsourcing of ash management to organized players.
- Indian RMC market expected to grow from 240 million cubic meters (valued at Rs 1.25 lakh crore) in 2025 to 317 million cubic meters (valued at Rs 1.74 lakh crore) by 2031 (7% CAGR).
- India added ~1.8 GW of wind capacity in July-September 2026 (30% QoQ, 21% YoY); total installed wind capacity reached 59.2 GW as of September 2026. MNRE indicates India could add up to 8 GW wind capacity in FY27.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Finance costs increased due to vendor payments routed through the TReDS platform for MSMEs, which simultaneously yielded additional discounts at the COGS level, positively impacting gross margins.
- Other expenses reduced QoQ due to the absence of one-time bank processing/prepayment charges of ~Rs 4.5 crore incurred in Q1.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- RMC business is adopting a capital-light, asset-light, lease-based model with zero capex for plants. Pune plant capacity is 60 cubic meters per hour, targeting 4,000 to 4,500 cubic meters per month.
- Backward integration / setting up an indigenous wind manufacturing plant is in full swing, expected to be operational within 12 to 14 months, with real profitability impact in 18 to 20 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital-intensive business with a receivable cycle of 120 to 130 days; cash flows remain strongly positive with zero bad debts.
- Promoter share pledge: ~Rs 400 crore to Rs 450 crore is a secondary pledge given for the acquisition of another business, which management expects to completely remove within 3 to 5 months; a small portion (~Rs 100 crore) is borrowed money. Management stated zero risk of forced selling by lenders.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Seasonal monsoon conditions in Q2 temporarily impact execution volumes and transportation in Northeast and Central India.
- Delays in billing and certification processes (taking up to 70 days) push up receivable cycles.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Udit Sehgal (PinPoint X Capital) asked about the wind energy order pipeline and pricing negotiations:** Management replied they are in the final price negotiation stage for large volumes, expecting closure within 30 to 45 days.
- **Saket Kapoor (Kapoor & Company) questioned the increase in finance costs despite high cash balances:** Management explained that payments through the TReDS platform increase the finance cost line item while lowering direct vendor costs (COGS), yielding net gross margin benefits.
- **Saket Kapoor asked about promoter share pledge risks given falling stock prices:** Management clarified that the bulk of the pledge is secondary collateral for a separate business acquisition, which will be lifted in 3 to 5 months as principals are repaid, assuring no forced selling will occur.
- **Sarthak Jain (Desvelado Advisory) asked about RMC plant capacity, utilization, and capex:** Anil Kulkarni (Chief Business Officer, RMC) stated the model is asset-light with no plant capex, and the Pune plant (60 cubic meter/hour capacity) targets 4,000 to 4,500 cubic meters per month.
- **Smith Jain (Flawless Company) asked about overdue receivables:** Management stated that overdue amount is zero, there is zero risk of bad debt, and collections are watertight, though billing certification takes time.

### One-line takeaway
Refex Industries delivered robust YoY revenue and PAT growth in Q2 FY27 despite monsoon headwinds, while expanding into the asset-light RMC business and advancing wind and mobility segments.
