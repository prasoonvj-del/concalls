# Hubtown Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-06 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management emphasizes strong execution, a large embedded revenue pipeline, and the transformative "Hubtown 2.0" merger process.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 156 crore (down 17% YoY, down 3% QoQ).
*   **PBT:** Rs 32 crore (down 55% YoY).
*   **PAT:** Rs 27 crore (down 68% YoY).
*   **Pre-sales:** Rs 535 crore.
*   **Collections:** Rs 320 crore.
*   **Total borrowings:** Rs 5,181 crore.
*   **Contracted revenue pipeline:** Rs 11,583 crore (yet to be recognized).

### Highlights
*   **Hubtown 2.0 Merger:** Two schemes have received key approvals and await NCLT sanction; the third scheme is in the regulatory approval process.
*   **Portfolio Expansion:** Post-merger, the development portfolio is expected to expand from ~7.13 million sq. ft. to over 34 million sq. ft.
*   **Project Status:** 25 South (Prabhadevi) is nearing completion; one tower is handed over, the second is expected this year, and the final tower is expected in March '27.
*   **Debt Reduction:** Management reported total borrowings of Rs 5,181 crore, noting that legacy bank, NBFC, and foreign fund debt have been retired.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Pre-sales Target:** Management targets Rs 6,000 crore for FY27.
*   **Collections Target:** Management targets Rs 3,000 crore for FY27.
*   **Debt Goal:** Management aims to be net debt-free by FY31.
*   **New Launches:** Planned for 25 Downtown (Tower 5), 25 Estates, 25 Chalets (Thane), and Chembur Phase 2.
*   **GDV:** Management expects total GDV to exceed Rs 1 lakh crore upon project completion.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Luxury Segment:** Demand remains strong despite a "lull" in Q1; management sees pricing upside of Rs 15,000–20,000/sq. ft. in the Downtown project.
*   **Mid-segment:** Walk-ins were relatively muted, though sales remain strong.
*   **Strategy:** Management will focus on MMR for the next few years; new projects will be executed on a partnership basis to avoid capital blockage.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Cost of Debt:** Currently varies from 14% to 20%.
*   **Refinancing:** Management is exploring options to refinance ~Rs 2,800 crore of high-cost debt to reduce financial expenses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Planned Development:** ~34 million sq. ft. across various projects including 25 Chalets, 25 Estates, and Hubtown Seasons Phase 2.
*   **Annuity:** Commercial assets are planned for launch later this year, with rental income expected to flow in after ~2.5 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Debt Composition:** Of the Rs 5,181 crore total debt, Rs 3,956 crore relates to companies to be merged; debt is project-linked and self-liquidating.
*   **Promoter Pledge:** Two pledges remain; management aims to release them by FY27.
*   **Promoter Holding:** Expected to reach ~68% post-merger.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Accounting Method:** The project completion method causes volatility in reported quarterly earnings.
*   **Approvals:** Statutory approvals are still pending for merger schemes and certain projects (e.g., 25 Estates).
*   **Market Sentiment:** Management noted that stock market volatility and global situations impacted pricing power in the first half of the calendar year.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: When will Tower 5 of 25 Downtown launch?** A: Management plans to launch in Oct-Nov to capture higher pricing.
*   **Q: Can you quantify refinancing savings?** A: Management declined to provide specific numbers until term sheets are finalized.
*   **Q: Why is ROE low?** A: Management attributed it to high-cost debt and project completion accounting; expects improvement via refinancing and price hikes.
*   **Q: Is the promoter pledge for personal use?** A: No, management stated it was used as collateral for funds made available to Hubtown.
*   **Q: Will promoters contribute to future fundraises?** A: Management declined to comment, citing market conditions and the need to maintain public float.

### One-line takeaway
Hubtown is prioritizing the "Hubtown 2.0" merger and debt reduction while leveraging a large, contracted revenue pipeline to drive growth in the luxury MMR segment.
