# Horizon Industrial Parks Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-29 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record revenue and EBITDA, strong leasing momentum, and a significantly strengthened balance sheet post-IPO.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 213 crore (up 30% YoY).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 173 crore (up 34% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 81%.
*   **Contracted revenue run rate:** >Rs 1,000 crore.
*   **Leasing (Q2):** 2.5 million sq. ft. across 14 transactions.
*   **Leasing (H1):** 4.4 million sq. ft.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 2,765 crore (reduced from Rs 4,967 crore as of June 30, 2026).
*   **Cash PAT (H1):** Rs 248 crore.

### Highlights
*   Company is now "in the black" on a reported basis.
*   Onboarded 8 new customers in Q2.
*   80% of H1 leasing came from industrial customers.
*   1.2 million sq. ft. re-leased at a 12% spread.
*   Stabilized portfolio occupancy at 94%.
*   Delivered 1.8 million sq. ft. in Q2 (6 new buildings); 2.7 million sq. ft. total in H1.
*   GRESB 2026 assessment awarded a 5-star green rating (score 99.2 on under-construction assets).

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to deliver 6 million sq. ft. in FY27.
*   Targeting operational area of ~35 million sq. ft. by end of FY27.
*   In-city portfolio expected to deliver ~6 million sq. ft. over the next 3-4 years.
*   Management expects value-added services (solar, etc.) to contribute 5-10% of future revenues.
*   Priorities for H2: Outperform operational targets and launch first multi-story in-city asset in Pune (Q4).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Demand remains robust, driven by "flight to quality," industrialization, and consumption.
*   Occupiers are shifting from fragmented, unorganized facilities to institutional-grade spaces.
*   Industrialization is supported by supply chain diversification ("China Plus One") and favorable government policies.
*   Domestic consumption remains resilient despite geopolitical uncertainties.
*   In-city demand is widening beyond quick commerce/cloud kitchens to include industrial service centers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin sustained at 81%.
*   Refinanced Rs 635 crore facility, reducing borrowing cost by 71 bps.
*   Weighted average debt cost is ~8.18%.
*   Incremental capex on rooftop solar yields >20% IRR.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Fully paid land bank supports ~30 million sq. ft. of future development.
*   Current portfolio: 9 states, 10 markets, 46 assets, 2,300 acres.
*   In-city Pune asset delivery expected in Q4.
*   Solar capacity: 38 MW operational/under implementation; targeting 10 MW rooftop open access project.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net debt reduced to Rs 2,765 crore from Rs 4,967 crore (June 30, 2026).
*   Deleveraging has lowered finance costs and improved credit profile.
*   Internal accruals and existing debt capacity to fund future growth.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   CWC litigation remains sub judice (last hearing Jan 2026; matters deferred).
*   Q3 may see a slight slowdown due to the festive season and global market closures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   *Q: Is leasing cyclical?* A: Management noted demand is becoming more diversified across sectors and geographies, mitigating historical cyclicality.
*   *Q: In-city rental expectations?* A: Management reiterated 2x-3x spread over big format parks (approx. Rs 60-90/sq. ft. range).
*   *Q: CWC litigation risk?* A: Management stated the court has not stopped development; they are continuing to build and are confident in a positive outcome.
*   *Q: Solar strategy?* A: Management clarified they are using rooftop capacity (not land bank) for solar, targeting 18-20% yield on incremental capex.

### One-line takeaway
Horizon Industrial Parks delivered record Q2 results with strong leasing momentum and significant deleveraging, positioning the company for long-term growth through its in-city logistics strategy and industrial portfolio expansion.
