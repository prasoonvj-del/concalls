# Runwal Enterprises Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong pre-sales growth, successful balance sheet deleveraging following the IPO, and resilient demand in the MMR residential market.

### Key numbers
- **Pre-sales:** Rs. 609 crores in Q2 FY27 (up 26% QoQ from Rs. 484 crores in Q1 FY27).
- **Gross collections:** Rs. 550 crores in Q2 FY27 (up 23.5% YoY from Rs. 445 crores in Q2 FY26).
- **Average selling price (ASP):** Rs. 14,907 per sq. ft. in Q2 FY27 (up 11.8% YoY from Rs. 12,600 per sq. ft. in Q4 FY26).
- **Area delivered:** 1.90 million sq. ft. in H1 FY27 (more than doubled YoY from 0.86 million sq. ft. in H1 FY26); nearly 1 million sq. ft. delivered in Q2 FY27.
- **Equity base:** More than doubled to Rs. 1,566 crores at the end of Q2 FY27 from Rs. 689 crores at the end of Q2 FY26.
- **Net debt-to-equity ratio:** Reduced from 3.1x to 1.5x at the end of Q2 FY27.
- **Development portfolio:** ~88.37 million sq. ft. total area in MMR (~18.14 million sq. ft. ongoing, ~56 million sq. ft. upcoming).
- **Annuity portfolio:** ~300,000 sq. ft. ongoing commercial projects (Runwal BKC) and ~800,000 sq. ft. retail mall at Dombivli.
- **Annuity debt:** Rs. 106 crores construction finance in the mall and Rs. 115 crores non-convertible debentures outstanding (total Rs. 221 crores).
- **Ready inventory:** ~0.38 million sq. ft. of completed saleable area (70% in Runwal MyCity Cluster 5).
- **FY27 Guidance:** Pre-sales growth close to 20% (targeting Rs. 2,500–2,600 to 3,000 crores); revenue recognition expected to be ~10% higher than FY26; margins expected to revert to FY26 levels.

### Highlights
- Net debt-to-equity improved to 1.5x, driven by IPO proceeds (Rs. 500 crores), conversion of convertible debentures, and debt repayment.
- Deliveries doubled in H1 FY27 due to OC receipt for certain towers in Phase 3 of Runwal Gardens.
- Management appointed JLL to monetize a land parcel in the MyCity project, receiving interest from 11-12 parties.
- *Continues pattern from earlier record:* Deleveraging post-IPO successfully improved net debt-to-equity to 1.5x.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided for FY27 pre-sales growth of around 20%, aiming for between Rs. 2,500–2,600 and 3,000 crores.
- Revenue recognition for FY27 is projected to be roughly 10% higher than FY26, with margins reverting to FY26 levels.
- Management expects to liquidate the entire ready-to-move-in inventory of ~0.38 million sq. ft. by the end of FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- MMR led India's top seven residential markets in Q3 CY2026 with ~31,750 housing units sold (per ANAROCK).
- Festive demand remained strong; Mumbai recorded 3,683 property registrations during the 10-day Diwali period with stamp duty collections up 13% to Rs. 384 crores (per Knight Frank India).
- Management and analysts noted Mumbai remains resilient against broader tech/IT job market downturns.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Affordable segment projects (Runwal Gardens, MyCity) have inherently lower gross margins, impacting H1 revenue recognition mix.
- Product mix target across segments: equal 33% distribution between affordable, mid-income, and luxury/township developments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pursuing an asset-light approach via JV, JD, and partnership models for new opportunities.
- Expanding footprint from eastern/central Mumbai suburbs towards South Mumbai (Mahalaxmi), Central Mumbai, and western suburbs.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity reduced from 3.1x to 1.5x.
- Total debt tied to annuity assets stands at Rs. 221 crores (Rs. 106 crores construction finance for the mall + Rs. 115 crores NCDs).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Reported revenue and margins can vary significantly quarter-to-quarter depending on when projects receive OCs and customer possession.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ramesh (TCS) on full-year revenue/profitability vs FY25-26 dips:** Shashi Bhushan explained that revenue recognition depends on OC timing; H2 will see mid-income project OCs, driving revenue ~10% higher than FY26 with margins reverting to FY26 levels.
2. **Shivam Agarwal on debt bifurcation:** Shashi Bhushan clarified that Rs. 221 crores of debt relates to annuity assets (mall and NCDs), while the balance relates to residential projects.
3. **Nikita Sharma on launch timelines and approvals:** Shashi Bhushan stated no running projects or upcoming launches are awaiting regulatory approvals, so no timeline extensions are expected.
4. **Nachiket Kale on average selling price (ASP) drivers:** Shashi Bhushan and Pradumna Kanodia attributed the 14% H1 ASP rise to contributions from the 7 Mahalaxmi project and strata sales of retail/commercial units.

### One-line takeaway
Runwal Enterprises delivered strong Q2 FY27 pre-sales and collections while successfully reducing its net debt-to-equity ratio to 1.5x following its IPO, backed by resilient MMR demand.
