# Ashiana Housing Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational and financial performance with significant growth in bookings, collections, and revenue, supported by successful project handovers.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained FY27 PAT target of >Rs 250 crore.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from Cautious to Positive; management highlighted strong booking momentum and successful handovers.
- **New topics:** Addressed a query regarding an alleged land encroachment at Lavasa (Apollo Hospitals matter), stating it is sub judice with no material developments.
- **Promises:** Deliveries for Ashiana Amarah Phase 1 and Ashiana Anmol Phase 3 were completed as planned.

### Key numbers
- **Booking Value (Q2):** Rs 834 crore (vs Rs 358 crore in Q1 FY27; Rs 303 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Booking Value (H1):** Rs 1,192 crore (+62% YoY).
- **Collections (Q2):** Rs 487 crore (+20% YoY).
- **Revenue from Operations (Q2):** Rs 576 crore (vs Rs 107 crore in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT (Q2):** Rs 51 crore (vs Rs 13 crore in Q1 FY27; Rs 28 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Average Realization (Q2):** Rs 8,813 per sq ft (+20% YoY).
- **Pre-tax Operating Cash Flow (Q2):** Rs 138 crore.

### Highlights
- **Senior Living Strategy:** Aiming for Rs 1,500 crore annual booking value by 2030; current Senior Living GDV >Rs 9,000 crore.
- **New Land:** Added a JDA project in Gurugram with an estimated GDV of Rs 1,300 crore (7.25 lakh sq ft).
- **Launches:** Ashiana Aranya (Chennai) expected in Q4 FY27; three new phase launches planned for H2 FY27.
- **Rating:** CARE Ratings upgraded issuer rating from CARE A to CARE A+.

### Guidance & outlook
- **FY27 Targets:** Management expects PAT of >Rs 250 crore and remains confident in meeting the >Rs 700 crore Senior Living booking target.
- **Long-term:** Medium-term PAT target of Rs 500 crore by FY30.
- **Slippage:** Cumulative PAT target of Rs 2,000 crore by 2030 is expected to slip into FY31 due to project timelines.

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Senior Living:** Management is actively building the category from niche to mainstream; views it as less cyclical than regular housing.
- **NCR:** Management expressed concern regarding potential construction bans due to pollution, which may cause minor delivery slippages.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Margins:** Q2 margins were impacted by historical high land cost projects (e.g., Ashiana Anmol).
- **Outlook:** Margins expected to improve in H2 as newer, better-margin projects (Advik, Tarang, Ekansh, Amodh) are delivered.
- **Senior Living:** Expected to yield ~5% higher gross margins than regular housing.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Bengaluru:** Launch expected late next financial year or H1 FY29.
- **Expansion:** Actively seeking Senior Living parcels; targeting another Rs 8,000 crore–Rs 9,000 crore of GDV acquisition over the next 24–30 months.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Liquidity:** Company maintains a net cash position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Legal:** Alleged encroachment at Lavasa (Apollo Hospitals) is sub judice; management stated no material developments.
- **Legal:** Class action suit on a project remains stagnant with no movement.
- **Operational:** Potential construction bans in NCR could impact delivery timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Apollo Hospitals/Lavasa encroachment (Shubh Kalra):** Management stated the matter is sub judice and there have been no material developments.
- **Margin pressure (Aditya):** Management clarified that current margins are understated due to historical high land cost projects (Anmol); expects normalization with new project deliveries.
- **Malhar Phase 4 launch (Rahul Iyer):** Management explained they are completing the project despite lower margins because capital is already deployed and they want to exit the project fully.
- **Senior Living competition (Rohit):** Management believes the market is too small for large developers to prioritize; emphasized Ashiana's 22-year track record and specialized design/service capabilities as a competitive moat.
- **PAT target (Ayush Shah):** Management confirmed they are on track for >Rs 250 crore PAT in FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Ashiana Housing delivered a strong Q2 with significant growth in bookings and revenue, maintaining its FY27 PAT guidance while continuing to scale its high-margin Senior Living business despite minor project timeline slippages.
