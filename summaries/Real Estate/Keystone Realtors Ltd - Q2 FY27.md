# Keystone Realtors Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance with record presales and significant YoY growth in EBITDA and PAT.

### Key numbers
*   **Presales:** Rs 1,423 crore (highest ever quarterly presales; +84% YoY).
*   **H1 FY27 Presales:** Rs 2,041 crore (+11% YoY).
*   **Revenue (H1 FY27):** Rs 967 crore (+25% YoY).
*   **EBITDA (Q2 FY27):** Rs 74.2 crore (+100% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q2 FY27):** 14.5% (vs 7.1% in Q2 FY26).
*   **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 100.8 crore (vs Rs 9.9 crore in Q2 FY26).
*   **Collections:** Rs 541 crore (Q2); Rs 1,141 crore (H1).
*   **Gross Debt:** Rs 838 crore.
*   **Net Debt-to-Equity:** 0.04:1.

### Highlights
*   **Launches:** Launched *Rustomjee Ozone Skye* (Malad West) and commenced construction of *25HQ* (Prabhadevi) with combined GDV of Rs 2,242 crore.
*   **Project Additions:** Added 2 new projects in H1 FY27 (GDV Rs 547 crore). Since FY23, added 27 projects (GDV Rs 31,079 crore).
*   **Construction:** Construction spends increased 21% YoY in H1 FY27 to Rs 547 crore. Completed 4 RERA projects (1.39 million sq. ft.) 9-15 months ahead of schedule.
*   **Technology:** Signed contract with Robin Village Development (subsidiary of Tiong Seng, Singapore) to implement precast construction technology.
*   **Fund Management:** Mt K Kapital announced a second fund of USD 450 million with Marubeni Corporation.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Presales:** Management maintained full-year presales guidance of Rs 5,000 crore (achieved 41% in H1).
*   **Collections:** Management expects full-year collections of Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 3,100 crore.
*   **OCF:** Management targets full-year Operating Cash Flow (OCF) of Rs 700 crore to Rs 800 crore.
*   **Launches:** Management expects to meet the Rs 8,000 crore annual launch GDV target, with a "launch-heavy" Q4 planned (including GTB Nagar, Avinash Tower, and projects in Thane/Dombivli).
*   **Investment:** Expects to spend ~Rs 500 crore on new projects and approvals in H2 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Sales momentum is driven by the Luxury and Super Premium categories.
*   Management noted that the first two quarters are generally slower, with Q3 and Q4 typically seeing maximum sales volume.
*   93% of H1 FY27 sales were sustenance sales; 7% from new launches.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   EBITDA margin for the mass market segment is expected to improve from 16% to 31%, primarily driven by the Virar project (JDA model).
*   Management expects overall EBITDA margins to move from 24% to 33% as projects mature and price realizations improve.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Investment in land and approvals reached Rs 425 crore in H1 FY27 (+41% YoY).
*   Commercial foray: *25HQ* (Prabhadevi) is designed as a Grade A lease asset; Thane commercial project (1.1 million sq. ft.) construction to begin this financial year.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Gross debt-to-equity ratio: 0.28:1.
*   Cash balance: Rs 705 crore.
*   Management stated they do not require equity capital in the immediate future.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Some project launches (Dindoshi, Om Nagar) have been pushed to H1 FY28.
*   Management noted a previous "stalemate in terms of approval processes," though stated this has now opened up.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the sustainability of high sustenance sales. **Management:** Attributed it to strong launches in the previous year (Rs 9,500 crore worth) and a sales philosophy of liquidating 30-40% of stock in the first 6-8 months.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the jump in net debt. **Management:** Explained that net debt increased by Rs 195 crore due to deployment into new projects (Rs 425 crore spent in H1); emphasized that cash on the balance sheet is better deployed into projects.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the commercial strategy. **Management:** Confirmed a 4-pronged strategy (redevelopment, plotted development, commercial leasing, and out-of-town expansion) to reach the 2030 target of Rs 10,000 crore.
*   **Analyst:** Asked about the margin guidance (24% to 33%). **Management:** Clarified that the improvement is driven by the mass market segment (specifically the Virar project) and better price realizations as projects mature.

### One-line takeaway
Keystone Realtors delivered a strong quarter with record presales and significant margin expansion, supported by a robust launch pipeline and a disciplined, low-leverage balance sheet.
