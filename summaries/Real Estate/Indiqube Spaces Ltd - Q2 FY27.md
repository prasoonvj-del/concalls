# Indiqube Spaces Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by improving steady-state occupancy and expanding value-added services.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained annual guidance to add close to 2 million square feet (~44,000 seats) in rent-paying area.
- Tone and demand view: Maintained an optimistic view on enterprise and GCC demand; management highlighted robust white-collar job creation and strong office absorption.
- New topics or risks: Proposed a ~Rs 35 crore investment in SaiEdge Realty as a pilot to co-develop land/assets in high-performing micro markets; expanded IndiQare retail into branch management for banks/fintechs. Old risks remained absent.
- Last quarter's promises: Continued steady expansion towards the 2 million sq ft annual addition target, backed by 3.87 million sq ft of headroom.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 475 crore, up 34% YoY
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 904 crore, up 35% YoY
- EBITDA (IGAAP): Rs 105 crore, up 40% YoY
- PAT: Rs 57 crore, up 63% YoY
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 92 crore, up 66% YoY
- H1 Value-Added Services (VAS) Revenue: Rs 154 crore, up 74% YoY (contributing 17% of operating revenue)
- Steady-state occupancy: Improved to 91% (compared to 89% last year)
- Portfolio scale: ~10.86 million sq ft across 138 centers in 17 cities; ~241,000 seats; 884 clients
- Rent-yielding area: 7.05 million sq ft (total portfolio signed: 10.86 million sq ft)
- Solar capacity: ~30 MW operational capacity with another 30 MW expected to become operational over the next year (H1 solar revenue: ~Rs 15 crore against Rs 46 crore investment)

### Highlights
- Global Capability Centers (GCCs) contribute 54% of revenue; 41% of revenue comes from clients occupying more than one IndiQube center.
- Clients requiring over 300 seats account for 65% of occupied space, with an average tenure of ~47 months.
- 63% of clients acquired through direct/brand channels; brokerage accounts for 2.29% of operating revenue.
- Initiated a pilot investment of ~Rs 35 crore in SaiEdge Realty Private to co-develop assets in tight micro-markets like Andheri, Bangalore.
- Expanded IndiQare retail into branch management, completing ~30 branches for a large gold bank.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management guided full-year EBITDA margin to be range-bound between 19% to 21%, translating to ~Rs 380 crore to Rs 400 crore in EBITDA.
- Expects to add ~2 million square feet in rent-paying area (~44,000 seats) in FY27, reaching 9.7 million square feet of rent-paying area by the end of FY27.
- Expects VAS revenue contribution to grow organically by at least 2% to 4% annually from current levels.
- Planned solar capex of ~Rs 100 crore for the ongoing 30 MW expansion, with a similar investment planned for next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Enterprise demand remains robust, led by GCCs, multinational corporations, and Indian enterprises.
- 80% of centers are located within 3 kilometers of a metro station; 3.46 million square feet is green certified.
- Management remains unconcerned about AI disruption, citing strong historical absorption (66 million sq ft in 9 months, up 10-11% YoY) and steady white-collar private sector job creation in India (~1 million jobs/year).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Full-year EBITDA margin guided at 19% to 21%. EBIT margin expected in the 12% to 14% range.
- Profitability growth supported by higher steady-state occupancy (91%), lower finance costs, improved VAS margins (~18% to 20% overall), and operating leverage.
- Fit-out investment stands at ~Rs 1,650 per sq ft, with an investment recovery period of ~36 months.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- 3.8 million sq ft headroom available (~85,000 seats), of which 2.67 million sq ft has signed LOIs expected to go live in 12 to 18 months.
- Solar expansion capex of ~Rs 100 crore underway with a 3-year payback period.
- SaiEdge Realty pilot requires ~Rs 35 crore investment with a 3-year gestation period.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Income tax paid was Rs 13 crore in H1 FY27 (vs Rs 22 crore in full-year FY26).
- Average customer lock-in is ~35 months (47 months for clients >300 seats); average landlord tenure is ~15 years with a ~38-month lock-in commitment for IndiQube.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as major operational risks by management, though quarterly occupancy and margin figures can fluctuate due to the timing of new space additions and ramp-ups (taking 6 months to break even and 12 months to reach steady state).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shamit Ashar (Ambit Capital) on SaiEdge Realty and Solar Capex:** Management explained the Rs 35 crore SaiEdge investment is a pilot to secure strategic supply in tight micro-markets (like Andheri/Bangalore) with potential capital appreciation, and confirmed ~Rs 100 crore annual capex for solar expansion across Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu.
2. **Adhidev (ICICI Securities) on Margin Uptick and VAS:** Management attributed margin expansion to higher occupancy (91%), lower finance costs, improved VAS margins, and operating leverage, reiterating that VAS operates as a "foot in the door" rather than being prioritized purely for near-term margin maximization.
3. **Mohit Agrawal (IIFL Capital) on Renewal Dynamics:** Management stated annual attrition is ~10%, and 90% of expiring contracts are renewed (80% on existing terms with normal escalations, 10% with adjusted escalations, and 10% with minor discounts), resulting in pure PBT generation since initial capex is mostly recovered within the first 3-year lock-in.
4. **Rishith Shah (Axis Capital) on Branch Management and Bandwidth:** Management clarified that retail branch management for fintechs/banks leverages existing vendor networks and the MiQube tech stack, consuming minimal management bandwidth alongside the core Grow model.
5. **Chayan Jain (Individual Investor) on One-time Revenues and Segments:** Management explained that non-recurring and recurring revenue distinctions create tracking confusion, emphasizing total revenue growth drivers like rent escalations, VAS growth, and higher steady-state occupancy.
6. **Hitaindra Pradhan (Maximal Capital) on AI Impact:** Management noted that AI has not impacted office demand, highlighting record real estate absorption and robust GCC expansion in India.

### One-line takeaway
Indiqube delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with revenue up 34% YoY to Rs 475 crore and PAT up 63% YoY to Rs 57 crore, propelled by higher steady-state occupancy, scaling value-added services, and solid enterprise demand.
