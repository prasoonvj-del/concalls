# The Phoenix Mills Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong operating momentum, broad-based double-digit consumption growth across all malls, and robust annuity income growth without any new mall additions in the last two years.

### Key numbers
- **Retail, Office, Hotel, and Asset Management Revenue:** Rs. 1,055 crore, up 16% YoY (H1 annuity EBITDA: Rs. 1,318 crore, up 20% YoY).
- **Office Segment Revenue:** Rs. 89 crore, up 64% YoY; EBITDA up 64% YoY to Rs. 56 crore.
- **Retail Consumption:** Rs. 4,685 crore, up 25% YoY (H1 consumption: Rs. 9,415 crore, up 28% YoY).
- **Retail Rental Income:** Rs. 605 crore, up 15% YoY (H1 retail rental income: Rs. 1,200 crore, up 16% YoY).
- **Retail EBITDA:** Rs. 639 crore, up 18% YoY (H1 retail EBITDA: Rs. 1,264 crore, up 17% YoY).
- **Consolidated Revenue (Q2):** Rs. 1,153 crore.
- **Consolidated Operating EBITDA (Q2):** Rs. 710 crore (EBITDA margin: 62%).
- **Profit After Tax and Minority Interest (Q2):** Rs. 326 crore.
- **Operating Free Cash Flow (Annuity Businesses, Q2):** Rs. 570 crore, up 28% YoY (H1 annuity free cash flow: Rs. 1,137 crore, up 30% YoY).
- **Gross Debt:** Rs. 6,117 crore; **Liquidity:** Rs. 2,638 crore; **Net Debt:** Rs. 3,479 crore (reduced by Rs. 179 crore QoQ from June 2026; net debt-to-EBITDA at 1.2x).
- **Average Cost of Debt:** 7.66%.
- **Office Occupancy:** 79% leased in September 2026 (70% in March 2026, 72% in June 2026); 54% rent-generating.

### Highlights
- Portfolio consumption growth was broad-based; every mall delivered double-digit consumption growth in Q2.
- Fashion and accessories grew 22%, F&B grew 21%, and entertainment grew 16%. Excluding jewelry and electronics, consumption grew 22%.
- Phoenix Mall of Asia (Bengaluru) scaled significantly with consumption up 68%, rental income up 44%, trading occupancy reaching 96% (up from 84%), and trading density crossing Rs. 3,300 pspm.
- Over 675 leasing deals completed in H1 covering approximately 1.8 million sq. ft. across operational and under-construction portfolios.
- NCLT approved the Selective Capital Reduction scheme for ISMDPL (Tranche 2 of CPP Investments stake acquisition, consideration of Rs. 1,350 crore), expected to be completed in November 2026, increasing PML's stake from 58.33% to 68.1%.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a 14%-15% rental growth guidance for FY27 and FY28, with management optimistic about crossing the 15% retail rental growth guidance for FY27 based on H1 performance.
- Festive season (October-December) expected to support healthy consumption, with October YoY consumption growth projected between 20% and 25%.
- Rent-generating office occupancy expected to reach 70% by March and 80% by June/July next year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Growth remains healthy across discretionary wallets and is not dependent on one or two categories.
- Approximately one-third of incremental consumption came from new stores, which uplift overall sales across existing brands by attracting affluent customers.
- September was seasonally slower due to Dussehra and Diwali falling later, compounded by 5% of leasable area undergoing active fit-outs/renovations (impacting quarterly consumption by 2%-3%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- St. Regis, Mumbai maintained healthy EBITDA margins at 44%, with Average Room Rates (ARR) up 20%.
- Retail EBITDA supported by rental growth, operating leverage, and cost discipline.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex outflow in Q2 was Rs. 389 crore (Rs. 349 crore towards construction, remainder towards FSI/land). H1 total land/FSI spend was ~Rs. 800 crore and construction spend was ~Rs. 700 crore.
- Ongoing retail expansions: Phoenix Palassio (adding ~100,000 sq. ft. and ~100 stores); Phoenix Palladium (adding 150 stores in Rise 1, 60% leased at rentals upwards of Rs. 500 pspm).
- Upcoming operational timelines: Rise mall in Bengaluru (Calendar Year 2027); Kolkata retail (fit-outs starting Q2, launch 6 months after); Surat mall (complete in 2027, open in 2028); Grand Hyatt Bengaluru and first phase of Bengaluru offices (~400,000 sq. ft.) and Rise offices targeted for 2028.
- Thane, Chandigarh, and Coimbatore retail malls targeted for operations by Calendar Year 2030, scaled up to 1.6 to 1.8 million sq. ft. each.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt decreased by Rs. 179 crore QoQ to Rs. 3,479 crore.
- Credit rating upgrades expected within the next 1-2 weeks.
- Residential revenue recognition is lumpy (H1 residential revenue at Rs. 62 crore due to handover timing; Rs. 120 crore sold out but unbooked, expected to reflect in H2).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary impact on trading occupancy and consumption (estimated at 2%-3% for Q2) due to ongoing active fit-outs, renovations, and asset optimization across properties like Palladium, Palassio, and Mumbai MarketCity.
- Regulatory approval delays and election timing pushed back Kolkata residential launch timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Puneet Gulati (HSBC) on capex divergence:** Management clarified that H1 capex included major land payments (such as Chandigarh land in Q1 for ~Rs. 700 crore, smaller parcels in Coimbatore/Kolkata, and an advance for an under-discussion acquisition), alongside ~Rs. 700 crore for construction.
- **Gaurav Khandelwal (JPMorgan) on September dip:** Management explained September's relative softness was due to the late festive season and 5% of leasable portfolio area undergoing active renovations/fit-outs.
- **Biplab Debbarma (Emkay Global) on Kolkata residential delay:** Management stated initial plans were for a different asset class before shifting to pure residential, and approvals took longer due to elections, with a launch expected in two quarters.
- **Parvez Qazi (Nuvama) on consumption growth drivers:** Management noted trading occupancy changes contributed only ~3 percentage points of the 25% YoY consumption growth, with the rest coming from natural portfolio growth.
- **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital) on asset occupancies:** Management clarified that lower trading occupancies in Mumbai MarketCity and Indore are deliberate, strategic churns to bring in newly entered international/national brands upon lease expiries.
- **Parikshit Kandpal (HDFC Securities) on new city additions (Slide 49):** Management noted that cities like Hyderabad, NCR, and Jaipur are potential targets for the 2035-2036 plan, and land acquisition structuring (JV vs. outright) will be decided on a case-by-case basis.

### One-line takeaway
The Phoenix Mills delivered a robust Q2 FY27 with 16% growth in annuity revenue and 25% growth in retail consumption, driven entirely by existing operational assets and active brand mix optimization ahead of upcoming commercial and retail expansions.
