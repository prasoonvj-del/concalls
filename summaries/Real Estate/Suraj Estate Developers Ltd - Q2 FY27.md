# Suraj Estate Developers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-07 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Healthy sales momentum, strong commercial traction, and clear guidance confidence.

### Key numbers
- **Sales Value:** Rs 206 crore (up 35% YoY from Rs 153 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 at Rs 347 crore (up 48% YoY).
- **Sales Area:** 41,487 sq ft (up 19% YoY) in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 at 70,321 sq ft (up 37% YoY).
- **Collections:** Rs 104 crore (up 46% YoY) in Q2 FY27; H1 FY27 at Rs 190 crore.
- **Total Income:** Rs 171 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 18% YoY from Rs 145 crore); H1 FY27 at Rs 317 crore (up 14% YoY).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 56 crore in Q2 FY27 with an EBITDA margin of 33%; H1 FY27 EBITDA at Rs 111 crore with ~35% margin (vs Rs 66 crore and Rs 116 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 23 crore in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs 33 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FY27 PAT at Rs 46 crore (vs Rs 54 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Gross / Net Debt:** Gross debt at Rs 800 crore; Net debt at Rs 710 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Cost of Debt:** 13% to 13.25% range.
- **Project Metrics:** Cumulative sold area of 6.36 lakh sq ft at average realization of ~Rs 46,170 per sq ft across ongoing projects. Balance receivables from sold inventory at Rs 1,168 crores; unsold area of 1.20 lakh sq ft with estimated GDV of ~Rs 708 crores. Total estimated receivables from ongoing projects at ~Rs 1,876 crores.
- **Suraj One Business Bay:** ~Rs 500 crores inventory sold; combined project saleable area enhanced to 3.59 lakh sq ft; GDV potential increased to ~Rs 2,000 crores.

### Highlights
- Recorded robust 35% YoY growth in Q2 FY27 sales value.
- Acquired development rights for the land parcel adjoining Suraj One Business Bay in Mahim, expanding total GDV to ~Rs 2,000 crores.
- Received Commencement Certificates for Suraj Nova (formerly Lobo Villa/Ellis Villa) and Suraj Madonna (Dadar West), with launches and RERA registrations targeted for Q3 FY27 (combined GDV of Rs 250 crores).
- Portfolio currently spans 13 ongoing sites (23.54 lakh sq ft development area, 7.57 lakh sq ft RERA carpet) and an upcoming pipeline of 18 projects (13.46 lakh sq ft carpet area).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated confidence in achieving the FY27 presales guidance of Rs 700 crores.
- Management targets reducing net debt to close to Rs 600 crores by the end of the financial year, aided by project handovers.

### Demand & industry commentary
- South and Central Mumbai continue to see strong demand in value luxury (1 and 2 BHKs) and commercial segments.
- Limited availability of Grade A office space in CBD, Worli, and Lower Parel supports commercial market pricing.
- Redevelopment continues to drive new housing supply given the scarcity of vacant land parcels.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin stood at 33%; H1 FY27 margin at ~35%.
- Cost of debt remains in the 13% to 13.25% range.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital deployment primarily directed toward business development and ongoing/upcoming projects, including ~Rs 85 crore for the Suraj One Business Bay land acquisition.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Gross debt stood at Rs 800 crores and net debt at Rs 710 crores as of September 30, 2026 (increase driven by business development and project deployment).
- Debt is expected to reduce by ~Rs 150 crores due to upcoming project handovers in FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management other than typical approval timelines and environmental clearance hurdles (though EC for the commercial expansion has already been secured).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Mittal (JH Investments) on guidance contribution:** Management clarified that the Rs 700 crore presales guidance will be driven primarily by already launched ongoing projects and upcoming residential components, alongside strong traction from the commercial segment.
2. **Kalpesh Jain (Jain Securities) on net debt increase and reduction plan:** Management explained that the Rs 96 crore net debt increase included ~Rs 85 crore for land acquisition, and guided for net debt reduction to ~Rs 600 crores by year-end via 5-6 project handovers reducing debt by ~Rs 150 crores.
3. **Rohit Kumar (ADM Advisors) on launch pipeline execution:** Management confirmed that out of the Rs 1,600 crore FY27 launch pipeline, Rs 800 crores (commercial) and Rs 250 crores (residential) are targeted for launch in Q3 FY27, with the balance Rs 550 crores in Q4.
4. **Sakshi Pratab (Pratab Securities) on Bandra project progress:** Management stated that all land acquisitions except one are closed, with formalities like charity commissioner approvals underway for the remaining components.
5. **Rohit Kumar (ADM Advisors) on commercial portfolio contribution:** Management highlighted that commercial projects like One Business Bay (Rs 2,000 crore GDV) will form a larger part of future cash flows and profitability due to sheer project size and buoyant commercial demand.

### One-line takeaway
Suraj Estate Developers reported a strong Q2 FY27 with a 35% YoY growth in sales value, underpinned by solid execution in South Central Mumbai, major commercial scale-up at Mahim, and clear debt-reduction visibility for the second half.
