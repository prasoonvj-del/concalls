# Ajmera Realty & Infra India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong revenue growth, consistent sales momentum, and successful deleveraging, despite ongoing regulatory delays regarding land conversion.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue:** Rs 320 crore (up 45% YoY from Rs 221 crore).
*   **EBITDA:** Rs 84 crore (up 40% YoY from Rs 60 crore).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 26%.
*   **PAT:** Rs 49 crore (up 55% YoY from Rs 31 crore).
*   **PAT Margin:** 15%.
*   **Sales (Q2):** Rs 224 crore (72,883 sq. ft.).
*   **Collections (Q2):** Rs 190 crore.
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 646 crore (reduced by Rs 34 crore during the quarter).
*   **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 0.44x.
*   **Weighted Average Cost of Debt:** 10.75% (declined below 11% for the first time).

### Highlights
*   **Operational Performance:** H1 FY27 sales reached Rs 370 crore (1,16,620 sq. ft.) with collections of Rs 363 crore.
*   **Revenue Visibility:** Total visibility exceeds Rs 10,500 crore (Rs 3,510 crore from ongoing projects and Rs 7,008 crore from upcoming launches).
*   **Project Updates:**
    *   *Ajmera Manhattan 1 (Wadala):* 95% sold; possession expected next year.
    *   *Ajmera Manhattan 2 (Wadala):* 56% sold; excavation/raft work in progress.
    *   *Ajmera Greenfinity (Wadala):* 94% sold; OC expected shortly.
    *   *Ajmera Vihara (Bhandup):* 84% sold; RCC work ongoing.
    *   *Ajmera Solis:* 87% sold (Phase 1); excavation/raft work ongoing.
    *   *33Fifteen (Bandra):* 25% sold; revenue recognition achieved this quarter.
    *   *Vann by Ajmera:* 4% sold; shoring/piling/excavation underway.
    *   *Bangalore Portfolio:* Ajmera Iris (94% sold, finishing stage); Ajmera Marina (76% sold, up to 8 floors).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Presales Target:** Management remains confident in achieving the FY26/FY27 presales target of Rs 2,200 crore, supported by 8 upcoming project launches in H2.
*   **Launch Pipeline:** 8 projects totaling 38 lakh sq. ft. and Rs 7,000 crore top-line potential are planned for H2.
*   **Commercial Strategy:** The company plans to launch a boutique office project in Wadala (1 million sq. ft.) by mid-to-end February. A second annuity tower is planned for FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Market Trends:** Strong demand for premium/lifestyle housing (units >Rs 1 crore) and Grade A commercial office space, particularly from GCC players.
*   **Interest Rates:** Management acknowledged the 25 bps repo rate hike by RBI but believes underlying demand for quality housing remains resilient.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Margin Outlook:** Management expects to maintain EBITDA margins above 30% historically, though they are monitoring commodity price volatility and labor cost inflation.
*   **Water Scarcity:** Management is preparing mitigation plans (tankers, optimized construction scheduling) for potential water shortages in Q4/Q1, though no current restrictions exist.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Kanjurmarg Land:** The company is actively pursuing land conversion to freehold status. Management is in discussions for a 7-acre plot tie-up (monetization or JV) but is prioritizing the conversion process first.
*   **Financial Closure:** Management stated that financial closure for the Wadala commercial project is already in place.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Regulatory Delays:** The Kanjurmarg land conversion has faced repeated delays due to legal and regulatory processes, with no fixed timeline provided.
*   **Launch Execution:** While management is confident, they acknowledged that 1 or 2 of the 8 planned launches might slip into the next financial year.
*   **Vann Project Sales:** Slow initial sales at the Versova project were attributed to the micro-market's preference for under-construction projects rather than pre-launch phases.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Kanjurmarg Delays:** Analysts expressed frustration over the repeated delays in Kanjurmarg land conversion. Management admitted the timeline is uncertain due to interdependencies between the regulator and legal framework but emphasized they are pushing for a resolution.
*   **Launch Pipeline:** Analysts questioned if 7 launches in Q4 are feasible. Management stated they are confident in their capacity to execute but noted that 1-2 projects might potentially slip.
*   **Balance Sheet Strength:** Analysts queried the ability to fund the heavy launch pipeline (including the Rs 1,000 crore+ commercial project). Management cited strong operating cash flows and a low debt-to-equity ratio (0.44x) as sufficient headroom.
*   **Vann Project:** When asked about the lack of sales at the Vann project, management stated they are not worried, as they are not compromising on price and expect sales to pick up as construction progresses.

### One-line takeaway
Ajmera Realty delivered strong YoY growth in revenue and PAT while reducing debt, and remains focused on executing a heavy launch pipeline in H2 despite persistent regulatory delays regarding its Kanjurmarg land parcel.
