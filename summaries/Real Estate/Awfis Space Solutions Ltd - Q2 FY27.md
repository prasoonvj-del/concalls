# Awfis Space Solutions Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent growth driven by enterprise/GCC demand, premiumization, and margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Normalized Revenue:** Rs 451 crore in Q2 (up 26% YoY); Rs 888 crore in H1 (up 30% YoY).
- **Reported Revenue:** Rs 439 crore in Q2 (up 20% YoY); Rs 864 crore in H1 (up 23% YoY).
- **Cash EBITDA:** Rs 49 crore in Q2 (up 45% YoY); Rs 93 crore in H1 (up 40% YoY).
- **Cash EBITDA Margin:** 10.8% in Q2 (up 140 bps YoY, up 70 bps QoQ); 10.5% in H1.
- **Reported EBITDA:** Rs 174 crore in Q2 (39.6% margin); Rs 336 crore in H1 (38.9% margin).
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Reported PBT nearly doubled to Rs 31 crore in Q2 and Rs 56 crore in H1; Operating PBT was Rs 13 crore in Q2 and Rs 25 crore in H1.
- **Co-working & Allied Services Revenue:** Rs 376 crore in Q2 (up 31% YoY); Rs 740 crore in H1 (up 34% YoY).
- **Transform Revenue:** Rs 75 crore in Q2 (up 8% YoY); Rs 149 crore in H1 (up 16% YoY). 3-year/3rd-party share at 93%.
- **Network & Capacity:** 244 centers across 18 cities; total supply (operational + fit-out) at 254 centers and ~1,74,000 seats across 8.7 million sq. ft. Gross additions of ~7,000 seats across 6 centers in Q2 (~12,000 seats in H1).
- **Occupancy:** 84.1% at mature centers (>12 months); 76.2% across the blended portfolio.
- **Client Metrics:** Weighted average client tenure at 38 months; lock-in tenure at 27 months (33 months for clients with >100 seats). 95% of base committed for at least a year.
- **Balance Sheet:** Net cash positive with net debt-to-equity at -0.12x; net debt negative of Rs 73 crore. H1 normalized operating cash flow at Rs 100 crore (1.08x cash EBITDA). Capex spend in H1 at Rs 113 crore. Fixed asset turnover at 1.5x.

### Highlights
- Enterprise and MNC clients account for 64% of the client base, SMEs 25%, and startups 10%.
- GCC portfolio contributes ~24% of rental revenue, serving 105+ unique GCC clients across 9 cities.
- Gold and Elite premium portfolio grew to 39 centers (29 Gold, 10 Elite) with ~33,000 seats (up 58% YoY). Incremental seats signed at ~35% higher pricing than flagship products.
- Transform order book and advance pipeline stands at Rs 220 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Reiterated full-year FY27 guidance: Gross seat additions of 22,000 to 25,000; co-working revenue growth of 23% to 25%; Transform growth of ~20%; total revenue past Rs 1,800 crore.
- Full-year cash EBITDA expected between Rs 190 crore and Rs 200 crore.
- Management expects Q4 cash EBITDA margins around 11.5% and exit March month margins touching ~12%. Management expects 12% to be the floor for margins entering FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's Grade A office stock across top 7 cities stands at 887 mn sq. ft.; 9M 2026 gross leasing reached 54.4 mn sq. ft. (up 7% YoY).
- Flexible workspace operators leased 12.6 mn sq. ft. in 9M (~25% of total leasing).
- GCCs leased ~8.7 mn sq. ft. in Q2 (41% of the market) and ~28 mn sq. ft. in 9M (42% of overall space). Two-thirds of GCCs plan to hold >10% of their portfolio in flex.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Incremental cost of borrowing remains at 8.5%; credit rating is A+ with a stable outlook.
- Profit share paid to landlords is part of EBITDA; other lease rentals are accounted under depreciation and interest per IndAS 116.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Deployed over Rs 460 crore of Capex in new centers since listing, entirely funded from internal accruals.
- H1 Capex was Rs 113 crore; expects to add another 2,500 to 3,300 seats in the premium portfolio in H2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt-to-equity at -0.12x (net cash negative of Rs 73 crore).
- Only equity taken since listing remains the Rs 128 crore raised at IPO.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged by management, apart from general risk disclaimers on forward-looking statements.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Adhidev (ICICI Securities) on FY28 operational seat expansion pipeline:** Management stated that gross additions in FY28 are expected to be 20% to 25% higher than FY27 if macros hold, remaining tilted towards premium.
2. **Adhidev (ICICI Securities) on margin trajectory / floor:** Management guided that Q4 cash EBITDA margin should reach ~11.5%, exit March at ~12%, and 12% will be the floor entering FY28.
3. **Rahul Kundnani (Nirmal Bang) on Transform business outlook:** Management highlighted that 93% of revenue is third-party, pipeline stands at over Rs 220 crore, and it acts as a cross-selling engine.
4. **Ishita Lodha (SVAN Investments) on ancillary income share and outlook:** Management noted ancillary income is ~16% of space revenue and expects it to grow at 25% to 30%+ over the next 12 months.
5. **Ayushi (Sai Capital) on the status of ~3,000 seats vacated by an enterprise client in May:** Management stated 57% to 58% of the capacity is already sold/committed, expecting 55% to 60%+ filled with revenues starting by December, with strong pipeline for the rest.

### One-line takeaway
Awfis reported robust Q2 FY27 performance with normalized revenue up 26% YoY to Rs 451 crore and cash EBITDA up 45% YoY to Rs 49 crore, driven by strong enterprise/GCC demand, premiumization, and margin expansion.
