# Runwal Enterprises Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Successful maiden listing, steady pre-sales growth, collection improvement, and a clear de-leveraging roadmap.

### Key numbers
- **Pre-sales:** Rs. 4,835 million (up 5% YoY from Rs. 4,600 million).
- **Collections:** Rs. 5,754 million (up from Rs. 4,993 million YoY).
- **Area Sold:** 0.35 million square feet.
- **Average Selling Price (ASP):** Rs. 14,200 per square foot.
- **Area Delivered:** ~1 million square feet.
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs. 3,400 million (compared to Rs. 4,462 million YoY).
- **Total Income:** Rs. 3,760 million.
- **EBITDA:** Slightly ahead of Rs. 1,000 million.
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs. 497 million.
- **Financing Cost:** Rs. 257 million.
- **External Debt:** Reduced to Rs. 1,900 crores post-IPO execution (total net debt down to Rs. 2,200 crores including related party loans).
- **Debt-to-Equity:** Improved from 3.1x to 1.5x post-IPO.

### Highlights
- Hosted maiden earnings conference call following the company's stock exchange listing.
- Total portfolio stands at ~88.4 million square feet of developable area (74.6 million sq ft residential, 6.37 million sq ft commercial), with ~20 million sq ft ongoing and ~56 million sq ft upcoming.
- Ranked 3rd in Mumbai for new launches/sales between CY23 and Q1 CY27, 1st in the eastern suburbs for sales, and 1st in Kalyan-Dombivali for new launches.
- IPO proceeds (~Rs. 325 crores) and conversion of HDFC Capital CCDs utilized for debt reduction, saving an estimated Rs. 50-60 crores annually in interest.
- Uncollected receivables from sold area stand at ~Rs. 36,000 million, providing strong future cash flow visibility.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management (Subodh Runwal) stated the company's endeavor is to grow pre-sales at a CAGR of ~20% to 25% year-on-year.
- Management expects blended project-level EBITDA margins to remain upwards of 25%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- MMR market remains deep and resilient with strong underlying demand and diverse customer segments.
- Quality inventory from established developers continues to attract strong demand despite supply concerns in certain micro-markets.
- Price hikes have been absorbed well due to market leadership, with projects commanding a 3% to 5% premium over competitors in micro-markets like Dombivli and Kanjurmarg.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Blended project-level EBITDA margins targeted at >25%, varying by product mix (affordable, mid-income, luxury).
- Annual interest savings of Rs. 50-60 crores expected following debt reduction measures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Pursuing an asset-light growth model via redevelopment, joint development (JD), and joint ventures (JV).
- Evaluating the monetization of ~18 acres of land in Dombivli (out of 250 acres) to data center operators (mandate given to JLL) to further reduce debt.
- Annuity asset projects (Nexus Runwal Garden City Mall and Runwal BKC commercial project) expected to be completed over the next 2.5 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- External debt reduced to Rs. 1,900 crores post-IPO; related party loans stand at Rs. 365 crores, bringing total debt down to Rs. 2,200 crores.
- Debt-to-equity ratio improved from 3.1x at the end of FY26 to 1.5x post-IPO.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Revenue recognition strictly follows project completion/occupation certificate (OC) basis, causing high quarter-on-quarter financial variability.
- Project approvals involve third-party dependencies that cannot be fully calibrated or guaranteed by management.
- Rising supply and potential oversupply noted in certain micro-markets.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Bhaskar Chaudhary (Individual Investor) on growth and margins:** Management stated an endeavor to grow pre-sales at 20% CAGR and maintain project EBITDA margins above 25%.
- **Rohan Singh (Pinpoint Capital) on debt reduction:** Management noted external debt is down to Rs. 1,900 crores and will be further reduced via internal cash flows and land monetization.
- **Abhinav Sinha (Jefferies) on data centers and commercial leases:** Management confirmed evaluating 18 acres in Dombivli for data centers, and expects mall rental income upwards of Rs. 100 crores in the first year post-stabilization (completion in 2.5 years).
- **Jia (Analyst) on approvals:** Management stated Bandra redevelopment and Marine Drive projects are awaiting approvals prior to launch.
- **Ramesh Jain (Consultancy Services) on IPO size reduction:** Management explained that SEBI granted a one-time 50% reduction concession due to macro challenges (wars), and proceeding with a primary dilution of 10-11% was deemed best to establish a listed compliance track record.

### One-line takeaway
Runwal Enterprises reported steady Q1 FY27 pre-sales and utilized IPO proceeds to significantly de-lever its balance sheet, targeting a 20%+ pre-sales CAGR and asset-light expansion within the MMR.
