# Max Estates Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-21 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by strong pre-sales growth (5x YoY to ~Rs. 1,100 crores), 100% occupancy across operating commercial assets, and a growing residential launch pipeline.

### Key numbers
- **Pre-sales:** ~Rs. 1,100 crores (up 5x YoY), driven by full sell-out of Phase-1 of The Terraces at Estate 361 Gurgaon (~Rs. 500 crores) and sustenance sales (~Rs. 600 crores).
- **Collections:** ~Rs. 575 crores (20%-25% of sales value).
- **Revenue Potential (Embedded Value):** Total launched residential and mixed-use portfolio stands at INR 17,500 crores; INR 13,500 crores already sold and contracted (INR 3,500 crores collected, INR 10,000 crores to be collected). Estimated embedded PBT of Rs. 4,500-Rs. 5,500 crores.
- **Financial Results (Q1 FY27):** Consolidated revenue of Rs. 52 crores; EBITDA of Rs. 8 crores; Profit Before Tax (PBT) of Rs. 11 crores; PAT of Rs. 8 crores.
- **Commercial Lease Rentals:** Up 5% YoY to Rs. 40 crores (Max Towers, Max House, Max Square).
- **Max Asset Services Revenue:** Rs. 15 crores in Q1 FY27 (up 16% YoY).
- **Net Debt & Cash:** Net debt of Rs. 234 crores as of June 2026; gross debt of Rs. 1,960 crores (including lease rental discounting borrowings of Rs. 934 crores); cash and cash equivalents of Rs. 1,727 crores.
- **Max One Pricing:** Highest sale value in Noida at Rs. 37,000 per square foot (excluding GST).

### Highlights
- Phase-1 of The Terraces at Estate 361 (Dwarka Expressway, Gurgaon) fully sold out in its launch quarter (GDV of Rs. 500 crores).
- Estate 105 Noida development mix revised to a residential portfolio, enhancing project GDV to Rs. 6,000 crores, with Phase-2 planned for launch in FY27.
- Max One (Delhi One revival project) underway across 2.5 million sq. ft. with a total GDV of ~INR 3,200 crores and Rs. 145 crores in annuity income.
- Assigned a first-time issuer rating of A+ with a stable outlook by ICRA on a consolidated basis (covering the company and 14 subsidiaries), backed by committed receivables of ~Rs. 9,500 crores and a cash-flow adequacy ratio of ~105%.
- All operating commercial assets (Max Towers, Max House, Max Square) maintained 100% occupancy. Max Towers signed a lease at Rs. 156 per sq. ft. per month (over 50% mark-to-market upside).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Collections & Deployment (FY27):** Management expects full-year collections in the range of Rs. 2,500-Rs. 2,700 crores. Project deployment is guided at Rs. 1,500-Rs. 1,800 crores, leaving OCF of Rs. 750-Rs. 1,000 crores for business development (BD).
- **Launches:** Planned launches of Rs. 5,000-Rs. 5,500 crores in the second half of FY27, alongside Rs. 3,000-Rs. 4,000 crores of available old inventory.
- **Annuity Income:** Target of ~Rs. 700 crores in annual rental income at peak occupancy from current under-construction commercial assets.
- **Guidance Policy:** Management explicitly stated they are not giving pre-sales volume guidance for FY27 due to a volatile microeconomic environment.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Delhi NCR residential market saw resilient Q2 2026 launches (~8,800 units), led by Gurgaon (70% share) and Noida/Greater Noida. While weighted average prices held stable QoQ, sales volumes moderated due to global uncertainty, tightening liquidity, and cautious consumer sentiment.
- Delhi NCR commercial office leasing reached 4.1 million sq. ft. in Q2 2026, driven by GCCs, IT flexible workspaces, and professional services.
- Management noted an ongoing consolidation wave favoring organized, trusted, listed developers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Advertising and marketing expenses appeared elevated YoY due to Ind AS 115 accounting principles (residential sales are recognized in P&L only upon possession, whereas marketing costs are expensed immediately).
- Antara acts as a development manager for senior living components, charging a fee of ~9.5% on the top-line of the specific senior living portion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Residential launch pipeline stands at ~Rs. 16,100 crores (Rs. 4,000 crores launched/available this year; ~Rs. 12,000 crores planned through FY27). The company targets adding 2 million sq. ft. of residential development annually.
- Max Square 2 (1 million sq. ft.) on track for OC by Q2 FY28, expected to add Rs. 125 crores to annuity; pre-leased 90,000 sq. ft. at a 25% premium.
- Max District (Gurgaon, 1.6 million sq. ft.) expected to receive OC across Q3 FY28 and Q3 FY29, adding Rs. 200 crores to annuity; pre-leased 200,000 sq. ft. at a 35% premium.
- Remaining commercial capex of Rs. 1,500-Rs. 1,800 crores is fully tied up via equity partner contributions and sanctioned debt from SBI, ICICI, etc.
- Target to add 1 million sq. ft. of new business development annually towards the commercial portfolio; core geographic focus remains on NCR and the larger NCR region.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Construction finance is drawn for commercial assets under construction (Max Square 2, Max District, Max One) and converts to Lease Rental Discounting (LRD) post-commissioning.
- Equity partner contributions are set at 40%, with the remainder funded via debt.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global uncertainty, tightening liquidity conditions, and cautious consumer sentiment leading to moderation in broader residential sales volumes across the sector.
- Management highlighted a volatile microeconomic environment as the reason for withholding forward-looking sales volume guidance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Parth Sodha (Trinetra Asset Managers) on employee/marketing cost run rate:** Management explained that Ind AS 115 defers residential revenue recognition until possession while immediately expensing marketing costs, making launch quarters look elevated.
2. **Aman (Unnamed Entity) on Antara's business model and fee structure:** Management clarified that Antara acts as an arm's-length development manager, charging ~9.5% of the top-line specifically on the senior living portion (~15-20% of project GDV), while bringing senior living expertise that commands a 7%-10% price premium.
3. **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital) on FY27 collections and OCF comfort:** Management confirmed comfort with full-year collections of Rs. 2,500-Rs. 2,700 crores, projecting an OCF of Rs. 750-Rs. 1,000 crores after project deployment of Rs. 1,500-Rs. 1,800 crores.
4. **Akash Gupta (Unnamed Entity) on the shift in guidance strategy:** Management stated that providing sales guidance in a volatile microeconomic environment is not ideal for long-term health, choosing instead to focus on the quality of sales.
5. **Jay Kant Beria (Unnamed Entity) on cash flow deployment and rising net debt:** Management explained that net debt and cash balances rose concurrently due to construction finance drawdowns for commercial assets under construction (Max Square 2, Max District, Max One) alongside land revenue shares to landowners.
6. **Tushar Agarwal (Unnamed Entity) on commercial pre-leasing premiums and yields:** Management clarified that pre-leasing premiums are based on today’s prevailing market rates, and Greenfield projects are underwritten at 12%-14% yields, translating to cap rates of 6.5%-7% upon delivery and leasing.

### One-line takeaway
Max Estates delivered a strong Q1 FY27 with Rs. 1,100 crores in pre-sales (up 5x YoY), 100% occupancy across operating commercial assets, and robust cash collection visibility, underpinning confidence in its Rs. 16,100 crores residential and growing commercial pipeline.
