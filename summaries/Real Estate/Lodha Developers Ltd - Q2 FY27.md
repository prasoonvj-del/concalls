# Lodha Developers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-05 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strongest H1 in company history with record revenue, PAT, and pre-sales, alongside confident guidance.

### Key numbers
*   **PAT:** Rs 2,386 crore (up 63% YoY).
*   **Revenue:** Rs 9,460 crore (up 30% YoY).
*   **Pre-sales:** Rs 96 billion (up 7% YoY).
*   **Net Debt:** Rs 52 billion (~0.2x equity).
*   **H1 Collections:** Rs 76 billion (up 19% YoY).
*   **Q2 Collections:** Rs 35 billion.
*   **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 27 billion.

### Highlights
*   H1 pre-sales achieved 40.3% of the annual target.
*   Bangalore Q2 pre-sales exceeded Rs 1,800 crore; company expects to cross Rs 3,000 crore in pre-sales from Bangalore in FY27.
*   Launched first project in Gurugram NCR.
*   Mulund-Airoli-Palava Freeway is "clinically complete" and expected to open in the coming weeks.
*   Data center: Power approval increased to 5 GW; definitive documents for the first Build-to-Suit (BTS) box expected this fiscal year.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects to beat the 20% PAT growth guidance (which would have been >Rs 4,000 crore).
*   Full-year pre-sales target maintained at Rs 24,000 crore.
*   H2 pre-sales mix: Rs 6,000-7,000 crore in Q3 and Q4 each, plus a Rs 1,500 crore data center land transaction in Q4.
*   Full-year operating cash flow expected around Rs 85 billion.
*   DevCo expected to be net debt-free in 2-3 years.
*   Management expects 1 data center land transaction every 6 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management sees no adverse impact from interest rate hikes on their segments (INR 1-5 crore and >INR 5 crore).
*   Middle East crisis impact limited to NRI demand (4-5% of sales).
*   Strong demand for Grade A/A+ developers; market is consolidating.
*   Bangalore market: Premium housing (>Rs 2 crore) contributes 50% of demand.
*   Management views AI as a positive for Indian jobs and housing demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Construction cost inflation is managed; company uses a 10% contingency for construction and 3% for revenue.
*   YTD price growth is ~4%; cost growth is ~4%.
*   Management expects 100-200 bps margin improvement annually through operational leverage and supply chain management.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Bangalore: 6 operating projects expected by the end of FY27.
*   Data Center: 5 GW capacity (up from 3 GW).
*   Construction spend expected to pick up in H2.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net debt: Rs 52 billion (~0.2x equity).
*   DevCo to be net debt-free in 2-3 years.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Interest rate hikes (though management downplayed impact on their specific segments).
*   Potential construction disruptions due to air quality/pollution measures (though management hopes for adherence).
*   Geopolitical/Middle East crisis (limited impact).

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Karan Khanna (Ambit):** Asked about launch pipeline reduction vs. sales guidance. Management clarified that launch timing shifts do not impact annual KPIs.
*   **Abhinav Sinha:** Asked about data center power/transmission. Management confirmed 5 GW is approved and requires no major network upgrades.
*   **Biplab Debbarma:** Asked about BTS data center deal size. Management confirmed it is a "multi-hundred megawatt" transaction.
*   **Gaurav Khandelwal:** Asked about data center land pricing. Management noted the next transaction is expected in the "high 50s" (crores per acre).
*   **Akash Gupta:** Asked about data center sustainability/water. Management emphasized the use of recycled municipal wastewater.

### One-line takeaway
Lodha delivered record H1 performance and remains confident in meeting annual pre-sales and profitability targets, supported by strong demand for premium housing and strategic data center expansion.
