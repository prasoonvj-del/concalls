# Max Estates Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong sustenance pre-sales of ~Rs. 2,100 crores with no new project launches, 100% occupancy in operating commercial assets, and healthy cash collections.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained construction spend guidance of ~Rs. 1,500 crores for FY27. No sales volume guidance was given, consistent with last quarter.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from relying on new launches (like The Terraces in Q1) to highlighting strong sustenance sales (~Rs. 2,100 crores from Estate 361, Max One, and Estate 105) with zero new launches in Q2.
- New topics or risks: Announced two new business development transactions (MoU for 9.7 acres in Ghaziabad with GDV of Rs. 2,500-Rs. 3,000 crores, and MoU for 84.7 acres in West Delhi). Old risks regarding global uncertainty and volume moderation persisted.
- Last quarter's promises: Sector 59 launch is on track for Q3 FY27 as guided.

### Key numbers
- **Pre-sales:** ~Rs. 2,100 crores across 274 units (all sustenance sales). H1 FY27 pre-sales at ~Rs. 3,200 crores across 521 units.
- **Collections:** Rs. 560 crores in Q2; ~Rs. 1,100 crores in H1 FY27.
- **Revenue & Financials (Q2 FY27):** Consolidated revenue of Rs. 53 crores (up 8% YoY); Profit Before Tax (PBT) of Rs. 7.2 crores; Profit After Tax (PAT) of Rs. 4.7 crores. H1 FY27 consolidated revenue of Rs. 375 crores, EBITDA of Rs. 13.5 crores.
- **Contracted Revenue / Embedded Value:** ~Rs. 15,700 crores (83% of Rs. 18,700 crores launched) sold, with estimated embedded PBT of Rs. 4,800 crores to Rs. 5,700 crores.
- **Commercial Lease Rentals:** ~Rs. 40 crores in Q2 FY27, broadly stable YoY across 1.3 million sq. ft. leased (100% occupancy).
- **Max Asset Services Revenue:** Rs. 17 crores in Q2 FY27 (up 28% YoY).
- **Debt & Cash:** External debt of Rs. 2,100 crores (including Rs. 950 crores lease rental discounting); cash and cash equivalents including other balances at Rs. 1,900 crores; net debt at ~Rs. 200 crores.

### Highlights
- Q2 pre-sales driven entirely by three ongoing projects: Estate 361, Max One, and Estate 105. Estate 128 and Estate 360 are fully sold at GDVs of Rs. 2,700 crores and Rs. 4,800 crores respectively.
- Average realization reached Rs. 24,700 per sq. ft. in Q2 FY27 vs ~Rs. 18,400 in FY24.
- Commercial pre-leasing progress: Max Square 2 pre-leasing increased to 3.1 lakh sq. ft. (~one-third of leasable area) at a ~25% premium; Max District (Gurgaon) pre-leased ~2 lakh sq. ft. at a >35% premium.
- Business development additions: Signed MoU for 9.7 acres in Ghaziabad (GDV Rs. 2,500-Rs. 3,000 crores, 1.5 million sq. ft.) and MoU for 84.7 acres in West Delhi. Total GDV pipeline stands at close to Rs. 29,000 crores.
- Execution milestones: Poured ~60,000 cubic meters of concrete in Q2 (cumulative 3 lakh cubic meters) and 7,000 metric tonnes of steel (cumulative 42,000 tonnes). Secured dual 5-star GRESB rating.

### Guidance & outlook
- Construction spend expected to be over Rs. 1,500 crores in FY27.
- Sector 59 (Golf Course Extension Road, Gurgaon) with a GDV of ~Rs. 4,000 crores and 1.3 million sq. ft. development potential is planned for launch in Q3 FY27.
- Potential to generate rental income of ~Rs. 700 crores on 100% basis on stabilization of commercial assets over next few years.
- Target to add ~2 million sq. ft. of residential development annually.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Delhi NCR residential market remained in a consolidation phase; July-September sales at ~14,000 homes (flat YoY). Average prices in NCR rose ~12%, with over one-third of new supply priced above Rs. 3 crores.
- Office leasing in NCR recorded gross leasing of ~3.8 million sq. ft. in Q3 (10 million sq. ft. in first 9 months); net absorption at ~2 million sq. ft. outpaced new supply (~1 million sq. ft.), reducing vacancy by ~200 bps YoY to ~17% with rentals rising 6% to 10%. Gurgaon accounted for 63% of leasing and Noida 27%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher advertising and marketing expenses of close to Rs. 20 crores in Q2 FY27 (vs Rs. 12 crores YoY) due to heightened sales activity across Estate 361, Max One, and Estate 105. Ind AS accounting expenses marketing costs immediately while deferring residential revenue until handover.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- In-house execution model using multiple contractors trade-wise rather than turnkey contractors.
- West Delhi land parcel approval and planning process estimated to take 18 to 24 months; minimal costs incurred currently except security.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Collections structured to yield 20% to 25% of sales value annually, funding residential construction without incremental debt on those assets.
- Net debt to equity remains well below the threshold of 0.5x.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Government and bureaucratic processes can impact timelines for regulatory approvals and auction finalizations (e.g., Noida auction assets).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital) on Sector 59 pricing and NCR demand:** Management stated demand for Max Estates remains robust despite broader market consolidation; pricing for Sector 59 will not be commented on ahead of final approvals.
2. **Karan Khanna (Ambit Capital) on Ghaziabad and Noida timelines:** Management noted the Ghaziabad project is a first calibrated step in the market, while Noida auction timelines depend on bureaucratic processes.
3. **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital) on Sector 59 monetization strategy and business development:** Management noted it is early to comment on monetization strategy for Sector 59 and reiterated the target to add 2-3 million sq. ft. annually.
4. **Ritwik Sheth (One Up Financial) on West Delhi land costs and timelines:** Management reiterated an 18-24 month timeline for approvals, with no significant costs incurred currently beyond land security.
5. **Vikas (Individual Investor) on Max One revenue recognition:** Management clarified commercial tower delivery is expected in FY28 and residential in FY30.

### One-line takeaway
Max Estates reported a strong Q2 FY27 with Rs. 2,100 crores in sustenance pre-sales, robust cash collections, and aggressive land additions expanding its GDV pipeline to nearly Rs. 29,000 crores.
