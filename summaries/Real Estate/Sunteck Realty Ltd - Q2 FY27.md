# Sunteck Realty Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 and H1 FY 2027
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered growth across presales, collections, cash flows, revenue, and profits, sustaining a 25% CAGR across major parameters over the last 5 years.

### Key numbers
- Presales: Rs 824 crore (Q2 FY27), Rs 1,611 crore (H1 FY27)
- Collections: Rs 371 crore (Q2 FY27), Rs 780 crore (H1 FY27)
- Revenue: Rs 3.2 billion (up 27% YoY in Q2 FY27), Rs 5.1 billion (up 16% YoY in H1 FY27)
- EBITDA: Up 13% YoY in Q2 FY27; Rs 1.6 billion (up 23% YoY in H1 FY27)
- Embedded EBITDA margin: Expanded 160 bps YoY to 30% in H1 FY27
- PAT: Up 17% YoY to Rs 1 billion (up 20% YoY in H1 FY27)
- Net operating cash flow surplus: Rs 3.5 billion in H1 FY27 (up 27% YoY)
- Net debt / Net cash: Net debt to equity at 0.09
- Annualized annuity income: Rs 86 crore
- Total GDV: Rs 469 billion (Rs 260 billion launched or to be launched)
- Launch pipeline for FY27 (excluding Dubai): Rs 7,000 crore
- Borivali West land addition: 4.5 acres acquired (total landholding 11.5 acres, estimated GDV Rs 80 billion)

### Highlights
- Acquired a new 4.5-acre land parcel at Borivali West via outright purchase, taking total landholding to 11.5 acres with Rs 80 billion GDV.
- Segment mix of Q2 presales: Uber luxury 25%, premium luxury 51%, aspirational luxury 25%.
- Moved the Dubai project from "to be launched" to "upcoming for-launch GDV" due to lack of launch visibility in the next 1-2 quarters; management remains bullish on its economics.
- GRESB Green 5-star rating retained for the second consecutive year (score improved to 98.8 in 2026 from 98.6 in 2025).
- 4th Avenue at Sunteck City achieved EDGE Advanced certification with over 50% savings in energy, water, and embodied carbon.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains expectation of 25% YoY growth in full-year FY27 presales, backed by a Rs 7,000 crore launch pipeline in H2.
- Reported margins are expected to converge towards the embedded EBITDA margin of presales (30% to 35% project-level minimum filter).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Uptick observed in the aspirational luxury segment, particularly in Naigaon and Kalyan projects.
- Inventory at BKC is diminishing, with larger uber luxury run expected from Nepean Sea Road post-launch.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Minimum project-level EBITDA margin filter maintained at 30% to 35%.
- Embedded EBITDA margin on H1 FY27 presales expanded by 160 bps YoY to 30%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Spent INR 3.9 billion in business development during H1 FY27 (following INR 800 crores in FY26).
- Key H2 launches planned: Sunteck Belvedere Park (Mira Road 2), Andheri Western Express Highway residential project, Altavia Tower 2 (ODC), a Borivali West phase, Sunteck Beach Residences (SBR) new tower, Sunteck World new towers, and Sunteck Crescent Park towers.
- Mira Road 2 GDV expected at Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,400 crore; Andheri West GDV expected at Rs 1,250 crore to Rs 1,500 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands low at 0.09.
- H1 net operating cash flow surplus reached Rs 3.5 billion (63% of FY26 record full-year surplus; 45% of collections). Trailing 12-month surplus at Rs 6.3 billion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Dubai project launch delayed by 1 to 2 quarters due to lack of near-term visibility, prompting a shift to upcoming for-launch status.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Harsh Pathak (Axis Capital)** asked about GDV and details of H2 launches. Management stated Mira Road 2 GDV is Rs 1,200-1,400 crore and Andheri West is Rs 1,250-1,500 crore, alongside ODC Altavia Tower 2, Borivali West, SBR, Sunteck World, and Crescent Park.
- **Harsh Pathak (Axis Capital)** inquired about full-year presales expectations. Management reiterated confidence in achieving 25% YoY growth for FY27.
- **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital)** asked if the new Borivali land is contiguous and about commercial ODC progress. Management confirmed the 4.5 acres adjoins the existing 7 acres to form an 11.5-acre single project, and commercial construction at ODC has commenced.
- **Abhinav Sinha (Jefferies)** asked about Nepean Sea Road approvals and RERA launch timeline. Management stated approvals are at an advanced stage, demolition of second building is ongoing, and a RERA launch is targeted before the end of FY27.
- **Vasudev (Nuvama Wealth Management)** asked about FY27 revenue recognition projects. Management listed Sunteck One World (Naigaon), ODC Avenue 1, 4th Avenue, Sunteck Pinnacle commercial, and ready inventory at BKC.

### One-line takeaway
Sunteck Realty posted a strong H1 FY27 with 27% revenue growth and robust cash flows, supported by a Rs 7,000 crore H2 launch pipeline and continued aggressive business development.
