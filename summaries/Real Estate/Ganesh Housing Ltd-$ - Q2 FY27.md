# Ganesh Housing Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reaffirmed FY27 guidance, reported strong leasing traction at Million Minds, and highlighted low debt amidst a rising interest rate cycle.

- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 98 crore
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 80.7% (recovered from 39.3% in Q1 FY27)
- **Q2 FY27 PBT:** INR 89 crore
- **Q2 FY27 PAT:** INR 67 crore
- **Q2 FY27 PAT Margin:** 55.1%
- **Q2 FY27 EPS:** INR 8.02
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 402 crore
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 208 crore
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 109 crore
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** INR 1,000 crore to INR 1,200 crore (reaffirmed)
- **FY27 PAT Guidance:** INR 300 crore to INR 325 crore (reaffirmed)
- **Malabar Retreat Completion:** 87% complete
- **Malabar Retreat Bookings:** ~50% by units (76 units sold; carpet area 53%)
- **Malabar Retreat Collections:** INR 132 crore
- **Malabar Retreat Expected Receivables:** INR 305 crore
- **Million Minds Tower 1 Leased Area:** 68.4% (4.21 lakh sq. ft. out of 6.16 lakh sq. ft. leasable carpet area) across 15 deals
- **Million Minds Tower 1 Contracted Rent Roll:** INR 55.7 crore
- **Million Minds Tower 1 Average Rental:** INR 110.20 per sq. ft. per month (new deals this quarter at INR 122.4 per sq. ft.)

### Highlights
- Q1 FY27 margins were impacted by inherited high-cost land from the 2020 amalgamation and a one-time tax charge on One 91 Thaltej land sale; Q2 represents normal margins.
- Million Minds Tower 1 added new marquee tenants this quarter including Airtel, Conga Software, Flagler Health, and iBase-t. Co-working concentration fell from 36% to 28.7%.
- Holding back 1.95 lakh sq. ft. across 4 contiguous upper floors of Million Minds Tower 1 for large single-occupier transactions.
- Million Minds Tower 2 (6.6 lakh sq. ft. leasable carpet on 14.5 lakh sq. ft. construction) plans submitted for approval; Bhumi Pujan targeted for Dev Diwali (November 24, 2026).
- Malabar Retreat on course for soft possession on January 15, 2027, and BU expected in mid-February 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reaffirmed FY27 guidance of INR 1,000 crore to INR 1,200 crore revenue and INR 300 crore to INR 325 crore PAT.
- Expects 90% of Million Minds Tower 1 to be leased out by March 2027.
- FY28 guidance will be provided in Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28.
- Monetization plans for Godhavi land expected to be announced in about 6 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Knight Frank data shows H1 CY26 housing sales across 8 major cities flat YoY (86,800 units), while ANAROCK data shows sales up 3% (over 1 lakh units) with prices up ~7%. Launches exceeded sales for 16 consecutive quarters; unsold inventory up 12%.
- Homes above INR 1 crore comprise 55% of sales; affordable segment continues to shrink.
- Office leasing steady at 66.7 million sq. ft. in first 9 months of CY26 (GCCs took 41%, flexible operators 24%).
- Ahmedabad housing sales up 2% YoY to 14,300 units in 9M CY26; weighted average price up 3.7% to INR 4,837 per sq. ft. Ahmedabad EMI-to-income ratio at 18%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- RBI raised repo rate by 25 bps to 5.5% on October 7, 2026 (calibrated tightening stance).
- Average rental for Million Minds Tower 1 rose from ~INR 90 in 2025 to ~INR 115 in 2026, and above INR 122 this quarter.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Million Minds Tower 2 targets an 18-to-24-month build, with towers ready for fit-outs between May and November 2028 and pre-leasing opening from late 2027.
- Million Minds Residential Phase 1 launch expected in Q4 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is "almost debt free" with existing debt restricted to lease-rental discounting.
- Management stated they will take on limited leverage if necessary to fund growth without risking balance sheet stability.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Rising interest rates (RBI rate hike) will weigh on stretched home buyers and leveraged developers.
- High supply in Ahmedabad office market (3.4 million sq. ft. completions) with rental growth at 3% (lowest across 8 major markets).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sahil (Ventura Securities) on H2 revenue visibility:** Management clarified that the bulk of the INR 600 crore to INR 800 crore required H2 revenue will come from Malabar Retreat completions, ongoing project sales, and land sales in Q3 and Q4.
2. **Nayan (Investo Pro) on Godhavi monetization timeline:** Management stated that concrete monetization plans will take another 6 months to finalize due to delayed government infrastructure announcements.
3. **Rishabh Bora (AB Advisors) on funding upcoming capex and debt comfort:** Management reiterated that residential projects are well-funded via customer bookings, land is fully paid for, and lease rentals and land monetization will generate sufficient cash flows without uncomfortable leverage.
4. **Jimeet Shah (Monarch AIF) on leasing strategy for upper floors amid high Ahmedabad office supply:** Management defended holding back the top 4 contiguous floors for large single occupiers rather than leasing piecemeal.
5. **Janak Shah (Wealth Finvisor) on Malabar Retreat construction status versus January 2027 handover:** Management stated that final finishing takes significantly less time and the remaining 13% construction will easily be completed within the 2-to-3-month window.

### One-line takeaway
Ganesh Housing reported a steady Q2 with normal margins, reaffirmed its FY27 revenue and PAT guidance, and drove strong enterprise leasing momentum at its Million Minds commercial campus.
