# Mahindra Lifespace Developers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted strong H1 pre-sales growth, a robust balance sheet with net cash, and a healthy GDV pipeline despite macro-driven market moderation.

### Key numbers
*   **Residential Pre-sales (H1):** Rs 1,988 crore (66% YoY growth).
*   **Consolidated Residential & IC Sales/Revenue (H1):** Rs 2,101 crore.
*   **PAT (H1):** Rs 102 crore (vs Rs 99 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **GDV Addition (H1):** Rs 7,100 crore.
*   **Operating Cash Flow (H1):** Rs 341 crore.
*   **Cash Balance:** Rs 1,036 crore.
*   **Cost of Debt:** 7.55%.

### Highlights
*   **Residential:** Successful launches included Rainforest (Bhandup), BeaconHill, Citadel Phase 3, and Lakewoods F&G. RERA received for Mahalunge Phase 1 (Rivenza).
*   **Sustenance:** Sustenance sales contributed 40% of total sales; management aims to increase this to reduce reliance on new launches.
*   **IC Business:** Revenue was muted due to the sale of legacy Outside Boundary Lands (OBL) which have lower realization than core industrial park land.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** Realized cost savings from projects where OCs were received >6 months ago (Tathawade, Nostalgia, Eden, Luminare, Vicino).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Pre-sales:** Management targets Rs 4,500–5,000 crore for FY27.
*   **BD Pipeline:** Management aims for Rs 10,000–20,000 crore in GDV additions for the year.
*   **Launches:** Management expects to launch remaining pipeline projects (Sai Baba, Navrat, Westera) in H2 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Management noted a slight slowdown in sales velocity due to macro factors (war, job market, financial markets/FII outflows).
*   Management believes corporate/branded developers are gaining market share from smaller players.
*   Strategy focuses on pricing discipline rather than artificially inflating velocity through discounts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Management noted a slight increase in costs across commodities following the Iran-US war.
*   Borrowings remain steady at Rs 600 crore.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Land Acquisition:** Spent Rs 332 crore in H1, plus Rs 400 crore for K2 in Q3.
*   **Origins Pune:** Land aggregation ongoing; management expects 1–2 years to achieve full contiguity.
*   **Origins Ahmedabad:** Special power and water approvals are in progress.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Net Debt to Equity:** Negative (-0.28).
*   **Net Worth:** ~Rs 3,600 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Land price expectations are currently high and "out of sync" with market economics.
*   Macro headwinds (war, job market, FII outflows) are impacting customer sentiment and walk-ins.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Synergies with Mahindra Holidays:** Management is exploring functional synergies (procurement, contractors) and business synergies, specifically in senior living, service villas, and branded residences.
*   **Industrial Revenue Drop:** Management clarified that the 50% revenue decline in IC was due to the composition of sales (34 acres of OBL land with lower realization) rather than a drop in core industrial land pricing.
*   **Redevelopment Timelines:** Management is working to expedite the Chembur project (originally slated for FY29) to FY28.
*   **Sustenance Strategy:** Management emphasized that focusing on sustenance sales (40% contribution) is a deliberate strategy to ensure disciplined execution and profitability.

### One-line takeaway
Mahindra Lifespaces is prioritizing disciplined, profitable growth and sustenance sales over aggressive volume chasing, maintaining a strong net-cash balance sheet despite macro-induced market moderation.
