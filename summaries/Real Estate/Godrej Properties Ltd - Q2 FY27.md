# Godrej Properties Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Real Estate*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance in sustenance sales, collections, and cash flows despite back-ended deliveries impacting reported profits.

### Key numbers
- **Booking Value:** Rs 8,008 crore in Q2 FY27 (down 6% YoY, down 7% QoQ); H1 FY27 booking value grew 7% YoY to Rs 16,669 crore.
- **Sustenance Sales:** Rs 5,054 crore in Q2 FY27, growing 83% YoY.
- **Total Income:** Rs 2,575 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 38% YoY); H1 FY27 total income grew 13% YoY to Rs 3,912 crore.
- **EBITDA:** Rs 171 crore in Q2 FY27 (down 72% YoY); H1 FY27 EBITDA declined 53% YoY to Rs 717 crore.
- **Net Profit (PAT):** Rs 154 crore in Q2 FY27 (down 62% YoY); H1 FY27 net profit declined 50% YoY to Rs 504 crore.
- **Collections:** Rs 5,318 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 31% YoY); H1 FY27 collections grew 25% YoY to Rs 9,666 crore.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 1,584 crore in Q2 FY27 (up 33% YoY and nearly 300% QoQ); H1 FY27 operating cash flow stood at Rs 1,983 crore.
- **Deliveries:** Delivered 2.5 million square feet in Q2 FY27.

### Highlights
- Achieved booking value from the sale of 3,658 units with a total area of 6.2 million square feet in Q2 FY27.
- Geographic breakdown of Q2 bookings: NCR (~Rs 2,000 crore), Mumbai (~Rs 1,900 crore), Bengaluru (<Rs 1,600 crore), Pune (~Rs 1,000 crore), Hyderabad (~Rs 700 crore), and other cities (~Rs 800 crore).
- Attributable share of sales was 85% in Q2 FY27 and 90% in H1 FY27.
- Sustenance sales were driven by Godrej Trilogy (Mumbai), Godrej Vanantara (Bengaluru), and Godrej Samaris (Gurugram), each contributing over Rs 500 crore. Godrej Brooklyn Avenue (Hyderabad) also contributed over Rs 500 crore.
- First half achieved 43% of annual bookings guidance despite completing only 1/3 of the expected annual launch pipeline.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains confident of surpassing full-year guidance across all key operating metrics.
- Expects H2 FY27 to be the best-ever period for both launches and sales, with an estimated launch pipeline of Rs 32,000 crore in H2.
- Guidance for H2 FY27 sales is estimated between Rs 22,500 crore and Rs 25,000 crore.
- Target for H2 FY27 collections is Rs 15,000 crore, with expected operating cash flow of Rs 7,000 crore.
- On track to deliver 13.5 million square feet of project completions in FY27.
- Expects H2 FY27 (specifically Q4) and FY28 to be free cash flow (FCF) positive, alongside targeting a 20% reported ROE in FY28 under project completion accounting.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Residential market underlying demand remains strong and resilient across major geographies.
- Price increases across portfolios remain muted/focused on quality end-user demand: North market prices saw <1% increases, South and Mumbai saw ~2% jumps.
- Specific projects with strong product differentiation (e.g., Godrej Crown Residences in Noida) achieved significant pricing power and near-tripled cluster profit targets.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Q2 profitability was impacted by the back-ended nature of project deliveries scheduled for the year.
- Reported margins in Q2 were distorted due to accounting adjustments from project exits/consolidation (such as JV partner exit in Mahalunge and settlement in Orris).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added 1 new project in Q2 FY27 with saleable area of ~1.1 million square feet and expected booking value of ~Rs 6,000 crore.
- H1 FY27 cumulative additions reached 4 projects with ~9 million square feet of saleable area and Rs 15,500 crore booking value (~78% of full-year business development guidance).
- Construction and related cash outflows increased 31% YoY in Q2 FY27 to Rs 1,292 crore and grew 42% YoY in H1 FY27.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flows turned substantially strong with Q2 OCF of Rs 1,584 crore and H1 OCF reaching Rs 1,983 crore.
- Company plans to exit 5 to 6 remaining legacy JV projects over time to shift focus towards a 100% owned portfolio.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential construction halts in NCR during winter due to seasonal NGT/GRAP restrictions.
- Delays in securing project launch approvals could impact execution timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Puneet (HSBC):** *What is the attributable share of sales in Q2 and H1?*  
  **Management:** Attributable share was 85% for Q2 FY27 and 90% for H1 FY27.
- **Abhinav Sinha (Jefferies):** *Are we on track to be free cash flow positive in H2 and FY28 despite business development spends?*  
  **Management:** BD spending remains opportunistic based on strict return thresholds (20% IRR, 15% booking-to-net profit margin). There is a high probability of turning FCF positive in Q4 FY27, and FY28 FCF positive status is almost certain based on cash flow projections.
- **Gaurav Khandelwal (JPMorgan):** *What drove the decline in reported EBITDA margins during Q2?*  
  **Management:** Line-by-line consolidation of Mahalunge (after buying out JV partner) combined with past DM fee recognitions resulted in recognized sales matching cost of sales. Additionally, consolidation losses from the Orris project exit/settlement impacted Q2 EBITDA.
- **Pritesh Sheth (Axis Capital):** *What is the strategy on new market entries, specifically group housing in Chennai?*  
  **Management:** Chennai is likely the next market to enter for group housing eventually, but focus over the next couple of years remains on deepening penetration in existing top 5 markets (Mumbai, NCR, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad) and expanding into untapped micro-markets.
- **Artha (Investor):** *Why is there a delay between receiving OC and physical delivery to customers in projects like Godrej Splendour?*  
  **Management:** Management clarified that they do not believe in creating gaps between OC and actual handover, committing to look into the specific project case to resolve any delays.

### One-line takeaway
Godrej Properties delivered strong H1 operational metrics with robust sustenance sales and collections, positioning the company for a record-breaking H2 launch pipeline and turning cash-flow positive.
