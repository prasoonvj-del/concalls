# Bajaj Auto Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-02 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Fifth consecutive record quarter marked by record quarterly volumes, revenue, EBITDA, PAT, and free cash flow despite sustained raw material inflation and supply constraints.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained export target run-rate of ~750,000 units/quarter (240k-260k/month); raised Chetak monthly capacity target to 80,000 by Q4 FY27 (vs 60,000/month in Q1); updated near-term enterprise plant capacity expansion target to 7,75,000 units/month over 6-9 months (vs 9 million/year medium-term target in Q1).
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains confident; festive period (Sept-Dec) expected to deliver double-digit growth, though moderating from Apr-Aug’s 18% YoY growth due to high base effect. K-shaped demand split continues to favor premium 150cc+ motorcycles and EV scooters over 100cc entry motorcycles.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Restructured front-end/back-end organization (created Chief Business Officer roles for Exports and 2-Wheelers; split India into India 1 and India 2). Ransomware attack mentioned in Q1 was not discussed. Currency realized at INR 95.5/USD in Q2, but October spot rates present a minor transient headwind.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Chetak monthly capacity reached 60,000 units/month (though Sept dispatches hit 40,000 due to supply disruptions); refreshed Classic Pulsar 125/150 launched in Aug-Sept; 2 new brands in 125cc-250cc space remain on track for FY27 launch.

### Highlights
- Enterprise EV portfolio (2W + 3W) scaled to nearly 2,00,000 units in Q2, contributing >34% of domestic revenue and >20% of enterprise revenue. The overall EV business generates double-digit EBITDA and is margin-accretive at the domestic level.
- Chetak crossed 1 million units cumulative road milestone; September retails hit 45,000 units, making Chetak 3503 the highest-selling electric 2W model in India with a 23% market share.
- Pro-Biking (KTM & Triumph) sold 40,000 units in Q2 (+31% YoY) and 80,000 units in H1 (highest ever H1); Triumph sold 11,000 units in Q2.
- 2W export market share from India expanded to 48% in H1 (vs 44% in FY26); 3W export share rose to 63% (vs 60% in FY26).
- Reorganized front-end structure into distinct business units under CBOs with regional Presidents across international (Africa, Asia, LatAm, MENA/Advanced) and domestic (India 1 and India 2) markets.
- Broke previous records by exceeding 7.8 lakh export units in a quarter and crossing Rs 20,000 crore quarterly operating revenue for the first time.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Management** expects to sustain an export volume run-rate of ~7,50,000 units/quarter (240k-260k units/month).
- **Management** plans to expand overall monthly plant capacity from 6,25,000 units to 7,75,000 units over the next 6 to 9 months.
- **Management** plans to scale Chetak capacity to 80,000 units/month by Q4 FY27 and add ~75 exclusive Chetak stores in Q3 (aiming for 1,000 stores in 12 months).
- **Management** expects domestic 2W festive period (Sept-Dec) growth to stay in double digits.
- **Management** expects PLI accrual rate to stay at the maximum applicable slab of 18% (16% base + 2% booster) on eligible EV revenue for H2 FY27 after crossing the Rs 10,000 crore cumulative revenue threshold.
- **Management** plans to introduce 2 new product brands addressing the 125cc to 250cc segment in FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Domestic 2W market grew 18% YoY in Apr-Aug; the first 16 days of the festive season saw 10% VAHAN registration growth, led by 150cc+ motorcycles and EV scooters (+24% YoY). Bajaj comparable growth was +32% (8 percentage points ahead of market).
- K-shaped market demand persists: bottom-of-the-pyramid economic stress and tight retail financing weigh on 100cc entry motorcycles, while higher income segments drive premium motorcycle and EV sales.
- 3W market growing at 8-12% YoY; Bajaj commands ~90% share in CNG passenger 3W and >50% in cargo 3W. Bajaj WEGO led e-autos with a ~32% market share in September.
- Electric scooter penetration reached ~25% of the overall Indian scooter category.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Material and input cost inflation added 3%-3.5% of revenue in Q2 (following 4%-4.5% in Q1). Cost increases were driven by electrical components (mid-teens), proprietary parts/conversion charges (double-digit), aluminum, copper, nickel, rubber, and late-Q2 increases in steel.
- Calibrated price hikes covered 50%-60% of cumulative H1 inflation; the remainder was offset by favorable realized USD/INR rates (95.5 vs 94.4 in Q1) and operational leverage.
- Implemented price hikes in early October to cover ~30% of expected Q3 cost escalation (~2 percentage points estimated inflation).
- USD/INR spot rates in October moved below 95.5, creating a potential transient realization headwind for Q3.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Enterprise monthly production capacity is being expanded from 6,25,000 to 7,75,000 units over 6 to 9 months.
- Chetak footprint reached 550+ exclusive stores across 850+ cities; 3W retail network expanded to 1,600+ stores; joint KTM-Triumph dual stores reached 120 operational locations (contributing >15% of combined volumes).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Q2 free cash flow generation reached ~Rs 4,000 crore, representing a PAT conversion rate of >100%.
- Surplus cash rebuilding continues supported by operating cash flow conversion.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continuous hyperinflation across steel, copper, electrical components, and proprietary parts entering a third consecutive quarter.
- Potential currency realization headwind in Q3 if USD/INR rates stay below Q2's 95.5 average.
- Deficit monsoons and drought-like conditions in select states (Maharashtra, Southern states, Northeast) could dampen Q4 rural cash flows and harvest demand.
- Component supply and logistics bottlenecks restricted EV production/deliveries to ~75% of planned levels in Q2 and limited Triumph availability by ~20%.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gunjan Prithyani (BofA):** Why isn't Pulsar market share gain clearly visible in aggregate VAHAN/SIAM numbers?  
  - **Management:** Aggregate metrics are diluted by deliberate profit-focused pricing in 100cc and channel stock transition of older Classic Pulsars. The N-series and NS-series (comprising 60% of the 150cc+ portfolio) are outperforming the market; newly launched Classic Pulsar refreshes will drive 125cc/150cc recovery post-festive.
- **Amyn Pirani (UBS):** Scope of the new FY27 platforms and sustainability of export volume run-rates.  
  - **Management:** Two new brands will complement Pulsar to address the 125cc-250cc space in domestic and global markets. Export volumes are targeting ~750,000 units/quarter, supported by share gains and market expansion.
- **Raghunandhan N. L.:** Quantum of October price hikes and strategy on inflation vs currency.  
  - **Management:** October price hikes covered ~30% of estimated Q3 cost inflation. Management will not hike prices to offset short-term currency strengthening, preferring to assess market absorption dynamically.
- **Kapil Singh (Nomura):** Assessment of rural demand and speed of 2W electrification.  
  - **Management:** Harvest impacts from poor monsoons may surface in Q4, but overall market trends mirror K-shaped GDP distribution. Electric scooters now account for 25% of scooters and will continue expanding toward 50% over time.

### One-line takeaway
Bajaj Auto achieved record quarterly revenues and profits in Q2 FY27 behind surging exports and profitable EV scaling, while managing three consecutive quarters of input cost inflation through operating leverage and calibrated pricing.
