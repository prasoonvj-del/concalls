# Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong domestic revenue and volume growth offset by JLR wholesale supply constraints, higher VME, and elevated commodity inflation.

### Key numbers
- **TMPV Revenue:** Rs. 95,800 Cr
- **TMPV EBIT Margin:** 2.4%
- **TMPV PBT:** Rs. 1,600 Cr (down YoY)
- **Consolidated Net Debt:** Rs. 42,000 Cr at the end of the quarter (India business net cash positive; JLR net debt at GBP 3.6 billion)
- **JLR Wholesales:** Down ~10% YoY (~8,000 units lower; ~3,000 lower than internal plan)
- **JLR Revenue:** GBP 6 billion
- **JLR EBIT Margin:** 2.8%
- **JLR PBT:** GBP 109 million
- **JLR Free Cash Flow (Pre-working capital):** Negative GBP 352 million
- **JLR Capitalization Level:** 74%
- **India PV Wholesales:** ~182,000 units (up 46% YoY)
- **India PV Market Share:** 14.3% (up 200 bps YoY)
- **India EV Mix:** 19% in Q1 (exited June and July at 23%)
- **India EV Volumes:** Over 34,000 units (up 100% YoY); market share at 43% in July
- **India Business Revenue:** Rs. 18,000 Cr (up 65% YoY)
- **India EBITDA Margin:** Flattish at ~4% YoY
- **PLI Accruals:** Rs. 313 Cr (largely from Nexon.ev and Harrier.ev)
- **India Capex:** Rs. 1,300 Cr for the quarter
- **India Free Cash Flow:** Rs. 1,100 Cr for the quarter
- **Commodity Impact:** 4.5% in Q1; expected 3% residual impact in Q2
- **Cumulative Price Increase:** 1% taken across April and July (0.5% each)

### Highlights
- JLR experienced supply constraints due to a fire at a chassis component supplier early in the quarter affecting Range Rover and Range Rover Sport production, temporary quality holds at quarter-end, and the run-out of legacy Jaguar models.
- Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, and Defender now comprise 81% of JLR sales.
- JLR announced a strategic goal of 10% revenue growth per annum and $1.7 billion in cost savings targeting ex-works costs, warranty, and fixed costs.
- India business achieved an industry-beating volume growth of 46% YoY, solidifying its #2 position in registrations.
- Refreshed Tiago ICE, Tiago.ev, and all-new Sierra.ev launched in India.
- Avinya launch shifted to 2027 due to a platform strategy shift to CJLR's Freelander platform.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated JLR Q1 results do not imply a need to change full-year guidance, though it requires strong performance for the remainder of the year.
- Management targets higher double-digit volume growth for the India PV business in FY 27.
- Management expects JLR EV production of about 12,000 cars in FY 27.
- Management expects JLR BEV rollout to skew toward the UK and Europe, with ICE continuing in North America.
- Definitive agreement with Stellantis for U.S. manufacturing targeted by the end of the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- JLR retail demand impacted in the Middle East due to geopolitical conflict and alternative routing.
- China market remains challenging with a 25% YoY drop in JLR wholesales due to economic conditions, retailer stress, and new luxury taxes.
- Indian PV industry crossed 1.3 million units in Q1 (24% YoY growth, aided by low base), with greener powertrains (CNG and EV) reaching 31% of industry sales.
- EV adoption in India is accelerating rapidly, with industry EV penetration reaching 8% in June 2026.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- JLR VME increased to 7.1%.
- JLR commodity headwinds mitigated partly by falling aluminum prices (from $3,500/tonne in March to $3,150/tonne in June) and hedges.
- India EBITDA margins remained flat at ~4% as 2% material cost reductions and fixed cost leverage were entirely offset by a 6% YoY commodity impact.
- India margins expected to step up in H2 via price increases, accelerated cost reductions, and PLI accruals.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- JLR investment levels remain high near the peak of its cycle; capitalization was 74% in Q1. Spend to shift from engineering to capital as four new BEV facilities come online.
- JLR signed an MoU with Stellantis to produce U.S.-specific vehicles in North America.
- India debottlenecking and capacity expansion initiatives underway; production targeted closer to 70,000 units/month ahead of the festive season.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt stood at Rs. 42,000 Cr; India business is net cash positive, while JLR net debt is GBP 3.6 billion.
- JLR experienced a seasonal working capital outflow in Q1 (free cash flow pre-working capital negative GBP 352 million; post-working capital just under GBP 1 billion negative).
- India generated Rs. 1,100 Cr free cash flow driven by favorable working capital releases.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain constraints, geopolitical conflicts (Middle East), and labor issues impacting production.
- Elevated commodity inflation and raw material price pressures (aluminum, palladium, copper).
- China market headwinds, retailer stress, and retrospective luxury taxes.
- Rating agencies maintaining JLR on investment grade with negative watch.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sridhar (Antique Stock Broking) on JLR volume recovery & Jaguar Type 01:** Richard Molyneux explained Middle East routes are adjusting, the supplier fire issue is fully resolved, and Jaguar Type 01 production starts early next year with material volume impact expected in FY 28.
- **Nishit (Axis) on India price increases vs. raw materials:** Shailesh Chandra noted that while raw material costs spiked, price increases are kept at 1% to maintain product competitiveness, with remaining impact addressed via cost reductions and progressive price hikes.
- **Timothy (Citi) on JLR FY 27 guidance & US localization:** Richard Molyneux confirmed guidance remains unchanged and stated that localizing low-volume existing models (30k-50k units) in the US is unviable, hence the Stellantis MoU for new U.S.-specific vehicles.
- **Jyothi Singh (Haitong Securities) on China outlook:** Richard Molyneux and P.B. Balaji cautioned that China conditions remain difficult due to domestic overcapacity and retrospective luxury taxes, and things may worsen before stabilizing.
- **Unidentified Analyst on Avinya launch timeline:** Shailesh Chandra stated the launch is shifted to 2027 due to transitioning the platform to CJLR's Freelander platform.

### One-line takeaway
TMPV delivered robust domestic volume and revenue growth offset by commodity inflation, while JLR faced temporary supply chain and regional headwinds ahead of a major upcoming BEV product cycle.
