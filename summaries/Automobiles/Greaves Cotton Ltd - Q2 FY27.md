# Greaves Cotton Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth and strategic execution across core businesses, despite margin pressure from input cost inflation.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 1,176 crore (+44% YoY).
*   **H1 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 2,150 crore (+38% YoY).
*   **Core Business Revenue:** Rs 768 crore (+24% YoY).
*   **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 6.2% (vs 5.8% in Q1 FY27).
*   **Core Business EBITDA Margin:** 11.5% (vs 14.8% YoY).
*   **GEML (EV) Revenue:** Rs 410 crore (+106% YoY).
*   **GEML (EV) Loss:** Rs 25 crore (vs Rs 42 crore YoY).
*   **Exceptional/Non-recurring items:** Rs 60.1 crore (Rs 27.3 crore accelerated depreciation + Rs 32.8 crore exceptional items).

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Progress:** Continued execution of "GREAVES.NEXT" strategy; acquired remaining 20% stake in Excel Controlinkage, making it a 100% subsidiary.
*   **EV Performance:** Greaves Electric Mobility (GEML) recorded 37,000+ e-2W units (+114% YoY) and 2,800 L5 3W units (+61% YoY).
*   **Technology:** Developed in-house rare-earth-free motor (ferrite-assisted synchronous reluctance motor) to reduce import dependence and improve performance; deployment to begin in Q3/Q4.
*   **Energy Solutions:** MHP genset (160-750 kVA) portfolio grew 35% in H1.
*   **Industrial Solutions:** Reported revenue flat, but grew 9% on a like-for-like basis (excluding exited farm equipment business).

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Margins:** Management expects H2 margins to be better than H1.
*   **Pricing:** Full company-wide benefit of price increases is expected to be reflected by Q4 FY27.
*   **GEML:** Management expects the EV business to break even "fairly soon."
*   **Funding:** No further funding required for GEML for the next 12-18 months.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Energy Solutions:** Healthy demand across infrastructure, manufacturing, and commercial/industrial applications.
*   **Automotive Engines:** Strong demand for 3W and 4W applications; grew 37% YoY.
*   **Exports:** Export business remains resilient; shipped first batch of enhanced gensets to the Middle East.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Inflation:** Margins pressured by rising costs of Aluminium, Iron, Copper, and Platinum Group Metals (PGM).
*   **Pricing Lag:** B2B/OEM contracts operate with a lag, delaying the pass-through of cost increases.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity:** GEML capacity is ~40,000 units/month; current utilization is ~40%.
*   **Investments:** Invested Rs 331 crore in GEML rights issue and Rs 112 crore for Excel stake acquisition.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Liquidity:** Core business has Rs 150 crore cash and Rs 40 crore debt (Net cash: Rs 110 crore).
*   **Credit Rating:** AA-.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Input Costs:** Continued commodity inflation remains a key headwind.
*   **Execution Lag:** Time lag in passing on price increases to B2B/OEM customers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is the breakeven volume for GEML?** A: Management did not provide a specific number but noted they are running ahead of plan and expect breakeven "fairly soon."
*   **Q: Are the margin gains in GEML sustainable?** A: Management stated that product/pricing strategies are mitigating commodity risks, and the trajectory of reducing loss per unit is expected to continue.
*   **Q: Any further write-offs expected?** A: Management clarified that the Q2 write-offs/accelerated depreciation were a regular review exercise and they believe there is nothing more to do at this stage.
*   **Q: Why is GEML capacity utilization low if demand is strong?** A: Management noted they are expanding the partner ecosystem to cater to higher growth rates and do not view current capacity as a constraint to their ambitions.
*   **Q: Any update on GEML IPO?** A: Management stated they are scanning the market and enterprise valuations; no definite date is set.

### One-line takeaway
Greaves Cotton is scaling its EV and core businesses while navigating input cost pressures, with management prioritizing margin recovery in H2 and confirming that major portfolio restructuring/write-offs are complete.
