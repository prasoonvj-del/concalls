# Mufin Green Finance Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-26 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Remarkable growth in numbers, profitability, reduction in cost of borrowing, and scaling of new digital lending products with minimal delinquency.

### Key numbers
- **Total Borrowings:** Rs 1,551 crore (compared to Rs 771 crore in Q1 FY26).
- **Cost of Borrowing:** Reduced by 263 bps YoY from 13.80% in Q1 FY26 to 11.17% in Q1 FY27.
- **Mediclaim Financing Portfolio:** Approximately Rs 677 crore (almost entirely NPA-free).
- **Employee Count:** Reduced from 499 in Q1 FY26 to approximately 367; target of ~300 by the end of the year.
- **Active Lenders:** Increased from 20+ in Q1 FY26 to 35+ in Q1 FY27 (including PSU banks for the first time).
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** 2.43x in March 2026 (compared to 2.65x in March 2025).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.91% (down from 1.94% in the previous quarter).
- **Net NPA:** Improved similarly on a quarter-on-quarter basis.

### Highlights
- Management transitioned the business model from a traditional branch-based, manpower-intensive setup to a digital lending model over the last three years.
- Entered innovative, tech-based products including Mediclaim Financing (medical insurance premium financing) and Salary Saathi.
- Credit ratings improved from BBB to BBB+, then A- (Stable), with a one-notch upgrade to 'A' expected in the next quarter.
- Operational leverage improved significantly, evidenced by rising AUM alongside a reduction in employee count and operational costs.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a one-notch credit rating upgrade to an 'A' rating in the next quarter, which will further reduce borrowing costs.
- Target to reduce employee count to approximately 300 by the end of the year.
- Significant volumes forecasted for the Salary Saathi product going forward due to deep integration with state governments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Mediclaim Financing and Salary Saathi are experiencing high demand and strong volumes as fully digital lending solutions with minimal-to-nil delinquency.
- Continued signing of new contracts with insurance companies for Mediclaim Financing.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Cost of borrowing dropped from 13.80% to 11.17% YoY (a 263 bps reduction).
- Manpower costs, operational costs, branch footprint costs, and credit costs/delinquencies have all reduced.
