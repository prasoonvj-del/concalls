# Ashok Leyland Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume and revenue growth were offset by commodity cost inflation impacting margins YoY and auditor-flagged system discrepancies at HLF.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Raised (Full-year domestic M&HCV industry volume growth upgraded to double-digit / mid-teens vs. high single-digit expected in Q1).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from positive to mixed due to raw material inflation and HLF accounting discrepancies; however, underlying domestic demand sentiment strengthened post-October festive performance.
- **New topics or risks:** HLF auditors flagged discrepancies in loan balances across underlying systems requiring an independent review; construction commenced for the new battery pack plant at Pillaipakkam; NCLT approved the HLF-NDL merger scheme on Nov 6, 2026. Disappeared: BS7 timeline discussions.
- **Last quarter's promises:** UAE plant (RAK) capacity normalized and supply chain restored post Q2 disruption; Switch UK debt reduction progressed via Rs 324 crore investment; HLF merger process achieved NCLT order.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 13,364 crore (up 39% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 1,350 crore (up 16.2% YoY, up 39% QoQ)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10.1% (flat QoQ, down 200 bps YoY from 12.1%)
- **PBT:** Rs 1,187 crore (up 14% YoY)
- **PAT:** Rs 869 crore (up 13% YoY)
- **Domestic M&HCV Volume:** Up 44% YoY; domestic M&HCV market share at 32.1% (up 120 bps YoY); M&HCV truck market share gain of +150 bps YoY.
- **Domestic LCV Volume:** 21,371 units (up 21% YoY); LCV VAHAN market share at 13.6% (up 30 bps YoY).
- **Total Domestic CV Volume (excl. Defence):** 59,575 units (up 34% YoY; highest-ever Q2).
- **International CV Volume:** 5,102 units (up 7% YoY; GCC volumes declined 20% YoY).
- **H1 Domestic CV Volume:** 105,877 units (up 24% YoY; highest-ever H1).
- **Non-CV Revenues:** Aftermarket up 20% YoY; Power Solutions up 33% YoY; Defence up 66% YoY to ~Rs 400 crore.
- **Material Cost:** 74.9% of revenue (up 340 bps QoQ from 71.5%).
- **Capex:** Rs 240 crore in Q2 (H1 cumulative: Rs 393 crore).
- **Subsidiary Investments:** Rs 472 crore in Q2 (H1 cumulative: Rs 483 crore), including Rs 324 crore for Switch UK loan repayment and Rs 150 crore into OHM.
- **Net Cash Surplus:** Rs 3,546 crore (positive swing of Rs 2,556 crore YoY).
- **Interim Dividend:** Rs 1 per share on face value of Rs 1 per share.
- **Switch Mobility (H1):** Delivered 439 e-buses and 732 e-LCVs; order book stands at ~2,800 e-buses.
- **OHM Mobility:** Operating fleet expanded to ~2,100 e-buses; won e-MaaS order for >200 cement distribution trucks.

---

### Highlights
- Recorded highest-ever Q2 domestic CV volumes at 59,575 units (ex-Defence).
- Launched air-suspension multi-axle trucks (up to 4-tonne payload advantage), 360 HP HIPPO tractors and TAURUS tippers, and GARUD 15-meter front-engine multi-axle bus coach.
- Multi-axle vehicles (MAV) increased to 25% of truck volume in Q2 (up from 20% in Q1).
- Added 43 touchpoints in Q2, taking total network touchpoints to 2,175 (1,205 M&HCV, 970 LCV).
- Commenced construction of a 4 GWh battery pack facility at Pillaipakkam near Chennai.
- Received NCLT approval on Nov 6, 2026, for the merger of Hinduja Leyland Finance (HLF) with NDL Ventures (appointed date April 1, 2026); consolidated HLF in AL on cost accounting basis.

---

### Guidance & outlook
- **Industry Growth:** Full-year FY27 M&HCV industry volume growth projected at double-digit to mid-teens; LCV expected to see similar growth momentum.
- **Margins:** Reaffirmed medium-term target of mid-teens EBITDA margins.
- **Pricing:** Implemented 100–150 bps price hike in Q3; further hikes planned in Q4 to pass through input cost inflation.
- **Exports:** GCC volumes expected to turn around in Q3 and Q4 as RAK supply chain constraints normalize.
- **Capex:** FY27 full-year capex guided between Rs 1,000 crore and Rs 1,200 crore.

---

### Demand & industry commentary
- Sustained underlying demand supported by fleet aging, lower TCO of BS6 vs. older fleets, replacement cycles, infra/construction activity, and strong consumption.
- Strong volume momentum continued into October despite facing a higher historical base; November market sentiment remains positive.
- Freight rates are moving upwards in the market; however, internal vehicle transport freight expenses per unit rose by >50% YoY.

---

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs surged 340 bps QoQ to 74.9% of revenue due to sharp price inflation in steel, natural rubber, forex, fuel, and conversion costs.
- Raw material inflation expected to remain high in Q3 before potentially moderating in Q4.
- EBITDA margin held at 10.1% QoQ due to operating leverage, net sales realization increases of 1%–1.25% QoQ (~2%–2.5% YTD), cost savings, and favorable product/business mix.

---

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Plant capacity utilization reached ~90% in Q2. Near-term strategy relies on debottlenecking facilities; greenfield plant planning is underway.
- Construction started for the Pillaipakkam battery pack plant (4 GWh initial capacity, expandable to 8–12 GWh), with Start of Production (SOP) scheduled for Q3 next fiscal year.
- Decision on captive cell manufacturing (Phase 2) expected in the next financial year.

---

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net cash surplus expanded to Rs 3,546 crore at September end.
- HLF + HHF consolidated book value in AL results stood at Rs 8,313 crore, with Q2 PAT of Rs 174 crore.
- Listing process of merged NDL/HLF NBFC entity expected to complete within Q4 FY27, pending RBI NBFC registration and ROC filings.

---

### Risks & concerns flagged
- HLF auditors issued a disclaimer of opinion regarding discrepancies in loan balances between underlying systems; AL is appointing an independent agency to conduct a 4-to-6-week comprehensive review.
- Persistently elevated steel and natural rubber prices causing near-term gross margin dilution.
- West Asia geopolitical crisis creating ongoing transit, freight cost, and logistics challenges.

---

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Chandramouli (Goldman Sachs):** *On H2 demand expectations and export outlook.*  
   **Management:** Expects M&HCV industry growth of double-digit to mid-teens for FY27 following strong October performance. GCC export volume dropped 20% in Q2 due to West Asia disruptions, but RAK facility supply chain is near normal and should drive Q3/Q4 rebound.
2. **Chandramouli (Goldman Sachs):** *On margin dilution due to sticky steel prices.*  
   **Management:** Suggested looking at absolute EBITDA growth (+39% YoY) temporarily during commodity cost spikes. Passing on prices in tranches leaves unrealized price pass-through that will benefit P&L when commodity inflation halts.
3. **Amyn Pirani (JPMorgan) / Vedant:** *On discrepancies in HLF loan balances and auditor disclaimer.*  
   **Management:** Differences exist in underlying loan transaction systems across HLF's large loan portfolio. A system audit has started, and an independent agency is being appointed to evaluate quantum and cause over 4–6 weeks.
4. **Kapil Singh (Nomura/Axis):** *On EV strategy and battery pack/cell manufacturing timeline.*  
   **Management:** Battery pack plant construction started at Pillaipakkam (4 GWh initial capacity; SOP in Q3 next fiscal). Cell manufacturing decision will be evaluated in FY28.
5. **Amit Hiranandani:** *On discounting trends and mid-teens margin target retention.*  
   **Management:** No price discounting is being offered; focus remains on raising net sales realization (+100–150 bps planned in Q3). Reaffirmed medium-term target of mid-teens EBITDA margins.
