# Indo Farm Equipment Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong YoY growth in revenue, EBITDA, and tractor segments, alongside an upbeat outlook and progress on new manufacturing capacities.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** ₹104.93 crore vs ₹91.26 crore (YoY growth of 14.98%)
- **EBITDA:** ₹13.09 crore vs ₹11.81 crore (YoY growth of 10.84%)
- **Tractor segment revenue:** ₹52.08 crore vs ₹38.21 crore (YoY growth of 36.29%)
- **Crane segment revenue:** ₹52.86 crore vs ₹53.05 crore (flat YoY)
- **Dealer network:** 250+ total dealers (approx. 225 in tractors, approx. 25 in cranes)
- **Tractor capacity utilization:** Assembly running at 35-40%; machine shop running at 80-85%
- **Tractor financing penetration:** Around 20% of new Indo Farm tractors financed by Barota Finance

### Highlights
- Tractor segment continued strong momentum, growing over 36% YoY.
- Crane segment remained flat YoY due to previous quarter impacts from emission norm transitions (Trem III to Trem V) and existing plant capacity constraints.
- Bhud site project civil and construction work (main shed, PEB structures) is in full swing; major machinery orders placed and units ready.
- Developed and successfully tested first tower crane prototype, clearing structural integrity, reliability, and safety evaluations.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintains FY27 overall revenue growth guidance of 20–25%.
- Tractor revenue expected to grow 25–30%.
- Crane revenue from the existing plant expected to grow 15–20%.
- Standalone EBITDA margin expected in the range of 12.5% to 13%; consolidated EBITDA margin expected around 14% to 15% (similar to last year).
- New Bhud site commercial production expected to begin by the end of November (current financial year), with the first-year capacity utilization expected around 30% for the 3,600-unit crane capacity.
- Long-term roadmap: Tractor business growth expected at 30%+ over the next 3 to 5 years; crane plant utilization targeted at 70% to 80% over 3 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Crane demand in Q2 is looking promising and better, allowing cost increases to be passed on to customers.
- Tractor growth is supported by wide product range (16 HP to 100 HP, all with 4WD options), fuel mileage, back-end integration, and retail financing support via Barota Finance.
- Tractor purchasing decisions take 10 years, requiring local service and neighborhood proof; mass demonstrations are ongoing at the village level.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Higher steel costs and emission norm transitions (Trem III to Trem V) impacted crane profitability in previous quarters.
- Management expects to fully pass on cost increases to customers from Q2 onwards due to improving demand.
- Fixed costs remain stable while variable costs scale with volume.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Bhud site commercial production targeted for end of November 2026.
- Total crane capacity after expansion: 1,200 (existing) + 3,600 (new) = 4,800 units.
- Machinery procurement for the Bhud site will be phased (buying 6-8 machines initially out of 10), with full deployment/funds consumption expected by March.
- Crane dealer network roadmap targets 60+ dealers over the next 1 to 1.5 years to cover the country.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- ₹45 crore deposited in banks remains available for capex utilization through the year.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Dealer network expansion for cranes was intentionally paused to avoid unfulfilled supply orders; underperforming dealers are being replaced as the new plant readies.
- Transition from Trem III to Trem V emission norms caused temporary sluggishness across the construction equipment industry over the past three quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rahul Gupta asked about crane cost pass-through and margin normalization:** Management stated that demand is better in Q2, allowing cost increases to be fully passed on to customers from this quarter.
2. **Sandesh Kumar questioned how 5,000 crane units can be absorbed with only 25 dealers, especially in South India:** Management replied that utilization will ramp up phase-wise (starting at ~30% in year one), and a roadmap to reach 60+ dealers is in place alongside replacing non-performing dealers.
3. **Shiv Shaant inquired about Barota Finance penetration and tractor operating leverage:** Management clarified that Barota finances around 20% of new tractors (plus refinancing old tractors), and that the tractor business is already profitable with fixed costs remaining stable as volumes grow.
4. **Manas asked about EBITDA margin guidance for FY27:** Management guided to 12.5%–13% on a standalone basis and 14%–15% on a consolidated basis.
5. **Yogesh Patil asked about long-term volume growth assumptions:** Management confirmed expectations of 30%+ growth in tractors and 70%–80% utilization of the new crane plant over 3 to 5 years.

### One-line takeaway
Indo Farm delivered strong Q1 FY27 revenue growth of 14.98% led by a 36% jump in tractors, while preparing for a major operational leap with the new Bhud crane manufacturing facility slated to start commercial production in November.
