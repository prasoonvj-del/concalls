# VST Tillers Tractors Ltd-$ - Q1 FY26 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-19 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY26/27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and core segments (power tillers, power weeders) grew strongly, though margins saw slight pressure from raw material inflation.

### Key numbers
- Revenue: Rs 313.4 crore (vs Rs 282.4 crore YoY, up 11%)
- Operational EBITDA margin: 12.85% (vs 13.3% YoY, down 45 bps)
- Operational EBITDA: Rs 40.3 crore
- PAT: Rs 48.7 crore (vs Rs 44.6 crore YoY)
- Power tiller volume growth: ~18% YoY
- Domestic tractor volume growth: 4.5% YoY
- Tractor exports: 275 tractors in the current quarter
- Power weeder volume growth: 56% YoY
- Retail financing through Bajaj Finance: ~1,000 units in Q1 (vs ~400 units YoY); ~8% to 9% of retail

### Highlights
- Power tillers and power weeders recorded strong volume growth of ~18% and 56% respectively.
- Retail financing partnerships (including Bajaj Finance, Cholamandalam, TVS) doubled retail financing volume.
- Seeded the 75 HP tractor in the African market.
- Expanded SFM retail counters by over 500 across the country within 3 months, targeting 2,000 counters by the end of the year.
- Administrative office located on roughly 19 acres of land in Bangalore; manufacturing has already been moved outside.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management aims for Rs 3,000 crore revenue by 2030.
- Tractor volume target: upwards of 15,000 to close to 20,000 numbers by FY30 (including 5,000 to 6,000 in international markets).
- Full-year FY27 outlook will be commented on in another month's time once monsoon progress becomes clearer.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Uneven monsoon and below-normal IMD outlook noted; next 15-20 days critical for standing kharif crops and rabi water table.
- Small and marginal farmer segment presents a massive opportunity for small farm mechanization.
- Tractor industry in the 40-50 HP segment is crowded and competitive.
- U.S. electric tractor market has taken a back seat due to reduced environmental emphasis and tariff uncertainties.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operational EBITDA margin dropped by 45 bps YoY to 12.85% due to raw material cost inflation (steel, forging, casting, aluminum, copper, and elevated natural rubber prices).
- Crude and freight costs remain high due to geopolitical and supply chain disruptions.
- Future Stage 5 emission norm technology (applicable in 2032 for full range) currently costs about 25% to 30% extra, though costs are expected to normalize globally over time.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Management plans to invest in a new production facility in the next 2 years or so to support long-term growth.
- International logistics infrastructure to cut down supply time to distributors is expected to become operational in Q3.
- Adding 50 to 60 tractor dealers across India this year (after adding 40-50 last year), focusing on North India.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Capital deployment strategy aims to maximize shareholder value; exploring opportunities to deploy capital over the next 18 to 24 months.
- No current development or monetization plans regarding the 19 acres of land in Bangalore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Uneven monsoon distribution and below-normal outlook pose risks to crop survival and rabi moisture levels.
- Commodity inflation (steel, forging, casting, aluminum, copper, natural rubber) and high crude/freight costs impacting EBITDA margins.
- Potential U.S. congressional tariff risks creating trouble for U.S. expansion plans.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Annamalai Jayaraj asked about the slow traction in higher HP tractors despite Zetor tie-ups.** Management stated production ramp-up issues in Q1 limited supply, but volumes will pick up in Q2, Q3, and Q4 with upcoming variants and the Phantom series launch.
2. **Shreyansh asked about tiller export potential and competition with China.** Management stated they export in small numbers (~300 units last year) but are developing a new geared tiller with differential and EV tillers for international markets.
3. **Manish Joshi asked about the decline in July volumes year-on-year.** Management responded that July last year had a very large base, and tiller/weeder demand remains strong through September barring adverse monsoon failure.
4. **Annamalai Jayaraj asked about the upcoming mid-product between tillers and tractors.** Management noted it is a unique VST R&D innovation priced close to a tiller with work output close to a tractor, rolling out from this month with commercial volume in Q3/Q4.
5. **Annamalai Jayaraj asked about capital allocation and non-core land assets.** Management stated capital will be deployed strategically over 18-24 months to maximize shareholder value, and there are no current developments on the 19 acres of Bangalore land.

### One-line takeaway
VST Tillers Tractors delivered an 11% revenue growth driven by strong power tiller and weeder volumes, though EBITDA margins contracted slightly by 45 bps due to raw material inflation.
