# Tube Investments of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY2027
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong volume growth in core and e-mobility, offset by margin compression due to steel price increases and high cell/BOM costs.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained double-digit consolidated revenue and profit growth targets, and EV breakeven timeline (trucks in Q4 FY2027, two businesses in Q4 FY2028).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly more cautious on near-term margins due to surging commodity prices, though volume demand remains robust.
- New topics or risks: Mention of adverse rupee exchange rate impacting EV BOM costs alongside cell price inflation.
- Last quarter's promises: Infused Rs. 250 crore into TICMPL this quarter, delivering on the capital commitment plan.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs. 2,710 crore vs Rs. 2,119 crore YoY
- **Standalone PBT:** Rs. 253 crore vs Rs. 250 crore YoY
- **Standalone ROIC:** 44% vs 44% YoY
- **Standalone Free Cash Flow:** Rs. 167 crore
- **Engineering Revenue:** Rs. 1,828 crore vs Rs. 1,382 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 171 crore vs Rs. 164 crore YoY
- **Metal Formed Products Revenue:** Rs. 473 crore vs Rs. 408 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 49 crore vs Rs. 44 crore YoY
- **Mobility Revenue:** Rs. 247 crore vs Rs. 194 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 8 crore vs Rs. 4 crore YoY
- **Others Revenue:** Rs. 260 crore vs Rs. 227 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 20 crore vs Rs. 18 crore YoY
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 7,330 crore vs Rs. 5,523 crore YoY
- **Consolidated Profit (before share of JVs, exceptional items, and tax):** Rs. 643 crore vs Rs. 459 crore YoY
- **CG Power & Industrial Solutions Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 3,802 crore vs Rs. 2,923 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 559 crore vs Rs. 388 crore YoY
- **Shanthi Gears Revenue:** Rs. 128 crore vs Rs. 132 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 20 crore vs Rs. 29 crore YoY
- **TI Clean Mobility (TICMPL) Volumes:** HCV trucks: 311 (vs 86 in Q1); Three-wheelers: 2,242 (vs 1,924 in Q1); Small Commercial Vehicles (SCV): 483 (vs 347 in Q1); Electric Tractors: 50 (vs 22 in Q1)
- **Exports (% of TI revenue):** Maintained around 15%
- **Subsidiary Investments:** Rs. 250 crore in TICMPL, Rs. 78 crore in Shanthi Gears

### Highlights
- Standalone volume growth stood at approximately 15% YoY, with the rest driven by raw material price pass-throughs.
- TI Clean Mobility (TICMPL) top line reached Rs. 476 crore in H1, surpassing the previous full financial year's revenue. Over 1,000 SCVs and 300 tractors deployed.
- Medical devices business (Lotus/Medicura) growing at 20%+ YoY; plastic medical devices facility (IV cannula, canisters) expanding with team placement expected by Q3.
- CDMO semi-commercial plant commissioned, supplying quantities to a European customer who provided next-year projections; clean room commissioning and validation batches underway.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of fully recovering raw material price increases from customers/OEMs within the next 1 to 2 quarters.
- TICMPL targets EBITDA breakeven for the truck business in Q4 FY2027, and for two other businesses in Q4 FY2028.
- Long-term aspiration of USD 1 billion revenue for TICMPL by 2030 remains unchanged.
- Medical business sustainable profitability targeted at higher double-digit teen numbers.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Three-wheeler electric penetration is upwards of 50% in Q2.
- HCV electric truck industry in H1 reached almost 170% of the last full financial year; SCV H1 industry volumes matched the last full financial year.
- TICMPL expanded HCV end-use cases beyond cement to metals, steel, mining, chemicals, sugar, retail, and tyre movement (cement dependence down to 60%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins impacted by continuous commodity price increases (especially HRC steel and battery cell prices) and adverse rupee exchange rates.
- Pricing contract adjustments with OEMs take 1 to 2 quarters with retroactive effect, delaying margin recovery.
- Intensified efforts on Bill of Materials (BOM) cost reduction across all EV businesses.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- TICMPL cash in hand stands at around Rs. 350 crore, sufficient for current financial year needs without raising external funds.
- Exploring adjacencies, new product categories, and metal injection molding (MIM) startup investments.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Incremental subsidiary investments of Rs. 412 crore in Q2 (Rs. 250 crore in TICMPL via CCPS, Rs. 78 crore in Shanthi Gears).
- Internal FCF to PAT target remains around 85%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High upfront product cost and dependency on external cell suppliers and charging infrastructure for EVs.
- Geopolitical issues continuing to cap export revenue growth below the initial 20% target.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gnanasundaram S. (Avendus) on TICMPL cash runway and breakeven:** Management stated TICMPL has Rs. 350 crore in hand, sufficient for FY2027, and targets EBITDA breakeven for trucks in Q4 FY2027 and two businesses in Q4 FY2028.
- **Naman Maheshwari (Individual Investor) on CDMO timelines:** Management clarified that commercial supplies will begin in one quarter, while full regulatory approvals will take about a year.
- **Rushabh Shah (Manager) on EV long-haul roadmap:** Management noted they are developing higher-density battery trucks for long-haul routes on existing platforms.
- **Vipulkumar Shah (Individual Investor) on EV market shares:** Jalaj Gupta noted they hold ~25-26% market share in trucks and SCVs, low single digits in passenger 3-wheelers, and are gaining traction in cargo 3-wheelers (~200 units/month).
- **Salil Desai (Analyst) on TICMPL cost surge:** Management attributed margin pressure to high cell price increases, adverse rupee exchange rates, and commodity hikes.
- **Anupam Gupta (Analyst) on standalone EBITDA growth:** Management confirmed that once cost recovery catches up over the next 1-2 quarters, absolute EBITDA growth will track volume growth.

### One-line takeaway
Tube Investments of India posted robust standalone revenue of Rs. 2,710 crore and strong core/EV volume growth of ~15%, but near-term margins remain constrained by steel and cell cost lags that management expects to recover over the next two quarters.
