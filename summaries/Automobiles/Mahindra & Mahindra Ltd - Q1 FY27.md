# Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Group delivered strong consolidated profit growth of 34% and ROE of 23%, driven by strong execution across multiple businesses despite commodity inflation and supply challenges.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated PAT up 34% YoY (EPS at Rs 48; up 34% YoY).
- Revenue up 28% YoY.
- ROE at 23%.
- Auto revenue up 32% YoY; SUV volume up 15% YoY (14.9%); LCV volume up 20% YoY; EV penetration at 12%. Auto PBIT up 28% YoY; PAT up 21% YoY.
- Farm revenue up 15% YoY. Tractor volume up 18% YoY. Farm machinery revenue at Rs 370 crore in the quarter. Farm consolidated PBIT up 9% YoY (after impairment); PAT up 15% YoY.
- Mahindra Finance PAT up 78% YoY; AUM/disbursements driving performance; NIM at 7.3%; ROA at 2.4%; Gross Stage 3 below 10% (GS2+GS3).
- Tech Mahindra EBIT margins up to 14.4%; PAT up 28% YoY; Free cash flow up 94%.
- Growth Gems profits up 3x (revenue up 39% YoY). Real Estate GDV added Rs 5,600 crore (up 60%); pre-sales Rs 925 crore (up 2x). Logistics M&M-level profit at Rs 14 crore; business-level at Rs 25 crore. Aerospace cumulated contract wins at $1.2 billion ($600 million in the last year).

### Highlights
- Auto business faced 400-500 bps commodity price pressure, offset by pricing actions and operating cost controls.
- EV business reached 12% penetration with 77,000 cumulative vehicles sold; XEV 9S is the single largest-selling EV across all passenger vehicles. EV business is EBITDA positive without PLI.
- Farm business solved and exited the Erkunt Foundry problem.
- Mahindra Finance experienced a successful growth pivot, with non-wheels business growing strongly (79% in non-wheels segment) and diversified into mortgage and SME.
- Mahindra Lifespaces added Rs 5,600 crore GDV, taking total GDV to Rs 50,000 crore; delivered OCs ahead of schedule with 99% zero-snag apartments.
- Logistics turned around, eliminating white space drag (reduced from 16 lakh sq. ft. to 2 lakh sq. ft., sold).
- Truck and Bus announced a combination with SML Isuzu to enhance competitiveness and synergies.
- Aerospace secured major fuselage contracts, including single-sourcing globally for 2 helicopters with Airbus.
- AI deployments scaled: Paint.ai, Service.ai, Reach.ai, and Samurai (65% loan files processed by AI in finance).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management remains cautiously optimistic, expecting Auto margins to improve or maintain from Q1 levels unless commodities deteriorate significantly.
- Farm business expected to see some temporary margin pressure due to unhedgeable steel (up 24%) and rubber (up 30% / 53% since year-start) inflation.
- Tech Mahindra on track to reach 15% EBIT margin by the end of FY27.
- Mahindra Lifespaces targeting Rs 10,000 crore pre-sales by FY30.
- Mahindra Finance targeting a 70-30 mobility-to-non-wheels book by 2031, with a Rs 3 lakh crore book target by 2031; ROA target range of 2.2%-2.5%.
- Auto capacity expansion plan: Target to reach 68,000/month by Sept (60k ICE + 8k EV), 82,000 by year-end, 92,000 from Chakan, and scaling up to 2,50,000 in Nagpur by H1 CY2029 (2x capacity increase by F31).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Rural sentiment is non-negative despite initial El Nino fears and rainfall deficit (now at 15% deficit, recovering); Rabi cash flows healthy (wheat procurement up 19%), reservoir levels improving, and Kharif sowing accelerating (shortfall over last year just over 4%).
- Farm labor shortage is accelerating mechanization in rural areas. Shift towards higher horsepower (69-70% in 40-50 HP range).
- Urban and rural auto demand remains strong; shift towards CNG and diesel observed alongside EV adoption.
- Real estate demand in Mumbai and top cities remains robust (70% of apartments represent potential target customers).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Auto margins impacted by 400-500 bps due to commodity inflation and an 85 bps hedging MTM loss.
- Price hikes taken: 1.5% earlier and an average of 2.7% in mid-July in Auto; tractor prices also increased.
- Steel prices up 24% and rubber up 30% (53% since calendar year start), impacting Farm margins unhedgeably.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Auto capacity to double from current levels by F31 across multiple phases (Chakan and Nagpur plants).
- Real estate scaling up industrial portfolio and expanding in Mumbai, Pune, and Bangalore.
- Aerospace looking at potential global acquisitions underwritten by OEM partner customers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Mahindra Finance lending book touching ~Rs 1,50,000 crore; GS2+GS3 below 10% for 7 quarters; GS3 for housing finance below 2.5%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in commodity prices (especially steel and rubber).
- Supply chain disruptions (e.g., supplier fires, 3 days of production lost in July due to flooding in Maharashtra).
- Season shift in tractors (festivals moved to 11th October, impacting Q2 operating leverage).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **EV Profitability & PLI (Chandru, Goldman Sachs):** Management clarified that the EV business is EBITDA positive even without PLI. PLI duration depends on industry scale and government dialogue; pricing power will improve as real savings become tangible past 20-25% penetration.
- **Tractor Inventory & Horsepower Mix (Chandru, Goldman Sachs):** Channel inventory is normal (30-40 days); horsepower mix has shifted towards 40-50 HP (69-70%) driven by implement adoption.
- **AI Differentiation (Kapil):** Management explained that differentiation comes from proprietary models built on 20 years of internal data and process-owner involvement rather than generic out-of-the-box tools.
- **Auto Commodity Impact & Hedging (Raghu / Binay):** Management confirmed Q1 Auto margins included an 85 bps hedging loss. Despite further commodity increases, 2.7% price hikes and operational efficiencies provide a buffer to maintain/improve margins sequentially.
- **Mahindra Finance Diversification (Raghu):** Raul Rebello noted that non-wheels (mortgage, SME, PL) are growing rapidly (79% growth in non-wheels), targeting a 70-30 mobility-to-non-wheels mix by 2031.
- **Slum Redevelopment Entry (Biplab):** Amit Sinha stated that Lifespaces has not prioritized slum redevelopment yet due to risk/reward dynamics, but evaluates policies and opportunities carefully.

### One-line takeaway
M&M delivered a robust Q1 FY27 with consolidated PAT up 34% and ROE at 23%, driven by strong execution across Auto, Farm, Finance, TechM, and Growth Gems, successfully absorbing commodity headwinds through proactive pricing and cost controls.
