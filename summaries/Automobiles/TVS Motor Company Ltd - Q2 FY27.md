# TVS Motor Company Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-04 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY 27  
**Tone:** Positive – revenue up 44 % YoY, PAT up 52 % YoY and EBITDA margin improved 30 bps.

### Key numbers
- **Units sold:** 1.9 mn (+27 % YoY)  
- **Revenue:** ₹17,103 cr (+44 % YoY)  
- **EBITDA:** ₹2,219 cr (+47 % YoY) – **EBITDA margin 13 %** (+30 bps YoY)  
- **PBT:** ₹1,865 cr (+52 % YoY)  
- **PAT:** ₹1,377 cr (+52 % YoY)  
- **H1 FY 27 operating revenue:** ₹30,999 cr (+41 % YoY)  
- **H1 FY 27 PAT:** ₹2,551 cr (+52 % YoY)

### Highlights
- Domestic ICE 2‑wheelers +16 % vs industry +10 %; International ICE 2‑wheelers +33 % vs +27 %.  
- Domestic EV 2‑wheelers +129 % vs industry +84 %; 3‑wheelers EV share 44 % (48 % in Sep 2026).  
- International units 5.39 lakh (+35 % YoY) – growth led by Africa, LATAM, Asia.  
- Milestones: TVS Jupiter 9 mn cumulative units; TVS iQube 1 mn cumulative units (“MillionR” edition).  
- Board approved de‑merger of Consumer Finance (TVS Credit) into a separate listed entity.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management said the 2‑wheel‑er and 3‑wheel‑er momentum will **continue and out‑perform the industry in Q3 and Q4**.  
- Expect strong Diwali‑season sales, further product launches and international roll‑outs.  
- No quantitative FY 27‑28 guidance disclosed.

### Demand & industry commentary
- ICE 2‑wheelers total (domestic + international) +20 % vs industry +14 %.  
- 3‑wheelers (all‑fuel) +47 % vs industry +26 %; domestic 3‑wheelers +65 % to 26,300 units.  
- EV penetration (Sept 2026): 2‑wheelers 11.6 % of total 2W sales; scooters ≈22 %; 3‑wheelers ≈47 % EV share.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin rose to **13 %** (+30 bps YoY).  
- Material‑cost increase Q1→Q2: **1.9 %** YoY.  
- Selling‑price increase Q1→Q2: **1.6 %** YoY; “small” hikes planned for Oct‑Dec.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current EV production capacity (domestic + export) **≈60‑65k units**, expansion to **≈80k units** underway.  
- Ronin classic bike demand “very good”; >15k units targeted, capacity can be added in **≈3 months**.  
- Planned annual capacity: **>8 mn 2‑wheelers**, **≈0.4 mn 3‑wheelers**; upgrades already in progress.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- No specific debt or working‑capital numbers disclosed for TVS Motor.  
- Consumer‑finance de‑merger: TVS Credit net‑worth **₹6,500 cr**; proposed equity infusion **₹1,200 cr**; secondary purchase **₹711 cr** of shares.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Base‑effect** from GST correction could temper YoY growth.  
- **Supply‑chain constraints** on EV components remain a concern.  
- **Regulatory & shareholder approvals** needed for the de‑merger (timeline risk).  
- **Material‑cost inflation** (1.9 % YoY) could pressure margins if prolonged.
