# Tube Investments of India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-20 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY2027
**Tone:** Positive - Driven by strong volume growth across segments, highest-ever quarterly turnover in e-mobility, and bullish near-term demand outlook despite steel price and non-steel inflation pressures.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs. 2,366 crore vs Rs. 2,007 crore YoY
- **Standalone PBT:** Rs. 213 crore vs Rs. 222 crore YoY
- **Standalone Annualized ROIC:** 41% vs 39% YoY
- **Standalone Free Cash Flow:** Rs. 174 crore
- **Engineering Revenue:** Rs. 1,566 crore vs Rs. 1,298 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 153 crore (flat YoY)
- **Metal Formed Products Revenue:** Rs. 408 crore vs Rs. 366 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 28 crore vs Rs. 37 crore YoY
- **Mobility Revenue:** Rs. 250 crore vs Rs. 198 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 9 crore vs Rs. 7 crore YoY
- **Others Revenue:** Rs. 256 crore vs Rs. 236 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 21 crore vs Rs. 17 crore YoY
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 6,215 crore vs Rs. 5,309 crore YoY
- **Consolidated Profit (before share of JVs, exceptional items, and tax):** Rs. 461 crore vs Rs. 449 crore YoY
- **CG Power & Industrial Solutions Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 3,281 crore vs Rs. 2,878 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 423 crore vs Rs. 364 crore YoY
- **Shanthi Gears Revenue:** Rs. 115 crore vs Rs. 135 crore YoY; PBT: Rs. 14 crore vs Rs. 31 crore YoY
- **TI Clean Mobility Volumes (Q1):** HCV trucks: 86; Three-wheelers: 1,924; Small commercial vehicles (SCV): 347; Tractors: 22
- **Exports (% of TI revenue):** 14%
- **Capital Infusion Guidance:** Rs. 250 crore infused in Q4 FY26; another Rs. 250 crore planned in Q3 FY27; total Rs. 750 crore over time.
- **Capex Plan (FY27):** Core TI business ~Rs. 350 crore; Shanthi Gears ~Rs. 100 crore; Group total (ex-CG Power) Rs. 600-700 crore.

### Highlights
- Engineering segment recorded a 17% volume growth YoY.
- TI Medical core surgical business grew close to 20% YoY; acquired Medicura (IV cannulas) with revenue expected from Q2.
- CDMO (3xper) 200 KL intermediate capacity commissioned; validation batches underway, clean room commissioning expected in 30-40 days.
- TI Clean Mobility (TICM) recorded its highest-ever turnover of close to Rs. 240 crore, driven by scaling across HCV, SCV, 3-wheelers, and tractors.
- Export breakthrough achieved for 3-wheelers in Nepal (100+ units shipped); exploring Sri Lanka, Tanzania, and Ethiopia.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is confident of fully recovering steel price increases and non-steel inflation with a lag of 2-3 quarters, restoring margins.
- Guided for double-digit EBIT margin improvement and revenue growth in TI Medical core business.
- Cycle business margin improvement targeted at least 2 percentage points for the full year.
- Near-term (next 1-2 quarters) demand environment for core business is viewed as bullish.
- Expects to break even in one EV business this year and two more next financial year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Engineering demand was robust across segments and geographies with double-digit export growth.
- Metal Formed Products faced a drag from railways and Hyundai; however, other units saw volume-led growth.
- EV demand is gaining traction, particularly with NCR commercial vehicle policy mandates approaching.
- Cell prices for EVs are not cooling down due to removal of Chinese tax exemptions and growing BESS demand; TICM is pre-booking orders and locking prices.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins in Q1 were impacted by steel price increases in Q4 and Q1, and non-steel inflation (fuel, freight, West Asia crisis impacts).
- Full recovery of cost hikes is expected in coming quarters through customer price negotiations.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Western region Greenfield plant launch in MFP division is running delayed by six months.
- Naidupeta semi-commercial facility supplying commercial quantities for an OTC product in Europe.
- Jayem set up a battery assembly line for in-house EV consumption.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Free cash flow for the quarter was Rs. 174 crore.
- Rs. 250 crore infused into new ventures in Q4 FY26, with another Rs. 250 crore tranche planned in Q3 FY27 out of the total Rs. 750 crore roadmap.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations could pose hurdles to export momentum.
- Cell supply bottlenecks and high cell prices are expected to persist for the next 2-3 quarters.
- Railway segment and Hyundai volumes acted as drags on specific product lines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Joseph George (IIFL Capital) on margin compression and steel price lags:** Management confirmed that steel price increases are recovered with a 2-3 quarter lag and assured full cumulative recovery including Q1.
- **Salil Desai (Marcellus Investment) on EV loss reduction drivers:** Jalaj Gupta explained that all four EV product lines saw strong volume traction (especially 3-wheelers recovering from Q4 supply issues), driving down losses directionally.
- **Ketan Sanghvi on external EV investors:** AN Meyyappan clarified that CCPS conversion for external investors will happen only at the time of an IPO, with valuations determined by market conditions at that future date.
- **Harshvardhan Kothari on cell pricing bottlenecks:** Management acknowledged cell prices are not cooling down due to removal of Chinese tax exemptions and BESS demand, but noted they are mitigating via long-lead pre-bookings and price locks.
- **Anupam Gupta on Orange Koi acquisition:** Management stated Orange Koi is a small startup acquired for additive manufacturing (metal injection molding) capabilities, and guidance will be provided in two quarters.

### One-line takeaway
Tube Investments of India delivered a resilient Q1 FY27 with consolidated revenue of Rs. 6,215 crore, underpinned by robust engineering volumes and record EV mobility traction, while management expects margin recovery from steel cost lags in upcoming quarters.
