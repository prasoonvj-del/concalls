# Hero Motors Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-20 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, margin expansion, and a significant reduction in debt following IPO proceeds.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 318 crore, up 27% YoY (from Rs 251 crore)
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 47 crore, up 35% YoY; margin at 14.4% (+99 bps YoY)
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 24 crore, up 49% YoY; margin at 7.3% (+112 bps YoY)
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 365 crore, up 21% YoY (from Rs 301 crore)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 47 crore, up 23% YoY; margin at 12.8% (+22 bps YoY)
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 18 crore, up 62% YoY (Note: our record states +65%); margin at 4.9% (+125 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** Rs 596 crore (+28% YoY); EBITDA Rs 92 crore (+57% YoY, margin 15.2%); PAT Rs 47 crore (+92% YoY, margin 7.8%)
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 684 crore (+23% YoY); EBITDA Rs 87 crore (+32% YoY, margin 12.5%); PAT Rs 28 crore (+84% YoY, margin 4%)
- **Segment Revenue (Q2 FY27):** Alloy and Metallics Rs 174 crore (+24% YoY, 48% share); Gears and Transmission Rs 142 crore (+16% YoY, 39% share); Bike Powertrain Rs 48 crore (+27% YoY, 13% share)
- **EV & Hybrid Contribution:** 26% of total revenues in Q2 FY27 (vs 20% in Q2 FY26)
- **Debt Repayment:** Rs 190 crore repaid via IPO proceeds, reducing total loans from ~Rs 410 crore to ~Rs 220 crore. Debt-to-equity improved from 0.89x in FY26 to 0.21x in H1 FY27.
- **Order Book Pipeline:** ~Rs 4000 crore lifecycle revenue across 5 to 8 years (yielding ~Rs 500-600 crore annually).

### Highlights
- First earnings conference call post-IPO.
- Powertrain solutions now account for ~52% of total revenue.
- Won a Formula 2 racing program project to design 6-speed transmissions, integrated starter systems, and advanced differentials (supplies starting late this year).
- Setting up a new EV system manufacturing facility in Bengaluru, targeted to be operational by March/April 2027 (revenue potential of Rs 60-80 crore in FY28 and ~Rs 200 crore in FY29). River is a major anchor customer.
- *Continuity note:* The record shows Q2 FY27 net profit at Rs 18 crore (+65%), whereas the call transcript states Rs 18 crore (+62%).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects full-year revenue growth of 20%+ YoY with higher EBITDA margins compared to last year.
- Steady-state EBITDA margins projected at 14%-15%.
- Full ramp-up of the ~Rs 500-600 crore annual order book expected by FY28, with initial execution starting this year.
- Subsidiaries are expected to break even over the next 6 months to 1 year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global powertrain demand remains strong, particularly in European and US markets for premium motorcycles, performance cars, and electric bicycles (via Enviolo partnership).
- Transitioning away from pure components toward complete system solutions and design-to-cost models.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gears and Transmission segment EBITDA margins range between 20% to 25% (some projects up to 30%).
- Alloy and Metallics segment EBITDA margins are lower at 6% to 7%, but management aims to raise this via high-precision forgings (15%-20% EBITDA margin).
- Quarterly interest costs expected to drop significantly from Rs 8-9 crore down to Rs 1.5-2 crore due to debt repayment from IPO proceeds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Historical annual capex run-rate is Rs 125-150 crore, supplemented by Rs 200 crore allocated from IPO proceeds over the next 2 years.
- Munjal STP joint venture (forging and machining facility) targeted to commence commercial production in Calendar Year 2027; total capex expected to be roughly half of the planned Rs 350 crore growth capex.
- Capacity utilization: Gears and Transmission operates at 70-75%; Alloy and Metallics operates at 70-75%; Bike Powertrain has significant headroom for growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total loans reduced to ~Rs 220 crore after repaying Rs 190 crore from IPO funds.
- Debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.21x in H1 FY27 from 0.89x in FY26.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions (e.g., war situation) caused slight delays in the execution timelines for the STP facility.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ronak Mehta (ICICI Securities) on order book visibility:** Management clarified that the ~Rs 4000 crore lifecycle order book comprises confirmed orders or high-visibility projects under New Product Development (NPD), with annual revenue realization of Rs 500-600 crore ramping up fully by FY28.
2. **Ronak Mehta on Marine application orders:** Management stated the initial design fee is Rs 5-6 crore, but successful validation could lead to large-scale serial production orders.
3. **Smith (Analyst) on subsidiary drag on consolidated margins:** Management explained that consolidated margins (12.5%) are lower than standalone margins (15.2%) due to investments in newly established subsidiaries (ESYNC, EDU, HYM), which are expected to break even over the next 6 to 12 months.
4. **Ayan Satyaki (Analyst) on capacity utilization:** Management noted Gears & Transmission and Alloy & Metallics operate at 70-75% capacity utilization, while Bike Powertrain operates at low utilization with substantial headroom for growth.
5. **Digant Shah (Analyst) on Alloy & Metallics growth:** Management attributed the 24% YoY growth to new domestic customers, new product additions, and initial sales to the European market.

### One-line takeaway
Hero Motors delivered robust H1 FY27 performance with 23% consolidated revenue growth and 84% PAT growth, significantly de-leveraging its balance sheet using IPO proceeds while scaling up high-margin powertrain systems.
