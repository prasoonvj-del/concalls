# Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consolidated revenue and PAT grew 25% YoY with 21% ROE despite raw material inflation and farm sector headwinds.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Cut domestic tractor full-year industry volume growth guidance from +5% to flattish (0%). SUV growth maintained at mid-to-high teens; LCV industry growth revised slightly upwards to double digits.
- Tone and demand view: Auto/SUV demand remains extremely strong and capacity-constrained. Farm sector tone turned cautious due to worsening monsoon deficit (87% LPA) in Maharashtra and Karnataka.
- New topics or risks: Severe monsoon deficit in South/West regions, reservoir deficits, and lower Kharif yield expectations. International farm subsidiary exit costs/losses are completing (Sampo and Erkunt completed; Mitsubishi Ag in Q4).
- Last quarter's promises: Auto margin expanded sequentially from Q1 levels (standalone Auto PBIT up to 9.8% vs 8.9% in Q1; consolidated to 8.8% vs 7.7%). ICE+EV capacity hit exit targets (surpassed 70,000/month in October).

### Key numbers
- Consolidated Revenue: Rs 57,553 crore, up 25% YoY.
- Consolidated PAT: Rs 4,588 crore, up 25% YoY.
- Year-to-date PAT: up 29% YoY.
- ROE: 21% (target remains 18%).
- Auto Revenue: up 55% YoY. Standalone Auto PBIT Margin: 9.8% (up from 8.9% in Q1). Consolidated Auto PBIT Margin: 8.8%.
- Farm Revenue: down 9% YoY.
- Other Business Profits YoY: Tech M PAT up 33%; Mahindra Finance PAT up 61%; Growth Gems profits up 3x; Accelo profits up 70%.
- Price Hikes Taken: 5% in Auto, 4% in Farm.
- PLI Incentive: Rs 1,000 crore booked in Q2.

### Highlights
- Automotive business set record monthly dispatches (>73,000 units in Oct including 8,000 EVs) operating at full capacity.
- XEV 9S remained the highest-selling EV in Q2; EV penetration stood at 12% of total SUV portfolio in H1.
- Electric 3-wheeler (Last Mile Mobility) segment reached 45-50% electrification in L5M category.
- Aerospace business won a major Airbus A320neo fuselage section contract; orders expanded 28x this decade toward a target of $1 billion annual orders by FY30.
- Restructuring of loss-making international farm entities completed for Sampo and Erkunt Foundry; Mitsubishi Ag closure scheduled for Q4 FY27.

### Guidance & outlook
- Full-Year Domestic Tractor Industry Growth: Guidance cut to flattish (0%) from +5%.
- Mahindra SUV Volume Growth: Maintained at mid-to-high teens YoY.
- LCV Industry Growth: Revised upward slightly to double digits.
- Product Pipeline: 5 major launches planned by June 2027 (first NU_IQ platform SUV, INGLO EV, Scorpio Lifestyler, 1 major refresh, 1 LCV).
- Financial Commitments: Group EPS CAGR target of 15%-20% and ROE target of 18%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- SUV order book and demand remain robust across Scorpio N, 7XO, Thar OG, Roxx, and EVs, with dealer stock at minimal levels.
- Powertrain Shift: Consumers shifting from petrol toward CNG, EV, and diesel options.
- Farm Demand Divergence: Severe drought in Maharashtra and Karnataka dragging overall domestic tractor growth; Northern states (UP, Haryana, Rajasthan) remain resilient. Non-agricultural income now represents ~70-75% of rural household income.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- YoY Raw Material Inflation: Steel up 17%, Copper up 42%, Rubber up 40%.
- Q3 RM Outlook: Marginal upticks expected in steel and rubber; precious metals moderating.
- Sequential margin expansion in Auto driven by price hikes, product mix, operational leverage, and PLI incentives.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Auto Capacity Roadmap: 64,500/month (current start), scaling to 82,000/month by FY27 exit, 92,000/month in FY28, and reaching 132,000/month by FY31.
- EV Capacity: Expanding from 8,000/month to 12,000/month by Feb-March.
- Greenfield Expansion: Phase 1 land acquisition commenced for the Nagpur manufacturing facility (planning 1,500-2,000 acres land footprint including a supplier park).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash balance increased over H1 FY27 despite record dividend payouts, supported by strong operating cash flows.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Monsoon deficit (87% of LPA) causing agricultural stress in key southern and western states.
- Raw material price inflation across steel, copper, and rubber.
- Severe capacity constraints limiting immediate delivery volumes on high-demand SUV and LCV models.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kapil (Nomura):** Are current capacity plans sufficient given persistent waiting periods?
  - *Management:* Auto capacity has grown 4x from 17,000/month 5 years ago to 66,000/month, exiting FY27 at 82,000/month. The 1,500-2,000 acre Nagpur plant provides space for rapid future doubling.
- **Yogesh (HSBC):** Will falling crude prices slow EV adoption, and what is the battery localization strategy?
  - *Management:* Fuel savings of Rs 1.5-2 lakh/year outweigh minor crude fluctuations. Battery packs are localized; cell sourcing remains a "make vs buy" decision based on global cost competitiveness.
- **Binay:** How will EV dilution and sub-4m models impact vehicle margins?
  - *Management:* Sub-4m models already constitute 40-45% of the portfolio with strong margins. PLI contributed ~Rs 1,000 crore in Q2; long-term EV margins will converge with ICE through scale, localization, and operating leverage.
- **Gunjan:** Is rural distress spreading beyond tractors into LCVs and SUVs?
  - *Management:* Slowdown is isolated to tractor agricultural usage in deficit states (Maharashtra, Karnataka). Non-agri rural income (~75%) keeps overall rural consumption and LCV infrastructure demand strong.

### One-line takeaway
M&M demonstrated strong operational resilience with 25% consolidated profit growth and expanding auto margins, offsetting farm sector drag through proactive price hikes, capacity expansion, and strategic growth gem contributions.
