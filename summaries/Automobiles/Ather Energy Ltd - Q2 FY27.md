# Ather Energy Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Stellar quarter with broad-based industry growth, new product/ecosystem launches, record capacity expansion, and the first-time achievement of positive EBITDA and PAT.

### Key numbers
- **Industry registrations:** 6 lakh sales, up 82% YoY.
- **E2W penetration:** Reached 11.2% (or 11.6% mentioned later) for the full quarter.
- **Top-of-funnel metrics:** Ather searches up 94% YoY; inquiries up 109% YoY (crossing 10 lakh); preorders up 108% YoY (hitting 1.8 lakh).
- **Wholesale volume:** 93,000 units, up 42% YoY (from 65,000 units).
- **Registrations:** 92,500 units, up ~60% YoY (from 57,000 units).
- **Manufacturing capacity:** Averaged 33,000 units/month in Q2 FY27 (up from 28,000 units/month in Q1).
- **Weighted average ex-showroom price:** INR 164,000 in Q2 FY27 (up INR 14,000 from INR 150,000 in Q4 FY26).
- **AtherStack Pro attach rate:** 94% for Q2 FY27.
- **Non-vehicle revenue:** 15% overall.
- **Gross margin improvement:** 5.3% improvement from structural changes (price hikes, VAVE, cost reductions) compared to Q4 FY26; commodity inflation hit gross margins by 9.4%; SKU management/incentives added 2.7%.
- **Adjusted Gross Margin (AGM):** 24% in Q2 FY27 (1.6% improvement over Q1 FY27); H1 FY27 AGM at 23%.
- **EBITDA margin:** Improved 1,450 bps YoY from -9.6% in Q2 FY26 to +4.9% in Q2 FY27. Positive EBITDA (~1% to 0.8%) even without other income.
- **PAT:** Registered positive PAT for the first time.
- **Capacity plans:** Hosur adding incremental 6,000 units/month this quarter; AURIC Phase 1 adding incremental 12,000 units/month by Q2 FY28, taking total installed capacity to 11.4 lakh units by Q2 FY28.

### Highlights
- Launched Ather Community Day (ACD) 2026 with ~6,000 attendees.
- Launched **Ather TrueHealth** (component health scores and 3+ year estimated resale value on the mobile app).
- Launched **Ather Node** (apartment EV charging infrastructure with smart load management and automatic queuing).
- Launched **Ather Bedrock** (5th-gen battery packs with micro leak detection, 5x faster cell balancing, and 10-year/1-lakh km warranty with 70% performance guarantee).
- Launched mainstream product **Konarc** (14-inch front wheels, long rear suspension, metal body, 1-year service interval, AirWalk, MagicKey, AutoPop, and AeBS).
- Staggered Konarc rollout started in UP and Rajasthan; expanding to MP, Bihar, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Punjab, and Southern markets later via AURIC.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the industry to remain supply-constrained and healthy in coming quarters.
- AURIC Phase 1 trial production starting in next few weeks; complete capex and capacity installation expected by Q4 FY27.
- Total installed capacity target revised up to 11.4 lakh units by Q2 FY28 (via an extra 6,000 units/month at Hosur starting this quarter and an extra 12,000 units/month at AURIC by Q2 FY28).
- Management does not plan further price hikes on the current portfolio over the next few quarters.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry E2W penetration crossed 10% for the first time at a quarterly level (11.2%).
- Growth is broad-based across zones: South India up 82%, Middle India up 85%, Rest of India up 79%.
- Demand remains strong with roughly 20,000 units/month of unrealized retail due to supply constraints.
- Tier 2 city penetration is rising faster than Tier 1 or Tier 3 (e.g., Maharashtra Tier 2 penetration at 18%-19%).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity pressures (aluminum, copper, plastics, memory) were worse than estimated, creating a 9.4% hit to gross margins.
- Average ex-showroom price increased by INR 14,000 to INR 164,000 in Q2 FY27.
- 5.3% gross margin improvement driven by sustainable structural price hikes and VAVE cost reductions.
- 2.7% margin boost driven by temporary SKU management and incentive pullbacks, which can be used as a lever if supply constraints or high commodities persist.
- Non-vehicle revenue margins: Accessories at 30%-40%, spares at 40%-45%, AtherStack Pro at 80%-90%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Hosur plant capacity capped at 35,000/month, adding an immediate incremental 6,000 units/month this quarter.
- AURIC Phase 1 base capacity of 42,000 units/month going live in Q4 FY27, plus an additional 12,000 units/month by Q2 FY28, bringing total capacity to 77,000 units/month initially and 11.4 lakh units annually by Q2 FY28.
- Network expansion to restart aggressively toward the end of Q3 FY27/Q4 FY27 as supply catches up.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- *No specific debt, working capital, or cash balance figures were discussed in the transcript.*

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Commodities (aluminum, copper, plastics, and memory) remained elevated longer than expected, impacting gross margins by 9.4%.
- Demand currently outstrips supply, leading to unrealized retail of roughly 20,000 units per month.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amyn Pirani (JPMorgan) on capacity ramp-up timelines:** Management stated Hosur's extra 6,000 units will ramp up later this quarter, while AURIC Phase 1 base capacity (42,000) will take 2 quarters, and the additional 12,000 units will ramp up through FY28.
2. **Amyn Pirani (JPMorgan) on pricing levers given high commodities:** Management stated they will not push prices up on the current portfolio over the next few quarters, relying instead on Konarc's more aggressive entry portfolio and better unit economics to improve margins without raising prices.
3. **Kapil Singh (Nomura) on Konarc bookings and cannibalization:** Management noted Konarc had only 1 month of sales in Q2 (<10,000 units), but early response and inquiries in the North are very strong, noting Rizta may need to make space as Konarc scales.
4. **Gunjan Prithyani (BofA) on the 2.7% SKU/incentive margin benefit:** Management clarified that the 2.7% is a temporary/lever-based benefit (unlike the 5.3% structural gain) that can be adjusted if supply-demand conditions or commodities normalize.
5. **Krupashankar NJ (Avendus) on dealer network expansion:** Management confirmed that hundreds of LOIs were held up due to supply constraints, but network expansion will restart significantly from the end of Q3/Q4 FY27.

### One-line takeaway
Ather delivered a stellar Q2 FY27 with 42% wholesale volume growth, positive EBITDA and PAT for the first time, and aggressive capacity expansion plans up to 11.4 lakh units, despite severe commodity cost headwinds.
