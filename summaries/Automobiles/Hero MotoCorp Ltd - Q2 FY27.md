# Hero MotoCorp Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong revenue and net profit growth, market share expansion in ICE & EV segments, and positive gross margins in the EV business ahead of guidance.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 14,687 crore, up 21% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 1,968 crore, up 8% YoY; EBITDA margin at 13.4% (+10 bps QoQ).
- **Standalone PAT:** Rs 1,456 crore, up 5% YoY.
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 14,971 crore, up 23% YoY.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 1,861 crore, up 41% YoY (includes Rs 397 crore one-time gain from associate equity consolidation).
- **Wholesale Volume:** Up 10.5% YoY (ICE +8.4%, EV +98%).
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 2,159 crore in Q2 FY27.
- **Parts Business Revenue:** Up 27% YoY in Q2 FY27.
- **EV Segment (VIDA) Revenue:** Rs 1,000 crore in Q2 FY27; EBITDA drag reduced to Rs 168 crore (from Rs 232 crore in Q1).

### Highlights
- ICE VAHAN market share expanded every month during Q2 FY27, with domestic ICE retail growing 24% YoY.
- ICE scooter dispatches grew 34% YoY in Q2 (>50% in H1 FY27); scooter mix in total business increased by 3 percentage points over 2 years.
- 100cc motorcycle segment grew 10% YoY in Q2; Splendor grew >20% YoY, with Splendor XTEC contributing >25% of Splendor volume.
- VIDA EV business achieved gross margin positive status in Q2 FY27 ahead of guidance, aided by Rs 63 crore PLI accruals (~70% of portfolio covered in Q2, 100% certified in October 2026).
- Xpulse retail volume grew >30% YoY in H1 FY27 with >35% market share in the adventure motorcycle segment.
- Dispatches of flex-fuel vehicles crossed ~15,000 units within two months of rollout.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Industry Growth:** Management expects mid to high single-digit volume growth for the 2-wheeler industry in H2 FY27 (ICE low single digits, EV higher).
- **Margin Corridor:** Management expects stable EBITDA margins in Q3 FY27 and maintains a medium-term EBITDA margin corridor target of 14%–16%.
- **Product Pipeline:** 3 to 4 major product launches planned in the premium bike segment for the remainder of FY27 across Xtreme, Xpulse, Karizma, and Harley-Davidson platforms.
- **Exports:** Expects dispatch growth in global markets to return to double digits in Q3 and Q4 FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand growth is broad-based; rural market growth was equal to or better than urban performance in Q2 and QTD.
- Festival period retail growth (across a 100-day window) is currently running at 24% YoY.
- Two-year industry CAGR stands at 11%, demonstrating healthy structural growth beyond post-GST pent-up demand.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Material cost inflation was ~1% QoQ in Q2 (~5.5% cumulative over H1 FY27, half of which was offset by calibrated price hikes).
- Took a price hike of ~Rs 1,000 per unit on October 1, 2026.
- ICE EBITDA margins remained stable QoQ at 15.6%; EV drag at the corporate level stood at ~220 bps.
- Discretionary cost controls, operating leverage, and LEAP cost-saving programs offset commodity price headwinds (steel, aluminum, plastics, rubber, freight).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Capex Guidance:** Maintained full-year FY27 capex guidance at Rs 1,500 crore (Rs 600 crore spent in H1 FY27).
- **EV Capacity:** VIDA monthly capacity doubled to 30,000 units/month in September 2026; targeting 45,000 units/month by Q4 FY27 and >80,000 units/month by Q2 FY28.
- **ICE Scooter Capacity:** Doubled to 80,000 units/month.
- **Parts Center Expansion:** Investing over Rs 700 crore to set up Global Parts Center 2.0 at Tirupati to double parts handling capacity (expected operational by end of CY27).
- **International Expansion:** Setting up a manufacturing facility in Brazil and expanding distribution into Mexico and Africa.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows doubled YoY to Rs 2,159 crore in Q2 FY27 due to working capital optimization and record low receivables in the preceding quarter.
- Dealer inventory in EV/select scooter lines remains low (~1 day stock), reflecting supply-constrained conditions.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia leading to fuel price hikes in overseas markets (e.g., Bangladesh).
- Short-term logistical and customs delays in key export markets like Colombia following a major earthquake.
- Persistent commodity inflation across key raw materials and currency depreciation.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amyn Pirani (JPMorgan):** *Query on high concentration in specific export markets like Colombia (25% exposure) and recovery timeline.*  
  **Management Answer:** Confirmed Colombia accounts for ~25% of exports and faced earthquake-related customs/port delays; however, retail demand remained strong at 19% growth, port operations normalized, and new market entry in Brazil, Mexico, and Africa will diversify exposure.
- **Gunjan Prithyani (Bank of America):** *Asked for EV revenue, EBITDA drag details, and commodity inflation impact.*  
  **Management Answer:** Q2 EV revenue was Rs 1,000 crore, EV EBITDA drag reduced to Rs 168 crore (down Rs 70 crore QoQ), gross margin turned positive with Rs 63 crore PLI benefit, and commodity inflation was ~1% QoQ.
- **Pramod Kumar (UBS):** *Asked whether market share expansion or 14%+ margins is the primary priority.*  
  **Management Answer:** Affirmed growth and market share expansion as the top priority; margin targets will be pursued via LEAP BOM savings, volume leverage, and EV unit economics rather than cutting brand investments or discretionary growth spend.
- **Raghunandhan (Nuvama):** *Asked about EV market share strategy and distribution expansion.*  
  **Management Answer:** Currently selling VIDA in ~700 outlets (450 regular supplies) against an 8,500 total touchpoint network, offering huge headroom to expand distribution; premium variants (4.4 kW) now constitute ~40% of VIDA sales mix.

### One-line takeaway
Hero MotoCorp demonstrated strong operational performance with 21% top-line growth, market share gains in ICE scooters/motorcycles, and early profitability milestones in its VIDA EV business.
