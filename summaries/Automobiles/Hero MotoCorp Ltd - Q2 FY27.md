# Hero MotoCorp Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong top-line growth, market share gains in ICE and EV, and VIDA turning gross margin positive.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue (Standalone):** Rs 14,687 crore (up 21% YoY)
- **Revenue (Consolidated):** Rs 14,971 crore (up 23% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 1,968 crore (up 8% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 13.4% (up 10 bps QoQ)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT - Standalone):** Rs 1,456 crore (up 5% YoY)
- **Profit After Tax (PAT - Consolidated):** Rs 1,861 crore (up 41% YoY)
- **H1 FY27 Revenue Growth:** 28% YoY
- **H1 FY27 PAT Growth:** 16% YoY
- **Total Wholesale Volume Growth:** 10.5% YoY (ICE up 8.4%, EV up 98%)
- **EV Retail Volume (VIDA):** 66,000 units (up 74% YoY)
- **EV Revenue:** Rs 1,000 crore
- **EV EBITDA Drag:** Rs 168 crore (down from Rs 232 crore in Q1)
- **PLI Accruals (VIDA):** Rs 63 crore (approx. 6.5% of EV revenue)
- **Global Parts Center 2.0 Greenfield Capex:** Over Rs 700 crore
- **H1 FY27 Capex Deployed:** Close to Rs 600 crore; full-year guidance maintained at Rs 1,500 crore
- **Price Hike (October 1):** Approx. Rs 1,000 per unit

### Highlights
- Gained VAHAN market share in the ICE portfolio in every month of Q2.
- Scooter ICE dispatches grew 34% YoY in Q2 and over 50% for H1. ICE scooter market share in South India crossed 10%.
- Splendor XTEC variant crossed 25% of Splendor volumes.
- VIDA EV retail grew 105% YoY in H1; gained 170 bps market share. VIDA business turned gross margin positive.
- VIDA EV manufacturing capacity doubled to 30,000 units/month in September; targeting 45,000 units/month in Q4 and over 80,000 units/month by Q2 of next financial year.
- Nearly 100% of the EV portfolio received PLI certification in October 2026, which will translate into ~13% revenue benefit starting Q3.
- Parts business revenue grew 27% YoY (second consecutive quarter of >25% growth). Announced greenfield expansion of Global Parts Center 2.0 at Tirupati.
- Carved out a dedicated Premium Business Unit with a new CBO and refreshed Xtreme 160R and Xtreme 250R. Premium franchise recorded >30% retail growth in H1 FY27 with >35% market share in the adventure bike segment.
- Consolidated PAT includes a Rs 397 crore one-time gain from the equity consolidation of an associate company following its capital raise.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the industry to deliver mid to high single-digit growth during the festival period.
- For H2, industry growth is projected at mid to high single digits (low single digit for ICE, high single digit driven by EV).
- Management expects Q3 margins to remain stable despite persistent input cost inflation.
- Medium-term EBITDA margin target corridor remains 14% to 16%.
- Full-year capex guidance maintained at Rs 1,500 crore.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Q2 2-wheeler industry VAHAN retail grew by over 27% (ICE up 23%, EV up 82%).
- Over a 2-year period, industry retail grew at an 11% CAGR.
- Rural demand is performing equally or better than urban demand. Sowing is on par with last year despite localized drought situations.
- 100cc category grew 10% in Q2 and 11% in H1; Splendor grew >20%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Industry faced broad-based commodity inflation across steel, aluminum, plastic, rubber, and freight, amplified by currency depreciation (material price inflation of ~1% QoQ in Q2).
- Mitigated costs via premiumization, LEAP cost savings, curtailed discretionary spending, operating leverage, judicious pricing, and improved EV unit economics.
- Gross margin compressed by 30 bps QoQ, but strict overhead control and operating leverage expanded EBITDA margin by 10 bps QoQ to 13.4%.
- ICE portfolio EBITDA margins were stable sequentially at 15.6%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Deployed ~Rs 600 crore in H1 out of the Rs 1,500 crore full-year capex guidance.
- Expanded Destini and Xoom capacities; total ICE scooter capacity is now close to 80,000 units/month.
- EV capacity scaled to 30,000 units/month, targeting 45,000 in Q4 and >80,000 by Q2 of next financial year.
- Expanded dedicated premium assembly line at Neemrana.
- Advancing Phase II expansion of Global Parts Center 2.0 at Tirupati (operational by end of next calendar year).
- Expanding international footprint in Brazil (including a manufacturing facility and flex-fuel portfolio), Mexico, and Africa (launches starting this month).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash flow from operations in Q2 doubled YoY to Rs 2,159 crores, driven by working capital optimization.
- Dealer inventory is low at approximately 1 day.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global business faced logistical challenges and custom clearance issues in a key Latin American market due to a devastating earthquake (Colombia represents ~25% of exports).
- Demand slowdown in Bangladesh due to a sharp fuel price increase from the ongoing Gulf War.
- Broad-based commodity inflation and currency depreciation creating input cost pressures.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amyn Pirani (JPMorgan) on export concentration and recovery:** Management stated Colombia and Bangladesh each constitute ~25% of exports and faced temporary headwinds (earthquake and fuel price hikes), but retail momentum remains strong (up 19%), and wholesale recovery is expected in Q3.
- **Gunjan Prithyani (BofA) on EV revenue, drag, and PLI:** Management clarified EV revenue was Rs 1,000 crore with an EBITDA drag of Rs 168 crore. PLI benefit will scale to ~13% of EV revenue starting Q3 as 100% of the portfolio is now certified.
- **Pramod Kumar (UBS) on growth vs. margin trade-off:** Management emphasized they are not compromising on growth investments; margin recovery will be driven by volume leverage, LEAP cost savings, and EV unit economics rather than cutting growth investments.
- **Raghunandhan (Nuvama) on EV market share and mix:** Management noted VIDA retail is operating through ~700 outlets (supplies to ~450) out of 8,500 total points of sale, leaving massive headroom for expansion. The top-end 4.4 kW variant pulls close to 40% of the VIDA portfolio.
- **Yash Agarwal (Nirmal Bang) on flex-fuel vehicles:** Management stated they have dispatched ~15,000 flex-fuel vehicles in a little over 2 months in a controlled launch, with customers adopting them even in E20 regions for future-proofing.

### One-line takeaway
Hero MotoCorp delivered a strong Q2 with 21% revenue growth, market share gains across ICE and EV segments, and VIDA turning gross margin positive ahead of schedule, despite commodity inflation and temporary export headwinds.
