# Eicher Motors Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Eicher Motors delivered record Q1 financial performance driven by highest-ever quarterly sales across both Royal Enfield and VECV.

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 6,632 crore, up 32% YoY compared to Rs 5,042 crore in Q1 FY26.
* **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 1,591 crore compared to Rs 1,203 crore in Q1 FY26.
* **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 1,463 crore, up 21% YoY from Rs 1,205 crore in Q1 FY26 (includes Rs 168 crore share of profits from VECV).
* **Royal Enfield Volumes:** Highest-ever quarterly sales of 332,940 motorcycles (Domestic: 301,174; International: 31,766) vs 261,326 in Q1 FY26.
* **VECV Revenue:** Rs 6,610 crore vs Rs 5,671 crore in Q1 FY26.
* **VECV EBITDA:** Rs 541 crore (margin of 8.4% vs 9.2% YoY).
* **VECV PAT:** Rs 300 crore vs Rs 288 crore in Q1 FY26.
* **VECV Volumes:** Record Q1 sales of 24,815 units, up 14.8% YoY (Heavy-duty: 5,275 units, up 15.2%; Light/Medium-duty: 9,993 units; Buses: 6,126 units; Volvo trucks/buses: 598 units; SCV Pro X: 1,041 units including 172 EVs; Exports: 1,450 units, up 14.7%).

### Highlights
* **Royal Enfield EV Launch:** Commenced customer deliveries for Flying Flea C6 (electric motorcycle) in Bengaluru; expanding to 10 local touchpoints before expanding city-by-city.
* **New Launches & Variants:** Launched Bullet 650, Guerrilla 450 APEX variant, and Hunter 350 updated colorways/variants.
* **International Milestones:** RE international quarterly revenue crossed Rs 1,000 crore for the first time, contributing ~15% to overall revenues. Achieved #2 market position in middleweight motorcycles in Brazil and Argentina.
* **Non-Vehicle Revenue:** Allied businesses (spares, apparel, accessories, services) reached ~15% of total revenues. Accessories penetration reached 87%. Averaging 9 lakh service job cards per month (+20% YoY).

### Guidance & outlook
* Management is optimistic about growth momentum continuing into upcoming quarters and the festive season.
* Strategic rollout of EV segment (Flying Flea) will follow a phased, city-by-city approach across 6 key target markets.
* Management expects international markets to sustain growth, backed by local CKD assembly investments and expanded footprint.

### Demand & industry commentary
* **Domestic Demand:** Funnel indicators (bookings, walk-ins, inquiries) are growing faster than domestic volume growth (+32% YoY). The 350cc portfolio (Classic, Bullet, Hunter, Meteor) grew 34% YoY in Q1.
* **Higher Displacement (450cc & 650cc):** Volumes returned to pre-GST levels (Guerrilla 450 at ~2,500/month; 650 Twins at ~4,000-4,200/month).
* **Demographics:** First-time buyers account for ~25% of overall RE sales (Hunter share of first-time buyers is ~50%), upgraders account for 70%+, and RE repeat buyers account for 5-6%.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* **Input Costs:** Raw material price inflation (aluminum, steel, copper, crude oil, precious metals) created a net cost impact of 4.0% to 4.5% in Q1.
* **Mitigation Measures:** Price hikes of 1.75% taken on majority of 350cc models in April 2026 yielded a 1.2% offset; value engineering added 0.4%. Input costs showed slight softening toward the end of Q1.
* **Other Expenses:** Declined 7% YoY due to ad spend control and high base from Q4 marketing spends (Cricket World Cup ad campaigns of Rs 20 crore and vehicle launch spends of Rs 10 crore).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Greenfield Expansion (Tada, Andhra Pradesh):** Board approved Phase 1 capex of Rs 1,225 crore to create 4.5 lakh units/year capacity, targeted for completion by FY30. Total long-term capacity target is 2.45 million units.
* **Brownfield Expansion (Cheyyar, Tamil Nadu):** Current build capacity stands at 1.5 million units/year (running three shifts). Cheyyar brownfield project (Rs 958 crore approved in Feb 2026) will scale total annual capacity to 2 million units by FY28.
* **Daily Run Rate:** Reached 5,000+ units per day delivery run-rate in July 2026.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* **Channel Inventory:** Extremely lean at 10 to 12 days at the dealer level (plus ~5 days in-transit). 
* **Supply Chain Measures:** Escalating direct dealer billing (to reduce 4-5 days of transit time) and running suppliers on 24/7 3-shift schedules to build channel stock for the festive season.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Macroeconomic uncertainties, geopolitical headwinds, and lingering trade/tariff barriers in select export markets (e.g., ASEAN high luxury taxes above 250cc like 160%+ in Indonesia, quota caps).
* Channel inventory remaining exceptionally lean ahead of the festive season peak due to high demand and recent supply chain bottlenecks (manpower/LPG issues resolved in Q1).

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Gunjan Prithyani (BofA):** *Status of plant capacity, manpower/LPG supply issues, and channel stock levels?*  
  **Management:** Production hit 4,500/day (1.16 lakh units) in June after converting LPG to PNG and fixing labor shortages. Current capacity stands at ~1.5 million/year with 5,000+ daily deliveries. Dealer inventory is lean at 10-12 days; increasing direct billing to streamline transit.
* **Kapil Singh (Nomura):** *Initial customer profile and strategy for Flying Flea EV?*  
  **Management:** Deliveries exceeded 100 units in 2 months in Bengaluru with 29,000 km logged. Customer age averages 25–30 years with strong interest from existing RE owners. Expanding to 10 stores in Bengaluru before entering 6 identified cities.
* **Chandramouli Muthiah (Goldman Sachs):** *How is RE positioning for post-GST base comparison from October onwards?*  
  **Management:** Middleweight market expanded from 70,000 to 120,000 monthly units industrywide. RE volume expanded from 61,000 to 101,000 monthly units. Funnel is strong, and growth will be sustained via new variants, marketing activation, and financing/assured buyback levers.
* **Pramod Kumar (UBS):** *Any plans to hive off and list VECV separately?*  
  **Management:** At Eicher Motors level, there are no crystallized plans for a 50:50 structure change or separate listing of VECV; focus remains on execution, EV, and commercial vehicle opportunities.
* **Amyn Pirani (JP Morgan):** *Why did depreciation increase significantly in Q1 FY27?*  
  **Management:** Capitalization of assets linked to the Flying Flea launch and start of production, along with an increase in average gross block (~Rs 346 crore) and tooling/die depreciation.

### One-line takeaway
Eicher Motors delivered strong Q1 FY27 results with record sales across Royal Enfield and VECV, backed by new product momentum, margin mitigation, and long-term capacity expansion to 2.45 million units.
