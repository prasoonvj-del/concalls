# Eicher Motors Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-04 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Both Royal Enfield and VECV registered their highest-ever quarterly sales, with consolidated revenue growing 32% YoY.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 6,632 crore, up 32% YoY from Rs 5,042 crore (Q1 FY26).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 1,591 crore, up 32% YoY from Rs 1,203 crore (Q1 FY26).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 1,463 crore, up 21% YoY from Rs 1,205 crore (Q1 FY26).
- **Royal Enfield Sales:** 332,940 motorcycles, up 27.4% YoY from 261,326 (Q1 FY26); India sales at 301,174; Global sales at 31,766.
- **VECV Sales:** 24,815 units, up 14.8% YoY.
- **VECV Revenue:** Rs 6,610 crore vs Rs 5,671 crore YoY.
- **VECV EBITDA:** Rs 541 crore vs Rs 511 crore YoY (Margin at 8.4% vs 9.2% YoY).
- **VECV PAT:** Rs 300 crore vs Rs 288 crore YoY.
- **International Revenue (RE):** Crossed Rs 1,000 crore (accounting for 15.3% of revenues).
- **Channel Inventory:** 10 to 12 days at dealers.

### Highlights
- Launched the first electric motorcycle, the Flying Flea C6, under the city+ electric mobility brand in Bengaluru; customer deliveries commenced.
- Expanded the internal combustion engine portfolio with the Bullet 650 and new colorways for the Hunter 350.
- Board approved Phase 1 greenfield investment of Rs 1,225 crore in Tada, Andhra Pradesh, with an annual capacity of 4.5 lakh motorcycles at full utilization, expected to complete by FY30.
- VECV signed an MoU for the fleet modernization scheme 'PARIVARTAN' for the NCR.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management is geared for a growth-led FY27 and remains cautiously bullish across both domestic and international markets.
- Cheyyar brownfield capacity expansion will take total capacity to 2 million motorcycles by FY27-28; Tada greenfield will take it to 2.45 million by FY30.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Inquiries, bookings, walk-ins, and telephonic inquiries continue to maintain strong growth momentum, outperforming volume growth.
- Hunter 350 sees ~50% first-time buyers.
- 450cc and 650cc models have recovered to pre-GST levels, led by Guerrilla 450 (running ~2,500/month) and twins (~4,000 to 4,200/month).
- International business crossed Rs 1,000 crore revenue; Brazil is the largest overseas market (number two in middleweight), with a CKD facility being set up.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Net impact of 4% to 4.5% headwinds from increased input costs (aluminum, crude oil, steel, copper, precious metals) and supply chain/logistics disruptions.
- Value engineering and cost reduction programs provided a benefit of about 0.4%.
- Advanced purchase programs implemented for critical parts.
- Price increases of 1.75% on majority of 350cc motorcycles in April 2026 mitigated commodity inflation by about 1.2%.
- Other expenses were lower due to the absence of specific Q4 ad campaigns and controlled marketing expenditures.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Oragadam and Vallam plants running in three shifts since April; production rate reached ~4,500/day in June and over 5,000/day currently.
- Cheyyar first module kicked in during late July; next module expected by October.
- Board approved Rs 1,225 crore for Phase 1 greenfield expansion at Tada, Andhra Pradesh.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Average gross block increased by approx Rs 346 crore, leading to higher depreciation due to capitalization of the new module and Flying Flea.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatile commodity and input cost environment.
- Supply chain, logistics disruptions, and past manpower/LPG shortages (which were managed).
- Market adjustment phase and economic pressures for dealers in Europe and parts of ASEAN (e.g., Thailand tourism dip, Indonesia quota and luxury tax restrictions).

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Gunjan Prithyani (Bank of America):** Asked about capacity, labor/LPG issues, and channel inventory ahead of the festive season. *Management stated that capacity is ramping up to 5,000+/day with new modules and exploring direct dealer billing to save 4-5 days of transit time.*
- **Kapil Singh (Nomura):** Inquired about Flying Flea customer profile and international market trends. *Management stated that initial adopters are in the 25-30 age bracket, and international markets like Brazil are showing strong 3x growth over 3 years.*
- **Pramod Kumar (UBS Securities):** Asked about VECV hiving off/listing plans and RE ASP drivers. *Management noted that EML's current focus is on EVs, and VECV is performing well operationally; Vidhya Srinivasan attributed ASP growth to price hikes, richer international mix, currency depreciation, and allied businesses.*
- **Chandramouli Muthiah (Goldman Sachs):** Inquired about the high base effect post-GST and rural/urban splits. *Management highlighted that the middleweight category base expanded from 70k to over 1 lakh units/month, and RE repeat buyers are ~5-6%, upgraders are ~70%+, and first-time buyers are ~25%.*
- **Amyn Pirani (JP Morgan):** Asked about depreciation increase and ASEAN expansion (Indonesia/Thailand). *Management confirmed depreciation rose due to capitalization of the new module/Flying Flea, and noted plans to evaluate a local CKD assembler in Indonesia to bypass import quota limits.*

### One-line takeaway
Eicher Motors kicked off FY27 with record Q1 sales across both Royal Enfield and VECV, driving robust revenue and EBITDA growth despite input cost headwinds.
