# Ola Electric Mobility Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-13 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a strong top-line recovery, sequential volume growth, industry-leading margins, and a leaner operating cost base.

### Key numbers
- **Deliveries:** ~39,200 units (nearly doubled QoQ)
- **Orders:** ~44,000 units
- **Auto Revenue:** Rs 455 crore (grew 72% QoQ)
- **Auto Gross Profit:** Rs 139 crore
- **Auto Gross Margin:** 30.5%
- **Market Share:** Increased from 5.1% to 8.4% (Industry grew ~17% QoQ, Ola registrations grew 97%)
- **Consolidated Operating Expenses:** Declined 22% QoQ to Rs 333 crore (or ~Rs 380-odd crore excluding a one-time reversal of Cell ACC PLI penalty provision of ~Rs 55 crore)
- **Consolidated Adjusted Operating EBITDA:** Negative Rs 195 crore (improved from negative Rs 326 crore in Q4 FY26)
- **QIP Raised:** Rs 780 crore
- **Cell Capacity:** 6 GWh Gigafactory expected to be operational this quarter (September)
- **Service Revenue Target:** Rs 400-500 crore by FY 2027-28
- **Cell Yields:** High-70s to ~80% before pausing production
- **Capex Guidance:** ~Rs 50 crore for the year (excluding Cell project completion)
- **Cell MoU:** 20 GWh over 5-6 years signed with Axis Energy for Mahashakti

### Highlights
- First full quarter operating on the reset base, resulting in material cost optimization and improved business performance.
- Evolved Auto distribution strategy from a single-channel company-owned approach to a multi-channel strategy, onboarding dealers starting early next month (Janmashtami).
- Signed first MoU for Mahashakti (utility-scale energy-storage product) with Axis Energy for 20 GWh over 5-6 years.
- 4680 NMC Bharat Cell commercially deployed; 46100 LFP cell received BIS certification and is vehicle-ready.
- Brought back Mr. B.V.R. Subbu to help select dealer partners.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Auto gross margins to remain in the 30-32% range over the next quarter or two, and improve further as commodity prices ease.
- Consolidated opex expected to decrease into the Rs 300-325 crore range over the next couple of quarters.
- ASP expected to remain broadly around 1.25L, plus or minus 5%.
- Cell production to ramp up later this month after full 6 GWh installation; contributions from the battery business expected from Q3 onwards.
- Most vehicles expected to be on in-house cells by the end of the year (management disclaimer: no firm guidance given).
- Expansion from 6 GWh to 20 GWh prismatic cell planned for FY27, to be funded via separate equity raised at the cell company level.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Electric two-wheeler market in India crossed 10% penetration for the first time in June.
- Broad-based volume growth with sequential improvement across every region.
- Over 1,000 expressions of interest received from dealers for the new multi-channel distribution strategy.
- Strong interest from global OEMs and Indian companies (e.g., drones) for in-house cells; utility platforms engaging for Mahashakti.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Auto gross margins sustained at 30.5% despite a challenging commodity upcycle.
- Depreciation policy aligned with industry standards, reducing depreciation by about Rs 10-20 crore.
- LFP cells noted to be costlier than NMC? *Correction from text:* NMC is costlier than LFP; LFP adoption expected to further optimize battery costs and gross margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Auto business requires minimal capex (~Rs 30-50 crore target) moving forward as the factory is scaled to 1 million units/year.
- Cell factory completing its capex cycle this quarter at 6 GWh; remaining cell capex funded through debt with equity contribution largely complete.
- Cell production paused in Q1 for 2-3 months to complete installation from 2.5 GWh to 6 GWh capacity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet strengthened via a Rs 780 crore QIP.
- Dealers under the new model will stock parts and buy on a cash-and-carry basis.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Brand weakened over the previous few quarters due to service and parts availability issues (noted as largely behind them).
- Managed vendor challenges and macro geopolitical situations impacting critical parts.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Rishi Vora (Axis Capital) on ASP decline:** Management attributed ~90% of the sequential ASP decline to product mix, expecting ASP to stay around 1.25L (+/- 5%).
2. **Rishi Vora on Cell integration and Shakti:** Management stated a few thousand vehicles use own cells. NMC-based Shakti production was limited due to sub-target margins; LFP-based Shakti Gen 2 to launch August 15.
3. **Akshay Satija (Alpha Invesco) on cell yields and mix:** Yields were high-70s to 80% before pausing; roadmap aims for >90% within a quarter of restart. ~20% of Auto portfolio will remain NMC for high-end products, rest moving to LFP.
4. **Akshay Satija on service parts issues:** Management explained parts shortages were due to vendor challenges/geopolitical factors, which will be mitigated via dealer cash-and-carry stocking.
5. **Udit Jaiswal on Axis Energy partnership & Roadster:** Axis Energy MoU is part of a massive energy-storage market estimated by CEA at 400 GWh over 5-6 years. Roadster contribution is a small minority due to 4680 cell supply constraints during capacity scaling.

### One-line takeaway
Ola Electric delivered a strong top-line recovery and sequential volume doubling in Q1 FY27 on a leaner cost base, while sustaining 30.5% gross margins and pivoting to a multi-channel dealer strategy.
