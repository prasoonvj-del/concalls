# Tata Motors Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Volume momentum, revenue growth, and strong free cash flow offset commodity cost headwinds and margin pressure.

### Key numbers
- **Wholesales:** 1,35,100 units (up 42% YoY, up 24% QoQ); H1 wholesales at 2,43,800 units (up 35% YoY).
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 23,900 crore (up 42% YoY).
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 2,800 crore (up 31% YoY); Margin at 11.7% (down 90 bps YoY, flat QoQ).
- **Standalone EBIT:** Rs 2,300 crore; Margin at 9.7% (down 50 bps YoY).
- **PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 2,700 crore (up 51% YoY).
- **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Rs 4,600 crore (vs Rs 2,200 crore in Q2 FY26); H1 FCF at Rs 5,721 crore.
- **Net Cash:** Rs 11,300 crore as of September 30, 2026 (vs Rs 7,500 crore at March end and Rs 7,100 crore at June end).
- **Auto ROCE:** 65% on a trailing 12-month basis.
- **Investment Spends / R&D:** Investment spend at Rs 600 crore; R&D at Rs 339 crore (Rs 209 crore capitalized, Rs 130 crore expensed).
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 25,300 crore (up 36% YoY).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 2,800 crore (up 31% YoY); Margin at 10.9% (down 40 bps YoY).
- **Consolidated Net Cash:** Rs 15,900 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- **Market Share (H1 VAHAN):** Overall 36.2% (up 110 bps over FY26); HCV 55.7% (up 50 bps); ILMCV 36.3%; SCV pickups 27.6% (up 130 bps); CV Passenger 41.1% (up 500 bps).
- **International Business:** Up 177% YoY.
- **EV Volumes:** Up 143% YoY; SCV EV retails crossed 4,400 units (up 2.9x YoY).

### Highlights
- Unveiled next-gen passenger mobility solutions at Prawaas 5.0 (Ultra Prime, Starbus Prime) and next-gen aggregates at Bauma CONEXPO India 2026.
- Crowned Best Truck of the Year at Logistics and Transport Award 2026 in the UAE.
- Signed MoU with Drivn for EV leasing/financing; collaborated with Petronas for used-oil recycling; partnered with Welspun Renewable Energy for an 86 MW wind-solar hybrid project.
- IVECO open offer acceptance period opened Sept 7, 2026, closed Oct 26, 2026; offer price increased to EUR14.40 per share as a best and final offer; IVECO EGM approved all resolutions; ~35% shares tendered as of Oct 22, with settlement expected Oct 30, 2026.
- Non-cyclical business contributes approximately 20% of revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects YoY volume growth rates to taper in Q3 due to a high base last year (Q3 FY26 grew 21.5% YoY).
- Q3 growth is expected to be high single-digit or early double-digit, with ILMCV and CV Passenger seeing double-digit growth, and HCV and SCV seeing high single-digit growth.
- Management expects a further commodity cost increase of 150 to 200 bps in Q3.
- SCV EV salience expected to reach up to 20% in Q4, driven by demand and Delhi-NCR regulations requiring only EVs for registration from Jan 1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry offtake grew 28.4% YoY in Q2, while Tata Motors' volumes grew 30.5% YoY.
- Demand supported by GDP growth, infrastructure execution, mining output, festive stocking, and E-way bill generation.
- Freight rates strengthened during Q2, supporting transporter economics after initial pressure from diesel price hikes.
- Fleet utilization remained stable to improving gradually through Q2.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity inflation impacted margins by 590 bps YoY and 200 bps QoQ in Q2; further 150-200 bps impact expected in Q3. Steel and rubber are the primary drivers (steel at 52-week high, up ~40% YoY).
- Implemented four price hikes in CY26: ~1% in January, ~2% in April, 2.5% in July, and 1% to 2% effective October 1.
- International business is margin accretive, though Indonesia LCV volume acts as an average selling price (ASP) dilutive factor.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Investment spending is running at ~2.5% of revenue, comfortably within the guided 2% to 4% range.
- Supply chain bottlenecks noted in common commodities (castings, machining capacity, sheet metal stamping), proprietary items, and imported EV battery cells.
- Undertook targeted in-house debottlenecking actions in Q2, with more planned for Q3 in anticipation of Q4 demand.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital released Rs 2,679 crore in Q2, driven by payables and acceptances releasing Rs 4,786 crore, offset by inventory absorption of Rs 1,717 crore and receivables of Rs 622 crore (partially related to Indonesia order build-up and festive stocking).
- Standalone net cash stands at Rs 11,300 crore; consolidated net cash at Rs 15,900 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Diesel price increases and interest rate hikes (25 bps hike already taken) remain key monitorable headwinds.
- Elevated rubber and steel prices impacting material costs.
- Supply chain constraints impacting ILMCV and electric small commercial vehicle deliveries.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Kapil (Nomura) on Indonesia order delivery and future visibility:** Management stated deliveries span FY27 and FY28 at 4,500–5,000 units/month out of 70,000 total, with efforts ongoing to build long-term retail financing, distribution, and service infrastructure in Indonesia.
2. **Raghu (Nuvama) on IVECO 80% threshold hurdles:** Management expressed confidence in a successful completion based on positive stakeholder engagement and EGM approvals, noting shares typically tender in the final 24-48 hours.
3. **Amyn Pirani on truck operator financial health and asset quality:** Management noted no widespread concerns based on financier interactions, and stated transporter profitability recovered through Q2 after initial diesel price pressures.
4. **Gunjan (BofA) on capacity constraints:** Management highlighted bottlenecks in common supply chain commodities, proprietary items, and imported EV batteries, and confirmed debottlenecking actions are underway in Q2 and Q3.
5. **Sridhar Kalyani (Antique Stock Broking) on Q2 working capital release and FCF sustainability:** Management explained that working capital releases were timing-related and part of strong volume ramps; Q3 and Q4 are historically strong cash-generation quarters, and FCF is expected to remain positive.
6. **Kapil (Nomura) on steel safeguard duty:** Management confirmed representations have been made to the government via SIAM to review safeguard duties on steel given the auto industry's cost pressures.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Motors delivered robust Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 42% and strong free cash flow of Rs 4,600 crore, successfully offsetting steep commodity headwinds through operating leverage, cost efficiencies, and price hikes.
