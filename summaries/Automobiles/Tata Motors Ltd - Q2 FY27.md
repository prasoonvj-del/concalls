# Tata Motors Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-27 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Strong standalone revenue (+42% YoY) and volume growth driven by broad-based segment demand and international expansion, offsetting notable commodity headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 23,900 crore, up 42% YoY.
- **Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 2,800 crore, up 31% YoY; margin at 11.7% (down 90 bps YoY, flat QoQ).
- **Standalone EBIT:** Rs 2,300 crore; margin at 9.7% (down 50 bps YoY).
- **Standalone PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 2,700 crore (walk details Rs 2,660 crore), up 51% YoY.
- **Standalone Cash PAT:** Rs 2,287 crore, up 26% YoY.
- **Standalone Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Rs 4,600 crore (vs Rs 2,200 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 25,300 crore, up 36% YoY.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 2,800 crore, up 31% YoY; margin at 10.9% (down 40 bps YoY).
- **Consolidated EBIT Margin:** 8.9%, up 20 bps YoY.
- **Consolidated PBT (before exceptional items):** Rs 1,800 crore (vs a loss of Rs 550 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Consolidated FCF:** Rs 3,000 crore (vs Rs 2,000 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Wholesales:** 1,35,100 units, up 42% YoY and up 24% QoQ.
  - Heavy Commercial Vehicles (HCV): +41% YoY.
  - Intermediate, Light & Medium Commercial Vehicles (ILMCV): +24% YoY.
  - Small Commercial Vehicles (SCV) Cargo & Pickup: +27% YoY.
  - Commercial Vehicle (CV) Passenger: +31% YoY.
  - International Business: +177% YoY.

### Highlights
- Secured an Indonesian order for ~70,000 units (delivering 4,500 to 5,000 units monthly over two years), driving a 177% YoY increase in export volume.
- Unveiled the Ultra Prime and Starbus Prime ranges at Prawaas 5.0 and next-gen aggregates at Bauma CONEXPO India 2026.
- Partnered with Drivn for EV leasing/financing solutions, Petronas for used-oil recycling, and Welspun Renewable Energy for an 86 MW wind-solar hybrid project.
- EV salience in SCV pickups reached ~12% in Q2, with total SCV EV retail sales reaching over 4,400 units (+143% YoY overall EV volume growth).
- Non-cyclical/downstream businesses (Parts, Services, Digital, Smart City e-bus operations) contributed ~20% of overall revenue.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Q3 FY27 Demand:** Management expects Q3 volume growth to taper to high single-digit or low double-digit YoY levels due to a high base post-GST rationalization in Q3 FY26. Double-digit growth is expected in ILMCV and CV passenger, with higher single-digit growth in HCV and SCV.
- **IVECO Consolidation:** Acquisition consolidation is scheduled to begin in Q3 FY27 following final offer settlement.
- **EV Salience:** Management expects SCV EV salience to rise toward 20% by Q4 FY27, assisted by Delhi-NCR mandates effective January 1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry CV offtake grew 28.4% YoY in Q2, supported by infrastructure execution, mining output, festive stocking, and automotive dispatches.
- Fleet utilization recovered progressively through Q2 after a weak July caused by monsoon disruptions and diesel price increases.
- Key monitorables for demand include diesel price movements and interest rate trends.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Commodity inflation impacted margins by 590 bps YoY (200 bps QoQ), heavily driven by steel (up ~40% YoY, at 52-week highs) and crude-linked synthetic rubber.
- Management expects further commodity cost inflation of 150 to 200 bps in Q3 FY27.
- Taken four price increases in 2026: ~1% in Jan, ~2% in April, 2.5% in July, and 1%–2% effective October 1.
- Margin pressure was largely offset by operating leverage, cost efficiencies, and net realization gains.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Q2 investment spending stood at Rs 597 crore (Rs 1,112 crore for H1), representing ~2.5% of revenue, well within the guided range of 2% to 4%.
- Direct Q2 Capex was Rs 483 crore; Q2 R&D spending stood at Rs 339 crore (Rs 209 crore capitalized, Rs 130 crore expensed).
- Supply chain bottlenecks were noted in castings, machining, sheet metal, and imported battery aggregates; targeted debottlenecking actions are underway across plants and suppliers.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Standalone Net Cash:** Improved to Rs 11,300 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up from Rs 7,500 crore at March 31 and Rs 7,100 crore at June 30).
- **Consolidated Net Cash:** Stood at Rs 15,900 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up from Rs 13,700 crore at March 31).
- Working capital released Rs 2,679 crore during Q2, driven primarily by Rs 4,786 crore in payables and acceptances build-up, partially offset by inventory (Rs 1,717 crore) and receivables (Rs 622 crore) absorption.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued input cost escalation in steel and rubber placing pressure on gross margins.
- Supply chain constraints in imported aggregates, specifically battery cells/packs for EVs.
- Potential normalization/reversal of payables working capital benefits in upcoming quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kapil (Nomura):** *What is the execution plan for the Indonesia order and export visibility post-FY28?*  
  **Management:** The ~70,000-unit order is being delivered over FY27–FY28 at 4,500–5,000 units per month. The company is using this scale to build local retail financing and service setups to introduce additional vehicle segments.
- **Raghu (Nuvama):** *Are there any hurdles in reaching the 80% acceptance threshold for IVECO?*  
  **Management:** The offer price was raised to EUR 14.40 per share as a best and final offer. Management remains confident of a successful open offer, noting tender offer share submissions typically concentrate in the final 24–48 hours before closing.
- **Raghu (Nuvama):** *Are export margins accretive despite flat ASPs?*  
  **Management:** International business is overall margin-accretive, though the Indonesia order consists of lighter commercial vehicles which dilute Average Selling Price (ASP). Domestic ASPs rose YoY and QoQ.
- **Amyn Pirani:** *Are truck operators experiencing stress given deteriorating NBFC credit costs?*  
  **Management:** Management reported no major systemic issues among financiers they interact with; operator profitability dipped early in Q2 due to diesel price increases but recovered via rate pass-throughs.
- **Gunjan (BofA):** *Do you need to add internal manufacturing capacity?*  
  **Management:** Primary constraints reside in supplier capacities (castings, stampings, proprietary/EV items) rather than in-house assembly. Specific debottlenecking activities were executed in Q2 and will continue through Q3 and Q4.

### One-line takeaway
Tata Motors delivered robust 42% standalone top-line growth and expanded net cash to Rs 11,300 crore, effectively cushioning elevated raw material inflation through volume leverage and export scale.
