# Automobiles - Two & three wheelers: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the two and three-wheeler sector reflected robust top-line growth and market share gains, driven by strong festive demand and accelerating EV adoption. Major players like Bajaj Auto, Hero MotoCorp, and TVS Motor Company reported stellar double-digit revenue and PAT growth, alongside expanding international footprints. However, the sector faced notable headwinds from persistent raw material inflation (steel, aluminum, copper, and styrene), which squeezed margins for component and accessory makers like Studds Accessories, while OEMs managed cost pressures through operating leverage, price hikes, and EV PLI accruals. Balance sheets generally strengthened, highlighted by Hero Motors' significant debt reduction post-IPO and Bajaj's robust free cash flow generation.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** - Indian two and three-wheeler manufacturers have historically relied on mass-volume ICE products distributed through extensive dealer networks, transitioning over the last 5 years toward direct-to-consumer digital touchpoints, in-house EV platforms, component vertical integration, and global market expansion [Background].

**Shifts underway**
- **EV scaling and profitability milestone:** Moving from early-stage cash burn to gross-margin positive operations and scaling manufacturing capacities. *Stage:* Scaling. *Evidence:* Bajaj's EV portfolio crossed ~200,000 units in Q2 (>34% of domestic revenue); Hero MotoCorp's VIDA achieved gross margin positive status with Q2 EV revenue hitting Rs 1,000 crore; TVS iQube crossed 1 cumulative million units [Bajaj Auto concall Q2 FY27; Hero MotoCorp concall Q2 FY27; TVS Motor concall Q2 FY27]. *Impact:* Transforms EVs from margin drags to accretive growth engines.
- **Aggressive premiumization:** Focussing on 150cc+ motorcycles, premium adventure segments, and lifestyle variants. *Stage:* Mainstream. *Evidence:* Bajaj's K-shaped demand split favors premium 150cc+ models; Hero MotoCorp's Xpulse retail volume grew >30% YoY in H1; TVS reports "very good" demand for its Ronin classic bike [Bajaj Auto concall Q2 FY27; Hero MotoCorp concall Q2 FY27; TVS Motor concall Q2 FY27]. *Impact:* Drives higher realizations and shields margins against mass-market volume volatility.
- **Component and powertrain integration:** Moving toward high-margin complete system solutions rather than pure-play parts manufacturing. *Stage:* Scaling. *Evidence:* Hero Motors reports powertrain solutions account for ~52% of total revenue, winning complex transmission and racing program projects [Hero Motors concall Q2 FY27]. *Impact:* Enhances enterprise stickiness and structural EBITDA margins.

**Government push**
- EV infrastructure support and localized manufacturing policies provide tailwinds for electrification [Govt: Rajasthan govt support up to 80% cost for EV infrastructure, Nov 2026; Govt ban on lithium-ion battery black mass exports, Nov 2026].

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
- **Consensus:** Managements across Bajaj Auto, Hero MotoCorp, Studds, and TVS Motor agree that domestic retail demand is robust, underpinned by strong festive season sales (running at double-digit or ~24% YoY growth windows). The K-shaped recovery persists, with the premium segment and electric scooters outperforming entry-level 100cc mass markets.
- **Divergence:** Hero MotoCorp reported that rural market growth equaled or outperformed urban performance in Q2 and QTD. In contrast, Bajaj Auto flagged potential risks from deficit monsoons and drought-like conditions in select states (Maharashtra, Southern states, Northeast) that could dampen Q4 rural cash flows and harvest demand.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Input Inflation:** Companies faced continuous inflation across key inputs. Bajaj Auto noted material cost inflation added 3-3.5% of revenue in Q2, driven by electrical components, aluminum, copper, nickel, rubber, and steel. Hero MotoCorp experienced ~5.5% cumulative H1 inflation. Studds Accessories was severely impacted by surging styrene-based raw material prices, dragging down EBITDA by 23.7% YoY.
- **Mitigation:** OEMs implemented calibrated price hikes (e.g., Bajaj covered 50-60% of H1 inflation; Hero MotoCorp took a ~Rs 1,000/unit hike on Oct 1; Studds took a ~9% net price increase) and leveraged operating leverage and PLI benefits (such as Hero MotoCorp's Rs 63 crore EV PLI accrual).

## Capex & capacity
- **Bajaj Auto:** Expanding overall monthly plant capacity from 6,25,000 to 7,75,000 units over 6-9 months; scaling Chetak capacity to 80,000 units/month by Q4 FY27.
- **Hero MotoCorp:** Maintained full-year capex guidance at Rs 1,500 crore; doubling VIDA monthly capacity to 30,000 units (targeting 45,000 by Q4 FY27 and >80,000 by Q2 FY28); investing >Rs 700 crore in the Tirupati Global Parts Center 2.0.
- **Hero Motors:** Deploying IPO proceeds for growth capex, including a new EV system facility in Bengaluru targeted for March/April 2027.
- **Studds Accessories:** Commercial production commenced at its new plant on Oct 30, 2026, adding 1.5 million units of capacity to reach ~11 million units total annual capacity.
- **TVS Motor Company:** EV capacity expansion underway from 60-65k units to ~80k units/month, targeting >8 million 2W annual capacity.

## What changed since last quarter
- **Bajaj Auto:** Raised Chetak monthly capacity target to 80,000 by Q4 FY27 (vs 60,000 in Q1) and updated near-term plant expansion target to 7,75,000 units/month (vs 9 million/year medium-term target in Q1). Restructured front-end/back-end organization into CBO-led business units.
- **Hero MotoCorp:** VIDA EV business turned gross-margin positive ahead of guidance, driven by PLI accruals and portfolio optimization.

## What to watch next quarter
- Sustainability of festive retail momentum into Q4 and the actual impact of monsoon deficits on rural cash flows.
- Trajectory of raw material inflation (particularly steel, aluminum, and styrene) and whether further price hikes will be required.
- Execution speed and capacity ramp-ups for EV portfolios (Chetak, VIDA, iQube) and export market recoveries (e.g., Colombia for Hero MotoCorp).
- Progress on TVS Motor's demerger of its Consumer Finance business.

## Company one-liners
- **Bajaj Auto Ltd:** Delivered record-breaking financial performance across all metrics, fueled by surging exports and profitable EV scaling.
- **Hero MotoCorp Ltd:** Posted strong 21% revenue growth and market share expansion while driving early profitability in its VIDA electric vehicle line.
- **Hero Motors Ltd:** Significantly de-leveraged its balance sheet using IPO proceeds while scaling high-margin powertrain system solutions.
- **Studds Accessories Ltd:** Achieved robust volume and export growth, though profitability was pressured by sharp raw material inflation.
- **TVS Motor Company Ltd:** Captured stellar revenue and profit growth with expanding margins, supported by broad-based ICE and EV demand.
