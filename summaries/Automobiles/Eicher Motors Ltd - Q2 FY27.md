# Eicher Motors Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly revenue and volumes across Royal Enfield and VECV, sustained strong booking momentum, and early operational progress in electric two-wheelers.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained capacity additions and rollout plans; preponing Cheyyar Module 2 from later to Q1 FY28.
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains firmly positive; post-festive booking momentum continues strong at ~20-21% YoY growth, with no rural slowdown signs observed in core markets.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: VECV flagged supply chain and West Asian geopolitical inflationary pressures; Vahan registration gap vs wholesale was raised by analysts; depreciation dropped QoQ as certain legacy asset useful lives ended in Q1.
- Last quarter's promises: Flying Flea C6 expansion progressed beyond Bengaluru to Hyderabad, Pune, and European cities (Paris, London, etc.), logging 125,000 km cumulatively.

### Key numbers
* **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 7,422 crore, up 20% YoY from Rs 6,172 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 1,760 crore vs Rs 1,512 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 1,573 crore, up 15% YoY from Rs 1,369 crore in Q2 FY26 (includes Rs 186 crore share of profit from VECV).
* **Royal Enfield Volumes:** Highest-ever quarterly sales of 376,722 motorcycles, up 15% YoY vs 327,067 in Q2 FY26 (Domestic: 340,847, up 16% YoY; International: 35,875).
* **VECV Revenue:** Rs 7,568.6 crore, up 24% YoY from Rs 6,105.8 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **VECV EBITDA:** Rs 587.7 crore, up 22.7% YoY from Rs 478.9 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **VECV PAT:** Rs 341.6 crore, up 37% YoY from Rs 249.3 crore in Q2 FY26.
* **VECV Volumes:** 28,071 vehicles, up 28.2% YoY (H1 total: 52,886 vehicles, up 21.5% YoY; Pro X cumulative sales crossed 5,000 units, including 1,400 EVs).

### Highlights
* Ranked #1 two-wheeler OEM in FADA Dealer Satisfaction Survey for the sixth year running.
* Launched Himalayan 450 upgrade, Gorkha Edition in India and Nepal, Continental GT feature updates, and Rough Crafts limited-edition Shotgun 650 (India allocation booked out in 18 minutes).
* Flying Flea C6 expanded into Hyderabad and Pune, and launched in Paris, Barcelona, Rome, Berlin, and London in September; customers have cumulatively ridden 125,000 km.
* VECV inaugurated next-generation AI-enabled Uptime Center at Pithampur tracking 100% connected fleet.

### Guidance & outlook
* Management expects upward trajectory to continue across both businesses in upcoming quarters.
* RE production target of 1.415 lakh units/month planned for Q4 FY27, with efforts to pull it forward to January.
* EV adoption will be treated as a long-term play; unveiled S6 scrambler at EICMA.

### Demand & industry commentary
* Royal Enfield retail demand grew 18% YoY April to October (YTD); booking run rate tracking at 20-21% YoY.
* Domestic core states (Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Rajasthan) grew 23% YoY in H1 FY27, showing no signs of rural stress.
* Premiumization trend remains intact; RE maintains 80-82% market share in middleweight (250cc-750cc) in India.
* VECV witnessing strong order book across bus and truck segments.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
* Taken a 0.7% price hike in October 2026 (fourth pricing intervention this calendar year, following hikes in January, April, and July).
* Taken price hikes cover about 50% of the commodity impact, balancing inflation pass-through with value perception.
* Softening noted in steel and aluminum, offset by currency weakness.
* Depreciation declined QoQ due to end of useful life in Q1 FY27 for certain assets related to 350cc and 650cc platforms.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
* **Cheyyar Brownfield:** Rs 1,050 crore investment across 3 modules. Module 1 completed; Module 2 (1 lakh units) preponed to Q1 FY28; Module 3 targeted for FY28. Total brownfield to add ~2.45-2.5 lakh units.
* **Andhra Pradesh Greenfield:** Board approval of Rs 1,225 crore for Phase 1 to add 4.5 lakh units capacity, expected completion by 2029-2030.
* Debottlenecking underway at Vallam and Oragadam via low-cost automations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
* Channel inventory post-Diwali is extremely lean at ~7 to 8 days (including in-transit stock).
* Direct dealer billing expanded from 1-2% historically to 18% during the festive season, cutting 4-5 days of transit inventory.

### Risks & concerns flagged
* Channel inventory remains exceptionally low, acting as a supply constraint against strong incoming bookings.
* VECV facing supply chain challenges and inflationary pressures linked to ongoing West Asian geopolitical tensions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
* **Pramod Kumar (UBS):** *Capacity ramp-up timeline and channel inventory status?*  
  **Management:** Dealer stock is lean at 7-8 days (including transit) post-Diwali. Preponing Cheyyar Module 2 to Q1 FY28; target to hit 1.415 lakh units/month run-rate as early as January 2027.
* **Gunjan Prithyani (BofA):** *Any signs of rural stress or regional weakness?*  
  **Management:** No slow-down observed; core states (UP, WB, Bihar, Rajasthan) grew 23% YoY in H1 FY27. Post-festive inquiries remain at double-digit growth.
* **Chandramouli Muthiah (Goldman Sachs):** *Pricing interventions and commodity pass-through?*  
  **Management:** Took 0.7% price hike in October (fourth this year); cumulatively offsets ~50% of commodity inflation under a calibrated value-based pricing strategy.
* **Joseph George (IIFL):** *Why is there a 55,000 unit gap between wholesale and Vahan registrations over April–October?*  
  **Management:** Attributable to daily registration spillovers and dealer inventory/transit pipeline; management will re-check exact gap reconciliation.
* **Kapil Singh (Nomura):** *Strategy on expanding below 350cc or new categories?*  
  **Management:** Intends to stay focused on middleweight (250cc-750cc), which has immense headroom; no plans to enter commuter segments below 250cc.

### One-line takeaway
Eicher Motors posted record consolidated revenue of Rs 7,422 crore on strong festive demand and lean inventory, accelerating capacity expansion to meet sustained 20%+ booking growth.
