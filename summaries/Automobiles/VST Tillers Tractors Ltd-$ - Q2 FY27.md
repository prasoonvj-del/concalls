# VST Tillers Tractors Ltd-$ - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue grew and power tiller/weeder segments performed well, but tractor domestic sales dropped due to severe drought in key markets and EBITDA margins contracted by 200 bps due to commodity inflation.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Management revised its domestic tractor outlook from growth to flat for the full year, citing severe drought in Maharashtra and Northern Karnataka and lower pricing from a competitor in Gujarat.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more cautious on tractors due to regional droughts and a 20%+ drop in the Maharashtra industry, while remaining highly bullish on small farm machinery (power weeders targeting 40%+ growth) and expecting power tiller momentum to continue.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Opening of the Netherlands warehouse to resolve logistics/working capital hurdles for European distributors; launching the 'score' platform (between power tiller and tractor) and rear rotary power weeders; specific competitor discounting in Gujarat.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines): The international logistics warehouse in Netherlands is set to ship out the first lot of tractors by the end of Q2 and commence complete operation in Q4 (as previously targeted for Q3/Q4).

### Key numbers
- Revenue for Q2: Rs 321.6 crore (vs Rs 315 crore YoY, up 2%)
- Revenue for H1: Rs 635 crore (vs Rs 597 crore YoY, up 6%)
- Operational EBITDA for Q2: Rs 35.1 crore (11% margin, vs Rs 40.9 crore YoY)
- Operational EBITDA margin change for Q2: down 200 bps YoY
- Operational EBITDA for H1: 12% margin (vs 13% YoY, down 100 bps)
- PAT for Q2: Rs 22.8 crore (vs Rs 25.4 crore YoY)
- PAT for H1: Rs 71.5 crore (vs Rs 69.9 crore YoY)
- Power tiller sales volume (Q2): 13,357 units (up 2% YoY)
- Power tiller sales volume (H1): 27,158 units (up 9% YoY)
- Tractor domestic sales volume (Q2): 940 units
- Tractor domestic sales volume (H1): 1,925 units (minus 4% YoY)
- Tractor export sales volume (Q2): 226 units
- Tractor export sales volume (H1): 501 units
- Power weeder sales volume (Q2): 2,396 units
- Power weeder sales volume (H1): 6,076 units (up 22% YoY)
- Power reaper sales volume (Q2): 1,143 units
- Power reaper sales volume (H1): 1,171 units
- Retail financing: currently at 11%-12%, targeting ~15% by the end of the year.

### Highlights
- Power tiller and power weeder volumes continued to grow; management expects power weeder growth to accelerate to 40%+ compared to last year.
- Reached over 800 dealerships and more than 1,000 counters, targeting over 2,000 counters by the end of the year through an omnichannel/phygital strategy.
- Set to launch the 'score' platform (intermediate between power tiller and tractor) in H2 with an initial target of 500 numbers, and rear rotary power weeders.
- Signed off on the Netherlands logistics warehouse; first lot of tractors to be shipped by end of Q2, full operations expected in Q4 to reduce delivery time to 2 weeks.
- Taken 2 price hikes each on power tillers and tractors during the financial year.

### Guidance & outlook
- Power tiller growth expected to continue into H2 on a good government order pipeline.
- Power weeder growth expected to accelerate, ending the year at upwards of 40% growth.
- Domestic and export tractor business expected to become flat for the full year.
- Overall Small Farm Machinery (SFM) expected to post double-digit growth for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Maharashtra and Northern Karnataka facing severe drought; Maharashtra compact tractor industry dropped by almost 20%+ and further drop expected in Q3.
- Maharashtra represents almost 40% of the company's compact tractor business (concentrated in vineyards/orchards like Solapur).
- A competitor in Gujarat sold at a much lower price for the last 2-3 months, which management expects to discontinue.
- Eastern states (Orissa, Assam) and Southern states (Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Southern Karnataka) have good rainfall and strong paddy/farming activity, though Andhra experienced a cyclone.
- Small and marginal farmer segment has massive untapped potential for mechanization.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operational EBITDA margin declined by 200 bps in Q2 (to 11%) and 100 bps in H1 (to 12%) due to material cost impacts from commodity inflation over the last 2 quarters.
- Management is evaluating further price hikes depending on commodity trends, noting recent drops in crude prices.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Expanding distribution network to over 2,000 counters by end of year.
- Setting up the Netherlands warehouse to ease European distributor working capital and delivery times.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Temporary hike in inventory and receivables noted (inventories higher due to TREM V transition and commodity fluctuations; receivables/payables also up); management states these are temporary and will normalize in Q3 and Q4.
- No issues with state government subsidy receivables flagged.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe drought in Maharashtra and Northern Karnataka impacting compact tractor demand.
- Commodity inflation putting pressure on gross margins and operational EBITDA.
- Cyclone impact in Andhra Pradesh causing temporary disruptions.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Shreyans asked why growth cannot be maintained in tractors despite northern expansion.** Management explained that the 20%+ drop in the Maharashtra industry (which accounts for ~40% of VST's compact tractor volume due to drought in Solapur) cannot be fully offset by northern growth.
2. **Shreyans asked about the target market for the 'score' platform and potential cannibalization.** Management clarified it targets small and marginal farmers in dry/sugarcane/orchard markets (not dominated by tillers) and offers tiller pricing with tractor comfort, without cannibalizing traditional paddy tillers.
3. **Arjun Khanna questioned why VST participated fully in the tractor downturn despite prior growth.** Management reiterated that compact tractor usage is heavily concentrated in drought-hit regions like Solapur, dragging down volumes disproportionately.
4. **Arjun Khanna asked if receivables are stuck with state governments.** Management denied this, stating the hike is temporary and will normalize in Q3 and Q4.
5. **Nihaar Shah asked if farm income pressure will cause down-trading from tractors to scores or tillers.** Management stated down-trading will not happen because tractor buyers have large landholdings or rental income needs, whereas small farm machines target unmechanized small/marginal farmers.
6. **Shreyans asked about future price hikes and margin protection.** Management noted they have taken two price hikes this year and are currently evaluating further increases while monitoring falling crude prices.

### One-line takeaway
VST Tillers Tractors reported 2% revenue growth in Q2 FY27 driven by steady power tillers, while 200 bps margin contraction and flat tractor guidance reflected severe drought headwinds in Maharashtra.
