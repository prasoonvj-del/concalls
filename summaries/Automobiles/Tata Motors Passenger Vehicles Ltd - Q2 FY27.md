# Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong domestic volume and revenue growth offset by severe China headwinds, commodity inflation, and JLR restructuring charges, prompting a guidance cut.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Cut. JLR EBIT margin guidance revised to 2%–3% (from earlier unquantified resilience) and cash outflow guidance revised to circa GBP 1.25 billion (from flat/similar to last year).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from resilient optimism to heightened caution due to an alarming decline in China and persistent commodity pressures.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: JLR voluntary redundancy program (4,000 salaried headcount reduction, 3,000 leaving this fiscal year) and exceptional charges of GBP 530 million (JLR) / Rs. 6,700 Cr (Consolidated).
- Last quarter's promises: Deliveries for three new luxury EVs at JLR announced for early 2027; voluntary redundancy actions initiated to lower break-evens to 300,000 units.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** ~Rs. 96,000 Cr to Rs. 97,000 Cr (up 34% YoY)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 6.2%
- **Consolidated EBIT Margin:** 1.1% (down from 2.4% QoQ)
- **Consolidated PBT (before exceptional items):** ~Rs. 400 Cr
- **Consolidated Exceptional Charges:** Rs. 6,700 Cr
- **Consolidated Net Debt:** ~Rs. 51,000 Cr (expected to close year at ~Rs. 47,000 Cr); domestic business remains net cash positive
- **India PV Revenue:** Record Rs. 20,000 Cr (up 50% YoY)
- **India PV Wholesales:** ~2 lakh units (up 40% YoY; average monthly offtakes 67,000 units)
- **India Vahan Market Share:** 14.3% in Q2; 14.8% in October 2026
- **India EV Penetration:** 23% volume penetration; EV volumes up 90% YoY (average monthly offtake 16,000 units); market share at 43%
- **India Alternate Powertrains Mix:** >50% of total volumes (CNG and EV)
- **India H1 Capex:** Rs. 2,700 Cr (~6% to 7% of revenue expected for FY27)
- **India H1 Free Cash Flow:** Positive ~Rs. 2,000 Cr
- **PLI Accruals (H1 cumulative):** ~Rs. 830 Cr (Q2 alone: Rs. 530 Cr)
- **JLR Revenue:** GBP 5.9 billion
- **JLR Wholesales:** 82,000 units
- **JLR H1 EBIT:** 2%
- **JLR H1 PBT:** GBP 115 million (Q2 PBT: GBP 6 million)
- **JLR H1 Free Cash Flow:** Negative GBP 1.5 billion (Q2 FCF: negative GBP 540 million)
- **JLR Exceptional Charges:** GBP 530 million (GBP 295 million for voluntary redundancy)
- **JLR H1 Investments:** GBP 1.76 billion

### Highlights
- Launched Tata.CARS customer-facing global brand identity and the all-new compact sedan Aeris (first month sales >4,000 units).
- Punch emerged as the highest-selling model in India in Q2; Nexon among top five models in H1 FY27. October 2026 recorded best-ever offtake and retail month (75,000 units).
- JLR unveiled Range Rover BEV (nearly 5,000 orders), Range Rover Sport BEV, and Jaguar Type 01.
- JLR initiated a voluntary redundancy program targeting ~4,000 salaried headcount reductions (~3,000 leaving in FY27, mostly by end of November).
- *Record Check:* The continuous scaling of EV capacity to 20,000 units/month breaks the past pattern of supply constraints mentioned in Q1, though demand continues to outpace current capacity.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management revised JLR FY27 guidance to 2% to 3% EBIT range and cash outflow of circa GBP 1.25 billion.
- Management targets double-digit EBITDA and cash generation for JLR in FY28, and a return to net cash by the end of FY30.
- Management expects India EV penetration to be 25% or higher next year.
- Management expects PLI accrual run rate of at least Rs. 530 million (likely a typo in transcript for crores, stated as Rs. 530 Cr run rate) to be maintained in H2.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian PV industry Q2 volumes reached 1.36 million units, growing ~29% YoY. Industry CNG penetration at ~25%, EV penetration at ~8%.
- Festive retail demand in India grew 10% YoY on a high base.
- JLR demand in China declined sharply (wholesales down 45% YoY in Q2, 36% down YTD) due to domestic players overcapacity and market contraction. MENA demand continues to struggle due to geopolitical conflict.
- CAFE III policy continues to reinforce the regulatory shift towards greener powertrains.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- India business absorbed elevated commodity costs (steel, rubber, copper) and FX resets, with H1 price increases and cost actions offsetting ~40% of commodity inflation.
- India price hikes: Cumulative ~1.7% across three hikes in April, July, and September; further hikes planned for Q3 and Q4.
- JLR material cost inflation (copper, aluminum, petrochemicals, plastics) was offset by ex-works cost reduction missions.
- JLR VME trended marginally up in Q2 and may rise further before declining in FY28.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- India H1 capex at Rs. 2,700 Cr; full-year capex guided at 6% to 7% of revenue, expected to run higher over next 18-24 months for capacity builds.
- India EV production capacity targeted to move from 16,000 to 20,000 units/month over the next 2-3 months. Aeris and Sierra capacities being expanded due to high demand.
- JLR H2 investment levels expected to be marginally below H1 (GBP 1.76 billion) and decline further into next year as new vehicle launches conclude their investment peak.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Consolidated net debt stood at ~Rs. 51,000 Cr, including working capital buildup for JLR new launches; expected to close lower at ~Rs. 47,000 Cr.
- India domestic business remains net cash positive.
- JLR working capital was negative in H1 due to seasonality, with most expected to unwind in Q4.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Severe contraction and price wars in the Chinese automotive market.
- Geopolitical conflicts and logistics issues impacting MENA demand.
- Elevated commodity inflation (steel, rubber, copper, aluminum) and periodic FX resets.
- Supply chain watchpoints on semiconductors and DRAM chips.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kapil (Unnamed Firm) on EV bookings and waiting periods:** Management stated EVs comprise ~30% of bookings and 40%–45% of pending bookings, with only 10 days of EV inventory compared to 27–28 days overall. Production is scaling from 16,000 to 20,000 units/month. *(Directly answered)*
- **Raghu (Unnamed Firm) on China impact on JLR guidance cut:** Management confirmed that if China had met original plans, guidance would not have been changed. *(Directly answered)*
- **Kumar Rakesh (Unnamed Firm) on JLR cash flow turnaround drivers in FY28:** Management explained that cash burn stems from investing GBP 11.5 billion out of a GBP 15–18 billion program ahead of five major vehicle launches, which will drive EBITDA up while investment peaks and declines. *(Directly answered)*
- **Dhruv Joshi (Unnamed Firm) on long-term plans for China:** Management stated they are taking time to read the market stress carefully without overreacting, while maintaining investments in technology innovation and the Chery Freelander JV. *(Directly answered)*
- **Gunjan (Unnamed Firm) on JLR enterprise missions and restructuring costs:** Management detailed that 4,000 redundancies will yield ~GBP 400 million in ongoing annual savings, and confirmed GBP 530 million in Q2 restructuring charges (GBP 295 million for voluntary redundancy). *(Directly answered)*

### One-line takeaway
Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles delivered robust domestic volume and revenue growth, but faced severe profitability pressures from China market collapse and commodity inflation, leading to a JLR guidance cut and major restructuring actions.
