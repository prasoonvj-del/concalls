# Zelio E Mobility Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported the strongest half year in the company's history with record revenue and profit growth.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated revenue from operations (H1 FY27): Rs 296.66 crore (more than double YoY)
- Standalone revenue (H1 FY27): Rs 289.10 crore
- Zelio Auto Components subsidiary revenue: Rs 7.56 crore
- EBITDA (H1 FY27): Rs 40.49 crore
- EBITDA margin: 13.6% (up from 12% YoY)
- Profit after tax (PAT) (H1 FY27): Rs 31.39 crore (more than 2.5x YoY); net margin above 10%
- Standalone PAT: Rs 30.53 crore
- Earnings per share (EPS) (H1 FY27): Rs 14.84
- Net worth: Rs 142.9 crore (up from Rs 111.5 crore in March)
- Borrowings: Rs 44.82 crore
- Debt-to-equity: 0.31x (against 0.18x in March)
- Receivables: Less than 6 days of sale
- Finance cost (H1): About Rs 1 crore
- Dealer network: More than 400 dealers across 25+ states
- Nepal sales: More than 160 scooters/month
- West Bengal sales: 1,400-1,500 scooters/month (up from 600)
- Tanga three-wheeler sales (H1): Around 600 units
- Two-wheeler sales (H1): 62,000+ units
- Average SKU price: Increased from ~Rs 45,000 to Rs 48,000-50,000 due to raw material and metal price increases
- Battery replacement ratio: 3% to 4%
- Preferential issue size: About Rs 168 crore

### Highlights
- Fourth plant at Patan (Hisar) started commercial production on 28th October 2026, adding 72,000 units/year capacity (48,000 two-wheelers, 24,000 three-wheelers); funded partly from IPO proceeds and internal accruals.
- Total installed capacity across 4 plants (Ladwa, Cuttack, Coimbatore, Patan) reached 3.12 lakh units/year.
- Monitoring agency report for September quarter shows no deviation in IPO proceeds utilization.
- Shareholders approved a preferential issue of about Rs 168 crore involving institutional investors and promoter warrants for R&D, backward integration, and future capacity.
- Continuing the pattern from previous disclosures, capacity expansions and plant milestones (Odisha, Coimbatore, Patan) are running on schedule as promised by management.

### Guidance & outlook
- Full year guidance: More than 1,25,000 units, more than 550 dealers, new high-speed models, and revenue growth of 75% to 80%.
- FY30 target: Sell more than 1 million scooters every year and achieve at least 25% market share.
- Management expects working capital to ease in H2 as festive inventory converts to sales.

### Demand & industry commentary
- EV demand is increasing day by day due to rising fuel prices; a 5-10% hike in ASP did not impact demand.
- Low-speed EV market is described as the backbone of two-wheelers, representing more than 50% of the market.
- MoRTH proposed increasing low-speed motor limits from 250W to 600W.
- Current low-speed market share is 8-9%, with a target of 25% within 2 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating cash flow for H1 was negative due to working capital build-up (inventory and supplier advances ahead of the festive season and new plants).
- R&D investments are expected to have a 1%-2% impact on EBITDA margins going forward.
- Import dependency varies by SKU (ranging from 30% to 100% imported parts); lead-acid batteries are imported.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Patan plant investment: More than Rs 20 crore, featuring backward integration.
- Capacity utilization: Hisar at 70-80%, Odisha at 50%, Coimbatore at 30% (3 months old), overall utilization at 40-50%. Patan plant targeted to reach 80% utilization in 2 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Borrowings increased to Rs 44.82 crore; debt-to-equity at 0.31x.
- Dealers pay in advance, keeping receivables under 6 days of sale.
- Shareholders approved an enabling borrowing limit of Rs 1,000 crores at the AGM with no immediate borrowing motive.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Working capital needs increased inventory and supplier advances, resulting in negative operating cash flow for H1 and higher borrowings.
- A customs show-cause notice of Rs 6.72 crore received (part of an industry-wide matter); management stated it is baseless, appealed against it, and expects a favorable outcome.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Taher Hyderbadwala (Grobiz Fund) asked about volume vs. high-speed SKU contribution and capacity utilization.** Management clarified that low-speed comprises the majority of sales, high-speed proportion is currently low with new models planned for launch this year, and plant utilization ranges from 30% to 80%.
- **Kushal Kasliwal (InVed Research) asked about competition from smaller players in low-speed and current market share.** Management stated they focus on quality and service, pegged their low-speed market share at 8-9%, and target 25% share in 2 years.
- **Bharath Shankar (Individual Investor) asked about potential B2B tie-ups with last-minute delivery fleets.** Management stated they are currently focused on B2C through dealers for better margins and brand visibility, deferring B2B for future excess capacity.
- **Naman (Shanghvi Family Office) asked about the rationale for entering the competitive high-speed market and R&D focus areas.** Management explained that high-speed and low-speed both have a future, and funds will be used to build proper R&D teams, motor, and battery pack capabilities.
- **Vaibhav Jain (Nucleus Capital) asked about the status of the Rs 6.72 crore customs show-cause notice and import exposure.** Management clarified they appealed against the notice, consider it baseless, and noted that import percentages vary across SKUs.

### One-line takeaway
Zelio E-Mobility delivered record H1 FY27 performance with revenue more than doubling YoY to Rs 296.66 crore and PAT rising over 2.5x to Rs 31.39 crore, driven by robust low-speed EV demand and expanded manufacturing footprint.
