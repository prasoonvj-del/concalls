# JK Tyre & Industries Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-30 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever turnover and strong volume growth across segments, despite near-term raw material headwinds.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs. 4,850 crore (up 20% YoY from Rs. 4,026 crore)
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs. 343 crore (up 28% QoQ from Rs. 268 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Consolidated EBITDA Margin:** 7.1% (vs 6.8% in Q1 FY27)
- **PAT:** Rs. 60 crore (up 40% QoQ)
- **Cash Profits (PBDT):** Rs. 234 crore (up 39% QoQ)
- **Consolidated EPS:** Rs. 2.29 (vs Rs. 1.55 in Q1 FY27)
- **Consolidated Net Debt:** Rs. 5,696 crore as of September 30, 2026 (up Rs. 751 crore QoQ)
- **Net Debt to Equity:** 0.94x as of September 30, 2026
- **ROCE / ROE:** 12% / 8.7%
- **JK Tornel Revenue / EBITDA:** Rs. 479 crore / Rs. 23 crore

### Highlights
- Achieved highest-ever quarterly turnover of Rs. 4,850 crore.
- Domestic sales grew 32% YoY, led by a 65% increase in OE sales.
- EV volumes surged 24% QoQ, with leadership in electric truck & bus; MG Hector Tomahawk equipped with Ranger HPe (18-inch) EV tyres.
- Premiumisation sustained: SKUs of 16-inch and above account for nearly 30% of passenger line volumes in H1.
- JK Tornel (Mexico) reported a turnaround with revenue of Rs. 479 crore and EBITDA of Rs. 23 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a further raw material basket increase of 4% to 5% in Q3 FY27.
- Management plans a further Net Selling Realization (NSR) increase of 4% to 5% in Q3 FY27.
- Full-year FY27 capex is guided at approximately Rs. 1,200 crore to Rs. 1,300 crore (Rs. 800 crore spent in H1, ~Rs. 500 crore balance for H2).
- Future asset turnover for new capex is expected around 1:1 to 1:1.1.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Indian real GDP growth projected at 7% plus for Q2 FY27; full-year FY27 revised to 7.1% by RBI.
- Auto industry demand remains healthy and broad-based, supported by GST 2.0, infrastructure push, and festive demand.
- Domestic volume growth in Q2 at 22% (OE up 25%, Replacement up 20%).
- Farm sector experiencing some traction and rural income growth, though rain deficits in some areas pose minor pressure.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Average raw material costs increased by ~9% QoQ in Q2, following a 22% increase in Q1 FY27 (cumulative ~30% increase over 6 months) due to the West Asia crisis and crude volatility.
- Crude-based raw materials constitute 65%-70% of the basket (carbon black, synthetic rubber, butyl, steel cord).
- Net Selling Realizations (NSR) increased by 7% QoQ in Q2, following 4%-5% in Q1 (11%-12% total in 6 months).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capacity utilization in India remained high at above 90% across key segments (TBR, PLR, 2/3W).
- Ongoing expansion projects for TBR and PCR, including upcoming phases of ~Rs. 1,130 crore and broader plans up to ~Rs. 5,000 crore.
- JK Tornel upgradation and modernization expansion progressing as planned.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt increased by Rs. 751 crore QoQ to Rs. 5,696 crore due to increased capex-led long-term borrowings and higher working capital requirements from surging raw material prices and volume growth.
- Inventory levels rose due to 30% higher raw material prices, higher volumes, and strategic raw material stocking.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Resurgence of the West Asia conflict impacting inflation, external balance, and corporate profitability.
- Lag effect in passing on raw material price hikes to replacement and OEM markets, creating near-term unabsorbed cost deltas.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Deepak Malhotra (CapGrow Capital) on raw material granularity and pass-through:** Management stated crude-based RMs form 65-70%, guided for another 4-5% RM increase in Q3, and noted 11-12% cumulative NSR hikes over 6 months with another 4-5% planned for Q3.
- **Vijay Pandey (Axis Capital) on standalone vs consolidated divergences and debt:** Management explained billing to Mexico is routed via the Mexico subsidiary on an arm's length basis, and debt rose due to inventory valuation spikes and capex.
- **Tanish Nahar (Nahar Finance) on volume guidance post-capex:** Management guided an asset turnover multiple of 1:1 to 1:1.1 for upcoming capex phases.
- **Aman K (Retail Investor) on inventory increase:** Management attributed inventory build-up to 30% higher RM prices, volume growth, and strategic stocking.
- **Abhishek Maheshwari (SkyRidge Fund Managers) on EBITDA margin trajectory:** Management noted lag effects in pass-throughs, but expects gradual margin improvement in Q3 driven by demand, high utilization, and cost efficiencies.

### One-line takeaway
JK Tyre delivered record Q2 revenue of Rs. 4,850 crore (up 20% YoY) and robust volume growth, successfully absorbing severe ~30% cumulative raw material inflation through price hikes and operational efficiencies.
