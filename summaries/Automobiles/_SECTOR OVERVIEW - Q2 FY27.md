# Automobiles: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian automobile and auto components sector in Q2 FY27 presents a dual-track performance. On one side, robust domestic festive demand, accelerating electric vehicle (EV) adoption, and strong export recoveries (particularly in 2W, 3W, and commercial vehicles) drove record revenues and top-line expansion for companies like **Bajaj Auto**, **TVS Motor**, **Hero MotoCorp**, **Tata Motors (Standalone/CV)**, **Eicher Motors**, **Ather Energy**, and **Zelio E Mobility**.

On the other side, managements across the board faced severe margin compression from elevated input costs (steel, copper, aluminum, plastics, rubber, memory, and styrene). Simultaneously, regional agricultural stress—caused by deficit rainfalls in Western and Southern India—weighed heavily on tractor and farm machinery volumes for **Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M)**, **Escorts Kubota**, and **VST Tillers Tractors**. Externally, international headwinds hit luxury passenger vehicles hard, as **Tata Motors PV** faced a sharp downturn at JLR in China due to aggressive local price wars and overcapacity.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
### Consensus
* **Two-Wheelers (ICE & EV):** Managements at **Bajaj Auto**, **Hero MotoCorp**, **TVS Motor**, **Eicher Motors (Royal Enfield)**, **Ather Energy**, and **Zelio E Mobility** agree that two-wheeler demand is broad-based and resilient. Electric two-wheeler category penetration reached quarterly highs of 11.2%–11.6% (and ~25% of overall Indian scooters according to **Bajaj Auto**). Domestic booking run-rates post-festive season remain strong (tracking at 20%–21% YoY for **Royal Enfield**, 24% YoY for **Hero MotoCorp**, and +32% during early festive registration for **Bajaj Auto**).
* **Commercial Vehicles & Construction Equipment:** **Tata Motors (CV)**, **Eicher Motors (VECV)**, and **Escorts Kubota (CE)** reported robust volume growth (+42% YoY for Tata Motors wholesales, +28.2% YoY for VECV, and +54% YoY for Escorts CE), underpinned by government infrastructure execution, mining activity, and fleet replacements.

### Divergence
* **Tractors & Farm Equipment:** Widespread divergence exists between regional markets and vehicle types. Deficit monsoons (87% LPA) and low reservoir levels in Maharashtra and Karnataka caused severe agricultural stress. **Escorts Kubota** noted a 21% Q2 industry drop in Western India and expects full-year FY27 tractor industry volumes to decline in low single digits. **M&M** cut its full-year domestic tractor industry growth guidance from +5% to flattish (0%). **VST Tillers Tractors** revised domestic tractor growth to flat due to a 20%+ drop in Maharashtra's compact tractor market. In contrast, Northern states (UP, Haryana, Rajasthan) remain resilient.
* **K-Shaped 2W Demand vs. Broad-Based Recovery:** **Bajaj Auto** highlighted persistent bottom-of-the-pyramid stress in entry 100cc motorcycles due to tight retail financing, with growth heavily tilted toward 150cc+ premium bikes and EVs. Conversely, **Hero MotoCorp** maintained that rural growth was equal to or better than urban growth, reporting 10% YoY growth in its 100cc segment and >20% growth in Splendor.
* **Global PV / Luxury Demand:** While Indian domestic passenger vehicles achieved strong festive growth (**Tata Motors PV** domestic sales up 40% YoY to ~2 lakh units), international passenger vehicle markets diverged sharply. **Tata Motors PV** saw JLR dispatches in China drop 45% YoY in Q2 due to local EV price wars, whereas global 2W powertrain demand in Europe and the US remained robust according to **Hero Motors**.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Elevated Raw Material Inflation:** Commodity pressures intensified through H1 FY27. **M&M** reported YoY raw material inflation across steel (+17%), copper (+42%), and rubber (+40%). **Tata Motors CV** noted steel hit 52-week highs (up ~40% YoY), creating a 590 bps YoY margin headwind. **Ather Energy** noted a 9.4% gross margin hit from elevated aluminum, copper, plastics, and memory costs. **Studds Accessories** and **Tube Investments** cited severe margin pressure from styrene and battery cell cost increases, respectively.
* **Pricing Actions & Pass-Through Lags:** OEMs responded with calibrated price increases:
  * **Royal Enfield** took a 0.7% price hike in October (its 4th hike in CY26), covering ~50% of inflation.
  * **Escorts Kubota** took cumulative price hikes of 6–7% across April, August, and November.
  * **Hero MotoCorp** implemented a ~Rs 1,000/unit price hike on October 1.
  * **Tata Motors CV** executed four price increases during 2026 (1% in Jan, 2% in April, 2.5% in July, 1–2% in Oct).
  * **Studds Accessories** implemented ~9% price increases.
  * **Tube Investments** noted that OEM pricing adjustments lag commodity spikes by 1 to 2 quarters, temporarily depressing near-term margins.
* **EV Unit Economics & PLI Accruals:** EV margins are gradually structuralizing. **Hero MotoCorp** turned gross margin positive in its VIDA EV business in Q2, aided by Rs 63 crore of PLI accruals. **Tata Motors PV** accrued Rs 530 crore in PLI incentives in Q2. **M&M** booked Rs 1,000 crore in PLI incentives during Q2 across its Auto portfolio. **Ather Energy** hit positive EBITDA (+4.9%) and positive PAT for the first time. **Bajaj Auto** confirmed its overall domestic EV portfolio operates at double-digit EBITDA and is margin accretive.

## Capex & capacity
* **2W & EV Capacity Ramp-Ups:**
  * **Ather Energy:** Expanding Hosur plant by 6,000 units/month immediately; AURIC Phase 1 adding 42,000/month in Q4 FY27 and 12,000/month by Q2 FY28 to reach 11.4 lakh units total annual capacity.
  * **Bajaj Auto:** Expanding overall monthly plant capacity from 6.25 lakh to 7.75 lakh units over 6–9 months; Chetak scaling to 80,000 units/month by Q4 FY27.
  * **Hero MotoCorp:** VIDA EV capacity doubled to 30,000 units/month in Sept, targeting 45,000/month in Q4 FY27 and >80,000/month by Q2 FY28. Investing over Rs 700 crore in Tirupati Global Parts Center 2.0.
  * **M&M:** Scaling SUV/EV capacity from 64,500/month to 82,000/month by FY27 exit, 92,000/month in FY28, and 132,000/month by FY31. Land acquisition commenced for greenfield expansion at Nagpur.
  * **Eicher Motors (Royal Enfield):** Preponing Cheyyar Module 2 (+1 lakh units) to Q1 FY28. Approved Rs 1,225 crore AP greenfield Phase 1 (+4.5 lakh units) for 2029–2030.
  * **Ola Electric:** Commissioned its 3.5 GWh Gigafactory in September.
  * **Zelio E Mobility:** Commissioned its 4th plant in Patan (Hisar) adding 72,000 units/year capacity, taking total installed capacity to 3.12 lakh units/year.
* **Components, Accessories & Agriculture Greenfield Spending:**
  * **Studds Accessories:** Commenced production at its new plant on Oct 30, 2026 (+1.5 million units annual helmet/box capacity; total capacity now ~11 million units).
  * **Escorts Kubota:** Completed greenfield land acquisition (Rs 400–450 crore) for a planned Rs 2,000 crore project spread over 3 to 4 years.
  * **Hero Motors:** Investing Rs 200 crore from IPO proceeds into growth capex over 2 years and setting up a Bengaluru EV system plant operational by March/April 2027.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance Upgrades:**
  * **Ashok Leyland:** Raised full-year domestic M&HCV industry volume growth guidance to double-digit / mid-teens (up from high single-digit in Q1).
  * **Bajaj Auto:** Raised Chetak EV monthly capacity target to 80,000 by Q4 FY27 (vs 60,000/month guided in Q1).
* **Guidance Cuts & Tone Shifts:**
  * **Tata Motors PV:** Cut JLR FY27 EBIT margin guidance to 2%–3% and cash outflow guidance to ~GBP 1.25 billion (due to a sharp 45% drop in China wholesales). Tone shifted from resilient optimism to heightened caution.
  * **M&M:** Cut full-year domestic tractor industry growth guidance from +5% to flattish (0%).
  * **VST Tillers Tractors:** Revised domestic tractor outlook from growth to flat due to severe regional droughts.
  * **Escorts Kubota:** Expected single-digit degrowth for full-year FY27 tractor industry.
* **New Operational Risks & Discrepancies:**
  * **Ashok Leyland:** Flagged accounting system discrepancies at Hinduja Leyland Finance (HLF) flagged by auditors requiring independent review.
  * **Ola Electric:** Flagged charger unavailability bottlenecks that delayed ~4,000–5,000 deliveries in October.
  * **Tata Motors PV:** Announced a voluntary redundancy program at JLR to reduce 4,000 salaried headcount (~3,000 leaving in FY27), driving GBP 530 million in restructuring charges.

### What we were watching
* — not discussed · None of the 17 companies discussed the government's November 2026 ban on lithium-ion battery black mass exports in their Q2 FY27 earnings call summaries.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Post-Festive Channel Stocking:** Monitor inventory drawdown post-Diwali. Royal Enfield reported lean channel inventory at 7–8 days, whereas Escorts Kubota noted inventory build-up that requires H2 industry normalization.
* **Input Cost Pass-Through:** Watch the execution of Q3/Q4 price hikes across OEM and auto component portfolios to counter 150–200 bps of additional expected RM inflation (noted by **Tata Motors CV**, **Escorts Kubota**, and **Tube Investments**).
* **JLR Restructuring & China Strategy:** Execution of JLR’s 4,000 salaried headcount reduction program and performance of Range Rover BEV orders (~5,000 initial bookings).
* **EV Capacity Ramp-ups:** Commercial commissioning of Ather's AURIC plant Phase 1, Ola Electric's Gigafactory yield roadmap, and Hero MotoCorp scaling VIDA capacity to 45,000 units/month by Q4 FY27.
* **Farm Machinery Recovery:** Monitoring H2 harvest cash flows, non-agricultural rural income contributions (~70-75% of rural income per M&M), and government order execution in power tillers (VST Tillers).
