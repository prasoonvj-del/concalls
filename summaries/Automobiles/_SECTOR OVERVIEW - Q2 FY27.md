# Automobiles: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian automobile sector reflected robust top-line growth, with companies reporting revenue expansions ranging between 20% and 44% YoY (e.g., TVS Motor up 44%, Greaves Cotton up 44%, Tata Motors up 42%, Bajaj Auto up 34%, Hero MotoCorp up 21%, JK Tyre up 20%). Volume growth was strong across domestic and international markets, driven by festive stocking, infrastructure activity, and surging EV adoption. However, this growth was universally challenged by severe, broad-based commodity and raw material inflation (steel, aluminum, copper, crude derivatives, and rubber), prompting widespread price hikes and strategic cost controls.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
* **Consensus:** Managements across the board noted healthy underlying demand supported by GDP growth, infrastructure projects, and the festive season. Electric Vehicle (EV) adoption hit new milestones, with Ather noting E2W penetration crossing 11.2%, Bajaj highlighting 25% scooter EV penetration, and TVS/Hero posting triple-digit or high double-digit EV retail growth. 
* **Divergence:** Managements held contrasting views on the state of rural and lower-income consumers. **Bajaj Auto** highlighted an increasingly accentuated "K-shape" recovery where the weaker sections of society are under pressure, posing potential risks for Q4 rural demand. Conversely, **Hero MotoCorp** and **JK Tyre** noted that rural demand and farm sector traction are performing equally or better than urban areas, supported by strong sowing and government infrastructure pushes.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Cost Pressures:** All companies reported margin headwinds from multi-quarter commodity inflation. **JK Tyre** noted a cumulative ~30% increase in raw material costs over six months due to West Asia tensions and crude volatility. **Tata Motors** cited steel prices at a 52-week high (up ~40% YoY), impacting margins by 590 bps YoY. **Ather Energy** faced a 9.4% gross margin hit from elevated aluminum, copper, plastics, and memory costs.
* **Mitigation & Pricing:** Companies implemented proactive pricing actions and cost efficiencies. **Bajaj Auto** took a ~30% price hike in October to cover Q3 inflation; **JK Tyre** implemented 11–12% Net Realization hikes over 6 months with another 4–5% planned; **Tata Motors** executed four price hikes across CY26; and **Hero MotoCorp** raised prices by ~Rs 1,000 per unit on October 1. **Greaves Cotton** and **JK Tyre** noted time lags in passing costs onto B2B and replacement/OEM markets, creating short-term margin compression.

## Capex & capacity
* **Ather Energy:** Revising total installed capacity target up to 11.4 lakh units annually by Q2 FY28, driven by expansions at Hosur and AURIC Phase 1.
* **Bajaj Auto:** Expanding total manufacturing capacity from 6.25 lakh to 7.75 lakh units/month over the next 6–9 months; Chetak capacity targeted at 80,000/month by Q4.
* **Greaves Cotton:** EV capacity running at ~40,000 units/month (~40% utilization); invested Rs 331 crore in GEML rights issue.
* **Hero MotoCorp:** Maintaining full-year capex guidance at Rs 1,500 crore (H1 deployed ~Rs 600 crore); EV capacity scaling to 45,000 units/month in Q4 and >80,000 units/month by Q2 of next financial year.
* **JK Tyre & Industries:** Guiding full-year capex at Rs 1,200 crore to Rs 1,300 crore (Rs 800 crore spent in H1) with capacity utilization above 90%.
* **TVS Motor Company:** Upgrading annual capacity to >8 million 2-wheelers and ~0.4 million 3-wheelers; EV capacity expanding from 60–65k to ~80k units.
* **Tata Motors:** Investment spending running comfortably within the guided 2% to 4% range (~2.5% of revenue); debottlenecking common supply chain commodities in-house.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Bajaj Auto:** Shifted tone from expecting supply chains to ease in Q1 to acknowledging "increasingly volatile and erratic" supply chains in Q2 that restricted EV plans to 75% of target. Explicitly flagged firming steel prices as a new Q3 cost pressure and noted the K-shape recovery becoming more accentuated. Delivered on the 150cc portfolio refresh and pushed Chetak capacity targets to Q4. *(Note: Other company summaries did not explicitly provide prior-quarter comparison details).*

## Company one-liners
* **Ather Energy:** Achieved historic profitability and positive cash flows while scaling capacity to meet surging E2W demand despite severe component inflation.
* **Bajaj Auto:** Delivered all-time high financial metrics backed by premiumization and scaling EVs, despite volatile supply chains and K-shaped demand pressures.
* **Greaves Cotton:** Accelerated its EV and core engine growth trajectory while navigating raw material lags and cleaning up legacy write-offs.
* **Hero MotoCorp:** Captured broad-based market share and turned VIDA gross margin positive while maintaining strict overhead control amid commodity headwinds.
* **JK Tyre:** Set record turnover records and robust volume growth, leveraging price hikes to offset a cumulative 30% surge in raw material costs.
* **TVS Motor:** Outperformed industry growth across ICE and EV categories, delivering strong margin expansion and moving forward with its finance arm de-merger.
* **Tata Motors:** Generated stellar free cash flows and robust revenue growth, successfully buffering steep steel and rubber inflation through operating leverage and price actions.

## What to watch next quarter
* The persistence of commodity and raw material inflation (specifically steel, crude derivatives, and rubber) and whether subsequent price hikes fully protect operating margins.
* The realization and absorption of festive season demand and whether rural demand trends validate Bajaj's K-shape caution or Hero/JK Tyre's optimism.
* Execution and ramp-up timelines of massive EV capacity expansions (Ather, Bajaj, Hero, TVS) and resolution of supply chain bottlenecks in EV batteries and proprietary parts.
* Progress on strategic corporate actions, including TVS Motor’s consumer finance de-merger timeline and Tata Motors' integration of the IVECO open offer.

*Based on: Ather Energy Ltd, Bajaj Auto Ltd, Greaves Cotton Ltd, Hero MotoCorp Ltd, JK Tyre & Industries Ltd, TVS Motor Company Ltd, Tata Motors Ltd*
