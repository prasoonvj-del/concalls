# Mufin Green Finance Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Automobiles*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial growth, successful pivot to digital lending, and improved profitability metrics, despite legacy climate finance drag.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained AUM target of ~Rs 2,500 crore by end of FY27.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from "transitioning" to "optimistic" about the new digital model; management now emphasizes "profitable lending" over pure AUM growth.
- **New topics:** RBI approval of AD-II (forex) license; increased focus on cybersecurity (hiring senior security personnel, migrating to AWS, deploying SentinelOne); Arthum platform launch for contract worker financing.
- **Old promises:** Employee count reduction target of 300 was met (current: 306).

### Key numbers
- **AUM:** Rs 1,766 crore (up 10.4% QoQ, 71.8% YoY).
- **Disbursements:** Rs 590 crore (up 31% QoQ).
- **Net Interest Income:** Rs 33.27 crore.
- **Profit Before Tax:** Rs 19.47 crore (up from Rs 18.82 crore in Q1).
- **Gross NPA:** 1.77% (improved from 1.91% in Q1).
- **Net NPA:** 1.50% (improved from 1.63% in Q1).
- **Employee Count:** 306 (down from ~500 in FY26).
- **AUM per employee:** Rs 5.77 crore (up from Rs 1.89 crore).
- **ROA:** ~4% (up from ~1.5% in FY26).

### Highlights
- **Portfolio Pivot:** Climate financing reduced to 19.1% of AUM; digital lending (Mediclaim Financing + Salary Saathi) now comprises 44% of AUM.
- **New Product:** Launched financing for contract workers via Arthum (20% stake held by Mufin); targeting 3-5% monthly pricing.
- **Regulatory Milestone:** Received RBI approval for Authorised Dealer Category II (AD-II) license.
- **Legacy Drag:** Climate financing portfolio has ~Rs 25 crore of "pain" remaining; management reiterated that 99% of current NPA relates to the old EV book.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Interest expense grew faster than interest income due to holding surplus liquidity for 3-4 months of EMI obligations.

### Guidance & outlook
- **AUM Target:** ~Rs 2,500 crore by end of FY27.
- **Capital Needs:** No fresh capital required until AUM reaches Rs 3,000–3,500 crore (expected by FY28).
- **Profitability:** Aiming for annual PAT of Rs 500 crore without equity dilution.
- **Future Growth:** Planning to launch life insurance financing in ~1 month.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Retail health insurance premiums growing at 31% YoY; 40-45% of first-time buyers opt for monthly payments.
- Management expects premium financing market to grow at 20-25% annually.
- GST on health insurance reduced to zero, acting as a growth driver.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- **Cost of Borrowing:** Declined by >100 bps over the last year.
- **Operating Expenses:** Increased 27.7% YoY, while NII improved 52.4%.
- **Borrowing Profile:** Diversified; onboarded IDBI Bank and South Indian Bank in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- *Not discussed.*

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- **Total Assets:** ~Rs 2,400 crore.
- **Net Worth:** Rs 604 crore.
- **Debt-to-Equity:** 2.89x (well within 4x lender limit).
- **Borrowings:** Rs 1,746 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- **Cybersecurity:** Management acknowledged heightened threats to digital lenders; investing in infrastructure (AWS, SentinelOne) and senior security staff to mitigate.
- **Legacy Impact:** Admitted that the climate financing business was a "mistake" that impacted the balance sheet by Rs 20-25 crore.
- **Competition:** Acknowledged that financial products are replicable (e.g., 50+ players in Mediclaim financing), leading to potential pricing wars.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: Why still doing climate finance?** A: To honor long-term lines from international funds (e.g., US DFC); however, 25-30% of lenders have already agreed to reclassify these as impact lending.
- **Q: How to protect the business from competitors?** A: By building a "vault" of proprietary tech (e.g., PPI license, ERP for contract workers) and continuous innovation, rather than relying on a single product.
- **Q: Why no AI in underwriting?** A: Management stated AI is for unsecured loans; Mufin's products are secured/anchored by third parties (insurers/governments), making AI underwriting less relevant.
- **Q: Is tech infrastructure expensive?** A: Yes, but economies of scale will kick in as AUM grows, making it manageable.

### One-line takeaway
Mufin is successfully pivoting to a high-margin, tech-led digital lending model, with management prioritizing profitability and ROA over aggressive AUM expansion, while actively managing legacy EV portfolio risks.
