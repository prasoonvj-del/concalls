# MTAR Technologies Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported record quarterly revenue, robust EBITDA growth, and strong demand across key segments with higher H2 performance anticipated.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 406.8 crore (YoY growth of 200%, QoQ growth of 12.8% from Rs 360.7 crore; H1 FY27 revenue compared against H1 FY26 not explicitly stated as a single total, but Q2 YoY revenue was Rs 406.8 crore vs Rs 135.6 crore).
- **EBITDA:** Rs 99.2 crore (YoY growth of 483.3% from Rs 17 crore; QoQ growth of 16.6% from Rs 85.1 crore).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 24.4% (vs 12.5% in Q2 FY26 and 23.6% in Q1 FY27).
- **PAT:** Rs 59.8 crore (YoY growth of 1,309.5% from Rs 4.2 crore; QoQ growth of 19.1% from Rs 50.2 crore).
- **PAT Margin:** 14.7% (vs 3.1% in Q2 FY26 and 13.9% in Q1 FY27).
- **PBT:** Rs 80.3 crore (YoY growth of 1,314.6% from Rs 5.7 crore; QoQ growth of 19.1% from Rs 67.4 crore).
- **Gross Margin:** 46.8% (vs 45.5% in Q1 FY27).
- **EPS:** Rs 19.45 for Q2 FY27; Rs 35.78 for H1 FY27.
- **Order Inflows:** Rs 4,102 crore during H1 FY27 (noted with an inaudible transcript marker near timestamp 13:00, but stated as record order inflows).
- **Civil Nuclear Order Book:** Rs 820 crore.
- **Working Capital Days:** 50 days as of September 2026 (down from 172 days in March 2026).
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Rs 350 crore in H1 FY27 (vs Rs 39.8 crore in H1 FY26).
- **Total Debt:** Rs 465 crore (Long-term debt: Rs 189 crore; Short-term debt: Rs 276 crore; Investments stand at Rs 358 crore, leaving net debt of Rs 107 crore).
- **Capex Plan:** Total planned capex of approximately Rs 500 crore (Rs 350 crore for fuel cells, Rs 150 crore for oil & gas, aerospace, and other verticals; funded 75% through debt and 25% through internal accruals).

### Highlights
- Delivered highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 406.8 crore in Q2 FY27.
- Fuel cells Phase 2 capacity expansion nearing completion, operational by end of November; Phase 3 expected to be completed by June 2028.
- Commenced exports of ball screws, marking a milestone in import substitution.
- Data center infrastructure solutions vertical working on first article orders valued at ~Rs 45 crores, with potential to scale to Rs 350–450 crores over the next 2 years.
- Oil and gas dedicated manufacturing facility expected to be operational by end of November; volume ramp-up expected from FY28.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1 FY27.
- Reaffirmed annual guidance of 80% revenue growth and 24% EBITDA margin, while stating they expect to perform on the higher side of the guidance.
- Working capital target is ~100 days by the end of FY27, with a long-term goal of ~150 days.
- ROCE expected to improve to ~23% by the end of FY27 (from 17.2% in FY26).
- Product division revenue expected to be close to Rs 500 crore for the year.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Civil nuclear sector backed by Government of India's target of 100 GW of nuclear power capacity by 2047; company submitted budgetary quotations for upcoming fast breeder reactor programs and private sector tenders for Mahi Banswara reactors.
- Fuel cells sector witnessing accelerated growth and strong industrial tailwinds from Bloom Energy.
- MNC aerospace business seeing robust demand with qualified products seeing volume ramp-up; targeting over Rs 150 crore revenue from MNC aerospace in FY27.
- Domestic defense focusing on structural assembly programs for AMCA (main landing gear support and fuselage door assemblies) and volume production for LCA Tejas actuators.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin expanded to 46.8% due to a better product revenue mix.
- EBITDA margin expansion driven by higher revenue, better mix, and operating leverage.
- Minor cost increases noted in certain consumables due to geopolitical situations.
- Forex gain of Rs 2.3 crore included in Q2.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Total capex outlay of ~Rs 500 crore across FY26 and FY27 to support multi-fold expansions.
- Fuel cell Phase 3 to be housed in a new dedicated facility completed by June 2028.
- Evaluating establishment of new greenfield manufacturing facilities for aerospace and defense.
- Planned capex expected to generate at least 3x revenue compared to FY27 targets.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Total debt at Rs 465 crore (Long-term: Rs 189 crore, Short-term: Rs 276 crore); net debt after adjusting investments of Rs 358 crore is Rs 107 crore.
- Working capital reduced to 50 days due to customer advances, better payment term negotiations, and expected GST refunds of Rs 70–80 crore per year.
- Operating cash flows surged to Rs 350 crore in H1 FY27.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Geopolitical situations causing minor cost increases in certain consumables.
- Long qualification periods required for aerospace components.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhijeet Singh (Systematix Group) asked about fuel cell Phase 2 capacity and medium-term order inflows.** Management replied Phase 2 is ~30% higher than existing capacity, multi-fold expansion ready by June next year, and order inflows remain continuous and substantial.
2. **Vipraw Srivastava (PhillipCapital) asked about sustainability of the sharp improvement in working capital days.** Management explained that 50 days was aided by customer advances and negotiations, but working capital will rise to ~100 days by fiscal end due to higher H2 execution and inventory stocking for next year.
3. **Balasubramanian (Arihant Capital) asked about the revenue potential and order inflows in the data center vertical.** Management confirmed an 8x requirement relative to the Rs 45 crore first article order, translating to a potential Rs 350–400 crore in order inflows over the next 2 years.
4. **Gaurav Nagori (Avendus Spark) questioned a sharp drop in product segment execution and classification.** Management and IR clarified that ~Rs 60 crores of box-build supplies were misclassified under clean energy instead of products, and restated that the product division is on track for a ~Rs 500 crore annual run rate.
5. **Mihir Manohar (Trust Mutual Fund) asked if local government challenges or project issues kept guidance capped despite strong H1.** Management clarified there are no local government challenges or hurdles, and they prefer not to alter guidance mid-year while remaining confident of beating it.

### One-line takeaway
MTAR Technologies delivered a record Q2 FY27 with a 200% YoY revenue jump and 483% EBITDA growth, backed by robust execution, substantially lower working capital days, and strong multi-vertical expansion visibility.
