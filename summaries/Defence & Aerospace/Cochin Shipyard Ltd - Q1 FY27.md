# Cochin Shipyard Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-18 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27  
**Tone:** Mixed - Revenue recorded modest YoY growth while net profit declined due to lower interest income and execution mix, alongside major strategic expansion announcements.

### Key numbers
- **Turnover (Revenue):** Rs 1,094.21 crore in Q1 FY27 vs Rs 1,068.59 crore in Q1 FY26 (up 2.40% YoY).
- **PBT:** Rs 202.49 crore in Q1 FY27 vs Rs 249.54 crore in Q1 FY26.
- **PAT:** Rs 151.45 crore in Q1 FY27 vs Rs 187.82 crore in Q1 FY26 (down 19.36% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** ~24% for Q1 FY27.
- **PAT Margin:** ~14% for Q1 FY27.
- **Order Book:** Current unexecuted order book stands at ~Rs 22,000 crore; expected to reach ~Rs 27,000 crore upon contract signing of Next-Generation Survey Vessels (NGSV).

### Highlights
- **Drydocks World (DDW) JV:** Board approved a 50:50 joint venture with Drydocks World, Dubai, to own, operate, and manage the International Ship Repair Facility (ISRF) at Kochi. ISRF will be transferred on a slump sale basis for Rs 1,800 crore (CSL receives 50% in cash, 50% in JV equity). The JV will add 10 workstations to ISRF.
- **Block Fabrication Facility (BFF):** Strategic talks with HD KSOE for a JV failed to reach mutually agreed definitive terms. CSL will proceed independently at Vallarpadam, Kochi with a smaller block fabrication capacity of 60,000 tonnes per annum.
- **Tuticorin Facility:** Won an e-auction for a 30-year lease of ~110 acres land and 17.29 acres waterfront at V.O. Chidambaranar Port, Tuticorin for Rs 305.76 crore to build a hybrid shipbuilding and repair facility.
- **Green Maritime Propulsion JV:** Incorporated in June 2026 with HBL Power Systems (CSL holds 40%, HBL holds 60%) to marinize battery energy storage systems. Target revenue is Rs 640 crore by Year 5 with an expected ~20% EBITDA margin.
- **Vessel Deliveries:** Delivered 3 vessels in Q1 FY27 (ASW Shallow Water Craft for Navy, MPV export for German client, double-ended Ro-Ro ferry). Subsidiary Udupi CSL delivered 3 vessels (two cargo vessels for Norway, one 70-tonne tug for Polestar Maritime).

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Management maintained annual top-line growth guidance at ~12% YoY (typically landing between 12% and 15%).
- **EBITDA Margins:** Management expects blended EBITDA margins to be ~14%–15% on a conservative basis going forward.
- **Contract Timeline:** Expects to sign the L1 NGSV contract (~Rs 5,000 crore) around November 2026. Repeat nomination order for a 12,000 m³ dredger from Dredging Corporation of India (~Rs 1,300 crore) expected within 2 months.
- **Deliveries:** Planned delivery schedule of 10 vessels in FY27 (including 2 ASW crafts by Dec 2026, 2 MPVs, 1 suction hopper dredger, 2 commissioning service vessels, 1 zero-emission container vessel).

### Demand & industry commentary
- **Defense Pipeline:** Navy RFPs/tenders in the pipeline include 4 Landing Platform Docks (LPDs) valued at ~Rs 32,000 crore, 12 Mine Countermeasure Vessels (MCMVs) at ~Rs 36,000 crore, and 7 P-17 Bravo frigates at ~Rs 49,000 crore.
- **Commercial Aggregation:** Government of India initiative foresees demand for ~432 commercial vessels over 10 years. Commercial pipeline includes MR Tankers (indicative bid cost ~Rs 1,700 crore) and Aframax Tankers (indicative bid cost ~Rs 2,600 crore).
- **Green Tug Transition Program (GTTP):** Mandates zero-emission harbor tugs aiming for 50% green tugs by 2035–2040, representing a ~Rs 1,000 crore investment opportunity in marine batteries over 10 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins in prior years were elevated due to high-margin nominated defense contracts (e.g., Aircraft Carrier construction/refits) and interest income from cash reserves of Rs 2,000–3,000 crore.
- Cash reserves were deployed into funding capex (ISRF and drydock), eliminating the high recurring interest income.
- Segment margin expectation: Shipbuilding ~10%–12% margin (70% of revenue mix) and Ship repair ~20%–24% margin (30% of revenue mix).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- **Total Capex Spend:** ~Rs 6,000–6,500 crore planned over the next 5 years, funded primarily via an 80:20 debt-to-equity ratio.
- **Government Subsidies:** CSL applied for a 25% capex subsidy under the GoI Shipbuilding Development Scheme and expects interest subvention of up to 3% under the Interest Incentivisation Fund.
- **BFF Capacity:** New independent facility will add 60,000 tonnes/year block fabrication capacity to CSL's existing 12,000 tonnes/year, taking total steel throughput capacity to 72,000 tonnes/year.
- **Vadinar Facility:** Joint project with Deendayal Port Authority featuring two floating drydocks (handling 250m long vessels). Environment clearance expected shortly; operationalization within 36 months of clearance.
- **Revenue Potential from New Capex:**
  - ISRF: Rs 600 crore over next 2 years, scaling to Rs 1,000–1,200 crore in 5 years.
  - Vadinar: Rs 300 crore initial, scaling to Rs 500–600 crore post operationalization.
  - Tuticorin: Rs 650–700 crore initial, scaling to Rs 1,800–2,000 crore over 7–8 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Operating cash flows were negative in FY26 due to tail-ended payment terms in European export orders (10% signing, 10% keel laying, 10% launching, 70% delivery).
- Management expects cash flows to turn positive in FY27 as 10 vessel deliveries take place and milestone cash inflows are collected.
- Target Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) for new project investments is 14%–15%.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Finalization timelines for naval defense contracts (CCS approvals, PNCs) remain prolonged and outside company control.
- Indigenous Aircraft Carrier 2 (IAC-2) lacks immediate visibility as it has not yet reached the Approval of Necessity (AoN) stage.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q (Mohit Chaurasiya):** Why was operating cash flow negative despite high FY26 PAT, and what is the cash flow outlook?  
  **A:** Cash flow was negative because European commercial export contracts pay 70% on final delivery. With 10 vessels delivering in FY27, cash flow will turn positive in FY27.
- **Q (Abhishek Poddar):** Why has guided EBITDA margin reduced to ~14% compared to previous years?  
  **A:** Past margins included significant nominated aircraft carrier orders and interest income from Rs 2,000–3,000 crore cash reserves, which have now been deployed into completed capex projects.
- **Q (Deepak Krishnan):** Which defense tender programs offer the highest probability for CSL?  
  **A:** While CSL bids for all programs, LPDs (~Rs 32,000 crore) and MCMVs (~Rs 36,000 crore) suit CSL's heavy platform infrastructure best due to existing aircraft carrier building docks.
- **Q (Dipen Vakil):** Will the DDW 50:50 JV lead to any market share loss or order conflict for CSL?  
  **A:** No. ISRF handles vessels only up to 130m / 6,000 tonnes. CSL defense/government vessels in this class will be taken by CSL and executed by JV, while DDW will bring incremental global clients. Larger vessels remain 100% with CSL.

### One-line takeaway
CSL is transitioning to lower normalized operational margins (14%–15%) while aggressively expanding capacity via a Rs 6,000+ crore debt-subsidized capex drive and strategic global JVs.
