# Bharatrohan Airborne Innovations Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-23 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue grew 28.6% YoY, EBITDA outpaced revenue growing 44.5% with a 200 bps margin expansion, and operating cash flow outflow narrowed by Rs 11.2 crore YoY.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 42.34 cr (up 28.6% YoY)
- **EBITDA:** INR 7.47 cr (up 44.5% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 17.7% (up 200 bps YoY)
- **Gross Margin:** 29.7% (up 480 bps YoY from 24.9%)
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT):** INR 6.17 cr (up 30.4% YoY)
- **Tax Expense:** INR 2.03 cr
- **EPS:** INR 2.08 for the half year
- **Net Worth:** ~INR 91.60 cr (Book value of INR 46 per share)
- **Borrowings:** INR 4.4 cr (Debt-to-equity ratio of 0.05)
- **Inventory:** INR 43.30 cr
- **Trade Receivables:** INR 27.07 cr
- **Capex Deployed:** INR 6.54 cr
- **Net Operating Cash Flow Outflow:** Narrowed by INR 11.2 cr YoY to INR 2.74 cr (pre-working capital cash generation of INR 7.56 cr)
- **Network Reach:** 2,66,537 acres and 60,196 farmers across 8 states; 28,212 paying customers for CropAssure (up from 12,729 in March 2025).

### Highlights
- Crop monitoring revenue grew 53.8% YoY, contributing one-third of total revenue.
- Deepened rural footprint by operating 3 new Pragati Kendra in Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Uttar Pradesh.
- US subsidiary BharatRohan INC became fully operational to establish a global export channel.
- Board approved a 15% stake in a soon-to-be-incorporated BharatRohan Defence Private Limited (funded via internal accruals); remaining 85% held by promoters.
- *Pattern check:* The positive H1 FY27 performance aligns with the recent investor deck released on November 17, 2026.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management outlined 5 strict priorities for H2 FY27: procuring exclusively against confirmed buyer demand, tightening working capital cycles, accelerating receivable collections, launching SafeAssure in the US by Q1 FY27 (backed by FSSC 22000 certification), completing IPO equipment procurement, and converting successful solar/tech pilots into binding commercial contracts.

### Demand & industry commentary
- El Nino weakened the Kharif crop in India, making farmers more conservative, but demand for high-value precision advisory remains strong as it reduces input costs and improves profit margins.
- Growing domestic and international consumer demand for traceable, residue-free spices, pulses, and oilseeds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Raw material costs grew 20.5% (slower than revenue growth), driving a 480 bps expansion in gross margins to 29.7%.
- Employee expenses front-loaded by INR 2.5 cr for tech, field, and international teams ahead of the rabi season and US launch.
- Depreciation increased to INR 1.06 cr as new IPO-funded equipment came into use.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Deployed INR 6.54 cr in capex targeted at hyperspectral imaging technology, drones, and revenue-generating equipment.
- Fleet manufacturing transitioned to 70% domestic component sourcing (Praveer X6 drone V3.1).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stands at approximately INR 91.60 cr with conservative borrowings of INR 4.4 cr.
- Inventory remained nearly flat at INR 43.30 cr, while trade receivables grew at a fraction of revenue growth to INR 2.74 cr (operating cash flow outflow narrowed to INR 2.74 cr).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Weather uncertainties (El Nino impact on Kharif crop) and conservative farmer behavior.
- Transition from the 3% Startup India tax rate to the standard corporate tax rate of approximately 33%, increasing tax expense to INR 2.03 cr.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Yash Vishariya asked about the El Nino impact on paying willingness, high receivables/inventory, and US subsidiary fundraise/valuation.** Management replied that paying subscriptions grew despite weather headwinds due to proven farmer ROI, receivables follow a 3-4 month post-harvest cycle (with collections improving), inventory churns constantly against order fulfillment, and US subsidiary valuation details will be disclosed upon definitive agreements.
- **Parin Gala asked about the FSSC 22000 certification stage, pharmaceutical quality screening, defense funding valuation, and the micro-irrigation MOU.** Management stated that the FSSC audit process is underway with no fixed timeline; pharma screening remains at an early POC stage pending regulatory validation; defense valuation cannot be disclosed pre-incorporation; and the micro-irrigation MOU targets precise per-plant fertigation/drenching for banana and onion crops.

### One-line takeaway
BharatRohan delivered strong H1 FY27 top-line and operating profit growth alongside gross margin expansion and improved operating cash flows, scaling its tech-driven precision agriculture ecosystem and expanding globally.
