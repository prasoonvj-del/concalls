# Astra Microwave Products Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-17 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Backed by a record-breaking order book, strong order intake, and reiterated revenue guidance despite a modest quarter due to temporary approval delays.

### Key numbers
- Revenue for Q1: Rs 182 crore
- Q1 order inflow: Rs 185 crore
- Standalone order book as of quarter-end: Rs 2,156 crore (66% defense, 34% space, metrology, and hydrology)
- Consolidated order book (including service orders of ~Rs 244 crore): Rs 2,849 crore
- Consolidated order book as of date (post-July order): Rs 4,300 crore
- July order from HAL for Uttam Radar critical subsystems: Rs 2,205 crore (~Rs 1,870 crore without GST)
- Astra Rafael Comsys (ARC) order book: Rs 836 crore (targeted sales of Rs 360 crore for the current year)
- FY27 revenue target: Rs 1,350 crore (+/- Rs 25 crore), representing >15% YoY growth (15% to 20%)
- Next year revenue guidance: Rs 1,600 crore (+/- Rs 50 crore)
- Space sector contribution to FY27 sales: Rs 120 crore to Rs 150 crore

### Highlights
- Secured a landmark order worth ~Rs 2,205 crore from HAL in July for Uttam Radar critical subsystems (execution period ~5 years, with Phase 1 of 12 units by September '27).
- Emerged as L1/L2 for the Active Antenna Array Unit (AAAU) of the AMCA program alongside BEL.
- Secured a ~Rs 45 crore contract from Bharat Electronics (BEL) for MMIC chipsets, plus contracts from BDL and the Space Application Center.
- Successfully conducted technology demonstrations in July in Jalandhar for two first-of-a-kind products: the electromagnetic wall and the vehicle-mounted anti-drone system.
- Successfully migrated to SAP/HANA without business disruption.
- Management transition: S.G. Reddy is stepping down as Managing Director, passing the baton to M.V. Reddy and Atim Kabra.
- Space and Weather divisions to be spun off as a separate listed company operating independently from April 1, 2027, with targeted first-year sales of Rs 300+ crore and 18%-20% PBT margins.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets top-line growth of 15% to 20%, resulting in ~Rs 1,350 crore for FY27.
- FY28 revenue expected at ~Rs 1,600 crore (+/- Rs 50 crore).
- Guided for order intake of ~Rs 1,600 crore for the entire financial year (excluding Uttam Radar).
- Long-term order intake visibility of Rs 8,000 crore to Rs 10,000 crore over the next 3 to 4 years to be executed over 5 to 6 years.
- Expecting a completely different growth trajectory from FY29 onwards as production orders for Uttam and QRSAM ramp up.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's defense industry is shifting toward indigenous design, development, and manufacture of advanced subsystems and systems.
- Astra is positioned across major airborne platforms: Tejas (Uttam Radar, AATRU pod jammer), AEW&C Mk-1 and Mk-2, Su-30 upgrades (Virupaksha radar, Angad EW suite), and AMCA.
- Strong sustained demand expected in metrology due to Mission Mausam and extreme weather challenges.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Interest costs down substantially in Q1 due to minimal utilization of overdraft facilities, backed by positive cash available at the end of the last financial year.
- Management expects EBITDA margins to be similar to last year with a positive delta increase.
- Management highlighted supply chain stretch risks globally as countries restock defense inventories.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Invested steadily in automated process engineering workflows and advanced infrastructure to handle volume and complexity.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Standalone order book at Rs 2,156 crore; consolidated order book at Rs 2,849 crore (reaching Rs 4,300 crore as of date).
- Positive operating cash flow achieved; no equity dilution planned for working capital.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Temporary delays in customer approvals and closing late-stage technical issues impacted Q1 revenue.
- Stretch supply chains across borders due to global defense restocking.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Ashok Thawani (Clear Blue Capital Advisors) asked about the revenue guidance discrepancy between press release and presentation.** Management clarified that the correct target is in excess of 15%, specifically 15% to 20% growth (~Rs 1,350 crore).
2. **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs) inquired about the opportunity size for EM walls and vehicle-mounted counter drone systems, and P-75(I) orders.** Management stated these product revenues are 100% upside and not factored into guidance. For Shatrughat and Samaghat (P-75(I)), they expect ~Rs 100 crore to Rs 120 crore combined.
3. **Bhavya Gandhi (Bajaj Alternate Investment Management) asked about the execution timeline for the Uttam Radar order.** Management replied the execution period is 5 years (by FY32), with Phase 1 (12 numbers) by September '27, and ramping up to 25+ units annually.
4. **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities) asked about the export segment roadmap.** Management explained that historical exports were BTP/offset-based, but they are now focusing on BTS solutions, MMIC components (with U.S. and European leads), and comprehensive defense solutions.
5. **Prerit Jain (Motilal Oswal) asked about the working capital trend for the full year.** Management noted that Q1/Q2 working capital looks better due to previous year execution comfort, while pressure builds in Q3/Q4; full-year working capital is expected to remain similar to the previous year.
6. **Mithoon (Kyaivah Advisors) asked about the demerged Space and Weather entity's financials.** Management stated the standalone entity is expected to do Rs 300+ crore with 18% to 20% PBT margins in its first year, launching its own satellite within the first 6 months.

### One-line takeaway
Astra Microwave reported a modest Q1 due to temporary delays but sits on a record-breaking Rs 4,300 crore order book, backed by a massive Rs 2,205 crore Uttam Radar order and a robust 5-year growth visibility targeting 15%-20% top-line growth for FY27.
