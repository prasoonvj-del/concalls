# Azad Engineering Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-14 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong financial performance with steady revenue growth, margin expansion, and a major strategic milestone of delivering India's first indigenous turbojet engine.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue from Operations:** Rs 170.5 crore, up 26.8% YoY (from Rs 134.5 crore in Q1 FY26) and 8.3% QoQ (from Rs 157.4 crore in Q4 FY26).
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 172.6 crore, up 25.9% YoY.
- **Standalone Reported EBITDA:** Rs 64 crore, up 32.1% YoY (from Rs 48.5 crore in Q1 FY26) and 10.9% QoQ (from Rs 57.8 crore in Q4 FY26).
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 64.4 crore (EBITDA margin of 37.3%).
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 37.6%, expanding 150 bps YoY (from 36.1%) and 90 bps QoQ (from 36.7%).
- **Standalone Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 36.4 crore, up 21.2% YoY (from Rs 30 crore in Q1 FY26), with a PAT margin of 21.3%; EPS of Rs 5.63 per share.
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 35.2 crore.
- **Aerospace & Defense Segment Growth:** Up 24.7% YoY.
- **Energy and Oil & Gas Segment Growth:** Up 26.7% YoY.
- **Other Income:** Rs 4 crore (down from Rs 9.1 crore in Q1 FY26 and Rs 17 crore in Q4 FY26 due to normalized foreign currency dynamics).

### Highlights
- Azad Engineering successfully manufactured, assembled, and delivered India's first indigenous expendable turbojet engine to GTRE, DRDO, and the Ministry of Defence, marking a strategic shift from component manufacturer to fully integrated propulsion system player.
- The company inaugurated its fourth dedicated lean manufacturing facility (7,600 square meters) custom-built for Baker Hughes in April 2026.
- 80% of expansion initiatives from FY26 are stabilized as of Q1, with remaining activities on track for mid-Q2 completion and full closure by Q3.
- Employee headcount/costs increased to build bench strength for anticipated Q3/Q4 volume ramps.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated long-term annual revenue growth guidance of over 25% while maintaining industry-leading profitability.
- Substantive revenue contributions from new dedicated facilities are expected to begin crystallizing in H2 FY27.
- Working capital days targeted around 200 days in H1 and 160 to 180 days by H2, driven by bill discounting facilities lowering debtor days from 170–180 days to 90 days.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Management noted synchronized high growth cycles and multi-decade structural tailwinds across energy security (advanced gas turbines) and aerospace/defense (aircraft demand and domestic self-reliance).
- Global OEMs have shown strong interest in Azad's upcoming hot section capabilities, with cross-sector applications for both land-based energy and aviation engines.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margin expansion supported by cost indigenization programs, onboarding domestic raw material suppliers (Sunflag and Star Wire), and backward integration.
- Sourcing local raw materials saves approximately 4% to 5% in global transportation costs.
- Foreign currency movements and treasury income moderated compared to elevated levels seen in Q4 FY26.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Civil construction for remaining units at the Azad Center of Excellence remains on track to wrap up within FY27.
- Machine installations are executed in modular, back-to-back phases aligned with customer roadmaps.
- Each dedicated customer plant at Tuniki Bollaram Industrial Park is poised to generate Rs 150 crore to Rs 180 crore at full capacity and utilization.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Finance costs reflect ongoing bill discounting charges, which will continue alongside operating cash flow improvements.
- Export receivables and foreign currency loans provide a natural hedge, which will cover the entire export book by Q4 through bill discounting facilities.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Complex execution environment involving simultaneous factory building, capacity ramp-ups, hiring, and training.
- Non-operating other income remains vulnerable to macroeconomic and foreign currency volatility beyond management's control.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Vikas Singh (ICICI Securities):** Asked about the timeline for turbojet engine orders and production scaling. *Management answered that weapon testing is 4 to 6 weeks away, followed by airworthiness certification, with production expected to scale up in a few months rather than years.*
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs):** Inquired about hot section TAM/adjacencies and margin trajectories compared to global peers. *Management stated that the MHI hot section facility will cater to both energy and aviation engines, and invited comparisons to high-cost global peers making healthy EBITDA margins.*
- **Gaurav (Avendus):** Questioned the value-add breakdown of the indigenous turbojet engine and why margin guidance remains conservative (32%-35%) despite delivering 37%-38%. *Management clarified that everything was done in-house except small washers/welding, and 32%-35% is maintained as a conservative calculation window.*
- **Basant Bansal (NBG Investment):** Asked about the sharp drop in raw material costs and key risks/worries. *Management attributed raw material drops to domestic sourcing (Sunflag/Star Wire) and local transport savings, while citing complex simultaneous execution as the primary internal focus area.*

### One-line takeaway
Azad Engineering delivered robust Q1 FY27 revenue growth of 26.8% and EBITDA margins of 37.6%, underscored by the historic delivery of India's first indigenous turbojet engine and steady stabilization of new dedicated OEM facilities.
