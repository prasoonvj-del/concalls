# Ideaforge Technology Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted highest-ever H1 revenue, sequential revenue growth, a return to profitability at the PAT level, and strong execution momentum.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (no explicit quantitative revenue/margin targets given, but delivery of remaining order book by Q3/Q4 reiterated).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more actively toward combat UAV roadmaps (ZOLT UCAV launch, hybrid variants) alongside fast-track procurements.
- New topics or risks: Mutual termination of the proposed U.S. joint venture agreement with First Breach; formal execution of the INR 151 crore debt funding agreement with the Technology Development Board (TDB) under the RDI scheme.
- Last quarter's promises: Execution of remaining opening order book is ongoing (expected to conclude in Q3); TDB funding moved from Letter of Intent to a signed agreement.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 91.3 crore (up 124% YoY, 33% QoQ)
- **Gross profit:** INR 49.6 crore (up 143% YoY, 47% QoQ)
- **Gross margin:** 54.3% (vs 50% in Q2 FY26, 49% in Q1 FY27)
- **EBITDA:** INR 23.2 crore (vs loss of INR 8.0 crore in Q2 FY26)
- **EBITDA margin:** 25.4%
- **PAT (Profit After Tax):** INR 6.5 crore (vs loss of INR 19.6 crore in Q2 FY26, loss of INR 2.6 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **H1 FY27 revenue:** INR 159.9 crore (highest ever H1)
- **H1 FY27 gross profit:** INR 83.2 crore (gross margin 52%)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 27.5 crore
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** INR 3.9 crore
- **Closing order book (as of Sep 30, 2026):** INR 202.2 crore (vs INR 256.8 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **TDB debt funding:** INR 151 crore (10-year tenure, 4% interest, matching principal grant/debt)

### Highlights
- Executed over 50% of the FY27 opening order book in H1.
- Launched the ZOLT UCAV variant in October, introducing combat capabilities and air-launched precision effects.
- ZOLT Hybrid platform demonstrated 100 km range and 6+ hours flight time with ISR payload and EW resilience in field testing.
- Signed an agreement with the Technology Development Board (TDB) for INR 151 crore in debt funding under the RDI scheme.
- Released automated stockpile monitoring and report generation on FLYGHT CLOUD.
- Mutually terminated the proposed joint venture agreement with First Breach in the U.S.; pursuing U.S. market independently via a wholly-owned subsidiary and partnerships.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to conclude remaining execution of the previous year's order book in Q3, with ongoing additions getting delivered through the year.
- Management anticipates further RFPs to emerge from the INR 20,000 crore fast-track procurement (FTP) opportunities and recent DAC approvals (INR 2.12 lakh crore).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand is supported by centralized defense acquisitions, command-level procurement (Delegation of Financial Powers to Defence Services 2026), and civil/law enforcement deployments.
- International engagements pursued in South America, Africa, and the U.S.; South American product demonstrations are progressing toward commercial opportunities.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin improved to 54.3% due to product mix variations, with heavier defense deliveries in the quarter.
- Persistent global supply chain constraints and customer delivery approval delays remain, though teams are working to minimize impact.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital deployment from recent fundraises targets debt repayment, product development, corporate purposes, and working capital.
- YETI logistics UAV program is advancing development milestones backed by the INR 151 crore TDB debt funding (matching contribution from ideaForge).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Closing order book stands at INR 202.2 crore.
- Secured INR 151 crore 10-year debt facility at 4% interest from TDB under the RDI scheme, structured as a matching milestone-based drawdown.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Persistent global supply chain constraints and delays in customer delivery approvals.
- Timing of procurement approvals, evaluations, and awards remain outside management's control.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Abhijeet Singh (Systematix) asked about the terms of the INR 151 crore TDB funding and ZOLT Hybrid timelines:** Management clarified it is a 10-year debt at 4% interest with a matching principal contribution from the company. ZOLT Hybrid is progressing to productionization this financial year, while YETI (100 kg / 100 km specs) will take well into the coming financial year.
2. **Dipen Vakil (PhillipCapital) asked about ZOLT variants and the INR 20,000 crore FTP opportunity pipeline:** Management stated electric ZOLT is delivered, hybrid/UAV variants are being fielded in FTPs, and RFPs for several opportunities are out or expected next quarter.
3. **Krishna Doshi (Ashika Institutional Equities) asked about export updates and Blue SUAS certification:** Management noted Blue SUAS paperwork is ongoing and takes time; international focus spans U.S., Canada, South America, and Africa, with civil/defense outcomes expected soon.
4. **Nikhil Gupta asked about combat capabilities and the ZOLT UCAV:** Management explained they use a mixed strategy of in-house platforms and partner ecosystems (especially for munitions/explosives), targeting multi-rotor loitering munitions and air-launched effects.
5. **Midhun James (Moat PMS) asked about eligibility for emergency/command procurements with combat drones in development:** Management stated they will participate directly where products fit (short-term multi-rotor loitering munitions) and partner with ecosystem players for integrated solutions where required.
6. **Tushar Khurana (Peace Wealth) asked about U.S. challenges, Middle East, and order book execution:** Management confirmed tracking U.S. challenges, exploring JVs/partnerships in the Middle East following a Raisina summit visit, and reiterated that prior-year order book execution concludes largely in Q3.

### One-line takeaway
ideaForge delivered a profitable Q2 with INR 91.3 crore in revenue and INR 6.5 crore PAT, driven by heavy defense execution, while expanding aggressively into combat UAVs and securing INR 151 crore in RDI debt funding.
