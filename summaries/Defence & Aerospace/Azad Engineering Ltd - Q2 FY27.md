# Azad Engineering Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-14 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Highest-ever quarterly revenue, 150 bps YoY EBITDA margin expansion, and completion of all foundation setups with revenue ramp-up starting.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained long-term annual revenue growth guidance of over 25%+.
- Tone and demand view: Shifted from setting up foundation to executing and converting capacity into revenue, with throughput beginning in Q3 and revenue ramping up from Q4.
- New topics or risks: Added updates on securing 50% advance against purchase orders ($4.5 million) from an OEM and activating bill discounting lines with ICICI Bank and DP World starting Q4 FY27 to manage working capital. Old costs (depreciation, employee costs, finance costs) highlighted as largely in the base.
- Last quarter's promises: Commissioned the remaining GE Vernova facilities in September, completing all six customer-dedicated facilities as promised for mid-Q2.

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue from Operations:** Rs 187.8 crore, up 31.6% YoY and 10.1% QoQ.
- **Consolidated Revenue from Operations:** Rs 190.0 crore, up 31.1% YoY.
- **Standalone Reported EBITDA:** Rs 70.4 crore, up 37.1% YoY and 9.9% QoQ.
- **Consolidated EBITDA:** Rs 72.3 crore (EBITDA margin of 37.9%).
- **Standalone EBITDA Margin:** 37.5%, expanding 150 bps YoY from 36%.
- **Standalone Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 30.8 crore (PAT margin of 16.4%).
- **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 30.9 crore.
- **H1 FY27 Standalone Revenue:** Rs 358.0 crore, up 29.3% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Standalone EBITDA:** Rs 134.5 crore, up 34.7% YoY (margin 37.5%).
- **H1 FY27 Standalone PAT:** Rs 67.2 crore, up 6.6% YoY (margin 18.7%).
- **Segment Revenue Growth (H1):** Energy, oil and gas grew 28.5% to Rs 290.0 crore (81% of revenue); Aerospace and defence grew 28.3% to Rs 60.4 crore (17% of revenue). Domestic revenue grew over 2.5x to Rs 43.0 crore (12% of revenue).
- **Other Income:** Rs 4.7 crore (including interest on fixed deposits of Rs 1.7 crore and foreign exchange gain of Rs 2.4 crore).
- **Depreciation:** Rs 21.8 crore.
- **Finance Costs:** Rs 12.2 crore.
- **Employee Costs:** Rs 50.3 crore.
- **Gross Margin:** 45.2% in Q2 (expansion of 150 bps YoY).
- **Total Debt / Cash:** Bank borrowings at Rs 536.4 crore (Term loan Rs 340.6 crore, working capital Rs 195.8 crore); cash and bank deposits of Rs 112.6 crore; net debt of Rs 423.8 crore.
- **Capex:** H1 capex spent at Rs 139.3 crore.

### Highlights
- Inaugurated two new dedicated lean manufacturing facilities for GE Vernova in September, completing all six customer-dedicated facilities (44,800 square meters for Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, GE Vernova, Siemens Energy, and Baker Hughes) at Tuniki Bollaram.
- BHEL approved Azad as a long-term source with a two-year rate contract worth about Rs 52.68 crore for gas turbine airfoils.
- Baker Hughes extended its supply agreement with Nuovo Pignone up to 2030.
- Baker Hughes presented Azad its Growth Collaboration Award, and GE Vernova presented its Delivery Partnership Award.
- Secured an agreement with one OEM to provide a 50% advance on each purchase order, expecting $4.5 million under this arrangement.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated long-term annual revenue growth guidance of over 25%+.
- Revenue expected to ramp up from Q4 FY27 onwards; all plants to be fully ramped up by Q2 FY28.
- Projected annual revenue run rate (ARR) of over Rs 1,300 crore by the end of FY28 (more than double FY26 revenue).
- Energy contribution expected to moderate to 55%-60% by FY30-FY31, with A&D taking the rest.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Structural multi-decade high-growth cycle across energy, oil and gas, aerospace, and defence remains intact and stronger.
- Engine makers continue to raise output; precision rotating parts remain a tight supply chain link.
- India's push for self-reliance in engines is moving from intent to programs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage, local sourcing, and backward integration lifted gross margins to 45.2%.
- Indirect corporate and administrative overheads held steady at 7.7% of revenue (INR 14.4 crore).
- Lower treasury income (Rs 4.7 crore vs Rs 12.7 crore YoY), doubled depreciation (Rs 21.8 crore), and higher finance costs (Rs 12.2 crore) impacted below-EBITDA profitability during the asset capitalization phase.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capital deployment plans are on schedule; treasury funds utilized into capex and infrastructure.
- Mangampet land kept ready for Phase II expansion beyond FY30.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net debt stands at Rs 423.8 crore (0.26x of equity and 1.6x of annualized EBITDA).
- Out of Rs 700 crore raised via QIP, Rs 621.8 crore has been utilized, leaving Rs 78.2 crore in fixed deposits for balance capex.
- Inventory increased by Rs 98 crore in H1 to Rs 425 crore (including Rs 72 crore of WIP on new lines undergoing delta qualification); trade receivables rose by Rs 92 crore to Rs 402 crore. Operating cash flow was negative at Rs 45.8 crore for H1.
- Bill discounting lines with ICICI Bank and DP World set to be used from Q4 FY27 onwards to reduce debtor days.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Time lag between capacity commissioning and revenue realization due to mandatory delta qualifications.
- Elevated working capital lock-in during the facility ramp-up phase prior to commercial billing.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs):** Asked about aero contract transitions and when peak cash burn/free cash flow generation will occur. *Management stated throughput started in Q3, supplies begin late Q3/Q4, and cash flow will improve by Q4 FY27.*
2. **Gaurav Nagori (Avendus):** Inquired if the new dedicated lines in Tuniki Bollaram account for the Rs 1,300+ crore ARR guidance and if client advances impacted margins. *Management confirmed ARR exceeds Rs 1,300 crore, remaining space in Tuniki can accommodate aerospace talks, and advances did not involve material margin concessions (hardly 1%).*
3. **Vikash Singh (ICICI Securities):** Asked about growth drivers beyond FY29 and status of engine tests. *Management noted future capex (FY29 onwards) will open up after current aero-engine qualifications conclude, and GTRE engine testing is ongoing.*
4. **Suraj Malu (Investor):** Clarified order book terminology. *Management clarified the Rs 6,500+ crore figure represents multi-year rolling contracts/demand rather than backlog.*
5. **Alisha (Analyst):** Asked about delta qualifications with non-Rolls Royce aero customers (Pratt & Whitney, Safran) and future segment mix. *Management confirmed ongoing qualifications with PWC and Safran, and guided that energy contribution will move to 55%-60% by FY30-31 as A&D scales.*
6. **Kanwalpreet Singh (Analyst):** Inquired about labour scaling, automation, and aftermarket replacement cycles. *Management explained manpower cost is front-loaded for new capacity and will scale down relative to revenue, while cold section gas turbine replacement cycles run 10-12 years.*

Azad Engineering reported its highest-ever quarterly revenue of Rs 187.8 crore up 31.6% YoY with expanding gross and EBITDA margins, completing all six customer-dedicated facilities and positioning for accelerated throughput from Q4 FY27.
