# Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-24 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Revenue and PAT grew YoY, backed by the signing of the massive Next-Generation Corvette (NGC) order and robust order book expansion.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** The Next-Generation Corvette (NGC) order was officially signed on November 2, 2026 (valued at over Rs 32,000 crores), matching prior expectations.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted to higher optimism with the order book surging to Rs 44,846 crores following the NGC contract.
- **New topics or risks:** Detailed brownfield expansions at Timber Pond (Rs 100 crores) and Howrah (Rs 70 crores) introduced; Raichak greenfield cost estimate updated to Rs 2,900 crores (from Rs 2,200 crores previously). 
- **Last quarter's promises:** Management successfully delivered the NGC contract signing in Q2/early November as promised.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 1,827 crores, up 9% YoY (from Rs 1,677 crores).
- **Q2 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 172 crores, up 12% YoY (from Rs 154 crores).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 3,641 crores, up 26% YoY (from Rs 2,987 crores).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 345 crores, up 26% YoY (from Rs 274 crores).
- **Order book (as of 30 Sept 2026):** Rs 12,977.93 crores (Shipbuilding: 96%; Defence: 70%; Non-defence: 29%).
- **Current order book (with NGC):** Approximately Rs 44,846 crores across 13 projects and 50 platforms.
- **Next Generation Corvette (NGC) contract value:** Over Rs 32,000 crores (specifically listed at Rs 32,308 crores).
- **Capacity:** Concurrent construction capacity at 28 platforms, moving to 32 platforms next month.
- **Expansion costs:** Timber Pond Rs 100 crores; Howrah Rs 70 crores; Raichak greenfield Rs 2,900 crores.

### Highlights
- Secured four new shipbuilding/marine orders since the last call: ONGC (4 Platform Supply Vessels), Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port (1 battery propulsion tug), West Bengal tourism (200 passenger electric ferries), and the Next Generation Corvette (NGC).
- P-17 Alpha: 3-ship project; 2 delivered ahead of schedule, 3rd undergoing trials for delivery this month.
- ASW Shallow Water Craft: 8-ship project; 5 delivered (5th on Nov 5), 6th at 99% progress (delivery next week), project completion expected in H1 CY2027.
- NGOPV: 4-ship project; 3 launched, 4th planned for launch in Q4 FY27.
- Bangladesh dredger launched; delivery planned by April 2027.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management stated that FY26 and FY27 are peak execution years, anticipating a natural revenue trough before NGC and other large projects scale up substantially by FY29.
- Management expects revenue recognition from the NGC project to commence moderately in FY28 (~7.5% to 8% in pre-production phase), with the first vessel delivery in the latter half of CY2032.
- Management expects P-17 Bravo L1 declaration during the current financial year and contract conclusion by Q4 FY28.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Defence RFPs closed/live include 22 interceptor boats (Rs 1,000–1,100 crores), 31 water jet FACs (~Rs 15,000 crores), 120 Fast Interceptor Crafts (Rs 4,500–5,000 crores), and 6 NGOPVs (~Rs 3,500 crores).
- Major upcoming high-value defence projects on the anvil: P-17 Bravo (~Rs 70,000 crores AON value), Landing Platform Docks (~Rs 33,000 crores), and Mine Countermeasure Vessels (~Rs 44,000 crores).
- Commercial shipbuilding demand aggregation by the Ministry stands at roughly 437 platforms.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- NGC margins stated by management to be adequate and akin to the profitable ASW Shallow Water Craft project.
- Approximately 70% of procurement is sourced from MSMEs; long-term rate contracts established to ensure visibility.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Shipbuilding capacity to increase from 28 to 32 concurrent platforms next month.
- Timber Pond (Kolkata) expansion: ~Rs 100 crores (features a 130m dry dock and 120m slipway).
- Howrah (Kolkata) expansion: ~Rs 70 crores (catering to medium/small vessels, tugs, and ferries).
- Raichak greenfield facility: ~Rs 2,900 crores (200-meter dry dock for commercial ships up to 60,000 DWT), expected completion in 3.5 to 4 years.
- West Coast expansion: DPR and partnership activities underway, expected completion in 3.5 to 4 years.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Working capital and supplier cycles for MSMEs addressed via long-term rate contracts and Ministry demand aggregation initiatives.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management acknowledged a natural revenue trough in the interim period before high-value projects like the NGC ramp up revenue contribution by FY29.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs) on NGC delivery and revenue start:** Management stated the first vessel is scheduled for delivery in the latter half of CY2032, and revenue recognition will start in FY28 (~7.5% pre-production phase).
- **Dipen Vakil (Capital) on post-delivery spares and margins:** Management clarified that base and depot spares (~10-12% of project cost) remain for execution for about a year post-delivery, and NGC margins are profitable and akin to ASW craft.
- **Milind Samit (Break Thoughts) on FY28 revenue sustainability:** Management stated project execution tempo will continue in FY28 driven by maturing research and export vessels alongside NGOPVs.
- **Saksham Tyagi (Desvelado Advisory) on robotics adoption:** Management confirmed enhanced usage of robotics and collaborative robots (cobots) for curved shipbuilding surfaces and bailey bridge fabrication.
- **Sunil Shah (Asset Manager) on 32-platform concurrent capacity:** Management explained that capacity spans pre-launch and post-launch assets (dry docks, slips, jetties) and noted GRSE utilizes public-private partnerships (PPP) to handle overflow.

### One-line takeaway
GRSE reported Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs 1,827 crore (+9% YoY) and PAT of Rs 172 crore (+12% YoY), underpinned by the Rs 32,000+ crore NGC order win and aggressive capacity expansion plans.
