# Bharat Electronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27  
**Tone:** Positive - Management reported strong H1 performance, evened-out execution across quarters, and reiterated/upheld robust order inflow and margin guidance.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 12,824 crore (up 25.97% YoY from Rs 10,180 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Profit Before Tax (PBT):** Rs 3,467 crore (up 14.66% YoY from Rs 3,023 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 2,589 crore (up 14.80% YoY from Rs 2,255 crore).
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 27.43%.
- **H1 FY27 EPS:** Rs 3.54 (vs Rs 3.09 in H1 FY26).
- **Order Book (as of Oct 1, 2026):** Rs 68,717 crore.
- **Order Inflow (FY27 till Oct 1, 2026):** Rs 7,334 crore.

### Highlights
- **Order Book Major Projects Breakdown (as of Oct 1, 2026):** Electronic Business (~Rs 4,200 crore), LCA Mark 1 (~Rs 3,200 crore), LRSAM (~Rs 2,900 crore), BMP 2 Upgrade (~Rs 2,600 crore), Ashwini Radar (~Rs 2,500 crore), Electronic Warfare Suite for Mi (~Rs 2,200 crore), and an export order (~Rs 1,700 crore).
- **Next Generation Corvette (NGC):** Parent PSUs (GSL and GRSE) signed contracts. BEL expects buyer-nominated equipment (BNE) orders worth ~Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 11,000 crore in a staggered manner over the next 6 months.
- **QRSAM Order Status:** Management expects the order shorty via Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS) clearance; BEL developed the complex area defense system jointly with DRDO over 10-12 years.
- **AMCA Project:** Submitted joint bid in a 50:50 consortium with L&T (L&T front-ending); expects technical evaluations to complete in 2-3 months.
- **Civilian Cybersecurity:** Created a dedicated Network and Cyber Security SBU 2.5 years ago; currently executing ~Rs 400 crore of orders with a prospective pipeline of thousands of crores.
- **Seeker Capabilities:** Combined IR and RF seeker manufacturing capacity stands at 1,000+ units/year, expandable by 2-3x if required.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Revenue Growth:** Maintained target of 15%+ YoY for FY27 (expecting between 15% to 20%).
- **EBITDA Margin:** Maintained at ~28% for FY27.
- **Order Inflow Guidance:** Maintained at Rs 25,000 crore (excluding QRSAM) and Rs 50,000 crore (including QRSAM) for FY27.
- **R&D Expense:** Target of Rs 2,200 crore for FY27.
- **Capex:** ~Rs 1,200 crore for FY27.
- **Defense vs Non-Defense Mix:** Target remains 90:10.
- **Execution Timelines:** NGC orders expected to be executed over 3 to 4 years post confirmed order; fuze orders have 6-7 years remaining, while most other major orders will execute within 3 to 3.5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Focus remains heavily on air defense, missiles, drones, counter-drones, and electronic warfare due to evolving global conflicts.
- Counter-drone systems feature hard-kill options including in-house and DRDO-developed microwave Directed Energy Weapons (DEW).
- Defense electronics content accounts for 60% to 70% of total missile cost and 20% to 30% for missile seekers.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Margins on competitive bids remain similar to nomination-based contracts.
- Direct raw material cost of mechanical items/alloys is small (~1% to 2% of overall product cost); focus is primarily on electronics indigenization.
- Partnered with DRDO to indigenize critical EO/IR detectors.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex planned at ~Rs 1,200 crore for FY27.
- Acquired 30-year lease/ownership of Tamil Nadu Explosives Limited for hot integration facilities for missiles.
- Created Industry 4.0 compliant optical IR seeker manufacturing setup at Nimmaluru, near Machilipatnam.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Efforts ongoing to even out production across all four quarters to reduce heavy Q4 loading.
- On-time delivery scores improving, with proactive pre-investments made for QRSAM FOPM realization along with 200+ MSME partners.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Procedural/administrative delays in big-ticket defense project clearances (e.g., QRSAM slipping by a few months).
- Airborne platform qualifications (such as Virupaksha radar for Su-30/LCA) present high technical complexity and extended development timelines.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q:** Will private sector entry reduce BEL's estimated ~Rs 25,000 crore share in QRSAM?  
  **A:** Management is >99% confident QRSAM will come to BEL due to 10-12 years of complex system-of-systems expertise. Private sector entry rumors pertain to standalone missiles, whereas QRSAM involves complex radars, launchers, and communication networks.
- **Q:** What is BEL's expected order value and timeline from the Next Generation Corvette (NGC) contract?  
  **A:** Management expects ~Rs 10,000 crore to Rs 11,000 crore worth of BNE orders (excluding missiles), with formal order placing starting over the next 1 day to 6 months (majority within FY27).
- **Q:** With H1 revenue growing 26%, is BEL upgrading its FY27 revenue guidance?  
  **A:** No upgrade; H1 growth reflects strategic evening out of quarterly production rather than Q4 concentration. Guidance remains at 15%+ growth.
- **Q:** How is BEL positioning itself in drones and counter-drones?  
  **A:** Working with 6-8 startups alongside in-house programs; expects 1-2 large drone orders within 1-2 years and foresees strong export prospects for counter-drone solutions.

### One-line takeaway
BEL delivered strong H1 FY27 growth driven by improved quarterly execution, maintaining its FY27 guidance of 15%+ revenue growth, ~28% EBITDA margin, and Rs 25,000-50,000 crore order inflow target.
