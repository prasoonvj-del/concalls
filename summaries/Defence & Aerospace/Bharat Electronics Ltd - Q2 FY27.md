# Bharat Electronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-06 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong H1 financial performance, robust execution, and management confidence in meeting guidance.

### Key numbers
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from operations:** Rs 12,824 crore (YoY growth of 25.97%)
- **H1 FY27 Profit before tax (PBT):** Rs 3,467 crore (YoY growth of 14.66%)
- **H1 FY27 Profit after tax (PAT):** Rs 2,589 crore (YoY growth of 14.80%)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA margin:** 27.43%
- **H1 FY27 Earnings per share (EPS):** Rs 3.54 (vs Rs 3.09)
- **Order Book (as on 01/10/2026):** Rs 68,717 crore
- **Order Inflow (H1 FY27):** Rs 7,334 crore
- **Guidance for FY27:** Revenue growth of 15%+, EBITDA margin around 28%, Order inflow of Rs 25,000 crore (excluding QRSAM) / Rs 50,000 crore (including QRSAM), R&D investment of Rs 2,200 crore, Capex of around Rs 1,200 crore, Defense-to-non-defense ratio of 90:10.

### Highlights
- Management stated that H1 performance benefited from efforts to make production more uniform across quarters rather than loading the bulk into the final quarter.
- Top order book segments as of October 1, 2026: Electronic business (Rs 4,200 crore), LCA Mark 1 (Rs 3,200 crore), LRSAM (Rs 2,900 crore), BMP 2 upgrade (Rs 2,600 crore), Ashwini radar (Rs 2,500 crore), EW suite for Mi (Rs 2,200 crore), and an export order (Rs 1,700 crore).
- Next Generation Corvette (NGC) contract signed with GRSE and GSL; formal sub-system orders expected within 1 to 6 months (by end of the financial year).
- Tamil Nadu Explosives Limited (now owned by BEL for 30 years) is seeing investments for hot integration of missiles.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained annual guidance: 15%+ revenue growth, ~28% EBITDA margin, and order inflow targets of Rs 25,000 crore (ex-QRSAM) and Rs 50,000 crore (inc-QRSAM). If NGC orders converge before March, management noted it could provide a positive surprise.
- QRSAM order expected very shortly (potentially cleared in the next CCS).

### Demand & industry commentary
- High demand globally and domestically for drones, counter-drones, and missiles.
- BEL is working closely with 6 to 8 start-ups on drone technologies and expects 1-2 big orders next year.
- High-end counter-drone systems with hard-kill options (including microwave DEW) are seeing strong interest, including foreign export leads.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Nomination-based orders do not carry higher margins than competitive bids; margins are driven by product uniqueness and indigenization.
- Material cost is predominantly electronic; mechanical/alloy materials account for only 1% to 2% of product cost.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Capex guidance for FY27 is around Rs 1,200 crore.
- Combined IR and RF seeker manufacturing capacity stands at 1,000+ units currently, scalable by 2x to 3x if required.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not explicitly discussed in detail, aside from planned capex of ~Rs 1,200 crore and R&D spend of Rs 2,200 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Procedural delays and discussions can cause minor line-item slippages into the next financial year (e.g., potential slippage of P-75I or specific QRSAM sub-items).

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs) on QRSAM timeline and private sector threat:** Management stated QRSAM is a complex "system of systems" developed over 10+ years with DRDO, and is 99% confident the order will come to BEL shortly. Private sector entry may start with basic missiles eventually, but not complex systems currently.
2. **Mahesh Raut (Nomura) on NGC order sizing and execution guidance:** Management confirmed expectations of ~Rs 16,000 crore to Rs 17,000 crore from NGC in a staggered manner within this financial year, sticking to the 15-20% revenue growth guidance despite a 26% H1 growth.
3. **Subramaniam Yadav (SBI Life) on AMCA project:** Management confirmed a joint bid with L&T (50:50 consortium with L&T front-ending) has been submitted, expecting price bid opening and potential L1 outcome in 2-3 months.
4. **Jyoti Gupta (Ashika Institutional Equities) on HAPS program:** Management assessed that High Altitude Pseudo Satellite (HAPS) technology is still maturing and they are carefully evaluating partners to minimize development risks.
5. **Sahil Karia (Whitepine Investment Management) on Seeker capacity:** Management stated current combined IR and RF seeker capacity is 1,000+ units annually, with no bottlenecks, and seekers constitute 20-30% of a missile's cost.
6. **Ajinkya Jadhav (Krish Portfolio) on material indigenization:** Management clarified that mechanical materials/alloys are only 1-2% of BEL's cost base (unlike HAL or shipyards), making electronic material indigenization far more critical.

### One-line takeaway
BEL reported a strong H1 FY27 with 26% revenue growth, maintained its full-year 15%+ revenue growth and margin guidance, and anticipates major inflows from QRSAM and NGC before fiscal closure.
