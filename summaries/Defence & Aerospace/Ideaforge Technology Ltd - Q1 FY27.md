# Ideaforge Technology Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-18 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted a revenue revival, positive EBITDA, strong order book execution (over 20% of opening order book), and a favorable policy/demand environment.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 68.6 crore (vs INR 12.8 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Gross profit:** INR 33.6 crore (vs INR 7.9 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Gross margin:** 49% (vs 61.7% in Q1 FY26)
- **EBITDA:** INR 4.3 crore (vs negative INR 15.1 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **PAT (Profit After Tax):** Negative INR 2.6 crore (vs negative INR 23.6 crore in Q1 FY26)
- **Opening order book (FY27):** > INR 300 crore
- **Order book as of June 30, 2026:** INR 256.8 crore
- **Capital raised via QIP:** INR 500 crore
- **Letter of Intent (TDB assistance under RDI Scheme):** Up to INR 151 crore for YETI

### Highlights
- Delivered >20% of the FY27 opening order book in Q1.
- Deployed UAV fleet crossed 1 million customer missions.
- Advanced key developmental programs: combat drone capabilities (air-launched effects, fuel-hybrid long-endurance for ZOLT), YETI technical demonstrator completed tethered hover test, and DGCA Type Certification received for Q6 V2 GEO UAV (making 6 type-certified platforms).
- Strengthened financial foundation via a INR 500 crore QIP and a Letter of Intent for up to INR 151 crore low-cost long-term debt funding under the RDI Scheme.
- Secured market-ready capabilities on FLYGHT CLOUD (automated video summaries, AI-enabled event annotation, etc.) and strengthened the EW resilience tech-stack.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to complete the delivery of the remaining order book of INR 256.8 crore by Q3 as per customer timelines.
- Blended gross margin for the year is expected to hover between 50% and 55%.
- No major capex planned for factory setups as current capacity uses a single shift and can be scaled to 3 shifts.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Industry demand revived in FY26 after a muted FY25.
- INR 20,000 crore worth of drone procurements announced through Fast Track mode by the Indian Army; one opportunity is in the RFP stage while others are in the approval pipeline.
- Ministry of Defense doubled operational procurement limits for field commanders (revision of Delegation of Financial Powers to Defense Services 2026), expected to accelerate procurement in Q3 and Q4.
- Civil side of the business shows regular cycles leaning towards increased procurement in Q3 and Q4.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Gross margin dropped to 49% from 61.7% YoY due to product mix variations (60:40 defense-to-civil ratio in Q1 vs 70:30 historically, with EW resilient defense products carrying higher margins).
- Geopolitical conflicts create pressure on the supply chain for thermal cameras and electronic components, causing timeline shifts, but management does not anticipate delivery delays for current commitments.
- Import substitute revenue is roughly 20%-22%, and international/indigenized content run rate is about 60%-65%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No dedicated capex plan for new factories; operations can scale from single-shift to 3 shifts within current space.
- US operations: Working on operationalizing the JV and awaiting Blue sUAS certification (work in progress). International expansion includes exploring partnerships in Europe, Middle East, and live opportunities with NFR units in South America and Africa.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Raised INR 500 crore via QIP for product development, working capital, and capability building.
- Received a Letter of Intent from the Technology Development Board for up to INR 151 crore under the RDI scheme (low-cost long-term debt against milestones).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global supply chain disruptions and component availability (thermal cameras, electronics) causing timeline shifts.
- Quarterly results can vary due to opportunity cycles, product mix, delivery schedules, supply disruptions, working capital requirements, and customer acceptance.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Dipen Vakil (asked about US operations scale-up and market size):** Management explained they are operationalizing the JV and pursuing Blue sUAS certification, which is required before gaining traction in US defense/federal markets.
2. **Tushar Khurana (asked about YETI's positioning against competition like BluJ Aero for logistics drones):** Management stated YETI is designed for a 6,500-meter takeoff altitude carrying up to 200 kgs, making it uniquely capable for high-altitude logistics compared to others.
3. **Rajveer Singh (asked about combat drone opportunities vs R&D):** Management stated development is a mix of foundational experiments and specific projects aligned with visible customer requirements.
4. **Alisha Mahawla (asked if gross margin pressure will continue due to supply chain issues):** Management clarified that margin movement was due to product mix (60:40 vs 70:30 historical), but full-year blended gross margin is still expected to hold in the 50%-55% range.
5. **Shashank Jha (asked about market share capture against large players like Adani and Tata):** Management stated market share depends on final tender specifications and ready products, but their intent is to participate in maximum opportunities.

### One-line takeaway
ideaForge posted a turnaround Q1 with positive EBITDA and INR 68.6 crore in revenue, backed by a strong opening order book execution, a INR 500 crore QIP, and an expanding opportunity pipeline in defense and civil segments.
