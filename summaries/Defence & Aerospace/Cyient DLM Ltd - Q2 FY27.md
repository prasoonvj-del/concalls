# Cyient DLM Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-23 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, record order book, and robust pipeline, with near-term margin impact offset by deliberate investments for future growth.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue:** Rs 393 crore (Rs 3,931 million), up 26.5% YoY
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 767 crore (Rs 7,669 million), up 30.2% YoY
- **Order Backlog:** Rs 2,813 crore (Rs 28.1 billion), up Rs 210 crore sequentially; book-to-bill ratio >1.6x
- **Pipeline:** In excess of $1 billion (unweighted pipeline)
- **EBITDA:** Rs 34.2 crore (Rs 342 million), up 9.5% YoY
- **EBITDA Margin:** 8.7% (lower by 135 bps YoY)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA Margin:** 9.6%
- **Normalized PAT:** Rs 15.8 crore (Rs 158 million), up 25.8% YoY (normalized PAT margin stable at 4%)
- **Reported PAT:** Rs 34 crore (Rs 340 million), including a one-time earn-out reversal of Rs 18.2 crore (Rs 183 million for H1)
- **H1 FY27 Normalized PAT:** Rs 32.1 crore (Rs 321 million), up 60.4% YoY
- **Segment Growth (YoY):** Aerospace up 62% (47% mix), Industrial up 29% (31% mix), Defense up 13% (7% mix), Medtech up 14% (14% mix)
- **Product Mix Growth (YoY):** Box build up 85% (27% mix), PCBA up 19% (48% mix)
- **Geography Mix:** International 93%, India 7%
- **Net Working Capital:** 153 days (improved from 161 days in Q1); Inventory days at 162; DSO at 73; DPO at 68
- **Operating Free Cash Flow (Q2):** Rs 17.2 crore (Rs 172 million)

### Highlights
- Order backlog sustained positive momentum for 6 continuous quarters, reaching an all-time high of Rs 2,813 crore.
- Integrated Altek Electronics, renamed as Cyient DLM Electronics, Inc., to expand global footprint.
- Shift in product mix towards higher-value integration, with box build growing 85% YoY.
- Added 3 new customer logos in Q2.
- Completed integration and name change for the US entity.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects FY27 EBITDA margins to remain at the higher end of single digits (9% to 10% range).
- Management expects double-digit EBITDA margins from FY28 onwards due to revenue growth and absorption of recent investments.
- Build-to-spec (electronic products) programs expected to see production start around FY29 and generate significant sustained growth from FY30 onwards ($1 billion across 7-9 year programs).
- Revenue growth targeted around 30% to 35% for the full year FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global EMS market expected to rise from ~$650 billion to ~$1.1 trillion; high-reliability EMS segment estimated at ~$230 billion.
- Structural tailwinds include AI and data center infrastructure (addressable EMS opportunity of ~$58 billion), semiconductor capital equipment, and robotics.
- Strategic pivot phases: Strengthen (FY24-FY26, 4 core industries, 9-11% margin framework), Expand (FY27-FY29, 7 industries adding robotics, AI data center, semi-cap equipment, 11-13% margin framework), and Transform (FY30 onwards, product/platform moat, 13-18% margin framework).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margin moderated by 135 bps YoY to 8.7% due to revenue mix, product development costs, and employee cost investments in go-to-market/sales capability.
- Product development and sales/marketing expenses are fully expensed in the P&L (not capitalized); investment impact estimated at 100 to 150 bps.
- Finance costs declined 10.4% YoY to Rs 6.1 crore due to lower working capital borrowings.
- Supply chain challenges, longer lead times, and geopolitical uncertainties persisted, notably driven by the memory market.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No specific capex guidance number disclosed; focus remains on expensing product development and expanding sales capabilities.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net working capital improved sequentially to 153 days (from 161 days in Q1).
- DSO improved from 77 days to 73 days; DPO increased from 63 days to 68 days; inventory days stable at 162.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Continued supply chain shortages, longer lead times, and geopolitical uncertainties (specifically in the memory market).
- Near-term margin compression due to upfront investments in sales and product development without immediate revenue absorption.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Balasubramanian (Arihant Capital) asked about the exact quantum of product development spend and capitalization policy.** Management clarified that costs are 100-150 bps of impact, fully expensed (not capitalized) under a conservative accounting policy, and will not be individually budgeted as they depend on market opportunities.
2. **Santhosh Seshadri (Avendus Spark) asked about the timeline for order book execution and semiconductor ramp-up.** Management stated the execution window is typically 18 to 24 months, and semiconductor capital equipment revenues have already started seeing a ramp-up over the last 2 quarters.
3. **Umang Gada (Avener Investment Management) questioned why margins are squeezed despite higher gross margins and whether sales/marketing investments will cease.** Management responded that sales and marketing investments are fully loaded now and no net new additions will be made until revenues double, enabling operating leverage and margin expansion to 10%+ in FY28.
4. **Nishita (Sapphire Capital) asked about the conversion timeline for the $1 billion pipeline and revenue growth expectations.** Management explained that the pipeline is a point-in-time "balance sheet" metric with constant additions rather than a fixed period pool, and reiterated a 30-35% revenue growth expectation for the full year.
5. **Adityapal (MSA Capital Partners) asked about the overlap of Honeywell's IndiGo avionics wins and eVTOL timelines.** Management noted that Honeywell build-to-print prototypes are in testing/engineering modification phases, while eVTOL build-to-spec programs are slated for production revenues around FY29.

### One-line takeaway
Cyient DLM reported robust H1 revenue growth of 30.2% and a record order book of Rs 2,813 crore, while absorbing upfront investments in sales and product development that depressed near-term EBITDA margins to 8.7% ahead of anticipated double-digit margin expansion in FY28.
