# Astra Microwave Products Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-19 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered highest-ever H1 revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by a favorable product mix and strong order inflows.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of ~15% (targeting Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,350 crore) and steady-state EBITDA margin guidance of 26%–28%.
- Tone and demand view: Tone remains highly confident, backed by a landmark Rs 1,844 crore order from HAL and new in-house IP product launches (counter-drone radar, bird detection radar, and low-level lightweight radar).
- New topics or risks: Detailed explanation of the demerger structure for Astra Space Technologies Limited (ASTL) where every Astra shareholder receives 1 shares of ASTL for every 1 share held.
- Last quarter's promises: The delivery timeline for the first phase of 12 units of the Virupaksha/Uttam radar shifted slightly from September 2027 to March 2028 per customer discussions, though 2 numbers are targeted by March 2027.

### Key numbers
- H1 FY27 Revenue: Rs 437 crore
- H1 FY27 EBITDA: Rs 120 crore (margin of 27.4%)
- H1 FY27 PAT: Rs 57 crore (margin of 13%)
- Q2 FY27 Order Inflows: Rs 2,036 crore (Radars: Rs 1,967 crore, Electronic Warfare: Rs 36 crore, Telemetry: Rs 17 crore, Space: Rs 16 crore)
- Standalone Order Book (as of 30 September 2026): Rs 3,937 crore
- Consolidated Order Book (as of 30 September 2026): Rs 4,708 crore (including service orders of Rs 251 crore)
- JV (ARC Limited) H1 Sales: Rs 334 crore; Order book: Rs 913 crore (Full year revenue target: ~Rs 600 crore to Rs 700 crore)
- FY27 Revenue Target: Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,350 crore (~15% growth)
- Long-term Targets: Rs 2,000 crore turnover in FY29 (+/- Rs 100 crore), reaching Rs 2,000 crore to Rs 3,000 crores a few years thereafter; Consolidated order book target of Rs 5,500 crore to Rs 5,600 crore in FY28.

### Highlights
- Dr. M.V. Reddy took charge as Managing Director effective October 1, 2026.
- Successfully realized a counter-drone radar and delivered it to DRDO for final integration and trials.
- Delivered on the promise to ready three in-house IP products by Diwali: a complete counter-drone system (D4 radar backbone), a bird detection radar, and a low-level lightweight radar (40-50 km range).
- Space and weather divisions are being demerged into Astra Space Technologies Limited (ASTL); shareholders will receive 1 ASTL share for every 1 Astra share held. ASTL holds an order book of ~Rs 220 crore currently, expected to reach ~Rs 900 crore carry-forward by year-end, with a 4-year cumulative revenue plan of Rs 2,250 crore to Rs 2,500 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to book another Rs 200 crore worth of orders in FY27 (with Rs 600 crore concluded and expected next month).
- JV (ARC Limited) targets ~Rs 1,000 crore revenue in FY28.
- Target to become near debt-free on working capital limits in the next 1 to 1.5 years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Government reforms (updated DPM, draft DAP with up to 60% local content mandates, 75% capital acquisition budget earmarked for domestic industry) and DAC approvals provide long-term structural clarity.
- National targets aim for Rs 3 lakh crore in annual defense production and Rs 50,000 crore in defense exports by 2029.
- Global space economy growth and domestic space sector opening (targeting $44 billion by 2033) support demand for space-grade components and constellations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- EBITDA margins stabilized at 26%–28% for the next 2-3 years, aided by higher contribution from production orders and operating leverage.
- Inventory increased due to stockpiling of components with extended lead times to ensure uninterrupted execution for Q1 of the next financial year.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Continuous investment in R&D, production capabilities, and parallel testing infrastructure.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash conversion cycle targeted to improve toward 350 days by the end of the year.
- Goal to reduce working capital CC limits to zero, achieving a debt-free status on working capital within 18 months.
- Operational surplus/cash generation expected between Rs 150 crore to Rs 300 crore.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Global supply chain lead times have increased for certain components, requiring preemptive stockpiling.
- International customer qualification and order conversion take time.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs) asked about market potential for the three new products and working capital inventory buildup.** Management stated bird detection potential is Rs 200–300 crore over 2-3 years, LLLR has ~Rs 1,000 crore visibility over 5 years, and counter-drone market is Rs 3,000–5,000 crore over 5 years. Inventory rose due to stockpiling for early FY28 execution amid extended component lead times.
2. **Pinakin Parekh (Parekh) asked about Uttam AAAU radar delivery timelines to HAL given HAL's delays.** Management clarified HAL wants 4 numbers by March 2027 (committed to 2), with 12 numbers required by March 2028 per contract, and does not foresee delays on their end.
3. **Abhijeet Singh (Systematix) asked about the counter-drone TOT uniqueness.** Management stated that out of 7 companies given TOT from DRDO, Astra and BEL are the active makers, and Astra has successfully demonstrated and booked sales.
4. **Shirom Kapoor (Analyst) asked about QRSAM order timelines.** Management noted BEL expects the contract between December and March, with Astra's execution likely starting in FY28 last quarter or FY29.
5. **Sahil Karia (Analyst) asked about Virupaksha radar timeline shift and SDR ground segment status.** Management explained the March 2028 timeline for 12 units is based on the actual contract signing date. For manpack SDRs, initial trials missed due to a software bug, but issues are fixed and they are ready for the second phase of procurement.
6. **Manish Maheshwari (Office) asked about free cash flow generation and working capital targets.** Management expects operational surplus between Rs 150 crore to Rs 300 crore, aiming to bring the cash conversion cycle to 350 days and eliminate working capital debt by year-end.

### One-line takeaway
Astra Microwave delivered its highest-ever H1 financial performance, backed by a robust order book of Rs 4,708 crore, new in-house IP product readiness, and a strategic demerger of its space business.
