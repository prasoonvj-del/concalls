# Unimech Aerospace and Manufacturing Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-31 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth, significant order book expansion, and successful execution of strategic initiatives across aerospace and semiconductor segments.

### Key numbers
*   **Revenue (Q2):** Rs 123 crore (+98% YoY, +14% QoQ).
*   **Revenue (H1):** Rs 231 crore (+85% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin (Q2):** 40% (H1: 38%).
*   **PAT (Q2):** Rs 32 crore (+103% YoY, +14% QoQ).
*   **PAT (H1):** Rs 60 crore (+71% YoY).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 855 crore (+715% YoY).
*   **Qualified Parts:** 250+ (H1 end); target 590+ by FY27 end.

### Highlights
*   **Strategic Transformation:** Evolving from short-cycle aerospace tooling to a multi-industry precision engineering platform (aerospace, defense, semiconductors, nuclear, energy).
*   **Acquisitions & JVs:** Hobel Bellows integration complete; Saudi Arabia JV (with Kanoo Group) construction on schedule for March/April '27 commercial readiness.
*   **Dheya Engineering:** 27% stake held; 40kg thrust engine in advanced stage; first private company to receive CEMILAC prototype certification pathway.
*   **Semiconductor Progress:** 60 FAIs (First Article Inspections) qualified; step-up in revenue expected in H2.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Management** expects H2 revenue to be higher than H1 with similar margins.
*   **Management** maintains an aspiration to reach Rs 1,000 crore revenue (timeline not specified).
*   **Management** expects working capital days to increase to 150-160 days by end of FY27 due to shift toward longer-cycle programs.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Aerospace:** Core tooling remains the anchor; transitioning to long-term supply agreements (LTAs).
*   **Semiconductors:** Demand is in an "upward tick" cycle; competitive landscape is global, but demand exceeds supply.
*   **Nuclear:** Participating in bids for 4 new reactors; 13-15 sub-assemblies/systems qualified.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Gross Margin (H1):** 67% (Q2: 69%).
*   **Cost Structure:** Subcontracting costs at 2% of revenue; employee and operational expenses at 15% and 14% of revenue, respectively.
*   **Margin Outlook:** **Management** anticipates potential 1-2% margin compression in the long term due to R&D investments and product mix shifts (targeting 50:50 split between tooling and precision components).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Capacity Utilization:** ~65%.
*   **Infrastructure:** Installing automated special process cells/assembly lines; commissioning by Dec '26.
*   **Fundraising:** **Management** evaluating equity fundraise in early 2027 to support capacity expansion and potential inorganic growth.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Working Capital:** 110 days.
*   **Gross Block:** Expected to reach ~Rs 500 crore by FY27 end.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Qualification Risk:** Need to convert FAIs into serial production; **management** noted they have turned down opportunities that required extensive prototyping without a horizon for serial production.
*   **Capacity Constraints:** **Management** noted the need to invest ahead of demand to avoid future bottlenecks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: What is the composition of the Rs 589 crore LTA/award letter order book?**
    *   **A:** It comprises 5-10 year contracts with FACC (aerostructures), Honeywell (engine components/control systems), and a French Tier 1 (door components).
*   **Q: How does the semiconductor business differ from aerospace?**
    *   **A:** From a fungibility and execution lens, they are similar, though surface finish nuances differ.
*   **Q: Will margins compress as the business scales?**
    *   **A:** **Management** expects to maintain H1 margins in H2, but long-term mix shifts toward precision components may lead to slight compression (1-2%).
*   **Q: Why the need for a fundraise?**
    *   **A:** **Management** stated internal accruals are sufficient for the Rs 1,000 crore vision, but a fundraise is planned for larger expansions and infrastructure required for the 18-24 month lead time on new capacity.

### One-line takeaway
Unimech is successfully transitioning from a short-cycle tooling business to a long-term, multi-industry precision engineering platform, evidenced by a 715% YoY order book growth and strong H1 financial performance.
