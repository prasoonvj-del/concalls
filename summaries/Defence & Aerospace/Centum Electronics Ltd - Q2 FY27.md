# Centum Electronics Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-10 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 / H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong execution across both BTS and EMS segments drove a 52% YoY revenue increase and 77% YoY PAT growth in Q2.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained medium-term revenue growth target of 25%-30% (same as last quarter).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted more confident on semiconductor equipment ramp-up (now eyeing $45M-$50M in 2 years vs $25M-$30M previously) and highlighted first major BTS export win.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter: Supply chain bottlenecks and component lead time extensions (memories, controllers, MLCCs, and bare PCBs extending to 40-50 weeks) newly highlighted. Overseas subsidiaries officially deconsolidated since Q1 remain out of the picture.
- Last quarter's promises: Capex deployment progressing as guided (INR 35 crore utilized of initial capex plan).

### Key numbers
- **Standalone Revenue (Q2):** Rs 312 crore (+52% YoY)
- **Standalone Revenue (H1):** Rs 517 crore (+32% YoY)
- **EBITDA (Q2):** Rs 40 crore (+63% YoY)
- **EBITDA Margin (Q2):** 12.69% (+87 bps YoY)
- **PAT (Q2):** Rs 23 crore (+77% YoY)
- **PAT Margin (Q2):** 7.31% (vs 6.28% YoY)
- **Order Book (as of Sept 2026):** ~Rs 2,000 crore (+38% YoY)
- **BTS Order Book:** Rs 959 crore (+47% YoY)
- **Net Working Capital:** 136 days (improved by 6 days)
- **Receivable Days:** 106 days (improved by 9 days)
- **Borrowings:** Rs 134 crore (increase of Rs 11.5 crore due to working capital)
- **Cash Balances:** Rs 127 crore (including Rs 49 crore restricted cash and Rs 53 crore QIP proceeds)

### Highlights
- **Segment Growth:** BTS revenue grew 84% YoY in Q2; EMS revenue grew 41% YoY (and ~42% QoQ).
- **Key Wins:** Secured a ~Rs 30 crore export order for an advanced electronic warfare subsystem (naval application) and a second order for tactical air navigation systems worth ~Rs 51 crores from GRSE.
- **Space Recognition:** Contributed over 200 subsystems and modules to the successful GISAT-1A (EOS-05) mission.
- **EMS Expansion:** Secured three new marquee customer wins in transportation (braking/door controllers), industrial (metrology capital equipment), and defense (drone payloads from the Middle East).
- **Board Approval:** Board took enabling provision to increase borrowing authorization from Rs 500 crore to Rs 1,000 crore for non-fund-based limits to support BTS order book growth.

### Guidance & outlook
- **Growth Rate:** Medium-term objective to grow at 25%-30%.
- **Margins:** Aiming to reach and breach the 13% EBITDA margin mark at a combined level, targeting 14%-15% over the next 3-4 years. BTS margins expected to stay around 20%-22%.
- **Semiconductor Segment:** Raised visibility to $45 million-$50 million annual run rate in the next two years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Demand driven by localization in India, supply chain diversification, and AI/data center infrastructure build-outs.
- Semiconductor equipment demand surging faster than initial expectations, largely driven by an anchor customer.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Material cost fluctuations caused minor quarter-on-quarter program-level margin variations in BTS.
- Customer prepayments cover approximately 50% of net inventory on hand.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planning a new facility dedicated to the BTS business to establish system-level capabilities, dedicated test infrastructure, and AIT (Assembly, Integration, Test) facilities for full satellites.
- Engineering bench strength expanded with skilled engineers for design execution.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Inventory increased due to business growth and supply chain constraints.
- QIP general corporate purpose proceeds of ~Rs 50.7 crore are currently held as reserve cash.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Supply chain shortages in memories, controllers, MLCCs, and bare PCBs (lead times extended to 40-50 weeks) increasing component prices, inventory requirements, and working capital.
- Quarterly variations and lumpy revenue phasing remain inherent to the BTS business.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Ankit Babel (Subhkam Ventures) asked about BTS margin stability:** Management explained that 20%-22% is the structural average for BTS, but quarter-on-quarter variations occur due to program mix and material cost differences.
2. **Tanay Shah (DAM Capital) asked about the visibility of the Rs 1,950 crore order book:** Management stated execution spans 2-2.5 years, with steady ramp-ups expected rather than equal quarterly distributions.
3. **Vayun Jain (Bajaj Alt. Inv.) asked about inventory and supply chain drivers:** Management attributed inventory buildup to soaring AI/data center demand diverting global component priorities and geopolitical logistics delays.
4. **Nirali Gopani (Unique PMS) asked about global BTS export traction:** Management stated they have broken through with a Middle East customer for subsystem EW applications and are in advanced talks with a European customer.
5. **Karan (Niveshaay) asked about semiconductor competition in India:** Management clarified they have no direct competitors in India for their specific scope of work, facing competition primarily from Southeast Asia.

### One-line takeaway
Centum Electronics delivered a strong H1 FY27 with 32% revenue growth and a record Rs 2,000 crore order book, though management acknowledged near-term supply chain headwinds and extended component lead times.
