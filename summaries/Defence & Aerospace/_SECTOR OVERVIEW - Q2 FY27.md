# Defence & Aerospace: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Defence & Aerospace sector reflects broad-based top-line expansion, record order books, and aggressive multi-industry diversification. Companies like **Bharat Electronics Ltd (BEL)**, **Cyient DLM**, **MTAR Technologies**, and **Unimech Aerospace** reported strong financial performances and robust revenue growth. However, execution divergence exists: while pure-play aerospace and defence electronics are thriving, **Aequs** faced severe headwinds in its consumer segment, forcing a withdrawal of its profitability timeline.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that structural tailwinds in aerospace, defence electronics, and high-reliability precision engineering remain exceptionally strong, supported by domestic indigenization and global supply chain shifts.
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   **BEL** highlighted surging domestic and global demand for drones, counter-drones, and complex missile systems like QRSAM and the Next Generation Corvette (NGC).
    *   **Cyient DLM** and **Unimech** are capitalizing on non-aerospace adjacencies: Cyient DLM is expanding into AI data centers, semiconductor capital equipment, and robotics, while Unimech is scaling up in semiconductors and civil nuclear bids.
    *   **MTAR Technologies** pointed to massive tailwinds in civil nuclear (targeting India's 100 GW target by 2047) and fuel cells via Bloom Energy.
    *   **Aequs** is the outlier, where healthy aerospace demand is completely offset by execution and supply chain bottlenecks in the consumer segment (toys and electronics).

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Margin Profiles & Divergence:** Margins vary widely by business model. **Unimech** leads with a Q2 EBITDA margin of 40%, followed by **BEL** at ~27.43% (H1) and **MTAR** at 24.4%. Conversely, **Cyient DLM’s** EBITDA margin moderated by 135 bps YoY to 8.7% due to deliberate upfront investments in sales and product development. **Aequs** maintains ~22% EBITDA in aerospace, but its consumer segment suffered widened EBITDA losses of INR 401 million.
*   **Raw Materials & Inflation:** **BEL** noted that mechanical/alloy materials comprise only 1–2% of its cost base (making electronic indigenization its primary focus), whereas **Cyient DLM** cited supply chain challenges and lead times specifically in the memory market. **MTAR** and **Unimech** noted minor cost pressures in certain consumables due to geopolitical factors.

## Capex & capacity
*   **BEL:** FY27 capex guidance stands at around Rs 1,200 crore, alongside an R&D spend of Rs 2,200 crore. Seeker capacity is currently at 1,000+ units annually.
*   **MTAR:** Executing a total capex outlay of ~Rs 500 crore across FY26 and FY27 (INR 350 crore for fuel cells, INR 150 crore for oil & gas and aerospace).
*   **Aequs:** Scaling back total FY27 capex from initial guidance, shifting focus to aerospace and Hosur Phase 1 development, backed by a INR 6,500 million promoter preferential warrant infusion.
*   **Unimech:** Gross block is expected to reach ~Rs 500 crore by FY27 end; management is evaluating an equity fundraise in early 2027 to support larger capacity expansions.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Aequs:** Significantly shifted tone by withdrawing its Q4 FY27 EBITDA breakeven target for the consumer segment due to slower learning curves and supply chain disruptions. 
*   **MTAR, BEL, Cyient DLM, and Unimech:** Reaffirmed their positive momentum, noting strong H2 outlooks, rising order inflows, and steady execution progress without structural negative revisions.

## Company one-liners
*   **Aequs Ltd:** Aequs continues to scale its high-growth aerospace business, but the consumer segment is facing significant headwinds, forcing management to pause its breakeven timeline and rely on a large promoter-led capital infusion to fund ongoing expansion.
*   **Bharat Electronics Ltd:** BEL reported a strong H1 FY27 with 26% revenue growth, maintained its full-year 15%+ revenue growth and margin guidance, and anticipates major inflows from QRSAM and NGC before fiscal closure.
*   **Cyient DLM Ltd:** Cyient DLM reported robust H1 revenue growth of 30.2% and a record order book of Rs 2,813 crore, while absorbing upfront investments in sales and product development that depressed near-term EBITDA margins to 8.7% ahead of anticipated double-digit margin expansion in FY28.
*   **MTAR Technologies Ltd:** MTAR Technologies delivered a record Q2 FY27 with a 200% YoY revenue jump and 483% EBITDA growth, backed by robust execution, substantially lower working capital days, and strong multi-vertical expansion visibility.
*   **Unimech Aerospace and Manufacturing Ltd:** Unimech is successfully transitioning from a short-cycle tooling business to a long-term, multi-industry precision engineering platform, evidenced by a 715% YoY order book growth and strong H1 financial performance.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Aequs:** Progress on the Hosur Phase 1 ground-breaking and signs of stabilization/volume recovery in the consumer segment.
*   **BEL:** Actual conversion and clearance of the QRSAM order via CCS and finalization of the Next Generation Corvette (NGC) sub-system contracts.
*   **Cyient DLM:** Operating leverage realization and margin progression back toward single-digit higher-ends / early double digits.
*   **MTAR:** Execution velocity in the fuel cell Phase 2 expansion and scaling of the new data center infrastructure vertical.
*   **Unimech:** Conversion of semiconductor FAIs into serial production and concrete details regarding the planned early-2027 equity fundraise.

*Based on: Aequs Ltd, Bharat Electronics Ltd, Cyient DLM Ltd, MTAR Technologies Ltd, Unimech Aerospace and Manufacturing Ltd*
