# Defence & Aerospace: Sector Overview (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Indian Defence & Aerospace sector is currently in a high-execution phase. Managements across the board are reporting record revenues and robust order books, driven by strong domestic defense procurement and a push for indigenization. While the core defense and aerospace segments are thriving, the sector is navigating a transition from pure-play manufacturing to complex system integration, with significant capital expenditure underway to expand capacity.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** There is universal agreement on the strength of domestic demand. Companies like **BEL** (15%+ revenue growth guidance), **Zen Technologies** (highest-ever revenue), and **MTAR** (200% YoY revenue growth) cite strong order inflows and government-led procurement (FTP/DAC) as primary drivers.
*   **Divergence:** A clear divergence exists between the high-growth defense/aerospace core and the consumer segment. **Aequs** reported strong aerospace performance but was forced to recalibrate its consumer segment path to profitability due to slower-than-expected volume ramps. Conversely, **Centum Electronics** and **Unimech** are seeing a surge in demand for semiconductor equipment and precision engineering, signaling a broadening of the sector's addressable market beyond traditional defense.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Margins:** Profitability remains healthy, with **Zen Technologies** (38.2% EBITDA margin) and **Unimech** (40% EBITDA margin) leading. **BEL** maintains a steady ~28% EBITDA margin.
*   **Supply Chain:** This is the primary headwind. **Centum** highlighted extended lead times (40-50 weeks) for memories and controllers, forcing inventory buildup. **Ideaforge** and **Aequs** also flagged supply chain constraints as a recurring operational friction point.
*   **Cost Pressures:** While raw material costs are generally manageable, **MTAR** noted minor cost increases in consumables due to geopolitical factors, and **Zen Technologies** saw higher warranty provisioning due to product mix shifts.

## Capex & capacity
The sector is in a massive capital deployment cycle:
*   **Cochin Shipyard** is expanding via the ISRF JV and new facilities in Tuticorin.
*   **MTAR** is investing ~Rs 500 crore across fuel cells and other verticals.
*   **Aequs** is developing the Hosur site.
*   **Digilogic** is progressing with "Project Udaan" (operational by Q1 FY28).
*   **Unimech** is commissioning automated cells by Dec '26.
*   **Zen Technologies** is scaling Hyper strike interceptor capacity to 180,000 units per annum.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Shifts:** **Aequs** withdrew its Q4 FY27 consumer EBITDA breakeven target. **Digilogic** clarified its EBITDA guidance to reflect core growth excluding interest income.
*   **Strategic Pivots:** **Ideaforge** mutually terminated its U.S. joint venture with First Breach, opting for an independent strategy. **Cochin Shipyard** terminated its JV talks with HD KSOE (Korea) for block fabrication, choosing to proceed independently.
*   **Funding:** **Ideaforge** formalized a Rs 151 crore debt funding agreement with the TDB. **Aequs** secured a Rs 6,500 million promoter-led investment.

## Company one-liners
*   **Aequs:** Scaling aerospace, but consumer segment headwinds force a pause in breakeven timeline.
*   **BEL:** Strong H1 with 26% revenue growth; eyes major QRSAM/NGC inflows before fiscal year-end.
*   **Centum:** Record Rs 2,000 crore order book, though supply chain bottlenecks persist.
*   **Cochin Shipyard:** Expanding footprint via strategic JVs and brownfield/greenfield projects; expects positive cash flow in FY27.
*   **Digilogic:** Strong H1 growth with Rs 74 crore order visibility; "Project Udaan" on track.
*   **Ideaforge:** Profitable Q2 driven by defense execution; launching combat UAVs and securing RDI debt funding.
*   **MTAR:** Record Q2 with 200% revenue jump; working capital improved and multi-vertical expansion visible.
*   **Unimech:** Successfully transitioning to a long-term precision engineering platform with 715% order book growth.
*   **Zen Technologies:** Highest-ever quarterly revenue with 38% EBITDA margin; positioned for upcoming domestic FTP tenders.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Order Finalization:** The market will watch for the formalization of major defense orders, specifically QRSAM and NGC (**BEL**), NGSV (**Cochin Shipyard**), and upcoming FTP tenders (**Zen Technologies**).
*   **Supply Chain:** Monitoring if lead times for electronic components (memories/controllers) stabilize for **Centum** and **Ideaforge**.
*   **Capacity Ramp-up:** Progress on **MTAR’s** fuel cell expansion and **Digilogic’s** "Project Udaan" construction milestones.
*   **Consumer Recovery:** Any updates on the "recalibration" of the consumer segment at **Aequs**.
