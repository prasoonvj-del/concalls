# Rossell Techsys Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Achieved record-breaking quarterly revenue, double-digit PBT for the first time, successful institutional capital raise, and strong non-aerospace growth traction.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 FY27 Revenue:** INR 185.56 crores (up 47% YoY)
- **Q2 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 29 crores
- **Q2 FY27 Profit Before Tax (PBT):** INR 12.91 crores
- **H1 FY27 Revenue:** INR 345 crores (Non-aerospace/defense contributed INR ~130-140 crores)
- **H1 FY27 EBITDA:** INR 52 crores
- **H1 FY27 PBT:** INR 22.3 crores
- **H1 FY27 Fresh Orders Secured:** ~INR 430 crores
- **H1 FY27 Bids Submitted:** ~INR 1,350 crores
- **Confirmed PO Order Book:** INR 910 crores (executable over 2.5 years)
- **Strategic Agreements Order Book:** INR 3,100 crores (executable over 5 years)
- **Employee Strength:** 1,352 professionals

### Highlights
- Delivered highest ever quarterly revenue and first-time double-digit quarterly PBT.
- Successfully onboarded another leading global semiconductor equipment manufacturer.
- Completed a INR 300 crores capital raise with marquee institutional investors, including SBI Mutual Fund and Kotak Mutual Fund.
- Inaugurated a new leased facility of ~140,000 sq ft (expandable to 210,000 sq ft) in the Aerospace Park, commencing operations early next quarter.
- *Record context check:* The earnings call and investor deck align on record-breaking Q2 performance driven by A&D and semiconductor growth alongside the INR 300 cr fundraise.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects Q3 and Q4 to be the strongest quarters of the year, maintaining historical trends.
- Growth visibility anticipated for the next 12-16 quarters.
- Semiconductor revenues expected to scale meaningfully over coming quarters and years.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Aerospace and defense business benefiting from strong global demand, increasing defense spending, and supply chain realignments.
- Semiconductor and space sectors showing rapid acceleration, with H1 non-aerospace revenue crossing INR 140 crores (compared to INR 100 crores for full year FY26 across space and semiconductors).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management maintains a long-term EBITDA margin target of 18% to 23%.
- Interest costs expected to reduce substantially starting Q3 due to debt repayment using fundraise proceeds.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- New ~140,000 sq ft leased facility inaugurated in the Aerospace Park to support semiconductor and non-defense growth headroom.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Balance sheet significantly strengthened via INR 300 crores equity capital raise used for debt repayment, operational excellence, and infrastructure.
- Inventory holding improved to 6.67 months in Q2 FY27 from 7.98 months in Q2 FY26; internal target is to become a 4-month inventory company over the next 24 months.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Management noted they remain mindful of the challenges that come with scaling a business rapidly.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nisarg Shah (Pkeday Investments) on margin trajectory:** Management confirmed they are well on track to achieve their target EBITDA margin range of 18% to 23% as operating leverage kicks in.
2. **Darshil Jhaveri on order book execution timelines:** Management clarified that confirmed POs of INR 910 crores are executable over 2.5 years, while strategic agreements of INR 3,100 crores span 5 years.
3. **Nitish Rana on fundraise utilization and interest costs:** Management stated that funds raised will repay working capital limits, resulting in significant interest cost savings visible starting Q3.
4. **Akash Jaiswal on MRO business progress:** CEO Senthil Balasubramanian noted that new MRO capabilities are driving partnership talks in India and aftermarket service work for Boeing in the U.S.

### One-line takeaway
Rossell Techsys delivered its strongest half-year ever, propelled by record Q2 revenue, a INR 300 crores capital infusion, and rapid scaling in the semiconductor segment.
