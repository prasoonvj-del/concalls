# Tonbo Imaging India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong revenue growth in Q2 and clear strategic roadmap for platform and Directed Energy Weapon (DEW) expansion, despite historical quarterly volatility.

### Key numbers
*   **H1 FY27 Revenue:** Rs 219 crore (vs Rs 77 crore in H1 FY26).
*   **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 183 crore (vs Rs 36 crore in Q1).
*   **H1 EBITDA:** Rs 31.15 crore (14.2% margin).
*   **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 44 crore (24.11% margin).
*   **H1 PAT:** Rs 5.8 crore (2.61% margin).
*   **Q2 PAT:** Rs 23.5 crore (12.71% margin).
*   **Order Book:** Rs 250 crore.
*   **Contracts under negotiation:** Rs 408 crore.

### Highlights
*   Revenue growth driven by a strong Q2 performance; management noted that quarterly revenue volatility is inherent to the business model.
*   Exports contributed 35% of H1 revenue.
*   The company secured a strategic export order from a European state-owned company for a multi-sensor stabilized seeker, with an option for EUR 13 million in follow-on orders.
*   *Record Check:* The call confirms the strong H1 FY27 recovery noted in our record.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management expects H2 revenue to be "materially better" than H1.
*   Declined to provide specific revenue or margin guidance due to the recent IPO.
*   Directed Energy Weapon (DEW) first delivery is targeted for 2029-2030.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Tactical systems (sights/binoculars) remain the primary revenue driver (70-80% of business).
*   Platform systems (gimbals, fire control) are expected to account for a larger share of revenue over time.
*   Strong demand noted for lightweight gimbals for UAVs and loitering munitions.
*   Management views India’s defense procurement (TPCR) and European re-armament as long-term tailwinds.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   H1 gross margin was 34.62% (vs 51% in FY26), attributed to product mix.
*   Domestic margins: Tactical (40-45%), Platform (45-50%).
*   Export margins: 60-65%.
*   Employee costs are fixed; management expects EBITDA margins to improve in H2 due to higher revenue scale.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Company operates a fabless model; no factory capex.
*   DEW project cost estimated at ~Rs 160 crore (funded by Rs 120 crore EXIM loan, Rs 25 crore iDEX grant, Rs 15 crore internal).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Net worth: Rs 564 crore.
*   Bank borrowings: Rs 169 crore (working capital).
*   Cash credit limit increased by Rs 20 crore post-quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Platform system timelines are dependent on the readiness of partner vehicles/vessels/aircraft.
*   DEW development is at TRL 4 and carries product engineering and testing risks.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **DEW Market Size:** Management clarified the total Indian market is ~Rs 28,000 crore (140 systems @ Rs 200 crore each), which is distinct from the 2030 TAM figure.
*   **Competition:** Management stated there is no direct Indian competitor for DEW; BEL Optronic Devices competes in tactical/optronics but lacks Tonbo's full portfolio.
*   **Order Conversion:** Tactical orders typically convert in 3-6 months; Platform orders in 6-18 months.
*   **Export Optionality:** The EUR 13 million European contract option is included in the Rs 408 crore "contracts under negotiation" figure.

### One-line takeaway
Strong H1 performance with a clear pivot toward platform systems and long-term DEW development, though quarterly revenue volatility remains a structural feature.
