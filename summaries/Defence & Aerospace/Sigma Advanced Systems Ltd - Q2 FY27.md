# Sigma Advanced Systems Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong operational performance, new acquisition wins, and margin expansion.

### Key numbers
- **Operational Revenue:** INR 417 crore (up 11.43% QoQ from INR 374 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Operational EBITDA:** INR 82.28 crore (vs INR 61 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **EBITDA Margin:** 19.7%
- **PAT (excluding share of profit/loss from associates):** INR 48.6 crore (vs INR 35.41 crore in Q1 FY27)
- **Order Book:** INR 10,000+ crore (90% international)
- **Total Term Borrowings / Debt:** INR 76 crore to INR 80-odd crore
- **UK Investment:** £8 to £9 million
- **Sri City Capacity Utilization:** 10% to 15% (Aerospace); 20% (Ammunition/shells)
- **FY27 Revenue Guidance:** INR 1,900 to INR 2,000 crore
- **FY27 EBITDA Margin Guidance:** ~20%

### Highlights
- Completed the acquisition of Bromford Precision Solutions (UK), deepening engagement with Rolls-Royce.
- Sri City facility commenced production in September, rolling out parts to the UK and global customers.
- Entered into an exclusive collaboration/MOU with Munitions India Limited (MIL) for course correction technology.
- Secured orders from the Government of India for the "Indrajal Ranger" vehicle-based anti-drone solution.
- *Vs Record:* The call confirms continued execution on global aerospace and defense wins without major disruptions.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management targets FY27 revenue of INR 1,900 to INR 2,000 crore with an EBITDA margin of ~20%.
- Targeting EBITDA margin improvement of 200 to 400 bps by end of FY27, and a further 400 to 600 bps by end of FY28.
- Aerospace capacity utilization at Sri City expected to reach 50% to 60% by end of FY28 and full utilization by FY29.
- Anticipates closing inorganic M&A transactions in Q4 FY27, backed by a potential fundraise of up to INR 3,000 crore (subject to approvals).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Global aerospace OEMs increasingly looking to India for engineering depth and scale.
- NATO and allied nations strengthening ammunition manufacturing capacity; counter-drone capabilities seeing everyday security demand.
- Approximately 90% of revenue is export-driven (denominated in GBP, USD, and EUR) with direct Tier-1 relationships with primes.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- UK OTIF (on-time-in-full) improved from 65% to 97% following investments in buffer stock, raw materials, and new equipment kits.
- Transfer of work from the UK to India (targeting a 30-70 model where 30% is retained in the UK and 70% moves to India) is driving structural margin expansion.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- No major fresh capex required in the near term; existing infrastructure and facilities are sufficient for the next few years.
- UK operations received £8 to £9 million in investments focused on automation (palletization) and process efficiency.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Company is nearly debt-free outside of working capital lines and term borrowings of ~INR 76 crore.
- Scheduled debt repayments are slated from January 1st to December 31st, calendar '27.
- Cash on balance sheet stands at ~INR 350 crore (partly from the June preferential placement).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- No specific operational risks flagged by management, though general forward-looking execution disclaimers were noted.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Nilesh Soni (Haitong Securities) on UK investments & EBITDA drivers:** Management explained that £8-£9 million was invested in working capital and capex, pushing OTIF from 65% to 97% and driving a 200% per quarter improvement in UK EBITDA.
2. **Rajesh Joseph (Individual Investor) on fundraise purpose:** Management stated the planned INR 3,000 crore fundraise is primarily to fuel inorganic growth and M&A opportunities expected to close in Q4.
3. **Dipen Vakil on capacity utilization:** Management noted current utilization is 15% for aerospace and 20% for shells, with single-shift operations leaving ample unutilized capacity without additional capex.
4. **Vaibhav Jain on acquisition strategy:** Management clarified that they target profitable, EBITDA-positive companies scaling between USD 100 to USD 150 million, rather than distressed/loss-making assets.
5. **Tejash on UK vs India work split and aerospace margins:** Management stated a 30-70 split (30% UK retained, 70% India transfer) and projected aerospace EBITDA margins to rise to 25%-30% by FY28.

### One-line takeaway
Sigma Advanced Systems delivered strong Q2 FY27 performance (revenue INR 417 crore, EBITDA margin 19.7%), underpinned by a robust INR 10,000+ crore order book, successful UK integration, and margin-accretive work transfers to India.
