# Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-11 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - 17th consecutive quarter of upward financial trends, robust revenue and PAT growth, and receipt of Navratna status.

### Key numbers
- Total income: Rs 1,914 crore, up 38% YoY (vs Q1 FY26)
- Revenue from operations: Rs 1,815 crore, up 39% YoY (from Rs 1,310 crore)
- Profit After Tax (PAT): Rs 173 crore, up 44% YoY (from Rs 120 crore)
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Rs 15.09, up 44% YoY (from Rs 10.49)
- Order book: Rs 13,596.26 crore (Shipbuilding: Rs 12,980 crore; Ship repair: Rs 96 crore; Naval vessel gun: Rs 219 crore; Bailey bridge: Rs 158 crore; Deck machinery: Rs 58 crore; Diesel engine plant: Rs 82 crore)

### Highlights
- Accorded Navratna status by the Government of India on June 19, 2026.
- Commissioned three ships (INS Dunagiri P17A Alpha stealth frigate, INS Sanshodhak Survey Vessel Large, and INS Agrai Anti-Submarine Shallow Water Craft) into the Indian Navy on June 21, 2026.
- Received letter of intent/award notification from ONGC for four Platform Support Vessels (PSV) and Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata for a fully electric tug.
- Order book dropped below Rs 15,000 crore for the first time in 10 years, reflecting a strong execution rate.
- Current order book comprises 11 projects and 44 marine platforms.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the Next-Generation Corvette (NGC) contract to be signed in Q2 FY27.
- Revenue recognition for the NGC project is expected to commence in FY28.
- Current execution momentum is expected to be replicated in coming quarters.
- Pay commission impact on employee costs is anticipated to be in the normal range of 15-20%.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Over Rs 80,000 crore worth of defense RFPs are currently live (including Fast Interceptor Crafts, water jet FACs, interceptor boats, and Coast Guard OPVs).
- Around Rs 70,000 crore worth of RFPs (Mine Counter Measure Vessel and Landing Platform Docks) expected in the current calendar year.
- Non-defense PSU demand aggregation (GAIL, ONGC, BPCL) includes tenders for MR tankers, VLGCs, Aframax vessels, and container ships, totaling roughly Rs 1,50,000 crore in potential defense and commercial opportunities.
- Water metro opportunities across various state governments could translate to 30 to 40 platforms worth approximately Rs 1,500 crore.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Other expenses dropped due to the absence of final commissioning expenses that were incurred in Q4 FY26 for three delivered ships.
- Contract employee deployment varies with the project execution phase, leading to a sinusoidal cost pattern.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Current shipbuilding capacity supports concurrent construction of 28 platforms, expected to reach 32 platforms by the end of the calendar year via ongoing modernization.
- Kolkata Port Trust (Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port) revitalization of two leased facilities: capital infusion of about Rs 200 crore, completion within 18 months.
- Greenfield shipyard at Raichak, West Bengal: expected capital infusion of Rs 2,200 crore; DPR in final stages; construction to start in 2027, taking 3 to 5 years.
- Western seaboard facility (Gujarat) via PPP model: total ship solution facility (shipbuilding, green ship recycling, ship repair); capital infusion of around Rs 2,000 crore; DPR complete, environmental clearance obtained; project to commence next year, taking 3 years to operationalize.
- Expansion projects will increase capacity from 32 to 43 platforms, enabling construction of larger commercial ships (Aframax and VLGC type) up to 10,000-ton displacement and 200 meters length.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Not explicitly discussed in detail, other than ongoing capital allocation for expansion plans.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- NGC contract has faced some delays compared to prior expectations (originally anticipated at the end of the last financial year or Q1).
- Riverine constraints and length/displacement restrictions in Kolkata necessitate greenfield/brownfield expansion for larger commercial vessels.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs) on NGC project delays and revenue start:** Management stated no red flags exist, expects contract signing in the current quarter, and noted revenue recognition starts in FY28 with an 8-year project completion timeline.
- **Dipen Vakil on Capex quantum and capabilities:** Management outlined Rs 200 crore for Kolkata leased facilities, Rs 2,200 crore for Raichak (West Bengal), and Rs 2,000 crore for Gujarat, raising concurrent platform capacity from 32 to 43.
- **Kavish Pare on indigenization levels:** Management highlighted 100% indigenization in hull/float, ~60% in fight (weapons/systems), 0% in marine propulsion engines (though power generators are 100% indigenous), and ~70% in survival equipment; overall ship indigenization reaches over 80% on completed projects.
- **Harshad Kapadia on other expenses and employee cost drops:** Management attributed the drop in other expenses and contract labor costs to the timing of ship deliveries and the project execution phase.
- **Pratap Maliwal on Next-Gen Destroyer (NGD) timeline:** Management stated NGD has not yet received DAC approval, and does not expect an RFP before FY29.

### One-line takeaway
GRSE reported strong Q1 FY27 financial performance with 39% revenue growth and 44% PAT growth, backed by a robust order book of Rs 13,596 crore and aggressive brownfield/greenfield capacity expansion plans.
