# Data Patterns (India) Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-16 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust order inflows in H1 FY27, strong execution visibility for the second half, and reiterated full-year guidance despite lower Q2 revenue on a high base.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (revenue growth of 20% to 25% and EBITDA margins of 35% to 40%).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted more towards internal product development for complete systems (radars, electronic warfare, communications, drones, satellites) and targeting direct MoD/global opportunities alongside traditional PSU work.
- **New topics or risks:** Discussed the internal decision to initiate design and development of agile defence microsatellites and advanced optronics; highlighted internal government approval delays holding up ~INR 956 crores of negotiated orders.
- **Last promises delivered:** ST Advanced Composites acquisition completed effective September 17, 2026.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations (Q2 FY27):** INR 133.3 crores (sequential growth of 14.8% over INR 116 crores in Q1 FY27; lower YoY due to a high execution base in Q2 FY26)
- **Revenue from operations (H1 FY27):** INR 249.3 crores
- **Profit before tax (Q2 FY27):** INR 30 crores
- **Profit after tax (Q2 FY27):** INR 22 crores
- **Profit before tax (H1 FY27):** INR 59 crores
- **Profit after tax (H1 FY27):** INR 44 crores
- **Fresh order inflows (H1 FY27):** INR 1,046 crores (including INR 929 crores in Q2 FY27)
- **Order book (as of April 1, 2026):** INR 927 crores
- **Orders on hand / closing order book (as of September 30, 2026):** INR 1,742 crores
- **Negotiated orders awaiting formal receipt:** INR 956 crores
- **Overall order book visibility:** Approximately INR 2,698 crores
- **International order book:** INR 32 crores
- **Cash, bank balances, and investments:** More than INR 500 crores as of September 30, 2026
- **Engineering team size:** More than 1,100 engineers

### Highlights
- Acquired a 100% stake in ST Advanced Composites Private Limited, effective September 17, 2026, enhancing advanced composite design and manufacturing capabilities for radars and aerospace programs.
- Secured INR 929 crores in fresh orders during Q2 FY27, taking H1 FY27 order inflows to INR 1,046 crores.
- Advanced product developments including drone defence systems (radars, passive RF detection, jamming), maritime/airborne radars, jammer pods, COMINT/ELINT, Radar Warning Receivers, and software-defined radios.
- Initiated design and development of advanced optronics and agile defence microsatellites, staffing ex-ISRO scientists.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20% to 25%.
- Management maintained EBITDA margin guidance of 35% to 40% for FY27.
- Management expects H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1 FY27, driven by a healthy order book and acceleration in production/deliveries.
- Management expects INR 1,500 crores to INR 2,000 crores of additional order inflows in the pipeline before March.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Customers are increasingly engaging for complete systems and integrated solutions rather than individual parts/subsystems.
- Budget allocations are increasing, and Acceptance of Necessity (AoNs) are being released more frequently.
- International enquiries are growing, with radar front-end deliveries to Europe expected between December and March, and co-investing in radars with a British company.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Quarterly revenue and profitability were lower YoY due to an exceptionally strong execution base in Q2 FY26.
- Business remains Q4-heavy due to program-based and project-based execution cycles.
- Active recruitment of fresh engineering talent continues to expand the 1,100+ engineer team.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Building another factory/building, with foundational work already underway.
- Investing in advance for production infrastructure, test equipment, and space ahead of anticipated large order inflows.
- Additional product roadmap investments will exceed the previously discussed INR 150 crores.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Strong balance sheet with cash, bank balances, and investments exceeding INR 500 crores as of September 30, 2026.
- Company maintains net cash position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Delays in contract finalization and order receipt are driven by internal government approval processes beyond management's control.
- Software integration for certain programs (such as HAWK radar) faces minor delays.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Abhijeet Singh (Systematix):** Asked about the delay in the ~INR 1,000 crores of negotiated orders and cancellation risks. *Management stated there is no cancellation risk as these are funded central government tenders, and delays are due to internal approval processes; expects contracts in 1-2 months.*
- **Amit Dixit (Goldman Sachs):** Inquired about re-entering the space segment and counter-drone traction. *Management confirmed an internal decision to design and build 100-500 kg class satellites in-house with ex-ISRO staff, and that counter-drone radars/jammers are nearing demonstration stage.*
- **Dipen Vakil (PhillipCapital):** Asked about the execution schedule difference between production and development orders. *Management explained execution varies from quarterly delivery schedules (back-to-back with PSUs) to multi-year windows (like HAL's 6-8 year windows), depending on the end application.*
- **Garvit Goyal (Fintech/Analyst):** Asked about the impact of Tejas Mk2 delays and the satellite monetization strategy. *Management stated Tejas Mk2 avionics are unaffected and satellites are being built for global product standards and space heritage rather than immediate contracts.*
- **Sagar Gandhi (Mutual Fund):** Asked about the single biggest risk to scaling. *Management cited government-driven business unpredictability, foreign competition pricing, and end-specification risks, but noted building blocks mitigate development risk.*
- **Keyurkumar Vadaliya (Niveshaay):** Inquired about capex utilization and dual-use applicability. *Management clarified capex covers customized building blocks, new factory construction, and production infrastructure for volume delivery.*

### One-line takeaway
Data Patterns reported muted Q2 revenue and profit YoY on a high base but posted strong H1 order inflows of INR 1,046 crores, maintaining its full-year guidance of 20-25% revenue growth and 35-40% EBITDA margins.
