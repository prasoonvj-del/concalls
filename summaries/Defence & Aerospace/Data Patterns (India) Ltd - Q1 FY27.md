# Data Patterns (India) Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-07 · Defence & Aerospace*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited healthy customer engagement, strong product transition, a robust order book/pipeline, and reiterated full-year growth guidance despite temporary quarterly margin and revenue fluctuations.

### Key numbers
- **Revenue from operations:** INR 116 crores (17% YoY growth)
- **Gross profit:** INR 91.5 crores (16% YoY growth)
- **Gross margin:** 78.9%
- **EBITDA:** INR 31.4 crores
- **EBITDA margin:** 27%
- **Profit after tax (PAT):** INR 22.1 crores
- **PAT margin:** 19%
- **Order book (as of June 30, 2026):** INR 920 crores
- **Order book including orders received in July and negotiated:** INR 2,654 crores
- **International order book:** INR 39 crores
- **Cash, bank balances, and investments (as of June 30, 2026):** INR 530 crores
- **Expected fresh order inflows (FY27 guidance):** Around INR 2,000 crores over and above received/negotiated orders

### Highlights
- Secured orders across avionics, radars, electronic warfare, and automotive test equipment programs from the Ministry of Defence, DRDO, and BrahMos.
- Initial traction and order inflows received in the drone and counter-drone business.
- Acquired ST Advanced (composite for radomes and advanced composites for aircraft systems) to enhance capabilities in composite structures.
- Transitioned several products from the development stage to complete system solutions.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20% to 25%.
- Management maintained full-year EBITDA margin guidance in the 35% to 40% range.
- Management expects to secure around INR 2,000 crores of fresh order inflows during FY27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Favorable policy environment with a continued focus on higher indigenous content and defense procurement.
- Growing international interest from European and US OEMs due to manpower shortages and faster turnaround requirements.
- Counter-drone demand is rising, with the MoD experimenting with 10-20 systems per vendor across multiple categories.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee costs remain elevated due to investments in engineering talent and future capabilities.
- Other expenses rose 64% YoY due to repairs and maintenance from facility revamping and a ~INR 2 crore provision for long-standing receivables.
- Product mix changes and uneven quarterly revenue distribution impacted quarterly EBITDA margins.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Planned capex of INR 150 crores to INR 200 crores (or INR 200 crores plus) over the next 2 years for infrastructure, clean rooms, integration facilities, and AI-driven IT investments/servers.
- Foundation work and facility expansion already underway.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company remains net debt-free with cash, bank balances, and investments totaling INR 530 crores as of June 30, 2026.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Dependence on customer inspection schedules and approval timings can cause quarterly revenue slippages.
- Protracted negotiations and approval delays can stretch expected order conversion timelines by several months.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Hardik Rawat (Investec/Individual):** Asked about the 64% rise in other expenses. *Management attributed it to facility revamping repairs and a INR 2 crore provision for receivables.*
- **Dipen Vakil (PhillipCapital):** Asked about counter-drone offerings and platform vs subsystem play. *Management clarified they supply sensors (active radars, passive electronic intelligence, jamming countermeasures) rather than full platforms.*
- **Vineet Prasad (Investec Capital):** Inquired about the status of the HimShakti project and HAWK radars. *Management stated BEL is discussing next-generation platforms based on Dhanshakti, and HAWK radar hardware is ready while software discussions are ongoing.*
- **Kavish Parekh (360 ONE Capital):** Asked about time lines for commercial orders for BrahMos seekers. *Management expects contract/order intake to happen before the end of the financial year.*
- **Neelotpal Sahu (JM Financial):** Asked about the addressable market size. *Management stated total addressable market for their products is around INR 30,000 crores, plus INR 10,000-12,000 crores in repeat contracts (~INR 40,000 crores total).*

### One-line takeaway
Data Patterns reported a 17% YoY revenue increase for Q1 FY27 alongside compressed quarterly margins due to elevated overheads and uneven revenue recognition, while reiterating its full-year guidance of 20-25% revenue growth and 35-40% EBITDA margins.
