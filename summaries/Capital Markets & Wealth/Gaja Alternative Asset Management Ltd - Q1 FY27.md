# Gaja Alternative Asset Management Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-09-17 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Consistent growth in total income and profit, alongside strong operational leverage and SEBI approval for new funds.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q1 FY27):** Rs 51.8 crore (up 26% YoY)
- **Total Income (LTM ended June '26):** Rs 168.4 crore (up 39% YoY)
- **PAT (Q1 FY27):** Rs 27.2 crore (up 35% YoY)
- **PAT (LTM ended June '26):** Rs 89.1 crore (up 38% YoY)
- **Fee Income Growth (Q1 FY27):** 7% YoY
- **Performance Income Growth (Q1 FY27):** 17% YoY
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio (Q1 FY27):** 38.4% (down from 42.3% YoY)
- **Annual EPS (Q1 FY27):** Rs 9.5 per share (up 28% YoY)
- **Book Value per Share:** Rs 56.1 (up 19% YoY)
- **Net Worth:** Rs 633.6 crore as of June 30, 2026 (or Rs 1,050 crore post-IPO including primary capital raise)
- **ROE:** 15.3% as of June 30, 2026 (vs 14.7% YoY)
- **Fund IV Deployment:** 75% deployed as of June 2026 (subsequently at 81%), tracking a gross IRR of 29% and 1.8x gross MOIC across 7 investments.
- **Dividend:** 15% (Rs 0.75 per share); payout ratio increased from 10% to 12.5%.
- **IPO Details:** Raised Rs 575 crore of primary capital; 33x oversubscribed with 1.7 million applications.

### Highlights
- First quarterly earnings call as a listed company.
- Declared a dividend of Rs 0.75 per share (15%) with payout ratio increased to 12.5%.
- Received SEBI approval for Fund V (flagship strategy) and Eastgate (secondaries strategy).
- Appointed Price Waterhouse (PW) as new statutory auditors.
- Post-IPO, the founding/internal team holds over 68% ownership of the AMC.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management declined to provide future earnings guidance or specific fund winding-up/raise timelines, citing company policy and board/peer advice.
- Management expressed high confidence in maintaining historical financial trendlines (historical total income CAGR of 23%, profit CAGR of 35%).

### Demand & industry commentary
- Alternatives industry in India grew 43x in the last 10 years and is expected to grow 6x to ~$1 trillion in the next 10 years (outpacing bank deposits at 2.1x and mutual funds at 3.3x).
- Gaja Capital acts as the bridge connecting India's savings system to alternatives.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Total expenses for Q1 FY27 grew 14% YoY to Rs 19.9 crore.
- Cost-to-income ratio improved to 38.4% from 42.3% due to operating leverage.
- Employee expenses in Q1 include seasonality related to bonus payouts.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Proposed **Fund V** (flagship strategy): Target commitments of Rs 2,500 crore, 10-year term, targeting 10-12 mid-market investments (enterprise values up to Rs 2,000 crore scaling to Rs 10,000 crore).
- Proposed **Eastgate** (secondaries strategy): Target commitments of Rs 1,500 crore, 5-year term, focusing on providing liquidity to PE/VC managers via secondary portfolio acquisitions.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stands at Rs 633.6 crore as of June 30, 2026 (and Rs 1,050 crore post-IPO including primary capital).
- Sponsor commitment across funds stands at ~Rs 589 crore (7.1% of total fund size), exceeding SEBI regulatory minimums.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Performance income (carried interest and sponsor gains) is inherently lumpy on a quarter-to-quarter basis, though smoothed out on an LTM basis.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Siddhant Lodaya (Sanshi Fund) on Fund IV dry powder and deployment:** Management stated Fund IV was 75% deployed across 7 investments as of June (subsequently 81%) and aims for 10-12 total investments.
2. **Mithun Aswath on performance income proportion and predictability:** Management clarified that performance income growth is a key secular driver of high-quality PE asset managers and should be evaluated on an LTM basis.
3. **Soumil Zaveri on carried interest sources:** Management confirmed carried interest currently stems from Fund II, while sponsor gains stem from Fund III and IV. Hurdle rates are 10% for rupee investors and 8% for dollar investors.
4. **Abhi Jain on AI strategy and valuations (Fractal and Sarvam):** Management clarified that deep-tech/AI investments are part of Fund IV (not AMC directly) and are approached as growth-stage opportunities with conservative relative valuations compared to sector medians.
5. **Jyothish Vijayan on Q1 performance fee jump:** Management explained that Q1 performance fee gains reflect market recovery from Q4 FY26 lows, captured via fair value gains on sponsor commitments.
6. **Sheetal Kumar on Fund III performance/hurdles:** Management reiterated that Fund III is already generating sponsor-gain performance income, while carried interest will trigger once the fund hurdle (8%-10%) and principal are returned.

### One-line takeaway
Gaja Alternative Asset Management reported a robust Q1 FY27 with a 35% YoY jump in PAT to Rs 27.2 crore, backed by operating leverage and ongoing expansion into Fund V and secondary strategies.
