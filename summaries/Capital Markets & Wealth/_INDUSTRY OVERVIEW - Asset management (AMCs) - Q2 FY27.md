# Capital Markets & Wealth - Asset management (AMCs): what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The asset management sector demonstrated continued expansion in Q2 FY27, with total industry quarterly average AUM (QAAUM) reaching roughly ₹87.2–87.5 lakh crore (up ~13% YoY) and monthly industry SIP collections hitting record levels of ~₹32,490–32,500 crore in September 2026. Topline operating metrics remained healthy across major players, driven by the expansion of equity-oriented assets. 

However, bottom-line profitability was mixed across the board due to idiosyncratic and non-operating headwinds. While HDFC AMC saw operating profit rise 16% YoY and PBT rise 12% YoY, Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC (ABSL AMC) experienced a YoY contraction in PAT due to stepped-up employee and ESOP costs. ICICI Prudential AMC and UTI AMC faced sharp sequential drops in headline PAT due to mark-to-market (MTM) losses on treasury investments/other income, while Nippon Life India AMC was hit by an exceptional regulatory settlement charge of ₹96.76 crore. Strategically, AMCs are increasingly shifting focus toward alternative assets (AIFs/PMS), passives, and newly structured specialized investment fund (SIF) offerings.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
### Consensus
* **Systematic flow resilience:** All managements agree that retail systematic investment plan (SIP) flows remain extraordinarily robust, providing predictable, sticky long-term capital despite choppy market environments.
* **Investor interest in equities:** Retail flows continue to bias heavily toward equity-oriented products, with mid-cap and small-cap strategies capturing disproportionate inflows across the industry.

### Divergence
* **Redemptions and investor behavior:** 
  * HDFC AMC took a decisively confident view, arguing that Indian retail investors have matured faster than anticipated and noting that softness in systematic flows was driven strictly by wealth managers pausing tactical systematic transfer plans (STPs), not retail SIPs.
  * In contrast, ICICI Prudential AMC highlighted elevated redemption pressures in hybrid categories (Balanced Advantage and Multi-Asset Allocation funds) and flagged that international advisory mandates continue to see persistent FPI outflows.
  * Nippon Life India AMC cautioned that overall industry-wide unique investor additions are slowing down, citing instances of high-net-worth individual (HNI) investors exiting large-cap funds amid broader market corrections.
  * UTI AMC noted specific distribution vulnerabilities, highlighting that while gross SIPs remain steady, equity outflows and redemptions have been particularly heavy through banking and national distributor channels.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
* **Yield trajectory:** Realized equity yields vary by asset mix and distributor arrangements. Equity yields sit at 73–74 bps for UTI AMC, 67 bps for ICICI Prudential AMC, 63–64 bps for ABSL AMC, 58–61 bps for HDFC AMC, and 52 bps (ex-arbitrage) for Nippon Life India AMC. 
* **Telescopic pricing & dilution:** Nippon Life AMC explicitly flagged ongoing yield compression driven by SEBI's telescopic pricing slabs as its equity assets scale up. Conversely, UTI AMC saw a marginal yield uptick caused by passing commission repricing back to intermediaries following SEBI TER-to-BER shifts. Passive funds continue to carry lower yields (8–12 bps across firms), though UTI AMC noted they require almost zero incremental running costs.
* **Employee expenses and ESOPs:**
  * ABSL AMC guided for existing ESOPs to cost ₹55–60 crore/year, with a new Q2 scheme adding ₹14–15 crore/year, keeping employee expenses elevated.
  * HDFC AMC maintained its FY27 ESOP cost projection of ₹79–80 crore (~₹18.6 crore in Q2).
  * ICICI Prudential AMC kept its annual ESOP guidance intact at ₹64–68 crore.
  * Nippon Life AMC guided for ~₹58 crore of ESOP expenses in FY27, while flagging 18–20% annual opex growth over the next 6–8 quarters to fund digital, brand, and senior AIF hiring.
  * UTI AMC maintained standalone employee cost guidance of ~₹95 crore/quarter and consolidated at ~₹130 crore/quarter.
* **Exceptional charges:** Nippon Life AMC recognized a ₹96.76 crore charge to settle SEBI proceedings regarding historical Yes Bank AT-1 bond exposures.

## Capex & capacity
* **Channel and distribution expansion:** ABSL AMC deployed 80 dedicated staff across 14 locations to scale its direct channel for HNI and existing clients. ICICI Prudential AMC is leaning into digital acquisition to expand its customer footprint into B30 locations.
* **Inorganic growth & team build-outs:** ICICI Prudential AMC’s board cleared the acquisition of ICICI Securities' PMS portfolio (~₹3,000 crore AUM). HDFC AMC expanded its alternatives investment bench to over 20 professionals. 
* **Alternatives & SIF product rollout:** 
  * ABSL AMC's SIF platform scaled to ~₹700 crore across 3 strategies.
  * HDFC AMC introduced two new AIFs (Category II private markets and Category III listed securities) and has SIF products in the pipeline.
  * ICICI Prudential AMC is running 4 SIF strategies and preparing a 5th.
  * Nippon Life AMC is awaiting SEBI clearance to launch two SIF strategies.
  * UTI AMC received SEBI approval to launch its first SIF offering (UTI Classif Hybrid Long Short Fund) in Q3 FY27.

## What changed since last quarter
* **Guidance and tone shifts:**
  * *HDFC AMC:* Shifted stance from "watchful" of potential downturns to "confident" in retail stability; confirmed the EPFO fixed income mandate officially went live.
  * *ABSL AMC:* Management transitioned from highlighting "strong AUM growth" to emphasizing direct channel execution, cost controls, and bottom-line margin preservation.
  * *Nippon Life AMC:* Pivoted tone from "record-breaking" to cautious on market volatility and broader industry retail flow moderation.
  * *ICICI Prudential AMC:* Tone shifted from a "rebound" in SIPs to "stable" inflows, while noting an uptick in hybrid redemptions.
* **Capital allocation and payouts:** UTI AMC reaffirmed its 90–95% regular dividend payout framework, explicitly quashing speculation around special dividends or distributing its standalone accumulated cash reserves. ICICI Prudential AMC declared an interim dividend of ₹30 per share, and Nippon Life AMC declared ₹9.50 per share.

## Bullish vs cautious

| Company | Tone | Key reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HDFC AMC** | Bullish | Strong double-digit operating profit growth (+16% YoY), retail SIP resilience, and successful scaling of high-margin alternative strategies. |
| **ICICI Prudential AMC** | Mixed | Stable market share and operating profit growth, countered by QoQ PAT contraction (-7.8%) from MTM hits and elevated redemptions in hybrid schemes. |
| **UTI AMC** | Mixed | Core PAT expanded 22% YoY, but reported PAT fell sharply QoQ (-55%) due to treasury adjustments, alongside sustained redemptions in flexi-cap schemes through banking channels. |
| **Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC** | Mixed | Group AUM surpassed ₹11 lakh crore, but profitability dropped YoY (PAT down 8.3%) under the weight of higher ESOP schemes and normalized staff cost provisions. |
| **Nippon Life India AMC** | Mixed | Operating profits hit all-time highs (+25% YoY), but bottom-line was dented by an exceptional regulatory charge (₹96.76 crore) and ongoing telescopic fee compression. |

## Company one-liners
* **HDFC AMC:** Delivered disciplined operating leverage and steady market share, leaning into alternative fund launches while retail SIPs anchored flows.
* **ICICI Prudential AMC:** Retained strong equity/hybrid positioning and greenlit a PMS acquisition, though other income mark-to-market impacts dragged sequential PAT.
* **UTI AMC:** Maintained core earnings resilience and high dividend payout commitments, even as flexi-cap outflows via distributor channels posed a headwind.
* **Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC:** Scaled total AUM past ₹11 lakh crore and expanded into passives, but reported compressed margins due to escalating ESOP outlays.
* **Nippon Life India AMC:** Matched industry-leading operating profit growth with top-tier ETF scale, but was burdened by a one-time Yes Bank AT-1 settlement and margin yield dilution.

## What to watch next quarter
* **Launch and scaling of SIF platforms:** Track early inflows and fee dynamics for newly approved SIFs across UTI AMC, ABSL AMC, and Nippon Life AMC once clearances finalize.
* **Trajectory of MTM and other income:** Monitor whether non-operating income stabilizes for ICICI Prudential AMC and UTI AMC following Q2 investment-book marks.
* **Hybrid category redemption rates:** Watch if redemptions in Balanced Advantage and Multi-Asset categories accelerate or normalize across the sector, particularly at ICICI Prudential AMC.
* **Impact of ESOP amortization on cost structures:** Gauge whether ABSL AMC and HDFC AMC can offset projected quarterly ESOP charges through operating leverage.

*Based on: Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Ltd, HDFC Asset Management Company Ltd, ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Ltd, Nippon Life India Asset Management Ltd, UTI Asset Management Company Ltd*
