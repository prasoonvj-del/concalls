# ICRA Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Revenue growth in Risk & Analytics is muted and acquisitions are currently loss-making due to amortization/depreciation, though management expects improvements in H2.

### Key numbers
- **Ratings business 5-year CAGR (FY21-FY26):** 12.4% (grown from Rs 187 crore to Rs 336 crore)
- **Ratings operating margins:** Expanded from 20.8% in FY21 to 37.6% in FY26
- **Risk & Analytics revenue contribution:** Reached 50% of group revenues by end of FY26 (from ~1/3rd 5 years ago)
- **Risk & Analytics 5-year revenue growth:** Grew from Rs 114.9 crore in FY21 to Rs 313 crore in FY26
- **Group revenues:** More than doubled from Rs 301 crore in FY21 to Rs 646.9 crore at the end of FY26
- **Anchor client (Moody's) revenue share in Risk & Analytics:** ~49% (down from 80% 5 years ago)
- **Ratings business H1 FY27 YoY growth:** 15.2%
- **Bank credit growth (H1):** 21.4%
- **Bond issuance volume growth (H1):** 3.7%

### Highlights
- Management focused the call on five strategic priorities: (1) Ratings business growth and margin expansion, (2) Risk & Analytics business expansion and portfolio rebalancing, (3) Human resources, (4) Technology and digital transformation, and (5) Inorganic growth.
- Operational integration of recent acquisitions (D2K Technologies and Fintellix) was completed over a 9-month period ending September.
- Risk & Analytics is now reorganized into three segments: Knowledge Technology, Banking Technology (BankTech), and Capital Markets Technology (CapTech).
- D2K became a 100% subsidiary 4-5 months ago, allowing management greater latitude for investments and changes.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the second half of the year (H2) to show a better run rate for BankTech and Risk & Analytics businesses.
- Management expects bank credit growth in H2 to remain as buoyant as H1.
- Management provided no formal quantitative guidance on organic revenue growth rates, timeline for acquisition profitability, or specific capital allocation amounts for future M&A.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Bond yields have remained elevated, subduing bond issuance volumes (growing at 3.7% in H1), while bank credit growth has been robust at 21.4%.
- BankTech typically experiences seasonality with sales renewals concentrated in Q1, leading to a more muted sequential Q2 before picking up in H2.
- RBI's Basel III risk weight reset effective April 1, 2027, is viewed positively by management, who anticipate potential gains due to a market preference for quality ratings.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Risk & Analytics segment profit grew ~35% before depreciation and amortization.
- Current profit margins in Risk & Analytics are impacted by amortization and depreciation costs related to tech investments and acquisitions.
- Margin compression in Risk & Analytics was anticipated as the revenue mix shifts toward BankTech and CapTech, which carry lower relative margins compared to KnowTech.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Significant investments are ongoing in technology, software platforms, AI, and agentic capabilities.
- International expansion is targeted specifically in Europe, the US, and the Middle East through both organic and inorganic avenues.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- The company is sitting on a reasonable amount of cash; capital allocation options (including buybacks, special dividends, and gradual dividend increases) are under review by the Board.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Potential risk of subdued bond issuance activity if bond yields remain elevated.
- Lower comparative margins in BankTech and CapTech verticals creating a downward drag on overall Risk & Analytics segment profitability during the investment phase.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Kunal Thanvi (Banyan Tree Advisors) questioned the drop in non-rating profit mix and muted quarterly revenue run rate (~Rs 80 crore) in Risk & Analytics.** Management explained that margin compression is a conscious result of scaling lower-margin BankTech and CapTech verticals, and H2 typically delivers stronger execution.
- **Balaji Subramanian (IIFL) asked about the impact of elevated bond yields and pricing environment.** Management noted that bank credit growth remains buoyant, offsetting subdued bond issuances, and surveillance fee clauses are actively negotiated with clients.
- **Abhijeet Sakhare (Kotak Securities) asked about organic growth rates, cash deployment for M&A, and profitability timelines.** Management declined to give growth guidance, stated no specific M&A budget is earmarked, and reiterated that while acquisitions currently face amortization pressure, a clear path to profitability is tracked internally.
- **Anuj Sharma (SteadFort Investment Managers) inquired about client engagement sizes in Risk & Analytics and the upcoming Basel III impact.** Management stated ticket sizes vary widely from Rs 10-20 lakhs to multimillion deals depending on the segment (NBFCs vs. Tier-1 banks), and welcomed the Basel III regulations as a positive catalyst.
- **Lakshminarayanan KG (Tunga Investments) asked about the alignment of domestic and Moody's global revenue targets.** Management clarified that 80%-90% of Moody's engagement is recurring/FTE-based, providing high visibility, while budgeting operates on a 1-year visibility framework with ongoing long-dated dialogues.
- **Ravi Purohit (Securities Investment Management) questioned the multi-year losses and write-offs in D2K and Fintellix.** Management explained that D2K was previously only a 60% subsidiary with constrained change management, but having achieved 100% control recently, investments are fully unlocked and tracked for sustainable profitability.

### One-line takeaway
ICRA's H1 FY27 performance reflects steady growth in its core Ratings business alongside an intensive integration phase for its Risk & Analytics acquisitions, which are currently dragging near-term segment profitability due to amortization.
