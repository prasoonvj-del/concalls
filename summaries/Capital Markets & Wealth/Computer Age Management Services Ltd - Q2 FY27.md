# Computer Age Management Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-03 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Credible performance with 10% YoY revenue growth and 14.5% PAT growth despite muted AUM growth of 12%.

### Key numbers
- Operating revenue: Rs 415 crore (up 10% YoY)
- EBITDA: Rs 191 crore (up 13.5% YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 46% (up 144 bps YoY)
- PAT: Up 14.5% YoY
- CAMS MF AUM: Rs 58.5 lakh crore (up 12.5% YoY, 67% market share)
- Equity AUM growth: 15.5% YoY (industry: 15.2%)
- Gross SIP collections growth: 16.5% YoY (industry: 12.4%)
- Non-MF revenue contribution: Just short of 16% (up 150 bps YoY)
- Non-MF business growth: 21% YoY, 11% QoQ; profitability upwards of 18% (close to 19%)
- CAMSPay year-on-year revenue growth: In excess of 50% (13% QoQ)
- Alternatives AUM: Crossed Rs 3.3 lakh crore (up 22% YoY)
- Demat policies repository: Crossed 15 million (1.5 crore)
- Account aggregator growth: 19%
- Headcount: Reduced from 8,450 at start of year to around 8,200

### Highlights
- First time operating revenue crossed the Rs 400 crore mark.
- Absorbed large-scale employee increments in Q2 and incurred ~Rs 4 crore in P&L for the new platform (cannot be capitalized).
- Secured InCred AMC as a new MF RTA client, taking the client count to 32 out of 64 licensed AMCs.
- ASK Investments and Carnelian went live with maiden funds; Oaklane and Neo expected to go live in Q3.
- Bima Central launched a module for brokers.
- ConsenPro (DPDP implementation product) under implementation with 20 clients.
- FUNDSNet 2.0 go-live completed; legacy utility planned to be sunset by March/April.
- Interim dividend declared: Rs 3.25 per share.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects CAMSPay to grow by late 20s to 30% each year for the next 2 years (moderating from 50%).
- Non-MF revenue contribution target: 20% within the next couple of years.
- Yield drop expectation maintained: ~3% YoY and sub-1% QoQ.
- Normal year margin expansion expectation: 100 to 200 bps.
- Non-MF steady-state margin target: 25%+ in a 3-year period.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Equity net sales share for CAMS has been upwards of 70% over the last year.
- SIF (Specialist Investment Fund) launched by 12 AMCs; overall AUM at Rs 20,000 crore.
- GIFT City retail funds AUM crossed Rs 1,500 crore with 5 launches and 2-3 more planned for the current quarter.
- Directional possibility of the mutual fund industry moving heavily into digital formats.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Expense growth on a YoY basis was a little more than 7% (including an Rs 8.5 crore impact from annual appraisals).
- Depreciation increased due to hardware capitalization and ~Rs 1.5 crore amortization of new modules from the rearchitecture project.
- Re-architecture opex impact is expected to increase from ~Rs 5 crore per quarter to Rs 8 crore - Rs 9.5 crore over the next 2 quarters, largely offset by internal productivity savings.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Company required to maintain 4 data centers (3 normal, 1 air-gapped).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Ended the quarter with a cash position of ~Rs 900 crore (after paying ~Rs 160 crore of dividends during the quarter).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- None explicitly flagged as systemic risks, other than noting that high-teens AUM growth baselines are moderating.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Swarnabha Mukherjee (360 ONE Capital)** asked about CAMSPay sustainability and KRA bounce back. Management stated CAMSPay will target ~30% growth for the next 2 years, while KRA growth was driven by usage and new broker client additions (with only ~Rs 1.5 crore coming from Phase 1 inquiry pricing).
- **Supratim Datta (Jefferies)** asked about yield drivers and AI/headless architecture risks. Management clarified yield drops are due to mix (debt replaced by passives) and telescopic pricing, not large discounts. On AI, management noted building complex transaction systems via AI is still distant and IP rights issues limit raw LLM usage.
- **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal)** questioned the risk of AMCs in-sourcing via AI tools. Management responded that mass-scale outsourcing of core processing to sub-5 bps costs is globally unprecedented and unlikely in the MF industry.
- **Divij Punjabi (Banyan Tree Advisors)** asked about direct plan impact on yields. Management clarified pricing is constant regardless of the plan or origin.
- **Dipanjan Ghosh (Goldman Sachs)** asked about FY28 AMC renewals. Management stated their stance on holding pricing and resisting disproportionate discounts will prevail.

### One-line takeaway
CAMS delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with 10% YoY revenue and 14.5% PAT growth despite modest 12% AUM growth, aided by non-MF expansion and structural cost controls.
