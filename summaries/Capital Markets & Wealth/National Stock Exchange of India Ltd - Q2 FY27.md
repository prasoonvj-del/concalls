# National Stock Exchange of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-09 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - resilient financial performance with 9% YoY growth in total income, 23% YoY growth in PAT, and robust primary and secondary market activity despite volatility.

### Key numbers
- **Consolidated Total Income:** Rs 4,554 crore (up 9% YoY from Rs 4,160 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Revenue from Operations:** Rs 4,067 crore (up 11% YoY), constituting 89% of total income.
- **Profit Before Tax (PBT) from continuing operations:** Rs 3,425 crore (up 13% YoY; up 9% YoY normalised for one-offs).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Rs 2,578 crore (up 23% YoY).
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Rs 10.41 (non-annualised).
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 9,807 crore (up 9% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Revenue from Operations:** Rs 8,627 crore (up 12% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 5,698 crore (up 13% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 EPS:** Rs 23.02 (non-annualised).
- **Transaction Charges:** Rs 3,035 crore (up 9% YoY).
  - Options: Rs 2,216 crore (up 6% YoY).
  - Nifty index options (weekly expiries): Rs 1,699 crore (accounting for 38% of total income).
  - Cash Market: Rs 474 crore (up 30% YoY).
  - Equity Futures: Rs 328 crore (including Rs 32 crore from NSEIX).
  - Mutual Funds: Rs 13 crore (3.5x YoY growth).
- **Listing Services Revenue:** Rs 150 crore (up 70% YoY) with 62 mainboard listings. Total equity raised in Q2 FY27: Rs 2.4 lakh crore (up 111% YoY).
- **Data-Related Services Revenue:** Rs 531 crore (up 8% YoY), contributing ~13% of operating income.
- **Consolidated Expenditure:** Rs 1,161 crore in Q2 FY27 (down 51% YoY due to a one-time SEBI settlement fee of Rs 1,297 crore in Q2 FY26). Excluding settlement fees, expenses increased 10% YoY.
- **H1 FY27 Consolidated Expenditure:** Rs 2,333 crore (down 31% YoY). Expense composition in H1 FY27: Technology 32%, Employee 20%, Volume-linked 18%.
- **Employee Expenses (Q2 FY27):** Up 18% YoY. (H1 FY27: Rs 463 crore, 5.4% of operating revenue).
- **Technology Expenses (Q2 FY27):** Up 22% YoY. (H1 FY27: Rs 751 crore, 8.7% of operating revenue).
- **H1 FY27 Operating EBITDA Margin:** 77%.
- **H1 FY27 Normalised PBT Margin:** 76%.
- **H1 FY27 Net Profit Margin:** 58%.
- **Net Worth:** Rs 28,907 crore (as of September 30, 2026).
- **Total Treasury Investments:** Rs 52,613 crore (Corporate treasury: Rs 34,699 crore).
- **H1 FY27 Return on Equity (RoE):** 19% (non-annualised).
- **Financial Safeguards (as of September 30, 2026):** Core Settlement Guarantee Fund: Rs 13,665 crore; Investor Protection Fund: Rs 2,973 crore; Risk reserves: Rs 4,805 crore.
- **Subsidiary Performance (H1 FY27):**
  - NSE Clearing: Total income Rs 1,152 crore (up 16% YoY); PAT Rs 597 crore (up 11% YoY).
  - NSE Data & Analytics: Total income Rs 278 crore (up 45% YoY); PAT Rs 123 crore (up 61% YoY).
  - NSE Indices: Total income Rs 104 crore (up 10% YoY); PAT Rs 63 crore (up 9% YoY).
  - NSEIX (Gift City): Total income Rs 79 crore (up 17% YoY); PAT Rs 15 crore (up 4.3x YoY).
- **Market Share (Q2 FY27):** Equity Cash: ~92%; Equity Futures: 99.8%; Equity Options (premium turnover): 68.0% (~70% normalised for holidays); Debt RFQ: 96%; CBRICS: 80%; Tri-party repo: ~100%; Mutual Fund orders: ~22%.
- **Average Daily Turnover (ADT) & Volumes (Q2 FY27):**
  - Cash Market ADT: Rs 1.18 lakh crore (up 23.7% YoY; down 13% QoQ).
  - Equity Options ADT (premium): Up 3.3% YoY.
  - Equity Futures ADT: Rs 1.25 lakh crore (down 11.7% YoY).
  - Debt RFQ ADT: Rs 6,508 crore (up 10% YoY).
  - Tri-party repo ADT: Rs 6,825 crore (up 152.8% YoY, up 5.4% QoQ).
  - Commodity Futures ADT: Rs 137.6 crore (up ~38% QoQ).
  - Commodity Options Average Daily Premium Turnover: Rs 296.8 crore (up 116% QoQ, 8x Q2 FY26).
  - Crude Oil Options Daily Traded Quantity: ~27.4 million barrels in H1 FY27.

### Highlights
- Completed initial public offering (IPO), allotting shares to over 34 lakh retail investors.
- Commenced live trading in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs) on May 18, 2026; empanelled Augmont as a strategic partner and launched the NSE EGR Mobile App.
- Signed MoUs with Steel Users Federation of India and Bharat Metal Exchange for steel and non-ferrous metal derivatives.
- Received SEBI No-Objection Certificate for Corporate Bond Index futures, subject to RBI approval.
- Executed India's first tokenised bond issuances (by REC and L&T, raising Rs 1,000 crore) under SEBI's Regulatory Sandbox via the NSE Electronic Bidding Platform (EBP).
- Launched 'Market LENS', an in-house stock-screening and analytics platform covering 2,000+ stocks.
- Social Stock Exchange (SSE): 209 NPOs registered (98 active); 16 projects raised Rs 44.5+ crore. Facilitated first blended-financing project by NSDC worth Rs 11.26 crore.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects employee expense growth to moderate once wage code impacts are fully baked into the base period.
- Technology expenses are guided to run at around 8.7% of operating revenue, with a growth rate in the mid-teens.
- Capex for H2 FY27 is expected to be in line with H1 (around Rs 450 crore range, inclusive of potential episodic co-location expansion).

### Demand & industry commentary
- India's GDP growth projected at 7.1% for fiscal 2027 by RBI.
- Total market capitalization of Indian equities reached ~$4.9 trillion; investor accounts crossed 27 crore; unique registered investors reached 13.7 crore.
- Domestic institutional investors were net buyers for the 22nd consecutive quarter, investing Rs 1.7 lakh crore in Q2.
- Primary market fundraising across equity, debt, and business trusts reached Rs 7.4 lakh crore in Q2 FY27 (up 64% YoY). Total mainboard and SME listings: 87 companies raising Rs 66,447 crore.
- SEBI Chairperson reiterated no current plans to introduce further regulations regarding equity derivatives expiry date frequency (moving to fortnightly or monthly).

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating leverage remains strong with H1 FY27 operating EBITDA margin at 77%, normalised PBT margin at 76%, and net profit margin at 58%.
- Total expenses grew 10% YoY in Q2 FY27 when excluding the base-quarter SEBI settlement fee.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Co-location racks increased by nearly 800 full rack equivalents since January 2025, reaching 1,868 total racks across ~300 members.
- Co-location rack expansion capital expenditure in H1 FY27 was approximately Rs 50 crore.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth stood at Rs 28,907 crore as of September 30, 2026.
- Total treasury investments at Rs 52,613 crore (corporate treasury: Rs 34,699 crore).
- Zero external debt mentioned; exceptionally strong net cash position.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility in global flows and equity markets driven by geopolitical tensions, fluctuating crude oil prices, and tighter financial conditions.
- Potential impact of global geopolitical factors on cash and F&O trading activity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sameet Sinha (Macquarie) asked about the impact of SEBI's Closing Auction Session (CAS) changes and RBI bank guarantee regulations.** Management stated that CAS performance was as expected (impacting settlement prices more than cash volume), and upcoming December changes will aid clarity. On RBI bank guarantees, brokers coped by extending bank guarantees or raising capital via bonds, resulting in no striking direct impact.
2. **Swarnabha Mukherjee asked about co-location revenue evolution and rack addition plans.** Management noted 1,868 total racks (addition of ~800 racks since Jan 2025) across ~300 members, with surplus capacity available for rapid scaling and ongoing evaluation of infrastructure upgrades.
3. **Uday Polishetty asked about options market share (68%) and strategies to improve it.** Management clarified that normalised market share for Q2 FY27 was ~70% (adjusted for holiday expiry timing), and NSE maintains ~100% share in monthly index options and single stock options.
4. **Vinod Rajamani asked about the revenue share of weekly options expiry and potential regulatory risks.** Management stated revenue breakdown by expiry can be shared offline, and reiterated that SEBI's leadership has confirmed no plans to alter expiry frequencies (fortnightly/monthly).
5. **Abhijeet Sakhare asked about potential pricing levers for data revenue.** Management highlighted the launch of 'Market Lens' (currently free with 10,000 data sets) as a potential area to introduce nominal subscription fees in the future, alongside co-location message and rack charges.
6. **Madhukar Ladha asked for capex guidance.** Management guided that H2 capex will likely mirror H1 levels (around Rs 450 crore), driven primarily by technology and episodic co-location data centre expansion.

### One-line takeaway
NSE delivered a resilient Q2 FY27 with a 9% YoY rise in total income, 23% YoY growth in PAT, and strong operating margins (77% EBITDA), supported by robust primary market issuances, scaling alternative segments like commodities and EGRs, and disciplined technology investments.
