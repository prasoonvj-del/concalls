# Share India Securities Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-07-31 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management highlighted the strongest quarterly performance to date despite regulatory headwinds, driven by a diversified business model and growth in new verticals.

### Key numbers
*   **Standalone Revenue:** Rs 349 crore (+28% YoY).
*   **Standalone PAT:** Rs 90.85 crore (+32% YoY; +21% QoQ).
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 448 crore (+31% YoY).
*   **Consolidated PAT:** Rs 124.41 crore (+48% YoY; +114% QoQ).
*   **MTF Book:** Rs 470 crore.
*   **PMS AUM:** Rs 150 crore (launched in Q1).
*   **Institutional Active Clients:** 212 (vs 186 in Q4).
*   **Share India Cred (New Division):** Rs 74 crore sales, Rs 1.08 crore EBITDA, Rs 40 lakh PAT.
*   **GIFT City Operations:** Rs 2 crore profit.
*   **Net Worth (Consolidated):** ~Rs 2,760 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Diversification:** Revenue streams now include broking, market-making, merchant banking, wealth management, treasury, and technology-driven businesses.
*   **Retail Expansion:** Opened 7 new branches in Tier-3 cities (Banaras, Indore, Bhopal, Raipur, Agra, Nagpur, Hyderabad). Target is 30 branches over two years.
*   **New Products:** Launched PMS in Q1; AIF launch planned for Q3.
*   **Merchant Banking:** Completed one SME IPO (~Rs 200 crore) and one main board IPO (~Rs 167 crore, >12x subscribed).
*   **Technology:** Acquired Silverleaf to integrate HFT protocol and support global expansion.
*   **Funding:** Initiated commercial paper program (rated Crisil A1+) and progressing with NCD issuance to optimize funding costs and support MTF growth.

### Guidance & outlook
*   Management remains confident in delivering ~20% growth for the current financial year, subject to market conditions.
*   Targeting an MTF book of Rs 1,000 crore in the next two years.
*   Targeting PMS AUM of ~Rs 250 crore by the end of the financial year.
*   Expects to start wealth distribution operations by Q3.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   Industry facing regulatory changes (SEBI derivative segment measures) and tighter funding norms (RBI prop desk funding).
*   Management believes major regulatory challenges are over.
*   Tier-3 cities show specific demand patterns: Hyderabad requires MTF focus; Calcutta is derivative/algo-heavy; Indore focuses on IPOs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Branch economics: Target is for each branch to be at par within 8 months, requiring a Rs 15 crore MTF book. Branches failing to be profitable after 12 months will be closed.
*   Proprietary trading contributes ~58-60% of revenue and ~50-52% of profitability.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   Acquired a company (Enshrine) for Rs 45 crore primarily to secure an 18,000 sq. ft. office space in Mumbai (Interface 11) to consolidate operations.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Strong capital base with consolidated net worth of ~Rs 2,760 crore.
*   Diversifying funding sources via CPs and NCDs to counter RBI restrictions on prop desk funding.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   Geopolitical uncertainties and global financial market volatility.
*   Regulatory changes in the F&O segment and RBI guidelines for prop desk funding.
*   Potential for short-term impact from regulations, though management believes they are well-prepared.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Q: Why the Rs 45 crore acquisition cost for a company with small turnover?** A: Management clarified the acquisition was primarily for the underlying property (18,000 sq. ft. in Mumbai) valued at over Rs 42 crore, intended to consolidate offices and improve efficiency.
*   **Q: Why is the stock not performing despite low PE and high growth?** A: Management stated they cannot comment on stock price, attributing it to broader industry consolidation and regulatory headwinds, but emphasized the company's strong fundamentals.
*   **Q: What is the split between Prop and Client business?** A: Management clarified that while Prop contributes ~58-60% of revenue, the company has successfully reduced dependency from ~90% (8-9 years ago) by growing other verticals.
*   **Q: What is the payback period for new branches?** A: Management targets 8 months for a branch to reach break-even, requiring a Rs 15 crore MTF book.

### One-line takeaway
Share India delivered its strongest quarterly performance to date, leveraging a diversified business model and new revenue streams like MTF and merchant banking to offset regulatory headwinds.
