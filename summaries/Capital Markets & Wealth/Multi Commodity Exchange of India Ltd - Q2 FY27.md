# Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects strong operational and financial performance with high growth in revenue, EBITDA, and PAT, driven by higher average daily turnover.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: None given (maintained stance of not providing explicit guidance, but management expects the baseline growth to sustain).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to emphasize a higher baseline with every step of growth, supported by broad-based participation across segments.
- New topics or risks that weren't there last quarter, and old ones that disappeared: Cybersecurity risks and AI-related threats received heightened focus this quarter following recent global incidents. SGF contribution rules rationalized by SEBI (Z-score method brought down from 10 to 5) reduced statutory fund contributions this quarter. Data center migration plans are under review following stakeholder inputs.
- Last quarter's promises (targets, launches, capex, timelines) and whether they were delivered: Silver 100-gram futures contract and far-dated futures contracts (aluminum, copper, zinc up to 12 months; crude oil up to 18 months) were discussed as active/launched. Single and distinct delivery locations for lead and zinc were introduced effective November 2026.

### Key numbers
- Consolidated total income (H1): Rs 1,600 crore (up 99% YoY)
- Revenue from operations (H1): Rs 1,501 crore (up 101% YoY)
- EBITDA (H1): Rs 663 crore
- EBITDA margin (H1): 75%
- PAT (H1): Rs 868 crore (up 117% YoY)
- Average Daily Turnover (ADT): Rs 12.6 lakh crore (up 20% QoQ)
- Traded clients (H1): 19.8 lakh
- Electricity futures ADT (Q2): Rs 77 crore per day (H1 ADT: Rs 57 crore; ~75% market share)

### Highlights
- Acquired new leadership: Sanjay Rajpal as ED (Technology & Market Operations) and Manoj Jain as ED (Regulatory, Compliance, Risk Management & Grievance). Ramesh Varakhedkar joined as Chief Business Officer earlier in the month.
- Conducted the first 3-day Global Commodity Conclave in August to bridge financial and physical market participants.
- Introduced single and distinct delivery locations for lead (Chennai) and Thane (zinc), effective November 2026, to ease GST and operational challenges.
- Technology capacity nearly doubled, handling peak volumes seamlessly.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the baseline growth to sustain with every step of growth, though specific quarterly earnings or EBITDA margin targets were not provided.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Traded clients reached 19.8 lakh in H1, approaching the full-year FY25 figure of 20.9 lakh, highlighting wider adoption.
- Healthy growth seen across hedgers: bullion (importers, traders, jewellers), energy (SMEs, industrial organizations like glass fabricators), and base metals (SMEs, fabricators, traders, larger organizations).
- FPI participation currently in late single-digit numbers, with heightened interest in India-specific price discovery contracts.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Technology costs increased due to incremental expenses associated with enhanced capacity.
- Product license fees increased due to linkage with turnover and CME crude pricing.
- Statutory and regulatory fund contributions were lower in Q2 due to SEBI's August 2026 circular rationalizing SGF calculation using the Z-score method (reduced from 10 to 5). Current SGF buffers are sufficient for a couple of quarters.
- Effective tax rate for H1 is close to 25%, and future effective tax rate is expected around 25%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Technology infrastructure capacity scaled significantly, nearly doubling peak volume support capabilities on a per-day basis.
- Data center relocation plans are under review following stakeholder feedback.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Volatility management and market surveillance are critical during high-volatility periods.
- Cybersecurity has heightened focus due to recent global incidents and potential AI-related attacks.
- RBI bank guarantee rules effective July have established controls and squeezed some level of credit to the market, though contribution proportions remain steady.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Sameet Sinha (Macquarie) on FPI access and H2 outlook:** Management stated FPIs are playing an increasing role in cash-settled energy contracts and expect higher participation upon final circulars; baseline growth is expected to sustain in H2.
- **Madhukar Ladha (JPMorgan) on IT and product license costs:** Management explained IT costs are running expenses for enhanced capacity (not one-off), and product license costs are tied to CME crude turnover linkage.
- **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities) on data center shift and prop trading:** Management clarified data center relocation is independent of FPI participation and under review; prop trading contribution mix remains proportional post RBI rules.
- **Devesh Agarwal (IIFL Capital) on tax rate:** Management clarified H1 effective tax rate is ~25% following subsidiary provisions and annual tax re-evaluation based on SEBI circulars.
- **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark) on SGF costs:** Management stated SGF buffer is sufficient for a few quarters, after which contributions will restart in an optimized manner.

### One-line takeaway
MCX delivered stellar H1 FY27 financial performance with triple-digit profit growth, robust ADT expansion, and optimized regulatory fund requirements.
