# Gretex Corporate Services Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Management cited robust domestic participation, steady liquidity, and progress across advisory mandates, alternative investments, and broking operations despite broader market deceleration.

### Key numbers
- Indian primary market Q1 FY27: 53 issues mobilized ~Rs 6,895 crores (8 mainboard IPOs raised ~Rs 5,000 crores; 45 SME platform issues raised the balance).
- FY26 (prior year reference): 108 mainboard companies raised over Rs 1.76 lakh crores.
- Upcoming listings expected by management in the current quarter: Minimum of 3 to 4 IPO listings.

### Highlights
- Signed 3 new advisory mandates.
- Secured a listing mandate with a West Bengal-based ferroalloy company.
- Filed the DRHP for Sky Alloys and Power Limited on both NSE and BSE.
- Bahutex received registration for a Category-II AIF during the quarter, with Gretex as a partner in the fund.
- Subsidiary Gretex Share Broking Limited progressed towards its proposed listing, supported by strong contributions during the quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a minimum of 3 to 4 IPO listings to happen in the current quarter.
- Management remains confident in the long-term growth prospects of Indian capital markets and believes the operating environment remains favorable.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Characterized by heightened geopolitical uncertainties and continued volatility in global financial markets.
- Mainboard activity saw a significant and disciplined deceleration compared to record-breaking volumes in FY26.
- SME pipeline remained active with steady filings and small individual issue sizes, protecting the segment from institutional capital volatility.
- Domestic participation in equity markets remains robust, supported by a growing investor base, financialization of savings, and stable secondary market liquidity.
- Gradual shift towards professionally managed investment solutions with rising interest across mutual funds, PMS, and alternative investment platforms.
