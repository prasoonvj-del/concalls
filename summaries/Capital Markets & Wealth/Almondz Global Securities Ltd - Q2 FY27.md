# Almondz Global Securities Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-17 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong consolidated performance driven by infrastructure consultancy and green energy segments, alongside clear progress on business demerger.

### Key numbers
*   **Consolidated Revenue:** Rs 40.09 crore (+17.9% YoY).
*   **Consolidated Profit:** Rs 9.13 crore (2.4x YoY).
*   **Profit Margin:** 22.8% (+11.4 bps YoY).
*   **Infra Consultancy Revenue:** Rs 31.35 crore (+21.1% YoY).
*   **Infra Consultancy Profit:** Rs 2.90 crore (+19.8% YoY).
*   **Premier Green Revenue:** Rs 202.24 crore (+55.7% vs previous quarter).
*   **Premier Green Profit:** Rs 12.59 crore (3.4x).
*   **Financial Services Revenue:** Rs 8.05 crore (+2.7% YoY).
*   **Financial Services Profit:** Rs 2.51 crore (lower YoY).

### Highlights
*   **Business Segments:** Company operates in three verticals: infra engineering, clean/green energy, and financial services.
*   **Infra Consultancy:** Secured Rs 154 crore of new orders in H1. Total order book exceeds Rs 400 crore, with a 3-4 year execution horizon. Key wins include a Rs 33 crore program management mandate from Odisha Industrial Infrastructure Development Corporation and DPR for the 422 km NaMo Expressway.
*   **Green Energy:** Premier Green Innovations (JV) Himachal unit operated at full capacity (285 KLPD). The 200 KLPD Odisha plant is fully operational.
*   **Demerger:** The demerger of the infrastructure consultancy business is filed; management expects NSE approval shortly, followed by SEBI and NCLT processes. The proposed ratio is 666 shares of Almondz Global Infra Consultant Limited for every 10,000 shares of Almondz Global Securities.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Growth Targets:** Management expects all three business verticals to grow at least 25% YoY.
*   **Capacity:** Premier Green targets 80% capacity utilization in Q3 and 100% in Q4.
*   **Financial Services:** Management expects growth in fee-based numbers in Q4 and the next fiscal year due to a strong pipeline of mandates.
*   **Revenue Projection:** Management projects Premier Green revenue to reach Rs 1,400 crore next year (vs Rs 1,000 crore expected this year).

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Infra:** Management sees significant headroom in railways, urban development, and geolab, alongside a strong position in highways.
*   **Financial Services:** Focus is on small-cap companies (Rs 100-500 crore valuation) for advisory and private placement mandates.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   Operating leverage is beginning to reflect across business segments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Premier Green:** Total capacity stands at 485 KLPD.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   Investment book is split equally between debt and equity markets.
*   Debt portfolio includes high-rated government PSU bonds and private sector bonds.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Market Volatility:** Financial services profit was impacted by weak capital markets and reduced mark-to-market (MTM) capital gains on the investment portfolio.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **Demerger Timeline:** Management stated the process is in final stages with NSE; expects completion in 6-9 months.
*   **Financial Services Strategy:** Management clarified they are not a retail broker but focus on HNI/family offices (20-25 clients). They aim to grow this segment through investment banking-led advisory.
*   **Premier Green Growth:** Management noted this is the highest growth vertical, with the Odisha plant ramp-up expected to drive revenue to ~Rs 350 crore per quarter from next year.
*   **Investment Banking Pipeline:** Management confirmed 3 active mandates, including one recently completed for Green Joules and another in final diligence for Daalchini.

### One-line takeaway
Almondz Global is delivering strong growth across its three verticals, with management prioritizing the demerger of its infra and green energy businesses to unlock shareholder value.
