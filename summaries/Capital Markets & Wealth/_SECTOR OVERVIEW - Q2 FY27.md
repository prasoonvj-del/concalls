# Capital Markets & Wealth: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector delivered **resilient top-line growth** driven by domestic financialization — SIPs at record highs, primary markets booming, and wealth flows holding up — but **profitability diverged**. Wealth managers (360 ONE, Nuvama, Anand Rathi Wealth) compounded ARR revenues and improved cost ratios. Traditional AMCs (HDFC, ICICI Pru, SBI, UTI) grew AUM 10-15% YoY but faced **yield compression from passive shift** and **MTM volatility in treasury income**. Discount brokers (Angel One, 5paisa, Groww) navigated **F&O volume declines post-SEBI rules** by pushing MTF, credit and wealth. Market infrastructure (NSE, MCX, CAMS, KFin, NSDL) posted strong operating leverage. The **regulatory overhang** (F&O curbs, MTF rules, insurance distribution, IPO GMP curb) is now a **permanent feature**, forcing business-model pivots toward recurring, capital-light fee income.

## Where the industry is heading
**Business model so far** – Indian capital markets monetised transaction velocity: brokers earned on turnover, AMCs on active equity AUM, exchanges on trading fees. Over the last ~5 years the model has shifted to **recurring revenue** (trail commissions, SIPs, advisory fees, MTF interest, data/subscription income) and **alternatives/wealth** (AIF, PMS, SIF, private credit, real estate) where margins are higher and stickier. Digital onboarding and fintech partnerships replaced physical distribution. [Background]

Matters: advisory yields (79 bps at 360 ONE, 2.5% at Nuvama Asset Services) are 3-4× pure distribution yields. ## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
**Consensus**: Domestic participation is structural — industry SIPs ₹32,490 cr (Sept record), unique MF investors 6.3 cr (+11% YoY), demat accounts 24 cr+. Primary market robust: ₹90,000 cr raised in Q2 (NSE), 108 IPOs (Anand Rathi Broking). DIIs net buyers 22nd consecutive quarter (NSE). Wealth creation in UHNI growing 13-15% (360 ONE).

**Divergence**: 
- **Trading volumes**: Angel One, 5paisa, Geojit, SMC, Rikhav cite **F&O volume drop post-SEBI rules** (CAS, STT hike, expiry rationalisation). MCX sees commodity volumes growing. NSE cash ADT +23.7% YoY but -13% QoQ; equity futures ADT -11.7% YoY.
- **Flow mix**: HDFC AMC, ICICI Pru note **STP pauses by wealth managers** hurting systematic flows, while SIPs stable. Nippon Life sees **industry-wide investor growth slowdown** and HNI exiting large-cap. UTI AMC sees redemptions from bank/national distributor channels. 360 ONE, Nuvama, Anand Rathi Wealth report **strong advisory/ARR net flows** (₹12,482 cr, ₹8,300 cr, ₹4,186 cr respectively).
- **FPI vs Domestic**: NSE, SBI Funds, Nippon Life flag **FPI selling** (crude, US yields, strong $). Domestic absorption (DII, retail, SIP) fully offsetting so far.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
- **Employee/ESOP costs rising across board**: 360 ONE +19.9% YoY (incl ₹9 cr restructuring); Aditya Birla Sun Life ESOP ₹55-60 cr/yr existing + ₹14-15 cr/yr new; ICICI Pru ESOP guidance ₹64-68 cr FY27; Nippon Life ESOP ~₹58 cr FY27; UTI standalone employee cost guidance ~₹95 cr/qtr; Nuvama new ESAR ₹20 cr/qtr for 4 quarters; Angel One flat ~₹1,100 cr FY27 via attrition/backfill reduction.
- **Tech/AI spend capitalised or expensed**: 5paisa capitalised ₹6.2 cr; CAMS capitalised ₹23.9 cr H1, re-architecture opex ₹5-9.5 cr/qtr; KFin cloud/infra costs doubled/tripled; Groww AI compute costs up; NSDL tech spend "flattish" next year as catch-up ends.
- **Yield compression in AMCs**: Blended yields falling due to passive/ETF mix (HDFC active equity 61 bps, overall equity 58 bps; ICICI Pru equity 67 bps, passive 12 bps; Nippon blended 37 bps, equity ex-arb 52 bps; UTI weighted avg 31 bps, equity 73-74 bps). Telescopic pricing and scale benefits passed to distributors (UTI).
- **Wealth cost-to-income improving**: 360 ONE 51.5% (target 46-47% in 12-18 months); Nuvama Wealth cost-to-income -200 bps YoY; Anand Rathi Wealth targets PBT >40%, PAT >30%.
- **Broker margins**: Angel One standalone OPM 48.9% (guidance 45%); SMC EBITDA margin 19.1% flat YoY; Geojit EBITDA margins subdued by tech/sales spend; Rikhav margins squeezed by flat income + higher STT, labour law, hardware costs.
