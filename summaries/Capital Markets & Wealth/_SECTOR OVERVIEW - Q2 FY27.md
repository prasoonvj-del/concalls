# Capital Markets & Wealth: what managements are saying (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The Q2 FY27 earnings season for the Indian Capital Markets & Wealth sector reflected a resilient domestic retail ecosystem successfully absorbing global macroeconomic headwinds, FPI selling, and intermittent equity market volatility (with indices correcting 5–8%). While structural drivers—such as record primary market IPO activity, surging SIP inflows (crossing an all-time high of ~Rs 32,490 crore in September 2026), and a burgeoning retail demat base (crossing 24 crore)—drew universal applause, managements faced distinct headwinds. Regulatory actions (such as SEBI's derivative volume curbs, closing auction session adjustments, and draft insurance distribution rules) and margin pressures from tech investments dominated calls. Asset management companies (AMCs) and wealth managers largely delivered healthy core operating profits, though several reported bottom-line contractions due to mark-to-market (MTM) treasury drags or one-time regulatory settlement hits.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** All managements agree that structural domestic retail participation remains robust, underpinned by consistent SIP flows and an insatiable primary market (with Q2 primary issuances raising up to Rs 90,000–1,17,000 crore). 
*   **Divergence:** 
    *   *Broking & Active Trading:* Discount brokers and traditional brokers diverged on derivatives. While platforms like **Angel One** and **Anand Rathi** pointed to volume recoveries and resilience, **5paisa Capital** and **SMC Global** acknowledged a "soft quarter" with a sharp drop in derivatives activity following cash and margin rollouts.
    *   *Wealth & Asset Redemptions:* While wealth managers like **360 ONE WAM** and **Anand Rathi Wealth** reported record net flows and surging UHNI demand, traditional AMCs like **UTI AMC** and **ICICI Prudential AMC** noted localized redemption pressures and outflows in hybrid/flexi-cap categories driven by banks and national distributors.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Technology & Infrastructure Spends:** Heavy investments in tech infrastructure, AI stacks (e.g., *COMPASS* at 360 ONE, *Aria.AI* at KFin), and app overhauls are universal. **NSDL** noted a transition from "catch-up" to "build-up" in tech expenses, while **CAMS** and **KFin** absorbed annual salary appraisals alongside rising cloud/infrastructure costs.
*   **ESOP & Employee Costs:** Elevated employee expenses due to ESOP charges and senior leadership expansion were reported across AMCs (e.g., **Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC**, **HDFC AMC**, **ICICI Prudential AMC**, **Nippon Life India AMC**). 
*   **Yield Compression vs. Stability:** AMCs like **Nippon Life** experienced yield compression due to "telescopic pricing" and equity AUM mix changes, whereas **SBI Funds Management** and **CAMS** maintained stable yield baselines.

## Capex & capacity & expansion
*   **Alternative Investments & SIFs:** A massive collective push into Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs), AIFs (Category II and III), and private credit/real estate funds was highlighted by **HDFC AMC**, **ICICI Prudential AMC**, **SBI Funds Management**, **KFin Technologies**, and **UTI AMC**.
*   **Inorganic Moves & Ecosystem Expansion:** 
    *   **5paisa Capital** board-approved the acquisition of *Trendlyne*.
    *   **ICICI Prudential AMC** and **IIFL Capital Services** announced the acquisition of PMS businesses.
    *   International and regional footprints expanded, with **Anand Rathi Wealth** opening in London, **IGI** expanding into Botswana, Italy, and Saudi Arabia, and **Geojit** building out its DIFC presence.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Guidance Adjustments:** **IGI** upgraded its full-year guidance upward from 15% revenue/20% EBITDA growth to match robust H1 outperformance. **SMC Global** lowered its NBFC AUM growth target from Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,125–1,150 crore due to a faster rundown of legacy loan books. **360 ONE**, **Anand Rathi Wealth**, **Angel One**, **KFin**, and **CAMS** maintained their existing cost-to-income and revenue growth targets.
*   **Tone Shifts:** Sentiment shifted from the cautious optimism of Q1 to a more execution-focused stance as regulatory shifts (SEBI's F&O/closing auction rules, IRDAI drafts) and treasury MTM volatility took effect.
*   **Delivery on Promises:** **360 ONE** successfully onboarded early UBS collaboration flows ($300–325M to date); **Anand Rathi Wealth** delivered its 20th consecutive quarter of >20% PAT growth; **CAMS** expanded its non-MF business past 16% of revenue.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Regulatory Implementation:** Finalization and operational impact of SEBI's derivative volume rules, Closing Auction Sessions (CAS), and the proposed IRDAI insurance distribution commission caps (impacting **Prudent Corporate Advisory** and **SMC Global**).
*   **SIF and Alternate Scale-up:** Progress on Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) product launches and asset scaling across major AMCs (**HDFC AMC**, **ICICI Prudential AMC**, **SBI**, **UTI**, **Aditya Birla Sun Life**).
*   **Inorganic Integrations:** Progress on **5paisa's** acquisition of *Trendlyne*, **IIFL Capital's** closure of the Fairfax transaction, and **ICICI Prudential AMC's** PMS portfolio absorption.
*   **Technology Cost Normalization:** Whether tech and AI-related capital expenditures begin showing productivity-led expense normalization at **NSDL**, **KFin**, and **CAMS**.
