# Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-13 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Delivered strong performance with client assets crossing Rs 5.5 lakh crore, operating PAT growing 26% YoY to Rs 319 crore, and robust growth across wealth and institutional businesses.

### What changed vs last quarter
- Guidance: Maintained (guidance unchanged on ARR net flows at 22-25% of opening balance; asset services growth at ~20%).
- Tone and demand view: Shifted slightly to acknowledge normalization in asset services yields and fixed income after exceptional quarters, while seeing strong primary markets offset secondary market headwinds.
- New topics or risks: Pre-closing the venture debt fund resulting in a Rs 16 crore post-tax one-time charge; deferring H1 FY27 dividend to support loan book growth and debt-to-equity targets (2.5x); new ESAR opex charge of Rs 20 crore/quarter for the next 4 quarters.
- Last quarter's promises: RTA and trustee services are progressing to go live between end of November and March (as promised last quarter).

### Key numbers
- **Client Assets:** ~Rs 5.5 lakh crore (25% YoY growth); Wealth management assets at Rs 3.7 lakh crore; MPIS and ARR assets at ~Rs 1.1 lakh crore (27% growth).
- **Revenue:** Rs 937 crore (21% YoY growth); H1 revenue at ~Rs 1,850 crore.
- **PAT:** Operating PAT of Rs 319 crore (26% YoY growth).
- **ROE:** ~29%.
- **Net Flows:** Nuvama Wealth at Rs 3,300 crore; Nuvama Private at Rs 5,000 crore.
- **Loan Book:** Rs 6,000 crore in Wealth (growing 60% YoY); Private lending book up 50% on H1 basis.
- **Asset Services:** Revenue at Rs 245 crore (53% YoY growth); client assets at Rs 1.63 lakh crore; retention/yield at 2.5% (H1 at 2.7%).
- **Capital Markets:** Revenue at Rs 175 crore (9% YoY growth).
- **Costs:** Employee cost at Rs 382 crore (19% YoY growth); total opex at Rs 507 crore (16% YoY growth).
- **Debt-to-Equity:** 2.9x (comfort level targeted around 2.5x).

### Highlights
- Wealth ARR net flows from beyond Tier 1 reached ~40%.
- Wealth business (wealth + private) crossed Rs 500 crore quarterly revenue mark; Nuvama Wealth PBT crossed Rs 100 crore in Q2.
- Asset management: First commercial real estate fund is 50-55% committed; second fund (target Rs 5,000 crore) launching in ~2 weeks; private credit fund launching in 25-30 days (target Rs 1,500-2,500 crore); SIF launch expected in 25-30 days.
- Pre-closed the venture debt fund launched in FY23 due to underperforming equity warrants, resulting in a one-time post-tax charge of Rs 16 crore.
- Board decided not to declare an H1 FY27 dividend to preserve capital for loan book expansion and syndication/asset management investments.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects ARR net flows to track 22% to 25% of opening balance.
- Management expects asset services revenue growth aspiration of 20% to remain intact.
- Management expects asset services yields to remain in the 2.6% to 2.8% range for the full year.
- Management guided full-year BAU opex at Rs 520 crore to Rs 530 crore, closing total opex around Rs 550 crore including new HO expenses.
- Investment banking pipeline: ~40 transactions (13 in ECM, 10 in advisory) targeting early three-digit revenue.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Asset class performance is cyclical, prompting Nuvama's multi-asset class strategy which has driven strong net flows despite flat markets for nearly two years.
- Generative AI tools (e.g., client profiling, meeting prep tool, liquidity events module) have increased RM net new money and client assets productivity by 25-30% YoY in Wealth and 15-20% in Private.
- Domestic setup of PMS and AIFs is increasing, with Nuvama capturing ~25% of new registrations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee cost increased 19% YoY to Rs 382 crore due to additions (7% RM growth), annual increments, variable costs, and a Rs 6 crore pre-tax ESAR charge.
- ESAR charge will be ~Rs 20 crore per quarter for the next four quarters before tapering.
- New BKC head office lease will add ~Rs 25 crore in ROU accounting charges for FY27.
- Wealth cost-to-income ratio improved by ~200 bps YoY; Private margins improved by ~70 bps.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Added ~90 RMs in Nuvama Wealth over the last year; added 14-15 RMs in Nuvama Private (~10% of base).
- Rent-free period for new HO in BKC starts in November.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Debt-to-equity gearing currently stands at 2.9x; management is comfortable around 2.5x.
- Internal accruals are viewed as sufficient to fund future lending expansion without needing capital markets access.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Venture debt fund strategy failed to generate expected returns due to underperforming equity warrants, forcing pre-closure and a Rs 16 crore charge.
- Introduction of CAS in secondary markets impacted derivative volumes and broking income temporarily in August and September.
- Public market AIFs continue to experience drag/losses during the transition phase to SIFs.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Prayesh Jain (Motilal Oswal) on AMC costs and private credit:** Management explained that AMC costs are elevated due to infrastructure requirements for SIF/mutual fund setup and new private credit team hiring, with profitability reflection expected in 12-15 months. The private credit fund will target 15-18% returns (performing to special situations).
2. **Lalit Deo (Equirus) on the Rs 16 crore one-time AMC loss:** Management clarified it is due to unamortized upfront distribution costs from pre-winding the venture debt fund, not related to client returns.
3. **Sanketh Godha (Nuvama institutional/other) on domestic asset clearing competition:** Management noted competition is robust (18-19 players), but Nuvama captures ~25% of new PMS/AIF registrations annually.
4. **Dipanjan Ghosh (Citigroup) on monetization vs organic flows:** Management stated ~35-38% of net new money over the last 15 months came from monetization clients, with 60% organic.
5. **Prayesh Jain on debt-to-equity and lending growth limits:** Management stated internal accruals and keeping debt-to-equity around 2.5x still allows ~Rs 3,000-4,000 crore of book expansion, supporting 40% growth without equity dilution.

### One-line takeaway
Nuvama reported a resilient Q2 FY27 with 26% operating PAT growth to Rs 319 crore and strong asset mobilization, offsetting a one-time venture debt fund closure charge by tightening capital deployment and scaling core wealth and institutional engines.
