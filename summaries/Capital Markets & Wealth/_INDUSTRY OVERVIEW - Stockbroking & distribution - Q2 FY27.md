# Sector Overview: Capital Markets & Wealth (Q2 FY27)

## Big picture
The sector is navigating a transition phase characterized by regulatory tightening and market volatility, yet underlying structural growth remains robust. While Q2 FY27 saw "soft" patches in trading volumes and profitability for some, the broader narrative is one of resilience. Companies are aggressively pivoting toward "capital-light" fee-based models (wealth, insurance, merchant banking) and internalizing technology stacks to drive long-term engagement. The industry is effectively absorbing regulatory shifts—such as SEBI’s derivative circulars and potential insurance commission caps—by focusing on productivity and high-quality, granular growth rather than raw client acquisition numbers.

## Demand outlook - consensus and divergence
*   **Consensus:** Managements across the board agree that the structural story of Indian capital markets remains intact. Despite short-term turbulence (geopolitical tensions, inflation, and market corrections), retail participation remains strong, evidenced by record SIP inflows and demat account additions.
*   **Divergence:**
    *   **Bullish/Resilient:** **Angel One** and **Prudent Corporate** reported strong performance, with Angel One noting a volume rebound in September and Prudent achieving record equity net sales despite market corrections. **Anand Rathi** and **Pune E-Stock** also maintained positive outlooks, citing strong growth in their respective segments.
    *   **Cautious/Mixed:** **5paisa Capital** and **Geojit Financial Services** were more circumspect, citing "soft" quarters with slower trading activity. **SMC Global** acknowledged a sequential decline in profitability due to regulatory headwinds impacting arbitrage strategies.

## Costs, raw materials & margins
*   **Tech & AI Spend:** A universal theme is the deliberate, heavy investment in technology. **5paisa** is sacrificing short-term profitability to fund a major product overhaul. **Billionbrains (Groww)** is internalizing its tech stack to reduce per-token AI costs. **Geojit** and **IIFL Capital** also highlighted ongoing tech transformation costs.
*   **Employee Costs:** There is a clear shift toward productivity over headcount expansion. **Angel One** reduced headcount/backfills to focus on talent density. **Geojit** is pivoting to increase the productivity of existing staff rather than aggressive hiring. Conversely, **Anand Rathi** saw a one-time spike in employee expenses due to moving outsourced staff to the payroll.
*   **Margin Pressures:** Regulatory changes are compressing margins in specific areas (e.g., insurance commissions, derivative volumes). **SMC Global** and **Prudent** are actively managing the potential impact of IRDAI consultation papers on commission caps.

## Capex & capacity
*   **MTF Expansion:** The Margin Trading Facility (MTF) remains a key growth engine. **Pune E-Stock** crossed Rs 100 crore in its MTF book, while **Anand Rathi** and **SMC Global** are actively scaling their books with strict risk controls.
*   **Diversification:** Companies are aggressively expanding into non-broking verticals to reduce cyclicality.
    *   **Merchant Banking:** **Pune E-Stock** and **IIFL Capital** are scaling this division.
    *   **Insurance/Wealth:** **Prudent** and **Pune E-Stock** are deepening their insurance distribution, while **Angel One** and **IIFL Capital** are scaling wealth management and credit distribution.
    *   **Global Access:** **Angel One** and **Pune E-Stock** are expanding into international markets via GIFT City/US stocks.

## What changed since last quarter
*   **Regulatory Tone:** The industry has moved from "anticipating" regulatory impact to "adjusting" to it. **Billionbrains** and **SMC Global** noted that while derivative circulars caused short-term volume disruption, they expect normalization.
*   **Guidance Shifts:**
    *   **SMC Global** cut its NBFC AUM target (from Rs 1,300 crore to Rs 1,125–1,150 crore) due to a faster-than-planned rundown of legacy loan products.
    *   **Anand Rathi** maintained its MTF and distribution targets, showing consistency.
    *   **Angel One** maintained its 45% OPM guidance, successfully delivering on margin efficiency.
*   **Leadership/Structure:** **IIFL Capital** announced a major leadership transition (Vasudev Jagannath to become MD in 2027) and the imminent closure of the Fairfax transaction.

## What to watch next quarter
*   **Regulatory Finality:** Clarity on the IRDAI insurance commission consultation paper and the impact of SEBI’s derivative circulars on volume normalization.
*   **Fairfax Deal:** The final closure of the IIFL Capital-Fairfax transaction in November 2026.
*   **Tech ROI:** Whether the heavy tech/AI investments by 5paisa and Billionbrains translate into measurable engagement or retention gains.
*   **NBFC Asset Quality:** How the shift toward granular, secured loan books (SMC Global, Anand Rathi) impacts NIMs and credit costs in a high-interest-rate environment.
