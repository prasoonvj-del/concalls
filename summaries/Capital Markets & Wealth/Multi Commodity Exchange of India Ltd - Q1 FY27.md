# Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-12 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Reflects healthy YoY growth across all key financial parameters and consolidation of performance following an exceptionally strong Q4 FY26.

### Key numbers
- Total income: INR752 crores (up 85% YoY)
- Revenue from operations: INR702 crores (up 88% YoY)
- EBITDA: INR544 crores (more than doubled YoY)
- EBITDA margin: 72%
- PAT: INR413 crores
- Average Daily Turnover (ADT): INR10.5 lakh crores
- Futures ADT growth: 47% YoY
- Notional Options ADT growth: 266%
- Traded client base: 13.72 lakh clients (doubled YoY)
- Float income: INR30 crores for the quarter
- Electricity futures Q1 ADT: INR37 crores (approx 55% market share)

### Highlights
- Successful launch of the Silver 100 Grams Futures contract.
- Over 50 AMCs are now using MCX bullion prices as a reference for AUM calculations.
- Expanded Good Delivery Norms to include silver, empanelling the country's first domestic silver refiner and three additional domestic gold refiners.
- Incorporated the MCX Coal Exchange of India, pending next steps upon regulatory approval.
- Ranked world's largest Commodity Options Exchange and 4th largest Commodity Derivatives Exchange by number of contracts traded (as per FY2025 statistics).
- Traded FPI count reached 220, contributing about 2.5% at a higher base.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the growth momentum at the fundamental level to continue to be good for FY27.
- Management expects higher traded UCC numbers this year compared to the full year FY25 closing of 20 lakh.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Traded client base doubled to 13.72 lakh clients, highlighting increasing acceptance of commodity derivatives for hedging and investments.
- Normalization of the premium ratio observed due to globally lower volatility trends in gold and silver compared to previous quarters.
- Power contract (electricity futures) open interest and participation showing positive lead indicators with participation from power players and DISCOMs.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Employee cost increase includes a one-time impact of around 8% to 9% due to additions, increments, and higher variable pay in a subsidiary.
- SGF contribution varies based on monthly SEBI-prescribed methodology calculations and buffer assessments.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Technology capacity has successfully scaled to handle over 3 billion transactions a day, with capacity to handle more than double that.
- Ongoing focus on driving technology resiliency and scale efficiently.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Float income stood at INR30 crores for the quarter.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- RBI bank guarantee regulations are now in force; management notes it will have an optimal cost of funds implication for some member flows, though no significant detrimental impact is expected at this stage.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Amit Chandra (HDFC Securities) on RBI bank guarantee impact:** Management stated that while it is a Q2 action and will impact the cost of funds for some member flows, they are not expecting a significant detrimental impact at this stage.
- **Shrenik Mehta (Indo Alps Wealth) on options premium compression:** Management and team explained that the decline in premium ratio is a market factor driven predominantly by normalized and lower implied volatility in gold and silver, rather than contract mix or participation shifts.
- **Supratim (Jefferies) on FPI participation in non-cash contracts:** Management noted they are awaiting progress on this front as details are worked through by SEBI and the commodity derivative action committee.
- **Niranjan Kumar (Avendus Spark) on employee cost growth:** Management clarified that the increase is a mix of headcount additions, increments, and a one-time 8% to 9% impact from variable pay/bonuses.
- **Sanketh Godha (Avendus Spark) on AMC price usage and float income:** Management stated that the immediate focus is on service orientation without complicating it with immediate revenue streams, though implicit and explicit streams will follow. Float income was stated as INR30 crores for the quarter.

### One-line takeaway
MCX delivered strong Q1 FY27 financial growth driven by robust ADT and options expansion, while actively managing technology scale and emerging regulatory shifts.
