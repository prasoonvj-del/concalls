# Rikhav Securities Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-12 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** H1 FY27
**Tone:** Cautious - Management cited a difficult operating environment with volatile equity markets, foreign portfolio investor selling, and higher derivatives taxes moderating volumes, offset by a focus on disciplined growth and recurring income streams.

### Key numbers
- Total income: Rs 869.07 crore (H1 FY27)
- EBITDA: Rs 24.47 crore (H1 FY27)
- Profit after tax (PAT): Rs 13.60 crore (H1 FY27)
- Earnings per share (EPS): Rs 3.55 (H1 FY27)
- Trading volume handled: Over Rs 73,000 crore across equity, F&O, and commodity segments (H1 FY27)
- Advisory vertical contribution: Rs 3.15 crore (H1 FY27)
- Institutional brokerage earned in H1: Rs 50 lakhs
- Expected annual brokerage from LIC empanelment: Rs 1.5 crore per year
- Target institutional brokerage revenue: Rs 5 crore per year within two years
- Margin Trading Facility (MTF) book size: Around Rs 15 crore
- Expected MTF book size jump next year: Minimum 50% (Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore)
- App metrics: Daily active users around 10,000; total logins created around 18,000

### Highlights
- Handled trading volumes exceeding Rs 73,000 crore across equity, F&O, and commodity segments in H1 FY27.
- Commenced ETF market making and plans to add more AMC companies.
- Empanelled with institutional clients including LIC and GIC, with an expectation of ~Rs 1.5 crore annual brokerage from LIC alone; also targeted SBI Group and 12 to 15 small institutions annually.
- Appointed Mr. Santosh as institutional head, bringing 20 years of experience in institutional dealings.
- Reduced equity market exposure and transferred the majority of proprietary funds towards algo-trading.
- Expanding geographic presence beyond Maharashtra and Gujarat into UP, Punjab, and Rajasthan through new branches and franchise partners in a measured manner.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects a 20% to 25% increase every year from the existing business.
- Institutional business revenue is expected to start from next year at around Rs 3 crore to Rs 4 crore, growing at 20% to 25% annually.
- Management targets Rs 10 crore in brokerage from institutional and other businesses within two years.
- MTF book size targeted at Rs 25 crore to Rs 30 crore next year (subject to market conditions).
- Capital augmentation is planned for March or April, depending on market improvement.
- Digital marketing for customer acquisition is scheduled to start after March '27.

### Demand & industry commentary
- H1 FY27 was described as a difficult period for Indian equity markets and the broking industry due to continuing West Asian conflict, sustained FPI selling, and revised STT on derivatives moderating F&O volumes.
- Retail participation in the secondary market remained cautious through most of the period.
- Management remains confident about the long-term opportunity in India's capital market driven by the rising financialisation of household savings, growing retail and domestic institutional participation, and digital adoption.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Operating margins decreased because income remained flat while expenses increased.
- Higher financial costs incurred due to changing SEBI laws.
- Investments made in new computers and IT software systems.
- Trading expenses increased due to higher STT rates on futures and options.
- Employee expenses increased due to new labour laws and standard salary increments.
- Hardware costs increased by 30% to 40% due to the AI bubble.
- Basic employee remuneration costs expected to increase by 10% to 15%.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Major technology capex directed toward acquiring servers for institutional and ETF market-making businesses to support colocation (colo-support) at NSE and BSE.
- Expenditure going into networking lines (P2P connections from offices to NSE/BSE trade locations).
- In-house development team continuing to build algo-trading software.
- Expanding distribution to include mutual funds, PMS, and AIF distribution.
- Selective expansion of MTF and franchise network due to regulatory compliance risks.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Maintained adequate liquidity currently, with capital increases planned if market demand rises.
- MTF funding and credit risks managed via selective deployment and script-exposure risk analysis.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Market volatility and geopolitical tensions (West Asian conflict).
- Regulatory changes, including revised STT on derivatives and new labour laws increasing operational overheads.
- Franchisee compliance risks, leading the company to expand selectively rather than aggressively.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Akash Mehta (Individual Investor)** asked about the strategy to expand the institutional client base over 2-3 years. *Management detailed empanelments with LIC and GIC, targeting 12-15 small institutions annually, aiming for Rs 5 crore in brokerage revenue within two years, and highlighted the hiring of institutional head Mr. Santosh.*
- **Gaurav (Investor)** asked about capital raising plans and key drivers of incremental profitability. *Management stated capital augmentation is planned for March/April if markets improve, and cited institutional business, algo-trading, and ETF market-making as profit drivers.*
- **Priya Jain (Investor)** inquired about the breakdown of operating expenses impacting margins. *Management explained that flat income combined with higher financial costs from SEBI norms, IT investments, higher STT on F&O, and increased employee costs under new labour laws squeezed margins.*
- **Rajesh Tiwari (Investor)** asked about capital expenditure and technology investments over the next 2-3 years. *Management pointed to investments in servers for co-location at NSE/BSE, P2P networking lines, and hardware/software for algo-trading.*
- **Udit Malhotra (Investor)** asked about the physical expansion strategy and proportion of recurring revenue. *Management stated they have started in UP, Punjab, and Rajasthan, are expanding selectively on franchises, and noted that institutional brokerage contributed ~Rs 50 lakhs in H1.*

### One-line takeaway
Rikhav Securities reported subdued H1 FY27 profitability due to regulatory and cost headwinds, but is pivoting toward institutional broking, algo-trading, and recurring income streams to drive medium-term growth.
