# Gretex Corporate Services Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 & H1 FY27
**Tone:** Mixed - Strong H1 performance driven by other income and prior execution, but Q2 saw revenue decline and significant margin compression due to lower deal flow.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Missed the Q2 listing target; management promised "a minimum of 3 to 4 IPO listings" in Q1 FY27 call, but only 1 listing (Vama Wovenfab) was completed in Q2.
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted from general optimism to acknowledging "moderation" in SME IPO activity and increased investor selectivity regarding fundamentals and valuations.
- **New topics:** Management introduced a specific strategy to transition from transaction-led revenue to recurring revenue via post-listing advisory retainers (targeting Rs 10 lakhs to Rs 50 lakhs, or Rs 1 crore per client).
- **Last quarter's promises:** Failed to deliver the promised 3-4 listings in Q2; only 1 was executed.

### Key numbers
- **Q2 Revenue:** Rs 75.60 crore (down 5% YoY; up 101.6% QoQ).
- **Q2 EBITDA:** Rs 9.6 crore (vs Rs 31.7 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 EBITDA Margin:** 12.70% (vs 39.82% in Q2 FY26).
- **Q2 PAT:** Rs 7.1 crore (vs Rs 12.8 crore in Q2 FY26).
- **H1 Revenue:** Rs 113.1 crore (up 11.4% YoY).
- **H1 PAT:** Rs 19.9 crore (up 43.2% YoY).
- **H1 PAT Margin:** 17.60% (vs 13.69% in H1 FY26).
- **Celestial Chakra Momentum Fund:** First close at Rs 25 crore.

### Highlights
- Executed 1 listing (Vama Wovenfab) in Q2.
- Progressed 4 IPO mandates to DRHP stage: Sky Alloys and Power Limited, Tenty Limited, Abacus Lifestyle Brands, and Oremet Alloys.
- Secured an underwriting mandate from Army Infotech Limited.
- Expanded broking operations with a new branch in Ahmedabad.
- H1 PAT growth was supported by other income of Rs 1.5 crore (vs negative Rs 11.5 crore in H1 FY26).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects the 4 current IPO approvals in hand to list in either the current or next quarter.
- Management aims to build a broader merchant banking franchise by extending issuer relationships post-listing through advisory retainers and alternative investment participation.

### Demand & industry commentary
- Mainboard IPOs raised ~Rs 84,400 crore in H1 FY27 (up 30% YoY).
- SME IPO segment activity moderated: 137 companies listed in H1 FY27 (vs 156 in H1 FY26), with funds raised declining ~11% to Rs 6,012 crore.
- Market environment is shifting toward "normalization" and "selectivity," with investors prioritizing business fundamentals and issue valuations.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Management attributed the decline in Q2 margins directly to lower turnover and fewer listings (only 1 listing executed in Q2).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Evolving geopolitical uncertainties and global economic conditions.
- Moderation in SME IPO fundraising activity.

### Notable analyst Q&A
- **Q: What is the reason for the significant decline in margins?**
  *Management:* Attributed the decline to lower turnover and fewer listings (only one listing done in Q2).
- **Q: Can we have any idea on the kind of transactions in the pipeline?**
  *Management:* Stated there are 4 approvals in hand and expects these to list in the current or next quarter.

### One-line takeaway
Gretex reported a mixed quarter with Q2 revenue and margin contraction due to lower deal execution, though management is pivoting toward a recurring revenue model via advisory retainers to stabilize future earnings.
