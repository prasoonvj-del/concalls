# International Gemological Institute Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-10-28 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Best quarter in company's history with record volumes, revenue, EBITDA, and profit.

### Key numbers
- **Volumes:** 4.35 million reports issued in Q2 (up 26% YoY); India volumes up 19% YoY. H1 volume growth at 22%.
- **Revenue from Operations:** INR 3,980 million for Q2 (up 31% YoY); H1 revenue at INR 7,688 million (up 27% YoY).
- **Certification Revenue:** INR 3,875 million for Q2 (up 32% YoY). Base business certification revenue grew 29% (excluding AGL).
- **EBITDA:** INR 2,445 million for Q2 (up 39% YoY); H1 EBITDA at INR 4,683 million (up 34% YoY).
- **EBITDA Margin:** 61.4% for Q2 (up 340 bps YoY); H1 EBITDA margin at 60.9% (up 310 bps YoY).
- **PAT:** INR 1,858 million for Q2 (up 43% YoY); H1 PAT at INR 3,516 million (up 37% YoY).
- **PAT Margin:** 46.7% for Q2 (up 400 bps YoY); H1 PAT margin at 45.7% (up 330 bps YoY).
- **Average Realization (ARP):** INR 891 for Q2 (up 4% YoY); H1 ASP at INR 945 (up 4% YoY).
- **Segment Growth (Q2 YoY):** LGD loose certification up 37%; LGD jewelry certification up 47%; Natural loose certification up 8%; Gemstones and other certification up 331%. H1 LGD loose up 31%, LGD jewelry up 46%, gemstones up 261%.
- **India Standalone Numbers:** Q2 certification revenue INR 3,040 million (up 28% YoY); Total revenue INR 3,056 million (up 27% YoY); ARP INR 827 (up 7% YoY); EBITDA INR 2,123 million (up 21% YoY); EBITDA margin 69.5% (down 340 bps YoY); PAT INR 1,697 million (up 22% YoY); PAT margin 55.5% (down 340 bps YoY). H1 India revenue INR 5,918 million (up 25% YoY); EBITDA INR 4,093 million (up 18% YoY); PAT INR 3,243 million (up 17% YoY).
- **Cash and Bank Balance:** INR 10,749 million as of quarter close.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** INR 4,157 million for H1 (89% of EBITDA, 118% of PAT), aided by a working capital release of INR 662 million as receivables dropped from a peak of INR 2,885 million to INR 2,402 million.

### Highlights
- Best quarter in IGI's history across volumes, revenue, EBITDA, and profits.
- Subsidiaries revenue grew 49% YoY in Q2 (31% on like-to-like basis excluding AGL; AGL added 3% incremental growth).
- Marketing B2B2C initiatives (IPL partnership, regional content like Puja campaign and Weaveone) yielded a 66% increase in brand research volume since March 2026, reaching 20 million unique audiences per quarter (150 million+ views year-to-date).
- Deployed AI-based work allocations and scheduling across laboratories, improving grader productivity while maintaining constant standards.
- Board declared and paid a first interim dividend of INR 2.55 per share in September 2026.
- Established a company in Botswana in September 2026; operations started in Italy; Saudi Arabia operations being prepared.

### Guidance & outlook
- Management expects to keep outperforming for the full year, delivering growth similar to H1 performance, thereby beating the earlier guidance of 15% revenue growth and 20% EBITDA growth given in May.

### Demand & industry commentary
- India festive stocking shifted slightly into October due to Diwali falling on November 8 this year (vs October 20 last year), but Q2 demand was healthy and initial October festive trends show good traction.
- High and volatile gold prices caused natural diamond jewelry buyers to take longer to decide, resulting in only 3% growth for natural jewelry certification; lab-grown shows no such hesitation.
- Lab-grown diamond (LGD) wholesale prices have stable ranges between 118 and 120 over the last couple of years.
- In the US, over 50% of all bridals are now lab-grown, with other markets (Europe, Latin America, Australia) following.
- Union Home Minister Shri Amit Shah urged keeping the entire LGD chain in Indian hands during a September meeting in Mumbai.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- India standalone EBITDA and PAT margins contracted by 340 bps YoY due to investments in employee leadership, additional marketing, and commission expenses.
- Consolidated EBITDA margins expanded by 340 bps YoY to 61.4% due to operating leverage.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Large Indian LGD growers are adding reactors and doubling capacity in 3 years; supply is expanding.
- Capital allocation priorities: 1. Investing for growth/capacities/geographies (Italy, Saudi Arabia, Botswana), 2. M&A opportunities, 3. Returning surplus cash via dividends.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Cash and bank balance stands at INR 10,749 million.
- Receivables reduced from a peak of INR 2,885 million to INR 2,402 million, releasing INR 662 million in working capital during H1.

### Risks & concerns flagged
- High and volatile gold prices causing decision-making delays for natural diamond jewelry buyers.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Harit Kapoor (Investec) on LGD growth sustainability vs. preordering:** Management stated that LGD momentum is driven by fundamental capacity additions at the grower level (Q2 LGD revenue at INR 2,111 million vs Q1 at INR 2,095 million) and do not expect a slowdown.
2. **Riddhesh Gandhi (Services Private Limited) on LGD certification necessity as prices drop:** Management noted certificate cost is only 2-3% of retail price, wholesale prices are stable, and retail demand drives certification. When asked about moats/risks of new players, management cited a 50-year track record, trust, and first-mover advantage.
3. **Rajesh Jain (RK Capital) on GIA shifting away from 4Cs vs. IGI's stance:** Management affirmed that the 4Cs establish commercial trade value and consumer understanding, and IGI remains committed to 4Cs and origin reporting, seeing strong traction without transitioning to simplified classification.
4. **Jinal Sheth (Independent) on grading consistency compared to GIA/HRD and certificate printing rights:** Management denied giving certificate printing rights to Surat manufacturers and defended their ISO and RJC accreditations, multi-layered human checks, and AI/ML data accuracy, stating there is no grading gap.
5. **Aliasgar Shakir (Investec) on guidance upgrade:** Management confirmed they are revising guidance upward from the initial 15% revenue / 20% EBITDA targets to match H1 outperformance rates due to strong industry tailwinds.

### One-line takeaway
IGI reported the best quarter in its history with a 31% revenue jump and 39% EBITDA growth, prompting management to upgrade full-year expectations above initial guidance.
