# Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd - Q1 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-08-05 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q1 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Record quarterly profits and strong performance across all business segments, with client assets crossing INR5 lakh crore.

### Key numbers
*   **Client Assets:** Crossed INR5 lakh crore (stood at INR5,36,000 crore).
*   **Quarterly Revenue:** Crossed INR900 crore (INR909 crore, +18% YoY).
*   **Quarterly PAT:** Crossed INR300 crore (INR306 crore, +16% YoY).
*   **ROE:** Near 30%.
*   **Wealth Management Revenue:** Grew 19% YoY (contributes ~50% of total revenue).
*   **Asset Services Revenue:** INR260 crore (+34% YoY).
*   **Capital Markets Revenue:** INR180 crore (flattish YoY).
*   **MPIS Assets:** INR42,500 crore (+32% YoY).
*   **ARR Assets:** INR58,000 crore (+20% YoY).
*   **Lending Book:** Crossed INR5,000 crore.

### Highlights
*   **Business Milestones:** Wealth and Private combined assets (MPIS + ARR) crossed INR1 lakh crore.
*   **Nuvama Wealth:** Net new money in MPIS crossed INR3,000 crore. Tier 2 and beyond regions now contribute >35% to MPIS.
*   **Nuvama Private:** Added 6-8 RMs in Q1; launched "Pinnacle" offering for super ultra HNIs.
*   **Offshore:** Dubai operations have broken even; Singapore expected to break even by year-end. Offshore revenue contribution expected to remain 5-7% this year.
*   **Asset Management:** Closed "PRIME" commercial real estate fund at INR4,000 crore (target was INR3,000 crore).
*   **Asset Services:** Strong growth; planning to launch RTA and trustee services in ~1 quarter.

### Guidance & outlook
*   **Asset Management:** Management expects cumulative loss of INR35-40 crore for the full year; cost run rate expected to peak at INR35-36 crore/quarter, with revenue growth expected from Q4 onwards.
*   **Asset Services:** Management expects moderation in growth compared to Q1, but full-year revenue growth is projected at >20-25%.
*   **Capital Markets:** Management expects ECM activity to pick up in the remaining 9 months.
*   **Cost-to-Income:** Management targets ~55% for the full year.
*   **RM Addition:** Aiming for 15-16% annual growth in RM headcount.

### Demand & industry commentary
*   **Industry Trends:** Management observes a convergence where wealth managers are building capital market capabilities and vice versa.
*   **Technology:** Investing heavily in AI (e.g., "Nuggets" chatbot, "RM Buddy"). Management believes AI will drive efficiency across the value chain.
*   **Offshore:** Management views offshore presence as a necessity for globally integrated clients, with potential for 15-20% allocation to offshore assets in client portfolios over 3-4 years.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
*   **Employee Cost:** Increased 17% YoY due to seniorization of the team and variable incentives.
*   **Opex:** Increased 24% YoY; management expects full-year opex growth of 15-16%.
*   **Yields:** Private ARR yield is currently 82 bps; management maintains a range of 80-90 bps.

### Capex, capacity & expansion
*   **Physical Presence:** Present in 65-70 cities directly; 400-450 pin codes via external wealth managers.
*   **Office Lease:** Management noted potential upfront charges under Ind AS 116 due to upcoming lease renewals.

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
*   **Rating:** CRISIL upgraded rating from AA- to AA stable.
*   **ESARs:** Management clarified that the proposed ESAR (Stock Appreciation Rights) plan will result in lower dilution for shareholders compared to traditional ESOPs.

### Risks & concerns flagged
*   **Capital Markets:** Potential impact of new regulations on derivatives (STT, intraday financing, bank guarantee collateral) is being monitored.
*   **Asset Management:** New product launches have a 1-2 year drag on profitability.
*   **One-offs:** Management noted ~INR15-20 crore of fixed income revenue in Q1 may not be repeatable in coming quarters.

### Notable analyst Q&A
*   **On Advisory vs. Transactional:** Management explained that while they are moving toward advisory, they are weeding out low-yield legacy corporate treasury mandates, not UHNI advisory.
*   **On RTA/Trustee Business:** Management clarified they are not currently targeting the mutual fund segment for RTA/Trustee services, as banks have a competitive advantage there.
*   **On Commodity Trading:** Management highlighted that adding commodities will improve capital efficiency for clients by allowing collateral usage on non-expiry days.
*   **On ESARs:** Management explained that ESARs are more efficient than ESOPs, with dilution estimated at <3% if the stock appreciates 20% annually.

### One-line takeaway
Nuvama delivered a record quarter with strong asset growth and profit, while successfully scaling new business lines like commercial real estate and offshore, despite some moderation expected in capital market-linked revenues.
