# Gaja Alternative Asset Management Ltd - Q2 FY27 concall
*Filed on BSE 2026-11-11 · Capital Markets & Wealth*

**Quarter:** Q2 FY27
**Tone:** Positive - Strong PAT growth (138% QoQ) driven by performance income and a strengthened, debt-free balance sheet post-IPO.

### What changed vs last quarter
- **Guidance:** Maintained (management reiterated steady progress on Fund V and Eastgate Secondaries targets).
- **Tone and demand view:** Shifted focus to the "fourth pillar" of income (treasury income) and the deployment of IPO proceeds.
- **New topics:** IPO proceeds deployment (10% of sponsor commitment, 80% of general corporate purposes utilized), tax write-back of Rs 15 crore, and the emergence of treasury income as a distinct revenue stream.
- **Last quarter's promises:** IPO proceeds were successfully integrated into the balance sheet; Fund V and Eastgate Secondaries are "making steady progress" as promised.

### Key numbers
- **Total Income (Q2 FY27):** Rs 65.5 crore (up 26% QoQ).
- **PAT (Q2 FY27):** Rs 64.7 crore (up 138% QoQ; 54% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 Total Income:** Rs 117.3 crore (up 6% YoY).
- **H1 FY27 PAT:** Rs 91.9 crore (up 48% YoY).
- **Cost-to-Income Ratio (H1 FY27):** 29% (vs 38% a year ago).
- **Net Worth:** Rs 1,106 crore (up 92% YoY).
- **Dividend:** Interim dividend of Rs 0.50 per share (10% of face value).

### Highlights
- IPO proceeds (Rs 450 crore fresh issue) fully integrated; company is now nearly debt-free (borrowings fell to Rs 0.2 crore).
- Performance income rose 55% QoQ, driven by carried interest from Fund II.
- Included a one-time tax write-back of Rs 15 crore due to revised tax provision estimates on sponsor commitment gains.
- Fund IV is 87% deployed (2 new investments during the quarter).
- Treasury income identified as a new, fourth pillar of income (11% of total income for 12 months to Sept 2026).

### Guidance & outlook
- Management reiterated that Fund V and Eastgate Secondaries Fund I are making steady progress toward capital raising targets.
- Management aims to raise sponsor commitment to 10% of fund size over time.
- Company has the capacity to sponsor approximately Rs 8,000 crore of new funds using current cash/liquid investments.

### Demand & industry commentary
- AIF commitments reached Rs 16.9 trillion in FY26; CRISIL projects 25-27% annual growth to FY30.
- Domestic institutions (NPS, insurers, banks) are increasingly opening up to AIFs.
- Management remains "perennially bullish" on India, viewing it as an independent asset class.

### Costs, margins & raw materials
- Total expenses for H1 fell 17% to Rs 34.6 crore.
- Cost-to-income ratio improved to 29% (from 38% a year ago).

### Capex, capacity & expansion
- Fund V (flagship): Seeking Rs 2,500 crore commitment.
- Eastgate Secondaries Fund I: Seeking Rs 1,500 crore commitment.
- Strengthened Eastgate team with a senior hire (20+ years experience).

### Balance sheet, debt & working capital
- Net worth: Rs 1,106 crore.
- Cash and liquid investments: Rs 690 crore.
- Debt: Nearly debt-free (Rs 0.2 crore).

### Risks & concerns flagged
- Performance income (carried interest and sponsor gains) is inherently lumpy on a quarter-to-quarter basis.
- Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties.

### Notable analyst Q&A
1. **Sharang Lele (IN360) on fee income vs opex:** Management clarified that fee income plus treasury income significantly covers total expenses.
2. **Sharang Lele (IN360) on Fund III carry:** Management explained they book carry only on a realized basis, not accrual; the fund must return principal and hurdle before carry is earned.
3. **Aman Agarwal (One Up) on investment opportunities:** Management highlighted four sectors: software/AI, financial services, consumer, and education/employability.
4. **Aman Agarwal (One Up) on worldview:** Management reiterated a long-term bullish stance on India, viewing it as an independent asset class.

### One-line takeaway
Gaja Alternative Asset Management delivered a strong Q2 FY27 with a 138% QoQ PAT surge, leveraging a debt-free, cash-rich balance sheet post-IPO to scale its multi-strategy platform.
